PCA and Ward clustering use the full seven-axis vectors. The ethics-map coordinates are Humane Regard = (P+A)/2 and Moral Structure = (C+T)/2. Cluster names remain intentionally provisional. v3 adds an eight-entry historical-agent layer (Lincoln, Amin, Hitler, Deng, Thatcher, Nasser, Johnson, Castro). Historical agents are shown as triangles, factions as diamonds, and fictional characters/entities as circles. The historical scores are provisional broad-period agent profiles rather than doctrine-only scores.
