# Cinderella Girls cross-property study

This is the sole first-read entrypoint for a retrospective comparison of the 2015 television anime, the Mobage and Starlight Stage mobile games, and the U149 television anime. The study asks which characterization and production patterns recur, how each property uses them, and what the evidence permits a reader to reconstruct about a particular incarnation.

The study is **active provisional**. Its analytical authority is comparative and bounded to existing integrated Git analyses. Each series retains authority over its own method, evidence, conclusions and revision history. The study's findings inherit the limitations of the specific sources cited.

## Current findings and limits

- The two anime foreground different conditions of participation. The 2015 series follows an ensemble through debut into competing production strategies. U149 centers the work adults must do to let children participate with agency. [Institutional comparison](CROSS_PROPERTY_SYNTHESIS.md#who-controls-the-conditions-of-participation)
- Akagi Miria's responsiveness to other people is a supported two-anime comparison. The game-to-2015 comparisons developed here concern how selected idols exercise care or negotiate their public personas. Their histories, relationships and performance registers remain scoped to their respective evidence. [Character comparisons](CHARACTER_INCARNATION_MAP.md)
- The 2015 corpus has a completed mainline analysis and a separately treated E26. The game materials have character-specific inventory and audio limits. U149's retrospective synthesis covers E01–E12 and OVA alongside a standalone E01–E09 and OVA corpus. No reviewed continuity bridge presently establishes a common timeline across these scopes. [Evidence and completeness by scope](CONTINUITY_AND_EVIDENCE_CROSSWALK.md)
