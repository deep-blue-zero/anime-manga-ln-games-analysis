# Evidence boundary and comparative method

The governing method for CGXP-V1 applies to retrospective, cross-property interpretation. The [crosswalk](CONTINUITY_AND_EVIDENCE_CROSSWALK.md) identifies admitted analyses and their retrieval order. The [entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) records scope and execution state.

## Evidence admission

Use integrated Git analyses of the 2015 anime, Mobage and Starlight Stage, and the U149 anime. Follow each analysis to its governing source declaration and locator where available. A series can be `GIT_PRIMARY` while an individual interpretation remains `active_provisional` or a specific reading is retained as `historical_legacy`. A historical artifact may establish its own knowledge horizon. Later corrections govern claims about the currently accepted interpretation.

The evidence actually examined for this study is repository text and metadata. Previously published visual, audio, and audiovisual findings retain the source project's inspection lane, sample and provenance. Written voice, audible delivery, staged performance, and production intent are distinct kinds of evidence. A source-located written line supplies wording and context. A claim about delivery also needs an inspected audio witness with an applicable locator. Primary media and extraction packets stay with their custodians.

The 2015 series distinguishes its mainline from E26 epilogue and paratext. U149 V1 is a transcript-derived recovery of prior episode analysis, including a retrospective synthesis with broader episode coverage than its standalone readings. The mobile project distinguishes Mobage and Starlight Stage source material even where its monographs reason across them. Keep game platform, event or commu, route or scenario where stated, and interaction context attached to each example. A unit name or reused song can help find comparable evidence. Its recurrence alone establishes no common occurrence.

Gakuen Idolmaster, the original THE IDOLM@STER, other franchise branches, U149 manga beyond the admitted U149 anime boundary, other manga and animation, drama CDs, concerts, supplementary publications and fan interpretation are outside the initial scope. Comparisons to those works inside an admitted document do not enlarge the evidence base. The broader [character discovery namespace](../../characters/registry/the-idolmaster.jsonl) serves identity lookup.

## What can be compared

| Object | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Franchise identity | Establishes why the properties are candidates for comparison. |
| Shared character entity | Establishes the franchise referent where the registry or explicit source identification supports it. Use existing registry identifiers verbatim and leave unregistered subjects without an invented ID. |
| Continuity-specific incarnation | Track the person as depicted within the admitted work or declared game synthesis boundary. Keep development, capacities, relationships, role, preferences and states bounded to that witness. |
| Adaptation-specific characterization | Attribute a feature to the work and medium that supply it. Cast role, dialogue, episode framing, player-facing interaction and gesture can establish different aspects of a depiction. |
| Source-specific event | Keep the local actors, before/after state, scenario and causal role. Parallel scenes can perform similar work while depicting distinct events. |
| Cross-media resemblance | Compare separately evidenced behavior in comparable situations and test whether the resemblance survives different role demands or stakes. |
| Continuity relationship | Requires a source-grounded bridge such as explicit event reference plus identifying context, or documented scope that establishes continuity. Test chronology, alternatives and conflicts before calling events shared. |
| Thematic or production parallel | A thematic parallel is an interpretation about narrative function. A production-history claim needs an admitted production witness establishing the relation. Surface recurrence alone leaves production intention and timeline open. |

## Claim strength and provenance

| Kind of claim | Minimum standard |
| --- | --- |
| Directly supported finding | Attribute the source observation to the analysis that records it. Provide work/platform, unit, section and claim ID or timestamp/scene locator when available. Record the evidence channel actually used. |
| Source-bounded interpretation | State whose interpretation is being used and its scope. Check it against the current claim or audit record and name material qualifications. |
| Cross-property inference | Supply an independent evidence route within each compared scope, state the common dimension, name asymmetries, and delimit the inference. A provisional source remains provisional even when another work offers a close parallel. |
| Competing interpretation | Give the rival a specific evidentiary basis. Say what each reading explains and what kind of observation or source could adjudicate it. Account for chronology, role, channel and inventory before calling a difference a contradiction. |
| Speculation or unresolved hypothesis | Mark it open and identify the evidence that would resolve it. A missing row or apparently missing behavior is a coverage limit unless the scope supports an absence claim. |

When using a source claim, prefer its latest eligible formulation, then follow the cited locator and any counterevidence. Record inherited qualifications alongside the claim. Treat two analyses that depend on the same source moment as one evidentiary route. Agent review tests the reasoning and attribution.

For character reconstruction, start from a named subject and distinguish behavior, interpretation, narrative role and medium. Require positive evidence for each transferred feature. Preserve mismatches in preferences, relationships, persona, voice and agency where known. Keep any future explicitly authorized composite labeled hypothetical and distinguish its borrowed features from supported incarnation models. The initial study builds no composite or capability grade.

Suspected source errors go to the owning project's correction route with claim or section, exact scope, counterevidence and proposed disposition. Compare before suggesting a change.
