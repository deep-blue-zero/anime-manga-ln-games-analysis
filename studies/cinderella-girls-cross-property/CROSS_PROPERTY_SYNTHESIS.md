# Cross-property synthesis

The current corpus supports a comparison of how idol work becomes possible, who can shape public visibility, and what performance can show about a relationship. It supports selected recurring forms of characterization. The [comparative method](EVIDENCE_BOUNDARY_AND_COMPARATIVE_METHOD.md) governs the distinctions between source interpretation and new inference. The [crosswalk](CONTINUITY_AND_EVIDENCE_CROSSWALK.md) supplies source authority and completeness.

## Who controls the conditions of participation?

The 2015 final synthesis reads the E13 ensemble breakthrough as a cause of the second cour's new institutional problem. Once the project can succeed, 346 Production has competing ways to value and develop its performers. Mishiro's Project:Krone confers genuine opportunities (`CG-PROD-0041`). The Autumn Live tests the ability to respond under pressure and what counts as evidence in managerial evaluation (`CG-PROD-0045`, `CG-INST-0030`). The finale leaves competing production philosophies in operation (`CG-INST-0035`, `CG-INST-0040`). [2015 full synthesis §§IV, X-A–D, XIII, XVI](../../series/the-idolmaster-cinderella-girls-2015-anime/08%20Final%20Synthesis%20and%20Release/CINDERELLA_GIRLS_FULL_SERIES_SYNTHESIS.md) · [2015 specialist 06 §§XXI–XXVIII](../../series/the-idolmaster-cinderella-girls-2015-anime/05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/06_346_PRODUCTION_INSTITUTIONS_AND_PHILOSOPHIES_OF_PRODUCTION.md)

U149's synthesis follows a group of children whose competence and eligibility are judged by adults. It connects the E09 rejection of the division's live proposal, when executives treat the Third Entertainment Division as still in a training phase, the E10 handmade live and the E12 scheduling accommodation that allows the group to participate within working-hours limits. These latter two details come from retrospective synthesis. The synthesis reads the children's involvement as real labor and advocacy as a responsibility attached to adult authority. It also identifies the limits of reforms that depend on an exceptional advocate and observes that emotionally meaningful repair can leave supervision procedures underdeveloped. [U149 E09, live proposal and institutional education](../../series/the-idolmaster-cinderella-girls-u149/V1%20Analysis/02%20Sequential%20Episode%20Readings/U149_E09_DEEP_READING.md) · [U149 synthesis §§3–4, 11, 20](../../series/the-idolmaster-cinderella-girls-u149/V1%20Analysis/06%20Full-Series%20Synthesis/U149_FULL_SERIES_SYNTHESIS.md)

**Comparative inference.** Each anime treats a professional opportunity as a designed condition of participation. The 2015 contest concerns who allocates resources, timing and evaluation after ensemble formation. U149 asks who can change rules and arrangements that would otherwise exclude children or expose them to risk. A competing reading would put care at the center of both series, and 2015's treatment of failure and developmental risk supports it. The distinction is one of emphasis: in U149 age and delegated adult responsibility shape who gets to participate in the first place. Both projects leave questions of durability and institutional scale unresolved. ## How the evidence portrays development

The 2015 reading of Nitta Minami links early peer trust, E13 illness and substitution, and E21 solo work in a bounded ensemble chronology. The mobile audit tests Minami's care and difficulty accepting care across dispersed interactions. It treats *Secret Daybreak* as a crystallization of earlier desires about work and family and gives Hayami Kanade a meaningful role in that event. The comparative inference is that the game evidence allows the analyst to test several formulations across repeated situations, while the anime evidence makes a smaller set of consequential transitions legible within the ensemble's chronology. The game event also has its own narrative development. The degree of evidence assembled, including inventory asymmetry and the monograph's selection of salient scenes, may account for some of the difference. This case supports a distinction in how these corpora represent development and gives limited grounds for a general account of either medium. [2015 specialist 04 §III, `CG-CHAR-0027`, `CG-REL-0017`, `CG-CHAR-0052`, `CG-REL-0048`](../../series/the-idolmaster-cinderella-girls-2015-anime/05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/04_CINDERELLA_PROJECT_CHARACTERS_UNITS_AND_UNIT_ECOLOGIES.md) · [Minami audit §II claims 18–22, 26, 28–29 and §§III–IV](../../series/the-idolmaster-cinderella-girls-mobile-games/08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/CINDERELLA_GIRLS_NITTA_MINAMI_MONOGRAPH_AUDIT.md)

Ranko offers a more controlled comparison of written speech choices. The 2015 E08 reading follows the Producer learning how Ranko's theatrical idiom communicates. Deresute Story Commu 16 and Mobage Refresh Room 46 show Ranko choosing registers appropriate to two different listeners. Because the comparison concerns when an idiom becomes communicatively available, it survives the change in setting and interlocutor. An account of acoustic delivery or a shared sequence of relationships would require further evidence. ## Who can choose what a persona reveals?

The 2015 specialist account of Ranko and Nana treats deliberately authored styles as a form of expression under production demands. In the mobile Kaede corpus, *Pretty Liar* tests how deliberately chosen glamour can belong alongside the dimensions of Kaede emphasized by ordinary-scale material. In U149 E04, commercially framed versions of Momoka impose incompatible demands, including a version of ordinariness imposed from outside. The prior reading emphasizes Momoka's choice about what she will express through cultivated style. Across these examples the study finds that authorship and use of a persona better explain its authenticity than visual plainness alone. The comparison has different stakes in each case, from negotiating enterprise branding to a child's authority over what a production reveals. ## Performance and the audience

2015 specialist 07 distinguishes relationship, professional competence and institutional stakes when it assesses performances. E06 dramatizes a misreading of audience response (`CG-PERF-0004`, `CG-PERF-0006`), and the E15 Kaede example makes a previously established audience relationship relevant to venue choice (`CG-PERF-0013`, `CG-PERF-0030`). U149 E03 puts Akagi Miria's accidental livestream in a different position. Attention can become warm reciprocation while the broadcast also opens questions about commerce, strangers' access and adult supervision. A comparative inference is that attention is only one component of success. The 2015 analysis asks whose recognition matters and how competence becomes legible. U149 puts the obligations created by child visibility in the foreground. The scenes differ in format as well as participant age. Further comparisons should pair comparable venue, audience and supervision conditions before attributing the difference to age. ## Producer authorship and access to interiority

The U149 specialist's comparison with TakeuchiP emphasizes how readily YonaiP's uncertainty and failure become legible. Its description of TakeP as comparatively monolithic is most useful as a claim about social readability. The 2015 specialist documents TakeP's development in practice, from communication failures and repair in E05–E07 through the pressures of Krone and the later articulation of developmental authorship. These accounts can coexist if the comparison distinguishes access to interiority from observable revision in professional conduct. The shared occupational role supports comparing professional ethics. Neither the framing contrast nor this difference in analytical emphasis supplies identity or continuity evidence. More extensive material for a character in one property can make the same feature easier to notice there. Seek positive local counterexamples and aligned situations before proposing any three-property reconstruction.
- **Scene-level U149 ending and mobile performance.** The missing U149 E10–E12 readings leave event-level claims in the final movement dependent on the retrospective synthesis. Future authorized work should test the scheduling and handmade-live interpretations with bounded source locators. For spoken performance in the mobile comparisons, match equivalent speech situations against actual audio and retain any sample limitations. Expanded Mobage recovery would help test the apparent distribution of relationship and register patterns. These questions remain open within the study's current admitted evidence.
