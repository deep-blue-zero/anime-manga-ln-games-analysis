## Maintenance and expansion

For each proposed comparison, verify the continuity and subject against the series maps and [character discovery rules](../../characters/README.md). Then trace the current interpretation to the most specific evidence in each scope. Where a registered subject is unavailable, use named source sections and record the discovery gap without creating a registry entry.

The incarnation map accumulates supported comparative portraits. The synthesis owns comparative arguments and rivals. The originating claim records remain the route for source-level revisions. Revise the dependent study comparison when stronger evidence or a narrower scope warrants it. Preserve the prior formulation and disposition in Git history and keep a brief in-place revision note when a material change would otherwise obscure the earlier reading. Archive or supersede eligible artifacts through the [archive policy](../../governance/source-policies/ARCHIVE_AUTHORITY_AND_SUPERSESSION_POLICY.md). Discovery and reconstruction metadata changes go to their designated maintainers.

Verify the evidence routes and reconcile material conflicts before extending a character comparison or adopting it in synthesis. Keep in-scene knowledge and development separate from retrospective explanation, especially across frozen 2015 horizons and distinct game scenarios. A comparison is ready for provisional use when its source authority, locators, counterevidence and limits have been reviewed. Repository validation governs publication. The first study generation remains mutable and has no frozen release. No sequential or longitudinal source completion is claimed by the study.

Use `BOUNDED_STANDARD` for retrieval and link checks, `SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS` for bounded character comparisons and `DEEP_SYNTHESIS` for cross-property contradictions or interpretive integration. Review currentness, evidence routes, limitations and named subjects before acceptance. The integrator owns study edits and publication. Read-only reviewers can challenge comparative reasoning against the same integrated evidence.

Future studies of U149 E10–E12 at scene resolution, expanded Mobage evidence, or genuinely aligned audiovisual performance require separate source authorization through the appropriate series project. Evidence-backed additions to the comparative boundary should first revise this architecture and the method. Until then, retrospective work proceeds through the indexed analytical evidence.
