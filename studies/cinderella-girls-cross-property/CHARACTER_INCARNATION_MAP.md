## Reconstruction limits

The supported pairs give repeatable prompts for incarnation-specific work, such as how Ranko adjusts to a listener or what competing responsibilities guide Mika's response to Rika. Use the corresponding locator to determine what the character knows and can do at that point. Test preferences, counterpart relationships, written voice and actual vocal delivery separately. Comparable action under different conditions supports a bounded recurring pattern. A franchise-wide composite, a three-property personality profile or a shared timeline remains open.
