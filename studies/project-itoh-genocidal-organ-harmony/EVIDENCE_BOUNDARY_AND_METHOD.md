# Evidence boundary and comparative method

## 1. Task and analytical responsibility

This is an **already-completed-novels, retrospective comparative study**, not a new prospective sequence. It assesses (a) chronological event identity across *Genocidal Organ* and *Harmony*; (b) the extent to which Clavis Shepherd's actions explain the Maelstrom; and (c) thematic transformations between the first novel's violence-protection calculus and the second novel's welfare, medical control and consciousness projects. New claims belong to this study; existing sequential readings and their freezes retain their historical knowledge boundaries.

The **[entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md)** owns current project routing, the **[chronology](CHRONOLOGY_AND_CAUSAL_RECONSTRUCTION.md)** owns event reconstruction, and the **[comparative synthesis](COMPARATIVE_ETHICS_AND_FORM.md)** owns the interpretive argument. ## 3. Evidentiary categories

Use exactly the following comparison-specific grades. A higher grade concerns **textual support for the proposition as stated**, not political approval, truth outside the fiction, or the omniscience of a narrator.

- **D — Directly depicted/explicitly reported:** character speech, narrative action or embedded historical entry exists in the witness. Always identify **who** says it and whether the statement reports action, belief or institutional history.
- **C — Strong cross-work continuity inference:** the distinct works furnish specific mutually reinforcing identifiers, matching event structure and distinctive wording; no explicit on-page identity statement joins every step.
- **P — Plausible mediated causal hypothesis:** a proposed link extends beyond both novels' directly displayed stages, potentially involving additional agents or mechanisms. A missing link must remain visible.
- **T — Thematic/structural interpretation:** explanatory reading supported by patterns across scenes; competing interpretations and disanalogies are retained.
- **U — Unresolved/unsupported:** an attractive but unverified claim, not promoted to chronology.

These categories are not probability percentages. Do not collapse a fictional dictionary's reported date into an independent real-world historical fact. Do not make Clavis's self-reported expected future identical to a later character's historical memory by silent narrator fusion.

## 5. Adversarial alternative readings and tests

**Alternative A — An homage rather than shared world.** Two novels by the same author can repeat motifs without diegetic continuity. The unusually distinctive pair of US-origin epidemic killing and "organ for genocide", together with the American-to-global order of events and the 2019 historical dating, makes this alternative materially weaker than connected-event interpretation. It remains true that *Harmony* never prints "Clavis Shepherd caused the Maelstrom".

**Alternative B — The English grammar alone mechanically accounts for all later calamity.** It explains the initiation in GO's focal account. It does not by itself document each later ethnic faction, seized warhead, nuclear user, medical catastrophe or decision by post-state institutions. Those outcomes remain mediated, with independent agency.

**Alternative C — The health society is merely authoritarian backlash.** The source also gives a serious survival case: radiation, mass illness, social breakdown and real medical benefits. The more critical conclusion concerns how emergency necessity can support unconsented intervention and outlive the emergency, not a claim that medicine is inherently tyranny.

**Alternative D — Clavis, Nuaza and Miaha are equivalent villains.** Their targets and rationales differ: Clavis's self-punishment coupled to sacrificing compatriots; Nuaza's fear-driven emergency reserve with limits; Miaha's attempted elimination of consciousness in a developed order. Compare their authority claims without erasing their distinct decision situations.

**Alternative E — Public truth alone prevents catastrophe.** Lucia's demand for exposure and public answerability is serious, not foolish. The epilogue nevertheless stages one disclosure channel that enables harmful transmission. The contrast challenges naïve publicity-as-sufficiency; it does not vindicate secrecy or surveillance as an unqualified alternative.
