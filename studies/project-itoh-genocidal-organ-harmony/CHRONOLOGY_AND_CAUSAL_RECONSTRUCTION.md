# From the English Grammar to the Maelstrom: A Cross-Novel Chronology

## Thesis and evidentiary caution

*Genocidal Organ* closes with Clavis Shepherd intentionally releasing English-language genocidal grammar into the United States; its epilogue already reports widespread American riots, armed repression and deaths, but explicitly holds civil war and genocidal mass killing ahead of its observed endpoint. *Harmony* later describes the Maelstrom (**大災禍**) as major riots centered on North America and the English-speaking world **in 2019**, followed by widespread interethnic slaughter, international disorder, the dispersal and use of American nuclear weapons, radiation-related medical crises and a postnational welfare-medical response.

The correspondence is unusually specific. Keita Saeki's retrospective description of Americans as if born with **an organ for genocide** closely evokes the *Genocidal Organ* mechanism and title. **The strongest supported reconstruction is that Clavis initiates the American phase of the Maelstrom.** A detailed history of how every subsequent foreign government, faction, nuclear weapon and health institution behaved is **not** supplied by *Genocidal Organ*. The novels offer an event-level bridge and a historical aftermath, not an unbroken camera recording of each causal intermediate.

Evidence grades **D** (direct source report or depicted event), **C** (strong cross-work continuity inference), **P** (plausible but unverified intermediate cause), **T** (thematic interpretation) and **U** (unsupported) are defined and owned by [the method](EVIDENCE_BOUNDARY_AND_METHOD.md). Direct speech is not automatically an omniscient fact: Clavis's retrospective self-report, Harmony's fictional dictionary, Miaha's recollection and Keita Saeki's political interpretation have different access and incentives.

## 1. Before America's breakdown — protected peace and outsourced killing

**D / *Genocidal Organ*, GO-U027-E001–E003.** John Paul explains the geographical partition behind his intervention. He deploys the fictional genocidal grammar in distant societies, adapting it to their languages, and claims to have withheld an English-language version to protect his own American world from uncontrolled spreading effects. His violence is neither random nor only an attempt to prove humanity evil. John acknowledges that care for a protected group and violence against outsiders can coexist; he refuses to call this “justice” and does not regret knowingly weighing lives. His wife and child's loss gives biographical force to his declared priorities but does not establish moral authorization by his victims.

The social background is not the moral opposite of the later *Harmony* world merely because Americans retain private consumption and civilian habits. Surveillance, identity verification, covert force, remote warfare, consumer comfort and professionalized moral distance are already part of the earlier order. The comparative hinge is a protected domestic normality that systematically depends on danger being located **elsewhere**. See [GO-U027](../../series/genocidal-organ/01%20Sequential%20Readings/GO_U027_PART_V_SUBSECTION_5.md) and the [GO integrated synthesis](../../series/genocidal-organ/06%20Novel%20Synthesis/GENOCIDAL_ORGAN_INTEGRATED_NOVEL_SYNTHESIS.md).

**Temporal caution:** the work's publication in 2007 does not establish that its diegetic epilogue happens in 2007. The 2019 anchor comes from *Harmony*, later than the earlier novel's publication. Nothing in the inspected GO epilogue explicitly labels its events “2019.”

## 2. The counterproposal — witness, trial and accountable disclosure

**D / GO-U027-E004–E009.** Lucia demands that the mechanism and its effects be exposed to a public capable of judgment; John is pressed to submit himself to legal and civic answerability. The plan is disrupted by deaths and Clavis's decision to kill John. However, the opportunity to go public survives in another form: John's notebook and the address for a fictional grammar-generating editor reach Clavis. The epilogue subsequently shows hearings and the circulation of Clavis's testimony.

This is causally important, not an aside to the biological-fiction device. **The infrastructure of public account-giving becomes the route for a harmful linguistic performance.** The novel does not thereby make Lucia's political demand foolish or justify permanent secrecy: the actual disaster emerges from **Clavis's choice** to weaponize access. The distinction matters for the later *Harmony* world's tendency to treat unfettered human choice as a danger to be managed.

## 3. Clavis deliberately initiates American disorder

**D / GO-U028-E004/E007–E009; original `text/part0039.html`.** Clavis leaves the service, accesses his mother's preserved records and recognizes the limits of his previous moral self-account. He uses the address left by John, a fictional text-producing editor, to compose a piece he treats like a musical score, deliberately attending to the sonic rhythm of his testimony. The target is Americans; he wishes them to kill one another as populations abroad have killed. Transcripts, video, sound and public hearing materials circulate what he presents as English-language genocidal deep grammar: `英語による虐殺の深層文法`.

The source explicitly attributes the nationwide escalation to the grammar in Clavis's retrospective account. This is not a merely coincidental pairing of a strange speech and unrelated riots. His claimed intention and the narrated countrywide effects converge, and the special meaning of English reverses John's earlier restriction. This gives strong **in-novel** support for violent intentional authorship. It does not provide a real-world psychological algorithm, show every individual's causal exposure, or prove Clavis's exclusive responsibility for each act.

At the narrated endpoint, Clavis reports **existing** riots, many deaths, state forces firing on civilians, looted military stockpiles and disintegrating consumer normality. Surviving networks **predict** civil war, and he explicitly says killing on the scale he calls genocide has **not yet** occurred. He prepares and defends his own private food supply while believing he has chosen national sacrifice for the good of people outside America. The distinction between what **has happened** and what he **expects** is a hard boundary, not a stylistic technicality. [GO-U028](../../series/genocidal-organ/01%20Sequential%20Readings/GO_U028_EPILOGUE.md), especially E007–E009.

## 4. The 2019 historical anchor in *Harmony*

**D / *Harmony*, HAR-U003-E002; original `text/part0006_split_003.html`.** An in-world dictionary entry discussing American **RRW** nuclear warheads calls the Maelstrom the major riots that began **in 2019**, centered on North America and English-speaking countries: `二〇一九年に発生した北米を中心とする英語圏での大暴動〈大災禍〉`.

The description supplies three distinctive historical identifiers:

1. The disturbances **begin around North America and English-speaking regions**, matching the decisive switch to **English** in Clavis's deployment.
2. The now-collapsed American national framework had manufactured significant stocks of replacement nuclear warheads.
3. The chaos permits nuclear matériel to escape, setting up later international wars.

The dictionary attributes its counts to an in-fiction IAEA source: **35 missing warheads**, **14 recovered**, **two on American territory**, **19 used in conflicts around the world**. These figures partition the 35: 14 + 2 + 19 = 35. “Recovered” does not mean never lost, and foreign uses do not prove that each weapon was used by the same type of actor. Treat the numbers as the fictional dictionary's claims, not as a primary historian's audited physical inventory. [HAR-U003](../../series/harmony/01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U003_PART_I_03_DEEP_READING.md), E002.

**C / Event identity:** the GO American riots and HAR 2019 American/Anglophone riots are strongly supported as **the same initiating historical event**. The rare convergence of locus, linguistic range, catastrophe and the distinctive organ-for-genocide allusion makes an independent unrelated riot a weak explanation. This is more than a general literary resemblance. Nevertheless, the historical entry gives no personal identification of Clavis and does not specify every step between his performance and its official dating. “*Genocidal Organ* ends at the start of the Maelstrom” is the strongest useful shorthand, with that boundary attached.

## 5. What the later witness says happened next

**D / HAR-U009-E006–E008; original `text/part0008_split_003.html`.** Keita Saeki discusses the collapse of the previously dominant American state. His memory encompasses mass riots, ethnic massacres involving Americans of different backgrounds, the spread of unrest to other nations, and escaped nuclear warheads put to use in nuclear terrorism. He compares the intensity of the killing to people being born with an **organ for genocide**: `虐殺するための器官を生得的に持っているかのように`.

This is the most precise **cross-work verbal pointer**, and it is also evidence that the catastrophe has become an **officially taught memory**. Keita is a later historical witness and interested defender of the benefits of the present medical order, not a narrator with access to Clavis's private intentions. Another remembered observation gives a North American toll of **ten million dead**, spoken in Miaha's earlier historical talk and later recalled by Tuan. A numerical historical claim should retain this channel and not be conflated with the casualty count observed at the end of GO. [HAR-U009](../../series/harmony/01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U009_PART_II_03_DEEP_READING.md), E006–E008.

**D / HAR-U002-E016 and HAR-U003-E002.** *Harmony*'s remembered schoolbook account has widespread fallout, cancer and illnesses; it speculates about novel viruses associated with nuclear effects. The nuclear crisis and environmental cleanup are present within the story world, including engineered sunflowers drawing pollutants from damaged soil. The biological origin of **every** described disease is not scientifically established by the fictional history lesson. The novels' representation of consequences can be analyzed without treating speculative epidemiology as verified real-world biology.

**P / The missing middle:** the most plausible macro-history is that American mass violence and collapse provide opportunities for seizure and external proliferation of American weapons, which in turn compound already troubled international conditions. Neither witness provides a person-by-person history of arms transfers, official containment attempts, every nuclear detonation and each later institutional settlement. The Maelstrom is larger than Clavis's original performance. An intentional initiating act does not equal sole or necessarily foreseeable authorship of the later worldwide sequence.

## 6. From disaster relief to health government

**D / HAR-U002-E016, HAR-U009-E006–E008.** The later history describes a movement away from the old political-economic state order toward **life governments (生府)** and an extensive medical-welfare regime. What becomes normative is the reciprocal duty to protect life and treat human bodies as socially valuable resources. Preventative medicine, WatchMe, continuous monitoring, censorship or disfavor of certain bodily pleasures, rapid infectious-corpse disposal, and pressure to be considerate are not identical technologies or rules. Their shared political logic is that crisis recovery makes health and personal conduct matters of collective obligation.

The social response cannot simply be reduced to “bad people exploited an emergency.” Saeki's recollections foreground real survivors, mass sickness, contaminated environments and significant medical improvement. His analogy of society as a spaceship needing life support is a serious justification **within the story**. Tuan nevertheless asks what happens when inherited precautions and moral obligations persist as dangers diminish. A later commentator's description of the regime as “benevolent” does not exhaust the experience of people unable to refuse its care.

### Institutional-memory mechanism

The Maelstrom persists at least three ways:

- **Materially:** contaminated landscapes, cleanup programs, changed bodies and practices around burial and medicine.
- **Politically:** long-serving survivors retain positions of authority, binding emergency memories to governance.
- **Pedagogically:** history curricula give extensive time to the catastrophe and compress more distant events, shaping what citizens perceive as possible alternatives.

All three make the Maelstrom an enduring political resource. The later regime is not merely an emotional recoil: it becomes a durable system of incentives, expertise and narratives. [HAR-U009](../../series/harmony/01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U009_PART_II_03_DEEP_READING.md), E006–E008.

## 7. The next protective escalation: governing volition

**D / HAR-U014-E003–E005/E011–E012 and HAR-U019-E008–E012.** Nuaza Kirie and associated elites describe research toward managing decision and reward systems; prevention of another catastrophe supplies a rationale for exceptional emergency capability. The group reportedly installs a dormant means of intervention without informed public consent. Miaha and another faction press to turn the potential capacity into a present transformation by arranging conditions that make the decision-makers activate it. Their schemes differ, even as both involve authority over people who have not freely authorized the relevant manipulation.

The society is responding to a crisis historically connected, at least by strong inference, to **coerced collective behavior**. Yet its proposed safety mechanism also involves **coerced behavioral or consciousness modification**. This is the central historical irony of the paired books: the means and ends of protection become dangerously difficult to separate.

**U / Technological identity not established.** No original witness shows that the genocidal language system is an ancestor, software component, or scientifically equivalent mechanism of WatchMe, neural networks or harmonics. The parallel concerns **who controls behavior, whose refusal counts, what protective rationale is offered, and how authority over another person's body or mind is justified**.

## 9. Strongest counterarguments

**“The novels merely share a motif.”** Possible in the abstract. It is weak against (i) GO's English-specific USA disaster, (ii) the HAR fictional 2019 description centered on American/Anglophone riots, and (iii) Keita's precisely corresponding “organ for genocide” simile. The safest formulation remains **deliberate, strongly implied event continuity**, not an explicitly named Clavis biography in HAR.

**“Clavis must have known about the later nuclear exchange.”** He knows and intends American chaos; he does not, in the observed epilogue, supply a complete forecast of 35 missing warheads or named future nuclear users. Retrospective recognition of consequences should not turn partial intentions into omniscience.

**“The world simply learns the correct lesson and thus becomes peaceful.”** The reality of catastrophe and medical benefit is acknowledged. But *Harmony* repeatedly asks whether avoiding violent conflict by guaranteeing socially agreeable action transfers rather than resolves the problem of consent, moral conflict and who gets to define safe choices.

**“Two variants of mind control establish one technological canon.”** Unsupported. The novels make different fictional technological claims. Their **institutional and ethical topology** is comparable without declaring a shared codebase, scientific explanation, or succession of researchers.

## 10. What remains open

- Does some part of another authorized primary witness or Project Itoh paratext name Clavis or give an unambiguous authorial chronology? Not established by this **two-novel** witness boundary.
- What happens between the first American riots, international weapon dispersal and the establishment of life governments? Partly narrated in retrospective compressed form, not fully reconstructible.
- How reliable are specific numerical and national-origin claims of *Harmony*'s in-universe authorities? They are strong *diegetic* claims but have contested speakers and no independent in-world audit reproduced here.
- Can the collective sacrifice in Clavis's ending be distinguished coherently from the collective sacrifice imposed by later emergency custodians? See the [comparative synthesis](COMPARATIVE_ETHICS_AND_FORM.md) for the strongest affirmative and critical accounts.

The resulting historical proposition is narrow enough to defend and strong enough to matter: **Clavis's American act is the best-evidenced onset of a disaster *Harmony* remembers as the Maelstrom; the later worldwide and institutional history cannot be telescoped into the last pages of *Genocidal Organ*.**
