# Project Itoh — *Genocidal Organ* and *Harmony*

## Current state and sole first-read entrypoint

This is the sole current entrypoint for the **two-novel comparative study** of *Genocidal Organ* (虐殺器官, 2007) and *Harmony* (ハーモニー, 2008), by Project Itoh (伊藤計劃). It asks how the American collapse engineered at the end of the former relates to the catastrophe called **大災禍** (the Maelstrom) in the latter, and what the connection does to their accounts of violence, protection, responsibility, information, government and consciousness.

### Findings at a glance — with graded authority

1. **Strong textual continuity:** *Harmony* describes an American-origin catastrophe in 2019, with widespread interethnic killing and the pointed expression **虐殺するための器官** (an organ for genocide). This closely and deliberately evokes the *Genocidal Organ* ending. The origin of the crisis is strongly supported as the same fictional event.
2. **Clavis's role:** *Genocidal Organ* directly depicts Clavis Shepherd's purposeful distribution of English-language genocidal grammar, the sudden US-wide violence, and his declared wish to plunge the United States into chaos. That supplies a highly supported initiating cause for the American phase later historicized in *Harmony*.
3. **Do not erase the temporal gap:** *Genocidal Organ* ends while further civil war/genocide is still prospective. *Harmony* retrospectively reports ensuing international spread, missing nuclear warheads, nuclear use, health crises and institutional reconstruction. **The complete intervening causal chain is not shown.**
4. **Central comparative argument:** deliberate catastrophe justified as protection of a chosen population eventually becomes a formative justification for comprehensive medical and behavioral protection; this recurrence is ethically revealing, but it does not make John Paul, Clavis, Nuaza and Miaha identical actors.
5. **No new series-canon admission:** these are retrospective cross-work claims derived from the completed novel witnesses and existing finished branch readings; neither series' frozen sequential texts, predictions or evidence ledgers are rewritten.

### Known limits / review obligations

- The specific event identity is stronger than a mere thematic parallel, but no named posthumous Clavis confession appears in *Harmony*. The phrase about an organ for genocide is Keita Saeki's **historical comparison**, not a demonstration that all later killings were individually caused by exposure to linguistic grammar.
- *Harmony*'s 2019 dating comes from an in-universe dictionary; *Genocidal Organ*'s epilogue itself supplies **no independently explicit 2019 timestamp**. Date alignment is retrospective, not independently synchronized watches.
- The novels' Japanese witnesses were hash-matched and the key original passages inspected. This is **targeted comparative verification**, not an independent full sequential re-reading of both books or a certification of every edition/translation.
- Interpretations and original novel source excerpts remain separated. No source EPUB, private verification packet, credential or substantial copyrighted prose is committed in this public study.
- Review should test naming clarity, timeline boundaries, strength-of-causation grades, links, and whether ideological parallels are overclaimed. The study is not author-accepted merely because it exists on a branch.
