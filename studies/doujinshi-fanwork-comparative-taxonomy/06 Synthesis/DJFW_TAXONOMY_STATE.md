## Taxonomy update — DJFW_CASE_0002-0010 continuous-sequential tranche

The 0002-0010 tranche materially extends the taxonomy in six ways.

1. **Creator-side character-collapse awareness.** Fanwork authors can themselves reason about the boundary DJFW measures. The telomereNA Minami afterwords show distinct forms of self-audit: recognizing when visual appeal can overwhelm character, using a canon trait as a transformation bridge, stating that stronger role change would require diegetic time, retaining Minami-specific limits on what the author wants to depict, and retrospectively criticizing earlier work for approaching character collapse. Paratext should therefore be coded not merely as intent but as evidence of the author's transformation model.

2. **Transformation distance and bridge sufficiency.** `Maid Shujuu Lovers` makes explicit a general distinction between surface role-play and deeper personality change. A fanwork may intensify an existing capacity immediately, but a larger character-state transition requires a bridge: time, causation, altered relationship state, AU ontology, or other narrative mechanism. DJFW should distinguish `surface intensification`, `bridged transformation`, and `unbridged replacement` rather than treating all OOC distance as one variable.

3. **Form-relative fidelity.** `Shirayoi no Yado` and the mameojitan Hiro segment demonstrate that long-form and short-form fidelity should not be forced onto one scalar. The former achieves broad structural translation across body, vocation, atmosphere, and relationship; the latter achieves unusually dense relational/voice compression in a few safe pages. Comparative claims should specify whether they concern longitudinal depth, structural translation, micro-fidelity, or short-form compression.

4. **Authored difficulty versus usable fragility.** The Hiro cases sharpen a collapse-risk distinction. A work can accurately notice a vulnerable body while still misread the character if it turns that vulnerability into an externally activated mechanism. Hiro fidelity depends on preserving her authorship of difficult conditions, internally coherent low-force evaluation, ordinary longing, and the ethical change produced by accountable attachment. Frailty alone is not fidelity.

5. **Visual presence versus character authorship.** `DJFW_CASE_0010` establishes a clean lower-bound control: a recognizable design can be visibly present while voice, decisions, relational grammar, and subjecthood are unnecessary to the work's mechanism. DJFW should therefore treat `visual identity retained / character authorship absent` as distinct from ordinary `out of character` writing. The latter still attempts a character model; the former may only route an interchangeable design through a creator-specific machine.

6. **Negative controls as positive taxonomic evidence.** Cases 0006 and 0010 show that low literary/character fidelity can have high taxonomic value when they isolate a failure mode cleanly. `frailty-as-switch` and `creator-machine interchangeability` should remain explicit negative-control categories rather than being discarded as analytically weak works.

Revision state: **STRENGTHEN + REVISE**. The inherited axes remain valid, but the tranche adds transformation-distance/bridge sufficiency, creator self-audit, form-relative fidelity, visual-presence-without-authorship, and negative-control value as explicit comparative concepts. The current control sheet and continuous-sequential case readings remain the evidentiary homes for individual judgments.
