# DJFW_CASE_0009 — GakuMas Icha Love Ero Goudou: Dosukebe mode!! — Continuous Sequential Reading V2

## 0. Governing judgment

The continuous pass materially revises the first-pass judgment. Hiro fidelity is no longer OPEN.

The anthology's Hiro contribution, by **まめおじたん / mameojitan**, is printed p19-p26 and appears in archive-image order at approximately p021-p028. It is one of the strongest short-form Hiro fanwork segments currently in DJFW because its non-graphic setup and closing dialogue independently reconstruct the canonical relation between **frailty, impossible aspiration, Producer attachment, and chosen difficulty**.

The erotic middle remains outside detailed description. It is not needed to establish the key finding.

## 2. Opening: Hiro's body as a real problem, not the whole person

The Hiro segment opens in the school infirmary after another minor physical mishap. The exchange immediately does something `Ichijou 10-man Yen` does not.

P treats Hiro's fragility as consequential to her idol trajectory. Hiro responds in her low-force, faintly dry register: she notes that she knows how to break a fall and acknowledges the recurring problem without turning it into melodrama. P apologizes for worrying her / expresses concern that the physical limitation could become a hindrance as she advances.

This is baseline-aware because the body is neither ignored nor fetishized as the sole source of interest. It is a **constraint on a shared project**.

## 3. The shared-dream grammar

The next safe conversational movement strengthens the relation. P frames the pair as bound together in pursuing a shared dream; Hiro reacts with a mix of embarrassment, teasing, and evaluative calm rather than generic submission or generic romance.

Most importantly, the segment uses P's attraction to Hiro's dangerous/difficult form as something Hiro can notice and tease him about. She effectively asks whether he has developed a habit of satisfying a kind of self-destructive impulse through her. The joke is character-shaped because it recognizes a real canonical ambiguity: P follows someone who repeatedly chooses paths that make ordinary success harder.

Hiro's response is not “please save me from being fragile.” She understands the strange reciprocal appeal of a relationship where each party can become a constraint on the other.

## 4. Closing line as Hiro thesis

The final archive page of the segment, p028, returns to non-graphic relational dialogue. Hiro describes the pair as continuing down a path that will not go smoothly while placing “shackles” or constraints on one another, and concludes that this sounds **very fun**.

That is unusually strong fanwork compression of the canonical Hiro arc.

Canonical mature Hiro does not stop liking difficulty. What changes is that difficulty is no longer privately consequence-free. Producer, friends, fans, rivals, and the shared future make it accountable. The mameojitan segment understands this by making mutual constraint itself part of the pleasure.

The key distinction is:

> **difficulty remains attractive because attachment makes it harder, not despite attachment making it harder.**

That is much closer to “chosen impossibility becoming accountable attachment” than any other short R18 Hiro segment currently processed.

## 5. Voice

Voice fidelity is **medium-high to high in the safe dialogue frame**, with lower authority for the explicit middle because it is not transcribed.

Preserved:

- low-force delivery of strange ideas;
- bodily fragility treated analytically rather than hysterically;
- teasing that emerges from coherent Hiro logic;
- embarrassment as a local perturbation rather than personality replacement;
- attraction to a difficult path;
- ability to reinterpret relational constraint as interesting rather than purely burdensome.

The segment does intensify romantic/sexual confidence beyond ordinary canon, but it does so using Hiro-shaped reasoning rather than replacing reasoning with generic affection.

## 6. Producer relation

This is the strongest element.

P is neither a pure caretaker nor a pure access device. He worries about the cost of Hiro's body to her future, accepts that their dreams are linked, and is himself teased for being attracted to the difficult life that relationship creates.

The relation is fanwork-romanticized, but the romance grows from an already canonical structure: **our futures have become entangled enough that your difficulty constrains me and my attachment constrains you.**

That is accountable attachment, not ownership.

## 7. Contributor afterword as ethical boundary

The p059 contributor page adds a subtle but important constraint. Mameojitan states a self-imposed limit against depicting Hiro/P as wholly consequence-blind in a particular way. The explicit detail is unnecessary here; what matters is the authorial principle.

Even inside an R18 anthology, this contributor does not treat “anything goes once the scenario begins” as the pair's grammar. Some form of caution/responsibility must remain.

That meta-evidence aligns with the segment's closing mutual-constraint thesis and raises confidence that the fidelity is intentional rather than accidental.

## 8. Subjecthood

Hiro subjecthood is **substantially preserved for short-form anthology scale**.

She has a recognizable problem, a view of that problem, a view of P, an interpretation of their shared future, and a preference about what kind of difficult life is interesting. The segment would not survive character substitution without rewriting its opening and conclusion.

This is still only eight printed pages. It cannot contain the breadth of a full character study. But within micro-form, Hiro is author rather than decoration.

## 9. Canon relation and transformation pressure

Primary pressure: **intimacy as mutual constraint**.

The work is not canon-compatible as an event, but its relational thesis is strongly canon-derived.

## 12. Canonical formulation

> **The mameojitan Hiro segment in `Dosukebe mode!!` is a compact but unusually accurate transformation of Hiro's mature relational problem. It begins with bodily fragility as a real obstacle to a shared idol future and ends by making mutual constraint itself part of the appeal of the path. The segment understands that Hiro does not want difficulty eliminated; she wants a difficult form she can still choose after other people have become accountable to it.**
