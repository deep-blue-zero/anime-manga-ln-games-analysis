# DJFW_CASE_0006 — Ichijou 10-man Yen — Continuous Sequential Reading V2

## 0. Governing judgment

The continuous pass confirms the first-pass negative-control judgment and makes its failure mode more precise.

`Ichijou 10-man Yen` recognizes **Hiro's bodily limit** but does not adequately preserve **Hiro's authorship of difficulty**. The book is built around an externally introduced pharmacological premise whose consequences overtake her body. That uses a real Hiro vulnerability—low physical reserve and unusual bodily consequences—but removes the distinction that makes canonical Hiro difficult: she is not interesting merely because bad things happen to a fragile body. She actively chooses impossible conditions, evaluates them with strange coherence, and eventually learns that chosen difficulty becomes ethically different when other people are attached to its outcome.

The fanwork converts that architecture into a switch.

## 1. Source lock and sequence

Locked source: 10 pages, SHA-256 `a717e21c6850ede93b02655ebd44ffcc3e3d547ce0a609b2a47be2b7f56ad1f2`.

Sequentially:

- **p001-p002:** the pharmacological premise is established immediately; Producer is the source/mediator of the trigger.
- **p003-p008:** the imposed bodily consequence dominates the work. Hiro has reactions and some recognizable calm/oddity around the situation, but the scenario's mechanism rather than her decision-making organizes the sequence.
- **p009-p010:** the book ends in an exaggerated dark-comic aftermath emphasizing that the effect has pushed her far beyond an ordinary physical limit.

There is almost no external story ecology, career context, friendship network, or longitudinal relationship movement. The premise is the story.

## 2. The canonical Hiro distinction it misses

The high-load Hiro comparator is **chosen impossibility becoming accountable attachment**.

Canonical Hiro has a structurally weak body, but competence does not mean becoming physically normal. Her mature form gains expressive surplus while remaining mismatched. Early on, difficult experiences can be pleasurable because they are consequence-light and privately hers. Later, friends, fans, rivalry, shared dreams, and Producer make failure less privately consumable. Her relationship with Producer becomes entangled enough that “my interesting failure” can also become “our future's cost.”

`Ichijou 10-man Yen` removes that chain. The unusual physical state is not meaningfully authored by Hiro as a chosen challenge. The fanwork therefore mistakes a **surface condition** for the **decision structure** that gives the condition meaning.

## 3. Voice

Surface voice has scattered Hiro-shaped traces: she is not rewritten as a loudly normative or conventionally panicked character, and the scenario uses a contrast between strange circumstance and relatively matter-of-fact response.

But the work gives her too little discursive room for the full low-force Hiro grammar to emerge. There is little sustained analytic reframing, little deadpan coherent logic that forces P or the surrounding cast to supply alarm, and little sense that she has mentally chosen the terms under which the difficulty is worth experiencing.

**Voice outcome:** low-to-medium surface resemblance; low behavioral depth.

## 4. Producer relation

Producer is the largest relational distortion.

Canonical P increasingly functions as witness, counterweight, co-traveler, and responsible participant in a future bound up with Hiro. Here P primarily enables the risky premise and then reacts to its consequences. That is almost the inverse of accountable attachment.

The work is useful precisely because it shows what happens when “Producer who follows Hiro into abnormality” becomes “Producer who triggers abnormality for the scenario.”

## 5. Subjecthood

Subjecthood outcome: **reduced**.

Hiro is recognizable enough that the work is not wholly interchangeable, but her body carries more narrative responsibility than her mind, values, or choices. The scenario uses her fragility as content rather than asking what Hiro would make of fragility.

This is not subject annihilation in the Typehatena sense. Hiro still has local reactions and identity. But the work makes **usable fragility** more important than **difficult form**.

## 6. Canon relation and transformation pressure

Canon relation:

> **frail-body literalization / pharmacological risk exaggeration / Producer-responsibility downgrade.**

Primary pressure: involuntary bodily overdrive.

Secondary pressure: dark-comic consequence escalation.

The work is canon-aware at the level of a famous vulnerability but shallow at the level of longitudinal character architecture.

## 7. Revision disposition

Relative to the cluster-first-pass analysis:

- **PRESERVE** — negative/control status.
- **STRENGTHEN** — the distinction between canonical chosen difficulty and fanwork imposed bodily state.
- **STRENGTHEN** — Producer distortion: responsibility is replaced by premise enablement.
- **PRESERVE** — high taxonomic value despite low character-writing value.

## 8. Canonical formulation

> **`Ichijou 10-man Yen` understands that Hiro's body can fail spectacularly; it does not understand that the meaning of Hiro's difficulty depends on who chooses it, why she chooses it, and who becomes accountable for its cost. It is therefore a clean DJFW negative control for the difference between “Hiro as usable fragility” and “Hiro as difficult authored form.”**
