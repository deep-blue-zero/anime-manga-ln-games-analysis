# DJFW_CASE_0005 — Otona no Sei ni Shite — Continuous Sequential Reading

## 0. Governing judgment

`Otona no Sei ni Shite` remains the strongest telomereNA Minami case for the **adult/desire/self-authorization axis**, but the audited canon baseline changes how that claim should be phrased.

The fanwork does not reveal a hidden personal desire that canonical Minami only learns very late. Official mobile-game Minami already possesses direct first-person desire, body confidence, flirtatious play, and interest in glamorous adulthood early. What the doujin does is **isolate and intensify** those capacities inside a deliberately adult-coded private trip.

Its most important page may again be the afterword. Gustav retrospectively calls the earlier swimsuit work potentially borderline character collapse and states the general principle that prioritizing erotic appeal too strongly can break a character. He describes this C100 book as an attempt to return to an affectionate Minami/P mode. This creates a small but unusually explicit longitudinal poetics across the telomereNA cluster: the creator experiments, perceives a fidelity risk, and recalibrates.

The story uses a trip and adult-coded leisure to literalize that threshold.

## 2. Canonical adulthood versus fanwork adulthood

Canonical Minami's adult/child ambiguity is not solved simply by reaching an age threshold. Official material allows her simultaneously to want adulthood and to want room to remain a child; *Secret Daybreak* makes adulthood a problem of family, career, responsibility, uncertainty, and the legitimacy of present-tense enjoyment.

`Otona no Sei ni Shite` translates that complicated problem into a much simpler private-symbolic grammar:

> adulthood = birthday threshold + private trip + permission to choose intimate pleasure.

That is a reduction, but it is not random. It selects a real Minami theme—how to authorize what she wants when “adult” behavior is culturally legible—and moves the conflict away from family/career into romance.

The work is therefore strongest when read as **fanwork literalization of an existing adulthood pressure**, not as a psychologically complete adulthood thesis.

## 3. Minami's first-person desire

The audited baseline is essential. Earlier synthesis risked saying Minami only learns late that desire itself can be authoritative. The mobile-game corpus shows otherwise: she can want idolhood strongly, call herself greedy, deliberately provoke, and seek glamorous possibilities early.

This fanwork's Minami is therefore not a radical opposite self released by sexuality. She is an **intensified private Minami**: confident enough to choose, embarrassed enough for the choice to matter, affectionate toward P, and interested in adult-coded self-presentation.

That is why this work feels more character-aligned than a generic “mature onee-san” scenario. Its Minami is not merely mature-looking; she is actively negotiating what being “adult” permits her to claim.

## 4. Voice and behavioral grammar

At structural level:

- Minami retains social fluency and initiative;
- the trip is jointly inhabited rather than imposed as a pure access mechanism;
- she moves between ordinary public composure and private vulnerability/play;
- the work treats her attraction/desire as hers, not merely as P's reward;
- her adult self-presentation remains mixed with embarrassment and uncertainty.

Missing are the external obligations that make canonical Minami's desire ethically difficult: family, career, unit responsibility, fans, professional standards, and the pleasure/burden of being useful.

**Voice outcome:** medium-high behavioral/relational fidelity within a heavily narrowed romantic frame; medium line-level confidence.

## 5. Producer relation

P is a romantic attachment figure and companion. This is a major fanwork transformation but less generic than simple “Producer access.” The trip presupposes a relationship whose history makes Minami's choice meaningful.

Still, canonical Producer's career and evaluative functions are nearly absent. The work takes the safe-audience/attachment dimension of P and makes it dominant. It is better described as **romantic extrapolation of one canonical function** than faithful reproduction of the complete Producer relation.

## 6. Subjecthood

Among the four telomereNA works, subjecthood is comparatively strong.

Minami is not only a role or costume. The premise depends on her own relation to adulthood, her willingness to go on the trip, her private desire, and her affection for P. The work still narrows her drastically and gives the erotic scenario most of the page budget, but the narrative would lose a meaningful part of its logic if Minami were replaced with a wholly different idol.

Subjecthood outcome: **subject-preserving romantic intensification with major contextual subtraction**.

## 7. The p040 afterword: creator self-revision

The afterword provides unusually direct evidence of longitudinal authorial self-critique.

Gustav says the C100 work is intended as a return toward Minami/P affectionate romance. He explicitly reflects on the earlier swimsuit work and says, in substance, that it may have gone too far: when erotic appeal is prioritized too aggressively, the character can collapse. This is not DJFW imposing a fidelity vocabulary from outside; the creator independently identifies essentially the same failure mode.

That matters for the cluster. The four works are not merely four arbitrary scenarios. They reveal an author repeatedly testing how far Minami can be transformed while still remaining the Minami he wants to write.

The C100 adulthood device is part of that recalibration. Instead of using only an external costume hook, the book uses age/birthday, travel, affection, and a familiar P relationship as additional authorization layers.

## 8. Canon relation and transformation pressure

Canon relation:

Primary pressure: **adulthood and self-authorization translated into romance**.

Secondary pressure: **Producer attachment privatization**.

It is not canon-compatible in event terms, but it is character-aware.

## 10. Canonical case formulation

> **`Otona no Sei ni Shite` is telomereNA's clearest attempt in this sample to make erotic romance answer to a Minami-specific theme rather than merely place Minami inside a visual premise. It literalizes her adult/child tension through a birthday threshold and private trip, intensifies an already canonical capacity for first-person desire, and preserves her as a choosing partner. Its afterword is especially important because Gustav explicitly recognizes character collapse as a risk and frames this work as a recalibration toward relationship-first Minami/P.**
