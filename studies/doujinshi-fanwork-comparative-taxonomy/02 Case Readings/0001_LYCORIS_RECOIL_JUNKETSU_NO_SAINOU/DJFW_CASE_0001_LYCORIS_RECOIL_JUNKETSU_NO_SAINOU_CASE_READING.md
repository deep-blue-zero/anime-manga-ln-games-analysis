Primary category:

> institutional-training comedy / DA-service-skill parody / Chisato-Takina skill-domain reversal / R18 yuri intensification.

The work begins from a mission/training premise: the Lycoris operational world is treated as capable of converting even intimate or service-coded behavior into a skill deficit, training need, or undercover competency. ## 7. Character voice outcome

### Chisato

Chisato is mostly `voice-intensifying`, with some expected R18 genre flattening.

The better parts of the work preserve recognizable Chisato features:

- elastic role-play;
- ability to enter service-like performance registers;
- comic recovery;
- teasing and sudden embarrassment;
- cheerful surface control meeting a domain where bodily/social control becomes harder;
- the sense that she treats roles as playable but not always harmless.

This maps plausibly onto canonical Chisato because the baseline character already moves among waitress, guide, operative, mock delinquent, action hero, patient, friend, and performer registers. The fanwork exaggerates that flexibility through an R18 training premise.

Where the work weakens, Chisato becomes more scenario vessel than self-authoring agent. Those moments should be classified as genre flattening rather than deep character writing.

### Takina

Takina is the more interesting fidelity case. The work often preserves her through intensification rather than generic role assignment.

Her response to the premise remains Takina-shaped when it is:

- literal;
- procedural;
- competence-oriented;
- direct rather than coy;
- emotionally intense but organized through a problem-solving frame;
- jealous or possessive in a contained, specific way;
- oriented toward Chisato rather than abstract erotic conquest.

The fanwork's best move is to let Takina translate intimacy into a task domain she can understand while gradually revealing that the real pressure is not generic training but Chisato-specific attachment.

### Chisato/Takina dyad

The main dyad is best classified as:

> voice-preserving to voice-intensifying under R18 pressure.

Chisato expands/performs; Takina compresses/insists. Chisato tries to keep the situation playful; Takina increasingly makes it specific. Their official relationship grammar—timing coordination rather than identity merger—survives more than expected for this kind of work.

The fanwork does not simply copy the canon relationship. It turns the canon dynamic into a genre scenario. But it retains enough of the underlying Chisato/Takina grammar to be analytically useful.

### 15.1 Why the afterward matters

The afterword is not disposable paratext. ChickeIII explicitly frames the work against consequence-free IF treatment. The comment states a dislike for IF routes, especially when a character's survival/death is involved, because a character's death changes the whole scenario and the accumulated relationships around that person. In this case, the Yoshimatsu/Mika continuity note signals that the supporting-character material is not intended as pure canon amnesia. It asks the reader to treat the scenario as an IF-adjacent branch that still cares about the weight of relationships.

4why's afterword is even more directly relevant to DJFW. It says that Chisato and Takina's relationship in the original is interesting, and that the book emphasizes the two characters' respective specialties to create stronger gaps and exchanges. This is an authorial confirmation of the reading: the work is not only a body-access scenario, but an attempt to convert Chisato/Takina complementarity into a doujin premise.

### 15.2 Bonus setup: training as Chisato's conversion engine

The bonus material begins by returning to the same core machine as the main story: Chisato turns a domain into training. She recruits Takina with bright, almost overexcited initiative; Takina responds with suspicion and a dry awareness that Chisato has become unusually invested. Chisato then treats the subject as something she has studied and as something she can personalize around Takina.

That matters because it preserves the dyad's asymmetry. Chisato does not approach the situation as pure confession or pure desire. She converts embarrassment into performance, study, preparation, and mischievous competence. Takina does not simply melt into the premise. She watches, questions, and measures whether Chisato has gone too far.

### 15.3 Bonus boundary gag: usable chemistry, unsafe detailed scope

The p068-p075 bonus range includes boundary-testing gag material that should not be analyzed in graphic detail. Its usable analytical value is categorical and relational rather than erotic.

Within that constraint, the range strengthens rather than weakens the chemistry read. Chisato overextends the service/training premise into an ethically impermissible direction; Takina interrupts it, names it as wrong, and becomes the moral brake. This is a recognizable Takina function: she is direct, literal, and corrective, but the correction is not emotional withdrawal from Chisato. It is regulation of Chisato's reckless premise-extension.

The final beat is especially important. Takina rejects the public/service expansion but does not reject the private training structure with Chisato. In other words, the gag differentiates between an unacceptable externalized scenario and the dyad-specific intimacy/training frame. That distinction preserves Takina's attachment: she is not ending Chisato/Takina; she is setting the boundary that allows Chisato/Takina to remain legible.
