# DJFW_CASE_0002 — Mizugi no Shita no Yuuwaku — Continuous Sequential Reading

## 0. Governing judgment

`Mizugi no Shita no Yuuwaku` is the most **costume/body-premise-driven** work in the telomereNA Minami quartet, but the continuous pass raises its taxonomic value because the afterword shows that the author is consciously negotiating the exact problem DJFW is designed to measure: when does visual/erotic appeal stop intensifying a recognizable character and start replacing her?

The work preserves a recognizable subset of Minami: body confidence, playful willingness to test a situation, ease with Producer, and the ability to enter a performance frame without becoming socially inert. It reduces the much wider Minami architecture—curiosity across domains, usefulness, standards, family, audience, leadership, and the tension between responsibility and first-person desire—to a narrow intimate register. The result is **partially subject-preserving erotic intensification**, not a full Minami character study.

The most important new evidence is authorial. In the afterword Gustav explicitly worries that a swimsuit/tan premise can be perceived as prioritizing sexual appeal over character coherence and invokes an official trait—Minami tans easily—as the bridge that makes the visual premise feel less arbitrary. That does not make the story canon-compatible, but it demonstrates **character-collapse awareness** rather than indifference to it.

## 2. Minami baseline comparator

The governing mobile-game Minami model is not “mature pretty onee-san.” She is a high-capability, high-curiosity generalist who genuinely likes competence and usefulness, responds strongly to expectations, and can perform the socially legible “proper Nitta Minami” without that performance being false. The audited baseline corrects a too-linear developmental story: Minami has first-person desire early. Her growth is not the late discovery of desire but learning to keep desire legitimate when responsibility, usefulness, family, audience, and professionalism compete with it.

That correction is important here. The fanwork does **not** need to invent a secretly sensual or self-directed Minami to justify its premise. Canon Minami already has body confidence, playful adult-coded provocation, curiosity, and a capacity to enjoy glamorous performance. The fanwork's transformation is therefore one of **concentration**: it isolates those capacities and makes them carry almost the entire character.

## 3. Voice and behavioral grammar

Line-level Japanese voice remains below high authority because the earlier OCR pass was noisy and this reread deliberately avoided broad OCR of explicit pages. At structural level, however, the work preserves several Minami-shaped behaviors:

- she is not written as socially helpless;
- she participates in the frame rather than merely being acted upon;
- embarrassment and confidence coexist rather than replacing one another;
- Producer familiarity is assumed rather than constructed from generic stranger access;
- the costume premise is something Minami can inhabit as a role, not merely something placed on an empty character model.

What is missing is equally important. Minami's competence does not become a problem to solve; her tendency to organize, care, over-prepare, or negotiate expectations is largely absent. Her curiosity is reduced to intimate play rather than generalist exploration. Her voice therefore survives better at the level of **social posture** than at the level of full longitudinal psychology.

**Voice outcome:** medium at behavioral/relational level; low-to-medium at line-level register authority.

## 4. Producer relation

The Producer here is best read as a mobile-game-compatible romantic P rather than the 2015 anime TakeP. There is no meaningful trace of anime TakeP's institutional/developmental authorship. The relationship is already privatized and intimate.

That privatization both helps and hurts fidelity. It helps because Minami is not interacting with a faceless generic aggressor; the work depends on ease, recognition, and reciprocal familiarity. It hurts because the canonical Producer relation contains evaluation, possibility-opening, professional witnessing, and longitudinal career support that are almost entirely removed.

The fanwork therefore preserves **attachment familiarity** while dropping **developmental/professional relational grammar**.

## 5. Subjecthood

Subjecthood is **partially preserved**.

Minami retains preferences, reactions, playfulness, and participation. The work does not annihilate her into a purely interchangeable visual object. But page function progressively shifts away from what Minami chooses, values, or interprets and toward what the visual premise can display. Her personhood becomes narrower than her body without becoming wholly absent.

This is a useful middle case for DJFW. It is neither character-faithful romance nor pure artist-machine replacement. It shows how a work can preserve enough agency and social voice to remain recognizably character-specific while allowing a fetish/costume engine to dominate narrative bandwidth.

## 6. Afterword as meta-evidence

The p067 afterword materially changes the reading. Gustav says that a tan-focused secondary work risks being seen as sexual-appeal-first character collapse, then points to the official fact that Minami tans easily as a canon-facing warrant for the premise. The afterword also describes the earlier 72-page scale as a benchmark that the author unintentionally repeated.

This should not be treated as proof that the resulting characterization succeeds. It is evidence that the author is **self-consciously solving a fidelity problem**. In DJFW terms, telomereNA is not merely asking “what visual scenario do I want?” but also “what source trait can make this feel like a Minami scenario rather than a generic body scenario?”

That authorial self-audit becomes even more important when read longitudinally against the later C100 afterword, where Gustav retrospectively questions whether this swimsuit work may actually have pushed too far.

## 7. Canon relation and transformation pressure

Canon relation is best classified as:

> **canon-trait-anchored romantic/erotic intensification with strong costume/body pressure.**

The official tanning trait is used as a literal bridge into the visual premise. Canon personality is not rejected, but only a small subset of it is required.

Primary transformation pressure: **costume/body-image concentration**.

Secondary pressure: **private Producer-access romanticization**.

The work is not plausible as ordinary game continuity, but it does demonstrate source awareness.

Compared with the earlier cluster first pass:

- **STRENGTHEN** — the classification of this as the most costume/fetish-triggered telomereNA Minami work.
- **STRENGTHEN** — its anthropological/taxonomic value, because the afterword explicitly names the danger of character collapse.

## 9. Canonical case formulation

> **`Mizugi no Shita no Yuuwaku` concentrates genuine Minami traits—body confidence, playful role-entry, reciprocal familiarity, and willingness to test a situation—into a swimsuit/tan premise that increasingly dominates the book. Its strongest DJFW value lies in the author's explicit awareness that sexual appeal can cause character collapse and his attempt to anchor the scenario in an official Minami trait. The work preserves Minami as a participant, but not Minami in full.**

Current authority: **canonical within the stated non-graphic, structure/voice/subjecthood scope.**
