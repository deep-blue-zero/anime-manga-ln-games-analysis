## 2. Admission, witness and inspection

The first unit is a volume, with chapter and dramatic-movement boundaries inside it. Use the collected edition's actual ordering after mapping its spine and visibly identifying chapters; do not infer chapters from another edition or arithmetic. Scene segmentation follows a change in goal, location, configuration, knowledge or formal organization, rather than extraction batch size.

Before any reading, require an owner-authorized run, the initiation gate satisfied, and an OPEN lock with consistent execution fields. Reconfirm transport integrity, bind the exact witness in the private evidence receipt, and give it a public-safe alias in the source inventory. Distinguish availability, import integrity, admission and inspection. The historical import checks establish reported package validity and copy integrity; they do not establish legibility, page completeness, spreads or narrative coverage.

Verify actual STILL_IMAGE capability on authorized manga pages and record the route before interpretive work. TEXT supports transcriptions and glosses. OCR, extracted text and thumbnails assist retrieval but cannot certify inspection, visual form or quotation accuracy. A unit with unresolved essential pages cannot be marked complete. Audio, acting, music and adaptation knowledge are outside this manga boundary.

Bind every image occurrence to its witness, spine item and reading-order index. Preserve covers, colour pages and spreads in the evidence plane; classify them before reading. A cover, preview, profile, joke page or author note is not automatically narrative evidence. Spoiler-bearing apparatus remains quarantined unless the run explicitly admits it.

## 3. Prospective state and later knowledge

Record prior exposure, including remembered plot or adaptations, before the first unit. Quarantine it rather than claiming ignorance. Freeze the entering state before opening each next unit. Maintain three distinct horizons: what a scene's participants know, what is disclosed by the end of the authorized volume, and what the cumulative project has admitted.

Write movement-level interpretation from its disclosure frontier. A late-volume explanation may be discussed in an explicitly retrospective section, but cannot be written back as an earlier character's knowledge. Do not identify foreshadowing from remembered later events. Predictions state what would support or falsify them and remain unchanged after testing.

At the present acquisition limit, a V03 exit state would be a partial-corpus checkpoint, not a conclusion about the ending. Unresolved motives or relationships must remain unresolved. A later witness may support REVISE or REVEALED_NOT_NEW, but cannot erase the original account of uncertainty.

## 4. What evidence permits

Use these labels wherever status could blur: OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, EVALUATION and UNRESOLVED. Pair each material claim with HIGH, MODERATE, LOW or UNRESOLVED confidence. Outside material is EXTERNAL_REFERENCE and excluded unless separately admitted. Record support and counterevidence rather than treating a confidence word as justification.

Separate speech, private thought, narrator text, editorial captions, small asides, diegetic writing and effects. Attribute the speaker, thinker, observer and recipient independently. A person's self-account is evidence that the person says or thinks this, not automatic proof of the motive. Narration is not necessarily authorial judgment.

For a causal explanation, reconstruct the entering conditions, the named actor's perceived alternatives, the decision or interruption, the immediate consequence and the evidence linking them. Distinguish material constraints, deliberate action, misunderstanding, coincidence and later rationalization. The explanation must say what the event changes and why a competing causal account is less or equally plausible.

Use names once the source establishes them within the relevant boundary. Preserve source-required anonymity and aliases until resolved. “The girlfriend,” “the rival,” “the teacher” or another role may describe an analytically relevant position, but may not become a substitute for an established personal identity. Do not import a cast list now.

## 5. Character development and relationship conditions

For each materially active person, preserve current goals, perceived stakes, constraints, decision evidence, stated self-understanding, behavior, uncertainty and counterexamples. Separate disposition, developmental state and transient situation. When apparent development may instead be a change of setting, audience, knowledge or revealed information, retain those alternatives.

Track relationships directionally. A's desire, trust, obligation, pressure or recognition toward B must not imply B's reciprocal state. Distinguish expressed wishes, observed behavior, the other person's response and the analyst's inference. A triangle or larger configuration earns an ensemble discussion only after its individual edges are established; an attractive diagram cannot supply missing evidence.

Ordinary conversation, routine, humor, care, frustration and work or study, where actually present, are first-class evidence. Compare them with salient conflicts before proposing a stable trait. Record the absence of a diagnostic ordinary-life sample as a gap. The future analysis should test whether a behavior is person-specific, role-specific, relationship-specific or situational; it must not assume these domains from the title.

Institutional or household constraints, if encountered, distinguish rules, customs, individual assertions and inferred pressures. They initially remain grounded claims and state constraints. A separate institutional specialist is earned by recurring explanatory work, not by genre expectation.

## 6. Japanese register and visual narration

For language claims inspect the Japanese lettering, including address terms, self-reference, politeness, sentence endings, omissions, ruby/furigana and discourse markers. Provide a brief source quotation and labeled gloss only where needed. Record addressee, audience, situation and attribution confidence. A marked utterance requires comparison with ordinary utterances before it becomes a voice rule. Translation cannot silently settle uncertain wording.

Read silent and OCR-resistant pages fully. At a consequential panel, page or spread, describe the formal feature, explain its proposed effect, and test another explanation. Attend to grouping, distance, gaze alignment, withheld reaction, backgrounds, screentone, gutters, panel size, repetition and contrast. A recurring image becomes a motif only with evidence that links its occurrences.

Inspect facing-page layout before a page-turn or spread claim. A digital image index is not a printed page number. Distinguish drawn action from inferred transitions across gutters. An expression does not uniquely reveal an inner state. Comic or symbolic rendering changes the presentation, not automatically the literal event. Sound effects and state effects are written graphic conventions, not acoustic measurements.

For intimacy, coercion, injury or other contentious content if encountered, describe only what the argument needs, distinguish depiction, framing, desire, consent, consequence and evaluation, and locate the evidence. Neither sympathy nor later affection establishes earlier consent; explanation does not justify conduct. Do not make a predetermined moral verdict the governing thesis.

## 7. Causal prose, claims and counterreadings

Every volume reading must develop a governing argument in continuous prose supported by movement-level causal analysis. A synopsis alone is insufficient. So are lists of themes, psychological labels or ledger rows. Explain how the pages produce the interpretation, retain ambivalence, and address the strongest plausible competing reading of each load-bearing thesis.

For a major claim preserve the observed basis, inferential step, significant exception, competing account and disconfirming observation. A cumulative pattern cites representative precise instances plus its checked scope; repeated assertion is not recurrence evidence. An unresolved contest remains OPEN. Later evidence uses PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT or OPEN in the claim ledger, with old wording retained.

No universal word ceiling governs readings or synthesis. Adequacy depends on whether the argument preserves meaningful causal links, distinctions, alternatives and source limits. If transfer or revision shortens prose, compare the actual incoming and outgoing text for lost reasoning and counterevidence.
