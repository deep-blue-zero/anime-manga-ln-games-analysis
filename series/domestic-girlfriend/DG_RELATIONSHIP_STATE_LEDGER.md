# Directed relationship states

The unit of preservation is one person toward another at a stated horizon. No mutual relationship label, preferred pairing or inferred triangle is seeded from title familiarity.

## Exact row schema

Append one DG-R0001-style event for each material directed-state change or meaningful confirmation.

| Event ID | From subject | To subject | Unit / event time / disclosure horizon | Prior edge event | Expressed position | Observed conduct | Knowledge / power / constraint asymmetry | Inferred relationship state / confidence | Recipient response and limits | Evidence / counterevidence / alternative | Claim IDs / reading anchor / locators |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Both subjects reference source-established keys in the character ledger. `expressed position` records who actually says or thinks it. `observed conduct` records what the pages show; the inference is separately labeled. An unanswered gesture does not prove rejection, reciprocity or indifference. The recipient's observed response belongs in this event's evidentiary context; a claim about the recipient's own directed state requires a separate reverse-edge event.

Use explicit UNKNOWN for an unestablished event time or mental state, and NOT_APPLICABLE only with a reason. A current edge index may later contain `from | to | latest_event | through_unit | live_alternative_claim_ids`; it never replaces events. No event or current-edge rows exist.

## Update and history rules

Append at each material unit transaction. Do not insert exhaustive pair matrices for people with no demonstrated interaction. When a group configuration becomes causally significant, create a GROUP-tagged claim in the claim ledger identifying the supported constituent directed events and the unproved edges. Only an independently recurring ensemble question earns a separate study.

A later interpretation preserves the previous event and appends a transition, evidence and downstream impact. Frozen entry/exit states stay unchanged. Do not retroactively make the earlier edge certain because later evidence resolves it. Separately record change in a person's desire, change in the other's knowledge and change in the analyst's understanding.

## Acceptance checks

A reading must explain what changed between people, what made it change and what remains asymmetric. Check named agents, speaker/recipient attribution, relationship-conditioned versus general behavior, ordinary versus exceptional situations and causal consequences. Distinguish consent, desire, obligation, care, dependence and social pressure when evidence makes the distinction relevant; a later outcome cannot prove an earlier state.

The full relationship argument lives in reading/study prose with rival interpretations. This table is a route into that reasoning. A general label such as “love” or “conflict” without direction, evidence, temporal scope and alternatives is inadequate.
