# Domestic Girlfriend synthesis architecture

## Purpose and the partial-corpus design

The project has one canonical root, `series/domestic-girlfriend/`, and one [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md). Generation DG-G1 begins with a Japanese manga acquisition covering V01–03. [The method](DG_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) governs reading; this architecture governs preservation, revision and synthesis. No prior architecture is superseded.

The intended literary corpus must explain how people and relationships develop through decisions and consequences, how Japanese wording and page form shape that understanding, and which interpretations survive competing explanations. Character reconstruction is an intended, separately validated derived use. These are future questions, not findings about unread content.

Partial coverage determines the design. The first possible checkpoint is an opening-tranche account with explicit limits, while the state and revision infrastructure can extend to later acquisitions. Neither an anticipated long developmental trajectory nor an eventual ending is assumed from familiarity. A full-series synthesis cannot be commissioned from the present acquisition merely by relabeling its final available volume.

This architecture follows the initiation, archive-authority, manga lane, reasoning, long-series and handoff policies linked below. Its canonical authority is limited to INITIAL procedural responsibilities. Semantic review and owner authorization remain separate: a valid bootstrap may guide architecture review while its sequential lock stays CLOSED.

## Source model and chronological discipline

The only inventoried witnesses are the Japanese digital manga V01–03 in [DG_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md](DG_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md). Publication-volume order supplies the planned reading order after file identity and chapter ordering are verified. Story chronology, internal retrospection, names and narrative frame are unknown until inspected.

| Source class | Initial treatment | Admission / conflict rule |
|---|---|---|
| Main Japanese volumes | Available metadata, unread, unadmitted | Exact witness and actual ordering must be bound before authorized reading |
| In-volume covers, profiles, omake, notes and previews | Classification pending | Narrative, paratext, joke and spoiler-bearing preview roles remain separate |
| Later volumes | Outside verified acquisition | New metadata/source verification and explicit run scope; no automatic continuation |
| Other editions or serialization | No admitted witness | Independent witness and source crosswalk before revision claims |
| Translations | Not admitted | Original Japanese governs wording; a later translation is a named aid, not silent replacement |
| Adaptations, external summaries and remembered plot | Excluded | Separate assignment/source track required; no repair of missing manga evidence |

An event's story time, its presentation position, the reader's disclosure horizon and each person's knowledge are different coordinates. A later explanation can change a current synthesis, but not what an earlier reading legitimately knew. Source-supported contradiction may reflect ignorance, self-presentation, changing circumstances, edition differences or genuine inconsistency; none is presumed in advance.

Future `readings/`, `checkpoints/`, `studies/`, `reconstruction/` and `audits/` directories appear only with their first justified artifact. There are no placeholder monographs or synthesis files. A reader-facing guide comes after a mature synthesis; the operational entrypoint remains singular.

A complete authorized volume transaction includes the full method-compliant reading, its entering/exit freezes, all material state and directed-edge changes, Japanese register and ordinary-life evidence, claim/prediction adjudication, source coverage and current-state advancement. A prose-complete reading with unsynchronized mandatory homes is incomplete. Conversely, a fully populated ledger does not rescue thin causal prose.

The reading owns movement-level explanation: agent, perceived stakes, decision/constraint, consequence, inferential warrant and live counterreading. Cumulative rows point to stable reading sections and precise locators. They retain enough context for retrieval but never become the sole support for claims whose reasoning they omit.

## Earned studies and substantive acceptance

A recurring person earns a monograph when evidence across more than one admitted unit establishes a distinct developmental question and supports contrast across situations or relationships. A consequential one-unit case may instead justify a bounded study with its narrower scope declared. No chapter-count or popularity threshold substitutes for the question.

A character monograph must explain transitions rather than list traits. It must distinguish self-report from conduct, developmental change from changed knowledge, relationship-specific behavior from generalized disposition, ordinary from crisis evidence, and literary function from behavioral predictability. It uses claim IDs and source locators, not “see volume 2” alone. A directed relationship study must explain both persons' evidence separately, the asymmetry between their perspectives and the causal consequences of changes; an ensemble account integrates those edges without flattening them.

The minimum mature literary responsibilities are development/causality, directed relationships, Japanese expression, page form and the interpretation's counterevidence. In an opening-tranche synthesis these may be substantial sections of one artifact; create separate specialists only when their independent questions require sustained treatment. Potential social-role or intimacy/ethics specialists remain anticipated until source evidence earns them.

A mature artifact needs an argument appropriate to its responsibility. It cannot pass as a monograph because headings are filled, or as synthesis because summaries were concatenated. Require explanatory mechanism, representative and disconfirming evidence, transitions, unresolved alternatives and boundary limitations. An auditor compares transferred versions for lost causal steps, source distinctions, named actors, everyday evidence and counterreadings. Length alone passes or fails nothing.

## Reconciliation and dependency order

When evidence conflicts, first confirm witness, locator, wording, subject and knowledge boundary. Then consider changed context/state, inaccurate self-report, a new disclosure, paratext status, translation/edition difference or actual inconsistency. Open a stable question and retain both formulations with support. If the dispute is load-bearing, commission bounded source review and hold the dependent conclusion. Do not erase disagreement by averaging personality descriptions.

Production order:

`source verification + owner run + OPEN gate → full ordered readings and synchronized ledgers → scoped checkpoint → coverage/claim audit → role-gap audit → bounded character/relationship/form studies → convergence → scoped synthesis → release review`.

Reconstruction uses the same admitted evidence but remains a derived branch of that dependency graph: prospective coverage capture during readings; evidence-ledger and operational-model authorship only after a subject/state boundary is adequate; independent validation before any maturity claim. A monograph is neither required to invent a model nor sufficient to certify one. [The reconstruction specification](DG_RECONSTRUCTION_SPECIFICATION.md) governs the separate pathway.

| Gate | Acceptance condition |
|---|---|
| Bootstrap review | Method/architecture, controls, schemas, routing and truthful CLOSED state reviewed |
| First admission | Verified source route, witness binding, required infrastructure, owner-defined run and OPEN lock recorded |
| Unit completion | Full reading contract, meaningful ledger synchronization, coverage and exit freeze accepted |
| V01–03 checkpoint | All authorized available units completed or exact exclusions stated; no implied series ending |
| Longitudinal reconciliation | State/knowledge chronology, both directed relationship edges where evidenced, ordinary/register samples, open questions and revision history reconciled to the same admitted horizon |
| Specialist readiness | Claim/locator stabilization and role-gap audit; ordinary/contextual evidence adequate or explicit bounds |
| Specialist convergence | Character, relationship and formal arguments compared for contradiction and lost detail; if independent specialists are unnecessary, record why substantial integrated sections satisfy each responsibility |
| Integrated scoped-synthesis readiness | Convergence accepted, required evidence debts resolved or scope legitimately bounded, acquisition/admitted boundary stated and full argumentative inputs retained |
| Full-series synthesis | Separately established official/work boundary and adequate admitted coverage; unavailable at bootstrap |
| Reconstruction maturity | Model-specific independent testing and limits; evaluator alone assigns registry capability |
| Final validation | Full synthesis checked against claims, source boundaries, competing explanations and named-subject attribution; required repository checks pass for the exact candidate |
| Release/freeze | Semantic, source, name/reference and repository checks passed for declared scope; owner/CI integration route respected |

## Lifecycle, review and preservation

Lifecycle is INITIAL. Review after the first authorized volume, at the V03 acquisition frontier, whenever a new witness/medium enters, when a recurring dimension lacks an adequate home, before specialists, before synthesis and before release. The first-volume review checks whether the planned state/relationship/expression schemas capture the actual narrative rather than imposing a generic romance pattern.

At each review record all relevant duties as SATISFIED, PARTIALLY_SATISFIED, MISSING, MISROUTED, TOO_THIN or NOT_APPLICABLE, with exact artifact/section, defect, consequence and repair owner. Add a ledger only if recurrence, independent evidence accumulation and retrieval need justify it. Add targeted backfill where a material evidence gap requires it, not a ritual reread of the corpus.

Entry, method, architecture and current tables are mutable through targeted edits with history. Append-only events, source bindings, predictions, entering/exit freezes and accepted checkpoints are retained. Correct frozen readings by attributable errata or successor interpretations; do not edit their historical knowledge. A new model version preserves the assessed old version and reciprocal supersession. Completion seals follow the initiation policy when actually applicable; no fictitious run closure is created at bootstrap.

## Execution ownership and handoff

| Stage | Preferred owner / lane | Reasoning class |
|---|---|---|
| Source identity and safe mapping | Source operator, TEXT metadata | BOUNDED_STANDARD |
| Architecture and amendments | Adequately grounded architecture author; fresh cloud preferred for substantial redesign | DEEP_SYNTHESIS; PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST for consequential global rerouting |
| Sequential readings and mutable ledgers | Local analytical executor, verified STILL_IMAGE plus TEXT | SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS; escalate actual ambiguity |
| Checkpoints, monographs, relationships and models | Fresh synthesis session against frozen evidence; local convergence owner | DEEP_SYNTHESIS |
| Final integration and adversarial global interpretation | Fresh synthesis/reviewer with local evidence completion | PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST, justified by cross-state propagation risk |
| Repository publication | Parent/local authorized operator; housekeeping owns global routing | Checks appropriate to authored change; no own merge |
| Discovery / capability grading | Designated discovery agent / reconstruction evaluator | Outside series author's write scope |

This local, metadata-grounded bootstrap is an adequate route permitted by topology policy; no narrative perception capability is claimed. Future stages verify actual evidence access and record observed execution settings rather than inherit a presumed capability from the desktop.

A substantial handoff supplies full files, exact base and output hashes, source/admission horizon, required inputs and inspection state, unresolved obligations, target paths and next owner. Receiver compares base, candidate and current state, reads the full outputs, preserves meaningful reasoning and checks affected evidence. Newer branch state cannot be overwritten by a stale packet. Stage acceptance, semantic acceptance, preflight, publication, exact-head audit and main integration remain separate.

Retrieval is entrypoint → method/architecture → scoped ledger or study → claim ID → reading anchor → exact witness locator. Resume from the durable current state and applicable governance, never from remembered story or a previous chat summary.
