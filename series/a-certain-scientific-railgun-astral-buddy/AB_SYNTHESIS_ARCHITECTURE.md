# A Certain Scientific Railgun: Astral Buddy — synthesis architecture

## 1. Purpose and proportion

This is an `INITIAL` architecture for compact independent spin-off, adapted from the [generic architecture template](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MULTI_DOCUMENT_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE_TEMPLATE.md). Its organizing burden is **compact four-volume acquisition without assuming whole-series completeness**. It governs bootstrap responsibilities only; no owner run approval, source admission or literary conclusion is asserted.

Four observed volumes support a compact project with one sectioned state/claim notebook rather than a long-series specialist tree. Official title metadata identifies a Railgun spin-off, but its own source sequence and disclosure order govern its readings. Shared creators or characters with Mental Out do not establish a direct sequel relationship. The design must preserve substantial character, relationship, formal and counterreading argument without manufacturing many empty artifacts for a short supplied corpus.

## 4. Character, relationship and specialist synthesis

Character and relationship interpretation remains required whether separate monographs exist or not. A subject study is justified by a sustained question across contexts, temporal development or a reconstruction target, with ordinary-life and contrary evidence represented. A protagonist name or high citation count alone is insufficient. Monographs must integrate behavior, psychology, language, relations, visual form and thematic role, citing claim versions and precise sources rather than broad “see volume” references.

The anticipated specialist question is **work-local relationship argument and franchise correspondence**. Work-local identity and knowledge selectors are mandatory even in a compact corpus. A relationship study is earned only by a distinct sustained question, with both directions and contrary contexts represented; otherwise the continuous bounded synthesis discharges that responsibility directly. Cross-title comparison is postponed until each side has independently admitted evidence and frozen prospective findings. It activates only when direct reading establishes recurrence, independent accumulation, later interpretive consequence, no adequate existing home and retrieval need. Otherwise the continuous synthesis explicitly discharges the dimension, recording why no independent specialist was needed. A shared-franchise study is not created just to route four titles.

Reconstruction follows its separate evidence dossier → state-bounded model → independent evaluation pathway. Evidence capture begins during sequential work; modeling is future derived use and never replaces the source-facing literary layer. Its dossier, model and any monograph are separate responsibilities, and evaluator capability grades remain outside this author's authority.

## 5. States, contradictions and revision

State selectors include work, immutable edition/language, source disclosure frontier, depicted time/uncertainty, continuity/frame, character state, knowledge and relationship direction. The supplied manga is one witness track, not an assertion of linear story time. A new frame or continuity is added when source-established. Shared franchise identity does not erase local horizons.

Retain previous states and claims append-only. Later knowledge is a new retrospective layer, not a correction to what an earlier character knew. Conflicts first receive a source/attribution/knowledge/time/edition/franchise/interpretive classification; unresolved alternatives remain OPEN. Mutable current pointers reference full history; frozen readings/checkpoints retain bytes and receive errata or superseding generations with reciprocal authority metadata. A source change gets a new alias and locator crosswalk before any citation moves.

## 6. Dependency order and distinct gates

Production order is verified source map → explicit source/run admission → complete chapter/causal-unit readings and ledger transactions → volume/boundary reconciliation and checkpoints → longitudinal reconciliation → role-gap/readiness review → earned studies → convergence → continuous bounded synthesis → final validation → release/freeze. The analyst's retrieval order begins with the current entrypoint, source/state horizon and relevant full artifacts. A public reader guide, if needed, is written last.

| Gate | Concrete exit condition |
|---|---|
| Bootstrap | Method, architecture and required controls initialized, metadata limits explicit, lock closed |
| Source/run admission | Witness/locator/perception verified for exact scope, exposure and owners recorded, bounded authorization recovered |
| Sequential coverage | Every admitted page/chapter/extra has a disposition, full readings and all transactions accepted; gaps cannot hide behind last-page frontier |
| Longitudinal reconciliation | State, claims, form/register, source and reconstruction coverage consistent with full readings and all material revisions |
| Character/specialist readiness | Role-gap review checks peripheral perspectives, ordinary-life contexts, directionality, language and visual-form omissions; independent questions justified |
| Specialist convergence | Required studies complete, conflicts resolved or retained explicitly, responsibilities disjoint; explicit evidence-backed no-study decision permitted |
| Integrated-synthesis readiness | Exact input/horizon frozen, every recurring dimension assigned, counterreadings and limits visible, full artifacts available |
| Final validation | Independent return to decisive pages, causal and alternative-interpretation checks, name/reference review, source locator and authority verification |
| Release/freeze | Exact admitted scope and validated files, limitations, authority state, retained immutable history and next operation recorded |
| Reconstruction evaluation | Separate state/coverage/held-out criteria met and evaluator decision recorded; literary release alone never suffices |

V01–04 coverage alone cannot establish a final whole-series endpoint; a future release may be explicitly limited to the acquired horizon. Later acquisition requires intake and scope review rather than automatic continuation.

## 7. Extension, roles and handoff

Amend the architecture only with source-earned need, changed responsibility, dependency impact, full-row/ID crosswalk and backfill/review plan. Lifecycle becomes EVOLVING through actual reviewed use, STABILIZED/FROZEN at real corpus boundaries, never merely because files were published. No empty ledger/folder exists for visual symmetry.

Reasoning classes: metadata/locators BOUNDED_STANDARD; substantive reading and interpretive updates SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS; character/contradiction/reconstruction DEEP_SYNTHESIS; architecture and integrated synthesis PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST with independent source/visual/knowledge/compression review. The intended reliability gain is detection of propagating source/attribution errors and omitted interpretive roles. Resolve actual models and tools at dispatch under current routing. Fresh-context substantial synthesis receives the full admitted corpus and returns to sources; summaries are navigation only.

The future run owner controls scope/admission/coordination; readers deliver full units and cumulative transactions; independent reviewers test semantics/compression; housekeeping owns routing outputs; discovery and reconstruction evaluators own their global products. Handoffs include complete files and hashes, target root/branch, source and knowledge boundary, drift assessment, pending defects and next owner. Verify full delivered content before authority promotion; never replace a maintained artifact from a partial preview.
