# Bonds and disclosure ledger

Narrative baseline: **NONE**. The relationship dynamics, significant alternative explanations and prospective adjudications are indexed here together because a character's behavior can be reinterpreted when they tell someone what they knew. [office and season ledger](AFS_OFFICE_AND_SEASON_LEDGER.md) owns enacted role/power and institutional effects; the source-facing reading owns scene detail and longer argument.

| Record | Retain for retrieval |
| --- | --- |
| Directed bond | Named actor → other person, context and role, apparent duty versus self-stated concern, grant/refusal of help and choices available, recipient’s evidenced interpretation, consequence or meaningful failed opportunity, ordinary preference/communication and source evidence |
| Knowledge and recall | Holder, proposition, witnessed event vs report/inference, event time and when learned; when disclosed to reader; certainty boundaries, memory or perspective conflicts and named witness/locator |
| Claim and frozen test | Claim/test ID, proposition, actor and scope, original frontier, rival explanations and deciding observation, provenance of independently frozen states, dated outcome/revision with evidence and link to the full chain of argument |
| Person-level qualification | Stable named subject, current horizon and outcome, evidence for an independently usable portrait, largest gaps, reassessment trigger, actual monograph path when produced and separate self-containment/fidelity outcomes |

Record absence only where the person had reason and opportunity to respond, and never treat a belief, duty or bond as automatically reciprocal. Later revelations can revise the current relationship hypothesis and leave the earlier reasoned alternative intact. No bond, claim, forecast or qualification row exists yet.
