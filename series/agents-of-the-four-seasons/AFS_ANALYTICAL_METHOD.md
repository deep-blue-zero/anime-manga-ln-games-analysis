## Test how a season is enacted

Read each authorized upper/lower part and each separately scoped Archer book in Japanese and in its own disclosure order. Before placing a scene in a common chronology, distinguish the book's release, the sequence of its narration, event time, each participant's knowledge, and the chosen admission position. Keep unplaced memories, illustrative myths and disputed accounts unplaced. Conclude a part at its actual end; never use the lower part to settle an upper-part forecast or recap until that later part is admitted.

In scenes about an office, make separate observations for a person, the office/role claimed or conferred, the institutional authority or resources actually exercised, the work needed to perform it, the effect the scene depicts and who can detect that effect. Qualify geography and timing rather than extrapolating from a single location. An explanation in a myth, proclamation or prayer can have causal force for what a person chooses without establishing a literal world rule. Ritual sequence and visible seasonal change may support causation; seek the late or absent change, independent timing or alternative mechanism that could weaken it. Mark focalized perception, physical depiction, institutional report and inference separately.

Preserve named agents, protectors or officials as persons with distinct goals. For a material decision, ask who could authorize, enforce, request or decline the act; how a personal bond changes that account; what actions a partner actually reciprocates; and whether apparent deference comes from office, reliance, coercion, loyalty or chosen care. Follow ordinary routines, costs, preferences and speech across different audiences and roles. In recollection distinguish why a participant now interprets a past action from what they knew when acting; beware promoting an associative title, seasonal imagery or narrator’s sympathy into an explanation.

## Prose and original art

Japanese narration, dialogue and interiority govern wording. Attribute excerpts/ellipsis; preserve Japanese titles, name readings, honorifics and official versus personal forms of address where their distinctions affect power, familiarity or attribution. Label source observation, narrator presentation, testimony/self-report, inference and open uncertainty; use a brief verified Japanese anchor and marked gloss only where needed. Pair title/part, chapter/section and paragraph with a public evidence key resolving privately to digest, spine member and range. Inspect only authorized same-edition illustrations in the still-image lane and classify map, plate, cover and preview separately; symbolic seasonal framing and placed illustrations can shape reader exposure without establishing an occurrence.

## Read-through transaction

Obtain the approved configuration and record actual exposure separately for every participant. Freeze the entering state, unresolved rival readings and any diagnostic prospective tests before exposure; within the unit stop at disclosure boundaries that can resolve those tests. Record what each person or group was in a position to know, and what the text or page has told the reader, at that boundary. Quarantine outside knowledge and accidental later exposure. Document the affected claim and remove prospective credit for affected contributors; a separately frozen uncontaminated account is attributable in its own right.

After brief witness and entering context, place a clearly labeled **Current-unit story synopsis** before the thesis, close reading or ledger inventory. Once the applicable prospective captures are frozen, write coherent, accessible prose following the participants, stakes, concrete actions and mechanics, material disclosures, causal turning points and ending. Attribute testimony, inference and disputed outcomes; retain actual source limits and give stable evidence pointers for load-bearing events. It is a spoiler-bearing orientation to the admitted unit only, kept from all earlier-horizon participants. It neither substitutes for causal argument nor supplies independent evidence.

Complete the canonical reading with a witness/coverage receipt, entering and exit records, the synopsis, causal and formal close reading of decisive and countervailing scenes, character and directed relationship changes, independent review and reconciliation, and a ledger delta or explicit no material change in each required home. Cite source evidence, distinguish reports and inferences, and keep each unresolved material question or coverage debt visible. Freeze prospective judgments separately from later outcomes and synopsis. Unit completion requires the architecture's source and contribution checks, not just a readable draft.
