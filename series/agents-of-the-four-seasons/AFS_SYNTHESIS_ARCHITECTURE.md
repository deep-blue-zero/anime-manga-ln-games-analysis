# Synthesis architecture

This is the INITIAL architecture for Agents of the Four Seasons, governed by [method](AFS_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md), the [initiation policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md), the [archive authority policy](../../governance/source-policies/ARCHIVE_AUTHORITY_AND_SUPERSESSION_POLICY.md), and the [continuation policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_SEQUENTIAL_EXECUTION_SCOPE_AND_CONTINUATION_POLICY.md). [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) is the sole first-read surface. This generation has no predecessor or admitted analytical claims. [source register](AFS_SOURCE_REGISTER.md) scopes candidate units, conflicts, and exclusions one witness at a time.

## Two views of the same chronology

Keep office and world consequences in [office and season ledger](AFS_OFFICE_AND_SEASON_LEDGER.md) and relationships, knowledge disputes and claim history in [bonds and disclosure ledger](AFS_BONDS_AND_DISCLOSURE_LEDGER.md). Both need the same event and disclosure clocks but different retrieval keys. An office can pass from one person to another; a bond requires a particular pair and a particular direction. Stable event and claim IDs connect adjacent rows and full argument anchors, while the source-facing reading retains scene detail. Index a named place or family connection only where it affects a material claim.

| Responsibility | Capture at source boundary | Durable route / anticipated use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seasonal duty, act and effect | Title vs holder, adopted responsibility, demonstrated work, has the alleged seasonal mechanism been tested, affected territory and witnesses | [office and season ledger](AFS_OFFICE_AND_SEASON_LEDGER.md); anticipated institutional/seasonal-causality study |
| Protection and knowledge | Actor → recipient and real options, what knowledge could move, practice vs proclaimed bond, what a flashback reveals and who could remember it | [bonds and disclosure ledger](AFS_BONDS_AND_DISCLOSURE_LEDGER.md); person-level work and, if diagnostic changes recur, relationship or protection synthesis |
| Authority and story time | Distinguish narration, in-world origin story and institutional self-description; release, narrative disclosure, event, knowledge and admission clocks | Crosswalk in [office and season ledger](AFS_OFFICE_AND_SEASON_LEDGER.md), disputed attribution in [bonds and disclosure ledger](AFS_BONDS_AND_DISCLOSURE_LEDGER.md) and original reading; informs both specialist strands |
| Person-level synthesis | Recognized and personal roles, ordinary life, register, practical judgment, distinctive commitments across different listeners and available alternatives | [bonds and disclosure ledger](AFS_BONDS_AND_DISCLOSURE_LEDGER.md) qualification view, then `characters/AFS_<SUBJECT>_MONOGRAPH.md` |
| Art and localized textual form | Repeated motifs, placed maps or plates and onset of reader information | Reading as primary home; link decisive recurrence to the specialist argument |

Apparent conflicts can arise from local variation in a seasonal effect, uncertain chronology, offset viewpoints, competing myth accounts, a different title/edition or real contradiction. Check the source route and independent material consequences before choosing. In the bonds/disclosure ledger append `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT` or `OPEN`, with counterevidence, any change in provenance and current scope. A later-arising memory or Archer state enters on actual admission and can support a new interpretation without becoming contemporaneous proof for an earlier unit. Translation and adaptations remain out of the same continuity-state ledger.

## Standing source-reading topology

The proposed configuration requires an accountable principal analyst who inspects the whole admitted unit and is also the analytical integrator; separate independent specialists on seasonal office, enacted rules and institutional authority; Japanese focalization, event/disclosure ordering and terminology; and personal choices, protective bonds and ordinary behavior; and a distinct independent critical reviewer. The specialist contexts form their own observations from assigned original-language evidence before receiving the principal's interpretations. The critical reviewer directly inspects decisive and contradictory source evidence, checking the difference between sacred or institutional explanation and depicted effects, chronology and disclosure leakage across book pairs, specific actor and recipient attribution, counterinstances of care or refusal, biographical continuity and loss of ordinary-life evidence, before acceptance. The reviewer receives the interpretation after inspecting decisive evidence where practical. No present unit type qualifies for the reduced configuration; record any later authorized change under [topology §4.1](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EXECUTION_TOPOLOGY_AND_CAPABILITY_ROUTING_POLICY.md#multi-agent-source-reading). Verify these roles, evidence access and separation before first start; a runtime without adequate independent contexts is a required-capability stop. An owner-authorized deviation must state its exact scope and is recorded as a deviation, without ordinary acceptance or high-water advance unless explicitly authorized.

The unit checkpoint is the contribution and reconciliation record. Keep participant identity/role and separation, assignment, actual exposure and inspected coverage, material locator-backed observations, rivals and corrections, unresolved evidence obligations, and explicit adoption, qualification, rejection or open disposition for each material finding. Keep the finding and its reasoning recoverable; no generic participation summary, duplicated canonical artifact, or permanent per-agent ledger. Freeze prospective captures before resolution, separately by context where needed. Only the integrator updates shared readings, cumulative state and entrypoint after reconciliation. Record observed execution facts without inferring model settings. Keep source material and private transcripts in the evidence plane.

## Convergence and acceptance

Review initial ledgers after the first completed upper/lower pair or first approved arc boundary and again when the first Archer book is actually admitted. Check whether institutional state, personal choice and disclosure position remain recoverable without guessing inter-book chronology. When the authorized tranche closes, stabilize the release/admission and narrative-order crosswalk and reconcile open claims. The office and seasonal-causality account supplies tested constraints to the protective-bond account; the latter supplies evidence of real discretion, cost and response that can correct an empty model of a formal office. Where book structures make timing independently consequential, mature formal and chronology analysis must precede integration; where diagnostic patterns are absent, retain further formal profiling in the readings. A qualified character workstream and reconciled specialist arguments underpin the final bounded integration of cyclical obligation and personal action. Treat an Archer and Dance comparison as an evidence-dependent result after both horizons exist.

The principal closes each source transaction only with full assigned primary coverage, source-grounded independent contribution, independent critical examination, reconciled material disagreements, preserved evidence and counterexamples, consistent cumulative history and truthful exposure and authority boundaries. This is the sequential completion gate; the admitted, committed run boundary must also reconcile with its declared stop. Confirm longitudinal state and still-open evidence before a role-gap and specialist readiness review; require the qualified character work and the material specialist roles before authorizing a full synthesis. Review fidelity, locator retrieval, counterreadings, referential clarity and handoff acceptance separately from the decision to freeze or release. Any separately commissioned reconstruction model needs evidence-bounded validation of its own; a monograph or qualified portrait supplies no capability grade.

Use [initiation §5.5.1](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md#self-contained-character-monograph-standard) for qualification and complete standalone portraits. Assess every person with differentiated evidence, not merely leads; record stable identity, boundary, outcome, rationale, largest gaps, reassessment trigger and a path once a monograph actually exists. A qualified person creates a production obligation. An untested dimension remains insufficient evidence; an apparent absence needs an opportunity test. Include supported ordinary preferences, speech and address, decision patterns, relationships, changes, exceptions and limits. Document separate self-containment and source fidelity results before acceptance; no current portrait silently replaces a frozen earlier one.

Prefer fresh synthesis contexts for longer work after local source binding, multi-agent unit reading and a frozen owner-authorized arc/tranche handoff under the [long-series protocol](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_LONG_SERIES_HYBRID_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md). Specialists work against that bounded snapshot; local reconciliation resolves narrative and illustration evidence debts before acceptance and authorized repository integration. No continuous audiovisual work is planned.

Under the [reasoning policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_REASONING_AND_MODEL_ROUTING_POLICY.md), use `ROUTINE_FAST` for deterministic bookkeeping, `BOUNDED_STANDARD` for metadata and locator checks, `SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS` for unit interpretation, `DEEP_SYNTHESIS` for sustained specialist/character work and `PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST` for foundation, high-impact disputes and final integration review. A scoped transfer under the [analytical handoff contract](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md) includes the frozen admitted/exposure horizon, retrievable evidence and argument records, open obligations, and named output scope and receiver; reconcile against live state, retest material claims and names/references (§15) before any promotion.

Keep the entrypoint and cumulative views mutable; dated claim changes preserve the older version, counters and new evidence. Complete prospective captures, accepted historical checkpoints and any authorized release remain intact; later repairs use linked errata, claim revision or an attributable successor. Revisit INITIAL design at the first meaningful source boundary, at the planned title-specific checks above and before integrated synthesis. Add cumulative structure only when independent recurrent evidence or a retrieval gap warrants it; revise the architecture and entrypoint together and backfill only a material gap existing records cannot responsibly repair.
