# Office and season ledger

Source horizon: **NONE**. The method produces narrated observations; this home preserves material state transitions for the institutions and manifest effects. Full reasoning about causes and separate historical claims follows [bonds and disclosure ledger](AFS_BONDS_AND_DISCLOSURE_LEDGER.md) to the reading. Never fill a slot because the season has a name.

| Record | Retain for retrieval |
| --- | --- |
| Role, responsibility or capacity | Named person holding or claiming a role; designation and speaker; practical authority, recognized duty, demonstrated capacity, limits and refusal options; before and after with unit and source ID |
| Seasonal action and effect | Particular action, place, period and source frontier; invoked mechanism or custom and stating party; agency, enabling or constraining actors, actual manifestation and who observes it; consequence, scale, confidence and counterexample |
| Institutional response | Institution or group, actor making a decision, stated commitment, action and beneficiary/cost, evidence of compliance versus formal rule, protection or transfer of obligation, event/disclosure clocks and locator |
| Boundary crosswalk | Candidate/actual witness, published/prior role, admitted part, event-time range with uncertain links, agent-perspective information and cross-arc evidence IDs where established |

Do not project one office holder's experience onto another era, place or holder. Claims involving a social bond, an actor's belief, a later reveal or a forecast cross-reference the linked fields in [bonds and disclosure ledger](AFS_BONDS_AND_DISCLOSURE_LEDGER.md); those claims are not adjudicated by the presence of an office row.
