# Honkai: Star Rail — reconstruction contract

Reconstruction is an intended future derived use where evidence supports a named subject/state. It is not the master literary layer, a new canon, or an automatic purpose of a character-oriented source bundle. The historical target-profile label `PARTIAL` and its line counts are ingestion metadata. This project's state is **NOT_ASSESSED**; no evidence dossier, operational model, simulation, capability grade or subject readiness finding exists.

Adapt the [generic reconstruction method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md). Source evidence must first resolve identity, narrative foundation and contexts; source counts cannot satisfy that gate. A form-family identity crosswalk and separate character/relationship/epistemic states are mandatory from the first admitted unit. Source acquisition must recover a main-story foundation before target-focused retrieval can justify longitudinal claims. The targeted relationship projection remains a retrieval lead; a directional relationship synthesis needs independently inspected contributions from both participants and the relevant story horizon. Profile/voice-text coverage cannot close mission or visitor gaps. A form-specific model may never borrow another form’s biography or register merely because both share a family key.

## Evidence, model and monograph are separate

Future per-subject, per-state evidence dossiers route directly to full readings/native locators. They capture behavioral choices and motivations; developmental and knowledge boundaries; directional interlocutor-conditioned responses; ordinary behavior and expressive repertoire; source-language register; values in conflict; stress/operational-role effects; contradictions; negative constraints distinguished from missing opportunities; and explicit uncertainty. No diagnostic or universal psychological label is inferred from a normalized data field.

An operational model formalizes only the supported state and use conditions, including abstention when a scenario needs unobserved relationships, later knowledge, different variants/frames or unavailable performance channels. A literary monograph integrates development, form and meaning; it does not certify operational accuracy. Each future artifact has its own governing question and authority metadata. Do not create empty per-character folders now.

## Operational model contract

A future model must be more than a personality summary. It names its exact source and valid state, separates relatively stable dispositions from developmental state, current knowledge, relationship context and temporary conditions, and expresses conditional rules: given this situation and information, these goals or appraisals make this response plausible; these exceptions, rival motives and falsifying observations limit the rule. Every rule routes to specific evidence and counterevidence. Preserve the evidence's original scope instead of treating a repeated phrase as a universal law.

Separate appraisal and decision from their expression: what the subject notices or misunderstands, competing goals and value priorities, choice/avoidance/repair, then interlocutor-conditioned wording, address, register and silence. A linguistic model carries native examples, permitted variants, negative constraints and uncertainty; it does not imitate an adaptation's uninspected performance. Describe confidence through evidence support and distance from observed contexts, with explicit abstention outside supported identity, time, knowledge, relationship, continuity or modality. No invented numerical likelihoods substitute for calibration.

Before an authorized prediction test, freeze the model version, state selector, input situation, permitted evidence, predicted response/rationale and falsifiers without access to the withheld outcome. The independent evaluator then adjudicates against actual source evidence and a documented simpler baseline, preserving failures, competing interpretations and leakage limits. Testing a revised model on the answer that motivated its revision is retrospective consistency, not fresh confirmation. Keep prospective prediction, observed outcome, adjudication and model revision as distinct records. This operational specification supplements the adopted generic reconstruction method's §§9–12 and 14–18; no model or prediction is created by defining it.

## Evaluation and synthetic boundary

Before model construction, the independent evaluator controls a held-out split by source/context with identity and state boundaries, leakage audit and exposure log. Preserve complete evaluation answers outside the model's construction packet. Test ordinary conduct as well as crises, register/addressee shifts, motive conflicts, counterexamples, historical-state selection and impossible-information temptations. Record qualitative failure and coverage limits; do not invent precision percentages from intuition.

Material already consumed in construction may support `RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST` only. It cannot be renamed held-out validation. If genuinely independent contexts are unavailable, record the evaluation gap and retain provisional or unusable scope. A separate session does not itself erase source exposure. Independent branch observations must be genuinely distinct rather than shared dialogue reprinted in different views.

Generated dialogue, predicted scenarios and simulations are labeled synthetic and kept outside source evidence and literary observation stores. They cannot train later literary claims or validate themselves. A model revision follows actual new evidence, with changed states and affected tests named; new source release alone does not advance reconstruction-current. Only the designated evaluator writes capability grades in its governed registry. This project never assigns a tier or edits global character/reconstruction discovery outputs.

## Readiness record contract

**Row unit:** one named subject and source/state boundary after appropriate evidence exists. Fields: identity/crosswalk; continuity, form, unlock and valid period; admitted source lock; evidence coverage by domain/context/relationship; missing versus negative evidence; contradictions; dossier/model routes; held-out membership/exposure record; evaluator and test class; failure/abstention limits; independent assessment disposition; predecessor; next operation. Update only when inspected evidence, a corrected join or independent evaluation materially changes scope; preserve previous decisions.

Current readiness rows: **none**. Narrative foundation and identity/graph binding remain blocked by source-register obligations. All modeling and testing here is a future design, not an executed analysis.
