# Visual, language and recurrence register

## Responsibility

Track page construction, Japanese written/graphic expression and evidenced recurrence without replacing full close readings. No page or quotation is inspected at INITIAL.

## Schemas

| Record / key | Required fields |
|---|---|
| Page form `CM-V-nnnn` | Exact page/panel/spread; verified reading/facing order; framing/gaze/gutters/borders; silence/reaction/omission; described feature; effect hypothesis; rival; source frontier and full analysis |
| Japanese expression `CM-L-nnnn` | Speaker/thinker/narrator/recipient; exact balloon/region; short verified Japanese; quote state; ruby/base/address/modality/ellipsis; speech act/objective; register; gloss alternatives; graphic lettering distinction; contextual controls |
| Rendering/tonal shift `CM-F-nnnn` | Exact pages; local rendering convention; change and possible function; literal versus framing alternatives; causal/ethical limits; counterexample; full argument |
| Recurrence `CM-X-nnnn` | Paired/multiple exact occurrences; repeated and changed feature; selection basis; thematic/formal relation proposed; counterinstances; confidence; competing interpretation |
| Visual/text debt `CM-VD-nnnn` | Missing/unreadable/misordered region; affected claim; required inspection/retrieval; owner; actual resolution |

Quote states are EXACT_VERIFIED / WORKING_TRANSCRIPTION / PARAPHRASE. OCR alone is never exact verification. Sound effects are drawn sound/state/manner conventions, not performed audio. Still panels do not supply exact omitted motion. A page-turn argument needs verified facing-page layout.

## Update and anti-compression rules

After each full accepted reading append evidence with source-specific feature/effect/alternative reasoning in its full prose home. Include silent and routine pages where consequential, not only striking images. Record scoped no-material-addition when appropriate; uninspected pages never count as reviewed.

Compare voice across recipients and ordinary/pressured contexts. Trace appraisal, objective, speech act and recipient-conditioned register. Tone, symbolism and recurrence need variation and counterexamples. A stylistic change is not automatically a literal change in fictional conditions.

A corrected page mapping triggers retrieval review and claim dependency updates. Preserve prior form/voice interpretations through versioned claims. Checkpoint review tests memorable-example bias and whether a summary discarded the graphic or Japanese basis of an argument.
