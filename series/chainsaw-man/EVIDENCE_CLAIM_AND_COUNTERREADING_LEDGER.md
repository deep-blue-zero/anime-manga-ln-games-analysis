# Evidence, claim and counterreading ledger

## Responsibility and schemas

This ledger preserves recoverable evidence, serious competing interpretations and claim history. No literary claim, primary quotation or forecast exists now.

| Record / key | Required fields |
|---|---|
| Evidence `CM-E-nnnn` | Witness/version/digest route; chapter/image occurrence/panel/balloon; spine/member/map version; inspection lane/channel; observation or short quote/paraphrase and status; frontier; retrieval result; full argument; limits |
| Claim `CM-C-nnnn.vN` | Proposition; warrant class; exact source/knowledge/state boundary; confidence/reason; support; counterevidence; strongest rival; falsifier/discriminator; full prose/current authority; dependencies |
| Revision `CM-R-nnnn` | Prior/new formulation/version/boundary; transition; decisive evidence and reasoning; rival disposition; affected state/form/study/model; propagation status; reviewer/date |
| Dispute `CM-D-nnnn` | Incompatible claims/routes; source and knowledge position; possible causes; live alternatives; needed evidence; retained resolution history |
| Forecast `CM-FC-nnnn` | Prediction/rival; known boundary and exposure; exact pre-source freeze; target test evidence; later outcome; independent evaluator; opportunities/confounds |

Warrant classes: OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, CONTRADICTION, EXTERNAL_REFERENCE, EVALUATION, UNRESOLVED. Confidence high/moderate/low/unresolved needs a reason. Revision vocabulary: PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT, OPEN.

Dispute causes distinguish witness/page mapping, chronology/disclosure, knowledge asymmetry, report reliability, role/audience, state/context, rendering, interpretation, missing evidence and possible textual inconsistency. Multiple explanations may remain open.

## Update and preservation

Full readings/studies own developed causal, relational, linguistic and visual arguments. This register routes them; it cannot replace them with thesis labels. Create and retrieve evidence before promoting central claims. A missing locator stays unresolved.

Append claim versions without deleting former formulations or full counterarguments. A later interpretation records why it changes the earlier one and whether the earlier prospective account remains valid at its boundary. Propagate to all named dependencies. Frozen prose and predictions are immutable under the project freeze rules.

A locator correction retains old/new mappings and checks equivalence; changed evidence is a substantive revision. Independent review of major dispute closure must examine primary routes rather than the winning claim's summary.

Forecasts freeze before source exposure. Exposed tests are RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST. Synthetic outputs remain separate derived objects, never source quotations or independent evidence.
