# Chainsaw Man synthesis architecture

## 1. Purpose, boundary and authority

This design preserves **state-specific interpretation and its revision history** across a supplied Japanese V01–24 manga sequence. It prevents a later disclosure, a dramatic event or a final-state characterization from erasing earlier uncertainty, ordinary behavior, relationship conditions or page construction. No such event or character feature is asserted at bootstrap.

The [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) is the single present authority route. The [method](CM_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) governs source-facing work; the [initiation policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md), [generic architecture](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MULTI_DOCUMENT_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE_TEMPLATE.md), [reconstruction method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md) and archive/execution/handoff policies govern their own responsibilities.

INITIAL canonical scope covers rules and attributed metadata only. No pages, cast, plot or source unit is admitted; no architecture owner acceptance or model readiness is claimed. Numbered source availability is not an official completeness or ending declaration.

## 2. Source hierarchy and admission

Exact locked and inspected Japanese main pages govern their text/art claims. Language/publisher/import metadata govern reported identity, not narrative content. Readable rendering, correct ordering, complete component accounting and actual visual inspection are separately established.

The proposed lane is CM-JP-V01–24 after exact order verification. Do not infer Japanese chapter or arc boundaries from a foreign publisher box-set grouping. Serialization, revised editions, adaptations, companion material and unrelated works by the same creator remain excluded until independently identified and explicitly admitted. A later acquisition extends inventory, not authorization.

Extras, title pages, previews, author statements and other components have distinct source types and spoiler boundaries. Authorial intention does not settle narrative meaning. Secondary analysis may be an expressly admitted hypothesis/context index, never primary evidence. Derived readings and ledgers do not independently corroborate their own source.

## 5. Prospective sequence and checkpoint design

A verified volume is the source-facing unit, with chapter/movement knowledge frontiers. A later passage may change the interpretation of an earlier one, but the earlier prospective state remains recoverable. Keep exact entering/exit freezes and record any pre-exposure prediction.

The authorized run defines start, terminal unit, finer boundaries and checkpoint cadence. An initial planning proposal is no more than four volumes between full reconciliation checkpoints, shortened for material state/disclosure changes or unresolved source/claim problems. This is not a run, story-arc assertion or permission to read. A commercial grouping can prompt review of a possible checkpoint only after Japanese source structure is verified.

At closeout, submit the complete reading and five synchronized registers. An explicit no-change finding states inspected scope. Unread pages and missing updates are not no-change evidence. Checkpoints retrieve full prior readings, reconcile state/version differences and audit role gaps; they do not compress the corpus into a shorter substitute.

## 6. Temporal, epistemic and continuity state

The common state vector is `witness/version + admitted frontier + disclosure position + story-time constraints + source-established subject identity + knowledge/belief + physical/affective condition + role/resources + directed relationship + interpretive mode`. Unknown values and alternate identity/time mappings remain explicit.

Modes are PROSPECTIVE_AT_BOUNDARY, RETROSPECTIVE_WITHIN_ADMITTED_BOUNDARY, EDITION_COMPARATIVE and UNRESOLVED. Source facts, reported claims, a subject's belief and analyst inference are distinct. Later knowledge must not leak into earlier mental states. If identity or continuity is disputed, store competing mappings and their evidence rather than forcing one timeless model.

Change causes include DISPOSITION, KNOWLEDGE, RELATIONSHIP, CONTEXT, ROLE, RESOURCE, REVEALED_NOT_NEW and UNRESOLVED. Neither dramatic action nor repeated appearance proves durable disposition. Routine interactions and contrary cases are preserved as controls. An unobserved opportunity cannot support negative evidence.

Page order is disclosure order until actual evidence establishes story succession or simultaneity. A replay, memory, juxtaposition or rendering shift requires its own account; no exact intervening motion is inferred from still-image endpoints.

## 7. Contradiction, revision and full argument preservation

Classify conflict as witness/page mapping, chronology/disclosure, knowledge asymmetry, reliability of report, role/audience, state/context change, rendering convention, analyst disagreement, missing evidence or possible textual inconsistency. Several alternatives can remain live. Do not turn an unresolved cause into a confident psychological label.

Use PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT and OPEN. Every version records former/new wording, source and knowledge boundary, evidence, counterevidence, rivals, confidence reason and affected destinations. A rejected claim's argument and historical value remain retrievable.

Propagate consequential changes through state, directed ties, language/form claims, specialists and models. Preserve valid unaffected analysis. Frozen readings and forecasts keep their historical bytes; later corrections use errata or current superseding claims with reciprocal authority metadata. A polished synthesis is not allowed to silently replace extensive contrary reasoning with a convenient summary.

## 8. Monographs, specialists and convergence

A character monograph is commissioned only after adequate contextual, temporal, relational, ordinary-life and expressive evidence supports a developed question. It integrates action, motive alternatives, narration, state change, language and literary function; it does not output a personality checklist.

A directed-relationship study earns an independent home when asymmetry, mutual development, institutional conditions or persistent interpretive conflict cannot be responsibly assigned to one monograph. It keeps the broader relational network and meaningful counterexamples.

Potential form, institution, body/identity, ethical or thematic studies remain **anticipated questions**, not established themes. Commission only after recurring evidence demonstrates a load-bearing role. The role-gap audit may decide no separate specialist is needed, but must justify that choice from actual evidence and assign responsibility for the dimension.

Specialists receive frozen complete readings and tested evidence routes; they return full arguments with source returns, alternatives and limits. Convergence compares their dependencies and disagreements before integrated synthesis readiness. Continuous synthesis must explain the corpus's connected development and unresolved tensions, not concatenate summaries or make earlier evidence merely foreshadowing.

## 9. Reconstruction pathway, uncertainty and assessment

Reconstruction is an intended future responsibility for sufficiently evidenced, source-established subjects. No subject model or readiness grade exists. Designated agents own global character discovery and capability registries; this corpus supplies evidence and limitations.

A future dossier separates evidence/retrieval, operational model, independent tests and any literary monograph. Its identity is subject@witness/frontier/state, with excluded sources and latent familiarity. Twelve descriptive dimensions are goals/values, threat/appraisal, motive conflicts, knowledge/errors, attention/reasoning, affect/regulation, disposition versus condition, directed relationships, role/audience, repertoire/inhibitors, Japanese/graphic voice, and development/exceptions.

A model is a set of conditional rules, not a summary personality. Each rule includes trigger, stakes, current state, likely response family, inhibitors/overrides, exceptions, support, counterevidence, rival and falsifier. Trace appraisal → decision → communicative objective → recipient-conditioned expression; distinguish stable disposition from temporary states. Record confidence, evidence distance and abstention/multiple-response criteria where the request lies outside observed contexts. OBSERVED, CANDIDATE_POLICY and PROMOTED_POLICY remain distinct.

Coverage considers ordinary interaction, pressure/conflict, uncertain/false knowledge, changed recipient/relationship, role/audience shift and consequential choice. Missing contexts restrict scope. Written/graphic expression testing separates decision fidelity from language/presentation fidelity; no vocal or continuous-motion capability is inferred.

Freeze genuinely held-out predictions before source exposure. Separate author and evaluator; disclose familiarity and leakage. Exposed exercises are RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST. Independent outcomes are SUPPORTED, PARTLY_SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY, CONFOUNDED and UNRESOLVED. Use diagnostic perturbations, plausible decoys, negative controls and baselines with explicit opportunities/denominators; no invented accuracy metric.

Synthetic dialogue and scenarios are derived tests, never primary evidence, canonical quotations or independent corroboration. Literary monograph completion does not certify predictive readiness.

## 11. Execution, handoff and growth

Use SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS for future readings and interpretive updates; DEEP_SYNTHESIS for checkpoints, specialists, monographs and model/test work; PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST for architecture and continuous synthesis because state/knowledge errors propagate. Stable indexing is BOUNDED_STANDARD and hashes ROUTINE_FAST. Resolve actual configuration under the [reasoning policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_REASONING_AND_MODEL_ROUTING_POLICY.md), recording only observed settings.

The [long-series protocol](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_LONG_SERIES_HYBRID_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md) prefers local source-capable sequential work/ledgers, fresh fully supplied synthesis sessions, independent review and designated integration. Current access establishes metadata/documents only. Under [capability routing](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EXECUTION_TOPOLOGY_AND_CAPABILITY_ROUTING_POLICY.md), verify transport, processing, STILL_IMAGE/TEXT inspection, interpretation and persistence separately.

The [handoff contract](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md) requires base snapshot, full exact input/output paths/digests, authority, source/admission/knowledge boundary, owners and open obligations. Returned work includes complete outputs and change/evidence routes. Integrators compare base, candidate and current target, retrieve central evidence and check lost ordinary-life, linguistic, causal or competing-argument detail. One mutable owner per register applies.

Add new volumes/editions only after identity reconciliation; inventory extension never authorizes reading. Add infrastructure for proven recurring responsibilities, record schema migration and proportional affected-evidence backfill, not a blanket reread. Current ledgers preserve append-only versions; accepted readings/checkpoints freeze; historical containers exist only for real superseded work.
