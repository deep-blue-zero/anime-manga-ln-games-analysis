# State, knowledge and relationship ledger

## Responsibility

Retain exact subject, causal, information and directed relationship states without collapsing them into a final personality or later explanation. No subject identity, event or relationship is established at INITIAL.

## Operational schemas

| Record / prefix | Required fields |
|---|---|
| Identity/state `CM-S-nnnn` | Source-established subject key/name; alias or concealment at frontier; witness/version; disclosure/story-time constraints; behavior/speech/thought/report channel; physical/affective condition; role/resources; disposition hypothesis; alternatives; evidence and full argument |
| Knowledge `CM-K-nnnn` | Subject/narrator/reader; proposition; known/believed/reported/unknown/withheld state; acquisition route/time; audience; who lacks it; reliability of this act; evidence; prospective/retrospective mode |
| Causal decision `CM-A-nnnn` | Conditions; actor perception/appraisal; affect; options/inhibitors; decision/non-action; consequence; supported/unknown links; competing cause; evidence; claim version |
| Directed tie `CM-REL-nnnn` | A→B; source frontier; A's knowledge/expectation; action/speech; source-earned trust/dependence/pressure/access or other dimensions; role/audience/resource conditions; counterevidence/rival; independent B→A link |
| Ordinary opportunity `CM-O-nnnn` | Subject/tie; routine context or real opportunity; stakes/options; observed action/non-action; comparable contexts; negative-evidence denominator; limits and counterexample |
| Institutional condition `CM-I-nnnn` | Source-established group/rule; speaker/authority; stated requirement versus observed custom/enforcement; resources and exceptions; affected subjects; change evidence; full argument |

Unknowns remain UNKNOWN/UNRESOLVED with reasons. No field requires a positive psychological claim where the source withholds evidence. Change causes: DISPOSITION / KNOWLEDGE / RELATIONSHIP / CONTEXT / ROLE / RESOURCE / REVEALED_NOT_NEW / UNRESOLVED.

## State versioning and update

Use witness/version + admitted frontier + disclosure + story-time constraints + knowledge + condition/role/resources + directed relationship as the state key. Preserve alternate identity/time mappings where needed. A later revelation adds a version; it never edits the earlier person's knowledge or the reader's uncertainty.

After each accepted reading append state/knowledge/causal/tie changes and scoped no-change findings. Cite exact evidence and the full argumentative section. Ordinary behavior and counterinstances remain first-class support. A crisis choice cannot alone establish stable disposition, and absent depiction is not an observed refusal.

Checkpoint review compares causal alternatives and state boundaries, checks directionality and ordinary controls, and propagates consequential changes through claims, form/language studies and model coverage. One sequential owner mutates this ledger. Frozen readings are corrected via revision, not overwritten.
