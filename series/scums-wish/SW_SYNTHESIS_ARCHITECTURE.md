# Scum’s Wish synthesis architecture

## 1. Purpose, authority and two evidence horizons

The canonical root is `series/scums-wish/`; [CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) is its sole current entrypoint. SW-G1 pairs this architecture with [SW_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md](SW_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md). No previous project architecture is superseded.

The intended corpus explains the relations among a person's avowed desires, private or reported perspectives, observed conduct, directed relationships and consequences, and how Japanese expression and page form mediate those relations. No specific discrepancy, relationship or theme is asserted at bootstrap. The work's title is not evidence of anyone's moral character.

The source topology is bounded but asymmetric: eight inventoried main volumes and one separately classified decor volume. This design preserves a main-only horizon and a supplementary horizon instead of making nine files one undifferentiated source pool. It also avoids multiplying a compact work into a large franchise-style directory tree. Substantial interpretation can occupy a small number of studies when their arguments cover the required responsibilities.

Authority is canonical only for this INITIAL procedural foundation. Sources are unadmitted and uninspected; run authorization, independent review and semantic findings are absent. The architecture cannot confer them by front matter.

[SW_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md](SW_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md) owns nine witness rows and seven control/gap records. The planned sequence is MAIN V01–08. The source receipt labels V09 decor an epilogue/spin-off, but its actual story chronology and relationship to the main sequence remain unverified.

Separate admission does not assert separate fictional continuity. Conversely, the same creator or volume numbering cannot establish identity of every narrative frame. Investigate actual frames, event chronology and retrospective function when authorized. Retain edition/translation disagreements as witness differences until supported reconciliation.

## 3. Corpus layout and full-reading transaction

Day-one files comprise the method, architecture, source inventory, perspective/state ledger, directed relationship ledger, expression/situation ledger, claim revision ledger, reconstruction contract, producer review, current entrypoint and routing descriptor. These are source/control and methodological surfaces, with zero invented analytical entries.

Future homes are `readings/` for volume arguments, `checkpoints/` for frozen horizon states, `studies/` for earned interpretive work, `reconstruction/` for distinct evidence/model/testing artifacts, and `audits/` for actual review. Create each directory with its first substantive artifact. Do not create empty character dossiers, thematic files or supplemental readings.

An accepted volume transaction consists of the full causal reading and entering/exit freezes; state/perspective events; directed relationship changes; source-verified expression and ordinary-situation evidence; claims, questions and forecast adjudications; actual page coverage; and current-state advancement. A table is retrieval apparatus. It cannot stand in for the argument that explains motive, ambiguity, formal effect or causal consequence.

Each major reading thesis must keep its inferential warrant, material evidence, strongest plausible counterreading and disconfirming case. A later specialist may challenge that thesis through visible claim transitions; it may not silently replace a complete argument with an abstract.

## 6. Studies and depth obligations

The main-sequence literary integration must answer three connected questions: how perspective or testimony permits an account of each material person's conduct; how directed interpersonal states change and produce consequences; and how page form and Japanese expression shape those interpretations. These are mandatory responsibilities, not necessarily three documents.

Create an independent character monograph when a named person's development, multiple situations or distinct relationships require sustained explanation beyond the integrated argument. A one-unit subject can earn a bounded case study, with its limits stated. A relationship study requires source-supported directional evidence, consequential change and a distinct question. Do not flatten people into objects in another person's desire, or deduce reciprocity from the label of a relationship.

A monograph must account for self-report, behavior, access to interiority, ordinary-life controls, changes, relationship conditioning, counterexamples and literary function. A relationship study must distinguish each actor's knowledge and appraisal, the evidence for an asymmetric account and the consequences of their interaction. Both need claim IDs and precise locators. Reconstruction formalizes conditional behavior and testing separately.

A study of ethics or social constraint becomes mandatory only when the role-gap review demonstrates that the integrated interpretation cannot responsibly answer the source without it. Conversely, when a separate specialist is unnecessary, record why substantive integrated sections cover its responsibility. A missing document is not automatically a missing analysis; a titled document is not automatically adequate analysis.

No universal word cap governs a mature study. Acceptance examines whether substantial causal mechanisms, alternative explanations, representative evidence, exceptions, named actors and temporal distinctions are preserved. Cross-session compression of those elements is a defect even if every heading survives. A continuous synthesis must reconcile its arguments, not concatenate specialist summaries.

## 7. Evidence routes, contradictions and revision

A mature proposition routes through claim ID → exact reading/study anchor → witness/track/unit/image/panel locator. Japanese quotations are brief and source-verified. Raw pages, text dumps and private receipt details remain outside Git. A convenient corpus summary may orient retrieval but cannot replace the full argument or underlying evidence.

Before reconciling an apparent contradiction, check speaker/thinker attribution, witness, chronology, narrative frame, disclosure frontier and wording. Then test change in state/context, self-description versus conduct, reported belief, retrospective revelation, edition/translation difference, paratext or genuine inconsistency. Record competing accounts and a diagnostic requirement where the evidence does not decide.

The claim ledger uses PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT and OPEN, retaining prior formulations and affected downstream responsibilities. MAIN-only and DECOR-aware claims use explicit horizons. A supplementary interpretation may qualify the mature account without making the prospective main interpretation dishonestly certain. If a central claim changes, reopen its dependent specialist argument rather than append an isolated note.

## 8. Dependency graph and distinct gates

Production order is:

`source verification + authorized OPEN run → MAIN readings and synchronized ledgers → main completion audit → longitudinal reconciliation → main role-gap/specialist readiness → substantial main arguments → convergence → main-only integrated synthesis readiness → validation/freeze`.

A separately authorized continuation may then take:

`preserved main-only checkpoint + DECOR source verification → DECOR reading and state/claim deltas → cross-horizon reconciliation → affected-specialist revision → supplement-aware convergence/synthesis → validation/freeze`.

A main-only release can be valid within its stated boundary; it must not pretend to include decor. The first path is not permission to execute either. A pre-supplement main-only checkpoint is mandatory even if the same owner instruction later authorizes both tracks.

| Gate | Condition and consequence |
|---|---|
| Bootstrap review | Current-eligible method/architecture, schema/control homes, descriptor and truthful CLOSED state reviewed |
| First-source admission | Witness mapping and adequate image/language route, owner-defined run and consistent OPEN state |
| Unit completion | Full causal prose, counterreading, exact coverage, ledger synchronization and exit freeze accepted |
| MAIN source completion | Actual V01–08 coverage/exclusions accounted for; metadata presence alone insufficient |
| Longitudinal reconciliation | State, person knowledge, directed relationships, expression/ordinary coverage and claims share a consistent horizon |
| Character/specialist readiness | Role-gap audit and evidence/locator stabilization; ordinary/contextual limits visible |
| Specialist convergence | Interacting arguments and contradictions reconciled; explicit reason if independent specialists unnecessary |
| Integrated-synthesis readiness | Required inputs complete, substantive reasoning retained, boundary fixed and debts legitimately bounded |

| Supplement reconciliation | New event/state versus new analyst knowledge separated; affected claims/studies reviewed |
| Final validation | Interpretation tested against source routes, counterevidence, referential clarity and required repository checks |
| Release/freeze | Accepted declared-scope artifacts preserved; owner/CI integration route, with no self-merge |
| Reconstruction acceptance | Separate subject/state model tests and independent review; capability grading reserved to evaluator |

## 9. Lifecycle and preservation

INITIAL means the source has not tested this design. Review after first accepted MAIN volume, at MAIN V04 as a practical workload checkpoint (not an asserted story arc), at MAIN V08, before DECOR, after DECOR if admitted, before specialist/synthesis work and before release. Also review whenever a material new source, frame or recurring dimension appears.

At review record duty, artifact/section, SATISFIED/PARTIALLY_SATISFIED/MISSING/MISROUTED/TOO_THIN/NOT_APPLICABLE, evidence, consequence and next owner. Extend only when a dimension recurs, accumulates independently, changes interpretation or needs retrieval that existing homes cannot adequately provide. Material amendments update the architecture history and entrypoint. Targeted backfill repairs real evidence gaps; do not reread everything merely because the architecture evolved.

Current entrypoint, source controls and current-view indexes remain mutable by targeted edits. Events, forecast freezes, entering/exit states and accepted main-only checkpoints preserve history. Corrections use attributable errata and claim revisions. A frozen reading's bytes and historical knowledge cannot be silently rewritten. Model revisions retain assessed versions and reciprocal supersession. Bootstrap creates no fictitious run-stop/terminal seal.

| Stage | Preferred owner and lane | Class |
|---|---|---|
| Metadata/witness routing | Source operator, TEXT metadata | BOUNDED_STANDARD |

| Sequential reading and cumulative state | Local executor, verified STILL_IMAGE plus Japanese TEXT | SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS |
| Main checkpoint and specialist/model work | Fresh synthesis session on exact frozen evidence | DEEP_SYNTHESIS |
| Cross-horizon convergence and final integration | Fresh substantive analyst/reviewer, local evidence completion | PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST where propagation-sensitive conflicts justify it |
| Publication | Authorized repository operator; specialized index owners remain separate | Actual check/semantic workload |
| Discovery and capability assessment | Designated discovery agent and reconstruction evaluator | Outside series-author scope |

The local bootstrap uses verified metadata access; it does not claim actual manga-image capability. Future stages probe source transport, processing, inspection, interpretation scope and persistence. No textual or static-image route may claim acoustic performance. Models/effort belong in observed execution records, not enduring artifact names.

Handoff must include full files, hashes, exact base, source and knowledge horizons, inspected versus missing inputs, claim transitions, unresolved duties, target paths and preservation instructions. Receiver verifies bytes and baseline drift, reads the complete arguments and checks substantive loss. Substantial cloud authorship remains the preferred synthesis route when adequately grounded; receiving integration may revise supported defects without flattening the work. Repository pass, semantic acceptance, branch publication and main integration are distinct facts.
