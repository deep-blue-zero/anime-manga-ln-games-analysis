## Ensemble and horizon rules

When a multi-person configuration matters, open an ENSEMBLE-tagged claim identifying the actual directed events, who knows which part and the missing edges. A diagram or triangle label cannot infer motivations. A study must retain each person's agency and named causal role.

MAIN and DECOR remain separate evidence horizons. A later event may be a new relationship state, newly disclosed prior state or an observer's revised account. Preserve these alternatives until evidence resolves them. Main-only exit state stays frozen even if the supplementary account changes the mature reading.

## Update/history and acceptance

Append events with stable IDs during the volume transaction, preserving prior events. Correct a mistaken attribution or interpretation through a dated correction and claim transition, never by deleting the earlier relation. A current-edge view may route `from | to | horizon | latest_event | live_alternatives` without replacing history.

For acceptance, check direction, named subjects, speech/thought attribution, event/disclosure timing, constraints, counterexamples and consequences in the full reading. An emotional label alone is inadequate; show why the evidence supports it and what plausible rival explanation remains.
