# Japanese expression and situated behavior

## Responsibility and controls

The model or critic must not learn a person's baseline only from salient conflict or convert private thoughts into public speaking habits. This ledger retrieves contextual speech/thought and ordinary-behavior samples. It does not assert that any particular situation appears in the unread source.

| Control | Present value | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Japanese source-verified expression samples | 0 | No register or written-voice model |
| Ordinary / crisis comparison samples | 0 / 0 | No general behavioral conclusion |
| Speaker/thinker attribution | UNASSESSED | No cast established |
| Audio/performed voice | OUT_OF_SCOPE | Lettering does not establish acoustic delivery |
| MAIN/DECOR comparison | NOT_AUTHORIZED | Supplement cannot supply an early baseline |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use SW-X0001 onward. Layer distinguishes SPEECH, THOUGHT, NARRATION, DIEGETIC_TEXT, ASIDE and EFFECT. Pragmatic purpose is an inference unless explicitly established. Preserve Japanese address, self-reference, politeness, endings, omission, ruby and contextual contrast where material. A phrase observed once is a sample, not a general rule. Glosses remain translations, and quotations are brief.

## Situated-behavior schema

One SW-O0001-style event uses `sample_id | subject_key | interlocutor/audience | track/unit/horizon | situation | stakes(ORDINARY/CRISIS/MIXED) | opportunity | observed_action_or_inaction | source_layer/confidence | state_and_edge_links | competing_explanation | reading_anchor/locators`.

Potential categories include routine, humor, care, work/study, conflict, solitude and other source-established situations. Record whether the sample gives a real diagnostic opportunity. Missing contexts are GAP; an evidence-backed refusal or inability requires observed opportunity and support. An apparent counterexample may reflect changed audience or stakes rather than inconsistency.

Neither analytical table contains rows.

## Updating and preserving evidence

Append material samples in the corresponding reading transaction; stable IDs survive reclassification. Correction records carry `correction_id | prior_sample | date | old/new | source_basis | affected_claims`. Keep earlier attribution recoverable, and do not rewrite frozen knowledge horizons. A later DECOR sample adds a separate horizon; it cannot be silently counted as main-only evidence.

Checkpoint coverage uses `subject | horizon | situation_types | interlocutors | speech/thought_samples | missing_opportunities | linked_claims`, with qualitative limits rather than a score. The interpretation must retrieve the actual examples and full reasoning, not infer a trait from counts.

Page form belongs in the reading and FORM-tagged claims; cite it here when needed to understand a sample. Repeated visual motifs earn a separate retrieval structure only if the architecture review demonstrates an independent need.
