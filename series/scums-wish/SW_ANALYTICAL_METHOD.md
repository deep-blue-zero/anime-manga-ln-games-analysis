# Scum’s Wish analytical method

## 1. Read the main sequence without borrowing the supplement

This project analyzes the Japanese manga クズの本懐, creator metadata 横槍メンゴ. Inventory covers the main volumes 01–08 and a separately labeled volume 09 decor. The [source inventory](SW_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md) distinguishes present files from inspected evidence. No content has been read in this bootstrap.

The method's central design problem is preserving what the main sequence permits at each point while keeping a later supplementary witness available for a separately labeled reassessment. The supplement's actual temporal and interpretive relationship to the main sequence must be established from authorized inspection. Its metadata label cannot tell an analyst what the main work means or how a person ultimately develops.

A relationship-centered inquiry should distinguish what a person wants, says, believes, does and causes, and how the pages grant or withhold access to those differences. These are questions for the unread source, not asserted findings. The English title is not a moral diagnosis or a substitute for individualized characterization.

The [generic manga method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MANGA_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) supplies the evidence discipline adapted here. [The synthesis architecture](SW_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) governs cumulative homes, main-only checkpointing and later convergence.

## 2. Entry conditions and unit order

No sequential run is authorized. Before a future volume reading, require source binding, a verified STILL_IMAGE/Japanese lettering route, satisfied day-one infrastructure and an owner-authorized run recorded with an OPEN lock. Technical source inspection must remain within the permitted reconnaissance or run boundary; a source's presence gives no admission authority.

The primary unit is the collected volume in verified spine order. Within it, identify actual chapter labels and dramatic movements by shifts in focalization, goal, configuration, disclosure or page form. Do not substitute processing batches for dramatic segmentation. A scene crossing chapter boundaries remains causally connected even when the output needs subheadings.

Planned main reading order is V01 through V08. DECOR09 is separately gated: preserve the accepted main-only exit state and interpretive checkpoint before admitting the supplement. This ordering protects evidence states; it does not predeclare decor's story chronology. No future source, edition, translation, anime/live-action track or plot summary is admitted by this foundation.

Before reading a witness, verify its immutable private identity and public alias, classify its apparatus, map image occurrences and verify legibility. Historical archive checks do not establish missing pages, spreads, chapter boundaries or image review. Exclude or separately admit covers, profiles, jokes, notes and previews according to their function and spoiler risk.

## 3. Perspective, testimony and temporal state

Disclose prior exposure before a run, including adaptation memories or remembered outcomes. Quarantine it. Freeze the entering state before each next authorized volume. Keep separate scene-level knowledge, end-of-unit analytical knowledge and project-level admitted knowledge.

For each movement identify the visible actor, focalizing subject if established, source of any account, addressee and audience. Distinguish speech, thought, narration, retrospective narration, publisher captions and diegetic writing. A thought balloon or narration box may offer privileged presentation without proving every accompanying belief correct. Another person's report of a motive records the reporter's belief unless independently supported.

Preserve the gap between avowal and action without diagnosing every mismatch as deception. Possible causes include changed context, uncertainty, different aims, misunderstanding, limited access, retrospectively revised self-description or inconsistent evidence. The source must decide which are supportable. Wordless composition may support an inference, but does not give direct mind access.

Track event time separately from the order and horizon of disclosure. An insight received in MAIN V08 cannot become a fact known in a prospective MAIN V02 account. A later decor claim is supplementary hindsight unless it establishes a within-story earlier event; even then, it does not rewrite the historical main-only reading. Mark REVEALED_NOT_NEW separately from actual behavioral change.

## 4. Causal reading and named people

Every important movement needs more than a synopsis. Explain its entering situation, the named person's perceived stakes and possible actions, the choice or constraint that changes the situation, the consequence, and the evidence for the causal link. Separate coincidence, interruption, institutional/customary pressures and a character's own explanation from demonstrated causation.

Use the person's source-established name or an unambiguous natural form once available within the relevant horizon. Do not habitualize “the girl,” “the boy,” “the teacher,” “the object of desire” or “the scum” as substitutes for an identified individual. Preserve intentional anonymity and unresolved aliases. A role label is useful when the role itself explains an obligation or perception; name the actual actor where causality requires it.

Do not turn a relationship into a single shared feeling. Record A toward B separately from B toward A: desire, trust, dependence, pressure, recognition, refusal, care and knowledge may diverge. Who initiates, who interprets, who responds and who bears consequences must remain identifiable. Multi-person patterns require the constituent directed evidence and cannot be inferred from a schematic triangle.

## 5. Evidence and interpretive status

Use OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, EVALUATION and UNRESOLVED, with HIGH, MODERATE, LOW or UNRESOLVED confidence. External material is EXTERNAL_REFERENCE and excluded until separately admitted. Narrative assertions and value judgments retain their own attribution.

For each governing claim write the observation, inferential warrant, strongest competing explanation and what would weaken it. An inconclusive source supports an OPEN conclusion. A narratively salient crisis does not prove a stable disposition; a minor ordinary scene may undermine an apparently broad characterization.

When intimacy, consent, sexual conduct, coercion or injury is actually depicted, distinguish desire from consent, consent from reciprocity, an act from its framing, and an explanation from moral approval. Use the minimum description necessary for analysis with precise locators. Neither subsequent affection nor narrative sympathy repairs earlier consent. Avoid both condemnation by epithet and apologetic harmonization. Ethical evaluation should answer what the source establishes and what the analyst is adding.

## 6. Ordinary contexts and Japanese expression

Deliberately inspect ordinary interaction, humor, routine, care, work/study or idle behavior when available. Preserve opportunity: an absence can test a behavioral rule only where the person had a real chance to act. If the source provides no diagnostic ordinary-life sample, record a coverage limit, not a personality conclusion.

Inspect Japanese speech and thought at the page. Record address terms, self-reference, politeness, endings, omissions, discourse markers and ruby/furigana where load-bearing. Attribute speaker/thinker and recipient with confidence. A private expression is not automatically something a person would say aloud. A marked utterance needs comparison across contexts before it becomes a voice rule.

Keep the brief attested form, a clearly labeled gloss, context, interlocutor, speech act and counterexample. A translated summary cannot establish Japanese voice. Written-register analysis cannot infer acoustic tone, delivery or actor performance; no audiovisual evidence is inside the present boundary.

## 7. Page form and embodied presentation

Read all authorized pages, including silent ones. Consider point of view, panel scale, framing, proximity, barriers, gaze, withheld faces/reactions, repeated poses, tonal fields, backgrounds, gutters and pacing where consequential. The claim must describe the feature, explain its possible effect and consider another reading. A striking image is not inherently symbolic.

Do not equate a face with a unique emotion or a bodily pose with a confirmed motive. Word/text juxtaposition may qualify a self-account; explain the relation rather than simply quote the balloon. Distinguish ordinary, comic, stylized and symbolic rendering registers. A shift in register is presentation, not necessarily a literal event.

Preserve whole spreads and confirm facing-page layout before a page-turn claim. Digital image occurrence and printed page number remain different. Gutters establish selected moments, not continuously observed motion. Sound effects and state effects are written graphic conventions with source-language form and placement; they do not provide acoustic measurement.

## 8. Locators, coverage and claim history

Use `SW-JP-M01-W01 / MAIN-V01 / Ch.<verified label or UNKNOWN> / i0001 / p<visible label or NONE> / panel 1 / balloon 1`; supplement locators use `SW-JP-D09-W01 / DECOR-V09`. Witness aliases must be bound before they become usable locators. Image occurrences are one-based in actual spine reading order including apparatus. Do not renumber duplicates away. Panels/balloons follow the inspected reading order; ambiguous sequences are flagged, and crops retain full-page parent and region coordinates.

L1 locators identify exact image/panel/balloon, L2 a bounded scene, L3 a unit only and L4 a pattern with representative L1 instances. Unverified and L3-only locators cannot carry contested quotations or model validation. A claim links to its stable reading section, exact source route and admitted horizon.

Keep per-image review states in the evidence plane: REVIEWED_MATERIAL, NO_MATERIAL_ADDITION, DUPLICATE, EXCLUDED or UNRESOLVED_READABILITY. Record actual scope, exceptions and legibility debts in Git through safe aliases. Bulk source images, OCR, copied dialogue, private acquisition records and access metadata stay outside Git.

Material claim changes use PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT or OPEN with prior wording retained. Questions and forecasts keep their original horizon. DECOR may trigger a new supplementary formulation and a cross-horizon comparison; it cannot falsify the fact that the earlier uncertainty existed.

## 9. Required future reading and acceptance

Create `readings/SW_MAIN_VNN_DEEP_READING.md`, or separately authorized `readings/SW_DECOR_V09_DEEP_READING.md`, with `artifact_type: deep_reading` only during a legitimate run. Required content:

1. Witness, admitted horizon, exposure disclosure, inspection coverage and exclusions.
2. Entering state and unresolved claims before this unit.
3. Governing thesis and strongest plausible rival reading.
4. Movement-by-movement causal prose with named actors, focalization/disclosure frontiers and locators.
5. Person/state changes, directed relationships, ordinary situations and contextual constraints.
6. Japanese register and consequential visual form, including text/image tension if present.
7. Counterevidence, competing explanations, claim transitions and forecast adjudications.
8. Exact ledger-update manifest, exit state and remaining debts/completion decision.

A full reading is an argued interpretation, not a filled table or collection of labels. Preserve causal mechanisms, significant examples and counterexamples, ambiguity and temporal transitions without a universal word ceiling. Each mandatory ledger advances atomically with the accepted unit; a reading with only prose or only rows is incomplete. “No material change” belongs in the transaction manifest with a reason, not a routine filler row.

Before acceptance, perform the handoff §15.6 bounded name/reference check on opening/synopsis, a substantial middle section, exit synthesis and each major relationship. Classify purposeful role labels, source-required anonymity and knowledge-boundary needs separately from unnecessary genericization, ambiguous referents or incorrect attribution. Correct only supported defects and record scope.

Receiver review tests source accuracy, full causal reasoning, competing readings, ordinary/register coverage, directional states, named subjects and preserved incoming substance. A passing repository validator does not certify literary quality. Hold affected completion when essential pages, language, attribution or causal support remain unresolved.

## 10. Main-only and supplement-aware interpretation

After accepted MAIN V08, perform the main-source completion and longitudinal audits, preserve a main-only checkpoint and review specialist responsibilities. That checkpoint must retain the main sequence's supported conclusions and live alternatives without consulting DECOR. It can support a clearly scoped main-only synthesis when its own gates are met.

Only a later authorized supplement stage may compare that frozen interpretation with DECOR. First inspect the supplement on its own actual evidence, then classify each consequential effect as confirmation, development, retrospective explanation, newly disclosed prior fact, divergence or unresolved conflict. Separate new character state from revised analyst knowledge. Preserve both the main-only and supplement-aware horizons; “later” is not synonymous with “better” or “automatically overriding.”
