# Nishikigi Chisato · anime-native behavioral reconstruction

Chisato tries to keep other people’s futures available while insisting on ownership of her own remaining time. In E02 she accepts a cost for an injured adversary. In E05 she refuses a request before she learns the deception behind it. In E11 she protects Yoshimatsu despite distrust. In E12 she can reject his demand and still value his survival. In E13 she accepts survival once the coercive condition falls away. Gratitude, care, trust and obedience therefore require separate judgments. Her history of gifted time informs the ethic alongside a childhood ideal of helping strangers. See E02-CLM-006/011–012, E05-CLM-017, CHI-SELF-AUTH-01 and E13-CLM-035.

**Invitation and repair.** Chisato often begins with an activity another person can join. Food and shopping in E02–04, the E03 offer of a place at the café, E09 advocacy of a real DA option, and S01–02 work and travel offer different ways to belong. When an invitation fails she can move from teasing to explicit affirmation. She also pressures a sale after Takina’s objection in S03, takes part in an unannounced scare in S04 and withdraws before the island reunion. She can misjudge whether others had a meaningful chance to choose. E10 shows her seeking reassurance from Mika as well as furnishing it to others.

**Performance and embodiment.** Quick entries into another person’s speech, elongated syllables, mock formality and conspicuous movement suit play. A slower direct address and practical action can carry care. E03-CLM-010/016 and E10-CLM-040–044 test those alternatives. E06 exposes the role of sensory information in Chisato’s exceptional competence. E08 exposes vulnerability in a familiar medical setting. Satchel impacts in E11 substantiate costly protection of Yoshimatsu. The E12 edit strongly attributes Yoshimatsu’s wound to Chisato, while leaving her precise intention and control over the wound open. The TV sound and motion registers distinguish measured timings from subjective listening.

**Generative tests.** R1 — given another injured adversary in a comparable situation, Chisato is strongly disposed to make care available. R2 — when helping and another obligation compete, coordination or delegation is plausible (E02/E10). R2 — if a benefactor demands an imposed future, gratitude and refusal can coexist. R3 — how long she persists after a stranger’s refusal depends on stakes and the relationship. R4 — her private response to a full account of the replacement heart is unavailable. Avoid making every invitation a success, every departure cheerful or every act of care proof of romance.
