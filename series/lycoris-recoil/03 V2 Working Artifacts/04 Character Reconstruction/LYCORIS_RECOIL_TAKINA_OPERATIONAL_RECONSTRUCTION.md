# Inoue Takina · deciding with the information she has

**Operational reconstruction.** This successor predicts classes of response for Inoue Takina (井ノ上たきな). The input is a particular point in her life, the people and information actually available to her, and a decision she can make. The output is a likely appraisal, the action and speech appropriate to somebody in her position, a credible alternative, and a reason to revise or abstain. Canonical Japanese phrases below are identified by their source. Newly composed lines have an explicit extrapolation label. The separate literary monograph develops the meaning of these events across the work.

**Authority.** Use the thirteen TV episodes and six *Friends are thieves of time* shorts (A1) for originating behavior. The frozen [CP5 Takina profile](LYCORIS_RECOIL_TAKINA_BEHAVIORAL_RECONSTRUCTION.md) and [nineteen-unit record](LYCORIS_RECOIL_CP5_ANIME_NATIVE_BASELINE.md) retain their historical roles. This successor preserves their predictions and incorporates attributable later corrections. The three Asaura novels (A2) can extend an explanation in a comparable setting. Abe Kanari’s *Recollect* (B1) and individually credited anthology stories (C1) supply selectable realizations. Their actors’ extra knowledge, speech, histories and frequencies are local. An interviewee can describe production but cannot supply Takina with an in-world memory.

## How to make one prediction

1. Locate her in a disclosed state below. Record who has told her what. Knowing an institution is compromised, knowing the partner’s heart is artificial and knowing it has been damaged are three different things.
2. Identify her role and the other person’s authority to accept, refuse, correct or release her. Write down who bears the cost if she is wrong. Include an affected civilian even when Takina does not ask them.
3. Find the closest observed decision and name a case in which the same surface tendency produced a different act. Ask what Takina can actually change, whose purpose counts as the goal of the work, whether a delay matters, and what is still her own choice.
4. Give an initial response and one alternative that survives the evidence. Identify what new information, action or refusal would lead her to switch, or what would remain contested. A probable event earns no greater authority through being written fluently.
5. Report **source class**, **observation confidence**, and **distance** separately. R1 is a close reconstruction, R2 a constrained interpolation, R3 an extrapolation with at least one major untested variable, and R4 an open simulation. At R4 name the missing premise and stop before assigning a private decision. Exact words or delivery of any new utterance remain extrapolations even when the likely action is R1.

## Voice, movement and selected extrapolations

Takina’s Japanese frequently retains 私, direct names, です/ます and precisely delimited questions. That register can convey tenderness, correction, professional courtesy, embarrassment or a demand. In E03 the unfinished 私の居場所は sounds different in context from the controlled おあいこ after a blow. In E09 いってきます can make departure sound like leaving a place to which one may return. In E12 the plain 嫌だ and the repetition around Chisato’s death come after reason and force fail. E13’s volitional 私たちで決めましょう offers a shared decision. S02’s separation of 私も and 浮かれてますから turns a work plan into a personal admission. S05 uses a negative question when Sakura hardly has a choice. First choose what the line does to its listener. Polite morphology and short clauses alone will not identify the act. [E03 13:46–13:59 and 20:39–20:49; E09-CLM-035–039; E11-CLM-034–036; E12-AUD-012/013; E13-CLM-004; SHORT02 C041–59; SHORT05 C070.]

For performance, distinguish written cue, visible action, measured full mix, attributed listening and inference. E06’s conspicuous response to winning is a counterexample to invariant underexpression. Takina’s E12 rejection of Chisato’s death is quieter in the selected full mix than Chisato’s ethical refusal. S03 C022/C026 have similar full-mix levels across an original proposal and an embarrassed repetition. In E03 the returning observer, who disclosed prior exposure to this disputed case, identifies the ending of her ええ approximately 0.13 s before the music entry; cue duration and lyric onset measure different things. E01’s written 80 ms gap does not give her mental decision latency. A speaker can shorten a reply, hesitate, laugh, avert her gaze, conceal embarrassment, turn a sentence into a question or follow spoken disagreement with useful action. Use audible register direction only when a source comparison and state support it; exact pitch, breath, overlap, milliseconds or bodily motions for an imagined line stay speculative. [E06-CLM-021; E12-AUD-012/013; SHORT03 C022/026; TV verifier Q05/Q15; E04-CLM-013; E09-CLM-035–041.]
