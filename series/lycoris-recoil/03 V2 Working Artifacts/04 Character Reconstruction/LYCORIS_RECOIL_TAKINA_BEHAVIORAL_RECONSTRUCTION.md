# Inoue Takina · anime-native behavioral reconstruction

Takina tests what works, who can act and what an action costs. What those questions serve changes across the series. In E01 she acts under pressure after Erika asks for help. Saori later bears risk without that opportunity (REV-E01-003). In E03 DA standing still measures belonging, but joint action and her demand for reciprocal settlement already complicate a prestige-only account. E06 separates Kurumi’s causal role from responsibility for Takina’s own choices. E08 gives her authority to manage a workplace she values. E09–11 show her using institutional access, then leaving when it impedes Chisato’s rescue. E12 brings care into direct ethical collision with Chisato. E13 distributes work by competence with Fuki and reopens a shared future. TV Q14 attributes the first visible island discharge to Takina. No global conversion in the use of force follows.

**Ordinary initiative.** Takina can learn a preference through a concrete activity (E04), enjoy a competitive game (E06), plan an imperfect outing (E09) and originate a commercial idea (S01). In S02 the trip is her own pleasure. In S03 she starts the menu problem, offers a solution that friends interpret differently, and retains the veto on cost. In S05 she privately tries a red uniform she publicly polices and pressures a witness. Curiosity and embarrassment have direct situational support. A declared career ambition does not. In S06 her nearly literal disclosure succeeds as cosplay in the fans’ eyes. A precise answer can have an unintended social effect.

**Language, care and permissions.** Takina’s procedural language can calculate, comfort, refuse or negotiate. Her quieter S02 excitement and E12 insistence that Chisato live defeat the assumption that motive grows monotonically with loudness. E06’s game offers the converse. In S03 the proposal and its embarrassed repetition have similar full-mix levels (C022/C026). E05’s heart question, E12’s rescue and S05’s treatment of Sakura put hard limits on the idea that her care always respects the recipient’s preferred boundary. Strong attachment does not eliminate disagreement; the café remains a place where a cost veto can stand.

**Generative tests.** R1 — in an ordinary menu decision, ask for costs and decide whether the offer serves the café (SHORT03-CLM-003–006). R2 — an E03 and an E11 offer of the old post should yield different decisions when the latter obstructs a rescue. R2 — after a dispute, expect continued work more confidently than agreement. R3 — a stranger receiving unwelcome personal attention may respond differently from Chisato or Sakura. R4 — exact thoughts behind the private uniform experiment and future romantic arrangements exceed the A1 record.
