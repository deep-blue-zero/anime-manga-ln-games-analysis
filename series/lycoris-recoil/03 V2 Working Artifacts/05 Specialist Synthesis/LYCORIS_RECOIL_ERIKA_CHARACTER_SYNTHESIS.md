# Janome Erika

Erika is alive when she hears that someone intends to avenge her. She objects to the word. Takina is the person whose intervention broke the hostage crisis. Against Hibana's reminder that Takina's gunfire nearly killed her, Erika can put the fact that she survived and the equally uncomfortable fact that she was the one captured. The [owner hears Erika](../07%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/TV%20Audiovisual%20Reconciliation/OWNER_LISTENING_RETURN_20261123.md#speaker-and-wording-dispositions) refer to herself as あたし the first time she speaks over Hibana in this exchange (E03 C0339). When Hibana brings up the arms dealer who was supposed to be taken alive, Erika has to concede the point. The two girls have barely been on screen together since the rescue. Erika has had to live among people who tell the story at her rescuer's expense. She can object to that story sooner than she can talk to Takina about it. [E03 C0333–0342, 16:17.17–16:44.90.](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/Anime/LYCORIS_RECOIL_E03_DEEP_READING.md)

## The person in the line of fire

Erika belongs to Fuki's team of Lycoris, the girls Direct Attack deploys in secret. During a failed operation an arms dealer holds her on the floor and begins counting down. The picture at 02:39.930 puts Erika on her knees among standing legs. Her words reach the others from that position. *Go ahead and shoot*, she calls. Hibana appeals to Fuki. Fuki has already asked headquarters for permission to fire. The connection failed before a fresh decision could reach her, and the order she has is to wait. Takina opens fire. Fuki asks whether she meant to kill Erika. Takina answers that Erika is alive. In a subsequent scene the commander cites both Takina's disobedience and her field leader's report in assigning her elsewhere. Erika has practically no part in the ensuing argument about her own survival. [E01 C0048–0059, 01:55.72–02:22.54, C0068–0083, 02:39.93–03:26.40, C0089–0095, 03:41.58–04:04.31.](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/Anime/LYCORIS_RECOIL_E01_DEEP_READING.md)

Her words change how the action ought to be read. Erika expressly asked the people outside her captor's grasp to intervene. Later in the same episode the civilian witness Saori gets no comparable say before Takina uses her exposure to draw out a threat. Erika's request matters precisely because its scope is finite. She does not select Takina, prescribe the means, decide what should happen to the dealers or release Fuki from the standby order. Her immediate willingness to face danger is poor evidence of any general indifference to her life. And the institutional decision costs *Takina* her place. Years of service, an exact age, a full account of Erika's capture and a private explanation of what she expected in those seconds are all outside this scene. [E01-C02, 02:39.93–03:21.97 and 16:11–18:47.](../07%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/TV%20Audiovisual%20Reconciliation/CLAIM_MATRIX.md)

## What Hibana can hear

At headquarters Erika actually does call to Takina. Other Lycoris are trading stories about the supposed ally-killer whose partners end up in hospital. Takina tells Chisato she will go to the training facility. Erika's *Wait, Takina!* comes as Takina is already leaving. The episode doesn't give Takina an answer to deliver to an apology that hasn't been spoken. Later, when Hibana invites Erika to see Fuki avenge her in a practice match, there is time to argue over a word. In Erika's account, the decisive fact about the hostage is that she got out alive. Her argument for Takina is strongest where her own experience counts most. She loses the easy answer once Hibana recalls the objective the team failed to meet. The broken-off concession, *Yes, but… I know that, but…*, leaves room for two claims to stand. Takina saved her. Someone also died whom the team was supposed to capture. [E03 C0110–0124, 06:15.73–06:44.09 and C0333–0342, 16:17.17–16:44.90.](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/Anime/LYCORIS_RECOIL_E03_DEEP_READING.md)

A bystander wonders aloud whether Takina's return might depend on winning the match. Erika gets to her feet. Afterward Hibana tells her Takina has already gone. Erika says the story that she would come back if she won was false. She also says she couldn't think what to say to her. Hibana tries the name on her once more, pretending to spot Takina. Erika responds at once. Hibana has been critic, confidante and tease in the space of an afternoon. With her, Erika can be angry at an insult, hopeful at a rumor, embarrassed at having hoped and eager to see the person who occasions all three reactions. Her difficulty speaking to Takina would be invisible to anyone who judged it solely by her vehemence in Takina's defense. E04–E10 offer no comparable private settlement between them. That interval gives Erika's later “I've been wanting to talk to you” its weight without establishing how often she tried. [E03 C0343–0349, 16:44.90–16:57.87, C0448–0455, 21:31.52–21:45, and E11 C0002–0007.](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/LYCORIS_RECOIL_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md)

## The place she offers to fill

When Takina returns to DA, Erika welcomes her during the briefing. A commander's voice through the monitor competes with the small opening Erika is trying to make. She is glad Takina is back. She starts to explain how long she has wanted to talk. Takina notices something that makes her ask whether Chisato has been called, then whether Mika too is out of contact. Her alarm has an identifiable object. Erika's address and Takina's distraction can both be genuine. The Japanese captions interleave the two speaking situations. Independent listening recorded in the AV reconciliation leaves a piece of the background briefing masked. The important distinction here is between the attempt Erika makes and the conversation the two actually manage to have. [E11 C0001–0012, 00:05.99–00:33.01.](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/Anime/LYCORIS_RECOIL_E11_DEEP_READING.md) [Q11, competing voices in the briefing.](../07%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/TV%20Audiovisual%20Reconciliation/VERIFIER_RETURN_20261122.md)

On the exterior stairs Takina tells Fuki that she is going to Chisato. Erika turns to the person whose permission counts. *Fuki, let her go.* With the familiar name she pairs a quite specific undertaking, 私がたきなのポジション埋めますから, “I'll fill Takina's position.” The offer comes **before** Sakura mocks her. Sakura says Erika's mistake got Takina fired. Erika declares it was all her fault, crosses the space to Takina and apologizes to her directly. The two are together at the railing in the retained pictures at 10:40.830 and 10:44.000. Hibana calls after Erika. Fuki then warns Takina about the cost of leaving and ultimately tells her to go. Erika has given Fuki a way to release a member of her team and Takina a reason to believe her place will be covered. The decision to leave belongs to Takina. Fuki is the field leader who actually tells her to go after warning that leaving could cost her the possibility of DA return. [E11 C0187–0208, 10:15.35–11:11.28, E11-CLM-011, E11-VIS-005.](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/Anime/LYCORIS_RECOIL_E11_DEEP_READING.md)

Erika may well have wanted both to repay Takina and to help two partners reach each other. The order of the exchange gives a firmer conclusion than choosing between motives. She volunteers before anyone presses her to admit a debt. When pressed, her *all my fault* goes beyond anything she could establish about how headquarters reached its decision. It nevertheless tells Takina something important about how *Erika* has lived with that decision. 「あのときはホントにごめんなさい」 addresses what happened “back then.” The offer about the next few minutes makes that difficult sentence possible in a way the unhurried conversation she wanted in E03 never was. The open question is whether having a useful act to offer generally helps her speak. One especially well-observed instance cannot answer it.

The next episode shows what she meant by taking someone's place. When surviving members of Alpha emerge, Erika credits Takina with helping them again. Sakura disputes the way she puts it. Fuki assigns Erika **and Hibana** to lead the Thirds in taking other teams' wounded down. Erika wonders quietly whether someone has managed to meet her partner, a natural reference to Takina and Chisato and an admission that she doesn't know. When Hibana asks what she said, Erika lets the subject drop. Only after Hibana says this is the last of the evacuation group does Erika tell her, *I have to take Takina's place.* Hibana accepts it. Erika rejoins the fighting and calls out Takina's name when Takina comes to their aid. Someone can earnestly undertake another girl's work and still need her help. Her own use of *partner* leaves Takina's relationship with Chisato intact. [E12 C0239–0250, 12:37.49–13:04.60, C0281–0289, 14:16.84–14:38.57, C0310–0330, 15:20.61–15:55.14, E12-CLM-032/033.](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/Anime/LYCORIS_RECOIL_E12_DEEP_READING.md)

## What finally passes between them

Takina is the one who says thank you. The edit returns briefly to Erika's earlier offer on the stairs and makes the object of Takina's gratitude intelligible. Erika protests that she herself owed the thanks. Sakura intervenes once more, this time to explain the matter to Takina. In Sakura's reckoning Erika could stay because Takina was thrown out in her place. Takina's *Oh, that's what you mean* is a measure of how far apart the two girls' understandings had been. Erika identifies a particular omission of her own. She should have spoken properly to the commander. Even now she says this to Takina. The episode supplies neither a belated interview with Kusunoki nor an administrative reversal. Sakura's explanation tells us how a peer apportions the cost. No published DA rule says that one girl had to be dismissed in order for the other to stay. [E12 C0442–0455, 20:59.95–21:33.15, E12-CLM-043/044.](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/Anime/LYCORIS_RECOIL_E12_DEEP_READING.md)

Takina replies, in effect, *You really are awful.* Erika's answer repeats the words she gave Hibana in E03, 「そうだけど…そうなんだけど！」. In the first scene Erika was defending an absent Takina while conceding a serious objection to the rescue. Now Takina is right beside her, looking back at her, and the judgment Erika partly accepts is about herself. She can let the girl she is indebted to say something quite unflattering, answer back, and remain there. Their conversation has room for a joke at her expense alongside a real failure which neither girl has to erase for the other. She still trails off. This time the girl who acted when she was held at the warehouse is there to hear the unfinished answer. [E03 C0341–0342, 16:39.31–16:44.90.](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/Anime/LYCORIS_RECOIL_E03_DEEP_READING.md) [E12 C0453–0455, 21:23.51–21:33.15, retained stills #001258–001260.](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/Anime/LYCORIS_RECOIL_E12_DEEP_READING.md)

Erika's concern can extend to a girl who has done little to spare her embarrassment. When Sakura is injured, Takina asks Erika to keep pressure on the wound. Erika answers, asks if they still have gauze, and tells Sakura to look at her. Fuki's proposal to go up despite the evacuation order makes Erika challenge her about the wounded girl. Yet when Takina asks leave to go alone, Erika immediately offers, *Then I'll go too!* Fuki refuses. Takina and Fuki work out who can reach Chisato and who can save Sakura. The offer does not place Erika alongside Takina for the ascent. Nor does it cancel the immediacy with which she had just attended to Sakura. She wants more than one outcome and belongs to a team whose leader can tell her where she is needed. Sakura in pain gets a direct, practical invitation to look at Erika, without an apology or a reconciliation of their own having to come first. [E13 C0006–0019, 00:12.16–00:40.90, C0051–0062, 02:28.67–02:54.99, C0072–0081, 03:18.05–03:51.09.](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/Anime/LYCORIS_RECOIL_E13_DEEP_READING.md)

## Wanting to stay

At the café in *Friends are thieves of time* Short 06, Erika offers the proverb about gossip lasting seventy-five days. A film and its fans have put the Lycoris uniform in the public eye. Fuki expects the film to attract little notice. Erika's remark offers reassurance on a different basis, the likelihood that attention will pass. Neither of them knows the size or persistence of the audience. Later Fuki says Sakura is away sick. Sakura herself gets the line about wanting parfait. Erika laughs and says how unfortunate it is, and Chisato immediately doubts her sincerity. The sequence permits delight at the company Erika actually has, amusement at the situation and pleasure at Sakura's absence. Chisato's teasing does not decide which of them moved her. [Short 06 C0059–0067, 02:35.84–02:50.06.](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/Anime/LYCORIS_RECOIL_SHORT06_DEEP_READING.md)

Her wish a few moments later is considerably clearer. Fuki says they are going home. Erika grips the chair. When Fuki tells her to let go, Erika asks, 「お泊りは！」, *What about the sleepover?* She scarcely needs a verb. This time she is asking a superior for something she wants for herself, with no injury to avenge, debt to repay or duty to a teammate. Fuki still says they are going home. In the later exterior sequence Erika passes Fuki in the direction of Mika's vehicle and disappears behind it. That movement does not settle whether she got aboard, spent the night at the café or traveled to Hawaii. The movement supplies independent evidence of Erika's route across that part of the scene. The resistance establishing her preference occurs earlier, beside the chair. The portrait would be markedly poorer if all her efforts to prolong someone's company had to count as acts of restitution. [Short 06 C0074–0079, 03:08.25–03:19.76, still #000184 at 03:12.901.](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/Anime/LYCORIS_RECOIL_SHORT06_DEEP_READING.md) [Exterior movement at 03:39.55–03:42.55, SR06 and SX02.](../07%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/Shorts%20Audiovisual%20Reconciliation/COVERAGE_AND_OPEN_ITEMS.md)

## Three ways other tellers put her among people

Abe Kanari gives the waiting its own comedy in *Recollect*. In volume 1, Erika wants both to thank Takina and to apologize. Going to the café without authorization risks making further trouble. Hibana suggests a letter. They lack the address, and now Erika can already imagine the ordeal of waiting for a reply. She settles on speaking in person when the chance comes and expressly denies Hibana's suggestion that she's in love. The reader then goes where Erika cannot, to a café game in which Takina and Chisato enlist Kurumi to adjudicate a rule. Mizuki comments on how comfortable the girls have become together. Mika asks her to ease up on the teasing. Erika has to work out how to approach someone whose current life the comic can show us and she cannot see. These are Abe's reasons for this postponement. They make sense of the broad difficulty the anime depicts without supplying a lost TV conversation. [B1, *Recollect* V01, chapter 5, OPF S55–66, especially S59–66.](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/Manga/LYCORIS_RECOIL_RECOLLECT_V01_DEEP_READING.md)

In Abe's second volume Fuki reports a fourth victim. Erika worries about Takina, outside the protection of headquarters. Hibana points out the constraints on their own movements. A phone call connects Mika to someone the reader eventually recognizes as Fuki. He says Kusunoki already gave him some information, thanks his caller and invites her to eat sweets. The exact substance of Fuki's message stays off the page. When Erika sees Fuki's flushed face later she asks her if she has a cold. The gap between the reader who has seen both ends of the call and the girl who has seen her colleague's face makes her question funny and quite reasonable. She pays attention even when she lacks the knowledge to explain what she sees. [B1, *Recollect* V02, chapter 11, OPF S37–48.](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/Manga/LYCORIS_RECOIL_RECOLLECT_V02_DEEP_READING.md)

Asaura lets her be socially inconvenient in *Gluttony days*. Fuki has gone to real trouble to cook Sakura monjayaki, assuming it will be a first taste. Sakura had already eaten it at a restaurant the previous day. As the others try to spare Fuki's feelings, Erika nearly gives the game away. Hibana manages the awkward encounter and subsequently makes her own apology. The story gives unusual access to Fuki's discomfort. That access cannot tell us whether Erika resented the attempt at discretion or simply hadn't understood it. Her contribution here has none of the flattering clarity of a timely apology on a staircase. One can care about the participants and still be the person about to say the least helpful thing in the room. [A2, *Gluttony days*, Japanese spine 12 ¶477–498, 549–574, 607–685.](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/Prose/LYCORIS_RECOIL_GLUTTONY_DAYS_DEEP_READING.md)

In 葉来緑's *しっぽのきもち* in *React*, Chisato helps Erika make an actual visit in disguise. Takina offers the unlikely-looking squirrel dessert. Kurumi explains what a squirrel's tail can do to distract a predator and that it won't grow back. Takina objects to losing something irreplaceable. Erika enjoys the dessert and manages to thank Takina without identifying herself. The contributor's note identifies the visitor for the reader. Takina's recognition remains open, as does how much of the thanks she understands. Here gratitude can reach its recipient under the cover of an ordinary customer transaction. In Abe's chapter, waiting preserves Erika's hope of speaking openly. The different authors give the same difficulty two different endings. Neither supplies an intervening event in the anime. [C1, 葉来緑, *React*, OPF S17–30 and contributor note S31.](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/Manga/Anthologies/LYCORIS_RECOIL_REACT_CONTRIBUTOR_READINGS.md)

## Where the portrait holds

With Hibana present and Takina absent, Erika can name an injustice and acknowledge the weakness in her own argument. With Takina in front of her, the approach is harder until there is something concrete she can do. With Fuki in charge, she can propose, protest and even hang on to the furniture while her superior decides. With Sakura hurt, she can turn directly to the need in front of her. These differences support a limited expectation. If someone describes Takina's rescue as harm to Erika, Erika has strong grounds to correct the account. If the task Takina must leave can be taken up by a teammate, there is good reason to expect Erika to consider offering. Where someone else holds the decision, a proposal can still cost her an answer she dislikes. A private conversation without a practical opening, an unfamiliar civilian's claim on her or a conflict between DA policy and her own future has much less evidence behind it. A later scene in which Erika chooses such a course for reasons she gives herself would change the portrait more than another affectionate tease from a peer.
