# Chisato and Takina · Learning what the other can choose

This synthesis follows Chisato Nishikigi and Takina Inoue in both directions across the thirteen TV episodes and six *Friends are thieves of time* shorts. At each stage it asks who initiates, who discloses, who withholds, who decides and who cares for whom, and how each of those directions changes. The project’s frozen account of the anime alone, called CP5, supplies the comparison. The Japanese novels and the credited comics can change who acts, consents or knows the answer. Their separate realizations specify, extend or pressure what the anime establishes about partnership, risk, intimacy and the choices each person makes possible for the other.

## Who they are and how the evidence is read

Chisato is seventeen and Takina sixteen when they meet; Chisato notes that she is one year the elder (E01 C0158–160). Both are Lycoris, girls taken in as children by DA, the clandestine agency that trains them and sends them out in schoolgirl uniforms (E03 C0266–267). Chisato wears the red of a First. She works at Café LycoReco, a DA branch run by Mika, her former instructor and the nearest thing she has to a father, and lives alone in one of four safehouses she keeps (E06 C0028, C0044; E08 C0401–402). She lives on an artificial heart received as a child through the Alan Institute and its agent Shinji Yoshimatsu, fires only nonlethal rounds and will not kill. Takina wears the navy of a Second. She was transferred from Kyoto the previous year (E01 C0157) and is sent from DA headquarters to the café after firing on arms dealers who held a fellow Lycoris, Erika, hostage. The people around them recur below: Mika; Kurumi, the hacker Walnut, who fakes her death and then lives at the café on condition that she helps with its work (E02 C0430–431); Mizuki, who works there; Fuki, a First at headquarters who was once Chisato’s partner and roommate, and who at the transfer was Takina’s partner and, until the day before, her roommate (E01 C0188–189); Sakura, Fuki’s new partner; Commander Kusunoki; the terrorist Majima; Yoshimatsu; and Himegama, an agent working for him. In a short released after the series, whose place in the TV chronology is not fixed, Fuki asks whether the two now live together; Takina says she is looking for a room, and Chisato says she can stay for good (Short 05 C027–029).

The TV episodes and shorts (A1) govern. The three novels (A2) are by Asaura, credited with the anime’s story concept, and are creator-proximate prose. Takina focalizes most of them. Chisato’s narrated thought appears only in a few passages of *Ordinary days* (10.178–182, 10.549–553, 10.584–591, 14.53–65, 14.176–183) and two short pieces of *Gluttony days*, and nowhere in *Recovery days*. Abe Kanari’s two *Recollect* manga volumes (B1) and the multi-author comic anthologies *Reload*, *Reload 02*, *Reload 03*, *Repeat*, *Repeat 02*, *Repeat 03* and *React* (C1) are separate realizations, each anthology comic its own contributor’s. The *Heroine Archive* fanbook (P_PARATEXT) contributes attributed testimony by the director, writers, designers and actors. Supplementary findings are retrospective. They carry no predictive credit and never silently rewrite A1, so each paragraph that bears on an A1 claim states its comparative ruling: SPECIFY (narrows an A1 claim), EXTEND (adds cases of the same kind), PRESSURE (tests or limits it), SOURCE_SPECIFIC (holds only in that source) or CARICATURE (deliberate comic exaggeration). ## A partnership gives each of them a way to disagree

Mika assigns Chisato Nishikigi and Takina Inoue to work together in E01. Most of their circumstances at first are involuntary. Takina has lost her position at DA, and Chisato is asked to absorb someone she did not choose. Chisato makes the assignment an occasion to claim a new relationship. She had watched the warehouse shooting that cost Takina her post from an outside stair (C0067, C0075, #000175), and her first evaluative words to Takina are praise for it, この前のアレ すごかったねえ, before she asks about the bruise (C0164–165). Her immediate よろしく 相棒！, taking up Mika’s word for the assignment (C0151, C0153), her interest in Takina’s age and injury, and her request that Takina drop the honorific (“さん”は いらないからね, C0161–162) set a relationship pace that Takina does not adopt. Takina goes on saying 千束さん through E02 and most of E03 (E01 C0192, C0197; E02 C0210). Takina can decline that pace and still answer the assignment. Her measured speech at the meeting remains constrained compared with Chisato’s. The exchange tells us who sets the terms of this greeting. No signal measures who will care more by the end of the series. The gap becomes a durable formal resource, because a later use of plain speech, conspicuous laughter or a short forceful line can then mean a changed situation, changed familiarity, or both. On the phone to Fuki, in Takina’s hearing, Chisato objects to the blow Fuki gave Takina and tells her to think for herself instead of repeating 司令 (C0168, C0171). An early stated value in the partnership sides with self-directed judgment against command. See E01-CLM-004 and REL-CHI-TAK-E01-001 at 06:05–07:01.

Early photographs and café greetings give the two a visible place together before they share an account of what they are doing there. Chisato asks about Takina’s decision to fire when Erika was captive. She limits the blame threat, calls the rescue of a comrade admirable (仲間を救った かっこいいって, C0347) and offers her help getting back to DA (C0348). Takina says やります with pronounced activation after Chisato links recovery of the guns to a possible return. Takina’s newly visible participation belongs to recruitment into café service and pursuit of DA. Her own comments keep their distance. Asked if she has any questions after Chisato asks for her help, she answers あり過ぎますね (C0303–306), and to the client Saori she says that Chisato makes her uneasy (C0394). The director, Adachi Shingo, says he built the episode so that viewers would doubt through Takina’s view whether Chisato is really excellent before seeing the counter-evidence (*Heroine Archive* S073/p071); Takina’s two remarks give that doubt its words. The terms can change later because the show leaves room for both. It gives Chisato an attachment-generating practice before Takina begins to return Chisato’s attention in particular ways. Chisato can like having Takina around while making it easier for Takina to leave. E01-CLM-006/012 and REL-CHI-TAK-E01-003/006.

Chisato acquires a colleague who takes the consequences of being excellent seriously. Takina acquires one who asks who should bear those consequences. The danger involving Saori exposes the asymmetry. Takina’s estimate of what she can achieve is better evidence about her own shot than about Saori’s freedom to assume risk. Chisato disputes use of Saori as bait. When the fight comes, she asks Takina to shoot the drone that is watching them (C0456–458), then sends her to Saori (たきな 沙保里さんを！, C0478) while she takes the attackers herself. Erika, in the earlier hostage scene, had called on the team to fire regardless of her position (構わず撃って, C0068). The cases differ in the participation of the person at risk. Neither validates a general justification. Takina names the material basis of Chisato’s rule as she sees it, 非殺傷弾 (C0477), then asks whether “life matters” covers enemies too, and Chisato says that it does (C0483–485). The ally who challenges Takina also depends on her precision, and Takina can follow the division of labor while withholding a moral agreement. E01-CLM-002/008/009, REL-CHI-TAK-E01-004/005 and the later REV-E01-003 qualification.

## Trust precedes ethical agreement

On the train Chisato persists after Takina initially refuses food with 結構です (C0098). Chisato offers the bite with あ～ん. Takina echoes あ～ん, accepts the food and says おいしいです. What the moment gives Takina is a specific social pleasure she can allow herself to join while speaking in her existing register. The shot also gives Chisato a warning for later scenes. An eventual acceptance makes that bite permissible. It does not authorize every subsequent attempt to make Takina comfortable. The close choreography is one reason the scene can be read as courtship-like. Its evidentiary value is the actual turn from refusal to acceptance. REL-CHI-TAK-E02-001 at 05:15–06:11.

Takina’s own form of trust is easiest to see when there is an observable job to share. Chisato identifies a precision task she does not trust herself to do and gives it to Takina. Takina executes it. When the job later requires leaving an injured attacker behind, Chisato stays to treat the person and Takina urges withdrawal. The movement separates Chisato, treating the attacker, from Takina and the client on the extraction route. Initial acceptance on the train and consent to the precision assignment coexist with this separation. No one speech or genre signal settles all three. When defeat appears to have followed, Takina apologizes and Chisato takes blame away from her. Chisato separates Takina’s sense of failure from the exact causal contribution of Takina’s own choices. Chisato can offer that relationship repair even when she will not change her own conduct. The client’s death was staged, and neither girl had been told. Mizuki explains that Chisato is a poor actor and that the adults wanted the pair’s natural reaction together (C0383–387). At this stage both partners stand on the same side of the adults’ secrets. E02-CLM-005–007, REL-CHI-TAK-E02-002–005, 11:13–11:29, 14:37–15:43, 16:29–17:39 and 19:41–20:15.

The café argument supplies their still competing grounds in speech. Takina invokes the duties and expectations of people trained and authorized to kill, and the costs that unilateral decisions impose on the team. Chisato gives priority to stopping an immediately preventable death and questions whether a designation as enemy is permanent. The relation survives the exchange and Mika intervenes. The shared table does not reconcile the argument. E02-CLM-007/011.

Chisato’s other immediate recruitment in E02 supplies a comparison for her manner with Takina. Chisato readily treats the recovered Kurumi as a companion in ordinary activities. Rapidly reducing provisional social distance belongs to her broader repertoire. Its repetition limits the claim that the E01 greeting alone individuates Takina’s eventual place in Chisato’s life. Subsequent appraisals of Takina’s particular presence do individuate it. The same early Chisato can extend company to more than one person without making any offer empty. Her first errand for Kurumi also serves Takina: find the buyer in Saori’s photograph, the lead on the stolen guns that Takina hopes will take her back to DA (C0433–434). E02-CLM-008.

## Chisato affirms a person whose institution still matters

At the beginning of E03 Takina declines an ordinary game and evaluates the café’s offer through whether it helps her get back to DA. Chisato makes DA access possible when a conversation with Kusunoki becomes available. At headquarters she admits to Fuki that she does not understand why Takina wants to be there (なんで ここがいいんだろうね, C0149). Chisato directly challenges the official account and asks that Takina be allowed back. Takina experiences the refusal after she has already tried to make her work count as a reason to restore her standing. In the earlier colleague encounter Sakura names herself as Fuki’s new partner, in the position Takina held. Chisato’s register turns harsher there than anywhere else in the early episodes, and most of it is spent on Takina’s behalf: 黙れ 小僧 to Sakura after Sakura tells Takina her seat is gone, and sharp calls to Kusunoki and Sakura when Takina’s request is refused (C0222–224, C0237–240). In between she objects when Sakura calls her, Chisato, “this” (C0228–229). Erika locates herself in the event that led to Takina’s removal and rejects the rumor that Takina acted without care. Sakura addresses Takina. Erika later speaks to her friend Hibana and nearby girls, after an earlier attempt to reach Takina. The episode lets viewers weigh Erika’s defense without giving Takina the benefit of having heard it. Those other relationships complicate any reading that Chisato is the first or only person prepared to recognize Takina’s care. E03-CLM-002/006–012, REL-CHI-TAK-E03-001/002, the replacement scene at 10:41.5–11:20.58, Erika’s 06:15–06:44 effort and her 16:17–16:44 account.

Takina later gives voice to the problem of where she belongs at the headquarters fountain, where Chisato guessed she would be (ここだと思った, C0264). Her last 千束さん in the series comes here, as she asks Chisato to understand that headquarters was her goal (C0272). She calls the outcome 理不尽 (14:47.03). Her sentence about her place stops at 私の居場所は… and ends もう ここにはない (C0277–278). Chisato changes the form of her response. She argues that Takina’s desire to save Erika has moral significance independent of how the institution scores her performance. Chisato says she is glad that she met Takina. The embrace takes place inside that conversation, with the fountain behind them (#000795). Chisato provides an opportunity to spend time with the whole shop and leaves a future return to DA available (C0315–320). The option matters more because Chisato has already pursued it with Takina. Other characters’ joking words about the hug and 青春 are evidence of the social availability of an intimate reading. Chisato owns the personal affirmation. Takina does not give a reciprocal declaration in the embrace or authorize a group description of their bond. Adachi says he agonized over this fountain scene. Chisato had to prompt Takina’s change without preaching, because her stance is “I think this, but you may do as you like” and never “you should”; Takina had to choose by her own will, with Chisato’s words one material for her judgment. The more Chisato talked, the less like herself she became, until, he jokes, she suddenly made “that” move (*Heroine Archive* S077/p075). He does not name the move. Takina’s actor, Wakayama Shion, values that Chisato is not preachy here (S106/p104), and Asaura names the fountain as the scene that struck him (S138/p136). These describe intention and reception; the scene itself supplies the offer. Chisato then names her own motive for the mock battle that follows: she wants to thrash the people who said cruel things to Takina (C0325–326). E03-CLM-009–011 and REL-CHI-TAK-E03-003 at 13:21–15:52, C0272–326.

Takina subsequently chooses two different activities. The mock battle gives her a way to reenter a contest after replaying Chisato’s words. Before she arrives, it is Fuki who says Takina is not someone who fights outside a mission (C0353). Chisato answers that Fuki cannot read people, having already said she is saving “the ace” (C0352–355). Takina’s firing decision relies on Chisato’s ability to dodge. An interpretation in which Takina’s trust has solved the E01 risk problem misses exactly what the combat scene repeats. Takina still makes someone else’s safety part of a decision she believes her understanding of exceptional competence can justify. Chisato’s capacity to evade supplies a reason for the result, and success gives no formal assent to future uses of that judgment. On the train home Takina calls Chisato 非常識 and uses the bare name for the first time: 非常識な人ですよ 千束は (C0462, 22:08.64). No later 千束さん from Takina occurs anywhere in the anime. When Chisato says the fight felt good, Takina answers ええ (C0463–464), her first acknowledged pleasure in a fight they shared. The game invitation どうする～？ (C0469) comes after the closing music begins and receives no spoken answer; the next picture shows Takina at the café game she had declined. The second movement is Takina’s choice about what to do with her time. The structure makes Takina’s return to the group legible without dissolving her other connections. E03-CLM-013–016 and REL-CHI-TAK-E03-004/005.

## Choosing for oneself includes choosing to ask

In E04, Takina first seeks functional characteristics or Chisato’s guidance in clothes shopping. She delegates a choice and then makes Chisato’s behavior part of her own evidence by asking. Chisato still initiates every outing and purchase in the episode. What is new is Takina’s sustained inquiry within one conversation. She calls Chisato 謎だらけ (C0261) and questions her directly about leaving DA, nonkilling and the unknown benefactor. When Chisato twice turns a question back as a tease, Takina says ちゃかすなら もういいです, and Chisato answers seriously at once (C0245–249). It is the first time Takina sets a condition on Chisato’s play, and Chisato complies. That sustained inquiry reverses the initiative of their first meeting. Chisato supplies autobiographical information about her desire to thank the person who gave her the pendant. Asked what talent the pendant marks, she does not say, and Takina is unsure she has any talent of her own (C0281–286). After the garden-eel play Takina tells Chisato she ought not hide それ, then says めっちゃ かわいいですよ. The sentence keeps an informal intensifier alongside a polite ending. The noun is unstated. In the E04 images the pendant is visible outside Chisato’s shirt, and Chisato raises a hand at her neck and chest during her response. The E13 exchange C0431–439 links Chisato’s hesitation about giving that keepsake up to the same appraisal and names Takina as its source. The pendant is a strong reading. The earlier performance gives the exchange a playful setting, but the picture and later dialogue make a direct compliment to Chisato’s appearance too specific. We can trace Takina’s willingness to appraise something personal to Chisato without assigning a physical confession to the line. Her open laughter at the episode’s end belongs to a different context. E04-CLM-004–006/009/013, REL-CHI-TAK-E04-001/003–005 and TV Q16.

The station emergency tests a different direction of influence. Takina moves to take responsibility as if her training continues to assign a role when she is out of uniform. Chisato physically interrupts and states a boundary for off-duty behavior. Takina has a demonstrated desire to act, and Chisato sets the terms of whether it can proceed. Their relationship here expands Takina’s possibilities by letting her put a learned obligation down. It also shows that Chisato herself will override another person’s immediate initiative. Chisato’s rhetoric of self-authorship operates within her own judgments about role, law and danger. E04-CLM-008 and REL-CHI-TAK-E04-002 at 11:51–12:00 and 18:42–18:50.

Chisato discloses her prosthetic heart in E05, and the first person she tells in Takina’s hearing is a client; Takina’s えっ？ shows the news is new to her (C0064–068). When Takina starts to ask about it, Chisato cuts her off with たきな 行くよ～！, and Mika tells Mizuki to leave it to Chisato (C0076–083). The partner learns as a bystander and has her question deferred. Takina wants direct verification. In the public occurrence Chisato objects. In the later private moment Takina recalls the condition, leans in and Chisato permits it. Takina confirms by listening that there is no heartbeat. Takina’s progression from wanting an answer to taking account of the other person’s conditions shows relational learning with a recognizable goal held constant. The disclosure, the recipient, the place and the permission stay specific, which makes a firmer account than any inference from a picture of proximity. Adachi says he did not want the heart read tragically, kept it as part of Chisato’s individuality, and wanted the episode to end on the impression of Takina listening to the sound of Chisato’s chest (胸の音) (*Heroine Archive* S080–081/p078–079). E05-CLM-004/010/011 and REL-CHI-TAK-E05-001/004 at 04:21–05:08, 10:50–11:06 and 22:21–22:35.

Takina also reads Chisato’s phrase about everything being false after the assignment reveals a false client premise. Earlier the client had praised Chisato as a guide, and Chisato had tried the praise out in Takina’s hearing as an answer to the question of her talent, 才能あるかも～ (C0185–186). She holds on to a particular true proposition about how Chisato behaved as a guide. Chisato’s ありがとう acknowledges a repair that Takina made possible by reasoning narrowly. This is part of why early discipline remains recognizable in a relationship that grows warmer. Takina need not replace precision with a new, expansive language of reassurance. She finds a precision that the other person can use, although an audience with a different need might experience the same method as correction rather than help. Earlier in the same assignment Takina chose to engage Jin, the veteran hired gunman sent after the client, and assigned evacuation to Chisato. Both in danger and in a difficult conversation Takina, introduced as the lesser social initiator, can now author the next step. E05-CLM-012/020 and REL-CHI-TAK-E05-002/003 at 13:22–16:52 and 21:58–22:10.

## A shared household puts an argument into the form of a game

In E06 Takina proposes remaining together after Lycoris working alone have been attacked, with alternating sleep and 24-hour proximity (C0021–027). The same opening has her relaying a message from Dr. Yamagishi, who gives Chisato her check-ups, that Chisato skipped this month’s (C0017–020), and Chisato’s first act as host at the safehouse is to ask after Takina’s injured leg (C0039–041). Takina then offers a fair allocation of chores. Chisato experiences the schedule as boring, つまんな～い, and Takina repeats the word back, つ… つまらない？ (C0046–048). Anzai Chika, who voices Chisato, hears a new, slightly Chisato-like nuance in that reply. Wakayama says that, watching Anzai’s acting up close, she wanted to try it, that from there Takina grew more Chisato-like, and that Adachi told her どんどん千束に似てくるね; she thinks living together influenced Takina (*Heroine Archive* S106/p104 and S109/p107). Anzai’s remark concerns that one line, and Adachi’s is reported by the actor. Takina changes the arrangement into a game (C0049) and eventually changes how the game starts when she understands why Chisato wins. This preserves Takina’s attention to causal mechanics and shows her taking responsibility for what a household feels like. The protection Takina organized also meets its limit. When Chisato goes out on a delivery, Takina offers to come, and Chisato twice declines and says she is looking forward to Takina’s dinner (C0281–293). Majima ambushes her on that trip. Chisato’s wish to keep up 同棲 is expressed after a working routine, crisis and rescue have put shared life into practice, and its stated reason is that two together are safer (C0480). Takina attaches a real condition to staying, makes continuation depend on Chisato winning, and wins herself. Chisato may be sincere about enjoyment, safety or both. They can enjoy living together for a time, disagree about extending it and remain attached. E06-CLM-001/004–006/020–023, REL-CHI-TAK-E06-001/002/004 and REV-E06-001, with the final proposition at 22:08–22:36.

Takina’s loud delight at winning is no scale of feeling: she is as loud under danger, and in E12 her refusal to let Chisato die is comparatively quiet. E06-CLM-020/021 and E12-AUD-013.

The rescue repeats the distinction between independence and invulnerability. Kurumi’s information makes it possible for Takina to intervene. Chisato, earlier prepared to manage intrusions through separate refuges and familiarity with risk, is still vulnerable and needs help. Takina immediately mobilizes. The picture and written dialogue support a specific rescue and a discriminating use of her precision in that scene. Their mutual capacity to save one another cannot become a promise that they will agree how someone should be saved. Afterward the apology to Takina is Kurumi’s, not Chisato’s (C0450, #001107), and Chisato laughs quietly when Takina declines to blame Kurumi (C0451–452). Earlier, asked what Lilybell, DA’s male counterpart, actually does, Chisato had turned the question into a tease about boys, and Takina had refused the turn (C0206–209). Deflection by teasing is becoming a pattern Takina can name. E06-CLM-003/017–020, REL-CHI-TAK-E06-003 at 18:55–20:12 and TV Q06. The later dressing supports a bounded injury. Side attribution and unshown care or transit remain uncertain.

E07 continues the test in a different idiom. Takina notices that something is off with Chisato and says so, then teases her eyesight with a callback to E06 (C0068–069, C0081). Told the café cannot survive without its First, Takina offers to be the one who goes back; Chisato says that would be lonely and clings, but the stated option remains available (C0093–094). Takina later shares the blame for reading Mika’s message, then leaves Chisato room to speak privately with Yoshimatsu (C0340, C0346–347), and afterwards ordinary café work gives her a reason to send Chisato to change. Chisato’s 愛の形は様々なんだよ gives an explicit interpretation of love in relation to Mika and Yoshimatsu that Takina is invited to consider. Its acceptance of different forms of attachment makes a queer reading of the story well grounded. Chisato is speaking about them in that exchange. The line allows variety without declaring either member of the active dyad’s status. Work, physical closeness, privacy and leaving the room can all be ways of respecting a relation. In the same episode they remain divided about whether killing wrongdoers can be justified. REL-CHI-TAK-E07-001–005 at 05:24–05:42, 14:54–14:56, 16:16–16:18 and 20:44–21:48.


## The new object of competence

E08 tests whether Chisato can receive limits from Takina. The café deficit makes choices about labor and ammunition consequential. Takina accepts an obligation to preserve the café and Chisato visibly revises some expenditure under that constraint. Chisato seeks Takina’s approval when the change works, and gets it (どうよ～ / よくできました, C0099). In the other direction Chisato spares Takina’s first menu idea: she starts an honest verdict on the parfait, is shushed by Mizuki and offers いいんじゃないかな？ (C0110–114). At the Halloween outreach Chisato asks publicly for a present from Takina too, and Takina rebuffs her with 子供ですか！ (C0190–191). In the evening Takina hands over a treat bag of the kind given out at the nursery and asks 欲しかったんでしょ？; inside is a pug charm in a red bandana (C0419–424, #001098). She gives her own reason for the hard work. This place is 大切 to Chisato (C0426–427), and Chisato thanks her (C0429). The gift refuses a childish request in public and fulfils it in private. Just before it, on the bench, Chisato has said that Yoshimatsu has not come since the spring day Takina arrived (C0416–418). Anzai picks that line as rather weak-spirited for Chisato and thinks it would not have come out unless the listener were Takina; Wakayama says that for one moment their positions reversed (*Heroine Archive* S106/p104). The gift is discretionary. It follows a sustained choice to look after a setting in which Chisato can choose how to be useful and how to spend time. E01 showed Chisato noticing a concrete objective Takina already owned. E08 returns that move with the café as the object, and Chisato is the person whose judgment and pleasure set part of the criterion. Takina’s practical decisions still impose costs on coworkers. Cafeteria economics, professional accountability and a friend’s particular valued place are contributory reasons rather than simple rivals. E08-CLM-024–026, E08-AUD-012/013, REV-E08-002 and REL-CHI-TAK-E08-001/004.

Takina also makes a verification routine for contact (C0395–399). Chisato jokingly draws out what it would feel like to have someone come wherever she was (C0400), and turns a promised visit into 同棲？, which Takina dismisses with はいはい 仕事しましょ！ (C0403–405). That register can make a conditional safety arrangement into an intimate line while leaving the actual obligation precise. Earlier Takina had taken Yamagishi’s incoming call on Chisato’s own phone and booked her check-up over her protest, calling the crew’s health her job (C0215–222, #000640–642). On the walk home Chisato admits she fears injections because she cannot dodge them, and Takina laughs (C0432–440). The explanation about a foreign object in the body comes later, in the clinic, and is addressed to the nurse, who is Himegama in disguise, not to Takina (C0446–450). Takina monitors the visit. The protection protocol matters in E09 because nonresponse really brings Takina to look for her. She arrives calling Chisato’s name and turns on Himegama with お前… 何をした！ (E09 C0011–012). Repeated jokes alongside actual follow-through help the viewer distinguish care that is made legible from care merely assumed. Takina proposes eliminating the person who damaged the heart when the threat is known. Chisato rejects the reversal as useless to the future she has. After the prognosis Takina apologizes for making Chisato run and tells her not to (C0148, C0154). The urgency is successfully shared and the answer remains divided. Chisato gives her only stated reason for having kept her condition from Takina to Mizuki, in the car: Takina has become self-conscious about it, which is why she had not wanted to say (C0075–077). Adachi says he wanted Chisato’s bewilderment at Takina’s changed manner conveyed with as little expression and dialogue as possible (*Heroine Archive* S088–089/p086–087). REL-CHI-TAK-E08-002/003/005 and REL-CHI-TAK-E09-001, E09-CLM-001–007.

Chisato bargains for Takina’s reinstatement after learning how little time she is thought to have. This repeats the support she previously gave under highly altered costs. Takina has overheard Mika and Kurumi connect Majima with Chisato’s heart (C0272–275). She treats the offer as an opening to learn how to save Chisato. Wanting to return and wanting to remain present can both have causal force. Takina voices uncertainty instead of retrospectively explaining that institutional membership never mattered. She decides to explain the move herself. The old goal still supplies access and standing, while the object of the effort has changed. Chisato’s support cannot be reduced to a test of whether Takina will stay, given that she actually helps arrange the exit. In the opposite direction, Takina cannot be understood as leaving Chisato to pursue an undisturbed old ambition. E09-CLM-012–016/028–030/035 and REL-CHI-TAK-E09-002.

## When a missed objective still makes a good day

Takina designs their E09 day with a schedule. She opens it in the café with a formal ちょっと お話が, and Chisato’s puzzled 何ごっこ？ crosses with two え？ (C0307–311). Takina is wearing the summer outfit Chisato chose for her and has no other. Chisato starts choosing her a winter version, and Takina’s own schedule alarm cuts the coat purchase short (C0317–326). Snow is part of the hoped-for experience, and the individual activities do not simply work because she plans them. She asks whether Chisato is having fun in her familiar polite form. Chisato locates the pleasure in being with Takina (楽しいよ / たきなと いればさ). She answers the particular effort rather than assuring Takina that every part of the sequence came off as intended. The answer carries more specificity than Chisato’s early way of inviting the nearest person to play. When Takina sneezes, Chisato hands her own scarf over (C0346–350). The scene makes both kinds of contribution intelligible. Takina arranged a day they could actually spend together. Chisato recognizes the criterion under which the spent day succeeds and names the person who made it matter. Chisato can enjoy novelty and still articulate attachment directly. Takina can author the pleasure and still care whether a plan actually worked. E09-CLM-031–034/036–038 and REL-CHI-TAK-E09-003, C0339–340.

As the day ends Chisato guesses the DA news. Asked whether she is glad, Takina says she does not know (C0359–363) and calls things 理不尽 (C0366). Chisato’s answer is to accept what cannot be helped, give it everything, and call Takina’s plan a great success (C0367–371). A fist meets a fist (#001141–001142); whether Takina speaks the やったぜ under it is not established (C0374). When Takina starts to give the scarf back, Chisato calls it a 餞別 and tells her to keep it (C0377–378, #001150). Chisato’s farewell gift and Takina’s ありがとう and いってきます make departure answerable as an ordinary movement inside their relationship. Near this goodbye the unmarked forms coexist with Takina returning to DA. Growing informality need not mean that she accepted a new exclusive affiliation. Conversely, using her professional capacities there need not mean she took back the gratitude. Adachi wanted to convey that the 理不尽 Takina feels over the date and the parting differs from her E03 理不尽, which concerned only herself, and hoped the shared word would express her change; he leaves what the two feel to viewers (S088–089/p086–087). Maruyama Yūsuke, assistant director and the episode’s director, says Chisato was given halo-like backlight to mislead viewers into suspecting a final farewell, and that Takina’s slightly red nose, an ad-lib by chief animation director Yamamoto Yumiko and the only cut in the series where her nose is red, shows she had been holding back tears (S142/p140). The red nose is visible; its meaning is his. Snow begins only after Takina leaves (about 21:39), and Chisato has time to see alone the thing Takina tried to arrange before the closing song. That reading of the placement is formal interpretation. E09-CLM-039–041, REL-CHI-TAK-E09-004 and C0359–379.

## What attachment permits while they are apart

E10 keeps Chisato and Takina apart while each makes consequential choices. As Chisato closes the café, she tells the others to follow Takina’s example and return to their own paths, proposes destinations that suit Mizuki and Kurumi, and asks Kurumi alone to invite Takina someday (いつか たきなを誘ってあげて, C0030–049). Listing what Mika has given her, she counts meeting Takina among his gifts (たきなと出会えたのも, C0279). Neither gesture requires Takina to be in the room or leave DA. Takina in turn presses a detained client for a lead on Yoshimatsu, admitting that life outside was rather fun and that being fired again might not be bad (C0127–129). She seeks the lead despite the real risk that her restored position may again be lost. She tells Majima she has no interest in him, only in where Yoshimatsu is (C0226). In the closing minutes Chisato decides to go to Yoshimatsu because she cannot leave him to die and wants to ask him directly (C0431–434). The reassurance that Takina and Fuki will hold Enkūboku, the new tower DA is defending against Majima, is Mika’s, spoken to Chisato with his hand on her head, and Chisato answers うん！ (C0440–442, #001036). That exchange shows Mika’s confidence in his two former pupils and Chisato’s acceptance of it. It is not Chisato’s own statement of trust in Takina, and no line in the episode supplies one. Sakaki Kōsuke, who voices Mika, reads the “excellent students” line as a sign that by now Takina has become one of Mika’s own (*Heroine Archive* S117/p115). Each person makes relational evidence available to a different audience. Takina has no established access to every private acknowledgment Chisato makes, and Fuki retains her own responsibility at the tower. The episode supplies professional divergence and parallel action while their attachment remains operative without co-presence. E10-CLM-007/008/011/020/021 at 02:32–03:18, 06:59–07:39 and 14:20.71, with the audit recheck’s disposition of C0440–441 at 22:20.73–22:30.

## Who decides what rescue must cost

Institutional work supplies an opening and also supplies a limit. In E11 Takina’s first move on finding the café closed and Chisato’s phone left behind is to report it to Kusunoki. Asked who Yoshimatsu is, she tells the commander he is 千束の恩人, confirms Kusunoki’s guess that he is the heart donor, and adds that Majima knew Chisato was searching for him (C0069–073); ordered back, she complies (C0074–078). Chisato’s private history passes to DA through Takina before Takina leaves DA. Takina later has to act when DA cannot meet her purpose. Fuki warns that leaving the mission means she can never return, and Takina answers that she cannot save Chisato there (C0202–206). Fuki and Erika have roles in the departure. The practical permission, the cost of relinquishing a return and the earlier responsibility between Takina and Erika belong to their own relationships. Chisato did not need to solicit that cost or demand that Takina cut her connections. Takina arrives in danger before the pair can renew anything verbally. She has no spoken line in that closing fight; the jibe お前には用がない って言ったろ is Majima’s (C0430). In E12 their coordination resumes and Takina says 辞めてきました in answer to the question about DA. The reply, by sequence Chisato’s, is フッ バカだね～ (C0035–037). This offers strong evidence that their partnership survived physical and institutional separation. It says little about how Takina would prioritize the café and the institution when the threat, information and access differ. REL-CHI-TAK-E11-001/002, REL-CHI-TAK-E12-001 and E12-CLM-001/002/032–033/043.

Yoshimatsu supplies a solution to Chisato’s survival with a demand already built into it. He is willing to die and calls Chisato’s objection a refusal of her own worth. The line 狂ってる that follows his offer falls inside a close-up of Takina and remains unattributed (C0115). Takina fires, says that if Chisato cannot do it she will, and Chisato holds her back (C0119–125). Adachi says the scenario had Takina enter after the conversation, and that he had her overhear it at the storyboard stage to raise her hatred of Yoshimatsu (*Heroine Archive* S095/p093). Takina sees a causal alternative to letting Chisato die. When Yoshimatsu puts Takina in immediate danger, he makes Chisato’s concern for her a practical vulnerability. The report is placed over the exterior and the following view shows Chisato aiming. The edit then shows Yoshimatsu wounded. A protective shot from Chisato in response to Takina’s danger is strongly supported. Its precise damage, intended medical outcome and load remain open. Hanging from the beam edge after Himegama’s attack, Takina sends Chisato on with 行って！, and Chisato answers うん！ (C0153–154, #000566). In the whole tower sequence it is the one point at which Takina yields to Chisato’s priority of her own accord. Chisato goes to the wounded Yoshimatsu and still treats him as someone with a life he cannot oblige her to take. She finds the bullet has passed through; his complaint that this will not kill him is his own line (C0156–157), and her 命を粗末にするヤツは嫌いだ answers it (C0159). This scene makes three incompatible views of care concrete. Yoshimatsu values a realization and is willing to die for it. Takina wants a particular person to live. Chisato wants Takina to live and will not redefine survival as a compelled violation of her own commitments. E12-CLM-005–018, REL-CHI-TAK-E12-002 and TV Q17.

To Yoshimatsu, at 09:14, Chisato says she has many things more important than the world, and that the time he gave her let her notice them (C0164–166). She then sends him away (C0178). The later sequence at 10:05–10:10 shows Chisato restraining Takina as she asks to be released and cries out that the heart is getting away (C0182–185). The high activation of that scream belongs to the disappearing rescue opportunity. Chisato tells her that to live by killing Yoshimatsu would no longer be herself (C0186). The next verbal exchange tests a different question from who can secure a heart. Takina’s unmistakably personal 嫌だ 千束が死ぬのは嫌だ can be delivered comparatively quietly. Chisato answers ありがとう, takes the care seriously and keeps her refusal of the means. Then, to Takina, she says that because Yoshimatsu kept her alive she met Takina too, たきなにも (C0193, 10:36). Both the particular name and the additive particle matter. The evidence supports a unique history between these two people across a plurality of valued goods. It leaves open whether a viewer understands the form of that affection as romance. More importantly for the scene, gratitude for a life and recognition of care fail to transfer either person’s authority to make the other live or die in a prescribed way. Wakayama says she was glad to show Takina’s human, soft side across this passage (S109/p107). Adachi says that against an unwavering Chisato it is Takina who changes. He first imagined a low voice for her, thinking it necessary for a late explosion of feeling to show that change (the interviewer cites 心臓が逃げる！), until Wakayama’s audition exchanges convinced him she could express it without one (S123/p121). E12-CLM-019–025/048/050, E12-AUD-012/013 and REL-CHI-TAK-E12-003/004, C0164–166 and C0182–197.

Takina’s strongest evidence of particular attention appears when that care is least guaranteed to be welcome on its own terms. Chisato’s strongest insistence on doing things in her own way appears while she is trying to keep Takina safe. The incompatibility survives sympathy with either cause. At the end of E12 Chisato nevertheless turns back for her own bag, あ～っ 私のバッグ！ (C0471), and, cradling it, says ほら これ！ たきなに もらったやつ (C0473, 22:15.31–22:17.77). The satchel is the standard Lycoris bag. The pug charm on its front panel is Takina’s E08 gift (#001296–#001298 against E08 #001098), and it is the reason she names. The retrieval exposes the group to Majima’s renewed fire (C0474–476). The object completes an arc that began with a public request Takina rebuffed (E08 C0190–191) and continued through a private gift (C0419–424). It is worth the inconvenience even after their conflict, and it complicates any direct path from the later withdrawal to indifference. A design caption in the *Heroine Archive* likewise says the dog keyholder was given by Takina and hangs on Chisato’s Lycoris bag (S025/p023), an editorial note consistent with the frames. E12-CLM-046 as corrected by the audit recheck, and REL-CHI-TAK-E12-004. The narrative lets Takina pursue an unwelcome means of saving Chisato and lets Chisato withhold assent without denying that Takina cares; their earlier habits of permission cannot settle this new case.

## Survival does not settle who should be allowed to leave

In the triage after the fighting Takina keeps gauze back for Chisato while she treats Sakura with Erika’s help (千束の分が まだ, E13 C0006–008). Fuki and Takina negotiate how to share responsibility after Fuki’s injuries and the institutional crisis. Takina’s 私たちで決めましょう has a plural subject. Fuki’s 千束を頼む makes room for Takina’s particular responsibility without dissolving Fuki’s concern for either woman. The rescue from Majima is a different act from the argument over Yoshimatsu. Takina can take Chisato out of immediate danger without demanding acceptance of a specific donor’s death. It is therefore an instance of their ongoing attachment and competence, with a distinct ethical premise. Chisato’s statement to Majima about an earnest friend belongs among the café, its setting and people who matter to her. The Japanese phrase 一生懸命な友達 is unnamed in that sentence. The dyad’s accumulated scenes support a likely relation to Takina without making that identification the spoken wording. E13-CLM-001–005/013–019 and REL-CHI-TAK-E13-001.

Chisato then leaves rather than attend the kind of farewell she dreads. Other people bear the consequences of that decision. Takina benefits from civilian observation, a detail in Saori’s photograph that Kurumi relays, to find her (C0374–381), confronts her, and brings her knowledge of a changed survival situation. Chisato’s autonomy is therefore a force capable of making other people do unwanted work. Takina’s direct objection is one of the things the relationship has taught both people to make possible. It does not require endorsing every prior use of pursuit or pressure. The island encounter gives a concise controlled instance of Takina reassuring Chisato that she can live. The first visible shot in the reunion is Takina’s; later shots from concealment stay unattributed. The quarrel that follows drops Takina into plain speech, アホは そっちでしょ！ (C0349–357). At the island café counter, in the owner’s presence, Chisato says そう 私の相棒～ and then 千束の相棒だよ？, and Takina turns to listen (C0369–370, #000938, #000944); the question being answered is not captioned. Asked why she disappeared, Chisato says she woke alone after surgery, thought she was about to die, did not want anything 湿っぽい and wanted to find a good place while she could still move (C0389–396). It echoes the reason she gave Mizuki in E09, that knowing had made Takina self-conscious; the link between the two is this synthesis’s inference. Takina says she has brought something from Mika, explains what Mika brought, and tells her あなたは死にません (C0402–406). Her person-specific seriousness can then be expressed in arrangement of future action, ordinary complaint and repeated play, apart from the absolute urgency of E12. Asked for the most memorable scene, Asaura says the E03 fountain first struck him but is too obvious an answer, and names this café scene instead, singling out the wrist movement as Chisato holds out her palm to ask how Takina found her (*Heroine Archive* S138/p136). E13-CLM-025–030 and REL-CHI-TAK-E13-002.

Chisato accepts continued life without having to enact the personal killing she refused. Mika’s physical confrontation, Yoshimatsu’s death, the relevant exchange and the existence of a working heart do not give a view of the exact lethal act. Yoshimatsu’s retrospective claim about his death reaches the viewer over a separate image of Mika holding the written farewell. Chisato finds a birthday greeting with a blank visible interior and the charm. She hears Takina’s account of what Mika brought. No image or dialogue gives Chisato the decorated farewell’s wording. The sequence makes the gift and the donor’s attempted interpretation available to different people (E13 C0404–405 and C0422–425, #001049/001051 versus #001087/001089/001093). Chisato discards the pendant, then tells Takina that she had hesitated because Takina called it cute. The attribution becomes a joke between them. Takina denies saying it, with embarrassment in her stated reason. Their physical play and disagreement about the line can coexist with a consequential separation of gift and obligation. The callback supports the pendant as the referent in E04 and shows that the appraisal persisted for Chisato. E13-CLM-033–039, E13 C0431–439, REV-E13-006 and TV Q13.

The next jointly authored proposition gives the ending a modest future. Takina suggests starting with what Chisato had given up on because she expected to die. Chisato chooses an adventure. Chisato calls her 相棒, now after Takina has actually had to decide a life direction independently several times. The term has become observable in disagreements, farewells, rescue decisions and a resumed plan. It also remains the word Chisato says, before strangers since Takina’s first working day (E01 C0260–261; E13 C0369–370) and at the beach (行くぞ 相棒！, C0445); its distribution is set out under address below. There is no necessity to replace it with either an exclusive legal or romantic category, or a denial that the staging invites romance. In the Hawaii epilogue Chisato keeps Kusunoki’s call from Takina with 千束のファンから～ (C0463–464), a small, play-framed concealment of the kind E09 already showed. Asaura says Chisato was fixed early as a complete character who does not grow, and that Takina’s role was to grow until she could become a partner able to help Chisato in a pinch (S136–137/p134–135). That is creator intention for an asymmetric arc. The A1 record shows its course in Takina’s address, initiative and decisions, and it also shows Chisato learning to receive limits. E13-CLM-040/041/047 and REL-CHI-TAK-E13-003.


## Six short tests of the life they make

The Friends shorts supply particular situations after the TV presentation, with each situated vignette entitled to its own evidence. The series gives neither a complete calendar for the vignettes nor independent confirmation through repeats.

Short 01 makes stewardship and joint interpretation separable. Takina anticipates a stock shortage and proposes supplies and a future commercial venture. Chisato reads Takina’s response to the event as liking blossom viewing. Takina’s explanation is about opportunity. Chisato can get that wrong while noticing the conspicuous change in Takina’s private enthusiasm. Later Chisato challenges Takina’s instinct to rely on a trained capacity for what they are doing in a civilian setting. The dispute can belong to their shared life without making their ordinary preferences converge or obliging either person to give up the competence involved. SHORT01-CLM-003–008/011–019 and REL-CAFE-SHORT01-001.

In Short 02 Chisato proposes attractions, including a photograph, and volunteers to deal with someone calling while the café is closed. Her imitation of the caller, Takina’s marked laugh, her denial of it and the phone on the counter make shared joking possible at the edge of a genuine work demand. After Chisato asks about her nonkilling condition, Takina takes the call, uses her full name and apologizes. She tells Kusunoki she is a little 浮かれて, then protests about herself and insists all is well (C041–047), while Chisato reads out tour attractions beside her. Takina then proposes finishing promptly to leave time for travel preparations. The text suspends 私も before she finishes expressing her own excitement (C058–059). The private admission reverses the half-denial she gave her superior, and its 私も answers Chisato. The later scene gives Chisato a reason to reread the admonition. Takina is doing more than keeping an unruly partner on task. She is partly trying to protect their ability to do something she wants too. Chisato supplies concrete attractions, and Takina supplies time and shared intent. Neither books or completes that trip here. The later shots of them looking at phones establish that action independently of the proposed photograph. SHORT02-CLM-001–006, C018–022, C035–049 and C050–059, with C054–055 carrying the proposed order of work and C058–059 the admission.

Short 03 gives Takina a social and epistemic consequence she does not intend. Chisato comes home and reads Takina’s sustained attention to a glass as an occasion to imagine psychic powers. Takina is considering additions to the menu. Chisato hears a new word, recognizes the film likely to have prompted it and makes a performance of recognizing it. Takina’s イノベーション can become a joke they produce jointly without stripping the word of meaning for her. The next labor matters as labor. Chisato supplies the earlier ideas, Takina credits Mika, Mizuki recalls the cost and Takina demands the receipt for the peach. Kurumi’s direct comparison on seeing the result provides corroboration of what several viewers think the object looks like. Takina twice objects to that interpretation and refuses to sell it at the cost revealed. Love of the company does not require acceptance of its attribution to her, particularly on her own project. SHORT03-CLM-001–006.

Short 04 makes the conditions of private intimacy and its display visible. The recorded Chisato tends Takina’s hair and performs a salon voice. Kurumi asks about it in the viewing room. Present Takina gives the literal reply that she is just having her hair combed. The depiction distributes the activity, theatrical line, inquiry and gloss across recording and playback. The image shows activity Takina accepts in the recorded relationship. It does not supply a rule of permission to record, review and circulate every private act. The later prank places that question within a more dangerous asymmetry of knowledge. Chisato and other café members participate in a frightening surprise. Takina says she thought she would die (C078). Chisato subsequently imitates Takina’s scream and recognizes that startling her risks lives. Takina can still pursue the earlier cost of the alcohol and dish. The scream makes the prank’s effect evident without disclosing anyone’s private judgment. SHORT04-CLM-001–006, C033–037 and C073–087.

Short 05 brings the right to make a choice down to its exact object. Chisato teases Takina that she would not understand Fuki’s eagerness to stay near Mika (C014–015), one more instance of Chisato’s running joke that Takina is slow about feelings. Asked by Fuki whether they now live together, Takina says she is looking for a room, and Chisato says she can stay for good (C027–029). Chisato can lend her own uniform to Sakura while Fuki refuses consent to wearing hers. Later Takina privately tries a red role she publicly polices. Sakura laughs and Takina demands a version in which she saw nothing as Sakura is physically restrained and unable to answer in an ordinary free register. Here someone reared inside institutional hierarchies uses discretion like power. The probable joke about social embarrassment cannot give Sakura a choice that the depicted situation takes away. Meanwhile Chisato keeps playing with an arrest call even after Fuki voices irritation. The café is a place where these people may choose to stay, put on clothes or make trouble, and no two of those permissions have the same owner, recipient or scope. SHORT05-CLM-002–006 and REV-SHORT05-004.

Short 06 makes differential knowledge and care an ordinary collective problem. Takina’s first attempt at a cover is a flat line the captions print in katakana, ソンナワケ ナイジャナイ デスカァ… (C007). She cannot perform the lie, so she starts explaining that the uniform is issued according to assessment. Chisato interrupts with a made-to-order cover. Takina calls it concealment (これが隠ぺいです, C019), turning back a jibe of her own from Short 04, and three fans take both replies as committed role-play. In the later café conversation, Chisato has a practical reason to signal Takina to put away an object she has offered to demonstrate. Locally successful performance does not prove that the public authorizes DA. Plans for the airport, travel, board games and a stay at the café belong to different people. Chisato’s wish to travel with her companions is a genuine future orientation. It is not the only orientation allowed in the group. SHORT06-CLM-001–006 and REV-SHORT06-001–003.

## Names, address and register

Address is where the asymmetry of the first meeting can be measured. Chisato calls Takina たきな from the start and asks to be called 千束 without さん (E01 C0161–162). Takina keeps the honorific until the E03 conversation at the fountain (C0272) and drops it on the train home (C0462, 22:08.64). No later 千束さん from Takina occurs in the TV episodes or the shorts, and every later vocative is the bare name. Dropping the honorific does not drop the polite verb forms. Takina keeps です/ます to Chisato through E13 and the shorts, and calls Mika 店長 where Chisato says 先生 (E13 C0402). Her plain forms break out under pressure or in quarrels: 放して 千束！ and 嫌だ 千束が死ぬのは嫌だ in the tower (E12 C0125, C0190), アホは そっちでしょ！ in the forest (E13 C0357). あなた marks distance in E01 (C0451) and anger at the fountain (E03 C0276); its one late use is the reassurance あなたは死にません (E13 C0406). Toward people who threaten Chisato she drops to お前: to Himegama (E09 C0012) and to Yoshimatsu in E12. Sakaki recalls loving that move from ヨシさん and 吉松さん to お前 (*Heroine Archive* S118/p116).

Chisato’s naming runs the other way. She gives herself an honorific in mock seniority, 千束さん (E04 C0165; E09 C0321). She introduces Takina to a gang boss’s men as 私の相棒 with a mock-polite たきなさん (E01 C0260–261). In *Recovery days*, when Fuki asks whether she is getting on with “that one”, she defends うちのたきなさん, who came though unwell, while Takina sits silent in the helicopter (13.767–771). In *Gluttony days* she cuts into a standoff with うちのたきなの方が一〇〇倍美人, and the narrated effect on Takina is that her readiness to kill dulls (10.564–567). うち places Takina inside Chisato’s household, and Chisato is glad when Takina herself calls the café うち (*Gluttony days* 11.15–59). Between the two of them, 相棒 is Chisato’s word aloud in every source. Mika introduces it with the assignment (E01 C0151), and Majima and Erika use it of the pair (E08 C0370; E12 C0281; E13 C0043). Chisato says it at E01 C0153 and C0261, *Recovery days* 9.321 (行くぞ、相棒！) and E13 C0369–370 and C0445. Takina never says it aloud in the anime or in *Recovery days*. It appears only in her focalized narration and dream reasoning there (9.300, 14.310), and in *Gluttony days* she rejects it as a ground for a request: 相棒は関係ないじゃないですか (14.145). This is Takina’s only spoken 相棒 in the sources cited here. Chisato’s sigh 私の相棒はこんなんばっかかよ covers Fuki and Takina alike (*Gluttony days* 13.39–40). Asked by a stranger in a queue whom the red rice is for, Takina says it is for a 友人 who is ill in bed (*Recovery days* 12.571), the only spoken name for the relation she gives in that book. The words for the bond are therefore unevenly distributed. Chisato claims it before third parties as a partnership and as part of her household. Takina, when she names it at all to an outsider, chooses the ordinary word for a friend. The *Heroine Archive* editors call Takina Chisato’s 無二の親友 (S026/p024). That is the editors’ word.

The comics keep the pattern with local variation. In *Recollect* V01 Takina says 千束さん once, with a regular present, and the bare name elsewhere (S39; S90–119). In V02 she keeps です/ます to Chisato at home and in the bonus, and her plain form appears only in narration boxes, thought balloons and asides (S54–56, S94, S108). In 稲岡和佐’s *Repeat 03* comic, after a blurred shot Takina apologizes as 千束さん, and an untailed burst balloon, by its content Chisato’s, registers that the form of address has slipped back (S13). Chisato’s nicknames add a teasing register of their own, as when she calls Takina’s sickroom regime スパルたきな (*Recovery days* 12.55). Takina’s register toward Chisato therefore records the relationship’s history twice: the honorific drops once, and the politeness stays.

## What the works let each person know

A2 gives the reader experiences withheld from both viewers and people in the story. B1 and C1 give the same people to other hands, whose page order and comic premises distribute knowledge differently again. Each finding below keeps its source and its ruling.

### *Ordinary days*

Asaura’s first novel gives Takina the interior view of the partnership’s working method. In the gunfight story she judges that Chisato never kills but goes to the edge of it without mercy, as when she shoots a downed man in the nape (12.61–62). When Takina asks for calmer conduct in case of a mistake, Chisato answers that Takina will be there. When Takina says even live rounds could disable without killing, Chisato hands that work to her as their division of labor and postpones her own reasons with いつかわかるよ (12.73–95). Against the armored guard Bulldog, Takina proposes withdrawal, concedes when Chisato decides to charge, and covers her. When he draws a knife on Chisato, Takina cries her name and empties her magazine into the blade; her fire maims his knife hand, and nobody dies (12.134–148, 12.197–204). Takina suggests leaving the fugitive to the police; Chisato refuses and explains the value of live capture, and Takina calls off the film they had planned to watch before opening (12.233–255). The same order recurs at the café. Takina names the cost or proposes the safe plan, then executes Chisato’s (10.220, 10.280, 10.373). She still watches the films Chisato presses on her while calling the loans a nuisance (15.66–72). SPECIFY (OD-13, OD-14, OD-20).

The first story pressures any claim that Chisato’s attentiveness gives her access to Takina’s motives. Chisato reports that Takina “let slip” an interest in a gloomy regular, Doi, a paraphrase she qualifies at once (10.120, 10.128). Her theory rests on her rule that Takina notices only skill or threat (10.203) and hardens when Takina smiles at him (10.335–355). Takina tells Mizuki and Kurumi that she disliked him, 嫌いです, for a seat held over one coffee, the lowest spend of any customer and a gloom that darkened the room, and revises to 嫌いでした once he orders sweets. She disowns the outings as Chisato’s doing (10.616–636). Kurumi keeps this from Chisato, and Chisato’s wink three weeks later implies the theory survives (10.641–643, 10.686). Concern for the man himself remains a live rival reading (10.97, 10.128). SPECIFY (OD-09, OD-10).

In the staff-meal story Chisato focalizes and diagnoses Takina as too earnest to find a point where something is good enough (14.53–65). Her narrated reason for valuing Takina’s protein-shake lunch is that life is short, nobody knows how many meals are left, and this one is a memory she will keep until she dies (14.175–183). Takina says she will think about it. Takina’s own section gives her reason for going along with Chisato’s trick: Chisato very occasionally shows her a view she had not known, あの、噴水の前の時のように (14.281–290). The book does not narrate that conversation. An A1 reader will think of the E03 fountain, but the referent stays open. When her rice balls come out misshapen, Chisato blocks Takina’s どうせ three times and praises their taste. Takina turns to the sink, and the narration gives a pleasure she likens to being accepted and embraced and calls unfair (ズルイ) (14.355–385). The simile is hers; no embrace occurs. EXTEND (OD-04, OD-17).

In OD-05 Takina entertains a two-person future in a dream focalized through her, whose ownership the text leaves open. Inside it she murmurs that she would not mind staying alone with Chisato, wants to count the words as a private remark, and turns down dream Chisato’s proposal that they go away together because she wants them both to survive (16.365–406). A genre explanation and a two-serving espresso pot alternately dismiss and revive a personal reading of the invitation, and the text leaves the object of her wish unnamed (16.407–461, 16.546). Earlier in the dream, when the imagined Chisato presses closer than Takina wants, she tells her to stop and decides to push her away (16.224–259). In waking action she pushes sleeping Chisato toward the car door and is irritated when she slumps back (16.602). She asks Mizuki to forget the sounds she made and allows only that the dream was not so bad (16.608–615). It matters that the two reactions go in apparently opposite directions. The dream establishes a subjective possibility within an Asaura configuration. The waking body asserts an immediate boundary. The two need no single hidden answer. Chisato’s sleep-talk about the peace of the world and “the two of us” echoes the dream’s ending (16.616), but her own dream is inaccessible. Later the bonus places a meal and a candid photo after a posed arrangement. Accepting food and being photographed are distinct. The posed shoot, for a magazine feature written by the café regular Tokuda, was Chisato’s request. The magazine’s unnamed photographer takes the candid from behind Tokuda, Tokuda expects it to be used and the published issue supplies the result. The narrative withholds assent for the later photo. The distribution of access makes any direct inference from smiled attachment to permission as weak in the prose as it was in the shorts. OD 16.143–148, 16.351–431, 16.443–461 and 16.519–617 for dream and waking. OD 19.28–45 and 22.25–56 for the shoot and the candid. SOURCE_SPECIFIC for the dream (OD-05); EXTEND for the photographs, against CP5’s account of social cover (OD-18).

### *Recovery days*

*Recovery days* has no Chisato-focalized passage, so Chisato’s intentions reach the reader through her speech and Takina’s guesses. The book opens and closes at the cinema. Takina objects to the venue on price and time, not to films (9.105–112), privately decides she could go after closing (9.130), and agrees to a second trailer so readily that it surprises her (9.410–415). At the wrong building Chisato takes her hand with 行くぞ、相棒！ (9.319–321). In the dark Takina judges her greedy, childish and sometimes detached beyond her years, still a 変な人, and suspects she has become one of the people who indulge her (9.401–418). In *Safety work* Takina admires Chisato’s handling of a gun and resents being outclassed when Chisato’s revolver reaches her temple during a modeling pose; she senses that Chisato counts on her to understand and stop (10.244–278). When a rifle each believed the other had cleared discharges in a civilian’s flat, Takina first rounds on Chisato, then reconstructs the handover and takes the larger share (10.579–588). Asked which night was more fun, she will not say, and Chisato tells her she must know, since it is about herself (10.689–697). In *Dog*, after Takina’s live round grazes the man they were hired to protect, Chisato tells her not to shoot, and later silences her with a smile to stay and talk with him (11.229, 11.241–253, 11.373–406). EXTEND shared leisure with differing tastes (RD-01); SPECIFY Chisato’s nonkilling beside a partner firing live rounds, and Takina’s view of Chisato’s competence (RD-08, RD-12).

*Cough* reverses who nurses whom, then reverses it again. Takina denies being worried and cites the café’s duty as a food business (12.42–46). She sniffs Chisato’s hair, and when Chisato’s face reddens under her hand she diagnoses the start of a fever; a reader can see embarrassment where she sees symptoms (12.121–137). She bases her firmness on understanding Chisato well enough after a short acquaintance (12.142–146) and turns away when Chisato asks for privacy while changing (12.360–371). When Chisato arranges for her to join the game night, Takina answers 知ってますよ to its being fun, and thinks it is no longer clear which of them is caring for the other (12.471–479). She privately counts herself among ordinary people whom nobody much worries about (12.664–673), a belief the regulars’ gifts later revise (14.357–358). Her plan for a combined walk breaks off at 千束となら、もっと── and is never proposed (12.682). She catches Chisato faking a fever and grants that only Chisato knows why she had been sent home (12.685–705). EXTEND care in both directions and Takina’s efficiency-framed plans (RD-04, RD-10); SPECIFY the gap between her public denials and private pleasure (RD-09).

In *Common occurrence* dream Chisato proposes saying they are dating as a supposed means to survive a slasher plot. Takina questions why it would work and does not join the declaration (14.244–270). She reasons that the rule-keeper is herself and the free, selfish one Chisato (14.277–280), offers Chisato her hand into the escape truck, the reverse of 9.321 (14.295–297), and then entertains a description of a “couple” as any working pair. If that is so, she thinks, the dream need not be hers at all (14.309–312). On waking she cannot recall it. Their raised hands overlap across two futons, and whether by chance or intention is expressly unknown (14.336–339). She lets go and sits up (14.341). Chisato’s proposed expression is an instrument in the dream. It cannot fill the living blank left by someone who does not remember it or supply shared intent toward the waking contact. The two Asaura dreams therefore offer two different mechanisms through which a romantic possibility reaches the reader. They cannot be counted as independent waking declarations. In the fever story, the gifts brought by Mika and Mizuki and the ordinary acts of care become more visible, while the partner who often takes practical responsibility can receive care herself; Chisato has put her to bed in return for the earlier nursing (14.334). In the film follow-up, Takina realizes that her criticism has committed her to choosing the next film and is sure Chisato knows she has noticed. She admits to herself that the day was enjoyable and that this is a little galling (18.11–29). Chisato then offers an explicit way out, and Takina interrupts it to say she would like another outing (18.30–32). She already saw a preview that looked interesting and wants to look up further information once it is available before choosing the particular film. At such moments she sees an adult in Chisato rather than a playmate (18.34–37). Each change enlarges the possible future without forcing its completion. ### *Gluttony days*

*Gluttony days* tests whether shared work requires shared rules. On a staged protection job Takina answers Chisato’s 命、大事に with 了解です while already resolved to kill, uses protecting the client as her reason to separate from Chisato, and privately calls Chisato 邪魔 to that purpose (10.195–233). Whose life Chisato meant is not said. In the standoff with the assassin Madame, Chisato’s voice nearly turns Takina’s head, like a dog hearing its name. She keeps her eyes on the opponent, but her resolve to kill dulls (10.564–567). Afterward Chisato wipes Takina’s nosebleed with her own handkerchief and praises her effort. When the client apologizes, Chisato pats Takina’s head, which Takina privately wants to brush away and allows for the girl’s sake (10.745–819). The care ran both ways: during the retreat Takina had pressed her own handkerchief to Chisato’s brow (10.117). Takina rates Chisato’s reading of people above her own (GD-07), and Chisato declines a park outing partly because Takina, sent for a medical check, could not come; Takina is not displeased (10.935–960). PRESSURE and SPECIFY (GD-06, GD-07).

The hair dryer in *Gluttony days* offers a waking countercase that a flattering interpretation of either partner must retain. Takina twice refuses. To Chisato’s appeal to their partnership she answers that being partners has nothing to do with it (14.143–145). Chisato persists strongly enough that Takina accepts to save time (14.146–149). Chisato’s handling is gentle once she proceeds. The prose gives Takina embarrassment and bodily response, and she hopes her blush will pass for the bath’s heat (14.159–162). The drying stops when Chisato raises the question of food (14.163–179). Takina finishes her own hair, notices a practical difference and later dries Chisato’s hair herself. Chisato calls the exchange wordless understanding, and Takina says that is a stretch given how obvious the cue was (14.241–242, 14.259–276). Later that night she tells Chisato not to come into her futon (14.364–388), and on waking she pushes away Chisato’s sleeping attempt to use her lap as a pillow (15.14). The later reciprocal gesture is welcome evidence of agency. It still has a later occasion than the original objection. Embarrassment can coexist with interest, care and an earlier dislike of how the experience began. The prose has access to that variation and the sequence of action. Its narrator does not convert the pressured first concession into an unqualified yes. In one of the volume’s two Chisato-focalized pieces she thinks Takina looks prettier than yesterday and decides she was probably always this pretty (13.18–27). The book ends with each resolving to diet and the other offering to join (15.44–47, 17.17–54). EXTEND both partners’ tastes, boundaries and second thoughts (GD-05); SOURCE_SPECIFIC for Chisato’s focalized appraisal (GD-09). GD 14.139–179/218–276.

### *Recollect*

Abe Kanari uses page order in place of direct access to each mind. Takina can react to the film while later describing the quantifiable reasons she changes how she watches. In V01 she had treated a bag of Chisato’s films as image training under a quota of her own and skipped the credits; Chisato had set no quota (S47–52). When Chisato leaves an awkward film scene, the reader learns what Chisato intends to avoid. The return to the sofa reveals that Takina paused it. The gesture supports doing something for a shared activity; Takina accepts it as obvious courtesy (当然です), while what she knew of Chisato’s reason for leaving stays unlettered. Curation of a pleasant encounter includes recognizing when it may fail. In the café wager Chisato herself calls involving an unasked customer very rude and proposes apology and payment. Takina asks what Chisato gains and declines to offer anything (いえ なにも). Chisato names Takina’s playing along as her reward (S88–91). For a newcomer the two agree to cancel the bet and split the cost of a welcome item (S93). What the arriving customer, who speaks as a regular, agrees to remains unresolved when the guess and the wager are interrupted (S94). In the pottery pun Takina, after an earlier water-pistol trial, creates a situation for Chisato and wins a lexical game under the rule Chisato herself set. The water pistol was Takina’s first attempt: she reasoned that a muzzle quickens her own pulse, tried it on Chisato and judged it ineffective (V02 S80–81). Chisato says the requested emotional effect did not occur. Successful reference, measured outcome and the person’s report about her feelings split cleanly. ### The anthologies

The anthologies draw alternate local conditions. In GUNP’s comic Chisato asks for a hug, citing its soothing effect. Takina answers with café costs, then opens her arms, names what Chisato herself wanted and admits she was soothed too. Chisato promises to ask directly next time, asks again at once, and Takina agrees to a second hug (*Repeat* S151–158; EXTEND). In 花田ももせ’s, Takina declines Chisato’s offer to share a bed, accepts a late-night outing for ice, names DA as her own wish, offers to go along with Chisato’s, and again refuses the shared bed (*Reload* S99–108; SPECIFY). In めきめき’s, Takina oversleeps her breakfast duty; Chisato cooks and guesses fatigue, while Takina’s narration attributes the deep sleep to feeling safe and aloud she calls it Chisato’s fault (*Repeat* S165–176; SOURCE_SPECIFIC). ## What the makers say

The *Heroine Archive* fanbook adds what the director, writers, designers and actors say they intended or perceived. It enters this synthesis only as attributed testimony beside the A1 scene it concerns. It never supplies a fictional event, line, motive or knowledge state, and it cannot close an A1 OPEN item. Several statements already stand beside their scenes above. Others bear on the relationship as a whole. The character designer Imigimuru says Chisato was designed as a set with Takina (S129/p127). Actors offer readings of their own. Kuno Misaki, who voices Kurumi, says Takina at first walled herself off and Chisato actively closed the distance and took the walls down, while Kurumi kept a distance suited to Takina (S114/p112). Sakaki takes 決心 as the work’s keyword, citing Chisato’s “you chose it yourself” and Takina’s choice to go to Chisato against orders (S116/p114). In his E13 commentary Adachi says that even Chisato broke her no-kill rule, albeit to save Takina (千束ですらも…不殺を破ってしまった). He names no act (S097/p095). His sentence is a retrospective moral summary. It cannot be attached to any particular A1 event, and it changes no A1 OPEN item. One anecdote is uncorroborated. Wakayama recalls that her first take of E09’s last scene did not quite sit right with her and that she recorded it again after asking Adachi about the scene’s background. Anzai does not remember the exchange, and the interviewer reports that Adachi, asked shortly before, does not remember it either. Wakayama jokingly asks whether it was all her illusion; Anzai puts it down to her own and Adachi’s memory, and Wakayama holds that the scene moved her most even if it was an illusion. The editors head the section 第9話の収録で見た幻（？） (S106/p104).

## Intimacy remains a relationship question

Both partners are minors, and this account names nothing the sources do not show. The A1 record ends with a romantic status that is not explicitly declared, and no supplementary source supplies a waking declaration by either partner. The strongest romantic reading draws on an accumulated structure of images and phrases. The pair eat a bite face to face, share a household under a readily legible cohabitation word, reach toward another’s chest under changed privacy conditions, and return with person-specific plans. On two major occasions Chisato chooses to say how Takina makes something valuable. Takina has an intelligible personal fear of losing Chisato and can voice it outside a task register. Their relationship survives ordinary embarrassment, abrupt separation and drastic disagreement. The public joke in E03, the charged possibility of closeness under the cover of innocent practical work, and recognition of forms of love elsewhere create an affirmative case for yuri coding that does not wait for an author to attach a category. Selected comics intensify some particular readings and some depicted relationships. That intensification can be cited by creator rather than converted into how often or exactly what happens in A1.

The novels sharpen the case and its limits together. Their two closest scenes are dreams: Takina’s assent and refusal in a dream of open ownership (*Ordinary days*), and dream Chisato’s dating declaration, which Takina declines to join (*Recovery days*). The counterweights set out above stand against both: awake, the push toward the car door (*Ordinary days* 16.602); in the dream itself, Takina’s reasoning that a “couple” may only mean a working pair, and awake, the released hand (*Recovery days* 14.309–312, 14.341); and the refused futon and lap (*Gluttony days* 14.364–388, 15.14). Takina’s spoken word for Chisato to a stranger is 友人 (*Recovery days* 12.571); Chisato’s is 相棒. Creators frame the bond in their own words: GUNP as a friendship watched from afar (*Repeat* S159), the *Heroine Archive* editors as 無二の親友, and めきめき a future of living and working together that the creator labels personal imagining (*Repeat* S177). None of these names a relationship status for the A1 pair.

A chosen-partner reading has comparable affirmative support on its own terms. Chisato actually names 相棒, makes the collective café a meaningful alternative, and helps Takina accept membership in another institution. Takina understands Chisato’s need for a private conversation distinct from a wish for company. The domestic routine begins in response to a concrete safety situation, Takina ends its extension through a condition she sets, and an open-ended invitation in a short can coexist with looking for her own room. The two are held inside a broader set of companions and practical collaborators who can oppose either of them. This account can take particular affection seriously because it already has a vocabulary of repeated choice in the face of real alternatives. The text leaves room for intimacy within both readings.

For prediction the label matters less than demonstrated abilities. Takina can reassure without copying Chisato’s voice and oppose an action without withdrawing attachment. Chisato can acknowledge the care in an unwanted solution. Either can withdraw and thereby impose work on the other. When the evidence points to an intimate wish, seek its owner, its audience, the action it authorizes and the possible refusal.

## What stays open
