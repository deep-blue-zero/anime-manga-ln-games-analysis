# Reload · contributor readings

Takina asks to play again after losing ikra’s board game. In 秋タカ’s comic it is Takina who wins so often that she asks for another game, and the cooperative one Kurumi supplies has her playing *with* Chisato. Neither creator needs the other to be wrong. The reversal brings out different reasons that someone might keep playing. Across the volume, changes of who holds the camera, who asks for company, and who gets the last look at an imagined scene matter more than a tally of recurring traits. The fifteen credits below each have their own beginning, ending and authority.

| ikra · *れっつぷれい！* | S15–22 | Chisato wins again and Kurumi solicits Takina’s cooperation. Chisato calls a break, Mika brings drinks, and different players describe what they want next. Chisato puts the three-day request down to her hating to lose (負けず嫌い); Takina answers that the point is a real contest (勝負にならないと), Kurumi says that is still hating to lose, and the comic leaves their reading contested (S21). | ヨコシマペンギン · *Seeing is believing* | S35–42 | Before opening, Chisato photographs Takina unasked. Takina asks Chisato to pose, finds her performance irritating, and prefers a simpler picture. Kurumi demonstrates how far an app can alter a face. | 苗川采 · *猛犬注意* | S43–54 | A repeated waking composition at S43 and S52 changes the status of the intervening panels. The dog-eared Takina can take the lead, stun Majima with a bark and demand the praise Chisato “always” gives by ruffling her head in Chisato’s dream. Even there Chisato asks whether it is really all right and whether Takina will bite or get angry (S45–51). Awake, Takina says closing work cannot proceed while Chisato sleeps there and that she may leave early if tired. She approaches when beckoned. Chisato pulls her into actual contact. Startled, Takina threatens to hit her after the dog comparison (S52–54). | **CARICATURE / SOURCE_SPECIFIC.** The wish for habitual petting is dream material, and the dream already contains Chisato’s doubt about Takina’s response. Chisato’s waking embrace and Takina’s objection are enacted. Her threatened blow is not. |
| 山村うみ · *ふたりの一番* | S55–62 | At the safehouse Takina cooks breakfast for her host and fries the egg through on both sides because that is the most efficient and quickest method (S55–57). Chisato wants a runny yolk and soy sauce, calls her an efficiency nerd (効率厨) and asks for bread sometimes; Takina answers 善処します (S57–59). Further differences over navigation, food and animals follow. Takina completes Chisato’s sentence by proposing to dissolve the partnership (相棒解散), which the accumulating small panels make comically disproportionate (S61). Back at the café, a man asks how it turned out. Kurumi calls to the girls and asks them to name their favorite place. Chisato answers at once and Takina after a hesitation; both name LycoReco (S59–62). | **SPECIFY.** The shared answer concerns a place. The earlier preferences are allowed to differ, and the threatened breakup never occurs. Yamamura’s comment describes imagining this domestic mismatch (S144–145). |
| 秋タカ · *ゲームしようよ* | S63–70 | Repeated positions and escalating scores show Takina running up wins while Chisato stays on zero. Smiling, Takina asks whether Chisato now understands how she felt at rock-paper-scissors; the inset is a chore chart with her name in every slot (S65–66). She then thinks she may have overdone it, asks Kurumi for another game, and puts Kurumi’s pick, a cooperative mode, to the sulking Chisato (S67). Crowded together, the partners enjoy making common cause. The last screen handle, かべねじ, plays on “wall” and “nut.” Kurumi’s aside confirms that she was the stronger challenger and had wanted to join them (S64–70). | **EXTEND.** Takina first uses winning as payback, then changes the terms of the contest for someone else. A switch to cooperation yields pleasure and another loss. This story distributes winning differently from ikra’s. |
| 幌田 · *Not quite the clean potato* | S71–78 | At two in the morning Takina cites the curfew for minors and moves to leave; Chisato presents the restaurant visit as practical training (S71–72). To Chisato’s list of ordinary pleasures Takina replies that the two of them are not ordinary and begins to place herself as DA’s (私はDAの…). Chisato cuts in that she wants to do these things *with Takina* (S74). Takina initiates discussion of an unnamed overseas police series. She likes the realistic entry scene and dislikes a betrayal Chisato enjoys. Asked whether she had fun, she stays silent, starts when Chisato says she seemed quite into it, and deflects by likening Chisato’s clinging to Mizuki’s when drunk (S76). She says she cannot imagine herself at twenty at all, then offers to drink with Chisato if they are still together (S77). Chisato puts a hand to her chest, pauses, and declines (やだ), giving as her reason that Takina would be a bad drunk (S78). | **SOURCE_SPECIFIC.** The juxtaposition permits a reader to feel the cost of imagining adulthood. Chisato supplies no prognosis. Takina’s offer is conditional and Chisato turns it down in jest, and their opinions about the show remain distinct. |
| ぺけ · *ネギとキャベツとまごころと。* | S79–86 | Takina says she sent the sick Chisato home once, but Chisato would not rest, so she brought her to the café by force (強制収容) and Mika closed the shop (S79). Chisato refuses medicine, which Takina has tried hiding in syrup and jelly and now in porridge; asleep, Chisato turns her face from the spoon (S81). Takina approaches, Chisato wakes and teasingly recasts what she would allow. Takina flees. On the last page Chisato hurries to the now-ill Takina carrying a leek and a cabbage, the remedy Takina had used on her (S83–86). | **EXTEND.** Reversing their positions makes reciprocal care observable. By Takina’s own account, her care proceeds by compulsion: relocation (強制収容) and medicine concealed in food. The proposed remedy’s efficacy, a particular route of transmission and the act in Chisato’s teasing suggestion remain unestablished. |
| タチ · *ミズキの合コン大作戦* | S89–98 | The title page announces practice for a mixer, with Fuki present in DA uniform (S89). | 花田ももせ · *Midsummer Night’s Dream* | S99–108 | The posted rota gives Takina most of the work under a supposedly fair rock-paper-scissors arrangement. The swing panels hold them side by side while an aquarium, Harajuku, a group picnic and spring flowers take shape as possible outings. Takina initially names DA as her own destination, then offers to accompany Chisato and again draws the line at sharing a bed (S99–108). | **SPECIFY.** The swing scene takes place. | もけお · *レッツゴーウチュウキャットランド* | S109–116 | Chisato invites Takina to meet the theme park’s mascot, ウーミャ (S110), and Takina agrees once the park’s spaceship and gunfight scene come up (S111). She turns the invitation into a detailed itinerary of food, rides and a parade. Chisato’s private thought is that Takina seems to be enjoying the planning, and the clock reads 03:20 (S112). Oversleeping, queues and sold-out goods dismantle the plan. When Chisato asks what Takina now wants to do, Takina recalls Chisato’s wish to meet the mascot, takes her hand and leads her. The final page first shows their completed picture with the mascot, then Takina framing a second picture of the two on the train, with Chisato asleep on her shoulder (S111–116). | **EXTEND.** The original wish is Chisato’s, and choosing what to salvage becomes Takina’s. | すのはら風香 · *あなたの好きが好き* | S117–126 | The disguised Majima demands the video shop’s masterpiece, rejects Chisato as chooser and, after her protest, accepts a film Takina likes (S119–120). Takina’s thought is that she does not know which films she likes, since the nightly viewings follow Chisato’s taste (S121). A dark-backed memory holds the title’s ☆1 rating, the structural weakness Takina noticed, and Chisato’s answer that her own liking is a feeling she wants to treasure (唯一無二の私の感情, S122). Takina says she knows too little about films to call anything a favorite (S124). Her spoken criterion is the film at which she most liked *Chisato* (S125). In the last panel the sidelined customer thinks of using Robota’s streaming account instead (S119–126). | **SPECIFY.** A recommendation made because of how someone enjoyed a film does not entail the same taste, a completed rental or a new A1 encounter. |
| 安房さとる · *It’s not a piece of cake!* | S127–134 | Chisato offers Takina a wish in thanks for past rescues and because the café does not turn a profit without her (S127). Takina wants her to correct her habits. Compressed panels show a week in which Chisato can perform the program, then the drawing exaggerates the collapse that follows it. Chisato distinguishes being able to do unwanted work from choosing to do it. In unballooned text marked with irritation, which no one answers, Takina objects that she does not do tedious work because she wants to, nor because she is good at it (S132). Mika calls Chisato 自慢の娘, which Takina takes for the root cause, and Kurumi defends Chisato’s pace (S133). Chisato resumes talking to customers, and Takina ends with both affection and more work (S127–134). | **PRESSURE.** Ordinary service has a cost that remains after the joke. A successful week cannot establish a permanent change of habit. |
| 竹本蒼太郎 · *ユメノオワリ* | S135–142 | Narration focalized on Mika frames the comic: someday it had to be ended, illusions (幻想, read ゆめ) fade, the kind saviour Chisato imagines does not exist, and the gun was handed over to kill many (S135–136). In memory, the young Chisato wants to help many people like the saviour, to make her gun one that does not kill, and to make the shop a place where everyone smiles, in the hope that the saviour will visit. Mika answers かも…な; the next box, which has no lettered subject, asks how long the illusion is to be kept up (見せ続けるつもり), and beside his answer it reads most naturally as Mika’s self-reproach (S135–137). Young Chisato points to a drawing labeled with her teacher, herself and a generic benefactor. A close view of Mika’s eye carries the reader into a bright, unspoken image of Mika and Shinji holding Chisato’s hands. The return to the child and Mika’s recognition of impossibility distinguish the image from the drawing itself. A spent casing and Mika alone above the night city end the comic with his verdict, いい幻想だった (S142). | **SOURCE_SPECIFIC.** The narration, focalized on Mika but unattributed in the lettering, judges the saviour Chisato imagines and, beside Mika’s own かも…な, questions his part in sustaining her belief; at S141 the boxes also call the drawing something Chisato shows him. Shinji is identified in the adult’s pictured alternative. The child’s label does not independently name him. The omitted action between images supplies no unique impact, additional witness or newly established TV outcome. |

## The end of the volume

S23–24 and S87–88 are dividers. S143 is the continuation card beyond the last comic. All fifteen signed comments occupy S144–145. They document what contributors say they borrowed or imagined and do not merge their stories. S146–147 advertise a separate art book. S148–151 reproduce jacket, credit and cover material. S152 gives the Japanese colophon and S153 the BookWalker mark. The navigation spells 竹本蒼太朗. The pictured credit, signature and publisher’s list support **竹本蒼太郎**. The S143 continuation belongs to the collection and cannot settle what happened between the final comic’s images.
