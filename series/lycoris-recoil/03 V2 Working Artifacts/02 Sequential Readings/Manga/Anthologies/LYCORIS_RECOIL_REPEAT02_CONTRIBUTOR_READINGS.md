# Repeat 02 · contributor readings

Chisato calls the film she made with Takina a documentary. Takina defends it as nonfiction about their daily lives, while Mizuki asks whether it is a home video. Elsewhere, Takina likes Chisato's mascot design and still vetoes the expense. In the final comic, Takina explains how she located Chisato but fails when Chisato asks what she is thinking *now*. These twelve comics keep returning to the moment when an attractive interpretation meets an object, a cost or a reply its maker cannot control.

The strongest shared comparison lies in who gets to name the outcome. In 筒井いつき, Mizuki's question and Kurumi's skepticism survive Chisato and Takina's defense of their film. In ゆいち, the group can agree on a design and still have its project cancelled. In 森永ミキ, a photograph can show a change in Takina's expression without granting the café the right to post it. Those are independent creator realizations. One cannot authorize the next.
