# Reload 02 · contributor readings

Willis chooses Takina's lap after she dozes off. At the kindergarten, Takkun stamps on the other children's sandcastle and refuses Kurumi's offer even after she praises his talents. 幌田's Fuki offers to join Sakura's training. 大倉ナタ's Fuki takes Takina's request for a memorial visit to Kusunoki, then tells Chisato how Takina has been doing. These fourteen comics offer separate answers to what it means to help and who gets to decide whether the help is welcome.

Their simultaneous, uncounted shots bring the door down; Chisato remarks that now they are in sync (揃ってんじゃん), Takina calls it the quickest way, and they return to the pancakes (S41–46). **SPECIFY** coordination by domain: counted kicks fail, while wordless shooting succeeds at once. When Itō puts Takina on top, Chisato rebukes herself in thought for becoming self-conscious (わたしが意識してどーすんだ, S64), and both flush at the next pose (S65). | 耳式 · *クルミ my love* | S69–78 | Kurumi resists her kindergarten cover, costume and photography. Watching Chisato with the children, Takina thinks she looks like a professional nursery teacher and that such work might be nice after retirement (引退後は, S73). A boy the children call Takkun stamps on their sandcastle (S75). Kurumi checks her angry response and tells him the teacher praised his drawing and clay work. She urges him to use his talent and offers to take him as a junior, 舎弟, glossed in ruby as オトモダチ, if he develops it (S77). He blushes, refuses and runs away. Chisato later relays that he is building castles every day and teases Kurumi about sowing love. In a separate speech balloon Takina relays the teacher's request for another visit. Kurumi refuses another visit (S75–78). **EXTEND** an aptitude for addressing one child on his own terms. The child never calls Kurumi his friend; the “friend” reading is Kurumi's own ruby. | どま · *今日も明日もここに* | S79–88 | Chisato and Takina cannot place the shop that stood on the vacant lot. Back at LycoReco, Mika identifies it as the ramen shop. Mizuki reports a job: the landowner, who heads a ring selling other people's land, is confining the proprietor (軟禁), and Takina's silent close-up follows the news (S80–81). Black margins hold a remembered communal ramen meal and the proprietor's kindness (S83–84). The magazine identifies prison serialization, and Chisato notices the resemblance (S94–98). **EXTEND** practical encouragement compatible with accountability. The resulting work is a comic within this comic. |

Mika would welcome help, and Takina recalls the condition on which Kurumi is sheltered, helping with the work (仕事を手伝う条件で匿う, S101). Mizuki's felt kinship, Kurumi's cover and her imagined next joke warrant different claims. |
| かしわぎつきこ · *まず、おやすみから* | S109–118 | Takina's fear of losing Chisato opens into her waking in Okinawa after finding her. Chisato is there, brings tea and touches her hair. They exchange goodnights, then dispute whether to set an alarm (S113–118). **SOURCE_SPECIFIC** access to fear and the present relief of company. The trip plans and clothing remain prospective. |
| 幌田 · *Every dog has its day* | S119–128 | Juniors notice that Fuki has been listless since Enkūboku. Declaring her wound healed, Sakura challenges Fuki to a one-on-one and loses at once (S119–123). She says the bout was meant to show she is fine, and guesses that Fuki is upset at having got her partner hurt. Fuki rejects the guess, and Sakura admits that about thirty percent of her (3割くらいは) really thought she could win (S123–124). Fuki remembers nearly dying and acknowledges a debt to Chisato's group while still calling them betrayers of DA. She wants power like Chisato's (アイツほどの力) to protect herself and her peers (S125). Sakura calls the fixation on Chisato clinging (未練がましい) and objects to her carrying the problem alone (S126). Hibana's message raises training for the others. Fuki prescribes more work for Sakura's cheek, offers to join her, and vows they will retrain and crush アイツら; the referent (Chisato's group at S125, the attackers she remembers from Enkūboku, or the trained opponents her new menu envisages) is left open (S127–128). **EXTEND** peer solidarity expressed through rivalry, a correction and a practical commitment. Sakura's challenge is itself care for Fuki. Chisato is both Fuki's model of strength and the object of her grievance. Gratitude has yet to settle Fuki's institutional judgment. |
| 大倉ナタ · *安心しとけバーカ* | S129–136 | Chisato revisits a drawing of her younger self between two adults. Takina points out what the man on the right did to Chisato. Chisato still calls him, Shinji Yoshimatsu, a benefactor and family (S129–130). Takina says that, having nearly lost Chisato, she now understands painfully what it is to care for someone, but that she feels Chisato has been taken from her and is a little frustrated (少し悔しい, S130). Later, Chisato calls **Fuki**, who identifies it as a work phone, to ask whether Takina has been lonely. Fuki recalls Takina asking for the fallen Lycoris' grave locations. She refuses the classified information. Takina separately asks for access to Enkūboku. Fuki calls any equation of the dead with Chisato a nuisance and states that surviving Lycoris should complete as many missions as possible (S133). Takina says that living at LycoReco taught her that her standing depends on her companions' support, and that she wants, by her own thinking (私が私の考えで), to tell the fallen her thanks directly (S134). A memorial visit follows, and Takina acknowledges Fuki's intervention with Kusunoki and the resulting approval (S131–135). Fuki tells Chisato that Takina is steadier than Chisato imagines, with a hundred times Chisato's core, and to tell Takina not to let Chisato's influence make her an idiot. Sakura observes the unusual length of the call (S136). **EXTEND** mourning and lateral advocacy while retaining the classified boundary. Takina calls Fuki surprisingly caring, second in a small unballooned aside to Chisato (S135). Fuki's duty ethic and her verdict on Takina are her own judgments. Takina's explicit words of gratitude at S134 are addressed to the fallen in intention. |
| りた · *ウワサの喫茶リコリコ？* | S137–144 | Kurumi reads posts about the Hawaii café. Ordinary glimpses and incomplete information become stories of Takina the secret agent, Chisato the hula pupil, Mizuki the spy and Kurumi the competitive eater. Set against these are a client's actual message, customers' thanks and requests from Japanese regulars for the café to return. Chisato still wants to help people with the group and chooses a while longer in Hawaii (S138–144). **SOURCE_SPECIFIC.** Each post identifies its speaker's inference. The identities guessed online and the date of return remain open. |

A useful comparison runs between the two stories about Fuki. 幌田 gives her an opportunity to reject Sakura's diagnosis and state her own reason for training. 大倉ナタ lets Fuki tell Chisato about Takina's mourning. Fuki's intervention with Kusunoki complicates her own rebuke of Takina. Combining the two into a single sequence would grant each contributor evidence that only the other supplies.
