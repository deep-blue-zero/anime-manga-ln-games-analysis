# Repeat · contributor readings

GUNP lets Takina see through Chisato's reason for requesting a hug. Takina holds out her arms anyway. In 若鶏にこみ's comic, Takina takes a photograph before a turn of the page reveals how far her unrequested care has gone. In めきめき's, Chisato takes over a morning's work and never learns why Takina slept through it. A gesture acquires its meaning from the particular exchange that precedes it. Fourteen separate comics and five signed illustrations give this first *Repeat* its range.

Some contributors put reciprocity in the answer. Takina can enjoy one more arcade round while objecting to the hundredth, thank Chisato for a fragrance and still contest the bill, or receive a hug and recognize that it served them both. Others make the gap between a response and a motive central. The breakfast story grants access to Takina's thoughts precisely where she withholds an explanation from Chisato. In the tree episode, Chisato objects to Takina's conduct. Takina's definition of kindness cannot decide what Chisato should welcome. None of these creators can supply advance permission in another's story.
