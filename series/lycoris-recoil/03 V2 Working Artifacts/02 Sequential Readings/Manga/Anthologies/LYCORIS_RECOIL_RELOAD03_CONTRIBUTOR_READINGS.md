# Reload 03 · contributor readings

In しーめ’s café scene, Takina can give an ordinary meaning to someone simply calling her name. The hard question is whose call she has learned to understand. On the last page a tiny face inside the balloon identifies Chisato. Kurumi and Mizuki are watching, wondering what she meant. Elsewhere in this book the advantage belongs to the visitor who overhears a conversation, the reader who sees an unidentified opponent return to his car, or the friend who can finally say what bothered her. The thirteen contributors parcel out this knowledge in different ways. Their agreements and contradictions are part of what the collection preserves.

## The opening credit

**いみぎむる · *Short story collection*, S5–16.** The S4 contents list one creator, S5 prints one byline for this collection, and the signed S29 production comment belongs to that same contributor. Each Act heading marks a change of premise within this credit.

| Printed heading | OPF span | Local action and limit |
|---|---|---|
| わらしべ千束 | S5–9 | Chisato trades her remaining potato chips to Mizuki for a mask, then fails to exchange it with anyone else. By the end of the day Takina thinks wearing it for so long means Chisato must like it. The reader knows the failed exchange accounts for the same behavior. |
| バーバーフキ | S10–11 | Sakura asks Fuki to cut her hair, confident in a simple clipper method. The next day Fuki relays Sakura’s refusal to appear in public like this. The comic cuts before the haircut and withholds the result from the reader too. |
| ロボとリス | S12–14 | Robota remembers Majima telling him to eat vegetables. He helps Kurumi reach a high shelf and passes the advice on, taking her for a child. Her choice of snack surprises him. The two can help and misjudge each other without discovering each other’s identity. |
| ミズキドライバー | S15–16 | Takina uses the staff’s real names in a role-playing game. Four party slots leave Mizuki out until Takina finds another character to name. The page turn delivers Mizuki’s protest that her namesake is the horse pulling the wagon. Inclusion as Takina understands it produces the insult. |

**SOURCE_SPECIFIC.** The jokes work through a concealed outcome, a misread reason, a repeated admonition and a belated change of category. They provide neither a second author for an inset nor a continuous sequence across the four.
