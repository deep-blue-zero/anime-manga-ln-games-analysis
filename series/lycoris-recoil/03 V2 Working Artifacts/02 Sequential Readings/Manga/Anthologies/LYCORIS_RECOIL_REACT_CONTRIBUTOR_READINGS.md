# React · contributor readings

**Accepted source-native reading.** The [supplementary reading receipt](../../../07%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/LYCORIS_RECOIL_SUPPLEMENTARY_READING_RECEIPT.md) records whole-volume inspection and independent review.

These seven contributors give the café’s surrounding people unusually different kinds of space. Kusunoki sorts the institution’s common needs and unusual personnel. A disguised visitor tries to thank Takina. Fuki retrieves an early memory. Nii’s suite moves abruptly from comic exaggeration to the administration of casualties. Ino’s volume-ending contribution returns to collective play and the costs of serving customers. The contrast enriches the authority question without making any contributor an alternate witness to a TV event.
