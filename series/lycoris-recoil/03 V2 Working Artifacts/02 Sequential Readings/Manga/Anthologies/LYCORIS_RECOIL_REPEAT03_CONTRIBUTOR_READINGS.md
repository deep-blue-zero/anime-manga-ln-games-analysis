# Repeat 03 · contributor readings

Takina and Chisato each make a different request about photographing Takina. In 稲岡和佐's comic, Takina specifically asks Chisato to take her picture without warning her of the instant. In くぅ's, Takina says no even after Chisato offers to keep the photograph private. A smile at the end of either story cannot stand in for the words spoken before the shutter. Elsewhere, distinct contributors let a reflected dress, a dating profile, a ramen queue or a pile of candles generate equally revealing errors of interpretation.

The difference between the photo stories runs deeper than whose smile looks natural. 稲岡和佐 lets Takina articulate what she wants an image to do for the café group and invent a way to take it. くぅ gives Takina words for why even private use would worry her. Takina does smile after photographing Chisato. Chisato says she missed that expression (S136). Both creators give Takina control of an action. The actions point in opposite directions. Repeated motifs can make the question of consent more salient across licensed works while leaving each participant's answer local to a particular request.
