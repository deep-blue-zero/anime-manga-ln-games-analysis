# Recollect V01 · manga reading

**Accepted source-native reading.** Whole-volume inspection and independent review are documented in the [supplementary reading receipt](../../07%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/LYCORIS_RECOIL_SUPPLEMENTARY_READING_RECEIPT.md).

Abe Kanari repeatedly puts a person’s wrong definition of a task on one side of a page turn and its consequence on the other. A mission briefing becomes a lesson about smiling. Film homework becomes an argument over whether the credits count. A wager supplies a theory of customer service and ends before anyone can collect. The captioned institutional opening makes work at the café look like a departure from the Lycoris role. The chapters then find work, interpretation and disagreement waiting there too.

The two recurrent motifs work on different objects. The film sequence (S14→39→46–52) turns a taste into apparently assigned labor. The optional garment sequence (S74–78→116–122) turns a service idea into a negotiated preference. Pages often make the reader grasp the frame after Takina’s first practical answer. Abe also gives Takina the authority to question that frame or create a deadpan one of her own. Scene repetition in this volume supports a B1 motif. It cannot establish how frequently she literalizes social situations in the originating anime.
