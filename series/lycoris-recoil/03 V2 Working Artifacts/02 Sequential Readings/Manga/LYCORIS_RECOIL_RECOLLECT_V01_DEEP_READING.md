# Recollect V01 · manga reading

Chisato wants to see Takina smile. Takina wants to know who requires it and why. The difference between those questions survives eight chapters. Kurumi decides which café activities she will join. Erika wants to make amends but cannot see the life Takina is leading. A customer can become the subject of a game before anyone has asked what the customer wants. Abe Kanari's comedy repeatedly gives the reader access to another side of an exchange, then leaves the characters to negotiate with the information each actually possesses.

### Chapter by chapter

The subsections below follow each chapter leaf by leaf. In this image EPUB every leaf is a separate screen, so "the next leaf" is the unit of delay; print facing pages are not inferred. Comparisons with A1 cite anchors already used in the TV readings or the crosswalk.

#### 1 · S7–14 · What Takina lacks

Chisato opens in a serious register, chin on hand across a low table, and asks whether Takina knows what she lacks (S7). Takina, with a plaster on her left cheek, answers with her own candidate: mission experience, 任務経験 (S8). Chisato's answer, 笑顔, arrives with a drop into comic register. Takina bows and starts to leave. She had expected advice for a Lycoris and calls the expectation wasted (S9). When Chisato calls her cute and tells her to smile more, Takina names the demand スマハラ, a term Chisato does not know. Takina adds that DA never asked her for a smile. Chisato, still joking, replies that a smile attached to killing would be wrong (殺人に笑顔), and returns to the café (S10). Her analogy is exact: the café uniform is the equipment and the smile is the weapon, said with a poke at Takina's face (S11). Takina will *treat* the reasoning as understood (わかったことにしておきます). She then argues on the café's terms: she does the work she was taught, her expression is not needlessly sullen, and the shop loses nothing. Chisato half-concedes and pouts. Takina's own question about any other reason draws out the personal wish (S12). Takina calls it selfishness, and Chisato does not deny it. Takina then says she smiles when she is having fun (S13).

A silent blank stare from Chisato separates that answer from 言ったな. On S14 Chisato turns Takina's condition into a project of films and manga, and the chapter ends on Takina alone with 意味不明です. Takina keeps です/ます and cuts with short verdicts (タチは悪いですね, S13). Chisato calls herself 千束さん (S14), a line the jacket reuses as its blurb (S124, paratext). The plaster on Takina's cheek, drawn through ch.1 and again at S17, is never explained; S91 gives the volume's only reference to a blow. Her DA-first answer is compatible with the A1 Takina who weighs café life against a return (E03-CLM-002), and dates nothing. Abe adds a sequence in which the smile is refused as a requirement and answered as a wish. The chapter shows no enjoyment yet, and Chisato's poke belongs to the gag register.

#### 2 · S17–22 · Coffee notes and a shampoo

Mika serves Takina the day's cup (S17). She writes the features and her impressions in a notebook, and Mika explains that he has poured one cup a day for some time because knowing the differences can only help (S18). Mizuki doubts that customers can tell; Takina says the coffees differ considerably. Mika states a maxim: without interest or familiarity differences are hard to perceive, but someone who likes a thing can tell. He cites Mizuki's range of drinks, and she agrees about alcohol (S19). Chisato arrives, Mizuki calls her the shop's background music, and Chisato asks for a cup (S20). Chisato then notices Takina's shampoo. Takina says the old bottle ran out yesterday. Chisato asks, admiringly, whether she is someone who does not stick to one brand, and Takina deflates it: she simply has no particular preference (こだわり). Chisato offers her own as a recommendation (S21).

Nobody joins the two halves aloud. A wide panel gives Mika's sidelong look at the pair (S21), and Mizuki watches with narrowed eyes beside a wordless balloon (S22). The adults' silent reactions set Mika's maxim beside Chisato's perception for the reader, who may draw the inference that no character states. Takina's aside, that Chisato could have changed while waiting, keeps her own attention on the schedule (S22). The chapter closes on Mizuki wanting a drink before opening.

Takina studies coffee daily and in writing, and keeps no fixed shampoo. Chisato recommends her own things here, as she recommends films and manga (S14) and later buys clothes for Takina (S104). The maxim names interest or habituation, and Takina's daily cups supply the second, so it can bear on her coffee (結構違うものですよ, S19) as well as on Chisato. The adults' looks are reactions, not reports of belief. No A1 tasting routine is cited here, so the notebook stands as a Recollect-local habit.

#### 3 · S25–40 · Walnut becomes Kurumi in public

A wordless splash and unattributed narration give the legend: Walnut, the world's best hacker, is officially dead and lives at the café as Kurumi (S25–26). Chisato brings a uniform, and Kurumi slides her closet door shut (S27). Her refusal is professional: front-of-house work, 表《みせ》の仕事, lies outside her specialty. Chisato forces the door and cites local custom (S28). Kurumi says the café is too quiet to need her (S29). She emerges in uniform, blaming Chisato's persistence; the uniform and the introduction are pressed on her for cover, and her own terms come later (S39, S78, S116–118). Mika proposes introducing her at least to regulars, and Chisato adds that hiding her would look suspicious (S30). Takina teaches that the job is carrying orders safely to the table (S31). When Gotō, a named regular, arrives, Takina tells Kurumi not to copy Chisato's style (S32).

Mika supplies a cover: the girl is in his care for a while and will sometimes help (S33). Kurumi insists she is not staff and invites Gotō to order sweets for the sales, which she will eat. Gotō laughs, then treats her as an apology for laughing and a welcome (S34), and Mika returns a free second cup (S35). Chisato notices that Kurumi held her spoon in the right hand yesterday and the left today. Kurumi says she has never thought about it (S36), as Takina had no preference about shampoo. Gotō suggests piano training. Chisato supplies the keyboard association, drawn as Kurumi typing, and Kurumi rejects the comparison without answering the piano question (S37).

Gotō proposes the existing game night for introductions, and Chisato describes it as gatherings with regulars after hours or on closing days (S38). Kurumi offers her board games because she has had no one to play with, and more players make them better (S39–40). Takina declines to join because she must watch the films Chisato gave her. Chisato is delighted, then hears 義務感 in the answer (S39). Only here in V01 does Takina say 千束さん, with Gotō in the scene. She shouts a kana ちさと at S64 and uses bare 千束 at S90, S92, S100, S106, S110, S111 and S119. The distribution may reflect a customer's presence; episodic chapters give no dated progression. Gotō never learns who Kurumi is. A1 also has Kurumi as a game player and chosen participant (REL-CAFE-E02-001/E03-001/E04-001), and Takina refusing and then joining the gathering (E03-CLM-016). V01 is compatible with both without being placed against them. Abe adds Kurumi's reasons, a cover offered to a named regular, and a refusal by Takina that rests on Chisato's films.

#### 4 · S43–52 · The films

Three nearly wordless pages follow Takina off shift (S43–45). The title page shows her at home in a T-shirt printed with a grinning flower in sunglasses, and the same figure stands in a pot on her windowsill (S43). Nobody comments on these things, and whether Takina chose them is not shown. At S46 Chisato calls after her with 新しいやつ, "the new ones", so this bag is not the first. Takina pauses before agreeing and is next seen at home after a bath. The bag is labelled 千束セレクション (S47). While the disc plays, two hatch-bordered inset panels show Chisato looking, then a foreground hand with a buttoned cuff aiming a finger-gun at a startled chibi Kurumi and a sweating Chisato in café uniform. They alternate with Takina's narrowing gaze, deliberately illegible film audio and a film still of a gunman (S48). No caption says whether Takina's attention drifts to Chisato or the film's gun hand is transposed onto the café, which would fit the training premise of S51. At S49 Takina sways with the gunfire. On the table lies a notebook labelled 映画ノート, and the only words, presumably Takina's, are a small objection to plausibility, いやムリでしょ. Black bars and a daylight exterior close the night on the same leaf.

A later day opens on Chisato's outburst about the skipped ending, with Kurumi startled in the background. Takina answers that the credits are irrelevant to the story (S50). Chisato insists that a film lasts until the credits finish. Takina gives a second reason: there were too many films to meet the quota, ノルマ, without skipping. She returns the bag as fully watched and asks whether this was not image training, イメージ訓練. Chisato denies it outright, dropping into ちっげぇーよ (S51). Only now does Chisato understand why all of Takina's impressions had taken a combat-practical view (S52). Takina says she deserves the sympathy for having watched seriously as training. Mika agrees that "that much every time" (毎回) is a lot, and Chisato protests that she curated it (S52).

Before the explanation, the reader has seen a body that sways, a notebook and a plausibility objection. Chisato had been hearing practical feedback without its premise, and neither knew the other's assumption until S51. ノルマ is Takina's word, and Chisato rejects it. No panel shows Takina smiling at a film. The notebook joins the coffee notes of S18 as a written study habit. A1's Takina measures leisure by its use for a return (E03-CLM-002); Abe gives that disposition a domestic object and a single mistaken premise.

#### 5 · S55–66 · Erika's letter and the game night

The title page sets Erika in the foreground, hand at her chest, with a long-haired navy figure seen from behind in a separate inset (S55). At HQ Erika sighs over when she will next see someone the page has not yet named (S56). She wonders whether there will even be a chance; Hibana turns in her chair and teases her as a girl in love, which Erika denies (S57). Hibana reads HQ's attitude (あの様子) as showing no intention to return Takina, and states that an unauthorized visit outside a mission breaches discipline. Erika will not cause trouble for Hibana (S58). She has not yet thanked or apologized (S59). Hibana suggests a letter, since paper might pass the package-inspection gate, hedged with たぶん (S59). An imagined Erika plans a letter now and a conversation later (S60). Nobody knows Takina's address. The café's public SNS account gives none, and Hibana finds the account irregular for a DA branch (S61). Erika thinks of asking Fuki (S61). Hibana warns that asking would expose the plan and that Takina's name is taboo to Fuki at present; an inset beside the warning shows Fuki with a dressed cheek (S62). Erika drops the letter because no reply would come and she would keep waiting for one. She will wait and say it in person (S63). Hibana's ラブレター renames a letter Erika has just abandoned.

The cut goes where Erika cannot. The café is closed for the night, regulars are laughing, and Takina calls Chisato's name in anger (S64). In Lycoris uniform after hours, Takina calls a move cheating. Chisato says the rules allow it, and Takina claims the rules for herself. Kurumi announces that she is the game master, and both appeal to her (S65). Ch.3's proposed game night has become a gathering in which Kurumi judges. She rules for Takina this time, and Chisato complains of favoritism. Takina smiles, eyes closed and faintly flushed: ルールの勝利です (S66). A silent close-up of Mizuki's eye precedes her remark, with no lettered subject, that they have really opened up, and Mika agrees and tells her not to tease (S66).

Erika's prediction that Takina would not reply is her own model of Takina, untested here. The café party knows nothing of Erika. Reading this as the smile Takina's S13 condition promised is an inference; pride in a ruling is a rival reading. Chisato, sulking with her eyes shut, is not shown noticing it, and nobody names it. One panel is not a base rate. A1 has Erika defend Takina in E03, apologize in E11 and receive thanks in E12 (REL-TAK-ERI-E03-001/E11-001/E12-001). V01's unapologized state is compatible with A1 and places the chapter against no episode. The gate, the discipline rule and the SNS account are Abe's institutional additions.

#### 6 · S69–78 · Changing for summer

Chisato arrives out of the wind with a leaf in her ribbon and a short-sleeved uniform (S69–70). She explains that Lycoris change when ordinary students change so that they can blend in (S71). She turns to Kurumi's unchanging clothes. Kurumi calls them "the same but different", and a panel shows a rail of identical hoodies. She rarely goes out, and the indoors is air-conditioned. Chisato finds this lacking in 風情 (S72) and greets Takina's summer uniform with delight (S73). Kurumi returns the question: does the café uniform change (S74)?

The proposal is Chisato's. A limited change of about a week would give the shop a seasonal feel, and Mika chooses yukata as summery and in keeping with the café (S75). Asked whether she has worn one, Takina hedges with 記憶には, and Chisato offers to dress her (S75). Mika sets the practical terms. The garments must be prepared, and the season will suit them better once the rains end. Chisato adds the summer festival. Mika offers to hear preferences of color and pattern, and Mizuki calls him a doting parent (S76). Kurumi declines because she will not work the floor or go to the festival in one. Mika answers that without a preference she would get Chisato's old yukata altered (S77). Mizuki urges her to please "grandpa". Mika objects to the title, will not force her and will prepare one anyway (S78). Kurumi, slightly smiling and flushed, calls him an eccentric. She reasons that if one is being prepared she should choose, and asks for a catalogue. Chisato reads enthusiasm, and Kurumi denies it (S78).

Kurumi's request is a choice within an arrangement she cannot stop, not consent to wear the garment, and her refusal of floor and festival stands on the page. Mika has kept an old yukata of Chisato's (千束の昔の); that it dates from her childhood is inferred from its alteration for Kurumi. Takina's uncertain memory is her own, unexplained report. Nothing here dates the chapter against A1.

#### 7 · S81–94 · The rainy-day wager

The wordless title page puts Chisato skipping under a spotted umbrella beside an impassive Takina carrying shopping (S81). The rainy season has begun (S82); S76 had set the yukata for its end. Against Mizuki's complaint about trade, Chisato sketches a stranger who shelters in a café and becomes a regular (S83). Takina finds that unlikely in steady rain, and Mika agrees (S84). After a time skip Chisato proposes a game: guess whether the first customer will be a man or a woman (S84–85).

Takina's objections arrive in order. They are on duty (S85). What is the point (S86)? She explains why rain keeps people in unless their 仕事 compels them, the word read にんむ, "mission", while Chisato's 店 is read うち (S87). Chisato wants to serve those who come anyway. Takina asks whether that service becomes the answer to a staff game the customer knows nothing about. Chisato herself says that involving customers unasked is very rude and that she knows it, and Takina calls it the worst (S88). The plan is to explain, apologize and give a free item of the customer's choice. Takina objects that they cannot give away something that affects the takings; Chisato answers that the loser will pay out of pocket, which Takina has just grasped (S89). Seeing nothing in it for herself, Takina finds Chisato's offer of one wish 微妙, and asks whether Chisato gets nothing if she wins (S90). Chisato asks whether Takina is offering something, and Takina says いえ なにも. Chisato replies that she proposed the game and that Takina's playing along is enough to please her, and a silent profile of Takina follows (S91). Takina's next concern is a customer who stays offended despite the apology. Chisato asks why someone who can think that far once said things that got her hit by Fuki, and Takina says she only stated facts (S91). In Chisato's experience the first rainy-day customer is usually a regular who knows the café's mood. Takina notes that this knowledge favors Chisato (S92). Chisato then adds the newcomer clause herself, with no game and a welcome item; 割り勘で is most likely hers. Takina accepts and is given the first choice (S93).

Her answer is held for the next leaf, where the door cuts her off at だん… (S94). The visitor, tagged as a manga artist, says that work does not go well at home and that she came out despite the rain. After a silent panel Takina turns away, says 女性です and claims she had not finished. Chisato calls no count. The visitor speaks as a habitual visitor known to both girls, so the newcomer clause probably does not apply (inference). Chisato's own guess is never voiced, and the visitor is not shown learning of the game. Takina, who claimed the rules at S65, wins here by a technicality Chisato rejects; the parallel is the reader's.

Takina's question about Chisato's stake, and the answer, were omitted from the earlier reading. S91 is the volume's only reference to Fuki hitting Takina. It invites a reading of Takina's plaster (S7–17) as aftermath, which no page states. V01 gives no cause for the dressing on Fuki's cheek in the inset at S62. The reference is compatible with A1's E01 strike (E01 reading §5.3) and dates no chapter. A manga-artist regular belongs to A1 (E07 reading §§5, 21), and V01 does not name this one.

#### 8 · S97–112 · The nursery pool

After a departure check at 8:25 (S97), Chisato hands Takina a heavy cloth bag (S98). The title page belongs to the absent Kurumi: 今日は休み (S99). Takina asks what the bag holds. Chisato calls it a gift for their destination and gives a riddle: round and packed with red fruit (S100). Takina's 首ですね draws Chisato's protest about warlords. Smug and flushed, Takina says that she too can make a joke and that it is too big for a head. Chisato registers a change, 言うようになったな, but says it does not sound like a joke from Takina (S101). The children greet Chisato by name and Takina as たきなおねーちゃん, and an older staff member welcomes both (S102). Chisato presents the gift in words while Takina holds the bag, and a silent full-width panel discloses a whole watermelon. Takina already knows that the first job is cleaning the pool (S103).

Chisato produces clothes "you can get wet in", bought a while ago because she thought they would suit Takina (S104). She applies sunscreen by squeezing Takina's cheeks, and Takina covers up against the strong sun (S105). When Takina says a children's pool hardly needs fighting spirit, Chisato states her rule that full effort in small things is the secret of enjoying life. Takina replies that this is very like Chisato (S106). Chisato sprays her and slips while Takina keeps scrubbing (S107), and the clean pool gets a silent panel (S108).

In the water Takina gathers the children with water pistols to teach grip and aim, and Chisato forbids serious training (S109). A boy challenges Chisato, who accepts and theatrically takes the hit. Takina's remark that Chisato is hard to hit shares the panel with that hit, and her next close-up is a shocked !? (S110). She runs in. Chisato hails her as rescue, but Takina fires at Chisato, who dodges everything. Takina cannot accept the result even against a child and wants to land one herself, and Chisato calls her 負けず嫌い, one who hates to lose (S111). The children's accounts differ. A boy says Takina is still green, and a girl admires Chisato's dodging (S112). The watermelon draws cheers. Takina asks whether it will not be cut, and the reply that a nursery would hardly keep a kitchen knife most likely comes from the staff member. Chisato concedes a wrong choice and settles for the children's delight (S112).

Chisato chooses the gift and the clothes in advance and alone, and the practical question comes from Takina afterward. Chisato appears to lose on purpose to a child (inference from S110–111) and refuses to lose to Takina, whose stated motives are that she cannot accept the result and wants to score herself. Takina's offer to teach aiming brings DA competence into children's play, and Chisato objects to it. A1 grounds Chisato's evasion in reading the line and timing of fire (E03-CLM-015). Here the same skill serves a gag and a refusal.

#### Bonus · S115–122 · Trying on yukata

The opener is a wordless portrait of Mizuki in kimono, raising a sake cup against fireworks (S115). Kurumi reopens the yukata purchase. Mika says he wants them to try the garments on and decide (S116). Chisato looks for the yukata and finds a headset. Kurumi explains that a fitting needs no physical object and that samples have been entered as data (S117). Takina tries first. A full-height panel shows her avatar, and she calls it amazing. Mizuki points out that Kurumi is enjoying this despite her talk. Kurumi, flushed, says she made the device because she was bored (S118).

Chisato asks to see what Takina sees, and Kurumi shares the screen (S119). Chisato's ワァオ プリティキュート is followed by advice on Takina's color and two candidate patterns. Takina lifts the headset and gives Chisato the next turn (S119). Chisato finds it futuristic that choosing completes the change, and Kurumi notes that nothing changes in reality. Chisato decides quickly on a clover pattern, announcing it with アタシ. Takina expected more deliberation, and Chisato explains that she had narrowed her candidates beforehand (S120). Chisato calls Kurumi and Mizuki next, and both avatars are drawn, Kurumi's in a cloud pattern (S121). Mizuki sighs that she has no man to show hers to. Kurumi offers a virtual one, and Mizuki refuses in tears (S121). Mika asks whether they have decided, and Takina says yes. Mika is then revealed in an actual striped yukata, because it is a little hot. Chisato complains that Sensei got there first, and Mizuki says he looks no different (S122).

The largest frames go to virtual garments (S118, S120). The material one comes last, worn by Mika, who arranged the fitting. Kurumi has moved from "no need" (S77) to building the device and taking a virtual turn. Actual wear and the festival lie outside V01. Takina's final choice, and whether it follows Chisato's advice, is not shown. How Chisato narrowed her candidates is not shown; S78 shows only a request for a catalogue.
