# Recollect V01 · manga reading

Chisato wants to see Takina smile. Takina wants to know who requires it and why. The difference between those questions survives eight chapters. Kurumi decides which café activities she will join. Erika wants to make amends but cannot see the life Takina is leading. A customer can become the subject of a game before anyone has asked what the customer wants. Abe Kanari's comedy repeatedly gives the reader access to another side of an exchange, then leaves the characters to negotiate with the information each actually possesses.

## Chapter reading

| Component | What happens and who knows it |
|---|---|
| 1 · S3–14 | The institutional opening and close-ups of equipment prepare a professional explanation for Takina's supposed deficiency. Chisato supplies the word “smile.” Takina challenges a service demand with スマハラ. Chisato likens choosing equipment for different DA assignments to carrying a smile for café work. Takina understands the analogy and says she has been doing the job without one (S10–12). Pressed, Chisato admits that she personally wants to see the expression. Takina says she smiles when she is enjoying herself (S7–14). The exchange gives her authority over the conditions of her response. Chisato's final plan still awaits a result. |
| 2 · S17–22 | Mika lets Takina compare coffees and record impressions. Mizuki's ability to distinguish drinks offers another route to understanding the exercise. Chisato notices that Takina's shampoo has changed, and Takina explains that the old bottle ran out. The juxtaposition pairs two forms of attentiveness. The explanation comes from the person who made the change and gives the reader a specific reason to resist a more elaborate account of her intention. |
| 3 · S25–40 | The public legend of Walnut gives way to a sliding door and a reluctant café worker. Mika limits the proposed introduction, and Takina shows Kurumi how to serve. Gotō treats Kurumi to dessert and Mika offers the regular a coffee. Chisato notices Kurumi switching her spoon hand. Gotō guesses at piano training, while the reader sees an association with a keyboard. At S38 Gotō suggests using the existing game night for introductions. At S39 Kurumi offers her own board games and says she lacks people to play with. Public table service and chosen participation in an after-hours group make different demands on her. |
| 4 · S43–52 | The packet of Chisato's films travels home with Takina. She leans with the action on the screen and then questions its plausibility (S48–49). Daylight and conversation replace the domestic sequence. Takina has treated the stack as an assignment in visualization. She deliberately skipped the credits to fit the films in and because she considers them irrelevant to the story. Chisato had set no quota and wanted her to experience the credits too. Takina talks about practical impressions, and Mika acknowledges the size of the stack (S50–52). The chapter earns its disagreement by first letting us see how actively Takina watched. |
| 5 · S55–66 | Erika wants to thank Takina and apologize. An unauthorized visit could cause trouble. Hibana proposes a letter, but they lack the address, and waiting for a reply would give Erika a new worry. Erika prefers to speak in person when she can. Hibana teases her about love, and Erika denies it (S59–63). The cut then takes the reader somewhere Erika cannot go. At the café Takina and Chisato dispute a game rule and ask Kurumi to judge (S64–66). Mizuki remarks on how much the girls have opened up to each other. Mika agrees and asks Mizuki to refrain from teasing them (S66). Erika has yet to visit, send a letter or receive an answer. |
| 6 · S69–78 | The explanation for a summer uniform is to blend in with ordinary students. Kurumi's repeated identical outfits set up a different wardrobe argument. Mika offers a period for wearing yukata after the rains and leaves staff a choice. Kurumi, who has challenged the need to dress for public work or a festival, asks for the catalogue. The request is the visible change in her position. |
| 7 · S81–94 | Rain and an empty room give Chisato time to invent a wager on the first customer. Chisato proposes explaining the circumstances, apologizing and offering the customer's choice. She assigns the out-of-pocket cost to the loser (S89). Takina questions the right to involve someone unasked and presses the possibility that even a regular may object. For a newcomer the two expressly cancel the wager and split the price of a welcome item (S88–93). S94 interrupts Takina mid-word with the arrival of a woman identified on the page as a manga artist. Takina completes a different guess, claiming she had yet to finish, and Chisato contests it. The reader gets the verbal payoff. Customer assent, selection and payment remain unshown. |
| 8 · S97–112 | A heavy bag sustains Takina's deliberately grim joke until the watermelon is disclosed. The girls help to clean the children's pool, then join the water game. Chisato plays as though outmatched. Takina calls out and enters the game to try for a hit on Chisato. At S112 the children give differing accounts of skill and outcome. Their delight at the melon also precedes Takina's question about how to cut it at the nursery. The gift's social success and its practical inconvenience both belong in the reading. |
| Bonus · S115–122 | The fireworks opener frames the return to the yukata plan. Kurumi supplies a virtual fitting device. Chisato asks to see Takina's view, and Kurumi enables sharing. While Takina is trying on the avatar outfit, Chisato suggests a color for her and considers two patterns. Takina then offers Chisato the next turn. Chisato says she has made her choice and had already narrowed her options (S119–120). Mizuki's complaint that she has no man to show her outfit to prompts Kurumi to offer a virtual one. Mizuki refuses that offer (S121). Mika appears already wearing an actual yukata and cites the heat (S122). Fireworks attendance and actual future wear by the others remain open. |

The film pile acquires a history across S14, S39 and S46–52. Takina first misunderstands the expected labor, yet her moving body in front of the screen makes mere compliance too thin a description. The clothes similarly change meaning between S74–78 and S116–122. Mika offers a choice, Kurumi finds a way to try it, and Takina and Chisato take turns. Abe gives access to participation decision by decision. An invitation, an accepted turn, shared viewing and an actual change of clothes each require their own page evidence.
