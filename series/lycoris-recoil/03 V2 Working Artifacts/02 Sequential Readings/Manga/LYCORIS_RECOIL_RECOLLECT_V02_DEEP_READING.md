# Recollect V02 · manga reading

What does it cost to call someone a partner when she may leave? Chisato wants the two girls to make up for each other's weaknesses. Takina points out that returning to headquarters would take her away. The book finds less final ways to answer. Takina pauses a film when Chisato gets up. As a guest in Chisato's home, she cooks their soba and asks Chisato to make it next year. Abe Kanari gives particular weight to the distinction between an act that another person can see and the motive that person reads into it.
