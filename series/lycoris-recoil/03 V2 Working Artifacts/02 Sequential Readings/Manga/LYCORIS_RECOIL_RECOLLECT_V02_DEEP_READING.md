# Recollect V02 · manga reading

What does it cost to call someone a partner when she may leave? Chisato wants the two girls to make up for each other's weaknesses. Takina points out that returning to headquarters would take her away. The book finds less final ways to answer. Takina pauses a film when Chisato gets up. As a guest in Chisato's home, she cooks their soba and vows to make Chisato cook it next year. Abe Kanari gives particular weight to the distinction between an act that another person can see and the motive that person reads into it.

### 9 · S5–14 · The range under the café

A caption places the scene in the café's underground range (S6). Takina puts a tight group into a silhouette target, and Chisato salutes her accuracy as usual (S7). Chisato admits she lacks confidence as a shot. Takina attributes the problem to Chisato's special rounds, shown in an inset as rubber frangible ammunition, and says she could not hit with them either (S8). With live rounds, Takina says, Chisato could match her. Chisato concedes a static target at that range but calls Takina's shot at a moving drone another dimension (S9). Takina's diagnosis is 訓練不足, and the training should at least double; Chisato replies that Takina overdoes hers (S9).

Chisato then gives her reason. Raising her accuracy in training is pointless when she never uses live rounds in real combat. Takina accepts the premise (それはそうですけど) (S10). A tall screentoned panel holds Chisato's next admission: with Takina here, she may be leaning on her a little (甘えてる…少しは…かもね) (S10). Only then comes the formula that partners cover each other's gaps (S10–11). Takina asks what happens when she is gone, and Chisato's comic tears follow. Takina states the fact: returning to headquarters means leaving (S11). After the turn Chisato says she supports that wish, then repeats it in a drawn-out sulk (応援してるけどォ～～…). Takina answers with a silent balloon, her face cropped out of the panel (S12). On the stairs she announces a correction and, on the facing page, denies having any gaps at all (S12–13). The bickering ends in chibi exchanges. After the next turn Kurumi, at the doorway, hears only the noise and asks what the two are talking about (S14).

The chapter separates Chisato's reasons from her feelings: a practical rationale, then a hedged dependence, then a theory of partnership. Takina grants the rationale, quantifies the deficit and refuses the role of the partner with gaps. She never answers the departure question in feeling, and her silence and change of subject leave her motive unstated. Chisato's support of the DA return is sincere enough to be said twice and reluctant enough to trail off. Takina keeps polite forms even while bickering (ありませんよ / ありませんね); Chisato's register is casual and theatrical. Nonlethal rounds and the drone shot are A1 premises (E01's 非殺傷弾 and the drone; E02's rubber rounds). Abe adds Chisato's own account of why accuracy training does not serve her purposes and her admission of reliance. The scene ends in disagreement about competence, not in a partnership vow.

### 10 · S17–34 · Summer horror night

Before the title page, Chisato in yukata announces a special summer horror edition of game night, and two regulars are introduced by caption: the manga artist Itō and the university student Kitamura (S17). The title illustration shows Kurumi in a yukata outdoors, outside the café sequence (S18). In the café, the staff wear yukata. Kurumi shrugs with a sweat drop; an untailed いやぁ in the facing panel has no lettered speaker. The regular Yamadera compliments everyone (S19). Kitamura, short of money and calling the café her only entertainment, asks Mika for a job in its uniform (S20–21). Mika says the café is not busy enough, and Mizuki adds that it is already in the red. Itō prefers a quiet café to work in, Yamadera fears fewer game nights, and Kitamura reverses herself (S21–22). The café's finances are discussed openly.

Chisato, torch under her chin, announces the guests' stories while Takina lights the candle (S23); the narrator is an AI (S24). She names it クル死ミ; Kurumi disowns the name (S24). Kurumi explains that the system fills in submitted stories and generates video (S25), so the black-margin pages are the system's narration and pictures, shown on a screen. Kitamura recognizes the first title as her own (S26). The story runs to a balcony death on a left-hand page (S29). After the turn Mizuki, calling it a friend's experience, objects that the friend has died (S30). Kitamura says her contribution ended at the shoes and the rest is the system's embellishment. In a small aside she adds that even the shoes were fiction told as true (実話風フィクション) (S30).

Takina then asks whether guns work on ghosts (S31). Chisato chokes. When Takina says ghosts would be harder than humans one can deal with, Chisato's aside records her relief that the question is too outlandish to be taken as anything but fantasy. Itō says photographs damage ghosts; Takina thanks her for the lesson and asks where such cameras are issued, and Kurumi twice says that is a game (S31). Takina asks sincerely; Chisato monitors the cover.

A regular in a hat asks for a story made from scratch. Kurumi warns that the system was built in a hurry, trained only on occult channels and blogs, and agrees without promising much (期待すんなよ) (S32). Itō suggests the keyword 喫茶店. The result is titled 喫茶リコリコ; Kurumi says it also learned from her data, and its narrator promises a secret that cannot be shown publicly (S33). The turn brings a full-width panel of Mika smashing the laptop as the balloon breaks off at 秘密組織ディッ. He blames a mosquito, Kurumi protests the broken monitor, and Mizuki's thought assigns the fault to Kurumi (S34). Mizuki's blame has a basis Kurumi has just supplied. The guests react to Mika's violence, not to the words. Whether Kurumi chose the yukata gladly is not stated in this chapter.

### 11 · S37–48 · Headquarters

At the DA dormitory Sakura asks whether there has been another attack. Fuki names the リコリス狩り and counts the fourth victim (S38). When Sakura scorns the victims, Fuki says Lycoris are basically excellent. An inset caricatures Chisato and Takina as rule-breaking or incomprehensible fools (バカA, バカB), and a muttered ムカツクけどな follows, but Fuki calls their ability certain (S39). If two such people (二人も) have been taken, the enemy holds information, something that can identify Lycoris. Fuki breaks off at 下手すりゃ…, and when Sakura asks, she says it is nothing (S40). The page does not reconcile "two" with the fourth victim. She warns that the enemy chooses its ground and that numbers beat skill, including her own (一騎当千 is fiction) (S41).

In a dormitory room a Lycoris with a hairband mentions Fuki's warning to the freckled girl whom Fuki later names Erika (S42, S47). V02 never letters the hairband girl's name; Hibana is a project-level identification (A1 E01 and E11 subtitles, the V01 reading). Erika says their work was never safe; Hibana replies that Lycoris had not been targets (S43). Erika worries about Takina, who is always outside headquarters. Hibana asks whether this is the time to worry about others and says they must look to themselves first (S43–44). Erika reasons that the victims were on solo missions, so pairs or more would be wiser. She then notes that missions are not theirs to decide, and Hibana counsels trust in DA's allocation (S44–45).

The next panel cuts to the café telephone. Mika, his back turned, says Kusunoki has already told him something and thanks the caller (S45). The page turns. The caller's sleeve and figure are labeled フキ before Mika invites her to come for sweets at his expense (S46). A close-up then hatches Fuki's face, eyes lowered, and she closes with 失礼します 先生 (S46). In the courtyard Fuki finds Erika alone, tells her the warning has reached her and that next time no one will be there to help, and on Erika's question about her red face says it is nothing (S47–48). Erika has no access to the call. Fuki's warning presupposes an earlier rescue that V02 does not specify. Fuki's register divides cleanly: rough ねぇ and だな to Sakura and Erika, 先生 and 失礼します to Mika. She uses the address in A1 E09 and Short 05, and the closing in E09. The flush is drawn within the call, immediately after the invitation, so the call is its shown occasion. Its feeling remains unnamed. A1 E06 opens with the same fourth attack. Abe adds Fuki's analysis and Erika's reasoning.

### 12 · S51–70 · Watch shifts and a film

Before the title page, Takina reports by phone that all four Lycoris were attacked by many assailants while working alone (そうです marks hearsay) and proposes acting as a pair from today (S51). The doorbell rings while Chisato is still on the call. Takina stands at the door with a bag and assigns the night's alternating sleep; Chisato lights up at a sleepover (S52). The plan and the arrival coincide, so Chisato's consent follows the fact. Takina's line repeats A1 E06's. Her rule of acting in pairs is the remedy Erika proposed at headquarters (S44); neither knows of the other's reasoning, though the reader does.

Several days later the chore chart contains only Takina's name, and narration boxes give her first-person account: she does all the housework, she cannot beat Chisato at rock-paper-scissors, and the streak is statistically odd. She tested herself against Kurumi and did not keep losing, so she is not especially weak (S54–55). A thought balloon adds a plan: after a few more losses she will consult Mika and the others (S56). These boxes are rare B1 access to Takina's reasoning in her own voice, and that reasoning is empirical.

Dinner talk is drawn in pictogram balloons (S57). After a bath Chisato demands a film (S58–59). Takina tells her to sleep while she keeps watch and offers sleeping medicine. Takina objects that Chisato watched a film earlier; Chisato answers that this is a different one and that she wants to watch it *with Takina*, and Takina allows one (S60). She then enforces the shift: since Chisato chose to cut her own sleep, Takina will sleep when it ends (S61). On the sofa Chisato defends losing sleep to films: a life holds only so many (S62). Takina imagines Chisato as a director if she were not a Lycoris and rejects actress outright (それはないです) (S62). When Chisato praises directors' travel, Takina allows that they go places but calls it work. Chisato says that once you go, it is yours; Takina names the principle as やりたいこと最優先, and Chisato ratifies it. Takina concludes that Chisato is unsuited to directing too. When Chisato falls back on actress, Takina sends her to bed (夢を見るなら寝てください), and Chisato calls her cruel (S63). This is the A1 motto of E03 and E04, revisited. Takina turns Chisato's motto against the career she has just imagined for her, and her punchline enforces the sleep rotation she set at S61.

Chisato remembers what scene comes next and goes to the toilet (S64). There she says watching it with Takina would be a little awkward and wonders how Takina reacts, picturing a blank face (S65). When she returns, Takina answers from behind, her face withheld. After the turn the screen shows the pause symbol, and Takina says she stopped it when Chisato left her seat (S66). Chisato screams. Her praise comes in flat katakana (キガキクナータキナハ), and Takina, smiling with closed eyes and a small blush mark, says 当然です (S66). She accepts the description of her act as considerate and calls it a matter of course. Chisato's reason for leaving reached her only as a toilet trip (S64); the awkwardness stays in Chisato's private speech (S65). Three identical wide panels follow. Takina's face does not change, Chisato flushes, fidgets and squeezes her eyes shut, and the kiss exists only as sound lettering (S67). Takina then observes that the films Chisato hands her often contain such scenes and asks whether she likes them. Chisato denies it. Takina asks what intention puts them there, and Chisato asks to drop the subject (S68). Chisato's prediction of no reaction is right about the face and wrong about the attention. Whether Takina suspected why Chisato left is not lettered; the comedy depends on her not knowing.

Asked about travel, Takina says she does not particularly want to go. She allows that it would probably be fun (S69), but after the turn she adds that she cannot picture it (S70). Chisato says it would be nice to go someday (いつか行けるといいよねー). The line has no subject, and no "together" is lettered. She suggests that Kurumi could fix things, perhaps a long holiday in Hawaii. Takina grants that the passport problem could be solved and says other problems remain. For now (とりあえず) she would rather return to DA, and Chisato's only answer is なんだかなぁ (S70). Any echo of A1 E13's Hawaii belongs to a retrospective reader.

### 13 · S73–84 · The doki game

Before the title page Chisato claps to start a ドキドキゲーム, and Takina objects to an unexplained game (S73). Chisato says their life lacks romance. Kurumi explains that Chisato has been reading shōjo manga and warns Chisato against becoming like Mizuki, who protests (S75). Chisato asks Takina to make her heart race. Takina answers that Lycoris do not need romance and objects that no one could judge whether Chisato's heart raced, so the game fails. Chisato responds by setting the rule: she will say ドキッ when it happens (S76). Takina, unsure what moves Chisato, is told to try what would move herself (S77).

While Takina is away, Chisato tells Kurumi her plan. From observation and from living together (同棲), she knows that Takina idles without knowing what to do and will then go along with trivial things (S78–80). The illustrated "standby mode" belongs to Chisato's account (S79). Takina returns and levels a water pistol at Chisato (S80). She explains that a muzzle pointed at her quickens her own pulse and judges the water pistol ineffective. Chisato says that pulse is certainly not romance, and Mizuki privately counts it as progress that no real gun appeared during business hours (S81). After the turn Takina shows Chisato an article. Chisato is eager to know what Takina finds interesting, reads 縄文土器 aloud and wonders whether Takina secretly loves history, while a close-up shows Takina's smirk (S82). On the facing page Takina claims the word and, framed by a large どやァ with a smile and blush, the win (S83). A silent Chisato then appears in profile against screentone densely lettered ドキ and ドキドキ before she shouts 縄文ドキ (S83). That lettering may echo the word she has just said or stand for a heartbeat. The page does not settle which, and Chisato denies any tokimeki on the next page. After the turn she protests that she felt nothing. Takina replies that a ドキ is a ドキ, that she too is learning (私だって学習しているんですよ), that against Chisato an indirect method beats a frontal one, and that Chisato's own rock-paper-scissors wins were near-cheating (S84). Chisato wishes she had made the forbidden word トゥンク; Kurumi says her terms were careless (S84).

Takina turned an emotional goal into a checkable one by objecting. Chisato supplied the token, and Takina then exploited it, using Chisato's curiosity about her tastes as the bait. Her knowledge that Chisato wins by something like cheating is new against Chapter 12's statistical puzzlement. In V02 the only pointer is her plan to consult the adults (S56), which is never shown; A1 E06 supplies a route that cannot be imported as a V02 event. The chapter's other heart images are Chisato's hand-heart with a small drawn heart (S76) and Takina's own 鼓動の高鳴り under a muzzle (S81). Nothing lettered in the chapter refers to Chisato's heart condition. Neither page settles what Chisato felt or whether the joke carries any romantic intent.

### 14 · S87–102 · Code name G

Chisato sees off the last customer and, alone at the door, smiles without a word before turning the sign to CLOSED (S87). The title page then gives Mika a grim, sweating profile in the volume's most realistic register, a serious frame for a comic cause (S88). After closing Chisato goes for water, and only she sees something move (S89–90). One shout of 敵襲 spans reaction panels of Takina, Mika and Mizuki. No tail attributes it, though context makes it Chisato's. Takina bursts in with her pistol drawn and finds Mika and Mizuki already slumped (S91). She asks whether the enemy is the male Lycoris mentioned before (前に言ってた男版リコリス, ruby リリベル) (S92). No subject is lettered. Chisato, whom she addresses, is the natural referent, and A1 E06 shows Chisato telling Takina about Lilybell. The page itself fixes only Takina's knowledge, and Chisato does not explain the term. She describes the enemy over a censored silhouette (S92), forbids its name in a restaurant and assigns the code name G. Takina asks whether Chisato is enjoying this, and Mika mutters that he was careful, yet one appeared (S93). Takina is surprised that everyone fears insects. Chisato says the problem is having seen one in a restaurant, with game night due the next morning (S94). When Takina proposes calling its name to draw it out, Chisato says bugs do not come when called, and a large hand-lettered aside from Takina notes the contradiction (S94).

With Kurumi asleep after late nights (S95), Takina asks whether "命を大事に" applies this time too, a question that ends the left-hand page (S95). After the turn come Chisato's wide-eyed close-up, a silent two-shot, two swirling panels and then やむを得ん; Takina answers 了解 (S96). Chisato adds that putting it outside would be fine if possible, and Takina says that is harder than with humans (S97). The search finds nothing. Takina suggests a misperception, but Mika says that if Chisato's eyes saw it, it was there. He apologizes for losing his composure and says Mizuki has gone to buy countermeasures (S98). Mika plans traps and a cleaning, and Chisato groans about overtime (S99).

Kurumi wakes up and says there is no G (S99–100). For some time, she explains, she has been building a small drone to monitor for pests, to contribute to the shop for once (S100). She finished it that morning and checked the premises on its test run (S101). It can spray strong insecticide on discovery and repels intruders as it patrols. Chisato calls the build quietly impressive and wonders aloud about commercializing it; she says nothing about the insecticide (S101). When Chisato asks what she saw, Kurumi says it was probably the drone. A censored robot then scuttles past, and Chisato recognizes it (S101). After the turn Mizuki returns and yells at the design. Kurumi says she modified a commercial G robot because the shape suits places where pests hide. Mizuki answers that a lookalike roaming the café is no different from the real thing (S102).

No actual pest is shown, and Kurumi's drone reports none, so Chisato's answer stays hypothetical. Its form is still informative. Takina treats the principle as a rule with a scope, Chisato decides visibly and reluctantly, and the household's own device is built to kill pests. Mika never sights the object. Address terms record different histories with him: Chisato's センセ, Takina's 店長 and Fuki's 先生 (S46, S91–98).

### 15 · S105–112 · Fifteenth night

A caption marks Chisato's home over an unfurnished view, followed by the same angle furnished, with Chisato seated (S105). The page does not state how the two states relate. The title page shows Takina in an apron holding eggs and thinking of an unlettered glossy sphere that can be read as yolk or moon (S106). Takina serves moon-viewing soba and asks whether a guest normally cooks the dinner (S107–108). Chisato first blames the rain that hides the moon, then corrects herself: Takina cooks because she lost at rock-paper-scissors. An aside from Takina says the contest itself is wrong (S108). Chisato praises her for breaking the sure-win method (必勝法) but warns against overconfidence. Takina says she was confident. Over empty bowls she asks what one does on 十五夜 (S109). Chisato proposes a film; Takina says they always do that. Chisato cannot say what moon-viewing was for beyond a guess at harvest prayers or thanks (S110). Chisato defends enjoying customs without knowing them. This year's 十五夜 is the day for soba and a film with Takina. She does not know how next year will go, and they can decide each time (S111). Takina says she has glimpsed the origin of cultural decay (S111).

After the turn Chisato vows to research a perfect moon-viewing for next year, and Takina predicts she will forget by tomorrow (S112). Takina then adds something of her own. Over an exterior panel of cloud with a faint moon behind it, she vows that next year she will make Chisato cook the soba (来年こそ…作らせます). Chisato, grinning in chibi, protests playfully with そんなことぉ～? (S112). Under the janken custom (S108–109), the vow implies a contest to win rather than a polite request. The moon is visible only to the reader. Chisato's uncertainty about next year carries no medical reference here. By inference, the vow presupposes a next 十五夜 in Chisato's home, while Takina has twice named a return to headquarters or DA as her goal (S11, S70); no line reconciles the two. Whether the next meal happens, and Chisato's view of the vow, are not lettered.

### Bonus · S115–118 · A new gift

In the café's raised seating, both in overall dresses, Takina watches Chisato silently and explains that Chisato seems to eat snacks indiscriminately less often (S115). The right-hand column is read first. There Takina recalls that Chisato used to snack even before health checks and asks whether something is wrong with her; Chisato says she is well (S116). A1 E03 supplies a health-check occasion compatible with that knowledge. Only then, in the tall left panel, does Takina ask about a change of heart (心境の変化). Chisato says it is nothing grand, and an aside adds that she has not stopped snacking (S116). Because of her heart (心臓のこともあって) she never expected a long life, so distant health did not matter (S116).
