# 2. Executive thesis

Episode 03 is the first V2 episode to make Takina's DA attachment legible as an identity-and-belonging problem rather than merely a career preference. DA is not just her employer. The episode explicitly frames DA as the institution that rescued orphaned girls, raised them, gave them uniforms and status, and supplied a coveted home. Takina's expulsion therefore removes a place, a future, and a criterion of worth at once.

Against that loss, Chisato does not demand that Takina replace DA with LycoReco. Her intervention is deliberately non-exclusive. She validates the autonomous choice Takina made when she tried to save Erika; disputes the fairness of DA's treatment; states that people in the city already need Takina; asks Takina to *try* the time available with the cafe community; and explicitly leaves open the possibility of returning to DA later if that is still what Takina wants.

The episode's central developmental movement is therefore not:

`DA belonging -> LycoReco belonging`

but:

`institutionally assigned belonging as the only imaginable place -> permission to test plural, provisional, self-chosen belonging without forfeiting the old desire in advance`

E03 also turns that argument into action. Takina begins the episode refusing the off-hours board-game gathering and evaluating even leisure through whether it will help her return to DA. She ends by choosing to join the group after Chisato asks what she wants to do. Between those points, Takina voluntarily enters a mock battle, chooses a personally meaningful but tactically inefficient punch against Fuki, and uses Chisato's extraordinary evasion skill as part of her own tactical solution. Her operational rationalism survives, but it is no longer sufficient to describe all of her behavior.
