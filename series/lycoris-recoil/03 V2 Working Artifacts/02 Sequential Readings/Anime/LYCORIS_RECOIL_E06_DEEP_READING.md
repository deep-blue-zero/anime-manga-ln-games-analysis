# 2. Executive thesis

Episode 06 is about **what happens when hidden systems become legible enough to target—and when people inside those systems are forced to decide how causality, responsibility, and closeness should be allocated**.

The four attacked Lycoris initially appear to be victims of mysterious identification. The actual route is institutional: DA's own drone footage from the earlier gun transaction was exposed through the Radiata intrusion, and the attackers are using that leaked surveillance material to identify individual Lycoris. A security system designed to make hidden violence governable has become a targeting dataset against its own agents.

Takina's response to danger is relational but operationally legible. She does not wait for Chisato to propose greater closeness. She announces that they should remain paired, sleep in shifts, and stay together twenty-four hours a day until safety is restored. This creates the series' first sustained shared-household condition for the pair. Yet E06 immediately prevents the arrangement from being flattened into permanent domestic fusion. Takina formalizes household labor; Chisato calls the schedule boring; Takina adapts by proposing rock-paper-scissors. Chisato then exploits the same motor-prediction ability that makes her extraordinary in combat to become effectively unbeatable after an initial tie. Takina investigates why she cannot win, learns the mechanism, changes the opening condition, and finally beats Chisato.

The final wager is therefore analytically decisive. Chisato proposes that they should continue `同棲` because two people together are safer. Takina replies that they will continue **if Chisato wins** the game. Takina then wins. E06 gives domestic familiarity, explicit cohabitation language, and playful reciprocity, but also gives Takina a successfully exercised exit condition. Closeness does not remove self-authorship.

The same causal precision governs Takina's response to Kurumi. Kurumi discovers that the facial leak used to kill Lycoris came from the DA hack she performed under contract. Takina's immediate activation is high when she names the downstream consequence: unknown terrorists acquired guns and Takina was dismissed. But after the fight, when others invite Takina to blame or punish Kurumi, she refuses the simple scapegoat story:

> `あれは私の行動の結果で クルミのせいじゃありません`

This does not mean Kurumi had no causal role. Takina then requires her continued cooperation until the attacker is caught. E06 therefore advances Takina beyond undifferentiated self-blame: she can distinguish **authorship of her own choice**, **external causal contribution**, and **forward repair obligation**.

Majima and Yoshimatsu provide the hostile counterpart. Majima's stated objective is not merely killing individual Lycoris: he wants to destroy DA, the hidden mechanism that erased his associates and maintains Japan's concealed order. Robota is simultaneously being pressured through Himegama by an unnamed boss to make Majima interested in Chisato. After Robota shows Chisato's combat footage, Majima shifts from dismissing ordinary Lycoris as expendable pieces to selecting Chisato as the opponent capable of balancing him. Yoshimatsu's assigned-purpose project and Majima's balance project therefore become operationally coupled without becoming ideologically identical.

E06 also converts Chisato's combat legend into a bounded mechanism. She can be hurt. Majima pushes her into a condition where visual access is impaired, and the episode later makes the vulnerability explicit through Takina's `千束の弱点は目ですね`. Chisato's evasion is not supernatural invulnerability; it depends on perceptual information. At the same time, even while Majima is beating her and explicitly taunts her for using rubber rather than live rounds, Chisato does not abandon nonlethality.

The episode's governing movement is therefore:

`hidden systems -> exposed causal chains -> targeted bodies`

answered by

`differentiated responsibility + bounded partnership + self-authored adaptation`.
