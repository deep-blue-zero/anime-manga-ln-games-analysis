# 2. Executive thesis

Episode 07 turns the previous episodes' conflict over **who gets to decide what a life is for** into a conflict over **who gets to interpret another person's place**.

Four competing placement systems become explicit.

Takina still retains DA as a live possible destination. When Chisato fears DA might reclaim her and Café LycoReco might therefore collapse, Takina immediately offers:

> `じゃ 私が戻りますよ`

That offer comes after E06 domestic cohabitation and expanding partnership. E07 therefore supplies direct negative evidence against a model in which closeness with Chisato has already erased Takina's institutional exit options.

Chisato's own self-placement is equally clear. After learning that Yoshimatsu is the person connected to her artificial heart, she does not interpret the gift as a command. She tells him:

> `私も頂いた時間で ヨシさんみたいに誰かを…`

The time was given; the use of that time is authored by Chisato. She wants to help someone as she believes Yoshimatsu helped her.

Yoshimatsu's model is the opposite. His ideology becomes more precise than simple personal ownership. He tells Mika:

> `才能とは神の所有物だ`
>
> `人のものではない`
>
> `まして 私たちのものでもない`

He therefore does **not** imagine himself as Chisato's owner. He imagines talent as belonging to a transcendent order whose human bearer is obligated to manifest it. This makes his coercion more, not less, severe: Chisato's preference is subordinate because the talent is supposedly not hers to interpret.

Mika contests that conclusion directly:

> `千束を自由にしろ！`

Yet Mika cannot complete the threatened violence against Yoshimatsu and later admits:

> `覚悟なんか あるわけないだろ`

Mika is therefore caught between fatherhood, an earlier promise, and a deep personal bond with Yoshimatsu. E07 shows resistance to instrumentalizing Chisato without pretending that Mika has become emotionally unencumbered.

Finally, Yoshimatsu attempts to recruit Takina into the placement project. After Takina tells him how happy Chisato was to meet him and invites him back to the café, he answers:

> `君なら分かるはずだ`
>
> `千束の居場所が ここではないと`
>
> `君には期待しているよ たきなちゃん`

The wording is diagnostically exact. Yoshimatsu does not merely tell Takina what Chisato should *do*. He tells her where Chisato supposedly *belongs* and treats Takina as a potential proxy for enforcing that interpretation.

E07's governing movement is therefore:

`gift / institution / talent / relationship -> competing claims about where a person belongs`

answered provisionally by

`self-authored use of one's life + relationships that can survive nonexclusive choices + unresolved coercive pressure`.
