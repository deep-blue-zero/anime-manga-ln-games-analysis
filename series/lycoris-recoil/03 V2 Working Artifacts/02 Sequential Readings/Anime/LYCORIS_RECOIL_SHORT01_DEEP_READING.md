# 2. Executive thesis — competence acquires ordinary objects without losing its old grammar

Short 01 is a compact validation of one of the strongest TV-wide Takina conclusions: **ordinary-life development does not require operational competence to disappear; it changes what that competence is used for while leaving some old habits available.**

Takina opens the short already handling supply logistics by telephone. During LycoReco's hanami-related service operation she monitors stock, places an additional alcohol order before Chisato asks for one, immediately identifies the commercial value of alcohol sales, and independently proposes expanding the next event with yakitori.

Her ordinary participation is therefore not passive. She is not merely attending an activity Chisato designed for her. She is helping operate, optimize, and imagine the future of a civilian social institution.

At the same time, when Chisato comments that leaving the window open all morning is careless, Takina answers:

> `平気ですよ`

The visual answer is that Takina has a handgun and multiple magazines tucked under the rear sash of her festival clothing.

Chisato reacts with alarm and tries to take the weapon away:

> `危ないのはお前だ！ よこせ`

Takina answers:

> `大丈夫… 上手にやりますから`

The joke works because Takina's confidence in competence has not vanished. Civilian integration, profit-minded initiative, festival work, and armed contingency planning coexist inside the same person. Chisato's response supplies the complementary rule: **capability does not automatically make an operational solution context-appropriate.**

The short therefore does not depict “Takina becoming civilian instead of Lycoris.” It depicts a more interesting coexistence:

`administrative precision / commercial initiative / ordinary social participation / retained armed readiness / partner correction`.

# 4. LycoReco as ordinary seasonal infrastructure

The short begins inside a decorated LycoReco during cherry-blossom season. The cafe is not waiting passively for a security plot; it is participating in a local seasonal economy and social event.

Chisato moves between the cafe and outside service area in a red event outfit. Takina wears a coordinated blue outfit. Mika prepares food and stock inside. Mizuki and Kurumi participate in the nearby gathering.

Chisato requests more sakura tea:

> `桜茶 ５個追加ぁ`

Mika observes that they keep getting tea orders. Chisato explains that someone is pouring alcohol into it and drinking—immediately followed by Mika identifying Mizuki as the obvious concern.

**SHORT01-CLM-001 — HIGH:** LycoReco's ordinary-life function is active infrastructure rather than decorative downtime. The cafe supplies food/drink, staffing, seasonal service, logistics and social gathering in response to a local civilian event. `[JP][VIS][ORDINARY]`

**SHORT01-CLM-002 — HIGH:** the ensemble's ordinary membership is role-differentiated rather than homogenized: Mika cooks/manages, Chisato carries service requests, Takina handles stock/logistics and outside work, Mizuki turns the event into adult social leisure, and Kurumi participates from within the social group. `[VIS][ENSEMBLE]`

This strongly tests `PRED-CP4-010`: ordinary food/work/leisure remains the social medium of the series rather than merely a pause between missions.
