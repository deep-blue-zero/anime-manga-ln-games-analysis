# 2. Executive thesis

E11 turns the series' earlier language of freedom, belonging, and self-authorship into **costly choice under crisis**.

Takina's new relationship to DA is no longer hypothetical. She is inside the institution, on a major operation, and is explicitly told that abandoning the mission means she will never return. She accepts the cost:

> `分かってます`
>
> `ここでは千束を救えない それだけです`

The decisive shift is not that DA suddenly becomes worthless. It is that **institutional belonging no longer outranks the person-specific end for which Takina had repurposed DA in E09-E10**. When DA ceases to be the best route to Chisato, Takina leaves it.

Chisato is placed under the inverse pressure. She goes alone to rescue Yoshimatsu, the man whose gift and coercive purpose have structured the entire Alan conflict. Majima attempts to turn Yoshimatsu into a ballistic constraint and later into ideological common ground: both Majima and Chisato hate having a life-path imposed on them. Chisato refuses the equivalence:

> `一緒にすんな！`

That refusal clarifies a distinction already implicit in E08-E10: **self-authorship is a necessary component of Chisato's ethics, but not a sufficient moral principle**. Majima also rejects imposed purpose, yet he creates armed civilian experiments, uses hostages, and accepts other people's lives as instruments of his balancing project.

At the institutional level, Majima attacks DA's core epistemic strategy at exactly its weak point. He does not rely on video alone because video can be dismissed as fabrication. He creates public embodied witnesses to Lycoris violence and then broadcasts the interpretation. DA's secrecy is converted from protective capacity into operational constraint: once civilians can identify Lycoris, DA must order firing restraint even while armed civilians and Majima's forces remain dangerous.

E11's governing movement is therefore:

`institution as identity / secrecy as protection / freedom as slogan`

becoming

`institution as defeasible tool / secrecy as operational liability / freedom judged by what it permits one to do to other people`.

**E11-CLM-001 — HIGH:** Takina does not treat unexplained Chisato/Mika nonresponse as a private distraction separable from operational risk. She interprets the disappearance as anomalous under the current Majima/Yoshimatsu crisis and initiates her own check. `[JP][REL][AGENCY]`
