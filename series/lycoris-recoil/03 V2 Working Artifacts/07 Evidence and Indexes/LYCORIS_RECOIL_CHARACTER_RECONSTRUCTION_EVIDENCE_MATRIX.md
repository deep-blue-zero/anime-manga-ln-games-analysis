## State and evidence required before applying a rule

| State or evidence condition | How to apply it |
|---|---|
| TV E01–E03, from initial assignment to the first voluntary step into café life | Takina’s command training, wish to return and protection of Erika can operate together. Chisato can welcome her while helping her pursue a destination Chisato does not control. The E03 ending shows Takina choose to join the café game while keeping DA an option (REL-CAFE-E03-001, E03-CLM-010). The evidence of early initiative does not grant Takina later knowledge of Chisato’s lifespan or motives. |
| TV E04–E08, developing café and partner familiarity | Chisato creates occasions for clothes, film or games. Takina can oppose the terms, join anyway or initiate a turn of her own. The relationship has specific shared routines. Neither the crisis exceptions nor the later housing proposals supply ordinary permission in these episodes. |
| TV E09–E12, after the medical facts become usable to the character in question | Distinguish who has learned what and when. The threatened loss changes which objections Takina will act on and why she contests an order. Chisato’s rule about the character of survival still governs Chisato’s own refusal. An observed struggle beside Yoshimatsu gives evidence of conflicting aims. It does not decide the answer to every rescue scenario involving another person. |
| TV E13 and short comparators | E13 supplies an aftermath and a changed life horizon. Shorts 01–06 supply bounded ordinary configurations with separately recorded event order. Use their distinctions between buying and pricing, giving and accepting, offering and moving in, receiving help and expressing agreement. Do not supply a calendar or make a promised trip a completed journey. |
| Local narrators, pictures and witness reports | A1 governs originating behavior and performance. A2 may give direct access to one focalizer at one point in a story. B1 and C1 can sharpen the definition of a circumstance, give a contrasting optional portrayal or provide parody. Test whether a duplicated premise, picture, rumor, dream or report actually adds a new observation. Reference material may identify a creator’s explanation without supplying a scene that the originating work withholds. |

## Use in the later validation

The reconstruction should identify an evidence boundary before it produces an action or a line of Japanese. The same elicitation varies with developmental state, knowledge, stakes and interlocutor. Tests can withhold the intended diagnostic and ask an independent assessor to compare the answer with the relevant row. Select prompts that put two supported tendencies in tension so a generic personality sketch cannot pass. Preserve the specific behavior each response predicts, any plausible counterexample and what new primary evidence would move an open result. Evaluation prompts, the models’ replies and independent judgments belong in the reconstruction evaluation record.
