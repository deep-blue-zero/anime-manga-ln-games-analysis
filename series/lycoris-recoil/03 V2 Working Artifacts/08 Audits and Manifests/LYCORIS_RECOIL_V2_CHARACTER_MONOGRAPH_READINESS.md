# V2 character monograph readiness

The [series architecture](../../02%20V2%20Planning%20and%20Protocol/LYCORIS_RECOIL_V2_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md#121-character-monographs) assigns qualification here under [project-initiation policy §5.5.1](../../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md#551-self-contained-character-monograph-standard). This assessment uses the frozen CP5 anime boundary, its attributable rechecks, and the [accepted cross-source comparisons](../06%20Cross-Media%20Analysis/LYCORIS_RECOIL_CROSS_SOURCE_CHARACTERIZATION_CROSSWALK.md). Each A2-local subject below retains a separate source boundary. The creator-specific comics do not supply more anime evidence. *Heroine Archive* production testimony remains attributed paratext. The Bizen adaptation remains outside this assessment.

The principal integrator reviewed the scene-grounded eligibility work of `/root/pilot_e13`, including the anime cast with differentiated evidence and any concentrated cases in the three novels and credited comics. The review considers variation among relationships and settings, choices enacted within a sustained sequence and evidence against a single generic role. Reassess if admitted evidence materially changes the person’s decisions, history, speech or relationships. `QUALIFIED_PENDING` creates an outstanding portrait obligation. `COMPLETE_AT_BOUNDARY` requires recorded passes for both self-containment and source fidelity. A deferred row gives the present evidence gap and its own review trigger. All 21 qualified portraits are complete at their respective boundaries. The [adoption record](LYCORIS_RECOIL_V2_COMPLETION_REGISTER.md#reviewed-portraits-and-v2-release) binds each new subject to its independent checks. The deferred subjects keep their existing evidence and reopening limits.
