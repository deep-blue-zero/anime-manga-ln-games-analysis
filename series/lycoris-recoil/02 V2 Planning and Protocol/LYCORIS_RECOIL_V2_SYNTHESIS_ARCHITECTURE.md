# Lycoris Recoil V2 Synthesis and Corpus Architecture

## 1. Governing purpose

This document defines the canonical V2 analytical architecture for *Lycoris Recoil*.

V2 is not a simple enlargement of the V1 full-series synthesis. It is a materially restarted analytical generation built around:

1. a fresh prospective reread of the TV anime and six *Friends are thieves of time* shorts using current corpus-wide deep-reading standards;
2. systematic extraction of character behavior, relationship state, voice/performance, ordinary-life behavior, institutional evidence, visual/action evidence, and claim revision;
3. a frozen anime-native reconstruction baseline established **before** supplementary prose or derivative comics are allowed to modify integrated character models;
4. source-native reading of novels, *Recollect*, and official anthology material as works deserving analysis in their own right;
5. provenance-controlled cross-source integration that distinguishes originating audiovisual evidence, creator-proximate supplementary material, adaptations, official single-author spinoffs, and multi-author licensed interpretations;
6. explicit character-reconstruction infrastructure capable of answering not only “who is this character?” but “given state X, relationship Y, and stimulus Z, what is this character likely to perceive, want, say, and do?”;
7. deterministic routing from mature claims back to source-unit readings, Japanese language evidence, performance/audio evidence, visual evidence, and source locators;
8. preservation of V1 as a prior analytical generation rather than retroactive rewriting of its conclusions.

The architecture standardizes semantic responsibility, not a rigid folder tree. Folders or specialist artifacts should be created only when the corresponding analytical responsibility becomes real.

# 3. Source topology and authority model

## 3.1 Source classes

V2 distinguishes source provenance from epistemic confidence.

A statement may be a **fact within a source** while still having limited authority for the integrated anime-centered character model.

Use these source classes unless a later source audit justifies refinement:

### `A1_ORIGINATING_AUDIOVISUAL`

- TV anime Episodes 01-13;
- six *Friends are thieves of time* shorts;
- original Japanese audio;
- Japanese subtitle/transcript derivatives;
- episode bundles;
- continuous-video escalation material when required.

Primary responsibility:

- governing dramatic events depicted by the anime;
- performed characterization;
- audiovisual timing;
- voice;
- blocking;
- action;
- visual grammar;
- sound/music;
- anime-native behavioral baseline.

### `A2_CREATOR_PROXIMATE_SUPPLEMENTARY`

Expected to include the Asaura prose volumes when edition/credit audit confirms the currently understood authorship and supervision:

- *Ordinary days*;
- *Recovery days*;
- *Gluttony days*.

Primary responsibility:

- supplementary characterization;
- additional ordinary-life situations;
- prose-specific narrative and thematic analysis;
- possible direct or focalized access to cognition;
- extension into behavioral state spaces absent from the anime.

A2 material may strongly extend or pressure an anime-derived model, but must not be retroactively blended into the frozen anime-native baseline.

### `B1_OFFICIAL_SINGLE_AUTHOR_DERIVATIVE`

Expected to include *Lycoris Recoil Recollect* when source audit confirms its production/credit status.

Primary responsibility:

- analysis of the derivative work on its own terms;
- new everyday/comedic situations;
- source-local character behavior;
- cross-author characterization comparison;
- hypothesis generation and behavioral-envelope testing.

B1 material does not, by itself, revise a high-confidence A1 behavioral rule.

### `B2_OFFICIAL_ADAPTATION`

Applies to a direct adaptation of the anime story, including the main Bizen Yasunori manga if later admitted.

Primary responsibility:

- adaptation choices;
- retained/omitted dialogue;
- panel selection and pacing;
- added connective material;
- alternative facial/body emphasis;
- corroborative characterization.

Because the primary narrative events are already available in A1 audiovisual form, B2 is optional unless it introduces material analytical value.

### `C1_MULTI_AUTHOR_LICENSED_INTERPRETATION`

Official comic anthologies and similar multi-author licensed works.

Primary responsibility:

- source-native story analysis;
- creator-by-creator characterization;
- recurrent franchise characterization attractors;
- characterization invariants;
- exaggeration and genre deformation;
- licensed interpretation range.

C1 evidence should normally expand the **characterization envelope** more readily than it changes core behavioral base rates.

### `P_PARATEXT`

Production interviews, official staff commentary, publication notes, creator statements, and other paratext.

Use only for clearly identified production, authorship, supervision, continuity, or reception questions. Paratext does not override the work itself.

---

## 3.2 Source authority is multidimensional

Do not reduce source integration to a single “canon/noncanon” rank.

For each non-A1 source, source inventory and integration work should record as applicable:

- continuity authority;
- characterization authority;
- creator proximity;
- supervision/approval evidence;
- medium independence versus adaptation;
- genre pressure;
- temporal placement;
- compatibility with anime continuity;
- whether claims are source-local or proposed for integrated promotion.

Absence of public evidence for close supervision should be recorded as absence, not converted into an assumption of either no supervision or full supervision.

---

## 3.3 Source-local fact versus integrated claim

Always preserve the distinction:

> “Character X does Y in Source S.”

versus:

> “Character X generally tends to do Y.”

The first can be a source-local fact.

The second is an integrated model claim and requires promotion through compatibility, repetition, source authority, cross-source convergence, or another explicit integration rationale.

# 4. Anime sequential reread architecture

## 4.1 Fresh prospective pass

V2 re-reads the anime sequentially from E01.

The new pass is not a rewrite of the earlier episode analysis. Its purpose is to exploit the much richer current framework and episode-bundle infrastructure.

The pass should preserve prospective discipline:

- do not project mature E13 knowledge backward into E01;
- distinguish character knowledge from analyst knowledge;
- record provisional claims at the source-unit boundary;
- preserve predictions before later evidence adjudicates them;
- distinguish revelation of a previously latent trait from genuine character development;
- allow later evidence to revise earlier interpretation explicitly.

## 4.2 Sequential source units

Canonical anime source-unit scopes:

- `E01` through `E13`;
- `SHORT01` through `SHORT06`.

Recommended filenames:

`LYCORIS_RECOIL_E01_DEEP_READING.md`

...

`LYCORIS_RECOIL_E13_DEEP_READING.md`

and:

`LYCORIS_RECOIL_SHORT01_DEEP_READING.md`

...

`LYCORIS_RECOIL_SHORT06_DEEP_READING.md`

The exact folder structure may distinguish TV and shorts for retrieval, but filename scope must remain stable and sortable.

## 4.3 Minimum architectural outputs of each anime deep reading

A source-unit reading is not complete merely because its standalone prose exists.

Each completed anime reading must, where evidence warrants:

1. produce the deep-reading artifact;
2. advance character-state observations;
3. advance relationship-state observations;
4. route diagnostic microbehavior evidence;
5. route voice/register/performance observations;
6. route ordinary-life/humor/play/social-repair evidence;
7. route institutional/world-state evidence;
8. route visual/action evidence;
9. record new claims and pressures on earlier claims;
10. adjudicate prior prospective predictions;
11. create new prospective predictions where analytically useful;
12. record evidence locators;
13. record continuous-video escalation state;
14. update the current-state/corpus map if project state materially changes.

Not every episode must produce a major finding in every domain.

# 5. Episode-bundle and continuous-video contract

The corpus-wide `MANGA_ANIME_EPISODE_BUNDLE_SPECIFICATION.md` governs episode-bundle semantics.

V2 is **bundle-first**.

Episode bundles are the standing analytical derivative and should be used for:

- Japanese dialogue and timing;
- dense timestamped visual sampling;
- contact-sheet reconnaissance;
- frame/shot context;
- complete analysis audio;
- source and extraction provenance;
- evidence localization.

The approximately 20 GB `00_prepared_japanese_mkv` / `02_media` material is not required to reside permanently in the Drive analytical source corpus.

Continuous video should be escalated only when a material claim depends on temporally continuous evidence that the bundle cannot establish.

Use:

- `VIDEO_NOT_REQUIRED`;
- `VIDEO_TARGETED_ESCALATION`;
- `VIDEO_FULL_EPISODE_ESCALATION`.

Prefer targeted bounded intervals whenever sufficient.

Do not infer unseen motion, reaction timing, camera trajectory, expression trajectory, edit rhythm, or audiovisual synchronization from sampled endpoints.

If an escalation remains unresolved, the claim must remain correspondingly limited.

# 6. Anime-native character reconstruction baseline

## 6.1 Purpose

Before supplementary prose, *Recollect*, or anthologies are permitted to modify integrated character models, V2 must establish a frozen anime-native reconstruction baseline.

The baseline source boundary is:

> TV E01-E13 + Shorts 01-06 only.

## 6.2 Required baseline domains

For major characters where evidence supports them, reconstruct:

- stable temperament;
- values and moral constraints;
- behavioral policies;
- relationship-conditioned behavior;
- state transitions;
- social initiation and response behavior;
- humor/play mechanisms;
- repair and recalibration behavior;
- conflict/escalation behavior;
- ordinary-life preferences and habits;
- competence behavior;
- risk behavior;
- linguistic/register behavior;
- performed voice behavior;
- body/visual behavior;
- known unknowns;
- negative constraints (“do not model this character as...”);
- source-state coverage;
- confidence and extrapolation boundaries.

## 6.3 Reconstruction observation schema

Microbehavior evidence should, where useful, preserve:

- context;
- interlocutor;
- relationship state;
- stakes;
- character state;
- trigger/stimulus;
- initiator;
- apparent appraisal;
- immediate social/operational objective;
- behavior/tactic;
- speech act;
- register;
- humor mechanism;
- physical/body behavior;
- partner response;
- adaptation/recalibration;
- outcome;
- supported policy/trait;
- source locator;
- evidence-stream tags;
- confidence.

The schema may evolve; semantic recoverability matters more than literal field names.

## 6.4 Reconstruction confidence classes

For counterfactual use, distinguish:

### `R1_DIRECT_RECONSTRUCTION`
A near-equivalent source situation exists.

### `R2_CONSTRAINED_INTERPOLATION`
No identical situation exists, but multiple high-confidence policies strongly constrain the likely response.

### `R3_WEAK_EXTRAPOLATION`
Important situational variables are under-tested.

### `R4_OPEN_SIMULATION`
The source provides little meaningful constraint.

Generated counterfactuals are **tests or uses of the model**, never new evidence about the source character.

# 8. Source-native supplementary reading architecture

## 8.1 Governing rule

> Every supplementary source is first interpreted as an authored work within its own medium and provenance. Character evidence is integrated only afterward.

This rule prevents novels from becoming mere fact mines and prevents licensed comics from silently contaminating the anime-native model.

## 8.2 Prose volumes

Each admitted prose volume receives a source-native deep reading.

Recommended filenames:

- `LYCORIS_RECOIL_ORDINARY_DAYS_DEEP_READING.md`
- `LYCORIS_RECOIL_RECOVERY_DAYS_DEEP_READING.md`
- `LYCORIS_RECOIL_GLUTTONY_DAYS_DEEP_READING.md`

The prose method should preserve:

- narrative architecture;
- story/chapter structure;
- focalization;
- narrator authority;
- direct thought;
- free indirect or focalized narration;
- external narration;
- dialogue;
- prose rhythm;
- thematic purpose;
- ordinary-life construction;
- character behavior;
- medium-specific affordances;
- relationship evidence;
- source-local contradictions;
- evidence locators.

Interiority evidence must distinguish direct cognition from analyst inference.

## 8.3 Recollect

Read *Recollect* volume-by-volume or chapter-by-chapter as analytically justified.

Preserve:

- Abe Kanari’s source-local characterization;
- comedic structure;
- everyday-life architecture;
- genre-pressure effects;
- physical-comedy and reaction grammar;
- recurrent character interpretations;
- divergence from anime-native behavior;
- possible compatible extensions.

Do not assume “official” means creator-identical characterization.

## 8.4 Official anthologies

Anthologies require contributor-level attribution.

The basic evidence unit should be:

> creator + story + character realization

rather than “the anthology says.”

Track:

- writer/artist;
- story title/scope;
- genre/tone;
- apparent temporal placement;
- characters used;
- characterization emphasis;
- compatibility with anime-native model;
- exaggeration;
- source-specific interpretation;
- contradictions;
- recurrence across independent contributors.

Independent convergence may establish a high-salience **franchise characterization attractor**, but does not by itself establish anime-native base-rate frequency.

# 9. Multi-source character integration firewall

## 9.1 Promotion vocabulary

When supplementary evidence is compared against the frozen anime-native model, classify it as appropriate:

### `CONFIRM`
Reproduces an already established behavior.

### `EXTEND`
Adds a previously under-tested situation while remaining compatible with the core model.

### `SPECIFY`
Adds a conditional modifier to an existing rule.

### `PRESSURE`
Plausible but sufficiently surprising that the current model requires review.

### `CONTRADICT`
Conflicts with a strongly evidenced rule under apparently comparable conditions.

### `CARICATURE`
Amplifies a real trait in frequency or magnitude, often under comedy/genre pressure.

### `SOURCE_SPECIFIC`
Useful for understanding that source’s realization but not promoted into the general model.

### `OPEN`
Evidence is insufficient to integrate or reject.

These integration labels do not replace the corpus-wide V1→V2 revision vocabulary.

## 9.2 Base-rate firewall

A derivative source may establish that behavior X is **possible** without establishing that X is **typical**.

Low-authority or gag-heavy sources should expand the plausible behavior envelope more readily than they alter behavioral base rates.

## 9.3 Asymmetric revision threshold

No weakly supervised or unsupervised derivative source may, by itself:

- `REVISE`;
- `DOWNGRADE`;
- or `REJECT`

a high-confidence anime-native behavioral rule.

It may:

- `PRESSURE` the rule;
- establish a source-local alternative;
- add an `OPEN` contradiction;
- contribute to later revision if stronger evidence converges.

## 9.4 Preserve contradictions

Do not invent harmonizing conditions solely to erase cross-source conflict.

Where evidence genuinely diverges, retain:

- source A behavior;
- source B behavior;
- authority difference;
- possible explanations;
- unresolved status.

# 10. Source-local and integrated character models

Major character reconstruction should preserve four conceptual layers.

## 10.1 Core invariant model

Strongly grounded in A1 and stable across high-authority evidence.

## 10.2 Supplementary extension model

High-confidence behavior from strong supplementary sources extending into conditions absent or weakly represented in A1.

## 10.3 Characterization envelope

Plausible licensed-source behaviors that expand the recognized range without earning strong base-rate expectations.

## 10.4 Source-local variants

Author- or medium-specific interpretations retained for comparative analysis and selectable when the analytical question explicitly targets that realization.

The integrated default simulator/reconstruction should normally use:

> Core invariant + supplementary extension

unless the user explicitly requests a broader characterization envelope or a specific source-local realization.

# 11. Validation architecture

## 11.1 Anime-internal prospective validation

Where practical, freeze a model before later anime evidence and predict behavior categories rather than plot.

Examples:

- E01-E03 model → test on E04-E06;
- E01-E06 model → test on E07-E10;
- E01-E10 model → test on E11-E13;
- E01-E13 model → test on the shorts.

Score qualitatively or structurally on:

- action class;
- social initiative;
- affective direction;
- register;
- moral choice;
- humor/play behavior;
- relationship response;
- recalibration;
- surprise.

Prediction failure may indicate:

- bad model;
- genuine development;
- previously hidden capability;
- changed relationship state;
- changed stakes;
- a new source modifier.

Do not automatically classify failure as analytical error.

## 11.2 Prospective supplementary-source validation

Where chapter/story premises can be known without reading the content, the frozen anime-native or current integrated model may make predictions before source reading.

Prediction is then frozen before opening the story.

Comparison outcomes should be preserved.

This is particularly valuable for distinguishing:

- model incompleteness;
- creator-proximate extension;
- derivative-source drift;
- genre caricature.
