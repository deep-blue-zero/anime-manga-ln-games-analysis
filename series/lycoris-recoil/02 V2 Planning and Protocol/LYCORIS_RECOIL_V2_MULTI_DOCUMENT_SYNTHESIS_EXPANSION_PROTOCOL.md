# Lycoris Recoil V2 Multi-Document Synthesis Expansion Protocol
## Governing instruction set for a source-grounded, Japanese-original, audiovisual second-pass synthesis

**Project:** *Lycoris Recoil* TV anime Episodes 1–13 + all six *Friends are thieves of time* shorts  
**Purpose:** Produce a definitive V2 multi-document synthesis that expands the existing ~32,000-word V1 corpus only where the source evidence supports materially greater depth.  
**Target:** Approximately **45,000–55,000 words of reader-facing analytical prose**, plus provenance, evidence, locator, manifest, and audit documents that are **not counted toward the interpretive target**.  
**Governing principle:** V2 is a **revision and evidentiary-deepening pass**, not an unstructured rewrite and not an attempt to imitate the length of larger projects such as *Revue Starlight* or *Legend of the Galactic Heroes*.

---

# I. V2 governing objective

The V2 corpus should preserve the strongest conclusions of the first synthesis while substantially improving four things:

1. **evidentiary traceability** — major mature claims should be recoverable through episode/short evidence, Japanese dialogue, performance/audio observations, and audiovisual locators;
2. **underdeveloped evidence streams** — especially Japanese vocal performance, sound/music/silence, DA's political and epistemic legitimacy, Mika/Yoshimatsu/Alan, selected action/visual anchor sequences, and secondary-character institutional life;
3. **revision transparency** — the corpus should record how provisional episode-level hypotheses were strengthened, qualified, rejected, or transformed by later episodes;
4. **cross-document discipline** — each major claim should have a primary analytical home, while other documents reference rather than redundantly re-prove it.

V2 should not be judged by whether it becomes dramatically longer than V1. It should be judged by whether **each additional section introduces a genuinely new analytical dimension, stronger primary-source grounding, or a necessary correction**.

The central mature interpretive model remains provisional until the V2 audit is complete, but the current strongest formulation should be used as the starting hypothesis:

> *Lycoris Recoil* repeatedly asks who has authority to determine what another person's finite life is for. Its ethical answer is not autonomy in isolation, but self-authorship bounded by reciprocal recognition: **my life is mine to interpret, and therefore your life is not mine to erase, own, script, or reduce to a function.**

V2 must actively search for counterevidence to this formulation rather than merely decorating it.

# IV. Evidence and confidence discipline for V2

Retain the governing four-level epistemic system:

### Fact
Directly established by dialogue, visible action, or unambiguous audiovisual evidence.

### Strong inference
Not directly stated, but the audiovisual/textual evidence makes competing explanations substantially less plausible.

### Interpretive hypothesis
A coherent literary/thematic explanation that organizes multiple facts but remains contestable.

### Speculation
A possible explanation or extrapolation not strongly constrained by the text.

For V2, add **evidence-stream tags** where useful:

- **JP** — Japanese wording/register;
- **PERF** — vocal performance/prosody/timbre;
- **VIS** — framing/blocking/image evidence;
- **ACT** — action/choreography/weapon behavior;
- **SND** — music/sound/silence;
- **INST** — institutional/worldbuilding evidence;
- **REL** — relationship behavior;
- **SHORT** — supplementary short evidence;
- **REV** — later evidence revising earlier interpretation.

These tags are optional in reader-facing prose but recommended in ledgers.

Never convert qualitative performance listening into pseudo-precise acoustic science. Exact F0, dB, spectral, or formant claims require an explicit measurement workflow and clean enough audio to justify them. Otherwise describe heard properties qualitatively and accurately.

# VII. Areas that should receive little or no length-driven expansion

## 1. Core thesis repetition
The central self-authorship / reciprocal-personhood model should be stress-tested, not restated in multiple philosophical vocabularies.

## 2. Basic Chisato arc
The general arc is already well understood. Expand through new evidence streams, not another generic character summary.

## 3. Basic Takina arc
Do not repeatedly explain "Takina learns to live." Preserve the stronger formulation: her traits survive while the purposes they serve expand.

## 4. Yuri-proof accumulation
Do not turn V2 into a dossier of romantic hints. Only retain examples that change the relational model, linguistic intimacy, domesticity, or future orientation.

## 5. Shorts inflation
The shorts' main role is low-stakes characterization and durability testing. Short 6 deserves disproportionate institutional attention because of fictionalization; the others should not be forced into grand thematic status.

## 6. Thin minor-character psychologizing
Do not invent interiority for Himegama, Robota, Jin, or other thinly drawn characters.

## 7. Motif hunting
Do not expand motif sections merely by increasing the object count.

## 8. Shot-by-shot treatment of every action sequence
Use anchor scenes. Action analysis should identify moral/character grammar, not become exhaustive combat transcription.

## 9. Artificial parity with larger projects
Do not target 100,000+ words merely because *Revue Starlight* or *LOGH* reached that scale. Their analytical demands are different.

# X. Shorts integration policy

The six shorts should be classified claim by claim as:

### Interpretation-changing
Material that substantially alters the mature TV reading.

Expected to be rare.

### Interpretation-strengthening
Material that confirms, extends, or clarifies an important TV-series conclusion.

Examples likely include:

- Takina's independent investment in leisure and future;
- durable managerial/optimization identity;
- Chisato's indefinite cohabitation invitation;
- Fuki/Mika warmth;
- LycoReco as DA-member social refuge;
- Short 6 fictionalization mechanism.

### Texture-only enrichment
Material that deepens personality or group rhythm without altering the master thesis.

Examples likely include:

- horror fear;
- embarrassment-to-firearm gags;
- drunk karaoke;
- menu comedy;
- vacation clothing;
- household teasing.

The V2 corpus should value texture without pretending texture is thesis-changing.

# XII. Word-count discipline

The target is not a quota.

Recommended reader-facing analytical scale:

- **minimum satisfactory V2:** ~40,000 words;
- **preferred range:** ~45,000–55,000 words;
- **possible upper range:** ~60,000 words if new evidence genuinely warrants it;
- **warning zone:** beyond ~60,000–70,000 words, audit aggressively for repetition and false depth.

The following do **not** count against the interpretive target:

- source inventory;
- provenance index;
- evidence ledger;
- checksum files;
- manifest;
- QC report.

A longer V2 is justified only when it adds:

- a new evidence stream;
- a new independently supportable analytical problem;
- a necessary qualification;
- stronger Japanese/audiovisual grounding;
- or materially better retrieval/provenance.

The V2 synthesis is complete only when all of the following are true:

9. selected action sequences receive deeper formal treatment without exhaustive fight transcription;
10. supporting characters are expanded only to the level source evidence supports;
11. yuri coding remains evidentially disciplined;

19. final files pass duplication, link, checksum, and archive-integrity checks;

> **V2 does not merely tell the reader what *Lycoris Recoil* means.
