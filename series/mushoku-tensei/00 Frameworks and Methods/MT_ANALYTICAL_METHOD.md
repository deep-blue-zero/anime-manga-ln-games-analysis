# Mushoku Tensei: analytical method

## 1. Purpose and limits

Build three connected but non-interchangeable products: a literary account of the published Japanese novels; evidence-bounded reconstructions of their characters; and an investigation of textual history and public interpretation. Read literature first, not a predetermined defense or prosecution. Sexual ethics deserves sustained attention without becoming the compulsory explanation of every scene.

This document governs observation, inference, reading order, and per-volume analysis. [MT_SERIES_ARCHITECTURE.md](MT_SERIES_ARCHITECTURE.md) owns destinations, dependencies, initialization, and completion. The character, normative-framing, textual-history, and discourse specifications own their narrower responsibilities. Repository governance outranks all of them.

This method was adopted at bootstrap against the recorded basis commit. No purported quote or conclusion in the preceding conversation is automatically admitted evidence. Design reconnaissance is not a completed V01 reading.

## 2. Primary object and evidence grammar

The principal object is the acquired Japanese commercial light-novel continuity. Establish edition identity in the source lock. The web novel, translations, supplements, illustrations, author commentary, manga, and anime are separately identified witnesses; none silently fills a gap in another.

Use these classes for consequential claims:

| Class | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| `OBSERVATION` | A recoverable feature of the inspected source. |
| `CHARACTER_REPORT` | A represented assertion, thought, memory, belief, or self-description; its truth remains a separate question. |
| `STRONG_INFERENCE` | A supported explanation with alternatives and counterevidence considered. |
| `WORKING_HYPOTHESIS` | A plausible explanation that has not yet been adequately discriminated. |
| `SPECULATION` | An extension beyond close source support. |
| `VALUE_JUDGMENT` | An evaluation with its criterion and descriptive premises stated. |
| `UNRESOLVED` | Missing evidence or viable alternatives prevent adjudication. |

An observation that someone thinks X is not an observation that X is true. A narratorial assertion is not automatically omniscient or sincere. An interpretation may be strongly supported without being explicitly announced by a character.

Keep five questions distinct: what happens; why a character acts; how the work frames the act; what the creator intended; and how an actual reader responded. Evidence for one is not an automatic answer to the others.

## 3. Atomic reading and prospective discipline

One numbered LN volume is one sequential transaction. Chapters and scenes are internal working units. Read all admitted narrative prose in spine/chapter order, including separately accounted interludes and after-stories. Do not infer coverage from a keyword search, a summary, an extracted character count, or file retrieval alone.

Before substantive inspection of VNN, record the entering freeze from the last closed volume. Identify current claims, unresolved alternatives, available character states, relevant source limitations, and a small number of genuine discriminating expectations. Questions need not be converted into predictions.

The analyst may have prior franchise knowledge. Record meaningful exposure and exclude it as evidence; do not call the result blind. A fresh session reduces conversational carryover but does not erase model familiarity. The prior conversation already exposed this project to later material and strong interpretations. Preserve that limitation rather than staging a fictitious first encounter.

During a volume, mark important immediate impressions separately from conclusions informed by the rest of that volume. At exit, the reading may synthesize the complete admitted volume but not later sources. Preserve the entering and exiting freezes. Later reinterpretation belongs in a dated revision entry and the current topical home, not a silent rewrite of the earlier reading.

A missing V12 need not block V01-V11 once they are individually verified and authorized. It blocks crossing V12 in the principal sequential chain. Do not substitute the anime, WN, or a plot summary to manufacture a continuous LN high-water mark.

## 4. Exposure control across lanes

Default to an **LN-only accumulation lane**. External reviews, retrospective interviews, WN comparisons, and adaptation material are not loaded into this reader's active context before the relevant LN reading closes.

The optional paired-comparison arrangement proposed in discussion remains available: finish and freeze an LN unit, then assign an isolated comparison session to eligible WN territory. Do not feed its version-specific findings into the next LN entering freeze. If the same session reads both, record mixed-source exposure and maintain an explicit LN-only evidential projection; do not describe that as an uncontaminated first pass.

A WN chapter mapping must be checked for reordered or later-revealing material before release to a prospective reader. Mapping by title or volume number is insufficient. When safe boundaries cannot be established, defer comparison to a later checkpoint or the end of the main LN pass.

Later-published supplements may disclose earlier events while changing what a reader could have known. Track publication, diegetic placement, and admission date independently. Covers, previews, appendices, and afterwords can contain spoilers; classify them rather than treating everything inside an EPUB as automatically eligible.

## 5. Full reading with selective close analysis

Maintain a compact chapter/scene coverage map. Every admitted narrative unit must be accounted for; not every paragraph needs an evidence row. Use ordinary continuity summaries sparingly and give diagnostic material the space it needs.

A separate opening synopsis should let a reader understand what happens across the present volume in connected prose without decoding the locator table. Compose it after the complete volume pass. For V02 onward, prepare a short, distinct account of developments through the last closed volume before inspecting the next source. That prior-volume account records the inherited narrative situation and open tensions, while the entering freeze preserves the separately timed analytical state and prospective questions. Neither account supplies evidence for an unread unit or replaces the complete coverage map and diagnostic close reading. The [volume template](MT_VOLUME_READING_TEMPLATE.md) implements these reader-facing sections.

A close-reading selection should include both hypothesis-relevant scenes and scenes chosen because they are central to the volume's literary construction. This prevents a controversy search from masquerading as reading the novel. Record significant negative or disconfirming cases, mundane interaction, and events not centered on Rudeus.

Use a second, targeted pass for consequential wording, formal ambiguity, chronology conflicts, or claims whose support may be narrower than the initial interpretation. A full reread is justified only by a material coverage defect or a newly authorized task, not as a ritual.

## 6. Prose, form, and reader position

Analyze the work as prose rather than as an anime transcript:

- narrative voice and the distance between the experiencing person and the narrating voice;
- focalization changes, restricted knowledge, irony, unreliable judgment, and competing accounts;
- scene versus summary, pacing, ellipsis, withheld information, chapter architecture, and delayed disclosure;
- register, syntax, rhetorical questions, self-correction, euphemism, metaphor, and patterns of naming;
- tone shifts among comedy, embarrassment, desire, dread, grief, wonder, and ordinary domesticity;
- motifs, repeated situations, foils, genre expectations, and structural reversals.

Do not assume all distance is irony or all first-person access is endorsement. Explain the concrete textual feature that warrants a formal claim. A joke can undermine, normalize, deflect, or complicate a judgment; its target and placement matter.

Inspect illustrations when making claims about their visual rhetoric. For a prose-complete but not illustration-inspected pass, say so. Illustrations are distinct presentational evidence, not automatic proof of every diegetic detail. Vocal timbre, performed pitch, and actor delivery cannot be inferred from punctuation or remembered anime performances.

## 7. Character cognition and development

Use a diagnostic sequence where it fits:

`represented event -> perception -> interpretation -> affect -> available options -> choice -> consequence -> later self-account`

Distinguish stable tendency, temporary state, role pressure, strategic performance, newly acquired knowledge, newly disclosed information, and genuine revision of commitments. Preserve contradictions that the evidence does not resolve.

Do not collapse development into success. Track competence, confidence, material security, status, attachment, responsibility, moral understanding, and respect for other people's agency separately. Increased power or a happy relationship does not demonstrate changed sexual ethics; continued flaws do not prove that every other domain is unchanged.

Claims of moral learning should identify the relevant earlier baseline and later diagnostic opportunity. Recognition, restitution, restraint, changed practice, and transfer to a new context are different observations. An apology alone does not establish durability. Conversely, the absence of a repeated situation may leave learning untested rather than disproved.

The [reconstruction specification](MT_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_SPEC.md) governs operational models. Daily habits, humor, practical choices, mild frustration, and low-stakes care are necessary evidence, not decorative trivia.

## 8. Rudeus-specific questions without predetermined answers

Track physical age, elapsed second-life time, remembered first-life biography, explicit subjective-age claims, social treatment, and demonstrated maturity as different variables. Do not compute a universal mental age by adding years, subtracting isolation, or adopting whichever age favors the current argument.

Ask how continuity and discontinuity are represented across embodiment, memory, desire, shame, knowledge, relationships, and responsibility. The narrative can provide different answers for different dimensions. Metaphysical continuity does not by itself settle every moral judgment; a moral judgment does not establish a metaphysical fact.

Investigate self-excusing and self-condemning narration symmetrically. Neither must be accepted wholesale. Seek cases where his self-account is accurate, inaccurate, strategically protective, emotionally distorted, or incompletely understood. Do not define every other character by their contribution to his improvement.

## 9. Relationships, agency, and the wider world

Model A->B independently from B->A. Record trust, desire, obligation, dependency, information, power, refusal, initiative, rupture, and repair. Romance, care, family loyalty, and dependence can coexist without being equivalent.

For women and other less-focalized characters, track independent goals, non-romantic commitments, friendships, competencies, mistakes, choices, and worlds not reducible to Rudeus. Do not equate low narrative access with absence of agency, or invent unseen autonomy to compensate for missing evidence.

Treat institutions, labor, household resources, education, status, discrimination, violence, religion, magic, and world rules as possible causes of choices as well as worldbuilding. Distinguish explicit world fact, a character's cultural generalization, local custom, and an inference about broader society. A fictional society's tolerance is not automatically the novel's endorsement or the analyst's justification.

Allow major literary questions to emerge: second chances, self-authorship, competence, belonging, shame, kinship, mortality, freedom, obligation, and the relation between changed circumstances and a changed self. These are research domains, not prewritten series conclusions.

## 10. Normative and discourse work

Apply [MT_NORMATIVE_FRAMING_PROTOCOL.md](MT_NORMATIVE_FRAMING_PROTOCOL.md) to diagnostic cases, including nonsexual ones. Do not infer approval merely from depiction, sympathy, pleasure, absence of punishment, or eventual success. Do not infer critique merely from unpleasant content, self-reproach, retaliation, or the author's stated intention.

Do not impose a fixed moral-improvement trajectory. Compare affected-person access, consequences, recurrence, tone, and later re-evaluation while retaining competing interpretations. A normative contradiction can be an actual finding, but it must be demonstrated rather than used as a safe compromise.

[MT_DISCOURSE_HYPOTHESIS_METHOD.md](MT_DISCOURSE_HYPOTHESIS_METHOD.md) governs reception claims. No requirement exists to keep weak hypotheses alive for balance. Reject, narrow, or reformulate them when the evidence warrants it.

## 11. Japanese wording and locators

Preserve exact wording only when necessary to the argument, with a short contextual explanation and a clearly labeled analyst translation. Do not convert a slang label into a clinical diagnosis, an ambiguous image reference into a specified medium, or a euphemism into a stronger act than the passage supports.

For important claims record witness ID, edition fingerprint, exact chapter label, EPUB spine item/path, and a stable paragraph/element locator. Printed pages are optional and must actually be present. Reflowable display pages are not stable book pagination. A normalized-text locator must map back to its source HTML; record normalizer version and ruby handling.

Suggested local evidence identity: `MT-E-LNJP-V01-001`. The volume reading owns the analytical observation and its locator. Ledgers and models cite it rather than creating duplicate discoveries. Raw text and deterministic locator maps remain in the evidence plane.

Keep sexual-abuse analysis non-graphic and proportionate. Brief, accurate description is normally sufficient; do not reproduce explicit sexual passages involving children in public artifacts. A private lawful source route can preserve verification without a public excerpt.

## 12. Closing a volume

A completed transaction requires the deep reading, complete declared coverage, source locators, entering/exiting freezes, affected ledger updates, claim revisions, relevant model/readiness updates, and an accurate current-state record. For each required ledger, record either an update or an explained no-material-update finding. Do not pad a ledger to satisfy a row count.

Separate drafted, semantically reviewed, transaction-closed, committed, branch-published, and integrated-to-main states. A successful write is not proof of semantic completeness. A closed volume may remain branch-local pending the repository's publication lifecycle; report that state exactly.

Never advance the high-water mark after a partial reading. Never cross the owner's authorized terminal volume. Bootstrap authorization is not authorization to read V01, and an open readiness gate does not create run authorization.

## 13. Quality and amendment

Evaluate depth by explanatory mechanisms, exact evidence, state specificity, formal analysis, consequential alternatives, and preserved uncertainty. Neither a word count nor a filled template is a quality certificate.

Run maintenance checkpoints after V05, V10, V15, V20, and V26 if that corpus remains the approved scope; these are administrative boundaries, not assumed arcs. Add semantic checkpoints for a major disclosure, changed source access, or a newly emergent analytical responsibility. Amend the architecture deliberately and backfill only the gaps that matter.

Methodological lineage: RAG's prospective transaction/evidence grammar and Tomozaki's state/self-theory discipline, adapted to prose and this project's goals. The repository documents are methodological precedents, not Mushoku Tensei evidence.
