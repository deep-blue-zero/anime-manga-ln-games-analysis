# Knowledge, trust and divided duties through V11

This checkpoint responds to major new access to Norn's account and to the correction of Nanahoshi's earlier teleport-location claim. It uses the complete [V11 reading](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MT_V11_DEEP_READING.md), the [V01–V10 cumulative checkpoint](MT_V01_V10_CHECKPOINT.md) and the earlier [V09 disclosure checkpoint](MT_V09_DISCLOSURE_AND_RECOVERY_CHECKPOINT.md). It is not another scheduled cumulative review; V15 remains due. Source observations stay in readings, history in six ledgers, operational rules in character packages. Prior franchise exposure is disclosed and excluded as evidence.

The shared consequence is proposition-level correction, not a global ranking of reliable and unreliable people. Norn's view is indispensable partly because it contains both a false rumor and an accurate account of her fear. Nanahoshi's practical help is indispensable despite earlier withholding. Rudeus changes several judgments while continuing the habit of filling gaps with plausible stories. C003/009/014 therefore need narrower knowledge states rather than a single revelation that makes everyone transparent.

## Assistance and understanding remain separable

The V10 checkpoint distinguished care, technical help and relational repair. V11 strengthens that distinction: Rudeus helps Norn precisely when he relinquishes a confident explanation, while the reader sees that her own thought has prepared the encounter. He later offers lessons, but she chooses when he may meet her friends and what she can contribute to a book. Assistance is not ownership of the recipient's account. This revises C012/015 without making the earlier intervention useless or crediting all subsequent development to it.

Aisha exposes a parallel problem in a different form. A gifted child's efficient performance can conceal strain and unspoken interests. Good care requires responding to those interests even when they are not useful to the household. Wages and decoration are small-scale evidence of expanded room to choose, but they coexist with inherited service rank and sibling contempt. Neither child should become a convenient measure of Rudeus's redemption.

Cliff and Elinalise similarly combine useful support with interpretive limits. Cliff offers a practicable response to waiting while speaking dismissively and imagining a future the narrator expressly qualifies. Elinalise protects Rudeus, supplies experience and names kinship, yet misunderstands part of his reaction to killing and escalates toward conflict with Carmelita. C007/008/013 retain independent purposes and real contributions without upgrading helpers to universal moral or epistemic authorities.

## The departure cannot be judged through an unavailable outcome

The decision begins with competing family claims, a delayed letter and an unexplained warning. Elinalise's intention to travel already exists; Norn's intervention changes Rudeus's appraisal of his own capacity and responsibility. The later map reduces separation costs after he chooses to go. Treating the final short route as something he knew during the initial decision would corrupt the sequence. Treating Norn as solely responsible for the adult decision would repeat her frightened self-account as a verdict.

The journey tests available capacities but does not settle the warning. Elinalise's expertise, Rudeus's magic, a merchant's route and guards' scouting jointly make progress possible. His escape from the ambush is defensible on known danger and uncertainty, while Tonto's lost life and Carmelita's grief remain real. A thought that direct killing never entered his options is not the same as a principled nonviolence rule. Possible indirect deaths are conjectured, not counted. The volume ends at Rapan; rescue, reunion and the warning's meaning remain beyond the verified boundary.
