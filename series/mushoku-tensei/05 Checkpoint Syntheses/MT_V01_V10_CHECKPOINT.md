# V01–V10: capability, care and the work of trust

Across these ten volumes, people acquire abilities and relationships without acquiring a common timetable of emotional or ethical change. The [V01–V05 checkpoint](MT_V01_V05_CHECKPOINT.md) argued that recognizing exceptional competence can create burdens: others assume its bearer knows more, needs less or can bear more. V06–V10 preserve that mechanism and add a sharper limit. Correcting a proposition does not necessarily change the feeling or practice organized around it. Roxy recognizes a mistaken threat while fear persists. Norn hears a fuller account while trust remains absent. Rudeus acknowledges harm and nevertheless repeats a stated boundary violation. More information can matter decisively; its reception cannot stand in for every other required change.

## 1. Capability is acquired within systems of help

V01's extraordinary magical capacity is real; so are practice, books, shelter and Roxy's teaching. V02's instruction succeeds through adaptation, meaningful tasks and other adults' labor. Travel adds language, money, local knowledge, bodily constraints and Ruijerd's protection. The eye in V04 supplies anticipated motion without guaranteed interpretation or execution. V05's exceptional spell ranks likewise fail as complete measures of fighting or teaching ability. These cases reject the sufficiency of giftedness without denying it (V01:005–008; V02:006–011; V03:005/019–020; V04:003–005/014; V05:018–019).

Later evidence changes the scale, not the core distinction. Orsted exposes severe vulnerability despite enormous output. Northern work remains possible during distress but requires specialists and contracts. The university converts unusual ability into institutional access while craft work exposes differences among strength, mana and precision. V10 then gives the strongest linked demonstration: the house investigation preserves an artifact whose structure becomes an indispensable idea for another person's research. The eventual bottle is produced through differentiated contributions, including Nanahoshi's implementation. It is not just a hero accepting ceremonial help (V06:016/018; V07:007–010/015–019; V08:013–016; V10:004–007/021).

The alternative merit reading remains partly right: effort and unusual ability repeatedly change outcomes. It fails when generalized into comprehensive independence or deserving authority over collaborators. Aisha's fast route exhausts both children; her planning still requires adult protection. Ghislaine values knowledge even while her teacher assesses slower martial growth. Paul expressly asks that ordinary Norn not be measured by exceptional siblings. The cumulative question is therefore how capability changes a task and a claim on care, not whether one person possesses more capability overall (V10:017/024/029–030).

## 2. Belonging can depend on usefulness without being exhausted by it

The first checkpoint joined Lilia's dependence, Geese's lost party role, Eris's desire for work and Ruijerd's warrior expectations. Later volumes make self-assessment more dangerous. Eris leaves to become a fit partner, assuming her meaning will be understood. Rudeus reads rejection. Sara falsely presents her approach as repayment, and the account he receives confirms a different fear from the one she is trying to defend. Sylphiette distinguishes helping Rudy from standing beside him. Zanoba asks to contribute more than money, and Nanahoshi's project cannot be assimilated to Rudy's contentment in his new home (V06:024–026; V07:020–025; V09:029–032; V10:006/016/021–022).

A wholly instrumental theory cannot explain the same corpus. Ruijerd's escort, Norn's care, Roxy's parents, Soldat's listening and Zanoba's admission that he cannot understand Rudy's feeling include support that is not paid back by immediate excellence. Nor does dependency make every chosen relationship false. Sylphiette's work, savings, requests and marriage choice are represented actions. Elinalise's guard relief turns a newly recognized family bond into practical help. The distinction is between usefulness as one valued contribution and usefulness as a condition whose failure would allegedly remove the right to care (V05:004/013/023; V07:021–024; V10:002/008/015–016/020/032).

This framework must also resist turning everyone into an illustration of Rudy's need. Nanahoshi retains an incompatible final destination. Ghislaine's educational value is her own. Ruijerd leaves for his search, and Norn's attachment to him creates a problem the protagonist cannot resolve simply by possessing a home. These independent purposes are not narrative defects awaiting a single shared goal.

## 3. Information has several different failure points

The cumulative evidence supports a useful distinction among absent information, concealed information, mistaken interpretation and information that is acknowledged without governing feeling or conduct. V05's unreceived notices differ from Eris's assumption that Rudy already knows; Gustav's recognized reason for correction differs from both. V06's short departure message supplies words without shared meaning. V07 adds defensive falsehood and clarification that arrives too late. V08 adds a heard objection bypassed during planned coercion. V09's language exclusion and Fitts role complicate who can evaluate a proposition at all (V05:007/017/027–028; V06:025–026; V07:020–025; V08:019–025; V09:006/011–014/030).

V10 prevents a simple remedy of supplying facts. Roxy's earlier knowledge becomes usable after Lilia connects the identity, yet fear survives recognition of the mistake. Norn has heard Rudy's hardships but is still frightened by a drunk arrival and uncertain attachment. Rudy can imagine an accurate explanation for Eris without possessing confirmation or feeling repaired. These states are not interchangeable stubbornness. They differ in evidence, affective history, available action and the cost of trust (V10:023/026/030–034).

The distinction does not absolve every failure. Explicitly hearing a boundary and disregarding it raises a different responsibility from not knowing it. Likewise, Nanahoshi's confidence cannot substitute for a collaborator's safety understanding. Her failed test and global conclusion are different propositions; the later redesign shows why that difference matters. C003 retains proposition/access distinctions, C009 concerns how presentation shapes audiences, and C014 concerns what a person can responsibly do with available information. Their overlap is intentional interaction, not duplicate ownership.

## 4. Local improvement needs comparable tests

A no-change interpretation would discard real events: Rudeus ventures outside with help, adapts lessons, receives correction, offers immediate aid, develops consultation, accepts care, honors some privacy and builds a household. An automatic-redemption interpretation fails equally specific evidence: staged danger, delayed rescue, repeat bodily violations, punitive captivity and ignored objections. These are not points on a moral balance sheet. The relevant comparison concerns an available action under stated knowledge, power and relationship conditions (V01:008/011; V02:003/009/014; V03:012–020; V08:019–025).

V10 offers a particularly strong test because the same partner names a boundary that later recurs. Rudy's first apology and subsequent repeat cannot be explained by permanent ignorance. At the same time, he stops before entering the sisters' room and honors Nanahoshi's privacy conditions. Those acts show a possible practice without proving consistent selection. His acknowledged harm in the earlier intimate encounter is newly material self-judgment; it does not retroactively change the original conditions or serve as sufficient proof of future restraint (V10:007–010/015/018/025).

Other characters prevent a protagonist-only scheme. Eris's discipline does not make obstruction-triggered aggression proportionate. Zanoba's attentive help coexists with striking Cliff and retaining an enslaved child. Sylphiette can advocate a useful invitation and recommend punitive enforcement in the same discussion. Paul can arrange material safety while his daughter's fear remains substantial. These are specific conflicts requiring explanation, not generic assertions that everyone is complex.

## 5. Closure is plural, and form keeps reopening its limits

A property can become safe while its history stays conjectural; a bottle can arrive while a human return remains unproved; an escort can succeed while the household lacks trust. Earlier volumes already differentiated administrative disaster closure from survivors' lives, public settlement from coerced silence, bodily recovery from ethical repair, and a father's reconciliation from a daughter's response. C015 gathers those distinctions without claiming every ending is false. Each achieved outcome must be named before its limit can be assessed (V08:001/025/028; V09:029–032; V10:005/021/026/034).

The formal means differ. Rudy's intimate narration makes his fear accessible while often reserving others' motives for conjecture. The bard frame, renovation performance, repeated mornings, retrospective extras and scene-specific role disclosure reorganize the reader's access in different ways. Juli's titled extra does not automatically give her interiority; Norn's V10 focalization does. Eris's prolepsis is genuine information in an admitted volume but belongs to a different story-time position. Treating all these devices as an undifferentiated unreliable narrator loses the exact corrections they enable.

Comic treatment can soften perceived harm or hurry attention past an affected person; that is a material counterreading. It cannot be resolved by a universal claim about endorsement. The books also preserve refusals, pain, shame, countervoices and care that exceed the protagonist's favored account. This checkpoint evaluates those textual mechanisms. It makes no inference about authorial intent, audience prevalence or an adaptation's performance.
