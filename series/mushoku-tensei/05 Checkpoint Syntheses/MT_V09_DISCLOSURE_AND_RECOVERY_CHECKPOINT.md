The new material changes causal explanations rather than simply adding facts. Sylphiette's silence no longer needs to be explained by a permission that she did not have. Rudy's confident victory cannot explain away a later mask-triggered collapse because the perceived threats differ. A useful change in Nina's practice cannot prove that her estimate of his combat superiority was correct. C003 and C014 therefore retain proposition-level access: who learned what, from which source, at which time, with which alternative still open.

## Recovery is consequential but has several measures

The sequence from V07's injury and failed reassurance, through V08's school pleasure and unresolved condition, to V09's recognition, disclosure and morning report establishes real movement. It would now be inaccurate to preserve a current claim that nothing about Rudy's condition changes. But the source does not isolate a single cause. Recognition, trust, fear of departure, support, substance, intoxication and continued presence occupy the same chain. Nor does recovery license the methods automatically: partial risk disclosure, bodily cost and Rudy's acknowledged lack of restraint remain represented.

Sylphiette's ending has a different measure. She has been recognized and has helped; she is less certain that she stands beside him in the sense she desired. Her satisfaction and pain are both given direct access. This prevents two reductions: treating her solely as an instrument of his cure, and treating her expressed happiness as a complete answer to every consent or power question. C012 separates kinds of support and outcomes, C013 separates utility from equal standing, and C015 separates a locally satisfying ending from comprehensive affected-person repair.

Comparison with earlier coercive scenes must retain differences. The staged rain conceals a plan and produces real risk. Yet Rudy offers privacy, and Sylphiette requests help. Those immediate conditions differ from V01's forced exposure and V08's captivity. A romantic recollection cannot repair an old violation, but neither can the old violation erase every present choice. The substantive task is to identify what changed, what was known and what remained constrained, without forcing the cases into one undifferentiated category.
