# Knowing vulnerability and choosing for others

V06 warrants a targeted checkpoint because new access changes how earlier actions can be explained. Eris's sustained retrospective account supplies motives that Rudeus never received. Lilia's account expands the affected person's history and makes her reasoning about Aisha available to the reader. Hitogami offers explanations with large consequences but incomplete independent support. These disclosures require different updates: established new access to a person's experience, a character's interpretation of someone else's experience, and an interested speaker's proposed account of the world cannot enter the same certainty category.

This checkpoint owns that dependency review. The [V06 reading](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MT_V06_DEEP_READING.md) owns all new observations; `V06:024`, for example, means `MT-E-LNJP-V06-024`. The [claims ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MT_CLAIMS_AND_REVISIONS_LEDGER.md), [knowledge ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MT_CHRONOLOGY_AND_KNOWLEDGE_LEDGER.md) and [state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MT_CHARACTER_STATE_AND_READINESS_LEDGER.md) own their numbered records. The [V01–V05 checkpoint](MT_V01_V05_CHECKPOINT.md) remains an unchanged historical synthesis. Its distinction between competence, care and recognition survives; the new question is what happens when someone correctly recognizes vulnerability and still acts on a mistaken theory of what care requires.

The frozen input is final audited V05 head `3dc6b173b044abdafc013dc989bd96914620d13d`; the V06 entering freeze preceded inspection and remains unchanged. The containing publication identifies the new candidate's accepted bytes. No later LN, WN, adaptation, reception or remembered continuation is evidence. This is a source-bounded diagnostic review, not a prospectively blind test.

## 1. A corrected explanation can leave a second error intact

Rudeus interprets Eris's departure as rejection. The reader has already received her quite different account: she admires him, fears inadequacy and wants the strength to stand beside him. Her departure for training follows that reasoning. It is therefore possible to reject Rudeus's breakup explanation within this volume without predicting reunion or endorsing Eris's theory of the relationship. Affection is established more clearly than the adequacy of her communication or her estimate of his resilience (V06:023–026).

The new focalization revisits the earlier tutoring and journey. Eris reports pressure, changes in appraisal and wishes not accessible in Rudeus's earlier narration. These are newly revealed historical states, not changes occurring at the instant the reader learns of them. They revise the current Eris model while leaving earlier observations and freezes intact. Even this fuller account is partial: she interprets his post-attack magical experiment as renewed resolve, whereas he frames it through fear and the need to escape. Her report of hitting a distracted Ruijerd also requires retaining a discrepancy with Rudeus's earlier account, rather than silently replacing one remembered statistic with another (V06:014,018,024).

V05 showed Paul and Eris assuming that a competent Rudeus already possessed information. V06 adds a different mechanism. Eris recognizes his vulnerability but concludes that her own inadequacy is the central obstacle to helping him. The burden explanation can therefore survive evidence of his need. This revises C012 and strengthens the narrower C013 question about the costs of making usefulness a condition of one's own belonging. It does not support a general claim that the relationship is merely transactional: her concern, his care, and their history remain substantial counterevidence.

The distinction matters for C014. Eris knowingly withholds her destination to prevent intervention. She is not shown knowing that Rudeus interprets the note as rejection and then choosing to preserve that specific error. Gustav's V05 case involved recognized error and an available correction; these conditions do not automatically transfer. The material result can still be harmful without inventing knowledge or an intention to inflict that harm. The [relationship ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MT_RELATIONSHIP_AND_AGENCY_LEDGER.md) records the unequal decision access rather than reducing this to either malicious abandonment or blameless love.

## 2. New affected-person access does not erase earlier judgment

Lilia's retrospective narrative gives her resistance to Paul's earlier assault, subsequent emotional and behavioral effects, and her father's willingness to support a different future. It supports the existing identification of harm through her own focalized history. This adds evidence; it does not turn a previously justified judgment into a judgment that had to wait for graphic detail. Her later affection, family membership and care for Aisha do not retroactively authorize the earlier act (V06:030; compare V01's household history).

Her present reasoning also becomes more complex. She wants Aisha to be safe and loved, interprets service to Rudeus as protection, and recognizes an unsettling likeness between what she prescribes and her own history. The final embrace matters as care. It does not establish that she has withdrawn the assigned role or that Aisha has freely selected it. Aisha's recognition of her brother, observational ability, criticism and learned presentation show agency within this arrangement, not unrestricted control over its terms (V06:006,013,029–031).

The update belongs to C005's household protection, means and negotiation question, with C002/C007/C013 and the normative/agency records carrying related comparisons. C004 remains the V01 Sylphie boundary claim. A new survival report for Sylphie and an unrelated later relationship do not revise the evidence of her original refusal. Keeping these IDs distinct prevents an expansive label such as “consent” from replacing the particular people and situations each claim originally analyzed.

Lilia's account also changes the scope of the Paul package without creating a new present-day Paul state. It is additional evidence about an earlier act and its aftermath. The existing V01/V05 caregiving model remains bounded; it receives a historical check, not a new pass on listening, parenting or restraint. A model that measures only newly favorable family interactions would omit the relevant history, while a model that turns this disclosure into an unseen current action would collapse chronology.

## 3. Explanations can be useful before they become established laws

The Orsted encounter supplies severe observed asymmetries in knowledge and power. His familiarity with the companions, surprise concerning Rudeus and reaction to Hitogami's name are observations. Hitogami's subsequent account of curses, visibility and attenuation supplies a possible explanation with practical and emotional value, especially for Ruijerd. Rudeus also notices an inconsistency. That account therefore belongs in attributed knowledge records, not as an independently established universal mechanism (V06:015–019).

Ruijerd's response shows that tentative information can open hope. Rudeus gives the explanation with a qualification; the relationship permits emotion without requiring certainty or an immediate strategic use. The later farewell supplies reciprocity and continued obligation without retaining the party's previous dependence. These scenes strengthen an account of care that includes shared uncertainty, practical gifts and respect for departure. They do not establish that social hostility is only supernatural or that the past can be reduced to a single explanatory cause (V06:019–020).

The same discipline applies to the search interlude. Kishirika's sight produces a lead about Zenith and an approximate place, not a completed rescue or full account of her condition. Roxy probes the report and organizes action while maintaining a private wish for reunion. Her later mistaken assumption about Kishirika's travel preserves a local countercase to comprehensive knowledge. Rudeus has not yet received the lead at this boundary (V06:027–028). W013 and K053–055/K061–062 retain these limits; C003/C010 can strengthen without making all uncertainty equivalent or all testimony unreliable.

## 5. Exit questions

The next source can test whether people communicate needs and revise burden assumptions, whether new search information actually arrives, and whether skill practice alters choices under changed support. These are questions, not imported outcomes. V06 strengthens the need to track who knows what, when the knowledge becomes available, and what options remain open to the person ostensibly being helped. Correctly identifying love, vulnerability or a plausible cause is only one step in explaining what a decision does.

The targeted checkpoint is complete only with the V06 reading, six ledgers, five revised model/index packages and verified source lock. Publication, remote readback, source audit, housekeeping and final exact-head audit remain separately required. V07 stays unopened until that transaction closes; the next scheduled cumulative reviews remain V10 and V15.
