# V01–V05: learning, dependence and the limits of recognition

This first cumulative checkpoint asks what the five complete numbered-volume readings support together. It is a historical synthesis at the V05 boundary, not a claim that five volumes form a completed narrative arc. The central finding is that learning enlarges people's options through relationships and resources, while recognition of that ability can create new forms of misjudgment. Exceptional competence repeatedly invites someone to assume that its bearer knows more, needs less or can bear more than the evidence warrants. Repair occurs when those assumptions become particular enough to challenge. Its success remains local: care for one person does not automatically become a general practice of listening, restraint or equal treatment.

## 1. Competence is personal, distributed and uneven

V01 gives Rudeus unusual magical capacity, curiosity and sustained effort. It also gives him books, shelter, time, parental decisions and Roxy's teaching. Her role in helping him cross the boundary of his family enclosure is specific enough that later claims of wholly independent genius cannot explain the same event. V02 makes the reciprocal structure clearer. Teaching Eris requires adaptation to a learner, practical uses, familiar bodily skill, rest and other adults' work. Ghislaine has her own learning needs and reasons to value education. The fact that Rudeus is exceptionally capable does not make his attempted transfer of incantationless magic universally effective, nor does useful teaching establish that the manufactured kidnapping was necessary (V01:005–008; V02:003,006–011).

The journey intensifies this dependence without reducing him to a passive beneficiary. Roxy's resources and Ruijerd's expertise enable survival; Rudeus supplies language, money plans, magic and instruction. Eris can supply the practical market suggestion he overlooks. V04's eye gift requires calibration, and accurate anticipation of motion still fails when interpretation and bodily response are inadequate. Geese and the sacred beast change a fight's outcome. These are actual causal contributions, not ceremonial acknowledgments of teamwork (V03:003–005,013,019–020; V04:003–005,008,014,017–018,022).

V05 repeats the distinction through Cliff. His spell rank is impressive; the spell is poorly suited to collecting goblin ears and coordinating with an ally. Eris's own skill solves a forest problem after pride helps cause it. Sylphie's newly reported silent healing demonstrates a competence Rudeus lacks. The restaurant owner makes the same separation in a comic register: great martial standing does not secure good trade or cooking. The synthesis therefore strengthens C001/C008 as a claim about the relation among capacity, task, practice and support. A counterreading that stresses exceptional endowment remains strong; the evidence rejects its sufficiency, not its existence (V05:012,018–019,025).

## 2. Admiration can distribute burdens as well as credit

The new usefulness/belonging question C013 began explicitly in V04: Geese loses a role when a party breaks up, Eris wants a task when others perform the camp work, Ruijerd treats a recognized warrior as self-sufficient, and Fitts fears losing shelter if no longer useful. Earlier evidence can be compared without pretending the claim was already formulated: Lilia's material dependence, Ghislaine's educational need and the party's worry about paying for protection provide distinct precursors. The retrospective comparison does not rewrite their original source-era interpretations (V01:004,013,016; V03:007,009,018; V04:018–025).

Paul's V05 account offers a particularly explicit mechanism. He admires Rudeus's capability and therefore assumes that his son should know the disaster, investigate the missing and manage a dangerous journey. Geese's correction must separate strength from information and care. Eris produces a quieter version of the same error: because Rudeus is competent and protective, she assumes he has read the notices and need not mention them. Roxy's praise similarly imagines an easy journey and minimizes her own formative contribution. These speakers do not share one motive, but their errors share an inference from ability to comprehensive self-sufficiency (V05:006,009,017,024).

An exclusively transactional account would fail the counterevidence. Ruijerd offers escort without payment, parents care for Norn without exceptional performance, Eris comforts Rudeus in failure, and Roxy's parents seek her company despite the communication difference. Usefulness explains some pressures within belonging; it does not exhaust affection. The stronger formulation asks who is allowed to be incapable or uninformed while retaining care. That question integrates the new evidence better than either an inspirational merit narrative or a claim that every bond is exploitation.

## 3. Accounts can help people act together, or hide what they need to contest

V01 already distinguishes hearing an account from accepting its first explanation: the bullying dispute, Sylphie's misidentification and the household crisis turn on uneven information and competing stakes. V02 adds staged rescue, official publicity and political recruitment. V03 makes the danger of managing gratitude unmistakable: Rudeus waits to increase its value and a child dies. A favorable interpretation from a beneficiary or protector does not establish the motive the reader has been shown. In the later guild crisis, a public narrative protects the children by assigning agency and blame to Ruijerd, even against Rudeus's protest (V01:009–013; V02:003–005,013; V03:012–018).

V04 and V05 prevent treating all planning as manipulation. Immediate assistance can precede reward language. Consultative meetings can bring in useful information. Most decisively, the V05 reunion is deliberately reenacted. Rudeus asks Paul to enter the scene with him rather than secretly arranging danger to secure a response. Its sincerity is compatible with performance because its terms become shared. Smiley and Ariel likewise cooperate in a transparent cover identity, while the customs scene mixes genuine need with invented status claims. C009 must distinguish what an account makes available for consent or contest, not merely whether it is crafted (V04:003,005,013,017; V05:002,011,014,021,026).

The final investigation adds C014. Gustav's initial error relies on limited testimony, but later he recognizes a reason to correct it and chooses not to act. This is analytically different from Rudeus never receiving a notice or Eris assuming someone else has it. Responsibility changes with actual knowledge and the opportunity to repair. An all-perspectives-are-partial thesis cannot explain that difference unless it also attends to what a speaker does when the limit becomes visible (V05:007,017,027–028).

## 4. Particular restraint and repair do not prove general ethical maturation

The admitted record contains actual care, actual restraint and actual repeated harm. V01's refusal, apology and continued caution are not annulled by later affection. V02's interrupted intention on one occasion cannot guarantee respect on another. V03's comfort and ongoing outside restraint distinguish an available better choice from its consistent selection. V04 supplies a more diagnostic unpoliced illness-care scene, then renewed pressure and a privacy violation. These comparisons require knowledge, dependency, opportunity and the affected person's options; they cannot be reduced to a balance sheet of good and bad deeds (V01:011–013; V02:009,014; V03:009,020; V04:008,018,021).

V05's new consent-respecting intention matters because it identifies refusal beyond a simple age condition. Its future reliability is not yet observed. The father–son repair likewise matters, but Rudeus still misreads the women supporting his father and later humiliates the cook. Eris's care neither makes her revenge proportionate nor disappears because she seeks revenge. Paul's rescue work does not absolve his assault history or initial blow, and those harms do not make his care for Norn fictitious. This is a demanding differentiated judgment, not mixedness as an automatic compromise (V05:004–015,019,022,025).

The strongest objection is that recurrent comedy can soften the apparent cost of misconduct. That objection has local support: exaggerated reactions, sexual jokes and Cliff's comic injury direct attention toward protagonists' experience. But the source also repeatedly gives refusals, injured responses, outside restraint, alternative accounts and consequences that exceed the joke. The analytical task is to identify where those mechanisms operate and where affected-person access remains thin. It cannot infer authorial intent, audience prevalence or a universal endorsement from register alone. The [normative ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MT_NORMATIVE_FRAMING_LEDGER.md) retains the case conditions and matched comparisons.

## 5. Movement and recognition are structurally incomplete

The five volumes move from a gate crossed with help to forced removal, displacement, regional travel, detention and another sea crossing. These are different changes in agency. Going farther does not mean choosing more freely, knowing more accurately or arriving home. Guild rules, fares, money, seasonal rain, official classifications and patronage repeatedly determine which movement is possible. The household tutor's access to an influential family can help, recruit or constrain; the same ambiguity persists at the customs office (V01:008,015; V02:013,017–019; V03:006–007,010–018; V04:001,008–015; V05:014,020–022,026).

Personal recognition can interrupt exclusion without changing its rule. The village that thanks Ruijerd refuses the figurine. Therese supplies passage because of obligations while retaining anti-demon commitments. Smiley helps a particular princess through discretion once his loyalty becomes salient. These are strong limits on a simple model in which visible good conduct necessarily converts a hostile institution. They do not prove reform impossible. They show what this source boundary has actually achieved: specific openings, not general equality (V05:016,021–022,026).

Form makes incompleteness legible. Alternative viewpoints do more than reveal secret facts; they reveal different frames for facts already encountered. Repeated scenes, delayed interludes and backward-moving extras separate reading order from event order. V05's chapter title places reunion after recognition and quarrel, while its later meal retains refusals outside the successful dyad. The final inquiry offers multiple evidential stages rather than a single omniscient correction. There is nevertheless sufficient local authority to distinguish an explicitly false report from unresolved interpretation. A formal account that made everything unknowable would discard some of the book's clearest distinctions.

## 7. Missing perspectives and next discriminating questions

The source gives limited direct access to many displaced survivors, enslaved people, servants and harmed bystanders. Their absence is a real limit on conclusions about rescue, household arrangements or repair. Paul reports Zenith and Lilia's negotiation; that is not their new interior account. Sylphie's study is reported, not a current encounter. Neither Fitts's original identity nor missing relatives' current fates can be filled from familiarity. The final extras announce consequences without granting detailed future knowledge. These are interpretive/source-bound limits, not missing EPUB pages: all five admitted witnesses have complete declared coverage.

The next useful tests concern transfer under changed conditions: whether an expressed need changes a capable person's assigned burden; whether recognized error produces repair before it threatens the speaker's advantage; whether care remains available after refusal or failure; whether consultation conveys unwelcome information before a plan is fixed; and whether a personal exception becomes a changed general rule. Counterexamples include continued concealment, narrowed compassion, newly coercive care or refusal of correction. No particular future plot is predicted.
