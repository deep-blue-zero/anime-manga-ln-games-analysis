## What changes in the argument

V06 established that Eris intended a future partnership while Rudeus received rejection. V07 lets that false belief persist through useful work and several new relationships. It is therefore inadequate to treat competence as either a cure for distress or evidence that distress is insincere. The bear expedition restores a will to continue; the ruins supply reciprocal protection; the forest rescue supplies gratitude; ordinary labor supplies contact; and Soldat eventually supplies a practical route away. Each changes something real. None alone supplies secure interpretation of another person's feelings or restores the bodily response Rudeus has lost. The epilogue's functioning arrangement is evidence of accommodation, not proof that all these domains have converged. See V07 observations `002–005,007,009,013–024,026` in [Section D](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MT_V07_DEEP_READING.md#d-diagnostic-close-readings).

Sara's final section adds an independent but also limited account. Her affection was real, her claim of mere obligation defensive, and her explanation of the later night false. These corrections do not erase her injury when she hears Rudeus demean her. Nor does understanding her injury prove her accusation that their whole relationship was a fraud. The two people have acquired enough information to reconsider at different moments; the opportunity for direct repair closes before their knowledge becomes mutually available. The reader is better informed than either, without becoming entitled to treat a reconciliation as completed. Observations `018–020,023–025` supply the sequence.

The extra supplies a useful countercase to an exclusively compassionate account of misunderstanding. Ariel's group does not merely fail to communicate. It designs a difference between what the targets can hear and what the public can hear, then disseminates a selectively true account of the confrontation. The prior bullying is real and the relief is real, but the perception of unprovoked innocence is manufactured. This is a different relation to truth from Sara's initial ignorance, her resistance to contrary evidence, or Rudeus's inability to ask for clarification. Observations `028–030` prevent C014 from treating all partial knowledge as one kind of responsibility.

## Model decision and calibration

The [Rudeus model](../04%20Character%20Analysis/rudeus/RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md) now includes explicit new selectors for arrival, renewed work, increasing attachment, disclosure and supported departure. An all-purpose “responds to stress” rule would explain too much. The comparison must distinguish the quiet acceptance of abuse from the later drunken outburst, and identify both the different immediate injury and the changed availability of disclosure. It must also resist inferring that his own negative summary accurately describes every observed act. The [evidence index](../04%20Character%20Analysis/rudeus/EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) routes the new contrasts without rewriting earlier tests.

A first [Sara model](../04%20Character%20Analysis/sara/RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md) is warranted for a restricted responsibility. She has independent focalization, ordinary work and financial choices, an initially false category, repeated contrary evidence, costly protective action, growing affection, defensive communication and a later but unperformed correction. These are different contexts, not repeated citations to one rescue. A bounded operational account can distinguish her responses before and after each change in knowledge. Its [index](../04%20Character%20Analysis/sara/EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) keeps the near analogies narrow. It cannot supply a later adult state, universal jealousy, a generic romantic script or knowledge of a condition she explicitly lacks.

Soldat receives a substantial ledger assessment but no standalone model here. His change of conduct and practical care are clear; much of his broader pattern is reported by himself or through Rudeus's retrospective evaluation. Timothy, Suzanne, Elise and Ariel's group likewise remain interpretable without proliferating new operational packages. This is a scope decision, not a denial of their agency. Existing Eris, Roxy, Ruijerd and Paul packages need no new present-state event: recollection and influence are not direct new access to them.

All readiness remains BOUNDED_PROVISIONAL in specified domains. Rudeus1.4 adds ST16–20 and seven checks V24–30, including a substantive role qualification to rule008; Sara1.0 contains six rules, six states and seven checks. The comparisons are retrospective fitting and negative-constraint review. The entering freeze registered questions, not outcome predictions; there is no forecast score or clean holdout to claim. A useful failure test is whether a proposed rule makes Rudeus unable to perform mundane work whenever distressed, Sara incapable of recognizing contrary evidence, Soldat's past cruelty secretly benevolent, or Ariel's victory wholly spontaneous. Each would conflict with the admitted sequence.

Recovery should be represented across these responsibilities, rather than owned by a new diagnosis file. No specialist monograph is needed simply because the theme recurs.
