# Rudeus through V10: becoming useful, becoming known

Rudeus repeatedly makes himself useful before he becomes securely understood. Magical practice, teaching, travel work and research give him ways to act, earn a place and seek approval. Those accomplishments are substantial. They also leave a recurrent problem untouched: he can mistake usefulness for control over how another person will interpret him, or mistake affection for access to what that person wants. Across V01–V10, development therefore appears less as a continuous rise in maturity than as an expanding set of practices whose selection remains uneven. He learns to ask, disclose, comfort and sometimes stop. He also knows reasons that fail to govern his next choice.

This monograph explains that interaction rather than deciding whether all gains cancel all harms. Its input is audited V09 `40018b5caedfba456da199ed2fea613ec991015a` plus completely inspected V10, bounded by the [V01–V10 checkpoint](../../05%20Checkpoint%20Syntheses/MT_V01_V10_CHECKPOINT.md). The [evidence index](EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) routes references such as V03:014 to canonical volume observations; the [model](RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md) owns operational rules and the [shared ledger](../../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MT_CHARACTER_STATE_AND_READINESS_LEDGER.md) owns observed states. No new evidence IDs, later LN, WN, adaptation, reception, diagnosis or remembered future enter this argument. Prior franchise familiarity is disclosed. The argument is mature enough for this partial boundary; it is not a completed-life or full-series judgment.

## Competence gives him a route into the world

The second life makes unusual capacity available, but practice turns that capacity into usable work. Rudeus tries, compares and repeats. He has books and time, receives Roxy's instruction and eventually crosses an enclosure whose significance exceeds mere physical distance. The teacher's contribution matters precisely because his account elsewhere can exaggerate the sufficiency of his own effort or someone else's genius. The first state transition is neither effortless rebirth nor an isolated act of will: a specific relationship changes what he can attempt (V01:005–008).

Teaching Eris makes this process reciprocal. A capable performer is not automatically a capable teacher, and the learner does not respond uniformly to instruction. He adjusts difficulty, connects work to recognizable purposes and learns from responses. Other adults sustain the arrangement. The pleasure of successful instruction combines care, accomplishment and approval. That mixture is a better explanation than either wholly altruistic vocation or wholly counterfeit service. It also makes failure diagnostic: impatience, overpraise and the learner's resistance prevent a rule that his intelligence always produces the right method (V02:006–011).

Travel and school expose further limits. Language and magical output cannot supply every institution's rules, anticipate every weapon or make the body execute a foreseen movement. High capacity coexists with dependence on Ruijerd, Geese, specialists, money and political access. Zanoba's craft difficulty defeats a supposedly transferable method. In V10, Rudy cannot acquire Sylphie's wordless healing while she partly learns his disruption technique. The failure is not simply humiliation; it offers a moment in which she can experience reciprocal competence rather than stand under comprehensive superiority (V04:003–005/014/017; V07:007–010; V08:013–016; V10:016).

These ordinary tasks are central character evidence. Shopping, studying, preparing materials, discussing equipment and cleaning a home show how a wish for a meaningful life becomes repeated labor. They also reveal limits without catastrophe. He can accept someone else's useful idea and perform an imperfect share of a chore. The model's learning and teaching rules001–002 describe this capacity for adjustment under available feedback; they do not authorize an all-purpose genius or predict ethical learning from technical success.

## Being needed can become an attempt to control recognition

Rudeus sometimes treats other people's interpretations as part of a solvable project. Staged danger in V02 and delayed rescue in V03 seek a desired response through withheld information. The latter ends in a child's death. Subsequent gratitude or a protector's favorable reading cannot establish the motive the narration has already disclosed. The mistake is not merely insufficient power. It includes deciding what another person's experience should mean and controlling conditions so that the desired meaning will follow (V02:003; V03:012–018).

Yet not every plan or performed account belongs to that mechanism. Immediate assistance can precede reward fantasy. In the V05 reunion, Rudy asks Paul to join a reenactment; the performance can support sincerity because its participants share the premise. Consultation can produce actual information and release a decision to the people concerned. In V10, a surprise house expresses generosity and a desired competent-provider image. He avoids consulting Sylphie because he fears appearing incapable; her pleasure is real, but it does not mean the avoidance never occurred. Later joint choices show a different available practice (V04:003/013; V05:002/011; V09:004; V10:003/007/018).

The distinction matters for model003. It concerns a specific opportunity to control an impression under apparently manageable risk, not a theory that all aid conceals exploitation. If every joke, gift, lie and plan counted equally, the rule could never fail. The house provides a narrower comparison about managing approval; it is not another manufactured rescue. What he knows, when he acts and what the other person can contest must remain visible.

Rudeus is also acted upon by other people's accounts. Hitogami offers selective advice; Eris compresses a purpose into words he reads oppositely; Sara supplies a defensive explanation; friends stage the disclosure of Sylphie's identity. His perspective does not always occupy the controller's position. This reversal prevents the monograph from making him a mastermind whose every setback is intentional. It also makes received interpretation a central vulnerability.

## Fear and practical agency follow different trajectories

The capacity to work survives states in which valued belonging feels threatened. Paul's accusation, Eris's departure and Sara's response do not produce identical events, but each exposes a gap between an intellectually available explanation and what Rudy can immediately enact or believe. V05 gives him reasons to apologize before he can return. Care and a changed perception make the encounter possible. V07 allows professional work, companionship and some pleasure while intimate insecurity persists. V09's local bodily recovery is consequential, yet does not erase fear associated with Orsted or certify every relationship (V05:007–011; V06:025–026; V07:003–005/013/020–026; V09:010/025–029).

An unchanging cowardice label fails because he acts under danger and tolerates some ridicule. An uncomplicated recovery story fails because a gain in one domain leaves another vulnerable. Model004 therefore conditions response on credible threat, felt control, the particular relationship and access to correction. It permits withdrawal, performance, disclosure or a concrete role. It does not diagnose a disorder or predict an extreme crisis from ordinary refusal.

V10 gives this distinction a fresh form. Ruijerd suggests that Eris may have meant something other than rejection. Rudy can imagine that possibility, but imagination is neither confirmation nor restored security. He has lived with the opposite account for years and now has a marriage he values. His immediate fear partly turns the conversation into a demand Ruijerd has not made. New information changes the available explanation without automatically determining the response (V10:023).

His care for Nanahoshi also shows the limits of analogy from personal suffering. He initially misjudges her resilience and later considers whether she wants solitude because he might. He revises that thought by considering her isolation. Zanoba notices his distress and admits not understanding it; that admission gives useful company without pretending identical experience. The narrative supplies a reciprocal need for support, not a transition from former sufferer to infallible caregiver (V10:019–021).

## Recognition can interrupt harm without becoming a governing habit

Rudeus can perceive a need and choose differently. A valued gift, expressed fear, known trust or a concrete refusal sometimes arrests an intention. He can comfort, apologize, give privacy and accept another person's independent decision. These are represented choices, not invented possibilities. The question is whether their governing reason transfers to another occasion or person (V02:009/014; V03:009/020; V04:005/008; V06:012/022; V09:019/024).

The same record supplies severe limits. Earlier affection does not erase a childhood refusal, and later cooperation does not authorize intrusive conduct. In V08, the captives' constrained position, heard objection and Rudy's recognized wrongdoing prevent explanation solely through ignorance or uncontrollable rage. He can coordinate a harmful project effectively. Better practical reasoning is therefore not identical to granting other people decision rights (V01:011–012; V04:018/021; V08:019–025).

V10 makes the strongest comparison local to a newly committed relationship. Sylphie states a public-touch boundary; he stops, then later repeats the conduct. He recognizes the need for care, explicitly reappraises harm in their earlier encounter and shares genuine affection. None makes the recurrence imaginary. Conversely, the recurrence does not make his pause at the sisters' door or respect for Nanahoshi's bathing conditions fictitious. The defensible conclusion identifies what changed and what failed under comparable conditions (V10:007–010/015/018/025).

Model005 remains useful only at that restricted scale. Salient vulnerability makes care or interruption possible; it never guarantees autonomy-respecting action. This is not a license to explain every countercase by an invisible fluctuation in empathy. Where the source supplies the same person's explicit boundary and an actual recurrence, a durable-transfer claim fails. The burden is on any stronger theory to name additional evidence. Model006, by contrast, retains one tightly specified protective-entrapment scenario; it must not expand to excuse every later use of force.

The ethical account also distinguishes involuntary difficulty from conduct. His bodily condition is not a moral offense. Harmful speech, threats, assault and disregard are actions with their own circumstances. Receiving care for the former does not acquit the latter; condemning the latter does not require denying the former's distress. This distinction allows both his suffering and affected people's claims to remain legible.

## Relationships change what he can know and what he owes

Roxy supplies formative instruction and becomes an idealized figure in his self-understanding. Her own effort and limitations resist the image of an effortless source of rescue. Eris is learner, companion and independent agent; his narration repeatedly mistakes her meanings, while her eventual departure also fails to communicate adequately. Ruijerd supplies expertise and unpaid protection that undermine Rudy's initially transactional model of dependence. Their regard still includes projection and incomplete disclosure (V01:006–008; V03:004/017–019; V05:024; V06:019–026).

Paul's relationship makes a different error reciprocal: extraordinary ability encourages the father to assume comprehensive knowledge, while Rudy initially presents a journey whose pleasures conceal costs the father expected to hear. Their eventual repair is real and local. Norn does not inherit their reconciliation as a settled emotional fact. In V10 she has reasons partly available only through the extra. Rudy's home and competence cannot simply require her trust (V05:006–015; V10:026/030–034).

Sylphie makes some of the most important domestic corrections through small, explicit requests. She wants continued work, advance notice, a lesson previously promised and a straightforward apology. Her contribution includes income, instruction and care. Reducing her to the person who resolves his bodily problem would reproduce his own risk of confusing usefulness with full knowledge of a partner. Her relief at his limitation exposes that risk from another direction (V09:026–032; V10:002/008/016/018).

Nanahoshi provides a collaborator whose final goal remains openly different. Zanoba and Cliff contribute expertise that he cannot substitute for by having more mana. Juli exposes the sharpest limit in equating helpful instruction with equality: better conditions and learning coexist with ownership and fear. Across these relations, model008 describes a process of consultation that may serve just or unjust ends. Model007 remains Ruijerd-specific, and model009 distinguishes written self-presentation from truth. Neither a respectful address nor sincere affection supplies all missing consent and knowledge.

## The voice creates access and then tests its privileges

Rudy's internal narration offers immediate access to embarrassment, fear, ingenuity and desire. Its comic, game-inflected and sexualized comparisons can invite the reader to follow his priorities or hurry past another person's cost. His courteous spoken presentation often differs from that interior register. The difference is evidence of audience-sensitive performance; it does not mean politeness is always false or roughness always truthful.

The work repeatedly supplements this access with another person's account, a retrospective scene or a disclosed performance. Sara's perspective separates what he actually said from what she imagined he had said. Sylphie's account distinguishes helping from equality. The renovation passage briefly sounds external before revealing Rudy's borrowed presentation. Norn's extra explains a response that his present narration cannot fully interpret. These devices do not make every proposition equally uncertain; some correct definite mistakes, while others disclose a competing value or a feeling left intact by correction (V07:025; V09:030–032; V10:007/026/030–034).

The strongest counterreading is that comedy and protagonist access repeatedly soften misconduct. That has local textual grounds. The contrary mechanism is not an abstract claim that the author must condemn him: it is the actual preservation of refusal, pain, fear, contradictory accounts and consequences. This monograph attends to both without inferring creator intent or measuring audience response. Illustrations contribute emphasis within their inspected scope; they do not verify inner motive or performed voice.

Through V10, Rudeus has more than intentions. He has acquired work habits, skills, relationships and some methods of listening and restraint. He has also acquired forms of influence that can magnify disregard. The explanatory task remains to specify when a recognized reason becomes a selected action, and when being useful gives way to allowing another person a purpose he cannot control. The present corpus supports that bounded problem and its countercases; it does not yet supply a final answer about his life.
