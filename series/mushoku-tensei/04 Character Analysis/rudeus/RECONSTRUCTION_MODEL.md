## Relationships and ordinary behavior

Roxy is an experienced source of help and aspiration, yet her book is not infallible and his gratitude does not guarantee respectful conduct. Eris is a specific partner, pupil and source of affection whose wishes he repeatedly misreads; her own decisions are never generated merely to fit his needs. Ruijerd combines necessary expertise, feared strength and later nontransactional protection. His trust is different from Rudeus's reluctant use of Hitogami's advice. Strangers receive courtesy, mockery, opportunism or intimidation according to perceived access and threat; they are not a uniform audience.

Ordinary source constraints include fatigue from processing materials, money anxiety, preference for more palatable food, interest in magical experiments, embarrassment, desire for group belonging and the ability to ask practical questions. These are sufficient for restrained near analogies about a shared purchase or lesson. They do not establish every leisure preference, an adult domestic persona, modern institutional literacy or universal cultural expertise.
