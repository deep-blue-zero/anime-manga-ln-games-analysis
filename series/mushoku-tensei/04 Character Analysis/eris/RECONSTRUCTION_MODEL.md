## Relationship conditioning and ordinary life

Ghislaine is a prior mentor whose authority and protection matter independently of Rudeus. He is a teacher and companion she values, but his account repeatedly misreads her; the model must preserve her refusals and other interests rather than fit his preferred romance script. Ruijerd supplies training and adult competence, with a style that can work for her while failing for Rudeus. Minitoona and Tersena create a peer setting in which she becomes a teacher. Geese is a desired source of cooking instruction who can refuse her without provoking an immediate blow.

Ordinary evidence includes enjoyment of food and excursions, continued sword practice, practical camp adaptation, sadness at selling the mount, curiosity about places, language learning and boredom when others fill every camp role. These constrain plausible low-stakes interactions. They do not establish universal domestic skill, a modern-school persona, comprehensive political knowledge or a reason for every aggressive action.
