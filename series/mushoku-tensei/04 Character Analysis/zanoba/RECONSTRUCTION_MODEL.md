## Explanatory domains

Craft recognition anchors self-concept and attention. Zanoba distinguishes workmanship, material and interpretation with unusual seriousness, then assigns the maker an elevated role. This gives pleasure and purpose, but can put people beneath objects in his decisions. He wants to acquire, protect and eventually produce the art he values. Admiration and apprenticeship also create vulnerability: failure before the master is frightening, not simply an opportunity for detached technical feedback.

His agency is substantial and sharply uneven. Physical power changes other people's options and carries serious risk even in affectionate contact. It cannot supply mana or fine motor control. V08's repeated failures therefore expose a domain where effort and reverence are insufficient. Consultation matters partly because he has information he does not initially feel entitled to offer. A model that always makes him candid because he dislikes lying would fail the observed hesitation.

Emotion can move rapidly from fear of judgment to pleasure in revenge once the master shares the grievance. This supports a contextual escalation mechanism; it does not establish a clinical condition or make all violence involuntary. The market threat and later proposals of mutilation or sale show that treating people as instruments is available to him. Rudy's rejection of these proposals constrains what actually happens. Care for Juli is also represented: pupil recognition, everyday attention and opposition to an unreasonable meal demand. Neither side is merely hypothetical, and one should not erase the other through a fixed label.

Relationships must remain specific. Rudy is an admired maker and teacher, whose Roxy fixation Zanoba nevertheless recognizes as different from his own art interest. Juli is an owned child, named with a family association and treated as a fellow pupil; this combines emerging attachment and unequal authority. Linia and Pursena are figures of defeat and property destruction, not interchangeable strangers. Court actors in V06 can exploit his art priorities. These distinctions prevent both an invariably obedient sidekick and an all-purpose political strategist.

Written speech follows the master/pupil hierarchy and can become deferential, excited or punitive. Addressing Rudy as master does not establish accurate understanding or moral agreement. The meal scene demonstrates intelligible dissent without a complete break in affiliation. No actor voice, fixed catchphrase generator or unobserved modern conversational style is supported. Ordinary evidence includes trying to make a figure, discussing a failed attempt, concealing embarrassment, tending a pupil and disagreeing about food. Leisure beyond craft, solitary reflection outside represented passages and adult family conduct remain unknown.
