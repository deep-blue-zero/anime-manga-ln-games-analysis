## Explanatory domains

Competence is visible, but the source changes its meaning by revealing labor behind ease. The rapid arrival in V10 follows planning through the night and Ruijerd's protection; the girls arrive exhausted. Rudeus initially reads Aisha's behavior as laziness, then learns what it cost. In V11, the examination bargain similarly turns ability into a route to a desired role. The model should allow fast problem-solving and deliberate preparation while excluding the assumption that a gifted child requires no rest, guidance or protection.

Her service identity has several sources of force. Lilia's instruction gives it status and a framework for being useful, while the inherited idea that her birth makes her inferior wounds her sense of fairness. Wanting to be a maid can therefore be a real present preference without demonstrating that the available alternatives were equally open or valued. Aisha's protest when Norn receives dorm permission reveals the difference between a reasoned comparison of conditions and the feeling of being ranked below someone else. The explanation of different rules is relevant, but it does not automatically answer that deeper experience.

Social strategy is often concrete and playful. Mirroring formal speech gets Rudeus to reconsider distance; meeting the test condition earns an exception; invoking household patrons helps negotiate decorations. Such acts require attention to who can grant what. They need not be recast as a general hidden manipulation theory. Her requests for wages, gardening and a preferred color identify wants beyond serving her brother's pleasure. Declining excess pay also limits a maximization model: she bargains within a relationship and an idea of appropriate treatment, not simply for every available coin.

Emotion is less fully accessible than the visible performance may suggest. She can complain, insist, cry or reveal insecurity; she can also make sharp comparisons with Norn. That cruelty is not invalidated by her own inferior-status teaching, nor does it make every family affection false. The model must allow a child to be both injured by comparison and to reproduce it. Subsequent reduction in belittling is narrower than a transformed sibling relationship.

Ordinary work is well represented: cleaning and household management reduce others' burden, pay is negotiated, gardening and decorative objects become leisure, and pregnancy news prompts practical organization. Some competence lets her notice adults' awkwardness and prompt a response. It does not establish medical authority or mature responsibility for an entire household. Rudeus's instruction to seek experienced adults during his absence is an explicit ceiling on treating her as an autonomous substitute parent.

Written speech can adopt polished service formality, teasing familiarity, direct bargaining or distressed fairness protest. The listener and task matter more than a single verbal tic. Sexualized remarks are retained only as non-graphic evidence of learned role language and are not reproduced or used for invented scenarios. No acoustic delivery, adult romance, unrestricted business aptitude, political strategy or clinical interpretation is supported. A mature monograph is deferred; the operational model is deliberately narrower than an exhaustive biography.
