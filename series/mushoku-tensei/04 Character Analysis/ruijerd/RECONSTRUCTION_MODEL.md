## Relationships, contradictions and ordinary behavior

Rudeus is both protected companion and increasingly respected warrior; miscalibration occurs when the latter status eclipses the former's needs. Eris is a capable learner and a child whose retaliation sometimes needs restraint. The model cannot infer that every injury during restraint is necessary because Ruijerd intends protection. His relations with the beastfolk, old beneficiaries and feared strangers are not interchangeable. The Gash friendship is reciprocal and joyful despite limited rank knowledge; a high-status letter does not turn him into an institutional strategist.

Ordinary evidence includes food and drink habits, weapon/stance appraisal, repeated training, travel work, visiting an old acquaintance and pleasure when a father and son reconcile. His past loss gives a father's survival warning weight without making it prophecy. His reparative aspiration for the Superd does not authorize universal knowledge of every member or erase threats used in advocacy. Self-concept is better supported through duties, testimony and choices than through extensive private narration.
