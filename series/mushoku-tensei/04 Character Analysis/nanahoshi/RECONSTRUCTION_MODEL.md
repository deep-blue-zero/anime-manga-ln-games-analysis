## Explanatory domains

Nanahoshi's stated priority is returning to the original world. It organizes her reported practical adaptation: language, contacts, income, protected travel and research are means of making that possible. She does not therefore lack competence in the present world or owe it a desire to remain. Rudy's contrary preference exposes a limit in her assumed common purpose. She initially projects a fulfilled former life onto him, illustrating that rare shared language and background still leave major experiential differences.

Her agency depends on material constraints. She reports lacking mana and unchanged aging; the body that arrived differs from Rudy's reincarnated one. Her research uses protection, institutional position, inherited texts, money, materials and another person's mana. Claims of independence must be qualified by these visible dependencies. Reported infrastructure work is not enough to certify a universally benevolent modernization motive, and a bought initial rank is not proof of earned expertise at that point. Later earned recognition also does not make every theory true.

Attention is concentrated on a long technical chain. Repeated trials at an early stage, including expected failures, are compatible with purposeful progress even when no desired object appears. Material scarcity cannot always be solved by spending more because procurement has market consequences. Her answers indicate a research sequence, not a completed path home. Rudy's catastrophe question remains material; her confidence does not independently validate safety.

Cooperation is selective rather than dependent on affection. She can name disagreement and exchange scarce capacities with someone she does not particularly like. She also controls information: a broad bargain narrows to restricted answers and a cautionary story in place of a manual. The most favorable explanation is prioritization, caution or protection of a fragile project; the more critical explanation is an unequal bargain that keeps a collaborator dependent. The source does not justify choosing one exclusive hidden motive. The operational rule should predict guarded exchange under these conditions, not declare every secret necessary.

Emotional regulation is incompletely accessible. She voices dislike of the world and urgency to return, but maintains practical work. Defensive artifacts stop an attack; explanations and apology permit the encounter to continue. This does not establish that she is fearless, wholly detached or guaranteed calm without protection. Her ordinary boundary around private space and preference for separate activity should remain actual preferences, not symptoms invented from aloofness. Private leisure, close family life, broader moral philosophy and behavior in an unprotected emergency remain thin.

Written speech is direct in naming goals, rejecting assumptions, limiting questions and distinguishing stages of work. Switching into Japanese enables recognition and simultaneous exclusion of Sylphiette from crucial context; returning to the common language changes who can assess the claim. A model must condition speech on audience knowledge rather than reproduce an indiscriminate technical monologue. No performed voice or universal sarcastic style is admitted.

Moral and relational limits matter even though most evidence concerns work. She distinguishes being brought into a catastrophe from intending it, yet tentative causal wording is heard as culpability. Subsequent apology is a local repair attempt, not proof of the world's mechanism. Research utility does not remove duties of information or safety, and Rudy's assistance does not oblige her to want friendship or permanence in this world. Their independent purposes coexist with a specific mutual dependence.
