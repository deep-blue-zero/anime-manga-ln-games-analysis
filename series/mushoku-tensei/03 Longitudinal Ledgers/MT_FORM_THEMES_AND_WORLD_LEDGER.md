# Form, themes, and world ledger

## Responsibility

Owns recurring prose and structural devices, motifs, thematic questions, and consequential world/institution constraints. Character reports remain separate from established world facts.

## Record format

`pattern ID | kind | proposed pattern/rule | source observations | recurrence/variation | formal or causal mechanism | character report versus world fact | counterexamples | bounded confidence | specialist destination`

Every future record needs a stable local ID, source/witness and volume boundary, a link to the canonical volume observation, claim class, and explicit uncertainty. No sample rows are treated as evidence.

## Update and ownership rule

Append a pattern when multiple or uniquely diagnostic observations warrant it; patch current scope on disconfirmation while retaining the history. Split an independently growing dimension only by an architecture amendment and ownership transfer. The analytical integrator synchronizes this ledger with each closed volume transaction; a reviewed no-material-update is recorded in the volume closure without padding this ledger.
