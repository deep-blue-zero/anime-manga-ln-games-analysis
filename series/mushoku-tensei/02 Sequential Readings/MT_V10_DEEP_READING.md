# V10 — maintaining a home, revising trust

## Developments through V09 (spoilers through V09)

Rudeus's second life has combined unusual magical ability and sustained practice with relationships that expose uneven judgment. Roxy taught him and helped him venture beyond his family's enclosure. He befriended and taught Sylphie, overrode an explicit bodily refusal and later apologized. Paul's infidelity and Lilia's pregnancy caused a household crisis; Rudeus opposed expulsion through a selective account, while Zenith chose continued family life and care for both daughters. Gratitude and later care did not erase Paul's earlier assault or settle every injury. Paul then forcibly separated Rudeus from Sylphie and sent him to tutor Eris. Adapted lessons, Ghislaine's instruction and other people's work enabled progress; growing affection coexisted with coercion and boundary failures.

The Fittoa displacement disaster stranded Rudeus and Eris on the Demon Continent. Ruijerd rescued them and offered an unpaid escort. Their party, Dead End, developed trust through travel, practice and mutual dependence, while also concealing information and manipulating impressions. A rescue delayed to produce gratitude ended in a child's death. Later, apparent guild entrapment led Rudeus to prepare devastating force; Ruijerd interrupted and took the blame. Consultation became useful without guaranteeing transparency. Hitogami's advice brought benefits, including Kishirika's eye for possible futures, but neither those benefits nor accurate individual statements established the adviser's full purpose.

Travel required money, languages, local knowledge and help. The party freed abducted children, and Rudeus was imprisoned after rescuers misunderstood his presence. He later helped defend the beastfolk village. Roxy's search party passed nearby without meeting them. At Milishion, Rudeus unwittingly attacked Paul's rescue group; father and son then fought over incompatible assumptions about what the other knew. Geese challenged Paul, Eris comforted Rudeus and Ruijerd restrained retaliation. A second meeting repaired that local connection without ending Norn's rejection, Eris's resentment or every other harmful pattern.

Further rescue in Shirone depended on Aisha's recognition, Zanoba's intervention and Ruijerd's work beyond Rudeus's knowledge. Rudeus refused one proposed dependent arrangement for Aisha while retaining other unequal assumptions. Orsted nearly killed him after learning of his association with Hitogami; a masked companion's intervention contributed to his survival. Ruijerd later left the group. Returning to Fittoa exposed Eris's family losses. She sought a close bond with Rudeus, then left to become strong enough to stand beside him. Her compressed message failed to convey that aim. He understood it as rejection and went north to search for Zenith. The reader had information about Eris's purpose and Roxy's search lead that he did not possess.

Northern adventuring renewed Rudeus's work and companionship while distress persisted. Temporary membership in Counter Arrow required cooperation with specialists, not merely superior magic. He recovered Mimir's remains and rescued Sara, whose own efforts also sustained her survival. Her suspicion changed toward affection. When his bodily difficulty disrupted attempted intimacy, she misread it as rejection and defended herself by falsely describing her approach as repayment. He believed her. Soldat listened after his aggression, obtained professional help and interrupted an acute suicidal act. Sara heard his actual demeaning speech but also imagined mockery that had not occurred. Later clarification did not reach him in time for repair. He continued mobile work, and Elinalise followed his growing reputation while carrying news of Zenith.

In V08 Elinalise delivered the report that Zenith had been located near Rapan on Begaritt. Her reassuring assessment remained testimony; rescue was not shown. Winter delayed travel. Rudeus initially prioritized his family over a university invitation, then changed course after Hitogami promised a path toward resolving his condition through school study and warned against going to Begaritt. He entered Ranoa's University of Magic as a special student. Technical ability helped him pass an examination, but unfamiliar rules and relationships still required explanation. Fitts became a trusted helper and research partner. At that source boundary the full identity arrangement remained unresolved.

Zanoba's failures in precise figure-making led to a division-of-labor solution. Rudeus and Zanoba purchased the enslaved child Juli, treated and taught her, and gave her better conditions without freeing her. Her learning, smiles and fear all mattered; none established consent to ownership. When Rudeus learned that Linia and Pursena had broken a valued figure, he planned retaliation despite Elinalise's objection. Defeat was followed by captivity, assault, deprivation and intimidation. The possibility of repairing the object reduced his anger; the students' frightened public denial stopped institutional inquiry. Later sociability and ordinary teaching supplied real local changes without proving comprehensive repair. Zanoba's concern for Juli could produce dissent within his devotion to his master.

V09 first develops Cliff's perspective. His diligence and family expectations coexist with pride and poor fighting judgment. Observed accomplishments eventually make him acknowledge Rudeus's ability without immediately liking him. Rudeus stops a group beating before recognizing Cliff as its victim, and Cliff asks for an introduction to Elinalise. Fitts advises the mediator to leave the pair's choice to them. Cliff accepts the constraint of Elinalise's reported curse, promises to work toward a cure and begins a relationship with her. Months of research yield no cure, but he continues and asks Rudeus for information. His qualified offer to listen to Rudeus's difficulty supplies meaningful care independently of technical success.

Seasonal suitors challenge Rudeus as the supposed authority over Linia and Pursena; Fitts denies that victory gives him disposal rights. Badigadi arrives and defeats the challengers. A negotiated trial allows Rudeus a prepared strike against a non-evading, regenerating opponent. Spectators mistake the victory for broader superiority, although Rudeus knows its limits. The institution eventually accommodates Badigadi as a useful guest. Rudeus's reputation helps deter aggressors but frightens others, and respect for bodily privacy remains selective.

The displacement inquiry brings Rudeus to Silent Sevenstar, whom he recognizes as Orsted's masked companion. Fear overwhelms him before a clarified immediate situation permits conversation. Japanese speech and a remembered face identify Nanahoshi Shizuka's connection to his former world. She reports bodily transfer, no mana and unchanged aging, whereas he has been born and grown anew. She wants to return home; he wants to remain. They negotiate research cooperation despite those opposed goals. Her account introduces reports of surviving transfer circles and an unnamed specialist without independently verifying them. A tentative link between her arrival and the catastrophe provokes Sylphiette into attack before Rudeus supplies the involuntary-transfer context excluded by their earlier Japanese conversation. Neither intention nor the disaster's actual mechanism is established by the timing alone.

The volume explicitly establishes Sylphiette as a performer of the Fitts role, while also confirming that Ariel sometimes substitutes. Attribution therefore remains scene-specific. Sylphiette studies, works, helps with research and fears being forgotten. Rudeus values her company independently of how much mana she contributes. His attraction becomes conscious before he recognizes his childhood friend, and a transient bodily response does not yet amount to recovery. Ariel and Luke help Sylphiette arrange a concealed errand and artificial rain. In their shelter Rudeus offers privacy; Sylphiette requests help and ultimately names herself. They declare affection and discuss their histories, but his condition initially persists. She asks for less formal speech and later asks for help with Ariel's cause while explicitly allowing him to decline.

Luke gives Sylphiette an empathetic interpretation of Rudeus's injury alongside inaccurate advice about his tastes. She visits with food, drink and a substance intended to help his condition. She rejects covert dosing and states the treatment aim, but does not adequately disclose its specific nature, risk advice or dosage. Both drink and take it. In the morning Rudeus acknowledges causing pain and exercising insufficient care, fears she will have left, and finds her still present. He reports recovery and gratitude. Her own account preserves pain and happiness, a fulfilled wish to help, and uncertainty about whether she truly stands beside him as she hoped.

The late Sylphiette interlude changes the explanation of her earlier silence: Ariel had allowed disclosure before enrollment. Fear, a false assumption that Elinalise was Rudeus's wife, and the option of building a new relationship impeded naming herself. It also confirms her part in the entrance and dormitory incidents. The extra returns to Eris's arrival and training at the Sword Sanctuary. Her purpose remains becoming capable enough to stand with Rudeus; no corrected account of her departure reaches him. Nina's jealous school visit overlaps the Badigadi duel. Misunderstanding its conditions leads her to overestimate Rudeus, yet also to practice more seriously. Useful change does not make that mistaken premise true. Zenith's rescue, Cliff's cure project, Nanahoshi's return and the catastrophe mechanism remain unresolved through V09.

## Story synopsis of V10 (spoilers through V10)

After recovering from his bodily difficulty, Rudeus resolves to care for Sylphie and remain faithful to her. He considers leaving the university now that his initial aim has been met, but his relationships, unfinished research and wait for Paul's letter keep him there. Ariel calls him to discuss his intentions toward Sylphie. Pressed to make those intentions concrete, he announces that he wants to marry her. Sylphie accepts while insisting that she will continue serving Ariel. Ariel declines to make Rudeus a subordinate she could not adequately control, instead offering reciprocal political support.

Rudeus asks Cliff and Zanoba how to prepare for marriage and decides to buy a house. He avoids consulting Sylphie at first because he fears appearing incapable. A large, affordable property has defeated previous attempts to resolve its supposed haunting. Cliff and Zanoba accompany him to investigate. The attacker turns out to be an automatic doll rather than a spirit. Rudeus is frightened and wants to destroy it; Zanoba recognizes its value and protects it, while Cliff's knowledge helps the group investigate. Their cooperation exposes a hidden workshop and research materials. Zanoba asks to take responsibility for studying the mechanism, seeking a useful contribution beyond financing his teacher. Rudeus agrees. They clear the property, though their reconstruction of its former owner's activities remains partly conjectural.

With skilled workers and Elinalise's connections, Rudeus renovates the house. Sylphie participates in choosing furniture and is pleased with their future home, but she also challenges his lack of communication and establishes a boundary against public touching. The couple discuss her work, shared chores, insecurity and expectations. She asks him not to disappear without warning. He recognizes how much fear of abandonment and gratitude have shaped his proposal, and resolves to exercise more care in intimacy. These conversations create practical agreements without making every later interaction considerate.

They prepare a wedding reception for their school friends. Sylphie corrects his assumption that Nanahoshi should simply be omitted because she is likely to refuse, and Nanahoshi does attend. The party brings together differing ranks, customs and relationships; Badigadi disrupts the arrangements but also helps make the celebration convivial. An emotional exchange reveals that Elinalise is Sylphie's grandmother. Elinalise explains that hostility toward her had also harmed her descendants, leading her to conceal her identity and withdraw after raising her children. Sylphie accepts the connection. Rudeus expects Cliff to recoil from this history, but Cliff instead asks for more help toward understanding Elinalise's curse.

Ariel and Luke ask Rudeus to participate in a sword duel without initially explaining its personal purpose. He wins with the aid of his eye and long practice. Luke asks him to care for Sylphie, and Ariel speaks of the danger of her eventual return to contest the Asuran throne. She hopes Sylphie's marriage will give her reasons to stay behind, even asking Rudeus to stop her from following. Rudeus privately imagines helping them himself. These plans concern Sylphie's future without being settled with her.

Married life develops through school, guard duty, research, shopping and housework. Elinalise helps relieve Sylphie's work schedule. Rudeus and Sylphie exchange instruction: he teaches spell disruption, while his attempt to learn her wordless healing exposes a limit in his own ability. He respects some boundaries and repeatedly crosses others. When he distracts Sylphie during cooking, she cuts herself and explicitly rebukes him; she then asks for a straightforward apology rather than an exaggerated display of submission. Their affection and their difficulties appear within the same routine.

Paul's letter announces that Norn and Aisha are being sent north under a trusted escort while the adults continue toward Zenith. It explains the competing risks of taking the children to Begaritt or sending them away, and asks Rudeus to look after Norn without measuring her against her unusually capable siblings. Zenith's anticipated rescue remains a report about plans and prospects. Rudeus shares the letter with Sylphie and secures her agreement to the girls living with them. Their discussion of children also reveals her anxiety about fertility and his recognition that some of his romantic language may place pressure on her.

Nanahoshi's long-prepared summoning experiment fails. She interprets the failure as proof that she cannot return home, destroys her work in distress and harms herself. Rudeus intervenes, then receives help from Zanoba, Juli and Sylphie in caring for her. Their support is imperfect and sometimes based on guesses, but it provides a setting in which the technical problem can be reconsidered. A gap in the circle had remained unresolved. Zanoba's knowledge of the automaton suggests a layered construction; Rudeus, Cliff and Nanahoshi contribute different parts of the solution. A revised experiment successfully summons a plastic bottle. This is a real result, not proof that a person can yet return.

Nanahoshi thanks the collaborators with a celebration. Shared enjoyment does not erase their divergent aims: she still wants her former home, while Rudeus values his present one. Nor does conviviality guarantee that a complaint will be heard; Badigadi interrupts Nanahoshi when she reproaches Rudeus about disturbance during her stay. After the party, the sisters arrive with Ruijerd, sooner than Rudeus expected. Aisha's resourceful travel arrangements helped them move quickly but exhausted both girls. Ruijerd's account raises the possibility that Rudeus misunderstood Eris's departure. Rudeus can now imagine that explanation, yet remains hurt and afraid; no message from Eris resolves it.

Ruijerd leaves to continue searching for his people. His tense encounter with Badigadi remains unexplained beyond an old connection. Norn wants to go with her escort and still distrusts Rudeus, despite having heard more about his past difficulties. Ruijerd asks her to rely on her brother and explicitly entrusts both girls to Rudeus and Sylphie. The household has expanded, but trust has not automatically followed.

The Eris interlude shows solitary practice, reflection, exhaustion and continued longing. An encounter with the visiting North Emperor Auber becomes a dangerous fight that Gal interrupts. Ghislaine challenges Gal's teaching, and he explains his plan to expose Eris to techniques beyond a single school. The narrator briefly announces a certification she will receive a year later; that forecast belongs to this volume's narration, not Rudeus's present knowledge.

The final extra returns to East Port roughly a year before Rudeus receives Paul's letter. Norn experiences separation from Paul as the loss of her safest relationship. After an argument she runs away and is threatened by drunken men; Ruijerd protects her, listens and promises to escort her. Roxy initially mistakes him for a danger because of childhood stories about the Superd. She recognizes her error after clarification, although her fear persists. Paul weighs conflicting advice and accepts Ruijerd after observing Norn's trust and recognizing a pattern of protective conduct. The extra explains how the escort began and why its completion means more than successful transport.

### MT-E-LNJP-V10-001 — Recovery changes self-appraisal without erasing others' fear

**Source:** spine8 `text/part0007.html` p6–124.

Rudy's account of becoming human again expresses the intensity of relief, not a criterion the analyst endorses. Fidelity and help become declared purposes, while Juli still startles at touch and classmates still fear him. His decision to stay at school follows relationships and unfinished obligations as well as treatment success. **Alternative:** recovery might be read as the conclusion of his whole developmental problem. The adjacent fear and continued dependence resist that inference. Minimum: a consequential local improvement changes available aims without establishing broad ethical repair. C001/002/012/015.

### MT-E-LNJP-V10-002 — Commitment and service are negotiated on different axes

**Source:** spine8 `text/part0007.html` p173–192; spine10 `text/part0009.html` p1–167.

Ariel presses for concrete intentions, but marriage exceeds the minimum commitment she demanded. Rudy combines gratitude, desire and fear of abandonment. Sylphie accepts marriage and explicitly retains service; she rejects being dismissed from a valued purpose. Ariel's reciprocal patronage also reflects her judgment that Rudy cannot be controlled as a subordinate. **Alternative:** either pure romance or pure political coercion oversimplifies the distinct choices. Minimum: affection, institutional calculation and agency coexist; a marriage label does not settle work or allegiance. C007/013/014.

### MT-E-LNJP-V10-003 — Consultation stops at an insecurity boundary

**Source:** spine10 `text/part0009.html` p182–317, p523–559.

Minimum: adaptive information-seeking is audience-conditioned, and imagined displeasure is not another person's actual state. C003/007/009/014.
