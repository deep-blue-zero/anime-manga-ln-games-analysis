# V07 — recognition without shared understanding

## Developments through V06 (spoilers through V06)

Rudeus's second life has given him unusual magical ability, sustained practice and relationships that both help him and expose his failures. Roxy taught him, helped him venture beyond the family enclosure and left to improve her own skills. He befriended and taught Sylphie, violated an explicit boundary and later apologized. Paul's infidelity and Lilia's pregnancy created a household crisis. Rudeus argued against Lilia's expulsion through a selective account; Zenith chose continued family life and later cared for both daughters, Norn and Aisha. Lilia's gratitude and dependence coexisted with an earlier history of assault by Paul. Continued attachment did not erase that harm.

Paul forcibly separated Rudeus and Sylphie and placed his son in Roa as Eris's tutor. Teaching worked through adaptation, practical use, repetition and other adults' work. Ghislaine supplied sword instruction and had educational needs of her own. Rudeus and Eris developed affection beside aggression, unequal roles and repeated boundary problems. An unexplained disaster displaced the Fittoa region, separating households and stranding the pair on the Demon Continent.

Ruijerd rescued them and offered an unpaid escort home. His account of a wartime catastrophe explained the Superd people's feared reputation, which Rudeus promised to improve. Roxy's parents and guide helped the travelers. Dead End's cooperation developed alongside secrecy and dangerous attempts to control other people's responses. Rudeus delayed a rescue to increase gratitude, and a child died. Later guild extortion made him feel trapped; he prepared to devastate the town to escape with Eris. Ruijerd intervened, exposed his identity and took blame. The renewed partnership did not include complete disclosure of that contemplated violence.

The journey required money, language learning, training and consultation. Hitogami's dream advice produced useful outcomes without establishing benevolence. One instruction led Rudeus to feed Kishirika and receive an eye for possible near futures; it required calibration and could not replace bodily execution. A prohibitive Superd fare led the party into a smuggling arrangement. They freed abducted children, but Rudeus was wrongly imprisoned by rescuers who misread his actions. During an attack on the beastfolk village, he gave up escape to help. Geese and the sacred beast contributed decisively to survival. The rainy season allowed work, practice and Eris's independent friendships. Roxy's search party passed close by without meeting them, hindered by rumor, fear and mistaken assumptions.

In Milishion, Rudeus intervened in what he thought was an abduction and instead attacked members of Paul's rescue group. Paul assumed his capable son knew the disaster's regional scale and had neglected the missing; Rudeus had never received the notices. Their reunion became a quarrel and fight, with Norn defending the father who cared for her. Paul's separate account disclosed sustained rescue work and care alongside worsening drinking, exhaustion, harmful expectations and his earlier wrongs. Geese challenged his assumptions. Eris comforted Rudeus, while Ruijerd prevented her revenge. At the second meeting, Rudeus recognized Paul's distress and deliberately began the reunion again, asking for an embrace. The repair was real but local: Norn still rejected him, Eris still resented Paul, and he continued to misread other people's lives.

Rudeus chose to complete Eris's escort while increasing attention to the displaced. Paul supplied advice, money and family information. Sylphie's silent healing exceeded Rudeus's own skill, but her location was unknown. Eris independently helped a knight during a hunt; that knight, Therese, later secured the party's passage through personal obligation while retaining anti-demon prejudice. Roxy returned to her parents and received their care through tears and an embrace despite continued telepathic exclusion. She then resumed the search for the missing family. Rudeus's later outburst at a cook showed that family reconciliation had not produced general restraint.

The palace extras followed Fitts, Ariel and Luke through service, dependence and political flight. Fitts's prior identity remains unestablished. An investigator wrongly reported Ariel dead and, after receiving corrective testimony, knowingly left the report unchanged. Reader knowledge and the information available to characters remained distinct.

On the Central Continent, Hitogami directed Rudeus toward Aisha in Shirone. Rudeus concealed the adviser's identity and wrote prematurely to Roxy, wrongly assuming she still worked at court. He promptly rescued Aisha from soldiers but was injured. Her criticism of his earlier conduct embarrassed him; he retained an alias and hoped rescue would secure admiration. A false invitation brought him into Pax's anti-magic prison. Liberation depended on Ginger's initiative, soldiers' plans, Ruijerd's rescue of captive families and Zanoba's violent intervention. Zanoba admired Rudeus's craft, but that narrow sensitivity coexisted with lethal history and coercive power. Both princes were exiled. Lilia and Aisha received an escort to Paul. Rudeus refused Lilia's proposed separation and sexualized service arrangement for Aisha; Aisha's farewell revealed that she already knew his identity.

Ruijerd recognized Eris's sustained progress by naming her a warrior. In the mountains, Orsted recognized the companions but not Rudeus and initially intended to pass. Rudeus stopped him to ask questions, then inadvertently disclosed contact with Hitogami. Orsted attacked and rapidly defeated all three. Rudeus's magic and foresight could not overcome the difference in skill; grievously injured, he attempted overwhelming force without considering his companions. He survived through Nanahoshi's intervention and Orsted's healing, as Eris later reported. Hitogami subsequently offered curse explanations, including a claim that Superd fear was fading. Rudeus noticed an inconsistency and later passed the account to Ruijerd with a qualification. It offered hope without becoming independently established world law.

At Fittoa, Ruijerd completed his escort and left on his own task. The farewell affirmed reciprocal help rather than Rudeus's claim to have contributed almost nothing. Eris reunited with Ghislaine and learned that her parents had died and Sauros had been executed. Alphonse proposed a political arrangement to support reconstruction; Ghislaine opposed sacrificing Eris's welfare. Rudeus insisted that Eris decide. Inquiry at the missing-person list established that Sylphie had been reported alive, without a known contact address. Zenith remained missing from his perspective.

Eris sought a family bond with Rudeus in grief and fear of separation. They became intimate without making their future mutually explicit. Her retrospective account disclosed parental pressure, love, hard practice, fear of replacement and repeated idealization of his ability. She recognized his vulnerability yet concluded that she burdened him. She left with Ghislaine for sword training, hoping to return as an equal partner, and concealed her destination to prevent pursuit. Her brief note meant inadequacy on her part; Rudeus read it as rejection of him. During a week of distress he still built defenses for recovering settlements, then departed north to search for Zenith. The reader knows Eris's intention; he does not.

Roxy's interlude supplied a lead he had not received. Kishirika reported Zenith alive near Rapan on Begaritt but could not clearly see her circumstances. Roxy and Talhand headed toward Paul, while Elinalise and Badigadi arranged to inform Rudeus. Delivery and rescue remained unobserved. The final extra returned to Lilia and Aisha's earlier escorted journey, confirming Aisha's recognition and newly chosen admiration. Lilia's history added resistance and aftermath to the already established assault, and her father's alternative to forced marriage. Her maternal protection and final embrace were real; she nevertheless continued directing Aisha toward future service. The volume ended with several people acting on care, partial information and different intended futures.

## Story synopsis of V07 (spoilers)

Rudeus travels north after the separation that ended V06. He has promised Paul to search for Zenith, but his journey is also shaped by his belief that Eris abandoned him. The reader already knows that her departure had a different intention; Rudeus does not. On the road to the northern city of Rosenburg, the adventuring party Counter Arrow notices a withdrawn young traveler who answers kindness with little enthusiasm. Its deputy leader, Suzanne, keeps trying to speak to him. Its archer, Sara, resents his attitude and suspects that his noble surname and expensive staff conceal a privileged runaway playing at danger.

In Rosenburg, Rudeus takes lodgings, dissolves Dead End's guild registration and plans to make his name widely known so that information about his missing mother can reach him. His practical resources have survived the journey better than his confidence. He still has substantial money and exceptional magical ability, yet chooses a dangerous solo request with little concern for survival. Suzanne invites him to work with Counter Arrow. She and her companions mistakenly believe his former party died, but they correctly recognize that leaving him alone could be dangerous. Their invitation also serves Sara's education: she needs to learn cooperation with unfamiliar people.

Their first expedition targets a group of Luster Grizzlies. Rudeus accepts a restricted support role while observing how the party coordinates its limited resources. The operation goes wrong when a much larger group, concealed by mud in the darkness, charges them. Suzanne offers to stay behind, and the others refuse to abandon her. Their frightened determination to live affects Rudeus more deeply than the approaching monsters do. He destroys most of the threat with magic and helps finish the battle. They have needed his power, but their mutual loyalty has supplied something he needed. Back in his room, he recalls Roxy's teaching and the people who still care about him. He rejects his earlier indifference to death and burns a keepsake associated with Eris. The act marks a resolve to continue, rather than the completion of grief.

Over the following months, Rudeus rebuilds routines of training, prayer and adventuring. He offers inexpensive temporary support to different parties, hoping they will spread his name and his search. His repeated use of mud magic earns him the name “Quagmire,” or “Mud,” Rudeus. Counter Arrow remains especially important to him even though repeated work with the same people brings diminishing publicity. He feels comfortable with their practical cooperation and with no longer having to decide everything as party leader. Sara remains prickly, but she repeatedly invites him along. Her own viewpoint shows that distrust rooted in the failure of nobles to protect her childhood village is increasingly difficult to reconcile with his actions.

A scale-gathering expedition to Galgau Ruins tests that change. Snow Drakes suddenly pour toward the party. Rudeus covers the retreat, but his defensive wall and cold magic fail to stop them. Counter Arrow returns for him, with Sara taking the initiative. Their support allows him to recover his composure and fight effectively. Another party, the powerful Stepped Leader, arrives while pursuing the monsters. Its leader, Soldat Heckler, strikes Counter Arrow's leader Timothy, believing that the party has interfered with his job. Timothy establishes that two supposedly separate locations have become connected underground. The misunderstanding is settled, although the distribution of the spoils and Soldat's conduct leave resentment.

Soldat later apologizes, then turns a drunken encounter into an attack on Rudeus's smiling deference and apparent misery. Rudeus endures the abuse without answering. Sara, angered on his behalf, begins to recognize that she does not actually dislike him. She also defends Timothy's conciliatory manner as a way of protecting the party, rather than evidence of cowardice. Winter brings continued work and increased local recognition, but no information about Zenith. Rudeus remains vulnerable to Soldat's repeated insults and reluctant to leave the city.

An ordinary snow-clearing job briefly gives Rudeus a different kind of satisfaction. He joins collective labor, operates a snow-melting device and heals a child's injured feet without payment. The child had feared that unsolicited treatment would become an unpayable debt. Shortly afterward, he learns that Mimir, Counter Arrow's healer, has died during an expedition and that Sara disappeared in the same snowstorm. Timothy regrets ordering the surviving members to retreat; Suzanne and Patrice insist that the decision protected the others and was shared. Remembering Sara's earlier return for him, Rudeus sets out alone. He is uncertain whether he wants to repay care, obtain a definite result or refuse to abandon someone as he feels he was abandoned.

In the forest he recovers Mimir's remains and finds Sara's earring, initially concluding that she too has died. That conclusion proves false. A huge Icefall Treant has captured her alive. Rudeus first struggles against it, then identifies its limited attack pattern and cuts away enough of its defenses to retrieve her. Sara's own survival efforts also mattered: she used an enchanted decoy, improvised shelter and treated her injury before being captured. After Rudeus heals her, she helps carry Mimir's remains home. Her open gratitude gives Rudeus the feeling that he has himself been rescued. Counter Arrow, preparing to resume its search at dawn, welcomes them back with relief.

By summer, Rudeus has spent a year in Rosenburg and works with Counter Arrow most of the time. He and Sara share more of their histories. Her hostility gives way to affection, although their discussion of nobility leaves unresolved differences and omissions. A trip to buy a replacement dagger becomes a date. Rudeus enjoys her company and recognizes her interest, while fearing another repetition of Eris's departure. When they attempt intimacy, he discovers that he cannot respond physically. Neither understands the other's distress. Sara interprets the difficulty as evidence that he finds her unattractive; she protects herself by claiming that she had only intended to repay a debt. Rudeus hears another rejection.

He drinks heavily and, when Soldat provokes him again, finally strikes back and voices his fear of being disliked and left alone. Soldat accepts the blows without retaliating, listens privately, and acknowledges that he has been wrong to keep pressing a wound he did not understand. He takes Rudeus to a professional sex worker, Elise, hoping that experience might help. It does not cure the problem. Elise nevertheless offers care and observes that Rudeus seems afraid of women and of rejection; she suggests that trust would have to grow gradually. She also knows of his earlier kindness to the child at the snow-clearing job. Their encounter has a commercial setting without being exhausted by that setting.

On the way home, Soldat urges Rudeus to speak to Sara again. Rudeus momentarily accepts the need for dialogue, then drunkenly disparages her just as Sara and Suzanne arrive looking for him. Sara is deeply hurt and tells him not to appear before her again. Rudeus's fear expands from her rejection to the imagined loss of the entire party. When he tries to kill himself, Soldat immediately intervenes. After considering other support, Soldat offers to take him to another city without requiring him to join the party. Rudeus accepts, explicitly recognizing that he is fleeing. He resolves not to become dependent on a single party again.

Sara's subsequent section reveals the other half of the failed exchange. She had loved Rudeus and had used gratitude to conceal her fear of admitting it. She knew nothing of his condition and wrongly imagined that he had spent the night mocking her with Soldat. Suzanne and Timothy persuade her to seek more information. By the time she does, Rudeus has left. Elise explains his distress in an angry disclosure of a client's private circumstances. Sara comes to regret her part in the hurt but fears that an apology would itself be rejected. She hopes to apologize if they meet again. No reconciliation occurs within this volume.

For a little more than another year, Rudeus moves between towns with Soldat's support, working temporarily with other parties and spreading his name. He postpones addressing his condition until he finds Zenith and partly justifies the break with Counter Arrow as necessary for that search. Elsewhere, Elinalise hears a rumor locating him near Pipin and sets out to tell him that his mother has been found. The message has not yet reached him. An internal extra follows a separate route: Ariel, now student council president at Ranoa Magic University, has turned the defeat of school bullies into political influence. Fitts proposed the operation, Ariel secretly provoked the confrontation, and her attendants promoted its public account. Their recruitment plans bring Rudeus's name onto a list. Fitts recognizes it with joy, but the volume ends before any resulting invitation or meeting.

Sections C and D provide the complete unit route and the evidence for these events and distinctions. Medical explanations remain character interpretations; the synopsis does not supply a diagnosis or a later cure.

### MT-E-LNJP-V07-001 — Attention begins before its explanation is correct

**Source:** spine 8, `text/part0007.html` p4–119. **Representation:** third-person caravan narration, dialogue and sometimes conjectural access to Rudeus's thoughts. Suzanne presses beyond ordinary traveler reserve to ask about the withdrawn boy. His missing-mother answer changes her account of his condition without explaining all of it. Sara objects to what she perceives as rudeness toward someone trying to help. The northern travel advice later proves useful.

One reading makes Suzanne simply intrusive and Sara merely hostile. Another makes the intervention wholly accurate care. Both miss the difference between noticing distress and knowing its cause. The prologue grants the newcomers practical perception while retaining their errors and Rudeus's reluctance. C003/C007/C012 strengthen; confidence is high about the intervention and limited about motives the narration only proposes.

### MT-E-LNJP-V07-002 — A search plan can coexist with indifference to survival

**Source:** spine 8 p123–373; spine 10, `text/part0009.html` p1–29; illustration spine 9. **Representation:** first-person self-appraisal, guild transaction and dialogue. Rudeus uses Suzanne's lodging advice, keeps money from the earlier journey and plans to attract search information through fame. Yet dissolving Dead End makes him cry, and his choice of a dangerous solo job proceeds without the reconnaissance he would normally undertake. The supposed deaths of his old party are other people's inference, not disclosed fact. Suzanne's argument persuades him that others must know and transmit the specific purpose of his search.

The practical plan prevents calling him wholly inactive; it does not refute the represented wish to cease struggling. His preserved resources also qualify an exclusively self-made restart. C001/C003/C010/C012 distinguish capacity, planning, self-regard and received information. The source supports acute distress, not a clinical diagnosis supplied by the analyst.

### MT-E-LNJP-V07-003 — Being stronger does not eliminate a coordination problem

**Source:** spine 10 p34–209. **Representation:** preparation, introductions, tactical discussion and Rudeus's appraisal. Rudeus considers their scarce margin for mistakes, their uncertainty about him and Suzanne's explicit effort to educate Sara in cooperation. Sara's distrust contributes to inefficiency, but unrestricted power is not automatically the safest arrangement among unfamiliar partners. Rudeus's own explanation remains a fallible reading of their reasons. C001/C008 support task-specific practice and distributed judgment; this is not proof that every restriction was necessary or that his deference is always wise.

### MT-E-LNJP-V07-006 — Sara's evidence changes before her category does

**Source:** spine 12 p125–202. **Representation:** Sara's first-person account and conversation with Suzanne. The surname, resemblance and staff lead her to classify Rudeus as a privileged runaway. Her complaint concerns an unequal distribution of risk: some novices can retreat to wealth while others must live with the damage. ## E. Character changes and their limits

Rudeus's central change is not a single movement from despair to health. Work habits return before self-confidence; recognition produces pleasure before trust becomes secure; magical adjustment remains possible while interpersonal inference fails. The bear expedition restores a commitment to live, the ruins provide received rescue, the winter work provides ordinary contact, and Sara's gratitude supplies a sense of being valued. These are distinct events with different supports. The later crisis is therefore a regression in some capacities without proving that every earlier change was unreal. His new travel arrangement again permits effective work while leaving the intimate problem unresolved (`002–005,007,009,013–024,026`).

His explanations are also actions. Calling himself useful at a low price helps organize a public strategy; calling every relationship a liability helps justify departure; calling the forest search a desire for results protects him from admitting need. None is entirely empty. None exhausts the observed conduct. The model must preserve alternatives when he himself cannot settle his motives. It must also preserve the bartender-directed threats, public insult and intrusive appraisal: suffering does not monopolize his position in every encounter (`014–015,019–024`).

Sara has an independent history of lost protection, skilled work and attachment to her party. She is not merely a cure opportunity placed beside him. Her hostility tests noble status against responsibility; his conduct gradually defeats the identification of ancestry with disposition. Her actual return to help him precedes an unqualified admission of liking him. Ordinary borrowing, making equipment and negotiating purchases reveal a preference for reciprocal obligation that later provides a vocabulary for concealing affection. In the final misunderstanding, her self-protective account changes again after new knowledge, but the fear of another rejection prevents repair. The last wish to apologize is an intention with no completed recipient (`006,012,016,018–020,025`).

Soldat changes how he treats Rudeus after learning what his behavior conceals. His insistence on exposing a “real” self was coercive and harmful; his own acknowledgment makes that more than an analyst's contrary judgment. He then listens, seeks help, interrupts danger and adjusts the offer of support to what Rudeus can accept. His advice remains uneven and sometimes stereotyped. The account supports a relation-specific change and a reported pattern of helping others, not a universal safe mentor. Timothy and Suzanne offer another leadership arrangement: explicit authority and active coordination, public de-escalation and private argument, tactical caution and shared responsibility. Their mistaken judgments do not cancel their care (`003,010–014,017,021–026`).

Elise's care is grounded in work, observation and a concrete network of gratitude. Her later anger also leads to a confidentiality breach and a premature judgment of Sara's purpose. Ariel and Fitts operate in another register: real loyalty and protection combine with knowingly selective public information. Their agency is not interchangeable with the distressed pair's limited understanding. Roxy, Ruijerd, Paul and Eris have continuing effects through memories and beliefs, but V07 does not give them new directly observed present choices. The school reference to Roxy is a report of standing; the references to Zanoba and Cliff establish enrollment and perceived promise, not their assent to Ariel's plans (`022,025,028–030`).

## F. Relationships and agency

Rudeus -> Counter Arrow combines effective service, gratitude, dependence, a wish to stay and formal resistance to membership. Counter Arrow -> Rudeus combines incomplete knowledge with repeated invitations, adapted teamwork, return under danger and respect for exhaustion. No inference that all four survivors reject him at the end is licensed. Sara -> Rudeus moves through distrust, return, growing affection, concealed confession, anger and regret. Rudeus -> Sara moves through wary acceptance, comfort, attraction, fear, hurtful defensive speech and flight. These directions intersect without sharing the same knowledge or timetable.

Debt is particularly ambiguous. Sara uses it to preserve reciprocity when accepting money; Rudeus can honor it without insisting on cash. A balanced account at the gate becomes permission to continue together. But a claim of obligation can also disguise affection and be received as its denial. The same vocabulary does not supply the same intention in every scene. Practical equality, emotional safety and accurate understanding must therefore remain separate relationship variables (`016–020,025`).

Support sometimes expands available options by reducing its demands. Soldat first urges a conversation, then offers travel without party membership when Rudeus cannot act on the first suggestion. This is a concrete response to disclosed inability. It does not eliminate the costs to the people left behind. Conversely, Elise's protective disclosure gives Sara knowledge while her admonition narrows the apparent legitimacy of approaching him. Help, privacy and repair can come into conflict even when the helper's concern is sincere (`024–025`).

Material structures matter throughout: winter limits travel and food; guild registration determines who can work together formally; party rules and low pay shape reputation; noble decisions expose villagers to danger; a commercial institution houses dependent young workers; school protection is compromised by status and the anticipated cost of enforcement. These are represented constraints, not an invitation to infer a uniform culture beyond the admitted scenes.

## G. Focalization, language and form

The prologue initially makes Rudeus the object of other people's attention. First-person access then reveals how little his functioning appearance settles. Sara's early first-person sections expose the conflict between class judgment and contrary evidence. Her final labeled viewpoint shifts to third-person narration, which goes beyond reporting her belief: it marks her conspiracy explanation as false and supplies counterfactual limits on what she could have noticed. That stronger local correction does not make her eventual reading of departure the whole truth. Readers know the fear driving the departure as well as the rejection she experiences (`001,006,012,025`).

Repeated failures to understand are varied formally. Rudeus's lack of arousal first appears as an unnamed absence and acquires an explanation later. An earring appears decisive until another scene supplies its function. A publicly visible kiss and an actually spoken insult become the basis for an invented account of an entire night. The school confrontation makes a still sharper distinction: ordinary witnesses see a real attack but cannot hear its calculated provocation. Here narrative access exposes an asymmetry that the characters deliberately manufacture (`015,020,023,025,029`).

Written register is practical rather than diagnostic by itself. Rudeus's courteous `僕` and honorific presentation are useful at work and can conceal defensive distance. Soldat requests `俺` during disclosure, but the pronoun does not independently certify truth or give Soldat correct knowledge of Eris. Timothy's smile is named as a form of protective action by Sara. Courtesy can be sincere, strategic, avoidant or several at once. No acoustic voice, actor performance or adapted scene has been admitted (`003,007,010–012,021`).

The large alternation of scene and seasonal summary gives repeated labor and attachment time to develop. It also resists dating every episode from a single age statement. Morning returns create a conspicuous contrast: the rescue return, the destructive encounter outside the entertainment district, and Sara's final morning all involve changed possibilities of contact. The epilogue's mobile routine is an accommodation after that contrast, not a narrative demonstration that all is settled.

The images concentrate attention differently from prose: the empowered caster, the thankful Sara, the threatening and then guiding Soldat, and the separate grieving Sara foreground successive relations. Character designs preserve equipment and presentation outside event time. These illustrations were actually inspected, but none independently proves a motive, age in every scene or future relationship.

## H. Normative framing

The strongest care findings have concrete recipients and costs: the party returns for Rudeus; he searches for Sara and retrieves Mimir's remains; Sara's survival labor and mourning remain visible; public work benefits a child; Soldat stays to help and adjusts his plans. These are not canceled by mixed motives. They also do not produce a general moral score that absorbs later injury. Rudeus's threats to the bartender and his disparagement of Sara are specific harmful acts within a period in which he also suffers (`004,009,013–017,021,023–024`).

The analysis keeps desire, consent, conduct and physical response separate. Sara's initiative and her later-confirmed affection are represented; Rudeus's fear and bodily difficulty are represented too. Neither physical difficulty nor hurtful denial proves a complete absence of affection. The alcohol context is recorded without an invented assessment of capacity. His idea that medical exposure is a reward is an interpretation he supplies, not a conclusion licensed by Sara's gratitude. The commercial episode contains consent and payment rules as institutional claims, but also dependent child labor and a sexualizing narrator. Care from one worker does not settle those structural questions (`016,019–022,025`).

Privacy is not automatically subordinate to a desired reconciliation. Suzanne asks permission before sharing a restrained account with Timothy. Elise knowingly discloses a client's private circumstances because she wants to protect him; this explains the breach without making it costless or giving her full knowledge of Sara. The narrative supplies new access to the reader through both acts, but their ethical conditions differ (`025`).

Ariel's operation ends real bullying and produces real relief. It also uses degrading group-directed insults, manufactured public innocence, bodily intimidation, humiliation and selective dissemination. The beneficiaries' applause does not erase those means. Conversely, identifying the manipulation does not turn the prior bullies into innocent targets. The persistence of their resentment and their privileged survival at school further limits any equation of domination with reform (`028–030`).

## I. Recurrences, world and time

The volume develops the relation between usefulness and belonging through opposed cases. Exceptional magic attracts employers, but the party supplies care before fully knowing its value. Sara's debt language both protects and conceals a relationship. Elise's paid role contains additional care. Ariel seeks talent partly as political capacity. These cases make C013 more discriminating: exchange can structure a relationship without determining every reason to sustain it.

Cold is both material and figurative. Weather closes roads, constrains food and injures bodies; fires and shelter supply literal relief. Rudeus later describes a coldness that clothing does not remove and uses alcohol to address it. The juxtaposition links embodiment and distress without proving one universal bodily mechanism. His named-muscle routine similarly promises a reliable return on care that his later bodily experience refuses to guarantee.

Chronology is anchored relatively: a few months after Eris's departure at arrival, roughly three months of early reputation work, winter around six months in Rosenburg, a year by the closer relationship with Sara, and more than another year of travel in the epilogue. Sara's explicit age of sixteen belongs to the later retrospective section; earlier estimates near fifteen need not conflict. The Rudeus design's fifteen-year label is separate from scene dating. Ariel's extra uses a current third year and a retrospective second-year election; the exact synchronization with the main narrative is not specified. No calendar is reconstructed beyond these anchors.

Knowledge remains distributed. Dead End's supposed annihilation is an uncorrected public belief. Mimir's death is confirmed, while Sara's earring temporarily supports a false conclusion.
