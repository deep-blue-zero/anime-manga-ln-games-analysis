# V13 — ordinary practice and the limits of recognition

## Developments through V12 (spoilers through V12)



Rudeus's second life begins with unusual magical capacity, sustained practice and help from others. Roxy teaches him and enables a consequential venture outside his family's enclosure. He befriends and teaches Sylphie, but also overrides an explicit bodily refusal and later apologizes. Paul's infidelity and Lilia's pregnancy cause a household crisis. Rudeus opposes expulsion through a selective account; Zenith chooses continued family life and care for both daughters. Later care does not erase Paul's earlier assault or every injury within the household. Paul forcibly separates Rudeus from Sylphie and sends him to tutor Eris. Adaptive teaching, Ghislaine's instruction and other people's work support progress, while growing affection coexists with coercion and boundary violations.

The Fittoa displacement disaster strands Rudeus and Eris on the Demon Continent. Ruijerd rescues them and escorts them home. Their party develops trust through travel and mutual dependence, but also conceals information and manages appearances. Rudeus delays a rescue to maximize gratitude, and a child dies. Under later guild pressure he prepares devastating force; Ruijerd interrupts and takes public blame. Consultation becomes a useful practice without guaranteeing transparency. Hitogami's advice brings benefits, including Kishirika's eye for possible futures, but those benefits do not establish the adviser's full purpose or make every explanation reliable.

The journey requires money, languages, local expertise and protection. The group frees abducted children, Rudeus is mistakenly imprisoned, and he later helps defend the beastfolk village. Roxy's search party passes nearby without meeting them. At Milishion, incompatible knowledge and expectations lead Paul and Rudeus to fight. Geese challenges Paul, Eris comforts Rudeus and Ruijerd restrains retaliation. A later meeting repairs the father–son relationship locally without compelling Norn's trust or Eris's forgiveness. Rescue in Shirone subsequently depends on Aisha's recognition, Zanoba's intervention and Ruijerd's unseen work. Rudeus rejects one proposed service arrangement for Aisha while retaining other unequal assumptions.

Orsted nearly kills Rudeus after learning of his association with Hitogami. A masked companion's intervention contributes to his survival. Ruijerd eventually leaves the party; return to Fittoa exposes Eris's family losses. Eris seeks a close bond with Rudeus, then leaves to train until she believes she can stand beside him. Her compressed message fails to communicate that purpose. He understands rejection and goes north to search for Zenith, while the reader knows more about Eris's intent and Roxy's search lead than he does.

Northern adventuring restores work and companionship without ending distress. Counter Arrow's specialists and Sara's own survival efforts qualify a lone-rescuer account of her rescue. Sara's suspicion turns toward affection, but Rudeus's bodily difficulty disrupts attempted intimacy. She mistakes it for rejection and defensively describes her approach as repayment; he believes her. Soldat listens after Rudeus's aggression, obtains professional help and interrupts an acute suicidal act. Sara hears real demeaning speech but also imagines ridicule that did not occur. Clarification arrives too late to produce a meeting. Rudeus continues mobile work, and Elinalise follows his reputation carrying news of Zenith.

Elinalise reports that Zenith has been located near Rapan on Begaritt; rescue has not been shown. Winter delays travel. Rudeus initially favors going to his family, then accepts a university invitation after Hitogami promises a path toward resolving his condition and warns against Begaritt. At Ranoa's University of Magic, he becomes a special student. Fitts supplies instruction, testimony, research help and valued companionship. Zanoba's craft limits lead to a division-of-labor scheme in which he and Rudeus buy the enslaved child Juli. Better food, treatment and teaching do not free her; her learning and fear coexist.

After Linia and Pursena damage a cherished figure, Rudeus retaliates despite Elinalise's objection. Defeat becomes captivity, assault, deprivation and intimidation. Repairing the object reduces his anger, while frightened public denial ends institutional inquiry. Later sociability does not retroactively establish free agreement or comprehensive repair. His powerful reputation deters some aggression and frightens others. A conditional duel with Badigadi later produces a misleading public impression of unrestricted superiority, although Rudeus knows the fight's limits.

V09 gives Cliff independent work and perspective. Rudeus rescues him before knowing the victim's identity, then helps introduce him to Elinalise and leaves the choice to the pair. Cliff accepts her reported curse, promises research toward a cure and enters a relationship; persistence has not yet produced a cure. The displacement inquiry also brings Rudeus to Nanahoshi Shizuka, Orsted's masked companion and a person connected to his former world. She reports transfer rather than reincarnation, no mana and unchanged aging. She wants to go home; he wants to remain. They negotiate research despite those different goals. A tentative link between her arrival and the catastrophe provokes Sylphiette before she receives the missing language context. The cause remains unverified.

The Fitts arrangement is disclosed: Sylphiette performs the role, but Ariel also substitutes, so attribution remains scene-specific. Sylphiette's delayed disclosure reflects fear rather than a standing prohibition from Ariel. With Ariel and Luke's assistance she stages a private encounter, requests help and names herself. The pair declare affection. After further help, drink and an openly offered treatment substance with inadequately shared details, Rudeus reports bodily recovery. His account acknowledges pain and insufficient restraint; Sylphiette's account preserves pain, happiness and uncertainty about whether helpfulness has made her his equal. Eris meanwhile trains at the Sword Sanctuary. Nina's mistaken interpretation of Rudeus's conditional victory changes her practice without making that interpretation true.

In V10, Rudeus commits to marrying Sylphie and remains at school because of relationships and unfinished work. Sylphie accepts while retaining service to Ariel; Ariel offers reciprocal patronage rather than making him a subordinate. Rudeus buys a supposedly haunted house without first consulting his fiancée. Cliff and Zanoba help investigate. An automatic doll and concealed workshop replace the original haunting explanation. Zanoba protects the artifact, recognizes its value and asks to undertake its research. Skilled workers, Elinalise's contacts and Rudeus's materials produce the renovated home. Sylphie welcomes it while challenging concealment and stating a public-touch boundary. Later repetition shows that an understood limit has not become consistent practice.

The reception reveals Elinalise as Sylphie's grandmother. Elinalise reports stigma affecting descendants and explains concealed identity and withdrawal; Sylphie accepts the relation. Cliff continues his commitment after the disclosure. A duel with Luke leads to Ariel's protective wish that marriage keep Sylphie from following her dangerous political cause, a plan not settled with Sylphie. Domestic routine includes guard work, money, shopping, lessons and care. Elinalise relieves some guard duty. Sylphie partially learns spell disruption, while Rudeus fails to acquire her wordless healing. She names boundaries, contributes resources and requests a plain apology after his interference causes a cooking injury. Affection, local restraint and renewed failures occur together.

Paul's letter announces the children's northward journey under a trusted escort, weighs competing risks and specifically asks that Norn not be measured against unusually gifted siblings. Rudeus obtains Sylphie's agreement to taking them in. Nanahoshi's major summoning experiment then fails. She concludes she cannot return, destroys work and harms herself. Rudeus intervenes and receives help from Zanoba, Juli and Sylphie. A remaining design gap becomes solvable through Zanoba's layered-artifact knowledge, Rudeus's proposal, Cliff's technical contribution and Nanahoshi's implementation. A plastic bottle is successfully summoned. Human return, overall safety and the catastrophe mechanism remain unproved. The celebration brings real pleasure while interrupting Nanahoshi's complaint about her stay.

Ruijerd delivers Norn and Aisha sooner than expected. Aisha's resourceful travel plan depends on his protection and exhausts both girls. Ruijerd cautiously suggests that Rudeus may have misunderstood Eris. Rudeus can imagine that explanation, but no direct communication confirms it or removes years of hurt. Ruijerd's tense encounter with Badigadi remains unexplained. As he leaves to search for his people, Norn asks to accompany him. She has heard more about Rudeus's difficulties and still distrusts him, citing his drunken arrival and changed companion. Ruijerd entrusts the girls to both spouses; completed delivery does not establish household trust.

Eris's interlude shows persistent training, dependence on dojo resources, painful longing and avoidance. Exhaustion and an obstructed path lead her to attack Auber; Gal stops the escalating encounter. Ghislaine challenges apparent neglect and values knowledge apart from martial rank. The narrator explicitly announces Eris's North Saint recognition one year later, an admitted prolepsis rather than present shared knowledge. The final extra returns to the escort's origin in East Port. Norn experiences Paul as her safest person despite understanding the separation's rationale. Ruijerd rescues her from a drunk, listens and offers protection. Roxy initially misreads him through inherited Superd fear, corrects her judgment after clarification and remains afraid. Paul weighs testimony and Norn's visible trust before delegating. Zenith's rescue, Cliff's cure, Nanahoshi's return and direct repair of the Eris rupture remain unresolved through V10.

Norn and Aisha move into Rudeus and Sylphiette's household with different hopes and different wounds. Aisha wants to work as a maid rather than attend school. Rudeus agrees that she may do so if she passes an examination, which she does with outstanding marks. Norn enters school but asks to live in the dormitory. Rudeus accepts, hoping that she will gain friends and a life beyond the family. Aisha protests the apparent unfairness: she had to prove herself to secure an exception, while Norn receives permission without that condition. Her complaint discloses how adults have taught her to regard her birth as making her inferior. Rudeus rejects that ranking of his sisters and explains the different decisions, though neither their rivalry nor the effects of those lessons disappear immediately.

The household develops routines alongside school and research. In an interlude, Zanoba's investigation of the mechanical doll is interrupted by Ginger's return from escort duty. She asks to educate Juliette, then objects to Rudeus addressing a prince informally. Zanoba seriously injures her for interfering with the closeness he values. Rudeus intervenes, heals her and demands an apology, but his criticism does not dismantle their hierarchy. Later scenes show Ginger teaching and correcting both Juliette and Zanoba, complicating Rudeus's fear that she cannot advise her master.

At school, Rudeus learns that Linia and Pursena have been collecting clothing from other students as a gift for him. He did not order it, but had previously dismissed warning signs and now recognizes how hard it would be for weaker students to refuse them. His rebuke mixes that recognition with indulgence toward his friends. A later account of Ariel's investigation narrows the initial report of coercion; Norn nevertheless hears the false rumor that Rudeus ordered the collection. She already finds his status difficult to reconcile with her memory of him striking Paul. Comparisons with her gifted siblings, missing school basics and fears of rejection accumulate until she shuts herself in her room.

Rudeus first imagines a repetition of the bullying that preceded his own withdrawal in his previous life. He intimidates her class before learning that his assumption is wrong. When he reaches Norn, he abandons threats and instant solutions, admits that he does not know what she needs, and stays. The narration then gives Norn's own account. She has already begun to understand the earlier quarrel with Paul, but does not know how to face her brother. Seeing his uncertainty and fear of her rejection changes her sense of him. She apologizes and accepts his comfort. Rudeus does not hear the full explanation available to the reader and refuses to claim that he solved everything himself.

Over the following month, Norn makes friends, asks for daily help with lessons and contributes stories to a proposed account of Ruijerd's life. Aisha negotiates wages and room for her own interests in gardening and decoration. Nanahoshi explains more of her staged summoning project while retaining her wish to return home. Cliff makes progress on a device that delays Elinalise's curse rather than curing it. These small developments make home a place of work, learning and chosen attachments, even as insecurity and unequal relationships persist.

Sylphiette's pregnancy brings Rudeus intense happiness and a renewed commitment to their family. Two months later, an express letter from Geese, already six months old, asks him to come to Begaritt to help rescue Zenith. Hitogami again warns that going will cause regret, refuses to explain, and proposes an alternative that conflicts with Rudeus's declared fidelity. Rudeus initially decides to stay with his pregnant wife. Elinalise prepares to go herself. Norn's attempt to undertake the journey makes the difference between wanting to help and having the capacity to do so unavoidable for him. He decides to go, relying on the people available to support Sylphiette and his sisters.

Preparations change the choices again. Rudeus persuades Elinalise to speak to Cliff, who accepts that he cannot safely accompany her and proposes marriage without waiting for a cure. Nanahoshi unexpectedly supplies a secret map of teleportation sites used during her travels with Orsted. Her earlier claim not to remember those sites was incomplete: she had retained records under a prohibition on disclosure. The map reduces an anticipated absence of well over a year to an estimated few months, making separation much more bearable, especially for Elinalise after Cliff's proposal. Sylphiette accepts Rudeus's departure with specific fears about teleportation; Aisha and Norn receive responsibilities at home. None knows the rescue's outcome.

Rudeus and Elinalise reach a concealed teleportation site and test the route back after crossing to Begaritt. Their desert journey depends on complementary abilities. Elinalise detects threats, supplies travel experience and protects Rudeus; he creates shelter and water, fights at range and heals. Succubi impose a magical state in which he makes unwanted advances toward her. She repeatedly enforces her boundaries and directs him to use detoxification magic. Outside those encounters, they also have to manage the limits of her curse device and their promise not to become sexual partners. Their skill makes survival possible without eliminating mistakes or danger.

They rescue a merchant's party from griffins and reach a bazaar. Rudeus initially judges the merchant ungrateful when the party hurries away, then learns that they had retrieved runaway camels and returned to thank him. With no guide available at this intermediate stop, he proposes joining a caravan, borrowing Aisha's earlier travel idea. The rescued merchant, Garvan, hires them for the route to Rapan. During the journey, the warrior Carmelita explains local customs, and Rudeus wrongly extrapolates that the warriors have no special personal attachments. A bandit ambush kills Tonto. Rudeus's barriers, fog and mud enable the survivors to escape, but Carmelita demands vengeance. Learning that Tonto fathered her child forces him to reconsider his assumption. Elinalise and the leader defend the retreat, although Rudeus recognizes that direct killing had not even occurred to him during the attack. The main narrative ends with arrival in Rapan, before a reunion with Paul or a rescue of Zenith.

The extra returns to Norn one month after the departure. She fears that her appeal may have endangered Rudeus and ruined Sylphiette's future. Prayer briefly comforts her, then gives way to a sense of helplessness. Cliff approaches her at the church where he is training. His manner is abrasive, and she challenges both his confidence and his interpretation of a religious story about trusting an endangered friend. Yet his account of preparing through study, knowledge and relationships gives her a course of action she can choose. She leaves intending to develop her capacities and pray at the end of her efforts. This resolves an immediate question of how to live while waiting, not the dangers faced by the absent family.

## Story synopsis — V12 (spoilers through V12)

Rudeus and Elinalise reach Rapan and reunite with the party searching for Zenith. Paul is exhausted, but the arrival of his son and news of a coming grandchild revive him. He and Elinalise exchange apologies without explaining their original quarrel. The search has acquired a second emergency: Roxy disappeared in a teleport trap a month earlier. Rudeus's book about the labyrinth provides useful routes, although he first checks that its account matches this actual place. Geese organizes provisions and navigation; Paul leads the fighting party, while Lilia, Vera and Shierra support it from town.

An independent first-person chapter shows how Roxy has survived. She studies the monsters' spawning patterns, marks routes and repeatedly escapes to a small refuge. Exhaustion, dwindling supplies and enemies obstructing her retreat eventually defeat that strategy. Facing death, she thinks of teaching, an unfinished reconciliation with her former teacher, marriage and her parents. Rudeus reaches her by breaking through labyrinth walls. She is immediately attracted to her rescuer before recognizing her grown former pupil. Her embarrassment and his mistaken interpretation of her reaction produce a painful misunderstanding, later corrected. The rescue succeeds, but the group explicitly criticizes his dangerous disregard for collapse.

With Roxy restored to the party, complementary skills and the guidebook let the expedition advance. Rudeus needs her tactical corrections as well as the experienced adventurers' protection and knowledge. When the known route ends, he recognizes a familiar layout and has Geese investigate before opening a concealed passage. Beyond it, they find Zenith inside a crystal guarded by a hydra that nullifies ordinary magic. Paul rushes forward, the monster regenerates its severed heads, and the party must retreat. Roxy identifies the creature from a book. Rudeus proposes cauterizing the severed necks, an uncertain idea the party combines with coordinated defense, healing and close-range attacks.

The second fight nearly succeeds, but the creature's final movements exceed Rudeus's ability to interpret what his foresight shows him. Paul throws him out of danger. Rudeus loses his left hand, and Paul suffers a fatal wound. Roxy finishes the hydra; the surviving party releases Zenith and returns with Paul's remains. Zenith wakes but does not recognize or converse with her family as they expect. Rudeus initially describes her condition in devastatingly total terms. Later observations show that she can learn simple routines, take an object and express a preference, while its cause and prospects remain uncertain.

Rudeus withdraws in grief. Memories of avoiding his former parents' funeral combine with the realization that Paul had always treated him as a son. His companions also mourn and disagree about helping him. Roxy learns only now that he is married and expecting a child. She approaches him, offers comfort, and an intimate encounter follows under emotionally vulnerable conditions. It gives Rudeus temporary relief but also guilt; his own account does not establish a cure. When he describes his former life as a fictional example, Roxy listens and redirects him toward caring for the family still present. Lilia undertakes Zenith's care, Geese recovers the party's resources and investigates her condition, and they arrange the journey home.

Roxy acknowledges both care and romantic desire. Rudeus admits that he loves her and has broken his promise to Sylphiette. Roxy refuses his suggestion of a temporary relationship. Elinalise later urges marriage and reports a possible pregnancy. Rudeus proposes, but Roxy asks him to obtain Sylphiette's agreement first and then ask again. The journey ends with Rudeus fearing that Hitogami's warning may concern a disaster at home. He finds the household safe; this corrects his fear without independently explaining the warning.

The family hears the account of Paul and Zenith together. Norn's initial protest gives way to grief and acknowledgment of Rudeus's effort. She takes Paul's sword, Aisha takes his dagger, and Zenith reaches for his armor. Norn also notices that Aisha is holding back joy at Lilia's return amid everyone else's sorrow and enables their reunion; Rudeus realizes he has misread her restraint. The family brings Zenith and Lilia into the home. The adventurers leave to pursue their own lives, after mutual acknowledgments of loss and help.

Rudeus then asks to bring Roxy into the marriage. Norn condemns the broken promise and speaks of Sylphiette's loneliness, but also attacks Roxy and assumes the authority to speak for Sylphiette. Sylphiette intervenes, welcomes Roxy and insists on her own decision. Norn remains dissatisfied while continuing to value her brother. The three adults negotiate their household arrangements; Roxy explicitly says she is not pregnant, contradicting the premise that helped move the proposal. Rudeus concludes that Elinalise manipulated him, but there is no independent confirmation of her exact intent. In private, Sylphiette admits that she feared being displaced, and Rudeus acknowledges that he would react very differently if their positions were reversed.

Roxy becomes a university teacher. Zenith receives a medical consultation and a rehabilitation plan, without a known cure. A month later Sylphiette gives birth to Lucy, with Lilia's experienced assistance and Aisha's help. Rudeus, overwhelmed despite his magical abilities, recognizes a parallel with Paul. He takes news of his daughter to Paul's grave and acknowledges himself as a son and a still immature parent. His renewed commitment to living earnestly closes this volume as an undertaking, with grief, failure and obligations continuing beyond it.

## Present-volume story synopsis — spoilers through Japanese LN V13

Rudeus's third school year begins with a larger household and a changed body. A flashback to the week after his return explains how Roxy sought a teaching post at the university and reconciled with her former mentor Jinas. Rudeus supports her wish to work, but his extravagant praise sometimes misses what she asks of him: she wants recognition as his wife as well as his teacher. Meanwhile, Zanoba and Cliff have made a magical hand that Rudeus can use as a prosthesis. It restores useful movement and some sensation, although its strength, precision and mana requirements need care. Rudeus promptly damages a treasured object by handling it too forcefully.

School and home settle into routines sustained by several people's work. Sylphiette returns to her duties, Roxy prepares lessons, Lilia and Aisha care for the household, and Suzanne—Rudeus's former adventuring acquaintance, now married to Timothy—works as Lucy's wet nurse after losing her own third child. Rudeus delights in his daughter while trying to imagine himself as a dignified father. Zenith remains unable to explain her experience, but increasingly joins family activities. She tends plants with Aisha, approaches Lucy, and shows particular interest in Norn. These changes offer hope without explaining her condition.

Cliff challenges Rudeus over his broken promise to Sylphiette and asks him to consider Norn's dependence on the household. Norn begins demanding sword lessons. Rudeus trains her seriously, worries about injury, and adopts methods whose severity he does not fully trust. When her admirers mistake a lesson for bullying, he explains her own wish to learn, then turns their informal gathering into a regulated fan club. His protective effort also reveals how readily he assumes authority over her affairs. Aisha likewise resists his judgment when he wants to destroy the small animated plant she has raised. Roxy's knowledge of cultivated treants makes him reconsider, and the family keeps it under safety conditions.

Roxy eventually agrees to teach Rudeus and Sylphiette water king magic. Rudeus reproduces Lightning, while Sylphiette reaches water saint level but cannot yet perform the harder spell. The journey home gives the two women space to discuss happiness, insecurity and their place in the household. Rudeus recognizes that sustaining their trust will require conduct, not simply declarations. Cliff and Elinalise marry in a Millis ceremony. Later, a shopping trip combines gifts for Norn and Aisha with a surprise for Roxy; an overnight stay follows conversations in which the wives acknowledge the plan and agree with each other.

The delayed celebration of the sisters' tenth birthdays takes place when both are already eleven. A fishing excursion allows Roxy and Norn to become more comfortable together. At home, Zenith briefly smiles in a way the family recognizes, and Lilia gives both daughters matching gifts while still retaining some of her old service distinctions. Sylphiette then presents Roxy with a new hat and welcomes her through an explicitly shared family ideal. Roxy later says that this was when she first felt fully recognized by Sylphiette.

A royal escort assignment brings Rudeus back into contact with Sara. Roxy wins the adventurers' respect through her professional judgment, while Rudeus initially avoids Sara out of embarrassment. With encouragement from Sylphiette and an opportunity arranged by Ariel, they finally speak. Sara apologizes for what she said before understanding his illness; Rudeus acknowledges his avoidance and his earlier lack of sincerity. They agree to treat each other as former companions, and Sara explicitly rejects reopening the romance. Afterward, Sylphiette replaces Rudeus's unstable promise of no further wives with concrete expectations of openness, mutual affection and consultation.

The school year ends. Linia and Pursena graduate, fight to decide which of them will return to pursue tribal leadership, and choose separate paths. Pursena wins; Linia plans to travel and enter business. Nanahoshi challenges Rudeus's joking treatment of their serious decision and helps the injured Pursena. Nanahoshi herself has suffered recurring illness, even as her research progresses. The collaborators summon a watermelon, an imperfectly specified result that they nevertheless regard as a successful third stage. At the celebration, Norn reveals that she has already helped the student council for more than a year. Sylphiette's testimony corrects Rudeus's fear that she cannot manage the work. Nanahoshi proposes an introduction to Perugius for the next research stage, with the possibility that his knowledge could also help them understand Zenith. The visit has not yet occurred.

The closing interlude returns to Eris's training. The Water God Reida brings her pupil Isolte to the Sword Sanctuary. Isolte defeats Eris, Nina defeats Isolte, and subsequent practice establishes a three-way pattern of advantages rather than a simple ranking. Their discussions expose both technical limits and mistaken assumptions about one another. Eris shares what Ruijerd taught her and participates partly by recalling how Rudeus once exchanged ideas with her. The narrator projects forward to her eventual improvement against Isolte; Rudeus has received none of this information within the present-volume story.

## E. Character deep readings and state changes

Rudeus now has enough stability for repeated practice to become visible. He supports Roxy's work, enjoys childcare, pursues research, apologizes to Sara and changes his judgment of Norn when given evidence. These are specific achievements. His repeated failure to distinguish his own affectionate intention from another person's preferred terms remains equally observable: Norn's unwanted kiss, Roxy's resisted lesson request, Aisha's private belongings and the club organized around Norn all test different boundaries. Neither confidence nor parenthood produces uniform ethical competence. His admission that Sara was a rebound relationship is consequential because it relinquishes a more flattering account without requiring renewed romance as a reward.

Roxy's teaching is both employment and a continuing relation to learning. She wants recognition, fears being surpassed, supplies knowledge unavailable to Rudeus, earns respect in a new party, and declines a recruitment offer. These contexts prevent reducing her to either a flawless mentor or a comforting wife. The disagreement over the plant and the successful escort are not merely demonstrations that Rudeus was correct to idolize her: both establish her own reasons and capacities. Her later report about the hat supplies a precisely dated change in felt belonging, not proof that every earlier kindness had been insincere.

Sylphiette's voice varies across situations. She can be insecure in comparison, direct about unwanted coercion, attentive to Aisha's tastes, confident about Norn's work and firm about secrecy in marriage. Her endorsement of a plural household does not require inventing an unspoken rejection; her self-minimizing account of luck does not require endorsing its accuracy. The progression toward clearer terms is locally supported, while unequal dependence and earlier fear remain part of the model's available context.

Norn demonstrates persistence in training, independent council service, developing authorship and increasingly easy conversation with Roxy. Those achievements precede some of Rudeus's recognition. She also needs her stated privacy and contact limits respected. Aisha combines practical skill with interests in plants, attractive clothes and equal family recognition. Resistance over Beat and surprise at matching gifts show why competence cannot stand in for a full account of her position. Their agreement about conduct around Lucy is a shared decision, not evidence that rivalry has vanished permanently.

Sara receives a new temporal state after a long source gap: an experienced adventurer, a mentor to a younger colleague and a person with a specific repair intention. Her explicit romantic closure governs interpretation over a familiar expectation of renewed attraction. Suzanne likewise has a present household, work, grief and preferences. Lilia and Zenith's exchanges warrant attention without invented interior dialogue. Zenith's observable initiative and corroborated smiles expand the evidence; the cause, consciousness and prognosis of her condition remain unresolved.

Cliff's criticism, technical collaboration and material wedding preparation supply different tests of conviction and care. Zanoba's craft-oriented aid and persistence at the unfinished core show purposes beyond serving his master. Elinalise listens, prepares for marriage and facilitates others' desires, while the drug bargain retains a consent question the scene does not answer. Nanahoshi's research urgency coexists with illness, practical help to Pursena and correction of Rudeus's spectator stance. None of them is adequately described by a single helping function.

Eris's learning is neither solitary raw effort nor simple obedience. She admits a missing skill, listens, shares technique and uses an earlier relationship as a model for discussion. Nina and Isolte contribute independent goals and corrective interpretations. The narrated three-way advantage is especially resistant to a global combat ranking. The closing projection is a limited narrator statement about a later result; it does not supply the intervening year or current information to Rudeus.

## F. Relationships, agency, and household practice

V12 established a declared household arrangement; V13 asks how particular people can inhabit it. Work schedules, childcare, individual earnings, gifts and agreed terms give that arrangement material form. Roxy-to-Sylphiette recognition and Sylphiette-to-Roxy welcome cannot be collapsed into two parallel relations to Rudeus. Their mutual conversation before the overnight stay is also different evidence from his earlier reliance on Elinalise's assurance.

Rudeus-to-Norn protection includes encouragement, treatment and a willingness to reconsider, but also conditional instruction, intrusive contact and decisions made about her admirers. Norn-to-Rudeus trust is visible in requests and continued involvement; it does not remove her right to say no. Aisha-to-Rudeus opposition is not merely manipulative charm: she first offers relevant evidence about the plant, then resorts to leverage. His eventual correction comes through Roxy's expertise as well as the disclosure pressure. These sequences require tracking both direction and timing.

The late birthday modifies Aisha's standing through a shared gift while leaving Lilia's service vocabulary intact. Zenith's gestures toward both daughters and Lilia constrain a description of her as wholly absent from relationships, but do not authorize an account of what she silently knows. The family ideal invoked by Sylphiette is selected from this present cooperation; its earlier coercive and unequal formation remains part of the admitted history.

Sara and Rudeus repair a relation by naming their former failures and accepting its changed form. Neither apology requires a reunion as lovers, and Sara's career does not depend on his renewed approval. Linia and Pursena similarly define their own separation, even though Rudeus questions their plans. Nanahoshi's objection to his jokes makes the witness's conduct part of the relationship, rather than treating his narration as a neutral account of theirs.

## G. Focalization, voice, and form

The opening flashback, year of seasonal routines, late gifts and narrator projections complicate simple chronological summary. Source order remains fixed while the chronology ledger distinguishes event time, disclosure time and projected future. Rudeus repeatedly fills missing information with a plausible script: Cliff's intended performance, Sara's anger, Norn's capacity and Nanahoshi's illness. Later speech corrects some of these accounts. A pattern of corrigibility is supported; an inference that every uncorrected judgment must be false is not.

The eleven rumor codas produce a public character different from the reader's situated Rudeus. They compress deeds into intimidating, flattering or comic claims. Some distort an event; others recast its tone. The missing twelfth rumor coda allows the Perugius disclosure to open outward rather than close in reputation. The third-person opening to the water lesson withholds the request's object while making refusal and bystander fear legible. Its joke therefore works through a real discrepancy of access, not through the absence of represented pressure.

Rudeus's sports commentary over the graduation duel turns action into entertainment until Nanahoshi explicitly objects. The manga-like illustration reinforces the comic register, while wounds, refused magical healing and chosen departure restore personal stakes. The wedding image uses luminous scale; the hat scene uses parallel faces; the watermelon image isolates an instant of joy from ongoing illness. Images add emphasis without proving consent, diagnosis or a permanent emotional state. The written voices are analyzed as prose; there is no audio performance or adaptation-camera evidence.

The Eris interlude permits movement among Reida's expectations, Nina's appraisals and Eris's reasons. A reader learns what Rudeus does not. The final further-year statement is a prolepsis already contained in V13, not evidence that later volumes have been inspected. The fictional epigraph's two languages and the profile's author-attributed tolerance statement retain separate status from narration and characters' speech.

## H. Normative-framing findings

The recurring distinction is between a benevolent aim and the authority claimed to realize it. Training can be wanted while its severity remains contestable; regulating admirers can reduce risk while excluding the admired person's voice; offering affection can still override a refusal. V13 supplies concrete counterevidence to both a fully redeemed parent and an incapable one. Respect for different preferences is tested most clearly when equal affection cannot be expressed through identical treatment.

Marital scenes are analyzed non-graphically. The relevant evidence includes voiced refusal, physical restraint, intermediary persuasion, practical concerns, direct agreement and the acknowledged possibility of compliance at a private cost. The water-lesson reveal does not revoke Sylphiette's principle that marriage is not blanket permission. The later wives' mutual agreement matters in that particular scene. The aphrodisiac transfer is represented; its administration and Cliff's informed agreement are not. Those gaps must remain gaps.

Cliff's account of dependence changes the analytical interpretation of a seemingly settled dispute without claiming to read Norn's mind. Sylphiette's voluntary terms and insecurity likewise coexist. Sara's apology does not make her responsible for treating Rudeus's illness, and his greater attachment to people who helped him is a self-explanation rather than a moral ranking of women. Her warning against denigrating one person to praise another is directly pertinent to how such comparisons should be read.

Zenith's silence is neither consent nor absence of personhood. Repeated actions and corroborated smiles justify revising a totalizing initial label; they do not establish a cure. Rudeus's lifestyle theory about Nanahoshi and Suzanne's apparent cheer after bereavement are similarly limited forms of access. This is literary analysis of represented care and judgment, not medical guidance. The author's tolerance statement cannot eliminate the need to distinguish coexistence from permission for specific harms.

## I. Themes, world, and chronology

The volume's durable theme is learning through correction in ordinary life. Prostheses translate collaboration into bodily capacity; a garden turns local knowledge into a challenge to categorical fear; a birthday redistributes recognition; an old acquaintance permits accountability without renewed romance. Repetition matters because promises have to survive small, inconvenient situations. Domestic peace is labor, rather than an event that makes history cease to matter.

Research retains technical uncertainty. Mana activation, device cost, imperfect sensory feedback, cancellation exceptions, Lightning's compression and the mixed summoning result each have local evidence and an explicit limit. Public sales, autonomous dolls, universal anti-magic, combat use of Electric and precision summoning remain unfinished. A useful experimental result is not proof of every theory attached to it. The world also limits opportunity through household rank, educational status, ceremony and expected tribal marriage; characters work within or contest these structures differently.

The opening first teaching day looks back to a week after the V12 return. Subsequent markers include one month, a further three months, the wedding after another month, the shopping trip two weeks later, autumn's approach to winter, graduation and the experiment several days later. Rudeus approaches eighteen; Lucy is a little over one. These relative markers and approximate year/half-year recollections are retained without manufacturing a day-exact calendar. Norn's council work predates its disclosure by over a year. Suzanne and Sara supply different stages of knowledge about Patris. The interlude's one-month training period and further-year projection are not forcibly synchronized with the household's latest day.
