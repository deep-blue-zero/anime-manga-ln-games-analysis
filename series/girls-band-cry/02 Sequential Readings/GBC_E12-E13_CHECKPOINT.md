## What changed since the earlier checkpoints

At E04 the five had not formed. E08 established the importance of who can speak for another musician and authorized ledger materialization. Through E11, getting back to work after a correction, an expressly shared song, and the request to continue even if the festival failed became better tests of group durability. E12–E13 supply the first contract, a measurable shortfall, a contested restructuring and the first shared action after the decision to go freelance. The sequence tests several earlier propositions under genuinely changed working conditions.

- **Character and voice.** Nina's claim that she likes the self who went to help is compatible with acknowledging the harm that followed. Hina gives her own account of the price of staying close to Nina. Tomo says Hina's course was more sensible and identifies what she values in Nina’s voice. Her qualification about the others prevents a single line from speaking for all five. Written register is established; delivery at these turns remains subject to listening.
- **Behavior and relationships.** In E12 Nina loses a vote and later asks to reopen the issue. In E13 an ostensible improvement to the event now carries an identity concession demanded in private. At the agency the same girl can bow after objecting to bowing at the rehearsal. Recipient, object of the concession and shared responsibility distinguish the cases. Rupa and Subaru can make room for others’ judgment, without a general claim about either’s private state. Nina and Hina’s history is clearer; friendship repair remains open.
- **Music, performance and authorship.** E11’s credited lyric work remains part of Nina's history. E12 attributes the hot-pot observation and new draft to Momoka. E13 gives Nina a guitar and a public account of how another person heard an older song. Distinguish whose recording a listener first heard, who drafted the current song, who helped revise it and who performs it. A specific chord or isolated instrumental change remains outside the evidence.
- **Visual form.** A publicity image, a release counter, Hina’s provocative note, and the final drawn music player convey different acts of address. The wide concert view and the five-person thanks anchor the literal performance. The credit illustrations give an intelligible proposition of collective travel but no dated day-by-day account. Keep the family looking at a phone apart from the adjacent roadside panel.
- **Industry and labor.** Salary, a share of earnings, event placement, capacity, presales and plays carry different units. The agency’s willingness to handle funds and contracts makes the five’s gratitude intelligible even as they leave. The offered repayment has no shown settlement. A small audience that listens cannot turn an interim tally into a final revenue result.
- **Revision history.** Two mistaken E12 attributions were rejected, several ED claims were narrowed from dated events to strongly implied or symbolic imagery, and Hina’s motives were reopened where previously inferred from others. The earlier V1 E13 gap remains a gap. The seven ledgers each retain the E11 bootstrap and both later changes.
