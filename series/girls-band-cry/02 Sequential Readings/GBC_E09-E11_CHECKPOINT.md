# Girls Band Cry E09-E11 - V2 Checkpoint

## 1. Checkpoint purpose and authority

This checkpoint freezes the third prospective tranche of the *Girls Band Cry* V2 reread after Episodes 9 through 11 and before Episode 12 is admitted.

It has six responsibilities.

First, it reconciles E09-E11 into one E11-boundary character, relationship, musical, formal, material, and institutional model. Episode readings remain authority for exact Japanese wording, local chronology, measurements, visual locators, and claims whose meaning depends on a particular audiovisual sequence. This checkpoint is authority for what the three episodes establish **together**.

Second, it closes the CP2 held-out experiment. `GBC_E05-E08_CHECKPOINT.md` froze twenty-seven prospective expectations before E09 was opened. E09-E11 were then read against that unchanged register. The resulting prediction-by-episode event tally is:

- **33 CONFIRM**;
- **8 PARTIAL**;
- **1 CONTRADICT**;
- **39 NON-DIAGNOSTIC**.

Those eighty-one cells are audit events, not eighty-one independent propositions. The checkpoint therefore judges each of the twenty-seven frozen predictions exactly once at the unique-rule level.

Third, it must preserve the experiment's most important falsification. E09 directly contradicted `GBC-CP2-P20` by giving Tomo the permanent forecast `一生かかったってまともにならないわよ`. E11 then supplied the bounded branch the frozen rule had expected: `時間なくなるわよ` and `ヘタクソ` can remain task-specific while developmental time and participation stay open. The checkpoint may not choose whichever episode is more convenient. It must build a branching Tomo model that explains both.

Fourth, it must adjudicate two intentionally non-directional triggers. `P26` withheld a strong `conflict becomes composition` claim until direct process evidence arrived. E10-E11 finally provide that evidence in a bounded domain: Nina becomes explicit lyric author, names the invalidating propositions the song answers, and immediately performs the Nina-authored lyric. `P27` required direct Hina-side or Nina/Hina evidence to revise a trigger-only model without predicting motive. E11 supplies the school-period confrontation and therefore forces a Hina model revision while leaving motive open.

Fifth, it freezes the final prospective register for E12-E13. The architecture explicitly requires:

`E09 -> E10 -> E11 -> checkpoint E09-E11 -> E12 -> E13 -> checkpoint E12-E13`.

Accordingly, no E12 or E13 evidence is admitted here. The final two episodes must be read prospectively against the E11-current state frozen in this document.

Sixth, it resolves an infrastructure debt. Checkpoint 2 explicitly authorized immediate materialization of seven standalone longitudinal ledgers from the E08-reconciled state. That materialization did not occur. Because the ledgers are mutable infrastructure rather than frozen evidentiary releases, the correct response is not to manufacture obsolete E08-only files now. They should be bootstrapped from the **E11-reconciled state**, with episode/checkpoint provenance retained and E09-E11 represented as the first post-E08 development tranche.

The evidence boundary is strict:

- `GBC_E05-E08_CHECKPOINT.md` is admitted as the frozen E08-boundary model and source of the immutable CP2 register;
- canonical E09, E10, and E11 readings are admitted in full;
- V1 enters only through the already-declared revision-target framework and post-prospective adjudications in the canonical episode readings;
- no E12-E13 scene, result, dialogue, song, industry outcome, Hina evidence, ending image, or retrospective series memory is admissible;
- no later supplemental motion source is silently backfilled into E11. If an E11 concert/preparation video audit is performed later, it is a separately routed audiovisual audit and does not mutate this frozen checkpoint without an explicit later-version process.

Once canonical, this checkpoint is frozen.

## 2. Executive checkpoint thesis

E09-E11 change the central question of *Girls Band Cry* from **whether conflict can be survived** into **what survived conflict can become**.

The E05-E08 tranche ended by dismantling a preservation contract. Nina rejected being used as Momoka's remembered younger self. Momoka re-entered the band's future. Nina named her attachment `告白`. The five-person band survived an exit-level crisis. But the checkpoint deliberately stopped short of several mature V1 claims: Tomo's permanent-judgment pathology was not yet licensed, Rupa's bereavement was biography without a present causal rule, Hina remained psychologically underdetermined, the confession did not establish a conventional couple, and strict `conflict becomes composition` remained unproven.

E09-E11 answer many of those questions without producing a simple maturation story.

E09 is the tranche's most important falsification episode. Tomo really can turn present inadequacy into a permanent forecast:

> `そんなままごとギター`
>
> `一生かかったってまともにならないわよ`

The technical premise is partly correct: Nina is not yet ready to play guitar at the professional level she wants. The error is temporal and jurisdictional. Tomo converts a present skill gap into a life sentence.

The same episode also explains why Tomo can suppress criticism. A requested opinion about Momoka's song is interrupted by remembered messages from an earlier group whose members left after conflict. Tomo returns to the present with `いいんじゃないの 別に`. Rupa later attributes the pattern cautiously: Tomo may have learned that expressing full seriousness destroys the group she wants to keep.

Yet E09 refuses to make the pathology total. Nina is hurt enough to say `やる気 なくなった`, seeks independent evaluation, learns that Subaru and Momoka substantially agree she is not stage-ready, and continues practicing. Tomo later calls her `ヘタクソ` in an interaction whose future remains open. Finally Tomo tells Momoka that the song is still boring, and Momoka answers `だよな`.

The episode therefore establishes two distinctions that become checkpoint-critical:

> **present inadequacy is not permanent incapacity**;

and

> **harsh criticism is not one behavioral function.**

A severe word can close developmental time, or it can remain inside continuing work. The model must classify scope, temporal horizon, target, and relational consequence rather than merely lexical roughness.

E10 moves from criticism to reconciliation. Its governing achievement is **reconciliation without rollback**.

Momoka is approached by an industry representative who prefers old Diamond Dust and dislikes the current band. Momoka refuses the flattering purity narrative:

> `それを間違っているとは思ってません`

Current Diamond Dust can have made a legitimate strategic choice, found an audience, and still be the rival against which Momoka wants to test her own music. Recognition does not require surrender.

Nina's family conflict follows the same architecture. Her father eventually acts as an ally against the school and tells her `お前は被害者なんだ`. Her sister tells her she is loved. None of this retroactively proves that her father's earlier failure was harmless or that Nina should return to the old life plan. Nina can accept love and support while retaining jurisdiction over her future.

Most importantly, E10 adds a second ground of selfhood. Nina says:

> `でも 今の私が一番好き`

and can describe her present self as real even while admitting uncertainty about whether her leap will become flight or fall. This is not the end of `間違ってない`. It is a new basis that no longer requires every external result to certify the person she has become.

E10 also turns artistic transmission into explicit authorship. Momoka asks:

> `見てみたいんだ 「私の歌」が書いた私の歌を`

The person once reached by Momoka's song is invited to write back. Nina returns from Kumamoto saying `私の作った歌` and then `できました いい歌が`.

E11 makes that authorship public.

The band does not discover one shared reason for music. Its members articulate different functions: honesty, freedom, connection, proof, continuation. Tomo's answer is not even supplied in the same symmetrical form. The episode therefore resists turning the five into a philosophical chorus.

What changes is their ability to turn difference into one work.

Immediately before `空白とカタルシス`, Nina names the social sentences she is answering:

> `「諦めろ 叶うはずがない」`
>
> `「そんなの無理 現実を見ろ」`
>
> `「このくらいでいいんだよ」`

Then:

> `だから この歌を貫いてやります`
>
> `この歌で信じていない人たちを信じさせてやります`
>
> `間違ってないって叫んでやる`

The Nina-authored song begins immediately afterward. This is the direct process bridge V2 had withheld. Social/interpersonal conflict is now explicitly converted into lyric and performance purpose.

But the checkpoint must preserve the remaining formal boundary:

> **conflict -> lyric/performance form is established; conflict -> specific harmony/rhythm/chord/instrumental-motif causality remains OPEN.**

E11 also activates Hina as a historical subject. The school-period memory gives:

> `仁菜が間違ったんだよ`
>
> `これ以上やっても 損するだけ`

This establishes that Hina knew the conflict closely enough to issue a severe pragmatic judgment. It does not establish why she did so. `Hina was bullying Nina`, `Hina was secretly protecting Nina`, `Hina wanted Nina to surrender`, and `Hina provokes Nina because she knows Nina needs opposition` all remain stronger than the source allows.

The tranche's most important unresolved point arrives after its largest public achievement. E10 gave Nina self-liking. E11 gives authorship, an audience, a collective work, and a strong performance. Nina still finishes by turning privately to Momoka:

> `桃香さん`
>
> `私 間違ってないですよね`

The checkpoint therefore rejects a linear developmental arrow in which `rightness` is replaced by `self-liking` and then disappears.

The E11-current model is additive:

1. **correctness ground** - the old injury still creates a need for the past to be validated;
2. **self-inhabitation ground** - Nina can like the person she is now even without guaranteed success;
3. **contribution ground** - she can create, work, and become useful to others;
4. **relational ground** - she remains attached to people who do not simply ratify every judgment.

These grounds can support one another and can conflict.

The third-tranche thesis is therefore:

> **E09-E11 establish that durable creative relationships do not require the disappearance of judgment, injury, rivalry, dependence, grief, or unequal competence. They require those differences to stop functioning as automatic endings. Truth can be spoken without necessarily destroying belonging; reconciliation can occur without restoring the old hierarchy; rival legitimacy can coexist with competition; a listener can become an author; and conflict can become public artistic form without becoming psychologically resolved. The band's maturity lies less in harmony than in its growing capacity to keep the future open while telling one another and the audience difficult truths.**

# 3. Three-episode longitudinal movement

## 3.1 E09 - truth becomes survivable inside belonging

E09 lowers the dramatic temperature after E08 and thereby reveals a more reusable social problem.

The central object is not whether the band survives a spectacular departure. It is whether people can say `this is bad` to one another and remain collaborators.

Tomo begins unable to do so. Her old-band loss interrupts requested criticism. She says the song is fine when she does not believe it is. Her suppression is not simple kindness; it is a defensive attempt to prevent seriousness from destroying belonging again.

Nina then becomes the test case for the opposite problem. She wants guitar feedback because she wants to move from recipient to contributor:

> `桃香さんに頼るんじゃなく`
>
> `力になって 一緒にやっていきたいって`
>
> `もちろん みんなともだけど`

Tomo's `一生` judgment wounds her. But the group does not solve the problem by pretending Nina is already good enough. Subaru substantially agrees on the present skill gap. Momoka does not offer false reassurance. Nina keeps practicing anyway.

Rupa separates seriousness from present excellence through `智の物差し`. She does not abolish standards. She rejects the assumption that Tomo's elite developmental path can be used as a universal ruler for another person's future.

The ending changes the meaning of criticism rather than eliminating it. Tomo can say the song is boring; Momoka can agree; nobody leaves.

E09's tranche contribution is therefore:

> **truth becomes a continuing relationship event rather than an automatic terminal event.**

It also forces the CP2 experiment to record a real contradiction rather than a victory lap.

## 3.2 E10 - reconciliation without restoration

E10 applies a similar distinction to larger institutions and older wounds.

Momoka can recognize that Diamond Dust's adaptation was not simply wrong and still choose another route. Nina can learn that her family loves her and still refuse to return to the old path. Her father can act as an ally now without erasing the fact that he was not the ally she needed before.

The episode repeatedly separates propositions that simpler narratives collapse:

- love from correctness;
- support from jurisdiction;
- apology from surrender;
- return from regression;
- industry participation from artistic capitulation;
- reconciliation from restoration.

The band's own care remains morally mixed. It refuses to become an avoidance refuge, but changing Nina's apartment lock to force the family confrontation is still coercive. A helpful result does not sanitize the method.

Nina's new self-ground is the episode's largest developmental advance. `今の私が一番好き` means she can value the current self without knowing whether the professional gamble will work.

The authorship transfer is equally important. Momoka no longer asks Nina to preserve a remembered version of Momoka. She asks Nina to produce something Momoka herself cannot pre-author.

E10's tranche contribution is therefore:

> **agency becomes compatible with relationship repair, and artistic inheritance becomes reciprocal authorship rather than preservation.**

## 3.3 E11 - difference becomes public work

E11 converts the preceding two episodes into ensemble form.

The opening rehearsal is already diagnostic. Arrangement quality requires subtraction as well as addition. Momoka can remove her own part because the texture is too dense. Different members can have different technical functions and different reasons for singing without one reason becoming the official explanation of the band.

Subaru makes the positive band choice explicit before the festival result:

> `ずっと続けようよ このバンド`
>
> `もし今日がダメでも`

This is unusually strong because it removes success as the condition of belonging.

Her drummer model also becomes direct text rather than elegant analyst metaphor. She identifies with the musician who works while seeing the others' backs. The relational role previously inferred from her social behavior is now explicitly connected to her chosen instrument.

Rupa and Tomo deepen their reciprocity. Rupa distinguishes estrangement from irreversible death without commanding forgiveness. Tomo answers absence with `私がいるでしょ`. Neither is reduced to caretaker or dependent.

Diamond Dust then becomes a legitimate rival through performance. The current group has been delegitimated as an idolized commercial replacement and answers by becoming better enough that its seriousness reaches the crowd. Nina can dislike the situation, admire the response, and want to take the audience anyway.

Finally the new song supplies the process evidence that previous episodes lacked. E09 made compositional criticism speakable. E10 made Nina an author. E11 tells us what the song is answering and performs it.

The episode does not end in self-sufficient triumph. Nina still asks Momoka for a verdict.

E11's tranche contribution is therefore:

> **the band can make unresolved difference public without first making the people identical or fully healed.**

# 5. Nina checkpoint model after E11

## 5.1 Correctness is no longer Nina's only self-ground, but it remains active

At the E08 boundary, Nina's professional program was still heavily organized around proof: Diamond Dust victory, public success, and Momoka's recommitment could certify that the refusals structuring Nina's life were `not wrong`.

E10 adds a genuinely new basis:

> `でも 今の私が一番好き`

The source does not require the professional gamble to have succeeded before Nina can value the person produced by it.

E11 then blocks the attractive linear interpretation that this new basis has replaced the old one. After the festival performance she still asks Momoka:

> `私 間違ってないですよね`

The current model therefore has multiple active grounds rather than one developmental ladder.

### Ground A - correctness validation

Nina still wants the past injury and subsequent refusals to receive a verdict that they were justified.

### Ground B - self-inhabitation

She can prefer the current self even while admitting uncertainty about outcome.

### Ground C - contribution

Guitar practice, lyric authorship, separate work, rehearsal, and performance let value emerge from doing rather than only from being vindicated.

### Ground D - durable relationship

Nina can remain connected to people who criticize her, disagree with her, or fail to provide full validation.

The final tranche must test what happens when these grounds conflict, especially if professional evidence is negative or ambiguous.

## 5.2 Stake-graded compression is now stronger because a clean counterexample exists

E11 gives a clean local failure:

> `ギター 弾けません`

Nina abandons the immediate live-guitar plan. She does not say that she can never become competent, that the band has failed, or that her whole musical choice is invalid.

This matters more than another dramatic outburst would. A rule becomes reconstructively useful when it has a scope boundary.

Current formulation:

> **Nina's evaluative compression is stake-graded. Actionable technical failure can remain local; shame, historical invalidation, attachment, and proof stakes are more likely to widen judgment into identity, permanence, or global correctness language.**

This does not mean low stakes are always proportional or high stakes always catastrophic. It identifies a reliable probability gradient.

## 5.3 Positive music desire is now authorship, not merely commitment

E09 confirms independent practice. E10 gives explicit lyric authorship. E11 gives public performance of that authored material.

The earlier question `does Nina actually like music, or only what Momoka/music proves?` is no longer open in its simple form.

Current rule:

> **Music is a positive chosen practice for Nina: she trains, writes, works, rehearses, performs, and seeks differentiated contribution even when immediate Momoka approval is absent.**

Proof, rivalry, and attachment remain motives. They no longer exhaust the motive set.

## 5.4 Criticism can hurt without becoming abandonment

E09 prevents a resilience caricature.

Nina says `やる気 なくなった`. She is not unaffected by Tomo.

What matters is the sequence after injury:

1. seek another evaluator;
2. receive substantially similar present-skill feedback;
3. ask Momoka directly;
4. accept that the current live role is unrealistic;
5. continue practicing;
6. later abandon the immediate guitar-performance plan without abandoning music.

Current formulation:

> **Nina can remain relationally engaged with a critic after severe discouragement when the relationship and developmental future remain open; persistence is compatible with hurt and tactical retreat.**

## 5.5 Irreversibility is no longer a general sincerity metric

Earlier Nina often made commitments physically or materially harder to reinterpret. E10 still contains strong acceptance of no-return language.

But E11 supplies a decisive limit. She wants to play guitar, learns she is not ready, and does not force the irreversible display merely to prove commitment.

Current formulation:

> **Nina may accept high material or irreversible cost when identity stakes are high, but cost itself is not necessary proof of sincerity. Self-authored reversibility and calibration can coexist with serious commitment.**

## 5.6 Family love and paternal failure coexist

E10 destroys two symmetrical simplifications:

# 12. Correctness, self-liking, failure, and non-regret

## 12.1 `間違ってない` is still a wound, not a solved thesis

E11 makes the need explicit twice:

- Nina's reason for singing is tied to wanting to believe she was not wrong;
- after public achievement she asks Momoka for the verdict again.

This confirms that correctness is not merely a slogan she has outgrown.

But E10 changes what the slogan can do.

`今の私が一番好き` creates a basis for continuing even when the tribunal is unresolved.

The current model should therefore distinguish:

- **historical correctness** - was Nina right about what happened and what she refused?
- **prudence** - was the chosen response likely to preserve options?
- **professional efficacy** - does the chosen artistic strategy work in the market?
- **self-liking** - does Nina value the person she has become?
- **non-regret** - would she choose the path again despite costs?
- **relational acceptance** - do others continue choosing her even when they disagree?

The series has increasingly prevented any one of these from serving as a master verdict for the others.

## 12.2 The final tranche's highest-value test is negative evidence

E11 gives a strong performance and an emotionally elevated state. That is not the hardest test of Nina's new self-ground.

The harder prospective question is:

> **What happens if professional evidence does not validate the story Nina wants to tell about herself?**

No result is predicted here. The checkpoint predicts only a model distinction:

- professional failure can be real;
- strategic error can be real;
- another person's caution can be partly correct;
- none of those facts automatically proves Nina's injury unreal or her current self worthless.

If Nina can preserve those distinctions under negative evidence, the E10 self-liking ground will have become much stronger.

## 12.3 Non-regret is not factual infallibility

The corpus increasingly supports a crucial ethical distinction:

> **A person may have been wrong about a prediction, inefficient in a strategy, or injurious in a relationship and still refuse the conclusion that the entire act of resistance should be regretted.**

This distinction is likely to matter more in the final tranche than any simple `who was right?` verdict.

## 12.4 Hina is a useful adversarial test because consequence accuracy is not moral omniscience

Hina's `損するだけ` may identify a real consequence structure.

Even if later evidence shows that she predicted costs accurately, that would not by itself settle:

- whether Nina was ethically wrong to resist;
- whether the school deserved accommodation;
- whether Hina's motive was care;
- whether the chosen compromise would have been psychologically survivable for Nina.

This is precisely the kind of distinction the final checkpoint should preserve.

# 14. Music, performance, authorship, and audiovisual form

## 14.1 The tranche forms a three-stage conflict-to-art chain

E09-E11 give the strongest formal progression in V2 so far.

### Stage 1 - E09: conflict -> compositional permission

Tomo begins unable to say what she thinks about Momoka's song. By the end she can tell Momoka the song is boring, and Momoka can accept the judgment without rupture.

The work has not yet demonstrably encoded the conflict. The relationship has become safe enough for craft disagreement to enter the room.

### Stage 2 - E10: lived conflict -> authorship catalyst

Momoka asks Nina to write lyrics. Nina initially cannot externalize what she wants to say. The family confrontation changes the condition under which she can write. She returns claiming the result as `私の作った歌`.

This securely links lived experience to the emergence of authorship, but E10 withholds the finished lyric-to-conflict mapping.

### Stage 3 - E11: conflict -> lyric/performance form

Nina names the invalidating propositions the song answers, says what the song will do to them, and immediately performs the lyric she authored.

This is the missing process bridge.

Checkpoint conclusion:

> **`conflict becomes composition` is now supportable only if `composition` is specified carefully. Conflict -> lyric authorship and performance purpose is directly established. The source has not yet directly traced interpersonal conflict into a specific chord, harmonic move, rhythm, or instrumental motif.**

## 14.2 `空白とカタルシス` belongs to all five in a bounded sense

The V1 phrase `first work that fully belongs to all five` survives if `belongs` means:

- differentiated ensemble performance;
- shared arrangement process;
- multiple causal contributions;
- Nina's lyric authorship recognized and collectivized;
- five-person visual/interpretive framing;
- collective public ownership.

It does **not** mean:

- all five wrote the lyrics;
- all five composed equal shares;
- all five have identical musical authority;
- every visual memory is diegetically known to Nina;
- the song expresses one psychologically identical motive.

The distinction between collective ownership and equal authorship is now essential.

## 14.3 Arrangement maturity includes subtraction

E11's rehearsal discussion shows that differentiated musicianship is not merely `everyone adds more`.

Momoka can remove her own part because the texture is overcrowded. This supports a broader ensemble principle:

> **mature contribution includes knowing when another person's part should carry the space.**

This is both musical and relational evidence, but the musical fact should remain primary.

## 14.4 Soundcheck makes professionalism procedural

The E11 soundcheck matters because it interrupts any fantasy that the climactic live emerges directly from emotion.

The band must coordinate:

- drums;
- vocal levels;
- bass;
- keyboard sounds;
