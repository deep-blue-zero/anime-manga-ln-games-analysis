## E03 - complete / canonical

`GBC_E03_DEEP_READING.md` is approximately 13,700 words and tests the E01-E02 models under ordinary trio formation, first rehearsal, and first public live conditions.

### E03 governing formulation

> A performed form can make truth communicable, a repaired relationship can still remain unstable, and a person can begin choosing a life long before she can say that she wants it.

### Most important E03 character-model results

**Nina**
- E02's `totalization under shame` is narrowed into a graded **evaluative-compression** model: low-stakes failure can still become all-or-nothing accounting (`two pages` becomes `a whole day wasted`) without automatically escalating into a permanent identity/future verdict;
- direct interpersonal repair is now demonstrated: when Subaru names avoidance without totalizing Nina, makes withdrawal genuinely available, and preserves Nina's agency, Nina can apologize (`ごめんなさい`), remain in the conversation, and explain the embarrassing mechanism rather than flee;
- the anti-intimacy mechanism becomes direct primary-source evidence: when someone approaches wanting closeness, Nina can experience reciprocation as surrender to the other person's authored script (`お前の思い通りになるか / 私はそんなに簡単じゃない`);
- positive musical desire is now behavioral fact before explicit self-description: she voluntarily spends extraordinary hours composing, resists deletion, and later hears her own material inside the trio arrangement;
- once inhibition breaks, commitment can overshoot technical limits: she sings until hoarse, separating emotional totality from sustainable vocal technique.

**Momoka**
- E02's `explicit reasoned no can stop her` rule does **not** generalize: E03 shows that Momoka's response depends on how she classifies the refusal; if she sees it as genuine preference she can stop, but if she sees fear/self-betrayal she may escalate pressure;
- her interventionist care becomes more clearly paternalistic when artistic confidence is high: she books the live before Nina agrees, physically stops attempted departure at a level visible/heard in the locked evidence, uses the band as leverage, and deliberately chooses a costume Nina will hate;
- expressive success does not retroactively make those procedures ethically neutral;
- producer cognition is increasingly constraint-responsive rather than purity-oriented: absent bassist, loop machine, digital backing, amateur venue, and Nina's novice material are all incorporated into a workable live form;
- her E02 theory of co-creation gains formal evidence because Nina's solitary composition is literally incorporated into the trio arrangement.

**Subaru**
- polished public warmth is established as a **mode**, not a complete mask: acting-school competence, weak-tie friendliness, direct rupture naming, private resentment, self-disclosure, and rougher affiliative teasing all belong to one adjustable social repertoire;
- competence inside acting does not establish that acting is her chosen future; her grandmother/family trajectory makes externally assigned role and personal desire explicitly divergent;
- she provides the first direct evidence that constructed social performance can support honesty rather than oppose it;
- `バンドやめます / ごめん!` at the coda prevents premature promotion of band membership into a stabilized positive life choice.

**Trio / music**
- E03 strongly strengthens Momoka's co-creation theory: Nina's digital experiment, Momoka's arrangement/guitar leadership, and Subaru's drums become a shared musical object that surprises all three;
- the opening visual fantasy of Nina alone among virtual instruments is answered by embodied trio performance;
- `conflict becomes composition` is **strengthened but remains OPEN** because E03 proves heterogeneous contribution and unresolved-person collaboration, not yet detailed preservation of incompatible member positions inside musical structure;
- artificial mediation is no longer the opposite of authenticity: costume, stage role, microphone, programmed backing, and arrangement can make affect communicable while remaining constructed.
