# Girls Band Cry - V1 Baseline and V2 Revision Targets

## 1. Purpose

This document converts the mature V1 interpretation into an explicit hypothesis bank for the V2 reread.

It does **not** pre-approve V1.

Every claim begins Phase 1 as `OPEN` for V2 adjudication, even when the claim currently appears persuasive.

During the reread each claim may transition through:

**PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN / REVISE / DOWNGRADE / REJECT / OPEN**

The point is to prevent the mature V1 synthesis from silently becoming the premise of V2.

## 2. Baseline sources

Primary historical analytical sources:

- root-level verbatim V1 episode deep readings for E01-E12;
- root-level E13 archival-gap record plus later ED/epilogue analysis;
- `GIRLS BAND CRY Full Series.md` as the mature V1 synthesis;
- later episode-specific V1 refinements incorporated into the recovered archive, especially E01, E08, and E11.

These sources are `historical_legacy`. They are provenance, not current interpretive authority.

## 3. Adjudication schema

Each claim below has:

- a stable V1 claim ID;
- domain;
- baseline formulation;
- main V2 test;
- initial V2 status.

Initial status is `OPEN` unless the item is a provenance fact rather than an interpretation.

## 5. Character-reconstruction targets that V1 did not adequately supply

V1 provides useful literary psychology but does not yet provide sufficient structured evidence for high-fidelity reconstruction.

V2 must add, for Nina, Momoka, Subaru, Tomo, Rupa, and Hina:

- ordinary baseline behavior;
- listener-specific speech/register;
- characteristic syntax and lexical habits;
- vocal pace, tension, laughter, crying, shouting, hesitation;
- gesture and posture tendencies;
- decision thresholds;
- escalation sequences;
- recovery and repair strategies;
- behavior when wrong;
- behavior when praised;
- behavior when criticized;
- help-seeking and help-giving style;
- relationship-conditioned exceptions;
- practical judgment under money/work constraints;
- humor and teasing;
- private versus public self-presentation;
- negative constraints: what the character is unlikely to say/do;
- explicit uncertainty boundaries.

These are **new V2 responsibilities**, not claims inherited from V1.
