# Relationship ledger

The episode and section numbers below refer to the canonical V2 readings in [02 Sequential Readings](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/). Times use the episode clock. The three frozen checkpoints govern successive cross-episode states. This materialization routes their findings and the episode-local details that test them. No fresh audio or frame inspection is claimed here.

The routes name each partner because a change in one direction may have no reciprocal counterpart. Trust, disclosure, practical dependence, ordinary proximity, touch, institutional authority and speech are reported where the readings establish them. Sharing a stage, house or frame supplies no general permission to infer physical comfort. Confidence describes the identified interaction. Family and industry routes are subdivided when members have different experiences.
