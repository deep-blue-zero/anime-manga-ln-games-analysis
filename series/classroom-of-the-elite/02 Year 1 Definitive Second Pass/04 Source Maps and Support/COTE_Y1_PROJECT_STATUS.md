## Locked Year 1 synthesis constraints

1. Ordinary life is a real self-chosen Ayanokōji project, not merely camouflage.
2. Self-authorship and reciprocal authorship are distinct; a self-chosen developmental project can still overreach another person's autonomy.
3. Horikita's endpoint is integration/self-authorship, not purification from influence.
4. Kei romance is explicitly reciprocal but remains entangled with hidden curriculum, dependency history, and unresolved irreplaceability.
5. Ichinose's solidarity is real capacity; her problem is not reducible to kindness but includes adversarial security and result conversion.
6. `実力` must distinguish possession, development, expression, legibility, ecological fit, social usability, distribution, generation, political power, recording, and model limitation.
7. Institutional measurement can be accurate about a bounded object without exhausting the person.
8. Tsukishiro's detailed White Room testimony remains tiered where Year 1 itself withholds verification.
9. `OAA_LEGIBILITY` is active at `Y1FF` only as explicitly retrospective/counterfactual paratext.
10. No Year 2, Volume 0, *Second List*, Year 3, adaptation, wiki, or later-franchise answer is permitted to settle a Year 1 boundary claim.
11. ANHS and the White Room must not be collapsed into either simple opposition or simple equivalence: Year 1 distinguishes total developmental authorship from a plural institution whose coercive powers remain partially contestable, while preserving direct evidence, informed testimony, adversarial testimony, and inference as separate authority tiers.
12. Beneficial outcomes do not retroactively authorize coercive methods: explanation, effectiveness, sincere choice, protection, dependency, forgiveness, development, rule compliance, survival value, and recognition must remain distinct from justification, legitimacy, consent, ownership, love, innocence, justice, human worth, and jurisdiction.

13. Japanese narration, character register, genre, humor, and visual paratext are primary evidence, but none is omniscient: Ayanokōji is selectively unreliable rather than globally false, alternate viewpoints limit narratorial sovereignty, illustrations establish editorial and embodied emphasis rather than transparent motive, and dossier forms remain bounded models rather than complete persons.
14. Revision authority is explicit: first-pass claims remain historical provenance, *First File* is separated into `FF-DATA`, `FF-RATING`, `FF-EDITORIAL`, and `FF-FICTION`, and major claim transitions must use `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT`, or `OPEN`; later-year answers may not be back-projected into frozen Year 1 analytical states.

Evidence IDs may not be silently renumbered, deleted, or repurposed; specialist omission does not invalidate source-grounded evidence.
