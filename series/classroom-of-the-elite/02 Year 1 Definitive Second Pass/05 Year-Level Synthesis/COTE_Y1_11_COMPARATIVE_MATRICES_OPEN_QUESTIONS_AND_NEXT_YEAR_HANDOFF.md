# 『ようこそ実力至上主義の教室へ』
## Year 1 — Comparative Matrices, Open Questions, and Next-Year Handoff
### A frozen end-of-year state model for comparison without retrospective answer leakage

> **CANONICAL YEAR-1 HANDOFF AUTHORITY**
>
> This document converts the frozen Year 1 specialist corpus into cross-axis matrices and an explicit inheritance contract for the next school year. It does not attempt to discover another master thesis. Its task is to preserve differences that become easy to flatten once later developments are known: differences between power and legitimacy, affection and reciprocity, ability and legibility, protection and jurisdiction, visible leadership and hidden sovereignty, direct evidence and attributed testimony, development and revelation, and present state versus future possibility.
>
> The Year 1 boundary is binding. Every future-facing statement below is a **question, test, or handoff obligation**, not an answer borrowed from later material.

# 0. Scope, responsibility, and handoff discipline

`Y1_11` is the final specialist document before the evidence/index layer and the continuous Year 1 synthesis. Its responsibility is therefore different from `Y1_01–Y1_10`.

Those documents answer primary questions:

- what causal architecture Year 1 develops;
- who Ayanokōji and Horikita are at this boundary;
- how relationships, class politics, measurement, institutions, ethics, and form operate;
- and how the first analytical pass was revised.

This document asks instead:

> **What must a later analyst be able to compare without re-reading the entire Year 1 corpus, and what must remain unresolved when Year 2 begins?**

Three rules govern the answer.

## 0.1 Matrices are descriptive instruments, not aggregate rankings

A single score would reproduce the very mistake Year 1 repeatedly exposes. A person can possess enormous ability while withholding it from collective use. A class can win points while centralizing too much knowledge. A relationship can be emotionally intense while weak in safe refusal. An institution can be educationally productive while procedurally coercive.

Accordingly, every matrix separates heterogeneous dimensions rather than adding them together.

## 0.2 Handoff claims have a temporal coordinate

A Year 1 endpoint claim means:

> this is what can responsibly be said **after `Y1V11.5` and *First File*, before later-year answers are imported**.

Later evidence may change the current understanding. It does not change the historical fact that this was the warranted boundary state.

## 0.3 Questions are part of the corpus

An unresolved question is not analytical failure. In a serial work, uncertainty may be a deliberate literary structure.

`Y1_10` preserved the open Year 1 questions. `Y1_11` turns them into a tracked handoff ledger so later documents can distinguish:

- **answered by later revelation**;
- **answered by later development**;
- **reframed because the original question was too coarse**;
- **still open**;
- or **made undecidable by contradictory evidence**.

No later document should simply delete a question because the analyst now knows the answer.

# 1. Executive handoff thesis

Year 1 ends by making **answerability** the hidden common denominator across its major systems.

A good leader is not merely the person with the best plan. The polity must be able to correct the plan.

A good relationship is not merely mutually affectionate. Each person must be able to refuse, contradict, change role, and remain valuable.

A useful measurement is not necessarily illegitimate. Its object must remain bounded, revisable, and smaller than the person.

A developmental intervention is not justified merely because the subject later improves. The method must remain answerable to consent, necessity, proportionality, alternatives, and risk distribution.

A school is not free merely because students make choices inside it. The authorities who author scarcity, surveillance, rules, and records must themselves be contestable.

A psychological model is not wrong merely because it is incomplete. The danger begins when accuracy about part of a person is converted into jurisdiction over the whole.

The Year 1 handoff can therefore be summarized as:

> **The next school year inherits a world in which nearly every major actor has become better at seeing, measuring, developing, or coordinating other people. The unresolved question is whether that increased understanding will become more reciprocal—or simply produce more sophisticated forms of control.**

That question applies to Ayanokōji most dramatically, but not only to him.

Horikita must discover whether developmental leadership can survive without an invisible sovereign. Ichinose must discover whether solidarity can defend itself without ceasing to be solidarity. Ryūen must discover whether voluntary loyalty can limit the ruler rather than only strengthen him. Sakayanagi must discover whether superior judgment can remain fiduciary when private rivalry becomes politically costly. Nagumo must show whether individual mobility can exist without patronage. ANHS must show whether plural authority and ordinary life are robust institutional protections rather than temporary accidents.

Year 1 therefore hands forward **tests**, not conclusions.

# 2. How to read the matrices

The matrices use several recurring dimensions.

## 2.1 Capacity

What can the person, relationship, class, or institution actually do?

## 2.2 Legibility

How accurately can other actors observe that capacity?

## 2.3 Authority

Who is allowed to convert knowledge into decision?

## 2.4 Answerability

Can the decision be questioned, refused, corrected, appealed, or reversed?

## 2.5 Development

Does the system make participants more capable of independent action over time?

## 2.6 Dependency

What must a participant continue receiving from the system or relationship to remain safe, recognized, or functional?

## 2.7 Information symmetry

Do relevant parties know enough about the structure governing them to choose intelligently?

## 2.8 Record integrity

Does actual performance become official consequence without unauthorized distortion?

## 2.9 Model limitation

Does the evaluator understand that a useful model remains smaller than the person or institution represented?

These dimensions recur because they connect the specialist corpus without erasing the differences among its questions.

# 4. Ayanokōji comparative state matrix — capacity versus legitimacy

Ayanokōji is especially vulnerable to scalar reading because he is so often the most capable person in the room. Year 1 demands a disaggregated model.

| Affective/relational life | clearly real but underarticulated | friendship, attraction, ordinary pleasure, rivalry, and desire accumulate | can he tolerate reciprocal vulnerability rather than only particular attachment? |
| Self-authorship | substantially strengthened | `生まれて初めて` and ordinary-school-life goal mark positive choice (`Y1V11.5-E084–E089`) | can a self-chosen purpose avoid reproducing the White Room's grammar? |
| Moral restraint | inconsistent | low-coercion scaffolding coexists with manufactured dependency and crisis intensification | will he select less coercive methods when they are available but slower? |

The Year 1 endpoint is therefore deliberately unstable:

> **Ayanokōji has become more capable of choosing his own life before he has shown that he can consistently leave other people ownership of theirs.**

This is the central individual handoff problem.

| Protection model | Representative case | What it preserves | Dependency risk | Answerability | Year 1 judgment |

| Authoritarian protection | Ryūen → followers | security through ruler's capacity and retaliation | high dependence on ruler and fear | low but rising through follower dissent | effective, coercive, incompletely constrained |

The handoff test is not whether protection disappears. Year 1 never argues for atomistic self-sufficiency.

The test is:

> **Does protection increase the protected person's future ability to choose among protectors, contradict the protector, and remain safe after the original role changes?**

## 8.1 Legitimacy is multidimensional

| Legitimacy type | Question | Strong Year 1 examples | Failure mode |

The Year 2 handoff therefore prohibits the sentence:

> “Polity X is the best class.”

unless “best” is explicitly defined by a bounded dimension.

# 10. Ability and legibility matrix

The Year 1 ability model must be carried forward in separated layers.

| Distribution | can unlike abilities be assembled into group performance? | Paper Shuffle; Event Selection | average individual score |
| Generation | can the person produce answers outside another actor's model? | Horikita's independent judgments; Kei's rooftop refusal | obedience to successful mentorship |
| Political power | can the actor change incentives, rules, interpretations, or coalitions? | Ryūen, Nagumo, Sakayanagi, Ayanokōji | personal talent alone |
| Recording | does performance enter the official record intact? | Tsukishiro chess interference (`Y1V11-E133–E145`) | actual performance |
| Model limitation | does the evaluator know the file is incomplete? | *First File* ratings/OAA (`Y1FF-E041–E048`) | total personhood |

This taxonomy creates a strict Year 2 comparison protocol.

A later higher or lower rating should trigger four separate questions:

1. did possession change?
2. did expression change?
3. did institutional legibility change?
4. did the metric or environment itself change?

Without those distinctions, apparent “development” may actually be revelation or measurement change.

# 17. Handoff transition vocabulary for Year 2

Year 2 should not use the `PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN / REVISE / DOWNGRADE / REJECT / OPEN` vocabulary mechanically for every scene. It should use it when a **Year 1 boundary claim or question changes authority**.

For each `Y1H-###`, later analysis should record one of the following:

- `ANSWERED_BY_REVELATION` — later evidence discloses something already true at Year 1;
- `ANSWERED_BY_DEVELOPMENT` — the character/institution genuinely changes after Year 1;
- `REFRAMED` — the original question turns out to use an inadequate conceptual model;
- `PARTIALLY_ANSWERED` — later evidence resolves one dimension but leaves another open;
- `CONTRADICTED_BY_LATER_EVIDENCE` — a Year 1 inference loses current-state authority;
- `STILL_OPEN` — no adequate answer yet;
- `UNDECIDABLE_AT_CURRENT_BOUNDARY` — evidence remains materially conflicting or source-tiered.

Example format:

```yaml
handoff_id: Y1H-011
y1_question: "Does Kei become genuinely irreplaceable to Ayanokōji?"
y1_state: OPEN
later_transition: STILL_OPEN
later_boundary: "populate only when later evidence is actually analyzed"
evidence: []
```

The point is not bureaucracy for its own sake.

It prevents the later corpus from turning **development** into supposed earlier essence.

# 18. Development-versus-revelation transition matrix

The Year 2 analyst should explicitly classify major changes.

| Later observation type | Correct interpretation test | Common error to avoid |

# 24. What Year 2 must inherit about measurement and merit

The Year 1 merit problem has four mandatory constraints.

## 24.1 Real ability exists

The series is not anti-evaluation. People differ substantially in academic, physical, strategic, relational, political, and creative capacity.

## 24.2 No single metric captures total capacity

A score may be accurate about a bounded performance and radically incomplete as a person-model.

## 24.3 Measurement environments alter what they measure

Exams create scarcity, incentives, coalitions, betrayal opportunities, and liabilities. The resulting behavior is evidence, but it is evidence produced inside a designed ecology.

## 24.4 Record integrity is part of meritocracy

- object measured;
- weighting;
- visibility;
- strategic manipulability;
- developmental time horizon;
- consequence severity;
- appeal/correction mechanism;
- and record integrity.

# 25. What Year 2 must inherit about narration, voice, and formal evidence

The Year 1 formal model is an epistemic safeguard.

Ayanokōji's first-person narration is **selectively incomplete**, not globally false. He is often reliable about:

- immediate event mechanics;
- rule structures;
- observed incentives;
- tactical constraints;
- and what he consciously notices.

He is less complete about:

- advance planning;
- the full scale of his ability;
- responsibility for environments he helped author;
- emotional investment;
- and motives that become legible through behavior before he can name them.

Year 2 should therefore distinguish at least four levels whenever his narration becomes important:

1. **what he says happened**;
2. **what he says he intends**;
3. **what his behavior demonstrates**;
4. **what alternate viewpoints or later events constrain**.

This is not a license to declare inconvenient narration false.

The burden of revision remains evidentiary.

The same caution applies to character registers.

- Horikita's increasingly cooperative leadership should be tracked through changes **within** her controlled, formal voice rather than by expecting a personality replacement.
- Kei's `あたし`, social fluency, directness, and fear-fragmentation remain continuous aspects of one person.
- Kushida's public cushioning and private hostility are both genuine social performances with consequences.
- Hirata's `僕` and inclusive language are sincere care and defensive structure simultaneously.
- Ryūen's rough register encodes command without proving intellectual simplicity.
- Sakayanagi's refined register can intensify rather than soften hierarchy and aggression.

Address changes also remain bounded evidence. A surname-to-given-name shift can indicate intimacy, role change, or private recognition. It does not independently prove romance, equality, or moral legitimacy.

Illustrations remain primary visual paratext. They can establish:

- bodily distance;
- pose;
- injury;
- clothing;
- expression;
- and editorial emphasis.

They cannot independently settle hidden motive.

The Year 2 handoff principle is:

> **No single representational layer—narration, dialogue, illustration, metric, dossier, or metaphor—may become the whole person.**

# 32. Counterarguments to the handoff model

A comparative matrix can create false neatness. Several objections therefore deserve preservation.

## 32.1 Counterargument: the dimensions are still analyst-created categories

Correct.

The matrices do not claim that characters think in terms such as “epistemic legitimacy” or “role-change durability.” The categories are analytical abstractions built to compare recurring textual problems.

Their legitimacy depends on whether they preserve differences visible in the primary corpus rather than forcing every scene into the same theory.

## 32.2 Counterargument: Year 1 is too early to classify polities constitutionally

The labels are provisional historical models, not claims of permanent essence.

Their value lies in describing how each class currently organizes:

- authority;
- information;
- dissent;
- protection;
- weakness;
- and strategic action.

Later years may transform them. The transition is part of the intended analysis.

## 32.3 Counterargument: “reciprocity” privileges liberal autonomy over other relational values

The corpus does not treat independence as the only good.

Dependence, loyalty, hierarchy, teaching, protection, and sacrifice can all be legitimate.

The answer-back standard asks a narrower question:

> does the relationship preserve the other person's standing as a subject whose ends can differ?

This is compatible with chosen dependence and unequal expertise.

## 32.4 Counterargument: the White Room comparison over-centers Ayanokōji

Correct if applied indiscriminately.

ANHS is meaningful for many students who have no White Room relation. Its class politics, friendship structures, labor of teaching, point economy, and ordinary adolescence must be analyzed independently.

The White Room is a comparative extreme of developmental authorship, not the only lens through which the school exists.

## 32.5 Counterargument: keeping old questions open can become artificial once later answers exist

The project distinguishes archival and current-state purposes.

A later document may answer the question decisively.

What it may not do is erase the fact that Year 1 had not yet answered it.

That distinction is essential to literary analysis of serial revelation.

# 37. Final synthesis — the Year 1 handoff is a map of constrained freedom

The most useful way to understand the end of Year 1 is not as a completed transformation.

Almost every major figure has acquired more capacity.

Horikita can lead more people and use more kinds of ability.

Sudō can regulate himself and contribute beyond athletics.

Kei can refuse and choose inside a relationship that began through coercion.

Ryūen understands that fear is not sufficient to explain loyalty.

Ichinose survives the collapse of a pure public identity without losing her class's trust.

Sakayanagi demonstrates extraordinary command while making the costs of centralized interpretation visible.

Ayanokōji has acquired something the White Room did not assign: ordinary interests, particular attachments, a desire to remain a student, and the beginnings of a self-chosen legacy.

Yet increased capacity does not automatically produce increased freedom.

It can create new forms of jurisdiction.

A more perceptive leader can become a more sophisticated paternalist.

A stronger relationship can make dependency more exclusive.

A more accurate rating system can produce more persuasive totalization.

A more capable school can create harsher scarcity.

A more self-authored Ayanokōji can become freer to control other people's development.

That is why the Year 1 endpoint is best handed forward as a set of **constraints on interpretation** rather than as a triumphant thesis of completed growth.

The central question entering the next school year is:

> **Can people and institutions become more capable without allowing capability itself to become a title to rule?**

The strongest Year 1 counterexamples already exist.

Sudō can answer Horikita back.

Keisei can define support as disagreement when necessary.

Hiyori can remain loyal while correcting Ryūen.

Ichinose can trust Ayanokōji while remaining his class rival.

Kei can give an answer that exceeds the dependency model that produced her initial role.

Manabu can leave Suzune a principle without prescribing the person she must become.

Ayanokōji can sometimes choose not to optimize.

ANHS can contain teachers and students who resist institutional misuse from within.

These cases do not prove that reciprocal authorship will win.

They prove that Year 1 has made it imaginable.

The handoff therefore closes with a deliberately unresolved proposition:

> **Freedom in *Classroom of the Elite* is not the absence of influence, dependence, hierarchy, measurement, or development. It is the preservation of enough authorship that a person can answer the systems that know, protect, rank, teach, love, or develop them—and can become someone those systems did not already know how to contain.**

That is the Year 1 state that Year 2 must inherit.
