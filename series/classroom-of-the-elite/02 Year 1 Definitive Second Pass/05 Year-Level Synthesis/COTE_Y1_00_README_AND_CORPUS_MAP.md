# 1. Mature Year 1 thesis

The completed second pass supports one governing literary thesis:

> **Year 1 treats ability and development as problems of authorship. People and institutions can become genuinely good at seeing, measuring, protecting, coordinating, or developing other human beings without thereby acquiring ownership over them. The ethical and political question is whether asymmetry remains answerable: whether the person being understood or developed retains the power to refuse, contradict, reinterpret, leave, and become someone the more powerful actor did not design.**

This thesis joins several strands that the first pass had often treated separately:

- `実力` is real but multidimensional and environmentally expressed;
- measurement can be locally accurate without exhausting the person;
- development can be genuine even when the method used to produce it is coercive;
- beneficial outcomes do not retroactively legitimate the means;
- protection can become ownership when safety is monopolized or dependency is authored;
- leadership becomes more legitimate when the developed or governed person can answer back;
- ordinary life is not merely camouflage or respite but a positive counter-curriculum in which self-chosen ends acquire value;
- institutional power includes the power to decide which performance becomes the official record;
- and a dossier, score, psychological model, narration, reputation, or analysis can be useful while remaining smaller than the person it represents.

The compact final principle is:

> **The file is evidence about the person. The file is not the person.**

For the fully developed argument, use `COTE_Y1_FULL_SYNTHESIS.md`.

The Year 1 corpus owns the second category while preserving the first through immutable volume deep readings. It deliberately does not claim the third.

# 4. Source hierarchy and evidence authority

Authority is **question-relative**, not simply chronological and not determined by filenames such as “definitive.”

## 4.1 Primary source layers

### `LN-TEXT`
Original Japanese novel prose and dialogue.

Strongest for:

- event sequence;
- dialogue;
- interior narration;
- explicit rules;
- local motive claims;
- narratorial language;
- ambiguity;
- development across scenes.

### `LN-ILL`
Original novel illustrations.

Strongest for:

- composition;
- pose and bodily distance;
- clothing and visible physical detail;
- editorial visual emphasis;
- scenes selected for embodied representation.

Illustrations establish what is shown and emphasized. They do not grant transparent access to motive.

### `FF-FICTION`
Exclusive *First File* fictional material.

This carries meaningful literary authority because it presents narrated experience, dialogue, and sometimes interiority rather than merely summarizing a profile.

### `FF-DATA`
Direct guidebook factual data.

Useful for dates, records, institutional facts, and officially presented documentary information.

### `FF-RATING`
Ratings, hypothetical OAA-style measurements, or other official modeled evaluations.

These are strong evidence for **what is being measured or officially modeled**, not for a person's complete latent capacity or moral worth.

### `FF-EDITORIAL`
Official retrospective/editorial framing.

Useful for how the publication retrospectively presents Year 1. It can compress ambiguity and therefore does not automatically override the more granular novel record.

### `PRIOR`
Earlier project analysis.

Provenance and retrieval aid only. Never primary evidence.

### `LATER`
Later-year evidence.

Excluded from Year 1 adjudication except as an explicit note that a future corpus may revisit the claim.

## 4.2 Claim-type vocabulary

The governing analytical method uses:

- `TF` — textual fact;
- `CI` — character interior/self-report;
- `IR` — institutional rule or record;
- `VF` — visual fact;
- `SI` — strong inference;
- `IT` — interpretive thesis;
- `UA` — unresolved ambiguity;
- `VJ` — explicit value judgment;
- `RC` — retrospective correction;
- `RR` — retrospective recontextualization.

Optional confidence grades are:

- `H` — high;
- `M` — medium;
- `L` — low.

Ayanokōji's `CI` material requires particular care. His first-person narration is privileged evidence but not omniscient access to his own motives. The Year 1 model is **selective narratorial incompleteness**, not global deception and not total transparency.
