# Relationship ledger through Volume 10

## Major Volume 10 changes

### Horikita–Manabu

The relationship moves from fearful imitation toward chosen counsel. Suzune initiates the meeting and asks for courage; Manabu tells her to grow as herself rather than chase either him or Ayanokōji (`Y1V10-E045–E048`). The older brother’s approval becomes less valuable as a score and more valuable as permission to stop using him as the score.

### Ayanokōji–Horikita

Ayanokōji remains an invisible information source but does not fully author Horikita’s public judgment. Their relationship therefore shifts slightly from developer/visible proxy toward **environmental catalyst / emerging independent political actor** (`E049–E055`, `E089`).

### Horikita–Hirata

The pair become explicit constitutional opposites under forced scarcity. Horikita accepts the need to judge and own the decision; Hirata rejects the class’s moral jurisdiction to rank peers and psychologically ruptures when she acts (`E050–E057`). Their conflict is ideological before it is interpersonal.

### Ayanokōji–Hirata

Ayanokōji tries to reframe Hirata’s impossible sacrifice question and later helps the class protect Hirata from his own self-expulsion attempt (`E034–E035`, `E083–E084`). The relationship is not yet intimate, but Ayanokōji treats preserving Hirata’s social function as important while increasingly understanding the trauma beneath it.

### Ayanokōji–Kei

Kei is explicitly protected, but the relationship’s asymmetry deepens ethically: Ayanokōji can imagine Manabe’s expulsion as a way both to reduce Kei’s fear and increase her trust in him (`E028`, `E093`). Protection continues to risk becoming proprietary evidence of indispensability.

### Ayanokōji–Ichinose

Ayanokōji’s alternative allows Ichinose to refuse Nagumo’s unwanted dating condition and save Class B, but he privately treats the intervention as a useful trust-building event (`E096–E103`). The relationship therefore grows through an action that is simultaneously autonomy-enhancing and strategically instrumentalized.

### Ichinose–Nagumo

Nagumo’s earlier trust-access relationship becomes explicit coercive leverage. Ichinose asks whether reciprocal feeling exists, fears becoming someone else if she accepts, and later states relief at not having to date him (`E070–E072`, `E103`).

### Ayanokōji–Ryūen

The relationship now exceeds pure threat management. Ayanokōji admits both strategic reasons and the simple fact that he did not want Ryūen expelled (`E099`). Rivalry is becoming a form of selective attachment without yet being named friendship.

### Ryūen–Ishizaki/Ibuki/Hiyori

Ryūen’s followers act voluntarily to preserve him after coercive authority has weakened (`E091–E101`). This is a major shift from obedience toward chosen political loyalty.

### Sakayanagi–Ayanokōji

Sakayanagi protects Ayanokōji from the Class Vote while simultaneously using Yamauchi to provoke him. Tsukishiro’s arrival then gives the pair a common external threat without making them allies; Ayanokōji responds by requesting a formal direct contest (`E077`, `E111–E123`).

## Current relationship thesis

> Volume 10 repeatedly distinguishes attachment from ownership. People protect one another, but the ethical quality of protection depends on whether the protected person retains authorship over the terms of rescue and whether hidden costs are displaced onto third parties without consent.

# Relationship ledger through Y1V08
## Volume 8 delta

### Manabu–Nagumo

- **Visible relation:** rival student-council leaders competing through large-group score.
- **Hidden relation:** Nagumo uses a field Manabu tried to exclude, making Tachibana and Class A's reserves part of the contest (`Y1V08-E043–E047`, `E128–E142`).
- **Trust structure:** Nagumo's prior promise-keeping becomes the credibility required for one decisive betrayal (`E134–E135`).
- **Current state:** Manabu wins the formal measure; Nagumo dictates the political meaning.

### Manabu–Tachibana

- Tachibana conceals danger to avoid burdening Manabu (`E107–E109`, `E132`).
- Manabu refuses to let her value be reduced to the score and finances her rescue with Class A (`E137`).
- Their care is reciprocal but communication is distorted by exemplary self-denial.

### Ayanokōji–Kei

- Private naming and intelligence exchange persist (`E068–E069`).
- Ayanokōji places Kei inside his guaranteed protection category (`E033`).
- He promises to prevent her expulsion by any means (`E143`).
- Reciprocity grows, but information and protective authority remain radically asymmetric.

### Horikita–Kushida

- Horikita asks for trust rather than tactical compliance (`E114–E115`).
- Kushida explains why secret knowledge itself is coercive even when unused (`E116–E117`).
- Horikita continues the project without Ayanokōji's guarantee (`E118–E119`).
- The relationship is self-authored but not yet mutually chosen.

### Keisei–Hashimoto–Ishizaki–Albert–Ayanokōji

- Temporary cohabitation moves from class mistrust to voluntary disclosure and ordinary assistance (`E088–E105`).
- Hashimoto integrates without claiming formal office (`E091`, `E102`).
- The group ends with handshakes and reluctance to separate (`E103`, `E120`).
- Compulsory proximity produces a real but time-bounded community.

### Ichinose–Hiyori–Mii-chan

- Ichinose's patience creates space for Hiyori to approach Mii-chan voluntarily (`E065`, `E076–E085`).
- Leadership here works by waiting and invitation rather than command.

### Hashimoto–Ryūen

- Hashimoto offers information and future betrayal for a guaranteed route to Class A (`E110–E113`).
- The encounter remains brokerage rather than loyalty (`E149`).

## Current relational thesis

> Volume 8 proves that trust is both the condition of genuine community and the vulnerability through which political actors gain access.

## Endpoint assessment

The principal relationship change is not romance completed but **reciprocity becoming negotiable**. Kei can now contest the role assigned to her and offer protection in return. Ayanokōji still controls the information and safety architecture that makes the relationship possible.
