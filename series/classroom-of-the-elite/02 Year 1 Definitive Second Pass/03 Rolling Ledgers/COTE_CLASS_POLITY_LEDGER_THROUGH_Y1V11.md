## Constitutional synthesis through Y1V11

`POLITY-HORIKITA` now offers the strongest Year 1 evidence for a polity whose leader **increases the usability of other people without claiming to possess their abilities personally**. Horikita cannot force Kōenji’s talent into collective ownership, but she can build around the capacities that are voluntarily available. Sudō’s development further proves that a polity’s portfolio is dynamic rather than a fixed census of entrance-state ability.

`POLITY-RYUEN` becomes more legitimate in the sociological sense because followers choose to preserve the leader after fear has been broken as an exclusive basis of rule. It remains ethically authoritarian because external coercion and `勝つための悪逆` are still accepted.

`POLITY-ICHINOSE` demonstrates that solidarity is itself political capacity, but capacity without adversarial security can be exploited. The required correction is not necessarily abandonment of trust; it is the development of defenses that do not require remaking the polity in Ryūen’s image.

`POLITY-SAKAYANAGI-ORIGIN` remains the most centralized high-capacity order. Volume 11 strengthens both its effectiveness and its succession/fiduciary risk: Sakayanagi’s personal contest with Ayanokōji increasingly matters to decisions whose consequences are borne by classmates.

Above all four student polities sits a newly explicit fifth level: **administrative procedural sovereignty**. Tsukishiro can alter the record on which student competition depends. A polity can therefore govern itself competently and still lose control over the institutional meaning of its actions.

## Current questions

1. Can Horikita preserve distributed leadership when Ayanokōji has consciously made her development a private project?
2. Can Kōenji’s autonomy ever become contractually compatible with collective survival?
3. Can Ryūen convert voluntary loyalty into institutions that survive his absence?
4. Can Ichinose add adversarial security without destroying the trust that constitutes her polity?
5. Can Sakayanagi separate personal rivalry from fiduciary responsibility to Class A?
6. What internal or external authority can constrain an administrator capable of altering the official record?

# 2. `POLITY-HORIKITA`

## Provisional constitutional description

**Developmental pluralism under hidden environmental sovereignty.**

The class contains multiple forms of ability that do not naturally coordinate:

- Horikita’s academic and planning strength;
- Hirata’s emotional legitimacy;
- Kushida’s social network;
- Kei’s control of female atmosphere;
- Sudō’s physical capacity;
- Ike’s practical knowledge;
- Airi’s contextual courage;
- Kōenji’s unavailable excellence;
- Ayanokōji’s concealed synthesis.

## Source of authority

No single stable public source yet exists.

- Hirata possesses voluntary social authority.
- Horikita accumulates visible strategic credit.
- Kushida possesses classwide accessibility.
- Ayanokōji controls hidden information and decisive contingencies.

## Information system

Fragmented and asymmetric.

Ayanokōji increasingly centralizes private knowledge while delegating public legitimacy. The class does not know the true authorship of its island victory or the existence of the Kei network.

## Treatment of weak members

Initially dispositional and punitive. Horikita’s developmental turn introduces repair and trajectory, while Ayanokōji protects some students and speculates about pruning others (`Y1V04.5-E056`).

## Main strength

Heterogeneous capacities and high developmental potential.

## Main vulnerability

The system may become dependent upon an invisible coordinator whose decisions cannot be challenged because they are not visible.

## Current succession question

Can public leadership and class integration survive if Ayanokōji stops privately correcting the environment?

# 3. `POLITY-RYUEN`

## Provisional constitutional description

**Authoritarian mobilization through fear and information monopoly.**

## Source of authority

- Ryūen’s willingness to use violence;
- offensive success;
- central control of information;
- credible retaliation.

## Information system

Strongly centralized. Phones, favored-person data, and operational tools are placed under Ryūen’s control (`Y1V04-E027`).

## Treatment of dissent

Suppressed through fear, threat, and hierarchy. The absence of reports allows the school to treat coercion as non-actionable (`Y1V04-E028`).

## Treatment of weak members

Primarily instrumental; value depends on usefulness within the ruler’s plan.

## Main strength

Speed, offensive unity, and willingness to exploit formal gaps.

## Main vulnerability

Distrust prevents delegation, creates bottlenecks, and may convert followers into silent rather than corrective subordinates.

## Current succession question

Does any class constitution remain if Ryūen is removed, or is the polity merely his extended will?

# 4. `POLITY-ICHINOSE`

## Provisional constitutional description

**Consensual solidarity and distributed legitimacy.**

## Source of authority

- trust in Ichinose;
- accessibility;
- voluntary cooperation;
- rule-conscious internal support from Kanzaki.

## Information system

More distributed and voluntarily shared than Ryūen’s. Ichinose uses social diagnostics and disclosure rather than seizure.

## Treatment of dissent

Kanzaki can object while remaining inside the leadership structure. This suggests early constitutional capacity for loyal opposition.

## Treatment of weak members

Current evidence emphasizes protection and inclusion rather than pruning.

## Main strength

Broad legitimacy, social coordination, and willingness to aid even rivals when the rule system itself is threatened.

## Main vulnerability

Trust may be exploitable by actors who do not honor reciprocal limits; class cohesion may depend heavily upon Ichinose’s personal legitimacy.

## Current succession question

Can the polity function if Ichinose is absent, discredited, or unable to decide?

# 5. `POLITY-SAKAYANAGI-ORIGIN`

## Provisional constitutional description

**Defensive elite hierarchy with incomplete factional visibility.**

At this boundary Katsuragi is the principal visible leader. The local evidence does not yet justify a complete account of Sakayanagi’s philosophy or control.

## Source of authority

- current top rank;
- academic/strategic reputation;
- hierarchical coordination;
- information restriction.

## Information system

Defensive and compartmentalized. Katsuragi treats silence as rational strategy suited to the leading class (`Y1V04-E030`).

## Treatment of dissent

Insufficient evidence for mature assessment. Internal faction and elite competition are suggested but not yet fully established.

## Treatment of weak members

Current evidence is too limited for a responsible general conclusion.

## Main strength

Stability, caution, and ability to make the status quo strategically valuable.

## Main vulnerability

Rigid defense may be vulnerable to asymmetric disruption; concealed factional conflict may weaken unified response.

## Current succession question

Who actually governs the polity, and can visible leadership survive the emergence of a stronger internal claimant?

# 7. Cross-polity conclusions through Y1V04.5

1. No class is governed solely by the student who appears most visible.
2. Information structure is as important as formal leadership.
3. The school’s class system creates internal constitutions without providing explicit constitutional rules.
4. Ayanokōji’s polity is developing a **shadow constitution**: he does not hold office but governs survival, information, and developmental opportunity.
5. The strongest current class systems combine multiple abilities; the most centralized systems act fastest but carry greater single-point failure.
6. Cross-class ordinary relationships prove class allegiance is powerful but not total.

# 8. Open polity questions

# 9. Volume 5 polity delta

## `POLITY-HORIKITA`

The sports festival reveals that the class still lacks a settled rule for converting exceptional ability into legitimate leadership. Sudō can dominate physically without being followed; Horikita can design an efficient allocation without winning consent; Hirata retains voluntary authority because he adapts to weaker members; Ayanokōji remains the hidden actor deciding when the class should be helped or allowed to fail.

The most important constitutional improvement is the Horikita–Sudō bond. A visible planner and a visible athlete begin coordinating through reciprocal recognition rather than pure command. This reduces, but does not eliminate, dependence on Ayanokōji's shadow constitution.

## `POLITY-RYUEN`

Ryūen's polity becomes more sophisticated under surveillance. Control no longer depends only on secret violence; it can organize real injury and genuine evidence into a false causal narrative. This increases offensive flexibility while deepening the polity's ethical dependence on a ruler willing to use subordinates' bodies as strategic capital.

## `POLITY-SAKAYANAGI-ORIGIN`

Sakayanagi's emergence complicates the earlier Katsuragi-centered picture. The volume establishes her as a major internal intellectual/political force and gives her unique knowledge of Ayanokōji, but the complete constitutional balance inside Class A remains unresolved locally.

### Constitutional conclusion through V7

`POLITY-RYUEN` provides the first direct succession crisis. Once the central sovereign loses legitimacy, the class has no settled replacement constitution. Yet follower refusal prevents the polity from simply dissolving with the ruler. `POLITY-HORIKITA` faces the inverse problem: visible leadership is becoming more distributed while the hidden security sovereign claims he can withdraw. Both classes now test whether development survives the central author.


# 10. Volume 7 polity delta

## `POLITY-HORIKITA`

The polity’s visible developmental structure is now strong enough for Ayanokōji to imagine withdrawing from coerced class intervention. Horikita has improved political perception; Sudō obeys relationally internalized restraint; Hirata, Kushida, Kei, and the friendship-group network provide differentiated functions. Yet the class does not know that its hidden constitution includes a coerced Kei network, adult sanctuary, rooftop violence, Manabu as witness, and evidence-based containment. The succession thesis is therefore plausible but untested.

## `POLITY-RYUEN`

Ryūen’s personal sovereignty is comprehensively defeated. The polity does not immediately dissolve: Hiyori and Kaneda can perform some visible leadership functions, while Ibuki, Ishizaki, and Albert preserve Ryūen as a person and possible future actor. This is the first serious evidence that the class may contain political relationships not reducible to fear, though they remain products of a coercive history.

## Comparative result

Volume 7 makes succession the central constitutional question. `POLITY-HORIKITA` may be able to survive the hidden author’s withdrawal; `POLITY-RYUEN` must discover whether a class survives the ruler’s defeat. Both cases remain unresolved at this boundary.

## Constitutional synthesis through Y1V10

The Class Vote is the clearest comparative test of the four polities so far because it holds the scarcity mechanism constant while exposing different theories of legitimate membership.

- `POLITY-HORIKITA` accepts selection but demands visible argument and liability.
- `POLITY-ICHINOSE` treats membership as nonfungible and distributes unavoidable risk equally rather than hierarchically.
- `POLITY-SAKAYANAGI-ORIGIN` centralizes authorship before procedure begins.
- `POLITY-RYUEN` discovers that voluntary attachment can preserve a leader whom the formal vote would remove.

Ayanokōji’s shadow constitution intersects all four. He does not publicly rule any class, yet he alters information, private-point markets, rescue feasibility, and the cost borne by third parties. Volume 10 therefore sharpens the legitimacy problem already present in the class system:

> **A polity may reach a strategically competent result while leaving unresolved who possessed the right to author the conditions under which the result became rational.**
