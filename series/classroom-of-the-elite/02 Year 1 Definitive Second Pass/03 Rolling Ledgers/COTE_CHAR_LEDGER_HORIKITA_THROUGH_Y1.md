# 堀北鈴音 / Horikita Suzune
## Canonical Year 1 state through *First File*

Horikita's Year 1 endpoint is best described as **integrated self-authorship plus distributed, correctable leadership**.

## Integration, not purification

The V11.5 mirror prologue blocks a simplistic liberation story. Horikita calls the personality, appearance, grades, and hairstyle organized around the sister she thought Manabu wanted a `偽者`, but immediately insists that the years lived in that form are also `紛れもない私` (`Y1V11.5-E003–E005`). She does not excavate an untouched authentic self underneath influence. She changes the governing purpose of a life whose prior years still belong to her.

The haircut is therefore a chosen reorientation, not deletion of Manabu's causal importance.

## Succession without imitation

Manabu's final recognition is anti-prescriptive. He does not tell Suzune to reproduce him. He tells her to grow as herself, and the farewell transfers moral vocabulary rather than a fixed personality template. The mature sibling relationship becomes possible through separation: influence remains, but imitation loses jurisdiction.

## Leadership as distributed and correctable

The V11 boundary already established that Horikita increasingly maps, trains, delegates, and coordinates capacities she does not personally possess. V11.5 adds several constitutional refinements:

- she accepts that a leader can be supported when right and challenged when wrong;
- Ayanokōji transfers the future Kushida decision to her even though his preferred answer remains expulsion;
- she seeks an auditable comparison with Ayanokōji rather than merely receiving his hidden solution;
- and she can relate to other class leaders through respect that includes separation rather than permanent alliance.

This is why *First File*'s July `協調性 E` rating is useful rather than embarrassing to the later reading (`Y1FF-E012–E014`). It is an archival snapshot of a genuine early deficit. It is not a timeless essence. By the year endpoint, treating that E as the total Horikita would erase the developmental process the novels themselves depict.

## Kushida: jurisdiction without guaranteed doctrine

The final lock rejects the stronger claim that Horikita has already adopted an unconditional never-expel-Kushida principle. What V11.5 establishes is that Ayanokōji yields the problem to Horikita because he wants to see how she resolves it. Her future solution is not settled inside the Year 1 boundary. The analytically important fact is **jurisdiction**: she is allowed to generate an answer that may diverge from his efficient one.

## The class as a dynamic portfolio

*First File* retrospectively describes Horikita's class as containing explosive individual potential while still suffering from insufficient cohesion and aggregation (`Y1FF-E025`). That official summary strongly corroborates the second-pass Year 1 model: the class's problem is not low human capacity in the abstract. It is the political task of converting differentiated, changing, partly voluntary capacities into collective action.

The leader's mature task is therefore not perfect measurement. It is:

1. make local knowledge better;
2. remain aware that the knowledge is incomplete;
3. develop and coordinate heterogeneous capacity;
4. allow domain experts and dissenters to correct her;
5. take public responsibility for consequential judgments;
6. and avoid treating knowledge of another person's strength as ownership of its use.

## Final contradiction

> **Horikita becomes more independent not by becoming uninfluenced, but by integrating influence into judgments she can own, defend, revise, and sometimes direct against the expectations of the people who shaped her.**

Her unresolved Year 2-facing problem is constitutional: can that independence survive when part of the class's causal architecture still depends on Ayanokōji's hidden knowledge and intervention?

# 堀北鈴音 / Horikita Suzune

## Current state through Volume 11

Volume 11 is the strongest evidence yet that Horikita’s leadership is becoming **distributed developmental pluralism** rather than individual excellence projected onto a class. She maps capabilities she does not personally possess, delegates domain authority, recognizes limits on her jurisdiction, detects hidden influence, and learns fast enough to create meaningful new options under pressure.

## Volume 11 delta

- **Class-wide coordination:** she explicitly concludes that `全員が協力し合う必要` and builds the Event Selection portfolio around heterogeneous strengths (`Y1V11-E050–E053`).
- **Domain humility:** she relies on Sudō for athletic judgment instead of treating leadership as expertise in everything.
- **Autonomy boundary:** Kōenji’s `その才能を君のために使うかどうかを決めるのは私自身だ` forces her to distinguish recognizing talent from owning its use (`Y1V11-E062–E066`).
- **Imitation is dismantled:** Manabu identifies her deficit as `心の成長` and tells her development need not mean becoming either him or Ayanokōji (`Y1V11-E078–E080`).
- **She detects Ayanokōji:** after the hidden coordination she tells him he had read how she would act; she is increasingly difficult to steer invisibly (`Y1V11-E085–E087`).
- **Chess becomes a generative test:** she learns rapidly and creates a position valuable enough that Ayanokōji delays taking control to see what she can produce (`Y1V11-E137–E139`).

## Current contradiction

> Horikita is becoming independently capable precisely while Ayanokōji consciously decides to make her development his personal project. The next test is whether her independence yields choices he cannot assimilate into his intended curriculum.

# 堀北鈴音 / Horikita Suzune
## Current state through Volume 10

Volume 10 marks Horikita’s clearest transition so far from **high-performing solver** to **accountable political leader**. Her central achievement is not discovering an objectively correct sacrificial target; it is publicly owning a reasoned judgment inside an institution that otherwise permits anonymous bloc warfare.

## Changes since Y1V09

- **She stops treating Manabu as the specification she must reproduce.** Manabu explicitly separates his ability from hers and later tells her that she need not catch Ayanokōji; she should grow as herself (`Y1V10-E010`, `E047`).
- **She initiates the decisive sibling conversation.** Ayanokōji encourages the opportunity but preserves the requirement that she approach Manabu herself (`E011–E012`).
- **She asks for `勇気`, not a tactical answer.** The problem she recognizes is one of public responsibility and fear, not missing information alone (`E045`).
- **She rejects anonymous coalition politics as an adequate examination.** Her `こんなもの、試験とは呼べない` objection identifies that bloc protection can expel students independent of substantive contribution (`E050`).
- **She publicly names Yamauchi and explains her criteria.** Academics, physical capacity, discipline, growth, contribution, and political betrayal all enter her retention judgment (`E051–E053`).
- **She does not exempt Kushida’s popular legitimacy from responsibility.** Kushida’s social influence makes participation in the Yamauchi coalition more consequential (`E055`).
- **She forces Sudō’s friendship objection into substitution form.** Saving Yamauchi means exposing someone else, and Sudō is made to confront that cost (`E058–E059`).
- **Her decision remains morally contestable.** Chabashira’s developmental-uncertainty observation and Sudō’s own trajectory prevent “least current utility” from becoming certainty about future human worth (`E006–E007`).
- **Ayanokōji’s hidden contribution remains substantial but bounded.** He routes the conspiracy information toward her and later treats her action as part of his own strategy, but the text does not show him writing her public speech or her governing principle (`E049`, `E089`).

## Current leadership thesis

> Horikita’s leadership becomes legitimate not because she can prove who deserves to disappear, but because she makes her criteria visible, accepts that others may judge her in return, and begins to produce a political self that is neither Manabu’s copy nor Ayanokōji’s public mask.

## Strongest unresolved question

Can Horikita preserve visible accountability while continuing to benefit from an invisible information architecture she does not control?

# 堀北鈴音 / Horikita Suzune
## Current state through Volume 9

Horikita’s Volume 9 role is quieter than in Volumes 5–8, but it provides meaningful evidence of maturing inter-class leadership. She wants to assist Ichinose, accepts that an allied rival retains a legitimate private sphere, and treats Ayanokōji’s strategic coldness as input rather than command.

## Changes since Y1V08

- **She accepts another leader’s information boundary.** Ichinose refuses to confirm which rumors are true because the classes remain future rivals, and Horikita does not force disclosure (`Y1V09-E033–E034`).
- **She distinguishes alliance from ownership.** Her concern for Ichinose does not become a claim to the whole truth.
- **She receives Ayanokōji’s strategic case for nonintervention without surrendering judgment.** The decision whether Class C becomes involved remains hers (`E036`).
- **Her Kushida project continues in the background.** She reports slow relational movement while Ayanokōji privately preserves an expulsion contingency (`E049`, `E083`). This enlarges the gap between visible leader policy and hidden sovereign insurance.

## Current contradiction

> Horikita is becoming better at respecting another polity’s autonomous boundary while remaining unaware that the person she most relies upon for hidden security is independently preparing an answer to her Kushida experiment.

# 堀北鈴音 / Horikita Suzune
## Current state through Volume 8

Horikita accepts formal leadership on the women’s side while openly compensating for her continued social limitations. Her most important development is not an exam result but her decision to pursue trust with Kushida even when the asymmetry of secret knowledge makes that trust presently impossible.

## Changes since Y1V07.5

- **She accepts visible responsibility.** Hirata nominates her and she takes the female leader role rather than remaining a detached analyst (`Y1V08-E028`).
- **She recognizes complementary ability.** Horikita acknowledges that her accessibility lags behind Hirata's and recruits Kushida as the interface she cannot yet be (`E028–E029`).
- **She distinguishes trust from mere cooperation.** Her request is not simply that Kushida stop sabotaging the class; she asks to become someone Kushida can trust (`E114–E115`).
- **Kushida exposes the unresolved asymmetry.** Knowledge of the hidden self cannot be returned or neutralized by a promise; it remains a knife at Kushida's neck (`E116–E117`).
- **Horikita chooses the project independently.** She continues despite Kushida's rejection and despite the possibility that Ayanokōji will not support the decision (`E118–E119`).

## Current contradiction

> Horikita's refusal to discard Kushida is a genuine act of self-authored leadership, but autonomy of judgment does not yet establish prudence, legitimacy, or the consent of classmates who bear the risk.

# 堀北鈴音 / Horikita Suzune
## Current state through Volume 7.5

Horikita remains outside the Christmas volume’s central romance but produces one of its clearest acts of self-authorship. She refuses to join the student council even after Manabu directly confirms his approval and Ayanokōji argues for the political value of the role.

## Changes since Y1V07

- **She actively pursues Kushida rather than merely responding to attack.** Horikita invites her to a public café and continues trying to create a workable relation under unresolved hostility (`Y1V07.5-E44`).
- **Her transparency principle remains risky.** She insists class-related matters should not be hidden from Kushida, even though Kushida’s private hostility remains unresolved (`Y1V07.5-E44–E45`).
- **She refuses inherited validation.** Manabu’s approval no longer settles the decision; she judges the council against her own goals and declines (`Y1V07.5-E45–E46`).
- **Ayanokōji still imagines her as future visible leader.** His class architecture places Horikita in front with Hirata and Kei as supporting structures (`Y1V07.5-E84`).
- **The distinction between independence and correct judgment becomes important.** Her refusal may be strategically mistaken, but it is hers.

## Current contradiction

> Horikita is becoming capable of choosing against both Manabu and Ayanokōji, while still pursuing an integration project with Kushida whose risk is borne by the wider class.

## Current comparative identifiers

- **Relation to power:** emerging visible leadership grounded in judgment rather than status alone.
- **Relation to autonomy:** strongest evidence yet of decision independent from brother and hidden strategist.
- **Relation to truth:** prefers inclusion and open class discussion; may underestimate the danger of hostile access.
- **Relation to relationships:** treats Kushida as a person to integrate rather than remove.
- **Principal virtue:** willingness to assume responsibility for a difficult path rather than inherit an easier answer.
- **Principal failure mode:** may universalize her personal rehabilitation project without class consent.
- **Strongest unresolved question:** can she accept strategic office later without turning the decision into delayed compliance with Ayanokōji’s plan?
