## Authority and quotation contract

1. The Japanese primary-source wording and deterministic locator govern over the working English translation.
2. Working translations are retrieval glosses. Where Japanese permits multiple emphases, the range remains open until a claim-specific translation is verified in context.
3. A `Y1_12` route means that the lexical locator falls inside a canonical scene-evidence record; it does **not** mean the anchor alone exhausts that record's claim.
4. Multiple evidence IDs are retained when scene-evidence ranges overlap the same lexical locator. The audit does not collapse them merely to manufacture one-to-one correspondence.
5. A source-local lexical-only route means the anchor remains canonically useful for language retrieval but is not promoted into an independent evidentiary proposition.
6. `Y1FF` remains question-relative: `FF-DATA`, `FF-RATING`, `FF-EDITORIAL`, and `FF-FICTION` are not interchangeable authorities. `もしもOAAがあったら` remains explicitly retrospective/counterfactual.
7. Exact speaker or POV attribution is only asserted where the existing locked context supports it. Otherwise the routing table deliberately says `source-local speaker/POV` and sends retrieval back to the canonical volume artifact.
8. Direct quotation beyond the short anchor remains a primary-source verification operation. This file is an index, not a quotation anthology.
9. Later Year 2/3 uses of the same word may develop or revise its significance but may not silently rewrite this Year 1 lexical state.
10. Full-synthesis prose should normally route **claim → specialist topical home → `Y1_12` evidence ID → `JY1-###` lexical anchor when exact Japanese matters → primary source locator**.
