# Year 3 Volume 1 — Evidence and locator ledger

This ledger was established after the complete sequential primary read, chapter mapping and targeted checks, before drafting the final deep reading. It contains concise analytical descriptions rather than redistributed source passages. Every range is inclusive and confined to one source spine.

`LN-TEXT` identifies original Japanese prose. `TF` is textual fact; `CI` is character interior or self-report; `IR` is institutional rule; `UA` preserves ambiguity. A mixed row separates an observed utterance from the truth of what the speaker claims. Confidence A means clear local evidence for the narrowly stated proposition, not certainty about a speaker’s entire model. Interpretive conclusions and value judgments are argued separately in the deep reading. `LN-ILL/VF` records appear in the visual audit; frozen Years 1–2 are `PRIOR`. No `LATER` evidence has been used.

The named development labels concern the stated proposition. For example, Nanase’s phone scene reveals an already-operative arrangement; it does not date that arrangement’s creation. A later correction of an earlier reader inference does not change the event itself.
