The strongest visual sequence contrasts occupied and unoccupied social space: the anticipated neighboring seat, the public café, Horikita’s isolated bench, and the frontispiece’s joined hands. The two forms of force are also concrete: Ibuki’s uninvited kick and Kitō’s chosen challenge do not share identical consent conditions, even though both are rendered dynamically. Ryūen’s wall image and Kitō’s kneeling image preserve different relations between defeat and continued will.

Images corroborate embodiment and framing; prose supplies internal purpose and exact knowledge. Combining them requires their actual insertion position. In particular, an image seen early during an analyst’s inspection is not therefore early in the book. This audit uses the OPF/source positions to avoid that error.
