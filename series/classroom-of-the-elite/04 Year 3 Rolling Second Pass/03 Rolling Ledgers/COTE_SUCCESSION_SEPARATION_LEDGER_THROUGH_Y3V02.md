All twelve IDs preserve the predecessor's history. None is renumbered, and no concentrated function is falsely reported as newly withdrawn. The contrast between A and C remains instructive: A coordinates after an actual departure; C displays initiative while relying on an imported exceptional center. V2 adds evidence of ordinary policy reproduction in C without yet proving that it can constrain or replace that center.

Role-surviving relationships also acquire more evidence. Haruka's contact does not restore the former group, Kei's support does not restore the romance, and Hiyori's cross-class repair does not erase political conflict. Each can produce a new relation with a history rather than merely returning to an old role. The outstanding tests remain duration, independent purpose, permission to refuse, actual costs and who supplies a missing function when the central person cannot.
