## Boundary synthesis

Three cross-cutting changes matter for current retrieval. First, the transfer redistributes information unevenly: Matsushita is exposed, Kei chooses a particular confidante, and the final C-class trio hears something the leader is not shown knowing. Second, role survival is concrete in Miyake’s friendship and the Kei–Horikita bond. Third, the new C-class students are not created by Ayanokōji’s arrival: the narrative newly exposes their judgment, habits and motives. Some events are development; some are reader-level revelation of people already present.

The inherited categories of autonomy remain directional. Kei’s peer agency increases without making the ended romance retrospectively informed. Kushida can own a continuing cooperation while facing a new blackmail threat. Hashimoto can successfully recruit competence while becoming less able to question its use. Each transition requires its own axis and source boundary.
