## Choice under authored necessity

The alliance supplies a concrete ten-dimension consent test. Information excludes deliberate exposure; alternatives narrow through loss; exit from compulsory school competition is constrained; material scarcity raises costs; institutional expulsion risk concentrates stakes; shame and dependence create emotional pressure; Kiyotaka compounds school-made scarcity; time converts postponement into urgency; post-choice C conditions persist alongside command transfer; revision fails to make Yamamura's objection consequential. Benefit, development and legitimacy require separate judgments.
