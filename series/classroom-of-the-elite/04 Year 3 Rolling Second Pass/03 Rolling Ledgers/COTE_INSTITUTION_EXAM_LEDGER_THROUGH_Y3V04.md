## Token exam mechanics

Three nights/four days place ten mixed groups into individual, class-team and whole-group tasks. School-adjusted bottle allocation and the smaller group do not establish random equality. Majority finish fixes arrival rank; multipliers are 100/95/90/85/80/75 percent, nonfinish 70, rounded up. Finish private rewards are 100,000/80,000/50,000/30,000/20,000/10,000. Token-group leader awards are +100/+50/+20, ranks four through nine zero, lowest −50; two leaders each receive the relevant award. [E002](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e002), [E004](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e004).

One representative per class receives all team/group earnings, with discretionary allocation. Each award produces a front entitlement using floor division among participants, and those rights accumulate; ordinary gifts permanently become back tokens. Both types count for total holdings, but the individual front winner earns the class +100 and self 500,000, divided among ties; each front token also yields 1,000 private points. Phone/location use costs tokens, and ordinary transfer requires both PINs and watch contact. [E006](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e006), [E015](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e015)–[E018](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e018).

Final lowest is expelled with June 1 OAA then earlier months resolving ties. First zero during play is separate, with protection allowing withdrawal. Nonfinish costs five own-class points; after group majority finishes, each delayed member costs one own-class point per thirty minutes. Adult-area absence costs a token per hour before the final race waiver. Acute retirement fixes holdings but permits remote incoming aid and requires all other group members to finish. [E005](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e005)–[E007](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e007), [E056](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e056), [E063](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e063).

At 17:00 the final F7-to-F13 race ends at 19:00; thirty seconds in the goal adds three tokens and freezes all further transfers. This makes later accurate-state information insufficient once an unseen transfer or finish changes the available rescue routes. The authentic fifty shown by Ibuki and deceptive public fifty-one floor must remain separate from the unprinted actual broadcast threshold. [E056](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e056), [E064](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e064), [E069](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e069)–[E076](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e076).
