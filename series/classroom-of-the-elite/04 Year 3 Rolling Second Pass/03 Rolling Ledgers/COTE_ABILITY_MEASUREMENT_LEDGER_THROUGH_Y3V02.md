## Measurement and expression

The displayed OAA components are 96/81/66/77, total 80; the recalled earlier components are 51/60/37/60, total 51. Kiyotaka explicitly distinguishes making an existing capacity visible from acquiring capacity, as Sudō has done. These figures supersede no earlier dated score. They are interface observations, not complete ceilings or a personality ranking. E013.
