## What the exam can prove

The raw table is independently legible evidence of an unusual academic performance. The revised mandate additionally depends on how classmates interpret an enemy’s extraordinary caution. That political judgment is understandable but cannot be treated as the same kind of proof as the marked test. Maximal caution can show the rival’s model, while the rival’s model might itself be mistaken.

Prediction also requires the correct object. Ayanokōji need not guess five arbitrary names when he can identify an opponent’s incentive to retreat and insure against stronger candidates. The source explicitly denies perfect reading of a genuinely random choice. Hypergeometric baselines belong to random selection, not a validated probability of this strategic encounter. The [source audit](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V01_SOURCE_AND_RECONCILIATION_AUDIT.md) records the exactly/at-least distinction.

## Generativity and current uncertainty

C’s established study culture, Katsuragi’s teaching, Hirata’s organization, Ichinose’s intelligence and Kei’s ability to help Horikita show capacity distributed through people. The degree to which that capacity can reproduce exceptional strategy or constrain its leader remains open. Publicly recognizing competence is one developmental event; giving another person a power to correct its use is another.

The inherited Year 2 rule against total OAA ranking is strengthened, not reversed. New results update expression, task conversion and observation. They do not authorize invented ceilings, refreshed absolute class-point totals or a global ranking of human value.
