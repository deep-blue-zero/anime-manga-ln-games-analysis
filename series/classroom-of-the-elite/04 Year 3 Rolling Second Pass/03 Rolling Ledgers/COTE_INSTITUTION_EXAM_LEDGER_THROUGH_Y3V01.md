## Y3V01-EX01: comprehensive academic test

Original title: `全体、少数戦学力総合テスト`. The full rule block is `COTE:Y3V01:spine16:para0067–0098` (`E022`). The observed exam occurs after approximately two weeks of preparation during the first term. Pairings are Horikita A versus Ichinose D, and Ryūen B versus the Sakayanagi-origin C led by Ayanokōji. Ayanokōji’s inference about why the school chose these pairings is not part of the formal rule.

## Competing strategies and public proof

Ryūen’s one-win defense saves the perfect-loss penalty and preserves funds, but fails his further aim of postponing Ayanokōji’s recognition. Ayanokōji enters to make maximal targeting visible, uses strong ordinary representatives and marks academic threats as insurance. Random-name prediction is neither necessary nor possible in the absolute sense he rejects. The class revises its original criterion after seeing raw performance and the rival’s fear. Both the official loss and the strategic gain must remain in the record (`E026`, `E036`, `E040–042`).

Against A, Ichinose’s contribution is more than willingness to obey. She supplies information about who met with Hirata and about paid first-year spies. Ayanokōji interprets patterns using his older knowledge of the class. Their complementary work yields an observed alliance benefit. The promised rule for a direct future C–D meeting—yield to the lower-point partner—remains untested (`E011–012`, `E039`, `E042`, `E050`).

## Rule, implementation, enforcement and external power

The transfer demonstrates a functioning membership rule and record: twenty million private points finance the lawful move; OAA verifies the new roster. Chabashira’s prevention of mob blame is a concrete local counterweight. In contrast, Sakagami’s nonintervention during Ryūen–Tokitō conflict and the post-exam intrusion show that formal procedure does not exhaust the political environment (`E002–003`, `E036`, `E040`).

Nanase’s two devices conflict with the stated general one-phone rule. Her surveillance statement and address to Tsukishiro make a hidden channel materially visible. Authorization, jurisdiction and the unseen response remain unknown. This strengthens the inherited permeability problem without proving complete institutional capture or eliminating ordinary educational plurality (`E057`).

Current formal office is not a new constitution: Horikita’s competent council work shows functioning and recovery, but no fresh allocation rule, appeal process or independent audit authority is enacted here. The frozen earlier institutional records remain the history; the first-year cohort of Second List is not relabeled as Ayanokōji’s former Year 1. No refreshed absolute class-point table is manufactured from differing dates.
