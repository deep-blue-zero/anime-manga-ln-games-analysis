# Horikita Suzune / 堀北鈴音 — Through Year 3 Volume 1

**PROVISIONAL SUPERSESSION NOTICE — This artifact records the state of evidence through Year 3 Volume 1. A later Year 3 snapshot may supersede it for current-state reference. It remains authoritative for what had been established at this source boundary.**

This is the current character model at `Y3V01`, carrying the [Frozen Year 2 primary home](../../03%20Year%202%20Definitive%20Second%20Pass/03%20Rolling%20Ledgers/COTE_CHAR_LEDGER_HORIKITA_THROUGH_Y2.md) as its historical baseline. It supersedes that earlier model **only for current-state reference**; the frozen Year 2 file remains authoritative at Y2SL. New facts below come from the complete Volume 1 read, with no later-volume evidence.


## Current model

Horikita remains leader of `POLITY-HORIKITA`, Class A, and student-council president. The class loses the first post-transfer exam 1–6 while she is significantly impaired by the loss of Ayanokōji’s presence and withheld explanation. She delegates because she doubts her ability to decide calmly; Hirata and peers actually prepare and conduct the class’s response. Afterward, Kei’s care and chosen disclosure help her regain the capacity to act. The next day she works competently with Nanase and invites her for tea (`E021–023`, `E037–038`, `E045–047`, `E056`).

The current formulation is **independent judgment with newly observed system stress and an initial peer-supported recovery**. It replaces “post-transfer entirely unobserved” with a particular result. It does not replace “independent judgment established” with “all competence was borrowed.” Her own harsh assessment is a grief-conditioned interior claim, evaluated against the preserved Year 2 decisions and this volume’s delegation.

## Motives and permissions

Her Class A purpose is collective and remembers sacrifice; the opening plate does not erase Yamauchi, Sakura or Maezono. She also wants consultation, recognition and continued proximity. The transfer injures the expected relation as well as class capacity. Her repeated wish to meet Ayanokōji and the café’s denied private alternative should be retained when interpreting the duration of her distress (`E001`, `E004–006`).

She can receive care, revise her view of Kei and return another person’s handclasp. Kei’s boundary about who ended the romance is not forced open. The larger history is then volunteered. Horikita’s knowledge of the earlier rooftop/rescue sequence expands through the subject’s own account after incomplete information from Ibuki. This is a later recipient-knowledge transition; it does not give earlier Horikita that knowledge or count as Ayanokōji’s voluntary disclosure (`E045–047`).

Romantic attraction is strongly suggested by her interrupted denial and remembered sensations. The source does not yet supply a settled love declaration. Her renewed purpose includes both graduating with classmates and making Ayanokōji face or regret leaving them. These motives can coexist now; their possible divergence remains a later test.

## Evidence against easy endpoints

Ibuki’s kick causes injury and leaves her distressed. Concern inferred from waiting does not make it harmless or the demonstrated restorative mechanism. Recovery is shown through a different encounter. At the other extreme, mutual tears and smiles are not proof of permanent recovery. No subsequent successful independent exam is present in this source.

Her council work supplies continuity of office competence. It does not establish a new constitution of contestable resource allocation or show that she knows Nanase’s surveillance role: she leaves before the overheard call. Her private thought that Nanase may lead better than Hōsen is tempered by awareness that intervention across cohorts is problematic (`E044–047`, `E056–057`).

## Surviving tests

`Y1H-012` stays partially answered; `Y2H-002` now has a first observed failure-and-recovery sequence, with long-term performance open. `Y1H-016` stays strictly open: she has not witnessed an unplanned failure of Ayanokōji’s capability or control. Future analysis must test peer coordination across more than one emotional recovery and determine whether her own class purpose can remain authoritative when Ayanokōji’s recognition points elsewhere.
