## Conduct test: announced, inferred and reported states

Announcement: test starts the next day; details follow a week later. Announced awards: +50/+20/0/−25 class points. Tie treatment and detailed scoring are initially withheld. Conduct is therefore part of the already-running test, not an ordinary preparation week. Kiyotaka triangulates teacher diction and withholds the A channel; Horikita independently reaches the conduct hypothesis. All four classes adopt it. E011–012, E025, E029–030.

## Council and records

The Kusanagi/Maki hearing shows a camera blind spot, disputed accounts, equal testimony and rejection of class-rank privilege. A possible third-party girl remains unidentified and unproved. Reasons are made relevant, but a fully contestable allocation/exit/correction structure is not established. Horikita's willingness to consider Nanase for succession is a preference, not appointment. E018–020.

Expanded cameras, score rules and office procedures create partial constraints; covert observation and arranged private access remain possible within them. Visibility, factual proof and independently enforceable accountability are different properties.
