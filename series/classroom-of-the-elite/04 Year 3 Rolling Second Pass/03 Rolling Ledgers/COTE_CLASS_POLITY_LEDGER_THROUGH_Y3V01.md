## The first observed separation result

For Horikita, Year 2’s “post-transfer unobserved” condition changes to “one lost exam with delegated preparation and a first peer-led recovery.” Neither the loss nor recovery alone can adjudicate an entire political system. A surviving capacity to deliberate and continue matters independently of the score. The absence of an Ayanokōji rescue does not mean absence of all influence: he advised the opponent and expected classmates to restore the leader (`E013`, `E023`, `E037–038`, `E045–047`, `E056`).

For C, a mandate is not identical to constitutionalization. Shimazaki’s initial criterion is not fulfilled; he explains why another signal has persuaded him. That is genuine constituency judgment, but the pressure of a governing vacuum and paid investment affects its freedom. Morishita’s later warning and Kitō’s condition retain nontrivial disagreement. The next test is whether that disagreement can change a costly policy after the leader’s strategic value is secure (`E009–013`, `E024`, `E040–042`, `E052–054`).

For B, restraint in spending and willingness to listen improve method while continuing coercion shows that governing ends remain concentrated. For D, the alliance produces measurable output through distinct contributors, while Ichinose’s encounter with Kei introduces a sharper boundary around chosen beneficiaries. Care and ability cannot be collapsed into a class letter or a single constitutional adjective (`E029`, `E033–036`, `E042`, `E050`).

## Class costs and obligations

No current expulsion occurs. Prior losses remain causally present: Horikita remembers the sacrifices, C preserves study culture after losing its sovereign, and Kitō judges the cost of being governed by someone’s private enjoyment. Ayanokōji’s protection rule toward Kitō has a concrete beneficiary, Sawada, but its unequal exception for powerful noncontributors prevents its immediate promotion into a universal constitution.

The C–D alliance is bounded by a future balance condition and chosen without a written contract. That arrangement offers flexibility and can pool complementary resources. It also lacks the same independently inspectable terms as Horikita’s earlier teacher-observed alliance. The current record preserves both the claimed benefit and the still-unexercised refusal condition (`E011–012`).

## Current transition

`DEVELOPMENT`: actual post-transfer outcomes and public C leadership. `REVELATION_OF_PRIOR_STATE`: C’s preserved culture and Kitō’s explanation of allegiance. `PROVISIONAL_TRAJECTORY`: differentiated peer capacity can survive a central departure, while exceptional replacements still risk concentration. Unfinished proof concerns repeated performance, ownership of purposes, safe refusal and reproduction without a hidden or imported guarantor.
