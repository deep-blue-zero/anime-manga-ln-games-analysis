## Candidate-reading corrections made during this transaction

The source audit rejects a set of possible misreadings: Ichinose’s claimed White Room disclosure is not true; the scoreboard lists raw marks with separate penalties; a rules-based loss is not a capability failure; rumor does not establish Shiraishi’s biography; Kitō’s distrust is not uniquely Sakayanagi loyalty; Nanase’s call does not disclose every origin or sponsor; an image’s inspection order is not its placement in the book. These corrections concern this analysis’s candidate inferences. They are not a claim that the frozen Year 2 corpus contained those Year 3 errors.

The prior strict questions retain their standards. `Y1H-004`, `016`, `026` and `035` remain open. New ordinary responsiveness does not cancel the absence of consequential informed disclosure; Ayanokōji’s designed score loss does not supply Horikita with a witnessed genuine failure. Hiyori is not shown changing Ryūen’s moral decision. Bounded assistance and tactical adjustment are not delegated protective authority.

The current interpretive trajectory is that public recognition advances faster than accountability while peer recovery begins to realize the separation test. This is `PROVISIONAL_TRAJECTORY`, not a completed Year 3 conclusion. A later source may change it only through a separately dated transition.
