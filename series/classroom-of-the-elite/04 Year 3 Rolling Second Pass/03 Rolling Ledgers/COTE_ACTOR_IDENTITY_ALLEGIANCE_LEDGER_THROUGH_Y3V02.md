# Actor identity and allegiance — Through Year 3 Volume 2

**PROVISIONAL BOUNDARY SNAPSHOT — through Y3V02.** This successor carries the complete unchanged history and findings of the [through-Y3V01 primary home](COTE_ACTOR_IDENTITY_ALLEGIANCE_LEDGER_THROUGH_Y3V01.md), including its links to frozen Year 2 evidence. It supersedes that home only for current lookup in this responsibility. The earlier artifact remains authoritative at its own boundary. This document states V2 additions and explicit limits; absence of a new event does not delete an inherited finding. E001–E066 below refer only to Y3V02 evidence.

## Roles, affiliations and knowledge remain separate

| Actor | Identity/history established here | Role or contact | Own aim / knowledge limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nanase Tsubasa | Hospital memory shows Eiichirō alive in that scene with prolonged impaired consciousness | Tsukishiro recruits her using care and a truth/lie persona; covert contact with Shiraishi | Original hostility toward Atsuomi differs from present prevention-of-harm aim; complete affiliation and training origin unresolved |
| Tsukishiro | Secures care and supplies recruitment instructions | Names Shirogane, directs construction of a persona | Known intermediary is not automatically the complete sponsor chain |
| Shiraishi Asuka | Ordinary classmate presentation now coexists with covert capability | Declares assigned observer role and ordinary three-year-student condition; two years of nonintervention | Declines Nanase's expulsion request; other schoolmates' exact roles remain outside her stated knowledge |
| Shirogane | Named in recruitment and conditional warning | Wishes invoked by Nanase | No direct complete instruction or independently established command structure |
| Ishigami | Admits arranging a private council encounter | Supplies Nanase lead under negotiated anonymity | Class-only account is addressed speech; full motive and sources remain unresolved |
| Nishikawa | Classmate with suspicion and reported ordinary habits | Shiraishi disclaims operative status | A participant's disclaimer is not independent proof of every aspect of his identity |

E001–003, E055–057, E060–062. The hospital disclosure is **REVELATION_OF_PRIOR_STATE**. It corrects an unqualified literal-death story without proving Eiichirō's present condition. Nanase's genuine attachment and instructed deception explain each other rather than requiring that either be wholly false.

Nanase requests early unpredictable expulsion as help for Kiyotaka and possible future victims. The future evil is her forecast. Her intermediate affiliation does not make her assignment identical to Shiraishi's; the latter's refusal shows a real divergence of immediate purpose. Shiraishi's knowledge of his location supplies operational evidence without identifying its channel. Her description of Arisu as an obstacle does not establish responsibility for Arisu's departure.

Keep reader knowledge separate from character access. Horikita does not know the hospital scene; the final prolepsis is not scene-time White Room disclosure. Shiraishi's rumor history remains unresolved, and a physical feat by an unnamed first-year girl supplies no shortcut to identifying an operative. E006–008, E051, E063.
