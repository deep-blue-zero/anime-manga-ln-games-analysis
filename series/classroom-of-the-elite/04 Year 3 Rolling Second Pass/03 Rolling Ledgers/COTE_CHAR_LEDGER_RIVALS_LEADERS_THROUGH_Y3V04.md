## Rival ends and the authority of followers

Ryūen recognizes Hiyori as Kiyotaka's particular concern and makes her survival a test. She contributes a no-financial-rescue condition, making the threat credible; Kaneda opposes it in moral language and confronts him physically. Ryūen preserves the end and requires force for reversal. Tactical daring, selective cooperation and unprotected dissent remain separate judgments. His final individual second place by two tokens is reported, not his guessed Kiyotaka count. [E013](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e013)–[E014](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e014), [E058](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e058)–[E063](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e063), [E073](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e073).

Katsuragi's hidden decoy protects Ibuki and is grounded in Yahiko's loss and his own transfer history. The performance nevertheless makes Ibuki believe her class has discarded her. He accepts another class's removal as the price of protecting his own. Earlier merit criteria were stated and actual distributions occurred; the later reveal changes abandonment intent without making the experience harmless. [E034](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e034)–[E036](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e036), [E047](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e047), [E074](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e074).

Ichinose's solidarity is explicitly bounded to her own class. She accepts a rival victim floor and holds Horikita to their pact while their group wins. This is effective adversarial cooperation rather than a collapse of public command. The final observer's wounded jealousy introduces a different instability: context and art strongly identify her, but prose leaves her unnamed and no harmful act follows. Strategic composure and personal emotional control should not be equated. [E011](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e011)–[E012](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e012), [E065](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e065)–[E066](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e066), [E073](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e073), [E087](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e087).

Hiyori's class responsibility helps Ryūen's end rather than morally redirecting it. Her relationship with Kiyotaka receives the major separate development: a costly rescue, explicit mutual affection and continued uncertainty about his durability. Kaneda's courage does not become a protected veto merely because his objection is powerful. Hirata's earlier broad aid coexists with spectator interest in another class's danger, and both observations belong to his ongoing history. [E057](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e057), [E059](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e059)–[E063](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e063), [E082](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e082)–[E086](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e086).

Kōenji gives Wang substantial but unquantified resources and constrains onward use through immediate finish. This is selective care with control, not obedience to Horikita or a demonstrated answer to paternal conditions. No new Arisu off-campus action is shown.
