## Five propositions that must stay separate

Origin, sponsor, assigned instruction, contribution to an event and personal aim are different claims. A named contact or exceptional performance does not fill the missing columns. The current source adds a concrete Nanase operational statement and a new Yuki recollection, while leaving several identities and purposes unresolved.

## What the ending actually upgrades

At Y2SL, Nanase’s deeper role was a hypothesis with incomplete patronage. At Y3V01, her own statement explicitly names continuing surveillance, an addressed Tsukishiro contact and concern about other actors. The presence of two phones is directly observed against a stated general one-phone rule. This substantially strengthens an operative-arrangement claim. It does not confirm the complete knight hypothesis, prove that her origin is White Room training, or establish every part of the Matsuo/Eiichirō account as either true or false.

The knowledge holders are specific: Morishita, Hashimoto and Shiraishi hear and see; Nanase discovers them; Horikita has already left. Ayanokōji is not shown receiving their report. The source’s own Nanase estimate about Ishigami remains `CI/UA`, not a fact supplied by an omniscient institution.

## What remains inherited

Yagami’s origin and removal, Shiba’s local operational role, Atsuomi’s proprietary project, Chairman Sakayanagi’s bounded protection and Kanzaki’s non-operational family connection remain as previously audited. None gains a fresh hidden biography here. The Volume 0 revelation ledger remains frozen; new childhood interest is a current response to history, not a rewrite of that history.

`Y1H-036` remains partially answered and `Y2H-012` receives a partial operational revelation. Missing sponsor, instruction and personal-purpose columns must remain visible in the next reading rather than being filled by genre expectation.
