## History, assignment and private purpose

Nanase's dossier supplies reported Ishigami history: early admiration of Atsuomi, junior-high contact through family connections, valued intelligence, desire for recognition and a monitoring assignment. Its source is the document she provides and her explanation, not an Ishigami monologue. Her claim that his thinking matches or exceeds White Room students is contested. Monitoring, origin, sponsor and current expulsion orders remain separate; no current order to expel Kiyotaka is shown. [E038](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e038)–[E040](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e040).

Nanase says Atsuomi's objective is regaining control, with expulsion only one possible means. Her own aim becomes changing Kiyotaka's heart and taking initiative over him. The unnamed stronger camp is not identified. Tsukishiro's indifference to an earlier expulsion attempt and Kiyotaka's expected willing return are attributed reports, partly compatible with Amasawa's expectation and Kiyotaka's university-cover interior; they are not a complete authenticated chain. [E026](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e026), [E040](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e040)–[E041](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e041).

Amasawa's White Room belonging includes an excluded home and a desired future beside Kiyotaka. She initially objects to changing his chosen course. The threatened overseas sale is Nanase's allegation, which Amasawa cannot verify; the conditional protection offer has not become accepted recruitment. Her request for time is granted. No completed sale, sponsor intervention or rescue is established. [E042](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e042).

Haruka's assistance to avert responsibility expulsion is newly revealed V3-related history with undisclosed content. Kushida separately refused Kiyotaka's VIP signal; refusal is not a confirmed spying agreement. Katsuragi's apparent abandonment of Ibuki is a concealed protective decoy, with selective consent to another class's loss. [E044](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e044), [E052](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e052), [E074](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e074).

Shiraishi's earlier observer history remains inherited; Nishikawa's concern does not reveal a new handler. The final female observer is unnamed in prose, strongly identified as Ichinose by context and illustration 18. She knows an indirect rescue report and sees the arrival, not the beach confession itself. No new Arisu off-campus action or complete external allegiance map is supplied.
