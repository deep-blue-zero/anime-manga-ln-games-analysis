# Rivals and leaders — Through Year 3 Volume 1

**PROVISIONAL SUPERSESSION NOTICE — This artifact records the state of evidence through Year 3 Volume 1. A later Year 3 snapshot may supersede it for current-state reference. It remains authoritative for what had been established at this source boundary.**

This is the current character model at `Y3V01`, carrying the [Frozen Year 2 primary home](../../03%20Year%202%20Definitive%20Second%20Pass/03%20Rolling%20Ledgers/COTE_CHAR_LEDGER_RIVALS_LEADERS_THROUGH_Y2.md) as its historical baseline. It supersedes that earlier model **only for current-state reference**; the frozen Year 2 file remains authoritative at Y2SL. New facts below come from the complete Volume 1 read, with no later-volume evidence.


## Ryūen Kakeru / 龍園翔

Ryūen remains the coercive leader of Class B. He has genuinely learned to consider retreat: he declines an expensive academic contest, secures one duel to avoid a sweep, and aims to damage the opponent’s mandate instead. This is developed strategic flexibility. The class’s preparation is nevertheless governed by fear and undisclosed selections. Tokitō objects to the use of collective labor; Katsuragi and Ibuki have strategic voice, while the final choice stays with Ryūen (`E029`, `E036`).

The intended secondary victory fails. Ayanokōji turns maximal targeting into a visible credential. Ryūen’s wall-punch and question about winning alone reveal a renewed limit in his self-sufficiency model (`E040–043`). The event is his strategic and psychological defeat, not proof that every past growth was false. Whether the question becomes shared authority is unobserved. Hiyori does not redirect his moral decision in this volume; `Y1H-026` remains open.

## Ichinose Honami / 一之瀬帆波

Ichinose converts social reach into strategic information, destabilizes Amasawa’s probe and helps her class win. The ally relation is productive in both directions. Her own intelligence work is a necessary part of the explanation; Ayanokōji’s advice alone is not the complete cause (`E019–020`, `E038–039`, `E042`, `E050`).

Her care also becomes explicitly selective. The narrator confirms her sincere preference to give without demanding return, then shows her retracting help for Kei and deliberately worsening Kei’s injury. A valid observation of sincerity does not validate her causal account of the breakup, and finite capacity does not require extra cruelty (`E033–035`). Preserve the Year 2 conclusion that solidarity can support adversarial competence. Add the sharper current question of which people she excludes and what she feels permitted to do to them.

She does not possess a disclosed White Room history. Her institutional-name claim is a bluff; her current knowledge is limited. Strategic opacity, attachment and incomplete information coexist. The alliance’s promised acceptance of a single C-class dissenter is not yet exercised (`E012`, `E020`).

## Sakayanagi Arisu / 坂柳有栖

No new off-campus conduct is shown. Her absence and the consequences of her departure remain active within the original polity. Kitō now explains his earlier allegiance as betting on Class A prospects rather than personal devotion, which revises a follower model without supplying Sakayanagi’s new interior (`E052`). Academic culture survives her exit; importing Ayanokōji fills the central function without proving the class can reproduce it. Keep individual self-authorship and fiduciary cost distinct; `Y2H-006` remains unanswered.

## Kōenji Rokusuke / 高円寺六助

His ordinary written-exam reliability informs Hirata’s selection, although he was not told the final choice. He produces 72 raw, 47 after penalty, and loses the duel. This is actual task participation, not his maximal ability or a demonstrated response to the paternal freedom condition. Ayanokōji also cites his occasional protection of classmates when rebuking Kitō; that comparison does not confer moral exemption on either man (`E037–038`, `E052`). `Y2H-007` stays unanswered.

## Nagumo Miyabi / 南雲雅

The inherited model of patronal mobility, later bounded rivalry and departure remains current historical knowledge. No new conduct or retrospective mission revelation is admitted. Horikita’s council work does not retroactively democratize Nagumo’s system, and no new allocation rule is shown in this source.

## Constraints shared across rivals

Changing a tactical method is not automatically changing a governing end. Performing care is not proof of indiscriminate benevolence. A score is not a scalar biography. The rivals remain meaningful because each combines capacities, attachments and constraints that the others do not exhaust; the first volume supplies outcomes, not their final-year resolution.
