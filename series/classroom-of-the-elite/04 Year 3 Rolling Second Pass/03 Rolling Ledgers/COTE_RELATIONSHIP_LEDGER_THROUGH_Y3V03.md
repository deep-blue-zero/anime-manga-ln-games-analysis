# Relationships and irreplaceability — Through Year 3 Volume 3

It replaces that home only for current lookup in this responsibility; the earlier artifact remains authoritative at its own boundary. V3 additions below do not delete inherited findings when no new event occurs. Evidence IDs E001–E070 refer only to V3.
