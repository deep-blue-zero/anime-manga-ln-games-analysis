# Ayanokōji Kiyotaka / 綾小路清隆 — Through Year 3 Volume 1

**PROVISIONAL SUPERSESSION NOTICE — This artifact records the state of evidence through Year 3 Volume 1. A later Year 3 snapshot may supersede it for current-state reference. It remains authoritative for what had been established at this source boundary.**

This is the current character model at `Y3V01`, carrying the [Frozen Year 2 primary home](../../03%20Year%202%20Definitive%20Second%20Pass/03%20Rolling%20Ledgers/COTE_CHAR_LEDGER_AYANOKOJI_THROUGH_Y2.md) as its historical baseline. It supersedes that earlier model **only for current-state reference**; the frozen Year 2 file remains authoritative at Y2SL. New facts below come from the complete Volume 1 read, with no later-volume evidence.


## Current model

Ayanokōji is now the accepted strategic leader of the Sakayanagi-origin polity, officially Class C. He is no longer a hidden guarantor inside Horikita’s A class. His 100 raw score, 100 penalties and deliberately accepted representative defeat produce a public mandate. The mechanism is expression and interpretation of existing exceptional capacity, not newly acquired academic ability. Classmates’ consent is real but qualified by unequal knowledge, paid investment and a changed recognition criterion (`E002`, `E009–013`, `E024`, `E038–042`).

| Layer | Current tendency | New evidence and limiting condition |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary | Curious, unevenly culturally informed, increasingly able to participate without knowing every convention | Song ignorance, social help and childhood interest; no inference of total emotional absence (`E007`, `E030–032`, `E048`, `E051`) |
| Relational | Can recognize a person’s contribution while reserving consequential information | Ichinose contributes independently; Horikita’s request for privacy is refused; Hirata’s is granted (`E006`, `E014`, `E042`, `E050`) |
| Strategic | Reads incentives and characteristic aims; permits robust insurance against a wrong guess | The representative names need not be read when their selection signals retreat; random outcomes remain unreadable (`E026`, `E041`) |
| Authorial | Arranges information, timing and alternatives so other agents’ motives help realize his aims | Transfer shock, sunk cost, surveillance bait and Kushida’s multiple response branches (`E013`, `E041`, `E049`) |
| Public sovereign | Makes capacity visible enough to be recognized by a constituency | Class debate and Kitō’s distrust survive; no established independent limit on the new leader’s ends (`E024`, `E040–042`, `E052–054`) |

The most important new tension is between an open display of strength and closed jurisdiction over purpose. He can tell classmates why an exam strategy worked without telling Horikita enough to choose the terms of separation or telling Ichinose his background. Public explanation should therefore increase the visibility state, not automatically the accountability state.

## Change since Y2SL

**Development:** actual transfer aftermath, public leadership, wider ordinary participation, reliance on Ichinose’s intelligence and a renewed interest in former White Room peers. **Revelation:** the later explanation supplies grounds for an earlier spy inference, rather than making the inference magically self-evident. **Relationship-specific expression:** Yamamura’s protected pace coexists with an explicitly instrumental developmental aim. **Continuity:** concealed purposes and the wish for outcomes beyond straightforward victory. **Unresolved:** whether someone’s informed refusal can change his end at substantial cost.

His curiosity about Yuki is neither a reunion nor a proof of permanently absent feeling. He remembers the name through a tentative association with Tsubaki, thanks her for prompting interest, then predicts that meeting would produce no new emotion. This prediction is his current self-model. It may constrain the next decision but cannot be promoted into an omniscient account of his possible future (`E048`, `E051`).

## Portable constraints

He should not be modeled as knowing what the final three listeners heard from Nanase, knowing Tsubaki’s full external connection, or having secured Kushida’s betrayal. None is shown. Likewise, his confidence that classmates will restore Horikita is not evidence that he composed Kei’s testimony or possesses the resulting private exchange.

The limits on fallibility remain specific. He lacks a cultural reference, benefits from Shiraishi’s timing, and adjusts when Kitō endures a blow. These observations reject total omniscience. They do not establish the strict vulnerability or Horikita-witnessed failure tests. `Y1H-004`, `016` and `035` remain open. Written self-analysis, understatement and humor are supported; an invented vocal performance is not.
