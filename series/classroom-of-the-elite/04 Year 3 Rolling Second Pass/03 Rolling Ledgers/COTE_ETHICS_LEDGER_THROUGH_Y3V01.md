## Current normative state

The inherited distinctions remain governing: explanation is not justification; effective development is not consent to its method; autonomy is compatible with support; protection does not confer ownership; a lawful result does not exhaust legitimacy. This volume strengthens the need to distinguish **having alternatives**, **understanding them**, and **being able to revise the stronger actor’s end**. ## Responsibility for environments

Formal refusal remains meaningful in this book. Shiraishi gets Yoshida to retract a demand; classmates withhold welcome; Kitō does not have to pretend trust. Calling every response puppetry would remove evidence of real agency. It would also weaken criticism by making responsibility depend on literal control of another mind.

The sharper criticism concerns arrangement of consequences. Ayanokōji can leave another person free to choose while making the available branches costly, partly undisclosed or useful to his own purposes. He is responsible for those contributions even when the eventual answer is the other person’s. Conversely, anticipating that peers will recover Horikita does not make him the sole author of their care. Moral evaluation must keep both causal responsibility and limits on authorship.

## Reciprocity is not a demand for symmetry in every resource

Kei and Horikita are not equally knowledgeable at the start of their exchange. Their care is still comparatively reciprocal because information is offered by its subject, a withheld detail does not incur a newly imposed penalty, and each person lets the other’s account alter her view. Ayanokōji and Ichinose also contribute different resources, which is a genuine functional reciprocity. That cooperative result does not settle whether either can revise his developmental end at real cost.

Irreplaceability is a separate question again. Kei’s description of Ayanokōji as indispensable evidences attachment, not his mutual obligation or the impossibility of a meaningful future without him. No person’s usefulness or exam mark fixes moral worth. No present expulsion occurs, and later recoveries cannot cleanse the earlier sacrifices remembered in the opening.

## Current proof boundary

The positive development is actual peer care and some effective local correction. The negative continuity is unequal jurisdiction over timing, secrets, force and purpose. The unresolved test is a consequentially informed refusal that the stronger actor accepts even when it changes his own preferred end. `Y1H-004` and `035` remain strictly open; the deliberate score loss does not supply protective vulnerability.
