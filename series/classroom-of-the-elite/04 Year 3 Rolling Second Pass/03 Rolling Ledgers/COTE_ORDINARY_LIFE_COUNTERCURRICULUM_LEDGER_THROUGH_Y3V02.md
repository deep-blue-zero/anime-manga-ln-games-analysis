# Ordinary life and counter-curriculum — Through Year 3 Volume 2

**PROVISIONAL BOUNDARY SNAPSHOT — through Y3V02.** This successor carries the complete unchanged history and findings of the [through-Y3V01 primary home](COTE_ORDINARY_LIFE_COUNTERCURRICULUM_LEDGER_THROUGH_Y3V01.md), including its links to frozen Year 2 evidence. This document states V2 additions and explicit limits; absence of a new event does not delete an inherited finding. E001–E066 below refer only to Y3V02 evidence.

## Emergent experience and its possible recapture

| Encounter | Purpose not reducible to class victory | Kiyotaka's participation / capture risk |
|---|---|---|
| Book shopping | Shimazaki's university project; Yoshida's balanced ambitions | Helps select a book and feels pleasure, then closes a functional classmate assessment |
| Rain conversation | A boy discusses love without demanding mastery | Voluntarily admits lack/envy; no protective jurisdiction is delegated |
| Horikita's dinner | Familiar conflict restores a livable peer world | An informational purpose coexists with hospitality; Kiyotaka need not author every benefit |
| Shared library time | Hiyori reads and maintains other friendships | Unchosen happiness and particularity develop, later described as experiment |
| Single flower | Hiyori values one beautiful object and accepts a gift | He wants joy and memory; quantity is not the measure of relational value |
| Haruka/Airi contact | Airi's outside work and a possible repaired friendship | Surprise and interest coexist with cultivation of intelligence |
| Gym proposal | Watanabe's romance, ordinary exercise and possible reformer reintegration | A requested cover becomes a broader political environment |

E004–010, E022, E037–038, E043–054, E065. These events show why ordinary life is a counter-curriculum only conditionally. It generates values that a design did not fully specify; a participant can subsequently make those values instrumental. Neither capture risk nor later strategy proves the initial experience unreal.

The tea bottle and poppy offer a recurring distinction between quantity and fitting value. Shimazaki's black coffee separates instrumental consumption from taste. Hiyori's flower choice provides a particular preference observation, not a general ranking of flowers. Morishita's expensive beverage comedy likewise requires context. Preferences should be recorded with motive, occasion and confidence, especially for the planned monograph layer. E007–008, E046, E066.

The most substantial new reciprocal development is affective: Hiyori changes the felt value of time and the category in which Kiyotaka places her. Yoshida helps articulate a lack without coercive interrogation; Hashimoto helps him name a possible feeling. None yet supplies the stronger test of informed correction of his governing ends. The counter-curriculum is demonstrably productive while its ownership remains contested.
