## Independent action under emotional and structural pressure

Hirata's challenge makes Horikita examine her image of Kiyotaka as invincible and her reluctance to credit Ryūen. She can acknowledge those feelings while judging the ambush well and refusing complacency. The later revelation of deliberate exposure means this apparent loss does not close Y1H-016. V3 also reports that she paid for the previous conduct bargain, adding history beyond V2's agreement without changing that earlier boundary. [E025](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e025), [E065](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e065).

Horikita retains her own blackout, coordinates multiple attacks and accepts Kushida's structural advice. Protective formations work but expose VIP identities to Kiyotaka's comparative observation. She diagnoses his time trap too late, anticipates the futile morning chase, cannot stop 16 resentful guards and does keep Sudō back. Her competence, partial command failure, class resentment and eventual first wipe all remain visible. None implies he supplied every local tactic. [E049](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e049)–[E059](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e059).

Kei's friendship allows intimate questions. Horikita instantly denies romance with Sudō while hesitating about Kiyotaka and cannot yet name why. An unfinished thought or delayed denial is not an explicit love confession. Her practical dependence, grief, attraction possibilities and governing judgment require separate fields. [E048](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e048), [E051](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e051).
