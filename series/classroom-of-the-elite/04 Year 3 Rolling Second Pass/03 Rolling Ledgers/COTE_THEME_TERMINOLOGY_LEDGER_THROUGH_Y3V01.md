## Load-bearing semantic distinctions

`見返り` is contested return/reciprocation in an intimate relation, not automatically selfish payment. `自由` must be read beside the threatened cost of refusal. `対等` describes a local alliance aspiration; its practical test is an exercised capacity to disagree. `敗北` can name an official score outcome, an unwanted strategic reversal or a crisis of self-understanding, and this book gives different actors different combinations.

`コンコルド効果` explicitly identifies sunk-cost pressure. `生きる敗戦` names a loss that can serve an aim. `道具` marks instrumental purpose without proving that no protective act or feeling exists. `幼馴染` is a tentative ordinary analogy for a former White Room peer. `監視` is part of Nanase’s own operational statement, while the purpose of that surveillance remains a separate proposition.

## Motifs as retrieval aids

Seats and benches organize expected presence, isolation and shared space. Hands can protect, join, expose, intimidate or accept a provisional compact. The remaining year makes time an object of both ordinary attachment and strategic pressure. Names and role labels can individualize someone or narrow them to a function. The koi joke and the childhood-name association must not be converted into romance or kinship proof merely because they are suggestive.

These motifs support interpretation (`IT/SI`) when tied to actual scenes. They are not substitute evidence for motives or future outcomes. Current state is an opening trajectory, never a completed-year theme verdict.
