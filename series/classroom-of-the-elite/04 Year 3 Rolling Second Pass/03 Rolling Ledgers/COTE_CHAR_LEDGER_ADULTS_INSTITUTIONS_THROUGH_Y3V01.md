## Institutional interpretation

The school can make a lawful transfer legible and prevent mob blame while remaining permeable to secret surveillance. These are simultaneous observations. The two-phone case supports an operational exception or breach of the general rule; its authorization is unknown. It neither establishes that all school life is externally scripted nor demonstrates that ordinary council friendliness guarantees safety.
