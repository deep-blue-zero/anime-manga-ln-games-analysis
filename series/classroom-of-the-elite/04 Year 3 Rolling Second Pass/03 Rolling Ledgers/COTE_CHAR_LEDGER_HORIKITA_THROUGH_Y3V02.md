# Horikita Suzune / 堀北鈴音 — Through Year 3 Volume 2

**PROVISIONAL BOUNDARY SNAPSHOT — through Y3V02.** This successor carries the complete unchanged history and findings of the [through-Y3V01 primary home](COTE_CHAR_LEDGER_HORIKITA_THROUGH_Y3V01.md), including its links to frozen Year 2 evidence. This document states V2 additions and explicit limits; absence of a new event does not delete an inherited finding. E001–E066 below refer only to Y3V02 evidence.

## Independence after separation: additional observed performance

V1's injury, delegation and recovery remain historical facts. V2 adds residual fatigue, candid reassessment of Kei, restored peer contact, independent exam inference and executed collective conduct. Her public claim to be fine is qualified by her private admission. A helpful kick remains objectionable in method; gratitude does not erase the risk. E016, E021–027, E065.

Kōenji defeats her initial appeal to ordinary membership, then sells compliance for 500,000 private points. Her unspoken 300,000 limit is not the agreement. The price purchases one week's conduct even if the deduction is wrong or the class loses elsewhere. She can negotiate and fund a solution while still governing a polity whose duties are unequal. Sudō's objection and subsequent support are independent contributions, not evidence that the contradiction disappears. The actual result is A tied for second at +10, total 1,240. E017, E026–027, E052.

## Council and informational boundaries

Her hearing rejects A-status privilege and seeks testimony from both injured boys. She will not dispose of a possible assault simply because an easy settlement satisfies the immediate participants; future victims enter her purpose. A possible third-party female attacker remains a hypothesis, not a solved case. Nanase's potential succession is considered irrespective of class rank, not enacted. E018–020.

Ishigami authors the circumstances of their private meeting and supplies the Nanase lead. Horikita retains a serious-report duty, rejects the prospect of a binding wager/contract and asks for evidence before changing belief. Her decision to request his help is real participation inside an environment partly designed by him. Scene-time ignorance remains distinct from the closing retrospective voice. E060–063.

## Portrait implications

Her speech varies from public reassurance to candid private admission, procedural questioning, bargaining and friction among friends. Recovery is supported by familiar interaction rather than an abrupt disappearance of grief. Curiosity about Kiyotaka's history is growing; it has not delivered that history. Independence here means originating judgments, negotiating terms and accepting non-identical allies. Y1H-016 remains open: she has not witnessed a genuine personal failure of Kiyotaka merely because his class loses points through illness.
