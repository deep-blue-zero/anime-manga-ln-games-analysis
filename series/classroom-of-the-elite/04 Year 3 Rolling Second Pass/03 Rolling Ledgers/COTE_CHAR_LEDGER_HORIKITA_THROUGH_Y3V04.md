# Horikita Suzune — Through Year 3 Volume 4

**PROVISIONAL BOUNDARY SNAPSHOT — through Y3V04.** The complete unchanged history is retained in the [through-V3 primary home](COTE_CHAR_LEDGER_HORIKITA_THROUGH_Y3V03.md) and its predecessor chain to frozen Year 2. This successor becomes the current lookup for this responsibility; prior snapshots remain authoritative at their own boundaries. The additions below neither delete inherited findings nor backdate newly revealed information. Evidence IDs here refer to V4.

## Independent competence with an actual protection failure

Horikita welcomes Ichinose's reliability yet challenges compulsory pooling where it would suppress A members' individual-prize ambitions. She identifies a real difference in constituencies instead of assuming D's consensus applies to her own class. Their own-class protection pact later constrains opposition to a known rival victim. This is both active negotiation and a limitation created by her accepted commitment. [E011](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e011)–[E012](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e012), [E065](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e065)–[E066](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e066).

She infers a decoy because Katsuragi's apparent willingness to discard Ibuki and Ryūen's apparent nonresistance do not fit her knowledge. Her instruction to inspect Ibuki's watch succeeds at verification: fifty is the real holding at that moment. The subsequent secret route through Kushida defeats the protective inference, not the accuracy of the inspection. Kiyotaka credits her reasoning while controlling more information and timing. [E069](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e069), [E074](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e074)–[E077](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e077).

G8's first finish and token rank give A and D one hundred class points each. A also earns an individual award, but its winner is not named. Horikita loses Shinohara despite these successes and recognizes the remaining work with Ike and her class. Net totals and all hypothetical rescue alternatives remain unproved. Finishing quickly helps the group while reducing her remaining means of intervention; the text does not establish a mathematical impossibility of every alternative. [E073](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e073), [E077](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e077), [E080](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e080).

The accepted punch is not evidence of Horikita witnessing uncontrolled Kiyotaka failure. Hiyori's rescue is a real personal cost, but Horikita's later knowledge is not the exact witnessed-failure criterion. The inherited stricter question remains open. No new council appointment, adjudication or Manabu event occurs, and the V2 council findings remain in force without invented development.

Kushida's refusal to supply the prior VIP signal is newly reported; Kiyotaka interprets changed regard for Horikita. That supports a developing allegiance without proving Horikita authored every Kushida decision or obtained consent to all earlier preservation costs. Her independent class work and her inability to protect everyone must remain in the same portrait. [E052](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e052).
