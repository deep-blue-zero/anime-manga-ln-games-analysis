# Actor identity and allegiance — Through Year 3 Volume 3

**PROVISIONAL BOUNDARY SNAPSHOT — through Y3V03.** This successor carries the complete unchanged history and findings of the [through-Y3V02 primary home](COTE_ACTOR_IDENTITY_ALLEGIANCE_LEDGER_THROUGH_Y3V02.md), including its predecessor links to frozen Year 2 evidence. It replaces that home only for current lookup in this responsibility; the earlier artifact remains authoritative at its own boundary. V3 additions below do not delete inherited findings when no new event occurs. Evidence IDs E001–E070 refer only to V3.

## New perspective without a new complete affiliation

Shiraishi's unusually firm confidence makes Kiyotaka infer prior knowledge beyond their visible acquaintance. He considers a channel involving Arisu or a similar student, while treating the early classroom encounter as coincidental. The V2 observer revelation remains reader knowledge; V3 does not give Kiyotaka its full handler chain or establish a White Room upbringing for Shiraishi. [E030](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e030).

Kiyotaka's gun instruction privately invokes prior White Room curriculum while an Ijūin story supplies a public explanation. Record concealed learning origin separately from the cover story. Kanzaki's respect for Atsuomi is reported, but he also grounds his regard for Kiyotaka in his own two-year observations; inherited family admiration is not the whole explanation. [E015](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e015), [E042](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e042).

The end reveals Kiyotaka's intended alliance mechanism as prior intent, not a new identity. C/D cooperation, a limited C-to-B information mission and Kōenji's independently useful actions represent different degrees of allegiance. Shared effect does not establish common command. The eliminated envoy's name and agreement terms remain undisclosed. [E033](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e033), [E059](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e059), [E065](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e065).

Nanase's recruitment history, her preventive aim, Shiraishi's assignment, Shirogane's invoked role and Ishigami's arranged inquiry carry forward unchanged. No new direct event resolves the external sponsor chain, Eiichirō's later condition or Arisu's off-campus purpose. Operational names verified from ruby remain distinct, especially Tsukaji/塚地 and Tsukasaki/司城.
