## The school delegates allocation without guaranteeing delivery

The token exam makes class representatives responsible for receiving and distributing earned group/team awards. The teacher acknowledges hoarding is legal after initially minimizing advantages of the office. The institutional design therefore separates earning from control of payment; it does not merely observe interpersonal abuse from outside. Watch verification can prove a count without giving the observer control over later transfers.
