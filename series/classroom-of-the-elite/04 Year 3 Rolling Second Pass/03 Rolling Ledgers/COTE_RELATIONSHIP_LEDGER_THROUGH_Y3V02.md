E009, E021, E033–038, E043–048, E053–054, E058. The Hiyori update is the largest change from V1. Its proper statement is not merely that an exception might emerge: the source supplies specific interior development. Equally, her growing indispensability in his self-experience does not automatically create a shared constitution for the relationship. Emotional irreplaceability, instrumental usefulness, accepted corrective power and informed exit require separate evidence.

Hiyori's friendship with Kaneda confirms a life proceeding outside Kiyotaka's attention. Her withdrawal is motivated partly by class conflict, not an analyst-invented test. His apology and intended pursuit limit should count as local responsiveness. The experiment/disassembly language then leaves open whether a future refusal changes his project or becomes more data within it.

No relationship receives a universal simulation-readiness label here. Kei's address change, Hashimoto's nickname use and Kiyotaka's unplanned given-name address are recipient-specific acts. The later monograph layer must synthesize them with inherited histories and explicit unknowns.
