| Consent / request | Classmates themselves seek an alliance after induced losses | Sincere request is not independent preference formation |
| Equality | D members refuse to humiliate weaker guests | Hospitality does not distribute combined command equally |
| Protection | Saves bodies, exposes VIP identity, creates promises or imposes shields | Intent, consent, tactical outcome and human value differ |
| Visible / invisible | GPS can freeze while behavior reveals hidden conditions | Screens require inference; the observer can also misread |
| Ordinary story | Hashimoto can tell one, Kiyotaka lacks a shareable equivalent | A specific deprivation does not eliminate present enjoyment |
| Bleak opening | Hiyori refuses to guarantee consoling literary resolution | A character's poetics is not a spoiler or universal law |
