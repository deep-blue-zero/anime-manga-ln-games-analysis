# Year 3 Volume 4 — Authored revenge and a costly particular choice

**Complete local reading; provisional Year 3 argument through Y3V04.** This analysis follows the entire supplied Japanese EPUB, including its afterword and visual surfaces: 55 sequential chunks, 3,674 normalized paragraphs, 156,237 characters, 30 OPF spines, 24 art/paratext occurrences and three separately verified glyph assets. V3 was frozen before V4 was opened. No subsequent novel, advertised short story or adaptation supplies evidence. The [source audit](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_SOURCE_AND_RECONCILIATION_AUDIT.md) records hashes, normalization and unresolved details. The governing Year 3 method and binding monograph amendment remain in force.

## A chosen person inside a designed field of choices

Volume 4 makes two substantial developments coexist. Kiyotaka brings an expulsion scheme to completion by arranging what other people know, fear and want. He then gives up a likely individual victory to keep Hiyori in his school life. The latter choice is stronger evidence than another statement that he might be interested in affection: he incurs a concrete competitive cost, rejects his first experimental explanation as a pretext, and tells her he is in love. The former choice prevents the reader from treating that recognition as a general conversion toward accountable care. His capacity to value one person and his willingness to govern others through engineered necessity develop together. [E074](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e074)–[E086](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e086).

Kushida makes the central political distinction visible. She insists she could have kept the money and let Ibuki disappear; her final choice belongs to her. Kiyotaka is nevertheless confident because he has spent the exam shaping the hatred through which she chooses. Agency survives inside manipulation. Conversely, Hiyori's particular value changes Kiyotaka's conduct without yet granting her informed authority over his larger purposes. The volume's strongest claim is therefore a local crossing of the costly non-substitution threshold, alongside continuing control over the conditions of other people's freedom. It is not a guarantee of lasting love, universal compassion or relinquished sovereignty.

## The four-volume movement and narrative construction

V1 established the force of exceptional competence in the class Arisu left. V2 complicated its reach with illness, observer uncertainty, self-owned peer purposes and Hiyori's emerging indispensability. V3 revealed deliberately induced losses behind the class's request for an alliance. V4 shifts from engineering a constituency's need to engineering a particular actor's revenge, then places the engineer inside a rival's test of attachment. The same method that lets Kiyotaka make others predictable cannot completely settle what his own costly choice means to him.

The opening belongs to Ibuki's anticipated departure and one remaining wish. Its eventual placement near the goal makes the punch both the fulfillment of her demand and an unforeseen opportunity in someone else's operation. The token-rule chapter creates unequal information and mixed loyalties; the long first-task chapter turns minor slights, representative selection and cooperative arithmetic into future danger. The day-two chapters juxtapose contribution with distribution, then interrupt the island with Nanase and Amasawa's competing account of protection. Subsequent chapters move from withholding to humiliation, from humiliation to provisional solidarity, and from a publicly designated victim to a concealed switch. The final chapter first gives the ship's result, then reconstructs the transfer scheme and the earlier rescue. [E001](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e001), [E015](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e015)–[E042](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e042), [E049](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e049)–[E087](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e087).

This order matters. At several points the reader has correct observations but an incomplete causal sequence: Ibuki really has fifty when inspected; Katsuragi really behaves coldly; Kushida really transfers tokens; Kiyotaka really says he will not abandon his class for a friend. Later disclosure changes what these actions accomplish and what preceded them. Not every earlier apparent intention survives. A careful reading must keep deliberately concealed causality separate from an unexplained contradiction in private narration.

## Polities and the exam's political economy

Letters remain fixed until month-end despite the prior exam's reordered standings. Throughout this exam, Horikita leads A, Ryūen B, Kiyotaka C and Ichinose D. Stable polity identities prevent a passing rank reference from making a character appear to change membership. V4 confirms the previous placement order D/C/B/A; it does not retroactively supply V3 with an announced final score table. [E002](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e002)–[E003](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e003).

| Stable polity | Present capacity | Present political cost |
|---|---|---|
| Horikita / A | Productive alliance, independent deduction, group and individual awards, varied capable members | Internal coercion survives outside the leader's immediate reach; a classmate is lost despite collective victory |
| Ryūen / B | Competitive accumulation, practical aid within G2, Katsuragi's protective initiative | Hiyori becomes a deliberately endangered test; Kaneda's moral opposition has no protected stopping power |
| Sakayanagi-origin / C | Reliable performance, flexible transfers, Yoshida/Sanada initiative and Morishita's scrutiny | Kiyotaka owns crucial information and the selection environment, then delegates costs of a personal rescue |
| Ichinose / D | Own-class protection, shared G8 victory and effective cooperation | Solidarity accepts a known rival victim; personal attachment remains less controlled than public cooperation |

The token system separates four things that everyday language might join: earning, entitlement, possession and value toward a prize. Team and group awards pass through representatives. An earned front-token allowance survives delayed distribution, but the student cannot simply take it. Generic gifts become back tokens; the representative's earned allocation has a specific front-token exception. Both kinds count toward survival and group totals, but only front tokens count for the individual award. A rescue can preserve a person while reducing an individual advantage. [E004](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e004)–[E007](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e007), [E015](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e015)–[E018](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e018).

The school also makes final arrival a closure of agency. Thirty seconds in the goal fixes holdings and prevents further transfers. A majority fixes group arrival rank, while nonfinishers suffer a seventy-percent multiplier and class-point costs. Earlier arrival makes group tokens more valuable, but it removes possible rescuers from circulation. Retirement has a humane exception for genuine illness/injury and remote incoming aid, yet burdens the remaining group with an all-finish requirement. The system offers protections while making them costly to others. [E007](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e007), [E056](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e056).

The final-lowest rule guarantees a comparison problem rather than an independently culpable loser. Its separate first-zero trigger creates additional hazards; consuming a protection point at zero cannot erase the lowest-at-end contest. OAA resolves ties, but it does not make Hirata's unremembered comparison between Kaneda and Hiyori an established fact. His confidence is an inference about Ryūen's planning. The distinction matters because a confident character explanation can appear to close an alternative the source has not numerically demonstrated. [E005](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e005), [E063](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e063).

The announced results are limited but consequential. G8 finishes first and wins the token-group ranking, awarding A and D one hundred class points each. A also receives the one-hundred-point individual award; its winner is not named. Ryūen places second individually, two tokens behind the winner, and G2 supplies B's fifty-point group award. These are awards, not a full net standings table. The source does not give G3's final rank, Kiyotaka's final holdings or all delayed-finish deductions. Shinohara is the actual expellee. Naming a particular A student as individual winner or adding precise final class totals would exceed the record. [E073](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e073), [E080](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e080).

## Kiyotaka's five concurrent roles

As an ordinary participant, Kiyotaka enjoys attention that serves no immediate exam purpose. He repeatedly looks toward Hiyori, recognizes the time as inefficient and feels a small elevation of mood. His university conversation presents another limit: he can research a socially plausible future without privately expecting to live it. Ordinary competence in conversation and actual possession of an ordinary horizon are different. [E010](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e010), [E026](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e026).

As a relational actor, he can accept Haruka's renewed nickname and future invitation, provide Kushida a place to speak frankly, recognize Nishikawa's concern and seek Hiyori. These are differentiated relations with separate permissions. Haruka's invitation does not restore the whole old group; Kushida's frankness does not authenticate a romance; Nishikawa's particular attachment also motivates willingness to sacrifice other people. The volume refuses a simple division between human attachment and morally compromised action. [E027](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e027), [E044](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e044)–[E045](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e045).

As a strategist, he solves a voting problem, observes card order, tests transfer mechanics and revises a transfer route when the punch creates a distraction. His methods use known constraints and manufactured tempo. The card scene explicitly denies that Wang's first choice can be read straight from her personality. Her sorted hand and his pressure provide information that would disappear if she reshuffled. This is one of the volume's clearest limits on an overgeneralized genius model. [E021](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e021), [E032](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e032)–[E033](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e033), [E076](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e076).

As an author of circumstances, he rejects sincere cooperation, inflates Kushida's visibility and allows jealousy to accumulate. The opening couple is not already an undifferentiated pair of malicious fools: he privately recognizes their good faith and growth. The later wrongdoing is theirs, but the volume supplies causal evidence that he cultivates the conditions in which it becomes useful. Efficiency is a public justification for exclusions that themselves waste time. [E009](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e009), [E022](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e022)–[E023](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e023).

As a publicly responsible leader, he accepts being named the architect of sacrifice. Concentrating guilt on him gives others relief and makes collective acquiescence easier. This is a real departure from entirely hidden intervention, but public attribution alone does not give the affected students power to revise his purpose. Taking blame can stabilize sovereignty as effectively as hiding it. His rescue then shows that the same public leader can choose a personal end at a cost partly carried by his own members. [E067](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e067), [E081](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e081).

## Kushida, Ibuki and the limits of a liberating ending

Kushida's persona is both social power and a surface on which other people can make demands. Outsiders credit her kindness; classmates who know her secret can threaten exposure. Shinohara uses precisely that uneven audience knowledge. A representative chosen through pressure can withhold an earned share, intercept contact with Horikita, monitor token exchanges and require humiliating proof of goodwill. Kushida's ability to perform does not make her immune to domination. [E017](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e017), [E027](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e027), [E049](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e049)–[E053](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e053).

Her endurance is purposeful. She places remaining at school above preserving outward pride, and she considers increasingly costly ways to obtain help. These choices should be described as strategies under extortion, not evidence that she likes submission or freely offers intimacy. Kiyotaka's probing question receives an angry refusal. The scene supplies an audience-dependent boundary, not a hidden confession of attraction. Her stated future preference is clearer elsewhere: A graduation, parental and peer respect, and an admired adult life. [E053](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e053), [E055](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e055).

Ibuki begins with almost the opposite horizon. She sees poor academic and cooperative performance as making her expendable and identifies one concrete unfinished act. Her intervention in the humiliation does not grow from a sudden universal love of justice. She still dislikes Kushida and admits contradictory impulses. Yet she interrupts another person's domination and ends the immediate scene. Action exceeds the narrow moral identity she gives herself. Kushida's anger at the intervention also matters: Ibuki has not solved the material constraint that makes endurance rational for its victim. [E001](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e001), [E050](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e050).

The shallow-water encounter gives both characters relief through a change in interaction. It begins with an uninvited tackle, continues through a counter-trick and ends in mutual laughter. The beneficial result should not convert the initiating aggression into retroactive consent. What becomes newly possible is a brief relationship without the full burden of Kushida's pleasing performance or Ibuki's expectation of uselessness. Their aspirations remain different afterward. [E054](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e054)–[E055](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e055).

At the goal, Kushida's rejection of the nice persona is consequential and selective. She gives Ibuki the hidden rescue payload, locks in their arrival and turns openly against Shinohara. She later claims ownership of that decision: keeping the tokens and letting Ibuki lose was possible. Kiyotaka's counterpoint is causal rather than mechanical. He trusts what the cultivated hatred will make her want. She chooses within a field he has shaped; she is neither an inert puppet nor the unconstrained author of all the available options. [E069](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e069)–[E071](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e071), [E076](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e076), [E079](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e079).

Nor does the outcome match every actor's design. Kushida wanted Ike to disappear while Shinohara remained. Kiyotaka did not know which partner would fall. The partial repayment following the couple's earlier division makes Shinohara the actual loser. Ike's devastation and the need to restrain him keep a human cost visible even while Kushida enjoys revenge. Her greater expressive freedom does not establish ethical redemption. Her classmates' failure to prevent coercion and her own delight in the punishment both belong in the account. [E080](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e080).

## Horikita: victory, independent reasoning and failed protection

Horikita's collaboration with Ichinose begins in a real recognition of reliability, but she does not simply import D's social constitution into A. She objects that suppressing everyone's individual-prize pursuit may be unacceptable to her own members. That objection protects a constituent preference at the negotiation stage. Later, the no-loss pact makes it harder to oppose a plan that protects A and D by designating someone elsewhere. A constraint she helped accept becomes part of her own dilemma. [E011](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e011)–[E012](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e012), [E065](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e065)–[E066](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e066).

Her final deduction is also hers. Katsuragi's willingness to sacrifice Ibuki and the apparent absence of Ryūen's resistance do not fit her models. She tells Shinohara to verify actual holdings. That advice succeeds at the narrow task: Ibuki really has fifty when inspected. The defeat lies in the later concealed channel and irreversible finish, not a foolish inability to read the watch. Kiyotaka credits the reasoning while retaining control over more of the operation. [E069](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e069), [E077](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e077).

Horikita nevertheless fails to save Shinohara. Her group victory and class awards cannot stand in for protecting every member, just as the loss cannot erase independent thought and effective cooperation. Holding back outside the goal might preserve a phone and transfer ability, but distance, information and available resources would still matter. The source does not prove every alternative mathematically impossible. Its concrete outcome is a leader who must now address Ike's distress and the remaining class's interpretation of the loss. This is functioning after separation under genuine limits, not completed replacement of Kiyotaka's role.

## Ryūen, Hiyori, Kaneda and Hirata: protection under challenge

Ryūen recognizes Hiyori's importance before Kiyotaka has supplied a public explanation of it. His plan puts a valuable class member beyond ordinary timely rescue with only one token. Hiyori understands the strategic aim and asks him not to nullify the threat with a twenty-million-point rescue if she falls. Her contribution makes the danger credible, but it does not turn the situation into a morally uncomplicated act of free sacrifice. Class responsibility, rival pressure and the school's elimination rule define the available choices. [E013](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e013), [E058](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e058)–[E060](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e060).

Kaneda refuses the policy in explicitly moral terms. He demands recall, physically confronts Ryūen despite fear and objects to treating Hiyori this way. Ryūen responds by making successful force the condition of reversal. This is important evidence of follower judgment, courage and particular loyalty; it is also evidence that loyalty has not acquired protected authority. A leader's ability to tolerate angry words while maintaining the same course is not the same as accepting correction. [E061](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e061), [E063](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e063).

Hirata supplies the more uncomfortable contrast. He actually helps two students in his mixed group and negotiates broadly protective aims. Yet in the Hiyori dispute he restrains Kaneda and becomes interested in what Kiyotaka will do. He criticizes the method while helping the scene continue. Kaneda's accusation of outsider partiality has force precisely because Hirata's earlier care is real. The episode tests the distance between protecting people under one's immediate charge and consuming another person's danger as a revealing experiment. [E014](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e014), [E057](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e057), [E062](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e062).

Hiyori's loyalty does not morally redirect Ryūen here. Her request strengthens the credibility of his threat instead. This leaves the strict inherited redirection question open even though her relationship with him is substantial and her own choices matter. The rescue supplies a different relational development: her particular existence changes what Kiyotaka is willing to lose. These two directional relationships should not be merged into one general statement that she humanizes leaders.

## The rescue and the durability problem

Kiyotaka's first public reply refuses to rank a friend over class advantage. His later action makes that answer incomplete. He leaves without finishing, tells Yoshida she is special and uses Yoshida's attachment to Shiraishi to make opposition difficult. Yoshida then independently gathers help from Sanada and Morishita. These contributions are real, and so is the unequal information under which they are recruited. A private rescue can reveal affection and still distribute its cost politically. [E060](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e060), [E081](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e081).

On the beach, Kiyotaka first describes an interest in what an irrational mistake might feel like. The narration does not simply endorse this as the definitive explanation: speaking it forces recognition that it is a pretext. He wants Hiyori not to leave and wants more time in school with her. He says that another person would not have brought him there. The confession, uncertainty over her answer and material transfer make the scene a demonstrated costly refusal of substitution. This goes beyond the earlier volumes' gaze, pleasure and progressive indispensability. [E082](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e082)–[E085](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e085).

Hiyori's apology belongs to reciprocated affection and guilt over using it, not rejection. Her hoped-for rescue was uncertain, and her belief that she should bear class responsibility exposes a vulnerability of her own. Kiyotaka challenges that burden even as he has used other people's responsibility elsewhere. Their brief reciprocal grip is a present relational event. The text supplies neither exhaustive disclosure of his White Room history nor a settled compact allowing her to govern his future choices.

The final reflection must remain in the same interpretation. He imagines possibly releasing the hand after exhausting the novelty of emotion and asserts enduring action for himself. Those lines place a limit on an ethical completion claim. They do not annul the explicitly corrected explanation a few paragraphs earlier. A model that calls everything fake has to ignore the self-correction and cost; a model that declares lasting transformation has to ignore the future conditional and self-conception. The stronger account admits a real event whose persistence remains unproved. [E086](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e086).

## External actors and an alternative control project

The private-pool chapter extends the volume's central concern beyond school tokens. ## Japanese narration, embodiment and motifs

Written voice tracks audience and knowledge. Kushida's public reassurance, private irritation, pressured promises and final insults belong to different social situations. Treating the final register as the only real one would discard the actual work and costs of her public self. Ibuki's direct demand condenses her remaining purpose; Morishita's elaborate names and comic evasions coexist with exact objections; Wang's qualified speech can carry a firmer moral judgment than a confident command. Nanase's controlled formality and Amasawa's playful evasions change under pressure. No acoustic qualities can be inferred from these written patterns.

Kiyotaka's narration requires a further distinction. An inner account can state a real limitation, as in the card scene, or recognize genuine surprise. It can also withhold a planned continuation, and its candidate language can conflict with a later assertion of prior intent. The beach sequence explicitly stages an explanation being displaced by a more particular one. The analytical response should follow each local epistemic signal instead of assigning the narrator one globally reliable or globally deceptive status. The [Japanese index](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_JAPANESE_PASSAGE_INDEX.md) preserves bounded passages and short retrieval keys.

Hands recur as different relations to agency: Ike's coerced vote, the humiliating bow, Ibuki's collar grip, required watch contact, Kushida's movement toward the goal, Kōenji's spatial pressure and the beach's reciprocal handhold. Their visual similarity does not make their permissions equivalent. Front and back tokens offer another productive motif: apparent ownership depends on the history of acquisition, just as apparent choice depends on the history of preference formation. The finish line makes a moment's assent irrevocable for the remaining exam.

The illustrations strengthen these distinctions without independently revealing hidden motives. The humiliation image separates Shinohara's pleasure from Ike's unease; the later distress portrait refuses to leave expulsion as an abstract subtraction. Haruka's smiling shore portrait accompanies reopened contact, while the Hiyori profile precedes rather than depicts the mutual grip. ## Literary strengths and reservations

The volume's strongest formal achievement is that the same set of mechanics bears strategic, ethical and relational weight. A transfer can be earnings, coercion, camouflage, retaliation or rescue. Accurate verification can fail to protect because the state changes afterward. Responsibility can mean bearing cost, relieving others of guilt or appointing oneself the chooser of a victim. The Hiyori scene then turns a system of fungible units toward a person Kiyotaka says cannot be substituted. This is more persuasive because the cost is enacted rather than merely promised.

The ensemble also resists a simple ranking of human worth by competence. Weak academic performers can make legitimate procedural objections; a capable public performer can be coerced; an eccentric contributor can identify a serious moral cost. Kaneda's objection and Hirata's ambivalence are especially valuable because they prevent the rival's test from remaining an admired strategic spectacle. The source makes the treatment of Hiyori itself disputable.

There are material reservations. The withheld continuation and concealed larger transfer produce a satisfying reversal but limit the reader's ability to reconstruct a complete numerical contest in advance. Earlier private inclusion of Kushida as a candidate conflicts with the ending's claim that her removal was never intended. This deserves acknowledgment rather than a frictionless explanation that every line was always transparent. The day-three call chronology and a Moriyama/Sumida address sequence remain locally unclear. A full final score table and individual winner are absent. [E020](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e020), [E028](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e028), [E048](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e048), [E073](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e073)–[E078](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V04_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v04-e078).

The narrative also risks letting a victim's later liberation or another student's expulsion make humiliation appear necessary for growth. Its own evidence supports resistance to that reading: Wang's unretaliatory care and the couple's initial sincere cooperation show alternatives to the path Kiyotaka cultivates. A desired outcome does not prove that the preceding manufactured injury was the only viable route. Likewise, Nanase's protection offer needs evaluation as a claim with conditions, even if it opposes an abusive institution.

## Boundary corrections, cumulative delta and remaining questions

New evidence revises current lookup without rewriting earlier readers' knowledge. ## Provisional synthesis and routes

Through V4, the series supplies its strongest evidence that Kiyotaka can choose a particular person at real strategic cost while remaining an author of other people's constrained choices. The emotional event is real enough to revise the earlier ledger; the unresolved jurisdiction and durability are real enough to prevent completion of the larger ethical argument. Keeping both conclusions is the task of this boundary snapshot.
