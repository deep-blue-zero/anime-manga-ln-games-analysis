# Classroom of the Elite — Year 3 Volume 3 deep reading

## Boundary and central reading

Year 3 Volume 3 makes consent part of the contest. Kiyotaka's decisive achievement is to make his new class ask for an alliance it had resisted, after losses have changed what its members believe they can afford. The ending explicitly reveals that he invited both the opening ambush and further attrition to produce this request. That revelation does not make every intervening action his creation. Kitō buys escape time; Ichinose tries to warn the commander; Kōenji exceeds the expected effect of a limited provocation; Yamamura identifies avoidable harm; Horikita organizes an effective counterattack; D's ordinary members welcome defeated allies more generously than Kiyotaka anticipates. Their agency is real within conditions he partly controls. [E018](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e018)–[E025](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e025), [E033](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e033)–[E040](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e040), [E054](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e054), [E065](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e065).

The central distinction is therefore between contributing to an outcome and having authority over the ends and conditions that make it desirable. Kiyotaka can listen, accept a role refusal, share tactical decisions, assume personal risk and provide effective care while withholding why the crisis exists. His classmates' later assent is sincere. It is also formed through losses he deliberately permits and an account of those losses he controls. The novel makes that distinction legible before its final explanation through Yamamura's question: even if the alliance is now right, could an earlier decision have spared Morishita? Her challenge addresses the production of necessity rather than denying the need that now exists. [E011](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e011)–[E013](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e013), [E027](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e027), [E037](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e037).

## Narrative architecture: apparent failure, actual contribution and withheld cause

Hiyori's opening makes the reader encounter possible personal loss before learning the game's rules. An irreplaceable sunset, an unspoken confession, resolve to oppose Kiyotaka and a lonely anticipated farewell create a problem that survivor arithmetic cannot yet explain. The main narrative then moves backward into announcement, allocation and combat, with repeated changes of focal position. The ambush appears first as an emergency, then as an event Ichinose tries to warn against, then as a victory Ryūen nevertheless refuses to overextend. These revisits distinguish what happened from what each participant can infer. [E001](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e001), [E018](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e018)–[E023](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e023).

The middle sequence converts strategic pressure into altered political judgment. Food retrieval, cramped tents and morale work make the costs of losing people materially legible. Kōenji's encounter and the G11 attack interrupt any simple descent from error to inevitable defeat: one development exceeds Kiyotaka's expectations, while the other is more deliberate than he publicly admits. The alliance chapter then slows into welcome, rice and interrogation. Later the title “The real aim” promises clarification but initially presents another set of partial purposes. The timed A ambush is first a combat humiliation and only later disclosed as reconnaissance of VIP identities. [E026](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e026)–[E047](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e047), [E049](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e049), [E059](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e059).

The last chapters widen action to secondary participants before narrowing to the beach duel. That duel resolves the question of how Kiyotaka can respond while apparently disarmed, but its explanation also reinterprets the class's earlier losses. The school's immediate new announcement then prevents this explanatory mastery from becoming final institutional closure. Kiyotaka has decoded and manipulated much of one exam; the school still controls when the exam sequence ends and what follows. Hiyori's monologue likewise remains unplaced. The volume closes several tactical explanations while leaving its opening emotional frame and official result unsettled. [E055](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e055)–[E068](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e068).

## The exam as a political institution

The school distributes capacities rather than simply testing who shoots best. Commander, VIP, guard and specialist roles separate knowledge, communication, action and value. One commander sees class-colored positions on five-minute updates from fixed headquarters but cannot attack or be eliminated. Three VIPs carry the direct communication channel and100 survival points each but cannot fire. Guards can attack and are worth one point each; analysts reveal supply information, while a scout detects nearby presences subject to GPS stoppage. Specialists can be replaced from guards with authorization, but cannot simply switch back. Choosing an information role therefore carries an opportunity cost in weapons and attack capacity. [E003](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e003), [E007](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e007), [E009](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e009), [E012](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e012)–[E015](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e015).

| Mechanism | Strategic effect | Political or ethical consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Five-minute positions, hidden enemy identities, limited inquiry and stoppage tactics | Makes timing, interpretation and role inference decisive | A commander can miss a pattern despite effort; information concentration produces a bottleneck |
| One direct commander/VIP line at a time | Coordinates a dispersed class through an exposed intermediary | A busy line can deny a local group timely warning; channel design matters alongside personal competence |
| Scarce food, ammunition and complementary supplies | Forces retrieval and tradeoffs between preservation and exposure | Hunger is an institutional pressure on what choices remain practical |
| Progressive forbidden areas and a shortened final period | Compresses surviving forces into encounters | Withdrawal remains possible only within school-defined space and time |
| Three VIP losses wipe the class; first wipe requires an expulsion selection | Gives a few bodies very high score value while exposing the whole polity to a decision | Tactical protection and human worth can diverge; designation raises jurisdiction and consent questions |
| Sensor reports, self-report duties and school review | Makes a hit a rule-governed event rather than a purely visible splash | Proof and adjudication matter; known false SAFE is not permission to continue fighting |
| Prohibitions on restraint, theft and after-hours attacks | Makes otherwise vulnerable movement or negotiation possible | The rule system both constrains violence and enables exploitation of predictable retaliation |

The award schedule is+150/+100/−100/−150 class points. Protection can cancel a selected holder's expulsion, but neither possession nor a leader's expectation proves consent or eventual use. Serious violations have separate penalties; the ordinary first-wipe selection is not a universal ceiling on every possible expulsion. Similarly, the 200-point penalty for forcing an ill VIP onward concerns exam points, whereas the major violation penalty concerns100 class points. These distinctions matter more than accumulating every equipment number. [E004](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e004)–[E007](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e007).

The map has15 columns and15 rows, with remembered terrain modified since earlier island exams. Initial area choice and later closures create different constraints; the starting lottery should not be described as a direct random assignment in every account. At night students can move, but the next morning must begin in their previous 18:00 area. This is why Kiyotaka can camp inside the hostile group and still use the ban on restraint to escape. He explicitly says this method would fail on a real battlefield: its power comes from being protected by exam rules. The scene is a strong limit on transporting fictional tactical feats into unconstrained combat models. [E008](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e008), [E050](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e050)–[E053](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e053).

The source contains several bounded ambiguities. Its opening distinguishes one-hour advance closure notice from five-minute forbidden-area exposure; a later60-second cumulative exposure rule describes the prior allowance as one hour. A later reference to D contact mismatches the shown C envoy. A paint-stained Hashimoto shoulder does not produce OUT, with no local explanation that fully settles the earlier edge/splash exceptions. These should remain visible reservations in rule analysis. They do not authorize inventing a second envoy, a new sensor exception or an exact exposure schedule unsupported by the text. ## Four polities and the unannounced result

The stable class identities matter more than a predicted letter change. Through the survival game, Horikita's polity is A, Ryūen's B, the Sakayanagi-origin polity now led by Kiyotaka C, and Ichinose's D. A enters with 1240 class points, B1081, C867 and D864 from the last official V2 table. C's three-point lead over D determines the alliance's immediate allocation of first place; the bargain turns a tiny numerical difference into a large strategic obligation. [E016](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e016).

| Polity | Demonstrated governing strength | Constraint exposed here | Result boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horikita | Independent defensive reasoning, retained tactics, distributed attack and capable protective followers | Transfer resentment limits command; enemy reconnaissance converts protection into information | First complete VIP wipe is observed; projected fourth |
| Ryūen | Prepared ambush, disciplined fire, adaptive restraint, coordination with Katsuragi | Busy communication, Kōenji contingency and the leader's competing desire to defeat Kiyotaka | Projected third; leader's final OUT is observed |
| Sakayanagi-origin / Kiyotaka | Rapid crisis management, information use, role flexibility and coalition design | Consent shaped by concealed attrition; continued reliance on imported leadership | Projected second; no official announcement yet |
| Ichinose | Independent inference, generous integration, trusted coordination and practical mutual aid | Concentrated command legitimacy, material burden of care and unclear scope for dissent | Projected first; no official announcement yet |

At15:00 A's last VIP is eliminated, making it the first wiped class. The last directly stated nine-person field configuration gives B 102/C102/D201. Kiyotaka and Ryūen then both register OUT. If no other survivor changes, C101/B100/D201 follows arithmetically. Kiyotaka anticipates D/C/B/A placement and no expulsion because A contains protection holders. Classmates celebrate C's second, but neither their expectation nor his near-certainty is an official result. The volume shows no named selection or consumed protection point. [E060](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e060), [E064](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e064), [E066](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e066)–[E068](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e068).

Applying the anticipated awards would produce Horikita1090, Ichinose1014, Ryūen981 and Sakayanagi-origin967. These are conditional projections, not a replacement current table. The distinction is substantively important: the school immediately starts another exam before providing the final settlement on the page. The architecture must preserve this stopping point instead of borrowing closure from a later source.

## Kiyotaka: responsibility, instruction and authorship of preference

Kiyotaka's self-nomination is not empty theater. The proposal follows legitimate objections: Sanada anticipates the designated student's ordeal, and Matoba disputes whether a leader can decide everything. Morishita's nomination of Hashimoto exposes how existing stigma can make one person appear an acceptable answer to everyone else's problem. Kiyotaka then offers himself and asks Mashima to fix the designation so he cannot retract it or substitute another person. The teacher cannot do so at that stage. The attempt has evidentiary weight even though it does not become an enforceable agreement. [E010](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e010)–[E011](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e011).

That weight does not settle its political function. By assuming the threatened outcome, Kiyotaka makes his mandate harder to contest: later he requires further dissenters to occupy the same position of expulsion exposure. The move conflates responsibility for deciding with permission to challenge a decision. Classmates do not possess his information, capability or control over the situation, so nominally matching his risk is not equal political standing. The ending further reveals that their apparent common danger is partly something he chose to impose. [E027](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e027), [E065](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e065).

He nevertheless demonstrates specific forms of responsiveness. Morishita's refusal of command is accepted. Shimazaki volunteers for a role suited to his reported preference, receives reassurance about responsibility and can choose a different VIP contact. Rokkaku's lost weapon becomes a reported problem and an accepted promotion rather than a punitive retrieval demand. Kanzaki's H10 proposal changes positioning. Kiyotaka slows for exhausted classmates, allows illness retirement, orders Ninomiya's immediate pickup and distributes supplies fairly. These actions should be credited at their actual scale. They show feasible discretion, competent care and effective consultation; they do not retroactively disclose the alliance's manufactured need. [E012](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e012)–[E013](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e013), [E019](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e019), [E022](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e022), [E028](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e028), [E046](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e046).

| Layer of Kiyotaka's action | V3 finding | What remains unproved |
|---|---|---|
| Desired outcome | Establish continuing C/D cooperation and obtain C's request for it | That this outcome expresses fully informed collective ends |
| Strategic authorship | Invite losses, alter coalition size and incentivize rival attacks | Exact control of every encounter or participant |
| Local command | Give effective instructions, share contingencies selectively and accept useful proposals | Protected opposition to the governing purpose |
| Personal stake | Offer expulsion and seek to bind the offer | Actual accepted removal, enforceable agreement or follower authority to revise his ends |
| Generative instruction | Teach equipment, role camouflage and ways of comparing rival plans | Followers independently reconstructing the entire hidden design |
| Ordinary experience | Enjoy camp novelty, warm rice and affiliation while lacking an ordinary shareable childhood | Completion of an ordinary social identity |

The last distinction becomes sharp when he gives Kanzaki spare magazines and keeps 22 rounds. Supplying the VIP guard is rational and useful. Kiyotaka also privately wants the encounter made less certain now that expulsion danger for his class has passed. Avoiding last place is not identical to securing the second place offered to his constituency. His experimental pleasure can therefore diverge from the precise promise even while his wider strategy succeeds. He acknowledges that Ryūen might simply shoot him; predicting delay with high probability is not knowing it must happen. ## Consent examined across ten dimensions

The alliance is not best described by a single binary of voluntary or forced. Its formation, implementation and consequences distribute choice unevenly. The dimensions below concern this specific process, rather than a clinical label or an all-purpose verdict on every relationship.

| Dimension | Evidence at this boundary |
|---|---|
| Information | Classmates hear an alliance formula and see losses but do not know Kiyotaka invited the conditions producing them |
| Alternatives | Initial resistance and postponement are possible; later hunger, attrition and shrinking space make refusal markedly worse |
| Exit | A role can be refused locally, but leaving the school-imposed competitive setting is not an equivalent ordinary option; early alliance refusal is framed as jeopardizing future cooperation |
| Material pressure | Ammunition, food, fatigue and reduced numbers raise the cost of maintaining the original preference |
| Institutional pressure | Expulsion selection and role restrictions concentrate stakes; school declines to fix Kiyotaka's designation in advance |
| Emotional pressure | Class shame, fear of loss, dependence on the new leader and Hashimoto's persuasion make dissent socially costly |
| Manufactured scarcity | The school creates scarcity generally; Kiyotaka deliberately worsens C's position to make alliance necessity self-evident |
| Time | An initially postponable decision returns under immediate competitive pressure after additional avoidable exposure |
| Post-choice ownership | C states a top-two condition and members can contribute operationally, while combined field command passes to Kiyotaka |
| Revision | Yamamura questions the route and remains unpersuaded; no consequential mechanism compels reconsideration or full disclosure |

This account preserves the strongest counterevidence to a total-control reading: a local role refusal works, class conditions on acceptance remain expressed, and D's reception is not merely scripted obedience. It also preserves the strongest objection to treating assent as sufficient: Kiyotaka deliberately changes the practical alternatives and conceals that authorship. Benefit and development can coexist with that objection. Neither a good projected placement nor the absence of an eventual expulsion, itself unconfirmed here, answers it. [E011](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e011)–[E017](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e017), [E027](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e027), [E036](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e036)–[E042](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e042), [E065](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e065)–[E066](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e066).

## Horikita and the class after Kiyotaka's departure

Horikita's difficulty begins with an image of Kiyotaka, not an inability to reason. Hirata asks her to face the apparent loss; she recognizes reluctance to credit Ryūen and the inferiority stirred by comparing her safer decision with his attack. She still analyzes the danger, credits the successful timing and refuses to treat C's losses as permission for complacency. The ending changes the causal interpretation of those losses, so the scene does not close the strict test of witnessing genuinely uncontrolled Kiyotaka failure. It does establish that her own attachment to his invincibility can be made an object of reflection. [E025](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e025), [E065](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e065).

Her practical command is mixed rather than uniformly restored or uniformly defeated. She retains her whole-class blackout, supports protective formations and coordinates more than one attacking group. Kushida contributes structural advice when Kiyotaka's apparent influence threatens to absorb all explanation. Sudō and Hirata enact successful parts of A's battle. Against this, Horikita relaxes too soon before the evening ambush, diagnoses its time trap too late to stop four shots, and cannot keep16 resentful guards from wasting the following morning chasing Kiyotaka. She accurately anticipates that waste and keeps Sudō back because he can actually follow far enough to be endangered. Partial effective constraint is neither absolute command nor helplessness. [E049](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e049), [E051](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e051)–[E058](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e058).

Kiyotaka's reconnaissance makes protection double-edged. A's reflexive moves to cover its VIPs are competent responses, but they disclose identities when he compares them with earlier positions. Satō's being inside a tent does not defeat this method because the informative behavior belongs to the guards moving toward her. The later distribution of that intelligence to B helps turn A's structural position into a pincer. Horikita can author good local tactics and still lose against hostile information and incentive design. Explaining this layered causality is more useful than reducing her outcome to either independent failure or Kiyotaka owning every action. [E054](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e054), [E056](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e056), [E059](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e059).

Kei's conversation supplies a different form of post-transfer development. The two can call their relationship friendship, ask about Kiyotaka and reveal unequal information without recreating Kei's former romance. Kei still likes him and chooses how to speak about Ichinose; she also permits Horikita a possible shared crush. Horikita immediately rejects romance with Sudō but cannot settle why a comparable answer about Kiyotaka freezes. The contrast is strong evidence of an unresolved difference, not an explicit self-recognized confession. Both girls remain interpreters of their own attachments rather than completed romantic classifications. ## Returning and secondary characters as causal agents

Sudō's performance combines athletic capability with serious practice and tactical judgment. At least seven B eliminations occur in the relevant sequence; Yukimura's loaded magazines materially support them within his non-shooting VIP role. Sudō rescues Satō, infers the information leak and resists treating her offer of sacrifice as an easy solution. His protection of this classmate should not be translated into a romance with her or a reward from Horikita. It shows contribution under conditions in which several people have distinct needs. [E055](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e055)–[E056](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e056).

Hirata defeats Hashimoto and helps hold back C/D, which gives him tactical agency beyond soothing others. The ensuing threat is also serious. After Hashimoto is OUT, Hirata points the rifle at his forehead and nearly fires before stopping. The text depicts neither a second shot nor a settled explanation for restraint. His eventual turn back toward helping A does not erase the intimidation, and Hashimoto's fear should not be dismissed because the weapons belong to a game. Conversely, treating the nearly fired shot as actual assault would overwrite the boundary. The scene complicates an account of recovered gentleness without licensing an invented diagnosis or single causal story about his past. [E057](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e057)–[E058](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e058).

Hashimoto's different scenes resist a single trait label. Around the camp he creates reciprocal laughter and supplies an ordinary childhood anecdote; Matoba can expose his own fault within it. He also defends Kiyotaka, overstates the necessity of the earlier alliance offer and offers his body as protection. In combat he weaponizes abandonment and an unsupported claim that Kiyotaka regrets leaving Hirata. Social talent can repair morale, sustain belonging, legitimate manipulation and injure a particular opponent. None of these capacities alone proves moral rehabilitation or undifferentiated cynicism. [E029](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e029), [E038](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e038), [E057](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e057)–[E058](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e058).

Yamamura's contribution is quieter and analytically decisive. Early encouragement allows survival itself to count as useful; later she remains with Shiraishi and eventually retains ammunition she has not used. Yet her most important independent act is her causal objection after Morishita's elimination. Hesitant syntax and apologies do not weaken the content: she accepts the alliance now while contesting why it was not reconsidered before further loss. The final bluff works because her real stealth is credible, but it is explicitly a bluff. She is not actually behind Ryūen and does not rescue Kiyotaka. Her established perceptiveness and survival should not be inflated into protective authority she has not been given. [E024](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e024), [E037](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e037), [E059](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e059)–[E063](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e063).

Morishita's accepted role refusal and earlier supply proposal show real participation before the mock death scene. Her embrace of Yamamura is ambiguous: Yamamura reads rescue, Kiyotaka suspects a failed attempt to use a shield, and the illustration cannot adjudicate between them. Her heroic farewell, practical interruption and Amazoness talk are a comic performance. They can inform speech modeling under playful stress; they cannot furnish an actual jungle upbringing. Similarly, Shimazaki's preference for headquarters thinking, lost opportunities to notice warnings and genuine communication effort require a situated model rather than “smart” or “incompetent” as a permanent category. ## Rival ends, hospitality and relationships that are not interchangeable

Ryūen's first ambush is effective, and his decision to halt pursuit is reasoned. Kiyotaka remains alive, Kitō has bought time, the hidden rival may prepare a counterattack, and other classes benefit from B exhausting itself. Katsuragi's arguments matter within that decision process. Later the communication bottleneck and Kōenji's encounter defeat an assumption that local confidence with guns is enough. B's followers can identify causes beyond a convenient scapegoat; trained signals and written notes also preserve coordination when ordinary radio use becomes awkward. This is an organization with reproduced skills, not simply the leader's intimidation extended over anonymous bodies. [E023](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e023), [E031](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e031), [E033](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e033), [E043](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e043), [E054](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e054).

Its limits appear when Ryūen wants class victory and Kiyotaka's defeat as competing ends. Ibuki originally favors pursuing VIPs; on the beach he wants to see whether the opponent can escape even at the risk of allowing it. He recognizes the familiar danger of delaying a winning shot and continues checking for traps. Curiosity nevertheless determines which risk he accepts. His later acknowledgment that Ibuki was right is evidence of retrospective recognition, not an earlier exercised follower veto. His future vow intensifies rivalry rather than demonstrating Hiyori's moral redirection. [E061](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e061)–[E066](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e066).

Hiyori does have particular access. Ryūen adjusts his pace and takes her literary question seriously. Her example of a bleak opening acknowledges both consoling reversals and works that remain bleak; it refuses to promise that a story's emotional investment guarantees a favorable ending. When asked about following a cruel choice, she qualifies her resolve through the class and its members. That is not an unconditional blank check, but neither does she change Ryūen's chosen end here. Affection for Kiyotaka can coexist with resolved opposition to him. The volume strengthens her own directional attachment through the opening, while leaving its temporal and personal consequences unresolved. [E001](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e001), [E044](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e044).

Kiyotaka's search for her after returning to the ship continues the particular attention established much more fully in V2. He sees her apparently well beside Kaneda and considers approaching before Yoshida interrupts. There is no depicted reunion. Limited new interaction does not cancel earlier involuntary happiness or progressive indispensability; equally, a search does not establish full reciprocal knowledge or shared authority over future choices. Hiyori has a class, a reading companion and a relation to Ryūen beyond Kiyotaka's attention. Those independent connections are part of what makes her a particular person rather than only the object of his experiment. [E067](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e067); inherited V2 Hiyori findings.

Ichinose's first contribution is an attempted warning, not just confidence in Kiyotaka. She infers danger from another commander's conduct and frozen positions, attempts to attract Shimazaki's attention within restrictive rules, and withdraws when the attempt fails and risks her own class. Later her assertion changes from “he will not lose” to “I will not let him lose.” The change gives her an active purpose. It does not prove that she knows all his concealed reasoning, some of which the scene withholds. [E021](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e021), [E040](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e040).

D's members then accomplish something Kiyotaka underestimates: they welcome the weaker class as equals without a long interval of suspicion or humiliation. Their conduct is a class resource produced through relationships, not reducible to Ichinose's private attachment. Shiraishi can name its importance. Shared supplies and warm rice make this practical hospitality visible, while later illness makes its costs visible too. Kindness is not invalidated because Kiyotaka engineered the need for aid. The ethical question concerns both the manipulation and what hosts freely do with the people who arrive. [E040](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e040)–[E046](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e046).

Command introduces the necessary qualification. Ichinose gives Kiyotaka control of the combined force; Kanzaki reports that nobody in D now opposes her. His own interrogation remains intelligent and skeptical, and his H10 proposal has an effect. The resulting arrangement permits genuine local agency within concentrated authority. Kiyotaka's invitation to develop confidence or learn more about Ichinose remains a proposed intervention, not an observed transformation. Equality in interpersonal reception and equality in deciding ends must remain separate findings. [E041](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e041)–[E042](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e042), [E046](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e046).

Kōenji supplies the clearest limit on class ownership of useful behavior. His self-described pleasure, the opponents' provocation and his desire to depart explain the nine eliminations and spared VIP more directly than obedience to Horikita. A benefits enormously without acquiring command of him. Kiyotaka can seek to stimulate a possible encounter yet misjudge retirement timing and the encounter's magnitude. Voluntary mercy toward Morofuji also carries an absolute warning against obstructing his departure. Neither instrumental value to A nor one spared opponent demonstrates a general ethic of benevolence. ## Identity, ordinary life and the school beyond the game

Shiraishi's certainty is now a problem inside Kiyotaka's perspective. He asks for criticism but also uses the answer diagnostically: the visible acquaintance does not seem sufficient to justify such confidence. He infers prior knowledge, perhaps through Arisu or a similar channel, while treating an earlier classroom encounter as coincidental. The reader's V2 knowledge of an observer assignment does not mean he knows the handler chain, and her supportive presentation does not settle her private purpose. Nanase's origin/mission distinctions therefore remain necessary inherited categories, with no new full external-system explanation in this volume. [E030](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e030).

Ordinary scenes also test what the strategic register cannot supply. Hashimoto can tell a socially usable childhood story; Kiyotaka cannot readily offer an equivalent and finds a genuine errand with which to leave. That absence is specific, not proof of emotional emptiness. He enjoys warmth, food, novelty and classmates' unfamiliar expressions. The lantern and tent become small forms of mutual shelter within imposed insecurity. These experiences carry value without requiring them to terminate his strategic project. The strongest reading keeps enjoyment, exclusion and opportunistic withdrawal together. [E028](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e028)–[E030](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e030), [E041](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e041).

The institution remains an active designer throughout. It combines compulsory assessment with a manufacturer's prototype trial, removes ordinary private resources, centralizes communications, assigns unequal point values and progressively removes space. Kishinami's equipment and environmental assurances are supplier claims. School review can restore a false OUT or sanction a false SAFE; displayed data therefore require a procedure that decides what counts. The evening trap does not defeat this authority. Kiyotaka wins through it, turning opponents' anger into officially valid penalties. [E003](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e003)–[E007](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e007), [E049](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e049), [E052](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e052).

At the close, the school reasserts control of scope. Bottled water with different labels seems like a minor consumer choice, teachers look unusually grave, and last survivors must gather without the rest earlier eliminated students enjoyed. The purpose of labels and cartons is not disclosed. Mashima distinguishes the ended survival game from a newly beginning island exam. Kiyotaka's earlier suspicion that four days might serve another purpose becomes relevant, but it does not prove he knew the next rules. Institutional authorship exceeds the tactic that currently appears to explain everything. ## Literary craft and reservations

The volume's strongest formal choice is to distribute causal knowledge across participants. The ambush, failed warning, halted pursuit and later explanation make the same event mean different things without making every earlier interpretation foolish. Secondary confrontations carry their own stakes: Hashimoto/Hirata's exchange is about abandonment and anger as well as shots; Morishita/Yamamura combines bodily comedy with unresolved care; Sudō/Satō makes protection a practical decision. The map and rules graphic promise abstract legibility, while the illustrations of paired bodies restore the people hidden by point arithmetic. [E018](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e018)–[E025](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e025), [E035](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e035), [E055](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e055)–[E058](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e058); visual auditill07–08,14,18–20.

Several motifs connect the layers. Visibility can be stopped on a screen but restored through observing another person's attention. Protection hides a vulnerable body while exposing its identity. A leader's apparent weakness strengthens his governing mandate. Hunger makes consent urgent; warm food makes the resulting alliance humane in everyday practice. The final empty space behind Ryūen turns a credible relationship into a weapon through its imagined presence. Hiyori's unspoken confession and Kiyotaka's interrupted approach, meanwhile, preserve forms of significance the battle does not settle.

The delayed disclosure also has a cost. Kiyotaka's prolonged interior access withholds major intentions while encouraging an apparent-error reading. Some earlier qualifications and his diagnostic interest prepare the reversal, but the final account can still feel like a concentration of explanatory privilege: readers cannot reconstruct every hidden step in advance. The paintball has a preparation clue in the dirty hands and a later account of testing, yet the precise technique is largely retrospective. Its achievement should be judged as a staged reveal rather than a fully transparent puzzle. [E030](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e030), [E034](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e034)–[E036](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e036), [E045](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e045), [E064](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e064)–[E065](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e065).

The extensive combat geography and repeated forecasts sometimes slow emotional movement, and not every named participant receives development proportionate to the attention demanded by tracking them. Rule wording and the local sensor ambiguity reduce confidence in treating the contest as a perfectly specified simulation. The cliffhanger opens a further test before formally settling this one's projected outcome, which strengthens institutional unease but withholds some payoff expected from a whole-volume competition. Hiyori's unresolved opening is therefore a maintained tension at this boundary, not already a demonstrated successful resolution. These reservations do not erase the effective distributed action or the ethical force of Yamamura's question.

The afterword's account of a broad cast and historical production difficulties contextualizes intended scale. It does not diagnose a cause for individual prose choices or exempt them from assessment. Its plans for a narrower subsequent installment remain prospective paratext, not imported evidence about V4. ## Longitudinal adjudication and next obligations

Relative to Year 2's frozen horizon, V3 strengthens the distinction between reproducing capability and redistributing sovereignty. Kitō, Sudō, Hirata, Kanzaki and the welcoming D members do consequential work. C learns and chooses inside a framework whose hidden costs Kiyotaka authors. Horikita's polity can act after his departure while still suffering conditions he creates; the continuation of action is not proof that separation is emotionally or politically finished. Arisu's former classmates retain differing values and memories while depending on a new leader. These are developmental findings, not retroactive changes to earlier volume judgments.

The four strict Year 1 questions remain open. Kiyotaka's selective explanations do not provide sufficient voluntary history for informed relational choice. Horikita's observation of an engineered apparent failure and an unwitnessed mutual duel OUT do not meet the genuine-loss condition. Hiyori's conditional resolve and special listening access do not morally redirect Ryūen's own end. Yamamura's actual objection and fictional rescue bluff do not grant consequential corrective/protective authority through disclosed weakness. The12 Year 2 handoff tests retain their prior histories; answerability gains a stronger attempted self-binding observation but no demonstrated completed transfer of authority. Council succession receives no invented development.

The rolling updates should therefore preserve the 70 evidence entries, refine the 17 materially affected ledger homes, maintain all 54 handoff IDs with dated V3 adjudication, and extend only the provisional Year 3 synthesis. No retrospective prediction-success score is warranted: the reconstruction protocol remains frozen at its Year 2 boundary, and a character's percentage estimate is not an analyst's prospective calibration record. Class totals and protection use remain projected or unannounced as specified above.

The mandatory monograph layer remains an explicit obligation. This volume adds useful personal-history reports, written-voice distinctions, preferences under pressure and strong negative constraints. It does not itself complete the systematic qualification sweep or produce individual monographs. Qualified characters will require substantial evidence-bound histories and behavioral/speech/preference models, with scenario-specific limits and unknowns. A role, joke, taunt, one act of mercy or an unspoken question cannot fill missing biography. Existing strong V2 affect evidence must carry forward when V3 supplies little new interaction.

V3's provisional contribution is that actual care, effective cooperation and sincere assent can all occur inside deliberately manufactured dependence. Its counterweight is equally important: those people still act, judge, resist, misread and surprise. The next source can test whether the new institutional situation changes their authority, but it cannot be used to settle this volume before its own boundary is locked.
