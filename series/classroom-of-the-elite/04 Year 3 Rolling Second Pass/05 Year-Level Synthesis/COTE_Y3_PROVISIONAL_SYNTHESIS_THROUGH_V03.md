# Year 3 provisional synthesis — Through Volume 3

**PROVISIONAL SUPERSESSION NOTICE — evidence through Y3V03 only.** This snapshot carries the complete [V2 synthesis](COTE_Y3_PROVISIONAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_V02.md) and its inherited Year 2 history. Earlier findings remain authoritative at their own boundaries. V4 remains unread; this is neither final Year 3 nor all-series synthesis.

## From recognized competence to authored consent

V1 made Kiyotaka's exceptional competence persuasive in the polity Arisu left. V2 complicated its reach through illness, independent peer purposes, observer uncertainty and unusually strong particular feeling toward Hiyori. V3 shows that actual care and sincere collective assent can coexist with concealed control of the conditions producing them. Kiyotaka deliberately exposes C to losses until classmates request his proposed alliance. He seeks to make himself bear expulsion responsibility but later requires dissenters to match that exposure. Responsibility becomes both protection and a condition limiting political opposition. V3 [E010](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e010)–[E017](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e017),[E027](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e027),[E065](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e065).

This sharpens the existing distinction between generative capacity and shared authority. Kitō buys retreat time, Yamamura questions avoidable loss, Kanzaki changes positioning and D's members integrate weaker allies faster than Kiyotaka expects. These are actual contributions. Their reality does not give the contributors informed command over the purpose for which their circumstances were arranged. The strongest new ethical evidence is therefore not simply that Kiyotaka manipulates, but that his manipulation can leave meaningful local choice, kindness and judgment intact while withholding the power to revise his larger end. V3 [E018](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e018),[E037](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e037),[E040](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e040)–[E046](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e046).

## Separation and the four class constitutions

Horikita's post-transfer functioning now includes a substantial hostile test. She identifies weaknesses in her image of Kiyotaka, plans independently, deploys retained tactics and receives consequential contributions from Kushida, Sudō, Hirata and other classmates. Her command also fails to stop anger-driven waste, and A is the first class wiped. Loss does not erase independent authorship, just as good local strategy does not guarantee freedom from an opponent's information and incentive design. V3 [E025](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e025),[E049](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e049)–[E059](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e059).

C continues to speak with more than one voice. Matoba's jurisdiction objection, Motodoi's wish not to surrender first place, Morishita's role refusal and Yamamura's causal question should not be collapsed into a single defective mentality waiting for a genius. The imported leader nevertheless makes C's changed preference secure his mandate. Arisu's absence has not produced an empty class, but independent contribution still falls short of a constitution capable of compelling the new sovereign to answer for his hidden costs. V3 [E009](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e009)–[E018](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e018),[E037](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e037),[E065](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e065).

B reproduces competence through ambush preparation, disciplined fire, signs, written notes and followers' reasoning. Ryūen can recognize advice and still subordinate class risk to curiosity about defeating Kiyotaka. Hiyori's particular access produces serious listening, not yet moral redirection. D contributes the clearest collective hospitality: equal treatment of the weaker ally, practical help and food sharing. Its kindness has material costs, while Kanzaki's report of no opposition to Ichinose and transfer of combined command expose concentrated authority. Each polity combines strengths and limits; the forecast rank does not settle legitimacy. V3 [E023](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e023),[E043](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e043)–[E046](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e046),[E054](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e054)–[E066](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e066).

## Particular people and ordinary purposes

V2's evidence that Hiyori has become affectively particular to Kiyotaka remains strong. V3 adds her own irreplaceable memory and unspoken confession, a class-conditioned readiness to oppose him, and his interrupted search after the battle. It neither completes the relation nor cancels the earlier development. Hiyori also retains Kaneda's companionship and distinctive access to Ryūen. Her opening's time and farewell referent remain unresolved through this source. V3 [E001](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e001),[E044](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e044),[E067](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e067).

Kei's friendship with Horikita and control of information extend post-romance purposes while attachment persists. Hashimoto's shared anecdote shows ordinary sociability and moral self-presentation; Kiyotaka's inability to supply an equivalent marks a specific deprivation alongside present pleasure. Warm rice, tents and reciprocal jokes provide actual ordinary value inside compulsory competition. Hirata's frightening stopped threat, Ibuki's imposed shield, Morishita's ambiguous grab and Sudō's protection of Satō require separate accounts of care and coercion. A single favorable or unfavorable trait would lose their differences. V3 [E029](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e029),[E035](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e035),[E041](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e041),[E048](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e048),[E055](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e055)–[E058](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e058).

The monograph amendment remains mandatory for every sufficiently evidenced character. These scenes add history, preferences, written speech and operational constraints, but systematic qualification and individual production remain pending. A joke is not biography, a taunt is not verified quoted memory, a role is not a complete personality, and an absent rescuer is not evidence of accepted protective authority. Models must preserve those limits and carry earlier evidence rather than replace it with the latest scene.

## Knowledge, institutions and the open ending

V3 gives Kiyotaka reason to suspect Shiraishi's prior knowledge; it does not give him the reader's full V2 observer information. Nanase's recruitment history and the unresolved external chain remain inherited. School procedure is both a genuine constraint and something Kiyotaka can use: sensor review and time rules validate his evening tactic. His explicit statement that the tactic depends on exam protections restricts transfer into unrelated simulation settings. V3 [E007](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e007),[E030](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e030),[E049](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e049),[E052](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e052).

The school then controls the boundary of success. A's first VIP wipe and both duelists' OUT are actual. D/C/B/A placement, no expulsion and derived class totals remain forecasts; no selected student, protection use or official final table is shown. The school immediately begins another island exam, with unequal rest and apparently minor water-label choice still unexplained. The institutional author of scarcity has not exhausted its plan when Kiyotaka explains his. V3 [E060](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e060)–[E068](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y3_V03_EVIDENCE_AND_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md#y3v03-e068).

All54 inherited handoff IDs retain their histories. Four strict Year 1 questions remain open; Year 2 answerability remains unanswered at its specific informed, consequential threshold despite meaningful partial observations. LC01–18 and SS01–12 continue in their dated ledgers; council evidence is carried rather than invented. No reconstruction-calibration success or retrospective prediction score is claimed. V3's contribution is a sharper account of how real agency and real care can persist within authored dependence, and of why their existence alone does not establish accountable control of ends.
