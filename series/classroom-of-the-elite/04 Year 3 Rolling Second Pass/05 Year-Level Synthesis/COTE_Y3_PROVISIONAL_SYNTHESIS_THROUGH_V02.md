# Year 3 provisional synthesis — Through Volume 2

**PROVISIONAL SUPERSESSION NOTICE — evidence through Y3V02 only.** This snapshot carries the [V1 synthesis](COTE_Y3_PROVISIONAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_V01.md) and its inherited Year 2 history. Earlier findings retain their source-boundary authority. V3–4 remain unread; this is neither a final Year 3 nor an all-series conclusion.

## From visible competence to the limits of its jurisdiction

V1 made Kiyotaka's exceptional competence publicly persuasive in the polity vacated by Arisu. V2 tests the limits of what that competence can know and own. He correctly interprets a conduct examination, yet illness costs C points. He protects the sick classmates instead of turning score pressure against them, and Hashimoto has already transmitted the no-blame norm. Public authority can therefore generate care and subordinate initiative without becoming a constitution in which the governed possess a demonstrated right to revise its ends. V2 E012–015,E049,E052.

The earlier gap between recognition and answerability persists, but it now has stronger counterevidence against a wholly instrumental portrait. Kiyotaka enjoys helping Yoshida find a book and voluntarily admits an experiential lack. Most substantially, Hiyori becomes affectively particular through an unchosen change in attention and self-understanding. He experiences happiness, disturbed imagined replacement, tentative liking and emerging indispensability. This is new development, not a finding to be backdated into V1. V2 E005,E009,E043–048.

His experiment and disassembly language remains inside that development. The most discriminating next question is consequently not whether the feeling is real enough to count, but whether recognition of another's value becomes an accepted practice of answering to that person. Hiyori's consent to a repaired conversation and gift is local and real. Sufficient disclosure, durable refusal rights and authority over the larger inquiry remain unshown.

## Separation produces different capacities in different polities

Horikita's class now adds independent rule inference and implemented conduct to V1's failed contest and initial recovery. Her continuing fatigue prevents an implausibly complete cure; familiar peer friction and distinct support make action possible. Sudō does not claim to replace Kiyotaka wholesale. Kushida's offered help and Kei's negotiated disclosure have their own authors. Independence becomes more observable as coordination among non-identical contributors. V2 E021–027,E058,E065.

The Kōenji bargain also makes its limits concrete. Horikita purchases compliance for 500,000 private points under terms detached from the accuracy of her hypothesis or others' performance. Her resourcefulness is real, as is Sudō's unresolved fairness objection. Personal payment does not make the class's obligations equal. The current result—A/B tied at +10, C−25, D+50—cannot itself decide which constitution is legitimate. Final totals are A1,240/B1,081/C867/D864, with stable polity identities and rank order unchanged. V2 E026–027,E052.

C displays a different partial development: initiative inside imported leadership. The class has memories, habits and disagreement before and after the new sovereign's arrival. Hashimoto's wish for active belonging exceeds passive insurance, but neither participation nor useful anticipation proves rehabilitation. B's wider consultation and Katsuragi's promised constraint likewise require an actual consequential test. D's disciplined victory and Ichinose's independent reading of reform opportunities strengthen competence without settling future alliance refusal. V2 E014,E028,E036,E039–040,E049,E053.

## Ordinary lives exceed assigned functions

The second volume gives supporting actors projects that need not originate in the class contest: university plans, leisure, reading companionship, confidence, romantic cover, repaired contact and outside work. Shimazaki's black coffee separates use from taste. An oversized bottle and a single flower separate quantity from fitting value. These are small scenes with consequences for how a character is modeled: an observed purchase, reported preference and stable disposition are different claims. V2 E004–008,E034,E038,E043–046,E050.

Their independence does not prevent recapture. Yamamura originates a wish and receives a selected surveillance task. Watanabe seeks romantic cover and becomes the opening for a reform environment. Haruka initiates repair while Kiyotaka supplies comfort partly through deception and anticipates intelligence value. The year therefore supports neither total designer ownership nor an account in which every ordinary scene has already escaped strategy. V2 E035,E038,E050.

Kei's and Haruka's successor relations deserve separate histories. Consultation and chosen address do not restore a romance; a nickname and possible call do not restore the original group. Airi's reported television work confirms activity beyond expulsion without justifying the expulsion. Hiyori has another library companion and a class-conflict reason to withdraw. Each life continues outside Kiyotaka's immediate attention, which is part of why his renewed attention cannot exhaust its meaning.

## Institutions and the unseen observer system

Nanase's hospital memory revises the literal death story and shows sincere affect used in a constructed persona. Eiichirō is alive in the recalled hospital scene; the evidence does not establish every later medical state. Shiraishi's declared observer role and Nanase's preventive-expulsion request show distinct immediate purposes, not a complete common affiliation. Identity, training origin, assignment, sponsor, information channel and personal aim remain separate columns. V2 E001–003,E055–057.

Horikita's hearing provides partial evidence of procedural equality through rejecting A-status privilege and considering future victims. Its third-party-attacker hypothesis remains unresolved. Ishigami then arranges the conditions of her own inquiry, while she retains a serious-report duty and rejects binding wager-like terms. Institutional procedure and environmental authorship operate together. The final retrospective voice signals later involvement without furnishing scene-time White Room discovery. V2 E018–020,E060–063.

The four strict Year 1 questions remain open, and council contestability gains a partial observation. LC-01–18 and SS-01–12 retain their identities in their respective successor ledgers. The reconstruction protocol remains frozen at Year 2; no new simulation calibration or retrospective prediction score is claimed.

The character-monograph guarantee is adopted. These readings and ledgers add history, written voice and preference evidence, but systematic corpus-wide eligibility review and portrait production remain explicit pending work. A bounded volume lock is not completion of that layer. Model readiness will require scenario- and domain-specific evidence, including unknowns, rather than a universal fidelity claim.

The current Year 3 trajectory is that public competence, distributed recovery and particular affect develop faster than demonstrated shared authority over ends. V2 strengthens both sides of that statement: another person has become harder for Kiyotaka to treat as interchangeable, while his language and practice still allow him to turn the discovery into a project he directs. The next volume must be read after this boundary is locked, preserving the distinction between what is now felt, what is chosen together, and what remains to be tested.
