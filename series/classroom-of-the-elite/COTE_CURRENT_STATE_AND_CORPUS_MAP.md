# Classroom of the Elite — Current series state and corpus map

The completed narrative-analysis boundary is **Y3V03**. Year 1 and Year 2 are closed at their recorded boundaries; Year 3 remains provisional. Japanese Y3V01–V04 are inventoried; V1–3 have been read completely, and V4 remains unread. The current user goal authorizes sequential deep reading through Y3V04. Y3V04 is next after the V3 local lock; each volume must be read and its local analysis locked before the next volume supplies narrative evidence.

The series now requires a detailed individual monograph for **every character with sufficient evidence**, covering known personal history, personality, behavior, written speech, preferences, and a bounded operational model. The requirement is adopted; the systematic eligibility review and monograph production have not yet occurred. No character is recorded as qualified or rejected merely by adopting the rule.

## Character-monograph work

The [amendment](00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/COTE_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH_ARCHITECTURE_V1.md) defines qualification, required coverage, claim ownership, storage, and modeling limits. The first substantive monograph operation reviews candidates across the completed corpus, including supporting characters and adults, records evidence and domain gaps, and produces the required portraits for qualifying characters within its declared scope. Pending qualifying characters remain explicit production obligations.

The prospective home is `05 Character Monographs/`. Its local index is created with substantive eligibility assessments and links to actual monographs as they are completed. No index, candidate decisions, monograph files, or simulations are represented as already delivered by this amendment. Future updates to this current map must route that work when it exists and report its real completion state.

Model readiness remains domain- and scenario-specific. Unknown history, preferences, or speech behavior stay unknown; a strong personal portrait is not evidence of universal simulation fidelity. No final Year 3 or all-series synthesis is implied by this new layer.
