# Year 2 — Japanese terminology and load-bearing passages

## 1. Responsibility, quotation and source authority

This index provides exact-language retrieval across the completed Y2SL boundary. It centralizes **43 passage entries** in five required categories. The entries select short source phrases whose speaker, grammar, institutional use or narrative ownership materially affects interpretation. They do not replace the source-local readings, the [written-voice ledger](../03%20Rolling%20Ledgers/COTE_JAPANESE_VOICE_LEDGER_THROUGH_Y2.md), [Y2_09](COTE_Y2_09_JAPANESE_NARRATION_VOICE_GENRE_HUMOR_AND_VISUAL_PARATEXT.md), or the [source lock](../04%20Source%20Maps%20and%20Support/COTE_Y2_SOURCE_INVENTORY_AND_LOCK.md).

Every displayed excerpt was checked as a literal substring of the indicated Japanese source paragraph(s). Ruby annotation nodes are omitted and inter-node whitespace is collapsed; Japanese wording, punctuation and fullwidth forms within the excerpt are otherwise retained. Where multiple snippets are printed, each is separately delimited: intervening prose has not been represented as continuous quotation. Translations are working glosses of those snippets, not substitute translations of whole scenes. NFKC used by the locator parser is a retrieval convention; it does not license silently modernizing a displayed quotation.

`JP-A01` and similar labels are **navigation keys in this index**, not source evidence IDs. When an existing evidence row fully covers the displayed paragraph range, its original ID is supplied. Otherwise the entry explicitly routes to its owning canonical reading and a directly verified source subsection; it does not borrow a nearby micro-route's ID and pretend that it covers the quoted words. The source-local analysis continues to own the interpretation and historical boundary. The [Y2_12 router](COTE_Y2_12_VOLUME_BY_VOLUME_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md) and its source map supply complete filenames, source-byte hashes, original namespaces and the per-volume spine conventions.

No EPUB print pages are invented. Source order remains V01–V08, then Volume 0, then V09–V12.5, then Second List. SL bonus-fiction scene-time precedes its boundary acquisition and must remain distinct from the later endpoint. Year 3 provides no evidence here.

## 2. Term-family retrieval guide

These are lexical and analytical search families, not a claim that every listed form occurs in every linked excerpt. For example, institutional card ownership and ethical ownership of a future require different evidence; the presence of 操作 may refer merely to operating a device. Search finds a surface. Speaker and scene establish its use.

| Required family | High-value forms / distinctions | Passage keys |
|---|---|---|
| Ability and evaluation | 実力, 能力, 評価, 優秀; ability possessed vs displayed/measured; ＯＡＡ / OAA and its categories | JP-A01–A04 |
| Defect and exceptionalism | 不良品 labels a person under dispute; 欠陥 labels a rule here; 天才 and 最高傑作 carry a speaker's criteria | JP-A04–A07 |
| Freedom and control | 自由, 支配, 所有, 操作; chosen life, permission, domination, institutional possession, accounting intervention | JP-B01–B02, B05–B06, B09 |
| Protection and dependency | 保護 / プロテクト, 依存 / 寄生虫, 自立, 選択; support, coercive diagnosis, practical social independence and ability to refuse | JP-B03–B04, B07–B08; JP-C04–C05; JP-D02; JP-E01–E02 |
| Relationship and trust | 信頼, 仲間, 友達 / 友人 / 親友, パートナー, 恋人 / 恋愛 | JP-B08; JP-C01–C02, C06–C09; JP-E10–E11 |
| Particularity and address | かけがえのない versus 必要不可欠; given names, surnames, honorifics, あたし / オレ / 俺 | JP-C03–C05, C07, C10; JP-E09 |
| Institutions | クラス, 満場一致, 退学, クラスポイント, プライベートポイント, プロテクトポイント, 生徒会; ホワイトルーム, 政府直属, 内閣総理大臣 | JP-A02, A05, A07; JP-C02; JP-D01–D06 |

For written voice, do not infer a universal character register from one crisis. An unchanged オレ can contain a new relational permission; a changed surname can coexist with continued attachment. Punctuation, hesitation and typography are written evidence, not measurements of a performed voice. No diagnostic psychological label is inferred solely from a pronoun, metaphor or honorific.

## 8. Translation-sensitive comparisons the index preserves
