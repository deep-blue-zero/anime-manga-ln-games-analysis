# Classroom of the Elite
## Year 2 - Architecture and Volume Progression
### From legibility and jurisdiction to succession, counter-authorship, and the cost of relinquishing control

> **CANONICAL YEAR-2 SPECIALIST SYNTHESIS AT THE Y2SL BOUNDARY**
>
> This document is the primary home for the causal architecture and volume progression of the completed Japanese-primary Year 2 second-pass corpus.
>
> It synthesizes `Y2V01 -> Y2V02 -> Y2V03 -> Y2V04 -> Y2V04.5 -> Y2V05 -> Y2V06 -> Y2V07 -> Y2V08 -> V00 -> Y2V09 -> Y2V09.5 -> Y2V10 -> Y2V11 -> Y2V12 -> Y2V12.5 -> Y2SL`.
>
> The source-local volume readings remain immutable evidence-boundary records. This synthesis may compare, route, strengthen, reframe, and integrate those readings, but it must not silently rewrite what a volume locally established before later revelations arrived.
>
> Volume 0 is retrospective primary fiction and is inserted at the mandated analytical position after Y2V08. It can reveal what was already true, correct earlier causal models, and provide genealogy. It cannot retroactively make earlier characters or earlier readers know what had not yet been disclosed.
>
> `Second List` is a mixed paratext/fiction archive and closes the current Year-2 analytical boundary. It is not a replacement novel and is not treated as omniscient commentary.
>
> Year 3 narrative evidence is excluded.

# 0. Purpose and primary analytical home

This file owns five responsibilities.

First, it identifies the **causal spine of Year 2**. The year contains many exams, first-year actors, White Room revelations, class-point movements, relationship changes, and institutional interventions. The purpose here is not to summarize all of them equally. It is to explain which changes enlarge the governing problem and why the order matters.

Second, it identifies the **architectural job of every volume**. A volume can be narratively eventful without changing the conceptual system very much; another can appear quiet while changing the standard by which all later action must be judged. The decimal volumes and retrospective materials are therefore treated by function, not by presumed secondary status.

Third, it tracks the Year-2 transformation of the major inherited systems:

- ability and institutional legibility;
- Ayanokoji's environmental authorship;
- Horikita's leadership and class succession;
- the four second-year class polities;
- relationship dependency and reciprocal authority;
- ordinary life as counter-curriculum;
- institutional surveillance and adult power;
- truth, records, provenance, and model limitation;
- expulsion and disposability;
- and the difference between development, revelation, succession, and counter-authorship.

Fourth, it routes later specialist work. This file is the primary home for **when and why the year changes**. It is not the final home for every character or ethical judgment. More specialized questions route outward:

# 1. Executive thesis

Year 1 ended with a difficult ethical and epistemic problem.

Ayanokoji had become capable of developmental authorship: he could make other people's strengths visible, alter the conditions in which they acted, preserve futures, produce crises, engineer dependencies, and help people develop. Year 1 also increasingly showed that such skill creates a danger. Understanding another person does not automatically create legitimate jurisdiction over that person's future. The file is not the person. The model is not the person. The developer's desired outcome is not automatically the developed person's purpose.

Year 2 does not abandon that problem.

It **operationalizes it at larger scale**.

The year begins by making students more legible than ever. OAA turns demonstrated ability into a searchable institutional object. Inter-year examinations turn those profiles into markets, risks, prices, and bargaining power. White Room politics simultaneously demonstrates the inverse problem: the most consequential rule affecting Ayanokoji is hidden from the people exposed to it. Transparency increases downward faster than upward.

The early volumes then move through a precise sequence:

> **legibility -> jurisdiction -> attribution -> sovereignty**

V01 asks what happens when people become measurable and targetable.

V02 asks who may bind the measurable person's future.

V03 asks who actually authored an event when plans, identities, records, and causes are deliberately obscured.

V04 asks whose authored environment becomes binding when several competent authors collide.

That sequence changes the year from a simple White Room pursuit story into a political theory of **authored conditions**.

The next movement makes the issue social rather than merely strategic:

> **embeddedness -> responsibility -> distributed agency**

V04.5 establishes that people are embedded in relationships, ordinary routines, reputations, family expectations, friendships, debts, embarrassment, attraction, and social knowledge that no single formal score contains. That embeddedness creates both meaning and leverage.

V05 then forces the class to decide what those relationships mean under sacrifice. Secret ballots preserve individual choice while unanimity creates collective coercion. Horikita makes one of her strongest independent judgments by preserving Kushida. The same choice produces Airi's expulsion and exposes the class to a harsher truth: genuine agency, competent leadership, and ethically good outcomes do not necessarily coincide.

V06 asks whether a class can live after that wound by **redistributing the capacity to act**. Kushida can appropriate an imposed preservation project for her own reasons. Sudo can solve problems without Ayanokoji. Onodera can become Sudo's immediate teacher. Horikita can author an alliance. Ayanokoji begins intentionally reducing his direct operational necessity.

The year then changes the mechanism of control:

> **distributed agency -> staged agency -> relational legibility**

V07 shows a hidden author arranging conditions under which independent motives collide. The people on Ayanokoji's stage are not puppets. Their motives are real. That makes the method more sophisticated and the responsibility harder to assign.

V08 makes people legible to one another as people rather than scores, class roles, or tactical pieces. The school itself deliberately engineers mixed social contact. This broadens human knowledge while also making relational knowledge more politically useful. Care and leverage increasingly emerge from the same informational depth.

At exactly this point, the analysis is interrupted by Volume 0.

Volume 0 supplies the **genealogy of authored contingency**. It reveals the White Room as a political project using education as its medium, Atsuomi's preference-engineering logic, Kiyotaka's non-reproducible interaction between unusual adaptive capacity and an inhuman developmental environment, and the fact that the opportunity to enter ANHS existed inside adult political design. Yet the prequel repeatedly defeats its own strongest deterministic temptation: Yuki smiles, Shiro wants freedom, Kiyotaka generates curiosity and preference, the institution cannot reproduce him, and an opening created for one purpose can produce a life with another purpose.

After that revelation, Year 2 resumes with a more demanding problem:

> **unmodeled agency -> desire -> stakes**

V09 is the volume where development becomes visible as **model failure**. Horikita, Kushida, Sudo, Ichinose, Kiriyama, and others increasingly produce answers that older models did not contain. The best proof of successful development is no longer that the target performs the lesson correctly. It is that the target becomes difficult to predict.

V09.5 locates one source of those unmodeled answers in desire. People want things the institution, leader, rival, or developer did not assign. Ordinary desire is not morally pure, but it creates purposes whose ownership lies with the person who wants them.

V10 turns desire into stakes. Once another person matters, that person can become irreplaceable before the optimizer has language for the irreplaceability. Protection under scarcity exposes what leaders actually value and what their models omitted.

The final movement then tests whether superior recognition can stop becoming jurisdiction:

> **recognition without ownership -> relational jurisdiction -> separation -> counter-authorship**

V11 gives Ayanokoji his clearest positive stopping rule. He can recognize another person's hidden need, create an opening, and then acknowledge that the answer is no longer his territory. Recognition can justify attention without granting ownership.

V12 supplies the darkest counterexample. Direct command becomes unnecessary when trust, love, fear, loyalty, or desire can be modeled accurately enough to make the preferred answer likely. Formal freedom survives. Hidden authorship survives with it. Sakayanagi can genuinely choose the outcome Ayanokoji wants while Ayanokoji still bears responsibility for creating the relational signal he expects her to interpret.

V12.5 then makes the year-end test irreversible. Ayanokoji actually separates from Kei, silently exits Horikita's class, and transfers into the former Ichinose class. Yet separation alone does not prove that development has become independent. The strongest proof comes from **counter-authorship**: the developed person generates an answer the developer did not supply, predict, or own. Ichinose's third answer is the clearest terminal example.

Second List closes the boundary by turning the whole year into an archive of measurements, profiles, rankings, relationships, summaries, and official framing. Its own internal plurality repeats the year's conclusion. Measurements are useful. Profiles are useful. Historical records are useful. None is exhaustive.

The completed Year-2 thesis is therefore:

> **Year 2 transforms the problem of developmental authorship into a problem of succession and counter-authorship. It asks whether people, relationships, and class polities can become capable not merely of functioning after a superior developer leaves, but of producing purposes, judgments, refusals, and futures that the developer cannot legitimately claim as outputs of his own design.**

The year does not produce a clean victory for either autonomy or control.

Ayanokoji becomes more capable of ordinary desire, care, participation, restraint, and bounded intervention.

He also becomes more conscious and more ambitious as an environmental author.

Horikita becomes an independent political actor.

Her class still reaches its Year-2 endpoint inside conditions Ayanokoji partly designed and then deliberately exits.

Ichinose develops counter-authored agency.

Her deepest reciprocal arrangement with Ayanokoji remains ethically entangled with earlier manipulation.

Ryuen's polity gains voluntary legitimacy and delegation without constitutionalizing dissent.

Sakayanagi's polity demonstrates the succession failure of aristocratic optimization.

Kei develops horizontal autonomy while remaining the strongest negative case for informed relational choice.

Ordinary life generates genuine surplus purpose while institutions and strategists increasingly learn how to harvest ordinary-life knowledge as leverage.

The resulting boundary principle is:

> **Influence is unavoidable. Ownership is not. The strongest evidence of autonomy is not isolation from influence but the retained authority to redefine the goal, refuse the prepared answer, surprise the model, and make the more powerful actor answer to a future he did not write.**

# 2. Year 2 has six major movements, one retrospective interruption, and one boundary audit

The fiction sequence is best understood as six movements. Volume 0 is not counted as an ordinary sequential movement because its narrative chronology predates the school story while its analytical position deliberately interrupts the year after V08. Second List is not counted as a fiction movement because it is a mixed paratext/fiction boundary archive.

## Movement I - Legibility becomes jurisdiction
### Y2V01-Y2V02

The year opens by expanding institutional visibility.

OAA does not simply rank students. It changes what information can do. A profile can now be searched, compared, recruited, priced, protected, avoided, or attacked. The first-year partnership exam makes the political consequence immediate because asymmetric penalties turn first-year capacity into bargaining power.

V01's core problem is therefore not whether measurement is accurate. It is whether a useful measure becomes mistaken for sufficient knowledge of the person, and how visibility changes incentives around the measured person.

V02 then moves from the file to the future. Once ability can be made actionable, the next question is who can create binding commitments around it. Contracts, rescue points, transfer possibilities, group structure, student-council office, patronage, and hidden rules all become forms of jurisdiction.

Horikita's development is especially important here. Her leadership begins to shift from direct correction of individuals toward institutional design: equal inter-year agreements, bounded contracts with Koenji, distributed work, and recognition that authority has scope.

Ayanokoji's development is equally double-edged. He increasingly chooses ANHS life for himself and begins protecting more than his own survival, yet he continues shaping other people's environments through selective disclosure and asymmetric knowledge.

Movement I establishes:

> **When people become legible, politics immediately asks who is allowed to do something with the legibility.**

## Movement II - Attribution becomes sovereignty
### Y2V03-Y2V04

V03 turns the island examination into a problem of causal opacity. GPS traces, injuries, tools, movement records, alliances, identities, and testimony create evidence without automatically creating true attribution. A hidden author can design an event so that the visible action and the real causal structure diverge.

V04 escalates from identifying authors to comparing authors. Tsubaki, Sakayanagi, Ryuen, Nagumo, Yagami, Tsukishiro, Ayanokoji, and others create overlapping plans. None simply controls the field. A plan becomes sovereign only if it remains binding when another competent actor refuses its premise.

This is where the year begins consistently preferring **resilient plural structures** over singular command.

Nagumo's third-year polity demonstrates the power and fragility of centralization. Its scale is extraordinary, but disabling the central node can disrupt the whole architecture.

Ryuen's polity becomes structurally more sophisticated because followers can possess reasons, delegation becomes meaningful, and physical defeat does not automatically settle political leadership.

Horikita's cooperation with Ibuki becomes a small but important model of explicit, bounded, contestable coordination.

Ayanokoji defeats Tsukishiro not only through superior physical capacity but by creating independent institutional provenance: witnesses and procedures that hostile record-authorship cannot fully suppress.

Movement II establishes:

> **The strongest author is not necessarily the person with the best private plan. Durable authorship depends on procedures, autonomous actors, alternative sources of judgment, and the ability to survive counter-authorship.**

## Movement III - Embeddedness becomes responsibility, then distributed agency
### Y2V04.5-Y2V06

V04.5 is not detachable social filler. It changes the ontology of the year.

People are not abstract strategic units. They are embedded in ordinary relations, domestic knowledge, embarrassment, friendship, attraction, family expectation, reputation, shared hobbies, small obligations, and remembered acts. This social embeddedness gives school life value that institutional ranking cannot exhaust.

It also gives other people leverage.

V05 takes that embeddedness into a terminal decision structure. The unanimous vote exam preserves private ballots while forcing collective unanimity. It therefore demonstrates that formal individual choice can exist inside intense structural coercion.

Horikita's choice to preserve Kushida proves independent judgment. It also creates an unresolved jurisdiction problem because someone else must now bear the sacrifice. Airi's expulsion reveals the culling branch of environmental authorship: a person can author the final dignified act while a stronger actor has already authored much of the choice environment.

V06 refuses to let the class heal through narrative amnesia. Haruka's grievance remains legitimate. Horikita does not retract her judgment merely because it hurt someone. The class instead begins redistributing agency.

Kushida converts imposed preservation into a self-owned continuing project.

Sudo solves a social problem himself and learns peer-to-peer from Onodera.

Horikita owns the Ryuen alliance.

The class wins an important event while Ayanokoji is operationally absent, even though he still influenced the broader environment.

Ayanokoji explicitly begins thinking in succession terms: training wheels should eventually come off.

Movement III establishes:

> **A polity does not become independent because the hidden solver disappears from one scene. It becomes more independent when useful agency reproduces horizontally among people whose motives the hidden solver does not own.**

## Movement IV - Distributed agency becomes staged agency and relational legibility
### Y2V07-Y2V08

V07 changes the form of environmental authorship.

Ayanokoji increasingly arranges **stages** rather than issuing commands. Yagami's removal is the clearest example. Independent actors bring independent evidence, grievances, memories, authority, suspicion, and motive. Ayanokoji secretly arranges the conditions of convergence.

This produces a difficult causal structure. The participants are not puppets. Their choices are genuine. The stage-setter nevertheless bears responsibility for knowing which independent motives he is deliberately making collide.

The cultural festival gives the same problem a public form. Rivalry can be partly staged while audience attention, spending, embarrassment, creativity, and class image become real.

V08 then makes relational knowledge itself the object. Mixed-group school travel is deliberately designed to make rivals encounter one another beyond class role. The school authors proximity. Students counter-author what the proximity means.

This is an important alternative to total curriculum. A designed environment can create opportunity without fully specifying the answer.

But relational legibility has a dark side. The more deeply another person is known, the easier it becomes to protect them and the easier it becomes to manipulate them. V08's Ichinose scenes sharpen the distinction between sincere comfort and private jurisdiction over when another person is allowed to fail.

Movement IV establishes:

> **Humanization does not eliminate power. Intimacy can increase moral friction while also making intervention more precise.**

## Retrospective interruption - Volume 0
### Inserted after Y2V08 by method

Volume 0 is not the next event in Year 2 chronology. It is a retrospective causal layer inserted after the pre-retrospective local-truth lock of V08.

Its architectural job is threefold.

First, it reveals the White Room as a political project rather than a neutral educational experiment. Education is the medium of a struggle over elite reproduction, political loyalty, developmental control, and institutional competition.

Second, it provides a genealogy for Ayanokoji's preference-sensitive environmental authorship. Atsuomi's political theory explicitly treats superior power as the ability to alter the preference field rather than merely obey stated desire. The White Room designs thresholds, options, social scarcity, and available futures.

Third, Volume 0 defeats simple determinism. Kiyotaka is not a blank child produced according to plan. The institution cannot reliably reproduce him. Yuki and Shiro develop divergent purposes under common conditions. The opportunity to enter ANHS can be politically anticipated without thereby fixing what school life will mean to Kiyotaka.

The correct retrospective transition is therefore:

> **genealogy without determinism**

Volume 0 strengthens causal explanation of Ayanokoji's methods while making it less defensible to treat those methods as his only possible repertoire.

## Movement V - Unmodeled agency becomes desire and stakes
### Y2V09-Y2V10

V09 begins after Volume 0 with a question that now has greater philosophical force: what does successful development look like if the developer's model is itself part of the problem?

The answer is **unmodeled agency**.

Horikita solves without receiving the answer.

Kushida appropriates office for reasons she can own.

Sudo's growth survives rejection.

Kiriyama uses the patronage constitution against the patron.

Ichinose and her class become strategically dangerous because opponents continue reasoning from an obsolete model of them.

Development becomes most visible when prediction fails.

V09.5 asks where those new answers come from. Its strongest answer is desire: inefficient, ordinary, relational, sometimes selfish purposes that the formal system did not assign. Wanting something can become a form of counter-authorship because it inserts a goal whose source is the participant.

V10 then gives those desires stakes. Once attachments matter, leaders discover that contribution models are incomplete. Kamuro becomes irreplaceable before Sakayanagi has a conceptual category for that irreplaceability. Horikita's relation to Ibuki changes her decision field. Koenji distinguishes genuine obligation from purchased obligation. Hiyori identifies the difference between Ryuen's personal brilliance and development of the polity.

Movement V establishes:

> **The model is tested not only by whether it predicts performance, but by whether it can remain answerable when people generate purposes and attachments that the model did not assign.**

## Movement VI - Recognition confronts jurisdiction; separation tests succession
### Y2V11-Y2V12.5

V11 produces Year 2's strongest positive local stopping rule.

Recognition does not create ownership.

Ayanokoji can understand why Yamamura is hurt, arrange contact, and still acknowledge that the next answer is not his territory. He can reopen a future for Amasawa without deciding what that future must contain. Low-stakes exchange-camp activity also demonstrates how much valuable knowledge appears when immediate competitive conversion pressure decreases.

V12 then proves that knowing the stopping rule is not the same as internalizing it.

Relational jurisdiction is more subtle than direct coercion. Another person's attachment can become part of the control surface. Sakayanagi's intelligence and love allow her to infer the outcome Ayanokoji wants. Ichinose's trust can be attacked precisely because it is real. Maezono can be maneuvered through a procedurally valid choice architecture that Ayanokoji knows is disproportionate.

V12 therefore gives the year its strongest negative theorem:

> **Formal choice does not settle autonomy when a stronger actor controls information, timing, dependence, and the relational meaning through which the choice is interpreted.**

V12.5 then converts theory into succession.

Ayanokoji separates from Kei.

He silently leaves Horikita's class.

He enters the former Ichinose class after Sakayanagi's withdrawal creates a leader vacuum in another polity and after the four-class ecology has been reconfigured.

Yet departure is not proof of independence.

The Year-2 endpoint distinguishes three levels:

1. behavior that continues according to the developer's expected lesson;
2. succession competence that can function after the developer departs;
3. counter-authorship that produces a new answer the developer did not supply.

Ichinose's third answer is the strongest terminal case because Ayanokoji cannot simply call it the success of the binary experiment he prepared.

Movement VI establishes:

> **The strongest proof of development is not survival after the developer. It is the ability to become a source of new purposes and new constraints upon the developer himself.**

## Boundary audit - Second List

Second List closes the current analytical boundary by converting Year 2 into an official retrospective archive.

Its data, profiles, class reviews, relationship summaries, timelines, and late fiction do not speak with one evidentiary voice. That internal plurality is productive.

The guidebook confirms that students can be measured.

It also confirms that displayed performance is not total capacity, that outcomes do not prove succession, that relationships compressed into captions still exceed those captions in lived fiction, and that Ayanokoji can prepare conditions without successfully predicting every answer.

Second List therefore stabilizes chronology and official framing while refusing to settle the deepest causal problem.

The Year-2 boundary remains:

> **legibility is real without being exhaustive; influence is real without automatically becoming legitimate ownership; development is strongest where the developed person can answer beyond the model.**

# 4. Y2V01 - legibility becomes a political resource

Y2V01 reopens the series by altering the school's information architecture.

OAA makes performance publicly searchable across year and class lines. This is a genuine increase in legibility, not a sham. The rating can identify real academic, athletic, adaptive, and social differences. Separate annual records can preserve developmental movement rather than silently replacing history.

But visibility immediately changes incentives.

High-scoring first-years become scarce resources in a pairing market. Weak students become risks. Classes can purchase, recruit, protect, price, or avoid people based on public profiles. Ryuen can distort the market; Sakayanagi can spend prestige and points; Ichinose can mobilize trust; Horikita can attempt equal cooperative architecture.

The crucial institutional lesson is:

> **reducing one information asymmetry can create a new power relation around the information exposed.**

Ayanokoji's mathematics 100 demonstrates the difference between capacity and visibility policy. The ability did not newly develop in that moment. His willingness to let the ability enter the public record did.

Horikita's class politics also show the limits of visible constitutional development. Her reasoning against Hosen is coherent inside the information available to her, but the hidden bounty creates a rule environment she cannot see. Ayanokoji's concealed intervention remains the security layer behind visible leadership.

The opening Year-2 paradox is therefore:

> **the governed become more transparent while the most consequential acts of governance remain hidden.**

Architecturally, V01 establishes OAA, inter-year politics, White Room identity uncertainty, persistence of Year-1 development across the year boundary, and the rule that competence is not identity proof.

# 5. Y2V02 - legibility becomes jurisdiction

Y2V02 asks what actors do once visible ability can be organized into contracts, groups, rescue options, and political obligations.

The island exam begins before students reach the island because group composition already allocates risk and future possibility. OAA becomes exchange infrastructure. Rescue points turn wealth into continuation rights. Transfer and group cards price person-specific value.

Horikita's Kosenji contract is structurally important because it recognizes bounded obligation. Koenji can be bound where he explicitly agrees without the class gaining a universal claim over him.

Horikita's student-council objective also changes ownership. The goal increasingly belongs to her rather than merely to Manabu's legacy or Ayanokoji's instruction.

Ayanokoji crosses a clearer developmental-author threshold. He protects, nudges, withholds, and arranges conditions for Kei, Ichinose, Horikita, and others while increasingly valuing ordinary life. His repertoire broadens, but asymmetry remains.

Ichinose and Kanzaki begin converting solidarity from sentiment into political infrastructure. Ryuen treats class membership as tradable capital. Nagumo's institutional project reveals how formal meritocratic language can coexist with personal patronage.

Architecturally, V02 adds the question:

> **Who has standing to bind another person's future, and by what mechanism does obligation become legitimate rather than merely enforceable?**

# 6. Y2V03 - jurisdiction becomes attribution

The first half of the island examination changes the epistemic problem.

Once multiple actors possess plans, hidden identities, private motives, and different information, the analytical task is no longer simply deciding whether an action occurred. It is deciding **who authored which layer of the event**.

V03 therefore trains the corpus against hindsight contamination and single-cause explanation.

Nanase can possess genuine grief and still operate inside an engineered political chain.

Amasawa can plant evidence without every planted clue settling her entire identity.

GPS data can be accurate while causal interpretation remains incomplete.

A record can show position without showing intention.

Ayanokoji's ability to infer hidden structure is impressive without converting inference into omniscience.

The deeper development is that other students increasingly become authors of local environments rather than only targets inside Ayanokoji's plans. The island's large distributed field makes singular control difficult.

Architecturally, V03 establishes:

> **evidence of event, evidence of actor, evidence of motive, evidence of institutional origin, and evidence of causal responsibility must remain separate until the source actually joins them.**

# 7. Y2V04 - attribution becomes sovereignty

V04 asks what happens when the analyst can identify several authors at once.

Tsubaki, Sakayanagi, Yagami, Utomiya, Nagumo, Ryuen, Hosen, Tsukishiro, and Ayanokoji each control some part of the field. No actor possesses total sovereignty.

This produces one of Year 2's governing political tests:

> **A structure is not sovereign merely because it looks dominant while everyone complies. Its real test is what remains when another competent actor refuses its premise.**

Nagumo's third-year command network is powerful but centralized.

Koenji's autonomy is difficult to coordinate but difficult to decapitate.

Ryuen's polity increasingly survives through delegation, follower judgment, and redefinition of what counts as victory.

Horikita's cooperation with Ibuki models explicit bounded coordination rather than covert behavioral ownership.

The Tsukishiro confrontation scales the problem to adult institutional power. Ayanokoji uses procedure and independent witnesses to make a privately controlled extraction environment publicly inspectable. This is not merely combat victory. It is counter-authorship of the record-production environment.

V04 also confirms Amasawa's White Room origin while using her to defeat the assumption that common institutional origin creates common personality or allegiance.

Architecturally, V04 closes the island movement with the principle:

> **distributed counter-authorship is more resilient than singular control, but distributed causation also makes responsibility harder to allocate.**

---

# 8. Y2V04.5 - sovereignty becomes embeddedness

V04.5 interrupts the high-stakes institutional field with ordinary social life, but this is an architectural continuation rather than a retreat.

The question becomes: **what are the people inside these political systems actually embedded in?**

Ordinary life reveals social facts that do not appear in OAA:

- who is comfortable with whom;
- who knows domestic routines;
- who can share embarrassment;
- who understands gifts, food, hobbies, family expectations, and gendered social risk;
- who is becoming capable of friendship outside tactical utility;
- and which relationships generate motives that formal competition did not assign.

The volume therefore deepens the counter-curriculum established in Year 1.

Ordinary life is not simply the absence of manipulation. It is a domain in which **surplus purpose** appears. People can do things badly, inefficiently, privately, humorously, or for no class-point reason at all.

That surplus meaning matters politically because later volumes increasingly convert relationship knowledge into leverage.

V04.5 therefore establishes both sides of embeddedness:

> **relationships create human meaning that exceeds institutional function, and the same relationships can become the most precise map of where pressure will work.**

---

# 9. Y2V05 - embeddedness becomes responsibility

The unanimous vote exam is the Year-2 constitutional tragedy.

Its rules maximize private ballot secrecy while requiring collective unanimity. This creates an institutional paradox: individual will is formally protected even as the class becomes structurally forced to produce one collective answer.

The four classes reveal different constitutions.

Ichinose's class refuses the sacrifice route because solidarity is treated as a substantive principle rather than merely an emotional preference.

Ryuen's class uses managed dissent: opposition can be identified, pressured, and partially preserved without becoming democratic.

Sakayanagi's class pre-solves the problem through centralized optimization.

Horikita's class combines public leadership, hidden support, promises, exposed identities, moral disagreement, and the irreversible sacrifice structure.

Horikita's decision to preserve Kushida is one of the strongest proofs that she is no longer merely executing Ayanokoji's judgment.

The consequence is equally important. Independent judgment can be morally serious and still impose severe harm.

Ayanokoji's use of OAA, social ties, self-assertion, and developmental probability to identify Airi as the easiest loss completes the dark side of Year 2's opening measurement problem. The file does not become the whole person, but increasingly rich person-modeling becomes a disposal technology under scarcity.

Airi's final act remains genuinely hers. The environment that makes the act psychologically likely is heavily authored by Ayanokoji.

Architecturally, V05 establishes:

> **choice does not distribute responsibility evenly. A person can author a final decision while a stronger actor remains answerable for designing the conditions under which that decision became the expected path.**

# 10. Y2V06 - responsibility becomes distributed agency

V06 begins by refusing a clean moral reset.

Haruka remains injured. Horikita still believes she made the correct preservation choice. Ayanokoji still judges the choice suboptimal while deciding that Horikita's independent judgment has value precisely because it can diverge from his own.

The class cannot recover by letting Ayanokoji repair every relationship.

It begins recovering when agency disperses.

Kushida's remaining time becomes self-authored rather than an externally imposed redemption program. She can cooperate for herself without pretending affection or gratitude.

Sudo becomes capable of solving peer problems and learning from Onodera.

Hirata and Kei are pushed away from using Ayanokoji as a universal help desk.

Horikita owns the Ryuen alliance.

The sports festival demonstrates useful class coordination in Ayanokoji's direct absence.

The evidence must be interpreted carefully. Ayanokoji still shapes the environment and treats the event as a test. The class is not proven independent.

What has changed is the **source of motion**. Useful action can now originate from several people.

Ayanokoji's training-wheels metaphor makes succession explicit. His developmental project increasingly aims at a class that can survive his absence and eventually defeat him.

The ethical contradiction becomes sharper:

> **he wants people to escape his necessity while still treating that escape as the desired output of his own experiment.**

Architecturally, V06 establishes distributed agency as a prerequisite for succession but not sufficient proof of autonomy.

# 11. Y2V07 - distributed agency becomes staged agency

V07 formalizes the year around performance.

The cultural festival is a literal stage. The Yagami operation is a causal stage.

Ayanokoji no longer needs to command every important action. He can place a bait, route information, predict likely motives, and allow independent actors to converge.

This makes the other actors more real, not less.

Horikita's handwriting discovery is hers.

Ryuen's pursuit is his.

Nagumo's suspicion is his.

The victims' recognition is theirs.

Amasawa's response to Yagami is hers.

The adults' removal decision is institutional.

Yagami's prior misconduct remains his responsibility.

Ayanokoji authors the convergence conditions.

The method therefore requires **shared causation with asymmetric epistemic responsibility** rather than either puppet-master reduction or authorial innocence.

The Ichinose-class reform offers a more constructive version. Ayanokoji helps create a starting condition and then deliberately refuses to own the continuing reform. Whether that withdrawal is sufficient is still open, but the direction matters.

Haruka and Airi supply an especially important correction. Airi's future outside ANHS becomes evidence that expulsion is not equivalent to ontological destruction. Haruka revises her own assumption rather than simply receiving Ayanokoji's lesson.

Architecturally, V07 establishes:

> **a good stage allows actors to contribute causes the stage-setter cannot simply claim as his own, but stage-setting remains an exercise of power for which the setter is answerable.**

---

# 12. Y2V08 - staged agency becomes relational legibility

V08 is the last local-truth volume before retrospective interruption.

Its school trip deliberately creates cross-class encounters. The school is not merely permitting ordinary life; it is trying to repair a social deficit created by its own competitive design. Students know one another too well as rivals and too little as people.

The institutional solution is another authored environment.

What prevents the result from becoming White Room-like is that the environment does not fully determine what the encounters mean.

Students become closer, remain distant, gather intelligence, form ordinary impressions, discover ambitions, notice invisibility, and experience care or discomfort. The school authors proximity. Students counter-author interpretation.

This produces the concept of **relational legibility**.

Ayanokoji can increasingly read the needs and vulnerabilities of people around him. That capacity makes care more effective. It also makes governance more precise.

The Ichinose material provides the strongest warning. Comfort can be sincere while the comforter privately claims jurisdiction over the timing of the other person's collapse.

Sudo provides the strongest positive development example. The original romantic motive no longer governs his improvement.

Yamamura provides a quieter version: being noticed can matter without the observer owning what recognition must turn into.

V08 also destabilizes the current White Room causal model. Ayanokoji increasingly doubts whether Tsukishiro's behavior fits a straightforward expulsion objective. The correct local endpoint remains hypothesis, not solved conspiracy.

Architecturally, V08 establishes:

> **the more humanly legible another person becomes, the greater both the moral reason to respect the person's future and the strategic capacity to govern it.**

That is the exact point at which Volume 0 enters.

# 13. Volume 0 - the genealogy of authored contingency

Volume 0 reveals that the White Room is political before it is pedagogical.

Its purpose is elite production, ideological alignment, political competition, and the conversion of children into long-term human capital. Atsuomi's governing political intuition is preference engineering: superior power does not merely satisfy stated wants; it shapes the field so that an imposed outcome can become acceptable.

That logic gives a retrospective genealogy to Ayanokoji's environmental authorship.

But Volume 0 also rejects the strongest deterministic model of that genealogy.

Kiyotaka is not born fully formed.

He is not a reliably manufactured output.

The White Room cannot reproduce him at the same level.

Yuki, Shiro, and Kiyotaka develop different purposes under shared conditions.

Emotion is not metaphysically absent; expression becomes causally useless inside the facility.

Ayanokoji's opportunity to enter ANHS is anticipated within adult political design without every later school purpose being reducible to the adults who made the opening possible.

The retrospective rule is therefore:

> **authorship of conditions is not authorship of every meaning generated inside the conditions.**

This distinction prevents two errors.

One error would be to treat school development as proof that the White Room had no durable effect.

The opposite error would be to treat every later intervention, attachment, or preference as merely the execution of White Room programming.

Volume 0 increases causal explanation while preserving contingency.

Its analytical position after V08 is essential because V01-V08 uncertainty remains historically valid even when the reader later learns more.

# 14. Y2V09 - development becomes unmodeled agency

V09 is the first post-Volume-0 sequential volume, and its governing insight is model obsolescence.

The year has produced many skilled modelers. Ayanokoji is the strongest, but Nagumo, Sakayanagi, Ryuen, Horikita, Ichinose, Hashimoto, and others all act through models of other people.

V09 repeatedly rewards the person who has **changed beyond the opponent's model**.

Horikita solves without being given the solution.

Her student-council settlement becomes her own constitution rather than Nagumo succession-by-imitation.

Kushida enters the council after a reason appears that she can appropriate.

Sudo's development survives the original desire.

Kanzaki's opposition begins becoming a genuine internal second brain.

Kiriyama uses the Nagumo system's own authority against Nagumo.

Ichinose becomes dangerous because Ryuen and Sakayanagi continue treating her earlier vulnerability as current state.

This turns development into an epistemic event.

A developmental system succeeds most radically when the result can no longer be compressed into the developer's expected answer.

For Ayanokoji, this is both progress and temptation. He values the surprise while still wanting to be the person who designed the proving field.

Architecturally, V09 establishes:

> **counter-authorship begins where another person becomes a source of information the superior model did not already contain.**

# 15. Y2V09.5 - desire becomes counter-authorship

V09.5 gives positive content to the unmodeled answer.

People want things.

Some wants are inefficient.

Some are ordinary.

Some are relational.

Some are embarrassing, selfish, romantic, domestic, cultural, or difficult to justify through institutional utility.

The yogurt maker matters precisely because cost-performance logic does not settle the desire.

Hiyori matters because Ayanokoji can express a participant-owned desire to spend time with her without manufacturing a crisis or pretending the desire belongs to her.

Kiryuuin's inefficient freedom, Yamamura's wish not to be disposable, Sakayanagi's disruptive love, Ichinose's photograph, and Kei's peer friendship all show purposes not exhausted by formal systems.

The volume does not romanticize desire. Desire can become domination or leverage. Its importance is authorship of ends.

The key distinction is:

> **a person-generated purpose can resist total curriculum even when the person remains embedded in influence, dependency, and strategic systems.**

V09.5 also makes Ayanokoji's internal contradiction unusually explicit. He can enjoy manipulation and still genuinely want ordinary life. Humanization does not automatically suppress the strategic repertoire.

Architecturally, V09.5 establishes desire as one mechanism through which counter-authorship enters the system.

# 16. Y2V10 - desire acquires stakes and protection becomes valuation

Once desire produces attachment, loss becomes meaningful.

V10's special exam forces leaders to protect selectively. Protection becomes a public valuation under scarcity.

The volume distinguishes:

- institutional value;
- strategic value;
- contractual value;
- relational value.

Sakayanagi's Kamuro failure is the central case. Her contribution model treats vulnerable members as strategically comparable until loss reveals that Kamuro had become relationally irreplaceable.

Horikita's Ibuki hesitation shows that friendship changes the cost function without automatically overriding political duty.

Koenji distinguishes genuine reciprocal obligation from an attempt to purchase future compliance.

Ichinose demonstrates that solidarity can become adversarially competent rather than merely protective.

Hiyori identifies the hidden structural weakness of Ryuen's victories: a win that depends upon Ryuen's irregular personal genius may improve Ryuen more than it improves the polity.

Hashimoto identifies a real fiduciary problem in Sakayanagi's private priorities while proposing a dangerous solution: replace one insufficient sovereign with an even stronger one.

Ayanokoji's cross-class project becomes more explicit. He increasingly treats the whole year group as a developmental ecology whose classes should reach maximum potential.

The blank sketchbook and involuntary smile create a counterimage. Technique does not answer what should be created; affect can arise before the plan.

Architecturally, V10 establishes:

> **the value of another person is partly revealed by what becomes costly to lose, and that value may exceed every model that was used to manage the person before the loss occurred.**

# 17. Y2V11 - recognition is not jurisdiction

V11 creates the strongest positive ethical architecture in the year.

Its exchange camp reduces immediate terminal stakes and allows hidden competencies, ordinary play, craft, generativity, social invisibility, and cross-year relationships to become more visible.

Yamamura provides the conceptual center. To be recognized is not merely to be assigned strategic use. A person wants evidence that her existence enters another person's decision field.

Ayanokoji diagnoses the relationship between Yamamura and Sakayanagi and arranges a meeting. The crucial development is his stopping rule: beyond the opening, the answer is not his territory.

A similar structure appears with Amasawa. He can communicate continuing value without dictating what her future must become.

This does not mean Ayanokoji has generalized the rule. He still withholds major decisions from Kei and Horikita. He still thinks in trajectories and experiments.

But Year 2 now has a clear positive criterion:

> **superior understanding can be used to create opportunity while leaving the interpreted person authority over the answer.**

The Sakayanagi-Ryuen wager supplies the political counterexample. Two leaders autonomously choose a duel whose consequences will fall upon classes that did not authorize the wager.

Architecturally, V11 establishes that autonomy for the powerful can itself become jurisdiction over others.

# 18. Y2V12 - formal freedom becomes a medium of control

V12 is the strongest negative test of the V11 stopping rule.

Its central concept is relational jurisdiction.

Maezono's expulsion shows procedurally valid choice inside a deliberately designed terminal condition.

Ichinose's defeat shows how years of genuine trust can become an attack surface when one party controls the hidden meaning of the relationship and the timing of disclosure.

Sakayanagi's official defeat shows the most sophisticated form. She is not commanded. Her intelligence, knowledge of Ayanokoji, and attachment to him allow her to infer the preferred outcome. Her choice remains hers. Ayanokoji's causal authorship does not disappear because he used her autonomy rather than suppressing it.

This is the year's clearest reason to reject formal-choice tests as sufficient ethics.

Ayanokoji can increasingly avoid direct coercion while obtaining similar control through:

- information asymmetry;
- timing;
- predictable attachment;
- prepared contingencies;
- role design;
- and private knowledge of how another person interprets his wishes.

Horikita's 0-10 loss to Ichinose is an important counterpoint. Failure does not erase her leadership development. Her class has become plural enough that Ayanokoji can point her toward comrades rather than presenting himself as the only solution.

Yet he still withholds the coming separation.

Architecturally, V12 establishes:

> **the ethical question is no longer whether another person was allowed to choose. It is how much of the choice architecture was controlled by the actor who later claims to have respected the choice.**

# 19. Y2V12.5 - separation is not proof; counter-authorship is

V12.5 is the fiction-boundary test of everything Year 2 has built.

The volume is aftermath, accounting, separation, and institutional reconstitution.

Maezono's removal remains morally unresolved. Ayanokoji admits that a no-expulsion victory was not impossible, only less probable. The class therefore cannot treat procedure as sufficient justification.

Sakayanagi owns her withdrawal and begins imagining a future outside ANHS. Her former class immediately exposes the constitutional weakness of aristocratic optimization: losing the sovereign produces a succession vacuum.

Ryuen refuses to treat the official result as the victory he wanted. Meaningful rivalry now contains a standard beyond formal result.

Kei performs the independence Ayanokoji hoped to teach while the breakup itself reveals the cost of a relationship whose decisive timetable she did not co-author.

Horikita reaches Class A and possesses genuine distributed competence, yet her first real post-Ayanokoji succession test has only begun when he silently disappears.

Ayanokoji's transfer changes the four-class ecology instead of simply leaving it alone. He removes the training wheels from one class while becoming a new strategic center elsewhere.

Ichinose gives the terminal conceptual answer. Faced with a binary Ayanokoji prepared, she creates a third route and makes the more powerful model update on her rather than simply completing it.

That is why counter-authorship is stronger than mere survival after separation.

A successor can reproduce the old system.

A counter-author can redefine the system's problem.

Architecturally, V12.5 establishes:

> **development survives the developer most convincingly where the developed person can generate an answer that changes what the developer must now understand or do.**

# 20. Second List - the archive becomes a limit on its own authority

Second List converts the completed year into an official retrospective object.

This resembles First File at the Year-1 boundary but the Year-2 analytical problem is more advanced.

The guidebook contains measurement and profile layers that make people highly retrievable. It also contains caveats, class reviews, relational framing, and fiction that repeatedly show why retrieval is not total knowledge.

Second List therefore becomes a meta-example of the year's governing issue.

A record can be useful without becoming sovereign.

A profile can summarize demonstrated traits without settling latent capacity.

A class review can describe an outcome without proving the polity can survive leader loss.

A relationship caption can identify attachment without exhausting lived meaning.

A late checkpoint can summarize Ayanokoji's board preparation while still confirming that another actor generated an answer outside his prediction.

The guidebook's strongest architectural contribution is therefore negative and constructive at once:

> **the archive is allowed to know much without being allowed to become the whole person.**

This closes the current boundary at `Y2SL`.

# 21. The Year-2 progression of ability and `jitsuryoku`

Year 1 established that ability is possessed, developed, expressed, made legible, coordinated, recorded, and modeled.

Year 2 adds several further distinctions.

## 21.1 Ability as publicly searchable state

OAA makes demonstrated ability persistent enough to become political infrastructure.

The key lesson is not that OAA is false. It is that **institutionally legible performance under observation is not latent ceiling or total personhood**.

## 21.2 Ability as market value

Y2V01-Y2V02 make ability tradable. A strong student has different value depending on risk structure, class need, card allocation, social network, and willingness to cooperate.

## 21.3 Ability as availability

Koenji demonstrates that capacity without reliable availability does not equal class-owned strength.

A polity needs ways to coordinate with capability without pretending capability is public property.

## 21.4 Ability as trajectory

Sudo repeatedly proves that a current score may lag current person-state.

Development makes frozen classifications obsolete.

## 21.5 Ability as generative capacity

The most important form of ability becomes the ability to make other people more capable.

Onodera's contribution to Sudo, Horikita's visible leadership, Kushida's social repair, Hirata's integration, and opposition cells inside other classes all demonstrate generative capacity that cannot be reduced to individual OAA rank.

## 21.6 Ability as constitutional capacity

A leader's brilliance is not the same as the polity's ability to function without the leader.

This distinction becomes decisive by V12.5.

Sakayanagi's class possesses enormous accumulated capability and still fails the immediate succession test.

Horikita's class has become less individually elegant but more distributed.

Ryuen's class possesses adaptive leadership without protected succession.

Ichinose's class develops a second brain without fully solving conformity.

## 21.7 Ability as model-breaking agency

V09 and V12.5 add the strongest Year-2 category:

> **a person can be capable because the person can produce a valid answer the existing evaluator did not predict.**

This is ability as counter-authorship.

## 21.8 Ability as politically consumed measurement

Year 2 repeatedly demonstrates that measurement does not merely observe ability. It changes how ability is consumed.

OAA affects recruitment, bargaining, threat perception, disposability, and expectations.

The metric therefore participates in the ecology it claims to describe.

The Year-2 `jitsuryoku` progression is:

> **demonstrated capacity -> public legibility -> market value -> availability -> trajectory -> generative capacity -> constitutional resilience -> counter-authored answer**

The completed-year warning remains:

> **no single score, leader judgment, retrospective file, or predictive model is entitled to collapse those categories into one total ranking of the person.**

# 22. The progression of Ayanokoji's authorship

Year 2 is the year in which Ayanokoji's environmental authorship becomes an increasingly conscious project.

## Y2V01 - self-authored visibility and hidden security

He chooses greater public visibility while continuing to provide concealed enforcement behind Horikita's visible politics.

## Y2V02 - developmental jurisdiction expands

He increasingly arranges conditions for growth, protection, and future behavior across several relationships.

## Y2V03 - information architecture becomes developmental terrain

He operates through incomplete knowledge, causal inference, and the shaping of what others can know.

## Y2V04 - systems authorship

He can affect outcomes by changing central nodes, witness structure, procedure, and institutional inspectability rather than directly controlling each participant.

## Y2V04.5 - ordinary participant mode remains available

His repertoire includes nonoptimization, awkward participation, ordinary friendship, and social curiosity. Later coercion therefore cannot be explained as absence of alternatives.

## Y2V05 - authorship acquires a culling branch

The same person-modeling capacity used to cultivate people can classify and remove the person judged easiest to lose.

## Y2V06 - succession becomes a declared project

He begins deliberately reducing direct necessity and explicitly wants the class to become capable of defeating him.

## Y2V07 - command becomes stage-setting

He designs convergence conditions in which autonomous motives do much of the causal work.

## Y2V08 - relational knowledge deepens jurisdiction risk

He can comfort more precisely because he understands more precisely; the same knowledge allows private claims over another person's developmental timing.

## V00 - genealogy of preference engineering

The White Room and Atsuomi supply a causal history for treating environments, preferences, thresholds, and people as adjustable systems. This is genealogy, not destiny.

## Y2V09 - surprise becomes desired output

He increasingly values actors who exceed his model, but their surprise can still become experimental data.

## Y2V09.5 - declared desire versus hidden curriculum

The contrast between Kei and Hiyori demonstrates that Ayanokoji can either secretly author another person's relationship environment or participate through a declared desire that risks refusal.

## Y2V10 - whole-year developmental jurisdiction

His project expands toward maximizing all four classes, revealing a benevolent purpose with a serious ownership claim hidden inside it.

## Y2V11 - bounded intervention appears

He articulates and practices a stopping rule: recognition does not make the answer his territory.

## Y2V12 - indirect jurisdiction through autonomous choice

He demonstrates that direct command is unnecessary when relationship, trust, timing, and prediction can recruit another person's agency to complete the preferred outcome.

## Y2V12.5 - separation becomes another authored environment

He leaves one class and relationship but does not exit the board. He transfers into another class and repositions the four-class ecology.

Ichinose's counter-authored answer becomes the strongest challenge to his ownership of the experiment.

The completed progression is:

> **hidden security -> developmental intervention -> information architecture -> systems authorship -> culling -> succession design -> stage-setting -> relational modeling -> preference-engineering genealogy -> surprise-seeking -> cross-class ecology -> bounded intervention -> relational jurisdiction -> separation and counter-authored correction**

The Year-2 Ayanokoji problem is therefore not whether he is becoming more human.

He is becoming more human in several genuine ways.

The problem is whether new human capacities become **constraints upon power** or simply make power more relationally informed.

# 23. Horikita's Year-2 architectural progression

Horikita's Year-2 development is one of the corpus's clearest demonstrations that independent judgment and system independence are different claims.

## Y2V01 - reciprocal political design

Her preservation of Kushida is not Ayanokoji's answer.

## Y2V06 - visible leadership and distributed competence

Horikita authors the Ryuen alliance and increasingly presides over a class that can act without direct hidden-solver instruction.

## Y2V07-Y2V09 - constitutional pluralization

## Y2V10 - relational friction enters leadership

## Y2V12 - failure becomes part of leadership

## Y2V12.5 - separation test begins

The Year-2 Horikita progression is:

> **leader with hidden security -> constitutional designer -> consequential dissenter from Ayanokoji -> coordinator of distributed expertise -> office-holder with correctable authority -> leader capable of failure -> polity entering a real succession test**

The boundary conclusion is precise:

> **independence of judgment is established; independence of system remains to be tested after actual separation.**

This is why Year 3 cannot be imported backward to settle the claim.

# 24. The four class polities become succession constitutions

Year 1 established four leadership styles.

Year 2 asks a harsher question:

> **what remains when the leader is wrong, absent, privately misaligned, defeated, expelled, transferred, or no longer the only source of coordination?**

## 24.1 Horikita polity - developmental pluralism under a real separation test

Strengths:

- distributed competence;
- explicit contracts;
- public reasons;
- capacity for correction;
- multiple social and operational nodes;
- growing tolerance of conflict without immediate expulsion of the dissenter;
- and visible leader development.

Vulnerabilities:

- Ayanokoji remained a hidden security and developmental layer through much of the year;
- Koenji remains only partially constitutionalized;
- the class bears unresolved moral residue from expulsion decisions;
- post-transfer resilience is not yet observed at the Y2SL boundary.

Boundary question:

> **Can the polity remain adaptive when the hidden security layer is genuinely external?**

## 24.2 Ryuen polity - adaptive authoritarian legitimacy without protected dissent

Strengths:

- fast model updating;
- ability to recruit expertise;
- meaningful delegation;
- follower loyalty beyond simple fear;
- willingness to redefine the contest;
- tactical creativity under uncertainty.

Vulnerabilities:

- dissent remains unsafe;
- too much value remains concentrated in Ryuen's personal irregularity;
- academic weakness limits pure strategic sovereignty;
- succession is not institutionalized;
- victory can develop the leader more than the polity.

Boundary question:

> **Can a follower meaningfully redirect Ryuen rather than merely advise within the sovereignty he retains?**

This is why `Y1H-026` remains open.

## 24.3 Ichinose polity - solidaristic pluralism with an unfinished second brain

Strengths:

- extraordinary trust and social knowledge;
- preservation without treating people as disposable by default;
- emerging loyal opposition;
- increasing adversarial competence;
- ability to coordinate while presenting a misleading state to opponents;
- and an increasingly counter-authoring leader.

Vulnerabilities:

- conformity pressure remains real;
- dissent is still emotionally costly;
- leader distress can become system distress;
- the second brain is not yet a complete alternative executive structure.

Boundary question:

> **Can solidaristic pluralism remain plural under sustained future conflict without reverting either to passive consolation or leader monopoly?**

## 24.4 Sakayanagi-origin polity - aristocratic optimization and succession failure

Strengths:

- elite judgment;
- rapid coordination;
- strong information processing;
- efficient allocation;
- high baseline talent.

Vulnerabilities:

- insufficient protected dissent;
- private leader priority can override fiduciary duty;
- relational value was under-modeled;
- succession was not reproduced;
- followers developed insurance and betrayal rather than a legitimate internal replacement process.

V12.5 supplies the decisive result:

> **the polity loses its sovereign and immediately reveals that leader excellence had substituted for constitutional succession.**

This answers the old aristocratic-optimization question negatively at the Year-2 boundary.

## 24.5 Comparative Year-2 conclusion

The four constitutions increasingly resemble four theories of independence:

| Polity | Primary coordination logic | Strongest Year-2 gain | Strongest Year-2 succession risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horikita | developmental pluralism | distributed competence and public accountability | hidden security layer has only just exited |
| Ryuen | adaptive authoritarianism | delegation and voluntary legitimacy | no protected challenge to sovereign |
| Ichinose | solidaristic pluralism | loyal opposition and strategic hardening | second brain remains unfinished |
| Sakayanagi-origin | aristocratic optimization | elite coordination and high interpretive capacity | catastrophic leader-private-priority and succession failure |

The year therefore changes the comparison standard.

The question is no longer only which leader is strongest.

It is:

> **which polity can reproduce useful agency without requiring permanent ownership by the exceptional person who made it work?**

# 25. Relationship architecture - from reciprocity to counter-authorship

Year 1 asked whether unequal relationships could become reciprocal.

Year 2 asks whether reciprocity survives superior modeling.

Ayanokoji increasingly understands the people around him well enough to predict what they will do. That makes the strongest relationship test more demanding:

> **Does the other person remain an author after being understood?**

The year develops four increasingly strong relationship states.

## 25.1 Persistence

A relationship survives pressure.

This is meaningful but weak evidence of autonomy. Dependency can persist too.

## 25.2 Role survival

The relation can continue after its original function changes.

Sudo development after rejection and horizontal friendship after group restructuring are examples.

## 25.3 Successor structure

The relationship's useful function can be carried by someone other than the original superior actor.

Peer teaching and plural class networks matter here.

## 25.4 Counter-authorship

The other person can reinterpret the relationship, refuse the supplied choice, define a new purpose, or force the stronger model to update.

This is the strongest Year-2 state.

The major relationships occupy different points.

Kei becomes more socially capable and horizontally supported while the romance remains the strongest negative case for informational reciprocity.

The completed Year-2 ordinary-life thesis is:

This does not make ordinary life politically innocent.

It makes it the strongest recurring domain in which a person can become more than the role a strategic model expected to consume.

# 27. The school as a changing political actor

> **institutional legibility downward grows faster than institutional transparency upward, but ANHS remains internally plural enough that no single adult, student leader, or hidden strategist possesses uncontested sovereignty over the whole school.**

That pluralism is ethically important without making the institution innocent.

# 29. Development versus revelation versus retrospective recontextualization

Year 2 contains unusually high hindsight risk.

The method therefore preserves three distinct operations.

## 29.1 Development

A capacity, motive, relationship, preference, leadership structure, or behavioral repertoire actually changes over time.

Examples include:

- Horikita's increasing constitutional leadership;
- Sudo's self-sustaining development;
- Kushida's self-authored continuing cooperation;
- Ichinose's strategic hardening;
- Ayanokoji's growing ordinary desire and bounded-intervention repertoire.

## 29.2 Revelation of prior state

The reader or another character learns something that was already true.

Examples include:

- Ayanokoji's hidden academic capacity becoming publicly legible;
- Amasawa's White Room identity being confirmed;
- Volume 0 revealing the founding conditions of the White Room.

## 29.3 Retrospective recontextualization

Later evidence changes how an earlier act should be interpreted without claiming the earlier act itself changed.

Volume 0 is the major Year-2 example.

Second List can also provide official retrospective framing.

The critical rule is:

> **later truth does not erase earlier uncertainty.**

A source-local reading may be revised for current interpretation while remaining historically correct about what the evidence allowed at its own boundary.

This distinction is indispensable for White Room identity, Tsukishiro motive, Ayanokoji's developmental genealogy, OAA history, and character growth.

# 30. Year 1 -> Year 2: what the architecture actually changes

Year 2 does not merely continue Year 1 with stronger opponents.

It changes the unit of analysis.

## 30.1 From developmental authorship to succession

Year 1 asks whether Ayanokoji can develop others without owning their goals.

Year 2 asks whether developed people and institutions can **survive his absence**.

## 30.2 From reciprocity to counter-authorship

Year 1 makes reciprocal answer possible.

Year 2 demands more: can the other person generate an answer the superior model did not supply?

## 30.3 From hidden ability to public measurement ecology

Year 1 repeatedly distinguishes ability from recognition.

Year 2 institutionalizes recognition through OAA and shows the political economy created around it.

## 30.4 From class leadership styles to succession constitutions

Year 1 identifies four political models.

Year 2 tests what happens when the sovereign is absent, defeated, privately misaligned, or no longer sufficient.

## 30.5 From ordinary life as counter-curriculum to ordinary life as surplus purpose

Year 1 establishes that life without assigned score matters.

Year 2 shows institutions themselves trying to author social environments while participants continue generating meanings not reducible to the institutional purpose.

## 30.6 From manipulation to relational jurisdiction

Year 1's strongest interventions often involve crisis, fear, leverage, hidden information, or future protection.

Year 2 shows that direct coercion is no longer necessary. Accurate knowledge of attachment can recruit another person's free choice into the design.

## 30.7 From file/person boundary to model/person boundary at every scale

First File demonstrates that the archive is not the person.

Year 2 generalizes the claim:

- OAA is not the person;
- White Room developmental theory is not the person;
- Ayanokoji's psychological model is not the person;
- a leader's contribution model is not the person;
- a relationship role is not the person;
- a polity's current success is not its succession capacity;
- Second List is not the person.

The Year-1 -> Year-2 transformation can be compressed as:

> **developmental authorship -> reciprocal authorship -> succession -> counter-authorship**

# 31. The governing Year-2 tension at the final boundary

The completed year produces a paradox.

Ayanokoji increasingly wants a world that can answer him.

He wants Horikita's class to become strong enough to continue without him.

He values Ryuen as a rival whose development can make defeat meaningful.

He wants all four classes to remain viable.

He is interested in people who surprise him.

He develops ordinary desires whose outcomes he cannot guarantee.

He discovers stopping rules.

He recognizes futures outside the institution.

He is capable of sincere care.

Yet he often remains the person who chooses the **test through which independence must prove itself**.

This is the governing contradiction.

A developer can want the student to surpass him while still claiming ownership over the curriculum of surpassing.

A class can be designed to become independent while its independence remains a planned output.

A lover can be allowed to choose while the relationship timetable is secretly prewritten.

A rival can be preserved for future development without consenting to serve the developer's ecology.

A leader can be allowed to leave while the developer decides which class should inherit his strategic presence next.

Year 2 therefore refuses to define autonomy as successful performance after separation.

Its stronger standard is:

> **Can the developed person force the developer to confront an answer he did not choose?**

Ichinose provides the clearest Year-2 positive case.

The full class-transfer and post-separation consequences remain Year-3 tests and are outside this artifact's source boundary.

# 35. Canonical Year-2 architectural synthesis

The completed Year 2 can be read as a long attempt to answer a problem inherited from Year 1.

If another person can be understood, developed, protected, measured, and made more capable, who owns the resulting future?

The opening volumes answer first through institutions.

The school measures students.

Classes price them.

Contracts bind them.

Hidden actors create rules the measured students cannot see.

The island exam then turns the question into sovereignty. Multiple competent authors collide. The strongest structures are not those with one brilliant plan, but those capable of surviving other people's independent reasons.

The middle year turns sovereignty into social responsibility.

Relationships make school life valuable. They also create leverage. The unanimous vote exam proves that private choice can coexist with structural coercion. Airi's expulsion proves that agency can be genuine inside a severely authored choice environment. V06 responds by spreading the capacity to act across peers, leaders, critics, and relationships rather than allowing one superior solver to repair everything.

V07 and V08 then reveal the mature environmental method. Ayanokoji can create stages rather than commands. The school can create encounters rather than lessons. Participants increasingly author what those environments mean. This is the constructive possibility of environmental design.

Volume 0 makes the danger impossible to ignore. Ayanokoji's skill has a political and developmental genealogy in a system that tried to engineer preference, options, emotional usefulness, and human futures. But the same prequel proves that environments do not own every answer generated inside them.

The late year therefore begins valuing answers the model did not contain.

V09 makes surprise a mark of development.

V09.5 makes desire a source of participant-owned purpose.

V10 makes attachment a source of irreplaceable value that strategic models can recognize too late.

V11 discovers a stopping rule: recognition does not grant jurisdiction.

V12 shows how easy it is to evade that rule while preserving its surface form. A person may choose freely and still choose inside an environment whose decisive information, timing, and relational meaning were engineered by someone else.

V12.5 finally makes separation real.

But Year 2 refuses the cheap equation:

> separation = independence

A class can survive because it still reproduces the developer's curriculum.

A person can behave independently because the developer successfully taught independence.

A rival can remain because the developer preserved the proving field.

A lover can leave because the developer authored the breakup timetable.

These outcomes may all involve real agency. None alone proves that the developer relinquished ownership.

The strongest evidence arrives where another person generates an answer the developer cannot absorb without changing his own model.

That is counter-authorship.

Second List then closes the year with an archive that knows much and still cannot become the whole person.

The Year-2 architectural lock is therefore:

> **Year 2 moves from legibility to jurisdiction, from jurisdiction to authored environments, and from authored environments to the problem of succession. Its deepest developmental achievement is not the production of stronger students or stronger classes, but the emergence of people capable of becoming counter-authors: actors whose purposes, refusals, corrections, and futures can force the superior model to update. Its deepest unresolved ethical problem is that Ayanokoji increasingly desires exactly such independence while continuing to reserve extraordinary authority over the environments in which independence must prove itself.**

That contradiction is the correct boundary inheritance for the remaining Year-2 specialist corpus.
