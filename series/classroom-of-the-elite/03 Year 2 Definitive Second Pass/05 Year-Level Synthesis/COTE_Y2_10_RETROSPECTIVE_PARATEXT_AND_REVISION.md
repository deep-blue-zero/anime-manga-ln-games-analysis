# Year 2 — Retrospective paratext and revision

## 1. Responsibility and boundary

This is the primary Year-2 home for what later evidence changes about earlier interpretation. It consolidates the revision consequences of Volume 0, the two kinds of retrospective knowledge in *Second List*, and the completed specialist layer. It neither supersedes the source-local readings nor creates a second Volume-0 revelation ledger.

The governing sequence remains:

`Y2V01 → Y2V02 → Y2V03 → Y2V04 → Y2V04.5 → Y2V05 → Y2V06 → Y2V07 → Y2V08 → V00 → Y2V09 → Y2V09.5 → Y2V10 → Y2V11 → Y2V12 → Y2V12.5 → Y2SL`.

The central result is an asymmetry. Retrospection gives the reader substantially more knowledge of the origins and purposes of power. It does not give powerful characters total knowledge of the people they shape, nor does it confer the right to determine those people's ends. Volume 0 strengthens the genealogy of environmental authorship; *Second List* strengthens the evidence that environmental authorship does not exhaust personhood. The two additions belong together without canceling one another.

The distinction is especially important for Ayanokōji. An early origin for a capacity is evidence against the claim that the capacity first appeared at school. It is not evidence against every later change in what he wants, whom he recognizes, or when he refrains from using it. Conversely, genuine later attachment does not erase earlier instrumental conduct or prove that the attachment now limits his power in every relationship.

This specialist uses `TF`, `CI`, `IR`, `VF`, `SI`, `IT`, `VJ`, `RC`, and `RR` in the governing sense. The interpretive model in this document is synthesis (`IT`), supported by the routed primary facts and bounded inferences below. Confidence in an event's occurrence is separate from confidence in an exhaustive causal explanation.

## 2. Four temporal positions and three kinds of change

Every retrospective claim needs four positions: when the event happened, when the publication made it available, when it enters this governed reading sequence, and which character could know it. None can silently replace another.

| Evidence | Story position | Governed availability | Knowledge restriction |
|---|---|---|---|
| A locally frozen Year-2 volume | Its own events | That volume's analytical boundary | No later explanation enters its prospective state |
| Volume 0 | Pre-series political and childhood history | After Y2V08, before Y2V09 | Narrated adult and childhood facts are unevenly known; ordinary classmates do not inherit reader knowledge |
| Second List documentary pages | Dated ratings, event summaries, and boundary records | After Y2V12.5 | A published retrospective description is not automatically an in-world disclosure to every student |
| Second List bonus fiction | Earlier Year-2 episodes specified in each story | After Y2V12.5 | The characters experience those earlier scenes then; the analyst acquires the additional evidence at Y2SL |

Three distinct classification systems must also survive consolidation.

**State delta** asks what changed in the person or in our access to the person: confirmed continuity, genuine development, revelation of prior hidden state, retrospective recontextualization, correction of prior inference, relationship-specific expression, or unresolved contradiction.

**Revision action** asks what to do to a proposition: `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT`, or `OPEN`.

**Handoff status** asks whether a particular inherited question has received its required answer: `ANSWERED_BY_DEVELOPMENT`, `ANSWERED_BY_REVELATION`, `REFRAMED`, `PARTIALLY_ANSWERED`, or `STILL_OPEN`.

A new fact can strengthen a partial answer while leaving the handoff status unchanged. An earlier inference can require revision while the earlier scene remains accurately recorded. A relationship-specific expression can be authentic without becoming a portable trait. These are normal outcomes, not inconsistencies requiring a forced global verdict.

### 2.1 What a retrospective correction changes

A correction must name the proposition it replaces. “Volume 0 explains Ayanokōji” is too broad. “The school originated his selective underperformance” is a proposition that childhood concealment can actually revise. “His school concealment therefore has no ordinary-life purpose” is a new, unsupported universal claim.

The responsible form is:

`earlier claim + its original evidence boundary → later admissible evidence → exact revision action → surviving limit`.

The source-local artifact remains the authority for the earlier reading. The through-Y2 ledger and specialist become the authority for the current formulation. This preserves the record of learning instead of manufacturing an analyst who always knew the answer.

## 3. Volume 0: stronger genealogy, bounded explanatory reach

The [Volume-0 retrospective revelation ledger](../03%20Rolling%20Ledgers/COTE_RETROSPECTIVE_REVELATION_LEDGER_VOLUME0.md) owns all 62 detailed transitions and their source locators. Its distribution remains unchanged: 7 confirmations, 22 sharpenings, 10 corrections, 9 recontextualizations, 1 overturning, and 13 additions of prehistory without revision. Its action totals are 13 `PRESERVE`, 26 `STRENGTHEN`, 19 `REVISE`, 0 `DOWNGRADE`, 1 `REJECT`, and 3 `OPEN`. These counts describe the ledger's adjudications; they are not a numerical measure of how much of the earlier analysis was right.

### 3.1 Institutional opposition becomes a political genealogy

`V00-RR-001–007` and `V00-RR-014–016` establish that the White Room is not an ahistorical laboratory outside the politics of ANHS. It originates as a rival elite-formation project within a political struggle, acquires vulnerable children and dependent expertise, and later becomes a durable private power base. `V00-E008–E009` locate the founding comparison at `COTE:V00:spine12:para0208–0237`.

The revision is to genealogy, not to every institutional difference. ANHS still permits forms of plurality, peer-generated purpose, jurisdictional contest, and counterforce that the White Room's total curriculum suppresses. Political entanglement does not establish organizational identity. Equally, institutional plurality does not establish innocence or guarantee that formal records are neutral.

The resulting comparison asks which actors can challenge rules, preserve independent evidence, refuse the assigned purpose, and survive outside a sovereign's favor. This is more discriminating than assigning freedom to one institution and control to the other. Y2_07 remains the primary home for the full institutional comparison.

### 3.2 The institution can author the evidence of its own success

`V00-RR-011` identifies denominator control: failed children can disappear from the account of success (`V00-E038–E039`, `COTE:V00:spine15:para0080–0087`). `V00-RR-028` identifies benchmark curation: later cohorts receive an institutionally selected representation of Kiyotaka (`V00-E103–E104`, `COTE:V00:spine40:para0035–0045`).

These are distinct mechanisms. The first changes who counts as an observed subject. The second changes what comparison object a subject is allowed to see. Neither is simply an incorrect score. The institution controls the conditions under which a score can appear meaningful.

This sharpens the Year-2 record problem without making every record false. A score can be accurately recorded and still support an invalid inference because its population, comparison standard, or collection conditions are hidden. The strongest counterforce is therefore independent provenance, not confidence in a rival narrator alone.

### 3.3 Reproducibility is the narrow overturned claim

`V00-RR-037–039` must be read together. The researchers' claim that broad upper-tier competence is reproducible is distinct from the claim that the exceptional Kiyotaka result is reproducible. The ledger's sole `REJECT`, `V00-RR-038`, rejects the latter, not the existence of trained achievement or all educational efficacy. The controlling routes are `V00-E150`, `V00-E152–E154`, `COTE:V00:spine61:para0014–0032` through their individual evidence rows.

The reader may conclude that the narrated research program does not establish repeatable production of another Kiyotaka. The reader may not turn that limit into a scientific proof that inheritance alone explains him. The source sustains a nature-and-environment problem and exposes the overreach of the institution's preferred explanation. A researcher's assessment also remains an assessment within the fiction, not an externally verified experiment.

### 3.4 Childhood counter-authorship is not mature ethical restraint

`V00-RR-023`, `V00-RR-026`, and `V00-RR-027` show early false-binary recognition, selective performance, and modeling of the testing apparatus. The third answer in a childhood task (`V00-E078–E081`, spine31) and later school-level environmental authorship share a recognizable cognitive structure: he can analyze who constructed the available choices and what that constructor expects.

That is a genealogy of cognitive repertoire. It is not a genealogy of a fully mature ethics. Preserving weaker peers as observational subjects supplies one early reason for underperformance; it does not make the preservation equivalent to reciprocal care. Detecting a hidden test does not itself show respect for another person's right to refuse a test.

The completed year consequently supports both continuity and development. The capacity to exceed an examiner's framing predates ANHS. The purposes to which that capacity is applied, the ordinary experiences it coexists with, and the limits another person may place on it still require later evidence.

### 3.5 Yuki and Shiro prevent an empty-origin account

`V00-RR-024–025` preserve Yuki's taste question and smile as early encounters with preference and expression. They prevent the claim that no relational or affective material existed before school. They do not establish later romantic reciprocity in miniature. Bounded protection in the Yuki episodes, followed by refusal to become a permanent therapeutic anchor, is not a universal theory of care (`V00-RR-030–031`).

Shiro's choice of failure for freedom creates a different limit (`V00-RR-032–033`). The childhood Kiyotaka does not simply share Shiro's desire. The later pursuit of ordinary school life therefore cannot be described as an unchanged childhood preference finally receiving an opportunity. The difference is evidence that a developmental question remains necessary even after origin is known.

The White Room also discovers spontaneous child sociality before formally incorporating it into teaching (`V00-RR-009–010`). Life supplies something the curriculum then attempts to appropriate as its own output. This is the earliest structural version of the completed-year problem: an environment can enable an event without being the sole author of its purpose.

### 3.6 Paternal authorship does not make the son's later ends inevitable

Atsuomi's theory of redirecting desire while preserving satisfaction gives environmental authorship an explicit political formulation (`V00-RR-017`; `V00-E010–E011`, `COTE:V00:spine12:para0253–0268`). His use of conception, family display, and future succession as project proof further clarifies the asymmetry of paternal responsibility (`V00-RR-018–021`, `V00-RR-040`).

The structural comparison with Kiyotaka's later conduct is strong. Direct transmission of that exact doctrine is not established merely because father and son exhibit related methods. Nor does an adult plan for the child establish the child's consent to it. The apparent ease with which a subject performs the assigned role can conceal a disagreement about the role's ultimate end.

### 3.7 The blank year stays incompletely explained

Prepared identity, the withheld task, and the arrangement leading toward ANHS complicate the binary of escape versus completely authored admission (`V00-RR-047–050`; `V00-E164–E166`, spine68). They do not narrate every causal link through the intervening year.

The correct result is not to retrofit every earlier threat as theater. Nanase's deeper function, Matsuo/Eiichirō's full causal role, and the exact scope of Tsukishiro's objective remain bounded by what the Year-2 sources actually establish. `V00-RR-049`, `V00-RR-058`, and `V00-RR-060` retain their `OPEN` actions. Y2_11 must carry the missing conditions rather than supplying a total hidden plan.

### 4.1 Kei: an earlier ordinary future matters at the breakup

The first bonus story, `模擬デート`, is explicitly before the island examination (`Y2SL-E093`). Kei's narration distinguishes proximity from direct words, eye contact, touch, and public recognition (`Y2SL-E094–E097`, `COTE:Y2SL:FIC1:P048–P062`). The kitten-strap gift produces ordinary pleasure while its indirect delivery preserves distance (`Y2SL-E098–E101`, P028 and P070–P082).

This adds historical texture to the relationship's end. The relationship contained a positive ordinary future-image, not dependency alone. Responsive conduct is real evidence even when it avoids exposing emotional intent face to face. Neither fact supplies the missing timely disclosure required for informed consequential choice.

The documentary breakup sequence further preserves cognitive understanding, outward composure, and private grief (`Y2SL-E075`). The revision action is predominantly `STRENGTHEN`: the autonomy model must remain multidimensional. Behavioral independence and horizontal friendship are not disproved by grief; grief is not evidence that the breakup's educational rationale was jointly chosen.

### 4.2 Ichinose: recovery is not the developer's property

The second bonus story, `あの頃から───`, is positioned after the Survival and Dropout exam (`Y2SL-E103`). It supplies three limits on an exclusive developer-subject account.

First, classmates coordinate protection when Ichinose cannot immediately author an answer without endangering privacy (`Y2SL-E105–E107`, `COTE:Y2SL:FIC2:P045–P090`). The class contains distributed relational competence even where its competitive conversion remains weak.

Second, Ayanokōji attributes her recovery to her own power (`Y2SL-E108`, P092). This is a narrator's explicit limit on his claimed causal ownership. It does not deny his influence; it denies that influence exhausts the result.

Third, Amikura speaks as an independent witness to the consequences of that influence (`Y2SL-E109–E112`, P099–P134). She can make a moral appeal without being a strategist who outperforms him. Ichinose's refusal to force Amikura's disclosure adds a local practice of respecting informational boundaries.

The concluding recognition of growth and new discovery (`Y2SL-E113–E114`, P138–P142) supplies an earlier substrate for the V12.5 third answer. The completed-year sequence is not simply an unexpected final move. A person previously regarded as modeled can change enough to reopen the model. The V12.5 fiction owns the late action; `Y2SL-E076` and `Y2SL-E083` supply official retrospective corroboration.

This strengthens counter-authorship without establishing total informed reciprocity or ethical purity. Surprising Ayanokōji does not mean Ichinose knows every hidden purpose. Authoring an independent answer does not make every means used in that answer beyond criticism.

### 4.3 Horikita: preparation has occurred; independence still needs its test

`Y2SL-E027–E028` and `Y2SL-E081–E082` explicitly distinguish preparation for separation from proven post-transfer performance. They support the synthesis of Horikita's real independent judgment and class formation. They do not certify that the polity can already reproduce those achievements after the hidden guarantor leaves.

The retrospective guidebook therefore strengthens the correct temporal boundary. The absence of a post-separation result at Y2SL is not an analytic omission to fill with confidence. It is the condition that makes the next test meaningful.

### 4.4 Ryūen and Sakayanagi: success, legitimacy, and succession separate

The Ryūen class review credits coordination and Katsuragi's arrival while identifying dependence on Ryūen as a developmental risk (`Y2SL-E035–E036`). Growing voluntary legitimacy cannot be simplified into distributed sovereignty. Hiyori's diagnosis matters without already constituting binding moral redirection (`Y2SL-E034`; `Y1H-026`).

The Sakayanagi review records withdrawal, member despair, and no internal successor (`Y2SL-E046–E047`). The arrival of a new strategic center is a real reconstitution of the class, not proof that it independently reproduced leadership. Personal recognition and post-withdrawal futurity can develop while institutional succession fails. The scales must not be averaged into one score for the character.

## 8. Consequences for synthesis and reconstruction

The full-year integration must retain three simultaneously supported propositions. Ayanokōji's environmental control is more historically intelligible. His ordinary participation and relational range have developed. His control is not yet generally answerable to the people it affects. A formulation that deletes any one produces a cleaner but less faithful character.

The same discipline applies beyond him. Horikita can build a polity before its independence has been proven. Kei can have an autonomous social life while emotionally attached and insufficiently informed. Ichinose can become a counter-author without becoming omniscient or ethically uncomplicated. Ryūen can gain voluntary legitimacy without institutionalizing a veto on himself. Sakayanagi can become more personally corrigible while leaving a succession vacuum.

For behavioral reconstruction, later reader knowledge must never become unexplained character intuition. The current model is temporally indexed, relationship-sensitive, and allowed to remain branched. The protocol's hypothetical calibrations test whether these limits are operationally respected; they do not add evidence that a new capacity has occurred. Its later audit must compare all calibration families with the completed specialists and full synthesis, preserving abstention where the handoff remains open.

The proper retrospective effect is therefore greater discrimination, not greater certainty everywhere. Volume 0 supplies origins without destiny. *Second List* supplies a better archive without total ownership. The completed Year-2 model must be strong enough to explain how people are shaped and restrained enough to recognize where those people can still answer beyond the explanation.
