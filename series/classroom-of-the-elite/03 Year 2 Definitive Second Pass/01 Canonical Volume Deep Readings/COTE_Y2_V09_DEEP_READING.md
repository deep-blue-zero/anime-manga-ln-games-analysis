# 『ようこそ実力至上主義の教室へ ２年生編9』
## Year 2 Volume 9 — Second-Pass Deep Reading
### Unmodeled agency, constitutional counter-authorship, and the ethics of learning from people one has already begun to design

> **Analytical boundary:** this document uses the complete original Japanese Year 2 Volume 9, the frozen Year 1 V2 corpus, canonical Y2V01–Y2V08, and Volume 0 only in its architecture-mandated retrospective reader position. Volume 0 may change what the reader can recognize about the genealogy of Ayanokōji's methods; it does **not** grant V09 characters knowledge they do not possess. Y2V09.5, Y2V10–12.5, *Second List*, Year 3, adaptations, wikis, and remembered later outcomes are excluded from V09-local adjudication.

---

# 1. Scope, provenance, and post-Volume-0 reading discipline

`Y2V09` is the first ordinary sequential volume after the mandated Volume 0 retrospective interruption. That makes its evidence protocol different from V01–V08 in one narrow but important respect.

Before Volume 0, the analysis had to preserve uncertainty about the political and developmental prehistory of the White Room. After Volume 0, the reader now knows substantially more about:

- Atsuomi Ayanokōji's political formation;
- the White Room's origin as a politically sponsored counter-project;
- the institutional logic of environmental authorship;
- the sushi/curry model of preference engineering;
- Kiyotaka's childhood relationship to display, observation, curriculum, and other children;
- the prepared outside identity/environment connected to his later exit;
- and the fact that opportunity structures may be authored without fully determining the purposes later created inside them.

But this retrospective knowledge must be used carefully. In V09, Kiyotaka does **not** narrate his own actions by saying that he is reproducing Atsuomi's method. Kei does not know the White Room genealogy of the person testing their relationship. Horikita does not know the political history that makes Ayanokōji's hidden developmental role intelligible to the reader. Ichinose does not know every purpose he has assigned to her future.

The correct analytical separation is therefore:

> **V09-local fact:** Ayanokōji deliberately arranges or withholds conditions, observes other people's responses, and sometimes treats those responses as experiential data.
>
> **Post-V00 reader-level recontextualization:** the form of that behavior resembles a developmental grammar the reader now knows existed in Atsuomi's political philosophy and the White Room's institutional world.
>
> **Not established:** that Kiyotaka consciously imitates Atsuomi, remembers a named doctrine while acting, or shares his father's ultimate political purpose.

# 2. Executive thesis — authored environments begin producing actors the author cannot fully model

The central concept of V09 is **unmodeled agency**.

By this point in Year 2, nearly every major political actor has become skilled at modeling other people.

Nagumo models third-year ambition and converts it into patronage.

Sakayanagi models ability, status, and private desire.

Ryūen models fear, incentives, and weakness.

Horikita increasingly models class capacity and institutional procedure.

Ayanokōji models nearly everyone, often more accurately than they model themselves.

The danger of that competence is easy to state: once another person becomes predictable, the predictor may begin treating the person's response as an input rather than an answer.

V09 repeatedly produces the opposite event. Someone acts in a way that exceeds, invalidates, or complicates the model that was supposed to contain them.

- Horikita solves the cooperative written exam without Ayanokōji giving her the answer.
- Horikita reconstructs Kiriyama's hidden command chain and creates a constitutional settlement Ayanokōji deliberately waits to see whether she can reach.
- Kushida accepts student-council participation only once Horikita stops treating humiliation as a sufficient purchase and instead offers a reason Kushida can appropriate as her own.
- Sudō's development survives romantic rejection and begins reproducing itself in academically weaker classmates.
- Kanzaki's reform cell starts becoming a genuine internal second brain rather than a one-man anti-Ichinose faction.
- Kiriyama uses the very authority and transfer privilege Nagumo's system gave him to constrain Nagumo's future use of that system.
- Ichinose's class survives Ryūen's sabotage partly by allowing him to believe the sabotage is working.
- Ichinose herself becomes strategically opaque to Ryūen and Sakayanagi precisely because they continue reasoning from an older model of her vulnerability.

This is why the best V09 formulation is not simply “growth.” Growth is present everywhere, but V09 is interested in what growth does to **prediction**.

> **A developmental system succeeds most radically when the developed person becomes capable of producing answers the developer did not already possess.**

That proposition also exposes the ethical contradiction at the heart of Ayanokōji's current project. He genuinely wants other people to become independent, surprising, and capable of defeating inherited expectations. Yet he remains fascinated by those surprises as data. He often welcomes unmodeled agency because it makes the experiment more interesting.

Post-Volume-0, that is no longer an ethically neutral character quirk. The reader now has a genealogy for the temptation to treat human development as an engineered environment whose value lies in what it reveals.

V09 therefore asks a harder question than V06's “does development survive the developer?”

> **If the developer values independence partly because independent people generate better data, has he surrendered ownership—or merely upgraded the experiment?**

The volume does not answer that question. It makes it unavoidable.

# 3. Position in the Year 2 architecture

The sequential Year-2 conceptual path now reads:

> **V01 — legibility**  
> **V02 — jurisdiction**  
> **V03 — attribution**  
> **V04 — sovereignty / counter-authorship**  
> **V04.5 — embeddedness**  
> **V05 — responsibility**  
> **V06 — distributed agency**  
> **V07 — staged agency**  
> **V08 — relational legibility / moral friction**  
> **V00 — retrospective genealogy of environmental authorship**  
> **V09 — unmodeled agency / constitutional counter-authorship**

Volume 0 is not another sequential school-year concept in the same way as the numbered volumes. It functions as a retrospective lens inserted between V08 and V09. Its greatest effect is to change what “staged agency” and “environmental authorship” can mean to the reader.

V09 then tests that retrospective knowledge against living school institutions.

Atsuomi's political model was interested in arranging environments so that the desired preference becomes subjectively natural. Nagumo's third-year polity does something recognizably adjacent in political rather than familial form: it makes individual mobility possible, but only by placing the resources of mobility under the custody of one patron and conditioning access on compliance. Horikita's emerging council, by contrast, begins with a case in which legitimacy requires **publicly constraining** the patron's discretionary authority. Ichinose's class reform begins by adding a second cognitive center so that one beloved leader cannot monopolize interpretation. Sudō's growth becomes strongest exactly when it stops depending on Horikita's approval.

V09 therefore develops several possible answers to the same question:

> **What would it mean for a system to produce capability without permanently owning the capable person?**

No single institution yet solves it. But the differences are now politically visible.

# 4. Narrative architecture — seven collisions between prediction and independent answer

## 4.1 Nagumo's monologue — a ruler discovers that supremacy without counterforce cannot authenticate itself

The volume opens inside Nagumo rather than Ayanokōji. That choice matters.

Nagumo remembers being first in academics and sports almost by default. He understands popularity itself as a talent. He has repeatedly accumulated visible proof of superiority. Yet the very ease of that superiority creates a private epistemic wound: if nobody capable of threatening him actually opposed him, what exactly has been proven?

His language arrives at the problem directly. He wonders whether his `実力` is genuine or whether he has been a `裸の王様`—a naked emperor whose dominance has never been properly tested. Manabu refused the contest Nagumo wanted. Kiyotaka therefore becomes more than an object of vanity. He becomes the opponent through whom Nagumo hopes to establish that his own strength is not merely an artifact of the field.

The political implication is larger than Nagumo himself:

> **A ruler may possess enormous power while lacking the counterforce required to know whether the power deserves its own self-description.**

This is one of V09's recurring ideas. Horikita will later receive legitimate authority precisely because other actors can challenge, expose, and constrain what happens under that authority. Nagumo's crisis begins because his older order made counterforce too difficult.

## 4.2 The cooperative written exam — measurement becomes valuable only when development changes the measured person

The second-term special exam is structurally elegant. Every student must contribute a limited number of answers, and the point value of a correct answer rises as the student's OAA academic rating falls. An A-rated academic performer earns only one point per correct response; the weakest institutional categories can earn several times that value.

This does not make OAA false. It changes the political meaning of the measurement.

Earlier volumes repeatedly showed how legibility creates targets. V09 creates a system in which a low score can become **high marginal value** if the student has been developed enough to answer correctly.

Horikita's class is unusually well prepared for this because its lower academic performers have been receiving sustained help. Sudō's growth is the most visible example, but the class has increasingly normalized study structures rather than treating weak students as permanent liabilities.

The special exam therefore asks a question that OAA itself cannot answer:

> **What is the current institutional label worth after people have changed?**

The school's own measurement system becomes the basis of a contest that rewards the class most capable of producing development beneath the measurement.

That is a strong argument against reading OAA as ontology. The score remains administratively real; the person remains developmentally open.

## 4.3 Student-council succession — Horikita does not inherit Nagumo's constitution

Nagumo accelerates the transition to a new student-council president. Horikita becomes the uncontested successor, but V09 immediately refuses to treat office as identity.

Manabu, Nagumo, and Horikita each embody different political failures and possibilities.

Manabu possessed legitimacy grounded in competence and restraint, but could become emotionally and politically distant.

Nagumo expanded opportunity and mobility but personalized the institution around himself, converting office, money, status, and succession into patronage.

Horikita enters with less institutional prestige than either predecessor. Her first real test is therefore not whether she can reproduce their command presence. It is whether she can make the office into a venue where rival claims become **auditable and answerable**.

That test arrives through Kiryūin, Kiriyama, and Nagumo rather than through a formal school examination. This is analytically appropriate: V09's most important exam is political.

## 4.4 Kushida's recruitment — integration becomes legitimate only when continued participation has an internal reason

Horikita's attempt to recruit Kushida into the new council is one of the most important continuations of V05.

The easy reading would be that Horikita “rewards” the person she irrationally saved. The Japanese scene is more exact.

Horikita wants Kushida because Kushida has genuine social intelligence, recognition hunger, and institutionally useful capacity. She also knows that preserving Kushida created a burden of proof: if the class paid an enormous cost to retain her, Horikita must produce results sufficient to justify that decision.

But the recruitment only becomes analytically significant when Horikita refuses the logic of simple purchase.

Kushida challenges her to kneel and perform a humiliating apology. Horikita is willing to do so. Yet she stops short of using that humiliation as a transaction that mechanically buys office participation. Her objection is developmental: if Kushida joins only because a ritual has forced her into reluctant compliance, the resulting officer will lack the motive the role actually needs.

Horikita instead makes the office speak to something Kushida herself owns—status, recognition, influence, and a renewed path toward Class A. Kushida can still say no. She ultimately chooses yes because the role offers real benefit.

This does not retroactively make V05 consensual. Kushida did not choose to be preserved at the point when Airi paid the cost. V09's narrower achievement is:

> **A later future can become self-authored even when the intervention that preserved the possibility was not.**

That distinction is essential to the project's authorship framework.

## 4.5 The Ichinose reform group — solidarity acquires a second brain

Kanzaki's post-V07 reform effort now begins to develop institutional form.

The group around Kanzaki, Himeno, Watanabe, Amikura, and Hamaguchi is not merely a conspiracy against Ichinose. Its central diagnosis is that the class's extreme cohesion has created **epistemic closure**. People trust Ichinose, like Ichinose, and want to protect the atmosphere she created. That affection can make disagreement feel socially illegitimate.

Himeno gives the sharpest experiential version of the problem. She dislikes compulsory social togetherness but has found it easier to remain silent than to become the person who introduces friction. That silence is not evidence that the class is harmonious. It is evidence that harmony can contain suppressed difference.

Kanzaki's proposed answer is effectively constitutional: create another cognitive center around the leader. The class needs people who can give Ichinose opinions she does not already possess. The metaphor of a **second brain** is useful because it avoids replacing Ichinose with Kanzaki. Loyal opposition should not become a new monism.

V09 immediately stress-tests even the reform group. Hamaguchi criticizes Kanzaki's severity. Watanabe does not simply defer to Kanzaki. The opposition itself contains opposition.

This is one of the strongest examples yet of the series' emerging political ideal:

> **plural competence is more resilient than benevolent singularity.**

Ayanokōji helps seed this reform. He also deliberately stops short of becoming its visible leader. That restraint is real. His curiosity about what the class will become remains equally real.

## 4.6 The Kiryūin incident — truthful reports do not guarantee true attribution

The attempted shoplifting frame against Kiryūin is V09's strongest `TRUTH_PROOF_RECORD` case.

The apparent contradiction is simple:

- Nagumo truthfully says he did not order the operation.
- Lower participants can truthfully say they acted under what they understood to be Nagumo's order.

Both statements can be true if the hidden variable is **proxy authority**.

Horikita's task is therefore not merely to decide who is lying. She has to reconstruct the provenance chain that made several sincere descriptions point toward a false author.

Kiriyama is the missing node. As a long-serving vice president and trusted intermediary, he has enough borrowed authority to make subordinates accept a false instruction as Nagumo's without fabricating Nagumo's personal participation.

That structure is politically important because it shows how institutions create causal ambiguity. Power can travel through a role, reputation, office, or intermediary long after the nominal sovereign has spoken.

Horikita solves the case by treating provenance as a system rather than testimony as isolated statements. The result is more than detective competence. It is evidence that she can use an institution to make hidden authorship answerable.

## 4.7 Ichinose versus Ryūen and Sakayanagi — old models become liabilities

The volume's final major movement concerns Ichinose.

Ryūen targets her class because the old model says pressure should work. Her class is academically strong but psychologically vulnerable; Ichinose is emotionally weakened; visible disruption should consume study time and fracture confidence.

The sabotage produces visible distress. Ryūen's information stream therefore tells him the strategy is working.

It is not.

Ichinose's classmates continue producing study time behind the disruption. Some fear is genuine, but they also allow the opponent to read them as more damaged than they are. The class has learned to operate inside an adversarial observer's expectations.

Sakayanagi encounters the same problem at the level of Ichinose herself. She approaches Ichinose as someone who can be warned, redirected, or used. Ichinose detects that motive, rejects Sakayanagi's attempt to define her relation to Ayanokōji, and even turns the interpretive gaze back on Sakayanagi by suggesting that Sakayanagi's own attachment is affecting her judgment.

The common structure is clear:

> **the stronger analyst loses accuracy when she mistakes a prior model of the person for the person herself.**

That is V09's clearest answer to V01's legibility problem. More data does not guarantee better understanding if the model is stale.

# 5. The cooperative written exam — OAA becomes a theory of marginal value rather than total merit

The mechanics of the special exam deserve sustained treatment because they function as an institutional thought experiment.

Every class must coordinate contributions from every student. The strongest academic student cannot simply answer everything. The weakest student cannot simply be hidden. Each person must enter the production process.

The point schedule then gives lower OAA academic ratings greater value per correct answer.

This reverses the ordinary intuitive hierarchy. A correct answer from a student the institution considers weak can be worth more than the same answer from an academic star.

The rule does **not** say weak students are secretly better. It says collective value depends on context.

That distinction matters for `実力 / jitsuryoku`.

If ability means only present individual score, Sakayanagi's class possesses the obvious advantage. Its raw accuracy remains superior. But if ability includes the capacity of a polity to develop, deploy, and coordinate people whose measured weakness can become situational value, Horikita's class has created another kind of strength.

Horikita wins despite lower raw accuracy because she reads the rule as a system rather than a test of prestige. The class alternates ability levels, lets top students complete their own minimum burdens quickly, and uses the transition architecture to communicate question numbers without transmitting answers or hints. The method remains within the stated rules.

The result does three analytical things at once.

First, it vindicates long-term development of low performers without pretending those students have become identical to the high performers.

Second, it distinguishes **measurement from political use of measurement**.

Third, it gives Horikita an independent strategic victory over Sakayanagi.

That last point is crucial. Ayanokōji deliberately does not hand her the solution. He can see the exam's architecture; the question is whether she can see it too.

She can.

The volume therefore moves Horikita from “leader who can sustain disagreement with Ayanokōji” toward “leader who can produce strategically original answers in domains he deliberately leaves to her.”

# 6. Ayanokōji — from hidden solver to observer of systems that may no longer need him

## 6.1 Deliberate non-solution is now part of his method

V09 repeatedly shows Ayanokōji withholding help not because he is indifferent, but because solving the problem would destroy the developmental information he wants.

During the student-council investigation he thinks, in substance: if Horikita asks for help he will extend a hand, but before that he wants to see whether her own thinking can produce an answer.

This is a very different posture from Year 1's early hidden correction of her failures.

It is more respectful because it creates space for independent cognition.

It is also still authored because **he decides when the space exists**.

That duality defines his current developmental ethics.

He is becoming better at not substituting his answer for another person's.

He has not yet surrendered the authority to choose the developmental test.

## 6.2 He is learning that some human competencies are not reproducible through analysis alone

The Ichinose material gives another important limit.

Ayanokōji can analyze social networks, incentives, reputations, and interpersonal probability. But he recognizes that Ichinose possesses a friendship-building competence that is not reducible to a manual. She creates connection through embodied repetition, warmth, memory, attention, and a style of interaction that he cannot simply reconstruct from observing its outputs.

That is another anti-White-Room result.

The issue is not that elite training failed to teach a particular technique. It is that some social knowledge may be **constituted by participating in the relationship rather than representing it from outside**.

This is consistent with V04.5's observation that making friends cannot be put into a textbook and V08's decision to encounter skiing through failure rather than pre-study.

Ordinary life is increasingly valuable because it contains knowledge whose acquisition requires being affected.

## 6.3 Chosen solitude proves that ordinary life has changed the meaning of being alone

V09 includes a quiet but significant distinction between isolation and solitude.

Ayanokōji has time alone and experiences it as something close to bliss. But he specifically distinguishes this from the earlier kind of aloneness produced by having no friends.

The external condition—one person by himself—can be identical while the relational meaning is opposite.

> **Isolation is absence without secure return. Solitude is distance chosen from inside an existing social world.**

That distinction is a compact measure of his ordinary-life development. A person must have relationships before being alone can become a positive option rather than the default state of non-attachment.

## 6.4 His physical decline is ordinary-life evidence, not merely power scaling

At the public gym, Ayanokōji notices that his physical condition has declined from his White Room peak over two school years.

The scene can obviously be read as a power-state note. More importantly, it makes embodiment part of ordinary life. He can choose maintenance, routine, and health rather than treating the body solely as a maximized instrument produced by compulsory curriculum.

The public gym is familiar technology in an unfamiliar social setting. The difference is not equipment. It is ownership of use.

## 6.5 Kei becomes an experimental perturbation

The harshest Ayanokōji material in V09 concerns Kei.

He knows the ordinary reconciliation script. He knows why she is upset. He knows what explanation and reassurance could reduce the conflict.

He deliberately does not use it.

His internal reasoning is explicit: predicting an answer is not the same as actually deriving it through experience. He therefore wants to see what happens in the reverse case. He chooses behavior that will deepen the distance because the response itself is informative.

This is ethically more serious than simple emotional incompetence.

The conflict becomes an **experiment performed inside an existing intimate relationship**.

Three objects of observation are present:

1. what Kei will do;
2. what the relationship will do;
3. what Ayanokōji himself will feel and do.

This means Kei is not merely being used to learn “about women.” Ayanokōji is also testing whether his own interior changes under relationship pressure. That makes the scene psychologically richer and ethically no cleaner.

Post-Volume-0, the reader can recognize the structural resemblance to preference-sensitive environmental experimentation. The crucial difference is that Kiyotaka's stated object is not Atsuomi's political reproduction project. The resemblance concerns **form of control**, not demonstrated identity of purpose.

The ethical judgment should therefore be precise:

> **Ayanokōji is genuinely trying to become capable of relationship. In V09 he pursues that knowledge by withholding available care from the person whose relationship is supplying the lesson.**

That is development purchased with asymmetrically assigned cost.

# 7. Kei — dependency does not erase competence, pain, or horizontal autonomy

V09 is easy to misread if one accepts Ayanokōji's host model as exhaustive description.

He continues to think of Kei as unable to detach because she remains psychologically dependent upon the `宿主 / host`. That model has substantial evidence. V08 already made him her complete psychological pillar, and V09 shows the conflict producing severe distress.

But Kei's point-of-view material prevents the model from becoming total.

She continues studying through the distress. She preserves her public social function. She repeatedly considers how to repair the relationship. She consults Satō rather than existing in a closed dyad. Satō independently judges Ayanokōji's behavior and tells Kei he is wrong to meet Ichinose privately without proper explanation.

This is exactly why the project has kept **vertical dependency** and **horizontal autonomy** separate.

Kei can be deeply dependent on Ayanokōji while still possessing:

- self-discipline;
- friendships;
- social judgment;
- alternative advice;
- and emotional claims that are not merely outputs of his model.

Her anger is also not irrational noise. She wants ordinary disclosure from a boyfriend. The fact that Ayanokōji can explain his motives strategically does not invalidate the relationship norm she is asserting.

V09 therefore strengthens the negative answer to disclosure sufficiency while preserving the positive answer to broader social identity.

It also creates a future test for irreplaceability. Ayanokōji currently predicts that dependency will prevent Kei from exiting. A relationship cannot become reciprocal merely because that prediction is accurate. Reciprocity requires the possibility that the other person can refuse, leave, or redefine the terms without the hidden experimenter assuming the answer in advance.

# 8. Horikita — independent strategy becomes constitutional governance

Horikita's V09 development occurs on two different scales.

The first is strategic.

She solves the cooperative written exam independently and defeats Sakayanagi's class under the exam's actual scoring constitution. The result is not a triumph of raw academics. It is a triumph of class development, rules literacy, and collective deployment.

The second is political.

The Kiryūin/Kiriyama case is her first serious test as incoming student-council president. She must handle:

- conflicting testimony;
- a powerful former president;
- a vice president who abused borrowed authority;
- a victim who used force in investigating the incident;
- third-year resentment;
- class-transfer rights;
- pooled funds;
- and a private political system operating partly inside the school's formal structure.

Her settlement is important because it does not reproduce Nagumo's personal sovereignty.

Kiriyama must acknowledge responsibility and apologize. Kiryūin receives recognition of the wrong. The council does not invent a punishment power it does not possess; instead, a limited period of voluntary absence/reflection is used. Most importantly, Nagumo is prevented from retaliating by revoking Kiriyama's already-earned transfer right, and the third-year pooled funds are constrained to third-year purposes rather than Nagumo's personal inter-year rivalry.

Nagumo ultimately accepts the restriction as a `正論`—a correct argument.

This is precisely the kind of case `Y1H-015` was waiting for.

Horikita does not yet have a long enough record to close the question. But V09 supplies strong local evidence that she can use public authority to **limit discretionary personal authority**, even when the target is the predecessor who built the dominant order.

Her independence from Ayanokōji is also becoming more concrete. He remains nearby, observes, and would help if asked. But the governing answer is hers.

# 9. Kushida — self-authored integration without moral purification

Kushida's post-exposure development continues to resist the usual redemption template.

She does not become nice in the simple sense.

She does not decide that Horikita was right to save her.

She does not lose vanity, recognition hunger, irritation, or hostility.

V09 instead demonstrates that those very motives can be integrated into legitimate institutional participation.

The student-council offer works because Horikita stops requiring moral purity. She identifies a role in which Kushida's existing strengths and desires can produce value:

- social intelligence;
- broad recognition;
- status;
- influence;
- and contribution toward Class A.

That is more sustainable than asking Kushida to become the public persona she now knows is impossible to restore fully.

The crucial legitimacy condition is voluntary appropriation. Kushida has to be able to say, in effect:

> **I am doing this because the future available through it serves a purpose I recognize as mine.**

This is why `Y1H-019` remains answered by development. The later project has become Kushida-authored even though the original act that preserved her was not.

V09 also strengthens `Y1H-014` without closing it. Horikita is increasingly producing benefits from the preservation decision and taking responsibility for proving its value. That does not erase the unchosen danger transferred to Airi in V05. Results can justify confidence in a policy without retroactively consenting the person who paid its earlier cost.

# 10. Sudō — development survives both the developer and the original desire

Sudō is one of the clearest positive cases in the entire Year-2 succession ledger.

His academic growth began partly because of Horikita. Romantic aspiration supplied motive. Horikita's recognition mattered. The class's competitive structure supplied external incentives.

V08 then severed the romantic reward: Horikita rejected him.

V09 shows the growth continuing anyway.

Studying has become enjoyable. Academic competence has become part of his own future planning, including the possibility that professional basketball may not work out. He can now sit with weaker students not merely as another remedial case but as someone whose own transformation gives his advice credibility.

This creates a generative chain:

> **external motive → practiced competence → self-owned motive → peer transmission.**

The original developer is no longer necessary to sustain the behavior.

That is the strongest form of development this project recognizes.

It also gives a useful contrast with Nagumo's mobility system. Nagumo can motivate performance by controlling scarce exit resources. Sudō's current study behavior no longer requires an external custodian to hold the reward.

# 11. Ichinose — desire stops functioning only as vulnerability

V09 is a major Ichinose volume because it does not “darken” her by simply making her more ruthless.

It reorganizes the meaning of her desire.

## 11.1 Resigning from the council is an act of priority, not collapse

Ichinose has long treated public institutional service as compatible with class leadership. By V09 she recognizes that the current crisis of her class requires a different allocation of attention. She resigns from the council while still wanting Class A.

The old shoplifting wound is briefly activated by the Kiryūin framing incident because the language of theft remains painful. But the wound no longer dictates the choice. She does not reverse the resignation, perform repentance through over-service, or stay because she fears how others will interpret departure.

That is self-authored prioritization.

## 11.2 Her relation to Ayanokōji becomes an organizing desire rather than a disabling secret

During the private outing and room sequence, Ichinose sees clear evidence of Kei's place in Ayanokōji's life. She does not interpret that fact as an instruction to stop feeling.

Her position becomes more difficult and more coherent:

- she wants Class A;
- she wants Ayanokōji;
- she recognizes that Kei is presently his girlfriend;
- she does not treat that as permission to expel Kei for personal reasons;
- she intends to become someone who could be chosen in the future.

This is not moral simplicity. Pursuing another person's boyfriend can obviously create conflict. The analytically important point is that desire has stopped automatically producing shame, retreat, or political paralysis.

Ayanokōji himself recognizes the shift and describes the dependence as powerful but different from Kei's. That comparison should not be overextended. Ichinose's dependence currently mobilizes action; Kei's relationship currently creates a different vertical psychological structure. Both remain ethically significant.

## 11.3 The class gains adversarial competence without abandoning solidarity

Ryūen's failed sabotage gives the strongest answer to `Y1H-020` yet.

The class does not defeat him by becoming Ryūen's class.

It does not abandon mutual help, social cohesion, or Ichinose's norm against casually sacrificing classmates.

Instead, solidarity becomes operationally more sophisticated. Students continue creating hidden study time, absorb disruption collectively, refuse bribery and coercion, and allow the opponent to observe enough visible fear to keep his model stale.

The same social density that once made the class vulnerable to shared pain becomes a medium for coordinated resilience.

That is a genuine developmental answer:

> **yes, Ichinose's solidaristic class can acquire adversarial competence without ceasing to be solidaristic.**

The answer is local rather than final. Future conflicts can stress it further. But V09 supplies the missing successful case.

## 11.4 Her ethical boundary remains visible

Ichinose says she will no longer show mercy in pursuing victory and will consider whatever means are necessary. The sentence is intentionally alarming.

The following qualification matters more than generic declarations of ruthlessness. When Ryūen raises Kei, Ichinose refuses the idea of expelling her from personal romantic motive. That would be a different category of act.

So the volume does not present “development” as becoming amoral.

It presents a person trying to become more strategically capable while retaining distinctions about what counts as a legitimate reason.

Whether those distinctions survive future pressure remains open.

## 11.5 The snow incident must not be retrospectively rewritten into a master plan

V09 lets Ichinose reprocess the V08 snow scene strategically. Looking back, she can model how other people would respond and recognize how likely Ayanokōji's arrival was.

That does **not** prove she staged the original disappearance as a calculated manipulation.

The text preserves that original movement as instinctive, desperate, and attached to a very small possibility. What changes is her later capacity to understand the relational field she acted inside.

This is a clean example of **retrospective cognition without retrospective falsification of motive**.

# 12. The Ichinose-class second brain — loyal opposition becomes constitutional pluralism

The reform group deserves treatment beyond Kanzaki because it represents one of Year 2's most promising political structures.

The class's historical strength was mutual trust.

Its historical weakness was that the same trust could make disagreement feel like betrayal.

Kanzaki's earlier response was too individualized. He could see the weakness but lacked a legitimate constituency through which to contest it. V07 began changing that. V09 gives the change architecture.

Himeno contributes social dissent: not everyone experiences constant togetherness as care.

Watanabe and Amikura contribute ordinary peer perception rather than ideological leadership.

Hamaguchi contributes the capacity to disagree with Kanzaki himself.

Kanzaki contributes urgency and strategic critique.

Ayanokōji contributes diagnosis and selective information, then deliberately remains outside the visible structure.

The resulting reform has at least the possibility of becoming something no single person owns.

That possibility is the real importance of the “second brain” concept. A second brain does not replace the first brain; it makes cognition distributable.

The class may therefore be moving from **solidarity as unanimity** toward **solidarity as a polity capable of contradiction**.

# 13. Nagumo — meritocracy revealed as patronage constitution

V09 finally gives enough mechanical detail to answer one of the longest-running Nagumo questions.

His mobility system is real.

That must be stated first because dismissing it as a sham would miss why students accept it.

Lower-class third-years who would otherwise have almost no route to Class A can genuinely receive transfer opportunities. Kiriyama has already secured such a right. Additional students may receive opportunities through pooled funds and a final lottery. Nagumo has therefore created possibilities the ordinary class system closes.

But the institutional form of those possibilities is patronage.

The key elements are:

- most lower-class third-years transfer **75%** of monthly private points;
- participants accept non-opposition / obedience conditions;
- Nagumo centralizes the pooled resources;
- performance and eligibility are mediated through his system;
- transfer resources are delivered only near final class fixing;
- disobedience can jeopardize even an apparently earned transfer right;
- A-class students also contribute;
- and the high extraction rate deliberately prevents rival financial power from accumulating.

This answers `Y1H-030` locally:

> **Nagumo does not produce mobility without patronage. His mobility project is patronage.**

That is not the same as saying it produces no liberation.

The system can genuinely free particular students from collective class failure. It can also intensify competition among people whose access to exit depends on one patron's rules and tolerance.

This is why `Y1H-031` should be **reframed** rather than simply answered “yes” or “no.”

> **Mobility and abandonment are not mutually exclusive outcomes. The same system can liberate selected talent by making other people compete under a centralized scarcity regime.**

The case of Suchi Moeka provides the human cost. Asahina connects growing third-year pressure to a friend who ultimately leaves school after a serious rules violation, while Nagumo appears barely interested in the loss. The exact causal responsibility for the expulsion should not be invented; the political atmosphere is nevertheless relevant.

# 14. Kiriyama — counter-authorship from inside dependency

Kiriyama is one of V09's most interesting political actors precisely because he is not outside Nagumo's system.

He has benefited from it.

He has served inside it.

He has accumulated enough authority that others treat his word as a credible extension of Nagumo's.

He is also increasingly trapped by the system's final form.

His response is not open revolt. It is **counter-authorship from inside dependency**.

He engineers a false-order chain designed to be exposed. Kiryūin is selected because he expects her to detect the frame. The point is not successfully to brand her a thief. The point is to create an incident strong enough to force third-year grievances into a forum Nagumo does not completely control.

This is ethically compromised.

Kiryūin is used without consent.

Other students are induced to participate through false promises.

The plan risks reputational and disciplinary harm.

Kiriyama is therefore not a clean democratic hero.

But the goal is politically intelligible: prevent a centralized fund created from third-year sacrifices from becoming Nagumo's personal wager against Ayanokōji, and force the new council to rule on the patron's discretion.

The most important result is that the plan works **because it can be exposed**.

That makes it almost the inverse of Ayanokōji's ideal hidden authorship. Kiriyama wants the causal chain to become legible once the correct institutional observer reconstructs it.

His plan is covert in execution but publicity-seeking in endpoint.

# 15. Horikita's first constitutional settlement — authority becomes legitimate by making itself answerable

The resolution of the Kiryūin case is one of the strongest institutional scenes in Year 2.

Horikita does not simply identify the guilty party and declare victory.

She has to decide what the council can actually do.

That distinction is the beginning of constitutional thinking.

A ruler asks: what outcome do I want?

A constitutional officeholder must also ask: what authority does this office legitimately possess, through what procedure, against whom, and with what protection against retaliation?

Horikita's settlement respects those limits better than Nagumo's personalized order typically has.

Kiriyama must answer for the deception and apologize.

The response is proportionate to available council authority rather than invented coercion.

Nagumo's pooled resources are bounded to their represented constituency.

Kiriyama's transfer right cannot simply be confiscated as personal revenge.

The victim's grievance is recognized without granting Kiryūin unlimited authority to punish.

The result is not a perfect constitution. It is a first demonstration that Horikita can use public office to transform a conflict among powerful personalities into rules that bind the powerful person too.

That is why `Y1H-015` moves to `PARTIALLY_ANSWERED`.

# 16. Ryūen — a rational strategy fails because the opponent has changed

Ryūen's V09 strategy should not be dismissed as stupidity merely because it fails.

His class lacks the academic developmental runway Horikita's class has built. Under short time constraints, attempting to close the gap through ordinary study would probably be inefficient. External disruption is therefore rational within his available toolset.

The problem is epistemic.

He attacks the class Ichinose **used to lead**.

The visible signals reinforce the old model: disruption works, students appear shaken, the leader has resigned from the council, and the class remains emotionally dense.

Ryūen therefore has reason to believe the pressure is accumulating.

What he does not fully model is that the class has learned to coordinate under being watched.

His defeat is accordingly not proof that ruthless strategy is inherently self-defeating. It is proof that ruthless strategy becomes brittle when its information model stops updating.

To Ryūen's credit, he revises after receiving the result. His direct conversation with Ichinose changes his threat assessment.

That capacity to update remains one of the reasons he is dangerous.

It does not make the sabotage ethically benign.

# 17. Sakayanagi — elite interpretation encounters a person who interprets back

Sakayanagi's most interesting V09 scene is not the exam loss by itself.

She correctly recognizes the exam's scoring structure and accepts that losing under the rules is still losing. She also extracts information from the result: Horikita's class possesses more developmental depth among low-OAA performers than raw academic prestige reveals.

Her greater failure occurs with Ichinose.

Sakayanagi approaches the conversation from a position of interpretive superiority. She believes she can warn, categorize, and potentially redirect Ichinose because she sees more of the strategic field.

Ichinose does something earlier versions of herself were less able to do: she interprets the interpreter.

She asks whether Sakayanagi's warning is itself a control attempt and points toward Sakayanagi's special private orientation toward Ayanokōji.

The moment is small but structurally important.

> **A hierarchy of intelligence becomes unstable when the object of interpretation can model the interpreter's motive.**

That is one of the cleanest forms of counter-authorship available in a social world dominated by hidden models.

Ichinose's assertion that she is not controlled by Ayanokōji should still be treated as a character claim rather than omniscient proof. The project has abundant evidence that Ayanokōji reserves hidden developmental jurisdiction over her future. What V09 establishes is narrower: she is increasingly capable of detecting **other people's attempts** to acquire that jurisdiction.

# 18. Class constitutions — four answers to the same exam

V09 continues the Year-2 transition from “four leaders” to **four political constitutions**.

## Horikita's class — developmental pluralism

Its competitive advantage comes from raising weak members enough that a scoring system can make them valuable. Leadership increasingly distributes teaching, study, and decision-making. Ayanokōji remains a hidden reserve, but the class is becoming less dependent on his active solution.

## Sakayanagi's class — aristocratic optimization

Its raw academic accuracy is highest. It can interpret loss without denying the superiority of its individual performers. Its weakness remains leader-centrality and the risk that Sakayanagi's private priorities distort fiduciary judgment.

## Ryūen's class — adaptive coercive realism

It uses external pressure because its internal academic base gives it fewer short-term options. Its strength is willingness to seek asymmetric tools; its weakness is that coercive models can become stale if the target learns to perform vulnerability.

## Ichinose's class — solidaristic pluralization

Its social density remains the central resource. The new development is that solidarity is acquiring internal contradiction, adversarial camouflage, and a second cognitive layer. The class is becoming more politically capable without becoming a copy of Ryūen's polity.

The special exam therefore measures more than academics.

It reveals which class has converted its history into usable collective structure.

# 19. Truth, proof, record, and provenance — V09's institutional epistemology

V09 repeatedly shows that truth can fail politically even when nobody at the most visible layer is lying.

The Kiryūin case is the clearest example, but the pattern appears elsewhere.

Nagumo's third-year system can truthfully be described as meritocratic opportunity and truthfully be described as patronage. The disagreement concerns which causal layer the observer treats as governing.

Ichinose's class can truthfully appear distressed under Ryūen's pressure while also preserving operational resilience. Visible affect is not a complete measure of strategic state.

Kei can truthfully continue functioning in class while privately experiencing major romantic distress. Public performance is not evidence that the relationship cost is small.

Ichinose can truthfully say she is acting from her own desire while Ayanokōji can truthfully remain an important developmental influence. Influence and self-authorship need not be mutually exclusive.

The epistemic lesson is therefore:

> **A true observation is not yet a complete causal model.**

This is a direct continuation of the Year-1 rule that the file is evidence about the person rather than the person itself.

V09 adds:

> **The causal chain that produced the file may itself be politically authored.**

# 20. Post-Volume-0 retrospective annotations — genealogy without determinism

This section is deliberately separated from the V09-local reading.

Volume 0 changes what the reader can recognize about the form of several V09 actions.

## 20.1 Ayanokōji's Kei test resembles preference-environment experimentation

The resemblance is structural:

- the actor controls part of the environment;
- the subject is not fully informed of the test;
- the response is treated as data;
- the tester is interested in what the subject chooses under altered conditions;
- and the outcome also teaches the tester about the self.

Volume 0 gives the reader an Atsuomi-side genealogy for this way of thinking about people.

But resemblance is not identity.

V09 does not establish conscious imitation, shared political telos, or a remembered doctrine.

The correct transition is `RETROSPECTIVE_RECONTEXTUALIZATION`, not `REVELATION_OF_PRIOR_STATE` about a conscious motive.

## 20.2 Horikita's counter-authorship now has greater philosophical weight

Volume 0 revealed that Atsuomi's preferred environments aim to make outcomes predictable enough to reproduce desired people and preferences.

V09's most hopeful development is therefore not merely that Horikita grows stronger.

It is that she produces answers Ayanokōji **waits to discover**.

If the answer is genuinely unknown to the developer, the developed person has ceased to function merely as execution of the developer's prior solution.

That is why Ayanokōji's desire to be defeated remains meaningful even after Volume 0 complicates the provenance of his school life.

## 20.3 Nagumo is another environmental author, but with a different object

Nagumo does not need White Room ideology to reproduce a related political problem.

He engineers incentives, scarcity, mobility, and obedience so that students rationally orient themselves toward his order. The system works because the available options make compliance attractive or necessary.

This parallel demonstrates that developmental/political authorship is not unique to Atsuomi or Kiyotaka. It is a general problem of institutional power.

## 20.4 V09 strengthens the case for ANHS as an arena of counter-authorship without proving institutional innocence

Volume 0 already destroyed any easy claim that ANHS is pure by origin.

V09 instead provides functional evidence for the more demanding standard established after V00:

- Horikita can constrain Nagumo through procedure;
- Kiriyama can turn insider authority against patron discretion;
- students can build an opposition network inside Ichinose's class;
- Sudō can repurpose an externally initiated development into his own future;
- Kushida can appropriate a later institutional role for her own motives;
- and rivals can surprise the hidden observer.

These do not prove ANHS is liberatory in total. Expulsion, rank scarcity, surveillance, and Ayanokōji's own hidden experiments remain.

They do prove that the school contains **counter-authorship not fully monopolized by its designers**.

# 21. Japanese conceptual vocabulary and register

## `実力 / jitsuryoku`

Nagumo uses the concept as something that requires adversarial proof. The exam uses performance in a way that detaches total value from raw academic ranking. V09 therefore continues to destabilize any one-dimensional translation of `実力` as “merit.”

## `裸の王様 / hadaka no ōsama`

Nagumo's self-question is political as well as psychological: a ruler without credible opposition may be unable to distinguish genuine supremacy from an environment that no longer tests him.

## `人気は才能だ`

Nagumo's claim that popularity is talent should be taken seriously. Social recognition is politically productive capacity in this series. The problem is whether the resulting authority remains answerable.

## `承認欲求 / shōnin yokkyū`

Horikita treats Kushida's desire for recognition not as a defect to be purified away but as a motive that can be routed into institutional work. This is a pragmatic, non-redemptive model of integration.

## `宿主 / shukushu`

Ayanokōji's continued host language toward Kei is important because it shows how strongly he still interprets intimacy through dependency architecture even while wanting to learn ordinary romance.

## `正論 / seiron`

Nagumo's acceptance of Horikita's settlement as a correct argument matters because it converts the conflict from personal contest into a claim that public reason can bind even a powerful patron.

## `容赦しない / yōsha shinai`

Ichinose's declaration that she will show no mercy should not be isolated from the immediate ethical boundary she draws around Kei. The Japanese juxtaposition matters: strategic hardness does not yet equal abolition of reason-sensitive restraint.

## `話が違う / hanashi ga chigau`

Ichinose's refusal to expel Kei for personal romantic motive is a categorical distinction: that would be a different matter. The phrase marks the continued existence of a normative boundary inside her new competitive posture.

## `今はまだ / ima wa mada`

The V09 visual and relational framing repeatedly works through futurity rather than present possession. “Not yet” is the relevant structure: Ichinose does not demand present ownership of Ayanokōji; she imagines a future in which the choice could change.

# 23. Formal and literary strengths

## 23.1 The exam is thematically economical

The OAA-inverted scoring structure is one of the cleaner special-exam designs in Year 2 because mechanics and theme coincide. The class that has treated weak students as developable can convert that work into competitive value. The exam does not need an external speech about human potential; the scoring rule makes the argument operational.

## 23.2 Nagumo receives interiority at the right moment

Opening with Nagumo prevents his later patronage system from reading as one-note villain machinery. His order is connected to a genuine problem of self-authentication. That does not excuse the order. It explains why a person already at the top still needs to manufacture a meaningful opponent.

## 23.3 V09 gives political procedure dramatic weight

The Kiryūin/Kiriyama sequence is unusually important because it lets a student-council hearing function as a real conflict rather than administrative cleanup. The scene turns testimony, authority, money, retaliation, and jurisdiction into dramatic stakes.

## 23.4 Ichinose's development is shown through adversaries misreading her

The novel does not rely only on Ichinose announcing that she has changed. Ryūen's failed model and Sakayanagi's momentary interpretive disruption make the change externally observable.

## 23.5 Ordinary scenes remain analytically productive

The gym, café, dorm room, phone calls, study sessions, and leisure time continue Year 2's argument that ordinary life is not filler around the “real” exams. These are the environments in which dependency, friendship, desire, political information, and self-understanding become legible.

# 24. Critical reservations and counterreadings

## 24.1 Ayanokōji's narration can make ethically invasive behavior sound methodologically neutral

The prose's calmness is part of the character, but the analysis must resist inheriting its moral temperature. Deliberately deepening conflict with Kei to observe the result is not made ethically minor because the narrator describes it analytically.

## 24.2 Ichinose's new strategic capacity risks being overread as total transformation

V09 supplies strong evidence of adversarial competence. It does not prove every earlier disposition has disappeared. Her solidarity, care, and aversion to certain motives remain visible. “Dark Ichinose” would be a reductive summary.

## 24.3 Horikita's first council settlement is promising, not sufficient proof of a mature constitution

One successful case cannot establish how she will behave when her own class interests, attachments, or prestige are directly threatened. `Y1H-015` should move only to `PARTIALLY_ANSWERED`.

## 24.4 Kiriyama's democratic function should not sanitize his methods

He uses Kiryūin and lower-status students as components of a plan they did not fully authorize. His goal of forcing public constraint on Nagumo does not erase the instrumental harm built into the staging.

## 24.5 Nagumo's patronage system should not be flattened into pure tyranny

The system is coercively centralized, but students accept it partly because it creates real mobility. Its political danger comes from combining benefit and dependency, not from benefit being fake.

## 24.6 Post-Volume-0 parallels require restraint

Atsuomi's environmental philosophy is now admissible reader context. It would still be methodologically wrong to label every instance of Ayanokōji staging an interaction as conscious reproduction of his father's doctrine. The archive should preserve genealogy without determinism.

# 25. Historical first-pass revision audit

The historical first-pass Year-2 synthesis broadly described the mid-to-late year as a transition toward Ayanokōji deliberately shaping the conditions under which other major actors evolve, break, or are tested. The Japanese-primary V2 reread preserves that macro-direction but materially revises its emphasis.

| Earlier formulation | V09 disposition | V09 correction / strengthening |
|---|---|---|
| Mid/late Y2 increasingly concerns Ayanokōji shaping other actors' development. | `PRESERVE` | Environmental authorship is unmistakably central, especially in the Kei test and Ichinose-class interventions. |
| Ayanokōji is becoming the experimental force inside the school. | `REVISE` | V09 shows multiple **counter-authors** whose answers exceed his active design. The school is not becoming a one-man laboratory. |

| Nagumo enters his final-stage political positioning. | `STRENGTHEN` | V09 provides the actual constitutional mechanics: extraction, obedience, revocable transfer rights, centralized custody, ranking, and lottery. |

The second pass therefore changes the year-level working thesis from a primarily unilateral model—Ayanokōji develops others—toward a more reciprocal and politically difficult one:

> **Ayanokōji increasingly authors developmental environments, but the success of those environments is producing actors capable of counter-authoring him, one another, and the institutions that made them legible.**

# 33. Final synthesis — development becomes real when it becomes difficult to predict

V09 closes the second term by turning one of *Classroom of the Elite*'s oldest strengths against itself.

The series has always privileged the person who sees more.

Ayanokōji sees hidden incentives.

Sakayanagi sees ability beneath presentation.

Ryūen sees fear beneath social order.

Nagumo sees ambition beneath class loyalty.

The school sees measurements other characters lack.

V09 does not reject superior perception. It asks what happens when the object of perception **changes faster than the model**.

That is why the volume's most consequential victories are epistemic before they are competitive.

Horikita becomes someone whose answer Ayanokōji waits to learn.

Sudō becomes someone whose development no longer needs its original reward.

Kushida becomes someone who can appropriate a future Horikita cannot simply purchase for her.

Kiriyama becomes someone Nagumo's own political system accidentally equips to challenge him.

Ichinose's class becomes capable of showing Ryūen one state while operating in another.

Ichinose becomes capable of looking at Sakayanagi and asking what *her* hidden motive is.

These are not escapes from authored environments in an absolute sense. Every actor remains situated inside school rules, class histories, relationships, scarcity, and prior interventions.

The deeper movement is from **authored person** toward **answering person**.

An answering person can say no.

An answering person can reinterpret the offered goal.

An answering person can continue after the original motive disappears.

An answering person can use the institution differently from the person who built it.

An answering person can become strategically inconvenient to the observer who thought the file was complete.

This gives V09 its strongest connection to Volume 0.

Volume 0 revealed the attraction of a world in which environment can be designed so well that preference itself becomes predictable. V09 shows why that ambition can never be the whole meaning of education. The most important result of development may be the answer the developer **could not have written in advance**.

Ayanokōji increasingly seems to want precisely that answer.

The ethical problem is that he still often wants to be the person who chooses the test through which the answer emerges.

Accordingly, the Year-2 governing question becomes sharper:

> **Can a person who escaped another institution's curriculum create developmental environments that genuinely permit other people to exceed him—or will even their independence remain something he treats as the successful output of his design?**

V09 does not resolve that contradiction.

It gives the contradiction opponents.

That may be the more important development.
