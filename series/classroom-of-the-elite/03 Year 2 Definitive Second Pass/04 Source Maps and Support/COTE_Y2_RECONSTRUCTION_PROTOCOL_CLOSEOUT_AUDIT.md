## Cross-cutting findings

The protocol correctly separates demonstrated support from a narrowly determined outcome. A response can be well supported while several branches remain possible. Its reconstruction-permission ladder also distinguishes portable bounded evidence, a particular context, structural role evidence and abstention. Its voice ladder can decline more quickly than behavioral permission. None of those distinctions is replaced by a numerical confidence score.

The full synthesis's tension between ordinary growth and effective authorship is compatible with the protocol's mode-sensitive inference. Participant behavior in a leisure scene does not certify generalized answerability; harmful crisis intervention does not erase every low-stakes preference. The strongest defense of beneficial asymmetric assistance also leaves the existing consent, refusal and goal-conflict checks necessary. Capacity, likely choice, ethical adequacy and exact wording remain separate questions.

The inherited tracker remains authoritative for all 42 adjudications: 15 development, 3 revelation, 7 reframed, 13 partial and 4 open. The strict opens Y1H-004, Y1H-016, Y1H-026 and Y1H-035 retain their discriminating conditions. The twelve Y2H questions are likewise unanswered at this boundary. No calibration answers them, and apparent narrative coherence cannot convert a partial or open claim into demonstrated development.

The three closeout corrections have no hidden calibration consequence: the younger-cohort point table is not a competing record for Ayanokoji's first year; the V01 companion behind Hosen is Nanase; and the transfer destination is the former Sakayanagi polity. No worked case relies on the erroneous visual identification or cohort attribution, and the role-loss case preserves the correct polity distinction. The repair therefore belongs in routing and correction overlays, rather than an invented recalibration result.

## Minimum changes and release disposition

The protocol keeps all 23 case bodies, R1–R4 history, worksheets, support/determinacy rules, handoff constraints and voice-abstention controls. The bounded update records this completed audit, adds the institutional-character home, routes current specialist/evidence/language findings, and requires the same nonclosure discipline for Y2H-001–Y2H-012. Such a ledger becomes meaningful only when an admissible later source can be compared with a previously frozen prediction; hypothetical calibration alone supplies neither half of a real pair. Later Year-3 bootstrap requires completion and verification of the Year-2 release gate, and this task performs no such narrative analysis.
