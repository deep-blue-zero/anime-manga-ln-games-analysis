## 4. Changes since Year 1

**Continuity:** the central terms already raised questions about agency, ability and legitimacy. **Development:** class and personal capacities change. **Revelation:** OAA operation, identities and mobility architecture become clearer. **Retrospective context:** Volume 0 adds political genealogy; SL contributes distinct fiction, documentary, editorial and interview layers. **Correction:** the SL first-year table is routed to the younger cohort, with both original tables preserved. **Relationship-specific expression:** autonomy, recognition and attachment cannot be measured globally from one dyad. **Unresolved contradiction:** opposing outcomes are retained where real; semantic distinctions must not conceal rather than explain them.
