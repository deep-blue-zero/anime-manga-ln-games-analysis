## 3. Revision discipline and no circular deferral

Each row states its answer here; the primary home supplies the longer argument and exact evidence. **Confirmed continuity** does not imply no development. **Genuine development** requires changed conduct or capacity, not just newly narrated childhood. **Revelation** changes reader knowledge of prior state. **Retrospective context** changes explanation at the later boundary. **Correction** retracts an inference, as in LC-18. **Relationship-specific expression** is essential to LC-03/05/09. **Unresolved contradiction** must remain explicit when evidence conflicts; uncertainty alone is not a contradiction or license to choose a convenient answer.
