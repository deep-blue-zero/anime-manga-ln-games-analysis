# Horikita Suzune
## Canonical character ledger through Year 2 / Second List

# 0. Ledger function

This is a structured Year-2 boundary snapshot, not the full Horikita specialist synthesis. It records state transitions from the frozen Year-1 endpoint, distinguishes genuine development from later revelation, identifies the principal constitutional and relational mechanisms of her leadership, and freezes the questions that cannot be answered until the post-transfer period is actually observed.

The governing Year-1 endpoint was:

> **integrated self-authorship plus distributed, correctable leadership**

Horikita had stopped treating Manabu as a specification she must reproduce. She could absorb influence without treating influence as ownership, delegate capacities she did not personally possess, accept correction, and take public responsibility for consequential judgments. The major unresolved problem was whether this independence could survive while Ayanokoji remained a hidden security architecture underneath the class.

Year 2 strongly validates the first half of that model and deliberately refuses to certify the second half too early.

# 1. Canonical Year-2 boundary state

## 1.1 Horikita is now a genuinely independent political actor

Year 2 contains repeated decisions that cannot be reduced to Ayanokoji supplying a hidden answer that she merely executes.

She:

- designs a reciprocal first-year class agreement in Y2V01 rather than simply imitating Sakayanagi, Ryuen, or Ichinose;
- negotiates a bounded Kōenji bargain in Y2V02 and later respects its actual scope instead of claiming unlimited rights over his ability;
- builds explicit cooperation with Ibuki under conditions where neither woman is required to pretend affection;
- accepts and uses specialists whose capacities exceed her own;
- preserves Kushida in Y2V05 against Ayanokoji's intended solution, despite understanding that the choice may damage her legitimacy;
- converts the preserved Kushida relationship into an explicit, self-interested compact in Y2V06 rather than demanding gratitude;
- authors the Ryuen sports-festival alliance herself and places it in an auditable teacher-observed contract;
- increasingly allows Sudo, Hirata, Kei, Kushida, Onodera, and others to solve local problems without funneling all agency through the leader;
- enters the student-council presidency without treating either Manabu or Nagumo as the constitution she must copy;
- recruits Kushida into the council only after finding a reason Kushida can appropriate as her own;
- makes independent protection decisions in V10 and respects the boundary of Kōenji's prior promise;
- continues leading the class through the V12 year-end exam even after a catastrophic personal defeat against Ichinose.

The mature Year-2 formulation is therefore stronger than "Horikita can disagree with Ayanokoji." She can build institutions, contracts, alliances, and role distributions that remain intelligible independently of him.

## 1.2 Independence of judgment is established; independence of system is not yet proven

This distinction controls the Year-2 boundary.

Horikita's class rises extraordinarily far and reaches the provisional A position. `Second List` records the class at 1233 points and explicitly credits Year-2 growth (`Y2SL-E006`, `Y2SL-E027`). But the guidebook also identifies the post-Ayanokoji period as an unresolved problem and specifically points to Kōenji's voluntarily withheld capacity as an important future variable (`Y2SL-E027-Y2SL-E028`).

That is not a downgrade of Horikita. It is the correct proof standard.

Ayanokoji deliberately spends Year 2 reducing direct intervention, yet he never becomes absent in the strong sense before the final transfer. He still:

- supplies hidden deterrence and enforcement in Y2V01;
- remains a private source of advice, information, and terminal security;
- arranges his own absence in Y2V06 as part of a developmental test;
- stages environments whose results strengthen the class;
- guarantees the V12 exam after Horikita loses her representative match;
- withholds the class-transfer plan until the separation is effectively irreversible.

Therefore the boundary claim must be split:

> **Horikita's judgment is independently authored. The class's ability to reproduce its Year-2 results after the hidden guarantor leaves remains an empirical succession test that Year 2 does not yet contain.**

This preserves `Y1H-012` as strongly advanced but not fully closed by pre-transfer evidence and keeps `Y1H-016` genuinely open: Horikita has still not witnessed Ayanokoji suffer a failure of capability/control that forces her to reorganize her model of him.

## 1.3 Her leadership becomes constitutional rather than merely personal

The strongest development from Year 1 is not simply improved charisma. Horikita increasingly creates **rules of relation**.

### Bounded obligation

The Kōenji bargain is the cleanest example. She does not claim that class membership gives her unlimited rights over his talent. She negotiates a defined exchange. When later pressure makes his help desirable, she returns to the actual scope of the agreement rather than retroactively expanding it. This is a major maturation from early Horikita's tendency to treat superior ability as something that should obviously be made available to collective purpose.

### Auditable contracts

The Ryuen sports-festival alliance is put into a teacher-observed written form. Trust is not treated as a substitute for institutional clarity. Horikita increasingly prefers arrangements whose obligations can be checked after the fact.

### Distributed expertise

Sudo, Hirata, Kei, Kushida, Onodera, and other classmates increasingly exercise domain authority. Horikita's role shifts from personally solving every problem to maintaining an architecture in which the right person can act.

### Legitimate dissent

The Kushida compact is adversarial by design. The left-handed handshake explicitly preserves hostility. Cooperation does not require false affection. The class can therefore include a highly capable member whose motive is self-interest rather than loyalty to Horikita.

### Office without predecessor imitation

As student-council president, Horikita does not simply reproduce Manabu's restraint or Nagumo's patronage. V09's Kiriyama/Nagumo problem becomes a test of whether office can make competing claims answerable rather than converting authority into private discretion.

The Year-2 leadership model is therefore:

> **legitimacy through visible responsibility, bounded obligation, distributed expertise, auditable procedure, and increasing tolerance for motives the leader does not own.**

## 1.4 Kushida is the strongest proof that Horikita can preserve without permanently owning

Year 1 ended with the Kushida question transferred into Horikita's jurisdiction, but without proof that preservation was correct or that Kushida had agreed to Horikita's project.

Y2V05 makes the asymmetry unavoidable. Horikita preserves Kushida against both Ayanokoji's intended solution and Kushida's own immediate preference. The choice is self-authored by Horikita but not consensual for the person preserved. Airi bears the terminal cost.

Y2V06 is therefore the decisive development. Horikita stops asking Kushida to cooperate because it is morally right, because the class needs her, or because Horikita deserves gratitude. Instead she tells Kushida to use her abilities for **herself**. Kushida accepts on explicitly self-interested terms: she will contribute for the remaining school period for her own reason.

That changes the status of the ongoing project.

> **The rescue was Horikita-authored. Continued participation becomes Kushida-authored.**

This is one of the strongest Year-2 examples of development moving from imposed preservation toward legitimate cooperation without requiring emotional reconciliation.

Later student-council recruitment deepens the point. Humiliation is not treated as a sufficient purchase price for Kushida's participation. Horikita gives her a reason involving status, influence, recognition, and Class A that Kushida can genuinely own.

The project therefore answers an inherited Year-1 question affirmatively without retroactively sanitizing V05.

## 1.5 Error becomes part of leadership rather than proof of disqualification

Year 2 repeatedly teaches Horikita that self-authorship does not mean being right.

The most important cases are not tactical mistakes but morally consequential judgments.

- The Kushida preservation decision may be independently hers while still imposing a cost on Airi and Haruka.
- She later tells Haruka that with her current knowledge she would still preserve Kushida and expel Airi, but identifies her earlier irresponsible promise as a genuine mistake.
- Friendship with Ibuki makes clean strategic sacrifice emotionally harder without giving Horikita an easy doctrine for what to do.
- V12 destroys any idea that visible leadership means personal invulnerability: Ichinose defeats her completely in the representative contest.

Horikita's growth is therefore not purification into the always-correct leader. It is increasing capacity to:

1. choose visibly;
2. explain the choice;
3. accept criticism;
4. preserve disagreement;
5. revise the rule or promise that failed;
6. continue leading without requiring proof of infallibility.

This is why "correctable leadership" remains the strongest continuity from the Year-1 ledger.

## 1.6 Her relation to Ayanokoji becomes reciprocal enough to matter, but not symmetrical enough to certify separation

The relationship develops on several levels at once.

### Strategic recognition

Horikita increasingly understands that Ayanokoji is not simply a supporting classmate. His public mathematics reveal, widening reputation, exam performance, and repeated hidden interventions make his scale harder to ignore.

### Consequential disagreement

Her preservation of Kushida is the strongest case. Ayanokoji explicitly thinks the answer is irrational by his model and still allows Horikita's choice to stand. This is not the same as him supplying the answer she chooses.

### Mutual teasing and spontaneous affect

V10's involuntary smile occurs in a low-stakes verbal exchange with Horikita (`Y2V10-E072-Y2V10-E079`). The scene matters because neither the expression nor its immediate meaning is something he consciously authored. Horikita becomes one of the contexts in which spontaneous affect appears rather than merely a developmental target.

### Care

At the V12.5 boundary Horikita can offer ordinary bodily/social care, and Ayanokoji can receive or register it. The relation has acquired ordinary residue beyond class strategy.

### Recognition hunger without submission

`Second List` editorial topology indicates that Horikita wants Ayanokoji's recognition. This should be treated as an attachment vector, not a total motive. The novels have already established decisions that oppose his expectations. Wanting recognition and possessing independent judgment coexist.

### Disclosure failure

The decisive asymmetry is temporal information. Ayanokoji knows the separation project long before Horikita does. She treats him as part of the class's coming future while he is already preparing to leave. When the transfer occurs, she did not receive the information early enough to meaningfully consent to the separation conditions.

This is why Year 2 ends with an unresolved succession test rather than a triumphal declaration of independence.

## 1.7 The class reaches A through plurality, not through a single leader becoming omnipotent

Horikita's Year-2 polity works best when it aggregates heterogeneous forms of ability:

- Hirata: mediation and social stabilization;
- Kei: girls' social coordination and information flow;
- Kushida: extraordinary reach and social memory, now operating under a changed public/private constitution;
- Sudo: self-authored academic/behavioral growth plus athletic leadership;
- Onodera: domain coaching that becomes generative for Sudo;
- Kōenji: enormous voluntarily controlled reserve capacity;
- Ayanokoji: hidden/visible strategic surplus that the class increasingly must learn not to require;
- smaller contributors whose value becomes context-specific under special-exam rules.

The polity's achievement is therefore not "Horikita becomes the strongest person in the room." It is that she increasingly governs a system in which the strongest answer may come from someone else.

That is why the transfer matters so much. The final proof of the polity cannot be another Ayanokoji-authored rescue. It must be whether this distributed architecture continues to generate answers once the hidden guarantor is an external opponent.

# 2. Changes since prior snapshot

## CONFIRMED_CONTINUITY

- The Year-1 model of **integrated self-authorship** remains correct. Manabu's influence persists without functioning as a specification she must copy.
- Leadership remains increasingly distributed and correctable rather than a projection of individual excellence.
- Horikita continues to prefer explicit responsibility over invisible sovereignty.
- She remains willing to pursue difficult integration rather than default immediately to disposal.
- Respect for another person's distinct competence does not automatically become ownership of that competence.
- Her strongest leadership often appears when she can make other people useful **without requiring them to become versions of herself**.

## GENUINE_DEVELOPMENT

- **Inter-year institution building:** Y2V01 reciprocal first-year agreement expands her political scale beyond her own class.
- **Bounded contracting:** Kōenji bargain establishes explicit limits on class claims over individual ability.
- **Adversarial cooperation:** Ibuki and later Kushida relationships prove that legitimate coordination can survive dislike.
- **Consequential independence from Ayanokoji:** preservation of Kushida directly opposes his intended solution.
- **Self-authored external alliance:** the Ryuen sports-festival alliance is Horikita's design, placed into written auditable form.
- **Student-council sovereignty:** she accepts office without reproducing either predecessor's complete model.
- **Institutional recruitment:** Kushida's council role is framed through reasons Kushida can appropriate rather than through humiliation or gratitude.
- **Moral friction from relationship:** friendship makes costs such as Ibuki's possible removal harder to treat as clean variables.
- **Leadership under defeat:** V12 demonstrates she can remain a leader after being personally outperformed rather than equating leadership with never losing.
- **Preparation for succession:** the class operates increasingly through distributed actors even before Ayanokoji's final transfer.

## REVELATION_OF_PRIOR_HIDDEN_STATE

Volume 0 is not primarily a Horikita-character revelation source. Its main effect is to change the reader's understanding of the hidden actor she has been developing beside.

Relevant revelations are therefore contextual:

- Ayanokoji's environmental-authorship grammar has a deeper political/White Room genealogy than Horikita can know.
- His ANHS opportunity was partly architected before he independently assigned school life its later meaning.
- The hidden security layer beneath Horikita's class was produced by a person whose relation to environmental control predates ANHS.

These are reader-level revelations. They do not retroactively grant Horikita knowledge she lacked while making earlier decisions.

## RETROSPECTIVE_RECONTEXTUALIZATION

- Her Year-1 concern about hidden Ayanokoji security becomes more serious after Volume 0 because the reader now knows how deeply environmental control is built into his prior formation.
- Her insistence on visible/auditable leadership becomes a stronger institutional countermodel to both White Room total curriculum and Ayanokoji's hidden-stage methods.
- Her Kushida project becomes an especially important test of whether an initially imposed intervention can transfer ownership to the person preserved.
- Her distributed class architecture becomes the appropriate empirical alternative to centralized developmental sovereignty.

## CORRECTION_OF_PRIOR_INFERENCE

The canonical Year-1 Horikita ledger requires no major rejection. Several stronger possibilities are narrowed:

1. **"Independent judgment already proves the class can function without Ayanokoji" - REJECT.** Year 2 carefully separates judgment independence from system independence.
2. **"Preserving Kushida establishes a categorical never-expel principle" - REJECT.** V05-V06 show a contextual decision whose ongoing legitimacy must be earned through results and self-authored participation.
3. **"Wanting Ayanokoji's recognition makes her leadership derivative" - REJECT.** Recognition desire coexists with consequential disagreement and independent institutional authorship.

## RELATIONSHIP_SPECIFIC_EXPRESSION

### Ayanokoji Kiyotaka

He is simultaneously collaborator, hidden guarantor, developmental observer, affective peer, and eventual external opponent. Horikita can use his advice without treating it as command and can choose against his preferred answer. The relationship becomes more reciprocal in ordinary life while remaining radically asymmetric in information about the coming transfer.

### Kushida Kikyo

The relationship moves from unilateral rehabilitation project to adversarial/self-interested cooperation. Horikita's mature achievement is not making Kushida like her. It is creating a political relation in which Kushida can contribute for a reason Kushida owns.

### Ibuki Mio

Ibuki becomes one of Horikita's clearest cases of relation emerging without ideological compatibility. Competition, irritation, physical challenge, assistance, and ordinary familiarity create real moral friction. The relation demonstrates that friendship need not begin as declared affection.

### Sudo Ken

Sudo increasingly ceases to be a direct "Horikita improvement project." His study, restraint, advice-seeking, and peer learning become self-sustaining. This is exactly the kind of generativity a mature leader should want: development that no longer requires the original motivator's approval.

### Hirata Yosuke

Hirata remains a complementary social center rather than a subordinate deputy. Horikita increasingly allows him to mediate domains in which his social instincts are better than hers while retaining visible responsibility for class-wide decisions.

### Kei Karuizawa

Kei provides social coordination Horikita does not naturally reproduce. The class architecture benefits from treating that role as independent expertise rather than merely "Ayanokoji's girlfriend."

### Koenji Rokusuke

Kōenji is the sharpest test of membership without ownership. Horikita can negotiate with him, benefit from him, and hold him to explicit obligations without pretending class membership gives her command of his talent. `Second List` makes his post-transfer contribution a future class problem rather than assuming she can simply order it.

### Ryuen Kakeru

Ryuen becomes a rival with whom Horikita can form bounded cooperation without erasing future competition. Her sports-festival alliance is one of the clearest proofs that she can author inter-polity agreements in her own right.

### Manabu Horikita

Manabu remains an integrated formative influence rather than an active specification. Year 2's presidency is important because Suzune takes an office associated with him while governing through a constitution that is increasingly her own.

## UNRESOLVED_CONTRADICTION

1. **Independent leader versus hidden guarantor:** Horikita's judgments are hers, but the class's full post-transfer resilience has not yet been observed.
2. **Rehabilitation versus externalized cost:** the Kushida project succeeds developmentally only after Airi pays the irrecoverable membership cost of V05.
3. **Responsibility versus promise-making:** Horikita becomes more accountable while also learning that promises made before she knows the governing principle can create illegitimate expectations.
4. **Recognition desire versus autonomous judgment:** she wants Ayanokoji's recognition without becoming reducible to it.
5. **Distributed polity versus Kōenji reserve:** the class is more plural, but one of its largest capacities remains voluntarily unavailable except on terms he owns.
6. **Separation as proof versus separation as authored shock:** Ayanokoji's departure is necessary for the succession test but is imposed without sufficient advance disclosure.

# 7. Behavioral and decision invariants for downstream modeling

These are Year-2 boundary constraints, not deterministic scripts.

## High-confidence tendencies

- Prefers reasons she can publicly defend over unexplained hidden authority.
- Increasingly asks who is actually entitled to decide, not only what outcome is efficient.
- Is comfortable using specialists whose ability exceeds hers in the local domain.
- Does not require collaborators to like her if their obligations and incentives can be made explicit.
- Can be stubborn when she believes a long-term developmental possibility is being undervalued.
- Is willing to accept visible responsibility for unpopular decisions rather than displace all blame onto advisers.
- Learns from failure and can revise a rule or promise without pretending the original injury did not occur.
- Values recognition from highly capable people, especially Ayanokoji, but can still choose against their preferred answer.
- Becomes more sensitive to history-dependent relational value; people such as Ibuki can no longer be treated as cleanly interchangeable pieces.
- Treats leadership increasingly as architecture for other people's action rather than as proof that she should personally dominate every task.

## Common modeling errors to avoid

- Do not reduce her to "Ayanokoji's student." His influence is large, but Year 2 repeatedly establishes independent institutional authorship.
- Do not reduce her to Manabu imitation. The Year-1 ledger already rejected this, and Year 2 presidency strengthens the rejection.
- Do not treat the Kushida outcome as proof that every rehabilitation gamble is justified. The Airi cost remains part of the state.
- Do not equate low/modest OAA quick-thinking with poor leadership judgment.
- Do not model her as emotionally indifferent to rivals or classmates once strategic stakes rise; relational knowledge increasingly produces moral friction.
- Do not claim the class has already proven independence from Ayanokoji after transfer. The relevant evidence lies beyond the Year-2 boundary.
- Do not interpret desire for Ayanokoji's recognition as obedience. The strongest Year-2 decision directly contradicts his preferred answer.

# 8. Open Year-2 boundary questions

## `Y1H-016` - witnessing Ayanokoji fail

What would it mean for Horikita to witness a genuine failure of Ayanokoji's capability/control rather than merely experience her own failure before he supplies terminal security? Year 2 does not contain that event.

## Succession test

Can Horikita's polity preserve competitive performance, social cohesion, and independent judgment after Ayanokoji becomes an external opponent rather than internal insurance?

## Koenji constitutional question

Can Horikita obtain enough voluntary contribution from Kōenji without violating the bounded-obligation principle that made their Year-2 bargain legitimate?

## Kushida legitimacy question

Can the long-run benefits of Kushida's self-authored cooperation coexist with full acknowledgment that Airi bore the irreversible initial cost? Year 2 supports utility and development; it does not erase the distributive ethical burden.

## Recognition and attachment

Can Horikita's desire for Ayanokoji's recognition be integrated into a relation where she can judge him, refuse him, and care for him without making his evaluation the final court of her worth?

## Leadership under externalized uncertainty

How does her decision architecture change when she no longer has reliable access to the person whose hidden capacity previously reduced tail risk?

# 10. Boundary synthesis

The Year-1 Horikita ended as a leader who had learned to integrate influence rather than imitate it and to distribute authority rather than pretend leadership meant total competence. Year 2 proves that this was not a temporary endpoint. She builds contracts, alliances, offices, and hostile cooperations; makes a consequential decision Ayanokoji would not make; allows Kushida's continued participation to become Kushida-owned; receives moral friction from friendship; and continues leading after personal defeat.

The strongest Year-2 result is therefore:
