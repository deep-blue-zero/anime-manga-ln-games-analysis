# Ayanokoji Kiyotaka
## Canonical character ledger through Year 2 / Second List

# 0. Ledger function

This is a structured Year-2 boundary snapshot, not the full specialist character essay. Its job is to record what has changed since the frozen Year-1 state, distinguish development from revelation, route the major longitudinal transitions to their canonical source homes, and state the unresolved contradictions that the later Year-2 specialist synthesis must explain in full.

The governing Year-1 endpoint was:

- ordinary school life is a genuine self-chosen project;
- developmental authorship is becoming one of Ayanokoji's self-chosen purposes;
- reciprocal authorship is the unresolved ethical constraint;
- a useful model of another person is not the person.

Year 2 does not overturn that endpoint. It scales it from local hidden support into a conscious multi-class developmental project, reveals the political and childhood genealogy behind some of his methods, and finally tests whether development can become independent of the developer.

# 1. Canonical Year-2 boundary state

## 1.1 Self-authorship is real, but it is second-order rather than pristine

Ayanokoji's ordinary school life remains a genuine positive end. Across Year 2 he protects continued enrollment, values friendship and romance as experiences, chooses low-stakes desires that cannot be justified by optimization, becomes increasingly aware that the remaining school time is finite, and produces spontaneous affect that he did not deliberately script.

The strongest ordinary-life markers are cumulative rather than isolated:

- Y2V01: continuing school life is valuable enough to justify greater public disclosure of ability;
- Y2V03-V04.5: romantic touch, embarrassment, friendship, cross-class familiarity, and social density become real experience rather than camouflage alone;
- Y2V08: he deliberately leaves skiing partly unstudied because learning through failure has value;
- Y2V09.5: he wants an inefficient yogurt maker because he wants it, and casual invitation exposes him to the risk of ordinary rejection;
- Y2V10: the blank sketchbook separates trained technique from self-generated creation, while an involuntary smile with Horikita appears before he specifies what the expression should be;
- Y2V11: low-stakes making and mixed-year exchange again reveal capacities and pleasures that OAA does not capture;
- Y2V12.5: even after breakup and class transfer planning, ordinary celebration, food, friendship, and bodily care continue to matter.

Volume 0 retrospectively changes the genealogy without nullifying the development. The opportunity to enter ANHS was anticipated and partly architected by Atsuomi/Tsukishiro, so the school cannot be described as a purely external escape that no prior author touched. But authorship of an opening is not authorship of every purpose generated inside it. Year 2 therefore supports a second-order model of freedom: Ayanokoji becomes more self-authored by generating purposes, attachments, refusals, and experiments that exceed the purpose of the environment that gave him the opening.

## 1.2 Environmental authorship becomes a conscious project

Year 1 established a recurring grammar: observe a person, infer the limiting condition, alter the environment, and watch what the person does. Year 2 makes that grammar increasingly explicit and large-scale.

The development is not simply "he manipulates more." It changes form:

1. **Authored visibility** - Y2V01: he changes what of his ability may become public while preserving control over motive/history.
2. **Developmental pacing** - Y2V02: he consciously regulates Kei's romantic progression and Ichinose's developmental pressure.
3. **Information architecture** - Y2V03: he withholds a rescue route from Ike so that Ike's determination remains fully engaged.
4. **Counter-institutional staging** - Y2V04: he defeats Tsukishiro's private control by routing facts through a procedure and witnesses the hostile author does not fully control.
5. **Curriculum inside intimacy** - Y2V04.5: genuine affection with Kei coexists with an explicitly conceptualized romance curriculum.
6. **Culling** - Y2V05: the same modeling capacity used for development can rank disposability and organize Airi's expulsion.
7. **Succession design** - Y2V06: he begins withdrawing from first-line problem solving and names himself the class's "training wheels," whose successful endpoint is removal.
8. **Staged agency** - Y2V07: he increasingly sets conditions under which independent actors complete the event through motives that remain their own.
9. **Relational timing jurisdiction** - Y2V08: he comforts Ichinose while privately reserving the timing of the collapse he expects to be developmentally useful.
10. **Unmodeled agency as desired output** - Y2V09: developed actors become valuable partly because they produce answers he did not already possess.
11. **Enjoyment of manipulation** - Y2V09.5: he recognizes that manipulating through speech can itself be fun and epistemically stimulating.
12. **All-class ecology** - Y2V10: he openly tells Sakayanagi that he intends to draw out the maximum potential of all four classes.
13. **Bounded intervention** - Y2V11: he identifies a legitimate stopping rule - open a future, preserve refusal, then stop owning the answer.
14. **Relational jurisdiction** - Y2V12: he can make direct commands unnecessary by designing information/timing/attachment so that another person's own agency completes the preferred outcome.
15. **Separation experiment** - Y2V12.5: he executes the class transfer and breakup, removing himself from one environment while taking a central strategic position in another.

The mature Year-2 formulation is therefore:

> **Ayanokoji's distinctive power is environmental authorship: he is unusually capable of modeling the conditions under which other people will act, then changing those conditions so that the target's own agency becomes part of the causal mechanism.**

This is neither literal puppetry nor neutral observation. Agency remains real; responsibility for staging remains real too.

## 1.3 The ethical problem shifts from coercion to jurisdiction

Year 2 sharply improves the vocabulary needed to judge Ayanokoji. The central question is no longer "does he manipulate people?" He plainly does, and sometimes with benign or defensive ends. The more diagnostic questions are:

- Did the other person understand the material conditions of the choice?
- Could the other person refuse without punishment authored by him?
- Did he select the goal of the development, or did the person appropriate the goal as their own?
- Did he merely open an option, or did he engineer the motive structure that made one option overwhelmingly likely?
- When the developed person produces an answer he did not specify, can he accept it as theirs rather than absorb it into his own experiment?
- Can relational knowledge become a restraint on strategy rather than a more precise strategic instrument?

V11 supplies the best positive local rule: recognition may justify an opening or bounded intervention, but there is a point after which the answer is not his jurisdiction.

V12 demonstrates the negative case. He can preserve formal choice while weaponizing trust, love, information asymmetry, and timing. Sakayanagi chooses; Ichinose chooses within the bargain she understands; Maezono lies. Yet the hidden designer remains causally responsible for constructing the choice environment.

V12.5 turns the problem into a test of separation. Removing the developer does not itself prove independence if the target merely executes the behavioral answer he expected. The strongest evidence of autonomy is counter-authorship: an answer he did not supply, predict, or possess.

## 1.4 Counter-authorship becomes the limit of his model

The decisive Year-2 result is not that Ayanokoji becomes less competent. It is that competence encounters people who become meaningfully non-exhaustible.

Examples include:

- Horikita repeatedly produces political judgments he does not dictate, including preserving Kushida, designing the Ryuen alliance, building a student-council settlement, and administering class strategy without direct command;
- Kushida appropriates continued participation for her own self-interested reason rather than becoming the grateful object of Horikita's rescue;
- Sudo's development becomes peer-generative through Onodera and later helps weaker classmates;
- Haruka revises her expulsion ontology because Airi creates a life outside ANHS that neither the class nor Ayanokoji's culling model determines;
- Sakayanagi discovers relational value in Kamuro after her own optimization model failed to register it;
- Ichinose eventually rejects the binary Ayanokoji prepared and creates a third answer that he explicitly does not possess.

`Second List` strengthens this rather than creating it. Its late Year-2 Check Point explicitly frames Ichinose's action as exceeding Ayanokoji's prediction, while the bonus fiction `ano koro kara` has Ayanokoji himself deny sole authorship of her recovery, credit her with standing back up by her own power, and acknowledge that she generates new discoveries after he thought the analysis was complete (`Y2SL-E089-Y2SL-E114`, especially the fiction and late-checkpoint entries routed in the canonical paratext audit).

The strongest terminal principle is:

> **Development survives the developer most convincingly where it becomes counter-authored.**

## 1.5 Ordinary affect develops without yet becoming protective vulnerability

The Year-1 ledger already rejected the claim that Ayanokoji is simply affectless. Year 2 strengthens the distinction between affect, disclosure, and vulnerability.

He can:

- enjoy friendship and purposeless conversation;
- desire objects and outings;
- experience romantic touch as genuine sensation and desire;
- become embarrassed, curious, disappointed, pleased, or amused;
- smile involuntarily;
- admit ignorance in low-stakes domains;
- ask Hiyori to spend time together because he wants to;
- accept some risk that another person may refuse.

But this does **not** satisfy `Y1H-035`. He still does not voluntarily expose a helpless weakness in a way that grants another person protective authority over him. `Second List` strengthens the distinction by showing ordinary imperfection and socially visible knowledge gaps while leaving protective vulnerability open.

## 1.6 Disclosure remains the major relational deficit

Year 2 gives repeated examples of disclosure that is technically real but structurally insufficient.

- Public ability: he reveals mathematics and later more of his competence while controlling which slice becomes visible.
- Kei: she experiences genuine affection and ordinary relationship development, but remains outside decisive information about his family, White Room history, class-transfer plan, emotional assessment of the romance, and timing of separation.
- Horikita: he increasingly recognizes and even cares for her as a person, but does not tell her that the "training wheels" are about to leave until the transfer is already an institutional fact.
- Ichinose: he reveals or frames truth at moments that serve his developmental/strategic purpose rather than as a consistent practice of reciprocal informed choice.
- Amasawa: relative informational symmetry is higher because she recognizes some of the developmental tethering and can answer it more directly.

`Y1H-004` therefore remains `STILL_OPEN` at the complete Year-2 boundary. The problem is not whether he can tell truths. It is whether he can disclose enough of the truth **in time** for another person to make a genuinely informed relational choice.

# 2. Changes since prior snapshot

## CONFIRMED_CONTINUITY

- Ordinary school life remains intrinsically valuable rather than mere camouflage.
- Strategic concealment remains a chosen tool even as the default policy becomes less absolute.
- Ayanokoji continues to model people through incentives, likely reactions, and environmental conditions.
- Care and instrumentality can coexist in the same action; neither cancels the other.
- The Year-1 principle "a useful model of another person is not the person" remains valid and becomes central to `Second List`'s OAA audit.
- Self-preservation remains a strong invariant; V07 indicates that even lover/family status does not automatically override it.

## GENUINE_DEVELOPMENT

- **Public self-authorship:** he deliberately changes his visibility policy beginning with the Y2V01 mathematics score.
- **Social embeddedness:** friendship, romance, rivalry, ordinary consumer desire, shared hobbies, and finite school time acquire greater positive value.
- **Generativity pressure:** the blank sketchbook and spontaneous smile make the difference between trained replication and unscripted creation personally legible.
- **Distributed-agency tolerance:** he increasingly allows other students to solve problems without becoming their first-line answer.
- **Explicit succession purpose:** he consciously plans to remove himself as Horikita class's hidden security layer and eventually oppose the class.
- **Bounded-intervention concept:** by V11 he can articulate and sometimes practice a stopping point where another person's future is no longer his area to enter.
- **Participant-mode relation:** V09.5 Hiyori interaction is evidence that he can state his own desire, allow refusal, and seek shared experience without inventing a hidden developmental crisis.
- **Recognition of counter-authorship:** by the Y2SL boundary, he has direct evidence that some developed people can produce answers outside his completed model.

## REVELATION_OF_PRIOR_HIDDEN_STATE

Volume 0 is the principal source of revelation, not character development at the school boundary.

- The White Room is political before it is pedagogical: an elite-development project embedded in factional political conflict.
- Atsuomi's governing grammar includes preference engineering - shaping the field in which another person comes to accept or desire the imposed outcome.
- Kiyotaka as a child was not born with finished performance. His abnormality includes extraordinary rate of absorption, adaptation, and generalization under extreme curriculum.
- He is not a reproducible White Room product: the source describes an exceptional interaction between unusual potential and environment rather than a reliably manufacturable result.
- Emotional capacity was not metaphysically absent; ordinary expression and relational vocabulary were starved and made causally ineffective.
- Kiyotaka already displayed a data-seeking environmental grammar as a child, including preserving weaker peers in the field to gain experience rather than from solidarity.
- His route toward ANHS was anticipated and partly prepared by adults before he independently assigned the school its later meaning.

## RETROSPECTIVE_RECONTEXTUALIZATION

- Year-1 and early-Year-2 environmental authorship now has an institutional/political genealogy. This strengthens explanation of form without proving conscious imitation of Atsuomi.
- Ayanokoji's ordinary-life project becomes more significant because ANHS is not simply "outside the design"; counter-authored purposes arise inside an opportunity that was itself partly designed.
- His ability to hide/display performance gains a childhood analogue without converting every concealment choice into an immutable White Room reflex.
- The ethical contrast between White Room total curriculum and ANHS plurality becomes sharper: ANHS can also manipulate and cull, but it permits relations, dissent, rival institutions, and self-owned desires that generate answers no central curriculum can fully pre-author.

## CORRECTION_OF_PRIOR_INFERENCE

No load-bearing claim in the canonical Year-1 Ayanokoji ledger requires rejection. Two stronger possible inferences are corrected:

1. **"ANHS is a purely self-created escape outside Atsuomi's design" - REVISE.** The opportunity was partly architected; the meanings and purposes generated inside it were not thereby owned.
2. **"Romantic form will necessarily produce conventional love if the curriculum is completed" - REJECT as a forecast.** By V09.5 Ayanokoji explicitly distinguishes completing romantic behavior from acquiring the feeling he hoped the behavior would teach.

The Year-1 ledger's own cautious formulations therefore age well: romance was genuine but its endpoint unresolved; ordinary life was genuine; developmental jurisdiction remained ethically unsettled.

## RELATIONSHIP_SPECIFIC_EXPRESSION

### Kei Karuizawa

Ayanokoji can be affectionate, attentive, physically desirous, protective, and behaviorally responsive with Kei while simultaneously treating the relationship as developmental/experiential curriculum. `Second List` bonus fiction strengthens his accurate attentiveness: he can notice her preference and later act on it while minimizing the interpersonal vulnerability of directly presenting care as care. The breakup confirms that behaviorally real affection and instrumental architecture were both present. Kei's autonomy after separation does not retroactively repair the information asymmetry.

### Horikita Suzune

Horikita increasingly becomes the person against whom his developmental theory is tested. He respects decisions he regards as irrational, allows her authority to remain consequential, wants her class to function without him, and eventually removes himself as the hidden guarantor. At the same time, he scripts the timing of separation and withholds decisive information. His involuntary smile with her is important affective evidence but not proof of full vulnerability. By the boundary, she is both a developed leader and an unresolved test of whether he can accept a polity that answers him rather than fulfills him.

### Ichinose Honami

Ichinose produces the clearest escalation from care to jurisdiction and then to counter-authorship. Ayanokoji comforts, protects, pressures, times, and models her; later he weaponizes her trust. Yet she finally supplies the strongest Year-2 evidence that a developed person can refuse his binary and force his model to update. `Second List` explicitly strengthens this route.

### Sakayanagi Arisu

Sakayanagi is rival, observer, person with partial childhood knowledge, and later a relationally powerful interpreter of Ayanokoji's preferences. In V12 he exploits the fact that she can infer what he wants without being commanded. Her withdrawal is therefore genuine agency under relationally authored conditions, not simple obedience.

### Hiyori Shiina

Hiyori is one of the cleanest low-control relational contexts. Shared reading, quiet time, declared desire for an outing, and acknowledged right of refusal show Ayanokoji participating rather than always designing. This is evidence of available relational modes, not proof they have generalized.

### Amasawa Ichika

Amasawa is important because White Room origin does not determine allegiance and because she can perceive some of Ayanokoji's tethering efforts. He can offer future possibility without requiring gratitude or obedience, approximating V11's bounded-intervention model.

### Nagumo Miyabi

Nagumo gives Ayanokoji an opponent whose desire for authentication produces a comparatively explicit contest. Ayanokoji's intervention on the island also teaches the opposite lesson: acting on a central node can create system effects far beyond the local target, expanding responsibility beyond intent.

## UNRESOLVED_CONTRADICTION

1. **Self-authorship versus environmental sovereignty:** the escaped subject chooses purposes for himself, but one of those purposes is designing developmental fields for others.
2. **Counter-authorship versus experimental appetite:** he genuinely values surprising independent answers, yet surprise itself can become the next data product.
3. **Care versus jurisdiction:** accurate care can coexist with timing, disclosure, and choice structures the cared-for person did not authorize.
4. **Affect versus vulnerability:** spontaneous affect has grown; protective vulnerability remains unproven.
5. **Separation versus ownership:** he removes himself from Horikita's class but chooses the timing, withholds the plan, and assumes a new central position elsewhere.
6. **Freedom versus inherited design:** his school-life purposes exceed Atsuomi's demonstrated aims, but post-school external jurisdiction remains unresolved.

# 4. Ability, measurement, and self-presentation at the boundary

`Second List` records Ayanokoji's March OAA as:

- academics A (87)
- physical B (73)
- quick-thinking C (54)
- social B (70)
- overall B (71)

These are `SL-RATING` institutional snapshots (`Y2SL-E010`), not a total ability model. The guidebook itself places this file beside editorial knowledge that he is the White Room's highest "masterpiece" and that he has increasingly intervened across classes (`Y2SL-E009-Y2SL-E012`).

The correct model is:

`latent/possessed capacity -> chosen or situationally elicited performance -> institutional observation -> recorded OAA`

The Year-2 developmental inference is therefore not "his ability rose from mediocre to elite." The major change is what he is willing to display, how he uses public legibility, and how ordinary/social capacities genuinely develop in domains the original file could not infer.

# 6. Behavioral and cognitive invariants for downstream modeling

These are boundary-level model constraints, not claims that behavior is deterministic.

## High-confidence invariants

- Observes systems and incentive structures before treating surface behavior as sufficient explanation.
- Separates possessed capacity from displayed capacity and frequently manages the latter.
- Prefers interventions that operate through conditions, information, timing, or incentives over unnecessary overt command.
- Can maintain sincere care and instrumental calculation simultaneously.
- Is unusually tolerant of interpersonal ambiguity when it yields information or future optionality.
- Protects self-preservation strongly and does not automatically subordinate it to relationship category.
- Finds human unpredictability epistemically attractive; by late Year 2, manipulation itself can be pleasurable.
- Increasingly values ordinary, inefficient, or non-instrumental experience despite retaining the experimental gaze.
- Does not naturally equate accurate understanding with a duty to disclose that understanding.
- Is increasingly capable of stopping an intervention, but the stopping rule is not yet generalized under high stakes.

## Common modeling errors to avoid

- Do not model him as emotionless. The corpus contains direct desire, pleasure, embarrassment, disappointment, attachment, and spontaneous affect.
- Do not model him as conventionally emotionally transparent. Affect and disclosure remain sharply separated.
- Do not treat every kind act as secret manipulation or every manipulative act as proof that the care was fake.
- Do not equate White Room origin with robotic social behavior; Volume 0 and Amasawa/Yagami evidence reject a single fixed outcome.
- Do not treat his predictions as omniscience. Year 2 increasingly centers on actors who exceed his completed model.
- Do not let later counter-authorship erase his causal responsibility for deliberately staged conditions.

# 9. Boundary synthesis

The Year-1 Ayanokoji ended as a boy who had begun to choose his own purposes while remaining ethically uncertain about the right to cultivate other people. The Year-2 Ayanokoji turns that possibility into a full architecture. He makes himself more visible, becomes more socially embedded, experiences desires and affects that his curriculum did not prescribe, and learns that people can generate answers his models do not contain. At the same time, his developmental authorship grows more sophisticated: from direct assistance to pacing, staging, culling, succession design, relational jurisdiction, and finally class-level ecological engineering.

The strongest Year-2 judgment is therefore not that he has become "more human" or "more manipulative." Both are too flat.

> **He becomes more capable of participating in ordinary human life at the same time that ordinary human intimacy gives him more precise causal access to other people. Year 2's ethical test is whether the first development can become a limit on the second. At the boundary, that test remains open.**
