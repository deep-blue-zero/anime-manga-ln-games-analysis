# Actor identity, knowledge, allegiance, and authorship

## 1. Boundary rule

The architecture's cumulative filename is retained. Its present frozen state is **through Y2SL**; the filename is not a license to import subsequent findings. Origin, learned information, a sponsor's instruction, personal desire, temporary cooperation, and authorship of a particular operation are separate propositions. Exceptional performance proves none of the missing columns.

## 3. Attribution checkpoints that must not be collapsed

| Knowledge boundary | What becomes available | What stays prohibited |
|---|---|---|
| Y2V04 | Amasawa's origin; fifth-generation communication; coercive I2 intervention and counter-witnesses | Treating origin as identical allegiance; GPS or presence as sufficient causal proof |
| Y2V04.5 | Yagami's origin is disclosed | Giving earlier classmates or earlier prospective readers that resolved identity |
| Y2V07 | Multiple investigations converge on Yagami; an unnamed caller acknowledges a bounded role | Naming the caller in V07's local freeze; making Kiyotaka the direct commander of every investigator |
| Y2V08 | Ishigami identity and Atsuomi relation; Nanase/Tsukishiro purpose reopened | Calling Ishigami White Room born; treating the knight theory as a confirmed assignment |
| Volume 0, read after V08 | Earlier institutional and personal provenance, including Ishigami/Kanzaki context | Replacing the V08 hypothesis state with certainty about the entire operation |
| Y2V09–Y2SL | Later conduct and documentary/fiction calibration at their own positions | Treating the guidebook as omniscient mission disclosure or using any Year-3 solution |

The V07 removal is particularly diagnostic. Horikita's handwriting inquiry, Ibuki's action, Nagumo's grievance, Ryūen's island investigation, witnesses, and Yagami's own conduct converge. Kiyotaka engineers an opportunity and routing chain, but the convergent agents have their own evidence and reasons. The adults' claim that the chairman was informed is a **reported authorization**, not independently verified by the claim itself. Removal establishes neither a clean public adjudication nor what happens after retrieval.
