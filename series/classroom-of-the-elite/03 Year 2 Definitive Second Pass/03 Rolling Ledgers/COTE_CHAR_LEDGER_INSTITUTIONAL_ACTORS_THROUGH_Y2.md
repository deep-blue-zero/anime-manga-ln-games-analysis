## 4. Written voice and reconstruction limits

Office, social mask and physical competence are not interchangeable voice specifications. For a novel-situation reconstruction, first retrieve the [Japanese written-voice ledger](COTE_JAPANESE_VOICE_LEDGER_THROUGH_Y2.md) and the relevant local scene. Preserve who knows what, who may speak openly, the addressee and the power relation. Written dialogue supports register and interaction analysis; it supplies no performed pitch, rhythm or delivery. A sparse interior record, especially Shiba's or a political actor's, warrants abstention about intimate private feeling rather than a fluent invented biography.

Amasawa's deference, Yagami's collapse and Ishigami's paternal allegiance must remain relationship- or stress-conditioned. Chabashira's more answerable practice is development; the newly supplied cause of an earlier action is revelation. Treating those as one category would produce a false uniform adult-redemption arc.
