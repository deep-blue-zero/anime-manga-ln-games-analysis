# Ability and Measurement Ledger
## Canonical current-state ability / OAA / merit-model reference through Year 2 / *Second List*

# 0. Ledger function

The title `ようこそ実力至上主義の教室へ` invites a recurrent analytical mistake: imagining that the series contains one hidden, objective leaderboard called `実力`, and that every examination, OAA value, class rank, social judgment, or White Room comparison is a noisy attempt to discover that single latent number.

The completed Year-2 corpus does not support that model.

Ability differences are real.

Ayanokōji is not secretly average. Kōenji is not secretly ordinary. Sudō's athletic ability is not a social construction. Sakayanagi's physical impairment is not erased by strategic intelligence. Kushida's interpersonal memory and access are real capacities even when used destructively. Hiyori's quiet judgment is real even when a public metric under-describes it. Ichinose's relational memory matters in environments that reward knowledge of particular people. Ryūen's political causality does not disappear because a prosocial-contribution axis rates him poorly.

The problem is not whether people differ.

The problem is:

> **Which difference is being measured, under which observation regime, at which date, after which developmental history, with what degree of voluntary expression, for what task, and with what consequence attached to the result?**

This ledger therefore separates ten objects that ordinary power-scaling language tends to collapse:

1. **possessed capacity** — what the person can in fact do;
2. **developed capacity** — what has changed through learning, practice, relationship, injury, failure, or self-authorship;
3. **expressed performance** — what the person actually displays;
4. **available performance** — what the person is willing to make usable by others;
5. **institutional observation** — what the school has access to and chooses to record;
6. **recorded rating** — the numerical or categorical model produced from those observations;
7. **task value** — how useful that capacity becomes under a particular examination or environment;
8. **coordinated / generative value** — what the person enables other people or the group to do;
9. **record provenance** — whether the official result faithfully represents the underlying event;
10. **model use** — what other actors do once the measurement becomes public.

The central Year-2 development is the tenth step.

Year 1 established that a file could be accurate and incomplete.

Year 2 makes the file **public infrastructure**.

Once OAA becomes searchable across classes and years, the measurement does not merely describe the social field. Students recruit, avoid, price, protect, target, and develop one another partly in response to the measurement.

The Year-2 measurement chain is therefore reflexive:

> **capacity → development → expression / withholding → institutional observation → recorded model → public legibility → strategic use → changed environment → new expression**

The measurement enters the causal system it claims to describe.

That is the governing problem of this ledger.

# 1. Executive thesis — Year 2 turns the file into part of the environment

The strongest Year-1 ability formulation was:

> **possession → development → expression → legibility → coordination → recording → model limitation**

Year 2 preserves every link and adds two decisive variables:

- **performed availability** — whether a person chooses to make ability usable;
- **model feedback** — how public measurement changes how other people treat the measured person.

The completed Year-2 thesis is:

> **Ability is real, plural, developmental, selectively expressed, selectively available, environmentally valued, socially coordinated, institutionally observed, and politically consumed. OAA is useful because it records institutionally legible performance under an observation regime. It becomes misleading when the observer silently promotes that bounded record into latent ceiling, moral worth, total social capacity, universal task value, or legitimate jurisdiction over the person.**

This is not an anti-measurement conclusion.

The corpus repeatedly shows why measurement is useful:

- weak students can be identified for support;
- exceptional physical or academic strengths become visible;
- development can be tracked across annual snapshots;
- leaders can allocate people more intelligently;
- institutions can make some forms of performance auditable;
- an old rating can be deliberately used as a baseline against which development becomes valuable.

The problem begins when the object silently changes.

A valid statement such as:

> **“the school has observed this performance”**

becomes an invalid statement such as:

> **“this is what the person is.”**

Year 2 supplies a complete set of counterexamples.

- Ayanokōji shows **managed visibility**.
- Kōenji shows **voluntary illegibility / unavailability**.
- Sudō shows **developmental staleness**.
- Horikita shows **generative capacity that exceeds personal scalar performance**.
- Ryūen shows **social causality distinct from prosocial contribution**.
- Hiyori shows **relationship-specific judgment that a broad axis under-describes**.
- Kei shows **social coordination that academic profile cannot explain**.
- Kushida shows **capacity without moral self-interpretation**.
- Sakayanagi shows **aggregate distortion through disability and task mismatch**.
- Ichinose shows **relational knowledge becoming high competitive value under the right task**.
- the island exam shows **recorded rank with complex causal authorship**.
- V09 shows **the same low rating becoming high marginal value after development**.
- V11 shows **low-stakes environments revealing capacities that harsher tests miss**.
- V12 shows **relational history becoming formal competitive capital**.

The terminal distinction is therefore not:

`measured / unmeasured`.

It is:

> **measured object / unmeasured remainder / use made of the measurement.**

# 2. Frozen Year-1 baseline — what survives unchanged

The Year-1 specialist remains immutable historical authority at `Y1FF`. This ledger inherits six propositions without reopening them.

## 2.1 `実力` is not one universal stat

Year 1 already established that practical usefulness changes with:

- environment;
- preparation;
- health;
- motivation;
- information;
- cooperation;
- willingness to contribute;
- and trustworthy recording.

Ike can be weak under the classroom's default hierarchy and highly useful on the island.

Horikita can be academically strong and socially ineffective early.

Kōenji can possess extraordinary capacity without making it collective property.

The correct question is therefore:

> **actual ability for what purpose, under what conditions, and available to whom?**

## 2.2 Every measurement has an object and a date

A score can accurately describe:

- one written test;
- one physical event;
- one month of classroom conduct;
- one observation period;
- or one public behavior state.

The error appears when the record is made timeless.

A dated file must remain dated.

## 2.3 Expression mediates observation

Ayanokōji deliberately suppresses visible performance.

Kōenji often refuses serious participation.

Matsushita also supplies Year-1 evidence that underperformance can be chosen.

An honest observer cannot measure an output the subject elects not to provide.

## 2.4 Development can make a once-correct model stale

Sudō is the Year-1 prototype.

The initial file can be accurate about the student who entered the school and become inadequate because that student changes.

The error is not necessarily poor observation.

Sometimes:

> **the person moves.**

## 2.5 Coordination is itself a form of ability

Horikita's Year-1 growth already shows why individual scalar models are incomplete.

The leader need not personally possess every useful capacity.

She can:

- identify;
- teach;
- allocate;
- protect;
- combine;
- and make other people's abilities usable.

This is **generative / distributed ability**.

## 2.6 An honest model can remain incomplete

*First File* is the Year-1 terminal proof.

Official ratings and retrospective hypothetical OAA are useful documentary objects.

They are not omniscient character databases.

Year 2 does not overturn this.

It operationalizes it.

# 3. Japanese conceptual vocabulary

# 3.1 `実力 / jitsuryoku`

`実力` concerns actual, demonstrated, or effective ability rather than mere prestige, title, pedigree, or reputation.

The Year-2 correction is that “actual” does not mean context-free.

A person's effective ability depends partly on:

- what they have learned;
- what they reveal;
- whether they participate;
- whether others can coordinate the ability;
- and what the task makes valuable.

`実力` should therefore not be translated analytically as a hidden power level.

# 3.2 `能力 / nōryoku`

`能力` is the broader capacity term.

The distinction is useful:

> **capacity can exist before it becomes institutionally demonstrated effectiveness.**

Ayanokōji is the obvious case.

Kōenji is the more politically revealing case because the missing step is not concealment alone but refusal to make capacity available.

# 3.3 `学力 / gakuryoku`

Academic ability remains one of the most legible domains because written tests provide standardized evidence.

Year 2 nevertheless demonstrates several non-equivalences:

- academic rating != total ability;
- academic rating != current task value;
- academic rating != teaching ability;
- academic rating != developmental potential;
- academic rating != willingness to contribute.

V09 makes this distinction mechanical.

# 3.4 `身体能力 / shintai nōryoku`

Physical ability is institutionally derived from PE, club activity, physical special-exam performance, and related observations.

The axis is real.

Its aggregate meaning is conditional.

Sakayanagi's disability is a genuine limitation in embodied tasks but does not imply low strategic or political ability.

# 3.5 `機転思考力`

The live OAA system uses a broad quick-thinking / adaptive-social axis incorporating:

- friend network;
- communication;
- applied improvisation;
- social position;
- situational adaptation.

This is not identical to:

- abstract intelligence;
- strategy;
- empathy;
- leadership;
- or moral judgment.

Ryūen and Hiyori are especially important boundary cases.

# 3.6 `社会貢献性`

The social-contribution axis includes observed:

- classroom conduct;
- lateness / absence;
- problematic behavior;
- school contribution;
- morality / manners as institutionally seen;
- broader conduct reputation.

This is not “social power.”

A person can have low prosocial contribution and enormous social causality.

Ryūen is the clearest case.

Nor is a high score proof of moral trustworthiness.

Kushida is the clearest counterexample.

# 3.7 `総合 / overall`

OAA aggregates selected axes.

The social-contribution dimension is half-weighted in the live Year-2 formulation.

The aggregate is mathematically meaningful for the model the institution chose.

It is not a proof that every axis should be commensurable for every purpose.

# 4. Live OAA — what the institution actually builds

Y2V01 turns the Year-1 file problem into a live public system.

`Y2V01-E007–E016` are the core source route.

The OAA system:

- exposes profiles across classes and years;
- records academic, physical, quick-thinking/adaptive-social, and social-contribution dimensions;
- produces an aggregate;
- preserves annual pages rather than silently replacing prior-year states;
- is presented as generally applied;
- and is explicitly not claimed to be omnipotent.

Ayanokōji's opening profile is recorded as:

| Axis | Y2V01 opening profile |
|---|---:|
| Academic | `C (51)` |
| Physical | `C+ (60)` |
| Quick-thinking / adaptive-social | `D+ (37)` |
| Social contribution | `C+ (60)` |
| Overall | `C (51)` |

The important point is not that the numbers are “wrong.”

Ayanokōji himself judges them reasonable given what he has made visible.

The object is therefore:

> **observed, institutionally legible performance**

rather than:

> **latent maximum capacity**.

This makes OAA a **measurement system with an explicit observation dependency**.

# 5. OAA is not merely a profile — it is a public causal intervention

The most important Year-2 change is not the extra numerical precision.

It is publicity.

Once the file is searchable, students can use it to:

- recruit;
- avoid;
- price;
- protect;
- target;
- classify;
- negotiate;
- and infer likely risk.

The V01 pair exam immediately turns OAA into market infrastructure.

A high academic first-year can command a premium.

A weak profile can make a student unattractive.

A class can allocate its strongest partners to protect weaker second-years.

Thus:

> **measurement changes the social field around the measured person.**

This produces a reflexivity problem.

The sequence is no longer:

`person -> measurement`.

It is:

`person -> measurement -> social response -> changed environment -> changed person/performance -> new measurement`.

OAA therefore becomes partly performative.

It does not merely discover the market value of students.

It helps create that market value.

# 6. Ayanokōji — managed legibility and visibility-state change

Ayanokōji is the cleanest demonstration that OAA measures what enters its observation regime.

## 6.1 Opening state

At the start of Year 2, the school records the profile above.

That profile is not random error.

It is the institutional result of a deliberate performance policy.

The Year-1 source corpus had already established:

- exact entrance-score suppression;
- deliberately mediocre visible output;
- selective athletic display;
- incomplete social participation.

The school sees the authored surface.

## 6.2 The mathematics 100 is not “academic growth”

At the end of V01, Ayanokōji deliberately scores 100 in mathematics (`Y2V01-E058–E059`).

Some questions extend beyond ordinary school curriculum.

The correct classification is:

- **capacity:** largely preexisting / revealed;
- **expression policy:** changed;
- **institutional legibility:** changed;
- **public reputation:** changed.

The event is therefore not evidence that he suddenly developed elite mathematics during Year 2.

It is evidence that:

> **a change in display policy can look numerically similar to a change in ability if the analyst does not preserve the causal layer.**

## 6.3 *Second List* gives the cleanest visibility sequence

`Y2SL-E086` preserves the school database across three Year-2 snapshots:

| Snapshot | Academic | Physical | Quick-thinking | Social | Overall |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| April | C 51 | C+ 60 | D+ 37 | C+ 60 | C 51 |
| May | A- 81 | B- 61 | D+ 40 | B 68 | B- 62 |
| March | A 87 | B 73 | C 54 | B 70 | B 71 |

The same person appears to “improve” dramatically.

Some of that movement is genuine Year-2 development.

Much is not.

The correct model is layered:

`latent capacity`
`+ changed willingness to demonstrate`
`+ new school observations`
`+ real development in ordinary/social life`
`= changed OAA`

This is why the ledger must reject both simplistic readings:

### Wrong reading A
> The April rating proves he was only C-level then.

### Wrong reading B
> Because he hid ability, none of the later increases represent development.

Both are false.

Revelation and development coexist.

## 6.4 Measurement policy becomes self-authorship

The decision to expose ability matters psychologically and politically even when the ability itself is old.

He increasingly chooses what kind of student the institution is allowed to know.

This is a development in **public self-authorship**.

Thus an ability ledger must record not only:

> “what can he do?”

but also:

> **“what is he now willing to let the environment know he can do?”**

# 7. Kōenji — voluntary illegibility and the difference between possession and availability

Kōenji is the strongest counterexample to the assumption that known ability becomes collective resource.

*Second List* explicitly notes that his published numbers under-describe true capacity because participation is elective.

The island exam confirms enormous physical, strategic, and adaptive capacity when he chooses to deploy it.

The key distinction is:

> **capacity availability is itself a political variable.**

A class may correctly know:

> Kōenji can probably do this.

That does not mean:

> the class can use Kōenji to do this.

This produces four separate states:

1. **unknown capacity**
2. **known capacity**
3. **available capacity**
4. **contractually obligated capacity**

Kōenji moves among states 2–4 without becoming class property.

The island bargain and later bounded obligations therefore matter as much to ability measurement as the physical performance itself.

The school can improve its estimate of his capacity.

It cannot infer collective availability from capacity alone.

This is **voluntary illegibility** in one sense and **voluntary unavailability** in another.

Ayanokōji and Kōenji therefore produce opposite routes to undermeasurement:

- Ayanokōji curates what the observer can infer.
- Kōenji often refuses to make capacity relevant to the observer's institutional purpose.

# 8. Sudō — developmental staleness and generative ability

Sudō is the strongest longitudinal argument against static merit.

## 8.1 The old rating can become stale before the file updates

Y2V01 has Horikita judge Sudō's present academics around D to D+, already above the historical snapshot (`Y2V01-E023`).

This matters because the error is not concealment.

Sudō is not secretly elite.

He **changed**.

Thus:

> **a valid old observation can become an invalid current predictor through development.**

## 8.2 Development survives the original reward

Much of Sudō's early discipline is attached to Horikita.

Year 2 tests whether the ability remains after the incentive changes.

It does.

After romantic rejection:

- study continues;
- self-regulation continues;
- social judgment improves;
- he helps weaker classmates.

V09 turns this into the strongest possible measurement challenge.

The school's academic OAA is used to weight correct answers so that lower-rated students can generate higher marginal class value.

Sudō therefore demonstrates:

`low old rating + real development + correct answer -> high present marginal value`

## 8.3 Generativity is stronger evidence than personal improvement

The most important late Sudō evidence is not that he studies better.

It is that development begins reproducing itself in other people.

He becomes a peer resource.

That is **generative ability**:

> capacity whose output is increased capacity elsewhere.

A simple individual rating tends to under-measure this because the causal benefit appears in another person's performance.

# 9. Horikita — distributed and generative ability are not scalar personal ability

Horikita's Year-2 growth cannot be represented adequately by asking whether her personal OAA rises.

Her most important ability increasingly consists in:

- identifying differentiated capacities;
- assigning roles;
- creating contracts;
- asking for advice without surrendering judgment;
- letting other people own bounded domains;
- tolerating disagreement;
- and making a class operate without personally possessing every needed skill.

This is **distributed ability** at polity scale.

Her Year-1 specialist had already identified the emergence of generative leadership.

Year 2 strengthens it.

V01 shows her distinguishing:

- academic weakness;
- negotiation weakness;
- social access.

V06 shows class performance in a major festival with Ayanokōji not acting as direct field solver.

V09 rewards the class for development beneath old ratings.

V12 tests her limits and gives Ichinose a domain where relational/perceptual competence is superior.

This is important.

If Horikita's growth were represented as a scalar “leadership stat,” every later loss would look like regression.

The corpus instead supports:

> **development means acquiring more reliable ways to mobilize other people's different abilities, not becoming the best person at every task.**

At `Y2SL`, *Second List* confirms leadership growth while refusing to certify post-transfer class independence in advance.

Thus:

- personal ability growth: strong;
- generative leadership: strong;
- polity self-sustainment after hidden guarantor removal: `OPEN`.

# 10. Ryūen — social causality is not social contribution

Ryūen is one of the most useful OAA interpretation controls.

A low prosocial/social-contribution rating can coexist with enormous ability to:

- intimidate;
- recruit;
- negotiate;
- delegate;
- manipulate expectations;
- coordinate followers;
- create adversarial information fields.

*Second List* explicitly warns that his numerical profile does not exhaust leadership/strategic capacity.

The measurement lesson is:

> **prosocial contribution, social influence, coercive coordination, strategic intelligence, legitimacy, and morality are different variables.**

A model that compresses them into one “social” stat becomes unusable.

Year 2 also demonstrates real development:

- Katsuragi recruitment;
- bounded contracts;
- more delegation;
- improved model updating;
- increasing voluntary follower legitimacy.

But a higher-functioning polity does not imply that all relevant ability should appear as a higher social-contribution number.

The axis is measuring a narrower object.

# 11. Ichinose — relational memory becomes competitive ability when the task changes

Ichinose is a strong example of **task-value transformation**.

Her Year-1 high-trust social ecology can be misread as softness if the only valued output is adversarial disruption.

Year 2 increasingly demonstrates that the same relational density can support:

- information circulation;
- mutual protection;
- strategic concealment;
- coordination under observation;
- adversarial signaling;
- fine-grained memory of people.

V12 is the decisive measurement environment.

The year-end hidden-role exam rewards:

- personality knowledge;
- microbehavioral familiarity;
- relational history;
- social inference.

The task therefore converts a form of ability that OAA can only approximate into direct competitive power.

Horikita can be highly developed and still lose to Ichinose on this terrain.

The lesson is not:

> Ichinose has higher total social ability.

It is:

> **the exam finally selects a task unusually fitted to a capacity her class has cultivated through dense relationship history.**

This is why task fit must remain separate from total capacity.

# 12. Sakayanagi — disability, aggregation, and domain relevance

Sakayanagi exposes the politics of aggregate scores.

Her physical disability is a genuine disadvantage for embodied tasks.

The school must represent that fact somehow.

But once physical performance is incorporated into an overall aggregate, the analyst can commit a category error:

> lower composite -> lower total ability.

The aggregate is mathematically correct relative to its formula.

It is interpretively wrong if promoted into a universal ranking.

Sakayanagi remains:

- strategically elite;
- politically powerful;
- cognitively formidable;
- socially consequential.

The correct measurement principle is:

> **a domain-specific impairment should reduce predictions for tasks requiring that domain, not automatically reduce unrelated predictions by philosophical fiat.**

OAA's aggregate can be operationally useful without becoming the final object of character comparison.

# 13. Hiyori — quiet judgment and relationship-specific intelligence

*Second List* explicitly undercuts a naive reading of Hiyori's quick-thinking profile.

Her formal rating is comparatively modest relative to the quality of judgment the fiction demonstrates.

The reason is not necessarily “the school scored her incorrectly.”

Her most important capacities are often:

- quiet;
- relationship-specific;
- exercised in low-visibility moments;
- diagnostic rather than commanding;
- insufficiently tied to office or public output.

She can:

- understand people;
- identify developmental bottlenecks;
- recognize when a leader's victory does not equal class development;
- preserve cross-class relation without losing competitive awareness.

This suggests a general model:

> **institutional observability favors repeated, public, countable behavior; relationship-specific judgment can be real while generating too few standardized observations to dominate a broad rating.**

The model may be accurate about the observation set and incomplete about the capability.

# 14. Kei — social coordination outside academic hierarchy

Kei is another case where a conventional profile can radically under-describe actual class function.

Her ability includes:

- reading girls' social atmosphere;
- rumor/navigation;
- status management;
- coordination;
- public composure;
- rapid social adaptation.

These capacities matter politically even when they do not map cleanly onto academic ability.

*Second List* recognizes relatively strong quick-thinking/social performance.

The character ledger adds the important control:

> **social competence is not the same thing as emotional independence.**

Kei can be:

- socially competent;
- behaviorally functional;
- horizontally supported by Satō;
- and still emotionally dependent within the romance.

Measurement must therefore resist a different category error:

> high social function -> complete psychological autonomy.

No OAA axis warrants that inference.

# 15. Kushida — capacity is not morality

Kushida is the clearest reason never to treat a social metric as an ethical metric.

Her capacities are real:

- memory;
- network access;
- social calibration;
- trust acquisition;
- information flow;
- public cooperation;
- interpersonal responsiveness.

High social/quick-thinking measurement therefore need not be “fake.”

The moral problem lies in **use**.

Before exposure, the same capacities can support:

- intimacy;
- coordination;
- sabotage;
- secret extraction;
- reputational attack.

After the public/private equilibrium collapses, many of the same underlying capacities remain and can be repurposed into:

- class contribution;
- council work;
- independent investigation;
- self-interested cooperation.

Thus:

> **ability is morally non-self-interpreting.**

A metric can correctly identify interpersonal capacity while saying nothing sufficient about whether the capacity will be used benevolently.

# 16. Hirata — adaptive coaching and invisible generativity

Hirata is less spectacular than many elite performers and therefore analytically useful.

His ability includes:

- mediation;
- learner-sensitive explanation;
- emotional regulation of group atmosphere;
- adaptive coaching;
- broad legitimacy.

Some outputs can appear as his own social contribution.

Others appear as **someone else's later performance**.

That is the measurement problem of generative ability.

If Hirata helps a weaker student become functional, the final scoreboard may credit the learner.

A person-centered rating can therefore systematically undercount:

> **capacity that operates by improving another person's capacity.**

This is also why leadership and teaching cannot be reduced to personal task performance.

# 17. Hashimoto and Kanzaki — intelligence without the same correction structure

The Year-2 character ledgers provide an important ability contrast.

## Hashimoto

Hashimoto possesses:

- social perception;
- adaptability;
- information acquisition;
- courage under uncertainty;
- brokerage skill.

His weakness is not lack of ability.

It is the absence of a trusted corrective relationship with standing to revise his model before he acts.

This means:

> **high inference capacity can coexist with weak epistemic correction.**

The ability is real.

The architecture around the ability is fragile.

## Kanzaki

Kanzaki's value increasingly lies in making dissent structurally available inside a solidaristic class.

That is not simply “leadership charisma.”

It is a form of **epistemic infrastructure**:

- alternative hypothesis;
- permission to disagree;
- second cognitive center.

Ability measurement that only rewards correct individual answers may miss the political value of creating a system in which errors can be challenged.

# 18. Nagumo — merit, mobility, office, and the problem of self-authentication

Nagumo is visibly capable.

His problem is not undermeasurement in the same sense as Ayanokōji or Kōenji.

It is the opposite danger:

> **institutional success can become evidence that is too easy to overinterpret because the successful person also shaped the field.**

Nagumo's school reform:

- increases individual mobility;
- rewards visible performance;
- expands merit-sensitive opportunities.

But the mechanism is deeply patronal.

His office, pooled resources, ranking authority, and network power alter who can demonstrate mobility.

V09's opening self-doubt is therefore epistemically important.

He asks whether his supremacy has actually been tested or whether he is a `裸の王様`.

This produces a general measurement rule:

> **dominance inside a field partly authored by the dominant actor requires counterforce before it can authenticate itself.**

Merit evidence is strongest when the evaluated actor cannot monopolize the terms of evaluation.

# 19. Measurement failure taxonomy

The Year-2 corpus supports a stable failure taxonomy.

# 19.1 `UNDEREXPRESSION`

The person possesses more capacity than they display.

**Primary case:** Ayanokōji.

# 19.2 `VOLUNTARY_UNAVAILABILITY`

The person may display enough ability to establish capacity but refuses to make it institutionally or collectively usable.

**Primary case:** Kōenji.

# 19.3 `DEVELOPMENTAL_STALENESS`

The rating was reasonable when created and becomes a poor current predictor because the person changes.

**Primary case:** Sudō.

# 19.4 `AXIS_UNDERDESCRIPTION`

The axis measures a narrower object than readers infer.

**Primary cases:** Ryūen social contribution; Hiyori quick-thinking.

# 19.5 `MORAL_NONIDENTITY`

The capacity is accurately measured but says insufficiently about ethical use.

**Primary case:** Kushida.

# 19.6 `AGGREGATE_DOMAIN_MISMATCH`

One genuine limitation depresses an aggregate even when it is irrelevant to the target task.

**Primary case:** Sakayanagi physical disability.

# 19.7 `RELATIONAL_SPECIFICITY`

The ability exists in knowledge/history with particular people and is poorly represented by generic scalar measurement.

**Primary cases:** Ichinose, Hiyori, Kei.

# 19.8 `GENERATIVE_INVISIBILITY`

A person's ability increases other people's performance, causing the observable output to appear elsewhere.

**Primary cases:** Horikita, Hirata, late Sudō.

# 19.9 `TASK_VALUE_INVERSION`

A low rating becomes highly valuable because the rule assigns greater marginal reward to success from that category.

**Primary case:** V09 cooperative written exam.

# 19.10 `RECORD_PROVENANCE_FAILURE`

The underlying performance occurs but the official record/communication layer can be altered or captured.

**Primary cases:** Year-1 chess/Tsukishiro; Year-2 island administrative interference.

# 19.11 `MODEL_FEEDBACK`

Public measurement changes how people treat the subject, changing later behavior and opportunity.

**Primary case:** OAA partner market beginning in V01.

# 19.12 `IDENTITY_OVERINFERENCE`

Exceptional observed ability is used as proof of hidden biography or institutional origin.

**Primary case:** V01 White Room suspicion field.

The correct rule is:

> **competence is evidence about competence; it is not automatically identity evidence.**

# 20. OAA across examinations — the same number changes meaning when the rule changes

The examination ledger is the primary home for mechanics.

The ability ledger extracts one cross-exam proposition:

> **task design determines the practical meaning of a rating.**

## 20.1 V01 — rating as market signal

Academic OAA helps create partner prices and avoidance.

Low measured academics can increase expulsion risk through pairing.

## 20.2 V05 — rating as disposability input

The Unanimous Vote exam does not require expelling the lowest OAA student.

After the class locks itself into a sacrificial branch, Ayanokōji uses measurable value—including OAA and social context—as part of the culling criterion.

Thus the metric becomes a disposability tool because scarcity has already been institutionally created.

## 20.3 V06 — ability becomes convertible mobility

The sports festival makes athletic performance valuable not only as class contribution but as access to private points or a transfer ticket.

The same physical capacity acquires a new political meaning.

## 20.4 V07 — contribution and public performance become economic capacity

The cultural festival rewards:

- hospitality;
- presentation;
- logistics;
- marketing;
- reputation;
- attention management.

This expands the practical ability field beyond conventional OAA emphasis.

## 20.5 V09 — low rating becomes high marginal value

This is the centerpiece.

Every class member must contribute.

Correct answers from lower academic OAA categories are worth more.

An A-rated student's correct answer is worth only one point; weaker categories can be worth several times that amount.

The rule therefore makes:

> **development beneath the label**

more valuable than static excellence in some circumstances.

This does not prove OAA was wrong.

It proves:

> **rating and marginal task value are different variables.**

## 20.6 V10 — broad knowledge defeats academic totalization

The survival/elimination exam includes nonacademic categories such as:

- entertainment;
- life knowledge;
- subculture.

A student can be academically elite and still vulnerable outside that domain.

The exam mechanically rejects `学力 = 実力`.

## 20.7 V11 — lower stakes reveal unmodeled capacity

The exchange camp is not a formal special exam.

Its nineteen varied games reveal:

- craft ability;
- unfamiliar task adaptation;
- social adjustment;
- play;
- fine-motor skill;
- hidden interests.

Lower stakes reduce optimization pressure.

The result is often **better character information with less institutional consequence**.

## 20.8 V12 — relational history becomes formal ability

The hidden-role final exam rewards:

- knowledge of particular people;
- microexpression;
- social history;
- personality inference.

The school therefore converts something close to **relational memory** into formal competitive capital.

This is the Year-2 endpoint of measurement expansion.

# 21. Negative controls — why ordinary life belongs in an ability ledger

If analysis only studies formal exams, it will systematically overestimate:

- optimized behavior;
- risk response;
- adversarial intelligence;
- willingness to sacrifice.

Year 2 repeatedly uses lower-stakes life to reveal capacities that formal testing underweights.

Examples include:

- hobbies and craft;
- reading companionship;
- ordinary social repair;
- humor;
- dating competence;
- shopping;
- study help;
- friendship;
- refusal;
- care without class-point reward.

This matters because a person can be highly capable at:

- making another person comfortable;
- noticing distress;
- sustaining a friendship;
- finding pleasure;
- teaching;
- apologizing;
- creating.

Those may never maximize a class score.

They remain abilities.

The measurement ledger therefore uses ordinary life as a **calibration control** against exam exceptionalism.

# 22. Volume 0 — the difference between maximal curriculum and complete personhood

Volume 0 enters only at its architecture-mandated retrospective position.

Its relevance to ability measurement is structural.

The White Room attempts extraordinary control over:

- curriculum;
- exposure;
- developmental environment;
- performance expectation.

It can produce enormous measurable competence.

It does not follow that it can fully author:

- desire;
- value;
- attachment;
- ordinary preference;
- independent purpose.

The retrospective lesson is:

> **increasing control over developmental inputs can improve prediction of trained outputs without creating ownership of all purposes generated by the person.**

This becomes especially important for Ayanokōji.

His elite capacities are partly products of an authored curriculum.

His later ordinary-life learning contains real development not reducible to revealing old White Room ability.

The measurement discipline is therefore:

> **do not use hidden preexisting competence to erase later genuine development in domains the earlier environment did not fully cultivate.**

# 25. Ability-state coding for future retrieval

Later synthesis and character-modeling work should avoid generic statements such as “X is smarter than Y” when the source supports a more precise state.

Prefer one or more of these labels.

## `POSSESSED_CAPACITY`

Evidence supports underlying capability.

## `DEVELOPMENTAL_CHANGE`

The person genuinely becomes more capable over time.

## `EXPRESSION_CHANGE`

The capacity may be old, but visible output changes.

## `AVAILABILITY_CHANGE`

The person changes willingness to make capacity usable by the group/institution.

## `OBSERVATION_CHANGE`

The institution receives better or different evidence without necessarily implying personal change.

## `RATING_CHANGE`

The official model changes.

Do not infer which upstream cause changed without evidence.

## `TASK_VALUE_CHANGE`

The same capacity becomes more or less useful because the environment changes.

## `COORDINATION_CHANGE`

The person becomes better/worse able to make other capacities jointly usable.

## `GENERATIVE_OUTPUT`

The person's ability increases another person's capacity.

## `MODEL_REVISION`

A previous model becomes inadequate because new evidence, development, or context changes the inference.

## `COUNTER_AUTHORSHIP`

The modeled person produces an answer the model/developer did not already possess.

This is not simply “surprise.”

It is especially strong evidence that prediction is not ownership.

# 26. Reconstruction / character-modeling implications

This ledger has direct implications for any future behavioral or simulation model.

# 26.1 Never use OAA as a personality vector

OAA should be treated as:

- date-bound;
- observation-bound;
- institutionally selected;
- behaviorally affected by concealment/participation;
- and context-dependent.

A character simulator that converts OAA directly into:

- confidence;
- intelligence;
- morality;
- dominance;
- social warmth;
- or universal competence

would be analytically invalid.

# 26.2 Separate capacity from default behavioral policy

Ayanokōji can do more than he usually displays.

Kōenji can do more than he usually contributes.

Hiyori can diagnose more than she usually announces.

Sakayanagi can strategically dominate tasks her body cannot perform.

Simulation needs both:

- **can do**
- **typically chooses to do**

# 26.3 Preserve relationship-conditioned capability

Some abilities emerge only with specific partners or histories.

Examples:

- Kei's social coordination among girls;
- Hiyori's quiet corrective role around Ryūen;
- Ichinose's memory of classmates;
- Hirata's learner-sensitive mediation;
- Horikita's ability to mobilize different specialists.

A generic “social intelligence” variable is too coarse.

# 26.4 Preserve generativity

A character can be important because they alter other characters' future performance.

This matters for:

- Horikita;
- Hirata;
- Sudō;
- Ayanokōji;
- Kanzaki.

A model that measures only immediate personal output will systematically miss this.

# 26.5 Model confidence separately from score

The series repeatedly gives:

- reliable score / incomplete interpretation;
- noisy score / strong contextual evidence;
- strong capacity inference / weak availability inference.

Every model state should therefore distinguish:

`rating`
from
`confidence that rating predicts target behavior`.

# 26.6 Treat counter-authorship as a feature, not noise

When Ichinose exceeds Ayanokōji's prediction, the correct response is not to “fix” the character model until surprise disappears.

A strong model should preserve:

> **the possibility that developed actors can produce new answers.**

Perfect prediction would itself contradict one of the corpus's strongest developmental findings.

# 27. Meritocracy at the Year-2 boundary

The series remains neither simply pro-meritocracy nor anti-meritocracy.

Year 2 supports a more demanding position.

A legitimate merit system would need at minimum:

1. **bounded measurement object**  
   State what is being measured.

2. **date / observation context**  
   Preserve when and how the evidence was obtained.

3. **development sensitivity**  
   Permit the person to outgrow the file.

4. **participation awareness**  
   Distinguish inability from withholding/refusal.

5. **task-fit discipline**  
   Do not universalize a score beyond the task it predicts.

6. **multi-domain structure**  
   Academics, physical performance, social influence, judgment, teaching, and generativity are non-identical.

7. **record integrity**  
   The official output must be trustworthy.

8. **model-use restraint**  
   A profile should improve coordination without becoming title to the person's future.

9. **proportionate consequence**  
   A bounded performance deficit should not silently become total personhood judgment.

10. **counterforce / corrigibility**  
    The measured person and other observers must be able to challenge the model.

ANHS repeatedly satisfies some conditions and violates others.

That tension is the analytical substance of `実力至上主義`.

# 28. Year-1 → Year-2 claim-transition ledger

## PRESERVE

1. Ability is real and plural.
2. `実力` is not one universal hidden stat.
3. Every measurement has an object, date, observer, and consequence.
4. Possessed ability and expressed ability are distinct.
5. Development can make old data stale.
6. Contribution and personhood are distinct.
7. Kōenji proves capacity is not automatically collective property.
8. Sudō proves static selection underestimates developmental uncertainty.
9. Generative/distributed ability is politically important.
10. Record integrity is necessary for meaningful merit claims.
11. An honest file can remain incomplete.
12. Ordinary life contains valuable capacities formal exams do not exhaust.

## STRENGTHEN

1. **Measurement is reflexive.**  
   Public OAA changes how other students treat the measured person.

2. **Performed availability is a distinct variable.**  
   Kōenji makes this impossible to ignore.

3. **Revelation and development can coexist in one rating trajectory.**  
   Ayanokōji's OAA sequence is the canonical case.

4. **The same rating can have opposite practical meanings under different rules.**  
   V01 vulnerability versus V09 marginal value.

5. **Social contribution is not social causality.**  
   Ryūen makes the distinction explicit.

6. **High social capacity is morally non-self-interpreting.**  
   Kushida strengthens this.

7. **Generative capacity may be measured indirectly in other people's outputs.**  
   Horikita, Hirata, and Sudō strengthen this.

8. **Relational history can be genuine competitive ability.**  
   V12 formalizes it.

9. **Low-stakes environments can have high diagnostic value.**  
   V11 provides the strongest control.

10. **A better model can improve prediction without granting ownership.**  
    *Second List* strengthens the final formulation.

## REVISE

1. **“OAA measures ability.”**  
   → OAA measures institutionally legible performance across selected proxy axes.

2. **“A rating change means the character improved.”**  
   → Rating change may reflect development, expression, participation, observation, or some combination.

3. **“Low OAA means low value.”**  
   → Task design determines marginal value; V09 can invert the hierarchy.

4. **“High social score means good person.”**  
   → Capacity and ethics are separate.

5. **“Low social contribution means weak social ability.”**  
   → Prosocial contribution, influence, coercion, leadership, legitimacy, and strategy must be separated.

6. **“Overall rating is the best universal comparator.”**  
   → Aggregate is useful only for the selected weighting and purpose.

7. **“Leadership ability belongs to the leader.”**  
   → Distributed/generative leadership exists in the performance of the network.

8. **“If a hidden elite ability existed earlier, later growth is fake.”**  
   → Preexisting competence in one domain does not erase genuine development elsewhere.

## DOWNGRADE

1. OAA overall score as a universal power-ranking tool.
2. One exam result as a complete character ability statement.
3. Class points as a direct measure of class talent.
4. Formal office as proof of superior leadership ability.
5. Visible confidence as evidence of measured competence.
6. Relational popularity as a complete proxy for social intelligence.
7. Quietness as evidence of low judgment.
8. participation refusal as evidence of incapacity.
9. rapid rating rise as evidence of equally rapid latent-capacity growth.
10. “unexpected action” as proof the earlier model was irrational; development itself can invalidate a once-reasonable model.

## REJECT

1. `実力` is one context-free stat waiting to be discovered.
2. A measurement becomes total because it is official.
3. The school can directly measure latent ceiling.
4. OAA is an IQ test.
5. OAA social contribution is a morality meter.
6. High strategic ability implies high prosocial contribution.
7. Low academic rating implies low marginal value in every exam.
8. A known capacity is automatically available to classmates.
9. A disabled student's lower physical aggregate implies lower general capability.
10. A developed person's later usefulness retroactively validates coercive treatment used earlier.
11. Exceptional observed competence proves White Room origin.
12. The goal of a good character model should be to eliminate every future surprise.

2. Can ANHS ever represent generative ability without over-rewarding visible officeholders?
3. Can the institution represent voluntary unavailability without treating autonomy as deficiency?
4. Can relationship-specific judgment be measured without converting intimacy into surveillance capital?
5. Can a composite handle disability without encouraging irrelevant cross-domain inference?

# 32. Canonical through-Year-2 synthesis

Year 1 asked whether the school could see the student.

Year 2 asks what happens after the school publishes what it sees.

That shift is decisive.

A private file can misdescribe a person.

A public file can reorganize the person's environment.

OAA changes:

- who gets approached;
- who is avoided;
- who is expensive;
- who appears safe;
- who appears disposable;
- whose development becomes surprising;
- and what strategies become rational.

Yet the Year-2 corpus repeatedly shows the person escaping the profile in different ways.

Ayanokōji changes the amount of himself he permits the file to see.

Kōenji refuses to let known capacity become automatic collective property.

Sudō develops fast enough that an old weakness label becomes a strategic asset under the right exam.

Horikita becomes strongest not by maximizing one personal stat but by making different people more usable without fully absorbing their agency.

Ryūen demonstrates social causality that a prosocial-contribution metric cannot contain.

Hiyori demonstrates judgment that may remain quiet and relationship-specific.

Kei demonstrates social competence without total emotional autonomy.

Kushida demonstrates that measured social capacity does not contain its moral use.

Sakayanagi demonstrates that a mathematically valid aggregate can become philosophically invalid when one domain is generalized beyond task relevance.

Ichinose demonstrates that dense knowledge of particular people can become elite competitive ability once the environment finally asks the right question.

V09 then gives the system its most elegant self-critique:

> **the institution can use its own low rating to make the developed student more valuable.**

That rule acknowledges, mechanically, that the file and the person can diverge over time.

And *Second List* closes the year by making the file richer without pretending it has become omniscient.

The terminal Year-2 ability principle is therefore:

> **A measurement is legitimate when it remains a claim about observed performance under stated conditions. It becomes epistemically dangerous when it silently changes into a claim about latent ceiling, universal task value, moral worth, or entitlement to direct the measured person's future.**

The terminal developmental principle is stronger:

> **The best evidence that a person exceeds a model is not that the model was useless. It is that a once-useful model can be outgrown, contradicted, or answered by a subject who has become capable of generating something the model did not already contain.**

That is the current through-Year-2 meaning of `実力`:

> **not a single score, but real capacity moving through development, expression, availability, environment, relationship, observation, and choice—always partially measurable, never safely identical to the file.**
