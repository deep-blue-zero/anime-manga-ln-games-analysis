# Succession and separation: does development survive the developer?

## 1. Responsibility and observation window

This is the architecture's cumulative succession ledger frozen at `Y2SL`. It records the withdrawal of a function, not merely the departure of a person. Independent judgment before separation, a surviving group immediately afterward, and durable institutional survival are three different tests. A transfer at the terminal boundary permits a departure finding; it does not supply next-year performance.

## 4. Revision ledger

**Confirmed continuity:** centralized competence remains effective and risky. **Genuine development:** Horikita, Sudō, the successor friendship and rival polities acquire capacities not exhausted by the initiating relation. **Revelation:** private motives and previously unrecognized attachment become visible; they are not automatically newly born. **Retrospective context:** Volume 0 explains the developer/dependent problem's institutional genealogy. **Correction:** survival after sacrifice does not prove the sacrifice necessary; a talented replacement does not prove successful succession. **Relationship-specific expression:** Kei's peer autonomy and romantic dependence can move differently. **Unresolved contradiction:** the boundary contains tensions between growth and ownership, but no invented post-departure evidence resolves them.

## 5. Handoff test

A future succession update must record who actually performs the missing function, with what information, whether they can refuse the predecessor's objective, who absorbs failure, and whether the result persists when the former center does not intervene. Mere victory, affection, formal replacement, or the predecessor saying they have grown is insufficient.
