# 1. Governing distinctions

Each row preserves five questions that later synthesis tends to collapse:

1. What does Volume 0 establish as primary-source fact?
2. What did the earlier source record actually establish?
3. What was the strongest reasonable inference before Volume 0?
4. Does the new fact confirm, sharpen, correct, recontextualize, overturn, or merely add prehistory?
5. Which characters know the fact, as distinct from what the retrospective reader now knows?

Revision actions have the project-wide meanings `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT`, and `OPEN`. Status labels follow the Volume 0 protocol exactly: `CONFIRMS`, `SHARPENS`, `CORRECTS`, `RECONTEXTUALIZES`, `OVERTURNS`, and `ADDS_PREHISTORY_WITHOUT_REVISING`.

# 4. Highest-impact claim transitions

## 4.1 The institutional ontology changes

Volume 0 changes the White Room from an apparently self-contained training institution into a political counter-project to ANHS, then into a patronage network, and finally into Atsuomi’s private counterstate. The earlier descriptions of control and experimentation remain true. Their political genealogy is now explicit.

## 4.2 Kiyotaka becomes less reproducible, not less formed

The mature formulation is neither “the White Room made him” nor “the White Room merely discovered him.” Its own strongest scientist judges that broad elite production is reproducible while Kiyotaka’s highest ceiling probably is not. Singular capacity and extreme environment are both necessary.

## 4.3 Later freedom remains development

Shirō wants freedom while childhood Kiyotaka rejects it. The prepared ANHS doorway therefore does not turn later ordinary-life attachment into an unchanging hidden desire or a complete paternal script. Volume 0 establishes constraints and mediation, not total predestination.

## 4.4 Environmental authorship receives a political genealogy

Atsuomi’s sushi/curry doctrine gives explicit form to the practice of causing another person to prefer a different outcome while preserving subjective satisfaction. Kiyotaka later modifies this grammar through staged agency, developmental separation, and partial handoff, but he does not invent its jurisdictional logic from nothing.

## 4.5 ANHS can only be an alternative through counter-authorship

Because ANHS and the White Room share political genealogy, institutional purity is no longer an available criterion. The stronger test is whether participants can dissent, create independent evidence, leave, form purposes the institution did not assign, and preserve futures not exhausted by institutional ranking.
