# Blue Box synthesis architecture

## 1. Purpose, scope and authority

This design preserves **independent manga and prose interpretation before comparison**. It must support longitudinal character/relationship and ordinary-life analysis without treating prose interiority as manga narration, a supplement title as chronology, or a merged summary as a full account of either medium.

The metadata boundary is Japanese main manga V01–27 plus prose Interlude/Prologue. No narrative or continuity relation is admitted. The [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) alone routes authority. The [manga method](BB_MANGA_METHOD.md) governs the main lane, and the separately adapted [prose method](BB_PROSE_SUPPLEMENT_METHOD.md) governs any explicitly authorized supplement operation. INITIAL canonical scope is rules/metadata only, with owner acceptance and readiness unclaimed.

The [initiation policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md), [generic architecture](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MULTI_DOCUMENT_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE_TEMPLATE.md), [reconstruction method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md) and current archive/execution/handoff controls retain authority over their responsibilities.

## 2. Source classes and precedence

Locked, readable, inspected manga pages govern claims about their text/art and disclosure. Locked, inspected prose governs its narration, represented interiority and wording. Neither medium has automatic universal precedence over the other. A source-specific claim is evaluated in its own witness; a comparative claim requires both exact routes and a justified relation.

Import metadata establishes reported presence, medium and credits, not content, authorship of specific decisions, chronology or continuity. The inventory includes 三浦糀 and 七緒, but creative responsibility and narrator authority require actual source-specific evidence.

Illustrations, covers, notes, afterwords, bonus material and previews are separate components with spoiler boundaries. Author statements support stated intent, not automatic literary interpretation. External/secondary information retains its declared context/hypothesis role. Adaptations and other editions remain excluded until independently identified and admitted.

Interlude/Prologue are separate prose witnesses, not main V28/V29, not automatically a prequel or chronological insert. Similar names/events are candidate correspondences until exact evidence supports alignment. No prose statement silently fills manga ambiguity or an absent inner monologue.

## 5. Admission, sequence and checkpoint contract

The candidate main source unit is a verified manga volume, with chapter/movement frontiers. Verify numeric order and actual chapter/image mapping. Each prose supplement is separately authorized with its actual chapter/section frontier; its title does not insert it into the manga sequence.

A run declares lane, exact start/terminal scope, exclusions and checkpoint cadence. For the 27-volume main planning range, a provisional ceiling of four volumes between full reconciliations can be considered when the run is defined; this is neither authorization nor an arc claim. Prose segment checkpoints follow actual complexity rather than being forced into that cadence.

Each accepted operation closes full independent reading and synchronized registers atomically. Freeze entering/exit knowledge, claims and prospective forecasts. Scoped no-change findings reflect actual inspection. Unread pages, uninspected prose, illustration debt or missing updates remain visible holds.

Before comparison, ensure both source-facing readings exist and were accepted independently. A comparative operation does not reopen frozen prospective knowledge or automatically create a combined continuity. Checkpoints preserve full local arguments and ordinary material, then reconcile role gaps, claims and possible comparison dependencies.

## 6. Time, knowledge and continuity

The state key combines lane/witness/version, admitted frontier, disclosure position, story-time constraints, subject identity/knowledge, physical/affective condition, role/resources, directed relationship and interpretive mode. Modes are MANGA_PROSPECTIVE, PROSE_PROSPECTIVE, RETROSPECTIVE_WITHIN_ADMITTED_LANE, CROSS_MEDIUM_COMPARATIVE and UNRESOLVED.

Narrator presentation, subject thought/belief, reader knowledge and analyst inference remain separate. A prose narrator's access must be established; it does not grant the manga reader or subject that information. Similar actors or events may have verified, possible or unresolved correspondence.

Change causes distinguish DISPOSITION, KNOWLEDGE, RELATIONSHIP, CONTEXT, ROLE, RESOURCE, REVEALED_NOT_NEW and UNRESOLVED. Source variation can be a different account, not an in-world psychological change. Preserve ordinary controls and opportunities; dramatic or interior passages alone do not establish a general trait.

A comparative state never overwrites lane-specific history. If integration is later justified, record which claims are shared, source-specific, compatible additions or unresolved contradictions, with exact retained routes.

## 7. Contradiction and revision

Conflict types include witness/mapping, chronology/disclosure, narrator/focalizer access, subject ignorance/report reliability, medium affordance, source revision, role/context, interpretation, missing evidence and possible continuity inconsistency. Do not force harmony or presume the prose is a correction merely because it is explicit.

Use PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT and OPEN. Record prior/new formulation, source/knowledge boundary, evidence/counterevidence, rival disposition, confidence and affected outputs. A comparison may strengthen a comparative claim while leaving the earlier manga uncertainty valid.

Propagate changes separately through each lane's states, form/language, monographs, comparisons and models. Frozen readings/forecasts retain their historical bytes. Corrections use linked errata or superseding current claims with reciprocal authority metadata; full displaced arguments remain retrievable.

## 8. Specialists, monographs and integrated synthesis

A monograph needs adequate time, relationship, ordinary-context and expressive coverage. It integrates motivation/rivals, behavior, presentation and function, with source lane explicit. A manga-only monograph cannot silently adopt prose interiority; a cross-medium monograph requires an explicit integration scope and retained differences.

A relationship study earns an independent home when directed development, asymmetry or surrounding institutional conditions require sustained work beyond subject monographs. Small practical interactions and contrary evidence must survive alongside dramatic statements.

Potential form, narration, institution or cross-medium specialists remain anticipated. Commission only when actual recurring evidence creates an independent load-bearing question, using complete frozen readings and tested source routes. If no independent specialist is needed, give an evidence-based rationale and assign responsibility for the role.

Convergence reconciles or retains specialist conflicts and medium differences before integrated-synthesis readiness. Continuous synthesis connects the accepted corpus without collapsing its media into one narrator or concatenating abstracts. It can remain manga-centered with clearly bounded supplement comparisons; no compulsory composite canon is assumed.

## 9. Reconstruction and evaluation

Reconstruction is intended later for sufficiently evidenced, source-established subjects. Models, evidence/retrieval, monographs and independent tests have distinct homes. No subject roster or readiness grade exists; global discovery/capability registries belong to designated agents.

Model identity is subject@lane/witness/frontier/state. A manga-only model excludes prose-only interiority; a comparative model requires a documented scope and conflicts policy. Narrator access and subject knowledge remain different even within prose.

Twelve descriptive coverage dimensions are goals/values, threat/appraisal, motive conflict, knowledge/errors, attention/reasoning, affect/regulation, disposition/condition, directed ties, role/audience, repertoire/inhibitors, written/graphic expression and development/exceptions. Missing contexts and source discrepancies restrict scope.

Each policy specifies trigger/stakes/state → response family, inhibitors/overrides, exceptions, support/counterevidence, rival and falsifier. Distinguish disposition from condition; trace appraisal → decision → objective → recipient-conditioned expression. Record confidence, distance from observed contexts and abstention/multiple-response criteria. OBSERVED, CANDIDATE_POLICY and PROMOTED_POLICY are separate.

Test ordinary interaction, pressure/conflict, uncertain/false knowledge, changed recipient/tie, role/audience shift and consequential choice where supported. Separate decision fidelity from Japanese/prose/graphic voice fidelity. An absent audio witness bars performance claims.

Freeze genuinely held-out predictions before exposure; authors and evaluators are independent and latent familiarity disclosed. Exposed exercises are RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST. Outcomes are SUPPORTED, PARTLY_SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY, CONFOUNDED and UNRESOLVED, with meaningful baselines, perturbations, decoys/negative controls and opportunity denominators. No fabricated accuracy metric.

Synthetic outputs never become primary evidence, canonical quotations or independent corroboration. A well-written monograph does not certify operational prediction scope.

## 10. Separate gates

| Gate | Acceptance responsibility |
|---|---|
| B0 Foundation | Both adapted methods, source/medium boundaries, sixteen duties, schemas and depth reviewed |
| B1 Admission/run | Exact lane/source/capability verification, owner-bounded scope and OPEN precondition; absent now |
| B2 Sequential completion | Full independent manga/prose source-facing outputs and actual component coverage |
| B3 Longitudinal reconciliation | Lane-specific causal, time/knowledge, character/relationship and form histories reconciled |
| B4 Claim stabilization | Central retrieval, preserved alternatives and revision propagation within/between lanes |
| B5 Character/specialist readiness | Role-gap and ordinary/context/linguistic/visual coverage assessed by scope |
| B6 Specialist completion | Full scoped monographs/studies and authorized comparisons with evidence/limits |
| B7 Specialist convergence | Conflicts/dependencies/medium differences addressed; no-specialist route justified/owned |
| B8 Integrated synthesis readiness | Complete full inputs, adequate role coverage and explicit integration boundary |
| B9 Synthesis acceptance | Sustained argument retaining medium differences and causal/relational/linguistic depth |
| B10 Reconstruction evaluation | Independent exposure-controlled model assessment at exact lane/state scope |
| B11 Final validation | Actual files checked for routes, authority, depth, names/references and remaining debts |
| B12 Release/freeze | Defined immutable corpus, source/medium limits, current routes, history and integration acceptance |

Source-complete is not synthesis-ready. Main completion does not close supplements or prove official work completion. A bounded release states included/excluded lanes. Genuine interpretive openness can remain; missing required inspection cannot be hidden.

## 11. Execution, transfer and extension

SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS covers future source readings and interpretive updates; DEEP_SYNTHESIS covers checkpoints, specialists, monographs, comparisons and model/evaluator work; PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST covers architecture and integrated synthesis because medium conflation propagates. Stable mapping uses BOUNDED_STANDARD; hashes ROUTINE_FAST. Resolve actual settings via the [reasoning policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_REASONING_AND_MODEL_ROUTING_POLICY.md).

The [long-series protocol](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_LONG_SERIES_HYBRID_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md) favors local source-capable readings/ledgers, fresh fully supplied synthesis, independent review and designated integration. Current access is metadata/documents only. Verify transport, processing, manga STILL_IMAGE/prose TEXT/needed illustration inspection, interpretation and persistence under [capability routing](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EXECUTION_TOPOLOGY_AND_CAPABILITY_ROUTING_POLICY.md).

[Handoffs](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md) include base snapshot, complete exact input/output paths/digests, both methods when relevant, authority, source/lane/admission/knowledge boundaries, owners and unresolved obligations. Return full outputs and change/evidence manifests. Compare base/candidate/current and inspect for lost ordinary-life, causal, linguistic, formal and counterreading detail. One mutable owner per shared register prevents concurrent lane overwrites.

New sources extend inventory only after identity/scope review and never automatically extend a run. Add schema for demonstrated recurring roles; record migration and proportional affected-evidence backfill. Preserve current register history, frozen accepted readings/checkpoints and genuine archived arguments with reciprocal authority metadata.
