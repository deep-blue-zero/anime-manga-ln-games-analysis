# Character, relationship and knowledge ledger

## Responsibility and schemas

All records are lane-qualified. No character, relationship, chronology or shared continuity is established at INITIAL. Manga expression and prose interiority are different channels, not duplicate statements of a single hidden truth.

| Record / key | Required fields |
|---|---|
| Subject/state `BB-S-nnnn` | Source-established identity/name at frontier; lane/witness/version; disclosure/story time; behavior/speech/represented thought/report channel; appraisal/motive alternatives; affect/role/resources; disposition/condition; evidence/full argument |
| Knowledge `BB-K-nnnn` | Subject/narrator/reader; proposition; known/believed/reported/unknown state; acquisition/disclosure; narrator-access warrant; lane/frontier; alternatives and later revision |
| Directed tie `BB-REL-nnnn` | A→B; lane/time/frontier; information/expectation; conduct; source-earned trust/conflict/obligation/access dimensions; context/audience; counterevidence; independent B→A route |
| Ordinary opportunity `BB-O-nnnn` | Subject/tie; routine/low-stakes situation; actual options/opportunity; action/non-action; comparable cases; negative-evidence denominator; contrary case/limit |
| Causal/institutional state `BB-A-nnnn` | Conditions; perceptions/appraisals; options/constraints; action/consequence; group rule/custom/report if relevant; unknown links; rival; evidence and full analysis |
| Change/correspondence `BB-CH-nnnn` | Before/after or paired lane states; DISPOSITION/KNOWLEDGE/RELATIONSHIP/CONTEXT/ROLE/RESOURCE/REVEALED_NOT_NEW/UNRESOLVED cause; same-subject relation confidence; source difference versus in-world change; revision route |

Modes are MANGA_PROSPECTIVE, PROSE_PROSPECTIVE, RETROSPECTIVE_WITHIN_ADMITTED_LANE, CROSS_MEDIUM_COMPARATIVE and UNRESOLVED. A prose-only thought never fills a manga unknown without an explicit comparative claim. Similar names or scenes do not alone prove identical continuity.

## Update and preservation

Create identities from admitted evidence, preserving concealment and later disclosure. Append actual states and scoped no-change findings after each accepted reading. Preserve ordinary behavior and directed asymmetries; a dramatic gesture is not a complete relationship.

Full readings develop motives, causal alternatives and language/form warrant. This ledger routes them. A source difference can be narrator access or changed presentation rather than development; retain alternatives. Negative evidence requires an actual opportunity.

Checkpoint review separately checks each lane's chronology/knowledge and coverage, then examines justified correspondence links. Material revisions propagate to form, comparisons, studies and models without overwriting the other lane. One active owner controls this shared surface.
