# Blue Box prose supplement analytical method

## 1. Independent lane, independent warrant

This method adapts the [generic novel method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_NOVEL_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) for the separately reported **Interlude** and **Prologue** prose witnesses. It is not a manga page-reading shortcut. The [manga method](BB_MANGA_METHOD.md) governs the main sequence, while the [architecture](BB_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) owns eventual comparison.

No prose passage is admitted. Titles do not establish chronological placement, narrator, cast, story contents or a pre-main reading order. Combined inventory credits include 三浦糀 and 七緒; exact source/component roles need verification. Do not assign individual intentions or authority from those names alone.

A future prose operation needs its own exact authorized witness, segment and terminal scope. Neither completion of the manga nor presence of a supplement authorizes it. Record prior exposure and exclude unadmitted knowledge.

## 2. Witness and textual coverage

Use `BB-PROSE-INTERLUDE` and `BB-PROSE-PROLOGUE` with immutable bytes, exact edition/language and versioned spine/extraction map. Keep present, received, verified, admitted, inspected and accepted distinct. New bytes are a separate witness pending equivalence.

Map actual headings/chapters and spine components: narrative prose, illustrations, front matter, afterword, bonus, preview or unresolved. Preserve the supplied order and stated exclusions. Metadata and keyword searches do not establish full prose coverage. Illustration/title/afterword spoilers need their own frontier.

Read all selected prose in TEXT with Japanese comprehension and actual extraction fidelity. If page images are needed to verify layout or a difficult textual object, record that route. An illustration claim requires STILL_IMAGE inspection, not a textual caption.

## 3. Prose locators and linguistic evidence

Use witness/version + verified chapter/section + scene/movement + economical contextual cue. Deterministic identity includes immutable digest, spine/member, extraction/map version, paragraph/block identity and short verified anchor or character range. Record normalization and retain original Japanese where transformed search forms differ. Reflow page numbers are not printed pages.

Quote briefly and mark EXACT_VERIFIED, WORKING_TRANSCRIPTION or PARAPHRASE. Preserve consequential original wording, base/ruby, agency, tense/aspect, modality, address, ellipsis, politeness and reported-speech framing. English glosses state important ambiguity. Test central retrieval in the locked witness; pending locators remain unresolved.

Public analysis carries eligible quotations and sanitized routes, not full prose or private source identifiers.

## 4. Narration, focalization and knowledge

Distinguish narrator assertion, focalized perception, represented thought, speech, memory, testimony and analyst inference. Reliability belongs to a particular statement or access claim, not a global label assigned because one assertion is doubtful.

Keep source disclosure, inferred story chronology, subject information acquisition, publication history and analyst admission separate. A chapter's later explanation cannot be read into its earlier scene prospectively. Preserve intentional anonymity and exact naming boundaries.

Prose may represent interiority that manga does not depict, but its actual access must be established passage by passage. A thought in one witness is not automatically the inner truth of the corresponding manga moment. Narrator stance, source authorship and continuity remain separate questions.

## 5. Causal, character and relationship depth

Develop condition → perception → appraisal → affect → options/constraints → choice/non-action → consequence, identifying unsupported links and viable rivals. A first-person account or narrator explanation need not exhaust motive. Pacing, ellipsis, ordering and descriptive emphasis contribute evidence without automatically settling causality.

Preserve ordinary routines, small interactions, pauses and practical choices as controls for dramatic behavior. Distinguish disposition from temporary condition, knowledge, role, resources and relationship context. A change can be REVEALED_NOT_NEW; no later passage retroactively changes what was knowable earlier.

Track directed A→B/B→A relationships with separate beliefs, expectations, speech/action, access, obligation, trust/conflict and alternatives. Situate Japanese utterances by appraisal, objective, speech act, recipient and register. Written voice is not performed audio. Depiction, explanation, evaluation and endorsement are distinct.

## 6. Form, images and cross-medium limits

Analyze focalization, narration/interiority contrast, scene transitions, repeated words/images, rhythm of sentence/paragraph/chapter and withheld information. A motif needs variation and counterevidence. Do not import manga composition as if it were prose syntax, or infer a panel adaptation where no exact correspondence exists.

Inspect illustrations separately, recording placement, visible content and source frontier. Uninspected images remain a debt; a prose reading cannot certify their effect. An illustration does not automatically overrule ambiguous narration.

After independent readings and explicit comparison authorization, pair exact passages/pages and classify relation: verified overlap, compatible addition, changed focalization, changed event/causal relation, contradiction, unmatched material or unresolved. Keep each witness's full argument and knowledge boundary. No composite “definitive scene” silently replaces them.

## 7. Output and acceptance

A future prose reading includes exact receipt/coverage, entering state, full movement-level argument with thesis/rival, narration/focalization/knowledge, character and directed relationships, ordinary controls, Japanese language, prose form, inspected illustration/paratext and debt, counterevidence/revisions, synchronized registers, exit freeze and limitations.

Use the shared warrant and revision vocabulary defined in the manga method, with an explicit PROSE source lane and channel. Confidence needs evidence; conflicting interpretations remain retrievable. Full reasoning is mandatory—no synopsis, extracted quotations or manga comparison table substitutes for an independent prose analysis.

Before semantic acceptance, promotion or integration, perform the same bounded name/reference check: introduction, substantial middle, conclusion/exit and major relationships/ambiguous actors, preserving speaker/thinker/recipient and source-required concealment. Use the six classifications in the manga method and record actual scope/material findings.

Accept full prose and five registers atomically. Missing components, untested central routes or essential uninspected images remain holds. Frozen prospective readings preserve their original knowledge and source boundary.

### Current-unit story synopsis

*Prospective amendment, 2026-11-13, under initiation policy v1.11 §5.3.*

Each completed plot-bearing prose reading includes a clearly labeled **Current-unit story synopsis** near its front, after brief witness/coverage and entering context, before thesis and extended analysis. Declare the independently authorized Interlude or Prologue witness and actual segment/terminal scope. The title establishes no position in the manga chronology, and a shared register does not create a combined narrative unit.

Use connected, accessible prose to explain the opening situation, named participants and stakes, consequential actions and decisions, causal turns, disclosures and ending state within that prose boundary. Preserve concrete scene and physical-event mechanics where the text establishes them. Distinguish inherited developments from newly presented events; distinguish narrator presentation, focalized perception, represented thought, testimony or belief, and analyst conjecture or unresolved outcome. Narrator access must remain source-supported. Material event claims have retrievable prose locators or stable observation references; they support a readable account rather than replacing it with an evidence table. Scale detail to the actual segment without a fixed quota.

Compose or finalize this unit-end, spoiler-bearing account after all applicable entering and internal prospective captures are frozen; shield it and the completed reading from earlier-horizon analysts. It may disclose outcomes established in the current authorized prose unit, but must not borrow later unadmitted prose or manga events to fill gaps. Keep any separately authorized comparison distinct, with each source's own warrant. The synopsis supplements the full independent causal, narrational, linguistic, character, relationship, formal and counterreading argument, image/paratext accounting and five-register transaction. Acceptance requires both the synopsis and every existing duty; frozen readings are not rewritten and the CLOSED analysis lock remains governing.

## 8. Model and handoff boundary

A prose-derived model has its own witness/frontier/state and narrator-access limitations. Any cross-medium model needs an explicit integration decision, admitted paired evidence and unresolved differences; it cannot silently pool sources.

Follow the architecture's conditional policy, confidence/distance/abstention and independent held-out evaluation contract. A monograph is not an operational model. Exposed tests are RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST; synthetic output never becomes primary evidence. Handoffs carry complete prose readings and counterarguments beside manga readings, not a merged summary that erases modality and provenance.
