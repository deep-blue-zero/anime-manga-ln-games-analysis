## Thesis

**SEM001.** Seminar is an institution in which making something answerable can mean several different things: whether a structure can bear a load, whether an idea is new, who will pay, whose information may be used, and who must keep working. These questions often produce useful scrutiny. They also expose a selective standard when reputation, affection or a desired remedy changes what counts as enough evidence. The resulting institution has real administrative capacity and real shared pleasures, while its formal warrants, complete accounts and recipients’ permission remain narrower than its members’ confident language. [SC01] [SC02] [SC03] [SC08]

**SEM002.** Yuuka, Noa and Koyuki are distinct participants rather than one council personality. Yuuka enjoys calculation, asks others to share work and can revise a judgment; she can also press a declined activity or retain unwilling helpers. Noa likes reviewing inventions, poetry, plain tastes, company and her own private diary; practical help and affection can coexist with a request to stop that she records rather than honors. Koyuki can complete useful work yet dislike repetitive calculation and further battle duty. Her play, clover, manga, chosen objects and wanted companion have value before their utility to an emergency is decided. These lives make the distribution of resources and information consequential. [SC02] [SC03] [SC04]

**SEM003.** The central argument is that practical contribution, welcomed company and legitimate authority require different evidence. A built device is not a calibrated predictor; a successful computation is not indefinite availability; a fitting gift is not consent to confinement; and a privacy intervention is not permission for every later question. Keeping these distinctions does not make ordinary achievements lesser. Shared making, a few wanted games, a night’s sleep, a gift actually accepted and a local apology are substantial endings even when stronger technical, legal or restorative claims remain open. [SC01] [SC02] [SC04] [SC08]

## Fifteen institutional dimensions

### 1. Formal authority

**SEM004.** The group account securely identifies the local Seminar context through its meeting room, accountant and secretary functions and explicit institutional self-reference. Yuuka describes the twenty-eighth deliberation as a quarterly review of research and development plans for next-period allocations, with increased budgets for guaranteed utility and practicality. ### 2. Practical authority

**SEM005.** The accountant’s questions have immediate force over whose project proceeds. ### 3. Legitimacy

**SEM006.** Noa’s defense of curiosity is a substantive institutional position: researchers choose a direction, and work that appears meaningless may become a future foundation. Yuuka recognizes possibility while distinguishing uncertain future connections from an immediate budget. This disagreement concerns allocation under uncertainty, rather than whether minor wants deserve a place in analysis. The represented standards become less defensible when a student’s reputation prevents a hearing or a prestigious inventor substitutes for missing guarantees. The question of legitimacy is therefore claim-specific: which test is explained, whom it hears, and whether the decision responds to its own unanswered objection. [SC01] [SC02] [SC03]

### 4. Resource control

## Eight serious rival readings

**SEM025.** Useful load, novelty and payer questions support a rational-allocation reading. ### R2 — private greed explains the whole council

**SEM026.** Yuuka’s hoped financial remedy and prestige exception make personal investment relevant. ### R3 — benevolent care authorizes discipline

**SEM027.** Fitting pajamas, accepted gifts, ordinary games, replacement labor and finite apologies support real care. ### R4 — Noa’s neutrality is complete knowledge

**SEM028.** Precise observation, useful review and indispensable build help support competence. ### R5 — talent creates indefinite availability

**SEM029.** The cipher bottleneck and participant-corroborated solve make reliance on Koyuki reasonable at a concrete point. ### R6 — continuing company completes repair
