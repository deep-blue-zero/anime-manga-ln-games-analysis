## 4. Eight serious competing readings

### R01 Only coercion defines the institution

**WHCLAIM046.** Compulsory living arrangements, pressured participation, explosion and repeated detention make a strong coercion reading necessary. It becomes incomplete when it cannot explain the actual off-ramp, chosen later contribution, protected question/canvas boundaries, useful cleanup and ordinary company. These positive acts do not erase force, but they prevent a claim that every recipient's art, enjoyment and choice is only an institutional illusion. The better account asks which particular demand, helper and recipient has changed, while preserving the restrictions that have not. [WH01:L93,103,119-169.]

### R02 Successful art vindicates every method

**WHCLAIM047.** The beautiful illumination and named praise supply a plausible success narrative. The source counters its universal form through initial pressure, unnotified activity, guard sleep/work costs and Rena's second detention for forbidden entry and ringing. Artistic accomplishment is a finite good, not a legal pardon or evidence that every risk was understood. Proposed future access and display also remain prospective. A success reading can therefore be retained only if it keeps a distinct ledger of permission, consequences and uncompleted material results. [WH01:L149-169.]

### R03 The ritual proves a supernatural institution

**WHCLAIM048.** Eri's prophecy, circle interpretation, moving statue and unresolved voice make supernatural reading a live literary possibility. They do not form one independently verified mechanism. Kanoe admits an initial prank but denies the later unidentified voice; narrator-observed statue movement faces competing explanations; the actual final supplies are LEDs/tape and guards expose their pretended weakness as playing along. A bounded account preserves strangeness and different beliefs while withholding demonstrated summoning, prediction accuracy, soul identity and universal magic-law conclusions. [WH01:L77,101-115,141-169.]

### R04 The chair's praise certifies complete legitimacy

**WHCLAIM049.** Milia's relayed named chair message provides institutional recognition with more weight than an unacknowledged private aspiration. It remains a message with proposals: no onsite chair, built viewpoint, permanent exhibit, acquired collection image or formal future visit is printed. Guards' appreciation likewise does not cancel rules. This rival fails when it treats mediation as inspected governance or praise as completed rights implementation. The recognition can be positively received at its actual mode without giving it authority over every earlier or future recipient boundary. [WH01:L159-169.]

### R05 Liking is either trivial or universal aesthetic consensus

**WHCLAIM050.** Kanoe's explicit liking without a special reason and Tsumugi's wish for company oppose dismissing ordinary taste as analytically worthless. Eri's confidence and local light appreciation likewise have recipient meaning. But liking need not prove a shared universal ranking, all wishes fulfilled or a finished future collection. Rena's calm preference and skepticism remain alongside the artwork. The rival therefore works only as a particular response: small goods are fully eligible while retaining who enjoys what, when, and without converting one pleased audience into every recipient's assent. [WH01:L127-131,157-169.]

### R06 Rena is merely antisocial or secretly converted

**WHCLAIM051.** Rena's objections could be read as an unchanging contrarian persona, while her later bell act could be used to dismiss them as hidden belief. Both flatten the represented sequence. She names enjoyed company, a fashion dream and other enjoyable alternatives, declines one actual dangerous operation, receives an off-ramp, then helps and continues to dispute wish fulfillment and irregular access. Later help supplies neither total conversion nor proof her earlier no was false. The source supports a recipient who can care, differ and refuse in connected circumstances. [WH01:L109,131,139-169.]

### R07 Recognition closes Eri's development

**WHCLAIM052.** Eri's felt possibility and explicit acknowledgment of impatience support a real confidence change.
