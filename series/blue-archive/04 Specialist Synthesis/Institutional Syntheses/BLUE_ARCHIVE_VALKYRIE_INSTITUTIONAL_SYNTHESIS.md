## 1. Core thesis and distinct institutional responsibility

Valkyrie's declared protective purpose does not tell us how its work is allocated, what its members want, or what a person encounters when that purpose is put into practice. The accepted stories make those questions materially different. Returning a child's hairpin, reaching a hostage quickly, threatening a park camp, detaining students, restoring a lost child's family contact, guarding a leisure venue and helping an abducted teacher are all called useful police work or justified through office. Their means, recipients and endings differ. This synthesis argues that Valkyrie's service becomes legible through those differences: **an official account of useful work can diverge from the labor actually done, the leisure actually wanted and the affected person's response**. No one measure—capture, obedience, praise, diligence, gratitude or apparent toughness—successfully contains the institution.

This is an institutional mechanism, rather than three private portraits added together. Fubuki's macro displaces an unanswered equipment request between bureaus; Kirino's desire to show ability overcomes a dispatch boundary; Kanna's record-copying and meal constraints condition who can receive help and who has time to enjoy it; a deputy's affectionate secondment creates another work arrangement; an owner, shopkeeper, child or detainee can contradict the authority's interpretation of the encounter. The work moves across roles, resources, procedures and audiences. Its consequences are often distributed while its credit is concentrated. That pattern provides an independently retrievable question under the existing Valkyrie home. It does not supply an academy charter or comprehensive police law. [G270; K17; F18; I17; E837; M4C1; M4C2]

Wanted rest and food are part of the question in their own right. A peaceful afternoon, particular doughnut, savory oden, mystery novel, shared spa, air-conditioned room or desired meal with a superior need not first cause a rescue to deserve interpretation. Conversely, their value cannot make the methods of obtaining them harmless. The narrative can grant Fubuki pleasure in doing nothing while showing that an automated reassurance obstructs a colleague; it can grant Kanna an enjoyable meal while preserving the subordinate invitation she refused. The claim is neither that leisure is failure nor that every sincere protective act validates governance. **Useful service and a wanted life have to be examined together with the people whose labor or freedom supplies them.**

The source scope remains a comparison of situated practices. MAIN provides ordered public operations and disputed authority; GROUP270 provides a locally continuous ordinary-to-incident case; the private accounts preserve whole message tails and conditional written forms; E837 and E856 provide complete accepted event arguments with contrary afterwords; E860 provides a specifically dream-qualified police encounter. Their comparison does not create a global chronology, population frequency or predictive model. The mandatory Misuzu section in §9 preserves the correctional interlocutor's full available literary responsibility without assigning every speech turn to one securely identified person.

## 3. Formal office, practical authority and resource dependence

Valkyrie has differentiated bureaus, commands and work routes in the received texts. Life Safety, Security, Public Security and the correctional/fire-service comparison cannot be collapsed into one interchangeable officer. GROUP270's dispatch expressly tells Life Safety that it need not deploy. Its unnamed chief remains an unjoined role; resemblance to a familiar office is not evidence that Kanna issued that instruction. In the private macro case, an unnamed Security executive needs a borrowed barricade returned, while Fubuki's automated reply sends that executive back to Security. The failure has an institutional shape: a resource obligation disappears into an answer that recognizes words but not the requesting role. No repaired program or returned barricade is shown. [G2702701u0024–0032; F18 base16008E002]

Those differentiated roles do not establish a complete faction map. A local chief, executive, deputy or correctional interlocutor can have a distinct obligation without representing a unified bloc. Likewise, a suspension report, chosen secondment or threatened reflection exercise does not expose the full appointment, appeal or funding system. The positive argument is about how these represented arrangements distribute work and authority; missing administrative records limit stronger governance claims rather than making the ordinary encounters analytically ineligible. [G270; F18; E837; E856; M4C1; M4C2]

Kanna's Schale duty makes administration concrete. Receipt sorting, mapped sightings, rejection rules and record-room copying are different burdens from field toughness. She describes Schale's relative freedom, but does not thereby acquire Schale's authority or establish that every Valkyrie rule is pointless. Adult disorder among security-expense receipts calls for sorting, not an automatically proven embezzlement charge. Her patience with accidental mistakes has a distinction between corrigible errors and malicious acts; that aspiration remains separate from her public compromise defense. A rule can obstruct useful work, and a procedure can also preserve an evidentiary difference the institution needs. [K17 base20023E002; M4C1E019]

Resources and corporate sponsorship become coercive in V004. Kirino's budget-shortage report, the visible Kaiser Industry guns and Kanna's sponsor-backed eviction account are not a complete fiscal ledger, but they show why armed capacity cannot be treated as self-sufficient moral legitimacy. A school's capacity to enforce a removal can increase while the justification for using it is contested. The rebate hypothesis later gains document encounter and non-denial support; it never becomes a fully audited corporate chain merely because the protagonists find it plausible. In Chapter 2, Kaiser Security's practical street power exposes the converse: a corporate force can control passage while ordinary medical access depends on a police student's negotiated exception. [M4C1E009/E015–E020; M4C2E009–E011]

E837 brings a quieter but equally institutional dependence into view. A private waterpark lacks lifesavers; public students are placed into its staffing arrangement. Kanna questions that use of police personnel and initially identifies the Life Safety route. Konoka reports approvals and personnel arrangements already made. The later admission that Kanna could refuse and chose the opportunity prevents describing her as wholly compelled; it does not erase the initial consultation limit or answer every funding and authority question. The assignment serves citizens, commercial opening needs and a deputy's wish to help an overworked superior at once. Those motives coexist without supplying the complete contract. [E837001–002/007]

The owner also tries to assign the costs of an unsuccessful hazard search to the officers' labor. Teacher's Schale identity changes the owner's manner, and the resulting takoyaki work is shown, but a formal damages adjudication is not. Later the owner apologizes when the hazard is demonstrated and offers support. Chihiro separately promises Engineering responsibility for the damage. These are distinct changes in response and promised liability, not evidence that every affected visitor or worker has been compensated. Actual power to condition work, credible testimony, institutionally recognized status and eventual accountability remain separable. [E837005–006/008/013]

E841's sponsor assignment makes institutional image into another labor obligation. Kanna explains company supplies in exchange for advertising and an improved Valkyrie image, invokes superior orders, and avoids Fubuki's invitation to share the costume. Kirino and Fubuki object to unexplained clothing and public performance; they also want unlimited and new doughnuts. The familiar shop makes refusal difficult in Fubuki's own account. A declared strike is not a completed strike. Their actual help for Mari, her thanks and Fubuki's reported easing of tension show useful interaction within the arrangement without proving that the original assignment was freely wanted. A narrated more-than-doubled sales report is not audited revenue. Here material support, publicity, command, labor, pleasure and felt refusal cost converge in a distinct institutional transaction. ## 4. Recruitment, belonging and the definition of a useful police student

The school has a public image of capability, but the stories contest the capacities it recognizes. Kanna reports that she wanted Life Safety as a first-year and was told her face would make citizen-facing work difficult. That is appearance-based career advice, not demonstrated innate incapacity or an opened rejected application. Her lost-child encounter does show a failed attempt to reassure: the child cries, the suggested smile worsens it, and she needs assistance. The actual successful return then combines her noticing and staying, Teacher's calming and information gathering, and nearby Life Safety students' radio handoff and transport. The institution's useful service is distributed. Neither the old advice nor Teacher's encouragement exhausts what happened. [K17 base20023E006]

Kirino wants recognizable ability and a transfer, but she expressly denies wanting crime and values returning lost children. Her satisfaction in that work is reported memory, not a newly enacted rescue in GROUP270. In the private children's encounter, patient questions and explanations do produce actual praise for fun, learning, kindness and being listened to, despite a failed handcuff demonstration. A passing Valkyrie student supplies the release. The positive recipient endpoint does not require pretending that the demonstration was competent, nor does its practical failure eliminate the pedagogic value of the encounter. Her later reported Guard-transfer refusal in V004 Chapter 2 belongs to that chapter's letter-level administrative account; we cannot date every private ambition into a conversion that caused it. [G2702701u0006–0018; I17 base13012E006; M4C2E024]

Fubuki wants an unhurried civil-servant life rather than wealth, fame or continuous dangerous work. That written profile is neither a false want nor the whole of her behavior. A request about a grandmother-made hairpin tests her institutional rationale about personnel, emergency readiness and future demand. The child contests that rationale by noticing habitual naps. Fubuki then searches outside her announced policy, admits that the child's face moved her, accepts Teacher's help, finds the hairpin and returns it. Her proposed concealment of the search prevents interpreting this as an installed new request policy. It also prevents public recognition from being the sole measure of useful care: something valuable happens that she wants credited as accidental incoming lost property. [F18 base16008E006]

None of these students is an unlimited substitute for another bureau or colleague. Fubuki's assumption that Kirino will handle exceptions does not establish Kirino's capacity or agreement; Kirino's private mistakes and desires give that person a finite repertoire. Kanna's judgment that Life Safety members are good police but not yet suited for serious crime is conditioned written speech, not a final restriction on growth. Training, citizen-facing skill, local knowledge, stamina, record work and material equipment remain different capabilities. The school's myth of spectacular suppression can undervalue quiet competence while still being right that a particular dangerous operation needs better resources. [F18 baseE002; K17 written Lobby5322–5323; I17; G270]

## 5. Ordinary life: food, rest, company and a cost that must not erase their value

Peace is a wanted public condition and a wanted personal afternoon. Fubuki's enjoyment of no complaints and a nap is not evidence that she wishes citizens harm. Kirino's wish for chances to show ability is not a wish for chaos; the teasing question and her explicit denial must stay apart. Their unequal preferences already matter before the hostage alarm. So does the old-fashioned doughnut Fubuki associates with the shop. The shop is consequently more than a tactical location: it belongs to an ordinary taste-world that the subsequent police method damages. [G2702701u0001–0026]

Kanna's cafeteria account locates a subtler constraint. Food is available within limited hours, but absorbed work can outlast them; no rule prohibits going out, while she feels uncomfortable leaving unfinished work. Her peers want to celebrate with her and talk to their chief. She declines, partly because she expects her presence to suppress their speech or enjoyment. Their insistence is contrary evidence to that expectation. Her subsequent chosen stall meal establishes the worth of flavor, warmth and trusted company; it does not show the peer gathering happened or that every work explanation was deceitful. The institution loses a wanted peer opportunity while the individual achieves another wanted meal. [K17 base20023E005/whole200230210]

At the stall she says she can remove armor and settle. Her theory that revealing an ordinary self would embolden criminals is image management, not criminology. Tender beef-tendon oden and special tea lead to a narrated enjoyable meal, with no need for a crime resolution to make that pleasure substantial. In the swimsuit cleaning episode, completed work precedes initiated spa rest and a return when the manager calls. She expressly enjoyed herself, despite a principle against seeking fun on duty. Felt fatigue easing is a situated report rather than clinical cure. These scenes give ordinary feeling a place in police life without proving that every colleague now knows her private self or that earlier public obligations have been discharged. [K17 baseE005; swimsuit10096E005]

Fubuki's pleasure also has specific successes and failures. She actually enjoys the Old Fashioned with coffee on a stakeout, while the suspect remains uncaught and identification remains weak. The apparent success in food is not the operation's success. Her isolated poolside paradise produces heat, warm cola and painful furniture, then loses the doughnuts and cold drinks available during an official break. The manager mistakenly praises her for working through that break. The error criticizes the way she obtains rest and the way the institution recognizes work; it does not make unproductive time intrinsically worthless. Her whole MomoTalk tail after the float rescue separately preserves delicious ice cream and a local willingness to patrol together occasionally. Neither the failure nor the promise can erase that small positive remainder. [F18 base16008E005; swimsuit20037E003/E005]

The public gift-and-patrol sequence keeps the same ordinary objects without becoming one private festival itinerary. E810's working-holiday doughnuts, smiles, tiredness, apology and practical cooperation belong to different actors' wants and contributions. A gift's offer is not automatically consumption: the current comparison expressly keeps Fubuki's unconsumed offer and Kirino's revised edible-cuff form. E811 then lets Fubuki be bored and attracted to beautiful clothes and a mask. Those tastes need not first contribute to containment to count as her interests. Kirino's offered food method, proposed alternative dinner and learning ideal remain proposals where no eating or tested interrogation method is supplied. The narratives retain gifting, chosen company, fatigue and useful help alongside security failure, without making any one of them a universal police ethic. [E810 narrative001–003/current gift qualifications; E811001/005–006]

Receiving a benefit also includes choosing its form. Fubuki dislikes the cucumber doughnut she is pressed to finish; a food form does not guarantee taste. Kanna refuses another slide ride. Kirino's conditional mission writing prohibits tickling even while it represents laughter. After E837, Fubuki wants leave or doughnuts, Kirino wants recruitment but has sore muscles, and Kanna refuses return tickets while keeping the reason for her smile confidential. ## 6. Recipient-defined service: children, customers, suspects and peers

Kirino's holiday patrol gives recovered property, a citizen's thanks and food subsequently shared with Teacher, while the thief escapes. These outcomes answer different questions. They do not certify every earlier suspicion, complete criminal adjudication or a universal entitlement to intimate gratitude. [K17 baseE006; F18 baseE006; I17 baseE002]

The hostage/shopkeeper's voice exposes the danger of compressing service into capture. ## 7. Internal allocation, recognition, discipline and institutional memory

Two private failures show how labor can be concealed or misread. Fubuki's macro asks a Security executive to consult Security about Security's own missing barricade. The material request remains unanswered, and visiting criticism creates more written work. Her poolside account later receives exemplary-lifesaver praise precisely where the audience misunderstands her conduct. Compare the hidden hairpin search that achieves care she prefers not to acknowledge. Official praise can attach to misdescribed duty while actual useful labor can be kept outside public credit. The mechanism is not simply hypocrisy: it is a mismatch among intention, task completion, description and what the audience can see. [F18 baseE002/baseE006/swimsuitE005]

Internal discipline is present and still bounded. GROUP270 confirms suspension after the shop explosion; the macro confirms severe scolding and explanations; Kirino's hostage injury case gives a reported reprimand. These are different grades of consequence. Suspension is not the half-month special leave Fubuki requested, nor restitution to the shopkeeper. A report of reprimand is not a completed victim-repair programme. Later useful work cannot be made evidence that no discipline occurred, while discipline cannot be used to invent demonstrated reform. The institution's response must be evaluated at the endpoint actually supplied. [G2702702u0060–0063; F18 baseE002; I17 baseE003]

The waterpark's deputy coda tests how a manager distributes leisure and labor. Konoka works quickly on some approvals, leaves disliked paperwork and assumes subordinates want a pool. Compulsory combat swimming comes with a promised preferred ramen, while a subordinate wants cake or macarons and others object to their limits. Her own superstition prompts refusal to enter; peers lift her despite release/down objections. No complete laps, agreed meal, finished paperwork or cured superstition follows. The affectionate project of giving Kanna rest cannot validate this treatment of other workers, and the peers' response does not convert a refused touch into permission. [E837019]

That uneven allocation can be compared with positive coordination. Nearby Life Safety students finish the lost-child return; a passing student frees Kirino from handcuffs; police peers coordinate station access; Public Security students later join a crisis despite missing orders. These are source-local contributions with their own knowledge and means. They neither make all junior work invisible nor prove an institution has no internal capacity. [K17 baseE006; I17 baseE006; M4C2E018; F00E013]

Festival cooperation makes that distributed capacity retrievable at smaller scale. In E818007, Hasumi credits prompt police reports while Kirino credits earlier shooting help; these are reciprocated actor reports, not freshly enacted earlier incidents. Heat, a promised robot-made doughnut and Fubuki's suggested fake illness coexist with Kirino's explicit rebuke and formal patrol commitment. In008, sightings alone are insufficient. A stallholder's observation, equipment tags, access records and the officers' recognition combine into a location inference. The accepted raw-text actor qualifications distinguish Fubuki's forgotten-memory lines from Kirino's formal self-reproach. Police contribute to an actual inquiry without owning all knowledge, securing every suspect biography or demonstrating lasting interschool affection. Nor can these two objects alone supply the later confrontation's outcome. [E818007u0095–0123; E818008u0001–0063]

Institutional memory is likewise selective. Public credit in V004's ending omits RABBIT/FOX's operational roles; Kanna's reinstatement and Kirino's declined transfer are reported after a distributed intervention. The public account is not the direct operation. Private imagery of armor, a frightening nickname and doubts about the unchosen bureau make that gap personally consequential, but cannot authenticate a public record. ## 8. Crisis, other institutions and Sensei's limited counter-authority

MAIN gives the school a genuinely adverse public history. Kanna's sponsor-backed pressure on RABBIT and defense of dirty compromise must remain beside her later intervention. The sequence has stronger support than a vague allegation alone—located document encounter, non-denial, public report—while still lacking a complete ledger and judgment. It would be equally misleading to erase compromise because she rescues people or to declare every later helpful act merely false because she compromised. Her changed participation is actual; its legal and institutional aftermath is incomplete. [M4C1E016–E020; M4C2E018–E024]

Chapter 2 differentiates institutional belonging from valid command. FOX's resources attract RABBIT, but a civilian bombing order is refused, and the other members return voluntarily. Kanna and Life Safety contribute despite suspension; Decartes supplies an imperfect diversion; Sensei supports rather than supplies every tactical act. The existing police procedures quoted by characters remain assertions, not opened statutes. This is a distributed refusal and intervention against a particular command, not a universal doctrine that a favored teacher or student can lawfully override every office. [M4C2E012–E024]

Final Chapter 1 exposes vulnerabilities in representation and control. A false Valkyrie escort works because the office's appearance can be borrowed; a Kaiser-held Valkyrie site does not mean every Valkyrie student shares Kaiser's purpose. Wounded Kanna's extraction, her return of seized devices, Kirino/Fubuki's help and later student participation carry real risk and local effects. Her corrupt self-description and protective explanation for juniors do not become a full legal verdict or immunity instrument. Arona's brief local signaling, RABBIT's response and Sensei's field-command deference to Miyako keep the operation collective. Communications return; not every governing arrangement is formally restored. [F00E007–E015]

Sensei can provide a different receiving condition. He supports testimony the waterpark owner dismisses, interrupts an unsupported accusation, helps with the hairpin search and calms a frightened child. Such assistance has specific good endpoints. He also repeatedly brings food after Kanna's refusal, accepts a food-inspection fiction with Fubuki, pressures an unwanted tasting and participates in Kirino's inverse-aim drill despite her refusal to target an innocent person. Her actual target success and reported exam pass do not erase that refusal. His care and authority require the same recipient tests as the officers' care. A useful adult presence is not a blanket consent mechanism. [E837005–006/010; K17 baseE003/E006; F18 baseE006/swimsuitE002; I17 baseE005]
