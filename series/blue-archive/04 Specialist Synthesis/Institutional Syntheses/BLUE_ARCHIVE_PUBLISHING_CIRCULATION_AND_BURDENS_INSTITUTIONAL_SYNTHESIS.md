## Thesis

### Thesis — Circulation as a conditional encounter

[PUBCLAIM001] Publishing makes an encounter between wanted work and possible readers materially possible, and changes who can judge, keep, share or ask for more of that work. The strongest positive case is not a forecast of sales: it is Mel completing a wanted manuscript, Momiji's actual assistance, Takane's reading, Trinity readers buying and sharing, and Mashiro eventually reading and asking for a continuation. These particular ends deserve attention even if a future book is never finished or an institution never becomes just. They also differ: a manuscript accepted by a recipient is not a print run; reader pleasure is not permission to appropriate the maker's next work; an actual settlement is not an inspected final royalty ratio. Publishing is answerable through these differences rather than through a single success label. [PS02:98–132; PS03:99–159]

### Thesis — Burden and refusal inside useful work

[PUBCLAIM002] The same circulation that opens a new audience can turn hunger, time, property, admiration or publicity into leverage. Yakumo brings useful distribution and later acknowledges book quality, yet presses a consignment decision against immediate payment and uses a false princess biography that Takane rejects. Takane gives real criticism, yet unfinished work can meet a demand to finish before sleep. Mel wants creation and sometimes company, yet presses the adult's declined portrait and objects when she is physically carried to a meeting. These are consequential actions with separate agents and recipients. Later gratitude, renewed willing work or pleased readers support positive afterstates without retroactively authorizing each earlier act or supplying permanent assent. [PS01:279–303; PS02:104–132; PS03:92–135; PS04:80–118]

### Thesis — Roles, local sequence and the ordinary

[PUBCLAIM003] This institution comprises makers, critics, distributor, helpers and readers whose ends can cross without becoming identical. Takane's cooking, sports company and landscape photography, Yakumo's planning pleasure, railway rest and refusal to waste food, Mel's tools and imagined café, and readers' quiet, sharing and private interpretation remain intrinsic evidence. They are not material discarded because they lack a crisis endpoint. The current argument uses four complete maintained analytical carriers received through EOF, preserving their producers' underlying source scopes and modes. [PUBCLAIM041] Tea, photographs, hockey, beansprout craft, rail rest, books, company, money received and creative wishes are directly wanted activities, with their own disappointments and refusals.
