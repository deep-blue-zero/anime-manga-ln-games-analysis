## 1. Core thesis

C&C makes two kinds of work visible at once: forceful operative assignments and the cleaning, serving, maintenance, study and company through which its members want to live. The institutional problem is how those capacities become obligations to particular people. A mission, a maid outfit, pleasure in serving, reported internal delegation and a useful result each supply some evidence; none supplies all the others. The complete café sequence gives this distinction practical form. C&C undertakes a temporary civic request and Yuuka reports a successful financial result. Neru nevertheless rejects Yuuka's proposal to make café work permanent. A valuable contribution has occurred without extinguishing the contributors' right to distinguish one occupation from another. [G12:L52–78]

The strongest bounded account is therefore an institution whose work becomes answerable through allocation, recipient response and correction, rather than through an assumed identity of perfect maids or indispensable combatants. C&C can defend school resources, contribute to a rescue, enjoy a costume and do actual housework. Its initiatives also create damage, surprise a leader with an already reported commitment, misread a customer, pressure a companion or extend help beyond the recipient's request. These are different failures and achievements. They make governance a set of contested relations, including who is informed, who may decide, what was actually requested, who bears costs and which result is shown. [P1:L46–56; P2:L40–60; G12:L56–78; T01:L298–302]

Chosen work deserves a precise meaning here. Akane's attachment to being a maid, Karin's affection for C&C despite earlier disappointment, Asuna's spontaneous enjoyment of service and Toki's wish to be useful are affirmative evidence. They do not prove that every task, disguise, bodily demand or employer arrangement is chosen on the same terms. Neru's jacket preference and refusal of permanent café conversion are equally intrinsic to the occupational account. Quiet evidence enters because it reveals what people want and how they negotiate a role; it need not first produce danger, income or a major plot turn. [A01:L242–268; K01:L237–253; AS01:L335–341; T01:L230–238; N01:L222–258]

This package owns the institution's student/operative labor responsibility and its two literal named arguments below. The differentiated-company ensemble, the individual character monographs, Seminar's wider allocation problem and the directed relations among particular members retain their separate homes. The institution argument draws on those people's acts and desires without claiming that institutional completion completes their friendship or every individual life. It is a bounded literary synthesis, with ROOT receiving and canonical adoption pending. [C01:L315–368; C05:L1–356; C07:L1–30]
