## 2. Core thesis: particular care does not confer a general claim over others

Pandemonium's recurring institutional difficulty is a mismatch between the purpose it announces and the interests it makes answerable to that purpose. An audit announces scrutiny but preselects rejection; publicity announces collective greatness but seeks Makoto's recognition; hospitality offers a guest something wanted but converts a missing drink or a fall into a budget penalty. These are neither interchangeable acts nor proof that care is unreal. The strongest reading is that the office can generate genuine pleasure, accommodation and useful action while using its institutional position to magnify one participant's purpose into a demand on other people. The literary argument concerns that conversion and its resistance, not a rule predicting every later decision. [PS05] [PB14] [PS02]

The audit makes the conversion unusually visible. Makoto admits the proposed training paperwork has no problem and says no adjustment could alter her refusal. Iroha invokes inspection, applies reported publicity requirements the Prefects may not have heard, and makes cocoa availability a hospitality criterion. When Ibuki falls, she says she is fine and will not cry, but Iroha recasts the event as making her cry and announces zero funds. The wanted drink and concern for a child remain real; the inference that an unrelated training budget should answer for them is the institutional excess. The Prefects' objections and the final processing limit supply internal contrary evidence rather than an external moral standard imposed without a witness. [PS05]

The reverse simplification also fails. Removing ordinary pleasures from analysis would make it harder to see what authority threatens or supports. Ibuki's cocoa and pudding, Iroha's reading and rest, Satsuki's wanted food and requested company, Chiaki's search for good article material, and Makoto's shopping, pictures and concern for Lionmaru have intrinsic ends. They do not become analytically important only when they interrupt a war. Their particularity defeats the notion that every member is simply a component of a government machine or that useful service is the sole measure of belonging. A failed photograph or incomplete collage is consequential as a frustrated want even when no public order changes. [PS01]

The office's limits are also practical and sometimes avowed. Iroha says redistribution needs formal processing; in the holiday account Makoto rejects forcing Iroha to stay because loyalty should be voluntary; MINI150 has an apology qualified by Ibuki's demanded promise. These are meaningful contrary cases to unrestricted coercion. They are not durable institutional safeguards merely because they were spoken once. The proper comparison retains actual local acceptance, refusal, compensation or cooperation and asks separately what remains unprinted: future compliance, treasury transfers, fair burden sharing, victim consultation and safety. [PS05] [PS04] [PS35]

The central institution therefore has no single measure of success. Makoto can gain attention without admiration; a party can resume without settling accounts; a cannon can fire without a validated mechanism; a child can forgive without consenting to every official act; an official can help effectively while withholding information or rejecting imposed work. Pandemonium is narratively productive because these measures intersect and diverge. Its mythology of Gehenna greatness is repeatedly forced to meet the ordinary persons whose lives would have to make that greatness desirable. [PS02] [PS04] [PB16]
