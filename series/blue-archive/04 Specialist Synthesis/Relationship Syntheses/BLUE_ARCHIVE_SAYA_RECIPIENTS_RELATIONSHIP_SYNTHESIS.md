## 1. Core thesis

[SR001] Saya's relationships repeatedly turn on the difference between another person answering an invitation and another person being made useful to a project whose success she defines. She wants lasting inquiry, recognition, delicious food, play, and company. These wishes make her more than a dispenser of harmful concoctions. Yet the same wish to continue can turn an objection into a new experiment, a symptom into usable information, or a material request into the next demand. The relationship question is therefore whether the other's answer can alter the encounter, including the purposes and costs of participation. Saya sometimes hears that answer, sometimes accommodates it, and sometimes evades its force. The local differences matter more than a verdict that all her care is fraudulent or all her trouble harmless. [N03:L37,L41; N05:L37,L41; N10:L41,L49,L53; N20:L124]

[SR002] The directed sides are heterogeneous. Sensei can worry, refuse a sample, object to risks, help finish a task, or share a cake. Club members object to earlier effects and decline further experimentation; Saya later admits deceptive invitations even as she denies force. Nezusuke is her family-like companion, but his motives are mediated by narrative gestures, other people's conjectures, and her translations. Shun refuses unfamiliar food and interference, subsequently asks for a remedy, and still wants indulgence after returning to her usual appearance. Kisaki can seek medicines and reject prescribed rest or Kai's conditions. Rumi's food can recruit Saya without proving that the whole political or technical arrangement is freely accepted. These recipients do not form one interchangeable audience. [N03:L37; N08:L41,L57; N10:L41,L53; N17:L76,L88,L90,L92; N18:L95,L97; N19:L116; N20:L104,L114]

[SR003] Quiet goods are central to the mechanism. Saya explicitly wants an invitation to mean food rather than an experiment; she wants someone to stay until cake is finished, play a two-player game, visit unfamiliar restaurants, and enjoy the smell of a flower-filled park. She can thank a collaborator for searching or helping with work. These ends are positive evidence even when they cause no public crisis. Their particularity also sharpens the contrary evidence: inviting someone to remain for company is different from lying about a pill, and a pleasant shared meal does not answer another recipient's hunger or weakness. A relationship can contain real attachment and fail to protect the other's independently wanted life. [N05:L41; N06:L37,L41,L65; N08:L49,L61; N09:L41,L61; N10:L41,L57; N13:L41,L49]

[SR004] The strongest supported conclusion is a repertoire of differently bounded reciprocity, not a finished conversion from experimenter to carer. Saya has practical compassion, valued pleasures, fallible diagnoses, dangerous assumptions, useful expertise, evasions, and appreciable acts of help. The sources print several completed encounters and local corrections, but omit clinical verification, all delayed repair, durable consent practices, and a universal chronology.
