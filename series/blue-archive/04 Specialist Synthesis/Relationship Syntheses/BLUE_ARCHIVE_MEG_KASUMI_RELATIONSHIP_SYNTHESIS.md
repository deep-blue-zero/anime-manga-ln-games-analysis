## 1. The relationship's distinct question and source boundary

Kasumi offers to make Meg's preferred activity possible. Meg makes Kasumi's attachment to that activity personally intelligible. Their relationship therefore involves two directions that a simple division between strategist and excavator cannot hold. Kasumi handles possibilities, membership, plans and encounters with authority; Meg brings labor, remembered care, enjoyment and an account of what she wants. Each can give the other something valuable. Each can also misunderstand an act's meaning, or participate in a project whose effects exceed a preferred craft. The question of this synthesis is how that enabling arrangement supports ordinary life while transmitting leverage and pressure, and how their own exchanges test the difference between following someone and choosing a life with them.

## 2. Remembered origin: comfort given before its meaning was fully understood

GROUP3501 gives a conversation about a first encounter, rather than a performed flashback. Meg selects a smaller snow-viewing bath although other baths are hotter or larger. She explains the preference as Kasumi's, then admits Kasumi never stated it. Her inference rests on a memory: Kasumi appeared to be sleeping in snow and distressed; Meg dug a spring, put her in it, and perceived relief. Kasumi recognizes the episode, asks about her expression and ultimately gives hot springs exceptional personal value. The exchange supports converging recollection and the present importance of remembered comfort. It supplies no observed original incident, clinical revival, cause of distress, dated origin, or authenticated faction history. (G35 §4, `BA:group:3501:scene:001:u:0069–0106`.)

Meg's inference is fallible in a precise way. A remembered change in expression becomes a guess about taste. Kasumi's question about when she stated the preference makes the gap between perception and declaration visible. It is a correction inside an affectionate exchange, not evidence that Meg fabricated the entire memory. Kasumi then endorses snow baths on her own terms. Meg does not simply define what Kasumi must like; her offered explanation becomes something Kasumi can recognize, question and appropriate. Care may precede shared understanding, and later talk can make its meaning less unilateral.

The encounter also reverses a convenient hierarchy. Meg is often described as the worker whose possibilities depend on Kasumi's planning, but here Meg's craft creates comfort for Kasumi. The person who supposedly supplies the brains has been the recipient of another person's practical attention. Kasumi's private closing that of course she would come to love baths preserves a special attachment without giving unrestricted access to her past. Hina's later unnamed “over there” aside and Kasumi's autobiographical voice claim do not automatically turn this memory into one coherent political or medical explanation. The relation is already significant in the relief remembered and pleasure presently shared.

Kasumi invites rest and warns Meg about slipping. Such small care cannot be reduced to a symbol of rescue or ideological recovery. It addresses Meg's immediate body and activity. Conversely, Kasumi's exuberant claim that a bath can wash away pain or revive someone at death's edge is her affective statement, not a clinical warrant. The warmth survives that limit. They can find something worth doing together without the narrator proving every therapeutic proposition attached to it.

## 10. Serious competing readings and a bounded longitudinal judgment

**A benign complementary partnership.** Meg reports wanted work; Kasumi cares about her wishes, invites rest and warns about slipping; comfort is remembered; peers return voluntarily. This rejects the claim that all affection is manipulation. Yet taught leverage, proposed transport seizure, imposed plans and recipient refusals persist. Complementarity is real but cannot absolve every means of sustaining it.

**A strategist exploiting an uncomprehending worker.** The script and Meg's inability to explain gifts give this reading weight. Confident logistics leave missing buses to a coercive proposal. Total exploitation nevertheless fails against Meg's practical care, bus question and volunteered company, and Kasumi's warning against automatic obedience. Asymmetry and dependence risks are warranted; deletion of all preference and acknowledgment is not.

**Restored obedience disguised as freedom.** The post-crisis activities resemble earlier life, and another ambitious target follows. Influence remains a serious concern. But E020 names the danger of obeying Kasumi, asks a want and gives an answer including activity and company. Dismissing an answer because it agrees with an older pattern repeats the rejected yardstick. Local choice remains positive; total cognition and permanent reform are untested.

**Complete liberation.** Commitment, rescue contact and the autonomy warning form supported local change. They do not establish the exact mechanism, population, redress or permanent independence. Repair is meaningful without being exhaustive. A complete-liberation claim would also obscure practical burdens and continuing public costs.

**Harmless comedy.** Shrieks, songs, failed plans and bath exaggeration are funny and socially meaningful. Genre does not cancel Hasumi's refusal, Nodoka's livelihood, lost housing/water, damaged commissions or delegated pressure. Nor must every laugh become only misconduct evidence. Pleasure keeps its literal local value; unprinted clinical/legal outcomes remain unprinted.

**A hidden origin explains everything.** Snow distress, voice testimony, the unnamed aside and Bodensatz suspicion invite a unified biography. Their disclosure limits do not establish it. Centering that account would make choices expressions of an authenticated past the sources have not supplied. Memory and uncertain history affect trust without exhausting its present meaning.

Within the supported MAIN sequence, enabling is tested by a disputed wish, turns toward protection, and asks whether agreement is automatic. Across unordered ordinary/event contexts, work, care, humor, requests, guesses, delegated knowledge and company recur beside costs and refusals. The relation has reciprocity and self-questioning whose reliability remains particular. Neither timeless benevolence nor timeless exploitation follows; no reconstruction model is promoted.
