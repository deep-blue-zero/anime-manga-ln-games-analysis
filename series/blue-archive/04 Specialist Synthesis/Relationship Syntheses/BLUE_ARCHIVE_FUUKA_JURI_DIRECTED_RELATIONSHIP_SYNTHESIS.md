## 1. Core thesis: cooperation needs two willing lives and usable information

Fuuka and Juri's relationship is neither a completed equal partnership nor a one-directional story of a competent caretaker carrying a useless dependent. They repeatedly help one another, take another's wanted life seriously and produce real shared good. Their most persuasive positive achievement is therefore particular responsive cooperation: Juri helps a worried Fuuka; Fuuka supports Juri's chosen service and thanks her attempt to protect a holiday; later Juri offers food with refusal expressly allowed, and Fuuka accepts.
