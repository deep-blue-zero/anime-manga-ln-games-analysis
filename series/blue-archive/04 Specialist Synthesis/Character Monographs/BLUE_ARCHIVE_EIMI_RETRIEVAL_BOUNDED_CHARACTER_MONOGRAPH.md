# Eimi: wanted company, useful work, and what remains unfinished

Author body-first draft. Its source reception is personal; independent body QA and ROOT body adoption remain separate. The existing fixed home is retrieval-bounded, with no person-key merger or count grant.

## Efficiency does not finish the account

**EIMI-P001.** Eimi can call an arrangement efficient while wanting something that its practical explanation does not exhaust: shared ice cream, a song, a liked swimsuit, a quiet sky or another person's company. Her useful work also has distinct failures. A prepared fan can become hot air; a protective gift can be worn incompletely; a successful return can leave understanding and future memory uncertain. The strongest account keeps these particular wants and results together. It does not make ordinary pleasure wait for a public achievement, or make an affectionate explanation certify every method.

**EIMI-P002.** The name of this responsibility is a retrieval boundary. The private materials preserve BA_PERSON_EIMI for variants10001/20032 and BA_PERSON_CH0337 for 10136. Those printed routes are compared here without being merged into one registered person or one developmental sequence. The public G150 speaker is named Eimi but has an empty person join; she remains that printed source-local participant. Similar wording, equipment or affection cannot repair the missing join, and a future identity decision is not supplied by this monograph.

**EIMI-P003.** There are usable local continuities: an invitation leads into its paired encounter, a shopping promise is recalled, and a message tail can qualify what seemed a happy ending. There is no equivalent warrant for arranging all variants, written triggers, main crises and events into a single calendar. Self-reported change and remembered solitude matter as testimony. They do not prove the first time a feeling ever occurred, or that a later-looking outfit makes an earlier conflict obsolete.

**EIMI-P012.** Her outward agreement is immediately qualified by a private thought that deliberate injury might bring more attention. This is dangerous conditional reasoning, not an injury the episode shows her performing. It is also not a wish Sensei is shown to know or endorse. The thought makes desired care an uneven relation rather than a painless lesson in self-preservation. Retaining it does not require a clinical label or a forecast of future conduct.

**EIMI-P013.** At the desert search, finding nothing is locally useful negative data. Eimi worries about Sensei's heat, has brought water, and has prepared a small modified fan instead of undressing. Those are particular acts of attention. The account does not identify who made the modification or prove a general absence of anomalies. **EIMI-P014.** The fan's narrow reach becomes Eimi's reason for close contact despite Sensei's hesitation. Narration then reverses the hoped-for cooling into hot airflow from her body heat. The encounter therefore contains company, concern and pressure, as well as a material failure. Her earlier respect for one exposure boundary cannot establish mastery of every later boundary; an appreciative alternative response cannot stand for blanket permission to repeat the contact.

**EIMI-P015.** The postscript leaves diagnosis incomplete and countermeasures planned. Its different teacher responses cannot be combined into one certain physiological explanation. ## Readings that leave too much out

**EIMI-P057.** An instrument-only reading has efficient work, reconnaissance and equipment language to cite. **EIMI-P058.** An affectless reading can appeal to terseness, unreadability and others' reported labels. **EIMI-P059.** The flawless-technician reading has local protection, analysis, preparation and outside-hardware work behind it. **EIMI-P060.** An ideal-care reading retains real gifts, water, treatment, adapted cocoa, rescue and encouragement. **EIMI-P061.** A danger-as-whole-person reading can cite combat language, proactive force, an unsafe proposal and the private injury calculation. **EIMI-P062.** A complete-socialization reading has current comfort, wished cooperation and self-reported change to cite. The opposite claim of absolute isolation loses actual company and distributed help. **EIMI-P063.** A wholly teacher-created change or exclusive-romance reading loses Eimi's own inquiry, gifts, object making, requests, judgments and decisions to stop. It also loses peers, staff and different public contributors. Sensei's presence matters locally, and possible partnership or present sufficiency should not be dismissed. **EIMI-P064.** Finally, an achieved-mastery ending overreads the public acts. Eimi can keep an entrusted gun without fully understanding its giver's meaning; the party can escape without durable repair; hardware can enable a crack without finishing the later battle. Complete reading is not completed action inside the story. Hope, pleasure and commitment retain force precisely because future memory, reunion, safety and accountability have not all been supplied.
