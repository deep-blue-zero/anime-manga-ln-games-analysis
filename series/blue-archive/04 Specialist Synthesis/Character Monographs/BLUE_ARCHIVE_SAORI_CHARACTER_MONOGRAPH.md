## 1. Core thesis: usefulness can open a life without determining its worth

Saori repeatedly meets institutions and people who value what she can do while discounting what she needs. Her military training makes her usable under coercive command, attractive as a guard, productive as a laborer and vulnerable to assignments she does not fully understand. The available future becomes richer when usefulness ceases to be the only measure: a broken mask can remain a companion, transient drawing can please someone, a walk can be enjoyed before it provides samples, and dance practice can matter after a paid scene is cancelled. None of those ends requires permanent employment security to be worth doing. [V003C003/C004; BA:bond:10048:002/003/005/015;10101:002/003;10116:003 and wholeMM101160130.]

Her agency is nevertheless not a story of innocent intentions against a uniformly malicious world. She participates in serious violence and contests Azusa’s acquired life; later professional work includes a troubling coercive method and disagreement over its acceptability. Nor is every acceptance of help free of pressure. The strongest development is a growing repertoire of answerable choices: she can seek knowledge, terminate a deal, refuse pictures, choose quieter company, accept a meal, value clothing and repay care in a form another person can negotiate. These acts widen what she may do without erasing responsibility for what she has done. [V003C003E021–024/C004; EVENT834E009–015; private ten complete pairs.]

The central tension is economic as well as interpersonal. Opening an account and reporting later wages are real positive changes, while arrears, unread contracts, interrupted work, failed transfer and renewed exploitation remain. Self-blame may express her commitment to learning, but it cannot establish that every predatory demand was her fault. Conversely, an exploitative context cannot automatically justify how she treats every recipient of her work. The customer who seems opportunistic still voices a boundary; the employer’s approval does not certify her method as safe. [10048:002/003/005;10101:002;EVENT834E010–012.]

Her future therefore has concrete contents and continuing costs. The analysis must hold a valued object, skilled labor, taste, privacy, company and public accountability together. A mature Saori argument cannot end at either “rescued former antagonist” or “competent mercenary.” Both roles matter, and neither describes the whole range of her chosen life.
