## 1. Core thesis

Hinata often measures her place among others through usefulness, but her life is larger than the service she thinks she can supply. She carries heavy objects, maintains a meticulous ledger and cooks a delicately seasoned lunch; she also struggles to listen, makes mistakes, wants company, enjoys an exhausting day and learns to rest in particular situations. These distinctions matter because “only this” is her self-reduction, not an adequate account of what she does or wants. Her central tension is between the wish to help and the fear that accepting help, leisure or affection will make her a burden. The represented answers are concrete shared practices rather than a settled cure: another person takes light items, lunch is eaten together and a nap actually occurs. [U01:325–371]

The tension has public consequences. Gentle intention worsens a fragile scripture; a protective shot damages ruins; secrecy complicates access and reporting. Hinata can respond with apology and chosen responsibility without possessing control over every circumstance. When the ceremony schedule advances outside her knowledge, she still insists on doing the last step herself. She also asks Ui to preserve the book regardless of ceremony utility and says the tiring day was enjoyable. This is independent recognition of a peer's value and her own experience, not merely acceptance of a teacher's rescue script. [E01; E12:s1:u0001–0047; A01:007/010/015]

The monograph therefore treats helpfulness as both a genuine wanted activity and a fallible ethical practice. Strength does not certify handling judgment, guilt does not decide sole blame and a successful crisis outcome does not settle permission or harm. Equally, error does not make her ordinary pleasures worthless. Cooking, inviting, prayer, beautiful language, shared food, sea walks and simply wanting more time with someone belong to the person the duties are meant to sustain. They need no crisis justification to enter the argument. [E01:s1:u0001–0038; E14; U01:345–371/449–453/469]
