## 1. Core thesis

Koharu's vigilance is an unreliable interpretation of other people and a usable way of approaching them. A monitoring mission can give her a reason to call a teacher, reserve a seat and keep him nearby when the more vulnerable request is for help or company. Yet the request is not thereby counterfeit. She actually needs explanations, wants to apologize, buys a thank-you gift, worries at an absence, enjoys stars and asks someone to remain until she calms. A full account must keep those wants as ends, rather than convert every ordinary scene into evidence of a hidden policing identity or a stage in heroic rehabilitation. Her strongest continuity is this negotiation between the authoritative watcher she presents and the student who needs, chooses and sometimes protects others. [K01:143–220]

The negotiation does not culminate in an all-purpose cure. Actual calmer tutoring and an official 91 coexist with renewed suspicion, flight, frightened crying and injurious comic endings. Nor is vigilance always a meaningless false alarm: the adult has deliberately teasing or frightening choices, asks about a private book and fails to anticipate a reassurance's consequence. The represented choices are not compounded into one selected course. Her accusation about intent may remain unproved while a recipient's distress is real. The monograph therefore distinguishes the truth of a need, the truth of a consequence and the reliability of its explanation. A character can be wrong about another's motive, right that something hurts and still capable of a brave choice elsewhere. [K01:145–151/171–220; M02:third official exam; M03:E016–E023]

Public protection supplies an essential comparison, not the price of admitting her quieter life. Koharu helps Mika amid immediate abuse, refuses to erase Azusa's earned effort and preserves accessories to return. These actions show that fear and awkwardness do not exhaust her. The cookies, waterfall nap, scenery and requested company would remain literary evidence even without those crises. Their recipients and unfinished tails also prevent courage from being borrowed backward as a guarantee that every accusation, threat or encounter is safe. [M03:E016–E023; M04:E026; K01:182–220]

## 6. Crisis behavior

Her Mika intervention responds to actual immediate abuse, and her defense of Azusa answers a real threat to erase earned life. The tide incident distinguishes felt fear from both invented danger and guaranteed safety. The return path has become water; Koharu accuses the adult of knowing or withholding the tide. ## 10. Competing readings

An embarrassment-comedy account has genuine support: abrupt accusation, flight, prohibitive language, repeated startle and slapstick are major forms. It becomes inadequate when comedy is treated as either the absence of consequences or the absence of wants. Actual unconsciousness, offered gratitude, a genuine completed lesson and requested calming remain. The full tails deny both a harmless reset and a purely humiliating routine. Comic dreaming about food can be positive ordinary texture without being converted into an actual feast or a reason to ignore the preceding injury. [K01:153–220]

A growth-and-cure account also has support. The attempted apology, calmer basics, successful official91, admitted worry, protective acts and thankful pool gathering are material changes or accomplishments. Its total form fails because suspicion recurs, the apology exchange ends in flight, a successful lesson ends violently and desired reassurance can fail. Not every route's calendar is fixed. The stronger claim is a capacity for locally different responses amid continuing habits, not an upward variant ladder or permanent safety. [K01:145–151/281–283; M02/M03; E01:015]

A vigilant-protector account finds support in her Justice identification, genuine Mika care and defense of Azusa. It is too broad if it treats the danger script as a reliable detector: alleged intentions and audience readings remain unproved, and the private mission is self-assigned. Conversely, a wholly irrational or incapable account cannot explain earned91, completed basics, material care, brave public intervention or useful island help. One must distinguish justified acts from overextended explanations instead of letting either side cancel the other. [K01:143–151/245–277; M03:E016–E023; L02]

Finally, adult-led trust or a fixed romantic reading can point to contact, carrying, gifts, worry and proximity. It cannot transform conditional company into blanket assent, holiday repertoires into accomplished dates, embarrassment into an unseen act or unknown gift patterns into a visual fact. Independent peer agency and represented adult intimidation limit a wholly benevolent cure story; local achieved care limits a wholly exploitative one. Koharu's wanted life is intelligible without resolving every feeling into one genre or certifying every method. [K01:182–220/267–283; L02; E01:003–015]
