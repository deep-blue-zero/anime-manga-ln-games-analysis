## 1. Core thesis

Shizuko wants to make occasions that people enjoy, and she wants the enterprise that makes those occasions possible to succeed. Those desires are compatible in much of the received evidence. Tea, a remembered cup, a quiet willow walk, festival laughter and a successful shop are goods she values, rather than decorations around a hidden crisis identity. Her difficulty appears when she tries to secure the response she wants by controlling its presentation, its scale or another person's participation. Some attempts become responsive craft; some exhaust her; some apply unwanted pressure; one summer encounter proceeds past a request to stop before the adult calms her. A serious account needs all of those possibilities. Neither a permanently manipulative merchant nor a wholly harmless hostess explains the actual range. [Private account, whole; BA:bond:23006:002/003/005/006/015; BA:bond:26008:002/003/005; BA:event:810:047; GROUP2301–2302.]

The strongest developmental movement is a distinction she begins to make between finding an appealing performance and choosing the work she herself wants to do. In the ordinary clumsy-service experiment, the adult's lack of the expected response leaves her attempting another tactic. In the summer sequence, ambitious expansion and distress about customers lead into renewed service, a self-directed business plan and an insistence that the decision is hers. This supports a local shift in how she names her choice, not a complete conversion from commercial vanity to authentic care. She still wants growth, praise, funding, a good shop and the Teacher's company. Those persistent wants are part of the development, and the violent embarrassment response is part of its adverse evidence. [BA:bond:23006:002:u0063–0084; BA:momotalk:23006:thread:230060010, whole2449–2471; BA:bond:26008:003:u0013–0073; BA:bond:26008:005:u0011–0055.]

Her public competence is real at its proper extent. She negotiates preparations, serves food that is actually appreciated, weighs proposals against expense and injury, recognizes another organizer's achievement, and joins a dangerous public undertaking after initially questioning it. Competence does not supply control of all outcomes. Festival damage, refused requests, failed plans, overwork, unfinished expansion, unverified property claims and other people's dissent remain. Her ability to make welcome meaningful is therefore best read as work among independent people whose responses matter, rather than an ability to manufacture universal happiness.
