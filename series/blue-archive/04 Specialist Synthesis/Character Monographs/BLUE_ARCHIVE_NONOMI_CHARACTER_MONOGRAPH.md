## 1. Core thesis

Nonomi makes belonging concrete by imagining things people can enjoy together. Snacks, cleaning, shopping, games, picnics and an expanding private list are ways of giving a threatened school a wanted everyday future. Their value comes from the activities and company themselves. They need not repay debt, restore institutional prestige or symbolize recovery from a catastrophe to deserve attention. Her central strength is this capacity to keep shared life desirable while hardship invites everyone to think only about survival.

The important contradiction is that she can protect other people's freedom through warmth and still pressure them through it. She hides her list because she expects her friends would help and does not want to increase their burden; she later invites Sensei into that intimate project. Yet she can also pursue a desired outing, costume or future favor past another person's reluctance. Generosity is therefore a practice with judgments and mistakes, rather than proof that every offer is welcome. Her heavy purchases, failed coconut harvest, excessive dumbbells and unequal game results further prevent abundance or affection from becoming an infallibility claim. (`BA:bond:13004:005`, `:006`, `:008`, `:015`; `BA:bond:10044:002`, `:003`; `BA:event:814`; `BA:event:841:003–004`.)

MAIN gives this everyday practice a political counterpart. Nonomi chooses Abydos despite family expectations around Nephthys and Highlander, refuses an offered presidency, and asks for help that would preserve both the former council and the present Committee. The executive converts family connection and material access into leverage against her. The resulting captivity is not evidence that her choice was unreal; it shows how vulnerable a choice can be when others claim power over its conditions. Her apologetic self-erasure under pressure must be compared with her explicit attachment, rather than promoted into a settled wish to disappear. (`BA:main:001:003:011:scene:005:u:0032–0061`; `:012:scene:001:u:0165–0200`; `:013:scene:001:u:0028–0141`.)

The thesis is consequently about **chosen participation under unequal resources**. Nonomi is a giver, a recipient, a fallible learner and a person who wants things. She can use resources without controlling the institution that supplies them; she can be kind without always recognizing a recipient's limit; she can contribute to belonging without being its sole cause. These qualifications make her more legible than either an uncomplicated maternal support figure or a hidden corporate solution to Abydos's crisis.

## 2. Longitudinal arc

### A member before a family connection becomes leverage

The early Abydos arc begins with a functioning committee, not five interchangeable dependents waiting for an adult. Its members find, feed and guide Sensei, supply different kinds of intelligence, discuss repayment and make judgments about acceptable means. Nonomi's practical warmth belongs within this distributed competence. C001's bank operation and C002's rescue cannot be reduced to her money, nor can the whole committee's ethics be inferred from her gentleness. The students' refusal to retain seized cash for repayment is a separate collective decision about the school they intend to remain. (`BA:main:001:001:001–008`, `:013–015`; accepted V001 arc §§3–4.)

C003's recollections disclose an earlier Nonomi who is already choosing and correcting. She and Hoshino offer immediate shelter and possessions to the girl who calls herself Shiroko. They cannot establish that girl's origin, but can respond to her present need. In the scrap episode, Nonomi and Hoshino object and apologize rather than accept stolen goods as an ingenious debt solution. Payment or durable reform is not printed. At the Committee's founding, Hoshino rejects both Shiroko's bank idea and Nonomi's gold-card solution. Nonomi's imagined way out is thus subject to a peer's limit from the beginning. (`BA:main:001:003:012:scene:001:u:0002–0049`, `u:0082–0140`; unstable corrective labels at `u:0091/0096` remain exposed.)

This matters causally. The school is attractive to Nonomi as a place where she can participate with particular people, not simply as an institution she can purchase. She credits Shiroko with helping her choose Abydos and helping Hoshino smile again. That is Nonomi's account of a relationship, not an independently exhaustive explanation of Hoshino's recovery. Her preference for preserving the Committee's name survives her acknowledgment that memory could continue without it: the shared institution has value beyond bare mnemonic necessity. (`:012:u:0165–0200`.)

### A public test of choice, followed by coercive narrowing

E011 presents a higher-limit card and the possibility of Highlander presidency. Nonomi declines the card and the transfer. The executive's claim that her choice damaged a partnership does not establish a duty to enroll elsewhere. Shiroko protects her from that personal pressure, while Serika refuses the proposed sacrifice. Nonomi's refusal is therefore both her own act and something peers support. It is not a favor Sensei first teaches her to request; the adult is absent from this exchange. (`BA:main:001:003:011:scene:001:u:0001–0029`; `scene:005:u:0032–0043`.)

E013 then shows her initiative becoming a vulnerability. She goes on an undisclosed errand and appeals to Nephthys for help, refusing transfer again. Her objective includes the old council as well as the present Committee; she is trying to avoid making institutional history the price of saving current friends. The executive calls her selfish, invokes unspecified middle-school damage and secures an apology before disclosing his stated double play. His account of a board decision, property and intended troop use is testimony and threat, without inspected minutes, title or a completed deployment. It nevertheless makes his manipulation textually explicit. (`:013:u:0028–0093`.)

Her later italic message asks the Committee to forget her, calls her unfit because of her Nephthys connection and hopes Sensei will destroy the gun. The executive relays it with `……とのことです`. Neither the precise medium nor a freely settled preference is authenticated. The words have literary force precisely because they conflict with E012's desire to keep the Committee and E013's effort to save it. Her self-blame shows pressure altering the terms in which she can speak about belonging; it does not establish actual responsibility for every corporate act. (`:013:u:0128–0141`.)

By E020, Nonomi is among the peers urging Hoshino to wait and act together, and Ayane states that Nonomi has been rescued. Her card is visibly displayed and she is surprised it was taken: the removal is now supported beyond E013's earlier order. These developments restore participation locally. E022 establishes a successful group dismount after the twins' diversion; the control system's prior activation invites a card/Suou hypothesis, not a witnessed login. (`:020:u:0001–0018`, `u:0058–0084`; `:022:scene:002:u:0016–0021`, `scene:003:u:0011–0015`.)

## 3. Public and private self

The public profile's gentle, emotionally supportive wealthy daughter is a useful editorial description. Its precision matters: **most snack costs** come from her allowance. This is not a balance sheet for every school expense or a proof that she can discharge the debt at will. The profile also says the wealth is not much advertised. MAIN's later family disclosure can be compared with that reserve without treating every early smile as a deliberately executed concealment plan. (`BA:character_data:13004:profile_and_dialog`, ProfileIntroductionJp; `BA:main:001:003:002–011`.)

Privately she has preferences that resist the support-only role. She enjoys sweets, objects, games and novelty; her secret notebook records activities she wants with the Committee. It is significant that the list includes school-wide cleaning as well as eating sweets and a spicy-pizza challenge. Work and play can both become remembered common achievements. She reports red circles around completed aims and recalls Serika's red face, while acknowledging many unfinished aims. This is her retrospective account, not a separate inspected performance of every marked item. (`BA:bond:13004:015:u:0014–0035`.)

Her secrecy has two edges. It protects friends from a burden she expects they would accept; it also keeps them from deciding for themselves how to help. The source does not show the concealment producing a crisis, and it would be excessive to identify it with Hoshino's unilateral sacrifice. The comparison is narrower: both give protective intention a possible cost in shared knowledge. Nonomi's later permission to read the notebook shows a negotiable boundary rather than absolute secrecy. Her warm invitation is a real local disclosure; promised confidentiality and every future shared memory remain untested. (`:015:u:0018–0025`, `u:0041–0045`.)

Her private hesitation also prevents a simple contrast in which the public self is a mask and the private self is the one authentic person. Embarrassment about overbuying, uncertainty after a coconut failure, coyly withdrawn thoughts and grateful receipt of help are all situated acts. They do not cancel the public warmth; they show its costs, dependencies and less fluent moments. The public refusal of Highlander and the private refusal or condition around intimate care likewise belong to one varied capacity to choose, without proving that it is exercised uniformly in every relation.

## 4. School and club role

Nonomi sustains the Committee partly by making life together inviting. Food and imagined outings have institutional weight because the threatened thing is a lived school, not only an account or land title. Her contribution is distinctive beside Ayane's documents and logistics, Serika's service and wages, Shiroko's tactics and Hoshino's historical judgment. The distinctions do not distribute every episode into permanent jobs. They identify different available ways of carrying a shared situation.

GROUP2101/2102 is the strongest ordinary test because Sensei is absent. Nonomi proposes a picnic and breathing room; other members imagine lottery gain, aggressive recruitment or profitable work. These are not one common motive. The peers want to relieve Ayane, while ill and guilty Ayane prepares welcoming snacks and drinks. Their wrong-gym escalation then demonstrates that affectionate collaboration can act on a mistaken premise. Nonomi's leisure idea remains legitimate despite the failed plan; the failure is not proof that rest was inherently irresponsible. At the same time, the collective wish to help cannot be used as informed authorization from Ayane for combat and property damage. (`BA:group:2101–2102`; complete accepted deep argument.)

Her attachment to the Committee's name in C003 further distinguishes belonging from formal power. She can acknowledge that memory does not literally depend on a name and still refuse an executive's erasure route. The name marks participation she helped choose. The later internal election of Ayane, merger assertion and reported Hoshino presidency are separate governance states. Nonomi's family connection does not automatically grant external authority over any of them. Nor does affection authenticate the council's charter, gun title, debt ledger or full railway agreement. (`BA:main:001:003:012`, `:016–020`, `:043`.)

The club's educational importance lies partly in its limits on her resources. Hoshino's rejection of the gold-card solution makes a quick financial intervention insufficient as a founding norm. It need not prove that every future use of Nonomi's money is wrong. Snacks, gifts and limited practical help can be welcome in the very institution that resists becoming an extension of corporate or familial control. This distinction explains why her material generosity and her struggle with Nephthys are connected but not interchangeable.

## 5. Ordinary life

### Giving is also something she wants

The parfait encounter has actual shared consumption but an unfulfilled desired treat: Sensei prevents Nonomi from paying. To describe it only as an adult providing for a grateful student would miss her intended direction of care. To describe it as successful reciprocity without qualification would miss the refusal of that direction. Company is achieved while her preferred payment is not. The school-cleaning episode reverses a self-sufficient provider image more positively: joint labor and gratitude for help received are printed. She is able to contribute without doing everything. (`BA:bond:13004:002`, `:003`; whole paired MomoTalk arguments.)

Shopping supplies taste, quantity and consequences. Intended presents coexist with overbuying, a heavy return, embarrassment and uncertainty over the adult's comfort. Generosity can therefore be sincere and poorly calibrated. The specified excess dumbbells sharpen the same distinction: purchasing power does not confer knowledge of an appropriate load, and a future school use is only proposed. These mistakes are not clinical compulsions, nor do they establish permanent incompetence. They are occasions on which her ability to obtain things exceeds her ability to assess them. (`BA:bond:13004:005`, `:008`.)

Her game success has an equally important comparator. Local wins over Sensei are achieved; failure against peers is her report; learning is a hope. A universal “talented at everything” claim cannot absorb these different baselines. Neither should the wins be withheld merely because the opponent is less successful. Ordinary skill is relative and consequential at its own scale. (`BA:bond:13004:006`.)

### An unfinished list is an available future

The notebook does not only compensate for deprivation. Nonomi says the Committee has fun even while debt remains difficult. Its expanding aims make the future desirable instead of postponing pleasure until total repayment. Sensei's represented favorable response interprets unfinished aims as more possible memories; it does not complete them. Her roughly four-thousand-metre skydiving proposal meets the adult's inward refusal and is retracted as a joke. That ending preserves both imaginative appetite and a limit accepted in this encounter. (`BA:bond:13004:015:u:0031–0045`.)

This makes the notebook a useful counterpoint to corporate valuation. The executive's offered status and weapon utility are not the only possible meanings of an abundant future. Her list measures it through remembered smiles and varied experiences with particular people. That is a literary comparison, not a claim that she consciously wrote the list as a political manifesto or after the rail crisis. Its privacy also distinguishes it from a public program: no idol project, complete outing schedule or finished list can be inferred from the recorded wish.

### Summer preserves awkwardness and finite achievement

Picnic preparation and wanted company do not coincide with a need to have all carrying done for her. She declines carrying help and values the person accompanying her. However, the postponed request carries future-refusal pressure, so desired company cannot be universalized into perfectly open consent. The coconut encounter is more plainly fallible: she wants to refresh everyone, harvesting fails, and an awkward shoulder ride introduces environmental, safety and privacy concerns. A ladder or other retry remains future. The care motive is secure without a completed supply of coconuts. (`BA:bond:10044:002`, `:003`.)

Summer005 gives quiet regret, uncertainty, companionship and a withdrawn unspecified thought. Its five student-only messages do not permit a fabricated Sensei reply, and its desire for sleep or a limited stay does not certify an overnight event. Quiet company is already a meaningful endpoint; the unsaid thought retains its privacy. Reading every hesitation as a known romantic confession would substitute a genre expectation for the actual textual boundary. (`BA:bond:10044:005`; `BA:momotalk:10044:thread:100440200`.)

EVENT810's Nonomi gift encounter042 likewise values small rest, snacks and variety. EVENT814 makes a holiday worth wanting even when a noncash opportunity cannot repay debt. These are not rewards that become legitimate only after heroic performance. The summer-interface costume adds playful resort welcome, curiosity about prizes, care over heat and uncertainty about item value. Its forty-seven records belong to event814/10814/900814 selectors; they are condition-bearing writing, not forty-seven narrated actions or several lived reruns. ## 6. Crisis behavior

Nonomi's most consequential crisis initiative is to seek a less destructive path through people and resources to which she has access. In C003 E013 she asks Nephthys to preserve the old council and the Committee. This is neither passive dependence nor an omniscient intervention. The attempt is active, its objective coherent, and the executive's manipulation successful at narrowing her position. Her mistake lies partly in the trust or access assumptions under which she acts; his coercion remains his act and cannot be reassigned to her as deserved punishment for choosing Abydos.

The executive exploits the contradiction between her chosen school and her family tie. Alleged old damage, a demand for apology, an offered role and a hostage threat invite her to understand the conflict as personal unfitness. The source provides no accounting of the claimed middle-school damage. Her distressed message consequently cannot authenticate that accounting. It does show the relationship between guilt and threatened withdrawal: she asks to be forgotten while still wishing to preserve the institution and hoping the gun will be destroyed. Care and self-erasure appear together, but the source does not make self-erasure the ethical solution. (`BA:main:001:003:013:u:0046–0065`, `u:0111–0141`.)

Hoshino's response is also complex. She reports that Nonomi rescued her when the world felt hostile and says she cannot lose another dear person. The report establishes the significance Hoshino gives the relationship, not a complete biography in which Nonomi singly cured grief. Her subsequent attempt to carry risk alone reproduces a form of exclusion that the peers resist. Nonomi's rescued presence in E020, urging planning together, is an important counterpoint: the person being protected is again part of the deliberating community. (`:014:u:0011–0018`; `:020:u:0058–0084`.)

The later joint response to Hoshino's altered crisis broadens the same answer. Peers address her in a contact domain and she returns to cooperative external speech. Nonomi can be considered within that distributed relational response without receiving exclusive causal credit for a technical reversal. Neither the Yume-labeled contact nor the two-OS process authenticates an original notebook chain, an exact mechanism, a permanent cure or the abolition of future grief. The school's debt remains and rail operations are partial or prospective at the chapter endpoint. (`BA:main:001:003:033–043`; complete C003 checkpoint §§1–2.)

Final and the associated protection arguments offer further institutional comparison: defense, local care and coordination have distinct participants and evidence conditions. They do not license a generic assertion that Nonomi personally performs every Abydos or shelter action. EVENT821's operation reports preserve actual reported accomplishments, including the Binah result, while revival and later resetting qualify permanence. The bounded point is that crisis accomplishment can be real and incomplete. It need not consume the ordinary future for which the Committee acts. (`BA:main:100:002`; `BA:event:821:002–003`, `:015–016`.)

## 7. Sensei relationship

Sensei receives her care as well as helping her. She can invite, prepare, offer payment, provide a pleasant setting and propose memories. The adult can carry, clean or share company and can be needed in the public crisis without becoming the origin of all her attachment. Her Committee list predates its disclosure to him; its imagined participants are her peers. Her choice of Abydos and refusal of Highlander are not generated by an adult answer option. This independence is essential to interpreting the relationship as one part of her life.

The private evidence also differentiates care received from care prevented. Joint cleaning is cooperative help. Preventing her parfait payment blocks a chosen treat even while the shared consumption succeeds. The awkward shoulder ride assists a failed attempt but does not complete harvesting or every later privacy request. Ordinary intimacy is therefore composed of different directions and outcomes, not one undifferentiated pattern of adult provision and student gratitude. (`BA:bond:13004:002–003`; `BA:bond:10044:003`.)

Her written special-lobby address invites ear cleaning and asks the recipient to stay still, tell her if ticklish and lie properly. Other lobby lines defer spoiling until work ends or a lap pillow until later darkness; surprise brings a request not to startle her. These expressions can support affectionate role reversal, conditions and embarrassment. They do not establish that the interface was enacted, that every instruction was obeyed or that the intimacy is globally exclusive. The source's special secret phrasing describes that service, not all peer relations. (`BA:character_data:13004:UILobby:320–334`; `UILobbySpecial:336–342`.)

The contrary case is Nonomi's own insistence. EVENT814's firmer pressure and the summer picnic's postponed favor show that the caring student can also make refusal harder. EVENT841's clothes sequence supplies an even clearer peer setting: her enthusiasm and research coexist with Serika and Ayane's objections and an earlier promise being used to press participation. Warm intention does not settle recipient assent. Conversely, the later chosen rehearsal must not be erased because the earlier recruitment was troubling. The adult's framing and help belong within the same scrutiny; a favorable eventual outcome is not retroactive permission. (`BA:bond:10044:002`; `BA:event:814`; `BA:event:841:003–004`, `:012`.)

The relationship's strongest ethical possibility is mutually directed companionship: she can give, receive, disclose, joke and encounter a limit. Its most important continuing question is whether offers remain revisable by the person receiving them. No settled romance, universal confidentiality, clinical recovery or all-situations consent law is asserted. Sensei options remain alternatives; inward reflections are not automatically speech Nonomi hears; whole messages retain their own sender conditions and tails.

## 9. Language and written register

Nonomi's written register combines polite address, affirmation, playful ornaments and a readiness to offer. Repeated `うんうん`, laughter and `☆` make agreement and encouragement conspicuous; they are orthographic features, not an inspected pitch, rhythm or vocal performance. Her notebook scene adds stammering, secrecy and modest disclaimers: the warmth does not eliminate hesitation. The progression from an initially withheld object to permission and a joint invitation gives intimacy a sequence of speech acts rather than an automatic state. (`BA:bond:13004:015:u:0004–0025`; `BA:character_data:13004`.)

`みんな` is consequential. In the list it names the Committee as imagined companions; red marks and remembered smiles make collective achievement personally pleasurable. In MAIN the Committee and former council are names worth preserving, while the executive offers role labels such as president and hostage as instruments of control. These uses do not form one meaning of belonging. The monograph distinguishes affectionate inclusion, chosen membership, proposed office and coercive assignment. A role word by itself does not supply a signed appointment or legitimate command. (`BA:bond:13004:015:u:0014–0039`; `BA:main:001:003:011–013`.)

Her `ダメです` and conditioned intimate service complicate an always-accommodating voice. The written refusal of spoiling before work finishes gives care a timing rule in that line; the startle request gives its speaker a limit. Neither is an empirical rule predicting every later encounter. The same general capacity for firm address can regulate an adult's behavior or pressure another recipient. Similar wording is not enough to infer that the acts have the same ethical footing. (`BA:character_data:13004:UILobby:322`, `:334`, `UILobbySpecial:342`; accepted EVENT814 comparison.)

## 10. Competing readings

### “The gentle mother of the group”

This reading captures recurrent provision, practical warmth, snack funding and emotional support. It is useful if treated as a description of some relations. It fails as a total identity because it obscures Nonomi's own tastes, embarrassment, uncertainty, wanted treat, failed skill and gratitude for help. It also makes her recipients too passive and her pressure too easy to excuse. The better reading preserves a person who enjoys caring and being with others, and whose judgment remains fallible. Her private list is not simply a roster of duties to serve everyone else.

### “Wealth solves the problem; she only declines to use it”

The gold-card idea and visible family connection make this a plausible temptation. The text defeats the stronger conclusion: a peer rejects that founding solution, the profile describes limited snack funding, and the executive manipulates access rather than obeying Nonomi's preferences. No accepted argument establishes that her allowance can discharge the debt, command Nephthys, guarantee title or prevent armed coercion. Her refusal of Highlander is an enacted choice; it is not evidence that she freely selected all the harms later attached to it.

### “Her cheerful everyday life conceals one continuous guilt narrative”

The hostage message and desire not to burden peers give guilt or burden sensitivity real local grounds. The reading becomes excessive when it turns parfaits, games, souvenirs, a quiet stay or resort play into coded symptoms. The notebook explicitly retains fun amid debt, and her tastes and skill gaps have their own objects and outcomes. No global chronology makes all private scenes responses to captivity. A psychological interpretation can connect protective concealment and public self-erasure as a comparison, but cannot establish a single underlying cause for every pleasure or diagnose her.

### “Affection makes all participation voluntary”

This reading gains plausibility from genuine company, jokes and later enjoyable rehearsal. It fails against the postponed-favor pressure, clothes refusals, reluctant presentation and peer objections. The contrary overcorrection also fails: initial pressure does not make later chosen rehearsal or actual shared food unreal.
