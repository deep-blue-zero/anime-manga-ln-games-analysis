## 1. Core thesis: a protected child whose answer is not public property

Ibuki's strongest through-line is the ability to give a particular answer within relationships that readily turn her into a justification. She wants identifiable things—games, a carousel, a parade, toys, preferred food, lessons, art materials and somebody's company—and she can help, decline, ask, forgive or remain uncertain. The people caring for her often value those wishes. They also speak about her feelings, invoke her welfare or use her as grounds for administrative action. Her characterization depends on retaining the difference between what she chooses and what others claim on her behalf. Protection is necessary in particular encounters; it does not supply her answer in advance. [IB01–IB13; IB-CMP §§2–8; G1902; MINI150; M2C2E011.]

The distinction has concrete positive forms. A failed park excursion ends in actual pleasant indoor play, rather than either an entirely failed day or a secretly fulfilled parade. When a gull takes Sensei's food, Ibuki shares her surplus and they actually eat enough. In the pudding dispute she wants to stay with Problem Solver68, denies kidnapping, widens Makoto's promise to protect everybody's pudding, and then forgives her. At the institutional split she appreciates Iroha's offer but chooses to stay beside lonely Makoto; Iroha checks and accepts her stated wish. These are different kinds of authored response, not one general rule of obedience or limitless generosity. [IB02:16014:003:u63/raw198328; IB07:20060:005:scene002:u33–34/raw247500–501; MINI15010–15030; M2C2E011:u41–63,u97–110.]

The thesis must also retain vulnerability and pressure. She needs help down from a tree and is carried away after a fall near the rocks. An incomplete collage remains incomplete. Work and conflict can isolate her, and a decision made from concern that somebody will otherwise be alone should not become a child's standing duty to keep an institution or an adult functioning. Makoto's eventual speculation that Ibuki may be the Emperor's legacy is consequential evidence about Makoto's interpretation, not an explanation of Ibuki's biology, memory, political intention or inevitable future. Her ordinary interests remain evidence in their own right before that mystery is resolved. [IB05–IB08; M2C2E011:u1–63; M2C2E021:u96–103.]

## 10. Competing readings and the evidence that resists each

The helpless-mascot reading rightly notices age, protection and episodes requiring help, but cannot account for her chosen food, practical sharing, provisional learning, art interest, explicit departure or broader pudding condition. Those actions are not merely praise spoken about her. Conversely, a prodigy or perfect-junior reading cannot explain uncertainty about careers, failed plans, the fall, adult assistance or incomplete art. A hundred-point report and an approximate ballistic table do not certify universal competence. The better account allows local initiative and local dependence without ranking one as the true self and the other as error. [IB01–IB10; MINI150; E860:008–011.]

The universally forgiving conscience reading draws support from concern for lonely Makoto and the achieved pudding reconciliation. A critical reading that treats every concern for Makoto as coercively assigned labor also overreaches: M2C2E011 explicitly gives Ibuki's decision, Iroha's check and acceptance, and Makoto's later permission to choose without blame. The harmless-happiness reading can retain games, full food and peaceful time while overlooking failed destinations, hidden resource limits, danger and unfinished promises. Its rival total-instrumentalization reading can retain public uses of her welfare while overlooking how food, toys, studying and company are genuinely wanted. Neither is adequate.
