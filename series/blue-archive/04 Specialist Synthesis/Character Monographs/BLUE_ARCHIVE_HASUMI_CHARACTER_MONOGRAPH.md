# Hasumi: making room for students, and the difficulty of including herself

ROOT accepted the complete eleven-section literary argument **WITH LIMITS** on 2026-10-10. The [semantic acceptance audit](../../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/BLUE_ARCHIVE_PHASE3_SEMANTIC_ACCEPTANCE_AUDIT.md) and [exact receiving record](../../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/BLUE_ARCHIVE_HASUMI_MONOGRAPH_ROOT_RECEIVING_AND_ACCEPTANCE_V1.json) govern this derivative. ## 1. Core thesis

Hasumi's administrative care is most intelligible when measured against the lives it is meant to enable. Her notebook records allergies, anniversaries, interests and different strengths because members are students with wanted activities, not interchangeable instruments of Justice. She tells inexperienced guests that knowledge can enrich tea without becoming a qualification for enjoying it. Her own wants are equally particular: sweets, a brief pale-purple sky, reading, clothes she chooses privately, a companion's time, a summer meal and fireworks. Yet she repeatedly makes herself the person who must remain on duty so that others can rest. Her central tension is the unequal application of her own principle: she knows that chosen club work should enrich student life, while finding it difficult to grant her own life the same room. [H01:243–516; G01; E03:014; E19:006]

This is neither a uniformly gentle manager nor a person whose every pleasure is a disguised failure. Practical competence and wanted company have achieved local results. The notebook is returned, preparations are completed, lemon preserves are made together, a festival is enjoyed, the beach meal is eaten and fireworks are watched to their end. Other wishes remain unfulfilled: arranging others' holiday does not give her leave, a purchased swimsuit does not establish its comfort, and a promise to return next summer is still a promise. Her value does not depend on turning every food, body or social difficulty into a completed improvement story. [H01:Hasumi13003:015; 16011:002/003/006; 10124:002–006; E18:019; E03:014]

Her care also has adverse recipients. The proposal to destroy a guest's Gehenna-associated tea is not excused by earlier enjoyment of its aroma. Intimidating venue negotiations and political threats do not become harmless because the subsequent party is generous. Body secrecy cannot be translated into transferable permission to touch, and teacher encouragement can be implicated in pressure even when she denies blaming him. The sustained argument is therefore about finite, selective care: effective and sometimes proportionate, genuinely directed toward student pleasure, but vulnerable to prejudice, self-exclusion and coercive means. [G01:1802; E19:007/010–011; H01:13003:002; 10124:003/005]

## 2. Longitudinal arc

The source classes supply connected questions without establishing a universal growth order among three variants. Eden's early Hasumi is an access authority whom others must interpret. She states a grade-based restriction on Koharu's room entry and holds an opaque private conversation. The narrator's academic evidence, Koharu's later claim of an elite spy role and the teacher's misleading teaching-book pretext are different facts. The conversation cannot be reconstructed from the version most flattering to any participant. Later encouragement and apology reach the remedial group through a Justice member; their actual four-pass result does not prove Hasumi's direct opening of a perimeter or formal reinstatement of Koharu. Administrative presence matters, but does not grant omniscient control of the educational crisis. [M01:E004–E005/E015; M02:E018–E019]

Her larger public action is similarly finite. During the cathedral disaster, Hasumi and Tsurugi hold an evacuation rear guard while Hinata physically frees Sensei and other rescuers work. Subsequent injury reports and responsive states belong to the actual sequence; neither establishes immediate full recovery or sole authorship of rescue. In an earlier interschool incident, Hasumi seeks Schale and remedial handling of the Gourmet case to avoid an overt Trinity–Gehenna confrontation during negotiations. The visible loading for a medical handoff is achieved, whereas final custody, treatment and Izumi's location are not thereby settled. The cut-off vow which could sound like mobilization is clarified as dieting after Makoto's humiliation. Political care, personal injury and appetite coexist without requiring one to conceal another. [M02:E004–E005; M03:E006–E018]

The private sequence makes her finite knowledge and desires more accessible. Two early consultations fail to reach their intended subject. A misunderstanding of “big” produces conditional secrecy around a referent the adult has got wrong, then flight; another attempt yields genuinely enjoyed milk tea and varied desserts, body questions and another abrupt departure. Neither ending can be turned into completed advice or agreement. The twilight meeting instead achieves what it quietly wants: sitting with someone through a short changing sky. She can call the time wonderful even when “nothing” was done. That is a positive endpoint, not a precursor whose value must be earned by subsequent efficiency. [H01:13003:002/003/005, complete MomoTalk endings]

Stress, clothing and summer provide recurrence rather than a fixed cure. A headache reportedly lifts during reassurance and a shared bakery visit, but the later messages retain dieting and fear of losing to herself. A too-tight tracksuit remains troublesome even as the document consultation and escort occur. Summer shopping actually ends in her buying a barely fitting item; the subsequent beach day combines play, refused food, dizziness, a shaded awakening and an actual enjoyable meal. The last episode completes fireworks and a report of unusually enjoyable summer time, but does not retroactively finish the interrupted volleyball or prove swimming proficiency, safe dieting or next year's visit. Each actual relief is retained without making the variants stages of a single medical or romantic progression. [H01:13003:006; 16011:002; 10124:003/005/006]

Public ordinary events test the same principle in different settings. The summer wish list originates in Hasumi's own tired wish, yet the outing and its cleanup chiefly show her facilitating others' pleasure; her leave is not completed. The athletic festival allows actual exhilaration and later tea even with a sixth-place result and renewed duties. Christmas lets novices enjoy a hard-won party and permits proportional reconciliation after injury. The Tea Party vacation gives her real company and shared labor while preserving prejudice, concealment and an enforcement burden she cannot discharge alone. These are local before-and-after relations. Event placement, private callbacks and release dates do not establish a single calendar of steadily improving Hasumi. [E03:001/014; E18:012/019; E19:006–011; E48:002–011/015–016]

## 3. Public and private self

The calm deputy is a legitimate public aspect, not the whole person. Written introduction associates intelligence and tactical support with a willingness to act recklessly; the accepted stories substantiate both administrative attention and impulsive reactions. In private she can be evasive, embarrassed, pleased, exhausted and eager for help while unable to formulate its subject. Her members receive considered allocations; the adult sometimes receives a consultation which ends before the problem is discussed. That asymmetry need not mean the public self is fraudulent. It shows that providing a structure for others is different from entrusting another person with her own difficulty. [H01:13003 written introduction; 13003:002/003/015]

Privacy has real objects. A returned notebook is more than a generic competence symbol: three years of personal notes matter to her, and the annual books contain information about identifiable members. The adult's detached note and apology do not license a complete reading of all those records. Likewise, the late question about a matching birthday and hobby does not reveal the unprinted subject of the entry or establish a romantic identification. Her evasion and urgent pretext are part of the represented exchange; the later statement that the pretext is resolved is not an inspected emergency. Responsible interpretation can recognize intimacy without pretending to know every private referent. [H01:13003:015, whole130030330 and tail110–170]

The body is both a wanted presentation and a vulnerable topic. Her wings are visibly material to presence and play, but the adult's misunderstood conditional permission is not consent for the subsequently clarified body part. At the mall, distance and prior shop inquiry serve her concern about privacy and suitable sizing. Her account of a trusted craft supplier is not an audit of its stock, and a worker's largest-size claim is not independent measurement. She chooses and pays for the item despite concerns, which preserves agency; it does not justify telling a story in which embarrassment has been cured or in which her choice makes every pressure beneficial. [H01:13003:002; 10124:003]

Food is not reducible to the body's problem. She names daily sweets as a cherished time, including when alone; the counterfactual attraction of the Sweets Club tells us what she might want, not an actual membership. She enjoys milk tea and desserts before fleeing an unresolved conversation, and later warmth, company and a beach-house meal are directly valued. The notebook's self-management principle cannot erase this wanted life. Neither an exclusively indulgent reading nor a punitive “discipline failure” reading accounts for her simultaneous attention to diet, shame, hospitality, pleasure and other people's burdens. The claims concern represented experiences and choices, not a clinical diagnosis or dietary prescription. [H01:13003:003/006; 10124:005; G01:1802]

## 4. School and club role

Her most explicit institutional theory is that members were students before becoming workers for the committee, and that they chose a demanding club. The deputy's book translates that theory into practical detail: contacts, allergies, anniversaries, interests, habits and strengths or weaknesses for assignments. Such knowledge can make work humane without proving that each member enjoys every allocation or that all privacy obligations are satisfied. It is an argument for differentiated responsibility, not an inventory-based assertion of universal good management. Her own refusal to redistribute summer work because it would burden someone else exposes the unfinished part of that theory. [H01:13003:015; 10124:002]

Justice work is neither assumed criminality nor automatic moral correctness. In the PS68 group account, Hasumi and Mashiro arrive in response to a report of an illegal briefing and find no one there. That is actual institutional intervention with a reported characterization, not a trial, an arrest or a completed confrontation with the business group. Eden's Gourmet handling likewise distinguishes observed coercion, contested consent claims, an intended medical route and unprinted final disposition. The institutional label supplies a responsibility to inquire and intervene; it does not replace evidence of what an intervention achieves. [G02; M02:E004–E005]

The tea-party contrasts expose methods as well as purposes. In GROUP1802 she first enjoys a guest's tea, then proposes burning it after its qualified Gehenna association is revealed. No actual burning is shown, but recipients' fear and avoidance matter before any fire could occur. In Event819 she admits using Tsurugi's intimidating presence in difficult venue negotiations, while Mine calls that unfair; her warning of political escalation is prospective rather than a legal judgment. These details resist a reading in which the deputy merely offsets the commander's social difficulties with universally kind diplomacy. Hasumi can protect institutional rest through means which carry costs for outsiders and her own peers. [G01:1802; E19:007:s1:u0029–0063]

Her Christmas teaching offers a different practice. Novices may enjoy scones, cake and tea without mastering the cultural repertoire first, and the party recognizes their year's effort. The members actually choose relaxed enjoyment. After conflict she rejects Mine's demand for life-for-life repayment, accepts cleared misunderstanding and reported recovery as a sufficient local resolution, and invites the rescuers into an actual party. This is proportionate repair by the wronged group, not a certificate of legality, complete restitution or lasting reform. Tsurugi's reply is nonlexical and Hasumi interprets it; that distinction prevents her role as interpreter from becoming total ownership of another member's assent. [E19:006:s1:u0002–0048; 010:s1:u0033–0069; 011]

## 5. Ordinary life

Twilight provides a precise example of pleasure that needs no crisis function. After duties she ordinarily returns toward the dorm, notices a pale-purple sky which lasts only a few minutes and accepts sitting with the adult until dark. Solitude is not thereby her only preferred life; the source expressly complicates an easy claim that she especially loves loneliness. Her later thanks value time which she describes as doing nothing. The rain episode reuses the park relationship but changes its result: overnight company, her being woken after sleep and concern about returning unnoticed. A believed dorm notification is not an audited attendance record, and intended return is not shown arrival. The wish to bathe and rest remains future, while the actual company has already occurred. [H01:13003:005/008 and their complete codas]

Lemon preserves are a chosen gift as well as work. She brings the adult's portion in advance because he will be busy the next day, asks about burden and is pleased by the chance to thank him. The exchange about being a manager or big sister matters because she wants to be received as a student too. Both actually bottle the committee's portion together, apparently enjoying the long task. This achieved collaboration is not unlimited availability for later calls. A subsequent early lunch scene shows how pleasure and anxiety remain entangled: she assesses her modest lunch, hears exaggerated calorie claims from unnamed participants, accepts a little of the preserved lemon and ultimately eats all the gift. Apology and a promise to remake it do not establish an accomplished replacement. [H01:16011:003/005 and full tails]

Exercise also opens a wanted possibility without becoming a corrective regimen. She explains movements learned through operations and declines the label of professional coach. An actual set and manual correction occur; her fear about heavy pressure and the adult's inward sense of soft contact are distinct perspectives, not proof of hidden mechanical intentions. They agree to reverse roles. Later messages report a café visit and considerable dessert, treating movement as a possible enjoyable practice rather than a demonstrated weight or health intervention. The whole ending keeps both activity and food in the life she wants, with a next occasion still future. [H01:16011:006, whole scene and tail530–620]

Summer does not have to end flawlessly to count as enjoyed. A schedule crowded with her name, a refusal to transfer burdens and deferred clothes shopping establish that preparation has a cost. After the actual shopping, the beach brings sandcastle and burial play, water and wing-balancing pleasure, but also food refusal and an interrupted volleyball game. She awakens in shade, with carrying reported by the adult, having eaten nothing; neither a clinical cause nor a harmlessness certificate follows. Her subsequent meal is explicitly delicious and happy. Fireworks last until they are used up, and she later describes exceptional enjoyment. The promised future visit remains unshown, as does the completion of volleyball. A conclusion which keeps only the dizziness discards her actual wanted life; one which keeps only fireworks erases the cost. [H01:10124:002–006, all MomoTalk endings]

Outside the private pairs, the same ordinary interests have their own public recipients. She can enjoy corn, parfait and tea at the festival, take exhilaration in a relay result despite sixth place, and look toward another occasion. Tea at Christmas is both her interest and an accessible shared pleasure. Vacation company includes a milk-first/tea-first disagreement rather than a scientifically settled method. Her summer wish list remains an unfulfilled wish for her own leave even after successful facilitation. Ordinary life therefore includes achieved enjoyment, desire that has not been met and tastes shared without complete agreement. None is demoted because it is quiet or comic. [E18:012/019; E19:006/011; E48:007; E03:014]

## 6. Crisis behavior

The evacuation rear guard demonstrates courage without making every earlier or later use of force correct. Tsurugi and Hasumi remain together while Hinata, Hina, rescue teams and other students have their own consequential actions. Injury reports and later responsiveness are separate endpoints; the text does not certify the absence of harm. The earlier Gourmet transfer shows another response: preserving negotiations by involving Schale rather than escalating a school conflict openly. That can be practical restraint while still leaving the abducted Fuuka's agency and the captors' consent gloss central to the case. A manager's chosen route is evaluated against what is shown for its recipients, not solely her reputation for calm. [M02:E004–E005; M03:E006–E018]

The vacation shows the limit of that calm. Hasumi condemns Gehenna after the Hot Spring attack, is physically affected by it, and resists dialogue. Ichika's attempt to distinguish a specific club from a whole school has independent force; Mika retains suspicion even after the correction. Hasumi's later agreement to an explanation and practical cooperation need not be invented as an ideological conversion. During the Gourmet conflict, Hasumi and Ichika move toward force while Mika seeks dialogue; Izumi and Junko have their own reasons for care and acceptance of food. Hasumi's recognition-based culprit inference is not a trial finding, and a force threat is not a shown shot. The subsequent meal, cleanup and gratitude keep a useful response visible without removing suspicion or lost play time. [E48:003/006/007/009]

Christmas gives the most sustained test of inquiry and proportionality. Hasumi's recovery of an item is first misread as unjust taking because Mine blocks the explanation. The delinquents' deception is real, but their pain is not therefore imaginary. Hasumi reports coercive sales and threats once the cause becomes explicit; numerical heat and chemical claims remain attributed, as do the opponents' defensive rationalizations. Mine's useful care and the juniors' treatment are preserved alongside injuries caused by premature force. The opponents ultimately escape. Hasumi refuses a dramatic life repayment which would impose another extreme demand on the wronged group, accepts local treatment and recovery reports, and returns everyone to wanted company. Listening was not the error; halted inquiry and force on an incomplete case were. [E19:003–010]

That resolution remains deliberately smaller than universal reconciliation. Tsurugi recovers enough to join renewed action; local treatment is reported complete; the party actually occurs. Mine promises improvement while Tsurugi and Hasumi continue to doubt its direction, and tea advice is accepted without endorsing the earlier attack. There is no printed arrest, restitution, clinical audit or lasting transformation. Hasumi's proportionate generosity is strongest precisely when it is not made omnipotent: she can create a good immediate ending while larger liabilities, recipients' futures and the durability of reform remain open. [E19:009–011]

## 7. Relationship with Sensei

The adult offers different kinds of help, and Hasumi does not accept them in one uniform way. She initiates consultations, recommends food, wants sunset company and brings a gift; she also withdraws before advice, evades notebook questions and worries about disappointing him. His alternatives in the dessert, stress and fitting scenes remain alternatives, not a continuous itinerary of everything he might say. A conditional secret arrangement based on the wrong referent cannot license a later bodily action. The relationship's intimacy includes misunderstandings and boundaries, not merely a progressive accumulation of permissions. [H01:13003:002/003/006/015; 16011:002]

Adult reassurance can have a real local benefit. She reports that the headache has gone, insists on the bakery visit and later thanks him. He shares park time, wakes her after overnight sleep and helps with the practical lemon task. But her subsequent caution against pressure, her desire to meet an ideal summer presentation and his own apology after the beach episode are consequential. Her denial of blame does not establish that the encouragement had no effect. Nor can concern authorize overruling every chosen purchase or meal. The relationship is ethically mixed because reassurance, attraction, dependence, desire and self-expectation interact while the actual results remain finite. [H01:13003:006/008; 16011:003; 10124:002–006]

Hasumi's initiative deserves independent description. She keeps records, finishes preparation, arranges member rest, decides to gift early, requests or extends company and values the adult as a recipient. The teacher does not invent her interests or single-handedly create her institutional principle. Conversely, his own failings are not absorbed into her viewpoint: the Eden book pretext is admitted misleading conduct, and his limited hearing does not disclose her private message to Koharu. Public Schale support is one channel of cooperation rather than proof that her every intervention is teacher-directed. The account therefore resists both a teacher-exclusive rescue story and a reading which treats all meaningful contact as purely instrumental. [H01:13003:015; 16011:003; M01:E015; M02:E004–E005]

The ending of each meeting is evidence. Shared twilight is completed company; milk tea is real pleasure followed by unresolved flight. Rain company does not show dorm arrival. Document work and escort can succeed while the zipper remains troublesome. Lemon-making completes a joint task while remaking an eaten gift stays future. Beach care ends with an actual meal and later fireworks, not all earlier desired activities. These differences let a mature relation include gratitude, uncertainty and further wanted time without treating every birthday, holiday or lobby promise as a completed romantic contract. [H01:all Hasumi private and written clauses]

## 8. Major peer relationships

Tsurugi is neither simply an intimidating tool nor an incompetent superior for Hasumi to replace. Hasumi takes on external festival responsibilities in response to Tsurugi's difficulty, interprets some nonlexical answers and offers reassurance about tense company. Yet venue negotiations expose her willingness to use that very intimidation. Tsurugi independently chooses easier outside guard work, protects others' rare rest, accepts hospitality in some circumstances and holds a rear guard with Hasumi. GROUP1802's sweets reception can feel welcoming to Tsurugi while the reader sees peers' fear; Hasumi's relief does not make all recipients comfortable. Nor does her interpretation of Tsurugi's Christmas reply become a complete verbalized assent. The relationship combines trust, practical accommodation, asymmetrical interpretation and ethically questionable deployment. [H01:16011 written; G01:1802; M03; E19:006–011]

Mashiro has her own idea of enjoyable duty. Hasumi's accessible party does not supersede Mashiro's stated pleasure in protecting others' enjoyment, and another member's invitation to try the party does not invalidate that preference. Tsurugi's offered guard shift and Mashiro's voluntary refusal to leave her senior alone are concrete reciprocal actions. The PS68 intervention puts Hasumi and Mashiro on the same actual response route without proving a completed confrontation. Their common institutional affiliation is therefore context for different responsibilities, not evidence that one person's mind or leisure can be copied into the other's package. [E19:006:s2:u0003–0059; G02]

Koharu's relation exposes the tension between rules and encouragement. Hasumi's grade/access bar is actual stated administration; the elite spy story is Koharu's uncorroborated claim. The later relayed apology and encouragement qualify a reading of unalterable exclusion, but do not erase the earlier barrier or establish formal return. Koharu's own study, 91-point official result and later protection of Mika are independent achievements, not outputs that can be assigned to Hasumi alone. The adult's misleading book account and inability to hear the conversation remain part of why this relationship cannot be reconstructed as either secret recruitment or complete administrative repair. [M01:E004–E005/E015; M02:E018–E019; M03:E017]

Ichika supplies judgment and capacity which Hasumi cannot silently appropriate. On vacation she distinguishes a club from a whole school, identifies shared cleanup needs, seeks a practical explanation from Kasumi and later agrees to a renewed cooperation request. Hasumi explicitly recalls the difficulty of managing organized groups alone, drawing on Tsurugi's experience; Ichika's aid is not proof of a finished operation. Ichika also feels the pressure of Tea Party hierarchy when she cannot comfortably refuse. Hasumi's gratitude and negotiations belong beside this peer's own time and constraints. Mika's invitation to enjoy the holiday and acknowledgment of Hasumi's company for the other two members are real affirmations without making all enforcement or social consequences complete. [E48:002–011/015–016]

Mine is a remembered acquaintance and a genuine adversarial recipient of Hasumi's reasoning. Recollected cake and pudding incidents are not completely known historical events. Their conflict instead supplies a shown present distinction between recovery and unjust taking, interrupted explanations, injuries, disclosed deception and local forgiveness. Hasumi's refusal of Mine's life-for-life offer limits the burden that self-condemnation would place on others; Mine's actual tea expertise remains useful. Serina and Hanae independently choose forms of aid, treat wounded colleagues, apologize and receive hospitality. Their care is neither erased by the mistaken operational choice nor reassigned to Hasumi as the only author of repair. [E19:007–011]

At the festival, Hasumi recognizes a use for Tsukuyo's height in a formation while Tsukuyo remains the person experiencing distress about it. Chise and Izuna have their own lookout and creative contributions, and the result is a team's win, not Hasumi's solitary causal effect. Later relay exhilaration coexists with Tsurugi's rejection of monopoly and recognition of less visible workers by others. The summer wish list likewise includes Hifumi's tank and sea wishes, Azusa's first sea experience, and Tsurugi and Mashiro's different companionship and craft questions. Hasumi's facilitating position must not convert peers' fulfilled wishes into her own completed leave or erase their independent wanted lives. [E18:011–012/019; E03:001–021]

## 9. Language and written voice

Hasumi's language makes an argument about the order of responsibilities: students precede workers, and voluntary club membership does not abolish enjoyment. The notebook's concrete particulars protect this from becoming a generic kindness slogan. At Christmas, knowledge is presented as enriching pleasure rather than granting permission to have it. In the dispute, admitting 回収 does not admit the stronger 奪う accusation. Justice as an institutional name cannot settle whether its own act was just, just as Mine's 救護 vocabulary cannot make every corrective attack care. These textual distinctions explain the conflicts more accurately than an English paraphrase which converts every retrieval into theft or every rescue into benign treatment. [H01:13003:015; E19:006/009; E19:checkpoint:L118]

Her requests, evasions and reports have different audiences. Some teacher lines are inward or alternatives, while a later response may follow them in the representation. Private claims about size, weight, sleep, paperwork and notification are not automatically narrator measurements. A message coda records a later report or promise rather than replaying the entire meeting as a second event. The timed rain and fitting logs repeat represented situations in a written mode; they do not create another treatment, purchase or night. The meanings of wing secrecy, matching notebook entries and an urgent pretext depend on what their recipients actually know. No acoustic performance, camera response or heard laugh has been received for this monograph. [H01:13003:002/008/015; 16011:002/005; G01; E19:checkpoint:L120]

The three profiles preserve literal metadata and different descriptions without becoming a universal age progression. Their present routes all belong to the declared Hasumi family; their school-year, age, birthday, height and interests are written fields rather than measurements of growth between stories. Base café and lobby lines include reading, rest, food resistance, work and threats; sports and summer lines anticipate movement, seasonal meetings, birthdays and protection. Such wishes and pledges are meaningful written characterization, but “never lose” or future availability is not a demonstrated invariant. The summer introduction's qualified expectation of enjoying a guard assignment is not proof that the entire holiday is carefree. [H01:449–516, five Hasumi written objects]

Two costume objects retain empty profiles and event-addressed lines. Their route links to the festival and later vacation contextualize work, joy, trust and questions about other people; they do not produce two new persons or silently add complete plotted scenes. Literal mission-buy fields are not payment records. Hasumi's reference to “that child” stays qualified where the subject is not printed, and a conditional thanks to Mika does not itself replay a complete event. Cross-mode comparison can strengthen the picture of interests and obligations while preserving missing performances, unknown address conditions and unproved transactions. [H01:1900906901/1900940201 written clauses]

## 10. Competing readings

A manager-only reading rightly notices detailed records, practical delegation and political judgment, but cannot explain the wanted twilight, sweets, chosen gift or unresolved consultation on its own terms. Nor does it survive the limits of that management: Koharu's status is only partly visible, summer leave remains unmet and venue diplomacy uses intimidation. The stronger thesis treats management as a means toward wanted student life, with Hasumi's own inclusion and the means chosen still in dispute. This preserves practical achievement without making it a total measure of the person. [H01; M01–M03; E03; E19]

A comic appetite or body-failure reading has real evidence in repeated embarrassment, restrictive intentions and food contradiction, but fails if it turns enjoyment into a penalty. Desserts and milk tea are genuinely cherished; the beach meal, fireworks and park company really happen. A carefree self-acceptance reading fails in the other direction: flight, a barely fitting purchase, refused food, dizziness, recurring dieting and fear of disappointment remain consequential. The bounded interpretation is recurrence with local relief, pleasure and wanted future time, not a clinical syndrome, a medical cure or a fixed maturation ladder. [H01:13003:002–008; 16011:002/005/006; 10124:002–006]

A purely benevolent Justice reading points to accessible hospitality, rear-guard service, reported coercive sales and proportional forgiveness. Its serious counterevidence is Hasumi's prejudice toward Gehenna, the guest's feared tea destruction, using Tsurugi's intimidation and political threats. Conversely, a hypocrisy-only reading cannot account for the novices' actual relaxed enjoyment, her chosen proportionate resolution, peer-specific care and a practical attempt to avoid interschool escalation. Ethical assessment must follow directed acts and effects. Mere moral vocabulary does not certify conduct, but mixed conduct does not make every generous or courageous act false. [G01; M02–M03; E48:003/006; E19:006–011]

A teacher-led cure or settled romance would compress the intimate scenes into reassurance producing self-acceptance. It misses Hasumi's initiatives, the unresolved consultation, the private referent which the adult misunderstands and the coda's recurring caution. A wholly transactional reading would lose wanted company and pleasure that complete no administrative task. Shared twilight and fireworks are achieved experiences; future gifts, visits, exercise and protection remain promises. This allows affection and meaningful contact without asserting an unprinted contract, universal permission or final dependence mechanism. Peer agency is independently extensive, so Hasumi's life cannot be organized solely by her relation to the adult. [H01; G01; E03; E18; E19; E48]

The final rival is a success metric in which rest exists only after all duties and harms are resolved. That would exclude most of the represented life: a sixth-place relay can be exhilarating, a tiring day can offer company, Christmas can end in a real party while opponents escape, and a facilitated summer can still leave the facilitator's own holiday unfinished. The inverse metric, counting every happy ending as complete repair, is equally defective. Actual pleasure and unfinished obligation can coexist. Keeping both is the central explanatory gain of the monograph. [E18:019; E19:010–011; E03:014; H01:10124:005/006]
