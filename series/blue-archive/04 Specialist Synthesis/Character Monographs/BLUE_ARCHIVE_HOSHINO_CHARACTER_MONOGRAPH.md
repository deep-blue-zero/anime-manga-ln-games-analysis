## 1. Core thesis

Hoshino values lives that can be lived together: the school with its inconvenient juniors, an adult who can be helped and worried over, a forgotten treasure hunt, fish, a comfortable nap, a quiet view and future outings. Protection matters because these lives matter. Her central difficulty is that she repeatedly treats responsibility as something only she can carry, until protecting companions entails denying their right to accompany, disagree with or protect her. The mature contrast is between solitary exceptional responsibility and reciprocal membership. Her pleasures are intrinsic ends of that membership, not merely rewards for useful service or symptoms of concealed grief.

The sea scene makes that distinction explicit. Hoshino says she is not troubled, clarifies that she was simply looking at the water and asks Sensei to recognize the view. She likes the surface precisely when she cannot see everything beneath it. The jumping fish then surprise and excite her; embarrassment follows enjoyment, not a revealed secret emergency. Her later message says that the description has no special deep meaning, before opening a further wish to see jumping whales together. The fish are observed, the whale outing is unfulfilled. (`BA:bond:10045:005:scene:001:u:0007–0019,0023–0043`; whole `BA:momotalk:10045:thread:100450250`, especially records `100450420–430,100450460–570`.)

That ordinary stance does not cancel her grief. Main's later crisis returns to Yume's death, Hoshino's self-blame and the notebook she could not find. Its endpoint expressly resists an uncomplicated recovery: Hoshino says being all right is not the right description, acknowledges recurring regret and gives both pain and happiness value within her life. She does not have to relinquish what was lost in order to return the hands offered to her. This is a changed stance toward living with loss, not evidence that grief has ended. (`BA:main:001:003:041:scene:001:u:0159–0176`; the late written exchange's shared Shiroko header requires the accepted counterpart-context qualification.)

The two passages constrain one another. Reading every quiet pleasure as concealment disregards Hoshino's explicit distinction; reading every nap as carefree ease disregards the repeated cost of solitary duty. A defensible account lets her be curious, affectionate, controlling, frightened and capable of change without forcing these into one hidden cause. It also preserves actual contrary conduct: she threatens the young Nonomi, excludes peers from a lethal decision, helps enforce a coerced sports routine and participates in pursuit after Serika and Ayane refuse an invitation. Care can become paternal control. (`MAIN001:003:002,007,025–027`; `BA:group:2101:001` and `2102:001`; `EVENT841:003–004`.)

Her most consequential development is therefore not that the formidable fighter learns to appear soft. Softness, play and desire already belong to her. It is that those she protects become subjects whose offered help has standing. Main's apology for going alone, resumed collective action and willingness to begin telling Yume's story matter because they expose the previous refusal to share responsibility. They do not retrospectively turn every private episode into a step in that crisis, certify a permanent cure or complete every wanted future. (`MAIN001:003:037–043`.)
