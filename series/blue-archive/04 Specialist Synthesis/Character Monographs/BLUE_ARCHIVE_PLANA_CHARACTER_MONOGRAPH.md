## A name offered and accepted

**PLANA-P001.** Plana's accepted name opens a relation in which company and judgment can coexist without becoming the same thing. The OS who accepts Arona's invitation can join a shared home, worry about her effect on another, and still make judgments that deserve resistance. Her characterization gains force from that combination. Being welcomed does not settle every ethical or epistemic question about her; having unsettled judgments does not make the welcome empty. The small acts of accepting a name, taking hands and negotiating address belong at the center of this account. [S09:28; S08:42; S02:181-183]

**PLANA-P002.** The naming exchange matters partly because the other OS first occupies the control room of a hostile crisis. Her counterpart identifications and survival forecast belong to that operational situation. The maintained account later resolves the recipient of Arona's name as this existing control-room subject. This supplies a consequential change in the terms on which she is addressed and received. It does not require imagining a new third OS, or treating the provisional shared label as proof that she and familiar Arona were one participant. [S08:30-34; S09:18-28]

**PLANA-P003.** Arona provides the name and its planetarium or night-sky image. Plana receives something another person has made for addressing her distinctly. That direction gives the exchange its relational character: the new name is an offer reaching toward a particular recipient, rather than a label the reader can treat as a complete explanation of its bearer. The image tells us about Arona's naming gesture. Plana's response gives it a different significance, because an offered address can be taken up. [S09:28]

**PLANA-P004.** Plana's response is qualified: she does not dislike the name. Its modest wording is part of the positive evidence. Acceptance need not be rewritten as exuberance to matter. It lets her answer the offer in her own terms, while the subsequent use of the Plana tag makes the distinct address available to the scene. The characterization here depends on an answer, not merely on being named by someone else. A restrained affirmative response can carry more specificity than an invented declaration of complete happiness. [S09:18-28]

**PLANA-P005.** The scene continues through going home together, taking hands and negotiating an affectionate older-sister versus Arona-senpai address. These acts give the invitation practical and conversational form. Shared hands make company enacted; negotiated address gives that company a shape the participants are still working out. Neither gesture needs a later rescue or increased system capacity to become meaningful. Nor must the address settle a biological family relation. Its force lies in how these two participants agree to speak and proceed together. [S09:28]

**PLANA-P006.** The home offer is not left wholly in the future. The chapter depicts Arona and Plana enacting a welcome home, then sustaining an affectionate shared routine with Sensei, the adult teacher. This matters for the local arc: an accepted invitation leads into represented company. Yet routine does not mean that every difficulty has disappeared. What changes is that Plana has a place in a shared encounter, not that the account has completed every question about how long it will last or what it will demand. [S08:38-42; S08:51]

## Remaining with others

**PLANA-P007.** Plana's private concern about Arona's sleepiness complicates that place. She attributes the sleepiness to added load from her own presence. The account does not independently measure the causal connection, so it cannot establish that her staying harms Arona. But the thought remains important as Plana's interpretation of company: she considers what her presence may cost someone else. That concern is not invalidated merely because the proposed explanation is unproved. It gives staying an ethical and affective question within the relation. [S08:24; S08:42; S08:51]

**PLANA-P008.** Arona separately says the two-OS Chest is more powerful. The two statements do different work. One voices a benefit of being together; the other privately considers a possible cost. Treating either as a final audit would erase the situation the other helps disclose. Plana's welcome should not depend on proving she adds net capacity, just as her private concern should not become a verdict that she must disappear. The relation can matter while its practical effects remain incompletely understood. [S08:42; S08:51]

**PLANA-P009.** Ordinary concern also appears when Sensei wakes after the dream involving a watch and an unfamiliar city. Plana has no memory of the watch, while Arona has only vague familiarity. Their concern at four in the morning and invitations to sleep, eat or seek a check support a practical response to the adult before the mystery is solved. These invitations should remain invitations, not imagined ingestion, an examination or a cure. Their value is that uncertainty does not suspend the possibility of attending to someone. [S04:68-72]

**PLANA-P010.** Plana's institutional situation is consequently best described through the specific work and relations available to her. She participates through the Shittim Chest, an apparatus involved in Sensei's privileged access and support, and through inquiries whose consequences reach the federal council. That is a substantial situation, without an evidenced school or club membership to supply a conventional student biography. A technical role places her in relations of authorization, dependence and consequence. It does not tell us that she owns the institution or decides every use of its power. [S08:30-34; S04:88-94; S01:58-66]

## Judgment with consequences

**PLANA-P011.** The immediate halo-destruction proposal in the Abydos account is serious counterevidence to any account that makes Plana's helpfulness harmless by definition. Sensei rejects the proposal. At the maintained paraphrase scope, that is enough to keep its severity active: an offered solution can be consequentially troubling even when it is not carried out. The scene cannot be absorbed into the warmth of the naming exchange as if welcome had endorsed everything she might propose. Her participation makes judgment answerable rather than automatically right. [S02:181-183]

**PLANA-P012.** Sensei's refusal also gives this relation a boundary. The adult does not merely receive technical proposals as self-validating outputs. At this point he rejects one, and the rejection must retain its own actor. The supplied account does not print a complete inner response from Plana that would let us call this a settled moral conversion. What it does preserve is an encounter in which her judgment meets resistance. Company and cooperation can contain such resistance without becoming either total approval or proof that the relation has failed. [S02:181-187]

**PLANA-P013.** Her crisis representation likewise needs more than a general verdict of danger or rescue. The control-room OS forecasts extremely low ship survival if the Ark detonates. The condition matters; a forecast is not an observed casualty. Elsewhere the chapter separates coalition work, a proposed two-OS rescue and the report of present Sensei's safe return. Those distinctions retain the force of actual cooperation without turning the whole outcome into one exclusive Plana feat. Competence here has participants, conditions and incomplete mechanics. [S08:22; S08:30-34; S08:58-60]

**PLANA-P014.** The report that Sensei returned safely is still a meaningful endpoint. Limits on the unseen mechanics do not reduce it to nothing. It gives the later home invitation a crisis aftermath in which return and company can be represented, rather than an obligation to reconstruct every omitted event first. For Plana's characterization, the distinction is useful: participating in care for a particular adult and being credited with every cause of his survival are different claims. The first can sustain a relation without requiring the second. [S08:22-34; S09:22-30]

**PLANA-P015.** The opening farewell passage of the later episode needs separate caution. Its maintained prose says one OS could finally help her teacher and worries about burden, while the initial tags name both voices alike. The later clear namegiving does not securely identify that earlier speaker or teacher. It therefore cannot carry a definite explanation that Plana acts from grief for Prenapates, the counterpart adult involved in the crisis. Her directly located acceptance of home and separate private concern can be analyzed without filling this unresolved passage with a biography. [S09:18-22; S09:28-30; S08:49-60]

## Distinct knowledge, unfinished inquiry

**PLANA-P016.** Different memory is one way the two OSs remain distinct beyond their names. Plana does not remember the watch that Arona vaguely recognizes. This difference does not prove why they differ, but it prevents their being treated as a single interchangeable witness. It also makes absence of recollection a situated report rather than a diagnosis of general ignorance. The scene lets them respond to Sensei while contributing unequal information. Their shared location and concern do not make their memories common property. [S04:68]

**PLANA-P017.** The disputed federal-president claimant sharpens that distinction into disagreement. Plana judges an ignorance mismatch and proposes mimesis, an imitation account; Arona offers an unproved memory-loss alternative. These are rival explanations, not one merged OS verdict. Plana's judgment matters because it presses on whether a presented identity should be accepted. Its consequential role does not authenticate it. The serious alternative remains in the scene, so a study that celebrates her discernment must also retain what that discernment has not established. [S04:82-88; S05:51-57]

**PLANA-P018.** The attempted inspection supplies a related limit. Sensei authorizes biometric-assisted pairing, and logs report connection and completed pairing. Core inspection then overflows capacity and ends in safe mode and backup restart, with apparent paired-data loss and no recovered core content. This is neither no operation at all nor successful access to the answer. Keeping both parts gives technical participation a history of attempted inquiry and failure, rather than making every connection proof that Plana knows what lies within it. [S04:88; S05:57]

**PLANA-P019.** The later proposed app remains unfinished in a different way. Arona says installation is possible and Plana offers capacity, while Sensei agrees to try bounded evidence collection without accepting an alliance. An offered resource can be a meaningful contribution before the project succeeds. Yet the account shows no installation or collection result. Plana's readiness to help should therefore be understood as an offer at this endpoint. Turning it into a proven diagnostic success would take away the uncertainty under which the offer is made. [S04:92-94; S05:61-63]

**PLANA-P020.** Plana's private claimed proof about why she and Arona are similar yet different is another kind of confidence. She addresses the hidden federal president as being here, but the account gives no derivation, location, reply or independent identity check. The claim lets the reader encounter thought not shared with all the people around her. It can deepen the difference between public cooperation and private interpretation without making the reader inherit her certainty. Her having an answer in her own account is not the same as the story supplying its demonstration. [S08:42; S08:54-60]

**PLANA-P021.** At the later rope-ball encounter, the chapter credits Sensei, Arona and Plana with finding the object together. The claimant infers distress; Minato, the ball's maker, supplies the inscription account that revises that interpretation. Plana's participation belongs in this strand of shared activity, without borrowing either the claimant's inference or the maker's knowledge. Nor should the other participants' subsequent grief become her private feeling by proximity. Ordinary participation remains worth retaining even when it is not the scene's decisive explanation. [S07:22-28; S07:46]

## What the relation establishes

**PLANA-P022.** The written presentation supports these distinctions by moving among system report, dialogue, negotiated address and private assertion. A later distinct name makes some attribution clearer; it does not retrospectively clarify every initial shared tag. A private thought can be available to the reader without being available to Arona or Sensei. These differences are part of Plana's narrative voice at the scope of the maintained written accounts. They do not establish performed tone, a universal verbal manner or sensory knowledge drawn from an uninspected video. [S09:18-28; S08:42; S08:59-60]

**PLANA-P023.** Her clearest local development is consequently specific: a control-room subject accepts a distinct name and offered home, then appears in shared routine. The later inquiry scenes let that relation contain unequal memory, disagreement, an attempted inspection and an uncompleted offer. That is richer than an isolated profile, while still short of a complete reform biography. In particular, the documentary placement of the Abydos material cannot be used to turn the halo proposal into a precisely dated relapse or proof of a completed conversion. [S08:30-42; S09:28; S04:68-94; S02:181-187]

**PLANA-P024.** Several exhaustive readings therefore fail for different reasons. An interface-only account misses her accepted address, concern and answers; a threat-only account misses enacted company and support. A permanent-redemption account cannot erase the severe proposal, and an infallible-knowledge account cannot absorb the rival hypotheses and unsuccessful inspection. None of those failures supplies an equally exhaustive opposite. The point is to keep the particular positive relation and the consequential contrary judgments answerable to one another. [S09:28; S08:42; S02:181-183; S04:88-94]

**PLANA-P025.** Plana's accepted company is an achievement in the relation, not a certificate that all her judgments are settled. Her name allows a particular participant to be addressed; her answer, shared hands and routine give the address lived consequences. Her worries and proposals then keep that participant from dissolving into the welcome itself. The strongest account preserves a place for her without making usefulness its entrance condition, and preserves scrutiny without making uncertainty a reason to discard what she has actually chosen and joined. [S09:28; S08:42; S02:181-183; S04:88-94]
