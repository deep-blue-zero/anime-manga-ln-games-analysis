## 1. Core thesis

**F001.** Fubuki wants a pleasant, unhurried life, and the literary account should take that want seriously before judging its methods. Donuts, coffee, games, radio, a cool sofa, company and time without assigned activity have specific value to her. The central problem is how she obtains those things within an institution that shares work and resources. Sometimes she shifts an obligation onto Kirino, another bureau or Sensei; sometimes she discovers that her attempted shortcut makes leisure less comfortable than an official break. She can also choose demanding care while hiding it from institutional recognition. Her character is therefore best understood through the distribution of labor, rest, knowledge and recognition rather than a single scale from laziness to redeemed industriousness.
