## 1. Core thesis: a particular animal rather than a borrowed human interior

Sammy has a represented life before the humans' decision becomes urgent. He sniffs visitors, participates in familiar play, moves through a fleet he knows and approaches a person after she falls. These acts do not require a crisis to matter. The later illness changes his capacity and the stakes of care, but it does not retroactively turn every toy, food interest or familiar place into a leadership test for Minato.

His subjecthood is also not secured by giving him an invented human monologue. There are three different warrants. Movement and contact are represented acts. Caregivers offer reports, memories and guesses about what those acts mean. In the final voyage, narration itself gives Sammy's view of fellow sailors and a contented expression. That last channel is stronger than a caregiver guessing his concern, while remaining much narrower than complete knowledge of an animal's understanding or an informed medical choice.

The most adequate reading therefore retains his activity, familiar belonging and vulnerability together. Sammy is neither an inert symbol of human reform nor a fully articulate moral decision-maker who knowingly chooses every event. The chapter gives particular comforts and a strong narrative depiction of death. It does not give a veterinary counterfactual showing whether shore care would have extended life, or an animal preference test proving that the voyage was the best available treatment. ## 10. Public and private self: an explicit limit with positive value

Sammy's represented public life includes movements among visitors, work sites, play and the final collective farewell. Familiar cushion, favorite toy and remembered habits give a more intimate spatial and relational scale. Calling these private-staged conditions does not establish a private written family, direct inward monologue or secret autobiography.

The relevant distinction is between particular familiarity and complete access. Minato's remembered bubbles and sunlight say something substantial about the relationship as she recounts it. Students' reported favorite foods say something substantial about their caregiving knowledge. Narration's final perspective says something substantial about the represented animal. None closes his entire historical preference set or his understanding of the proposed shore transfer.

After death, changed soap use, automatic tuna-can reaching and the retained cushion belong to survivors. They preserve continuing absence; they are not Sammy speaking through an object, evidence that he returns, or a universal grief cure. Minato's best-possible and everyone-satisfied appraisal is her evaluation. Her prospective new ties and self-authorship hope remain hopes. Sammy's value does not depend on supplying a human-style inward or private-join channel. (SM13, SM15–SM17, SM19.)
