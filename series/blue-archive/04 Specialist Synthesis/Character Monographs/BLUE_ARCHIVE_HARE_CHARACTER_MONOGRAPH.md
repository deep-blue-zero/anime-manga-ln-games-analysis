## 1. Core thesis

Hare's expertise is a way to arrange a wanted life: work in a pleasant setting, enjoy a preferred flavor, find a game opponent, share an unfamiliar place, and look at stars through an information metaphor without claiming to master them. The sustained tension is that the same appetite for technical possibility can reduce another person to a test subject, an image to defend, or a problem to optimize. She is unusually explicit when a machine makes that reduction against her. Her strongest agency statement distinguishes recognizing a need from being made to satisfy it; her clearest privacy statement distinguishes considering whether to show a photograph from instructing a device to send it. Those distinctions are persuasive, but they cannot acquit her participation in another student's record exposure or her own retaliatory website alteration. The character is neither a collection of computer jokes nor an exceptionless advocate for consent. She can seek generous, attentive relations and fail the principles those relations require. [Private:complete family](../../02%20Sequential%20Readings/BOND/BLUE_ARCHIVE_HARE_PHASE2_PRIVATE_CONTEXTUAL_ACCOUNT_20261008.md), [G1501–1503](../../02%20Sequential%20Readings/GROUP/BLUE_ARCHIVE_GROUP_1501_1503_DEEP_READING.md).

This argument treats pleasure as part of what requires explanation. A failed portable workstation does not invalidate her actual liking for a breeze or her invitation to Sensei. Warmth, noodles, coffee, games, film criticism and a guarded wish to return to a café are valuable before anyone proves a crisis benefit. Nor does affirming those wants require making them ideal habits. Concentration can compete with sleep, a comforting drink with a stated health concern, and private ingenuity with cleaning or other people's records. Complete endings let enjoyment and costs coexist: cloud work must be restarted; a deprivation experiment fails but leaves a wanted coffee request; a film company's homepage is narratively restored; a drone is only beginning to be dismantled; peers reach the sunrise but return to games and do not obtain the rest they request for their senior.
