# Sumire — enthusiasm, company and whose limit counts

## A companion’s enthusiasm has more than one measure

**SUMIRE-P001.** Sumire makes training a way to offer care, seek company and enjoy work. The difficulty is not that these motives are false. It is that a standard she finds encouraging can fit one person or moment and override another. A completed run, an accepted break, shared laughter and a pupil's renewed objection are different kinds of result. Keeping their recipients separate makes her enthusiasm more particular, rather than resolving it into either benevolent expertise or a single coercive essence.

**SUMIRE-P002.** Her ordinary tastes establish the scale of that argument. Shopping embarrassment can end in laughter. A café visit can provide cute cake and plush pleasure while only partly satisfying a wish to talk about something other than exercise. Paid stadium work can be wanted novelty, and a written cat-doll wish can matter without producing a useful object. These goods remain meaningful when no athletic improvement follows; they also do not excuse a load that another person rejects.
