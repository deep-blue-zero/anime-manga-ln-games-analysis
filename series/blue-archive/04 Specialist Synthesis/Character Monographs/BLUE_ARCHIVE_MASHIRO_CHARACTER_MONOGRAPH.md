## 1. Core thesis

Mashiro seeks an intelligible relationship between useful work, justice and a life she wants to share. She initiates difficult reading, invites observation of chosen practice, asks for clothing advice and wants another person to linger above a beautiful city. These are different forms of agency, rather than a blank seriousness which the adult fills with feeling. Her quietness does not abolish humor, enthusiasm, taste or talk. The sustained question is how a sincerely wanted practice becomes an experience for someone else: her own bodily limits can be precise, while her reading of another participant's fatigue, withdrawal or preferred leisure can fail. [P01:20003:002–015; 20004:002–006]

That asymmetry is a literary argument, not a permanent diagnosis of hypocrisy. A neck-touch objection is explicit; a later adult request to wait is refused. Homemade cooling food has real taste and care value; the special prize nevertheless ends in strenuous exercise with no swimming. By contrast, an actual clinic return and narrated recovery cannot be downgraded into a mere hope because its diagnosis is provisional. Mashiro's meanings require separate accounts of intention, permission, action, recipient experience and achieved ending. None of those levels supplies all the others. [P01:20003:005/006; 20004:003/005]

Her justice vocabulary is neither automatically empty nor an institutionally valid warrant. It can frame an unproved ambition to save everyone, make a commercial cuteness slogan sound like a rule, justify a personal gift or support a decision to rest. Public contexts also show actual restraint: she refuses to fire across a boundary until express permission arrives, offers a caring relay while guarding a venue and recognizes another audience's discomfort. Those acts complicate the private pressure without erasing it. The strongest portrait holds real ethical search and particular ordinary pleasures beside incomplete judgment about means, other people and institutional consequences. [P01:20003:003; 20004:002/003; L06; E51:004–014; L13; G01:1802]
