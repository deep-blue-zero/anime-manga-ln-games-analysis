## 1. Core thesis

Neru can dominate a confrontation without controlling how she is recognized or how company becomes mutual. Her power, formidable jacket and rough address are useful and sometimes coercive; they do not explain away her independent liking for the jacket, a failed can challenge, a robot toy, a pond, a rooftop view or a holiday. Across the received public and private arguments, the strongest sustained question is what happens when a leader used to decisive action wants participation whose value depends on another person's response. She sometimes obtains practical help or pleasure, sometimes imposes terms, and sometimes changes a request. These are distinct outcomes rather than a single conversion from threatening agent to harmless child. [N01:222–262; H01–H17; M21; M22; E06/E06C; E43]

The private sequence most clearly revises a demand after its apparent scene ending. On the last temporary-uniform outing she presses for future company, while the complete MomoTalk aftermath explicitly permits refusal: 『嫌だったら断って良いからな』. She also extends the invitation beyond the uniform to the same particular companion. That correction is real communicative action. It establishes neither universal consent reform nor the next completed outing. Earlier compulsory eating, a death threat attached to a secret roof, pressure around bandaging and a requested release from a hold remain adverse evidence. The full account must preserve both the change and the conduct it cannot retrospectively repair. [N01:238–258; H12:10111:005, whole thread101110190; H03/H05/H08/H11]

Her public relations pose a related problem at a larger scale. In Pavane she tests Alice's capability and later participates in an operation whose knowledge and initiative are distributed among students; in the ship event she helps companions while participating in intimidation and an unwanted pursuit. In the EXPO argument, her refusal to let Rio enlist companions and then exclude them through self-sacrifice is substantive care, and her objection to being dismissed as simple-minded is a substantive boundary. Neither is evidence that she grants every other recipient equal freedom. Strength can make company possible and can constrain it; the literary argument concerns how those uses coexist at exact achieved endpoints. [M21:019; M22:021–025; E06:001–011; E43:006–008]

The thesis therefore gives ordinary life its own weight. A wanted toy is not important only because it softens a fighter, and a quiet pond is not covert training. Those preferences show what she wants from time that work has previously consumed. Her authority and her pleasures illuminate each other without becoming each other's justification. The unfinished offer of a shoulder, a proposed shared day or an unprinted photograph deletion remains consequential even when no civic emergency follows. None is discarded for being low stakes. [H06/H10/H11/H12; H14–H17; E10/L67]

## 10. Competing readings

The combat-instrument reading explains the superlative profile, genuine assignments and confrontational competence, but fails to explain chosen jacket taste, can play, toy ownership, pond attention and finite company without artificially translating them into training. The reverse meek-child reading fails against initiative, resisted child address, explicit rejection of docility, coercive actions and public objection to disparagement. She can choose a child-branded toy without agreeing to infantilization, and wear an unfamiliar costume without acquiring a new obedient self. These counterexamples establish range rather than an exception list around a single essential role. [H01/H06/H08/H11/H13/H15; M21/M22; E43:006]

An ideal-care reading has real evidence: requested bedside presence, respected changing-room privacy, permitted rest, partial reciprocity, a specific Toki apology and resistance to Rio's isolation. It fails if it erases pressure around treatment, compulsory eating, fear, explosive entry, unconfirmed photograph deletion or the captive's resistance. A uniformly coercive reading has equally real adverse evidence but cannot explain the achieved privacy, rest accommodation or later permission to refuse without declaring every positive recipient response false. A serious synthesis preserves both, and asks what each act achieves and leaves unfinished for its actual recipient. [H02/H03/H07/H08/H11/H12; E06/E06C; E25:016; E43:007–008]
