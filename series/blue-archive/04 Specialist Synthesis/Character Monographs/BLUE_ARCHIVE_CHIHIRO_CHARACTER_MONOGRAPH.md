## 1. Core thesis

Chihiro tries to make protection usable. A password must be stronger than the convenient one an adult can remember, but she permits a limited concession to memory. A maintenance window should inconvenience fewer users, but she admits that some people still want access then. A student club should be recognized for its expertise, yet a client will not listen without an adult whose status he already trusts. These are practical problems of living with others, not demonstrations that the cleverest person can remove every difficulty. Her characteristic strength is to translate a norm into an ordinary arrangement while keeping at least some of the arrangement's costs visible. [P9 L162–182][private] [20013:002 u0013–0027;003 u0025–0046;005 u0007–0010][private]

That strength is vulnerable to self-exception. Anger, concern for Veritas's reputation and perceived restrictions on legitimate reporting lead her toward an unfair disclosure route that she herself condemns. In another peer context she rejects the idea that a technically impressive attack becomes acceptable because the defeated attackers belong to her own club. Both positions matter: she articulates a norm that can oppose group prestige, and she can fail that norm while trying to protect the group. A profile that calls her ethical cannot settle this contradiction, and a single failure cannot explain every kind of care she gives. [20013:006 u0020–0056][private] [G1503 s1u0021–0042][privacy]

Her wanted life is also more specific than being useful. Electronics shopping is a hobby; coffee from a particular quiet vending machine tastes especially good after work; she wants ordinary birthdays protected and enjoys company, books, cooking and the outdoors. She can request somewhere to sleep rather than disappear into service. The camping repertoire brings those preferences into leadership and reveals another tension: a person who wants others to rest orders their attendance and withholds the camping plan, then later gives them a genuine option to stop. When the club subsequently asks her to rest, she refuses because work is important. Pleasure, effort, care, authority and stopping are therefore related questions, without becoming identical ones. [P9 L156/174–192][private] [20013 written HobbyJp/3387–3402][private] [E832 E001–003/E007–010][camp]

The public technical work makes these questions harder, rather than replacing them. Chihiro can acknowledge a fragile connection, infer a possible computational opening and implement a tactic that produces a local result. Her competence includes saying what is not known or not yet accomplished. The crisis does not make every later demand on her time appropriate, nor does her need for rest make her contribution illusory. Her mature literary function is to keep protection connected to finite workers, different recipients and permission to choose a life, while exposing the occasions on which her own practice loses that connection. ## 3. Public self, private self, and who hears the norm

In the office Chihiro is a student teaching the adult responsible for important records. In a company she presents herself formally as a professional; in Veritas she is the vice-president who interrupts prestige-based excuses; in the private disclosure coda she asks a trusted adult whether he is disappointed. The difference is partly audience and partly practical goal. It does not require a concealed true self beneath a false professional mask. Her security norm can be sincere, her care can be real, and her acknowledgement can still occur in a conversation that omits the people affected by the act. [20013:002 u0013–0018;003 u0013–0036;006 u0047–0056][private] [G1503 s1u0040–0042][privacy]

The secrecy request changes what the club can receive. She wants to become a better example, but asks that the younger members not learn of the incident. This creates a gap between a private intention and public exemplary practice. Sensei's future-oriented response may help her continue trying; it does not show a revised curriculum, disclosure to peers, restored user privacy or an accountable public process. The vulnerable admission is consequential precisely because it is not automatically equivalent to those outcomes. Nor does the brief initial distancing from the anonymous hacker become an indefinitely sustained denial once the later acknowledgement is received. [20013:006 u0043–0056][private]

The company scene tests another audience boundary. The president discounts students before Sensei's reputation changes the encounter, and states that the offer would not have been made to them alone. Chihiro anticipates that prejudice and arranges a normal introduction. Calling the adult a human backdoor both captures her ingenuity and makes trust sound like an obstacle to bypass. The source supports this instrumental tension without showing coercion, forged credentials or an enacted software intrusion. Her stated distinction between gaining trust and wrongdoing remains a norm that the later selected repertoire can test. [20013:003 u0010–0012/0025–0045][private]

Her quieter written address adds warmth and limits to the public corrector. She laughs about Maki's expected painting, wishes Sensei ordinary happy days, overlooks her own birthday and admits that she cannot know all of Himari's thoughts. None is merely evidence that a disciplinarian is secretly soft. They are positive relations and incomplete knowledge in their own contexts. Affection for Maki does not supply blanket assent to painting without asking; a birthday oversight does not diagnose chronic self-neglect; a claim of knowledge's freedom and weight does not give her unrestricted access to every secret. [20013 written3384/3387–3394][private]

The camping and celebration peers resist a teacher-centered account of this self. Hare and Kotama give their own mixed evaluation of the outing; Maki wants a game contest partly to see Chihiro lose; the club asks Chihiro to rest; Noa hears her distinction between freedom and disorder. These people do more than confirm the adult's approval. Their actual taste, pressure, concern and conversation reveal what Chihiro's plans allow them to do and what they independently want from her.
