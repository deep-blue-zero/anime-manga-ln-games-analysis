## 1. Core thesis: the value of relief exceeds her proposed means of securing it

Satsuki's characterization turns on a tension between wanting relief and trying to command it. She seeks a world without suffering, valued company, comfortable leisure and reassurance. She also proposes recruitment, leverage and control over others, sometimes treating cooperation as already owed. The private family refuses to make those wishes and methods identical: manipulation fails, recipients object, and beneficial encounters still occur through protection, shared attention and particular assent. The public crisis adds useful investigation and collective action without validating every private claim about hypnosis. Her care has real local value; her wish to remove pain does not authorize deciding everybody's answer. [SA01–SA15; SA-CMP:Q01–Q06; M2C2E001,E009,E010,E018,E019.]

The positive evidence is concrete. Sunscreen is applied, a shallow-water fear ceases locally during company, a hug and cleanup are narrated, a person nearly drowning is pulled from the water, grooming and curry-udon eating occur, and night paperwork, comfort, a meal and a dorm escort are completed. These are not imaginary benefits inferred from institutional rhetoric. Equally concrete are refused recruitment, unaccepted loyalty, restraint until others release Sensei, failed techniques and her admission that she would exploit a reversed vulnerability. Genuine help cannot turn that last admission into a permanent conversion. [SA01–SA10; SA15:P02–P04.]

Her ordinary wants matter independently: shopping, chosen clothes, swimming, a view, food, a warm hand, atmosphere and walking beside someone are not reducible to professional cooperation. Their inclusion prevents a portrait consisting only of occult ambition or technical usefulness. The resulting thesis is comparative rather than clinical: Satsuki repeatedly wants company and relief, but different encounters expose whether another person's permission remains available. Nothing here certifies a hypnosis mechanism, diagnoses her motive, guarantees safety or establishes a complete chronology of reform. [SA03–SA05,SA11–SA14; SA-CMP:Q01,Q06.]
