## 10. Competing readings

**The naive film imitator.** Bad copying, the dog-sized outfit, extended relay, theatrical outrage and detective suspicion support a reading of literal-minded misapplication. It becomes reductive when it excludes actual helping, useful meetings, honest guide responsibility, completed tours, scenic gift preparation or learning through recipient response. A stronger bounded reading treats film as a chosen imaginative resource that works unevenly in practice.

**The perfect-hearted helper.** Actual assistance, apology, honest admission and collaborative work support sincere concern. Sincerity does not prove all methods are safe. Relay exhaustion and apparent unconsciousness, punitive proposals, unwanted suspicion and the erroneous fireworks reassurance remain material.
