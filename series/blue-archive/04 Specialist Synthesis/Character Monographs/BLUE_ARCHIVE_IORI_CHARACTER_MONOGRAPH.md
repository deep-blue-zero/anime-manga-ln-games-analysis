## 1. Core psychological and literary thesis

Iori's central tension is the relationship between competent intervention and the boundary that makes an intervention protective. She can intercept, pursue, inspect, ask for command, accept practical assistance and recognize a good result. She can also make a premature accusation, fail to notice a trap, threaten disproportionate force under the name of official work and become caught in comic or coercive situations she did not choose. Those are competing facts about the same represented repertoire. A reading that makes her either an infallible professional or an exclusively foolish victim removes the tension that gives her decisions significance.

Her boundaries are unusually concrete. She asks for a wound to be wrapped while limiting the helper's gaze. She entrusts an album while prohibiting disclosure. She accepts swimming instruction with a touch condition. She enjoys a sand bath before objecting to what is done with her sleeping body and its image. She wants warmth from a cherished blanket while refusing the adult's interest in its scent. She calls for rescue from a pit and separately requires photographs to be deleted. Each invitation names an object and purpose. Practical success can be real without answering a different claim over her body, property or privacy. Rescue does not retroactively make a photograph welcome; gratitude for a bandage does not make every future contact authorized. [Private family, source004/005/007/009/010/013/015/021.]

This precision changes the comic reading. Her indignation may be staged for amusement, but the represented acts distinguish an erroneous inference from an accurate refusal. The coin search begins with her mistaken suspicion of peeping. By contrast, the pit photograph really is taken during a situation in which she wants help, and her protest really remains unanswered in the later message route. Both can be funny in narrative function. They do not have the same evidential status. Calling all anger a personality trait hides whether she has misread an event or correctly identified an intrusion. Calling every refusal a concealed wish similarly turns a character capable of answering into an object whose answer the analyst replaces.

Her protective purpose is also a contested judgment, not a halo. The early Abydos encounter makes official work a rationale for treating interference as hostility. The later Gehenna crisis makes the protection of ordinary students a reason to resist her own seniors. These positions share a concern with what the Prefect Team is for, but they cannot be collapsed into one already perfect principle. Early force remains adverse evidence; later dissent is a meaningful decision. Private trust scenes cannot supply the missing causal history between them. The public sequence shows situated changes in the application of authority, while accountability and institutional repair remain incomplete. [MAIN V001 C001 E020; S2 V002 C001 E017; C002 E010/E016/E017/E019.]

An adequate thesis therefore holds three lines together. Iori has actual capacity, limited by attention, resources, fatigue and other actors. She has ordinary wants whose value survives duty framing. She can distinguish wanted assistance from an unwanted method, although her own official methods sometimes fail the analogous test for others. Her best public direction is not the disappearance of severity. It is the ability to ask what severity is serving, including when an institutional superior commands it. Her private life supplies particular answers about what care must respect; it does not prove she applies that lesson universally.
