## 1. Core thesis: modest satisfaction and dissatisfaction are both meaningful

Junko wants food, but she also wants the right to define the occasion around it. She considers price and finite appetite, records personal restaurant judgments, objects to a screenshot suggestion, likes an outfit while worrying about its cost, refuses exaggerated praise and resists being described only through hunger. She can want quiet company rather than another maximized meal. The ordinary practices make her dissatisfaction intelligible without making it her only identity. [J01–J13; J-CMP §§1–6]

Several stories actually give her what she wants at a local scale. Three bowls of simple noodles are narrated, a notebook outing enacts a meal, side-by-side cakes are eaten, and a final New Year greeting is reciprocal. Care until the kelp is expelled and a comforting return to Schale are stronger endpoints than a proposed cure or later visit. These successes matter alongside failed substitutes, distress and denied tastes. An unlucky-pattern account becomes false when it treats every occasion as another failure. [J03/J04/J06–J09]

Her limits are neither uniform tolerance nor an exemption from responsibility. She criticizes the club's methods and calls its surveillance stalking, yet joins kitchen help that overrides the cook and leaves the meal unresolved. She refuses insulting praise and has legitimate grievances, yet condemns Izumi and directs accumulated anger at a local gang whose first-meeting denial remains contrary evidence. Her own deprivation does not establish that every target caused it or that retaliatory violence restores the desired meal. [G1101–1104; MINI06010/06020; E810010]

The strongest portrait therefore concerns a selective, economically attentive person whose pleasures, privacy and company deserve independent consideration, whose judgments can be reasonable locally and excessive elsewhere. She needs no major crisis to make those interests analytical evidence. Their value also does not turn all of her actions into a rule of good sense. The monograph keeps wanted experience, practical objection, actual modest fulfillment and adverse response together.
