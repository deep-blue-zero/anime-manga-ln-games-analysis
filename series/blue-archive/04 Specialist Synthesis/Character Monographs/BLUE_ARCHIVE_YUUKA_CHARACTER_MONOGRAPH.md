# Yuuka — calculation, chosen pleasure, and the right to have limits

This is a Phase 3 literary and psychological synthesis for the positively joined 早瀬ユウカ / `BA_PERSON_YUUKA`. It uses the accepted arguments at their current Cycle008 scope. Earlier producer statements that admission or Phase2 remained pending are preserved in their owners as historical stages; they do not override the current admission. Ordinary, athletic and pajama records establish different settings and repertoires, not a three-stage life chronology. ROOT has received the complete monograph and its receiving record, checked the exact input bindings, and accepted the literary argument WITH LIMITS under the [semantic acceptance audit](../../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/BLUE_ARCHIVE_PHASE3_SEMANTIC_ACCEPTANCE_AUDIT.md). It creates no reconstruction model, prediction register or frozen eligibility denominator; the full-cast milestone thresholds remain unmet.

## 1. Core thesis

Yuuka's organizing commitment is to make shared activity answerable: to its resources, its stated purpose, the people doing the work, and the evidence used to justify a decision. Calculation is an important way she does this. It is also something she enjoys. The character becomes distorted if the first function is treated as her entire identity, or if the second is treated as an embarrassing exception to a supposedly joyless personality. Her pleasure in mathematics, games, efficient work, a chosen gift, music and a possible future home belongs to the same person who asks where money went and who must actually do the paperwork.

The strongest concise expression of this distinction is her complete MomoTalk attached to `BA:bond:13010:020`. She explicitly denies wanting a world in which numbers explain everything. Her wish is that others understand their importance enough to reduce her workload. The fresh Japanese witness contains both the denial and that labor motive. This is a practical complaint from someone situated in a particular division of work, not a declaration of universal numerical ontology. Her game interest is equally direct: the whole thread attached to E008 says she enjoys board and online games and finds working out an opponent's strategy pleasurable. Neither fact has to be rescued from a crisis scene to become analytical evidence. [Private33][private] [Mathematics encounter][math] [Poker encounter][poker]

Yet answerability does not make Yuuka an unerring judge. She can apply a double standard, mistake a prestigious name for adequate evidence, misunderstand an unfamiliar kind of value, or press another person into an activity she enjoys. The costly gift's split receipts, the Engineering budget story, and her insistence on a declined arithmetic drill are genuine contrary cases. A sympathetic account must let them count. Conversely, her fallibility does not erase her useful skill, her capacity to ask for help, her fair-treatment principle, or her willingness to accept a reciprocal rule when it inconveniences her. [Gift018][gift] [Engineering GROUP][group31] [Work and help][work] [Reciprocal rest][nap]

The monograph therefore treats her central tension as **responsibility with finite knowledge and finite availability**. She wants to be dependable, wants others to recognize the work behind dependability, and also wants activities and relations that do not consume her as an administrative resource. Public judgment becomes strongest when she remains able to revise it in response to an actual person or a concrete failed premise. Private closeness becomes strongest when the other person returns labor, respects a refusal or permits an incomplete disclosure. The same repertoire becomes ethically weaker when efficiency, fairness, curiosity or affection is used to override a recipient's control.

This is a situated character argument. It does not assert that a single mechanism predicts every future choice. Public coalition work, ordinary accounting, peer games and intimate embarrassment are different states with different stakes and knowledge. Their connection is demonstrated by the contrasts developed below, rather than assumed from the nickname “human calculator,” a romance convention, or a preexisting partial model.

## 2. Longitudinal arc and legitimate ordering

### From academy representative to revisable council judgment

The Prologue first establishes Yuuka among the academy representatives responding to an unfamiliar adult and a public disorder. Sensei's useful coordination is shown alongside the students' own protective labor; it does not create an omniscient or invulnerable adult. Yuuka's movement toward continued contact is understandable within that encounter. Base BOND E001 explicitly recalls the recapture operation, giving a local public/private bridge. This particular recollection permits more than disconnected comparison, but it does not date every private story relative to every later chapter. [Prologue][prologue] [Private33][private]

Pavane chapter 1 makes her council responsibility a serious obstacle to the Game Development Department. A deadline, dissolution criteria and limited resources are not merely a disguise for affection. The crucial complication is that membership cannot be settled by headcount alone: Alice's willingness to join is explicitly checked. Yuuka's later game participation, apology for calling the work junk, and temporary reprieve are meaningful revisions. The ending remains a provisional deferral until the next term, not permanent institutional security or proof that the original structural problem has disappeared. The reader can recognize a change in this local conflict without converting it into an irreversible transformation from cold bureaucracy to perfect understanding. [Pavane1][pavane1]

This sequence is especially important because her private enjoyment of games is already positively established elsewhere. It prevents an argument in which the club first teaches a wholly game-hating person to have fun. The public issue is the status of particular students' collective work under council criteria. Playing what they made and apologizing can change her judgment of that work even if strategic play was never alien to her. No unproved ordering between the private game encounter and the main chapter is needed to make that distinction.

### Extending intervention beyond a bounded assignment

Pavane chapter 2 places Yuuka and Noa inside a conflict whose technical and moral premises exceed their initial assignment. They recover records and supply Eridu's location, then continue helping. E023 expressly distinguishes the original plan of transmitting coordinates from Yuuka's insistence on helping to the end. The power cut interrupts Key's resource acquisition; Key's own report confirms immediate acquisition failure and shutdown. This gives Yuuka's intervention a concrete result. It does not establish permanent removal of Key, universal containment of followers, or a harmless shutdown. Engineering, Eimi, C&C and Himari independently broaden the available response. Alice's eventual retrieval depends on additional people and acts. [Pavane2][pavane2] [Pavane E023][pavane23]

Yuuka's objection to Rio also remains concrete. She accuses Rio of diverting Seminar's budget for Eridu and promises a later reprimand. This is neither a completed financial audit nor an imposed punishment. The chapter's later resignation, apology and disappearance leave accountability unfinished. Her protest against Rio's departure resists equating an apology with adequate institutional repair. This gives the public arc a firmer ethical content than simply taking Sensei's side: it identifies a problem whose resolution cannot be supplied by immediate rescue alone.

### Final: useful judgment inside an interdependent coalition

The Final chapters broaden her working environment. She attends emergency deliberation, participates in ship and defense work, questions a missile's provenance and a purportedly limitless energy mechanism, and helps operate time-critical coordination. These are distinct contributions. The coalition contains uncertain forecasts, failed tests and expertise that belongs to other people. E002 of Final chapter 2 explicitly shows actual meeting attendance and divided district responsibilities; it does not establish that all evacuation and defense promises have been carried out. E003 of chapter 3 keeps the failed missile contact separate from Hanako's untested physical analogy and Himari's currently blocked computational proposal. Yuuka's thermodynamic objection is a useful question within that inquiry, not an independently validated theory of the threat. [Final2][final2] [Final2 E002][final202] [Final3][final3] [Final3 E003][final303]

After the ship is seized, her inference that the backdoor required inside physical contact connects present failure to possible earlier intrusion. The inference has a basis in the represented diagnosis; it is not a forensic identification of who touched which system. Final chapter 4 then assigns her and Game Development to the western release device. The subsequent source reports east and west device destruction, the lower terminal strike, disconnection, and stabilized suppression. Yuuka cues and calls for action within this chain. She is neither the sole cause nor an expendable background name. [Final3 E016][final316] [Final4][final4] [Final4 E002][final402] [Final4 E004][final404]

Rio's later cooperative contribution can therefore be used without rewriting the earlier dispute. A temporary nine-second hold and a request for others' help are actual operational changes. They do not annul Yuuka's financial accusation, settle Rio's authority, or constitute a full restored council relationship. The reported return of Sensei and the coalition's local successes likewise do not certify every participant's bodily recovery or institution-wide legitimacy. A mature account keeps improved cooperation and unfinished accountability in the same frame.

## 3. Public self, private self, and the costs of being legible

Yuuka's public self is not simply a stern mask hiding a wholly opposite private person. Her work pleasure and self-standards are visible in private as well: she enjoys fast conversion of handwritten inventory units, wants her competence recognized, negotiates a manageable share of the adult's backlog, and accepts that an attendance rule applies to her. Her private self also contains real administrative disagreement. She refuses to convert every Schale toy purchase into a proper expense and distinguishes personal money advice from a right to control holiday arrangements. The continuity is substantive, while the settings change how much she must explain and what others think they may demand. [Work][work] [Nap][nap] [MINI32][mini]

The distinction between **being known** and **being made available** is central. In MINI040, Sora assumes that access to fiscal information may imply access to Sensei's personal schedule. Yuuka corrects the assumption. Her permission to ask is conditional on being able to answer; her student–teacher financial relation does not become a general entitlement to private details. She normally avoids nonurgent holiday contact and states an intended Seminar occasion and concern for a junior. The schedule is not shown completed and the unnamed junior is not securely identified. Her local power-bank need because a calculation failed is ordinary limitation, not an exposé of general incompetence.

That distinction becomes sharper when peers seek to make private information public. GROUP1502 gives Yuuka both temptation and a fairness standard: she admits the attraction of changing a body-related record but refuses privileged abuse because an accountant must be fair. The unchanged initial password then works. A serious reading retains both facts: expressed ethical restraint and insufficient operational protection. Veritas's unauthorized alteration and the false 100 kg retaliation transform a supposedly corrective or comic act into reputational harm. The coda reports a correction notice, yet other students continue repeating the falsehood. This is not her true weight or a clinical finding; neither is it a complete repair that makes the experience harmless. [Veritas GROUP][group15]

EVENT842 makes her a different kind of privacy holder. Noa asks what she thinks of Sensei. Yuuka says she does not dislike him, then objects to answering this way in front of everyone and takes the juice penalty. The fresh Japanese witness preserves the sequence: conditional agreement to play, the unwanted question, incomplete disclosure, the unpleasant taste, accusation that Noa meant to target her, and continued questions until almost all the mixture is consumed by Yuuka. Liking games and initially accepting the rules do not entail a wish to disclose this information or a fair distribution of the later burden. The penalty is not a secretly completed confession. [EVENT842][event842]

Nor is private disclosure an all-or-nothing measure of closeness. In pajama E003 she imagines a sunny detached house and life with someone loved, starts an unfinished “せんっ……,” then retreats from identifying that person and ends the late visit. The home is a positive wanted future even while its companion is unspecified. The fragment is suggestive literary evidence with an explicit limit; it is not a completed marriage proposal, an actual joint household, or permission to ignore her request to leave. [Electric sheep][sheep]

Her private self is thus more expansive than either “efficient accountant” or “secretly romantic girl.” There is pride, competitive play, novice uncertainty, aesthetic enjoyment, tiredness, chosen company, embarrassment and a capacity to withhold. Public legibility can help others recognize this repertoire. It can also become a burden when a visible relationship or a body-related record is treated as common property. Yuuka's attempts to manage impressions deserve analysis without making every embarrassment proof of deceit or every protest proof of assent.

## 4. School and club role: resources, authority, and revisable standards

### Accounting protects real work, but does not certify every decision

The council role gives Yuuka reasons to ask questions that others would prefer to avoid. Funds are finite. Work has owners. A successful festival or a promising invention needs maintenance, reimbursement and people willing to perform the necessary labor. GROUP1201–1203 makes her receipts and aftercare part of C&C's material situation. A polite request for merchant support, later thanks, and reported earnings that cover costs matter. The proposed continuation of profitable maid work then meets Neru's refusal. Financial success does not dispose of the workers' preferences. [C&C GROUP][group12]

Yuuka's finance encounter E003 develops a different limit. Faced with a painting offered as collateral, she does not decide rashly; she asks for an outside view and openly acknowledges limited art knowledge. Materials, socially conferred artistic value, and authenticity are separate questions. The borrower reports a celebrated artist and a 100-million-yen valuation; Sensei's eventual zero rests on a claimed provenance correction, not proof that all art is worthless apart from its materials. The purportedly valueless painting still hangs on the council wall. The comic remainder matters because the object's presence outlasts the transaction's rejection. Sensei's useful explanation and delayed disclosure of the decisive fact both remain visible. [Art encounter][art]

The same distinction between purpose and evidence governs the public club conflict. Game Development's desire to continue is not automatically enough to satisfy every resource constraint. Equally, calling its output junk is a judgment that can be revised after attending to the actual work. Checking Alice's voluntary membership prevents procedural headcount from becoming sufficient by itself. The resulting deferral and apology give substance to administrative learning without pretending that the source has supplied permanent funding or a comprehensive reform of club policy.

### Prestige and desire can alter her burden of proof

GROUP3101–3103 is the most serious ordinary counterexample to an ideal of uniformly evidence-led allocation. Yuuka begins with usefulness and practical criteria. She rejects unproved load-bearing cement and an individual flamethrower duster, and dismisses Momoi before fully hearing the proposal. Noa argues for curiosity and future possibility. Yuuka can acknowledge that value while insisting on a budget. The dispute is legitimate on both sides: demonstrated utility cannot exhaust all research value, but an attractive possibility cannot establish a cost or an outcome. [Engineering GROUP][group31]

The prestigious association with Himari changes the treatment of an inadequately specified design. Procedure and cost guarantees remain absent. When Noa objects that the proposal would consume several clubs' quarterly funds, Yuuka still wants to try it. Noa later reports that Himari does not recognize the design and that Rio provided no funding instruction. The project continues; Yuuka volunteers personal payment and hopes forecasting will recover a recent loss in her managed funds. Those wishes reveal personal investment in the purported machine's promise. They do not establish institutional misappropriation, a professional investment credential, or a legal breach.

The construction is hard, and Yuuka explicitly thanks Noa, saying she would otherwise have given up. That is genuine dependence and appreciated companionship. Noa enjoys helping but keeps a suspicion to herself because she was not asked. Help and affection coexist with an information asymmetry. The device's answer to a market question becomes a birthday/Pisces horoscope and lucky titanium, not a numerical forecast. The later titanium rise and an apparently satisfactory financial outcome do not validate a predictor: the favorable coda is qualified, and the device's displayed method still differs from what Yuuka expected. Neither inevitable bankruptcy nor successful scientific forecasting follows.

This episode should remain uncomfortable for a flattering account. Her standards become more flexible when prestigious expertise and a desired personal remedy are involved. It should also remain bounded. A single comic investment does not prove every earlier budget judgment corrupt, and Noa is not an omniscient puppeteer merely because she entertained a suspicion. The story makes room for enjoyment in difficult shared making while also criticizing the criteria by which the project was accepted.

### Situated authority is not comprehensive office ownership

The profile's council-president aspiration is a desire, not an accomplished election or a new role replacing her known responsibilities. Her threatened sanctions in Pavane1 are not shown issued. Her accusation against Rio is not audited. EVENT842's reflection-room confinement and accusations against Koyuki occur within represented Seminar discipline, but the account supplies neither an audited missing amount nor a legal warrant for confinement. These distinctions matter because an institution monograph must eventually address collective authority, accountability and effects on recipients. A character monograph can analyze Yuuka's contribution without certifying all those stronger outcomes. [Private33][private] [Pavane1][pavane1] [Pavane2][pavane2] [EVENT842][event842]

She is one participant in an institution that includes Rio, Noa, Koyuki and multiple clubs with their own reasons and capacities. This argues against both treating her as the entire council and treating every council outcome as an expression of her personal disposition. The separate Phase3 institution and relationship owners remain responsible for full-cast comparison and unresolved authority transfer.

## 5. Ordinary life: pleasure, material wants, and finite availability

### Mathematics and games as positive ends

The private sources allow Yuuka to like what she is good at. Converting inventory units quickly and receiving praise are enjoyable. Mental arithmetic is described as fun. Strategy games supply curiosity and competition. These statements have analytical value before any later emergency applies the skill. A labor-only interpretation misses the satisfaction; a pleasure-only interpretation misses the workload and the people she asks to share it. [Work][work] [Math][math] [Poker][poker]

The poker encounter should be read to its actual ending. Yuuka overstates the security of mathematical advantage and accepts an apparent winner's lesson about instinct. But the table contains two aces of hearts. She notices the impossible premise for a single-deck game, suspects a concealed second deck, and rejects the adult's final excuse. The fresh canonical witness also gives two final reply alternatives, including a request to call it a draw. Its older analytical owner calls all groups single prompts; this draft corrects that source-form point without replaying a raw command audit. The scene cannot honestly prove that intuition defeated calculation in fair play. Nor can Yuuka's discovery prove that she always catches deception. It establishes a specific change from confidence, through provisional deference, to a successful objection when concrete evidence appears.

The mathematics drill gives a parallel, with a different ethical cost. Sensei's fast answers are wrong; Yuuka rejects speed as sufficient evidence of correctness. That success belongs to her checking, not to adult pedagogical wisdom. She then prepares more training despite the adult's decline. Enjoyed competence becomes pressure to participate. This is a genuine countercase to a universally respectful caretaker, even though it remains an ordinary scene rather than a catastrophic failure. The refusal must survive the comic tone.

### Taste, making, gifts, and a possible home

The athletic private family includes chosen water, cooking, a food gift, baseball enthusiasm, photo exchange, a favorite walk, and a reported permission question around sports photographs. Cooking uncertainty and a stamina mistake broaden the character beyond ideal calculation. They do not diagnose general weakness or produce a developmental rank below the base version. The image records are part of the complete messages, but their pixels were not received; discussion of a photograph does not license an invented expression, inscription or composition. [Private33][private]

Her holiday chocolate planning is especially revealing because it gives ordinary desire an actual material problem. She considers likely flavors, attractive contents, the abundance of gifts Sensei may receive, business courtesy and a 10,000-yen price. On the holiday morning she says not all the chocolate needs to be eaten. The stated meaning is business-related. The receipt explanation says the cost is 5,000 yen, while narration shows a second 5,000-yen split-payment receipt. That tension qualifies the total-price presentation and her concern with consistency. It does not independently prove reimbursement, embezzlement, fraud or mutual romantic commitment. Sensei chooses not to challenge it. [Gift018][gift]

The gift is therefore neither worthless because it is purchased nor important only if it reveals romance. Taste-sensitive selection, a self-imposed spending standard, a wish not to disappoint, and impression management are sufficient literary contents. A kept, partly eaten or uneaten present can retain significance. No actual consumption endpoint is invented here.

The sunny-home wish is similarly valuable without fulfillment. She imagines a large comfortable house and a loved companion, retreats from naming that companion, and ends the visit. This is a future different from the immediate council desk and sleepless workload. Its importance need not depend on proving whom she meant. The written profiles add ordinary desires for fresh chicken, decoration, chosen birthday company and an aspiration toward council leadership. Written costume lines add campfire invitation, care for retail workers and an explicit stop to belly-touching. These are heterogeneous interests and limits. They are not a checklist proving every desire enacted or a chronology of growing intimacy. [Private33][private] [Electric sheep][sheep]

### Rest that has a material provision

The punctuality episode gives care an actual labor cost. Yuuka proposes reciprocity, then works late, sleeps at her desk and is late herself. She refuses an exception to her own rule. Sensei commands a nap and handles accumulated Seminar work while she sleeps. The alternative to punitive humiliation is not merely a soothing instruction; it is someone else doing work that would otherwise remain hers. This matters because E005 had shown the reverse risk: the adult tries to pass a neglected three-month backlog to a useful student, and she accepts only some help while requiring the adult to work. The pair makes responsibility bidirectional. [Nap][nap] [Work][work]

The pajama sources preserve partial improvement and recurring difficulty. She can report sleeping better while still going to bed after midnight and later say insomnia is not fully cured. The radio scene does not overwrite that qualification with a permanent clinical outcome. Kotama's recommendation, the program's gentle music, the DJ's candid lack of personal expertise and listeners' empathy form a support environment. One night's better sleep is narrated. The anonymous Schale's S contributor strongly suggests Sensei to Yuuka, but neither the broadcast nor the full later MomoTalk contains an express adult admission. Musical enjoyment is a positive experience even before any sleep benefit. [Radio][radio] [Electric sheep][sheep]

EVENT817 supplies a public-facing rest conflict: she worries because other helpers are working, and her nightmare of missiles and bankruptcy is not an actual sanction or financial collapse. The next-day opening gives a local result amid imperfect preparation, tired helpers and complaints. Her invitation for Sensei also to rest is a reciprocal request; acceptance and performance are not shown. Across these sources, rest is neither effortless exemption nor a clinical solution that fixes the division of labor forever. [EVENT817][event817]

## 6. Crisis behavior and what ordinary evidence changes

Yuuka's crisis competence is best described as contributing accurate, useful decisions while depending on others' reports and capacities. Pavane's cutoff has a confirmed immediate effect, yet its safety and permanence are open. The Final terminal sequence has convergent reports of success, yet rescue and longer-term security require more acts. The ordinary sources sharpen these distinctions: the power-bank error, weak password, split receipts, failed premise in poker and prestigious-machine experiment all oppose an assumption that her calculations or safeguards are automatically perfect. They do not make her crisis contributions merely lucky. [Pavane23][pavane23] [Final404][final404] [MINI32][mini] [Veritas GROUP][group15] [Engineering GROUP][group31]

The public arc also makes her willing to move beyond a narrowly completed task. Noa says the initial assignment was coordinates; Yuuka insists on further help. The intervention becomes ethically and practically valuable because there is still a person to reach and an active danger, rather than because every formal boundary is intrinsically bad. Full rescue remains distributed. Yuuka neither substitutes for Alice's own agency nor alone defeats Rio's technical power. The coalition's additional defenders and risky proposed dive preserve the real work of other people.

The Ark event accounts provide further public context with their own source-class boundary. EVENT822 positively reports a communications hack and its assistance in contacting the captive Shiroko; the subsequent transmission breaks and rescue/listening remain unfinished at that point. Familiar cycling, teasing, belongings and remembered restraint make recognition relational. The account's native/printed attribution seam at the cycling testimony is not silently repaired. Yuuka's communications contribution advances access; it cannot alone authenticate the complete identity or close her council-transfer gap. [EVENT822][event822]

EVENT823 separates intention, preparedness, successful reports and departure cues. Yuuka urges action amid unstable communications and incomplete escape preparation. Later she reports two people escaped, with Japanese “safely” and Korean explicit naming of Ako and Kayoko kept distinct. The Japanese text also gives a complaint mixed with gratitude about Rio's escape provision, absent in the Korean form. Her written teleport before the remaining sixteen is positive scoped departure evidence. It is not a complete roster of twenty-three installations, an independently tested return mechanism or durable ground safety for everyone. Gratitude and criticism coexist rather than forcing an all-or-nothing judgment of Rio. [EVENT823][event823]

Sports administration is a different crisis scale but still claim-bearing. EVENT818 requires joint inquiry, tags and finite access lists; stolen gear and limits of cameras complicate a simple efficient-host story. Protesters' demand for less work is a real ordinary grievance even where their methods are wrongful. Yuuka's school-race ambition coexists with host fairness. The source shows classmates making their participation conditional on her anchoring the relay; her reported uncertainty about running therefore belongs beside the apparent voluntariness of the competition. Neither universal enthusiasm nor complete freedom from peer pressure is established. [EVENT818][event818]

Her curiosity about sampling in the field and later wanted dance are positive elements of this public occasion. The latter is a future offer, not a performed dance. One printed intuition remark belongs by the accepted raw qualification to Hasumi, so it cannot be used to complete a story in which Yuuka has finally embraced intuition. The borrowed-item prompt and Noa's teasing do not securely reveal a confession or the exact memorandum. The final tea and body-loosening report gives an actual ordinary sensory afterstate without universal recovery. These details prevent the festival from becoming only evidence of management capacity.

The comparison across crisis states remains bounded: public danger can justify urgent shared action, but it does not erase financial accountability, bodily costs, or the words of unwilling recipients. Ordinary pleasure can motivate competent work, but it does not automatically legalize intervention. Yuuka's most defensible actions combine a concrete problem, a revisable inference, shared labor and attention to the affected person. Where one of these is absent, the account says so.

## 7. Relationship with Sensei: reciprocal dependence and adult fallibility

Yuuka's relationship with Sensei cannot be reduced to one direction of care. The early adult coordinates, learns from student expertise and is protected by students. The private adult receives help with documents and finances; the student sometimes receives practical assistance, a nap accompanied by replacement labor, concern after an apparent message error, shared drone retrieval, and possibly a supportive radio letter. The usefulness of these acts is source-specific. None gives Sensei unlimited authority over Yuuka's money, body, time, disclosure or future. [Prologue][prologue] [Work][work] [Nap][nap] [Electric sheep][sheep] [Radio][radio]

The paperwork distinction is particularly clear. Yuuka can enjoy being capable and praised while rejecting the dumping of the adult's neglected duties onto her. Her eventual help is conditional on Sensei working too. Conversely, Sensei's handling of her accumulated work during a nap is an actual contribution, not a claim that commanding rest solves everything. This gives the relationship a serious material reciprocity beneath its comic rebukes.

Adult errors must also remain part of the relation. The art encounter includes useful explanation but unnecessarily delayed decisive information. The poker demonstration is invalidated by duplicate cards. The arithmetic speed boast substitutes a proxy for accuracy and then tries to defend the mismatch. In the sleepy and domestic scenes, inward text and replied-to thought cannot always be promoted into secure public speech. A monograph that turns every lesson into adult wisdom would misread the represented failures and erase Yuuka's successful corrections. [Art][art] [Poker][poker] [Math][math] [Electric sheep][sheep]

Her own intervention can cross a boundary too. The declined arithmetic training does not become consent because she finds math enjoyable. Requiring next-afternoon helpers in EVENT842 to keep working until Sensei's unfinished tasks are done has a practical purpose, but Maki, Hare and Koyuki object. The endpoint still has ongoing work; neither completed filing nor fair compensation is narrated. The adult's earlier avoidance of duties and games invitation coexist with student-company pleasure, while physical pulling and mouth-covering of Koyuki are represented acts whose later fun cannot retroactively provide prior assent. The relationship owner must not let a flattering central-adult account hide the other recipients. [EVENT842][event842]

Yuuka's private affection is real as a field of desire and interpretation, while its exact meaning often remains bounded. The gift's business vocabulary, the home fragment, Noa's questions and her choice of birthday company provide more than neutral administration. They do not together establish mutual romantic commitment. Her refusal to answer peers is a choice about audience, not an empty coyness to be overturned. Ending a late room visit and stopping a touch are stronger direct limits than an observer's inference from embarrassment. A personal bond can be valued while these limits remain operative.

EVENT811 supplies a public reminder that appreciation is not all-owned by the adult: help protects students' enjoyment through different student contributions. Its accepted raw qualification identifies Yuuka at E010u42 despite a printed Suzumi label. Chihiro's later teasing is not proof of a fixed rivalry or a full romantic explanation of Yuuka's motive. Gratitude for a specific contribution does not certify every technique or later relationship state. [EVENT811][event811]

The most sustainable interpretation is reciprocal dependence with unevenly successful respect. Sensei can be useful and caring, can be evasive and misleading, and can be corrected. Yuuka can be generous and grateful, can negotiate a limit, and can impose her own preferred activity. The ethical question is answered by the particular act and recipient, rather than by the existence of affection alone.

## 8. Major peer relationships

### Noa: assistance, pleasure, and information asymmetry

Noa is an important partner whose relationship with Yuuka cannot be summarized as either benevolent teasing or manipulation. In Pavane, joint inquiry and intervention have real public consequences. In the Engineering episode, Noa supplies company and work without which Yuuka says she would have given up. This companionship is valued in the source. Noa also entertains a suspicion without proactively sharing it. Her help does not erase that choice, and her qualified suspicion does not make her an all-knowing controller. [Pavane2][pavane2] [Engineering GROUP][group31]

The Engineering conversation about Sensei needs an exact attribution limit. A line printed as Yuuka at GROUP3103u21 belongs to Noa by the accepted raw actor qualification. Yuuka's clean u22–23 objects to being asked in this manner; Noa's u24 notes the absence of a denial. This is a prompted exchange with interpretation, not an unprompted Yuuka confession.
