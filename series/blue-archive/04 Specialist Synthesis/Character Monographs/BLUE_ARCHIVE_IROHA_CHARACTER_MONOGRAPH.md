## 1. Core thesis: rest and competent service do not require unconditional obedience

Iroha's characterization gives substantial value to a life that does not have to justify every moment through work. Reading, snacks, comfortable rooms, a breeze, a quiet beach and selected company are wanted ends. She also undertakes useful work, notices other people's fatigue, helps finish an adult's tasks and coordinates a difficult public rescue. These are compatible capacities rather than evidence that one side of her life is a disguise. The defining tension is who may claim her effort and decide its purpose. She withdraws from demands she resents, chooses particular acts of help and can reject the institution she ordinarily serves. Her capacity to choose does not make every use of that capacity fair: punitive auditing, burdens imposed on others and evasive pretexts remain contrary evidence. [IR01–IR14; IR-CMP; G190; M2C2E005,E016,E018.]

The private repertoire therefore qualifies both a universal-laziness reading and a selfless-guardian reading. The first loses completed work, concern and pleasure in company. The second loses convenience, secrecy, recruitment tactics and the people who bear her or Pandemonium's demands. Her later resistance to coercive unity is a consequential choice, but it does not retrospectively convert arbitrary means into justified service. A literary account needs room for a person who wants relief, can give real help and still needs her relationships and institutional practices examined. [IR05–IR09; IR-CMP; G190; M2C2E005,E011,E016,E018,E019.]

Her right to withhold an answer is also reciprocal. Sensei refuses recruitment; Ibuki chooses to stay with Makoto; Iroha refuses water play and some photographs; coworkers accept or dispute distinct assignments. Particular acceptance is neither general permission nor proof that everybody shares an institution's aims. This thesis is a retrospective textual synthesis of the accepted arguments, not a reconstruction rule predicting what Iroha must do next. [IR02,IR05; MINI120,150; E860:002,004,013; M2C2E005,E010,E011.]

## 10. Competing readings and the evidence that resists them

A laziness-centered reading has genuine evidence: work avoidance, official pretexts, pleasure when burdens move elsewhere and resentment of assignment. Its contrary evidence is not merely a good intention. She helps finish work, cleans, directs equipment tasks and coordinates a risky coalition while naming uncertainty. It cannot explain those endpoints by treating all useful action as an exception that means nothing. A duty-centered reading makes the reverse error, treating rest as a defect eventually redeemed by productivity. The concrete pleasure of books, a breeze and selected company is sufficient reason to retain it. [IR01–IR09; MINI120; E860:019,021; M2C2E016,E018.]

A selfless protector reading draws on provision for Ibuki, fatigue recognition, practical help, Makoto's rescue and the personal appeal. G190's punitive use of cocoa and falling is a serious contrary case, as are secrecy, recruitment pretexts and shifting burdens. Concern for Ibuki has positive literary content and can still be used unfairly. Ibuki's independent decision to stay with Makoto further defeats a claim that care gives Iroha authority to determine the child's answer. The better account keeps beneficial acts and the costs of methods together, without global moral acquittal. [IR05–IR07; G190; M2C2E011; E860:010–013.]

A disillusionment narrative might arrange private refuge, institutional disappointment, resignation and rescue into a linear transformation. Main E005–E019 supports a local crisis sequence, but no source bridge dates every private or Event860 encounter around it. Even within the Main ending, formal personnel status, restitution and future duties are not completed. The claim of a stable conversion therefore exceeds the printed sequence. A freedom reading is defensible at the level of chosen answers and refusal, while reciprocity requires examining Iroha's own punitive and evasive acts. [IR-CMP; E860:004,016–027; M2C2E005,E011,E018,E019.]

A romance or exclusive-dependence reading may appeal to invitations, future cohabitation wording, beach company and refuge language. Its limits are the outsider's label, prospective grammar, locale differences, particular refusals and independently wanted solitude. A pure-comedy reading may use those same limits to dismiss company or fatigue as inconsequential. Both lose actual dinner, shared reading, help, selective access and the possible new association of the repurchased book. Those ordinary acts matter even without certifying romance, lifelong dependence or a clinical explanation. [IR02–IR11; IR-CMP.]

A competence-as-causality reading might make Iroha's coordination explain the entire recovery or treat Toramaru, a successful cannon shot and technical vocabulary as certification. E016's own three failure modes, E018's concurrent signal and personal interventions, Event860's untested weapon assumption and the independent collaborators resist that conclusion. Her represented work is substantial without a mechanism model. A harmless-subordinate reading then fails in the other direction: reluctance does not erase the punitive audit, and a public break does not complete accountability. The account remains strongest when it preserves the local power of her actions and their exact limits. [G190; E860:008–010; M2C2E016,E018,E019.]
