## 1. Core thesis

Hanae repeatedly starts projects that mean something to her: learning to make an injection less frightening, enjoying exercise and its small rewards, tending morning glories, inhabiting a Santa role, making a stocking and welcoming a giver. These projects supply her own ends. They are not merely preliminaries to a larger emergency or evidence that she deserves a caretaker label. Her difficulty is often the passage from an attractive end to a warranted method: if care should reduce fear, if rest is good, if Santa brings happiness, she can assume that an intense technique or a demanding routine must realize that good. Other people sometimes change the method, sometimes bear its cost, and sometimes have a final objection left unanswered.

That is a recurrent problem rather than an exhaustive flaw. In Valentine gift `BA:event:810:007:124`, Sensei objects to a concentrated caffeine drink and asks for hot chocolate. Hanae accepts that concrete preference, makes the drink with cute rabbit marshmallows, and enjoys discovering that she has given a Valentine present. A selected drinking action occurs; full finishing or restored health does not. This useful local revision is a serious counterexample to any thesis that her care is always imposed intensity. Conversely, normal bond E002 ends in repeated injections beyond her one-attempt promise, with Sensei's objections and an arm covered in needle marks. A benevolent learning wish cannot erase that actual breach. (`810:007:124:scene:001:u:0011`–`:0026`, `:choice:003`–`:005`; `BA:bond:23002:002:scene:001:u:0013`–`:0025`.)

The quiet garden supplies a second decisive contrast. Hanae gets up early because she wants to see morning-glory flowers, waters buds and talks about cultivation with an adult whose taste she asks. The anticipated bloom and office placement are not shown, but the watering, curiosity and shared conversation are. When she demands holiday exercise elsewhere, early rising is imposed against Sensei's desire to sleep. His subsequent freshness is real subjective testimony without settling his request to protect holidays. The contrast locates the importance of whose project is being pursued, not a simple opposition between energetic care and laziness. (`23002:008`, u0002–0014 and whole thread330; `23002:005`, u0002–0013 and whole thread150.)

Hanae therefore needs an account that retains delight and ambition alongside particular failures of knowledge, proportion and recipient attention. Her selective opposition to exploitative resale and her distinction between a child's nonmalicious lie and bad intent complicate universal gullibility. Her hazardous proposals, delayed warnings and expectation of Sensei's catch complicate a harmless innocent. Her actual hospitality, gifts and accepted hot-chocolate request complicate a sinister manipulator. Her distinctive literary work lies in these situated differences, not in a diagnostic label or a formal prediction rule.

## 10. Competing readings

**The invariably gullible innocent.** Believing Sensei's flight lie, expecting Santa and accepting doubtful method/product claims support this reading in specific scenes. Recognizing nonmalicious child lies, opposing exploitative resale, admitting a failed attempt and choosing her own projects resist its universal form. Her Santa role explicitly distinguishes literal truth from seriousness. A stronger incapacity-to-judge thesis would require evidence that these distinctions never matter, which the actual argument contradicts.

**The dangerous caregiver whose pleasure is only a pretext.** Repeated injections, threats, late warnings, proposed extreme procedures and collective excess are substantial contrary evidence to unqualified benevolence. They cannot be erased as jokes. Yet the gardening inquiry, handmade hospitality and wanted gifts are pleasures with their own ends; the nap, narrower wound care and requested hot chocolate have positive actual outcomes at finite scope. A stable intent to injure is not established by method error, a rejected proposal or euphemistic opponent conversion.

**A learner who becomes permanently competent through Sensei.** His objection can change a method; warm tea, a plaster and hot chocolate are secure local revisions. The root-tea postscript, plaster-fit explanation, unresolved fire inference, holiday-rest plea and Mach2 proposal are serious obstacles to a completed learning arc. The chapters do not certify credentials or durable safe transfer. A stronger claim requires subsequent comparable practice with recipient evidence and reliable knowledge, not a later affectionate profile line.

**A selfless medic whose ordinary details are incidental.** Care and giving are recurrent motives, but flowers, knitting, animals, friends, festivals, pretty scent, desired time and festive appearance are not merely diagnostic signs of helpfulness. She authors projects and wants others to like what she gives. The best limited version recognizes service as an important end; the exhaustive version fails to explain independent pleasure and gratitude toward a giver.

**Comedy removes the recipient's claim.** Exaggerated methods, Santa training and euphemistic “good children” can explain comic construction. Actual needle marks, adult refusals, a fall, surrender pain and Fuuka's protest remain consequential represented facts. Conversely, exaggeration is not a laboratory certificate of toxicity, healing, supernatural purification or lethality. Form can be funny while leaving an ethical or epistemic question unclosed.

**All growth is adult-created.** Sensei's permission to listen, affirmation of a modest errand, rescue and method objections are important. Gardening, knitting, the parkour conclusion, selective moral opposition, craft gifts and the sports-festival articulation also belong to Hanae. Serina, Mine, Utaha and particular recipients supply independent relations and reasons. A monograph should recognize what the adult makes possible without reallocating every child's project or peer act to him.
