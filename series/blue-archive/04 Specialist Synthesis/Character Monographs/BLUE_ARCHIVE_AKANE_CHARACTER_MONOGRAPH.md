## 1. Core thesis

Akane makes service into a way of choosing company and presenting herself, but neither successful work nor an appreciative recipient settles who she is. Her strongest continuity is the effort to make a relation hospitable: cleaning a room, preparing tea, helping a teammate dress, giving handmade sweets, trying a classmate invitation, or asking to be seen beyond an assigned usefulness. That effort has its own pleasures. It also has costs, misunderstandings, failed desired endings and forceful methods. Polished courtesy can coexist with consequential demolition, an unfulfilled conversation, or a recipient whose ability to decline is restricted. A mature account therefore asks what she wanted, whom her action answered, what they could answer back, and what actually happened. [A01:13000/20019/20057; G12; E06; L65]

This is a continuity of questions, rather than a single transition from perfect servant to authentic student. In the uniform private sequence, she corrects the adult's assumption that a true self is waiting to be revealed: she fears others discovering that she does not know herself. That disclosure makes earlier adaptive behavior legible without converting every earlier smile into a lie. She has already enjoyed cleaning, praise, tea, company and making sweets. She can sincerely like the things she does while remaining unsure how to recognize a self apart from other people's expectations. The classmates who welcome her glasses and permit her to give no explanation supply a relation that is not founded on a successful performance of an invented ideal. [A01:20057:003/005; L65:u17–32]

Nor does private uncertainty suspend public responsibility. On the ship, a familiar explosive routine breaks a changed brief, and Akane admits getting carried away. In another private operation, opponents end by seeking police protection from her while she reaffirms the use of bombs. These are serious contrary cases to automatic gentleness. Yet on the same ship she and Neru interrupt Karin's absorbed action against a resisting captive. She can identify and stop excess in another person's method while her own familiar methods remain insufficiently examined. The contradiction belongs in the character, rather than being repaired by a universally harmless comedy rule or a timeless cruelty diagnosis. [E06:004–006; A01:20057:006]

Her wanted life is consequently more than the avoidance of failure. It includes a freely chosen outing, a quieter early conversation, being the first face someone sees on waking or returning, an outfit properly noticed, learning a different bearing, confectionery craft, and enough proximity to feel company. Some are fulfilled locally; some remain requests or wishes. Their literary value survives whether they advance a mission. They expose the limits of reducing her to either an efficient C&C tool or an adult's desirable maid, and they also prevent the self-knowledge problem from consuming every ordinary satisfaction. [A01:13000:008/009,20019:002/005/006,20057:003/005/written; L65]

## 10. Competing readings

The perfect-servant reading explains her hospitality and useful accomplishments but fails the error, incomplete repair, wanted attention, missed conversation and uncertain self. The stronger interpretation keeps service as a real choice and capability while denying that capability settles identity or guarantees an appropriate method. The converse reading that every service act masks an empty self fails the explicit pleasure in craft, learning, cleaning, company and an achieved outing. Self-uncertainty is not proof that no preference is sincere. [A01; M22:021; L65; E06]

A simple growth ladder from base obedience through bunny self-display to uniform authenticity can organize an attractive story but outruns the chronology and misses the endpoints. Base already contains chosen affiliation and company valued before success; bunny contains both genuine learning and continued misunderstanding; uniform contains peer welcome and self-disclosure alongside bombs, pressure and unsolved company.
