## 1. Core thesis

Ayane's practical attention helps make Abydos governable and inhabitable. She grades evidence, maps routes, keeps records, learns repair, requests specific help and corrects other people's assumptions. But the same attention also serves her own pleasures: old finds, familiar drinks, a treasured flower-shaped pin, list-making, chosen treasure hunting and the wish to stay in bed. Her central tension is between being recognized as the person who makes things work and being recognized as a person whose wants need not be justified by work.

The helicopter photograph gives this tension its clearest small scene. After shared repair, Ayane initially tries to make the machine prominent; she understands the photograph as a record of the repair. The explanation that the photograph concerns **her** changes the situation. Embarrassment and agreement then belong to an understood personal purpose, rather than to the earlier machine-centered premise. The work is still incomplete, so personal recognition does not wait for perfect completion. (`BA:bond:26007:005:scene:001:u:0029–0068`.)

Her ability to choose is not uniformly protected. In another swimsuit encounter she insists on sleeping and asks the presumed Serika to give up. The visitor is Sensei, who enters and repeatedly shakes the futon before identification. Concern over garbled messages explains the visit; it does not convert the protests into permission. Her subsequent assent to leaving and the later privacy assurance are new states, without retrospectively authorizing the intrusion. The counterexample is essential to any claim that benevolent attention always recognizes her personhood. (`BA:bond:26007:003:u:0001–0040`; complete paired MomoTalk.)

MAIN enlarges the issue from personal recognition to institutional participation. Ayane already has a veto, evidence judgment and jurisdictional voice before she assumes temporary internal presidency. C003's vote, merger declaration and later account of Hoshino accepting presidency show differentiated governance, not a journey from incapacity to adult-authored agency. She can lead and then decline the office for herself. Yet she also authorizes coercive means, makes legal inferences beyond the inspected documents and misjudges some practical situations. Competence is real and fallible. The mature reading preserves her directed agency, wanted ordinary life and answerability for methods together.

## 10. Competing readings

### “The competent secretary is the complete person”

This is the strongest reductive reading because administration and correction recur. It captures much of her public contribution. It fails against her aesthetic interest, treasured object, wanted sleep, chosen treasure hunting, list-making pleasure, snacks and personal recognition. It also loses her presidency and later refusal of that office. The better account treats competence as a capacity through which she participates and sometimes enjoys herself, not a complete explanation of every desire or role.

### “Her real problem is that she cannot relax”

Work concern, guilt during illness and reluctance to burden the adult support this locally. The stronger universal claim fails against chosen idleness, treasure hunting and explicit determination to enjoy sleep. She is not always unaware of what she wants; sometimes others fail to respect a want she states clearly. Helping her relax can be welcome in one encounter and an imposed itinerary in another. No complete clinical overwork cause or durable recovery is established.

### “The adult recognizes her better than she recognizes herself”

The photo and accepted repair help give this reading some force. Personal recognition changes a machine-centered premise, and shared labor allows her to continue without carrying it alone. It fails as a universal hierarchy of knowledge: she knows repairs and directs work; she asks for specific assistance; the adult can misunderstand, joke poorly, declare limited expertise or intrude on sleep. Care works through negotiated information, not privileged adult access to an infallibly known inner person.

### “Procedure guarantees ethical restraint”

Veto, evidence grading and jurisdictional objections make this attractive. The records-seeking raid and C003's unadjudicated authority assertions prevent its universal form. Knowing that evidence is needed does not determine a proportionate means of obtaining it. Internal governance acts matter without validating all legal consequence. Her role is best understood as practical judgment that can be scrutinized, rather than a moral control device that automatically corrects everyone else.

### “A pleasant ending repairs earlier pressure”

Later agreement, privacy assurance, enjoyable rehearsal and warmth are genuine states. They do not authorize earlier shaking, costume pressure or a photographic purpose she did not initially understand. The contrary extreme also fails: early difficulty does not make her later chosen search, understood photo or rehearsal unreal. Completion belongs to a particular act under particular conditions. A single total verdict on the relationship or holiday loses this structure.

Confidence is high for source-bounded acts, reports, refusals, partial repairs and distinct office states. Confidence is strong but revisable for recognition beyond utility as the organizing literary interpretation. Motive exhaustiveness, global chronology, complete legal effects, bodily afterstates, technical safety and audiovisual performance remain limited. There is no held-out diagnostic, independent blind reading or model promotion.
