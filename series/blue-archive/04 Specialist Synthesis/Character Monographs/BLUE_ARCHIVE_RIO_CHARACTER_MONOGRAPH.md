## 1. Core thesis: revision that still needs other people

[RIO-P003] Rio's central contradiction is not that an emotionless engineer unexpectedly develops feelings. The available evidence already gives her taste, curiosity, embarrassment, fear of being disliked, wanted recognition, and a wish to be irreplaceable to someone. The sharper problem is how she decides whose reasons may alter a protective or technical plan. Her earlier control treats danger, assigned function, and confident calculation as reasons to exclude another person's participation. Later evidence repeatedly makes revision possible through a specific person's response: Alice asks to remain Alice, Himari dissents and then accepts limited help, Neru objects to being enlisted and sent away, Seia recognizes an impossible demand for self-proof, and the adult refuses more hacking or gives her making a different value. Their responses matter because they change the local decision, not because warmth supplies an automatic moral cure. [S06 §§2–3; S01 P03/P06/P07; S08 E006–008; S02 E006/E012.]

[RIO-P004] Rio explicitly identifies fear of what she did not understand about Alice and says she should have accepted Alice's individuality. She calls that realization a starting line. This is strong evidence of a revised self-account, particularly beside the earlier coercive seizure and proposed halo destruction. In the parts encounter she also rejects her former control belief, then offers to hack infrastructure again for the return journey, while written contexts request schedules and constant proximity before questioning whether such demands are appropriate. The contradiction is therefore inside the accepted ordinary repertoire, not an objection imported solely from an older dramatic scene. A person can acknowledge a wrong principle and still repeat some of its practical habits. Her acceptance of the refused return plan's alternative—the taxi—supports a finite instance of responsiveness without proving a permanent change in every use of power. [S01 P03/P06/W02; S06 E006–011.]

[RIO-P005] The same structure appears in a less dangerous form when Rio thinks a hobby with no output may be illegitimate. Industrial making without a use is wanted; poor reception makes her suspect she herself is wrong. The adult's art framing gives her relief, but her inference that art cannot be proved wrong remains fallible. The meaningful result is permission she begins to give herself to continue a pleasure, not a universal theory of art or immunity from criticism. Event843's refusal to enjoy the EXPO without having proved herself belongs beside this scene: usefulness is a recurring route through which she tries to earn participation. Her interest in gifts, food, and company shows why participation matters before any evaluation of its productivity. [S01 P07; S08 E008; S05 u0014–0079.]

[RIO-P006] The strongest longitudinal claim is that Rio becomes more capable of admitting uncertainty, receiving help, and recognizing a recipient as a chooser while unresolved habits of control remain visible. This is more precise than either total redemption or total stasis. It explains why accepted calculation, temporary suppression, actual thanks, unpacking after a limited invitation, and a middling meal can all count as positive endpoints without becoming a complete acquittal. It also explains why requests for information, unannounced assignments, hurt, refusal, and unfinished repair remain consequential after a successful operation. The narrative distributes the ability to correct Rio among peers, students, an adult, and Rio herself. It does not grant any one of them authority to declare all the others' objections exhausted. [S01 P04–P08; S02–S03; S08 E005–008; S09 E003/E023.]

## 2. Ordinary life: making, taste, food, and company

[RIO-P007] Rio's household machine introduces her through actual practical success and contested taste. Sensei finds a clean room, and she credits a modified household Avant-Garde. Creating the device and cleaning the room are linked in her account; the room's cleanliness is narrated. She regards the machine as cute, while the adult disputes its appearance and maid presentation. She excludes C&C and Alice from the robot category because she calls them human. Her pleasure in the machine's shape deserves analytical weight independently of whether it meets somebody else's design preference. The disagreement shows a particular taste, a particular audience, and a proposed attempt to gather more opinions. It does not show the audience vote, a worn costume, successful resizing, or mass production. [S01 P01, bond10133:002 and whole MM101330010.]

[RIO-P008] The whole MomoTalk tail matters because Rio reports she could not borrow the clothes. Rio nevertheless restates her role-based syllogism and obtains the adult's yielding response. This can be comic stubbornness, a desire for recognition, and a continued reliance on functional definition at once. The maid-clothed virtual avatar in P02 extends the same aesthetic curiosity: she says she made it from curiosity but has not tried the proposed virtual communication. A reader who takes only the working cleaner as evidence misses the failed borrowing and untested communication; a reader who takes only the odd design misses a room actually made clean. The ordinary scenes qualify technical perfection through small, specific outcomes without needing to become miniature versions of the public crisis. [S01 P01/P02.]

[RIO-P009] P07 makes the intrinsic value of making explicit. Rio resumes industrial object-making without a specified use, accepts offered space, and asks whether it burdens the receiver. Ventilation concerns lead to waiting outside while the printer works. Her zero-output judgment and report of poor reception reveal how an activity she enjoys can become a reason for self-doubt. The adult offers a different appraisal, and she expresses thanks without turning around as the printer announces completion. The finite outcome is an actual completed printing operation accompanied by relief and gratitude. Product quality, workplace safety, and a fulfilled near-you wish remain unverified. The pleasure is character-bearing whether or not the product contributes to a future plot. [S01 P07, bond20041:005/whole MM200410390.]

[RIO-P010] Food adds preference and warmth rather than merely fuel. The detour to protect a concealed location, discounted or frozen favorites, concern about delivery logs, and nutritional defense give Rio a situated routine with costs and wants. She then admits difficulty cooking. The adult also needs recipe help; shopping, online-assisted preparation, serving, and eating follow. Rio calls the stew neither bad nor especially tasty, yet warm in the chest. That response distinguishes sensory excellence from the experience of being cared for. It is meaningful because she ate this meal with this person under these conditions. Her nutritional claims are not an independent adequacy assessment, and affective warmth is not a clinical temperature finding. [S01 P05, bond20041:002.]

[RIO-P011] The complete thread later reports a cooking Avant-Garde finished and functioning in principle, but without the response produced by the adult's meal. A further modification is reported to improve flavor in P06's thread. These tails prevent an account that fixes Rio forever at initial difficulty or treats the machine as actually incapable of cooking. They also keep reported performance separate from narrated tasting. A future food offer and a three-day boxed-meal interval reach agreement, not an observed long-term routine. Wanting a repeat meal is sufficient evidence of an ordinary relational end; it need not prove dependency, perfect nutrition, or an exclusive source of care. [S01 P05/whole MM200410010; P06/whole MM200410270.]

[RIO-P012] The Valentine's gift preserves expertise while unsettling Rio's attempted exclusion of feeling. She surveys divergent preferences, forms a situated account of why people devote themselves to gifts, and presents crest-shaped chocolate after describing rare cacao, reverse engineering, and a purpose-built chocolatier. The represented object and her account of the procedure have different warrant. She chooses Millennium's crest because she says the school helped form who she is, then becomes embarrassed despite the rational process. Thanks makes her embarrassment more intense and she finally acknowledges happiness. Craft, institutional self-understanding, and giving are already affectively invested before the final word names the feeling. This quiet gift directly defeats a portrait made only from emergency control. [S05 u0020–0079.]

[RIO-P013] Ordinary pleasure does not erase complications within ordinary encounters. Rio withholds the card questions while wishing some answers were sincere; the other player cannot understand the symbols. She loses track of time behind closed curtains, only partly cleans a room, leaves bags, and falls. She threatens compensation for exposing the secret base, requests deletion afterward without a printed deletion, and asks for future time only if the recipient wishes. Yet local tidiness, requested contact with assent, shared dinner, gratitude, and a felt new perspective are positive. The scene does not require perfect housekeeping to make company valuable, and affection does not convert secrecy terms or unknown card answers into unlimited permission. [S01 P03/P08.]

## 3. Goals, implicit needs, fear, shame, and self-concept

[RIO-P014] Rio's explicit goals vary with context: contain a danger, complete an upgrade, perform a bodily test, retain concealment, produce a design, feed herself, repay help, remain near an irreplaceable person, or assist a coalition. Treating these as one undifferentiated efficiency drive loses their objects. In P02 she wants to surprise others with an upgrade and becomes absorbed; the thread reports completion with less improvement than expected. In P07 she wants to make something without a use. In P08 she wants to repay and learn domestic work she says Toki once helped with. A protective goal does not make every action protective, and a productive goal does not explain away the curiosity or pleasure accompanying it. [S01 P02/P07/P08.]

[RIO-P015] The inferred need is for ways to participate that do not require proving she is fully correct or uniquely useful first. This is an interpretation, not an unprinted diagnosis. It is supported by her zero-output concern, her relief at the art frame, her wish to be equally irreplaceable to Sensei, her worry about being disliked after hurting Seia, and her refusal to enjoy the EXPO without qualification. The sources also prevent turning that need into helpless dependence. She makes requests, offers expertise, accepts some limits, and can agree to remain for a specified period. What changes locally is the standard she allows to admit her into company; no source demonstrates that this standard has ceased to trouble her everywhere. [S01 P06/P07; S08 E006/E008.]

[RIO-P016] Fear is explicitly named in the Alice retrospective and materially situated in V002's danger. The device-linked damage, Momoi's serious injury, and Key's resource process are not imaginary concerns manufactured merely to excuse Rio. The error lies in extending that concern to an exclusive remedy and a person's total assigned identity. In later bodily work she also admits uncertainty: incomplete analysis can leave a fatal error possible, an HMD intervention has serious risk, and she can promise effort rather than assured survival. These admissions distinguish later reasoning from omniscience. They do not independently establish acceptable risk, informed agreement by every affected person, or a calibrated safety model. [S06 E006–024; S01 P03; S09 E003/E013.]

[RIO-P017] Shame and remorse can themselves become modes of exclusion. Rio calls Big Sister's creation and use a stain, claims responsibility, and offers to let companions leave while she proceeds alone. Seia asks why the condemned system remains usable; Neru and Seia reject being used and then excluded. Their objection shows that assuming sole burden may deny others' participation as effectively as asserting sole expertise. In V002 her proposed fatal shutdown has a real possible cost, but the available alternative comes from overlooked collaborators' concrete labor. Neither proposed sacrifice establishes malicious indifference; neither settles responsibility. The monograph therefore reads remorse as a consequential motive whose chosen expression can still require correction. [S08 E007; S06 E022–024.]

[RIO-P018] Self-concept differs from others' judgments without making every negative judgment unjust. Rio wrongly credits Sensei alone for the coalition's defeat of her system in V002 and Akane corrects the attribution. Himari's criticism later remains alongside requests for calculation and thanks for a contingency. Neru can promise direct objection and company, then actually object when Rio treats past data as proof that she will always speak openly. The gift recipient can value a rationally made object, while design audiences remain unreceptive. These responses demonstrate differentiated relationships, not unanimous rejection or acceptance. Rio's growth partly consists in encountering recipients whose speech cannot be safely predicted from her preferred account of them. [S06 E021; S02 E006/E015; S08 E004/E006; S01 P07; S05.]

## 5. Public role and private behavior

[RIO-P025] The public and private evidence share a concern with shaping environments, but the consequences differ. Rio's public authority can place people and resources under plans they did not help decide. A private cleaner, printer, meal machine, or suit test works on smaller objects and brings the recipient's answer into closer view. The relation is interpretively useful without equating a warm room's overlooked temperature with coercive seizure or a failed clothing request with disputed public spending. The ordinary failures demonstrate fallibility and differing preferences; the public failures add institutional power, injury, secrecy, and obligations of repair. Source-class difference is part of the explanation rather than a reason to dismiss either side. [S06 §§2–3; S01 P01/P04/P07.]

[RIO-P026] Concealment appears both as a practical security concern and a burden redistributed to other people. The food scene worries about location, face, and delivery logs. The parts scene leaves the adult carrying a large delivery and waiting until night while Rio reports traffic-light and camera hacking. The repayment scene threatens compensation for disclosure and later requests deletion. These are not independent infrastructure or security audits; they are represented demands and her reports. They nevertheless make the cost of maintaining her hidden life concrete. A desire for company can coexist with arrangements that constrain or inconvenience the person whose company she seeks. The refusal of further hacking leads to a taxi alternative actually accepted, not a reason to pretend the earlier burden never occurred. [S01 P05/P06/P08.]

[RIO-P027] Written public-facing and personal contexts preserve contradictions within the declared persona. One profile names design as a hobby and places her in Seminar; another couples means-at-any-cost safety with distinctive design and a hearsay Big Sister label. Lobby language can qualify rationality, speculate about hope, demand schedules or constant proximity, and then question appropriateness. Holiday goodwill is received even when the custom is not understood. These are contextual possibilities, not a new sequence of performed events. They support an account of a repertoire that contains self-questioning alongside control, not a clean partition in which all public power is bad and every private line is already reformed. [S01 W01/W02.]

[RIO-P028] There is also a gap between technical self-presentation and affective experience. The gift's exposition reaches embarrassment and happiness; the apparently unproductive making reaches gratitude; a meal of middling taste produces warmth. These endings do not contradict her ability to reason or engineer. They show that her account of an action can omit a component that becomes visible in doing it with a recipient. The other person's thanks or framing changes the meaning without auditing the manufacturing process. Public accountability therefore cannot be discharged by a private emotional endpoint, but those endpoints deserve full weight in understanding what Rio can want and learn. [S05 u0031–0079; S01 P05/P07.]

## 7. Competence, failure, crisis behavior, and use of power

[RIO-P034] Rio's competence is substantial but situated. A household machine cleans, parts support real work, a printer completes, calculation contributes to barrier breach, temporary suppression buys time, and Event854's cracking progresses through other people's actions. The argument does not need to deny these achievements to criticize her authority. The whole private threads also report finished upgrades and cooking improvements; those reports qualify initial difficulties without supplying independent measurements. The critical distinction is between local effect, represented procedure, performance report, and a forecast that claims exclusive necessity. Expertise remains useful even when its possessor misjudges recipients, overlooks temperature, or fails to anticipate a different technical contingency. [S01 P01/P02/P04–P07; S02–S03; S09 E003.]

[RIO-P035] V002 is the strongest case against exclusive-remedy reasoning. Rio's fear rests on an actual dangerous incident, but her percentage claims and forecasts cannot establish that unilateral destruction is the only available response. The rescue depends on resources and decisions that her account overlooks, including the student's earlier idea and a newly available helper. Her own proposed sacrifice later confronts a backfire risk from concentrated resources. The alternatives are neither an uncosted magical solution nor a refutation of all analysis: they are hard, distributed work with injuries, lost communications, depleted capacity, and further risk. This makes the chapter's critique a question of whose knowledge and participation are included in a plan. [S06 E006–024.]

[RIO-P036] Final C3 does not abandon calculation; it changes the relation in which calculation is used. Himari challenges past distrust while requesting help under safeguards, and Alice's chosen request to Kei is distinguished from subjugation. Rio's proposal contributes to an actual breach, but the ship's vulnerability and enemy backdoor defeat a reading of total mastery. Her return contingency is appreciated as a backup while criticism remains. Final C4 then requires an explicit request for coalition help within a temporary technique. These are serious changes in practice, not solely apologetic speech. They also leave safety, physical whereabouts, and institutional accountability open. [S02 E006/E012–016; S03 E001–005.]

[RIO-P037] The temperature test shows a different scale of failure. Rio insists testing is needed and accepts a back-only limit; checks, excess warmth, a hot forehead, fanning, and quick uneventful recovery are narrated. She has warmed the room for the guest and overlooks the environment while concentrating on the result. Technical care and an avoidable oversight coexist. This supports fallible attentiveness, local recipient negotiation, and received help. It does not establish a medical clearance or general suit safety; it also does not justify treating any consent to this particular test as unlimited bodily access. The local recovery is positive precisely at its printed scale. [S01 P04.]

[RIO-P038] Event854 supplies both an admission of uncertainty and a serious continuing misuse of technical suitability. Rio arrives when remote penetration fails, describes HMD failure risk, infers that Himari would intervene if the chance were too low, and promises effort rather than guaranteed survival. Later she keeps Kei informed about a machine plan while others remain uninformed, invokes Yuzu's experience, and orders Yuzu aboard after Yuzu protests she was never told she would pilot. Prior design feedback is not advance consent to deployment. The source's clear recipient response prevents technical competence from becoming an all-purpose authority over the body of the person best suited to a task. [S09 E002/E003/E008/E023 scene001.]

[RIO-P039] The machine's destruction, Rio's report of opened parachute and safe escape, and Yuzu's later pleasure at helping are all positive or material endpoints. They do not retroactively make the surprise assignment consensual or the prototype infallible. Similarly, recovered noncentral areas, interrupted core capture, returned communications, an apparent stopped belt, and a launched final reversal are successive local outcomes, not a finished whole battle. Rio's suspicion that an encrypted invitation may be a trap retains useful caution without certifying the voice's identity or every subsequent choice. The event ends in unresolved action; the monograph does not supply the missing victory from memory. [S09 E023 scenes002–003/E026–029.]

## 8. Sensei: particular care, boundaries, and irreplaceability

[RIO-P040] Rio calls Sensei irreplaceable and wishes to be valued in the same way. The parts encounter places that statement beside his real inconvenience and her renewed hacking proposal; his refusal and taxi provision produce an accepted alternative. Affection and ethical correction are therefore not separate lanes. A relationship can contain wanted closeness and a limit on what one participant may ask or do for it. The declaration is particular, not evidence of a complete reliance model, romance settled by every intimacy, or universal trust in adults. Eimi's position enters through Rio's report and remains reported. [S01 P06.]

[RIO-P041] The adult's ordinary care often changes a frame rather than supplying unrivaled expertise. He needs online recipe assistance, offers art as an appraisal, assists with work, and gives daily student life value when Rio worries about wasting his time. Warmth from the stew and relief about making belong to those finite interactions. They do not make him the sole origin of Rio's feelings or erase other relationships' contributions. Rio herself asks whether space is a burden, accepts competing duties in P02, notices hunger in P08, and conditions future time on his wish. These are acts of attention within an asymmetric adult/student relation whose obligations and differences remain relevant. [S01 P02/P05/P07/P08.]

[RIO-P042] Care also includes requests and limits that must stay local. Rio accepts back-only contact for a suit check, later requests proximity after a fall and receives assent. The first permission does not authorize the second, and neither grants future access. In the secret-base scene security terms and a deletion request coexist with care and dinner. In card play the adult participates without knowing the questions and protests their withholding; Rio's wished-for sincerity does not make the chosen answers mutual promises. The monograph treats these scenes as varied boundary negotiations rather than proof that every desired closeness has been transparently agreed. [S01 P03/P04/P08.]

[RIO-P043] The gift makes the recipient's response constitutive of the giving. Rio's manufacturing explanation cannot account for embarrassment and acknowledged happiness until she confronts the action of presenting something to this person and receiving thanks. The adult's printed alternatives converge on her written response without establishing that every option was spoken. Her own claim that Sensei influenced her changed interest is evidence about how she understands the relationship, not an independently dated cause of all later decisions. The particular positive reaction is sufficient to characterize the encounter; moral restitution and broad educational success require their own evidence.
