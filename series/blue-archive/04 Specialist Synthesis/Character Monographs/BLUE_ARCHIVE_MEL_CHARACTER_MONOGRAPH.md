## 1. Core thesis: creative ownership has intrinsic value and reciprocal limits

Mel's central struggle is to make something she can own as a choice while accepting that other people have tastes, bodies, belongings and labor of their own. Her defense of artistic independence is strongest when she declines imposed content and recognizes creation as her own wish.
