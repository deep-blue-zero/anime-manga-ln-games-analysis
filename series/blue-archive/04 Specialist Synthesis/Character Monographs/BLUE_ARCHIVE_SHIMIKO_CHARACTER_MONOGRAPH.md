## 1. Core thesis

Shimiko wants reading to be a place someone can choose, inhabit comfortably and enjoy with her. Her agency lies in particular recommendations, reader questions, quiet work, cushions, repairs, finding and lending the wanted book, gratitude and shared food. These are substantial ends rather than incidental evidence for her competence in a crisis. Her birthday desire for something other than another book prevents even her sincere bibliophilia from becoming an instruction to give the same thing in every relationship. A mature portrait therefore asks whether her enthusiastic care receives the other person's preference, and what happens when care becomes discipline or imposed labor. [P:complete Shimiko argument and written desire, L413–461/507–509](../../02%20Sequential%20Readings/BOND/BLUE_ARCHIVE_HINATA_UI_SHIMIKO_PRIVATE_CONTEXTUALIZATION_CHECKPOINT.md)

The answer is materially mixed. She checks whether shared sorting is unwanted work, prepares a comfortable setting, finds an actual requested book and gives a locally successful rest. Her rare-book expedition fails to find its advertised treasure while she and the adult enjoy the activity. Her library enforcement, by contrast, includes frightened responses and a narrator's account of coercive “education”; rules, claims to controlled force and adult praise do not establish safe or proportionate punishment. The same person can host attentively and enforce harmfully. Neither outcome substitutes for the other or yields an immutable gentle/violent essence. [P:hosting, enforcement, failed treasure and achieved ends, L415–445](../../02%20Sequential%20Readings/BOND/BLUE_ARCHIVE_HINATA_UI_SHIMIKO_PRIVATE_CONTEXTUALIZATION_CHECKPOINT.md)

Public comparisons widen this thesis without replacing the ordinary repertoire. In812 she admits a professional restoration limit and refers to Ui rather than pretending mastery. In851 she chooses media research and costume craft, makes a deliberately timed appearance and helps the audience contribute to a pursuit; her work also costs Ui sleep and has unshown material-use permission. These are different boundaries of competence and recipient care. The practical library, expert ancient-text work, performance and quiet company remain distinct responsibilities, with their actual successes, fears and unfinished consequences visible.
