## 1. Core thesis: pleasure becomes a vocation with unequal permissions

Meg's defining literary tension is that a wanted craft can make her attentive to another person's discomfort while making her destructive toward another person's right to use, govern or survive the product of that craft. She wants to dig, build, eat, play and spend time with people. This argument gives her ordinary wants their own evidentiary weight. Quiet noodles have a different value from a spectacular spring.
