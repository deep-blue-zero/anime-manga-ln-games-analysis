## 1. Core thesis

**Sakurako wants to be approachable without giving up a vocation she values. Her repertoire tests the difference between making a sincere offer and deciding how someone else must receive it.** Her most convincing successes occur when welcome has a particular form and another person can answer: a private conversation with permission to leave, a reported new friendship, a community poster that she can enjoy without displaying it everywhere, an unexpectedly enjoyable course followed by a direct question. Her failures arise when a favored form is enlarged into a demand: the public certification of her normality, outfits for every Sister, a frightening institutional invitation or an image campaign that costs a junior freedom. This pattern is an argument across specific scenes, not a universal incapacity, a personality diagnosis or a claim that all her affection hides domination.

Approachability is an intrinsic wish here. Three hours of karaoke, a friendly nickname, wanting the first taste of a coveted cake and a moment resting before a poster deserve attention without proving crisis competence. These pleasures also make her public position legible: the person who wants ordinary company is the same administrator whose presence changes the stakes of an invitation. A good intention cannot erase that difference in power. Conversely, another person's refusal does not prove the offered care was insincere or that the pleasure she actually received was false.

The sources give her knowledge and action at different grades. She can acknowledge a private burden without telling its contents. She can actually overlook Ui's unconsulted copying without receiving all the island findings. She can introduce an ancient scripture without this reader possessing its doctrine. A monograph therefore needs two limits together: neither treating her as an omniscient institutional authority nor reducing her to a harmless joke. The distinctive figure is a serious, often kind leader who learns some local forms of reciprocity while retaining blind spots and unresolved responsibilities.
