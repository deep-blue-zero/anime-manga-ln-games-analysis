## 1. Core thesis

Hanako seeks room to live with other people without being enclosed by what they want her potential to become. The evidence for that question is public and private: deliberate academic failure attributed to escape from elite expectations, a counterplan that uses her knowledge decisively, a wish for uncomplicated talk, and an island disclosure that she wanted a break and sea company too. Her wit and competence are real; so are play, embarrassment, quiet pleasure and difficult gratitude. The joined thesis does not identify every provocation as necessary camouflage or every quieter moment as disclosure of one final true self. It asks what kinds of company permit response without becoming another demand that she perform a prescribed role. [P03; M02; E07:E008]

That question must keep other people's room intact. Hanako can invite responsive company while making refusal hard, impose a soaking under a pretext, continue teasing after an objection or conceal information to keep an investigation going. Her desire does not certify the method. The adult leaves the water encounter, Koharu asks her to stop approaching, Ichika resists provocative framing, and Ui remains suspicious under pressure. These are consequential recipients, not background that disappears because a later disclosure is sympathetic. [P03:summer E003/E005; E07:E003/E010; E08:E020]

She is also not simply a calculating mask with vulnerability safely underneath. The first handmade gift encounter gives actual embarrassment and a requested private opening, while its contents remain undescribed. Her difficult spoken thanks at sunset is accomplished; the wished-for joint return after the soaking is not. Quiet company can occur without a trick in the bus-stop walk, but elsewhere provocation continues immediately after a claim of relaxation. The sources require a person whose purposes and methods vary, rather than a fixed two-layer decoding rule in which competence proves a plan and feeling proves a concealed innocence. [L06; P03:ordinary E020/summer E002–E005]

## 3. Public and private self

The public/private distinction in Hanako is not a distinction between fake obscenity and hidden purity. Publicly she can reassure Ayane about an audience's gaze, then disregard Ayane's microphone warning and deliver provocation that brings staff intervention. The event therefore supports neither a purely nihilistic scandal-maker nor a benevolent speech whose good intention certifies everyone’s assent. [L08:u0038–0082]

## 10. Competing readings

The strongest rival makes every provocation camouflage protecting a brilliant student from institutional capture. Her own account of deliberate failure and suffocation, plus actual planning ability, support the reading. It fails as a universal key. The sources also give wanted amusement and response, an admitted difficulty refusing, actual recipient objections and continuing teasing after relief. Camouflage may explain part of a choice without converting every method into necessity or every boundary into an obstacle she may rightly bypass.

A second reading treats her as an omniscient benevolent manipulator whose eventual results disclose a perfect prior plan. Actual tactical success and the island arrangement give it substance. Contrary evidence is serious: an access forecast fails, the traitor theory remains initially unsupported, she calls a catastrophic account many-leap reasoning, her private survey acknowledges luck and failed plans, and other people contribute materially or refuse. The group's positive outcomes do not remove those limits. Her competence is better understood as situated and collaborative, with sometimes troubling methods.

A third reading confines sincerity to a hidden quiet self and treats the teasing exterior as wholly false. Handmade effort, embarrassment, bus-stop company and difficult thanks give it attractive evidence. But the same quiet routes can coexist with the wish for reactions and immediate provocation. There is no need to deny sincerity to play, or to make every inconvenient joke an unreal self. Conversely, an emotionless-teaser reading cannot dismiss the explicit sensitive-heart objection and requested privacy merely because the box remains mysterious.

Finally, a romance or therapy ladder can convert all reciprocal teasing into assent and every summer scene into healing. The preserved departures, gift refusals, unmet return wish, privacy request and local rather than permanent gratitude defeat that total account. The stronger thesis permits desired company, particular achieved closeness and real relief while maintaining the other person's limits and Hanako's unfinished responsibility for how she seeks response.
