## 2. A useful conversation that also exposes the cost of visibility

Its printed accomplishment is modest and specific: Hina and Sensei read prepared documents and exchange opinions. Her embarrassment, wish to avoid inconveniencing him, fatigue and appreciation are also substantive characterization. The opening narrates arrival at an agreed meeting place on Gehenna's outskirts late at night, with nobody immediately around. That local emptiness is not evidence that the city is empty or that all peers are absent. Japanese gives a somewhat lonely impression; Korean gives an eerie feeling. Sensei's time question at `u4/raw128043` is an `[ns]` internal record, not a request Hina is shown hearing.

At `u31–u32`, Hina shares the current security situation and requests advice for a joint investigation in the next quarter. At `u33` she explicitly denies a personal purpose and identifies work as the reason. Sensei's date claim in `C006` meets her response at `u34`. The investigation is prospective. In contrast, `u35/raw128087` actually narrates reading the prepared documents and exchanging opinions. This distinction supplies the episode's institutional result without inventing a completed later investigation.

Her appreciation at `u36–u39` concerns a changed perspective useful for objectivity and the rarity of hearing Sensei's thinking. ## 5. Departure is printed; rest is intended

At `u44` Hina says it is too late and intends to return and rest. `u45` is the farewell; `u46/raw128104` narrates her tired departure. Arrival home, rest and sleep are not printed endpoints.

The fatigue and private-time constraint therefore remain present after the useful conversation.
