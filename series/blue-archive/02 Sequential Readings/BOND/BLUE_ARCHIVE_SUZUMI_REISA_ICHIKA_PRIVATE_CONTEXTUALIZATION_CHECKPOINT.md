The public and peer comparison therefore asks who defines safety, when help is wanted, how recipients respond, and which results are actually complete. Vigilance's nonofficial local work and Justice's institutional assignments differ. The final interpretation is plural and source-located, with no clinical diagnosis, legal endorsement, universal calendar or promoted reconstruction model.
