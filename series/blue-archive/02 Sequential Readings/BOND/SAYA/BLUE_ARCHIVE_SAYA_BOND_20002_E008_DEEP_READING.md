## Bond argument

Saya reports a changed bodily response when she spends time with or sees Sensei, says clubmates suggested love, and admits that she knows little about it. She then tries to understand her feelings through an experiment rather than accepting their interpretation. Her claimed love potion and comparison of expected responses are a proposed method, not a successful proof. Pressure on Sensei to test it, her claim of harmlessness and a disrupted bottle precede the serial written sequence in which Sensei appears as cheese and she bites his arm. The narrative afterword reports continued biting until the drug effect wears off. This does not erase the original reported attraction or uncertainty, but it prevents using the induced behavior as confirmation of mutual romance or a harmless experiment.
