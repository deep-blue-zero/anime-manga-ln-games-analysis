## Title

Quiet tea, cheese wishes and resistance to a fashion she created

## Thesis

A popular invention does not exhaust Saya’s wishes: she wants quiet tea and imagines a cheese fashion while rejecting indiscriminate use of her successful spice. The source supports a local change of attention and decision to stop production without proving an enduring abandonment of research.

## Reconstruction

Momo says sauce demand has displaced immortality research and tentatively proposes help from Nezusuke; Sensei refuses before any specified action is shown. In the tea shop Saya invites Sensei to join her. Alternative responses contrast rest with suspicion of tampering. After hearing of the ramen success, a worker asks her to taste a planned new menu and give an opinion, then offers a mooncake. Excess spicy fashion invades tea; Saya objects and calls for production to stop. Narration says the craze ends after a short time and the remaining stock apparently trades at a high price.

Production-stop ordering and disappearance are local outcomes, not permanent scientific reorientation. Remaining-stock prices are narrated as apparent/hearsay.
