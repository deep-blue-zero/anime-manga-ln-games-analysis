## Title

Restaurant hospitality, mobile leisure and failed instruction

## Thesis

Casual Saya’s skateboard is a practical means of pursuing restaurant exploration and ordinary pleasure. Her boastful self-description and poor instruction limit any conversion of agility or inventiveness into general competence.

## Reconstruction

She arrives on a board after an offscreen warning and explains how it serves narrow routes and hidden restaurants. She lends it to Sensei and claims natural genius cannot explain the skill. Sensei’s inward encouragement precedes a fall. Japanese narration says Saya catches up to and stops Sensei after about twelve and a half rotations; the next day Sensei cannot rise because of bruises.
