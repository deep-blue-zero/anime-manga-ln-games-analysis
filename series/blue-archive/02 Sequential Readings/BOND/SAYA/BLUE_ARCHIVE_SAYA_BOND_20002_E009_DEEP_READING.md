## Title

Attachment, experimental entitlement and qualified repentance

## Thesis

Saya treats Nezusuke as a long-standing companion whose disappearance matters to her, yet the apology does not establish relinquishment of experimental entitlement. Concern, relief and affectionate recognition coexist with a rapid qualification of her promise.

## Reconstruction

Saya reports Nezusuke missing and searches the laboratory. Club members propose that repeated mistreatment drove him away; this remains their suggested cause. Sensei stays through the search. Nezusuke returns, and Saya interprets his sounds as saying he went to the commercial association after hearing that it was distributing large sunflower seeds. She apologizes for harsh pranks, then distinguishes serious experiments and tries to restrain him while he struggles. The narration restores a friendly atmosphere without establishing a durable ethical change.
