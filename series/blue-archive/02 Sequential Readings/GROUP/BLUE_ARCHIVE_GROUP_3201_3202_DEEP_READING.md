### 3201: being allowed to prepare is itself a meaningful choice

Kaho routinely prepares the event and sees the new one as relatively similar to previous occasions (u0001–0004). Chise interrupts to request that she prepare it herself: this performance is special (u0005–0015). She offers an affirmative wish, not evidence of incapacity. Kaho first invokes existing props and modest scale, then agrees while offering extensive purchasing and setup assistance (u0012–0019). Her concern is not a single possessive motive. The parenthetical delight at Chise's cuteness coexists with the perceived duty of handover, guilt about offloading her own work and worry (u0016, u0022–0025).

Niya suggests Chise can manage and ought to be given this chance (u0020–0021, u0035–0036; u0020 canonically Kaho/raw text-bearing Niya). Chise says Kaho should rest in the warm sunlight and leaves for supplies (u0026–0030). Care goes in both directions: the usually supported performer makes rest available to the habitual organizer. Chise's specific wish to own the preparation is analytically distinct from both Kaho's duty and Niya's curiosity.

Kaho briefly proposes adolescence as an explanation for unusual initiative (u0033–0034). Niya calls that exaggerated. The source supplies Kaho's interpretation, not a developmental diagnosis or a universal personality change. Likewise, feared crowds and kidnapping are possibilities Kaho raises, not events that happen (u0037–0042). She decides to watch over Chise and draws Niya along despite Niya's reluctance (u0043–0045). Their secret following is actually acknowledged as suspicious by Niya; Kaho says it is the only way to respect Chise's wish while protecting her (u0049–0055). That justification does not constitute Chise's consent or demonstrate that no other arrangement was possible.

## 5. Ordinary value, repertoire and contrary readings

The packet grants Chise agency through deliberate attentiveness: sunny-day rest for Kaho, selected colors and food, representational dolls, altered familiar practice and gratitude spoken publicly. A relaxed register is compatible with planning. It is therefore insufficient to reconstruct her as passive, thoughtless or only a star whose meaning comes from others' admiration.

Kaho combines affection, practical duty, overprotection, excitement over tradition and learning to wait. Her parenthetical guilt about shifting labor is evidence against reducing concern to jealousy or control. Her covert following is evidence against taking all care as consent-respecting. Her apology admits doubt, but does not state that she disclosed or apologized for the following itself. Hold those distinctions open.

Niya supports giving Chise room, enjoys hidden participation, is nevertheless drawn into following and can feel personally unsettled by a chosen likeness. Director status does not remove small vanity or taste. The mutual pudding preference and juice habit remain positive ordinary evidence, not merely indicators of administrative style.

Directed relations are specific: Chise → Kaho rest; Kaho → Chise proposed comprehensive assistance and covert protection; Niya → Kaho encouragement to allow Chise agency; Chise → both individualized gratitude; Kaho → Chise admitted doubt and thanks; Niya → Chise playful appreciation with a residual daruma question. The spectators' affection is distinct from these peer relations, and the shops' courtesy is distinct from an official status instrument.

**Counterreadings:** Kaho's preparations really do have a continuity/handover basis. Chise's new objects do not prove rejection of every old tradition. Fan delight really occurs despite the host's script difficulty, but cannot become full audience consensus. Public gratitude genuinely moves the peers, without fully settling boundaries. Her closing wish for continued togetherness is a wish and present affiliation, not a guarantee against future conflict.
