## 1. Ordered synthesis

### E001-E005: from ambiguous signal to the care dilemma

Sensei, Arona and Plana find a rope ball marked `S.O.S.`; the claimant infers Odysseia and orders fleet recall without verifying distress. Flagship Olympos reaches Aohama port. Island captain Umiji Minato and deputy Tomoshita Ami, Trident's Sumika and Cruise Operations appear through a hospitality tour, but one docked ship does not prove the whole fleet's state. Minato says she made the ball and later explains the letters as a Sammy-related inscription, reversing the working emergency interpretation without resolving every step of how the ball left the fleet. Sammy is at least about fifteen by participant account, with reported health concerns and a familiar shipboard life. A one-week observation period opens an unresolved shore-versus-ship question. The taboos have social force, but their claimed protection from bad luck is untested. ### E006-E009: injury, scapegoating and external authority

Students associate Sammy's cough with a squall and other mishaps despite no causal evidence. Sammy slips on wet decking, Sumika nearly falls and Sensei prevents it but injures his back. Physician Funamori Sanae diagnoses a lumbar sprain and warns Sammy's health makes shipboard risk serious, while resisting a simple directive without considering the cat's will. Minato recognizes that old taboos help assign blame for frightening sea uncertainty; she loves Sammy but struggles to decide as captain. The identity-disputed claimant proposes a Federal Student Council hygiene declaration to remove him while she absorbs resentment. No inspected hygiene problem, written order or authenticating office record supports that proposed ground. Minato verbally assents; next day narration calls removal decided. Students are sad, angry or hopeful and prepare farewell gifts, but no one openly defies it yet. The claimant describes strategic unpopularity and hopes resentment will make students question authority; this is her theory, not a proven reform effect. Sensei promises to support a future desire outside the role she says defines her. ### E010-E011: an unsigned handover and reversal

After days and formal document exchange, Sammy's departure is prepared. Minato/Ami report minor disruptions, and students directly hide Sammy during the attempted capture, qualifying E009's earlier no-defiance snapshot. The chase exploits his ship familiarity but cannot disclose whether he understood the transfer or preferred a permanent destination. Ami warns of a sea fall; Minato falls and Sammy approaches her. She interprets this as concern. E011 returns them to the officers' room: Minato says she is physically all right, Sammy is present, and the claimant asks for the **still-unsigned handover form**. Thus E010's completed paperwork did not complete custody transfer. Minato admits taboo fear remains, asks to cancel departure, and the claimant agrees verbally. Neither formal rescission nor clinical plan is shown. Minato proposes patiently living alongside students who still fear bad luck, rather than forcing instant persuasion. Sensei's momentary hope of an unharmed ending is interrupted by Sanae's urgent call that Sammy is at a critical point. ### E012-E014: critical care, last voyage and a new title question

Sanae reports temperature 36°C, blood pressure 190 mmHg, pulse above 220, shallow open-mouth breathing and a grave prognosis. She hypothesizes renal and cardiac involvement but explicitly rejects Minato's single-cause guilt over the attempted removal; exact cause and counterfactual survival are not established. Ami says shipboard cat death is a major taboo and claims all prior ship cats ended ashore, without a checked record. Minato asks Sensei to let Odysseia decide, broadcasts Sammy's condition, orders Olympos to sail at 06:00, permits students to leave without penalty and defers objections until after the voyage. Olympos actually passes the breakwater. Golden Elysium joins with after-the-fact permission despite unverified guest schedules; Trident requests permission, and other clubs request sailing without a printed full fleet roster. Sammy closes his eyes among friends at a familiar view and narration ends his voyage. Half a month later, Sumika's habits and Minato's retained cushion show grief. Sumika reports no **known** major mishap but expressly lacks full accident knowledge and still practices right-foot boarding. Minato hopes for gradual taboo change and Odysseia's self-direction, not demonstrated lasting reform. At the station the claimant calls her not the “real captain” and departs before explanation.
