Sensei describes a jail cell and narration says it is completely cut off from outside (`scene:001:u:0001-0002`). Makoto visits, apologizes for the setting, says some unnamed people regarded Sensei as their greatest concern, and acknowledges agreeing to this method (`u:0003-0008`; `choice:001`). He recognizes that Sensei values all schools and might oppose harm to Kivotos, then argues that subordinating diverse schools to Gehenna would end wasteful conflict and release collective resources (`u:0009-0016`). These are Makoto's predictions and political theory, not measured counterfactual outcomes. Sensei answers that the earlier free Gehenna Makoto wanted was better. Makoto visibly reacts with pain, recovers, says he understands Sensei's view, threatens persistence and promises basic comfort in the cell (`u:0017-0027`; `choice:002-003`). The pain alongside a reminder of earlier freedom parallels E011/E012 tension, but does not prove a specific device effect, countermeasure or lasting reversal.

Shoko then visits and says she overheard Makoto. She correctly anticipates Sensei would not endorse harm to students, and calls Makoto and Hina too lenient. She argues that uncoordinated student search wastes scarce care and creates victims, that no one can make every student happy, and that oppressive control promises better aggregate benefit (`u:0028-0051`; `choice:004-007`). The resource-limit challenge is a substantive objection to Sensei's ethic, but her claims of net benefits and universal failure are unmeasured, and the prison itself is direct evidence of coercive cost. Sensei answers that struggle can be part of finding an answer and effort toward happiness must continue. This is an ethical commitment rather than proof everyone can be reached (`u:0042-0048`).

Sensei recalls Shoko's earlier ideal of someone who decides correctly without hesitation and suggests she may fear making the wrong choice. She is startled (`u:0052-0059`; `choice:008`). This is Sensei's interpretation of her inner state, not an externally verified diagnosis. Shoko then makes the decisive provenance disclosure: she says **she recommended Sensei's confinement to Makoto and Hina** and stands on the opposite side of the cell (`u:0060-0062`; `choice:009`). It identifies a participant in the custody decision and strengthens the explanation for E012's proposal. It does **not** by itself identify who operated the earlier broadcast, who created NK Ultra, who gave the original guard order, or whether Shoko alone decided confinement. Sensei acknowledges knowing; she asks why he does not hate her, and he answers that hatred is easy but not his work. Sensei suggests she wants to be hated because she cannot forgive herself; Shoko lashes out and tells him never to look at her that way again (`u:0063-0070`; `choice:010-011`). Her strong reaction supports an emotionally contested encounter but does not certify Sensei's self-forgiveness account as the sole cause or establish repentance. No release occurs.
