This is detailed **training/intention testimony in a dream-framed scene**, not a safety specification, tested bomb efficacy, measured Seia vulnerability or completed detonation in scene 1. Seia asks whether the visitor can bear becoming a killer. She concludes by asking whether the visitor actually sought advice on how to struggle rather than came to kill (`u:0047-0048`). There is no answer, no bomb act, no body/halo outcome and no final content of advice in this scene.
