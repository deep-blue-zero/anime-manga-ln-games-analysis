## 2. Treaty, Arius coalition and scapegoat: a stated political design

Sensei challenges her earlier E001 claim that the Eden Treaty was a martial alliance; Mika apologizes for deceiving them and says **it truly is a peace treaty**, not a usable armed alliance for Nagisa (`choice:002; u:0051-0052`). This substantially revises E001's partisan frame; it remains Mika's new admission, not independent treaty-text inspection.

Mika says she genuinely wanted Arius reconciliation but for a shared anti-Gehenna campaign (`u:0053-0060`). The `アリウスの生徒A` tags at `u:0055-0060` conflict with first-person sentences about what “I” told Sensei, whom “I” approached and whom “I” secretly supported; the safest attribution is a **Mika-context confession with corrupted labels**, not six secure statements by a generic student. This confirms an explicit **scapegoat plan** in Mika's words and changes E014's speculative blame-shift: it is no longer merely Hanako's hypothesis. It does not prove a completed framing or that Azusa was Mika's informed co-conspirator. Mika says she was surprised that the remedial club, rather than her expected attacker, got to Nagisa first (`u:0069-0070`).

Under Sensei's third singleton question, Mika says hostship is a means, not her desired prize: she wants Gehenna eliminated from Kivotos. She proposes removing Trinity moderates, filling seats with Arius, perhaps a new council, then reconstituted Trinity attacking Gehenna in full war (`u:0071-0076`).
