## 1. Seia's covenant theory: interpretive account, not omniscient statute

Seia addresses Sensei as an outside adult and develops an analogy among contract, promise and `戒律`: even a paper or oral promise may bind/define powerful parties (`u:0001-0006`). She says Trinity scripture speaks of ten ancient `戒命` and a paradise-expulsion promise (`u:0007-0008`), scripture report rather than independently established cosmology. She invokes Abydos/Gematria as a remembered example, asserting Gematria could only demand a contract and had to withdraw when it failed (`u:0009-0011`). That is **Seia's retrospective analogy**, not a fresh audit of every Abydos mechanism or a general impossibility theorem.

She says the Eden site and representative qualifications made a distorted First Council re-enactment, allowing Justina guardians to appear as special-method `複製（ミメシス）` tied to a bent covenant (`u:0013-0020`). This connects E006 venue history, E008 Arius/Maestro mimesis labels and E009 Saori's ETO claim into a proposed mechanism. Yet Seia expressly says she **did not know all this beforehand**, only observed via Sensei's dream, and that Gematria exceeds her basic understanding (`u:0022-0023`). Her “Gematria behind Arius” and “army cannot fall or die” conclusions (`u:0024-0025`) are informed dream-frame assertions, supported partly by Maestro's E008 appearance and Hasumi's E007 combat impressions, not a full proof of Gematria command hierarchy or universal immortality. Her `大人のやり方` is an ethical characterization of contract manipulation, not an endorsement by Sensei.

## 2. Saori's account: textual insertion and coercive “home”

Azusa attacks Saori head-on and loses an exchange; Saori says this ignores Azusa's own guerrilla strengths (`u:0027-0035`). This establishes immediate disadvantage, not a final defeat, injury diagnosis or full duel result. Misaki estimates around five minutes before reserves arrive, not exact measured time (`u:0036-0038`). Saori says Azusa's betrayal disrupted but did not overturn the plan, originally expected Mika, identifies Nagisa as a danger who attended signing, and calls Sisterhood losses an unexpected gain (`u:0039-0045`). These are her strategic appraisal and victim claims, not independently verified deaths.

Saori says they **stole Eden**, put a cruise missile into the ceremony cathedral, cleared obstacles and bent treaty content (`u:0046-0049`). Misaki claims Arius retained a formal Trinity-faction qualification because it was excluded when First Council factions merged; this is partisan historical/legal reasoning without reviewed charter (`u:0050-0053`). She presents apparent treaty wording: ETO intervenes in Trinity–Gehenna conflict, with an added clause assigning ETO to **Arius Squad** (`u:0054-0055`). This is the most specific in-story account yet of what Arius claims was written, but it remains **oral quotation by a participant**, not the instrument itself, and substitution/signature lawfulness is unresolved. Hiyori calls the covenant “real” and links it to mimesis; Saori says reproduced Justina protect the bent rule and that school hostilities become suppression targets (`u:0056-0060`). Azusa infers the rewrite was to secure anomalous troops (`u:0061-0063`); her inference fits speaker testimony but cannot independently establish mechanism or every motive.

Atsuko gestures, then `u:0065` carries an Atsuko tag while addressing “Atsuko, that cannot be done,” a clear voice-context fault. Saori tells “Princess” to desist, says Azusa's stubbornness was encouraged by a nearby adult, calls that teacher a liar and claims already to have disposed of them (`u:0064-0069`). E009 directly showed Sena initiating care for a live Sensei; Saori's death/termination belief must not replace that medical state. Saori recognizes Azusa enjoyed Trinity friendship but reframes it as a `甘い嘘` and claims only Squad accepts her (`u:0070-0075`). This is coercive belonging rhetoric, not evidence Sakurako's formal status or Hifumi's care was unreal. Saori invites Azusa to destroy her halo and claims the precept persists while its Arius subjects exist (`u:0076-0084`): a challenge and theory, not proof halo destruction is necessary/sufficient or a completed attack on Seia. Saori notices a plush and asks whether Azusa intends to flee again (`u:0085-0087`); neither a completed exit nor the plush's causal role is printed. Seia's discovery remains Saori's future threat.
