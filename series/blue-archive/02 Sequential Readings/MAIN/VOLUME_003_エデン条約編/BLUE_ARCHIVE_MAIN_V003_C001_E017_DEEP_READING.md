Those are Mika's assertions; no escort inventory or Nagisa knowledge check is printed. She predicts Nagisa asked Sensei to find a “Trinity traitor” and asks what explanation Sensei received (`u:0010-0015`). Sensei's first three choices are silence/question/silence. Mika infers that Nagisa gave “nothing,” but E009 actually shows Nagisa told Sensei her alleged treaty-sabotage rationale, group-box design and threatened collective expulsion, while withholding evidence/identity. Thus Mika's inference is **too broad** if treated as a statement of the earlier conversation's contents; Sensei's silence does not override E009. Her knowledge of the request is not itself proof of either an identified traitor or coordination with Nagisa.

Sensei now explicitly says they **refused Nagisa's proposal** and that suspect-hunting is not their role (`choice:004-005`). This sharpens E009's `私のやり方` and E012's promise to Hifumi: the adult rejects conscription into Nagisa's proposed method, though no full alternative plan is shown. Mika frames Schale as an external third party to Trinity, then asks whose side Sensei is on. Sensei answers `私は、生徒たちの味方だよ` and specifically affirms being on Mika's side (`choice:006-007`). Mika says this pleases her but could also mean being no one's ally if interests conflict (`u:0018-0034`). Her challenge is a real stress test, not a proof of duplicity or of unlimited adult capacity. Sensei's declarations cover students as students; they are not a guarantee to endorse each student's every plan.

## 2. Mika's offer and contested identification

Mika offers a transaction: she says she will tell Sensei who Nagisa seeks (`u:0034-0038`). She says she was the one who invited Sensei as club teacher despite Nagisa's opposition and wanted an actor who was neither Trinity nor Gehenna (`u:0039-0042`). This is new actor testimony about E003's delegation context, compatible with her earlier advocacy but not an independently inspected invitation record. She also says there are “larger, more complicated” matters without describing them (`u:0037`). No terms of a completed bargain or Sensei acceptance are printed.

Mika names **Shirasu Azusa** as the member Nagisa calls a traitor (`u:0043-0045`), then claims Azusa came from the Arius branch, long separated from Trinity (`u:0051-0052`). Transfer itself was already confirmed by Azusa in E006; **Arius origin and the political characterization are Mika's account**, not an inspected transfer file. Mika's question whether someone who does not “learn” can be called a student (`u:0053-0054`) is a stigmatizing normative challenge, not an objective revocation of Azusa's status or proof she cannot learn; E010/E014 showed her study commitment and peer questions. The source intercut adds Azusa answering an unknown voice's progress query with `今のところ、計画通り` (`u:0046-0049`). This directly shows she speaks of a plan, but not its content, goal, sponsor, location, exact timing or relation to treaty sabotage. Saori appears by a silent named line; assigning both `？？？` utterances to Saori is plausible but **not secure**. Neither intercut nor Mika's allegation establishes an actual betrayal or who, if anyone, intends to harm the treaty.

When Sensei asks why Mika tells them, she says she wants **Azusa protected** (`choice:011;u:0055-0058`). This complicates a simple “deliver suspect to Nagisa” reading. The episode does not specify from whom, by what means, at what cost, or whether Mika's professed aim is complete/candid. Sensei does not accept an articulated bargain or choose a protection plan before the continuation card. E009's alleged security threat, E012's Hifumi assignment and the E017 Azusa disclosure are now related narrative pressures, but none supplies independent proof of a just collective expulsion.
