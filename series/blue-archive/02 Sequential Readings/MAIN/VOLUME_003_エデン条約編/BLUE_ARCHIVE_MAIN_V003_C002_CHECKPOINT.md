## 1. Chapter proposition and adversarial qualification

> **The remedial four really do pass the third official exam together, with narrator-certified marks of Hanako 100, Azusa 97, Koharu 91, and Hifumi 94. That outcome defeats the threatened expulsion route premised on failing the academic condition. It does not retroactively make Nagisa's coercive construction benign, validate the lost-paper second exam, or establish formal administrative restoration. Concurrently, Mika identifies herself as the anti-peace insider, admits a Seia attack order, and describes an Arius-backed coup/war design that would scapegoat Azusa. Her self-incriminating confession revises the suspect case; it does not supply written orders, complete forensic history, a finished treaty, or a safe future.**

This synthesis resists two attractive but unsound reductions. First, “all passed” is an exceptionally strong **academic** resolution, not evidence that every police, personnel, medical, or treaty process has closed. Second, Mika's confession does not collapse every earlier uncertainty into her sole agency: Arius's own plan, Saori's next preparation, Nagisa's procedural actions, Hanako/Azusa's ethically costly response, and independent Sisterhood intervention remain distinct. The E020 epilogue explicitly refuses end-credit closure.

## 2. Prospective sequence and exam evidence

### E001–E004: competing treaty account, mock failure, and student agency

Mika gives Sensei a partisan Arius history, claims a martial Eden/ETO design, acknowledges forging Azusa's Trinity admission, and relates a Seia attack allegation. Her version is later **self-retracted in E017** on the treaty's nature, so C002 E001 cannot be promoted as an objective constitutional account. The second **club mock** yields Hanako 8, Azusa 58, Koharu 49, Hifumi 64; only Hifumi passes. Azusa explicitly values learning, meals and friend contact, admits a night-watch pretext, and fears she **might** betray their trust someday; a modal fear is not a completed offense. Nagisa then articulates four **different** suspect rationales: Koharu/Hasumi leverage, Hanako's unexplained deliberate grades, Azusa's background/violence reports, and intelligence about Hifumi's criminal-group “Faust” role. V001 documents Hifumi's coerced cover amid a real Black Market bank action; the mixture is not a proof of enduring voluntary criminal leadership. ### E010–E013: official procedural failure and unstable preparation

### E014–E018: a two-part student counterplan and insider disclosure

Hanako reports, secondhand, a Tea Party document-protection request that she expects to isolate the exam annex. She claims Justice stand-down and Tea Party-reaching obstruction, an Arius-backed replacement force, Nagisa's removal, her own host accession and war to erase Gehenna; she explicitly proposes Azusa as scapegoat. Direct self-incrimination and on-scene nonresponse/Arius arrival make the insider design strong **actor evidence**, but not a signed order archive or completed coup.
