## 2. From the unknowability of minds to expulsion

Sensei starts to call Hifumi's criminal-leader charge a misunderstanding and offers to explain, but Nagisa interrupts to ask how another person's heart, true intention or truth can be proven (`choice:004; u:0017-0022`). Sensei's explanation is not printed; Nagisa does **not** hear and reject a completed V001 reconstruction here. Her `所詮「他人」` claims an epistemic limit even for a person whose kindness and courtesy she knows. That limit is broadly true as a caution against mind-reading, but Nagisa then draws the non sequitur `ですから、退学させるしかない` for treaty success (`u:0023`). Inability to prove innocence or inner goodness is not proof of guilt or of the necessity/proportionality of expulsion. The source makes her fear intelligible without making her threat intelligence, procedure or remedy validated.

Choice `009` offers either to bring Nagisa out of that darkness **or** definitely make all remedial students pass. Both options oppose her sorting logic; neither is an observed cure or exam result. The duplicated Nagisa `ふふっ、そうですか` follows convergence. She says the matter has become simple, encourages Sensei and declares she will work her own way (`u:0029-0031`). This is a reciprocal adversarial commitment, not agreement to cancel expulsion, endorse Sensei's method, or an explicit new tactic. Sensei inwardly notes leaving (`u:0032`).
