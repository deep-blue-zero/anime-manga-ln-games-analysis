Hiyori/Misaki say Squad is surrounded; Saori counters that endlessly multiplying Justina makes every effort meaningless (`u:0139-0144`). “Infinite” is her adversarial claim, not a counted force inventory. Then `u:0156-0202` alternates generic `ナレーション` tags “Hifumi,” “Remedial Club,” first-person declarations and “Blue Archive.” Its wording is Hifumi-perspectival—she refuses a story where Azusa must become a killer, prefers friendship, effort, comfort, smiles and happy endings, and claims the group decides its own story—but the tagged structure is **not a normal uninterrupted spoken monologue**. It is a thematic montage, not narrator-certified guaranteed happiness or reality-changing speech. It answers Seia/Saori's “ending” at the level of chosen narrative/effort, while no final battle result follows.

Hiyori sees rain clouds change; Misaki asks if weather was manipulated or a precept was involved, and Hiyori asks if it is a miracle (`u:0203-0207`). Those are **questions**, not a mechanism finding. Saori rejects “happy ending” as empty words; Sensei's final choices say an adult's duty is to support students' own dreams (`u:0228-0230; choices 013-014`).
