## 2. Azusa makes a unilateral lethal plan, not an accomplished killing

Azusa says someone must stop the crisis, tells Hifumi not to come closer and insists the path ahead is her own place, unsuitable for Hifumi's goodness (`u:0018-0030`). The italic `「人殺し」` (`u:0032`) and `人殺しになった私は` (`u:0034`) are dangerous self-labeling in a prospective separation speech, **not evidence that she has already killed someone**. She accepts global blame for wounds, Sensei's shooting, Seia's coma and school damage (`u:0037-0041`); that is her guilt attribution, not a demonstrated sole causal chain. In particular, E002's protective false-death account and unexplained continuing sleep cannot be replaced by her compressed self-blame. Hifumi disputes the blame and hopes Sensei will awaken (`u:0042-0044`); hope is not medical prognosis. Azusa rejects a happy ending as unavailable in “this world” (`u:0046`), an embattled belief rather than the story's adjudicated ontology.

Azusa explicitly states that she is going to `壊しに` Saori's halo and says no other method can stop the situation (`u:0048-0049`). She repeats `私はこれから、人を殺す`, invokes her training in a place where killing was normal and calls that her “real self” (`u:0051-0054`). The source establishes **intent and a claimed necessity**, not a fired shot, broken halo, death, Saori's location, tactical feasibility or proof alternatives are absent. Her Arius training does not automatically nullify her observed learning, friendship or formal Trinity status.

## 3. The farewell carries an alternative identity without proving resolution

Italic `ナレーション` gives Azusa-addressed thanks for friendship, the `アズサちゃん` address, plush gift, sea trip and days of learning with the remedial club (`u:0056-0063`). The content is strongly Azusa-perspectival, but the generic narration tag means these are **not spoken lines Hifumi demonstrably hears**. Azusa then directly says learning was fun, she will not forget the time even in death, and she is glad she could briefly be a club student (`u:0064-0066`). That speech undercuts her own claim that lethal training exhausts her identity, while the text does not declare a settled self-revision. She says goodbye. Hifumi pleads for another way and invokes the still-unfulfilled promise to go to the sea together and watch Peroro's adventure anime (`u:0068-0074`). The episode ends on Hifumi calling her name, without a narrated travel or confirmed successful interception. The title's “unreachable other side” is a present relational divide, not a guaranteed permanent rupture.
