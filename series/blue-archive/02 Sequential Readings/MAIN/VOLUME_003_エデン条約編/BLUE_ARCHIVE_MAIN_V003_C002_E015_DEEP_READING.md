She claims Justice Realization has already been informed and warns them to flee (`u:0072`); a beep follows, but the dispatch, recipient, receipt and response are not shown. A student calls this a bluff, asserting that if their own information is correct Justice Realization cannot move, and the commander orders continued advance (`u:0075-0078`). This is adversarial confidence in an **unidentified information channel**, not narrator confirmation that Justice is immobilized or that Hanako's suspected traitor exists.
