## 3. Distributed ETO authority: a countermodel, not an inspected charter

Hina says she sees the Eden Treaty as a genuine peace treaty, not an armed alliance, even if ETO is called an armed group (`u:0054-0060`). She asserts Nagisa cannot control ETO alone: Makoto would have equal authority, other Tea Party and Pandemonium members would share some, and only broad concerted collusion could theoretically repurpose it. She argues that if both schools could coordinate such a takeover they might simply merge; Makoto is, in her judgment, incapable of that cooperation (`u:0056-0061`). This is a **structural countermodel to Mika's E001 Leviathan fear** and contrasts with Nagisa's E009 neutral-peacekeeper presentation, but no treaty text, vote rules, command chart or dissenting office-holder response is produced. The E005 Makoto meeting supplies one local example of poor cooperation; it does not prove she can never cooperate or that distributed authority is fail-safe. Hina herself concedes theoretical collective abuse, so her model narrows one-person capture, not all danger.

Hina says she, not Makoto, pushed the treaty on Gehenna's side and guesses Makoto “probably” has not thought it through (`u:0062`). She explains her own reason in weary terms: if ETO improves Gehenna order, she might retire as Prefect chair (`u:0063-0064`). This is a direct self-report of motive and an expected institutional effect, not a signed resignation, proven order improvement, or Makoto's own account. It also gives Gehenna-side agency beyond the Mika/Nagisa polarity: Hina's peace-order motive is distinct from both. Sena calls her back before longer testing. Hina asks whether Sensei will protect the remedial club; Sensei answers **yes** (`u:0065-0070; choice:018`). That is a more specific promise than E017's general student-side declaration, still without a visible protection method or official second-exam outcome.

The four students then reflect on the long day. Azusa says it was fun; Koharu is pleased she first fought alongside Hasumi and felt useful; Hanako links that joy to needing to avoid failure and says she too will work; Hifumi proposes sleep before next study (`u:0071-0083`). This reinforces E004's ordinary belonging and E003 Hanako effort pledge but does not show exam passage, completed security learning, or disclosure of the hidden collective sanction to Koharu/Azusa. The next-title card promises hope without supplying its content.
