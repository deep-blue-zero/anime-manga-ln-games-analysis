## 2. Misaki's bridge risk and the refusal to abandon her

The group reaches a long-abandoned high bridge. Misaki describes a deep, fast-flowing river below (`scene:002:u:0002-0008`). She recognizes Saori's choice to bring Sensei and says she and the others were offered Arius return for killing Sensei. Saori confirms being told Sensei's killing would excuse their betrayal, though she says her offer was somewhat different (`u:0012-0019`). This **partially corroborates delivery** of Beatrice's E005 claimed kill-for-reprieve task through two Squad members, but not that either accepted it, who authored every variant or any attempt here. Sensei responds that if Saori meant to kill them, they would not be safe now (`choice:003`); a local trust judgment, not global certainty.

Misaki stands in danger near the edge and argues Atsuko cannot be rescued against all Arius students and likely unknown weapons before the deadline; even success, she says, would leave them homeless in pain (`u:0020-0035`). Her `vanitas vanitatum` is her claim that futility is the only satisfactory truth, not narrator certification. Sensei warns her not to move farther (`choice:004`), without delivering a philosophical answer. Saori recognizes a self-harm threat, says she would jump in after Misaki and repeatedly perform resuscitation, and says prior attempts failed (`u:0036-0045`). These lines are **serious risk evidence** and Saori's history claim, not proof of a current jump, exact past methods/frequency or guaranteed rescue. No one enters the river. Misaki does not deny the pattern, loses confidence in this attempt and agrees to continue with Saori, partly in leader-obedience terms (`u:0046-0053`). Hiyori is relieved; no durable mental-health recovery is shown.

Misaki says about ninety minutes remain to reach the **entrance**; Saori interprets that as ninety minutes until **midnight**, not until the dawn ritual itself (`u:0054-0056`). They set off while Saori promises to explain en route. This is a movement commitment, not achieved entry, successful rescue or verified timetable beyond their own reckoning. `u:0060` is E009 title `次回;アリウス自治区へ（１）`.
