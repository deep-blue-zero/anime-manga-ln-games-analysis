## 1. Mika's history: federation and a suppressed remainder

Mika resumes E017's Azusa-protection request and describes Trinity as a union of many factions, with Pater/Filius/Sanctus central and other predecessors including Rescue Knights and Sisterhood (`scene:001:u:0001-0016`). She narrates an earlier period of conflict, a First Council promise to join and stop fighting, and Arius as the dissenter (`u:0017-0022`). She says Arius's scriptural difference had been slight and its school/life resembled the others, yet newly powerful Trinity brutally suppressed, expelled and dispersed Arius (`u:0019-0029`). This is **Mika's historical account**, including hedges `だったはず`, `かもしれない`, `多分` and admitted ignorance of Arius's present location; no council minutes, casualty record, map or GSC knowledge check is shown. Her claim that most students forgot the conflict is an estimate, not a survey. The account does, however, give a coherent political rationale for why she regards E017's Arius-origin disclosure as fraught rather than a neutral transfer detail.

Mika then calls the Eden Treaty a replay of the First Council: the apparent Trinity–Gehenna peace would create a combined armed Eden Treaty Organization (`u:0030-0036`). E009 Nagisa had described an ETO neutral peacekeeping force; Mika emphasizes `武力同盟` and worries that concentrated force will again crush disliked outsiders. Both are actor framings, not a signed treaty or operating ETO. Her hypothetical Nagisa attack on the leaderless GSC, seizure of its presidency or preemptive strike on Millennium (`u:0037-0042`) is **speculation**—she expressly says she does not know detailed aims. The “Leviathan” analogy later is political argument, not literal monster existence.

## 2. Seia allegation: secrecy without independent verification

Mika breaks off with a comparison to Seia, says it may be a slip, and initially repeats the E003 hospitalization account (`u:0043-0046`). When Sensei presses, Mika says she cannot return after telling this and fears betrayal, then asserts Seia was **not** hospitalized but had her halo broken after a sudden attack by an unknown assailant last year; “hospitalized” is the public cover (`u:0047-0064`). She says Tea Party knows, Sisterhood possibly does, and even she does not know the culprit, though she has a guess she will not state (`u:0060-0066`). This is a consequential direct disclosure by Mika and an explicit revision of **her own** earlier E003 story, but no witness record, injury/status examination, perpetrator or independent confirmation is in this unit. Do not infer a death beyond the printed `ヘイローを、壊された` or identify the attacker. E001 Seia's dramatic discourse was already read as Seia-associated and typographically ambiguous; this new account does not settle its scene chronology or ontology.

## 3. Azusa's transfer as Mika's admitted procedural breach

Mika says **she** brought Azusa from Arius into Trinity without Nagisa's knowledge by forging roster and related papers (`u:0067-0070`). The confession supplies a concrete actor account for the false-background component of E017's accusation, but no original documents or independent admission from Azusa are shown. It is not, by itself, proof Azusa knowingly betrayed anyone; Mika says Azusa was drawn unwittingly into politics. Mika describes Arius as still resentful, materially deprived and refusing overtures from Trinity/GSC, then says she wanted reconciliation before the treaty closed the chance (`u:0071-0084`). Those conditions, refused offers and treaty irreversibility are her reports/forecasts. Her own admitted limited knowledge of Azusa (`u:0080`) constrains her assertion that Azusa was unaware of the larger struggle—especially after E017's unlocated “plan” line. Mika wanted Azusa as a **symbol** that an Arius student could live happily in Trinity, a benevolent hope that still instrumentalizes the student without a represented Azusa consent process.

Mika says Nagisa and Seia opposed her approach on political grounds and that she doubted Nagisa would listen (`u:0076-0083`). She does not show an attempted formal proposal, so their reasons and the necessity of forgery are unverified. She says she does not know why Nagisa came to suspect a traitor and might herself have made a mistake (`u:0085-0088`). This marks a real information limit; her later reconstruction of Nagisa's club-member selection is not Nagisa's internal record.

## 4. Four-member selection as Mika's interested causal map

Mika says all four are objects of Nagisa's suspicion (`u:0088-0089`), then differentiates them:

- **Hanako:** Mika calls her exceptionally able, reports former Tea Party candidacy and Sisterhood recruitment, recalls personally seeing a chapel swimsuit incident, and connects her sudden grade collapse plus upper-echelon knowledge to suspicion (`u:0090-0096`). Her chapel memory is direct actor testimony; candidacy/recruitment/secrets are reports. E016's Hifumi papers independently *as a different actor report* support prior academic brilliance, not the cause of current 2/4 or treachery.
- **Koharu:** Mika says she is unrelated to politics herself but would be a **hostage** against an uncontrolled, heavily armed Justice Realization Committee and especially anti-Gehenna Hasumi (`u:0097-0103`). `と思う`/`多分` mark her inference; she says Hasumi “probably” knew about expulsion. E015's opaque private Hasumi talk cannot be filled with this guess.
- **Hifumi:** Mika says Nagisa likes her but came to suspect forbidden Black Market visits and links to a “criminal group” (`scene:002:u:0012-0020`). Prior V001 C001 readings independently establish Hifumi's Black Market presence with Abydos and her Masked Swimsuit Gang participation; that context can explain a report without proving a malicious conspiracy, a school-rule violation in this episode, or Nagisa's precise intelligence. Sensei's silence is not assent to Mika's suspicion.
- **Azusa:** Mika again says the forged Arius-origin background makes her the answer to Nagisa's `経歴を偽って`/spy suspicion (`u:0022-0028`). Because Mika claims authorship of the forgery, “false background” and “Azusa personally plotted treaty sabotage” are not interchangeable.

Mika theorizes Nagisa shifted from “perhaps a traitor exists” to “which of these is the traitor?” (`u:0018-0021`). This is an illuminating accusation of confirmation bias but remains Mika's model of Nagisa. She calls herself also a “traitor” by anti-treaty politics, then reverses the label onto Nagisa for making Trinity a `巨大な怪物（リヴァイアサン）` (`u:0029-0036`). These are **competing definitions of betrayal**, not three proven culprits. She explicitly calls the account one-sided (`u:0036`), but frames the teacher's choice as Azusa protection versus finding a traitor, or trusting Mika versus Nagisa (`u:0037`). These alternatives may be falsely exhaustive; neither Sensei choice accepts the frame or decides between them.
