## 1. Chapter proposition and adversarial qualification

> **Arius attacks the treaty gathering and wounds students and Sensei; a distributed set of Trinity, Gehenna, Abydos, Sisterhood, Rescue Knight and remedial-student actions keeps people moving and eventually disrupts Arius's immediate Justina-centered control. Azusa's separate-killer premise is challenged by Hifumi and her friends, and Atsuko explicitly identifies Squad hatred as learned rather than inescapably their own. Sensei survives, wakes and later uses an “adult card” in a Maestro encounter. Yet neither Arius's asserted ETO clause nor Sensei's rival ETO claim is independently validated as constitutional authority; the card's cost/effect is not audited; the Squad escapes the search and an unidentified actor orders a renewed lethal pursuit. The ending creates room for trust and conversation without certifying a signed-peace regime, universal safety or repaired institutions.**

Three reductions fail against the record. “Sensei saved everyone” erases student plans, medical work, cross-school offers and rescue choices, while Sensei is critically wounded and absent from large portions of the response. “The treaty is valid/invalid because the mimesis changes” promotes Saori, Maestro and Seia's theories into an inspected instrument that the chapter never supplies. “Azusa is naturally a killer” contradicts her formal Trinity-student declaration, ordinary learning, Hifumi's direct bridge, her own later refusal and Atsuko's testimony about taught hatred. Conversely, friendship and trust do not retroactively make the explosive tactics harmless, nullify Arius coercion or immunize the escaped Squad from the closing order.
