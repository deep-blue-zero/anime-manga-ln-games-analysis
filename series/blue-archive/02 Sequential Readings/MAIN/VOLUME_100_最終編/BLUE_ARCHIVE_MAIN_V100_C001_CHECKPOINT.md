The chapter moves from ominous but unverified claims through real institutional action and coercion. Seia trades her future sight in a Kuzunoha daydream and later reports it gone. Kaiser reports a desert finding; federal sensors show six unexplained concentrations; Rin calls a cross-district committee. ## 1. Prospective sequence and revisions

E001's unidentified voice says the story is overturned but shows no transformed world or identified speaker. In E002 Kuzunoha names Color in a Seia daydream, offers escape from exposure for future sight, and Seia accepts; her later self-report supports loss of sight, not a measured rite mechanism. Beatrice admits signaling Color and predicts approach, while Gematria's fleeting ark observation and priest discussion remain participant accounts. Sensei wonders whether an anonymous warning voice is Shiroko, without confirmation. Seia's fatal dream lacks a date; Rin begins evidence-gated records and roster checks. E003's Kaiser PMC reports an approximately 135×23×13 m desert structure and orders notices, not an object analysis. Abydos sees unusual PMC movement and plans Sensei contact. These units supply leads rather than one unified mechanism.

### E004–E006: local social fractures and federal escalation

Sakurako finds regalia she identifies as the last Justina president's in Arius, claims succession, and sees an **unidentified second thing**. Her historical account and guessed motive are not archival proof. A three-session hearing removes Mika's Tea Party powers and privileges, retains Patar representative authority until a successor is chosen, ends confinement and permits academic return while requesting wider restraint. Protesters call that lenient; Azusa refuses mob hostility, a protester pushes Airi and a fight begins, with Justice intervening but no disposition printed. The Perorozilla search instead reveals a shop object Azusa/Hifumi call Perorosaurus, without purchase or theft (E004).

Rabbit Squad removes a hornet nest for a merchant in a redeveloping town losing services and receives fresh food; Saki/Moe claim independence from Sensei while Miyu wishes for contact. Kaya receives an opaque call. At Gematria, Beatrice declares a Color-linked world-power/revenge aim; Black Suit expels her and Golconde attacks with an unspecified prepared measure. A vacant seat is discussed, but her final fate and the measure's ability against Color are unproved (E005). Rin dreams of the missing president calling her `リンちゃん`, then wakes to Momoka's report of six intense sensor readings with no camera counterpart. Machine fault remains possible. Rin declares a committee and summons district heads; Aoi challenges her mandate and jurisdiction while urging rest, and Kaya offers to arrange Schale attendance/Valkyrie escort before an unheard private instruction (E006). The dream is not a present president sighting; E005/E006 calls remain unattributed despite later revealed Kaya–Kaiser collaboration.

### E007–E010: actual abduction, Shiroko's disappearance precursor and coup

Invitations are delivered and Arona passes Rin's brief to Sensei. District reactions differ and several labels self-address. Two escorts presenting as Valkyrie are Kaiser PMC; Sensei notices their fiscal claim conflicts with Kirino's prior account, recognizes them and is secured. Kaya directly collaborates with Kaiser General and expects Schale dissolution, but he rejects that as **her** goal, reveals a corporate takeover plan and turns on her. A Kaiser President claims a desert `オーパーツ` makes defense-office/Gematria alliance unnecessary; its function is not shown. He orders council seizure; General orders Kaya to a PMC prison and Sensei hidden and shot in the leg, with those latter orders unfulfilled in E007 (E007).

Hoshino/Nonomi say Shiroko lacks memory from before Abydos, without cause or prior identity. Shiroko observes mass PMC traffic toward the desert, detours, reports ground movement and a light she hesitantly describes as black/rainbow. PMC attempts to shoot Sensei fail; Arona says she protects him, then senses an approach as he perceives Chest power loss and headache. Abydos cannot reach either Shiroko or Sensei, with a brief silent Shiroko connection (E008). No wound or Shiroko fate is shown there.

Sensei wakes caged at a Kaiser-held Valkyrie site; guards report the Chest inert and an eight-firm shutdown from a data-center connection, not independently inspected. Injured Kanna frees him, Kirino/Fubuki help, and she reports the General seized Sanctum Tower and imposed D.U. martial law six hours earlier. Her ensuing account depicts the **earlier** committee convening without Sensei, breaking into disputes and walkouts; Aoi cites finance ledgers for anonymous PMC purchases, then presents a six-member no-confidence majority and effective suspension order. Her allegation that Rin orchestrated the crisis or president's disappearance is not proved (E009). E010 shows the assault after staff disperse: a soldier reports tower control, the President says full authority acquisition takes more time and orders martial law, and the General declares citywide shutdowns. Kanna returns Sensei's seized Chest and phone, credits his teaching, and cannot continue far while wounded. Sensei recites the seven-lament/Jericho words; Arona reconnects and confirms a temporary outage, without a technical mechanism. E009's present rescue frame and E009–E010's retrospective must remain distinct.

### E011–E013: a local route through the shutdown

An unprinted fight bracketed by E011's two scenes lets the group survive locally, while Fubuki says numbers, support and supplies remain inferior. E013's narration confirms escape from the PMC base to Rabbit Park. Arona describes a no-confidence/martial-law governance loop and identifies Schale's underground Craft Chamber, a president-left matter generator, as the apparent tower-control route through an unspecified Kaiser authentication bypass. Rin is reportedly confined at Schale. Kaiser General orders SOF to defend, with “fox” referent unassigned in his line. Public Security students join despite absent orders; Sensei explicitly leaves field command to Miyako and promises support. The team secures a Schale first-floor foothold and meets Sora. Momoka/Ayumu recover tower communication logs identifying an unused northern Schale residence room as Rin's location. Nothing in E013 yet rescinds martial law or rescues Rin.

Black Suit says the six readings are **not yet known** to be Color; he distinguishes Kaiser's Abydos OOPArt from Gematria's ark and predicts Kaiser President cannot control it. A voice says Golconde is gone and self-names Francis; treat one qualified continuity/alias subject. Full administrative restoration, martial-law repeal, the basement object's identity, Shiroko's location and any cosmic mechanism remain open (E015).

- The six sensor signatures remain **unidentified** at the chapter close. Momoka's instrument report, Shiroko's light, Kaiser desert find, Gematria ark interest, Beatrice's Color forecast and Black Suit's Anubis inference are related narrative leads but not interchangeable physical observations. Black Suit expressly says the six are not yet known to be Color and says the Kaiser artifact is not Gematria's ark; both statements remain attributed, not instrument-tested.
- Kaiser's physical takeover and a martial-law regime are supported by direct council intrusion, a tower-control report, President/General orders and Kanna/Arona's later conditions. The President explicitly says full authority acquisition takes time. The Chamber's role and bypass are Arona's account; Miyako's later `確保` does not alone prove control restored. Momoka's intervention plus Arona's message flood confirm communication return, not every administrative or legal reversal.

Rabbit's lack of formal command and martial-law notice is self-report, not exemption.
