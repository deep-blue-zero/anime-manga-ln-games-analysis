Himari/Chihiro say the structure is about 75,000 m high and currently beyond ordinary attack; Himari cannot inspect the interior through an unexplained membrane. She proposes an Eridu weapon Rio prepared against an aerial threat and asks Chihiro to hack it while Engineering helps. Chihiro identifies it as a missile and agrees, though reluctantly (`scene:001:u:0001-0016`). The launch/approach is represented by Engineering's distance calls. Kotori asks whether it hit; Utaha says it passed the target and distances grow afterward (`u:0017-0020`; `scene:002:u:0002-0005`). This is a local **failed contact test**, not proof that no physical object could ever interact with the structure. Himari/Utaha infer physical noninteraction; Yuuka questions the missile's provenance. Himari criticizes absent Rio and says no further method currently, without establishing Rio's present location or all possible defenses (`u:0006-0026`).

Hanako says Sisterhood clergy, old-library staff and Tea Party combined information with Millennium data to propose an **untested hypothesis**. She describes the new towers and overhead structure as a probabilistic coexistence of states across dimensions; she expressly uses quantum/many-worlds language as a close analogy, not a literal verified physical derivation (`scene:002:u:0027-0052`). She suggests only the outer membrane may be in this state, because an unstable interior would not transfer energy. Himari recasts it as a barrier; both propose that breaking it might permit a body attack (`u:0053-0060`). This is a conditional mechanism that fits the missile observation, not proven ontology or a validated barrier design.

Himari proposes entering the same state by matching a vibration pattern; Hanako elaborates and Kayoko likens it to noise cancellation. Himari says calculating that pattern is currently impossible without a quantum computer, so this route is a **conditional plan blocked by a named computational requirement** (`u:0061-0077`). Hanako expects repeated Sanctums unless the putative source is stopped, but generation and inexhaustibility are still hypotheses. Yuuka's thermodynamics objection and Kayoko's “anything goes” gloss are reactions, not scientific proofs (`u:0078-0092`).

Black Suit separately approaches Sensei after others notice him absent. He says he knows a way to reach Plenapates, probes what price Sensei might pay, jokingly proposes Gematria membership and then retracts it because the group is dissolved (`u:0093-0103`; `choice:001-003`). No actual bargain or accession is recorded. He warns using the unnamed means could irreversibly damage Sensei's body and even kill him; Sensei inwardly accepts the risk (`u:0104-0111`). Black Suit finally says the method is **in Abydos**, without naming, producing or testing it (`u:0112`). The next card points to `アビドスに在るもの（１）` (`u:0113`). Black Suit's risk estimate is consequential advice but remains his claim; Sensei's assent is direct willingness, not actual use or injury.
