Francis plans a new Gematria move. Plana privately says she has solved the difference between the two OSs and addresses the hidden president, without printing her derivation or an independent response. **The world is shown in repair and the principal immediate threat is interrupted, while several fate, governance and identity questions remain open.**

## 1. Sequential synthesis

### E001–E004: counterpart claims and a coalition link cut

Control-room A.R.O.N.A. identifies local and hostile Shiroko as other-time-axis counterparts, and familiar Arona's biometric check supports Prenapates as another Sensei counterpart. The hostile Shiroko admits local Shiroko's abduction and claims she killed her own Sensei, but her one-Shiroko-per-world and fixed-doom rhetoric is not confirmed as a law. The timer continues while forced disconnection fails. Rio uses multidimensional suppression to hold the count at a repeating nine seconds; she describes it as temporary and explicitly asks for help. Hanako maps simultaneous east/west release cuts to a lower-terminal window. Toki independently holds a newly appearing site while Fuuka drives Gourmet despite protesting Haruna's manipulation. Nonomi/Abydos and Alice/Yuzu/Game Development report the two releases destroyed; Akari/Junko's lower strike follows. Chihiro/Kayoko report link severance at one second, with Rio/crew corroborating a local suppression success. The exact whole-Ark state and all ground sites are still unverified. With ship systems back, Himari starts resetting controls and plans an Ark self-destruct. Toki asks Rio for a suit self-destruct code because she cannot move and imagines one life should save others. Rio denies a code, rejects the isolated trolley framing, and C&C seniors arrive; E006 later reports Toki escorted and the temporary Sanctum canceled. The control-room OS reports Ark self-destruct preparing and predicts extremely low ship survival **if it detonates**, a forecast rather than an observed casualty. Gourmet, Abydos and Game Development head for Sensei rather than escape ahead of him. Sensei and Prenapates display adult cards; the text does not print their activation result. E007 abruptly moves to an escape-sequence alarm, zero uses remaining, an OS report of explosion protection but inability to slow a teacher's fall, and the two OSs' proposed joint rescue. Its final scene is only a video marker without admitted visual content. E008 supplies the follow-up report that present Sensei safely reached students, while omitting landing, injury and reunion detail. Local Shiroko gives the alternate an **unnamed** precious Foreclosure Task Force keepsake, contests her one-per-world rule, and leaves long-term coexistence untested. Familiar Arona invites the other OS home, names her Plana and receives acceptance. [E005](BLUE_ARCHIVE_MAIN_V100_C004_E005_DEEP_READING.md)–[E008](BLUE_ARCHIVE_MAIN_V100_C004_E008_DEEP_READING.md).

Rin's supporters stand by her before a still-future reconfidence vote. Niya reports the ninjas returned but did **not** verify Kuzunoha; a self-attributed letter asserts no Color reversal and invites Sensei.
