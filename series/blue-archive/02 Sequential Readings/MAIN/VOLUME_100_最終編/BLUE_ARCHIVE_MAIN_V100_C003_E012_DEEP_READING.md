Rio orders the battleship slowed because the changed multidimensional barrier is ahead. She predicts collision while unmatched would either absorb the vessel into an unknown dimension or shatter it; Ayane says slowing alone cannot avert contact, and a crew voice counts six minutes (`scene:001:u:0001-0017`). The countdown and Ayane's navigation report establish acute danger, but Rio's binary outcome is a forecast. Rio calls the barrier conceptual and proposes that only an identical Ark can intervene in its copied concept (`u:0007-0011`). Her “only one method” statement is a constrained expert proposal, not an exhaustive proof. Yuuka objects to the apparent further risk before Alice says she understands; Rio's role is an AMAS/drone presence, with Momoi explicitly questioning why she sent a drone rather than appeared in person (`u:0014-0023`).

Alice identifies herself as the Nameless Gods' princess, Game Development Club member, Millennium and Schale student, `魔王` and `勇者`, and the Alice she wants to be (`u:0024-0043`). This is a **self-definition that joins** the assigned origin and chosen affiliations; it does not erase her origin or turn every self-description into equal verified institutional membership. She asks Kei to use the Ark inside her to address this crisis, hoping a weapon assigned to destroy the world can instead save it (`u:0044-0056`). Yuzu and the club voice concern about repeating a prior danger, while Sensei asks everyone to settle and inwardly chooses to hear Alice out (`choice:001`; `u:0028-0038`). He does not order Alice to activate.

Kei refuses at first. Kei claims Alice survived Utnapishtim's startup because the adult took all the recompense and wounds, and predicts Sensei will soon die under that injury; generating an Ark again would endanger Alice (`u:0057-0067`). The risk claim is highly salient and consistent with Sensei's acute E010 symptoms, but E012 does **not** show medical confirmation, actual death, a quantified causal transfer, or Alice's injury outcome. The u0065-0068 Alice/Kei tags drift across the exchange; the warning content and refusal are clearer than exact ownership of every short line.

In an italic exchange, Alice apologizes for refusing and fearing Kei, says she should have faced someone she could not understand, and **imagines** the pain of being assigned a world-destroying key role (`u:0069-0077`). She offers Kei Sensei's principle that one may choose what to become and need not seek permission; she values `ケイ` as an accidental name like `アリス`, with no prescribed special purpose (`u:0078-0083`). Kei asks whether Alice wants to help even Kei; Alice says she does not know whether she can, as an apprentice hero, but believes wanting to help someone is the qualification of a hero (`u:0084-0089`). Alice's empathy is expressly an attempt to take Kei's perspective, **not** mind access proving Kei's internal pain.

Alice initiates activation, reports `AL-1S` ready and `ATRAHASIS` execution, and tells worried peers she remains Alice (`u:0090-0106`). Rio rejects a “subjugation” reading; Alice says Kei listened to her request, which supports cooperation rather than coerced obedience, though the full internal protocol remains unaudited (`u:0104-0107`). Sensei inwardly chooses to trust Alice (`u:0100`). Alice says she still needs everyone and Rio, addresses her as `リオ先輩`, asks to see her face, and observes she looks thinner; this supports an apparently visible Rio interface/appearance, not her physical presence aboard (`u:0108-0118`). Rio starts a self-incriminating account of her earlier choice against Alice, while Alice first says Rio need not apologize, then says forgiveness is unnecessary because comrades can understand each other. Alice calls Rio a comrade based on her present help and thanks her (`u:0119-0134`). The exchange is **Alice's offered relationship**, not formal absolution, complete accountability, or proof that Rio accepts an uncomplicated forgiveness. Rio remains hesitant and unfinished.

Alice announces her turn with club encouragement.
