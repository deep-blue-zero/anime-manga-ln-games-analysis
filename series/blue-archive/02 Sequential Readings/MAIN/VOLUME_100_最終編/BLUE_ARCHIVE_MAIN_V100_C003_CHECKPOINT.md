The chapter moves from a renewed sky crisis to an actual attempt against the high-altitude Atrahasis Ark. A Kaiser-held ancient Utnapishtim ship is discovered, staffed by a multi-school coalition and launched through Sensei's Shittim Chest. Alice asks Kei to activate an Ark protocol through her; her transformed Light Sword breaks the enemy's multidimensional barrier, and the damaged ship penetrates its outer wall. The first defensive wave is repelled. An ominous Shiroko-labelled figure intrudes, and by chapter end an enemy backdoor has seized Utnapishtim. The planned self-destruct fires prematurely with only a limited blast, while an A.R.O.N.A.-named control-room voice announces both Ark restoration and a future final ship detonation. Sensei and another Shiroko-labelled companion reach the traced hack site and confront Prenapates, but do not stop the hack on page. **Barrier breach and entry are achieved; occupation, Ark destruction, rescue, return and world safety are not.**

## 1. Sequential synthesis

### E001–E004: the target and a risky ship lead

Nagusa gives the ninjas an unopened scroll for Sensei after reporting Twilight Temple destroyed and Ayame inaccessible. A Shiroko-tagged speaker claims fixed fate, self-directed Color use and asks Sensei to leave; her identity relation to Abydos Shiroko remains open. ### E005–E009: discovery, remote Rio and coalition decision

Abydos enters contested Kaiser territory, encounters a guard and finds a deep machine interior Himari identifies as a battleship. Veritas/Engineering inspect but initially cannot control it; measurements and calculations make it a flying computation device in Himari's estimate. The small AMAS is strongly revealed as Rio's remote interface, not evidence of her bodily presence on ship. Himari challenges Rio's prior conduct and requests calculation help under present safeguards. Rin briefs an occupation/destruction plan with unvalidated membrane matching, a low simulation probability and time pressure; Sensei takes overall lead while Rin shares risk. Alice says Kei remains within her and decides to join as her chosen Alice/hero/club self, with the whole Game Development Club. Other schools divide ship and ground duties. Aoi apologizes to Rin, who says she has no resentment, without legal no-confidence reversal. E009 ends at departure readiness, not flight. ### E010–E013: startup cost, choice, and successful barrier breach

The launch roster names ship operators, ground support, Ark assault and food teams; Fuuka protests her placement. Arona says the Chest can power Utnapishtim but cannot quantify bodily cost to Sensei. A remembered federal-president saying completes Rin's second ancient question, while Rin defers her answer. Sensei connects the Chest, Utnapishtim launches to 780 m and the multidimensional system starts; he suffers shock, pallor, shaking and fading consciousness. These are acute symptoms, not observed irreversible injury. An italic president-address scene then appeals for adult choice and preserved bonds; its time, location and physical status are unestablished. A ship/Ark mismatch follows; Rio hypothesizes an inaccessible new dimensional axis, not independently verified physics.

Facing a reported collision countdown, Rio proposes an identical Ark as the one intervention she sees. Alice joins assigned princess origin with self-chosen student, club, `魔王` and `勇者` identities, asks Kei to help and extends to Kei the right to choose an identity. Kei warns Sensei took startup wounds and predicts his death; this is a grave claim without a medical verdict. Kei begins protocol ATRAHASIS at Alice's request, which Rio distinguishes from subjugation. Alice offers Rio `仲間` status under current help, not formal absolution. The protocol transforms Utnapishtim data into a huge Light Sword. Alice fires; Hanako/Kayoko report barrier shattering and final-function stop. Alice then loses consciousness, with peers planning infirmary observation. Ayane rushes through the disabled barrier and impacts the Ark wall; Ayumu reports actual interior entry. An italic Kei speech proposes disappearing in Alice's stead, but no independent deletion or permanence check follows. [E010](BLUE_ARCHIVE_MAIN_V100_C003_E010_DEEP_READING.md)–[E013](BLUE_ARCHIVE_MAIN_V100_C003_E013_DEEP_READING.md).

### E014–E016: stranded defense, Shiroko and control reversal

Ayumu clarifies that Utnapishtim is only half embedded in the Ark wall. Engine, main control, communications and computation are damaged despite the multidimensional system remaining active. Sanctum-like foes close in; Abydos, Game Development and Gourmet take defensive roles while Sensei inwardly accepts requested overall command. Fuuka again protests her involvement. Ayumu reports the first attack repelled, then a Shiroko-labelled intruder appears around a blast/grenade alarm and disappears. The first blast's initiator, exact identity and movement mechanism are not shown. Engineering begins repair analysis and hopes for at least restart. Rio proposes a possibly one-use ground-return jump if Ark computation can be captured; Himari thanks the backup while retaining criticism of Rio's past distrust. Ayumu announces Ark occupation as the next objective.

E016 begins after some unshown occupation advance. Veritas has prepared a simulated Ark-destroying self-destruct pending evacuation. Rio/Himari find Utnapishtim hacked; an early self-destruct sequence causes a limited blast. Himari reports enemy backdoor ownership of the ship, with Yuuka inferring a prior physical intrusion. Utnapishtim's dimensional computation is said to aid the Ark, and ground observers see the sky change. The number and operating status of any re-created false Sanctums are unconfirmed. Veritas traces hacking to Ark Area 4, `ナラム・シンの玉座`. Abydos fears the obstructing “Shiroko” only bought time, but another Shiroko-labelled speaker with Sensei says they are going to stop the hack. She identifies Prenapates as her abductor and threatens him; her close-range shots miss. Sensei warns her back. An A.R.O.N.A.-named voice authenticates Sensei from a `シッテムの箱` and announces replacement/repair of the Ark in roughly 30 minutes and Utnapishtim final self-destruct in 800 seconds. Neither timed outcome occurs in the chapter. The end card explicitly continues in Chapter 4.
