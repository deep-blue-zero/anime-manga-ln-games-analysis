## 1. Sequential synthesis

### E001–E005: explanation and coordination

E001 shows the tower strike, administrative paralysis, public evacuation warning and Sensei's adult-card gesture. Black Suit claims Color invaded, Gematria fell, six inverted towers appeared and a guide named Plenapates exists. His wolf-god/Anubis account is an interpretation; he neither shows the guide nor names Shiroko. In E002, a flashback directly shows Shiroko knew only her name on first arriving at Abydos and Hoshino offered care. This strengthens a bounded memory-history point, without proving origin or relation to current disappearance. Rin convenes a six-tower meeting; Himari gives five guardian/site names, the sixth guardian remains unknown, and Momoka insists on a parallel civilian-defense plan. Forecasts of signal effectiveness and time remaining are uncertain and not harmonized into one exact clock.

E003 assembles Ayane/PS68 for a Binah rail decoy, Eimi/Trinity/C&C for Chesed factory entry and Yuzu/Hina/Alice/Rabbit for the Slumpia route. E004 adds Hanako's fourth-site coalition, Rio/Eridu cipher bottleneck at Hod, Koyuki's contested past and D.U. shelter feeding. Niya and Kaho send the ninjas covertly toward an inferred snowfield temple to find Hyakka's missing chair and a possible Kuzunoha route; registry absence, folklore and former-chair accounts do not establish contact or cure. Makoto overhears and orders an unspecified preparation. E005 brings Rin's six-route standby report and Sensei's accepted command of the overall offense/defense. These are mobilization and plan states, not guardian defeats.

### E006–E012: separate fronts begin

### E013–E018: rescue, rear guard and unresolved operators

Trinity opens old-library shelter and coordinates Justice, Sweets and Tea Party defense. Shimiko's collapse-resistance estimate, a reported mimesis weak point and an L118 artillery plan are not an audited shelter or firing result (E013). Seia's hedged warning of missed evacuees is locally corroborated by Mashiro finding people near ruins. Mika reaches scratched Koharu amid hostile cries; Koharu says Mika helped and Hasumi thanks her for that bounded act, without general reinstatement, full evacuation or enemy defeat (E014). Hanako faces one current approach and thin rear security at the fourth site; Arius Squad offers help, Saori expects distrust and Hanako assigns rear guard based on Sensei's summons and an unnamed shared friend. Sakurako states an anti-hatred resolve in ceremonial wear she describes, but the costume exchange has severe tag drift. The basilica attack begins without result (E015).

At Millennium, AMAS appears to help an overloaded Utaha/Sumire/Hibiki team. Hare thinks only Himari could hack it, Himari denies doing so, and Kotori's ghost idea is not identification (E016). Koyuki/Noa report Rio's cipher solved, but the Hod surface lure and barrier dismantling remain assignments. Hot Spring Club actually demolishes road/building infrastructure near the fifth site for its own hot-spring priority, while Hare/Chihiro worry about damage and Kotama challenges geothermal prospects; no pillar-removal or guardian outcome is printed (E017). The ninjas are then encircled on the supposedly lower-risk snowfield. Makoto/Iroha arrive by airship and deploy Toramaru; Iroha says enemies are mostly cleared and the ninjas report no injury. Makoto says she acted because Ibuki values them and she wanted to repair a pudding slight, partly supported by Ibuki's help request. Attacker origin, Makoto's prior knowledge path and chair/Kuzunoha contact remain unknown (E018).

### E019–E021: D.U. defense and chapter transition

Himari forecasts an unusually dangerous sixth guardian from detected energy but says its identity is still unknown; she reinforces D.U. defense/recon rather than rushing an assault. Gourmet Research and Hifumi/Azusa join Shiratori defense. Five masked Kaitenger role labels appear; Red later reports local clearance, without shown target, casualty count or district-wide safety proof. Toki finishes one assignment and takes another. Himari says dismantled Eridu removes Abi-Eshuh's former city-scale basis, although she has modified the remaining suit; her appraisal of Rio's prior plan and her theory of Toki's guilt are conjectures. Toki says she is used to fighting alone, not that she feels guilty or wants C&C reunion (E019).

Council speakers report first-five site preparations and all district-defense battles complete, then Momoka reports a Schale perimeter attack. A Valkyrie student takes the front; delinquents, named Helmet Gang officer Love and Wakamo volunteer or pledge protection. No fight outcome is printed. Rin says a countdown is imminent and Sensei assents to her signal (E020). In E021, Momoka/Rin observe the sky return to normal; Sensei asks inwardly `やったのかな……？` and `あれは……シロコ？`. A Shiroko-tagged figure is silent, followed by the Chapter 3 card. The intervening assaults, sixth guardian and Schale-defense result are not displayed, and the sighting's significance is unresolved.
