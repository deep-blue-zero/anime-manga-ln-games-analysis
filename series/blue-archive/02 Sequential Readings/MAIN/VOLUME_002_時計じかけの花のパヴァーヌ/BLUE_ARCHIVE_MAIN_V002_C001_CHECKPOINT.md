## 1. Chapter thesis and its limits

The chapter's strongest positive proposition is:

> **A game becomes consequential through labor and relations among makers, players and evaluators, not through a secret algorithm or an award alone. Alice's appreciative play and Yuzu's risk of public exposure help the club continue making; the judges can translate that lived value into an exceptional form of “utility.” Yet recognition only temporarily protects a shared room. Creative worth, jury approval and secure institutional belonging remain different things.**

This is not a claim that love always makes good games, that all criticism is wrong, or that the council permanently accepts the club. `G.Bible` displays `ゲームを愛しなさい` and denies its own error, but does not show a production method; `TSC2` is made and released through subsequent work. Its `特別賞` is real and not a ranked top-seven prize. Yuuka explicitly calls the council response `臨時の猶予`, deferring confiscation/dissolution until next term with paperwork still needed.

A second, unresolved chapter question is whether Alice can belong as a student/person rather than merely as a credential, game character, useful player, decoy or weapon. The club feeds, names, plays with, arms, exposes, rescues and wants to keep her; Yuuka verifies willing membership but her school identity/origin remain incomplete. The final unattributed `Divi:Sion`/`AL-1S` restoration text threatens no automatic reversal of her lived relationships. It is audience information, not Alice's recovered memory or a named agent's confession.

## 2. Prospective narrative architecture

### E001–E004: petition, results gate and an unknown person

The club appeals to Sensei after failure, not because a hero can instantaneously generate a successful game. Yuuka states the threatened dissolution and a Millennium Prize route under a finite deadline. A proposed secret becomes a dangerous ruins expedition. Alice is found where a gate/system responds to a relation the group cannot explain. Immediate care precedes full identification, but the club also imagines her as a membership solution. Rescue and instrumental recruitment begin entangled, not cleanly separated.

### E005–E009: playing together becomes membership evidence

The club gives Alice game language and a school-facing identity while her credential provenance remains suspect. Alice's play reveals enjoyment and blindness in the makers; Yuzu's hidden listening and later emergence make the room an affective social space, not just a storage location. A chosen weapon and an engineer's possible combat-design inference introduce force without settling origin. Yuuka checks whether Alice wants to be there, not merely whether a headcount satisfies a rule. Recognition is real but time-limited and results-conditioned; Yuzu moves from concealed supporter toward explicit shared risk.

### E010–E017: a hoped-for shortcut generates an ethically mixed operation

The renewed factory expedition puts Sensei at risk and leads to a `Divi:Sion System` terminal. It recognizes Alice as `AL-1S` while declining to answer her origin question, transfers a password-locked `G.Bible.exe` and reports Momoi's saves deleted. Veritas appraises file provenance and limits, then the coalition seeks seized `鏡` as a bypass. E013's plan deliberately uses Alice as a detained decoy; council/C&C defenders have a real security assignment, while the club's dependence on the file does not automatically license the breach.

The operation succeeds tactically in bounded ways but repeatedly exceeds forecasts. Karin's fire, Akane's escape, Asuna's intervention and Yuuka's reserves undermine clean victory claims. Yuuka threatens week-long student sanctions and a Schale complaint; their issuance is not shown. Alice escapes on her own cue and rejoins the twins. The E015 beam's shooter is strongly contextual but unlabeled; E019 later provides an explicit Alice shot, not retrospective proof for every earlier firing line. Alice holds Mirror in E016 and names first fear near Nel; Yuzu uses a false Seminar/berserk-robot story to protect the hiding party. Nel's response to apparent danger and Yuzu's enacted rescue coexist with the deception's costs.

E017 returns the party to its room, with Mirror reportedly returned and Bible opened by Maki. A separate `<Key>` folder remains undeciphered; the route/bypass mechanics and Rio/Himari intentions remain unverified. Nel reports Rio withdrew C&C's assignment yet commissions further inquiry independently. The chapter does not narrate the complete exit battle or settle accountability.

### E018–E020: from maxim to audience to exceptional prize

Bible's lone displayed answer is `ゲームを愛しなさい`. Alice's repeated-play account counters the club's total self-negation without proving market quality. Yuzu recounts earlier hostile reception, the sisters' praise and collaborative beginning; the six-month flashback makes her fear and desire for an audience intelligible. Momoi initiates `TSC2`, then E019 shows registration and separate public web release. First comments are mixed and mostly pre-play. Alice's proposed beam retaliation against a critic is directly stopped by Midori. Incoming fire prompts evacuation to spare Sensei and the room; Nel's test of Alice produces a destructive floor shot and Alice's 48% self-reported harm. Nel survives and stops pursuit. Her boasts, her interpretation of Rio and later C&C romantic teasing are not objective findings.

E020 starts with Midori's view that Alice's body has repaired but maid imagery still frightens her. Yuzu says C&C had building damage processed as a club-activity accident, not that all harm has disappeared. The club misreads exclusion from the seven ranked prizes as total failure, even though Momoi articulates growth and Yuzu imagines dorm return with continued friendship. Sensei's Schale offer is internal and unacted. The judge then announces a special prize for remembered play and possibility; Yuuka supplies a conditional council deferral and an apology for `ガラクタ`. Their home is kept for now, not made secure forever.

## 3. Literary mechanism: failed shortcuts, real work, different audiences

The secret-search promise, robot/terminal, locked file, Mirror bypass and one-sentence Bible repeatedly convert technical access into an apparent creative answer. Each level yields something real—source file, access, maxim—but never the guaranteed method Momoi imagined. The answer is not a simple anti-technology moral: tools, code, Veritas skill, engineering equipment and web distribution matter to the outcome. What fails is the inference that possession of a correct artifact eliminates the human tasks of making, testing, receiving criticism and repairing relationships.

Audience is plural. Alice enjoys the old game as a companion journey. Yuzu's past hostile commenters wounded her; the sisters' praise sustained her. E019's first web comments are mostly anticipatory and mixed. E020's selected high-approval comments, in-scene traffic report, judge and Yuuka offer stronger reception but different evidentiary scopes. None stands for all players. The judge does not abolish `実用性`; an exceptional ruling expands what can count as useful, while council enforcement remains conditional.

The physical room is also plural: workshop, refuge, hideout, potential site of battle damage and governed school resource. Momoi's E019 decision not to fight there protects both Sensei and place; E020's imagined loss makes Yuzu's dorm and Alice's uncertain placement emotionally concrete. Love of games alone does not confer title to the room; a special prize changes an institution's decision temporarily.

## 6. Institutional and ethical reconciliation

Millennium governance is not merely the Abydos rescue model in new clothes. Yuuka's results requirement is a rule with incentives, a security rationale for seized property and enforcement capacity. Its risks are instrumental headcount, deadline pressure, risky expeditions, teacher/student disciplinary exposure and a competition where only seven ranked works receive ordinary prizes. Its virtues are a check on voluntary membership, a potentially reviewable output standard, exceptional adjudication and an apology when her earlier dismissal is disproved. The episode does not establish that the original rule was just, or that one exception solves structural precarity.

The raid is ethically mixed. The club/coalition needs a locked file and believes Mirror can help; Alice serves as decoy and later acts independently, Yuzu lies, robots and defenders are attacked, Sensei is exposed, and school property is damaged. C&C has an assignment, Nel later denies revenge and stops pursuit; Yuuka's threatened sanctions and C&C's reported accident handling are not a full accountability settlement. Aid is distributed among engineers, Veritas, Sensei and club, but pressure and proportionality remain live.

An Abydos-derived agency test applies locally only with adaptation: the club does author its game, Alice's wish to belong matters, and Yuuka's eventual change responds to the work, but there is no equivalent Kaiser-scale engineered debt capture in this chapter. **Creative legitimacy, institutional standing and safety must be evaluated separately.**
