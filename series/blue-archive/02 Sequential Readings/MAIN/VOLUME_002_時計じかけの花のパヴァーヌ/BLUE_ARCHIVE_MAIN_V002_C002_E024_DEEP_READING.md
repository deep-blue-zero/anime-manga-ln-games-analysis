## 2. Alice's self-erasure logic versus witnessed belonging

Alice says she cannot return because everyone is hurt near her (`u:0033-0042`), names herself a `魔王` instead of `勇者` and says she may have been born to destroy Kivotos (`u:0043-0047`). Her `かもしれない` registers possibility rather than demonstrated future inevitability. She wants to stay with loved ones yet concludes that, if they suffer, she should disappear (`u:0048-0051`). This is a serious self-erasure wish under fear, guilt and Key's selected display, not a freely settled consent to death or proof of a world-destroyer essence. The prior Momoi injury and current Nel injury matter to her argument; no blanket reassurance can simply negate them.

Momoi interrupts with the actual `『テイルズ・サガ・クロニクル2』` special prize and the club's continued existence (`u:0052-0063`). She attributes their making, award and survival, shared play and even a changed relation to formerly frightening Nel partly to Alice (`u:0064-0069`). C001 supports those links, but the project's full credit remains distributed across club, allies and award process; Momoi is giving counterevidence to “my presence only harms,” not establishing Alice as the sole maker or that no risk exists. Midori/Yuzu join the refusal to let her vanish (`u:0070-0079`). Yuzu says `仲間（友達）`, a direct friend/party-member address that restores the named relation Key denies. Midori recalls Alice's earlier game rule that protagonists do not abandon companions, with Sensei's secure `choice:003` completing that thought (`u:0080-0082`). The source uses the club's own prior vocabulary as evidence, not a generic motivational slogan.

Momoi then treats `魔王` as a revisable game `ジョブ`, not a birth verdict. “Who you are” is something Alice decides; she can change class to warrior, knight, mage, priest or hero (`u:0083-0089`). This does not independently prove the technical AL-1S/Key risk is null; it rejects the inference that assigned origin/function exhausts moral identity and forecloses choice. Sensei's `u:0090` wish to hear Alice's own feelings is `心の声`, not a secure spoken solicitation. Alice asks whether she may continue adventures with Momoi, Midori, Yuzu and Sensei despite believing she is a demon king (`u:0091-0104`). The broken `u:0101` text is not a separate clear proposition; `u:0102-0104` clearly state her desire. Momoi answers that wanting it is enough for their companionship; the club says a demon king can become a hero and, as game developers, they can make stories that allow it (`u:0105-0111`). This is relational permission plus creative capacity, not a unilateral ability to rewrite all danger.
