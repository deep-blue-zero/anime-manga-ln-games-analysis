## 1. Momoi's condition is serious; causation and prognosis remain bounded

Narration states Momoi collapsed and lost consciousness during the day of Alice's out-of-control incident. She was brought promptly to Schale's infirmary and had **not regained consciousness after two full days**. This is the E007 cliffhanger's first direct outcome. It is not a death report, prognosis, full injury diagnosis or proof of exactly which blast/action struck her. The narrator says unknown robots began moving at the same time as Alice's state change and the clubroom quickly became a sea of fire; C&C's arrival quieted the situation. The immediate event is over, but the story explicitly says questions and wounds remain.

Two post-incident accounts offer competing causal leads. Hare thinks Alice's behavior probably arose from “contact” with the robots. Midori says the game console Momoi held suddenly started when Alice's demeanor changed. Sensei recalls that console came from the ruins. These are Hare's hypothesis, Midori's remembered observation and Sensei's recollection—not a demonstrated trigger sequence. A `Divi:Sion System` welcome and `あなたはAL-1Sですか？` question are displayed as narration; they may connect to the ruin console/`<Key>`, but the source itself asks whether they do. The question is not proof of identity, an actual response or a direct owner of the system text. Sensei tries Momoi's console and it will not power on; the “as if empty” comparison is figurative appearance, not forensic proof of wiped storage. E017's undeciphered `<Key>` and E020's stinger remain separate unresolved evidence. No one in this scene demonstrates a successful technical inspection.

## 2. Alice's guilt is direct experience, not a complete mechanism

Midori and Yuzu report Alice has not come out of the clubroom despite repeated attempts to call her. Sensei's choice says they heard she has been shut in without eating; this is a reported concern, not a documented nutritional timeline. Midori/Yuzu say they do not know what to do. Sensei says `ここは私に任せて`, then a run of `先生（心の声）` lines asks permission/announces entry in the printed text. Because those lines are explicitly inward, do not reclassify them as secure audible permission or a verified exchange, even though the ensuing conversation takes place with Alice. The speaker/scene mapping is imperfect.

Alice says she cannot come out because Momoi was hurt “because of Alice,” adding that she did everything and does not know why it happened. Her account has moral force as guilt but is not an independently established total causal explanation. She describes something like an unfamiliar `セーブデータ` inside her: no explicit memory of those actions, yet her body reacted as if it could perform them without tutorial or after repeated play. This is her analogy for dissociated procedural familiarity and amnesia, not proof of a literal internal game save file or voluntary knowledge of ATRAHasis. She says she cannot remember what she did; her conviction that she harmed Momoi coexists with that memory gap. Sensei's printed choice urges calm; no resolution of Alice's distress is shown.

An unidentified voice then asserts Alice injured Momoi and calls that inescapable truth. Midori/Yuzu announce the president, and Rio appears, names herself `調月リオ`, says the feared outcome happened and offers to tell Sensei and the girls “the truth.” This is a strategically placed claim by the same actor who in E001 framed Alice as an existential weapon and attempted to detain Himari. The observed incident gives Rio material to point to but does not validate her entire origin/threat interpretation or prove her later account before she gives it. Her arrival cannot be treated as neutral adjudication or a completed explanation. The chapter cuts off at her promised revelation.
