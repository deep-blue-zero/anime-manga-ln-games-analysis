## 2. Alice's choice meets physical limits, risk, and a changing permission

Alice becomes visibly excited at `光の剣` and directly says she wants it. That is a specific preference under a game-shaped name, distinct from Momoi's original motive to arm her. The engineers resist because the prototype is too large and heavy for an individual: Utaha says more than 140 kg basic weight, Kotori adds optics/battery and recoil exceeding 200 kg. These are their technical specifications, not measurements performed by the analysis. Alice interprets the refusal through an RPG equip-level rule; Utaha corrects it to a material constraint. Momoi first guesses money and even offers to sell Midori's console, but Utaha says money is not the immediate obstacle. Alice asks whether the offer is sincere and then tries to lift the weapon. The narrated strain, surprised witnesses, and Alice's own `持ち上がりました` establish that she raises it unaided in this scene. They do not by themselves measure her maximal strength or identify her construction.

Hibiki warns her to stop as Alice looks for a B-button, but Alice fires indoors. The blast damages the workshop ceiling; Kotori reacts to the damage. Alice calls it impressive and chooses to equip the weapon. The physical act demonstrates unusual ability *and* an immediate safety failure: lifting/firing is not the same as controlled, authorized use. Utaha nevertheless gives permission, judging Alice uniquely able to use it; Hibiki is asked to add straps/handles and notes that field data could be useful. Kotori still objects on budget/other grounds. The gift is a decision amid competing engineering, safety and resource interests, not simply a reward for strength.

After first saying prototypes may be taken, Utaha then calls out drones/robots previously marked for disposal and frames a combat test as `資格` for this particular railgun. Scene 2 does not show Alice accepting a clearly disclosed test in advance. The practical condition is therefore imposed late, and a colleague's `倒してから` formulation conflicts with the earlier unqualified offer. Scene 3 shows the club has passed: Utaha praises the outcome, Kotori concedes, and the weapon is affirmed as Alice's. The source does not describe precise tactics, individual contributions, or damage to the drones; do not reconstruct a battle from the result. These test robots have their own disposal history and are not identified with the ruins' patrol robots.
