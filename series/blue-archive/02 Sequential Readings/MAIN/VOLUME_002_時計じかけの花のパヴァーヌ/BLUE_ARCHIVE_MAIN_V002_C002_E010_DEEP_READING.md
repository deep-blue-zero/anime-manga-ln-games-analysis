## 2. Adult responsibility is invoked to exclude the child at issue

Rio asks why Schale's adult Sensei did not act first, asserting adults are less ruled by emotion and should judge coolly. Removing a symbolic prop cannot by itself decide whether Alice is a person, student, hero or danger. It does, however, damage the exact sign through which Alice had understood her chosen role.

## 3. Alice's farewell is a pressured protective choice, not confirmation of the theory

Alice calls to Midori, Yuzu, Nel, Sensei and Momoi, asks whether everything happened because she was there and because she is a `魔王`, and says she will disappear. Her printed explanation says she does not want Sensei or the others hurt, remembers pain at Momoi's injury, still does not understand why it happened, but takes Rio's account to mean all is her fault and future presence will injure everyone. She repeats Rio's “not a life-form/not a Millennium student” premises while thanking friends for shared adventures and saying she was happy. This is direct evidence of fear, care, gratitude, self-negation and an immediate withdrawal decision. It is not independent causal confirmation, proof of voluntary protocol-state harm, or evidence of unpressured informed consent to halo destruction. The threat to her friends, restraint of would-be protectors, her uncertainty and loss of the sword all condition her decision.

Sensei's `choice:006` specifically urges Alice not to take Rio's words at face value and to talk. An earlier `心の声` says there is no need to go, but Alice appears to answer it; as with E009, this printed thought-to-response anomaly cannot be treated as secure audible speech. Sensei's explicit appeal has no visible effect before departure. Rio offers to apologize later if Sensei was hurt by her acts/words; this concerns the adult's distress rather than correcting the immediate coercion or proving her remedy sound. Narration confirms Rio and Alice leave while AMAS holds the group, then says Alice disappears from before them. The text describes removal from this scene, not proven annihilation or a completed halo action.
