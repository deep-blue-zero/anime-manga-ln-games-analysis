Her intervention rekindles purpose and acknowledges shared obligation, but `根性` (“guts”) cannot be converted into healing, an ethically settled decision to risk further injury, or a moral duty for the children to overcome fear. Midori objects to her physical condition; Momoi wants to continue but asks *how* against the suit. The episode sustains both urgency and a material safety problem rather than solving one with the other.
