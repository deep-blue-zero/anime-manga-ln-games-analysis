## 1. What Yuuka can verify, and what she chooses to value

Yuuka opens with a quiet left-eye-blink test in case the club threatened Alice into attending. Alice gives a question mark, not a clear affirmative or denial. Momoi protests and displays the ID. Yuuka says she herself checked Alice's entry on the student roster. This upgrades E006's Momoi-only roster claim to an independent observer's confirmation of **presence in the register**. It does **not** independently verify who created the entry, lawful admission, the rehearsed transfer story, or Alice's knowledge of those acts. Yuuka says she will not be easily deceived; Momoi and Midori visibly fear exposure. The first pressure is thus about consent and authenticity, not whether the card is physically there.

After a label-conflicted question about why she came, Alice checks a near-admission that she was simply here when she became aware, then offers `『魔王城ドラキュラ』` as a reason to discover the club. Momoi's stare is called out by Yuuka, who tells her to stop. The review therefore occurs under visible club pressure, even though Yuuka cannot inspect Alice's origin from these answers. Yuuka distinguishes a game-development club from a retro-game club and asks what Alice will *make*. Alice starts with `タンク兼光属性アタッカー`, stumbles into `プログラマラス`, accepts Yuuka's correction to programmer and asserts perfect skill. Asked about difficult work, she describes programmers fainting from overwork; Yuuka immediately says this is not okay and to rest. Alice then says an inn or paid church service revives companions, revealing that her working picture of fainting/death comes from games, not demonstrated programming experience. Yuuka rejects the literal claim. Alice praises two games as `神ゲー`. Momoi expects failure, but Yuuka's later secure ruling does not rely on the programmer story as verified fact.

The label-corrupt `u:0043-0044` express a reading of Alice as suspicious in details yet genuinely interested in games, adventure and completing something with companions. Yuuka's *secure* subsequent line says such a person being a club member is unsurprising; she formally recognizes the club again because the number is met. Later she explicitly says Alice's identity remains suspicious but her pure love of games seems real to her. The defensible account is that Yuuka makes a discretionary credibility judgment about interest/belonging in the face of a weak credential story. She does not certify Alice's unknown origin, genuine programming ability, fully informed consent to roster work, or freedom from Momoi's pressure. Yuuka had considered asking them to leave immediately but instead offers time for results. Her protective concern about overwork and her procedural skepticism complicate a simple “hostile bureaucrat” model, without making her infallible.

## 2. Recognition is real and still conditional

Yuuka explicitly grants renewed formal club recognition, budget and continued use of the clubroom—then limits these to `今学期`. She says membership number **and** demonstrable club outcomes are now required, with a grace period until this month's end. If the club cannot produce results in that period, four or four hundred members will not prevent dissolution. This is Yuuka's account of a recently changed policy; the written rule and exact calendar are not shown. E002's two-week extension and this month's-end language should not be forced into a fabricated date or treated as mathematically contradictory without a date anchor. The relief is therefore genuine but time-bounded, not a total reversal.

Yuuka says the requirement was announced at a club-heads meeting which Yuzu missed, and assigns responsibility to the club. The statement is her report about notice, not direct evidence of the meeting proceedings or why Yuzu was absent. After Yuuka leaves, Yuzu directly apologizes for missing the meeting. In a corrupted cluster, a reply says an older sister was supposed to attend in her stead and a double item-drop campaign explains a missed obligation; do not assign that excuse, accusation or a game-deletion demand to Yuzu, Momoi or Midori as a precise voice/agency fact. The secure transition is that the group recognizes the need for a prize-worthy game and talks about returning to seek `G.Bible`. No one has shown the object exists at the site, is a game-making shortcut, or would actually win Millennium Prize. Yuuka invokes Momoi's earlier contest promise and expects an improved game; the club feels pressure, but she does not order a ruins expedition.

## 3. Yuzu leaves concealment for a shared risk, at least in intent

Yuzu says `責任、取らないと`, then directly offers to go with the group if they return to the ruins for `G.Bible`. Midori reports Yuzu has barely left the school building for almost half a year and takes classes online; this is a peer report, not a clinically explained condition. Yuzu's own stated reason is more than guilt: the clubroom is no longer hers alone and she wants to protect it **together**. That is a meaningful change from E005's locker presence and E006's welcome, but it is a declared future action, not yet an observed departure or proof that the journey will be easy or safe. Alice celebrates Yuzu's party participation and explicitly equips `光の剣：スーパーノヴァ`; role-flipped `u:0100-0101` do not ground a precise command by Alice. Momoi calls for a renewed `G.Bible` search and Midori agrees they will protect the room as a group. The unit closes before they travel.
