## 1. Apparent suit immunity and a bounded rooftop counter

Sensei inwardly notes many attacks with no visible scratch. Chihiro moves from a special-metal hypothesis to an active neutralization reading: bullets are shot down before arrival and blind-side attacks evaded (`scene:001:u:0001-0006`). She says Eridu-wide power and computing have concentrated in the suit and speculates that its enhancement could enable even future prediction (`u:0007-0008`). These are in-scene technical readings, partly supported by observed evasion, not independently instrumented energy totals or literal precognition proof. Yuzu/Midori call it a final-boss cheat; this is game analogy and alarm, not a device specification.

Rio names Sensei the “only variable,” says she will remove their command ability and orders Toki; Chihiro urges Sensei to flee (`u:0012-0015`). Rio's sole-variable account remains interested, against the shown coalition. Akane's italic `陸上専用` hint at `u:0017` suggests a land-only limit, but the delivery/technical basis is not secure. Sensei's `u:0019` “Nel, change the battlefield” is `心の声` despite Nel's apparent response, so it cannot be made a clean audible order; `choice:003` explicitly directs them to the roof. Nel says she trusts Sensei and takes the move (`u:0020-0024`). Rio incorrectly guesses the goal is a narrow space; `choice:004` rejects that (`u:0025-0030`).

Nel uses height and pushes Toki outside the building, trying to exploit airborne combat while falling (`u:0031-0044`). A rough Nel-like line is printed under Toki at `u:0034`; do not use that attribution literally. Toki reports an impact with light damage, then starts gravitational-acceleration compensation and says Eridu backup preserves her action in air (`u:0045-0048`). Nel reacts with pain and calls the counter a cheat (`u:0049-0055`). A later rear attack/shot sequence does not secure a final outcome; Toki says zero injury from successive battles at `u:0058`, a self-report about then-current state, not a permanent invulnerability theorem. Sensei then notices Nel's wound, and Nel downplays it (`u:0061-0065`). Yuzu's “fell from the building without a scratch” at `u:0067-0068` refers to Toki in context, not a clean bill of health for Nel.

## 2. Rio reveals a second contingency and a deadline-bound defense

Rio says the suit was made with the fortress for future Kivotos threats and also prepared in case C&C “betrayed” her (`u:0069-0074`). This is a direct claim of institutional distrust and dual-purpose planning, not proof C&C's resistance is illegitimate or Alice a world threat. Rio orders Toki to recover Sensei; Toki complies, then notices apparent damage to her machine (`u:0075-0077`). Thus the prior zero-injury line and the later machine-damage line must be kept distinct by time and referent. Chihiro calls a gap and cues Momoi; Momoi acts, Toki reports the target escaped (`u:0078-0081`). The exact action/device is not identified, so E017's unnamed gift must not be retrospectively named here. Rio orders no pursuit, saying their objective is to defend *here* until `刻限` (`u:0082-0084`). The time limit adds urgency but has no clock value or observed completed procedure. Rio predicts the others will return and plans to end everything then (`u:0085-0089`), a prospective threat, not outcome.

- Chihiro's `無力化`/pre-arrival bullet interception and blind-side evasion are local observations. `全域の電力と演算機能が、すべて` and `未来を予知する事だって` are her escalating inference/possibility, not an audited grid measurement or established precognition.

- Rio's `裏切った時に備えて` marks her C&C-contingency rhetoric. `刻限までここを防衛` gives a mission deadline without its time or Alice procedure status.
- **Quarantine:** `u:0034` Toki label voices Nel's rough register.
