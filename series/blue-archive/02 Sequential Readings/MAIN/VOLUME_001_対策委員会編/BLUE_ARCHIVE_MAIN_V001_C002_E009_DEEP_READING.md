## 1. Reconstruction

After the first clash, the committee distinguishes individual strength from organizational difficulty: the soldiers are not necessarily exceptional combatants, but they are unusually troublesome, possibly more so than the Prefect Team in this situation.

Ayane verifies a facility mark; Hoshino and Ayane identify both installation and force as **Kaiser PMC**. Ayane explicitly calls it a Kaiser Corporation affiliate (`系列会社`). This crosses one Kaiser firewall: PMC↔Corporation affiliation is now in committee knowledge and project fact through a direct identification. It does not establish Loan/Construction/Black Suit relations.

Nonomi expands `PMC` as private military company. Ayane distinguishes its organized professionals from Helmet Gang street forces. Nonomi reports only a rumor that corporations recruit expelled or delinquent students as private soldiers; personnel origin remains unverified.

Alarm, helicopters, tanks, armored vehicles, and a large encircling force transform the encounter into a scale problem. Ayane orders escape before the ring closes and explicitly asks Sensei for instructions. Sensei's command role is requested under crisis, but no order text or outcome appears in E009.

## 3. Central reading

E009 sharpens the difference between violence as individual force and violence as institution. The threat lies in alarms, perimeter, hierarchy, vehicles, encirclement, and replaceable scale. Corporate capacity becomes army-like without becoming public authority.

This strongly strengthens `BA-C014`: capability and ownership can create effective territorial government, but neither the affiliate label nor private professionalism supplies legitimacy toward Abydos.

Sensei remains additive. Ayane generates the analysis and decision to break contact, then commissions command assistance. The text neither depicts personal combat supremacy nor transfers institutional judgment to Sensei.
