# BLUE ARCHIVE — MAIN V001 C002 E013 DEEP READING
## 対策委員会編 — 第13話「手をすり抜けたもの」

## 0. Boundary and form

This reading covers only `BA:main:001:002:013`, raw groups `12130|12135`, two canonical scenes, 74 utterance records, and no choices. No E014 evidence is used.

Scene 1 intercuts Hoshino's farewell letter with the live signature/transport sequence and the committee's reading/reaction. Italic Hoshino passages are letter contents; non-italic dialogue establishes present action. Scene 2 is only the next-episode card `アビドス攻防戦（１）` and supplies no additional event.

## 1. The contract crosses E012's remaining firewall

Hoshino's letter says the arrangement is mercenary work for Kaiser PMC in exchange for Kaiser assuming most of Abydos's debt. The live sequence then shows Hoshino asking whether the signature is sufficient and Black Suit confirming receipt. He claims that the signed contract transfers all of Hoshino's rights as a student to him and that his side will assume most of the debt.

This establishes signature and acceptance, which E012 did not. It does not establish the contract's legality, enforceability, or metaphysical scope merely because Black Suit asserts `全権利` transfer. Nor does it fully settle organizational identity: PMC employment is Hoshino's account of the bargain, while rights transfer is to Black Suit personally in his wording.

The numerical language shifts. E012's recalled offer covered `ほぼ半分` (nearly half); E013's letter and Black Suit say `大半` (most/the greater part). The source provides no reconciliation. Analysis should retain the discrepancy rather than manufacture a precise percentage.

## 2. Responsibility collapses into unilateral self-disposal

Hoshino apologizes for failing to keep her Black Market words and calls the bargain her responsibility as Abydos's last student council. She expects departure not only from school but from Kivotos. Her reasoning converts inherited office burden into exclusive ownership of the solution.

Serika immediately identifies the contradiction: Hoshino spoke authoritatively about not doing anything under pressure while knowing she could do the same. The letter is therefore not the fulfillment of the committee's method ethic. It is crisis exceptionalism by the person who previously enforced that ethic.

Hoshino's act is protective in purpose but anti-collective in form. She prevents discussion, decides the acceptable price of her person, and presents the others with a completed transaction. The title's thing that slipped through the hand is not only Hoshino; it is the committee's capacity to keep responsibility collectively governable.

## 3. Adult trust is entrusted support, not replacement

Hoshino tells Sensei that she once disliked and distrusted adults, including judging Sensei a useless adult on arrival. Meeting an adult like Sensei changed that trajectory enough for her to entrust Shiroko's future support to him.

The request is specific: stand beside Shiroko so she does not stray onto a bad path. It is neither a request to govern Shiroko nor evidence that student relationships are dispensable. Indeed, its danger becomes visible immediately. Shiroko responds to Hoshino's sacrifice by proposing to rescue her alone so the committee will not be burdened. Ayane interrupts the replication and insists on coordinated action.

Hoshino's trust therefore strengthens Sensei's relational legitimacy while also burdening him with a task no adult can perform through possession. Support must preserve Shiroko's participation in the group that can correct her sacrificial impulse.

## 4. Signature as pretext for coordinated territorial seizure

A PMC soldier orders Hoshino aboard and says she is being taken to the Abydos desert. At the same time, Kaiser PMC attacks the district, including indiscriminate fire in the city and an eviction order. The director declares all conditions cleared: because the last student council has withdrawn, Abydos High School has effectively disappeared, permitting Kaiser Corporation to absorb/merge Abydos and occupy the school.

This sharply advances the capture model. Hoshino's exit was not merely a debt-reduction transaction; the director treats it as the final institutional condition for territorial seizure. The timing connects recruitment, school dissolution rhetoric, civilian displacement, occupation, and corporate absorption in one executed plan.

Yet the director's statements remain the aggressor's legal/political theory. The continued students, school defense, and civic population materially contest his assertion that Abydos has disappeared. E013 also corrects any impression that the district is wholly uninhabited: named role-level Abydos civilians flee the attack.

## 5. Emergency governance after chair loss

The committee has no time to complete a rescue plan. It prioritizes immediate defense and civilian evacuation under overwhelming numerical disadvantage. Ayane first demands unity, then orders citizen evacuation, school security, and explicit protection of Sensei. Shiroko and Serika engage nearby scouts; Nonomi insists that an attack on Abydos cannot be ignored.

Chair absence does not erase the institution. The remaining members distribute judgment and act for residents, school, and vulnerable adult simultaneously. Their conduct is a direct practical rebuttal to the director's claim that withdrawal of one officeholder makes the community nonexistent.

## 6. Central theses

### Captured consent can become the switch for wider coercion

Hoshino signs intentionally, but within a choice field engineered through debt and threatened school extinction. Kaiser then uses that individual signature to justify harms against nonconsenting students and civilians. Consent to self-sacrifice cannot authorize annexation of a community.

### Responsibility becomes dangerous when treated as exclusive ownership

Hoshino's protective purpose does not cure her unilateral method. Shiroko instantly reproduces the same logic—`I alone`—showing that sacrificial responsibility is contagious. Ayane's coordination demand is the episode's clearest alternative.

### Institutional existence is enacted, not exhausted by a chair's paperwork

The director equates office withdrawal with school disappearance. The committee's coordinated defense and evacuation show continuing membership, governance, and obligation. Formal dissolution rhetoric and lived institution diverge.

## 8. Epistemic adjudication

### Established

- Hoshino signs a contract presented by Black Suit and is taken toward the Abydos desert by a PMC soldier;
- the bargain is described as Kaiser PMC mercenary work/debt assumption in Hoshino's letter;
- Black Suit claims all student rights transfer to him and promises assumption of most debt;
- the director treats Hoshino's withdrawal as the final condition for absorption and orders occupation;
- PMC forces attack the city indiscriminately, issue an eviction order, invade the school, and encounter Abydos civilians;
- the committee chooses coordinated defense and civilian evacuation.

### Strong inference

- Hoshino's recruitment and withdrawal were designed as the final step in Kaiser's territorial-capture plan;
- her self-sacrificial method reproduces itself in Shiroko's immediate solo-rescue proposal;
- the contract is coercively conditioned even though Hoshino intentionally signs.

### OPEN

- contract validity and the actual content beyond spoken descriptions;
- the meaning/enforceability of transferred `student rights`;
- exact Black Suit/Kaiser/Gematria hierarchy;
- Hoshino's intended treatment and whether she will be made an enemy;
- reconciliation of `nearly half` in E012 with `most` in E013;
- whether the director's dissolution/absorption theory has any legitimate legal force.

### Rejected

- Hoshino's signature authorizes violence against the rest of Abydos;
- one chair's withdrawal proves the school/community no longer exists;
- protective motive makes unilateral self-disposal collectively legitimate;
- Hoshino's enemy/halo request proves a later confrontation will occur;
- Sensei's entrusted role replaces peer coordination.
