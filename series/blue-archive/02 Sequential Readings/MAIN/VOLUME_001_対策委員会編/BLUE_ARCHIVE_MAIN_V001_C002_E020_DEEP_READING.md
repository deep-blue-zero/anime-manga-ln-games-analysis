# BLUE ARCHIVE — MAIN V001 C002 E020 DEEP READING
## 対策委員会編 — 第20話「エピローグ」

## 0. Boundary

This reading covers only `BA:main:001:002:020`, raw group `12200`, one canonical scene, and no choices. No Chapter 3 evidence is used.

Ayane alternates direct meeting speech with epilogue voiceover. Her causal question about Trinity action (`のでしょうか`) and doubts about federal investigation remain explicitly uncertain rather than promoted as fact.

## 1. Recognition is repaired without installing a ruler

Sensei's public certification formally recognizes the Countermeasures Committee as an Abydos High School committee. It also assumes the role of the student council. This directly repairs the formal defect Kaiser used to call the students nonexistent.

The repair does not dictate internal leadership. Ayane wanted Hoshino as president; Hoshino firmly refused; the presidency remains undecided. Adult authority supplies recognition while leaving office choice within the community. This is the chapter's clearest institutional form of autonomy amplification.

The committee now has both lived continuity and documentary standing. Neither alone was sufficient under attack: practice survived without papers, while papers now reduce vulnerability without creating the relationships they recognize.

## 2. Material victory is intentionally incomplete

Hoshino's immediate case is described as resolved, but the source does not separately adjudicate every clause of Black Suit's contract. The observable result is that Hoshino is back and active.

The ¥900 million principal remains. The artificially increased interest becomes an official issue and is reduced to far below its prior burden. Kaiser Loan's illegal Black Market finance is exposed and a General Student Council investigation is expected. Ayane doubts the institution will investigate effectively.

Most of the Abydos district remains Kaiser Corporation property because the underlying transactions were not judged illegal. Kaiser's unknown desert purpose remains unknown. The chapter therefore defeats the forced-exit escalation without reversing the accumulated dispossession that made it possible.

## 3. Accountability is fragmented and externalized

The Kaiser director is wanted as the principal suspect in a student-kidnapping case, but weak federal function may prevent arrest. Kaiser Corporation immediately dismisses him to claim separation.

This is partial accountability. The individual is named as suspect and loses office, but the corporation that benefited from his multi-entity roles retains land and attempts to externalize responsibility. Dismissal does not erase the E011 evidence that he simultaneously led Corporation, Loan, Construction, and PMC surfaces.

Black Suit remains unidentified. Research based on Sensei and Hoshino's information yields no name or identity; the visible crimes appear assigned to the director. The committee now shares enough Black Suit information to investigate, closing the earlier private-knowledge firewall in broad form, but exact disclosed details remain unstated. Continuing investigation is entrusted to Schale.

## 4. Shiba Seki and ordinary recurrence

Shiba Seki operates again as a stall. Customers return, Serika resumes work, and the master abandons near-term retirement. E017's theory becomes observed state: the destroyed building did not end the relational practice.

At the committee meeting, debt urgency persists. Serika proposes a dubious cryptocurrency-mining trend; Shiroko proposes another infiltration route; Hoshino rejects both. The comedy repeats earlier patterns, but repetition is now the point. Hoshino is present to constrain desperate methods, members can disagree, and routine survives.

Ayane says nothing has changed and they have returned to the usual—but that this is genuinely good. The ordinary miracle from E019 becomes institutional rhythm rather than a one-time emotional beat.

## 5. Continuing obligation

The final line asks Sensei for continued support. This is not closure through adult completion. The committee remains responsible for an enormous debt, land loss, uncertain federal action, and Black Suit's unresolved threat. Sensei remains a continuing advisor/investigator to a functioning student institution.

The title card `Lost things, things not let go` precisely describes the settlement: land, buildings, prior population, and financial security remain lost or damaged; membership, shared method, ordinary life, and the capacity to continue were not relinquished.

## 6. Central theses

### Legitimate adult power repairs the conditions of self-government

Sensei uses certification to make the committee legible, then does not appoint its president or absorb its office. The power is legitimate because it reduces external vulnerability and leaves internal choice open.

### Rescue is not restoration of everything

The coercive escalation is stopped and Hoshino returns, but debt, ownership, corporate impunity, and weak public enforcement remain. The chapter refuses to equate emotional reunion with structural repair.

### Ordinary recurrence is a political achievement

Meetings, bad proposals, work shifts, ramen, naps, and refusals are what coercion tried to make impossible. Returning to them is not narrative reset; it is survival with unresolved burdens.
