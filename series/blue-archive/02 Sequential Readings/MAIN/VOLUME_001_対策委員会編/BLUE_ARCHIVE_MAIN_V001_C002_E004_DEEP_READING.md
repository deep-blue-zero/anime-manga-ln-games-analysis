## 1. Narrative reconstruction

### Nonomi practices inference without premature extraction

Nonomi meets Sensei while cleaning because she cannot remain idle. She asks about the proprietor, detects the qualification in Sensei's `身体の方は` report, and infers that the missing Ayane/Serika pair are investigating another problem. Yet she declines to press: the proper time is when everyone is present.

She then surveys the accelerating sequence since Sensei arrived—material successes alongside Helmet Gang, PS68, Prefect Team, and Kaiser pressures—and asks whether Sensei will remain. Both choice variants affirm continued presence. The reassurance is relational support, not a plan or guarantee of victory.

Shiroko arrives visibly anxious about Hoshino. Nonomi and Sensei notice but do not force an immediate account.

### Shiroko challenges Hoshino's sleep story

Inside, Shiroko has physically confronted Hoshino and asks how long he will pretend ignorance. She says he is lying. When Nonomi and Sensei enter, Shiroko asks for privacy; Nonomi initially rejects a two-person secret because the committee is a `運命共同体`.

Hoshino supplies the same oversleeping explanation and redirects the group toward the meeting. Shiroko does not retract her suspicion; she yields without disclosing its basis. Nonomi recognizes that something remains unsaid and revises her stance: everyone has things they do not want to say.

The scene therefore preserves two legitimate but competing needs:

- shared danger makes hidden leader action institutionally consequential;
- membership in a collective does not eliminate personal privacy or justify forced disclosure without evidence.

Sensei asks whether Nonomi is all right but does not compel Hoshino or Shiroko to explain. Hoshino's absence remains unresolved; Shiroko's accusation raises the probability of concealment but is not independent proof of Black Suit causation or a specific lie.

### The cadastre tells an institutional story no member knew

Ayane and Serika return with an Abydos district land register (`地籍図`) recording recent transactions. It shows that not only Shiba Seki's building but most of the autonomous district is no longer registered to Abydos High School.

The current owner is explicitly named:

> `カイザーコンストラクション`

Hoshino, Nonomi, and Shiroko react with shock. This crosses E003's owner firewall, but only to a new entity: Kaiser Construction. The episode does not state its legal relation to Kaiser Loan, Kaiser PMC, Kaiser Corporation, or Black Suit.

## 2. Central theses

### Thesis 1 — agency-preserving solidarity includes both transparency and privacy

Nonomi first treats secrecy as incompatible with a shared-fate committee, then recognizes that forced disclosure would also violate a member's agency. Her revision is not indifference. She keeps the concealment visible and continues with the group.

E004 therefore complicates the inherited thesis: meaningful participation requires enough knowledge to govern, but it cannot be reduced to unlimited access to every person's interior history.

### Thesis 2 — documentary infrastructure can reveal dispossession hidden from lived governance

The committee lives, works, fights, and claims autonomy in Abydos while official title to most of that space belongs elsewhere. The cadastre makes the gap legible.

This strengthens `BA-C012`–`BA-C015`: debt pressure has become territorial capture, and administrative records are again the mechanism that lets students see a coercive structure previously operating around them.

### Thesis 3 — a shared brand vocabulary is evidence for investigation, not organizational identity

Kaiser Construction is the verified current owner. Its `Kaiser` name sits beside previously encountered Loan, PMC, and Corporation labels, but naming similarity does not prove corporate structure, common command, or Black Suit affiliation.

The right transition is to add a differentiated actor and a stronger network hypothesis, not collapse the network.

### Thesis 4 — support is affectively real but not omnipotent

Nonomi asks Sensei to remain because new threats keep arriving. Sensei promises presence, not a solution. The episode immediately places that promise beside a hidden interpersonal conflict and near-total district dispossession, preserving the gap between solidarity and control.
