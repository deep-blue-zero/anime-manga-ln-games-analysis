## 1. Narrative reconstruction

### PS68 leaves because both employer and authorities have become threats

PS68 packs its office. The Prefect Team now knows the location, the Abydos job failed, and Mutsuki expects the client may pursue them. Departure is therefore material displacement, not merely a graceful farewell.

Aru is despondent. She refuses to resume attacking people beside whom PS68 just fought: `一緒に背中を合わせて戦った人たちを今になって狙うなんて……できるわけない`. Kayoko reveals that all remaining money in the bag was left for Shiba Seki repairs.

This is the first direct reparative act after the demolition. It does not settle legal ownership of the money, identify the client, make Aru confess the internal sequence, or show Haruka recognizing her interpretive error. It nevertheless revises PS68 responsibility from adopted villain performance toward material repair and refusal to renew violence.

Haruka says she likely would not be alive without Aru and identifies Aru as the person she respects most. The line supplies a concrete existential basis for loyalty, but not the underlying event or justification for self-erasure.

### Sensei permits farewell without demanding possession or adjudication

Sensei visits and tells the group to take care. PS68 members distinguish Sensei from the Abydos job: Mutsuki wants friendship, Haruka recalls Sensei's help against Prefect Team, and Kayoko sees no malice or reason to continue hostility.

Sensei asks whether they are leaving and says `また会おうね`. Aru answers through business-partner language, then PS68 admits Abydos was a good place and anticipates returning for ramen. The departure is not banishment. The social bridge survives in memory and projected return even though the restaurant is physically destroyed.

### Shiba Seki's destruction reveals prior property dispossession

Sensei, Ayane, and Serika visit the proprietor. His injury is minor, but he says the shop had already been scheduled to close because of an eviction notice. Years earlier, when the Abydos student council could not repay debt, ownership of buildings and land transferred away from the school.

Ayane and Serika did not know. The information changes the meaning of the restaurant loss: demolition accelerates closure, but debt-driven property transfer had already made continued operation insecure.

Sensei can guess either Kaiser Loan or Kaiser Corporation. The proprietor only vaguely recognizes the name and cannot confirm it. The canonical fact is transferred ownership after default; a Kaiser link is a live suspicion, not established identity.

Ayane decides to verify something elsewhere with Serika. Sensei agrees to wait at school and says there is separate information to share—the Hina warning is not yet spoken to the group in this unit.

The proprietor then reports the bag of money left at the shop. Sensei tells him to use it for rebuilding. That choice directs PS68's reparative resource toward its intended community purpose without claiming credit or compelling forgiveness.

## 2. Central theses

### Thesis 1 — responsibility becomes reparative before it becomes fully truthful

Aru still has not publicly separated Haruka's act from her own post hoc ratification. Yet she refuses renewed violence and leaves all remaining bag money for repairs. E003 therefore distinguishes at least four layers:

1. Haruka's direct act;
2. Aru's earlier false adoption of intent;
3. PS68's collective benefit/exposure under the client contract;
4. Aru's present material repair and nonaggression.

Repair is morally meaningful without being a complete confession. The text preserves imperfect responsibility rather than requiring psychological purity before restitution can begin.

### Thesis 2 — hospitality persists as future relation after the place is destroyed

PS68 says Abydos was good and intends to return for ramen. Aru's quiet `本当に美味しかった` abandons grand villain diction for direct attachment. Shiba Seki's contact-zone function survives as remembered experience and promised return, even while the proprietor's property security collapses.

This strongly strengthens `BA-C018`: conflict de-escalation produced by hospitality can outlast the physical site, though not substitute for rebuilding it.

### Thesis 3 — debt operates spatially by transferring the ground of community life

The debt is no longer only a recurring payment schedule or creditor incentive. Default transferred land and building ownership, enabling eviction of a community institution without the current committee's knowledge.

This strengthens the debt/coercive-capacity claims while adding an epistemic dimension: a student body may nominally govern an autonomous district while lacking both title and knowledge about its built environment.

### Thesis 4 — choice discipline protects the Kaiser firewall

The source invites the player to guess `カイザーローン` or `カイザーコーポレーション`, then makes the witness unable to confirm either. The choice is an inference-space, not a revelation.

Hina's separate warning makes Kaiser Corporation suspicion salient, and Chapter 1 established Kaiser Loan's debt relation. Neither adjacency licenses a precise owner claim at E003.
