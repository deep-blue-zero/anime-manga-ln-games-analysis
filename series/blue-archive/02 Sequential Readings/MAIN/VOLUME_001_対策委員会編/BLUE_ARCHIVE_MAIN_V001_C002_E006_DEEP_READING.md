## 1. Narrative reconstruction

The expedition reaches the last rail-accessible point and proceeds on foot toward the preexisting Abydos desert. Ayane identifies roaming broken drones, security robots, and automata as ordinary hazards and orders weapon checks. Investigation therefore begins as a committee-managed field operation, not an impulsive walk.

Serika challenges the provenance of the warning: why does another academy's disciplinary chair know more about Abydos territory than Abydos does? Ayane first offers only a hypothesis about Prefect Team intelligence capacity.

She then re-reads Ako's earlier language after the cadastral discovery. The Prefect side had described the operation as near another academy's district, not inside it, and said its conduct was not yet clearly illegal. Ayane now considers that they may already have known Abydos lacked title to the location.

Shiroko refuses the implication that this retroactively invalidates Abydos's resistance. Even if registered ownership was elsewhere, the Prefect Team displayed conduct reasonably understood as infringement, and Ako's Schale-custody operation was unmistakably hostile. She tells Ayane her judgment was not wrong.

Ayane accepts the reassurance while retaining the epistemic point: Gehenna officials may possess facts the committee did not. Hina's warning is therefore plausible without making Gehenna's earlier conduct legitimate.

Hoshino returns the group to the agreed method—direct observation—and the expedition continues.

## 2. Central theses

### Thesis 1 — property title and political legitimacy are related but not identical

E004 established that Abydos lacks title to most of the district. E006 refuses to let cadastral ownership erase lived jurisdiction, community presence, or the character of armed coercion.

Shiroko's position is not a complete legal theory. It is a necessary limit: a stronger actor cannot make hostile seizure agency-preserving merely by exploiting an ownership fact unknown to the resident institution.

### Thesis 2 — retrospective correction need not become self-nullification

Ayane responsibly revisits her earlier judgment in light of new evidence. Shiroko distinguishes correction from repudiation: the property premise may change while the coercion judgment remains sound.

This is a strong instance of `BA-C017`. Agency includes the ability to revise reasons without surrendering the right to have acted under the evidence and threat then available.

### Thesis 3 — superior information does not cure unauthorized action

The Prefect Team may have known cadastral facts Abydos did not. That informational advantage explains some wording and Hina's later warning; it does not authorize Ako's political operation or forced custody.

Knowledge, mandate, means, and legitimacy remain separate variables.
