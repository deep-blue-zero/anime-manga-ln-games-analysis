# BLUE ARCHIVE — MAIN V001 C002 E010 DEEP READING
## 対策委員会編 — 第10話「カイザーPMC（２）」

## 0. Boundary and anomaly

This reading covers only `BA:main:001:002:010`, raw group `12100`, one scene, and no choices.

`u:0016` is an isolated Black Suit line repeating the earlier proposal (`私の提案を受け入れますか？`) between the director's address and Hoshino's parenthetical `あの時の……`. It is best treated as recalled speech, not proof that Black Suit is physically present. The canonical projection does not explicitly mark the transition, so physical presence remains quarantined.

## 1. Reconstruction

The committee fails to break encirclement; communications with Ayane fragment. A `カイザーPMC理事` addresses Hoshino by name and former vice-presidential role, asks whether she will carry the debt without a council president, and claims new facility damage can simply be added to the school debt.

The director then recognizes Hoshino as the president/vice president targeted by `例のゲマトリア`. He says a new idea may be better than hiring PS68 or the Helmet Gang. This is direct self-incriminating knowledge: the director acknowledges both proxy hires and awareness that Gematria targeted Hoshino.

When the students demand identity, he says he is a Kaiser Corporation director and the party to whom Abydos owes its debt. He proposes discussion of the old debt while his force holds the committee surrounded.

## 2. Firewall transitions

E010 crosses several previously protected edges:

- **Corporation ↔ PMC:** already explicit in E009; the site director also self-identifies as a Corporation director.
- **Corporation ↔ Abydos debt:** the director says the Corporation is the creditor; Kaiser Loan can now be modeled as its debt interface, though exact legal structure remains unstated.
- **Director ↔ PS68/Helmet Gang:** he directly says he hired them, confirming common client control over both proxy pressures.
- **Director ↔ Gematria knowledge:** he knows Gematria targeted Hoshino. Knowledge/contact is established; membership, hierarchy, and Black Suit affiliation are not.
- **Construction:** still not explicitly connected by E010 dialogue despite the shared Kaiser name and committee theory.

The audience's C1 view of the director speaking with Black Suit is now more structurally relevant, but it still does not become full committee knowledge. What the students hear is the director's Gematria reference and proxy admission.

## 3. Central readings

### Debt is a platform for expandable coercion

The director treats armed resistance damage as an amount he can append to inherited debt. Property control, military exclusion, creditor power, and negotiation are not separate systems; debt becomes the accounting surface through which corporate force rewrites obligation.

### Capture replaces failed proxies with direct leverage

The director's `面白いアイディア` follows recognition of Hoshino and is contrasted with hired proxies. Exact content is open, but the structure is clear: Hoshino herself may become the new instrument or bargaining object when indirect pressure fails.

### Information inequality isolates Hoshino

The director knows her office, debt succession, and Gematria interest. Hoshino recognizes the earlier proposal through recalled speech. The rest of the committee lacks that full history, intensifying the cost of Hoshino's concealment and Sensei/Shiroko's temporary secrecy.

## 4. State and role deltas

- **Hoshino:** is directly addressed as debt successor and Gematria target; private burden becomes externally weaponized before she explains it internally.
- **Kaiser director:** moves from audience-only strategic actor to direct antagonist, creditor, proxy employer, and controller of armed negotiation space.
- **Committee:** learns the common client behind PS68/Helmet Gang and the Corporation creditor identity while under encirclement.
- **Ayane:** communications fail, limiting her operator support; the requested E009 command has not secured escape.
- **Sensei:** no dialogue/choice or documented successful order; coordination has real limits under infrastructure and scale asymmetry.

## 5. Epistemic adjudication

### Established

- the speaker is a Kaiser Corporation director and Abydos creditor;
- he controls/represents the current PMC confrontation;
- he hired PS68 and Helmet Gang;
- he knows Gematria targeted Hoshino;
- he proposes debt negotiation under encirclement and claims damages can be added.

### Strong inference

- Kaiser Loan is a Corporation debt/collection interface rather than an unrelated namesake;
- the director has operational contact or information exchange with Gematria/Black Suit;
- Hoshino's prior proposal is active leverage in the director's new idea.

### OPEN

- exact corporate structure of Loan/PMC/Construction;
- director/Black Suit/Gematria membership or command relation;
- exact new idea and proposal terms;
- whether Hoshino's withdrawal form responds to that proposal;
- legal enforceability of added damages;
- outcome of the encirclement.

### Rejected

- isolated `u:0016` proves Black Suit is physically present;
- shared information proves director and Black Suit are the same person or organization;
- every Kaiser entity is now legally unified;
- negotiation under surrounding force is meaningfully voluntary.
