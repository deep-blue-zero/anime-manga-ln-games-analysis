## 1. Narrative reconstruction

### Hina terminates Ako's political operation

Ayane identifies Hina as Gehenna's Prefect Team chair and an exceptional combatant. Ako begins to explain that she was arresting badly behaved students, but Hina observes that PS68 is gone while the deployed force now faces Schale and Abydos.

Hina refuses an exhaustive field explanation because she can already infer the design: verification and removal of factors Ako considered dangerous to Gehenna. She then supplies the institutional correction Ako omitted:

> `私たちは風紀委員会であって、生徒会じゃない。`

Schale, the Tea Party, and the General Student Council president belong to the political domain Hina assigns to Pandemonium Society, not to the Prefect Team's self-expanded jurisdiction. Her contempt for Pandemonium does not become a claim that disciplinary competence authorizes institutional substitution. She orders Ako to cut communications, return to the school building, and remain suspended pending a fuller account.

This converts E001's chair-level nonauthorization into direct accountability. Ako's risk theory is not ratified merely because Hina can reconstruct it.

### Abydos and Hina negotiate fault without collapsing it

After a long silence, Shiroko initially proposes resuming combat. Ayane stops her and prefers negotiation because Hina's strength changes the practical risk.

The attribution-shifted exchange nevertheless establishes two secure positions:

- Hina recognizes unannounced troop use in another school's autonomous district and the clash with that school's students;
- Hina also observes that Abydos interfered with a Prefect Team public duty.

Neither side treats one fact as erasing the other. Abydos does not withdraw its position. Hina does not claim the earlier operation was legitimate. This is the first direct negotiation of the sovereignty dispute, though the source corruption prevents fine-grained allocation of every sentence.

### Hoshino returns without explaining the absence

Hoshino appears and claims he overslept. The committee's reactions confirm that his absence mattered operationally. The source does not show where he slept, why he could not be reached, or whether Black Suit affected the delay. His comic explanation is character speech, not independent causal proof.

Hina recognizes him as `小鳥遊ホシノ`, says he has changed radically since first year, and recalls him from her time in an intelligence division. She references `あの事件`, says she thought he had left Abydos afterward, and appears to connect his presence with Schale in an unfinished inference: `だからシャーレが……`.

This opens historical relations without resolving them. The incident, Hina's exact knowledge, Hoshino's earlier status, and the content of Hina's inference remain OPEN.

### Hina converts command supremacy into institutional retreat

Hoshino offers renewed combat. Hina declines: she did not come to fight. She orders Iori and Chinatsu to prepare withdrawal, then bows and formally apologizes in her own name as Prefect Team chair for unannounced troop deployment and disorder inside another academy's autonomous district.

She also promises:

> `今後、ゲヘナの風紀委員会がここに無断で侵入することは無い`

The apology does not declare Abydos blameless for obstruction or PS68 innocent. It accepts institutional responsibility for the Prefect Team's means and supplies a forward restraint. The troops withdraw in exact formation at extraordinary speed, so retreat is not incapacity. Hina has the power to continue and chooses institutional limitation.

PS68 escapes during the confrontation. Its disposition is therefore not adjudicated; the alliance ends through absence rather than settlement.

### Hina gives Sensei a warning instead of taking custody

The committee disperses to rest and plans to reconstruct events the next day. Hina privately tells Sensei that Kaiser Corporation is plotting something in the abandoned Abydos desert. She says neither Pandemonium Society nor the Tea Party knows this information yet.

Hina frames disclosure as discretionary—Gehenna ordinarily owes little to a school expected to close—but gives it anyway. Unlike Ako's custody plan, the warning transfers actionable knowledge without taking control of the recipient, the territory, or the response.

When Shiroko asks what was said, Sensei promises to tell everyone later. The episode therefore closes by routing privileged intelligence into collective Abydos deliberation rather than creating a private adult monopoly.

## 2. Central theses

### Thesis 1 — Hina limits her own institution at the point of maximum capacity

Hina is introduced as nearly unmatched in combat and then demonstrates command over a large, disciplined force. Her most important action is not victory. It is scope correction, discipline, apology, promise, and withdrawal.

This is strong evidence for the inherited agency-preserving-power hypothesis because restraint is costly and chosen under conditions where coercive continuation remains possible. Yet the scene does not make Hina universally legitimate: she still characterizes Abydos as having obstructed Gehenna public duty, and her initial information about Hoshino and Kaiser remains partly opaque.

### Thesis 2 — jurisdictional settlement requires differentiated responsibility

Hina can acknowledge Prefect Team wrongdoing without denying that Abydos interfered with enforcement. Abydos can maintain its refusal without pretending PS68 did nothing wrong. The settlement succeeds because fault is separated by actor, act, and jurisdiction.

The episode rejects two shortcuts:

- target wrongdoing does not authorize any enforcement method;
- institutional apology does not erase every countervailing grievance.

### Thesis 3 — warning is an agency-preserving alternative to protective custody

Ako treats uncertainty by attempting to possess the uncertain actor. Hina treats a different security risk by sharing information with someone positioned to act on it.

The contrast is not simply kind versus cruel. Both decisions use privileged intelligence and discretionary power. Their ethical difference lies in control:

- custody selects the protected person's location and removes refusal;
- warning enlarges the recipient's knowledge while leaving response with Sensei and Abydos.

This strongly supports `BA-C007`, `BA-C010`, `BA-C016`, and `BA-C017` while providing a concrete rival model to Ako's approach.

### Thesis 4 — Hoshino's return expands the mystery rather than solving it

The `昼寝` explanation restores Hoshino to the comic surface but does not close the causal question. Hina's first-year comparison, intelligence-division memory, `あの事件`, and unfinished Schale inference all suggest a history unavailable to the committee and audience at this boundary.

The responsible conclusion is not that Hoshino lied or that Black Suit detained him. It is that E002 supplies new reasons to preserve rather than dissolve the open question.

### Thesis 5 — Schale remains additive because Sensei returns intelligence to the group

Sensei receives a warning the committee does not initially hear. The final choice commits to sharing it later, and Shiroko accepts that timing. Sensei's role is connective rather than substitutive: trusted recipient of cross-school intelligence, then conduit back into student governance.
