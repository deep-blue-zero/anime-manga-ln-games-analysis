# BLUE ARCHIVE — MAIN V001 C002 E012 DEEP READING
## 対策委員会編 — 第12話「幾つかの秘密」

## 0. Boundary and topology

This reading covers only `BA:main:001:002:012`, raw group `12120`, two canonical scenes, and fifteen normalized choice groups. No E013 evidence is used.

Choice groups 1–4 and 6–15 are single-line interventions. Choice 5 contains two mutually exclusive presentations of the withdrawal form that converge on the same response; `scene:002:u:0054-0055` duplicate the convergence. Black Suit's `scene:002:u:0078-0080` is Hoshino's recalled proposal, not his physical presence in the walk. Scene 1 is a prior-time exchange between younger Hoshino and the unnamed former president.

## 1. The poster and the first meaning of `miracle`

The former president proudly gives younger Hoshino an old sand-festival poster and imagines crowds returning to Abydos. Hoshino rejects `奇跡` as irresponsible wishfulness and insists that a president face present conditions. A tearing sound follows. It strongly suggests damage to the poster in this confrontation, but the source does not explicitly narrate actor or object; those particulars remain unpromoted.

This flashback makes the lost festival personal rather than merely archival. It also establishes a philosophical inheritance: the president holds restoration as possible through miracle, while first-year Hoshino treats realism and office responsibility as defenses against false hope.

## 2. The committee rejects an expedient crime

Back at school, the committee rejects the director's treasure explanation and notes that old surveys found no oil or useful resources. Yet rejection of his story does not solve a 3000-percent rate increase or the one-week ¥300 million demand.

Shiroko proposes a prepared infiltration of the PMC because legitimate repayment is impossible. Serika supports her under existential pressure. Ayane recalls the bank-robbery plan and asks whether they will voluntarily become criminals when Hoshino stopped them before. Shiroko and Serika withdraw the proposal.

Hoshino neither shames Shiroko nor converts desperation into permission. She calls Shiroko a good person, ends the meeting, and sends everyone to rest. The committee therefore preserves a method boundary while conceding that no acceptable route is visible.

## 3. A private document becomes an accountability confrontation

Shiroko asks Hoshino to talk, Sensei adds the same request, and Hoshino defers until tomorrow while acknowledging what they want to discuss. After Shiroko leaves, Sensei directly presents the withdrawal form. Hoshino recognizes that Shiroko searched her bag and says Sensei should scold her.

The intervention is ethically mixed. It responds to imminent collective danger and gives Hoshino a chance to explain rather than accusing her publicly. But Sensei uses a private document obtained by an unauthorized search and makes clear that Hoshino cannot easily escape the conversation. Accountability here protects the group while narrowing Hoshino's immediate ability to withhold.

On their walk, Hoshino says Abydos has always appeared broken to her: the present school is an annex, the old building is buried, and predecessors are gone. The current members nevertheless make her think she may have come to like the school. Institutional attachment is grounded in present people, not denial of ruin.

## 4. Black Suit's proposal and Hoshino's bounded disclosure

Hoshino discloses that Kaiser Corporation has tried to scout her repeatedly for two years, beginning immediately after enrollment. In the most recent approach, Black Suit offered to cover nearly half the school debt if she left Abydos and joined `our company`. Hoshino says she repeatedly refused because the school would collapse without her.

She believes Kaiser is collecting personnel useful to its PMC. That recruitment-purpose statement is Hoshino's interpretation, not an admitted Kaiser policy. She does not know Black Suit's identity, only the descriptive name; she finds him unlike anyone else and observes that even the Kaiser director seemed afraid of him. This establishes leverage or superior standing in that encounter, not a legal title, exact hierarchy, or Gematria membership.

The withdrawal form was a moment of doubt. Hoshino says she will throw it away and tell everyone tomorrow because keeping secrets is undesirable, while also saying she can see no route besides accepting the proposal. Sensei insists another way must exist and promises, as an adult, to do something.

Hoshino answers with the same word she once rejected: perhaps a `miracle` will occur. The repetition is not simple conversion to optimism. It registers exhaustion, inherited memory, and a shrinking field of ordinary action.

## 5. Promise, disappearance, and evidentiary restraint

The next morning Ayane finds Hoshino's withdrawal form and a letter on the desk; Hoshino is absent. This proves departure after promising tomorrow's disclosure. It does not yet prove that she signed Black Suit's offer, joined Kaiser, or gave the letter any particular content.

The episode therefore stages two failures at once. Hoshino breaks the temporal promise that would have returned information to collective governance. Sensei's adult promise does not prevent her disappearance. Neither failure makes care insincere, but both expose the limits of reassurance when one person believes self-removal is the only viable protection.

## 6. Central theses

### Legitimate information claims can still become coercive

The committee needs to know whether its chair may leave. Sensei's private confrontation is more proportionate than public exposure, yet the evidence came from a bag search and the conversation is made difficult to refuse. Agency-preserving authority must therefore be judged by means as well as benevolent purpose.

### Captured choices can recruit self-sacrifice

Black Suit does not merely threaten Hoshino. He attaches massive collective debt relief to her individual exit and employment, making self-erasure resemble responsibility. Her two years of refusal show agency; the present crisis shows how structural coercion can exhaust it.

### A school survives through people, but people cannot be treated as collateral

Hoshino values Abydos because of its current members. The offer exploits that attachment by pricing her departure as institutional rescue. The result is a contradiction: devotion to the group is redirected into unilateral removal from it.

## 8. Epistemic adjudication

### Established

- younger Hoshino argued with the former president over miracle/responsibility and received the old poster;
- Kaiser approached Hoshino repeatedly for two years;
- Black Suit offered nearly half the debt in exchange for Hoshino leaving Abydos and joining `our company`;
- Hoshino repeatedly refused and considered the withdrawal form during a moment of doubt;
- she promised group disclosure the next day, but the next morning was absent and left a form plus letter;
- the committee rejects planned PMC infiltration after comparing it to the earlier bank-robbery method.

### Strong inference

- Black Suit had leverage or standing over the Kaiser director;
- the offer is designed to turn Hoshino's protective attachment into coerced self-removal;
- the tearing sound concerns the poster during the flashback.

### OPEN

- who or what exactly produced the tearing sound;
- former president identity and fate;
- Black Suit's legal/organizational relation to Kaiser and Gematria;
- whether Hoshino accepted or signed the offer;
- Hoshino's destination, letter contents, and immediate plan;
- a viable noncriminal path to protect Abydos.

### Rejected

- Hoshino's disappearance proves contractual acceptance;
- Black Suit's `our company` identifies a complete corporate hierarchy;
- the director's fear proves a formal superior/subordinate relation;
- Sensei's benevolent intent removes the privacy and pressure problem;
- the committee's momentary criminal proposal means it has abandoned method constraints.
