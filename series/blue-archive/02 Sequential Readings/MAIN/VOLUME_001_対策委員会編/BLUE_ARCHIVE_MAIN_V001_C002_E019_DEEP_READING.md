## 1. The director admits a campaign against morale

Ayane locates Hoshino beneath a bunker, but the Kaiser director blocks the route. He says the committee has always been an obstacle, that he used every method, punished and made them suffer, and still watched them remain, repay debt, and live joyfully. Their persistence ruined `my plan`.

This is an important admission. Kaiser's operation was not merely neutral collection or land management. It deliberately sought behavioral surrender and treated continued joy as resistance. The students answer at the level he targets: Serika says he cannot break their hearts, Shiroko repeats the demand to return Hoshino, and Nonomi rejects a pathetic adult.

Ayane requests Sensei's direction as combat begins. The next scene shows the group at the bunker, strongly implying a successful breakthrough, but the director's precise defeat, injury, capture, or escape is not narrated.

## 2. The ordinary miracle returns as relational inheritance

Hoshino hears/remembers an unnamed senior describing their first meeting. The senior calls having a cute, strong, reliable junior nearby a miracle. Younger Hoshino replies that daily togetherness—yesterday, today, tomorrow—is ordinary and that miracles should be rarer and greater.

The senior disagrees and begins: when Hoshino someday has cute juniors of her own—. The sentence is interrupted by the present rescue. The structural bridge to the former president/Yume is extremely strong, but the voice remains `???`; identity is not promoted to explicit fact.

The callback revises `miracle` again. It is not only impossible institutional restoration or desperate hope. It is continued ordinary presence, recognized as precious because it can be lost. Hoshino now occupies the senior position the memory anticipated.

## 3. Rescue combines student action and Schale logistics

The rescue team reaches a sealed door. A speaker identifies arrival by a helicopter borrowed from Schale, and the group breaks through. The line sequence strongly identifies Ayane as the helicopter respondent, but `???` metadata prevents overconfident attribution of every adjacent turn.

Schale contributes transport/logistical access, not an adult-only extraction. The students locate, breach, call, and enter together. This continues the chapter's layered model: Sensei supplies resources and command when requested; student action remains constitutive.

Hoshino perceives regained bodily freedom and the group's voices, initially treating them as a final dream. The source does not explain the restraint mechanism, whether experimentation began, or what physiological effect occurred.

## 4. `Welcome home` restores agency through reply

Sensei addresses Hoshino. She recognizes that everyone and the adult came. The flattened narration then preserves a reunion sequence: Serika says `welcome home` first despite claiming she would not; Nonomi protests playfully; Shiroko and Ayane welcome Hoshino; the group waits for the expected reply.

Hoshino is not merely declared returned. She notices the expectation, delays in her familiar `うへ` register, frames compliance as a request from cute juniors, and answers `ただいま`. The return becomes reciprocal speech.

This matters to the possession test. Sensei and the committee overcome a coerced/deceptive contract, but the scene completes rescue through Hoshino's own responsive re-entry into relationship. Accountability promised in E016 is not yet enacted; neither is Hoshino's guilt fully resolved.

## 5. Central theses

### Joy is a form of institutional resistance

The director treated daily enjoyment as proof that suffering had not achieved its intended effect. Community persists not only through defense and paperwork but through the ability to keep living together without allowing the coercer to define the meaning of effort.

### Miracle is ordinary continuity seen from loss

The unnamed senior's account transforms Hoshino's skepticism. The rescue does not erase debt or ruin; it returns yesterday–today–tomorrow togetherness that she had treated as disposable in order to save it.

### Rescue culminates in reciprocal recognition

External force opens routes and Schale supplies logistics, but `welcome home` requires `I'm home`. The recovered person is not just an object transferred between adults; she participates in the relation that contests the contract.
