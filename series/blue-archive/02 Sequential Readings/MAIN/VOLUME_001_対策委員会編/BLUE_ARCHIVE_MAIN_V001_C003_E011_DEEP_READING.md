The Nephthys executive arrives with Suou, says Suou is guarding him as a Nephthys-side contractor, brings a higher-limit card and asks Nonomi to return to Highlander. Nonomi declines the card; Shiroko protects her from the personal pressure and asks for the topic. Suou says Nonomi's choice of Abydos nearly broke an intended Nephthys–Highlander partnership and challenges Shiroko, while the executive asks Suou not to provoke. These are their positions, not proof Nonomi was obliged to enroll or that a partnership failed solely because of her (`scene:001:u:0001-0029`). The card is brought, not accepted or exchanged.

The executive says an undisclosed **asymmetric weapon project** developed between Abydos student council and Nephthys behind the trans-desert railway. Suou names it the train gun **シェマタ**. The executive describes a planned mobile fortress gun firing a bomb over one ton up to 500 km and asserts it could deter neighboring districts, while conceding he himself was unsure it could be built. Suou supposes it was meant to repay debt through weapon utility; this is an inference, not a disclosed project purpose. Ayane describes a legendary earlier president nicknamed シェマタ and an era of 70 presidents, but neither a historical archive nor a naming record is shown (`scene:001:u:0030-0059`). The device's physical dimensions, range, origin and performance are not inspected in this unit.

Asked whether the railway was built to hide the gun, the executive denies it and places weapon planning **after** the rail business began. He guesses an unnamed adviser influenced the Abydos council, says the old project failed because its engine never ran and claims an unfinished gun lies somewhere in the desert. He then says a recent simulation made feasibility high, without showing test method, results, design or object. Suou treats this as strategic leverage. The executive says a private fund now seeks railway rights to obtain the gun, that Nephthys had joined the funding to preserve rail assets before learning of a restart, and that it later could not exit. These are insider claims with no corporate minutes or financial ledger in the scene (`scene:001:u:0060-0083`). Shiroko proposes finding and destroying the device, but its site is unknown and no search starts here (`u:0084-0091`).

The executive says Highlander CCC does not know of the gun, expects work to expose it, and says the fund is trying to assemble a formal rights position. He reports a designer also has some rights, although no instrument or identity is shown. His account of Kaiser ignorance/interest in Sanctum Tower is explicit uncertainty and hearsay. He asserts the Yume-name agreement prevents the fund from acquiring gun rights, but E006 printed only **railway facility-use rights**, not title to a gun. No chain showing that facility use conveys or blocks weapon ownership is printed. Hoshino asks what Nephthys knew about the contract and Yume's fate; the executive says they knew neither, then offers a bankruptcy-cash explanation as conjecture (`scene:001:u:0092-0113`). Around `u:0107` the **Hoshino** speaker tag carries a line apologizing on Nephthys's behalf; as with local tag drift, do not attribute it to Hoshino without corroboration.

The executive repeats the investor deadline: a noon assembly tomorrow at the old station, where cancellation **or nonattendance**, he says, voids the agreement and moves rights to Nephthys; joint purchase would leave the fund a stake. These consequences and the designer's share are uninspected legal assertions, not clauses in the E006 excerpt. Hoshino plans to attend and declare continuation, pay gradually, then find the gun. This is a proposed path, with no legal confirmation, paid installment, attendance or gun recovery yet. Suou predicts armed obstruction at the station (`scene:001:u:0114-0132`).

The next cuts directly show coordinated orders at east, west, south and north sides of the old station. The private-debt union head assigns a fluffy Helmet unit; a confiscated-goods auction head orders helicopter, tank and Market Guard positions; the pawnshop association head hires Meg, who says she will work for the amount paid; and a ZK company president directs construction vehicles into barricades. The pawnshop head calls the gun a legacy of Gehenna's Thunder Emperor, a separate unverified provenance claim that is **not** reconciled with the executive's unnamed designer. The creditor representative boasts that nonarrival by noon will hand the gun to them (`scene:002:u:0002-0003`; `scene:003:u:0002-0003`; `scene:004:u:0002-0004`; `scene:005:u:0002-0005`). These orders establish mobilization and intent; exact troop numbers, ownership of the ground and completed sealing of every approach remain unverified.

Serika wishes Sensei could help, but Suou challenges the public basis for Schale entering a purported private transaction. Hoshino accepts the visitors' own-property defense as a tactical problem; E005-E006 already show incomplete rights documentation, so this is not a legal adjudication. Suou also asks whether they would attend as the named Abydos student council or as the Countermeasures Committee, exposing the difference between the E006 contract's historic addressee and the present operating body (`scene:005:u:0006-0031`). No Sensei response or recovery after the Schale explosion is shown.

The executive offers Nonomi a transfer to Highlander, its presidency and the exercise of Nephthys authority to cancel the contract by alignment of two of three forces. She refuses to leave Abydos, and Serika explicitly rejects her sacrifice (`scene:005:u:0032-0043`). His further option is to **declare Yume and Hoshino's two-person council unauthorized**, thereby treating the contract as nonexistent and moving railway rights to Nephthys. He says Yume's show-of-hands appointment makes legitimacy challenge possible, then concedes Nonomi would find this impossible. It is a proposal, not a formal finding, vote or executed nullification. E009 gave Yume's own account of willing acceptance and the two-person council, so the offer would also erase a meaningful part of Abydos's institutional past to solve a present rights problem (`u:0044-0061`).
