## 1. Narrative reconstruction

### The committee reconstructs how lawful authority may have enabled dispossession

The land records show that only the current school building and a small surrounding area remain untransferred. Desert, former main building, and developed urban property have passed outside school ownership.

Hoshino identifies the only actor normally capable of authorizing school-asset transactions: the former Abydos student council. Ayane confirms it was the transaction principal and that transfers stopped after the council disappeared two years earlier.

This does not make the sales illegitimate on their face. It shows how formal authority under crisis can bind later students without their knowledge.

### Hoshino supplies a collapsed-institution history

Hoshino confirms she was vice president of the final council. By the time she joined, most prior members had quit, enrollment was in double digits, teachers and classes were gone, buildings repeatedly moved to escape desertification, and no meaningful handover files remained. The final council consisted only of a newly appointed president and first-year Hoshino.

She calls the president reckless and foolish and herself an unpleasant new student. The contemptuous/comic language does not erase grief or responsibility; `なんにも知らないまま` makes institutional ignorance part of her retrospective self-judgment.

Shiroko answers with a public affirmation: Hoshino created the Countermeasures Committee after dissolution and always takes the front at decisive moments despite laziness and deflection. Sensei corroborates this bounded claim. The affirmation repairs relational strain without resolving the withdrawal-form problem disclosed later.

### The debt-land trap is constructed as a strong hypothesis

The committee proposes a mechanism:

1. an already desperate school receives unmanageable Kaiser lending;
2. interest pressure makes apparently useless desert land sales seem reasonable;
3. low land value means sales do not materially reduce principal;
4. repeated transfers gradually move the district into Kaiser control;
5. after the council disappears, hired Helmet Gang pressure targets the final unsold school land.

Evidence makes this a strong working theory: Kaiser Loan is creditor; the collection record funds Helmet Gang; Kaiser Construction holds most title; the current school is the remaining property. But the original decades-long intent, exact inter-company command chain, and every inducement described by Ayane remain inference.

Sensei explicitly proposes `悪質な罠` as possibility, not certainty. When Serika condemns the earlier council as incompetent, Sensei redirects blame: deception is more culpable than being deceived. This preserves victim agency without making pressured decisions consequence-free.

Hoshino's remark that desperate people may do anything sounds personally weighted, but the episode does not state whom she means beyond the general crisis context.

### Hina's warning enters collective knowledge and produces investigation

Once Nonomi asks why anyone wants the desert, Sensei shares Hina's warning: Kaiser Corporation is plotting something in the abandoned Abydos desert. The committee now shares the information previously held by Sensei.

Serika cuts through speculative overload by asserting local standing and proposing direct observation. Shiroko, Hoshino, Ayane, and ultimately the whole group agree. The expedition is student-initiated, group-ratified, and Sensei-supported.

### Shiroko discloses evidence obtained through a privacy violation

Before departure, Shiroko privately gives Sensei a committee withdrawal form bearing Hoshino's name. She found it by searching Hoshino's bag because the unprecedented absence persisted until the group was nearly overwhelmed.

Shiroko recognizes the search as wrong and accepts possible discipline. The form is textual evidence of contemplated or prepared withdrawal, not proof of submission, final intent, timing, or cause.

Sensei and Shiroko keep it temporarily between them because too much remains unknown. This is neither cleanly exemplary nor plainly corrupt:

- the search violated Hoshino's privacy;
- the document may concern collective governance and imminent abandonment;
- immediate accusation could force interpretation before facts exist;
- secrecy withholds potentially material information from the committee.

Hoshino's closing silence does not securely establish that she overheard this exchange.

## 2. Central theses

### Thesis 1 — formal consent under desperation may be procedurally valid and substantively captured

The former council apparently possessed asset authority. The committee's theory is that debt pressure structured its options until repeated sale looked necessary.

This sharpens `BA-C016`: legitimacy cannot be inferred from a signature or competent office alone when a stronger actor may have engineered the decision field. Meaningful agency requires more than formal authorization; it requires alternatives not manufactured by coercive dependency.

### Thesis 2 — explanation must distinguish evidence from network collapse

E005 creates the strongest Kaiser convergence so far, but not identity:

- **Kaiser Loan:** creditor and collector;
- **Kaiser Construction:** documented title holder;
- **Kaiser Corporation:** committee umbrella term and Hina's named desert actor;
- **Kaiser-funded Helmet Gang:** documented subsidy after collection;
- **Kaiser PMC / Black Suit:** no new direct E005 relation.

The committee reasonably forms a coordinated-land-capture theory. The project must preserve which links are documented transactions, which are actor statements, and which are synthetic inference.

### Thesis 3 — responsible adulthood can redirect blame without monopolizing action

Sensei's most important ethical statement protects prior students from victim-blaming. Yet Serika supplies the decisive operational answer—go see—and the committee agrees. Sensei shapes moral interpretation and supports action without replacing the group's initiative.

### Thesis 4 — privacy and governance collide when hidden documents concern exit

E004 established a right not to disclose every personal secret. E005 makes the limit harder: Hoshino's bag is private, but a committee withdrawal form may directly affect shared institutional survival.

The episode does not solve the conflict. It shows why agency-preserving practice needs proportionality, verification, and a path from temporary confidentiality toward accountable clarification.

## 7. Epistemic adjudication

### Established

- transfers were executed by the former Abydos student council and ceased after its dissolution;
- Hoshino was vice president of the final two-person council after most earlier members left;
- only the current school and nearby area remain untransferred;
- Sensei tells the full committee Hina's desert warning;
- Shiroko found Hoshino's withdrawal form by searching her bag;
- the group decides to investigate the desert.

### Strong inference

- Kaiser lending, low-value land sales, Construction title, Helmet Gang funding, and desert activity form a coordinated capture pattern;
- Hoshino's withdrawal form is connected to her recent concealment;
- prior councils operated under severely constrained choice.

### OPEN

- whether Kaiser Construction is formally a Corporation affiliate despite Nonomi's phrased inference;
- original intent and command chain behind the lending/land system;
- Black Suit and PMC relation to the land project;
- exact acts of the earlier versus final student councils;
- prior president's identity/fate and `あの事件`;
- whether Hoshino signed/submitted/intends to submit the form;
- what Hoshino knows about Shiroko's search and private disclosure;
- when and how the confidentiality should end.

### Rejected

- every committee causal inference is already documentary fact;
- Hoshino personally sold the district;
- desperation eliminates prior council agency or consequence;
- the withdrawal form proves completed resignation;
- privacy makes Shiroko's search harmless;
- group risk automatically authorizes unlimited surveillance.
