## 0. Boundary

## 1. Deniable support opens, but does not own, the route

At two kilometers from contact, L118 fire strikes the forward enemy. Ayane identifies Trinity equipment; Hifumi appears under the `Faust` identity and insists the gun and shooters have no relation to Trinity. The denial is transparent cover, consistent with Nagisa's E017 constraints.

Hifumi apologizes that this is all she can do; Shiroko and Nonomi call it decisive help. Shiroko identifies the standard use of immediate assault after supporting fire, and Ayane orders the breakthrough while the enemy is confused.

The support therefore increases Abydos's practical choices without replacing its command. Hifumi supplies a corridor; Ayane and Shiroko decide how to use it. Political deniability remains ethically ambiguous, but it does not hide the recipient's operational authorship from the analysis.

## 2. The buried school is the laboratory site

At Sensei's coordinates, the committee finds school traces in the desert. The director identifies the site as the original Abydos High School main building and former center of an academy he calls once Kivotos's largest and strongest. The site confirms Hoshino's E012 account of the buried old building; the superlatives remain adversarial testimony.

The director says Gematria demanded a laboratory there. He locates Hoshino in a nearby building and speculates that the experiment may already have begun. Location is established; experiment commencement is not.

The spatial convergence is thematically exact: the institution's buried center becomes the place where an institutionally isolated student is to be turned into an object. Kaiser's available force concentrates around it for a total engagement.

## 3. Coalition interrupts recurring solitary sacrifice

The director says the committee must break through the entire force to reach Hoshino. Shiroko begins `then leave this to me—`, again moving toward the one-person holding action seen in E013.

An explosion and PS68's arrival interrupt the sentence. Aru explicitly tells Abydos to go ahead and leave this position to them. This redistributes risk across willing allies instead of requiring Shiroko to remove herself from the rescue group.

The redistribution is valuable but not frictionless. Aru immediately panics internally about what to do and considers escape; Mutsuki says the declaration leaves no route but fighting. Persona capture again produces prosocial courage while narrowing Aru's capacity to reconsider.

## 4. Reciprocity points forward

Serika refuses immediate thanks but promises ramen together after the crisis. Nonomi promises the debt will be repaid; Shiroko thanks them directly. The relationship is no longer a closed ledger of past damage. It projects a future meal and continued contact as the form of repayment.

This future orientation matters. The coalition fights not only against capture but for a social world in which the groups can meet again. Yet E018 ends before PS68's holding action or Abydos's breakthrough outcome is shown.

## 5. Central theses

### Good support creates passage

Trinity fire and PS68's holding offer do not decide what Abydos should protect. They open physical and tactical room for the committee's already-authored rescue purpose.

### Institutional memory can be occupied but not exhausted

Gematria locates its laboratory inside Abydos's buried origin. The site is both captured infrastructure and evidence that the present committee belongs to a longer history the occupiers cannot simply rename.

### Distributed risk resists sacrificial singularity

Shiroko's instinct is again `I will remain`. Coalition converts that singular burden into differentiated roles. The cost is real, and Aru's constrained commitment prevents simple celebration.
