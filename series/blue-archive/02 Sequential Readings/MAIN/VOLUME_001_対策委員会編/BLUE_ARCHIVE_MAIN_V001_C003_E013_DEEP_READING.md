The Committee discusses Sensei after the E012 broadcast. Hoshino says he has been taken to hospital, seems out of mortal danger, has not awakened, and may have escaped without a scratch; those are received reports, not direct medical observation. The group expects no teacher assistance for the immediate station attempt, doubts it can get another school involved without him, and faces a departure deadline. Nonomi has separately left on an undisclosed errand (`u:0001-0027`). The news in E012 called Sensei injured, so Ayane's `傷ひとつなかった` should not erase that broadcast or supply a diagnosis; the account is internally unsettled.

Nonomi meets Suou and the Nephthys executive after coming secretly as instructed by someone she addresses as her steward. Suou says she followed Nonomi from the school, while the executive professes surprise that Nonomi arrived and asks whether she has changed her mind about Highlander. This does not identify the original instruction's sender or establish a fully planned summons. Nonomi rejects transfer and asks Nephthys to use its ties to the fund and Highlander to save both the old council and the present Committee (`u:0028-0045`). The executive recasts her appeal as selfishness, invokes unspecified damage she allegedly caused Nephthys in middle school, says she could be enrolled by force but prefers her choice, and demands an apology before revealing another method. She apologizes under that pressure. His promised self-apology and revelation follow, but there is no accounting here of the alleged past damage or consent to transfer (`u:0046-0065`).

The executive says Nephthys expected the Committee to fight for contract continuation, even using Schale against the investor fund's soldiers. He describes a plan for Nephthys troops to exploit losses on both sides and seize the advantage; its visible fund partnership is, he says, only outward. This is unusually direct evidence of **his own stated adversarial intent**, and corrects his E011 presentation as an ally seeking to keep the gun from investors. It is still not a shown deployment or corporate order. He says the gun had been Nephthys property and would revive the group, without title or technical proof (`u:0066-0085`). Sensei's absence upsets the expected collision. The executive tells Nonomi that her arrival gives him another route; Suou says she was used (`u:0086-0093`). A cut to Hoshino calling for missing Yume and Nonomi's thought that they could have escaped suffering by leaving belongs to recalled/inner material, not simultaneous evidence that Nonomi knows Yume's final location (`u:0094-0110`).

The executive orders Suou to take Nonomi's student ID and card, calls the proposed Highlander presidency a lie, and assigns Nonomi the role of **hostage**. He claims a Nephthys board decision and promises no harm. The text prints the order and threat, but not a completed confiscation, board record or guarantee of safety. He plans to contact the Committee: if they fail to attend, he says, Nonomi transfers to Highlander; if they do attend, he wants them to declare Yume/Hoshino's Abydos student council lacked genuine organizational standing, voiding its railway agreement so Nephthys gains rights. These are coercive demands and asserted legal consequences, not an executed transfer, declaration or adjudicated title. Suou questions the treatment of a Nephthys daughter and then understands the scheme (`u:0111-0127`).

Nonomi's italic message apologizes to the Committee, calls herself unfit for Abydos because of Nephthys ties and guilt, asks them to forget her and preserve Yume's council, and expresses hope Sensei will destroy the gun. The executive follows with `……とのことです`, repeats the invalidation demand and claims her true wish is to remain with them (`u:0128-0141`). The source does not identify the exact transmission medium or prove that these distressed words reflect Nonomi's settled preference. The contradiction between self-erasure and E012's explicit commitment to the Committee is itself evidence of pressure, not a reason to dismiss either scene. Sensei's actual availability is not established by Nonomi's hope.

Hoshino heads for the assembly, saying she cannot lose another dear person. The others worry about the force there; Hoshino claims she alone can win and that the Committee will bear no responsibility, separating her own burden from the Schale-recognized Committee. Shiroko asks whether she intends to fight both investor fund and Nephthys after freeing Nonomi; Hoshino does not directly answer. Her solo assault and future violence are therefore risk and intention signals, not completed outcomes (`u:0142-0168`). Shiroko interrupts with a challenge to a contest, recalling their E012 historical recruitment terms; Hoshino accepts the prospect, while Ayane and Serika are alarmed and Serika decides she must go (`u:0169-0180`).
