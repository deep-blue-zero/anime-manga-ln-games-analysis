# BLUE ARCHIVE — MAIN V001 C002 E014 DEEP READING
## 対策委員会編 — 第14話「アビドス攻防戦（１）」

## 0. Boundary and form

This reading covers only `BA:main:001:002:014`, raw groups `12140|12145`, two canonical scenes, 136 utterance records, and no normalized choices. No E015 evidence is used. Scene 2 is only the next-episode card.

Scene 1 intercuts the committee's confrontation with the Kaiser director and Hoshino's separate exchange with Black Suit. Italicized dialogue around `u:0037-0074` marks the crosscut; information disclosed to Hoshino is not committee knowledge.

## 1. Formal nonrecognition becomes a weapon

Nonomi and Ayane distinguish ownership from coercive authority: even a landowner has no right to attack a city, the school remains Abydos's, and invasion is an unlawful act they can report to the General Student Council. The director answers with institutional abandonment. Abydos has petitioned before without action; the General Student Council currently cannot act; no other school has helped.

He then exploits a real procedural weakness. Hoshino was the last student-council member, while the Countermeasures Committee was never formally approved because no council remained to approve it. He translates missing paperwork into ontological erasure: the students are `nothing`, Abydos cannot exist autonomously, and Kaiser may rename it a corporate vocational school.

Ayane confirms the recognition defect and later despairs over what an unofficial committee can still do. The defect is established. The director's conclusion is not. The students remain present, govern emergency action, contest the attack, and have relationships capable of producing aid.

## 2. Black Suit separates himself from Kaiser

Hoshino learns that debt repayment and school seizure are compatible parts of the trap: Black Suit says his side will honor assumption of most debt while Kaiser's corporate-school plan proceeds.

He then crosses a major firewall:

- he was never affiliated with Kaiser;
- he never said `our company` meant Kaiser Corporation;
- his side cooperated with Kaiser because interests aligned;
- Kaiser's corporate-school experiment is only entertainment/side activity to him;
- his side's original target was Hoshino, her signature, and all rights concerning her.

This replaces the prior affiliation possibility with an explicit cooperation relation between differentiated actors. It does not name Black Suit's company or prove the full Gematria structure.

Black Suit also rejects the mercenary-use premise. He calls Hoshino Kivotos's greatest `mystic`, says wasting her as a resource would be undesirable, and intends to study, analyze, and understand her as an experimental subject. `Greatest` is his valuation, not objective ranking. His research intent and objectification are direct.

Hoshino concludes that an adult deceived her `again`, exposing an earlier deception history without identifying its event or actor. In apology she names the current members, `Yume-senpai`, and Sensei. Yume is now a named absent senior of high emotional significance; identifying her conclusively as the former president remains a strong but not yet explicit bridge within the present boundary.

## 3. Despair is rationally grounded but politically captured

The director mocks the committee's repayment effort as performative preparation for eventual surrender. Ayane's despair is not mere cowardice: even tactical victory leaves debt, alienated land, missing Hoshino, absent student council, and an unofficial committee. She asks what fighting can change.

Those facts make the crisis genuine. But the director uses formal weakness to make surrender appear like the only reasonable interpretation. His claim that no one will extend a hand is the episode's immediately testable proposition—and the story refutes it before the scene ends.

## 4. Problem Solver 68 returns as chosen aid

Explosions cut off northern and eastern reinforcement units. Kayoko invokes the Masked Swimsuit Gang motto; Aru interrupts the despair with `だから何なのよ`, insisting that a companion is in danger and that surrendering everything cannot be acceptable. Mutsuki and Haruka reveal pre-positioned explosives, while Kayoko explains a plan to sever reinforcements and neutralize the commander. The tactic was prepared for the Prefect Team and is repurposed against Kaiser.

PS68 says it had only come to eat ramen, linking the intervention back to Shiba Seki hospitality and the repaired E003 departure. Former enemies return voluntarily and act as allies without a new contract, official mandate, or Sensei command.

The alliance is not ethically frictionless. Mutsuki's lethal language and Haruka's extensive explosives preserve PS68's violence risk. But here the force targets an invading command structure and makes continued community resistance possible.

## 5. Sensei joins a student-authored collaboration

Aru calls for `協業` and asks whether Sensei can keep up. Sensei's internal assent closes the unit. This is not adult rescue arriving from outside: PS68 authors the intervention, Ayane has already framed the structural problem, and Sensei joins a coalition initiated by students.

The episode thereby counters the director twice. Formal institutions may have failed Abydos, yet relational history generates material assistance. And an unrecognized committee can still form legitimate defensive cooperation because recognition is not the sole source of obligation or capacity.

## 6. Central theses

### Formal invisibility is not social nonexistence

The committee lacks approval documents, but performs governance, commands loyalty, and receives voluntary aid. Paper recognition matters—it materially weakens defense—but cannot exhaust institutional reality.

### Coalition can answer abandonment without becoming sovereignty

PS68 does not acquire Abydos. It intervenes for a companion and offers a tactic. Sensei accepts collaboration rather than displacing the local group. Aid gains legitimacy from enabling resistance to seizure, not from claiming the territory.

### Deception exploits category collapse

Hoshino inferred Black Suit's `company` was Kaiser. Black Suit exploited the ambiguity while never stating it. Precise entity separation is therefore not archival fussiness; it is the condition for seeing how aligned but distinct powers divided the operation.

## 8. Epistemic adjudication

### Established

- the Countermeasures Committee lacks formal approval documents;
- the General Student Council has not answered prior Abydos petitions, per the director's uncontested account;
- Black Suit expressly denies Kaiser affiliation and states interest-aligned cooperation;
- his side targeted Hoshino from the beginning for signature, rights, study, analysis, and observation;
- PS68 interrupts Kaiser reinforcements and proposes commander neutralization;
- Sensei accepts collaboration.

### Strong inference

- Black Suit deliberately allowed Hoshino's Kaiser-company inference to stand;
- the corporate-school plan and Hoshino capture are coordinated but differently valued projects;
- Yume-senpai is the previously unnamed former president.

### OPEN

- Black Suit's company name, membership, and complete Gematria relation;
- the legal effect of missing approval documents or Kaiser's school claim;
- the earlier adult deception implied by `again`;
- Yume's exact office/identity and fate;
- whether the coalition can defeat the invasion or rescue Hoshino.

### Rejected

- Black Suit belongs to Kaiser;
- `our company` referred to Kaiser Corporation;
- Hoshino was actually acquired for ordinary PMC deployment;
- lack of formal committee recognition means the students have no collective existence or obligations;
- no one will aid Abydos;
- PS68 intervention is ordered by Sensei.
