# 1. Story placement and local chronology

E001 begins in the unresolved aftermath of E020. Haruka remains armed and affectively fixated. Ako is now speaking to Abydos after the Prefect Team's defeat, with her troops still surrounding the participants. Hoshino is still absent.

The episode moves through three political configurations:

1. **armed explanation** — Ako asks for cooperation while the Prefect Team still controls the physical perimeter;

# 2. Narrative reconstruction

## Scene 1 — Explanation under encirclement

Haruka opens by repeating `許せない` until eye contact with Sensei briefly interrupts her loop. Sensei looks away and treats the contact as something better left unacknowledged. The small exchange preserves Haruka as an unresolved danger rather than converting her into immediate comic relief.

Ako minimizes her own position as merely secretary-like support to the Prefect chair. Shiroko tests the claim against the surrounding students' visible tension. Ako responds by naming Shiroko and demonstrating prior information about Abydos's remaining five students.

Ayane corrects Ako twice. Hoshino is absent, and the surviving body is the Countermeasures Committee, not the dissolved student council. Serika nevertheless claims de facto representative standing because there is no other student council left.

Nonomi then identifies the contradiction between dialogue and coercive staging: a conversation conducted under encirclement and aimed weapons is not yet an adequate conversational posture. Ako accepts the criticism and orders the weapons lowered. The order is immediately obeyed.

Ako apologizes for the earlier action, but the corrupted Iori/Ako cluster prevents exact allocation of the next dispute. What remains secure is that the authorized tactical instruction is described as fire support within a necessary range followed by infantry deployment, not indiscriminate shooting. Another-district proximity is explicitly raised as requiring care.

Ako identifies the Prefect Team's stated purpose: arrest Gehenna students who violated Gehenna rules. She asks Abydos to understand the undesirable preceding events as not yet clearly illegal and unavoidable. Ayane refuses. Formal purpose does not answer the territorial question.

Ayane states the jurisdictional objection in stronger terms than E020: another school openly conducting combat on a different school's grounds is a clear violation from the standpoint of `自治権`. Abydos, not Gehenna, will decide PS68's disposition.

Ako notices that the committee remains firm despite the encirclement and asks whether confidence comes from a trustworthy adult. Sensei's silence precedes Ako's direct question. The three-option choice then defends PS68 without declaring it innocent.

Serika objects because Shiba Seki was destroyed. Shiroko supplies a causal rival reading from tactical logic: if PS68 intended to attack Abydos, detonating the restaurant when nobody was present was a strategically senseless, nonrepeatable act. She infers an accidental explosion followed by face-saving concealment. Serika accepts the logic provisionally. Shiroko nevertheless preserves responsibility: PS68 did attack the restaurant in effect and cannot simply be surrendered before Abydos obtains an account. Nonomi adds the still-unresolved sponsor question.

The first negotiation therefore fails without collapsing distinctions. Abydos can believe the explosion was probably accidental, insist that the harm remains real, require information, and reject foreign seizure at the same time.

Ako says peaceful resolution is no longer possible and moves toward force. Haruka interrupts by attacking the Prefect line, knocking Iori down and enabling the rest of PS68 to break the first encirclement. Her subsequent self-condemnation is immediate and extreme: she treats the failure of timing as a reason she might deserve death. Mutsuki answers with flippant conditional acceptance of blame but refuses the death conclusion.

Kayoko then identifies a deeper inconsistency. Pursuing PS68 into another autonomous district is inefficient and inconsistent with the Prefect chair's usual method; the deployed force is far larger than needed for four fugitives. Kayoko concludes that Ako planned around a confrontation with Schale.

Ako only half-confirms the accusation. She denies intentionally creating this exact situation but admits that a clash with Schale was anticipated. The initiating information came from Gehenna intelligence: Trinity's Tea Party possessed a report about Schale. Ako later checked Chinatsu's report and concluded that the President-created, adult-run, extralegal organization was a dangerous uncertainty for the prospective Gehenna–Trinity treaty.

Her proposed solution is framed politely: until the treaty is safely concluded, the Prefect Team wants to `お迎え` Sensei into its `庇護下`. PS68 would be processed incidentally. Yet the surrounding force, lack of prior request, anticipated resistance, and threatened use of force make this protection nonvoluntary custody in practical effect.

Kayoko offers Aru a chance to escape before combat makes withdrawal impossible. Aru refuses—not to protect Abydos at first, but because turning her back after such treatment would be beneath the outlaw identity she performs. Kayoko immediately notes the contradiction with Aru's recent flight, keeping the performance visible.

Serika then makes the alliance operational: PS68 should execute a pincer attack. Shiroko says they can serve as Sensei's shield, while Nonomi states the invariant objective plainly: everyone will protect Sensei.

Aru accepts and translates cooperation into reciprocity: `信頼には信頼で報いる`. Haruka adds that PS68 owes Sensei for prior help. Aru then reasserts that the restaurant explosion was intentional, and Mutsuki marks the claim as self-incriminating `墓穴`. The alliance does not repair the prior relationship or produce truthful accountability. It suspends those disputes around a common protection objective.

Ako orders the Prefect Team to suppress both groups and secure Sensei safely, emphasizing that Sensei is external to Kivotos and must not be injured. Ayane requests that Sensei command both Abydos and PS68.

# 3. Central theses

## Thesis 1 — `庇護` becomes coercive when the protected person cannot refuse

Ako's stated concern is not transparently irrational. Schale is unusual, cross-jurisdictional, adult-run, created by the missing federal president, and potentially consequential for an inter-academy treaty. Nor does she order Sensei harmed.

The legitimacy failure lies elsewhere.

Sensei is not asked whether Gehenna protection is desired. Ako arrives with overwhelming force, anticipates a clash, plans to suppress local defenders, and defines success as `安全に確保`. `お迎え` and `庇護下` therefore describe custody from the actor exercising control, not agency from the person supposedly protected.

E001 gives the Chapter 1 support/control thesis its cleanest semantic test so far:

> **Protection is not agency-preserving when the protector unilaterally decides the threat, location, duration, and acceptable means while the protected person and local institution lack a meaningful refusal.**

This strongly strengthens `BA-C016` and `BA-C017` without proving that all protective intervention is illegitimate.

## Thesis 2 — the unexpected alliance is issue-specific coordination, not reconciliation

Abydos and PS68 do not become friends in a comprehensive sense.

- Serika still calls PS68 evil because Shiba Seki was destroyed.
- Shiroko still insists PS68 cannot simply leave.
- Nonomi still wants the sponsor's identity.
- Aru still lies about the explosion.
- Haruka has not processed her misunderstanding.

What changes is narrower and politically important: both groups reject Ako's attempt to take Sensei by force. Abydos supplies the protective objective and local decision-right. PS68 supplies armed capacity and a reciprocity rationale. Sensei is asked to coordinate both.

The alliance demonstrates that coalition does not require moral homogenization. Actors can preserve unresolved accountability while coordinating around a shared limit on third-party power.

## Thesis 3 — institutional knowledge is not the same as institutional authorization

Ako's operation begins from real information work. Gehenna intelligence knows the Tea Party possesses a Schale report; Chinatsu has written a report; Ako identifies uncertainty around the federal president's unexplained institution.

Yet better information does not supply authority to seize the object of concern. Hina's closing intervention exposes the distinction inside Gehenna itself. The administrative deputy's knowledge and planning capacity do not authorize independent cross-district mobilization.

The episode therefore adds an internal version of the Chapter 1 principle:

> **An institution can possess intelligence, coercive capacity, and a plausible risk theory while still lacking the authorization required for the action it takes.**

## Thesis 4 — Sensei is simultaneously protected object and delegated coordinator

Ako initially treats Sensei as something to be secured. Abydos and PS68 instead keep Sensei inside a field of reciprocal relations:

- Sensei evaluates PS68 rather than being spoken for completely;
- Abydos refuses custody;
- PS68 cites prior help and reciprocal trust;
- Ayane requests Sensei's command rather than presuming it;
- the combined force remains composed of student institutions with distinct motives.

Sensei's agency is not total—the available choice cannot accept Ako's proposal or independently terminate the battle—but the allied structure is still more agency-preserving than Ako's custody plan because it treats Sensei as a participant whose coordination is requested.
