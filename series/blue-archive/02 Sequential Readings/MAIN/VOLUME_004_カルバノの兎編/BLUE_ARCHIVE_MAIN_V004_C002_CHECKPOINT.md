# BLUE ARCHIVE — MAIN V004 C002 CHECKPOINT
## カルバノの兎編, Chapter 2: the limits of institutional restoration

## 0. Authority and method

This checkpoint reconciles the 24 sequential readings from `BA:main:004:002:001` to `BA:main:004:002:024` at the audited `a038020f` source. It replaces their provisional chapter-level synthesis while retaining the episode readings as evidence records. The preceding [V004 C001 checkpoint](BLUE_ARCHIVE_MAIN_V004_C001_CHECKPOINT.md) remains authority for Chapter 1. Source IDs, locators and the V1-to-current drift are documented in the [source reconciliation](../../../01%20Source%20Lock%20and%20Inventory/BLUE_ARCHIVE_SOURCE_RECONCILIATION_20260928.md); the current main denominator is 480. Episode order is canonical order, not an inference about exact elapsed time. Side stories, profiles, MomoTalk, voice performance, adaptations and wikis have not entered this synthesis.

The chapter tests whether SRT's ideal can survive when school restoration is offered through an abusive chain of command. It supplies a bounded answer: RABBIT leaves an untenable park protest for FOX's resources and affiliation, but Miyako refuses an attack on civilians, her squad returns by choice, and they prevent the immediate station plan with help from Sensei, Kanna, Life Safety and Decartes. Public resolution grants park residence and credits Kanna while keeping the SRT intervention obscure. The ending does not reopen SRT, audit all corrupt transactions or finish the warhead/court record.

## 1. The chapter's proposition and unsafe reductions

Yukino's dream-framed past depicts SRT justice and alleged Defense/Kaiser evidence before the waking commander calls it a `甘い悪夢` (E001). Its file contents are not shown. Her present operation incapacitates opposition, sabotages rail and positions FOX for Kaya while senior members object to civilian harm yet comply (E001). Thus the nostalgic institution and present chain of command are different evidentiary objects. FOX's promise to restore SRT attracts RABBIT amid food, hygiene and equipment need, but Kaya explicitly defers restoration until she controls the council (E010), then orders a civilian subway detonation (E013).

RABBIT's commitment to justice is not proved by its initial refusal of FOX in E005: it *does* enter FOX command in E012. Nor is it erased by that choice: Miyako's uncertainty survives the transfer, and E016 makes her rejection of the station mission explicit. The other three return voluntarily in E019, rather than being ordered back by Sensei. The resulting operation stops the immediate threat and FOX surrenders in E022. It would flatten the chapter to call FOX uniformly cynical, RABBIT unwavering, or adult trust alone sufficient. Niko's kindness and route disclosure, Kurumi/Otogi's refusal to abandon Yukino, and Yukino's eventual surrender all complicate those readings without undoing their earlier participation.

The external political ending also has levels. Kaya's no-confidence/succession broadcast is observed but its tally and presidential-letter authority are not opened (E006–E008). Her own admissions establish expelled Rin supporters and Kaiser sanction bargaining (E010). RABBIT directly detains her, and a recording challenges her denial to Heine (E023). E024 reports impeachment proceedings/prosecution, Rin's return and rebate findings; it does not quote the judgment, trial file, corporate books or custody chain. The public gives Kanna credit and does not identify RABBIT/FOX's roles. This is a chapter resolution of the immediate coup and station attempt, with deliberately incomplete public and institutional knowledge.

## 2. Prospective sequence and discriminating tests

### E001–E006: the seniors' trap and a divided loyalty

FOX's opening scene gives Yukino's idealized SRT memory, contemporary coercive orders and objections from Kurumi/Niko, not a verified historical Defense dossier. RABBIT's own FOX hero memory is juxtaposed with its present hunger and park life (E002). Sensei's beef meal is actually shared in E003; subsequently Schale footage shows masked people carrying an unidentified extra load, and apparent SRT gear draws RABBIT's suspicion. Their private trace, not direct object identification, leads to a rainy tactical site in E004. Yukino ambushes Miyako, and all four are captives by E005. FOX frames the action as a test, offers food and a place under its command, yet refuses to explain the Schale removal or federal attack. RABBIT's silence is no assent. Sensei finds them and secures a local delay; the squad later discloses its withholding and apologizes (E006). No uninterrupted Schale-item chain or final FOX employment decision exists at this stage.

### E006–E012: Kaya's office and the material bargain

Kaya publicly claims Rin's displacement and acting succession (E006). E007 rewinds to the meeting: Rin rejects unilateral repression as a `毒の入った聖杯`, while Kaya offers a purported presidential letter and suspension rule. E008 places Sensei with the newly installed acting office: Kaya admits unauthorized Schale access/removal and reliance on Kaiser, then proposes council control of Schale and transfer of even misconduct liability; Sensei refuses. Authenticity of the letter, exact item route and legal tally remain uninspected. The midnight order gives Kaiser Security practical street policing, with document burdens, detention threats and deployment of corporate force (E009). Kaya acknowledges expelling Rin supporters and trading Kaiser sanction relief for assistance (E010). Her administration cannot readily answer the reported cash-train hijack; Finance rejects defective claims, and Red Winter protest turns against her. E011 shows a resident actually delayed from hospital transit until Kanna assembles an accepted exception, while Kaiser's crime-reduction advertisement lacks independent data.

The same pressure makes FOX's offer concrete. Sensei finds Miyako's letter and an empty camp in E012. At the secret training ground Miyako declares RABBIT's independent command dissolved into a `RABBIT支隊`, and Yukino accepts. This is a completed local command transfer, not public SRT reopening or advance consent to Kaya's unseen next order. Miyako's letter says she is grateful to Sensei but still unsure of her own conviction.

### E013–E016: the station order and refusal

Kaya orders a thermobaric warhead moved to the station, detonated beneath civilians, falsely framed as an accident and used to displace Traffic, in exchange for Kaiser rail rights (E013). Yukino warns of civilian and neighboring-station harm, then accepts the FOX1 order; physical transport is not yet shown. Sora's branch food-order disruption remains her report, while Niko/Otogi/Kurumi bring Sensei inari and challenge the teacher's trust (E014). Niko independently reveals RABBIT's secret route. RABBIT actually uses the camp's showers, gear and training but Miyu, Saki and Moe each question the loss of their own judgment (E015). Sensei reaches the camp and is interrogated; Miyako allows a quiet exit and is pressed about her own responsibility (E016). Yukino then briefs her on the planned platform delivery and underground detonation. Miyako refuses a civilian attack. Her claim that only the missing president could lawfully command SRT is a character legal assertion, not a charter text opened here. At that point the other three's complete knowledge and decision are not yet printed.

### E017–E022: distributed intervention and the ignition threat

Miyako returns alone to the park, briefs Sensei and reaches the station at 1030 (E017); the earlier scheduled 0900 delivery is not independently confirmed by that arrival. Decartes, then a Kaiser guard by his report, turns to protect the people he identifies as his comrades and summons 所確幸. His premature charge creates only an imperfect diversion. Kanna, still under Public Security suspension by her account, coordinates Kirino/Fubuki and invokes a Life Safety temporary-detention rule; their route and smoke get Miyako onto the platform (E018). The rule itself is not opened. A rail-linked hangar exists beneath the station, though Miyako's account of its commercial purpose is inference (E019).

Saki, Moe and Miyu return of their own accord, affirm their shared SRT justice and join Miyako's `カルバノグの兎` operation (E019). Otogi confirms downing the drone that Miyako had attributed to her; Kurumi and Otogi are overcome, but exact incapacitation mechanics are partly omitted (E020). Moe breaches the elevator door and RABBIT rappels. Yukino threatens to press a purported self-destruct device if they enter (E021); Saki's bluff reading is unsupported at that point. E022 confirms Yukino privately intended to press it, then prints an explosion of unspecified origin that knocks the ignition device loose. Sensei secures the device. FOX seniors choose to share Yukino's risk against her retreat order, and Yukino eventually declares surrender after Miyako's challenge and Sensei's answer about learning and bearing responsibility tomorrow. The thermobaric station blast is not narrated. The warhead's final physical disposal and official custody are not printed.

### E023–E024: prosecution reported, park authorized, school still closed

Kaya expects station explosion news, receives the General's FOX-failure report and discovers her policing suspensions/orders reduce her own reinforcements (E023). Miyako secures her and invokes an emergency arrest exception; the asserted provision is not independently opened. Kaya offers Sensei resources and Schale autonomy, while the teacher redirects her toward apology to the harmed students. Heine's earlier belief is challenged by a FOX-provided recording and she demands impeachment, before any chapter-local formal vote is inspected.

Mai reports the attempted bombing, Kaya's impeachment trial/prosecution, a Kaiser Security rebate finding and Kaiser's subsidiary-blame position (E024). Her report is a canonical depiction of a public account, not an inserted legal or corporate file. Decartes's on-air bento story about Kaya and denial of SRT involvement are misleading against the direct E008/E014/E023 sequence. Niko is directly shown in Correction Bureau custody and the other FOX members join the conversation. Miyako's letter says Rin returned as acting president, restrictive orders receded, Kanna received public credit and early Public Security reinstatement, Kirino declined a Guard transfer, and RABBIT received formal permission to live at the park. The four are also seen using or seeking Schale shower access. The letter's administrative claims remain report-level; its park authorization is distinct from reopening SRT. Intercut letter, Schale and prison voices in scene 3 cannot be assigned one physical location.
