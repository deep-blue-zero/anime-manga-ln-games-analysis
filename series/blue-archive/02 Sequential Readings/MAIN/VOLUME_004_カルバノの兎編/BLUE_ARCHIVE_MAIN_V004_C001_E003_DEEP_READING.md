## 1. Saki's strict ideal and admitted exception

Record `977-R-1` names Valkyrie third-year **Ogata Kanna** interrogating SRT first-year **Sorai Saki** (`scene:001:u:0001-0003`). Kanna's “mad dog” warning is self-described reputation; Saki says SRT trained her for capture and confidential-information restraint (`scene:002:u:0002-0015`). Kanna reports GSC files showing nearly perfect written/practical performance and no school-rule violation, not a reproduced transcript (`u:0016-0019`). Saki distinguishes SRT's regulated daily training and maintenance from even Public Security's off-duty freedom and chooses proud “wolf” over “dog” as her own identity metaphor (`u:0020-0027`). The language is Saki's valuation, not a measured comparison of the schools' service quality.

Kanna then challenges why Saki did not stop Moe's relay-drone ammunition waste. Saki admits training never allowed wasteful firing and she too wanted to try it once (`u:0028-0033`). This revises an easy E002 account in which Moe alone corrupted a perfectly rule-bound Saki: the same student who argues constant discipline also desires an exception. It neither proves Saki commanded every round nor validates Moe's belief that weapons may be used without limit. The narrator spells Saki's name and stamps `不適格` (`u:0034-0038`), without a sanction explained.

## 2. Moe and Miyu resist transfer for different reasons

Record `977-R-2` names first-year **Nakatsukasa Kirino** questioning first-year **Kazekura Moe** (`u:0039-0042`). Moe wants the interview over before her sweets run out. Asked why she occupied the park rather than surrender, she says she did not want to leave SRT. Kirino initially supplies a comrade-memory explanation, but Moe rejects it, complains of Saki's nagging and Miyu's crying, and says access to SRT's heavy weapons is what she wants (`u:0043-0063`). Missile/armor descriptions are Moe's claims, not an inventory audit. Kirino insists SRT as well as Valkyrie should protect civilians; her inward recollection of an indiscriminate-fire shopping-street incident is unverified hearsay (`u:0064-0068`). Moe answers “yes” to whether citizen safety means nothing to her (`u:0069`). This is direct provocative speech in an interview, not proof she has no future protective impulse. `不適格` follows (`u:0070-0075`).

Record `977-R-3` names first-year **Nemugaki Fubuki** and first-year **Kasumizawa Miyu** (`u:0076-0078`). Miyu volunteers her sniper role, age 15, July 12 birthday and pebble-hunting hobby, then fears imagined torture; Fubuki says that will not happen (`u:0079-0088`). Miyu clarifies that transfer frightens her **not because Valkyrie is dangerous**, but because fresh social ties must be built and she fears being forgotten/unrecognized. Miyako sometimes cares for her even though RABBIT is not uniformly close (`u:0089-0100`). She says she entered SRT hoping to change this trait; this is self-report, not diagnosis. Fubuki suggests Life Safety as a small, quiet unit but acknowledges citizen complaints and contact, after which Miyu declines (`u:0101-0108`). The suggestion is not admission, acceptance or actual transfer. `不適格` follows (`u:0109-0113`).

## 3. Miyako's justice ideal and Sensei's limited response

Record `977-R-4` lists **Schale Sensei** as interviewer and first-year **Tsukiyuki Miyako** as subject (`u:0114-0116`). Paired introductory choices converge; Miyako initially treats Sensei's welfare question as possible exchange for information and says her SRT loyalty will not change (`u:0117-0127;choice:001-002`). She explicitly rejects “I hate Valkyrie” as the reason for refusing transfer: leaving SRT is the loss (`u:0128-0130`). To her, SRT uniquely pursued `正義` as consistent, truth-grounded principle regardless of school interests; she believes other Kivotos security bodies bend justice under their entanglements (`u:0131-0142`). That is a substantive institutional ideal and a self-attributed motive, not an audited description of all SRT operations or proof her own park tactics met it.

Miyako blames herself for not bringing out the squad's strengths before closure and compares herself unfavorably with Sensei's short, successful counteroperation. She rebuffs praise from someone barely known (`u:0143-0148;choice:006-007`). Asked what she needs, she requests reversal of SRT closure. Sensei says one adult cannot immediately do that; she vows to keep fighting even with thin chances, preserving SRT's name while they do not give up (`u:0149-0153;choice:008-010`). The record ends `保留` (`u:0154-0159`), not a release order.

Kanna thanks Sensei and forecasts detention, GSC punishment because of strong council demands and serious damage, possibly no school transfer in the worst case (`scene:003:u:0002-0006`). The damage and council pressure are her report; no file or ruling is printed. She says GSC decided closure and even Schale's exceptional authority may not reverse it (`u:0007-0010`). Sensei suggests asking the council. A new **Kaya**, self-identified as Federal Student Council Defense Office member, enters to answer (`u:0011-0014`). No answer or new policy is yet given. `u:0015` previews E004 `別側面の事情`.
