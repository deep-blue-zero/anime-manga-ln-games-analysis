## 3. The third-book account and threatened revelation

Kokuriko says 花鳥風月部 originally had three books: `稲生物怪録` reflecting desire, `稲亭物怪録` reflecting fear and `稲生霊怪録` reflecting **people themselves** (`u:0061-0064`). These are three distinct Japanese titles. The first two categories fit E023 Azami's explicit contrast and the prior visible uses, while the third category is new **Kokuriko testimony** without a demonstrated rule here. She says Shuro thought the third vanished on the Great Snowfield twenty years ago, then qualifies that “lost” is partly true because it lost original form and was forcibly reconstructed from scattered parts (`u:0065-0067`). The provenance, restorer, present binding/location and relation to the E024 restored blank *稲生物怪録* remain uninspected; do not merge the two restorations because their titles and claims differ. Nor does this yet explain Shuro's personal origin or the Ayame shadow's mechanics.

Nagusa says she will not retreat regardless of book number, invokes promises before Kuzunoha/Sensei/Hyakka and vows not to leave Ayame alone, then asks Kokuriko where Ayame is (`u:0068-0071`). The episode has not shown a new vow uttered in one formal scene; this is Nagusa's present summary of commitments across earlier encounters. Kokuriko calls Kuzunoha, a perfect chair and unfailing friendship fictions, then threatens to show a reality everyone averts their eyes from (`u:0072-0075`). Her rhetoric is interested attack, not adjudication of the temple meeting, Ayame's true identity or whether friendship ever existed. Sensei inwardly warns the group (`u:0076`); no third-book activation, vision, strike or rescue is printed before the `暗く、深い影の底から` teaser (`u:0077`).
