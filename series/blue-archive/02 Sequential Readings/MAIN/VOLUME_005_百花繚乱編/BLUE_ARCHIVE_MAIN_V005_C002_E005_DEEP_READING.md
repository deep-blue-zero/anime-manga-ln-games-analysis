## 1. Nagusa dates the search and remembers a painful refusal

Nagusa says Ayame entrusted her with 百蓮 and went north to seek Kuzunoha about ten months ago; unable to accept the acting chair, Nagusa later followed alone across the Great Snowfield and found the ruined 黄昏の寺院 (`scene:001:u:0001-0014`). `10か月くらい前` is her approximate chronology, not a dated travel record. She remembers hoping Ayame would welcome her and resume the chair. The represented Ayame instead asks why she came, refuses to return without meeting Kuzunoha and criticizes Nagusa for thinking she understood her motives (`u:0015-0036`). Nagusa interprets this as an unfamiliar coldness. Her own claim that Kuzunoha was only legend and that Ayame sought advice to protect everyone is exposed as **her inference about Ayame**, not an established account of Ayame's reason. Ayame neither explains that reason nor agrees with the protective-duty account in this unit.

Kokuriko then appears in the remembered encounter, belittles Nagusa, calls them both travelers with different destinations and says she knows a route toward “there,” while claiming she herself cannot touch the far-shore boundary (`u:0037-0046`). Ayame asks to be guided. When Nagusa calls Ayame her friend and begs Kokuriko not to take her, represented Ayame says she never considered Nagusa a friend (`u:0047-0050`). This gives a firsthand **Nagusa testimony** basis for the line Shuro used in C001 E023 and Nagusa dreamed in C002 E001. It does not independently certify Ayame's enduring beliefs, why she spoke so harshly, whether Kokuriko influenced her, or whether the exact remembered words are complete. The juniors explicitly find the turn inconsistent with their known Ayame (`u:0051-0055`); Nagusa herself says she considered whether it was a bad dream.

Nagusa says her right arm was swallowed together with Ayame, identifies the wound as proof to herself that the event was real, and says she collapsed afterward (`u:0056-0060`). This considerably strengthens E004's broad 黄昏 association and supports an injury during the same encounter; it does **not** show the full swallowing event, the exact physiology of the visibly present arm, or the visitor's sole-cure prognosis. Shuro's C001 E023 claimed sequence (“rejected reach” causing the swallowed arm) is only partly aligned: rejection and injury are in Nagusa's account, but the causal link and physical mechanics are not independently demonstrated. Nagusa reports a rescue while unconscious in a deserted snowfield, then darkly resists Yukari's “luck” interpretation (`u:0061-0064`).

## 2. A Kuzunoha-labeled rescuer and an uninspected scroll

The represented aftermath has a `クズノハ`-tagged speaker addressing Nagusa after she wakes. The speaker says they maintained her strength but cannot cure her here, and tells her to go home (`u:0065-0070`). Nagusa sees winter blossoms and the speaker says time flows differently in the 黄昏の寺院, where spider lilies and cherry blossoms may bloom together (`u:0071-0074`). These are the speaker's explanation of the setting, not an independent time measurement. Nagusa asks if this is the great prophet Kuzunoha; the speaker answers conditionally, asking what Nagusa would want if so, and refers to her as a Hyakka successor (`u:0075-0078`). Nagusa rejects that identity because she believes the real founding chair would not have ignored Ayame's calls while rescuing her. The speaker does not settle the identity challenge, instead saying interpretation is Nagusa's choice (`u:0079-0084`). The source tag and her denial coexist; no authentication, impostor reveal, or identity resolution is supplied.

The speaker gives Nagusa a scroll to deliver to Schale's Sensei and says it contains a way to save “someone” (`u:0084-0088`). The scroll text, intended beneficiary and successful handoff are **not shown here**. C001 E001 had a Kuzunoha-labeled warning/invitation in a temporally unlocated Sensei thought sequence; this memory does not prove that sequence's date, that it depicts this handoff, or that the scroll addresses the same rescue. It does add a concrete reported object and a second Sensei-directed contact route. Nagusa says this is all she saw. Her immediate closing summary says she was rejected, could not save Ayame and lost useful right-arm function, then admits she hid the event from fear that colleagues would be disappointed (`u:0089-0093`). The fear is her motive report, not evidence that the others actually condemned her.

## 3. The juniors reject her self-condemnation; Niya summons them
