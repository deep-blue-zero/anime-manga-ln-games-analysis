The chapter asks whether a committee can continue when its acting leader feels like a fraud, its juniors feel abandoned, a family expects a student to perform a duty she did not choose, and an antagonist turns those fears into a public destructive narrative. The supported answer is local and conditional: Yukari and Nagusa choose to keep serving Hyakka with help from Kikyou, Renge, Sensei and other clubs; the group stops Shuro's immediate attempt, but she escapes and Onmyou puts dissolution **on hold** rather than cancelling it. Kokuriko states future hostile intent. No mechanism or administrative detail should be inferred past those limits.

## 1. The chapter's proposition and unsafe reductions

Nagusa's `コスプレ`/`演技` language begins as shame. She says she performed an ideal senior to stand beside Ayame, then feared that colleagues expected her to replace the missing chair (E001/E016/E021). Shuro's book display makes that fear material (E019), yet her conclusion that every affection was false is contradicted by Kikyou's explicit E018 wish for **Nagusa herself**, Yukari's first wanted choice to join Hyakka (E014/E023), and Nagusa's concrete care in the selected E024 memories. Sensei's answer does not prove that performance and concealment are harmless: he and Yukari expressly allow that they hurt others, then commit to the work of reconciliation (E023–E024). Nagusa's E024 request for help changes her practice from isolated role-playing to a shared acting-chair effort. It does not authenticate an unprinted appointment or cure her arm.

Yukari's agency also cannot be flattened into Shuro's rumors. E012 shows her choosing Hyakka against a festival miko request; E013 wounds her, E014–E015 show her retreating to household duty under pressure, and E020's servant frames the role as repairing an inherited family `汚点`. Shuro claims to have planted succession/youth/return prompts (E020), and the mapped concerns are real, but the delivery/degree of control is not independently shown. Yukari awakens, admits continued hurt, refuses Shuro's proxy claim, desires repair, loses an observed succession bout without resentment and later reports telling her household she will leave miko duty (E024–E026). These first-person choices do not require pretending the earlier hurt was imaginary or that every formal family consequence is settled.

The supernatural and institutional endings are similarly bounded. Ordinary attacks fail against Kurokage and Shuro, and she claims only Ayame can hurt them (E019–E020). A later attack actually lands after Nagusa asks for help (E024). That refutes the asserted practical invulnerability in this encounter, but the chapter does **not** show a 百蓮 handoff, a ritual qualification test or a complete new rule. Shuro claims in E023 to have completed a city-burning 百物語; in E026 she admits to Kokuriko that she failed, and Kokuriko accepts that failure. The burning streets, apparent monsters and katashiro are still real chapter events. The intended completed work is not thereby established. Shuro escapes after tearing her book (E025), with Onmyou losing the trail outside Hyakkiyako (E026). Yukari's E024 arrest declaration is not an effected arrest.

## 2. Prospective sequence and discriminating tests

### E001–E008: return, succession pressure and missing colleagues

Nagusa interrupts Arata's troublemaking group, says she is no longer Hyakka and has returned an unnamed `アレ` (E001). Niya invites Sensei into a festival that marks twenty years since an earlier interrupted rite. Yukari tours the district with Sensei, recalls Hyakka's mediation ideal and the gun/acting-chair succession, while Onmyou discusses an unstable committee and a purported challenge procedure (E002–E007). An anonymous warning attributed to 花鳥風月部 first enters as a letter; Shuro later self-attributes it, but the original dispatch and authority are not authenticated. Renge is found with Shugyoubu, while Kikyou's strategist role and Nagusa's absence become the practical succession obstacles (E008). Early rumor, legal manual report and student hopes have different evidentiary grades.

### E009–E015: the seniors' refusals and Yukari's household retreat

Renge directly refuses Yukari's request and describes Ayame's disappearance and Nagusa's leaving as the committee's break (E009). Yukari persists, but a family servant calls her home and Sensei separately meets the anonymous former member later confirmed as Nagusa (E010–E011). Shizuko asks the Kadeno family for a festival miko. She says records assign a dance twenty years earlier yet it never happened. Kikyou refuses Yukari's planned challenge against Nagusa, says Nagusa returned Ayame's `証`, and declares a **different** contest for her strategist seat; its fight is skipped and no formal office result is documented (E012–E013). Kikyou speaks cruelly about Yukari's Hyakka membership while trying to spare her a further loss, and Yukari returns toward household duty (E013–E014). Her private account that meeting Nagusa revealed something she *wanted*, unlike inherited obligation, remains evidence of a sincere Hyakka wish. Shuro claims authorship of the earlier letter, Shizuko reports Yukari's miko decision, and Sensei asks the Ninja Research Club to find Nagusa (E015). The servant/household does not yet pronounce a formal resignation.

### E016–E020: public crisis, incomplete testimony and Shuro's causal boast

Nagusa admits she tried to return the `証` and considers herself an unfit deputy; E018 shows her offering it to Kikyou, but only E021 directly displays Kikyou holding `百蓮`. E016's black-square text stays absent. The festival opening brings attacks, fires and injury reports (E016–E018). Nagusa distinguishes smaller `幻魎付喪神` from the large `幻魎百物語`; she and Shuro identify a catlike Kurokage, against which ordinary gunfire fails (E018–E019). Shuro uses `稲生物怪録` to expose Nagusa's fear of being a counterfeit; Kikyou and Renge witness it but do not thereby validate all Shuro's conclusions (E019). In E020 Shuro says she planted distinct prompts for Yukari, Renge and Kikyou and wanted Yukari as the next wounded protagonist. These are her causal claims; student actions also have independent direct motives. A servant reports that the miko assigned twenty years before vanished immediately before dancing, with the retained record still unseen and cause unknown. Yukari questions whether she is valued only to erase family shame; an unattributed, Shuro-like voice urges revenge, without assent shown.

### E021–E023: Ayame account, broadcast coercion and guilt assignments

Nagusa gives first-person testimony that Kokuriko, 花鳥風月部 director, turned Ayame into a `百物語` and took her beyond `黄昏` at the Great Snowfield (E021). It is stronger than rumor but is not an incident replay or proof of Ayame's death/present state. Shuro then hijacks an Onmyou broadcast, calls silent Yukari its protagonist, narrates real hurts into a universal “nobody cares” claim and threatens the district (E021). Kikyou directly holds `百蓮`, says Nagusa left it with her and asks Nagusa herself to save Yukari while still admitting resentment; Renge also wants to apologize. Nagusa agrees to try. Niya, Chise and the Ninja Research Club help open the route (E022). At the stage, Yukari sees burning streets, hears Shuro call herself her `代理人`, and adopts guilt for a faceless `無貌の形代`. The source shows her distress and local damage, **not** that she authorized the fire or caused the entity through a thought. Shuro's claimed origin mechanism and labels must not be silently fused with Kurokage. E023's purported Ayame never-friend quotation and rejected rescue reach are only Shuro's taunt; Nagusa protests part of the story. A book display contains Yukari's heir duties and sincere Hyakka longing, then Nagusa's self-blame. Shuro declares a finished 百物語 and Sensei moves toward Yukari while contesting the idea that hidden fear makes a person worthless.

### E024–E026: repair intent, local victory and a held institution

Sensei recalls Nagusa's specific attention to the three juniors and says a performed role can become real to others, while quarrels require repair (E024). Yukari wakes, says she still hurts, and chooses reconciliation. She and Nagusa both explicitly seek help from the group; Renge/Kikyou arrive and fight. An attack on Shuro lands despite her earlier absolute rhetoric; Renge clears immediate numbers, Kikyou breaks the floor under Shuro and Yukari says the katashiro is no longer here. The absence is local and its mechanism uninspected. Shuro concedes this round but tears her book and escapes in E025. Yukari's immediately accepted succession challenge ends with Nagusa the acknowledged winner; it is a represented bout, not a printed office-transfer instrument. Yukari continues to admire Nagusa and calls Hyakka home. Kikyou and Nagusa still hesitate over a direct conversation.

E026 depicts community repairs and a resumed festival, with lanterns actually flowing. Niya reports the committee's dissolution is `保留` because membership attrition still threatens its future; an activity-funding cut is her possible example, not an enacted order. She lost Shuro's trail outside the district and promises an archive search. Yukari reports telling household servants she will stop miko duty, after repeated thought, and says they understood; no family head decree or dance performance is shown. The four gather for an initial patrol but take part in lanterns before a patrol route is printed. Nagusa's right arm remains impaired. In the final cut Shuro directly admits she failed to make the intended 百物語, and Kokuriko accepts that while claiming the twenty-year festival was reenacted and inviting gradual construction of another, world-burning story. This is an open Chapter 2 threat, not a completed capability.
