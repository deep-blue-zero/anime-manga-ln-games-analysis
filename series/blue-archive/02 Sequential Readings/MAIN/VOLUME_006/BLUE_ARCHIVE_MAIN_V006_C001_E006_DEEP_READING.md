## 2. Maia's beauty outside does not erase exclusion there

At Arius, Subaru calls for applause for a returning friend, Maia. Maia is worn down, and Subaru says her halo is intact and that there is still time to rebuild what was lost; she welcomes her back (`u:0050-0059`). The intact-halo observation is Subaru's, not a medical clearance or a statement that Maia has received treatment after E001's coughing and cold. Her return is now witnessed by community applause, but the scope of that community and its material capacity remain unshown.

Invited to describe the outside in her own words, Maia does **not** say it was simply bad. She describes glass buildings, lit river bridges, a purpose-built music hall, food markets she could only observe/smell, and clean water used for recreation (`u:0060-0080`). She recalls being able to play music at Arius only secretly while Madam was absent; that is her private-life history, not proof of current policy after Madam's departure. The described architecture and leisure are her testimony; no city/site is named or independently visited in this cut. Beauty made her aware that people spend time and resources for reasons beyond bare survival and profit, but her limited ability to buy food or belong remains explicit.

Maia says nowhere outside felt like a place she could be. Her picture of collapsing unnoticed in a city corner is a feared possibility—**not an observed collapse** (`u:0081-0089`). Subaru recognizes the glare of other people's comfort and reasons that some people elsewhere must live without it (`u:0090-0093`). Their critique of “you can do anything” is about being offered freedom without guidance or means. Maia says nobody taught her how to live outside; Subaru calls the maxim “live as you please” irresponsible under those conditions (`u:0094-0099`). This is a substantive argument about unsupported choice, not proof that every outside person rejects Arius students or that return is the only ethical route. Subaru says many have come back after leaving, but no names/counts or follow-up records are given (`u:0100-0102`).
