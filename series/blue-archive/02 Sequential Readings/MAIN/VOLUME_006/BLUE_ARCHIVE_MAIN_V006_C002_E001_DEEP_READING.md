## 1. Sensei asks the Rescue Knights to conceal their school

Mine repeats that she sensed a need for rescue, while Subaru and Maia question what that means. Sensei gathers the parties; Hanae now speaks directly, confirming more than E012's Sensei-only naming (`scene:001:u:0001-0014`). Maia recognizes them as the **Trinity Rescue Knights**, and Atsuko/Serina recall a prior Fourth Sanctum encounter without a complete account here. Misaki's Sakurako reference is quickly deflected toward exam busyness; Sakurako is not present (`u:0015-0021`). Mine asserts aid must share priority with the approaching exam, trusts her team to manage both and claims she herself remains top of the class; no marks, schedules or certification are opened (`u:0022-0027`).

Sensei asks them to omit Trinity and Rescue Knights labels locally (`choice:005`, `u:0028-0044`). This is a **cover request**, not a factual change of allegiance. Serina infers it might protect the aid work and persuades a reluctant Mine, invoking a religious maxim about hidden charity. Mine says her order is not Sisterhood and would rather proclaim aid openly, but agrees to suppress the names. Serina suggests calling her `先輩` instead of `団長`; Mine accepts and warms to the more intimate address (`u:0030-0056`). No one is compelled by a written order, and no larger Trinity authorization is shown. Maia already knew the affiliation before the cover; later Subaru recognizes it independently, so concealment is incomplete even locally.

Saori restates the agreed public story as a goodwill volunteer group visiting Arius to spread rescue spirit. Serina worries about working beside Arius Squad; Atsuko calls Squad Arius students yet presently outsiders, and Misaki suggests their standing may be worse. Hanae's cheerful “all solved” is premature (`u:0057-0068`). Atsuko jokingly asks to keep Hanae because her brightness helps, causing Serina/Hiyori alarm; an Atsuko-tagged `u:0076` sounds like Hanae's bewildered question, and Atsuko explicitly withdraws it as a joke (`u:0069-0079`). No transfer or custody offer is actually concluded. Maia finds the group's energy overwhelming. Mine announces a temporary aid station as the first task of the newly named `名もなき奉仕活動部` (`u:0080-0085`). This is an improvised cover name and activity, not an inspected registered school club.

## 2. The station exists; Subaru recognizes who built it

Scene 002 is explicitly located at `臨時救護所`. Hanae says construction is complete, Serina thanks Sensei/Mine, and Hanae says they are practiced (`scene:002:u:0002-0005`). The setting and dialogue support a **completed temporary aid station**, though no patient, treatment, supplies inventory, staff authorization or occupancy is shown. Subaru approaches the “volunteers,” asks why they came, and notes the thin “premonition of need” explanation. Sensei catches two near-disclosures before they finish speaking (`u:0006-0022`). Subaru frames their arrival as reckless pity amid unknown danger, a suspicion rather than a proven motive.

Mine answers that providing more than enough aid to relieve suffering is her order's purpose regardless of danger or outside appearance (`u:0023-0033`). She calls it the volunteers' principle while respecting the cover. Subaru then names their Trinity Rescue Knights identity. She says she recognizes a Trinity “air” and knows an Arius/Trinity common-school history, but no method or record of identification is inspected (`u:0034-0049`). She will not start a fight if they stay discreet and are not agents of the Tea Party or Sisterhood, and Mine agrees to that condition (`u:0041-0053`). This is **conditional local tolerance**, not community-wide consent, a legal permit or verified organizational independence.

Subaru mistakenly concludes the Rescue Knights' captain is absent. Serina and Mine play along rather than disclose that Mine is standing there (`u:0054-0060`). Subaru repeats a rumor of the captain's inflexible charge ahead; Mine objects in third person, somewhat proving the temperament Subaru describes, while avoiding a direct correction (`u:0061-0069`). This scene is comic but also preserves a real deception. Subaru leaves with an offer of help if needed (`u:0070-0071`). Mine takes a break, and Serina/Hanae support her; a raw Hanae tag at `u:0078` addresses Hanae as though spoken by Serina (`u:0072-0079`). Mine's composure costs effort; her eventual private discussion matters more than her public rhetoric alone.

## 3. Mine encounters anger she cannot simply treat

Mine tells Sensei she senses deep anger only just contained. She counts receiving Arius hatred as aid, but warns that one-sided aid loses meaning. Her personal maxim is that persuasion works only once someone is at least partly ready to be persuaded; she also imagines a person pressing to the limit while wishing to be stopped (`u:0080-0092`). These are **Mine's inferences and practical ethic**, not a diagnosis of Subaru or proof of any student's hidden desire. Sensei's choice refers to failing to attend fully to an earlier case; the episode does not identify which case, despite the E011 Subaru exchange replayed later (`u:0093-0095`). Mine assures him he did his best, without access to a complete counterfactual.

Mine admits a Trinity student cannot straightforwardly claim to understand Arius and that even the word `理解` may feel arrogant, while still choosing incremental efforts toward mutual understanding (`u:0096-0104`). She asks what supports someone at the end of hope. Sensei silently recalls Subaru's E011 hypothetical, then says a person may be supported by what they believe—whether a great being or inward trust. He says conviction and emotion can move action and that a path must be proven by how one lives it; his teacher test is not turning away from students asking for help (`u:0105-0127`). This is **in-scene ethical dialogue**, not an empirical guarantee that belief suffices in a life crisis or that every attempted rescue succeeds. Mine thanks him and leans briefly against him, eyes closed and calm (`u:0128-0133`). It does not complete wider Arius reconciliation or define a romantic relationship.

An abrupt sound interrupts. Mine asks Sensei whether he heard it; Hanae independently calls it a trumpet-like noise, Serina asks what to do, and Mine says they should continue their task (`u:0134-0142`). The multi-person reactions establish a perceived external event more strongly than Saori's solitary E010 sensation. They do **not** establish that it is the same trumpet source, an Arius signal or a supernatural mechanism. The `次回;ラッパの音` line is a teaser (`u:0143`).
