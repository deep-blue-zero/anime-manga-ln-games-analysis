## 3. The archive account is detailed yet still secondhand

Ui/Shimiko speak of empathy across history, and Atsuko says inherited anger can be transmitted in more than one way (`u:0124-0128`). According to Atsuko, the civil-war victors who rejected the gate as a peace symbol built a large underground record repository to preserve later guidance and warning; it recorded Arius events and became a library (`u:0129-0135`). She says an Arius ruler designated gate and repository off-limits **more than about 150 years ago**, but admits the reason is unknown. Her thought that accumulated dark emotion became unbearable is explicitly conjecture (`u:0136-0140`). Ui/ Shimiko generalize about information control, political use of anger and suppression of agitators; those are interpretive analogies, not a demonstrated specific policy chain at Porta Pacis (`u:0141-0148`).

Saori/Atsuko infer that repeated Arius rulers used oral history and information control through Beatrice's final domination; Misaki notes retelling can omit or exaggerate, so their own inherited account may be distorted (`u:0149-0158`). The title's “forgotten origin” and E005's archive proposal therefore gain a concrete epistemic reason for investigation, without making the reported archive automatically reliable. She speculates Subaru discovered “something” after Beatrice left and disorder preceded her restoration of order; Maia confirms disorder but no specific discovery is shown (`u:0161-0165`). Atsuko then says she knows of a hidden Arius `秘儀`, perhaps now also known by Subaru. She has not yet explained it (`u:0166-0170`). Do not import a later revelation into this cutoff.
