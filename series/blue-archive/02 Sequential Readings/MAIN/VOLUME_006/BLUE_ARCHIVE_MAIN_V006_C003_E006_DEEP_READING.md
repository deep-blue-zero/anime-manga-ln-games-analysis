## 1. The Saori/Subaru partnership had friction and complementary care

Subaru recalls first meeting Squad leader Saori on a mission when both were first years. Saori arrives with a finished plan and treats individual coordination as unnecessary; Subaru asks about capacities and contingencies, while Saori describes unilateral completion as consideration (`scene:001:u:0001-0022`). Subaru judges Saori cold and difficult outside Squad and says she alone could absorb her personality (`u:0023-0025`). A second-year cut recalls Subaru in Saori's line of fire and challenging her speed/victory doctrine; Saori states that failure is sin and accepts the implication that other costs are subordinate (`u:0026-0032`). The represented exchange supports a real values conflict, though the incidents' full combat context and consequences are absent.

Subaru next recalls Saori failing to advance to third year. Subaru privately suspects it was Madam's design to keep Saori usable, a situated hypothesis rather than a school record or proven retention order (`u:0033-0039`). Saori says she minds little because she can lead Squad another year. She tells Subaru younger students rely on her and asks her to cover their mental care after Saori's training; Subaru says that training hurts many students, while Saori thanks her (`u:0040-0054`). This clarifies E002's strict-instructor/kind-centurion pair with dialogue: Subaru's kindness was also compensatory labor within a coercive training system. It does not mean Saori deliberately sought injury or that the number affected is known. Subaru concludes she never thought Saori could change (`u:0055-0057`); that is a retrospective belief now challenged by Saori's E002 appeal.

## 2. Subaru awakens, but the danger and responsibility are not formally settled

Sensei inwardly asks if Subaru is awake; she asks where she is and is told Porta Pacis. She asks how long she was unconscious; Sensei says about ten seconds (`u:0058-0063`, choices 001-002). That establishes the exchange's reported duration, not how she was incapacitated, how the figure vanished, or whether all figures are gone. When she asks whether everything is over, Sensei says he too wants to know (`u:0064-0067`, choice 003). Her subsequent private conclusion that all is over, that she achieved nothing, and that her self-will led everyone to this is self-condemnation, not an objective crisis/status report (`u:0068-0076`). The narration explicitly hears her crying and has Sensei sit beside her and wait until she settles (`u:0077-0079`).

Subaru asks what she wanted. Sensei recognizes that she tried hard to protect Arius while she insists the result makes it meaningless. She admits motives for wounded children, the unplaced and herself, and a wish not to drift as before (`u:0080-0092`, choices 004-005). Sensei's next branches value trying and a student's process, but Subaru challenges this against impure motive and the apparent dead end of her claimed Arius method (`u:0092-0103`, choices 006-007). His assessment does not erase harms to Maia or adjudicate the school-wide consequences.

Subaru calls her life at the bottom and uses the image of garbage that cannot be polished into a jewel. She asks not to be shown impossible dreams and rejects the irresponsible promise that effort always pays (`u:0104-0109`). Sensei explicitly concedes that effort may not change things, may fail or make them worse (`choice:008`). His later inward bottom/upward quip is immediately challenged by Subaru as poor encouragement; it should not be substituted for a guaranteed upward path (`u:0110-0111`).

## 3. Squad and residents offer accompaniment, not a certified turnaround

Saori says their lives were truly low, with little room to fall or flee, and that they still have to try; Misaki and Hiyori add bleak humor rather than a reward guarantee (`u:0112-0124`). Atsuko says Arius must go together because alone they can do nothing, explicitly setting aside a fairy-tale explanation that togetherness magically generates power (`u:0125-0128`). This revises Subaru's E002 identification of herself alone with Arius and E005 demand to remember abandoned lives without pretending that injury is now compensated.

Resident A invites Subaru to get up because she is muddy. Subaru inwardly says her clothes are already dirty; Maia answers that even so, she need not make them dirtier, and Subaru agrees (`u:0129-0135`). The small exchange models limited, practical protection after damage, not institutional settlement or confirmed performance of the harmonica. The italic style makes the exact channel of Maia/Subaru's last replies uncertain, though the relation is represented directly.

The scene cuts to Serina and Hanae reacting as Mine wakes in the Rescue Knights infirmary (`u:0136-0139`). Mine immediately asks how many days remain before the exam and calls preparation essential; Serina urges recuperation, then agrees there is still time to prepare (`u:0140-0149`). No date, medical diagnosis, discharge, exact exam schedule, scores or club outcome is given. `次回;試み` is an end card at `u:0150`, not a completed attempt. Mine's awakening narrows the earlier open question of whether she survived, while recovery and exam participation remain open.
