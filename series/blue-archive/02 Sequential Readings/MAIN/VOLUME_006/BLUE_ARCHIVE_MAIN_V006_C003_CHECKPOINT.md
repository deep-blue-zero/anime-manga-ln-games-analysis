## 1. Prospective sequence and corrections

### E001–E003: who may decide for Arius

Subaru challenges Sensei's school-based student definition and asks why his earlier care reached Mika/Squad but not the stayers. A Sensei recollection shows two **distinct unnamed** Patar/Sanctus interim Tea Party representatives asking him to step back from Arius, claiming a majority and student self-government; no vote record or his explicit assent appears. Subaru suspects outside backers without evidence, rejects his return to Arius, and treats Porta Pacis records as Arius study rather than outside curriculum. She asks Sensei and Squad to leave and never return, conditionally promising safety while claiming untested control of the figures. Saori asks what future this offers (E001).

Saori remembers their strict-instructor/kind-centurion division. She accepts inherited training and past wrongdoing yet argues one can live differently after encountering an outside world. Squad say they have jointly decided to surrender **after** the crisis; Saori considers claiming broad responsibility partly to protect others, so that prospective confession is not a full culpability map. Subaru resents returnees judging the stayers and identifies herself with Arius's painful past. Saori says her own life is precious, while Subaru says all suffering must mean something and claims she ended children's anxiety without an outcome audit. A seventh-trumpet log appears, not a verified sound count (E002). Saori later minimizes an apparent wound; residents hear a sound and see daylight darken but dispute whether it is the sixth, a repeat or fourteen total sounds. Maia's call reaches Subaru after Saori's apparently does not. Atsuko says Maia has been present and that Subaru failed to look at her, then labels Subaru a phenomenon/trumpet angel, promising reasons in the next episode (E003).

### E004–E006: an actor explanation and Subaru's remembered alternative

Atsuko describes Royal Blood as a burden that restricted her speech and access to an Arius `秘儀`. She says accumulated anger, grief and resignation unconsciously took form through Subaru; the figures are **not** literal scriptural seven angels. A perfect-judge/trinity-recompense scenario is conditional. Atsuko says Subaru's treatment of Maia defeats a claim to judge for all, distinguishes medium from would-be judge, and offers a counterfactual about Maia turning to Sensei that cannot be tested or used to blame Maia. Maia says not everything is okay, yet loves Arius/Squad/Subaru/Sensei and asks Subaru to help make the school better. No reply is printed (E004).

Maia returns Subaru's harmonica and asks to hear it someday. Subaru inwardly invokes responsibility, disappears from view, and residents report a third figure resembling her, with the earlier two apparently absorbed. That observation does not independently prove fusion. Her interior monologue asks who remembers Arius's discarded people, intercut with ambiguous harmonica recollection and Korean-formatted Subaru log shells. Atsuko seems to address her during a prayer-like gesture, acknowledges Trinity harm, and offers shared responsibility and Sensei's help; Subaru refuses to bury Arius's past without judgment. Atsuko proposes an Arius-style way of waking her with Sensei and Maia, but it is not enacted on page (E005).

Subaru's subsequent memory shows first/second-year missions with Saori, conflict over speed-at-any-cost training and Subaru's care for younger students affected by it. She guesses Beatrice caused Saori's failure to advance, without an advancement document. Subaru wakes at Porta Pacis after Sensei estimates ten seconds unconscious; he explicitly cannot say whether everything is over. The action that brought her back is omitted. She cries, calls her efforts meaningless and admits motives to aid hurt/unplaced children and herself. Sensei values a student's process while conceding that effort may fail or worsen things. Squad/Atsuko offer nonmagical plural continuation; Maia urges not adding dirt to already muddied clothes, and Subaru agrees. Mine separately wakes in a Rescue Knights infirmary and presses exam preparation, with treatment course and scores then unknown (E006).

### E007–E008: care and proposed public record

In a later treatment phase, Subaru says the trumpets stopped and Trinity Rescue Knights, Gehenna Emergency Medicine and other helpers came to Arius, moving patients into the Knights' ward. She thanks caregivers while asking for time for residents unsettled by aid from former enemies. Mine is ambulatory, then urges study; Maia wants to try. Arius transfer students A/B demonstrate CQB and two distinct Trinity students A/B invite them to teach and lead a study club, without a charter or clearly free office acceptance. Maia asks if a new nest can be made; Subaru agrees to try. Sensei's E006 words replay rather than being spoken anew (E007).

Nagisa offers to arrange free use of an unused Trinity building for Arius classes/exams and proposes comparing conflicting Arius/Trinity histories toward a public record, anticipating friction and specialist help. No handover, archive or publication occurs. She says she wants students to remember Trinity as a treasured school. Sensei apologizes for prior distrust and harsh speech; Nagisa retains her remedial-group responsibility while saying his exceptional severity hurt, then they agree to be more careful and rarely reopen the topic. Sensei tells her of the E001 representatives. Nagisa claims she can roughly identify them from reports and alleges poor work and tidied submissions; no names, records or process are shown. She contemplates a political response and improving Mika's treatment. Mika/Seia arrive and all four spend tea time together; Seia's lost-future-reading remark is a self-report (E008).

### E009–E010: legal conditionality, school practice and mixed results

Named Valkyrie deputy Konoka interviews sentenced Saori, who says she regrets the attack daily while owning her former self. Konoka announces **all Squad** voluntarily surrendered, have fixed charges/sentences, and are approved for parole. She attributes the decision to remorse, petitions from Sensei/Trinity/Gehenna and Sensei's responsibility pledge, as well as correctional aims/costs. Hina directly confirms her own petition in an inserted passage; its physical setting is unclear. Konoka rejects Saori's extreme halo-break pledge and stresses parole is not innocence. She says the Federal Student Council's decision on Arius's future is near, not already issued. Hifumi, Azusa, Hanako, Koharu and Sensei conduct an Arius study session. The room is not identified as Nagisa's offered building. Maia/Azusa reunite; Hanako tutors a question Koharu cannot recall; Hanako/Atsuko make a first friendly greeting. Narration confirms daily preparation for a first exam, with no score yet (E009).

The test is actually sat and celebrated afterward; pre-result “all failed” talk is anxiety. Source log calls it `総合試験`, while Atsuko later calls this sitting a no-penalty `模試`; retain the ambiguity of formal status. Subaru admits resistance but chooses to trust Arius students' wish to try and Sensei, who thanks her and Maia and says teachers also feel uncertainty. At tea, Atsuko speaks of Squad's intended interim student council, names herself provisional president, tentatively describes Misaki/Saori/Hiyori offices, and asks Subaru's Nicomedia group to take discipline duty. Subaru accepts and later reports a patrol, then pledges maximum help despite possible failure. Saori's contract-review/job and uniform ideas remain proposals. Atsuko says Mine recovered, contained four figures and prevented sevenfold release until Subaru was driven off; this provides an actor account, not an observed battle, verified count or occult mechanism. Atsuko evades Subaru's question why Mine hurts while claiming rescue. Restoration is said to reach only “here” for now. Participants later report mixed results: Atsuko about 90 average; Misaki several red marks; Hiyori and Maia just clear the red-mark line; Subaru average; Saori full marks. No sheets, subject weights or graduation consequences are printed (E010).
