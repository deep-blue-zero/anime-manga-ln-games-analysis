The history lines say Trinity excommunicated Arius, thereby excommunicated itself, drove Arius to `ニコメディア`, and attacked first. Subaru asks whether that is this place's original name and says the attack story is familiar to her (`u:0012-0023`). These are text/testimony and her inference, not an independently dated place-name record or proof of first-shot responsibility.
