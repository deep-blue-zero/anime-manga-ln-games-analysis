Chapter 1 establishes a **first real Arius class and outing**, while refusing an uncomplicated school-rescue ending. Two Arius-to-Trinity transfers ask for schooling for those left behind. Nagisa and Sensei route lessons through Squad, which returns to a community that regards it as having abandoned Arius. Subaru initially makes armed victory the price of entry, and a resident is reported unconscious after a further shooting immediately before class. The lesson and outing nevertheless elicit substantive questions, curiosity, play and some easing. Saori faces Sensei's scar and takes ownership of future choice; Subaru describes feeding and sheltering Arius through coercive paid work. ## 1. Prospective sequence and corrections

### E001–E003: returning, testing and transfer testimony

Maia returns ill and disillusioned from attempts to live outside Arius, after work, loan and criminal pressures represented in a partly unheaded sequence. Subaru welcomes her and prioritizes rest, without a clinical recovery or a complete record of those outside encounters (E001). Trinity separately prepares an externally written **comprehensive** all-student exam with supplemental/retest consequences rather than direct expulsion. Club-student reactions and Sisterhood study logistics show institutional breadth, not a scored sitting (E002). Two shy visitors disclose they are Arius transfers at Trinity. They report a limited Tea Party-associated transfer offer under a rural-school paperwork cover, old deprivation/training, new beds/food/peers, continuing Arius nightmares and a wish that stayers might study normally too. Rumors of resistance or lost contact are not live census evidence. Sensei's first attempted head touch prompts recoil and a later touch is allowed, a local consent difference. The unnamed transfer emissary is **not identified as Nagisa** by E003 or Nagisa's later sponsorship (E003).

### E004–E007: apology, a request and two possible paper routes

Atsuko asks that the whole Squad, including Saori, meet Sensei. Squad challenges why education matters after violent training and insecure outside work; Sensei offers knowledge of the world as a reason, Saori invokes contract literacy, Misaki partially assents and Hiyori worries about stayers (E004). Nagisa hosts a meal aboard a rented, moored ship. She apologizes for Trinity's expulsion/neglect and explains both political-cost and conscience motives; Atsuko apologizes for Squad's terror. Neither apology cancels the other's history. Nagisa notices aid can become condescension (E005). Her concrete request is that Sensei teach Arius; she offers indirect safer routes, but he elects to go in person with Squad escort (E006). Subaru, meanwhile, hears Maia's account of outside beauty and exclusion and reports some contact loss without verified harm; she blames Trinity and especially Saori/Squad for absence from Arius. Nagisa proposes spare Trinity exam papers reaching Arius under a delivery-error cover, and Sensei wants an Arius-named title; no delivery or renamed test is shown at E007's cutoff. Subaru plays harmonica for Arius students, ordinary care that should not be lost inside the political argument. A messenger reports Squad's return and residents are angry. Subaru first tells her people to keep gun safeties on.

### E008–E010: armed gate, actual teaching and scars

Saori says she has exam papers with her, but no pages or administration are displayed. Sensei calls residents students and proposes trying once with an option to stop; Subaru's apparent assent flips the word **test** into an armed qualification of Squad's right to enter and orders safeties off (E008). E009 skips the fight itself and opens with Saori's narrow-win assessment. Residents remain hostile. Witnesses report one resident unconscious after chest/head shots, strongly implying Atsuko fired after insults against Sensei, but no neutral shooting caption, treatment or recovery appears. Sensei immediately begins a short class in a damaged room and spontaneously makes Squad practice teachers. Saori teaches contracts/social theory, Misaki physics, Atsuko biology, Hiyori outside life/fashion and Sensei a fragment. Hiyori is narrated as most popular; students vary in engagement and some become less wary. That success cannot retroactively give consent to the armed entry or settle the injured resident's state. It is not the comprehensive exam.

Saori's pending consultation becomes a direct request to see Sensei's abdominal scar. With his assent she touches it, trembles and apologizes, then says the past cannot be reversed and she must bear her chosen action. Sensei accepts enduring marks and has her repeat a future commitment to pause and choose better; neither supplies a legal pardon or complete injury audit. Saori reports better food and outside supplies, but explicitly does not know their funding source. Her half-sincere request to sleep with Sensei ends with her departure, not a shared night. Maia asks Subaru about food money. Subaru presents coercive Arius-specific mercenary work, with a live rival intimidated into subordination instead of being killed in the represented vignette. Speaker tags drift and there is no payment trail; it cannot establish the whole school budget. Subaru admits the work wears her down and wants a limited nest for abandoned children. Protective purpose and coercive means coexist. Saori hears Subaru's harmonica just before an unidentified dark/fog/trumpet-like interruption, still unanswered by chapter end (E010).

### E011–E012: the outside world and divided belonging

Atsuko gives a small quiz, prompting the students' first explicit confusion about why asking classmates is allowed in study but not in a test. No result is printed. Sensei takes Subaru and Arius residents on a city outing while wanted Squad remains at Arius because public exposure is unsafe. The children learn road-signal and shared-dispenser rules, see clean pool water and buildings, eat, play arcade games and rest. They also describe former threat-based adult control and severe ration-line punishment. Maia's earlier outside distress briefly returns. In a park, Subaru asks seriously whether a hypothetical person who wants to give up life can be stopped. Sensei refuses to promise a sure technique, speaks of earlier help and mutual reasons to live, and hopes he can be a reason for her. The person imagined, any immediate risk and any outcome remain unidentified (E011).

Maia and Subaru try to rescue a stuck kitten; Subaru fires toward it to induce a leap and catches it. The child and sister reasonably object to risk, then bystanders recognize the Arius emblem and extend criticism into school-wide stigma. Maia is shaken by memories of exploitation; Subaru reassures her but calls the hope stirred by Sensei a short dream (E012). Direct student speech confirms tea-time improvement and credits Subaru, without tracing its money. Suzumi and Reisa meet the outing group: Reisa's exuberant unpaid-patrol account and photos attract two residents, while two others remain angry and self-blaming. Suzumi says she pursues justice despite expected failures, with no guarantee of immediate ideal attainment. Subaru validates the hurt students but also argues that abundance enables easy optimism and that Trinity averts its role in Arius's history. These are contested character claims, not demographic proof or an adjudicated record. An unexplained change in her face alarms C/D. One outing student gives an easy positive answer, another a hesitant one. At Arius, Mine and Serina are present for an as-yet unspecified Rescue Knights intervention; Hanae is named in a Sensei choice but has no direct speech. The chapter end card opens Chapter 2.
