## The failure of emotional exclusion

Rio then becomes troubled by embarrassment. Her error lies in equating the manufacture of an object with the whole relational action of presenting it. This is an interpretation grounded in her explicit puzzle, not an invented unconscious motive.

Sensei’s thanks intensifies rather than resolves the embarrassment. She finally says that receiving the response makes her happy (u:0071–0079; choice:009–010).
