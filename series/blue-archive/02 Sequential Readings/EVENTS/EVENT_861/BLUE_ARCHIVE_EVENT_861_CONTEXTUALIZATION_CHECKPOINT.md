Central account

Event861 gives underwater activity value before it gives the diving club a useful institutional achievement. Whales, bubbles, fish-flavoured ice cream, kelp tea, an awkward greeting, familiar routes and the wish simply to dive are substantive reasons to remain together. Financial dependence then makes the club vulnerable to a false choice: accept work or extraction that conflicts with its values, or lose the activity around which its members have organized themselves. The climax is a refusal to disturb the whales' resting place and a hazardous escape from a deliberately coercive commission-giver. The epilogue preserves a less triumphant institution: work and debt continue, gratitude does not erase accountability, Ami retains her rule against exceptions, and the club still bends nervously before power.

The achieved outcome is therefore neither permanent emancipation nor mere competence being rewarded. The divers keep acting together and accept ordinary company; they defeat an immediate captor, receive rescue and institutional recognition, and obtain reported partial debt remission through continued cleaning. Their equipment debt is not shown cancelled. Their powerful attack remains subject to an acquisition question. The final ice cream is welcome without another service exchange being demanded. These positive and contrary endings belong to the same account.
