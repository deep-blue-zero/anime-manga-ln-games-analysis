## Complete local sequence and literary stakes

Aru advertises the previous year's many troubles as difficulties overcome; Mutsuki asks whether the negative proportion was too high, while Kayoko's early silence does not settle that dispute (s1:u:0001–0009). That register difference matters before any dangerous task begins: the group is preparing an ordinary New Year outing and choosing how to describe its own life. The wish destination is appealing in part because it promises a large change without further work. Aru publicly redescribes attendance as an underworld business opportunity, and Kayoko questions the rumor's social-media basis (s1:u:0010–0026).

The scene does not make participation one shared motive. Aru orders everyone to bring their money; Kayoko registers that it is a boss's order, and Haruka offers conspicuously large resources in deferential terms (s1:u:0027–0032). Mutsuki proposes a relation between offering amount and fulfilled wishes; Haruka follows that logic more literally than Aru intends, and Aru rejects the implication (s1:u:0033–0038). A caution against costly overcommitment is therefore present inside the same episode that starts the money request. Neither line alone can establish a reliably protective or uniformly coercive leader.
