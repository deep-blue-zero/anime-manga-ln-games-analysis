## 5. Comparative argument from independent encounters

### Giving has several kinds of value

Rei's mold assistance does not remove hand labor (:126 scene:001:u:0020–0031; choice:004). Aoba's cheap bought chocolate expresses constrained effort (:129 scene:001:u:0012–0026), while Nozomi explicitly chooses a bought object for the teacher's actual day (:128 scene:001:u:0007–0011). Ritsu makes an art object whose value lies in likeness and instruction rather than edibility (:136 scene:001:u:0024–0031). Yakumo exploits the difference between a baker's handmade cake and the implication that the giver made it (:140 scene:001:u:0025–0043). These contrasts defeat one uniform equation between gift price, homemade status, sincerity and worth.

The objects are individual preferences. Hikari wants a particular new snack; Nagusa shares her own favorite; Fuyu selects a special long-lasting edition; Takane and Yakumo name different favorite cakes. Miyo's writing thought arises from powder on mascarpone. Subaru expresses a food-waste ethic and reports experiments for Arius children. Pleasure here is not an interchangeable sign that each person has a “soft side.” Its materials, habits, reasons and social audience differ.

### Time can be the desired result

Hikari's sour joke is a completed shared pleasure (:127 scene:001:u:0016–0017). Nozomi's actual play precedes work reciprocity (:128 scene:001:u:0025–0026). Aoba accepts rest and tentatively sees another tomorrow (:129 scene:001:u:0044–0048). Miyo's cooling time becomes enjoyable conversation (:134 scene:002:u:0034–0041). No major crisis outcome is needed for these scenes to matter. Takane supplies the principle explicitly: finding brief happiness in daily life (:139 scene:001:u:0006–0011).

The package also contains incomplete time. Love urges duty but loses the hoped-for sharing; Yakumo tries to leave before consenting to company; Kanoe pressures the teacher to taste now despite a work limit. These are different negotiations, not a generalized rule that the teacher should always stay or always leave. Fuyu's wish to be remembered for the candy's duration is a desire, not an observed ongoing effect.

### Appreciation and boundaries can diverge

Sensei apologizes when Aoba asks that the letter be read later; no opening or content is shown (:129 scene:001:u:0027–0033; choice:003).
