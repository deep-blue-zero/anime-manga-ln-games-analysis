## Current institutional thesis

**STRENGTHENED but provisional:** Schale remains a bridging/corrective capacity multiplier rather than a replacement sovereign. E008–E009 now provide a paired institutional test: Ayane can veto Sensei in ordinary policy yet explicitly request Sensei's `出動命令` during armed crisis. ## New institutional risk

E007–E009 establish a distinct **proxy / procurement / paid-coercion** problem. The chain now appears as hidden sponsor → Problem Solver 68 → hired day-labor mercenaries. Intermediation creates opacity and fragmented responsibility rather than frictionless control. Future readings must track arms regulation, sponsor motive, contractor knowledge, confidentiality, subcontracting, responsibility distribution, and Black Market mediation.
