## Current institutional thesis

**STRENGTHENED but provisional:** Schale remains a bridging/corrective capacity multiplier rather than a replacement sovereign. ## New institutional risk

Intermediation creates opacity and fragmented responsibility rather than frictionless control. Future readings must track arms regulation, sponsor motive, contractor knowledge, confidentiality, subcontracting, responsibility distribution, and Black Market mediation.
