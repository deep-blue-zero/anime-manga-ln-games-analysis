## Current institutional thesis

**STRENGTHENED but provisional:** Schale remains a bridging/corrective capacity multiplier rather than a replacement sovereign. E014 additionally demonstrates that high student institutional competence can coexist with ethically questionable extralegal coercion; autonomy is not infallibility. E008–E009 now provide a paired institutional test: Ayane can veto Sensei in ordinary policy yet explicitly request Sensei's `出動命令` during armed crisis. Authority is therefore contextual and functionally delegated. External capacity remains normatively neutral; Problem Solver 68 shows that paid coercion can be resource-constrained, morally contested, confidentiality-protected, and bounded by subcontractor labor terms.

## New institutional risk

E007–E009 establish a distinct **proxy / procurement / paid-coercion** problem. The chain now appears as hidden sponsor → Problem Solver 68 → hired day-labor mercenaries. E009 proves that lower layers retain their own agency: Aru knowingly proceeds after the target becomes personal, while mercenaries can refuse overtime and leave when compensation ends. Intermediation creates opacity and fragmented responsibility rather than frictionless control. Future readings must track arms regulation, sponsor motive, contractor knowledge, confidentiality, subcontracting, responsibility distribution, and Black Market mediation. E011 confirms the Black Market as a plausible city-scale procurement ecology but still does not connect a specific seller to the discontinued weapon. E013–E014 separately establish a Kaiser Loan–shadow-bank cash interface and successful seizure of collection records, while still not merging Kaiser Loan, Kaiser PMC, the shadow bank, or the weapon-supply chain without evidence.
