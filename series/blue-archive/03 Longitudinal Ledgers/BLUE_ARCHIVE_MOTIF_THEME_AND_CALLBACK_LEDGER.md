## Prologue-level motif warning

The Prologue plus eight Abydos units support a coherent cluster, but no motif should yet be promoted to a full-series thesis without later arc testing. E007 adds a second major axis: the same world of asymmetric capacity also contains prohibited-arms supply, paid proxies, and replaceable coercive agents. This strengthens the need to analyze power by **purpose, relationship, and institutional channel**, not by capacity alone.
