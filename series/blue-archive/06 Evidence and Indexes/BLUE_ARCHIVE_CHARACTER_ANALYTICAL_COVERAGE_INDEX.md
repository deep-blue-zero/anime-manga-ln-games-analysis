# BLUE ARCHIVE CHARACTER ANALYTICAL COVERAGE INDEX
## Evidence availability, inspected coverage, model readiness, and missing domains

## 0. Responsibility

This index answers four questions at the current Chapter 1 boundary:

1. which character evidence has actually been analyzed;
2. which source classes exist in the promoted corpus but remain outside the analytical boundary;
3. which behavioral domains are strong, narrow, or missing;
4. whether a project-local reconstruction claim is currently warranted.

It does not rank character importance, duplicate the character-state ledger, create global registry records, or certify unrestricted simulation.

## 1. Status vocabulary

Source-class cells use:

- `ANALYZED` — admitted into the current analytical corpus;
- `AVAILABLE_NOT_REVIEWED` — the promoted source/derived corpus exposes the class or retrieval route, but this project has not inspected it for the subject in this tranche;
- `ROUTE_NOT_VERIFIED` — the corpus class exists, but a subject-specific usable route has not been confirmed here;
- `NOT_APPLICABLE` — the class is not a sensible direct subject source;
- `NOT_ADMITTED` — deliberately outside the present source boundary.

Domain cells use:

- `H` — repeated or strongly triangulated within the stated current boundary;
- `M` — meaningful but context- or relationship-limited;
- `L` — one or a few narrow observations;
- `GAP` — no responsible current operational claim;
- `SPECIAL` — requires a separate treatment, such as Sensei choice-space modeling.

Readiness uses the project-local specification:

- `UNMODELED`;
- `PARTIAL_MODEL`;
- `OPERATIONAL_CANDIDATE`;
- `BOUNDED_VALIDATED`.

All standalone model artifacts are currently `NONE`. A `PARTIAL_MODEL` row means that canonical checkpoint/ledger analysis already supports at least one conditional mechanism, not that a finished model exists.

## 6. Evidence concentration and sampling bias

### 6.1 Crisis- and institution-heavy subjects

- Ayane, Shiroko, Hoshino, Sensei, Kayoko, Chinatsu, Iori, and Ako are disproportionately observed under institutional, investigative, command, or armed-pressure conditions.
- Rin and Wakamo are almost entirely Prologue-crisis subjects at this boundary.
- Hifumi supplies a useful hobby and ordinary-affinity sample, but her current time range remains narrow.

### 6.2 Relatively stronger ordinary or private controls

- Serika has the strongest current main-story ordinary-life control: paid work, customer-service code-switching, money reasoning, ramen/community attachment, peer friction, and reciprocity.
- Problem Solver 68 has a valuable restaurant/scarcity/contract ensemble sample. Aru, Mutsuki, Kayoko, and Haruka nevertheless remain mostly observed through one organization and conflict arc.
- Nonomi and Hoshino have low-stakes group behavior and familiar banter, but little private evidence outside the committee.

### 6.3 Domains that remain systematically thin

- low-stakes dyads outside the home institution;
- non-Sensei private behavior;
- leisure and domestic routine;
- long-term response after failure or repair;
- repeated appraisal-to-action paths across unrelated contexts;
- disagreement without institutional or physical stakes;
- written speech across multiple audiences and source classes;
- any performed-voice claim.
