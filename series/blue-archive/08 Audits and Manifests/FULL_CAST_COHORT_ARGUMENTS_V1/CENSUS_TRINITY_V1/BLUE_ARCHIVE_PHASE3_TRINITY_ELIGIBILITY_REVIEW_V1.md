## 1. Decision rule and scope

An independent character package is eligible when the accepted material supports a connected, defeasible account of that character's agency, ordinary wants, conduct, relationships and changes or continuities, with enough contrary evidence to prevent a single trait or public role from explaining everything. A quiet purchase, prayer, photograph, garden or wish for company can supply essential evidence. Neither stakes, popularity, MAIN appearance, episode totals nor assessed model grade decides eligibility. Counts below describe admitted retrieval pools; they supply no verdict by themselves.

A relationship question requires directed conduct and consequential responses between specified parties, including refusals, uneven permissions and failed repair. A club, institution or ensemble question needs an argument about a shared practice, authority, distribution of burdens or coordinated activity whose explanation is not already exhausted by the individual biographies. Co-occurrence and a roster do not establish that argument. Proposed questions require ROOT's allocation review before they can enter a fixed denominator; overlapping questions can be joined or assigned without discarding their evidence.

An unreceived roster, unproved identity or insufficient independent argument produces a visible pending duty. It is not an exclusion. A role can require coverage in another package without supporting a separate monograph. A literal label repeated in several episodes is not automatically one person. Narration, an announcement channel, a device and an unidentified speaker remain distinct responsibilities until the source warrants another disposition.

The current Cycle008 paragraphs control admission: selected supplemental pools are ADMITTED_WITH_LIMITS, complete contextual meanings are accepted WITH LIMITS, and earlier producer intake/proposal words retain historical meaning only. All fourteen original gaps remain OPEN at their actual claim scope. Written voice does not establish performed delivery. No new source-reading credit, source admission, registry repair or repository write is claimed here.

The eleven required monograph dimensions below are a feasibility audit, not eleven finished monograph sections: central thesis; longitudinal structure; public/private comparison; school/club; ordinary life; crisis; Sensei; major peers; language/written voice; rivals and opposed recipients; evidence and limits. A dimension may legitimately conclude that the available account does not establish a claim. Mandatory writing debt is distinguished from an enduring evidentiary limit.
