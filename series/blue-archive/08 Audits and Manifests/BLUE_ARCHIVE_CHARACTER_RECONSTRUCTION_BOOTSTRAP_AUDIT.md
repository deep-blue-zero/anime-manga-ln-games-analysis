## 3. Question 1 — Who already has enough evidence for local readiness?

- **Sensei** — structural action and choice-family evidence support a partial autonomy-amplification/institutional model, subject to player-variability controls;
- **Ayane** — operator attention, evidence caution, procedural braking, technical classification, and jurisdictional reasoning;
- **Shiroko** — operational attention, objective discipline, explicit risk choices, and epistemic restraint;
- **Nonomi** — group maintenance, material care, political cognition, and Hoshino-directed longitudinal witnessing;
- **Serika** — work/scarcity routine, boundary defense, reciprocity, school identity, community attachment, and crisis vulnerability;

- **Aru** — aspirational identity performance, contract autonomy, care/performance conflict, and persona capture;

## 5. Question 3 — Which evidence dimensions are missing?

The largest cross-project gaps are:

1. repeated **attention and appraisal** evidence across unrelated contexts;
2. explicit or triangulated **motive conflict** rather than action summaries;
3. demonstrated **inhibition and escalation** conditions;
4. **aftermath, self-account, repair, and learning** after consequential mistakes;
5. low-stakes dyads and **ordinary-life breadth** outside one home institution;
6. relationship switches involving peers, strangers, superiors, subordinates, and Sensei;
7. written-Japanese profiles across multiple audiences and source classes;
8. long-term state transitions rather than one chapter's variation;
9. clean source routes for lines affected by speaker-mapping anomalies;
10. formal separation of Sensei invariants from optional persona tones;
11. variant-specific continuity and context adjudication;
12. any admitted performed-voice evidence.

These are evidence gaps, not claims that the characters lack the corresponding capacities.

Mature model construction can then compile those deltas with the existing seven ledgers.

## 8. Question 6 — Which pilots would test different methodological problems?

The recommended future pilot set is deliberately heterogeneous:

| Pilot | Methodological problem | Why useful | Gate before creation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ayane | Heavily institutional/public subject | Tests whether operator competence, evidentiary caution, and jurisdictional reasoning are being mistaken for a whole personality | obtain ordinary/private and non-operator evidence; define narrow domain if still sparse |
| Serika | Ordinary/private-adjacent subject | Strongest current work, money, customer-service, boundary, reciprocity, and community material | admit broader low-stakes/private evidence and preserve non-Sensei relationships |
| Aru | Persona/performance complexity | Tests public self-fashioning, sincere care, client autonomy, and persona capture without reducing performance to falsity | add response-to-failure and non-crisis ordinary evidence; freeze discriminating identity rules |
| Haruka | Relationship-conditioned variation | Tests whether loyalty/usefulness and command interpretation are truly Aru-specific or generalized | obtain non-Aru authority and ordinary contexts plus aftermath of the E018/E019 failure |

Sensei should be a separate special-method pilot only after invariant action, optional choice tone, and student-specific relational evidence are formally separated. A player-variable subject should not be used as the first ordinary reconstruction template.

## 9. Question 7 — What validation checks should the project use?

### Before a future unopened main unit

For any pilot with a drafted rule set:

1. freeze the exact model revision and Git basis;
2. select at most a few discriminating predictions;
3. name state, knowledge, relation, role, stakes, and alternatives;
4. define a disconfirming observation;
5. do not inspect the later outcome until the freeze is committed;
6. adjudicate with `SUPPORTED_IN_THIS_TEST`, `PARTLY_SUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`, `NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY`, `CONFOUNDED`, or `UNRESOLVED`;
7. preserve the freeze and failure even after model revision.

### Retrospective checks available now

- construct a rule from earlier C001 evidence and test it against a declared later C001 scene, explicitly labeled hindsight-exposed;
- leave one relationship context out, then test whether the rule transfers;
- compare ordinary restaurant/work scenes against crisis scenes;
- test public versus private/institutional register where both exist;
- adversarially search for a scene that supports the opposite mechanism;
- test both members of a dyad for knowledge and directional consistency;
- verify every load-bearing line against stable IDs and mapping cautions.

### Suite-level checks

- two interacting models must not make one character accurate by forcing the other into an unsupported role;
- no model may import future knowledge or side-source knowledge outside its state;
- no generated output may enter evidence ledgers;
- no test tally may be reported as an unrestricted accuracy rate.

## 10. Question 8 — Which claims must not be made yet?

Do not claim:

- any character is whole-person or unrestricted-simulation ready;
- any subject is `BOUNDED_VALIDATED`;
- Aru's performance is wholly false or wholly transparent;
- Hoshino's `old man` presentation has one proven cause;
- Haruka's loyalty failure generalizes beyond Aru/PS68;
- Ayane's operator behavior is her private resting baseline;
- Shiroko's sparse speech means sparse interiority;
- Nonomi's soft register implies naivete or absence of anger;
- Hifumi's one main-story tranche supplies a complete private model;
- Chinatsu, Iori, or Ako can be reconstructed outside their narrow formal conflict context;
- Wakamo's first Sensei encounter establishes stable romance, motive, or ordinary behavior;
- Sensei has one canonical private persona assembled from all mutually exclusive choices;
- bond/MomoTalk/event/package availability equals analyzed evidence;
- transcript wording supplies performed voice;
- a speaker-mapping anomaly can be silently repaired;
- the next unopened unit confirms, tests, or changes any current model.

These are mostly `EVIDENCE_GAP` limits. Where the C001 checkpoint supplies contrary evidence—such as Nonomi's soft register coexisting with punitive resolve—the limitation is `EVIDENCE_BACKED`.

## 11. Question 9 — Should any prototype model be created now?

**No.**

The architecture is intentionally completed before prototype authoring because:

- the canonical checkpoint's contextual-backfill decision is `DEFER`;
- the most promising subjects still have uneven ordinary/private coverage;
- no prospective model freeze exists;
- the sequential corpus currently supports distributed partial mechanisms, not a mature reusable model;
- a prototype would likely privilege the crisis/institution evidence already overrepresented;
- creating one would pressure the project to treat architecture completion as capability completion.

The next model-eligible operation should be a separately authorized, evidence-bounded pilot after its admission gate is satisfied. This audit does not pre-commit which source class must be opened first; the main story remains sequentially primary.
