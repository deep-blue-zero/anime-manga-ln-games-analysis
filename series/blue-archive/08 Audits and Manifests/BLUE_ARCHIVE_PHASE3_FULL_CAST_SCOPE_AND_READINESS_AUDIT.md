## 3. Literary eligibility test

Each candidate receives a reasoned decision with the inspected owner scope, source classes, consequential positive and contrary evidence, uncertainty, downstream target and review responsibility. The decision tests the intellectual responsibility, not an automatic threshold for source quantity.

1. **Identity and retrieval:** the subject or source-local role has a recoverable identity boundary. Uncertain aliases remain uncertain; stable source-local labels may support bounded analysis without a registry person join.
2. **Distinct argument:** there is a sustained thesis about agency, self-presentation, development or situated repertoire, relations, language or narrative function that warrants independent retrieval. The argument must do more than list scenes or paraphrase the source projection.
3. **Relevant breadth:** all relevant accepted source classes and known contradictory or limiting contexts enter the argument. Evidence density can be ordinary/private, institutional, peer, formal/linguistic or crisis-centered. Extensive MAIN presence, performance inspection and a private pool are not universal eligibility requirements.
4. **Counterreadings and recipients:** the package tests plausible rivals, failed or partial outcomes, refusals and differing recipients. Positive company and ordinary pleasure are independently meaningful; affectionate endpoints do not cancel earlier objection, costs or missing repair.
5. **Architecture sections:** a character monograph answers thesis, longitudinal arc, public/private self, school/club role, ordinary life, crisis behavior, Sensei relationship, major peers, language/voice, competing readings and evidence routes. If a channel or setting is unrepresented, the section states that exact bounded finding and its consequence instead of inventing evidence or pretending the entire character is ineligible.
6. **Scope:** source pin, textual modes, local chronology, knowledge/audience and variant/counterpart boundaries remain explicit. Written register cannot certify performed voice. Printed alternatives remain alternatives, reports remain reports, and retrospective material cannot become prospective validation.
7. **Useful home:** independent character, directed relationship/ensemble and institutional responsibilities are distinguished. A package may reuse source-facing readings without duplicating their detailed narration or creating a competing ledger/model authority.

**Prohibited exclusion bases:** low stakes, lack of crisis contribution, supporting event priority, comedy, sparse MAIN frequency, popularity, lack of a playable variant or profile, UNMODELED status, lack of an operational reconstruction mechanism, and lack of every possible audiovisual or future-release channel. A quiet preference, complete message coda, failed outing, refusal, local game or labor recipient may carry the central argument.
