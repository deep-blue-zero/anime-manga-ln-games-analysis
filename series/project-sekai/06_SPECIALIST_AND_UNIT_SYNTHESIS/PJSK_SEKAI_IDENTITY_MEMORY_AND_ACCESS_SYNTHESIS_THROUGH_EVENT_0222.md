# Feelings made encounterable — SEKAI, identity, memory and changing access

## Responsibility and interpretive claim

This first specialist generation integrates the five unit syntheses with the franchise-level SEKAI and thought-shard readings through the fixed EVENT_0222 lock. It owns the comparison among represented spaces, memory encounters, alternative experience, singer identity and changing access. It is not a complete cosmological manual. The [epistemic ledger](../04_LONGITUDINAL_LEDGERS/PJSK_EPISTEMIC_STATE_LEDGER.md) owns the sequence of established capabilities and recipient-specific knowledge; local readings own individual causes; character packages own what an encounter changes for its particular person.

The synthesis uses Japanese textual narrative and existing complete-envelope analysis, with targeted primary checks. A video pointer, CG cue or described sound is not independent audiovisual inspection. The record's repeated representations of light, music and spatial change therefore support textual claims about witnessed or reported phenomena, not an independently analyzed soundtrack, image or physical mechanism. The [phase record](../08_CURRENT_RELEASE/PJSK_RETROSPECTIVE_0222_EXECUTION_RECORD.md) identifies the source and capability boundary.

SEKAI repeatedly makes a feeling available as something a person can encounter: a place, object, figure, route, song or another position from which to see. That availability changes the work of interpretation rather than ending it. **To meet an externalized feeling is not yet to know its complete cause, accept its proposed answer or share it with everyone else.** A threatening self-position may express a real wish and draw a false conclusion from it. A remembered scene can change present understanding without changing the past. A new connection can make friendship practical without merging identities.

The latest material also requires acknowledging actual expansion. E0218 no longer leaves common access merely hoped for: coordinated arrival and subsequent deliberate returns are shown. Caution must preserve unknown mechanisms without denying demonstrated capabilities. The comparative argument therefore holds two developments together: the worlds become more usable and connected, while the right interpretation of what happens in them remains situated and incomplete.

## Distinguish the agent, setting and channel before interpreting the scene

School, Stage, Street, Wonderland and Empty singers have local histories and different relations to their human participants. The six Interstice observers are separately situated agents. A shared name does not transfer one manifestation's memory, method, temperament or audience to another. E0140's observers explicitly compare their counterparts, and E0179 lets selected local singers learn through visiting a different world. Their capacity to compare depends on their difference. [E0140](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_EVENT_0140_DEEP_READING.md); [E0179](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_EVENT_0179_DEEP_READING.md).

Origin and access likewise differ. Wonderland's origin in Tsukasa's feelings does not make Emu, Nene and Rui irrelevant occupants; Empty's origin in Mafuyu's feelings does not make Kanade the owner because her music supplies an important route. School, Stage and Street origin histories collect related feelings before or across later formal group formation. E0202/E0205 make humans discuss these differences rather than discovering that every world must have four equal owners. The broader shared place does not repeal the local histories. [E0140](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_EVENT_0140_DEEP_READING.md); [E0202](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_EVENT_0202_DEEP_READING.md); [E0205](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_EVENT_0205_DEEP_READING.md).

Channels impose further distinctions. A singer talking through a human's phone is not a body carrying shopping in the ordinary world. A temporary guest in another local SEKAI is not a permanently relocated counterpart. Interstice observation does not normally let the observed humans see, hear or touch the observer. The shared Great Tree setting later permits direct encounters under different conditions. My SEKAI is another condition-labeled setting, explicitly distinguished in dialogue from school and the Great Tree world; no single access rule should be inferred merely because all are called SEKAI. [E0215](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_EVENT_0215_DEEP_READING.md); [E0218](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_EVENT_0218_DEEP_READING.md); `PJSK:mysekai:16_8672:01:001:0001–0004`.

## Origin knowledge belongs first to its observers

E0140 revisits the period before the human groups recognize their worlds. The Interstice observers see related feelings accumulate and local singers prepare, practice, wait and attempt contact. These histories give the reader a wider explanation of the foundations: support does not begin only when a human notices it, and a formal group is not always the prerequisite for related feelings to shape one space. The histories do not become new current memories in all twenty humans. `PJSK:event:0140:14:002:0003–0049`.

The observers themselves retain only the routes they have seen and the accounts they exchange. Repeated observation from the Interstice and occasional weak sensory leakage are greater capabilities than a single accidental meeting, but not an unrestricted historical archive. The shared tree displays recognizable works and milestones. Miku interprets its growth through the humans' feelings and the singers' supporting wishes, while explicitly leaving its future unknown. The tree's particular contents do not establish a searchable record of every private thought. `PJSK:event:0140:14:003:0003–0032`.

This asymmetry has a narrative function beyond withholding information for suspense. The reader can understand care that the recipient cannot yet recognize; a local singer can act without knowing who encouraged them; an observer can care deeply while being unable to make contact. Greater perspective is not unlimited power or infallible judgment. Later direct meetings matter partly because they replace some of that distance with an actual reply.

## Personal fragments make an avoided position speak without making it sovereign

The repeated pattern established under `EPI-PJSK-THOUGHT-SHARD-E0153` is narrower than the whole SEKAI topology. Rui's E0144 field is followed by Haruka's and An's independent encounters. An avoided or younger self-position becomes personified; the environment can alter accessible memory or apparent embodiment; recognition and return can leave retained encounter memory. The shards are found rather than deliberately produced. Entry, duration, effect and exit are not a controlled procedure. [E0144](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_EVENT_0144_DEEP_READING.md); [E0153](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_EVENT_0153_DEEP_READING.md).

Their offered relief has different stakes. Haruka's younger position converts parental worry into a demand to quit idol work; she can acknowledge the worry while refusing that conclusion. An's offered relief removes people from photographs and progressively obscures faces, names and painful memory; choosing to remember does not mean she has easily forgiven the concealment around Nagi. Rui can refuse deletion of loneliness without claiming that every past refusal was someone else's fault. What becomes visible is a conflict within the person's relation to history, not an infallible diagnosis delivered by an independent authority.

Later cases preserve the distinction between retaining a self and resolving a problem. Shiho's E0191 choice keeps painful separation and is followed by a particular act of patience. Nene's E0209 recognition restores her apparent body before she elects to stay and address the younger self through song; wanting less shyness and retaining that origin coexist. ## Alternative lives are actual experiences of nonactual histories

The black-fragment sequences across VBS E0163, WxS E0167, N25 E0170, Leo/need E0171 and MMJ E0176 place already developed people in lives where consequential relations took other forms. Their emotional force depends on real attractions as well as missing attachments. The alternatives cannot simply be labeled worse worlds that prove the actual route was necessary. Nor can what happens there be added to ordinary biography: Kanade's healthy father, Mizuki's school departure, Rui's alternative career and the others' changed paths remain within their represented scenarios.

The mechanism also differs from ordinary sleep and individual recollection. Local singers encounter unresponsive humans, try to reach them and sometimes fail to enter the field. Particular calls or songs are later recognized; those receipts do not give the singers the full inner experience. In N25's case Mafuyu remembers earlier than the others but does not thereby exit, while initially withholding because their other lives appear happier. Music, images, messages, gathered wishes and the outer singers' work have different roles. No single act has been experimentally isolated as a universal release mechanism. [E0163](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/VBS/PJSK_EVENT_0163_DEEP_READING.md); [E0167](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/WXS/PJSK_EVENT_0167_DEEP_READING.md); [E0170](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/N25/PJSK_EVENT_0170_DEEP_READING.md); [E0171](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/LEO_NEED/PJSK_EVENT_0171_DEEP_READING.md); [E0176](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MMJ/PJSK_EVENT_0176_DEEP_READING.md).

Return produces choices and new ways to value current life, not a proof of destiny. Ichika cannot simply reproduce the alternative's song. MMJ's actual Airi rejects the alternative's division between variety and idol work. WxS can pursue different dreams together without a promise of identical careers. Actual cards show breakfast, puzzles, rehearsal, invitations and ordinary company after the experience. Such conduct gives return a practical life without certifying permanent cohesion or ideal pacing.

Tree growth after painful encounters supports the represented association between experience and change. It does not demonstrate that suffering is necessary nourishment. The singers propose explanations, and Nene challenges a broad pain-based account. The counterfactual proposition that these people could not have grown with less harm remains unproved. The analysis should neither deny their changed commitments nor make their suffering a requirement imposed by the world. [WxS synthesis](PJSK_WXS_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md); [E0180](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_EVENT_0180_DEEP_READING.md).

## Ordinary correspondence matters beside extraordinary access

E0215 organizes connection through labor and communication rather than a new portal. Street Luka and Stage Len coordinate a party at the closed WEEKEND GARAGE; the twenty humans and thirty local singers contribute in differentiated teams. Humans carry supplies, while phones permit conversation and shared viewing. Interstice singers remain separately desired participants. The later tree-song attempt produces a short unidentified response heard by Street Luka, whose certainty that the message arrived is her conclusion. An's question does not identify the singer or certify receipt by everyone. `PJSK:event:0215:08:002:0014–0062`.

Its smaller aftermath is equally important to identity and knowledge. School Rin worries that she overwhelmed Empty Rin. Rather than treating reassurance as proof, School Miku proposes asking; Saki and Mafuyu mediate actual letters, and the replies distinguish being startled from being displeased. Wonderland singers use illustrated diaries and photographs because memory is already imperfect. Message boards carry chosen thanks without requiring every signature. Connection becomes durable through an answer and an external record, not because extraordinary worlds eliminate ordinary uncertainty. ## Memory retention does not remove privacy, mediation or fiction

Shared access creates opportunities to tell a story; it does not perform every disclosure. Saki and Tsukasa exchange broad world descriptions without sharing all private feelings. Slade's hearing history remains with Toya and accidentally informed Street KAITO. Mizuki's disclosure has its actual audience and an untranscribed account. Mafuyu's quieter manner around selected acquaintances does not publish the family history. The unnamed people helped by the cohorts do not automatically learn the mechanism that first drew the helpers toward them. The [care specialist](PJSK_CARE_DISCLOSURE_AND_FAMILY_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md) owns the relational consequences of these limits.

My SEKAI evidence also needs its own conditions. Short talks can support particular tastes, jokes, competence limits or preferences; unknown dates cannot be filled by intuition, and duplicated bodies are not independent meetings. Rui's inability to explain or reproduce the nonmechanical doorway is direct counterevidence to an omniscient-inventor reconstruction. Pleasant talk in this setting does not decide Leo/need's E0220 dispute, VBS's warehouse mystery or Nightcord's unresolved questions. [My SEKAI supplemental reading](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_SUPPLEMENTAL_20261030_MY_SEKAI_READING.md), `PJSK:mysekai:16_8671:01:001:0001–0004`.

Finally, not every inward or represented scene is a SEKAI event. Sleeping dreams, imagined bereavement, deliberate role construction, scripted violence, a retrospective narrator and a reported memory have different truth conditions. Tsukasa's real distress during acting is consequential without making a fictional crime actual. E0221/E0222 publish selected VBS/MMJ history; they do not show every cast member watching a shared recap or receiving all its information. The unexplained photograph in E0191 is an unresolved observation, not sufficient proof either of a wholly private hallucination or of a fully specified material mechanism. ## Current authority and downstream use

The principal franchise access owner is `EPI-PJSK-SEKAI-E0218`; the separate thought-shard owner remains `EPI-PJSK-THOUGHT-SHARD-E0153`. Local School, Stage, Street, Wonderland and Empty epistemic histories retain their scopes. `CR-PJSK-SEKAI-040` records revised permeability, `CR-PJSK-SEKAI-041` retains a provisional connector explanation, and `CR-PJSK-SEKAI-042` prevents association with painful growth from becoming a universal benefit or necessity claim. E0179 guest relations and E0215 organized cooperation remain specific relationships, not a fused global singer or human unit. The ledger is the authority for exact historical succession.

For reconstruction, choose the actual temporal boundary before using an ability. At an earlier boundary a person may hope to return, fail to call, remember only a fragment or know no other world. At the latest boundary usable common access is available, while personal history still requires a receipt. An encountered self-position can change what a character considers without determining the ensuing choice. A retained memory can remain painful. A hypothetical alternative can become meaningful without becoming past fact.

The full-series synthesis can therefore treat SEKAI as an active narrative medium for relation, self-interpretation and creative possibility without claiming a universal therapy, destiny system or error-free archive. It changes what people can encounter and do together; the humans and singers must still interpret, ask, refuse, revise and live with the answer. This first generation becomes frozen at the accepted release and authorizes no new source admission or silent revision of earlier evidence boundaries.
