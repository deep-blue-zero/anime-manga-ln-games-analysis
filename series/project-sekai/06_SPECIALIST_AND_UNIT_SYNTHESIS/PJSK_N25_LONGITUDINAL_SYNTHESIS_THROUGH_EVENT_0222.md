## Four different difficulties make a shared place necessary

Nightcord begins as a working creative group before its members know one another's offline lives. Composition, lyrics, illustration and video already create precise forms of attention. That competence does not give the members a location for an absent friend, full access to a collaborator's private history or a common understanding of what being helped means. The main-story crisis exposes the difference between knowing someone's work and knowing how that person can continue living with it.

The four difficulties concern first-person authority in different ways. Mafuyu's accumulated adaptation to other people's expectations has made her own preferences hard to identify and sustain. Kanade can identify a valued activity, but guilt turns its capacity to help into a condition of personal legitimacy. Ena knows that serious art matters; uncertain talent and recognition threaten her right to claim that future. Mizuki knows many likes and forms of presentation, while others' categories make their continued ordinary legitimacy precarious. A single account of hidden authentic selves or identical suffering cannot explain all four. [Foundation](../02_MAIN_STORY_FOUNDATIONS/N25/PJSK_N25_MAIN_STORY_SYNTHESIS.md).

| Person | What is difficult to authorize | What the others can offer | What they cannot supply on that person's behalf |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mafuyu | A usable, sustainable account of wanting | Particular questions, remembered evidence, companionship, a place to refuse or test a choice | A complete true self, a vocation or a guaranteed family resolution |
| Kanade | A life and creative pleasure beyond compulsory rescue | Received effects, shared labor, personal recognition, challenge and concrete care | An answer to the rescue-end question or a reliable capacity to rest |
| Ena | A serious artistic life under uncertain evaluation | Specific critique, expressive recognition, an actual subject and practical encouragement | Talent certainty, admission or immunity to comparison |
| Mizuki | Continued belonging without unwanted recategorization | Respect for likes, options, owned attachment, protected knowledge and particular acceptance | Universal safety, painless disclosure or permanent ease in receiving kindness |

This difference explains why their care can be reciprocal without becoming symmetrical. Each has something usable to contribute and an area in which the group's best intentions can miss the actual problem. The evolving unit is not an institution that cures its members once its rules are correct. It is a place repeatedly made usable by choices, work and correction, while also carrying pressures that can injure.
