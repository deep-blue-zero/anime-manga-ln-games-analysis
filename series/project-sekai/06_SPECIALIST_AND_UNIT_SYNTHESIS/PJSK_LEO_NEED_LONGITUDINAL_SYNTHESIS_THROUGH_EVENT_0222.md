## Central argument

Leo/need's development makes a shared bond more capable of action without making its members want identical futures. At formation, affection persists behind failed access, fear and defensive decisions. Repeated creative work turns choosing one another into something observable: practice, criticism, composition, negotiated opportunities, care, public performance and rest.
