## Central argument

Wonderlands×Showtime develops through a repeated distinction between a valued end and the particular arrangement that once made it possible. The park can remain Emu's responsibility without requiring her solitary physical stay. The four can work together without wanting identical careers. An audience effect can survive the failure of a device, and an authored interpretation can survive correction of its execution. Each change depends on other people having actual knowledge, contributions and choices; the troupe's imaginative power alone does not supply those conditions.

The later work also prevents this history from becoming a smooth progress toward good collaborative practice. Learning to ask, share credit and adapt does not eliminate coercion, manipulation, overwork or delayed intervention. Nene's received trust, Nagamine's improved take and Tsukasa's successful final performance all coexist with unresolved questions about their preparation. The company has acquired real professional opportunities and deeper reciprocal commitments. It has not acquired a universally reliable way to protect its members while they pursue them.
