# Making something reach someone — authorship, labor and recipient agency

## Responsibility and argument

This first specialist generation compares a recurring artistic problem across the five completed unit syntheses: how a wish becomes a work, how the work reaches someone, and what that receipt can actually establish. It owns the comparison among creative decisions, material means, institutional judgment and audience participation. Event readings own local causes; unit syntheses own developmental histories; character packages own person-specific explanation. Family power and intimate disclosure belong to the care specialist, while SEKAI mechanisms belong to the identity/memory/access specialist. Neither is redescribed here as a general theory of art.

The argument receives the [Leo/need](PJSK_LEO_NEED_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md), [MMJ](PJSK_MMJ_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md), [VBS](PJSK_VBS_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md), [WxS](PJSK_WXS_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md) and [N25](PJSK_N25_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md) generations at the fixed source lock. It includes admitted supplements, not a new exhaustive inspection of older non-event material. Artistic effects discussed below are textually represented acts and attributed judgments. No independent listening, image assessment or recorded-performance inspection is claimed.

Across these histories, creative agency grows when people can distinguish an end they value from one supposedly indispensable way to reach it. The distinction is practical: a failed machine can be replaced, an interpretation revised, a stage emphasis redistributed, a working arrangement declined. It is also relational. Someone must contribute, permit, receive or refuse the change. **A work can be owned without being solitary, and received without its author owning the recipient's response.** This comparative proposition explains much of the development, but it is not a law that all successful work follows or a moral doctrine all characters have mastered.

The source repeatedly separates achievements that a simple success narrative would combine: useful expression, technical competence, selection, emotional effect, financial viability and humane working conditions. They may support one another without being equivalent. Nene can develop and lose an audition. Ena can find expressive pleasure while receiving a modest grade. Airi can help another person continue without producing victory. Rui can help obtain a take through a harmful process. A synthesis that makes all these outcomes synonyms would lose both the art and its costs.

## The first audience can become a maker

The five foundations begin with uneven access to making, not a distribution of finished talents waiting for a stage. Leo/need's problem is how existing attachment can become repeated common activity. MMJ's novice can give experienced idols something they need while still needing their instruction and recognition. VBS's new listener can discover a wanted act before possessing an established place in the musical community. WxS has complementary skills before it has a just account of how its members should work together. Nightcord already has collaborative practice, but the meaning of its music is unequal: one person's evidence of saving can be another's unfinished search. These are different starting relations between maker and receiver, preserved in the five unit syntheses above.

They also prevent a clean division between those who create and those who are helped. Minori's reception of Haruka's work becomes consequential testimony; Minori then has to receive her own membership from the others. Kohane's musical response helps create a partnership, but neither An's perception nor later audience praise substitutes for Kohane's choice to return. In Nightcord, Mafuyu's response to Kanade's earlier song is real without proving the rescue Kanade wants it to prove. The same exchange can matter deeply while carrying incompatible descriptions of what has happened.

Later recipient contributions are often more precise than encouragement. Kohane helps Rui identify why the lost lantern effect matters in E0152. The replacement light road depends on willing participants' labor, not only his ingenuity. Honami's need to recognize a depicted subject helps Ena discriminate between useful technique and unhelpful correction in E0070; a child's corrected crocodile demonstrates the opposite danger, where improvement can take away the maker's relation to the picture. The recipient helps specify the task, but the task still requires making and judgment. [E0152](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_EVENT_0152_DEEP_READING.md); [E0070](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/N25/PJSK_EVENT_0070_DEEP_READING.md).

This is a recurrent change in the distribution of authorship, not the abolition of expertise. A viewer can identify an effect they miss without designing its replacement. A learner can need technical help without yielding the right to choose the subject. When Ena offers Toshiki background work in E0214, she names her limited manga experience, leaves refusal possible and explains what time the assistance would release. The accepted offer makes another person's search more feasible; it does not make Ena the author of that search. Airi, Nene and Akito contribute through different capacities. [E0214](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_EVENT_0214_DEEP_READING.md), `PJSK:event:0214:06:001:0052–0057`.

## Intention, interpretation and the brief are different authorities

Ichika's later authorship provides the clearest resistance to treating artistic intention as control of reception. Her personal work has a chosen meaning and a specific production history. In E0182 she nevertheless welcomes interpretations that differ from it, and the exchange with Aoi involves permission and reciprocal creative influence. Intentional making and plural reception coexist. Her earlier refusal of a standing production mandate concerns what she will undertake, not a demand that all listeners obey her explanation. [E0182](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/LEO_NEED/PJSK_EVENT_0182_DEEP_READING.md), `PJSK:event:0182:06:002:0019–0039`; `PJSK:event:0182:08:002:0030–0060`.

MMJ's E0183 trade live sets a different problem. The brief asks for an interpretation that retains a meaningful relation to the exchanged song while making the performing group audible. Accomplished copying can miss one part; transformation that loses the song's meaning can miss another. Shizuku's solution does not refute Ichika's openness. One case concerns what an author welcomes from later listeners; the other concerns obligations the performers have accepted for this particular production. Their authority relations differ before their aesthetic judgments differ. [E0183](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MMJ/PJSK_EVENT_0183_DEEP_READING.md).

Ena's E0184 art-class work adds a third relation. The assignment, the teacher's assessment, Ena's desired image and her pleasure in painting constrain each other without collapsing. Her initial apple is graded C− and last; the later personally meaningful work earns C, with no new rank supplied. Finding the image she wants is consequential without becoming admission to the named art school or proof that fundamentals no longer matter. Deleting the selfie account preserves the art account and changes one attention channel; the later sense of absence prevents the decision from certifying freedom from recognition. [E0184](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/N25/PJSK_EVENT_0184_DEEP_READING.md).

These distinctions support a practical comparative question: **who has authority over which part of this work?** The answer can include the maker's aim, a collaborator's contribution, the client's request, a performer's body, the institution's selection and the viewer's interpretation. None is automatically sovereign over all the others. Nor does the story require every party to approve every outcome. Rivalry, disappointment and rejected interpretations remain part of artistic life.

## Authentic expression is made through form

The corpus often places a sincere wish beside an inadequate form. That pairing defeats both an effort-only theory and a theory that authentic feeling will communicate by itself. Akito's E0146 global aspiration lacks the concrete force he expects until Slade's challenge makes another possibility present. E0187 then asks for changed means: a bilingual concept, Toya's arranging and collective rehearsal answer a professional test. His later wish to prove himself through ability alone remains; the enacted use of shared means does not erase that tension. [VBS synthesis](PJSK_VBS_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md); [E0187](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/VBS/PJSK_EVENT_0187_DEEP_READING.md), `PJSK:event:0187:08:001:0052–0084`.

An's E0201 language work makes the difference finer. Correct words and intended human effect are related but not identical. Study, questions, feedback and authorized practice make recipient-directed expression possible; memory of listening in the Street gives the work a particular orientation. A failed food order and continuing unknown words keep that development local. It is neither effortless emotional translation nor complete linguistic mastery. `PJSK:event:0201:06:002:0033–0072`; `PJSK:card:1368:02:002:0029–0067`.

WxS similarly distinguishes personal stake from copied technique. Rui's E0172 imitation of Tom's devices cannot produce the depth he seeks. Asking for help and using his own loneliness changes the work, yet the workshop receives it unevenly and its audience fit remains imperfect. Nene's development requires singing within a role and a whole play, not simply singing more impressively. Emu's intended comedy has to be trained, observed and revised after people respond differently. These are separate processes by which form becomes responsive; no common category of creative sincerity explains all three. [WxS synthesis](PJSK_WXS_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md), especially its differentiated learning and professional-recognition discussions.

MMJ's filmed life makes apparent spontaneity equally dependent on context. Minori's continuous effort to display ideal hopefulness can disrupt familiar performance. Her unplanned response to Hikarin matters because she has heard a particular account; it does not mean preparation should cease. Airi still calls for review, and an overlong introduction still needs redirection. The contrast is between particular forms that permit or obstruct a response, not between a false trained self and an invariably true untrained one. [E0174](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MMJ/PJSK_EVENT_0174_DEEP_READING.md).

N25 gives the same issue a more difficult emotional consequence. Kanade's song can reach Mafuyu without completing the search it opens. At E0192, imagining what happens if Mafuyu no longer needs her music exposes a question about Kanade's place, not a compositional defect solvable by one better song. At E0199 she pauses a maternal-warmth concept while continuing new demos. The work remains possible while its intended responsibility is reconsidered. Artistic agency can include suspending a particular explanation of what the work must do. [N25 synthesis](PJSK_N25_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md); [E0192](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/N25/PJSK_EVENT_0192_DEEP_READING.md); [E0199](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/N25/PJSK_EVENT_0199_DEEP_READING.md).

E0206 gives expression a purpose that need not be measured by either professional advancement or rescue. Kanade offers Mizuki a qualified personal method for giving difficult feeling form; Mizuki chooses lace, and the bereaved owner actually accepts it. The already meaningful costume project also becomes part of a received MMJ memorial performance. Neither work reverses death or proves the animal's inner happiness. Mizuki's later account gives Kanade a little courage while she explicitly acknowledges that making can fail. Here the creative result matters as an owned act and shared farewell, with no need to invent a solved problem. [E0206](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_EVENT_0206_DEEP_READING.md), `PJSK:event:0206:07:001:0012–0057`; `PJSK:event:0206:08:001:0004–0076`; `PJSK:card:1403:02:001:0018–0082`.

## Institutions make possibilities and impose measures

The units' growing range is materially organized. Freelance work requires scheduling and support. Overseas music requires money, lodging, translation, equipment and contracts. A professional theater experiment requires a proposal that can answer objections about price, staffing and audience. A school musical needs more than its director. These conditions are part of causation, not incidental obstacles surrounding an otherwise complete private talent.

They are also not uniformly hostile. No.9 addresses a real resource problem for VBS, although the full contract terms remain unshown. Emu's institutional proposal becomes more credible through inquiry and coauthorship, not merely family access. Shindo can help obtain time for Leo/need without deciding what the four ought to want. MMJ's competition gives exposure and measurable selection while also increasing labor and privacy costs. Treating every institution as either corrupt gatekeeper or neutral validator would erase these different relations. [VBS synthesis](PJSK_VBS_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md); [E0164](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/WXS/PJSK_EVENT_0164_DEEP_READING.md), `PJSK:event:0164:07:003:0013–0084`; [Leo/need synthesis](PJSK_LEO_NEED_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md); [MMJ synthesis](PJSK_MMJ_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md).

Different measures produce genuinely different results. RAD BLAST surpasses RAD WEEKEND; the analysis must retain that achievement. VBS also qualifies for the main tournament without having performed it. WxS receives a special judges' award while Arcland wins the top award, then completes its allocated performance week; neither distinction is merely semantic. MMJ's second-place qualification follows its cram-school production, but does not prove the cause of every vote or any pupil's future examination result. Haruka's next plan is explicitly adapted to different judging conditions and remains unperformed. [E0135](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/VBS/PJSK_EVENT_0135_DEEP_READING.md); [E0204](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/VBS/PJSK_EVENT_0204_DEEP_READING.md); [E0178](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/WXS/PJSK_EVENT_0178_DEEP_READING.md); [E0185](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/WXS/PJSK_EVENT_0185_DEEP_READING.md); [E0190](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MMJ/PJSK_EVENT_0190_DEEP_READING.md); [E0213](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MMJ/PJSK_EVENT_0213_DEEP_READING.md).

Institutional success also cannot decide the desired scale of a life. Ichika can value individual making while declining an agency that would add obligations. Saki can contribute to a large concert, enjoy it and still want proximity to individual faces. Shiho's larger-stage ambition has a recipient purpose of its own. Honami's coordinating skill does not yet reconcile these ends. E0220 therefore shows a limit to the recurring method of changing means: before a workable arrangement can be made, people may have to acknowledge that the destination itself is disputed. [E0212](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/LEO_NEED/PJSK_EVENT_0212_DEEP_READING.md); [E0220](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/LEO_NEED/PJSK_EVENT_0220_DEEP_READING.md).

## Shared work does not require equal roles or permanent co-location

The corpus values groups while refusing to make every legitimate choice a vote for sameness. Haruka's E0213 plan gives Shizuku greater visual emphasis after explanation, challenge and considered assent. The unequal arrangement does not expel the others from authorship. It also cannot authorize any future hierarchy without another decision. Shizuku's earlier help to Cheerful＊Days requires MMJ's time agreement and the rivals' request; a better shared live can be worth a competitive cost, without obliging everyone to forgive or cooperate forever. [E0213](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MMJ/PJSK_EVENT_0213_DEEP_READING.md), `PJSK:event:0213:08:001:0001–0105`; [E0183](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MMJ/PJSK_EVENT_0183_DEEP_READING.md), `PJSK:event:0183:06:003:0055–0099`.

WxS's departure from direct park employment becomes possible because stewardship is redistributed. Emu's initial refusal is real; staff and family commitments alter the choice before she joins the freelance company. E0160 supplies a complementary ending: Inuyama asks for a remembered performance as his park closes. The successful response honors that request rather than treating preservation of the institution as the only acceptable result. Common work can carry an ending well. [E0104](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/WXS/PJSK_EVENT_0104_DEEP_READING.md); [E0160](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_EVENT_0160_DEEP_READING.md).

E0214 makes separation available without making artistic effort worthless. The brothers' renewed submission fails; their chosen separate careers precede only a possible future collaboration. In E0199, Mizuki's attempt to sustain clothing work and Nightcord produces a completed test alongside lost sleep and school/work consequences. The conditional commitment is neither enrollment nor a sustainable regime already achieved. Mafuyu's reassurance that a future limit could be reconsidered is her statement, not a binding collective agreement. These cases prevent the present value of a group from becoming a rule that belonging requires unlimited output or permanent shared employment. [E0214](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_EVENT_0214_DEEP_READING.md); [E0199](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/N25/PJSK_EVENT_0199_DEEP_READING.md).

## Effective results leave the process open to criticism

The most difficult comparative evidence concerns productive harm. Airi's E0198 request for one continuous take is bounded by the director's schedule permission and Mao's accepted participation. That negotiated process cannot certify unlimited immersion, but its sequence matters. Rui's E0200 provocation instead elicits a take through an insult, followed by Nagamine's hurt flight, an apology and only then a fuller account of the actor's difficulty. The next rehearsal separately obtains permission for coaching. Similar artistic gains do not make these procedures equivalent. `PJSK:event:0198:07:003:0038–0112`; `PJSK:event:0200:07:001:0002–0119`; `PJSK:event:0200:08:001:0009–0025`.

Rui's E0185 uncertainty about pressuring Nene and Tsukasa's E0208 refusal to promise that he will never repeat dangerous immersion keep evaluation inside the story. Nene's thanks are a real receipt; Rui still cannot know that the method was best. Tsukasa's successful performance and the group's promise to retrieve him do not show an implemented reliable safeguard. Toya's E0193 requested performance while ill similarly has conditions, a result, incomplete memory and later rest; the story does not provide a generally safe technique. [WxS synthesis](PJSK_WXS_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md); [VBS synthesis](PJSK_VBS_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md).

The analytical conclusion is bounded: the narrative permits artistic success and criticism of its means to coexist. It does not prove that every painful method is necessary, or that removing the pain would have left the result unchanged. Those are counterfactual claims the corpus does not establish. Retrospective gratitude is evidence about the recipient's later position, not prior permission, complete safety or an experiment isolating the method's causal contribution.

## Comparative findings and open trajectories

| Recurring problem | Supported cross-unit finding | Counterexample or limit |
|---|---|---|
| Owning a wish | Chosen aims can use inherited skills, received care and collaborators' work. | Origins and institutions still shape what choices cost; ownership is not self-sufficiency. |
| Making an effect | Recipient information can change form, timing, medium or allocation of work. | A maker may misunderstand a recipient; some desired ends conflict rather than combine. |
| Recognizing achievement | Local receipt and formal results deserve their exact scale. | Popularity, grades, admission, health, profit and future success are not interchangeable. |
| Sharing authorship | Different roles and expertise can support equal membership. | A particular assent does not authorize permanent hierarchy or unlimited labor. |
| Continuing together | Institutions and responsibilities can be reorganized to preserve valued relations. | Closure, separation and conditional future reconsideration can also be chosen. |
| Judging methods | Results can be meaningful while the process remains harmful or disputed. | Neither gratitude nor a good performance establishes necessity or safety. |

At the cutoff these are usable capacities and unresolved tensions, not a completed artistic philosophy. Leo/need has not agreed on its future scale. MMJ has not performed its semifinal. VBS has not performed the main tournament or resolved the warehouse suspicion. WxS has not completed the future audition or proved its protective commitment reliable. Nightcord has not solved Kanade's rescue obligation, Ena's technical ambitions or Mizuki's dual workload. These open trajectories constrain the full-series convergence and every reconstruction scenario that uses it.

This generation becomes frozen upon the phase's accepted release. It changes no historical state owner and authorizes no new source admission. Later evidence or substantive correction requires a successor synthesis; the mutable ledgers and character models retain their separate update responsibilities.
