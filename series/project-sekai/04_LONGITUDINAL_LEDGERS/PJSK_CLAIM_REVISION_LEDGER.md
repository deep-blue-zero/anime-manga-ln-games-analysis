# Project SEKAI Claim Revision Ledger

## 1. Purpose

This is the canonical routing ledger for substantive analytical claim transitions. It prevents early or superseded interpretations from remaining in circulation without an authority state.

Allowed transition states:

- **PRESERVE** — current evidence leaves claim intact.
- **STRENGTHEN** — current evidence increases confidence/scope.
- **REVISE** — claim remains partly useful but formulation changes materially.
- **DOWNGRADE** — claim may apply narrowly but should no longer be used as a general account.
- **REJECT** — claim should not be used as current authority.
- **OPEN** — evidence insufficient; keep unresolved.

A transition applies to the claim, not necessarily to the source document containing the earlier formulation.

The ledger begins with the consolidated P01→P05 N25 main-story claim history from `PJSK_N25_MAIN_STORY_SYNTHESIS.md` §16 and currently integrates longitudinal claim authority through EVENT_0051. EVENT_0030, EVENT_0031, EVENT_0037, EVENT_0040, EVENT_0043, and EVENT_0046 were screened I0; EVENT_0032, EVENT_0038, and EVENT_0041 add evidence without governing claim transitions; EVENT_0033, EVENT_0034, EVENT_0035, EVENT_0036, EVENT_0039, EVENT_0042, EVENT_0044, EVENT_0045, EVENT_0047, and EVENT_0051 supply the later transitions/refinements recorded below.
