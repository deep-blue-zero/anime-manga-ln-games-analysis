# Project SEKAI Theme and Motif Ledger

## 1. Purpose and caution

This is the canonical mutable ledger for recurring themes, conceptual structures, motifs, and symbolic/creative patterns that are useful **across multiple readings**.

Unlike the state ledgers, theme claims require recurrence. Positive N25 thematic evidence is analytically integrated through EVENT_0051; EVENT_0030, EVENT_0031, EVENT_0037, EVENT_0040, EVENT_0043, and EVENT_0046 were screened at I0, EVENT_0038 supplied bounded Rui-side contextual support, and EVENT_0041 produced no theme transition. EVENT_0045 and EVENT_0047 add further cross-event recurrence, with EVENT_0047 especially strengthening origin-level creative reciprocity, negative-affect self-evidence, accompaniment below full rescue, ordinary-life-without-cure, and care-without-total-disclosure. Selected entries may therefore be cross-event validated through EVENT_0051 where recurrence is demonstrated. Do not promote these into franchise-wide theses without additional unit foundations.

Status vocabulary used here:

- `FOUNDATION_ESTABLISHED` — strongly supported across the N25 main story.
- `CROSS_EVENT_VALIDATION_PENDING` — plausible recurring N25 theme; later events must test persistence/transformation.
- `CROSS_UNIT_VALIDATION_PENDING` — do not generalize to Project SEKAI as a whole yet.
- `OPEN` — insufficient evidence.

## 2. Theme: first-person authority

**theme_id:** `TH-N25-001`  
**Status:** `CROSS_EVENT_VALIDATED through EVENT_0036`; `BOUNDED_CROSS_UNIT_SUPPORT` via EVENT_0007 and EVENT_0019; broader validation pending.

### Definition

First-person authority is the practical ability to treat one's own preference, feeling, interpretation, value, or chosen identity as meaningful evidence about how to live.

### N25 expressions

- **Mafuyu:** external judgment increasingly replaces inaccessible internal preference.
- **Kanade:** moral legitimacy of self becomes contingent on successful rescue effect.
- **Ena:** external artistic recognition threatens to adjudicate whether her chosen artist identity is legitimate.
- **Mizuki:** personal preference is comparatively clear, but social systems challenge its right to be self-authorizing.

### Main-story movement

The ending does not produce isolated self-sufficiency. It begins to establish **contact without total definition**: another person can mirror, care, or state relational stakes without claiming complete authority over the self.

### Later-event questions

- Does N25 relationship increase each member's first-person authority?
- When does care become another externally authored demand?
- Does Mafuyu recover internally usable preference?
- Does Kanade learn ordinary permission to create beyond rescue outcome?
- Does Ena tolerate an artist identity not continuously proven by external hierarchy?
- Does Mizuki gain relationships in which preference does not require defense?

## 3. Theme cluster: saving, finding, reaching, recognition

**theme_id:** `TH-N25-002`  
**Status:** `CROSS_EVENT_VALIDATED through EVENT_0002`.

These terms should remain analytically distinct.

### Saving

Primarily Kanade's initial category. It implies successful outcome and encourages responsibility inflation. It is not simply condemned; Kanade's persistence matters. The risk is treating another person's recovery as a result Kanade must personally produce.

### Finding

Primarily Mafuyu's category. The search concerns access to self/feeling, not external acclaim. The main-story breakthrough is that finding can become relational without becoming wholly externally authored.

### Reaching

Miku's mediating category. It permits partial efficacy: contact can matter without solving the entire crisis. This is a potential correction to Kanade's saved/failed binary.

### Recognition

Especially central to Ena, but relevant to all four. Recognition can reflect, command, judge, or condition legitimacy. The key analytical question is:

> **What kind of recognition is being offered, and does it increase or reduce first-person authority?**

### Later-event questions

Track whether these four categories converge, diverge, or change meaning across later N25 stories.

## 4. Theme: creative work as psychological evidence

**theme_id:** `TH-N25-003`  
**Status:** `CROSS_EVENT_VALIDATED through EVENT_0036`.

Nightcord members often perceive changes in one another through creative output before verbal disclosure.

### N25 foundation functions

- Ena distinguishes K and OWN through affective temperature.
- Kanade recognizes traces of Snow in OWN.
- Mafuyu uses creation as both expression and search instrument.
- Kanade's music functions as expression, contact, moral evidence, and intervention.
- Ena's art is the site where chosen identity and legitimacy conflict.
- Mizuki's visual creation and personal aesthetics overlap with first-person authorship.

### Guardrail

Creative work is not a literal transcript of conscious intent. Treat formal/aesthetic shifts as **character-state evidence requiring contextual interpretation**, not as transparent confession.

### Later-event questions

- Which artistic changes precede explicit psychological change?
- Can creative output reveal a transition before the character can name it?
- When do other characters interpret that output correctly or incorrectly?

## 6. Theme: anonymity, embodiment, and chosen relation

**theme_id:** `TH-N25-004`  
**Status:** `CROSS_EVENT_VALIDATED through EVENT_0002`.

### Anonymity as enabling structure

Handles and online collaboration allow creative intimacy without importing every offline role or stigma.

### Anonymity as crisis limitation

When Snow disappears, the group lacks address, family access, school contact, and emergency pathways. Knowing someone's work deeply is not the same as knowing how to reach the person materially.

### Embodiment

Real names, faces, SEKAI co-presence, and later offline meeting destroy some protective distance but create new forms of responsibility and ordinary intimacy.

### Chosen relation

The endpoint does **not** require compulsory total disclosure. Nightcord becomes more connected while knowledge remains partial and privately owned.

### Later-event questions

- Which disclosures are chosen versus forced?
- Does offline embodiment improve support or increase pressure?
- Do handles continue to carry distinct relational meaning after real-name contact?

## 8. Theme: hope as burden / search exhaustion

**theme_id:** `TH-N25-005`  
**Status:** `FOUNDATION_ESTABLISHED for Mafuyu`; `CROSS_EVENT_VALIDATION_PENDING`.

Mafuyu's terminal state shows that hope can become painful when it repeatedly activates a search that ends in failure. Disappearance appears attractive partly because hopeless certainty is less exhausting than repeated hope → failure → despair.

The main-story endpoint therefore means **continued search despite fear of hope**, not restored optimism.

### Later-event questions

- What kinds of hope become tolerable?
- Does relational support reduce the cost of failed searching?
- When does renewed hope create progress versus destabilization?

## 9. Motif/theme: curse, obligation, and life-sustaining dependency

**theme_id:** `TH-N25-006`  
**Status:** `CROSS_EVENT_VALIDATED through EVENT_0002`.

Kanade experiences her father's words and musical ideal as an obligation capable of sustaining life and constraining it. Mafuyu recognizes this structure as a curse. The endpoint creates another reciprocal promise that can simultaneously:

- interrupt isolation;
- sustain continued creation/search;
- increase mutual disclosure;
- and create dependency/obligation risk.

### Guardrail

Do not pre-classify the relationship as either healthy mutual support or pure codependency. Later events must show whether the bond increases flexible agency or hardens into externally required continuation.

## 10. Theme: relational mirroring versus externally authored identity

**theme_id:** `TH-N25-007`  
**Status:** `CROSS_EVENT_VALIDATED through EVENT_0035`.

The main story distinguishes two ways another person can tell someone something about themselves:

- **external authorship:** “this is who you should be because we say so”;
- **relational mirroring:** “this is something I perceive in you/your work; you decide what it means.”

Kanade telling Mafuyu that she hears a recurring Mafuyu sound, followed by Mafuyu asking to hear more about it, is the main-story seed for relational mirroring.

### Later-event questions

- Does Mafuyu increasingly use others as mirrors without surrendering interpretive authority?
- When do friends accidentally reproduce the same external-authority pattern they oppose?

## 11. Theme: conflict tolerance as relationship maturation

**theme_id:** `TH-N25-008`  
**Status:** `CROSS_EVENT_VALIDATED through EVENT_0002`.

Nightcord begins with productive anonymous harmony and develops into a group capable of surviving:

- exposure;
- anger;
- refusal;
- unequal intervention ethics;
- resentment;
- incomplete reconciliation.

The endpoint's important change is not absence of conflict but **higher tolerance for disagreement without automatic dissolution**.

### Later-event questions

- Does conflict become safer over time?
- Which members interpret argument as care, rejection, or threat?
- What repair behaviors emerge?

## 12.1 EVENT_0004 motif and theme refinements

### Motif: performed smile ↔ spontaneous smile

**motif_id:** `MO-N25-004`  
**Status:** `CROSS_EVENT_VALIDATED`; cross-unit generalization pending.

EVENT_0004 makes the expression/affect gap unusually explicit through Emu's observer language. Mafuyu's school smile is socially legible and rewarded, yet Emu sometimes experiences it as a smile that does not feel like one. **Interpretive guardrail:** do not turn “real smile” into a metaphysical test or let Emu define Mafuyu's identity. The motif marks variation in role optimization and affective leakage, not an infallible facial diagnostic.

EVENT_0002 established creative externalization as an affect-access route. EVENT_0004 broadens the mechanism: coordinated physical action, competition, novelty, and discrepant social feedback can also produce self-relevant affect before explicit interpretation.

This strengthens first-person authority rather than weakening it: observers may notice incongruence or salience, but the final meaning remains open until Mafuyu can integrate it herself.
