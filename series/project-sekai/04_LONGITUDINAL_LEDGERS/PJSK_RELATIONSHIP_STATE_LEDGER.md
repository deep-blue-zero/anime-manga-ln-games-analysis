# Project SEKAI Relationship State Ledger

## 1. Purpose

This is the canonical mutable ledger for **relationship state and relationship transitions**. It exists because character behavior is conditioned not only by individual psychology but by what one participant expects, knows, trusts, fears, resents, admires, or permits with another.

Relationships are directional and may be asymmetric. A trusts B does not imply B trusts A to the same degree. A understands B does not imply B understands A. Relationship state can change without a new global character state.

Current N25 entries integrate the completed main-story foundation plus positive relationship evidence through EVENT_0053. EVENT_0030, EVENT_0031, EVENT_0037, EVENT_0040, EVENT_0043, and EVENT_0046 were fully screened at I0; EVENT_0038 adds audience-side corroboration of Rui's outsider history; EVENT_0039 advances Ena-Mizuki to explicit friendship/non-extractive waiting under deferred disclosure; EVENT_0041 adds later ordinary Mizuki-An friendship continuity; EVENT_0042 advances Kanade-Mafuyu into reciprocal positive-affect recognition, establishes a bounded Mafuyu-Miku dyad, initializes Mafuyu-Ichika and Kanade-Ichika cross-unit states, and extends the Kanade-Honami bridge; EVENT_0044 broadens the bounded Shinonome sibling/father relationship model; EVENT_0045 strengthens second-year N25 ritual continuity and Ena-Mizuki waiting; EVENT_0047 reconstructs Kanade-Mafuyu reciprocity at origin, strongly deepens Kanade-Honami causal care, and strengthens group/Ena-Mizuki/Mafuyu-Miku continuity without successor relationship IDs; EVENT_0051 strengthens Mizuki-An safe normality, personalized N25 reciprocity, bounded Virtual Singer companionship, and Shinonome seasonal familiarity without successor IDs; EVENT_0052 initializes bounded Mizuki-Shizuku practical creative collaboration. Other units and material later than EVENT_0052 remain pending.

## 2. Relationship record schema

Each mature relationship record should track:

- `relationship_id`
- participants / group
- temporal boundary
- relationship form
- trust and intimacy
- admiration / resentment / rivalry
- dependency / caretaking pressure
- disclosure and knowledge asymmetry
- conflict grammar
- repair grammar
- address/register changes where relevant
- behavioral consequence
- transition trigger
- evidence
- confidence
- next state
