## Two standards for seriousness

Akito's soccer history explains an existing value hierarchy rather than documenting a new present-day psychological state. Being naturally adept and externally expected to succeed once allowed him a vague professional aspiration; meeting a nationally strong team showed him a different level of dedication. RAD WEEKEND became the thing for which he says he would sacrifice everything. He evaluates An's choice of a novice as a betrayal of the seriousness he had expected from another direct witness (`05:002:0003–0018`). The inference from no event experience to insufficient resolve is his inference. It is not validated simply because he speaks from genuine prior disappointment.

An's criterion works through relational possibility: she believes enough practice and her own presence will let Kohane begin. The invitation has practical features that make this plausible: next month, a vacant slot, and a venue An considers relatively small (`05:001:0004–0012`). Those are conditions for a beginner's opportunity, not proof of readiness for every event disruption. Kohane takes comfort specifically from the thought that An will be beside her. Record this dependence precisely rather than converting it either into parasitism or into already secure equality.

Kohane's answer to Toya supplies the central prospective ambiguity: `思ってる……って、思いたい` (`08:004:0016`). She does not falsely claim an old, fully understood RAD WEEKEND ambition. She says that learning about it is recent, that singing with An produces unprecedented pleasure, and that she now wants to pursue the dream together. Her goal is already more than passive admiration, but the distinction between personal choice and confidence borrowed from a partner is not settled. Candor about incompleteness is evidence of epistemic honesty; it should not automatically be scored as either insufficient commitment or complete maturity.

Toya refuses Akito's premature certainty in episode05 and independently witnesses Kohane's effort and the pair's successful street exchange in07. His question in08 therefore emerges from sustained attention. His own repeated silence after hearing “half-hearted” is meaningful evidence of discomfort and incipient self-comparison, but its full family cause is not yet revealed. P02 must not state it as already disclosed to the audience or group. The phase ends with an attempted intervention whose effect is still unknown.
