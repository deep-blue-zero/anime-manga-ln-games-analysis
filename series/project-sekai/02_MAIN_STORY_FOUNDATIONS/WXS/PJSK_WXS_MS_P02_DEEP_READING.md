# Wonderlands×Showtime main story — Phase 02

## Scope and causal claim

Episodes 05–08 assemble the four-person company and its collective name while leaving their reasons for participating imperfectly aligned. [P01](PJSK_WXS_MS_P01_DEEP_READING.md) supplies the pressured starting assignment. Recruitment succeeds through distinct channels: Tsukasa's recognition of craft, Rui's conditional acceptance, a technical means for Nene to participate, and Emu's inclusive enthusiasm. Company formation is not yet repaired trust or unified purpose.

## Narrative and causal structure

Rui's unfinished alchemist show draws Tsukasa's specific appreciation of delivery, movement and the capacity to make audiences care about robots. Security interrupts because Rui is performing without authorization. A classmate's report and an initially unhelpful peer allow Tsukasa to find him at school, but the rumors are social judgments rather than objective diagnoses. Rui has been observing Tsukasa by drone; his amused interest does not initially amount to accepting recruitment. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_05:01:001:0003–0029` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_05:01:002:0007–0014` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_05:01:003:0003–0007` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_05:01:004:0005–0023`

Tsukasa's extravagant promise of responsiveness makes collaboration sound attractive. Rui states that he prefers solo work and wants audiences to enjoy it, then conditions agreement on including a second person. The glance toward Nene and his private remark about an opportunity show a distinct recruiting purpose, but its complete history is not yet explained. Tsukasa accepts on an assurance of ability. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_06:01:001:0005–0036`

Nene's robot-mediated audition becomes an argument over what can count as stage participation. Tsukasa initially rejects the medium, then hears and sees enough within the reported scene to concede competence, while fearing displacement from attention. Emu both welcomes the robot and explicitly recognizes Nene as the person behind it. Emu's inference that skill reflects love and practice matters because it values labor; it is not the analyst's independent assessment of a watched performance. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_07:01:001:0004–0014` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_07:01:001:0023–0069`

Tsukasa writes the script, yet cannot impose his preferred troupe name. Rui turns Emu's account of theatrical worlds into a naming principle, Nene suggests the combined wording, Emu inscribes its final form and explains the multiplication sign, and Tsukasa accepts the outcome while redirecting attention to the show. The name is produced through negotiation and asymmetrical contribution, not unilateral leadership. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_08:01:003:0002–0045`

## Psychological states

**Tsukasa:** confidence now organizes real work: sustained searching, a script, accepting a capable but abrasive colleague, and relinquishing a preferred name. He can perceive others' craft and still measure it against his visibility. At home, he immediately performs to lift Saki's spirits when she worries about burdening others. This preserves an audience-responsive capacity outside his conscious star rationale. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_05:01:001:0010–0012` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_07:01:001:0048–0063` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_08:01:001:0008–0022`

**Emu:** the goal remains a busy, smile-producing stage. She readily admits people and unusual media; she treats Nene's skill as a reason for belonging and articulates a generative vision of many theatrical worlds. Her stage attachment remains incompletely explained. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_06:01:003:0003–0017` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_07:01:001:0058–0064` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_08:01:003:0023–0039`

**Nene:** direct verbal resistance through the robot contrasts with remaining physically hidden. Her admission to Rui that she had put shows behind her conflicts productively with actually coming to the audition. Rui's prompt identifies an ambivalence; it does not authorize us to supply the undisclosed cause. Games are a practical framing he offers for re-entry, not proof that theatrical desire is just a game. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_07:01:001:0026–0055` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_07:01:001:0071–0080`

**Rui:** curiosity, technical authorship, playfulness and conditional cooperation coexist. He directly claims solo preference and an audience-enjoyment aim, while making room for Nene's participation. At this point both preference and protective accompaniment deserve preservation; a full compensatory-solitude explanation remains OPEN. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_06:01:001:0005–0006` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_06:01:001:0028–0035` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_07:01:001:0004–0010` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_07:01:001:0074–0079`

## Relationships and knowledge

Tsukasa–Rui becomes a performer/director collaboration grounded in stated recognition and extravagant promises; the mutually agreed first-name transition marks an observable change. Tsukasa–Nene begins through challenge, demonstration and reluctant concession, rather than uncomplicated admiration. Emu–Nene begins through welcome and attention to effort. Rui–Nene already has childhood show contact, technical support and private teasing that the new group does not yet fully understand. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_06:01:001:0031–0036` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_07:01:001:0046–0067` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_07:01:001:0071–0079`

Rui knows where Nene is and supplies the robot's operating account. Tsukasa and Emu know the operator's name and abilities as demonstrated through that device; they do not yet know a prior failed performance or its consequences. Both Rui and Nene react to Emu's surname, but recognition of her family is only suggested, not certified by completed disclosure. Saki recalls puppet shows and the rabbit at home; Tsukasa's response remains uncertain. No scene transmits this family conversation to the company. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_06:01:003:0010–0020` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_07:01:001:0014–0022` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_08:01:001:0017–0021`

## Behavior and speech

Tsukasa's grand numerical promises are offered without a factual basis, which he openly admits when challenged. Rui answers grandiosity with amused questions and conditional assent; Nene uses short, cutting challenges when mediated by the robot, while speaking hesitantly about withdrawing from shows in private. Emu's invitations can read unusual participation as immediately normal. The exchanges supply contrasting context-dependent registers, not catchphrase substitutes for motive. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_06:01:001:0015–0020` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_07:01:001:0046–0057` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_07:01:001:0072–0079`

## Theme, alternatives and continuity

The stage begins to accommodate differences in body, medium, role and communicative ease. The robot is simultaneously authored technology, a means of performance, and a protective distance; only the first two functions are explicit enough here to establish firmly, while the third is a strong but bounded inference from Nene's hidden participation.
