# Wonderlands×Showtime main story — Phase 05

## Scope and causal claim

Episodes 15–18 test whether [Tsukasa's recovered purpose](PJSK_WXS_MS_P04_DEEP_READING.md) can reach three differently situated people. Repair requires recognizing Emu's stake, listening to Nene's disclosed fear and addressing Rui's belief that their values are incompatible. Their returns are not interchangeable forgiveness scenes, and intrusive pursuit must remain visible alongside later assent.

## Three distinct repair problems

Emu chooses the Ferris wheel to show the stage within its setting and disclose her grandfather's authorship, his death a year earlier, and her father's conditional willingness to reconsider demolition if enough customers make it financially viable. She says she withheld the situation so others could concentrate on making the show, following her grandfather's account of necessary support work. Her proposed surrender stems from blaming her own wish for the loss of everyone's smiles. Tsukasa challenges the exclusion of her own happiness, accepts responsibility and asks to work together again; she affirmatively agrees. Her account is direct evidence of her beliefs and reported family conditions, not an independently witnessed meeting with her father. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_15:01:002:0007–0042`

Nene returns of her own initiative to retrieve the robot, apologizes to it, experiences the stage from its position and sings privately. These actions establish continuing desire before anyone persuades her. When discovered, she escapes on the robot. Tsukasa and Emu pursue and physically intercept her; Tsukasa then recognizes that she may not want to face him and permits her to remain turned away. That accommodation is meaningful, but the encounter is not a wholly pressure-free invitation. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_15:01:003:0002–0019` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_16:01:002:0002–0038`

He names his failure to consider others' feelings and asks for renewed collaboration. Nene responds with desire and discloses a junior-theater performance in which she could not retrieve a line, followed by trembling and difficulty speaking before people. Her claim that she ruined everything is retrospective self-blame; the source shows her failure experience, not an objective judgment of total responsibility. Tsukasa replaces the demand for perfect success with recognition that attempting a show entails possible failure, specifically acknowledging that he should not have blamed a colleague who tried. Nene agrees to return. No clinical cure or general freedom from fear follows from this decision. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_16:01:002:0034–0078`

Nene leads the attempt to reach Rui and gives the others childhood-neighbor history. His resumed alchemist story narrates how joint work can begin happily and then dissolve as ideas diverge. Its statement that neither side need be at fault belongs to the performed story; the autobiographical resonance is supported by Nene's account but remains interpretive rather than a universal exoneration of every proposed device. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_17:01:001:0002–0028`

Rui acknowledges Nene's return yet distinguishes her forgiveness from his own willingness to collaborate. Tsukasa's apology does not answer Rui's belief that stardom is merely a private end. Nene and Emu then actively help Tsukasa devise another approach. This preserves Rui as a participant with a separate judgment, and Nene as an agent of the repair rather than only its beneficiary. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_17:01:001:0037–0060` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_17:01:002:0006–0013`

The three intercept Rui, restrain him and transport him to the SEKAI despite his refusal. There they present a self-implicating show modeled on the company's collapse. Tsukasa performs his forgotten motive and explicitly defines his aim as a show that gives audience and colleagues joy. KAITO leaves the alchemist's next decision unwritten and yields the role. Rui accepts through renewed directing while maintaining critical standards. The staging offers room to answer at its decisive point; it does not erase the coercive route into the audience seat. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_18:01:002:0003–0011` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_18:01:003:0007–0066`

## Psychological and relationship consequences

**Tsukasa:** repair becomes differentiated action: admitting fault, recognizing Emu's inclusion in the collective, allowing Nene not to face him, hearing her history, and making his own motive accountable in a show for Rui. He retains assertive tactics and performance-based problem solving. The old rhetoric is partly redirected, not uniformly eliminated. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_15:01:002:0031–0042` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_16:01:002:0032–0072` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_18:01:003:0040–0054`

**Emu:** she moves from concealed burden and intended surrender to shared intention with Tsukasa, then helps reunite the group. Her independent grief and desired stage persist. At this boundary only Tsukasa has explicitly received the financial conditions; a reunited company is not yet evidence of completed all-member disclosure. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_15:01:002:0014–0042` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_17:01:002:0006–0011`

**Nene:** she moves from private retrieval and desire to directly stated recommitment and active mediation with Rui. Her disclosed symptoms remain history and present vulnerability, not a diagnostic label. Her decision to appear in the repair play provides a bounded instance of participation before an unusually small, familiar audience. It does not by itself predict general public ease. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_16:01:002:0046–0078` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_17:01:001:0004–0015` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_18:01:003:0017–0018`

**Rui:** remembered classmates' accusations and withdrawals contextualize his distrust without proving that their reading of his intent was correct. He can accept Nene's independent decision and still refuse Tsukasa. His eventual return follows a demonstrated articulation of compatible purpose; his immediate direction that enthusiasm or momentum alone cannot move people preserves artistic judgment inside renewed affiliation. This line does not explicitly criticize their physical coercion; that earlier conduct has its own separate evidence. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_18:01:001:0003–0007` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_17:01:001:0047–0057` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_18:01:003:0045–0066`

## Epistemic and speech boundaries

Tsukasa learns Emu's family condition privately on the ride. Tsukasa and Emu hear Nene's past from her; they learn the childhood-neighbor connection from her afterward. Nene learns Rui's earlier statement about needing collaborators only when Tsukasa recounts it. Rui hears the redefined stardom claim and its sister-linked history within the play. The script conveys selected experience, not automatic access to every childhood memory or every backstage conversation. The singers participate in the repair show but are not granted unbounded access to Emu's finances or another SEKAI. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_15:01:002:0008–0018` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_16:01:002:0048–0061` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_17:01:001:0002–0015` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_18:01:003:0031–0047`

Apology becomes specific acknowledgment and a request for renewed collaboration; Nene's hesitation carries chosen disclosure rather than silence alone. Rui's refusal is measured and explicit, and his acceptance arrives in role as the alchemist. The distinction between performed accusation and actual personal endorsement is especially important in this self-representing play. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_16:01:002:0034–0046` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_17:01:001:0047–0057` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_18:01:003:0022–0025` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_18:01:003:0060–0066`

## Theme, revisions and handoff

The governing revision is who counts within the promised audience of happiness. Emu must not erase herself; Nene must remain a valued collaborator even when an attempt fails; Rui must be addressed as someone whose trust matters independently. Creative work becomes a form of communication, but persuasion remains ethically uneven.
