## The foundational mechanism

The company forms twice. The first formation assembles workable roles before the meaning of their shared work is secure. The second re-forms those roles around a more inclusive account of whom the show should serve. The technical failure exposes the distance between those formations; it does not by itself create their ethical disagreements. A show can have talented, hardworking contributors and real moments of pleasure while remaining unable to treat a colleague's failure without destroying trust. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_07:01:001:0048–0065` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:001:0018–0038` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_13:01:001:0007–0058`

Tsukasa's arc is therefore not adequately described as discovering kindness or abandoning ambition. The opening supplies both his childhood desire to make Saki smile and his present inability to explain that desire. His care continues to appear in ordinary family interaction and in an impromptu performance for her even before the crisis. What has become misaligned is the conscious hierarchy that makes visibility and uninterrupted success the route to being a star. The decisive recovery gives an already observable value explicit self-knowledge and makes it available for relational repair. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_00:01:001:0001–0017` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_00:01:003:0011–0030` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_08:01:001:0008–0021` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_14:01:002:0059–0088`

The ending preserves his distinctive self-presentation. He still enjoys praise and performs grandiose confidence. Its practical meaning changes when he asks what others want, shares credit for the song, calls for Emu's ritual and promises that Nene has colleagues available if something goes wrong. These actions, rather than a purified personality description, are the main-story evidence for development. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_19:01:001:0011–0017` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_19:01:002:0014–0022` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_20:01:002:0026–0050`

## Failure has more than one causal owner

The first performance fails through an identifiable chain: prolonged robot practice and a missed charge check, a loss of control during Nene's solo, an attempted improvisational recovery, a fall onto Tsukasa and a forced ending. Rui acknowledges responsibility for not checking charge; Tsukasa and Emu demonstrate real efforts to keep the fiction coherent. Nene's statement that all of it is her fault exceeds this distributed evidence. Treating her mediation as inherently disqualifying would also erase the successful rehearsal and the earlier audience response. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:001:0046–0064` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_12:01:001:0023–0064` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_13:01:001:0005–0020`

Relational collapse occurs when a valid concern for this audience becomes the requirement never to fail and then an attack on a person's vulnerability. Rui correctly identifies the need for collaborators but overreaches when he turns Tsukasa's current conduct into an absolute judgment of motive and future. The analysis can hold harmful behavior, professional commitment, real effort and incomplete self-understanding together. No accusation needs to become omniscient narration. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_13:01:001:0012–0046`

## The group is a network of unequal relationships

Rui–Nene precedes the company: familiarity permits teasing, technical help, accompaniment and, later, Nene's challenge to his retreat. The same protective concern can become withdrawal on her behalf when Rui leaves the robot behind and says she need not perform. Her independent return to retrieve it and her later mediation keep her own preference separate from his protection. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_07:01:001:0071–0079` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:001:0049–0064` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_13:01:002:0002–0010` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_15:01:003:0002–0017` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_17:01:001:0037–0040`

Tsukasa–Emu begins with misrecognition and pressure, then becomes a relationship in which he can include her own happiness in the meaning of everyone. ## Spaces, media and collective labor

The old stage first looks like a failure of prestige, then becomes a site of memory, work and chosen continuation. Emu's familial stake adds institutional pressure to its symbolic importance; profitability is a condition she reports, not an abstract antagonist without practical force. The saved stage results from repeated shows, publicity and effort as well as changed interpersonal understanding. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_01:01:004:0024–0036` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_15:01:002:0007–0021` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_20:01:002:0002–0024`
