# Wonderlands×Showtime main story — Phase 03

## Scope and causal claim

Episodes 09–11 turn [the assembled company](PJSK_WXS_MS_P02_DEEP_READING.md) into a working production. Shared pleasure and reported rehearsal competence increase, yet performer safety, emotional exposure, technical preparation and the meaning of success are not thereby settled. The boundary is the opening call of the first public show; its outcome is unknown in this snapshot.

## Narrative and causal structure

Rui proposes spectacular effects and demanding physical work. Tsukasa first objects to explicit risk, then accepts after Emu emphasizes visibility and attendance. Rui invokes the earlier promise and threatens withdrawal. Nene supplies a history of collaborators leaving in response to his staging, but that account must remain attributed. The cooperation achieved is real, yet it is organized partly by status incentives and contingent pressure. Nothing in the text permits treating the devices as independently certified safe. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_09:01:001:0008–0046`

Nene notices Rui's unusual good mood as he goes to acquire parts. His later thanks for Tsukasa realizing his plans provide direct corroboration of experienced creative pleasure. This is stronger than the initial detached solo-preference claim, but does not yet explain its origin. Tsukasa's performance receives qualified recognition from Nene and corrective direction from Rui, motivating a visit to the SEKAI for a concrete acting hint. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_09:01:002:0002–0007` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_09:01:003:0003–0019` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:001:0023–0026`

KAITO demonstrates the prince after examining the script. Tsukasa says he has gained a hint and wants to return to practice, while still resisting questions about why he wants stardom. Receiving useful instruction does not entail accepting the singers' account of his deeper purpose. Miku separately encounters the hidden Nene and permits her to speak when ready. Neither conversation is automatically shared with every person in the space. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_10:01:002:0019–0050` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_10:01:002:0032–0043` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_10:01:002:0062–0072`

Preparation includes actor training, Nene teaching Emu, repairing audience seating and assisted publicity. A rehearsal judged successful increases collective hope. Nene nevertheless stays to practice because she does not want to fail; Rui offers support, advises against overdoing it, then chooses not to interrupt renewed practice. At the boundary the audience has assembled and the company begins. ## Psychological states

**Tsukasa:** effort, excitement and self-celebration coexist. He explicitly realizes that his restless anticipation is enjoyment, and he recognizes the others' work through a condescending star-centered register. His response to Nene's nerves is ambiguous: Rui suggests encouragement, while Nene hears unreflective self-importance. Preserve both readings instead of declaring a secretly perfect comfort strategy. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:001:0024–0030` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:002:0002–0005` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:003:0007–0012`

**Emu:** she seeks help with singing, receives it, participates in repair and publicity, and directly thanks Tsukasa because the next day's show seems likely to realize her dream. The dream is explicit; the optimism is prospective. Her practical request qualifies a reading that she only supplies exuberance. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:001:0005–0015` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:001:0032–0038`

**Nene:** the robot enables pointed criticism and teaching, while Miku's face-to-face attention produces hesitation and visible difficulty speaking. She wishes to please an audience and fears failure, pursuing extra practice despite a successful rehearsal. This is domain- and medium-conditioned agency, not incapacity or a diagnosis. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_10:01:002:0032–0043` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:001:0005–0009` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:001:0046–0064`

**Rui:** investment becomes increasingly observable in parts acquisition, exacting guidance, gratitude and accompanying Nene's practice. Creative experimentation can strain others; warm support does not negate that tension. His choice to leave her practicing is a bounded judgment, not evidence that he knows or intends a later problem. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_09:01:001:0026–0045` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_09:01:002:0002–0007` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:001:0049–0064`

## Relationships and epistemic divisions

The group has functional role differentiation and enjoyable routines. Tsukasa–Rui grows through unusual artistic responsiveness; Emu–Nene through requested teaching; Rui–Nene through attunement to anxious preparation. Yet Tsukasa's attention remains largely on product and prominence, and he has not learned the history behind Nene's difficulty. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:001:0005–0009` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:001:0024–0038` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:001:0049–0064`

Rui and Nene acquire direct experience of the Wonderland SEKAI; Rui's attempted explanations are hypotheses generated by that experience, not established ontology. Miku knows Nene has difficulty with face-to-face attention from their encounter. Emu senses dejection afterward and is rebuffed, which is weaker than knowing its cause. The group hears Emu's smile-centered dream but not the undisclosed institutional condition. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_10:01:002:0012–0018` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_10:01:002:0032–0043` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_10:01:003:0003–0005` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:001:0034–0038`

## Speech, theme and claim review

Rui's measured technical language, playful challenges and sudden absorbed theorizing differ by activity; his claims should not be imported as narrator-certified science. Nene can laugh with Emu while teaching and soften into requests with Rui. Tsukasa's self-praise now accompanies sustained labor, so effort and ego cannot be separated into opposite character types. Audience response becomes both creative aim and imagined confirmation. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_10:01:002:0014–0016` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:001:0005–0009` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_11:01:001:0052–0055`

STRENGTHEN the group's actual pleasure and uneven access to vulnerability. DOWNGRADE a simple equation between Rui's solo claim and absence of desire for collaborators. PRESERVE as OPEN whether shared work can withstand a failure, whether Tsukasa's reassurance is deliberate, and whether performer/technical contingency arrangements suffice. No accident or rupture belongs in these phase claims.
