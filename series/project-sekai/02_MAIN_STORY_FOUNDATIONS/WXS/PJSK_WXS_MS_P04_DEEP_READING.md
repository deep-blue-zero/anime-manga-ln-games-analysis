# Wonderlands×Showtime main story — Phase 04

## Scope and causal claim

Episodes 12–14 keep the first failed performance, the group's collapse and Tsukasa's recovered purpose within one causal crisis. The technical interruption and his subsequent treatment of colleagues are separate causes. His private insight changes his next available action; it does not itself reconcile the people he hurt. [P03](PJSK_WXS_MS_P03_DEEP_READING.md) retains the preceding hopeful state.

## Performance failure and relational failure

The show begins with reported laughter and a promising solo. Nene privately links success to the possibility of returning to a stage she has feared. A lost microphone/control connection interrupts that possibility. Rui suspects exhausted charge after prolonged practice; recharging later restores operation, and he admits failing to check it. These records warrant a specific preparation failure, not a verdict that Nene's use of a robot caused moral or artistic illegitimacy. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_12:01:001:0002–0035` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_13:01:001:0005–0011`

Tsukasa first buys time within the fiction and invites audience support. Emu improvises a way for the dragon to be defeated without the intended song. The robot then falls onto him; Rui identifies an autonomous-control problem and ends the show through a makeshift narrative. Several audience members express disappointment. The sequence matters: improvisational capacity exists and still proves insufficient for this compounded disruption. Neither one mistake nor one person's disposition explains every part of the outcome. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_12:01:001:0036–0064`

Backstage, Tsukasa's insistence that today's audience cannot be replaced by a later one has an intelligible professional object. He converts it into a demand for uninterrupted success and attacks Nene's way of appearing. Her total self-blame and his accusation should be quoted or paraphrased as their states, not adopted as causal narration. Rui accepts part of the technical responsibility, stops the attack, and criticizes the loss of regard for collaborators. He also condemns Tsukasa's motive and future, exceeding what the narrative has established about his whole person. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_13:01:001:0007–0046`

Nene flees, Rui withdraws and leaves the robot behind, Tsukasa rejects Emu's attempt to preserve another chance, and Emu experiences the loss of the very smiles she sought. This relational damage follows the public failure through specific choices; it is not an inevitable effect of imperfect performance. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_13:01:001:0043–0058` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_13:01:002:0002–0010`

## Recovered purpose without completed repair

Tsukasa chooses the SEKAI as a place to seek help, recalling KAITO's earlier invitation. His response to a crying rabbit supplies the crucial experiment. Grand display fails to settle it; he borrows Emu's participatory greeting and enters shared make-believe. His attention shifts from showcasing himself to engaging this distressed recipient. The rabbit's marked foot and surrounding objects enable recognition of Saki's toy and his childhood creations. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_13:01:002:0013–0024` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_14:01:002:0002–0036`

Miku and KAITO interpret this small play as stardom because someone is smiling. Tsukasa remembers wanting to give Saki the joy he received from the childhood show and recognizes his later fixation on success and status. He then recalls each colleague's stated desire for smiles. The singers resist his new self-denigration; the alternative is to act on the recovered aim, not replace grandiosity with a global worthless-self verdict. He leaves intending to find the others. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_14:01:002:0037–0088` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_14:01:004:0002–0011`

## Knowledge, speech and structure

Tsukasa gains personal recognition of the rabbit and the motive. His recovered self-knowledge is known to the singers participating in that scene; Emu, Nene and Rui do not automatically receive it. Nene leaves before Rui's extended rebuke, so do not give her that speech merely because she was initially backstage. `PJSK:main:wonder_01_13:01:001:0023–0046` `PJSK:main:wonder_01_14:01:002:0031–0088`
