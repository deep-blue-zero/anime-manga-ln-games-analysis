# MORE MORE JUMP! P03 — Reciprocal legitimacy, accountable repair, and the next limit

## Scope and governing problem

Canonical reading of every canonical record in `mmj_01_09–12`, master episodes 10–13, following [P02](PJSK_MMJ_MS_P02_DEEP_READING.md). [Phase map](PJSK_MMJ_MAIN_STORY_PHASE_MAP.md) supplies the governing methods, source inventory, and locator grammar. `11/002:0034–0036` identifies exact records within `PJSK:main:mmj_01_11:01`. The phase follows Airi's reopened agency through a second crisis, public accountability, and a mutually chosen partnership with Shizuku. It closes when that repair's attempted extension to Haruka exposes a different barrier. It does not project the next phase's explanation backward.

## Causal structure

The rupture has two consequences for Airi: she cannot contact Shizuku, and her sense of failed idol legitimacy threatens even her right to teach Minori (`09/001:0002–0009`). Stage Rin's visit reenacts the ideal Airi has abandoned—an idol wants to encourage someone who is down—and prompts memory of her own norm about aspiring idols (`09/001:0011–0034`). Rin supplies a nudge, not the entire human resolution.

Returning to Minori, Airi asks whether she still wants the profession despite frustrations and obstruction by other people. Minori explicitly admits she cannot imagine either Airi's past or all future suffering; she nevertheless owns her chosen path (`09/002:0010–0022`). The answer does not claim superior knowledge of the industry. It helps Airi separate desire from external permission. Airi discloses the agencies' role-redirection and chooses to begin again as Minori's fellow aspirant, while admitting that agency and work arrangements remain unknown (`09/002:0025–0042`).

This first recommitment does not itself repair the friendship. Airi tells Minori what she said to Shizuku and identifies envy as her likely motive. She remembers mutual encouragement as trainees while acknowledging comparison with Shizuku's growing success (`10/001:0009–0019`). Minori proposes accompanying her to apologize. Before they can do so, a news alert reveals Shizuku has left the group and agency (`10/001:0021–0038`).

In the ensuing confrontation, Airi's attempt to praise Shizuku as naturally suited to idol work repeats the language that hurt her. Shizuku finally explains that colleagues and even Airi have treated her appearance and inherited qualities as reasons to attack her despite her effort for fans. She distinguishes Airi's latest injury from a long-standing decision process: she had considered quitting since group relationships worsened; initially the group's competition had been enjoyable, but selection became nominal and opportunities increasingly concentrated on her (`10/002:0012–0034`). Airi's claim that it is all her fault is consequently a self-blaming interpretation, not a complete causal summary.

Airi runs to the theater. Haruka warns Minori that behind the stage idols are people capable of envy; Minori prioritizes her loved senior over protection of her idealized view, and Haruka accompanies her (`11/001:0002–0013`). The theater encounter is a moral test because Airi recognizes her own envy in the members' accusations. She defends Shizuku's effort and hope-giving work, explicitly includes herself among those who resented her, and apologizes to Shizuku (`11/002:0014–0036`). The members dismiss responsibility and continue insulting Shizuku. Minori intervenes as Airi escalates, naming her as an idol and invoking the obligation to give hope; Airi stops and thanks her (`11/002:0038–0049`). The transcript supports threatened harmful escalation and successful restraint, not a detailed physical blow that it does not describe.

Shizuku receives the recognition she needed: someone has seen her labor and her intention. She calls Airi her idol, but confirms that she will not return to Cheerful＊Days and says goodbye with thanks and an apology for being an inadequate center (`11/002:0051–0062`). That courteous departure is not agreement with the members' account, nor reconciliation with them.

Afterward Airi apologizes again without demanding forgiveness. Shizuku asks her to return to idol work because Airi's words gave her hope and a dream. Airi answers that Shizuku is her own ideal, admits painful comparison, and asks Shizuku to return with her. Shizuku explicitly agrees and reveals she had long wanted to work together (`12/001:0005–0042`). Their reciprocal legitimacy is specific: each has already done something the other experiences as idol work, before an agency supplies authorization. Airi's condition also retains guilt—she says she cannot return alone after believing she drove Shizuku away—so the resolution should not erase that burden.

Their plan is modestly concrete: practice together on the rooftop; later decide agency and activity arrangements. Haruka refuses their request for teaching, is triggered by the wording of the request, and exclaims that she has no qualification to be an idol. She retracts it as a verbal mistake and leaves. The three do not accept that explanation at face value, but they still do not know the cause (`12/001:0048–0077`). An intervention successful for one relationship has reached a new limit.

## Relationship transitions

`MMJ-REL-AISZ-02 -> MMJ-REL-AISZ-03`: rupture becomes accountable repair, reciprocal recognition, physical affection initiated by Shizuku, and an agreed joint aspiration (`12/001:0034–0042`). Airi's embarrassment at the hug is not rejection of the partnership. Their different valuations remain: Airi admires Shizuku's stage presence and labor; Shizuku understands Airi as the person who taught and gave her hope. They need not possess identical professional strengths to be each other's ideal.

`MMJ-REL-MNAI-01 -> MMJ-REL-MNAI-02`: formal mentor/novice inequality now includes Airi's disclosure, Minori's encouragement, a fellow-aspirant promise, and an instance of Minori regulating Airi's action. This is a three-person practice configuration with concern for a fourth, not the final named unit. Stage Rin's contribution to Airi becomes demonstrated local encouragement; no other SEKAI manifestation acquires it.

## Epistemic changes and exclusions

Minori learns Airi's professional history from Airi and the specific hurtful statement before the theater visit (`09/002:0029–0038`; `10/001:0009–0019`). Airi, Minori, and Haruka hear Shizuku's account in the courtyard. All four witness the theater conduct and apology. These are distinct access routes from the reader's earlier private access.

Shizuku knows her departure was considered before the latest rupture; Airi is still speaking as if personally responsible for the whole loss. The reading must preserve both. The other three hear Haruka's disqualification claim and find the “mistake” explanation implausible (`12/001:0065–0077`). They do not know who asked for teaching in the memory, what happened, or whether a medical/physical barrier exists. Airi's and Shizuku's own new understanding of mutual admiration should likewise not be projected into their earlier quarrel.

`MMJ-EP-P03` should record the separate knowledge of: Airi's professional cause, Shizuku's cumulative group harm and departure, Airi's accountable envy, chosen partnership, and Haruka's unexplained disclosure. It must not import the next phase's name or injury into any phase-03 row.

## Behavior and Japanese register

Airi's assertion `わたしがアイドルになりたいからなる` replaces the agencies' evaluation as a decision premise (`09/002:0035`), while `まだどうすればいいのかはわからない` preserves practical uncertainty (`09/002:0037`). Minori's response is an explicit limit of knowledge followed by commitment, not a claim to understand what she has never experienced (`09/002:0020–0022`).

At the theater, Airi's pronouns move responsibility into the collective `わたし達` as she contrasts their resentment with Shizuku's effort (`11/002:0026–0032`). Her apology changes from interrupted fragments in P02 to specific, sustained admission. Minori's `ダメです` is forceful despite continued `桃井先輩` address (`11/002:0043–0045`): hierarchy does not prevent moral intervention. Shizuku's `私のアイドル` recasts recipient experience as a legitimate judgment (`11/002:0056`; `12/001:0012–0019`). Airi's `ズルい` in the subsequent exchange expresses moved resistance in an affiliative repair context, distinct from the accusatory comparison of P02.

Low-stakes and practical continuity includes unsolicited praise of improved form, locating Shizuku through Minori's social information, communication failures through unread messages, and choosing a familiar rehearsal location (`10/001:0002–0007`; `12/001:0048–0052`). These are usable baseline evidence, not exhaustive ordinary-life coverage.
