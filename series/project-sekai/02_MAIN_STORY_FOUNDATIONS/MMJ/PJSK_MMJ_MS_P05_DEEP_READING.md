## Causal consolidation

The changed `Untitled` is first shared evidence of something occurring on all four phones. Stage Miku explains it as a song arising from the feelings they have found. Haruka articulates the common wish: to become or remain idols who give hope. Airi and Shizuku identify Minori as the person who kept that wish when they were losing it; Minori still initially discounts her own contribution because she has received so much assistance (`19/001:0002–0018`). This is not an unearned label for a novice: the preceding phases have demonstrated what the three received from her, while preserving their indispensable labor in helping her deliver it.

Miku asks to sing with them; Rin takes the MC role. After the source's music-video marker, the characters report enjoyment and recognize the song as `アイドル新鋭隊` (`19/001:0019–0039`). These records establish a shared song and reported successful experience. They do not supply the unseen video's choreography, arrangement, staging, vocal qualities, or direct observational proof of Haruka's unaided physical capacity. Miku and Rin welcome future visits and performances; the humans explicitly thank them (`19/001:0041–0053`).

Haruka then tells Mai that she plans to become an idol again. Agency choices are still unformed, but she has something she wants to attempt (`19/002:0002–0009`). This is a real shift from the earlier school-only account and gives Mai new information. It does not establish that Haruka disclosed the entire causal truth previously withheld, that Mai's own injury resolved, or that either returned to ASRUN.

The next rooftop scene deliberately retains the world's resistance: Minori fails the audition for which the seniors trained her. Airi criticizes the judges and Shizuku offers udon as comfort. Minori is upset partly because others invested effort. The seniors remind her that she has already encouraged them as an idol; she reframes fifty failures becoming fifty-one and renews her effort (`20/001:0002–0018`). The number of failures changes; the competence gap does not vanish, and neither does the emotional sting. Crucially, Airi does not enforce the original “next failure ends teaching” rule. Instead she proposes more practice (`20/001:0021–0022`), a demonstrable consequence of their changed relationship.

Haruka proposes creating their own four-person group from zero, grounded in the common dream and the specific people who gave her hope (`20/001:0027–0043`). The seniors do not merely accept celebrity authority. Shizuku agrees while checking Airi's assent; Airi tests whether Haruka really wants this instead of an easy agency route, then accepts while retaining rigorous teaching expectations. Airi's assertion that any agency would take Haruka is a character judgment, not verified job offers.

Minori initially places herself outside the group as prospective fan number one. Haruka explicitly includes her and names her hope-giving contribution. Minori still invokes lack of audition credentials and weaker singing/dancing as reasons she may not belong. Shizuku answers that they can cover one another's deficits, and Haruka affirms that she wants continuing shared stage work. Minori accepts through trust in their judgment and a commitment to improve (`20/001:0044–0064`). Membership is not conferred by pretending the four have equal skills; mutual need and demonstrated contribution create a legitimate place for learning.

The name is made interactively. Airi initiates; Minori proposes a too-literal hopeful name; Shizuku tries English and mistakenly keeps `FIGHT`; Airi corrects her; Haruka selects Minori's recurring “more and more” as expressing persistence; Minori supplies `MORE MORE JUMP!`; the others connect jumping with reaching for their shared ideal and crossing the barriers that stopped them (`20/001:0065–0083`). Naming captures both persistence and the change from isolated striving to four-person action. They begin practice and Shizuku invites Minori to drop senior titles in favor of member familiarity (`20/001:0084–0091`). The Stage singers' final ribbon adjustment supplies a small practical instance of their own continuing care before performance (`20/002:0002–0007`).

## Final relationship topology

The named unit is `MMJ-REL-GROUP-05`. Shared aspiration, practical mutual support, and explicit acceptance bind the four, but their expertise, confidence, and modes of dependence differ.

- `MMJ-REL-MNHR-04`: admiration and gratitude now coexist with explicit fellow membership. Haruka takes initiative to include Minori and treats her contribution as causally necessary. Minori continues to aspire toward her skill and image.
- `MMJ-REL-MNAI-03`: rigorous instruction within membership. Airi's teaching no longer expires at the next rejection, and Minori has already been allowed to encourage and restrain her.
- `MMJ-REL-MNSZ-03`: supportive instruction, practical comfort, and explicit mutual accommodation; Shizuku initiates first-name/member address.
- `MMJ-REL-AISZ-04`: repaired desired partnership expands into four-person work. The earlier mutual ideal remains; Shizuku actively solicits Airi's assent rather than abandoning the pair's promise.
- `MMJ-REL-HRAI-04`: professional respect and earlier conflict become chosen collaboration; Airi preserves exacting standards even for Haruka, and Haruka thanks her as senior.
- `MMJ-REL-HRSZ-04`: professional acquaintance and accepted care become an affirmed working relationship oriented toward mutual help.
- `MMJ-REL-HRMAI-01` is preserved with the bounded update that Mai now hears Haruka's intention to return and expresses relief. No wider reconciliation knowledge is invented.

Stage `MMJ-VS-STAGE-02` / `MMJ-REL-HUMAN-STAGE-02` can be recorded as the local Miku/Rin support and performance group after the supported return. Miku's singing and Rin's MC role are distinct; the closing ribbon help is Miku-to-Rin. No other singer or manifestation inherits these acts or knowledge.

## Epistemic exit

All four know the common wish, the changed song title, their shared performance, Minori's latest failure, the proposal they accepted, the group name, and the invitation to drop Minori's senior titles. Haruka hears the audition outcome when she arrives (`20/001:0019–0025`); she was not present for the preceding udon offer and exchange unless separately told. Mai knows Haruka intends to return but is not present for the final proposal/naming, and the text does not show her being told the group's exact membership or name. Stage Miku/Rin know the shared feeling/song experience; the rooftop naming scene is not automatically their information. The school or public at large is not shown to know any new unit plan.

Minori's final inclusion is itself an epistemic correction: she initially interprets the proposal as a trio and learns she is intended as the fourth. It also changes self-placement, but her nervous doubts show that factual inclusion and stable confidence should not be conflated.

## Behavior, Japanese register, and ordinary-life evidence

The ending retains differentiated rhythms. Airi reacts to rejection with direct indignation and practical next steps; Shizuku offers food before abstract reassurance; Minori laughs awkwardly, worries about wasted help, then intensifies effort; Haruka listens and initiates a considered proposal (`20/001:0005–0034`). Udon is an offered comfort outing and a restaurant Shizuku calls delicious, not an eaten meal or a comprehensive food preference profile.

The naming exchange is low-stakes evidence of Minori's spontaneous slogan-making, Shizuku's fallible English, Airi's quick correction, and Haruka's attentive use of Minori's wording. It should not be generalized into global incompetence or perfect managerial roles. `桃井先輩` / `日野森先輩` become `愛莉ちゃん` / `雫ちゃん` only after explicit invitation (`20/001:0086–0088`). Minori's continued polite responses immediately afterward show that permission does not instantaneously erase every formality. Haruka still uses `先輩` with Airi (`20/001:0043`); do not universalize Minori's negotiated change.
