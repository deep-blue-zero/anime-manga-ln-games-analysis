## Scope and boundary

Canonical reading of every record in `mmj_01_05–08`, master episodes 6–9. [P01](PJSK_MMJ_MS_P01_DEEP_READING.md) is the entering analytical state; [phase map](PJSK_MMJ_MAIN_STORY_PHASE_MAP.md) supplies source lock and exact-locator expansion (`05/002:0008` = `PJSK:main:mmj_01_05:01:002:0008`). ## Causal structure

Shizuku's successful professional surface is first juxtaposed with the colleagues' private evaluation of it. After praise at a shoot and a packed work schedule, she hears members attribute her cover work to favoritism and beauty, while claiming superior dance and singing. Her private `また` marks recurrence; she enters, retrieves her towel, and still invites collective effort for the next live (`05/001:0004–0007`; `05/002:0004–0013`). That is evidence of an effort to maintain working cooperation under hurt, not proof that there is no hurt.

At Minori's lesson, anxiety over appearance prompts Shizuku to say the idol's heart and effort for fans are central (`05/004:0011–0019`). This advice now has an immediate professional counter-context. Minori sees reassurance; the reader can connect it with an experience she has not witnessed. Haruka's checking of Minori's twisted ankle and warning against overdoing practice supply direct care while she remains outside the teaching arrangement (`05/005:0005–0014`).

The shared SEKAI visit opens a space in which idol enjoyment reappears without resolving the injuries attached to it. Stage Miku identifies the SEKAI as made from the four people's feelings toward idols and describes the singers as helpers; the humans question how virtual singers can converse and be touched (`06/001:0011–0024`). During the performance, Shizuku reads Miku/Rin's coordination as trust, Airi wishes she had performed on such a stage, and Haruka describes blue audience lights as a sea seen from the stage (`06/001:0045–0060`). These are differentiated responses to the same encounter. Minori's excitement does not prove the experience restores everyone.

Haruka asks to leave; Miku gives the exit procedure despite Rin's protest (`07/001:0002–0013`). Haruka calls the live enjoyable but leaves nonetheless. Minori recognizes sadness without knowing its cause. The reader receives only a fragment of Haruka's remembered accusation and her judgment that she has no qualification to be an idol (`07/002:0006–0010`). Neither the accuser nor the intervening causal history can yet be supplied.

The Airi–Shizuku exchange then explains Airi's exit in a different register. Airi initially valued variety work and deliberately studied talk and comedy; being accepted and making viewers happy was rewarding. She accepted missing an idol live because she believed publicity would help the group and its audience (`07/004:0019–0040`). The injury is therefore not simply hatred of variety work. It develops when repeated substitution becomes a plan to sell her as a variety personality while deeming her ordinary as an idol, and when changing agencies does not secure her desired work (`08/001:0005–0010`; `08/002:0002–0005`).

Shizuku's affirmation cannot reach Airi while it appears to come from someone naturally granted the recognition she lacks. Airi attacks precisely those inborn qualities, catches herself, and begins an apology as Shizuku leaves (`08/002:0006–0019`). The source then reveals why this is a specific relational wound. Shizuku remembers anxious entry into idol work after friends submitted an application, and Airi's initial rival declaration becoming reassurance, practical help, and a promise to strive together. Airi had taught her that heart mattered more than appearance or talent (`08/003:0007–0028`). Airi's new words align her with the colleagues' dismissal, destabilizing the person whose ideal helped Shizuku persist. The injury is not adequately described as generic jealousy or a misunderstanding that a single clarification has already fixed.

The failed lesson at the end shows consequences without pretending Minori witnessed the rupture. Neither senior arrives or reads her messages. Haruka announces a different reading location, offers continued contact and occasional walks home, and privately rejects Minori's idealized account of idol work (`08/004:0002–0028`). Leaving rehearsal proximity is not total rejection of Minori.

## Behavior and register

The most useful ordinary evidence is corrective, practical, and context-sensitive: Airi checks photographs against the real applicant, asks for review of missed steps, and grows concerned at injury; Haruka checks swelling and recommends a compress; Shizuku keeps track of application photos while carrying a crowded work schedule (`05/004:0006–0010`; `05/005:0004–0011`). Airi's cheek-pinching as a reality test and Minori's protest supply a brief comic interaction (`06/001:0027–0031`); they do not establish a general license for aggression.

Airi's `やめて` escalates from resisting affirmation into `やめてよっ`; comparative descriptors turn personal recognition into an accusation against Shizuku (`08/002:0008–0014`). The truncated apology is an observable immediate reversal. Shizuku's private `愛莉ちゃんまで` makes the particular speaker decisive (`08/003:0003`). Haruka's courteous thanks and `さよなら` coexist with private self-denial (`07/001:0008–0013`; `07/002:0010`). Minori's written request remains polite and her spoken thanks preserve affiliation even when the arrangement recedes.
