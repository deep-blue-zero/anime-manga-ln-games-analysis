The phase sizes are intentionally unequal (4 / 5 / 3 / 9). Episode 03 ends with the shift from ordinary school avoidance to shared-space entry. Episode 08 completes the first practice initiative while demonstrating that improved music and a sympathetic invitation have not solved Honami's refusal; its retrospective records clarify the questions taken into the next phase. Episode 11 supplies a durable relationship transition, not merely a passed musical assessment. Episodes 12–20 form one continuous Honami crisis and its consequences. Splitting after Saki's farewell, Shiho's confrontation, the rooftop invitation or the arrival of the other two would separate attempted solutions from the evidence that qualifies them. Episode 20 belongs to that same causal block because the classroom conversation tests the central false choice in a social setting outside SEKAI.

It does not certify a reconstruction model or interpret a later event. The established baseline now permits ordered interpretation of valid historical Leo/need routes from the franchise routing ledger; unresolved earlier universal-screen gaps must be closed before dependent routes advance. No event impact is assigned here, and no shared N25 or other-unit historical state is overwritten by this parallel foundation.
