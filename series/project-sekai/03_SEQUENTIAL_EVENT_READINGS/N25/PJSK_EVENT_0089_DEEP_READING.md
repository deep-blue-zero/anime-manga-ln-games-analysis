Kanade can recognize that shared work already helps without declaring Mafuyu saved. The mother sees fatigue, recalls the earlier music wish, searches the computer and finds hidden lyrics, illustrations and Nightcord. A new demo message identifies K as composer and enables direct contact. This is now **shown discovery**, replacing the earlier analytical uncertainty; it is not evidence that she learns the SEKAI, every member's identity or every conversation (`PJSK:event:0089:01:002:0005–0040`; `PJSK:event:0089:01:004:0003–0016`; `PJSK:event:0089:01:005:0002–0027`).

She therefore takes the mother's concern seriously, but refuses to decide against Mafuyu's stated wish. ## Character handoff: change and continuity are uneven

### Kanade — `K-E0063-01` → `K-E0089-01`

The successor is **recipient-directed protection after failed parental trust, with reciprocal warmth and persistent rescue obligation**. Its durable trigger is not simply anger at a difficult adult. Kanade begins by assuming a mother can be reached through the child's distress and wishes, encounters contrary evidence, and explicitly changes what authority she will recognize. She can address an adult politely, hear the opposing rationale, qualify her interpretation and still refuse cooperation. Her continued presence is now consciously maintained against that adult's demand, rather than dependent on successful persuasion. The post-meeting card repeats the commitment, supporting a state handoff rather than a single climax utterance (`PJSK:event:0089:08:002:0024–0057`; `PJSK:card:0612:02:001:0020–0044`; `PJSK:card:0612:02:001:0053–0063`).

This change **inherits** K-E0063-01's bounded self-permission and the EVENT_0084 refinement of supported ordinary competence. It does not replace her with a purely instrumental protector. Before the meeting, solitary composing fails to settle her; shared work with Ena and Mizuki makes an awkward transition fall into place. She accepts their practical availability and thanks them. Afterward, she is shaken enough to let water overflow while thinking, then chooses Honami's herbal tea and experiences bodily and emotional warmth. Received care becomes a model for what she hopes to offer Mafuyu (`PJSK:card:0612:01:001:0024–0082`; `PJSK:card:0612:02:001:0038–0063`).

Rescue compulsion remains. “I want to remain” becomes “I must,” explicitly to save Mafuyu; work displaces intended sleep, and the meeting's effects persist after she comes home. She has not corrected all guilt about her father, acquired robust body regulation, rejected all parents or learned that her own judgment is infallible. The marionette recollection is **Kanade's newly formed causal interpretation**, not proof that she possessed this account during EVENT_0002. Her belief that the mother will never change is a participant prognosis, not future authorial certification. ## Speech, ordinary behavior and structural interpretation

Kanade moves from formal accommodation to a clear refusal while retaining qualified first-person judgment. That is a contextual ability to disagree with authority, not a new uniformly forceful voice. Mafuyu's polished school coaching and publicly agreeable dinner responses contrast with short private answers, strained negation and specific self-directed musical action. Ena is sharp toward pressure, candid about worry, technically attentive about art and openly grateful for Mizuki's help. Mizuki uses jokes, imitation, fashion specificity and practical proposals to sustain contact; not every cheerful remark conceals distress. KAITO's clipped imperatives and hostile challenges are distinct from his privately revised strategy. Textual register supports these distinctions; no vocal-performance claim is made.

Several ordinary details prevent the crisis from consuming characterization: Kanade's approximately five-minute timing skill is **Mizuki's report** during introductions; Ena objects to the “overedited selfies” tease; the blouse choice is first motivated by the meeting, then becomes a playful future request she conditionally accepts. Miku reports finishing an Ena-given picture book and finding it interesting. Its identity is not shown to match the book Len borrowed in EVENT_0087. Kanade's latest father visit includes relief at talking despite nonrecognition; remembered parental warmth is not evidence of present recovery (`PJSK:card:0615:02:001:0017–0025`; `PJSK:area:areatalk_ev_night_12_001:01`; `PJSK:event:0089:02:004:0002–0005`; `PJSK:event:0089:01:006:0002–0008`). Shoe advertisements remain claims discussed by characters; this reading endorses neither their promised effect nor a general account of health.

**First-person authority and usable choice** (`TH-N25-001`, `TH-N25-034`) are tested by several helpers, not just the mother. Kanade must learn that benevolent wording can bypass the recipient's meaning. Ena's direct exhortation cannot by itself change the conditions for refusal. KAITO treats resistance as indispensable, while Rin and Len insist that refuge still matters. Kanade's own protective secrecy also limits Mafuyu's access to a conversation about her life. None of these tensions is settled by ranking one character as the perfect speaker of truth.

**Warmth/coldness** (`MO-N25-001`) crosses the meeting, childhood care, received tea, restaurant companionship and music. The welcoming cafe is not sufficient for felt safety. Kanade's aftereffects let her reinterpret the earlier marionette episode, but the remembered puppet is not new direct testimony from Mafuyu. **Music as psychological evidence and partial support** (`TH-N25-003`, `TH-N25-025`) has concrete mechanisms: shared work steadies Kanade; sustaining the return-place steadies Ena; Mafuyu's listening becomes lyric writing. N25's value cannot be reduced to a solitary pastime that can be replaced without losing the relationships.

**Empty SEKAI as refuge** (`MO-N25-007`, the established non-demanding-refuge extension) requires refinement: the space can contain conflicting support practices, and Mafuyu can seek it while avoiding a particular occupant. KAITO's own reconsideration prevents freezing the new mode as infallible harshness. No new recurring motif ID is needed, and these N25 findings do not certify a franchise-wide comparative theory before the other foundations.
