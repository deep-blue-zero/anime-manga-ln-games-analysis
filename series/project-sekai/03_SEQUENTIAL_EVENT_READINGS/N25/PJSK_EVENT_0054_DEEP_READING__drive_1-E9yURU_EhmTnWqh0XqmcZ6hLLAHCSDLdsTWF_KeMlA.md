# Project SEKAI — EVENT_0054 Deep Reading: セカイの桜、つながる想い


## 1. Purpose, authority, and longitudinal role


EVENT_0054 is the first release in the prospective universal-routing sequence whose central mechanism is explicitly franchise-wide rather than merely mixed-unit. Its 42-surface envelope uses the five established SEKAI contexts to stage parallel desires: humans want to share spring/cherry-blossom experience with their Virtual Singer companions, and Virtual Singers want to answer, enrich, or give something back to the humans whose feelings sustain their worlds. Those wishes converge through `想いの欠片` into a temporary connective field containing a tree on which multiple units' thoughts appear together.


For N25, the release is not primarily a new human-development event. The four-human tuple remains:


`MF-E0042-01 / K-E0026-01 / E-E0053-01 / MZ-E0039-01`.


Its durable N25 change is instead relational and manifestation-specific. N25 Hatsune Miku, already established as a waiting presence, self-search companion, ordinary-care recipient, and participant in the Empty SEKAI's social life, now explicitly recognizes **her own happiness in the presence of Mafuyu, Kanade, Ena, Mizuki, and the other N25 Virtual Singers**. She turns that affect into a future-oriented wish to keep doing things for them. The humans then answer her sakura desire with a deliberately Virtual-Singer-centered hanami inside the Empty SEKAI.


The resulting successor relationship state is:


> **`REL-N25-GM-E0054 — reciprocal chosen companionship / mutual care and shared-world enrichment`**


This extends `REL-N25-GM-E0009`. The change is not that Miku suddenly begins caring about the group. She has long cared. The change is that the relationship is now explicitly bidirectional at the level of first-person desire and world-making: Miku identifies that being with them makes **her** happy and wants to act for them; the humans recognize what Miku and the other manifestations cannot experience directly and modify the Empty SEKAI's ordinary environment so that the Virtual Singers can share a valued seasonal experience.

## 4. Significance triage


### 4.1 Tier B — Relationship / SEKAI ontology


EVENT_0054 is primarily about relationships being materially written into and across SEKAI spaces. It does not advance a human protagonist by replacing a current global state. It advances what a manifestation can want for itself and for its humans, how humans reciprocate that care, and what the franchise permits separate SEKAI manifestations to perceive about one another.


### 4.2 N25 impact: I3


The release crosses I3 because it creates a durable relationship successor state and adds a franchise-relevant epistemic/ontology rule:


- `REL-N25-GM-E0009 -> REL-N25-GM-E0054`;
- N25 Miku explicitly recognizes her own positive relational affect rather than only describing Mafuyu's or the group's state;
- Miku forms a future-oriented prosocial desire grounded in that affect;
- N25 humans collectively build a seasonal social environment for Miku/VS rather than bringing them into a human-centered ritual as incidental participants;
- the event establishes a transient cross-SEKAI connective phenomenon in which distinct manifestations can briefly perceive one another without acquiring the other units' human biographies;
- the Empty SEKAI remains Mafuyu-originated while continuing to become more socially furnished and relationally inhabited.


No new global human state is warranted.


## 5. N25 causal reading


### 5.1 Ordinary boredom proves the Empty SEKAI has become lived-in space


The event begins with Miku, Rin, MEIKO, and Luka doing coloring, reading Mizuki's magazines, discussing cat's cradle, and complaining about boredom. Analytically this is valuable precisely because nothing is wrong. A space that originally functioned as Mafuyu's stark psychological manifestation has accumulated enough ordinary life for its residents to become bored with familiar leisure.


When the four humans arrive, Miku privately notices how much livelier the space becomes. The social population is no longer merely crisis infrastructure.


### 5.2 Mafuyu's `あたたかくて、いいと思った` is continuity evidence, not a new Mafuyu state


Kanade brings a new demo. Ena and Mizuki offer positive reactions; Mafuyu says it feels warm and that she thinks it is good. Kanade visibly values the judgment. This is consistent with `MF-E0042-01`: positive relational/aesthetic affect remains accessible enough to become first-person evaluative evidence.


The scene is important continuity evidence but does not exceed the established Mafuyu state. Do not create `MF-E0054-*` from it.


### 5.3 Sakura converts absence into a relational question


Miku has seen petals but never a full tree. Mizuki can promise to show a human-world tree through a device, yet Luka identifies the material limit: the Virtual Singers cannot catch those petals themselves. The difference between **seeing an external object** and **sharing an experience inside the same world** becomes the event's governing problem.


This is echoed in the other SEKAI. The desire is repeatedly not just “I want information about sakura” but “I want to experience this with you, here.”


### 5.4 Miku's search becomes first-person self-discovery


Miku independently searches the Empty SEKAI. When she fails to find a tree, she notices that the space is still objectively sparse, but compares it with its earlier condition: once only Mafuyu and Miku existed there; then Kanade, Ena, Mizuki, Rin, MEIKO, and Luka entered its relational history.


She concludes that Mafuyu seems to be changing little by little. The key movement then turns inward:


- her chest feels warm;
- she tentatively asks whether she is happy;
- she decides that simply being able to spend time with everyone makes her happy;
- she says searching was worthwhile because it taught her this;
- she wishes that she can continue doing many things for everyone.


The source therefore gives N25 Miku something stronger than mirror-function characterization. She has first-person affect, learns from it, and converts it into future relational intention.


### 5.5 `想いの欠片` supplies the franchise ontology bridge


Miku identifies a fragment as a thought that has not become a SEKAI. She explicitly distinguishes:


`someone's thought -> may create a SEKAI`


from


`all thoughts -> SEKAI`.


Some thoughts instead drift into existing spaces as fragments, and touching them can reveal part of the thought. EVENT_0054 then introduces an exceptional case: related wishes from separate SEKAI contexts converge around the same temporary field/tree.


The event does not establish routine travel among SEKAI or a stable cross-unit communications network. It establishes **permeability under exceptional convergent conditions**.


### 5.6 Shared care creates temporary adjacency


The parallel unit movements have different concrete objects but the same relational grammar:


`human seasonal experience -> desire to share with SEKAI companion`


and/or


`manifestation receives human care/inspiration -> desire to return care`.


At the tree, different manifestations see multiple sets of thoughts growing together. Several independently feel that another presence is beside them. A gust briefly lets the manifestations perceive different-SEKAI versions of Miku/Rin/Len/MEIKO/Luka/KAITO.


The mysterious final voice offers, but does not prove as mechanical law, the causal interpretation that the shared desire to help “those kids” connected them. Preserve the modality: the source strongly suggests convergent care as the connector but does not deliver a complete cosmological specification.


### 5.7 The petal is a bounded residue, not proof of free inter-SEKAI transport


When N25 Miku returns, a sakura petal remains on her head. She tells Rin she caught one. This provides material continuity between the temporary field and Empty SEKAI.


The correct ontology claim is narrow: the connection can leave at least a small physical residue in this event. It does not justify assuming arbitrary object transfer, repeatable travel, or controllable access.


## 6. Associated-card integration — N25 Miku `0404`


Card `0404` turns the core's abstract desire into reciprocal ordinary care.


Miku asks the other N25 manifestations what a full sakura tree is like because she wants to search. Luka deliberately frightens Rin with old folklore, while Miku ultimately preserves the desire: even if sakura is not only beautiful, she wants to seek what Mafuyu once found beautiful.


The aftermath is more consequential. The humans organize hanami in the Empty SEKAI. Kanade and Mafuyu visit multiple flower shops. At Luka's request Mafuyu personally chooses the branch that seems best so Miku can encounter, as closely as possible, “the sakura Mafuyu thought was beautiful.” Mafuyu carefully preserves epistemic precision: the new branch does not trigger as clear a `綺麗` response as the earlier one, but it is the best she can presently select.


Ena and Mizuki bring sakura-patterned environmental substitutes; the group organizes food and drinks; Miku helps distribute plates rather than remaining a passive recipient. She explicitly calls herself very happy and later judges hanami itself as warm even while noticing that falling flowers are a little sad.


This is the strongest evidence for the relationship transition because care circulates rather than moving in one direction.


## 7. Relationship transitions


### 7.1 `REL-N25-GM-E0009 -> REL-N25-GM-E0054`


**New state:** `reciprocal chosen companionship / mutual care and shared-world enrichment`.


Preserve from EVENT_0009:


- Miku as celebration participant;
- Miku as stable waiting presence;
- humans providing ordinary comfort/seasonal objects;
- Empty SEKAI as non-crisis social venue.


Strengthen/change at EVENT_0054:


- Miku identifies **her own** happiness in the group's presence;
- Miku forms a future-oriented desire to do things for the humans;
- Miku actively monitors whether they have been doing better and wishes for reduced pain without claiming capacity to guarantee it;
- the humans deliberately construct a seasonal experience for Miku and the other manifestations;
- Mafuyu's own limited first-person aesthetic judgment becomes part of the gift;
- Miku participates in hosting/logistics after receiving the gift;
- the relationship now modifies how the Empty SEKAI is inhabited, not merely how often characters meet there.


**Guardrail:** Miku remains manifestation-specific and non-omniscient. Caring strongly does not mean knowing the humans' complete internal states or having power to eliminate their suffering.


### 7.2 Mafuyu–Miku — strengthen `REL-N25-VS-MIKU-MAFUYU-E0042`


No successor ID is needed. Mafuyu responds to a Miku-specific desire by going with Kanade to multiple flower shops and choosing the best available branch from her own first-person perception. Miku values the branch specifically because it carries Mafuyu's perception rather than because it is an objectively perfect substitute.


This strengthens promised-world/self-search companionship into reciprocal sensory-world sharing.


### 7.3 N25 group


Preserve the current human-group relationship state. EVENT_0054 strengthens ordinary companionship and reciprocal care but does not alter the human group's governing disclosure, conflict, or support grammar enough to warrant another group-state ID.


## 8. Epistemic transitions


### N25 Miku


Miku now directly knows:


- she can experience her own chest warmth/happiness because the others are present;
- this relational happiness can motivate a future desire to act for them;
- thoughts that do not become SEKAI can exist as fragments and may drift into existing spaces;
- an exceptional fragment can lead to a space carrying multiple distinct thought signatures;
- she briefly perceived people resembling other manifestations and tentatively infers that they may have been present observing the same thoughts.


She does **not** know the full identities or human histories associated with those other SEKAI.


### N25 humans


The humans know Miku/VS wanted to experience sakura enough for them to organize the hanami. The envelope does not establish that N25 humans learn the complete cross-SEKAI connective event. Do not grant them that knowledge by audience inference.


### Cross-SEKAI manifestations


Selected manifestations acquire bounded direct/strongly inferred evidence that other SEKAI and other versions of themselves exist and may under rare conditions become perceptually adjacent. The event repeatedly preserves uncertainty (`見間違い`, `もしかしたら`, questions about what the place was). Model this as extraordinary but incomplete knowledge, not a settled travel protocol.

**Transition:** **OPEN -> canonical bounded ontology claim**.

### `CR-PJSK-SEKAI-041` — convergent care is a supported connector hypothesis, not a complete law


**Transition:** **OPEN -> supported provisional claim**.


The mysterious speaker links the encounter to the manifestations' common desire to help their humans. Parallel event construction strongly supports this reading. Preserve uncertainty about exact mechanism.


## 10. Theme and motif integration


### `TH-N25-015` — seasonal ritual / continuity as presence


**STRENGTHEN / broaden.** New Year had shown seasonal continuity through repeated presence. EVENT_0054 shows seasonal experience becoming deliberately co-created inside the Empty SEKAI for residents who cannot directly participate in the human world's physical season.


### `MO-N25-007` — Empty SEKAI as furnished/social/withdrawal space


**STRENGTHEN.** The space now supports ordinary boredom, magazines, coloring, cat's cradle, picnics, flowers, food, environmental decoration, and Miku's own relational happiness. Sparse visual ontology and rich social history coexist.


### New motif: sakura as shared-but-imperfectly-transferable experience


The sakura motif distinguishes seeing from sharing, external beauty from co-present experience, and literal tree absence from relationally constructed substitutes. The branch, patterned sheet, umbrella, picnic, and retained petal show that a valued experience can be translated rather than perfectly duplicated.


### Franchise ontology theme: thoughts can connect without collapsing distinct worlds


EVENT_0054 creates adjacency without fusion. Each SEKAI retains its identity; its manifestations return home; the humans' biographies remain partitioned. Connection is real without total transparency or permanent merger.

Do not reconstruct manifestations as possessing a routine multiverse directory. EVENT_0054 supports a rare shared encounter, not stable inter-SEKAI communication.
