# Project SEKAI — EVENT_0042 Deep Reading: 交わる旋律 灯るぬくもり

## 1. Purpose, authority, and longitudinal role

EVENT_0042 is the next major Mafuyu developmental release after `EVENT_0035 — 灯のミラージュ`. EVENT_0035 established that Mafuyu could notice a positive bodily response, connect it to autobiographical care memory, and deliberately preserve the trace in her own words without first possessing a complete label for the feeling. EVENT_0042 does not repeat that accomplishment. It changes **where positive self-evidence can come from and what Mafuyu can do with it**.

The governing question is:

> **What is different after EVENT_0042 than before it?**

Before this release, `MF-E0035-01` allowed Mafuyu to treat warmth as personally meaningful affect and to preserve it through self-authored lyrics. The strongest positive-affect pathway still ran through Mafuyu's own recovered memory and Kanade's Mafuyu-directed music/care.

EVENT_0042 adds a second, relational pathway. Mafuyu feels warmth while watching Kanade become happy, later recognizes that a very small action of her own helped produce Kanade's happiness, deliberately investigates why that matters, follows the resulting question across ordinary social contact and Virtual Singer interaction, and finally judges the unexplained warmth as `悪い感覚ではない`. The associated cards prove that the event is not a transient emotional beat: Mafuyu voluntarily reports the feeling to N25 Miku, begins asking what Miku herself means to her, uses the chronological N25 song archive as a self-search instrument, makes and keeps a promise to show Miku snow, and participates in an ongoing sensory-retrieval exercise.

The resulting successor state is:

> **`MF-E0042-01 — relational warmth / contribution-linked self-evidence`**

The state does **not** mean Mafuyu has recovered broad preference access, can name friendship/love reliably, has become generally prosocial as a stable identity, or has solved the mother/autonomy conflict. It means she now has durable first-person evidence that another person's happiness—and her own role in making that happiness possible—can generate positive affect worth investigating, preserving, and sometimes acting on.

EVENT_0042 also produces a major but non-successor Kanade refinement. Kanade experiences ordinary social/music conversation outside N25 as simply enjoyable, retrieves a forgotten childhood memory of having fun with friends, recognizes that the rescue/penance years may have caused parts of ordinary life to become inaccessible, and treats noticing that loss as potentially good. This materially broadens `K-E0026-01` without displacing its persistent guilt, rescue mission, responsibility inflation, or poor self-care.

## 2. Significance triage

### 2.1 Tier A — Developmental

EVENT_0042 changes Mafuyu's future response distribution in five durable ways:

1. **Positive affect becomes relationally generative.** Mafuyu can feel warmth because someone important to her is happy, rather than only because music or autobiographical memory directly reaches her.
2. **Contribution becomes self-evidence.** Mafuyu notices that helping Kanade even indirectly can make her own chest feel warm. Her action need not be dramatic or identity-defining to become psychologically informative.
3. **Inquiry becomes more self-initiated and relationally targeted.** Mafuyu chooses to attend the Ichika/Kanade meeting because she wants to understand the warmth; later she independently asks what Miku means to her.
4. **Creative authorship becomes tentatively outward-facing.** Mafuyu remembers that she once wrote only to expel internal material, then recognizes that recent lyrics were written because she wanted to preserve her own feeling and that she would rather someone hear them than no one. This does not establish a stable communicative mission, but it exceeds purely self-addressed preservation.
5. **Positive affect can motivate follow-through.** Mafuyu remembers Miku's wish to see snow, checks the forecast, returns to tell her, and physically shows her snow despite the cold. This is concrete prosocial persistence tied to a promise rather than externally assigned duty.

### 2.2 I3 — State-changing release

The release changes:

- Mafuyu's current global state: `MF-E0035-01 -> MF-E0042-01`;
- the Kanade–Mafuyu relationship: `REL-N25-KM-6 -> REL-N25-KM-7`;
- Mafuyu's manifestation-specific relationship with N25 Miku enough to justify a dedicated bounded dyad state;
- bounded cross-unit Mafuyu–Ichika and Kanade–Ichika relationship states;
- participant knowledge about public creative identities while preserving private N25 boundaries;
- claims about relationally generated positive affect, contribution-linked self-evidence, and emergent communicative authorship;
- Kanade's model of ordinary life without requiring a successor global state;
- the warmth motif and ordinary-activity/creative-work themes.

Ena and Mizuki receive no new global-state changes from this envelope.

## 3. Causal architecture

### 3.1 Movement A — Public competence continues while other people's `好き` becomes contrast (`0042:01`)

Mafuyu begins in a familiar high-functioning public-school mode. She finishes one teacher-assigned task, moves immediately to class-representative work, apologizes for being late, organizes survey data, and proposes inclusive event planning. Nothing here indicates broad relief from externally reinforced competence. Her public-role machinery remains efficient and costly.

Ichika's limited-edition CD introduces a different kind of motivation: a desire that is obvious, self-authored, and worth reorganizing time around. Mafuyu recognizes the creator because Kanade previously wanted to hear the same person at Fan Festa. She remembers Kanade's enthusiasm and privately wonders whether Kanade will buy the CD.

At the store, Kanade and Ichika reach for the last copy. Kanade gives it to Ichika even though she wanted the new track as compositional reference. Her reason is not self-sacrifice in the rescue/penance register. She is happy that another person likes the same creator and wants that person to hear the music.

When Kanade later tells N25 she is disappointed but still glad that a fellow fan received the CD, Mafuyu observes a legible preference system: Kanade wanted something, lost access to it, and can still derive positive affect from another fan's enjoyment.

The event therefore begins by putting Mafuyu beside two people whose `好き` is easy to identify and behaviorally consequential. The point is not that Mafuyu immediately learns to imitate them. Their clarity becomes an external contrast against which her own uncertain affect can later become visible.

### 3.2 Movement B — Mafuyu becomes a careful bridge without leaking private N25 information (`0042:02-03`)

Ichika tells Mafuyu about the stranger who gave up the CD. Mafuyu recognizes Kanade from the description and agrees to return the CD to her. She gives Ichika only bounded public information: Kanade has composed for a long time, participates in a music circle, and has publicly available work. She does not disclose Mafuyu's own crisis history, Kanade's father/guilt history, the Empty SEKAI, or N25's private support structure.

Ichika explains that Leo/need wants to make music that reaches listeners' hearts. The language matters because it resembles the recipient-oriented vocabulary Kanade has been developing since EVENT_0026 while arising from an independent cross-unit source. Mafuyu becomes interested enough to want to hear Leo/need's music.

The L/n side then listens to public N25 work. Honami notices resemblance to her domestic client's music, but the source carefully stages recognition rather than omniscient inference. Ichika wants to meet the unknown creator because of shared musical interest, not because she knows N25's private history.

This is the first major boundary rule of EVENT_0042:

> **cross-unit creative overlap can increase contact without collapsing epistemic partitions.**

### 3.3 Movement C — Kanade's happiness produces first-person Mafuyu warmth (`0042:04`)

Mafuyu gives Kanade the CD. Kanade's relief and happiness are explicit: she thought she might never hear the material and says she is very glad.

Mafuyu responds conventionally at first—`奏、よかったね`—but then notices something that the public script does not explain:

> `……胸が、あたたかくなった気がした`

This is the event's first decisive transition evidence.

The feeling is related to EVENT_0035 warmth but has a different immediate trigger. No Kanade song is playing. Mafuyu is not currently retrieving a childhood care memory. She is watching **Kanade be happy** after receiving something Mafuyu helped deliver.

The distinction matters. EVENT_0035 established that positive bodily affect can belong to Mafuyu and can connect present experience to autobiographical continuity. EVENT_0042 establishes that Mafuyu's self-evidence can be **relationally generated in the present**.

The loose-leaf lyrics found with the CD create a second contrast. Kanade calls Ichika's language strong and straightforward. Mafuyu recognizes that she could not write in the same way and becomes curious about what Ichika wants to communicate. Her private comparison is not merely technical. Ichika appears to possess an outward-directed intention that Mafuyu cannot yet confidently attribute to herself.

### 3.4 Movement D — Mafuyu chooses an ordinary outing as an experiment on herself (`0042:05`)

Honami's role connects the previously separate networks. Kanade learns that Honami knows Ichika; Ichika learns that the mysterious composer is Honami's domestic client. Kanade agrees to meet because she genuinely wants to speak with another young person who makes music, and she asks Honami to attend because ordinary social initiation remains difficult for her.

In the Empty SEKAI, Mafuyu returns to the unexplained warmth. She tells Miku that seeing Kanade smile made her chest feel warm and explicitly says she does not understand why.

Kanade invites her to the meeting. Mafuyu initially sees no reason to go: she already sees Ichika at school. Miku then asks whether the meeting might help Mafuyu understand the feeling.

Mafuyu reconsiders and chooses to attend.

This is developmentally important because the action is not assigned by school, family, N25 production, or Kanade's rescue logic. Mafuyu voluntarily treats ordinary social participation as a self-search instrument.

The event therefore generalizes the search method:

`unexpected affect -> preserve uncertainty -> identify possible relational context -> voluntarily enter that context -> observe self/others -> update first-person evidence`.

### 3.5 Movement E — Cross-unit music talk becomes a low-pressure laboratory (`0042:06`)

The meeting among Ichika, Honami, Kanade, and Mafuyu is structurally valuable because it is neither a crisis intervention nor a formal N25 production session.

Kanade recognizes Ichika's voice from earlier performances and gives specific praise about improvement. Ichika explains wanting music to reach people's hearts. Kanade says she wants something similar. The conversation therefore gives Mafuyu a comparison between two people who can articulate creative desire in recipient-directed language.

Mafuyu also displays ordinary practical knowledge of Kanade's poor self-care. Her comments about sleep/food are grounded in real familiarity, while Honami's presence gives the conversation a second form of care: domestic maintenance that does not require access to N25's private crisis history.

The four-person meeting makes relational difference observable without demanding that anyone solve Mafuyu. This is exactly the kind of ordinary activity that the longitudinal method should treat as reconstruction evidence rather than filler.

### 3.6 Movement F — Other people hear Mafuyu in her lyrics before Mafuyu can confidently name what is there (`0042:07`)

When Ichika and Honami ask about N25's lyrics, Mafuyu initially minimizes her authorship. She says good lyrics emerge because Kanade provides good music and frames herself as fitting words to the song.

Ichika and Honami independently disagree with the strongest version of that self-description. They say Mafuyu's own feelings appear to come through.

The source does **not** authorize their interpretation to become authoritative simply because it is affirmative. Mafuyu remains the final authority on what she can support. But their feedback becomes relational evidence that prompts Mafuyu to compare earlier and current writing.

She remembers the former OWN state:

- she did not have a desire to deliver something to another person;
- she did not particularly want anyone to hear;
- writing functioned primarily as expulsion/search.

She then contrasts the current process:

- she believed she still had no thoughts and was only matching Kanade's music;
- when Kanade's song made her smile, she wanted to leave **her own feeling** in words;
- she recognizes that being heard by someone is preferable to no one hearing it;
- she asks whether she may actually be putting something into the lyrics.

This is not a completed declaration of communicative purpose. The interrogative ending matters. But the earlier claim that Mafuyu's recent lyrics are purely mechanical accompaniment is no longer adequate.

EVENT_0035 established self-authored preservation. EVENT_0042 adds **emergent outward orientation**.

### 3.7 Movement G — Kanade rediscovers ordinary social pleasure (`0042:08`)

At the end of the meeting, Ichika asks to meet again. Kanade reciprocates: she rarely has opportunities to talk like this outside her circle and would be happy to continue.

Her private reflection is unusually important. Kanade judges the meeting as simply fun. She remembers enjoying time with friends in childhood and says she had forgotten that memory.

For years, her conscious life has been organized around the imperative to create music that saves people. EVENT_0042 exposes a domain that the rescue/penance architecture had made difficult to access: **ordinary companionship as valuable without being instrumental to salvation**.

Kanade does not abandon the mission. She does not revise the father-guilt belief, begin healthy self-care, or stop measuring music partly through rescue efficacy. Therefore no successor to `K-E0026-01` is warranted.

But the reconstruction model must now allow:

- genuine non-instrumental enjoyment with peers;
- curiosity about people outside N25;
- future contact chosen because conversation was enjoyable;
- recognition that years of mission-narrowing may have caused ordinary parts of life to be forgotten or lost.

Card `0327:02` makes the last point explicit rather than leaving it as a scene-local inference.

### 3.8 Movement H — Contribution-linked warmth becomes accepted self-evidence (`0042:08`)

Kanade thanks Mafuyu for the chain of events that led to the meeting and says she is glad she met Ichika and Honami.

Mafuyu again feels warmth.

This repetition makes the core endpoint stronger than the first occurrence. Mafuyu identifies the causal structure with unusual clarity:

- she only told Ichika about Kanade and passed along a CD;
- the action was small;
- nevertheless, it helped Kanade;
- knowing that it helped Kanade makes Mafuyu's chest warm.

The endpoint is not a categorical label. Mafuyu does not say “this is friendship,” “I like helping people,” or “I love Kanade.” She says:

> `——悪い感覚ではない、かな`

This restrained appraisal is developmentally appropriate to `MF-E0042-01`. Mafuyu can now:

1. notice the sensation;
2. link it to a relational outcome;
3. recognize her own contribution;
4. evaluate the sensation as non-negative/worth retaining;
5. remain uncertain about its final meaning.

That sequence is enough for a state transition without pretending that categorical self-knowledge has arrived.

- she can voluntarily enter an ordinary social situation to investigate unexplained affect;

- she can form and keep a low-stakes promise because another person's wish matters to her;
- she can tentatively imagine her lyrics as carrying something outward to listeners, not only preserving material for herself.

#### Preserved vulnerabilities / negative constraints

Linked area `_002` is especially important here. Mafuyu loses lunch time to school obligations and immediately considers simply not eating. EVENT_0042 therefore **does not** establish broad improved self-care. Relational warmth and prosocial follow-through coexist with the old public-role overfunctioning/body-neglect problem.

Likewise, her ability to care about Kanade/Miku does not establish stable access to her own ordinary likes. The state must not be simplified into “Mafuyu learns she likes helping people.”

### 4.2 Why this is I3 rather than an I2 extension of EVENT_0035

If EVENT_0042 only repeated warmth during another Kanade song, `MF-E0035-01` would be sufficient. Instead, the source adds a new trigger class, a new action rule, and persistence across multiple contexts:

- Kanade happiness -> warmth;
- uncertainty -> self-chosen mixed-unit meeting;
- Ichika/Honami feedback -> revision of lyric self-model;
- Miku relation question -> ordered-song inquiry;
- Miku wish -> promise, forecast monitoring, follow-through;
- reciprocal Miku warmth -> relational effect becomes bidirectional;
- tea/flavor exercise -> ongoing sensory self-retrieval beyond the core.

That changes future prediction. Post-EVENT_0042 Mafuyu is more likely than `MF-E0035-01` alone would predict to treat another person's positive response to her actions as first-person evidence and to follow a relational clue without waiting for complete categorical understanding.

## 8. Associated-card audit

### 8.1 Mafuyu `0324` — relation becomes an object of inquiry

Ichika's account of Miku is useful because it contains ordinary language for a personally meaningful Virtual Singer relationship: freedom, expression, help, and the ability to communicate to important people.

Mafuyu asks what Miku means to Ichika and then turns the question inward: `……私にとっては、なんなんだろう`.

In the Empty SEKAI she asks Miku to sing N25's songs in creation order, using the shared artifact sequence as a temporal index of relationship/self change. When this does not immediately produce an answer, she asks to stop analyzing and simply listen.
