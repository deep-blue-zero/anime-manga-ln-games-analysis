## 2. A future question exposes duty without desire

Kanade’s grandmother asks what Kanade herself wants after graduation and promises support for any path. Kanade initially has only obligation: she must keep composing and cannot imagine a future outside it. Ena and Mizuki make the distinction explicit between `must` and `want`; they value Kanade’s care while asking her to include her own happiness. `PJSK:event:0116:01:001:0052–0071`; `PJSK:event:0116:02:001:0042–0100`.

The event does not resolve schooling or work. Its change is prior to a career decision: Kanade recognizes that the absence of a personal answer is itself meaningful and begins a directed search.

## 3. Music began as curiosity, imitation, and reciprocal warmth

At the lake, Rin asks to hear Kanade’s concern because she wants to support Kanade, not only Mafuyu. Kanade traces music back to watching her father compose and wanting to create the same happiness. `PJSK:event:0116:04:002:0011–0038`; `PJSK:event:0116:04:004:0002–0019`.

The first song is not a rescue transaction. Child Kanade experiments with an old keyboard, imitates observed technique, improvises through mistakes, incorporates her mother’s words, and gives the song as a birthday gift. Both parents receive it with surprise, praise, and a request for more. `PJSK:event:0116:05:003:0010–0034`; `PJSK:event:0116:05:004:0014–0050`.

This reconstructs the prosocial origin of Kanade’s music without pretending later guilt is false or trivial. Music was loved before it became compulsory.

## 4. `K-E0116-01` — permitted desire beside unresolved penance

Remembered warmth immediately activates loss and self-accusation. Kanade concludes that the former family time cannot return and retreats to the duty to create in her father’s place. Empty KAITO identifies the action precisely: she is killing a wish because she believes she has no right to hold it. `PJSK:event:0116:06:002:0016–0049`; `PJSK:event:0116:07:001:0062–0113`.

Ena supplies recipient-side evidence and a relational demand. Kanade’s music helped Ena continue drawing; N25 made it possible for Ena to face a future at all; therefore Ena wants Kanade to choose a path that can include Kanade’s happiness. KAITO exposes the practical contradiction between telling Mafuyu to protect her feelings and refusing Kanade the same rule. `PJSK:event:0116:07:001:0072–0141`.

Kanade’s action is bounded but durable enough for I3. She composes a brighter song from the first melody and the wish for restored warmth, allows N25 and the Empty singers to hear it, and tells her father that she still hopes to be remembered and praised. `PJSK:event:0116:08:002:0010–0037`; `PJSK:event:0116:08:004:0003–0033`.

It does not certify acquittal, healthy workload, rescue completion, career choice, family restoration, or paternal recognition.
