## 4. Core chapter 01 — public Mafuyu is not merely masking passively; she is socially causally effective

Chapter 01 opens in the Miyamasuzaka kyudo-club context. Mafuyu performs the highly competent public register already established elsewhere:

- she acknowledges shared fatigue after practice;
- checks Shizuku's physical condition;
- praises Shizuku's diligence;
- tells the other club members to rest and return ready for the next practice;
- notices Shizuku's concern and offers to listen without demanding disclosure.

The important refinement is that public competence is not just an externally pleasing shell. Mafuyu can make useful observations and produce good social advice while operating inside that mode.

When Shizuku explains that she is struggling to devise a livestream idea, Mafuyu does not pretend to possess idol expertise. She explicitly limits her authority, offers to listen, and then generates seasonally appropriate possibilities: autumn food, books, and moon-viewing. The moon-viewing suggestion becomes a causal input to Shizuku's event plan.

This supports a more exact rule:

> **Mafuyu's public role can contain genuine cognitive competence, situational attention, and prosocial usefulness even when the activity itself is not privately meaningful to her.**

The correct contrast is therefore not `public = fake / private = real`. The distinction is between a highly optimized public-social mode and a lower-burden private mode with different access to spontaneous first-person reaction.

## 5. Card 0278 — the same seasonal object exposes the public/private distinction

### 5.1 Front half: smooth public affiliation, weak autobiographical access

After morning kyudo practice, Shizuku invites Mafuyu to walk with her. Mafuyu accepts in a socially warm, polished register and continues the conversation naturally. Shizuku thanks her for the earlier event advice and says that the moon-viewing plan resulted from it.

When Shizuku asks about moon-viewing in Mafuyu's own family, Mafuyu says that they have not done anything recently and probably will not this year. Asked about childhood, she can retrieve a generic fact: kindergarten and elementary school held seasonal events, and she thinks she participated.

The contrast with Shizuku is explicit. Shizuku remembers her own childhood moon-viewing vividly. Mafuyu internally fixates on the fact of **remembering** itself.

After they separate, Mafuyu predicts that on the moon-viewing night she will probably study or work on Nightcord material and concludes that the ritual is not really relevant to her.

This is not evidence that the public conversation was a lie. It is evidence that socially appropriate engagement can exceed private autobiographical salience.

### 5.2 Back half: N25 turns an irrelevant ritual into weakly positive shared experience

Later, all four N25 members are on Nightcord. Ena shares a photograph of the moon and the group discusses ordinary seasonal food. Mafuyu says she did nothing for moon-viewing and initially treats the object literally: the moon can be seen at other times too.

Mizuki proposes that everyone look outside together. Mafuyu explicitly predicts a null result: she thinks she probably will not feel anything even if she looks.

The scene then supplies a useful sequence:

1. **Mizuki creates the low-pressure shared activity.** No demand is made that Mafuyu produce the “correct” emotional response.
2. **Kanade offers a descriptive frame rather than an interpretation of Mafuyu.** She describes the moon's blurred outline as if it might dissolve into the sky.
3. **Mafuyu finds that description legible.** She agrees and says that looking at it is not boring.
4. **The group preserves individual response styles.** Ena evaluates composition, Mizuki romanticizes simultaneous viewing, Mafuyu immediately notices the time-zone problem in Mizuki's world-opposite hypothetical.
5. **Mafuyu privately registers weak positive valence.** She notes that they only looked at the moon, but that it does not feel unpleasant.
6. **Mafuyu initiates a small relational return.** Before Mizuki leaves, Mafuyu calls them back and offers thanks.

This is stronger than simple compliance. Mafuyu does not merely remain in the activity because others asked. She notices a non-negative internal result and independently closes the exchange with gratitude.

It is still weaker than recovered categorical preference. The source does not authorize:

- “Mafuyu likes moon-viewing”;
- “Mafuyu now understands her seasonal preferences”;
- “Kanade can tell Mafuyu what Mafuyu feels”;
- “N25 has cured the detachment demonstrated earlier in the card.”

The supported formulation is narrower:

> **Relational co-attention and another person's concrete sensory/metaphoric framing can make an otherwise irrelevant object affectively available enough for Mafuyu to register weak positive valence before categorical preference is accessible.**

That is a meaningful I2 refinement because it connects several earlier strands—affect before appraisal, first-person authority, ordinary companionship, and Kanade's relational searching—inside one controlled low-stakes scene.

## 10. Claim transitions

### PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN — public competence is real competence, not proof of private investment

EVENT_0029 already established that Mafuyu's polished public mode remains highly capable. EVENT_0033 strengthens the claim by showing that the public mode can generate useful, causally consequential advice while the same subject remains privately low-salience.

A more precise formulation is:

> **socially optimized presentation may be affectively overdetermined by role demands, but the cognition, observation, and practical help enacted through that role can still be genuine and effective.**

### STRENGTHEN — weak valence can precede categorical preference

Earlier events established affect before appraisal and weak comparative valence. EVENT_0033 provides a low-stakes ordinary example in which Mafuyu moves from expected null affect to `退屈じゃない` / `嫌な気分じゃない` without saying `好き`.

The model should therefore preserve graded evidence:

> `predicted null -> lived non-null valence -> possible salience -> preference remains unresolved`.

### REJECT — relational framing equals external authorship

Kanade's imagery and Mizuki's invitation create conditions for attention; they do not author Mafuyu's response. Mafuyu independently evaluates the experience. The scene strengthens rather than weakens first-person authority.

## 12. Reconstruction consequences

EVENT_0033 adds several high-value D0/D1 rules without changing state IDs:

1. **Public Mafuyu:** in school/club contexts, default to smooth prosocial competence, situational noticing, practical advice, and conventional warmth unless stress breaks the role. Do not render public mode as obviously robotic merely because the audience knows it is role-heavy.
2. **Private Mafuyu under low-stakes invitation:** low initial interest does not guarantee refusal. If demand is low and trusted peers invite participation, she may comply and discover weak valence only afterward.
3. **Mafuyu preference language:** treat `not boring`, `not unpleasant`, salience, repeated attention, or voluntary return as meaningful but sub-categorical evidence. Do not translate automatically into `likes`.
4. **Kanade support:** useful affect-search prompts can be first-person sensory/metaphoric descriptions rather than interrogation or diagnosis.
5. **Mizuki facilitation:** small group activities may be initiated playfully without requiring everyone to share the same emotional interpretation.
6. **Mafuyu-Shizuku:** use a comfortable public peer register with bounded trust, no Nightcord-private disclosure, and plausible ordinary consultation.
7. **Group co-presence:** N25 ordinary sociality does not require physical co-location; synchronized attention through Nightcord can function as a shared ritual.
