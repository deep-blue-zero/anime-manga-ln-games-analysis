# Project SEKAI — EVENT_0029 N25 Integration Checkpoint
## 夏祭り、鳴り響く音は — ordinary happiness, post-event absence, and care that does not force disclosure

## 1. Analytical responsibility and triage decision

`EVENT_0029 — 夏祭り、鳴り響く音は` is a mixed event whose central developmental narrative belongs to Shinonome Akito: childhood athletic aptitude, the first serious defeat that made him reinterpret his own commitment, withdrawal from soccer, Ena's casual but consequential suggestion that he try music, and a return to the same summer-festival setting after music has become his chosen serious pursuit. That material is analytically substantial. It should be preserved for later Vivid BAD SQUAD and Shinonome-family work.

It is not, however, sufficient reason to turn EVENT_0029 into an N25 full-event deep reading. The current sequential program is integrating Nightcord longitudinal authority from the already completed N25 foundation. Within that responsibility, the event does something narrower and still valuable: it tests whether EVENT_0019's Mizuki disclosure conflict survives successful ordinary companionship, clarifies Ena's support strategy, expands N25's public ordinary-life repertoire, and supplies several reconstruction-grade relationship and register observations.

For the **N25 analytical layer**, EVENT_0029 is therefore classified:

- **Tier B — Relationship**;
- **secondary Tier C — Characterization**;
- **I2 — Interpretive refinement**.

The I2 decision is important. EVENT_0029 does **not** produce a durable new global psychological state for Mafuyu, Kanade, Ena, or Mizuki. It does not resolve Mizuki's guarded issue, make Mizuki disclosure-ready, create a new group relationship form, or replace Ena's EVENT_0014 artistic state. Instead, it strongly confirms and sharpens already-current models:

- `MZ-E0019-01` remains current: genuine enjoyment and future attachment coexist with a painful nondisclosure conflict;
- `REL-N25-EMZ-1` remains current: Ena and Mizuki have reciprocal ordinary refuge, bidirectional concern, and incomplete knowledge;
- `REL-N25-G-7` remains current: N25 is a future-oriented ordinary companionship whose closeness does not require complete disclosure;
- `E-E0014-01`, `MF-E0002-01`, and `K-E0026-01` remain current with bounded refinements.

The strongest EVENT_0029 contribution is not “Mizuki gets better.” It is the much more precise proposition:

> **Mizuki can sincerely enjoy being with N25, preserve that enjoyment as something precious, and still experience a sharp post-event sense of absence because the relationship now matters while an important part of the self remains guarded.**

The event also clarifies Ena's side of the dyad:

> **Ena can notice that direct pressure is not solving Mizuki's nondisclosure, accept a long campaign rather than demand immediate disclosure, and continue creating ordinary invitations whose value does not depend on obtaining the secret.**

That combination materially improves reconstruction without changing state IDs. A focused integration checkpoint is therefore the proportional artifact.

## 4. The Akito developmental spine — preserved context, not N25 authority

Because EVENT_0029's main plot is not N25-centered, the checkpoint must preserve enough of its actual structure to avoid misreading the N25 scenes as isolated festival fluff.

### 4.1 Early aptitude and the first encounter with a higher standard

Child Akito is presented as unusually physically capable and quickly bored by activities he can master without much difficulty. Soccer lasts longer because the field, positioning, coordination, and team interaction create complexity. He believes himself serious about it.

The decisive childhood defeat exposes a different scale of commitment. Akito encounters peers who live apart from family and organize nearly all available time around becoming professional players. The loss itself hurts, but the deeper wound is comparative: Akito realizes that the “seriousness” he had credited himself with existed inside a smaller frame than the one he has just witnessed.

He responds by quitting rather than gradually renegotiating what seriousness might mean.

Card `0254` makes the mechanism unusually explicit. Akito later describes the experience as shame at having thought he was serious. Yet the same card also shows that he still likes soccer: helping the school club practice makes intuitive sense to him because he understands the pain of wanting to improve, and touching a ball still excites him enough to override some of the old bitterness.

This matters structurally because the event is about **all-or-nothing seriousness being interrupted by permission to begin before certainty**.

### 4.2 Ena's casual sentence becomes causal history

After Akito quits soccer, the earlier summer festival becomes the bridge to music. Ena sees that a live performance has made him look interested for the first time in a while. When he objects that he cannot simply start music without knowing whether he can be serious about it, Ena rejects the premise.

Her practical rule is:

> **try it first; decide whether to become serious after experiencing it.**

This is not delivered as a solemn life-changing speech. It is ordinary sibling advice from someone who notices that Akito looks alive again. That casualness is analytically important. The later Akito does not build a verbal cult around Ena's sentence; he nevertheless remembers it as part of the causal route by which he encountered the people and music that now organize his life.

At the end of EVENT_0029, the siblings remember the same old festival and the shoe-delivery incident. When Ena tells Akito that continuing music is impressive, Akito privately thinks that he met his present companions because of her. He says it too quietly for Ena to hear.

The event therefore adds a strong Shinonome-family claim:

> **Ena is not merely a sibling who criticizes, demands favors from, and receives practical care from Akito; she is also one of the causal witnesses who recognized a viable interest during his post-soccer collapse and gave him permission to explore it before certainty.**

This should extend `REL-FAMILY-ENA-AKITO-E0014` rather than create a new N25 character state.

## 5. Core chapter 03 — Ena deliberately uses ordinary life as Mizuki support

The first direct N25 scene is deceptively ordinary: the members discuss going to the summer festival.

The key evidence is Ena's internal reason for wanting the group there. EVENT_0019 left Ena with clear but causally incomplete concern about Mizuki. EVENT_0029 shows that Ena has not abandoned that concern just because Mizuki can joke, work, and appear ordinary again. The festival becomes an opportunity to invite Mizuki into a low-pressure shared activity.

This is not a rescue operation disguised as leisure. Ena does not announce that Mizuki needs treatment, demand an explanation, or condition the invitation on disclosure. She creates a situation in which:

- the whole group has a legitimate reason to attend;
- Mizuki is not singled out as a problem;
- fun can be intrinsically worthwhile;
- concern can motivate an invitation without controlling what the outing must produce.

Mizuki initially dislikes the heat/effort but joins because the others are going. Kanade is interested in hearing live music; Mafuyu can attend after cram school. The plan is therefore a normal negotiated group activity rather than a therapeutic assignment.

This strengthens Ena's EVENT_0019 support grammar:

> **when causal access is absent and direct questioning has not opened the guarded issue, Ena may keep the relationship available through concrete ordinary invitations instead of escalating interrogation.**

## 6. Core chapter 06 — the festival is genuinely fun, and that does not solve Mizuki

The festival chapter gives some of the best ordinary-life N25 evidence yet integrated.

### 6.1 Distinct participation modes remain intact

The shooting-gallery sequence is useful because the four do not collapse into a generic friendship register.

- **Mizuki** supplies energy, commentary, aesthetic interest, and social momentum.
- **Ena** becomes competitive, visibly frustrated at poor performance, and easily activated by Mafuyu's matter-of-fact competence.
- **Mafuyu** succeeds immediately despite no prior experience and says the task is simple from her perspective; the resulting bluntness is recognizably the more candid N25 mode rather than her polished public role.
- **Kanade** experiences the physical burden of the equipment more strongly and tires quickly.

This is reconstruction-grade evidence precisely because everyone can occupy the same leisure context without converging behaviorally.

### 6.2 The souvenir is attachment evidence

Mizuki wins an odd local cat keychain and attaches it immediately because it commemorates **everyone coming to the summer festival together**. Ena notices the significance privately.

The object matters less as merchandise than as an external memory marker. EVENT_0019 made future duration emotionally dangerous because Mizuki now has something worth preserving. EVENT_0029 shows Mizuki actively preserving ordinary shared time as memory.

This is evidence **for** attachment, not evidence that attachment pressure has disappeared.

### 6.3 Future language reactivates the conflict

Mizuki suggests that they could reproduce a festival-like experience in the Empty SEKAI for Miku and the other Virtual Singers. The idea is consistent with N25's established tendency to translate ordinary-world experience into shared social/creative space.

Then Mizuki begins imagining another future opportunity for everyone to go together and falters.

This is the same temporal pressure identified in EVENT_0019. Present-tense enjoyment is safe enough. A cue that asks Mizuki to imagine **continued future belonging** makes the guarded issue relevant again. Ena notices the interruption but does not receive the cause.

The correct interpretation is therefore not:

- “Mizuki was pretending to enjoy the festival,” or
- “the festival cured the EVENT_0019 problem.”

It is:

> **The enjoyment is real. The future threat is also real.**

That coexistence is one of the most important reconstruction constraints supplied by the event.

## 9. Cards 0257-0258 — the Ena-Mizuki relationship is strengthened, not transitioned

### 9.1 Ena accepts a long campaign

A week after the festival, Airi and Shizuku ask Ena how Mizuki is doing.

Ena does not claim a breakthrough. Her answer is essentially **no major progress**. More important is the method she has accepted: she is prepared for a `持久戦`—a long campaign.

This is a meaningful refinement of Ena's support behavior.

Ena also says she has seen moments when Mizuki looked somewhat better, probably because someone in the circle did or said something helpful. That gives her a new anti-despair rule: if good experiences can sometimes lift Mizuki, then it is not true that **nothing anyone does can matter**.

This does not authorize Ena to infer the guarded issue. Instead it stabilizes persistence under uncertainty.

Airi's concern is bidirectional: she worries about Mizuki, but also about Ena becoming consumed by the problem. Ena says that being able to complain/talk to Airi already helps. This places the Ena-Mizuki support process inside a wider social support network rather than making Ena an isolated rescuer.

### 9.2 Mizuki's post-festival emptiness is direct evidence

Card `0258` opens after the successful festival. Mizuki explicitly remembers that, **in the moment, they really were having fun**. The card then immediately gives the counterfact to any simple recovery reading: afterward Mizuki feels as though there is a hole in the chest.

This is unusually clean evidence for `MZ-E0019-01`.

The structure is:

`genuine enjoyment → return to solitude/reflection → attachment becomes salient → guarded asymmetry remains → absence/void feeling`.

The event therefore strengthens a non-monotonic model of Mizuki recovery and disclosure. Positive social experience can improve mood without resolving the thing that makes durable closeness threatening.

### 9.3 Toya provides a structural comparison, not an identical biography

Mizuki meets Toya and learns bounded information about his earlier forced classical training. What matters to Mizuki is seeing how sincerely Toya can smile around chosen music after a history in which music was also coercively structured.

Toya says that companions helped him face his father somewhat. Mizuki responds to the value of being able to do what one likes.

This can be useful as a structural analogue—chosen activity and supportive relationships can alter the meaning of a previously painful domain—but must not be turned into “Toya solved the same problem Mizuki has.” Their causal histories and disclosure problems are different.

### 9.4 Akito independently authorizes self-interested creation and reciprocal reliance

Later, Akito says that N25 making music “for themselves” is not a defect. He credits the circle with helping Ena when she was badly destabilized after conflict with their father and expresses real gratitude that Ena has people around her.

He then gives Mizuki an ordinary sibling-derived rule: Ena causes people trouble all the time, so Mizuki should feel free to cause trouble back.

The significance comes from convergence. Mizuki remembers Airi earlier saying that if Mizuki is in trouble, relying on Ena is legitimate because Ena will help. Two different people who know Ena well independently frame the relationship as one in which Mizuki need not remain the low-burden, entertaining companion.

Mizuki's response does not become disclosure. Instead, Mizuki explicitly describes Ena as someone who is meddlesome in the constructive sense: good at taking care of others and unable to leave someone in trouble alone.

Immediately afterward, Ena sends another casual invitation to go buy a new lip product together. Mizuki's closing thought is affectionate exasperation: Ena really does not leave them alone.

This is the correct endpoint for the relationship in EVENT_0029.

> **Reliance becomes more imaginable; disclosure is still not enacted.**

That is strengthening, not a state transition.
