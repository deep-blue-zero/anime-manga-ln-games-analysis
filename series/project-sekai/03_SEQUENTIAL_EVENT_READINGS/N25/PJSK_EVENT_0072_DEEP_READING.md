## 2. Family control remains mixed with genuine care


The opening preserves EVENT_0061's mixed-valence family model.


Mafuyu's mother:
- checks where Mafuyu is and why she is late more closely after the missed mock exam;
- pre-buys a recommended medical-school study book before Mafuyu can obtain it herself;
- plans the weekend around study and a supposedly favorite stew;
- frames intervention as concern and support.


Mafuyu remains attached and responsive. Nothing here authorizes a simple “mother does not care” reading.


At the same time, the pattern narrows Mafuyu's action space. Even ordinary delay and weekend use are increasingly legible as matters requiring justification.


## 3. Nursing/medical salience appears, but it is not yet a vocation


Mafuyu notices a `聖花看護大学 過去問題集` on the shelf and pauses. Later the volunteer form's `救護` role catches her attention. These are genuine salience events.


They must remain epistemically modest.


The source does **not** establish:
- “Mafuyu wants to become a nurse”;
- “Mafuyu has chosen medicine after all”;
- “the mother's doctor path was secretly correct.”


What it establishes is that concrete care/medical activity is no longer psychologically empty in the way an abstract externally authored career label can be.


## 4. Tactical autonomy becomes more sophisticated


A teacher asks Mafuyu to volunteer at Shibuya Festa and explicitly permits refusal. Mafuyu's first private thought is relational:


`（ボランティアで参加すれば、みんなと……）`


She accepts.


At home, however, that motive would not fit the family permission structure. Mafuyu therefore gives a different, institutionally legible rationale: volunteering may help school records and recommendation interviews.


This is not evidence that the stated admissions benefit is wholly false. It is evidence that **a self-chosen action can be protected through an acceptable secondary justification when direct desire remains difficult or unsafe to state.**


Compared with EVENT_0061, autonomy is becoming more strategically organized rather than simply erupting through somatic refusal.


## 5. Being asked what she wants exposes the remaining deficit


At volunteer assignment Mafuyu is told she may choose among roles. Her response—`え……選んで、いいんですか？`—is itself diagnostic.


Before she can make a preference claim, a staffing shortage appears and she volunteers for stage-area guidance. When asked whether another task was desired, she says:


`特にやりたいことは——ないので`.


This should not be read as proof that the earlier rescue-role salience was meaningless. It shows the state at the beginning of the event: Mafuyu can notice salience, but not yet reliably promote it into a positive preference statement.


## 6. Other people's self-authored goals become repeated comparison stimuli


Throughout the festival, Mafuyu encounters people who can say what their difficult effort is for:
- Shizuku/MMJ want to deliver hope;
- Honami/Leo/need want to become professionals and will attempt uncomfortable outreach to widen their audience;
- WxS accepts dangerous/tedious production labor because the show itself is something they genuinely want to do.


These encounters repeatedly produce the same unnamed chest/moya signal.


Mafuyu does not simply envy their success. She is affected by the causal structure:


`difficulty + self-authored purpose -> effort that remains meaningful because the actor wants the destination`.


Her own externally authored study path does not provide the same first-person legibility.


## 7. Practical care provides a positive self-evidence route


Rui is injured while testing a wind-sensitive show device. When the rescue staff are absent, Mafuyu volunteers to treat him.


Her competence is concrete:
- recognizes need for prompt care;
- checks tenderness and severity;
- identifies a likely light sprain;
- cools and stabilizes it;
- explains `Rest, Icing, Compression, Elevation`;
- gives follow-up activity restrictions.


Emu and Rui praise the skill and map it onto the visible “medical-school” future. Mafuyu falls silent when Rui says that explains why she wants to become a doctor.


That silence is crucial. **Competence is not identical to ownership of the career identity.**


## 8. Rui's intervention: desire may exist before it is recognized


Rui and Emu explain that the show is difficult but is still something they want to do. Mafuyu responds that she has nothing like that.


Rui does not tell her what she wants. He offers a first-person analogy: he once believed his desire was simply to make his own shows, but later discovered that what he actually wanted included doing that work with people who wanted it too. He had failed to recognize the fuller desire partly because he had given up on the possibility.


His proposition is deliberately bounded:


`気づけていないだけかもしれませんよ`.


This is analytically compatible with first-person authority because Rui does not claim privileged knowledge of Mafuyu's content. He expands the hypothesis space: **absence of a fluent answer is not proof of absence of desire.**


## 9. Gratitude for care becomes affective evidence


After the successful WxS show, Rui, Emu, Tsukasa, and Nene explicitly thank Mafuyu because her treatment enabled something deeply important to them.


Mafuyu later recognizes that the thanks made her chest feel lighter.


The final chapter repeats the route with a lost/injured child. Mafuyu treats the abrasion, adds a remembered childhood soothing phrase—`痛いの痛いの、飛んでけ`—and receives direct thanks from the child and mother.


Kanade observes that Mafuyu smiles slightly.


Mafuyu can then connect two cases:
- helping Rui / receiving gratitude;
- helping the child / receiving gratitude.


This is real positive self-evidence. It remains **evidence about caregiving experience**, not proof of a settled medical vocation.


## 10. The first explicit positive desire statement


The strongest transition occurs at the street piano.


Music triggers memory of the surrendered synthesizer and of the externally enforced “I will quit music” line. Kanade asks a first-person question rather than assigning a feeling:


`……まふゆ。ピアノ、弾きたい？`


Mafuyu initially repeats the verb as if testing it:


`……やりたい……`


Then confirms:


`……うん`
`——少し、弾いてみたい`


This is qualitatively different from the EVENT_0061 boundary. In EVENT_0061 Mafuyu could choose Phoenix Wonderland and curfew violation before she could explain what she wanted. Here she can translate salience into an affirmative action-specific desire statement.


## 11. Desire remains bounded rather than globally decoded


The piano does not produce categorical recovery.


Kanade asks whether Mafuyu is having fun. Mafuyu answers:


`…………。……わからない、けど`
`もう少しだけ、こうしていたい`


This is the correct new-state boundary.


Mafuyu still cannot reliably name `楽しい`. But she can know **continuation preference**: she wants the present activity/state to continue a little longer.


Reconstruction rule:


`salience -> direct first-person question -> tentative action-specific want -> enacted participation -> continuation preference`.


Do not jump from this to broad preference fluency.


## 12. Why the piano matters


Mafuyu chooses a song associated with the first time Kanade's music helped her recover a smile and then permits Kanade to join the performance.


The instrument therefore connects several previously separated lines:
- music as a surrendered/controlled personal domain;
- N25 as chosen relational warmth;
- creative work as evidence;
- action-specific self-authored wanting;
- shared performance that is neither work obligation nor rescue crisis.


The event does not establish a music career choice. It establishes that music can again be something Mafuyu herself can want to **do**.


## 13. Card 0511: post-event persistence and ordinary embodiment


Card `0511` confirms several consequences.


After the piano, Mafuyu can simply agree that it was good and join ordinary festival activity. She notices a crepe stall that might interest the N25 manifestations and chooses to try a crepe herself.


The sensory deficit remains: she still does not meaningfully taste it. Yet eating with everyone does not feel bad, and she infers the relational context may matter.


A week later, the “moya” signal still reappears around other people's chosen goals. It is not solved by one piano scene.


Emu reports Rui's recovery; Mafuyu shows concern, gives follow-up restrictions, and smiles when told that her care mattered. This cross-event persistence strengthens the caregiving-evidence line without converting it into vocational certainty.
