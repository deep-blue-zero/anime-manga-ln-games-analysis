# Project SEKAI — EVENT_0002 Deep Reading: 囚われのマリオネット

## 1. Purpose, authority, and longitudinal role

This document is the first post-main-story longitudinal deep reading for Nightcord at 25:00. It inherits the canonical N25 foundation state from `PJSK_N25_MAIN_STORY_SYNTHESIS.md` and asks the governing event question:

> **What is different after EVENT_0002 than before it?**

The answer is substantial enough to classify the event as **Tier A — Developmental** and **I3 — State-changing**.

The event does not resolve Mafuyu's identity crisis. It changes the *operational model by which Mafuyu and the others can search for a self that Mafuyu still cannot directly describe*. The main story ended with Mafuyu provisionally agreeing to keep looking because part of her wanted to be found. EVENT_0002 supplies the first concrete technique for that search:

> **an affect does not have to be pleasant, fully understood, or propositionally explainable in order to count as evidence that it belongs to Mafuyu.**

Disgust, pain, discomfort, bodily pressure, attraction, relief, and the impulse to express can all be treated as first-person data. Creative work becomes not only evidence *about* a hidden psychological state but an interface through which otherwise inaccessible affect can be externalized, returned to Mafuyu in transformed form, and examined without requiring prior self-understanding.

This is the first durable post-main step from **“I want to be found”** toward **“something I feel can be noticed, expressed, and retained even before I know what it means.”**

# 2. Significance triage

## 2.1 Classification: Tier A — Developmental

EVENT_0002 changes a durable character-state rule for Mafuyu and a durable interaction rule for N25.

### Before the event

At the main-story endpoint:

- Mafuyu has recovered the desire to be found and agrees to continue searching, but she still struggles to identify ordinary preferences and does not possess a stable method for locating the self she seeks.
- Kanade promises to continue creating until Mafuyu finds herself, but her operative model remains strongly rescue-centered.
- Ena's attachment to Mafuyu is durable but antagonistic and poorly integrated into ordinary collaboration.
- Mizuki understands the danger of externally imposed identity and has become a social/autonomy mediator, but the group has not yet developed an explicit practice for receiving Mafuyu's raw affect.
- Nightcord can survive serious conflict, but it has not yet demonstrated that Mafuyu's *unfiltered* reactions can become ordinary collaborative input rather than only crisis speech.

### After the event

- Mafuyu has a usable rule that unpleasant and ambiguous feeling states can count as **her** feelings rather than useless noise.
- Mafuyu can externalize affect into lyrics without first knowing exactly what the affect means.
- Mafuyu experiences hearing those lyrics transformed into a song as a differentiated bodily-emotional event and chooses to try expressing future feelings.
- N25 explicitly permits and even invites Mafuyu to say what she actually feels around them, including reactions that are blunt or inconvenient.
- Kanade's rescue method becomes more relational: rather than only making increasingly powerful music *at* Mafuyu, she seeks traces of Mafuyu's interiority and composes *from/with* those traces.
- Ena and Mafuyu discover that abrasive honesty can function as a real creative feedback loop without destroying the relationship.
- Mizuki's role as boundary-aware mediator and voluntary companion is strengthened.
- N25 Miku is established as a mediator with privileged contextual access to the SEKAI but **not** an omniscient reader of Mafuyu's current mind.

These are not one-scene moods. They change what later behavior is plausible.

# 3. Causal architecture

The event is best read in five movements rather than as eight isolated episodes.

## 3.1 Movement A — Post-main promise under ordinary production pressure (`0002:01`)

The event begins by refusing to let the main-story resolution become abstract reassurance. Kanade is already composing again and immediately measures a new demo against the promise she made to Mafuyu. Her private question remains rescue-binary in form: can this song really save Snow?

At 25:00 the group resumes work. Mizuki and Ena return easily to ordinary banter. Mafuyu is present but quiet. Kanade specifically asks Mafuyu how the demo feels.

Mafuyu's answer is crucial because it is neither complete numbness nor ordinary preference:

- she recognizes it as Kanade's music;
- she says she probably does not dislike it;
- when Mizuki proposes that this means she likes it, Mafuyu rejects the inference;
- she reasons that if she liked it, she should experience the corresponding feeling, and she does not.

This is an **access/labeling problem**, not good evidence for a simple binary “has feeling / has no feeling.” Mafuyu can discriminate some relations—recognition, not-dislike, mismatch between a category and her experience—while remaining unable to make the positive first-person judgment “I like this.”

Ena reacts as though Mafuyu's flat honesty is insulting Kanade. Kanade accepts the answer and thanks Mafuyu for being direct. This establishes the event's first interactional conflict: **truthful first-person reporting may sound socially worse than the old Snow role, but it is more useful for the search.**

After Mafuyu logs off, Ena makes explicit that intellectual knowledge of Mafuyu's crisis has not taught her how to deal with the present Mafuyu. Mizuki counters that Mafuyu is at least showing more of herself than before. That distinction becomes the social premise of the event: the group must learn to tolerate an inconveniently under-socialized form of honesty if they want access to anything more authentic than the “good girl” presentation.

Kanade takes another lesson from the exchange. A song cannot be tailored to Mafuyu's rescue if Kanade does not know what Mafuyu feels. She decides that she needs to know Mafuyu more deeply.

## 3.2 Movement B — Knowing another person without claiming ownership (`0002:02`–`0002:03`)

Kanade asks Honami a generalized question: how does one help a person who does not know what kind of help they need?

Honami's answer is modest. She suggests learning more about the person and listening, while also noting that some people dislike intrusive questioning. This is important because EVENT_0002 immediately tests both halves of that advice.

The doll-exhibition ticket creates an opportunity for N25 to leave the usual online production environment and encounter Mafuyu in an unfamiliar aesthetic space. The plan also produces another example of soft external authorship at home. Mafuyu's mother does not simply forbid the exhibition. She proposes that paintings might be more educational and better for cultivating sensibility, then allows the outing. The mechanism remains consistent with the main-story foundation: preference is not always crushed through explicit command; it is redirected through an already-legible hierarchy of sensible/value-adding choices.

Mafuyu privately assumes the distinction is meaningless because she expects to feel nothing either way.

At the exhibition the others engage naturally with the dolls. Kanade remarks that each doll seems to possess its own world. Mafuyu shows a small reaction before encountering a marionette that triggers a much stronger embodied response. The marionette is paired with memory of the “good girl” identity and produces physical distress, disgust, and panic.

Kanade immediately recognizes the reaction as valuable evidence and begins asking what Mafuyu feels. Her inference is understandable but the method becomes too intrusive while Mafuyu is visibly unwell. Mizuki tells Kanade to stop. Kanade does so and apologizes.

This is a significant Kanade reconstruction point. Her rescue compulsion can make her **information-hungry under crisis**: if a reaction looks like a clue, she may pursue it beyond the other person's immediate comfort. But she is not boundary-incapable. Direct social correction from a trusted person can make her stop and acknowledge that she pushed too far.

## 3.3 Movement C — Marionette as self-analogy and the limits of privileged mediation (`0002:04`–`0002:06`)

The marionette becomes an external image through which Mafuyu can approach a self-description she cannot yet state directly.

A dream makes the analogy explicit from Mafuyu's side. She imagines herself unable to move or speak except through external control. The frightening feature is also tempting: a doll does not have to think, search, choose, or risk failure. If it simply moves as instructed and makes everyone happy, the burden of first-person authority disappears.

This deepens the main-story account of the “good girl” structure. External authorship is painful not only because it suppresses preference. It can also be **relieving**, because choice itself has become exhausting and dangerous. A model that treats Mafuyu as straightforwardly wanting autonomy at every moment would therefore be too simple.

Kanade visits the Empty SEKAI seeking information. N25 Miku cannot simply tell her what Mafuyu currently feels. This establishes an important epistemic rule:

> **The SEKAI is generated from Mafuyu's feelings, but neither the SEKAI nor its Miku functions as a transparent mind-reading database.**

Miku can observe, infer, notice symbolic structures, and sometimes possess unusual contextual knowledge. She still distinguishes uncertainty from knowledge.

When the marionette appears in the SEKAI, Miku says it was not newly created by the exhibition. It was there from the beginning; Mafuyu had not been able or willing to see it. The analytic value of this should be preserved without turning the SEKAI into deterministic symbolism. The marionette is source-supported as a representation that becomes newly available to awareness.

The cut strings are especially important. A marionette without strings no longer functions as a marionette—but it is also free. Miku has repurposed the string for cat's cradle, a practice Mizuki taught her. The same material can therefore signify:

- external control;
- constraint;
- connection;
- play;
- collaborative pattern-making.

The symbol does not say “all bonds are strings, therefore all bonds are control.” It creates a contrast between **strings that move the self from outside** and **connections that can be voluntarily reconfigured**.

## 3.4 Movement D — Affect before explanation (`0002:06`–`0002:07`)

After the dream Mafuyu tells Kanade that ordinary life often feels like someone else's and that she knows something important is absent but cannot explain it.

Kanade offers a method rather than an answer: use lyrics.

This is one of the event's most important developments. Mafuyu is not required to produce a correct psychological theory before expression becomes valid. She can begin from the marionette image and the bodily reaction it provoked. The resulting lyrics are therefore **pre-interpretive expression**: affect is converted into form before Mafuyu can give a stable conceptual account of that affect.

Mafuyu later visits the Empty SEKAI on her own. That initiative matters. She tells Miku that disgust, pain, and similar feelings seem useless. Miku's response reframes the category: they are important because they are things Mafuyu is actually feeling. They belong to Mafuyu; therefore they are not nothing.

This produces the event's key first-person-authority rule:

> **The value of a feeling is not exhausted by whether it is pleasant or useful. Its occurrence can itself be evidence of the experiencing self.**

This does not romanticize suffering. Mafuyu explicitly does not want the painful reaction. The point is epistemic: even an unwanted feeling can carry information about what matters to her.

## 3.5 Movement E — Returned expression and a new group norm (`0002:08`)

Mafuyu's lyrics surprise the others because they feel more vivid and unlike the controlled Snow writing they know. Ena's irritated admiration is significant: she can dislike Mafuyu, resent her talent, and still register the work as compelling.

Kanade changes the production plan and composes directly to Mafuyu's lyrics.

When Mafuyu hears the finished song, she still cannot give a simple positive preference statement. Instead she reports a bodily state: her chest hurts. Crucially, she can distinguish this pain from the earlier pain in the SEKAI and the exhibition. It is not simply “bad.” The transformed work lets her encounter her own affect from a different angle.

Kanade interprets this as evidence that Mafuyu may not be incapable of feeling but may believe she is. That inference contains insight but must not be promoted into omniscient fact. The source continues to show:

- taste loss;
- uncertainty;
- derealized daily experience;
- inability to identify many preferences;
- difficulty converting bodily affect into categorical emotion.

The current authority should therefore be:

> **Mafuyu has meaningful affect that can be difficult to access, label, value, and integrate into self-concept; creative mediation can make some of that affect more available.**

Mizuki generalizes the practical lesson: when something is felt, Mafuyu can try putting it outside herself in whatever form works. Words are useful but not mandatory. More importantly, the group gives Mafuyu permission to do that **around them**.

The closing production scene tests whether this permission survives inconvenience. Mafuyu begins giving Ena and Mizuki blunt criticism:

- color feels dirty;
- a line looks warped;
- a video effect hurts her eyes;
- overall balance feels strange.

Ena gets angry. Mizuki jokes that this resembles rehabilitation and admits that criticism hurts when directed at them. Yet the session continues. Ena even revises artwork partly because Mafuyu failed to understand the intended emotional distinction, then insists that Mafuyu's understanding matters.

Kanade describes the scene as fun. When she asks Mafuyu, Mafuyu says she **might feel the same**, although she still does not understand properly.

That sentence is a better endpoint than a sudden declaration of happiness would have been. It combines:

- affective uncertainty;
- tentative positive identification;
- social participation;
- first-person ownership;
- preserved incompleteness.

# 4. Character-state analysis

## 4.1 Asahina Mafuyu — from provisional continuation to affect retrieval

### Pre-event inherited state

`MF-M3` / main-story endpoint:

- agrees to keep searching;
- wants to be found;
- retains severe preference-access difficulty;
- can initiate selectively with trusted people;
- can say “I don't know” rather than automatically produce the socially correct answer;
- remains vulnerable to disappearance under sufficient exhaustion.

### EVENT_0002 transition

EVENT_0002 does not give Mafuyu a coherent identity. It gives her a **method**.

The event establishes at least four affective layers:

1. **categorical absence/uncertainty** — she cannot say she likes Kanade's demo;
2. **embodied negative affect** — marionette disgust, pain, panic, chest pressure;
3. **relationally modulated affect** — Kanade's arrival makes distress feel slightly lighter; talking with Miku produces a non-painful feeling she wants to keep;
4. **creative-return affect** — hearing her lyrics as a finished song produces differentiated pain that is not equivalent to the earlier distress.

This makes “Mafuyu feels nothing” analytically untenable as a literal universal claim.

But “Mafuyu actually feels normally and only thinks she does not” is also too strong.

The event supports a model of **impaired affect access/integration**. Mafuyu can sometimes feel before she can name, evaluate, or recognize ownership of the feeling. Art, bodily sensation, and relational context can reveal distinctions that direct introspection cannot.

### New decision rule

Before EVENT_0002:

> If I cannot identify what I want or feel, there may be nothing there.

After EVENT_0002:

> If something hurts, disgusts, attracts, relieves, or presses on me, I can treat that reaction as possible evidence and try expressing it even before I understand it.

This is a genuine developmental change.

### Agency evidence

Mafuyu is not merely acted upon in this event. She:

- reports the dream to Kanade;
- attempts the lyric exercise;
- goes to the SEKAI alone;
- initiates a request to sing in Miku's card story;
- asks a spontaneous philosophical question about why feelings exist;
- interrogates Ena's claim that frustration is constitutive of Ena's identity;
- begins volunteering blunt creative reactions;
- tentatively identifies shared fun.

This strengthens the reconstruction rule that post-main Mafuyu has **selective but real initiative**, especially when a topic directly touches the search for self.

### Supplementary-card strengthening

`PJSK:card:0114` is particularly important. Mafuyu privately notices that distress becomes slightly lighter when she remembers Kanade immediately coming to her at the exhibition. She compares the resulting non-painful feeling with being with Miku and decides she wants to keep that feeling.

That is an early example of **affective preservation**: Mafuyu does not know exactly what the feeling is, but she can distinguish it from suffering and decide not to discard it.

The same card's later discussion with Ena gives Mafuyu another conceptual foothold. Ena says a self without frustration would be a different Shinonome Ena. Mafuyu does not immediately generalize this into a theory of selfhood, but she becomes interested in the idea that unwanted emotion can still be constitutive of identity.

### Current event-era state

Create `MF-E0002-01` as the current state within the EVENT_0002 analysis cutoff.

Core formulation:

> **Mafuyu remains unable to access a stable identity or many ordinary preferences, but she now possesses an explicit affect-retrieval practice: treat even unwanted/ambiguous reaction as self-data, externalize it, and examine what returns.**

## 4.2 Yoisaki Kanade — from rescue output to relational elicitation

Kanade's fundamental salvation structure survives the event.

She still:

- asks whether a song can save Mafuyu;
- interprets progress through rescue language;
- promises Honami she will definitely save her;
- repeats the vow to N25 Miku;
- skips food in order to finish work quickly.

So EVENT_0002 does **not** justify “Kanade learns boundaries” or “Kanade abandons the rescue complex.”

What changes is her **method**.

The main-story Kanade often responds to failed rescue by increasing output. EVENT_0002 teaches her that she cannot design a rescue artifact while treating Mafuyu as an unknown target. She needs access to what Mafuyu experiences.

This creates a new sequence:

> observe reaction → ask → listen → accept incomplete expression → translate expression musically → return it to Mafuyu → observe again.

That is substantially more relational than “compose harder until she is saved.”

### Boundary risk remains

The exhibition scene also reveals the danger of this method. Kanade can become so focused on a newly visible clue that she questions Mafuyu during active physical distress. Mizuki has to stop her.

Kanade apologizes immediately. This means:

- rescue urgency can override pacing judgment;
- Kanade is corrigible by trusted interpersonal feedback;
- boundary correction is not the same as internalized healthy boundaries.

### Honami as an advice / ordinary-care relationship

The associated Kanade card and linked area dialogue add an important secondary axis.

Honami:

- provides domestic support;
- offers a listening-oriented model of help;
- provides the exhibition ticket as a practical opportunity rather than trying to solve Mafuyu herself;
- encourages persistence based on her own experience of being reached by friends.

Kanade also shows ordinary relational curiosity around Honami: she notices tea aroma, asks to hear Honami's band recordings, helps shop, and attempts to identify foods she herself wants rather than defaulting entirely to “anything.”

This does not repair Kanade's self-neglect. The restaurant card immediately shows she can still go from the previous day without eating to finish an upload.

### Current event-era state

A modest new state `K-E0002-01` is warranted because Kanade's rescue decision procedure changes even though her deepest goal does not.

Core formulation:

> **Kanade remains rescue-compulsive and self-neglect-prone, but begins to treat the other person's partially expressed interiority—not merely her own musical output—as necessary input to intervention.**

## 4.3 Shinonome Ena — antagonism becomes productive feedback

EVENT_0002 does not require a new global Ena psychological state. Her main-story `E-M3` remains adequate for her broad self-structure:

- artistic recognition remains central;
- shame/anger/competitive effort remain active;
- Mafuyu still irritates her intensely;
- care continues to coexist with hostility.

The event strongly **refines the relationship behavior**.

Ena initially interprets Mafuyu's affectively flat critique as rude. But once N25 agrees that Mafuyu should express what she actually feels, Ena does something revealing: she uses Mafuyu's failure to understand intended nuances as a reason to improve the illustration.

She is angry precisely because Mafuyu's response matters.

In `PJSK:card:0118`, Ena revises the work after Mafuyu criticizes it, then insists that the revision has no point if Mafuyu still cannot understand the intended emotional distinctions. This transforms the Ena–Mafuyu dynamic from pure recognition injury into a potentially productive adversarial feedback channel.

The back half of the card also confirms that Ena is genuinely worried about the cost of probing Mafuyu. She fears another shock could intensify Mafuyu's wish to disappear and also hurt Kanade. She is not yet prepared to adopt Kanade's absolute commitment. This preserves her boundary-oriented skepticism.

No new global state ID is necessary yet; route the change primarily into the relationship and claim ledgers.

EVENT_0002 supplies enough evidence to initialize a narrow N25-Miku epistemic model without pretending the Virtual Singer is globally characterized.

That distinction is central to avoiding a false autonomy binary in later analysis.

# 5. Relationship-state analysis

## 5.1 Kanade ↔ Mafuyu — reciprocal continuation becomes affect collaboration

Inherited relationship: `REL-N25-KM-3` — reciprocal-continuation bond.

EVENT_0002 does not remove the dependency risk. It changes the form of interaction inside the promise.

### Before

Kanade: “I will keep making music until you are found.”

Mafuyu: permits the promise and uses Kanade's presence as a reason to continue searching.

### New relationship behavior

- Kanade asks Mafuyu for actual affective response rather than accepting socially appropriate praise.
- Mafuyu gives unpolished answers that can hurt Kanade.
- Kanade thanks her for directness.
- Kanade probes too hard at the exhibition, is corrected, and stops.
- Mafuyu later voluntarily tells Kanade about her dream and inability to explain ordinary life.
- Kanade proposes lyrics as a medium rather than supplying Mafuyu with an identity explanation.
- Mafuyu produces material; Kanade composes to it.
- the finished music returns Mafuyu's own affect to her in a new form.

This warrants `REL-N25-KM-4`:

> **reciprocal continuation + collaborative affect retrieval**.

The relationship remains asymmetrical—Kanade still assigns herself extraordinary rescue responsibility—but Mafuyu is increasingly a source of material and initiative rather than only the object of rescue.

## 5.2 Ena ↔ Mafuyu — from antagonistic attachment to adversarial creative exchange

Inherited: `REL-N25-EM-2` — antagonistic attachment.

New state: `REL-N25-EM-3` — **adversarial creative feedback**.

Ena's anger remains real. What changes is that Mafuyu's blunt reaction becomes creatively consequential.

Ena:

- defends Kanade against what she perceives as rude flatness;
- is furious when Mafuyu fails to understand her art;
- revises the work because Mafuyu failed to understand it;
- wants Mafuyu specifically to perceive the intended emotional distinctions;
- worries privately about exposing Mafuyu to further destabilizing experiences.

Mafuyu:

- names that Ena is particularly irritated with her;
- asks Ena serious questions about whether frustration is part of identity;
- thanks Ena in a halting, affectively uncertain way;
- continues giving honest critique despite Ena's anger.

Conflict is therefore functioning as communication rather than relationship negation.

## 5.3 Mizuki ↔ Mafuyu — structural empathy becomes chosen companionship

Inherited: `REL-N25-MZM-2` — structural-empathy endpoint.

New state: `REL-N25-MZM-3` — **chosen autonomy-conscious companionship**.

Mizuki does not tell Mafuyu “I know who you really are.” Mizuki instead:

- recognizes a structural similarity;
- protects Mafuyu from over-probing;
- creates opportunities to encounter art and possible preference;
- invites expression without requiring immediate explanation;
- says their involvement is something they personally want.

Mafuyu still does not naturally classify the relationship with conventional friendship language. Her question—why do Mizuki and Ena care if they are only members of the same creative circle?—shows that relational significance is still not obvious to her.

The relationship grows before Mafuyu has a stable category for it.

## 5.4 N25 group — candid affect becomes part of collaboration

Inherited: `REL-N25-G-2` — chosen embodied creative relationship.

New state: `REL-N25-G-3` — **candid-affect creative container**.

The group establishes a new norm:

> Mafuyu does not need to manufacture a socially acceptable reaction before speaking around N25.

The rule is not consequence-free. Ena gets angry; Mizuki's feelings can be hurt; criticism can be difficult. The important development is that the group can metabolize those costs without demanding a return to the old Snow performance.

This is a stronger form of relationship security than simple friendliness.

# 6. Epistemic-state analysis

EVENT_0002 produces unusually important knowledge-state changes.

## 6.1 What Mafuyu learns

Mafuyu gains direct or near-direct evidence that:

- she is not entirely empty; Kanade's song was present in her SEKAI from its beginning;
- unpleasant affect can belong to her even when she does not want it;
- a reaction can be worth retaining even when she cannot name it;
- artistic expression can contain her affect without her first understanding it;
- another person's prompt does not automatically make the resulting affect externally authored;
- Kanade, Ena, Mizuki, and Miku can respond to unfiltered expression without immediately abandoning her;
- relational presence can modulate pain even when she cannot label the resulting feeling.

She does **not** learn a complete causal theory of her family conditioning or a stable answer to who she is.

## 6.2 What Kanade learns

Kanade learns that:

- Mafuyu's flatness contains discriminable reactions;
- asking “what do you feel?” can reveal useful evidence but can also overwhelm Mafuyu;
- Miku/SEKAI cannot simply hand Kanade the answer to Mafuyu;
- Mafuyu herself may need to notice or express what others cannot directly find for her;
- lyrics can serve as a route to affect unavailable in direct conversation;
- receiving Mafuyu's expression is necessary input to composing for her.

Kanade does **not** gain omniscient access to Mafuyu's private history or feelings.

## 6.3 What Ena learns

Ena learns that:

# 8. Thematic and motif integration

## 8.1 First-person authority — cross-event validated

The main story asked whether Mafuyu could possess an internally authoritative self at all.

EVENT_0002 answers with a small but durable proposition:

> **ownership can begin before understanding.**

Mafuyu may not know what a feeling means. If it is genuinely occurring in her, it can still be treated as hers.

This is a direct cross-event continuation of `TH-N25-001`.

## 8.2 Saving / finding / reaching — cross-event validated

Kanade continues to use **saving** language.

Miku and the group increasingly operate through **finding** and **revealing**: the marionette was already present; Mafuyu had not seen it. The feeling already existed; it was not yet recognized. The lyrics make something available without completing the search.

The distinction between “creating a self for Mafuyu” and “helping Mafuyu notice something already hers” becomes even more important.

## 8.3 Creative work as psychological evidence — strongly cross-event validated

This is the strongest thematic confirmation in EVENT_0002.

The sequence is:

> embodied reaction → metaphor/image → lyrics → Kanade's composition → returned listening experience → new affect discrimination.

Creative work is therefore not merely symbolic decoration. It is an **iterative affect-retrieval mechanism**.

## 8.4 Marionette / string motif — event established

Create `MO-N25-003`:

**marionette / strings / cut strings / cat's cradle**.

Current formulation:

- marionette = externally moved self / relief from choice / controlled identity;
- cut string = loss of control mechanism and possibility of freedom;
- repurposed string = connection and voluntary pattern-making;
- interpretive caution: not every bond is a controlling string, and the event deliberately contrasts coercive control with chosen connection.

Status after EVENT_0002: `EVENT_ESTABLISHED`; future recurrence validation pending.

## 8.5 Relational mirroring versus external authorship — cross-event validated

Mafuyu's mother continues to offer normatively attractive alternatives that redirect choice through external criteria.

By contrast, Kanade and Miku increasingly say:

- here is a medium in which you might express it;

Even then, Kanade sometimes edges toward imposing a rescue interpretation, so the distinction remains contested rather than solved.

## 8.6 Conflict tolerance — cross-event validated

The event provides the first post-main test that N25 can survive **ordinary bluntness**, not just existential confrontation.

Mafuyu's criticism hurts. Ena yells. Mizuki complains. Nobody demands that Mafuyu resume the old Snow role to preserve harmony.

That is a significant group-development marker.

## 8.7 Curse / obligation / dependency — cross-event validated with warning

Kanade's promise persists and her self-neglect persists. The new relational method is healthier in some respects than unilateral rescue-through-output, but the underlying responsibility inflation has not disappeared.

The event therefore supports both:

- greater responsiveness to Mafuyu as a subject;
- continued risk that Kanade's self-worth remains tied to Mafuyu's rescue.
