# Project SEKAI — EVENT_0063 Deep Reading: みんなでエンジョイ！スポジョイパーク


## 1. Authority and promotion result


`EVENT_0063 — みんなでエンジョイ！スポジョイパーク` is promoted to a full deep reading because it does more than add ordinary-life material for Kanade. It turns an earlier *recognition*—that Kanade can experience non-instrumental social pleasure and may have lost ordinary parts of life—into a tentative **self-permission rule**.


The event therefore advances:


> **`K-E0026-01 -> K-E0063-01 — reciprocal self-permission / bounded non-instrumental living`**


The phrase is deliberately bounded. Kanade does **not** abandon the rescue mission, stop believing she must save Mafuyu and others, resolve father-guilt, or acquire healthy self-care. Instead, she becomes capable of recognizing that people who care about her may want *her* to smile, and of using that reciprocal desire as permission to occasionally choose enjoyment for herself.


**Classification:** Tier A — Developmental; **I3**; **R3**.

## 3. Entering state


After EVENT_0061 the N25 tuple is:


`MF-E0061-01 / K-E0026-01 / E-E0053-01 / MZ-E0039-01`.


Kanade's global state remains `K-E0026-01 — positive-destination rescue / persistent-penance`. However, EVENT_0042 already added a major refinement:


- Kanade can enjoy social contact without rescue utility;
- she can want future contact because it was fun;
- she can recover memories of ordinary childhood friendship;
- she can notice that mission-narrowing may have cost her ordinary life.


The unresolved question entering EVENT_0063 is whether that recognition can alter Kanade's decision rule when pleasure conflicts with `I should be using this time to save people`.


## 4. Physical maintenance begins as pure instrumentality


The event opens with Kanade's body becoming difficult to ignore. Carrying clothes to her father's hospital in summer heat leaves her dizzy enough that Minori intervenes and carries the load. Kanade accepts because she is no longer confident she can reach the hospital alone.


Back at 25:00, the problem is immediately translated into mission language:


- physical heaviness might interfere with composition;
- loss of composition capacity would interfere with saving people;
- burdening others is undesirable;
- therefore exercise becomes necessary maintenance.


This is not yet self-care for Kanade's own sake. It is **instrumental body maintenance in service of rescue productivity**.


Ena recognizes the risk immediately. She agrees to exercise with Kanade partly because leaving Kanade to regulate her own exertion is unsafe.


## 5. Walking: Kanade can overwork at self-care


The outdoor walking attempt is a useful reconstruction warning. Kanade tires after a distance that is modest for the others, is startled by a cicada into falling, and eventually says her legs are barely moving.


Ena proposes stopping. N25 Luka, however, provocatively reminds Kanade that improved stamina could affect future composition. This framing activates Kanade's pathology: because the activity has now been defined as necessary for music, Kanade becomes reluctant to stop even when stopping is physically sensible.


The pattern is:


`health activity -> linked to rescue/composition -> activity becomes obligation -> Kanade risks overdoing the very behavior intended to preserve health`.


This is important negative reconstruction evidence. Kanade's poor self-care cannot be repaired merely by giving her a healthy task. If the healthy task becomes part of the rescue mission, the same compulsive overfunctioning can colonize it.


## 6. Cross-unit ordinary sociality and register flexibility


Minori redirects the group to the indoor Sports Joy Park, where Ichika joins.


The resulting mixed group supplies unusually rich ordinary-social evidence:


- Ichika praises Ena's N25 artwork directly, and Ena accepts the praise with modest but real pride;
- Minori mistakenly assumes Kanade is the same age because she looks small/cute;
- Kanade is not offended and explicitly permits whichever name/register Minori finds comfortable;
- Ena similarly rejects unnecessary age hierarchy in this casual context;
- Ichika remains more comfortable with respectful speech, and Kanade tells her to use the form that feels natural.


Kanade is therefore not generically rigid or hierarchy-sensitive. Her low-temperature social style is permissive and low-demand.


## 7. The first desire signal: watching other people enjoy themselves


At the sports facility Kanade sees children and teenagers playing, competing, and laughing. She notices a vague chest-level `moya` before she can identify its meaning.


Luka offers the obvious invitation—why not join the others? Kanade initially answers through utility: she does not decorate her room with plush toys, so there is no reason to play the crane game.


This is a familiar Kanade cognitive filter: an activity without instrumental purpose is difficult to justify. The event begins to loosen that filter by allowing the vague affective signal to persist rather than treating lack of utility as the final answer.


## 8. Roller skating: remembered childhood activity, dependence, and teachability


Kanade selects roller skating because she remembers doing it as a child. Her physical skill is extremely poor: shaking legs, wall dependence, falls, and panic when uncontrolled speed develops.


Minori becomes a concrete coach rather than merely a cheerleader. She teaches stance, knee bend, body position, and incremental movement. Kanade responds well to procedural instruction and explicitly feels safer because a coach is present.


The scene also retrieves a father-memory: being taught physical skills as a child was once part of ordinary family life.


This matters for reconstruction because Kanade's ordinary past is not empty. Her present narrowness is partly a **loss/constriction of once-available life**, not proof that she never possessed recreational preferences.


## 9. Bubble sumo: low-stakes agency under anticipated incompetence


Kanade enters the team game with a predictive self-model:


- she probably cannot contribute;
- the realistic goal is not to burden Minori.


But Kanade is observant. When Ena and Ichika redirect attention toward Minori, Kanade recognizes an opening and initiates a lateral hit on Ena. The team wins because she acted on the opportunity.


This is useful D0/D1 reconstruction evidence: Kanade's low physical confidence does not imply passivity when a simple tactical opportunity becomes legible. She can act decisively once the problem is cognitively tractable.


Her satisfaction is similarly bounded: she is pleased that she actually helped rather than merely being protected.


## 10. `楽しい` followed by guilt


Core `0063:06` is the decisive internal sequence.


Kanade is exhausted and anticipates muscle pain, but nevertheless recognizes plainly:


> `……楽しいな`


She also retrieves more childhood leisure memory with her father.


Then the rescue system reasserts itself:


- she came here to build stamina for composition;
- she has to write songs to save Mafuyu, her father, and suffering people;
- therefore simply enjoying herself feels like a misuse of time.


This is stronger than EVENT_0042 because the conflict is now explicit. Kanade does not merely discover pleasure; she discovers that **pleasure triggers guilt because it competes with moralized rescue productivity**.


## 11. Luka's intervention: own feelings become a legitimate object


Luka asks why enjoyment would be wrong and tells Kanade to value her own feelings sometimes.


Kanade initially struggles with the proposition because her own enjoyment has no rescue justification. Minori then independently reaches the same problem from ordinary human care: Kanade looks strained, she has an important goal, and Minori wants to help her keep moving—but also wants Kanade to smile *now*.


Kanade is struck by the asymmetry because `I want Mafuyu to smile` is central to her own rescue model. Minori effectively reverses the direction:


> people who care about Kanade may also want Kanade's happiness.


This does not dissolve obligation. It creates a **reciprocal permission route** through which self-directed positive affect can become morally legible to Kanade.


## 12. A self-authored recreational choice


When asked what she wants to do next, Kanade chooses strike-out/target throwing because she remembers enjoying it with her father.


Her motive is unusually explicit:


> `すごく楽しかったから、またやってみたい`


and then:


> `せっかくみんなでやるんだし……、わたしも、楽しもう`


This is a meaningful threshold. The activity is not selected because it improves composition, helps Mafuyu, supplies creative research, or repays another person. Kanade selects it because **she remembers liking it and wants to experience it again with the group**.


The father's old instruction about throwing rhythm becomes useful in the game, but that later instrumental crossover does not erase the motive that selected the activity.


## 13. Group enjoyment is not a performance metric


The strike-out sequence also changes the evaluative environment. The group explicitly tells Kanade that the prize is secondary and enjoying the game is what matters.


Kanade's final successful throw earns the bingo prize, but the meaningful outcome is not competitive competence. Ena observes that Kanade is smiling in a way she has not seen before and feels happy because of it.


This gives direct evidence for the reciprocal premise Minori proposed: Kanade's ordinary happiness itself can be valuable to people who care about her.


## 14. Endpoint: tentative self-permission and future continuity


At the end of the outing Kanade:


- says the day was genuinely fun;
- accepts a sustainable rather than maximal exercise model;
- credits Minori's support with helping her continue;
- agrees that she would like to gather again occasionally;
- does so for a non-composition activity;
- notes that she spent a long stretch not thinking about music;
- tells Luka she wants to try valuing her own feelings a little more.


The exact wording remains cautious:


> `もう少しだけ——自分の気持ちを大事にしようと思う`


and immediately:


> `ちゃんとできるのか、わからないけど`


This caution is why the state is `bounded non-instrumental living`, not recovery from rescue pathology.

Kanade's practical vulnerability to simplistic efficiency claims should be retained: when a tool promises to preserve output with minimal time cost, she is susceptible because efficiency is deeply attractive to her mission-oriented life.

## 17. N25 Luka: provocative autonomy catalyst, not a neutral comforter


Card `0461` clarifies Luka's role.

Her intervention style differs from Miku/MEIKO:

- she sometimes uses the target's existing motivation strategically (composition benefits) to keep them engaged;

The card's Empty SEKAI tag game gives dense low-stakes evidence: Luka becomes `it` partly because she wants to observe deception/game dynamics, catches people too quickly, then admits she ruined the interesting part by optimizing too hard.


This is not omniscience. Luka is an experimenter/provocateur who forms hypotheses about human change.
