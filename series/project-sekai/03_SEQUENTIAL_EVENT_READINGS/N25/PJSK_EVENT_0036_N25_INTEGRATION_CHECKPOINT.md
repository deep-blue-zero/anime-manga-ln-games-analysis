## 4. Mafuyu: public praise, private no-response, and EVENT_0035 persistence

The source later gives direct private counterevidence: when Kanade asks whether any Fan Festa music affected her, Mafuyu says there was no particular response; in `0294:02` she again says she felt nothing.
