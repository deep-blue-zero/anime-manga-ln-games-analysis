## 2. Central analytical thesis


The event puts two propositions side by side:


1. **Mizuki can move toward a wanted life while still being afraid.**
2. **The fact that a guarded problem remains undisclosed does not prove that nothing else is changing.**


The historical Mizuki who joins Kanade and Ena is not fearless, newly cured of school-based rejection, or suddenly trusting. Mizuki wants the collaboration and wants the people, anticipates that being seen as strange may again make a desired relationship disappear, and chooses a small approach anyway after their sister distinguishes fear from having given up.


The present-day Mizuki then looks through accumulated ordinary-life photographs and reaches a first-person conclusion: they are actually enjoying the life that grew from that decision.


The event's strongest causal sequence is therefore:


`identity-sensitive rejection history -> withdrawal / reduced school investment -> preserved agency through fandom and video creation -> unexpectedly resonant creative encounter -> verified low-risk collaboration -> desire for continued contact + fear of attachment loss -> sister authorizes movement toward desired/comfortable place without requiring fear to vanish -> tentative approach -> N25 belonging accumulates -> later disclosure risk increases precisely because belonging became real`


This history sharpens rather than contradicts EVENT_0039.

## 4. Mizuki before N25: known self, hostile social legibility


### 4.1 School disengagement is not academic incapacity


The flashback and linked area reinforce that Mizuki is capable of learning quickly. Attendance and assignment behavior are the problem, not generalized cognitive weakness. School is aversive because it repeatedly becomes a site where difference is scrutinized, marked, or translated into pressure to learn how to behave acceptably around everyone else.


The present area `night_10_002` keeps this distinction alive: Mizuki can keep attendance and test performance within workable bounds yet still be called in over unsubmitted worksheets and other obligations. Future reconstruction should separate:


- academic comprehension;
- bureaucratic compliance;
- attendance;
- identity threat;
- social appetite.


They are not one variable.


### 4.2 Fandom/video creation preserves agency


As school investment falls, Mizuki does not become globally inert. Anime, character attachment, editing, and online creative culture remain active domains of pleasure and competence. The `Amia` handle grows from a beloved character rather than being a random alias, giving the later editor identity an autobiographical fandom root.


The ending of a favorite anime creates a recognizable void, but the response is again active search rather than total collapse: Mizuki looks for material that can produce a similar emotional charge and discovers K's song and Ena's illustration.


## 5. Ena before N25: blocked art and alternate recognition


The event pairs Mizuki's coping structure with Ena's without claiming they are the same.


Ena remains desperate to draw yet wounded by comparison, criticism, and uncertainty over whether her effort can produce the ability she wants. Social-media recognition based on appearance offers a lower-friction feedback loop: it produces visible approval more reliably than art at this stage.


This does not mean the selfie account proves Ena never cared about drawing. Its analytical function is almost the opposite. An easier recognition channel cannot extinguish the blocked creative need. K's song reactivates the desire to draw, and the resulting image becomes the surface Mizuki encounters.


Thus the two future N25 members approach the same creative object from different compromised self-efficacy structures:


- Mizuki: `socially punished difference -> school withdrawal -> active fandom/video agency`;
- Ena: `creative recognition wound -> art blockage -> easier appearance-based recognition -> unresolved need to draw`.


Their connection is not “two lonely people meet.” It is two different self-preservation systems becoming mutually useful through creation.


## 6. Mizuki's first MV: creative absorption plus procedural self-correction


Mizuki is strongly affected by the relation between K's music and Ena's image: painful and gentle qualities seem to coexist. The response is immediate enough that Mizuki begins constructing an MV around the art.

Reconstruction rule:


`high creative salience -> rapid assembly / experimentation -> recognize ownership/permission boundary -> seek consent before public use`.


This is valuable evidence for Mizuki's practical digital ethics and should coexist with the character's playful spontaneity.

## 8. Creative disagreement becomes a new social possibility


The first collaboration matters because Mizuki and Ena do not initially imagine the visual solution identically. For Mizuki, this activates an old inhibition: expressing a different preference has repeatedly risked having the preference itself treated as strange.


Yet the collaborative environment rewards articulating the difference. The mismatch can be discussed, tested against the song, and used to improve the shared object.


This creates one of the deepest origin links between N25's creative structure and Mizuki's later relational safety:


> **difference does not have to end belonging; in a sufficiently safe creative container, difference can become useful information.**


This does not mean N25 is universally safe or that identity-sensitive disclosure becomes easy. It explains why creation can become a privileged zone of negotiated self-expression before broader personal disclosure is possible.


## 9. Kanade's invitation: being needed plus being personally wanted

## 10. Sister relationship: permission to move while afraid


Mizuki's older sister is not merely a supportive relative inserted to reassure them. The event gives the relationship a reciprocal creative structure.


### 10.1 The sister notices surrender, not just sadness


Her retrospective diagnostic distinction is precise. At Mizuki's lowest point, the concerning sign was not only fear or unhappiness. Mizuki had stopped wearing a favorite ribbon and carried the presentation of someone for whom everything had become irrelevant.


Now Mizuki is frightened and conflicted, but **still actively struggling with a choice**. The sister treats that as evidence of movement rather than failure.


The distinction is:


`fear + deliberation` is not the same state as `fear + surrender`.


### 10.2 “Go where you want to go” does not erase risk


The sister does not promise that the new people will understand Mizuki, nor does she claim the fear is irrational. She authorizes Mizuki to choose a place that feels wanted/comfortable even before safety can be guaranteed.


Her advice works because it preserves first-person authority:


> Mizuki may decide that wanting the place is sufficient reason to try approaching it.


The endpoint `まだ怖い……怖いけど` must remain in the model. Courage here is behavior under persistent threat expectation, not successful cognitive elimination of fear.


### 10.3 Support is reciprocal


The sister also reveals that her own fashion dream repeatedly approached abandonment. Mizuki's delight in her clothes and ribbons helped her continue. This revises any one-way “older sister saves Mizuki” reading.


Their relationship is better modeled as:


`older sister validates Mizuki's self-authored presentation and movement toward desired belonging <-> Mizuki's delight in sister's designs validates sister's self-authored creative vocation`.


Distance abroad does not erase the relation. Clothing, ribbon, sweets, photographs, and letters maintain it materially.
