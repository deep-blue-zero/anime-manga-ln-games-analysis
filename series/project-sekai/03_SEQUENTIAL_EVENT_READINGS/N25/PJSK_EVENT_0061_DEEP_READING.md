# Project SEKAI — EVENT_0061 Deep Reading: 迷い子の手を引く、そのさきは


## 1. Authority and analytical role


`EVENT_0061 — 迷い子の手を引く、そのさきは` is a major Mafuyu developmental release and the first event after `EVENT_0042 — 交わる旋律 灯るぬくもり` that requires a successor global Mafuyu state.


The release does not simply provide another example of Mafuyu feeling relational warmth. `MF-E0042-01 — relational warmth / contribution-linked self-evidence` established that positive affect could arise from another person's happiness, from Mafuyu's own contribution to that happiness, and from low-stakes relational follow-through. EVENT_0061 makes that affect **behaviorally consequential under direct conflict with externally authored obligation**. Mafuyu's body and actions diverge from the path she continues to describe as correct before she can explain the divergence in words.


The resulting successor state is:


> **`MF-E0061-01 — embodied autonomy divergence / chosen relational refuge`**


This state is deliberately narrower than `rebellion`, `independence`, or `family rejection`. Mafuyu still loves and values her mother, still recognizes genuine maternal concern, still believes much of her mother's future-oriented reasoning is intended for her benefit, and still lacks a stable self-authored future plan. What changes is that she can no longer treat externally prescribed correctness as behaviorally total. Her body, her spontaneous choices, and her experience of relational safety can pull her elsewhere even while conscious explanation lags behind.


**Final classification:** Tier A — Developmental; **I3 — state-changing**; **R3 reconstruction yield**.

## 3. Prospective boundary entering EVENT_0061


The pre-event human-state tuple is frozen as:


`MF-E0042-01 / K-E0026-01 / E-E0053-01 / MZ-E0039-01`.


The relevant pre-event model says:


- Mafuyu can use relational warmth as first-person evidence without naming it categorically;
- she can act on small promises and investigate positive affect;
- public competence remains high while private self-knowledge remains incomplete;
- family autonomy conflict is unresolved;
- Kanade-Mafuyu is current as `REL-N25-KM-7 — reciprocal positive-affect recognition / bidirectional ordinary support`, still carrying rescue asymmetry;
- N25 human/manifestation sociality is current through `REL-N25-GM-E0054`.


The question for EVENT_0061 is therefore not whether Mafuyu can feel warmth. It is whether that warmth has become strong enough to alter choice under serious competing obligation.


## 4. The opening pressure system: externally authored success still functions normally


`0061:01` begins by showing that the old system has not vanished. Mafuyu attends cram school, notices the accelerated pace before a university-specific mock exam, and immediately frames the task through performance obligation: she needs a good score, with the mother-child conversation concretizing Meiyo/Minato university A-rank expectations.


The mother's language remains caring on the surface and is not reducible to overt cruelty. She tells Mafuyu not to overdo it and says she expects Mafuyu will be fine. But the future itself is already narrated for Mafuyu: the prestigious-university path is treated as the obvious destination, while Mafuyu responds with the polished agreement expected of the `good child` role.


This matters because EVENT_0061 does not create autonomy by introducing a newly hostile mother. It creates autonomy conflict inside a relationship that Mafuyu still experiences as important, caring, and morally binding.


## 5. The synthesizer: concealed self-authorship becomes materially vulnerable


`0061:02` turns the latent family conflict into an object-level crisis. Mafuyu's mother enters Mafuyu's room while she is absent, finds the synthesizer, and asks whether Mafuyu has been hiding something.


The synthesizer carries two temporal layers:


1. **past attempted disposal** — Mafuyu's mother previously treated it as an unused obstacle and arranged for removal;
2. **Mafuyu's concealed counter-action** — before collection, Mafuyu told the worker it had been discarded by mistake and reclaimed it because she still used it.


This earlier action is extremely diagnostic. Long before EVENT_0061's overt divergence, Mafuyu had already been capable of a quiet, tactical act against maternal instruction when something personally necessary was about to be erased. Yet she did not narrate that act as independence. She hid it, preserving the good-child surface while protecting a private route to music.


The current discovery collapses that compartmentalization. Music is no longer safely concealed from the family-authority system.


## 6. N25 learns the family/music conflict: epistemic topology changes


In `0061:03`, the group meets the newly manifested N25 Len, but Mafuyu's distress rapidly becomes visible. Ena refuses the `nothing happened` answer and asks Mafuyu to put something into words.


Mafuyu then tells Kanade and Ena, in addition to Mizuki, that:


- her mother found the synthesizer;
- her mother wants her to prioritize study;
- music is not an accepted part of the expected path;
- the synthesizer had nearly been disposed of before;
- Mafuyu secretly recovered it;
- she expects the current confiscation may again become disposal.


This is a meaningful epistemic transition. Earlier N25 knowledge of family pressure was partial, inferred, or unevenly distributed. EVENT_0061 gives the human group a concrete mechanism connecting family authority to Mafuyu's music and private selfhood.


The guardrail is equally important: the group still does **not** receive every audience-known childhood memory, every private maternal interaction, or full causal certainty about the mother's motives. Mizuki has comparatively strong contextual intuition; Ena and Kanade learn the concrete present conflict. None becomes omniscient.


## 7. `If you really thought she was right, you wouldn't be here`


`0061:04` provides the event's first explicit contradiction test.


Mafuyu repeatedly says her mother is thinking about her future and is `right`. Ena initially reacts in her own confrontational family register, but then identifies a sharper contradiction: if Mafuyu truly endorsed the mother's prescription without remainder, she would be studying rather than coming to the SEKAI.


The event does **not** allow Ena to author Mafuyu's conclusion. Mafuyu answers:


> `私は—— …………わからない……`


This `わからない` is not empty stasis. It marks a split between declarative belief and enacted behavior. Mafuyu cannot yet say `I want music more`, `Mother is wrong`, or `I choose N25`, but her repeated return to SEKAI already falsifies total internal agreement with the prescribed path.


Mizuki performs an important corrective role by reminding Ena that Mafuyu's mother is genuinely important to Mafuyu. The event therefore refuses both simplifications:


- `mother cares, therefore the control is harmless`;
- `mother controls, therefore Mafuyu's attachment is false or irrational`.


Kanade similarly frames her concern relationally rather than ideologically: she understands wanting to value family, but does not want Mafuyu to keep forcing herself when she is visibly suffering.


## 8. The mock exam: autonomy emerges first as embodied refusal


`0061:05` is the decisive state-transition chapter.


Mafuyu leaves home intending to take the university-specific mock exam. She knows the route, the expected score, and the importance assigned to the result. Yet the closer she gets to the venue, the more her body resists:


- her legs feel heavy;
- the walk feels abnormally long;
- her chest feels cold;
- her insides feel as if they are turning;
- breathing becomes difficult;
- a supportive message from her mother intensifies rather than relieves the distress.


The crucial analytical point is that Mafuyu does not yet possess a fluent psychological explanation. She continues to repeat the obligation: her mother is looking forward to the result, so she must respond to that expectation.


Then Mizuki's message arrives: the others are at Phoenix Wonderland, and Mafuyu can join if she finishes early.


Mafuyu does **not** finish early.


She goes to Phoenix Wonderland **instead of taking the mock exam**.


This is not casual truancy. It is the first major post-main event in which Mafuyu's unarticulated selfhood produces a concrete choice against a high-salience externally authored obligation. She reaches the park and asks herself why she came. She knows she could turn back. She still does not turn back.


That makes EVENT_0061 qualitatively different from EVENT_0042. Relational warmth has crossed from evidence into behavioral selection.


## 9. Phoenix Wonderland as a mixed-valence site, not a simple escape fantasy


The park is analytically effective precisely because it belongs to **both** sides of Mafuyu's emotional history.


In the present, N25 offers a low-demand social field. Ena explicitly says Mafuyu does not need to force the usual public performance while they are there. Kanade repeatedly checks whether she needs rest. Mizuki tries to provide ordinary fun without demanding an emotional result.


But Phoenix Wonderland is also a genuine childhood memory with Mafuyu's mother.


`0061:07` shows young Mafuyu:


- excited by the lights and smiling people;
- choosing between rides;
- wanting to meet Fenny;
- accepting a churro from her mother;
- genuinely enjoying the outing;
- deciding, against instruction, to leave briefly because she wants to shake Fenny's hand.


This memory matters enormously. Mafuyu's mother is part of a real history of warmth and care. The current family conflict cannot be reconstructed by deleting that history.


At the same time, young Mafuyu's small unauthorized pursuit of Fenny is an early example of first-person desire producing behavior before adult self-erasure became dominant. The event places childhood micro-autonomy beside present mock-exam refusal without claiming they are psychologically identical.


## 10. Lost-child structure: fear, finding, and relational orientation


N25 Len is introduced as a manifestation who initially wanders the Empty SEKAI alone. Card `0447:01` makes the experience explicit: the space appears endless, featureless, and empty; Len fears that he may remain alone forever. Miku eventually finds him, takes his hand because he appears cold, and tells him he is no longer alone.


Len later becomes frightened when separated from the others at Phoenix Wonderland because the situation recalls that first isolation.


His experience prompts Mafuyu's childhood lost-child memory. The parallel is structurally strong but must remain bounded:


- Len is not `Mafuyu as a child` in literal identity;
- his fear is not authoritative interpretation of Mafuyu;
- the Empty SEKAI is not merely a trauma allegory.


What Len provides is a legible emotional relation that Mafuyu can compare against her own more difficult-to-name state: being alone can feel cold; being found can feel warm.


## 11. `Here is warm`: relational safety becomes first-person context knowledge


`0061:08` produces the clearest new first-person formulation.


After Kanade, Ena, and Mizuki find Mafuyu again, Kanade takes Mafuyu's hand to keep her from falling. Mafuyu notices that Kanade's hand is physically a little cold, but nevertheless **feels warm**. She immediately broadens the observation:


- Kanade's presence is comfortable;
- what Ena gives her is comfortable;
- what Mizuki gives her is comfortable;
- the coldness inside her has receded.


Len supplies a hypothesis from his own experience: perhaps Mafuyu feels warmer because she is with everyone.


Mafuyu's resulting statement is deliberately contextual rather than categorical:


> **`“ここ”は……あったかいんだ`**


She does not say `I love them`, `these are my true friends`, or `my mother makes me cold`. She can now identify a **relational environment** as warm and relieving even though the underlying emotional category remains incomplete.


This is an important epistemic gain: context itself becomes usable self-knowledge.


## 12. Curfew choice: a second autonomous divergence in the same day


The mock-exam refusal could be dismissed as acute distress alone if the event ended there. It does not.


When the group discusses the night show, Mafuyu remembers that staying will make her late for curfew. She understands the consequence. She then says she came all this way and wants to watch a little before returning.


Mafuyu card `0445:01` strengthens the decision further. Asked again whether she should leave, she states:


> `……大丈夫。最後まで、見ていく`


The autonomy pattern therefore repeats under lower immediate physiological pressure:


`external rule becomes salient -> Mafuyu notices competing relational/experiential value -> she knowingly chooses additional time with N25`.


This still does not establish generalized defiance. The significance lies in repetition: choice against prescription is no longer confined to one crisis moment.


## 13. Mother versus N25: differentiation begins without moral simplification


The event's endpoint asks a question that Mafuyu is not yet able to answer fully:


- her mother's concern, expectations, and past warmth are real;
- N25's concern, warmth, and acceptance are also real;
- yet the two forms of care do not feel the same.


Mafuyu begins asking what the difference is.


This is a major developmental advance because the earlier good-child structure tended to collapse `care`, `correctness`, and `obedience` into one system. EVENT_0061 starts separating them.


The event supports the following formulation:


> **Care can be genuine while still constraining self-authorship; relational safety can be genuine without giving the other person authority to define Mafuyu's future.**


It does **not** yet support:


- `Mafuyu consciously judges her mother abusive`;
- `Mafuyu wants to sever the relationship`;
- `N25 replaces family`;
- `Mafuyu has selected music as a settled career`;
- `Mafuyu understands exactly why the two care systems feel different`.


## 14. Post-event home state: distress persists after the autonomous act


Card `0445:02` prevents a triumphalist reading.


After skipping the exam and staying for the show:


- the synthesizer remains outside Mafuyu's control;
- her mother begins asking more detailed questions about school friends after Mafuyu explains the missed exam through a friend/rest cover story;
- Mafuyu sleeps poorly;
- the home/bed context feels cold;
- she does not understand why.


The autonomous act creates neither relief everywhere nor family resolution. In fact, greater divergence may increase internal conflict and vigilance.


This is why the successor state uses **autonomy divergence**, not `autonomy achieved`.


## 15. Empty SEKAI becomes a deliberately chosen soothing refuge


The same Mafuyu card supplies one of the most reconstruction-diagnostic low-temperature scenes in the event.


Unable to sleep, Mafuyu enters the Empty SEKAI. Miku offers Mizuki's cushion as a pillow and worries that Mafuyu lacks a blanket. Mafuyu refuses a search for bedding and instead asks:


> `ここに、いてほしい……。ミクも……レンも……`


She then confirms to herself:


> `やっぱり。ここは、冷たくない……`


This is a meaningful expansion of the Empty SEKAI's function.


Earlier releases had already disproved a crisis-only model and developed the SEKAI into a place of leisure, ritual, companionship, and shared material care. EVENT_0061 adds **chosen low-demand co-presence for physiological/affective settling and sleep**.


This should be modeled carefully. The SEKAI is not a magical cure, and Miku/Len are not therapists. What matters is that Mafuyu can now intentionally seek their presence because she knows, through bodily evidence, that the context is less cold and more tolerable.


## 16. Kanade-Mafuyu: `found again`, not a solved rescue relationship


Card `0449:01` produces powerful Kanade-Mafuyu language.


In the post-show crowd, Kanade almost gets separated and reflexively clings to Mafuyu because she is afraid of leaving **Mafuyu** alone again—even though Kanade is the person actually being swept away. This is a clean demonstration of the rescue schema's persistence and its tendency to distort Kanade's self-positioning.


Mafuyu then helps guide Kanade and says:


> `奏は、また見つけてくれたから`


Kanade promises:


> `わたしでよければ、何度でも見つけるからね`


This strongly **strengthens `REL-N25-KM-7`** but does not yet require a successor ID. The existing state already contains demonstrated reach, bidirectional ordinary support, and persistent rescue asymmetry. EVENT_0061 adds a durable `finding / being found` relational grammar and shows Mafuyu positively receiving it.


The guardrail remains necessary: Kanade's promise must not be treated as healthy symmetric dependency, romantic exclusivity, or proof that Kanade alone can stabilize Mafuyu.


## 17. Mafuyu continues lyric work under restricted access


Card `0449:02` prevents another possible overread: Mafuyu's autonomous divergence does not instantly produce open rebellion at home.


Her mother expresses genuine concern about Mafuyu's health after the missed exam and recommends earlier sleep / morning study. Mafuyu therefore expects she will not be able to join the 25:00 work session normally for a while.


But Mafuyu explicitly tells Kanade:


- she will continue writing lyrics;
- the scheduling restriction does not mean relinquishing the work;
- she is still waiting for Kanade's song that might save her.


This is developmentally important because it converts the earlier hidden-synth persistence into a current, consciously preserved creative line under family restriction.


Again, the mother scene is mixed-valence. Health concern is real. The broader autonomy system remains constraining. Both must be retained.


## 18. Ena's correction: strong judgment without stealing first-person authority


Ena card `0448` gives one of the clearest examples of Ena learning how to care for Mafuyu without simply projecting her own family strategy.


Ena says that if her own parent behaved the same way, she would fight. She then explicitly recognizes that this is **what Ena would do**, not what Mafuyu must do.


Her frustration centers on a first-person-authority problem: if Mafuyu wants something, nobody else can tell Mafuyu's mother what Mafuyu wants on Mafuyu's behalf. Ena recalls Mafuyu's main-story outburst positively because, however painful, those words unmistakably belonged to Mafuyu.


Kanade's group-level formulation follows:


> N25 can continue being a place where Mafuyu does not have to be the `good child`.


This is a strong group-role refinement. It does not mean N25 should pressure Mafuyu into anti-family disclosure. Their value lies partly in suspending the role that dominates elsewhere.


## 19. Mizuki: autonomy-sensitive help without forced interpretation


Mizuki remains consistent with `MZ-E0039-01` and the EVENT_0058 refinement.


They:


- recognize that Mafuyu's problem is not solved by simply declaring the mother wrong;
- moderate Ena when Ena's own conflict style becomes too directive;
- offer Phoenix Wonderland as an optional escape/input rather than an ultimatum;
- notice afterward that Mafuyu probably could not fully enjoy the trip and hope there will be another chance;
- tell Rui only bounded information necessary to answer Emu's concern, preserving N25 privacy.


Mizuki therefore acts as a strong reconstruction example of **autonomy-respecting social facilitation**: provide options, reduce pressure, notice context, do not demand the guarded interior.


No successor Mizuki state is warranted.


## 20. N25 Len: shy manifestation, lostness, and self-recognized excitement


EVENT_0061 establishes a new manifestation-specific model for N25 Len.


Baseline features:


- shy, hesitant speech;
- strong separation/loneliness sensitivity;
- initially hides behind Miku;
- seeks safety through a known person before exploring others;
- can ask direct questions once the environment feels safe;
- interprets warmth relationally;
- worries whether he deserves a welcome celebration;

Card `0447:02` is especially important. The humans and Virtual Singers recreate aspects of earlier shared gatherings so Len can learn the group's history. MEIKO articulates a principle that understanding someone requires knowing them. The group does not demand immediate openness from Len; they create repeated opportunities for participation.


Len's arrival therefore strengthens the broader manifested-companionship ecology already established by `REL-N25-GM-E0054`.
