# Project SEKAI - EVENT_0047 Deep Reading: いつか、絶望の底から

## 1. Purpose, authority, and why this event matters

EVENT_0047 is an origin story, but its analytical importance is not that it suddenly creates a new present-day Kanade or Mafuyu. The complete envelope instead reconstructs the causal history beneath states already established by the main story and later events. It answers several questions that the existing N25 model could previously describe only from retrospective testimony: what Kanade's rescue compulsion looked like before Snow entered her life; what Mafuyu's affective flattening looked like before she had a creative relationship with K; why K's music could matter before Mafuyu possessed a stable preference label; what Snow's first arrangement gave Kanade in return; how Honami entered Kanade's life; and what the earliest Kanade-Mafuyu collaboration actually meant to both participants.

The governing question is therefore different from a forward-state-changing event:

> What becomes causally and relationally clearer after EVENT_0047, and which present models survive that clarification?

The answer is that the current human-state tuple survives:

`MF-E0042-01 / K-E0026-01 / E-E0014-01 / MZ-E0039-01`.

That preservation is not a statement that EVENT_0047 is minor. It is evidence that the current states were already broad enough to contain the present-day behavior shown here. The event changes the confidence, provenance, and internal structure of those models rather than replacing them.

The most important revisions are:

1. Kanade's salvation command is directly shown as post-trauma self-worth regulation before Snow becomes her collaborator. Snow does not create the rescue compulsion.
2. Mafuyu's earliest self-directed search is triggered by painful, non-null affect from K's music before she can call the music a preference or explain why it matters.
3. Kanade and Mafuyu are reciprocal from the formation of their collaboration. Kanade gives Mafuyu affective movement and a possible route out of stasis; Mafuyu gives Kanade a unique depiction of despair that expands Kanade's creative possibilities and makes the prospect of reaching someone feel concrete.
4. Honami is not merely a later domestic support figure. She is the middle-school person who finds Kanade after physical collapse and enables emergency intervention, after which the household-support relationship begins.
5. Present-day N25 can now treat shared meals, after-work gatherings, teasing, practical concern, and partial autobiographical disclosure as ordinary. This normality is contrasted directly with the pre-N25 desire to disappear and the earlier comfort of anonymity.
6. Mafuyu can now say that meeting Kanade, making music, and meeting Ena, Mizuki, and Miku were, in aggregate, good for her, while still lacking broad ordinary preference access and retaining major sensory/self-definition limits.
7. N25 Miku explicitly frames the SEKAI as a continuity witness for once-born feelings even if access changes. That is an in-world ontology/relationship statement, not permission to reconstruct Miku as an omniscient memory database.

## 4. Temporal architecture: the event is a contrast machine

EVENT_0047 alternates between a present in which N25 ordinary life has become routine and a past in which both Kanade and Mafuyu are approaching different forms of self-erasure. The present does not erase the past; the past explains why the present counts as change.

At the present boundary, Kanade can eat food prepared by Honami, recognize that she is calmer than before, accept the possibility that trusted people contribute to that calm, enter the Empty SEKAI and group conversations without every scene being crisis-driven, and later notice that eating together at a restaurant has become ordinary. Mafuyu can participate in that normality, notice Kanade's expression, ask practical health questions, and use the SEKAI as a place to rest rather than only a crisis/search chamber.

The flashback then shows that the earlier versions of these behaviors were not available in the same form. Kanade converts guilt into an escalating duty to save increasingly many people, treats bodily needs as obstacles, and connects successful rescue to whether she has a right to live. Mafuyu can perform expected school emotion while privately finding no desired graduation gift, no obvious enjoyment, and no internally authorized future. The first thing that cuts through that flattening is not a pleasant preference. It is pain.

The event's structure therefore provides unusually strong longitudinal evidence because the contrast is source-authored rather than analytically manufactured.

## 5. Kanade before N25: aspiration already converted into command

### 5.1 Snow does not create the salvation project

The pre-contact Kanade sequence strongly strengthens `CR-N25-K-001` and `CR-N25-K-002`. She is already publishing music under a rescue logic before she knows Snow. Positive listener response briefly gives relief, but that relief immediately escalates into obligation: more people must be saved, and failure to save becomes evidence against Kanade's moral legitimacy.

This matters because a weaker reconstruction could treat Mafuyu's testimony that K's music affected her as the origin of Kanade's rescue identity. EVENT_0047 rejects that chronology. Mafuyu becomes the most consequential recipient, but she enters a pathology already formed by the father's collapse and Kanade's interpretation of it.

### 5.2 Bodily neglect is not a side effect of later N25 workload

The flashback shows severe food/sleep deterioration before N25 forms. Kanade eats minimally, loses hunger cues, cannot remember adequate sleep, experiences pain/nausea/numbness/tinnitus/visual disturbance, and continues composing through escalating physical warning signs.

The eventual collapse is therefore not best modeled as a freak incident. It is the predictable endpoint of a governing rule in which body maintenance has less moral priority than producing rescue-capable music.

This is crucial for reconstruction. Later ordinary-food scenes should not be mistaken for evidence that Kanade simply has quirky dietary preferences. Food neglect is historically connected to mission narrowing and can reactivate when rescue urgency becomes high.

### 5.3 Snow's arrangement gives Kanade something she did not already possess

When Kanade encounters Snow's arrangement, she does not experience it as interchangeable fan feedback. The work depicts a world of raw despair in a way that generates new compositional possibilities for Kanade. Technical imperfection does not cancel psychological/creative specificity.

Kanade's response establishes a reciprocal causal direction before direct collaboration begins:

`K song -> Mafuyu painful affect / self-search -> Snow arrangement -> Kanade creative expansion -> new K song -> Mafuyu renewed movement`.

This loop is foundational to the later relationship. Kanade is not only the actor and Mafuyu the object of intervention. Mafuyu's work changes Kanade's music from the start.

## 6. Mafuyu before N25: painful affect as the first usable difference

### 6.1 Public success and private nullity coexist

The flashback version of Mafuyu is socially polished and externally legible. Graduation, peer conversation, family gift discussion, and the medical future can all be handled in the expected register. The internal problem is not social incompetence. It is the inability to locate an internally authoritative desire inside the successful performance.

The graduation-gift scene is especially diagnostic: the mother offers a choice, but Mafuyu cannot find an object she wants. The difficulty is not decision paralysis between two liked options. It is absence/inaccessibility of preference itself.

### 6.2 K's music matters because it hurts and cannot be ignored

A mainstream song recommended by a friend can be praised appropriately without moving Mafuyu. K's music produces a radically different first-person event: bodily pain, emotional disturbance, and inability to stop listening.

EVENT_0047 therefore pushes the negative-affect-as-self-evidence model backward in chronology. Long before Mafuyu can name positive warmth, painful affect already distinguishes one stimulus from the surrounding flatness.

This is not evidence that suffering is desirable. It is evidence that a non-null aversive response can supply information when positive preference language is inaccessible.

### 6.3 Creative imitation is self-directed inquiry, not passive fandom

Mafuyu does not stop at repeated listening. She asks how she might understand why the song affects her and acts on the suggestion to make music herself. The old synthesizer becomes an investigative instrument. She works after her parents sleep, reconstructs the song's formal choices, alters them, and tries to discover what the emotional mechanism means.

This is a major historical antecedent to later first-person-authority work. Before she possesses a theory of herself, Mafuyu already performs a form of experimental self-inquiry:

`unexpected affect -> isolate stimulus -> reconstruct form -> alter form -> compare internal response -> continue search`.

That architecture later becomes much more relational and articulate, but its root is visible here.

### 6.4 Snow is both absence and remaining desire

Mafuyu's arrangement does not merely depict despair. The event also shows an important contradiction inside it: the wish to disappear coexists with something that does not want disappearance to be final. Kanade recognizes a small-light possibility inside the same work.

The significance is not that Kanade discovers Mafuyu's true meaning with perfect authority. Mafuyu herself remains uncertain. The collaboration becomes productive because Kanade treats the ambiguity as something to compose with rather than something to resolve by fiat.

## 7. Kanade-Mafuyu: reciprocity is constitutive at origin

### 7.1 The relationship does not begin as unilateral rescue

The event's largest relationship revision is historical. Later N25 stories naturally foreground Kanade's promise to save Mafuyu, creating a risk that the dyad is reconstructed as unilateral at first and only later becomes reciprocal.

EVENT_0047 shows the opposite. Before they know one another's faces or real names:

- Kanade's music is the first thing Mafuyu identifies as moving her internal state;
- Mafuyu uses that movement to begin a self-directed creative search;
- Mafuyu's arrangement gives Kanade an unprecedented depiction of despair and new creative material;
- Kanade composes toward the small light she perceives within that despair;
- Mafuyu accepts collaboration because K may help something change;
- Kanade accepts collaboration because Snow may help her create music capable of reaching someone;
- their technical/affective exchange immediately becomes more fluent than ordinary biographical intimacy.

They are asymmetrical, but the asymmetry is not one-way causation.

### 7.2 The relationship grows through controlled distance

Their early use of handles, Nightcord, late-night timing, and limited biography is not merely an aesthetic internet detail. It gives both participants a relationship format that is possible under their current conditions.

Mafuyu can work only after parental surveillance/expectation recedes for the night. Kanade can share music without ordinary social demands. They can reveal despair through work before revealing names, faces, family history, or full biography.

The event therefore strengthens the existing theme that intimacy can develop under incomplete disclosure without making incomplete disclosure universally healthy.

### 7.3 Register change is relational evidence

The transition from formal address to K/Snow naming is small but significant. The source shows both parties actively negotiating a less formal collaborative relation. Kanade later remembers that non-music conversation with Snow did not feel unpleasant and that Snow could avoid areas Kanade did not want touched while still being direct.

This helps explain why trust becomes unusually fast and durable: Snow is not only a recipient; she is an interlocutor whose interaction style is compatible with Kanade's boundaries at that historical moment.

### 7.4 Present-day explicit valuation

At the present boundary, both participants can say versions of what the relationship came to mean. Mafuyu states that accepting K's invitation was good. Kanade is glad they can now talk as they do and recommits to creating alongside the current relationship.

This is stronger than simple survival of the bond. It is retrospective endorsement without pretending the intervening years were painless.

## 8. Honami: from literal emergency intervention to reciprocal ordinary care

EVENT_0047 materially deepens `REL-CROSS-KANADE-HONAMI-E0002`.

The historical revelation is causal: middle-school Honami, present through work experience/household-service context, hears/follows the emergency situation, finds Kanade collapsed, and enables the ambulance response. Kanade's grandmother explicitly treats Honami as having saved Kanade and uses the event to insist that Kanade accept continuing household support.

This makes later domestic care structurally different from a generic service relationship. Honami's current cooking, cleaning, flowers, tea, monitoring, and social facilitation sit on top of a history in which her practical presence literally prevented Kanade's isolation from continuing through medical collapse.

Card `0361` then provides the reciprocal present-day counterpoint. Kanade notices the care, wants to thank Honami, makes tea for her despite limited competence, directly expresses gratitude, and asks that the relationship continue. Honami accepts the imperfect tea as valuable because Kanade prepared it for her.

The correct model is therefore not `Honami cares / Kanade receives`. It is a bounded but increasingly reciprocal care channel with professional-service structure, confidentiality, historical emergency significance, and genuine personal gratitude.

Because Leo/need has no completed main-story analytical foundation, this event must not assign Honami a Leo/need I0-I3 state. The route is preserved at high priority for later baseline-aware interpretation.

## 9. Present Kanade: progress without wellness

### 9.1 Calmer is source-supported

In the opening frame Kanade explicitly compares her present condition with the past and feels more able to settle. Honami suggests that having people with whom Kanade can share feeling may contribute. Kanade accepts that possibility.

That is real progress and should be usable in reconstruction.

### 9.2 One meal is also source-supported

The linked area deliberately prevents a wellness overread. When Miku/Luka ask how many meals Kanade ate, the answer is one. Kanade promises to be careful, but the event does not show a completed behavioral correction.

This is why `K-E0026-01` remains current. Ordinary pleasure, gratitude, relational support, and weakly improved self-care coexist with rescue obligation and bodily neglect.

### 9.3 Accompaniment counts below the rescue threshold

Area `night_07_001` is particularly important for the rescue model. Kanade recognizes that the first joint song may not qualify as full salvation, yet still hopes it can remain beside a listener who repeatedly returns to it.

This directly strengthens the partial-efficacy/reaching correction inside `K-E0026-01`: music can matter through accompaniment even when the binary category `saved` is unavailable.

## 10. Present Mafuyu: more explicit relational appraisal without broad preference recovery

### 10.1 The old dragon no longer fits

Card `0362` returns to an old psychological-test answer in which Mafuyu chose a dragon as the threatening creature. Mafuyu privately recognizes that this answer no longer feels right, but she cannot say what should replace it.

The scene is useful only if treated carefully. The in-story personality test is not scientific authority. The evidentiary value lies in Mafuyu's own comparison: an old self-description no longer fits, and she can notice the mismatch without possessing a replacement definition.

That is exactly compatible with `MF-E0042-01`.

### 10.2 Concern can be enacted before it is fully named

After hearing more about Kanade's living situation and collapse history, Mafuyu gives concrete advice about cleaning, ventilation, and health. When Kanade thanks her for worrying, Mafuyu becomes uncertain whether the behavior counts as worry and later asks Ena/Mizuki.

The peers do not become final authorities over Mafuyu's inner state. They offer a plausible relational interpretation: Mafuyu seems to want Kanade to remain well. Mafuyu connects that to the continuing promise and then acts on the hypothesis by asking Kanade whether she ate.

This is an extension of the EVENT_0042 rule:

`relational action -> another person's interpretation -> Mafuyu compares against first-person evidence -> low-stakes behavior can continue before categorical certainty`.

### 10.3 Explicit positive appraisal is real but bounded

Card `0365` gives Mafuyu a stronger sentence than the earlier cautious warmth language. After reconstructing the past, she says that encountering Kanade's music, making music with Kanade, and meeting Ena, Mizuki, and Miku were, in aggregate, good.

This is a significant strengthening of relational self-knowledge. It still does not justify a successor global state because the same card and linked area preserve major limits: ordinary school memories are largely blank, preference categories remain weak, seasonal preference is absent, and temperature sensitivity is still unusual.

The new evidence is best integrated as a higher-confidence explicit appraisal inside the existing state rather than as broad identity recovery.

### 10.4 The SEKAI can be used for rest

Mafuyu visits the Empty SEKAI simply because homework is done and it is too early for the normal 25:00 session. She is not there because a new crisis occurred. She sits with Miku, asks Miku to listen, reflects, and says she will return.

This extends the established non-crisis SEKAI function from shared leisure/search into low-demand personal rest and relational presence.

## 11. Ena: causal help can be received without surrendering artistic authorship

Card `0364` adds an important retrospective route. After paternal discouragement and failure, Ena remembers lashing out at family and withdrawing. Her mother gets her to watch television because Airi, whom the family knows as Ena's friend, is appearing as an idol. Ena sees Airi visibly pursuing the dream she had talked about and remembers that this helped her decide to try again.

This does not make Airi the author of Ena's artist identity. Ena's later persistence remains her own. The card instead strengthens a recurring N25 pattern: external relational evidence can reopen agency without replacing first-person authorship.

In the present half, Ena asks for Mafuyu's early arrangement, experiences its cold/empty affective world, and uses the experience to push her own illustration closer to what she thinks Kanade's current song requires. The result connects retrospective support, present creative translation, and chosen artistic effort.

For MMJ, Airi's role is a deferred cross-unit route rather than a baseline-relative impact score.

## 12. Mizuki: another origin story increases the value of preserved time

Card `0363` has Mizuki interpret Kanade and Mafuyu as two people who struggled forward until they found each other and could illuminate one another. That interpretation then turns toward Mizuki's own unresolved situation.

The source deliberately leaves one internal comparison incomplete. It must remain incomplete. The analysis should not force the unfinished `for me, K is...` thought into a specific person or role not stated by the text.

What is explicit is enough: Mizuki remembers Ena's decision to wait and later concludes that the time Ena has given must be valued. This strongly reinforces `MZ-E0039-01` and `REL-N25-EMZ-2` without moving disclosure forward.

Area `night_07_005` adds participant-side confirmation. Mizuki observes that Kanade/Mafuyu may be able to talk about the past now because enough time/change has made it tolerable. Ena immediately reaffirms that she will wait for Mizuki. Mizuki thanks her. The event thereby contrasts voluntary historical disclosure with disclosure that is still not ready.

## 13. N25 group: normality, history, and incomplete disclosure

EVENT_0047 strengthens `REL-N25-G-7` in three distinct ways.

First, ordinary meals have become normal. Kanade notices the transformation; area `night_07_004` shows the group joking that Ena/Mizuki arguments and Mafuyu's blunt responses have become predictable social routine. The point is not frictionlessness. Familiar friction is itself part of the stable relationship grammar.

Second, Kanade explicitly wants the four to continue making music together. Ena and Mizuki immediately agree; Mafuyu anchors her agreement in the continuing promise. The group is not merely still functioning. Its continuation is explicitly desired.

Third, autobiographical sharing becomes more possible without becoming compulsory. Area `night_07_002` has Ena acknowledge that the group historically avoided personal/past material and hope that some of it can be shared gradually. She immediately preserves the limit that everything may never be told. This is a strong post-EVENT_0039 guardrail against equating intimacy with total transparency.

## 14. N25 Miku and the SEKAI as continuity witness

Card `0365` gives Miku a distinct present relational function. Mafuyu explains that ordinary school life from two years earlier is largely blank while memories tied to Kanade's song and her first creative response became recoverable during the retelling. She cannot determine why.

Miku does not claim diagnostic certainty. She offers a possibility: perhaps those memories mattered and Mafuyu wanted to retain them. That remains a suggestion, not an externally imposed truth.

Mafuyu then asks whether the SEKAI will still remain if she eventually finds her true feeling/song. Miku says once-born feelings may be forgotten or change but do not simply cease to have existed; Miku and the SEKAI will remember Mafuyu's feelings.

Analytically, this should be stored as manifestation-specific ontology and relational assurance. It supports continuity across inaccessible memory states. It does not mean Miku can retrieve any lost memory on demand, read current mental content, or provide an objective archive of every prior feeling.

This strongly extends `REL-N25-VS-MIKU-MAFUYU-E0042` and the populated/relational Empty SEKAI motif without requiring a new dyad ID.

## 15. Epistemic architecture and leakage guardrails

EVENT_0047 is unusually dangerous for epistemic overread because the audience sees long internal flashback sequences while the present group is also listening to an origin story.

The following must remain separate:

### Audience-level historical access

The audience receives detailed internal material about:

- exact bodily deterioration before collapse;
- Mafuyu's empty graduation-gift response;
- painful first response to K's music;
- Mafuyu's private motive for creative reconstruction;

### Participant-level present transmission

Ena and Mizuki explicitly learn at least that:

- Kanade and Mafuyu began as K/Snow collaborators;
- Kanade collapsed during the pre-N25 period;
- a household-service worker who became Honami found Kanade and helped enable emergency response;

Later card/area dialogue confirms some of this knowledge directly.

Do not automatically give Ena/Mizuki every exact internal sentence shown to the audience merely because the event visually frames a retelling.

The same caution applies to Virtual Singers. Miku/Luka/Rin/MEIKO receive retold origin material through explicit present scenes/card dialogue, but none becomes a mind reader or retroactive witness to every private thought.

## 17. Theme and motif consequences

### 17.1 `TH-N25-003` - creative work as psychological evidence becomes origin-constitutive

EVENT_0047 shows creative work not merely revealing an already formed relationship but creating the conditions for the relationship. Mafuyu learns about herself through reconstruction of K's song; Kanade learns about Snow through the arrangement; each new artifact becomes input to the other.

### 17.2 `TH-N25-009` - negative affect as self-evidence gains pre-N25 provenance

The earliest recoverable differentiation in Mafuyu's flattening is painful rather than pleasant. This strengthens the theme while preserving its anti-romanticization guardrail.

### 17.4 `TH-N25-024` and `TH-N25-014` - ordinary life is meaningful precisely because pathology persists

Restaurants, tea, shopping invitations, health questions, routine teasing, and resting in the SEKAI are not cure markers. The same envelope still shows one-meal Kanade and preference/sensory-limited Mafuyu.
