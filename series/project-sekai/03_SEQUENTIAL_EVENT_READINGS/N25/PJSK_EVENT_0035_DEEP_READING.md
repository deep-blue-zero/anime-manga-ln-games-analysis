# Project SEKAI EVENT_0035 Deep Reading — 灯のミラージュ

## 1. Authority, scope, and analytical result

`EVENT_0035 — 灯のミラージュ` is a **Tier A developmental event** for Nightcord at 25:00 and an **I3 state-changing release** in the current analytical framework.

The decisive transition belongs to **Asahina Mafuyu**. The event does not solve her identity problem, restore a normal preference map, settle her relationship with her mother, or remove her tendency to overperform social and institutional roles. What changes is narrower and more durable: Mafuyu becomes able to recover a positive bodily feeling through autobiographical association, recognize that the feeling is accessible even when its categorical name remains unavailable, and deliberately preserve it **for herself** through **her own words**.

The resulting current human-state tuple is:

`MF-E0035-01 / K-E0026-01 / E-E0014-01 / MZ-E0019-01`.

The primary relationship transition is:

`REL-N25-KM-5 -> REL-N25-KM-6`.

A new bounded family relationship extraction is also warranted:

`REL-FAMILY-MAFUYU-MOTHER-E0035`.

This artifact is the canonical EVENT_0035 deep reading. The mutable character, relationship, epistemic, claim, theme/motif, and release-impact ledgers remain the canonical longitudinal homes for the extracted state changes.

## 3. Narrative and causal spine

EVENT_0035 begins from a residue left by EVENT_0026 rather than from an unrelated new problem. Mafuyu remembers that Kanade's recent song made her chest feel slightly warm and that she smiled without first understanding why. Earlier events had already established that Mafuyu can show affect before she can interpret it, and EVENT_0033 had sharpened the distinction between weak non-null valence and categorical preference. EVENT_0035 asks what happens when Mafuyu herself treats such a residue as a question worth pursuing.

That difference is crucial. The event is not merely another scene in which somebody else notices Mafuyu's reaction. Mafuyu becomes the investigator.

The search moves through four stages:

1. **Somatic residue:** warmth and the remembered smile are present before explanation.
2. **Autobiographical search:** Mafuyu turns toward photographs and childhood memory despite bodily distress.
3. **Relational/creative cueing:** Kanade explains the positive memories and desired affect behind her own composition; Miku later becomes a performance surface for Mafuyu's lyric discrimination.
4. **Self-authored preservation:** Mafuyu cannot fully name the feeling, but decides that it should be retained for herself and rendered in her own words.

The event therefore transforms the question from “Can Mafuyu feel?” into a more precise problem: **What operations are available between feeling and full conceptual understanding?** EVENT_0035 answers that memory, analogy, creative form, and deliberate preservation can occupy that middle space.

## 4. Mafuyu: from affect ownership to autobiographical affect retrieval

### 4.1 What EVENT_0002 had already established

`MF-E0002-01` remains the necessary predecessor. EVENT_0002 rejected the idea that Mafuyu is literally affectless. Negative, aesthetic, and relational reactions can occur before she understands them; creative externalization can return those reactions to her in a form she can inspect; and another person can prompt expression without owning the feeling expressed.

Subsequent events broadened that state without replacing it. Physical activity could produce spontaneous-looking positive affect; ordinary experiences could acquire salience before preference language; the aquarium could matter behaviorally without Mafuyu being able to say she liked it; moon-viewing could move from predicted null response to weak non-negative valence.

EVENT_0035 crosses a threshold because the affect is no longer only present-tense reaction. It becomes **autobiographically connective**.

### 4.2 Self-initiated investigation

At the opening, Mafuyu asks why Kanade's song alone produced warmth and why she smiled. This is not trivial curiosity. The main-story Mafuyu often experiences externally supplied questions about her own preference as impossible or exhausting because they require an answer she cannot access. Here, the unresolved reaction itself has become salient enough for Mafuyu to generate the question.

Luka's key/keyhole language is useful because it preserves the incompleteness of the state. Mafuyu possesses something like a key—an affective response—but cannot yet find the structure that makes it intelligible. The event does not solve this by giving Luka privileged access. Luka can articulate the search problem; she cannot declare the answer.

The same pattern governs the album. Mafuyu chooses to retrieve it and later brings it into the Empty SEKAI because thinking at home is difficult. Mizuki and Rin help inspect it, but the evidentiary project remains Mafuyu's. Mizuki's most important contribution is not interpretation but pacing: noticing deterioration and telling Mafuyu that they can continue later rather than forcing the answer through exhaustion.

### 4.3 The body is simultaneously evidence and neglected infrastructure

The event uses the body in two opposite ways.

First, Mafuyu's body is an epistemic surface. Chest warmth, pain around memory, feverish associative recall, and the later recognition that the memory can still be felt all provide information that propositional self-description cannot initially supply.

Second, Mafuyu remains poor at treating the body as something deserving practical authority. Her schedule accumulates school committee work, study, prep-school preparation, kyudo, social obligations, and Nightcord production. She misses lunch, minimizes strain, and ultimately collapses.

This distinction is important for reconstruction. EVENT_0035 is not a generic “Mafuyu is getting better at listening to herself” state. She becomes better at treating certain affective traces as self-relevant evidence while remaining substantially impaired at body-state monitoring and self-protective prioritization.

The successor state therefore includes **greater affective agency alongside persistent self-care failure**.

### 4.4 The fever memory and the return of a self-originated preference

The fever sequence provides some of the strongest evidence in the event because it recovers a childhood Mafuyu whose preference is neither inferred by the audience nor supplied by another character.

Young Mafuyu asks for rabbit-shaped apple because she likes rabbits. She says the apple tastes good. She asks her mother to stay until she falls asleep and to hold her hand. These are direct, first-person wants.

This matters for several existing claims. Mafuyu did not begin life as a person without preference or attachment. Her later problem is loss, suppression, mistrust, and inaccessibility of first-person authority—not original absence.

But the scene's importance is not just forensic. Present Mafuyu experiences the remembered care as affectively available. In card `0284:02`, the critical formulation is that remembering still hurts a little, but she can **feel** it. The recovered material is therefore neither a neutral memory fact nor an instantly comfortable nostalgic truth. Pain and warmth coexist.

### 4.5 Feeling, labeling, and explaining are separable

EVENT_0035 makes a major methodological distinction explicit:

`affect access != categorical labeling != causal explanation`.

Mafuyu can:

- feel warmth;
- recognize recurrence;
- associate it with an earlier care memory;
- decide it matters;
- preserve it creatively;

while still truthfully saying she does not know exactly what the feeling is.

That is why the correct state transition is not “Mafuyu understands love” or “Mafuyu realizes she loves her mother.” The source is more restrained. The developmental achievement is **usable access under incomplete interpretation**.

This distinction should become a governing reconstruction rule. Mafuyu's `わからない` after EVENT_0035 is not necessarily evidence that no internal state exists. It can coexist with strong behavioral salience, bodily valence, autobiographical association, and deliberate preservation.

### 4.6 “For myself” and “in my own words”

The strongest state-changing evidence comes in the lyric sequence.

Mafuyu concludes that the feeling should be preserved `自分のために` and decides to make it into lyrics `私の言葉で`. These formulations matter because they shift the goal of the self-search. She is not producing the socially correct answer to satisfy her mother, teachers, Nightcord, or even Kanade. Nor is she merely supplying data because Kanade needs it for a rescue composition. She identifies preservation itself as something she wants to do for herself.

Card `0284:02` strengthens this beyond a single dramatic utterance. Mafuyu asks Miku to perform the draft because hearing it externally can reveal whether the words match what she wants to communicate. She changes phrasing in response to that comparison. This is a concrete authorship loop:

`internal trace -> chosen words -> external performance -> comparison against intended meaning -> self-directed revision`.

At the group review, Mafuyu says she wrote what she wanted to write; Ena recognizes that this is unprecedented in their shared experience. The change is therefore both first-person and externally observable.

### 4.7 Endpoint and remaining limits

The quiet breakfast endpoint refuses triumphalism. When apple appears again, Mafuyu softly produces the rabbit association but retracts when questioned. This is a trace, not a stable new preference declaration.

Accordingly, `MF-E0035-01` should predict that Mafuyu can sometimes pursue salient affect more actively, especially when it is supported by creative or autobiographical cues. It should **not** predict that she can readily answer ordinary “what do you like?” questions, reliably assert preferences against family expectations, or maintain healthy workload boundaries.

## 5. Kanade and Mafuyu: intended positive destination becomes demonstrated reach

EVENT_0026 created `REL-N25-KM-5`: Kanade could articulate a positive destination for rescue—self-discovery, broader feeling, warmth, genuine smile—and could recognize a small smile as real progress without calling Mafuyu saved. EVENT_0034 then showed that this intermediate-efficacy correction had generalized into compositional method.

EVENT_0035 provides the strongest downstream validation so far.

Kanade's song was built partly from positive memories of her own parents and the wish for Mafuyu to smile genuinely. Mafuyu does not simply report liking the song. Instead, it becomes a cue through which she reaches an autobiographical affect state and then creates language of her own. Kanade's desired effect thus becomes **causally relevant without becoming authorially sovereign**.

That distinction warrants `REL-N25-KM-6`.

### 5.1 Embodied caregiving

When Mafuyu collapses, Kanade moves from compositional support into practical bodily care. She brings Mafuyu home, provides cooling, prepares grated apple, stays nearby, and holds her hand when Mafuyu asks not to be left alone.

The scene deepens relational intimacy, but its fever context should constrain interpretation. Mafuyu's plea and hand-holding resonate with the childhood memory that follows. They are strong evidence that Kanade occupies a position from which Mafuyu can seek immediate bodily reassurance, but they should not be converted into a stable fully conscious declaration with a stronger category than the source supplies.

### 5.2 Reciprocal ordinary intimacy after the crisis

Card `0288:02` is important because the relationship does not end with one-sided rescue. Mafuyu returns to Kanade's home with cup noodles and apples in thanks, retrieves her student identification, notices Kanade's awkward tea-making, and gives practical instruction. The scene restores Mafuyu as an active participant rather than permanent patient.

Kanade serves apple-flavored tea; Mafuyu still cannot perceive the taste. Kanade privately hopes they might someday enjoy the same taste. This preserves the event's graded logic: relational warmth and developmental movement coexist with persistent sensory/preference limits.

### 5.3 The asymmetry remains

Nothing in EVENT_0035 removes Kanade's persistent-penance architecture. She remains rescue-oriented, and Mafuyu's progress still has unusually high moral significance for her. The correct relationship update therefore includes both demonstrated positive reach and continuing dependency risk.

## 6. Mafuyu and her mother: a nonbinary family model becomes mandatory

EVENT_0035 materially improves the family model because it supplies evidence that resists both simple villainization and simple exoneration.

The childhood fever memory is genuinely warm. Mafuyu makes self-originated requests; her mother responds; physical presence and hand-holding matter. The scene should not be redescribed as covert manipulation merely because later family dynamics become harmful.

At the same time, the event surrounds that memory with current and remembered structures of expectation. The Snow White costume memory is retrospectively narrated through a mother who already decided what Mafuyu would probably prefer. Present-day conversation continues to assume the medical-school trajectory and frames school obligations around what best protects that path. `いい子` remains an important reinforcement language.

The analytically precise formulation is therefore:

> **genuine care can participate in an autonomy-eroding relationship without becoming fake, and autonomy-eroding effects can become severe without requiring every caring act to be malicious.**

This is why `REL-FAMILY-MAFUYU-MOTHER-E0035` is a bounded relationship state rather than a verdict on maternal intention.

For behavioral simulation, the family relationship should now be expected to evoke mixed states: attachment traces, compliance habits, pain, remembered warmth, difficulty opposing expectations, and possible inability to sort those components into a single clean judgment.

## 7. Mizuki: Mafuyu's progress intensifies the disclosure mirror

Mizuki's card does not create a successor to `MZ-E0019-01`, but it is highly relevant to that state.

Mizuki helps with Mafuyu's album search and supports pacing rather than demanding revelation. This is consistent with the established autonomy-sensitive support grammar. Later, however, Mafuyu's progress becomes comparative evidence. Mizuki explicitly sees Mafuyu, Kanade, and Ena as moving forward while feeling that only Mizuki is not going anywhere.

This matters because EVENT_0019 established that increased attachment can make nondisclosure more painful rather than automatically making disclosure easier. EVENT_0035 strengthens the same architecture. Watching another member recover self-relevant material does not simply inspire Mizuki; it can sharpen awareness of their own unresolved boundary.

N25 Miku's response is also important. Miku sits beside Mizuki, communicates that Mizuki is not alone, and agrees not to tell Kanade or Ena when Mizuki asks for confidentiality. This is a model of support under explicitly preserved disclosure ownership.

No state change occurs because Mizuki still does not cross the guarded boundary, generate a new disclosure strategy, or materially revise the future-attachment conflict. The event strengthens the tension rather than resolving or restructuring it.

## 8. Ena and the N25 group: care without homogenized support style

Ena's event function is characteristically confrontational. She becomes angry that Mafuyu failed to recognize her own condition and again interprets the problem through a distinction between ordinary failure and Mafuyu-specific difficulty. Luka's correction—that noticing may be difficult rather than impossible—helps keep the model precise.

Ena's card then makes the care operational. She researches practical illness support, considers messaging Mafuyu directly, retracts the message, and later tells Kanade that she can skip school and come help if needed. This remains compatible with `E-E0014-01`: the event does not touch Ena's central artistic-worth architecture enough to require a new global state.

The group as a whole also remains `REL-N25-G-7`. EVENT_0035 extends distributed care, but the governing structure is unchanged:

- different members help in different ways;
- vulnerability can belong to any member;
- first-person information can remain partial;
- care does not require total disclosure;
- ordinary banter resumes without making the event's developmental change disappear.

## 9. N25 Virtual Singers: support without omniscience

EVENT_0035 continues the project's strongest anti-mind-reading rule for SEKAI manifestations.

### Luka

Luka can formulate the search problem as a key without a visible keyhole, ask direct questions, and recognize progress. She cannot provide the missing feeling as an answer.

### Miku

Miku functions in two distinct but compatible ways:

- with Mafuyu, she becomes a performance surface and co-memory holder: she sings the draft, asks whether the wording fits what Mafuyu wants to communicate, and promises to remember the recovered trace with her;
- with Mizuki, she offers presence and confidentiality without extracting the guarded content.

### Rin

Rin notices the humans' distress and acts relationally despite incomplete knowledge. Her card shows ordinary concern and regret that the album search may have continued too long, and she chooses singing as a comfort behavior.

These are manifestation-specific relationship methods. They do not authorize a franchise-wide claim that Virtual Singers know hidden human truth.

## 10. Epistemic-state consequences

EVENT_0035 increases the gap between **audience knowledge** and **group knowledge** in a useful way.

The audience directly sees the detailed childhood fever memory, including the rabbit-shaped apple and hand-holding. Nightcord does not automatically receive this full sequence. What the group receives at the lyric review is bounded: Mafuyu associates the feeling in Kanade's song with an old apple-related memory, has managed to preserve something important, and still cannot fully explain it.

This has several reconstruction consequences:

- Kanade may know that the song reached an old memory without knowing every maternal detail.
- Ena may recognize changed authorship without knowing the full developmental mechanism.
- Mizuki may understand the structural importance of Mafuyu's progress without acquiring the memory transcript.
- Shizuku, from card `0284:01`, sees fatigue and difficulty around the “natural self” question but remains outside N25-private knowledge.
- Miku has bounded confidentiality knowledge about Mizuki and bounded lyric-intent knowledge about Mafuyu, not omniscience.

## 14. Reconstruction rules after EVENT_0035

For Mafuyu scenarios bounded at or after this event, the following are now source-grounded:

1. **An unexplained positive reaction can become an investigative target.** Mafuyu may return to it voluntarily rather than waiting for others to ask.
2. **“I don't know” is compatible with meaningful affect access.** Do not treat absent labels as absent feeling.
3. **Autobiographical association can unlock usable affect.** Music, objects, bodily states, or care patterns may cue memory before abstract explanation.
4. **Creative externalization is now more self-authored.** Mafuyu can deliberately use lyrics to preserve something she wants to keep for herself.
5. **Relational prompting does not erase ownership.** Kanade/Miku/Mizuki can change access conditions without becoming authoritative interpreters.
6. **Family cues should allow mixed valence.** Warmth, attachment, pain, compliance, and autonomy threat can coexist.
7. **Overwork remains highly plausible.** Psychological progress does not make Mafuyu reliably notice hunger, illness, or unsustainable workload.
8. **Kanade is a high-trust embodied-care person.** Mafuyu can accept care and later reciprocate ordinary domestic gestures, but dependency risk remains analytically live.
9. **Mizuki may react to another member's progress with self-comparison.** This can intensify the disclosure conflict without making disclosure imminent.
10. **Ena's anger can be care.** Illness or self-neglect may trigger confrontational concern and concrete action rather than gentle reassurance.
11. **N25 Miku can honor confidentiality.** Presence without forced disclosure is now source-grounded.
12. **Do not upgrade the breakfast “rabbit” trace into stable preference assertion.** It is evidence of re-emergent autobiographical salience, not a recovered comprehensive preference map.

## 16. Final synthesis

`灯のミラージュ` is the first event since `囚われのマリオネット` to justify a successor global Mafuyu state because it changes the **method and evidentiary threshold** by which Mafuyu can continue searching for herself.

EVENT_0002 established that affect could exist before comprehension and that creative work could return otherwise inaccessible internal material. EVENT_0035 adds three things that materially change future behavior:

- **autobiographical continuity:** a present positive bodily feeling can connect to a remembered earlier self;
- **graded epistemic confidence:** Mafuyu can know that she feels something without pretending to know exactly what it is;
- **self-authored preservation:** incomplete understanding no longer prevents her from deciding that the feeling matters enough to preserve for herself in her own words.

The event's strongest achievement is therefore not the recovery of one happy childhood memory. It is the creation of a new operating rule:

> **A feeling does not have to be completely named before Mafuyu can recognize it as hers, preserve it, and use it as evidence in the continuing construction of a self.**

That is a durable change in response distribution and warrants `MF-E0035-01`, Tier A, and I3.
