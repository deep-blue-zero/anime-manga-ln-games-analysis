## 2. Success opens a place that still carries shame

JET succeeds through mutual response rather than four isolated feats. Toya's new track and An's lyrics enable Kohane's lead; Akito feels both freedom and an urge to intensify the audience's response. EVER accepts a late order/set-list change and develops its own work in response to VBS. Akito apologizes for the logistical burden and can acknowledge the other group's experience. A competitive aim already coexists with practical consideration and reciprocal artistic stimulus (`PJSK:event:0079:01:003:0002–0038`).

At the delayed celebration, Ken welcomes Arata without demanding an account of every absence; Arata credits VBS with creating a productive atmosphere. Toya can name the satisfaction of translating his feelings into a track, while Kohane says she hopes eventually to learn that skill too. The group's competencies are neither identical nor static (`PJSK:event:0079:01:003:0040–0076`). Ken's report of a usually critical customer's praise is reported reception, separate from the audience responses shown in the performance memory.

Arata's Crawl Green proposal gives forward momentum a historical charge. It is RADder's debut venue, with an owner who screens recordings and distinguishes performing there from earning permission to host a whole event. Arata has already been negotiating since the first event; his past appearances are useful access, not automatic approval. An volunteers her longstanding acquaintance with the owner. The group agrees to seek the venue, but permission remains pending (`PJSK:event:0079:02:001:0001–0045`).

For Akito, the name also means an old failure. He does not initially disclose it, but insists that this time he has companions and must use every available moment to prepare (`PJSK:event:0079:02:002:0005–0026`). The productive and costly aspects of this urgency remain connected. An and Kohane eagerly use Ken's new equipment; Toya attends his family's early Christmas meal; Akito runs. Different activities do not indicate different degrees of commitment.

## 3. Trying, committing and meeting a hostile standard are separate stages

On the night of RAD WEEKEND, young Akito repeatedly watches RADder videos and discovers a bodily intensity beyond what he remembers from soccer. He had thought himself serious about soccer, yet could not promise the life-scale commitment he saw in others. Music too may prove uncertain. Ena's remembered permission to try before deciding supplies a way to begin without fraudulent certainty (`PJSK:event:0079:04:003:0002–0036`). This reiterates the causal suggestion already integrated from EVENT_0029; it does not make Ena responsible for every later choice.

Initial practice is poor and copying recordings does not immediately teach him how to sing. Seeking another live encounter, he learns RADder has suspended activity and COL is closing. The staff member also offers another route into the street's music through flyers and present performers. Akito appreciates its free, competitive vitality; disappointment does not exhaust his response (`PJSK:event:0079:04:005`; `PJSK:event:0079:04:007`; `PJSK:event:0079:04:008:0002–0039`).

An's accomplished singing and stated wish to surpass RAD WEEKEND sharpen the vague wish into a standard of commitment. Akito first considers the goal impossible, then reads the force of her voice as connected to that resolve. He wants to surpass everyone on the street and asks permission to sing beside COL. Bystanders hear conspicuous volume and missed notes; An notices briefly. This is asymmetrical influence, not a fully formed mutual partnership at first sight (`PJSK:event:0079:04:008:0040–0068`).

A year later, older musicians use his reputation and lack of present skill to belittle his ambition. His own sharp responses worsen conflict, but their hostility is not rendered deserved simply because he is socially clumsy. Kotaro observes the problem from outside. In the present, Kotaro recalls being surprised when a smoother public Akito appeared; Akito explains that desperation had previously left little room to think about social relations (`PJSK:event:0079:04:009:0002–0038`). The polite mode is learned and context-sensitive, not proof every courteous action is false.

At the later invitation to Crawl Green, Akito **knows he is not yet ready**. The taunt that refusal means running away turns an opportunity into a test of his declared seriousness. He accepts and intensifies practice (`PJSK:event:0079:05:002:0007–0029`). The venue magnifies the mismatch: musicians he finds impressive are ordinary by its audience's standards. Staff believe he is a late replacement; the owner does not initially recognize him. The text does not show him passing the usual recording screen. He finishes despite lost audience interest, then recognizes that the antagonists really can restore its excitement.
