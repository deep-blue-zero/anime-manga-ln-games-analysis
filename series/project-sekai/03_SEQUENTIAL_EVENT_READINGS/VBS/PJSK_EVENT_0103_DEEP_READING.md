## 4. Kohane turns frustration into an owned forward rule

The group reaches an impasse. It believes demonstrated strength could restore others' confidence, but it does not yet know how to gain that strength. At the Street café, Kohane wishes she had sung strongly enough to keep allies from leaving. The statement is a counterfactual expression of responsibility, not proof that her singing alone caused their departure. Miku's response changes its function: the timid newcomer can now feel frustrated about losing to a singer like Taiga. `PJSK:event:0103:05:001:0001–PJSK:event:0103:05:004:0047`.

Miku identifies the underlying change. Because Kohane is serious about singing, believes her voice can do something, and wants to progress, defeat produces frustration. Miku calls this singer pride and tells Kohane not to release it because it can support the search for a way forward. MEIKO then asks the whole group to compare losses with what it has gained. Toya names greater access to his own feelings and emerging composition skill; Akito names wider attention and reliance; An names her partner and group; Kohane traces her current self through companions, Street singers, the community, Nagi, and Taiga. `PJSK:event:0103:06:001:0001–0082`.

Kohane does not erase Taiga's harmful act or flatten him into an enemy. She recognizes him as a teacher, holds gratitude and frustration together, and chooses what to do with the latter. At the airport she thanks him for prior instruction, says she will hold this frustration and continue even when frightened or physically frozen, and vows that the group will surpass his song and intent as part of surpassing RAD WEEKEND. Taiga receives the challenge. `PJSK:event:0103:07:001:0050–0069`.

This advances `VBS-EP-KH-E0028 → VBS-EP-KH-E0103`. The previous operative selected a trusted evaluator's grounded belief as provisional confidence when self-belief was inaccessible.
