## 3. Skill grows while self-control becomes a ceiling

Near-daily live-house battles produce real improvement and narrow wins. They also produce visible exhaustion. Akito notices the group's fatigue and continues to interpret the remaining gap as a demand for more force. Ken instead identifies a self-created ceiling: the same pressure that drove Akito's growth now prevents full use of what he already has. `PJSK:event:0109:03:002:0002–0017`; `PJSK:event:0109:03:004:0011–0058`.

One-to-one work initially intensifies the problem. Akito can now read Ken's cold precision as labor-built heat and understand it more deeply than he could as a novice. Repetition alone changes neither the sound nor Ken's response. `PJSK:event:0109:04:004:0027–0063`.

The risk remains material. Training extends from morning through school, external events, and late practice. Ken calls limits and provides water or a drive, but Akito still wants rest time converted into more practice. Akito cannot compress it into one clean answer; Ken treats that uncompressed response as the needed material and tells him he already possesses it. `PJSK:event:0109:05:003:0015–0053`.

Akito then seeks Street-singer help. Luka hears technical power but also unused pleasure, anticipation, gratitude, and tension. She answers in her own strongest medium: a musical exchange built from songs tied to Akito's history. Luka obtains a partial change, then deliberately stops so the decisive address can remain Akito's own exchange with Ken. `PJSK:event:0109:06:003:0063–0108`.
