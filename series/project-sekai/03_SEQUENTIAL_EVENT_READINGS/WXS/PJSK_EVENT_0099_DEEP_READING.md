## 3. Complete causal envelope

### 3.1 Rui's initial model makes interest conditional on show work

Rui expects limited value from a trip where shows and prop construction are unavailable. Tsukasa reframes the school trip as a once-only performance, giving Rui an initial analogy that makes participation imaginable. The analogy prompts interest; it does not itself produce the later friendship. `PJSK:event:0099:01:002:0010–PJSK:event:0099:01:002:0023`.

Rui assumes nobody will choose him for a group despite increased class contact since the sports festival. Miyake, Hayashi, and Taniyama overturn that expectation by approaching from reciprocal interest in gadgets, film, and anime. Rui accepts because the invitation itself matters to him. `PJSK:event:0099:01:003:0013–PJSK:event:0099:01:003:0038`.

### 3.2 Ordinary shared time demonstrates compatibility without a production goal

At the temples, Rui's speculative show thinking receives curiosity and humor rather than rejection. At the inn, the four classmates share cards, teasing, and mischief; Tsukasa joins without treating social categories as barriers. `PJSK:event:0099:02:002:0002–PJSK:event:0099:02:003:0072`.

The relation is reciprocal. The classmates gain pleasure and recognition from Rui's interests, while Rui experiences unstructured travel and play as worthwhile rather than as downtime around performance.

### 3.3 A mixed group extends the trip beyond Rui's class

Rui and Tsukasa encounter Shizuku, Airi, and Mafuyu and choose a shared excursion. Shopping decisions are recipient-specific: Mafuyu chooses oil-blotting paper for Ena, Airi considers gifts, Tsukasa looks for Saki's gift, and Shizuku connects him to star-like konpeito. `PJSK:event:0099:03:002:0001–PJSK:event:0099:03:003:0089`; `PJSK:event:0099:04:001:0001–PJSK:event:0099:04:003:0021`.

Shizuku recounts Tsukasa resolving a childhood disagreement by inventing an activity that let both sides participate. Rui interprets Tsukasa's pleasure in his new friendship as a broader rule: a friend's happiness can be experienced as one's own. `PJSK:event:0099:05:001:0015–PJSK:event:0099:05:001:0064`.

### 3.4 Rui chooses a shared finale over passive convenience

When Rui and Shizuku become separated, waiting until the parade ends would make reunion easier. Rui rejects that passive solution because he wants the same people to reach the finale together. `PJSK:event:0099:06:001:0070–PJSK:event:0099:06:001:0097`.

Rui combines Shizuku's white haori, her controlled public performance, and his snow device to enter the parade and become visible. The improvised snow-woman appearance succeeds as a reunion signal. `PJSK:event:0099:06:001:0098–PJSK:event:0099:06:001:0107`; `PJSK:event:0099:07:001:0001–PJSK:event:0099:07:001:0050`.

Show craft serves the chosen relationship aim in this crisis. It does not define the relationship's ordinary basis or establish performance as a necessary friendship mechanism.

Rui initiates a group photograph because the shared time feels irreplaceable. Miyake later explains that pre-WxS Rui sometimes appeared bored or lonely, and that seeing him laugh with Tsukasa made approach seem possible. `PJSK:event:0099:07:001:0087–PJSK:event:0099:07:001:0097`; `PJSK:event:0099:08:002:0030–PJSK:event:0099:08:002:0048`.
