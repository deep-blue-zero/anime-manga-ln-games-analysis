## 3. Causal reading: admitting attachment into the decision

### 3.1 An ending can mark success and still hurt

The promotional performances end because they have achieved their attendance purpose and the park now needs WxS present for visitors who want to see them. The administrative explanation is not a judgment of failure (`PJSK:event:0074:01:003:0021–0052`). Card 0526:02 supplies Emu's corresponding interpretation: the park's renewed liveliness matters because the four helped protect its smile-giving purpose. She can feel sad that the tour has ended and recognize the success that makes its ending possible (`PJSK:card:0526:02:002:0010–0040`).

Rui enters with another possible ending already in mind. Recalling Nene's thought that eventual separation could mean progress toward their dreams, he resolves to do what he can while they are together. His opening impulse is to show the group his new portable projection device immediately. Shared pleasure and artistic invention already belong together in his present life (`PJSK:event:0074:01:001:0002–0026`). The later professional choice threatens this concrete relationship, not merely a vague dislike of change.

The exchange is also not imposed as a punishment. Rui recognizes its educational value and wants it himself (`PJSK:event:0074:01:003:0062–0080`). This matters causally: the opportunity that unsettles him arises through pursuing a real artistic good.

### 3.2 Acceptance outside the group makes departure a credible option

Rui's concern about whether outsiders will accept his direction is qualified by prior successful encounters. He does not enter believing that literally everyone outside WxS rejects him (`PJSK:event:0074:02:001:0010–0018`). Asahi then greets him as an admirer of his earlier Miles production. Professional prestige becomes interested attention rather than the anticipated distance (`PJSK:event:0074:02:003:0032–0049`).

The exchange validates more than unusual technical spectacle. Actors test the five-metre pole entrance, discuss safety equipment and improve its expressive execution. Rui observes an unfamiliar degree of receptive, technically capable collaboration (`PJSK:event:0074:04:002:0004–0041`). Nene learns embodied military movement, Emu participates in acrobatic exchange, and Tsukasa attends to Asahi's acting and audience visibility (`PJSK:event:0074:04:002:0050–0089`). The external group can support the same seriousness and adventurousness that make WxS valuable to Rui.

This is why a simple hostile-world/safe-group account would fail. The offered world is hospitable and potentially excellent for his work. The story requires him to decide between goods rather than escape a clearly bad employer.

### 3.3 Shared purpose deepens professional affinity without making experience identical

Over dinner, Rui describes his admiration for Tom Gray's capacity to bring people divided by social circumstances into shared feeling. Asahi explains why that aim matters personally to him (`PJSK:event:0074:04:003:0021–0041`). His account of armed conflict, separation from his parents, refuge amid language barriers, a volunteer performance, and eventual reunion through a published photograph is **Asahi's self-report** to those at dinner (`PJSK:event:0074:04:003:0041–0081`). It is neither Rui's own childhood nor independent documentary evidence about a real external conflict.

Asahi's desired international charitable work remains a future goal attached to professional preparation; it is not already accomplished (`PJSK:event:0074:04:003:0082–0098`). Tsukasa infers that Asahi's experience helps explain a particular performance's force. That local inference does not establish that trauma is necessary for artistic depth or explains all of Asahi's ability. Shared artistic values allow affinity without erasing different histories.

The earlier joking invitation becomes a concrete private recruitment offer only later. Asahi proposes involvement compatible with Rui's schooling first and hopes for a fuller future role. He wants a director with whom he can keep growing; the offer is not merely praise without possible consequences (`PJSK:event:0074:05:004:0008–0033`). It is also not a binding employment outcome.

### 3.4 The bird supplies evidence that success does not settle every want

The bird's flying-train idea has practical constraints: the train cannot be controlled as required, wires introduce entanglement risk, and the others cannot fly or are afraid. Rui has considered and rejected an unsafe implementation. The bird chooses a solo performance to achieve a vision the group cannot presently realize (`PJSK:event:0074:02:001:0025–0040`).

Its exhaustion and loneliness do not make that wish false. The bird explicitly enjoys realizing its own imagined performance; Miku recognizes the achievement while caring about its cost (`PJSK:event:0074:06:002:0016–0049`). Card 0525:01 adds that it wants audiences to experience the feeling of flight and does not want friends endangered. Miku listens and refrains from pressing her own wish for group participation (`PJSK:card:0525:01:001:0043–0063`). Individual creation is not framed as mere selfish rebellion.

Rui recognizes his own wish to remain with WxS through this situation. When he tells Miku about his dilemma, he still calls attachment childish and treats career direction as the more legitimate demand. Miku grants both wants importance, including their importance to his future, without supplying a ready practical solution (`PJSK:event:0074:06:002:0030–0039`, `PJSK:event:0074:06:002:0053–0074`). Her function is to widen what can count in a decision, not to order him to stay.

The cards complete the bird's independent sequence. Care is not conditional on abandoning the solo show. After accomplishing it, the bird develops a new wish for a shared performance with changed staging. Wires and a moving background produce a different show rather than secretly solving the original flying-train design (`PJSK:card:0525:02:001:0012–0045`; `PJSK:card:0522:02:002:0030–0072`). This supplies an event-local analogy for revisable means and plural desires. It does not prove that Rui has already found the human institutional equivalent.

### 3.5 Performance reveals that the present group is also a site of growth

Rui initially postpones final role allocation because he does not yet know Asahi well enough. Alternating the roles makes judgment answerable to observed possibilities (`PJSK:event:0074:03:002:0010–0016`). Later he selects Asahi for Alfred's subtle emergence of feeling; Tsukasa accepts Bartlett while recognizing the skill involved (`PJSK:event:0074:05:001:0066–0083`). The allocation is not evidence that Rui values Tsukasa less as a person or that Tsukasa has failed out of the group.

Teaching derives movement and speech from the imagined android's purpose. Tsukasa revises initially flat delivery because a machine built for human communication needs legible expression (`PJSK:event:0074:03:003:0031–0053`). Rui then gives Asahi a demanding affective direction: hold abundant feeling inward so a small outward emergence becomes perceptible. Asahi's readiness to attempt more depends on the particular director's guidance and trust (`PJSK:event:0074:05:003:0011–0034`).

During the performance, Tsukasa applies that principle in his own part, surprising Asahi. Rui sees the four respond to direction and advance their own dreams through the show (`PJSK:event:0074:07:002:0126–0147`). Remaining with WxS is therefore not necessarily the suspension of artistic growth. The practical question is how that shared development could coexist with the scale of everyone's aspirations.

The performed play's rejection of simply erasing Bartlett's ten years parallels the problem of treating valuable existing experience as disposable (`PJSK:event:0074:07:002:0112–0125`). This is structural interpretation of fiction staged within the event. Its android memory mechanism is not an assertion about actual human psychology or Virtual Singer ontology.

### 3.6 The ending authorizes a search, not a guaranteed reconciliation

Rui initially says the professional case for joining is compelling. Asahi notices the emotion that this reasoning does not resolve and recognizes how important the current group is to him (`PJSK:event:0074:08:001:0002–0045`). Asahi's response preserves an invitation at graduation and the hope of meeting on a world stage (`PJSK:event:0074:08:001:0047–0051`). The exchange ends without casting him as a defeated rival or proving that the opportunity had no real value.

Rui subsequently uses Miku's language of both wants mattering when the group considers continued outside growth alongside park obligations (`PJSK:event:0074:08:003:0009–0028`). His private ending remains less settled than a triumphant solution: he doubts whether he could bear eventual separation and still judges his wish harshly, but commits to searching for a way to pursue his dream together with the others' dreams (`PJSK:event:0074:08:003:0035–0048`). No permanent membership promise, agreed career plan, or abolition of fear is established.

## 4. Care and learning under incomplete knowledge

**Nene and Emu perceive distress without knowing its cause.** Emu notices Rui's altered mood; Nene connects his evasive manner with a familiar tendency not to say what troubles him. They offer to listen and respect his lack of readiness (`PJSK:event:0074:06:001:0061–0098`). Their planned conversation before the performance still lacks the offer's content (`PJSK:event:0074:07:002:0017–0025`). After the private decision discussion, Nene explicitly knows only that he seems better, not what happened (`PJSK:event:0074:08:002:0004–0020`).

Card 0522:01 sharpens Nene's method. She makes space during cleanup to tell him he need not disclose the reason, while worrying that saying not to worry could itself burden him. Her reassurance models the recipient's likely experience rather than requiring an explanation as the price of support (`PJSK:card:0522:01:001:0013–0039`). His enthusiasm for underwater, flying and other future shows then gives concrete expression to wanting this collaboration to continue (`PJSK:card:0522:01:001:0041–0055`). Enthusiastic proposals and comic escalation are not records of every proposed stunt being performed unsafely.

**Nene's reserve coexists with purposeful initiative.** She wants advice but misses an opening, prepares honey-lemon as a reciprocal conversational approach, and ultimately asks directly. Emu encourages her without requiring prior disclosure of the specific wish (`PJSK:card:0524:01`). The preparation's health rationale is a claim within the story, not a medical finding. Later Nene identifies an important information limit: while turned away in the blocking, she cannot see another actor's expression. She deliberately asks observers and uses their feedback to improve her own response (`PJSK:card:0524:02:002:0040–0068`). A convincing reconstruction must include this active attempt to obtain missing evidence.

**Tsukasa can accept a gap without translating it into hopelessness.** Asahi presents his own interpretation as an interpretation rather than the only correct answer. Tsukasa works on how remaining humanity should appear in an android's behavior and feels encouraged because he can now identify a direction for improvement (`PJSK:card:0523:01:002:0009–0044`). Asahi's notebook records collaborators and ordinary gestures; those observations explain a work practice, not a validated universal science of personality. His later praise recognizes what the WxS four contribute, preserving reciprocity despite differences in experience (`PJSK:card:0523:02:004:0015–0045`).

**Emu's imaginative register is usable creative material.** Tsukasa initially assumes familiar peers will need to cover her improbable improvisation. The visitors immediately add coherent, playful possibilities of their own. Rui has to prompt the surprised Tsukasa and Nene to join (`PJSK:card:0526:01:002:0021–0047`). The episode undermines a model in which Emu can only function inside a group that compensates for her. She and Nene can then name sadness about the exchange ending while committing to a good performance (`PJSK:card:0526:01:002:0048–0057`).
