## 3. Causal reading

### 3.1 Parallel support desires precede the bridge

The event does not begin with cross-SEKAI travel as an independent spectacle. Local manifestations first inspect what their humans now need. Wonderland singers recognize that the four have chosen a new path outside Phoenix Wonderland. Stage singers want MMJ to see that its accumulated work already delivers hope. School singers respond to Leo/need's professional start. Street singers respond to a period of severe effort. Empty Rin responds to reports that song helped Mafuyu and Ena. The common input is recipient-directed care, while each SEKAI translates that care into a different performance grammar. `PJSK:event:0105:01:002:0002–PJSK:event:0105:01:003:0028`; `PJSK:event:0105:02:001:0001–PJSK:event:0105:02:003:0030`.

This parallel construction strengthens `CR-PJSK-SEKAI-041`: convergent care is again the supported connection hypothesis. It remains a hypothesis rather than a complete mechanical law because no participant can deliberately reproduce or fully explain the phenomenon.

### 3.2 Song is modeled as accompaniment rather than substitution

School Miku tells Len that a song cannot directly solve someone's problem, but can shake, accompany, and help a recipient move. Len uses this to stop pursuing imitation as an end and to locate his performance in relation to Leo/need's needs. Empty Rin independently frames Kanade's song as warmth that enters loneliness and tries to sing in a way that carries that relation. `PJSK:event:0105:04:001:0011–0039`; `PJSK:event:0105:04:002:0002–0008`.

This is a major thematic statement but not a new human state. It is singer-side support practice that becomes causally legible when the humans later receive the performances.

### 3.3 EVENT_0054 perception becomes direct reciprocal contact

EVENT_0054 established an exceptional connective field where selected manifestations could briefly perceive counterparts. EVENT_0105 adds several independently visible capabilities:

- an interstitial observer's encouragement reaches Wonderland Miku as a voice;
- fragments appear in local SEKAI after convergent support activity;
- touching a fragment can move a manifestation into a temporary common field;
- the field can display multiple SEKAI;
- selected manifestations meet distinct counterparts and converse directly;
- the counterparts convey specific appreciation for local roles and performances;
- local manifestations return with the meeting remembered;
- those memories can be reported locally and used as later motivation.

The clearest direct evidence is the paired encounter sequence and its differentiated messages. Empty Rin receives praise for singing with feeling; Empty MEIKO receives recognition that presence can support without constant speech. Street KAITO is thanked for free, bright musical support. School Len is praised for leading through performance. Stage Luka is told her idol work can energize another observer. Wonderland Miku learns that her shows help someone outside her own SEKAI. `PJSK:event:0105:06:001:0011–0095`.

This warrants `EPI-PJSK-SEKAI-E0105`, a successor to `EPI-PJSK-SEKAI-E0054`. It does not warrant a relationship state between every counterpart pair because the contact is brief, involuntary, and not shown to continue.

### 3.4 Return preserves memory without creating a network

After the field disappears, the local manifestations remember the encounter and interpret it in their own contexts. Wonderland Miku tells KAITO. Street KAITO recognizes that support exists beyond his local group. Stage Luka reports the counterpart's message. School Len sings with renewed motivation. Empty Rin and MEIKO perform together. `PJSK:event:0105:07:001:0001–PJSK:event:0105:07:006:0016`.

The persistence of memory is stronger than EVENT_0054's momentary perception. Yet no one can reopen the field, contact the counterpart on demand, or guarantee another meeting. The cards repeatedly preserve uncertainty about recurrence. `PJSK:card:0707:02:001:0047–0066`; `PJSK:card:0710:01:001:0015–0038`.
