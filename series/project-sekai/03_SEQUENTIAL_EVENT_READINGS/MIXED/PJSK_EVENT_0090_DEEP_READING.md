Ena then identifies shortcomings in perspective, depth, shading and reflected light, while allowing that the fin-driven water movement is better than before. An and Shiho receive the peaceful, warm atmosphere she wanted to express. Ena can accept that success without retracting the defects. The second card makes the operational refinement explicit. Technique serves an expressive decision; copying every visible property is not always the best route to that decision. She also qualifies the favorable reception: both viewers had seen the same fish.
