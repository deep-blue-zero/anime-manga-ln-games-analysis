## 2. Admiration for perfection obscures what competence costs

Mafuyu recognizes that contribution while competently organizing club life (`PJSK:event:0080:01:001:0002–0025`). Their bond already contains reciprocal observation and useful work. The first disruption comes through an apparently encouraging career conversation. Shizuku has heard younger students report Mafuyu buying medical-school reference books; she treats becoming a doctor as fitting a kind person. Her encouragement triggers Mafuyu's memory of her mother's words. Mafuyu returns to a socially plausible account of exam-related heaviness, but Shizuku recognizes a look she has seen before (`PJSK:event:0080:01:002:0024–0069`). The public account is not an autonomous vocational decision. Shizuku knows an observation and hears an explanation; she does not see the maternal memory supplied to the audience.

The long historical section explains why Shizuku once depended on the apparent completeness of Mafuyu's example. External branding becomes a task she applies to herself beyond work.

The older member also expresses care, wants every member to shine, recommends loosening up and suggests school clubs as enjoyable youth. She reports improved posture from archery but explicitly tells Shizuku to choose what suits her. Shizuku herself wanted to study; the manager recommended the school (`PJSK:event:0080:02:004:0016–0033`). Preserve these differentiated adults and peers. Mafuyu notices the effort implied by improving an already good posture, handles introductions fluently and learns the exercise quickly. ## 3. Archery offers a usable practice, not an invulnerable identity

Neither Airi's complete managerial dispute nor a subsequent successful new contract is disclosed here.

A month later, Shizuku can send arrows forward but still has not hit the target; she considers stopping after the competition so she will not burden instructors. Mafuyu arrives for morning practice she began a month earlier. The changed attentional task, technical practice and Shizuku's effort together explain the result (`PJSK:event:0080:03:004:0051–0088`). This is not a general clinical treatment claim. It is a locally effective practice within these characters' activity.

At the newcomer competition Shizuku hits, feels she can contribute and credits the help. Mafuyu places fourth; peers joke about her becoming a future captain, which is not an appointment.
