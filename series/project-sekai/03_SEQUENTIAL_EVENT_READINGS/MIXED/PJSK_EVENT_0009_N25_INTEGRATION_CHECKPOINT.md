# Project SEKAI — EVENT_0009 N25 Integration Checkpoint
## セカイのハッピーニューイヤー！ — from projected companionship to enacted ordinary social life

## 1. Analytical responsibility and triage decision

`EVENT_0009 — セカイのハッピーニューイヤー！` is a franchise-wide New Year event whose N25-bearing material is concentrated in a small, clearly bounded subset of the complete release envelope. It therefore receives a **focused N25 integration checkpoint**, not a whole-event literary deep reading.

This is not a downgrade of significance. For N25, EVENT_0009 is classified:

- **Tier B — Relationship**;
- **I3 — Relationship-state-changing release**.

The event does **not** produce a new global psychological state for Kanade, Mafuyu, Ena, or Mizuki. Its durable change lies elsewhere: it converts the ordinary-life companionship that EVENT_0007 could only imagine and negotiate into an **actually enacted social practice**. Nightcord gathers voluntarily for a seasonal celebration with no production emergency and no crisis requiring intervention. The Empty SEKAI correspondingly gains a new function: it is no longer only a manifestation of Mafuyu's interiority, a crisis site, or a place used to search for feelings. It can also serve as a shared social environment into which the members bring food, decorations, clothes, gifts, jokes, and ordinary ritual.

The governing relationship transition is:

> **`REL-N25-G-4 → REL-N25-G-5 — enacted ordinary-life companionship / shared seasonal-social container`.**

A second bounded relationship entry is warranted for the human group and N25 Miku:

> **`REL-N25-GM-E0009 — reciprocal ordinary care / shared-space warmth`.**

EVENT_0009 also materially strengthens Ena↔Mizuki as a reciprocal ordinary friendship. EVENT_0007 showed that Mizuki wanted Ena present in ordinary life; EVENT_0009 shows Ena herself initiating a New Year's shopping outing with Mizuki and relying on Mizuki as a coordinated partner.

### Why I3 without a new human character state

The release-impact vocabulary defines I3 as a durable **character, relationship, epistemic, or governing-model** transition. EVENT_0009 changes future response distributions at the group/context level:

- N25 can now plausibly gather for ordinary social reasons without needing work or crisis as justification;
- Mizuki has demonstrated an adaptive invitation strategy that preserves other members' constraints;
- Ena has demonstrated reciprocal voluntary ordinary-life initiative toward Mizuki;
- the Empty SEKAI can function as an ordinary gathering place rather than only an exceptional psychological site;
- N25 Miku is now explicitly a recipient of mundane care from all four members and conceives herself as waiting there for the whole group;
- seasonal objects materially accumulate in the SEKAI as signs of relationship history.

Those are durable relationship/context changes even though the four human psychological state IDs remain preserved.

## 3. Inherited pre-state from EVENT_0007

EVENT_0007 ended with an important but still partly **projected** social development.

Mizuki had begun to model N25 as people who might participate in ordinary life together. During the Kamiyama festival Mizuki repeatedly imagined how Kanade, Mafuyu, and Ena would react to attractions, then explicitly proposed that all four attend a future festival. Kanade showed interest, Ena allowed that she might go, and Mafuyu conditionally agreed. Mizuki privately considered helping with future festival preparations partly so that the others could enjoy themselves.

This produced `REL-N25-G-4`:

> **creative/candid-affect container + embodied chosen group + projected mundane future companionship**.

The unresolved question was whether this was merely Mizuki's aspiration or a genuinely available group mode.

EVENT_0009 answers that question by putting the group into an ordinary seasonal situation and requiring no crisis to hold them together.

## 4. Episode 02 — Mizuki wants ordinary company and adapts to constraints

The N25 section begins during ordinary Nightcord work. After the day's production activity winds down, Mizuki asks whether the others have plans the following day and begins proposing that they go somewhere together.

The first proposal fails immediately:

- **Ena** refuses because it is cold and she does not want to go outside.
- **Kanade** likewise says cold makes her unable to move.
- **Mafuyu** has winter cram-school classes and expects her availability to be constrained.

This scene is valuable precisely because the refusals are mundane. Nobody is rejecting N25 as a relationship. Nobody is in acute crisis. Their preferences and obligations simply do not align with Mizuki's first plan.

Mizuki also reveals why the invitation matters. An anime-song countdown live that they had hoped to attend fell through in the lottery. Their parents are abroad visiting Mizuki's older sister, leaving Mizuki without the family New Year context that might otherwise structure the holiday. Mizuki says they had hoped that **the N25 members** might be available.

This strengthens EVENT_0007 rather than repeating it: ordinary companionship has become an active want rather than merely a hypothetical image.

### 4.1 The important behavior is adaptation, not persistence alone

Mizuki does not respond to the first refusal by:

- guilting Ena or Kanade;
- dismissing their dislike of cold;
- demanding that Mafuyu skip an obligation;
- interpreting refusal as relational rejection;
- abandoning the desire for social contact entirely.

At a café the next day, hearing other people discuss a countdown party gives Mizuki a new idea. The problem is not “how do I make everyone accept my original plan?” but “what form of gathering satisfies the actual constraints?”

That yields a useful D0 reconstruction rule:

> **When Mizuki wants shared activity and others reject the first format for concrete reasons, Mizuki may preserve the social goal while redesigning the environment around those constraints.**

This is consistent with EVENT_0007's broader autonomy model. Inclusion is more likely to work when participation does not require people to surrender what matters to them.

## 5. Episode 03 — the Empty SEKAI becomes a chosen social venue

Mizuki's revised proposal is simple: hold the countdown party in the Empty SEKAI.

This solves several problems at once:

- Ena and Kanade do not need to travel through the cold;
- Mafuyu can join after cram school without a conventional outing structure;
- N25 Miku can participate;
- the group can gather without depending on anyone's home or family situation.

Ena accepts under the changed conditions. Kanade agrees and explicitly notes that she would like to see Miku. Mafuyu says that although New Year's Day will be occupied by visiting relatives, the evening should be possible. Mizuki volunteers to handle preparation.

The event thereby converts a psychologically exceptional location into social infrastructure.

### 5.1 They arrive to play, not to rescue

N25 Miku's opening reaction is especially important. She notes that the others have not been visiting much recently and considers that their absence may actually be a good sign. When the four appear, Ena reassures her that there is no crisis: **they came to play**.

That one fact materially changes the meaning of the Empty SEKAI.

Earlier functions remain valid:

- manifestation of Mafuyu's inner state;
- site of crisis encounter;
- setting for affect/search work;
- place where Miku can mirror or mediate without omniscience.

EVENT_0009 adds another function rather than replacing those:

> **chosen ordinary gathering place.**

This is a relationship/context transition because future appearances in the Empty SEKAI can no longer be reconstructed as inherently crisis-coded.

### 5.2 Everyone contributes

Although Mizuki initially says they will prepare everything, the others independently arrive with contributions:

- **Mizuki:** party snacks and organizational initiative;
- **Ena:** expensive fruit-parlor juice from a family year-end gift;
- **Kanade:** limited seasonal roast-chicken chips;
- **Mafuyu:** plates, cups, and stylish napkins she thought would be useful that day.

This matters more than the objects themselves. The party is not one member dragging three reluctant people into a personal project. Once the plan becomes compatible with their constraints, all four participate in making the occasion work.

Mafuyu's contribution is particularly useful low-stakes evidence. She does not merely show up after being asked. She anticipates a practical party need, selects unused household items, brings them, and explains that she thought they would be used today. This is modest but real prosocial initiative in an ordinary context.

Ena likewise shifts from immediate refusal of the original outing to supplying a contribution once the format is acceptable. Kanade brings something of her own and treats the event as novel enough to feel slightly awkward or “ticklish,” but does not withdraw from it.

### 5.3 Miku names the transformation

N25 Miku observes that everyone is enjoying themselves and expresses relief.

At the year transition she internally contrasts the present with the origin of the space: this was once a SEKAI where nobody but Mafuyu existed. Now, because of everyone, enjoyable things can happen there. She allows that difficult things may still happen in the future and then resolves that she will continue waiting there for everyone.

This should not be overread into omniscience or ownership of the group's future. Its importance is relational and spatial:

- Miku now understands the Empty SEKAI as a place belonging to the continuing relationship among multiple people, not only as Mafuyu's isolated interior manifestation;
- “waiting” becomes a stable availability relation rather than an emergency summons;
- continuity is imagined through both future difficulty and future ordinary presence.

## 6. Card 0152 — Ena's ordinary-life world expands around N25

`PJSK:card:0152 — 東雲家の年末` is the most important associated story for this checkpoint because it shows the event's social consequences from Ena's side rather than only through Mizuki's initiative.

### 6.1 Part 1 — Ena chooses to bring something

Before the party, Ena recalls Mizuki saying they will prepare everything. Her immediate private response is that showing up empty-handed would be inappropriate. She therefore goes looking through the Shinonome family's year-end gifts for something suitable and ultimately chooses two bottles of high-quality juice.

This is ordinary reciprocal investment. Ena's acceptance of the party is not passive attendance.

The family scene with Akito supplies useful baseline evidence without changing Ena's global state:

- sibling bickering is easy and habitual;
- they share snacks and tea;
- Ena can coordinate household tasks while complaining about them;
- Akito recognizes her habits and motives readily;
- Ena refers matter-of-factly to a countdown party with her circle.

Most importantly, after reflecting on a year that included Mafuyu, the SEKAI, and many surprising developments, Ena concludes that it **may not have been such a bad year after all**.

That is not evidence that her artistic-worth vulnerability is resolved. It is a relational/affective revaluation: N25's difficult year can nevertheless be retrospectively valued as part of a life containing worthwhile attachment and experience.

### 6.2 Part 2 — Ena invites Mizuki

On New Year's morning, Ena and Mizuki are together at a shopping mall for the first sales.

This reverses the direction of ordinary-life initiative seen in EVENT_0007 and early EVENT_0009. Mizuki points out that Ena rejected the previous invitation because it was cold but then invited Mizuki to this outing. Ena's answer is revealingly practical: this mall keeps the queue indoors and makes it easy to move between shops.

Again, the issue is not simple sociability versus antisociability. Ena has context-specific constraints and preferences.

The two then operate as a highly competent pair:

- jointly plan store priority;
- divide targets;
- purchase desired items for each other;
- send rapid updates when stock changes;
- trust one another's fashion/product understanding;
- reunite to divide the results.

This justifies strengthening the Ena↔Mizuki relationship from “easy banter with serious trust layer” into **reciprocal ordinary-life friendship with practiced coordination**.

### 6.3 Ena's mundane care toward Mafuyu survives antagonism

An incorrectly sized item leads Mizuki to suggest that it might suit Mafuyu. Ena's response is not rejection of the idea; she wonders whether Mafuyu would accept it.

They visit the Empty SEKAI. Mafuyu has arrived after a family relatives' gathering and looks visibly more gloomy than usual. Ena notices and offers a change of activity through clothes. Mafuyu initially refuses. Later Ena complains that the clothes suited Mafuyu but Mafuyu ultimately would not take them, implying that the interaction proceeded far enough for Ena to see Mafuyu wearing them.

This is not reconciliation in the strong sense. Ena remains abrasive and the Mafuyu relationship remains antagonistic. But it confirms an important rule:

> **Ena's antagonism does not exclude mundane care, aesthetic attention, or attempts at low-pressure diversion.**

The source therefore strengthens `REL-N25-EM-3` without creating a new dyad state ID solely from EVENT_0009.

### 6.4 Airi remains a bounded cross-unit ordinary relationship

The card ends with Ena receiving a New Year's message from Airi and warmly proposing that they shop together again. This is useful evidence for Ena's broader social world but should remain bounded until MMJ and cross-unit relationship infrastructure is developed. It does not require an N25-state change.

## 12. Theme and motif integration

### 12.1 Empty space → furnished relational space

The Empty SEKAI begins as radical absence. EVENT_0009 does not erase that ontology but overlays it with accumulated human traces: food, tableware, decorations, clothing, blanket, scarf, socks, gloves.

The material change mirrors relational change:

> **absence → visitation → crisis work → shared ritual → accumulated comfort objects**.

### 12.2 Warmth

Area `_005` makes warmth explicit rather than merely metaphorical. Miku learns the concept through objects given by four different people. Warmth therefore functions simultaneously as:

- physical sensation;
- care behavior;
- material evidence of repeated visits;
- contrast with the Empty SEKAI's original affective/spatial barrenness.

This motif should remain N25-specific until other unit foundations are analyzed.

### 12.3 Seasonal ritual and continuity

A New Year's countdown is structurally about continuity across a boundary. Here it becomes a relationship statement: there may be future difficulty, but the group can expect continued presence. N25 Miku's “waiting” frames continuity without promising cure.

### 12.4 Ordinary life as evidence of recovery without cure

The event is valuable precisely because nobody is “fixed.” Mafuyu remains constrained, Kanade remains unusual around leisure, Ena remains abrasive, Mizuki remains sensitive to isolation, and the Empty SEKAI remains psychologically meaningful. Yet ordinary enjoyment is possible within those unresolved states.

That reinforces a governing analytical principle:

> **increased ordinary-life capacity is evidence of expanded behavioral possibility, not proof that the underlying pathology/conflict has disappeared.**
