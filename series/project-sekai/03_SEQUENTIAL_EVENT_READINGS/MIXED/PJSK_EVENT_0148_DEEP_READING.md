## 2. Rest begins as a choice negotiated around serious work

After Jam Fest, Shiho's excitement becomes repeated late practice and visible sleep loss. Shizuku notices without treating the work itself as meaningless. Her consultation with MORE MORE JUMP! and the Stage singers separates professional commitment from the need to protect capacity: concern may be voiced, but the decision remains Shiho's. Shizuku therefore offers a sibling outing rather than issuing an order. `PJSK:event:0148:01:001:0011–0024`; `PJSK:event:0148:01:003:0002–0004`; `PJSK:card:1029:01:001:0026–0055`.

The invitation matters because both sisters retain an unfinished childhood memory: a promised red-leaf trip was lost to rain, and Shiho has privately assumed that travel with Shizuku may no longer be possible. Shiho first protects practice time, then accepts after naming conditions and hearing a workable purpose. Her choice is not surrender to pressure or a rejection of ambition. It is a bounded decision that rest and family time can belong beside professional effort. `PJSK:event:0148:01:003:0055–0078`; `PJSK:card:1028:01:001:0061–0074`.

## 3. Planning remains enabling infrastructure, not the enemy

Saki, Tsukasa, and Shizuku begin with an overfilled collection of destinations. Shiho does not abandon structure; she turns it into a practicable route by choosing the red leaves as the purpose, adding a nearby café and grape farm, and checking movement and timing. She then makes a distinct decision to enjoy the trip she has helped make possible. `PJSK:event:0148:02:002:0021–0042`; `PJSK:event:0148:02:003:0002–0015`.

During travel, the others' excitement and inefficiency repeatedly activate Shiho's belief that she must keep everyone moving. That competence is real and useful, but her self-description also reveals the pressure built into it: responsibility can narrow attention until every delay looks like a planning error. The event does not condemn practical judgment. It tests whether structure can serve shared time without exhausting the value of that time. `PJSK:event:0148:04:001:0064–0076`.

The souvenir episode confirms the distinction. Shiho permits a detour, Shizuku independently finds delivery options and favorable terms, and Shiho can laugh at the outcome rather than reclaiming total control. Receiving someone else's competence expands the plan instead of destroying it. `PJSK:card:1028:02:001:0010–0019`; `PJSK:card:1028:02:001:0042–0057`.

## 4. Another person's attention reveals value efficiency would miss

Shizuku asks Shiho to look again at an ordinary streetscape. What first appears undifferentiated becomes a composition of homes, shops, paths, and individual moments that matter to the people living them. Shiho follows the offered perspective and relaxes into the scene. The change is perceptual and enacted: she does not merely agree that leisure is good, but begins attending to value not captured by destination or schedule. `PJSK:event:0148:05:001:0048–0079`.

At the grape farm, Saki and Tsukasa connect self-picked fruit to shared effort and remembered family pleasure. Shizuku's prior work experience makes her the competent teacher; Shiho accepts instruction and help, joins the activity, and later participates in the playful competition. The source therefore reverses neither sister's role permanently. It shows that Shiho can remain practical while allowing Shizuku to lead where Shizuku knows more. `PJSK:event:0148:06:001:0013–0029`; `PJSK:event:0148:06:001:0034–0058`; `PJSK:card:1029:02:001:0031–0065`.

## 5. Contingency is not automatically relational failure

Rain arrives at the red leaves—the same contingency attached to the childhood disappointment—and Shiho initially treats it as evidence that her plan failed. Shizuku, Saki, and Tsukasa do not deny the weather or pretend the intended view is unchanged. They notice rain-darkened color, sound, and light available only in the altered conditions. Shiho recognizes that they value each unfolding moment and reveal things her efficiency would otherwise cut away. `PJSK:event:0148:07:001:0021–0062`.

The later sunlight does not retroactively make the rain worthwhile. Both views are received, and Shiho's evaluative standard changes before the weather clears: a deviation need not equal failure when the people involved still find situated value together. She says she is glad she came, receives their thanks, and locates part of the result in their smiles. This supports a contingency-tolerant method, not a universal claim that disruption is beneficial. `PJSK:event:0148:07:001:0051–0087`.

On the return train, Shiho explicitly judges the trip and rest worthwhile after debut, thanks Shizuku, and allows the possibility of another shared trip and continued planning. Recognition, reinterpretation, changed action, and future continuation support I3. The future remains open rather than guaranteed. `PJSK:event:0148:08:001:0012–0056`.
