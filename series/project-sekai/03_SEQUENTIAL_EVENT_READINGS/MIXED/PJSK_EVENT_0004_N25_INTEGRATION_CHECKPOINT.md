# Project SEKAI — EVENT_0004 N25 Integration Checkpoint

## 1. Purpose and triage decision

This checkpoint records the N25-relevant analytical value of `EVENT_0004 — 走れ！体育祭！～実行委員は大忙し～` without pretending that the event is primarily an N25 story.

The full event envelope was screened before selecting analytical depth. Its central developmental arc belongs to Haruka and Emu: school participation, committee work, social belonging, shared effort, and the relationship between enjoyable experience and renewed hope. Mafuyu enters as a meaningful supporting participant, and the associated Mafuyu card plus linked area conversations substantially refine her post-`EVENT_0002` reconstruction model.

**Classification:** **Tier C — Characterization**, with secondary relationship and ordinary-life value.  
**Release impact:** **I2 — Interpretive refinement**.

The event does **not** justify a new global Mafuyu state. `MF-E0002-01` remains current. What changes is the precision of that state: EVENT_0004 demonstrates a route by which spontaneous positive affect can appear in embodied social activity before Mafuyu can identify its meaning, and it supplies an external observer who can sometimes perceive affective incongruence in Mafuyu's public mask.

The governing answer to “what is different after this event?” is therefore:

> We can now model Mafuyu's public presentation as **socially effective but not affectively opaque**, and we have direct evidence that **salient, genuine positive affect may emerge through activity and relationship before Mafuyu can convert it into explicit self-knowledge**.

That is an interpretive refinement, not a durable state transition.

## 3. N25-relevant core: the public mask meets an unusual observer

### 3.1 Mafuyu's public presentation remains highly functional

In `0004:04`, Mafuyu meets Emu as a school upperclassman. The surface behavior is exactly what the main-story model predicts from the school-facing “good girl” role:

- warm introduction;
- easy senpai politeness;
- socially correct encouragement;
- modest acceptance of praise;
- competent procedural guidance for the race;
- seamless response when friends call her academically and athletically “perfect.”

Ordinary peers do not flag anything as wrong. Honami, encountering Emu's nervousness, assumes Emu is simply intimidated by a popular, beautiful, high-performing upperclassman.

This matters because the mask is not a failed performance. It is **socially successful**.

### 3.2 Emu detects an affective mismatch rather than hidden biography

Emu reacts differently. She experiences Mafuyu's smile as frightening because it looks like a smile while somehow not feeling like one.

The source does not authorize an occult or omniscient “Emu can read souls” model. Emu does not know:

- Nightcord's crisis history;
- the Empty SEKAI;
- Mafuyu's family-developmental history;
- the internal mechanics of the good-girl adaptation;
- why Mafuyu's affect is difficult to access.

What she appears unusually sensitive to is **expression–affect incongruence**. The same presentation that ordinary classmates reward as admirable competence produces in Emu a bodily sense that something is off.

This gives the reconstruction project a useful observer-dependence rule:

> Mafuyu's public mask is convincing at the level of social convention, but it is not guaranteed to be affectively indistinguishable from spontaneous emotion to every observer.

The probability of detection therefore depends on the observer's perceptual/social style, not merely on Mafuyu choosing whether to “drop the mask.”

## 4. Competitive embodiment and spontaneous positive affect

The two-person three-legged race provides the strongest EVENT_0004 characterization evidence.

Mafuyu and Emu must coordinate bodies, pace, rhythm, and immediate tactical adjustment. The context is unlike school role-performance or Nightcord introspection: it requires action before extended self-explanation is possible.

They win. Mafuyu thanks Emu and attributes the victory partly to her. Emu's immediate internal reaction is that Mafuyu has just **properly/actually smiled**.

The inference should be conservative:

- the scene supports a spontaneous positive-affect leak;
- it does not prove Mafuyu has recovered stable ordinary enjoyment;
- Mafuyu herself does not immediately label the experience as happiness;
- the effect is context-bound and observer-mediated.

But in combination with EVENT_0002, it is analytically important. EVENT_0002 established that affect may exist before Mafuyu can name it. EVENT_0004 shows the same principle with **positive social/embodied affect**, not only pain, disgust, artistic resonance, or relief.

The source therefore strengthens the model:

> **Mafuyu's first-person access can lag behind her own enacted affect.**

Her body/behavior may instantiate a meaningful response before her explicit self-concept catches up.

## 5. Card 0127: salience before explanation

The Mafuyu card turns the event from a useful external observation into a genuine post-EVENT_0002 refinement.

### 5.1 Emu's difference becomes salient to Mafuyu

Mafuyu notices that Emu does not react to her like ordinary juniors. Archery-club juniors look at her with straightforward admiration; Emu becomes pale, trembles, and behaves strangely.

Mafuyu does not initially know what to make of this. She even jokes about whether something is on her face. Emu's absurd literal answer makes Mafuyu laugh, and Emu again interprets the resulting expression as “real-looking.”

The analytical point is not that Emu gets to certify which Mafuyu is “real.” It is that Emu's reaction **breaks the normal feedback loop that stabilizes the good-girl persona**. Most school interactions tell Mafuyu that the role is functioning exactly as intended. Emu's unease is discrepant data.

### 5.2 Mafuyu remembers what she was not asked to remember

Later at Nightcord, Mizuki asks generally about the sports festival. Mafuyu initially gives a generic, low-information answer. Yet when discussing her events she moves on her own to the unusual junior.

She describes Emu's fear responses as “a little fresh/new” because no one at school normally treats her that way. When Ena suggests Emu may simply be afraid of her, Mafuyu answers that this would be acceptable. Her private thought explains the likely attraction:

> Emu may not see her as the “good girl.”

This is not a declaration that Mafuyu likes Emu, nor a major relationship commitment. It is **salience**.

Kanade notices that difference before Mafuyu does. She points out that Mafuyu merely answered questions about the festival but seemed to volunteer the Emu story herself. Mafuyu denies deeper meaning; Kanade suggests she may simply not understand the meaning yet.

That exchange directly operationalizes EVENT_0002's affect-access model:

1. something stands out;
2. Mafuyu returns to it without fully deciding to;
3. another trusted observer notices the pattern;
4. Mafuyu cannot yet assign stable meaning;
5. uncertainty is allowed to remain open rather than being replaced by an externally authored answer.

This is precisely the kind of evidence useful for reconstruction without manufacturing a new state.

## 6. Linked areas: preference for unfiltered interaction

### 6.1 `areatalk_ev_shuffle_01_007`

Mafuyu directly asks why Emu sometimes seems frightened by her. Emu nearly tells the truth, then retreats to “I'm nervous.” Mafuyu responds that, with Emu specifically, she would be happy if Emu stopped worrying about senpai/kouhai conventions and simply talked **like herself**.

Emu again experiences the phrase “I'd be happy” as somewhat more genuine than Mafuyu's usual presentation.

This scene supplies two useful facts:

- Mafuyu notices and is curious about Emu's unusual response rather than merely enduring it;
- Mafuyu expresses a preference for Emu's unfiltered interaction style.

That preference should still be modeled modestly. It is a small, low-stakes relational choice, not proof of deep intimacy.

This matters because it shows the interaction persisting after the novelty of the race. The relationship can support ordinary school contact rather than existing only as a symbolic one-scene encounter.

## 7. Relationship model: Mafuyu ↔ Emu

EVENT_0004 is enough to initialize a **bounded cross-unit relationship state**, but not enough to call Emu a major Mafuyu attachment.

### Mafuyu → Emu

- initially treats Emu through competent senior-role conventions;
- notices that Emu responds unlike other juniors;
- becomes curious about that discrepancy;
- finds the possibility that Emu does not see her as the “good girl” acceptable or even implicitly relieving;
- volunteers Emu as a topic to Nightcord without immediately understanding why;
- asks Emu to speak in her ordinary self rather than over-regulating for hierarchy;
- permits/initiates future low-stakes contact.

### Emu → Mafuyu

- initially experiences Mafuyu's socially polished expression as frighteningly incongruent;
- can distinguish some spontaneous-looking smiles from the standard mask;
- wants Mafuyu to smile “for real,” but lacks the history needed to understand what that means psychologically;
- fear decreases enough to support ordinary interaction, but unease does not instantly vanish.

### Guardrail

Emu's sensitivity is evidence about **detection**, not authority over Mafuyu's identity. A later reconstruction should not let Emu tell Mafuyu what Mafuyu “really feels” merely because she can sometimes notice expression–affect mismatch.

## 8. Epistemic effects

### Mafuyu

Learns/infers:

- Emu reacts to her differently from most school peers;
- Emu may not interpret the school-facing “good girl” persona in the expected way;
- this discrepancy is salient enough to revisit voluntarily;
- she may have an interest or feeling before she understands its meaning.

Mafuyu does **not** learn why Emu detects the mismatch or acquire a stable label for what Emu means to her.

### Emu

Directly observes:

- the standard school smile can feel affectively wrong/incongruent;
- some smiles after shared action feel more spontaneous;
- some of Mafuyu's low-stakes “happy/glad” language can also feel more genuine.

Emu does **not** know the causal history behind these differences.

### Nightcord

Through Mafuyu's own account, the group learns that an unusual junior produced a response Mafuyu found “fresh/new.” Kanade notices the unsolicited topic shift and forms the reasonable hypothesis that the salience may have meaning Mafuyu has not yet identified. The group does not gain Emu's exact internal perception unless later disclosure establishes it.

## 9. Reconstruction-model consequences

EVENT_0004 adds several D0/D1 constraints for Mafuyu.

### 9.1 Public-mask detectability

**D0:** ordinary school peers can accept the polished presentation at face value while an observer like Emu experiences it as affectively incongruent.

**Rule:** do not model public-mask success as universal perceptual invisibility.

### 9.2 Positive affect may precede explicit appraisal

**D0:** shared embodied competition can produce a spontaneous-looking smile even when Mafuyu does not immediately report a stable positive preference.

**Rule:** when context generates action faster than reflective role management, allow genuine affective leakage without automatically promoting it into self-understanding.

### 9.3 Salience can precede meaning

**D0:** Mafuyu may voluntarily return to a person/event because it was unusual or “fresh” while sincerely denying that she knows why it matters.

**Rule:** `attention / memory / return-to-topic` can be evidence of latent significance without being equivalent to a conscious preference.

### 9.4 Unconventional feedback can be attractive because it bypasses the expected role

**D1:** Mafuyu appears interested in Emu partly because Emu's behavior does not reward or mirror the “good girl” role in the normal way.

**Rule:** people who respond unpredictably to the mask may become salient, but do not generalize that Mafuyu automatically likes anyone who rejects social convention.

### 9.5 Observer detection does not create epistemic ownership

**D0:** Emu can notice “not a real smile” while knowing almost nothing about why.

**Rule:** distinguish perceptual accuracy from explanatory accuracy.

## 10. Claim transitions

### C1 — Mafuyu's affect is impaired in access, not absent

**Transition:** `STRENGTHEN`.

EVENT_0004 supplies positive/embodied evidence to complement EVENT_0002's negative/aesthetic evidence. Mafuyu's behavior can carry affect before her explicit appraisal catches up.

### C2 — Mafuyu's public mask is socially effective but perfectly opaque

**Transition:** `REVISE`.

**Current form:** the mask is socially highly effective but not affectively undetectable to every observer. Detection is observer- and context-dependent.

### C3 — meaningful self-data must first appear as verbal self-report

**Transition:** `REJECT / STRENGTHEN prior rejection`.

Salience, memory, behavior, embodied response, and spontaneous expression may precede verbal categorization. They are evidence, not a substitute for first-person authority.

### C4 — a third party who detects mismatch thereby knows Mafuyu's true state

**Transition:** `REJECT`.

Emu detects incongruence but lacks causal knowledge. Perceptual sensitivity does not make her an omniscient interpreter.

## 11. Theme and motif delta

### Performed smile ↔ spontaneous smile

EVENT_0004 adds a recurring **real/performed smile** motif with unusually explicit observer language. Its function is not to establish a binary in which one facial configuration is the metaphysical “true Mafuyu.” It visualizes the gap between socially correct expression and affect that emerges with less deliberate role optimization.

This extends the N25 first-person-authority theme in an important direction:

> other people may notice that a performance is incongruent, but they still cannot author the final meaning of the feeling for Mafuyu.

### Activity as an affect-access route

The event also expands the creative-work model from EVENT_0002. Music/lyrics are not the only access route. Coordinated physical activity, competition, novelty, and unexpected social feedback can also produce self-relevant affect before explicit interpretation.

## 12. Why no new Mafuyu state

`MF-E0002-01` already describes a character who:

- has incomplete self-concept;
- can possess affect before understanding it;
- is learning to notice/externalize reactions;

EVENT_0004 supplies a new **domain and observer** for the same emerging mechanism. It makes the model better and broader, but it does not yet alter Mafuyu's goals, vulnerabilities, agency profile, coping architecture, or relationship center enough to justify `MF-E0004-*`.
