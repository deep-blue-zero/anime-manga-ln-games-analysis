## 2. Practical help begins with observation, permission, and the recipient's criteria

Saki first intervenes when Kanade appears unable to decline a persistent haircut-model solicitation, but immediately checks whether her intervention was welcome. Kanade says it helped and permits Saki's preferred form of address. The sequence distinguishes perceptive aid from entitlement: Saki acts under uncertainty, then returns interpretation authority to the person affected. `PJSK:event:0155:01:003:0027–0055`.

During the clothing search, Saki does not substitute her own taste for Kanade's. She elicits warmth, weight, maintenance, and simplicity requirements; redirects the search toward outdoor clothing; supports trying options; and helps Kanade complete a purchase that she says she might otherwise have abandoned or settled carelessly. Practical fit precedes aesthetic projection. `PJSK:event:0155:02:001:0010–0046`.

The direct channel is chosen rather than assumed. ## 3. A technically adequate image can still fail its intended expression

At the café, Saki asks Kanade to model for an art assignment on “shine.” Kanade accepts partly because Saki helped her, while Saki prepares multiple coordinated looks and explains fashion as a practice that can make an ordinary day feel good. Kanade recognizes the sincerity of that interest even though she does not adopt it as her own. `PJSK:event:0155:03:002:0052–0088`; `PJSK:event:0155:04:002:0023–0075`.

Saki consults Kohane about photography and receives concrete advice about location, composition, light, and the relation between theme and subject. Shizuku and Mizuki lend clothes rather than allowing lack of materials to collapse the planned variations. The first shoot nevertheless leaves Saki dissatisfied: the images are attractive, but the “shine” she intends is absent. Kanade recognizes the problem through her own experience of composition and chooses to stay with the process rather than treating completed photographs as sufficient. `PJSK:event:0155:05:004:0011–0054`; `PJSK:event:0155:05:006:0002–0070`.

This distinction is the event's central craft threshold. Execution quality and intended meaning are related but not identical; a creator must be able to detect their separation before additional technique can become useful.
