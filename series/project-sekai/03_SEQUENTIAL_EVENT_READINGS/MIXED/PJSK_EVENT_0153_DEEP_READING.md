# EVENT_0153 — Unfading wishing — care becomes durable when burden, grief, and choice can be named

New scoped I3 `REL-CROSS-AIRI-AKITO-E0153` reconstructs Airi's middle-school request that Akito keep watching Ena, confirms that the request changed his conduct, and turns their previously unspoken debts into reciprocal gratitude, encouragement, and usable ordinary contact. New franchise-epistemic I3 `EPI-PJSK-THOUGHT-SHARD-E0153` records a repeated local-SEKAI pattern in which cracked thought shards open temporary personal fields, personify an avoided self-position, and can alter the human owner's apparent body or accessible memory before return.

## 2. Airi's request changes Akito's decision without assigning him sole responsibility

The middle-school reconstruction begins with Akito close to disengaging from Ena's crisis. Her conflict with their father, isolation, discarded art supplies, and refusal of contact have made further intervention seem useless to him. Airi seeks Akito because Ena will not explain herself and Airi's idol work prevents the proximity she wants. She asks him to keep watching Ena, while explicitly calling the request selfish and acknowledging that family status does not erase burden. `PJSK:event:0153:04:002:0002–0008`; `PJSK:event:0153:04:003:0017–0045`.

Airi explains why Ena remains her closest friend and benefactor: Ena defended her during school ridicule, supported her idol dream even while suffering, and continued to give her strength. Akito receives a version of Ena's care that his conflict-saturated household view had not made legible. He then decides to stay involved rather than withdraw. The causal claim is narrow but explicit: Airi's account and request move Akito's choice; neither person becomes responsible for producing Ena's later recovery. `PJSK:event:0153:04:003:0047–0073`.

Present Akito separately recognizes that Airi continued contacting Ena and that both Nightcord and Airi mattered to Ena's movement forward. He wants to thank Airi, while Airi has independently carried fear that her request became an unfair obligation. The event therefore reconstructs two parallel private debts before letting either participant know the other shares one. `PJSK:event:0153:04:003:0076–0081`; `PJSK:event:0153:06:002:0013–0035`.

## 3. `REL-CROSS-AIRI-AKITO-E0153` — delegated care becomes reciprocal recognition

Airi thanks Akito for supporting her friend and apologizes for having imposed on him. Akito rejects the burden account: he says her words caused him to keep engaging with Ena, thanks Airi for the screen-mediated support she continued to give, and guarantees that she helped both siblings. This exchange confirms the inference left open under `REL-CROSS-ENA-AIRI-E0022`—Akito did remember and act on Airi's request—without transferring Ena's private Nightcord history to Airi or making either helper the author of Ena's agency. `PJSK:event:0153:06:002:0023–0050`.

They then recognize one another as valuable supporters of the same person and exchange encouragement for separate futures: Akito tells Airi that her idol work can strengthen people like Ena; Airi endorses Vivid BAD SQUAD's world-facing dream while acknowledging the difficulty. Later they coordinate unusually smoothly in sports hanetsuki. The combination of reconstructed causality, corrected burden inference, direct mutual gratitude, future-facing encouragement, and enacted ordinary cooperation creates a reusable relationship rather than a single historical handoff. `PJSK:event:0153:06:002:0051–0069`; `PJSK:event:0153:07:003:0019–0036`.

Create scoped I3 `REL-CROSS-AIRI-AKITO-E0153`. Its state is: **historic delegated concern for Ena + request-caused sibling re-engagement + independently carried gratitude and burden fear + direct correction and mutual credit + reciprocal encouragement for distinct public goals + comfortable ordinary cooperation**. It does not establish close or exclusive friendship, sibling substitution, an ongoing caretaker contract, complete knowledge of Ena, or responsibility for either career's outcome.

## 5. `MMJ-EP-HR-E0153` — family-directed guilt does not get to choose Haruka's vocation

The loss of outside work has become a family-information problem. Haruka's parents notice that she no longer asks them to record television appearances; she reports streaming and preparation while withholding the work drought because she fears making them worry again. One local radio offer is hopeful evidence, not proof that the booking crisis has ended or that concealment is now harmless. `PJSK:card:1057:01:001:0005–0022`.

A cracked shard draws Haruka, Stage MEIKO, and Stage KAITO into a personal field. A younger Haruka embodies the proposition that idol work inevitably makes her parents sad and that love for them should therefore require quitting. Haruka's own history gives that proposition force: early criticism, ASRUN comparison, Mai's injury, bodily inability to perform, parental leave and medical searching, retirement, her mother's tears, and both parents' permission to return. The source establishes real parental care and real Haruka guilt; it does not certify the younger self's equation of concern with vocational wrongness. `PJSK:card:1057:01:002:0007–0032`; `PJSK:card:1057:02:002:0028–0066`.

MEIKO and KAITO do not answer for her. They make first-person speech possible, after which Haruka refuses to quit, names her love for idols and their hope-bearing function, and chooses the harder aim of becoming an idol her parents can be proud of. The vow is a self-authored direction, not evidence that all future parental sadness will be preventable or that Haruka's only deficit is professional immaturity. `PJSK:card:1057:02:002:0071–0105`.

Create `MMJ-EP-HR-E0153` at I3: **detect family concern under professional strain; reconstruct the long guilt chain rather than hiding it from herself; distinguish parental love and worry from an externally mandatory exit; state the vocation she still wants; convert guilt into a bounded pride-directed aim; return with the conflict remembered**. Preserve `MMJ-EP-HR-E0142` as producer/public-challenge authority, `MMJ-EP-HR-E0094` as chosen-friendship authority, and `MMJ-EP-HR-E0023` as self-directed-rest/capacity authority.

Create bounded high-I2 `MMJ-REL-HARUKA-PARENTS-E0153` for **long-term worry + practical support during collapse + non-coercive permission to return + Haruka's gratitude and guilt + current protective concealment**. The evidence is principally Haruka's account; the parents do not receive the current work drought or her shard-field vow on page, so their present interpretation and response remain open.

## 6. `VBS-EP-AN-E0153` — keeping grief is chosen memory, not easy forgiveness

The coming America trip reactivates the false story that Nagi had simply remained abroad. A cracked shard then opens a personal field where other people disappear from An's photographs and a younger An offers relief through forgetting everyone who lied or left. The field progressively blocks access to faces, names, and RAD WEEKEND memories; Street Miku infers that the effect can operate because some part of An wanted that erasure. This is Miku's situated explanation, supported by An's later admission, not a universal formula for memory. `PJSK:card:1058:01:002:0007–0034`; `PJSK:card:1058:01:003:0011–0037`; `PJSK:card:1058:02:001:0007–0011`; `PJSK:card:1058:02:001:0060–0077`.

An cannot immediately choose between recovery and relief. She wants the memories and fears their pain at the same time. Rin answers an earlier failure to help by promising presence rather than deciding for her. With that support, An retrieves Nagi, Taiga, her father, the town, Vivid BAD SQUAD, the deception, and the collective horizon. `PJSK:card:1058:02:001:0078–0105`; `PJSK:card:1058:02:001:0118–0130`.

An then corrects the fragment's accusation of easy forgiveness. She names crying, anger, resentment, irreversible death, and continuing love together. She chooses to remember even the wish to forget because loss and Nagi's dream remain part of what she carries. Advance `VBS-EP-AN-E0129 → VBS-EP-AN-E0153` at I3: grief-aware pursuit and fear-aware rivalry now include **acknowledged erasure desire + supported first-person choice to recover painful memory + continuing anger without relational deletion + deliberate retention of grief as part of an informed collective future**. `PJSK:card:1058:02:001:0131–0148`.

Preserve `VBS-REL-AN-KEN-E0097` and `VBS-REL-AN-NAGI-E0097` at strong I2. The card clarifies An's continuing love and legacy choice but does not complete paternal repair, erase betrayal, restore Nagi, or require forgiveness as the price of moving forward.
