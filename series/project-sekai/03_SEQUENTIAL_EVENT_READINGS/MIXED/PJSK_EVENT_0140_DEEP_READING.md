A light unlike an ordinary feeling fragment appears; Miku interprets it as the SEKAI trying to communicate, and the six choose to touch it. The light separates them into fragmentary histories rather than a complete archive. `PJSK:event:0140:01:001:0007–0010`; `PJSK:event:0140:01:001:0029–0043`.

This is more structured and repeatable than EVENT_0105's accidental temporary contact, but the source still does not establish unrestricted travel or exhaustive access. `PJSK:event:0140:03:002:0002–0055`; `PJSK:event:0140:07:001:0052–0055`; `PJSK:event:0140:13:003:0091–0103`; `PJSK:event:0140:14:002:0003–0049`.
