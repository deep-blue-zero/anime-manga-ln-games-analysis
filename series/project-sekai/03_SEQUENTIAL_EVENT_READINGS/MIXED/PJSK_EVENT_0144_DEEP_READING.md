## 2. Nene chooses an unfamiliar leadership problem

Nene initially catches herself falling back into the habit of assuming that a difficult social task is beyond her. She accepts the cultural-festival committee because organizing a large production can teach her something useful for future shows. In a room of roughly fifty people, she then initiates contact rather than waiting for a familiar person to mediate. `PJSK:event:0144:01:001:0041–0070`; `PJSK:event:0144:02:001:0010–0024`.

Her proposal makes difference operational. A musical can use acting, music, visual art, costume, backstage work, and publicity, so students who do not share one specialty can still build a coherent stage. Nene accepts the leader role after stating that inclusive production logic; the event therefore begins from a chosen responsibility, not sudden universal sociability. `PJSK:event:0144:02:001:0054–0068`; `PJSK:event:0144:02:003:0002–0018`.
