## 3. Causal reading: becoming someone who can return hope

### 3.1 Admiration already helps, but its first career model is incomplete

The flashback does not begin with a uniformly defeated Minori. Chihiro has already observed that admiration for Haruka makes her more positive, and Minori interprets misfortune through hopeful possibilities. Yet this effect coexists with a limited model of becoming an idol: reproduce excellent singing and dancing, apply widely, perhaps be discovered. Her imagined recruitment by Haruka is a fantasy inside the conversation, not a prior encounter or an actual offer (`PJSK:event:0073:02:003:0008–0033`; `PJSK:event:0073:03:004:0012–0042`).

Chihiro supplies a different kind of attention. She notices that the chorus movement is simpler so spectators can participate; Minori had learned it without recognizing that design purpose (`PJSK:event:0073:03:004:0010–0011`). This anticipates the event's distinction between reproducing a visible form and understanding what it enables between people. Chihiro's usefulness does not depend on already sharing Minori's fandom: she attends partly to study dance and initially resists being called a fan (`PJSK:event:0073:03:003:0004–0009`; `PJSK:event:0073:03:006:0011–0014`).

The arena makes the burden of performance newly imaginable. Hearing varied demands, Minori asks whether she could perform before this many expectant people even if instantly accepted into ASRUN (`PJSK:event:0073:03:006:0020–0036`). Her concern is no longer only admission to the admired group. It now includes the capacity to respond to an audience.

### 3.2 The live encounter enlarges the dream while reducing easy confidence

Haruka's invitation to share an enjoyable interval and the audience's answering calls make Minori experience encouragement as an exchange. Chihiro's spontaneous enthusiasm independently shows that the performance can reach someone beyond an established fan. Minori recognizes the collective atmosphere as something produced by idol and audience together, while still identifying the performer's strenuous, attentive work at its center (`PJSK:event:0073:04:001:0005–0034`, `PJSK:event:0073:04:001:0052–0089`).

This recognition has two simultaneous consequences. Minori loses confidence that copying a dance makes her ready to be an idol, and wants more strongly to stand on that stage and give others hope (`PJSK:event:0073:04:001:0098–0128`). Neither consequence cancels the other. Haruka's close-range invitation becomes a concrete image of a future beside her (`PJSK:event:0073:04:001:0137–0161`). At this point the audience knows Minori's interpretation; it does not know that Haruka remembers Minori personally.

### 3.3 Serious commitment accepts uncertainty and asks for support

The family conversation supplies the decision rule behind later persistence. Minori distinguishes this request from her previous declarations, anticipates practical and financial burdens, and asks for help. Her father states that effort may never produce success and that he wants to protect her from disappointment. She does not defeat that uncertainty with a guarantee. She argues that refusing before trying would leave regret, whereas a serious attempt could make even failure intelligible to her (`PJSK:event:0073:05:002:0013–0034`).

Her father ultimately recognizes the decision as hers and offers support. Subsequent teacher-assisted lessons and physical training are described, but the cost of a training institution blocks that option (`PJSK:event:0073:05:002:0035–0046`). This is evidence of supported agency with material constraints. It does not establish either limitless family resources or a universally hostile family.

Present Minori then corrects her own premature conclusion that standing beside Haruka means the whole dream has been fulfilled. The more exact dream is to connect with an audience and give it hope for tomorrow (`PJSK:event:0073:05:002:0054–0068`). The first solo concert becomes a setting in which she can attempt that responsibility, while nervousness remains acknowledged (`PJSK:event:0073:05:004:0017–0027`).

### 3.4 Practiced technique becomes legible as a desire to reach people

The performance is not explained as a miraculous ability that appears under pressure. Airi identifies practiced gaze movement and large gestures that make Minori visible across the room; the associated training card specifies distant audience stand-ins and additional practice requested by Minori (`PJSK:event:0073:06:001:0086–0088`; `PJSK:card:0519:02:003:0007–0019`, `PJSK:card:0519:02:004:0014–0039`). In the core, MEIKO resists Len's initial suggestion that Minori might simply be strong in live situations (`PJSK:event:0073:06:001:0077–0081`).

Nor does Minori's success render the experienced three passive supporters. Airi, Shizuku and Haruka respond competitively as performers. Their internal perspectives and later spoken feedback establish that being pleased for Minori is compatible with wanting to meet and exceed the standard she sets (`PJSK:event:0073:06:001:0089–0101`; `PJSK:event:0073:08:001:0013–0020`). The competition expands shared performance rather than threatening membership.

Fans provide several kinds of evidence, not an omniscient verdict. Yuina compares this performance with what she has watched before; other fans infer unseen effort from improved skill; former idols recognize mutual trust and renewed commitment in the group's concert. Those reports support observed reception. They do not establish that every spectator thinks identically or that all professional inequalities have vanished (`PJSK:event:0073:06:001:0015–0023`, `PJSK:event:0073:06:001:0047–0051`, `PJSK:event:0073:06:001:0082–0085`).

### 3.5 Public gratitude recognizes dependence without surrendering agency

Minori's speech names the people whose contributions made the concert possible: peers, discreetly described idol seniors, practical helpers, and fans. She explicitly recognizes that an idol receives hope as well as gives it. Fan encouragement matters while she still believes she has much to learn; gratitude does not require a finished or invulnerable self (`PJSK:event:0073:07:001:0018–0061`).

Haruka answers publicly that Minori has given her courage and hope. Her card adds the private comparison with Minori's earlier role in bringing her back to performance, and her deliberate use of the invitation to continue together (`PJSK:card:0518:02:003:0023–0038`). The relationship is therefore simultaneously aspirational, reciprocal, and professionally collaborative. Minori still calls Haruka a lifelong goal. Mutual recognition does not erase the asymmetry of admiration.

The mistaken exit extends the response beyond a prepared stage interaction. A child names Minori as the idol she wants to become, and Minori welcomes that hoped-for future (`PJSK:event:0073:08:001:0057–0063`). This repeats the form of the earlier invitation while changing Minori's position within it. It is evidence that the performed hope reached at least this child, not proof of a guaranteed future career for either person.

### 3.6 Fulfillment opens a larger problem of reach

Requests for recordings, regional events, tours and television exposure distinguish the concert's success from the group's capacity to distribute that experience. Airi explicitly says there is no recording-release plan. Later, alone with online responses, Minori wants the group to reach more people and recognizes that fans share that wish (`PJSK:event:0073:08:001:0048–0056`; `PJSK:event:0073:08:002:0016–0056`).

The prospective endpoint is a personally articulated expansion of ambition. It is not a group decision, an agency contract, a booked tour, or an accomplished mass audience. Card 0517's two fans could not afford this concert; their enthusiasm through a friend's report preserves an additional access limit that enthusiasm alone has not removed (`PJSK:card:0517:02:001:0026–0036`).

## 4. Relational and professional evidence beyond the climax

**Airi's care includes design, instruction, observation and restraint.** A change in equipment position improves sightlines but increases travel distance. She explains the problem to Minori through the performance sequence, distinguishes the group's desired showcase from what audiences enjoy, and proposes extending an interlude while obtaining the needed permission (`PJSK:card:0519:01:002:0017–0055`). She does not discard artistic quality reflexively; she searches for a solution preserving it alongside access. Separately, she initially suspects anxiety behind Minori's request for practice, then observes her actual manner and chooses not to introduce unnecessary self-consciousness (`PJSK:card:0519:02:004:0021–0034`). That is an explicitly bounded revision of a local inference.

**Shizuku's responsibility reaches the objects and people surrounding performance.** Her former group's leader once taught her merchandise checks; she now passes that practical knowledge to Minori and wants to retain direct familiarity with what fans receive (`PJSK:card:0520:01:002:0022–0029`, `PJSK:card:0520:01:002:0045–0049`; `PJSK:card:0520:01:003:0002–0029`). This useful memory qualifies any totalizing account of her former group as offering nothing of value; it does not settle that group's entire relationship history. At the celebration she asks staff what was difficult. Fewer setup problems coexist with insufficient guides and confused role assignments (`PJSK:card:0520:02:001:0038–0072`). The result is mutual learning, not organizational perfection.

**Staff support is neither invisible nor merely purchased atmosphere.** Saito reports that helping produce the earlier fan event strengthened her wish to work behind idol stages, prompting further practical experience and study (`PJSK:event:0073:01:001:0019–0036`). Her competence matters to the concert, and the concert matters to her chosen work. Minori, Shizuku and the staff can therefore participate in reciprocal support without having identical roles or expertise.

**Haruka's expertise has a learning history.** Reviewing ASRUN recordings recalls coordination difficulty, exhaustion that led to more endurance training, and a staff reprimand for leaning too far out of the moving platform (`PJSK:card:0518:01:002:0018–0035`). Her current practiced competence must not become a claim that earlier performance was effortless or faultless. These are Haruka's retrospective reports, not evidence that every incident is known to MMJ.

**KAITO's support does not entail emotional invulnerability.** He, MEIKO and Luka review logistics, while he praises and wants to borrow Airi's clear scheduling format (`PJSK:card:0521:01:002:0012–0049`). The advice relationship permits human-to-Virtual-Singer learning. The later card explicitly distinguishes his private anticipation anxiety from his calm manner with the performers: he does not want to add worry before their show (`PJSK:card:0521:02:002:0010–0023`). Afterward his overflowing fan response coexists with earlier attentive watching (`PJSK:card:0521:02:004:0028–0040`). This applies to MMJ KAITO only.

## 6. Reconstruction yield and limits

Minori's ordinary register can be exuberant, fantasy-prone and comically overformal while her consequential decisions still acknowledge cost and uncertainty. Her unsuccessful toast phrasing and family embarrassment must coexist with the serious parental appeal and clear desire to reach listeners (`PJSK:card:0520:02:001:0015–0031`; `PJSK:card:0517:01:002:0048–0065`; `PJSK:event:0073:05:002:0013–0034`). A reconstruction that makes every utterance an inspirational slogan would erase this contrast.

Her practical learning is also specific: repeated bodily attempts, counting to improve efficiency, note-taking during production discussion, and voluntarily requesting mentorship. Airi treats one successful turn as something to test for repeatability and synchrony, not automatic mastery (`PJSK:area:areatalk_ev_idol_10_002:01:001:0004–0010`). Public fan contact brings pleasure and remaining self-consciousness; Airi supplies a reminder of public responsibility after approving the encounter (`PJSK:card:0517:02:001:0037–0061`).

Shizuku combines professional care with acknowledged navigation difficulty. Minori can correct an impractical plan and offer help without treating her as generally incompetent (`PJSK:area:areatalk_ev_idol_10_003:01:001:0004–0010`). The sister-role play similarly supplies ordinary affection and taste differences, not a literal change of kinship. Chihiro's message preserves an external friendship in which Minori's progress provokes her own renewed dance ambition, despite the two having attended different schools and not recently met (`PJSK:card:0517:02:001:0066–0072`).

These are D0 observed/reported behaviors and D1 local inferences suitable for later model construction. Generalizing them into stable invariants or assigning temporal state IDs requires the absent MMJ foundation and ordered historical integration. There is no performed-voice claim: textual timing, wording and register do not substitute for aligned audio analysis.

## 7. Structural meaning, claim constraints and prospective closure

The event joins two concerts through repeated invitation, remembered recordings and a changing position within reciprocal encouragement. A DVD gives Minori access to a formative experience and Haruka access to a previously unretained encounter; the absence of a planned recording for MMJ's concert then makes preservation and distribution a live practical issue. This is event-local structural interpretation, not a promoted franchise-wide motif.

The strongest claims to preserve for MMJ backfill are: commitment can survive acknowledged non-guarantee; admiration can remain while reciprocity grows; expression depends on practiced, audience-facing technique; backstage labor and fans can receive hope as well as provide support; and fulfillment of one concert can enlarge ambition without resolving organizational constraints. Counterevidence blocks effortless talent, complete self-confidence, one-way rescue, uniformly harmful past relationships, invulnerable seniority, and a prematurely agreed expansion plan.
