## 2. The origin establishes a purpose, not a perfect childhood

The opening juxtaposes an idol ideal of giving tomorrow-oriented hope with a child who does not resemble its polished image. Airi confronts older boys over their repeated treatment of her younger sister, is hurt and threatens them. The scene cuts to her mother disinfecting her injuries. The boys' retreat or defeat is not depicted. Maternal scolding mixes practical care with appearance and gender expectations; Airi protests the relevance of the daifuku anecdote before complying. This is a situated family exchange, not a license to diagnose the household or treat every childhood fight as justified (`E01/001:0001–0028`).

A television idol supplies an attractive alternative. Several days later Airi admires the singing, dancing and entertaining talk; her sister says Airi also makes her smile. That recipient response helps her imagine herself in the vocation before she has acquired its form. Her desire is not produced solely by a wish to escape roughness or look cute. The remembered ability to receive and give pleasure is central (`E01/001:0029–0034`; `E01/002:0001–0013`). Positive interest in entertaining talk also fits the foundation's distinction between enjoying variety work and being hurt when it replaces desired idol work.

Card0129a gives this history ordinary material continuity. Searching for QT notebooks and magazines, Airi finds stored objects and admits losing track of their location. Her own mistaken autograph and the autograph received from her first favorite idol are different objects. Private excitement about the old handshake event leads into warming up to the song the next morning. She describes that idol to the group as a catalyst, while clarifying that she subsequently liked many idols; this is not the same continuing exclusive attachment Minori associates with Haruka. Shizuku already heard some childhood history during trainee days. Airi's present boast that she never lost a fight remains a boast, not missing footage of the opening encounter (`C129a/001:0002–0026`; `C129a/003:0002–0030`).

## 3. Agencies test a condition the group has already chosen

The first agency encounter separates several forms of recognition. Staff recognize three former idols and mistake Minori for an accompanying visitor. Airi corrects the mistake; Minori interprets it through insufficient idol aura and prior audition failure. Shizuku's response to talk of innate aura emphasizes continued effort and the distance from her own ideal. The meeting then offers all four affiliation but refuses their debut as the same unit. Haruka declines that arrangement. The refusal is therefore not a claim that the agency will accept only three people under any conditions; the decisive incompatibility is keeping the four-person group together (`E02/003:0002–0024`; `E02/004:0002–0026`).

For Minori, rejection has acquired a changed cost: earlier failures obstructed her own wish, whereas now she fears delaying people she admires. Haruka refuses the resulting idea that they might proceed without her and recalls the hope she gave them. Airi pushes continued effort; Shizuku suggests cooperation across agencies. Those responses preserve founding membership while contributing distinct kinds of work. They do not erase Minori's discomfort by decree (`E03/001:0002–0022`; `E03/003:0002–0022`).

Repeated refusals then require a different explanation. A producer known to Haruka reports that agencies fear appearing to poach from the seniors' former agencies and potentially losing work for other idols. The rumor he mentions is their search, not an exposed malicious story about the four. His account makes institutional interdependence intelligible; it does not establish that a powerful agency actually retaliated, that every refusal had the same cause or that a blacklist exists. The group also considers possible harm to other idols as a reason not simply to insist. Their vocation's intended beneficiaries cannot justify ignoring others' interests (`E03/004:0008–0035`; `E04/001:0002–0025`).

Airi's private last resort shows how much the shared condition matters and how costly her available strategy has become. She asks her former president to accept all four, offering to obey any direction, including variety or talent work, without complaint. This risks precisely the loss of vocational choice the main story repaired. The president refuses because of possible effects involving Haruka and Shizuku, while saying he would consider Airi alone. She leaves without accepting that narrower possibility. Her subsequent faltering is private; the others do not automatically know either the bargain or its aftermath (`E04/003:0002–0031`).

The contrast with her public commands matters. Airi can urge the others forward while being close to stopping herself. Her proposed solution is still institutional: even relinquishing her preferences seems more imaginable than working without an agency. The chapter's last-resort language marks exhaustion of that route, not proof that every objectively possible route has been investigated.

## 4. Receiving hope changes the question

At home Airi uses music to improve her mood, remembers why idols mattered, and decides to seek a hint in SEKAI. Rin notices her changed expression. Airi initially denies difficulty, then says there is no need to put on a brave face before someone who has already seen her very low. Their trust is prior history being used. Rin's extravagant claim to know everything is immediately exposed as a joke; its conversational effect is a little relief, not a new supernatural knowledge rule. Airi asks for a song that will make her smile (`E05/001:0002–0035`; `E05/002:0002–0039`).

The song helps without finishing the problem. Airi again treats Rin's brightness as a standard she cannot meet. Rin invites her to talk, explaining that even making the burden lighter would matter, and explicitly permits refusal. Airi chooses to describe childhood aspiration, rough behavior, family expense and support, and effort to become an idol. The current difficulty is partly elided. Rin receives an account, but no exact audition narrative or full list of the last-resort bargain's terms is established (`E06/001:0002–0042`).

Rin's answer combines recipient evidence with a question. She says Airi's performances make her smile and want to dance and sing together. She then asks whether an agency is necessary. Airi supplies real practical objections about stages, television and finding viewers. Rin admits the difficulty exceeds her understanding and offers confidence based on what their feelings have made possible in SEKAI. That analogy is encouragement, not a business plan or proof that the human world obeys the same rules. It recalls Minori's hope language for Airi, who decides to investigate the possibility (`E06/001:0043–0084`; `E06/002:0002–0005`).

The key revision is from treating an obstacle as the definition of the vocation to asking which means can serve its purpose. Card0129b makes Airi's criterion unusually explicit. In shared listening with Shizuku she distinguishes public Virtual Singer recordings from the Stage companions. Together they recall an English-class definition of idol as an object of admiration or respect. Airi reasons conditionally that an agency, stage and television are not necessary under that meaning. If the usual public image costs remaining MMJ, she says she will discard that image because these four can be idols together. This is her chosen criterion, not an exhaustive authorial definition or demonstrated public acceptance (`C129b/002:0002–0034`).

`MMJ-EP-AI-E0005` records this operative revision alongside newly acquired information. The broad human state remains a committed member, exacting teacher and organizer. Preserving `MMJ-AI-MS-04` therefore does not erase the shift in what she treats as necessary; a separate epistemic entry and the new unit state make the difference recoverable.

## 5. A shared route needs more than one person's insight

Shizuku arrives afterward, hears her explanation, values the idea and offers to help. The novice's contribution is a useful proposal and actual experimentation, not immediate mastery. At the meeting Airi proposes freelance activity. Haruka names scheduling, meetings, practice and promotion as work formerly done by staff. Airi proposes sharing management and production with Haruka, explicitly refuses to leave the burden to her alone, and welcomes Minori and Shizuku's contributions. Haruka acknowledges that the route is not singular, thanks Airi, says she will think through how the approaches fit, proposes a test that afternoon and asks to learn the recording method. The exchange moves beyond inspiration into scheduling, requesting knowledge and accepting distributed work. The I3 comparison is therefore narrow and firm: `MMJ-REL-GROUP-05` had a name, mutual membership and practice but no settled external operating approach; `MMJ-REL-GROUP-06` retains those relationships while adopting freelance work, self-management and video preparation. ## 7. Ordinary work and speech prevent a crisis-only model

Card0129a turns nostalgia into teaching. Shizuku requests a song important to Airi; Haruka values learning choreography immediately; Minori tries a remembered step. Airi's forceful correction supplies usable information about lower-body movement, hips, rhythm and coordination with the arms. The familiar warmth retains unequal expertise. Mastery of the whole dance is not shown (`C129a/003:0031–0043`).

The recording experiment makes that inequality local rather than universal. Minori knows how to operate the device and can guide Shizuku's hand away from the lens, yet initially forgets they must choose what Shizuku will say. Shizuku asks, follows instructions and produces unintended gestures and inputs while trying to fix the problem. In the later telling, Airi and Haruka imagine the scene and laugh; no video of the mishap exists. Minori protests and only tentatively accepts that the laughter may have helped after the others report relief. Shizuku returns them to filming. Care includes embarrassment and imperfect coordination, not only smooth reassurance (`C130a/002:0002–0054`).

Preparatory video research gives each member another task. Airi teaches Shizuku the search sequence and asks for her own search idea so that work is not duplicated. Shizuku finds an old Happy Everyday recording; Haruka says this is her first viewing, while Minori watched weekly. Airi briefly receives Minori's praise before trying to redirect the search and calling the upload a likely rules violation. No actual report or violation adjudication is shown. Haruka finds a timed challenge format in which personality emerges through the activity; Shizuku values enjoyment for both performers and viewers. Repeated accidental playback ends in laughter and Airi's intensified demand for the phone, without a shown handover (`C132b/002:0002–0046`).

Speech is conditioned by these purposes and partners. Airi's rough childhood threats, formal request to her former president, precise peer instructions, tentative musical comparison, heated identity declaration and subsequent apology are not interchangeable evidence of bluntness. Haruka's professional apologies can uphold a firm membership condition; with Airi she can qualify a flattering motive attribution and name her own limitation. Minori moves from polite service and formal stranger response to cheerful presenter speech, urgent technical fragments and familiar protest. Shizuku's hedges can accompany a question, experiment or firm desire; addressing the phone playfully does not certify general incompetence. These are textual registers; no vocal performance is inferred.

The cost of effort also stays visible: sleeplessness intrudes on Minori's job; Shizuku suggests a rest; Airi permits rest while offering practice as an alternative when Minori insists on doing something. Haruka and Airi practice while committee duties delay the other two. Family objects, offered food, a returned towel and playful listening give the unit a daily life around its ambition (`E04/001:0024–0032`; `E07/001:0002–0009`; `C130b/003:0046–0048`; `C133b/002:0002–0010`). None establishes perfect work-life balance or an inevitable pathology.
