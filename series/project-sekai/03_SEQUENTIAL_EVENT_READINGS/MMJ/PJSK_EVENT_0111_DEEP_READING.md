## 2. Growth makes informal support structurally insufficient

MMJ's regional and online media work is increasing, but follower growth does not prove Budokan readiness. The practical signal is internal strain: schedules are packed, Airi makes a recoverable timing error, all four recognize that message, venue, and calendar labor is exceeding what they can safely distribute. Saito detects the conflict immediately and already has event, scheduling, and manager-study experience. `PJSK:event:0111:01:001:0002–PJSK:event:0111:01:003:0084`.

The offer arises from observed work and trust rather than fan enthusiasm alone. All four ask Saito; Shizuku connects Saito's stated vocational dream with MMJ's own. Saito is moved but declines through an unspecified family circumstance, and the group initially respects that boundary. `PJSK:event:0111:02:001:0002–0042`.

## 3. The inn reveals an unshared feasible set

The work trip to Yanagimizuso is accidental. MMJ learns that Saito is the only daughter of the inn's family and is expected to become the next proprietress. Before that disclosure, the group gives Saito's mother direct evidence that Saito is a valued collaborator rather than a fan casually helping idols. `PJSK:event:0111:03:002:0028–0077`.

Saito later states both values. She loves the inn, its guests, and its family history; she also wants to continue idol-support work. Because she believes only she can prevent closure, she frames her vocational dream as the disposable side. She thanks MMJ for a treasured experience and attempts to close the question. `PJSK:event:0111:04:001:0031–0087`.

The conflict is not a simple opposition between authentic dream and false duty. Saito sincerely values both. The error lies in private assumptions about what others require, the absence of succession alternatives, and the belief that care demands unilateral self-erasure.

## 4. A past fan interaction becomes vocational origin evidence

Saito's grandmother supplies the missing history. At a handshake event three years earlier, Shizuku noticed Saito's distress and used touch and words to help her face forward. Saito then became interested in event work because she experienced an event changing a person's heart. Later MMJ work, audience response, practical study, and repeated family reports turn that interest into a serious vocation. `PJSK:event:0111:05:002:0040–0084`.

The same account exposes concealed mutual protection. The grandmother previously told Saito that the family values her happiness above the inn. Saito denied her interest and asked that her mother not be told; the family gradually accepted the inheritance claim while still doubting it. `PJSK:event:0111:05:002:0085–0127`.

This evidence neither makes Shizuku sole cause nor entitles her to choose. Saito's work, audience receipts, MMJ's opportunities, and family response remain jointly causal.

## 5. `MMJ-EP-SZ-E0111` — regret becomes a bounded support method

Shizuku recognizes Saito's duty language because responsibility and expectation once helped her persist in Cheerful＊Days and also became insufficient. She does not claim identical circumstances. She does not demand the manager job, dismiss the inn, or promise an easy answer. Even after the family opens alternatives, she returns decision authority to Saito and asks only for a path without regret. `PJSK:event:0111:07:001:0023–0049`; `PJSK:event:0111:07:001:0093–0111`.
