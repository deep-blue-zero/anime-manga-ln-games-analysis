## 3. A larger goal becomes legitimate before it becomes feasible

The successful solo concert produces specific, uneven relationships with an audience. Haruka reads letters through an anonymous receiving service, attends to individual stationery and remembers a fan's history of missed opportunities to see her. The writer now supports all of MMJ and hopes to see them at **武闘館**. Haruka's memory of performing there with ASRUN makes that wish emotionally concrete, but the present group has not booked it (`PJSK:event:0078:01:001`). Increased inquiries and attention partly follow web coverage of the independent group's success, including exaggeration. Reach already has both useful and distorting effects before the TV debate begins.

Minori proposes a tour and then a dome concert because she wants the group to bring hope to people across Japan, as ASRUN once did for her. Fan requests enlarge a desire she endorses; she does not simply treat customers' preferences as commands. She worries the proposal is premature and asks what the others think. Haruka and Airi's initial need for time concerns scale and readiness, not a refusal to dream (`PJSK:event:0078:01:003:0031–0088`).

After Minori leaves on an ordinary shopping errand, the experienced three distinguish having inherited fans from having unlimited demand. Airi and Shizuku help reconsider the assumption that a large goal belongs only in an indefinite future. The group can aim now while building the capacity to approach it (`PJSK:event:0078:02:001:0030–0064`). Forecasts of a 2,000–3,000-person next opportunity, the 1,900-seat Shibuya venue, the roughly 15,000-person intermediate venue and the larger dome are planning speech within the story, not bookings, measured present demand or external venue certification. Minori's relief the next day confirms that speaking a tentative want helped make it a shared direction (`PJSK:event:0078:02:003:0012–0034`).

Card 0549:01 preserves Shizuku's own route into that endorsement. Public fan comments recall the concert as something made with the group, including her costume designs and fans' realization of them. That appraisal is stronger than merely liking a cheerful junior; it gives Minori's orientation practical authority without claiming she supplies every technical answer (`PJSK:card:0549:01:001:0012–0037`).

## 4. Deliberation protects both the audience and the member who hesitates

Haruka proposes 武闘館 as an intermediate goal and recognizes that streaming alone may not produce the needed expansion. The group divides the work of reviewing emails, messages and comments. An implausible diet advertisement reveals the need to assess requesters; Shizuku worries that fans could be deceived too. Airi recognizes management work formerly done around them. A seemingly serious TV invitation therefore enters an established screening process, but Haruka's favorable reading of the producer's message is not an independent guarantee of safety (`PJSK:event:0078:03:001:0011–0036`; `PJSK:event:0078:03:002:0003–0016`).

The proposal for **アイドル大戦争！**, read as アイドルハザード and shortened to アイハザ, fits their hope-giving purpose. Haruka nevertheless fears that selection and editing could make them seem unlike themselves, damage a member, and distress existing fans. Airi supplies an actual prior case: a remark was edited to sound like an insult to an actor. Fans' defense contained escalation, but the episode caused trouble and remains remembered. Haruka's concern is neither irrational technophobia nor proof that all television is exploitative (`PJSK:event:0078:03:002:0019–0079`).

The members thank her for raising the issue and permit an overnight pause. Haruka privately understands why simply yielding would be inadequate: even a favorable outcome would leave her without ownership of the decision, while a harmful one would intensify regret. Agreement in action is not yet agreement in reasons (`PJSK:event:0078:04:001:0014–0047`). The pause makes dissent usable inside collaboration; it is not evidence that Haruka is disloyal to the new goal.

Stage MEIKO answers in an explicitly limited capacity: as a fan who knows MMJ's intentions, she would not have that understanding erased by every portrayal. Trust supplies a reason to act despite uncertainty, not evidence that uncertainty has disappeared.

## 5. The remembered origin restores the purpose of reaching an unprepared stranger

Haruka nevertheless privately worries that she disappoints her and is unlike other children. Felt joy, visible expression, maternal response and the child's interpretation must remain separate (`PJSK:event:0078:05:002:0002–0024`). This is not evidence of absent emotion or a diagnostic equivalence with Mafuyu.

Becoming lost exposes the limit of reading a composed face. The unnamed idol initially does not recognize the child's fear from her expression. Practical help follows discovery of the problem: the manager attends to finding the parents while the idol invites Haruka to watch. The performer is not a massive star attracting a guaranteed crowd; her small encounter is consequential because of what she does with the people present (`PJSK:event:0078:05:004:0029–0048`; `PJSK:event:0078:06:002:0030–0056`).

Clapping becomes supported participation on stage. Haruka hesitates because she does not know how, accepts the invitation, follows manageable demonstrations and experiences warmth while seeing others smile. The idol says Haruka's smile encourages her in return. This is reciprocal efficacy: the child can receive something and matter to its giver, without first possessing professional skill (`PJSK:event:0078:06:002:0065–0118`). Reunion with her parents follows, and the wish to become an idol grows from the experience of shared joy (`PJSK:event:0078:07:002:0027–0055`).

In the present, Haruka initially assumes her mother's newspaper item concerns MMJ. It instead reveals that the former idol now works as a local radio announcer and still performs for community audiences, including children and older people. Changed occupation and scale have not extinguished her hope-giving purpose. The printed article itself reaches Haruka with unexpected encouragement. This is the decisive bridge back to the media question: communication can reach a person who did not deliberately seek an idol performance (`PJSK:event:0078:07:002:0056–0083`). It does not prove every publicity channel or larger venue is necessary to every meaningful encounter.
