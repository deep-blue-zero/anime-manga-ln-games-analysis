## 1. The conflict tests a reunited group, not the possibility of reunion

The foundation's main achievement was making affiliation reciprocal and chosen. Saki could want the four together while respecting another friend's present ties; Ichika could act on her own desire; Honami could maintain old and current friends; Shiho could keep serious standards while allowing attachment and acknowledging harm. EVENT_0001 begins with that configuration operating. They practice together, tease one another and plan an outing. The crisis concerns what continued togetherness requires when one member experiences time differently.

The hospital dream reactivates separation rather than showing a new admission. Saki wakes at home and later privately identifies the dream. She avoids explaining it to Ichika because she expects renewed guilt about infrequent distant-hospital visits. Her surface energy therefore has two simultaneous foundations: genuine joy at participation and an effort to keep old pain from burdening others. Her final thought in the opening chapter is that she must recover the time she missed (`PJSK:event:0001:01:001:0003`–`PJSK:event:0001:01:001:0020`; `PJSK:event:0001:01:002:0014`–`PJSK:event:0001:01:002:0017`; `PJSK:event:0001:01:005:0014`–`PJSK:event:0001:01:005:0016`). This is stronger evidence than inferring a concealed crisis from every exuberant greeting.

Her planner is already full of band, soft-tennis club and café work. That abundance still feels insufficient. The observatory poster becomes the center of a day she wants to fill with shopping, food, sweets and other activities. The change from wondering whether everything fits to deciding they should do everything is the behavioral expression of her urgency (`PJSK:event:0001:02:002:0010`–`PJSK:event:0001:02:002:0032`). The ordinary interests matter in themselves: these are concrete pleasures that she wants, not interchangeable symbols of a diagnosis.

Shiho's initial reluctance is also intelligible within the baseline. She prefers using the time for practice and questions the need for a distant observatory when SEKAI has stars. Luka argues that sharing experiences outside music can support understanding and coordination within it. Shiho agrees to consider changing her work shift while retaining a condition that practice should not become careless. The episode thereby negotiates an actual resource conflict among valued activities; it does not reveal that musical rigor was secretly irrelevant (`PJSK:event:0001:03:001:0011`–`PJSK:event:0001:03:001:0041`).

## 2. Care is available before the crisis, but its meaning does not reliably reach Saki

Saki is not wholly unaware that she should rest. After dismissing Tsukasa's concern, she decides to avoid overdoing things and sleep early. When Honami notices her face is red, she thanks her for the suggestion to check her temperature. The next day her private speech acknowledges mild fever and intends an early return home. The subsequent shift request interrupts that intention: protecting Saturday's plan and helping coworkers become compatible reasons to work today. The manager is not shown knowing her condition (`PJSK:event:0001:04:001:0020`–`PJSK:event:0001:04:001:0027`; `PJSK:event:0001:04:004:0004`–`PJSK:event:0001:04:004:0011`; `PJSK:event:0001:04:006:0002`–`PJSK:event:0001:04:006:0018`).

Afterward she feels worse, considers missing practice, then remembers Shiho's condition and fears losing the group outing. This makes the condition causally relevant as Saki understands it. It does not mean Shiho required an ill person to rehearse: when fever is raised at practice, Shiho says to rest, Ichika says practice can happen another time, and Honami proposes individual work at different paces. Saki insists on playing together and denies fever despite her earlier private knowledge (`PJSK:event:0001:04:007:0002`–`PJSK:event:0001:04:007:0014`; `PJSK:event:0001:05:001:0003`–`PJSK:event:0001:05:001:0020`). The group's communication can fail despite already containing care, because she hears postponement as possible loss while they intend protection of an available future.

The successful run makes that discrepancy especially clear. Her friends hear improvement and coordination; these musical observations do not disclose bodily condition. Honami notices delayed response, asks before touching her forehead, and reports fever. Shiho's urgent reaction includes grabbing Saki's arm, which Saki says hurts, followed by anger about concealment. Neither concern nor friendship makes the intervention harmless. When Shiho says an outing can happen anytime, Saki protests that she has spent a long period unable to go. Their disagreement is over the assumed availability of time, not whether friendship or health has any value (`PJSK:event:0001:05:001:0023`–`PJSK:event:0001:05:001:0049`).

Honami restores practical order before anyone solves that disagreement. She asks Ichika for a chair, offers a drink and proposes taking Saki home. Her later explanation that she is accustomed to such care because of her younger brother adds a concrete ordinary-life source for competence. At home, Saki's apology for being a burden and Shiho's interrupted objection show that the moral account remains unsettled even after physical help is available (`PJSK:event:0001:05:001:0060`–`PJSK:event:0001:05:001:0065`; `PJSK:event:0001:05:002:0003`–`PJSK:event:0001:05:002:0014`).

This refines the existing reciprocal-care claims. The P03 insight that private protection may remove another person's informed choice applies in a new configuration: Saki limits what friends can respond to by withholding illness and grief; their need to protect her can, in turn, be expressed in language that understates what she fears losing. The answer is neither uninformed indulgence nor a verdict that the most worried friend owns every choice.

## 3. The disclosure gives an old pacing problem a more precise explanation

On Saturday, rain and the missed outing renew Saki's loneliness despite care from her brother and friends. She knows they remain with her and still calls herself foolish. That contradiction matters: more evidence that they care is not automatically a complete answer to her experience of missing time. She goes to SEKAI to see stars and initially feels alone there too. Luka notices her unusual appearance and low energy, but explicitly rejects Saki's suggestion that she knows everything. She asks and listens (`PJSK:event:0001:06:002:0002`–`PJSK:event:0001:06:002:0024`; `PJSK:event:0001:06:003:0008`–`PJSK:event:0001:06:003:0033`).

Saki's fuller account preserves complexity in hospitalization. Kind people, enjoyable activities and the hope of seeing stars with friends helped her bear unpleasant examinations. At other times she imagined the lessons and school trips she should be sharing passing without her. She feared that even what they had already shared might seem to disappear with that missing time. Her need to give shared time a form explains both urgent accumulation and the attraction of durable reminders (`PJSK:event:0001:07:003:0002`–`PJSK:event:0001:07:003:0028`). This is a newly articulated explanatory detail of an existing history and vulnerability, not evidence that the fear begins on this Saturday.

Luka's response separates wanting what was lost from condemning the person who wants it. She suggests Saki hurried and can proceed more slowly, presenting this as her impression. She does not prescribe an obligation to stop wanting, promise medical recovery or claim special access to the cause. The useful shift is from undifferentiated blame toward a modifiable way of pursuing a legitimate desire (`PJSK:event:0001:07:003:0029`–`PJSK:event:0001:07:003:0039`).

Ichika's route to the same encounter is different. She remembers visits in which Saki softened loneliness with considerate conversation, tells Honami and Shiho about repeated visits with her mother, and proposes that all three go to her. At the house she infers SEKAI from the wish to see stars after finding Saki absent. Her initiative is the use of an established strength in a new situation: person-specific memory becomes a practical invitation or visit, as it already did during the main-story repair. The event adds history and application, not her first independent capacity to act (`PJSK:event:0001:06:004:0002`–`PJSK:event:0001:06:004:0048`; `PJSK:event:0001:07:002:0010`–`PJSK:event:0001:07:002:0018`).
