## 2. One memory, two private meanings

Saki and Tsukasa decorate the Tenma family's seven-tier display. She saves the damaged top dolls so they can place them together and anticipates her first home Hinamatsuri with Ichika, Honami and Shiho after years of illness. Tsukasa reads the wear as a defect that could spoil the occasion. He remembers carrying the dolls to Saki's hospital, falling and muddying them, then interpreting her unusual smile as concealed disappointment. Young Saki verbally said she was delighted, but he converted his own guilt into a long-term plan to buy beautiful replacements. He never asks what the damage means to her. `PJSK:event:0016:01:001:0002–0055`; `PJSK:event:0016:03:003:0002–0103`.

Toya encounters Tsukasa while shopping and receives the private history. He helps reject gifts likely to burden Saki and search for an appropriate replacement.
