## Work, pleasure and attention coexist

Honami confirms the agreed cleaning tasks and offers tea after completing them. Her white-peach choice gives Kanade an ordinary sensory pleasure; Honami says she is relieved to see her smile because she seemed low earlier. Asked about her competence, she says she likes housework, enjoys helping and feels happy when someone in difficulty is pleased. She also recognizes that this can lead to meddling. The job came through a recommendation from her mother's acquaintance. These are explicit interests, judgments and a work arrangement, so helpful behavior should not automatically be reduced to fear of rejection (`PJSK:event:0002:02:002:0002–0039`).

That distinction does not prove a pure or single motive for every act. P04 shame and interpersonal fear remain available in the contexts where they are supported. Here the positive self-report and comfortable practical competence deserve their own evidential weight. Kanade's private appreciation of a clean workspace and good food is the recipient's view; her private explanation about her grandmother arranging support is not automatically something Honami hears.

The areas broaden this ordinary exchange. Honami enjoys menu planning and treats her impression of Kanade's preferences as a hypothesis to test before announcing it. No specific favorite food is established. ## Advice offers a method without access to the person being discussed

Kanade asks how to help someone when neither helper nor recipient knows what to do. Honami suggests listening more closely, learning the person's feelings and concerns, and recognizing that someone may hide a problem to avoid burdening others. She then supplies her own qualification: some people dislike being probed. Her hesitation is part of the advice, not evidence that she said nothing useful. Kanade thanks her and takes away a wish to know the person better (`PJSK:event:0002:02:002:0041–0057`).

The ticket offer is similarly specific and optional. Honami has other plans, knows the exhibition is near Kanade's usual hospital-visiting route, and remembers her account of getting composition ideas from paintings. She asks whether dolls interest her. Later Kanade recalls the listening suggestion when inviting Mafuyu into the group outing. This establishes a causal contribution to Kanade's choice without making Honami the author of every subsequent intervention or giving her the outing's private information (`PJSK:event:0002:02:002:0059–0070`; `PJSK:event:0002:03:002:0037–0053`; `PJSK:event:0002:03:002:0065–0087`). The N25 reading retains the later distinction between useful curiosity and over-probing; it should not be flattened into a verdict that Honami prescribed unlimited questioning.

Card0115 front follows the exhibition. Kanade asks whether Honami has time after cleaning, and Honami agrees to talk over muscat tea. Care can have real value while its extrapolation beyond known circumstances remains uncertain.

## A band member with reciprocal interests

When they meet outside, Honami says she is heading to practice with childhood friends. Kanade remembers earlier generic band talk and asks to hear her playing. Honami acknowledges their rough skill and some embarrassment, then offers to bring a rehearsal recording next time. The exchange supplies musical identity and willingness to share in response to interest. It does not show the recording being delivered, heard or critiqued, and does not identify every member to Kanade (`PJSK:area:areatalk_ev_night_01_001:01:001:0002–0009`).

Her speech is consistently polite toward Kanade, with modest corrections, thanks, soft invitations and requests for conversational permission. Warm laughter, pleasure in tea and meal planning, professional task confirmation, and an earnest advisory request are different modes within that relationship. Compared with P04 and EVENT_0001, these are further conditional examples rather than a new uniformly assertive or universally self-sacrificing personality.
