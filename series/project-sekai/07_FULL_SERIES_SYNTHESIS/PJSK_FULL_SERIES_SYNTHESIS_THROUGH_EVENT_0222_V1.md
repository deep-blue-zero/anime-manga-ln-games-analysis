## A shared wish is an achievement, not an initial fact

Project SEKAI repeatedly begins where a valued feeling cannot yet organize a workable relation. Someone may know a wish but discount its legitimacy, possess a skill but distrust its origin, love another person but decide on their behalf, or perform competence while losing access to preference. The initial unit is therefore more than a set of compatible talents. It is an arrangement through which people may become able to state, test and sustain what they want with others.

The five foundations give that arrangement different problems. Leo/need has a past bond whose continued existence does not make contact easy. Saki's return, Ichika's hesitation, Honami's fear of renewed exclusion and Shiho's protective withdrawal are different obstacles to renewed choice. Their reunion requires changes in what others are allowed to know and decide. Simply proving that all four care would not restore the repeated activity through which the bond becomes livable. [Leo/need synthesis](../06_SPECIALIST_AND_UNIT_SYNTHESIS/PJSK_LEO_NEED_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md).

MMJ instead forms through unequal professional histories and reciprocal recognition. Minori can give experienced performers evidence of received hope while lacking their technique and institutional standing. Haruka, Airi and Shizuku need different forms of repair: impossible responsibility, imposed role restriction and damaged working relations are not one interchangeable disillusionment. Minori's final audition rejection remains real after she has already mattered to them. Their recognition gives her membership and continuing work, not a fiction that expertise has suddenly become equal. [MMJ synthesis](../06_SPECIALIST_AND_UNIT_SYNTHESIS/PJSK_MMJ_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md).

VBS makes entitlement to desire especially explicit. Kohane's apparent incapacity understates her initiative; An's invitation combines perception with incomplete information. Akito mistakes social presentation for shallow commitment, while Toya doubts whether a rebellious beginning permits a sincere present attachment. Their repair does not purify each origin. It makes present choice and reciprocal need available despite those origins. The two particular partnerships remain consequential inside the quartet. [VBS synthesis](../06_SPECIALIST_AND_UNIT_SYNTHESIS/PJSK_VBS_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md).

WxS has complementary means before it has a fair account of shared responsibility. The failed first show reveals a technical and relational system that cannot be explained by blaming Nene alone. Tsukasa's attack injures; Rui's criticism identifies a wrong while exceeding his knowledge of Tsukasa's whole motive. The renewed company depends on Nene's own mediation and choice, Emu's shareable purpose and Tsukasa's recovered relation to an audience. Its meaningful outcome does not retrospectively authorize every forced encounter used to obtain it. [WxS synthesis](../06_SPECIALIST_AND_UNIT_SYNTHESIS/PJSK_WXS_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md).

Nightcord already works together before its members can locate an absent friend or understand what their work means to each other. Mafuyu's search for a usable self, Kanade's rescue-bound legitimacy, Ena's need for serious artistic recognition and Mizuki's struggle to keep known likes ordinary are distinct. Their founding promise helps preserve a relation and also binds it to an unfinished task. Reaching Mafuyu is real; completing her search is not. The group becomes a place to continue before it becomes an answer. [N25 synthesis](../06_SPECIALIST_AND_UNIT_SYNTHESIS/PJSK_N25_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md).

The comparative consequence is that togetherness cannot be treated as proof of preexisting sameness. It is something made through changed information, acknowledgment, practical contribution and a decision to remain involved. That decision has force precisely because refusal, injury, unequal ability and different histories are not erased. Later conflicts can test an achieved relation without revealing that it was always false.

## Development enlarges the means of acting together

Once the groups exist, development repeatedly changes how a valued end can be pursued. Leo/need moves from restored practice to composition, coauthorship, arrangement, public work and leadership. MMJ turns hope into teaching, promotion, managed space, audience inquiry and negotiated production. VBS expands from pair and local scene to a restored coalition, unfamiliar music and overseas resources. WxS reorganizes its relation to the park and learns across institutions and media. Nightcord's creative work becomes entangled with domestic refuge, chosen disclosure and future vocation. These are changes in actual capacities, not merely stronger declarations of the original feeling.

The distinction between an end and its present means becomes especially productive when something fails. Rui's lantern mechanism in E0152 cannot deliver its intended effect; Kohane's recipient perspective and willing participants' labor make a different light road possible. Airi's E0198 acting difficulty leads to a bounded request and co-performance, not simply more solitary exertion. Akito's E0187 professional test requires a changed method, bilingual work and Toya's arranging. Minori's E0205 help involves learning beside a dancer and accepting a revised step. In each case, care for the result can require relinquishing ownership of the first solution. [Authorship specialist](../06_SPECIALIST_AND_UNIT_SYNTHESIS/PJSK_AUTHORSHIP_LABOR_AND_RECIPIENT_AGENCY_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md).

This development does not make technique a secondary wrapper around feeling. An's language work needs study and feedback. Nene's singing has to function within a role and a whole play. Honami's leadership works through musical interpretation as well as interpersonal attention. Ena's personally meaningful painting still receives technical criticism. The desired expression becomes more usable through form, revision and knowledge of a particular task. Sincerity alone neither guarantees communication nor supplies a missing skill.

Nor does collective work make individual agency disappear. Ichika's personal channel and creator exchange remain related to Leo/need without automatically belonging to its public activity. Haruka's E0213 proposal distributes unequal stage emphasis after considered assent, not by making Shizuku the only active member. Mizuki's clothing trial uses supplied patterns and feedback while remaining an owned test. Different roles can support equal membership; they can also create burdens that require renewed discussion. The particular agreement is evidence, not a permanent license for any future arrangement. [Leo/need synthesis](../06_SPECIALIST_AND_UNIT_SYNTHESIS/PJSK_LEO_NEED_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md); [MMJ synthesis](../06_SPECIALIST_AND_UNIT_SYNTHESIS/PJSK_MMJ_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md); [N25 synthesis](../06_SPECIALIST_AND_UNIT_SYNTHESIS/PJSK_N25_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md).

The resulting image of growth is relational but not selfless. People can enjoy mastery, want recognition, protect a personal project, compete or choose a preferred audience. Their aims become more precisely connected to other people's capacities and responses. The source does not require desire to lose its personal stake before it becomes legitimate.

## A received work cannot own the life it reaches

The cast's recurring wish to reach someone is not one doctrine. Leo/need's connection, MMJ's hope, VBS's musical horizon, WxS's smiles and Nightcord's rescue differ in origin, method and risk. Their work can cross those vocabularies without making them synonyms. A listener's pleasure, a renewed attempt, an actual competitive result and a wish to continue living do not establish the same kind of outcome.

MMJ makes the difference between encouragement and victory unusually clear. Airi's childhood effort reaches a boy who tries at baseball without winning. Haruka's E0142 team loses the challenge while a particular viewer chooses to exercise again. E0214 gives renewed manga work an actual failure and leaves the brothers choosing distinct careers. These are meaningful received effects without hidden institutional victories. By contrast, VBS's RAD BLAST actually surpasses RAD WEEKEND. Avoiding inflated success claims must not become refusal to acknowledge an achieved result. [MMJ synthesis](../06_SPECIALIST_AND_UNIT_SYNTHESIS/PJSK_MMJ_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md); [VBS synthesis](../06_SPECIALIST_AND_UNIT_SYNTHESIS/PJSK_VBS_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md).

Artistic intention also has several legitimate limits. Ichika can welcome interpretations unlike her own while choosing what she will make. MMJ's trade-live brief can require a particular relation to another group's song without imposing that rule on all listeners. A client's task, an author's aim and a recipient's interpretation are different authorities. Ena's pleasure in her own seeing does not make a grade irrelevant; a grade does not exhaust the value of that seeing. The series repeatedly permits two evaluations to remain true at once. [Authorship specialist](../06_SPECIALIST_AND_UNIT_SYNTHESIS/PJSK_AUTHORSHIP_LABOR_AND_RECIPIENT_AGENCY_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md).

[E0206](../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/MIXED/PJSK_EVENT_0206_DEEP_READING.md); [care specialist](../06_SPECIALIST_AND_UNIT_SYNTHESIS/PJSK_CARE_DISCLOSURE_AND_FAMILY_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md).

## Care changes the conditions of choice and can also take them away

The groups' strongest forms of help often make a next action possible before they produce a settled self-understanding. A meal, a place to stay, a permitted pause, a ride, an invitation or a direct question can matter without solving the whole problem. These material acts also prevent the narrative from becoming a series of speeches that succeed simply because someone finally says the correct words.

Mafuyu's history makes the distinction decisive. She has spoken wants before; the conditions of their reception make them difficult to sustain. Kanade's refuge and the father's later actual intervention alter available actions, not only emotional vocabulary. An actual maternal apology and chosen return coexist with renewed pressure. Toya's later recognition from his father does not erase coercion, while new family help is still real. Ena can receive a difficult paternal account and choose painting without obtaining a full retraction of earlier injury. Family affection, practical authority and accountability must be assessed separately. Peer care is not exempt from the same scrutiny. Nagi's concealed history costs An an opportunity to know and mourn, despite protective intentions. Ena can protect Mizuki's guarded information while remaining uncertain whether pursuit is right. The analyst must keep both capacities in view instead of assigning one ethical essence to each helper.

WxS most visibly preserves disagreement after artistic success. Nene's thanks for Rui's belief coexist with his uncertainty about the pressure he applied. Tsukasa's E0208 performance succeeds while the method remains dangerous and he refuses to promise never to repeat it. The group's commitment to retrieve him is real but untested as a durable safeguard. The story has not supplied the counterfactual evidence needed to call the harm necessary. [WxS synthesis](../06_SPECIALIST_AND_UNIT_SYNTHESIS/PJSK_WXS_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_THROUGH_EVENT_0222.md).

Care can also state a personal desire without pretending to know the other's whole need. Kohane wants to finish a game with Mafuyu before a mechanism is found; Mafuyu then contributes the solution. ## The past becomes usable through retention and reinterpretation

Toya can reclaim classical wonder and skill without consenting to the whole former regime. Airi can integrate variety into idol work without accepting the restriction that once hurt her. Shizuku can use professional intensity from her earlier group without returning to its imposed isolation. Emu can preserve responsibility for the park through redistributed stewardship and outside learning. A past can contain resources and injury together. A wall, station, field of flowers, lake object or personified self-position lets someone encounter a relation to history that had been difficult to state. The form does not settle its meaning. Haruka can acknowledge parental worry while refusing a demand to quit. An can keep grief and anger without easy forgiveness. Alternative lives enlarge the comparison rather than rewriting biography. Their benefits can be real within the experience, and the wish to return can still be owned. Material reminders can hold more than one meaning as circumstances change. A photograph can support belonging without proving that every later wish aligns. Those contexts test whether an available method survives a different recipient. They also expose its limits: Akito's own aspiration does not initially supply the answer another artist needs, and Saki can help someone else disclose a wish she is still concealing in her own band. The mixed events make capacities portable without making them universally applicable. ## Ordinary life supplies value that an achievement arc cannot exhaust

The corpus gives food, games, shopping, jokes, fatigue, taste, study and incidental company substantial interpretive work. They show what people can enjoy, misunderstand, refuse or contribute when a climax is not forcing an answer. Toya's gaming, Haruka's penguin preferences, Rui's vegetable avoidance, Mizuki's clothing enthusiasm and the many uneven attempts at cooking or instruments are not merely comic deviations from a true serious self. They constrain what a plausible person can know and do.

Ordinary pleasure also need not function as evidence of cure. Nightcord's E0210 outing matters while the rescue, family and future questions remain unresolved. Mafuyu's narrow choices can be owned without becoming fluent global preference. Kanade can laugh and still struggle to rest. Ena can want to preserve the present image without having completed a future memory. The scene's value is not diminished by its limited duration, and it should not be inflated to resolve what it does not address. Shared leisure is similarly uneven elsewhere. Nene can welcome company while lacking ease with strangers. A party can be enjoyable for one singer and socially uncomfortable for another. These scenes preserve texture within relational change: a new agreement does not require every familiar habit to disappear, and a familiar joke does not cancel every development.

At the same time, ordinary conduct can expose costs a triumphant scene leaves open. Lost sleep, unfinished schoolwork, repayment-driven overwork, missed meals and difficulty switching out of grief recur after meaningful achievements. Practical assistance helps, but a successful performance or completed project does not prove a sustainable life has been organized. The everyday record is therefore both a source of value and a check on claims of completion. ## Convergence decisions and reconstruction use

The lower layers converge on a broad interpretive claim: agency becomes more substantial through relationships that make wants expressible, testable and practically sustainable, yet those relationships can also impose roles, conceal knowledge and create obligations. The story's repeated movement toward connection remains meaningful because the right to differ, refuse, fail and reconsider has not disappeared. Its positive achievements and its unresolved costs belong to the same account.

| Tempting whole-series shortcut | Reconciled decision |
|---|---|
| Every unit discovers the same authentic self. | Preserve distinct difficulties: restoring access, authorizing vocation, owning an origin, coordinating dreams and sustaining preference are different tasks. |
| Shared work requires identical ends or equal roles. | Preserve chosen unequal roles, independent creative routes, separate careers and Leo/need's unagreed future. |
| Art heals when it is sincere enough. | Preserve particular receipts, technical work, actual failure, limited relief and the unresolved rescue question. |
| A successful result justifies the process. | Keep permission, knowledge, harm and later gratitude in their actual order; success does not prove necessity or safety. |
| Painful history must be kept because harm was necessary. | Retention and meaning do not establish that growth required the harm or that a less harmful route was impossible. |
| Wider access produces common knowledge. | Preserve source-specific recipients, withheld accounts, distinct manifestations and unreceived private questions. |
| Continued conflict cancels development. | Preserve new capacities and real achievements while retaining the specific problem still unsolved. |
