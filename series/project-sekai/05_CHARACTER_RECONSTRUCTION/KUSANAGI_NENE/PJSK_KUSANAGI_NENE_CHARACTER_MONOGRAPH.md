# Kusanagi Nene — provisional character monograph

## Authority snapshot

Use `WXS-EP-NENE-E0114` for Nene's current collaborative-direction method and preserve `WXS-EP-NENE-E0095` as her own musical-acting authority. `WXS-MS-NENE-P06` remains the failure/anxiety formation baseline.

## Character thesis

Nene is a highly skilled performer whose desire survives a performance failure that made public exposure feel dangerous. Her caution is not lack of ambition, and her bluntness is not lack of care. Mediation through Robo-Nene initially lets ability reach the stage without requiring full bodily presence. Development expands the conditions under which Nene can choose direct performance, teach another person, distribute roles, and improvise under damage while keeping responsibility for coherence.

## Developmental architecture

The main story joins strong singing and theatrical desire to shame from a failed performance. Rui's robot provides an access method, not a proof that anxiety is unreal. Tsukasa and Emu's support helps Nene state that she wants the stage and perform, while nervousness and reliance on mediation remain valid afterward. Her criticisms often protect craft quality, though they can sound sharper than the underlying collaborative aim.

`WXS-EP-NENE-E0095` establishes a musical-acting authority in which her own performance problem remains central. EVENT_0114 changes the scale. Nene replaces type-based distance with questions about individual interests, skills, and desired roles; assigns work around the answers; coaches anxiety through tested experience; and retains responsibility for the whole. After injury, she predicts blame and regret, rejects passive compromise, recombines available resources, recognizes An's protective ad-lib, verifies the take, and returns credit. EVENT_0132 adds a reciprocal cohosting relationship with Minori and ordinary post-work access without replacing either artistic owner.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Performance anxiety coexists with high ambition and exact craft judgment.
- Blunt correction can be collaborative; withdrawal can still injure or block others.
- Technology/mediation is legitimate access, not an inferior false self.
- She can teach from lived fear without assuming another person is identical.
- Under production failure she is increasingly resourceful rather than resigned.

## Relationship conditioning

Rui knows her history and designs access, but his confidence does not authorize unlimited risk. Emu provides warm invitation and audience intuition. Tsukasa attracts blunt correction and earns trust through work. Classmates activate an older anxiety that EVENT_0114 revises through person-specific collaboration. Minori draws out a gentler public-teaching and ordinary-friendship mode.

## Current state and open limits

At the cutoff Nene can lead a differentiated production, improvise through failure, and share credit. Anxiety, nervousness, and a preference for mediated or familiar contexts remain. Family breadth, hostile celebrity exposure, and prolonged solo leadership remain below R4.
