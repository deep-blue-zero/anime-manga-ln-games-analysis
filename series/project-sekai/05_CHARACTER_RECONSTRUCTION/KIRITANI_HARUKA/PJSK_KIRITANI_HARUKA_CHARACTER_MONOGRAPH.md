# Kiritani Haruka — provisional character monograph

## Authority snapshot

`MMJ-EP-HR-E0133` is the current producer-method owner. Preserve `MMJ-HR-MS-04` for formation, `MMJ-EP-HR-E0094` for chosen friendship, and other bounded capacity/rest evidence rather than treating producer competence as a total successor.

## Character thesis

Haruka is a disciplined public professional whose strongest virtue and danger share a source: she takes her effect on others seriously. She can deliver hope, organize work, and protect people with composed courtesy; when Mai's injury becomes linked to her encouragement, the same ethic expands into impossible responsibility. Her development depends on distinguishing accountable influence from total causal ownership, and on allowing first-person desire, rest, friendship, and received help to coexist with public duty.

## Developmental architecture

The main story shows that Haruka's retirement is neither whim nor simple fear of performance. She has tried to return, withholds details to protect Mai, and compensates through more effort until movement toward the stage becomes impossible. Minori's performance helps because it supplies specific recipient evidence and asks what Haruka wants. Haruka can then state the wish to see the stage view again and accept physical support. Founding MMJ is her action: she chooses the particular people who gave her hope.

Later evidence widens the model beyond recovery. `MMJ-EP-HR-E0094` establishes that chosen leisure and friendship are not failures of professionalism. EVENT_0115 shows public boundaries and ordinary animal-care leisure. EVENT_0133 builds a producer method: define several criteria, seek outside perspective, separate reach from identity, compare risks, learn recipients on site, revise the plan, and inspect the outcome. She accepts a producer role only within collective governance. EVENT_0130 preserves hope delivery as a shared expansion criterion rather than a unilateral Haruka command.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Polished courtesy can express genuine care, professional control, or protective withholding.
- She is decisive in logistics while slower to admit a personal need.
- High endurance is real but can conceal harmful self-demand.
- She values evidence from recipients and can revise a plan without surrendering standards.
- Friendship and rest must remain chosen capacities, not proof that career injury vanished.

## Relationship conditioning

Minori is both admirer and causal witness whose specific testimony can reach Haruka. Airi permits exacting professional disagreement; Shizuku offers composed mutual aid. Mai remains a bounded relationship with incomplete disclosure and unresolved injury. Saki provides a distinct hope-reciprocity relation. As producer, Haruka owes the other members information, consent, and shared labor rather than substituting competence for governance.

## Current state and open limits

At EVENT_0140 Haruka can select, test, and revise public strategy while preserving group identity. Guilt, overwork risk, and protective withholding remain live. Adversarial industry exploitation and a renewed performance block are extrapolative beyond R3's strongest lane.
