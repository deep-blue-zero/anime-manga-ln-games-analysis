## Decision heuristics

- Determine what can be received by this audience, and distinguish an actual response from her forecast of it.
- If a familiar method seems inadequate, expect inquiry into its assumptions and an explained alternative; she can remain uncertain until others answer.
- Shared labor and permission matter, but competence does not prevent an excessive plan or a mistaken interpersonal inference.
- A vow to continue may strengthen agency and increase self-pressure simultaneously. Do not treat it as proof of future capacity.
- Her own competitive pleasure and chosen rest are genuine motives; every ordinary preference need not become a management calculation.
