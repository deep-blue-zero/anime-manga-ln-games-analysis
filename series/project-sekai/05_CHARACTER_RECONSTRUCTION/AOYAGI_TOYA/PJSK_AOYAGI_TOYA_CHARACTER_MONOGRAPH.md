## Character thesis

Toya is a disciplined musician whose restraint formed inside coercive excellence. Classical training gave him ability, perception, and genuine wonder while also making music a site of paternal control and shame. His departure is therefore not a rejection of every classical experience; it is a claim to authorship over how that history will be used. Quiet care, loyalty, and precise observation are stable. Under threatened belonging, however, shame can produce decisive withdrawal and words that hurt more than his usual calm suggests.

## Developmental architecture

The main story establishes a capable, controlled performer who is fragile about whether his chosen Street life is legitimate. Conflict with Akito makes Toya withdraw in a way that harms the partnership. Repair requires owning desire and accepting that ability and history need not dictate his father's route. `VBS-EP-TY-E0012` captures an early operative around chosen music and partnership.

EVENT_0103 advances the Toya–father relation to conditional permission without implying full reconciliation. EVENT_0125 makes the central integration explicit: preserve classical wonder, acquired skill, coercive harm, and chosen departure; place Toya's history beside his partners' histories; transform all four into music. Dizziness and sleep loss prohibit a safe-pacing claim. EVENT_0139 turns the integration outward. Toya recovers admiration, discloses bounded struggle, diagnoses a learner's mechanics, accepts peer insight, centers the intended sound, returns success to the learner, and retains first-sound joy as a future hardship anchor.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Calm and precision coexist with intense shame under threatened belonging.
- Classical knowledge is both strength and injury; neither cancels the other.
- He offers care quietly through attention, practice, and exact feedback.
- He can accept another person's insight without protecting expert status.
- Poor pacing remains possible despite sophisticated musical self-understanding.

## Relationship conditioning

Akito is a necessary chosen partner, not an obligation that Toya can repay into independence. Shared pleasure and practical availability precede the current crisis. An and Kohane are distinct coauthors and colleagues; English ability can help them without making Toya socially or linguistically flawless. Tsukasa and Saki retain longstanding welcome, while Nene's gaming relation and Kanade's composition exchange remain separate.

Harumichi supplies real skill and occasional recognition alongside coercive history; broad reconciliation is unshown. Shuji's acknowledgment and lodging help are actual, with further family conversation still open. Lloyd supplies a possible bridge rather than certified access to the father's mind. Slade receives equal rivalry and confidentiality; Cedric receives selected partnership history, while his current distrust remains unresolved. Toya can enjoy being cared for without a permanent cure for repayment-driven overwork.

## Current state and open limits

Toya can use mixed musical history across forms, teach responsively, ask for family help and act in more ordinary social settings. He still risks sleep loss, overpractice, self-disqualification and excessive repayment. His present agency includes declining violin. The main tournament, warehouse explanation and complete family repair remain open. R4 concerns a bounded textual model with counterexamples; it is not broad social mastery, clinical judgment or performed-voice reconstruction.
