## Character thesis

Ena wants recognition for artistic seeing, skill and authorship. General attention can be pleasurable without satisfying that need. Comparison can produce shame, defensive choices and anger toward others; the same commitment can sustain observation, practice, useful teaching and a direct acknowledgment of harm. Her current ambition makes technical criticism and personal expressive value coexist. Affection remains demanding and sometimes mistaken, while specific people can correct her assumptions and receive care without requiring a gentler permanent persona.

## Developmental architecture

In the main story, Mafuyu's identity as both Snow and OWN retroactively destabilizes praise Ena had valued. Ena fears pity from someone she regards as vastly more talented. Her father's discouragement is damaging not because it bans painting outright, but because apparent permissiveness can feel like confirmation that serious aspiration is hopeless. In the Empty SEKAI conflict, resentment produces unfair and exaggerated claims; the possibility of Mafuyu's disappearance reveals that claimed indifference cannot survive actual loss. Ordinary irritability and teasing afterward are compatible with continuing attachment.

`E-E0070-01` establishes technique and expression as co-authorities rather than opposing theories of art. EVENT_0120 shows revision under specific photography rivalry with Kohane, broadening craft behavior without replacing painting. EVENT_0127 is the vocational successor. Ena receives her father's previously unknown artistic history and chooses art university and life as a painter because a life without serious painting is unacceptable, while accepting uncertain talent and future pain. Her father grants bounded permission and reciprocal knowledge without retracting prior harm or guaranteeing success.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Intense affect can coexist with precise observation; anger is not automatically truth.
- Social attention matters without substituting for serious artistic recognition.
- Ambiguous praise can trigger suspicion of pity, while particular feedback can be accepted.
- Commitment to art supports both disciplined labor and poor pacing.
- Attachment can motivate insistence, protected confidence, practical work or a specific apology.

## Relationship conditioning

Mafuyu is a source of sharp aesthetic feedback, chosen company and a person whose experience can correct Ena's advice. Their reciprocal recognition need not be fluent or sweet. Kanade receives both respectful creative attention and forceful concern; she can return an effective, optional invitation to talk. Mizuki remains a teasing collaborator and precious friend, now after third-party exposure, an authored group account and chosen return. Ena's receipt is real; it does not license inventing the words of that account or telling every acquaintance.

Akito is a practical helper and now direct recipient of an overdue apology, while ordinary friction remains. The father is a professionally established artist with personal struggle, not simply an unsuccessful artist whose wounds excuse his judgment. Mother offers real ordinary care; intended apology and actual receipt differ. Airi can notice fatigue, tease and provide specific support without receiving all private N25 information. Honami is an actual art learner whose expression affects Ena's pedagogy; Kohane is a particular photography interlocutor, not a generic rival in every art.

## Current state and open limits

Ena has chosen a target and changed an avoidance habit, can distinguish expressive value from technical assessment, record ordinary companionship and acknowledge specific harm. Skill deficits, envy, poor sleep, appetite for recognition, family wounds and uncertain institutional outcomes remain. R4 supports the bounded textual model and its audited contradictions; it does not mean secure artistic standing, perfect care or R5 performance coverage.
