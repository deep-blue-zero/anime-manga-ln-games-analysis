# Shinonome Ena — provisional character monograph

## Authority snapshot

Use `E-E0127-01` as Ena's current global state. Preserve `E-E0070-01`, the N25 main-story artistic-self-worth foundation, family/sibling relations, and bounded cross-unit craft evidence as separate authorities.

## Character thesis

Ena's valued self is artistic. Social attention can be desired and enjoyed, but it does not substitute for recognition of judgment, technique, and expressive authorship. Comparison therefore strikes at identity quickly. Shame can become anger, anger can become work, and attachment can appear as demand or confrontation rather than comfort. These patterns make her perceptive as well as volatile: she detects aesthetic distinctions and status implications that smoother speakers may avoid.

## Developmental architecture

In the main story, Mafuyu's identity as both Snow and OWN retroactively destabilizes praise Ena had valued. Ena fears pity from someone she regards as vastly more talented. Her father's discouragement is damaging not because it bans painting outright, but because apparent permissiveness can feel like confirmation that serious aspiration is hopeless. In the Empty SEKAI conflict, resentment produces unfair and exaggerated claims; the possibility of Mafuyu's disappearance reveals that claimed indifference cannot survive actual loss. Ordinary irritability and teasing afterward are compatible with continuing attachment.

`E-E0070-01` establishes technique and expression as co-authorities rather than opposing theories of art. EVENT_0120 shows revision under specific photography rivalry with Kohane, broadening craft behavior without replacing painting. EVENT_0127 is the vocational successor. Ena reads her father's hidden artistic history and chooses art university and life as a painter because a life without serious painting is unacceptable, while accepting uncertain talent and future pain. Her father grants bounded permission and reciprocal knowledge without retracting prior harm or guaranteeing success.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- High affect and sharp judgment can coexist with excellent perception.
- Social validation matters, but cannot satisfy artistic recognition needs.
- Ambiguous praise may be tested for pity or condescension.
- Anger can injure and can mobilize disciplined work; it is not automatically honesty.
- Attachment may be visible as insistence, criticism, or refusal to accept disappearance.

## Relationship conditioning

Mafuyu is an aesthetic and relational provocateur whose opacity activates status fear and care. Kanade's work earns respect; Kanade can receive Ena's sharp challenge when self-exclusion becomes visible. Mizuki permits teasing and aesthetic collaboration while retaining a disclosure boundary Ena must not be assumed to know. Akito activates sibling irritation and practical reciprocal care. Ena's father is both wound source and newly legible failed artist; knowledge complicates rather than erases accountability. Kohane is a bounded craft rival.

## Current state and open limits

At the cutoff Ena has chosen a painter's life and can accept uncertainty as part of the choice. Talent anxiety, comparison, paternal harm, and future institutional judgment remain live. R4 is withheld pending longer vocational outcomes and a formal contradiction audit.
