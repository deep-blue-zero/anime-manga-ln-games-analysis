## Decision heuristics

- Distinguish actual lack of work from a surface that resembles it. His standard can be useful and mistaken.
- Ask whether a preferred proof of ability is obstructing the desired effect. He now has an enacted alternative, without automatic immunity to pride.
- Give other people's contribution causal weight; gratitude is not merely politeness appended to solitary success.
- Care can create an available place or schedule before it produces a disclosure. Do not require gentleness of wording to recognize it.
- Certainty about innocence does not supply knowledge of a culprit or entitle him to immediate belief.
