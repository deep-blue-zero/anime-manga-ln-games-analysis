# Shinonome Akito — provisional character monograph

## Authority snapshot

`VBS-EP-AK-E0109` is the current expressive-release operative. Preserve `VBS-EP-AK-E0021`, `VBS-AKITO-MS3`, family and fear-specific evidence as separate authorities. EVENT_0140 does not advance his state.

## Character thesis

Akito is an intensely labor-oriented performer who distrusts aspiration unsupported by work. His harshness can expose real gaps, but it is not a universal authenticity detector: he can misread surfaces, project his own standard, or take blame in ways that obscure other people's agency. He maintains strong context-dependent courtesy outside close rivalry and can revise when evidence defeats his judgment. Care is often expressed through preparation, challenge, risk reduction, and acceptance of dependence rather than gentle declaration.

## Developmental architecture

The main story joins ambition to humiliation and repeated practice. Akito initially tests Kohane and treats visible inexperience as evidence of insufficient commitment. Her return and performance require revision. The partnership with Toya also exposes a contradiction: Akito values equal chosen labor yet can make himself the causal owner of a shared conflict. Learning to accept care and distributed responsibility is as important as learning to praise talent.

`VBS-EP-AK-E0021` preserves an earlier high-intensity work and purpose authority. EVENT_0109 expands the model beyond disciplined anger. Akito recognizes suppressed joy, fear, grief, gratitude, and ambition; with the group's help he directs them toward Ken, treats the mentor's song as gift and origin, converts the whole history into performance, and receives listener verification. EVENT_0126 adds bounded, consent-sensitive fear work with Max: specific preparation and chosen exposure produce one workable partnership, not a universal cure for dog fear. EVENT_0135 preserves him as a strong causal contributor to surpassal without replacing the current operative.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Courtesy toward outsiders and bluntness with close peers are both genuine social modes.
- High standards can motivate improvement or become inaccurate gatekeeping.
- He notices concrete weakness quickly and is slower to identify exhaustion or emotional suppression in himself.
- He can accept dependence after resistance and return credit when outcomes make distribution undeniable.
- Fear-specific success does not generalize automatically.

## Relationship conditioning

Toya is the closest musical partner and a site of trust, blame, challenge, and dependence. An permits competitive directness tied to Street accountability. Kohane forces revision of status-based judgments. Ena activates sibling irritation, practical knowledge, and indirect care; neither sibling should become the other's therapist. Ken is mentor and recipient, not an infallible standard. Honami and Max evidence careful support under a specific fear condition.

## Current state and open limits

At the cutoff Akito can integrate suppressed affect into performance and receive specific listener verification. Overwork, impatience, comparison, and fear remain. Broad gentle-care contexts and sustained institutional subordination are weaker than his craft, rivalry, family, and mentorship lanes.
