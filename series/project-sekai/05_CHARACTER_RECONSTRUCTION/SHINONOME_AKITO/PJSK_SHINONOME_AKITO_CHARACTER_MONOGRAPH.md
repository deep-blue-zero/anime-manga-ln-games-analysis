# Shinonome Akito — provisional character monograph

## Authority snapshot

Use `VBS-EP-AK-E0187`, inheriting E0146 renewed world-directed heat and E0109 expressive release, while retaining independent E0021 reciprocal reliance. Group E0193 is actual signed support, E0204 is main-tournament qualification, and E0216 is unresolved accusation and divided rival trust. E0221's recap does not repeal the omitted E0187 method.

## Character thesis

Akito is an intensely labor-oriented performer who distrusts aspiration unsupported by work. His harshness can expose real gaps, but it is not a universal authenticity detector: he can misread surfaces, project his own standard, or take blame in ways that obscure other people's agency. He maintains strong context-dependent courtesy outside close rivalry and can revise when evidence defeats his judgment. Care is often expressed through preparation, challenge, risk reduction, and acceptance of dependence rather than gentle declaration.

## Developmental architecture

The main story joins ambition to humiliation and repeated practice. Akito initially tests Kohane and treats visible inexperience as evidence of insufficient commitment. Her return and performance require revision. The partnership with Toya also exposes a contradiction: Akito values equal chosen labor yet can make himself the causal owner of a shared conflict. Learning to accept care and distributed responsibility is as important as learning to praise talent.

`VBS-EP-AK-E0021` preserves an earlier reciprocal-reliance authority within sustained work and chosen purpose. EVENT_0109 expands the model beyond disciplined anger. Akito recognizes suppressed joy, fear, grief, gratitude, and ambition; with the group's help he directs them toward Ken, treats the mentor's song as gift and origin, converts the whole history into performance, and receives listener verification. EVENT_0126 adds bounded, consent-sensitive fear work with Max: specific preparation and chosen exposure produce one workable partnership, not a universal cure for dog fear. EVENT_0135 preserves him as a strong causal contributor to surpassal without replacing the current operative.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Courtesy toward outsiders and bluntness with close peers are both genuine social modes.
- High standards can motivate improvement or become inaccurate gatekeeping.
- He notices concrete weakness quickly and is slower to identify exhaustion or emotional suppression in himself.
- He can accept dependence after resistance and return credit when outcomes make distribution undeniable.
- Fear-specific success does not generalize automatically.

## Relationship conditioning

Toya is a chosen partner whose competence Akito can admire and whose dependence he does not need to force into speech. Reciprocal reliance remains independent of the current problem-solving method. An receives competition and blunt familiarity; Kohane receives revised judgment and usable advice, not perpetual novice status. Ken's importance does not make every mentor action infallible. Slade is a stimulating rival and later an explicit trusting witness; Akito does not receive his private hearing disclosure.

Alan is a particular professional whose feedback and institutional role matter, not an all-knowing savior. No.9's signed contract supplies resources with terms not fully displayed. Ena's apology is actually received while irritation survives; Airi's earlier request and later mutual thanks do not make Akito solely responsible for Ena's wellbeing. A struggling friend's career requires inquiry and adaptation rather than projection of Akito's chosen path. Honami and Max remain the bounded fear-specific relationship, not a cure for every dog encounter.

## Current state and open limits

Akito can mobilize present resources toward an intended effect, with real institutional support and international experience. His wish for proof through individual ability, overwork, comparison, impatience and fear remain. The warehouse accusation is unresolved; main-tournament performance lies ahead. R4 covers a reviewed textual model with ordinary, professional and relationship counterexamples, not permanent emotional regulation, guaranteed institutional resistance or heard performance.
