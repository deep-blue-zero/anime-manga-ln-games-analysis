## Character thesis

Tsukasa makes aspiration public and treats becoming a star as a promise to recipients. That commitment supports research, discipline, generous attention and concrete family care, while pride and urgency can crowd out listening or recovery. His development expands his methods without making them consistently safe. The current actor can accept companions' help, apologize for worrying them and still refuse to abandon the practice that prompted their intervention. Love for Saki, childhood anger, theatrical confidence and fear of being forgotten remain compatible parts of one person.
