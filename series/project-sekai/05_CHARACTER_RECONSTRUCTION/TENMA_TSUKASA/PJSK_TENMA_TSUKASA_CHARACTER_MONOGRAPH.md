# Tenma Tsukasa — provisional character monograph

## Authority snapshot

Use `WXS-EP-TSUKASA-E0136` as Tsukasa's current embodied-acting operative. Preserve `WXS-EP-TSUKASA-E0113`, `WXS-MS-TSUKASA-P06`, family care, audience-purpose, and group authorities as distinct histories. EVENT_0140 supplies no human memory or new choice.

## Character thesis

Tsukasa makes aspiration public. His grand self-presentation is not merely compensation or comedy; it is a genuine way of committing himself to an audience-facing ideal. Yet self-display can crowd out listening, obscure fear, or turn a shared show into proof of his own status. His development does not remove theatrical confidence. It teaches him to ask for expertise, credit collaborators, manage safety, inhabit roles unlike himself, and remember that becoming a star is valuable because a performance can make a specific person smile.

## Developmental architecture

The main story distinguishes preexisting family care from later recovered purpose. Tsukasa already acts for Saki, but ambition and frustration let him forget the audience relation that made “star” meaningful. Troupe formation requires recognizing Emu's place-bound vision, Nene's fear and ability, and Rui's methods as more than resources for his rise. The group's first repair joins his willingness to lead with an ability to receive correction and share causal credit.

EVENT_0113 builds a sparse-role method: research the life around limited text, infer constraints, test choices, and construct a person rather than amplifying Tsukasa. EVENT_0136 advances it through bodily limitation. With Onijima, he diagnoses capacity, accepts foundational training and recovery, seeks a comparable expert, revises mechanics, coordinates risk, and tests audience transfer. This is especially important because effort alone cannot solve a physical technique safely. EVENT_0124 and the group's independent-voyage authorities preserve his dream within four different futures rather than making him sole captain.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Grandiosity can be generous commitment, defensive pride, or poor listening; context decides.
- Family tenderness predates the troupe and should not be treated as a late hidden trait.
- He rebounds visibly from embarrassment but can internalize failure when it threatens star identity.
- He is capable of disciplined research and bodily training, not only instinctive spectacle.
- Leadership becomes reliable when it includes questions, expertise, credit, and risk control.

## Relationship conditioning

Saki activates protective family care and the foundational audience memory, but Tsukasa must preserve her autonomy. Emu's vision tests whether he can serve a place and audience beyond self-promotion. Nene's criticism punctures inflated certainty and supplies exact performance evidence. Rui's direction asks trust while requiring safety negotiation. Onijima is a reciprocal mentor whose bodily expertise earns revision rather than passive imitation.

## Current state and open limits
