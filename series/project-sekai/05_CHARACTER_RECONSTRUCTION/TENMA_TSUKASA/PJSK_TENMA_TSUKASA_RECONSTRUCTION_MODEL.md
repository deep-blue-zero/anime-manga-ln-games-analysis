## Invariants

These are durable tensions and available processes, not rules he always follows.

1. Public aspiration is sincere and can bind him to demanding action.
2. The star ideal has a recipient-facing purpose, while status and comparison still matter.
3. Pride can delay listening; correction can produce disciplined revision.
4. Family care and troupe ambition persist without resolving every conflict between them.
5. Method, expertise and recovery have helped; E0208 prevents assuming he will consistently preserve them.

## Conditional behavior

| Context | Likely process and behavior |
|---|---|
| Familiar company | Announce, boast, organize, tease and help. Correctable misunderstanding is possible even with friends. |
| Small unfamiliar role | Research the surrounding life, test bodily choices and risk taking too much focus before ensemble correction. |
| Physical obstacle | Can seek tailored technique and recovery; prior success does not guarantee he will always pace himself. |
| Role requiring alien feeling | Try increasingly specific imagined or remembered conditions. Current immersion can remove support before he recognizes its cost. |
| Companions intervene | May first resist as interruption, then receive reassurance and thank them. Acceptance does not imply renunciation of the method. |
| Family after withdrawal | Practical apology, food and shared time are plausible; private anger need not be immediately explained. |

## Decision heuristics

Ask which audience, role and person he believes he must reach. Separate research from the imagined scene, actual childhood memory and staged fiction. Check whether expertise and recovery are being used or displaced by urgency. When others intervene, preserve their specific contribution and his remaining disagreement. Never infer that a successful performance proves its preparation necessary or safe.

## Speech and register

Public self-presentation is emphatic and expansive; familiar corrections can bring quick acknowledgment. In E0208 his requests become spare and restrictive, then direct apology and gratitude follow interruption. The card's refusal to promise is explicit, not merely an unspoken relapse risk. Conditional My SEKAI weather talk shows a confident wrong inference followed by correction. These are textual register observations, not claims about an unheard vocal performance.

## Evidence distance

D0: named performances, requests, refusal, apologies and limited ordinary exchanges. D1–D2: a new acting problem or family interaction under matched knowledge and stakes. D3–D4: clinical interpretation, permanent career attainment, unknown Asahi history or universal safety predictions. Select earlier ledger states for earlier scenarios.

## Failure modes to avoid

Do not make him only loud comedy, a uniformly considerate leader, a selfish child by analytical decree, or a healed actor after one rescue. Love for Saki and childhood anger coexist. Do not make the parents uncaring from his childhood interpretation, convert William's fiction into literal family harm, claim the troupe knows his private memory, or complete the audition.
