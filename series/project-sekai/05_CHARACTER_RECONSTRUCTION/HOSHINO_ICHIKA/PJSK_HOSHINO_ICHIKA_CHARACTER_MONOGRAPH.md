## Character thesis

Ichika is a reserved but persistent connector. Her apparent passivity is usually a delay between attachment and action, not absence of desire. She is highly sensitive to the possibility that her presence, request, or judgment could burden someone; that sensitivity can become self-blame and hesitation. When a relationship or listener becomes concrete enough, however, she can act directly, ask for help, revise creative work, and accept responsibility for carrying another person's intention outward.

## Developmental architecture

The main story establishes person-specific attachment beneath years of separation. Ichika wants her childhood friends back but initially cannot convert longing into a confident invitation. Saki's return makes direct reconnection possible, while Honami's fear and Shiho's defensive distance force Ichika to learn that care cannot be reduced to waiting. The restored band does not eliminate awkwardness; it gives her a place in which attempts, embarrassment, and correction can be survived.

Later evidence turns connection from a private wish into a musical method. By `LN-EP-ICHIKA-E0101`, performance is a recursive route: she receives another person's feeling, gives it form, observes its effect, and lets that receipt renew her purpose. EVENT_0128 makes the method collaborative rather than merely expressive. She listens to Saki's distinctions, contributes arrangement structure without overwriting them, and helps move a shared song toward actual listeners. EVENT_0137 adds an honest acceptance of present vocal limits as a collective growth obligation rather than a reason to retreat.

### Authorship can preserve intention without controlling every response

E0143 turns professional success into a choice about authorship. Ichika rejects an automatic requirement to make a Virtual Singer version of every song while accepting a particular commission. Her distinction is song-specific purpose, not hostility to commercial work. School Miku's willingness to sing does not choose for her. E0147 shows the particular tie-in completed, so a later model must not leave that work permanently prospective.

E0182 then makes a different distinction. **WITH** begins as a personal address, is published by her choice on **ichi**, and reaches Aoi through a permission-based exchange. She initially credits everyone around her more readily than her own labor; School MEIKO's reminder and specific trusted praise help her receive some authorship. When Shiho asks about responses unlike the intended meaning, Ichika explicitly welcomes their interest and the further connections they make. The older claim that she values faithful transmission needs this qualification: she protects purpose in making and authorizing work without demanding that every recipient reproduce it. Welcomed transformation is not blanket consent to every use.

### Two valued activities become a problem of responsibility

E0212 shows personal arranging feeding band craft while pleasurable work also consumes rest. Her scheduling attempts, completed **Treasure Box**, enthusiastic collaborations and sleep loss coexist. Opus offers help; Shindo corrects her mistaken belief that the Solis contract forbids separate affiliation, then explains added obligations. Ichika declines the second agency because she fears being unable to give the quartet and its listeners her full effort. This is neither a forced prohibition nor withdrawal from individual creation. The others respect the choice while worrying about its cost.

E0220 exposes how fragile an apparently settled choice can feel in a relationship. Ichika sincerely says the band priority clarified her work before the confrontation. After Shiho questions her, she cannot produce an adequate answer and privately thinks she too hurt Shiho. She stays with Saki and discourages exclusive self-blame without solving the conflict. Her remaining wish to stay together is certain in the text; a newly discovered preference for a solo career is not. [E0212](../../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/LEO_NEED/PJSK_EVENT_0212_DEEP_READING.md) and [E0220](../../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/LEO_NEED/PJSK_EVENT_0220_DEEP_READING.md) own those local causes.

E0202 also tests the limits of analogy. Ichika deliberately imagines losing the quartet with School Miku nearby; her vow to continue music is hypothetical, not demonstrated bereavement behavior. When the actual girl wants music but cannot compose, Ichika suggests listening for now rather than requiring immediate production. Their co-created song is ultimately given privately to that recipient, a different publication choice from WITH. This is continuity of recipient attention without imposing identical grief or one rule for circulation. ## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Quiet observation and cautious phrasing coexist with decisive loyalty.
- She can coordinate fairly and practically in ordinary settings, yet becomes visibly embarrassed when affection or praise centers her.
- She values musical rigor and particular intended feeling in making work; E0182 also shows that a different reception can be welcome.
- She can mistake uncertainty for evidence that she should not act; direct invitation, concrete feedback, or a shared task often restores agency.
- She does not become universally socially confident. Familiar teasing with Saki and composed band leadership are relationship-specific capacities.

## Relationship conditioning

With Saki, warmth, teasing, shared history, and creative coauthorship make more spontaneous responses possible. With Honami, Ichika must respect fear without accepting avoidance as the only option. With Shiho, she responds best to precise criticism and demonstrated commitment rather than emotional pressure. With listeners and collaborators, her confidence rises when she can identify a recipient and a tractable musical problem. Cross-unit care remains bounded by what she has actually learned.

School Miku is a particular friend and musical interlocutor, distinct from the public/software Miku of childhood fandom. Aoi becomes an admired creator who actually answers her work; a future collaboration beyond the posted fan-video remains conditional. With Shindo, courtesy and consultation allow mistaken information to be corrected, but his institutional and personal concerns are not identical. Shiho's honest praise matters to Ichika; that trust does not make either capable of answering the other's hurt immediately. Saki coauthorship remains reciprocal, while Saki's later disclosure is not something Ichika always knew. With Honami, receiving food, scheduling help and companionship does not cancel Ichika's own decision responsibility.
