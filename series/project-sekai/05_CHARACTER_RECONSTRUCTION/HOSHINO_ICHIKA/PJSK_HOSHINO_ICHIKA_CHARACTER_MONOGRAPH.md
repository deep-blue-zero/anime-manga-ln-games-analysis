# Hoshino Ichika — provisional character monograph

## Authority snapshot

This generation models Ichika after Leo/need's debut release and shared decision to enter an undefined professional future, while preserving all earlier states. EVENT_0140 adds no human knowledge or choice; `LN-EP-ICHIKA-E0128` remains her current operative. This is an R3 textual model, not an R4 contradiction-complete or voice-performance model.

## Character thesis

Ichika is a reserved but persistent connector. Her apparent passivity is usually a delay between attachment and action, not absence of desire. She is highly sensitive to the possibility that her presence, request, or judgment could burden someone; that sensitivity can become self-blame and hesitation. When a relationship or listener becomes concrete enough, however, she can act directly, ask for help, revise creative work, and accept responsibility for carrying another person's intention outward.

## Developmental architecture

The main story establishes person-specific attachment beneath years of separation. Ichika wants her childhood friends back but initially cannot convert longing into a confident invitation. Saki's return makes direct reconnection possible, while Honami's fear and Shiho's defensive distance force Ichika to learn that care cannot be reduced to waiting. The restored band does not eliminate awkwardness; it gives her a place in which attempts, embarrassment, and correction can be survived.

Later evidence turns connection from a private wish into a musical method. By `LN-EP-ICHIKA-E0101`, performance is a recursive route: she receives another person's feeling, gives it form, observes its effect, and lets that receipt renew her purpose. EVENT_0128 makes the method collaborative rather than merely expressive. She listens to Saki's distinctions, contributes arrangement structure without overwriting them, and helps move a shared song toward actual listeners. EVENT_0137 adds an honest acceptance of present vocal limits as a collective growth obligation rather than a reason to retreat.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Quiet observation and cautious phrasing coexist with decisive loyalty.
- She can coordinate fairly and practically in ordinary settings, yet becomes visibly embarrassed when affection or praise centers her.
- She values musical rigor, but her deepest criterion is whether the work preserves and reaches a particular person's intention.
- She can mistake uncertainty for evidence that she should not act; direct invitation, concrete feedback, or a shared task often restores agency.
- She does not become universally socially confident. Familiar teasing with Saki and composed band leadership are relationship-specific capacities.

## Relationship conditioning

With Saki, warmth, teasing, shared history, and creative coauthorship make more spontaneous responses possible. With Honami, Ichika must respect fear without accepting avoidance as the only option. With Shiho, she responds best to precise criticism and demonstrated commitment rather than emotional pressure. With listeners and collaborators, her confidence rises when she can identify a recipient and a tractable musical problem. Cross-unit care remains bounded by what she has actually learned.

## Current state and open limits

At EVENT_0140, Ichika can lead recipient-aware arrangement, acknowledge limitations, and choose forward movement with Leo/need. She still risks over-attributing relational difficulty to herself, under-asserting an untested preference, and relying on familiar partners to make intent legible. Family breadth, unfamiliar-authority conflict, and performed vocal nuance remain below R4 coverage.
