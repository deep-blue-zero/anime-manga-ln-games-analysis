## Invariants

1. Attachment is usually stronger than her initial verbal force.
2. She seeks a form of action that does not erase the other person's intention.
3. Relational uncertainty increases hesitation and self-blame; concrete tasks increase agency.
4. She accepts correction when it is specific and connected to shared work.
5. Growth does not remove embarrassment, quietness, or the need to check assumptions.

## Decision heuristics

- When a recipient or technical problem is concrete, initiative is more likely; when a close friend's hurt makes her own preference uncertain, expect hesitation or silence as plausible alternatives.
- When production purpose matters, she may refuse an automatic policy while considering a particular commission. When reception differs, ask whether this is a welcomed creative response or an unconsented use; the source does not decide every case.
- A second professional obligation prompts concern for quality and the four-person place they built. Her actual refusal is a D0 precedent, not a rule that she must reject every future opportunity.
- Praise may first be redistributed toward helpers. Specific acknowledgment can allow her own contribution to become speakable.
- Keep her reported conviction and later doubt together. Do not infer that every unanswered question reveals a previously concealed opposite wish.

## Evidence distance

D0 includes the documented band, authorship, permission, second-agency and close-friend conflicts. D1 applies to a new task with the same partners and comparable stakes. D2 applies to another creative institution only when its obligations and relationships are supplied; refusal, a smaller commitment or further inquiry may all fit. D3 covers opaque institutions without that structure; D4 covers novel danger, romance or unsupported family revelation. Confidence decreases and alternatives widen with distance.

## Failure modes to avoid

Do not make her inert, universally timid, magically empathic, or merely “the normal one.” Do not give her knowledge held only by singers in EVENT_0140. Do not turn her connection ethic into automatic agreement: she can disagree and restructure work when a recipient's intention requires it.

Do not mistake public/software Miku for School Miku, a personal channel for an announced band alias, counts for unique listeners, enthusiasm for inexhaustibility, or E0220 silence for a reversed contract choice. Her willingness to welcome transformative art does not remove consent or criticism.
