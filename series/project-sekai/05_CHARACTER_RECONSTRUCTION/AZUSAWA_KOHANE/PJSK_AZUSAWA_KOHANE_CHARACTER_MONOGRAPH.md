## Character thesis

Kohane's courage is conditional rather than magical. She begins with real social hesitation and a habit of minimizing herself, but a sufficiently specific attachment, musical interest, or desired effect can outweigh fear and produce startling initiative. Growth changes what she is willing to act for and what evidence she has about her own capacity; it does not erase nervousness, dependence on preparation, or the need to distinguish chosen excitement from obligation.

## Developmental architecture

The main story gives Kohane an attraction to Street music before it gives her a secure identity inside it. An's invitation, supported practice, public collapse, and Kohane's chosen return matter as a sequence: confidence is not conferred by praise alone. She needs repeated embodied evidence that she wants to sing and can continue after failure. The group formation also teaches that partnership does not require Kohane to remain a protected novice.

Later evidence makes her agency increasingly recipient- and horizon-directed. EVENT_0103 creates a singer-pride operative within a mentorship relation with Ken. EVENT_0120 shows technical photography used toward a recipient and a bounded competitive relation with Ena; competence outside singing does not replace the musical owner. EVENT_0129 requires Kohane to receive An's fear without shrinking herself to protect the partnership. In EVENT_0135 she accepts repeated defeat and partner rivalry, separates duty from chosen excitement, reads plural audience attachments, and performs from a wish to carry the whole Street into an unknown world. The local benchmark is surpassed, but the new world horizon remains deliberately undefined.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Hesitation can coexist with decisive follow-through once the goal becomes concrete.
- She values preparation and expert feedback; support is not proof of incapacity.
- Praise is easier to receive when tied to observed work than to a global claim of talent.
- She can be competitive without becoming hostile or ceasing to care for An.
- Ordinary interests and technical curiosity broaden her beyond a linear “shy-to-confident” arc.
