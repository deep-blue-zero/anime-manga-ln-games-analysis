## Character thesis

Mafuyu's self is difficult to identify and sustain under accumulated expectations, not absent by nature. Public competence, Snow's collaborative manner, private uncertainty, OWN's search and crisis bluntness have different conditions. Increasing agency is neither a simple unveiling nor a steady recovery: she can speak, leave, trust, return, suppress herself again, accept inclusion and choose selective candor. Important relationships make some wishes more usable while the impulse to protect others can still endanger the wish to be found.

## Developmental architecture

The main story shows an accomplished public good-girl role and an artistically specific online collaborator alongside a private inability to identify ordinary wants. OWN is not simple authentic expression; it is a failed search made audible. Disappearance becomes attractive as escape from the search-and-disappointment cycle. The breakthrough is limited but real: Mafuyu wants to be found. She can choose contact, sit near Kanade, borrow a meal choice as scaffolding, initiate privately, use Kanade's name, and express gratitude while uncertainty remains.

EVENT_0100 advances this into enacted survival separation from her mother, not complete autonomy. EVENT_0112 adds a shared identity-search method: pause unsafe retrieval, return with companions, preserve a memory object holding warmth and pain, recover creative evidence, state a tentative wish to remain beside sufferers, and consent to change together. EVENT_0123 provides a qualified ordinary social test without proving recovered taste. EVENT_0134 separates fear from evidence. Mafuyu recognizes imagined paternal rejection as fear rather than memory, gathers mixed childhood and present evidence, and chooses full disclosure. Her father receives it, apologizes, and commits to autonomy; maternal fear, fatigue, preference difficulty, and long-term outcome remain active.

So are renewed suggestions about study and career, her mother's distress, and Mafuyu's conclusion that protecting her mother requires suppressing her own difficulty. ## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Helpful public competence can be real without being entirely self-authored or costless.
- A private answer can be quiet, precise, teasing or uncertain; crisis bluntness is not the universal real voice.
- A narrow preference can become usable before a general feeling or future can be named.
- Affection and guilt can inhibit action after earlier agency has been demonstrated.
- Care for another and preservation of her own need can conflict; recognizing this does not guarantee prevention.

## Relationship conditioning

Kanade is chosen company and a collaborator Mafuyu actively cares for; relief and reciprocity coexist with self-suppression and unequal information. Ena supplies demanding artistic attention, ordinary friction and pictures explicitly qualified as her own seeing. Mizuki's former temporary-retreat advice becomes part of a relationship that now receives Mafuyu's future support and returned thanks. E0150 gives Mafuyu Mizuki's chosen account without giving the reader its precise words.

Honami already knows a quieter domestic manner and asks rather than assuming what it means. Shizuku's E0207 welcome and Kohane's E0189 inclusion are distinct, directly received forms of support. Emu's response to smile differences does not give her the entire family history. The father has now acted after failed cohabitation; his earlier ally status was not a guarantee. Mother's love, actual apology, continuing pressure and Mafuyu's guilt belong in the same history. Empty Miku/KAITO witness E0200; other local singers, other manifestations and human acquaintances do not automatically share it.

## Current state and open limits

Mafuyu can choose limited candor, welcome shared success, advise from unfinished experience and identify particular wanted sensations or company. Family attachment, loss of self-directed need, uneven preference access and refuge dependence remain unresolved. R4 concerns the model's ability to explain uneven change and known counterexamples; it does not certify safety, complete emotion or sensory recovery, permanent housing or a chosen vocation.
