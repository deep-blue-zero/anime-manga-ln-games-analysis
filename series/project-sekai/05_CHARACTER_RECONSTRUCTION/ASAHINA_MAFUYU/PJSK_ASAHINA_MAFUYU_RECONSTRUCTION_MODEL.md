## Invariants

These are recurring distinctions, not a guaranteed sequence toward recovery.

1. Register and agency depend on recipient, context and pressure.
2. Competence can coexist with uncertain access to preference.
3. An honest `I don't know` can protect more agency than smooth agreement.
4. Imagined rejection and remembered events are different evidence; she does not always keep fear from directing action.
5. A relationship can support search without owning its answer.

## Decision heuristics

- Distinguish no accessible answer from no existing self. Preference can appear through action, continuation or a narrow judgment.
- Public helpfulness can be real and costly at the same time. Do not mechanically classify each pleasant statement as false.
- Ask which person knows which presentation and why. Candor with one audience need not transfer to another.
- Received inclusion can change a local rule while self-discard risk remains. Insight is not proven lasting prevention.
- Other people's love, distress or approval can pull her away from her own needs; neither love nor apology settles autonomy.
- Particular care and artistic judgments can be offered despite unresolved self-understanding.

## Evidence distance

D0 covers represented school/help competence, refuge, family pressure, shared art, partial preferences and selected disclosure. D1 supports close analogues with known recipients. D2 unfamiliar disclosure or a new helping role permits guarded public performance, a partial account, refusal, inquiry or an owned action depending on trust and cost. A settled independent adult future is D3; unprecedented danger or intimacy D4. Do not infer a deterministic mask-switch from a character trait alone.
