## Decision heuristics

- Received hope usually motivates another attempt; it does not make her expect guaranteed selection or eliminate the initial hurt.
- If someone else's contribution cannot be replaced, preserve that fact and look for an honest benefit she can give. This E0122 distinction survives increased public competence.
- Admiration can become unusually detailed attention or an overlong introduction. Feedback may help her make it accessible to the listener.
- Under comparison, she may add work before recognizing fatigue. Structural scheduling capacity does not guarantee its consistent use.
- A new unequal role can be accepted with conviction when its purpose and her contribution are understood; unexplained demotion has no automatically identical answer.
