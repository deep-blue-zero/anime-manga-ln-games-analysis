# Hanasato Minori — provisional character monograph

## Authority snapshot

Use `MMJ-EP-MN-E0122` for Minori's current recipient-hope method and preserve `MMJ-EP-MN-E0098` as the separate structural school/work-choice authority. `MMJ-MN-MS-04` remains the formation baseline. EVENT_0140 creates no new human knowledge or choice.

## Character thesis

Minori is an aspirant who converts received hope into repeated action. Her optimism is neither proof that she expects success nor ignorance of failure: it survives rejection because she treats effort as a way to make another next step possible. She is openly admiring and emotionally expressive, but capable of restraint, consultation, and technical work when she recognizes that enthusiasm alone cannot diagnose another person's need. Her persistent weakness is not lack of seriousness; it is a tendency to discount her own membership and competence when comparing herself with established professionals.

## Developmental architecture

The main story begins with Haruka's public words changing Minori's response to helplessness. Fifty-one failed auditions and weaker starting skill do not end her aspiration. Training under Airi and Shizuku gives persistence a corrective structure. When Haruka's crisis becomes legible, Minori worries that her encouragement may have caused harm, asks for help, and makes a recipient-specific performance whose effect she can describe without claiming a cure. At formation, the seniors must tell her that giving them hope already makes her a member.

Later evidence turns hope into a method under imperfect conditions. `MMJ-EP-MN-E0098` shows her making structural choices about school and work instead of assuming effort can fit anywhere. In EVENT_0122, an audience expectation cannot be met; she names that non-substitutability, identifies an honest benefit MMJ can provide, and works across production roles to deliver it. EVENT_0132 shows checked public-work competence beside Nene, plus voluntary ordinary time after work. EVENT_0130 situates her in a reciprocal audience circuit rather than a one-directional giver role.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Enthusiasm and awe coexist with careful respect for another person's decision.
- Failure hurts and can trigger self-comparison, but usually produces a new plan after a brief emotional response.
- She asks for expertise and can follow exacting instruction; persistence is not refusal to learn.
- She is comfortable praising others and less comfortable treating her own contribution as non-substitutable.
- Ordinary mistakes, energetic internal reactions, and professional reliability all belong to the same person.

## Relationship conditioning

Haruka remains admired but is also a chosen partner who can receive Minori's help. Airi's rigor creates instructional asymmetry without making Minori morally passive; Minori can stop Airi when conduct violates their shared idol ethic. Shizuku offers gentler instruction and familiar-address negotiation. With fans, clients, and cohosts, Minori should distinguish what was promised from what she can honestly deliver.

## Current state and open limits

At the cutoff, Minori can work independently, protect non-substitutability, and carry hope through disappointment. She still needs realistic scheduling, feedback, and protection against making belonging conditional on catching up. Broad hostile-publicity and long-term burnout evidence remain below R4.
