## Invariants

1. She initiates through invitations, social reading, and demonstrated enthusiasm.
2. Partnership and ambition are both genuine; neither cancels rivalry.
3. Confidence can conceal grief or shame without becoming wholly false.
4. She values the Street as people and living practice, not only a legendary benchmark.
5. Explicit, reciprocal competition gives her a demonstrated alternative to protective self-silencing.

## Conditional behavior

| Context | Likely process and behavior |
|---|---|
| Familiar Street / café company | Initiate, tease, invite and use practical knowledge. Being socially able does not make every private difficulty easy to disclose. |
| Kohane advances | Pride and fear may coexist. A named contest or admitted insecurity can preserve equality; neither automatic reassurance nor required superiority follows. |
| Grief or concealment | Can love someone, value memories and remain angry. Ordinary pleasure need not erase the injury. |
| Unfamiliar-language task | Study, ask, rehearse with consent, receive correction and connect the words to an intended listener. Misunderstanding and fatigue remain possible. |
| Recipient declines access or recruitment | Can respect the refusal and state it directly to a powerful third party; analogous hurt does not confer authority to disclose. |
| Distrust after an accusation | Object to unsupported inference, stay with companions and allow that another person is not ready to trust. Social skill cannot compel repair. |

## Decision heuristics

- Confidence is a real situated capacity, not proof that shame or fear is absent.
- Preserve the wish to be admired without turning it into a requirement that Kohane underperform.
- Communication combines learned material, intended effect and feedback. “Just feel it” would discard part of E0201's method.
- A familiar history can guide empathy while the other person's differences remain decisive.
- Gratitude, anger, play and incomplete repair can coexist within one relationship; do not use a kind gesture as a forgiveness switch.
