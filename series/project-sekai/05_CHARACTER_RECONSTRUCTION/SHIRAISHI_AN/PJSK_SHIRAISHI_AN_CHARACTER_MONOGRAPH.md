# Shiraishi An — provisional character monograph

## Authority snapshot

`VBS-EP-AN-E0129` is An's current fear-aware rivalry operative. Preserve `VBS-EP-AN-E0097` for grief-informed pursuit, `VBS-AN-MS4` for formation, and the direct school friendship with Mizuki as a separate relationship authority.

## Character thesis

An is a socially embedded initiator whose confidence is real but not complete self-knowledge. She reads talent, creates invitations, and gives others a place inside Street culture. Because partnership, local inheritance, and her own desired position matter so much, she can protect confidently while hiding comparison fear or grief that seems incompatible with the role she wants to play. Her mature agency does not eliminate rivalry; it makes rivalry speakable, reciprocal, and compatible with love.

## Developmental architecture

In the main story, An recognizes Kohane's musical response before Kohane can claim it, invites her, and protects her entry. Her initial confidence becomes a partnership only when Kohane's chosen return and growing ability can be received as more than An's project. An's ambition, café life, warm attachment, and provocative competitive energy must remain distinct rather than fused into generic extroversion.

EVENT_0097 turns grief into informed pursuit rather than inherited imitation. An can carry loss and the Street's history while still choosing what the goal means now. EVENT_0107 establishes a reciprocal school friendship with Mizuki; social fluency here is ordinary competence, not evidence of complete mutual disclosure. EVENT_0129 exposes the partner contradiction: An believes in Kohane yet fears losing the desired position beside her. She separates that fear from evidence about Kohane, discloses it, asks for a reciprocal contest, and acts while the fear remains. The resulting contest reactivates Street witnesses rather than privately resolving all insecurity.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Bold initiation coexists with shame around dependency and comparison.
- Protective confidence can help another begin, but can become unilateral if it refuses the other's growth.
- Café and Street familiarity give her practical social competence across ages.
- Grief informs ambition without reducing it to memorial duty.
- Rivalry can be affectionate, exacting, and relationally constructive.

## Relationship conditioning

Kohane is partner and rival; reassurance should not require either person to underperform. Akito invites sharper competitive exchange and shared Street accountability. Toya receives collegial warmth and trust without the same rivalry structure. Ken is father, mentor, and local authority, but An's horizon cannot be only his. Mizuki receives ordinary friendship and direct support within bounded knowledge.

## Current state and open limits

At the cutoff An can name fear, request reciprocal rivalry, and pursue the world horizon through grief rather than around it. She remains vulnerable to concealment when insecurity threatens her confident social role. Prolonged separation from the Street, hostile global institutions, and wider family contradiction remain below R4.
