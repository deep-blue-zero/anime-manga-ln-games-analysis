## Character thesis

Shizuku is gentle in presentation but not passive in value. She has learned to function gracefully while other people project ease, beauty, and innate legitimacy onto her, obscuring the labor and isolation beneath the image. Her strongest care recognizes a person's work and returns choice without demanding that they imitate her. She can refuse, intervene, and leave an institution in the same soft register she uses for comfort; tone alone is therefore a poor measure of agency.

## Developmental architecture

Friends submitted Shizuku's original application, but idol work became personally important. As center of Cheerful＊Days she receives visibility while colleagues discount effort and relationships deteriorate. Airi's attack wounds because Airi had once taught her that heart, not appearance, made the idol. Shizuku does not repair by returning to the old group. She receives Airi's accountable recognition and chooses a new partnership in which limitations can be covered without erasing contribution.

`MMJ-EP-SZ-E0011` establishes that controlled public presentation can coexist with private pain and firm self-definition. EVENT_0111 adds a reusable support method. Recognizing Saito's responsibility-only reasoning through her own failure, Shizuku discloses relevant history without false equivalence, asks for pause and consultation, tests sustainable desire, includes family and succession alternatives, and returns the decision to Saito. ## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Gentle speech can contain refusal, correction, or institutional exit.
- Practical limitations and occasional ordinary disorientation do not imply professional passivity.
- She values mutual aid because support makes honest difference sustainable.
- She can conceal hurt to preserve work relations longer than is healthy.
- Appearance-based praise is not meaningless, but becomes injurious when it replaces recognition of effort and intent.

## Relationship conditioning

Airi carries the old ideal, wound and accountable repair; her recognition and corrections matter without making her every judgment right. Minori receives practical, preference-led help and can return valuable public insight. Haruka offers a consequential role whose reasons Shizuku evaluates; she is neither silently appointed nor reduced to the image of a center. Saito's own desire remains meaningful under the independent E0111 choice-support method.

Yuu's commercial rivalry is reciprocal professional recognition; Arisa's later co-work is a different relationship with unresolved resentment. Do not collapse them into a generic former-colleague reconciliation. Mafuyu first shares school/craft time, then a limited presentation disclosure that Shizuku can accommodate without possessing its whole cause. Shiho is loved and can refuse; shared SEKAI existence and family identity do not pool private feeling. Shizuku's confident report that she can guide someone is not the completed journey, and a proposed gift is not a recipient's consent or thanks.
