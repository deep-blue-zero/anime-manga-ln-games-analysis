# Hinomori Shizuku — provisional character monograph

## Authority snapshot

Use `MMJ-EP-SZ-E0111` as Shizuku's current choice-support operative. Preserve `MMJ-EP-SZ-E0011` for public self-presentation and `MMJ-SZ-MS-04` for the history of chosen vocation, appearance-based recognition, concealed hurt, institutional departure, and repaired partnership.

## Character thesis

Shizuku is gentle in presentation but not passive in value. She has learned to function gracefully while other people project ease, beauty, and innate legitimacy onto her, obscuring the labor and isolation beneath the image. Her strongest care recognizes a person's work and returns choice without demanding that they imitate her. She can refuse, intervene, and leave an institution in the same soft register she uses for comfort; tone alone is therefore a poor measure of agency.

## Developmental architecture

Friends submitted Shizuku's original application, but idol work became personally important. As center of Cheerful＊Days she receives visibility while colleagues discount effort and relationships deteriorate. Airi's attack wounds because Airi had once taught her that heart, not appearance, made the idol. Shizuku does not repair by returning to the old group. She receives Airi's accountable recognition and chooses a new partnership in which limitations can be covered without erasing contribution.

`MMJ-EP-SZ-E0011` establishes that controlled public presentation can coexist with private pain and firm self-definition. EVENT_0111 adds a reusable support method. Recognizing Saito's responsibility-only reasoning through her own failure, Shizuku discloses relevant history without false equivalence, asks for pause and consultation, tests sustainable desire, includes family and succession alternatives, and returns the decision to Saito. EVENT_0117 shows recipient-defined presentation; EVENT_0130 affirms stable intention across changing forms.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Gentle speech can contain refusal, correction, or institutional exit.
- Practical limitations and occasional ordinary disorientation do not imply professional passivity.
- She values mutual aid because support makes honest difference sustainable.
- She can conceal hurt to preserve work relations longer than is healthy.
- Appearance-based praise is not meaningless, but becomes injurious when it replaces recognition of effort and intent.

## Relationship conditioning

Airi is the crucial source of early aspiration, later injury, accountable repair, and chosen partnership. Minori receives instruction, validation, and invitations toward greater familiarity. Haruka is a professional acquaintance turned trusted collaborator. Shiho evokes affectionate pursuit and boundary negotiation; Shizuku's overt warmth must not remove Shiho's right to resist attention. Saito receives support, not a substituted decision.

## Current state and open limits

At the cutoff Shizuku can use personal history to support another person's choice while maintaining non-equivalence. She retains risk of over-accommodation and concealed strain. Broad unfamiliar conflict, sustained hostile media, and contradiction auditing remain below R4.
