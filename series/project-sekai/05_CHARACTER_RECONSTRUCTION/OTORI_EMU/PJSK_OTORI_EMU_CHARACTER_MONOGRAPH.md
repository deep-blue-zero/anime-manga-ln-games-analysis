# Otori Emu — provisional character monograph

## Authority snapshot

Use `WXS-EP-EMU-E0131` as Emu's current audience-perspective acting operative. Preserve `WXS-EP-EMU-E0104` for stewardship, `WXS-MS-EMU-P06` for formation, and park/family/group relationship authorities separately.

## Character thesis

Emu is an intuitive audience reader and place steward whose exuberance carries precise values. She recruits, performs, and persists because Phoenix Wonderland is a material inheritance of shared smiles, not merely a venue for personal fun. Her brightness is genuine but can become a method of concealment when family power or institutional threat feels too large to share. Development means disclosing the burden, receiving coordinated action, and turning intuitive feeling into observable, revisable craft without losing spontaneity.

## Developmental architecture

The main story begins with a beloved stage under threat and Emu trying to protect it through relentless recruitment and positivity. She initially withholds the full institutional terms, leaving companions unable to choose the risk knowingly. When burden becomes shared, the group can act on a vision that is hers without treating her as its only labor source. Family authority persists after the first success; “saving the park” is not a permanent institutional resolution.

EVENT_0104 establishes stewardship after the troupe becomes independent: Emu can preserve the park's audience purpose while accepting organizational transition and group choice. EVENT_0124 maps the troupe's dreams onto a continuing voyage rather than fixing everyone at the park. EVENT_0131 makes her acting method explicit. She starts from an intuitive role feeling, maps it into trained bodily choices, solicits differentiated viewpoints, compares intended feeling with observed reaction, revises variables, and retests transmission successfully. The confirmed Mikazuki production validates work, not universal acting mastery.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Exuberance is authentic and can also conceal fear or institutional detail.
- She reads audience feeling quickly but benefits from external perspectives that make intuition testable.
- Place loyalty is not simple resistance to change; stewardship can authorize a new form.
- She initiates boldly and can need others to make risk visible.
- Family affection and family power can coexist without easy resolution.

## Relationship conditioning

Tsukasa is a recruited star whose ambition must serve the audience/place vision. Nene is a trusted performer whose anxiety requires support rather than cheer alone. Rui turns impossible wishes into methods, but safety and consent remain shared constraints. Her brothers and other family actors carry institutional authority that must not be reduced to personal dislike. Mikazuki collaborators activate a more formal peer-performance mode.

## Current state and open limits

At the cutoff Emu can solicit viewpoints and revise performance while preserving intuitive purpose. She remains prone to carrying fear privately and treating energy as protection for others. Long-term family governance, park economics, and sustained work outside familiar show communities remain below R4.
