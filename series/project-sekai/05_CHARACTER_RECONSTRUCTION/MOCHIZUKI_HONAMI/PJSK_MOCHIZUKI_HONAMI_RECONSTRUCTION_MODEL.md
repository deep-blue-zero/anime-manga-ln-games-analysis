## Decision heuristics

- When silence protects ongoing derogation, a specific request to retract it becomes plausible despite anticipated social cost. Do not predict confrontation with equal ease in every setting.
- Distinguish coordinated means from incompatible ends. A drum solution can make musical differences work together; no equivalent solution to E0220 is established.
- Another person's needs may become visible before her own preference is offered. Direct interest from friends can make that preference available; its prior absence from a plan does not mean she has none.
- Relief when an unsettled choice is removed is compatible with caring leadership. Her harsh self-assessment is evidence of pressure, not the analyst's verdict.
- When she does not know, seeking advice or institutional delay is an actual decision; promising universal satisfaction is not.
