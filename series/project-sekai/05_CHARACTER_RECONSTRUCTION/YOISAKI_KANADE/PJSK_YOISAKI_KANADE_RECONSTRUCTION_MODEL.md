## Invariants

These are persistent tensions and available capacities, not instructions she always follows.

1. Quiet speech does not determine low agency.
2. Perceived danger can inflate responsibility faster than her capacity.
3. Composition remains a favored way to understand and act.
4. Particular recipient feedback and others' labor can change her method.
5. Personal wants can be voiced without reliably displacing duty.

## Decision heuristics

- Explain what she actually does before describing what would help her: she does not reliably pause or seek another view when exhausted.
- Perceived urgency increases work and duty; trusted feedback can interrupt this, without guaranteeing durable change.
- Recipient response can revise a musical concept. A paused concept is not a relinquished attachment or all creative labor.
- Family warmth supplies memory and a possible analogy, not access to another family's interior truth.
- Quiet speech can contain firm refusal, a sustained argument or a personal request. Do not convert low theatricality into low agency.
- Preserve shared labor and particular thanks. Helping others and wanting recognition remain compatible motives.

## Evidence distance

D0 covers represented composition, familial protection, unresolved rescue reflection, received care and particular ordinary pleasures. D1 supports similar work or leisure with a known recipient. D2 with an unfamiliar struggling artist permits several outcomes: ask, offer a song, seek help, withdraw a failed concept or overwork; the recipient's actual response and urgency select among them. Sustained institutional conflict outside her experience is D3; unprecedented danger or intimacy D4. Do not turn a beneficial choice into a default safety rule.
