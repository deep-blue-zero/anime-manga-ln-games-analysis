# Yoisaki Kanade — provisional character monograph

## Authority snapshot

Current operative `K-E0192-01` inherits E0116's personal musical longing and E0089's recipient-directed protection. It records an unresolved question about care when Mafuyu no longer needs saving. E0199's communicated pause concerns the maternal-warmth composition, while ordinary Nightcord work continues. Preserve the foundation, `REL-N25-KM-E0165`, group `REL-N25-G-E0170`, father E0116, Honami and Toya's distinct relationships. Shared access through E0218 does not pool private knowledge.

## Character thesis

Kanade is an agentic composer whose real pleasure in music and care for others become entangled with permission to exist. Her interpretation of her father's collapse turns helping into an obligation larger than her evidence or physical capacity. Quietness is compatible with organizing collaboration, opposing an adult and choosing a particular personal pleasure. Later work permits longing and shared authorship, yet the question of who she can be when Mafuyu no longer needs saving remains unanswered. Her strongest available methods and her recurrent overextension must be explained together.

## Developmental architecture

Kanade begins with composition already transformed from curiosity and reciprocal family pleasure into compulsory rescue labor. Mafuyu's work is an existential test because it seems to prove that a song might reveal a person Kanade can save. When songs fail, Kanade overfunctions before collapse. She can recognize uncertainty and ego without releasing the obligation. The main-story endpoint is therefore neither cure nor healthy boundary: she can accept that one song was insufficient, receive Mafuyu's chosen contact, and continue in a reciprocal but potentially “cursed” commitment.

Subsequent states preserve and revise this architecture. `K-E0089-01` records protective commitment under escalating danger. EVENT_0116 reconstructs music before penance: childhood curiosity, family warmth, and shared pleasure. After Ena and KAITO challenge her self-exclusion, Kanade permits a wish for renewed recognition, composes from it, shares the song, and voices the wish to her father. Rescue duty, paternal guilt, uncertainty, and poor workload limits remain. EVENT_0123 adds ordinary relief as a chosen desire, while EVENT_0139 creates a reciprocal composition channel with Toya without granting either unrestricted knowledge.

## Later development through EVENT_0222

E0116 makes a desire admissible, not reliably livable. Kanade recalls music as curiosity and family pleasure, creates from wanting her father's recognition and voices that wish to him. His silence does not establish receipt. E0161 later shows her trying to understand Mafuyu through her own remembered maternal warmth while neglecting meals and rest until she collapses. Mafuyu's porridge, Ena's food and the group's concern are real support; they are not proof that she has adopted durable limits. [E0116](../../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/N25/PJSK_EVENT_0116_DEEP_READING.md); [E0161](../../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/N25/PJSK_EVENT_0161_DEEP_READING.md).

Her model of family can change under evidence without becoming infallible. E0089's hotel encounter makes her refuse the mother's demand that Mafuyu leave the group. E0165's actual apology gives her grounds for renewed hope, followed by evidence that the return home is again costing Mafuyu usable choice. She contacts the father and asks about Mafuyu's own wish; the father actually brings Mafuyu back to refuge. Neither the earlier mistrust nor the later hope is a timeless prediction. Care and coercion can coexist, and a remembered loving family is not an instruction for another family. [E0089](../../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/N25/PJSK_EVENT_0089_DEEP_READING.md); [E0165](../../03_SEQUENTIAL_EVENT_READINGS/N25/PJSK_EVENT_0165_DEEP_READING.md).

E0192 exposes Kanade's stake in the rescue arrangement. Her grandmother's account of attachment is a hypothesis; Empty Luka's claim about dependence is her private interpretation, disputed in method by MEIKO. The directly received question is what Kanade would do if Mafuyu no longer required her songs. She cannot answer. Ena and Mizuki propose time apart as an option for the family, explicitly limiting their analogy and declining to decide for Mafuyu. Kanade recognizes her own preference for family togetherness, thanks them and remains uncertain. Mafuyu has not received that whole discussion. The endpoint is a commitment to think, still expressed under pressure to find an answer. Pausing this strategy is not abandoning Mafuyu, ceasing composition, resolving dependence or choosing the proposed family distance. ## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Low theatricality coexists with decisive intervention, detailed craft and sustained reasoning.
- Composition is vocation, pleasure and an action channel; it is not sufficient for every problem.
- Awareness of uncertainty or ego can coexist with escalating responsibility.
- Practical creative coordination can be strong while food and sleep are neglected.
- Personal longing and ordinary enjoyment are possible beside unresolved rescue obligation.

## Relationship conditioning

Mafuyu is a creator, recipient, housemate and caring agent whose wishes Kanade can hear imperfectly. Their reciprocity includes meals and correction; it does not remove unequal rescue pressure or give either all the other's private knowledge. Ena can receive a chosen conversation, challenge family assumptions and remind Kanade that her happiness has more than one source. Mizuki's authored disclosure in E0150 changes the old nondisclosure boundary: Kanade receives the account and thanks them, but its exact words remain unavailable to the reader. Their fashion exchange later includes an accepted refusal and a new request.

Honami's paid household labor includes personal attentiveness and concrete reciprocal thanks; it must not disappear behind a story of solitary composition. Toya remains a particular composing peer, not an automatic recipient of N25 confidences. Grandmother offers affection and guidance at a distance; her health limits co-residence, not the reality of care. Kanade's father carries professional history, love, guilt and a still-unanswered present wish. Stage Miku, Ichika, Haruka and Emu participate in a distinct shared project, without learning every private rescue conflict.

## Current state and open limits

Kanade can protect, revise, collaborate, remember, enjoy and refuse. She also ruminates, withholds an unresolved concern and overworks. The mother-warmth song remains under reconsideration; Mafuyu's family future, Kanade's own answer and paternal recovery remain open.
