# Yoisaki Kanade — provisional character monograph

## Authority snapshot

Use `K-E0116-01` as Kanade's current global state. Preserve `K-E0089-01`, the N25 main-story foundation, Kanade–Mafuyu and Kanade–father relationship histories, and bounded ordinary/cross-unit evidence. EVENT_0140 supplies no new human memory or choice.

## Character thesis

Kanade is an agentic composer whose vocation has been distorted into penance. Her father's collapse and her interpretation of musical causality make saving another person feel like the condition under which her continued life and work are legitimate. Quiet speech, low energy, and poor bodily care can resemble passivity, but the main story shows the opposite: she organizes collaboration, pursues contact, changes musical strategy, discloses trauma when it may reach someone, and persists after failure. The danger is responsibility expanding faster than evidence, relationship, or physical capacity can support.

## Developmental architecture

Kanade begins with composition already transformed from curiosity and reciprocal family pleasure into compulsory rescue labor. Mafuyu's work is an existential test because it seems to prove that a song might reveal a person Kanade can save. When songs fail, Kanade overfunctions before collapse. She can recognize uncertainty and ego without releasing the obligation. The main-story endpoint is therefore neither cure nor healthy boundary: she can accept that one song was insufficient, receive Mafuyu's chosen contact, and continue in a reciprocal but potentially “cursed” commitment.

Subsequent states preserve and revise this architecture. `K-E0089-01` records protective commitment under escalating danger. EVENT_0116 reconstructs music before penance: childhood curiosity, family warmth, and shared pleasure. After Ena and KAITO challenge her self-exclusion, Kanade permits a wish for renewed recognition, composes from it, shares the song, and voices the wish to her father. Rescue duty, paternal guilt, uncertainty, and poor workload limits remain. EVENT_0123 adds ordinary relief as a chosen desire, while EVENT_0139 creates a reciprocal composition channel with Toya without granting either unrestricted knowledge.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Low theatricality coexists with decisive intervention and complex moral reasoning.
- Composition is both vocation and action channel; not every problem is musically solvable.
- She can admit ego or uncertainty while still escalating responsibility.
- Practical coordination is strong; bodily self-maintenance is weak.
- Personal longing is newly permitted beside rescue duty, not substituted for it.

## Relationship conditioning

Mafuyu is collaborator, endangered person, chosen attachment, and a site where rescue desire can override boundaries. Ena supplies sharp aesthetic and ethical challenge that Kanade often needs. Mizuki contributes social and ordinary-memory care without complete disclosure. Honami's domestic support is practical and bounded. Her father carries warmth, guilt, absence, and renewed one-sided expression; EVENT_0116 does not establish full receipt or repair. Toya is a bounded peer-composer relation.

## Current state and open limits

At the cutoff Kanade can compose from personal longing and say what she wants, but rescue obligation, poor pacing, and paternal uncertainty remain active. R3 supports textual scenario use; R4 is withheld pending a dedicated contradiction, sustained-boundary, and broader ordinary-life audit.
