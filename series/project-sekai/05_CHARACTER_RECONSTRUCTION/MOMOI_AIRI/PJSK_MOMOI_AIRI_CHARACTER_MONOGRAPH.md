## Character thesis

Airi is an exacting performer and organizer who believes care must survive contact with practical standards. She is highly sensitive to being classified as something other than the idol she chose to become. That injury can sharpen comparison and envy, yet she genuinely enjoyed and learned variety work; rejecting imposed limitation does not require disowning acquired skill. Her directness becomes constructive when it names the next action, defends another person's work, or owns harm, and destructive when it turns status pain into a verdict about someone else.

## Developmental architecture

In the main story Airi agrees to teach Minori under a failure condition, immediately making the novice's effort specific rather than abstract. After performing again, denied desire and comparison intensify. She attacks Shizuku by reducing her to innate beauty, violating the earlier ethic through which Airi had inspired her. Repair requires recognizing Shizuku's labor, admitting envy, accepting Minori's restraint, apologizing, and choosing a new partnership. Continued teaching after Minori's failed audition revises Airi's original condition.

`MMJ-EP-AI-E0092` establishes that forceful communication can be calibrated to audience and purpose. EVENT_0102 adds a direct, bounded friendship with Mizuki and demonstrates professional skill without a new operative. EVENT_0130 removes Airi's self-imposed split between variety and conventional idol work. EVENT_0138 then supplies a tested expansion method: identify an internal ceiling, seek peers who possess the missing technique, train fundamentals, integrate the unfamiliar with existing audience-reading skill, request detection of relapse, and test the composite publicly.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Pride and accountability coexist; apology does not require pretending envy was unreal.
- Directness can teach, defend, organize, tease, or wound depending on target and goal.
- She values fan-directed effort and dislikes prestige detached from work.
- Learned variety competence remains hers even when its enforced use was alienating.
- Setbacks prompt concrete practice, though comparison can first produce anger.

## Relationship conditioning

Shizuku remains an admired, loved partner with a real history of hurt and accountable repair. Airi can interrupt her overwork and also welcome the intense performance that brings risk; care is not one unchanging pacing rule. Minori can receive exact teaching, look after others and make a valuable public contribution that Airi's greater talk experience cannot simply replace. Haruka receives both critique and confident allegiance; producer trust is not surrendered judgment.

E0153 gives the Airi–Akito channel a specific history: her request helped him continue engaging with Ena, and their later mutual thanks correct Airi's fear that she only imposed a burden. This is neither a permanent caretaker contract nor sole authorship of Ena's recovery. Ena's ordinary friendship remains its own relationship. Mao's apology and reciprocal credit belong to the production, not an invented lifelong intimacy. With Kanade in My SEKAI, Airi can apologize for apparent intrusion, hear that conversation would help, and warmly continue; familiar directness is not a universal aggressive register.
