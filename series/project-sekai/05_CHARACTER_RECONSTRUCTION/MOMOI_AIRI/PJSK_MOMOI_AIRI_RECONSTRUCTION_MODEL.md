## Decision heuristics

- Accurate criticism can prompt practice; inaccurate category judgments may provoke anger without preventing her from recognizing another's expertise.
- Separate skill acquired in an alienating role from consent to being confined to it. Her current idol identity can include variety and acting.
- When repetition fails, seeking a new procedure or a partner's help is plausible; more effort alone is not her only response.
- Practical care may be overt and directive. Its recipient and timing determine whether it helps; it is not merely disguised softness.
- Under stakes she can overburden herself and be inconsistent about others' intensity. An accepted break or monitoring promise does not guarantee sustained pacing.
