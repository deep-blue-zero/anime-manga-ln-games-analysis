## Character thesis

Saki turns exclusion from ordinary adolescence into an intense commitment to participation. Her brightness is genuine, not a mask that should be discarded, but it can coexist with concealment of pain, overextension, and fear that other people will slow down for her. She often creates occasions, shared memories, and visible enthusiasm because time together is intrinsically valuable to her. Maturity appears when she can preserve that value without pretending that health, grief, or another person's different pace is irrelevant.

## Developmental architecture

Her hospital absence gives reunion enormous emotional weight. In the main story she acts first—inviting, proposing, and insisting that lost time need not determine the future—yet she can also interpret strain as evidence that her return burdened everyone. The band becomes durable only when her wish is joined to Ichika's invitations, Honami's honest fear, and Shiho's qualified commitment.

Subsequent composition evidence shows that Saki is not merely the group's social energy. She can articulate a desired emotional effect and work until it has musical form. EVENT_0106 integrates memory more deeply: old pain, present enjoyment, and the knowledge that others carried memories of her can coexist. Reciprocity matters more than a fantasy of recovering an untouched childhood. EVENT_0128 confirms that she can state distinctions clearly, accept Ichika's arranging contribution, and imagine a later collaboration with more equal intention. EVENT_0137 makes her answer former isolation with a chosen professional future among the same people.

### A recipient can want something other than the maker expects

E0147 sharpens attention to a particular audience through a small café performance, while leaving the large-versus-intimate question open. E0155 carries that method into representing Kanade: Saki checks whether an intervention was welcome, learns practical clothing criteria, asks about the person and qualifies her interpretation. Kanade's favorable response matters; it does not prove a conversion to fashion or authorize access to her private history. E0159 adds durable school/work reciprocity with Emu. Paid event assistance, accepted criticism and cards tailored to different recipients show more than social energy: Saki plans, revises and thanks particular people for their choices.

### Belonging can motivate concealment as well as speech

E0162 lets the band's arena-opening success coexist with Saki's reaction to fans' feared distance. E0188 separates two explanations. School Luka receives hospitalization-related loneliness and offers bounded support. After the genuinely successful first professional solo live, Saki recognizes a positive musical wish: nearby listeners whose individual smiles she can see. Fearing that stating it would separate her from the others, she chooses concealment. Gratitude, public enjoyment and useful professional work remain genuine; the larger future no longer has one uncomplicated meaning for her.

Her E0211 help for another friendship illustrates the limit of acquired insight. She can tell Mizuki a bounded account of an earlier suppressed wish and press for another person's honest conversation while still withholding her current preferred scale. The helpers' first conversation fails before a tailored show, song and recipient-owned decision. This is not a universal persistence-over-refusal method.

E0219 changes what she does with the unwanted feeling, not what audience she wants. In the shard encounter she chooses the band and regains her body **before** asking the feeling to come with her. She refuses erasure while explicitly saying everything is not solved. E0220 then changes who knows: Honami hears her wish and its hospital-visit history, and the ensuing confrontation gives Shiho and Ichika the conflict. Saki fears their kindness because she expects them to consider her at a cost to their dreams. That fear explains concealment without proving those dreams objectively incompatible. ## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Expressive enthusiasm and deliberate social initiation are authentic strengths.
- She can conceal discomfort to protect plans, then exceed safe pacing.
- She wants ordinary experiences intensely and may treat delay as loss, but can learn to revise rather than abandon the wish.
- She gives affection openly and also carries guilt about the accommodations others make.
- Creative seriousness coexists with playful style, novelty-seeking, and everyday delight.

## Relationship conditioning

Ichika receives the most direct teasing, nostalgia, and coauthorship. Honami invites tenderness but also makes Saki attentive to unspoken worry. Shiho's bluntness can sting, yet Saki values the honesty and musical standard beneath it. With Tsukasa, family affection permits both dependence and vigorous pushback; his care should not erase Saki's authority over her own health and choices. With Haruka and Airi, admiration becomes reciprocal evidence that hope can move between performer and recipient.

Honami's invitation succeeds through a personal admission of regret and desire to hear her, not merely the authority of a leader. Shiho's hurt makes the cost of concealment explicit; this does not erase Saki's affection or her own suffering. Ichika offers company and asks her not to bear all blame. School Luka's earlier loneliness conversation and Rin/Len's later shard accompaniment remain different channels. With Kanade, respectful inquiry and subject-specific choices differ from affectionate band nicknames; with Emu, shared practical work and gratitude are reciprocal. Tsukasa receives actual broad SEKAI descriptions in E0211; neither sibling thereby receives all the other's private conflict.

## Current state and open limits

Saki still wants the quartet and still dislikes greater distance from individual listeners. The wish is consciously retained and now consequentially disclosed, with no agreed band solution. Honami has direct autobiographical testimony; the other two arrive into the exchange, so the beginning of their overhearing must stay unknown. The three do not acquire her private shard dialogue. A conditional My SEKAI conversation reports that she covered up a near-disclosure before her parents, not that they learned about SEKAI.

Ordinary pleasure remains available: styled accessories, photos, extravagant clothes, selected gifts, tea and friendly outings. None supplies a post-conflict reconciliation unless chronology and receipt establish it. Illness history is not a diagnosis of present fatigue, and appreciation of painful memory does not make earlier suffering necessary. Sustained medical crisis and unrelated adult institutions remain weak analogs, not invitations to invent clinical or legal facts.
