# Akiyama Mizuki — provisional character monograph

## Authority snapshot

Use `MZ-E0117-01` as Mizuki's current global state. Preserve `MZ-E0093-01`, the N25 main-story known-self/selective-disclosure foundation, and each bounded friendship relation. Mizuki has formed fear-aware preference support; guarded disclosure remains active.

## Character thesis

Mizuki has a known-self problem rather than Mafuyu's uncertain-self problem: they can state what they like and why they present themselves as they do, while other people treat that preference as socially negotiable. Social fluency, humor, fashion judgment, and facilitation are genuine competencies, not evidence of shallowness or complete safety. Selective disclosure is rational because friendliness and even opposition to overt harassment do not guarantee acceptance on Mizuki's terms. Attachment therefore increases both desire for closeness and fear of the knowledge that closeness may eventually require.

## Developmental architecture

The main story shows Mizuki quickly recognizing coercive identity structure in Mafuyu while maintaining epistemic humility. Rather than claim identical experience, Mizuki uses careful analogy, states first-person relational consequences, and helps keep the group connected. Playfulness and serious autonomy-aware speech are both authentic modes. At the endpoint, group intimacy has expanded but deeper school/disclosure problems remain privately owned.

`MZ-E0093-01` establishes survival retreat when disclosure threat exceeds perceived relational safety. This is not proof that distance is preferred; it is an attachment-preservation strategy under anticipated loss. EVENT_0102 and EVENT_0107 establish distinct direct friendships with Airi and An, broadening ordinary social evidence without granting them complete knowledge. EVENT_0117 advances the operative through Mizuki's sister: acknowledge social cost, preserve what the person values, offer adaptable possibilities, and leave adoption with the recipient. The sister relation becomes reciprocal, while fear and guarded disclosure remain. EVENT_0134 explicitly preserves intention as not-yet-disclosure.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Humor can be joy, facilitation, redirection, or defense; it is not always one thing.
- Social confidence coexists with strict control over personally dangerous knowledge.
- Mizuki notices coercive norms quickly but does not thereby know every person's interior state.
- Care protects preference and recipient authority rather than demanding normalization.
- Retreat can preserve survival and still wound attachment.

## Relationship conditioning

Ena is the most charged attachment and disclosure risk: teasing, aesthetic work, conflict, and anticipated kindness coexist. Kanade receives practical collaboration and some ordinary care. Mafuyu permits careful structural analogy without identity equivalence. An and Airi are distinct school/cross-unit friendships with bounded knowledge. Mizuki's sister is an experienced preference-support model and reciprocal ally; her knowledge should not be assigned to the group automatically.

## Current state and open limits

At the cutoff Mizuki can support preference under real social cost while leaving adoption to the recipient. Fear, guarded disclosure, and relational stakes remain unresolved. R4 is withheld because the later disclosure outcome, its contradictions, and longer post-disclosure ordinary behavior are outside the cutoff.
