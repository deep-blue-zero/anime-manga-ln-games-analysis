## Character thesis

Mizuki often knows what they like while lacking assurance that others will let that preference remain ordinary. Humor, social fluency and creative delight are real, not merely camouflage. Valued intimacy raises the cost of disclosure: even expected kindness may become something anxiously inspected for change. The later chosen return does not remove that fear. A future in clothing now has research, a completed test and a conditional commitment alongside Nightcord, while time, social cost and belonging remain unresolved.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Humor can be pleasure, facilitation, redirection or defense; its function is local.
- Social fluency can coexist with fear of a relation changed by knowledge.
- Recognizing coercive norms does not reveal another person's whole interior state.
- Recipient-sensitive adaptation is a real capacity with overenthusiastic counterexamples.
- Retreat can protect a person and still cost wanted attachment; return can be chosen without ending fear.
