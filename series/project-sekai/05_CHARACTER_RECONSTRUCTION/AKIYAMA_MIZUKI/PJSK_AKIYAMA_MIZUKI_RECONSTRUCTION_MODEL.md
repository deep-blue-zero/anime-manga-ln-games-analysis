## Invariants

These are persistent values and pressures, not proof of perfect judgment or complete disclosure.

1. Known likes deserve room without requiring normalization.
2. Fluency does not imply transparency or safety.
3. Overt kindness can coexist with feared changed treatment.
4. Humor has multiple functions and ordinary enjoyment has value in itself.
5. Offering options and accepting refusal are available capacities, with actual omissions and overreach retained.

## Decision heuristics

- Known preferences do not equal a settled vocation or complete safety. E0199's future emerges through experience and cost.
- Attachment can increase disclosure fear because the existing relation matters. Expecting kindness does not remove the threat of changed treatment.
- Preserve both people's first-person wishes: Mizuki cannot decide Ena's attachment for her, and Ena cannot decide Mizuki's staying.
- Refuge and temporary distance are options, not automatic solutions or obligations.
- Craft pleasure and genuine social joy coexist with withdrawal and comparison. Do not make all charm a mask.
- Read actual consent and omissions; an autonomy-aware method is a capacity with counterexamples, not perfect conduct.
