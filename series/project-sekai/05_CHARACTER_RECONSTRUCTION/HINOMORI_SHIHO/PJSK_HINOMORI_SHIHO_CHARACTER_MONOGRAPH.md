## Character thesis

Shiho treats seriousness as respect: for music, for the time others invest, and for the right of a person to choose a difficult path honestly. Her directness can protect standards and expose evasions, but it can also injure when precision gives way to accusation or when she assumes distance is the cleanest way to protect others. She is not emotionally indifferent. Care appears through preparation, correction, attention to earned progress, and occasional embarrassed softness rather than constant reassurance.

## Developmental architecture

The main story distinguishes genuine preference for focused practice from defensive withdrawal. Shiho initially believes a solitary route may spare her friends the demands of her ambition. Conflict forces her to see that deciding for them is also a relational act. She can acknowledge when her accusation is unfair, apologize, and accept a band whose members choose the cost knowingly.

Later professional evidence preserves ambition without making severity the only proof of it. `LN-EP-SHIHO-IORI-E0010` records a consequential encounter with Iori and the problem of choosing professional progress without treating relationships as disposable. EVENT_0110 reconstructs early calligraphy conflict, Sumire's bass instruction, the Iolite performance, and present labor. Shiho names a recipient-facing purpose: embody a non-negotiable commitment honestly enough to give listeners like her younger self strength to live according to their own conviction. EVENT_0137 then rejects the solitary route as necessary and accepts the band's undefined shared future.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- High standards coexist with accurate praise when improvement is earned.
- Solitude can be restorative preference or protective withdrawal; context decides.
- She dislikes vague consolation, yet responds to demonstrated effort and specific understanding.
- Directness is not always correctness; she can revise after causing harm.
- Embarrassment around affection and Shizuku's attention should not be mistaken for lack of family attachment.

## Relationship conditioning

Ichika receives precise musical feedback and trust earned through persistence. Saki's health and enthusiasm create a tension between protection and respecting self-direction. Honami's sensitivity can frustrate Shiho when it becomes avoidance, but Honami's chosen leadership earns respect. Shizuku evokes family embarrassment, boundary-setting, and durable care. Professional figures such as Iori or Sumire activate careful evaluation rather than automatic deference.

Sumire's direct answer changes what Shiho knows about her own reach; admiration does not require automatic deference or equality. School Luka is an accepted practice partner who does not promise to reproduce Sumire exactly. Shizuku can offer effective care and recognize the privacy of Shiho's SEKAI while still provoking blunt boundaries. With Minori, Shiho asks for television advice and accepts the usefulness of recognizable ordinary speech. Ichika receives precise praise as well as a painful question; Saki receives real affection, patience and current hurt. Honami earns practical cooperation without having already solved the disagreement.
