# Kamishiro Rui — provisional character monograph

## Authority snapshot

Use `WXS-EP-RUI-E0200`, inheriting E0172, E0152 and E0119 for expanding direction across medium, feeling and performer. Preserve independent `WXS-EP-RUI-E0144`, inheriting E0099's ordinary friendship and retained loneliness, plus E0024's historically distinct outsider-collaboration responsibility. Group `REL-WXS-G-E0208` includes a retrieval commitment with unresolved practical limits. Current capability does not imply consistently informed consent or safe practice.

## Character thesis

Rui wants unusual experiences to become possible with people who can answer and reshape his ideas. Rejection contributes to defensive isolation, while later friendship is valuable beyond its usefulness for shows. His expanding craft includes accepting better solutions, learning a medium, expressing personal feeling and coaching particular performers. It also includes real manipulation, risky pressure and delayed intervention. Curiosity, care and accurate observation do not make him omniscient or consistently respectful of consent; his apologies and doubts must remain as concrete as his achievements.

## Developmental architecture

The foundation pairs technical possibility with incomplete understanding of other people. Rui's criticism of Tsukasa identifies a real failure while overreaching about his entire motive. Nene has agency within their old trust, and Rui's recommitment does not retroactively authorize the forced route to the repair performance. Shared limits and actual choices make a continuing company possible.

E0024 owns outsider collaboration through shared purpose and workable roles. E0099 makes ordinary friendship valuable without that task condition; E0144 retains earlier loneliness as part of understanding the present. E0119 establishes medium-aware production learning and acceptance of Ohara's better solution. Later E0152 recipient-led redesign, E0172 personal expression and E0200 performer-specific film work expand that professional strand. E0211 revises the historical picture by retaining an offered compromise Rui himself refused.

## Development through the current boundary

The foundation supplies perceptive criticism without omniscience. Rui identifies a real collaboration failure when Tsukasa attacks Nene, but his sweeping judgment of Tsukasa's whole motive and future exceeds what he knows. Nene can choose to mediate and perform rather than merely be protected by Rui. Rui's own forcibly arranged return is not retrospectively authorized by recommitment. E0099 establishes ordinary friendship that does not require a production. Tsukasa supplies an opening analogy, classmates choose Rui for shared interests, and Rui chooses the irreplaceable shared time. E0144 later lets him refuse erasure of the loneliness from which that value developed. This private fragment exchange does not make the other three privy to all its content. E0211 adds an older complication: a club president offered him a feasible approximation after others rejected a risky idea, and Rui refused the bridge while claiming incompatible commitment. His later account calls this bravado. Mizuki also reports a prank that injured a caretaker. Past exclusion is real, but neither innocence in every conflict nor an absence of all offered connection follows. E0185 shows why a benevolent-director invariant would fail. Rui pressures Nene shortly before performance; she later thanks his belief, but he explicitly says the intervention could have worsened her state and he remains unsure it was best. Rui then apologizes and explains; only afterward does the actor disclose the full reluctance and fear behind accepting the role. Thanks are actual but not prior permission. His help around Mizuki's disclosure supplies an important alternative to directive intervention. ## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Curiosity and critique persist as collaboration changes their use.
- Playful or polite speech can carry care, inquiry, excitement or deliberate provocation.
- Rejection history matters alongside offered connections and his own refused compromise.
- Negotiated design and permission are available, but E0185 and E0200 prevent treating them as universal practice.
- Accepting a better solution and returning credit strengthen authorship without erasing responsibility for harm.

## Relationship conditioning

Nene is neither his dependent project nor a universal permission source: she chooses, judges, refuses, thanks and asks him to rest or leave work aside. Tsukasa offers unusually compatible performance and friendship, yet Rui can overestimate him and fail to intervene early enough. Emu supplies a place, recipients and corrections without making her every impulsive plan workable.

Mizuki shares some middle-school history but did not know the club invitation until E0211. That account reaches Saki, Toya, Mizuki and Interstice KAITO, not automatically the troupe. Classmates remain actual ordinary friends. Ohara, Tom and Sakaki have different expertise and limits; none is an all-purpose moral authority. Nagamine's later cooperation follows an actual hurt and apology. Kohane contributes a recipient's desire and later photographs. El's father's illness disclosure reaches Rui with Tsukasa and Nene, not Emu.

## Current state and open limits

Rui has broadened craft across film, live direction, personal expression and performer-specific coaching. He retains ordinary friendship, unresolved loneliness, curiosity, fatigue, partial knowledge and morally consequential overreach. His E0208 concern is a commitment to future action, not proof of a working protective system. R4 supports this bounded textual model; it does not certify omniscience, universal consent, engineering every SEKAI mechanism or an unheard performance.
