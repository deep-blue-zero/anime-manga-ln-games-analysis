# Kamishiro Rui — provisional character monograph

## Authority snapshot

Use `WXS-EP-RUI-E0119` as Rui's current medium-aware direction operative. Preserve `WXS-EP-RUI-E0099` for ordinary social friendship, `WXS-EP-RUI-E0024` for outsider collaboration, and `WXS-MS-RUI-P06` for formation history.

## Character thesis

Rui is an experimental director whose central need is not freedom from all constraint, but collaborators able to understand, challenge, and safely realize unusual ideas. Earlier failed communication taught him that difference may lead to rejection; solo creation protects the concept at the cost of reciprocal work. The troupe matters because artistic recognition and Nene's trust make collaboration rational again. Mature direction preserves curiosity while adapting medium, performer, safety, and better solutions rather than defending the first plan as proof of genius.

## Developmental architecture

The main story presents technically compelling shows and a history of unsuccessful collaboration. Rui can diagnose stage possibility rapidly, but trust is not created by brilliance alone. He returns to a compatible team when Tsukasa, Emu, and Nene demonstrate that their different aims can survive his methods and that he can accept shared limits. Experimental desire and critical distance remain; belonging does not make him conventional.

`WXS-EP-RUI-E0024` records collaboration from an outsider position. EVENT_0099 establishes ordinary school friendship and participation beyond instrumental show needs, preventing the “isolated inventor only” model. EVENT_0119 makes his current direction method explicit: perform reliable production labor, study the new medium, diagnose the particular performer, make a causally useful proposal, accept a superior solution, and convert the result into renewed tested curiosity. EVENT_0126 shows observation becoming a workable rehearsal design without creating a successor. EVENT_0124 preserves failure-to-renewed-reach within the troupe's continuing voyage.

## Stable dispositions and contradictions

- Curiosity and critique are durable; collaboration changes method, not imaginative appetite.
- He may speak playfully while conducting serious causal observation.
- Rejection history can produce distance, but ordinary friendship is now operative.
- Safety improves through negotiated design and evidence; it should not be assumed from benevolent intent.
- Accepting a better solution strengthens rather than negates authorship.

## Relationship conditioning

Nene carries long trust, shared outsider history, and the strongest permission for quiet practical care. Tsukasa offers an ambitious performer who challenges risk and role design. Emu supplies place/audience values that experimental means must serve. Ordinary classmates test whether Rui can belong without performing uniqueness or extracting utility. Professional collaborators such as Ohara require medium-specific humility and reliable labor.

## Current state and open limits

At the cutoff Rui can study unfamiliar media, adapt to performers, accept superior solutions, and maintain ordinary social bonds. Novel safety-critical technology, prolonged institutional restriction, and deep family/private evidence remain below R4.
