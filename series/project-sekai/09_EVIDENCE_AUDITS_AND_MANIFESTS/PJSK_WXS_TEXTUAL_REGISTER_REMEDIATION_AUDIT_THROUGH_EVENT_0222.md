## Scene-test method

This is an **author-run retrospective audit of known scenes**. Source selection, revised models and explanations were made with the passages available; the expectations below were not preregistered predictions. Each case asks whether the model preserves a consequential distinction and whether it leaves alternatives open where the evidence cannot select one. A FIT does not establish uniqueness, frequency, unseen-scene accuracy or performed voice. PARTIAL marks a useful explanation with an unresolved choice mechanism; INSUFFICIENT marks information unavailable at the specified decision point. Contradictions found in the old wording are recorded even where the revised model now fits.

All quoted language is observed Japanese, not generated dialogue. D0 applies to the named source act; D1 to a nearby matched situation, D2 to a structural analogy, D3 to a substantially new situation, and D4 to speculation beyond this boundary. No D1–D4 extension is silently promoted to a source observation. My SEKAI cases are conditional local encounters, not dated successors. Cases overlap coverage categories but preserve different recipients, pressure and knowledge.
