## Decision standard and review boundary

This matrix records the individual decisions of the [retrospective phase](../08_CURRENT_RELEASE/PJSK_RETROSPECTIVE_0222_EXECUTION_RECORD.md). Each completed package requires a reconciled state/knowledge selection, broad longitudinal explanation, six distinct behavioral lanes and an adjudicated contradiction audit under the [character method](../00_FRAMEWORKS_AND_METHODS/PJSK_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md).

The six lanes are ordinary behavior, conflict, major stakes, multiple relationships, public/private presentation and textual speech. Their tests compare the model's conditional explanation with known scenes, including counterexamples to earlier generalizations. They are retrospective fit checks by the analysis author, not blind prediction scores or independent-agent replication. The linked evidence indexes own compact scene-by-scene decisions; the models own conditional rules and D0–D4 limits. Targeted Japanese probes supplement the reused complete-envelope readings. No performed-voice or continuous-AV evidence was inspected, so this phase cannot award R5.
