## Sequential account

### Chizuru extends ordinary access into her professional world

Nagomi tells Kazuya that Mami's account of the earlier alcohol incident matches his and describes Mami favorably. Kazuya also learns that Nagomi and Mami have met more than once around the proposed app work. He remains suspicious but has no factual basis for demanding that Nagomi end contact.

Chizuru then initiates a different route. She asks whether Kazuya is free on October 25 and invites him to a party in her acting network. Umi supplied one guest invitation, and Chizuru connects Kazuya's place there to his producer role and his chance to thank Umi for promoting the crowdfunding campaign. At the formal gathering, Umi introduces Kazuya to others as the film's crowdfunding producer. Chizuru affirms the account and credits his help. When Umi draws her away to show photographs, Kazuya becomes jealous and socially disoriented; Chizuru returns with food and says that because she invited him, she will not leave him alone. The attention is voluntary and unpriced, although the event remains grounded in their completed project and her professional network.

Umi separately praises Kazuya's work, asks about his relationship to acting, and posts a photograph with him to an audience shown at roughly 270,000 followers. He then asks how Kazuya interprets the phrase `好きじゃなくもない`, which Chizuru had previously used in response to Umi's question about Kazuya. Kazuya says it probably indicates liking, and Umi agrees. They exchange LINE contact. When Kazuya later asks Chizuru about the phrase, she blushes, deflects, and denies that there is anything he should read into it. Umi then asks to speak with her privately; the content is withheld. The sequence increases professional and romantic information pressure without supplying Chizuru's private answer or Umi's full purpose.

### A conditional partner account prompts Kazuya to end the Ruka trial

After the party, Kazuya and Chizuru rest in a park and discuss Mami's growing contact with Nagomi. Chizuru says Mami does not seem like a bad person, while Kazuya notes how quickly the contact has expanded. He then describes Chizuru as an actress and rental girlfriend whose closest support should eventually be a boyfriend rather than a client entangling her.

Chizuru answers at the level of possibility. She says having someone close might be good and acknowledges that she may be weak. When Kazuya asks whether she wants a boyfriend, she says that if she came to like someone, she thinks she would want to date him and remain by his side. This is a conditional account of a desired relationship, not a statement that she currently loves Kazuya or anyone else.

Kazuya interprets the exchange as requiring ethical correction of the provisional relation. At karaoke he asks Ruka to break up, states that he loves Chizuru from the bottom of his heart, says that he has no romantic feeling for Ruka, and apologizes for keeping her in a trial relation. Ruka says she already knew his preference, rejects the breakup, cries, insists that they are fated, and argues that her own feeling is enough reason to continue. She physically presses or grabs him during the protest and later says she will violate rental-girlfriend rules repeatedly if necessary. Kazuya's withdrawal of participation is explicit even though Ruka refuses the requested mutual recognition of an ending. The represented label therefore remains contested; her refusal does not create reciprocal love or consent to continue intimacy.

Kazuya resolves to confess to Chizuru and “make the lie true.” He rehearses and misses several opportunities. The effort becomes more deliberate, but no complete confession occurs in this volume.

### Chizuru reconstructs an information leak while Kibe makes truth a family obligation

Chizuru initiates another conversation after connecting Mami's remarks to a bag she had carried when Mami visited Kazuya's apartment. She checks the item's timing and concludes that Mami probably saw it there, which would let Mami infer that Kazuya and Chizuru still meet. The exact inference remains Chizuru's reconstruction rather than a direct admission from Mami at this point. Chizuru judges the situation more urgent, proposes that they avoid provoking Mami, and asks that she and Kazuya watch developments together.

Kibe then asks Kazuya which person he likes, Ruka or Chizuru. Kazuya answers Chizuru. Kibe treats that answer as sincere and advises him to value the partner he believes Kazuya already has. His advice becomes sharper when he invokes Nagomi's daily visits to the family grave and her prayers for the family's safety and Kazuya's happiness with Chizuru. He says that one should not lie to the person who cares most about one's happiness. The moral appeal is serious, but it is built on Kibe's false belief that the public couple is real. Kazuya therefore cannot follow the advice transparently without exposing the underlying rental and family deception.

### Mami consolidates evidence and approaches Chizuru directly

Mami approaches Chizuru at university by her rental name and asks to speak with her. The crowdfunding page gives Mami documentary evidence that Chizuru acted in the film, that the campaign raised 1,850,000 yen from 713 supporters, and that Kazuya served as executor. At Mister Donut, Mami says she found the page while researching the app project and kept Kibe from spreading it. She offers condolences for Sayuri and asks about Chizuru's acting and rental work. Chizuru says that her agency knows about the rental job and that Diamond Platinum permits it.

Mami asks how Chizuru views Kazuya's producer role. Chizuru distinguishes that response from her rental work and says she dealt with the project sincerely. Mami then reveals that she saw Chizuru's bag in Kazuya's room. Chizuru explains that it had been lent as a prop, while Mami treats that explanation as separate from the larger fact that the pair still share private access. Mami praises Nagomi but separates that favorable judgment from her appraisal of Kazuya.

Mami gives an interested first-person history of the former relationship. She says she initially thought she liked Kazuya, that her feeling cooled while they dated, and that his continued pursuit after the breakup felt nearly stalker-like and dangerous. She interprets Chizuru's kindness as encouraging him to believe he still has a chance. This account is evidence of Mami's represented experience and intervention strategy; the volume does not independently verify every characterization or establish that the account is fabricated.

Mami tells Chizuru not to be deceived, calls herself Chizuru's ally, invokes confidentiality, takes her hand, and proposes that they end “everything” together. The exact scope and intended method are withheld. In parallel, Kazuya resolves to confess and convert the false couple presentation into a real relationship, so the volume places opposing definitions of “ending” around Chizuru without giving her final response.

### Confession remains stalled as family contact becomes immediate

Kazuya repeatedly rehearses a confession, rushes to act, and again loses courage when he encounters Chizuru. The pressure also has ordinary costs: Ruka's LINE volume has increased by roughly fifty percent after the breakup attempt, he feels monitored, and a score shown at 27 after a prior score near 85 marks deteriorated study performance. These details do not establish a durable academic trait, but they make the immediate cost of romantic fixation observable.

Chizuru receives a call from Nagomi and later asks Kazuya how many nights he can go. The destination and full terms are withheld. She then practices batting alone while considering Mami's silence as a wait for opportunity and deciding that she will help Kazuya. Kazuya separately concludes that the Mami situation cannot continue and centers Chizuru's happiness in his renewed resolve. Neither interior sequence supplies a mutual plan or completed confession.

At the close, Mami calls Nagomi directly. Chizuru's final thought begins `その時 私は――` and is cut off. The direct family call operationalizes Mami's route, while both Chizuru's unfinished thought and the overnight question defer the next concrete event to V022.
