## Sequential account

### Ruka answers Kazuya's withdrawal with access pressure and a forced kiss

Ruka comes to Kazuya's apartment at night, attempts to remain there, and demands an explanation for his intended confession. Kazuya again says that he loves Chizuru. Ruka rejects the implication that the trial can simply end, forces another kiss, and says she will continue telling him that she loves him. Chizuru arrives in time to see the physical scene. Ruka's feeling is represented as sincere, but neither sincerity nor the old trial label supplies Kazuya's consent to continued status or touch.

Nagomi then proposes a two-night, three-day family trip to Spa Resort Hawaiians. She presents the trip as care for Chizuru after Sayuri's death. Kazuya and Chizuru try to coordinate a refusal around the rental rules, but Nagomi also invites Ruka, who immediately accepts. The overnight question at the V021 boundary therefore becomes a concrete family journey, lodging arrangement, and multi-party access problem.

### Ruka fabricates material evidence to shape Chizuru's belief

Ruka confronts Chizuru about failing to refuse the trip. Chizuru says she meant to decline but could not reject Nagomi's sincere wish to comfort her. Ruka then presents a torn condom wrapper and falsely claims that she and Kazuya had sex. Her private thought explicitly identifies the claim as a lie and a wedge: she suspects Chizuru may like Kazuya and wants to provoke or deter her. She leaves the wrapper with Chizuru, converting an invented event into portable material evidence.

Chizuru is visibly affected. She reflects on the trial relationship and the distance that has narrowed around Kazuya, yet decides to join the trip. The choice does not erase professional rules or prove romance. It shows that grief-linked family inclusion and her own unresolved relation to Kazuya now outweigh a clean refusal, while the fabricated evidence remains unverified in her information state. Kazuya, unaware of the lie, renews his plan to confess and correct the deception.

### The journey creates a split audience and a working Kazuya-Chizuru alliance

At Ueno, Nagomi unexpectedly adds Kibe and Kuribayashi so that Chizuru will have companions of similar age. Their presence creates a dangerous knowledge split. Kuribayashi knows the rental truth and the origin of Ruka's connection, while Kibe still accepts the public Kazuya-Chizuru couple account. When Ruka clings to Kazuya in front of the group, Kuribayashi privately asks why she is there. Kazuya asks him to help manage the incompatible accounts rather than disclose the history.

Chizuru privately shows Kazuya the Kinoshita family ring. She still intends to return it because accepting a family treasure after the hospital history under false terms is untenable. Kazuya acknowledges the weight of the object; Chizuru says she will help explain. They agree to get through the trip together. This is a concrete operational alliance around family truth and material obligation, although it does not itself classify their romantic relation.

During the journey, Chizuru says she has no comparable family-trip memories and suggests that she wanted this experience for herself as well as accepting Nagomi's care. She recognizes Nagomi's invitation and Kazuya's family memories as valuable forms of family attention. Kazuya responds internally by setting the trip as his deadline: he will confess and try to make the lie true. He begins to create an opportunity at the hotel entrance, but Nagomi interrupts before an answer can be sought.

### Mami enters the family trip through an established access route

Mami is waiting in the hotel lobby. She says that her parents know the resort manager and that she has been staying there since the previous day. Nagomi learned that she would be in Fukushima and incorporated her into the group. The encounter is therefore enabled by both Mami's pre-existing lodging and her active Nagomi route; the volume does not expose every causal detail of the V021 call.

Ruka recognizes Mami as Kazuya's former girlfriend, and Mami and Chizuru exchange guarded looks. Nagomi introduces Ruka as Chizuru's close friend, leaving the underlying rivalry illegible to her. Mami again frames the situation in terms of ending everything, but her exact operational plan remains withheld.

Nagomi also reveals that she knows about Chizuru's acting and Kazuya's crowdfunding and producer work. Kazuya realizes that Mami has carried substantial public-project information into the family route. The professional facts are accurate, but their circulation collapses another information compartment. He decides that he must confess to Chizuru immediately and, if the apparent relationship fails, tell the others rather than continue indefinitely.

### A legible confession attempt is evaded as the pool group converges

The room assignments intensify proximity and audience pressure. Kuribayashi privately asks Mami whether she became friendly with Chizuru and whether Chizuru still works as a rental girlfriend. Mami gives a limited confirmation. The exchange establishes that both understand a consequential part of the rental context, while neither supplies the whole network of relations.

Kazuya catches Chizuru in room 8504 and begins to answer the question she asked at Saizeriya about whether he loves her. Chizuru recognizes the direction of the speech and retreats into the bathroom, saying she has to go. Ruka and Nagomi arrive before he can resume. The attempt is direct enough to be legible to both participants, but it produces neither a completed proposition nor an answer.

Ruka then leads the group to the pool. Kazuya interprets Chizuru's flight as avoidance of a likely confession, yet also reasons that her reaction proves the intended speech is intelligible between them rather than irrelevant. He renews his determination to confess without another intermediate step.

Kibe notices that Kazuya is not behaving normally. He recalls Kazuya's earlier fear that the relationship might end and stresses how deeply Nagomi anticipated the trip after her grief. Because Kibe still believes the public account, his advice cannot solve the deception, but it renews the cost of betraying family trust. The volume closes when Chizuru and Ruka emerge in swimsuits and the pool group converges. The confession, ring return, family correction, condom-wrapper lie, and Mami intervention all remain active.
