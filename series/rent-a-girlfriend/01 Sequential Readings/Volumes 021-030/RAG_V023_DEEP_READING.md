## Sequential account

### Pool spectacle becomes an act-specific consent and care sequence

The group begins its resort day at the pool. Kazuya is intensely attracted to Chizuru and Ruka in swimsuits, but his internal sexualized attention is repeatedly separated from conduct: he averts or disguises his gaze and does not treat attraction as permission. When a two-person raft slide creates an apparent-couple expectation, Chizuru accepts the pairing and follows the attendant's safety instructions. The ride requires close contact, including her arm around him, but neither the resort staging nor Kazuya's reaction establishes romantic classification.

After the slide, Chizuru realizes that her bikini top has come loose and asks Kazuya what to do. He retrieves it, turns away, and offers to fasten it when she cannot reach the tie. She explicitly agrees. He completes the practical task and stops. This is consequential proximity governed by a specific problem and answer, not a general intimacy license. Chizuru later asks for his opinion of the swimsuit under a girlfriend-practice frame and accepts the detailed compliment with visible playfulness, but the role frame and mixed motives remain intact.

### Mami uses embedded access for separate probes and independent verification

Mami approaches Ruka privately and presents concern through the rental-service terms. She asks whether Ruka loves Kazuya, warns that Chizuru's conduct could create consequences for Chizuru and other customers, tells Ruka that she need not carry the problem alone, and says that she will contact her. The exchange is both a probe and a possible coalition opening; it does not establish trust or a settled alliance.

Mami also searches Chizuru's rental profile on her phone. Later, before Nagomi, she tells Chizuru that she will not suddenly reveal the rental arrangement without warning. The assurance temporarily limits the immediate threat while making Mami's disclosure capacity explicit. Chizuru remains wary and recalls Mami's prior offer to save Kazuya, but does not claim certainty about Mami's endpoint.

### Kazuya begins selective truth correction while tying public disclosure to confession

Kazuya voluntarily asks Mami to talk. He admits that he falsely said Chizuru was not on the trip, explains that Nagomi still does not know the rental truth, and asks Mami to act unaware because correcting the lie has become difficult. Mami asks whether he distrusts her and whether he intends to correct the story. Kazuya says he will do so after confessing to Chizuru. This is direct truth-management action and a renewed deadline consequence, but it also sequences family correction behind an unresolved romantic attempt.

Mami says that she wants Kazuya to be happy and recalls their month of dating positively. Kazuya receives the concern as sincere. Her later private affect leaves a strategic layer visible without proving that every caring statement is false. The volume therefore preserves mixed explanatory possibilities rather than assigning a single motive.

### Ruka converts status pressure into isolation, public claim, and repeated fabricated evidence

Ruka declares before Kibe and Kuribayashi that she is Kazuya's girlfriend. Kazuya objects because the claim conflicts with his attempted ending and trip purpose. Ruka then invents a lost-locker-key pretext to isolate him in the pool grotto, presses against him from behind, and invokes warmth and proximity. Kazuya rejects the false pretext and removes her. Ruka's later private account confirms that competition intensified her action; sincere attachment does not create reciprocal status or bodily consent.

Mami asks Ruka whether she is truly Kazuya's girlfriend and what she would do if he offered to make the relationship real. She then contrasts Chizuru's paid customer role with the family's belief that Chizuru is a future bride, asks whether Chizuru may actually like Kazuya, and tests whether Ruka would disclose the truth to Nagomi when the time comes. The conversation uses Ruka's insecurity to test both information and willingness to intervene; no completed alliance follows.

In the changing area, Ruka helps Chizuru with the swimsuit tie and the torn condom wrapper becomes visible. Ruka repeats the false sex claim, calls the wrapper a first-time keepsake, and later tells Kazuya in Chizuru's presence that the two of them have that kind of relationship. Kazuya immediately denies and challenges the claim. Chizuru then privately asks him whether he lied. Her question directly tests the source rather than accepting the object as conclusive, but the brief exchange does not produce a fully articulated resolution or a new relationship label.

### Chizuru recognizes chosen-family happiness while the underlying lie remains active

At dinner and the resort show, the mixed audience continues to treat Kazuya and Chizuru as a couple while Ruka and Mami retain separate intervention routes. Kazuya apologizes privately for continuing to burden Chizuru and promises himself that he will end the situation during this trip. The confession, family correction, ring return, and Ruka separation remain incomplete.

The family atmosphere activates Chizuru's memory of Sayuri. In the remembered teaching, material circumstances and the loss of parents or grandparents do not make happiness impossible; the person must recognize the happiness present and, with that disposition, people who want to remain nearby will gather. Chizuru looks at Nagomi, Kazuya, Ruka, Mami, Kibe, Kuribayashi, and the wider group around her. When Kazuya asks how the day was, she answers that it was the best. This is direct evidence that the group experience matters to her as family-like belonging. It does not repair the false premise under which Nagomi includes her or answer Kazuya romantically.
