## Sequential account

### The group resumes ordinary treatment while Kuribayashi tests the retained dating claim

The immediate post-explanation scene does not produce social expulsion. Chizuru apologizes to Kuribayashi, Nagomi frames close relationships as capable of accumulating secrets and distorted time, and the group moves toward dinner and continued lodging. That normalization is qualified rather than fully informed because genuine dating remains the sole lie in the account Nagomi accepted.

Kuribayashi then grabs and presses Kazuya about whether the pair are actually dating and repeatedly invokes the kiss as evidence. Kazuya answers that Chizuru matched his account. Nagomi stops the confrontation. Kuribayashi's question establishes an immediate peer-trust consequence, but neither his challenge nor the public kisses settles the private relationship classification.

### Ruka confronts Chizuru about the kisses and the professional boundary

Ruka follows Chizuru into the women's restroom and demands the meaning of the kisses. She asks whether Chizuru was serious and whether she likes Kazuya, rejects necessity or work as a sufficient explanation, and recalls that she herself once kissed him suddenly. Her anger targets a contradiction she sees between Chizuru's professional label and intimate conduct.

Ruka grabs Chizuru's clothing or shoulder, compares kissing a client while calling it work to off-book sex work, and starts to contact the rental agency. Chizuru stops her hand. Chizuru agrees that the act is a complete professional violation and says that if the office requires reopening or discipline, she will apologize. Ruka argues that Chizuru keeps hold of Kazuya while denying feeling. Chizuru answers that it may not be too late after Ruka examines her own true feelings. She then leaves Ruka clenching her fist and cursing. The exchange is a real boundary and status confrontation; it does not terminate Ruka's attachment or resolve Chizuru's own classification.

### Chizuru shares responsibility but interprets the kisses as emergency action

Kazuya privately replays the kisses and asks whether they mean that Chizuru likes him. When the two face each other, Chizuru says that the event was the second time, that rule-breaking is already a fact, and that she saw no other choice in the scene. She refuses to treat excuses as sufficient and says that a synchronized breakup account will eventually become believable.

Kazuya reads the explanation as relationship maintenance rather than a romantic answer. Chizuru says that part of Mami's accusation was factual and that she understands their guilt. She also reminds him that he tried through the end to tell Nagomi the truth, identifies her own responsibility, and rejects a version in which he heroically makes everything his fault. The private debrief therefore distributes responsibility and rejects unilateral self-sacrifice while leaving the kiss's romantic meaning unconfirmed.

### Chizuru's private reflection names a feeling she treats as professionally prohibited

Kazuya later reasons that Chizuru would say so if she liked him and remains unable to settle the difference between rescue and reciprocity. Chizuru, alone by the pool, replays his rescue, the film commitment, his ideal-girlfriend speech, and Sayuri's interpretation of their bond. She then thinks that it is unacceptable to like a customer and blushes.

This interior access is stronger than an external inference: attraction or attachment has become personally salient enough to conflict with her client rule. It still does not establish a final label, a mutual agreement, or a decision to pursue a relationship.

### Ruka demands renewed access limits, and Chizuru begins a prolonged avoidance

Two days after the return, Kazuya remains consumed by the kisses. He distinguishes the earlier rescue-breath contact from these kisses as kisses and reasons that Chizuru knew about his confession before acting. Ruka calls the Hawaiians contact a necessary evil that does not count, says it protected her boyfriend, and demands that Chizuru reconsider her position. She argues that a rental girlfriend should not continue ordinary train travel or comparable access to him. Chizuru apologizes and says that she must hurry.

Kazuya then takes a misdelivered yoga flyer to Chizuru's apartment and rings repeatedly. Chizuru is inside, listens without answering, and looks out only after he leaves. He concludes that she is absent; she collapses again and voices frustration. The sequence converts professional conflict into observable avoidance without giving Kazuya its reason.

### Silence extends for roughly three months

Kazuya messages Chizuru at 20:18 about the flyer and asks whether he should put it in her mailbox. She reads the message immediately but does not reply. Narration anchors the resort return to November 25 and states that the principals have no contact for roughly three months. Even Ruka's full-alert posture relaxes during the absence, while Kazuya becomes visibly disheveled and distressed.

The silence is an active informational consequence rather than proof that Chizuru hates him. She has read the message, controls the nonresponse, and remains physically nearby. Kazuya lacks that full access and interprets the absence through rejection fear.

### Mini returns and intervenes first with Kazuya, then with Chizuru

Mini returns from India after about a month away, finds Kazuya and his room in poor condition, and hears about the kisses and the long ghosting. Kazuya says that Chizuru must hate him. Mini calls that conclusion self-pity and argues that Chizuru has some favorable feeling toward him even if its exact form remains unsettled. She cries with relief that the relationship advanced and decides to contact Chizuru directly.

Mini rings Chizuru's door, encounters her returning with groceries, hugs her, and enters her room. This intervention creates contact and useful information, but Mini again acts from an interested romantic theory and moves faster than either principal requested.

### Chizuru cannot name the feeling, and Mini tells her to investigate it

Chizuru recounts the Hawaiians crisis and says that she does not regret her actions and would do the same again. She is troubled by harm to Ruka, whom she originally encouraged, and says that if her own boundary collapse produced the outcome she would find that unforgivable. She admits that she does not understand why she protected Kazuya before Ruka and that the feeling lacks a name strong enough to persuade or answer Ruka.

Mini labels the feeling love. Chizuru refuses to accept that classification as simple or obvious and envies the intensity with which Ruka can feel. Mini argues that rental work may have trained Chizuru to suppress or discount her own feelings. She tells Chizuru to examine them, contact Kazuya, stop fleeing, and face his feeling directly, while adding that he will wait until she is ready. Chizuru answers with a visible resolution to act. Mini's diagnosis remains an interpretation; Chizuru's commitment to inquiry is directly evidenced.

### Kazuya uses the rental system to reopen contact

On February 19, nearly three months after the resort, Kazuya tests whether Chizuru has blocked him and discovers that she has not. Mini tells him that she spoke with Chizuru, takes responsibility for pushing both principals, and says that Chizuru's feeling has no name. The report does not establish that Chizuru accepted Mini's love label. Kazuya instead takes relief from learning that Chizuru does not hate him and resolves to act with his own strength.

He requests Chizuru through the rental platform. The booking is accepted, and he reasons that because the relation began through rental work, he will seek resolution through that same route. On March 1 at 12:59 in Harajuku, Chizuru arrives for the paid date. Both remember the resort kiss and show strong bodily arousal or nervousness. The booking restores access while preserving the very client-provider frame Chizuru experiences as a barrier.

### The paid date reaches a direct-conversation threshold

Kazuya expects a performed girlfriend mode, but Chizuru's formal greeting is followed by a smile and invitation to go to a café. He resolves to ask what she feels. Attraction and self-consciousness repeatedly derail him while tea, dessert, and offered food create small acts of care inside the booked performance.

After roughly an hour and a half, a receipt shows a total around 28,860 yen and about thirty minutes remain. Kazuya still has not asked. He finally resolves to speak, but Chizuru says “Hey” first. The main story ends on their simultaneous seriousness before her content appears. The volume therefore establishes paid recontact and Chizuru's conversational initiative without supplying the answer or resolving whether the rental wrapper will be retained, suspended, or contested.
