## Sequential account

### Protective-lie reasoning meets explicit chosen-family language

During the night bath interval, Kazuya discusses deception with an older family-connected man. The man distinguishes lies used for self-protection from lies meant to protect another person and asks whether Kazuya's wish for Chizuru's happiness is false. Kazuya cannot conclude that the family fiction served Nagomi, but he can state that his wish for Chizuru's happiness is real. The exchange does not absolve the deception; it clarifies the value he wants a correction to preserve.

Nagomi separately tells Chizuru that Sayuri's death was painful and that people do not become strong alone. She cannot replace Sayuri, but many people want Chizuru to be happy. She therefore tells Chizuru not to think she has no family and says that Chizuru's family is here. The statement converts the earlier daughter-like pattern into direct kinship language. Chizuru is moved, while the false couple premise remains uncorrected.

### Ruka tests Nagomi, withholds disclosure, and renews direct pressure on Chizuru

Ruka asks Nagomi how she would respond if someone said that she, rather than Chizuru, were Kazuya's real girlfriend. Nagomi says that Chizuru meets every hoped-for condition, but she would support the person Kazuya chose because his happiness controls the answer. Ruka moves toward naming herself and then stops. Her silence preserves the family cover even as she continues to contest the relation privately.

The next morning, Ruka confronts Chizuru about marriage and the heirloom ring. Chizuru says there is no marriage plan, that the ring is held rather than accepted as her own, and that immediate return would be difficult because of Nagomi's feelings. Ruka demands a return and factual correction, asks whether Kazuya is Chizuru's favorite person or only a customer, and repeats the fabricated sex-and-wrapper account. Chizuru cannot provide the requested status answer. When Nagomi enters twice, Ruka invents a birthday-gift explanation instead of exposing the rental arrangement. Her moral accusation and her own cover maintenance remain in direct tension.

### An unreportable trip charge and Harumi's ring release separate obligation from care

At the vending area, Chizuru raises the trip's rental accounting. Because the lodging cannot be reported through the agency, she suggests an approximate two-date payment rather than treating the trip as automatically free. She also tells Kazuya that, despite Mami's pressure, Ruka's involvement, and the lie everyone is covering, the pool, volleyball, bath, and meal felt ordinary, refreshing, and fun. She thanks him. The statement gives positive first-person weight to the experience without defining romance or validating the deception.

Harumi then asks Chizuru about family and the inherited ring. She recounts marrying Kazuo at twenty-four, undergoing fertility treatment, and giving birth to Kazuya eight years later at thirty-two with Nagomi's support. Kazuya remains her precious only son, so she tells Chizuru that her treasure's treasure is also her treasure and offers herself as a future consultation route. Crucially, she says Chizuru may return the ring without resentment and need not be trapped by it. Chizuru is moved but cannot explain that she is not the real girlfriend.

In the souvenir shop, Harumi buys an inexpensive princess-like ring and has Chizuru try it. Mami watches the family-coded scene, compares it with Chizuru's earlier denials, and privately calls her a liar. The small souvenir ring repeats the inherited ring's imagery while making Mami an active evaluator of the gap between Chizuru's disclaimers and conduct.

### Chizuru questions Kazuya about Mami while Mami's intervention routes tighten

When Kazuya and Chizuru are briefly alone, Chizuru asks whether he knew Mami would come and whether his relation or feeling toward Mami has changed. Kazuya says he does not want Mami, describes her as dangerous, and praises Chizuru's considerate handling of her. He nevertheless offers to help because his prior attachment may let him understand Mami. The exchange gives Chizuru direct clarification of his present preference without revealing Mami's endpoint or completing the wrapper debrief.

A locked social account presented within Mami's sequence posts about observing the rental-girlfriend situation, expecting to be cast as the bad person, holding back before the apparent couple, and deserving Chizuru's gratitude. The sequence supports a private self-framing of restraint and resentment, but it does not establish that every public caring statement is sincere or false. Later, Nagomi messages Mami that she wants to talk. Mami's family-facing route therefore advances from observation and pressure toward a direct meeting whose content remains withheld.

### Family history, Kuribayashi's initiative, and the chapel deadline converge

Nagomi tells Kazuya that Hawaiians is where Kazuo and Harumi held their wedding. She praises Chizuru, says people never know when they will die, and frames the trip as a memory that will remain even if later events become difficult. She thanks Kazuya for twenty-one years. He cries, briefly fears that she knows the truth, and then rejects that inference. The family history and mortality language intensify both the value of the trip and the cost of continued concealment.

Kuribayashi directly seeks Ruka and tells her that he came to see her. This opens a new direct route between former client and provider, but the volume ends before his purpose or her response develops. In parallel, Chizuru waits at the chapel under same-day answer pressure, and Mami receives Nagomi's request to talk.

### Kazuya begins the confession, and the volume ends before its content

Kazuya reviews the accumulated deception, Nagomi's gratitude, Mami and Ruka's pressure, and his responsibility for Chizuru's burden. A memory sequence gathers Chizuru's paid dates, care, smiles, grief, and ordinary contact. He decides that rejection, loss, and pain are preferable to indefinite concealment and that a feeling abandoned merely because it may hurt would not meet his own definition of love.

He runs to the chapel, finds Chizuru, and calls for her to listen. This is a direct initiated confession event rather than another internal promise. The volume cuts before he states the proposition, supplies the wider truth, or receives any answer. The endpoint therefore advances action while preserving the exact content and outcome as open.
