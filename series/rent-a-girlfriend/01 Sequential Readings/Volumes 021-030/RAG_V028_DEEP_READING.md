## Sequential account

### Chizuru answers the paid-date threshold with apology, chocolate, and a direct question about feeling

As the booked date nears its endpoint, Kazuya interprets Chizuru's refusal of handholding and the visible clock as confirmation that he remains only a customer. Chizuru stops him from leaving, gives him Valentine chocolate roughly two weeks late, and apologizes for avoiding him and reading his practical message without replying. She says that concern for Ruka made contact feel wrong and also apologizes for disappearing after the Hawaiians trip.

Kazuya distinguishes the earlier rescue breathing from the resort kisses and asks whether the kisses had meaning beyond professional necessity. He says that he does not require an immediate answer but wants her honest account, and he restates his feeling for “Mizuhara.” Chizuru asks to speak frankly. The endpoint statement withheld by V027 therefore becomes an explicit account of silence, professional boundaries, the kisses, and unresolved feeling.

### Chizuru refuses both a false answer and indefinite ambiguity

Chizuru asks whether Kazuya likes her and then says that she does not know what her own feeling is. She treats the original customer-provider relation as a real obstacle rather than a disposable origin story. She accepts responsibility for kissing him twice and says she considered quitting the rental-girlfriend job because of the breach. She also explains that she values the work and depends on it for tuition and expenses. Kazuya rejects an outcome in which she punishes herself alone.

Chizuru says that she wants an answer she will not regret. Mini's claim that love can precede conscious recognition remains an interpretation rather than Chizuru's conclusion. Chizuru worries that Kazuya may love “Mizuhara Chizuru,” the professional presentation, instead of her whole person. Kazuya answers that a person cannot be divided so cleanly. Chizuru says that she wants to learn whether she can hold the feeling and explicitly commits to investigating it. The conversation produces a defined inquiry commitment without creating mutual dating status.

### Paid recontact immediately becomes ordinary private messaging

After they part, Chizuru sends an ordinary LINE message about the yoga flyer misdelivered during the separation. She thanks Kazuya and asks him to keep it until she comes to collect it. The practical message reverses the earlier read-without-reply pattern and moves contact beyond the paid booking. Kazuya walks roughly two hours home in elation and treats the chocolate as a precious object he cannot yet eat.

Mini returns and asks what progress occurred. Kazuya reports that Chizuru is investigating her feeling. Mini speculates about observation and bodily or romantic compatibility, while Kazuya turns the inquiry into exaggerated imagined tests. The speculation is not evidence of Chizuru's method.

### Chizuru enters Kazuya's room and makes Ruka part of the inquiry's operating conditions

Chizuru arrives to retrieve the flyer, asks whether they can talk, enters Kazuya's apartment, and tells him to close the door. She says that she wants to organize the situation. Her first substantive test is not a romantic act but a direct question about Ruka's current status. She says that roughly three months of ghosting to clear Ruka's feelings did not make sense, asks what has changed during approximately a year and a half, and hears that Kazuya has become more distant since Hawaiians while Ruka remains vigilant.

Chizuru says that Ruka's feeling is real and that she does not want to hurt her further. She recognizes that private meetings in Kazuya's room are difficult to justify under that concern. She also admits that she lacks a concrete investigation method and that the position may look childish or uncool. Even so, she promises that she will definitely give Kazuya an answer for what she did and asks him to permit this unresolved state for now. The inquiry therefore gains both a prospective answer and an explicit rival-harm limit.

### Unpriced time continues through an ordinary domestic visit

Instead of leaving after the serious conversation, Chizuru asks what they can do, and the pair watch YouTube together at Kazuya's computer. She laughs at Mini's channel and stays until shortly after 20:00 before leaving for intensified theater work. The time is private, ordinary, and unbooked, although closeness and enjoyment do not establish romantic status.

Kazuya tells her there is no need to hurry and says he can wait ten or twenty years until she is satisfied with her answer. Chizuru warns that his family expectations and marriage timing may make such waiting costly. He repeats that he will wait. She tells him to be reassured because she will try. The exchange converts patience into a mutual prospective understanding while leaving both result and duration open.

### A laundromat misunderstanding ends in a direct theater invitation

Kazuya encounters Chizuru at a coin laundry and mistakes an older customer's forgotten bra for hers. His attempt to manage the item produces a comic misunderstanding rather than intimate access. Chizuru returns for something she forgot, and the actual owner later identifies the underwear as hers.

Before the correction, Chizuru gives Kazuya a ticket to her next stage performance and tells him to deliver it promptly rather than hesitate. The invitation extends access to her vocational life outside a rental booking. The surrounding sexualized mistake remains Kazuya's misidentification and supplies no fact about Chizuru's property or consent.

### Movie closure and sustained LINE contact normalize ordinary communication

Kazuya returns stored film-project items and reports that the production work is officially complete. During his karaoke shift, Ruka appears in uniform, identifies herself through the girlfriend role, notices that he looks pale, and says that she worries because he nearly collapsed. Her care is direct, but it does not alter his earlier withdrawal or settle the disputed trial.

Chizuru and Kazuya then sustain ordinary LINE conversation for more than three hours. They discuss the returned film materials, storage, theater and rehearsal work, production experience, clothing, career prospects, and practical work life. Chizuru's replies are engaged rather than merely functional. A message from Nagomi says that winter passed without her seeing Chizuru, preserving the family route under the residual dating lie. Chizuru eventually falls asleep holding her phone. The sequence establishes a durable channel beyond paid time without turning message frequency into a relationship agreement.

### Kibe accepts an apology while preserving a demanding friendship ethic

On April 7, the third university year begins. Kibe has kept some distance since Hawaiians. Kazuya apologizes for the deception and for hurting him. Kibe says that the lie hurt because Kazuya had previously spoken earnestly, but he also says that people lie and that a lie does not automatically prove malice. He values the small number of people and things one can protect more than money or authority.

Kibe accepts the couple account as real and threatens comic future violence if Kazuya and Chizuru break up. The reconciliation restores friendship contact and revises his immediate punitive response, but it remains based on the residual false dating premise. Sumi appears silently near the campus sequence without an observed exchange, leaving her knowledge and purpose unavailable.

### Chizuru invites Kazuya into her childhood home for practical help

After roughly a week without a visibly legible investigation result, Chizuru comes to Kazuya's door and asks him to go out with her. She clarifies that the destination is not apartment 204 but her grandmother's house. Sayuri has died, the family house is empty, sale is under consideration, and Chizuru needs practical help sorting and lifting before a contractor handles the remainder.

At the gate, the Ichinose name makes the family setting explicit. Chizuru gives Kazuya access to an old kitchen, rooms, appliances, smells, and household objects tied to her upbringing. The invitation is purpose-bound labor, yet it materially expands his access from paid public performance and neighboring apartments into private family history.

### Stones, cleaning, and the altar turn family memory into shared labor

The house contains many stones collected by Katsuhito during taxi work. Chizuru says that Sayuri never discarded them. She and Kazuya divide the work: Chizuru sorts possessions and cleans while Kazuya moves stones and refuse. A teddy bear is retained; other objects are separated for disposal. The old house makes bereavement, property transition, and ordinary family materiality visible without reducing them to romantic scenery.

Kazuya strains under a heavy stone, and Chizuru steadies or catches him at close range. The contact is situational support rather than negotiated intimacy. She remarks that people can produce surprising strength when someone is in trouble. Kazuya then notices the household Buddhist altar and asks about its photographs. Chizuru identifies one image as her grandfather, and a photograph of the younger Chizuru with her grandparents becomes the volume's final revelation. The endpoint opens a family-history disclosure while the house sale, cleanup, investigation result, residual dating lie, ring, and Ruka boundary remain unresolved.
