## Volume thesis

Volume 3 converts rescue from an isolated heroic image into an obligation network. Chizuru saves Kazuya in turn through CPR; both receive medical care; Mami's private meeting is missed; Kibe interprets the act through the public couple story; and Chizuru publicly names the rescue as what a girlfriend would do. The emergency therefore disrupts the announced ending without resolving what either person feels. It supplies strong action evidence while each participant still contains its meaning through an existing role.

The attempted breakup is then reconfigured twice. First, Kazuya becomes conscious that he wants to know Chizuru and asks to keep renting her; she grants only “a little longer.” Second, Chizuru formalizes the extension as an interim route toward a real girlfriend, with the eventual real relationship functioning as the trigger for disclosure to their grandmothers. The story advances through a narrower negotiated continuation rather than a mutually acknowledged romance.

The hot-spring sequence also changes the source of pressure. Nagomi and Sayuri actively engineer shared space, but Sayuri's statement that everyone lies removes the assumption that Chizuru must preserve a flawless self-image to remain loved. Chizuru nevertheless does not disclose. Her choice to enjoy the trip, let Kazuya stay in the room, and permit continued rentals carries personal and family significance, while her repeated customer language prevents those choices from becoming unlimited access.

Ruka turns the same structure outward. She performs affection as Kuribayashi's girlfriend, recognizes Chizuru as a rental provider, tests the public couple through a demanded kiss, and gains leverage over the secret. Kazuya answers her threat by explaining what the arrangement has done for his family and by physically catching her when she falls. His defense that Chizuru is the “best girlfriend” affects Ruka, but her closing disclosure that she too is a rental girlfriend means her apparent relationship with Kuribayashi must be reevaluated before any motive is fixed.
