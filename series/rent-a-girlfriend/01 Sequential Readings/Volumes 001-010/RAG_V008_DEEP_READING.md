## Sequential account

### Satisfaction 59-60 — lost-key help becomes a private-space collision

Chizuru cannot enter her apartment after losing her key, so Kazuya lets her wait inside his while she works out access. Both remain acutely aware of the private setting. An accidental spill and fall place Chizuru over him; although aroused, he asks her to move rather than treating the accident as permission.

Ruka arrives unexpectedly before the situation is cleanly resolved. Kazuya and Chizuru try to prevent discovery, but Ruka detects the private presence and learns that the two were alone in his apartment. Chizuru explains the lost-key problem sufficiently to correct a sexual inference. The explanation does not eliminate Ruka's rivalry: practical access has produced new information and a new monitoring pressure.

### Satisfaction 61 — family history makes the acting goal personal

Chizuru tells Kazuya that her father left when she was young, her mother later died, and her grandparents raised her; her grandfather also died while she was still in school. She describes his forceful encouragement of her acting dream and her wish to show Sayuri that dream realized on screen. This supplies a first-person family and vocational account beyond Sayuri's earlier testimony.

Kazuya says he wants to see her fulfill the dream and wants to support or stand beside her even while doubting his adequacy. Chizuru answers that accompanying a lonely person is exactly the sort of need a rental girlfriend can fulfill. The exchange therefore increases personal knowledge and commitment while she re-bounds its intimate implication through the service role. Kazuya internally distinguishes his actual feeling from the available labels: rental provider, neighbor, client relation, and the provisional Ruka relationship do not change whom he wants to support.

### Satisfaction 62-65 — an ordinary-date initiative becomes an overnight consent test

Ruka visits Kazuya's university and enters a lecture despite not attending the school. She argues that roughly two months of provisional dating have not made him regard her as a girlfriend and decides to create more ordinary couple experience. She leads him through grocery shopping, cooks curry at his apartment, and makes visible domestic effort.

A typhoon disrupts transport and supplies a practical basis for staying overnight. Ruka showers, wears Kazuya's oversized shirt, displays condoms, and presses the possibility of sex. Kazuya refuses; no sex occurs. They remain in the apartment and negotiate sleeping proximity, while Ruka voices frustration that he will not act. Kazuya can recognize her effort and attractiveness without supplying reciprocal feeling or sexual agreement.

In the morning Ruka initiates a kiss at the door without shown advance agreement. The night expands her access and creates a private event that Chizuru does not know, but it does not complete the desired conversion of status into reciprocity.

### Satisfaction 66-67 — profile metadata becomes a paid gift inquiry

Kazuya sees on Chizuru's rental profile that her birthday is April 19 and wants to give an appropriate present. Because he lacks ordinary knowledge of her preferences, he books Sumi for a two-hour rental date specifically to ask for help. Paid access is thus redirected from simulated romance to information gathering for another person's birthday.

Sumi appears more composed and communicative than on the practice date while remaining visibly shy. After Kazuya explains the purpose, she takes initiative in the shopping trip, uses writing and gesture, tries options, and supplies a specific clue that Chizuru likes *Romance of the Three Kingdoms*. She also initiates girlfriend-role arm contact. The volume ends while she is still leading or redirecting him, before the gift choice and delivery are settled.
