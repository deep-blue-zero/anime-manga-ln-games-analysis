## Sequential account

### Satisfaction 50 — direct language retreats into the rental frame

Chizuru immediately asks what Kazuya meant. Under direct scrutiny he says he meant that he wants her as his rental girlfriend and would be lonely if she quit. She criticizes the confusing wording and accepts the professional explanation. In private, however, she returns home blushing, curls around herself, and calls him an idiot. The sequence supplies an observable response without converting the relation: public classification retreats while private affect remains active and unspecific.

### Satisfaction 51-53 — acting loss produces practical support

Kazuya attends Chizuru's play under her acting name, Chizuru Ichinose; Sumi independently attends as well. Chizuru's physical comic performance wins laughter and applause, and Kazuya judges her the standout. Another actress, Shiori, receives the next production's lead. The pages establish the result but do not prove that prior acquaintance caused it.

Chizuru reads the loss as evidence that she may lack talent. Kazuya rejects that judgment, praises the performance, asks her not to quit, and offers a concrete support mechanism: he will work and keep booking her so rental income can sustain her acting effort. She accepts bookings for the following weeks. Alone, she cries while annotating a script and recommits. The acting setback postpones the proposed retirement and turns Kazuya's admiration into labor and expenditure.

### Sumi supplemental vignette — persistence under communication difficulty

The labeled extra follows Sumi's routine, preparation for rental work, university life, continuing customer difficulty, and request for Chizuru's advice. She recalls Kazuya's acceptance and encouragement and decides to continue. An emerging personal feeling worries her as potentially improper for the job. Because the chapter is explicitly supplemental, it supports bounded Sumi reconstruction but does not carry the main causal sequence.

### Satisfaction 55 — a financial misreading creates a secrecy cost

Kazuya's father has seen an earlier money exchange and assumes his son borrowed from his girlfriend. He strikes Kazuya and says he must never borrow money from the woman he loves, connecting the rebuke to family financial responsibility. Kazuya could clear himself only by exposing Chizuru's rental work, so he accepts the injury. Chizuru says he could have told the truth; he answers that protecting her secret makes the injury cheap. She thanks him and says his statement that she has talent made her happy.

### Satisfaction 56-57 — Chizuru grants unpriced ordinary and family access

At the start of their second university year, Chizuru questions the scale and fairness of Kazuya's weekly bookings. He says he wants to support her as a customer. She then asks him to accompany her without a booking, takes him to a batting center, displays practiced baseball skill, and asks him to behave naturally rather than strain around her.

She next asks him to visit the hospital because Sayuri has been readmitted and wants to see him. The public boyfriend explanation remains false, yet Chizuru voluntarily brings Kazuya into ordinary recreation and a vulnerable family setting outside paid time.

### Satisfaction 58 — family testimony, direct declaration, and a practical cliffhanger

Sayuri describes Chizuru as a clingy child who cried when an older child took a toy and crawled into Sayuri's futon when lonely. She frames adult strength as armor around weakness and says acting is Chizuru's capacity to cover that weakness; even a strong person needs someone who accepts it. This is an intimate relative's report, not omniscient access to Chizuru's present interior.

Sayuri asks Kazuya about the relationship. He says plainly that he loves Chizuru. Sayuri says that is enough. Chizuru does not hear the exchange, so it produces truthful disclosure to a family witness without mutual romantic transition. Kazuya then decides that supporting Chizuru need not depend on being her boyfriend. The volume ends when Chizuru arrives at his apartment because she has lost her key.
