## Sequential account

### Satisfaction 33 — Ruka tests a boundary and accepts a refusal

Ruka uses the private karaoke room to press the difference between herself and Chizuru. She wants introduction to Nagomi as a concrete step toward recognition and argues that, unlike a rental girlfriend, she can become the real partner who meets the family's expectations. She then asks whether Kazuya could have sex with Chizuru, closes bodily distance, says she is prepared, and presents herself as the available real girlfriend.

Kazuya anticipates a kiss and retreats. He apologizes and leaves after Nagomi's message fixes a January 3 meeting with Chizuru. Ruka does not complete a kiss or sexual act. Alone, she records a pulse near ninety-one and tells herself that order matters and that she should not rush. The sequence supports both her willingness to create physical pressure and her capacity to stop after resistance. It does not convert the provisional label into sexual consent.

### Satisfaction 33-36 — New Year converts rivalry into a family contest

Chizuru appears for the January 3 family greeting because the public girlfriend role now carries a holiday obligation. Ruka arrives uninvited, places herself beside Kazuya, and tells Nagomi that she is his girlfriend. Kazuya immediately recasts the statement as Ruka's habit of lying so that the family does not infer two-timing. Nagomi reacts with anger at the imagined betrayal. The repair protects the central secret by humiliating Ruka in front of the family.

Chizuru takes Ruka aside. Ruka argues that the continuing family deception is unsustainable, that she can become the real girlfriend while Chizuru withdraws, and that lying to the grandmothers is ethically suspect. She asks whether Chizuru likes Kazuya; if Chizuru does not, Ruka says she should move aside because Ruka is serious. Chizuru does not supply a romantic self-report.

At the meal, Chizuru feeds Kazuya within the performed girlfriend role while Nagomi watches approvingly. Ruka protests that she is treated as a mere friend although nobody asked whether she could replace Chizuru. Kazuya acknowledges internally that Ruka may be the only person who genuinely likes him, even while he remains attached to Chizuru. Mami passes nearby and appears to notice the group or Kazuya's concealment maneuver, but the extent of her knowledge is not established in this scene.

Ruka then obtains a private opening with Nagomi and prepares to reveal that Chizuru is a rental girlfriend. Nagomi speaks first. She asks Ruka to teach her what Chizuru likes, dislikes, eats, and laughs at because Chizuru remains reserved around the family. Nagomi says she wants, even if it takes years, to love Chizuru like a daughter rather than merely receive her as a bride. Ruka abandons the disclosure. The family attachment she planned to challenge becomes a reason not to destroy the relation immediately.

Ruka's shrine fortune advises that this is not a good opportunity and that she should not rush. She tells Kazuya that she remains his girlfriend but will not break his grandmother's relationship with Chizuru. Instead, she will make Nagomi recognize her as the real partner. This is a strategic delay, not withdrawal from the rivalry.

### Satisfaction 36-37 — the job becomes routine, access, and money

Roughly one week into the new year, Kazuya is more accustomed to karaoke work. Ruka appears in school uniform and announces that she has taken a job at the same venue. Employment now changes recurring contact and secrecy logistics: their manager warns against flirting, while Kazuya must prevent Kuribayashi from discovering Ruka at the workplace.

Kuribayashi arrives with friends and remains visibly hurt by the apparent end of his relationship with Ruka. Kazuya reviews Kuribayashi's earlier public enthusiasm and recognizes that his friend treated the experience seriously. Kazuya receives his first pay. Rather than using the new income only for his own dates, he forms a reparative plan: he will pay Chizuru to meet Kuribayashi and create a context in which the rental-girlfriend truth can be understood.

### Satisfaction 38-39 — paid performance enables a costly truth

Kuribayashi meets Chizuru in Asakusa and learns that she is a rental girlfriend. He searches her profile and sees the highly rated professional presentation, then initially tries to leave. Chizuru chooses to continue the outing. She holds his hand, visits the places he wants to see, and keeps the date moving when his shame and suspicion threaten to end it.

The date remains compensated labor. Chizuru's skill and Kuribayashi's enjoyment do not transform it into private courtship. Its function is nevertheless not exhausted by commercial exchange: Kazuya has paid for the booking as part of an apology, and Chizuru voluntarily helps the plan work rather than performing the minimum opening encounter.

At the end, Kuribayashi tries to pay and learns that Kazuya has already covered the cost. Kazuya then reveals himself and confesses that he too hired a rental girlfriend, presented her as a real partner, and maintained the lie before family and friends. This disclosure supplies the missing context for Ruka's earlier role. Kuribayashi is embarrassed and angry, but he understands that Kazuya exposed a deeply humiliating secret in order to protect him from feeling uniquely deceived. The two laugh together, and Kuribayashi accepts the repair.

Chizuru later tells Kazuya that she knew Kuribayashi was his friend and understood that he was trying to encourage him. Kazuya's statement that he sees Chizuru and Ruka as exceptionally desirable women is still clumsy and romanticized, but the central act is concrete: first wages, paid service, self-exposure, and a repaired friendship form one causal chain.

### Satisfaction 40 — staged underwear produces rival signaling and mistaken possession

Two and a half months later, Kazuya finds women's underwear on his veranda and assumes it belongs to Chizuru. He tries to return or conceal it while Chizuru and then Ruka appear. Ruka has come to his apartment and creates close physical pressure while demanding that he show what he is hiding. Chizuru enters during the struggle.

The underwear is Ruka's. She says she deliberately hung it on the veranda so Chizuru would see it and infer intimacy between Ruka and Kazuya. Kazuya had mistaken the object for Chizuru's and pocketed it in panic. Ruka calls him a pervert after learning what he thought. The comic form sexualizes the misunderstanding, but the actual agency is asymmetric rival signaling: Ruka plants an object to manufacture evidence of private access, Kazuya conceals it, and Chizuru receives the confused result.

### Satisfaction 41 — Chizuru privately recruits Kazuya for Sumi's training

Chizuru leaves a handwritten instruction asking Kazuya to meet on the balcony at 9 p.m. She appears as Ichinose rather than in a booked Mizuhara encounter and asks for advice. A shy young woman has recently begun rental-girlfriend work but cannot communicate effectively with clients. Chizuru asks Kazuya to rent her so she can practice. He agrees because Chizuru requests it.

The balcony conversation also revisits Mami. Chizuru asks what happened after the Izu sequence and treats Mami's earlier kiss and approach as purposeful rather than harmless. Kazuya admits that he still cannot cleanly name his feeling. Chizuru encourages him to resolve the lingering attachment, but the volume does not show that resolution.

At 1 p.m. near Tobu-Nerima Station, Kazuya meets Sumi Sakurasawa, a first-year university student. Her social anxiety or extreme shyness is represented through silence, physical trembling, and difficulty producing ordinary greeting speech. Kazuya realizes that he will need to lead the practice date. Mami then sees Kazuya and Sumi walking together. The volume closes on her recognition, without showing what she concludes or does next.

## Causal and state deltas

### Kazuya

Kazuya refuses Ruka's private-room escalation rather than treating trial status as entitlement. He still protects the family fiction by publicly humiliating her, and he remains unable to offer a clean answer to the Mami question. His largest gain is reparative initiative toward Kuribayashi. This is the first completed truth disclosure to a friend, but it is deliberately bounded to Kuribayashi and leaves the wider deception intact.

### Chizuru

Chizuru directly contests Ruka's demand without giving a romantic self-report. She chooses sustained work in the Kuribayashi plan and later privately recruits Kazuya as a practice client for Sumi. These actions extend her role from provider to coordinator and trusted collaborator. Her acting ambition is not mentioned or given practical consequence in V005, so the model must preserve it as prior durable knowledge without inventing continuity evidence.

### Ruka

Ruka moves from immediate private escalation to strategic long-term rivalry. She can stop after Kazuya retreats and can abandon disclosure when Nagomi's family attachment becomes morally concrete. She does not relinquish the goal: she joins Kazuya's workplace and later plants underwear to manufacture visible evidence of closeness. Restraint, sincerity, pressure, and deception remain jointly required.

### Kuribayashi, Sumi, Mami, and Nagomi

Kuribayashi gains the basic rental truth and accepts Kazuya's apology; his reaction closes a V003-V004 information gap. Sumi enters as a new provider whose communication difficulty prompts the practice-date arrangement, but her broader life and durable rules remain underobserved. Mami re-enters only as an observer at the final page. Nagomi's wish to love Chizuru as a daughter deepens the causal cost of disclosure.
