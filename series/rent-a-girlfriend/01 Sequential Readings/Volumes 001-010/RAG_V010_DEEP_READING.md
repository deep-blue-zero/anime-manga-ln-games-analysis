## Sequential account

### Satisfaction 77 — Mami turns a sighting into direct and networked inquiry

Mami asks Kibe about Ruka and learns that she works with Kazuya, while Kibe still understands Chizuru as the girlfriend known to Kazuya's family. On the train, Mami recognizes Chizuru performing a rental date. She waits until the client leaves, calls Chizuru back at Ikebukuro, and asks whether she is still seeing Kazuya. Chizuru recalls Ruka's request that she stop and answers that she is not. Mami accepts the answer outwardly while judging the repeated contact too persistent to be ordinary rental distance.

Mami then finds and follows the social-media account for the Kinoshita family liquor store. Its profile identifies the owners' beloved only grandson and mentions his girlfriend. The sighting therefore produces both a direct test and access to Kazuya's family-facing representation.

### Satisfaction 78-82 — a paid dream date joins scripted intimacy to vocational disclosure

Ruka apologizes to Kazuya for her conduct during Mami's visit and explains that the overlap between ex-girlfriend and coworker provoked her. Her apology repairs the immediate interaction without revising the provisional status claim.

Kazuya notices that Chizuru has moved out of the beginner class after a year, with a 1,000-yen higher rate. He books May 20 and chooses the school-uniform option to approximate the high-school romance he feels he missed. At Tokyo Dome City, Chizuru treats the request as a legitimate service plan. She takes his offered hand, shares a churro, and repeatedly tells him not to hold back because she will accompany him fully for the day.

Sumi sees them by chance in matching uniforms, becomes flustered, and leaves before Kazuya can explain. In a photo booth, Chizuru selects the lovers mode and leads the required hand-heart, shoulder hold, embrace, and close-contact poses. Kazuya experiences the contact intensely but also identifies it as professional girlfriend work. When other girls in the same uniforms approach, Chizuru moves them away to protect her identity.

The date continues through rides and a Ferris-wheel rest. Chizuru says knowledge of uniforms and places can also become experience for acting. When Kazuya shifts seats to improve her view, the cabin jolts and he falls against her, touching her breast accidentally. She strikes him reflexively and immediately marks the bodily boundary; he accepts fault. The accident supplies no consent or romantic-status evidence.

Chizuru later explains that rental work can be enjoyable and that customers' reports of finally getting a girlfriend make even a relation built through a lie feel useful. She links the work to acting as a way of making someone happy and says she takes pride in doing work she chose. She also tells Kazuya that someone who saw her earlier performance contacted her and that she has obtained another stage opportunity. Kazuya responds with unqualified excitement and promises to attend and keep supporting her. He grips her hand hard enough that she says it hurts, then releases pressure. They buy the photo-booth print, and he leaves resolving to find a practical way to help her pursue the acting dream. The disclosure is genuine information inside a paid date; neither feature cancels the other.

### Satisfaction 83-85 — family access, rivalry, and a crisis message converge

Nagomi calls about Kazuya's June 1 family birthday gathering and proposes celebrating Chizuru's missed April birthday at the same time. Kazuya believes he cannot ask a rental provider to join a private family event and fears both exposure and the possibility that acting work will end Chizuru's rental availability. Chizuru overhears from the neighboring balcony. Because she had promised to visit the family, she volunteers to attend after a hospital appointment and says she remains his girlfriend until she quits the service. She also gives him a direct birthday greeting.

Ruka uses a video call to wish Kazuya happy birthday and gains separate real-time access to him. The next evening, Chizuru arrives with a gift and takes Kazuya's hand on the way to the family home. He discloses that Ruka is also expected. Ruka, who has followed them, appears and insists that she too will attend. At the Kinoshita home, Nagomi welcomes Chizuru into a joint celebration and reads her gifts and family-oriented remarks as ideal girlfriend conduct. Ruka presents herself as Kazuya's friend while competing through service, domestic performance, and bids for family approval.

When Chizuru is not yet present, Kazuya cannot use an established private-number route and struggles over contact through the university LINE connection. During the party Ruka initiates a kiss. Kazuya recoils and covers her mouth; Nagomi sees the gesture without the full context and scolds him. Ruka then makes her competition explicit, saying that the friendship description need not remain the truth and publicly presenting herself as the person who likes Kazuya most. The kiss and declaration remain unilateral; they do not convert the trial into mutual agreement.

Chizuru contacts Kazuya directly through LINE and asks to speak. She is at the hospital with Sayuri and identifies herself across the family boundary as Chizuru, also invoking the Mizuhara presentation. The volume closes on the hospitalization and the direct crisis contact. The new route has therefore moved from an unsolicited add to a chosen non-booking communication, while family and professional identities remain entangled.
