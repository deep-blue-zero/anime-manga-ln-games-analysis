## Sequential account

### Satisfaction 68-69 — effortful initiative completes the birthday plan

Sumi performs in a hero-show costume and works through visible shyness as part of her effort to improve at acting and customer interaction. She writes that a present is less about the object than the feeling conveyed, then actively asks Kazuya's own birthday. He answers June 1; she records a June 1, 2018 birthday event on her phone.

Kazuya selects a light, inexpensive gift: premium pickled plums costing 2,500 yen. He connects the choice to Chizuru's fatigue from acting work and wants to offer something useful without creating a heavy obligation. He has spent 17,000 yen on the Sumi booking in addition to the gift.

Kazuya leaves the gift and card outside Chizuru's door. A postscript says that he and Ruka still have not had sex, turning the private overnight consequence into a disclosure and reassurance bid. Chizuru understands the gift's fatigue-related purpose, eats it, smiles, and later thanks him. She says it was very good and answers the postscript by saying she believes him while also calling the explanation noisy.

### Satisfaction 70-73 — a social collision turns identity protection into care

Kazuya's university friends bring him to a group drinking event. Chizuru arrives in her campus presentation with glasses and braids; neither knew the other would attend. Their friends do not know their private connection, so the two improvise a partial account based on the sea-trip rescue while withholding the rental and neighbor facts.

Questions about Chizuru's boyfriend, the visible resemblance between “Ichinose” and “Mizuhara,” and drinking-game scrutiny raise the risk that the divided presentations will collapse. Kazuya deliberately accepts repeated losses and drinks to keep attention away from Chizuru. She notices the protective pattern.

When the group moves on, Kazuya is too intoxicated to continue. Chizuru stays, supports him home, uses his key, and remains while he is incapacitated. She challenges the unnecessary overdrinking. He says he thought he had to protect Ichinose even if it killed him, then vomits. Chizuru holds and tends him in the bathroom, tells him he does not need to act tough, and continues care despite calling him troublesome. The social-protection choice produces a bodily cost and unpaid private care rather than public exposure.

### Satisfaction 74 — the aftermath opens a direct contact channel

Kazuya wakes with a severe hangover and fragmentary memories of Chizuru's care. A group LINE chat created from the gathering exposes Ichinose's profile to him, and he adds her directly without asking. Chizuru criticizes the act, but the campus identity now has a contact path outside booking procedure.

Kazuya cannot initially distinguish memory from dream. Chizuru tells him not to overread the incident and repeats that he should not drink so recklessly. She later leaves a hangover remedy outside his door. The object confirms that her care was real and extends it beyond the immediate crisis while preserving interpretive distance.

### Satisfaction 75-76 — Ruka's claim gives Mami a new investigative route

Mami appears as a customer at Kazuya and Ruka's karaoke workplace. Ruka learns that Mami is the former girlfriend whose rejection helped lead Kazuya to rental dating. She interrupts Kazuya's attempts to keep the relation vague and tells Mami that she is Kazuya's current girlfriend.

Kazuya tries to correct the status to a provisional trial. Ruka asserts present priority, claims that she and Kazuya have already had sex while he immediately denies it, and challenges Mami for visiting an ex's workplace. She repeatedly says she loves him. Mami tests the coherence of the account, noting Kazuya's other women and Ruka's knowledge of rental girlfriends. Ruka cries after Mami leaves but keeps declaring that she is his girlfriend and loves him.

On the train afterward, Mami reviews the contradictions: Kibe told her about the karaoke job, Ruka knew about rental girlfriends, and the claimed relation does not fit Kazuya's conduct. She then sees Chizuru in full rental-date presentation with a male client. Chizuru recognizes her. The volume ends with prior workplace information, Ruka's claim, and direct visual evidence of Chizuru's service work converging in one observer.
