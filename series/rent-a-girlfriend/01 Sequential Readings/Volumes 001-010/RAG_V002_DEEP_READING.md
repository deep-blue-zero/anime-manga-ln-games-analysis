## Volume thesis

Volume 2 turns accumulated audiences into a causal system. The Izu trip is accidental for Kazuya and Chizuru, but once their university and rental presentations enter the same place, every attempted correction must account for friends, Mami, Kibe's moral judgment, Nagomi's discharge, and the paid schedule. Kazuya does publicly announce a breakup. He still does not disclose the underlying rental truth, so the act reduces one future obligation while preserving the deception that produced it.

Mami moves from ambiguous re-entry to observable intervention. She privately states an intention to split the pair, probes Kazuya's account, adopts Chizuru's rental identity before the group, engineers a kiss, and arranges a private meeting. Those acts support strategic destabilization. They do not settle whether her deeper motive is attachment, jealousy, status repair, control, or a mixture.

Chizuru continues to separate labor from unlimited personal access. She invoices the earlier extension, denies that her consoling words carried hidden depth, and repeatedly names the role as work. Yet she also chooses bounded conduct that the fee alone does not predict: she goes on the ferry after Kibe's appeal, recognizes something admirable in Kazuya while immediately returning it to the job frame, and remains exposed to the group's false account. Her brief darkened response to the possibility of Kazuya reconciling with Mami is represented affect, not an authorized romantic confession.

The final rescue attempt breaks the volume's ordinary price and audience logic. Kazuya has just said he will stop renting Chizuru and has a private meeting with Mami waiting. When Chizuru falls overboard, he dives after her without a contract, plan, or assured outcome. This is a high-cost action inconsistent with his claim that the rental relation has no personal claim on him. It establishes action under emergency, not mature romance, successful rescue, or reciprocation; all three remain outside the V002 boundary.

## Causal and state deltas

### Kazuya

Kazuya moves from generalized maintenance to attempted closure. He pays the prior extension, states a post-trip breakup plan, refuses the party-game kiss, publicly announces that he and Chizuru will separate, and forms a plan to tell Kibe the underlying truth. These are meaningful actions, but disclosure remains incomplete when the emergency interrupts the sequence.

His relation to Mami still produces projection. The kiss and first-date memory revive his wish to confess. Yet his refusal of the Pocky kiss shows that responsiveness is not pure compliance. At the volume boundary, the strongest discriminating action is the dive: with the rental formally ending and Mami waiting, he accepts immediate physical risk for Chizuru without an audience contract or promise of return.

### Chizuru

Chizuru's professional accountability becomes more explicit through the invoice and no-special-treatment rule. She also repeatedly reasserts interpretive boundaries after affectively suggestive moments. She tells Kazuya that her consolation was work, responds to his reunion question without disclosing a private motive, and names her favorable trip assessment as part of the job.

The Izu collision nevertheless expands her personal exposure. Her campus friends, Kazuya's friends, Mami, Kibe, and the public Mizuhara identity now coexist in one place. Kibe's appeal changes her action even though he acts from a false belief. Her final state is physically imperiled and unconscious; the volume supplies no represented knowledge of Kazuya's dive.

### Kazuya and Chizuru

The pair gain more direct negotiation about ending the relation and more evidence of chosen conduct under cost. They also suffer greater audience entanglement. The public breakup claim reduces one obligation in principle while Kibe's reaction immediately creates another. No private romance is mutually acknowledged. The closing rescue attempt changes the action evidence available to the reader, but not yet Chizuru's knowledge or the couple's declared status.

### Mami

Mami's initiative is no longer merely ambiguous approach. She names an external goal and repeatedly acts toward it. Her performance is audience-sensitive: private probing with Kazuya, identity appropriation before the group, a public-friendly surface, a deliberately staged kiss, and a scheduled private meeting. The goal of destabilization is supported. The emotional or strategic reason for that goal remains underdetermined.

### Kibe

Kibe becomes a major audience agent. Because he believes the couple claim, he interprets Kazuya's partial correction as betrayal, fights him, supplies a character defense, and purchases or transfers the ferry opportunity. His care for Kazuya and Chizuru is real in conduct while his premises are false.

## Information, transaction, and agency findings

The central information hazard is identity collision. Chizuru's campus presentation initially protects her, but Mami can weaponize the rental name because she knows the public girlfriend claim and suspects inconsistency. Kazuya's friends continue to believe the couple is real. Kibe's resulting intervention shows that a deceived audience is not passive: false belief can generate physical conflict, moral pressure, and new shared time.

The transaction remains concrete. Chizuru collects 17,000 yen and denies special treatment; Nagomi's discharge defines a final weekly booking; Kazuya says he will stop renting her. Against that boundary, the dive is analytically distinct. It is not compensated, scheduled, requested, or socially staged. That distinction supports personal stakes on Kazuya's side without settling their emotional label.

Agency remains distributed. Mami deliberately destabilizes. Kazuya conceals, refuses one kiss, makes a partial public correction, plans fuller truth, and dives. Chizuru preserves her identity, accepts one friend-mediated ferry ride, and repeatedly supplies boundaries. Kibe acts forcefully from incomplete information. The accident is unchosen; Kazuya's response to it is chosen.

## Manga form and written speech

The Izu setting repeatedly composes identities in the same panel while keeping knowledge uneven. Chizuru's changed styling permits the reader to see continuity that the male friends miss. Mami's doorway declaration then turns a name into a weapon: the spoken “Chizuru Mizuhara” is true of the absent role but false of the speaker.

Water changes function across the volume. The pool permits Mami to stage exposure and a kiss inside a playful social event. The sea removes play, audience permission, and transactional structure. The formal recurrence therefore changes stakes rather than repeating one romantic image.

Kazuya's dense interior fantasies remain unreliable as forecasts of action. He searches for reunion advice and imagines confessing to Mami, but refuses the Pocky kiss and later dives for Chizuru. Chizuru receives far less interior text. Her facial shadows, pauses, and private “idiot” response must remain represented affect with plural explanations.

Kibe's morning-glory account embeds a model of Kazuya inside friendship memory. Its emotional force matters because it moves Chizuru to take the ferry. Its metaphorical form also warns against treating the speech as neutral omniscient characterization.

## Progress, recurrence, and counterreadings

The volume contains real progress in action and incomplete progress in truth. Kazuya performs a public breakup announcement and refuses a second game-induced kiss with Mami. He still leaves the rental fact undisclosed. The result is a reconfigured lie: friends can believe a real couple is separating while remaining wrong about the relationship that existed.

The repeated ending mechanism persists with added consequence. “After the trip” becomes a breakup announcement; that announcement triggers Kibe's violence and tickets; the “final” ferry ride leads to the emergency. Each closure attempt retains a new audience action or physical cost.

Four counterreadings remain live:

1. **Mami's actions prove that she wants Kazuya back.** Her initiative and kiss support renewed personal stakes. Her explicit operational goal is separation, however, and her deeper desired endpoint remains undisclosed.
2. **Chizuru's expressions prove jealousy.** The reunion question, observed kiss, favorable appraisal, and “idiot” response make jealousy possible. They remain compatible with irritation, role strain, concern, or mixed affect.
3. **Kazuya's dive proves mature love.** The dive is costly personal action and strong counterevidence to indifference. It occurs in an acute emergency before outcome or reflection; it cannot establish a mature relational capacity by itself.
4. **The public breakup solves the deception.** It changes the group's expected future, but the rental truth remains hidden and continues to shape Kibe's judgment.
