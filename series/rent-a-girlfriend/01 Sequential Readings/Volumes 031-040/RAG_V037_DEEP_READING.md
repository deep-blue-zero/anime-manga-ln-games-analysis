V037 does not show the May 17 outing. It follows both principals preparing in parallel. Chizuru distinguishes an unpriced, ordinary occasion for knowing one another from the many date performances she has already done as a rental girlfriend. She worries that her feelings cannot be switched on and that Kazuya expects an answer. ## Sequential account

### Chizuru's concern and the shared-house game

Mini approaches Chizuru after work and excitedly asks about the May 17 agreement. Chizuru resists Mini's implication that the date mechanically proves love: feelings do not instantly change, and she fears failing to answer Kazuya's earnest expectation. She then goes to a batting cage, using an ordinary physical routine while thinking about his request. Kazuya separately worries about career timing after speaking with Kibe and wants his remaining house time to matter. In a three-person `Bounce Off` game at home, Chizuru learns the rules, competes, adjusts tactics, and plays again; Kazuya sees a precise and playful side not visible in paid dates. Her private thought also affirms that moving apart is sensible: she is still a rental girlfriend with customers, and their current closeness is unusually difficult to maintain without an answer. A promise to make red-bean porridge repairs the V036 spill in the household register. Neither competition nor domestic gratitude is a romantic verdict.

### Ordinary preferences against the fantasy of a perfect date

Kazuya searches for an ideal date and nearly traps himself in a one-sided plan. He sends a message asking whether there is anywhere she wants to go or food she wants. Chizuru says directly that she wants a **normal date**—time for the two to know one another and enjoy themselves, rather than a special production for her. Kazuya treats that as important evidence while still worrying how to deliver. Kuri later supplies an intimacy theory about numbered dates, leading Kazuya into hotel and sexual fantasies and a condom purchase. Kazuya himself notes that this is their first actual non-rental date. The purchase is real; the imagined sexual events and Kuri's theory supply no Chizuru consent or plan. Chizuru hears other actors' comments about dating and reflects privately, but no spoken consent follows.

Chizuru thinks over the food question while shopping and independently texts that she might like hotpot. Kazuya receives the specific answer with disproportionate relief and then researches hotpot restaurants and menus down to spice levels. Chizuru visits Sayuri's grave, tidies it, and in her private address says she has decided to go on the date, recalling Sayuri's advice that someone who supports Kazuya is valuable. This is strong evidence of deliberate participation, yet she says she will find the answer herself. Mini and Kazuya then go to a hotpot restaurant, with Mini playing a deliberately exaggerated trial-date role. They discuss taste, options, and what an ordinary conversation can be. Mini cautions that interrogation, overplanning, or concealing his own preferences will not solve the inquiry; she can advise him but cannot speak for Chizuru.

### The itinerary and Chizuru's shared departure

Kazuya builds a provisional May 17 schedule: morning meeting, travel, Odaiba/Joypolis activities, lunch, hotpot in the evening, and a walk to talk. He chooses an indoor activity as a rain hedge but the forecast still worries him. He is tempted to treat the date as a designed proof rather than reciprocal time. At home Chizuru asks about the departure plan, and he admits he had left the rendezvous undecided. She says they can simply go **together from the same house**; meeting elsewhere would be odd when they already live together. Her question also covers timing. He says around 9 a.m.; she accepts despite the early hour and tells him she is looking forward to it. This is a specific bilateral agreement on departure, more secure than his private itinerary. He marks 9 a.m. from home in his notes and recognizes that Chizuru is giving him time in addition to the agreed outing. The narration closes before the date begins.
