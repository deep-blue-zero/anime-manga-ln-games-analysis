### Silent sighting, family call, and a voluntary public role

She wonders what their apparent connection means, including her own position, but makes no approach or report. Mini recruits Kazuya to help sell her work at a cosplay event. The public gaze, Mini's enthusiasm, and Kazuya's sexualized internal reactions are not equivalent to Chizuru's private consent beyond this local role. Her answer recognizes his personal evaluation, not a love confession or a change in professional status.

### A fixed move day forces a request he can say aloud

On May 2, Kazuya reviews a rental contract showing May 18 as his move-in day, making the house's remaining time roughly two weeks. This supersedes his V035 tentative May 18 or 19 estimate and the earlier May 13 plan as a present scheduling fact, but it is still a future move, not an accomplished exit. He decides to ask Chizuru on a private date before moving. Drafted messages, rehearsal, and imagined refusal are Kazuya's preparation and fear. Chizuru independently texts that she wants to speak that night, saying she will be late because of work. At about 23:45, she returns and asks his availability. He initially mistakes the direction of the conversation; she names work commitments on the nearby weekend. He then speaks over his fear and directly asks to invite **her** on a date, specifying “not rental.”

Kazuya explains he wants her to see him beyond the guest/housemate role while she investigates her feeling, rather than spending their remaining time only waiting for a verdict. Chizuru asks about the particulars and says May 17 is open, the day before his move. He accepts. She says she too had thought she needed to do something to find an answer. Her proposal of the actual day and later calendar entry labeled `デート` make this her deliberate, reciprocal scheduling, though no specific venue or plan is yet agreed. On a train she hesitates over the word, then enters it. Kazuya tells Mini, who celebrates and urges him to plan an ideal outing. Her optimism is advice and conjecture; she cannot grade Chizuru's feelings. The agreed date is unpaid and future.
