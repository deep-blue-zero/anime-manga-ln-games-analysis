The volume still precedes the agreed May 17 outing. Kazuya tries to prepare his clothes, travel route and venue with a score-like fear of failure. Chizuru sees his Shibuya shopping by chance, follows unseen for a time, and independently faces the similar problem of choosing clothes for him. She does not resolve her feelings merely by choosing an outfit. Kazuya buys shoes but no jacket; on his own route check to Odaiba, he unexpectedly meets Chizuru, who says she has come to check for herself and suggests going in. Their joint Joypolis visit and subsequent lunch are actual events, but she and he repeatedly distinguish them from the upcoming date. Chizuru pushes against a planned “escort” that erases his own preferences. Her invitation to consider the date from her side is a meaningful test of her ordinary-date standard; it is not a final relationship answer.

## Sequential account

### Parallel preparation and Mami's interiority

After agreeing to 9 a.m., Chizuru looks at the weather and her own wording, worrying that saying she looked forward to the date may have raised expectations while admitting to herself that she does look forward. Kazuya, anxious about how to appear, goes to Shibuya to buy clothes and footwear. In a separate salon sequence Mami has spare time before an appointment, recalls Kazuya's direct invitation and privately asks whether she has ever really been in love. The employee's conversation offers general opinion, not reliable knowledge of Kazuya or Chizuru. Mami says nothing to the principals. Kazuya tries shoes, receives advice from a salesperson and purchases a pair; he spends a considerable amount and then postpones choosing clothes. Chizuru happens to spot him, follows at a distance without his knowledge, and recognizes he is making an effort for the date. She later shops for herself, tests contrasting outfits and asks why she wants to be thought cute by him. She does not buy a date outfit on page. Her deliberation is distinct from the imagined versions of her that Kazuya uses to simulate the future date.

### The route check becomes a real, unplanned visit together

Kazuya goes to Tokyo Teleport and checks the walk to Joypolis, including the route under cover if rain arrives. Some images of Chizuru on that route are his anticipated date and not present-tense events. At Joypolis, however, Chizuru actually appears and speaks to him. She says she has come to inspect for herself and, despite his embarrassment that his rehearsal has been discovered, invites him inside. The pair explore multiple floors. They briefly play a retro game, walk through a commercial/arcade area and discuss its attractions. Chizuru notes the venue can be crowded and that an anxious fixed schedule leaves too little room to talk. Their interaction is a real preview, but neither treats it as fulfilling the scheduled May 17 date.

Kazuya's responses expose the danger in his “escort” ideal: he keeps trying to guess which path, stop and pace will make her happy. Chizuru says a couple can coordinate walking pace, notice each other's needs, and simply be together. She explicitly distinguishes attention to another person from smothering their own choices or treating every moment as a test. She wants him to tell her what he finds interesting too. They leave the arcade area and choose lunch at the mall instead of forcing every part of his draft plan. Mami sees them from a cafe with another person and silently watches; the principals do not notice her.

### Lunch, mutual decision, and competing observers

At lunch Chizuru jokes with Kazuya's overstated planning anxiety, then gives serious advice. Their order is made together. She asks him not to convert her preferences into a pressure-filled attempt to optimize an “escort”; he should rest, choose and speak as himself. She says conversations about the mundane can reveal points of common feeling and difference. Kazuya is startled by the idea that shared amusement and ordinary adjustment count, because he has been focused on delivering a successful event. Chizuru is not claiming that they are already a couple or pronouncing love. When the bill arrives, she states that the person who has been invited can pay for her own food and offers a clear choice: if he pays, she can buy the later ice cream. The line preserves reciprocity within a non-rental outing, although this lunch remains their rehearsal-day meal.

The narrative then shifts to Takashiro Miho in Chizuru's acting sphere. Miho identifies her own professional name, describes acting as a harsh career stage where pleasant presentation and networking can feel hollow, and says she wants to stop for the day. Her severe thoughts belong to her viewpoint. A brief scene with Chizuru makes Miho visibly unsettled; no evidence yet warrants assigning Miho's attitude to Chizuru or treating it as a settled action against her. Kazuya separately resolves not to intrude on Chizuru's schedule and senses that asking for the date may have imposed a burden. The future date, inquiry and May 18 move remain open.
