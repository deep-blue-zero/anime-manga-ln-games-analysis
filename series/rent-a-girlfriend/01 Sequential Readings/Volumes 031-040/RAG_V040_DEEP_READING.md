## Thesis: a real date begins while the social debts remain real

V040 converts the carefully discussed May 17 outing into an actual unpaid, self-chosen date. The arrival of heavy rain alters Kazuya's planned route, but the pair still leave the house at the agreed time, board a crowded train, and reach Joypolis. Chizuru is not a passive object of his perfect-date plan: before the outing she initiates a naming question and invites shared attention to Sayuri's film; on the date she raises Ruka's well-being and eventually asks Kazuya to share her umbrella. The final request is a small, direct intimacy choice, not an answer to her romantic inquiry. Kazuya's effort is earnest and increasingly concrete, but nervous self-monitoring, sexualized interior reactions, and the hidden Ruka/cohabitation facts constrain any clean progress story.

The title's “secret” belongs especially to the Ruka encounter. Kazuya obtains a credit card for date expenses while Ruka accompanies him, and the new-home key exposes the approaching move. He tells her about the move but not that he has been living with Chizuru or is about to date her. Ruka notices his apparent good mood, helps him choose a fish keyholder, and remains affectionate; her knowledge remains incomplete. Chizuru's brief question about Ruka on the date acknowledges that this third-party obligation is still present. No one in V040 completes a breakup, a new couple agreement, a family correction, or the May 18 move.

## Sequential account

### Names, disclosure, and the pending move

In shared-house conversation Chizuru calls Kazuya by his given name. He realizes that he still calls her “Mizuhara,” the rental alias, and struggles to address her as “Ichinose.” His awkward assertion that the person he likes is Ichinose clarifies the referent of his earlier confession, but he cannot manufacture her answer from the change in name. Chizuru participates in the question rather than granting an official relationship label (RAG-E-V040-001).

Ruka then accompanies Kazuya while he obtains a credit card. She remarks on his improved mood and seeks ordinary closeness. He keeps the Chizuru date and cohabitation from her. When his new apartment key appears, he tells her that he moves next week; she presses to be included in significant matters, and the exchange does not provide the missing residence history. They select a fish-shaped keyholder for that key. An accidental stumble places them in an embarrassing bodily configuration in the shop. Their visible embarrassment and the public accident cannot be read as negotiated sexual escalation (RAG-E-V040-002 through -004). The key and keyholder later accompany Kazuya on the date, connecting logistics and unresolved disclosure.

### An inherited film and the final preparation

At home Chizuru presents a film featuring Sayuri and invites Kazuya to watch with her. She discusses her grandmother as an acting ideal and a person she wishes she could still speak with. This is present speech about a deceased figure and a professional/family goal, not new direct Sayuri conduct. Kazuya's wish to support Chizuru meets a goal that she names for herself (RAG-E-V040-005). The next chapters move through the two-day and one-day countdown. Kazuya prepares clothing, shoes, haircut, money and route; his anxiety is not a substitute for her evaluation. She confirms the nine o'clock departure, says she will make an effort too, privately checks the Joypolis plan, and acknowledges that she must decide. On May 16 he also confronts the fact that Ruka has not been honestly informed. Mini's date-related encouragement remains advice, not authority over either person's answer (RAG-E-V040-006, -007).

### The May 17 date in rain

Heavy rain greets May 17. Kazuya considers the disruption to the planned outdoor route, but both appear at the house's front gate and proceed with separate umbrellas. He manages to tell Chizuru that she looks cute. Her response turns promptly to Ruka: she asks whether Ruka is all right and says she is concerned, particularly after the two have reached this situation. Kazuya answers that he thinks Ruka can be all right because he told her he liked Chizuru, yet this does not disclose the cohabitation or settle Ruka's claimed status. The scene shows Chizuru's ethical awareness, not an informed Ruka endorsement (RAG-E-V040-008, -009).

The crowded train forces the pair close. Kazuya braces himself against the wall and tries to shield Chizuru, then accidentally presses into her chest amid passenger movement. He apologizes; she acknowledges the crowd and remains composed, but the contact is accidental and uncomfortable, not a chosen intimate act. At Tokyo Teleport, the rain keeps them under cover. Kazuya fumbles a planned escort gesture, and Chizuru makes her own movement toward Joypolis. He asks whether the train incident disturbed her, and she responds that she is aware she is beside him as a woman rather than performing a rental-girlfriend role. The spoken distinction advances the encounter's frame while leaving her eventual conclusion open. Before they reach the venue, Chizuru asks him to share her piano-key umbrella. Kazuya's final thought wonders whether he could have simply asked. The volume cuts off at their arrival before the date's activities or outcome (RAG-E-V040-010 through -012).
