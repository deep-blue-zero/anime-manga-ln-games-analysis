## Sequential account

### An assigned 100 becomes a proposal example, then a corrected inference

The three players finish the `ito` round. Mini's 74 and Kazuya's 56 sit beside Chizuru's 100, and the group tries to express the latter as an extravagant public marriage proposal with helicopter, fireworks, and a diamond ring. Chizuru states explicitly that she does **not** mean she wants that proposal: from a person she loves, even something simple would do. Mini tries to read the remark as a clue about Kazuya; Chizuru keeps it inside the game's ideal-example frame. Kazuya's 56 is his comparison with an ordinary or merely acceptable person, colored by his low self-regard, not a Chizuru rating. All cards are finally revealed. During the exchange Chizuru asks when he expects to move. His tentative answer is around the 18th or 19th of the following month, apparently shifting the earlier May 13 plan; the exact day is **not fixed**. He keeps replaying her conditional words after the game without acquiring their referent.

### Chizuru asks Kibe, and the question returns to her

On April 27, after Kazuya encounters Kibe at university, Chizuru independently asks Kibe for time. At the cafe she asks what Kazuya is like from his viewpoint, saying she needs to know more about him. Kibe's account mixes teasing familiarity with a specific claim that Kazuya thinks of her more than anyone. It is testimony from a loyal friend, not omniscient access. Kibe asks whether Chizuru likes Kazuya. She pauses and begins a response, but Kibe withdraws the demand, saying that seeing her expression is enough; the reader sees her blush but does not receive her answer. The inquiry has a new source and an observer of her reaction, yet the withheld classification remains withheld.

### Menstrual pain becomes a bounded household-care episode

Back at the house, Chizuru challenges Kazuya for being late and then says she was still in bed. He first assumes a cold or illness; she names menstruation and explains that the first two days can be heavy for her. She is uncomfortable and fatigued, not diagnosed with a separate fever. Kazuya searches for useful care, purchases supplies, and cooks a rice-and-vegetable dish. Mini visits briefly, learns the situation, and punctures his anxious secrecy; she is not the woman who grants access to Chizuru's room. Chizuru herself says he may come in, and he brings the meal. She eats, calls it good, and says she may have imposed on him. He tells her she can ask for help whenever something is difficult. She asks for one childhood snack associated with Sayuri and eventually rests. Kazuya's earnest acts coexist with intrusive internet stereotypes and a tendency to freeze rather than ask what she wants. His tray, treatment concern, and remaining near the threshold are local care, not an invitation to bodily intimacy. When he assumes she has fallen asleep and considers leaving, Chizuru quietly registers his presence; later she calls herself foolish as she sits near the doorway. The book supplies discomfort, gratitude, and ambivalence, but no explicit romantic answer.

### Nagomi buys professional time and offers a fraught apology

The next sequence returns Chizuru to rental work. She sees a booking at Shinjuku Station's southeast exit, then discovers that Nagomi is the client. Nagomi calls it a date and asks Chizuru to accompany her; the public outing includes clothes and a game arcade. Chizuru names fashion and customer service as features of the profession, while Nagomi pays, enjoys their time, and remarks on how well Chizuru works. Nagomi says she sought a chance to apologize: the first disclosure that Chizuru was a rental girlfriend had led her to look down on the profession and to impose family hopes. She says Sayuri's account of Chizuru and Kazuya at the hospital helped her see Chizuru differently. Her admission and request for forgiveness are direct; the bridal image she still voices is her own aspiration, not Chizuru's promise. Chizuru is visibly moved and uneasy. When the women enter the arcade, Sumi notices them from a separate position; no exchange among Sumi, Chizuru, and Nagomi occurs in V035. Nagomi says she hopes to witness Chizuru as a bride alongside Kazuya. Chizuru returns to the house conscious that she has not corrected the false dating premise. She tells Kazuya Nagomi rented her, and the volume cuts. The family misunderstanding, Chizuru's professional role, and a potential real relationship therefore remain distinct.
