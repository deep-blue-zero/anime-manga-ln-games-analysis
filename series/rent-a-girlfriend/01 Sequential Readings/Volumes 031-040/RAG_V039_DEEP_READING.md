## Sequential account

### A preview with shared risk and play

Chizuru suggests entering Joypolis despite their earlier reconnaissance, and the two split the one-day ticket cost. She succeeds at a claw game and proposes a photo booth, physically coaxing Kazuya into a closer pose. The photograph is an unpaid memento of this particular day, not a couple-status certificate. As they traverse the floors she selects Wild Wing. Kazuya worries that waiting gives him too little to say, and his attempt to compliment her exposes how much he is monitoring the apparent quality of his performance. The ride scares him and he reflexively draws Chizuru close. She is surprised, then says she enjoyed it and wants to continue. His protective motion is observable; its meaning to her is not fully narrated.

Chizuru next selects the romance category of the Fortune Forest compatibility activity after Kazuya reaches for the safer friendship category. She shields her input from his view. The printed results present 78% friendship and 90% romance compatibility. These numbers are a playful device that prompts them to consider their relationship; neither number is a narrator-certified measure of feelings. Chizuru says it is only a fortune, yet keeps the sheet. A horror attraction and another ride bring mutual fright, laughter and an unimpressive performance rather than the flawlessly executed date Kazuya had imagined. At a break she asks whether Kazuya has a girlfriend. When he answers no, they discuss loneliness and the need for each person to carry out their own decision. Chizuru compares it with an actor's work: support matters, but the actor must act. She recalls his earlier help and explicitly credits him as a supporter. Her gratitude does not amount to a request that he decide her inquiry for her.

### Mami's encounter and a clarified attachment

At the arcade the pair unexpectedly cross Mami with Tōru Hakuba. Hakuba and Chizuru step away separately, leaving Mami and Kazuya to speak. Mami asks about Chizuru and whether the two are dating. Kazuya says they will go on a date but cannot say that they are already a couple. He asks about Hakuba, and Mami says Hakuba is not her boyfriend. Neither her presence with him nor Kazuya's discomfort proves a current romance between them. Her subsequent private thoughts register the sighting and Kazuya's continued orientation toward Chizuru; they do not disclose a concrete plan to intervene.

After Mami leaves, Chizuru observes that Kazuya seems affected and asks directly whether he still likes Mami. His explanation distinguishes an old attachment and residual wish to please his ex from his present feeling. He also takes seriously her earlier point that another person cannot do the decisive work for him. He says plainly that he likes Chizuru, clarifying that she herself is the object of the statement. Chizuru is surprised but indicates she had grasped his feelings. The direct words are a change in what is openly shared, though she gives no reciprocal declaration or final decision. The scene is still within the preview day, not the scheduled May 17 outing.
