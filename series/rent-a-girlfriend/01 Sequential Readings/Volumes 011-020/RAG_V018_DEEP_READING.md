## Sequential account

### Satisfaction 149 — an unfinished film reaches Sayuri through bedside projection

Chizuru sits beside the unresponsive Sayuri and apologizes that the film is not ready and that she could not show her completed work in time. Kazuya arrives with a laptop and projector. He says that Sayuri wants to see Chizuru as an actress and converts the hospital wall into a screen. The copy is unfinished, but the opening footage plays at Sayuri's bedside. Kazuya's V017 LINE-and-running action therefore has a direct technical and delivery consequence, supporting RAG-PRED-065 without establishing a completed edit or public screening.

The projected footage places Chizuru's performed dream before the family member for whom the project was organized. Sayuri appears to watch during a brief return of responsiveness. The scene joins material production labor to care while preserving the difference between access to footage and completion of the promised film.

### Satisfaction 150 — the doctrine of protective lies meets a demand for self-directed speech

Chizuru recalls Sayuri explaining that acting is a profession of lies and that people may use many lies to protect one truth. The memory supplies a family origin for Chizuru's comfort-first reasoning; it does not make every lie benevolent or determine Sayuri's informed preference in the present.

Kazuya tells Chizuru that a funeral will be too late for words she still needs to say. He describes himself as only a neighbor and outsider, yet urges her to enter as herself rather than remain inside the professional `Mizuhara` role. Chizuru says she no longer knows what to do. Sayuri then regains consciousness enough to reach toward and touch Chizuru's face.

### Satisfaction 151 — attempted factual disclosure yields relational acceptance rather than a clean correction

Chizuru begins the disclosure Kazuya pressed for: she says that she and Kazuya are not dating and apologizes for lying. Sayuri does not demand an exhaustive factual account. She says, in substance, that she both wants and does not want to know and entrusts the answer to the granddaughter she loves. The exchange supports RAG-PRED-066 because a disclosure attempt and explicit audience response occur. It does not establish that Sayuri learns every rental detail or reaches a final factual belief about the pair.

Sayuri recalls how happily Chizuru spoke about Kazuya, thanks her for the wonderful film, and asks that Kazuya be thanked as well. She calls Chizuru her treasure and says she loves her. Chizuru embraces her and answers that she loves her too. Sayuri subsequently dies, as confirmed by the wake and funeral. The final exchange resolves mutual family love and reception of the projected footage while leaving the relationship deception factually incomplete.

### Satisfaction 152 — Sumi rejects the premise that Chizuru can or should endure alone

Kazuya tells Sumi that Chizuru is strong and may be able to survive the loss alone. Sumi cries and calls that appraisal foolish. She says that no woman, and no person, can live alone and pushes Kazuya to think about what he can do. Her response corrects his equation of controlled presentation with absent need.

Outside Sayuri's room, Chizuru says she stayed until morning and must handle funeral preparations. Kazuya asks whether she is all right and offers help while again calling himself an ordinary neighbor. She smiles, says she is fine, and leaves. He reads the continuing polished `Mizuhara` performance as beauty and strength, but the chapter has already supplied Sumi's counter-reading. RAG-PRED-068 is supported as attempted support and an explicit boundary response; Chizuru does not yet accept direct help.

### Satisfaction 153 — the funeral intensifies support pressure without correcting public status

Nagomi urges Kazuya to attend Sayuri's funeral after Chizuru shares the details. At the service Chizuru performs formal hospitality with controlled composure. Nagomi says that Chizuru cried through the night and tells Kazuya that there are things only a lover can do. Because he knows the public premise is false, the instruction increases his role conflict rather than authorizing lover access.

Kazuya notices that Chizuru has not shown him tears and again asks what action is available to him. He decides to rely on Sumi for advice. The funeral confirms Sayuri's death and creates a new support-planning step without romantic reclassification.

### Satisfaction 154 — Sumi turns grief recognition into a practical support heuristic

Kazuya meets Sumi during a practice booking and asks whether Chizuru seems all right. Sumi cries openly when he describes Sayuri's death and Chizuru's unchanged smile. Kazuya says that Chizuru appears to stand unsupported after losing her only close relative, then repeats that a person cannot live alone.

Sumi ends the formal consultation, takes Kazuya to Shinjuku Station, buys tickets toward Shonan, and communicates a plan: the sea helps with worries. Her action makes support instructional rather than merely sympathetic. She treats the remainder of the excursion as friendship rather than paid provider time.

### Satisfaction 155-156 — the excursion models relief, direct encouragement, and bounded companionship

On the train and at Enoshima, Sumi uses notes, gestures, touch limited to leading movement, sightseeing, food, shouting, and beach play to create a sequence of low-demand enjoyment. She openly weeps, laughs, and makes Kazuya participate instead of asking him to solve grief through argument. The excursion does not erase his concern; it gives him an experiential model for helping someone temporarily step outside it.

Sumi writes her advice in the sand. She frames smiling, play, and straightforward encouragement as meaningful to a suffering person and indicates that she herself would be glad to receive such care. Kazuya understands the day as a lesson for Chizuru's situation, thanks Sumi, and returns to Tokyo with a plan. Sumi's romantic feeling remains unspoken to him, and the friend framing does not create reciprocal status.

### Satisfaction 157 — advice becomes a paid, resource-backed grief intervention plan

At karaoke, Ruka recognizes that Kazuya is trying to encourage Chizuru during the truce. She shows sympathy, briefly imagines the force of direct encouragement, then warns against overdoing it. She also asks Kazuya to take her to an amusement park, preserving her own relationship claim and future access demand while allowing the immediate Chizuru-focused action.

Kazuya asks the karaoke manager for an advance and says that a girl needs encouragement. The manager grants the request. Kazuya reviews Sumi's method, Sayuri's gratitude, Chizuru's controlled pain, and his own prior support promise. He prepares money and an itinerary, submits a rental booking to Chizuru, and resolves to make the next day the best possible date. This is a concrete support plan with transactional access and real cost. V018 ends before Chizuru accepts, refuses, modifies, or experiences it.
