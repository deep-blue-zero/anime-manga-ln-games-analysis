## Sequential account

### The public screening completes one promise and preserves Chizuru's chosen vocation

The scheduled public screening of *The Aquamarine Constellation* occurs before an applauding audience. Chizuru stands and bows after the film, making the project publicly received rather than merely finished or privately delivered. Backstage she is visibly pleased. She says making the film was extremely enjoyable, identifies acting as something she herself likes, and expects to continue. Sayuri's wish therefore remains part of the vocation without exhausting Chizuru's own reason for pursuing it.

Kazuya decides not to force a discussion of the crying episode. He praises her acting and privately reaffirms his attachment. The film route has reached public exhibition and produced vocational continuation, but neither person uses the event to redefine their relationship.

The chapter also opens a separate route. Kibe meets with an older café contact, and Mami appears and greets him. The encounter supplies no explanation yet, but it ends the long inactivity of Mami's family-adjacent information route.

### Ordinary routine resumes while Ruka obtains specifically bounded contact

On October 2, university and work routine resume. Kuribayashi meets Mini, who calls Kazuya “Master” in public and makes their unusual connection visible without explaining the film group's full history. At karaoke, Ruka asks about Chizuru's condition and the support date. She reminds Kazuya that she allowed the Chizuru-focused intervention and asks him to attend to her feelings as well.

Ruka first proposes traveling together to a hot spring and spending the night. Kazuya refuses the overnight trip. She changes the request to nearby Hisamatsuyu for the next day and also asks for a five-second hug. She physically arranges the approach but waits for his verbal answer; he says he permits it, and the embrace occurs within that stated limit. This is a real improvement in request-and-answer form compared with her unilateral kisses, while the surrounding status pressure and unresolved trial remain.

At the local bath, the pair use separate facilities. Ruka appears afterward in a yukata, seeks a compliment, and accepts Kazuya's practical help with her outer layer and an umbrella. Kazuya recognizes her appeal and happiness but privately concludes again that he cannot stop loving Chizuru. The outing gives the V018 truce a later access consequence without converting his feeling or ending the triangle.

### Chizuru creates ordinary access and reaccounts for the grief date

Chizuru sends Kazuya a direct LINE invitation for Saturday. No rental booking, project task, family emergency, or platform transaction governs the meeting. Kazuya treats the message as a possible confession setting, loses sleep, and arrives highly dysregulated by his own romantic expectations.

At the café, Chizuru returns money from the ten-hour date because she regards her crying as a failure to maintain professional service. Kazuya objects that the response was real and that being useful to her mattered. Chizuru concedes that she may be “a little weak,” pays the current café bill, and jokingly describes the returned amount in relation to her tears. Her natural smile and direct accounting acknowledge the event while restoring a legible boundary around payment and performance.

Kazuya asks her to continue the meeting over food. Chizuru accepts. After rejecting unnecessary expense and crowded alternatives, she agrees to Saizeriya, where ordinary menu familiarity replaces the elaborate choreography of the ten-hour booking. She drinks wine, becomes visibly drunk, laughs freely, orders additional food and dessert, and allows a more playful register. The sequence establishes voluntary unbooked time and relaxed access; it does not create an agreed romantic date or couple label.

### Mini's disclosure produces a direct question, but the answer remains incomplete

Chizuru asks whether Kazuya likes her. She explains that his ideal-girlfriend speech sounded as though it described her and explicitly recalls Mini's prior statement that he likes her. She also invokes the rental-client relation and Ruka, making the question a practical classification problem as well as an affective one. Kazuya is preparing to answer when a server arrives with dessert and interrupts the exchange.

On the walk home, Chizuru says that his support helped and that she is grateful. She then restores the familiar arrangement: until he finds a wonderful girlfriend, she will continue doing her best as his rental girlfriend, and he must find the best one. Kazuya promises that he will protect her, but she asks how he could do that under their existing terms. His answer begins to break those terms. He says the grief speech was not temporary or false, states plainly that his ideal girlfriend is Mizuhara, and begins, “I, for a long time, you…” before Kibe arrives in a car and interrupts him.

The confession therefore crosses an important threshold without reaching a completed proposition. Chizuru hears enough to replay the exchange after returning to her room. She covers herself, recalls both her question and his unfinished answer, calls herself foolish, and says she has to do something. Her reaction makes the speech consequential while withholding a romantic self-classification or response.

### Mami converts an old family route into current access

Kibe later tells Kazuya that Mami contacted him through Twitter and asked him to join a meeting with Nagomi. Mami presents an app-support concept aimed at older smartphone users and proposes helping the family business develop it. Nagomi regards the idea as promising. The encounter turns Mami's earlier knowledge of the Kinoshita family account into direct, legitimate-looking project access through Kibe.

Mami then encounters Kazuya on campus. She says her interest is the app and future work, asks him to keep their past relationship private, and denies a romantic purpose. She follows with a message promising to organize the proposal by the next week. Kazuya remains suspicious but lacks evidence of a hidden endpoint.

Immediately afterward, Kazuya meets Chizuru in her Ichinose campus presentation with female friends. He tries to reopen the prior exchange by asking whether she remembers, but she says there is nothing to discuss and walks on. Kazuya again recalls his unfinished “for a long time” statement. The final pages juxtapose Kazuya, Chizuru, and Mami: one declaration is partly heard but denied a response, while an apparently professional former-partner route is now entering the family system.
