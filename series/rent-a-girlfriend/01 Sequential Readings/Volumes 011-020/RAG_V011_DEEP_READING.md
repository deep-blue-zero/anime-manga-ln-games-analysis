## Sequential account

### Satisfaction 86-87 — hospital contact returns Chizuru to the family party

Chizuru calls Kazuya directly from the hospital. Sayuri became ill and required tests, but the immediate crisis has eased enough for Chizuru to return to the Kinoshita gathering. The call changes both movement and family conduct: Chizuru arrives under the girlfriend identity while Kazuya remains shaken by Ruka's kiss and by the hospital news.

Before Chizuru leaves the hospital, Ruka tells her that she kissed Kazuya and says that she has made him hers. Chizuru rejects the claim outwardly; the final fragment of her private response is incomplete and cannot support a supplied romantic confession. At the party, Nagomi welcomes Chizuru, Ruka competes for attention, and the family shares childhood photographs, cake, and gifts. Kazuya repeatedly searches for a moment to disclose the false relationship, but the crowded celebration keeps defeating the attempt.

### Satisfaction 88-89 — unilateral kisses and a family heirloom raise the cost of concealment

Ruka pulls Kazuya into a private space and kisses him repeatedly. He shows surprise, tells her to stop, and physically recoils; no reciprocal agreement is shown. Ruka apologizes for causing trouble but says that she does not want to lose and does not regret acting. Kazuya later tells himself that he is the one who cornered her by allowing the provisional relation to continue. That causal self-assessment does not convert her kisses into consensual intimacy.

Nagomi gives Chizuru the Kinoshita family engagement ring. Both Kazuya and Chizuru resist, but the heirloom transfers the family's mistaken couple belief into material custody and future obligation. Kazuya finally begins to say that the relationship is a lie. A hospital call interrupts him before he completes the disclosure, and he and Chizuru leave for Sayuri.

### Satisfaction 90-91 — limited time produces an explicit disagreement about truth

Sayuri has suffered a serious collapse but is conscious after stabilization. Chizuru says that she has prepared herself since learning about the condition and has done what she can. She connects acting school, its financial cost, rental work as practice, and her wish to let Sayuri see her on screen.

Kazuya says that he has decided to tell Nagomi the truth and argues that he and Chizuru must also tell Sayuri that their relationship is a lie. Because Sayuri's remaining time may be limited, he judges continued concealment more harmful. Chizuru stops him. She says that she does not want to tell her grandmother that they broke up, that Sayuri likes Kazuya and would worry if Chizuru were alone, and that telling her now would create no benefit. Chizuru chooses the memory that her granddaughter found a wonderful person over factual correction; Kazuya remains unconvinced.

Nagomi privately renews the ring offer. Chizuru says that she cannot accept it. Nagomi responds that Chizuru appears strong but may lose her nearest support if Sayuri dies, and tells her to pawn the ring if financial or emotional burden becomes too heavy. Chizuru takes custody under that care rationale, then places the ring case in Kazuya's bag. Back at the apartments, Chizuru is visibly strained in the shower while Kazuya resolves that he will tell Sayuri the truth. Chizuru also contacts Sumi to ask whether she is free the next day.

### Satisfaction 92 — Ruka declares a truce without revising the status campaign

Ruka summons Kazuya to Omotesando for a delayed birthday outing. Over food she asks about Sayuri, declares a temporary truce concerning Chizuru during the family crisis, and insists that her own relation with Kazuya has advanced. She treats the earlier kiss as progress, gives him a birthday present, and at departure kisses his cheek while declaring the kiss ban lifted. The change is tactical restraint around Chizuru's crisis, not a withdrawal of her status claim or a restored consent rule.

### Satisfaction 93-94 — Sumi builds care through planning

Chizuru tells Kazuya that Sumi wants another practice date and gives him the meeting details. When he asks about Sayuri, Chizuru repeats that she has done everything she can. She also asks about Ruka and treats apparent progress in the provisional relation as good. Kazuya reads her attention to everyone else as evidence that she is carrying more than she says.

Sumi arrives at Shinagawa in a school uniform because the earlier Tokyo Dome sighting made her think Kazuya likes uniforms. The choice embarrasses them both, but it also shows that she observed and acted on a specific preference. She has independently planned an Aqua Park visit and says that she wants to entertain or care for him for the day.

Sumi leads Kazuya through a detailed schedule: shows, a ride, photographs, a crane game, coffee, the underwater tunnel, and seats for the dolphin performance. Kazuya recognizes that spoken communication remains difficult while her planning and execution have become substantial. His pleasure at finally seeing the dolphin show delights her. The volume ends before the performance begins and before any deeper conversation can be observed.
