## Sequential account

### Satisfaction 131 — Chizuru rejects a hierarchy produced by Kazuya's admiration

After the active shoot, Kazuya treats Chizuru's performance as evidence that their lives belong to different worlds and apologizes for burdening her. Chizuru rejects that hierarchy. She compares his convenience-store-family route and her acting route as different family-linked purposes, identifies his continued labor as work performed for her, and calls his life wonderful. She then says she has never regretted meeting him. Comic recollection preserves prior irritation, but it does not cancel the present direct statement.

The exchange makes Kazuya's producer labor interpersonally consequential. Chizuru does not grant a new romantic label; she corrects his status inference and validates his work. By the chapter close the summer shoot has nearly completed its schedule, with one final scene remaining.

### Satisfaction 132-133 — a final-location plan becomes an engineered two-person trip

The remaining scene requires a starry sky at a high-elevation location after the employment and job-search period. Kazuya initially assumes a larger crew will travel, but the equipment needs are limited and the others do not need to attend. On the following Thursday he meets Chizuru at Ueno Station with luggage and camera equipment and only then fully understands that the trip may involve the two of them alone.

Chizuru asks why Mini is absent and recognizes manipulation. A false illness or absence message is presented under Ruka's name, but Ruka later says she did not consent and that Mini confessed after questioning. Mini engineered the two-person arrangement because she believes Kazuya and Chizuru like each other. Chizuru identifies the strategy, and the pair proceed because the filming task remains. In private, she recalls her earlier `好きじゃなくもない` answer. That memory shows unresolved self-classification; it is neither a confession to Kazuya nor a mutual agreement.

### Satisfaction 134 — ordinary travel care coexists with project accounting

Chizuru closes the seat gap, sits beside Kazuya, and brings two crab bentos. Each initially offers the choice to the other; Chizuru eats happily and calls the food delicious. Kazuya experiences the shared meal and travel as unusually natural while remaining anxious about proximity.

He also reviews the project more soberly. Although he had promised to make the film, repeated failures and the labor of many supporters brought it to this stage. Chizuru begins a response, but Mini's call interrupts before its content becomes clear. Mini says production has reached its climax and that her evaluation of Kazuya keeps rising. The evaluation concerns his producer conduct, not Chizuru's acting.

### Satisfaction 135 — one hotel room forces an explicit access decision

Mini has reserved one room for two people. The inn has no spare room, the room contains two futons, and the charge is 18,000 yen. Chizuru chooses to share it, citing cost, student finances, and prior proximity. This is a deliberate relaxation of a spatial boundary under project and financial conditions; it is not a declaration of couple status or consent to sexual contact.

They scout the snow-free slope at Madarao and prepare for the night shoot. Kazuya admits that handling the one-camera work alone may fail. Chizuru later appears in a yukata after bathing; his erotic focalization continues, but so do the equipment checks. She says there is something she wants to discuss, and they begin the final scene.

### Satisfaction 136 — Chizuru names the film's family function and completes the last scene

Chizuru explains that her grandfather Katsuhito died in a traffic accident three years earlier. When she first said she wanted to become an actress, he promised full support, and she promised that he would see her become one. After his death, Sayuri became the remaining person she wanted to receive the film; an ordinary audition result might not arrive in time. Kazuya's offer to make a film therefore made her happy. She thanks him directly and says she had not properly done so before.

The remembered promise that sustained effort will bring a supporter is visually joined to Kazuya behind the camera. Chizuru performs the final scene beneath the stars. Kazuya cries and evaluates the performance as her brightest yet, and the last take completes principal photography. Her disclosure establishes gratitude and family purpose. It does not convert either into a romantic confession.

### Satisfaction 137 — accidental exposure and private affect stop short of proved intimacy

In the bath, Kazuya reviews Chizuru's expressions around bereavement and recognizes that she has hidden pain for three years. Chizuru then enters the open-air bath because of a mistaken or changed bathing schedule; both react, and she exits. The encounter is accidental rather than chosen sexual intimacy.

They sleep in separate futons. During the night Chizuru wakes, approaches the sleeping Kazuya, and recalls both Katsuhito's claim that a supporter will appear and Kazuya's film promise. The framing does not establish a kiss or any contact. Her `もう 最悪っ` reaction and later inability to sleep support private affective activation while leaving action and mutual knowledge unproved.

### Satisfaction 138 — return travel exposes both closeness and unresolved status

On the bus Chizuru asks how the provisional relationship with Ruka is going and stresses that its purpose is to search for genuine feeling, so feelings require careful handling. Kazuya says he cannot keep dragging his family through deception and that the next person he introduces should be someone he can truly say he loves. The statement is indirect and is not a confession addressed to Chizuru.

Kazuya says he had expected the end of the film to make them strangers again, finds that prospect lonely, and thanks Chizuru because making the film with her was enjoyable and dreamlike. Chizuru falls asleep on his shoulder. He remains still and internally repeats that he likes her. He also shouts `好きだーっ!!!` while she is asleep or immediately before waking; the pages do not establish that she heard or understood it. She apologizes after waking.

At Iiyama Station, Ruka confronts them. She says Mini's explanation was suspicious and that Mini confessed after questioning. Ruka calls the overnight arrangement improper and reasserts girlfriend status. Chizuru denies any kiss or comparable contact. Ruka declares that a future trip with Kazuya will produce far more intimacy; Kazuya says that is impossible. The confrontation escalates rivalry and boundaries without correcting the provisional relation.

By August 26, the footage has been sent to the film or research club for editing, completion is planned for September, and Kazuya is arranging a theater. Principal photography has therefore become an active postproduction and exhibition-planning process, though no finished film or screening is yet observed.

### Satisfaction 139 — Ruka turns a birthday wish into an unresolved consent demand

August 26 is Ruka's first birthday since the trial relationship began. She asks Kazuya to grant a wish or promise and takes him to a crowded pool. She frames the outing as both a birthday claim and compensation for her film labor and exclusion from the location trip. Her bikini, bodily proximity, and direct pursuit dominate the outing; Kazuya repeatedly tries to preserve distance and tells himself not to let reason fail.

Ruka ends the volume by asking Kazuya to touch her body. No answer, contact, or completed act follows within V016. The request is an explicit intimacy and consent threshold, not evidence that consent or mutual intimacy has occurred.
