## Sequential account

### Satisfaction 140 — an explicit request becomes bounded sunscreen contact

Kazuya asks what Ruka means by touching her and resists an undefined act in a public pool. Ruka specifies sunscreen on her back and frames it as ordinary couple conduct. Kazuya agrees to applying cream rather than to unrestricted touching. He applies it to her back and later her legs while his erotic attention remains intense; when the cream runs out, the task ends.

Ruka then checks her pulse at ninety-four beats per minute and treats the reading as confirmation that Kazuya excites her. The number is direct evidence of her self-monitoring and represented interpretation. It is not an objective measure of healthy or reciprocal love. The sequence supports RAG-PRED-061 because the prior request receives a negotiated scope and observable contact, not because provisional status itself supplies consent.

### Satisfaction 141-142 — Ruka narrates persistence and obtains a naming change

During the birthday outing, Kazuya tries to explain that Chizuru remains the person he likes and that Ruka need not force herself for him. Ruka rejects the premise that she is forcing herself, says she also wants to feel like a princess on her birthday, and calls Kazuya the single person of destiny she finally found. This is direct self-report about her belief and desire; it is not evidence of his reciprocity.

Ruka uses a transit choice to explain her conduct. A Chuo Line trip between Shinjuku and Tokyo would have been faster, but she previously chose the longer Yamanote route to extend time with Kazuya. She also says the film work was enjoyable even though watching Kazuya and Chizuru together made her anxious. Project cooperation and rivalry therefore coexist in her own account.

Ruka asks for an experience rather than an object and moves toward a hug. Kazuya raises his hands and tells her to wait; no completed embrace is shown. She then asks permission to call him `和也` rather than `和也君`. He says the form of address is not a major problem and agrees. Ruka repeatedly practices the given name, and he walks her home. The outcome is a mutually acknowledged naming change after an interrupted physical request, not a conversion of the trial into reciprocal partnership.

### Satisfaction 143 — Sumi's visit produces a screening date and Sayuri's relational inference

Chizuru visits Sayuri in the hospital and brings new clothes. Sayuri jokes, identifies herself as a person who expects good weather, and tells Chizuru not to worry, but her reassurance is character speech rather than medical evidence. Sumi arrives with flowers and uses a phone memo to ask about the crowdfunded film.

Chizuru says principal photography has wrapped, the film is being edited, and a screening is scheduled for September 30. She recounts production difficulties and Kazuya's persistence as producer. Sayuri observes the pleasure in Chizuru's account and asks when love sprouts in a person, answering that it is when someone talks happily about another person. This is Sayuri's close-observer interpretation. Chizuru neither adopts the label nor communicates an affirmative classification to Kazuya.

### Satisfaction 144 — the naming privilege becomes public status pressure

Mini returns to the apartment corridor and talks with Kazuya about the film and the September 30 screening. Chizuru says she will take Sayuri directly. When Ruka arrives and sees the group, she invokes ordinary girlfriend access, uses `和也` in front of Chizuru and Mini, and declares that she is Kazuya's girlfriend. The granted form of address becomes a public status marker even though the underlying relation remains provisional.

Ruka pulls Kazuya into his apartment, repeats that she is his girlfriend, proposes shared bathing, and performs laundry and other domestic work. Kazuya does not accept the sexualized proposal. The chapter makes the distinction among permission to use a name, access to an apartment, performed domesticity, and mutual romantic status materially visible.

### Satisfaction 145 — a cinema visit joins accessibility, gratitude, and status-independent support

Kazuya meets Chizuru and Sayuri at the cinema selected for the project. The accessible room has two hundred seats, and space is arranged so Sayuri can watch from her wheelchair near Chizuru. The edit is still in progress, so this visit is preparation rather than the completed screening. It nevertheless gives Sayuri direct access to the intended exhibition site and makes the September 30 plan concrete.

While Chizuru leaves briefly, Sayuri thanks Kazuya. She says that meeting him made both her and Chizuru happy and that, without him, she would not have been able to see the film route become real. Kazuya privately recognizes that their public relationship rests on deception and describes Chizuru as beyond him. He then tells Sayuri that even if they cease to be lovers, he wants to support Chizuru for the rest of his life. The statement is status-independent support language, not a corrected account of the current relationship.

### Satisfaction 146 — Chizuru affirms the dream before the family visit becomes an emergency

Sayuri asks Chizuru whether continuing to pursue acting causes suffering or whether obligation to her grandparents is forcing the effort. Chizuru says that hardship exists but that she likes pursuing dreams and expects to continue. A flashback shows Sayuri and Katsuhito disagreeing about the acting dream. Katsuhito argues that amid a harsh reality at least one person can keep saying the dream will come true and asks Sayuri to support Chizuru if he dies first. In the present, Sayuri judges that his support was not mistaken and anticipates seeing Chizuru on the cinema screen.

Chizuru waits for Kazuya after Sayuri's private conversation and thanks him again. The chosen interaction extends their non-rental support relation without changing its label. Moments later Sayuri collapses in the cinema lobby. An ambulance carries her to the hospital, converting the planned access event into acute medical urgency.

### Satisfaction 147 — terminal-risk information exposes the gap between speech and visible strain

At the hospital, Chizuru remains beside Sayuri while Kazuya waits outside. The physician says recovery is difficult and that the night may be critical. Chizuru tells Kazuya that Sayuri had seemed well earlier and may have overextended herself during the cinema visit.

Chizuru thanks Kazuya and says that because of him she did everything she could for her grandmother through the end, so she has no regret. Her tears, clenched hand, posture, and broken composure visibly complicate the completeness of that statement. Kazuya recognizes the discrepancy and thinks that her face communicates pain her words suppress. He recalls the years of savings, cheap housing, acting practice, and film labor organized around showing Sayuri the result.

Kazuya imagines what a real boyfriend could do and whether he could embrace her back, but he does not touch her. The visualized act remains counterfactual. The chapter ends with support desire and physical restraint rather than completed consolation.

### Satisfaction 148 — truth and comfort become an immediate decision conflict

Chizuru prepares to enter Sayuri's room. The doctor has said that Sayuri may hear speech although she is not responding. Kazuya asks whether Chizuru is truly willing to leave the rental and family deception uncorrected. Chizuru says they already settled the matter and resists making Sayuri's final period sad. She questions whether a belief that made Sayuri happy must now be replaced by painful truth.

Kazuya argues that Chizuru should put her own feelings first and asks whether she can leave without guilt or regret. Chizuru says she hates sad truths and accepts the possibility that Sayuri might resent her. Her response preserves agency and a coherent ethic; it does not prove the absence of conflict, especially after the prior visible strain.

Kazuya does not force disclosure or enter the room. He turns from argument toward action, asks what he can still do for Chizuru, opens LINE, and runs. V017 ends before the recipient, message, or result is shown. The endpoint freezes an actionable film-and-care route without importing its V018 consequence.
