## Sequential account

### Satisfaction 104-105 — the promise becomes a governed production plan

At a family restaurant, Chizuru classifies the film as acting work, says her agency and manager must know, and asks to be treated properly as an actress. The pair list script, staff, casting, shooting, editing, and miscellaneous work. Chizuru takes the acting side while Kazuya researches crowdfunding. They postpone telling Sayuri or Nagomi because an uncertain plan would burden Sayuri, and Chizuru insists on splitting the meal because she is not attending as his girlfriend.

Kazuya consults CAMPFIRE planner Toppe Shimizu. The planner rejects the undeveloped campaign page, explains the need for a key image, rewards, commitment, and initial momentum, and asks why a stranger would give ten thousand yen. Kazuya returns discouraged. Chizuru reports manager approval, accepts the risk that her real identity may circulate at university, and says failure is unavailable. She proposes continuing inside his room because it is late and she cannot invite him into hers.

### Satisfaction 106-109 — private collaboration produces a budget and reciprocal labor

New neighbor Mini Yaemori sees Chizuru and Kazuya at his door. She later identifies herself as an online creator and cosplayer, while Kazuya asks her to keep the sighting private.

Chizuru deliberately enters Kazuya's room for project work, explicitly denies that the meeting is a date, and helps construct a feature-film budget. Camera, sound, transport, screenplay, operator, cast, locations, staff, editing, and contingency produce a target of 1.82 million yen. She selects campaign imagery, discusses rewards, waives her acting fee, and offers savings; Kazuya refuses to make her carry both performance and finance. He says her dream is what he wants realized and that he hated seeing her cry. When she learns he has not eaten, she improvises omurice and asks for his reaction. The domestic-looking care remains embedded in shared project work.

### Satisfaction 110-111 — Mini converts discovery into recruited labor

Mini catches Chizuru leaving and demands an account. Chizuru states the rental-provider rule, denies a private romance, and explains that the room visit concerned the film for Sayuri. Mini cries, insists that they cannot lose, and offers to help. Chizuru resists the romantic interpretation but accepts that more hands will be useful, while insisting that the purpose is the film.

Mini tests the relationship by comparing Chizuru's omurice with her own curry and asks who the producer is. Chizuru identifies Kazuya as producer. Mini's offer changes the project from a two-person undertaking into a small team without changing the pair's declared status.

### Satisfaction 112 — family recognition and campaign publication

Chizuru visits Sayuri while recalling Kazuya's promise. Sayuri identifies Kazuya as the person Chizuru most wants to discuss, grants permission for Chizuru to write about herself publicly, and calls the film an excellent proposal. She says imperfect people compensate for one another's lacks, praises Chizuru, acknowledges Kazuya's weakness, and calls him uniquely suitable for her. Chizuru supplies no romantic agreement but later calls him a good person.

Kazuya arrives with notice that CAMPFIRE approved the campaign. Chizuru confirms Sayuri's permission. They publish quickly because time matters. Kazuya activates the page with a 1.82-million-yen target and forty-five-day window; both pray for success. The volume ends at zero yen and zero supporters, converting promise into a public, measurable undertaking.
