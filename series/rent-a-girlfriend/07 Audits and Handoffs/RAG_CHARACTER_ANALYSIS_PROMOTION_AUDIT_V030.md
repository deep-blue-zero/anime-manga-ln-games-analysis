## Why the block does not trigger another promotion

Harumi is the principal home change since V020, already made at V024 when a substantial, first-person family and ring conversation exceeded a router row. That ledger is justified as evidence ownership, but it supplies neither repeated cross-context response nor conduct after the central relationship premise is corrected. Kazuo's V026 anger and mistaken money inference are consequential but still too narrow for a standalone record that would merely restate the volume and family ledger. The fourteen-row review does not manufacture symmetrical files.

Nagomi's near-complete acceptance after the Paradise explanation remains an important observation, yet she is still told that the pair genuinely date. Kibe's V028 apology distinguishes lying from automatic malice, and his V030 suggestion is ordinary care, but neither is a test under the final status truth. Kuribayashi asks the sharper question without resolving it. These differentiated responses belong in evidence ledgers; assigning generalized corrected-facts rules would leak information they do not have.

Ruka and Mami gain substantial states without earning a higher readiness label. Ruka's V026 protection is evidence against a one-note coercion model but coexists with the wrapper lie and status pressure; V030 actively withholds the house facts from her. Mami's history and accusation make method more legible, while the V027 horizon failure and ambiguous V030 meeting keep her endpoint unresolved. Mini's useful mediation and residence do not validate her love label or privacy discipline. Sumi's V030 care corroborates a narrow method after absence but does not establish broad independent routine. These are precise updates to existing partial models, not invitations to predict whole persons.

The existing Kazuya and Chizuru models have enough cross-context states for bounded operational candidacy and were tested separately. Public kisses, the inquiry, and the household are analytically rich but still leave a private answer, truth obligations, and long-term outcomes open. A monograph could someday synthesize literary function and competing explanations, but this block's checkpoint and models already own the current argument and evidence. Creating one now would duplicate them without a distinct question. The governing architecture needs no amendment, and V031 is not admitted by this audit.
