This audit tests the six exact model files at the V030 closing commit against their time-indexed states, rule conditions, admitted evidence, and prospectively frozen next-volume predictions in the claims ledger. A prediction frozen before a volume tests the declared observable horizon, but it is not necessarily a prediction uniquely derived from one character model. Where a final V030 rule was written after the event, the check is explicitly retrospective. The same analyst read the source and tested the models, so none of these checks is blind or an independent generation trial. A successful test means the model distinguishes access, knowledge, consent, and plausible response form within a named domain; it does not make an unseen outcome certain. Dialogue generation and invented scenes supply no evidence.

## Kazuya Kinoshita — concrete action and evasive audience management

RAG-KAZ-R017 and R018 distinguish an explicit attempt to end Ruka's trial from her refusal to recognize it and turn repeated truth pressure into the V024 chapel approach. RAG-PRED-079 supports the V021 status-withdrawal response, while RAG-PRED-077 supports renewed confession pressure; the V024-V025 evidence supports initiated but interrupted speech, not a completed private confession. Under public exposure, RAG-KAZ-R022 describes the observed combination of real love declaration and fabricated dating timeline before he surrenders the cover (RAG-E-V026-007 through RAG-E-V026-012). These later rules describe response form retrospectively; the model must not call the fabricated claim truthful because the feeling is sincere.

The stronger V027-V030 discrimination is between unbounded reassurance and concrete terms. Silence produces severe rejection inference; Mini's limited correction permits a paid recontact (RAG-KAZ-R023; RAG-E-V027-008 through RAG-E-V027-015). Chizuru's direct, uncertain but bounded account lets him ask about the kisses, defer an answer, cooperate with ordinary visits and family-house labor, and accept a separate room (RAG-KAZ-R024; RAG-E-V028-001 through RAG-E-V030-004). RAG-PRED-113 and RAG-PRED-116 prospectively expected operating housing consequences and were supported, but the broader model did not predict that his practical adaptation would be honest to all audiences. V030 falsifies any simple “new maturity implies full disclosure” extension: he misstates the destination to Ruka and leaves Nagomi and Kibe incompletely informed. His apology to Mami is a counterexample to absolute avoidance; his birthday intention is not a completed act. The model remains operational only where the other person's expressed terms and his information state are specified.

## Ruka Sarashina — care does not neutralize coercion

The V021 breakup request tests RAG-RUK-R010: she knows Kazuya's preference but insists the trial is not severable and intensifies contact. V022-V024 test R011-R013: the wrapper and fabricated sex story are explicit manipulation of Chizuru's information, while Ruka repeatedly declines to expose Nagomi and instead continues private pressure (RAG-E-V022-003 through RAG-E-V024-012). RAG-RUK-R014's self-costly protection has an unusually discriminating V026 case. She could benefit from Mami's demand, yet rejects compelled proof for Kazuya and Chizuru while retaining her own claim. R015 then fits renewed anger and professional-access demands in V027; R016 fits concrete worry at Kazuya's karaoke work in V028 without mutual restoration. The V030 question about residence is tested only under the false family-home answer. RAG-PRED-115 is supported because concealment occurs, not because the model knows Ruka's informed response. The earlier V019 horizon failure in the V020 audit remains failed; later V021-V030 conduct cannot repair that prediction. Her family, school, independent goals, durable response to refusal, and accountability for the fabrication keep readiness partial.

## Sumi Sakurasawa — a narrow but repeated care route

V018's self-funded friend excursion, prepared activity, and grief listening supplied the V020 partial model's strongest tests. V019-V027 and V029 provide no substantial new direct conduct; V028 is a silent appearance. V030 therefore tests retention after a long gap rather than continuous growth. Sumi arrives at the house, responds to a cut with a bandage, listens to a concrete no-present concern, and gives Kazuya a perspective that does not speak for Chizuru (RAG-E-V030-008). RAG-SUM-R002/R003/R006 fit bounded practical help and frame-sensitive support retrospectively, though this short encounter is less formally prepared than the earlier outing.

Her V012 confession remains unheard by Kazuya. First aid is not a romantic act, her suggestion is not consent for a gift, and no birthday outcome is observed. A broad prediction about independent employment, spontaneous group speech, or direct romantic rejection would require new assumptions. This is a meaningful corroboration in one care domain, not a promotion to whole-person operational readiness.

All six models retain their V030 readiness labels: two `OPERATIONAL_CANDIDATE`, four `PARTIAL_MODEL`. Their failure modes are overgeneralized competence, romance inferred from access, motive solved from surface behavior, knowledge leakage across audiences, and turning later outcomes into earlier predictive successes. Future validation requires separately authorized evidence, especially direct informed Ruka and family responses, Chizuru's promised answer, sustained household conduct, Mami's declared purpose, Mini under a firm stop, and Sumi outside the care setting. No V031 prediction or scenario is created here.
