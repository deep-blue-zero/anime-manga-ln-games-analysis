## Why the block does not trigger another promotion

Kazuya and Chizuru gain the richest new evidence. Their formal date is not merely scheduled: it begins on May 17, and Chizuru directly distinguishes it from a rental-girlfriend role. Yet the V040 cut occurs at Joypolis arrival, before the date's outcome and her promised answer. Kazuya's increasingly direct communication is accompanied by concrete concealment from Ruka. More data make both existing models more precise; they do not erase their central abstentions or justify whole-person readiness. A monograph at this point would largely duplicate the checkpoint and models without a distinct integrated question.

Ruka has a meaningful V040 scene, but her knowledge state is the reason not to promote. She learns of a move and gives a keyholder while missing the Chizuru cohabitation and date facts. Her affection cannot be interpreted as an informed decision about either. Mami's V039 status probe adds direct method evidence, followed by a V040 no-action horizon. Mini's helpful advice does not settle whether she respects a firm stop or protects confidential information. Sumi's friendship permits Chizuru to articulate uncertainty, but work and conflict outside a helper role remain underobserved. These are specific partial-model updates, not evidence for global competence.

Among evidence-only homes, V034 gives Harumi a second concrete domain, the nursery, but no broader cross-context rule under corrected relationship facts. Kibe's V035 testimony is valuable precisely because he stops short of declaring Chizuru's feeling. Umi's V033 confession is explicit, yet he prevents the answer from being heard; a model of how he handles refusal would be speculative. Nagomi's professional apology does not correct her couple premise. Sayuri and Katsuhito remain historical records, and Kazuo and Kuribayashi have no material new breadth. No character deserves a symmetrical artifact just because the block ended.
