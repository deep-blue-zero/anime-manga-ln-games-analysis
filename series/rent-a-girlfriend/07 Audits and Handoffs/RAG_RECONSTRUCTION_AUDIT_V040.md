## Kazuya Kinoshita — concrete follow-through with a retained truth debt

The V030 model's distinction between real effort and anxious overreading survives this block. Kazuya acknowledges Chizuru's birthday request with a coupon rather than an expensive present; he works competently at Harumi's nursery; he prepares an unpaid date by seeking preferences, buying shoes, checking a route, arranging money and facing heavy rain (RAG-E-V031-006, -007; RAG-E-V034-002 through -005; RAG-E-V037-003 through -010; RAG-E-V040-002, -006 through -008). His internal scorekeeping is repeatedly more extreme than Chizuru's direct instructions. The V038-V039 preview and V040 rain date are discriminating because Chizuru corrects his overplanned escort assumptions, and he can still act after the plan is disrupted. In V039 he plainly says he likes her; V040's naming conversation clarifies that he means Ichinose, not simply a rental persona (RAG-E-V038-008; RAG-E-V039-009; RAG-E-V040-001).

The counterexample to a generalized mature-honesty rule is equally concrete. Kazuya lets Ruka assist with a card and keyholder, tells her he moves next week, but withholds Chizuru cohabitation and the imminent date. His V040 answer to Chizuru's Ruka question cannot turn Ruka's partial knowledge into consent. Under crowded-train pressure, he apologizes for accidental contact; this is a local correction, not proof of stable intimacy skill. The model remains operational only for bounded anxiety, care, disclosure and practical-action conditions, and must abstain on full truth and the final relationship (RAG-E-V040-002 through -004, -009 through -012).

## Chizuru Ichinose — self-authored access without a verdict

V031 directly tests a tempting but false collapse of gratitude into love: Chizuru tells Sumi that Kazuya matters and that she does not know whether her feeling is romantic. The model must preserve both parts. She then asks Mini and Kibe about him, volunteers for Harumi's nursery visit, asks for ordinary honesty at home, makes the first explicit date proposal, accepts Kazuya's later unpaid request and specifies an ordinary mutual encounter. The Joypolis preview provides repeated independent choices: photo, rides, romance-category quiz, questions about girlfriend and Mami, and later home television. V040 adds names, Sayuri's film, Ruka concern, non-rental framing and umbrella sharing (RAG-E-V031-004; RAG-E-V032-001; RAG-E-V033-010; RAG-E-V034-001, -007; RAG-E-V036-009; RAG-E-V037-001, -003; RAG-E-V039-001 through -005, -009, -010; RAG-E-V040-001, -005 through -012).

These actions support agency and chosen local access, not a final couple agreement. The V034 `ito` score, V039 fortune result, blushes, dreams and Kazuya's fantasies are adversarial cues; her own words and acts hold higher evidentiary weight. Her V033 partial response to Umi is interrupted by his request not to hear it yet, so neither acceptance nor rejection should be invented. Her V040 private intention to decide increases salience but the date stops at Joypolis arrival. OPERATIONAL_CANDIDATE remains appropriate for bounded inquiry and boundary behavior; any model that fills in the final answer would fail the current evidence boundary.

## Ruka Sarashina — partial knowledge is the central test

There is no substantial V031-V039 direct Ruka scene sufficient to assess an informed reaction to the cohabitation. V040 is more diagnostic but still incomplete: she notices the new key and Kazuya's mood, learns the move is next week, helps with a fish keyholder and remains affectionate. Her wish to participate in important matters is not refuted by the fact that Kazuya omits the most important facts. Nor does her gift repair her prior fabricated sex claim or make the disputed trial reciprocal. The shop stumble is an accidental physical event, not negotiated intimacy (RAG-E-V040-002 through -004). The model correctly conditions its expectations on what she knows and abstains on what she would do after full disclosure. PARTIAL_MODEL remains the responsible grade.

All six models retain their V040 readiness labels: two `OPERATIONAL_CANDIDATE` and four `PARTIAL_MODEL`. The principal failure modes are reading numbers or reactions as answers, inferring romance from chosen access, treating accidental contact as permission, leaking facts across audiences, and rescuing failed horizons with adjacent events. Next validation requires newly authorized evidence after V040, especially the actual date's continuation, Chizuru's answer, a full-information Ruka exchange, the housing transition, Mami's next declared purpose, Mini's response to a firm limit and Sumi outside a care role. No V041 scenario or prediction is admitted here.
