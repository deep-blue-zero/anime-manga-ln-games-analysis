## Written-speech and ordinary-conduct notes

- Harumi's substantive V024 speech is personal, direct, and care-oriented. She explains her attachment through concrete family history rather than demanding that Chizuru prove worth.
- She explicitly separates affection from compulsory retention of the inherited ring.
- The souvenir-ring scene introduces ambiguity between low-stakes play and continued bridal coding; both effects should be preserved.
- At the nursery she speaks concretely about children learning through being met directly and about Kazuya's habitual role, rather than only asserting that her son is good.

## Gaps and negative evidence

- V034 establishes a nursery work setting and one observed shift. Marriage dynamics, friendships, conflict behavior, and repeated decisions outside the apparent-couple context remain sparse.
