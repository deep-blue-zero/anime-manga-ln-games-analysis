## Written-speech and ordinary-conduct notes

- Direct represented speech is encouraging, dream-centered, and addressed to Chizuru.
- Taxi work is the only concrete independent routine shown, and it appears inside the fatal-event sequence.
- Later evidence is dominated by family memory and symbolic inheritance rather than contemporaneous observation.

## Gaps and negative evidence

- Broader history, friendships, work conduct, finances, conflict behavior, marriage, parenting, and relationships outside the household are not sufficiently represented.
- A terminal reassurance cannot be generalized into responses to ordinary disagreement or failure.
- Later recollection may accurately preserve importance while still selecting only family-salient moments.
- Death and the retrospective sample prevent prospective behavioral testing; a dedicated ledger is warranted, but an operational model is not.
