## The V031-V040 block's contribution

### Care becomes plural while Chizuru keeps the right to classify it

V031's April 19 birthday places Chizuru in several distinguishable relations at once. The theater troupe recognizes her work; Sumi offers a listening friend; Kazuya and Mini make a homecoming celebration. Chizuru tells Sumi that Kazuya has supported her and that she cares about him, but specifically declines to call the feeling love because she cannot yet distinguish gratitude and being rescued from romantic desire. This is a stated uncertainty, not a coy affirmative. Mini's later wish for a simpler answer likewise does not outrank Chizuru's words. The birthday service coupon is accepted without being used during the block (RAG-E-V031-001 through -010; RAG-E-V032-001).

V032-V035 broaden the evidence Chizuru chooses to gather. She asks Mini about Kazuya, permits help with house clearing and a public shopping trip, and initiates a short night errand. V033's Twister game creates local physical embarrassment and a group photograph, while Umi's direct confession at Sayuri's altar creates a competing proposal and an information asymmetry: Kazuya and Mini overhear, but Chizuru is not shown knowing they heard. Umi defers her answer, and she later asks Kazuya out herself. V034 converts a proposed outing into a visit to Harumi's nursery, where both Chizuru and Kazuya interact with children and Harumi offers observed testimony about him. Chizuru later demands truth even about shared body soap. V035 has her seek Kibe's testimony, accept bounded care during menstrual pain, and confront the still misleading family premise after Nagomi books her as a rental girlfriend. Neither Kibe's reading of a blush nor a card game's romantic prompt supplies her missing answer (RAG-E-V032-001 through -012; RAG-E-V033-002 through -012; RAG-E-V034-001 through -010; RAG-E-V035-001 through -012).

### A voluntary date moves from idea to execution

The May 13 anxiety that Kazuya first voices in V031-V033 becomes a specific May 18 move-in date when the new lease appears in V036. Kazuya asks to meet Chizuru as a man and woman outside the rental service; she chooses May 17 and writes “date” in her calendar. In V037 she clarifies that she wants an ordinary outing in which they can learn about each other, supplies a hotpot preference, and changes the meeting arrangement to a nine o'clock departure from their house. These direct terms matter more than Kazuya's private itinerary and online ideal-date prescriptions. They also keep the date distinct from paid rental work and from the V034 nursery visit (RAG-E-V036-006 through -010; RAG-E-V037-001 through -010).

V038-V039 deliver a substantial unplanned preview at Joypolis and lunch before May 17. Chizuru notices Kazuya's overplanned escort posture and asks for mutual walking pace, choices and conversation. She chooses games, a photo, rides and a romance-category compatibility quiz. The quiz's 90% result is a playful in-story fortune, not a measurement of feeling. The preview also brings Mami into direct contact after she silently sees them: she asks about their status, says Hakuba is not her boyfriend, and later reflects privately. Chizuru asks whether Kazuya still likes Mami. He distinguishes his former attachment and directly says he likes Chizuru. That statement makes his side explicit without giving hers. A final six-day marker, ordinary home television and clothing preparation return the narrative to the approaching formal date (RAG-E-V038-003 through -009; RAG-E-V039-001 through -010).

V040 executes the departure. Chizuru raises the difference between her rental alias and person, shares Sayuri's film and acting ideal, confirms the time, and privately reviews the date. May 17 brings heavy rain and a crowded train, but both proceed and reach Joypolis. Kazuya compliments her; she asks whether Ruka is all right; transit creates accidental bodily contact followed by his apology. Chizuru describes being beside him as a woman rather than as a rental girlfriend and asks him to share her umbrella at the venue threshold. The date is real and still in progress when the book ends. It has neither completed her inquiry nor moved him into his new apartment (RAG-E-V040-001, -005 through -012).

### Material keys increase the cost of unequal information

The temporary household has supported play, work, shared shopping, a birthday, illness care and ordinary evenings, but it is still bounded. The May 18 lease makes the exit clock more precise, not optional. Ruka's position becomes especially consequential in V040: while she helps Kazuya obtain a card and select a fish keyholder, he discloses only that he moves next week. He continues to withhold the Chizuru cohabitation and May 17 date. Her affection and gift are therefore observed under incomplete information. Chizuru's question about Ruka on the date shows she does not ignore the obligation; it does not certify that Ruka has accepted an informed separation. Nagomi retains the genuine-couple premise even after her professional apology and rental booking. Umi's postponed answer, the unused birthday coupon, the uncorrected family status, and the house's eventual disposition all remain separate debts (RAG-E-V035-010 through -012; RAG-E-V036-002, -006; RAG-E-V040-002 through -004, -009).
