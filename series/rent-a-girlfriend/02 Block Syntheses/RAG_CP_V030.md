## The V021-V030 block's contribution

### A proposed truth correction becomes a public, still incomplete one

At the V020 boundary, Kazuya's direct declaration had been interrupted and Mami's family access was growing. V021 makes the fork explicit. Chizuru brings Kazuya into her acting network, where his producer work is recognized, but her conditional account of wanting to date someone she came to like is no answer about him. Kazuya tells Ruka that he loves Chizuru and asks to end their trial; Ruka refuses to recognize an ending. Kibe demands honesty while believing the public couple account. Mami combines the crowdfunding record, a remembered object in Kazuya's room, and access to Nagomi to test Chizuru's account (RAG-E-V021-001 through RAG-E-V021-019).

The two-night Hawaiians trip places unequal knowledge in one audience. Nagomi treats Chizuru as family; Kibe believes genuine dating; Kuribayashi knows the rental origin; Ruka has a disputed girlfriend claim; Mami has collected the rental, money, ring, film, and family routes. Chizuru and Kazuya coordinate around returning the heirloom ring and eventual truth. In parallel, Ruka fabricates a sexual event using a torn condom wrapper, repeats the claim to Chizuru, and is directly denied by Kazuya. Chizuru questions the source rather than treating an object as proof. Harumi's private account of Kazuya's birth and her explicit permission to return the ring relieve one supposed obligation without correcting the family status premise. Nagomi calls Chizuru family, making both her care and the cost of deception concrete (RAG-E-V022-001 through RAG-E-V024-022).

Mami's V025 proposed rescue is also a timed, coercive plan: she demands the ring and a meeting with Nagomi, resists Chizuru's objections, and later bargains for a coordinated cover. Her account of a controlled childhood and thwarted Tarou relation supplies represented reasons for hostility to performed romance, not proof of one exhaustive motive or justification for controlling Chizuru. Chizuru rejects Mami's method while admitting her own role in the lie. Kazuya's chapel attempt is interrupted by Mami's message; his interpretation of Chizuru's flight as romantic rejection lacks the information she has. When Ruka's phone displays Chizuru's Diamond profile in the mixed group, V026 establishes that Mami dropped it, but the exact screen preparation remains unknown (RAG-E-V025-001 through RAG-E-V026-004).

The exposure produces neither clean confession nor total concealment. Kazuya first gives a false dating timeline and a real public declaration of love. Mami and then Kibe demand a visible kiss as verification; Kibe also punches Kazuya. Ruka rejects that verification regime despite its potential advantage to her own claim. Chizuru initiates two real kisses under public pressure, gives her real name and rental identity, and claims genuine girlfriend status. The pair subsequently give Nagomi an approximately two-hour explanation of almost the entire history while preserving exactly the dating claim as a lie. Nagomi's acceptance follows that explanation, but it is not informed acceptance of the final status truth. The kisses cannot be read as private mutual dating agreement, and the ring is discussed without a visible completed return (RAG-E-V026-005 through RAG-E-V026-016).

### Public survival is followed by a private information collapse

V027 begins with ordinary group treatment, not expulsion. Kuribayashi challenges the retained claim and Kibe later accepts Kazuya's apology, but both repair routes retain incomplete information. Ruka challenges Chizuru about the kisses and the rental rule violation, threatens agency contact, then demands reduced ordinary access. Chizuru accepts responsibility for the breach and later privately recognizes a customer-directed feeling as problematic. She still cannot name or rank it as a final answer, and her concern for Ruka is real without recognizing Ruka as the authority over her choice (RAG-E-V027-001 through RAG-E-V027-007; RAG-E-V028-011).

Chizuru reads Kazuya's practical message and does not respond for roughly three months. Kazuya experiences the silence as rejection; her reason is unavailable to him until later. Mini returns, challenges that conclusion, contacts Chizuru, and presses her to examine the feeling. Mini's love label is not Chizuru's own classification. Kazuya pays for a rental date on March 1 to restore access. The paid wrapper works as a contact route while remaining the professional condition Chizuru sees as a barrier (RAG-E-V027-008 through RAG-E-V027-015).

V028 converts the delayed speech into a direct apology and inquiry. Chizuru explains her silence, gives late Valentine chocolate, acknowledges the two kisses and her work violation, and refuses to answer with a feeling she cannot yet identify. She values the rental job and relies on it for tuition and expenses. Kazuya asks directly without requiring an immediate answer and rejects her taking all professional blame. Chizuru promises an answer and asks about Ruka first. Unpriced LINE contact, room conversation, shared YouTube time, a theater invitation, sustained work talk, and practical help at Sayuri's house then broaden the observation field. None independently establishes a couple contract. Mini's imagined compatibility tests remain speculation, while Chizuru's concern about harm to Ruka is an explicit limit of her own inquiry (RAG-E-V028-001 through RAG-E-V028-015).

### Family property becomes a bounded household, not a romantic verdict

The childhood house initially holds an altar, Chizuru's rare maternal photograph, Katsuhito's stones, Sayuri's retained objects, and labor toward a possible sale. A quake removes adjacent apartment access and creates a real housing problem. Kazuya shields Chizuru during the tremor; the close contact is safety caused. Mini proposes cohabitation and transport. During an alcohol-affected conversation Chizuru acknowledges loneliness and considers another kiss, but the taxi interrupts it. She later makes a sober, separate-room offer for one month and invites Mini. The offered shelter is chosen access under material constraint, not a retroactive completion of the kiss or final feeling answer (RAG-E-V028-013 through RAG-E-V029-012).

V030 implements the proposal: key, separate room, three-person occupancy, expenses, laundry, meals, bath order, and shared sink time. Domestic repetition produces both actual coordination and Kazuya's fantasy or overreading of ordinary phrases. Peter the neighborhood cat carries a Sayuri-linked household memory, not privileged evidence of Chizuru's romance. On April 16 Kazuya learns that her birthday is April 19; she requests no present during his financial strain. Sumi later bandages his cut and helps him think about a modest acknowledgment, but no birthday result occurs. Mami's public tea meeting reopens contact without declaring her aim. Kazuya conceals the house destination from Ruka, leaves Nagomi's couple premise intact, and does not inform Kibe when he offers rent-sharing advice. The domestic gain therefore increases the cost of audience-specific truth debt (RAG-E-V030-001 through RAG-E-V030-009).

The strongest block-level explanation is a **mismatch among audiences and forms of access**. The resort provides public bodily proof and a broad factual account, while the principals still lack private classification. The three-month silence then removes ordinary contact despite public survival. Paid recontact restores conversation; ordinary contact and residence grow afterward under new conditions. Each change is real in its own domain, and no single kiss, price, key, or family label can stand in for the others.
