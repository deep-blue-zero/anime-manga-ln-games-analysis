## The V011-V020 block's contribution

### A relationship becomes a production system

The block's largest structural change is the independent film. Kazuya's distress after hearing Chizuru's family and career history becomes research, a proposal, and a promise. Chizuru tests feasibility and requests the project. The work then acquires an agency boundary, budget, crowdfunding platform, public family story, source permission, director, crew, campaign strategy, backers, production roles, locations, editing, a private bedside projection, and finally a public screening.

This route changes the pair without making the project a disguised dating contract. Kazuya becomes useful under external standards and specialist authority. Chizuru becomes a principal who approves disclosure, contributes money and property, governs private access, uses her professional network, performs the lead, and names the film's family purpose. Mini and Ruka provide real labor; Tabuse and other specialists provide competence neither principal possesses. The strongest account is distributed agency around a measurable goal, not solitary rescue.

The completed screening matters because it closes a sequence that repeatedly remained incomplete: campaign success was not a finished film, principal photography was not editing, a reserved cinema was not exhibition, and bedside footage was not a public screening. V020 supplies the public result. Chizuru's backstage statement also prevents the film from functioning only as a memorial endpoint. She says production was enjoyable, acting is something she likes, and she expects to continue.

### Family truth becomes partial correction, irreversible loss, and grief support

The block reopens the founding deception under terminal pressure. Kazuya argues that limited time makes truth important; Chizuru defends a comforting final belief. The ring intensifies the inherited obligation without resolving it. When Sayuri becomes critical, Kazuya adapts unfinished footage into bedside access. Chizuru then states the central correction—that she and Kazuya are not dating—but does not deliver a complete rental history. Sayuri says she both wants and does not want to know, entrusts the answer to Chizuru, praises the film, exchanges love with her, and dies.

The result defeats a simple truth-versus-lie binary. Total concealment does not survive, but informed factual closure does not occur either. Emotional closure is direct and reciprocal; epistemic closure remains incomplete and cannot be repaired after death.

Bereavement then tests the pair outside project success. Chizuru's controlled smile and duty initially make need easy to underestimate. Kazuya seeks help from Sumi, converts her experiential lesson into a funded ten-hour booking, and eventually recognizes that activity has not removed grief. His revised ideal-girlfriend speech permits sadness without demanding touch. Chizuru approaches, collapses against him, cries for both grandparents, and later tells Mini that the release relieved loneliness she had experienced as nearly unbearable. The support is effective in that acute episode, while her non-boyfriend classification remains explicit.

### Transaction and intimacy become more differentiated

The block adds several distinct terms rather than one smooth movement from paid to private. Film planning is unbooked but project-governed. Campaign money is public and conditional. Shared travel and one room are production-scoped, with separate futons and explicit cost reasoning. The grief date is a paid ten-hour booking, but payment does not price crying or bodily reliance. Chizuru later returns money because she regards the crying as professional failure; Kazuya says the unperformed response was the valuable part.

V020 then adds an ordinary café meeting and inexpensive meal initiated outside the platform, project, or emergency. Chizuru asks whether Kazuya likes her, yet later restores the rental classification. These forms coexist. Unpriced time is a durable access gain, not automatic couplehood.

Consent evidence also becomes more precise. Kazuya refuses Ruka's sexual pressure and later differentiates sunscreen, a blocked hug, first-name permission, an overnight-trip refusal, a local bath outing, and a five-second embrace. Ruka's V020 timed request and his verbal permission are a better consent-form case than her earlier unilateral kisses. One negotiated act does not erase those violations, establish broad reform, or settle the nonreciprocal trial.

### Romantic information advances farther than official status

Kazuya's preference moves through several audiences and levels of directness: support independent of lover status, a shouted preference of uncertain reception, explicit internal love, and finally speech addressed to Chizuru. She asks twice whether he likes her after citing Mini, Ruka, the customer relation, and his grief speech. Kazuya says that speech was sincere, identifies Mizuhara as his ideal girlfriend, and begins saying he has felt something toward her for a long time. Kibe interrupts before the proposition is complete.

Chizuru hears enough to replay the words alone and say she must do something. She later tells Kazuya nothing happened when he raises the exchange before her campus friends. The durable change is partly shared romantic information and a direct question. The official relationship remains unresolved because the confession is incomplete and receives no answer.

### Intermediaries become both productive and dangerous

Mini is indispensable to campaign analysis and logistics, but she also uses deception to engineer the two-person location trip and discloses Kazuya's preference without authorization. The disclosure eventually prompts Chizuru's direct question in V020. Its usefulness does not retroactively authorize it.

Mami's route develops differently. After extended inactivity, her earlier research into the Kinoshita family becomes operational through Kibe. She pitches Nagomi a smartphone-support concept for older users, frames it as future work, asks Kazuya to keep their past private, and schedules follow-up. The business proposal can be genuine, instrumental, or both. The evidence supports access and opacity, not a specific sabotage or reunion plan.

## Cumulative interpretation through V020

### Progress occurs across dimensions rather than through one status label

The official Kazuya-Chizuru relation is still not a mutually acknowledged private romance. Treating that fact as total stasis would discard durable changes in knowledge, work, trust, grief access, ordinary contact, and direct speech. Treating those changes as completed romance would discard the rental classification, Ruka obligation, incomplete confession, and absent reciprocal answer. The cumulative model therefore remains domain-specific: production and support show major gains; truth is partial; romantic information advances; official status does not.

### Commercial form remains causally real without deciding authenticity

Across twenty volumes, payment governs scheduling, professional obligations, pricing, cancellation, extension, and privacy. It never provides a universal test for sincere or insincere feeling. Chizuru can exercise skill and personal agency inside a booking; Kazuya can use paid access without purchasing emotional disclosure; unbooked care can still be bounded by project, family, or practical purpose. V019-V020 make this distinction unusually explicit by moving from paid grief support to refund dispute and then ordinary unpaid time.

### Deception is a changing information network

The originating girlfriend lie spreads across family, friends, campus, work, clients, providers, and digital systems. Local truth can repair Kuribayashi without correcting Nagomi. Chizuru can tell Sayuri that the pair are not dating without explaining every payment and performance. Mini can know Chizuru's grief report while Kazuya does not. Chizuru can know Kazuya's partly delivered intent while Ruka does not. Mami can enter a family-business route while Nagomi lacks the former-partner context. The correct unit of analysis is who knows which proposition under which audience, not whether “the truth” is globally present.

### Agency and responsibility remain separable from good outcome

Kazuya's film and grief-support work show substantial planning, revision, persistence, and care. They do not erase unsafe climbing, costly overcommitment, harassment history, family concealment, or failure to resolve Ruka fairly. Chizuru's boundary control, work discipline, project leadership, partial truth attempt, and grief initiative are strong agency evidence. They do not make every concealment informed or reveal a private romantic answer. Ruka's labor, sympathy, and improved V020 request form are real. They do not cancel false status claims or nonconsensual kisses. Mini's operational value does not authorize manipulation. The checkpoint preserves these mixed records rather than awarding moral totals.

### Visual and serial form produces delay with retained change

Screens, platform counters, messages, theater space, projector light, money envelopes, clothing, rings, transit, food, and interrupted speech carry relationship information. Serial interruptions repeatedly prevent a clean endpoint, but the interrupted material is not erased: a film is completed after many partial milestones; grief returns after cheerful activities; an unanswered question produces a personally identifying statement; the unfinished sentence remains in Chizuru's private replay. Repetition therefore often stores changed information inside familiar delay.
