# Tokyo 7th Sisters analytical method

Read the Japanese game as an ensemble narrative with state, knowledge and performance constraints. This method implements approved specification 1.1. It governs source consumption and judgment; architecture owns cumulative destinations and promotion. It establishes no literary findings.

## G. Recommended reading/execution strategy

### Alternatives evaluated

| Strategy | Benefit | Cost/failure at this corpus scale | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Equal-depth episode essays | Simple episode accountability | At least 1,350 essays; source sizes range from a few records to nearly 2,000; fragments causal units | Reject as default |
| B. Spine first, routed Sub/Event | Preserves narrative foundations and controls workload | Can miss ordinary-life evidence if routing is optional or based only on prominence | Adopt its spine discipline, add mandatory coverage accounting |
| C. Global semantic screening first | Broad relevance map and efficient shared discovery | Exposes later revelations before prospective reading; encourages shallow mass summary | Use later within admitted horizons, not as unrestricted first pass |
| D. Hybrid chronology/character routing | Combines coherent story reading, reusable screening, and state-specific retrieval | Requires an explicit coverage ledger and horizon checks | **Primary model** |
| E. Character-first dossier mining | Fast access to popular-character material | Loses scene causality, ensemble context, and unknown-speaker evidence | A later retrieval operation, not the sequential foundation |

Observed Main episodes contain 7–1,972 text records, Sub 3–265, and Event 10–198. Therefore the fundamental sequential unit is a **bounded causal block**: a coherent chapter, event, sub-arc, or causal phase with a recoverable entering/exiting state. It may contain one episode, several episodes, or a justified section of a very large episode. Do not cross witness boundaries or conceal a perspective/time reset simply to hit a target length.

Initial block proposals use native family/chapter envelopes and source-size metadata. Actual causal boundaries are confirmed while consuming the authorized material; later plot is not read merely to precompute an elegant schedule. If a large episode is split, all document/page ranges, including non-dialogue and inline material, must remain accounted for before the episode is marked reconstructed. No fixed number of episodes or words is imposed.

### Coverage and routing contract

1. Inventory all 1,578 scenario resources and the complete supplemental namespaces structurally. Initialize exact source membership, not findings.
2. Close-read all 364 Main episodes over coherent blocks, covering every native family. Complete the entire 2034 major story structure—including its remaining Main families, routed non-Main obligations, era reconstruction, literary synthesis, declared character layer, warranted longitudinal syntheses, audiovisual requirements, and completion audit—before any semantic consumption of 2053. Within each structure, schedule prequels, side-family Main, and parallel lines through explicit dependency/exposure decisions.
3. Semantically screen **all 755 Sub and 231 Event episodes**, eventually covering complete native envelopes within admitted horizons. Reuse each screen across character/unit/theme queries; do not independently rediscover an event for each subject.
4. Route complete additional scripts and supplemental tranches into narrative, ordinary-life, presentation, system, duplicate-occurrence, or unresolved roles. Hash-identical resources retain logical occurrences and contextual differences.
5. Give each substantive story a factual reconstruction home. Compact ordinary-life mini-cases may share a bounded portfolio reading; complex or load-bearing scenes receive full deep reading. Screening alone does not satisfy reconstruction.
6. Revisit deferred material after the relevant foundation/state becomes available; before an era or character closeout, audit gaps and negative evidence across the full eligible pool.

Routing dimensions are independent: **narrative change, character/relationship value, ordinary-life value, formal/thematic value, and AV need**. Use `DEEP_BLOCK`, `TARGETED_CASE`, `ORDINARY_LIFE_CASE`, `CONTEXT_ONLY`, `SYSTEM_OR_PRESENTATION`, and `DEFERRED_PENDING_FOUNDATION` as dispositions, with a reason and evidence scope. They are workload decisions, not a hierarchy of human or literary importance. A mundane interaction can be mandatory for a reconstruction model and require no standalone essay.

The coverage ledger records a vector for each source: `inventoried`, `admitted_horizon`, `screened`, `narratively_reconstructed`, `closely_analyzed`, `integrated_into_state`, `AV_review_state`, `remaining_obligation`, and canonical artifact route. `NOT_APPLICABLE` requires a reason; `NOT_SCREENED` and `DEFERRED` are not completion. Preserve holes as explicit sets, not only a highest episode number. Shared-source discovery and each subject's integration status are separate fields.

### Prospective discipline

Before foundations, permitted corpus-wide reconnaissance is limited to schema, counts, hashes, IDs, native hierarchy, source-size and capability metadata. Titles themselves may reveal information; record that exposure. Do not search the whole future corpus for character dialogue, revelations, emotional keywords, or relationship outcomes.

Every reading declares `entering_knowledge`, admitted source IDs/ranges, retrospective sources allowed, and any prior exposure. Use a bounded packet/fresh context for prospective work. Preserve its conclusions at closure. Later retrieval creates explicitly retrospective integrations and claim revisions; it does not rewrite the earlier “known then” state.

Pre-bootstrap design inspection saw source labels, a small number of technical dialogue examples, prior playback records, and external chronology material containing plot discussion. A future reader must not claim a pristine first encounter on that basis. Carry an exposure note and distinguish prospective method from subjective novelty. An independent context receives only the admitted source packet and necessary prior state; it cannot erase a human reader's existing knowledge.

Once a foundation is established, global retrieval within its admitted witness/state horizon is encouraged. Out-of-horizon matches remain hidden or explicitly quarantined until a retrospective operation authorizes their use.

### Major-story-structure transition discipline

Treat native Main groupings `2034年` and `2053年` as separate analytical stacks, `T7S_STACK_2034` and `T7S_STACK_2053`, without treating either label as the date of every contained scene. Maintain an independent source frontier, semantic horizon, state horizon, and completion state for each stack. The project-wide sequential lock can remain open while a later-stack transition gate is closed.

Before the 2034 era-completion audit passes, permitted 2053 access is limited to non-semantic topology reconnaissance: IDs, hashes, category-qualified native hierarchy, counts, source size, media capability, and exposed titles when unavoidable. Do not read or search 2053 dialogue, plot, characterization, outcomes, performed content, or thematic material; do not use it to select, explain, validate, or complete a 2034 claim or character account.

The 2034 closeout operation must declare its exact Main and eligible non-Main pools; complete every required factual and interpretive home; produce the era-level narrative reconstruction and literary synthesis; finish the declared principal-character monographs with representative visual and actually listened-to performed-voice integration; resolve or explicitly classify secondary subjects; promote independently warranted relationship, unit, institutional, and specialist responsibilities; reconcile chronology and all cumulative ledgers; and pass the 2034 era-completion audit. An unresolved but intentionally open story outcome may survive that audit; an unexamined required source or missing analytical responsibility may not.

Only after the entrypoint records `T7S_STACK_2034 = ERA_RELEASE_COMPLETE` may `T7S_STACK_2053` become `ELIGIBLE_FOR_PREREQUISITE_AUDIT`. Eligibility is not authorization. The first 2053 packet still requires owner authorization, fresh source verification, a prospective prerequisite audit, independent line frontiers, and a bounded entering state. Any later 2053-to-2034 connection is retrospective evidence and must use an explicit typed bridge rather than silently altering the frozen 2034-only release.

## H. Detailed narrative-reconstruction model

**Default artifact:** `T7S_B0001_DEEP_READING.md`, with immutable block ID and explicit native source membership. Keep factual reconstruction and interpretation together in clearly labeled sections. Split into a narrative record plus an interpretation document only when independent retrieval or size makes the combined artifact unmanageable; declare the transferred responsibility and route the former home to the new one.

| Layer | Canonical responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Native source | Exact dialogue, commands, values, and source occurrences |
| Bounded narrative reconstruction | Concrete local events, motivations as evidenced, decisions, information movement, consequences, and unresolved situation |
| Deep reading | Interpretation of how that bounded material works; rivals and uncertainties |
| Causal story ledger | Cumulative event skeleton, dependencies, world consequences, thread status, and pointers to detailed reconstruction |
| Arc/era narrative synthesis | Compact causal/state reconstruction of a completed storyline across blocks, with the bounded interpretation needed to explain its result and limits |
| Arc deep reading | Optional sustained whole-arc literary/formal interpretation when cross-block comparison adds knowledge that the compact narrative synthesis cannot responsibly contain; never a competing state or local-reconstruction home |
| Major-story-structure narrative reconstruction | Complete causal/state integration of one native top-level story structure after its arcs, routed non-Main obligations, chronology, character roster, and promoted longitudinal responsibilities reconcile |
| Full-series narrative integration | Cross-structure connections between completed 2034 and 2053 releases, chronology alternatives, continuity boundaries, and routes into detailed era reconstructions |

Every substantive block must preserve the starting situation; participants and identities as known; location/context; goals; initiating conditions; consequential actions and decisions; important revelations/dialogue in concise paraphrase; misunderstandings; conflicts/reversals; commitments; failures and repairs; ending situation; immediate and durable consequences; open/closed threads; and dependencies on prior material. Retrospectively identified later dependencies are labeled as later annotations.

Use event IDs such as `EV-B0001-001`, independent of diegetic time. Every load-bearing event assertion has a locator or bounded evidence range. Distinguish an explicit cause from a participant's explanation and from an analyst's inference. Preserve uncertainty over participants, offscreen causes, and time. A summary such as “the group grows closer” is insufficient when the source establishes specific actions, misreadings, and repair.

The narrative portion uses `SOURCE-ESTABLISHED NARRATIVE FACT` and separately tagged `ANALYTICAL INFERENCE`; thematic arguments use `THEMATIC INTERPRETATION`. A reported belief is a fact about that report, not necessarily a fact about the world. Interpretive rejection must leave the factual sequence independently usable.

**End-of-block handoff: “What is now true that was not true before?”**

| Handoff field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| World/situation | New constraints, opportunities, institutional or environmental changes |
| Character | State transitions and unchanged bounds; avoid trait inflation |
| Relationship | A→B and B→A changes, obligations, trust, conflict, repair |
| Epistemic | Who learned/believed/misunderstood what, by which route; distinguish reader knowledge |
| Unit/group | Membership, roles, leadership, identity, resources, public/private changes |
| Plot threads | Opened, advanced, closed, or still unresolved, with event IDs |
| Dependencies | New causal/prerequisite edges and already evidenced next pressures |
| Revisions and uncertainty | Claim operations, rival explanations, unanswered questions, required AV checks |
| Closeout | Exact source coverage, updated ledger IDs, verified endpoint, next candidate within authorized scope |

“No supported change” is a valid value; blank fields are not assumed negative evidence. Side-story portfolios retain separate mini-case event chains and uncertain placement instead of pretending their source order describes one continuous day.

Consolidate at the completion of a coherent native sub-arc/season or independently justified storyline, then at major-story-structure closure after its parallel lines, character layer, and consequential supplemental obligations are reconciled. A 91-episode family may need several causal-phase readings before one family synthesis; no arbitrary every-five-episodes checkpoint is required. After that compact narrative synthesis exists, architecture may separately promote an arc deep reading when the completed scope sustains independent whole-arc literary work. Both consume the same reconciled substrate, but the narrative synthesis remains the publication prerequisite and authoritative route for causal/state closeout. The complete 2034 era pair and audit are mandatory before 2053; they are not optional arc promotions or franchise-wide substitutes.

## I. T7S analytical method

This method owns how to read and judge evidence: the bounded contract in H, horizon discipline in G, source distinctions in the evidence protocol (E), the AV decision procedure in N, and the following lenses. Lenses are prompts for relevant inquiry, not mandatory equal-length sections.

| Lens | Questions and required distinctions |
| --- | --- |
| Narrative and agency | Who initiates, chooses, resists, enables, or bears consequences? What is shown versus withheld? |
| Psychology and self-concept | Goals, values, coping, self-description versus conduct, contradictions, developmental conditions |
| Epistemic and relational | Knowledge provenance, asymmetric expectations, address, dependence, authority, repair |
| Idol/professional life | Private conduct, public persona, stage role, creative labor, management, institutional incentives |
| Ensemble and legacy | Membership, division of labor, collective identity, rivalry, mentorship, inheritance without erasing individual differences |
| Ordinary life and language | Humor, initiative, tastes, mundane competence, irritation, assistance, casual conflict, register and recipient-conditioned speech |
| Form and presentation | Theme/motif, point of view, visual framing, voice, music, performance-as-action where evidenced |
| Continuity and revision | Time/witness bounds, causal alternatives, counterexamples, and changes to prior claims |

A reading is complete only after source coverage, factual reconstruction, interpretation/rivals, state handoff, ledger synchronization, and material evidence limits are verified. Load-bearing AV uncertainty must be resolved or the dependent claim withheld/downgraded explicitly; unrelated text-supported findings may close with a bounded AV queue.

Sequential readings also feed later multimodal character work: record important visual/presentation observations, performed-voice observations when actually listened to, candidate character-defining visual/vocal patterns, counterexamples, and any required escalation in the claim/evidence ledger. Keep observed features separate from their interpretation and preserve exact native/media routes. A later monograph integrates this accumulated evidence; it does not postpone all AV investigation until its final maturity review.

| Claim class | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `SOURCE_FACT` | Source-established event, wording, explicit command, or attributed report |
| `BOUNDED_INFERENCE` | Supported interpretation within declared evidence/state conditions |
| `THEMATIC_INTERPRETATION` | An argument about meaning/form, separately revisable from factual reconstruction |
| `WORKING_HYPOTHESIS` | A live explanation awaiting discrimination from rivals |
| `RECONSTRUCTION_RULE` | Conditional behavioral rule supported and tested within a model boundary |
| `EXTRAPOLATION` | Extension beyond observed configurations, with explicit distance and uncertainty |
| `OPEN` / `UNKNOWN` | Unresolved question / presently unavailable answer; neither means false |

Revision operations are `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT`, and `OPEN`. Record old/new formulation, trigger, counterevidence, scope change, affected artifact IDs, and downstream repair. Do not quietly turn recurring conduct into a universal trait or retroactively make an earlier prediction correct.

Use the repository's stable workload classes: bounded source/locator audits `BOUNDED_STANDARD`; normal readings and established ledger updates `SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS`; mature character, relationship, chronology, and era integration `DEEP_SYNTHESIS`; propagation-sensitive final/adversarial work may warrant `PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST` with justification. Literal models, controls, and prices require the then-current provider verification; this method supplies no new product mapping. ## N. Audiovisual/performance integration

**Decision:** claim-driven review with bounded representative baselines. No exhaustive human review of 69,520 WAVs and 25,688 images is required.

| Voice/presentation channel | Evidence requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Linguistic voice | Native wording/register and adjacent turns |
| Performed voice | Listen to the correctly routed recording; record delivery, pauses, intensity, and uncertainty as observations |
| Relational voice | Compare the same subject across recipients and contexts |
| Embodied presentation | Inspect relevant sprite/body/face/CG/background, with native occurrence and state semantics |
| Stage/performance voice | Relevant song/performance edition and public-role context; do not substitute casual speech samples |

| Priority | Trigger | Completion action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AV-A` mandatory for dependent claim | Timing, irony/delivery, gesture, musical synchronization, disputed audiovisual fact, or performance is load-bearing | Resolve the diagnostic evidence, or withhold/downgrade that claim and record the remaining limit |
| `AV-B` targeted | A particular ambiguity or comparison could materially alter the reading/model | Review exact linked lines/assets and enough surrounding context |
| `AV-C` representative | Establish a character/era/role baseline or test apparent regularity | Sample contrasting ordinary, social-pressure, and public/stage contexts as available; expand for anomalies |
| `AV-D` text/native-state sufficient | Wording, explicit event order, or a native state claim is already adequately supported | Record why no further AV review is needed |

Default bounded reading inspects the complete text/native page context and checks relevant presentation references for escalation. It does not require listening to all lines. A character's first reconstruction baseline should include a small, contrastive voice/presentation sample when that capability is claimed; three to five clips is a starting heuristic, not a pass quota or completeness assertion.

For `MONOGRAPH_MATURE`, AV-C representative sampling is normally sufficient to establish stable visual/vocal baselines. AV-B addresses particular visual/vocal features that materially affect character interpretation; AV-A applies to load-bearing performance, staging or timing claims. AV-D remains valid for adequately supported textual/native-state claims, but does not waive the monograph's visual and performed-voice maturity reviews. Maturity requires deliberate, defensible review and integration, not inspection of every image, card, WAV or sprite, and not a percentage-of-assets score.

Queue entries carry claim/question ID, locator, asset/cue candidates, required modality, priority, selection rationale, context window, status, reviewer/tool, outcome, and claim revision. Store them in the claim/evidence ledger. Machine audio features, retrieval scores, and clusters are annotations/proxies; they do not by themselves establish emotion, motive, or performed characterization.

**Four evidence levels remain explicit:** native page value → derived effective state → interpreted transition → proven runtime observation. Page co-reference does not establish simultaneous playback, cue onset, duration, overlap, or mix. If exact behavior matters, inspect a bounded emulator recording or other primary runtime evidence with build/settings/branch and timing provenance. Source movies may be reviewed directly where they contain the required continuous evidence. A stitched asset slideshow is not runtime proof.

Visual classification records scene CG, character body/face, background, card illustration, standing art, portrait, promotional/system, and title/menu roles where supported. Card art can support costume, motif, branding, public persona, or aesthetic identity; it is not automatically a literal event. Labels such as “angry” or “affectionate” remain analyst annotations unless a native field actually supplies that semantic label.

### Representative visual character identity

Use the existing canonical image corpus and image browser to construct a subject- and horizon-qualified selection, checked against native identity/card/resource routes. Inspect the selected images themselves, retaining exact canonical asset IDs and native provenance. Treat cards and associated illustrations as a **longitudinal illustrative corpus**, not a list that requires a description of every card.

| Visual responsibility | Representative review and contrasts |
| --- | --- |
| Stable visual identity | Core design, silhouette, hairstyle, recurring palette, distinctive clothing/design elements, accessories/props, and differentiators from other characters |
| Context-dependent presentation | Ordinary/casual, private, professional/idol, unit, stage/performance, seasonal/special-event, and major developmental/era presentation where supported |
| Card/illustrative history | Earliest/base presentation where established; major costume families, roles, presentational modes, unit contexts, source-grounded narrative associations, recurring motifs, later presentation and exceptional deviations |
| Sprite/body/face presentation | Material recurring body presentation, representative face variants and major visual states; relationship/situation conditioning only where evidence supports it |
| Integrated visual interpretation | Costume patterns, private/idol contrasts, unit visual language, iconography and aesthetic presentation; distinguish recurring features from changes over time |

Select across analytically distinct modes rather than random images or volume. Retain unknown chronology as unknown; file/card order cannot establish periods or development. Test proposed stable features against common alternative costumes/presentations and notable deviations. Record selection rationale, covered modes, missing modes, counterexamples and limits in the existing evidence infrastructure.

Stop when the material contrasts and counterexamples have been examined and additional examples produce diminishing analytical returns, unless a specific claim or anomaly warrants AV-B/AV-A follow-up. No fixed image count establishes representativeness. An attractive selection or large card collection is not a maturity audit.

Label **`OBSERVED VISUAL FEATURE`** separately from **`ANALYTICAL INTERPRETATION`**. Native numeric variants or filenames do not establish “angry,” “embarrassed,” “affectionate,” or “anxious”; such meanings remain attributed analytical annotations unless native evidence supplies them. Keep card, promotional and other representational imagery distinct from proven literal narrative occurrence.

### Representative performed voice

Actual audio is required for performed-voice review. Native Japanese dialogue establishes linguistic behavior—vocabulary, pronouns, address, politeness, syntax, sentence endings, lexical tendencies and conversational structure—but does not establish how a line is delivered. Listen to correctly resolved samples with surrounding narrative context, preserving exact cue/sheet, source occurrence and canonical audio routes; unresolved cue identity cannot support a confident character-performance claim.

Use contrastive sampling, where available, across ordinary/neutral conversation, low-stakes casual interaction, a major relationship partner, comedy/embarrassment, medium-stakes disagreement or pressure, vulnerability/emotional stress, high-stakes conflict/crisis, public/professional/idol-facing speech, and stage/performance voice when relevant. Cover materially different states and recipients within the claimed horizon and seek departures that could falsify the proposed baseline; not every category must exist for every subject.

The goal is a **stable vocal envelope + relationship/context-conditioned variation + meaningful departures from baseline**. Where defensible, describe pitch tendency, vocal weight, tempo, articulation, breath, pauses, sentence-final delivery, intensity, softness, hesitation, laughter, emotional leakage, and public/private, recipient- or stress-conditioned variation. Identify auditory observations and interpretations separately; use numerical measurements only when actually measured with a documented method. Neither transcript wording nor machine features alone substitute for listening.

Record the selection basis, heard evidence, counterexamples, limits and integration into character interpretation. Stop on defensible coverage and diminishing returns, reopening review for material anomalies or claims; WAV counts and listening percentages do not establish maturity. The question is how vocal performance instantiates the fictional character. **Seiyuu performance is not seiyuu personality**: biography or presumed personal traits of the actor are not evidence of the character's psychology. State the song/stage boundary rather than require song analysis for every character.

## O. Music/song/performance treatment

Music dramaturgy is a **day-one method responsibility invoked selectively**, with a later specialist home when warranted. A reading records meaningful music/performance actions and unresolved evidence needs; it need not analyze a song in every story.

Possible functions to test, not preassigned T7S findings: identity/public self-definition; development or crisis; mature-state expression; relational address; inheritance/legacy; performance-as-proof; unit re-founding. Distinguish a song's text, speaker/persona, composition/arrangement, recording, in-story performance, game edit, MV, and live realization.

The existing `m_live_music_meta.json` has **215 nonempty lyric entries**; the inspected values contain Japanese text and line breaks. Its SHA-256 is `0960e010321f8ec6486f4a1d7cab10daa43de64160f1fc3108b398b5f69e2606`. This enables selective lyric analysis now, after horizon admission. It does not establish complete album/studio lyrics, coverage of every song, or exact sung timing. The `m_live_music` fields include song/artist identities, ACB reference, length, and preview offset; join by verified music ID rather than filename resemblance.

Lyrics should be referenced through the master-file row/key and field pointer. They are not currently a dedicated SQLite lyric table and must not be presumed included in `supplemental_text`. Preserve source line breaks; use bounded quotations only when analytically needed. No bulk lyrics or recordings enter Git.

Future ingestion is question-driven: an official lyric edition to resolve a variant/missing passage; an exact full recording to assess arrangement or delivery; a source MV or live recording for visual/stage claims; synchronized runtime evidence for scene music timing. Record edition, performer, credits, duration, hashes, and alignment limits. Unavailable evidence bounds the music claim rather than blocking unrelated narrative work.
