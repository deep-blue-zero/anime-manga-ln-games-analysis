# Tokyo 7th Sisters synthesis architecture

This V1 architecture implements the approved 1.1 design and its multimodal monograph amendment. It is the current project method for accumulating and integrating analysis on this branch, subject to repository integration. No prior T7S analytical architecture is superseded; the local design remains historical proposal provenance. The initial source scope is the preserved Japanese offline game, with separately admitted future witnesses.

Current route: [CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md](../CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md). Governing pair: [T7S_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md](T7S_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) and [T7S_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md](T7S_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md). Source recovery: [T7S_EVIDENCE_SUBSTRATE_AND_LOCATOR_PROTOCOL.md](T7S_EVIDENCE_SUBSTRATE_AND_LOCATOR_PROTOCOL.md).

## Dependencies, extension and authority

This architecture follows the repository's [project-initiation policy](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md), [authority and supersession policy](../../../governance/source-policies/ARCHIVE_AUTHORITY_AND_SUPERSESSION_POLICY.md), [sequential-execution scope policy](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_SEQUENTIAL_EXECUTION_SCOPE_AND_CONTINUATION_POLICY.md), and [change-integration checklist](../../../governance/policies/CHANGE_INTEGRATION_CHECKLIST.md). Current repository controls take precedence over historical examples. The source/extraction workspace remains outside Git analytical authority.

The operational dependency chain is: source lock and locator checks → exact source/horizon admission → bounded reading with factual reconstruction and interpretation → synchronized causal/entity/claim state → coverage and contradiction reconciliation → justified character, relationship, unit and specialist synthesis → coherent arc/era reconstruction → compact full-series narrative integration and separate literary synthesis → fidelity/role-gap/release audit. Smaller bounded specialists may develop during reading when their evidence is sufficient; final integration must reconcile their scopes and rivals. Reconstruction validation is independent of literary integration and cannot certify the latter.

Anticipated specialist responsibilities are idol/professional institutions and creative labor; public/private/stage identity; intergenerational inheritance; ensemble coordination and rivalry; music/performance dramaturgy; visual and Japanese linguistic form; and cross-witness comparison after admission. These are questions to test, not findings or mandatory empty documents. Initially preserve evidence in the three ledgers and readings. Promote a specialist only when recurrent evidence and an independent argument or retrieval burden justify it; record whether the responsibility becomes mandatory for the declared release.

Add or split a canonical home when a recurrent dimension cannot be represented adequately, has independent ownership/retrieval needs, or approaches eligibility limits. Record the reason, old/new responsibilities, preserved stable IDs, transferred material, route updates, downstream effects and any material backfill in the entrypoint and architecture history. Backfill only demonstrable evidence gaps. Do not retain two current copies or cosmetically normalize mature documents. Review at first block/first supplemental portfolio, major arc/era closeout, new witness, material contradiction, before specialist integration and before release.

Authority status (`canonical`, `active_provisional`, `draft_noncurrent`, `superseded`, `historical_legacy`) is separate from architecture lifecycle (`INITIAL`, `EVOLVING`, `STABILIZED`, `FROZEN`), source completeness, R0–R5, monograph maturity and execution authorization. Current entrypoint/ledgers are maintained through targeted edits to verified full contents. Prospective reading bodies and release audit observations freeze at closeout; later corrections are explicit errata or successor decisions. Git history preserves before-images. Supersession requires complete authority metadata, successor routes and repair of affected current citations.

## J. synthesis architecture

`T7S_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md` owns artifact responsibilities, dependencies, promotion, revision propagation, and completion. Its governing rule is one current entrypoint and one canonical home for each responsibility.

| Responsibility | Initial owner | Mature owner, if promoted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native facts and media identity | External source corpus | Same source corpus/witness bindings |
| Source-family chronology and reading dependencies | Topology/chronology document | Same, with source-backed revisions |
| Local factual causality and detailed reconstruction | Bounded reading | Same; arc/era synthesis integrates rather than replaces details |
| Event chronology, causal dependencies, world state | Story chronology/causal ledger | Same; era synthesis provides navigable explanation |
| Character, epistemic, relationship, unit state | Typed entity-state ledger | Same current state rows; history may move to an explicitly promoted state-history home |
| Current interpretive claims, themes/motifs, counterevidence | Claim/evidence ledger | Specialist arguments cite the same claim IDs |
| Character interpretation | Relevant readings/claim rows | Character monograph owns the integrated textual, linguistic, visual and performed-voice argument; textual readiness and multimodal maturity are separate promotion states |
| Conditional reconstruction | Entity readiness and bounded rules | Character reconstruction model with model version and horizon |
| Relationship mechanism / collective identity | Typed relationship/unit rows | Relationship or unit synthesis |
| Voice, visuals, performance findings | Claim rows with modality provenance and review status | Character monograph integrates character-identity findings; a specialist synthesis is promoted only for independent analytical/retrieval responsibility |
| Cross-era/generational causality | Event links and era reconstructions | Compact full-series narrative integration |
| Final literary/thematic argument | Converged lower-level claims | Full-series literary synthesis, separate from narrative reconstruction |

The mature narrative layer should consist of **several coherent arc/era reconstructions plus a shorter `T7S_FULL_SERIES_NARRATIVE_RECONSTRUCTION.md` integration**. It should preserve parallel lines, uncertainty, and retrospective revelations. `T7S_FULL_SERIES_LITERARY_SYNTHESIS.md` owns final interpretation. Neither file is a repository for unfinished local work.

The method and architecture must state a promotion/migration rule: move responsibility once, preserve stable IDs and history, add explicit routing/supersession, and stop maintaining the old duplicate content. A generated index is a projection, not another current authority.

The monograph's `MULTIMODAL CHARACTER IDENTITY` responsibility remains inside `T7S_<SUBJECT>_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md`. An evidence index routes textual, visual and audio evidence but does not own the integrated interpretation. Separate visual or voice monographs are not mandatory artifacts; later specialist promotion still requires independent responsibility or retrieval burden.

## K. longitudinal ledgers

**Three cumulative ledgers are day-one mandatory.** Typed records preserve distinct responsibilities without ten largely empty documents.

| Ledger | Why cumulative / required contents | Excluded responsibility | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `T7S_STORY_CHRONOLOGY_AND_CAUSAL_STATE_LEDGER.md` | Event IDs; block/native family; witness/time/confidence; prerequisites; participants; initiating condition; action; immediate/durable consequence; knowledge/state delta references; opened/closed threads; later dependencies; source locators | Full scene prose belongs to readings; family-level source topology belongs to F | Append event history; revise current ordering/thread status with explicit correction history |
| `T7S_ENTITY_STATE_LEDGER.md` | Separately typed character, epistemic, directional relationship, unit/institution records; native identity crosswalk; state transitions; separate reconstruction readiness, monograph maturity and capability coverage by scope | Literary monograph prose, global character registration, raw master dumps | Maintain current rows and dated transition history; unknown initial story state remains unknown |
| `T7S_CLAIM_AND_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md` | Claims/rivals/counterevidence; theme/motif observations; revisions; evidence distance; voice/visual/music findings; AV queue and outcomes | Source extraction authority, complete transcript snippets, duplicate narrative summaries | Stable claim/review IDs; append revisions and update current formulations through targeted edits |

Entity-state record types stay logically separate even when housed together:

- `CHARACTER_STATE`: developmental state, goals/values, agency, coping, habits, public/private/stage and professional roles.
- `EPISTEMIC_STATE`: proposition, knower, belief status, acquisition route/time, remembered history, uncertainty; separate reader knowledge.
- `RELATIONSHIP_STATE`: ordered subject→recipient, reciprocal counterpart link, expectations, trust/dependence, knowledge asymmetry, obligations and repair.
- `UNIT_STATE`: membership and roles by interval, authority/resources, shared identity, public image, internal tensions and inter-unit links.
- `IDENTITY_ROUTE` and `READINESS`: source/native aliases to local analytical subject; independently recorded `reconstruction_readiness`, `monograph_maturity`, and capability fields defined in L, with source/state scope, review references, transition history and unresolved obligations. These are analytical routing/readiness metadata, not new fictional facts. The monograph's visible maturity declaration must match its referenced ledger decision; no separate maturity registry is created.

The story ledger answers what changed in the world; entity rows give the reusable conditioned state; claim rows explain what the analyst thinks it means. State deltas cross-reference event IDs rather than reproduce event prose.

Keep thematic, AV-review, voice/performance, and readiness records in these homes initially. Promote a dedicated ledger only when independent queries/ownership, repeated use across blocks, or persistent retrieval/size problems warrant the split. Document the transfer and preserve IDs; do not maintain two live copies.

## Monograph maturity and promotion


`T7S_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md` governs these monograph promotion rules, with review procedures in `T7S_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md`. The existing reconstruction protocol continues to govern the independent R0–R5 model-readiness system.

| `monograph_maturity` | Definition and permitted use |
| --- | --- |
| `MONOGRAPH_NOT_READY` | Evidence is too thin, state-confused, narrowly contextual, or poorly integrated to justify a maintained character monograph. Continue accumulating readings, entity state, claims and evidence routes. Prominence, popularity, dialogue volume, card count and cast centrality do not establish readiness. |
| `MONOGRAPH_READY_TEXTUAL` | A sustained, source-grounded textual/narrative interpretation justifies a maintained character monograph within an explicit witness and temporal/state horizon, while its audiovisual character-identity layer remains incomplete and visibly marked `PENDING_MULTIMODAL_MATURITY`. |
| `MONOGRAPH_MATURE` | The monograph meets the textual foundation and has integrated an analytically representative, source-grounded review of visual character identity, applicable card/illustrative presentation, and performed voice, with counterexamples, exact evidence routes, bounded material AV questions, and a passing multimodal fidelity audit for its declared scope. |

**Promotion to `MONOGRAPH_READY_TEXTUAL`:** a sustained interpretive responsibility and sufficiently mature narrative/developmental foundation must support a coherent account without invented evidence. Within the admitted horizon, cover the supported identity/background and developmental history; self-concept, values/priorities, goals/motives, fears/avoidance and coping/defenses; agency and ordinary-life behavior; low-, medium- and high-stakes contrasts; social masks and public/private/professional roles; major relationships and epistemic constraints; contradictions/counterevidence; Japanese linguistic voice/register; and temporal/state boundaries. Include ordinary-life and contrasting relational/context evidence wherever the source supplies it; record genuine gaps and limit the claimed scope. This is not a lesser-quality textual monograph: its textual interpretation is ready for a canonical character home under the normal authority rules. Outstanding visual and performed-voice review remains a visible maturity obligation.

**Promotion from `MONOGRAPH_READY_TEXTUAL` to `MONOGRAPH_MATURE`:** all of the following are required:

1. Complete representative visual-design review and, where applicable, longitudinal card/illustrative and material sprite/body/face review under N.
2. Complete representative performed-voice review through actual listening under N; transcript wording is insufficient.
3. Check important visual/vocal counterexamples and common alternative presentations across relevant contexts, recipients and developmental/era states.
4. Give every modality-dependent claim exact source/media routes. Resolve or explicitly bound AV-A/AV-B questions material to the thesis; a bound must narrow or withhold the unsupported claim, not retain the same claim with a disclaimer.
5. Integrate the findings into the character argument, including reinforcement, complication and contradiction across modalities; an attached image/audio inventory is insufficient.
6. Pass X's multimodal fidelity audit (`PASS` or `PASS_WITH_BOUND` with the supported scope explicit) and record the scope, evidence coverage, limits, audit reference and promotion decision in the existing readiness infrastructure.

Visual-identity review and performed-voice review are **both mandatory for `MONOGRAPH_MATURE` when sufficient preserved evidence exists**. Wholly unexamined major visual identity or performed voice cannot satisfy maturity. If evidence is insufficient for a defensible representative review, retain `MONOGRAPH_READY_TEXTUAL` with that source limit, or establish a defensibly narrower scope in which both reviews can be completed; do not manufacture evidence or waive the requirement by calling an available modality non-applicable. Textually bounded completion remains possible under X.

Every mature monograph must contain a dedicated section or clearly recoverable responsibility equivalent to **`MULTIMODAL CHARACTER IDENTITY`**. Integrate textual characterization, Japanese linguistic voice, visual design, illustrative/card presentation, material sprite/presentation grammar, performed voice, and public/stage identity where relevant, while keeping each claim's modality and evidentiary function distinct. At `MONOGRAPH_READY_TEXTUAL`, visibly mark this responsibility `PENDING_MULTIMODAL_MATURITY` and identify the outstanding work. Song/stage-performance analysis is required only when analytically material; the monograph must state its boundary.

Material changes to the thesis or source/state horizon reopen affected maturity checks. Preserve earlier bounded decisions in history rather than carrying their maturity label into a broader unreviewed scope.

## M. Relationship and unit architecture

| Responsibility | Default home | Promotion criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A→B expectations/behavior | Directional relationship-state row | Repeated development or disagreement across multiple blocks requires an independently retrieved history/mechanism |
| Mutual bond, rivalry, mentorship, management | Linked directional rows and local readings | A sustained asymmetric process cannot be explained adequately inside either character monograph |
| Unit membership/roles/resources | Unit-state rows by timeframe | Changing collective identity, leadership, division of labor, creative work, or public definition becomes a recurrent causal object |
| Inter-unit/generational relations | Linked unit/relationship rows and causal events | Cross-line inheritance, competition, or institutional change needs a dedicated synthesis |

Do not create a document for every pair or every native unit label. One consequential interaction can deserve a targeted case without a permanent relationship monograph. Promotion uses independent analytical responsibility and evidence density, not a numerical popularity threshold.

Character monographs own the individual's relational policies and developmental interpretation. Relationship syntheses own the dyad's asymmetric history and mechanisms. Unit syntheses own emergent collective identity and coordination. Shared scenes are reconstructed once and cited by all three. Member identity, stage branding, official publicity grouping, and actual diegetic unit membership remain distinct assertions.

## U. Later/promoted artifacts

Create readings only after OPEN. Create monographs/models, relationship/unit studies, specialist work, and arc/era/full-series syntheses only under their inventory triggers. Promotion is part of normal analysis, not a separate infrastructure campaign that must finish before the first reading.

A character monograph may be created and maintained at `MONOGRAPH_READY_TEXTUAL`, visibly carrying `PENDING_MULTIMODAL_MATURITY`. Promote the same artifact to `MONOGRAPH_MATURE` only through L/N/X's representative review, integration and audit. Accumulated reading-level visual/vocal findings feed this transition. No mandatory visual or voice specialist artifact is added; specialist promotion remains conditional on independent responsibility or retrieval burden.

Review proportionality after the first completed causal block and first meaningful Sub/Event portfolio. Revisit block size or ledger partitioning if causal detail is lost, retrieval repeatedly fails, updates conflict, or a file approaches eligibility limits. Preserve responsibility and identifiers during any split. Do not reorganize mature files just to resemble another series.

## Sequential initiation and execution gate

The current entrypoint alone records the live lock; the bootstrap record preserves its transitions. Before the first substantive sequential reading, require the owner-authorized root, current eligible method and synthesis architecture, immutable witness/source bindings, explicit chronology/identity/horizon rules, the exact coverage ledger and three initialized longitudinal homes, and verified source/locator/first-boundary recovery. Review the actual artifacts and record an affirmative OPEN transition. Absent, malformed, contradictory or CLOSED state fails closed; scaffolding and source completeness cannot substitute for verification.

OPEN establishes architectural readiness. It does not start reading or expand execution authorization. A later single-block request consumes only its exact admitted source range and closes the entire reading/state/claim/coverage transaction before naming the next candidate. Continuous execution requires an explicit bounded continuation scope. Completed visual/voice reviews and mature monographs are later promotion obligations, not prerequisites for opening this initiation gate.
