# T7S_B0128 — Let's Go to School!

## Result and entering boundary

Monaka receives a direct offer for a minor television-drama role described as a contemporary high-school girl. As a self-described freeter who did not enter high school while searching for what she wanted to do, she worries that she cannot play the role convincingly. Kazumi suggests looking inside a school, and Jedah and the others turn that suggestion into visits to Ferb's, Jedah's, Kazumi's and Musubi's schools. Monaka wears a school outfit but her curiosity and personal praise repeatedly displace formal observation: she talks at length with Ferb's fans, communicates with Jedah's friend through fashion and gestures, embarrasses Kazumi by publicizing costume details, and prompts a school idol-club president to plan an unapproved live at Musubi's school. Monaka thanks the group for seeing their school lives. A later report says the filmed role was a high-school student working in a café, with no school knowledge required. The reversal removes the stated production need, not the actual social experience or the costs it imposed on others. ## Factual reconstruction by actor knowledge

### EV-B0128-001 — acting offer and an incomplete model of school

The Player reports a direct but minor drama offer and describes Monaka's part as a present-day high-school girl. Monaka initially guesses a shop-worker role, then says she is a freeter and may not understand high-school life. Asked by Ferb, she says she did not go to high school because she wanted to find what she wished to do first, leaving school as a possible later step. Jedah compares this to work-and-study routes in her country, but her account is situated testimony, not a survey of either education system. Monaka wants a school's atmosphere, not a credential. Neither Monaka nor her friends yet know from this description that the scene will be at a café. [A:P2–31]

### EV-B0128-002 — an improvised tour with access risk

Kazumi proposes entering a school to observe directly. Musubi objects that outsiders could cause a commotion if discovered; Jedah offers peer support, and Monaka quickly decides to visit all their schools, including Musubi's. The chapter does not show requests to school staff, formal visitor permission or a production research arrangement. The three sites beyond Jedah's school are not established as generally open to outside visitors. “Infiltration” is the group's comic plan, not an audited authorization. [A:P32–45; B:P1–9]

### EV-B0128-003 — Ferb's school makes the idol visible

Monaka appears in a school outfit and Ferb guides the group to her own school. Two students recognize Ferb from online streams and praise her appearance and music. Monaka enthusiastically adds insider praise; Ferb becomes embarrassed, while Monaka keeps talking. The students propose a later conversation over live footage. Only after leaving does Musubi ask about observing the school, and Monaka realizes she had forgotten the stated purpose. Ferb's public recognition is demonstrated by these particular students, not by a measured school-wide reputation. [B:P1–35]

### EV-B0128-004 — Jedah's open-day model and a limited cross-language encounter

Jedah describes her international school as primarily online, with a monthly in-person day, and says the campus is open during daytime for students' self-study and exchange. She introduces the visitors to her school friend Jesse. Monaka, anxious about language, gives a comic false self-description; Jedah instead says they are her friends and she is guiding them. Jesse welcomes them. Jedah then invites Monaka to talk with her; the later exchange turns to fashion and shopping, helped by gestures according to Kazumi. This shows a specific welcomed encounter, not fluency in English, institutional permission for every visitor or a universal school model. [B:P37–81]

### EV-B0128-005 — Kazumi's private/public boundary breaks

Kazumi asks her to lower her voice; classmates overhear and speculate. Kazumi says she is leaving early, and Musubi interprets the moment as unbearable embarrassment. ### EV-B0128-006 — Musubi's school and the proposed live

Monaka playfully says they could sing a song to please her; Musubi explicitly says they will not. The club president heads toward the broadcast room to tell the school, while Musubi protests. The scene cuts before showing an announcement, staff response or live. Monaka's wish to reward a fan cannot authorize Musubi's participation, a school event or a broadcast. [C:P18–38]

## Interpretation and alternatives

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0128-001.** The acting brief motivates a search for a generic “high-school girl,” but the outing actually yields person-specific glimpses of four friends' school worlds. **ETHICAL_THEMATIC_INTERPRETATION — CL-B0128-002.** Monaka's open praise creates connection with Ferb's fans and Jesse, yet the same unfiltered attention exposes Kazumi's costume life and strains Musubi's school office. The chapter distinguishes friendly intent from permission to share, visit or improvise a public event. [B:P11–35,46–81; C:P1–38]

Rival: every encounter is harmless because it is played for comedy. Kazumi asks for discretion and leaves; Musubi refuses the live. Equally, treating all four visits as equivalent trespass ignores Jedah's reported open campus and Jesse's local welcome. Institutional access remains unverified at every site.

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0128-003.** Monaka's non-enrollment is a stated search for purpose with a possible later school path, while Jedah describes another attendance configuration. Neither testimony proves that school is unnecessary, that Monaka regrets her choice or that one national model is superior. [A:P20–31; B:P37–45]

Rival: Monaka lacks interest in learning or has permanently rejected education. She asks to experience a school and explicitly leaves later entry conceivable.

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0128-004.** The comic final reveal revises what knowledge the role required, not the observed social risks or costs. The idol-club president's sprint toward a broadcast room cannot be counted as a completed school live, and the drama shoot cannot be judged from Monaka's report of its setting. [C:P22–55]

Rival: Monaka's apology proves every peer was satisfied or the live occurred. The final exchange shows amusement and fatigue but no complete consent or event record.
