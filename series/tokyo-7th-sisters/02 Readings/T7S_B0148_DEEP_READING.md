# T7S_B0148 — Momoka of the Street

## Result and entering boundary

Momoka looks for income without idol labor, buys shares with parental allowance permission and turns to day trading. Her quick research makes peers treat her as newly knowledgeable, and the Player accepts her explanation of an unfamiliar commercial client. Trading across market hours then displaces sleep, food and lessons. After Momoka collapses on the way to Nanastar, a doctor attributes her condition to malnutrition and overwork; the doctor initially assumes the agency caused it, while Momoka says she had been glued to trading screens and skipping meals. Sawara reminds her that this “not working” scheme has become more work than the idol work she wished to avoid. Momoka says she quits, later reports closing the account and spending the resulting money on anime/games, then uses her recent illness to resist another assignment.
