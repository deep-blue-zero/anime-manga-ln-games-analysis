## Interpretation, rivals and retrospective use

**THEMATIC INTERPRETATION — CL-B0100-001.** The story transforms lateness from an apparent failure of belonging into a route by which belonging acquires a public. Crosscutting prevents the reader from simply sharing the venue's uncertainty: we know the encounters while the group knows only absence. The repeated names give the journey a specific relational destination, and Kajika's objection reciprocates it before Saori can explain herself. The positive result is not merely endurance rewarded: people encountered outside the performance become people who want to attend it. [EV-B0100-001–003]

The strongest rival is a straightforward good-deeds-rewarded comedy. It accounts for the returning beneficiaries and joyful reception, but cannot turn every encounter into virtuous intention: the theft collision and cat recovery are accidental. Nor can it explain away the unsupported fan-discovery route. The text organizes reciprocity across different degrees of agency; it does not demonstrate supernatural luck, karmic law or a replicable recruitment method.

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0100-002.** Saori's fear of having failed the sisters is not confirmed by their represented wish. Kajika's insistence on her presence and later inquiry about injury are substantive care, not merely an audience-growth calculation. Yet wanting a person present and adequately protecting her are distinct responsibilities. Her extravagant recovery assertion allows the reunion to proceed; it is not medical evidence. The Player's closing safety maxim supplies no visible procedure capable of preventing the risks already borne. [A:P48–57; B:P1–12,32–46]

**THEMATIC INTERPRETATION — CL-B0100-003.** The ambulance makes rescue and spectacle coincide. A vehicle associated with injury becomes the means of a triumphant entrance; Saori calls the coincidence lucky and the sisters initially read it as staging. The pleasure of this reversal is genuine within the comedy, while the bodily cost remains legible. A purely punitive reading would lose the desired reunion and community response; an uncomplicated resilience celebration would lose her hospital refusal and unsupported insistence on performing. Neither is adequate alone. [B:P23–46]
