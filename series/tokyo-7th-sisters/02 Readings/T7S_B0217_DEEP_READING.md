She invites the manager into a family encounter, gives matching decoration a role in remembering, presents a gift and proposes ways to spend time together. Her own certainty about his importance does not automatically supply his knowledge or agreement.

These are distinct conditional cases, not stages of an established relationship or a dated progression toward marriage.
