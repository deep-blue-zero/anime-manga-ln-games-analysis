# T7S_B0152 — Principal Principle

## Result and entering boundary

Susu sees a Kuonji-supported visiting ballet company, recognizes its changed choreography, and decides to enter a special local audition. She practices intensively; peers first worry about possible conflict between a company place and idol work, but she wants to test her dancing before deciding what an offer would mean. The group assists with runs, diet restraint and basic practice. At the audition, other applicants treat her foreignness and idol status as evidence she is unserious. Peers' messages help her refocus on dancing, and Musubi asks her principle; she answers that it is to try. She is not selected. In public she congratulates the company's standards and accepts a celebration, then privately asks the Player to turn away while she cries about losing. She controls when the comfort ends and offers a private ballet performance. The case preserves both sincere confidence and real disappointment; rejection is neither a hidden victory nor proof she lacked skill.
