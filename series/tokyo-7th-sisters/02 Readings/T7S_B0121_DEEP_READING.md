Rival: Kyoko is simply coerced. She clearly interrupts the withdrawal and demands the job. The opposite rival that the first offer was fully informed ignores her retreat and Ei's corrective explanation.

It does not prove the production designed adequate support, especially when the Player expects camera-on separation. [B:P15–33; C:P60–83]

Rival: fear invalidates choice or successful work cures fear. Neither follows from the source; she both chooses the job and grips his arm off camera.

Rival: Ei's supernatural claims prove ghosts were present. They remain her beliefs, while the specific three scares have mundane explanations. The reverse claim that all spiritual experience elsewhere is disproved would overgeneralize this local case.
