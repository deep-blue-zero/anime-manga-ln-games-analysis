# Tomoe's wanted company, giving care and uncertain worth

Tomoe can ask for another person's presence and find concrete ways to give something in return. A day's pairing, a homemade lunch, tea, a proposed ramen treat, shopping accompaniment and a morning call make company specific. Her hesitations do not erase the initiative involved in making these requests or attempting these acts of care. The seven cases also show how readily she turns a local difficulty into a judgment about whether she is needed at all.

Recognition changes what she can imagine without settling that uncertainty. Coney's reported praise of her tea becomes an opportunity to belong, while her brother's praise of singing first prompts suspicion and then pleasure in being liked. After a karaoke disappointment, thinking of the manager helps her hold herself together, but she describes persistence partly as a debt to repay. Warmth, obligation and a need for reassurance coexist. The evidence supports neither an incapable dependent nor a completed confidence cure.
