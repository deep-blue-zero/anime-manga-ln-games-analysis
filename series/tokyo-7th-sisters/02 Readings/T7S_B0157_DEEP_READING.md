# T7S_B0157 — Nanastar Ninkyo Tale—Sisters' Code

## Result and entering boundary

Madoka is offered a first film role without audition. The Player initially knows only that she will play a middle-school girl and reports that the director saw a stream; Madoka elects to try. Once she reads the script, she discovers it is a ninkyo film in which Kanna, a gang leader's daughter, confronts adult men. She doubts she can speak forcefully, and the Player's premature enthusiasm has moved shooting forward. Friends' first practice ideas do not solve the problem: a running drill ends in a scrape, and shouted dialect/action noises remain cute rather than Kanna's anger. Honoka then helps her think of Kanna as another girl with intelligible fears and loyalties rather than a bundle of tough lines. At a filmed confrontation Madoka departs from the script; the director lets the camera roll and praises the result. After wrap, he admits he first chose her simply because she speaks Hiroshima dialect, but says her work grew the role. She takes from this a limited sense of something she can do; the film's release/reception, future acting career, and general permission to alter scripts are not established.
