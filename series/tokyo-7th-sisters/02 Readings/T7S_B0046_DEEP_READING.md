# T7S_B0046 — captured consent and the puppet claim

## Result

Haru answers the prior deadlock with practical work: a thick joint-live proposal designed to let both groups ascend without turning idol performance into attack. Nero discloses surveillance, admits AXiS is executing Dr.END's plan, calls its members marionettes, and apparently agrees to make the proposal real through a public announcement. Haru consents to that collaboration. Nero then uses the press conference to rename it a vote-decided live battle, adds an AXiS dissolution stake, and says the terms were jointly agreed. The title transfers at the moment of apparent rescue: Nero's account of her own constraint is credible, but she uses it to make Haru the `あやつり人形`. This does not prove that Nero is free or that the surveillance was invented. It proves that victimhood and downward domination can coexist. The decisive act is semantic capture. Haru's yes is preserved as a public sign while the object to which she said yes is replaced.

## Reading position and exposure boundary

The first pass used the completed Main sequence through AXiS episode 04. Episodes 06–13 remained withheld. Both attached blocking-movie transcript documents were read as text. Later confirmation that multiple levels of the project exercise control is a retrospective annotation; the present analysis preserves what Haru can and cannot verify in this encounter.

## A proposal rather than a wish

Nero wakes from a recurring dream in which an unseen person calls for her, asks where she went, and screams when Nero answers that she is present. A report then arrives, and Nero says the “sparkling heroine” has finally appeared. Haru's visit is anticipated.

At Nanastar, Susu understands that Haru went because she felt compelled to act, not simply because she wanted a private meeting. Hime dismisses what one person can accomplish, inadvertently repeating the objection made to her own earlier solo action. Their mirrored positions show how quickly a collective deadlock sends different members into isolated responsibility.

Haru brings a substantial written proposal. She acknowledges AXiS' obstruction rather than pretending goodwill already exists. Her claim is that, if attack is a mode of self-expression, another form should be possible in which both groups perform, entertain, and ascend. She supplies reasons and mutual benefits.

This matters to the interpretation of her later deception. Haru is not tricked because she substitutes emotion for thought. She has translated an ethical desire into an operational offer. Her vulnerability lies in assuming that disclosed suffering creates a shared context in which words retain their referents.

## Surveillance that is also persuasive evidence

Nero refuses to read the proposal in the office but signals Haru to follow. Haru notices an End Production guard behind them. Nero evades the tail and says people above AXiS in the special-zone government monitor every part of the members' lives, including bathroom use.

The physical tail gives independent support to at least part of the constraint account. Haru has not merely heard an unverifiable confession. She has observed monitoring and participated in an escape from it.

In private, Nero apologizes and shows what she identifies as Dr.END's plan. It specifies recruitment of six people whose voices resemble Seven Sisters, reproduction of Seventh Strike, simultaneous destruction of a popular group, obstruction and agitation methods, and a route to making AXiS immovable at the top.

Nero calls the members pieces. Their behavior, public characters, work, and private lives are prescribed. She says ordinary people with only famous vocal resemblance could not otherwise sell, and that the rewards satisfy them except for the damage to 777☆SISTERS.

The account is structurally plausible and materially supported. It is also perfectly shaped to activate Haru's ethic: someone apparently doing wrong discloses that she is controlled, asks for a route that helps everyone, and makes cooperation the means of resistance.

## The private alliance

Nero says she truly wants a fair joint live that makes people happy. Because Dr.END controls the project, she proposes announcing the collaboration before he can prevent it. Public commitment will force institutional acceptance. Haru agrees with relief.

The plan resembles Haru's own intervention logic: create an irreversible fact where formal authority blocks a needed relation. That resemblance makes it attractive. It also contains the mechanism of the trap. Whoever controls the announcement controls what the fact is.

Nero asks about Nanase Nicole and says she has long wanted to meet her. She describes her prior cruelty as performance. Haru answers with the Coney she knows—bright, kind, strong, and precious—and promises an introduction.

Haru's testimony is sincere but partial. She knows the accumulated Coney self, not the full Seven Sisters history Nero invokes. Nero receives both emotional information and a route to Coney through Haru. Whether her desire to meet Nicole is also sincere remains unresolved; manipulation does not make every expressed longing false.

## The title changes owners

After Haru leaves, delighted that she has recovered a live, Nero calls her the real puppet. The line does not simply reveal that the preceding conversation was fictional. A more exact reading is that Nero used selectively presented truth as a control surface.

She can be under surveillance and still direct Haru. Dr.END can have authored a plan while Nero authors this encounter. Agency is not allocated once at the top and then absent below. The episode's puppet image identifies a relation in which one party controls another person's available information, interpretation, and next act.

Haru returns to find a press conference underway. Nero announces not a joint live but a popularity battle decided by audience vote. She declares that AXiS will dissolve if it loses and says Haru shares the arrangement.

Haru agreed to AXiS and 777☆SISTERS appearing together. She did not agree to competition, voting, or dissolution. The public announcement retains her presence and presumed assent while changing all load-bearing terms.

## Consent as a captured sign

This is more severe than breaking a promise. A broken promise leaves the original agreement intelligible as something violated. Nero attempts to make the revised version the only public agreement. Anyone who sees Haru present at the announcement can treat objection as regret, cowardice, or breach rather than evidence that consent never covered the new terms.

The act repeats AXiS' wider media method at interpersonal scale. Egg Hall failure became an AXiS rescue; a joint live becomes a dissolution battle. In each case, an available fact is preserved while its causal and ethical meaning is replaced.

The audience vote intensifies the effect. Public participation gives the imposed structure a democratic surface. Spectators will appear to choose between groups, but neither spectators nor Nanastar chose the conditions under which their choice becomes existential.

The unilateral AXiS stake also creates pressure without explicitly announcing an equal Nanastar stake. If 777☆SISTERS refuses, it can be represented as unwilling to risk what AXiS risks. If it accepts, the public can infer symmetry that Nero never negotiated.

## The recurrent dream and recognition

The dream organizes Nero's private wound around failed recognition. She says she is present and receives terror rather than reunion. The episode does not identify the caller, the historical event, or whether the sequence is literal memory, recurring nightmare, or stylized mixture.

Its placement nevertheless clarifies the form of Nero's manipulation. Haru's proposal offers mutual recognition; Nero responds by controlling what Haru's assent will mean to everyone else. A person wounded by not being recognized can still seek safety through monopolizing recognition's terms.

Later source will explain more, but this boundary should retain the unknown. The dream makes the trap personal without yet making grievance an excuse.

## Exit state

- Haru has made a reasoned joint-live proposal and privately consented to collaborative performance.
- she has observed End Production surveillance and read a document presented as Dr.END's detailed AXiS plan;
- Nero has described AXiS as controlled pieces while using that disclosure to direct Haru;
- the public is told that Haru agreed to a vote battle and AXiS dissolution stake, neither of which she accepted;
- audience judgment is positioned to ratify terms authored through semantic capture;
- the exact truth distribution between Dr.END's authority, higher surveillance, and Nero's agency remains unresolved.
