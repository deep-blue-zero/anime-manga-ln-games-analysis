# T7S_B0133 — Tasha and the imagined lady

## Result and entering boundary

After watching Miwako, Mimi and Miu pose at a modeling shoot, Tasha wants to become a `ジェーンシチナ`, which she explains as an admirable grown woman. Her experiments with clay, Mimi's body, cosmetics and adult poses produce a conflict with the Player, who keeps telling her to value her present child role. At the sea he misses her immediate wish to change; the quarrel continues back at the office. Miu interrupts, asks why the Player scouts girls who want to change, and gets Tasha's own reason heard: she wants his praise, more work and more time with him. Both apologize. Tasha asks to learn makeup from Miu, and the Player agrees. This is permission for a lesson and a repaired conversation, not proof that makeup is applied, that Tasha becomes an adult, that the manager gives her new work, or that bodily/sexual boundaries are solved.

## Factual reconstruction by actor knowledge

### EV-B0133-001 — a shoot suggests an adult model of recognition

Miwako, Mimi and Miu ask the Player for reactions while posing at work; he calls their looks good but is embarrassed. Tasha has asked to observe the shoot and says she wants to be a `ジェーンシチナ` like them. She glosses this as a splendid lady and offers a Japan analogy the Player calls subtly wrong. His approval of her wish to study has not yet identified that she also wants *his* attention. The shoot is theirs, not evidence Tasha has been cast for the same adult-style modeling work. [A:P0–23]

### EV-B0133-002 — a body experiment and a rushed prohibition

At the girls' dorm Tasha shapes clay, asks Mimi to crouch, and touches her chest to study the form; Mimi yelps, then calls it ticklish. No advance agreement to that touch is shown. The Player enters without knocking, is teased for doing so, apologizes, then scolds Tasha and redirects her to work. She says the clay is a breast, and he rebukes her language and her attempt to borrow cosmetics; he does not ask why these experiments matter to her. The scene plays as comic surprise, but that framing does not establish consent to the touch or a lasting dorm-entry safeguard. [A:P26–55]

### EV-B0133-003 — the sea expedition does not settle the desire

Tasha seeks the sea, cites an enka line about women growing strong, and mentions milk, makeup and height as ways to become like Mimi and Miwako. The Player notes she even tried lipstick during a schoolbag advertisement shoot, where that adult pose would conflict with the assignment. He tells her to present her current childlike appeal and grow through experience. She rejects his answer, says she wants to be grown *now*, calls him names and walks away. She returns because she does not know the way home and then refuses conversation. The narrative establishes a genuine production-role mismatch, not that the Player's dismissal fully understood her motive or that milk/sea accelerate maturation. [B:P1–32]

### EV-B0133-004 — Matsuri's diversion and Miu's intervention

At the office Tasha still refuses to speak to the Player. Matsuri relays that Tasha wants him to acknowledge her as grown, then offers a joking game-input regimen for becoming an older sister; Tasha distinguishes Matsuri's example from the modeled women she means. The Player urges her to listen and promises she will grow later, but again calls her a child. Miu, who says she heard the exchange, stops him. She offers to show Tasha makeup suitable *for her* and asks why a manager who recruited girls wishing to change is now stopping this wish without hearing it. Miu's challenge is about listening and scope of change, not a claim that a child should take an adult sexualized role. [C:P1–53]

### EV-B0133-005 — Tasha names praise, work and shared time

Tasha tells the Player she wanted his praise like the older women receive. She imagined becoming cute enough to excite him, receiving more work, and spending more time together. The Player recognizes that he had not asked about her feelings and apologizes; Tasha apologizes for not saying them clearly and for lashing out. This disclosure explains the earlier experiments without proving a healthy or assured route from adult appearance to work allocation. Her articulated need should not be collapsed into an endorsement of adultifying her. [C:P54–66]

### EV-B0133-006 — limited permission and an open future

Miu offers a makeup lesson; Tasha directly asks the Player whether she may learn, and he agrees, asking Miu to teach her well. Tasha says she will slowly become a beautiful `ジェーンシチナ` someday; the Player says he will wait. Matsuri remains confused about being asked to apologize. The scene ends before the lesson, any new job decision, any changed dorm/body-touch practice or a demonstrated future adult identity. [C:P67–81]

## Interpretation and alternatives

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0133-001.** Tasha's drive to look older is also a bid for professional recognition and relationship time: the opening shoot's manager-facing praise and her later account supply that connection. Cosmetic interest alone does not explain the quarrel; nor does it show that adult appearance is actually required for work. [A:P0–23; B:P6–24; C:P47–58]

Rival: she only wants to imitate fashionable women. Her stated wish for praise, assignments and playtime is more specific, though it remains her expectation rather than an established hiring mechanism.

**ETHICAL_THEMATIC_INTERPRETATION — CL-B0133-002.** The Player has a legitimate role-based concern about a schoolbag ad and a minor copying adult poses, but his repeated `child` answer treats her present desire as something to wait out. Miu repairs the listening failure while offering age-suitable instruction, not an adult modeling assignment. [A:P36–54; B:P6–24; C:P31–70]

Rival: the Player must either forbid all makeup or approve the older women's style for Tasha. The ending chooses a narrower lesson after Tasha speaks for herself; it does not display its execution.

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0133-003.** Comic framing leaves bodily and access boundaries unresolved: Tasha's touch surprises Mimi, and the Player enters the girls' dorm without knocking. Mimi's subsequent teasing/ticklish response and the Player's immediate apology cannot retrospectively establish advance consent or a durable privacy rule. [A:P26–47]

Rival: a later joke makes the touch and entry harmless by definition. The script does not give the needed prior permission or future practice.

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0133-004.** The reconciliation repairs a conversation and authorizes Miu to teach a suitable makeup method. It does not establish that Tasha is adult, secure more assignments, make the Player's attention unconditional, or settle the potentially sexualized language in her wish for his reaction. [C:P47–81]

Rival: saying he will wait and welcoming her cute appearance resolves every practical need. These are future-oriented words; no labor or relationship allocation follows within the chapter.
