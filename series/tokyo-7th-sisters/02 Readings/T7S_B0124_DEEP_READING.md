# T7S_B0124 — Rely on Me

## Result and entering boundary

The Player tries to handle paperwork, outside calls and a stream while feeling weak; his busyness leaves Rona reluctant to raise a stained costume, and Tomoe fears changed choreography and nausea. Mimi notices he is hot to the touch and unsteady, suspects a cold and takes over with his assent. She completes administrative tasks and addresses each performer’s immediate concern; the stream is later reported completed. Her apparent ease has a cost: she says she held confidence partly so the Player would rest and is exhausted afterward. When Xiao brings a report of a harasser near Rona’s route home, Kyoko cannot reach Rona, and Mimi goes after her alone despite admitting fear. The still-ill Player follows and asks Mimi to rely on him; she recognizes her one-way helper habit and asks if she can. Coney then claims she caught a culprit, handed him to police and spoke with Rona. The resolution is reported, not witnessed. Care here is effective when tasks and worry can be shared, but neither Mimi’s substitution nor the Player’s pursuit proves a safe staffing or incident-response protocol.
