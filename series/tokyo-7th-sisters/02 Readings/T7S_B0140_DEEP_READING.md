# T7S_B0140 — Forget Lonely Not

## Result and entering boundary

Murasaki sings at her own girls' school's festival with Ferb and five other Nanastar members. Two school fans initially experience the visitors as a threat to the solitary `ミス・パープル` whose songs have voiced their loneliness. The collaborators privately show preparation and give Murasaki a kitten-shaped handmade charm; onstage she names Ferb's arrangement, introduces the others as artists and idols, and performs an arranged cover of 4U's `Hello…my friend`. The conclusion remains comic and unstable: the companions interrupt the stage, Murasaki scolds them, and the fans resolve to guard her mystique themselves. The case establishes a locally witnessed attempt to make changed artistic identity intelligible to an attached audience, not a wholesale fan conversion or a permanent festival unit.
