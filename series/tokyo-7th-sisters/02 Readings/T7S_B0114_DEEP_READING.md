# T7S_B0114 — Suss's Study Camp

## Result and entering boundary

Suss is failing most subjects in an English-language international online school. She treats repetition lightly until an email from her father says to come home if the poor results continue. Musubi organizes a one-week Nanasta study camp before a retest. The English materials initially expose a limit in her peers' competence; Musubi uses a dictionary and Haru later helps by looking up meanings. Sumire feeds Suss, whose sleepiness first blocks study; after Sawara's undisclosed talk, Suss asks seriously to be taught. Three hours without a break leave her nodding off, and Musubi chooses one last problem and rest rather than unlimited cramming. Suss then reports passing every retest. A reveal explains the changed motivation: Sawara promised that Suss could do what she liked with the Player if she passed and barred their contact until results, citing the Player's earlier broad offer to help. The Player was not consulted and explicitly objects when the reward is disclosed. The result is a real local academic success, not proof that threat, rest deprivation, or a third person's unconsented intimacy is a sound education policy.

## Factual reconstruction by actor knowledge

### EV-B0114-001 — a conditional return-home warning

Musubi rebukes Suss's failing marks in almost every subject of her international online school. Suss says she could simply repeat a year. Musubi says that a foreign student not following a Japanese compulsory-education-equivalent curriculum might be sent out of the country; this is Musubi's understanding, not a legal determination. An email from Suss's father says that if the poor marks continue she should come home. Suss trusts that he is serious and fears separation from Nanasta and the Player. Haru, Sumire and Musubi offer help; Musubi focuses on a retest in one week. [A:P2–40]

### EV-B0114-002 — language-specific effort and the study-camp command

The textbooks are all in English. Haru and Sumire cannot easily read them, and Suss worries no one can teach her. Musubi says the middle-school subject matter is familiar to her and an English dictionary can bridge terms. She orders a one-week Nanasta sleepover/study camp starting immediately. Suss resists the intensive schedule. Haru later says she did look up meanings while helping. No full curriculum, actual immigration file, grades before this episode or formal accommodation is shown. [A:P41–72; C:P15–18]

### EV-B0114-003 — food, fatigue and an undisclosed bargain

During a late visit, the Player finds Sumire has made fried udon because Suss said she was too hungry to work. After eating Suss is sleepy and says she cannot keep trying. The Player broadly offers to do anything to help. Sawara overhears, takes Suss aside for a private talk, dismisses the Player, and Suss returns apologizing and asking Musubi to teach her seriously. The chapter withholds the contents of Sawara's proposal here; a later flashback reveals it. A full stomach may precede drowsiness, but no nutritional or clinical causation is assessed. [B:P1–55; C:P51–63]

### EV-B0114-004 — the tutor imposes a stopping point

Musubi explains a diagram and Suss applies it, but after three hours without a break Suss keeps nodding off while insisting she must finish. Sumire notes the continuous time, and Musubi recognizes small yawns. Musubi says that overdoing it until illness would defeat the purpose; she proposes one final problem and then ending for the day. This is a local correction of her earlier `not one second` urgency, not proof the whole week had adequate sleep or a standing education/safeguarding rule. Sawara subsequently bars the Player from seeing Suss until the test, claiming a girls-only secret. [B:P56–96]

### EV-B0114-005 — passing grades and the price of motivation

The result arrives; Suss says all subjects passed, and Haru confirms all have passing marks. Suss thanks Sumire for food, Haru for dictionary-aided help and Musubi for instruction; Musubi hugs her in relief. The father is not shown withdrawing his threat or giving permission to remain. Asked how Sawara changed her mind, Sawara says she made an unwanted duty desirable by promising Suss that, if she passed, she could do as she liked with the Player, and required no contact until results. Sawara cited the Player's earlier `anything` offer without clarifying or obtaining his assent. Suss embraces/entangles him and asks for a strong hug; he asks her to wait and later says he had not been told. Musubi and Sumire protest and physically comic-punish him, while Suss announces another hug. The scene does not show the promised unbounded reward completed, nor grant Sawara authority to make it. [C:P1–78]
