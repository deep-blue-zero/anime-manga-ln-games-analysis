# T7S_B0129 — Shrine-Maiden Magic Show

## Result and entering boundary

Manon loves the animated shrine-maiden heroine Mikola and is distressed when schoolboys mock her belief that Mikola exists. Saori, Sisara, Jedah and the Player propose a one-day character show at Ei's family shrine, with Ei as the heroine. Ei is embarrassed and reluctantly agrees for Manon's sake. Rehearsal and Rishuri's costumes lead to a staged battle in which Manon and a boy visibly respond with delight and belief; Sisara later reports that Manon has reconciled with the boys. Ei values the children smiling and the shrine setting, but expressly declines recurring magical-girl work. A late call reports that the rights-holder wants an official show after Coney recorded the event; Ei's shocked question establishes she had not known she was filmed. The offer does not supersede her refusal. The recording's contents, circulation path, rights permission, any new contract and any later performance are not shown.

## Factual reconstruction by actor knowledge

### EV-B0129-001 — an animated fiction becomes a child's social stake

The opening lines by Darkness Moon, Doctor F, Lady Shesta and Mikola belong to `マジカルメイデン☆ミコラ`, which Manon is watching. Sisara says Manon told her about the shrine-based magical-girl anime; Jedah admires its blending of miko and magical-girl motifs, while Ei wonders why a shrine maiden uses magic. Later Saori reports Manon crying. Jedah and Sisara recount that boys at school told her Mikola was not real and laughed when she insisted. Manon is asleep when adults and peers discuss the response; her dispute and feeling are relayed, not directly witnessed in the chapter. [A:P0–40]

### EV-B0129-002 — a one-day request under social pressure

Saori proposes staging a Mikola show before Manon and the boys so the heroine can defeat villains. Sisara calls the character a support to Manon; Jedah casts Ei, whose family shrine is nearby and whose real shrine work has already caused children to tug at her hakama and ask for special moves. The Player and Saori add their requests. Ei says she is embarrassed but cannot refuse for Manon's sake and agrees for one day. This is assent to the child-directed local show under cumulative pressure, not advance assent to filming, an official series or an indefinite job. [A:P18–49]

### EV-B0129-003 — rehearsal exposes a proposed expansion and rights uncertainty

At rehearsal the peers play the villains. The Player praises the acting and asks Ei whether, if it goes well, she might do an official character show; Ei says no and redirects the idea to Musubi. Saori asks whether the team needs official permission for the present show. The Player says Coney knows the anime's staff, the location is the nearby 7th Shrine and they are not taking a general audience; no license or rights-holder approval is displayed. Jedah then brings in Rishuri to make lookalike costumes. Ei says the process feels so quick that she cannot escape, even while she recommits to doing the best Mikola she can for Manon. A claimed staff connection and later company interest are not documentary proof of prior authorization. [B:P1–29]

### EV-B0129-004 — performed battle and limited child reception

Manon and a boy attend the shrine show. Costumed peers play villains, and Ei initially hesitates, takes a breath, names herself Mikola and acts a transformation and attack. Native sprite states include a glowing overlay, staged costume changes, flashes and effects; these are presentation choices, not proof of literal magic or an independently heard performance. Manon cheers that Ei became Mikola and a boy says Mikola really existed. The Player internally praises the acting, while Saori briefly falls during the routine. These are local text reports and selected visual states, not a technical performance audit or evidence that children's beliefs endure. [C:P1–50]

### EV-B0129-005 — care accomplished, role still bounded

After the show Ei says she was particularly embarrassed by the short skirt. Sisara says Manon and the boys seem to have made up; their actual exchange is not transcribed. Ei sees many smiling children and imagines the shrine's god pleased. She calls herself an ordinary high-school student, shrine maiden and idol, says the magical-girl turn is over, and refuses the Player's suggestion that she keep it up. Positive care and satisfaction with this performance coexist with discomfort and a clear limit on repetition. [C:P51–64]

### EV-B0129-006 — a recording changes the audience without Ei's knowledge

Saori relays a call from Coney: the company behind Mikola has heard of Ei's show and wants the group for an official hero show. Asked how the company learned, Saori suggests Coney had been recording video. Ei reacts in surprise that filming occurred. The chapter does not present the recording, when Ei could have seen a camera, who received footage, its circulation scope, permissions, offer terms or Ei's answer to the new request. The Player's closing joke that magical-girl work may continue is not her acceptance. [C:P65–75]

## Interpretation and alternatives

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0129-001.** A fiction supplies a child with real social stakes: the group answers ridicule through theatrical presence and Manon visibly delights in it. The show can support her without establishing that Mikola exists literally, that the boys' belief is durable or that Manon's dispute is fully resolved beyond Sisara's report. [A:P0–40; C:P1–57]

Rival: the staged show proves supernatural reality or permanently settles childhood doubt. Costume, effects and acting explain the immediate reception; no later child testimony appears. The inverse claim that the play had no effect ignores Manon's shown joy.

**ETHICAL_THEMATIC_INTERPRETATION — CL-B0129-002.** Ei's care is chosen within strong peer pressure and remains scoped: she agrees to one child-directed day, improves the role in rehearsal, then explicitly refuses recurring work. Successful reception does not retroactively enlarge the permission she gave. [A:P36–49; B:P1–29; C:P51–64]

Rival: her effective acting means she secretly wants an official magical-girl career. She twice voices embarrassment and refusal. A rival that she had no agency at all misses her final commitment to doing the show well for Manon.

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0129-003.** The reported Coney recording turns a local performance into an outside company's solicitation without Ei's informed awareness of filming. Company interest is reported, not a contract, valid release, completed official show or proof of prior rights clearance. [B:P7–16; C:P65–75]

Rival: because Coney knows staff, all permissions are established. The text shows the Player's assurance but no rights document or performer release. Conversely, an assertion of definite infringement or public upload goes beyond the evidence.

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0129-004.** Rishuri's craft, villain roles and native transformation effects make the fiction convincing to some children; the sprite overlay remains a presentation layer, not a new supernatural entity or a separately auditioned show. [B:P19–28; C:P5–50]

Rival: visually glowing Ei proves a literal Mikola possession. The chapter frames the sequence as acting, costumes and effects and gives no independent magical event.
