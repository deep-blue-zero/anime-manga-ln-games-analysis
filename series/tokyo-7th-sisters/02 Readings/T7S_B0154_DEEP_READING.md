# T7S_B0154 — My Fans

## Result and entering boundary

Kajika assumes she has no individual fan letters and sees herself as an appendage to Sawara and Shinju. The Player and Coney then explain that a group of fans has petitioned to make a private fan club specifically for her and propose to cooperate with a video greeting. Her confusion is not resolved by peers listing traits or comparing her with more conspicuous idols. The letters that might explain the fans' own reasons have been held together with other Harumi-family mail by Sawara, who is away and unreachable before the recording. Kajika asks the Player directly; the source skips his answer, but her later recorded account says he immediately named many good qualities. She infers that fans, too, have found qualities she had overlooked, thanks them for seeing her, and promises to look for good in them. Sawara secretly views the recording and adds a teasing address to Kajika's fans, promising to hand over the letters tomorrow. No fan's actual explanation, confirmed letter handover, finished club operations or distribution of the video is shown.

Native `私のファン`, `EPISODE.3.5-011`; “My Fans” is a gloss. Earlier Harumi-family and Sanbon Ribbon readings establish stable family/unit identity, not a ranking of which sister deserves fans. B0153's Sawara photography trip is a bounded thematic comparison only: this chapter says Sawara is traveling out of town, but gives no specific itinerary proving the trips are the same or adjacent. The private-club petition is for Kajika, not 777☆S as a whole; the proposed cooperation does not convert the club into an official Nanastar association.

## Factual reconstruction by presentation and actor knowledge

### EV-B0154-001 — mail to peers, not a measure of worth

Haru, Musubi and Momoka read fan letters; Haru remembers a recurring correspondent's age, school and illustration interest from handwriting and past letters. Kajika assumes none are addressed to her, describing herself as an extra to Sawara and Shinju and asking whether letters to Sanbon Ribbon count. Haru rejects the absence assumption and wants her to ask the Player. Momoka jokes about receiving gifts; Musubi says the Player checks incoming packages for security. These exchanges establish actual letter reading and a stated screening practice, not a comprehensive privacy/security audit or evidence that Kajika's popularity ranks below anyone's. [A:P2–38]

### EV-B0154-002 — a private Kajika club is proposed

The Player and Coney explain that fans have sent a petition with signatures seeking a private club for one preferred idol. The Player distinguishes fan-run from official clubs: he says the former do not actually require Nanastar permission, whereas the latter would be created by the agency and are currently beyond its means. He would like to cooperate with this group, perhaps through a surprise video greeting, to hear fan requests, understand the community and learn for a possible future official club. Kajika initially assumes Sawara or Shinju must be the subject; they tell her it is Kajika. The narrative establishes a petition and managerial intentions, not an already functioning, authorized-by-law or official club. [A:P43–86]

### EV-B0154-003 — being first unsettles Kajika's self-image

Kajika thinks she is ordinary and lacks traits comparable with her sisters and peers. Shinju relays the Player's examples that private clubs do not form in simple center-first order; Musubi suggests a less assertive presence may itself stand out beside strong personalities; Momoka defends ordinary idols as legitimate; Haru rejects the premise that Kajika is only ordinary. These are peers' interpretations, not interviews with the petitioning fans. Kajika remains unable to imagine what specific people like about her or what to say to them. [B:P1–49]

### EV-B0154-004 — access to letters is mediated by her sister

Haru proposes reading Kajika's actual fan letters. Shinju says Sawara receives all Harumi-family letters together under a claimed “guardian authority.” Kajika recalls Sawara asking whether she wanted the letters, then saying she would give them when Kajika wanted them. The reason for withholding is not fully established; neither Sawara's legal status nor Kajika's informed consent to shared custody is documented. Sawara is away and not reliably reachable before the video deadline. Thus a potentially direct fan voice is unavailable exactly when Kajika wants it, even though a later handover is promised. [B:P50–72; C:P1–6,73–75]

### EV-B0154-005 — an offscreen answer becomes Kajika's inference

Pressed by the deadline, Kajika asks the Player what fans could like about someone she calls plain. The source transitions without transcribing his answer. In the video she says he immediately listed many qualities, some small and unknown to her, and perhaps overpraised her. She infers he *found* good in her rather than conjured a different person, and fans may likewise have found what she did not see. This is Kajika's reported experience and interpretation, not independent evidence of the petitioners' individual reasons. [C:P1–16,40–52]

### EV-B0154-006 — a recording of reciprocal attention

Kajika starts her greeting awkwardly, stumbles, thanks her fans, explains her tendency to see herself third or fourth behind others, and compares herself to the stock that supports the ingredients rather than the featured ingredient. She then thanks fans for finding her and asks them to keep watching; she promises to look at them and find their good qualities too. She worries that her talk is not entertaining enough and closes. The video turns “being watched” into proposed reciprocity, but neither knowledge of each fan nor personal access to Kajika is promised. Its delivery to the petitioners is not shown. [C:P17–60]

### EV-B0154-007 — Sawara's uninvited extra address

After a scene transition, Sawara says she secretly watched the message on returning to Nanastar and speaks toward Kajika's fans. She celebrates her sister being noticed, jokingly casts the reciprocal message as a love confession, then says romance would be too early and that would-be suitors must get past her. She promises to give Kajika the letters the next day. This is affectionate sisterly response and possessive fan rhetoric, not evidence of Kajika consenting to the viewing or the postscript, actual romantic interest from fans, handover, a complete edited video or recipient delivery. [C:P61–76]

## Interpretation and alternatives

**ETHICAL_THEMATIC_INTERPRETATION — CL-B0154-001.** Kajika moves from treating “ordinary” as invisibility to understanding recognition as another person's act of attention. Her thanks does not require inventing a uniquely exceptional trait; she can value being found and offer attention back. [A:P23–34; B:P1–49; C:P40–57]

Rival: a fan club objectively proves she is the group's best or that her self-doubt was irrational. Neither follows from one petition or peer praise.

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0154-002.** The fans' motives remain unknown. The Player's offscreen list and Kajika's inference explain *her* way to address them, while no letter or petitioner testimony confirms the same reasons. [B:P19–55; C:P9–16,40–57]

Rival: Musubi's “ordinary” theory, Haru's “cute” explanation or the Player's compliment list is the actual motive of all signers. The record does not tie any one to them.

**ETHICAL_THEMATIC_INTERPRETATION — CL-B0154-003.** Fan-run and official club governance are distinct. Nanastar's wish to cooperate brings access, learning and possible promotional value, but the story does not establish membership rules, data stewardship, safety screening, financial terms or approval of every future fan act. [A:P43–70]

Rival: the petition is already an official club or the manager's involvement gives the agency total control. He explicitly distinguishes both forms.

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0154-004.** Sawara's family-mail custody and covert video viewing create access and consent questions that affectionate intent cannot settle. Kajika later wants the letters; the source does not show their receipt, a legal guardian entitlement, or approval of Sawara's fan-facing postscript. [B:P50–72; C:P1–6,61–76]

Rival: because Sawara promises delivery and loves Kajika, all withholding/viewing was authorized. Those are distinct facts.

**ETHICAL_THEMATIC_INTERPRETATION — CL-B0154-005.** Kajika's proposed reciprocal looking is an ethical orientation toward fans, not an intimate or romantic contract. Sawara's “love confession” and gatekeeper jokes add a different interpretation that Kajika did not speak; fans' actual relation and the finished video's audience remain unspecified. [C:P29–76]

Rival: fans thereby gain personal access or romantic eligibility. The source supports gratitude and future attention, not that entitlement.
