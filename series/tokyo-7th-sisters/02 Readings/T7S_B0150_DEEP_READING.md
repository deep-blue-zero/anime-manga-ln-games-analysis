# T7S_B0150 — A Little Boy Meets Girl

## Result and entering boundary

Sui asks Nanastar peers to help plan a “date” requested by Masaru, a named schoolboy she has been coaching in swimming. The chapter then explains their relationship: Masaru first challenged her idol identity, lost a race, and asked her to help him improve for a meet. He reports winning but conceals that he missed his personal best, then asks for the date as a reward. Peers suggest adult-coded expensive destinations and press Sui to try feminine clothes; Shinju instead helps her plan from what Sui and Masaru actually enjoy. Their outing centers on a pool, food and amusement rides. Masaru confesses the missed time; Sui says she already inferred it and values his effort and win, and he calls himself her fan. She jokingly asks to keep the date secret because she is an idol. The peers have secretly followed both the coaching and the outing; when Sui realizes the second surveillance, she protests. The story supports a friendship/mentor bond and playful gender-role experimentation, not an adult romance, professional child-athlete protocol, or a license to spy on a schoolboy.
