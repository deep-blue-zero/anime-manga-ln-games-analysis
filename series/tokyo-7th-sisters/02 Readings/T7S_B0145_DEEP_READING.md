# T7S_B0145 — What the Detour Finds

## Result and entering boundary

Musubi makes `探求` (“search”) her idol aspiration after recalling the Player's invitation to make her own story. She researches earlier idols, triples practice and submits a resignation from her student-council presidency. Her technique and preparation improve by the surrounding characters' accounts, yet three lives fail to connect: she pursues a faultless show without remembering individual audience faces. The school idol-club president's harsh criticism and a council girl's public account of Musubi's hidden labor expose the same limit in both roles. Students offer to share council work and support her choice. At a later live, schoolmates' participatory banter and Musubi's spontaneous management of them accompany a better audience atmosphere. This is neither a simple endorsement of perfection nor proof that labor has become sustainable. The election speech cuts before Musubi's answer; no vote, accepted resignation withdrawal or formal new term is shown. Her social continuation as president is strongly implied by the later “president” chants and her uncorrected response, but institutional status remains unverified. Coney explicitly says the self-search will continue.
