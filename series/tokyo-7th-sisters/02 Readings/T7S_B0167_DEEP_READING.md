# T7S_B0167 — Wearing memories without surrendering privacy

## Result and entering boundary

Ei asks Rena, Jeda and Ayumu to help her choose ordinary, cute clothes after schoolmates call her private fashion unusual and "ダサ可愛い." Their remarks mix compliments, qualifications and an awkward label; Ei hears the insulting part most strongly and worries about the image of her fellow idols. A shopping outing offers three different ways to dress. Its decisive result is not a perfected coordination: she buys a hoodie from Rena's selection, boots and tights from Jeda's, and a skirt from Ayumu's, acknowledges that the combination is mismatched, and nonetheless calls the pieces treasures. She wants them because they contain the memory of a day when her friends took care in choosing for her.

Her explanation also changes how the older private outfit can be understood. Its trousers, blouse and hair ornaments carry remembered encounters with her grandmother, a friend and her mother. Player compares this accumulation to idol costumes becoming someone's own through use. Later schoolmates report that the clothing stories are circulating online, and their responses change. Ei is surprised at their knowledge and complains that her friends talked freely. Supportive public recognition therefore does not establish advance permission to disclose. One memory remains inward and withheld: she wore that private outfit when Player scouted her. A gift coda retains her unusual taste rather than announcing a fashion cure.

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0167-007.** Reader access exceeds shared knowledge.
