## Interpretation and alternatives

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0105-001.** Xiao's test design turns the difficulty of an activity into the measure of growth. Kazumi's picture book is useful precisely because it is accessible and absorbing, qualities Xiao first treats as disqualifying. She learns that enjoyment is possible, but her stated conclusion remains “training failed.” The scene tests a false opposition between pleasure and serious reading without proving she adopted a new long-term reading practice. [A:P0–20]

Rival: Kazumi knowingly humiliates Xiao with a childish book. She recommends a childhood favorite while explicitly describing its depth; Xiao's “trap” is self-ascribed after enjoyment, not a disclosed plan by Kazumi. Kazumi's earlier “suited to one's measure” phrasing can nevertheless sound condescending, so the comic exchange does not certify flawless pedagogy.

**THEMATIC INTERPRETATION — CL-B0105-002.** Xiao's food trials mismeasure strength because they place refusal above ordinary needs and care. Her concern for Murasaki's missed meal is generous, yet the recipient does not want sweet food and urges Xiao to eat before rehearsal. At the climax, the single cream puff's disappearance makes the narrow abstinence criterion “successful” while intensifying Xiao's sorrow. Sumire then reclassifies a similar dessert as a gift Xiao may receive. What changes is the social meaning and choice around food, not a proven physiological measure or durable cure. [A:P21–48; B:P1–46]

Rival: the final success is simply fake, because Xiao cries. She really does refrain from eating the guarded puff, and says so. The more exact problem is that her success metric is inadequate to her stated goal of becoming a strong idol who does not burden others; distress and lesson delay complicate, but do not logically erase, her narrow success.

**ETHICAL/THEMATIC INTERPRETATION — CL-B0105-003.** The group's care is distributed and imperfect. Kazumi offers an appropriately scaled book, Murasaki worries about lesson energy, Sumire offers graduated practice and prepares a timely reward. The Player fails to ask or heed the warning about an apparently available dessert; the resulting group scolding is comic accountability, not a procedure. Sumire's “idols should smile” line might restore pressure on Xiao even as her gift relieves it; the scene shows a local affectionate repair, not universal safety or a guaranteed smile regime. [A:P7–16,23–48; B:P6–51]

Rival: friendship simply replaces training. Xiao's training motive remains valid to her, and Sumire calls the replacement puff a reward *within* that idiom. The chapter offers a less punishing scale for effort, not proof that difficulty never matters or that all care will be correctly received.
