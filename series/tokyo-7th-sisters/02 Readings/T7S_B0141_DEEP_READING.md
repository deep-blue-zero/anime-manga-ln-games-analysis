## Factual reconstruction by actor knowledge

### EV-B0141-001 — distance alarms the Player, but no one yet knows why

The Player reports that Makoto now keeps distance, sends two or three messages rather than about a hundred daily and no longer supplies morning lunches. Sumire does not understand why this is worrying. Haru, Yumeno and Coney immediately blame the Player, with Coney urging apology even if he did nothing wrong; the Player agrees. The causal accusation precedes any inquiry into Makoto's reasons. The counts are Player's estimates and later reports, not an independently audited message log or a measure of acceptable contact. ### EV-B0141-002 — Sumire asks rather than treating Makoto as an extension of him

Makoto glares at Sumire as a rival, asks to be left alone and says she has no right to be near the Player. Sumire objects that Makoto may have her own reasons; she follows and brings her to a karaoke room to talk. Sumire says concern is not a profit calculation and that she wants to speak. Makoto initially says she does not want to sing or be there, then agrees to explain. Sumire's initiative opens the disclosure but her statement that she would not let Makoto go even if refused is not an ideal model of freely negotiated privacy. Neither Makoto's rival label nor the others' blame proves Sumire actually seeks the Player as a romantic prize. [A:P33–48; B:P1–8]

### EV-B0141-003 — autonomous enjoyment is experienced as disloyalty

Makoto explains that idol work had been only a way to please `お兄ちゃん`. She has begun enjoying singing and dancing with Nanastar and support from fans. At a recent live, she says, connection with the audience briefly took him out of her thoughts; she therefore feels broken, bad and traitorous and has avoided his face. These are Makoto's report and valuation, not proof that the Player required exclusivity or that the unseen live was objectively excellent. Sumire paraphrases the feared exchange: Makoto assumes being turned toward fans and peers means he will not look at her. [B:P9–20]

### EV-B0141-004 — a non-zero-sum reassurance is appropriated into omnipresence

Sumire says serious attention to fans and idol work is no betrayal; the Player watches their work even when they look elsewhere, and idol activity connects them. Makoto initially repeats that he can see her whether she looks at him or not. She then escalates to `お兄ちゃんは世界`, says he fills fans, friends and all places and imagines he cannot escape her until the world's end. Sumire expressly stops her and says that was not her meaning. This is a decisive correction: Sumire's narrower assurance should not be treated as her endorsement of ownership, omnipresence or unlimited access. Makoto says her distress has cleared and can face him again, but keeps calling Sumire a rival. [B:P20–39; C:P1–8]
