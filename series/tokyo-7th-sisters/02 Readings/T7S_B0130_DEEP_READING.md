# T7S_B0130 — Let's Meet a Magical Girl!

## Result and entering boundary

Manon asks to attend a mall character show for `超☆魔法少女マジでマジコ`; the Player agrees to accompany her after Coney arranges time off, and Momoka invites herself. At the mall they eat, joke about appearing to be a family, and then join a child-priority queue that proves much longer and hotter than expected. The Player has forgotten drinks; Momoka shares hers, and when the wait becomes unsafe to continue without help, Coney's advance bag supplies a cold sports drink and cooling items. Momoka escorts Manon to the restroom, then teases the waiting Player by delaying their return. They reach the front row. Coney unexpectedly hosts the show and, by the Player's account, directs a villain to take Manon as a staged hostage; the rescue leaves Manon crying with happiness, while Momoka is also absorbed. Both sleep afterward, and Manon murmurs a wish to go out with “Papa” again. The label arises earlier in Momoka's public family joke, but the sleeping referent is not fixed.
