# T7S_B0137 — Still on the way

## Result and entering boundary

Rishuri receives an overseas internship invitation from an admired fashion brand. She cares about both design and Nanasta; Sui urges her to take the opportunity while hiding her own grief, and Rishuri leaves abruptly. After she has gone, Sui cries with the Player and briefly misses lessons. Three months later Nanasta sees Rishuri's designs featured at a fashion show. Rishuri says she plans to return the next day and initially presents the internship as having shown her she is not ready for top-level design. Privately, she tells the Player she was asked to stay: her current design imagination centers on Nanasta's performers, and loneliness abroad made her doubt herself. She says she returns for her own creative life, not merely for Sui. The group welcomes the declared return, but actual resumed work, later career or a final choice between fields is not shown.

## Factual reconstruction by actor knowledge

### EV-B0137-001 — a craft friendship before the invitation

Rishuri is sketching; the Player and Sui praise a design. Sui asks her not to let Sui's opinion override her own sense, while Rishuri says Sui's view helps. They have been sewing together, and Rishuri has also taken swim instruction from Sui, whom she calls demanding but enjoyable. This establishes a specific reciprocal friendship and craft exchange, not that Sui owns Rishuri's designs. A joking privacy comment from Rishuri turns into explicit permission for the Player to look at the notebook; no broader permission is inferred. [A:P2–17]

### EV-B0137-002 — an overseas opportunity creates a pressured choice

Several days later Rishuri reports that a fashion brand she admired invited her to intern abroad; remaining in idol work may be hard. She says she is honestly torn between design ambition and current companions. The Player says he and Coney have reached a conclusion but does not state its terms, and Jedah asks for Rishuri's own wish. Sui urges a decisive move, calls hesitation unworthy of her and cites her daily design/sewing work. Rishuri chooses the internship, asks the Player to pack/send belongings immediately, says goodbye and predicts she will not return here. These are her stated decisions at that time, not a permanent contract, proved impossibility of dual careers or insight into every external term. [A:P19–50]

### EV-B0137-003 — Sui conceals grief to avoid influencing departure

Rona, Mimi and Jedah hurry to see Rishuri off, but Sui refuses to go because she fears weakening her friend's decision. She asks the Player to tell her when Rishuri has left and offers another errand as a reason to wait. Once apart, she says she wanted more shared time and recognition of her own growth, yet will not let that wish obstruct the dream. The Player praises her care, says he envies such friendship, and gives her permission to cry; both are shown grieving. Sui later pauses lessons for a while, returns outwardly composed, and the Player says three months pass. Outward composure is not proof that loss has ended; his general claim about adults prioritizing themselves is his reflection, not a universal fact. [A:P51–71; B:P1–43]

### EV-B0137-004 — public display and renewed absence anxiety

At a crowded fashion show three months later, Nanasta sees designs attributed to the intern Rishuri. Sui identifies one by familiar style; Mimi and Jedah see characteristic details and rejoice. Sui also says the success may take Rishuri beyond reach and asks the Player to allow some loneliness. The characters witness a show and recognize design authorship, but the chapter does not furnish a complete garment catalogue, independently assessed fashion success or a permanent employer offer. [C:P1–14]

### EV-B0137-005 — an announced return with a partial public account

Rishuri appears, says she plans to return to Nanasta the next day, and initially tells friends her skills were still immature for top-level design. Sui worries the dream has been surrendered; Rishuri says she is still in high school, has not abandoned design, and needs time to choose *with whom* to do what. Sui accepts that the path remains open and imagines someday wearing Rishuri's designs on a world stage; Rishuri promises to keep competing as an idol. These are future intentions, not a completed Nanasta work resumption, guaranteed top-designer career or proven failure of the internship. [C:P15–42]

### EV-B0137-006 — private correction: asked to stay, returning for herself

The Player asks privately whether the brand in fact tried to keep Rishuri; she confirms she was asked to stay. That is evidence of a request to continue, not necessarily a job offer or guaranteed employment. She says her best current design ideas arise around Nanasta members, and even imagined foreign designs were for Sui, Rona, Mimi and Jedah. She was lonely, knew no one there, felt dissatisfied with her designs and cried often. She insists the return is for herself rather than *for Sui*. The public `not good enough` account is therefore incomplete as an explanation of the decision, though her subjective self-critique need not be fabricated. This is a present creative preference, not proof she can never create elsewhere. [C:P44–60]

### EV-B0137-007 — private doubt and a welcomed declared return

Rishuri quietly asks whether someone who left so abruptly can come back and be accepted. The Player says she may change course while young and tells her friends still want her near. Sui interrupts; Rishuri asks that the private explanation stay between them. Prompted to speak the return ritual, Rishuri says `ただいま` and Sui says `おかえり`. That exchange confirms social welcome and Rishuri's current intention. The next-day move, re-enrollment, new work schedule and eventual fashion trajectory are outside the shown ending. [C:P61–82]

## Interpretation and alternatives

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0137-001.** Sui's forceful support protects Rishuri's apparent opportunity while concealing a real cost to herself. Rishuri's initial exit is her stated choice, yet the speed and Sui's `don't hesitate` pressure mean the scene should not be flattened into effortless consent or total certainty. Sui's later grief is compatible with genuine support. [A:P19–71; B:P1–36]

Rival: Sui secretly wants Rishuri to fail or, conversely, feels no loss because she encouraged departure. Her private account rejects both.

**ETHICAL_THEMATIC_INTERPRETATION — CL-B0137-002.** The later choice challenges a forced opposition between professional design and peer belonging. Rishuri discovers that, for now, her creative method is relational—she designs *for* familiar performers—without giving up a future top-designer aim. This is an actor-specific answer, not a general rule that artists need only one home. [A:P2–17,19–50; C:P15–60]

Rival: return means a failed dream, or design was merely a cover for Sui. She says she was asked to stay and returns for her own craft, while keeping both design and idol ambitions.

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0137-003.** Employer interest, Rishuri's subjective dissatisfaction, and her public inadequacy statement are different evidence. The private disclosure corrects the impression that she had no option to stay; it does not establish an employment offer, total external success or deliberate malicious deception of peers. [C:P1–60]

Rival: the show alone proves guaranteed professional acceptance, or the public `not ready` explanation is the whole reason. Neither covers the private request and motive.

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0137-004.** `ただいま`/`おかえり` repairs social belonging and makes the proposed return legible, but there is no scene of next-day Nanasta labor, school/visa/career terms or permanent co-location. The three-month gap is explicit inside this chapter; no other i-n-g case is thereby ordered. [B:P38–43; C:P15–82]

Rival: a welcome formula proves all institutional and future questions settled. It closes the interpersonal scene, not the whole career path.
