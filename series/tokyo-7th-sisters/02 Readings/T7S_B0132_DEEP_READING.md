# T7S_B0132 — Miwako Rock Festival

## Result and entering boundary

Nanasta has no `KUONJI-ROCK-FES` booking; Coney and the Player want members to experience a festival as audience and ask the experienced Miwako to guide them. She initially agrees, then nearly withdraws on seeing artists she strongly wants to watch: she fears her fan excitement will conflict with her escort role. Sumire's wish to learn and Ferb's wish to go with her help her recommit. Miwako then leads the group forward; peers report enjoying the intense experience, while two spectators remark on her energy.
