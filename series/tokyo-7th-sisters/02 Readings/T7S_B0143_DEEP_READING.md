# T7S_B0143 — Xiao's Sexy Assassin Training

## Result and entering boundary

The Player offers Xiao the lead in a foreign B-movie because of her kung-fu, but describes the role as a seductive assassin who lures men. He asks whether she wants to decline; Xiao, fifteen, elects to accept and recruits fourteen-year-old Susu, Miu and Makoto as informal teachers. Their lessons combine posing, reading another person's response and concealment/patience. Xiao then tests a violent `honey trap` on the Player, who calls out that it is not one and taps out. On the shoot a director praises her work, but she repeats a hold and flirtatious role-play on the Player despite his objections. Her altered speech impresses Makoto and affects the Player; Xiao herself becomes overwhelmed by a racing heart, cannot control the role, cries and accepts Miu's comfort. The chapter proves neither a finished film nor a maturely controlled seductive persona, and the comic presentation does not erase the Player's expressed stop cues or the unresolved safeguards around a minor's role.
