# T7S_B0149 — Your Back

## Result and entering boundary

After Sumire's well-received group stage, the Player proposes a solo live to help her “grow.” She accepts without sharing his clear growth horizon; what matters to her, increasingly, is whether he will see her work. His preparations keep him away from rehearsal for over two weeks by her account, and his replies arrive late. She asks him at least to attend the live. On show day a fragmentary report says he has been injured and is in hospital. Sumire briefly cannot rise from the floor and thinks she cannot sing without him, then recalls the brilliance of his forward-looking back and finds a reason to perform that is hers: she wants to shine beside him. She performs offscreen and visits his hospital room; he says he watched the stream and praises her, while declining to compare that mediated view lightly with Seven Sisters. This local recovery does not erase the poor communication, certify the live's sound/crowd response, or make her unfinished intimate thought into a confession.

### EV-B0149-002 — a solo plan with different meanings
