# T7S_B0118 — Swan Song for Someone Else

## Result and entering boundary

Tomoe struggles with a backing-dancer part for a Haru/Hime live and interprets Hime's demanding correction and Haru's defense as proof she is a burden. On show day, Haru and Hime are delayed by a reported monorail maintenance stop. Tomoe chooses to face their irritated audience alone, initially singing a self-deprecating introduction, then defending the absent performers and delivering an a cappella `SAKURA` while asking people to wait. Haru and Hime arrive, ask the Player to approve making Tomoe a co-lead, and secure a warm audience answer. Tomoe accepts the shared song and receives cheers; Hime apologizes for her harshness and she accepts continued membership. The new request for an encore with Tomoe as opening center, however, arrives without preparation and makes her vomit on stage. A fan's concern and promise to return follow. The chapter validates a specific protective action and audience reception without curing Tomoe's self-disqualification or physical overload. It also shows that peer correction can be incomplete: Hime apologizes for earlier pressure, then helps impose a fresh high-stakes center role.
