Calls or messages intrude on preparations for a three-person stream; the Player offers to excuse her if needed, and she declines. ## Factual reconstruction by actor knowledge

### EV-B0127-001 — interruptions with a withheld explanation

Miu appears frustrated by something she is trying to work out and by Holocom contact before the title card. During stream planning she barely registers the setlist, receives another contact, replies and says it is not an emergency. The Player offers to excuse her from the show if there is a serious matter; Miu declines. She asks whether adult friendships survive less frequent meetings, and the Player says he sees school friends less but still calls them friends. Murasaki summons her to change before he learns why she asked. No contact text or friend’s identity is exposed by the phone object. [A:P0–49]

### EV-B0127-002 — a scheduling conflict is mistaken for a trivial preference

After a stream Murasaki is dissatisfied and proposes more rehearsal with the same three members; Honoka agrees. Miu worries that time with a friend has already dwindled and reports being told she is less available. Murasaki initially says work should come before play and that a friend who cannot understand her idol work may not be much of a friend. Miu says the friend has no obligation to accommodate a vocation she chose and fears being selfish herself. Murasaki responds as if Miu would choose inferior performance; Miu denies that. The chapter reports Murasaki’s dissatisfaction, not a full technical performance audit. [B:P1–30]

### EV-B0127-003 — Honoka separates perspective from decision

Honoka stops the confrontation and asks for time to think. She describes having feared leaving old friends when she moved and still sometimes feeling lonely despite messages and new friends. Murasaki apologizes for imposing her own scale of value and suggests she and Honoka can practice while Miu considers. This revises Murasaki’s treatment of Miu’s concern, but the three do not yet negotiate a durable rehearsal schedule. [B:P31–48]

### EV-B0127-004 — Coney identifies the missing premise

Miu privately concludes she should concentrate on idol work so as not to burden the group. Coney asks whether her outside friend knows she is an idol; Miu says no. She has withheld it because telling the friend she pursues something she likes while reducing their time together feels hurtful. Coney recalls a friend she has not seen lately and says friendship can persist across absence, urging Miu to speak honestly. Miu says she will go to the friend. Coney’s aside about a “tofu girl” is not enough to identify that friend or establish a separate chronology. No direct conversation with Miu’s friend is presented. [C:P1–33]

### EV-B0127-005 — local team repair, external result unshown

The next day Miu joins Honoka and Murasaki for rehearsal. Murasaki apologizes for dismissing the worry; Miu also apologizes, then points to a nearly solved step and says she has a better understanding of friendship and will face what she wants to do. The group laughs when Murasaki starts before Miu has changed. Miu does not report the friend’s exact answer, the content of any disclosure, future invitations or a changed rehearsal calendar. Her renewed energy and local reconciliation are shown; a complete external-friendship repair is not. [C:P35–60]

## Interpretation and alternatives

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0127-001.** Miu’s conflict is sustained by information asymmetry as well as limited time. The friend’s complaint is known only through Miu, while the friend does not know the idol-work reason for repeated refusals; Murasaki’s first judgment rests on the opposite premise. [A:P18–40; B:P11–30; C:P8–18]

Rival: the friend knowingly disrespects Miu’s work. Miu directly says the friend has not been told. A reverse rival that Miu has no obligation to the group ignores the rehearsal/performance commitments she also values.

**ETHICAL_THEMATIC_INTERPRETATION — CL-B0127-002.** Honoka’s pause and Murasaki’s apology allow two memberships—friendship and team work—to remain serious without forcing immediate sacrifice of either. Miu’s initial wish to choose one simply to avoid burdening others is challenged, not replaced by a guaranteed balance. [B:P14–48; C:P1–4,35–58]

Rival: work always outranks friendship or friendship always outranks rehearsal. The episode refuses either fixed ranking; it also offers no worked scheduling solution.

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0127-003.** Coney’s example gives Miu permission to disclose and tolerate time apart; it is an autobiographical claim and situated advice, not a universal promise that every friendship survives absence. The offscreen friend’s answer cannot be inferred from Miu’s changed mood alone. [C:P8–33,35–60]

Rival: Miu demonstrably reconciled with the friend. She says she will go, then returns with insight, but the meeting and friend’s voice are omitted. The contrary claim that the friendship ended is equally unsupported.

**BOUNDED INFERENCE — CL-B0127-004.** Murasaki repairs her *team* relation by withdrawing the claim that she can measure Miu’s friendship from outside. This makes subsequent rehearsal possible without establishing perfect performance or a final allocation of time. [B:P20–48; C:P35–60]

Rival: her apology solves the schedule. Neither a calendar nor the friend’s response appears; local respect is the narrower demonstrated change.
