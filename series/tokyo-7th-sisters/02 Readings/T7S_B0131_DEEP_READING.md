# T7S_B0131 — Yumeno Amnesia

## Result and entering boundary

Yumeno repeatedly pursues Hime for unwanted physical intimacy. Hime resists, including a defensive kick, and Yumeno later runs toward her again before an accident whose exact mechanism is not shown. A doctor reports minor surface injuries and a memory impairment likely related to the shock: Yumeno retains daily functioning and performance skills but does not recognize Nanasta or her peers. During recovery she is polite, asks Ferb and Hime to be friends anew and says she could live without the missing memories, while trying to regain them because her friends are distressed. Peers offer cues; Hime even offers her hand, but none restores memory. Hime then reasons, without clinical authority, that a startling kiss might work. Before any kiss is shown, Yumeno notices danger, shields Hime and is involved in a second accident. Afterward she behaves as before and says she has returned, again pursuing Hime despite explicit refusal. Neither the temporal pairing of impact and memory return nor the comic ending validates a treatment, certifies her post-accident health or erases the consent problem that preceded the first accident.

## Factual reconstruction by actor knowledge

### EV-B0131-001 — eroticized pursuit meets Hime's clear refusal

Yumeno tells Ferb she is preoccupied with Hime and recounts an earlier encounter in which she moved toward Hime for a hug, then repeatedly bargained for a kiss on lips, cheek or forehead. Hime tells her to release her and refuses each substitute; she kicks to create distance, while Yumeno treats the kick as an exciting souvenir. Ferb calls it a hardship for Hime. Soon Yumeno sees Hime again and runs toward her to resume the kiss pursuit despite Ferb's warning. The episode's comedic form and Yumeno's pleasure do not turn Hime's refusal into consent. [A:P2–33]

### EV-B0131-002 — first accident and bounded medical account

At the hospital the Player says he contacted Yumeno's family and identifies himself as a related party rather than guardian. The doctor says she is asleep, with scratches and no immediate threat to life, but then Yumeno wakes unable to recognize those present. In the next scene the doctor calls it a probable shock-related memory disorder, says daily-life memories appear intact and offers only a possibility that contact with familiar things might trigger return; permanence is also possible. This is the chapter's clinician report, not a broader diagnosis or clearance for later injury. [A:P30–53; B:P1–11]

### EV-B0131-003 — one continuing person with a changed memory relation

Yumeno asks Ferb and Hime to become her friends again; they affirm they already are. After discharge she calls Xiao formally and asks for help living as before. She can still sing and dance, which Ferb observes and Yumeno speculates may be bodily memory. Yumeno says the missing memories might not trouble her much personally, but her friends' sad faces make her want them back. The contrast in manner is real, yet it does not create a replacement identity, demonstrate a change in orientation or license the group to define her welfare exclusively by their nostalgia.
