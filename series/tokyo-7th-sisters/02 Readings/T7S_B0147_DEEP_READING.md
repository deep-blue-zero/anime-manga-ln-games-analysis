# T7S_B0147 — Hime's Lady Training

## Result and entering boundary

Hime accompanies Shizuka to a Kuonji social party so Shizuka will have a friend among adult guests. She trains in formal dress, dining and dance, then enters under a fabricated identity as Shizuka's cousin. The training lets her pass as an elite “lady,” but it also hurts, exhausts and conceals her familiar speech and tofu-shop position. Coney has secretly arranged for the Player to attend; he fails to recognize Hime, praises and attempts to scout her disguised persona, leaving her stung that he cannot see *her*. When a camera-bearing intruder is announced, Hime pursues him through the venue, discards her heels and loses her wig. Shizuka says she has enlivened the party, and the Player finally recognizes and praises the expanded range of the Hime he already recruited. Hime asks him to lead a dance while knowing she is Nonohara Hime. The case adds a public form of self-presentation; it does not make the false cousin claim true, validate every coercive lesson or establish secure event procedures.

## Factual reconstruction by presentation and actor knowledge

### EV-B0147-001 — family continuity before the transformation

Hime's father urges her not to be late; younger brother and sister supply her bag and bicycle key, and she leaves them in charge of the tofu shop with him. She pauses to tell her mother she will be cuter than yesterday. After Hime departs, her father identifies the object of her daily speech as his deceased wife's portrait and treats Hime as already lovely. This establishes a continuing address to her mother and a functioning household exchange, not the mother's independent current judgment or a measured shift in shop labor. The foreground object is a tofu-shop facade, not an inspectable portrait in the selected art. [A:P2–19]

### EV-B0147-002 — Shizuka's loneliness and Hime's conditional offer

Shizuka's peers worry about a Kuonji party attended mostly by wealthy adults and without her grandfather. Shizuka says its purpose is family-affiliate networking, not her enjoyment, and calls solitude familiar. Hime volunteers to be a conversation partner. She first assumes this is an ordinary party, then learns dress, etiquette and ballroom dance are expected and protests that she lacks them. Shizuka says she will not force her; Hime nonetheless commits when she sees Shizuka's disappointment. Her care is chosen but made under friendship pressure, not frictionless leisure or a contractual requirement. Shizuka suggests she can represent Hime as a relation to gain entry; that presentation will be fabricated. [A:P23–74]

### EV-B0147-003 — strenuous training and the mother's remembered wish

With the Player away, Coney stays at the women's dorm to teach posture and etiquette. Shizuka corrects heels and small steps; Haru tries dresses, joking that the heels make escape hard; Rona studies dining manners to help; Sui practices as dance partner. Hime reports toe pain and resists being used as a dress-up doll. Coney threatens to withhold dinner for poor fork use, and Rona promises doughnuts later; delivery is not shown. The lesson is comic but the threatened food denial and painful footwear are real stated costs, not endorsed pedagogy. At home Hime's father recalls the mother imagining a grown Hime at a ballroom with a handsome escort. Hime privately promises to try being cute but asks her mother not to expect too much. This is family memory and Hime's response, not evidence that her mother would demand elite conformity. [A:P78–157]

### EV-B0147-004 — a fabricated cousin gains recognition

On party day Hime arrives nervous but Shizuka says Hime's presence makes this gathering enjoyable for the first time. Shizuka presents Hime to the guests as `久遠寺ヒメ`, her cousin from far away. Hime sustains formal speech and a long wig; male guests praise her beauty, composure and supposed Kuonji standing. She compares terrine to tofu under cover of etiquette and feels excessively watched. Shizuka's pleasure is real, but the guests' class and kinship assumptions rest on an agreed masquerade. Hime has not become a Kuonji relative or repudiated the tofu shop. [B:P2–52]

### EV-B0147-005 — surprise partner and the wound of misrecognition

When several men press to be Hime's first dance partner, Shizuka points to the newly arrived Player. Coney arranged his invitation without warning Hime; Shizuka repeats Coney's rationale that surprise tests a lady. The Player believes Hime is Shizuka's cousin, asks to dance and admits he is inexperienced. Hime guides the waltz, struggling not to reveal Nanastar knowledge. He admires her movement, family feeling and potential, then attempts to recruit this “new” idol prospect. Hime asks why he does not notice who she is. His second scout attempt recognizes qualities he values but fails the personal recognition she wants; it is not an actual second recruitment or evidence he prefers this constructed class persona. [B:P56–135]

### EV-B0147-006 — intrusion and unsafe pursuit

Hime hears a noise, security announces a suspected paparazzo in the hall and requests reports of suspicious conduct. Hime, wanting to protect Shizuka, confronts a camera-bearing man and pursues him after he flees. Along the route he pushes a maid and spills wine in the kitchen; Hime removes her heels and chases through the venue. Her strength is attributed to deliveries and lessons, not aristocratic training alone. The staged action culminates in the man's cry and Hime's victory line, but the source does not show a camera-content audit, formal detention, injuries, venue review or an approved security plan. Hime's protective action and the risk of an unsupervised chase must both be retained. [C:P10–80]

### EV-B0147-007 — the wig falls, but the event does not reject her

After the chase the wig falls and Hime apologizes for ruining the party. Two male guests instead call the supposed Kuonji daughter energetic; Shizuka says Hime became the gathering's center despite imperfect formal-lady conduct. The guests do not explicitly retract the false cousin premise. Their amused response is local social acceptance, not verified equality between elite visitors and tofu-shop workers or a security sign-off. [C:P81–94]

### EV-B0147-008 — recognition of the same Hime in more than one style

The Player finally recognizes Hime and says her wig and conduct changed his impression. Hime is hurt he missed her after their history, but he calls the new side a surprising talent and says he is proud to have recruited *Nonohara Hime*. She relates his praise to becoming cuter than yesterday, asks him to lead a new dance knowing who she is, and invokes her mother's ballroom dream. He agrees and takes her hand; Shizuka calls her a wonderful lady. The dance request is mutual and affectionate but does not by itself establish a romantic confession, future escort commitment or literal completion of all her mother's hopes. [C:P95–128]

The wig-off turn does not prove etiquette worthless; her prepared dance and manners helped her accompany Shizuka. It does deny that one elite performance exhausts Hime's worth. Rival: she must become an aristocratic woman to be cute, or authentic Hime can never wear a dress. Both are defeated by her own continuing use of dress/dance after recognition.

Rival: all preparation is harmless friendship or the disguise gives Hime real Kuonji status. The text establishes neither.

**ETHICAL_THEMATIC_INTERPRETATION — CL-B0147-003.** The Player's failure to identify Hime has two non-equivalent effects: a painful failure to see a known person and an unanticipated recognition of her range. Her final request asks to be seen as Hime *while* taking a new form, not to have the first hurt retroactively erased. [B:P80–135; C:P95–128]

Rival: the mistaken second scout proves he loves an unknown elite woman more, or his later praise means the hurt never mattered. Neither follows.

Hime's chase disrupts the intruder yet also moves risk through the venue. [C:P10–80]

Rival: a comic capture erases privacy/safety questions or establishes a formal arrest. The source ends short of those outcomes.

**ETHICAL_THEMATIC_INTERPRETATION — CL-B0147-005.** Hime's address to her deceased mother and the father's remembered ballroom fantasy give the final dance emotional weight, while keeping her mother a remembered relation rather than a present judge.
