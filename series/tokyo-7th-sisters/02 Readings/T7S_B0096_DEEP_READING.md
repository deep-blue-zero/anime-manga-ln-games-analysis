## Factual reconstruction

Shisara explains that she entered the old contest without deep investment, then tried to become the successor others expected after winning. Comparison to Seven Sisters damaged peer relations, and an unspecified failure preceded her disappearance months later. She values being seen on any stage but experiences Nanasta's group brightness as inaccessible. The Player and Coney build a holographic reconstruction of a historical Seven Sisters audience/stage environment. Other members begin a spontaneous one-song rehearsal. Shisara joins, asks them to help her, and experiences the stage as co-presence rather than judgment. She then chooses the collective future of surpassing Seven Sisters together. Tomoe is among the participants, but source order alone cannot establish this chapter after B0080 in a total chronology.
