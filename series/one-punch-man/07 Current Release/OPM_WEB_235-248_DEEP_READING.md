## Prospective synthesis

### 1. Competing institutions are competing ways of making people legible

Web 235-236 introduces Neo Heroes through invitations, facilities, money, recruitment and publicity rather than through a single moral doctrine. The Hero Association reads each defection as loss of institutional assets; Neo Heroes reads established people as transferable prestige and launch capacity. Both views are partially accurate and reductive. The alternative organization is materially credible, but its ethical authority remains unproven. [web235/pages 2-35; web236/pages 2-32]

That distinction matters because the tranche immediately leaves formal institutions and gives King the same problem in miniature. He says he is weak and seeks instruction; the people he approaches cannot fit that statement to the “King” they believe they know. His reputation functions like institutional data: socially powerful, practically obstructive and resistant to correction. [web237/pages 2-35]

### 2. Training becomes a question of consent and survival

Flash attempts to understand Saitama by assigning him a lower place in a familiar hierarchy. Saitama's refusal is not a counter-school or a rival doctrine; it is a refusal to let Flash's measurement create authority. [web238/pages 7-32]

The childhood material then shows why hierarchy matters to Flash and Sonic. The ninja village does not merely teach technique. It narrows attachment, converts children into instruments and makes survival itself the curriculum. Flash and Sonic form a bond inside that system, but the bond neither redeems the system nor emerges untouched by it. Their adult rivalry preserves intimacy, resentment, comparison and incompatible self-narration at once. [web241/pages 2-23]

### 3. Recovery and elimination are not the same heroic act

Blast has the strongest tactical position in the Empty Void material, yet his aim is not exhausted by defeating a threat. His behavior and explanation preserve a personal history and a wish to recover Void from God's influence. That makes restraint purposeful rather than merely merciful. It also keeps the causal model open: the captured material does not isolate how much belongs to Void, God, dimensional technique or their interaction. [web240/pages 8-27; web245/pages 8-20; web247/pages 2-15]

The direct counterexample is the village's production logic, where a damaged or disobedient person is replaceable. Blast's recovery aim assigns continuing value to a person whose body and action have become dangerous.

### 4. Saitama breaks the frame without supplying a theory

The visual scale of web 246-248 escalates through craters, huge bodies, spatial distortion and dimensional attack. Saitama's intervention is funny because it is materially decisive and interpretively incomplete. He does not validate Flash's hierarchy, Blast's model or Void's scale. He simply reveals that all three are insufficient to contain the event. [web248/pages 4-20]

That is not evidence that Saitama understands God, dimensional mechanics or Void's condition. The correct provisional claim is narrower: his action defeats the local measurement frame.

## Visual and register observations

- Neo Heroes is introduced through staged group compositions, invitation material and executive reaction shots; institutional scale precedes any settled ethical identity.
- King's training search repeatedly places his plain self-description against faces and bodies already responding to the myth.
- Flash/Sonic action uses mirrored motion and coordinated attack to show learned common grammar beneath verbal antagonism.
- Childhood pages compress bodies into regimented, hostile spaces; adult battle pages expand movement, making technical freedom visibly inseparable from traumatic formation.
- Blast/Void pages reserve large black fields, dimensional cuts and spatial discontinuity for mechanisms that remain only partially explained.
- Saitama's ordinary diction and blank framing refuse the elevated register around him without becoming explanatory exposition.

## Strongest counter-reading

One could read the tranche as disconnected setup: a Neo Heroes prologue, a King gag and a spectacular ninja rewrite. That is structurally plausible. The stronger connection is nevertheless repeated and source-grounded: every segment asks who gets to define another person's capacity—recruiters, public myth, masters, village, rival, former partner or cosmic patron. The answer is never simply “the strongest observer.”
