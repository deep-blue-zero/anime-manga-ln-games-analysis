## Executive synthesis

The uncollected layer extends V37's question—how is value known?—into a larger question: **what happens when institutions turn people into information systems?**

Neo Heroes promises to fix Hero Association weakness through capital, recruitment, equipment, telemetry, publicity and coordinated deployment. It produces real capacity. It also makes bodies, reputations and combat patterns administratively legible. Webigaza's modification, Darkshine's reassignment, Blue's inherited brand, Suiryu's livestreamed work and the New Blood operations show that legibility can support, market or control a person.

The same structure appears outside Neo Heroes:

- King's false reputation prevents accurate training.
- Flash's hierarchy fails to measure Saitama.
- The ninja village turns children into reproducible technique.
- Blast resists treating Empty Void as disposable output.
- Amai Mask's public image cannot contain the relation between body and conduct.
- Machine God Mirror turns readable hero techniques into a copied weapon.
- Garou and Suiryu learn one another through reciprocal adjustment rather than data extraction.
- Saitama repeatedly resolves a local event while remaining unavailable to the system that wants the result to be measurable, ownable and narratable.

The provisional arc is a conflict between forms of recognition and, increasingly, between claims of self-authorship and control over the conditions of action. In web285-287, a suit moves its wearer, Neo maintenance limits Webigaza's exit, satellite search pursues Amai, and A uses a hero affiliation while describing killing as a game. Child Emperor, Sekingar and Metal Bat's covert cooperation supplies a countercurrent inside the same institution.

## Major provisional findings

### 1. Neo Heroes is a credible institution and an unresolved threat

The organization recruits dissatisfied talent, mobilizes quickly and coordinates simultaneous response. Its competence is textually real. A critique that treats every Neo success as fake would reproduce the Hero Association's defensive blindness. [web235/pages 2-35; web266/pages 2-17; web268/pages 2-19]

The ethical danger comes from how success is produced: suits, modifications, operators, ratings, publicity, bodily maintenance and opaque handlers. Web285-287 changes the warrant: the wearer reports an involuntary lunge after an update; the superior describes exploiting public anxiety while accumulating data; Webigaza explicitly reports life-dependent maintenance and inability to leave. These are specific mechanisms and attributed statements, not merely a generalized suspicion of technology. [web251/pages 11-16; web253/pages 2-10; web267/pages 2-16; web285/pages 3-8; web286/pages 9-11; PR-CR-015,016,022]

### 2. Reputation is causal even when false or incomplete

King's legend prevents accurate instruction. Blue's lineage claim supplies authority before verification. Amai's ideal image disciplines his actual body. Garou's history shapes what others can see in his present. Reputation is not “mere misunderstanding”; it changes available action. [web237/pages 2-35; web253/pages 11-17; web255/pages 2-17; web279/pages 2-15]

### 3. The body is neither proof of virtue nor a neutral container

Darkshine's powerful body coexists with fear. Webigaza's engineered body coexists with self-claimed agency and explicitly reported dependency. Amai's monster body coexists with violent history and genuine aspiration. Genos's upgraded body is both capacity and readable machine surface. Garou's disciplined body preserves a history he is not simply repeating. The series keeps ethical judgment at the level of relation and conduct rather than silhouette. [web251/pages 2-16; web263/pages 2-15; web275/pages 2-17; web282/pages 2-24]

### 4. Care is the refusal to make a dangerous person disposable

Blast's aim toward Empty Void, Saitama's response to Amai and Bang's continued observation of Garou differ in tone and history. Each nevertheless preserves a person beyond a threat label. That preservation is not acquittal. It is the claim that control, defeat and moral knowledge are not identical acts. [web245/pages 8-20; web262/pages 8-21; web283/pages 9-17]

### 5. Saitama remains an epistemic interruption

Saitama does not found the better institution. He repeatedly notices or does something the surrounding model cannot process:

- he refuses Flash's disciple frame;
- he interrupts Empty Void's scale;
- he recognizes Amai's heroic conduct after bodily exposure;
- he defeats a machine assembled from legible hero signatures.

His limitation is equally important: these exact recognitions do not automatically become durable public systems. Saitama can reveal a model's failure without replacing the model. [web238/pages 7-32; web248/pages 4-20; web263/pages 2-15; web277/pages 9-17]

## Institutional model

The Hero Association remains burdened by corruption, sponsor logic, reputation error and weak coordination. Neo Heroes answers those failures with operational modernity. The current web layer warns that modernity can intensify the same basic mistake: treating a partial measure as the whole person.

A durable judgment therefore requires four separate questions:

1. Did the system produce useful action?
2. What did the action cost the acting body?
3. Who owns the data, equipment and public story?
4. Can the person refuse, leave or contradict the role?

The captured material answers the first question repeatedly and leaves the latter three increasingly urgent.

## October synthesis: agency under dependence

Webigaza is the crucial counterexample to a simple model of technological victimhood. She describes choosing strength as the expression she could not achieve through training, accepting modification, and refusing regret. She also says she cannot leave Neo because continued life depends on its maintenance facilities. Both claims matter. Choice at entry and control over future exit are different ethical questions; her refusal to blame others cannot make every future institutional act consensual. [web286/pages 4-11; PR-CR-021,022]

Child Emperor and Metal Bat do not respond by reducing her to a dupe. Child Emperor listens and tentatively interprets the resentment that sustains her ideal; Metal Bat sees serious resolve. Sekingar supplies the structural suspicion that adults preserved uncertainty around the bargain. Their coordinated plan to protect Amai and interfere with pursuit brings care into conflict with the organization employing them. The plan remains future action. [web287/pages 5-7; PR-CR-025,026]

The resulting institutional critique is precise: Neo's useful resources can produce real rescues while also making its members governable. The involuntary lunge, maintenance barrier, reported satellite pursuit and A's attack demonstrate different forms of constraint. They must not be collapsed into one proven controller or one universal mechanism. [web285/pages 3-8; web286/pages 9-11; web287/pages 2-4,9-12]
