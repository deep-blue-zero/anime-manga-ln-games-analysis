## Prospective synthesis

### 1. Neo Heroes becomes infrastructure, not merely a rival brand

Web 264-270 moves Neo Heroes from invitation to deployment. Facilities, suits, operators, data feeds and leader specialization produce genuine coverage across simultaneous incidents. “New Blood” is not empty propaganda: people are rescued and monsters are fought. [web266/pages 2-17; web268/pages 2-19; web269/pages 2-30]

Its ambiguity lies in the same infrastructure. The more the organization coordinates bodies through suits and telemetry, the more a hero becomes a measurable endpoint in a managed system. Performance data can support a fighter, market a fighter or make the fighter reproducible.

### 2. Webigaza makes system cost visible

Webigaza's solo effectiveness is among Neo Heroes' strongest demonstrations, yet the sequence keeps heat, modification and monitoring in the frame. The body does not disappear beneath the score. [web267/pages 2-16]

This prevents two reductions. She is neither a helpless machine nor proof that enhancement is frictionless empowerment. She acts, chooses and excels through a body whose continuing operating conditions other people can observe and potentially control.

### 3. The mass crisis defeats exclusive institutional credit

Web 271-273 distributes threats among Neo leaders, Hero Association figures and independent intervention. A single command center can see part of the field, but the page sequence repeatedly cuts beyond any one organization's ownership of the event. [web271/pages 2-20; web272/pages 2-17; web273/pages 2-15]

Tatsumaki's intervention sharpens the distinction between outcome and management. Her force can settle a local threat while media and institutions still struggle to classify what happened. Destructive capacity is legible; the whole causal field is not.

### 4. Machine God Mirror weaponizes legibility

The Machine God sequence makes combat data material. It reads, reproduces and recombines known signatures; the heroes' visible techniques become an attack library. Genos's upgraded body provides real resistance, but it also belongs to the technological field the machine can analyze. [web274/pages 12-16; web275/pages 2-17; web276/pages 2-12]

Saitama does not win by producing the better record. He breaks the machine's premise: the available model cannot make his action commensurable with the stored techniques. [web277/pages 9-17; web278/pages 2-8]

The correct inference is not that data is useless. Data coordinates Neo deployments and makes Mirror dangerous. It fails when treated as exhaustive.

### 5. Garou's return is social before it is heroic

Web 279 restores Garou through ordinary work, teasing, transit and training context. That is meaningful because reinsertion is not staged as an instant heroic absolution. He is still physically exceptional and socially unsettled. [web279/pages 2-15]

Suiryu's encounter converts their bodies into a mutual reading problem. Each recognizes unusual capacity; neither initially possesses the other's history. Their escalating exchange is playful, competitive and dangerous without becoming a settled moral ranking. Bang's observation supplies generational context, not a final verdict. [web280/pages 7-17; web281/pages 2-17; web282/pages 2-24; web283/pages 2-15]

Garou's restraint and labor strengthen a rehabilitation reading. They do not prove restitution is complete or that every harmed person would accept it.

### 6. The latest installment deliberately reopens the board

Web 284 returns to meetings, reports, injured or absent heroes, prison/containment problems and unresolved transformed actors. Its function is connective. It refuses the impression that the multi-city crisis, Amai fallout or Neo program can be evaluated through one battle result. [web284/pages 2-17]

Because the installment is setup-heavy and currently terminal, causal claims about a final conspiracy, controller or next opponent remain OPEN.

## Visual and register observations

- Neo combat is frequently paired with screens, operators and numerical framing; the hero is both body and data point.
- Simultaneous-city cuts make “the event” larger than any room that claims to coordinate it.
- Machine God Mirror's repeated limbs, copied forms and composite attacks visualize data aggregation as bodily monstrosity.
- Saitama's intervention is given less explanatory apparatus than the attacks it invalidates.
- Garou's work clothes, vehicle ride and street-scale setting reduce cosmic aftermath to awkward daily continuity.
- Garou/Suiryu choreography emphasizes reading, imitation, feint and adjustment rather than a simple finishing contest.

## Strongest counter-reading

The mass attacks could be read as simple tournament display for a new cast, and Garou/Suiryu as a detached fan-service fight. Both readings capture surface structure. The stronger connection is that every sequence tests what can be learned from a body in motion. Neo Heroes records it, Mirror copies it, Saitama exceeds it, and Garou/Suiryu learn it relationally. The difference between extraction and mutual adaptation is the tranche's most important formal contrast.
