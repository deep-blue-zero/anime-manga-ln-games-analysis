# One Punch Man — V13 Japanese Dialogue and Register Audit

## Audit result

**PASS.** The load-bearing V13 claims in `OPM_V13_DEEP_READING.md` are supportable from the Japanese tankobon at the V12 observational ceiling. No V14+ wording is required to stabilize them.

The highest-risk interpretive traps are:

- treating Monster Cell conversion as proof that every use of `怪人` refers to the same biological state;
- treating Haragiri's convergent body/identity/affiliation case as a universal monster ontology;
- converting Gyoro Gyoro's statement about monster violent impulse into the Monster Association's complete purpose;
- treating Suiryu's philosophical claims as narrator-certified truth;
- treating Saitama's martial-arts definition as narrator judgment that technique is purely cosmetic;
- treating Saitama's Charanko cover speech as a literal current self-report;
- treating Suiryu's official championship as proof that he is stronger than Saitama;
- treating Amai Mask's performance language as transparent private confession or, conversely, treating his lethal hero register as proof that all idol sentiment is false;
- treating audience/staff interpretations of Amai as direct access to his private motives.

# Register summary through V13

## Saitama

V13 remains blunt, low-ceremony, and analogy-heavy. His most characteristic language moves are:

- mundane category deflation (`トウモロコシ`);
- direct embodied requests (`体験`);
- practical irritation around the wig;
- advice framed through the other person's stated objective rather than elevated doctrine;
- intentionally shallow-looking labels when a domain's functional necessity is not available to him.

Do not mistake low-register wording for absence of inference.

## Suiryu

Suiryu's register is relaxed, self-assured, socially fluid, and frequently philosophical without becoming formally elevated. `楽しい`, `自由`, ease, boredom, and personal choice remain central. Under comparative defeat, the language tightens around `負けたまま` and finally the stark `負けた……初めて…`.

This is a high-value stress-register transition.

## Atomic Samurai

Rough, decisive, contemptuous when appropriate: `バカじゃねぇ`, `潰す`. He does not become ceremonially philosophical simply because he is among master swordsmen.

## Tatsumaki

Imperious and role-evaluative. `人手`, `働き`, and contemptuous group language make the efficiency standard explicit.

## Amai Mask

V13 strongly confirms register compartmentalization:

- idol: universal love/peace/gratitude;
- hero combat: cold/punitive lethal language;
- post-combat public role: immediate smiling continuity;
- staff relation: acknowledged boundary between accessible idol and forbidding hero side.

# Final audit disposition

**PASS / CANONICAL.**

The V13 deep reading's strongest claims survive source-language scrutiny provided the following controls remain attached:

1. Monster Cell conversion is an observed pathway, not universal monster ontology.
2. Haragiri's four-way convergence does not erase Garou's previously separate body/identity/affiliation/conduct profile.
3. Suiryu's hero/fatalism claims are character positions contested by adjacent action.
4. Saitama's martial-arts conclusion is observer-relative and does not become narrator doctrine.
5. the Charanko cover line is role-conditioned false-biography maintenance.
6. official tournament victory and experienced comparative victory remain distinct.
7. Amai Mask's performer and hero registers are both canonical behaviors; neither alone authorizes a total private-self claim.
8. audience and staff interpretations are evidence about public narrative/knowledge boundaries, not omniscient access to Amai's motives.

Checkpoint-A may now be reopened for genuinely diagnostic held-out adjudication. The Japanese audit itself does not choose validation scenes.
