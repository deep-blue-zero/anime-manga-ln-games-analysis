# One Punch Man — V12 Japanese Dialogue and Register Audit

## Audit result

**PASS.** The load-bearing V12 character, relationship, power-philosophy, institutional, and public-narrative claims in `OPM_V12_DEEP_READING.md` are supportable from the Japanese tankobon. No V13+ wording is required to stabilize them.

This audit protects against several especially easy V12 mistranslations or overextensions:

- Suiryu's fatalistic language is **his character position**, not narrator-certified social theory.
- Saitama asks to **experience** Bakuzan's martial art; the wording does not imply reverence or submission to a martial hierarchy.
- Genos explicitly accepts an opponent being superior in a **domain**; this is not generalized self-abasement.
- Fubuki's `味方` language and restraint establish real relational responsibility without erasing prior coercive hierarchy.
- Tatsumaki's `足手まとい` framing is dismissive/operationally hierarchical even though she protects Fubuki and nonlethally neutralizes the group.
- `規格外` means outside the expected standard/model in context; it is not an official disaster-level label.
- `怪人王` / `オロチ様` establishes an observed Monster Association hierarchy but not every hidden fact about ultimate authority.
- King's assassins are repeatedly **projecting** power and virtue onto him. Their conclusions cannot be cited as evidence that King possesses the physical/tactical abilities they imagine.

## 1. Suiryu — strength as easy-living autonomy

### Locator
`OPM|V12|chapter:62|image:0025`

### Source wording
`テキトーに楽して生きてくためだけど 俺の方が強くてごめんな`

### Audit
`テキトーに` gives the line its casual, unserious register. `楽して生きてく` is not a high-minded doctrine of freedom; it is closer to living easily / taking life easy without excessive burden.

`俺の方が強くてごめんな` is a taunting mock-apology. The line should not be softened into neutral confidence.

### Supported claim
Suiryu connects strength to low-burden personal autonomy and speaks with relaxed superiority.

### Do not promote
- “Suiryu trains primarily to protect freedom as a political value.”
- “Suiryu has no goals beyond laziness.”
- “Suiryu believes strength itself is morally good.”

## 4. Genos — domain-specific inferiority and growth

### Locator
`OPM|V12|chapter:64|image:0091`

### Source wording
`俺はこんな兵器は実装したくなかったんだが`

`クセーノ博士の言う通りだった…`

`どうしても相手が上回る分野については受け入れなければ成長することはできない`

### Audit
The load-bearing noun is `分野` — field/domain. Genos is not saying “I must accept that everyone is stronger than me.” He is saying that when there is a domain in which the opponent surpasses him, refusal to accept that fact blocks growth.

`実装したくなかった` also matters. He had resisted implementing the countermeasure. The later explanation confirms that using it irritates him because it acknowledges that his own speed does not match the opponent's.

### Supported claim
Genos combines capability pride with explicit domain-specific epistemic humility and pragmatic compensation.

### Do not promote
- “Genos has become humble in every respect.”
- “Genos no longer cares about matching opponents directly.”
- “Genos admits overall inferiority to Awakened Cockroach.”

## 10. `味方` versus `足手まとい`

### Locators
`OPM|V12|chapter:65|images:0131-0133`

### Fubuki
`待って お姉ちゃん！彼らは味方よ!!`

### Tatsumaki
`こういうのは味方じゃなくて足手まといって言うのよ`

### Audit
The lexical opposition is explicit.

- Fubuki insists on `味方` — allies/our side.
- Tatsumaki replaces that category with `足手まとい` — burden/hindrance.

Tatsumaki does **not** kill them; she immobilizes them. Therefore `足手まとい` supports dismissive operational classification, not indifference to whether they live.

### Supported claim
The sisters differ in whether weaker associated people remain meaningful allies under crisis.

## 12. Suiryu — talent, selection, and anti-obligation

### Locators
`OPM|V12|chapter:66|images:0143-0146`

### Load-bearing wording
`才能`

`弱い生き物が淘汰されていくのは自然の理だ`

`助けたければ勝手にどうぞ`

`その場しのぎで平和を演出したって 世界は大して変わらないだろう`

### Audit
`淘汰` and `自然の理` give Suiryu's speech an explicitly natural-selection/fatalist frame. But `助けたければ勝手にどうぞ` is equally important: he does not say rescue must be prohibited. He treats it as optional personal choice.

`平和を演出` frames local rescue as staging/producing an appearance of peace; it is dismissive of structural significance.

### Supported claim
Suiryu holds a detached fatalistic/anti-obligation position at V12: strong actors need not accept hero duty, weaker beings being selected out is natural, and local rescue does not transform the world in his view.

### Do not promote
- “The manga endorses social Darwinism.”
- “Suiryu wants civilians to die.”
- “Suiryu says helping others is wrong.”

The line belongs to Suiryu and is contested by Snek and by the surrounding action montage.

## 14. Drive Knight — source-seeking and coercive intelligence

### Locators
`OPM|V12|chapter:67|images:0171-0175`

### Source wording
`しかしヒーロー協会は情報不足ときた`

`そこで相談がある`

`飛んでるコバエをいくら潰してもキリがないからな ウジ虫の発生源を押さえておきたいのだ`

`お前達がどこから湧いて出てきたか教えてくれないか?`

### Audit
The apparently polite `相談` / `教えてくれないか` is immediately embedded inside extreme coercion. Drive Knight's calm register heightens, rather than reduces, the threat.

`発生源` makes the strategic goal explicit: stop treating outputs and locate the source.

`拷問`, `苦痛`, and `洗いざらい` leave no basis for sanitizing the method as merely “firm questioning.”

### Supported claim
Drive Knight prioritizes intelligence/source localization and is willing to threaten torture as an instrumental method.

### Do not promote
- ultimate allegiance;
- hidden moral motive;
- “Drive Knight enjoys torture” as a stable trait. The scene shows willingness/threat, not enough evidence for enjoyment.

# 21. Register summary by character

## Saitama
Still plain, compressed, low-deference, practical. Curiosity is expressed through direct embodied requests rather than formal respect language. Gaming frustration with King is ordinary and unguarded.

## Genos
Analytical declaratives remain prominent. V12 adds unusually explicit metacognitive language about accepting superior domains and growth. No collapse of serious/technical register after defeat.

## Suiryu
Casual `俺`, relaxed superiority, rhetorical ease. His philosophical speech remains conversational rather than solemn doctrinal exposition, which is important to his anti-burden stance.

## Snek
Internal register is more reflective and vulnerable than his earlier status-facing exterior. V12 materially expands his language model beyond professional defensiveness.

## Fubuki
Command/leadership register persists, but sister scenes and ally-protection language reveal genuine urgency and relational ownership. `お姉ちゃん` is a strong relationship-conditioned register shift.

## Tatsumaki
Imperious certainty remains stable. Protective content does not soften the hierarchical form; she often speaks as the person who will unilaterally solve the problem.

## Drive Knight
Controlled, calm, instrumentally polite phrasing can coexist with severe coercive content. Do not model him as emotionally explosive from this scene.

## Garou
Confident analytic self-talk remains central. `見切った` makes the certainty level especially visible and therefore especially useful when the next page stress-tests it.

## King
Large public myth and modest private language diverge. His actual speech is often restrained, ordinary, hesitant, or socially self-monitoring; observers write grand tactical/ethical meaning into it.
