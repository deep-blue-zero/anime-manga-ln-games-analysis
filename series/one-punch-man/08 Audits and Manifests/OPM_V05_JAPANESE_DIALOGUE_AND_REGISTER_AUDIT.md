# One Punch Man — V05 Japanese Dialogue and Register Audit
## Impossible-duty language, strategic false persona, public-image ideology, and mundane care

## 1. Audit disposition

**PASS WITH MODEL REFINEMENT.**

The Japanese checks support the V05 reading. The most important refinements are:

1. Mumen Rider's courage is explicitly self-aware rather than optimism-driven.
2. Saitama's post-victory speech is deliberately false outward self-presentation; literal dialogue cannot be equated with literal motive.
3. Genos understands the hidden intention and expresses deferential noninterference rather than blind confusion.
4. Amai Mask's V05 ideology concerns public reassurance and A-class-and-above management; V05 does **not** support calling him the gatekeeper of every B-class promotion.
5. The bonus confirms a plain, low-ceremony care register between Saitama and Genos.

No later translation, anime line, webcomic wording, or V06+ context is used as authority.

## 2. Mumen Rider — self-aware impossible-duty register

### `期待されてないのはわかってるんだ`
Locator: `OPM|V05|chapter:27|image:0125`

This explicitly removes the easiest heroic-fantasy reading. Mumen does not believe the audience rationally expects him to win.

### `俺じゃB級で通用しない`
### `自分が弱いって事はちゃんとわかってるんだ!`
Locator: `image:0126`

`俺` and the plain self-evaluation are direct rather than ceremonial. He names his own weakness and class ceiling.

### `俺が一番よくわかってるんだよォッ…!!`
### `それでもやるしかないんだ`
### `俺しかいないんだ`
Locator: `image:0127`

The repeated `俺` makes the decision personally owned rather than outsourced to Association policy. `しかない` is situational necessity language.

### `勝てる勝てないじゃなく`
### `ここで俺は お前に立ち向かわなくちゃいけないんだ!`
Locator: `images:0128-0129`

`～なくちゃいけない` expresses obligation. The contrast is explicitly **victory probability versus duty**, not strength-denial versus courage.

**Audit ruling:** model Mumen as self-aware and duty-driven under this crisis. Do not generate naive “I know I can win if I believe” rhetoric from this evidence.

## 3. Saitama — strategic false persona after Deep Sea King

The post-victory crowd sequence must be treated as a major warning against naive literalism in dialogue-based character reconstruction.

At `images:0151-0152`, Saitama loudly accepts/exaggerates the interpretation that the other heroes weakened the monster and that he arrived late to monopolize the credit. His outward persona becomes boastful, opportunistic, and deliberately repellent.

The key causal fact is contextual: this speech follows a civilian beginning to use Saitama's effortless victory as evidence that the defeated heroes were useless. The crowd then reorients toward gratitude for those heroes.

### `おいお前ら 倒れたヒーロー達をちゃんと看てやれよ`
### `死なれたら困るんだよ俺が利用できなくなるだろ`
Locator: `image:0154`

The first clause is a direct care instruction. The second masks it as self-interest. The mismatch is deliberate.

**Model consequence:** Saitama can knowingly produce an outward utterance that is false about his motive when doing so serves a social/ethical goal. Character simulation must use context and private/behavioral evidence, not surface text alone.

### Recognition ruling

- “Saitama does not want recognition” — **REJECT**.
- “Saitama always protects his own reputation” — **REJECT**.
- “Saitama may sacrifice desired recognition to protect others' deserved heroic standing” — **STRENGTHEN / canonical through V05**.

## 4. Genos — interpretive deference rather than doctrinal submission

### `サイタマ先生 それでいいのですね?`
### `それが先生の進む道だというのなら 俺は何も口を出しません`
Locator: `OPM|V05|chapter:28|image:0156`

The line contains familiar `先生` deference but the cognitive content is new. Genos has understood that Saitama is intentionally permitting a damaging public interpretation.

`何も口を出しません` is active noninterference: Genos could correct the public record but chooses not to.

**Model consequence:** Genos's respect includes allowing Saitama ownership of ethically costly self-presentation. This is not evidence that Genos is unable to perceive contradiction; it is evidence that he perceives it and defers to the choice.

## 5. Amai Mask — polished normative register

### `人々の不安を取り除くためにも`
### `ヒーローというものは 常にタフで力強く そして美しく`
### `速やかにスマートに悪を排除できる存在でなくてはなりません`
Locator: `OPM|V05|chapter:25|image:0057`

This is public, polished, normative speech. `～なくてはなりません` is prescriptive rather than merely descriptive. His hero ideal links social reassurance with toughness, strength, beauty, speed, and clean removal of evil.

**Model consequence:** Amai's aesthetics cannot be separated from a stated public-function theory at this boundary.

## 6. Amai Mask — institutional wording audit

At `image:0177` Amai says:

### `B級昇格か…そんなの僕を通さなくたっていいよ`
### `C級やB級なんてどうなったって構わない`

Association staff explain that consultation with him serves the Association's need for public support. Amai clarifies that the management he wants influence over concerns socially influential **A-class and above** heroes, because poor conduct by members of the same organization creates reputational harm.

**Correction against overreach:** V05 does **not** establish that Amai personally blocks or approves every B-class promotion. In this scene he explicitly says Saitama's B-class promotion need not go through him.

Current institutional formulation:

`public-image ideology + advisory/management influence over A-class-and-above hero composition/conduct`.

Later gatekeeping mechanics, if any, must wait for later evidence.

## 7. Saitama — child gratitude and money

### `お前にやる`
### `もっと大事に使え`
Locator: `OPM|V05|extra:買えないモノ|image:0206`

Saitama returns the child's offered allowance without a long moral speech. The register is plain, adult-to-child, and practical.

The scene is unusually diagnostic because Saitama is genuinely short of money. Refusal therefore cannot be explained by lack of need.

**Model consequence:** under low-stakes gratitude, Saitama may reject compensation he considers inappropriate even when materially useful.

## 8. Genos — mundane preference tracking

### `前から先生が気にしていた例のジュース取り寄せましたよ`
### `以前外出したときに自販機を見てるのを見てました`
Locator: `image:0207`

Genos reports that he noticed Saitama looking at the vending-machine drink on an earlier outing. Saitama had not needed to issue a request.

### `ダンボール一箱分買いました`
Locator: `image:0208`

The care mode is observational and practical, with mild overprovision. Saitama's response `金あるなお前` keeps the exchange colloquial and materially grounded.

**Model consequence:** Genos can express attachment through remembered mundane preferences rather than emotional declarations.

## 9. Sonic — non-Saitama tactical register

The early Deep Sea King sequence further supports the V04 differentiation. Sonic reads the rain/environmental change, recognizes the worsening matchup, and withdraws when his available equipment state does not support a rational continuation.

His language and behavior are markedly less obsession-loaded than when Saitama is the target.

**Audit ruling:** preserve rivalry-conditioned escalation. Do not generalize Saitama-facing humiliation speech to all threats.

## 10. Hero Association compensation terminology

The narration in `買えないモノ` states that:

- compensation is paid monthly according to ranking position;
- ranking uses activity points;
- the explicit point criteria are not publicly disclosed;
- Association assessors know the criteria;
- self-reports and civilian eyewitness testimony are considered;
- activity/achievement does not always map cleanly into rank;
- C-class can therefore be poorly paid.

This is institutional narration, not a character's possibly mistaken theory. Treat it as V05 canonical description of the compensation/evaluation system while remaining open to later qualification.

## 12. Final audit ruling

The V05 Japanese-dependent claims **pass**.

No correction is required to:

- Mumen Rider's self-aware impossible-duty model;
- Saitama's deliberate reputational sacrifice;
- Genos's recognition of that sacrifice;
- Genos's practical domestic attentiveness;
- Amai Mask's public-reassurance ideology;
- Sonic's context-sensitive retreat policy;
- the rank-linked compensation-system reading.

One important scope correction is enforced: **V05 supports Amai Mask's consultative/management concern over socially influential A-class-and-above heroes, not a blanket claim that he personally gatekeeps every lower-class promotion.**
