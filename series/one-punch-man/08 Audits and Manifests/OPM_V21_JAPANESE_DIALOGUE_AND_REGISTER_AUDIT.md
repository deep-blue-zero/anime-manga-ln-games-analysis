# One Punch Man — V21 Japanese Dialogue and Register Audit

## Audit result

**PASS.** The load-bearing Japanese-language claims in `OPM_V21_DEEP_READING.md` are supportable from the exact Japanese V21 tankobon at the frozen V20 observational boundary. The audit was completed before consulting the historical grouped `OPM_V19-V21_DEEP_READING.md`, V22+, or Checkpoint-A material for V21 interpretation.

## 1. Flashy Flash — technique, rank, and training register

### 1.1 `44期の元一位よ`

**Locator:** `OPM|V21|chapter:98|image:0096`

The ninja opponent addresses Flash as:

`44期の元一位よ`

Controlled reading:

> “former first place of the 44th class/cohort.”

Audit control:

- `44期` identifies a numbered intake/cohort/class in the shared ninja institutional context.
- `元一位` is literally former number one / former first place.
- Do **not** expand this into a complete ninja-village organizational chart from V21 alone.
- Do **not** change `44期` to another cohort number based on memory or adaptations.

### 1.2 `優秀な奴ほど基本に忠実な型を崩さない`

**Locator:** `OPM|V21|chapter:99|image:0113`

Flash says:

`優秀な奴ほど基本に忠実な型を崩さない`

Controlled reading:

> “The more excellent the practitioner, the less they break from forms faithful to the fundamentals.”

Key lexical controls:

- `優秀な奴ほど` means “the more excellent/skilled the person,” not “every strong person.”
- `基本に忠実` emphasizes fidelity to fundamentals.
- `型` is technical form/pattern; it should not be flattened to generic “move.”
- `崩さない` is “does not break/deviate from.”

This is a **Flash-authored specialist principle**, not omniscient narration.

### 1.3 `どんなに速くとも技の型を知っていれば容易に捌けるということだ`

**Locator:** same page, image `0113`.

Controlled reading:

> “No matter how fast [they are], if you know the form of the technique, it can be handled/read comparatively easily.”

`捌ける` matters. In this combat context it means being able to handle, process, deal with, or parry/read the action. It does **not** mean speed ceases to exist or that technique knowledge metaphysically negates speed.

The source supports a conditional mechanism:

`known form + trained response -> extreme speed becomes more legible/handleable`.

### 1.4 Simultaneous-kill explanation

**Locator:** `OPM|V21|chapter:99|image:0140`.

Flash states:

`二人同時に仕留めるタイミングを探していたたけだ`

and explains that allowing even one opponent to escape would cause later trouble.

Controlled reading:

> He had been looking for the timing to finish both at once.

This directly supports the claim that his apparent delay was strategic rather than inability to finish earlier.

He then says:

`俺以外のS級ではお前達のスピードに対応するのは難しいだろうからな`

Controlled reading:

> “Other S-Class heroes besides me would probably have difficulty responding to your speed.”

Audit control:

- `だろう` keeps the statement inferential/probabilistic.
- This is **Flash's comparative judgment**, not narrator-certified ranking of every S-Class hero.

### 1.5 `修行不足だな`

**Locator:** `OPM|V21|chapter:99|image:0144`.

Controlled reading:

> “Insufficient training.” / “You haven't trained enough.”

`修行` carries disciplined training/practice, often with a rigorous or ascetic shade. It is stronger than a casual “not enough practice.”

The line supports Flash's training-centered explanatory schema. It does not prove that monsterization supplied no power; V21 explicitly labels the transformed ninja pair Dragon-level.

## 3. Child Emperor — mission, competence, and emotional register

### 3.1 `勝つことは重要じゃない`

**Locator:** `OPM|V21|chapter:101|image:0190`.

Child Emperor tells Waganma:

`アレは強いよ… 相手にしてたら時間もかかるし`

`勝つことは重要じゃない`

Controlled reading:

> “That thing is strong… fighting it would take time, and winning isn't what's important.”

The second sentence is highly context-sensitive. Child Emperor is discussing whether to stop the **hostage extraction** to defeat a local opponent.

Do not translate or interpret it as a universal pacifist principle or as indifference to victory. The intended hierarchy is local:

`safe extraction > optional local win`.

### 3.2 `ワガンマ君を地上へ送り届けるのが僕の使命だから`

**Locator:** `OPM|V21|chapter:101|image:0191`.

Controlled reading:

> “Because my mission is to deliver Waganma-kun to the surface.”

`使命` is mission/duty/charge. It is stronger than merely “what I want to do.”

Child Emperor continues:

`今は戦うべきじゃない`

> “Right now, I shouldn't fight.” / “This isn't the time to fight.”

The `今は` is load-bearing. His decision is tactical and mission-relative, not a rejection of fighting in general.

### 3.3 `勝ったとしても怪我して僕が歩けなくなったら誰がキミの前を歩くの?`

**Locator:** same page, image `0191`.

Controlled reading:

> “Even if I won, if I got hurt and couldn't walk, who would walk ahead of you?”

This is unusually clean evidence that Child Emperor includes **preservation of his own protective function** inside the mission model. Avoid reducing it to cowardice or self-preservation detached from Waganma.

### 3.4 Child Emperor's first-person register

Child Emperor generally uses `僕`, preserving a youthful/polite-neutral self-reference even while functioning as S-Class commander and combat engineer. That matters for character voice: he does not routinely adopt a hyper-masculine adult-warrior `俺` register to perform authority.

His speech to Waganma remains explanatory, practical and relatively plain. The child/adult-role contradiction is therefore not only visual; the language does not erase his age-coded identity.

## 4. `ドンカンバード` and the resurrection hypothesis

### 4.1 Waganma's media knowledge

**Locators:** `OPM|V21|chapter:101|images:0193-0194`.

Waganma recognizes the costume character:

`「ドンカンバード」じゃん!!`

He explains that it is a mascot from a comedy anime and that the character frequently dies because it is foolish/oblivious, only to return in later broadcasts.

Child Emperor's immediate question:

`よく死ぬ……?`

and response:

`復活……`

show that he does not dismiss the information as irrelevant entertainment trivia.

### 4.2 `怪人の生態は予測がつかない`

**Locator:** image `0195`.

Child Emperor says/thinks:

`いや……テレビの話なんだけど……`

`怪人の生態は予測がつかない`

and continues that the roots/triggers of monster emergence are not fully predictable.

Controlled reading:

> “It's just a television story, but… monster ecology/biology is unpredictable.”

`生態` is broad: ecology, way of life, biological behavior/state. Do not narrow it automatically to genetics.

### 4.3 Costume-setting inheritance hypothesis

Child Emperor then thinks:

`着ぐるみによって怪人化した男が`

`もしそのキャラ設定を引き継いだ能力を秘めていたとしたら…`

Controlled reading:

> “If a man who became a monster through a costume were hiding an ability that inherited that character's setting/properties…”

Audit control:

- `もし` and `としたら` explicitly mark a hypothesis.
- V21 supports Child Emperor **forming** this model and then observing resurrection.
- It does not yet establish a universal law that all costume-based monsters inherit every fictional property of their costume character.

## 5. Phoenix Man / Gyoro Gyoro — near-death doctrine and transformation

### 5.1 `死に近づく地獄を乗り越えること`

**Locator:** `OPM|V21|chapter:101|image:0197`.

Phoenix Man says:

`ギョロギョロは言った`

`死に近づく地獄を乗り越えること`

`それが怪人を爆発的に成長させる秘訣だと`

Controlled reading:

> “Gyoro Gyoro said that overcoming a hell that brings one close to death is the secret to making a monster grow explosively.”

Audit control:

- The attribution `ギョロギョロは言った` is explicit and must be preserved.
- This is Phoenix Man citing **Gyoro's doctrine**.
- The ensuing transformation strongly corroborates the doctrine in Phoenix Man's case.
- It does not automatically convert the statement into narrator-certified universal biology.
- It must remain separate from Dr. Genus's V18 `リミッター` theory unless later evidence explicitly connects them.

### 5.2 `災害レベル:竜 転生フェニックス男`

**Locator:** image `0199`.

This is an explicit caption, not dialogue:

`災害レベル:竜 転生フェニックス男`

Controlled reading:

> “Disaster Level: Dragon — Reincarnated Phoenix Man.”

This licenses a hard V21 observation:

`Phoenix Man (Demon-class first state) -> apparent death -> Reincarnated Phoenix Man (Dragon-class state)`.

It does **not** by itself prove that the disaster-level increase was caused solely by generic near-death experience rather than a Phoenix-specific resurrection mechanism. The text presents both the Gyoro doctrine and the `ドンカンバード`/costume hypothesis; both must remain in the causal field.

### 5.3 Pronoun escalation and grandiosity

**Locator:** image `0213`.

Reincarnated Phoenix Man declares:

`今この瞬間 私が最強になったようだ`

then visually/rhetorically escalates through:

`私が`

`俺が`

`俺様が全生物でナンバーワン…!!!`

Controlled reading:

> “At this moment it seems I have become the strongest… I… I… I, the great me, am number one among all living things…!”

The pronoun progression is character-model evidence. `俺様` is boastful, self-exalting and domineering. The transformation is accompanied not merely by more power but by intensified grandiosity.

Do not treat `全生物でナンバーワン` as objective narrator scaling; it is Phoenix Man's euphoric self-claim.

## 6. Child Emperor — `悔し泣き`

### 6.1 `怖くて泣いてるんじゃない…`

**Locator:** `OPM|V21|chapter:101|image:0214`.

Child Emperor says:

`怖くて泣いてるんじゃない…`

`自分の弱さが悔しくてたまらないんだ`

Controlled reading:

> “I'm not crying because I'm scared… I'm unbearably frustrated at my own weakness.”

This line is the lexical center of the chapter title `悔し泣き`.

`悔しい` should not be flattened to ordinary sadness. It carries frustration, chagrin, mortification, or bitter regret arising from failure/inadequacy.

The source **explicitly rejects fear as the stated reason for the tears**. That does not prove Child Emperor experiences zero fear at any physiological level; it establishes how he identifies and frames the emotion.

### 6.2 Contingency failure

He continues:

`不測の事態も考えてたつもりだったのに`

`幹部ですらない奴が僕の想定を遥かに上回ってきた…`

Controlled reading:

> “I thought I had accounted for unforeseen situations, but someone who isn't even a cadre has exceeded my expectations by far…”

Key controls:

- `不測の事態` = unforeseen contingency/situation.
- `考えてたつもり` admits the limits of his preparation without implying he made no preparation.
- `僕の想定を遥かに上回ってきた` is explicitly model/expectation failure.
- `幹部ですらない` is Child Emperor's classification-based expectation, not narrator guarantee about Phoenix Man's ultimate capability.

### 6.3 `最終兵器`

Child Emperor says:

`もう… 最終兵器を使わなくちゃいけないなんて…`

Controlled reading:

> “Already… to think I have to use my ultimate/final weapon…”

This supports the claim that Brave Giant is a reserve/last-resort escalation **within Child Emperor's own equipment hierarchy**.

## 8. Waganma — entitlement, stress, and useful knowledge

Waganma's speech is childlike, demanding and informal. He repeatedly treats S-Class identity as assurance that Child Emperor should be able to win, and he repeats a paternal worldview that heroes are financially compensated and therefore should work harder for his rescue.

Two translation controls matter:

1. This is **Waganma reporting what his father says**, not narrator doctrine about hero motivation.
2. His entitlement coexists with fear, fatigue and genuine informational contribution. The same child whose institutional privilege distorts expectations supplies the `ドンカンバード` clue that helps Child Emperor update his model.

The character should therefore not be reduced to either “innocent victim” or “spoiled rich kid.” V21 supports both vulnerability and entitlement.

## 10. Register-level character modeling summary

### Flashy Flash

- cool, declarative, specialist-confident;
- terse dismissal (`修行不足だな`);
- high certainty when discussing his own technical domain;
- comparative claims can be grandiose and should remain speaker-qualified.

### Child Emperor

- `僕` first person;
- explanatory and mechanism-oriented;
- plain speech with Waganma rather than ceremonious commander register;
- emotion becomes explicit when preparation fails, but operational language returns immediately;
- does not conceal uncertainty behind false confidence.

### Phoenix Man

- transformed register becomes increasingly grandiose;
- `私 -> 俺 -> 俺様` escalation visually/linguistically dramatizes euphoric self-exaltation;
- confidently theorizes others' system limits after his own transformation.

### Waganma

- childlike informal demands;
- inherited wealth/status assumptions;
- ordinary media vocabulary becomes tactically relevant.

### Suiryu

- casual `俺` register retained;
- physical restlessness and confidence survive his earlier defeat;
- new goal is expressed without solemn conversion rhetoric.
