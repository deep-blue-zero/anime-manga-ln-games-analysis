- **PROMOTE — Suiryu:** `emerging -> moderate`; first defeat reveals that formal-status indifference coexists with strong sensitivity to experienced comparative superiority.
- **PROMOTE — Atomic Samurai:** `emerging -> moderate`; disciple/peer network, lethal-policy reasoning, Haragiri response, and strategic reprioritization broaden the earlier bounded combat model.
- **PROMOTE — Tanktop Master:** `emerging -> moderate`; post-defeat injured rescue and Mumen solidarity add longitudinal protective/failure-recovery breadth.
- **PROMOTE — Lightning Max:** `insufficient -> emerging`; professional-duty behavior becomes longitudinally coherent.
- **NEW — Flashy Flash / Death Gatling / Haragiri:** each enters `emerging` as a bounded but coherent model.
- **STRENGTHEN / DECOMPOSE — monsterhood:** Monster Cells canonize one repeatable human-to-monster bodily pathway; body, self-identity, organization affiliation, and conduct remain separate axes.
- **STRENGTHEN — Monster Association:** scalable monster-creation claim, human-specialist conversion, forced recruitment, symbolic/media targeting, and higher purpose beyond violent impulse.
- **STRENGTHEN — Amai Mask:** public idol register and lethal hero register coexist; staff explicitly acknowledge a role/knowledge boundary.
- **STRENGTHEN — hero institution:** coordinated A-class failure, elite S-class friction, injured hospital duty, and the formal Suiryu championship clarify that coordination, capability, role, and official outcome are distinct domains.
