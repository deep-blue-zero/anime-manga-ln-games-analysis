# One Punch Man — V27 Japanese Dialogue and Register Audit

## Audit result

**PASS.** The load-bearing Japanese-language, attribution, chronology, and register claims in the prospective portion of `OPM_V27_DEEP_READING.md` are supported by the locked V27 Japanese tankobon. No correction requires reopening the prospective thesis.

The audit does impose several precision controls that must remain in the final reading:

1. Orochi retains independent memory and appetitive agency at the opening of `侵蝕`; Psykos later **claims** control of Orochi's body. Do not turn her claim into narrator-certified permanent sovereignty over the fusion.
2. Psykos's `バリアの表裏を反転` is a mechanism/geometry intervention—reversing the barrier's inside/outside relation—not merely “using more psychic power.”
3. Darkshine's V27 diagnosis is unusually explicit: the narrator distinguishes strength from the kind of contest he actually sought, naming `気持ちの良い勝利`, avoidance of `勝敗の掴めない闘争`, and the return of `恐怖`.
4. Garou's `俺の為すべき悪` establishes a normative self-concept about the kind of “evil” he believes he ought to enact. His disgust at looking like a bully constrains that identity, but does not by itself convert him into conventional hero morality.
5. Fubuki explicitly expects Tatsumaki's power use to be shaped so that Fubuki is not harmed. This supports protective geometry/relationship-specific constraint rather than a generic claim that Tatsumaki always protects everyone equally.
6. `負けない!` is not a literal metaphysical claim that heroes cannot be defeated. Its chapter-level force comes from wounded/defeated heroes continuing to find useful roles after prior losses.
7. Suiryu's `俺 ヒーローになりたいんだ` is direct and unambiguous at this boundary.
8. Saitama's `俺はサイタマだ` / `よろしくな マナコ` makes Manako a named acquaintance. It does not establish a universal Saitama doctrine of monster rights.
9. Psykos reports a `神の如き何か`, stopped time, an inflow of mysterious power, and `天命`; the manga supplies strong visual corroboration of an extraordinary external phenomenon. Identity, motive, contract, and relation to the previously attributed `神` remain OPEN.
10. Psykos's `知性体の終着点` / `最終走者` / planetary assimilation language is her teleology, not narrator endorsement.
11. Tatsumaki explicitly frames Psykos as vulnerable to exploitation: `妙なのに口説かれて手先にされるのよ`. Her accompanying `いざという時 誰かが助けてくれると思っちゃいけないの` is a severe self-reliance doctrine and should remain speaker-position evidence, especially because her own conduct in the same volume is rescue-heavy and later accepts complementary Genos support.
12. Psykos's `お前も私を認めないのか` with the Fubuki image supports a recognition wound involving Fubuki/Tatsumaki. V27 does not itself supply the full earlier Fubuki/Psykos history.
13. The narration in `究極の叡智` explicitly supports Tatsumaki's energy-camouflage/root-infiltration tactic and her prior covert fine-wave extraction of heroes; this is tactical intelligence evidence, not merely spectacular output.
14. Genos's `先生のフォローは本来弟子の仕事だ`, `貴様`, and direct imperative to Tatsumaki are abrasive/assertive in register but occur inside a complementary division of labor. Do not mistake interpersonal hostility for refusal to coordinate.
15. Kuseno's `使えるフルパワーは10秒まで` is an exact hard-duration warning reactivated in live combat.
16. The final `究極の叡智` / dinner sequence is a deliberate register collision: cosmic wisdom language collapses into Saitama thinking about what to eat at home and Flash worrying about the embarrassment of being seen trapped.

## 1. `132撃目 侵蝕` — agency, control, and inverted geometry

### Orochi is not passive fuel at the chapter opening

Images `0015-0017` establish that the internal fusion is contested.

Psykos recognizes the threat in explicitly mental terms:

- `精神が侵蝕される…`

Orochi then addresses Psykos with continuity of memory and an independent appetitive agenda:

- `これまで私の成長を促すためにせっせと生贄を運んでくれたな`
- `感謝するぞサイコス`
- `次はお前だ`

This supports the prospective reading that the fusion has not begun as settled shared consciousness. Orochi remembers Psykos's prior role, thanks her for feeding his growth, and designates Psykos herself as the next object of consumption.

Psykos answers with her own sovereignty claim:

- `お前は私の力を増幅するための器に過ぎないんだ…!!!`
- `主人は私だ!!!`

Later, at `0019`, she states:

- `オロチの肉体は私のものだ`

That is source-secure as **Psykos's current claim about control**. Because the same chapter has just shown Orochi attempting to invade/consume her, V27 does not authorize the stronger narrator-level claim that Orochi's agency has permanently disappeared.

### Barrier inversion is mechanism match

Image `0025` gives Psykos's tactic:

- `バリアの表裏を反転させて貰った`

The important word is `反転`: she reverses the barrier relation rather than simply overpowering Tatsumaki head-on. The final reading may use this as a clean V27 example of geometry/mechanism match defeating a previously protective structure.

### Saitama's local annoyance damages a distributed system

Images `0027-0031` support the contrast between local perception and distributed consequence. When the flesh/root touches him, Saitama's register is ordinary irritation:

- `アタマに触んな!!`

The fused organism elsewhere experiences a large loss of cells and the strange `ツルッツル` sensation. The source supports a structural contrast between Saitama's tiny local problem frame and the larger system's damage; it does not require Saitama to understand the whole fused architecture.

### King report releases Tatsumaki's constraint

By the end of the chapter King reports that he protected the ordinary child and is heading upward. Tatsumaki then states that she no longer needs to worry/hold back. This is direct support for the prospective claim that earlier restraint was linked to civilian/hostage uncertainty, not merely an arbitrary “not serious yet” switch.

## 2. `133撃目 ひっくり返す!` — Darkshine's explicit fear model and Garou's bounded evil

### Darkshine's desired victory state

The narration around images `0045-0049` makes Darkshine's psychology more explicit than a generic confidence-loss reading.

The key contrast is:

- `求めていたのは戦いではなく`
- `気持ちの良い勝利`
- `勝敗の掴めない闘争に身を投じるつもりはなく`

The decisive internal shift is then framed as:

- `「この戦いは俺も無事では済まないかもしれない」`
- long-forgotten `恐怖`

The prospective formulation is therefore supported: Darkshine's training, body, and accomplishments are real, but the confidence structure depended on contests whose outcome/safety remained sufficiently legible. V27 does **not** say he never worked hard or never possessed genuine courage in any domain.

### Garou's disgust at ordinary bullying

Image `0057` gives Garou's reaction to Darkshine's frightened face:

- `チッ 情けねえツラしやがって`
- `まるで俺がいじめてるみたいじゃねーか`
- `俺の為すべき悪は…`
- `もっとこう…`

`為すべき` matters. Garou is not merely saying he wants “more evil.” He invokes an evil he believes he **ought** to enact or that properly belongs to his self-appointed role. Darkshine looking like a bullied victim makes the present relation feel wrong for that project.

This strengthens the contradiction already visible in Garou: he embraces `悪` as identity while resisting the phenomenology of one-sided bullying. The source does not yet supply the positive content of the “proper” evil he seeks.

### Fear does not erase Darkshine's concern for Garou

During Tatsumaki's restructuring, Darkshine warns Garou to escape because remaining there will get him crushed. This is important register/character evidence: the frightened Darkshine still recognizes danger to another person and issues a warning. Do not flatten his fear into total moral or social collapse.

## 3. `134撃目 タツマキ全開` — relationship-specific protection and rescue geometry

Image `0064` gives Fubuki's explicit model of Tatsumaki's power use:

- `私から離れないで!`
- `私の傍にいれば大丈夫なはずだから…`
- `お姉ちゃんが力を使う時は必ず私に危害が及ばないようになってるの`

This is strong source evidence that Fubuki expects Tatsumaki to shape her power so Fubuki is not harmed. It is simultaneously relationship evidence and power-constraint evidence.

The following rescue sequence visually and structurally supports broader evacuation accounting: multiple people are lifted/extracted in psychic spheres before Tatsumaki's much larger base/urban-scale deformation. The chapter title `タツマキ全開` therefore should not be paraphrased as “she simply decided to use full power.” The source spends substantial page space establishing what had to be removed from the danger geometry first.

Genos's later arrival and detection of an abnormal biological response near the protruding structure supports his role as mechanism-oriented battlefield observer.

## 4. `135撃目 負けない!` — defeat, continuing duty, and explicit hero aspiration

### `負けない` is perseverance language, not invulnerability doctrine

Metal Bat, still hospitalized, tells Zenko:

- `心配すんな`
- `ヒーローは負けねえよ`

He then re-enters action despite his condition. The surrounding chapter repeatedly shows injured or previously defeated heroes finding another useful task. Therefore `負けない!` is best read as a refusal to let prior defeat exhaust agency, not as a factual claim that heroes are never physically beaten.

### Mumen Rider distinguishes combat contribution from rescue contribution

Mumen Rider explicitly recognizes that he may not be useful as anti-monster combat power but reasons that civilians around City Z may still be waiting for rescue. This is an unusually clean statement of **domain awareness without self-exclusion**: acknowledging a power gap does not imply that one has no remaining heroic work.

Tanktop Master's response validates that continuing role rather than treating Mumen's limited combat output as disqualifying.

### Suiryu's aspiration is direct

Image `0111` first gives the chapter's clearest articulation of heroism's non-physical dimension:

- `ヒーローの真価は覚悟 心の強さだ—`
- `極限状態でも危機的状況でも迷いなく人を助けに飛び込む精神性`

Suiryu then concludes:

- `俺 ヒーローになりたいんだ`

The final line is not retrospective inference. It is a direct V27 statement of aspiration and therefore strongly confirms the durable movement begun after the Super Fight crisis.

### Saitama and Manako become named acquaintances

Image `0117` gives Manako's self-introduction and Saitama's response:

- `マナコっていう名前があるんです!`
- `俺はサイタマだ`
- `よろしくな マナコ`

Saitama also learns that Manako is female. The register is casual and low ceremony. The strongest safe claim is relational: the trio's earlier pragmatic cooperation now includes reciprocal naming, and Saitama treats Manako as an individual interlocutor rather than reducing the encounter to her monster category.

## 5. `136撃目 大いなる何か` — God-like encounter, mandate language, and predatory recruitment

### The external phenomenon is source-secure; its identity is not

Psykos says the anomalous presence appeared at the moment of fusion. Images `0136-0138` supply a striking visual sequence whose captions compare its form to:

- `脳細胞`
- `宇宙の構造図`
- `木星の表面にも似た`

The text then labels it:

- `神の如き何か`

Psykos reports:

- `それが止まった時の中で私を覗き込んできたのだ`
- `畏怖`
- `と同時に神秘の力が流れ込んでくるのを感じた`
- `瞬間…私は天命を悟った`

The final deep reading may therefore state at high confidence that **Psykos reports an extraordinary God-like encounter associated with stopped time and a mysterious power inflow**, and that the manga visually presents an immense nonhuman cosmic phenomenon. It may not yet identify that phenomenon conclusively with every earlier use of `神`, specify its motive, or treat Psykos's mandate interpretation as objective fact.

### Psykos converts the grant into a teleology of assimilation

Images `0139-0141` support Psykos's post-encounter ideology:

- roots will use living things as fuel;
- `これははじめから定められた大きな流れ`
- `知性体の終着点だ`
- `私はその最終走者に選ばれた`

This is her claimed theory of intelligent life's endpoint and her own elected role. It should remain clearly attributed to Psykos.

Tatsumaki's response—mocking the idea of devouring everything and ending alone—functions as an explicit counter-position inside the text.

### Recognition wound and exploitation diagnosis

Psykos attacks Tatsumaki/Fubuki's use of psychic power and asks why she, rather than the overwhelmingly stronger Tatsumaki, was selected. The exchange then becomes psychologically sharper.

Tatsumaki says:

- `バカバカしい`
- `超能力者の周りにはうまく利用しようって人間しか集まらないのはあんただって知ってるでしょう`
- `心構えがなってないから妙なのに口説かれて手先にされるのよ`

The wording `口説かれて` / `手先にされる` explicitly frames the external relation as recruitment/manipulation into another entity's service.

Image `0143` continues:

- `いざという時 誰かが助けてくれると思っちゃいけないの`
- `窮地でペットに助けを求めるような半端者だから目をつけられたんでしょうね`

This is a severe doctrine of self-reliance and should remain Tatsumaki's stated theory, not an unquestioned narrator maxim.

Psykos responds:

- `半端者だと…`
- `この力を見てまだ…`
- `お前も私を認めないのか`

The Fubuki image directly adjacent to the recognition language supports a recognition wound in which Fubuki is psychologically relevant. It does **not** itself reconstruct the full earlier Psykos/Fubuki history.

## 6. `137撃目 究極の叡智` — covert extraction, infiltration, and combative coordination

### Tatsumaki's tactic is explicitly narrated

Images `0170-0171` explain a mechanism that the spectacle alone could obscure.

The fusion had surrounded itself in a strong barrier, but there was one place that could not be shut off: the route through which energy was being absorbed. The narration then states:

- `タツマキは吸引されるエネルギーにカムフラージュして肉の根の中を遡上し サイコスの体内に侵入した`

This is explicit source support for energy camouflage, upstream movement through the flesh roots, and bodily infiltration.

The same sequence explains her earlier rescue method. Until then, because she feared heroes would be seized together with their barriers by the flesh roots, she had been using fine psychic waves too subtle for Psykos to recognize to lift/extract them. Once everyone was out, she could twist off the entire hideout.

This materially strengthens Tatsumaki's model as a tactical/information-processing combatant. V27's enormous environmental manipulation is enabled by prior covert precision work.

### Genos and Tatsumaki cooperate through abrasive register

Image `0187` preserves the exact interpersonal texture.

Tatsumaki:

- `ちょっと鬼サイボーグ キングが私に任せた相手よ 余計な手出しすんじゃないわよ`
- `またカベにめり込まされたいの?`

Genos answers:

- `キングはサイタマ先生が来るまでのつなぎを貴様に任せただけだ`
- `先生のフォローは本来弟子の仕事だ`
- `光線は俺がさばいてやるから さっさとこのバケモノを片付けろ タツマキ!!`

`貴様`, the direct imperative, and the use of `タツマキ` rather than deferential address make the exchange abrasive. But the propositional content is cooperative: Genos assigns himself the beam-management role so Tatsumaki can finish the primary target. His disciple identity (`先生のフォローは本来弟子の仕事`) is converted into operational responsibility rather than passive worship.

The following panel makes the constraint explicit: he can only deflect/redirect the enormous beam and his current equipment is being pushed to its limit.

### The ten-second limit is exact

Image `0188` reactivates Kuseno's warning:

- `使えるフルパワーは10秒まで`

Genos immediately asks:

- `間に合うか!?`

The hard duration therefore becomes live battle-state evidence in V27.

### `究極の叡智` receives an immediate register deflation

Psykos's claim to the culmination of human evolution / `究極の叡智` is followed almost immediately by the underground Saitama/Flash/Manako cut.

The cave scene replaces cosmic teleology with ordinary concerns. Saitama thinks about getting home and what to eat, including curry; Flash is relieved that nobody saw his humiliating trapped state. The language and setting create a deliberate anti-grandiose collision rather than a separate, analytically empty gag.
