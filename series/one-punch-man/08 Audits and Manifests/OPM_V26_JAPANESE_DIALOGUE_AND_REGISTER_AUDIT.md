# One Punch Man — V26 Japanese Dialogue and Register Audit

## Audit result

**PASS.** The load-bearing Japanese-language, attribution, chronology, and register claims in the prospective portion of `OPM_V26_DEEP_READING.md` are supported by the locked V26 Japanese tankobon. No correction requires reopening the prospective thesis.

The audit does require several precision controls that are carried into the final reading:

1. Evil Natural Water's reactivation should be described mechanistically without upgrading it into human-like tactical cognition.
2. Tatsumaki's reputation language is institution/narration-level praise, not a literal metaphysical guarantee that no being can defeat her.
3. Darkshine is technically perceptive enough to recognize Bang's superior skill and Garou's improving whole-body coordination; the V26 failure is not “he cannot understand technique.”
4. Garou's `ヒーローごっこ` argument explicitly targets publicly supported justice/hero legitimacy; it is speaker ideology, not narrator endorsement.
5. The King/Tareo/invisible-monster sequence is safest as reputation-driven deterrence under ambiguity. Do not manufacture certainty about exactly what King consciously perceived.
6. Psykos explicitly proposes combining with Orochi and using Orochi's bodily energy to amplify her psychic power; “fusion” is text-supported, not inferred only from art.
7. `大抜擢` contains genuine personnel-evaluation logic before the publicity-film punchline; “the Association is simply incompetent” would flatten the source.

## 1. `未知` controls

### Evil Natural Water / Child Emperor

Image `0014` gives Child Emperor's mechanism correction:

- `狙いは水分じゃなく油分…!!!`
- `油は水よりも凝固点が低く凍りにくい!`

The operative distinction is water versus oil and the lower freezing point of oil. This supports the claim that the earlier freeze solution does not fully contain the returning system.

Image `0015` is even more important for epistemic framing:

- `こんな液体にそんな知識や判断ができるとは思えないけど`
- `脳や神経がなくても最適解を出せるものなの!?`
- `僕の方が勉強してるのに…!!`

Child Emperor himself marks the uncertainty. He cannot square the apparently optimal response with a liquid body lacking ordinary brain/nervous-system structure. Therefore V26 supports **mechanism surprise** and an OPEN cognition question, not a settled claim that ENW “plans like a human strategist.”

### S-Class reputation and hidden Saitama history

Image `0031` frames S-Class standing as earned awe/respect rather than a meaningless label:

- `これは一度は最強を目指した男達からの畏敬の表れであり`
- `その存在はいかなる干渉を受けようとブレることはないと認められている`

The deep reading correctly preserves this as accumulated reputation evidence.

Image `0040` establishes the hidden-record contrast. After noting that humanity had encountered a comparable-scale threat before, the narration says of Saitama's history:

- `彼が自分の戦歴を語ることに無頓着な限り 誰も知る由のない話である`

The causal link is explicit: Saitama's indifference to narrating his battle record contributes to social ignorance of it.

Image `0041` describes Tatsumaki through maximal reputation language:

- `正攻法で彼女に勝てる生命体はこの世にいないとまで謳われる存在である`
- `S級1位ブラストよりもしばしば協会の最終兵器として厚い待遇を受けている`

`謳われる` matters. The text says she is **celebrated/said** to be unbeatable by orthodox means, not that the narrator has issued a physics-law proof of universal invincibility.

### Psykos hidden-ruler register

Image `0048`:

- `真の支配者とは表に出てこないものなのだ`

Psykos's diction is declarative and ideological: a “true ruler” does not appear on the surface. This supports treating concealment as part of her theory of rule, not merely tactical hiding.

## 2. `新フブキ組` controls

Image `0069` confirms Fubuki's protective contribution and hard limit:

- `今の私の防御がなかったら あなた達 跡形もなくなってたわよ`
- `でももう無理 次は防げない`

This is not a vague boast. She explicitly says the defense saved Bang/Bomb and cannot simply be repeated.

Image `0071` preserves the mixed register of genuine guilt and leadership self-conception:

- `みんな…ごめん`
- `リーダーがいなくなるなんて不甲斐ないわよね`
- `リーダーとして私が考えられる最善の策は—`
- `新フブキ組の二人とも!`

The tonal hinge is source-supported: sincere apology/responsibility immediately coexists with Fubuki's comic annexation of Bang/Bomb into her group grammar.

## 3. `おすわり` / route controls

Bang's `おすわり` command is literal dog-command language. Rover's sitting response is therefore safe to describe as learned/conditioned behavior. The source does not require a high-order account of Rover's reflective cognition.

The Saitama/Flash/Manako material supports practical cooperation. It does not establish a formal alliance, friendship, or generalized Saitama doctrine about monster rights.

## 4. `サイコス` controls

Image `0099` gives Saitama's casual evaluation of Flash:

- `結構速いじゃん`

Flash reacts to `結構だと!?`, while Manako's response is `強すぎ……`. The register difference matters: Saitama treats extraordinary speed as casually noteworthy; Flash hears status/pride friction; Manako recognizes overwhelming capability from below.

Psykos later senses the trio but asks:

- `このハゲもヒーローか…?`

This supports detection-without-understanding, not “Psykos knows Saitama is the greatest threat.”

## 5. `怪人ごっこ` controls

### Darkshine technical perception

Darkshine explicitly notices Garou's increasing whole-body integration:

- `全身の筋肉の連係度が格段に向上した`

His Bang recollection confirms that he can distinguish technical mastery from raw durability. Therefore any model of Darkshine as incapable of understanding technique is rejected.

### Darkshine's redemption frame

Darkshine tells Garou:

- `お前なら考えを改めて贖罪し 精神的筋肉を鍛え直せば一人前のヒーローになれるかもしれないな`

The phrasing genuinely attempts to assimilate Garou into a hero/redemption framework. Garou's answer that both associations will disappear preserves his rejection of both institutional poles.

### Garou's legitimacy critique

Image `0137`:

- `てめぇらは「ヒーローごっこ」じゃねぇつもりか?`
- `お前らヒーローは糞だ`
- `糞ほど民衆に支持されてるのが手に負えねぇ`
- `だからこそ俺は戦う`
- `この命をかけて正義を蹴散らす`

This is unusually explicit. Public support is part of what makes hero-side “justice” intolerable/dangerous in Garou's model. Preserve speaker attribution.

### Garou's embodied synthesis

Images `0144-0146` distinguish the two hands/schools and then narrate the synthesis:

- left: `流水岩砕拳`
- right: `旋風鉄斬拳`
- `稀代の天才は通常単独では為しえない連係奥義・交牙竜殺拳を無意識に会得していた`
- `格上との闘い 追い詰められた局面になればなるほど 真価を発揮し 成長する`

The deep-reading formulation “Garou converts pressure/contact into growth” is therefore directly source-grounded.

## 6. `融合体` controls

### Tatsumaki hostage accounting

Images `0164-0165`:

- `それより 子供はどこ?`
- `人質にされてる子の他に もう一人いるのは確認してるのよ`

This directly supports the claim that civilian/child location remains part of Tatsumaki's operational model during the esper fight.

Image `0175` preserves her demand for information even while dominating Psykos:

- `質問に答えなさい`

Image `0178` gives Psykos's bounded accounting of outsiders: one hostage has been carried out, while other outsiders remain, including Narinki's hired squad and Garou. Do not expand beyond the named/source-visible inventory.

### King / Tareo

Tareo addresses King as `キング` and immediately treats his presence as rescue/safety. The nearby invisible monster says it should have been `完全に透明化` and reacts as though King has exposed it. King later suggests hand-holding because monsters are frightening and visibly sweats during the route.

This supports a causal reputation reading. It does **not** force a claim about supernatural perception or conscious tactical omniscience by King.

### Orochi heart and regeneration

Image `0170` explicitly labels `オロチ心臓部`.

Image `0171` gives the heart/system's recovery logic:

- `血を取り込んで…`
- `身体を再生せねば…`

Thus V24's destroyed presented body can be revised to “not the whole surviving organism/system” without retracting the earlier destruction event.

### Hero communication / Tatsumaki attention split

Image `0179` has Tatsumaki polling communication and asking about heroes who have gone silent. The responding communication includes Amai Mask identification. Image `0180` shows multiple hero-side actors occupied by cadres and unable to provide easy support.

Psykos's internal reaction in `0181` is especially useful:

- she notes Tatsumaki is worrying about heroes during battle;
- concludes that Tatsumaki is already acting as though she intends to win.

This supports the reading that Tatsumaki's attention is distributed rather than merely distracted.

### Psykos/Orochi fusion

Image `0184` is decisive:

- `さあ来い…結合するんだ`
- `お前の肉体の莫大なエネルギーで私の超能力を増幅すれば必ず`

The final clause is interrupted by the action, but the intended system is explicit: combine, use Orochi's enormous bodily energy, amplify Psykos's psychic power. The deep reading therefore may call this an initiated fusion without waiting for V27 terminology.

## 7. `大抜擢` controls

The extra's opening institutional exposition is literal: the Hero Association is a young private organization launched after Agoni's initiative, rapidly expanded, supported by large funding/sponsor networks, and characterized by internal competition/promotion incentives.

The personnel-review pages give department-specific testimony and criteria. Sitch's crisis/prophecy work is treated as serious organizational value.

The final page redirects him toward responsibility for a new film centered on a six-member idol-hero group. The extra then explicitly returns to the Association's foolishness. Therefore the correct synthesis is not “evaluation is fake”; it is **real evaluation embedded in an organization capable of redirecting competence toward publicity/commercial spectacle**.
