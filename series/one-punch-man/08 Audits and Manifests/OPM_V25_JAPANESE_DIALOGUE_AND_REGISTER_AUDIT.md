# One Punch Man - V25 Japanese Dialogue and Register Audit

## Audit result

**PASS.** The load-bearing Japanese-language claims in `OPM_V25_DEEP_READING.md` are supported by the locked Japanese tankobon. No correction requires reopening the prospective interpretation. The audit imposes several attribution and scope controls, especially around Drive Knight's Metal Knight accusation, Nyan's autonomy language, Darkshine chronology, and Child Emperor's Evil Natural Water mechanism inference.

## 1. Audit method

The audit re-opened the high-load pages only after the full 200-image prospective sweep was complete. It checked:

- exact speaker attribution;
- whether a proposition is dialogue, internal thought, narration, or visual inference;
- lexical force where translation could alter characterization;
- whether strategic hypotheses were accidentally promoted into narrator fact;
- whether technical explanations were target-local or universal;
- whether later Garou/Darkshine or Evil Natural Water outcomes had leaked backward;
- chapter/extra boundaries against the tankobon contents page.

No V26+ source, no V1 grouped reading, and no Checkpoint-A registry was consulted.

## 3. Chapter-title controls

### 3.1 `遭遇`

Straightforwardly denotes encounter/contact. It supports the chapter's repeated first-contact classification problem but does not itself assert an epistemological thesis.

### 3.2 `遊び`

Means play. In Nyan's dialogue the chapter literalizes the word through `オモチャ` and his desire to play with victims until they break. The word should not be softened into harmless recreation.

### 3.3 `窮鼠`

Literally evokes a cornered/desperate rat and strongly recalls `窮鼠猫を噛む` - a cornered rat bites a cat. The Nyan matchup makes the allusion especially apt. The title supports desperate resistance against the “cat,” but does not certify that desperation permanently raises Gearsper's baseline.

### 3.4 `裏の気配`

Carries the sense of a presence/sign behind the visible surface. It is compatible with insider/hidden-actor suspicion. It does **not** linguistically certify Drive Knight's chosen culprit.

### 3.5 `必要なのは強さだけ`

Literally, roughly, “what is necessary is strength alone / all that is needed is strength.” The chapter can productively complicate that formulation through logistics and information, but the title itself should not be mistranslated into “strength isn't enough.”

### 3.6 `壊れている`

Means “is broken / is damaged,” with the subject left implicit by the title. Do not resolve the ambiguity retrospectively into “Darkshine is psychologically broken” at V25. PPP is visibly broken down physically, and several operational models are under stress, while Darkshine remains confident through the chapter endpoint.

## 4. Load-bearing lexical and attribution controls

### 4.1 Flash's first classification is inference, not arbitrary stupidity

At image 0021 Flash reasons from footsteps/body mass, location, and expected personnel before concluding that the approaching human-shaped figure is probably a monster. The Japanese presents a chain of inference. Preserve that competence even though the conclusion is wrong.

### 4.2 Saitama's response to S-Class status remains behavioral

Flash identifies himself as `S級ヒーロー 閃光のフラッシュ`. Saitama's answer returns to the practical fact that Flash attacked him. The exchange supports status-insensitivity and behavior-first evaluation. It does not mean Saitama literally cannot understand hero ranks.

### 4.3 Free rent is explicit ordinary-life motivation

At image 0031 Saitama explains his residence in the abandoned district and closes with `何より家賃タダだからな` - above all, the rent is free. This is direct low-stakes/domestic-economic evidence, not interpretive embellishment.

### 4.4 Saitama accompanies Flash for strong-enemy access

At image 0034 Saitama's logic is explicit: following Flash should lead toward strong enemies, so he will go with him. Preserve the distinction between **recognized route to possible strong opponents** and generalized enthusiasm about Hero Association operations.

### 4.5 Nyan's organizational exit and desire language

Nyan says he is a Monster Association cadre, then adds that he has already quit. He says that from here he will act as an individual monster `欲求のままに行動する` - according to/as his desires dictate.

This strongly supports self-direction. It does not support benevolence, political liberation, or respect for others.

### 4.6 `オモチャ` is explicitly dehumanizing in context

Nyan says he likes playing with toys until they break and later chooses a “play theme” involving tearing Waganma apart in front of the heroes. The toy language is not a neutral metaphor for sparring. It marks predatory objectification.

### 4.7 Gearsper's `ビジョン` is aspirational image, not stable new tier

Gearsper says that seeing Tatsumaki's power gave him a `ビジョン` beyond his usual level and that he will manifest it even at the cost of his life. His own dialogue also acknowledges poor control. Do not translate the scene into a claim that he has permanently acquired Tatsumaki-equivalent psychic capacity.

### 4.8 Drive Knight abandons live capture explicitly

During Nyan combat Drive Knight says `生け捕りは諦める`. The shift is from live capture to lethal elimination because the attempted approach is not working. This supports objective revision under constraint.

### 4.9 `戦術変形「金」` true function

Drive Knight says the temporary increase in base performance is merely a bonus and that the true value of `金` is its heat. The statement is target-local evidence about that tactical form. Do not generalize it into a claim that every Drive Knight form has a hidden property or that shogi labels map one-to-one onto ordinary shogi movement rules.

### 4.10 Drive Knight on Nyan strength

After victory Drive Knight corrects Sekingar's outcome-based inference. He says Nyan was strong and that in a straightforward fight even landing a decisive hit would have been extremely difficult. This is direct anti-outcome-scaling evidence.

### 4.11 `利用できるものは全て利用する`

Drive Knight says he uses everything available to increase his chance of victory. He adds that the fallen heroes should have that degree of resolve. The second clause is **Drive Knight's assumption about their professional acceptance**, not direct consent from the injured heroes.

Do not rewrite the exchange as “the heroes volunteered to be bait.”

### 4.12 Torture/intelligence gathering is explicit

Drive Knight states that he repeatedly tortured several monsters and lost time doing so. The verb/context supports deliberate coercive interrogation. Do not euphemize it into generic questioning.

### 4.13 Every Bofoi/Metal Knight culpability claim is speaker-attributed

Drive Knight explicitly says `“メタルナイト” ボフォイ博士だ` when Sekingar asks whom he suspects.

He then advances hypotheses involving:

- the captured/dismantled Metal Knight machine;
- possible deliberate presentation of weapon data;
- possible system hacking;
- possible compromise of headquarters defenses;
- private military buildup;
- eventual world domination.

Sekingar responds that the evidence remains circumstantial. Therefore:

- TF: Drive Knight suspects/accuses Bofoi.
- TF: he gives these reasons.
- OPEN: whether any of the alleged intent is true.

The audit prohibits wording such as “V25 reveals Metal Knight as the traitor.”

### 4.14 Blast availability is Sekingar's view

When Drive Knight asks whether Blast would move if headquarters were attacked, Sekingar says it would be difficult and that **in his view** Blast is effectively retired. This is a current institutional assessment, not omniscient proof of Blast's status or motives.

### 4.15 Drive Knight's energy depletion is explicit and local

After Nyan, Drive Knight says that after many hours of continuous operation and hard fighting he has used up combat energy and can barely move. Later he says he can return under his own power. Those statements are compatible: “barely combat-capable” is not “physically incapable of walking.” Do not manufacture a contradiction.

### 4.16 Genos recognizes the incoming energy reaction

Genos's arrival is preceded by the statement that he recognizes the energy reaction. This supports mechanism/signature recognition based on prior machine encounters. It does not by itself prove the exact organizational identity of every machine with a similar reaction.

### 4.17 Genos's growth-speed line is comparative, not universal

After destroying the damaged G5, Genos says `俺の方が成長スピードが速いようだな` - his growth speed seems faster. The line is a comparative judgment against this encountered machine trajectory, not a universal theorem that Genos will always out-upgrade the Organization.

### 4.18 Drive Knight's monster-body research is explicit instrumentalism

Drive Knight says he will dissect/research Nyan and use the result as reference for biological model weaponry. He invokes human technologies inspired by animal/insect structures and argues that if the target is a monster it should be made useful as material for justice.

Treat this as Drive Knight's normative/technical position. The manga does not narratively certify the morality of the practice merely because he calls the purpose justice.

### 4.19 `サイタマ先生は既に参戦している`

The large text on image 0161 explicitly states that Saitama-sensei is already participating in the battle. Genos then says he intended from the beginning to return to the scene regardless of Association instructions. This supports Saitama as a directional anchor plus Genos's independent operational agency. Do not reduce it to blind obedience: the following pages also show him accepting enemy intelligence and the evacuation-force logic.

### 4.20 Sekingar's operation-priority language

Sekingar says the operation's highest-priority item is Waganma's safe rescue. He later reasons that connecting S-Class combat force and finishing cadre combat may better protect the evacuation from pursuit. The sequence supports **priority revision by causal reasoning**, not abandonment of the hostage objective.

### 4.21 Food Battler Futoshi's physiology is character-stated and visually supported

He says he can convert ingested nutrition quickly into fat and burn it immediately, raising metabolism and accelerating wound recovery. Treat the exact physiology as the series' character-level power/body explanation; do not convert it into real-world medical science.

### 4.22 PPP calls himself the weakest S-Class

In his warning to Darkshine, PPP refers to himself as the weakest S-Class. This is speaker self-assessment, not a narrator-certified total ranking of every S-Class hero.

### 4.23 Darkshine remains confident in V25

Darkshine says there is no need to worry, notes that he is uninjured, promises to smash Garou in PPP's place, and wonders whether Garou is worth his full power. Nothing in V25 Japanese dialogue establishes the later confidence collapse.

Do not back-project future psychological state into this boundary.

### 4.24 Child Emperor separates fish from Evil Natural Water

Child Emperor initially asks whether the entity is controlling fish, then corrects himself: the fish are coexisting with the strange water. Preserve this as a live model revision.

### 4.25 Water-cutter inference is mechanism-sensitive

Child Emperor reasons from cleanly cut metal and the attack form toward a high-pressure water-cutter model. He says contact can be fatal and that ordinary combat would be extremely dangerous. This is an in-scene technical inference supported by visible attack effects.

### 4.26 Temperature-regulation conclusion remains a tested hypothesis

After deploying rapid freezing, Child Emperor says it appears the enemy cannot regulate its own body temperature. The freezing result supports the inference locally. Do not generalize to every water-like monster or claim permanent destruction.

### 4.27 `後頭部` preserves ordinary bodily concern

Training-era Saitama explicitly notices increasing hair loss, worries that his crown/back of head may be getting larger/thinner, and wants to inspect it. The closing narration humorously says that true or false, at that instant Saitama certainly saw the back of his head.

This supports body-image/self-consciousness and speed-comedy. It does not establish a serious supernatural visual ability separate from his speed gag.

## 5. Visual-language controls

- Garou's darkened, torn body is visibly transformed, but visual change alone does not settle completed biological monsterization.
- Gearsper's spectacle visually echoes high-end esper grammar; the exhaustion aftermath blocks tier-equivalence overreading.
- Drive Knight's `金` trap relies on visible form concealing decisive function.
- Nyan's body becomes increasingly object-like/captured after he has spent the prior chapter calling others toys; this supports reversal/irony, not a narrator moral theorem.
- Darkshine remains glossy, symmetrical, and visually triumphant through his V25 entrance into the Garou confrontation.
- Evil Natural Water's anatomical instability is load-bearing. Do not assign human-like organs or cognition that the page does not show.
- The enormous ice formation at the end is evidence of successful local freezing, not a corpse/death confirmation.
- `後頭部` deliberately uses speed/action grammar for bathroom vanity.
