## Targeted findings

- **0010–0044 — safety, signal and coercive protection:** the premium enclave is explicitly selective, while its block-four basement contains a camera-dead experimental monster floor. The Tsukuyomi operative says ordinary beauty supplements are easy to adulterate; his psychic signal is meant first to release a sleeping drug and then poison. Tatsumaki copies that wavelength and uses a scarred responder's location of the reacting inorganic object to neutralize the capsule. Her `オトモダチ` order concerns Fubuki's surrounding group, and her threat is to make them unable to continue hero work while stopping short of killing them. Saitama intervenes because the “little-sister lecture” has crossed into heroes fighting one another.
- **0046–0072 — no silent friendship upgrade:** Forte's hospital inference recognizes terrifying capacity but not Saitama's identity or scale. At 0059 Saitama rejects three increasingly broad labels—`友達`, `仲間`, and even `友達の友達って訳でもない`—before choosing `ただの知り合いだ`. Tatsumaki immediately challenges whether a mere acquaintance would be so desperate, but Saitama does not retract the wording. His conduct may exceed the label; the text does not authorize replacing it with friendship. `外でやれ` is a practical anti-collateral command, not a declaration that fighting itself is harmless.
- **0074–0108 — measurement and witness limits:** residents describe a thirty-five-year loan as the price of safety. Headquarters can detect abnormal vibration and demand S-Class response, while outsiders correctly recognize Tatsumaki's S-Class scale and repeatedly misread Saitama. Tatsumaki's increased output is her chosen escalation; the pages do not convert alarms or witnesses into a numerical equivalence between the two.
- **0110–0172 — dissolution, recommitment and collateral response:** Fubuki formally dissolves the Blizzard Group because she calls its members too weak. They later return by choice, acknowledge fear and accept the possibility of becoming targets in an S-Class conflict. That voluntary recommitment is meaningful without proving combat readiness. The monster and Metal Bat interludes preserve the volume's collateral/domestic register, while Sonic's intervention is a hostile misreading rather than an informed assessment. Ultra-high-energy detection again records an event without explaining it.
- **0174–0210 — output, narrator authority and the unknown limit:** Tatsumaki says she became absorbed and misjudged her `出力限界`; her thought that the output could send Saitama into space is internal self-assessment, not an independently measured feat. The narrator separately certifies Saitama as `当然のように 無傷`. Blast's memory shows the rescue, instruction and hoped-for recognition that shaped Tatsumaki's protective obligation; it explains but does not excuse coercion or destruction. Saitama joins recognition to an ethical limit—protect the place and do not destroy other people's things. At 0206 he says that before becoming a hero he could not defeat even a Wolf-level threat and that the limit of someone weak now is unknown. `未知数` therefore marks possibility, not guaranteed growth or victory.
- **0212–0236 — transaction and manufactured legibility:** Saitama's `仲間`/get-along language at 0213 applies to his same-A-Class neighbors and does not retroactively relabel Fubuki. Child Emperor identifies hidden wrongdoing rather than accepting the executive account. Fubuki's `取引` combines exposure of unlisted monster storage and outside dealings, evidence leverage, Psykos's extraction and restoration of institutional reputation. Tatsumaki uses the resulting public credit and recovered trust as leverage while acknowledging that Psykos escaped. The arrangement is protective and institutionally useful, but also a concealment; the public story is not the underlying event.
- **0237–0241 — `最初の勇気`:** the bonus does not promise that the bullied youth will win. The intervening hero recognizes daily fear, offers contact and says that communicating it to someone is the first courage. The youth's later reception of an A-Class arrival and his changed stance show transmitted support, not a completed cure or guaranteed triumph.
