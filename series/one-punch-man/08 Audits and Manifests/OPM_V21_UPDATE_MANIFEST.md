## Principal V20 -> V21 transitions

### Evaluation — STRENGTHEN scope discipline
Rhino Wrestler, threat labels, S-Class rank and ninja cohort rank all provide real but differently scoped information. V21 repeatedly punishes attempts to treat one scale as a complete ontology.

### Flashy Flash — STRENGTHEN / `emerging -> moderate`
V21 adds a second major context class to the V13 model: shared-origin adversaries, terrain choice, technique-form reading, genuine stress, delayed simultaneous-kill planning, and operational-device use. The promotion is driven by conditional breadth, not spectacle.

### Child Emperor — STRENGTHEN mission model / preserve moderate
`勝つことは重要じゃない` is explicitly mission-relative: delivering Waganma to the surface matters more than stopping for a winnable local fight. Phoenix Man's resurrection then exceeds Child Emperor's contingency model; `悔し泣き` is frustration at his own weakness/model failure, not stated fear. Brave Giant arrives as a last-resort tool with a hard visible activity timer. No strong-tier promotion is forced from one dense battle.

### Phoenix Man — NEW emerging / STRENGTHEN Gyoro doctrine
Initial Demon-class Phoenix Man apparently dies, cites Gyoro's near-death growth doctrine, and returns as Dragon-class `転生フェニックス男`. Child Emperor's `ドンカンバード` hypothesis keeps costume-character inheritance in the causal field. Universal biology and identity with the Genus limiter theory remain OPEN.

### Hellfire Flame / Gale Wind — NEW emerging
Monsterization produces explicit Dragon-class transformed states while retaining ninja technique. Flash's victory demonstrates that amplification does not erase training/tactical gaps; it does not show the transformation supplied no power.

### Suiryu — STRENGTHEN durable goal
The hospital extra confirms that V14's rescue-driven reorientation persists: he cannot stay still while heroes fight and says `俺にも目標ができたんでね`.
