# One Punch Man — V22 Japanese Dialogue and Register Audit

## Audit result

**PASS.** The load-bearing Japanese-language claims in `OPM_V22_DEEP_READING.md` are supportable from the exact Japanese V22 tankobon at the frozen V21 observational boundary. No V23+ source, grouped V1 hindsight, or Checkpoint-A prediction was used to rescue or reinterpret these claims before this audit was stabilized.

The audit specifically guards against five likely overreads:

1. Child Emperor's hesitation is **not** equivalent to intent to defect.
2. Phoenix Man's institutional allegations and costume-monster ontology remain **speaker claims**.
3. `中身なんだ` is **not** an anti-technology thesis.
4. Pureblood's `本物の怪人` is an essentialist self-claim, not narrator taxonomy.
5. Amai Mask's visible body anomaly is strong evidence of a hidden nonhuman-looking state, but V22 alone does **not** settle its final ontology.

## 1. Child Emperor / Phoenix Man recruitment audit

### 1.1 Recruitment is explicit

`お前をスカウトしたいのだ`

Locator: `102撃目 少年ヒーロー`, around image `0017`.

Controlled reading: Phoenix Man is directly recruiting Child Emperor. This is not merely an inference from combat dialogue.

### 1.2 Phoenix's diagnosis remains attributed

`童帝 お前には俺のようなナイスな着ぐるみ怪人になれる素質がある!`

`自覚はあるんじゃないか? ヒーローの中でお前が一番闇を抱えてるものな`

Locator: `0017`.

Controlled reading: Phoenix asserts that Child Emperor carries unusual darkness/stress. The grammatical source is Phoenix. Do not upgrade this to omniscient psychological diagnosis.

### 1.3 Organizational desire is explicitly assigned by Phoenix

`お前は心から信頼できる仲間と 自分を正しく導かせる組織を欲している`

Locator: `0019`.

Controlled reading: Phoenix says Child Emperor wants trusted comrades and an organization that can guide him correctly. Child Emperor does not independently formulate the desire in those words.

### 1.4 Ideal-organization pitch

`だから我々で手を組み 理想の組織を創ろうじゃないか! 怪人協会など比較にならんスケールの組織をな`

Locator: `0020`.

Controlled reading: Phoenix's proposal is organizational as well as biological/ideological.

### 1.5 Monsterization invitation

`着ぐるみから怪人化し 強化していくプロセスは俺が手ほどきしてやる`

`さあ 怪人化を受け入れて転生するんだ`

Locator: `0022`.

Controlled reading: Phoenix claims he can teach a costume-to-monsterization/growth process. This is source-valid as **his claim**; universality is not established.

## 2. Child Emperor hesitation audit

The decisive passage is around images `0044-0045`.

`僕は一瞬 戸惑ったのか……？ 敵に寝返るつもりなんて さらさらない だけど`

followed by remembered Phoenix Man:

`童帝 お前の力が必要なんだ`

and Child Emperor:

`ヒーローになってから どんなに我慢して頑張っても あんな風に言って貰えた事 あったかな…`

Controlled reading:

- `寝返るつもりなんて さらさらない` explicitly denies intention to change sides.
- `一瞬 戸惑った` explicitly admits a moment of hesitation.
- the textual object of the following reflection is being told that his power is needed.
- the safest formulation is therefore **recognition vulnerability without ideological defection**.

Reject: “Child Emperor decided Phoenix Man was right,” “Child Emperor wanted to become a monster,” or “the recruitment was emotionally irrelevant.”

## 3. Phoenix Man's institutional and salvation rhetoric

### 3.1 Organizational fragmentation

`ヒーロー協会も怪人協会も いつでもああなる危険性を孕んでいる 利害や哲学がバラバラの集団だからな`

Locator: around `0062`.

Controlled reading: Phoenix identifies heterogeneity of interests/philosophies as a source of organizational fracture. This is his political/organizational diagnosis.

### 3.2 Alleged Hero Association wrongdoing

`お前は何も知らんまま 悪事の片棒をかつがされてたんだ`

Locator: around `0063`.

Controlled reading: Phoenix says Child Emperor was unknowingly made complicit in wrongdoing. V22 provides no independent verification of the projected evidence. Keep the allegation speaker-qualified.

### 3.3 Child Emperor self-corrects against manipulation

`しっかりしろ 童帝! こんなのは動揺を誘う精神攻撃だ`

Locator: around `0064`.

Controlled reading: Child Emperor actively identifies the experience as a destabilizing psychological attack. His subsequent emotional vulnerability must not erase this resistance.

### 3.4 Phoenix's origin and salvation register

`怪人化は救いだよ`

and later:

`怪人化は救済 ヒトという病からの回復なんだ`

Locators: around `0065` and `0079`.

Controlled reading: `救い` / `救済` and `ヒトという病` make monsterization explicitly redemptive/medicalized in Phoenix Man's ideology. This remains character doctrine, not narrator ontology.

### 3.5 Recognition pitch

`俺は認めるぞ`

`童帝 俺と一緒に来い 共に真の栄光を掴もうじゃないか!`

Locator: around `0067`.

Controlled reading: recognition and belonging are explicit components of the offer.

## 4. Saitama audit

### 4.1 Glory and wrongdoing

`怪人化して悪さすんのが栄光な訳ないだろ!`

Locator: `0068`.

Controlled reading: Saitama rejects “monsterization + wrongdoing” as glory. Do not generalize to a metaphysical statement that every transformed being is evil solely because of body category.

### 4.2 Autonomy / anti-coercion

`やりたい事があるんだったら 趣味として自分で勝手にやってろよ 悪事だったらぶっとばすけど 子供巻き込んで困らすな!`

Locator: `0069`.

Controlled reading: this supports a three-part rule: self-directed eccentric activity is allowed; wrongdoing triggers intervention; dragging a child into the project is condemned. “Anti-coercion” is a defensible abstraction, but the quoted scope should remain visible.

### 4.3 Recognition of Child Emperor

`すげーなお前 子供かと思ったらヒーローなんだな`

Locator: `0094`.

Controlled reading: Saitama directly recognizes Child Emperor as a hero while retaining the `子供` frame. The line supports “child and hero” rather than “child replaced by adult-like hero.”

### 4.4 Name/register

After Child Emperor recognizes `ハゲマント`, Saitama answers:

`サイタマでいいよ`

Locator: around `0095`.

Controlled reading: low-ceremony self-presentation; no prestige demand.

## 5. Zombieman ↔ Child Emperor recognition audit

Around images `0071-0074`, Zombieman reports that Child Emperor's predicted weak points were useful. The decisive lines are:

`大の大人が揃いも揃って お前に頼りっぱなしですまん`

`皆に代わって礼を言うぜ ありがとう お前がいてくれてよかったよ`

Controlled reading:

- `頼りっぱなしですまん` is an apology for continued adult reliance on the child.
- `ありがとう` is direct gratitude.
- `お前がいてくれてよかったよ` recognizes Child Emperor's presence/value, not merely the abstract utility of his equipment.

The later milkshake invitation and reciprocal safety concern support an ordinary relational dimension beyond command traffic.

## 6. `中身` / shell / technology audit

### 6.1 Core conclusion

`中身なんだ`

`着ぐるみみたいな借りものの力に振り回されて 怪人化したのがあんただ`

`僕は僕のなりたいヒーローの背中を追うよ`

Locator: around `0082`.

Controlled reading:

- `中身` supports “the inside/person matters.”
- `借りものの力に振り回されて` targets being controlled by borrowed/external power.
- it does **not** say that machines or external tools are inherently false or corrupt.

The subsequent use of small robots and affectionate reunion with `カマセイヌマン` is strong contextual evidence against an anti-technology paraphrase.

### 6.2 Brave Giant sacrifice

`残存エネルギー 全解放`

`さよなら ブレイブジャイアント`

Locator: around `0083`.

Controlled reading: Child Emperor intentionally spends the machine's remaining capability. The farewell gives it affective weight; sacrifice of equipment is not contempt for equipment.

### 6.3 Phoenix's explicit identity fusion

`ドンカンバードは俺の魂!! 全存在!!`

`金輪際 脱ぐもんか`

Locator: around `0092`.

Controlled reading: Phoenix Man explicitly identifies the costume/character with soul and total existence and refuses removal. This strongly supports “identity-shell fusion.”

### 6.4 Threat-state result

`災害レベル:狼未満 怪人ヒヨコ男`

Locator: around `0093`.

Controlled reading: the local post-strategy state is explicitly below Wolf. Do not infer a universal de-monsterization mechanism.

## 7. Phoenix resurrection audit

The revived Hellfire Flame and Gale Wind remember their fight with Flash and express confusion about location before Saitama knocks them out again.

Controlled reading: Phoenix's revival ability can return at least these bodies to conscious behavior at this point. “Corpse puppet” is too narrow as a general description.

## 8. Pureblood audit

### 8.1 Talent / tricks

`嫉妬だよ`

`才能のない者が よくする勘違いだ`

`小細工をすれば 私との差が埋まると思ってる`

Locator: `0120`.

Controlled reading: Pureblood explicitly opposes innate superiority to compensatory `小細工`. “Aristocratic/essentialist superiority model” is supported as interpretation.

### 8.2 Authentic monster claim

`このヴァンパイア種は怪人協会の中で唯一の… 本物の怪人`

`先祖代々 異能の血を引く 吸血鬼だ`

Locator: `0126`.

Controlled reading: `本物の怪人` is Pureblood's self-classification based on inherited vampire lineage. It must remain speaker-attributed.

### 8.3 `不屈の正義`

Pureblood tells Zombieman:

`残念だが… 不屈の正義とやらが報われる事はないぞ`

Locator: `0126`.

Controlled reading: Pureblood explicitly names and dismisses Zombieman's indomitable justice. The eventual outcome creates dramatic counterevidence to Pureblood's confidence, not narrator proof of a universal moral law.

## 9. Zombieman attrition audit

### 9.1 `泥仕合`

`おい吸血鬼お前… 泥仕合だが覚悟しろよ`

Locator: `0144`.

Controlled reading: Zombieman knowingly accepts an ugly/protracted fight.

### 9.2 Thirty-minute cut and win accounting

After `30分後`, Zombieman says:

`ああ200回くらい お前が勝ってるよ`

`ようやく俺の1勝だな`

Locator: `0145-0146`.

Controlled reading: approximately 200 local “wins” for Pureblood versus the one engagement-deciding win for Zombieman. This is explicit metric pluralization, not merely visual inference.

### 9.3 Regeneration constraint

`流石にこの傷じゃ 完治まで数分はかかりそうだな`

`早く終わらせたい`

Locator: around `0151`.

Controlled reading: Zombieman's regeneration is extreme but not uniformly instantaneous. Damage and time remain real constraints.

## 10. Do-S / controlled humans audit

Iaian identifies the controlled people as Narinki's previously deployed private rescue squad. The disciples repeatedly treat them as externally controlled humans rather than willing monsters.

Controlled reading: this supports a morally relevant distinction between **agency capture** and **voluntary alignment**.

Do-S's control is discussed as hypnosis/conditioning through pain. Avoid treating every controlled victim as biologically transformed.

## 11. Amai Mask audit

### 11.1 Mechanism recognition and matchup judgment

Amai reads Do-S's control structure quickly and tells the disciples they are poorly matched to the situation. His tactical diagnosis and later moral solution must be evaluated separately.

### 11.2 The disciples explicitly distinguish controlled humans from monsters

Around `0185-0186`, the disciples note that the people Amai previously killed were monsters, whereas these are controlled humans who entered for the same hostage-rescue objective.

Controlled reading: the text itself marks the category distinction; an analysis that calls Amai's proposed killing “ordinary monster extermination” erases that distinction.

### 11.3 Amai's purity rule

`ヒーローでいる為には…`

`正義の味方でいる為には悪を討たねばならない—`

`悪に与した者も……`

Locator: `0194`.

Controlled reading: Amai's reasoning extends punishment from evil to those who have “sided with” evil. The page then presses the implication toward rescuers of those people. “Contamination logic” is a supported analytical label.

### 11.4 Hidden body anomaly

After a direct strike, Amai's face is visibly cracked/scaled/non-ordinary. Do-S begins:

`お お前… 人間じゃ…`

Locator: `0197`.

Controlled reading: V22 strongly establishes that Do-S sees something she interprets as inconsistent with ordinary humanity. The unfinished phrase and visual anomaly do **not** by themselves establish the final biological/ontological label.

### 11.5 Monster execution line

Amai attacks while saying:

`怪人は死ね`

Locator: `0198`.

Controlled reading: lethal/extermination intent toward Do-S is explicit. V22 should not assert a later survival/death state beyond what this volume shows.

### 11.6 Public image immediately restored

The rescued squad recognizes `イケメン仮面アマイマスク`, requests photos/autographs, and treats his visible injury as minor. Amai says the injury is not a problem / was only a graze.

Controlled reading: the visual-social contrast between hidden body anomaly and public celebrity face is deliberate and source-supported. Mechanism remains OPEN.

## 12. Extra `コーヒー` audit

The extra places Zombieman in a coffee shop, where civilians recognize his pallor/public identity. He receives another work call and requests replacement clothing, including mundane details.

Controlled reading: supports terse professional availability and practical material awareness. Do not convert the gag into evidence that Zombieman is indifferent to all bodily cost.
