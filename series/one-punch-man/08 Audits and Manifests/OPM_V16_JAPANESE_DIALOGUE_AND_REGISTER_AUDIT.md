# One Punch Man — Volume 16 Japanese Dialogue and Register Audit

## Audit result

**PASS.** The load-bearing language claims in `OPM_V16_DEEP_READING.md` are supportable from the Japanese V16 tankobon at the V15 observational boundary. No translation-dependent claim requires later material.

## 1. Death Gatling — rank, recognition, and worth

**Locator:** `OPM|V16|chapter:81|image:0054|anchor:Death Gatling ranking critique`

Japanese anchors:

- `ランキングなどが存在するせいで優劣がつき`
- `正当な評価が得られずにいる実力者も多い`
- `脚光を浴びるのは戦闘に特化したS級ヒーローばかりになりがちだ`
- `S級以外にも優秀なヒーローは存在するということを世間に知らしめてやる`

Audit:

- `優劣がつく` is comparative ordering/superiority-inferiority, not merely administrative sorting.
- `正当な評価` supports “proper/fair evaluation,” with normative force stronger than “attention.”
- `脚光を浴びる` is public spotlight/attention.
- `戦闘に特化した` specifically marks specialization in combat.
- `世間に知らしめる` is explicitly public demonstration/making society know.

**Finding:** high confidence. The institutional reading that Death Gatling sees a gap among rank, capability, specialization, and public recognition is textually grounded.

## 2. Death Gatling — `存在価値`

**Locator:** `OPM|V16|chapter:82|images:0113-0114|anchor:Death Gatling existence-value language`

Japanese anchor:

- `俺達の存在価値を示すために…`

`存在価値` is literally value/worth of existence and in context concerns professional/social worth as heroes. Rendering it merely “prove we're strong” would flatten the line.

**Finding:** high confidence. His motive is recognition of hero worth, not only tactical capture.

## 3. Garou — critique of rank fixation

**Locator:** `OPM|V16|chapter:82|image:0114|anchor:Garou attacks Death Gatling ranking fixation`

Japanese anchors:

- `ヒーローヒーローうるせぇよ`
- `一番ランキングを気にしてるのはテメェじゃねぇか`
- `くだらねぇ!!`

Register is contemptuous, highly informal, second-person `テメェ`. Garou does not offer a detached institutional critique; he attacks Death Gatling personally for being the one most preoccupied with ranking.

**Finding:** high confidence. Preserve the irony that Garou himself remains label-driven elsewhere.

## 4. Garou — Tareo collateral warning

**Locator:** `OPM|V16|chapter:82|image:0100|anchor:Tareo collateral warning`

Japanese:

`小屋の中にガキがいる その位置から撃てば巻き添えになるぞ`

- `ガキ` is rough “kid/brat,” fully consistent with Garou's gruff register rather than tender diction.
- `巻き添えになる` means being caught up as collateral/dragged into the harm.
- `ぞ` marks forceful warning/assertion.

**Finding:** high confidence. The protective significance comes from action and truth of the warning, not soft language.

## 5. Garou — monster-victory performance

**Locator:** `OPM|V16|chapter:82|image:0101|anchor:monster victory declaration`

Japanese:

`よく見とけ！ 怪人が勝つ瞬間を!!!`

Functional gloss: “Watch closely—the moment a monster wins.”

`怪人` is explicit self-categorization within Garou's ideological performance. The line occurs immediately around the Tareo-protection/Death Shower sequence, supporting juxtaposition between identity claim and conduct.

**Finding:** high confidence.

## 6. Glasses — support/pursuit role

**Locator:** `OPM|V16|chapter:81|image:0047|anchor:Glasses role statement`

Japanese:

`「逃がさない」はこっちの台詞だ`

`戦闘サポートの他に お前が逃走したとき追跡するのも僕の役割なんだよ`

`役割` is explicitly role/function. This is direct evidence that his contribution is designed around team function rather than individual finishing power.

**Finding:** high confidence.

## 7. Glasses — self-authored limit policy

**Locator:** `OPM|V16|extra:成長過程|image:0207|anchor:Glasses changed will`

Japanese:

`僕の中で変わったことは一つだけ 意志が固まったんです`

`自分の限界を決めないって`

Important qualification:

- `意志が固まった` = his resolve/will became firm.
- `自分の限界を決めない` = not to decide/fix his own limit.

It does **not** literally claim he has no limits or that effort guarantees unlimited growth.

**Finding:** high confidence. Use as a self-limitation/development policy, not power-scaling proof.

## 9. Genos — `お手`

**Locator:** `OPM|V16|chapter:83|image:0146|anchor:Genos dog-command joke`

Japanese:

`お手`

This is the common command for a dog to offer its paw. Applied to Garou's Watchdog-Man-derived movement, it deliberately collapses martial spectacle into mundane pet-command register.

**Finding:** high confidence; joke mechanism is lexical/register-based.

## 11. Garou — learning/survival as evidence

V16 repeatedly uses Garou's internal analytic register to convert opponent behavior into tactical information. His language remains rough and emotionally charged, but the cognition is highly structured: opponent speciality, geometry, stamina, weapon limitation, and prior-technique reuse.

**Finding:** strong qualitative replication of V15's “defeat as study” language policy.

## Audit disposition

No load-bearing V16 claim depends on an English localization. The main wording risks are:

1. translating Glasses' `自分の限界を決めない` as literal limitless potential;
2. reducing Death Gatling's `存在価値` and `正当な評価` to mere rank ambition;
3. softening Garou's rough Tareo language and thereby mistaking protective behavior for tender speech;
4. treating Bang's personal address as reconciliation rather than relational activation.

All four are controlled in the canonical deep reading.
