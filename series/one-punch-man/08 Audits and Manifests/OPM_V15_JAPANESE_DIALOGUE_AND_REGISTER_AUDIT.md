# One Punch Man — V15 Japanese Dialogue and Register Audit

## Audit result

**PASS.** The load-bearing claims in `OPM_V15_DEEP_READING.md` are supportable from the Japanese V15 tankobon under the V14 observational ceiling. No V16+ wording is needed to stabilize them.

Highest-risk interpretive traps:

- reducing Saitama's V15 dissatisfaction to lack of a strong opponent when `空回り` and the missed crisis introduce role/timing failure;
- treating King's `最強`/`最高` distinction as narrator doctrine rather than serious character counsel;
- claiming Saitama accepts King's philosophy as a settled life program;
- treating Garou's delight in learning as moral growth rather than combat/developmental growth;
- treating Sonic's decision to become a monster as proof that he actually becomes biologically nonhuman in V15;
- universalizing a biochemical rule that cooking always neutralizes Monster Cells;
- promoting Zombieman's conditional `俺の読みが正しければ` into confirmed conspiracy fact;
- treating disaster levels or hero-rank equivalences as exact power equations when the extra explicitly qualifies and revises them;
- treating `目撃` as reliable eyewitness causation when the extra is constructed around causal misattribution;
- scoring Genos's Kuseno-lab reflection as a mechanism-under-action Checkpoint-A test when it is post-defeat repair analysis.

## 1. Saitama — `空回り` and missed usefulness

### Locator
`OPM|V15|chapter:76|images:0020-0021`

### Source wording
After learning from the injured C-class hero that the major monster incident arose and ended while he was elsewhere, Saitama says:

`そんな一大事を見逃すなんて 空回りしてばっかりだな`

### Audit
`空回り` literally evokes spinning without productive engagement: effort/activity that fails to connect with an effective result. It is semantically different from simple `退屈`, `弱い`, or “there was nobody strong.”

### Supported claim
Saitama's frustration includes failure of timing/usefulness, not only combat-challenge deprivation.

### Do not promote
- “Saitama feels guilty for every injured hero.” The line is self-critical frustration, but V15 does not sustain a guilt sequence.
- “Saitama decides to change his patrol system.” No such follow-through exists in V15.

## 2. King — progression beyond level grinding

### Locator
`OPM|V15|chapter:77|image:0033`

### Source wording
`アイテムを全部集めたり 最速クリア記録に挑戦したり 他のプレーヤーと交流したり やりこみ要素はレベル上げ以外にも見つけられるよ`

### Audit
`やりこみ要素` refers to depth/completionist or advanced-play elements; `レベル上げ以外` explicitly separates them from level grinding. `他のプレーヤーと交流` introduces social engagement as one legitimate game activity alongside collection and record pursuit.

### Supported claim
King offers a domain-native model in which maxing one numerical progression axis does not exhaust possible mastery/engagement.

## 3. King — strength is not the destination

### Locator
`OPM|V15|chapter:77|image:0037`

### Source wording
`サイタマ氏は強くなっただけで目的地に辿り着いたと勘違いしてるんじゃないかな?`

`ヒーローとして進む道ってそんなに簡単にゴールできるものじゃないと思うんだ`

### Audit
`強くなっただけで` means “just by having become strong.” `勘違い` identifies a mistaken identification, while `ゴール` continues the journey/game metaphor.

### Supported claim
King explicitly disputes the inference physical strength completion -> hero completion.

### Do not promote
- “King denies that strength matters.” He denies that it exhausts the destination.

## 4. King — hero purpose and `最強` versus `最高`

### Locators
`OPM|V15|chapter:77|images:0038-0039`

### Source wording
`人助けや世の役に立つことにこそ ヒーローの存在意義があるのではないか`

King further distinguishes Saitama as potentially `最強のヒーロー` without yet being `最高のヒーロー` and frames `最高のヒーローとは何か?` as an open question.

### Audit
`最強` is strongest by strength. `最高` is highest/best/supreme in evaluative quality. The lexical contrast is deliberate and cannot be translated away without losing the argument.

`存在意義` is reason/significance for existence or role. King's syntax remains proposal-like (`のではないか`), not omniscient narration.

### Supported claim
King articulates a character-level ethical theory in which useful/prosocial hero purpose remains open after physical supremacy.

### Do not promote
- “The manga has objectively defined the single essence of heroism.” The source gives King's serious argument.

## 5. Garou — defeat is explicitly study

### Locators
`OPM|V15|chapter:77|images:0048-0049`

### Source wording
`勉強になったぜ 興奮が冷めねぇ`

`楽しくてしょうがねぇ! 俺はもっともっと強くなれるんだ!!`

### Audit
`勉強になった` literally marks the encounter as educational. `興奮が冷めねぇ` and `楽しくてしょうがねぇ` establish positive arousal/enjoyment, while `もっともっと強くなれる` is future-capability language.

### Supported claim
Garou consciously converts defeat/unfamiliar technique into learning and experiences continued growth as pleasurable.

### Do not promote
- moral improvement;
- proof that every defeat will be survived or successfully integrated.

## 7. Sonic — ordinary common sense alters the conversion procedure

### Locator
`OPM|V15|chapter:78|image:0098`

### Source wording
`これ…火を通して良かったんだよな?`

`常識的に考えて こんな得体の知れない生肉をそのまま食える訳がないしな…`

### Audit
`火を通す` is to cook/heat through. `常識的に考えて` invokes ordinary common sense. `得体の知れない生肉` is unknown/suspicious raw meat.

### Supported claim
Sonic deliberately deviates from the prescribed raw-ingestion procedure because ordinary food-safety logic overrides the transformation instructions.

### Do not promote
- a universal “cooking destroys Monster Cells” law;
- successful monsterization followed by reversal.

V15 shows no visible successful monster body and immediately follows with stomach illness.

## 8. Zombieman — explicit hypothesis status

### Locator
`OPM|V15|chapter:78|image:0087`

### Source wording
`俺の読みが正しければ…今回の騒動を裏で操っている黒幕はあの組織…`

### Audit
`俺の読みが正しければ` means “if my reading is correct.” It grammatically marks the following causal attribution as a hypothesis.

`黒幕` means hidden power/mastermind/actor behind events. `あの組織` is “that organization” and is not independently resolved by this line alone.

### Supported claim
Zombieman is pursuing a hidden-actor hypothesis.

### Do not promote
- the hypothesis to source fact;
- an identity for `あの組織` not established inside V15.

## 18. `目撃` — observation does not equal causal knowledge

### Locators
`OPM|V15|extra:目撃|images:0207-0209`

### Source structure
After a giant monster is destroyed, injured heroes report that it was “that man again.” The visual reveal supplies King, who is actually retrieving `萌え萌えクロニクル2` rather than having performed the kill.

### Audit
The Japanese plus visual sequence supports a wrong causal attribution built from genuine post-event observation.

### Supported claim
King's legend is strengthened by witness inference/reputation prior rather than direct observation of his causal action.

### Do not promote
- a conscious lie by King in this scene;
- a deliberate credit sacrifice by Saitama. V15 does not show Saitama making an explicit recognition tradeoff here.

# Final audit disposition

**PASS / CANONICAL.**

The source-language layer supports V15's governing distinctions:

- physical completion does not entail hero/personal completion;
- `最強` and `最高` are not synonyms;
- Garou's growth is explicitly learned and enjoyed, but not morally redeemed;
- Sonic's monster intent is not successful monster body status;
- Zombieman's hidden-actor claim remains conditional;
- classification is revisable operational modeling;
- Genos's maintenance attention is a real mundane relationship behavior;
- `目撃` demonstrates causal misattribution rather than reliable eyewitness knowledge.

No V16+ evidence is required.
