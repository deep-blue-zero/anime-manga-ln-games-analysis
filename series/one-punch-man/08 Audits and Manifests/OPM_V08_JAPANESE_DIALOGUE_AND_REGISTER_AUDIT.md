# One Punch Man — V08 Japanese Dialogue and Register Audit
## King confession, role/credit semantics, Genos mechanism cognition, mundane mismatch, and Garou self-authorship

## 1. Audit disposition

**PASS WITH MODEL REFINEMENT.**

The Japanese line checks support the V08 deep reading. Highest-value rulings:

1. King explicitly says he was merely present at scenes and has never fought, resolving the V06 reputation/self-report contradiction without importing later material.
2. King's `嘘の塊` self-description establishes shame and public/private identity split; it should not be rewritten as proof that he deliberately originated every false attribution.
3. Saitama's `手柄とか そういう問題じゃないだろ` makes prestige restitution secondary after learning King may hold Saitama's own achievements.
4. `お前は皆のヒーローなんだぜ` describes King's current social role, not the factual truth of his combat record.
5. `だったら 強くなればいいんじゃね?` is bounded competence advice, not a complete ethical solution to lying/institutional fraud.
6. `たまにゲームやりに来るから ヨロシク` establishes an ordinary friendship channel after full disclosure.
7. Genos's `無駄だ 湯気は光を拡散する` is a clean mechanism-under-action test and confirms G-A-02.
8. `迷い猫` supports both low-ceremony routine behavior and explicit role/competence limitation in Saitama.
9. Garou says he admired monsters, trained, and crushed martial-arts dojos; V08 does **not** establish later bodily monsterization.
10. Kuseno/Genos dialogue shows revenge persistence rather than post-Saitama resolution.

No V09+, anime, webcomic, translation, or later redraw wording is used as authority.

## 2. King — accidental attribution is explicit

King's retrospective account includes:

> `現場に居合わせただけで 俺が倒したと思ってる`
>
> `俺は喧嘩もした事がない`

The semantic distinction is important. `居合わせただけ` makes presence, not fraudulent combat action, the historical basis of the attribution. Surrounding people `勝手に` interpreted and rewarded him.

**Audit ruling:** the V08-canonical origin model is **passive/accidental causal misattribution that King subsequently failed to correct**, not “King fabricated all achievements from scratch.”

## 3. King — `嘘の塊` and public/private self split

King says:

> `何もかも間違ってやがる`
>
> `俺は嘘の塊のような男!`
>
> `世間が憧れるキングは俺ではない`
>
> `だが本物のキングはどこかに存在する!`

`嘘の塊` is self-condemnation under shame. It describes how King experiences the identity he occupies. It does not retroactively establish intentional authorship of each underlying rumor.

**Model consequence:** public persona and private self-conception are radically split; King can benefit from the myth while also experiencing it as alien/fraudulent.

## 4. Saitama — direct inquiry rather than prestige inference

Saitama knows King is S-class and still enters his apartment through a twenty-second-floor window, proposes games, and later asks directly why King fled.

His hypothesis—perhaps King is so strong that combat became uninteresting—maps King's inconsistent behavior onto Saitama's own known condition.

**Audit ruling:** this is strong evidence for **active but fallible/self-conditioned social inference**. Do not code the wrong hypothesis as stupidity or the act of inference as proof of accurate mind-reading.

Checkpoint S-A-06 receives `CONFIRM`: status alone does not produce deference.

## 5. Saitama — credit is explicitly not the controlling problem

After King says Saitama's own achievements may have been attributed to him, Saitama answers:

> `手柄とか そういう問題じゃないだろ`

The noun `手柄` is precisely achievement/credit/merit attribution. Saitama does **not** say credit has no value in general. The construction says **that is not what this problem is about**.

**Checkpoint ruling:** S-A-03 `CONFIRM`, high confidence. The V08 case is stronger than a generic humility claim because the misattributed achievement is explicitly connected to Saitama.

## 6. Saitama — `皆のヒーロー` is role language, not causal truth

Saitama tells King:

> `お前は皆のヒーローなんだぜ`
>
> `お前が何と言おうと皆は最強のヒーローだと思ってる`

The second sentence is explicitly about **what everyone thinks**, not about objective capability. Therefore the first sentence should be read as socially operative role/expectation, not as Saitama endorsing the false combat history.

**Canonical interpretation:** false history and present social role can coexist as separate facts.

## 7. `だったら 強くなればいいんじゃね?` — bounded competence repair

King asks whether he should continue the lie or quit being a hero. Saitama does not select either institutional option. He says:

> `だったら 強くなればいいんじゃね?`

This is not an argument that strength erases deception. It proposes one actionable way to reduce the current mismatch between reputation and ability.

**Model consequence:** Saitama often compresses complex problems into the concrete dimension he can act on or understand. The line should not be inflated into a general moral philosophy of “power makes legitimacy.”

## 8. Friendship closure

King asks why Saitama is not angry despite Saitama having seriously pursued heroism. Saitama says:

> `たまにゲームやりに来るから ヨロシク`

The casual `ヨロシク` and future-frequency framing are unusually important. Saitama is not merely tolerating King at the end of a crisis. He proposes repeated ordinary contact.

**Relationship ruling:** V08 creates a durable Saitama/King low-stakes channel centered on games. It is appropriate to call this an emerging peer friendship, while avoiding claims about later depth.

## 9. Genos — mechanism cognition during G4 fight

Genos says:

> `無駄だ 湯気は光を拡散する`

The grammatical structure is direct causal explanation inside combat, not retrospective analysis after danger ends.

**Checkpoint ruling:** G-A-02 `CONFIRM`. Genos can identify/exploit a mechanism while acting decisively.

This is stronger than V07's inquiry after Drive Knight's warning, which was only `PARTIAL`.

## 10. G4 salvage and capability acquisition

Genos brings Kuseno damaged G4 parts and describes the machine as possessing enormous power and advanced artificial intelligence. He asks for any usable components to be integrated and states:

> `俺はもっと強くなりたい`

**Model consequence:** opponent destruction can feed a technological learning/acquisition pipeline. Do not infer that Kuseno successfully integrates a specific component until later source evidence says so.

## 11. Genos — revenge remains active

When Kuseno asks about the rampaging cyborg, Genos says hero activity has slowed the investigation but reiterates that he will find and completely destroy the target:

> `奴だけは必ず見つけ出し この手で完全に破壊する`
>
> `何があっても 絶対に!`

Kuseno observes that even becoming a hero and finding a master has not made the hatred fade.

**Audit ruling:** V08 rejects a premature “Saitama healed Genos's revenge motive” formulation. Development and revenge coexist.

## 12. Kuseno — protective caution is explicit

Kuseno asks Genos to contact him if the target is found and warns him not to fight alone. He characterizes Genos as intelligent/calm while also warning that youth can lead to ruin.

This is a stronger relationship sample than technical maintenance alone.

**Relationship consequence:** Kuseno occupies a protective elder/technical mentor role with independent knowledge of Genos's revenge target.

## 13. Sitch — emergency recruitment separates morality and capacity

Sitch's briefing says repeated strong monsters/disasters will exhaust heroes and that the unprecedented crisis indicated by the prophecy is not waiting. He then asks for cooperation from experienced fighters in humanity's defense:

> `人格の善悪を問わず`

The wording explicitly suspends moral-personality sorting as a condition for requested combat cooperation.

**Institution ruling:** the Association is knowingly separating usable fighting capacity from moral/legal qualification under perceived emergency shortage. Do not rewrite this as blanket amnesty or full hero registration for all criminals unless the text says so.

## 14. Garou — monster aspiration without body transformation

Garou identifies himself:

> `俺はガロウ`
>
> `怪人に憧れて修行し 数々の武術道場を潰してきた男だ`

Key semantic point: `怪人に憧れて` = admired/aspired toward monsters. The line does **not** say his body is already a `怪人` or that a transformation mechanism has occurred.

His preceding enthusiasm for the unprecedented `災害レベル神` forecast and his confidence that the assembled fighters cannot kill him establish adversarial orientation and extreme self-confidence at V08.

**Canonical state:** human martial antagonist; monster-aspirational identity; later ontology OPEN.

## 15. `迷い猫` — routine task and hobby identity

Saitama becomes irritated by the crude lost-cat drawing, the added child-care burden, and the implication that the Association can simply assign any task. When threatened with being reported, he says being fired is not decisive because heroism was originally his hobby.

Yet when the child becomes upset, he immediately says he will search properly because he has time.

**Checkpoint ruling:** S-A-01 `CONFIRM`.

Correct model:

`trivial/routine assignment -> complaints / low ceremony / occupational resistance`, but **not** `trivial assignment -> abandonment of help`.

## 16. `迷い猫` — role mismatch and limitation

After the incident Saitama tells Genos:

> `俺プロヒーロー向いてないかもしれないな`

and says people have different roles. The final scratch gag closes with:

> `猫探しは向いてないね`

He nevertheless completed the search and protected the child.

**Checkpoint ruling:** S-A-05 `CONFIRM`. This is direct evidence for “admit role/competence limits and help concretely rather than pretend universal expertise.”

Do not broaden “not suited to cat-searching” into “not suited to heroism” as an objective narrator verdict; the line is Saitama's situational self-assessment.

## 17. `迷い猫` — rank boundary

Tanktop Tiger and Tanktop Black Hole enforce a sealed-area rule and explicitly dislike lower-ranked heroes whose rank advancement appears insufficiently earned. Saitama recognizes the description applies to him.

He does not respond with upward register or status anxiety and eventually enters when the practical objective requires it.

**Checkpoint ruling:** S-A-06 `CONFIRM` independently within V08.

The guards themselves also receive corrective evidence: their earlier status hostility coexists with genuine assigned perimeter duty.

## 18. `海老` — Saitama's care register

Bang asks for help consuming a large shipment of expensive lobster. Saitama is privately excited by the food while outwardly framing participation as help. He later says:

> `こういう時は助け合いだ`
>
> `孤独なジイさんとメシくらい付き合ってやろうぜ`

**Model consequence:** practical social care can be food-mediated and low ceremony. The presence of self-interested food enjoyment does not cancel the companionship motive; both are textually present.

## 19. `海老` — Genos domestic register

Genos reacts strongly to the absence of napa cabbage and to Charanko's lack of respect toward guests. He also explains Kuseno restored taste and that organic intake can serve as biofuel.

This broadens low-stakes voice but does **not** cleanly test G-A-04. It should not be forced into a held-out result merely because it contains practical detail.
