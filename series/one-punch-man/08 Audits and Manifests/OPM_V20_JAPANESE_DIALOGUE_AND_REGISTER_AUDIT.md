# 1. Mission-priority language

## 1.1 `人質救出作戦` / `そのオマケ`

Image `0019`:

`これはあくまで人質救出作戦であって 怪人討伐はそのオマケだ`

Safe sense:

> This is first and foremost a hostage-rescue operation; monster extermination is the secondary add-on here.

Controls:

- `あくまで` strongly fixes the operative frame/purpose.
- `人質救出作戦` is a hostage-rescue operation/mission, not merely “a fight where a hostage happens to be present.”
- `そのオマケ` is colloquial and deliberately dismissive in tone, but analytically it establishes **priority**, not permanent moral insignificance of defeating monsters.
- Do not translate the line into “killing monsters does not matter.” The immediately surrounding plan still treats cadres as lethal enemies who must be dealt with.

Child Emperor follows with a direct warning to Tatsumaki not to forget that priority and destroy the entire base in a large-scale opening attack, because his investigation has not identified the hostage/boss position precisely enough.

## 1.2 `遭遇した者が責任を持って仕留める`

Image `0020`:

`何かに遭遇したら何だろうとボスだろうと戦いは避けられない`

and:

`遭遇した者が責任を持って仕留めるんだ`

Safe sense:

> If someone encounters an enemy—even the boss—combat may be unavoidable; the person who encounters it is responsible for finishing it.

Controls:

- `責任を持って` is responsibility/accountability for completion, not institutional ownership of the enemy.
- `仕留める` is stronger than generic “handle”; it means finish off / bring down / decisively dispatch in this combat context.
- The line establishes decentralized encounter responsibility inside a centrally planned operation.

# 2. Sweet Mask — combat power, responsibility and control

## 2.1 `戦闘力のみで評価されて`

Image `0029`:

`戦闘力のみで評価されて S級という席を設けられてはいるが`

Safe sense:

> They may have been evaluated only on combat power and given seats in S-Class, but…

Controls:

- `のみ` is restrictive: combat power **only**.
- `席` is literally a seat/place; “S-Class position” is natural analytical English.
- The sentence is Sweet Mask's critique, not narrator-certified institutional history beyond what his wording itself asserts.

## 2.2 `力を持つ者の責任`

Image `0029`:

`力を持つ者の責任 果たしてないんじゃないか？`

Safe sense:

> Aren't they failing to fulfill the responsibility borne by those who possess power?

Controls:

- `力を持つ者` is “those who possess power/strength,” carrying normative status.
- `責任` here is responsibility/obligation, not causal responsibility.
- The line should not be softened into “they could try harder.” Sweet Mask is making a moral-professional claim about what power obligates.

## 2.3 `管理命令されてこそ`

Image `0030`:

`管理命令されてこそヒーローになれるのがキミ達S級なんだろう`

Safe sense:

> You S-Class are the sort who can function/become heroes only when managed and ordered, aren't you?

Controls:

- `管理` = management/control/administration depending context.
- `命令` = orders/commands.
- `されてこそ` gives a strong “only through / precisely by being…” construction.
- Do not reduce this to a neutral recommendation that S-Class heroes “benefit from coordination.” Sweet Mask is making a much more controlling claim.

## 2.4 `ただの危険人物`

Same image:

`でなければ戦力が高いだけのただの危険人物だ`

Safe sense:

> Otherwise you're merely dangerous people with high combat strength.

Controls:

- `戦力が高い` = high combat/war-fighting capacity.
- `だけ` and `ただの` jointly strip away normative hero status.
- `危険人物` is dangerous person/individual, not “villain” or “monster.”

The deep reading is correct to separate the **validity of the critique** from Sweet Mask's **self-authorized command solution**.

# 4. Genos and Kuseno — hard power constraint

## 4.1 Attack-specialized imbalance

Image `0066`:

Kuseno characterizes the upgrade as:

`かなり無茶なパワーアップじゃ`

and:

`バランスの悪い攻撃力特化型`

Safe sense:

> a fairly reckless power-up; an imbalanced attack-power-specialized configuration.

Controls:

- `無茶` signals imprudence/extremity, not simply “large.”
- `攻撃力特化型` is specifically attack-power specialization.
- Later scaling must preserve the stated tradeoff rather than treating the upgrade as uniform improvement.

## 4.2 Self-regulation

Image `0066`:

`装甲を削らぬ範囲でギリギリまで馬力を高めた`

`パワーを自分で制御しながら戦うんじゃよ`

Safe sense:

> Output has been pushed to the limit within what the armor/components can tolerate; Genos must fight while controlling that power himself.

The exact technical noun mapping can vary, but the decision constraint is unambiguous: **Genos is responsible for regulating the output in combat.**

## 4.3 Ten seconds

Image `0067`:

`10秒が限界じゃな`

and later:

`使えるフルパワーは10秒まで 忘れてはならんぞ`

Safe sense:

> Ten seconds is the limit.
>
> Usable full power is up to ten seconds. Do not forget it.

Controls:

- `限界` = limit/upper bound.
- `まで` establishes the usable window ceiling.
- This is not “roughly ten seconds if convenient” in the local text.

Kuseno also says that beyond the limit Genos's body will not hold and that an energy-core explosion could kill even a cyborg. The deep reading's catastrophic-risk constraint is therefore warranted.

# 5. Domestic/relationship register

## 5.1 Genos `正当性`

Images `0068-0069`:

`俺には先生の弟子だという正当性がある`

Safe sense:

> I have the legitimacy/justification of being Sensei's disciple.

Controls:

- `正当性` is conspicuously formal/grand for a dispute about constantly being in Saitama's apartment.
- “legitimacy” preserves the comic quasi-juridical register better than plain “I have a reason.”
- Fubuki's rent/tolerance response deliberately grounds Genos's abstract claim in mundane household reality.

## 5.2 `俺は先生を待つ`

Image `0077`:

`俺は先生を待つ`

Safe sense:

> I'll wait for Sensei.

It is short and unambiguous. Do not inflate it into a vow not to act independently under any future condition. Its evidentiary force is local: at this pre-raid moment, Genos makes his movement contingent on Saitama.

## 5.3 `新フブキ組`

Fubuki repeatedly uses `新フブキ組` for the Saitama-centered cluster.

Safe sense: “new Blizzard Group.”

Controls:

- `組` naturally marks a group/crew/faction.
- Fubuki's use of the label is evidence of **her categorization**.
- No assent from the named people is shown in V20; translation must not turn her naming into an objective organizational fact.

# 6. Tatsumaki / Fubuki plural reference

Image `0115` follows Jagan's explicit reference to Tatsumaki's younger sister/another esper.

Tatsumaki says:

`…よく喋るゴミ袋ね`

`いいわ…ちょっと教えてあげましょ`

`私達に手を出すとどうなるか`

Safe sense:

> …You really are a talkative garbage bag.
>
> Fine. I'll teach you a little what happens when you lay a hand on us.

Controls:

- `私達` is plural “we/us.” It must not be mistranslated as singular “me.”
- Given the immediately preceding younger-sister reference, treating the plural as relationship-relevant is warranted.
- The safest analytical formulation is that the line **binds Tatsumaki's threat to a plural that includes the sister just invoked in context**; it should not be expanded into a universal statement about all heroes without evidence.
- `手を出す` can mean lay a hand on / meddle with / make a move against. Here threat/aggression context favors “lay a hand on / go after.”

This materially strengthens, but does not simplify, the Tatsumaki→Fubuki protection/control model.

# 7. Lower-rank hero competence and rank language

## 7.1 `凄腕`

Image `0143`, Sekingar:

`彼らはA級ヒーロー S級がいなければトップを飾っていたはずのヒーロー達だからな`

`そして他のメンバーも B級やC級とはいえども凄腕だ`

Safe sense:

> They are A-Class heroes—people who would have occupied the top if S-Class did not exist. And the other members, though B- and C-Class, are highly capable/skilled too.

Controls:

- `凄腕` = highly capable/skilled/expert; do **not** translate it as “S-Class level.”
- The statement is Sekingar's contextual assessment, though the surrounding field evidence strongly supports his point that the selected group is unusually capable.

## 7.2 Double Hole's explicit generalization

Image `0144`:

`戦闘に長けてるのは上位ランカーだけとは限らないということだな`

Safe sense:

> Being skilled at combat isn't something limited only to the higher-ranked people.

Controls:

- `戦闘に長けてる` = skilled/proficient in combat.
- `上位ランカー` = upper/high-ranking ranked people.
- `だけとは限らない` explicitly denies exclusivity, not correlation.
- The line does **not** say rankings carry no information.

## 7.3 Twin Tail `自分の身は自分で護るさ`

Image `0119`:

`自分の身は自分で護るさ`

Safe sense:

> I'll protect myself / We protect our own selves here.

The singular wording belongs to the speaker; analytically it supports professional self-reliance rather than a universal policy against mutual support.

# 8. Captain Mizuki — athletic register as cognition

Mizuki asks Sekingar to blow the whistle and explains that it helps her get into form/rhythm. She then labels successive combat maneuvers `種目`—events/discipline events—and names recognizable track-and-field forms such as throwing and vaulting actions.

Controls:

- `種目` should retain the sports-competition frame rather than become generic “attack type.”
- Her combat behavior is therefore legitimately analyzed as **athletic procedural transfer**, not merely an aesthetic motif imposed from outside.
- When she later admits that outside track-and-field she cannot do grappling-type technique, that should be preserved as a domain limit rather than ignored because her physical performance is spectacular.

# 10. Rhino Wrestler — scoring and strongest-self language

## 10.1 `採点`

Rhino Wrestler repeatedly assigns point totals to incoming techniques. The ordinary noun/verb field is literal **scoring/grading**.

Controls:

- Preserve the evaluation/exam register. Translating every score as generic “damage” would erase the joke and the character motive.
- The values are Rhino Wrestler's private scale, not a narrator-certified power system.

Iaian receives a displayed `69点` after stopping the charge. Other attacks receive other scores. These numbers may establish **Rhino's comparative impression within his own test**, not universal cross-character ratios.

## 10.2 `最強`

Image `0187`:

Rhino Wrestler describes relentless training to become the strongest on the surface and to prove his own `最強`.

Safe analytical sense:

> He is motivated by demonstrative proof of his own strongest-self/supremacy.

Controls:

- `最強` can be “strongest” or “supreme strength”; in this scene it is an ego ideal he wants to prove.
- His statement is aspiration/self-belief, not narrator certification that he actually is the strongest.

## 10.3 Pleasure condition

Image `0194`:

`相手の技を受けきった上で 捻じ伏せる`

`これに勝る快感はねーぜぇ`

Safe sense:

> Taking everything the opponent's technique has and then forcing them down—there's no pleasure better than that.

Controls:

- The pleasure is not merely “winning.” It is **demonstrative domination after receiving the opponent's technique**.
- This supports the deep reading's evaluation/ego model.

# 12. Register notes for major recurring speakers

## Child Emperor

Still analytical, operational and qualifier-sensitive. He uses counts when useful, then explicitly identifies uncertainty. His language about weak waves (`だけじゃなさそう`) preserves hypothesis rather than certainty.

## Sweet Mask

Controlled, normative and managerial. Even while ordering cola, he frames the S-Class through abstract responsibility and rational management. The juxtaposition makes his authority performance slightly comic without making the underlying claim trivial.

## Tatsumaki

Blunt, contemptuous and compressed. `ゴミ袋`, `諦めなさい`, and `何で私がアンタ達を守らなくちゃいけないの？` preserve her low patience. The plural `私達` after a Fubuki threat is therefore particularly diagnostic because it appears inside the same hard register rather than a suddenly sentimental one.

## Bang

Old-man first-person `ワシ` remains stable. The repeated `ワシはワシの責任` is stronger than a generic “I should help”; it foregrounds personally owned obligation.

## Genos

Formal/absolute around Saitama. `先生`, `正当性`, and the simple `俺は先生を待つ` show that disciple identity can structure both comic domestic claims and serious action timing.

## Fubuki

Organizational naming remains natural to her speech/thought. `新フブキ組` should be preserved as her classificatory move, not normalized into narrator language.

## Rhino Wrestler

Boastful, evaluative, demonstrative. Point scoring, `最強`, and pleasure in receiving then crushing techniques form one coherent ego-performance register.
