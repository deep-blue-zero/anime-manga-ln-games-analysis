## Principal V11 -> V12 transitions

- **STRENGTHEN - capacity / purpose / domain:** V12 repeatedly separates possession of strength from the role, purpose, obligation, and experiential access attached to it.
- **STRENGTHEN - Saitama:** martial inquiry becomes explicitly experiential (`体験`); excess capability can itself become an experiential constraint by ending the test before he can learn from it.
- **STRENGTHEN - Genos:** accepts local inferiority in an opponent-superior domain, changes the matchup with an engineered adhesive counter, and later explicitly revises his model after a `規格外` defeat.
- **STRENGTHEN / REVISE - Fubuki:** her coercive hierarchy coexists with genuine subordinate care; she accepts tactical self-cost to avoid hurting controlled group members and names them `味方`.
- **STRENGTHEN - Tatsumaki:** sister protection remains genuine but is paired with unilateral control and treatment of weaker allies as `足手まとい`.
- **PROMOTE - Snek:** `emerging -> moderate`; professional-usefulness identity, existential doubt, rescue ethics, philosophical conflict, and defeat broaden the earlier status model.
- **PROMOTE - King:** `emerging -> moderate`; restaurant routine, self-produced-game script writing, ambiguous-threat handling, reputation dynamics, and recurring Saitama gaming add longitudinal low-stakes and stress breadth.
- **PROMOTE - Drive Knight:** `insufficient -> emerging`; V12 supplies an independent field/intelligence/coercion policy while allegiance remains OPEN.
- **NEW - Pig God / Watchdog Man:** both enter `emerging` as bounded but coherent hero models.
- **STRENGTHEN - Monster Association:** Gyoro Gyoro performs strategic force accounting and Orochi is presented as `怪人王`; complete hidden authority remains OPEN.
- **STRENGTHEN / DECOMPOSE - Hero Association:** Narinki's 7% funding share adds concrete donor leverage alongside responder shortage and information uncertainty.
- **STRENGTHEN - King public narrative:** reputation now generates deterrence and attributed moral greatness, while Watchdog Man supplies an accurate-reputation control case.
