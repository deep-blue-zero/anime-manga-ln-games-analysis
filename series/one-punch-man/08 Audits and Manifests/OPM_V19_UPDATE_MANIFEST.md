Garou still calls himself a monster while rescuing Tareo. Against Orochi he accepts strength but says a Monster King needs fear. His project therefore becomes more explicitly about producing a social/emotional condition rather than merely accumulating force.

V19 exposes both strength and error in formal planning: relationship-risk inference, explicit roster exclusion, professional-risk allocation, and King reputation imported as operational fact.

Gyoro states a repeatable near-death/stress experimental doctrine, denies Garou refusal, and treats Orochi as prior success. Orochi immediately copies Garou's martial form, making adaptive genius a contested rather than unique property.
