## 5. Principal V01 -> V02 claim transitions

- **Recognition:** REVISE. V01 correctly withheld recognition as a dominant need; V02 makes it explicit. The best current formulation is social legibility, attribution, and feedback rather than simple fame hunger.
- **Saitama affect:** STRENGTHEN. Mundane stakes and social consequences continue to activate him more reliably than ordinary physical danger.
- **Saitama strength explanation:** OPEN. He sincerely attributes his power to sustained ordinary training, but V02 does not establish that explanation as exhaustive reader-level ontology.
- **Saitama restraint:** STRENGTHEN. Hammerhead's surrender is accepted and Saitama explicitly says he attempted to stop his strike against Sonic.
- **Genos deference:** REVISE. Respect for Saitama coexists with direct epistemic disagreement; he is not blindly obedient.
- **Genos lethality:** STRENGTHEN. His polite register does not imply gentleness; he offers to eliminate Sonic if Sonic troubles Saitama.
- **Saitama/Genos relationship:** STRENGTHEN. The relationship becomes more reciprocal and instrumentally consequential while remaining asymmetric.
- **Saitama/Sonic rivalry:** REJECT as mutual. Sonic assigns rivalry-level meaning; Saitama does not reciprocate the stakes.
- **Heroism:** REVISE. V02 formally separates heroic action, self-identification, professional registration, public recognition, rank, pay, and moral worth.
