## 2. Chapter-title controls

- `大凶` carries severe/bad-fortune catastrophe framing; do not force it into a technical claim about one specific monster.
- `強敵` means formidable/strong enemy; the title does not independently decide who the chapter's "true" enemy is.
- `生贄` means sacrifice/offering and directly supports sacrificial framing in the Orochi/altar sequence.
- `フェイク` licenses attention to false/apparent status, but not the claim that every event in the chapter is ontologically fake.
- `再戦` is rematch/re-engagement and cleanly frames Fubuki/Do-S.
- `鏡` is mirror. The strongest lexical/structural anchor is the Genos self-model sequence itself.
- `強一般人` literally juxtaposes strength with the category of ordinary/general person; it supports the extra's ordinary-strength register.

## 3. Load-bearing lexical/attribution controls

### 3.1 Evil Natural Water
The source presents Evil Natural Water as a Dragon-level fluid target whose response is tied to aggressive/hostile engagement. Preserve the cautious formulation `hostility/attack-responsive mechanism` unless quoting a precisely transcribed line. Do not infer a complete emotion-sensing ontology or universal trigger threshold from V24 alone.

### 3.2 Saitama/Rover
Saitama's register toward Rover is ordinary and pet-like rather than disaster-class formal. This supports low ceremony. It does not prove that Saitama possesses correct biological knowledge of Rover or considers every monster redeemable.

### 3.3 Gyoro Gyoro and controller language
Tatsumaki's dialogue and behavior support the proposition that the visible Gyoro Gyoro body/surface is not accepted as the final controlling subject. Where V24 does not explicitly supply a name in the immediate line, use `controller`, `source`, or `main body` rather than importing later name certainty.

### 3.4 `重力 300倍`
The psychic contest includes an explicitly quantified gravity framing at `300倍`. This is speaker/attack-description evidence for the technique's stated scale. Do not convert the statement into a full physical measurement model without additional assumptions.

### 3.5 Orochi, altar, and `神`
The chapter title `生贄` and the altar/mural text make sacrifice language explicit. Orochi reads the scene in terms of an offering and the return/revival of `我々の神` / "our God" or equivalent God-language.

Authority boundary:
- the inscription/mural and Orochi's reading are source facts;
- the referent's ontological identity is **not** narrator-certified merely because `神` appears;
- equivalence with Homeless Emperor's V23 entity remains **OPEN**;
- do not say that the entity itself speaks in V24 unless a separate scene explicitly establishes speech.

### 3.6 `ガイア砲`
Orochi's named attack is `ガイア砲` (Gaia Cannon). Preserve the proper-technique label. Its visuals support enormous geological scale, but the name alone does not establish a scientific energy yield.

### 3.7 Saitama's Serious Series water attack
Saitama explicitly invokes `マジシリーズ` and answers with a water-squirting/water-gun style serious technique. The lexical mismatch between `Serious Series` prestige and mundane `water squirt` semantics is part of the joke. Do not normalize it into a generic "energy counterattack."

### 3.8 Do-S and control vocabulary
Do-S's language and power are domination/enslavement oriented. Fubuki's group/subordinate vocabulary can be hierarchical, but the Japanese should not be flattened so that both women are described with the same generic verb `lead`. V24's ethical contrast depends on the agency difference.

### 3.9 King reputation
Fearful enemy reactions around King support belief in the public King model. They do not become narrator confirmation that King's body possesses the attributed strength. Keep `believes`, `assumes`, `fears`, and reputation-language attached to the observer.

### 3.10 Genos and `鏡`
The chapter's mirror language is literal/technical enough to support self-model calibration. Do not treat `鏡` as automatic proof that every prior V24 matchup is intended as a mirror.

### 3.11 Genos `勝てない`
Genos's `勝てない` is a bounded statement that he cannot win against the tested/modelled opponent under the present configuration/conditions. It is excellent evidence of capability calibration. It is not a global declaration of helplessness, surrender, or loss of faith in Saitama.

### 3.12 `強一般人`
The extra's ordinary-person language should remain scale-sensitive. It is about strong people within ordinary training/social comparison, not a claim that Darkshine is literally an ordinary civilian.
