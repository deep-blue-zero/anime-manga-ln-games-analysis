# One Punch Man — V06 Japanese Dialogue and Register Audit
## Professional self-correction, elite heteroglossia, boredom ethics, adversarial inference, and indirect care

## 1. Audit disposition

**PASS WITH MODEL REFINEMENT.**

The Japanese line checks support the V06 reading. The highest-value refinements are:

1. Saitama's `趣味で…じゃなくてプロのヒーロー` is an explicit self-correction, supporting layered rather than replaced hero identity.
2. Saitama's language toward Boros distinguishes understanding of boredom from moral permission to victimize others.
3. Saitama can infer enemy priorities from conversational reaction, strengthening selective social/tactical intelligence.
4. Genos's confidence in Saitama is phrased as a near-universal belief and must be stored as **Genos's cognition**, not objective truth.
5. Tatsumaki's V06 register is status-policing and imperious, but her battlefield conduct independently supports protective competence.
6. `鮭` shows Saitama explicitly acknowledging limits of competence/authority while still performing immediate physical rescue and indirect availability.
7. Metal Bat, Child Emperor, Bang, Atomic Samurai, and Iaian have sharply distinct registers; S-class rank does not create a common elite speech style.

No anime wording, translation, webcomic phrasing, or V07+ context is used as authority.

### `興味ねーよ ジェノスやっとけお前`
Locator: `OPM|V06|chapter:30|image:0009`

Voice markers:

- rough/plain `興味ねーよ`;

**Model consequence:** prospective novelty can motivate effort; ordinary doctrinal recruitment rapidly loses salience.

## 7. Sicchi — institutional emergency register

The S-class briefing uses formal, managerial language. Sicchi explicitly distinguishes the period before disclosure from the period after disclosure.

Important semantic ruling:

- before classified information: withdrawal is allowed without loss of S-class registration;
- after disclosure: movement may be restricted to prevent panic/information leakage.

**Audit consequence:** preserve the real opt-out. “The Association simply imprisons the S-class into compliance” is too broad for the Japanese scene.

## 12. Saitama — adversarial conversational inference

Geryuganshoop orders Saitama to withdraw and, when Saitama claims to be lost, provides directions. Saitama deliberately chooses the opposite route:

### `じゃあ左に進もうっと`
Locator: `chapter:33|image:0147`

Gery's panic validates that the wrong-way choice is strategically useful.

**Model consequence:** Saitama can use another speaker's emotional/strategic reaction as navigation evidence. This is strong counterevidence to a global “cannot read people” model.

## 13. Bang / Atomic / Iaian / Metal Bat — distinct crisis voices

Iaian proposes withdrawal/reconsideration because ordinary physical damage appears ineffective. His register is cautionary and information-forward.

Atomic Samurai's response centers trust in sword capability and master identity. Metal Bat's response is violent persistence. Bang continues with calm elderly phrasing and then rapidly updates when the sphere weakness is revealed.

**Audit consequence:** S-class rank does not produce a common command register. Future dialogue generation should preserve individual epistemic style:

- Iaian: deferential but evidence-responsive warning;
- Atomic: confident sword-centered pride;
- Metal Bat: rough action-first persistence;
- Bang: relaxed elder speech plus rapid interpretation.

## 17. Saitama — explicit acknowledgment of nonphysical limits in `鮭`

### `確かに俺がオッサンを止める事は出来ないな`
### `リストラも家庭問題も解決できねぇし`
### `そもそも飛び降りるのはオッサンの自由だ`
Locator: `OPM|V06|extra:鮭|image:0197`

This is one of the strongest noncombat language atoms in the corpus.

Saitama does not claim counseling authority. `解決できねぇ` explicitly names limits. `自由だ` gives the other person agency rather than treating “hero” as ownership over him.

**Do not flatten into:** “Saitama is indifferent to suicide.”

The immediate fall/rescue sequence supplies contrary behavioral evidence.

## 18. Saitama — rescue without sermon

When the man slips/falls, Saitama catches him. Afterward he says:

### `オッサンのスキにすればいいけどさ`
### `また飛び降りたくなったら俺がメシ食ってるビルからにしろよ`
Locator: `extra:鮭|image:0205`

The phrase is intentionally casual. Saitama preserves `スキにすればいい` while inserting himself as a future practical safety condition.

**Model consequence:** care can be expressed as indirect availability and logistics rather than reassurance or moralizing.

The correct V06 conditional rule is:

`another person's complex life problem -> acknowledge limits / avoid false authority`; `immediate physical danger -> act`; `post-crisis -> offer low-ceremony availability`.

## 19. Omake — Genos's continual acquisition orientation

In the short psychic-power omake, Genos reads about supernatural power because he considers whether obtaining it could help in battle. Saitama points out that many examples—bending metal, causing ignition, throwing objects—already have technological equivalents in Genos's body.

Genos concludes the examples were poor.

**Model consequence:** even outside active crisis, Genos continues scanning for additional means of power acquisition. His self-improvement orientation remains active in mundane downtime.
