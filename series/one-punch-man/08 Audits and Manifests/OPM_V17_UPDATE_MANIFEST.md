## Principal V16 -> V17 transitions

### Garou — STRENGTHEN / readiness promotion
V17 supplies the missing causal and institutional breadth for `moderate -> strong`: childhood role assignment and popularity/majority grievance, explicit self-admitted theoretical incompleteness, direct Bang conflict, Monster Association treatment/recruitment, executive offer, behavioral monsterhood test, and resistance to organizational absorption. Promotion is longitudinal across V09–V17, not based on one flashback.

### Monsterhood — REVISE / DECOMPOSE
Garou's self-label, human body, hero-side threat classification, and Monster Association recognition are explicitly non-equivalent. The Association's `本物の怪人` test is behavioral: kill a hero without hesitation. Garou's response is OPEN at V17.

### King / Saitama — STRENGTHEN
King intentionally weaponizes public myth and Elder Centipede's Blast grievance to create an approach vector; Saitama supplies decisive force. False reputation remains false as physical description while becoming true causal leverage.

### Blast / Hero Association — STRENGTHEN
No.1 rank does not imply ordinary commandability. The Association explicitly describes Blast as acting by his own `自由意志` and not being deployable at its convenience.

### Metal Knight / Child Emperor — STRENGTHEN / OPEN
Bofoi explicitly warns of other evils, advises distrust even of nearby humans, and recommends enough independent power to enact justice alone. This is a speaker worldview, not narrator proof of all suspected actors.

### `組織` / Machine God G5 — STRENGTHEN hidden-actor evidence
G5 explicitly says it was dispatched by an external `組織` to assist the Monster Association. This establishes an organizational node but does not prove identity with every prior robot, battle suit, laboratory, or Zombieman hypothesis.

### Sonic — STRENGTHEN failure boundary
The `自信` extra preserves his instrumental desire for monster power but undercuts his sensed “new power” with `ソニックは2キロ痩せた`. Successful biological conversion remains unestablished.

### V1 chronology — REVISE
The historical grouped `OPM_V16-V18_DEEP_READING.md` places Elder Centipede and the King/Saitama solution under its nominal “Volume 16” subsection. The Japanese tankobon establishes those events inside V17. Preserve thematic V1 provenance; do not use its per-volume chronology as current authority.
