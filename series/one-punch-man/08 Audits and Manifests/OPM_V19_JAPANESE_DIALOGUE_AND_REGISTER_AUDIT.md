## 37. V19 Japanese-register closure

The load-bearing V19 character voices remain internally coherent:

- **Garou:** rough first-person register, anti-authority aggression, dismissive category language, and increasingly self-theorizing terms such as `怪人`, `竜`, `気合い`, `技`, and `恐怖`; protective action is usually linguistically disguised rather than sentimentally confessed.
- **Tareo:** direct child register and repeated requests make him an unusually effective pressure source against Garou's self-description.
- **Tatsumaki:** compressed imperatives and certainty (`ダメ`, `返事は?`) turn affection into command.
- **Child Emperor:** analytic/operational speech treats relation, information, and personnel as variables under uncertainty.
- **Gyoro Gyoro:** explanatory/scientific register slides easily into ownership and coercion; subjects are materials, failures, cards, and vulnerabilities.
- **Saitama:** plain low-temperature speech continues to flatten institutional and monster rhetoric; in `現実パンチ`, that plainness becomes explicit metacommentary on the kind of dramatic experience he cannot obtain.

**Final audit disposition: PASS.** No load-bearing V19 claim requires V20+, V1 analysis, translation substitution, or unmarked narrator attribution.
