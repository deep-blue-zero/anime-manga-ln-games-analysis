# One Punch Man - V23 Japanese Dialogue and Register Audit

## Audit result

**PASS.** The load-bearing Japanese-language claims in `OPM_V23_DEEP_READING.md` are supported by the locked Japanese tankobon. No correction requires reopening the prospective interpretation. The audit does, however, impose several attribution controls designed to stop character statements from becoming narrator-certified ontology.

## 1. Audit method

The audit re-opened the high-load pages only after the full 235-image prospective sweep was complete. It checked:

- exact speaker attribution;
- whether a proposition is dialogue, narration, caption, or visual inference;
- register and lexical force where translation could alter characterization;
- whether a speaker's theory was accidentally promoted into fact;
- whether a later-volume outcome had leaked backward;
- whether chapter/extra boundaries matched the tankobon contents page.

No V24+ source or V1 grouped reading was consulted.

## 3. Load-bearing lexical controls

### 3.1 Chapter 108 title: genuine/counterfeit discrimination

`真贋` explicitly denotes discrimination between genuine and false/counterfeit. The title therefore supports using genuine-versus-copy as the local conceptual frame for Atomic Samurai versus G5. It does **not** independently prove that all machine copying is false or worthless.

### 3.2 G5: combat-data copy is explicit

At image 0055 G5 says it has completely copied Atomic Samurai's sword trajectory and obtained Atomic-specific battle data. The language supports **copy/acquisition**. It does not say G5 has acquired Atomic's entire expertise, history, judgment, or identity.

Atomic's image-0056 reply calls the copy an advanced "toy" and jokes about ordering one online for disciple training. Tone is dismissive and amused, not threatened.

### 3.3 Mercenary justice line is speaker philosophy

At image 0068 a veteran says justice/ideals cannot really be discussed without knowing human filth and real hell. The line is rhetorically strong, but it remains one mercenary's experience-based philosophy. The surrounding exchange also grants value to heroes' willingness to place themselves before monsters. Do not convert the mercenary line into the volume's narrator thesis.

### 3.4 Saitama's noise complaint is deliberately colloquial

At image 0087 Saitama uses sound-symbolic `dokkan dokkan` language and a rough/plain complaint frame: the repeated booming was noisy/annoying, so he came to complain. This supports low ceremony and mundane subjective framing. It is not a formal claim that he understood the Monster Association operation before entering.

### 3.5 Child Emperor on role allocation

At image 0093 Child Emperor says that another child's rescue is now required, and that his technical information/analysis gives him a role he can still perform even after exhausting secret weapons. He refuses to thin surface defense and closes on each side fulfilling its own role. The language supports **distributed responsibility**, not martyrdom or a claim that only Child Emperor has moral standing to rescue Tareo.

### 3.6 Waganma's disclosure motive

At image 0094 Waganma says he had been in the same cell as Tareo, knew a monster took him, and when asked about other hostages thought he might be scolded; he was scared and could not say it. The causal wording supports fear/shame avoidance as the reason for withholding information.

At image 0095 Child Emperor answers with thanks for telling him, not with rebuke. `Sorry` and `thank you` belong to different speakers; do not merge them into a joint apology formula.

### 3.7 Black Sperm mechanism

At image 0103 Black Sperm enumerates cutting, tearing, striking, crushing, and twisting as actions after which he continues to increase. This directly supports a proliferation mechanism that punishes ordinary damage assumptions. It does not yet establish an exact conserved cell count or upper limit.

### 3.8 Homeless Emperor: reported God encounter

The most important attribution control in V23 concerns `神`.

At image 0144, inside Homeless Emperor's narrated memory, the entity says in substance:

- Homeless Emperor is right;
- humans are foolish;
- Homeless Emperor does not need to die;
- the entity will grant him power;
- he should know what to do with that power.

The final sentence is directive but underdetermined. It does **not** explicitly contain a command to exterminate humanity.

At image 0145 Homeless Emperor calls the power `神`-given supernatural/divine power and says he knows why he was chosen: in his interpretation, foolish humanity is harmful to the planet and the time for destruction has come.

Zombieman then asks why Homeless Emperor believed that "thing" was `神`. The demonstrative distancing matters. The scene itself preserves ontological skepticism.

At image 0146 Homeless Emperor says Zombieman does not need to believe in God and cuts off further argument. This is confidence in his own revelation, not independent verification.

### 3.9 Homeless Emperor ecological vocabulary

Images 0138-0141 move from social humiliation to planetary scale and then to condemnation of humanity. The ecological vocabulary cannot be detached from status grievance. He explicitly cannot tolerate being treated as inferior by beings he has newly judged foolish and harmful.

His suicide decision precedes the reported entity encounter. The encounter redirects self-destruction into power-backed outward judgment.

### 3.10 PPP comparative self-assessment

In the early part of chapter 110 PPP describes himself, relative to stronger S-Class heroes, as effectively close to an ordinary adult man. This is comparative capability calibration, not a stable modest-personality trait. His grandiose naming and relational self-framing continue immediately around it.

### 3.11 Nyan on PPP's adaptation

At image 0181 Nyan interprets PPP's dense body-hair change as accumulated damage awakening a defensive instinct and rapidly evolving the body. Preserve `Nyan inference` as the authority tag. Do not silently merge it with Gyoro Gyoro's experimental growth doctrine, monster-cell conversion, or limiter removal.

### 3.12 Body versus personhood in PPP/Nyan dialogue

Nyan says that once someone has become a monster, they cannot return to being human. Treat this as Nyan's proposition at this boundary.

PPP replies at image 0180 that if the heart is human, that is already a proper human being. This is an ethical/personhood definition voiced by PPP. It does not prove the prisoners' mental state, especially because the same prisoners repeatedly reject his claim that they are his romantic partners.

### 3.13 Nyan autonomy vocabulary

At image 0184 Nyan says PPP's forced affection resembles the former owner who imposed affection on him. At image 0188 he says he has decided to live as he pleases and chooses his own opponent. This supports an autonomy/anti-imposition character rule with high confidence.

### 3.14 Darkshine is not afraid in V23

Images 0209-0210 establish that Darkshine remained still because he wanted to experience Bug God's supposed insect strength. His language frames insects as an ideal of strength he had pursued and expresses disappointment after direct contact.

At image 0220 he says he does not know any method by which his own body could be wounded. The line is an explicit subjective invulnerability model. V23 provides no Japanese-language evidence of fear collapse, self-doubt, or trauma in this chapter.

Any V1 claim importing Darkshine's later psychological failure into V23 must therefore be revised chronologically.

### 3.15 King in Darkshine's speech is reputation evidence

Darkshine lists King among heroes especially dangerous when angered and repeats the public King Engine myth. This is evidence that **Darkshine believes/participates in King's reputation**, not evidence that King's physical power equals the description.

### 3.16 Extra title and Amai's exemplar doctrine

The extra title `範` carries the sense of model/example/norm. Amai's image-0223 speech says heroes should give others a human model/example and that analyzing human character is central to his evaluation practice.

This supports an exemplar doctrine broader than mere beauty, while the surrounding pages show that his implementation remains prestige-, image-, and ranking-oriented.

### 3.17 Pig God's quiet payment

At image 0225 Pig God gives money to the ramen shop and asks that his payment be kept secret so the other challenger will not worry if he finishes. The wording supports deliberate uncredited material consideration for a stranger. It is excellent low-stakes evidence precisely because Pig God does not turn it into public hero performance.

## 4. Visual-language controls

- Homeless Emperor's entity is visually unstable/sketch-like. Do not describe the form as a precise anatomical revelation.
- Darkshine's glossy body remains compositionally triumphant through the chapter endpoint.
- PPP's expanded body-hair defense and Nyan's crack-slipping movement create an intentional contrast between expansion/contact and evasion/autonomy.
- Pig God/Gums visual rhyme supports comparison of appetite/body grammar, not moral equivalence.
- The extra places Amai's scoring sheet before Pig God's uncredited act; the timing supports irony about legible versus unobserved exemplarity.

## 5. Prohibited overclaims after audit

Do not state as V23 fact that:

- G5 fully mastered Atomic Samurai's swordsmanship;
- justice requires mercenary experience;
- Waganma directly apologizes face-to-face to Tareo;
- Black Sperm has an already-known exact multiplication ceiling;
- the entity in Homeless Emperor's memory is ontologically proven to be God;
- God explicitly ordered the extermination of humanity;
- PPP's bodily adaptation is the same mechanism as limiter removal or Gyoro Gyoro's engineered growth;
- monsterization is universally irreversible because Nyan says so;
- PPP's prisoners consent to his romantic/sexual framing;
- Pig God defeats Gums in V23;
- Darkshine has already suffered a confidence collapse;
- King is physically strongest because Darkshine repeats the public myth;
- Amai's model of exemplary heroism exhausts what the extra endorses.
