# One Punch Man — V07 Japanese Dialogue and Register Audit
## Challenge activation, compassionate softening, justice rhetoric, self-authored service, and cross-institution legitimacy

## 1. Audit disposition

**PASS WITH MODEL REFINEMENT.**

The Japanese line checks support the V07 deep reading. The highest-value rulings are:

1. Saitama's Boros dialogue directly confirms increased engagement under credible resistance without establishing equality.
2. His brief agreement that the fight was equal is knowingly nonliteral/softening rather than a factual power judgment.
3. Amai Mask explicitly says he preserves A-class rank 1 in order to block unworthy S-class advancement; this proposition becomes canonical only at V07.
4. Genos explicitly compares Amai's lack of emotional room and urgent anti-evil posture with his own pre-Saitama state; his guess about Amai's past remains speculation.
5. Saitama's `「お願い」されて動くもんじゃない / ヒーローも ケーサツも` places normative authorization inside the service role rather than in external praise/request.
6. Saitama's register toward Tatsumaki remains status-insensitive, confirming the checkpoint prediction.
7. Metal Bat's rough voice coexists with direct tenderness/pride toward his sister; Amai's polished public persona coexists with ordinary manager/scheduling obligations.
8. Drive Knight's warning is a speaker-attributed claim, not objective fact about Metal Knight.

No V08+, anime, webcomic, translation, or later redraw wording is used as authority.

## 2. Boros — Saitama's engagement threshold

Saitama's `もう終わりなのか?` / `戦いは?` is unusually explicit. The compressed, plain register matters because he is not performing excitement for an audience; he is checking whether the encounter will continue.

**Audit ruling:** Checkpoint S-A-02 receives `CONFIRM`. The correct model is not “danger excites Saitama” in general. The linguistically supported trigger is continued **combat reciprocity**.

## 3. Boros's final evaluation and Saitama's soft agreement

Saitama tells Boros:

> `やっぱ強ぇーよ お前`

The contracted `強ぇー` remains Saitama's ordinary rough/plain register. It is real recognition, not ceremonial praise.

Boros describes the fight as equal. Saitama answers `ああ / そーだな`. Boros immediately says `嘘だな` and explains that Saitama retained余裕.

**Audit ruling:** Saitama's agreement should not be used as an objective equality statement. The conversational structure marks it as a softened/nonliteral concession. Because there is no strategic audience and Boros is dying, the most defensible interpretation is low-ceremony consolation/recognition rather than public-narrative manipulation.

**Model refinement:** Saitama can prefer a kinder or less humiliating conversational surface when strict corrective truth has little practical value.

## 4. Amai Mask — explicit S-class gatekeeping

Amai says:

> `僕がなぜS級に上がらないかわかるか?`
>
> `キミみたいな弱くて何の取り柄もない雑魚をS級にさせないためにA級1位を守っているのさ!`

Markers:
- polished `僕` / `キミ` self-presentation;
- contempt expressed without Genos-style rough contractions;
- deliberate institutional verb `守っている` applied to A1 position.

**Audit ruling:** V07 establishes intentional **S-class entry gatekeeping through A1 occupancy**. It does not retroactively make the broader V05 claim available earlier.

## 5. Amai Mask — `悪` / `正義` compression

After killing surviving invaders, Amai frames them as evil and the killing as rapid execution of justice.

The important semantic feature is categorical compression: `悪` identification and `正義` action are presented with little deliberative space between them.

**Model consequence:** at V07, Amai's justice register is outcome/eradication-centered. Do not yet infer how he handles morally ambiguous persons or his own failure.

## 6. Genos — developmental self-comparison

Genos observes Amai and thinks:

- Amai resembles Genos before he met Saitama;
- he lacks room in his heart / is in a hurry to eradicate evil.

This is not merely Amai analysis. It is Genos describing **his own earlier cognition from a later vantage point**.

His subsequent theory that Amai probably suffered some past event is linguistically/speculatively marked reasoning. It belongs to Genos's inference model, not Amai biography.

## 7. Genos — worry is not epistemic collapse

When the alien ship falls, Genos becomes concerned because Saitama was inside, while still thinking Saitama is probably safe.

**Checkpoint ruling:** G-A-05 is `NON-DIAGNOSTIC / boundary probe`, not contradiction. Attachment-driven concern can coexist with strong confidence.

When Saitama returns, Genos immediately asks whether he is safe and reacts to the visible blood. Saitama answers that it is返り血 and that he is fine.

**Relationship consequence:** visual bodily evidence can trigger checking even when Genos's higher-level belief remains stable.

## 8. Saitama — Boros as `過去一番` without equality

Saitama tells Genos that Boros was genuinely strong, perhaps the strongest so far. This is a relative ranking judgment. It must be read together with Boros's final recognition that Saitama retained余裕.

**Audit ruling:** preserve both propositions:
- Boros = strongest relative opponent so far;
- Boros ≠ demonstrated equal.

## 9. Tatsumaki / Saitama / Genos / Bang — rank does not produce one register

Tatsumaki invokes Saitama's B-class status while rebuking his independent action. Saitama does not shift upward into deferential language.

Genos's response is much rougher (`糞餓鬼`, threat language), showing that his respectful register is strongly relationship-conditioned rather than globally polite.

Bang intervenes with elder-regulator language, asking whether Tatsumaki's conduct fits an S-class rank-2 hero.

**Checkpoint ruling:** S-A-06 `CONFIRM` — status alone does not produce Saitama deference.

## 10. Drive Knight — warning attribution

Drive Knight:

> `メタルナイトは お前の敵だ`
>
> `気を付けろ`

and later tells Genos to avoid Metal Knight for now.

The syntax is declarative, but evidentiary authority remains speaker-bound.

**Audit rule:** record `Drive Knight claims/warns X`; do **not** rewrite as `Metal Knight is Genos's enemy`.

This distinction is especially important because V07 also supplies large-scale constructive Metal Knight evidence.

## 11. Tanktop Master — defeat acknowledgment and adaptation

Tanktop Master openly recognizes Tatsumaki's psychic output as overwhelming and later builds training goals around psychic and mechanical power.

The key character pattern is not embarrassment avoidance. It is:

`capability comparison -> explicit loss recognition -> training target formation`.

His rough masculine/tanktop rhetoric can therefore coexist with evidence-responsive self-assessment.

## 12. Metal Knight — construction claim

The extra ends with:

> `メタルナイトは7日で竣工させた`

The line is direct narration. It supports the seven-day completion fact, not any specific hidden motive.

Earlier planning dialogue presents a roughly ten-year conventional horizon. The contrast warrants “extreme infrastructure time compression” without importing later technological explanations.

## 13. Charanko — ordinary motive in `一番弟子の回想`

Charanko's retrospective motive is socially ordinary: he joined because he wanted popularity. He reports not feeling stronger, skips practice, and considers quitting. After discovering the other students have left, he reinterprets being the final student as effectively becoming the top disciple and regains motivation.

**Model consequence:** Charanko persistence is status-sensitive and imperfect. Do not write him as a pure martial ascetic merely because he remains Bang's student.

Bang's understated reaction preserves the asymmetry: master recognizes the motivational silliness without needing to humiliate him.

## 14. `カツ丼` — Saitama on service authorization

Key line:

> `「お願い」されて動くもんじゃない`
>
> `ヒーローも ケーサツも な?`

The construction rejects `お願い` as the causal/normative basis for the work. Saitama places heroes and police in parallel.

**Canonical interpretation:** protective service should not require external supplication before action. This strengthens self-authored heroism and extends it as a judgment about another public-service profession.

It does **not** mean requests are irrelevant operationally; the scene itself includes police calling for support. The point concerns normative motive/authorization, not communication logistics.

## 15. `カツ丼` — credit sacrifice

Saitama defeats the monster before the publicity-oriented Hero Association response arrives and leaves the scene. Media interprets the visible aftermath as a police victory.

There is no line where Saitama announces “I am giving the police my credit.” The inference is behavioral and contextual:
- he has already heard the police/Association legitimacy dispute;
- he acts independently;
- he does not remain for media/credit;
- police receive the public result;
- the chief later identifies him as the actual hero.

**Audit ruling:** Checkpoint S-A-03 `CONFIRM`, high confidence. The absence of a self-congratulatory explanation is consistent with the low-ceremony model but is not itself proof of motive.

## 16. Police chief — legitimacy without glamor

The chief's speech frames police work around ordinary public order rather than hero branding. Saitama can recognize this legitimacy despite the chief's hostility to the Hero Association.

**Model consequence for Saitama:** institutional respect can be function/evidence-based rather than in-group loyalty or rank-based.

Do not create a durable police-chief character model from one extra; route the material primarily to heroism/institution ledgers.

## 17. McCoy — publicity opportunism, not Association essence

McCoy forces the rival police institution through humiliating request language and arranges incentives/media so Hero Association superiority can be displayed.

**Boundary ruling:** attribute this strategy to McCoy and the operation room context. It is evidence of an incentive/pathology available inside the Association, not proof that every official or the entire institution shares his motive.

## 18. Metal Bat — family register

The final omake directly shows Metal Bat emotionally reacting to his younger sister's piano-recital result and regretting missing the event because of hero work. He then asks Amai for an autograph for her.

His rough everyday speech does not become sentimental literary language when family salience rises. The affect is carried by reaction, urgency, and practical action.

**Readiness consequence:** repeated family-conditioned evidence plus combat/professional contexts now supports `moderate`.

## 19. Amai Mask — professional scheduling channel

Amai has manager-mediated entertainment work and will leave a hero-to-hero confrontation because of the schedule. This supplies ordinary/professional behavioral breadth absent at V05.

His polished voice remains compatible with severe justice language. “Polished” must not be modeled as “gentle.”
