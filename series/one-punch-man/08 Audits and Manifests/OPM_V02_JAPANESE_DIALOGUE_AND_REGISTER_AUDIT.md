# 1. Saitama

## 1.1 Training explanation is sincere, plain, and didactic

Key lines:

- `大切なのは このハードなトレーニングメニューを続けられるかどうかだ` — `image:0070`
- `続ける事だ どんなに辛くてもな` — `image:0071`
- `腕立て伏せ100回 / 上体起こし100回 / スクワット100回 / そしてランニング10km これを毎日やる!!!` — `image:0072`
- `自分で変われるのが人間の強さだ!` — `image:0075`
- `んな事言われても 他に何もねーぞ` — `image:0078`

### Register finding

Saitama can sustain an explanatory/mentor stance without shifting into elevated language. He uses direct imperatives and plain masculine forms rather than a formal master persona. The diction supports the interpretation that he is sincerely reporting what he believes, not performing mystique.

`んな事言われても 他に何もねーぞ` is especially important: the contraction and sentence ending preserve his ordinary voice even while Genos demands a deeper ontology. There is no linguistic signal that Saitama is knowingly withholding an esoteric answer.

**Model transition:** STRENGTHEN epistemic sincerity; PRESERVE colloquial baseline.

## 1.2 Ordinary-life urgency remains linguistically stronger than combat urgency

- `今日がスーパーの特売日じゃねーか` — `image:0088`

The rough contraction `じゃねーか` arrives with conspicuous emotional activation. The line supports V01's finding that Saitama's strongest register shifts are not reliably correlated with objective physical threat.

**Model transition:** STRENGTHEN low-stakes salience.

## 1.3 Reputation concern is explicit, not inferred

- `これじゃ俺とかぶってんじゃねーか` — `image:0106`
- `せっかくヒーローやってても こんな真似されたら俺も悪者扱いされるじゃないか` — `image:0106`
- `俺は趣味でヒーローをやってる者だが 一身上の都合によりお前らをぶっ潰すわ` — `image:0149`
- `俺だよ俺!! 趣味でヒーローやってて割と活躍してる…` — `image:0164`
- `知名度が低い` — `image:0181`
- `俺の存在をもっと誰もが知ってていいんじゃないか?` — `image:0182`
- `もっとファンとかいても不自然じゃないだろ…!` — `image:0182`
- `昨日なんて言われたと思う? 「お前など知らん」だってよ` — `image:0183`

### Register finding

The language makes recognition a genuine conscious concern by the end of V02. It is not necessary to infer this from facial expression alone. At the same time, his wording emphasizes being known, correctly classified, and having actual activity acknowledged. He does not frame the problem through money, elite rank, honorific title, or social domination.

`割と活躍してる` is characteristically understated: even while defending his record, Saitama does not adopt grand hero rhetoric.

**Model transition:** REVISE V01 recognition state from OPEN to established-but-qualified.

## 1.4 Mercy and empathy are economical

Hammerhead:

- `待て! 俺はただ働きたくなかっただけで もう悪さやめるよ` — `image:0158`
- Saitama: `行け` — `image:0158`
- Saitama internal reflection: `社会への失望か? 俺も一歩間違えばアイツみたいになってたのかな` — `image:0159`

### Register finding

Saitama does not sermonize. The outward mercy is a one-word imperative, while the moral nuance appears in internal reflection. This split matters for reconstruction: outward brevity should not be mistaken for absence of ethical cognition.

**Model transition:** STRENGTHEN internal-external asymmetry.

## 1.5 Sonic interaction shows perceptiveness, attempted restraint, and nonreciprocal rivalry

- `嘘つけ お前はただ自分の技を試したいだけだろ? 無邪気な笑顔みりゃわかるぜ` — `image:0167`
- `あ! すまん` / `いや わざとじゃないんだよ 寸止めしようと思ったら勢いでぶつかっただけで` — `image:0176`
- after Sonic's death vow: `ああ! 頑張れよ!` — `image:0179`

### Register finding

Saitama's `嘘つけ` strips Sonic's professional/pride justification down to a simpler desire to test technique. He is capable of motive-reading when behavioral evidence is obvious.

The apology and `寸止め` explanation are direct evidence that he intended restraint. His final `頑張れよ` treats Sonic's theatrical rivalry with casual encouragement rather than mirrored hostility.

**Model transition:** STRENGTHEN perceptiveness, restraint, and rivalry asymmetry.

## 1.6 Hero Association decision

- `知らなかった` — `image:0185`
- `登録しようぜ! 一緒に登録してくれたら弟子にしてやるから!` — `image:0186`

### Register finding

Saitama reacts to institutional ignorance without face-saving. He then uses `〜しようぜ` and `〜てやる` in a colloquial, lightly assertive bargain. The teacher role is now something he can actively invoke rather than only resist.

**Model transition:** STRENGTHEN social adaptation / teacher-role ownership.

# 2. Genos

## 2.1 Deference does not suppress disagreement

- `トレーニング? 改造手術でも遺伝子操作でも秘薬でもなくトレーニング?` — `image:0071`
- `サイタマ先生の強さは明らかに身体を鍛えた程度のものではない! 俺はそれが知りたいのです` — `image:0078`

### Register finding

Genos retains `先生` and the polite explanatory ending `〜のです` while directly rejecting the adequacy of Saitama's explanation. This is strong evidence that respect and epistemic independence coexist.

**Model transition:** REVISE any simplistic "obedient/worshipful" reading; STRENGTHEN evidence-responsive skepticism.

## 2.2 Protective lethality is delivered as practical assistance

Discussing Sonic:

- `まぁ先生がお困りなら俺が消しますが` — `image:0180`

### Register finding

The conditional subordinate framing is polite toward Saitama, while `消します` normalizes killing/removal as a practical service. Politeness is therefore not softness; it coexists with extreme instrumental violence.

**Model transition:** STRENGTHEN calm lethal problem-solving.

## 2.3 Institutional exposition preserves high information density

Genos explains the Hero Association / `ヒーロー名簿` and recognizes the likely source of Saitama's public invisibility (`images:0183-0185`). His role as information broker fits the V01 pattern of dense causal explanation, but here the information is useful to Saitama rather than conversationally excessive autobiography.

**Model transition:** STRENGTHEN high-information baseline; add context-sensitive usefulness.

## 2.4 Joint registration

- Saitama offers apprenticeship conditional on registering together (`image:0186`).
- Genos: `いきましょう!` — `image:0187`

The response is immediate and affirmative. The disciple role remains strongly motivating.

# 3. Hammerhead

## 3.1 Collective ideology

Representative speech:

- `なぜ働かなければいけないのか` — `image:0091`
- `みな労働に縛られているではないか` — `image:0093`
- `我々は断固働きたくない` — `image:0094`
- `働きたい奴だけ働いて他は養ってもらえる社会に!!!` — `image:0095`

Hammerhead uses collective `我々` and declarative movement rhetoric to universalize his grievance.

## 3.2 Private motive after defeat

- `俺はただ働きたくなかっただけで もう悪さやめるよ` — `image:0158`
- later: `母ちゃん 俺…働くよ` — `image:0170`

### Register finding

The movement's `我々` collapses into `俺` once borrowed power and followers are gone. This linguistic narrowing supports the satirical reading that grand ideology partly masks a personal aversion to labor. The final address to `母ちゃん` further domesticates the would-be revolutionary into an ordinary social relation.

**Model transition:** NEW emerging model; high confidence in public-rhetoric/private-motive split.

# 5. Mumen Rider

- `正義の自転車乗り 無免ライダー参上!` — `image:0100`

The line is a compact public role declaration built around `正義`. It contrasts Saitama's private, deflationary `趣味でヒーロー` formula. At this boundary Mumen Rider's language is too narrow for a full voice model, but it is strong evidence that he consciously performs a recognizable justice-hero identity.

**Readiness effect:** no promotion beyond insufficient; one high-stakes slogan is not language breadth.

# 6. Saitama recognition correction from `じぶんみがき`

Training-year Saitama reflects:

- he is not doing hero activity *in order to be thanked*;
- `人の役に立ったと分かりやすく実感できるのは 感謝された時だけだ` — `image:0189`;
- the happiness/stimulation of gratitude gives him fulfillment and becomes energy for the next fight.

### Audit consequence

This is the strongest qualification on the V02 recognition model. Recognition should not be represented as a newly invented adult vanity. Social feedback already mattered during the training period because it made usefulness perceptible.

**REVISE current model:** recognition = social legibility / attribution / evidence of consequence, with celebrity prestige still unproven as the dominant end.
