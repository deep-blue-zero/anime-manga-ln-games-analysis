## Corrections resolved before retrospective reopening

1. **Modality in Fubuki's thought, 0119.** The rescue clause has `救ったかもしれない`; the following contrast asserts that Blast has put a curse on Tatsumaki's heart. The stable draft's broad “wonders whether” softened the whole thought. It now distinguishes a concessive may-have-saved clause from the accusation asserted within Fubuki's perspective. The latter remains a psychological interpretation, not narrator-confirmed supernatural causation or proven intention by Blast.
2. **State of speech, 0155.** Genos's `うわ言` means delirious/incoherent speech here, not necessarily speech while asleep. Bang says the patient is dazed and repeats the whisper in separate question fragments. The draft's sleep-talk shorthand and compound-like tank-top-magic typography are replaced with that actual sequence. `※正気` corrects their judgment of his mental state, without validating a universal magic mechanism.
3. **Earlier sequential correction retained, 0094.** `捨て鉢` describes desperate/reckless self-abandonment. The initial literal pawn-like gloss was discarded during direct review and does not enter the stable reading. The present audit confirms the idiomatic reading from Bang's appeal to a young person not to give up so readily.
4. **Action-causality check retained, 0154.** Genos prepares charged hands. The tank top slips and snaps back; the patient revives before any applied shock is shown. Preparation must not be rewritten as performed defibrillation. This was already correctly distinguished in the stable text.

The volume thesis survives these corrections. The distinctions between someone else's body, that person's agency, a speaker's explanation and the observed rescue remain supported.
