## Principal V03 -> V04 claim transitions

- **REVISE / STRENGTHEN — recognition:** Saitama wants social legibility but explicitly rejects public evaluation as the reason he performs hero work. Desired feedback and motive authority are separate variables.
- **STRENGTHEN — self-authored hero identity:** `俺はヒーローをやっている者だ` and `俺がやりたくてやってんだ` preserve internally authorized hero practice after registration.
- **STRENGTHEN — Saitama restraint/care:** under anger he uses nonlethal force against peer heroes; under catastrophe he entrusts depleted Genos to Bang before acting.
- **NEW / OPEN — Saitama catastrophe cognition:** V04 contrasts Genos's downstream-consequence analysis with Saitama's immediate-threat solution and rapid aftermath compression. Durability remains open.
- **STRENGTHEN — Genos analytical independence:** independent S-class duty, secondary-effect modeling, self-regulation, and cooperation attempts materially broaden the model.
- **STRENGTHEN — Genos self-sacrifice:** willingness to commit deep energy reserves persists outside revenge/Saitama contexts.
- **STRENGTHEN — institutional misrecognition:** the Association can issue elite summons and update ranks but cannot force response, fully attribute contribution, or control public causal narrative.
- **REVISE nuance — hero performance:** Stinger establishes that theatrical branding can coexist with genuine courage.
- **STRENGTHEN — hero status != moral worth:** Puri-Puri Prisoner is simultaneously S-class and a repeat criminal predator; institutional role does not provide moral absolution.
- **STRENGTHEN / broaden — Sonic:** prison evidence shows a cooler non-Saitama baseline, proving rivalry is a relationship-specific intensifier rather than the entire personality.
- **STRENGTHEN — heterogeneous monsterhood:** Sea Folk add a nonhuman collective/sovereign route without settling complete ontology.
