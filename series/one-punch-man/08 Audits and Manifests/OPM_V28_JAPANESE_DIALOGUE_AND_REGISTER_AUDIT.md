## 2. Chapter 140: Amai's concealed crisis and the surface explanation

Images 0078–0081 distinguish Amai's imagined judgments by others from what they actually say. Child Emperor addresses him respectfully and proposes a practical role. Amai experiences the child's concern through shame. His thought about attacking from behind and assigning the wounds to monsters is a contemplated concealment killing; the source does not show him carrying it out.

Iaian's concern at 0082 interrupts this movement. The rescue memory at 0083–0085 carries Blast's reassurance and visible identifying emblem. It is not dated eighteen years earlier: that date belongs to Tatsumaki's separate memory in chapter 142. A shared rescuer does not make the two scenes one event.

At 0086, Amai says the problem is on his side and that he almost forgot the basics. The following fashion language leads to `ガマン` at 0087. The surface explanation is not a confession of the earlier murderous impulse. The draft's reading of restraint and a remembered standard is supported as interpretation, while a completed moral cure or a later-sourced bodily diagnosis would exceed this boundary.

## 3. Chapter 141: self-verdicts, ideology, and a shared appeal

### Garou and Darkshine do not narrate the world's settled truth

Darkshine's forecast about Garou at 0091 includes `かもしれない`. It projects a possible danger; it does not establish that nobody can handle Garou. Garou's own account at 0093–0099 connects bodily change, unusually clear thought and a program of equal terror. His desired disaster level `神` is a threat-category aspiration. It is not an identification with an external entity or proof of a power transfer.

The projected end of bullying, discrimination and war belongs to Garou's theory. Its accompanying social images do not show that this worldwide result has already happened. The Tareo memory at 0100–0101 reintroduces a particular protected child and Garou's monster self-label. It interrupts his abstraction without supplying an explicit renunciation.

Darkshine's self-indictment at 0103–0105 repeats his desire for a pleasant victory and intoxication with his strength. These are valuable statements about his present understanding of himself. They are not permission to declare that every past act was worthless or that he never exerted effort. Amai's subsequent kicks leave him curled and apologizing; bodily force alone has no shown restorative success at that point.

### The encouragement has several owners and mixed methods

At 0109, Prisoner recognizes the still-undamaged muscular armor, admits his own defeat and asks for Darkshine's strength. At 0110–0111 he supplies the self whom Darkshine has protected and trained as someone worth preserving. This validates an existing investment rather than only denouncing failure.

The sharper challenge at 0112 is Amai's intervention. The composition reveals Amai in the foreground and the other two responding behind him. The following `ボクも同じだ` establishes shared struggle, then his account of repeatedly standing again continues across the aircraft/motorcycle pursuit at 0113–0115. This montage is an interpretive connection between language and depicted persistence; it does not rewrite Drive Knight's stated motives.

At 0116, the written `筋肉` and ruby `プライド` make the body and self-respect simultaneously present. Prisoner's whispered deep-kiss threat is coercive sexual pressure despite its comic framing. Darkshine's rise at 0117 is an observed local result of a mixed sequence, not proof of a uniquely sufficient technique or permanent recovery. The later missile attack strengthens the behavioral result but supplies no new Garou confrontation.

Atomic's proposal at 0118 is followed by `今回限りだ` and selective recognition of strong partners at 0119. Child Emperor's surprise supplies a peer's view of a departure from habitual independence. It does not establish that Atomic has never cooperated in his life. Zombieman's `少ーしだけ見直したぜ` at 0122 is explicitly a small revision in his view of Amai. Amai's response calls him unsettling; neither knows the other's complete interior. Child Emperor's renewed admiration at 0123 is immediately followed by bickering, including the `醜態仮面` insult that echoes chapter 140.

## 4. Chapter 142: role distinctions and the precise force of Blast's advice

Bang's explanation at 0127–0128 is a prospective acceptance of responsibility. He infers understandable concern about softness toward Garou and thanks Child Emperor for the chance to settle matters as a teacher. He has not yet subdued Garou. Atomic's earlier understanding that Bang could not be contacted remains a separate communication issue.

Fubuki starts with `お姉ちゃん`, shifts to Tatsumaki's public name, then returns to the intimate address while fearing she is already unconscious. Her `きっと` is an inference. Genos's close observation, `失神している` at 0133, subsequently corroborates the condition. The draft correctly preserves both sources and their order.

The facility is explicitly dated `18年前` at 0134. At 0135 the director dismisses the child's plateauing numbers and prioritizes promising subjects. This is an institutional judgment of usefulness. It is not an objective limit on her capacity. Blast's questioning at 0141–0142 offers deliberate suppression in hope of release as the explanation; its source is his perceptive question and her response, rather than independent laboratory narration.

Blast introduces himself with hero activities and then `趣味`, alongside having ordinary work (0140–0141). The hobby-hero parallel is textually grounded. His occupation, current affiliation and power mechanism are not supplied. The visible cube has no explained function here.

Tatsumaki explicitly says her parents sold her at 0143. Blast answers her feeling of being unwanted by pointing to her sister, then says `家族を守ってあげなさい` at 0144. Only after this rescue and affirmative purpose does the warning appear: `いざという時に誰かが助けてくれると思ってはいけない` (0145).

The prohibition governs **thinking/expecting that someone will rescue her in a crisis**. It does not literally command her to reject assistance offered. Its context includes rescue already given and a relationship she can protect. The current Fubuki call immediately promises collective help; Tatsumaki's `見くびらないで` at 0146 retains pride. Neither a complete rejection of Blast nor a cured childhood wound is stated. How Blast knows about Fubuki remains unexplained.
