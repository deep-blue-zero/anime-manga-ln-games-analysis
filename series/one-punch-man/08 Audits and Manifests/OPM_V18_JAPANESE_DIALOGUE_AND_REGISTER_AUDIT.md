# One Punch Man — Volume 18 Japanese Dialogue and Register Audit

## Audit result

**PASS.** The load-bearing Japanese-language claims in `OPM_V18_DEEP_READING.md` are supportable from the Japanese V18 tankobon at the V17 observational boundary.

This audit is selective rather than exhaustive. It verifies lines where lexical choice, register, address, grammatical framing, or speaker attribution materially affects the analytical model. It does not treat every dialogue balloon as a translation exercise.

The audit was stabilized before historical `OPM_V16-V18_DEEP_READING.md`, V19+, or Checkpoint-A validation material was consulted for V18 adjudication.

## 1. Human monster capitulation rhetoric — PASS

### Locator
`OPM|V18|chapter:88|images:0009-0013|anchor:human monster-worship movement`

### Finding
The human movement uses explicit accommodation/submission rhetoric such as `怪人様に迎合せよ`. It presents coexistence/collaboration with the stronger monster side as a survival strategy.

Garou's response `怪人を舐めすぎだぞテメェら` is contemptuous. He is not endorsing human submission merely because he self-identifies as a monster.

### Analytical implication
**PASS:** V18 distinguishes Garou's monster persona from human pro-monster collaborationism.
