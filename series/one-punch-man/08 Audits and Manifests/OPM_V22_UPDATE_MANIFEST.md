- **STRENGTHEN / REVISE - Child Emperor:** Phoenix Man finds a real recognition deficit but does not secure ideological conversion. Child Emperor explicitly denies any intent to defect; the wound is that sustained work and endurance have not produced the direct recognition Phoenix offers.
- **STRENGTHEN - Zombieman/Child Emperor:** Zombieman directly answers that recognition deficit by apologizing for adult reliance, thanking Child Emperor, and telling him the others are glad he is there. This is distinct from Saitama's separate non-coercive ethical contribution.
- **STRENGTHEN - Saitama:** permits self-directed eccentricity but rejects wrongdoing and coercion; explicitly objects to dragging a child into Phoenix Man's project.
- **REVISE - Child Emperor/tool relation:** the volume's `inside` claim is not anti-technology. Child Emperor continues to use and value machines; the distinction is between a person using borrowed/tools power and being subordinated to an external shell/role.
- **STRENGTHEN - Phoenix Man:** monsterization is offered as recognition, organizational belonging, salvation and recovery from the human condition. These are Phoenix Man's claims, not narrator-certified metaphysics.
- **STRENGTHEN - Zombieman:** Pureblood's lineage-essentialism is defeated operationally by attrition, finite-but-repeatable regeneration, weapons, patience and refusal to stop. Pureblood's approximately 200 local wins do not equal the engagement win.
- **STRENGTHEN - Amai Mask:** purity/justice logic expands into apparent willingness to kill mind-controlled human rescuers; a strike reveals a nonhuman-looking facial anomaly immediately before his public celebrity face becomes socially legible again. Final biological ontology remains **OPEN**.
- **OPEN - institutional allegations:** Phoenix Man's claims about Hero Association upper-management wrongdoing are not independently verified in V22.
- **OPEN - internal spy / additional captive:** Child Emperor's suspicion and Waganma's hint are preserved without importing later resolution.
