## Targeted findings

- **0010–0036 — recruitment is also control:** Association executives speak of exceptional S-Class heroes as hard to control and discuss replacement capacity as defections accelerate. Axel judges the Association's protection model rather than accepting its institutional judgment; Raiden discloses that he is already committed to the Neo Heroes. The pages establish recruitment success and comparative promises, not verified Neo Heroes field performance. Tatsumaki's destructive commercial is usable publicity and evidence of the very control problem the executives fear.
- **0038–0064 — capacity and readiness remain distinct:** Tanktop Master's restored body and increased strength are explicit, while returning and new tank-toppers speak through reciprocity, admiration and group commitment rather than certified matchup readiness. Child Emperor is collecting evidence and adversarial testimony. Darkshine can again perform extraordinary physical work, but the prospect of battle triggers fear and he says he cannot face forward. The Forest Tribe sequence credits low-ranked coordination without turning it into proof that rank is meaningless.
- **0066–0098 — Genos wants evaluative attention, not only hardware:** `雷光核` names a real upgrade, and Genos treats his firepower as potentially applicable to Dragon-level opposition; neither phrase supplies an independent ceiling. Saitama notices changes but cannot give the meaningful appraisal Genos wants. Genos then presents the Neo Heroes as a place where both might be valued, asks Saitama to transfer with him and abandons the move when Saitama dismisses it as troublesome. The sequence directly supports chosen proximity and a feedback failure; it does not show Saitama endorsing the Association or explicitly recognizing Genos's sacrifice.
- **0100–0140 — falsified metrics versus conduct:** the broadcast identifies `C級333位ヒーロー “パピヨン800”`, while the man is actually `B級4位ヒーロー “ワイルドヤンキー”`. The monster is presented as disaster level `鬼`; ranks, match conditions and odds are manipulated for gambling patrons. The hero is coerced through his mother's hospitalization and financial need. Saitama's intervention produces a real rescue outside the script, and Child Emperor identifies the fixed match and underground betting evidence. Mumen Rider does not validate the false rank: he calls the hero strong because he did not flee from someone stronger. Saitama later notices that profit has begun to replace rescue as his motive and returns the offered reward.
- **0142–0170 — investigation, kinship and multiple scales:** Amai Mask's pre-debut history is unknown; investigators discuss facial change, absent surgery records, possible special ability or monsterization and troubling combat conduct. Their language remains hypothesis and competing judgment, not a certified monster verdict; perceived sincerity is also evidence, not exoneration. Image 0149 identifies Amahare's daughter `ユタ`; 0150 identifies Zanbai's son `シドウ`. Nichirin explains circulating ki through a weapon until it becomes part of oneself and applies the same principle to blade and prosthetic body. His apple is genuinely cut, but the opening is too fine to separate and the fruit has not yet “noticed.” Nichirin describes Atomic as broadened by exposure to many strong people and to multiple measures of strength. That insight is real even though Atomic immediately misapplies it to King.
- **0172–0200 — the expert model makes the test unfalsifiable:** Atomic asks whether King is truly strong and says he must see strength with his own eyes. King's heartbeat, choking, sweat, tremor, evasions and inability to draw are literal bodily facts; arcane threat, feints and murderous intent are observer inferences. Atomic reduces the request to cutting one apple and says another person's cut reveals the life they have led. King strains for more than two minutes, never unsheathes the sword, places it down and leaves. Atomic then imports Nichirin's valid delayed-cut model into the non-event, imagining an imperceptible cut so perfect that neither apple nor sword noticed. The disciples share the self-confirming inference. The false premise nevertheless produces a real consequence: Atomic lowers his self-assessment, returns to basic training and receives the disciples' consent to follow.
