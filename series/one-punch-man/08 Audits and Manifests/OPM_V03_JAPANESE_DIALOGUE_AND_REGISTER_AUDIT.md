### Saitama register conclusion


V03 confirms a **context-switching colloquial system**, not a monotone “deadpan” voice.


- default: short, familiar, low-formality clauses;
- institutional situations: capable of local polite morphology without adopting institutional reverence;
- irritation/urgency: rough contractions and blunt exclamation;
- mentoring: can produce superficially didactic speech, but private monologue may reveal uncertainty or improvisation;
- meaningful interpersonal advice: becomes plain and direct rather than rhetorically heroic;
- low-stakes domestic/consumer contexts remain among his most naturally expressive registers.

# 8. Translation / interpretation cautions


1. `ヒーロー` must be context-labeled as self-authored identity, professional registry category, public symbol, or generic aspiration. V03 makes these meanings diverge.
2. `先生` does not prove Genos believes every explanation Saitama gives. V02-V03 direct evidence shows otherwise.
3. `限界` in `吹き込む新風` is the recruit's self-conception/ceiling language at this boundary. Do not retroactively convert it into later technical power-system terminology.
4. `遣り甲斐` in the recruit's narration is closer to worthwhile purpose / sense of meaningful work than simple fun or motivation. This matters because the extra deliberately compares hero faction life to company life.
5. Saitama's occasional polite endings do not overturn the established adult colloquial baseline; they show contextual range.
6. Sonic's grand challenge register should not be mirrored into Saitama's voice when simulating their dyad. The relationship is asymmetric in salience and rhetoric.
