## 2. Orientation and prospective thesis

V36 is organized around the gap between **what can be exerted, what can be measured and what another person may become**. Tatsumaki can tear apart buildings, trigger ultra-high-energy alarms and describe an output she believes could throw Saitama into space, yet she misjudges her own limit and cannot move or injure him. The volume does not answer this mismatch with a new numerical scale. Its title `未知数` becomes explicit through Saitama's claim that no one knows the limit of someone who is weak now (0206): the unknown quantity is prospective human capacity as much as present combat power.

That uncertainty also governs relationships. Tatsumaki tries to turn protection into exclusive control because Blast's rescue, instruction and recognition remained emotionally unfinished. Saitama refuses the expected labels—subordinate, friend or comrade—and calls himself only Fubuki's acquaintance, but his conduct still stops threatened maiming, redirects danger and later supplies Tatsumaki with recognition joined to a mundane ethical boundary. The Blizzard Group, meanwhile, chooses to return after formal dissolution. Membership becomes meaningful because the members act under risk rather than because a leader or rank guarantees their safety.

Institutions answer uncertainty by manufacturing legible accounts. Headquarters sells safety while hiding demon-level storage below its residences; its sensors detect energy while losing explanatory control; executives accept a bargain that moves evidence, repairs Tatsumaki's reputation and conceals Psykos's extraction. Child Emperor's independent probe reveals that the transaction has not made the account true. The bonus gives the same structure a smaller moral scale: courage is not victory or fearlessness, but the first act of telling, witnessing and standing again while the outcome remains unknown.

## 3. Strongest counter-reading

V36 should not be reduced to Saitama therapeutically fixing Tatsumaki or to an uncomplicated friendship breakthrough. He never retracts `ただの知り合い`, does not become Blast, tells her not to return and leaves her controlling protection only partially redirected. His head pat and praise risk paternalism when isolated from the exchange; their narrower force is that recognition is offered without accepting destruction of other people's lives and property as the price of power.

Tatsumaki's danger is not merely comic exaggeration. She rescues personnel, neutralizes the poison mechanism, protects Fubuki from Tsukuyomi and possesses the force her reputation implies. Her guilt and abandonment history explain the protective monopoly but do not excuse threatened disabling violence, city-scale damage or disregard for institutional occupants. Conversely, the Blizzard Group's solidarity is chosen and costly, but the volume does not establish that its members are presently strong enough to protect Fubuki from every threat.

Nor is Fubuki's institutional transaction simple reform. It exposes undisclosed demon-level storage and outside sales while also exploiting dead cameras, moving bodies, creating a false public account and removing Psykos. The Association's secrecy is real, but Child Emperor's detection shows that counter-secrecy remains concealment. The bonus likewise ends before the bullied youth's new stance succeeds. V36 values action under uncertainty without promising that good intent, solidarity or a useful lie will settle consequence.

## 5. Saitama state

Saitama begins as an ethical interruption rather than the initiator of the psychic conflict. He stops Tatsumaki before she can disable Fubuki's followers, objects to heroes crushing one another and orders Fubuki to evacuate people because he cannot predict Tatsumaki's conduct (0041–0060). He then redirects the fight away from headquarters and populated space. That response limits harm but does not prevent later collateral damage or make him institutionally blameless; he participates in the fight, loses clothing and shoes and returns to a damaged home environment (0061–0080, 0212–0216).

His explicit social vocabulary remains narrower than his conduct. Asked whether he is Fubuki's subordinate, friend or comrade, he rejects all three and says she is `ただの知り合い`—just an acquaintance. Tatsumaki immediately treats his urgency as inconsistent with that description, while Saitama later repeats the label when challenging her protective monopoly. The volume therefore establishes action beyond indifference without authorizing a friendship upgrade he himself refuses (0057–0061, 0185–0187).

The fight supplies a strong local result and no upper-limit calibration. Tatsumaki cannot force him away, he remains narrator-certified `無傷` after her escalated output and he is concerned about her exhaustion rather than his own injury. Her outer-space language is a surprised self-assessment, not an independently witnessed feat; his own `筋肉` explanation is comic bodily register, not a mechanism model (0179–0187).

His most consequential contribution is evaluative rather than victorious. He recognizes Tatsumaki's training, calls her impressive and potentially a great hero if she can avoid destroying other people's things. He then grounds `未知数` in his own pre-training weakness and the unknowability of another person's future limit. This combines status-insensitive refusal, practical accountability and permission to grow without becoming a general theory of psychic power (0193–0208).

By the endpoint he is back in room 104 among neighbors, Genos and care obligations. Forte's recognition and the offer of used clothes/food modestly thicken his local social world, while the public story still casts Tatsumaki as savior and Saitama complains from outside the recognized account. His housing and household remain materially precarious rather than reset to isolation (0212–0231).

## 6. Ensemble and readiness

**Tatsumaki:** extraordinary combat capability, acute psychic perception and decisive rescue coexist with self-misjudged output, exhaustion, destructive escalation and a protection model that threatens other heroes. She recognizes Saitama as an unresolved test and listens enough to stop, but the closing promise to test him again shows that neither her curiosity nor her coercive habits are finished. No readiness tier changes solely because she displayed or exhausted power already known to be exceptional.

**Fubuki:** identifies the capsule mechanism, interprets institutional vulnerability, attempts direct resistance, intercepts Tatsumaki's test of the group and engineers the later transaction. She temporarily accepts fighting alone, but her followers' voluntary return changes the meaning of the organization around her. Her manipulation of evidence and Psykos's removal demonstrate strategic agency rather than uncomplicated ethical maturation.

**Blizzard Group:** formal dissolution is followed by chosen recommitment. Members acknowledge weakness, reject guaranteed protection and accept danger in order to become stronger beside Fubuki. They withstand the immediate psychic clash as a group, but the result is evidence of solidarity and limited resolve—not parity with Tatsumaki or a new calibrated combat tier.

**Hero Association:** safety, containment, surveillance, accounting and public relations are fragmented across departments. Staff rescue civilians and monitor energy, yet cameras fail in the secret floor, demon-level bodies are off-list, executives entertain outside transactions and later accept a fabricated victory account. Child Emperor's independent detection preserves an internal accountability vector without proving institutional correction.

**Tsukuyomi:** remote poisoning, infiltration of ordinary supply, artificial psychic development and monster release are demonstrated tactics. The captured operative's identity, the organization's scale and its recovery plan remain open. The unfamiliar scarred responder assists Tatsumaki by locating the capsule but receives no complete identity or affiliation in V36.

**Forte, Chain'n'Toad and Butterfly DX:** Forte revises his appraisal of Saitama after witnessing his movement; the neighbors offer material help and future association. This is a local relationship/readiness change, not public rank correction. **Sonic** remains operational but his intervention is self-defeating and supplies no new durable assessment. **Genos** appears in the domestic endpoint and agrees to replace clothing; V36 does not materially advance his combat, core or future-memory state.

No formal readiness-tier table changes are warranted by V36. Displayed strain, courage and chosen affiliation affect interpretation without supplying the durable recovery, equipment or verified capacity change required for a tier move.

## 8. Heroism, recognition and institutional implications

Heroism repeatedly appears as a boundary on power rather than the possession of power alone. Saitama's criterion for Tatsumaki is modest and concrete: she may live as she wants and use what she developed, but not destroy other people's things. His intervention before she disables fellow heroes enacts the same boundary. This is not pacifism—he consents to combat in open ground—but proportionality and protection of third parties (0041–0069, 0197–0199).

Recognition operates through partial witnesses. Forte recognizes movement he cannot explain; residents and news audiences see Tatsumaki's public image; Fubuki's group witnesses risk; Saitama recognizes training and exhaustion; Child Emperor recognizes concealment in system records. Each sees something real, but no witness possesses the complete event. V36 therefore continues the series' separation of experienced truth, official account and public credit.

The Hero Association's shelter contains a structural contradiction: wealthy residents purchase protection above an undocumented monster-storage and transaction system. Its energy monitors work until overwhelmed, its cameras are already absent where accountability is most needed and its executives turn a real Tatsumaki intervention into a false causal story. The cover preserves trust and hides illegal practice, but the billion-scale damage, Psykos's disappearance and Child Emperor's audit remain unresolved liabilities (0010–0022, 0063–0080, 0217–0236).

Fubuki's bargain is simultaneously whistleblowing, blackmail and familial protection. She uses evidence of off-list storage and outside sales to force an exchange, then participates in evidence relocation and captive removal. The volume refuses a clean opposition between corrupt institution and virtuous outsider: accountability information becomes leverage for a different concealed objective.

The bonus reduces heroism to its first accessible act. A civilian intervenes despite fear, treats reporting as courage and passes that model to the bullied youth; a battered professional hero rises again while support approaches. Neither sequence guarantees victory. The common heroic unit is action that opens a possibility for help or change while vulnerability remains (0237–0241).

## 9. Body, personhood and power mechanisms

**Remote poisoning and psychic countermeasure:** Tsukuyomi turns an ingested supplement capsule into a two-stage threat activated by psychic signal. Tatsumaki imitates the signal wavelength and twists the capsule into a hair-thin needle after the scarred responder locates the reactive inorganic object. This establishes a specific countermeasure, not general immunity to poison or proof that all supply chains are infiltrated (0017–0032).

**Psychic output and measurement:** Association instruments identify ultra-high-energy reactions and then fail. Fubuki calls a display full power; Tatsumaki later admits misjudging her output limit, narration says she could throw full power at Saitama outside life-or-death conditions and Tatsumaki thinks she has output capable of blowing him into space. These statements have different authorities and contexts. The audit does not merge them into a numerical maximum or independent outer-space feat (0057, 0166–0187).

**Saitama's bodily result:** Tatsumaki cannot displace him as expected and narration certifies him `当然のように 無傷` after the strongest displayed sequence. Saitama's joking muscle explanation and scalp complaint supply embodied comic language but no biochemical or psychic-resistance mechanism (0057, 0145–0160, 0181–0187).

**Strain, limit and recovery:** Tatsumaki becomes winded, staggers and sits exhausted after misjudging output. Saitama notices and tells her to preserve enough strength to speak. She remains able to act, threaten, leave and later move monster bodies, so V36 establishes acute strain rather than permanent injury or a fixed recovery duration (0179–0209, 0225–0235).

**Blast memory and personhood:** the remembered rescue gives Tatsumaki an instruction to protect family but not a complete life practice. Her later belief that Blast would recognize her acquired strength and teach her a better way to live is explicitly excessive expectation. Saitama's present recognition does not identify him with Blast; the formal echo exposes an unmet relational need without replacing the absent person (0188–0199).

**Growth as unknown quantity:** Saitama's pre-training inability to defeat a wolf-level monster is self-report used to support an ethical inference: present weakness does not reveal a person's limit. It is not a universal guarantee that everyone will become strong, nor a retrospective measurement of his training mechanism (0202–0208).

**Custody and evidence bodies:** stored monsters, corpses, Psykos and the moved remains become institutional assets and evidence. Physical relocation enables narrative relocation: bodies displayed on the surface support the manufactured public victory while Psykos is extracted inside one. The operation establishes concealment mechanics without resolving Psykos's health, consent or legal status (0217–0236).

## 10. Satire and genre

The luxury refuge sells a life beyond monster fear immediately before a hidden monster floor, psychic duel and failing infrastructure shake it apart. The satire depends on the shelter being materially impressive and socially exclusive, not simply fake: real protection is converted into a total safety promise that the institution's own secrets undermine (0010–0022, 0063–0080).

Combat spectacle repeatedly collapses into bodily or household inconvenience. A city-scale psychic pursuit becomes scalp friction; Sonic's elite interception ends in dog feces and his own explosives; Saitama's final accounting centers ruined shoes, clothes, dinner and the dog. These gags do not negate the damage—news assigns it a billion-scale cost—but restore the people and objects erased by power-performance language (0143–0160, 0212–0231).

The Saitama–Tatsumaki contest parodies measurement escalation. Monitors fail, witnesses speculate, Tatsumaki reaches for outer-space language and the narrator answers with the flat fact that Saitama is unharmed. The title then transfers `未知数` from power ranking to the future limit of weak followers, undercutting the expectation that a battle arc must settle a hierarchy (0173–0208).

Chapter 188 turns institutional thriller into negotiated publicity. Off-list corpses, dead cameras and a captive removal become a news-ready victory tableau, while Saitama complains over dinner that the recognized hero was the person he stopped. Child Emperor's final system probe punctures the successful cover before the bonus moves from elite secrecy to schoolyard violence (0211–0241).

## 12. Visual and formal analysis

**Hands, restraint and permission:** the volume moves from remote signal control and Tatsumaki's threatened hand toward Fubuki's group, through Saitama's wrist grab and bodily carry, to a head pat and followers raising themselves into danger. Touch shifts among coercion, interruption, transport and recognition; no single image makes contact synonymous with intimacy (0015–0044, 0065–0069, 0193–0208).

**Vertical institution and horizontal escape:** secret floors, disabled shutters, elevators, ceiling breaches and apartment corridors stack danger beneath premium housing. The duel tears outward into a horizontal landscape only to loop back through homes and headquarters. “Outside” reduces immediate trapped-population risk without producing a clean space beyond consequence (0010–0028, 0063–0080, 0145–0178).

**Witness frames:** windows, monitors, hospital views, family windshields, news screens and distant crowds repeatedly frame fragments of the fight. The chapter title `目撃` is fulfilled by multiple partial witnesses whose classifications—monster, hero, earthquake, spectacle—remain incomplete. The later news montage transforms those fragments into a singular official story (0046–0108, 0174–0178, 0229–0236).

**Spirals and displaced centers:** psychic spheres, rotating rubble, torn ground and Tatsumaki's tornado place enormous force around Saitama's visually stable body.
