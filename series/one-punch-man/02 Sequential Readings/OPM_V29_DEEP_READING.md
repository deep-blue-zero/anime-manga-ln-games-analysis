## 2. Volume orientation

V29 begins with the underground trio, an unseen voice's offer and Blast's intervention. The surface spear finally strikes, but the end of the apparent boss encounter leaves a cadre battle, wounded heroes and contested evacuation routes. A chapter centered on the rear team asks what people with very different strength can usefully do. A hospital conversation gives Suiryu a new stated ambition and introduces Suiko through ordinary sibling friction.

The central run makes Fubuki's care for Genos and her argument with Tatsumaki matter to subsequent action. Tanktop Master's rescue contribution survives his catastrophic defeat as a real benefit to other people. Bang and Genos recover him, but recovering a body is only one stage of getting it to care. Atomic Samurai, Tatsumaki and Darkshine successively keep that possibility alive. The volume closes with Darkshine's renewed confidence provoking Black Sperm's anger; it does not finish the battle or the patient's treatment.

Saitama is physically present only in the early underground narrative and later as a remembered ethical example, aside from paratext. The volume therefore develops what others do with their strength while the protagonist is unavailable to solve their immediate problems. This absence does not make him knowingly indifferent to a battle he is not shown observing.

## 3. Central volume thesis and strongest counter-reading

### Thesis — V29 makes rescue a continuing obligation, not a single victorious act

**IR/TH:** V29 distinguishes possessing enough force to interrupt danger from sustaining the conditions in which someone else can live and act. A rescue requires recognition of the particular person, a workable role, acceptance of another's contribution, and a route to continued care. Sekingar's allocation of unequal abilities, Fubuki's treatment, Pig God's bodily transport, Bang's redirection and Genos's shielding are different practices within that problem. Even a surrendered hospital bed becomes part of a battlefield rescue through Tanktop Master's explicit acknowledgment.

The opposing tendency turns vulnerable people into objects: a proposed vessel for granted power, an impure or insufficiently beautiful body, a captive returned in pieces, a broken body treated as food, or a wounded group followed to its healer. Heroic care also has an unresolved controlling edge. Fubuki acts on a unilaterally claimed subordinate; Tatsumaki recognizes her sister's growth while immobilizing her; Genos learns an ethic beyond strength while still demanding greater strength from himself. The moral distinction is consequently made in specific acts and revisions, not by assuming that every hero's category or every helpful motive is pure.

The bodily ideal receives deliberately mixed treatment. Tanktop Master's belief is absurd but accompanies real generosity and endurance; Darkshine's shine is vain but his body actually shelters Iaian. Fuhrer Ugly's humiliation ideology receives a mirror of its own when his altered face horrifies him. The volume can value a person without validating that person's entire explanation of strength.

### Strongest counter-reading and limit

This remains an action serial built from reversals, spectacular injuries and rescue entrances. The relay of helpers can also function as a way of staging the ensemble, while Tanktop Master's revival may weaken the perceived permanence of violence. Darkshine's easy triumph may satisfy a conventional comeback fantasy more than it challenges that fantasy. Nothing requires every title, joke or villain to express a single ethical system.

The narrower thesis holds because the source repeatedly gives rescue operational dependencies and costs: Fubuki stops a displayed thermal crisis and collapses; the rescued captives reach medical staff; an arm is carried with its owner; carrying patients constrains fighting; pursuit threatens the healing destination; and Tatsumaki's restraint needs Darkshine's timely blow. These acts neither guarantee victory nor erase bodily damage. The final battle and care remain incomplete, making preservation of another person's chance the demonstrated achievement.

## 4. Sequential close reading

### 4.1 `ABYSS`: receiving a voice does not mean seeing or understanding its source

**TF/VF — OPM / V29 / chapter:144 / images:0009–0023.** The title image juxtaposes an astronaut/diver-like figure, Earth/moon forms and immense eyes/cube imagery; it does not place the trio in actual outer space. At 0010 Saitama and Manako explicitly report that they cannot see into the darkness. Manako tries searchlight, thermal and X-ray functions; reflected light from Saitama's head still cannot reach the other side. This supplies a crucial epistemic limit on the previous volume's reader-facing figure. The present clarification belongs to V29, not to an invented certainty at V28.

The disembodied voice proposes judging whether the three are worthy vessels, instructs them to touch and will/pray, offers power and threatens `没収` if unsuitable (0011–0012). The object of confiscation and the completed effect are unshown. Flash wonders about telepathy; Manako finds the speech ominous; Saitama treats the speaker as a possible Monster King and demands a face he can hit. These are different responses to incomplete information. No one accepts the transaction on-panel.

Blast arrives through a dark, bright-rimmed opening and checks that they have not accepted (0013–0015). He easily spins the previously heavy cube on one finger, calls it something like a communication device, says touching it distorts space to permit contact with `ヤツ`, and describes collecting the scattered objects as a hobby (0016). The handling is observed; the explanation and motive are his account. The voice knows his name, and his casual apology does not establish friendship. Flash's recognition contrasts with Saitama's uncertainty. Blast knows Flash's hero name and rank, but no complete knowledge of the trio follows.

Flash tests the apparent Blast with `最高速` / `閃光脚`; Blast is then behind him, hand on shoulder, telling him to settle down (0018–0020). The result supports local superiority to the attempted test, without a speed ratio or a known distinction between locomotion and spatial technique. Blast's restraint matters alongside his capability. He says time flows more slowly here and offers transport to the surface, without quantifying the difference.

When Blast notices that Manako is a monster and not apparently a captive, she fears the scrutiny (0021). Saitama describes her as their guide/flashlight and dexterous, with a casual touch to her head (0022). He supplies a concrete account of her usefulness rather than adopting an abstract monster doctrine; Blast accepts and includes her in the departure. Manako's fear is not proof that Blast intended to kill her. The infinity-like hand arrangement and opening at 0023 initiate transport; arrival on the surface is not shown by V29's end. The request to greet Sitch and Tatsumaki and the expectation of meeting again are prospective.

### 4.2 Leadership and personhood are contested before the spear lands

**TF/VF/IR — OPM / V29 / chapter:144 / images:0024–0036.** Child Emperor owns his mistake in excluding Bang and Genos, calling it his own misunderstanding, then asks Zombieman to organize the group. He values judgment of people and consideration. Amai's ideal of an exemplary human leader excludes those resembling monsters; the child's defense treats Zombieman as a person and leaves room for another suitable organizer without naming one (0024–0027). The head pat registers encouragement. Amai's held reaction to the human designation is psychologically charged by his prior conflict, but Zombieman is not shown knowing his hidden condition.

Zombieman immediately pushes Child Emperor out of a projectile's way. Homeless Emperor's `どこが人だ` accompanies graphic injury (0028–0029). The hostile category attacks precisely what the preceding social exchange has affirmed. Visible destruction is not an authoritative final death declaration. Personhood is at issue in how someone protects and is recognized, while the antagonist converts bodily abnormality into an exclusion claim.

The spear descends through the underground chamber and strikes the remaining target (0030–0035). Architecture, reliefs and stairs make the penetration spatially concrete. This resolves V28's withheld impact without establishing the destruction of every cell, Psykos's final fate or a ritual resurrection. The tiny `瞬殺丸` joke recalls Flash's broken sword beside this enormous weapon; attached Tatsumaki art at 0036 closes the chapter without becoming another act.

### 4.3 `石とダイヤ`: unequal force, usable roles and an unreliable reputation

**TF/VF/IR — OPM / V29 / chapter:145 / images:0037–0056.** King and Tareo meet Metal Bat and the rear group. Waganma and Tareo's tearful reunion is mutual; it does not erase earlier abandonment. Waganma now worries about Child Emperor's weapons and injuries and asks for rescue. Metal Bat notices a change, while the father's payment offer keeps money beside care rather than purifying the whole encounter (0039–0042).

Metal Bat owns the VIP-protection failure as his account. When lower heroes kneel in apology, he stops them in front of the children and says that debts to other heroes can be repaid in turn (0043). The shoulder touch and reciprocal account matter as much as his gendered motivational line about a man settling things. King's private inventory of unpaid debts supplies the joke's counterpoint. The weaker heroes' feeling of uselessness is then answered by Sekingar's distinction between stone and diamond: each can be treasure when doing appropriate work in an appropriate place (0044–0047).

Sekingar speaks with a bandaged missing hand and takes responsibility for making that role allocation work. He does not deny differences in power or promise combat equivalence. The proposed division gives the lower group protection/transport work and the available stronger figures a forward role. Metal Bat revises his blanket dislike of association personnel and calls him `ボス`; this is earned local confidence, not an institutional overhaul (0045–0048).

King's reputation remains a dangerous input into that otherwise practical organization. Sekingar imagines the centipede-slayer can draw the enemy's attention; Metal Bat reads King's self-description as a stone as modesty. The reader has King's fear and desire to escape, while the group does not (0048–0049). A useful plan can therefore coexist with a false model of one participant. Tareo asks King to help the unnamed uncle and not bully him, but withholds the monster label and supplies no identity (0050). King's promise is not an accomplished Garou rescue.

The truck/tyre/pole launch makes collective improvisation visible: Sekingar's eye beam cuts the rope; King clings while a strained thumbs-up becomes reassurance; his scream is heard alongside Metal Bat's war cry (0051–0054). A batting-centre net and rebound supply the landing gag at 0055. These are not evidence that every risk was calculated or that King secretly controlled the flight. His misread performance has actual social effects even as his private experience remains terror.

### 4.4 `捲土重来`: a hospital ambition and an interrupted coalition

**TF/VF/IR — OPM / V29 / chapter:146 / images:0057–0070.** The title portrait introduces Suiko before the hospital scene names her. She wakes Suiryu by poking his chest; he answers with a flick that bounces her away. Sibling bodily comedy follows his admission that he thought he would die. She urges him to inherit their grandfather's dojo, argues that her training has surpassed him, and the page names the deceased Suicho as the martial school's master (0058–0060). Her boast is not a tested ranking, and the lineage image is not a present-day meeting.

Suiryu now says he wants to become a hero and help people, associating that work with his grandfather and the Water Stream school's precedent. Suiko counters with his earlier model/actor ambitions; he admits the pattern but says this time is serious (0061–0062). The declaration is meaningful precisely because the interlocutor has grounds for doubt. It is an intention after fear and rescue, not a completed career or permanent reform. Her fruit care and the oversized citrus gag supply an ordinary relationship outside the fight. Television gives a partial account of Z-City's mysterious light; the displayed 7:06 is not assigned AM/PM.

The vanished dome lets rescue helicopters resume. Tanktop Master acknowledges Tatsumaki's tremendous work through his training/rivalry idiom, while his tight-tanktop language remains comic (0063–0064). He trained against psychic power after the alien attack; the wording does not establish a previous fight with Tatsumaki. Bang notes improved coordination and concern for others. Bomb asks about Garou, and Bang's confidence that he will not die easily is an inference from knowing/fighting him, not a new sighting (0065–0066).

Tatsumaki reports spatial distortion and someone apparently swallowed by it; her subsequent `Blast?` is a hypothesis associated with memory (0068–0069). It does not give her the reader's whole account of the trio. Homeless Emperor's claim that heaven chooses a vessel like him and rejects Gyoro Gyoro/Orochi remains self-authorizing ideology (0070). The echo of `器` with the underground offer is textually worth tracking, but it does not by itself identify every contact, moon image or source of power as one settled mechanism.

### 4.5 Predation turns fear and damaged bodies into opportunities

**TF/VF/IR — OPM / V29 / chapter:146 / images:0071–0088.** Black Sperm restrains Amai as he attacks Homeless Emperor. Fuhrer Ugly's appearance triggers terror, making the encounter a restraint-and-phobia exploitation rather than a clean strength comparison. `顔面崩壊パンチ` crushes Amai's face and the attacker tears him apart; Homeless Emperor treats this as punishment of superficial beauty (0071–0075). That explanation belongs to the antagonist, and catastrophic mutilation still does not settle irreversible death or the concealed bodily question.

Child Emperor drags the damaged Zombieman and transmits a warning about multiple cadres, but Fuhrer Ugly breaks their hold. He envies the child's talent, future and praise, claiming that greater feelings of inferiority make him stronger (0076–0078). The transformation of shame into punitive entitlement is explicit in his voice; it is not a general rule about unattractive people. Homeless Emperor orders him aside and an orb blasts Child Emperor away (0079–0080). The child's destination and condition are unshown; this is neither a deliberate heroic rescue nor a certified kill.

Atomic Samurai initially wonders whether approaching water is groundwater, but Iaian recognizes Evil Natural Water. The correction gives the subordinate useful perception without proving the teacher incapable (0082–0083). Pig God and Tanktop Master face Gums while the cadres compete over prey and leadership. Homeless Emperor welcomes open sky because he need not protect the underground structure; Black Sperm questions the apparently inexhaustible supply and privately sees the hubris in the speech (0084–0088). The enemy coalition combines temporary common purpose with rivalry, not a seamless shared will.

### 4.6 `とるべき態度`: a possessive leadership claim becomes expensive care

**TF/VF/IR — OPM / V29 / chapter:147 / images:0089–0102.** Genos falls amid heat and sparks; the core has exceeded its limit and is entering thermal runaway. Drive Knight cites accomplishment of his objective, separates and leaves, while the others react to the coldness (0090–0092). This is an observed failure of continued partner care. It is not sufficient evidence of his secret allegiance or the complete mission behind his earlier sample collection.

Genos urges evacuation and proposes amplifying the final explosion against enemies. His five-kilometre warning is an evacuation instruction, not a measured blast-yield calculation. His apologies name Saitama and Kuseno. Bang rejects the young man's `捨て鉢` response and wants to wait for Child Emperor; the idiom concerns desperation/recklessness, not a literal disposable pawn (0093–0094). The imminent hardware problem is real, but self-destruction is not the only response once someone else acts.

Fubuki approaches despite Genos's warning that even her level of power should not be wasted. Her `黙ってて` interrupts his dismissal. She touches him and calls him reckless, grounding her intervention in a leader's refusal to lose a subordinate (0095–0098). The claim of membership is unilateral: this scene does not show him joining her group. Yet the title's proper attitude is enacted as an obligation she bears, with visible strain rather than another recruitment demand.

Her stated treatment suppresses the core's thermal motion and stimulates recovery in the biological portion. The falling display and restoration of cooling corroborate removal of the immediate danger (0098–0100). It is not regeneration of all metal or a return to an undamaged combat state. She bleeds and collapses; the elders catch her while Genos reaches toward her. Their `名将の器かもしれん` is a tentative judgment of potential, not appointment to a rank. Genos does not verbally praise her or accept membership on this page (0101). The volume's first major rescue of the core therefore depends on a person he initially undervalues, with a cost he cannot treat as invisible.

### 4.7 `木星`: aesthetic mastery fails to perceive its target

**TF/VF/SF/IR — OPM / V29 / chapter:148 / images:0103–0109.** The title's intact group portrait is paratext. Homeless Emperor's luminous barrage becomes a symphonic performance in his speech and conducting gestures; Black Sperm dismisses the pretension (0104–0107). The image of beautiful orchestration belongs to the attacker while Tatsumaki and others experience pursuit. No audible external score or actual planetary location is established by the title.

His declared ending and explosion fail to settle the target: Black Sperm identifies a decoy, and the broken humanoid substitute is visible (0108). The observer sees a deception the performer missed. Homeless Emperor's next carpet-bombing proposal is not already accomplished. Black Sperm's further thought that the man's bodily specifications may be ordinary, perhaps permitting an attack from behind, is conditional tactical suspicion (0109). These limits prevent an apparently endless spectacle from becoming an omniscient or invulnerable power system.

### 4.8 The sisters: recognizing growth while retaining control

**TF/VF/IR — OPM / V29 / chapter:148 / images:0110–0119.** Fubuki sees Tatsumaki's bleeding hand and wounded body through a childhood memory. Tatsumaki angrily asks who brought her sister, blames the organizers and wants to punish whoever hurt her. Fubuki insists that she came of her own will and that the injury is her own responsibility (0111–0112). Tatsumaki's protection initially interprets the sister as someone acted upon, while the younger woman claims agency.

Tatsumaki pushes her away and calls her a burden. Fubuki restrains her sister's movement and refuses to let her fight with so little reserve (0113–0114). That temporary hold is not proof of general superiority. Fubuki's account of isolation links Tatsumaki's distrust of exploitative adults and attacks on other children to a protective cage; her own group-building grew partly from fear and the desire to escape it. This is Fubuki's retrospective understanding, not an excuse erasing coercion.

She says Saitama's intervention began her reconsideration of real strength, and promises to gather companions Tatsumaki can trust so she need not remain alone (0115). At 0116 she says she can help in ways her sister cannot, because she is a hero. Genos and the elders listen. The promise has behavioral support in the preceding treatment, but its future fulfillment and Tatsumaki's acceptance remain open.

Tatsumaki breaks the hold, immobilizes Fubuki and the elders, and explicitly says Fubuki has become stronger with a softened smile (0117). Recognition and control coexist. She repeats the maxim against expecting someone to save her and decides to set an example as the elder sister before leaving alone (0118). Fubuki privately concedes that Blast may have saved her sister's life, then judges that he has placed a curse on her heart (0119). The concessive `かもしれない` belongs to the rescue clause; the curse is asserted within Fubuki's thought. `呪い` remains her interpretation of the doctrine's psychological effect, not an identified supernatural spell or demonstrated malicious intention by Blast.

### 4.9 Genos carries forward an ethic while preserving his chosen affiliation

**TF/VF/IR — OPM / V29 / chapter:148 / images:0120–0123.** Genos, visibly strained, shakes out a garment and places it over Fubuki before going after Tatsumaki. The page does not explicitly identify the garment's owner. This small act of care gives the departure a different texture from an abstract strength speech. He says he will avoid full power and can at least be a shield. That is his operating judgment after cooling, not proof of risk-free hardware.

His private recollection of Saitama respecting Mumen Rider's life-risking spirit, regardless of ability, explains the ethical model he now tries to follow (0121). He asks whether this is right, teacher. Learning extends beyond how to gain power, but imagined approval still mediates the choice. The action is his, not a present command from Saitama.

As Tatsumaki's binding dissolves, Bang calls Fubuki their dear commander and offers help. Genos accepts company while denying that Bang can give him orders as a pupil: Demon Cyborg is Caped Baldy Saitama's disciple (0122–0123). Local collaboration and chosen affiliation are compatible. Fubuki's held garment and tears do not turn the exchange into a formal membership contract. The scene preserves distinctive voices inside cooperation instead of making support depend on everyone agreeing about rank and belonging.

### 4.10 Captives, ingestion and the victory made possible by a bed

**TF/VF/IR — OPM / V29 / chapter:148 / images:0124–0140.** Four swordsmen arrive at 0124; the comparison of Tatsumaki's destruction with a nuclear blast is their hyperbole, not a yield measurement. Their individual names are not supplied here. Pig God punches Gums to help free Tanktop Master, and Fuhrer Ugly presents a living blond combat-suited captive with other bodies nearby (0125–0126). No name is assigned to this victim from outside knowledge.

Fuhrer Ugly says he will return the captive little by little, tears off an arm and throws it toward the heroes. The horror distracts Tanktop Master into another bite (0127–0129). Tatsumaki arrests a new blow and forces Gums to release him (0130). Her useful intervention does not prove that Fubuki was wrong to fear her exhaustion. Pig God gathers the exposed people into his mouth for rescue, and Tanktop Master throws him toward safety (0131–0132). The same ingestion imagery can transport lives or consume them; purpose and subsequent treatment distinguish the acts.

Tanktop Master's message to Mumen Rider calls their coming victory the victory of the hero who gave up his bed. The declaration is not a guaranteed outcome, but the credit expressly connects ordinary generosity to available rescue strength. Fuhrer Ugly then breaks Tatsumaki's hold and crushes Tanktop Master's forearms and whole body with named attacks (0133–0137). The injured hero's unfinished immortality phrase is interrupted, not narrator-certified. His body is mangled, called meat mochi and taken into Gums's mouth (0138). The dehumanizing food language belongs to the attackers. Tatsumaki remains slumped and breathing beneath the approaching threat at 0139; the chapter ends before its next act.

### 4.11 `シルバーファング`: rescue includes the limb, the message and continued care

**TF/VF/IR — OPM / V29 / chapter:149 / images:0141–0166.** A tank-top strap snaps at the medical evacuation area and prompts an omen joke, without establishing supernatural linkage or exact simultaneity with the master's injuries (0142). Pig God arrives, discharges the rescued people and calls for bleeding control. The arm's owner is conscious and lashes out at him for arriving late. Pig God responds by returning the retrieved arm and offering reassurance about reattachment (0143–0144). Retrieval and nonretaliatory care are shown; a successful operation is not.

His report credits Tanktop Master and says the boss appears defeated. He was thrown away before the latest mutilation, so the report does not prove deliberate concealment. He delivers the bed message; Mumen and the tank-top followers turn recognition into a pledge to rescue people who can use the vacated beds (0145–0146). Pig God heads back while privately debating a reserved power and the timing of a prophecy. That is a disclosed decision and unknown reserve, not a known mechanism or fulfilled prophecy.

Fuhrer Ugly's question about Tatsumaki's underwear adds a threatened sexual humiliation to his sadism. Bang's arrival interrupts it; no completed stripping or sexual assault is depicted (0147). Bang extracts Tanktop Master through Gums's opened abdomen, and Genos blasts inside the monster to inhibit pursuit (0148–0151). The burn does not kill Gums, which soon attacks again. Bang redirects Fuhrer Ugly's fist into his own face while still carrying the patient (0152–0153).

Bang judges the heart stopped. Genos readies electrically charged hands, but before an applied shock is shown Bang's stretched tank top slips and snaps against the chest; the patient jerks upright and breathes (0154). The comic event is the observable sequence, not a medical law. Bang repeats the whisper as separate questions about this being tank top magic, then calls the patient dazed; Genos calls it `うわ言`, delirious/incoherent speech. `※正気` corrects their reading of the patient's mental state (0155). It confirms that he means the belief, not that the belief is a universal physical explanation or that his shattered limbs have healed. No sleep state is required by `うわ言`.

Gums bites and tears Genos's arm. Bang calls his style the ultimate protective fist that redirects any force, a sweeping practitioner claim locally supported by successful technique (0156–0157). Fuhrer Ugly's `尊厳崩壊パンチ` names dignity itself as the target; Bang redirects it into Gums and follows with precise counterstrikes (0158–0161). The counter protects another person while denying the enemy's attempted humiliation. It does not prove literal metaphysical control over dignity.

Gums bites its ally; Bang and the now one-armed Genos carry Tanktop Master and Tatsumaki away. The patient's shattered bones make waiting impossible, and Bang reluctantly proposes imposing again on Fubuki's healing. Genos privately thinks greater strength could have spared 'her' overexertion; the immediate conversation concerns Fubuki while he carries Tatsumaki, so the pronoun is not silently replaced with an explicit name (0162–0163).

Fuhrer Ugly tears out of Gums, looks in a hand mirror and recoils from his altered, viscous face. The printed label is `Gブサイク大総統`, with `ゲロ` above G; he vows revenge on the old man (0164–0165). Appearance-directed cruelty becomes appearance-directed self-horror. The source has shown rupture, liquid coating and injury, not yet corrosive tests, a maintenance requirement or a full new power specification. Bang portrait art at 0166 closes the chapter.

### 4.12 `THE BLACK SHINE`: renewed confidence is useful before it is tested again

**TF/VF/SF/IR — OPM / V29 / chapter:150 / images:0167–0180.** Evil Natural Water reforms after cutting. Atomic Samurai observes that even the eyes appear liquid; Iaian perceives stronger jets and wonders about groundwater absorption. His view of the sea leads to a feared globe-like ocean assimilation image (0168–0169). That image is conjecture, not an accomplished planetary feat.

A jet breaks Iaian's blade. Darkshine steps into the next volley and shelters him with a chest the water does not visibly penetrate (0170–0172). The shower joke comes after the rescue. He poses under the spray, says it increases his shine, withstands stronger streams and punches the water body into a broad dispersal (0173–0179). Its eyes scatter; complete destruction of every remnant is not certified. The pressure-washing thanks at 0180 turns hostile force into service for his bodily self-image.

The rendering spends lavish detail on both attacks and the shining body while the swordsmen react in simpler surprise. The comedy does not cancel his effectiveness. It restores the pleasure he associates with his body under a matchup he can handle; it does not retest Garou, prove general invulnerability, or resolve the deeper fear exposed in V27. ### 4.13 An evacuation requires several openings, and the chapter ends on an affront
