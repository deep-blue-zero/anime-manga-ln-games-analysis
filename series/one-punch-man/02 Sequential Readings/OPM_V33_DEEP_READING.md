## 2. Orientation and central thesis

**V33 makes the reproduction of power increasingly exact while exposing failures to preserve the people who give that power meaning.** Garou learns force, movement and eventually cosmic operations; he still cannot make gratitude signify fear, make Tareo need his absolute evil, or turn a personal bond into something morally neutral. Saitama sees the contradictions in Garou's conduct, but that perception does not secure a timely rescue. The ending brings an extracted core into one hand and an escalated punch into the other, leaving the efficacy of that response unresolved (168 / 0010–0049; 169 / 0056–0057; 170 / 0108–0138; 171 / 0191–0212).

The first movement repeatedly separates intention from consequence: attacks release shelter staff, divert lava and raise land beneath stranded students. Saitama's teasing interpretation of a heroic instinct is not the cause of those rescues. Their gratitude nevertheless has actual psychological uptake: Garou recalls being shaken and becomes angry at his own susceptibility. The later external grant changes his capacities and endangers everyone nearby, including the child from whom he imagines protective separation (168 / 0032–0049; 169 / 0057, 0067–0070; 171 / 0168–0173).

The donor encounter preserves a harder agency problem than simple willing acceptance or complete possession. Garou rejects the false Bang because its offer violates what he knows of his teacher. Contact during that rejection still conveys power; his account credits Bang's practice with retaining control. Bang senses another malign presence, while Blast infers incomplete bestowal and some remaining personality. Garou then hears the lethal consequences, embraces their value as terror, and deliberately attacks Genos to manipulate Saitama. Retained agency and external alteration are co-present facts of the account, with their exact division unresolved (170 / 0153–0159; 171 / 0163–0172, 0191–0200).

Care remains distributed and vulnerable: wounded Bang interposes, a ruined Genos intervenes, Blast attempts removal and reprioritizes evacuation, Manako struggles to escape, and the final montage preserves the rescuers' bodily positions among those harmed. The Drive Knight bonus adds an epistemic counterpart: a persuasive image of care arrives through a strategically managed publicity object. The reader must preserve both the depicted appeal and its revealed mediation (171 / 0162–0203; extra / 0213–0217).

## 3. Strongest counter-reading

The strongest alternative is that the volume chiefly stages a power escalation in which divine control replaces Garou's earlier ethical contradiction, while Saitama's eventual seriousness provides the promised answer. The new body, narrator's cosmic-knowledge account, nuclear effects, Bang's diagnosis and Blast's warnings strongly support a real change of mechanism and severe interference. They cannot be reduced to costume or rhetoric. Yet the same source shows refusal of the donor's identity play, qualified retained personality, continued memory of Tareo and Bang, and a deliberate relationship-based plan against Genos. Treating all subsequent acts as automatic puppetry would discard that supplied deliberation. Treating the control claim as complete freedom would discard the equally supplied alteration (0108–0116; 0154–0172; 0191).

A second challenge is that Saitama's early insight should already count as adequate heroism: he corrects causal credit, directs evacuation and deliberately lifts a dangerous attack away from the ground. Those are real protective acts. They coexist with provocation, incomplete understanding of the altered body and his later self-reproach. The volume does not prove that a single different earlier choice would have prevented every harm, or that his final punch abandons all concern for Earth; it ends before his response and Blast's intervention can resolve the danger. The counter-reading therefore limits causal blame and final moral judgment without erasing the timing failure he himself names (0033–0036, 0138, 0201–0212).

Finally, the bonus's publicity turn could invite dismissing all apparent Drive Knight kindness as fake. The official asks that question and the reply implies strategic involvement, but no page identifies a fabricated deed or discloses the production method. The warranted revision concerns source authority and media management. It establishes neither an angelic private biography nor a proved record of fraud (0214–0217).
