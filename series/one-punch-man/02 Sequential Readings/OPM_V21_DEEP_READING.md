# 2. Volume orientation

V20 asked who possesses legitimate authority to command, classify, deploy, and evaluate abnormal professionals. V21 asks a more difficult question: **what happens when those evaluation systems meet actors whose capability is conditional, specialized, transformable, or simply outside the evaluator's model?**

The first chapter literalizes the problem through Rhino Wrestler, who assigns points to attacks and behaves as though combat can be made fully legible by his own scoring system. The chapter then exposes him to heroes whose value is distributed across radically different domains. V20's proposition that rank is informative but incomplete therefore becomes a wider V21 proposition: **measurement is locally useful until the measured object changes domain, changes state, or exceeds the model's assumptions.**

Flashy Flash makes that problem technical. Speed is not treated as one scalar. His fight with Hellfire Flame and Gale Wind is a contest among perception, learned form, terrain choice, deception, reaction, synchronized timing, and bodily output. Their monsterization produces an explicit threat-level upgrade to Dragon, but Flash's response is not to deny their new power. He makes the new power legible through prior knowledge of ninja technique and deliberately structures the fight so both opponents can be killed together. When they ask what they lacked, his answer is brutally concise: `修行不足だな` — **insufficient training**.

Child Emperor makes the same problem institutional and emotional. His backpack is visibly a schoolchild's object while functioning as a modular weapons, analysis, communication, and rescue platform. He repeatedly chooses information gathering and mission completion over prestige combat. He refuses Waganma's demand that he stop and defeat every enemy because his mission is to deliver the hostage to the surface. Yet Phoenix Man returns from apparent death, reclassified from Demon to Dragon, and exceeds Child Emperor's contingency model. Child Emperor cries not because he says he is afraid, but because his own weakness and failed prediction frustrate him so deeply that he is forced to use his `最終兵器` much earlier than intended.

The volume therefore links three superficially different kinds of evaluation:

1. **Rhino Wrestler's numeric judgments** — an actor mistakes a personal scale for sufficient explanation.
2. **Flashy Flash's martial reading** — an actor uses a domain-specific model flexibly enough to turn extreme speed back into legible technique.
3. **Child Emperor's operational modeling** — an actor openly reasons under uncertainty, revises when evidence changes, and still reaches a case his preparation did not cover.

## Central V21 thesis

**V21 is about the difference between possessing power and possessing a model adequate to use, judge, or survive that power. Evaluation works only inside conditions. Rank, disaster level, speed, technique, equipment, and contingency planning all convey real information, but none is self-sufficient. The volume favors actors who can recognize conditionality, revise their models, preserve mission priorities, and treat capability as a system rather than a label.**

That thesis has an ethical side. Child Emperor's best decision in the volume is not his biggest weapon. It is his refusal to confuse defeating an enemy with completing the rescue. Flash's strongest sequence is not simply moving faster; it is arranging the conditions under which two extremely fast opponents cannot escape. Suiryu's extra likewise converts raw strength from an end in itself into a newly chosen goal shaped by having been rescued.

## Strongest limiting / counter-reading

V21 should not be flattened into “training beats monsterization,” “numbers are fake,” “S-Class rank is meaningless,” “Child Emperor is an exploited helpless child,” or “near-death growth is now a universal law.”

- Hellfire Flame and Gale Wind are explicitly labeled **Dragon** in their transformed states. Their monsterization is a real, source-certified escalation in this case.
- Flashy Flash is not ordinary humanity defeating corrupted shortcuts through moral purity. He is himself the product of an abnormal, violent specialist training culture and speaks with extreme confidence about his own superiority.
- Rhino Wrestler's scores are his scale, but they are not random noise. They register meaningful differences inside his own evaluative framework. The error would be treating them as narrator-certified universal power numbers.
- Child Emperor is genuinely S-Class and genuinely capable. His tears do not reveal that his competence was fake. They reveal that he has a strong self-expectation and can experience contingency failure as personal weakness.
- Waganma's entitlement is visible, but he is also a kidnapped child under severe stress. His behavior should not be reduced to donor-class satire alone.
- Phoenix Man cites Gyoro Gyoro's near-death doctrine and then demonstrably returns stronger. That materially strengthens the doctrine as an in-world explanatory model, but one successful case does not establish that every near-death experience produces the same process or that it is identical to Dr. Genus's `リミッター` theory.
- Phoenix Man's costume-character logic is strongly suggested by the text and used by Child Emperor as a hypothesis. The exact causal ontology of “costume setting becomes monster ability” remains an inferred mechanism at this boundary, not a complete general law of monsterization.

# 3. Sequential close reading

## 3.1 `97撃目 判定は？` — evaluation becomes an object of satire

### 3.1.1 The volume starts by refusing a single stable power scale

The opening returns briefly to Garou/Orochi monitoring and threat-level language, then shifts quickly into the surface raid. This juxtaposition matters because the volume immediately presents multiple parties trying to classify capability from partial evidence.

Rhino Wrestler embodies the impulse most literally. He invites attacks, grades them, and converts violence into points. His behavior is ridiculous, but it also mirrors systems already embedded in the story: hero rank, disaster levels, combat metrics, and public reputations all promise to make dangerous uncertainty manageable.

`OPM|V21|chapter:97|images:0009-0016|anchor:Rhino Wrestler scoring regime and Atomic Samurai entry`

The key distinction is that V21 does not answer bad measurement by abolishing measurement. It answers it by **changing domains**. Once Atomic Samurai enters, Rhino's confidence in judging attacks meets a type of mastery that does not behave like the previous samples.

### 3.1.2 Atomic Samurai turns technique into near-instant result

Atomic Samurai's intervention is characteristically direct. He does not share Rhino Wrestler's desire to prolong the test. His stance is essentially that the enemy exists to be cut.

The following pages render enormous monster groups as material for sword-path geometry rather than prolonged individual duels. The combat's analytical function is not simply “Atomic Samurai is strong.” It demonstrates that one evaluator can be overwhelmed by a capability whose relevant variables—precision, cutting density, timing, reach—do not fit the evaluator's prior sequence of bodily impacts.

`OPM|V21|chapter:97|images:0017-0024|anchor:Atomic Samurai mass-cutting field demonstration`

Atomic Samurai's limitation is equally visible at the character level: he frequently generalizes sword logic into a universal problem-solving language. That is a useful specialist confidence, not an omniscient worldview.

### 3.1.3 S-Class capability is heterogeneous rather than interchangeable

V21 cycles through Flashy Flash, Superalloy Darkshine, Tatsumaki, Child Emperor and other raid actors in rapid succession. The montage functions like a domain atlas.

Darkshine's body contact alone becomes destructive enough to astonish Captain Mizuki. Her reaction—wondering what training he performs—keeps her own athletic schema active: she interprets extraordinary bodily capability through training process rather than through rank mystique.

`OPM|V21|chapter:97|images:0025-0037|anchor:Flash speed Darkshine body Captain Mizuki training frame`

Tatsumaki's psychic action then switches the operative mechanism again. The point is not that these specialists can be ordered cleanly on one ladder from the pages shown. The formal sequence emphasizes **qualitatively different ways of solving battlefield problems**.

### 3.1.4 `判定` is not rejected; its authority is bounded

The title asks “What is the judgment?” V21's answer is not a final score. The chapter repeatedly withholds stable closure by presenting another actor whose capability forces a new basis of judgment.

This continues V20's rank critique but makes it more precise:

`classification -> local predictive value`

not

`classification -> complete person/capability ontology`.

That distinction should control later scaling claims. A Demon or Dragon label is evidence about institutional threat classification; a Rhino score is evidence about Rhino's local assessment; a hero rank is evidence about institutional placement. None automatically subsumes all tactical variables.

## 3.2 `98撃目 速い奴` — speed becomes information, terrain, and deception

### 3.2.1 Both organizations gather information by exposing bodies to risk

Before the Flashy Flash duel fully separates from the ensemble, V21 shows monster-side actors treating disposable fighters as probes. Weak units can be sent forward to extract information about hero capabilities even if they die.

`OPM|V21|chapter:98|images:0054-0059|anchor:Monster Association information extraction through expendable units`

This is a dark mirror of the Hero Association's own intelligence problem. Both organizations need models of opponents before committing scarce high-end assets. They differ morally and institutionally, but organized combat converts information into a resource on both sides.

### 3.2.2 Flashy Flash is not merely a “fast stat”

Flash uses Child Emperor's equipment/map support while moving through the underground complex. That immediately complicates a simplistic lone-wolf reading. His combat independence does not prevent him from using externally supplied navigation and operational infrastructure.

When Hellfire Flame and Gale Wind confront him, the encounter becomes a test of a shared technical culture. They know one another through ninja provenance and speak in terms of training generations, ranking, technique and speed. Flash is addressed as the **former first place of the 44th class** (`44期の元一位`).

`OPM|V21|chapter:98|images:0062-0096|anchor:Flash shared ninja lineage and former 44th-class first-place status`

The exact institutional history remains bounded to what these speakers reveal here. V21 establishes shared ninja-world knowledge and ranking language; it does not yet authorize a complete reconstruction of that institution.

### 3.2.3 Terrain selection is part of speed competence

Flash refuses to treat the corridor as a neutral stage. He explicitly moves the confrontation because the space is too narrow.

`OPM|V21|chapter:98|images:0081-0084|anchor:Flash deliberately relocates fight from confined corridor`

That decision is analytically important because it moves “speed” away from pure bodily maximum. A speed specialist needs usable geometry. Space determines possible lines, reaction windows, evasion, visibility, and whether multiple opponents can coordinate.

The ninja monsters also attempt to exploit the chosen arena. The encounter is therefore not “who has the larger speed number?” It is a mutual modeling contest over who better understands what the environment enables.

### 3.2.4 Monsterization produces an explicit escalation

Hellfire Flame and Gale Wind reveal transformed states explicitly labeled:

- `災害レベル:竜 疾風のウィンド`
- `災害レベル:竜 業火のフレイム`

`OPM|V21|chapter:98|images:0102-0103|anchor:ninja monster Dragon-level transformed states`

This matters because V21 does **not** satirically erase their upgrade. Their bodies, movement and danger genuinely escalate. The later outcome must therefore be read as a victory over a real power increase, not proof that monsterization was cosmetically meaningless.

The bounded conclusion is:

**monsterization can sharply amplify already-specialized humans; amplification does not guarantee superiority over a better-trained opponent who understands the shared technique system.**

## 3.3 `99撃目 一瞬` — technique makes extreme speed legible

### 3.3.1 The chapter stretches an instant into analytical space

`一瞬` is formally one of V21's strongest chapters. Dozens of pages expand a confrontation whose in-world premise is extreme speed. Murata repeatedly maps trajectories across caverns, cuts bodies into successive positions, and uses long directional lines to turn otherwise invisible temporal compression into spatial information.

`OPM|V21|chapter:99|images:0104-0139|anchor:high-speed trajectory choreography and temporal expansion`

The reader is given more time than the characters because the page is functioning as an analytical instrument. Manga form converts “too fast to see” into geometry that can be studied.

### 3.3.2 Cognition is part of speed

Flash's dialogue explicitly rejects a purely muscular model. The fight repeatedly turns on whether an actor can perceive, recognize and predict a technique quickly enough for bodily speed to matter.

His clearest formulation is:

`優秀な奴ほど基本に忠実な型を崩さない`

`どんなに速くとも技の型を知っていれば容易に捌けるということだ`

`OPM|V21|chapter:99|image:0113|anchor:Flash technique-form legibility principle`

A controlled English rendering is: **the more excellent the practitioner, the more faithfully they preserve fundamental form; no matter how fast they are, if one knows the form of the technique it can be handled/read.**

This is a domain-specific claim from Flash, not narrator-certified universal martial science. But V21 immediately supports its practical relevance by showing him use shared ninja knowledge to survive opponents whose transformed bodies are explicitly Dragon-class threats.

### 3.3.3 Flash experiences real pressure

The fight should not be summarized as effortless superiority. Flash registers dangerous follow-up pressure and has to restore posture/timing under attack.

`OPM|V21|chapter:99|images:0128-0132|anchor:Flash pressured by follow-up and forced to recover position`

This is character-model useful because it gives a stress sample rather than only victory posture. Flash remains highly controlled, analytic and lethal when the fight becomes genuinely dangerous.

### 3.3.4 The simultaneous kill is strategic, not decorative

After killing both opponents, Flash explains that he had been searching for the timing to finish **both at once**:

`二人同時に仕留めるタイミングを探していたたけだ`

He judged that allowing even one to escape would create later trouble. He also claims that other S-Class heroes would have difficulty responding to their speed.

`OPM|V21|chapter:99|image:0140|anchor:simultaneous-kill planning and Flash self-assessment of S-Class speed matchup`

The first claim is directly supported by his displayed tactic. The second is Flash's own comparative judgment and must remain speaker-qualified.

The important model update is that Flash's lethality is not simple impatience. He can delay a finish when delay creates a more complete strategic outcome.

### 3.3.5 `修行不足だな` closes the shortcut argument

When the defeated ninja monsters ask what they lacked, Flash answers:

`修行不足だな`

`OPM|V21|chapter:99|image:0144|anchor:Flash insufficient-training verdict`

“Insufficient training” is deliberately anti-grandiose after a Dragon-level transformation spectacle. But the correct reading is not “monster cells are useless.” Their escalation was explicit. Flash's verdict is that **added bodily capability did not compensate for the training/modeling gap inside a shared specialist domain**.

Immediately afterward, he checks his current position using Child Emperor's device. The transition is quiet but useful: even after asserting extraordinary specialist superiority, Flash returns to operational navigation supplied by someone else.

## 3.4 `100撃目 ランドセル` — a schoolchild's object becomes command infrastructure

### 3.4.1 The title makes Child Emperor's double status visible

The chapter title `ランドセル` points at an object associated with elementary-school life. Child Emperor's backpack is simultaneously:

- a child-coded personal object;
- a mobile arsenal;
- a sensor platform;
- a communications/navigation interface;
- a deployment bay for specialized tools;
- later, part of the path toward a much larger weapons system.

The formal joke is not that Child Emperor “is not really a child.” It is that the story refuses to let his S-Class competence erase his visible childhood.

`OPM|V21|chapter:100|images:0147-0153|anchor:school backpack as investigation and weapons platform`

### 3.4.2 Child Emperor treats uncertainty as a problem to investigate

Moving through the base, Child Emperor uses maps, remote observation, technical examination and environmental clues. He infers routes rather than simply following intuition. His competence is therefore procedural:

`observe -> hypothesize -> test -> route -> revise`.

This continues the V19/V20 command model but brings it down from strategic roster planning to individual field investigation.

### 3.4.3 Phoenix Man initially appears manageable

Phoenix Man's first V21 form is explicitly labeled `災害レベル:鬼 フェニックス男`. Child Emperor attacks with specialized equipment and apparently defeats him.

`OPM|V21|chapter:100|images:0155-0163|anchor:Phoenix Man Demon classification and first defeat`

At this point, V21 has not yet established resurrection. Child Emperor's working model—that the opponent has been neutralized—is reasonable from immediate evidence.

### 3.4.4 Waganma turns abstract mission priority into a person

The hostage rescue sequence is useful because Child Emperor has to manage another child rather than an abstract objective. Waganma is frightened, impatient, curious and demanding. He recognizes Child Emperor's S-Class status and complains that the rescue took too long.

`OPM|V21|chapter:100|images:0164-0174|anchor:Child Emperor rescues and manages Waganma`

Child Emperor responds through tools and process: he neutralizes specific monster mechanisms, moves Waganma, tests routes remotely, and uses equipment to externalize danger.

The social asymmetry is striking. Waganma gets to be the child whose fear, entitlement and curiosity are managed. Child Emperor is also a child, but he is the one expected to make the correct tactical decisions.

That does not by itself prove a total institutional thesis about child exploitation. It does provide direct evidence that **S-Class role competence places adult-grade rescue responsibility on a visibly child-coded actor**.

### 3.4.5 A machine opponent tests mission discipline

An armored/mechanical opponent survives Child Emperor's disabling measure. Child Emperor could continue the fight, but V21 soon makes clear that he does not treat victory over every encountered enemy as equivalent to rescue success.

This becomes the chapter-to-chapter hinge into `悔し泣き`.

## 3.5 `101撃目 悔し泣き` — model failure is not the same as cowardice

### 3.5.1 Child Emperor explicitly prioritizes extraction over winning

Waganma asks why Child Emperor does not stay and fight the robot when it looked winnable. Child Emperor answers:

`アレは強いよ… 相手にしてたら時間もかかるし`

`勝つことは重要じゃない`

Waganma responds from the worldview he has inherited: isn't defeating monsters a hero's job?

`OPM|V21|chapter:101|image:0190|anchor:Waganma defeat expectation versus Child Emperor mission priority`

Child Emperor then gives the governing rule:

`ワガンマ君を地上へ送り届けるのが僕の使命だから`

`今は戦うべきじゃない`

and:

`勝ったとしても怪我して僕が歩けなくなったら誰がキミの前を歩くの?`

`OPM|V21|chapter:101|image:0191|anchor:hostage extraction prioritized over enemy defeat`

This is one of the clearest hero-professionalism statements in the V2 corpus so far. It rejects battle victory as the automatic highest value. Winning is subordinated to **mission purpose and continued protective capacity**.

That makes Child Emperor an important counterpoint to several other power ideologies:

- Rhino Wrestler converts battle into scores.
- Garou increasingly wants strength to produce fear.
- Sweet Mask wants power tied to controlled responsibility.
- Child Emperor operationalizes responsibility by refusing an unnecessary win that could compromise the hostage rescue.

### 3.5.2 Waganma's donor-class worldview appears in child form

Waganma repeats a paternal belief that heroes are paid and therefore should work harder for his rescue. The exact social origin matters: this is not Child Emperor's belief, nor narrator endorsement. It is a kidnapped child's inherited understanding of the institution.

The scene preserves two truths:

1. Waganma's family wealth has shaped the rescue's institutional stakes.
2. Waganma is still a frightened child trying to understand why the powerful hero beside him will not simply defeat everything.

V21 resists making either truth erase the other.

### 3.5.3 Child Emperor learns from Waganma's media knowledge

The apparent Phoenix Man corpse triggers a surprisingly valuable exchange. Waganma recognizes the costume character `ドンカンバード` and explains that the mascot repeatedly dies through absurd accidents and returns in later broadcasts.

Child Emperor immediately converts the mundane information into a monster-mechanism hypothesis:

`怪人の生態は予測がつかない`

and:

`着ぐるみによって怪人化した男が もしそのキャラ設定を引き継いだ能力を秘めていたとしたら…`

`OPM|V21|chapter:101|images:0193-0195|anchor:Waganma media knowledge becomes Child Emperor resurrection hypothesis`

This is a high-value Child Emperor cognition sample. He does not dismiss knowledge because it came from a child, a cartoon, or a seemingly frivolous source. He uses it because it fits the observed anomaly.

The broader method is:

**source prestige is less important than explanatory relevance.**

That is unusually consistent with the series' wider suspicion of status labels.

### 3.5.4 Phoenix Man converts Gyoro's doctrine into a successful self-experiment

Phoenix Man cites Gyoro Gyoro:

`死に近づく地獄を乗り越えること`

`それが怪人を爆発的に成長させる秘訣だと`

`OPM|V21|chapter:101|image:0197|anchor:Phoenix Man cites Gyoro near-death growth doctrine`

He then returns in a transformed state labeled:

`災害レベル:竜 転生フェニックス男`

`OPM|V21|chapter:101|image:0199|anchor:Reincarnated Phoenix Man explicit Dragon classification`

This is one of the strongest pieces of evidence yet for Gyoro's V18-V19 stress/near-death cultivation model. The sequence is causal-looking and internally explicit: death-like threshold -> resurrection -> major power/state escalation.

The epistemic boundary remains important. Phoenix Man's case **supports** Gyoro's doctrine. It does not establish that every creature shares this mechanism, that the process is unlimited, or that it is identical to the Genus limiter model.

### 3.5.5 Resurrection makes the evaluator's previous success obsolete

Child Emperor's earlier “victory” was real in the sense that he successfully neutralized the opponent's then-visible state. Resurrection changes the state being evaluated.

This is a central V21 pattern:

`correct model at t1` does not guarantee `correct model at t2` when the target transforms.

That is why V21's evaluation theme is more sophisticated than “everyone's rankings are wrong.” Models can be locally correct and then become obsolete.

### 3.5.6 `悔し泣き` is specifically frustration with weakness and prediction failure

After Reincarnated Phoenix Man overwhelms Child Emperor's layered defenses, Child Emperor cries. The text explicitly blocks a cowardice reading:

`怖くて泣いてるんじゃない…`

`自分の弱さが悔しくてたまらないんだ`

He continues:

`不測の事態も考えてたつもりだったのに`

`幹部ですらない奴が僕の想定を遥かに上回ってきた…`

and:

`もう… 最終兵器を使わなくちゃいけないなんて…`

`OPM|V21|chapter:101|image:0214|anchor:Child Emperor frustration tears model failure and early ultimate-weapon use`

This is a major Child Emperor character-model transition.

His self-image is not simply “I am smart.” It includes an expectation that preparation should contain uncertainty. When an opponent exceeds his contingency space, he experiences the failure emotionally as **his own weakness**.

The correct reading is not that his planning was foolish. Phoenix Man has just undergone an unexpected state transformation from Demon-class apparent death to Dragon-class resurrection. The character insight is that Child Emperor has a severe internal standard for preparedness.

### 3.5.7 Brave Giant is power with a visible expiration condition

Child Emperor summons `ブレイブジャイアント`, assembled from pre-positioned components. The reveal is spectacular, but V21 immediately refuses to let spectacle become unconditional capability.

Phoenix Man infers that such a weapon must have an operating-condition limit. The interface confirms:

`活動限界まで 残り2分38秒`

Child Emperor responds:

`時間がない…!!`

`一気にカタをつけるぞ!!`

`OPM|V21|chapter:101|images:0225-0227|anchor:Brave Giant explicit remaining activity time`

The tankobon closes the main story **here**, before the matchup resolves.

That boundary matters. At V21:

- Brave Giant exists and has entered combat.
- It has a hard visible operational timer.
- Reincarnated Phoenix Man is Dragon-class.
- Child Emperor intends to finish quickly.
- The outcome is **OPEN**.

No V22 material may be used to retroactively convert this ending into a completed V21 result.

## 3.6 `番外編 待ってられない` — Suiryu converts rescue into self-authored direction

The extra returns to Suiryu during recovery. Medical staff repeatedly tell him not to move, but he refuses to remain still.

His reason is explicit:

`ヒーロー達が怪人協会と戦ってるんだからさ`

and finally:

`俺にも目標ができたんでね`

`OPM|V21|extra:待ってられない|images:0228-0231|anchor:Suiryu cannot sit still while heroes fight and says he now has a goal`

This materially strengthens V14's rescue-driven change without pretending that the goal is fully completed or institutionally realized at V21.

The important longitudinal transition is:

`strength as entertainment/status` -> `helplessness and rescue` -> `chosen future-oriented goal`.

The extra also preserves Suiryu's personality. Growth does not turn him solemn or passive. His response to new purpose is impatience, bodily restlessness and premature exercise.

# 4. Character-model transitions

## 4.1 Child Emperor — STRENGTHEN / REVISE toward strong conditional modeling

V21 is Child Emperor's richest collected-volume evidence so far.

### Stable traits strengthened

- mechanism-seeking cognition;
- externalization of uncertainty into tools and probes;
- rapid use of low-status/ordinary information when relevant;
- mission-centered planning rather than combat ego;
- willingness to disengage from a winnable fight when victory risks mission failure;
- explicit management of another child's safety;
- self-imposed severe standard for preparedness;
- emotional frustration when reality exceeds his contingency model.

### New high-value constraints

- preparation is not omniscience;
- large systems have operating/resource limits;
- his strongest tool can become necessary earlier than intended;
- his emotional regulation is strong but not affectless;
- he can cry under pressure while remaining operational.

### Voice / register

Child Emperor's Japanese remains analytical and concrete. He moves quickly from hypothesis to action, speaks plainly to Waganma, and does not perform adult grandeur. The emotional line `自分の弱さが悔しくてたまらないんだ` is especially important because it names frustration without abandoning task orientation.

## 4.2 Flashy Flash — STRENGTHEN specialist cognition, lethal patience, and self-confidence

V21 adds enough direct evidence that Flash should not be modeled as merely “arrogant fast swordsman.”

He:

- recognizes shared technique lineage;
- chooses terrain;
- studies transformed opponents rather than denying their escalation;
- models form and reaction;
- experiences genuine danger without panic;
- delays the finish to engineer a simultaneous kill;
- explains the escape-prevention logic afterward;
- returns immediately to operational navigation.

His `修行不足だな` line reveals a deep training-centered evaluative schema. His claim that other S-Class heroes would struggle with the duo's speed also demonstrates elite self-confidence, but remains his own comparative assertion.

## 4.3 Hellfire Flame / Gale Wind — emerging paired model

V21 establishes a coherent paired profile:

- shared ninja provenance;
- prior knowledge of Flash;
- competitive/elite self-conception;
- monsterization as an intentional escalation route;
- explicit Dragon-level transformed states;
- reliance on speed, shared technique and coordination;
- underestimation of how their familiar forms make them legible to Flash.

The pair should remain bounded rather than being promoted from one dense matchup into fully independent strong models.

## 4.4 Phoenix Man — emerging -> materially strengthened

V21 establishes several durable variables:

- monster identity tied to costume/character ontology;
- self-understanding through a former mascot role;
- death/resurrection as a capability pathway in this case;
- explicit use of Gyoro Gyoro's near-death doctrine;
- Demon -> Dragon reclassification across transformation;
- increasing omnipotence/grandiosity after power escalation;
- ability to inspect/infer Child Emperor's system constraints.

# 6. Institutional and heroism findings

## 6.1 Mission success is not identical to enemy elimination

Child Emperor's `勝つことは重要じゃない` is one of V21's most consequential institutional statements. In context it does **not** mean winning never matters. It means defeating the encountered robot is less important than delivering Waganma safely to the surface.

The hierarchy is:

`mission purpose > optional local victory`.

That is a practical hero ethic rather than an abstract slogan.

## 6.2 S-Class status generates expectations that can become dangerous

Waganma repeatedly treats “S-Class” as a promise that Child Emperor should be able to solve the situation. The reader has already seen the problem with that logic: Child Emperor is powerful, but his capabilities are conditional and his tools deplete.

S-Class is therefore both:

- evidence of exceptional capability;
- a social expectation that can erase conditionality.

## 6.3 Child Emperor shows leadership as uncertainty management

V19 made roster selection an epistemic problem. V20 made raid constraints explicit. V21 shows the same leadership disposition at ground level:

- inspect;
- probe;
- preserve the hostage;
- avoid unnecessary fights;
- infer mechanisms;
- revise when new information arrives;
- deploy limited reserves only when necessary.

This is a more durable command model than “child genius = gadgets.”

## 6.4 Informational value is not rank-coded

Waganma's television knowledge provides the clue that Child Emperor needs to formulate Phoenix Man's resurrection hypothesis. The scene quietly extends the series' repeated ranking critique into epistemology:

**useful information can come from a source with no formal technical authority.**

# 7. Monsterhood, body, and transformation

## 7.1 Monsterization can amplify a pre-existing specialist system

Hellfire Flame and Gale Wind retain ninja techniques after transforming. Their monsterization does not replace prior craft; it layers bodily escalation onto it.

This is different from a model in which monsterhood creates an entirely new person from nothing.

## 7.2 Amplification can increase power without improving every relevant variable

Flash's victory makes the distinction sharp:

- bodily speed/power increased;
- threat classification rose to Dragon;
- shared technique remained legible;
- training/tactical depth remained insufficient relative to Flash.

Power growth is therefore multidimensional.

## 7.3 Phoenix Man supplies a second transformation architecture

Phoenix Man's sequence is not the ninja duo's Monster Cell path. His body/identity is tied to a costume character whose repeated fictional death/revival becomes relevant to his monster capability.

Child Emperor's hypothesis is explicit enough to route this as a separate transformation mechanism candidate.

## 7.4 Near-death growth gains a strong case but not a universal law

Phoenix Man explicitly cites Gyoro's near-death growth doctrine and emerges from death-like defeat as Dragon-level `転生フェニックス男`.

Claim transition:

**V18-V19 Gyoro doctrine: speaker-qualified experimental theory**

->

**V21: materially strengthened by a directly observed compatible case**

Still OPEN:

- universality;
- repeatability limits;
- relation to Garou's exact body state;
- relation to Genus's limiter theory;
- whether Phoenix Man's costume mechanism is necessary to his resurrection.

# 8. Power and technique findings

## 8.1 Speed is a system, not one number

V21 supplies unusually explicit evidence that effective speed depends on:

- raw movement;
- reaction/perception;
- cognition;
- known technique forms;
- terrain;
- timing;
- opponent coordination;
- escape prevention;
- willingness to delay a finish.

Any later “speed scaling” that strips these conditions away would lose the manga's own model.

## 8.2 Technique can convert extreme motion back into predictability

Flash's `技の型` line establishes a locally explicit mechanism: familiar form gives predictive leverage even against very fast execution.

This is one of the strongest examples so far of the series refusing to treat a physical metric as independent of knowledge.

## 8.3 Brave Giant is an explicit bounded-resource system

Like Genos's V20 ten-second maximum-output ceiling, Brave Giant arrives with a hard temporal constraint. The similarity is analytically useful:

- extraordinary output;
- explicit engineering/system cost;
- user must manage a narrow operating window.

The series increasingly represents technological power as **conditional envelope**, not permanent stat increase.

# 10. Satire, genre, and public narrative

## 10.1 Rhino Wrestler turns power-scaling into diegetic comedy

A monster literally scores attacks. The joke works because the series itself contains rankings and disaster levels, and because readers of battle manga are trained to ask exactly these questions.

The satire does not say “never compare power.” It says that an evaluator's confidence can outrun the scope of the metric.

## 10.2 `修行不足` punctures transformation spectacle

The ninja monsters receive dramatic Dragon labels and spectacular transformed bodies, only for Flash to summarize the gap as insufficient training. The bathos is structurally similar to Saitama's genre punctures, but the mechanism differs:

- Saitama often makes the contest itself irrelevant.
- Flash keeps the contest meaningful and wins by specialist mastery.

## 10.3 Child Emperor weaponizes childhood iconography

`ランドセル`, toy-like gadget names, lollipops, mini-robots and a giant mecha coexist with hostage rescue and lethal combat. The humor is inseparable from the discomfort: a child-coded material world has become operational military equipment.

## 10.4 `ドンカンバード` makes television trivia tactically decisive

A cancelled mascot program with excessive black comedy becomes the clue to a Dragon-level resurrection mechanism. The series again makes “low” cultural knowledge more practically useful than formal prestige would suggest.

## 11.1 `一瞬` uses page expansion to represent compressed time

The high-speed fight is a formal paradox: an instant requires dozens of pages. This is not inefficiency; it is how manga exposes a perceptual regime too fast for ordinary observers.

# 18. Canonical V21 closure synthesis

The final V21 transition can be summarized as:

**evaluation under changing conditions -> specialist legibility -> contingency failure -> constrained escalation.**

Rhino Wrestler's scoring regime shows the temptation to convert combat into a stable metric. Flashy Flash demonstrates a better evaluative model because he understands the technique system producing the movement and adapts terrain/timing around it. Child Emperor demonstrates a better institutional model because he keeps mission purpose above optional victory and treats even childish media knowledge as usable evidence. Phoenix Man then demonstrates the unavoidable limit: the target itself can change state enough to invalidate a previously reasonable model.

The resulting V21 thesis is therefore not anti-measurement or anti-planning. It is anti-**unqualified certainty**.

The strongest actors in this volume are not simply those with the largest output. They are those who understand what their output depends on, what their information leaves open, and what objective they are actually trying to accomplish.

**Canonical sequential boundary after closure: V01–V21. Next source operation: V22 semantic source lock and `OPM_V22_DEEP_READING.md`.**
