## 2. Orientation and prospective thesis

**V34 separates the power to exceed an opponent from the ability to keep a human world worth returning to.** The held core gives the spectacular duel a persistent relational cost. Saitama finally has an opponent against whom he says he can use full strength, yet explicitly feels no excitement (172, 0026). Narration subsequently establishes accelerated growth driven by an unprecedented emotional surge (173, 0081–0084); it does not restore the anticipated pleasure of competition. The battle's magnitude culminates in ordinary bodily actions, while the action that changes the lost future depends on accepting Garou's instruction. The opponent becomes a contributor to repair precisely after his doctrine of salvation through terror confronts the child it has harmed (0105–0138). Superior force is indispensable here, but technique, information, restraint, another person's initiative and an object of attachment make it consequential.

The time intervention rescues the present without giving everyone the same knowledge of the loss or the achievement. The core carries memories; Saitama merges with his earlier self and does not retain an intelligible recollection; Genos builds a theory that overshoots the evidence into flawless, always-timely heroism (0141–0162). The reader must hold the actual aid together with the failures, instruction and self-doubt that the disciple's account compresses. The difference between a correct result and a complete causal story becomes one of the volume's central recognition problems.

The dawn therefore proceeds through several forms of repair rather than one universal absolution: a child prevents an execution, King deters a crowd, Bang proposes restitution and accepts responsibility as teacher, Metal Knight organizes medical/environmental response, Garou returns to supervised ordinary life and martial study, children revise their play, and Saitama/Genos salvage a household (174–175, 0174–0214). The sewing bonus adds Fubuki's awkward, laborious return of care (0216–0218). These gains do not certify the end of bullying, Garou's successful future rehabilitation, complete environmental recovery or a solved God cosmology. `得たもの` is best read against the particular things gained: renewed relationships, agency, a possible training path and ordinary shared activity, alongside a divine attainment that Garou can no longer recall.

## 3. Strongest counter-reading

The strongest limiting reading is that temporal reversal protects the series' recurring cast and comic baseline from the consequences of its escalation, while Garou's reform and institutional accommodation arrive rapidly. The source supports this pressure: the catastrophic future is displaced, Saitama loses its memory, the execution stops, police business is made comic and the main narrative ends on a clam. The unmatched-power spectacle is also real, not merely a device for discussing care. The core narration certifies actual growth and an actual reversed-causality victory; a reading that dissolves these into subjective grief would be false.

Yet the reset is not represented as uniform erasure. The future core persists and transfers memories, Genos acquires an epistemic burden, Tareo has a distinct uncertain encounter, residual nuclear contamination demands treatment, the home remains lost, Bang resigns and commits to guiding Garou, and the Association anticipates resistance to recruiting him. The ending has materially different bodies, obligations and relationships. Calling it costless ignores those residues; calling them full equivalent compensation for the lost future overstates what is shown. Genos's reassurance and the return of comedy are meaningful recovery, but they are not proof that Saitama had planned or understood every stage.

Garou's care for Tareo is likewise neither a secret excuse for all violence nor proof that his public terror project was always a disguised rehabilitation plan. He must confront having harmed the person he meant to save, ask the victor to do what he cannot, and later accept a teacher's path. The prospective model preserves the coexistence of responsibility, external empowerment, attachment, pride and a future that still needs work.
