## 2. Orientation and prospective thesis

V35 is organized around **classification without context**. Rank, property status, monster detection, confidential records, hero branding and prisoner designation all see something real, then distort it when the label is treated as the whole person or situation. Air sees a wounded S-Class body and converts recovery into a rank contest; the defense system correctly detects Pochi and Black Sperm as monsters but answers with lethal automation that cannot interpret Saitama's accompaniment; Association records capture fragments of Saitama while leaving Bofoi, Amai, Flash and the public to reconstruct the person from mismatched effects; the Association classifies Psykos as a monster and uses that category to erase human rights and sell experimental access. Fubuki's school hierarchy and Psykos's chosen-ones extermination doctrine show the same desire to turn power difference into a complete social order from opposite directions.

Against those systems, the volume repeatedly supplies **situated recognition through conduct**. Pig God ignores insult and accepts bodily injury to finish rescue work. King lends Saitama credibility through an ordinary relationship. Amai, Flash, Bofoi and Fubuki separately detect exceptional strength behind a low rank, though each bends that discovery toward a different private project. Saitama's own counter-classification is comic and incomplete: he sees Pochi as a dog and Black Sperm as a monkey, not as defeated monsters choosing concealment. That ordinary frame creates shelter and a pause in violence, but it is not informed absolution. The result is neither simple faith in institutions nor simple celebration of innocence: the volume tests whether recognition can remain relational, revisable and accountable instead of becoming a market category, threat file or ownership claim.

The title `会っちゃいけない奴` first becomes explicit when Black Sperm remembers Saitama as the one person he must not meet (0030). By the endpoint, that forbidden encounter has produced a precarious domestic refuge, while Saitama himself drops among demon-level captives and treats the secret floor as evidence that Pochi should be allowed at home (0195). Threat and household do not cancel one another; their unstable coexistence is the volume's governing tension.

## 3. Strongest counter-reading

The domestic comedy should not be read as proof that monster labels are empty or that unconditional trust reforms everyone. The gate sensors identify two actual Monster Association survivors; Black Sperm lies, seeks a protector, resents the heroes, considers future cell recovery and never renounces opportunism. Pochi remains powerful enough that Black Sperm fears a sudden attack. Psykos did propose human extermination and still attacks Fubuki. Tatsumaki's intent to finish her and the Association's confinement therefore answer genuine danger even when their methods remain contestable.

Nor does the volume establish that every institution is merely incompetent. The Association provides housing and mobilizes responders; the defense system detects hidden monsters before any human does; Bofoi's private analysis correctly identifies an extraordinary mismatch in Saitama's record; secrecy around `神` is defended through a plausible panic-and-targeting risk; professional records let Fubuki anticipate Tatsumaki; and Tatsumaki recognizes Tsukuyomi as a research threat from lived experience. Pig God's responsibility language and King's intervention also operate inside hero institutions rather than outside them.

The harder reading is that **real danger does not justify totalizing control**. Classification must be joined to history, motive and proportion. Fubuki is not a pure humanitarian alternative: she admits sealing Psykos from ego, needs losers beneath her summit and wants access to the future vision. Amai's authentic-strength standard resists manufactured branding but still rests on intimidation. Saitama's ordinary categories create mercy without full knowledge. V35 therefore leaves every apparent solution compromised enough to require continued judgment rather than a single anti-rank or pro-domestic moral.
