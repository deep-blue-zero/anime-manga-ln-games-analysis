# One Punch Man — Character Modeling Schema
## Evidence-grounded longitudinal state, relationship conditioning, voice, knowledge, and validation

## 1. Purpose

This schema defines how the One Punch Man V2 project represents character evidence for later literary synthesis and conditional novel-situation reconstruction. It does not authorize roleplay by archetype and it does not convert predictions into canon.

The governing question is:

> Given this chronology, knowledge state, relationship, role, audience, stakes, and stress regime, what response is best supported by the character's observed behavioral history?

## 2. Canonical ownership

Character-centered state lives in one canonical ledger:

- `OPM_SAITAMA_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md`
- `OPM_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md`
- `OPM_MONSTER_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md`
- `OPM_INDEPENDENT_CIVILIAN_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md`

Directional interpersonal state lives in `OPM_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md`.

Evidence-coverage status lives in `OPM_CHARACTER_MODEL_READINESS_INDEX.md`.

Concept-centered claims remain in the established thematic ledgers.

## 3. Character-state record

Preserve fields only when evidence exists.

### Identity and role
- current public/social role;
- institutional memberships;
- hero/monster/civilian/independent status;
- aliases, hero names, monster names, and naming preferences;
- public reputation versus actual capability;
- public persona versus private behavior.

### Psychological state
- desire hierarchy;
- fears/vulnerabilities;
- self-conception;
- pride/shame/status sensitivities;
- current sources of confidence/insecurity;
- unresolved contradictions.

### Values and power philosophy
- what strength means;
- what strength permits or obligates;
- relation to heroism, justice, recognition, rank, monsterhood, autonomy, protection, domination, or survival where supported;
- moral priorities and legitimacy assumptions.

### Perception and cognition
- habitual attention;
- blind spots;
- attribution habits;
- analytical style;
- danger calibration;
- power perception;
- common misreadings of others;
- known epistemic limits.

### Decision behavior
- default heuristics;
- action threshold;
- risk tolerance;
- uncertainty response;
- authority response;
- helplessness response;
- insult/shame/praise/defeat response;
- restraint/escalation policy;
- help/care behavior.

### Social and relational behavior
- baseline sociability;
- hierarchy sensitivity;
- dominance/submission patterns;
- conflict style;
- reconciliation/repair style;
- relationship-specific exceptions.

### Stress regimes
Track separately where evidence exists:
- low-stakes baseline;
- competition;
- acute danger;
- overwhelming power disparity;
- injury/exhaustion;
- humiliation/status collapse;
- moral crisis;
- grief/loss;
- public scrutiny;
- institutional pressure.

### Japanese voice
- pronouns;
- address terms;
- politeness/formality;
- sentence endings;
- contractions/slang;
- recurring phrases;
- insults/praise;
- silence/hesitation;
- grandiose versus plain register;
- serious/comedic switching;
- relationship- and audience-specific variation.

### Embodiment
- injury and bodily-cost behavior;
- transformation/body identity;
- combat habits;
- posture/gesture when analytically meaningful;
- noncombat bodily habits relevant to personality.

### State transition
- prior boundary;
- current boundary;
- `PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN / REVISE / DOWNGRADE / REJECT / OPEN` where an earlier modeling claim changes;
- current counterexamples;
- confidence.

## 4. Behavioral evidence atom

Preferred table:

| Context | Trigger | Partner | Observed behavior | Interpretation | Locator | Evidence class | Authority | Repeat status | Confidence | Available from |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

`Observed behavior` should be as close as practical to source-observable fact. `Interpretation` should state the conditional modeling consequence without pretending a local scene proves a global trait.

## 5. Evidence authority

Use at least:

- `tankobon_canonical`;
- `web_active_provisional`;
- `web_superseded_revision_evidence`;
- `comparative_non_manga` only when a separate comparative task explicitly requires it.

The stable model is tankobon-derived. Web evidence forms an overlay until collected/reconciled.

## 6. State versus trait classification

Classify apparent personality features as:

- stable tendency;
- developmental state;
- relationship-specific tendency;
- role-specific behavior;
- stress-specific behavior;
- exceptional one-off;
- unresolved hypothesis.

Do not upgrade to `stable tendency` merely because a scene is memorable. Repetition across independent contexts is preferred.

## 7. Relationship record

Relationships are directional unless explicitly reciprocal. Track:

- current relational definition;
- trust;
- affection;
- rivalry;
- fear;
- dependency;
- authority;
- resentment;
- idealization;
- protectiveness;
- communication style;
- recurring conflict;
- repair behavior;
- speech/register difference;
- knowledge asymmetry;
- misrecognition;
- major state transitions.

Do not average two participants' models of the relationship.

## 8. OPM-specific misrecognition rule

A character model must distinguish:

- objective capability known to the reader;
- public reputation;
- what the modeled character personally believes;
- what the modeled character has actually witnessed;
- what institutions record or misrecord.

King/Saitama-style reputation inversions are not edge cases; they are central evidence about how OPM characters perceive and behave.

## 9. Low-stakes rule

Preserve mundane behavior as first-class evidence. Shopping, food, games, casual visits, domestic intrusion, waiting, jokes, minor irritation, embarrassment, hobbies, paperwork, social awkwardness, and ordinary friendship can be more predictive of baseline personality than combat.

## 10. Saitama-specific rule

Do not infer Saitama's general emotional range from physical danger because ordinary combat usually fails to create meaningful risk. Prioritize evidence involving:

- boredom/meaning;
- recognition and misrecognition;
- friendship;
- inconvenience;
- money/sales/food;
- games and competition;
- social intrusion;
- ethical reflexes;
- unexpected emotional salience;
- ordinary-life attachment.

## 11. Knowledge-state lock

Every reconstruction specifies:

- chronology boundary;
- knowledge available to the character;
- private/public audience;
- relationship state;
- role/institutional position;
- stakes;
- stress regime.

Analyst hindsight may interpret earlier evidence but may not be given to the character as knowledge.

## 12. Model readiness

Use:

`insufficient → emerging → moderate → strong → specialist_ready`

Track:

| Character | Boundary | Context breadth | Relationship breadth | Language breadth | Longitudinal depth | Low-stakes breadth | Stress breadth | Readiness | Main missing evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

A single dramatic scene can sharpen interpretation without increasing readiness.

## 13. Dual authority horizons

### Collected model
Canonical Japanese tankobon only.

### Current-release overlay
Official uncollected web material marked `active_provisional`.

When material is redrawn/replaced/collected, reconcile each affected evidence atom. Never silently preserve an obsolete provisional behavioral claim.

## 14. Prediction discipline

A prediction may state:

- best-supported response;
- plausible alternatives;
- conditions that would change the response;
- confidence;
- evidence routes.

Predictions may not update observational fields and may not be cited as evidence for later predictions.

## 15. Held-out validation

At a validation boundary:

1. freeze the model;
2. permit only evidence with `Available from <= boundary`;
3. exclude later retrospective recontextualization;
4. predict the held-out response;
5. reveal canon;
6. classify any mismatch;
7. update the observational model from the canonical scene only after scoring the prediction.

Mismatch classes:

- missing context;
- wrong stable-trait inference;
- relationship-specific exception;
- role-specific exception;
- stress-specific exception;
- genuine character growth;
- genuine surprise;
- revision/redraw instability;
- insufficient evidence.

## 16. Volume propagation audit

After each volume, `OPM_VXX_UPDATE_MANIFEST.md` records every touched cumulative surface and explicit `NO CHANGE` where appropriate. This audit verifies that modeling-relevant evidence was propagated out of the sequential reading.

## 17. Governing prohibition

Do not model a character from fandom shorthand, hero/villain archetype, power level, isolated catchphrases, or dramatic convenience. Use conditional source-grounded behavioral history.
