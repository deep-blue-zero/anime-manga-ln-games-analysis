# 3. Six-volume cumulative thesis

Across V01–V06, One Punch Man does not merely ask what happens when a hero is stronger than every visible opponent. It progressively decomposes concepts that superhero and battle narratives often allow to collapse into one another:

- **power** is not the same as fulfillment;
- **winning** is not the same as duty;
- **hero identity** is not the same as professional registration;
- **professional registration** is not the same as rank;
- **rank** is not the same as capability, morality, contribution, or public reputation;
- **public recognition** is not the same as accurate causal knowledge;
- **recognition desire** is not the same as approval-dependent motivation;
- **failed resistance** is not the same as meaningless resistance;
- **monstrous body** is not the same as reduced personhood;
- **elite status** is not the same as common ethos;
- **shared suffering or boredom** is not the same as shared ethics;
- **overwhelming physical power** is not the same as universal competence or authority.

The first six volumes therefore move from a comic premise—Saitama wins too easily—to an increasingly precise system of **non-equivalent dimensions**. The more overwhelming Saitama's physical ability becomes, the more the manga shifts analytically useful difficulty into meaning, attribution, institution, relationships, social perception, moral choice, information quality, and problems that cannot be punched.

The cumulative Saitama problem is best stated as a **reciprocity/engagement deficit caused by extreme capability mismatch**, not a global absence of emotion. He remains highly responsive to money, food, inconvenience, games/competition, social intrusion, false blame, mundane desire, relationship behavior, and situations with genuine novelty. V06 then supplies both an ethical mirror (Boros) and a domain where physical power is inadequate to the underlying cause (`鮭`).

# 5. Saitama checkpoint model

## 5.1 Stable high-confidence traits/policies

### Internally authorized heroism
The deepest continuity from V01 through V06 is that Saitama acts because he has chosen hero activity as something he does. Registration, rank, pay and public recognition become real layers, but none replaces self-authorship as motive authority.

### Challenge/reciprocity sensitivity
Credible resistance and novelty activate him disproportionately. The Subterranean dream, interest in unusual situations, and V06 response to planetary-threat possibility all support this. Boros confirms that challenge deprivation is narratively real without proving ethical equivalence.

### Recognition is valuable but subordinate
He can want fans, attribution and gratitude; become angry about false blame; and still refuse to let evaluation define why he acts. V05 raises the bar: he can knowingly destroy his own reputation to protect the meaning of weaker heroes' failed resistance.

### Practical rather than ceremonial care
Concern often appears as action: entrust Genos to Bang, direct civilians to injured heroes, refuse a child's money, catch a falling stranger, tolerate/accept Genos's practical domestic care. Absence of sentimental vocabulary is not absence of attachment or ethics.

### Context-dependent social intelligence
Saitama misses conventions, rank cues and some media knowledge. But V05 shows deliberate crowd-narrative manipulation and V06 shows adversarial conversational inference. The canonical model is **selective salience**, not generalized social incapacity.

### Material ordinariness
Sales, limited cash, food/drink, housing and mundane inconvenience remain highly salient despite cosmic power. This is not decorative comedy; it supplies a stable low-stakes behavioral baseline.

## 5.2 Counterexamples the model must preserve

- He wants recognition, yet sacrifices it.
- He can be socially blunt, yet socially strategic.
- He is physically overwhelming, yet cannot solve another person's job/family despair.
- He often appears emotionally flat in combat, yet becomes intensely activated by relatively mundane irritation or genuine resistance.
- He can be rough/insulting under false blame, yet nonlethal restraint persists.
- He self-identifies from hobby origin, yet now corrects himself to professional hero when context makes that layer relevant.

A simulation that cannot reproduce these tensions is overfit to stereotype.

## 5.3 Conditional predictors frozen at V06

These are **predictions for later testing, not evidence**:

- When a problem is routine and physically trivial, Saitama will tend toward compressed attention/low ceremony.
- When novelty or credible resistance appears, engagement should increase sharply.
- When public recognition conflicts with a more important ethical/social value, he may sacrifice recognition rather than protect image.
- When confronted with false causal blame, he is more likely to reject the premise bluntly than perform conciliatory remorse he does not believe.
- When another person's problem lies outside his competence, he is more likely to admit limits and help concretely where possible than pretend expertise.
- Status alone should not produce deference; relationship and practical relevance should matter more.

These hypotheses must be revised if V07+ produces counterexamples.

# 6. Genos checkpoint model

## 6.1 Readiness transition

At V06 Genos moves **moderate -> strong**. This is not because he has become fully known; it reflects enough breadth across six volumes to support conditional reconstruction beyond a narrow disciple archetype.

## 6.2 Stable high-confidence policies

- Information-dense analytical cognition under threat.
- Very high bodily-cost tolerance.
- Independent professional/civic duty, not merely Saitama-following.
- Anonymous-civilian protection can override preservation of combat body.
- Respect for Saitama coexists with disagreement, experimentation and interpretation.
- Practical attachment is often expressed through observation, memory and provisioning rather than affective declaration.
- Continual capability acquisition remains a central means-goal.
- Cooperation is available when strategically useful; anti-affiliation language can follow rejection rather than represent a universal prior policy.

## 6.3 Principal model risk

V06 makes Saitama an almost axiomatic epistemic anchor: if an evil existed that Saitama could not defeat, Genos reasons, humanity would already be extinct. That belief is evidence about **Genos**, not about the universe. Later material should be watched for what happens when Saitama's limits are nonphysical, opaque, or uncertain.

## 6.4 Prediction freeze

- Genos should accept severe self-cost quickly when a concrete civilian is threatened.
- He should seek mechanism/explanation even while acting decisively.
- He should remain capable of challenging a claim while preserving respectful `先生` register.
- He should notice practical details about Saitama that Saitama himself does not verbalize.
- A situation that seriously contradicts Saitama-invincibility assumptions would be especially diagnostic.

Again: predictions are subordinate and non-evidentiary.

# 8. S-class / hero ensemble state

By V06 the S-class can no longer be modeled as “the strongest heroes” in a way that implies a shared culture or psychology. The Association assembles:

- Bang's experienced calm and ego-light evidence acceptance;
- Tatsumaki's contemptuous autonomy and large-scale defense;
- Metal Bat's family interruption and rough action-first persistence;
- Child Emperor's child schedule and tactical decomposition;
- Atomic Samurai's master/swordsman pride;
- Puri-Puri Prisoner's unresolved hero/criminal contradiction;
- Genos's analytical seriousness and Saitama anchor;
- King's public title versus self-report;
- absent Blast/Metal Knight and other attendance limitations.

The useful unit of analysis is therefore **conditional coalition behavior**, not “S-class personality.” Future ensemble synthesis should preserve disagreement and local cooperation rather than average them into a team ethos.

# 9. Antagonist, monsterhood, and body-state checkpoint

V01–V06 produce no single monster ontology. Instead they establish multiple routes:

- engineered organisms / House of Evolution;
- obsession/habit-linked transformations;
- natural/nonhuman collectives such as Sea Folk;
- human criminal/antagonist actors who are not thereby `怪人`;
- extraterrestrial organized persons with political command structures;
- multi-unit/shared-body ambiguity in Melzargard.

The archive must therefore keep **body origin**, **personhood**, **moral agency**, **organization**, **threat**, and **transformation mechanism** separate.

Boros is especially important because he is an antagonist mirror of Saitama's experiential deprivation. The checkpoint does not label him “evil Saitama.” The analytically useful relation is:

`similar challenge deprivation -> different moral entitlement -> different action policy`.

Garou remains mediated historical evidence only and must not be backfilled from later familiarity.

# 10. Relationship-state checkpoint

## Saitama / Genos
The relationship has moved from tolerated intrusion into a durable domestic/professional bond with asymmetric language and increasingly reciprocal practical trust. Genos's investment remains much more explicit. Saitama nevertheless accepts cohabitation/proximity, teacher-role expectations, practical care, and institutional routing through Genos.

The key asymmetry at V06 is epistemic: Genos treats Saitama's ability as nearly axiomatic; Saitama does not perform equivalent ideological investment in Genos.

## Saitama / Sonic
Still sharply asymmetric. Sonic's defeat/rivalry reorganizes his goals and rhetoric; Saitama does not grant the relationship equivalent status. V04-V05 prove Sonic retains cooler tactical/survival cognition outside Saitama conditioning.

## Bang / Saitama
Bang moves from witness/curiosity toward explicit recognition and recruitment without face-saving denial. Saitama is friendly/practical but rejects hierarchy/doctrine when uninteresting.

## Atomic Samurai / Iaian
New V06 mentor relation: concern and authority coexist with Iaian's willingness to transmit unwelcome failure evidence.

Do not create durable pair claims for incidental S-class interactions until recurrence warrants them.

# 11. Thematic checkpoint

## Heroism
The strongest early-series rule is now: **capacity and heroism are related but non-identical**. Mumen Rider, Genos, Saitama, Stinger, Bang and the Deep Sea King crisis provide different combinations of capacity, duty, sacrifice and outcome.

## Recognition / public narrative
Recognition matters to Saitama, but its value is conditional. Public narrative is fallible rather than uniformly foolish. Institutional and crowd sensors can both reveal and distort.

## Institution
The Hero Association is simultaneously:
- registry;
- ranking/payment system;
- threat sensor;
- dispatch/coordination network;
- public-legitimacy manager;
- emergency-secrecy authority;
- imperfect causal attribution mechanism.

“Bureaucracy bad” is no longer an adequate synthesis.

## Power
Power is not one ladder. Mechanism knowledge, environment, technique, cost, range, access and information all affect effective action. Saitama remains an extreme outlier, but the explanatory cause remains open.

## Satire
Comic puncture repeatedly coexists with sincerity. The series can mock hero branding while valuing courage, mock motivational speeches while valuing practical care, and puncture cosmic grandeur while preserving real ethical stakes.

## Visual form
Scale inversion remains central: tiny Saitama against huge threats, small mundane irritants against his emotional attention, and V06's cosmic ship followed by a rooftop salmon lunch. Visual anti-climax reallocates importance rather than simply erasing it.

# 13. Contradictions preserved rather than resolved

1. **Saitama wants recognition / Saitama sacrifices recognition.** Both are true under different value conflicts.
2. **Saitama is convention-blind / Saitama can perform sophisticated social strategy.** Salience/context explains more than a global trait label.
3. **Genos is deferential / Genos disagrees and experiments.** Deference is relational register, not cognitive surrender.
4. **Puri-Puri Prisoner is a real responding hero / Puri-Puri Prisoner is a repeat sexual predator and prisoner.** Institutional hero status cannot sanitize moral evidence.
5. **Tatsumaki is contemptuous/status-policing / Tatsumaki is decisively protective in crisis.** Interpersonal abrasiveness and duty competence are independent axes.
6. **The Hero Association protects society / the Association uses coercive secrecy and lossy evaluation.** Organizational function and ethical cost coexist.
7. **Saitama's power solves immediate physical threat / it does not solve meaning, job loss, family collapse or another person's reasons for living.** This is a domain distinction, not a power inconsistency.

# 15. Prediction freeze for V07+ validation

This is the first formal prospective prediction freeze. Because V01–V06 have already been read, there is **no claim of held-out validation within this checkpoint**. Instead, the following hypotheses are frozen *before opening V07* and can later be scored against V07+ evidence:

### Saitama
- ordinary/status-only conflict will usually produce low engagement unless a concrete value is implicated;
- challenge novelty should increase engagement without automatically changing ethical restraints;
- social bluntness should coexist with selective strategic inference;
- recognition should remain valued but subordinate when it conflicts with another person's deserved standing or Saitama's self-authored ethics;
- nonphysical human problems should elicit more honest limitation/practical support than grand ideological instruction.

### Genos
- will continue to combine deference with analysis/disagreement;
- concrete civilian danger should rapidly trigger self-costly protection;
- capability acquisition should remain salient;
- Saitama-related certainty is a likely pressure point for future mismatch.

### Bang
- should update when direct evidence defeats prestige/technique expectations rather than protect face at all costs.

### S-class ensemble
- cooperation should remain situational and capability/problem dependent rather than becoming frictionless team identity.

### Antagonist/body systems
- new hostile actors should not be forced into one monsterization pathway; source-specific mechanisms must be preserved.

**Scoring rule:** later evidence can confirm, partially confirm, contradict, or remain non-diagnostic. A prediction failure requires model revision; it must never be reinterpreted into “evidence that the prediction was secretly right.”
