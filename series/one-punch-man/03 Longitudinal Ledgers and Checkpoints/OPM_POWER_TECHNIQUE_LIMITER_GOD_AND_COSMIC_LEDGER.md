# One Punch Man — Power, Technique, Limiter, God, and Cosmic Ledger


## Evidence discipline


This ledger records what characters/text establish about power. Later explanatory terms in the filename do not become V01/V02 facts before they enter the source.


## V01 baseline


### Saitama
- observed capability gap is extreme;
- ordinary physical threats do not demand visible effort;
- Subterranean dream shows affective engagement returns when credible resistance is imagined;
- power therefore creates an experiential/reciprocity problem independent of simple victory.


### Other systems
- Fukegao's serum produces extreme bodily amplification;
- Genos demonstrates technological combat modification;
- visual/destructive scale is not a reliable proxy for difficulty to Saitama.


## V02: Saitama's training explanation


### Textual facts
Saitama reports:
- 100 push-ups;
- 100 sit-ups;
- 100 squats;
- 10 km running;
- daily persistence;
- environmental discomfort/no air conditioning;
- approximately three years of continuation.


He interprets the result through persistence and self-change (`OPM|V02|chapter:11|images:0070-0078`).


### Epistemic split


**Saitama belief:** high-confidence TF. He sincerely believes the regimen is the explanation available to him.


**Objective complete cause:** **OPEN**. Genos explicitly states the observed strength is obviously beyond ordinary bodily training and seeks another explanation (`image:0078`).


Do not silently upgrade either character's theory to narrator truth.


### Philosophical claim
`自分で変われるのが人間の強さだ!` (`image:0075`) establishes Saitama's value claim about strength even if causal ontology remains unresolved.


## Carnage Kabuto


Kabuto's instinct recognizes lethal danger from Saitama despite appearance/scientific expectations. Add a persistent distinction:


- measured/designed hierarchy;
- subjective instinctive threat perception;
- objective capability.


## Paradiser battle suits


External equipment massively amplifies ordinary users. The suits belong to a hidden supplier that records operational data. Power is therefore distributable, technological, and institutionally owned.


## Sonic


Extreme speed/afterimage technique creates visual and tactical superiority over ordinary opponents. Saitama's reaction demonstrates another ceiling problem: spectacular technique may still fail to create meaningful difficulty against absolute capability disparity.


## `じぶんみがき`
Day-300 training shows gradual adaptation and improved reflexive movement. This supports a real embodied training process without settling how that process reaches present-day Saitama's absolute scale.

## V04 additions

### Meteor intervention and secondary consequence
Saitama can shatter the main meteor with one hit, but fragments retain destructive energy and damage City Z. Extreme power solves the primary collision without becoming narrative omnipotence over aftermath.

### Genos output ceiling / cost
Genos explicitly evaluates whether his own power can destroy the meteor and contemplates committing his deepest available energy despite uncertain success. His power system has finite reserves and bodily cost.

### Metal Knight weapons test
A large remote missile barrage does not materially solve the meteor, demonstrating that technological spectacle and high hero rank do not guarantee parity with disaster scale.

### Deep Sea King / rank mismatch
Several registered heroes are severely overmatched by Deep Sea King. Institutional class/rank remains an imperfect predictor of encounter outcome.

### Still OPEN
No objective causal explanation of Saitama's absolute strength is added. V04 provides no basis to resolve later limiter/God/cosmic questions prospectively.

## V05 additions

### Contribution != victory
Genos can injure/pressure Deep Sea King without winning; Mumen Rider can provide protection/hope with virtually no victory probability. Power analysis must preserve intermediate contribution rather than reduce all encounters to win/loss.

### Genos body as expendable resource under protection priority
The child-shield sequence shows a deliberate trade: preserving a civilian at the cost of catastrophic combat-body damage. This is a behavioral use of power/resources, not merely a durability feat.

### Environment-conditioned opponent strength
Rain changes/restores Deep Sea King's effective state. Encounter outcome depends partly on environment rather than static rank labels.

### Saitama still ends the physical problem immediately
The one-punch resolution preserves the V01-V04 absolute-gap evidence at this boundary, but V05's moral climax occurs after the punch. Do not infer from combat supremacy that Saitama automatically dominates social/reputational problems without strategy.

### Still OPEN
V05 adds no objective causal explanation for Saitama's strength and no later limiter/God/cosmic ontology. Shibabawa's `地球がヤバい` is a threat signal, not yet a power-system explanation.

## V06 additions

### Boros — first explicit challenge-deprivation mirror
Boros reports that his own overwhelming strength removed satisfying opponents and that he crossed space after a seer promised an equal. This strengthens the **experiential** model of excessive power producing reciprocity deprivation while providing no new causal explanation for Saitama's strength.

### Boros armor / released state
Boros says his armor seals excessive power; Saitama breaks it, after which Boros releases greater power. V06 ends before the encounter resolves. Do not import later forms, techniques, or outcome.

### Psychic power — local capability, not a single scalar category
Geryuganshoop's telekinetic projectile use is trivialized by Saitama, while Tatsumaki independently stops and returns the alien ship's bombardment. The correct inference is not “psychic power is weak” but **user/context capability varies enormously**.

### Melzargard regeneration rule
Repeated cutting/blunt damage is insufficient until embedded spheres are discovered. Metal Bat's accidental/test destruction and Bang's inference convert an apparently invulnerable body into a rule-governed target. This is a strong example of **mechanism knowledge changing effective combat capability**.

### Saitama's power remains ethically non-authorizing
Boros gives the clearest early counterexample to the idea that superior power licenses taking stimulation from weaker beings. Saitama's condemnation makes ethics independent of capability.

### Genos continues capability acquisition
The V06 omake shows Genos researching psychic powers as a possible addition to combat. He remains committed to expanding available means even after becoming S-class and after V05's protection evidence.

### Still OPEN at V06
- objective cause of Saitama's strength;
- full upper bound of Saitama/Boros at the V06 stopping point;
- whether Shibabawa's prophecy is exhausted by the present alien event;
- any later `limiter`, `God`, or cosmic explanatory framework not yet textually introduced.

### Comparative adaptation

### Ontology exclusions

## V10 update — `技`, `気合い`, and the failure of one-dimensional strength

### `技` becomes an explicit power category
Mumen Rider and Tanktop Master identify Garou's advantage as `技`: highly developed martial technique, not merely more raw strength. Garou's fight with Golden Ball/Spring Mustachio then demonstrates trajectory-reading, timing, adaptation, and counter-organization under pressure.

### Saitama's interest is category-driven
The discovery of a strength type he has not been attending to causes Saitama to seek martial-arts exposure. This does not establish that technique can overcome his physical ceiling; it establishes that explanatory novelty still matters to him.

### Metal Bat `気合い`
Metal Bat says `気合いがありゃ大抵なんとかなる` and visibly becomes more dangerous after accumulating damage/activation. Treat `気合い` as a demonstrated performance-state variable at V10, not as a complete quantified mechanism or infinite scaling law.

### `オカメちゃん`: useful scalar, invalid universal model
Child Emperor's instrument measures physical/body strength. V10 directly displays excluded or problematic dimensions:
- psychic power (Fubuki);
- technological/weapon capability;
- martial technique;
- coordination/specialization;
- state-dependent transformation;
- upper-bound saturation (`9999` ceiling / Saitama `測定不能`).

Saitama and Fubuki show opposite failure modes: Saitama exceeds the device/model while Fubuki's relevant power lives outside its physical variable. The correct conclusion is domain limitation, not anti-measurement mysticism.

### Limiter/God/cosmic state
No new V10 source evidence warrants advancing limiter, God, or cosmic explanations. Preserve existing open states.

## V11 - dynamic output, technique, and coordinated power

### Metal Bat fighting spirit - STRENGTHEN / BOUND
V11 confirms a dynamic performance state: accumulated damage can coincide with increasing offensive pressure. The post-conflict collapse supplies the necessary bound. Fighting spirit can sustain or amplify performance under injury; it does not erase damage or establish limitless regeneration.

### Garou technique - STRENGTHEN recursive opponent modeling
Against Metal Bat, Garou cannot rely on a one-time read because the opponent's output changes. His martial strength again operates as continuous inference, redirection, timing, and adaptation.

### Team power - REVISE additive-only framing
`Sentai` distinguishes simple headcount from role-complementary coordination. Specialized teams can create capabilities that are not captured by summing individual raw strength, but those gains depend on current information and appropriate assignment.

### Elder Centipede - OPEN capability ceiling
V11 establishes extreme scale and durability against Metal Bat and Metal Knight testing, but the encounter remains unresolved at the volume boundary. Do not infer later outcome or full durability ceiling.

## V12 update — capability domains, experiential limit, and model-relative outliers

### Saitama — capability can obstruct experience
In `64撃目 限界`, Saitama explicitly asks to `体験` Bakuzan's martial art and allows the opponent to demonstrate techniques. His own automatic overwhelming response ends the bout before the desired experience is obtained: `しまった また試合できなかった`. This is not a physical ceiling. It is an **experiential limit produced by excess capability**: the very power that solves combat instantly can prevent sustained access to the domain Saitama is trying to learn.

### Genos — domain-specific inferiority acceptance
Against Awakened Cockroach, Genos identifies a speed/evasion problem, accepts that the opponent exceeds him in that specific domain (`相手が上回る分野`), and deploys `超強力な瞬間接着剤` rather than trying to win a pure speed contest. V12 strengthens a power-modeling rule: capability comparisons are multidimensional, and growth can require changing the problem rather than matching the opponent on its strongest axis.

The later `規格外だった…` reaction records a model-relative outlier after catastrophic defeat. `規格外` here is an epistemic/capability judgment—outside the expected standard—not an official disaster-level label.

### Metal Bat — fighting spirit remains costly
Association reporting places Metal Bat unconscious and seriously injured after the V11 sequence. `気合い` remains demonstrated as a performance-state amplifier under injury, but V12 further rejects a limitless-regeneration or cost-free-endurance interpretation.

### Snek / Suiryu — strength telos diverges
Snek's crisis asks what professional strength is *for* when much stronger outsiders exist. Suiryu explicitly frames strength as a means to live easily and treats natural elimination as a reason not to accept rescue obligation. Their physical contest establishes Suiryu's greater combat capacity without resolving the normative question.

### Drive Knight — transformation as information tool
Drive Knight's tactical transformations are used not only to destroy targets but to preserve a source, seek an origin point (`発生源`), and threaten coercive extraction. Power therefore functions as an intelligence instrument as well as direct force.

### Child Emperor / Pig God / Watchdog Man
V12 expands the series' heterogeneous capability domains: Child Emperor is equipment- and analysis-dependent; Pig God converts unusual bodily tolerance into rescue/combat utility; Watchdog Man's local combat performance creates a credible Q-City guardian reputation. These are different capability architectures rather than one scalar ladder.

### OPEN — Garou versus Watchdog Man
Garou believes he has `見切った` Watchdog Man's movements from observation and can respond to anything shown. The next impact immediately stress-tests that confidence, but V12 does not reveal the encounter's final result. Preserve the matchup as OPEN at this boundary.

## V13 update — technique as functional constraint, transformation, and observer-relative access

### Saitama / martial arts — STRENGTHEN capability-created epistemic limit
Saitama intentionally receives Suiryu's martial art but eventually defines it as `なんか動きがかっこいいもの`. This is not narrator exposition. V13 simultaneously renders Suiryu's technique as highly structured and effective against ordinary constraints.

Current claim: Saitama's extreme physical surplus removes many of the problems technique solves, so he can perceive external form without directly experiencing functional necessity.

### Self-restraint as a power-control problem
Saitama repeatedly calibrates downward because a normal counter would end the match and destroy the experiment. Maximum output is therefore not the only meaningful power variable; **minimum useful output / test-preserving control** can be an active problem.

### Haragiri — bodily enhancement does not erase technique hierarchy
Monster Cell conversion produces a dramatic strength/speed increase. Atomic Samurai still preempts and kills Haragiri before the transformed draw decides the exchange. Power enhancement and complete combat competence remain separate.

### Flashy Flash / Tatsumaki — distinct solution geometries
Flash attacks the giant octopus through precision and acceleration; Tatsumaki solves the entire spatial object at once. “Strength” remains multidimensional rather than a single scalar.

### Suiryu — first defeat as capability-model revision
Suiryu recognizes that Saitama stopped the decisive blow (`寸止め`) and privately accepts `負けた……初めて…`. The official tournament result does not prevent a direct internal capability update.

## V14 update — asymmetry, martial hierarchy, and observer resolution

### Suiryu under Gouketsu — STRENGTHEN domain ceiling
V14 places Suiryu's previously dominant martial model against an opponent whose strength and control make his established options functionally irrelevant. The result is not evidence that martial technique is meaningless; it establishes a local ceiling when capability asymmetry is extreme.

### Gouketsu hierarchy claim — TESTIMONY, not omniscient scale
Gouketsu tells Bakuzan that stronger monsters exist above him, including Gouketsu himself, and makes an absolute claim about the Monster Association's boss. Preserve this as informed in-world testimony. V14 does not independently certify the absolute ranking.

### Saitama/Gouketsu — STRENGTHEN observer-relative compression
The manga withholds the fight itself and returns only environmental effects and Gouketsu's severed head. Saitama later cannot meaningfully distinguish the opponent from other one-punch resolutions. Extreme surplus collapses comparative resolution for Saitama; this does not make all opponents objectively equal.

### Genos/Gouketsu — STRENGTHEN threat-model recalibration
Genos interprets Gouketsu as beyond prior monsters in his sample and plans repair plus a redundant collective response. His comparison to Saitama is explicitly bounded to the impression of bottomless strength (`...という点では`) and should not be read as equality or invincibility disconfirmation.

### Monster Cells — STRENGTHEN power acquisition with unknown dosage law
V14 repeatedly shows large post-conversion power gains, but provides no reliable quantitative mapping from cell amount to resulting strength.

## V15 update — multiple growth mechanisms

### Garou: learning-driven growth
Watchdog Man defeat is explicitly `勉強になった`; Garou reconstructs unfamiliar movement and attempts incorporation. Power development here is observation/adaptation, not a generic post-injury stat increase.

### Saitama: physical completion versus other mastery axes
King's game analogy gives a source-internal model for why maxing one progression dimension does not finish the larger activity. Do not infer a new physical growth mechanism for Saitama; V15 instead exposes the inadequacy of physical power as a universal development metric.

### King: rule-domain technique
The fighting-game `ハメ技` demonstrates that capability ranking depends on governing rules. King's mechanical knowledge can dominate when Saitama's bodily power is causally excluded.

### Genos: engineering revision loop
Defeat routes to Kuseno rebuild and new configuration. Preserve the distinction between increased hardware capability and proof of corrected tactical policy.

### Disaster-level estimation
Threat classification is explicitly revisable under new evidence; hidden abilities are a known error source. This is an explanatory-system result, not merely institutional satire.

## V16 update — adaptation, coordination, and constrained performance

### Garou adaptation becomes transfer learning
Watchdog Man-derived movement is redeployed against Genos. V15's defeat-as-study is therefore operationalized across a different opponent rather than remaining retrospective admiration.

### Multi-agent composition is not scalar addition
Death Gatling's formation and Bang/Bomb pairing both show that compatible roles/styles change the tactical problem. Effective capability depends on interaction structure, not only the highest individual rank.

### Condition labels are mandatory for V16 power claims
Garou's poison, blood loss, fatigue, narrowed vision, and damaged legs are explicitly foregrounded before Genos/Bang/Bomb. Any later comparison that omits these states is analytically invalid.

### Distinct martial lineages remain causally meaningful
`流水岩砕拳` and `旋風鉄斬拳` are shown as different systems whose combination creates a new constraint surface. Preserve technique/mechanism rather than collapsing martial arts into generic power.

## V17 update -- power as domain, information, and coordination

1. **Bang/Bomb versus Elder Centipede:** an elite combined technique fractures the shell but fails against molt/regeneration. Technical success against a surface is not a complete kill mechanism.
2. **Genos:** hardware upgrades expand options, but target analysis remains necessary. He changes geometry and attacks internally after exterior methods fail.
3. **King/Saitama:** information, reputation, and positioning create a usable path for overwhelming force. Saitama's output is decisive; King's plan is causally necessary to the particular safe interception shown.
4. **Monster Association:** raw membership and high-tier counts are separated from collective capability when Gyoro Gyoro identifies cohesion and leadership as deficits.
5. **Narinki suits:** claimed high equipment performance does not imply mission superiority under unfamiliar numbers, abilities, and terrain.
6. **Sonic:** subjective sensation is not reliable mechanism identification; weight loss is mistaken for transformation power.

V17 strengthens the anti-scalar rule: power is output plus mechanism, information, target relation, positioning, coordination, endurance, and command.


## V18 update — limiter theory enters canonical evidence

### Dr. Genus limiter model — NEW major OPEN theory
Genus defines `リミッター` as a `成長制御の仕組み`, a growth-control mechanism he believes constrains living beings within ranges compatible with survival purpose and reason. He theorizes that Saitama removed his limiter by pushing himself beyond ordinary limits.

**Authority:** in-world expert theory, not omniscient narration. Zombieman explicitly challenges its plausibility.

### `神` — NEW OPEN lexical node
Genus says organisms are designed by `神` under the limiter model. Preserve the word and its context without merging it with any specific later cosmic/divine actor. V18 alone does not establish direct knowledge or identity.

### Monsterization != limiter removal — STRENGTHEN anti-collapse rule
Genus explicitly separates multiple monster-transformation routes and ordinary hero exceptionalism from Saitama's alleged limiter removal. Technology, special talent, biological transformation, training, and limiter removal must not be flattened into one axis.

### Gyoro/Orochi growth engineering — NEW parallel mechanism, identity OPEN
Gyoro claims Orochi began as a human and was manufactured through repeated failures/sacrifices, then targets Garou as potential second success. The volume juxtaposes this stress-engineering model with Genus's limiter lecture, but does not prove the mechanisms are identical.

### Garou stress growth — STRENGTHEN / mechanism OPEN
Garou continues reading opponents under catastrophic damage and later survives an apparently fatal cutting sequence. This is high-value growth/body evidence but is not yet sufficient to label “limiter breaking.”

### Matchup (`相性`) — STRENGTHEN institutionally
Gyoro operationalizes compatibility in raid assignments. Individual monster boasts about beating heroes remain predictions until tested.

## V19 power update - adaptation, proxy limits, and coercive growth

### Garou adaptation - STRENGTHEN
Garou adjusts across three distinct Demon-level mechanisms, with Tareo independently observing continued growth. A formerly fast attack becomes perceptually slow to Garou after adaptation. Growth is directly observed, but no single universal mechanism is established.

### Threat levels - REVISE as bounded but sometimes informative
Garou sarcastically asks whether his performance makes him Dragon-level when opponents invoke measurements, then Rover's arrival produces a visible scale discontinuity and makes him take the Dragon category seriously. Threat labels remain imperfect/revisable proxies, not meaningless numbers.

### Gyoro near-death program - STRENGTHEN / keep separate from limiter
Gyoro explicitly describes calibrated death-survival cycles and extreme load as a route to explosive growth, claims many failures before Orochi, and treats Garou as a candidate. Do not merge this with Genus's limiter model merely because both concern growth ceilings.

### Fighting spirit concept transfer - STRENGTHEN Garou learning breadth
While adapting to psychic restraint, Garou recalls Metal Bat and asks whether his present state is what fighting spirit means. The question establishes conceptual borrowing from an observed opponent; it does not prove identical physiology.

### Technique versus body - STRENGTHEN
Garou explicitly relies on technique to bridge bodily disparity against Orochi and uses former-human anatomy as a target model. Orochi then immediately copies Garou's form, demonstrating that adaptive/copying competence is not uniquely Garou's property.

### Fear versus strength - NEW Garou power-purpose distinction
For Garou, raw strength is insufficient for the Monster King ideal; power must generate fear. This is an ideological use-condition for power, not a new numerical scaling category.


## V20 power/technique delta

### Genos ten-second full-power ceiling — NEW mandatory capability condition
Kuseno defines the current upgrade as a reckless, attack-specialized configuration with reduced balance. Full power is usable for **ten seconds**; exceeding the limit risks body/core failure and death. Future comparisons must condition any maximum-output feat on duration, self-regulation, defensive compromise and catastrophic failure risk.

### Same power category does not imply peer capacity — STRENGTHEN
Jagan and Tatsumaki both use psychic/telekinetic mechanisms, but Tatsumaki overwhelms him decisively. Mechanism labels remain insufficient for scaling.

### Distributed specialization — STRENGTHEN
The support montage expands power ontology away from one-dimensional output: sniping, restraint, poison-on-contact, athletics-derived movement, mechanized melee, tool use and unreliable-but-large psychic activation all produce conditional battlefield value.

### Rhino Wrestler scoring — REJECT as objective scale
His point totals provide information about **his** experience of attacks inside **his** chosen test. They are not narrator-calibrated cross-character measurements. Iaian's 69 points is therefore evidence of exceptional performance relative to Rhino's prior samples, not a universal numeric power value.

## V21 power / technique delta

### Speed as coupled system — NEW durable formulation
The Flash/ninja fight makes speed dependent on raw movement, perception, cognition, technique form, terrain, timing, cooperation, and escape prevention. Flash's `技の型を知っていれば容易に捌ける` gives direct source language for technique knowledge making extreme speed more legible/handleable.

### Flash simultaneous-kill timing — STRENGTHEN tactical power model
Flash delays the finish to create a moment when both opponents can be killed together. His effective power includes patience, prediction and strategic containment, not just maximum velocity.

### Monsterized ninja amplification — STRENGTHEN conditional scaling
Hellfire Flame and Gale Wind are explicitly Dragon in transformed state and still pressure Flash. Their defeat should not be converted into “Dragon is weak” or “monsterization supplied nothing.” It is a domain-conditioned matchup between related technical systems.

### Phoenix Man near-death escalation — STRENGTHEN Gyoro doctrine / preserve limiter separation
Demon-class Phoenix Man dies/apparently dies and returns as Dragon-class Reincarnated Phoenix Man after citing Gyoro's near-death growth doctrine. This materially strengthens that doctrine while leaving its relationship to Genus's limiter theory unproven.

### Brave Giant — NEW hard operating-envelope evidence
The V21 boundary displays `活動限界まで 残り2分38秒`. Extraordinary technological output is therefore explicitly time-conditioned. Like Genos's V20 ten-second maximum-output state, later feat interpretation must preserve the operating envelope rather than convert spectacle into an unconditional permanent stat.


## V22 power / technique delta

### Child Emperor wins by model revision and dependency attack — STRENGTHEN
When Brave Giant is spent, Child Emperor does not simply seek more output. He models Phoenix Man's dependency on the costume shell and uses a small specialized robot through an opening. V22 is a strong case where **correct mechanism inference + low-output tool** outperforms continued maximum-force escalation.

### Zombieman / Pureblood — NEW local-win versus engagement-win model
Pureblood can defeat Zombieman roughly two hundred times locally and still lose the engagement. Power analysis must distinguish exchange dominance from termination condition. Zombieman's regeneration is also finite enough that ordinary wounds can require minutes.

### Phoenix resurrection — REVISE from V21 simplification
The revived ninja pair show memory/conscious behavior before Saitama knocks them out, so Phoenix's revival effect should not be represented merely as corpse puppetry. Limits and persistence remain OPEN.

### Amai Mask embodiment — OPEN
The visible facial anomaly demonstrates unusual body properties but V22 does not explain durability/regeneration/mechanism. Do not power-scale the hidden body from one reveal.

## V23 power / technique / God delta

### G5 copies form; Atomic preserves embodied judgment — STRENGTHEN
G5's claim of a complete copy proves that technique data can be captured/reproduced at meaningful combat value. Atomic's victory proves only that the stored copy is not sufficient to equal his full combat performance in this encounter. Avoid the metaphysical claim that machines can never master technique.

### Black Sperm — NEW proliferation mechanism
Black Sperm explicitly says cutting, tearing, striking, crushing, or twisting him produces more bodies. This is a direct mechanism mismatch with conventional decisive cutting. Exact cell count, lower bound, upper bound, and termination condition remain OPEN.

### Homeless Emperor / `神` — NEW major evidence node
V23 supplies the first substantial collected evidence for a power-grant narrative. In Homeless Emperor's remembered account an entity says his judgment is correct, calls humanity foolish, tells him he need not die, says it will grant him power, and tells him he should know what to do with it. Homeless Emperor then interprets that grant as reason to destroy humanity and calls the power `神通力`.

Evidence hierarchy:
- luminous attacks: **directly demonstrated**;
- human-origin autobiographical narrative: **Homeless Emperor testimony**;
- entity appearance/speech: **reported memory rendered by the manga**;
- entity granted the power: **strong in-world attribution but mechanism not independently tested**;
- entity is literally God: **OPEN**, explicitly challenged by Zombieman;
- entity explicitly commanded human extermination: **NOT ESTABLISHED** in V23.

### PPP adaptation — speaker-qualified rapid-evolution hypothesis
Nyan attributes PPP's new dense hair defense to accumulated damage triggering defensive instinct/evolution. Keep separate from limiter, Gyoro, and monster-cell mechanisms.

### Pig God / Gums — unresolved consumption matchup
Both use consumption as combat mechanism, but V23 closes before resolution. No comparative ceiling can be assigned from the tankobon endpoint.
