# One Punch Man — Technology, Organization, and Hidden Actors Ledger


## V01 baseline


### Genos
A technologically reconstructed human combatant with autonomous goals/personhood. His body establishes high-level cybernetic capability without yet identifying the entire support infrastructure behind him.


### House of Evolution
Introduced as a coordinated source of engineered beings and an organized attack on Saitama/Genos. V01 leaves leadership/scale incompletely exposed.


## V02 additions


### House of Evolution / Dr. Genus
- organized research hierarchy;
- cloning of Genus;
- biological engineering of specialized combat beings;
- secure underground/compound infrastructure;
- institution collapses because Saitama invalidates its power hierarchy.


Track separately from mechanical/cybernetic technology.


### Paradiser battle suits
- stolen external suits can transform ordinary humans into large-scale threats;
- suits are not merely possessions: their operation supplies `実践データ` back to an originating organization (`OPM|V02|chapter:14|image:0170`);
- organization sends armed/robotic agents to retrieve/clean up after theft;
- Hammerhead is treated as expendable after data collection.


**OPEN:** identity, goals, scale, and relation of this organization to any other technological actor.


### Information/control theme
V02 introduces a recurring technological question:


> Who owns power-enhancing technology, who is being observed through it, and what does the supplier learn from real-world deployment?


This should remain independent from raw power scaling.

## V04 additions

### Metal Knight / Bofoi remote intervention
Remote platform deployment separates technical participation from bodily exposure. Metal Knight refuses Genos's collaboration request and treats the meteor as a field test for new weapons while still attacking the threat.

### Hero Association emergency system
The Association can issue S-class summons, circulate disaster-level information, evacuate personnel, and update rankings. It cannot guarantee hero compliance, complete causal attribution, or public interpretive control.

### Prison infrastructure
`ぷりズン` introduces a containment institution with surveillance and internal hierarchy adapted to unusually dangerous inmates. Puri-Puri Prisoner's breakout exposes the practical limit of conventional confinement against superhuman bodies.

## V05 additions

### Hero Association assessment economy
`買えないモノ` exposes the organization's information/compensation pipeline:
- ranking position determines monthly compensation;
- activity is translated into points;
- explicit point criteria are not publicly disclosed;
- assessors hold the criteria;
- self-report and civilian eyewitness testimony are inputs;
- recorded achievement and rank can still diverge.

This strengthens the information-architecture model from V03-V04: institutional sensors exist, but translation from field reality to status/reward is lossy and partly opaque.

### Promotion and public-image consultation
Chapter 29 shows Saitama's B-class promotion and Association consultation with Amai Mask. Amai explicitly limits his concern to management of socially influential A-class-and-above heroes; do not overstate lower-class gatekeeping at this boundary.

### Shibabawa warning channel
A respected fortune teller produces the planetary warning `地球がヤバい`. Record as a new nontechnical threat-sensing channel. Cause, accuracy mechanism, timing, and responsible actor remain open.

## V06 additions

### Hero Association classified-threat governance
Shibabawa's note becomes high-level classified intelligence. Sicchi stages disclosure with pre-brief opt-out, then secrecy/confinement after disclosure. The Association therefore acts as both **threat-sensing network** and **information-control institution** under planetary uncertainty.

### Shibabawa as probabilistic intelligence source
Association staff state that she had predicted only a subset of prior disasters and that her final wording `地球がヤバい` was unprecedented. Treat prophecy as an intelligence channel with an established record, not omniscience. The six-month interpretation belongs to Association reasoning.

### Metal Knight infrastructure
The Hero Association headquarters survives A-City annihilation because unusually strong construction had been commissioned to Metal Knight. His technological role therefore spans both remote weapons testing (V04) and hardened institutional infrastructure (V06), while his broader agenda remains open.

### Dark Matter command/information network
The alien force has named officers, subordinate damage reporting, telepathic/centralized communication, and strategic command. A subordinate reports one intruder causing roughly 23% ship damage in about four minutes. Yet Boros can act on stale officer status, proving centralized communication is not omniscient.

### Genos as self-directed technology user
The psychic-power omake shows that Genos actively searches for additional capabilities rather than merely receiving fixed upgrades. Technology is part of his continuing self-reconstruction project.

### Hidden-actor/open-system state
- Garou: only Bang-mediated historical actor at V06;
- Metal Knight/Bofoi: infrastructure + weapons-test role known, agenda open;
- Paradiser battle-suit supplier: still unidentified;
- Shibabawa's prophecy target: unresolved;
- Dark Matter origin/political context beyond Boros's account: unresolved.

### Drive Knight warning — NEW unresolved actor relation
Drive Knight privately tells Genos `メタルナイトは お前の敵だ` and advises avoiding him. This is direct evidence of Drive Knight's warning/suspicion only. Truth, motive, and source of knowledge remain OPEN.

### Metal Knight expands from weapon actor to infrastructure actor — STRENGTHEN
V07 adds:
- recovery/removal of the alien ship after the A-City incident;
- automated post-catastrophe construction;
- end-to-end design/construction/material handling capacity attributed to his system;
- completion of a project discussed on roughly a ten-year conventional horizon in **seven days**.

Combined with V04 weapons testing and V06 hardened HQ construction, this supports `emerging` operational modeling while leaving personal motive unresolved.

### Hero Association centralization
Metal Knight-enabled reconstruction produces fortress HQ, rapid roads toward the cities, and optional A-class-and-above residence. Technology is therefore not merely combat equipment; it reshapes the institution's geography and dispatch model.

### Hidden-actor epistemic control
Genos may hypothesize about Metal Knight after Drive Knight's warning, but neither warning nor hypothesis should be promoted into objective truth. Maintain explicit `speaker belief / institutional fact / observed effect` separation.

## V10 update — organization as specialization; measurement technology as bounded model

### Fubuki Group organization
`数字` confirms that Fubuki Group's scale is not empty headcount. Fubuki can identify a former professional gamer and deploy that specialist for a noncombat contest. Organization therefore produces real capability through member heterogeneity, even though King defeats the numerical strategy.

### Saitama network as non-organization comparator
Saitama denies that a `サイタマ組` exists, yet can assemble King, Bang, and Genos. This provides a useful comparator: high-capacity relational networks can exist without formal organization, rank rules, branding, or membership administration.

### Child Emperor's `オカメちゃん`
The device is legitimate measurement technology with a bounded variable (physical strength), calibration logic, and numeric output. Its failure on Saitama and Fubuki demonstrates an epistemic limitation of the model/inference layer, not evidence that the technology is fraudulent.

### Hidden-actor state
V10 does **not** materially advance Drive Knight/Metal Knight conspiracy questions, the Organization, God, or other hidden actors. Do not use the presence of measurement technology or rising monster incidents to force connections not made by the source.

## V11 - Monster Association becomes an observed strategic organization

V11 moves the Monster Association from implied monster convergence to direct strategic organization. Observed functions now include surveillance of Garou, recruitment, field discipline, synchronized multi-city attacks, resource conservation, selective retreat, threat distribution, and child-hostage acquisition.

**Boundary:** these functions are directly observed; leadership structure, ultimate purpose, and full membership remain open.

### Metal Knight / Bofoi - STRENGTHEN remote-risk and research model
Metal Knight remotely tests weapons against Elder Centipede, evaluates its extraordinary shell, and foregrounds experimental/resource value. The intervention is real and potentially useful, but the operator's risk exposure remains remote and the research/weapon motive is explicit.

### Hero Association live-capture operation - NEW incentive architecture
Urokodon's alleged medical/regenerative value creates pressure for live capture, while stale threat information and poor management make the assigned B-class team inadequate to the target's changed state. Institutional technology/data are useful but only as current as the system feeding them.

## V12 update — strategic hierarchy, donor leverage, and intelligence collection

### Monster Association — strategy becomes hierarchical
Gyoro Gyoro explicitly performs `戦力` accounting after the multi-city attacks, comparing Hero Association depletion with retained monster main force. V12 therefore strengthens the Monster Association from coordinated organization to **strategically self-monitoring campaign actor**.

The volume also introduces a visible hierarchy around `怪人王` / `オロチ様`. Orochi is presented and addressed as Monster King, while Gyoro Gyoro functions as planner/analyst. This narrows the leadership question but does **not** yet establish the complete hidden authority structure or ultimate purpose.

### Hero Association — donor architecture becomes concrete
The Association board states that Narinki supplies **7% of total Association funding** and immediately feels pressure to prioritize the rescue of his son Waganma. This is stronger than generic corruption rhetoric: the institution has a concrete funding-dependence channel capable of shaping mission priority while `ヒーローが足りない` and information remains incomplete.

Do not generalize one donor case into a claim that every dispatch is purchased or corrupt. V12 instead adds donor leverage as a distinct structural vulnerability alongside responder scarcity and information lag.

### Genos / Kuseno — adaptive engineering memory
The prepared adhesive counter against Awakened Cockroach embodies Kuseno's earlier advice to accept domains in which the opponent is superior. Technology here is compensatory design: it can change a matchup rather than merely add raw output.

### Drive Knight — hidden-actor dossier materially expands
Drive Knight explicitly says that endlessly killing local `コバエ` is insufficient and seeks the `発生源`. He preserves an informant and threatens `拷問`, offering transformation-based pain to extract `洗いざらい` all known information. The operational policy is calm, information-seeking, and coercive.

This materially strengthens Drive Knight as an independent actor but does **not** resolve the V07 warning about Metal Knight, his ultimate allegiance, or whether the information he provides is complete/truthful.

### Child Emperor — analysis as network service
Child Emperor extracts poison information and routes it toward antiserum preparation even while his own equipment is constrained. Analytical value can therefore persist after local combat dominance fails.

### King — information effect without technology
King's reputation deters crime and reshapes assassin decision-making despite lacking the attributed combat ability. This belongs primarily in the satire/public-narrative ledger, but organizationally it is a useful control case: **belief itself can become an operational resource**.

## V13 update — Monster Association as conversion/recruitment and symbolic-targeting system

### Scalable production claim

### Amai Mask — symbolic/media targeting
Concert attackers target Amai partly because his image supports Hero Association public legitimacy. Monster Association strategy now demonstrably includes attacks on **social-symbolic infrastructure**, not only physical combat assets.

### Current organization model through V13
The Monster Association now has direct evidence for:
- synchronized attack planning;
- surveillance / force accounting;
- hierarchy (`怪人王`, `オロチ様`);
- recruitment;
- scalable creation/conversion claims;

Complete leadership truth and ultimate objective remain OPEN.

## V14 update — Monster Association operational doctrine becomes explicit

V14 explicitly enumerates three functions of the current Monster Association campaign:
1. broad monster rampage to make humans understand/fear the Association's threat;
2. abduct a person with hostage value to the Hero Association;
3. monsterize promising humans and bring them onto the Association's side.

This converts earlier inferred coordination into direct organizational doctrine.

### Violent publicity — STRENGTHEN
Gouketsu describes the distributed city attacks as headquarters-ordered `宣伝活動`. The campaign is therefore partly public signaling/terror advertising rather than indiscriminate violence alone.

### Human-capital conversion — STRENGTHEN
The Super Fight operation demonstrates deliberate acquisition of external human skill through Monster Cells. Recruitment, transformation, and strategic force expansion are one connected organizational process, while individual consent histories vary.

### Command and extraction discipline — STRENGTHEN
Gouketsu follows recall rather than remaining for unlimited combat. The organization manages exposure, recognizes that aggregated S-class response can become troublesome, and extracts useful assets after objectives are achieved.

### Hostage operation — STRENGTHEN elite-leverage model
The captured executive-linked child is strategically valuable because of institutional leverage, not merely as a random civilian victim. Exact demands remain OPEN at V14.

### Higher purpose — PRESERVE OPEN
V14 deepens operational means but does not reveal the content of the `higher stage` identified in V13. Do not collapse current campaign objectives into the organization's ultimate purpose.
