# One Punch Man — Hero Character State Ledger


## Genos — V01 baseline


### Role
Cyborg combatant pursuing strength and an unresolved personal mission; not yet modeled here through later institutional knowledge.


### Desire / priorities
- increase power/capability;
- understand anomalous superior strength;
- pursue his underlying mission with extreme commitment.


### Perception / cognition
- highly analytical in combat;
- updates his model when direct evidence contradicts expectation;
- reacts to Saitama's strength with investigation and apprenticeship rather than denial.


### Decision heuristics
- rapid technical escalation against severe threats;
- very high bodily risk tolerance;
- self-destruction is acceptable when judged necessary to stop a threat;
- after observing Saitama, actively seeks instruction rather than preserving status pride.


### Social behavior
- information-dense and register-sensitive;
- persistent;
- becomes deferential toward Saitama after capability reclassification;
- assigns the relationship label `先生` before Saitama has fully chosen the role.


### Japanese voice
- masculine self-reference with `俺`;
- serious, explicit, explanatory;
- plain/rough tactical language toward enemies and in combat judgment;
- polite/deferential request forms toward Saitama after capability reclassification;
- uses `先生` immediately and attempts `師匠` when Saitama rejects that first label;
- much higher information density than Saitama, including extended causally ordered autobiography.

After hearing about Sonic, Genos calmly offers: `まぁ先生がお困りなら俺が消しますが` (`image:0180`). Elimination is framed as practical help, not rage.

## V04 additions

### Genos — V04 delta

**Independent civic duty — STRENGTHEN strongly.** Genos answers the Dragon-level S-class meteor summons independently of Saitama and remains engaged when the problem appears nearly impossible.

**Secondary-effect cognition — NEW.** He explicitly asks whether destroying the meteor could itself produce disastrous fragmentation (`OPM|V04|chapter:21|image:0053`). Target destruction is not equivalent to problem resolution in his reasoning.

**Self-regulation — NEW.** `まあ 落ち着け` occurs inside escalating causal analysis; Genos can notice his own destabilization and attempt regulation rather than simply escalating impulsively.

**Cooperation policy — NEW.** He asks Metal Knight to cooperate toward the shared civic threat. Strength orientation does not imply automatic rivalry with other elite heroes.

**Self-sacrifice — STRENGTHEN.** He is willing to commit his deepest available energy reserve despite uncertain success and bodily cost.

**Bang interaction — NEW.** He accepts senior crisis advice but analytically parses it rather than surrendering judgment. This broadens his relationship evidence beyond Saitama.

## V05 additions

V05 materially broadens Genos outside the master-disciple axis.
- independently responds to the Deep Sea King crisis as an S-class hero;
- can materially pressure the monster even without final victory;

### Mumen Rider — V05 transition `insufficient -> emerging`

### Amai Mask — V05 transition `insufficient -> emerging`

**Boundary control:** V05 does not yet support a blanket claim that Amai personally gatekeeps every lower-class promotion. Readiness **emerging**.

### Shelter defenders — bounded courage evidence
Allback-man, Jet Nice Guy, Bunbunman, Sneck, Lightning Max and other defenders contribute to the repeated `arrival -> hope -> overmatch` structure. Preserve their courage/response evidence proportionally; do not promote one-scene actors to reconstructive readiness without broader recurrence.

### V05 hero-modeling consequence
Heroism now clearly separates **capacity, expected success, duty commitment, public recognition, and institutional reward**. Mumen Rider is the strongest boundary case: almost no victory probability and still a coherent hero decision policy.

Professional classification is therefore neither a perfect capability sensor nor a complete moral/role-fit sensor.

## V09 update

### Fubuki — first durable model
- **Goal:** preserve/expand the Fubuki Group and her B1 position rather than advance into a hierarchy she believes contains hard ceilings above her.
- **Method:** recruitment, intimidation, rank control, and genuine group organization coexist; the group is neither merely friendship nor merely disposable muscle.
