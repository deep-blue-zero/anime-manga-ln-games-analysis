# One Punch Man — Independent / Civilian Character State Ledger

## Responsibility

Own durable behavioral state for recurring people who are not currently best modeled inside the registered-hero or monster/antagonist ledgers. Do not create durable models from incidental civilians merely because they appear in one scene.

## Charanko — V07 baseline `emerging`

### Role / boundary
Bang's martial-arts student. At the V07 boundary he is not a registered professional hero and is analytically owned here rather than in the hero ledger.

### Motivational state
- reports joining Bang's school about a year earlier because he wanted to become popular;
- does not present strength or martial self-cultivation as an original sacred goal;
- becomes discouraged because he does not feel substantially stronger;
- skips training for roughly two weeks and contemplates quitting;
- after discovering that all other students have left, reframes being the final remaining student as effectively becoming the top/first disciple and becomes motivated again.

### Decision heuristic
`effort feels unrewarding / inconvenience rises -> disengagement temptation`; `status opportunity becomes salient -> renewed effort`.

This is not yet enough to conclude that status is his only durable motive. It is the strongest current low-stakes pattern.

### Relationship: Charanko -> Bang
- recognizes Bang as master and continues returning despite weak intrinsic martial motivation;
- is willing to hear unflattering practical facts about the collapsed dojo;
- responds to the changed social structure by recommitting rather than leaving immediately.

### Relationship: Bang -> Charanko
- communicates the departure of the other students plainly;
- does not romanticize Charanko's sudden enthusiasm;
- tolerates the imperfect motive without performing a grand corrective lecture in the extra.

### Japanese/voice notes
Current evidence is conversational and youthful/ordinary rather than formalized martial-arts rhetoric. Preserve the gap between `disciple role` and `disciple-like ascetic motivation`.

## V18 update — Tareo, Waganma, and civilian response to monster war

### Tareo — STRENGTHEN emerging
Tareo apologizes for fleeing, interprets Garou's bullet protection as evidence that Garou is `正義の味方`, and thereby threatens Garou's preferred self-label. During captivity he tries to negotiate inclusion in Waganma's expected rescue, contributes public-news knowledge about defeated heroes, and repeatedly says he wants to go home. His model is no longer only passive victimhood: he observes, classifies, negotiates, fears, and maintains independent judgments.

### Waganma — NEW emerging
Waganma has internalized elite rescue entitlement. He says his father is important in the Hero Association, expects heroes to prioritize him, agrees Tareo can be rescued too only while declaring himself priority, then names Tareo his subordinate and demands future food. His confidence is partly reputation-dependent and becomes unstable when Tareo recalls hero defeats.

### Human monster-capitulation movement — NEW civic evidence
The `怪人崇拝党` publicly argues that humans should accommodate stronger monsters to survive. This is a human political response to fear, not Monster Association membership and not Garou's ideology. It demonstrates that monster pressure produces secondary social/political adaptation before the formal raid.

## V19 civilian update - rescue visibility, donor pressure, and child knowledge

### Tareo - STRENGTHEN emerging
V19 makes institutional invisibility explicit: Tareo is told the Hero Association knows about the other hostage but does not know he exists. This is different from a conscious decision not to rescue him. His survival therefore depends on actors outside the official mission model, most immediately Garou.

Tareo continues to read Garou by conduct rather than label, asks why Garou came, reports Garou's visible growth during combat, and reveals the existence of the second hostage. He is increasingly an information source and moral witness rather than a passive protected object.

### Narinki / Waganma rescue system - STRENGTHEN institutional-civilian boundary
Narinki's trust in the Association has been damaged by the destruction of his privately equipped rescue team. He offers additional money, while S-Class dialogue rejects money as a meaningful battlefield motive. Preserve both donor leverage at the organizational level and the evidence that individual elite heroes are not simply purchasable.

### Z-City civilian environment - NEW contextual state
The ghost-town reputation now has visible behavioral effects on ordinary movement and fear. Civilian absence is part of the operational environment, not just atmospheric labeling.
