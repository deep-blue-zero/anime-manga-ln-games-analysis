# One Punch Man — Independent / Civilian Character State Ledger

## Responsibility

Own durable behavioral state for recurring people who are not currently best modeled inside the registered-hero or monster/antagonist ledgers. Do not create durable models from incidental civilians merely because they appear in one scene.

## Charanko — V07 baseline `emerging`

### Role / boundary
Bang's martial-arts student. At the V07 boundary he is not a registered professional hero and is analytically owned here rather than in the hero ledger.

### Motivational state
- reports joining Bang's school about a year earlier because he wanted to become popular;
- does not present strength or martial self-cultivation as an original sacred goal;
- becomes discouraged because he does not feel substantially stronger;
- skips training for roughly two weeks and contemplates quitting;
- after discovering that all other students have left, reframes being the final remaining student as effectively becoming the top/first disciple and becomes motivated again.

### Decision heuristic
`effort feels unrewarding / inconvenience rises -> disengagement temptation`; `status opportunity becomes salient -> renewed effort`.

This is not yet enough to conclude that status is his only durable motive. It is the strongest current low-stakes pattern.

### Relationship: Charanko -> Bang
- recognizes Bang as master and continues returning despite weak intrinsic martial motivation;
- is willing to hear unflattering practical facts about the collapsed dojo;
- responds to the changed social structure by recommitting rather than leaving immediately.

### Relationship: Bang -> Charanko
- communicates the departure of the other students plainly;
- does not romanticize Charanko's sudden enthusiasm;
- tolerates the imperfect motive without performing a grand corrective lecture in the extra.

### Japanese/voice notes
Current evidence is conversational and youthful/ordinary rather than formalized martial-arts rhetoric. Preserve the gap between `disciple role` and `disciple-like ascetic motivation`.

## Non-instantiation rule at V07

The police chief, McCoy, the injured officer, and other `カツ丼` personnel carry high-value institutional evidence but do not yet have enough recurrence to justify durable individual character models. Their evidence belongs primarily in the heroism/institution and public-narrative ledgers until recurrence warrants promotion.

## V08 boundary note — no new durable independent/civilian model

`迷い猫` contains useful civilian/Association-employee behavior, but neither the lost-cat child nor the employee who secretly raised Grizz-Meow has enough recurrence or breadth for a durable character model. Their evidence is routed to the V08 deep reading and institution/monster ledgers instead.

Charanko remains the only populated independent/civilian state in this ledger at V08 and is strengthened indirectly by `海老`; his detailed continuation is also reflected in the hero/relationship infrastructure where dojo social behavior intersects the professional-hero cast.

## V10 update — Tareo enters; Charanko ordinary training bridge

### Tareo — **new `emerging` durable civilian model**
- strong hero fandom and active use of the Hero Almanac;
- low confidence in his own athletic/social position;
- ordinary, relatively unguarded conversation with Garou before understanding Garou's broader significance;
- lends Garou the Almanac, unintentionally supplying hero-target information;
- obeys Garou's immediate instruction to run home when a real monster emergency sounds.

Tareo is promoted to a durable civilian model because he now has stable preferences, vulnerability, repeated interaction, information asymmetry, and a relationship-state consequence. Do not yet model him from later Garou/Tareo developments.

### Charanko — remains `emerging`; practical bridge strengthened
Injury removes Charanko from Super Fight, but his registered entry becomes Saitama's access route. He explains ordinary martial training, basic forms, and the normal novice progression that Saitama never underwent, giving the corpus a useful “conventional training” comparator. He remains too narrow for `moderate`.

## V11 independent / civilian update

### Zenko - enters `emerging`
V07 established Metal Bat's attachment to his sister; V11 gives Zenko direct agency. She enters an active conflict, invokes a previously established promise against violence in front of her, and immediately stops Metal Bat despite his extreme combat activation. She also reacts to his physical condition as a sister rather than as a hero fan or institutional actor.

**Readiness:** `emerging`. The relationship-specific norm is highly diagnostic, but broader ordinary-life and independent-context evidence remains limited.

### Suiryu - enters `emerging`
The Super Fight material gives Suiryu a coherent bounded policy: relaxed confidence, prize/fun motivation, appetite for strong opponents, calm response to Bakuzan's killing intent, and immediate attention to Saitama's anomalously fast result. This is enough to begin durable tracking but not enough for `moderate`.

### Bomb - watch / `insufficient`
V11 directly establishes Bomb as Bang's older brother and a coordinated martial partner in the search for Garou. The evidence is important relationally but still too narrow for a broad standalone behavioral model.

### Tareo - remains `emerging`
No V11 material changes the durable V10 civilian model enough to justify a tier move.

## V15 update — Tareo

### Tareo — `emerging`, strengthened

V15 supplies a new ordinary social-pressure context independent of the earlier monster/hero information exchange. Other children exclude Tareo from their play, he returns distressed to the injured Garou, and he remains willing to converse with Garou despite recognizing him as the strange monster-loving adult from before.

Garou's reaction is relationally important but should not erase Tareo's own state. Tareo's vulnerability is now not only fear of dangerous adults/monsters; it includes peer humiliation, low social standing and desire for acceptance. His return to Garou makes the relationship behaviorally durable across multiple encounters.

**Knowledge boundary:** Tareo still does not possess the reader's full model of Garou's hero-hunting activity or the approaching hero operation.

**Open:** whether Garou's promised `周りにバカにされない方法` would help, harm, or simply project Garou's own grievance onto Tareo. V15 cuts off before the lesson is delivered.

Locators: `OPM|V15|chapter:80|images:0170-0174|anchor:Tareo exclusion distress and Garou proto-mentoring`.

## V16 update — Tareo

### Tareo — `emerging`, strengthened
- supplies Garou with the Hero Encyclopedia, becoming an active information participant rather than only a recipient of protection;
- remains physically vulnerable inside the hut while the hero encirclement unfolds;
- his presence materially changes Garou's behavior: Garou warns Death Gatling about collateral risk and redirects the barrage away from him;
- continued trust/approach toward Garou should not be generalized into a broad civilian view of Garou.

**Modeling implication:** Tareo is a relationship-specific trigger for Garou's protective behavior and social self-projection. His own independent behavioral breadth remains too narrow for promotion beyond `emerging`.

## V17 independent / civilian update

### Narinki -- emerging stakeholder model
- Paternal fear over Waganma combines with donor/status leverage: he demands priority rescue and directly pressures Hero Association leadership.
- When institutional response feels too slow, he dispatches a named private force equipped with special-route battle suits that he believes outperform ordinary professional heroes.
- The private intervention fails inside the Monster Association base, adding a confidence/operational-knowledge failure state.
- Preserve emotional motive and institutional leverage together; do not reduce him to either caring father or corrupt donor alone.

### Waganma
- Remains a hostage/object of rescue politics at the V17 boundary. Direct behavioral evidence remains insufficient for a broad model.

### Narinki private squad
- Named, technologically equipped, disciplined enough to deploy on command, but rapidly outmatched by unfamiliar base threats.
- Keep individual members insufficient unless later material supplies distinct behavioral breadth.


## V18 update — Tareo, Waganma, and civilian response to monster war

### Tareo — STRENGTHEN emerging
Tareo apologizes for fleeing, interprets Garou's bullet protection as evidence that Garou is `正義の味方`, and thereby threatens Garou's preferred self-label. During captivity he tries to negotiate inclusion in Waganma's expected rescue, contributes public-news knowledge about defeated heroes, and repeatedly says he wants to go home. His model is no longer only passive victimhood: he observes, classifies, negotiates, fears, and maintains independent judgments.

### Waganma — NEW emerging
Waganma has internalized elite rescue entitlement. He says his father is important in the Hero Association, expects heroes to prioritize him, agrees Tareo can be rescued too only while declaring himself priority, then names Tareo his subordinate and demands future food. His confidence is partly reputation-dependent and becomes unstable when Tareo recalls hero defeats.

### Human monster-capitulation movement — NEW civic evidence
The `怪人崇拝党` publicly argues that humans should accommodate stronger monsters to survive. This is a human political response to fear, not Monster Association membership and not Garou's ideology. It demonstrates that monster pressure produces secondary social/political adaptation before the formal raid.

## V19 civilian update - rescue visibility, donor pressure, and child knowledge

### Tareo - STRENGTHEN emerging
V19 makes institutional invisibility explicit: Tareo is told the Hero Association knows about the other hostage but does not know he exists. This is different from a conscious decision not to rescue him. His survival therefore depends on actors outside the official mission model, most immediately Garou.

Tareo continues to read Garou by conduct rather than label, asks why Garou came, reports Garou's visible growth during combat, and reveals the existence of the second hostage. He is increasingly an information source and moral witness rather than a passive protected object.

### Narinki / Waganma rescue system - STRENGTHEN institutional-civilian boundary
Narinki's trust in the Association has been damaged by the destruction of his privately equipped rescue team. He offers additional money, while S-Class dialogue rejects money as a meaningful battlefield motive. Preserve both donor leverage at the organizational level and the evidence that individual elite heroes are not simply purchasable.

### Z-City civilian environment - NEW contextual state
The ghost-town reputation now has visible behavioral effects on ordinary movement and fear. Civilian absence is part of the operational environment, not just atmospheric labeling.

## V21 civilian/hostage delta

### Waganma — STRENGTHEN emerging
V21 gives sustained rescue-state evidence: fear, fatigue, entitlement toward an S-Class rescuer, curiosity about Child Emperor's devices, and a paternal money-for-service worldview. Crucially, he also supplies useful `ドンカンバード` media knowledge that enables Child Emperor to hypothesize Phoenix Man's resurrection mechanism. Preserve both privilege-shaped demands and genuine informational contribution; neither “spoiled rich kid” nor “passive victim” is sufficient alone.


## V22 independent/civilian-state delta

### Waganma — STRENGTHEN hostage/information role
Waganma remains under Child Emperor's protection and supplies useful ordinary-media knowledge about `ドンカンバード`. Near the extraction boundary, Child Emperor asks whether anyone else was captured and Waganma indicates that another person was involved before escape is prioritized. Record the additional-captive lead as OPEN; do not import the later identity.

### Narinki private rescue squad — STRENGTHEN bounded recovered-victim state
The squad reappears under Do-S control. The Atomic Samurai disciples recognize them as humans who entered for the same hostage-rescue objective and preserve them through the encounter. After control breaks they are conscious and grateful. This is important negative evidence against treating coercive enemy alignment as equivalent to voluntary monsterhood.

## V23 independent/civilian-state delta

### Waganma — STRENGTHEN emerging through shame disclosure
V23 corrects a V1 chronology/relationship error. Waganma does **not** meet or apologize directly to Tareo. He tells Child Emperor that Tareo shared his cell and was taken away, then admits he had been too afraid of being scolded to report the second hostage earlier. His `ごめんよ` belongs to that confession. Child Emperor thanks him for telling the truth.

This is meaningful movement from entitlement/passivity toward self-implicating disclosure under shame, but it remains crisis-concentrated. Readiness stays **emerging**.

### Narinki private rescue squad — STRENGTHEN veteran/professional perspective
Freed from Do-S's control, members describe experience with ugly human violence and reject naive moral idealism from a veteran-survival perspective. Their statements are **speaker philosophy**, not narrator endorsement that only combat veterans can judge justice. They continue operating alongside the hero/disciple rescue effort, preserving them as private-sector rescue actors rather than monsters by association.

## V25 independent/civilian-state delta

### Waganma — STRENGTHEN emerging through consequence recognition
During the surface evacuation Waganma notices that Food Battler Futoshi, who had reassured/protected him, was in fact cut and injured. This is a small but useful continuation after V23's shame disclosure: the rescued child increasingly registers the bodily cost paid by others rather than treating protection as frictionless service. The datum is too narrow for a tier promotion.

### Wounded support-hero evacuation — bounded institutional/civilian interface
V25 keeps the rescued child and wounded heroes inside the same logistics problem. Survival depends on transport, food, triage, information, and force routing rather than a single victor. Preserve this as environmental/role evidence rather than inventing individual civilian models where characterization is insufficient.

## V26 independent/civilian-state delta

### Tareo — STRENGTHEN / RESOLVED rescue-state question
Tareo is alive and encounters King underground. He immediately treats King as rescue/safety and interprets King's visible fear/stress through the public King legend. King then escorts him toward an elevator route. This resolves V25's open location/survival question without importing later endpoint status.

### Civilian accounting as tactical constraint
Tatsumaki explicitly distinguishes the named hostage from a second child and demands current location information while fighting Psykos. V26 therefore strengthens the rule that noncombatant location is a direct determinant of available hero force, not background sentiment.
