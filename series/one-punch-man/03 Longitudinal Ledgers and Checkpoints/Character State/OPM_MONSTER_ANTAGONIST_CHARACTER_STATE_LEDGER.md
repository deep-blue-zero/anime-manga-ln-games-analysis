## V10 Garou update — human ontology, adaptive technique, child channel, and failed identity transmission

Mumen Rider explicitly calls Garou `人間だった` and then `怪人を名乗る人間`. At V10 Garou remains bodily/socially a dangerous human using `怪人` as a self-authored identity and program. No later transformation state is admitted backward.

Against Golden Ball and Spring Mustachio, Garou survives not merely through toughness. He reads ricochet geometry, timing, weapon interaction, and coordinated pressure, gets wounded, and adapts. `流水岩砕拳` and opponent-modeling remain central to his effective strength.

Garou borrows Tareo's Hero Almanac and speaks with him in an ordinary rough register. During a real monster emergency he orders Tareo to run home immediately. This is direct evidence that monster self-authorship does not imply universal hostility toward civilians.

### Hero Almanac as targeting infrastructure — NEW
Garou uses Tareo's book to learn about Watchdog Man, including fixed territory and rank, and becomes interested in testing that target. Information seeking is therefore part of the hero-hunting program rather than mere reactive violence.

### First Saitama contact — NEW asymmetric relation boundary
Garou attacks Saitama under the assumption that a hero has approached him; Saitama does not recognize Garou or the Hero Hunter identity and knocks him out as an ordinary street nuisance. Garou's self-authored symbolic role fails to transmit to the strongest person he has encountered.

### V10 readiness

## V13 update — Monster Cells, Haragiri, and scalable recruitment

### Haragiri — enters `emerging`
Haragiri begins as a master swordsman who prioritizes mutual elite strength refinement, then reveals that the Monster Association recruited him with `怪人細胞`. He says he joined because he was willing to exchange `人間を辞める` for greater strength. After visible transformation he states `ワシはもう人間じゃない`, attempts to coerce the other swordmasters into conversion, and believes his upgraded speed makes four-on-one victory possible.

Atomic Samurai kills him before the transformed advantage can be realized.

Current bounded model:
`strength-first martial identity -> voluntary conversion for greater power -> explicit nonhuman self-identification -> organization-aligned coercion -> post-upgrade overconfidence -> preemptive defeat`.

**Readiness: emerging.** Dense single-cluster evidence; no longitudinal/private breadth.

### Garou — PRESERVE separation from biological/organizational monsterization
V13 supplies a clean counterexample against retroactive category collapse. Haragiri aligns body transformation, monster identity, Monster Association membership, and monster-aligned coercive conduct. Garou through V12 aligns only some of these axes: human body observed, self-authored monster identity, explicit refusal of Monster Association affiliation, severe hero violence plus selective civilian care.

Do not rewrite Garou as Monster-Cell transformed or organizationally aligned.

### Gyoro Gyoro — STRENGTHEN strategic purpose model
Gyoro Gyoro explicitly distinguishes monsters' `根源的欲求` for violence from `我々の目的`, which is said to aim at another/higher stage. This strengthens the planner/strategist model and rejects a purely instinctual account of the Association. Exact higher purpose remains OPEN.

### Orochi — STRENGTHEN production/resource role
Orochi responds to hero success with `怪人など いくらでも作れる`, treating monster creation as a scalable strategic resource. V13 does not establish whether he personally creates every Monster Cell or every monster.

### Gouketsu — enters `insufficient` / high-priority watch

## V14 update — Gouketsu, Bakuzan, and coercive strength hierarchy

### Gouketsu — `insufficient -> emerging`
V14 supplies the breadth missing at V13. Gouketsu recounts defeat/capture as a human martial artist, accepts monsterization when offered a route beyond human limits, acts as a Monster Association recruiter, enforces conversion through death threats, evaluates successful converts, obeys strategic recall, and explains the organization's temporary publicity operation and internal strength hierarchy.

Current bounded model: `strength ambition after defeat -> accept transformation -> organization loyalty -> coercive recruitment -> dominance ritual -> strategic obedience/extraction`.

**Readiness: emerging.** The evidence is dense but still one operation with no private or longitudinal breadth.

### Bakuzan — enters `emerging`
Before conversion Bakuzan admits Suiryu is stronger and still growing, experiences envy, and deliberately chooses Monster Cells. He consumes the remaining cells despite the one-cell instruction; the source does not establish a dose-response rule. After successful transformation he interprets the new state as liberation of a pre-existing desire to torment weaker people and says that desire was why he sought strength.

This is Bakuzan's self-interpretation, not a universal law that monsterization reveals a hidden true self. His model is bounded by ambition, sadistic dominance, comparative resentment, and rapid adaptation to a hierarchy in which Gouketsu still outranks him.

**Readiness: emerging.**

### Gouketsu -> Monster Association — STRENGTHEN organizational agent state
Gouketsu treats the city attacks as headquarters-ordered publicity, carries out martial-artist conversion, recognizes a hierarchy above Bakuzan, and withdraws when instructed rather than pursuing gratuitous battle indefinitely. The organization is constraining monster violence through strategy.

### Monster Cell consent states — STRENGTHEN decomposition
V14 adds multiple pathways into the same observed bodily mechanism: ambition-responsive acceptance after defeat/capture, survival-coerced consumption, and Bakuzan's power-seeking voluntary use. Similar transformed bodies therefore do not imply identical motives or consent histories.

### V14 boundary
Do not use later material to settle Monster Cell origin, dosage effects, the accuracy of Gouketsu's absolute hierarchy claims, or whether Suiryu's hero aspiration persists.
