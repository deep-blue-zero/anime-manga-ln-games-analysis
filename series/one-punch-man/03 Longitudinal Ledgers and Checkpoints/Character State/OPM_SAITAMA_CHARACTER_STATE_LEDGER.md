# One Punch Man — Saitama Character State Ledger


## Baseline boundary: V01


### Role / self-definition
- self-identifies as a hero by personal choice rather than institutional authorization;
- representative line: `趣味でヒーローをやっている者だ` (`OPM|V01|chapter:1|image:0020`).


### Desire hierarchy
1. recover meaningful engagement/intensity;
2. continue acting as the hero he chose to become;
3. maintain ordinary-life comfort and autonomy.


### Self-conception
Plain rather than grandiose. Does not present himself as chosen, elite, or morally exalted despite overwhelming capability.


### Perception / blind spots
- accurately perceives most physical threats as negligible to himself;
- appears less practiced at interpreting persistent social attachment/expectation;
- Genos's adoption of `先生` creates a role Saitama did not anticipate.


### Decision heuristics
- direct intervention against obvious threats;
- minimal deliberation when capability gap is extreme;
- higher activation when something creates genuine friction, even if objectively trivial;
- improvises socially rather than following a pre-existing mentor doctrine.


### Stress and context
- **physical danger:** little observed fear in present-day state;
- **credible resistance (dream):** strong positive activation/excitement;
- **minor uncontrollable irritation:** mosquito produces disproportionate frustration;
- **social overload:** impatience with Genos's long explanation;
- **pre-hero mortal danger:** fear and determination coexist;
- **childhood:** ordinary peer vulnerability and embarrassment remain visible.


### Japanese voice
- adult/current baseline: plain, direct, colloquial masculine speech with low self-mythologizing;
- uses familiar `お前` casually as well as rougher forms under irritation;
- contractions/evaluative roughness include `〜てねー`, `〜やがる`, and `〜てんな`;
- can escalate to `テメー`, insults, and shouted imperatives when activated;
- conversational bandwidth is low: strongly prefers compressed explanation;
- twelve-year-old school Saitama uses context-appropriate `です/ます` politeness to a teacher, so adult roughness is contextual/developed rather than invariant.

## V02 cumulative delta


### Recognition / social legibility — REVISE
V01 correctly found that fame was not established as the motive that created Saitama's hero identity. V02 nevertheless makes recognition a real current desire. Preserve distinct components:


- **identity legibility:** Paradiser baldness makes him fear being classified as a villain (`OPM|V02|chapter:12|image:0106`);
- **attribution:** he objects that he has been heroing and `割と活躍してる` but remains unknown (`image:0164`);
- **name recognition:** explicitly identifies `知名度が低い` as a major problem (`image:0181`);
- **audience/feedback:** wonders why more people do not know his existence and why he has few fans (`images:0182-0183`);
- **meaning feedback:** training-year extra states that gratitude makes it easy to feel he was useful and gives him fulfillment (`extra:じぶんみがき|image:0189`).


Current model: Saitama wants his actions to be socially legible and consequential to others. Celebrity prestige, rank, and money are not yet established as the dominant end.


### Strength philosophy — STRENGTHEN
Saitama sincerely presents persistence and self-change as the basis of strength: `自分で変われるのが人間の強さだ!` (`image:0075`). This is his **subjective explanatory model**. Objective power causation remains OPEN because Genos explicitly judges ordinary training insufficient to explain the observed result.


### Ethics / restraint — STRENGTHEN
- releases Hammerhead after surrender with the economical `行け` (`image:0158`);
- recognizes Hammerhead's social disappointment as a path he himself might have taken: `俺も一歩間違えばアイツみたいになってたのかな` (`image:0159`);
- explicitly attempts `寸止め` against Sonic and apologizes for accidental contact (`image:0176`);
- answers Sonic's death vow with `ああ! 頑張れよ!` rather than reciprocal hostility (`image:0179`).


Current tendency: direct violence against active threats coexists with restraint, surrender sensitivity, and unusually non-theatrical treatment of human rivals.


### Perception / cognition — STRENGTHEN and OPEN
- accurately strips Sonic's pride justification to the desire to test technique (`image:0167`);
- recognizes Hammerhead as an alternate failed trajectory from social alienation (`image:0159`);
- remains strikingly uninformed about the public institution defining professional hero status (`images:0183-0185`).


Do not model Saitama as globally socially obtuse. His blind spots are selective.


### Ordinary-life salience — STRENGTHEN
The supermarket sale produces greater visible urgency than Carnage Kabuto's threat: `今日がスーパーの特売日じゃねーか` (`image:0088`). Ordinary deadlines/scarcity remain high-value predictors of activation.


### Hero identity as embodied practice — STRENGTHEN
`じぶんみがき` shows training-year Saitama repeatedly helping people while training. By day 300, rescue movement has become reflexive enough that narration describes him as moving in accordance with his image of a hero (`images:0190-0194`). Chosen role becomes habit, perception, and body.


### Teacher role — REVISE upward
Saitama now actively gives a training doctrine, tolerates Genos's disagreement, and finally says `一緒に登録してくれたら弟子にしてやるから!` (`image:0186`). The master-disciple role is no longer only an imposed nuisance; Saitama can invoke it instrumentally and relationally.


### Japanese voice — V02 additions
- plain didactic register while teaching;
- rough activation around ordinary-life stakes and identity threat;
- terse mercy outwardly paired with more nuanced internal reflection;
- casual motive-reading (`嘘つけ`) and casual encouragement (`頑張れよ`) toward Sonic;
- recognition language remains understated rather than heroic-grandiose (`割と活躍してる`).

## V03 cumulative delta


### Professional hero identity — STRENGTHEN / REVISE
- passes the Hero Association exam at 71 points and enters C-class despite record-breaking physical performance;
- cares enough about registered status to panic when Genos explains the C-class inactivity rule;
- does **not** equate rank with the hero he originally wanted to become: `なんか俺がなりたかったヒーローと違う気が……` (`OPM|V03|chapter:16|image:0028`);
- current model: wants legibility/credential access while retaining a self-authored hero ideal distinct from institutional rank.


### Institutional stress regime — NEW
C-class activity pressure produces visible urgency far exceeding most physical threats. Add **credential/professional survival** as a distinct stress context rather than treating it as combat anxiety.


### Mentorship — REVISE upward / QUALIFY doctrine
Behavioral responsibility is now substantial:
- accepts Genos's serious spar;
- withholds contact on a landscape-destroying punch;

But private monologue establishes pedagogical insecurity (`俺がジェノスに教える事なんてなに一つねーのに`) and shows that parts of the mental-training/top-ten assignment are improvised. Do not model Saitama as a confident doctrine-builder.

Genos's cohabitation request produces a clear conditional boundary:


`uncompensated intrusion -> refusal`  
`credible rent payment -> rapid acceptance + toothbrush logistics`.

### Strength philosophy applied to another person — STRENGTHEN

## V04 update — disaster responsibility, public blame, and motive authority

### Hero self-authorship — STRENGTHEN strongly
During the Dragon-level meteor emergency, Saitama answers Bang's identity question with `俺はヒーローをやっている者だ` (`OPM|V04|chapter:21|image:0066`). After professional registration, he still frames hero as an activity he performs rather than an identity made valid by rank.

### Recognition — REVISE / differentiate
V02-V03 established that Saitama wants social legibility, correct attribution, recognition, and even fans. V04 explicitly limits what that desire means. Under crowd hostility he states:

`俺はテメェらの評価が欲しくてヒーローやってんじゃねぇからな` / `俺がやりたくてやってんだ` (`chapter:22|image:0126`).

Current model: **recognition is desired feedback, not the motive authority for hero action.**

### Public-scrutiny stress regime — NEW
False collective blame produces the strongest roughening yet: `うるせぇ`, `だまれ`, `テメェら`, `ボケ`, `～じゃねぇ`. He first permits confrontation (`文句がありゃ言ってみろ 聞いてやる`) and then rejects the causal premise rather than appeasing the crowd.

### Care / relationship responsibility — STRENGTHEN
Seeing Genos depleted during the meteor crisis, Saitama tells Bang `じいさん こいつ任せるぞ` and takes over the catastrophic task. Concern is operational rather than verbally sentimental.

### Force restraint — STRENGTHEN
Even while angry at the Tanktop brothers' manipulation, Saitama incapacitates human peer heroes rather than applying monster-level lethal force.

### Catastrophe cognition — NEW caution
Saitama decisively removes the primary meteor threat but does not display Genos's pre-action secondary-effects analysis. After fragmentation he rapidly treats the primary problem as resolved despite citywide collateral. Preserve this as a cognition contrast, not a global irresponsibility trait.

### Low-stakes activation — STRENGTHEN
The short omake again makes food/shopping/sale information immediately salient. Across V01-V04, ordinary scarcity/timing reliably activates Saitama more than many combat threats.

## V05 update — impossible duty, reputational sacrifice, and material scarcity

### Recognition hierarchy — REVISE / STRENGTHEN
V02 established that recognition/attribution matters; V04 established that public evaluation is not the motive authority for hero work. V05 supplies the strongest test yet: after killing Deep Sea King, Saitama deliberately performs the false role of a lucky, credit-stealing rookie so the crowd will not use his effortless success to erase the value of the heroes who were defeated before he arrived (`OPM|V05|chapter:28|images:0149-0155`).

Current formulation: **Saitama wants recognition but can intentionally surrender it when keeping it would devalue other people's meaningful heroic action.** This is strategic reputational self-sacrifice, not generic indifference or humility.

### Situational social strategy — REVISE global-obliviousness risk
Saitama recognizes a public-narrative problem, predicts how a deliberately repellent persona will redirect the crowd, performs that persona, and accepts the reputational cost. Do not model him as globally incapable of reading social dynamics. His ordinary convention-blindness coexists with acute situational social intelligence when a salient value is threatened.

### Public speech may be strategically nonliteral — NEW reconstruction control
At `images:0151-0154`, surface claims about laziness, luck, credit theft, and exploiting injured heroes contradict his observed motive and redirect attention toward the injured. Future reconstructions must not equate Saitama's outward line with sincere self-description when context supports deliberate performance.

### Care for defeated heroes — STRENGTHEN
`倒れたヒーロー達をちゃんと看てやれよ` directs the crowd toward the injured heroes. He then masks that concern as self-interest (`俺が利用できなくなるだろ`). Care can therefore appear through practical redirection while affective motive remains concealed.

### Material scarcity — STRENGTHEN strongly
`買えないモノ` establishes direct financial constraint: Saitama has almost no money available, counts coins, and cannot afford a vending-machine drink he wants. Bargain/sale sensitivity is therefore grounded in real scarcity, not merely comic frugality.

### Gratitude / compensation ethics — NEW low-stakes atom
After helping a child, Saitama refuses the child's allowance despite his own scarcity: `お前にやる` / `もっと大事に使え` (`extra:買えないモノ|image:0206`). Need does not automatically justify monetizing gratitude.

### Genos mundane-care response — STRENGTHEN relationship baseline
Saitama responds positively when Genos produces the drink he had earlier noticed Saitama looking at, then bluntly evaluates the result when it tastes bad. He accepts practical care without ceremonializing it.

## V06 update — planetary threat, professional self-correction, adversarial inference, and nonphysical limits

### Hero identity — STRENGTHEN / integrate professional layer
Saitama begins his familiar introduction to Boros with `趣味で…` and then explicitly repairs it to `プロのヒーローをやってるサイタマという者だ` (`OPM|V06|chapter:34|image:0167`). The hobby-origin identity has not vanished; it remains habitual. But registered professional status is now consciously integrated into his self-description.

**Current formulation:** self-authored hero identity precedes the Association and continues to authorize action; professional registration is an added social/institutional layer rather than a replacement source of motive.

### Reciprocity / challenge deprivation — STRENGTHEN with ethical contrast
Boros provides the first explicit experiential mirror whose stated problem closely resembles Saitama's: overwhelming strength has deprived ordinary combat of stimulation, so he seeks a foretold opponent who can fight evenly. Saitama understands the premise but rejects Boros's response: `退屈な人生に刺激が欲しくて他の星を襲うなんてOLでも考えねぇぞ`.

**Model consequence:** challenge deprivation is a genuine Saitama problem, but it does not predict predatory entitlement. Shared affective deprivation must be separated from ethical action policy.

### Interest activation under unprecedented threat — STRENGTHEN
After hearing that Shibabawa used unprecedented wording indicating Earth itself may be in danger, Saitama says `来て良かった`. This is best modeled as activation by possible unprecedented challenge, not pleasure at civilian danger. Novelty/credible resistance remains a high-salience engagement trigger.

### Social/tactical inference — STRENGTHEN
When Geryuganshoop tells him how to leave the ship, Saitama deliberately goes the opposite way (`じゃあ左に進もうっと`) because the alien's reaction reveals strategic value. This repeats V05's correction against global social-obliviousness: Saitama can read incentives/reactions acutely when the problem is salient and concrete.

### Status/rank insensitivity — STRENGTHEN
Tatsumaki treats his presence among S-class as status trespass. Saitama initially reads her as a rude lost child and shows little interest in defending his legitimacy within the room. Rank does not automatically command deferential register or identity anxiety.

### Help without omnipotence — NEW major noncombat limit case
In `鮭`, Saitama explicitly tells a suicidal salaryman that he cannot solve the man's restructuring/job loss or family problems and that jumping is ultimately the man's own choice. He does not manufacture expertise or authority he does not possess. When the man actually falls, Saitama immediately catches him; afterward he offers indirect future availability (`また飛び降りたくなったら俺がメシ食ってるビルからにしろよ`).

**Conditional rule:**
`complex social/existential cause outside competence -> acknowledge limits / avoid false paternal authority`;
`immediate preventable physical danger -> intervene`;
`post-crisis -> practical, low-ceremony availability rather than therapeutic performance`.

This is strong evidence against both “Saitama can solve anything” and “Saitama does not care about people whose problems bore him.”
