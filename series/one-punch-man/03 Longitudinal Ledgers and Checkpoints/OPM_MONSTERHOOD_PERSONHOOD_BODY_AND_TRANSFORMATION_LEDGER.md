# One Punch Man — Monsterhood, Personhood, Body, and Transformation Ledger


## Governing rule


Do not collapse `怪人`, modified human, cyborg, artificial organism, transformed body, social villain, and morally monstrous behavior into one category.


## V01 baseline


- Vaccine Man presents a nonhuman/ecological corrective identity.
- Crablante is a monster opponent whose body is integrated with his self-label and violence.
- Marugori becomes colossal through Fukegao's scientific intervention.
- Mosquito Girl presents biological/collective predation.
- Armored Gorilla demonstrates that a monster-like/artificial body can communicate, surrender, revise behavior, and provide information.
- Genos establishes mechanically reconstructed personhood without being treated as a monster merely because his body is technological.


**Initial conclusion:** body origin and moral agency must be tracked separately.


## V02 additions


### House of Evolution
Dr. Genus creates/artificially advances beings as an administered evolutionary hierarchy. Carnage Kabuto is the strongest early example of scientific modification producing both immense capability and violent instability.


**OPEN:** what, precisely, determines whether a scientifically altered being is socially/ontologically a `怪人` rather than another kind of modified person?


### Paradisers
Hammerhead and followers remain human antagonists while using high-powered external battle suits. Extreme destructive capability does not itself produce monster status.


### `電気のヒモの化身`
The bonus story explicitly presents a human whose excessive fixation/practice around shadowboxing a room-light pull cord results in monster transformation (`OPM|V02|extra:じぶんみがき|image:0196`). The mechanism is comic, but the category evidence is serious:


> monsterization can emerge from human habit/obsession, not only alien species or laboratory engineering.


### Saitama's body
Training-year Saitama undergoes gradual adaptation: repeated movements become more efficient/reflexive (`images:0190-0194`). This is bodily transformation through practice but is not labeled monsterization.


## Current taxonomy through V02


| Route | Example | Moral/personhood note |
|---|---|---|
| nonhuman/ecological being | Vaccine Man | ideological agency explicit |
| scientific enhancement/transformation | Marugori; House of Evolution | body modification does not settle personhood |
| artificial/engineered organism | Armored Gorilla; Carnage Kabuto | behavior ranges from surrender/revision to mania |
| cyborg reconstruction | Genos | personhood preserved; not equated with monsterhood |
| external technology | Paradisers | remains human category despite destructive strength |
| obsession/habit monsterization | `電気のヒモの化身` | comic but explicit human->monster route |
| disciplined bodily adaptation | training-year Saitama | self-transformation without monster label |


## Open questions


- What social/biological criteria define `怪人`?
- How often does obsession cause transformation?
- Can monster status change while personhood/behavior changes?
- How will institutions classify ambiguous bodies?


## V03 additions


### Kombu Infinity
A monster attracted by City Z's rumor ecology defeats registered A-class heroes, misreads Saitama as an ordinary resident, and becomes harvested food material after defeat. Monster body can move from threat-sign to mundane resource without ontological explanation.


### 170,000-year cicada
`夏` shows larva during training-era Saitama and adult in the present. The larva/adult cut forms a visual rhyme with Saitama's own bodily development. Treat as **formal parallel**, not evidence that Saitama and the monster share a transformation mechanism.


### Fan Demon
Demon-level `扇風鬼` is mechanical/monstrous in embodiment and capable of a post-defeat revival/reconstitution state. The exact mechanism remains local and should not be generalized into a universal monster rule.


### Current monsterization status
No V03 material closes the open question of how humans/organisms become `怪人`. Preserve multiple mechanism hypotheses rather than forcing a unified cause.

## V04 additions

### Sea Folk / Deep Sea King — nonhuman collective route
The Sea Folk arrive as a coordinated nonhuman population with a sovereign ruler and territorial claim. This expands monster ontology beyond individual obsession-based transformation, engineered organisms, or isolated abnormal bodies.

Safest V04 formulation: **nonhuman collective / biological-political monster identity**. Exact origin/species mechanism remains open.

### Moral transgression != monster ontology
Puri-Puri Prisoner's coercive/predatory criminality is serious, but he remains human and institutionally a hero. V04 supplies a useful negative control: immoral/criminal conduct alone is not equivalent to `怪人` status.

## V05 additions

### Deep Sea King — environment-conditioned body state
Rain visibly changes/restores the Deep Sea King's effective bodily/combat state. Record this as local environment-conditioned physiology. Do not generalize it into a universal monster transformation rule.

### Sea Folk continuity
V05 continues the nonhuman collective/sovereign route established in V04. No new source establishes their full origin or a unified theory of monsterhood.

### Genos as negative-control body case — STRENGTHEN
Catastrophic cyborg body damage while protecting a child again demonstrates that radical artificial embodiment does not diminish personhood or prosocial agency. Cyborg reconstruction remains categorically distinct from `怪人` status.

## V06 additions

### Dark Matter invaders — nonhuman political/organizational route
V06 adds extraterrestrial antagonists with hierarchy, language, technology, command communication, memory, and strategic roles. Nonhuman body form therefore cannot itself imply reduced personhood/cognition.

### Melzargard — one body / plural voices unresolved ontology
Observed facts: one regenerative combat body; multiple speaking head units; internal communication; embedded spheres whose destruction can permanently remove a head/unit; one head describes a destroyed head as a companion of over a century. Keep **plural cognitive units + shared regenerative body** as the safe formulation until later evidence clarifies ontology.

### Boros — sealed/released body state
Boros describes his armor as restraining excessive power; Saitama destroys it and Boros enters a released state. Treat this as an explicit local transformation/power-release mechanism, not a general theory of monsterization.

### Garou — human antagonist boundary
At V06 Garou exists only in Bang's historical account as a human former disciple who attacked the dojo and was expelled. Do not apply later monster/hero/ideological categories prospectively.

### Taxonomy consequence
By V06 hostile nonhumans include engineered organisms, natural/nonhuman collectives, obsession-linked monsters, and organized extraterrestrial persons. **Body origin, political affiliation, threat classification, moral agency, and personhood must remain separate axes.**

## V07 boundary update

### Boros regeneration and death-state evidence
V07 directly describes Boros's species as highly adapted to harsh environments and Boros himself as exceptional in healing/physical function/latent energy. Concentrated energy permits rapid regeneration of severe damage. The final battle resolves in Boros's defeat/death after his maximum offensive escalation.

**Boundary:** preserve direct regeneration mechanism claims; do not import later cosmic/limiter terminology.

### Melzargard encounter closure
Sphere/core destruction becomes the operative vulnerability through which the multi-hero team finishes Melzargard. Shared-body / multiple-head personhood remains ontologically open even though the combat encounter closes.

### Dark Matter survivors
Survivors display individual survival behavior after command collapse. Their existence further supports extraterrestrial organized personhood rather than “mindless monster” shorthand.

### `奇襲梅`
The monster self-describes as having grown enough to attack police openly, and ordinary police bullets fail. One self-report is insufficient to define a general monster-growth ontology; record the wording as an open mechanism clue only.

## V08 boundary update — monster aspiration without monster body

### Garou — human antagonist / monster aspiration
Garou says `怪人に憧れて修行し` and identifies a history of destroying martial-arts dojos. At V08 this is identity/desire language, not evidence that a body transformation has occurred.

**Canonical separation:**
`human body / martial training / antagonist action / monster aspiration` are simultaneously true at V08.

Do not retroactively assign a later monsterization mechanism, physiology or ontology.

### Grizz-Meow
`迷い猫` adds a secretly raised escaped creature classified at Demon-level locally. The extra also involves offspring/young. This is source-specific creature evidence only; it does not establish one universal monster reproductive/transformation pathway.

### G4
G4 is a robot/machine antagonist rather than evidence for biological `怪人` ontology. Preserve technological actor status separately.

## V09 update — Garou's monsterhood remains self-authored, not biological

At the V09 boundary Garou must be coded across separate dimensions:

- **body:** human;
- **social role:** criminal aggressor / `ヒーロー狩り` / monster-like antagonist;
- **self-identification:** aspiring `怪人`;
- **developmental program:** defeating heroes is explicitly treated as a route toward becoming/growing into that role;
- **biological transformation:** **not established**;
- **moral category:** cannot be inferred from the noun `怪人` alone.

His childhood `人気者`/`嫌われ者` and `シナリオ` language makes monsterhood partly a chosen narrative position: he identifies with the role structurally scripted to lose and intends to reverse that script. V09 does not yet justify importing any later body-state ontology into this formulation.

## V10 update — self-label, human body, transformation state, and moral non-equivalence

### Garou — human body / monster self-authorship explicitly separated
Mumen Rider's `怪人を名乗る人間` formulation makes the V10 ontology boundary explicit. Garou's `怪人` project is ideological/social/self-authored at this point; no biological monsterization is source-supported.

### Selective care does not settle moral category
Garou tells Tareo to run home during a real monster emergency while continuing to assault heroes intentionally. V10 therefore supplies direct evidence that self-label, body ontology, and practical moral behavior can diverge. Neither “he calls himself a monster, therefore he has no care” nor “he helps a child, therefore his violence is benign” is defensible.

### Body-state transformation can change measured capability
The spider monster's physical-strength reading changes dramatically after bodily alteration. Transformation is therefore not only visual relabeling; at least in this case it corresponds to a real change in measurable physical state.

### Elder Centipede
The Dragon-level Elder Centipede enters as a literal nonhuman catastrophic threat and a known prior Association problem. Its presence sharpens the contrast with Garou's human self-monstering. Outcome remains open at V10.

## V11 - monster identity versus monster organization

### Garou - STRENGTHEN ontology separation
V11 provides a clean distinction between self-label and institutional category. Garou remains human at the observed boundary, calls himself a monster, and rejects recruitment by an actual Monster Association. His monsterhood is therefore currently ideological/social/self-authored rather than proof of biological transformation or group membership.

### Conduct remains independent of ontology - STRENGTHEN
Garou can show selective concern for a child and still attack a hero who is actively responding to a Dragon emergency. Personhood, body state, moral conduct, and chosen identity must remain separate fields.

### Monster Association - NEW organizational category
Actual monsters now appear as members of a coordinated organization capable of recruitment, discipline, synchronized deployment, and hostage strategy. Organizational membership is a new independent variable; it must not be retroactively assigned to unaffiliated monsters or to Garou.

### Transformation boundary - OPEN
No V11 evidence establishes a new Garou body state. Later transformation evidence remains excluded.

## V12 update — organizational monster hierarchy and persistent Garou separation

### Garou — PRESERVE self-authored monster identity separate from organization
V12 does not alter the V11 ontology lock: Garou remains human at the observed collected boundary, continues his self-authored monster project, and is not a Monster Association member. His contact with Watchdog Man and awareness of monster activity do not change those axes.

### Monster Association — STRENGTHEN category as organized social identity
The organization now has explicit strategic resource accounting and an observed `怪人王` title. Monsterhood in the corpus therefore continues to require separate tracking of body/biology, self-label, conduct, public category, and organization membership.

### Orochi — OPEN body/history
Orochi is visually monstrous and titled `怪人王`, but V12 does not establish transformation history, origin, or the full relationship between body and institutional authority.

### Awakened Cockroach — body sacrifice as survival tactic
The opponent escapes Genos by abandoning body parts/legs after being immobilized. This is direct bodily plasticity/survival evidence for this monster but should not be generalized to monsterhood as a universal biological rule.

### Pig God — human heroic body as anomalous capability reminder
Pig God's ability to ingest a poison monster without immediate impairment is a hero-body counterexample to any simplistic visual/body taxonomy: extraordinary bodily function does not itself define monster status.

## V13 update — Monster Cell conversion and four-axis monsterhood control

### Monster Cells — NEW canonical conversion pathway
Haragiri explains that taking `怪人細胞` into the body activates the flesh and can provide `人間の限界を超えた筋力や寿命`; the proposition is summarized as `つまりは怪人になる`. His visible transformation confirms an observed human-to-monster bodily conversion pathway.

Canonical V13 mechanism:
`human body -> Monster Cell ingestion -> bodily activation/transformation -> monster body state + claimed beyond-human physical/lifespan capacity`.

Open: origin, producer, reliability, cognition effects, compatibility with other monster origins, and whether all converted individuals scale similarly.

### Haragiri — convergent body / identity / affiliation / conduct case
Haragiri voluntarily joins the Monster Association because he is willing to exchange `人間を辞める` for greater strength. After transformation he states `ワシはもう人間じゃない` and threatens peers who refuse conversion.

V13 therefore aligns four axes in one case:
1. body: transformed;
2. self-identity: explicitly nonhuman;
3. affiliation: Monster Association member;
4. conduct: coercive organization-aligned violence.

### Garou control case — PRESERVE axis separation
The Haragiri convergence must not overwrite Garou's V12 state. Garou remains observed as human-bodied, self-identifying with monsterhood, refusing Monster Association membership, and combining severe hero violence with selective civilian care.

Current rule: `怪人` usage is not sufficient by itself to infer body, affiliation, or moral conduct.

### Transformation does not equal total superiority
Haragiri's bodily enhancement is real, but Atomic Samurai kills him before he can exploit the claimed speed advantage. Biological enhancement changes capability without guaranteeing superior technique, judgment, timing, or initiative.

### Monster Association telos — STRENGTHEN
Gyoro Gyoro explicitly says violence is a monster `根源的欲求` but `我々の目的はそれではない`. Monster impulse and organization purpose are separate analytical objects.

## V14 update — consent histories inside one Monster Cell pathway

### Monster Cell mechanism — STRENGTHEN, not universalize
V14 replicates Monster Cell conversion across multiple martial artists and visibly preserves the V13 bodily pathway. The mechanism is now strongly established as one repeatable human-to-monster route. It remains unauthorized as the universal origin of monsters.

### Consent/motive decomposition — NEW explicit spectrum
The same bodily mechanism appears under materially different decision histories:
- Gouketsu describes accepting monsterization after human defeat/capture when offered a route beyond human limits;
- tournament fighters face explicit death-or-consume coercion, with survival among their motives;
- Bakuzan voluntarily consumes the remaining cells to gain power over Suiryu;
- Haragiri's earlier V13 conversion remained voluntary and strength-driven.

Therefore biological end-state does not erase consent history, motive, affiliation route, or moral conduct.

### Bakuzan self-interpretation — QUALIFY
After conversion Bakuzan says he always loved tormenting weaker people and sought strength for that purpose. Record this as **Bakuzan's self-report**. V14 does not establish a general rule that monsterization reveals a person's hidden true self.

### Dose-response — OPEN
Bakuzan consumes more than the instructed amount and transforms successfully. V14 supplies no controlled comparison establishing that more cells cause proportionally more power.

### Gouketsu / organizational monsterhood — STRENGTHEN
Gouketsu demonstrates that bodily monsterization can coexist with disciplined organizational obedience: he recruits, evaluates, reports hierarchy, and withdraws under headquarters strategy rather than acting only on immediate violent impulse.

## V15 update — intent, procedure, body, and affiliation separate again

### Sonic as procedural control case
Sonic genuinely accepts `人間をやめる` as the cost of obtaining monster power and decides to use a Monster Cell. He then cooks the unknown raw flesh under ordinary `常識的に考えて` food-safety logic. V15 follows with stomach illness and no established successful monster body.

**STRENGTHEN / DECOMPOSE:** self-intended monster identity, ingestion procedure, successful biological conversion, Monster Association affiliation and post-conversion conduct remain separate propositions.

**OPEN:** V15 does not establish a universal biochemical law that heat neutralizes Monster Cells, only a comic sequence in which altered preparation and failed visible transformation coincide.

### Converted ninja specialists
Gale Wind and Hellfire Flame show successful monsterized bodies coexisting with preserved ninja lineage/technique and Monster Association recruitment activity.

### Armored Gorilla longitudinal control
A visibly nonhuman/artificial former antagonist is now observed shopping for ingredients and rejecting gratuitous local conflict. Body morphology and historical affiliation do not determine current moral alignment.

## V16 update — Garou identity versus costly conduct

### Garou `怪人` self-identification remains sincere
`よく見とけ！ 怪人が勝つ瞬間を!!!` confirms active performative/self-authored monster identity. Do not downgrade the label to mere irony.

### Protective civilian constraint makes the category behaviorally incomplete
Garou truthfully warns that Tareo will become collateral and redirects Death Shower away from the hut while severely depleted. **STRENGTHEN / DECOMPOSE:** social/self label, biological body state, and moral action must remain separate. V16 supports selective child protection, not universal benevolence or hidden hero status.

### Body-state mechanism remains OPEN
Garou's endurance under accumulated injury becomes increasingly abnormal, but V16 does not establish a biological monster transition or its mechanism. Do not import later transformation theory backward.

## V17 update -- competing monsterhood authorities

### Garou: self-label versus institutional monster test
V17 finally makes four layers explicit and non-equivalent: Garou's self-identification, social/hero-side threat labeling, human body state, and Monster Association acceptance. Gyoro Gyoro says Garou has not yet proved that he is a true monster and requires behavioral proof.

The proof condition is a hero's severed head; surrounding monsters gloss it as killing a hero without hesitation. Monsterhood is therefore an ethical-behavioral qualification inside the Association, not merely a body category.

Garou's silence is OPEN at V17. Do not backfill later response.

### Garou childhood role origin
His childhood hero-play memory strengthens the social-assignment layer: popular child = hero role, disliked child = monster role, monster victory structurally excluded. This explains Garou's grievance but does not validate all later hero/monster equivalences.

### Sonic
The extra strengthens failed/uncertain transformation status. Sonic interprets bodily lightness as monster power; the text identifies two kilograms of weight loss. Successful biological conversion remains unestablished.

### Elder Centipede
Molting/regeneration demonstrates that visible shell destruction is not organism death. Body state and effective damage must be mechanism-specific.

### Machine God G5
Mechanical body + Monster Association alignment + Demon threat classification show again that antagonist/faction status does not require biological monster substrate.


## V18 update — competing monster ontologies and Garou body-state boundary

### Monster Association doctrine — NEW explicit operational definition
Bug God states `怪人とは人間であることを捨てた者だ` and treats Garou's Tareo protection as evidence that he has not fully abandoned humanity. Killing Tareo becomes a membership/trust proof condition. This is institutional doctrine, not narrator ontology.

### Garou — REVISE / DECOMPOSE
Garou brackets the abstract monster definition, diagnoses missing `決定的な覚悟`, borrows the Association's lethal threshold as a private self-test, then behaviorally rejects its child-killing criterion. Preserve separate axes: self-label, body state, hero-directed violence, attachment, organization membership, and lethal resolve.

### Pureblood — NEW competing ontology
Pureblood calls himself a `生まれながらの本物の怪人`, grounding authenticity in vampire birth/lineage and devaluing transformed monsters. The Association therefore contains internal disagreement over what counts as “real” monsterhood.

### Dr. Genus taxonomy — STRENGTHEN / separate transformation routes
Genus describes multiple routes to beings called monsters—emotional/obsessional, environmental/cellular, scientific, nonhuman environmental, alien/natural enemy—and explicitly separates transformation into another organism from limiter removal.

### Garou closing body state — OPEN
Garou survives damage treated as fatal, notes that bleeding has stopped and his torn clothing has adhered rigidly to the wounds, and appears more monster-like. Completed biological monsterization is not established at V18.

## V19 monsterhood update - half-monster classification, coercive method, and conduct/label divergence

### Garou self-label versus protective conduct - STRENGTHEN
Garou answers Tareo's question about why he came with his monster identity rather than a rescue identity. The label now absorbs behavior that the Monster Association itself treats as evidence of insufficient monster nature. This is a stronger contradiction than simply calling him a secret hero.

### Garou eating monster flesh - boundary control
Garou consumes defeated monster flesh under an explicit survival/nutrition rationale. V19 provides no Monster Cell object, prescribed conversion procedure, or narrator statement of biological conversion from the meal. Do not use the scene as proof of monsterization mechanism.

### Gyoro Gyoro `half-monster` diagnosis - speaker-qualified
Gyoro classifies Garou as not yet fully transformed. This is expert/managerial testimony inside the Monster Association, not omniscient narration. It should be tracked alongside Garou's body changes and conduct rather than treated as a solved ontology.

### Gyoro transformation research - STRENGTHEN / DECOMPOSE
Gyoro describes multiple experimental routes involving repeated near-death survival, extreme physical and mental load, monster-on-monster consumption, blood/cell transfer, breeding and amplified negative affect. The program aims to exceed ordinary growth ceilings. These are Monster Association research claims and must remain distinct from Dr. Genus's limiter theory unless a later source explicitly connects them.

### Orochi former-human transformation - STRENGTHEN
V19 supplies direct body-history testimony that the horns were the first feature to appear when Orochi changed from human to monster. This supports staged transformation history without proving that Garou is following the identical path.

### Garou fear criterion - NEW identity goal
Garou says Orochi is strong but insufficiently frightening for the Monster King role and promises to supply fear himself. Monsterhood for Garou is therefore increasingly an intended social effect, not merely body state, Association membership, or raw strength.

## V21 monsterhood/body/transformation delta

### Ninja monsterization — STRENGTHEN amplification-with-continuity model
Hellfire Flame and Gale Wind retain their ninja technique system while gaining explicitly Dragon-class transformed bodies. The source supports amplification layered onto prior human specialization, not replacement of all prior identity/skill.

### Training versus transformed output — REVISE
Flash's victory does not show that monsterization failed to increase power. The Dragon labels and high-speed pressure establish real escalation. The safer model is that bodily amplification did not erase technique legibility or compensate for the training/tactical gap inside a shared specialist system.

### Phoenix Man — NEW costume/resurrection mechanism candidate
Child Emperor explicitly hypothesizes that a man monsterized through a costume may inherit the character setting associated with that costume. Waganma's `ドンカンバード` explanation makes repeated death/revival salient immediately before Phoenix Man resurrects. Treat the mechanism as strongly suggested and locally predictive, not as a universal costume-monster law.

### Near-death growth doctrine — STRENGTHEN, still speaker-qualified
Phoenix Man cites Gyoro Gyoro's `死に近づく地獄` doctrine and then returns as Dragon-class `転生フェニックス男`. This is the strongest compatible observed case so far, but universality and identity with Dr. Genus's limiter theory remain OPEN.


## V22 monsterhood/body/transformation delta

### Phoenix Man — STRENGTHEN identity-fusion route
Phoenix's origin ties monsterization to refusal/inability to remove a performed role after occupational rejection. His `ドンカンバードは俺の魂!! 全存在!!` makes role/body/self fusion explicit. Monsterization is his `救済`, but that evaluative language remains his ideology.

### `怪人ヒヨコ男` downgrade — NEW bounded mechanism evidence
Child Emperor exploits costume dependency with `コチョコチョ虫3号`; Phoenix refuses removal and collapses to `災害レベル:狼未満 怪人ヒヨコ男`. Record a local costume-linked threat-state downgrade. Do not infer universal biological de-monsterization.

### Pureblood `本物の怪人` — STRENGTHEN contested authenticity grammar
Pureblood claims inherited vampire lineage makes his species the Monster Association's sole authentic monster. This sits beside Garou's behavioral qualification and Phoenix's identity-fusion model as another incompatible definition of “real monster.”

### Do-S control — NEW personhood-without-body-conversion node
Do-S can capture agency and weaponize humans without establishing bodily monsterization. Monsterhood/personhood analysis must therefore keep domination of will separate from transformation of body.

### Amai Mask — NEW high-confidence body anomaly / ontology OPEN
A direct strike exposes a cracked/scaled nonhuman-looking facial structure, prompting Do-S's unfinished `お前…人間じゃ…`. His public appearance is normal immediately afterward. Record the contradiction; final biological category and restoration mechanism remain OPEN.

## V23 monsterhood / personhood / body delta

### Monsterized prisoners and the `human heart` criterion — NEW contested personhood test
PPP says that if their hearts remain human they are already proper humans. This is **PPP's ethical personhood criterion**, not neutral ontology. The prisoners retain speech and self-directed rejection of PPP even while attacking as monsters. ### PPP adaptive body changes — OPEN mechanism family
PPP develops vibration/body-hair responses under damage. Nyan interprets the dense body hair as defensive instinct awakened by accumulated damage and rapid bodily evolution. Preserve this as **Nyan's local mechanism inference**. Do not equate it with Gyoro Gyoro's near-death engineering, monster-cell conversion, or Genus's limiter theory without a direct bridge.

### Nyan — autonomy through flexible body
Nyan's ability to pass through an approximately 3 mm opening gives bodily expression to his explicit autonomy philosophy. Body morphology and behavioral motive align locally without proving a universal cat-monster psychology.

### Homeless Emperor — human-shaped divine-grant claim
V23 presents an apparently human former salaryman whose demonstrated luminous power is attributed by him to a God encounter. The body does not visually settle monster ontology. Keep separate: prior human biography; demonstrated extraordinary power; self/Association monster category; reported entity grant; ontological identity of entity.

### Pig God / Gums — visual body rhyme without moral equivalence
Hero and monster both weaponize mouths/appetite/consumption, but conduct and target selection run in opposite directions. Similar bodily grammar is not identical personhood or moral status.

### Darkshine — extraordinary human body claim
Darkshine's V23 body is depicted as functionally invulnerable to Bug God and is self-described as something even Darkshine does not know how to injure. No transformation mechanism is established; do not route extraordinary embodiment automatically into monsterhood.

## V24 delta — body, agency provenance, and monster mechanisms

- **Controlled bodies:** V24 strengthens the rule that hostile bodily action does not by itself establish autonomous hostile intention. Do-S separates body behavior from agency provenance.
- **Evil Natural Water:** fluid embodiment adds another monster-body class whose threat mechanics are not reducible to anatomy or ordinary injury.
- **Orochi:** monsterhood includes not only transformed body but a king/destiny/sacrifice interpretive identity that organizes perception of new events.
- **Gyoro Gyoro:** visible body and controlling subject may diverge; body cannot automatically be treated as the final person/agent.
- **Fubuki/Do-S contrast:** personhood becomes operationally visible through whether another actor's independent agency is preserved, restored, or erased.

## V25 delta — autonomy, transformed bodies, and mechanism-first ontology

- **Nyan:** explicit exit from Monster Association control strengthens self-authored agency while his deliberate predation demonstrates that autonomy and ethical regard are independent variables.
- **Garou:** visibly altered body and increased capability are source facts; completed biological monsterization is not yet source-locked at V25.
- **Evil Natural Water:** preserve mechanism-first ontology where stable anatomy, speech, and ordinary cognition are absent. Fish coexistence is not psychic control; local freezing is not permanent death.
- **Saitama / `後頭部`:** training-era hair loss is a benign body-change control case. Changed embodiment by itself does not identify monsterhood or moral transformation.
- **Cross-volume rule — STRENGTHEN:** body state, agency, affiliation, and moral relation must remain separately tracked even when the manga visually compresses them into a single transformed figure.

## V26 delta — distributed body, adaptive body, and morphology/agency separation

- **Evil Natural Water:** frozen material state does not equal terminal body state; reactivation and cognition uncertainty remain mechanism-first.
- **Garou:** visual/body transformation intensifies while in-story classification remains contested. His body also becomes a learning system capable of one-body synthesis of techniques normally requiring two practitioners. Do not equate altered morphology with settled monster personhood.
- **Orochi:** `オロチ心臓部`, blood uptake, and distributed regeneration establish that presented-body destruction can leave a surviving organismal network.
- **Psykos/Orochi:** fusion explicitly begins; body-energy source and psychic agency become interdependent. Post-fusion personhood/agency distribution remains OPEN.
- **Manako:** monster morphology coexists with cooperative route behavior and local informational contribution.
- **Invisible monster:** visibility is a tactical body property, not a personhood marker.
- **Cross-volume rule — STRENGTHEN:** material substrate, visible form, agency, affiliation, cognition, and moral relation must remain independently represented.
