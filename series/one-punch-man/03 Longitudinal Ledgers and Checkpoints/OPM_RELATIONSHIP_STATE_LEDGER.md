# One Punch Man — Relationship State Ledger


## Saitama -> Genos


- **Boundary:** V01
- **Definition:** stranger -> tolerated persistent follower -> provisional disciple
- **Trust:** emerging; Saitama permits proximity and cooperation without deep disclosure
- **Affection:** not yet established strongly
- **Authority:** reluctant/provisional teacher role
- **Dependency:** low from Saitama's side
- **Communication:** colloquial and concise; resists Genos's imposed `先生`/`師匠` labels and becomes sharply impatient with long explanations
- **Knowledge asymmetry:** Saitama knows his own training history but has no developed theory capable of satisfying Genos's explanatory demand
- **Transition anchors:** Saitama rejects both `先生` and Genos's substitute `師匠` (`image:0140`), yet continues allowing proximity and eventually inhabits the teacher role; Genos formally asks how to become strong like him (`image:0146`)
- **Confidence:** high for asymmetry; medium for durable attachment


## Genos -> Saitama


- **Boundary:** V01
- **Definition:** unknown civilian -> anomalous superior -> chosen master
- **Trust:** high relative to short acquaintance after direct capability evidence
- **Idealization:** emerging/high
- **Authority:** actively grants Saitama teacher authority
- **Dependency:** emerging instrumental dependency for hoped-for growth
- **Communication:** masculine but deferential toward Saitama; `先生`/attempted `師匠`; explicit and information-heavy
- **Knowledge asymmetry:** Genos knows Saitama is extraordinarily strong but does not understand why; this ignorance drives attachment
- **Transition anchors:** immediate `先生`, attempted `師匠` after Saitama rejects the first label (`image:0140`), then `先生のように強くなる方法 教えてください` (`image:0146`)
- **Confidence:** high


## Directional conclusion
The relation is deliberately asymmetric at inception. Genos treats apprenticeship as consequential immediately; Saitama treats it as an improvised social burden/opportunity. Do not average these into a single mutual "master-disciple" state.
