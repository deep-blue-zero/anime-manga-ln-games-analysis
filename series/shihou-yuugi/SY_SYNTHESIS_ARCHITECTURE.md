# Anthology synthesis architecture

## 1. One collection root, four independent reading scopes

Generation SY-G1 occupies `series/shihou-yuugi/` with one [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md). [The method](SY_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) governs how each story is read. This architecture governs how separate readings support a collection argument without inventing continuous cast or chronology. No predecessor architecture is superseded.

The bibliographic collection 四方遊戯 遠藤達哉短編集 has one inventoried Japanese EPUB. The publisher identifies 西部遊戯, 月華美刃, WITCH CRAZE and PMG-0, but the actual source spans/order and all narrative facts remain uninspected. The source/work map preserves this distinction. Other works by 遠藤達哉 and later works reusing a title are excluded.

The intended result is four source-bounded story interpretations and, when supported, a comparative collection synthesis that explains similarities, differences and limits through actual evidence. Reconstruction is a bounded derived option per story/person/state, never an obligation to invent a model. Canonical authority here is INITIAL procedural scope only; no source is admitted and no owner run exists.

## 2. Artifact homes and source model

Day-one homes are the source/work map, method, this architecture, story-local evidence ledger, claim/comparison ledger, producer review, current entrypoint and descriptor. Separate story roots, empty cast folders or anticipated specialists are unnecessary. Their identities remain work-qualified inside the collection root.

Future `readings/` holds one substantive reading per authorized story. `studies/` appears only with earned story-specific or collection-comparative arguments. `reconstruction/` contains independently justified evidence/model/testing artifacts. `audits/` holds actual reviews. A reading may contain its own immutable exit freeze; separate checkpoints are created only when needed for handoff/release.

The witness's narrative, covers, story title pages, notes, jokes and other extras need component classification before use. Paratext can inform a labeled interpretive question but cannot overwrite the pages. Alternative editions, translations and adaptations require separate admission and crosswalks. A single file grants no permission to inspect all its stories during a narrower run.

## 5. Full prose and earned specialist work

Each story reading explains how actions, knowledge, relationships, language and form produce the story's effects. The collection synthesis then asks which comparisons illuminate those local arguments and which erase relevant differences. It may reject an initially attractive unifying thesis.

A separate monograph or relationship study is earned by a distinct question requiring sustained evidence beyond the story reading. A short story may warrant a bounded character case without supporting a broad behavioral model. Do not multiply small casts into empty dossiers. A specialist on a formal or thematic dimension becomes necessary when the role-gap review shows the collection account cannot responsibly absorb its independent argument.

If no independent specialists are needed, identify the substantive sections in accepted story readings or integrated synthesis that carry those responsibilities. No file-count quota applies. The duty is analytical completeness, not a directory shape.

Major arguments must retain inferential warrants, representative precise evidence, significant counterexamples, alternatives, temporal distinctions and limits. Use names once source-established, not archetypes as habitual substitutes. No universal word cap governs mature prose. A collection of abstracts, a neat matrix or a high row count cannot substitute for causal interpretation.

## 6. Contradictions, dependency order and gates

First verify work identity before calling two observations contradictory. Different stories may simply have different premises. Within a work check witness, source layer, attribution, event/disclosure time and frame; then consider changed state, mistaken belief, deception only if supported, retrospective explanation, edition difference or genuine inconsistency. Preserve uncertainty and counterreadings.

Production order is witness/story mapping plus authorized run → independent story readings with synchronized ledgers → declared story-scope completion → work-local reconciliation → role-gap/specialist readiness → substantive local studies as earned → comparison/convergence → collection synthesis readiness → validation → scope-specific freeze.

| Gate | Condition |
|---|---|
| Bootstrap review | Meaningful story-local architecture, source limits, schemas and CLOSED state |
| Admission | Verified story span/witness/inspection route; owner scope and consistent OPEN gate |
| Story completion | Full causal prose, counterreading, exact coverage, local state/claim updates and exit freeze |
| Declared sequential completion | Every authorized story accounted for; unread stories explicitly excluded |
| Longitudinal reconciliation | Each work's states, edges, knowledge and expression aligned without cross-cast leakage |
| Character/specialist readiness | Work-specific evidence/locator stabilization and role-gap review |
| Specialist convergence | Contradictions compared; explicit integrated-section rationale if independent specialists unnecessary |
| Collection synthesis readiness | All works in declared comparison scope accepted; comparative axis, exceptions and distinct premises preserved |
| Final validation | Source, claim, named-reference and compression checks plus exact repository requirements |
| Release/freeze | Accepted story/collection scope and immutable records named; proper integration route |
| Model acceptance | Separate independent tests of a bounded subject/state; evaluator retains grading authority |

A partial collection synthesis may be legitimate if its exact subset and missing works are explicit. It cannot claim to explain all four without all required evidence. Reading the complete EPUB does not establish four completed analyses.

## 7. Story-local reconstruction and independent evaluation

Reconstruction remains derived use of literary analysis. Use distinct evidence map, operational model and validation record for each earned `work/person@state`; literary monographs remain separate. Cross-story resemblance never pools evidence into one subject without a supported identity decision. Limited source range may justify NOT_READY rather than a simulated personality.

The evidence map records work, subject, scene/state/horizon, situation/stakes, interlocutor, DS direct speech/DB shown behavior/NI interior presentation/RB report/OS observer-role/INF inference, source locator, full reading/claim route, Japanese status, counterexample and gap. Ordinary-life sample, direct-versus-reported interiority, directed relationship conditioning and negative-evidence opportunity are mandatory coverage checks.

The model binds exact evidence-base commit, witness/continuity, temporal boundary, excluded material and version. Separate disposition, developmental state and temporary condition; change causes include KNOWLEDGE, RELATIONSHIP, CONTEXT, ROLE, RESOURCE, DISPOSITION, REVEALED_NOT_NEW and UNRESOLVED. Rules specify trigger + perceived stakes + state → response family with inhibitors, overrides, rival explanations and falsifiers. Classify OBSERVED/CANDIDATE_POLICY/PROMOTED_POLICY by actual support and testing.

Written-register modeling derives from Japanese contextual speech, preserving private thought versus public utterance. Score decision fidelity and surface fidelity separately. Confidence is HIGH/MODERATE/LOW/OPEN; distances D0 attested, D1 near variant, D2 novel in-world/state, D3 cross-scenario. A D3 use states assumed chronology, relationship and setting and may require abstention. A different anthology story is not a same-character test environment.

An independent reviewer evaluates frozen predictions against genuinely withheld evidence. Declare author exposure and the test split before model fitting. A small one-shot may offer no valid unseen test; use RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST and retain that limit instead of pretending delayed rereading is prospective. A new source needs separate admission authorization.

Frozen test records include model/version, rules, state, assumptions, response family, confidence/distance and falsifier. Independent adjudication records diagnostic opportunity, exact evidence and outcome SUPPORTED/PARTLY_SUPPORTED/CONTRADICTED/NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY/CONFOUNDED/UNRESOLVED. Use plausible decoys, context/interlocutor perturbations and abstention controls, with a generic-personality baseline where useful. Report qualitative fidelity and denominators/limits, not unsupported accuracy percentages.

Generated dialogue/scenes/predictions are synthetic outputs, never canonical observations or literary evidence. No simulations are authorized now. The designated evaluator alone assigns capability grades; discovery agents alone curate global subject rows. Project coverage makes twelve dimensions and six scenario types assessable without self-grading.

## 8. Extension, lifecycle and history

INITIAL design is tested after the first accepted story, after each remaining story if new duties appear, before comparison/synthesis and before release. Review findings use SATISFIED, PARTIALLY_SATISFIED, MISSING, MISROUTED, TOO_THIN or NOT_APPLICABLE with exact home, evidence, consequence and repair owner.

Extend when recurring independent evidence cannot be retrieved adequately from existing homes; add story-specific duties without forcing all other stories into them. Backfill only material missing evidence. Preserve full local interpretations when forming collection claims.

Current method/architecture, entry and current indexes are mutable by targeted edits with history. Event records, witness bindings, forecasts and prospective freezes remain append-preserving; frozen readings are corrected through attributable errata/revisions rather than rewritten. Superseded models retain assessed bytes and reciprocal links. No invented run closure is created.

## 9. Execution and handoff

Metadata mapping uses BOUNDED_STANDARD; substantive story readings and interpretive ledger changes use SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS; architecture, monographs, comparisons and models use DEEP_SYNTHESIS. Escalate propagation-sensitive final convergence/adversarial review to PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST when justified by actual difficulty. Do not encode provider/model names as ontology.

Local executor owns verified STILL_IMAGE/Japanese TEXT readings and cumulative state; fresh adequately grounded sessions are preferred for substantial synthesis, with a local convergence/publication owner. Housekeeping, discovery and evaluator ownership remain separate. This metadata bootstrap does not claim image capability.

Handoff supplies full files, exact base/output hashes, work/source horizons, actual inspection status, missing obligations, stable targets and preservation instructions. Receiver compares base/candidate/current, reads complete outputs and tests source support and lost reasoning. Stage readiness, semantic acceptance, validation, publication and integration remain distinct. Resume through the single entrypoint, not chat memory.
