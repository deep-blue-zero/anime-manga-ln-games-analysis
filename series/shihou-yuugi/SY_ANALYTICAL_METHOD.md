# Story and collection method

## Read four works as works

The available object is one Japanese anthology, 四方遊戯 遠藤達哉短編集. Publisher bibliography identifies four one-shots, while their exact source spans/order remain unmapped. This method treats each story as an independent primary reading unit. The collection becomes a comparative object only after those local readings exist.

Do not impose a shared cast, continuous chronology or one character-development arc across the volume. A similar name, visual design, reused title or common creator is not continuity evidence. Later Endo works and adaptations are outside scope. [The source/work map](SY_SOURCE_AND_WORK_MAP.md) owns bibliographic identities and gaps; [the architecture](SY_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) owns cumulative/comparative duties.

This adapts the [generic manga reference](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MANGA_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md). The lock is CLOSED, with no authorized story. Before reading, an owner-defined story/run boundary, verified witness binding, adequate STILL_IMAGE/Japanese route and consistent OPEN gate must exist.

## Mapping, order and local knowledge

Map actual image occurrences and story spans in the bound EPUB, then follow its verified collection order unless the owner defines a justified alternative. Work keys do not assert order. Preserve full pages/spreads and classify title pages, covers, notes, jokes, extras and previews independently. Technical package validity does not prove readability, completeness or inspection.

Each story starts a fresh story-local cast, knowledge state and chronology. Retain prior exposure disclosure and quarantine title familiarity or remembered later works. Within a story, segment by goal, configuration, location, disclosure, viewpoint or formal shift. Freeze entering and exit states at consequential boundaries. An ending cannot retroactively make a character know what the earlier scene concealed.

The next story's evidence cannot resolve uncertainty in the current one merely because the stories share a volume. Collection-level comparison is retrospective and explicitly labeled; prospective local records remain intact. If actual source evidence links works, record a supported relationship and scope decision rather than silently merging namespaces.

## Causality, people and relationships

A complete story reading develops an argument in continuous prose. Reconstruct entering conditions, named actors' perceived stakes and alternatives, action/constraint, consequence and inferential warrant. Explain how local setup, development and closure work only after inspecting them; do not force every story through identical plot functions.

Names established within the story are the normal references. Preserve unknown identities and aliases where the source does. A role such as “hero,” “victim” or “villain” is not a replacement for an available personal identity; keep agent, observer, thinker, speaker and recipient unmistakable.

Separate OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, EVALUATION and UNRESOLVED with HIGH/MODERATE/LOW/UNRESOLVED confidence. Reports of another's motive establish the reporter's belief, not direct interior access. A crisis behavior is not automatically a stable trait. Track ordinary actions, humor and low-stakes contexts where present; missing opportunity is a gap, not evidence of absence.

Relationships are directed and story-local. A toward B and B toward A need separate support for knowledge, desire, trust, pressure, reciprocity or response. Record constraints and alternatives before assigning a shared label. A short appearance may support a rich bounded interpretation while still being insufficient for broad behavioral modeling.

## Language and form

Inspect Japanese speech, thought, narration, captions, diegetic text, asides and effects as separate layers. Attribute speaker/thinker with source-based confidence. Use brief attested forms and labeled glosses for address, self-reference, politeness, endings, omission, ruby and pragmatic contrast. Translation is not Japanese voice authority; private thought is not automatically public speech.

Analyze consequential panel/page/spread organization, gaze, proximity, grouping, withheld reactions, gutters, scale, backgrounds, recurrence and rendering-register changes. Describe the observed feature, explain the proposed effect and test an alternative. Silent pages need full inspection. Similar formal choices across two stories require a comparative argument with separate locators, not a declaration of authorial psychology.

Check facing-page layout before page-turn claims. Image ordinal and print page label differ. Panel endpoints do not establish unobserved motion, graphic effects do not establish acoustics and an expression does not disclose a unique emotion. Other-media performance is excluded.

When sensitive actions are actually encountered, distinguish behavior, framing, desire/consent, consequence and evaluation with minimal necessary description. Explanation does not justify conduct, and later affection cannot establish prior consent. Do not preassign stories moral messages from title/genre.

Image occurrences are one-based in full witness reading order, including apparatus; work-local indexes, if helpful, are secondary crosswalks. Keep source maps and image review states in the private evidence plane; Git carries safe aliases, scope, exclusions and remaining legibility debt. OCR/search is retrieval, not inspection.

Claims use stable work-qualified IDs and full reading anchors. Preserve material support, counterevidence, strongest alternative and falsifier. Revise with PRESERVE/STRENGTHEN/REVISE/DOWNGRADE/REJECT/OPEN, retaining earlier wording and prospective freezes. A collection-wide claim cannot silently promote a local hypothesis.

## Completed reading and review

A future `readings/SY_<WORKKEY>_DEEP_READING.md` has `artifact_type: deep_reading` only under an authorized run. It includes witness/admission/coverage; exposure and entering state; governing and rival theses; full movement-level causal prose; story-local people and directed relationships; ordinary and Japanese expression evidence; page form; counterreadings; revisions/questions; exact ledger-update manifest; exit uncertainty and completion decision.

The reading must be substantial enough to preserve explanatory mechanisms and ambiguity. A plot summary, motif list or completed ledger is not a substitute. No universal word ceiling applies. Update work/source coverage, story-local evidence and claims atomically; a justified no-change statement belongs in the reading rather than filler rows.

Before acceptance use handoff §15.6: sample opening, substantial middle, conclusion and material relationships for identifiable people, correct agency/attribution, purposeful roles and preserved concealment. Record findings as justified role, source anonymity, knowledge-boundary need or defects requiring correction. Passing syntax checks does not prove analytical fidelity.

Only after all works in the declared comparison scope are accepted may a collection synthesis compare formal strategies, development or differences. Retain exceptions and noncomparability. A repeated motif is not a shared diegesis, proof of influence, or evidence about the creator's private psychology.

### Current-unit story synopsis

*Prospective amendment, 2026-11-13, under initiation policy v1.11 §5.3.*

Each completed story reading includes a clearly labeled **Current-unit story synopsis** near its front, after the brief witness/coverage receipt and entering context, before governing theses and extended analysis. Its unit is the one mapped, authorized one-shot. Use that work's own participants, chronology and ending; sharing an EPUB with the other stories supplies neither a shared diegesis nor permission to read them.

Write connected, accessible prose explaining the opening situation, named participants and stakes, consequential actions and decisions, causal turns, disclosures and ending state. Preserve the concrete physical and scene mechanics established by inspected pages. Distinguish inherited context from newly presented events, shown or narrated events from testimony or belief, and source-established outcomes from analyst conjecture or unresolved possibilities. Names and identities remain bounded by the current story's disclosures. Material event claims have retrievable source locators or stable observation references without turning the synopsis into a table. Its length follows the actual story's complexity; there is no fixed quota.

The synopsis is a unit-end, spoiler-bearing orientation and may recount the current story's disclosed ending. Compose or finalize it after all applicable entering and internal prospective captures are frozen; shield it and the complete reading from earlier-horizon analysts. Another story, a later work reusing a title or outside familiarity cannot settle this story's uncertainty. Collection comparison retains its separate retrospective prerequisites. This addition supplements the complete movement-level causal, linguistic, visual, character, relationship and counterreading account and its source/state/claim transaction. Acceptance requires both the synopsis and all existing duties; frozen readings are not rewritten and the CLOSED analysis lock remains governing.
