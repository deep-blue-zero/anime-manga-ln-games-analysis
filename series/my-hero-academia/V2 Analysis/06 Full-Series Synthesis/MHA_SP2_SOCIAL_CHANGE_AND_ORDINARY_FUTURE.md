# MHA SP2 — Social change and ordinary future

## Three timescales must meet

The final social argument is strongest when emergency rescue, sustained care and prevention are kept distinct but connected. Winning a battle changes immediate danger; it does not alone secure a child's ordinary life, restore trust or remove the conditions that can produce another crisis. Conversely, long-term reform cannot excuse failing to respond to someone endangered now. This document integrates those timescales for the [definitive entrypoint](MHA_SP2_FULL_SERIES_SYNTHESIS.md), drawing on [S4: institutions](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_AND_DISTRIBUTED_RESPONSIBILITY_SYNTHESIS.md) and [S6: care and ordinary life](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CARE_AND_ORDINARY_LIFE_SYNTHESIS.md).

## Emergency action: widened participation still needs capacities

**Textual facts.** V01:p043–059 places attention, attempted action, professional constraints and All Might's effective intervention in the same event. Later cooperation relies on specialized means: the Big Three's distinct contributions (V36:p173–179), Gentle and La Brava's rescue architecture (V38:p054–059,p081–083), and Nagant's costly intervention (p065–068). [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md), education/final-war; [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), capacity and control; [U.A. students and staff ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Big Three; [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Gentle/Nagant.

**Interpretation.** The social alternative to a solitary Symbol is not an undifferentiated crowd. People contribute through distinct knowledge, bodies and roles, and their limits matter. The final V42:p118–119 discussion recognizes finite professional hands while retaining useful institutional functions. It invites others to notice and act appropriately; it does not instruct untrained civilians to imitate elite combat.

This matters ethically as well as technically. Wider participation can otherwise become a way to demand limitless sacrifice from everyone, including children. Eri's V41:p157–159 intervention retains adult objection and bodily uncertainty. A principle of distributed responsibility must therefore include protection from instrumental use, not only praise for contributions. ### Redistribution extends practices that already existed

The early institutional record makes redistribution a problem of revising practices and expectations already in operation. U.A.'s evaluation can distinguish the quality of decisions from nominal victory; Thirteen's instruction treats hazardous capacity as a trained responsibility; Iida's evacuation role makes obtaining outside help part of successful action. The festival then combines recruitment, course mobility, support advertising and personal promotion. Those mechanisms create opportunities and unequal forms of legibility at once. Bakugo and Ochako's match demonstrates that public or professional approval may misread competent participation rather than transparently measure it. [V02](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V02_DEEP_READING.md); [V03](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V03_DEEP_READING.md); [V05](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V05_DEEP_READING.md); [S4](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_AND_DISTRIBUTED_RESPONSIBILITY_SYNTHESIS.md), early institutions.

Kamino's coordinated professional arrival and V13's explicit policy of reliability through groups make plurality an attempted institutional solution before the collapse. The later crisis consequently tests the sufficiency of actual arrangements: finite people, damaged infrastructure, prison releases, distrust and withdrawal cannot be repaired by announcing teamwork. U.A.'s refuge makes civilian participation a question of the terms on which protection is shared; support engineering, maintenance and documentation make less spectacular dependencies visible. [V10](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V10_DEEP_READING.md), coordinated arrival; [V13](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V13_DEEP_READING.md), Commission policy; [V31](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V31_DEEP_READING.md), collapse; [V33](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V33_DEEP_READING.md), refuge; [V34](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V34_DEEP_READING.md)–[V38](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V38_DEEP_READING.md), material and informational support.

**Counterreading adjudication:** continuity limits a claim of wholesale replacement, but does not make change merely verbal. The later difference lies in widened acknowledged obligation, public recognition of finite heroic bodies, different forms of participation, and selected new resources. All Might's loss of indispensability makes room for continuing personal value; Bakugo's sustained contribution helps reopen a particular practice for Midoriya. Neither history proves that all people receive comparable support. The [All Might](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_ALL_MIGHT_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) and [Bakugo](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_BAKUGO_KATSUKI_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) studies supply those individual histories; the institutional conclusion remains bounded by the visible instruments and unresolved coverage below.

## Sustained care: the aftermath has independent obligations

The Todoroki family's aftermath makes emergency cooperation a poor proxy for reconciliation. Natsuo's boundary, Shoto's ordinary initiative and Endeavor's reparative responsibility are different decisions; Toya's V42:p064 apology is addressed to Shoto, not to a collective recipient entitled to declare everything settled. p167's altar supports death by the later setting while leaving the final event unshown. [Family and civilians ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md), V26/V39/V42; [Relationship state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), family asymmetry.

**Interpretation.** Accountability is not completed when an offender feels remorse, receives punishment or joins an emergency effort. It includes repair where possible and acceptance that others may choose distance. Equally, a survivor's future cannot be defined entirely by administering someone else's atonement. Shoto's p167–168 ordinary interests are not disloyalty to the dead or refusal to acknowledge history; they show a life with additional ends.

Ochako's grief and later counseling similarly refuse an automatic passage from tragedy to healed professional purpose. V42:p150 provides material conditions for preventive work; p180–183 provides a separate movement toward personal desire. Memory can influence practice without making work a full cure or a permanent debt to the dead. ## Prevention: visible instruments, incomplete outcomes

The ending names more than a change of feeling. Hawks's p118–119 discussion, Shoji's p132 work and shared credit, the suit's p140–141 network, counseling support at p150 and teaching at p159 locate responsibility in practices with resources. The text therefore supports a direction of concrete reform. It does not provide the evidence needed to prove comprehensive equal access, permanent success or the disappearance of every harmful incentive. [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md), V42; [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Hawks; [U.A. students and staff ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), adult teaching.

| Level | What is depicted | What remains to be demonstrated |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate civilian attention | A new child's distress receives a different response, V42:p119–123 | Full subsequent safety, care arrangements and long-term trajectory |
| Local sustained practice | Shoji's regional work and shared credit, p132 | Elimination of prejudice across places and generations |
| Organized prevention | Staff and public/sponsor backing for counseling, p150 | Coverage, durable outcomes and all safeguards against new misclassification |
| Material access | Collective suit development and financing, p140–141 | A general system offering comparable access to everyone who needs it |
| Educational continuity | Midoriya's chosen teaching, p134,p159 | Perfect detection of hidden suffering or guaranteed student outcomes |
| Public recognition | Wider recognition with continued institutional differentiation, p118 | Complete removal of ranking distortions and status competition |

**Textual facts** support the middle column. The interpretation of partial reform follows from their combination; the right column is a residual boundary, not a claim that the depicted efforts secretly fail.

### Personhood tests the arrangements but does not explain every mechanism

The integrated ethic identifies a normative question: do institutions and relations leave people room to live and act beyond an assigned use? It can also explain some decisions, such as whom a rescuer notices or why an imposed role is resisted. It is not a sufficient causal explanation of the social system. Recruitment markets, professional jurisdiction, financing, communications, regional stigma and political mobilization shape what can happen even where an individual's intention is understood accurately. An account that simply calls each failure a refusal to see a person loses the means through which the failure is organized. [S4](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_AND_DISTRIBUTED_RESPONSIBILITY_SYNTHESIS.md), institutions and access; [S5](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_AND_ACCOUNTABILITY_SYNTHESIS.md), organization.

The MLA and PLF provide a strong negative control: distributed capital, media, politics, doctrine and technology can coordinate domination. Real grievance and genuine belonging can likewise be mobilized into attacks on other vulnerable people. This defeats the inference that more participants or more recognized feelings necessarily yield a just distribution. **Adjudication:** the ending's promise rests on particular uses and constraints of plural capacity, not plurality alone. Respect for persons remains a criterion for evaluating those arrangements; institutional description supplies mechanisms that the criterion cannot replace. The resulting qualification belongs to the existing reform argument, with equipment access and reform durability still unresolved under R07 and R09. ## Memory and public legitimacy are contested

Spinner's postwar memory work preserves a view of Shigaraki as friend and leader that a victorious public account could flatten. It does not make all League action defensible, and his self-criticism does not establish a pacifist identity. Similarly, Nagant's return to helping particular people does not oblige uncomplicated faith in an institution that used her. [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Spinner V42; [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Nagant V32/V38/V42; [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md), legitimacy; [Relationship state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), League belonging.

**Interpretation.** A social future capable of learning must retain inconvenient memories without converting sympathy into absolution. That includes victims beyond the protagonists, harms committed by professional systems, and the actual care that harmful groups sometimes provided. The narrative gives several such perspectives, but not a comprehensive adjudication for every victim or a complete political settlement. Its selectivity is a real limit of the final social claim.

Media makes the contest visible. Gentle feels rescue as valuable before noticing the cameras at V38:p081–083, and the students' filming then becomes a form of participation. Visibility can support shared understanding without constituting the sole measure of an act's worth. Earlier failures of public representation are not solved by this one good practice. ## Why ordinary life belongs to justice rather than reward alone

Mirio's V42:p042–043 account of moving beyond zero articulates an end for collective effort: room to enjoy a future. The coda makes that end specific and unequal. Tamaki remains socially hesitant while caring and revising an inference (p149,p151,p153). Midoriya teaches because he values it, even in a counterfactual where OFA remained (p159). Shoto chooses an interest beyond his imposed and aspirational roles (p167–168). Ochako and Midoriya approach each other (p180–183). [U.A. students and staff ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), adult life; [Family and civilians ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md), ordinary future; [Relationship state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), peers.

**Interpretation.** If hero society celebrates only maximum usefulness, its improved distribution of power could repeat the same instrumental logic at a larger scale. Ordinary desire supplies a limit: someone can be worthy of care when they are not rescuing, producing, atoning or performing an exemplary recovery. Socially enabled capacities must leave room for the user's chosen ends.

The suit is therefore meaningful without making teaching worthless. It restores access to a loved practice, while the chosen profession already expresses identity. Likewise romance is meaningful without being the only proof that Ochako has a future; the depicted mutual movement is enough to analyze without inventing a label or marriage. Ordinary life is neither trivial nor a uniform final condition.

### Chosen contribution and enjoyment can coexist

The strongest counterreading is that the manga often legitimizes ordinary activity by widening usefulness: Jiro's music makes others smile, teaching serves students, and counseling helps future children. These purposes make chosen contribution part of the ordinary-life argument. Yet usefulness and imposed function are different claims. Ochako's early desire to relieve family pressure receives parental support without becoming an immediate repayment demand; Jiro elects to develop a valued skill for a shared event; Eri's enjoyment need not earn its place by producing a later benefit. The festival's protective labor makes pleasure possible without turning that pleasure into a duty. [V03](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V03_DEEP_READING.md); [V05](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V05_DEEP_READING.md), Uraraka family; [V19](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V19_DEEP_READING.md); [V20](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V20_DEEP_READING.md), music and Eri; [S6](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CARE_AND_ORDINARY_LIFE_SYNTHESIS.md).

Three categories therefore coexist: **imposed function**, where another's project determines the person's value; **chosen contribution**, where the person values helping or work; and **enjoyment without a contribution requirement**, where an end need not justify itself through external benefit. They are not successive stages that every character must pass through. Midoriya's teaching can remain a preferred vocation after the suit becomes available. Ochako can enjoy counseling's effects without that preference proving private deprivation, as Tamaki's corrected inference cautions. Shoto's tableware and food interests make additional ends visible without requiring him to abandon the heroic life he also chose. [V42](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V42_DEEP_READING.md), p149–153,p159,p167–168; [Midoriya](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_MIDORIYA_IZUKU_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md), [Ochako](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_URARAKA_OCHAKO_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) and [Shoto](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_TODOROKI_SHOTO_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) studies.

**Adjudication:** the source supports both a broader valuation of contribution and a limit on making maximum contribution the measure of a life. Care can support work someone wants, rest they need, or an interest with no heroic payoff. Endeavor's reparative duties also show why chosen ends are not freedom from all obligation: a wrongdoer may owe repair while those harmed retain different futures, including distance. The [Endeavor study](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_ENDEAVOR_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) connects professional competence, guilt and that reduced claim over others. The ordinary-future thesis survives this pressure in a more precise form: useful activity can belong to a self-authored life, while neither contribution nor exemplary recovery is the price of deserving care.

## Recurrence remains possible

Different villain pathways require different preventive responses. A civilian's attention may interrupt abandonment; it does not alone dismantle organized grooming, hidden state violence, entrenched stigma or a person's chosen appetite for harm. Negative controls such as Shoto and Shinso refute deterministic pathways without proving conditions irrelevant. [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md), negative controls and final prevention; [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), heterogeneous motives.

**Adjudication.** The ending is best read as a shift in practices and expectations with selected evidence of success. It is more concrete than merely declaring that everyone understands everyone, and more limited than a complete institutional solution. Continued vulnerability is compatible with meaningful reform. The unresolved burden is to sustain attention, resources and accountability without turning every life into an endless emergency assignment.

This conclusion connects to the [paired adjudication](MHA_SP2_RESCUE_POWER_PERSONHOOD_ADJUDICATION.md): a reached person needs conditions beyond the encounter, and even better conditions cannot guarantee every person's choice or survival.
