# MHA SP2 — Form, revision and residuals

## This document limits the definitive claim

The [full-series entrypoint](MHA_SP2_FULL_SERIES_SYNTHESIS.md) offers an integrated interpretation, not a claim that every scene says the same thing or that every uncertainty is resolved. This supporting document records how Japanese wording and construction constrain that interpretation, how actual V1 propositions change, and where the completed V01–V42 plus UA/UAN/UAG boundary stops. Detailed argument belongs to [S7: language and form](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_JAPANESE_LANGUAGE_AND_LONGITUDINAL_FORM_SYNTHESIS.md) and [S8: claim reconciliation](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_V1_V2_CLAIM_RECONCILIATION.md).

## Perspective prevents moral language from becoming omniscient narration

**Textual facts.** V39:p146 supplies Tsukauchi's reading of All Might's risk; p155 supplies All Might's own statement of win-directed intent. V41:p132 supplies AFO's engineering claim, while p116,p121–123 depicts resistant or League-directed will. V42:p064 supplies an apology with a particular recipient. [Japanese vocabulary ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_JAPANESE_VOCABULARY_LEDGER.md), attribution; [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), All Might; [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), AFO/Tomura/Toya.

**Interpretation.** These distinctions matter because a slogan can erase the scene's distribution of knowledge. Calling All Might suicidal, Tomura wholly without agency, or Toya universally repentant gives an observer or a local utterance more authority than the sequence warrants. The definitive reading therefore treats an attributed explanation as evidence to compare, not as a shortcut past contradiction. The same discipline applies to Nighteye's V18:p072 hypothesis after p038's observed discrepancy: a character's explanation of changed fate is not established physical law. [U.A. students and staff ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Nighteye; [Visual motifs ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VISUAL_MOTIF_LEDGER.md), Foresight construction.

The same attribution control applies to rescue itself. At V42:p030 Midoriya reports the failure to save Tenko's life; at p031 All Might supplies the supportive heart-rescue interpretation. Midoriya's uncertainty and the mentor's consolation must not be collapsed into a confident protagonist verdict. The ethical reading can argue that contact mattered while retaining the speaker and the loss that prompts the reassurance. [V42 reading](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V42_DEEP_READING.md), §3.2; [paired adjudication](MHA_SP2_RESCUE_POWER_PERSONHOOD_ADJUDICATION.md).

## Recurring forms invite comparison rather than automatic equivalence

Hands, bodies, names, masks, public images and repeated spaces connect distant scenes. They can also reverse function: a reaching hand can control, rescue or invite; a damaged body can be a cost freely risked, a burden imposed, or a child's ethically contested contribution. V41:p110,p116,p157–159 and V42:p009–011,p182–183 make those differences consequential. [Visual motifs ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VISUAL_MOTIF_LEDGER.md), hands and bodies; [Callbacks and payoffs ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_CALLBACK_PAYOFF_LEDGER.md), longitudinal returns; [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), jurisdiction.

**Interpretation.** The completed design supports a reading of changed relations, not an invariant visual dictionary in which one motif always has one meaning. Gentle's V38:p081–083 sequence similarly shifts from joy without perceived applause to discovered cameras. Both moments must remain in order. A scene list that preserves the topics but loses the turn can distort the argument even when its plot facts look broadly correct.

**Residual:** recurring imagery and successful payoff do not demonstrate that every detail was planned at inception. Later origin revelations can revise earlier causal readings without proving an author's original intention. The finished construction supports inference, while the specific attributed production histories admitted below narrow what remains unobserved. Neither reconstructs an exhaustive original plan. **Interpretation.** These scenes test whether the people who were mobilized by crisis can have other ends. They strengthen the central thesis more than another undifferentiated victory montage would. At the same time, the smaller sample prohibits broad claims about complete adult private lives. The distinction between modest narrative evidence and attractive paratext is analytically decisive, not clerical.

## Maturation qualifies the direction of change

The enlarged longitudinal history changes how the integrated thesis should be stated, without requiring a separate adversarial authority. Earlier cooperative institutions, acknowledged dependence, received care and ordinary enjoyment prevent the ending from being treated as their first appearance. Conversely, their earlier existence does not prove the later arrangements unchanged. The relevant test is what becomes newly possible, publicly acknowledged or materially supported, and for whom. [S4](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_AND_DISTRIBUTED_RESPONSIBILITY_SYNTHESIS.md) and [F3](MHA_SP2_SOCIAL_CHANGE_AND_ORDINARY_FUTURE.md#redistribution-extends-practices-that-already-existed) develop that historical comparison.

The four counterreadings have different consequences. The chosen-work reading **qualifies** an absolute opposition between usefulness and ordinary selfhood: imposed function, wanted contribution and enjoyment without repayment can coexist. The early-distribution reading **rejects false novelty**, while leaving particular changes in recognition, resources and obligation to be evaluated. The institutional and organizational reading **limits explanatory scope**: personhood is a normative test and a partial account of motives, not a universal cause that replaces markets, logistics, jurisdiction or political mediation. The unlike-consent reading **requires differentiated judgment**, since a child's coerced return, a student's refusal, an enemy's resistance and a particular fatal gift do not supply interchangeable permission. [F3](MHA_SP2_SOCIAL_CHANGE_AND_ORDINARY_FUTURE.md#chosen-contribution-and-enjoyment-can-coexist) owns the ordinary-life result; [F3's institutional comparison](MHA_SP2_SOCIAL_CHANGE_AND_ORDINARY_FUTURE.md#personhood-tests-the-arrangements-but-does-not-explain-every-mechanism) retains the MLA/PLF negative control; [F2](MHA_SP2_RESCUE_POWER_PERSONHOOD_ADJUDICATION.md#consent-and-intervention-differ-by-situation) owns intervention and refusal.

Formal development makes those qualifications intelligible. Successive disclosures about Tomura revise causal judgment without deleting the intervening agent, while Toga's remembered figure changes evidentiary ownership after death. The [Shigaraki](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_SHIGARAKI_TOMURA_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) and [Toga](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_HIMIKO_TOGA_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) studies preserve the complete trajectories; [S7](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_JAPANESE_LANGUAGE_AND_LONGITUDINAL_FORM_SYNTHESIS.md) retains speaker, image and disclosure order in one interpretive responsibility. Stronger historical development does not establish an unshown rehabilitation, independent posthumous agency, universal provision or original authorial plan. Those questions remain in R01, R03, R07, R09 and R11 below; no second residual registry is created.

## What validation adds—and what it cannot add

The [aggregate reconstruction report](../04%20Character%20Modeling%20and%20Reconstruction/MHA_SP2_AGGREGATE_RECONSTRUCTION_VALIDATION.md) reviews all 30 formal probes across action, motive, speech/register, emotion and relationship handling. It preserves five qualified V41→V42 checkpoint comparisons. These are retrospective chronological holdouts of selected earlier evidence; the analyst knew the later manga. They do not produce a blind accuracy rate.

**Interpretation.** The informative pattern is often a limitation: Toya's apology exceeds an invariant-hostility expectation, Toga's gift exceeds predicted magnitude, Aoyama's departure was one of multiple plausible routes, Tamaki's use of belief develops, and Spinner's changed body alters execution. Eri's horn route and Nagant's bodily cost are not predicted by gratitude alone. Hawks's original exercise remains contaminated after the V27/V28 source repair. A model that can acknowledge these differences is more usable than a claim that familiar traits explain every result beforehand.

Supplied initial conditions also matter. Giving the analyst a surviving person, a public award, available equipment or a later opportunity does not mean the model predicted those facts. The five checkpoint results therefore retain their original directional or open-question status, including mixed Kurogiri continuity without an eligible dossier. The thirty dossier-owning tiers remain 13 `specialist_ready` plus 17 `strong`; tier breadth is not the same as thematic importance or a full private-life sample.

The historical manga-only maturation support refinements in the Midoriya, Bakugo, All Might and Aizawa dossiers belong to reconstruction at that stage, not to their frozen formal probes. A more discriminative present account can distinguish care reception, partner-specific cooperation, learner-led teaching and a bounded child-information decision without claiming another prediction or cleaner historical validation. In particular, V19 All Might applies a teaching policy already explicit in V12, and Bakugo has noncombat cooperation before the festival. Additional literary depth therefore creates no automatic probe quota. Existing identifiers, input boundaries, contamination notices and readiness remain governed by their existing owners; the [maturation implementation record](../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/MHA_SP2_MATURATION_IMPLEMENTATION_RECORD.md) records preservation and checks for the local refinements.

The completed supplemental phase adds ninety source-labeled current-support units across all thirty dossiers and qualifies each current uncertainty paragraph. These composite support units are not ninety independent observations. The schema and aggregate report add four known-outcome calibration examples for Eri, Nagant, Chisaki and AFO; they are retrospective, nonblind and unscored. The original thirty formal probes, five qualified checkpoint comparisons and Hawks contamination notice remain unchanged. The [character reconciliation audit](../04%20Character%20Modeling%20and%20Reconstruction/MHA_SP2_SUPPLEMENTAL_CHARACTER_RECONCILIATION_AUDIT.md) owns all 111 subject decisions and the remaining census dispositions; it does not create probes 31–34 or a new success rate.
