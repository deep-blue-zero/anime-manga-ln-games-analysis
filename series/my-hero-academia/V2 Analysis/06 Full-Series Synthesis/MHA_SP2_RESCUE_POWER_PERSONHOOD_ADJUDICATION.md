# MHA SP2 — Rescue, power and personhood — paired adjudication

## What the pairs test

The major pairings test whether an intervention can oppose harm while taking another person's independent existence seriously. They do not all yield the same answer, and narrative symmetry does not equal moral equivalence. This document adjudicates those tensions for the [definitive entrypoint](MHA_SP2_FULL_SERIES_SYNTHESIS.md); the detailed evidence homes are [S2: rescue](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_HEROISM_RECOGNITION_AND_FAILED_RESCUE_SYNTHESIS.md), [S3: power](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_POWER_INHERITANCE_AND_SELF_AUTHORSHIP_SYNTHESIS.md) and [S5: formation](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_AND_ACCOUNTABILITY_SYNTHESIS.md).

## Midoriya / Shigaraki: a reached person is still not an authored outcome

**Textual facts.** AFO's V41:p132 account attributes extensive engineering to himself; other pages preserve contradiction and continuing League-directed will (p116,p121–123). The final transfer is an intervention against resistance, even though the transferring side expends power willingly. V42:p018 supplies terminal separability. At p030–035 Midoriya reports that he could not save Tenko's life, while All Might offers the supportive interpretation that the heart was saved. The latter is a mentor's response, not Midoriya's confident self-verdict or omniscient confirmation. [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md), V25/V41; [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), final transfer; [Recognition and failed rescue ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RECOGNITION_FAILED_RESCUE_LEDGER.md), V42; [Relationship state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), Midoriya–Shigaraki.

**Adjudication.** Two absolute readings fail. If recognition merely reveals that the adult Tomura was never an agent, the continuing affiliation and resistance become unintelligible. If agency means complete independence from engineered conditions, the origin revelation becomes irrelevant. A constrained person can still form attachments and make harmful choices. Midoriya's refusal to ignore the child does not grant him the ability to write a safe future for that person.

The reached-heart interpretation also cannot function as full compensation for lost life. The text keeps grief, and the terminal scene does not show ordinary rehabilitation. The moral value of reaching is therefore real but limited: it opposes complete reduction to a possessed weapon without guaranteeing survival, acquittal or acceptance of a future program. **Residual:** the success of any earlier or alternative intervention remains counterfactual; the series does not stage every possible rescue strategy.

## Ochako / Toga: accepting a person is not accepting every act

**Textual facts.** V39:p098–120 combines protection, personal interest and refusal of total affirmation; p125–139 adds Toga's particular costly gift. V42's grief and p180–183 dream/memory sequence develop Ochako's surviving relation. [Recognition and failed rescue ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RECOGNITION_FAILED_RESCUE_LEDGER.md), Toga final encounter; [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Toga; [Relationship state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), Ochako–Toga; [Family and civilians ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md), grief.

**Adjudication.** The pairing tests whether an ordinary desire can be heard without being equated with the harms committed in its name. Ochako's attention does not abolish danger or speak forgiveness for all victims. Toga's response does not establish universal benevolence, and its fatal magnitude is a genuine limit of the earlier reconstruction probe. A story about accepting a person's existence must not be converted into a rule that the person has to die to make acceptance morally permissible.

The later internal Toga figure matters to Ochako's ability to want a future; it is not independent observation of Toga after death. This makes the pairing asymmetrical over time: the dead person's observed agency ends, while the survivor's relation to memory changes. **Residual:** no objective vestige survival, complete alternative life, or inevitable therapeutic result is established. The case joins recognition and reciprocity without making them guaranteed partners.

## Shoto / Toya / Endeavor: a triangle of unequal responsibilities

**Textual facts.** Endeavor's earlier instrumental ambition, Toya's body conflict and later revenge, and Shoto's effort to choose a different use of inherited power form distinct causal positions. V26:p083–092 preserves Natsuo's boundary; V39's family intervention changes an emergency; V42:p062–064 moves from Shoto's food question to Toya's narrow apology. p167's altar strongly implies Toya's death by the adult setting. [Family and civilians ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md), Todoroki family; [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Endeavor; [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Toya; [Relationship state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), siblings/father.

**Adjudication.** The triangle prevents the easy symmetry “everyone hurt everyone, so everyone should forgive.” Shared participation in stopping catastrophe does not equal shared responsibility for its origins. Endeavor can owe atonement without being owed closeness. Shoto can seek a relation without settling Natsuo's decision. Toya can apologize to one brother without demonstrating total repentance.

Shoto's later ordinary interests matter because the alternative to being his father's instrument is not perpetual definition by opposing his father. He acquires other ends. That is self-authorship within history, not freedom from all inheritance. **Residual:** the exact death event, complete family aftermath and private adult patterns remain unshown; the meaningful small exchange cannot be expanded into comprehensive repair.

## All Might / AFO: two networks, different claims over persons

**Textual facts.** All Might has always depended on predecessors and supporters despite appearing singular. V39:p154–155 ties his intervention to received bonds and win-directed intent; V42:p140–141 makes another enabling network explicit. AFO's V42:p014–015 need for Yoichi appears with possessive demand and refusal of departure. [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), All Might; [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), AFO; [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), inheritance; [Relationship state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), mentor/successor and brothers.

**Adjudication.** The contrast is not social power versus isolated power: both operate through others. Nor is it feeling versus absence of feeling: AFO needs someone intensely. The ethical contrast concerns whether the other can have purposes that are not absorbed into the user's project. All Might's support can evolve toward enabling Midoriya's chosen future; AFO makes relation into ownership.

The comparison is not a claim that All Might never imposes pressure or makes mistakes. The Symbol's obligations and Midoriya's overextension expose costs inside benevolent inheritance. OFA's final nonconsensual intervention also prevents moral purity from being assigned to a power's name. The appropriate question remains how a particular act handles competing agency and danger. **Residual:** a complete moral calculus of every emergency act is not supplied, and win intent at V39:p155 is neither a survival forecast nor immunity to bodily risk.

### The intervening mentorship history supplies the ethical difference

V03 presents two successors being educated through an admired or commanding predecessor. AFO's patience after failure can develop initiative; All Might's generous public succession demand can burden Midoriya with reproduction of the Symbol. Good and harmful purposes do not make pedagogy on one side unreal or pressure on the other side harmless. The distinction develops as All Might permits a different method, explicitly practices learner-led teaching in V12, and applies that policy to bodily mismatch and support equipment in V19. V19 is an elaboration of an existing policy, not its sudden invention. The [All Might study](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_ALL_MIGHT_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) and [S3](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_POWER_INHERITANCE_AND_SELF_AUTHORSHIP_SYNTHESIS.md) connect that history to the final enabling network. [V03](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V03_DEEP_READING.md), paired mentorship; [V12](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V12_DEEP_READING.md), teaching and Shoot Style; [V19](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V19_DEEP_READING.md), p089–094,p117–120.

Inko's challenge and All Might's later conversation with Aizawa add a temporal obligation: living presence and continued guidance can matter independently of his former rescue capacity. That is not proof that he becomes an infallible caregiver; his inability to reach the overextended Midoriya alone in V32 preserves the need for other relations. AFO's contrasting failure is also more specific than emotional ignorance. He can recognize vulnerability, cultivate a successor and intensely need Yoichi while refusing another person's departure. Aoyama's imposed debt and Tomura's possession show practical consequences of that refusal; Toya's rejection of the substitute family shows a limit to AFO's capture. **Adjudication:** attachment and selective understanding can support either enablement or domination. The decisive test concerns what independent purposes and refusals the relation permits, across time and under pressure. [V11](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V11_DEEP_READING.md), Inko; [V26](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V26_DEEP_READING.md), p158–172; [V32](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V32_DEEP_READING.md), separation; [V34](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V34_DEEP_READING.md), Aoyama; [S5](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_AND_ACCOUNTABILITY_SYNTHESIS.md), Toya and AFO.

## Consent and intervention differ by situation

**Counterreading.** A personhood-centered interpretation risks treating every offered hand, refusal or bodily contribution as the same moral transaction. The middle history defeats that equivalence. Eri's coerced childhood, Mirio's hypothetical choice, a friend's exhaustion, Aoyama's threatened family and an enemy's catastrophic violence supply different information, capacities and claims. The comparison must specify whose action is authorized, by whom, for what purpose and for how long. Age, available knowledge, coercion, bodily and third-party cost, feasible alternatives, and the ability to stop or leave all affect the judgment; affection alone answers none of them.

**Adjudication.** The cases support a duty to attend to refusal without an absolute rule that every refusal forbids intervention. Stopping harm can require acting against a will; the resulting force still needs a situation-specific justification and does not purchase ownership of the person's later life. Conversely, honoring an ordinary boundary does not require treating all emergency cases as if they shared its conditions. The terminal pairs remain difficult because outcomes cannot retrospectively supply missing consent, prove exhaustive necessity or cancel the claims of people outside the central encounter. Those limits remain with R06, R10 and R13 in the existing [residual register](MHA_SP2_FORM_REVISION_AND_RESIDUALS.md#residual-register).

## What follows across the pairs

**Interpretation.** Respecting personhood requires opposing reduction, but it does not remove the need to stop harm or decide under uncertainty. Recognition, survival, forgiveness, accountability and authorship are related dimensions with different bearers. Victims other than the protagonist retain claims; the person being reached retains a possible refusal; contributors retain limits without acquiring ownership.

This adjudication rules out both a universal redemption template and a conclusion that failed survival makes every act of recognition meaningless. It also keeps material power in view: a rescuer may understand and still lack a feasible route. The [social-future adjudication](MHA_SP2_SOCIAL_CHANGE_AND_ORDINARY_FUTURE.md) addresses what must happen beyond the encounter if these recognitions are to become less exceptional.

**Evidence status:** depicted acts and attributed statements above are textual facts; Toya's later death is explicitly a strong inference; the cross-pair judgments are interpretations. Unshown futures and mechanisms remain residuals under the [source audit](../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/MHA_SP2_PUBLICATION_AND_SOURCE_REVERIFICATION_AUDIT.md), not gaps filled from adaptations or supplemental material.
