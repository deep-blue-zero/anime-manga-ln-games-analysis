# MHA SP2 — Definitive full-series synthesis

## The final argument: enable a future without owning its person

**Interpretation.** Across its completed Japanese main manga, *My Hero Academia* makes heroism answer to two demands that never become effortless partners: stop actual harm, and respond to the person whose life is caught inside it. The story begins with Midoriya noticing and moving before he has adequate power; it confronts dependence on a singular Symbol; it ends with differentiated people and institutions taking responsibility while characters acquire room for ordinary desires. The resulting ethic is neither limitless self-sacrifice nor recognition alone. It is the work of enabling a future without assuming ownership of the person who must live it.

This is the canonical entrypoint for the definitive **four-document** V2 synthesis corpus. V01–V42, including chapter 431 through V42:p183, is complete and canonical. V41 and V42 remain the canonical sequential readings; no next main volume is pending. Supplemental inventory remains provisional and is not part of this completion claim. Their [governing questions and ownership boundaries](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_SPECIALIST_SYNTHESIS_INDEX.md#individual-literary-character-responsibilities) keep those arguments retrievable without replacing the eight comparative specialists or the thirty reconstructions.

These histories qualify the synthesis's direction of change. Dependence, cooperative institutions, received care and ordinary enjoyment are already present before the ending. The later achievement is a changed arrangement of obligations, recognized possibilities and particular resources; it is not the first appearance of those practices. The supporting adjudications below test that qualified account against unlike consent cases, harmful forms of organized plurality and the coexistence of chosen work with enjoyment beyond contribution.

## 1. Attention acquires means and a wider field of responsibility

**Textual facts.** Midoriya's movement toward the endangered Bakugo precedes OFA, while All Might's capacity makes the physical rescue possible (V01:p043–059). Training then exposes the gap between aspiration and usable power (p069–085,p151–169). Much later, Gentle's feasible rescue, Nagant's medically risky assistance and coordinated final-war roles again make means consequential (V38:p054–059,p065–068,p081–083). Early recognition does not make skill unnecessary, and late technical success does not exhaust responsibility. [Recognition and failed rescue ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RECOGNITION_FAILED_RESCUE_LEDGER.md), V01/Gentle/Nagant; [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), training and `P-V08-1`–`P-V08-7`.

**Interpretation.** Heroism expands through interdependent practices: notice danger, learn how to act, accept assistance, and ask what happens to the person afterward. This is not a ladder in which physical rescue is replaced by emotional rescue. Both are present early and remain unfinished late. The scale changes from an admired exceptional body toward differentiated participation, while the basic problem—how to respond without reducing the other to a role—recurs.

## 2. The central rescues refuse a single verdict

Midoriya reaches a relation with Shigaraki/Tenko without saving his life; Ochako's personal recognition meets Toga's particular reciprocal gift and subsequent loss; Shoto's ordinary question elicits a narrow apology within a family that remains asymmetric. V41:p116,p121–123; V42:p018,p030–035,p062–064; V39:p098–139. These outcomes are deliberately difficult to reduce to success or failure alone. [Recognition and failed rescue ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RECOGNITION_FAILED_RESCUE_LEDGER.md), final-war/V42; [Relationship state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), the three pairs.

**Interpretation.** Recognition matters because it can acknowledge a person where an assigned category has closed inquiry. It does not make their harmful acts unreal, authorize forgiveness on behalf of others, or guarantee survival. Midoriya's and Ochako's grief prevents treating a reached heart as an uncomplicated prize replacing a lost life. Toya's apology matters precisely because it is local rather than a wholesale moral conversion. The pairings test limits of rescue as well as its expansion; the [paired adjudication](MHA_SP2_RESCUE_POWER_PERSONHOOD_ADJUDICATION.md) preserves their non-equivalence.

## 3. Inheritance is an obligation to interpret, not an identity already completed

OFA gives Midoriya access to extraordinary capacity but not instant bodily compatibility, expertise or freedom from pressure. The holders' V31:p164–174 discussion makes succession biologically conditional. Final expenditure separates identity from continuing factor possession; V42:p140–141 and p159 show another support architecture becoming possible without rendering teaching a mere consolation. [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), OFA/V31/final expenditure/V42; [U.A. students and staff ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), adult Midoriya.

AFO is the contrasting case of power converted into claims over persons. Yet V42:p014–015 also shows need and attachment; describing him as incapable of understanding relationships at all is less precise than identifying possessive refusal of another's independence. OFA's final transfer is not the pure opposite in every respect: the transferring side's willing expenditure does not establish the resisting recipient's consent. **Interpretation:** power must be judged by how it is used, by whose agency it enables or overrides, and by the constraints of the situation—not solely by whether it is inherited, taken or given. [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), AFO; [Recognition and failed rescue ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RECOGNITION_FAILED_RESCUE_LEDGER.md), jurisdiction; [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), final transfer.

## 4. Formation explains unequal possibilities without erasing authorship

V41 revises Tenko's origins through AFO's engineered conditions, while p116,p121–123 and V42:p018 preserve resistance and League-directed will. Toya's family/body conflict, Toga's coercive normalization, Twice's belonging, Spinner's stigma and instrumentalization, Nagant's HPSC history, Gentle's failed aspiration and Muscular's observed appetite for violence require different explanations. [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md), `VF-V08-1`–`VF-V08-6`, segmented V25 and V41; [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), named-character trajectories.

**Interpretation.** A theory that makes every antagonist one product of social neglect cannot explain those differences; a theory that makes all acts expressions of untouched individual essence cannot explain manipulation, coercion and available alternatives. Shoto, Shinso, Kota and Bakugo function as negative controls against determinism, while differing supports prevent them from proving that structural conditions do not matter. The correct preventive question is what mechanism should be interrupted and what new conditions can support choice. No surviving, rehabilitated adult Shigaraki is shown, so neither certain reform nor inevitable recurrence can be inferred for that counterfactual.

## 5. Public responsibility becomes plural without becoming uniform

All Might's success creates genuine protection and contributes to a brittle concentration of expectation. It is not the sole cause of institutional secrecy, family violence or prejudice. The completed story retains education, licensing, professionals and resources, while showing their failures and adaptations. Hawks's recognition reforms, finite hands, Shoji's sustained regional work, counseling support, collective engineering and chosen teaching make the ending concrete (V42:p118–119,p132,p140–141,p150,p159). [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md), `HS-V08-1`–`HS-V08-7`, HPSC and V42; [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), All Might/Hawks/Nagant.

**Interpretation.** Distributed heroism means complementary capacities and widened attention. Civilian response does not abolish institutions; institutions cannot use specialization as a reason that everyone else should wait. The older civilian's response to a new child at V42:p119–123 is one material difference, not proof of guaranteed safety or universal prevention. Institutional reform remains partial and unequally evidenced. [Social change and ordinary future](MHA_SP2_SOCIAL_CHANGE_AND_ORDINARY_FUTURE.md) evaluates the visible mechanisms without claiming utopia.

## 6. Ordinary life supplies the end toward which rescue works

V42:p042–043 gives Mirio's account of moving beyond a return from danger to zero. The adult coda supplies concrete content: Midoriya's preferred teaching, Tamaki's care and correctability, Shoto's tableware and food interests, and Ochako's movement toward desired contact (p149,p151,p153,p159,p167–168,p180–183). These are not interchangeable rewards for surviving battle. They reveal persons with ends beyond their usefulness. [U.A. students and staff ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), V42; [Family and civilians ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md), ordinary life; [Relationship state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), adult peers.

**Interpretation.** Atonement, grief and remembrance must make room for those ends. Endeavor's responsibility cannot purchase his children's forgiveness; Toya's later altar does not erase Shoto's present interest in living; Ochako's memory of Toga can shape prevention without permanently excluding her own wants. Ordinary life is therefore a normative endpoint, not triviality after the “real” story. Its smallness also limits inference: a shared preference is not complete reconciliation, and mutual approach is not a verified marriage or an unshown relationship history.

## 8. Form and revision constrain the thesis

Words belong to speakers and scenes: V39:p146's observer interpretation is not p155's actor intention; V42:p064's apology has a recipient. Recurrent hands and bodies carry both rescue and control. Callbacks alter meanings without proving every payoff was planned from the beginning. V38:p081–083's changing audience and V42's movement into ordinary narrative depend on sequence rather than a list of themes. [Japanese vocabulary ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_JAPANESE_VOCABULARY_LEDGER.md), attribution; [Visual motifs ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VISUAL_MOTIF_LEDGER.md), hands/bodies; [Callbacks and payoffs ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_CALLBACK_PAYOFF_LEDGER.md), longitudinal payoffs.

V2 preserves those insights while revising specific claims: an overly sharp sacred/social inheritance binary, an unqualified Mirio succession claim, a single-stage rescue ladder, compressed All Might causation, and under-described concrete reform. ## Scope of confidence

**Textual facts** are depicted acts and attributed claims; **strong inferences** include Toya's death by the adult setting from the altar at V42:p167; **interpretations** include the integrated ethical argument above. **Residuals** include exact terminal medical details, vestige metaphysics, unshown adult relationships, the extent and durability of institutional success, equipment access and capabilities, and counterfactual rehabilitation. Completion means the authorized corpus has been synthesized and its limits named, not that the manga answers every question.

The [source audit](../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/MHA_SP2_PUBLICATION_AND_SOURCE_REVERIFICATION_AUDIT.md) records actual inspections and inherited evidence. V37:p148 and V42:p144–145 remain paratext; chapter 431's narrative remains canonical. The [residual register](MHA_SP2_FORM_REVISION_AND_RESIDUALS.md) sets the boundary for future use. The final position is demanding because it keeps means, outcomes and persons distinct: saving the world is incomplete if its survivors have no room to choose how to live in it.
