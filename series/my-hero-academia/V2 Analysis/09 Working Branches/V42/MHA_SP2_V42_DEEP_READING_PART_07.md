# 22. First-pass / common-reading adjudications

## 22.1 “Midoriya says he failed to save Tenko's heart”

**REJECT / CORRECT.**

He says he failed to save Tenko's **life**: `命`.

The following response suggests that his **heart** may nevertheless have been saved/reached.

This distinction is mandatory.

## 22.2 “Deku definitively saved Shigaraki”

**REVISE.**

Recognition and interior contact substantially succeed; AFO possession is defeated; Tomura's life is not saved; rehabilitation never becomes possible; accountability remains.

Use multidimensional rescue.

## 22.3 “Tomura was fake; Tenko was the real person”

**REJECT.**

Tomura's final requested memory is explicitly under `死柄木弔`, and Spinner's relationship is to Tomura as adult leader/friend/hero.

## 22.4 “AFO was incapable of love or attachment”

**REJECT AS TOO SIMPLE.**

His Yoichi attachment is explicit. The pathological feature is possessive/non-reciprocal attachment, not affective emptiness.

## 22.5 “The old woman's scene says institutions are unnecessary”

**REJECT.**

The volume pairs civilian initiative with institutional reform. The model is layered responsibility.

## 22.6 “Midoriya becomes a teacher because he can no longer be a real hero”

**REJECT.**

Chapter 431 explicitly states he thinks he would value teaching even if OFA had remained. He keeps teaching after support technology returns hero activity to him.

## 22.7 “The support suit simply gives OFA back”

**REJECT.**

It is a distinct technological capacity produced from data, engineering, funding, and social support. Its ethical meaning is networked inheritance, not literal OFA restoration.

## 22.8 “Endeavor is forgiven by his family”

**REJECT AS A GENERAL CLAIM.**

He accepts lifetime restitution. Family members retain differentiated boundaries; Natsuo does not owe restored intimacy.

## 22.9 “Spinner's ‘Tomura was my hero’ proves the manga endorses Tomura's destruction”

**REJECT.**

It is relationship-specific testimony about belonging and meaning, not narrator endorsement of the political/destructive project.

## 22.10 “Toga literally lives inside Ochako as a vestige”

**OPEN / UNCONFIRMED.**

Ochako herself speculates about the possibility. The safer evidentiary model is internalized relationship/memory unless a separate source establishes literal mechanism.

## 22.11 “Midoriya and Ochako are explicitly shown as an officially labeled couple”

**REVISE / QUALIFY.**

The ending strongly establishes mutual romantic movement and more future contact. It does not supply an on-page formal dating/marriage label in this volume.

## 22.12 “Shoto's ending is becoming the hero he always wanted to be”

**INCOMPLETE.**

His deeper endpoint is discovering that selfhood exceeds even the ideal heroic self he consciously wants to become.

## 22.13 “The final social message is that everyone becomes a hero”

**REVISE.**

Heroic responsibility broadens; occupational roles remain differentiated.

## 22.14 “A society where heroes are idle has been fully achieved”

**REJECT AS TOO STRONG.**

Adult heroes still work and respond to incidents. The trajectory has improved; the ideal remains partly aspirational.
