# 18. Relationship-state updates

## 18.1 Midoriya -> Shigaraki

**Entering provisional V41 state:** rescue under refusal; refusal to erase adult Tomura; final intervention through self-dispossession and collective support.

**V42 endpoint:** Midoriya continues to judge Tomura's acts unforgivable, succeeds in interrupting AFO/hatred enough for Tomura to re-emerge, fails to save his life, carries Tomura's message to Spinner, and remains uncertain enough to need another person's interpretation of whether the heart was saved.

**Final relationship definition:** adversarial recognition + attempted rescue + accountability + terminal witness/courier.

## 18.2 Shigaraki -> Midoriya

Hostile opponent -> unwanted rescuer -> person who reaches the interior wound -> final witness trusted with a message to Spinner.

Tomura does not become a grateful rehabilitated recipient. Trust is narrow and terminal.

## 18.3 Shigaraki -> Spinner

The relationship receives its clearest terminal confirmation through the fact that Spinner is the person Tomura thinks of when deciding what message should survive him.

Spinner's later declaration that Tomura was his hero validates reciprocal relational importance without proving symmetry of every feeling.

## 18.4 AFO -> Yoichi

Possessive sibling attachment reaches full emotional exposure. Need, love-language, and control remain fused.

No late evidence supports respectful separateness.

## 18.5 Midoriya <-> Bakugo

The adult relation completes several reversals:

- Bakugo grieves Midoriya's Quirk loss.
- collective suit support helps restore Midoriya's frontline capacity.
- Bakugo still competes with him.
- Bakugo identifies Midoriya's self-undervaluation.
- Midoriya can receive Bakugo's help without reducing himself to dependency or rivalry debt.

**Final relationship definition:** high-trust rivalry + mutual enablement + blunt care + preserved competitiveness.

## 18.6 Ochako <-> Toga

Toga dies after saving Ochako. Ochako carries grief and an internalized representation of Toga into adulthood.

The final relation is not “resolved because one died.” It becomes memory that influences Ochako's counseling and permission to want.

Literal postmortem vestige continuity remains unconfirmed.

## 18.7 Midoriya <-> Ochako

War-period mutual recognition -> postwar grief support -> adult delayed self-recognition -> explicit mutual desire for more contact.

The final handclasp strongly closes the romantic trajectory while leaving formal relationship labeling unstated.

## 18.8 Shoto <-> Toya

Hostile family crisis -> terminal conversation -> shared ordinary preference.

Shoto's later attention to Toya's memorial folds the relationship into ordinary adult life rather than leaving it only as battlefield trauma.

## 18.9 Endeavor -> family

Authority/family project -> remorse -> restitution.

His final legitimate action is to continue paying the cost of repair without claiming control over whether the children restore relationship.

## 18.10 Class A as adult peer network

The cohort survives as a network without requiring constant physical proximity.

Work schedules differ; careers differ; adult contact is imperfect. Yet assistance, social familiarity, banter, and crisis cooperation persist.

This is stronger modeling evidence than an idealized “they are always together” ending would be.

### Voice-model consequences

- **Midoriya:** even at final moral confrontation, uses direct human-category language rather than grand punitive rhetoric. As an adult teacher he retains explanatory, possibility-oriented speech. Personal desire remains harder for him to name than moral obligation.
- **Bakugo:** blunt/aggressive surface remains stable; developmental change appears in what the bluntness serves. Adult care still sounds like Bakugo.
- **Ochako:** professional/social warmth remains, but V42 exposes a tendency to conceal grief and defer personal wants. Adult intimate speech becomes direct only after emotional permission.
- **Shoto:** clipped, literal style persists. His most important final identity discovery is expressed through concrete food/craft preference rather than abstract self-help vocabulary.
- **Spinner:** passionate declaratives around Tomura coexist with reflective counterfactual self-critique and historical commitment.
- **AFO:** possessive first-person/permission grammar survives even in emotional collapse; vulnerability does not produce reciprocal register.
- **Endeavor:** final speech becomes obligation-oriented and non-demanding. The key change is not verbal warmth but the removal of claims on others' responses.

# 20. Visual and formal grammar

## 20.1 Final AFO confrontation: grand form -> lonely human category

The battle strips away the mythic Demon Lord presentation at the same time the dialogue strips away the category.

Form and interpretation collapse together.

## 20.2 Disintegration does not equal narrative erasure

Tomura physically disappears, but the next chapters preserve him through:

- Midoriya's uncertainty;
- Spinner's memory;
- public debate;
- causal reform.

The body ends while historical contest remains.

## 20.3 Medical aftermath replaces clean heroic tableau

The immediate postwar pages foreground wounded bodies and reconstruction rather than a pristine victory pose.

This keeps cost inside the ending.

## 20.4 Hands complete the series' relational transformation

Hands move through several final modes:

- Midoriya reaching toward Tomura;
- the old woman reaching toward the endangered child;
- group assistance enabling Midoriya;
- Midoriya/Ochako's final hand contact.

The same visual object can possess, rescue, carry, or choose relation depending on agency and consent.

## 20.5 Private scenes resist camera/public totalization

Ochako/Toga's decisive emotional ending is marked as something public recording did not capture.

Visual absence from the public archive becomes part of the argument about incomplete social knowledge.

## 20.6 Eight-year montage distributes the page

The adult ending does not allocate the future to a single central icon. Multiple professions and trajectories occupy the social field.

This visually supports pluralized hero function.

## 20.7 The final chapter repeatedly lowers stakes before restoring intimacy

Dinner, career talk, craft plans, commuting/scheduling, and a small incident create a formal decrescendo from apocalyptic structure.

The point is not lack of importance. It is the recovery of scale.
