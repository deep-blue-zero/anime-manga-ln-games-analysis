# MHA SP2 — Post-Final-War / Volume 42 Character-Model Checkpoint

## 0. Purpose and authority

The V2 synthesis architecture calls for a character-model coverage audit at major structural boundaries, including **post-Final-War / Volume 42**.

This artifact performs that checkpoint without creating character dossiers or full-series synthesis.

It answers:

> After the complete manga source sequence, which characters have enough evidence breadth for reliable conditional reconstruction, what new V42 contexts materially changed the models, and what gaps remain even though the series is over?

It remains provisional because V41 and V42 cumulative promotion has not closed.

Readiness is evidence breadth, not importance, popularity, or thematic confidence.

---

# 1. Checkpoint method

The audit uses the five readiness dimensions defined by the character-modeling schema:

1. context breadth;
2. relationship breadth;
3. language breadth;
4. longitudinal depth;
5. contradiction resolution.

The final-volume audit also gives special weight to Chapter 431 because ordinary adult life supplies a kind of evidence that high-stakes final-war chapters cannot.

A character is not promoted merely because their story is finished.

---

# 2. Held-out validation opportunity created by the V41 prediction ledger

The V41 working packet froze explicit V42 questions before the V42 source was reviewed. That creates a useful validation set.

## 2.1 Shigaraki separability

**Pre-V42 model:**  
AFO's developmental engineering was much deeper than previously known, but Shigaraki's adult agency and League attachments should not be erased. Final separability remained open.

**V42 outcome:**  
Tomura re-emerges after AFO's defeat, distinguishes himself from `先生`, speaks as himself, remains concerned with Spinner, and asks to be remembered under `死柄木弔`.

**Validation result:** **strong hit**.

**Model lesson:**  
The segmented-agency model outperformed both extremes:
- “AFO made him, therefore Tomura was never real”;
- “AFO influence was superficial.”

## 2.2 Kurogiri / Shirakumo

**Pre-V42 model:**  
Mixed identity/continuity; pure Shirakumo restoration rejected.

**V42 outcome:**  
Terminal behavior remains Kurogiri/Shigaraki-oriented rather than reverting to a pure school-era Shirakumo identity.

**Validation result:** **strong hit**, though limited by terminal rather than ordinary-life evidence.

## 2.3 OFA identity versus factor possession

**Pre-V42 model:**  
Midoriya can relinquish OFA factors without losing the biography, skill, or identity formed through them.

**V42 outcome:**  
Embers eventually extinguish; Midoriya remains a teacher/mentor and later resumes hero activity through a different support architecture.

**Validation result:** **strong hit**.

## 2.4 Distributed heroism

**Pre-V42 model:**  
Plural participation should not collapse expertise or individual agency.

**V42 outcome:**  
Adult society is differentiated: heroes, teachers, counselors, reformers, civilians, engineers, institutions, and international collaborators contribute through different roles.

**Validation result:** **strong hit**.

## 2.5 Rescue outcome

**Pre-V42 risk:**  
A too-clean interpretation could assume that reaching Tenko would translate into physical rescue.

**V42 outcome:**  
Midoriya explicitly says he could not save Tenko's **life**; the heart is separately treated as plausibly reached/saved.

**Validation result:** **important correction**.

**Model lesson:**  
Recognition and rescue outcome must remain multidimensional. Moral success does not guarantee survival.

# 4. Highest-confidence specialist dossier candidates after V42

Once V41/V42 promotion closes and the cumulative ledgers are reconciled, the following characters can support full specialist dossiers without waiting for another primary-source volume:

- Midoriya Izuku
- Bakugo Katsuki
- Uraraka Ochako
- Todoroki Shoto
- All Might
- Aizawa Shota

These characters have strong combinations of:

- multiple relationship regimes;
- strong Japanese voice evidence;

## Tier B — specialist-ready but dossier scope should stay disciplined

- Iida Tenya

The canonical index already marks them specialist-ready. A dossier should still identify the domains where the source is thinner rather than treating the tier as total omniscience.

# 5. Strong models that should not be promoted merely because the series ended

## 5.1 All For One

The final chapter deepens exactly the domain already strong:

- Yoichi attachment;
- ownership;
- grandiosity;
- loss;
- manipulation.

What remains missing is not “more villain psychology.” It is **relationship diversity**.

The manga does not provide ordinary reciprocal relations from which to infer how AFO would behave in healthy low-stakes intimacy. Promoting to `specialist_ready` would confuse depth with breadth.

**Keep `strong`.**

## 5.2 Spinner

V42 adds exceptional history/bereavement evidence.

But Spinner remains heavily concentrated around:

- stigma;
- League;
- Shigaraki;
- political/villain identity;
- postwar memory.

Ordinary adult life outside those structures remains thin.

**Keep `strong`.**

## 5.3 Aoyama

V42 provides a valuable repair-state transition, but the adult/private context sample remains too narrow for specialist tier.

**Keep `strong`.**

## 5.4 Eri

The series gives major rescue, recovery, joy, bounded agency, and relation evidence.

But a robust later adolescent/adult ordinary baseline remains limited.

**Keep `strong`.**

# 6. Candidate promotion requiring exact reconciliation: Shoji Mezo

The V40 readiness index kept Shoji at `moderate (very strongly grounded)` because repeated adult/ordinary breadth remained missing.

V42 adds precisely the kind of longitudinal endpoint relevant to that gate:

- postwar anti-discrimination work;
- durable adult/professional application of his V37 values;
- social role beyond the immediate heteromorph uprising.

This may justify `moderate -> strong`.

However, the promotion should occur only after:

1. reading the exact canonical Shoji entry;
2. incorporating any V41 semantic changes;
3. verifying the V42 adult scenes and language breadth;
4. checking whether the new evidence broadens independent decision contexts or merely confirms the same normative domain.

**Checkpoint status: `strong candidate`, not yet promoted.**

# 9. Negative evidence and anti-hallucination controls after V42

The complete manga corpus still does **not** authorize:

- a literal Toga vestige model as fact;
- a formal Midoriya/Ochako marriage/dating status beyond what is shown;
- an exact Toya death scene/date;
- a healthy reciprocal AFO relationship model;
- an ordinary civilian personality model for the old woman or new child beyond their observed actions;
- perfect postwar institutional success;
- “everyone becomes a professional hero”;
- a claim that Shigaraki would certainly have accepted rehabilitation if he survived;
- a claim that Midoriya's support suit reproduces OFA mechanics one-for-one;
- later-life behavior beyond the manga boundary without labeling it prediction.

These limits should appear in any downstream dossier that touches the relevant character/domain.

# 10. V42 endpoint and dossier sequencing recommendation

After canonical promotion and consistency audit, the recommended dossier order is:

1. **Midoriya Izuku** — widest endpoint integration; tests power-loss, vocation, rescue, and relationship modeling.
2. **Bakugo Katsuki** — excellent longitudinal validation target for stable style / changed ethics.
3. **Todoroki Shoto** — unusually rich case for identity beyond imposed and ideal roles.
4. **Uraraka Ochako** — strong case for care orientation, self-suppression, grief, Toga relation, and adult desire.
5. **Shigaraki Tomura / Shimura Tenko** — requires segmented-agency architecture and explicit counterfactual limits.
6. **Endeavor** — atonement, authority, role-conditioned behavior, family asymmetry.
7. **All Might** — symbol/person transition and support-network leadership.
8. **Himiko Toga** — love/personhood/Quirk-speech model with strict postmortem boundary.
9. **Aizawa Shota** — mature professional/teacher/relationship-conditioned model.

All For One should remain a high-value **strong** model rather than being promoted by narrative importance.

# 11. Checkpoint conclusion

Volume 42 improves the reconstruction corpus most strongly not by adding new combat feats but by adding **ordinary adult differentiation**.

The final character-model lesson matches the final literary lesson:

> A person who has been modeled only through crisis has not yet been modeled as a whole person.

The adult coda shows why the V2 method's low-stakes requirement was necessary.

Midoriya can teach.
Bakugo can recruit badly and care bluntly.
Shoto can care about tableware.
Ochako can want.
Friends can miss each other because they are busy.
A small crime can be handled without becoming an apocalypse.
A relationship can begin moving because two people finally have enough ordinary future to notice it.

For reconstruction purposes, that evidence is not epilogue decoration.

It is what makes the models human enough to use responsibly.
