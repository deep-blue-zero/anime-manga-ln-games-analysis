Volume 41 shifts from **inheritance without possession** to **agency without self-sufficiency**.

Midoriya agrees to relinquish OFA even though he calls it All Might's `宝物`; after the transferable factors are lost he says their inheritance has soaked into his `無個性` self and `残り火` remain. His agency therefore survives the loss of possession.
