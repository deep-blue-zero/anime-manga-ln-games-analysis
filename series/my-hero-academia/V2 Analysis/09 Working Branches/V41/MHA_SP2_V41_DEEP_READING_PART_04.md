# 15. Character reconstruction deltas

## 15.1 Midoriya Izuku — specialist-ready, strongly refined

### New high-value evidence

- Treats Shigaraki as a person without pretending that personhood guarantees rescue success (`V41:p019`).
- Continues rescue attempt despite Shigaraki's explicit destructive identity and refusal (`V41:p028-035`, p069 onward).
- Agrees to relinquish a treasured inheritance when the rescue strategy requires it (`V41:p049-051`).
- Uses tactical deception and inherited abilities as flexible tools rather than sacred forms (`V41:p053-068`).
- When confronted with child Tenko being struck, rescue reflex overrides purely instrumental memory-combat logic (`V41:p104-108`).
- Does not erase adult Shigaraki's chosen relationships after encountering childhood trauma (`V41:p113-123`).
- Suffers catastrophic loss of arms and is directly rescued/re-enabled by others (`V41:p140-159`).
- Explicitly accepts reciprocal dependence under Aizawa's formulation (`V41:p175`).
- Reaches the final strike through a path built by others rather than treating assistance as authorship loss (`V41:p179-195`).

### Modeling consequence

Midoriya's mature heroism should no longer be modeled as a trajectory toward maximal self-sufficiency. His agency is stable enough to survive dependence, inherited-power loss and another person's refusal.

### Contradiction preserved

He remains willing to impose intervention on a person actively refusing him when catastrophic harm is ongoing. The volume validates the attempt without dissolving the ethical friction of coercive rescue.

**Readiness:** remains `specialist_ready`.

## 15.2 Shigaraki Tomura / Shimura Tenko — specialist-ready, causally revised

### New high-value evidence

- Danger Sense theft and continued tactical agency (`V41:p005-020`).
- Explicit rejection of Midoriya's reduction to the crying-child story.
- `僕が選んだ` / chosen League connection and adult villain identity (`V41:p113-123`).
- Identifies himself as a `ヒーロー` for villains/outcasts even if hatred is removed (`V41:p121-123`).
- Origin is dramatically recontextualized by AFO's manipulation and engineered Decay (`V41:p129-136`).
- AFO's return occurs after Tenko's inner state is overwhelmed, not as proof of prior voluntary obedience (`V41:p131`).

### Modeling consequence

Shigaraki must now be modeled through **segmented agency**:

- some foundational conditions were deliberately engineered by AFO;
- family and social wounds were exploited rather than entirely invented;
- adult destructive conduct remains attributable to Shigaraki unless the source specifically shows control/override;
- League attachment is genuine evidence of chosen relational identity even if the developmental field was manipulated;
- final separability/control remains V42+ open.

**Readiness:** remains `specialist_ready`; causal model strongly revised.

## 15.3 All For One — strong, very strongly strengthened without promotion

### New high-value evidence

- Long-horizon OFA-theft design through hatred cultivation.
- Manipulation strategy built around producing experienced/free-seeming choice rather than only commands.
- Direct interference in Kotaro/Shimura family development.
- Removal of Tenko's inherited latent Quirk factor and replacement with an engineered destructive copy stripped of reversibility.
- Explicit total-authorship claim `全部だ!!!`.
- Renewed body-erasure/possession objective.
- Yoichi's loss causes the world to lose color; singular attachment is genuine but possessively structured.
- Increasing inability to perceive the differentiated value of plural agents.

### Modeling consequence

AFO is not well-modeled as affectless optimizer. He has attachment, grievance, aesthetic/narrative ideals and intense loss. His pathological move is to translate relationship into possession and causal influence into ownership.

**Readiness:** remains `strong`. The new evidence is extraordinarily deep in origin/manipulation/attachment domains but still does not provide the ordinary reciprocal relationship variation required for `specialist_ready`.

## 15.4 Aizawa Shota — specialist-ready, strengthened

- Treats Kurogiri/Shirakumo continuity as mixed rather than pure-restoration binary.
- Current teacher duty controls use of old friendship.
- Builds a distributed reinforcement network without evacuating every specialist from necessary roles.
- Accepts Eri's bounded self-authored contribution and turns it into safe operational support rather than unrestricted child deployment.
- States reciprocal dependence to Midoriya explicitly.

**Readiness:** remains `specialist_ready`.

## 15.5 Present Mic / Yamada Hizashi — relationship-specific model strengthened

- Adult grief explicitly resists pretending the old school trio can simply be restored.
- Anger toward use of Shirakumo's remains coexists with willingness to test for meaningful continuity.
- Continues to operate with Aizawa when enough relational evidence appears.

No forced tier promotion; ordinary/private breadth remains comparatively narrow.

## 15.6 Kurogiri / Shirakumo problem — moderate, very strongly strengthened

- V41 provides direct relational response and renewed Warp Gate action.
- Aizawa's model explicitly rejects “pure white restoration.”
- Current identity can contain origin continuity and imposed/artificial history simultaneously.

**Readiness:** remains `moderate`; final independent desire, ordinary affect breadth and terminal identity state remain open.

## 15.7 Eri — strong, very strongly strengthened

- Later autonomous-control evidence arrives: she understands enough of Rewind to make a bounded contribution.
- Choice is supported rather than unrestricted.
- Future ordinary desire (`お歌したい`) is explicit and causally attached to why she wants everyone to survive.

**Readiness:** remains `strong`. V41 closes part of the autonomous-control gap but not the longer ordinary/adolescent breadth gate for specialist tier.

## 15.8 Sero Hanta — emerging becomes strongly grounded

Sero finally receives high-value motive/value evidence rather than only tactical presence.

- Ordinary school/friendship pressures can motivate him.
- Rejects the idea that tragedy is preferable/necessary because it produces strength.
- Re-enters lethal danger to rescue Midoriya and later articulates the anti-tragedy view under direct confrontation with AFO.

This is enough to move from a very thin `insufficient/emerging` profile to **`emerging (strongly grounded)`**, but not to `moderate`; relationship and ordinary-context breadth remain too limited.

## 15.9 Ojiro, Sato, Mineta, Aoyama, Hagakure — strengthening without tier inflation

- Ojiro/Sato join the direct rescue/reinforcement chain after Midoriya's catastrophic injury.
- Mineta continues the V38 prosocial-risk line by entering despite extreme depletion; humorous/rough surface does not nullify the action.
- Aoyama and Hagakure convert their earlier paired action into final path-making support.

These are meaningful longitudinal atoms, not sufficient reason for broad readiness promotions.

## 15.10 Kota — received rescue becomes reciprocal participation impulse

Kota's V08-V09 resentment/rescue history now has a later consequence: seeing Midoriya fight makes him want to do something despite self-described weakness. This is a clear longitudinal prosocial development, but still within a narrow relationship domain.

# 16. Power philosophy update — power can leave the body without leaving the self

V41 makes several power propositions unusually explicit.

## 16.1 Ownership is not permanence

Midoriya makes OFA “his” over the series by embodying it, interpreting it and acting through it. In V41 he can choose to give it away. Mature ownership therefore includes the ability to relinquish possession.

## 16.2 Power transfer does not transfer complete authorship

AFO tries to prove the opposite. He gave/manipulated powers and conditions, therefore he claims the resulting person. V41 rejects that inference at the structural level even while confirming extensive manipulation.

## 16.3 Skill/identity can outlast the factor

Midoriya's claim that what he inherited has soaked into his Quirkless self makes training/history non-fungible. A Quirk factor is not the only locus of what was inherited.

## 16.4 Bounded power is still meaningful power

Eri's small Rewind window, Aizawa's damaged Erasure-dependent operations, Kurogiri's unstable portal function, wounded heroes' limited actions and civilians' noncombat support all matter. V41 is hostile to the assumption that only maximal output counts as agency.

## 16.5 The strongest power architecture is plural and role-differentiated

The final assault is not “everyone's powers combine into one superpower.” Distinct functions remain distinct. That is ethically relevant: cooperation need not erase personhood to create collective capacity.

# 17. Recognition and failed rescue update — seeing the person is necessary but not sufficient

## 17.1 Midoriya recognizes personhood without acquiring certainty

`人だよ` establishes the category. The rest of the volume preserves uncertainty, resistance and accountability.

## 17.2 Childhood recognition cannot erase adult recognition

The crying Tenko is real. So is the adult who says he chose League relationships and must be their hero. Complete recognition requires both temporal layers.

## 17.3 AFO is an extreme case of recognition without respect

AFO knows Tenko's wounds, Kotaro's grievance, OFA psychology and Yoichi's significance with exceptional precision. His failure is not lack of information. It is what he thinks knowledge authorizes: design, possession and substitution.

This strongly validates a long-running V2 proposition:

> **Recognition quality requires a theory of what the recognized person is owed. Accurate perception without limits on jurisdiction can become more dangerous, not less.**

## 17.4 Aizawa recognizes continuity without claiming identity certainty

His Kurogiri/Shirakumo model is a mature recognition posture: enough continuity to attempt relation, enough uncertainty to avoid pretending the person is simply restored.

## 17.5 Midoriya's weakness becomes legible to others

The final inversion is that the rescuer becomes a rescue subject at system scale. Classmates, Eri, Aizawa, civilians and other heroes respond because Midoriya's need is visible.

The V01 rule “see the person asking for help” has become reciprocal.

# 18. Villain formation update — AFO engineering sharply increases causal responsibility but defeats monocausal models rather than restoring one

The project had already rejected a simple `society failed them -> villain` thesis. V41 does not replace it with `AFO programmed him -> villain`.

The new causal map is richer.

## 18.1 Established AFO interventions

- exploited the Shimura family wound;
- nudged Kotaro/family conditions;
- removed Tenko's latent inherited factor before normal manifestation;
- supplied an engineered destruction-only Quirk factor;
- cultivated hatred because it was instrumentally useful against OFA;
- attempted to make the process feel self-authored rather than visibly commanded.

## 18.2 Independent/other causal layers remain

- Nana's abandonment decision and family wound are real prior conditions;
- Kotaro's abuse is his own conduct, even if nudged/exploited;
- bystander/social failure around the post-catastrophe child remains a social condition;
- AFO's grooming/mentorship supplies containment, naming and destructive direction;
- Shigaraki later learns from Stain, Midoriya, League peers and actual political events;
- League members possess their own motives and shape Shigaraki relationally;
- Shigaraki makes adult choices under conditions that are manipulated but not shown to be moment-by-moment remote control.

## 18.3 The right causal language is segmented agency

A useful current model is:

`designed vulnerability + manipulated developmental environment + engineered Quirk catastrophe + grooming + social failures + self-authored learning/relationships + chosen harms`.

Different acts may have different agency distributions.

This is much more defensible than either:

`Shigaraki is solely self-created`

or

`Shigaraki never chose anything.`

# 19. Hero Society update — distributed heroism becomes executable architecture

## 19.1 The singular-symbol critique reaches an action-level answer

Earlier volumes diagnosed All Might concentration as both enormously effective and systemically fragile. V41 finally shows the replacement architecture working at full scale:

- Aizawa coordinates;
- Kurogiri transports;
- specialists remain at other fronts where needed;
- Eri contributes a limited restorative resource;
- students create openings/pathways;
- pro heroes return or volunteer;
- civilians supply equipment and social support;
- Midoriya carries a unique final action without owning the whole causal chain.

## 19.2 “Everyone can help” is not “expertise does not matter”

The volume repeatedly preserves expertise. Aizawa chooses available positions. Rewind's exact window matters. Quirk matchups matter. Wounded capacity matters. Evacuation roles matter.

Distributed legitimacy therefore does not mean anti-professional populism.

## 19.3 Public legitimacy becomes participatory rather than merely spectatorial

Earlier hero society often converted public into audience: stadium spectators, rankings viewers, news consumers, scandal judges. V41 retains watching but increasingly adds participation: supplies, encouragement, volunteering, movement toward shared stakes.

The public is not magically competent at frontline combat. It becomes less external to the system it asks heroes to sustain.

## 19.4 Retirement/failure need not be permanent moral essence

Returning heroes matter because institutional collapse previously produced attrition. V41 allows renewed choice without pretending earlier withdrawal did no damage. Identity can be revised through later conduct.
