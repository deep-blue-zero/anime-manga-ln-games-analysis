## Governing analytical transition

**V40:** inheritance without possession.

**V41:** **agency without self-sufficiency**.

> Power, identity and relation can remain authored without remaining individually self-contained. Midoriya relinquishes OFA but retains the biography it formed; Shigaraki is deeply engineered without becoming wholly AFO-authored; Kurogiri/Shirakumo is treated as mixed rather than purely restored; Eri contributes within a limit while keeping an ordinary future; and the final attack is individually enacted but collectively enabled.

Supporting rules:

- **rescue without annexation**;
- **designed conditions are not total authorship**;
- **weakness can be relationally generative without suffering being intrinsically good**;
- **distributed heroism preserves differentiated roles and expertise**.

## Major state closures

### Resolved

1. `危機感知` cliffhanger: Shigaraki stole Danger Sense; Fourth separates; Shigaraki can use it.
2. OFA transfer plan: Midoriya/vestiges deliberately expend inherited factors through Shigaraki's interior wound.
3. Shimura-memory entry: Midoriya reaches child Tenko and protects him from Kotaro's strike.
4. Shigaraki adult agency claim: `僕が選んだ` / League hands / `敵の…ヒーロー` are explicit.
5. AFO internal return: occurs after Tenko's interior state is overwhelmed.
6. AFO developmental engineering: direct Shimura-family manipulation, latent-factor removal, engineered destruction-only Quirk copy, hatred cultivation for OFA theft.
7. Midoriya arm loss/recent Rewind: both arms lost and restored within Eri's short temporal window.
8. Kurogiri/Warp Gate reinforcement route reopens.
9. Aizawa explicitly rejects pure-restoration Kurogiri/Shirakumo model in favor of mixed/altered continuity.
10. Eri's horn contribution is self-chosen, bounded and future-oriented through desire to sing.
11. Sero explicitly rejects the thesis that tragedy is desirable/necessary for strength.
12. AFO's Yoichi attachment is affectively unique: Yoichi's loss drains color from his world.
13. Midoriya states OFA has been lost while `残り火` remain at the V41 boundary.
14. Aizawa explicitly states reciprocal need between Midoriya and the group.
15. Final V41 strike is enabled by distributed path-making/support.

### Revised / complicated

1. **Shigaraki formation:** AFO's causal responsibility is much larger than previously authorized; Shigaraki's later agency is not thereby zeroed out.
2. **Rescue:** child-Tenko recognition must coexist with adult-Tomura recognition.
3. **AFO:** total-authorship claim becomes a central possession/ownership tell rather than neutral omniscient narration.
4. **Kurogiri/Shirakumo:** continuity is mixed; “restore the true Shirakumo” is too binary.
5. **OFA:** factor possession, embodied learning and identity separate.
6. **Hero society:** plural participation is a network architecture, not anti-expertise occupational flattening.
7. **Strength/trauma:** recovery after tragedy is possible; tragedy is not narratively justified as necessary strength production.
8. **Midoriya:** final strength includes visible need / reciprocal dependence rather than maximal self-sufficiency.
