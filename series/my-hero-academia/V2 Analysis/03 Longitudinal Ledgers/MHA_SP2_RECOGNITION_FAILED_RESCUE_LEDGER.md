# MHA SP2 — Recognition and Failed Rescue Ledger
































## V01 — recognition as structural engine
- Inko loves Midoriya but apologizes rather than affirming the dream he wants recognized.
- All Might initially says quirkless professional heroism is unrealistic, then revises after witnessing conduct.
- Sludge scene: Bakugo visibly needs help, specialists/crowd do not resolve the crisis, quirkless Midoriya moves.
- All Might's `君はヒーローになれる` is recognition forced by observed behavior.
- Uraraka recognizes Midoriya's rescue conduct and tries to transfer points.
- U.A. retroactively recognizes unscored rescue through rescue points.
































**Key locators:** V01:p22-24; p35-39; p43-59; p124-134.
































## V02 — recognition becomes allocation of function
- Midoriya recognizes Iida as the better functional leader and yields class presidency.
- Teachers recognize Iida's speed as the best available rescue/communication resource and entrust him with leaving USJ.
- Midoriya rejects Mineta's assumption that his Quirk is useless and constructs a team application for it.
- Yaoyorozu recognizes Iida as the strongest battle-trial performer despite nominal defeat.
- Bakugo is forced to recognize Todoroki's strength and Yaoyorozu's valid criticism.
































**Key locators:** V02:p069-070; p081-084; p101-104; p153-167.
































## Current synthesis
Recognition in early MHA is not only emotional affirmation. It increasingly means **correctly perceiving what a person can do, what role they should occupy, and what value is invisible to naive status metrics**.
































# Volume 3 cumulative update
































### Recognition becomes industrialized
V01's recognition problem is intimate: can one person see heroic worth before conventional ability proves it? V03 scales recognition into a mass institution. The Sports Festival packages student ability for stadium audiences, media, professional scouts, companies, and internal U.A. reclassification (V03:p091-115; p157-179).
































### Midoriya: private recognition → public existence
All Might asks Midoriya to make the world know that he has arrived (V03:p100-104). The obstacle race then culminates in the announcer calling attention to `緑谷出久の存在を!!` (V03:p175-178). Public legibility is achieved through Midoriya-specific ingenuity rather than resemblance to All Might.
































### Recognition can revise classification
Shinso's General Studies position and the transfer rules establish that public performance can force an institution to reconsider its earlier sorting decisions (V03:p113-115).
































### Recognition also creates vulnerability
The first-place student is immediately given a ten-million-point target, turning recognition into concentrated exposure (V03:p184-186).
































# Volume 4 cumulative update
































### Shinso — correctable misrecognition
- Brainwashing is socially coded as `敵向き`, and U.A.'s robot practical exam under-displays its usefulness (V04:p134-136; p153).
- The Sports Festival generates alternate evidence; after his loss, public observers recognize that Shinso appears Hero Course-capable (V04:p154-156).
- This is recognition failure with an available correction path, not yet catastrophic abandonment.
































### Midoriya — recognition of present privilege
- Shinso's accusation that blessed people cannot understand makes Midoriya remember his own old Quirkless resentment, then acknowledge `僕は恵まれた` (V04:p145-146).
- Recognition now includes accurately locating one's **current** position relative to another person rather than using past suffering to erase present asymmetry.
































### Todoroki — spectacular public body / private suffering
- The stadium sees overwhelming ice output; after hearing the family history, Midoriya sees Todoroki melting the ice with his left side and experiences the image as `ひどく悲しく` (V04:p164-171).
- Recognition depends on seeing the private meaning beneath publicly rewarded power.
































### Ojiro / Shoda — refusing misallocated recognition
- Both reject tournament advancement they cannot experience as self-authored because of Brainwashing (V04:p113-115).
- Recognition ethics therefore includes refusing status that contradicts private knowledge of agency.
































## Current synthesis through V04
> **Recognition in MHA is becoming bidirectional: society must learn to see people accurately, and people must also locate themselves accurately inside systems of privilege, support, authorship, and public evaluation.**
































# Volume 5 — recognition as accurate attribution
































## Uraraka: visible struggle versus concealed agency
- Crowd perception reduces her to a vulnerable target; Aizawa identifies Bakugo's vigilance as recognition of her actual strength (V05:p016).
- Her meteor strategy retroactively reclassifies earlier “failed” exchanges as deliberate material accumulation (V05:p007-025).
- Midoriya later corrects Bakugo's misattribution and explicitly returns authorship to Uraraka (V05:p031).
































## Todoroki: recognition without possession
- Midoriya recognizes that Todoroki's fire is not Endeavor's property: `君の力` (V05:p080).
- Endeavor recognizes output but repeatedly fails to recognize agency, reading Shoto's fire as acceptance of `俺の野望` (V05:p087; p098-100).
































## `余計なお世話` and the limits of rescue authorization
- All Might names unwanted/unrequested meddling as part of hero essence (V05:p104-105), explicitly validating the V01 rescue-before-permission pattern.
- The phrase preserves friction: perceiving need may justify intervention without making the intervener omniscient or automatically right about method.
































## The rescuer as someone others recognize and protect
- Recovery Girl and Inko force Midoriya to see himself as a person others may need to protect from his own method (V05:p101-108; p186-187).
- This is an early inversion of the series' rescue gaze: the person who sees everyone else's distress must become legible as vulnerable too.
































## Family recognition
- Uraraka's father sees the pressure inside her filial motive and tells her not to hurry (V05:p034-038).
- Shoto attempts to re-enter relation with Rei, but her present-day response remains unresolved; intention must not be mistaken for completed rescue (V05:p180-184).
































# Volume 6 — noticed distress, insufficient intervention, and experiential recognition
































## Iida: perception succeeds while intervention fails
- Midoriya and Uraraka notice Iida's abnormal state before internships, but Iida closes the conversation and they do not press further (V06:p037-038).
- Midoriya's retrospective narration says he later regretted not speaking more strongly.
































**Revision:** failed rescue is not always invisibility. A three-stage model is now required: notice → correctly infer severity → intervene enough to alter trajectory.
































## Gran Torino: causal recognition beneath visible technique
- He recognizes that Midoriya's problem is not only low skill but the conceptual hold of All Might admiration (`足枷`) (V06:p057).
- Effective mentorship is shown as diagnosis of the cause beneath the performance symptom.
































## Manual: correct recognition without sufficient leverage
- Manual identifies Iida's `私怨` and warns him directly (V06:p109-111).
- The warning does not change Iida's course, demonstrating that correct adult recognition can still fail when the distressed person refuses/overrides it.
































## Stain: motive recognition without personhood
- Stain correctly distinguishes Iida's revenge from Midoriya's rescue but converts recognition into execution rather than correction.
- Recognition quality alone is insufficient; the recognizer's theory of what another person is owed remains decisive.
































## Todoroki: trauma-conditioned recognition becomes help
- `恨みつらみで動く人間の顔なら よく知ってた` explicitly turns Todoroki's own history into perception of Iida's danger (V06:p184-185).
- His `なりてえもんちゃんと見ろ` redirects attention to desired self rather than hated offender (V06:p189).
































## Midoriya: hidden-person search
- In the Hosu spectacle, Midoriya reasons toward Iida's likely hidden danger and searches away from the central visible crisis (V06:p128-147).
- V01's “see the person asking for help” pattern is becoming inferential rather than only directly visual.

## Rescue success and rescuer degradation
































- Kota survives; Midoriya's body is severely damaged while the wider crisis remains active.

## Villain recognition of Bakugo
































- The League recognizes real surface aggression but may infer the wrong governing values from it.
































**New distinction:** accurate observation does not guarantee accurate person-modeling. Misrecognition can occur at the inference layer rather than the perception layer.

# Volume 13 cumulative updates


## Rescue recognition becomes diagnostic


Midoriya's opening HUC error demonstrates that seeing a person in danger is not enough; the rescuer must recognize what kind of help the person actually needs through breathing/injury assessment and `トリアージ` (`V13:p019-021`).


## Reassurance is part of being recognized as safe


Midoriya deliberately invokes All Might's reassurance function and tells victims they will be saved (`V13:p022-023`). HUC evaluates the hero from the recipient side: correct physical action and usable social communication are distinct.


## Bakugo correction: recognition without reassurance


Bakugo accurately recognizes which victims are lightly injured/mobile, but his delivery earns deductions (`V13:p035-036`). This is not failed recognition of need; it is failed translation of recognition into a civilian-compatible rescue relationship.


## Todoroki: misrecognition through resentment


Todoroki explicitly realizes `…見てなかったんだな本当に`: anti-Endeavor fixation caused him not to recognize Inasa as a person in his own right (`V13:p070`). The failure later contaminates professional cooperation before being corrected.


## Midoriya recognizes hidden Bakugo distress


At Ground Beta, Midoriya realizes Bakugo has carried Kamino guilt and the OFA/All Might secret without a receiver. `僕しかいないんだから` reframes his participation in the fight as relationship-specific burden reception (`V13:p180-184`).


## Rescue/system boundary


V13 validates networked rescue: large-scale saving requires role distribution, parallel processing and handoff; isolated heroic effort can itself waste time (`V13:p021-044`).

All Might admits that Bakugo's visible strength caused him to under-read the child's guilt and apologizes for letting him carry too much (`V14:p041-042`). This is a direct correction of failed adult recognition.

## Rescue/victory become reciprocal recognition

All Might identifies complementary deficits rather than assigning one boy the moral role and the other the combat role: each needs what the other has (`V14:p044`).

## Nighteye recognizes evidence while withholding legitimacy

Nighteye observes Midoriya protecting rare All Might memorabilia during a failed seal test and grants internship access, but still refuses to recognize him as the rightful OFA successor (`V14:p192-196`). Recognition is dimension-specific: competence evidence can be credited without conceding the larger identity claim.

## Eri: distress recognized, rescue status OPEN

Midoriya registers a frightened child's immediate condition at first contact (`V14:p199-202`), but the volume ends before the encounter becomes a completed rescue decision. Any claim that V14 shows Midoriya “letting Eri go” or learning strategic restraint is out of boundary and deferred to V15.


# Volume 15 cumulative updates

## Eri — recognized danger, incomplete rescue, preserved return path

- Midoriya recognizes visible fear and refuses to `やり過ごす` a frightened child (`V15:p015`).
- Chisaki's threat display induces Eri to release Midoriya and return; Mirio stops pursuit because deeper intrusion could compromise the investigation (`V15:p018-020`).
- The failure is therefore not misrecognition. It is a case where correct recognition does not yet produce safe/complete extraction.

**Current classification:** failed immediate rescue under constrained information, with later rescue capacity intentionally preserved.

## Failure residue becomes future action

At the briefing, Midoriya and Mirio reject scapegoating others and commit `今度こそ必ず エリちゃんを…!! 保護する!!` (`V15:p142-143`). The failed encounter remains morally active rather than being normalized as unavoidable procedure.

## Eri remembers recognition

In captivity Eri remembers Midoriya's hand as `優しかった` (`V15:p163`).

**Current proposition:** recognition can be causally valuable before rescue succeeds, but that value must not be used to romanticize continued captivity.

## All Might predicted death — recognition of future vulnerability

Midoriya learns All Might's death has been foreseen and refuses to normalize it as an already-completed fact, asking him to live to witness the successor's future (`V15:p047-055`).

**Current proposition:** failed rescue/recognition analysis extends temporally: recognizing a predicted loss can become grounds for prevention effort rather than anticipatory surrender.

# Volume 16 cumulative updates

## Kirishima — failed intervention as future action architecture

Kirishima's middle-school freeze is a recognition-without-action failure: he perceives the threat and knows the action he thinks he should take, but cannot make his body move (`V16:p132-137`). Crimson Riot's own failed-rescue story then gives him a framework in which the pain of not saving becomes a reason to enter danger later (`V16:p141-144`).

**Longitudinal significance:** failed rescue/failed intervention need not resolve into either denial or permanent paralysis. In Kirishima's case, remembered regret becomes a self-authored action threshold. The project should still distinguish this from healthy self-forgiveness; his anti-regression motivation remains partly shame-driven.

## Mina — recognition/action without fearlessness

Mina's successful middle-school intervention is followed by explicit fear (`怖かったァァ`). This is a useful control case against equating successful rescue action with low fear (`V16:p132-135`).


## V17 - from failed extraction to restored rescue agency

- V15 established correct distress recognition without sufficient leverage and Eri's threat-conditioned return.
- V17 makes the moral mechanism explicit: Eri chooses self-surrender because she expects other people to be harmed for her sake (`V17:p050`, `p145-148`).

- Chisaki again attempts to use other people's injuries to make Eri return; the pattern is therefore coercive conditioning, not preference discovery.
- Eri begins moving toward the rescuers, converting recognition into partially restored agency (`V17:p145-151`).
- At `V17:p189`, Midoriya asks Eri to lend her power. The rescue has become participatory, but physical victory/extraction remains OPEN for V18.

## Volume 18 - changed forecast, incomplete recovery, and unreadable outreach

- **Eri:** rescue succeeds physically, but medical/Quirk instability remains; extraction and restoration are different stages (`V18:p025-091`).
- **Nighteye:** explicitly recognizes a future different from his Foresight while leaving mechanism unknown (`V18:p038`). Recognition of branch possibility arrives too late to remove injuries already incurred.
- **Mirio/Midoriya:** Midoriya recognizes Mirio's loss as a possible legitimacy debt; Mirio rejects the zero-sum interpretation and returns recognition to Midoriya's actual conduct (`V18:p087-090`).
- **Endeavor/Shoto:** Endeavor recognizes rank alone is insufficient and proposes future change; Shoto does not grant immediate recognition/forgiveness (`V18:p172-174`).
- **Aoyama/Midoriya:** Midoriya notices unusual interpersonal behavior but cannot interpret motive. V18 intentionally ends with recognition-before-understanding (`V18:p179-183`).


# Volume 19 cumulative updates

- Learned guilt persists after Chisaki's physical removal (`V19:p049-051`).
- Eri cannot access a normal smile on demand; Midoriya explicitly concludes `この子はまだ 全然救われてやしない` (`V19:p050-051`).

## Recognition taxonomy — V19
- **similarity recognition:** Aoyama sees Midoriya through shared body/Quirk mismatch; care is sincere but projection-prone.
- **value recognition:** Class 1-A sees public value in Jiro's private music while returning authorship to Jiro.
- **recovery recognition:** rescuers recognize that safety without ordinary joy is incomplete.
- **public-inscription recognition:** Gentle/La Brava center recording, audience and historical/public visibility; significance does not itself confer legitimacy.

# Volume 23 cumulative update — failed rescue and interpretive capture

## Shigaraki/Tenko: AFO captures the meaning of failed rescue

## Volume 27 update - late rescue as threatened dispossession

Hawks/Twice adds a distinct failed-rescue structure.
