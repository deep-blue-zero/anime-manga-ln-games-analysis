# MHA SP2 — Power Philosophy Ledger
































## Purpose
Track what characters and institutions imply power is **for**, what legitimizes its use, and how possession differs from ethical mastery.
































## V01
































### Midoriya Izuku — entrusted rescue
- Heroic conduct precedes receipt of One For All.
- Power is desired primarily to save, not dominate.
- OFA is introduced through `受け継ぐ / 受け取る / 値する`: voluntary transfer and worthiness.
- Problem: good intent without bodily control is professionally unusable.
































**Locators:** V01:p43-59; p61-85; p107-134; p151-169.
































### All Might — reassurance and burden
- Power provides rescue, deterrence, and public reassurance.
- Public heroic body conceals finite private body.
































**Locators:** V01:p35-39; p43-59.
































### Bakugo — power as comparative proof
- Power anchors rank, identity, future legitimacy, and superiority.
































**Locators:** V01:p14-24; p171-181.
































## V02
































### Thirteen — dangerous capacity directed toward life
- Many Quirks can easily kill.
- Licensing/regulation makes dangerous embodied power socially manageable.
- Hero education teaches application `人命の為に`.
- Explicit doctrine: power is not for hurting people but for saving them.
































**Locator:** V02:p118-119.
































### Midoriya — inheritance toward self-authorship
- One For All is received from another person, but Midoriya intends to make it properly his own and surpass Bakugo with `僕の力`.
- Bodily preservation begins to become part of responsible power use: `腕は犠牲に出来ない！`.
































**Locators:** V02:p079-081; p163.
































### Bakugo — full-strength opposition as status validation
- A victory matters only if it proves superiority over the opponent's strongest available self.
- Extreme risk is accepted when Bakugo trusts his own control.
































**Locators:** V02:p032-057.
































### All Might — power as social infrastructure
- All Might explicitly says his disappearance would draw superhuman society toward evil.
- Responsibility therefore includes not only action but guidance.
































**Locators:** V02:p076-077.
































### Shigaraki — overwhelming power and effective quirklessness
- Describes Aizawa under overwhelming force as effectively `無個性`.
- Treat as an OPEN ideological seed rather than complete philosophy.
































**Locator:** V02:p196.
































### Nomu — body as instrument
- Introduced as `対平和の象徴`, a body/function designed against All Might.
- Personhood implications remain unresolved at this boundary.
































**Locator:** V02:p187.
































## Current synthesis
> **Power is morally underdetermined. Heroic legitimacy depends on entrustment, control, judgment, protection of life, and increasingly the capacity to keep persons from becoming mere instruments of someone else's objective.**
































## V03
































### Rival successor systems — reassurance versus fear
- All Might states that he entrusted Midoriya with power because he wants Midoriya to inherit him, then asks Midoriya to make the world know that he has arrived (V03:p100-104).
- Shigaraki's unseen teacher argues that those who cannot move freely need a symbol and asks Shigaraki to make his fear known to society (V03:p070-072).
- Both systems therefore understand power as something reproduced through a successor who must become publicly legible. The ethical opposition is not succession versus no succession, but what relation to society the successor embodies: reassurance/rescue versus fear/destabilization.
































### Midoriya — power without OFA
- The obstacle race is won primarily through observation, scavenging, environmental recombination, and risk timing rather than direct OFA output (V03:p136-180).
- This strengthens the proposition that Midoriya's analytical/adaptive competence is not reducible to inherited superpower.
































### Support and business capacities
- Hatsume treats invention/development as a power that must be publicly demonstrated to companies (V03:p157-158).
- Business Course observation frames hero activity as requiring management/market knowledge (V03:p178-179).
- Heroic power is increasingly embedded in technical, economic, and representational infrastructure, not only combat bodies.
































# Volume 4 cumulative update
































### Inheritance versus ownership
- Todoroki's fire is biologically his but psychologically experienced as Endeavor's claim because the body was deliberately produced through `個性婚` to surpass All Might (V04:p095-103).
- His right-only strategy therefore treats **non-use** as a moral act: winning without the father's side would `完全否定` Endeavor.
- This becomes the negative mirror of Midoriya's V02 desire to turn received OFA into `僕の力`.
































### Entrustment versus manufacture
- All Might recognized Midoriya before transfer and invited him to inherit power/role; Endeavor states that Shoto is a child `そうするべく つくった` for surpassing All Might (V04:p096-100).
- Current synthesis: legitimate inheritance leaves room for the successor to author what the inheritance becomes; instrumental inheritance authors the successor for someone else's objective.
































### Quirk moral coding is not moral identity
- Shinso's Brainwashing is repeatedly called crime-/villain-suited by peers, yet Shinso wants the Hero Course (V04:p134-156).
- Power's social symbolism can stigmatize its user before conduct is known.
































### OFA becomes relationally inhabited
- Midoriya sees predecessor-like silhouettes while Brainwashed, his body moves against conscious command, and he wonders whether he sensed the `気配` of people who carried OFA like a torch (V04:p140-144).
- Exact vestige ontology remains OPEN at the V04 prospective boundary.
































### Personhood / toolhood becomes explicit text
- Shoto refuses to become Endeavor's `屑の道具` (V04:p100-101).
- This is the strongest source-grounded support so far for the project proposition that illegitimate power converts persons into instruments.
































## Current synthesis through V04
> **Power is morally underdetermined, but inheritance is ethically structured. A power can be received, imposed, stigmatized, refused, or reclaimed. The central legitimacy question is whether the person who bears it remains an author of its meaning rather than a tool of someone else's project.**
































# Volume 5 — recognition without possession
































## Accurate recognition versus ownership
- Aizawa interprets Bakugo's vigilance toward Uraraka as `相手の力を認めてる`: taking another person's capacity seriously changes one's behavior (V05:p016).
- Midoriya corrects Endeavor through `僕は オールマイトじゃありません / 轟くんも あなたじゃない` and Todoroki through `君の力`, making personhood and power ownership explicit (V05:p039-043; p080).
- Endeavor demonstrates the opposite failure: he recognizes Shoto's enormous capacity while treating it as `義務`, `テストベッド`, `俺の野望`, and a superior version of himself (V05:p039-043; p087; p098).
































**Revision:** `STRONGLY STRENGTHEN` Hypothesis 7 — legitimate power relations increasingly require recognition of the power-bearer as a separate author rather than a tool.
































## Inheritance and generative succession
- All Might reveals he too was Quirkless and was raised by a predecessor who already had a Quirk (V05:p109-110).
- He then explicitly refuses simple reproduction: Midoriya has exceeded his imagination and must derive something unique (V05:p110-111).
- This contrasts with Endeavor's reproductive/genetic attempt to create a `上位互換`.
































**Current formulation:** healthy inheritance is not self-creation from nothing and not replication of the predecessor; it is entrustment that permits a different person to author what was received.
































## Self-sacrifice and the relational body
- Recovery Girl refuses repeat catastrophic healing and demands another method (V05:p101-108).
- Inko's fear makes Midoriya recognize that bodily risk is experienced by others; he seeks `誰も心配することのない 僕なりのやり方` (V05:p186-187).
































**Revision:** Midoriya's sustainability ethic develops from operational self-preservation (V02) into relational responsibility (V05).
































## Bakugo's absolute evaluation
- `世間が認めても俺が認めてなきゃゴミ` plus All Might's `不変の絶対評価` reveals that Bakugo's rank drive depends on internally valid proof, not mere public status (V05:p169-175).
- Power/victory therefore functions for Bakugo as evidence whose causal legitimacy matters.
































# Volume 6 — enactment, embodiment, and legitimate force
































## Power as continuous embodiment
- Gran Torino identifies All Might admiration as a `足枷` and Midoriya realizes `個性は体の一部` (V06:p057; p078-084).
- Full Cowling changes OFA from selected sacrificial discharge toward continuous bodily baseline.
































**Revision:** `STRENGTHEN` the V02 `僕の力` trajectory. Ownership now includes embodiment and successor differentiation, not merely verbal/psychological claim.
































## Claimed identity versus power direction
- Iida claims `インゲニウム` while using power for private vengeance (V06:p124-125).
- Stain's critique `己の為に力を振るうな` identifies direction of force as morally decisive (V06:p142-143).
































**Current formulation:** heroic identity does not legitimate force; force must remain other-directed and accountable in actual use.
































## Institutional restraint versus private moral sovereignty
- Manual explicitly rejects `私怨`/`私刑` and grounds hero coercive authority in conditional public trust and law (V06:p110-111).
- Stain grounds authority in his own moral criterion and turns those who fail it into `正しい社会への供物` (V06:p144).
































**Revision:** `STRONGLY STRENGTHEN` Hypothesis 7. Legitimate power preserves persons and limits the user's jurisdiction; illegitimate power converts private judgment into sovereignty over other lives.
































## Pain as an attention-direction test
- Iida's grief narrows action toward offender/punishment.
- Todoroki uses his own resentment history to recognize Iida and redirect him toward desired self (V06:p184-189).
































Pain is morally underdetermined: it can narrow the world around the self or enlarge recognition of others.

## Bodily consequence as moral memory

**Locator:** V08:p012-045; p047-068; p082-087; p096-105; p129-147; p184-186.

**Status:** `STRENGTHEN`
































Bakugo's register stays abrasive while his action becomes cooperative. Todoroki remains sparse while yielding planning authority to Momo. Jiro remains blunt while helping Koda access his own capacity.
































Power-sharing does not require personality homogenization; the relevant ethical criterion is whether another person's agency can enter the plan.

All Might explicitly says there are many people he could not save (`あるよ たくさん`) and `悔しいが 私も人だ` (V08:p151-153). His smile is therefore deliberate reassurance under known finitude, not evidence of a personal omnipotence delusion.
































**Consequence:** the Symbol's structural danger lies partly in how society consumes concentrated reassurance, not simply in Toshinori believing his own myth.
































## P-V08-4 — Destructive power becomes more socially dangerous when given a coherent symbolic object
**Status:** `NEW / STRONGLY STRENGTHEN`
































Shigaraki's `全部 オールマイトだ` and `今日からそれを 信念と呼ぼう` reorganize diffuse destruction into a consciously named anti-All-Might conviction (V08:p141-147).
































## P-V08-5 — AFO's social power includes incorporation
**Status:** `NEW`
































`排斥ではなく受容を` (V08:p114-116) establishes strategic absorption of heterogeneous recruits. This is not ethical pluralism; it is a power strategy that avoids wasting useful desire.
































## P-V08-6 — Quirk capacity is trainable bodily infrastructure
**Status:** `NEW / STRENGTHEN`
































Aizawa distinguishes mental/technical growth from Quirk growth and begins explicit `個性を伸ばす` conditioning (V08:p184-186). Power mastery now has a separately named embodied-capacity layer.
































## P-V08-7 — Training risk is justified as future survival capacity, not status alone
**Status:** `STRENGTHEN`
































`死ぬ程キツイが くれぐれも死なないように` makes the paradox explicit: supervised high strain exists so that future unsupervised emergencies are less likely to kill the user or the person depending on them.
































# Volume 9 cumulative updates
































## Power without designed guarantees
































V09 moves power use from teacher-authored adversity into an uncalibrated lethal environment. A correct tactic can fail because the opponent is simply beyond the user's current safe output; no external authority guarantees a `勝ち筋`.
































## Midoriya — sustainable embodiment versus value-conditioned override
































- Full Cowling has materially improved ordinary embodiment/control, but Muscular establishes that Midoriya can knowingly override sustainability constraints when a victim's survival appears directly contingent on him (`V09:p074-108`).
- This revises “self-injury = technical incompetence.” Current model distinguishes **control failure** from **value-conditioned catastrophic expenditure**.
- Local success creates a system cost: saving Kota leaves Midoriya degraded while the broader attack continues.
































**Open question:** how can entrusted rescue power remain willing to pay extreme cost without repeatedly destroying future rescue capacity?
































## `1000000%` epistemic constraint
































- Direct datum: Midoriya names the climactic Muscular strike `ワン・フォー・オール 1000000%` (`V09:p107-108`).
- Do not convert the label into a literal calibrated multiplier without source measurement support.
































## Muscular — power as voluntary domination
































- Muscular's power ethic is appetite-centered: lethal combat is play, strength licenses enjoyment, and moral rescue language is mocked as beautification.
- This is distinct from Stain's purification ethic and Shigaraki's developing symbolic strategy.
































## Tokoyami — output versus agency
































- Dark Shadow under darkness/stress demonstrates that greater output can reduce bearer control.
- Power magnitude and self-authored agency must remain separate variables.
- Peer/environmental regulation can make unstable power useful without pretending it is fully controlled.
















# Volume 10 update — protective authority, future capacity and skill residue

- Doctor explains `火事場の馬鹿力` as limiter override and warns that two or three comparable arm injuries could leave Midoriya unable to use his arms (`V10:p074-075`).
- V09's value-conditioned catastrophic rescue model therefore acquires an explicit longitudinal cost: future self-damage can destroy later rescue capacity.
















## Best Jeanist: possession is not mastery
















- AFO attributes Best Jeanist's effectiveness to `相当な練習量と 実務経験` and rejects the Quirk as unattractive for Shigaraki (`V10:p175-177`).
- **Proposition:** a Quirk's transferable capacity is not identical to the practiced expertise that makes a professional exceptional.
















## AFO: power curation is successor-specific
















- `弔とは 性の合わない“個性”` shows AFO evaluating abilities against Shigaraki's temperament rather than maximizing raw spectacle (`V10:p177`).
- Successor engineering therefore includes psychological fit and expected mastery burden.
















## Protection versus possession
















- AFO's `僕がいる` / protected-retry system gives Shigaraki real action space while increasing dependence on the person who controls resources, rescue and second chances (`V10:p152-181`).
- Protective action cannot be classified as non-possessive merely because the protected person experiences increased agency.
















# Volume 11 power / philosophy updates








## Finite embers and overlapping succession








- One For All persists in All Might as finite `残り火` after transfer; Kamino consumes the remainder and All Might states the embers are gone (`V11:p067-113`).
- Succession therefore includes an overlap period rather than instantaneous predecessor replacement.








## Symbolhood is enabled by power but not identical with the body








- After public exposure of his emaciated body, All Might states `私の心は 依然 平和の象徴!!` (`V11:p058`).
- After final expenditure he later says `“平和の象徴”は死にました` (`V11:p113`).
- Model: bodily power enables repeated enactment; enactment builds authority; authority can outlast visible bodily invulnerability briefly, but loss of power ultimately forces role transition.








## Nana's smile as inherited rescue technique








- Nana's remembered teaching connects smiling under fear with communicating `大丈夫` to the person being saved (`V11:p060-061`).
- All Might's reassurance style is therefore inherited affective pedagogy, not adequately modeled as publicity alone.








## `命に代えないで`: survival as care obligation








- All Might's initial `この命に代えても` promise is explicitly rejected by Inko, who requires him to live and continue protecting/raising Midoriya (`V11:p159-162`).
- This introduces a power-ethics limit: willingness to die is not always the highest proof of care when continued relational support is itself necessary.








## AFO: power through option-space engineering








- AFO explicitly values a teacher making a pupil `一人立ち` and treats mentor removal as fuel for successor leadership (`V11:p105-107`).
- His power model includes shaping emotional interpretation, allies, resources and future option space before withdrawal; autonomy can be engineered rather than simply suppressed.








## Sensory compensation through Quirk stacking








- In confinement AFO describes spatial perception through small air movement/vibration and infrared rather than ordinary vision (`V11:p103-104`).
- Accumulated Quirks function as compensatory systems architecture, not only additive attack power.








# Volume 12 power-philosophy updates

- Cementoss defines useful `必殺技` through stable action under variable circumstance and explicitly notes that it need not be an attack (`V12:p009-012`).
- Aizawa places combat beside information, judgment, mobility, communication and leadership rather than treating output as the profession's sole metric (`V12:p011`).




**Current proposition:** professional power is capacity made repeatable and socially usable, not merely maximum destructive output.




## OFA: inherited capacity → body-specific authorship




- Midoriya identifies an unconscious All-Might-shaped fist assumption and reroutes Full Cowling through leg-centered Shoot Style (`V12:p044-049`).




**Current proposition:** inheritance does not determine interface. Making OFA `僕の力` requires adapting expression to the successor's own body and constraints.




## Support gear as selective control




- Kaminari's equipment lets him route electrical output toward a chosen target without indiscriminately catching allies (`V12:p157-158`).




**Current proposition:** technical growth can mean narrowing/selecting power so others can safely coordinate around it. Precision and ally compatibility are positive power gains even when raw output is unchanged.


# Volume 13 cumulative updates


## Recipient-compatible power


HUC distinguishes physical judgment from social usability. Bakugo can correctly triage low-priority victims and still be professionally deficient because the instruction is delivered in a form the civilian relationship does not accept (`V13:p035-036`). Power/decision quality must therefore survive translation through communication.


## Rescue power as network function


`トリアージ`, evacuation routing, `橋渡し` to other emergency services and cross-school role distribution establish that professional rescue power is partly the ability to function as a node in a larger response system (`V13:p020-034`).


## Delegated authority


The provisional license authorizes emergency judgments/actions that may affect lives even absent direct pro-hero instruction (`V13:p106-107`). Capability becomes public authority only through institutional delegation and accountability.


## Inheritance through integration


Todoroki's `過去も 血も 忘れたままじゃいられねえ` shows that self-authorship is not produced by erasing inheritance; unintegrated history can continue governing attention from outside conscious control (`V13:p070-074`).


## Succession under finitude


AFO's `託す` theory treats passing power/value onward as a general human response to endings (`V13:p142`). Hero/villain distinction therefore lies in the content, relationships and social order reproduced through succession, not in succession itself.


## Survival as retained capacity


All Might's `私は死なないぞ` turns continued life into part of heroic responsibility after physical Symbolhood ends (`V13:p147`). Self-sacrifice is not the sole high form of commitment.


# Volume 14 cumulative updates

## Save and win as integrated professional competence

All Might explicitly refuses to let Midoriya's rescue emphasis and Bakugo's victory emphasis remain separate ideals. `救けて勝つ / 勝って救ける` names a reciprocal architecture in which victory is necessary to secure rescue and rescue determines the moral function of victory (`V14:p044`). Midoriya later internalizes this as `必ず勝って 必ず助ける` (`V14:p151`).

## Inherited power is not inherited competence

Mirio's Permeation demonstrates the distinction with unusual clarity. The raw Quirk is full of disabling constraints; elite performance is created by timing, prediction and repeated embodied practice. Mirio summarizes the conversion directly: `インターンで得た経験を力に変えてトップを掴んだ` (`V14:p111-116`).

## Transfer authority versus legitimacy

Nighteye concedes that OFA is transferred by `持ち主の意志` while still denying that Midoriya was the best successor (`V14:p178`, `p196`). Power can therefore be legitimately transferred in one sense while its recipient remains professionally contested in another.

## Information asymmetry as power

Nighteye's `予知` converts future-action information into near-unanswerable anticipatory advantage during the office trial (`V14:p175-181`). The scene reinforces that power is not only output; accurate information about an opponent's next state can dominate action space.

## Organization as power multiplier

Overhaul attacks the League's goal/plan gap and offers hierarchical integration under his command (`V14:p124-140`). V14 adds organizational architecture to the power ontology: a group with strong individuals but weak planning can be strategically challenged by a lower-temperature actor who controls structure, sequence and resource use.


# Volume 15 cumulative updates

## Uncertainty, prediction and action

V15 separates power from certainty. Midoriya/Mirio can recognize distress without possessing enough information for safe extraction; Nighteye possesses extraordinary future information but deliberately restricts its use. His stated doctrine is to maximize ordinary action-success probability first and use Foresight only as `勝利のダメ押し` (`V15:p149-151`).

**Current proposition:** more information is not automatically more agency. High-burden knowledge can become professionally harmful when it cannot yet be translated into an actionable decision.

## Fate as an action problem rather than a metaphysical conclusion

Nighteye reports no observed successful alteration of a predicted scene; All Might had internalized his foretold death as an endpoint. Midoriya refuses to treat that record as sufficient reason for passivity and joins Nana's `運命...ねじ曲げる` frame (`V15:p050-055`).

**Current proposition:** V15 does not prove fate is mutable. It establishes a normative stance that uncertain inevitability does not cancel the duty to try.

## Quirk destruction and the instability of power identity

The Hassaikai projectile is identified as part of a `“個性”を壊すクスリ` problem; Amajiki suffers temporary interference and recovered ammunition contains human blood/cells (`V15:p078-083`, `p132-141`).

**Current proposition:** Quirks are not guaranteed stable personal endowments. Technology can target the continuity between bodily capacity, identity and social/professional role.

## Personhood versus resource conversion

Eri is called `計画の“核”` and her biological material is connected to ammunition (`V15:p022`, `p135-141`). This is the strongest anti-relational power model yet in the Hassaikai material: another person's body is valuable to Chisaki insofar as it can be reorganized into plan capacity.

# Volume 16 cumulative updates

## Role conversion: power is what the network can make usable now

Fat Gum's defense is consumed by received impact and converted into offensive force; Kirishima's broken shield becomes useful by repeatedly reoccupying the protective role. V16 therefore treats combat role as relational and temporal rather than fixed by Quirk category (`V16:p101-123`).

## Embodied power as Rappa's normative combat criterion

Rappa rejects guns/blades as `不粋` and values `その身に宿した力`. This is a character-local combat ethic: bodily force is, for him, the authentic proof of strength (`V16:p099`). It contrasts with hero professionalism's acceptance of teams, tools, institutions and role delegation.

## Courage is not produced by Quirk spectacle

Kirishima's earlier belief that a flashier/stronger Quirk might produce hero confidence is contradicted by Mina acting while afraid and Crimson Riot explicitly grounding courage in a chosen response to fear/failure (`V16:p129-148`).

## Capacity enhancement preserves cost

Mimic's Quirk-enhancing drug increases environmental-control scope while imposing exhaustion/overextension. Enhancement relocates limits rather than abolishing them (`V16:p174`).


## V17 - identity beyond power / coupled dangerous systems

1. **Mirio after Permeation:** Quirk loss is catastrophic capacity loss but does not erase the person authored through training. Prediction, timing, protection and role commitment survive the mechanism (`V17:p083-096`).
2. **Chisaki's reduction:** he assumes destroying a Quirk makes cultivated aspiration meaningless and describes Quirk-driven dreams as pathology (`V17:p087-093`).
3. **Eri as process rather than resource:** Rewind returns living bodies toward earlier states. Chisaki extracts it into a product; Midoriya tries to coordinate with Eri as a person (`V17:p167-189`).
4. **Rate coupling:** Midoriya's V17 endpoint couples two dangerous processes - continuous 100% OFA self-damage and continuous Rewind - rather than achieving solitary mastery (`V17:p184-189`).
5. **Boundary:** Infinite 100% is initiated, not resolved, in V17. Do not cite victory/aftermath until V18 establishes it.

## Volume 18 - coupled power, portable identity and socially re-authored Quirks

- **Coupled exceptional power:** Infinite 100% is a temporary system in which OFA self-damage and Eri's Rewind counter-rate one another; it is not independent 100% mastery (`V18:p007-041`).
- **Safe shutdown is a separate role:** Aizawa's Erasure is required after the combat win, proving maximum output and safe completion are distinct capabilities.
- **Portable heroic identity:** Mirio remains professionally coherent after Permeation loss and rejects OFA as compensation (`V18:p087-090`).
- **Restoration is not entitlement:** Eri's possible future use is conditional on control and request/consent.
- **Quirk re-authorship:** remedial trainees transform ice/wind/explosion/illusion from combat-capable powers into play, awe and participation (`V18:p149-166`).
- **Power/rank distinction:** Endeavor's No.1 status does not automatically reproduce All Might's symbolic function.


# Volume 19 cumulative updates

## Midoriya — optionality, range, and support mediation
- 20% remains brief/costly rather than sustainable mastery (`V19:p089-094`).
- All Might has Midoriya recombine local percentage control with brief high output; Midoriya derives wind-pressure range rather than waiting for a new inherited function.
- `出来ることは多い方がいいに決まってる!` (`V19:p165`) crystallizes a mature power philosophy: strength includes having more appropriately scaled options.
- Hatsume support makes precision/repeatability a co-designed interface problem; equipment extends agency rather than replacing it.

## Gentle — Elasticity as environmental authorship
- `弾性` gives elasticity to touched matter/air (`V19:p154`), converting terrain and trajectories into staged movement.
- Power use matches self-conception: indirect, theatrical and spatial rather than brute-force identity.

# Volume 20 cumulative updates

## Love as relational power without moral authorization
- La Brava's `愛` Quirk turns genuine attachment into measurable temporary amplification (`V20:p007-024`).
