# MHA SP2 — Relationship State Ledger
































## Method
































Relationships are directional unless explicitly marked reciprocal. Track asymmetry rather than averaging two people's understandings.
































---
































# Midoriya Izuku → Bakugo Katsuki
































**V01 state:** long-standing fear/deference + admiration/history + refusal to abandon him in danger.
































- Bakugo's contempt still organizes Midoriya's emotional posture.
- Midoriya nonetheless reads Bakugo as a person in need before he reads him as an enemy or bully.
- Rescue action occurs without invitation and despite low expectation of gratitude.
- Important unresolved question: whether Midoriya's lack of hatred reflects generosity, habituated subordination, admiration, fear, or a mixture.
































**Key locator:** V01:p43-50.
































# Bakugo Katsuki → Midoriya Izuku
































**V01 state:** hierarchy-protective contempt with high threat sensitivity.
































- Midoriya is assigned the lower-status label `デク`.
- Midoriya's aspiration is treated as illegitimate encroachment.
- Help from Midoriya is experienced as humiliation/status inversion.
- Midoriya gaining visible power destabilizes the relationship's assumed hierarchy.
































**Key locators:** V01:p14-24; V01:p60-68; V01:p171-181.
































# Midoriya Izuku ↔ All Might
































**V01 state:** idol/fan asymmetry → evaluator/aspirant → mentor/successor.
































- Midoriya begins by idealizing All Might as model of reassuring heroism.
- All Might first denies feasibility, then revises after witnessing rescue action.
- Relationship becomes an entrustment relation through OFA.
- All Might's recognition is enormously identity-stabilizing for Midoriya.
































**Key locators:** V01:p35-39; V01:p57-68; V01:p69-85.
































# Midoriya Izuku ↔ Uraraka Ochako
































**V01 state:** first affirming peer relation in the new U.A. environment.
































- Uraraka helps before friendship is established.
- Midoriya rescues her in the exam.
- Uraraka rescues his falling body in return.
- She tries to protect his admission outcome by sharing points.
- She begins positive re-authoring of `Deku`.
































This is reciprocal rescue rather than one-way protagonist beneficence.
































**Key locators:** V01:p94; V01:p107-134; V01:p151-181.
































# Midoriya Izuku ↔ Iida Tenya
































**V01 state:** initial misrecognition → evidence-based respect.
































- Iida initially sees Midoriya as unserious/disruptive.
- Midoriya's exam behavior forces revision.
- Relationship begins through moral re-evaluation rather than instant affinity.
































**Key locators:** V01:p94-106; V01:p124-134.
































# Midoriya Izuku ↔ Aizawa Shota
































**V01 state:** teacher/student relationship organized around sustainability challenge.
































- Aizawa refuses to treat noble intent as compensation for unusable power.
- Midoriya's adaptation is evidence that he can respond to severe feedback.
- Future tracking should distinguish fear of Aizawa, respect, trust, and pedagogical internalization.
































**Key locator:** V01:p151-169.
































# Midoriya Izuku ↔ Midoriya Inko
































**V01 state:** deep maternal love with an early recognition failure.
































- Inko's protective grief tells young Izuku that his dream may not be viable.
- Izuku remembers wanting affirmation rather than apology.
- Later development must test whether the relationship shifts from protection-through-limitation toward trust in self-authored risk.
































**Key locator:** V01:p22-24.

# Volume 2 relationship deltas
































## Midoriya Izuku → Bakugo Katsuki — V02
































**State:** fear/deference + admiration/history → explicit rival benchmark + unusual disclosure pressure.
































- Midoriya says he wants to win because Bakugo is impressive (`君が凄い人だから`) (V02:p057).
- Bakugo's interpretation matters enough that Midoriya discloses a partial One For All truth even though secrecy is a serious obligation (V02:p079-081).
- Midoriya's goal is not to humiliate Bakugo but to make inherited power his own and then surpass him with `僕の力`.
































**Modeling delta:** Bakugo uniquely combines trauma/status fear with genuine aspirational importance for Midoriya.
































## Bakugo Katsuki → Midoriya Izuku — V02
































**State:** hierarchy-protective contempt → genuine status threat / rival recognition without relational repair.
































- Bakugo demands full-power opposition; Midoriya's restraint is experienced as contempt/patronization (V02:p032-057).
- He treats the battle-trial loss as real and does not accept Midoriya's secret as an excuse that nullifies it (V02:p081-084).
- His live-crisis behavior outside Midoriya interaction is markedly more tactical, strengthening relationship-conditioned dysregulation as a model rule.
































## Midoriya Izuku ↔ All Might — V02
































**State:** mentor/successor relation gains secrecy, pedagogical fallibility, and tactical internalization.
































- Midoriya inherits not only power but a responsibility to keep its origin secret (V02:p079-081, p110).
- All Might's affection for his chosen successor contributes to leniency during the battle trial; Recovery Girl explicitly challenges this (V02:p075).
- Midoriya later uses All Might's tactical teaching while planning the water-zone escape.
































## Midoriya Izuku ↔ Iida Tenya — V02
































**State:** evidence-based respect → explicit recognition of Iida as superior functional leader.
































- Iida uses Midoriya (and Ingenium) as reference points when deciding how a leader should act in cafeteria panic (V02:p101-103).
- Midoriya voluntarily transfers class presidency to Iida after witnessing the performance (V02:p104).
































## Iida Tenya → Ingenium — V02
































**State:** admired elder-brother hero as internalized leadership template.
































- Iida explicitly values Ingenium's discipline and capacity to guide people (V02:p097).
- Under crisis, he asks what his brother would do and converts that ideal into action (V02:p101-103).
































## All Might ↔ Recovery Girl — V02
































**State:** trusted professional-peer relation with real corrective authority.
































- Recovery Girl can rebuke All Might for student-safety and mentorship failures without ceremonial deference (V02:p075-077).
- All Might receives the correction rather than insulating himself through status.
































## Aizawa Shota → students — V02
































**State:** evaluator/disciplinarian → direct protective commander.
































- His severe V01 pedagogy is now paired with immediate role delegation and extreme bodily protection when students are truly endangered (V02:p130-134, p200-201).
































## Shigaraki Tomura → All Might — V02
































**State:** symbol-fixated lethal target.
































- The USJ operation is organized around killing `平和の象徴` (V02:p128).

## Shigaraki Tomura ↔ Kurogiri — V02
































**State:** leader/operations-support relation with asymmetrical emotional regulation.
































- Kurogiri is essential to dispersion, escape control, and reporting.

## Bakugo Katsuki ↔ Kirishima Eijiro — V02
































**State:** early live-crisis peer collaboration.
































- Kirishima is surprised by Bakugo's relatively cool strategic assessment at USJ (V02:p189-191).

## Asui Tsuyu ↔ Midoriya Izuku — V02

## Uraraka Ochako → Iida Tenya — V02

- Todoroki says his own ability is objectively higher and declares he will beat Midoriya after noting All Might's attention (V03:p121-124).

## Todoroki Shoto → Endeavor — V03
































**State:** hostile father salience established; causal history withheld.
































- `クソ親父が見てるんだから` makes the father's gaze part of Todoroki's competitive state (V03:p144-145).

## Uraraka Ochako → parents — V03
































**State:** reciprocal family care expressed through economic ambition.
































- Childhood wish to help the construction business develops into a goal of earning as a hero so her parents can live more comfortably (V03:p095-099).
































## Shigaraki Tomura ↔ unseen teacher (`先生`) — V03
































**State:** emotionally regulating but destructive mentorship; dependence and symbolic identity construction.
































- The teacher reframes USJ failure without humiliation, provides long-range strategy, and assigns Shigaraki the mission of becoming a fear-symbol known to society (V03:p070-072).

# Volume 4 relationship deltas
































## Midoriya Izuku ↔ Todoroki Shoto — V04
































**State:** public rivalry becomes confessional/philosophical relationship.
































- Todoroki privately explains Endeavor, `個性婚`, his mother's trauma, and his right-side-only strategy as the reason for his challenge (V04:p092-103).
- Midoriya responds not with pity but by articulating his own relational identity: `誰かに救けられてここにいる`, then renews the competitive declaration from his side (V04:p103-105).
- The relationship now carries mutual challenge plus unusually private disclosure; each becomes a mirror for the other's theory of inheritance.
































## Todoroki Shoto → Endeavor — V04
































**State:** explicit rejection organized around contested bodily authorship.
































- Todoroki describes Endeavor's reproductive strategy and refuses to become `そんな屑の道具` (V04:p095-101).
- His right-only competition is designed to `完全否定` Endeavor (V04:p101-103).
- Endeavor remains central even through rejection: Shoto's current autonomy strategy is still relationship-conditioned by the father he wants to negate.
































## Endeavor → Todoroki Shoto — V04
































**State:** instrumentalized successor / created solution to status failure.
































- Endeavor tells All Might that Shoto will eventually surpass him and calls Shoto a child `そうするべく つくった` (V04:p096-100).
- Current evidence supports achievement-centered ownership rather than independent recognition of Shoto's personhood.
































## Endeavor → All Might — V04
































**State:** long-term status fixation and comparative grievance.
































- All Might's superior position is the benchmark Endeavor cannot directly overcome.
- Endeavor converts that failure into a next-generation project through Shoto (V04:p096-100).
































## Midoriya Izuku → support network — V04
































**State:** explicit relational self-authorship.
































- Midoriya remembers All Might, Uraraka, Iida, and others and states `誰かに救けられてここにいる` (V04:p103-105).
- Help is not experienced as invalidating agency; it creates reciprocal obligation to become stronger and answer what was given.
































## Iida Tenya → Midoriya Izuku — V04
































**State:** friendship deliberately includes rivalry/individuation.
































- Iida refuses a tactically excellent Midoriya/Uraraka team because continual alignment has begun to feel like remaining `未熟者`; chooses to `挑戦する` Midoriya instead (V04:p017-019).
- Opposition here is a form of respect and self-development, not relational rupture.
































## Uraraka Ochako → Midoriya Izuku — V04
































**State:** warmth/trust differentiates from dependence.
































- Uraraka initially chooses Midoriya's cavalry team despite the ten-million target because she trusts him (V04:p016-017).
- Before the Bakugo match, she reflects that choosing familiar people may have become reliance and declines Midoriya's prepared strategy so she can challenge through her own thinking (V04:p176-190).
- She continues to admire Midoriya; individuation is not rejection.
































## Shinso Hitoshi ↔ Midoriya Izuku — V04
































**State:** asymmetrical encounter over privilege, stigma, and institutional access.
































- Shinso frames Midoriya as `恵まれた` and himself as delayed by a Quirk socially/institutionally mismatched to his goal (V04:p145-146).
- Midoriya recognizes the grievance's partial truth rather than erasing it with his own past: `僕もソレ昔思ってた / でも…そうだ 僕は恵まれた`.
- Their conflict therefore produces self-recognition in Midoriya even though Shinso is also using provocative speech tactically.
































## Todoroki Shoto → mother / family memory — V04
































**State:** trauma-conditioned bodily symbolism.
































- Shoto's remembered mother is associated with crying, rejection of his left side, and the boiling-water injury (V04:p100).
- The memory helps explain why body-side meaning cannot be reduced to simple father hatred; family trauma has fused Quirk use, face/body image, and parental identity.

# Volume 6 relationship deltas
































## Midoriya ↔ Gran Torino
- Gran Torino initially destabilizes rather than reassures Midoriya and identifies hero worship as the technical bottleneck.
- Midoriya responds to harsh critique by experimenting rather than defending the existing self-model.
- Relation type: abrasive high-trust technical mentorship in formation.
- Locator: V06:p040-089.
































## Midoriya ↔ All Might — differentiation delta
- All Might remains moral/predecessor ideal, but Gran Torino establishes that reproducing All Might's body/learning method is actively harmful to Midoriya's development.
- Healthy succession now requires difference, not just devotion.
































## Iida → Tensei / Ingenium
- `Ingenium` moves from admired elder identity to offered inheritance, deferred name, vengeance identity, and first reorientation toward desired hero self (V06:p021-023; p124-125; p139-141; p189).
- Relationship remains heavily idealized; injury to Tensei destabilizes Tenya's own moral regulation.
































## Midoriya → Iida
- Notices Iida's distress before departure but accepts the closed response; later regrets not intervening harder (V06:p037-038).
- In Hosu, actively searches for Iida from weak contextual evidence and invokes `余計なお世話` when Iida rejects involvement.
- Relation shifts from respectful friendship to intrusive protective responsibility under danger.
































## Iida → Midoriya
- Initially withholds family motive and later rejects Midoriya's involvement as unrelated.
- Midoriya's actual rescue-directed behavior becomes part of the evidence that reopens Iida's heroic self-conception.
































## Todoroki → Iida
- Todoroki recognizes Iida's resentment state because he knows it personally (V06:p184-185).
- His help is blunt, practical, and identity-directed: `なりてえもんちゃんと見ろ!!` (V06:p189).
- This is early evidence of trauma becoming prosocial perceptual expertise.
































## Iida → Todoroki
- Todoroki's words reach Iida at the moment procedure alone no longer has traction; peer experiential recognition becomes corrective input.
































## Iida ↔ Manual
- Manual identifies the hidden revenge motive and gives clear legal/moral warning; Iida hears but does not comply.
- Demonstrates limits of authority influence under acute family grievance.
































## Bakugo ↔ Best Jeanist
- Best Jeanist treats Bakugo's violent public presentation as a professional problem to correct.
- Bakugo resists but remains in placement; relationship begins as external civility discipline versus internal competence absolutism.
































## Shigaraki ↔ Stain
- Mutual hostility and ideological mismatch: Stain demands conviction; Shigaraki currently offers destruction/hatred.
- Shared opposition to the present order does not equal shared moral framework.
































## Shigaraki ↔ unseen teacher
- Teacher continues to metabolize encounters/failures into learning opportunities rather than simply punish poor outcomes.
- Destructive mentorship remains patient, developmental, and long-horizon.
































# Volume 7 relationship deltas

## Midoriya ↔ All Might
- All Might expands trust by disclosing OFA/AFO history.
- Midoriya explicitly links his confidence to All Might remaining beside him.
- All Might internally withholds the expectation that he may not be present later (V07:p124-126), creating an asymmetric knowledge state produced by protective care rather than manipulation.
































## Midoriya → Bakugo
- Midoriya explicitly names Bakugo's shouting as a barrier to real conversation (V07:p166-167), marking increased assertiveness within this specific relationship.
- He continues to admire Bakugo's refusal to surrender even while resisting his abuse and calls him back to that value when Bakugo would rather lose than rely on him (V07:p185-187).
































## Bakugo → Midoriya
- Midoriya's rapid growth intensifies comparative threat.
- Dependence on Midoriya is so identity-threatening that Bakugo says defeat is preferable (V07:p185).
- Midoriya's appeal to Bakugo's own victory ideal allows a first tactical breach; cooperation becomes possible without relational repair being complete.
































## Bakugo → All Might
- Childhood memory explicitly grounds All Might admiration in the hero who always wins from bad odds (V07:p181-183, p188).
- Bakugo therefore inherits a victory-centered fragment of All Might's heroic image even without inheriting OFA.
































## Shigaraki → Stain
- After capture, Stain's media centrality produces attention/status frustration in Shigaraki.
- The relationship's most consequential V07 effect is asymmetrical symbolic power: Stain attracts people whom Shigaraki's organization can receive.
































## Shigaraki ↔ All For One
- V07 history/current dialogue makes the mentor-successor architecture more explicit.
- All For One treats current disorder and recruit influx as developmental material in a long-horizon succession project.
