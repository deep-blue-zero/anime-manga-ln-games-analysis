# Volume 9 additions
































## Designed wall -> hostile terrain without a guaranteed route
































V08 repeatedly visualized developmental obstacles as diagnostic walls inside teacher-authored exercises. V09 converts the forest itself into occluded, hostile terrain: smoke, fire, gas, darkness, scattered bodies, and incomplete communication remove any assumption that the environment was built to reveal growth safely. The visual transition reinforces the conceptual move from calibrated adversity to emergency enactment.
































## The body as accumulated rescue-cost map
































Midoriya's body after Muscular is not only a spectacle of determination. Repeated close emphasis on damaged arms, impaired movement, carrying Kota, and then re-entering the larger crisis makes successful rescue visibly cumulative: the same body that solved one local emergency now has less capacity available for the next.
































## Kota as protected observer
































The Muscular sequence repeatedly positions Kota behind/under Midoriya's protective line. The staging matters because V08 supplied Kota with an inherited account of heroic sacrifice from the bereaved side; V09 visually gives him the protected person's position. The later `僕のヒーロー` recognition is therefore prepared spatially before it is verbalized.
































## Darkness as causal field, not mood decoration
































Tokoyami/Dark Shadow makes darkness itself operationally consequential. Night increases output while reducing control, converting lighting conditions into a relationship between environment, power magnitude, and agency. This should remain distinct from generic “dark = ominous” symbolism.
































## Public image -> capture target
































Bakugo's publicly legible aggression, previously part of tournament/social identity, is now re-read by villains as possible recruitability. V09 therefore creates a visual-social callback in which the outwardly visible self becomes adversary intelligence. The villains may be wrong about the person while still correctly observing the surface signal.
