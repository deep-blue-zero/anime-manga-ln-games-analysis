## Shigaraki Tomura / Shimura Tenko — internal self-recovery without innocence simplification

- `V38:p070-078`: Shigaraki says fusion with AFO genuinely occurred, but he hid `原点` where AFO would not notice it and recovered himself from within.

- `それだけが俺を救うんだ ヒーロー` makes present destructive desire part of his own salvation claim.
- **Model consequence:** reject a clean good-Tenko/bad-Shigaraki split. Internal authorship recovery and destructive continuity are simultaneously canonical.

## All For One — ownership model fails under convergence

- V38 extends AFO's causal-sovereignty model: people, Quirks, successors, Machia and even narrative are treated as possessable resources/roles.
- Repeated damage accelerates Rewind/youth expenditure; as he becomes younger, raw power rises while negative affect becomes harder to suppress.
- He dismisses differentiated coalition work as mere `支え`, a strategic blind spot because those support functions are what consume his remaining time and delay movement.
- He assumes Gigantomachia remains his resource; Machia's post-Brainwashing grievance falsifies the assumption.
- **Readiness:** remains `moderate`, exceptionally strengthened in strategic/affective failure behavior. Noninstrumental relationship breadth remains absent rather than silently invented.

## Gigantomachia — loyalty is narrow agency, not property

# Volume 39 cumulative updates

## Himiko Toga — self-authored identity, reciprocal recognition and total-blood gift

- She rejects pity as another imposed scale: `そっちの尺度で 私を 可哀想な人間にするな!!`.

- `V39:p125-139`: Toga distinguishes the knife once driven by becoming/love from the later knife held with anger/hatred; she notices Uraraka's stable non-harm policy; and she breaks her own `生存競争` logic when Uraraka is about to die.

- **Boundary:** total-blood donation is knowingly fatal in implication and presented self-elegiacally, but V39 contains no later medical confirmation. Formal death confirmation remains V40+ OPEN.
- **Readiness:** `strong -> specialist_ready`.

# Volume 40 cumulative updates

## All For One — origin, ownership heuristic and external death

- `V40:p137-150`: direct birth/childhood evidence adds extreme deprivation, maternal death, early extraction of the mother's abnormal factor, severe twin resource asymmetry and an unusually early possession orientation.
- Yoichi is retained as something AFO possesses rather than recognized as an autonomous brother. This gives the adult succession/ownership model a direct family-developmental antecedent.
- The same broad comic/narrative culture produces opposite aspiration: Yoichi wants to resemble a hero; AFO consciously selects the Demon King because the world can exist for him (`僕にも夢ができた`).
- `V40:p151-160`: AFO's manipulation decision policy becomes explicit: observe hated/desirable things, use affect/display as leverage and turn vulnerability into possession/control.
- Kudo's enduring offense is extending a hand to Yoichi. AFO's hatred therefore targets people who make exit from his ownership possible, not merely people with high combat output.
- `全因解放` expresses the same ontology mechanically: accumulated factors are concentrated under one objective and one owner.
- `V40:p178-190`: youth/negative affect weakens `支配権` and factor integration; AFO experiences dysregulation as `こんなの僕じゃない`, confirming that calm was regulation rather than weak affect.
- `V40:p185-191`: final plan is to reach Shigaraki, transfer/reinforce himself and regain control. He fails; Rewind reaches infancy and the external body disappears. `V40:p197` Shigaraki states `先生が死んだな`.
- The imagined return of stolen factors is immediately rejected by Hawks's vestige as an implausibly generous `サービス`; do not record literal automatic restitution.
- **Readiness:** `moderate (very strongly strengthened) -> strong`. Origin, family relation, chosen ideal, manipulation heuristics and long-horizon grievance close the previous breadth gate; lack of ordinary reciprocal relationship variation still gates `specialist_ready`.

## Shigaraki Tomura — AFO interference rejected; independent destruction persists
