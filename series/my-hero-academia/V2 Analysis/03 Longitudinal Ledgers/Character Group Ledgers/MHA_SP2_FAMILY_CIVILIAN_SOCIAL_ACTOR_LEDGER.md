## V17 update - Eri origin, conditioning and emerging agency

### Eri
- **Readiness:** `emerging -> moderate`.
- **Motive:** repeatedly prioritizes reducing harm to others, even by surrendering herself. Treat this as care filtered through coercive conditioning, not a stable preference for self-destruction (`V17:p050`, `p145-151`).
- **Learned pain response:** her body braces for pain she expects cannot be avoided; narration treats this as habit (`V17:p080-082`).
- **Origin:** Rewind appears as a mutation unlike either family line. As a very young child she unintentionally caused her father to disappear; her mother called her cursed and left her with the Hassaikai elder (`V17:p167-170`).
