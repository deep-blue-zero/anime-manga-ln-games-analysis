# Midoriya Izuku / 緑谷出久
































**Current scope:** V05  
**Model readiness:** `moderate`
































## Identity / role
- Quirkless middle-school aspirant → U.A. Hero Course student.
- Receives One For All from All Might after being judged worthy.
































## Desire hierarchy
- Primary: become a hero who saves people.
- Aspirational image: All Might's reassuring, smiling heroism.
- Secondary: prove that his dream was not delusional.
































## Fears / vulnerabilities
- Quirklessness as proof of social and practical illegitimacy.
- Deep sensitivity to Bakugo's hierarchy and contempt.
- At V01, external recognition is still needed to stabilize self-belief.
































## Self-model
- Begins with heroic desire partially internalized as childish fantasy.
- Ends with evidence that his conduct is genuinely heroic, but not yet a stable heroic identity.
































## Values / power philosophy
- Power is valuable primarily because it permits rescue.
- Admires strength through All Might's use of it, not for domination.
- OFA is understood as entrusted responsibility rather than entitlement.
































## Perception / attention
- Highly attentive to hero techniques and quirk use.
- Analytical overflow appears through muttering and rapid observation.
- In crisis, unusually attentive to visible need: reads Bakugo's face as a request for rescue even without verbal invitation.
































## Decision policy
- Strong action bias when another person visibly needs help.
- Can act before fully considering personal feasibility.
- Very high willingness to accept bodily risk.
- At V01, weak self-preservation integration.
































## Social behavior
- Hesitant and deferential in ordinary peer interaction.
- Prone to self-correction and nervous speech.
- More decisive when defending or rescuing someone else than when advocating for himself.
































## Stress behavior
- Under acute danger, analysis can collapse into immediate rescue action.
- Under evaluation, anxiety is high but does not erase effort.
- Under bodily failure, searches for workaround rather than withdraws.
































## Japanese voice
- Hesitation, muttering, self-correction, analytical overflow.
- Speech becomes more direct around urgent rescue need.
































## Embodiment
- Treats own body as expendable proof of commitment.
- OFA is physically alien and catastrophic to his current body.
- First adaptation is damage localization, not true bodily sustainability.
































## Current contradictions
- Heroic reflex is a major virtue.
- Self-disposability is already a major defect.
