# All Might / Yagi Toshinori / オールマイト / 八木俊典
































**Current scope:** V05  
**Model readiness:** `moderate`
































## Identity / role
- No. 1-style public Symbol of Peace figure in V01's social imagination.
- Secretly severely wounded and time-limited.
- Becomes Midoriya's mentor and OFA transmitter.
































## Desire hierarchy
- Continue saving.
- Preserve public reassurance.
- Prevent visible fear and symbolic collapse.
- Find/recognize a worthy successor.
































## Fears / vulnerabilities
- Inability to sustain the Symbol.
- Failure to meet public expectation.
- Exposure of bodily weakness.
































## Self-model
- Must not visibly yield to evil.
- Public theatricality is understood as a functional burden, not simply vanity.
































## Values / power philosophy
- Power for rescue, deterrence, reassurance, and public confidence.
- OFA is voluntary entrustment to someone judged worthy.
































## Perception / attention
- Initially foregrounds professional feasibility and bodily limitations when evaluating Midoriya.
- Capable of major judgment revision after observing actual conduct.
































## Decision policy
- Professional realism can initially override inspirational identification.
- Under acute moral stimulus, can be moved back into action by another person's example.
- Will entrust enormous power when he judges character sufficient, even before technical competence exists.
































## Social behavior
- Hero form: declarative, theatrical, reassuring.
- Diminished/private form: candid, looser, physically vulnerable.
- Mentor relation begins with a strong evaluative asymmetry but includes willingness to admit prior error.
































## Stress behavior
- V01 establishes physical time limit and burden-management.
- Sludge crisis shows a moment of paralysis/constraint broken by Midoriya's action.
































## Japanese voice
- Strong split between public heroic declaratives and private diminished-form candor.
































## Embodiment
- Giant public heroic body versus narrow wounded private body.
- Body is already carrying the cost of social symbolism.
