- Midoriya: hesitation/self-correction + analytic overflow; tactical compression under danger.

- Tsuyu: explicit low-filter directness.
- Yaoyorozu: formal, exacting `ですわ` register.
- Kurogiri: highly polite/formal villain speech.
- Shigaraki: rough, repetitive, game-coded speech with precise tactical cognition.

## V09 language constraint
































The volume repeatedly makes apparently simple words carry jurisdictional or causal weight. `遊び` differentiates Muscular's own self-description from socially explanatory villain frameworks; `戦闘を許可する` is an accountable professional order with a narrow emergency context; `ヒビ` gives Shigaraki a social-effect objective; and `僕のヒーロー` is a child's relationship-specific recognition rather than an abstract doctrinal statement.
