# Volume 4 cumulative update — Shinso as control case
















### Villain-coded Quirk does not determine villainy
- Shinso recalls peers repeatedly saying Brainwashing would suit crime or villains (`敵向き`) (V04:p153).
- He also experiences institutional mismatch: a robot-based practical exam poorly demonstrates a Quirk that acts through human response (V04:p134-136).
- He expresses envy/grievance toward the `恵まれた` (V04:p145-146).
- Yet his desired route is **into the Hero Course**, and he uses the Sports Festival as a legitimate second-chance mechanism.
















### Claim revision
`REVISE / QUALIFY` any deterministic version of the failed-recognition villain hypothesis.
















> Stigma, grievance, social suspicion, and institutional misfit may contribute to alienation, but they do not mechanically produce villainy. Moral commitments, available routes, mentors, relationships, recognition opportunities, and subsequent choices remain necessary parts of the causal model.
















### Todoroki as second non-villain comparison
Severe familial instrumentalization and trauma produce self-limitation and identity conflict without making Todoroki a villain. Suffering must never be treated as destiny or as exculpatory causal determinism.

## Volume 27 update - belonging can become a terminal loyalty structure

### Twice

V24 established that League belonging stabilizes Twice after identity trauma. V27 shows the endpoint of that process: Hawks offers an individually safer/reformable future, but Twice ranks League welfare above his own survival. His final clone rescues Toga/Compress and his terminal life appraisal defines League belonging as authentic happiness (`V27:p109-158`).

This is not evidence that the League caused all earlier vulnerability. It is evidence that late legitimate intervention now competes with a criminal community that successfully met a belonging need society previously left unanswered.

### Dabi as counterexample

V27 prevents a monolithic `League as found family` model. Dabi says League/Shigaraki are not ultimate commitments and frames Twice through usefulness to his own dream while invoking Stain's will (`V27:p166-172`). Villain-group membership therefore supports different motivational architectures: reciprocal belonging for Twice/Toga versus more instrumental ideological participation for Dabi.

### Garaki/AFO

AFO's explicit friend-language toward Garaki demonstrates that malignant systems can stabilize themselves through recognition and gratitude as well as coercion. Care-like reinforcement must not be treated as proof of prosocial formation.
