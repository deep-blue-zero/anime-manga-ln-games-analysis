# 1. Governing transition: plural judgment and situated trust

Volume 20 established **visibility under consequence / responsibility before multiple witnesses**. Gentle, La Brava, Class 1-A and Endeavor all learned that being seen creates obligations that cannot be settled by self-description alone.

Volume 21 goes one step further: **the witnesses do not share one court of judgment**.

The governing transition is:

> **visibility under consequence / responsibility before multiple witnesses -> plural adjudication / legitimacy as situated trust**

Compact formulation:

> **No single successful performance settles what a person is. Endeavor can earn a public victory without earning family forgiveness; Hawks must sustain incompatible forms of trust across public hero work, state intelligence and enemy infiltration; One For All opens into a history larger than its current holder; and students with stigmatized, unfamiliar or differently trained powers gain standing through accountable use under shared constraints rather than through pedigree alone.**

The volume repeatedly separates domains that a weaker reading would collapse:

- **public hero legitimacy** and **private family judgment** are not interchangeable;
- **professional trust** and **personal trust** are not identical;
- **being ordered to infiltrate** and **being trusted by the infiltrated group** are separate problems;
- **inheriting power** and **understanding its history** are separate states;
- **having a frightening or manipulative Quirk** and **using that Quirk unethically** are separate propositions;
- **real-villain experience** and **superior team performance** are not synonymous;
- **respecting an opponent** can motivate targeted suppression rather than kindness;
- **being recognized as capable** can create pressure to live up to the recognition rather than reassurance.

V21 is therefore a volume about legitimacy without a single sovereign evaluator. People are judged by publics, families, institutions, enemies, mentors, teammates, opponents and inherited predecessors. The resulting model of heroism is more difficult than simple approval: one can be successful in one jurisdiction and still owe a debt in another.

# 2. No.189 — Why Endeavor keeps standing

## 2.1 The first payoff is refusal of premature social collapse

V20 ended with Endeavor apparently down. V21 initially keeps the public at the same epistemic boundary: people watching the broadcast do not know whether No.1 can recover, and the absence of All Might makes that uncertainty socially contagious.

The panic is not just fear of High-End's body. It is fear that the institutional position called No.1 no longer guarantees the emotional effect once supplied by the Symbol of Peace.

A civilian boy becomes unexpectedly important because he rejects the crowd's premature conversion of visible injury into symbolic defeat. His point is essentially observational: Endeavor is still fighting for them, so treating the situation as already over is false to what is on the screen.

This is a bottom-up legitimacy event.

All Might often entered scenes with trust preloaded. Endeavor cannot. He must generate confidence from an observed sequence of action while already carrying a deficit of affection and symbolic capital.

That makes the boy's response more important than generic cheering. It models how a new public relation can form:

`witness actual persistence -> revise expectation -> provisionally invest confidence`.

It is not love inherited from rank.

## 2.2 Hawks identifies Endeavor's singular relation to All Might

Hawks's internal comparison strips away a common simplification. Many people admired All Might. Many heroes benefited from the stable order he created. But Hawks identifies Endeavor as the person who seriously tried to exceed him.

The key formulation is:

> `本気で超えようなんて人は 一人もいなかった`
>
> `あなただけですよ 本気で超えようとしてたのは`

The praise is bounded. V20 has already linked Endeavor's obsession with surpassing All Might to the successor-production project that damaged his family. V21 does not cleanse that history by revealing the same obsession as admirable persistence.

Instead, one trait is shown to have incompatible moral expressions under different objectives and constraints:

- in the family, surpassing All Might was used to justify instrumentalizing spouse and children;
- in the High-End fight, refusal to accept an unreachable standard as an excuse for passivity becomes professional persistence under lethal pressure.

Character reconstruction must therefore avoid trait moralization. `Relentless competitive persistence` is neither automatically vice nor virtue. Its ethical meaning is conditional on target, means, authority and who bears the cost.

## 2.3 The decisive action is co-produced

Hawks does not merely admire Endeavor. He contributes his remaining feathers to increase Endeavor's speed at the point when Endeavor's own damaged body and High-End's mobility make solo closure difficult.

This matters for the No.1-symbol problem.

The scene could have resolved post-All-Might insecurity by manufacturing another solitary icon. It does not. The apparent central hero receives distributed assistance:

- Hawks has already evacuated civilians;
- Hawks's feather control remains a support infrastructure around the direct combatant;
- Endeavor supplies the destructive output High-End requires;
- the final acceleration depends on Hawks adding mobility to Endeavor's attack line.

The new No.1's first defining victory is therefore **less self-sufficient than All Might's public mythology**.

That is not a diminishment. It is a different model of professional legitimacy: visible central responsibility can coexist with causal dependence on partners.

# 3. No.190 — Victory, pose and the impossibility of clean closure

## 3.1 Prominence Burn resolves the V20 battle lock

V21 now authorizes the result V20 had to leave open. Endeavor rises and destroys High-End with Prominence Burn.

The physical cost remains visible. His body is burned and torn, his face has been severely injured, and the victory does not resemble All Might's effortless reassurance.

This matters because public trust is being rebuilt through a form very different from the old Symbol:

`certainty before battle` becomes `confidence reconstructed after witnessing struggle`.

The crowd's emotional movement is not “of course he will win.” It is “he is still standing” and then “he won.”

The distinction is foundational to post-All-Might hero society.

## 3.2 The raised fist is quotation, not identity

Endeavor's raised-fist image clearly recalls All Might's Kamino endpoint. The volume invites comparison without claiming equivalence.

All Might's pose marked the end of his public career and a transfer forward. Endeavor's pose occurs near the beginning of his No.1 tenure and after a fight whose uncertainty was witnessed in real time.

The callback therefore works as a visual question:

Can an inherited public grammar carry different moral content when performed by a different body with a different history?

The answer at V21 is provisionally yes, but only as public professional meaning. Nothing in the fist image adjudicates his household.

## 3.3 Dabi prevents victory from becoming closure

The volume immediately destabilizes the triumphant image by bringing Dabi into the aftermath.

This is structurally important. Endeavor wins one public trial but does not restore the world to a safe baseline. High-End is not an isolated anomaly whose defeat solves the threat network.

Dabi's appearance converts the battle from endpoint into evidence of a larger adversarial system.

Mirko's arrival prevents a clean Dabi-Endeavor confrontation, and Dabi withdraws. The result is an intentionally incomplete victory state:

- High-End is defeated;
- Endeavor has acquired provisional public legitimacy;
- the League remains active;
- Dabi has entered direct relational proximity to Endeavor;
- the origin, production and deployment structure of High-End remains incompletely understood.

V21 therefore refuses a symbolic reset.

# 4. No.191 — Hawks: trust as operational medium

## 4.1 The Dabi conversation changes Hawks's model

V20 established Hawks as informal, media-literate, intelligence-minded and operationally serious. V21 reveals that the apparent rumor-investigation role is nested inside a much riskier institutional assignment.

The Dabi/Hawks conversation shows that the High-End attack did not occur under the conditions Hawks expected. Dabi altered time/place and civilian exposure. This matters because it shows Hawks does not control the adversarial relationship merely because he is secretly working another side.

Infiltration is not omniscience.

Hawks has to operate inside an asymmetric trust problem where Dabi can test him by changing the environment.

## 4.2 `敵連合に取り入れ ホークス`

The Hero Public Safety Commission's instruction is explicit:

> `敵連合に取り入れ ホークス`

Hawks is not merely independently “gray.” He is carrying a state-directed infiltration mission.

The Commission's logic is also important. After Kamino, available intelligence is insufficient to identify and destroy the League's network. Nomu/modified-human information is especially valuable. Hawks is selected because his public approval, information reach and ability to act over a long horizon make him unusually suitable for entering an adversarial social network.

This expands hero labor into a domain very different from visible rescue.

The series now contains at least four distinct professional functions:

- public symbolic reassurance;
- direct combat/interception;
- distributed rescue and logistics;
- covert intelligence/infiltration.

A single hero may occupy multiple functions whose behavioral demands conflict.

## 4.3 Double credibility

Hawks must simultaneously remain:

- credible enough to the Commission to carry state intelligence goals;
- credible enough to Endeavor and other heroes to perform real professional partnership;
- credible enough to Dabi/the League that infiltration remains possible;
- publicly legible enough that his ordinary hero persona continues functioning.

These are not the same trust relation.

Dabi explicitly refuses full trust. Hawks therefore cannot treat deception as a one-time costume. He must continually generate believable behavior under adversarial scrutiny.

This gives Hawks unusually high character-modeling value: his speech, affect and visible ease cannot be interpreted without role conditioning.

`casualness` may be genuine temperament, public-brand affordance, tactical social lubrication, concealment or several at once.

V21 does not yet supply the later childhood/institutional history needed to explain how this style formed. It does establish that the style can function across incompatible social roles.

## 4.4 Prevention as social ideal

Hawks's desire for a society in which heroes have too much free time becomes more meaningful under the infiltration reveal.

It is not laziness as a rejection of duty. It is an end-state preference in which successful structural prevention lowers the need for emergency hero labor.

This differentiates him from Endeavor's achievement/contest model and Midoriya's rescue-first urgency.

Hawks is willing to accept hidden, ethically uncomfortable labor because his preferred output is **less visible crisis**, not more visible heroics.

That tension should remain active rather than resolved into “pragmatic spy hero.”

# 5. No.192 — The Todoroki family as separate moral jurisdictions

## 5.1 Public victory does not determine private judgment

V20 ended with family members forced into the same live witness position as the public. V21 lets them speak after the outcome—and their judgments diverge.

Natsuo's position is especially important because it blocks automatic redemption logic.

He can acknowledge that Endeavor may be trying to face what he did while still saying, in substance:

> `向き合おうとしてることは確かだよ`
>
> `俺の中じゃイカレ野郎 絶賛継続中だよ`
>
> `変わったようで全然変わってない`

The formulation is not contradiction. It distinguishes **change effort** from **settled moral status**.

A person can begin behaving differently and still remain, to an injured family member, the person who caused injuries that have not disappeared.

## 5.2 Fuyumi's connection orientation is not family consensus

Fuyumi wants movement toward a more functional family relation. That desire should not be universalized into “the family wants reconciliation.”

Her position is one situated response to the same history.

The modeling rule is:

`shared history != shared desired repair pathway`.

Fuyumi's comparatively connection-seeking behavior, Natsuo's refusal, Rei's carefully bounded earlier willingness to recognize attempted responsibility and Shoto's watchful conditionality can all coexist without one being narratively designated as the correct victim response.

## 5.3 Shoto separates hero evaluation from father evaluation

Shoto can recognize Endeavor as a tremendous professional hero without granting him success as a father.

This is one of V21's clearest instances of plural adjudication.

The same human being occupies roles governed by different evidentiary standards:

- professional hero performance can be judged through rescue, combat, leadership and public responsibility;
- fatherhood must be judged through conduct toward spouse/children, safety, care, autonomy and repair.

Achievement in one cannot settle the other.

Shoto's stance is therefore not indecision. It is a principled refusal to collapse role domains.

## 5.4 Endeavor's arc remains asymmetric

Endeavor can choose to become different. He cannot choose the family's response to his change.

This preserves the moral architecture established in V18-V20:

`self-obligation to change -> observable new conduct -> others retain independent authority over trust/forgiveness`.

That structure is stronger than redemption as reward because it makes repair non-transactional. Better behavior is required even if absolution never arrives.

# 6. No.193 — One For All becomes partial historical consciousness

## 6.1 From predecessor traces to an inhabited history

Earlier volumes had already established that One For All may carry predecessor presence. V04's Shinso match gave the first striking visual/embodied anomaly, but exact ontology remained open.

V21 changes the evidence class.

Midoriya now experiences a sustained vestige-space encounter in which predecessor figures and historical events are presented from within the inherited power relation.

This is more than abstract “will.” One For All becomes a medium through which prior conflict can become partially experientially available to the present holder.

The safe formulation at this boundary is:

> **One For All is now source-grounded as inherited power plus partial historical consciousness.**

It is not yet safe to state the later full mechanics of predecessor Quirks, complete vestige communication or all identities/functions.

## 6.2 All For One's power relation is social domination, not only Quirk theft

The historical material expands All For One as a political/social actor.

His capacity to give and take Quirks lets him construct dependency. Power distribution can produce gratitude, obligation, fear and organized loyalty.

That makes his “All For One” logic different from mere accumulation.

He does not only possess capacities. He structures people around his ability to control access to capacity.

A useful contrast emerges:

- All For One treats power as something one actor can seize, allocate and use to subordinate;
- One For All emerges through power carried forward between people resisting that domination.

At V21, this contrast is historical and ethical before it is mechanically complete.

## 6.3 The younger brother and coercive gift

All For One's younger brother resists him despite apparent physical weakness and limited capacity to enforce that resistance.

All For One then imposes power on him.

The sequence is important because it prevents “giving a Quirk” from being interpreted as generosity independent of consent. The same act category—granting power—can be coercive when it violates authorship over one's own body and role.

This strengthens the project's recurring distinction between capability transfer and legitimate agency.

## 6.4 `君は一人じゃない`

The younger brother's communication to Midoriya culminates in:

> `君は一人じゃない`

The line works on several levels without requiring later mechanics.

Midoriya is not alone because:

- the power contains a chain of prior lives/struggles;
- his present burden belongs to a historical conflict he did not originate;
- inherited resistance is relational rather than solitary possession.

The immediate involuntary OFA discharge on waking then prevents the dream from remaining psychologically sealed. Historical consciousness has a bodily present consequence.

V21 still does not explain the full rule set. That uncertainty is analytically valuable and should remain OPEN.

# 7. No.194 — Shinso returns: professionalizing a stigmatized affordance

## 7.1 Quirk appearance is not ethic

Shinso's Brainwashing has always invited social suspicion because it interferes directly with another person's agency. V21 does not answer that suspicion by pretending the Quirk is harmless.

Instead it asks a better professional question:

**Can a coercive capability be constrained, trained and used under legitimate rules?**

That makes Shinso more valuable than a simple “villain-looking Quirk used by a good person” example.

His development is about professionalization:

- explicit trigger conditions;
- tactical restraint;
- equipment that changes available deception channels;
- supervised exercises;
- institutional evaluation;
- responsibility for how the coercive effect is elicited and terminated.

## 7.2 Persona Chords expands interface, not ethics

Shinso's new mask/vocal equipment lets him imitate other voices. It significantly expands the tactical space around Brainwashing because opponents can no longer identify the speaker only by voice identity.

But the underlying condition remains: the target must respond.

That is important for power modeling. Support equipment does not erase a Quirk's rule; it changes the interface through which the rule can be exploited.

The same pattern appeared with Midoriya/Hatsume and Kaminari's targeting support: technology can increase control, optionality and safe usability without replacing the user's judgment.

## 7.3 Aizawa's mentorship is serious rather than sentimental

Aizawa treats Shinso as someone attempting to meet Hero Course standards, not as a student who should be praised merely for overcoming stigma.

That means training includes honest performance assessment.

After the first match, Shinso himself is dissatisfied with what he failed to execute. Aizawa does not soothe the dissatisfaction into meaningless reassurance. The current level was already known; the exercise exists to expose what still has to improve.

This is recognition as developmental accuracy rather than morale management.

# 8. No.195–198 — Joint Training Match 1: distributed competence under communication uncertainty

## 8.1 Class A's experience is not a guaranteed hierarchy

The Joint Training arc arrives after Class 1-A has endured repeated real-villain crises. It would be easy to assume that trauma-tested experience makes them categorically superior to Class 1-B.

V21 does not adopt that hierarchy.

Class B has developed through a different ecology: structured training, unfamiliar Quirk combinations and more deliberate team-specific planning. Class A brings crisis experience; Class B brings coordinated preparation. The exercise makes those developmental histories commensurable without claiming they are identical.

This is important for character modeling because “more screen time” and “more crisis exposure” should not be converted into in-world superiority by default.

## 8.2 Brainwashing turns communication itself into contested terrain

Shinso's presence changes every conversational exchange.

The opponent cannot simply treat speech as neutral coordination because answering an imitated or real Shinso voice can trigger Brainwashing.

Class B responds with precommitment and communication discipline.

The result is a tactical environment where language has action cost.

This is an unusually clear example of a speech-conditioned Quirk changing group cognition rather than only individual combat.

## 8.3 Class B's first-match capabilities

The first B team offers several distinct pressures:

- **Shishida Jurota / Beast** combines mobility, physical power and enhanced sensing; transformed affect is hotter and more aggressive than his calmer analytic register outside Beast state.
- **Shiozaki Ibara / Vines** supplies wide-area capture and spatial control.
- **Tsuburaba Kosei / Solid Air** creates invisible physical constraints and defensive/containment structures.
- **Rin Hiryu / Scales** supplies ranged pressure and projectile control.

Their competence matters because Class A's victory should not be read as simply overpowering weaker students.

The B plan creates real problems, captures Kirishima early and forces the A/Shinso team to redesign its information environment.

## 8.4 Tsuyu turns identity information into a weapon

Asui's most diagnostic contribution is not raw strength.

She uses bodily properties—especially scent/mucus—to create uncertainty about which target is actually her. That degrades the opponent's sensory inference and makes planned capture less reliable.

The move fits her established model:

- calm information processing;
- practical use of non-glamorous capabilities;
- team-oriented rather than ego-oriented decision making;
- willingness to exploit environmental and bodily details that others may ignore.

V21 strengthens Asui without requiring a readiness promotion.

## 8.5 Kaminari converts a liability into deliberate pressure

Kaminari's earlier model treated broad electrical output as dangerous to allies and therefore difficult to integrate into team combat. V12 added support-mediated ally-safe targeting.

V21 adds another step: **the threat of indiscriminate area output itself becomes tactical information**.

Opponents must account for what Kaminari could do, letting his team use him as deliberate pressure/bait rather than as a comic liability.

Together with V12's independent peer judgment and safer Quirk control, this gives enough cross-context evidence to promote Kaminari from `emerging (strongly grounded)` to `moderate`.

The promotion is not because he “wins a match.” It is because multiple volumes now establish:

- social/comic baseline;
- ally-awareness;
- independent moral judgment;
- equipment-mediated technical adaptation;
- willingness to become tactical bait;
- team communication under uncertainty.

## 8.6 Koda and Kirishima: distributed victory survives local failure

Koda uses animals/environmental awareness as part of the team information field. This strengthens his earlier fear-to-action evidence but does not yet provide enough ordinary/relationship breadth for `moderate`.

Kirishima is captured relatively early. The team does not collapse into rescue panic or treat his removal as proof the plan failed.

That matters because the match is structured around distributed function. A member can be lost locally without invalidating everyone else's responsibilities.

## 8.7 Shinso wins without being the sole winner

Shinso's Brainwashing is decisive, but the post-match interpretation explicitly resists singular savior logic.

Teammates recognize that each member's contribution mattered; removing one could have changed the outcome.

Shinso himself remains dissatisfied because he knows his movement, timing and combat execution are below his target.

This combination is excellent modeling evidence:

`external success + internal calibration gap`.

He can accept participation in a win without treating the scoreboard as proof that development is complete.

Readiness remains `moderate`, now much more strongly grounded across training, stigma, equipment, team coordination and self-evaluation.

# 9. No.199 — Hawks as mentor: recognition of unused affordance

## 9.1 Tokoyami's internship lesson is not imitation

The second-match material brings Hawks back in a different relationship: mentor to Tokoyami.

Hawks does not tell Tokoyami to fight like Hawks. Instead he identifies a structural mismatch in Tokoyami's development: too much attention is being spent covering a close-range weakness while an existing strength remains underdeveloped.

His advice includes:

> `弱点の近距離カバーに尽力もいいけど 得意を伸ばす事も忘れないほうがいい`
>
> `君はもっと自由に動ける`
>
> `飛べる奴は飛ぶべきだよ`

The “fellow bird” framing is playful, but the pedagogy is serious.

This is **affordance recognition**: the mentor sees a latent use already compatible with the student's own power system.

## 9.2 `黒の堕天使` as self-authored integration

Tokoyami converts Hawks's recognition into `黒の堕天使` / Black Fallen Angel, using Dark Shadow to support aerial movement.

The technique matters because V09 had established that Dark Shadow's magnitude can exceed Tokoyami's control in darkness. V21 adds a different developmental axis: not just suppressing or regulating Dark Shadow, but reorganizing the partnership between user and manifestation to create mobility.

The character/power model therefore broadens from:

`environmental darkness -> output/control risk`

to:

`Dark Shadow as partner-like extension -> regulation + support + self-mobility`.

That, combined with V09's crisis evidence and V21's mentor/team context, is sufficient to promote Tokoyami from `emerging` to `moderate`.

## 9.3 Hawks's teaching style strengthens his own readiness

This scene is also evidence about Hawks.

His mentorship style is:

- sparse rather than micromanaging;
- strength-oriented rather than deficit-obsessed;
- metaphorically casual;
- oriented toward freedom/optionality;
- comfortable letting the student derive the actual technique.

When combined with V20 public/media/rescue/combat evidence and V21 state-infiltration/private-role evidence, Hawks now has enough cross-context and role-conditioned breadth for `strong` character reconstruction.

The promotion does not require later childhood history. That history may later explain him better, but V20-V21 already establish how he behaves across radically different audiences and responsibilities.

# 10. No.199–200 — Joint Training Match 2: strategic recognition as pressure

## 10.1 Kuroiro makes Tokoyami's own medium adversarial

Kuroiro Shihai can enter and move through black/dark material. Dark Shadow therefore becomes not merely Tokoyami's tool but a contested medium.

This is a strong training design because it attacks assumptions about ownership of one's normal affordances.

Tokoyami cannot simply deploy Dark Shadow as if the battlefield meaning of “darkness” belongs only to him.

Kuroiro also adapts when the initial line becomes less favorable, using the industrial environment and Aoyama's dark costume/laser-related positioning opportunistically.

His current evidence is tactically rich but still too concentrated in one match for a `moderate` model.

## 10.2 Komori and Fukidashi convert environment into pressure

Komori Kinoko's Mushroom spreads through the environment rather than acting as a narrow projectile exchange. The power changes usable space and information conditions.

Fukidashi Manga's Comic turns written/spoken onomatopoeic effects into physical battlefield phenomena.

Both broaden the series' team-combat ontology: effective Quirks can win by changing terrain, visibility, movement and timing rather than by dealing direct damage.

At the current endpoint, both receive first substantial baselines, but the second match is unfinished and their ordinary/relationship breadth remains sparse.

## 10.3 Kendo's respect for Yaoyorozu is expressed as suppression

Kendo's plan is one of V21's strongest examples of adversarial recognition.

She does not flatter Yaoyorozu. She identifies her as the opponent whose thinking time is dangerous and builds the team plan around separating her from the other three.

Once Kendo engages directly, the policy is explicit:

> `考える時間は与えない`

This is recognition through threat assessment.

Kendo respects Yaoyorozu's planning ability enough to make denying that ability a primary objective.

Combined with V09's forest-camp evidence—environmental inference under gas exposure and coordinated action under poisoning pressure—V21 supplies enough cross-context leadership/decision breadth to promote Kendo from an emerging baseline to `moderate`.

The promotion remains bounded by the unresolved match result.

## 10.4 Yaoyorozu converts being targeted into a demand on self

The endpoint turns Kendo's strategy into a psychological event for Yaoyorozu.

Observers recognize that if Class B truly fears Yaoyorozu's planning, isolating her is rational. Todoroki argues that if they intend to neutralize her, the strongest treatment would have been to crush her immediately with all four members.

Yaoyorozu, meanwhile, recognizes the meaning of Kendo's assessment. Her internal response includes:

> `そんな私を "上"だと仰って下さる貴方に`
>
> `情けない姿を見せるわけにはいきません!`
>
> `これまで培ってきた全て! 今の私を見せなさい!`

This is not the same recognition structure as V08, where specific peer confidence helped repair self-doubt after failure.

Here recognition arrives from an opponent as strategic hostility. Yaoyorozu experiences the opponent's respect as an obligation to instantiate the competence being respected.

That creates a useful model:

`being recognized as dangerous/capable -> increased self-demand -> mobilize accumulated training`.

The volume ends before we know whether she succeeds.

That boundary is essential. V21 establishes her response to pressure, not the second-match outcome.

# 11. Character-state synthesis

## 11.1 Endeavor — strong, public legitimacy gains without private acquittal

V21 strengthens the already-strong Endeavor model in three ways:

1. he rises after catastrophic injury and defeats High-End;
2. the victory is visibly co-produced with Hawks rather than mythologized as solitary omnipotence;
3. family discussion after the victory demonstrates that professional success does not settle intimate moral debts.

His decision policy now needs at least two independent evaluative domains:

- **professional:** move toward the threat, absorb visible responsibility, persist under bodily limits, accept useful cooperation even from very different personalities;
- **family/repair:** attempt changed conduct without entitlement to forgiveness or control over the witnesses.

Do not simplify these into “Endeavor becomes good.”

## 11.2 Hawks — `moderate -> strong`

V21 supplies the missing role breadth needed for strong reconstruction:

- public hero persona;
- media/public-opinion fluency;
- distributed rescue;
- high-speed combat support;
- private intelligence behavior;
- explicit Commission-directed infiltration;

# 12. Relationship-state synthesis

## 12.1 Endeavor <-> Hawks — asymmetrical but reciprocal professional trust

Hawks respects Endeavor's persistence and supplies indispensable assistance. Endeavor accepts Hawks's operational value despite stylistic mismatch.

The relation is not equal in public rank or mission knowledge: Hawks knows about his Commission assignment; Endeavor does not.

That creates a structurally asymmetric partnership in which the more casual-seeming participant holds the larger hidden context.

## 12.2 Hawks <-> Dabi — adversarial trust construction

Dabi does not trust Hawks and tests him. Hawks needs access without controlling the rules of the test.

This relationship should be modeled as **credibility under hostile verification**, not friendship or stable alliance.

## 12.3 Hawks <-> Commission — delegated covert responsibility

The Commission does not merely give Hawks a tip. It assigns him to enter the League's network because his social/operational properties are considered suitable for long-horizon infiltration.

This is an institutional authority relation that meaningfully constrains his behavior while also requiring field discretion.

## 12.4 Endeavor <-> Todoroki family — separate update rates

Endeavor's professional victory updates each family member differently.

- Natsuo: low/no forgiveness; observes attempted change without granting moral reset.
- Fuyumi: stronger desire to move toward family connection.
- Shoto: watches; professional respect and paternal judgment remain separable.
- Rei: prior V20 state of recognizing effort while keeping direct-contact safety boundary remains governing where V21 does not supersede it.

There is no single “family relationship score.”

## 12.5 Hawks <-> Tokoyami — strength-recognition mentorship

Hawks's mentor style creates freedom rather than dependency.

He identifies unused potential, gives a conceptual nudge and leaves Tokoyami to author the actual solution.

This relationship contrasts productively with coercive inheritance elsewhere in the volume.

## 12.6 Shinso <-> Aizawa — exacting sponsorship

Aizawa supplies training and institutional access without lowering the bar.

Shinso is supported through serious expectation rather than exceptionalized sympathy.

## 12.7 Kendo <-> Yaoyorozu — adversarial esteem

Kendo's esteem is operational: because Yaoyorozu is dangerous when allowed to think, she should be isolated and denied time.

Yaoyorozu receives that pressure as proof that her competence is visible and as a demand not to underperform it.

# 13. Power philosophy: capability is neither status nor ethic

V21 sharply strengthens several power-philosophy propositions.

## 13.1 Strength can be relationally assembled

Endeavor's final High-End line depends on Hawks's mobility assistance. The strongest visible actor is not necessarily causally self-sufficient.

This continues the distributed-rescue logic seen with Eri and the Hassaikai raid.

## 13.2 Giving power can be coercive

All For One's historical conduct makes consent central. Granting a capability can be domination when the recipient's body/role is not self-authored.

## 13.3 Inherited power carries history before it carries complete explanation

OFA's vestige encounter makes history present without yet resolving technical mechanics.

The project should preserve this sequence rather than reading later exposition backward into V21.

## 13.4 Stigmatized affordances can be professionalized

Brainwashing is intrinsically agency-affecting, but its ethical status depends on rule, authority, context and use. Persona Chords expands tactical reach without erasing responsibility.

## 13.5 Mentorship should expand option space

Hawks does not teach Tokoyami to become a weaker copy of Hawks. He notices that Tokoyami can fly and pushes him to explore that affordance.

This aligns with All Might's V19 move from imitation toward principle-based Midoriya mentoring.

## 13.6 Team combat is an information system

Joint Training repeatedly treats powers as information constraints:

- Brainwashing changes whether answering speech is safe;
- Asui changes scent/identity inference;
- Solid Air changes invisible spatial assumptions;
- Kuroiro contests ownership of black media;
- mushrooms alter terrain and visibility;
- Kendo targets Yaoyorozu's time-to-think resource.

Power is increasingly modeled as control of decision conditions, not only force output.

## 14.1 Public legitimacy can be generated without universal affection

This matters for full-series character reconstruction because screen-time hierarchy should not be projected into in-world competency hierarchy.

# 15. Japanese language / register notes

## 15.1 Hawks: casual compression across high-stakes roles

Hawks's register remains light, colloquial and compressed even when discussing serious matters. The stability of surface casualness across publicity, combat, infiltration and mentorship makes it a core voice feature rather than an indicator of low seriousness.

Diagnostic phrases include:

- `あなただけですよ 本気で超えようとしてたのは` — admiration delivered without ceremonial grandiosity;
- `君はもっと自由に動ける` — concise, permissive mentorship;
- `ホラ…鳥仲間のよしみさ` — playful metaphor carrying genuine professional advice;
- `飛べる奴は飛ぶべきだよ` — characteristic conversion of principle into casual aphorism.

## 15.2 Natsuo: direct colloquial non-forgiveness

Natsuo's family register is valuable because it resists polished moral language.

`俺の中じゃイカレ野郎 絶賛継続中だよ` keeps anger ordinary and contemporary rather than solemn. That surface matters: he is not making a tribunal speech. He is asserting a lived family judgment.

## 15.3 Shinso: competitive dryness and self-calibration

Shinso's speech remains comparatively blunt and dry. Success does not produce exuberant self-congratulation; his attention turns quickly to what he could not execute.

Any reconstructed voice should preserve that gap between strong motive and restrained affect.

## 15.4 Kendo: strategic directness

`考える時間は与えない` is a compact statement of opponent modeling. Her tactical speech tends toward actionable diagnosis rather than theatrical intimidation.

## 15.5 Yaoyorozu: formal self-demand under respect

At `V21:p193`, Yaoyorozu's internal language is characteristically formal even under pressure:

- `そんな私を "上"だと仰って下さる貴方に`

Respect from Kendo is metabolized through obligation and self-command rather than boastful rivalry.

# 19. Volume-level synthesis

Volume 21 begins with the apparent collapse of the new No.1 and ends with a student trying to live up to an opponent's estimate of her competence.

Those scenes are much closer than they appear.

Endeavor is being judged by a public that has no reason to grant him All Might's inherited trust. He earns a real victory, but Hawks's assistance prevents the victory from becoming a myth of solitary replacement. The public can update in his favor while the Todoroki family keeps separate authority over what he was to them.

Hawks then reveals the most extreme form of plural trust in the volume. He is simultaneously a celebrated public hero, Endeavor's genuine professional partner, a Commission operative and a would-be League insider whose credibility is actively tested by Dabi. His surface casualness must function across all four roles without making them morally identical.

One For All expands the same problem backward through history. Midoriya discovers that the power he carries is not only an instrument or inheritance from All Might; it is partly an archive of a conflict whose earlier participants can become present to him. All For One's history shows power distribution used to create dependency, while the younger brother's resistance frames inheritance as something morally different when carried through opposition to possession. The mechanics remain incomplete, but the ethical history becomes harder to ignore.

Joint Training then miniaturizes plural adjudication into controlled competition. Shinso is not admitted as hero-worthy because readers know he is “actually good”; he is tested. Class A is not superior because narrative focus gave them more disasters; they are tested. Class B's less-visible development becomes visible through coordinated plans. Tokoyami is improved because Hawks saw an affordance he had not fully used. Kendo treats Yaoyorozu as dangerous enough to isolate. Yaoyorozu experiences that hostility as respect she must answer with present performance.

The volume's deepest rule is therefore:

> **Legitimacy is local to relationships and responsibilities, but it is not arbitrary. It is earned through conduct under the standards of a particular role, and success in one role cannot automatically purchase trust in another.**

This is why Endeavor's public victory and Natsuo's non-forgiveness can both be true. It is why Hawks can genuinely save people while deceiving an enemy organization under state orders. It is why Shinso's Brainwashing can be professionally legitimate in supervised hero work without becoming ethically neutral. It is why Kendo's respect appears as an attempt to deny Yaoyorozu the very resource that makes her dangerous.

V21 thus moves MHA farther away from a moral universe where power, status, popularity, trauma, inheritance or narrative centrality determine worth on their own.

The operative questions become:

- What role are you occupying?
- Who bears the consequences of your action?
- What information do the relevant witnesses have?
- What constraints make the capability legitimate or illegitimate here?
- What kind of trust has actually been earned?
- Which judgments remain outside your authority to control?

That architecture is especially valuable for later character reconstruction because it prevents “heroic,” “villainous,” “forgiven,” “trusted,” “strong” and “worthy” from becoming global personality labels.
