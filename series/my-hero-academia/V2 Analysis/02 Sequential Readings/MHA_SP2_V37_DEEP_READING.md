# My Hero Academia - Second-Pass Deep Reading, Volume 37

## 0. Status, method, and prospective lock

This reading begins strictly from the frozen Volume 36 boundary at `V36:p197`. No Volume 38 or later event is used to settle a Volume 37 ambiguity. The governing method remains `MHA_SP2_ANALYTICAL_METHOD_V2_1.md`: Japanese-primary evidence, prospective truth freeze, page-bounded claims, attention to speech/register and visual construction, explicit uncertainty, cumulative character/relationship-state routing, and character-model updates only when the source actually expands behavioral evidence.

Volume 36 ended by asking what interdependence means when a technically competent plan still encounters overwhelming disproportionality. Volume 37 answers by shifting the analytical center from **coordination itself** to **causal propagation**.

The strongest volume-level transition is:

> **interdependence under disproportionality -> causal inheritance under propagation: wounds, loyalties, powers and ideals become system-level forces according to what people choose to do with what they have inherited.**

The title of No.364, `何の為に力を使う`, and the Second user's formulation in No.369 provide the clearest local grammar. A power is not ethically or narratively settled by its capability alone. What matters is what purpose it is made to serve. Volume 37 then extends that principle beyond Quirks.

- Edgeshot converts his own body into repair rather than attack.
- Mirio converts Nighteye's idealistic "clean words" into a costly professional practice of making ideals real.
- AFO converts a one-use Rewind derivative into a self-erasing instrument for extending domination.
- Shoji converts scars and inherited social injury into an intergenerational anti-revenge project.
- The heteromorph agitator converts the same real wounds into expendable `肉壁`.
- Koda converts anger inherited through his mother's experience into protection of a friend.
- Spinner's movement identity is imposed from outside, but his own residual agency remains organized around Shigaraki-specific friendship.
- Kurogiri's reactivation converts that friendship request into a battlefield-wide transport event.
- Dabi's persistence, Toga's grievance, Hawks' earlier decision about Twice and global weather all become components in the chapter explicitly named `Butterfly Effect`.

Volume 37 therefore does not say that small actions are automatically good, or that all inherited pain can be redeemed. It says that **causality remains open after injury**. A wound can be exploited, transmitted, transformed, resisted, or used to justify another wound. The same is true of admiration, friendship, institutional memory and inherited power.

This makes AFO's ideology unusually legible. His explicit dream is not merely possession of powers. He wants to obstruct the futures imagined by everyone else. His political ideal is anti-plural: other people are permitted wants only insofar as those wants become material for his own future. The hero-side countermodel is not innocence. It is the continued production of futures that are not owned by one person.

The heteromorph material is essential to this thesis because it prevents a cheap moral binary. The grievances mobilized at Central Hospital are real. The crowd contains people who have been refused shelter and people whose forms still mark them as socially contaminating outside the metropolitan center. Shoji does not defeat that history by declaring it illegitimate. He instead distinguishes between the **truth of the wound** and the **ethics of the method chosen because of the wound**. The agitator attempts to collapse those two variables. Shoji separates them.

The final chapter literalizes the volume's causal argument as meteorology: distributed temperature disturbance generates an extraordinary pyrocumulonimbus system, the jet stream links Japan to North America, and the text invokes the classic butterfly-effect question. The same chapter then crosscuts between American political response, Dabi's remobilization, Kurogiri's portals, AFO's long-range preparation, Spinner's achievement and the return of Twice-shaped mass violence. The structure makes the proposition formal:

> **No local act stays merely local once enough relationships, infrastructures and unresolved histories connect the system.**

# 1. Governing question: what is power for?

No.364 names the volume's central analytical question directly: `何の為に力を使う` — what is power used *for*?

This is not a generic superhero maxim. Volume 37 repeatedly separates three variables that earlier arcs sometimes allowed to travel together:

1. **capacity** — what a body, Quirk, institution or relationship can do;
2. **purpose** — the end toward which that capacity is directed;
3. **propagation** — what consequences that use creates once it enters a larger system.

The Second user later tells Midoriya that what must be watched is not simply the supernatural ability itself but the will governing why a received power is exercised. `その“意思”に 力は宿る` is not best read as sentimental magic. In context, it is a thesis about inherited capacity: Gearshift became overwhelmingly stronger inside OFA, but its ethical meaning still depends on a present agent.

The same structure governs the heteromorph sequence. Shoji and the crowd possess bodily power. The crowd has real grievances. The decisive question is whether those capacities will protect, transform, retaliate indiscriminately, or be used as another actor's disposable wall.

AFO supplies the negative limit case. He is extraordinarily competent at finding existing wishes and wounds, but his stable use-policy is subordination. Other people can have futures only as long as those futures are instruments of his own.

# 2. No.363 `禦ぐ者と侵す者` — after the apparent terminal endpoint

## 2.1 Bakugo's childhood ideal becomes a measure of disproportionality

The opening recalls Bakugo's old rule for legitimate strength:

- `勝負は必ず完全勝利`
- `4-0 無傷`
- `本当に強え奴の勝利`

The image is not merely nostalgic contrast. Bakugo once defined proof of strength through total control: complete victory, no injury, no ambiguity. V36 ended with exactly the opposite visual and bodily condition. The point is not that his childhood aspiration was "wrong" in every sense; it is that reality has become too complex for a clean scoreboard to measure the moral worth of action.

This helps explain mature Bakugo's shift toward distributed victory. He has not stopped wanting decisive success. He has learned that his own action may depend on people whose contribution is invisible to the scoreboard, and that severe damage does not make those contributions unreal.

## 2.2 `脈が無い` / `心臓をやられた` closes one V36 uncertainty but not the entire medical future

At `V37:p007`, the immediate medical assessment becomes more concrete:

- no pulse is detected;
- the heart has been damaged.

The fused enemy says `今度は死んだ`.

The source therefore upgrades V36's "catastrophic, apparently terminal cardiac/chest condition" into **cardiac arrest / absent pulse with severe cardiac damage as observed on the field**. It still does not make the enemy's declaration the final medical adjudication, because subsequent chapters immediately initiate extraordinary surgical/resuscitative intervention.

This is precisely why the prospective freeze matters. V37 itself authorizes the existence of a rescue attempt. It does not yet authorize importing its ultimate success or failure from V38+.

## 2.3 The enemy's strongest argument is structural, not merely personal

The fused Shigaraki/AFO entity tells the heroes that they attacked first and did their best, but that it was still insufficient:

> they lacked enough people, preparation and resources.

This is one of the cleanest articulations of the V36→V37 transition. The villain does not need to prove that the hero system was foolish. He only needs to demonstrate that competent preparation can remain **materially outscaled**.

Mirko's self-blame — she should have killed Shigaraki earlier — shows the psychological danger of this condition. Under disproportionality, participants can retrospectively convert a system-level mismatch into a single personal counterfactual: *if only I had done more*. V37 does not validate that simplification.

## 2.4 Toya learns through contact, not through inherited equivalence

Toya reproduces a Phosphor-like thermal idea after directly encountering Shoto's technique:

- contact gives him an intuitive sense for how it works;
- he checks the imitation against Shoto.

This is high-value evidence for Toya's technical cognition. He is not merely a high-output berserker. He can sample an opponent's embodied technique, infer its organizing principle and rapidly reproduce a version of it.

The source does **not** establish that Toya's body can sustain the technique as Shoto's body can. Technique comprehension and body compatibility remain separate variables.

# 3. No.364 `何の為に力を使う` — AFO converts Rewind into disposable time

## 3.1 The V36 contingency resolves into a one-use self-erasing Rewind

Volume 36 showed AFO initiating a contingency visually linked to the Eri-derived research chain. V37 specifies the mechanism.

The current-body Rewind effect is:

- derived from the Quirk-erasure-drug research lineage;
- usable once;
- causing AFO's body to become progressively younger;
- restoring him toward bodily prime;
- **eventually erasing the user himself**.

AFO explicitly says that once he uses this Rewind, he will ultimately disappear.

This matters because it rejects a generic-regeneration model. The contingency is not "AFO can simply heal indefinitely." It is a **terminally self-consuming resource**. The body becomes stronger while the remaining time of that body shrinks.

The structure is almost an inversion of OFA inheritance. OFA accumulates power across people so that successors can continue beyond predecessors. AFO's contingency burns the present body away in order to extend the predecessor's agenda long enough to reach the successor vessel.

## 3.2 `夢のバトン` remains possessive succession

AFO says the baton of his dream has been passed to Shigaraki Tomura.

Read prospectively, that language cannot be treated as benevolent entrustment. V30–V36 already established that AFO's "succession" includes body occupation, hatred cultivation and the subordination of Shigaraki's agency.

The baton metaphor therefore remains **possessive succession**: what matters is continuation of AFO's future, whether or not the continuing body or nominal successor wants that future.

## 3.3 AFO finally states the dream in anti-plural terms

AFO's comics-derived dream becomes unusually explicit.

He observes that people possess different cultures, values and imagined futures. Universal hatred emerges when someone blocks another person's future. His desired position is therefore the one that obstructs futures everywhere:

- `世界中の未来を阻みたい`

The line clarifies why AFO cannot be reduced to greed for Quirks. Quirk theft is one instrument. The deeper fantasy is **universal causal centrality through obstruction**.

Everyone else should have to account for him.

This also makes the hero-side distributed system ideologically relevant. AFO's system produces order by making all routes pass through one owner. The hero coalition produces action by allowing many non-identical people to remain causal.

## 3.4 International capitulation exposes hero society at planetary scale

The American/international discussion shows governments already thinking strategically about accommodation to Shigaraki/AFO. The logic is not simple cowardice: leaders are modeling survival under a power imbalance and asking who can secure favorable standing first.

But Star and Stripe's legacy becomes the moral counterpressure. Her choice makes the next generation ask what adult institutions are teaching the young by preparing to accommodate domination.

The intergenerational line is explicit:

- children grow by watching adults;
- adults support those children;
- people weave something together;
- it passes onward again.

This is V37's propagation thesis in civic form. Political conduct becomes inherited social instruction.

## 3.5 Edgeshot turns self-sacrifice into repair labor

Edgeshot's answer to `何の為に力を使う` is almost maximally literal:

- something is missing/broken in Bakugo;
- he will fill it and make it move;
- he will become the child's heart.

The sequence matters because self-sacrifice here is not spectacular attack. It is microsurgical **repair labor**.

That distinction should be preserved in the power-philosophy ledger. MHA repeatedly criticizes unsustainable self-destruction when it substitutes for judgment. Edgeshot's act is still life-costly, but it is targeted, technical and other-preserving rather than a reflexive proof of worth.

# 4. No.365 `No.4とNo.5` — bodies can be used to preserve another body

## 4.1 Foldabody becomes surgical infrastructure

Edgeshot has refined Foldabody through years of training until he can reduce himself to thread-like dimensions.

The procedure includes:

- disinfecting before entry;
- suturing internal tissue and vessels;
- repairing lung damage;
- supporting cardiopulmonary function.

His body is not functioning as a mystical resurrection spell. It is functioning as living surgical material.

This is important for both source accuracy and thematic interpretation. Bakugo's condition remains severe enough that an elite professional must consume his own embodied future to attempt resuscitative repair.

## 4.2 Tenko's destructive urge remains present inside the fused body

The childlike complaint that he already broke things and is still itchy preserves the Tenko/Shigaraki affective layer. It should not be flattened into proof that one personality has fully regained control.

V37 continues the V36 pattern:

- the fused structure can speak with AFO-like hierarchy and strategy;
- Shimura/Tenko-linked affect can still intrude;
- those intrusions have specific triggers.

The correct claim is therefore **nonuniformity**, not clean separability.

## 4.3 Bakugo becomes threatening because the fused hierarchy cannot explain him

The fused entity asks why Bakugo created panic and whether he felt `脅威` from a mere `一般人`.

The word matters. AFO's hierarchy assigns significance according to inherited/stolen exceptional power and role in the OFA conflict. Bakugo without OFA should not matter enough to produce threat.

That is exactly why he becomes conceptually offensive.

Bakugo's progress therefore has symbolic significance beyond damage output: he is proof that a person outside AFO's chosen dyad can become causally important enough to destabilize the sovereign.

## 4.4 `誰も僕を見てなかった` returns failed recognition to the center

The body produces family-memory forms and the child voice recalls that no one reached out before everything was destroyed:

- nobody extended a hand;
- nobody looked at him.

This strengthens the recognition/failed-rescue thesis without absolving later mass violence.

The point is causal: non-recognition belongs in the formation history. It is not exculpatory jurisdiction.

## 4.5 Morphology remains local adaptation, not a universal singularity law

The source describes the modified body as seeking forms appropriate to:

- the many accumulated Quirks;
- diverse external environmental conditions.

That supports **adaptive morphology in this engineered body**. It should not be generalized into a universal rule that every Quirk user inevitably produces such transformations.

# 5. No.366 `桃` — repair, humor and delayed arrival

## 5.1 Shigaraki's body shifts from offense optimization toward defense

After the finger-field offensive form and accumulating damage/Erasure pressure, the body explores defensive morphology.

The sequence is useful because it shows adaptation as a response to changing problem conditions rather than a fixed "final form." The body is an optimization process under stress.

## 5.2 `人が壊したもんを直すなよ`

The fused enemy tells Edgeshot not to repair what another person broke.

This compact line makes the volume's ethical antithesis almost embarrassingly clear.

AFO/Shigaraki's destructive grammar treats brokenness as jurisdiction: once something is broken, repair is an illegitimate reversal of the destroyer's will.

Edgeshot's entire action rejects that premise.

Repair therefore becomes political as well as medical. It says that harm does not acquire ownership rights over the harmed object/person simply because it happened first.

## 5.3 Mirio weaponizes social repertoire rather than damage output

Mirio still lacks a high-output offensive weapon against the fused body. His solution is not to pretend otherwise. He remembers Nighteye's insistence on smiling and uses an absurd naked-`桃` gag to force a momentary pause.

The beat is comedic, but analytically important:

- Mirio recognizes his actual constraint;
- he searches a different capability domain;
- social surprise becomes tactical time.

This is another V37 case where noncombat human repertoire becomes battlefield infrastructure.

## 5.4 Midoriya's arrival closes one V36 logistical OPEN state

Midoriya reaches floating U.A. at the end of the chapter.

The volume therefore closes the logistical question of whether he can return to the intended battlefield. What remains open is how he will regulate himself after seeing Bakugo and the battlefield condition.

# 6. No.367 `デク VS AFO` — anger becomes a prediction problem

## 6.1 Star's pilots remain because adult choices propagate through loyalty

The American pilots stay after formal withdrawal pressure because of Star's example and their relationship to her.

Their presence should not be over-romanticized into a general claim that institutions have fully rejected capitulation. The international political layer remains divided.

But Star's individual decision has acquired downstream force after her death. This is an early V37 butterfly-effect case before the title names the pattern.

## 6.2 AFO tries to convert guilt into behavioral predictability

On seeing the battlefield, Midoriya is immediately vulnerable to self-accusation.

AFO/Shigaraki asks whether Toga delayed him and offers a deliberately corrosive permission structure: call it unavoidable and escape responsibility like "the others."

The manipulation works by targeting a true trait. Midoriya **does** take responsibility seriously. AFO attempts to make that trait overrun judgment.

He also predicts that anger will make Midoriya's movement short and linear enough to counter.

This is high-value modeling evidence because the antagonist is not merely insulting him. He is using a behavioral model of Midoriya.

## 6.3 Mirio distinguishes idealism from denial

Mirio's intervention is one of the volume's clearest ethical statements.

He tells Midoriya:

- Edgeshot is treating Bakugo;
- the team is fighting on the assumption that Edgeshot will succeed;
- they have not surrendered anything;
- they are heroes.

When the fused enemy calls this `現実逃避`, Mirio answers that heroes are precisely the people who risk their lives to practice the "pretty words" that make ideals real.

The line should not be paraphrased as "optimism is always correct." Mirio's argument is conditional:

> an ideal becomes professionally serious when people organize costly action around making it real.

That is why saying "Bakugo can still be saved" is not empty positivity while Edgeshot is literally inside Bakugo's chest attempting repair.

## 6.4 `怒るのは良い` — emotional regulation is not emotional suppression

Mirio does not tell Midoriya not to be angry.

He says anger is fine; the important thing is controlling the heart/mind.

This revises any simple "mature Midoriya stops getting angry" model. Mature regulation means preserving anger without allowing the opponent to select the behavior that anger produces.

Midoriya apologizes. Mirio tells him to apologize after they win.

The exchange is friendly but operational: self-reproach cannot become the next delay.

## 6.5 Midoriya's first stabilized question is still about Shigaraki

Once regulated, Midoriya asks whether Shigaraki is still there.

This is crucial.

The answer to AFO's attempt to make him purely retaliatory is not emotional detachment. Midoriya restores the rescue/recognition question **inside** combat.

That makes V37 one of the strongest pieces of evidence that his Shigaraki-directed rescue interest is not a luxury available only in calm conditions.

# 7. No.368 `唸れOFA` — integration claims collide with behavior

## 7.1 The fused entity claims complete integration and AFO primacy

The entity says the two have fused and become completely integrated, while also suggesting that AFO has primacy because he has lived longer.

Taken alone, this would support a strong merger claim.

The chapter immediately gives counterevidence.

## 7.2 Mirio's social observation becomes identity evidence

Mirio notes that the response to his "no friends" provocation did not look like a smoothly unified person. The more pressure everyone applied, the more unstable the entity seemed.

This is valuable because it is **behavioral** evidence, not metaphysical declaration.

Nana then tells Midoriya that Shigaraki is still there.

The combined V37 position is:

- AFO-oriented control/primacy is strong;
- the fused structure describes itself as integrated;
- Tomura/Tenko-linked affect remains causally active;
- complete practical homogeneity is false.

What remains OPEN is whether this nonuniformity permits durable separation, rescue, independent control or anything else beyond temporary affective intrusion.

## 7.3 Gearshift is a terminal-window resource

The Second user warns that using Gearshift creates a very short decisive window.

Midoriya commits to settling the battle within that period.

This continues the volume's recurring resource grammar. AFO has a self-erasing Rewind clock. Midoriya has a Gearshift limit. Edgeshot is consuming his own body. The war increasingly runs on **finite embodied clocks**.

## 7.4 OFA output is explicitly multi-Quirk synthesis

Blackwhip/Fa Jin/Gearshift and the stockpiled OFA base are not independent trick cards. Midoriya now composes them into an integrated movement/combat system.

The text's "120%" description should be treated as the local portrayal of composite output, not as a permanent universal power number detached from conditions.

# 8. No.369 `連なる星霜` — inherited power has changed, inherited purpose remains adjudicable

## 8.1 Gearshift demonstrates inherited amplification

AFO remembers the Second user's original Gearshift as minor enough to dismiss.

Inside OFA:

- the Quirk has been amplified across generations;
- its effective domain now extends to extremely fine bodily scale;
- combined with OFA stockpile, it can change motion without normal inertial dependence.

This is a good technical example of why present OFA Quirks cannot be reconstructed solely from predecessor-era capability.

## 8.2 `注意すべきは異能じゃあない`

The Second user tells AFO that the important thing is not simply the ability.

The key is why the inherited thing is used; intention gives force its present meaning.

This is the cleanest direct support for the volume-level thesis.

It also avoids an essentialist moral taxonomy of Quirks. Brainwashing, copying, body folding, fire, decay, warp, animal communication and Rewind are not ethical identities. Their current use-policy matters.

## 8.3 OFA and AFO remain relationally resonant

AFO perceives Yoichi through the OFA encounter.

This continues the long-established sibling/vestige resonance without authorizing a complete model of range or mechanism beyond what the source shows.

## 8.4 AFO identifies the coalition as the reason the perfected body cannot use its full resource set

AFO recognizes that Erasure, containment architecture and the younger generation have prevented the perfected body from using even one Quirk freely.

This is an important correction to "the heroes are simply outclassed."

They are outscaled in many dimensions, but their system has already achieved an extraordinary constraint outcome. The battle's instability comes from the enemy's redundancy and adaptability, not from hero incompetence.

AFO then pivots to the contingency that depends on Spinner.

# 9. No.370 `HIStory` — discrimination is not metaphorical background

## 9.1 The volume makes heteromorph persecution explicit

The opening pages use direct exclusionary language:

- monster;
- stay inside;
- polluted/defiled land;
- cursed/impure blood;
- refusal to accept that blood.

This closes any interpretive route that treats heteromorph grievance as entirely fabricated by villains.

The history is materially real and socially persistent.

## 9.2 Metropolitan progress is unevenly distributed

The Central Hospital front reveals a cleavage between visible urban integration and exclusion outside the center.

Koda is dismissed as a city-raised traitor because his experience does not match the crowd's.

This is not merely geographic flavor. It is a theory of uneven modernization:

> a society can become more tolerant in its brightest spaces while leaving peripheral populations exposed to older violence.

The agitator's line that even strong light cannot illuminate everything is rhetorically manipulative **and** empirically grounded enough to work.

## 9.3 `暴徒` becomes a contested label

Present Mic/defenders can operationally see a dangerous mass attack.

Participants hear the category `暴徒` as another instance of people with ordinary faces refusing to understand what produced the assembly.

Both levels must be preserved.

The crowd is capable of dangerous collective violence. The label can also erase causal history.

V37 refuses to solve the problem by choosing only one description.

## 9.4 The agitator offers a coherent radicalization narrative

The speech invokes:

- failed Quirk-counseling / educational accommodation;
- named historical massacres;
- murder justified by appearance;
- a society that claims reflection without fully changing peripheral reality;
- people who were never reached by the light of reform.

The rhetorical endpoint is self-illumination and a representative figure.

This is politically potent because it supplies three things at once:

1. a history;
2. a community identity;
3. a legitimating symbol.

The next chapters reveal the exploitative machinery inside that rhetoric.

## 9.5 Spinner is already estranged from the role assigned to him

Internally, Spinner cannot follow the grand political speech.

Part of that is neurological degradation from AFO's enhancement. But even before complete collapse, the symbolic identity does not match his own organizing motive.

He is being turned into the `representative` of a grievance movement by others.

His actual goal remains narrower and relational: reach Kurogiri for Shigaraki and the League.

This distinction is crucial to any later synthesis of heteromorph politics. Spinner is not a clean ideological spokesman for the crowd simply because the crowd uses him that way.

# 10. No.371 `しょーじくんといっしょ。` — Shoji becomes a high-resolution moral and behavioral model

Volume 37 is the first volume that gives Shoji enough independent causal material to move beyond "quiet, protective classmate."

## 10.1 His first objection is practical responsibility, not respectability politics

Shoji asks what the hospital assault means for the patients inside.

This is important because his disagreement with the crowd begins from **who will be harmed now**, not from a demand that marginalized people remain decorous.

He does not deny the persecution history. He asks whether that history authorizes making uninvolved patients the next cost.

## 10.2 The crowd tries to force symbolic authorization from Spinner

Participants ask the `representative` to tell them their casualties mean something and to affirm that they are justice.

Spinner answers `知らネェよ`.

The gap is devastating.

The movement needs a person onto whom it can project coherent authorization. Spinner does not possess that coherent political voice in his current state.

The agitator immediately repairs the gap by narrating sacrifice himself.

This is a recurring AFO-side technique: where personhood is inconvenient, role language fills the space.

## 10.3 Shoji's persecution history supplies causal depth without converting him into vengeance

Shoji explains that his parents did not share his arm phenotype and that his village treated contact across visible difference as contamination severe enough to trigger communal violence.

This does several things for reconstruction:

- establishes the source of his scars;
- explains why he uses a mask;
- establishes early social surveillance;
- gives him first-person authority in the heteromorph conflict;
- demonstrates that his current calm is not produced by lack of exposure.

His anti-revenge position therefore cannot be modeled as naïveté.

## 10.4 Shoji deliberately prevents classmates from tiptoeing around his appearance

The ordinary-life detail matters as much as the trauma history.

Shoji:

- accepts the hero name Tentacole;
- reads silly appearance-based rankings;
- does not want classmates becoming awkwardly over-considerate around his visible difference.

The p148 paratext reinforces this low-temperature good-sport quality. Faced with being overwhelmingly ranked the Class A student who would look good in Japanese clothes, he responds in dry, overformal fashion that he will not become complacent and will continue striving in kimono.

This is rare mundane behavioral evidence and should enter reconstruction.

Shoji's comfort is not "appearance never matters." It is closer to:

> **do not let awareness of difference make ordinary relationship impossible.**

## 10.5 `俺は復讐者と思われたくない`

Shoji's mask is partly a control over social meaning.

Because scars and heteromorphic features invite other people to assign stories to his body, he does not want to be read as a revenge figure.

This is a sophisticated identity-management policy.

He cannot control the history written onto his body, but he can influence what role he publicly performs.

## 10.6 One good memory is enough to keep a different future imaginable

Shoji says he has many bad memories and will not forget them.

His counterweight is not statistical denial. He clings to even one memory in which having this body was good: saving a drowning child.

That is psychologically important.

He does not need to prove that suffering was "worth it." He needs evidence that the body associated with suffering can also become the means of preserving somebody else.

Classmates respond by promising more good memories.

The exchange establishes a reconstruction rule:

> Shoji processes injury by protecting a future in which the injured identity acquires additional meanings, rather than by requiring the past meaning to disappear.

## 10.7 His theory of social change is explicitly intergenerational

Shoji does not believe a social barrier more than a century old can become flat in one generation.

He therefore adopts a transmission model:

- predecessors carried change;
- he will continue it;
- become an admirable hero;
- pass good memories onward.

This is neither passive gradualism nor revolutionary cleansing.

It is **intergenerational norm production**.

The line belongs in the hero-society ledger because Shoji has now articulated a theory of how representation can change social expectation without making representative status a license to command others.

# 11. Shoji's anti-revenge doctrine does not erase grievance

## 11.1 He distinguishes cause from authorization

Shoji tells the crowd that he too has been persecuted.

He also says the method is wrong.

The important structure is:

- injury is real;
- people who inflicted that injury have no moral authority;
- but victimhood does not automatically authorize transferring injury to unrelated people.

That is one of the strongest examples in the corpus of **recognition without exculpation applied to a group grievance rather than a villain dyad**.

## 11.2 A current shelter rejection prevents "history is over" interpretation

A participant says shelters still refuse heteromorphs.

That line blocks a simplistic reading in which Shoji represents an enlightened present scolding people trapped in an obsolete past.

The exclusion is current.

Shoji's argument must therefore function while the grievance remains live.

## 11.3 `怒りの使い方を考えろ`

Shoji tells them to think about **how to use anger**.

The verb is central to V37.

Anger is not forbidden. It is a resource requiring purpose.

He says they can think because they have wounds.

This reverses the agitator's model. The agitator treats wounds as a reason to stop deliberating and move. Shoji treats wounds as a reason to become more careful about what one's force does.

## 11.4 `復讐者にならないでくれ`

Shoji's warning is future-facing.

If today's injured group reproduces indiscriminate harm, tomorrow's children may inherit the retaliatory category.

The point is not "be morally pure so oppressors approve of you." It is:

> do not make the next generation carry a new injury produced in your name.

This makes his anti-revenge stance deeply consistent with the volume's propagation thesis.

# 12. Koda Koji — anger becomes a family inheritance with a use-policy

V37 supplies enough Koda material to substantially change his behavioral model.

## 12.1 His battlefield choice is relational

Koda's decision is simple:

> if Shoji is going, he will go.

This is not merely obedience. The later material makes clear that Shoji is a person whose treatment activates Koda's strongest anger.

The relationship itself is causal.

## 12.2 Koda's mother supplies a family model of stigmatized embodiment

Koda's mother has horns and has experienced cruel treatment.

The family therefore has direct heteromorph/stigma history rather than only abstract sympathy.

His own changing horn/communication capability creates a visible bodily continuity with that inheritance.

## 12.3 The family's anger ethic is explicit

Koda's inherited lesson is not "never get angry."

It is that when something precious is mocked or harmed, he should become an adult capable of getting angry properly.

This is one of the most useful lines for simulation because it supplies a **conditional decision rule**:

- baseline: quiet, restrained, low verbal temperature;
- trigger: harm/ridicule directed at someone he values;
- desired response: protective anger under control.

When Shoji is mocked, Koda's anger becomes direct and verbal.

That is a major expansion over the older insect-phobia/courage evidence.

## 12.4 Quirk growth and emotional development align without collapsing into one another

Koda's expanded animal communication appears alongside a more explicit willingness to act from anger.

The source supports thematic parallel, not a mechanistic claim that emotional growth literally caused the Quirk change.

# 13. No.372 `NAKED` — the crowd refuses to become somebody else's meat wall

## 13.1 Shoji's hero identity requires taking this assignment

Before battle, Shoji says he could not ignore the threat to heteromorph hospital patients and still call himself a hero.

This is strong profession/identity evidence.

His choice is not simply "I am the heteromorph student, therefore I represent heteromorph issues." It is:

> his own history creates a special responsibility to notice a threat that others may under-read.

That is a more defensible model of representation than the one imposed on Spinner.

## 13.2 Present Mic recognizes generational strengthening without reducing students to weapons

Mic notes that the current students are stronger than Aizawa/Shirakumo/Mic were at the same age.

The line participates in the series' generational-power theme, but it is embedded in an adult/student relationship where strength does not eliminate adult responsibility.

Adults still coordinate and protect. Younger power is not an excuse for institutional abandonment.

## 13.3 The agitator admits instrumentalization

The agitator's mask drops.

The crowd is a `肉壁`.

He has intentionally sharpened their emotional heat.

He panics when they begin to become calm.

This is decisive for the villain-formation ledger. The grievance was not invented. The **conversion of grievance into disposable mass** was deliberate.

The causal exploitation sequence is:

1. real exclusion;
2. accumulated anger;
3. narrative simplification;
4. symbolic representative;
5. de-individuated sacrifice;
6. benefit to another actor's war plan.

## 13.4 The crowd's self-reflection preserves the uprising's moral information

Participants remember:

- the exhilaration of finally standing;
- the belief that silence itself was wrong;
- the desire to overturn a world that left them behind;
- the appeal of having a representative.

They do not decide that every motive was false.

They stop when the method becomes impossible to reconcile with attacking uninvolved patients.

This distinction is why Shoji's persuasion works where pure suppression could not.

## 13.5 Shoji does not tell them their action was meaningless

His post-deescalation language is unusually careful.

He tells them that the feeling that made them stand up was not necessarily meaningless or wrong.

He apologizes for not noticing.

He marks the person who continued thinking as bright.

And he redirects the same "light" metaphor toward changing those who injure heteromorphs until the injurers themselves become ashamed of their fists.

This is not pacification through denial.

It is **rechanneling political agency away from indiscriminate victimization and toward social transformation**.

The defenders explicitly recognize that force alone would only have subdued the crowd. Shoji changes what the crowd is willing to do.

That is a major heroism claim: persuasion can be a form of rescue when it preserves both immediate lives and the grievance's truth.

# 14. Spinner — imposed movement symbol, degraded cognition, persistent friendship

Spinner believes he could not become anyone.

Following Shigaraki offered the possibility of becoming something.

# 15. No.373 `FRIENDS` — friendship survives when ideology and cognition fall away

## 15.1 Spinner's remaining statement is relational, not doctrinal

Spinner says he simply wanted to follow.

The sentence should not be romanticized into healthy dependence. It is still part of a violent criminal relationship.

When elaborate political language falls away, Shigaraki remains.

## 15.2 Present Mic's grief contains two incompatible wishes

This is mature grief because it does not assume recognition guarantees recovery.

Spinner directly asks Kurogiri to save Shigaraki and the others.

Kurogiri awakens and defines himself as:

This is a major current-identity statement.

The source does **not** resolve the Shirakumo layer away. The very fact that friendship-address and memory have mattered remains part of the evidence.

The correct V37 state is therefore:

> Kurogiri's operative protector identity is reactivated; Shirakumo-linked continuity remains unresolved rather than erased.

# 16. No.374 `Butterfly Effect` — the volume makes propagation literal

## 16.1 Weather becomes a systems diagram

The extraordinary cloud system over Japan is linked to:

- distributed local temperature increases;
- wider pressure conditions;
- jet-stream propagation toward North America.

The narration invokes the classic butterfly-effect question.

The text is not presenting a precise meteorological treatise. Its narrative function is to show that many local disturbances can couple into a much larger system whose effects cross national boundaries.

That is exactly what the war has become.

## 16.2 The glowing-baby era is framed as a historical perturbation, not a deterministic proof

The commentary asks whether superhuman society was destined for this chaos.

The counterstatement is that the world can be changed by one person's power/action.

This should be read as an anti-fatalistic historical claim.

History constrains. It does not mechanically determine.

That principle has already been demonstrated at smaller scales:

- Toya's history does not make one present response inevitable;
- Shoji's persecution does not force revenge;
- Spinner's marginalization does not map cleanly onto the political role assigned to him;
- Endeavor's past does not disappear, but it also does not settle every future act.

## 16.3 American political conflict shows Star's choice continuing to act

Meryl explicitly opposes governments/world actors currying favor with AFO.

Star's earlier decision therefore persists as political evidence inside another person's argument.

This is the civic analogue of OFA inheritance: a dead person's action can remain causal without literally controlling the successor.

# 17. Dabi's remobilization: immediate defeat was not physiological closure

# 18. Monoma at floating U.A.: recognition-seeking survives catastrophe

The line is excellent reconstruction evidence precisely because the stakes are enormous.

# 19. Kurogiri's portals turn relationship into infrastructure

Once Kurogiri activates Warp Gate, fronts that had been deliberately separated become causally reconnected.

# 20. AFO's emotional theory: people are predictable through what they care about

AFO says he trusts human emotion.

This should not be misunderstood as empathy.

He trusts emotion as a **control surface**.

Throughout V37:

- Toga's grief can be targeted;
- Dabi's father fixation can be targeted;

Hero-side relationality makes the opposite wager: attachment can also create repair, restraint and distributed action.

# 21. Hawks, Twice and Toga: an earlier decision returns as present threat

Whether the current "Twice" threat is exactly what it appears to be, how Toga's blood mechanics operate here, and whether Hawks can or should execute that response remain V38+ questions.
