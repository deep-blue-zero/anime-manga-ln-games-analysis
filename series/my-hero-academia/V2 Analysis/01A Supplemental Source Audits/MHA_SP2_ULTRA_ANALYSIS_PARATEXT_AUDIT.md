Ultra Analysis expands characterization most usefully where it gives repeated ordinary practices, directed relationships and changing self-understanding. Daily technique exchanges, messages to family, music introduced by a friend and hosted leisure gatherings specify what characters do with one another between crises. These additions have more reconstruction value than an undifferentiated increase in profile facts, while still requiring source and temporal limits. The book also makes the limits of official editorial presentation visible: chart axes differ, historical news credits conceal actual participants, and some profile assertions conflict with other pages or earlier sources.

This audit owns the book-level argument. ## Repeated ordinary practices change the available questions

The early profiles give concrete ways in which relationships continue outside an exceptional rescue. Midoriya treasures Kota's letter and adds Eri's letter to those valued objects (s0023); Bakugo answers occasional parental emails in two brusque forms (s0025). Iida and Midoriya exchange kicking-technique ideas daily (s0031). Asui's family circumstances have changed enough for frequent video contact with her siblings (s0033). Such details can support bounded contexts—receiving gratitude, replying to family, discussing a shared skill—without proving an invariant response in every later life stage.

Reciprocity is more discriminating than a common hobby label. Momo now listens to metal introduced by Jiro, and Jiro is becoming interested in classical music introduced by Momo (s0037/s0045). Sato's weekly dorm event joins his baking with Momo's tea (s0059). Pony's anime screenings follow Yanagi's horror gatherings (s0077). These assertions show tastes circulating through relationships and people creating occasions for others. They enlarge the ordinary-life account beyond a private preference list.

They also qualify a simple opposition between usefulness and pleasure. Baking, music and shared media may give pleasure to the host and participants while expressing contribution. The text need not strip them of social benefit to establish their value. The existing S6/F3 distinction among imposed function, chosen contribution and enjoyment without a contribution requirement remains the useful test. A guidebook's recurring-routine assertion can deepen that account without making the main manga retroactively dramatize every gathering.

Newly specified repetition is still an editorial profile claim, not a set of independently witnessed episodes. A weekly event does not supply separate observed reactions for every attendee. Nor does sibling contact prove that family strain, guilt or distance has disappeared. A character may gain factual texture, relationship specificity or a plausible low-stakes domain without satisfying the separate gates for a readiness promotion.

## Relationship maps retain asymmetry and limited knowledge

Midoriya and Aoyama can be described as recently close while Midoriya still cannot read Aoyama's thinking (s0023). The perceived shared bodily difficulty and cheese care in Aoyama's profile must therefore remain distinct from later traitor knowledge. Neither the reader's eventual revelation nor Aizawa's earlier uncertainty gives another participant complete hidden knowledge at this stage.

Likewise, Inasa's determination to become Shoto's best friend is not a mutually settled best-friend status. Shoto's response that he need not force closeness preserves the recipient's different position (s0027). Shinso's general-course classmates preparing a farewell party that he does not know about (s0137) supplies another clear division: planned peer care is already present, while its receipt and the recipient's response are unshown.

Ochako's suppressed feeling and response to teasing differ from an acknowledged relationship. The map's mutual-interest wording is a question; Midoriya's attributed voice stresses repeatedly being helped (s0029). These voices may supplement relational characterization, but they are guidebook constructions rather than additional dated conversations. The audience is the book's reader unless a narrative interaction explicitly identifies someone else.

Bakugo's editorial development account connects peer rescue, All Might's retirement and learning Midoriya's Quirk truth with more attentive cooperative fighting (s0025). His guilt that he ended All Might is retained as his belief. Neither the profile's positive growth nor cooperation erases earlier bullying, displaced anger in UA's THE・DEPTH, continuing abrasiveness or the later need for apology. Character development must retain the situation in which a capacity has actually changed.

## Ratings and headlines can compress away the needed qualification

The HERO CARD key at s0020 labels identifiers, role-type icons, Quirk and radar chart. It supplies no empirically calibrated common personality scale. Actual fifth axes include Midoriya's aura, Bakugo's language use, Shoto's soba interest, Ochako's economizing and Iida's earnest seriousness (s0022–0030); later cards add still other dimensions. A shared letter or chart position does not make them commensurable with UA's cooperation axis. Grades are attributed editorial shorthand, not readiness measurements.

The Shoto profile shows a similar issue in prose. Its headline announces acceptance of father and flame, while its caption still identifies hesitation toward fire and its relationship map keeps him watching Endeavor (s0027). Read with UA:s0118's reduced hatred without liking, this develops a conditional history. It cannot certify forgiveness, complete family repair or submission to Endeavor's old ownership claim. The narrower detail has to survive the summary.

Mineta's presentation requires the same discipline. His intelligence, competence and participation coexist with ongoing desire, named recipients of misconduct and Asui's restraint (s0040–0041). A favorable capability score, friendship or later self-risk cannot establish acknowledged harm or changed sexual boundaries. The final character disposition must test those requirements explicitly.

## Public news and wanted files are selective accounts

HERO NEWS opens at s0205 and calls its spreads collected news articles. At s0209 the Stain resolution is publicly credited to Endeavor, while Native's voice thanks the boys; s0203 separately makes the actual student rescue explicit as editorial summary. These are not two competing versions of who acted. Read with UA's police cover account, the distinction concerns knowledge, lawful recognition and public narrative. Professional evacuation, Nomu fighting and adult responsibility remain real without transferring the students' intervention to Endeavor.

The format repeatedly signals uncertainty. A s0216 caption asks whether Nighteye took an attack to change the future; a question is not established intent. At s0217 the convoy report leaves the League's true purpose unknown even though participants and manga readers can have other access. Likewise Hawks's real name can be printed for readers while s0161 states it is not public. A guide that combines these registers cannot be assigned one universal audience-knowledge state.

Several wanted-file comparisons need distinct verdicts. Spinner's Gecko/wall-clinging information (s0233) resolves the earlier UA sword-based conjecture; his movement from Stain inspiration toward Shigaraki retains unease, rather than establishing complete ideological certainty. Magne's explicit martial-arts competence at s0234 narrows UA's earlier inferred weakness without proving a narrated training improvement. The same page connects Muscular to Dr. Daruma but expressly leaves the relation to his Quirk unknown. Chisaki's s0243 statement about treatment and Quirk modification must remain source-attributed; it is not proof that severed arms regrew or that unshown restored activation was demonstrated.

Stain's s0238 profile presents League affiliation/shared ideological cooperation more firmly than s0223's disavowal and the s0208 news speculation. Keep this internal inconsistency visible, separate from the public arrest-credit issue. Later-guide publication alone does not tell us why a caption conflicts with another page.

The glossary supplies a particularly consequential overstatement. Its CRC entry describes the organization as a coelacanth-like remnant in a modern era supposedly without prejudice against Quirks (s0285). Shoji's profile explicitly says prejudice against heteromorphs persists in his hometown (s0053), and s0261 preserves active CRC splinters. The unqualified disappearance framing cannot erase the local persistence. These pages do not establish CRC responsibility for Shoji's particular experiences or a chronology that dissolves the tension. Their conflict helps distinguish institutional/editorial optimism from conditions experienced by affected people.

Other source differences concern identity or presentation rather than development: UAN's explicit identification of Thirteen as a woman is more specific than Snipe's earlier UA pronoun; it is not a narrated gender transition. Tiger's 29 February/190 cm data agree with the preferred UA EPUB rather than its older CBZ. Agreement of dependent reference books is not a growth event, and period gender-history wording does not authorize an invented medical biography.

## Parameter claims need state and speaker controls

A narrow source check concerns Monoma. UAN:s0066 states a five-minute Copy duration; the facing profile s0067 expressly gives four currently held Quirks and includes later joint-training material. In the unchanged original manga at V23:p046, Ochako reasons from a five-minute limit, while captured Monoma raises the possibility of pretending five after extension to ten. At p047 he similarly raises four held slots while pretending three. The inspected ten-minute proposition is a conditional tactical disclosure in an attempt to gain advantage, not a stopwatch demonstration or omniscient measurement.

The book's unqualified five-minute parameter and its later-state profile therefore require explicit qualification. Carryover of an older parameter is plausible; its editorial cause is not established. UAN itself confirms the four-slot detail. Do not silently replace its printed five with ten or count the two sources as independent timed trials. No standalone Monoma dossier exists in the current baseline; his current readiness and distributed evidence are the responsible model homes.

## Production, promotional art and other contributions

The appendix contains no newly narrated sequential bonus episode comparable to UA's THE・DEPTH. Its added interpretive material instead comes from the creator conversation, comments on promotional art, the glossary, credited guest illustrations and afterword. Classify each by its actual form; the absence of another bonus story neither nullifies editorial evidence nor gives it the force of a newly enacted scene.

Horikoshi explains that the joint-training setting was chosen so all characters could contribute, while Kubo comments on the demanding density of the pipe environment. This is a specific construction choice, not proof that all participants receive equal interior depth. They discuss several ways characters, situations and desired dialogue can be developed together; a rigid visual-first versus dialogue-first opposition would exaggerate their difference.

For Hawks, Horikoshi identifies a needed story role while saying the appearance was unsettled at the storyboard stage. The Japanese ネーム refers to a storyboard/draft, not an undecided character name. Role planning and visual design therefore have different histories. For Mirio, he recalls initially showing too little emotion and seeking the force of a possible protagonist through the Eri-protection sequence. Kubo's positive response is an attributed reader/creator judgment, not universal reception or a verdict that Mirio should have inherited One For All.

Asked about heroes and evil, Horikoshi says he is still searching and hopes to reach an answer by the ending (s0269). This permits an account of moral exploration during composition; it does not establish that he had no plans. The hands, influence and central-axis material in UA should be reconciled at the level of the specific design or argument, not turned into either total preplanning or total improvisation. The Sad Man's Parade naming discussion similarly supplies a memorable, child-repeatable sound aim without exhausting the finished attack's thematic effects.

The dated Twitter gallery repeatedly names contingent visual choices: spare space, wanted poses, shoes or fluttering hair, watching football and wanting to draw people eating. The Momo/Ochako illustration's cat is explicitly the creator's (s0274), not the students' pet. A soccer ensemble does not give everyone a football hobby; an abandoned-dog pun does not supply Bakugo's childhood biography; joking film-crew art is not a production record for a real in-world movie. Such images can still enrich the work's register of ordinary presence and affection when their representational status remains explicit.

The colour gallery has explicit magazine credits. Its ordinary bench composition with Midoriya and thin All Might (s0007) can enlarge the visual register of mentorship without supplying an independently narrated conversation. Its theatrical Toga/Shigaraki composition (s0012) develops visual motifs without creating a literal coronation or common motive. The SOLITARY Endeavor composition (s0016) can focus public burden while remaining selective: it does not remove Hawks and other assistance from the enacted fight.

The SCHOOL LIFE music cover (s0017) is particularly useful for the boundary. Its microphone-bearing Midoriya and guitar-bearing Ochako are promotional casting, distinct from their actual festival roles. Bakugo's drums match an enacted role, but the reprint still does not create a second independent performance. The earlier UA band promotion and these later illustrations support a history of visual representation without proving that every later plot or musical ability was already fixed.

The glossary makes institutional life more concrete: agency sidekick hiring, differentiated work experience and paid internships, internal hero-course transfer, regulated support manufacture, villain-disaster insurance, hero-work deductions, professional-license-only information access, and ranking criteria that include public support as well as resolved cases. These details strengthen existing institutional distinctions. They do not establish equal access, successful compensation for every victim, unlimited provisional authority or a permanent late-series state. The 36 U.A. general-entry hero seats must be distinguished from the class total that also includes recommended entrants.

HUC's performed casualties and Thirteen's training facility make the work of teaching rescue visible without claiming that all simulated injuries are real or that any institution is prepared for every disaster. Nor do these fictional simulations validate this repository's character reconstruction. The Pussycats' ordinary formation history and Ragdoll's post-loss information/office work add a different institutional scale: friendship, invitation and continued belonging. Their counterreading remains live—does the account value belonging apart from usefulness, or largely secure it through another useful contribution?
