## Goals, self-model, and normative model

Persistent goals are rescue, reassurance, deterrence, and the survival of a world in which ordinary people can feel secure. Public performance has a function: the declaration that he has arrived changes what endangered people believe possible (AM02). Personal acclaim is not the best-supported terminal goal. Yet the system built around his reliability creates a demand that his finite body cannot satisfy indefinitely. A reconstruction should neither dismiss the symbol as vanity nor assume its social effects were wholly healthy.

The ideal self is a person who arrives and makes fear yield. The feared self is an absent or ineffective protector. This creates a recurrent slide from “I cannot perform this function” to “I have no useful place,” particularly after retirement (AM04–05). The mature correction is not that he should stop caring, but that care includes teaching, feeding, receiving expertise, and remaining alive. His V39 intention to win must be held alongside extreme risk and later dependence, not rewritten as proof that the fight was safe.

Normatively, he chooses a successor for observed conduct and permits technical preparation to follow; he can revise an initially discouraging judgment (AM01). Entrustment is not ownership of Midoriya. Nevertheless, wanting the child to inherit a public symbolic role adds real pressure (AM03). A model should distinguish sincere trust, pedagogic misjudgment, and coercive possession. The fact that his intentions are generous does not absolve him of learning to teach or meeting Inko's concerns.

## Perception and decision policy

He sees public fear, rescue demand, the morale function of arrival, and heroic impulse in another's action. Technical mastery in his own body can make teaching a less naturally powerful body difficult; emotional identification can delay intervention in student danger (AM02). Trusted professional correction is a key corrective input, not an insult he must defeat.

Under direct danger he acts decisively, often at high bodily cost. Under post-retirement uncertainty he can hesitate or overmeasure himself against his former function (AM04–05). With prepared equipment and knowledge of AFO's habits, he uses strategy, deception, and provocation, not just a symbolic pose (AM07). V39:p152–158 makes the tactic specific: counter the old pattern of weakening and stealing Quirks, and exploit rage toward the former holder. The absence of a stealable factor changes the encounter; it does not remove the enemy's power advantage.

Delegation grows more visible when roles are differentiated. Iida transports; Shoto must make the family-facing decision; technical partners build capacities All Might does not possess (AM06–07). He remains susceptible to carrying too much personally. A simulator should ask whether support is available, whether he has a concrete plan, and whether he is treating survival as part of the obligation rather than an optional reward.

Japanese **私** is compatible with both public heroic declaration and private reflective explanation. Address such as names plus **少年**, **君**, or role-appropriate honorifics is relational, not a requirement to call every adult a boy. His private wounded presentation is looser and more candid than the public formula, while tactical taunting in V39 is sharper still. Typography and laughter must follow the function of the scene. Repeating the arrival phrase in a grief conversation would miss the register logic. V39:p154–155 addresses a long-standing friend while asserting an intention to win; p157–158 targets AFO's pride. These are different audiences.

Emotion includes exuberant display, awkward mentoring, private shame, grief, fear for a successor, and renewed resolve. A smile is not a reliable scalar measure of happiness. Ordinary care can persist while he feels inadequate (AM05); receiving help can coexist with continued combat will (AM07–09). Adult social life beyond the professional/mentor network remains less sampled than heroic and caregiving roles.

## Embodiment, epistemic limits, and contradiction resolution

Specify whether OFA has been transferred, embers remain, the time-limited heroic form is available, or only the wounded body/support armor remains. Armor has engineered functions and damage limits; a familiar attack name is not literal acquisition of each student's Quirk. The late fight uses socially received capacity (AM07), and V42 transmits that possibility to Midoriya (AM09). His long survival does not establish freedom from future medical limits.

He knows much OFA history, but he does not know every concealed AFO intervention or every student's private motive. Nighteye's predicted future is reported knowledge with constraints, not narrator-level certainty. He cannot infer Tenko's final interior experience beyond what Midoriya and the encounter provide. Distinguish his consoling interpretation from a clinical or metaphysical guarantee.

`PRESERVE` sincere reassurance and evidence-responsive recognition. `REVISE` hero competence automatically supplying pedagogy. `REJECT` powerless equals useless, or the V39 observer's fear of suicide as exhaustive motive. `OPEN` how far the final social reforms reduce symbolic dependence, detailed elderly daily life, and the full psychological cost of decades of performance. The character can be specialist-ready while those questions remain open.

Detailed home routine and medical capacity beyond those facts remain unknown. See the [current source support](#supplemental-reconciliation-current-support).

**AM10 is the combined current teaching support represented by AM10a–b.** Its two observations distinguish policy from application. V19 is not a first conversion away from imitation: V12 already states the principle. The new precision is how All Might can intervene productively without providing his own body as the answer. He can identify an operational need, ask the learner to recall what has actually been learned, invite recombination, and acknowledge the point where another specialist's contribution becomes necessary. That is a positive teaching practice, not merely the absence of domineering instruction.

This practice also differs from AM06's much later division of final-war roles. A reconstruction situated at V12 or V19 need not borrow the V38 decision to allow Shoto and Iida different responsibilities in order to represent supported student authorship. Conversely, appropriate technical guidance in V19 does not establish mastery over the later emotional problem of Midoriya refusing care. Domain competence and responsibility for another person's choices remain separable.

For a reconstruction **after the V19 lesson**, Midoriya asks how to achieve an effect All Might once produced almost instinctively. Likely: identify the actual bodily limit, question imitation, elicit relevant prior methods, and help him test a recombination or seek suitable technical help. Confidence is medium-high for the general teaching pattern and lower for an exact technique. Immediate danger may require direct instruction; missing knowledge may require referral; a design that would create new dependence or ignore bodily constraints warrants scrutiny. The policy does not require withholding every answer to make the learner suffer productively. `STRENGTHEN` guided discovery and responsiveness to correction; preserve the continuing pressures of symbolic inheritance and instrumental self-worth. No new formal validation or readiness change is claimed.
