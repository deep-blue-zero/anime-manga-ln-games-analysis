## Motives, self-model and accountability

The recurring priorities are family safety under threat, equality/belonging with peers, a meaningful heroic self, and expressive visibility. Their ordering changes with coercion and exposure. Flamboyance is not enough to infer vanity as the dominant motive: AY01 makes conspicuousness useful, AY02 makes it an awkward form of care, and AY06 preserves it during accountability.

The feared self is fundamentally different, fraudulent or permanently unworthy of relation. Shame can compress what he did, why he did it and what he might still do into one condemning identity (AY03–04). Peer and teacher intervention does not deny the attacks. It makes an actionable distinction between past conduct and remaining capacity to choose. His ideal self is an equal who can offer light/help without concealing a deadly channel.

Normatively, guilt does not purchase innocence, and reparative action does not entitle him to dictate everyone else's response. AY06 is especially discriminating: he is allowed to stay, so leaving is not simply expelled-person compliance. It is his chosen route toward atonement and future equality. Do not attribute Mirio's graduation phrase about negative-to-zero to him; his own conduct and words carry the repair model.

## Attention, decision policy and emotion

He attends to bodily mismatch, others' signs of stress, threats to parents, exposure, and opportunities to be needed (AY01–05). Early observation is imperfect: he helps Midoriya by analogy with himself and assumes a surprise that would please him will please another. That is a real epistemic/social limit, not proof the concern is fake.

Under active coercion, immediate family danger can drive concealment and compliance. Under disclosure and shame, self-exclusion becomes plausible. With concrete trust and an actionable reversal route, he can take risky reparative action. In the postwar choice, he can preserve a future hero aim while declining the easiest offered institutional continuation (AY03–06). This sequence is more useful than one timeless “coward” or “redeemed” label.

Emotional regulation uses theatrical presentation, surprise and brightness, but exposure can overwhelm that style. Sincerity does not require permanent removal of the decorative voice. AY05's persistence means shame need not stop every subsequent action; it does not prove shame cured. Stable equality desire and expressive style must be separated from acute panic, coercion and post-confession self-condemnation.

## Embodiment, knowledge and conditional reconstruction

The acquired Navel Laser does not fit his body easily; belt support and bodily distress are historical constraints (AY02–03). Later paired refraction expands what the beam can do without proving unlimited solo capacity. He knows the family threat and his own acts before the class does; classmates' earlier ignorance must be preserved. He cannot know all of AFO's intentions merely because he carries messages. Shinso's compelled speech is a specific sensor workaround, not evidence that Aoyama independently became an undetectable liar.

`PRESERVE` expressive care and equality desire; `REVISE` vanity-only or innocent-passivity accounts; `REJECT` permanent enemy essence and forgiveness-by-success; `OPEN` adult private breadth and detailed legal aftermath. Given postwar Aoyama an opportunity to contribute without assurance of praise, participation is medium-high confidence from AY05. A new credible family threat or acute shame could change the route; his final sidekick position cannot be assigned backward to his student self.

**Current supplemental qualification (AY-SUP-01–03):** The later guide establishes judicial process, a subsequently obtained license and Hagakure’s sidekick position. Adult professional belonging is therefore supported, while earlier Midoriya still lacked the concealed origin/coercion knowledge and wider adult private responses remain thin.
