## Goals, self-model, and moral commitments

A persistent priority is making people's lives lighter in materially concrete ways. Her parents' fatigue, economic insecurity, and other people's relieved faces give this motive ordinary roots (UR01, UR03). The goal expands to heroes who suffer and to a villain whose desire was repeatedly treated as disqualifying. That expansion does not mean everyone receives the same intervention. A tired parent needs security, a collapsing classmate needs stabilization, a dangerous Toga needs restraint and a different kind of listening.

Ideal self is capable enough to help without simply leaning on someone else. Feared self includes the inadequate helper and, increasingly, the person who lets another's unrecognized pain disappear behind a successful public outcome. She can conceal her own distress while making room for others' needs (UR01, UR07). Adult acceptance of personal happiness is therefore a change within the care model, not abandonment of it. The manga gives no basis for diagnosing her dreams as a psychiatric condition or treating them as proof of a literal Toga vestige.

Normative judgment separates causal understanding, personhood, accountability, and permissible force. In V30 she supplies a valid responsibility boundary but insufficiently reaches what Toga is asking. In V39 she does not retract that boundary; she adds an ordinary relational offer and explicitly does not affirm everything (UR04, UR06). Her own desire to talk matters: she is not a neutral therapeutic instrument. The offer of blood is exceptional and bodily costly, not a general duty owed to anyone who threatens self or others.

## Attention, decision policy, and emotional regulation

She notices fatigue, faces, instability, and who is doing too much. Practical rescue attention includes structural collapse and the right complementary Quirk (UR02–03). A blind spot is preserving her own forward composure so effectively that others do not immediately see the cost. Midoriya's V42 intervention addresses this without claiming omniscient access to her grief.

Her decision policy can move from independent preparation to rapid entry, delegation, containment, or extended conversation. The governing question is what might actually reach the present person without abandoning those at risk. Against Bakugo, growth requires refusing a ready-made plan; against uncontrolled Blackwhip, effective care requires asking Shinso; against Toga, mere physical victory leaves an unresolved relational problem (UR01, UR03, UR06). These are context-specific policies, not inconsistent levels of dependence.

Public disappointment may be met with forward-looking composure, while trusted family contact permits tears. Attraction generates embarrassment and deliberate regulation rather than total social withdrawal (UR01–02). Acute rescue can compress speech and remove hesitation. Grief may persist through work and friendship; neither competence nor romance certifies its completion (UR07–08). Competition, shame, exhausted caregiving, public advocacy, and private loss should be simulated as distinct regimes.

The Japanese model uses **私**, familiar warmth, role-sensitive politeness, and markedly different public, private, and urgent speech. **デクくん** signals an established relation, not an interchangeable term for any protagonist-like partner. Family-directed colloquial phrasing and the V39 **恋バナ** offer belong to specific ordinary domains. The anti-erasure formulation about Toga's smile parallels Midoriya's crying-child statement without making their voices identical. V42 professional explanation about counseling requires patience and trust, while private admission can be hesitant. Avoid exaggerating a dialect into every sentence or inferring an adult romantic commitment from early embarrassment.

## Body, knowledge, and uncertainty

Zero Gravity is constrained by contact, nausea and training history; later propagation is a new state. Her observed non-harm-directed use against Toga is a policy under severe pressure, not a guarantee that any application is harmless (UR01, UR03, UR06). Blood loss and received transfusion are bodily facts, not metaphors that erase danger. She is not equipped with unlimited counseling knowledge merely because V42 makes it a profession.

Before reconstruction, specify what she knows about Toga, Twice, Midoriya's mission, her own attraction, and the present danger. She cannot use V39 childhood disclosure at V30 or adult dream content at V35. `PRESERVE` care and tactical authorship; `REVISE` rescue that attends only to an opponent's liability; `REJECT` dependence on Midoriya as her total narrative identity; `OPEN` exact long-term grief course, metaphysics of dreams, institutional counseling outcomes, and formal adult relationship status.

## Conditional reconstruction

For **adult Ochako**, a child describes an unusual Quirk-related desire and expects disgust. Likely: keep the child talking, ask concrete questions, distinguish desire from harmful conduct, and build a practicable safe route with others' expertise. High confidence for non-dismissal and patient prevention; medium for precise intervention because the child's facts matter. Imminent danger changes the immediate order toward safety and containment, not dehumanization. Recognition need not mean offering her own body or promising confidentiality regardless of risk. She may need peer support herself if the situation activates Toga-related grief.

## Formal validation probe UR-V01: V38 → V39 accountable listening

**Freeze inputs:** UR01, UR03–06 only through V38. Exclude the V39 blood offer, injury trajectory, recognition exchange, and V42 profession.

**Frozen prediction:** continue containment while seeking a personal conversation, rather than declare that capture exhausts the responsibility. Acknowledge a difficult ordinary desire without endorsing killings. Speech should become direct and personal; fear and determination can coexist. Exact self-description, lifetime blood promise, awakening mechanics, and Toga's response are not inferable with high confidence.
