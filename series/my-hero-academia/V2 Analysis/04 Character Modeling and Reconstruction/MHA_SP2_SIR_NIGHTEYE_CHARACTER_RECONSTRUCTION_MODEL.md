## Motivations, self-model and norms

His recurring priorities are reliable professional judgment, preventing catastrophic loss, protecting the hero future embodied by All Might and Mirio, and building a society with energy and humor. The first presentation's severity does not exhaust the person: exact fandom and humor are persistent ordinary/workplace features (SN01–02). The feared self is a witness whose information cannot avert a loved person's death—or might help fix it.

His ideal is an exacting, useful evaluator. He distinguishes factual power transfer from his own judgment of a suitable successor, and admits a trainee without conceding the broader succession dispute (SN01). A reconstruction should not equate disagreement with personal hatred. Nor should affection for All Might make every evaluation objectively correct. A preferred successor and a painful forecast create motivational pressures on interpretation.

Normatively, he protects people from retrospective blame for unavailable information (SN03). The Foresight doctrine is conditional: investigate and raise success probability first, then use prediction to reinforce a practical plan. Knowing more is not automatically better when the knowledge could be unbearable and no intervention route exists. That position is contestable within the story; Fat Gum challenges it. Do not turn either speaker's view into the manga's sole endorsed epistemic rule.

## Attention, decisions and emotional regulation

He notices fine behavioral details, scenario fidelity, preparation, humor quality and the exact boundaries of observed futures. A failed literal task can reveal a valued discipline (SN01). He is capable of narrow tactical use, but longer prediction can activate dread shaped by past failed attempts (SN04). The key variable is whether information can guide an actionable change.

Decision policy: demand usable evidence; separate what was knowable from hindsight; reduce uncontrolled variables; use Foresight when it has a decision role; revise when reality directly defeats the model. SN05 shows actual revision, but not a complete replacement physics. His conjecture that many people's energy converged on Midoriya remains his explanation, not established causal law. The fact that a forecast differed is firmer than why it differed.

Regulation uses formal distance, analytical precision and humor as a serious value. This can coexist with acute fear, attachment and terminal tenderness. He need not disclose emotional reasons in every office exchange. Deathbed speech is an exceptional relationship/stress state; do not project it backward as the default evaluator voice. Conversely, the evaluator's severity cannot erase the explicit emotional dependence on All Might/Mirio.

## Body, epistemic limits and contradiction resolution

Foresight has activation/target conditions, a roughly daily interval and a target-centered limited view (SN03). It does not provide an omniscient map, unrestricted simultaneous futures or knowledge of everyone's motives. Physical tactics still depend on body, timing and environment. SN04's belief that observing might determine the outcome is a hypothesis, not a demonstrated law. The later actual exception defeats certainty about inevitability without establishing total control of fate.

`PRESERVE` evidence-sensitive evaluation and attachment; `REVISE` fatal certainty; `REJECT` anti-Midoriya caricature, all-seeing information and V42 living behavior; `OPEN` the mechanism of changed prediction, exact content of the final Mirio vision and what a surviving Nighteye would have revised next. Death prevents observing stable practice after this update.

**Current supplemental qualification (SN-SUP-01–03):** Particular fandom/humor practices and Mirio-directed pride now sharpen ordinary and relational context. The workplace punishment gag still lacks established consent; a tentative sacrificial motive and posthumous remembered image do not become new living behavior. See the [current source support](#supplemental-reconciliation-current-support).

Given V15 Nighteye asked to scan a colleague's death with no actionable plan, refusal or a demand for prior investigation is high confidence. If a narrowed lead can secure a child, use becomes more likely (SN03). A V18 terminal response to decisive contradiction permits explicit revision; those two snapshots cannot be blended into a timeless certainty or timeless openness.
