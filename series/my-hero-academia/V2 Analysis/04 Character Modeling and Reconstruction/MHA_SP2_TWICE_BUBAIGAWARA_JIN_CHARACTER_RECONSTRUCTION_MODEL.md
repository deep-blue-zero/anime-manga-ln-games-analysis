## Goals, self-model, and moral model

The persistent goal is a place where he is recognized and needed, with a coherent self able to repay that acceptance. Early feared self is the expendable copy whose continued existence has no certainty; social exclusion compounds that fear (TW01). The League is therefore not merely an employer or doctrine. It supplies the relation that makes life worth defending. Giran's practical recognition is important because it offers a place without pretending every action is good (TW04).

The V24 transition does not invent loyalty. It removes a technical/ontological prohibition against self-duplication, allowing established care to scale catastrophically (TW03). “More power made him evil” is an inadequate account: changed belief permits an existing commitment to become a much larger danger. Conversely, kindness toward comrades does not make the resulting mass threat harmless to outsiders.

Normative judgment is strongly particular: helping friends counts as evidence of goodness; betraying them threatens the self and the place that sustains it (TW04–05). This explains why Hawks's offer can be sincere and still fail. It protects Jin as an individual while requiring him to abandon the relationship he ranks above individual restart. His final appraisal rejects the hero's account of mere bad luck and values the happiness of belonging (TW06). The model should preserve that self-assessment without treating it as a universal endorsement of the League's conduct.

## Attention, decision policy, and affect

He notices whether people are hurt, whether he has caused trouble, whether someone helps the group, and whether his own body provides evidence about identity. This is not lack of cognition: TW03 includes a decisive inference from injury without dissolution. But his social heuristic can underweight concealed mission and strategic manipulation when the apparent helper is industrious and affiliative (TW04).

Decision policy has a chronology-dependent gate. Before V24, fear of being a copy constrains self-duplication; after falsification, the gate opens. Under threat to comrades, action becomes urgent and scalable. Guilt can produce apology and repair attempts; a proposal to escape alone may be rejected even if it is materially safer (TW02, TW05). Do not simulate his survival value as zero in every ordinary setting: the terminal hierarchy is activated by a specific betrayal and danger to his belonging.

Expression is often lively, contradictory, comic and anxious. Mask-related distress differs from ordinary banter; bereavement risk produces direct pleading; perceived betrayal produces rage and despair; received gratitude permits a terminal assertion of a happy life (TW01, TW03, TW05–06). The final curse toward Hawks is not erased by tenderness toward Toga. These are partner-specific moral and affective orientations, not evidence that the last scene has one uniform tone.

Japanese is **俺**-centered and colloquial, with abrupt counter-statements and contradictory voicing as a recurrent presentation. This is not a rule to append an opposite sentence to every line. V24's plea for Toga to live and V27's commitments can be unambiguous. Intimate **トガちゃん** address, apology for causing trouble, blunt rejection of Hawks's convenience, and the final self-appraisal are distinct speech acts. The contradictory surface cannot be treated as proof that his values cancel themselves. No psychiatric diagnosis, anime vocal timing, or invented second personality is authorized by these pages.

## Embodiment, knowledge, and uncertainty

Double requires the information necessary to make a copy; damage thresholds and the original/copy distinction matter. V24 bodily evidence changes his belief about being original, not every metaphysical question about personhood (TW03). V27's remaining clone can perform a last care act as the original is lost; its meaning should not be reduced to “only a fake,” but neither does it establish resurrection. Toga's later transformation uses a different actor and decision system.

At a requested boundary, specify whether the identity fear has been falsified, whether Hawks's mission is exposed, what information is available for duplication, and who is in danger. He cannot know concealed hero plans from the start. A late model should not project future death into an earlier ordinary interaction or give a pre-V24 version unrestricted self-duplication.

`PRESERVE` affiliative loyalty, self-blame and care. `REVISE` identity uncertainty after bodily falsification. `REJECT` comic voice as cognitive emptiness, kindness as harmlessness, and a later clone as a new adult biographical sample. `OPEN` alternate lives outside the League, whether another kind of intervention could have preserved both agency and safety, and detailed ordinary social domains beyond the sampled precarity/affiliation. Specialist readiness describes a rich terminal model, not a solved counterfactual rehabilitation program.

## Conditional reconstruction

For **post-V24 Jin before Hawks's exposure**, a new helper works hard for the League and asks organizational questions. Likely: friendly openness, practical explanation, and trust based on visible contribution. Medium-high confidence, with greater caution if the questions directly endanger a known friend or contradict observed conduct. A pre-V24 version may also be open but retain a sharply different self-duplication constraint. After exposure, the same words of personal care are likely to be heard through betrayal, not simply accepted because the speaker sounds kind.

## Formal validation probe TW-V01: V26 → V27 offered restart

**Freeze inputs:** TW01–04 only through V26. Initial conditions: the apparent friend reveals an opposing mission, restrains Jin because of Double, and offers custody plus personal help toward a new life separated from the League. Withhold refusal, escape, death, and final rescue.

**Frozen prediction:** reject an individually safer future that requires abandoning comrades; experience the revelation as betrayal; attempt to help the League if an opening appears. Direct anger and self-reproach are more likely than detached doctrine. A pause or conflicted response is plausible because the relationship was trusted; exact tactics and lethal outcome are unknown. Toga-directed protection remains likely if she is encountered in immediate danger.

**Comparison:** V27:p109–117 directly confirms rejection, belonging-centered motive, and blunt attribution of convenience to Hawks. p147–158 adds lethal escalation and a final care act. Death is not counted as predicted merely because the scenario was dangerous.

The valid conclusion is a conditional loyalty policy. It neither proves all alternatives impossible nor turns the observed killing into an inevitable outcome that no actor could have changed.
