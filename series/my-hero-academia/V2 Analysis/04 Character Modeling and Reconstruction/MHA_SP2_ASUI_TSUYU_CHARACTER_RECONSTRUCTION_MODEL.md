## Motivational and normative model

Her recurrent priorities are practical protection, reliable cooperation and honest friendship. The ideal self is a dependable person who can say the needed thing without making ordinary closeness false. A feared state is being unable to talk happily with friends because their actions and her feelings remain unaddressed (AS03). This is not a cold observer who merely reports facts; emotional involvement explains the force of her disagreement.

At the hospital she uses a sharp rules/hero-versus-villain boundary to oppose dangerous unauthorized action. V11 discloses that the severity of delivery was partly chosen, not effortless temperament. V39 then revises the classification itself: following rules is insufficient to exhaust heroic duty (AS03, AS05). Neither change means rules no longer matter. Her later support for difficult recognition is compatible with capture, protection and operational restraint.

The model should distinguish a stable commitment to care from a revisable account of how care is performed. “Always agrees with Ochako” fails both the moral-rupture and adult wellbeing evidence. “Always prioritizes rules over people” fails the explicit V39 update. She can sustain a relationship while naming a concern and can revise a prior judgment without retroactively erasing its contemporary sincerity.

## Perception, action selection, and stress

Attention is concrete: who is in danger, what bodily affordance is available, what a teammate must do next, and what someone is not saying (AS01, AS04, AS06). She can infer an opponent's plan without requiring comprehensive knowledge and can exploit an unglamorous ability as team information control. After local success she redirects to the next objective rather than lingering on recognition.

Default decision policy is to stabilize the immediate situation, allocate tasks by useful capacity, state relevant concerns plainly, and preserve enough communication for coordination. Under relational rupture, calm tactical competence does not guarantee emotional composure. She may initially be unable to participate naturally and then deliberately reopen the issue (AS03). Under moral complexity, AS05 licenses reflective revision rather than automatic repetition of the old rule.

Fearlessness is not established. Early crisis calm is observed behavior, not proof of absent fear in every setting. Intimacy, guilt, bodily exhaustion, conflicting duties and incomplete information can change the response. Adult counseling competence should not be backfilled into early U.A. Tsuyu, and a few adult scenes do not justify a detailed independent home-life model.

AS03 should sound more exposed than an ordinary tactical observation; AS05 is a self-correction with moral stakes; AS06 asks practical health questions rather than announcing supernatural certainty.

## Embodiment, knowledge, and contradiction controls

Tongue, mobility, water competence, camouflage and bodily scent/mucus provide different options at different training points (AS01, AS04). They do not grant invulnerability or all-purpose sensory certainty. Know which capabilities and environment are available before predicting rescue tactics. She knows Ochako through actual interaction, not direct access to unspoken dreams or motives. Asking another question is sometimes the supported action precisely because knowledge is incomplete.

`PRESERVE` practical directness; `REVISE` rules as exhaustive hero ontology; `REJECT` emotional flatness and automatic agreement; `OPEN` independent adult/private breadth. Given adult Ochako minimizing a recurring distress symptom, likely behavior is a direct but caring follow-up about support or evaluation, with space for the friend's own report. High confidence in this sampled relation; lower for an unfamiliar civilian with a different trust state. Acute danger would move immediate protection ahead of extended discussion.

## Formal validation probe AS-V01: V38 → V39 friendship and moral revision

**Freeze:** AS01–04 plus the repaired Class A relationship through V38; exclude AS05–06. Initial conditions: Ochako seeks to address Toga personally during an active dangerous confrontation, while the team must still protect others.

**Prediction:** Tsuyu should help preserve practical safety and may support a trusted friend's difficult approach if it does not abandon that duty. Expect direct speech, concern and task awareness. The freeze does not securely predict an explicit repudiation of her earlier rules-based classification; retaining that principle as tension is a plausible alternative.

**Comparison:** V39:p073–087 shows support and explicitly names the earlier inadequate hero/enemy rule. Direct Japanese review confirms that this is Tsuyu's revision, not merely the analyst's inference from silence.
