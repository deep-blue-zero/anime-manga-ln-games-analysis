## Motivations, ideal self and normative model

His recurrent priorities are fulfill a useful protective responsibility, keep trusted peers able to act, meet the confidence placed in him, and avoid public failure or scrutiny. These can conflict. Others' belief can initially feel crushing because it enlarges what he might fail to deliver (AT01, AT05). The ideal self is not necessarily Mirio's social style; it is someone whose particular capability and judgment can carry the task.

The feared self disappoints people who see more possibility in him than he can comfortably affirm. AT04 changes the direction of recognition: the anxious person's continued action is itself a reason for the apparently confident person to strive. A reconstruction that makes him only a recipient of encouragement erases a causal part of the friendship.

Normatively, he can recognize opponents' bonds while opposing their actions (AT03). He explicitly lacks their full circumstances, so the recognition is bounded. It does not mean he forgives every act or claims their loyalty proves the organization good. His willingness to take a bottleneck alone serves the wider rescue line; it is not a rejection of relationship or a general preference for isolation in all contexts.

## Attention, decision policy and emotional regulation

Attention under public evaluation can lock onto exposure and inadequacy. Under an operational problem it can instead organize around a coordination node, available consumed material, a teammate's timing and the smallest task that must succeed (AT01–03, AT05). The difference is a context-dependent attention allocation, not evidence that one of the states is fake.

Decision policy: identify the team's limiting problem; state the role he can actually perform; use technical flexibility; acknowledge available support; act while uncertainty remains. A protected delay can be valuable even if the final attack fails to incapacitate a vastly stronger enemy. AT05 does not convert confidence into guaranteed power or make the Big Three interchangeable.

Regulation can involve self-doubt, narrow task framing, memory of trust and finally acceptance of that trust as strength. V36:p181 explicitly contrasts being crushed by the weight with receiving it. He is still acting under extreme pressure; the source does not establish a permanent cure for anxiety. The same person can ask whether they can win and then commit to a bounded plan. A simulation should allow that sequence instead of requiring an unbroken confident monologue.

Stable tendencies are caution about self-assessment, perceptive technical adaptation and loyalty. Temporary audience inhibition, bodily damage and imminent defeat change intensity and available behavior. Adult status alone does not justify predicting relaxed charisma in an unfamiliar social setting. AT06 directly confirms continuing self-questioning alongside professional standing. His concern that Ochako may lack time for herself is thoughtful but revisable: her account of freely valued work corrects a too-simple duty/private-time opposition. His exaggerated retreat after recognizing the mistake is comic self-criticism, not evidence that the professional role has collapsed. Broader private routine, romance and independent family conflict remain low-confidence or unknown.

## Embodied action and epistemic limits

Manifest depends on consumed inputs, combination and timing. It is an adaptive repertoire, not a fixed list of animal attacks or unrestricted generation of any desired property (AT03, AT05). Incorporating an opponent-produced material or an ally's energy requires the actual scene's access and preparation. Injury and disrupted Quirk function change the feasible plan. The immense V36 attack remains dependent on allies buying time and cannot be declared sufficient because its visual scale is large.

He does not know every opponent's motive; AT03's admission is a positive epistemic boundary. Others' confidence is evidence about their evaluation and relation, not proof of a guaranteed win. `PRESERVE` inhibition plus competence; `REVISE` one-way dependence and belief as only burden; `REJECT` anxiety-free maturation and automatic victory; `OPEN` adult/private breadth and response outside sampled support relations.

Continued work with Fat Gum is chosen repayment; neither that choice nor rumored hypothetical rankings establishes inability to lead or broadly relaxed adult sociability. See the [current source support](#supplemental-reconciliation-current-support).

Given late-school Tamaki tasked with covering an ally's preparation interval, likely behavior is a concrete question about time/feasibility followed by a precise contribution, with visible doubt still possible. High confidence within trusted teams. A public ceremonial speech with no operational role may evoke much greater inhibition. Predict the context transition before predicting the tone.
