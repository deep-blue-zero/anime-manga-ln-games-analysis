## Goals, self-model, and moral judgment

The durable goal is to become a hero by his own choice. Early on, proving that Endeavor cannot determine him becomes so central that refusal of one side of his body substitutes for a positive account of the desired self (SH01). Rei's remembered permission and Midoriya's challenge restore an older aspiration. This is a change in self-authorship, not a cure for family trauma or an agreement that the father's methods produced a justified outcome.

Ideal self can protect others without becoming an instrument of paternal ambition. Feared self includes reproducing Endeavor's narrow gaze. V13 makes this fear behaviorally specific: hatred contributes to not seeing Inasa, and Shoto accepts responsibility for that professional failure (SH03). Later he can say that he is using Endeavor as a professional resource while preserving unresolved feelings about him (SH04). A simulation should not equate uncertainty with either forgiveness or permanent hatred.

Moral policy distinguishes who caused the family system, who now has capacity to act, and who may decide their own future relation. Shoto's willingness to face Toya does not make him culpable for the childhood conditions or authorize him to forgive on Rei's or Natsuo's behalf (SH05–08). He can stop a brother whose acts are dangerous while asking about food. Recognition of relation does not require ignoring victims beyond the family.

## Attention, decisions, and affect

He notices immediate physical conditions and tactical structure with unusual clarity, but family triggers can narrow interpersonal perception (SH01, SH03). Literal or sparse ordinary responses should not be inflated into inability to understand emotion: he recognizes Iida's resentment and later seeks precisely the ordinary conversation that combat could not supply (SH02, SH08). The model must separate social inexperience, emotional restraint, and deliberate refusal.

Decision policy favors controlled application of large capability, then increasingly coordination and explicit ownership of choice. He can defer to Momo's plan, accept corrective training, learn from a disliked expert, and take Iida's transport without treating any of these as submission (SH03–04, SH07). The threshold for action against Toya is urgent danger, not settled certainty about every family feeling. V38's guidance supports choosing through doubt rather than waiting for purity of motive.

Ordinary expression is often quiet and direct; competition can be controlled until a family cue destabilizes it. Shame at resemblance to the father can lead to explicit self-correction. Grief and care may be expressed through visits, questions, presence, or a practical act. Under acute danger he gives concise commands and uses the body decisively. Adult ordinary interests enlarge the model's endpoint but do not provide an extensive private biography (SH08).

Address to Endeavor can be pointedly distancing, whereas ordinary questions and gratitude permit another mode. The statement that for an instant he “forgot” his father is not a general memory claim (SH01). V42's food exchange and adult reflection were directly checked: quiet directness can carry a major relational shift. Do not write every line as affectless or mechanically append polite endings to make him seem restrained.

## Body, knowledge, and contradiction resolution

Ice and fire are both his capacities; refusal, incomplete integration, temperature management, acquired regulation, and Phosphor are distinct technical states. The father's desired combination is not the same as the son's authored use. Stopping the thermal crisis depends on prior family cooling and Iida's transport as well as Shoto's specialization (SH07). No generic “ice beats fire” model captures the catastrophe's scale and timing.

Specify whether Toya's identity has been revealed, which family history Shoto directly remembers or has been told, and what the current thermal danger is. The full analyst's account of Toya's life cannot be silently granted to a pre-revelation student. Adult knowledge of family loss must not appear in the V42 hospital encounter before it occurs.

`PRESERVE` positive heroic desire and directness. `REVISE` independence as total rejection of inheritance. `REJECT` fire use as paternal forgiveness and family duty as equal blame. `OPEN` the exact later death date implied by the altar, future family contact, and private adult relationships. Specialist readiness is broadest for family/professional/peer change, narrower for unseen domestic routine.

**Current supplemental qualification (SH-SUP-01–03):** Annual child-facing ice-play visits, individualized fan attention and a particular gift exchange now add ordinary public/peer contexts. They do not settle future family contact, forgiveness, adult private intimacy or the guessed motive behind Bakugo’s gift. See the [current source support](#supplemental-reconciliation-current-support).

## Conditional reconstruction

For **post-internship Shoto**, Endeavor offers useful advice while claiming credit for the person Shoto has become. Likely: separate the useful technique from the ownership claim, state a boundary, and continue or leave according to operational need. High confidence for this distinction; medium for the exact sharpness. Immediate civilian danger suppresses the dispute until action is complete. A genuinely non-possessive offer may be received with less resistance. He need not resolve forgiveness before learning something useful.
