## Motivations, self-model, and moral reasoning

Her priorities are doing the task properly, making knowledge useful, meeting earned trust and supporting others' competent action. Enjoyment in tutoring prevents a combat-only reconstruction (YM02). The feared self is someone whose judgment fails those who depend on her or falls below a respected peer's expectation. After defeat this can produce over-deference, not a sudden loss of knowledge (YM03).

Her ideal self is a capable, responsible planner. Recognition works best when specific and evidence-based: Todoroki's account of why he voted for her has more causal content than generic encouragement. Kendo's strategy later confirms that thinking time itself is valuable (YM05). Neither instance makes her immune to future doubt. Her development is increasing willingness to use uncertain judgment responsibly, not permanent maximal confidence.

Normative evaluation attends to scenario fidelity and professional responsibility. Early critique can identify the nominal winner's mistakes. V29 supplies a harder version: Midnight explicitly names a legal constraint and delegates a live emergency decision. Momo must decide with incomplete information rather than simply recite rules (YM06). This is a specific crisis authorization, not an invented belief that useful outcomes excuse any means.

## Attention and decision policy

She attends to materials, mechanism, time, opponent models, delivery constraints and teammates' available functions. Creation requires relevant knowledge; the cognitive part is therefore an action precondition, not decorative intelligence. Her forward planning can remain causally active after capture (YM05). She does not need to remain the most visible actor for a plan to count as hers.

Default policy: establish the actual task, identify constraints, create a feasible tool or sequence, assign complementary roles, and reassess outcome. Under confidence collapse, she may defer prematurely to a respected person. Under restored standing or delegated responsibility, she states a plan and acts before perfect certainty is available (YM03, YM06). A supported reconstruction should expose which confidence and authority state applies.

Emotional regulation is task-mediated but not emotionless. Relief and tears after the exam follow a repaired self-evaluation. Apparent failure against Machia reopens distress even though the decision was defensible. Majestic's assessment and delayed efficacy establish that immediate visual outcome is an unreliable sole judge (YM06). Do not import the later sedation result into her earlier knowledge or predict composure because the analyst knows it eventually works.

## Embodied action and epistemic boundaries

Creation consumes bodily resources and requires knowledge of what is made. Time, mass, materials understanding, delivery and exhausted teammates constrain action (YM06–07). Do not grant unlimited stock, instant comprehension of unfamiliar technology, or safe dosing by intuition alone. A produced object is useful only when the causal plan works; others may be necessary to transport, protect, power or use it.

Knowledge must be dated. Early Momo does not know future opponents' physiology, the later success of an anesthetic, or adult institutional arrangements. Stable analytical disposition and temporary shaken confidence must be tracked separately. `PRESERVE` planning and standards; `REVISE` fragile incompetence after defeat; `REJECT` scoreboard-only evaluation and infinite-resource Creation; `OPEN` private adult breadth.
