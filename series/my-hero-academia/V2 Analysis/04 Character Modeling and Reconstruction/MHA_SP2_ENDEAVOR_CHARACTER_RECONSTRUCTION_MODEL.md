## Goals, self-model, and normative change

Early motivational hierarchy makes surpassing All Might the organizing measure of worth. When his own body cannot close that comparison, children become a means of solving it (EN01). This is not simply demanding coaching: he misreads the son's independent act as acceptance of his ambition. The feared self is the permanently inferior competitor. The professional ideal and the family project become destructively entangled.

Later the question shifts from winning that comparison to fulfilling responsibilities after the comparison has failed. Technical work, public trust, protection of younger people's futures, and restitution become distinct goals (EN02–07). A model must not announce a wholly new innocent person. V36 explicitly rejects that reset. The stable tendency is persistence; its earlier application sustains abuse and obsession, while later application may sustain obligations whose benefits he will not personally receive.

Normative change is strongest where another's refusal is allowed to remain effective. Natsuo need not forgive, introduce a spouse, or maintain contact for Endeavor to owe support and apology (EN07). Shoto can take instruction without accepting paternal possession (EN03). Rei and the children can intervene without becoming equally responsible for the original harm (EN04, EN06). An atonement model that compels reunion in return for remorse repeats the earlier ownership error.

## Attention, decision policy, and emotional regulation

He notices technical deficits, output, efficiency, comparative strength, and what a trainee must repeat until usable. This can make his instruction effective (EN03). Early on, the same comparative lens screens out the child's subjectivity; late emotional confrontation can instead overwhelm practical judgment (EN01, EN04). Neither technical intelligence nor later remorse grants complete self-knowledge.

In ordinary professional command, he is direct and demanding. In battle he can use others' openings and accept a specific role; in family-triggered crisis he may freeze, overidentify guilt with solitary action, or try to remove himself and the problem together (EN04–06). Colleagues and family are not merely emotional support: their different perspectives and capacities change what is possible. A reconstruction must distinguish well-prepared AFO combat from a Toya encounter at the moment of revelation.

Anger, frustration and pressure dominate early family scenes. Later shame, fear, grief, and helplessness can break the authoritative posture. The corrective movement is not endless self-condemnation; it is staying present to consequences and other people's words (EN06–07). His V39 request for more of Toya's hatred is especially important because it does not demand gratitude for attempted sacrifice. A simulation should allow the ugly feeling to remain while asking what responsible act follows.

Japanese **俺**, blunt declaratives, compressed commands and comparative vocabulary fit much professional and early family speech. Register changes when he acknowledges responsibility or listens. V39:p060's request to hear more and V42's belated conversation/restitution are not the same voice act as V05's proclamation of his ambition. The naming of individual family members matters because it resists an undifferentiated “forgive me, family” script. Do not replace moral change with a generally softer vocabulary or simulate every late line as an apology.

## Embodiment, knowledge, and unresolved boundaries

Fire output, overheating, injury, missing limb, available support, and later retirement are specific capability states. Endeavor's heat cannot substitute for Shoto/family cooling in the detonation crisis (EN06). Pain and expenditure may show commitment but do not themselves purify conduct. Professional retirement in V42 changes what he can do, not whether he remains responsible.

Specify whether he believes Toya dead, knows Dabi's identity, understands the current thermal risk, and knows the family's wishes. His later understanding of childhood failure cannot be retrojected as full contemporary awareness during the original harm. Conversely, lack of complete understanding is not lack of responsibility for choices he did make. `PRESERVE` expertise and persistence; `REVISE` their moral direction; `REJECT` rebirth, equal family blame, and entitlement through suffering; `OPEN` future contact, adequacy of restitution, and complete social accountability beyond the shown family scenes.

## Conditional reconstruction

For **V42 Endeavor**, an estranged child refuses a meeting but communicates a practical need. Likely: meet the obligation without making access or appreciation a condition. High confidence from EN07; medium for the exact administrative arrangement. A request that endangers someone else requires a separate safety judgment, not automatic compliance as penance. A pre-reform version is much more likely to subordinate the interaction to his own project. The chronology difference must be explicit.

## Formal validation probe EN-V01: V38 → V39 family intervention

**Freeze inputs:** EN01–05 and the V38 thermal predicament. Initial conditions: Toya is approaching detonation, Endeavor is present but his power cannot safely cool him, and catastrophic civilian harm is possible. Exclude Rei's arrival, the family cooling, and the later listening exchange.

**Frozen prediction:** accept grave personal cost and try to contain consequences; solitary burden-taking is a strong risk given guilt. He should not expect forgiveness as payment. If family members arrive with indispensable help, accepting it is plausible but not guaranteed by the frozen evidence. Predict terse urgency followed by shame/grief; exact expressions of listening remain open.

**Comparison:** V39:p015–020 supports the initial self-burdening route. p031 and the cooling sequence introduce family agency that changes it. p060's differentiated apologies and invitation to hear hatred strengthen a receptive component not adequately captured by a purely solitary atonement model.
