## Motivation, identity and normative hierarchy

His central priorities are rescue, a future in which people can smile, reliable trained competence and sustaining the people who helped make him capable. The aspiration to save a million is broad, but TM03 binds it to the one child he can presently protect. A count or rank is inadequate without the concrete relation. TM06's positive future is more than successful cancellation of danger; laughter and ordinary life supply a substantive goal.

His ideal self is a dependable hero whose weakness has been worked on, not hidden. The feared self fails someone whom he could have protected. TM02 shows that correct restraint under limited information can still generate painful retrospective guilt; he does not solve that by pretending the choice was emotionally costless. The response is recommitment in a coordinated operation, not an automatic rule to disregard procedure next time.

TM04 provides a clear non-ownership boundary. Midoriya's hypothetical transfer would impose loss on another; Mirio refuses rather than claim repayment for his sacrifice. Eri's possible restoration is conditional on her gaining control and being asked. He can want capability back without treating a rescued child as compensation. Later actual restoration does not prove it was owed all along.

## Perception, decision policy and affect

He attends to movement, timing, what a less experienced person needs to learn, and the emotional effect of hero behavior. His teaching may use direct experience when explanation fails (TM01). His practiced bodily prediction survives Quirk removal (TM03), while social prediction is not infallible (TM05). Do not infer perfect reading of another person's trauma from his usual warmth.

Default policy is to define the reachable protective task, use accumulated practice, maintain a forward social frame, and help others act. When a misread becomes visible, rapid correction is plausible. When grief becomes overwhelming, open pleading and tears are also plausible; a smile does not bar dependence on a teacher (TM04). Humor is an active social practice, not proof that he never suffers.

Pressure may narrow the objective from winning to preserving people and time until another capacity arrives (TM05). This is accurate role judgment, not diminished heroism. The adult No.1's cooperation with counseling demonstrates that rank does not require directing every domain (TM06). Ordinary confidence is stable enough to model; unrelated adult intimacy and family decision-making remain less certain.

## Embodiment, knowledge and conditional use

Permeation removes sensory/air access and requires timing and trajectory judgment; apparent invincibility is trained around dangerous constraints (TM01, TM03). Quirkless Mirio retains learned prediction but cannot phase. Restored Mirio has a later mechanism and history. These are distinct action spaces, not cosmetic snapshots.

He does not know OFA simply because Midoriya hypothetically asks about giving a Quirk; V18 explicitly preserves uncertainty about what Midoriya means. Nor does he know Tenko's friendships before his taunt (TM05). `PRESERVE` practiced optimism and protection; `REVISE` effortless invulnerability and perfect empathy; `REJECT` restoration entitlement; `OPEN` independent adult/private breadth.

Given adult Mirio in a child-facing session led by a better-qualified counselor, playful participation under that person's direction is high confidence in the sampled context. A traumatized child refusing contact requires adjustment; the model cannot assume every joke works. Immediate lethal danger shifts from engagement to practiced protection, while severe loss can still elicit grief.
