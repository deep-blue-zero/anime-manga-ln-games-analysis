## Motivations, self-model and norms

The hierarchy develops from escaping suffocation and finding a meaningful ideal toward preserving a particular friend and group, then ensuring that the dead are not erased. Stain supplies an initial interpretive opening; Shigaraki becomes more than a vehicle for that borrowed ideology (SP01–04). Gaming memory matters because it grounds mundane companionship, not merely worship of a destructive leader.

His feared self is someone who cannot become anybody, whose weakness and social exclusion make him irrelevant. His explicit admission that he rides with others is not the analyst's insult (SP02). The ideal need not be an autonomous great man; he can want meaningful participation and a person worth following. That makes him vulnerable to a controller who offers symbolic importance through service to the friend. It does not make the controller's assigned political narrative his complete inner motive.

His norms are selective and can authorize grave harm. Early ideological challenge shows judgment, but later friendship does not morally validate everything done for the League. V42's memorial project is adversarial and includes a terror-symbol intention (SP05). Writing instead of immediate attack in that scene is not evidence of a settled nonviolent philosophy. He can criticize his own failure to help Tomura while still refusing the heroes' preferred account.

## Attention, decisions and emotion

Attention centers exclusion, direction, Shigaraki's identity and traces of meaningful relation. SP03 demonstrates a high-value distinction: the same body can house someone he does not follow. Under augmentation, expressive and cognitive bandwidth collapses; the remaining intention becomes narrower, not necessarily more ideologically pure (SP04). Later medical intervention arrests deterioration, but does not retroactively make the cost negligible.

Default regulated policy is to seek purpose in affiliation, question an empty direction, act for a particular friend, and resist erasure of that relation. Under overwhelming bodily/cognitive stress, expect perseveration and reduced complex planning. Under bereavement, bodily expansion, anger, tears and self-questioning can coexist before a writing intention forms (SP05). A smooth rehabilitated spokesman would misrepresent the observed transition.

He does not simply want admiration from every audience. V42 authorial intent remains confrontational toward the heroes, while a brusque message to Shoji permits a more differentiated relation than total category hatred. Those are local behaviors, not proof of a complete new ideology. Stable belonging need and temporary physiological impairment require separate state variables.

## Body, knowledge and countermodels

His original Gecko capacity, added enhancements, cognitive deterioration and subsequent medical arrest have different consequences. More capacity is not automatically more agency. He cannot know every engineered detail of Tenko's origin or everything in the terminal inner battle; V42 information arrives through Midoriya. Treat the message as received testimony, not direct participation in the final encounter.

`PRESERVE` conscious affiliation and person-specific loyalty; `REVISE` passive follower or homogeneous movement spokesman; `REJECT` augmentation as costless empowerment and writing as proven pacifism; `OPEN` future readership, legal/reform trajectory, health course and a life beyond League-centered relations.

Given postwar Spinner asked to endorse a memorial calling Tomura only a monster, likely response is refusal and a demand that friendship and lived personhood remain recorded. High confidence in this relation. A memorial can be hostile to heroes without every factual claim being false; the model must distinguish testimony, interpretation and threatened symbolic use. Willingness to reconcile with a particular stranger remains low confidence.
