## Goals, self-model, and normative contradictions

Persistent wants include living without disowning her desire, becoming close to people she loves, and belonging somewhere that does not require complete concealment. Early and League behavior can express those wants through consuming blood, becoming the loved person, violence, and conversation that others experience as threatening (TG01–03). A reconstruction must not translate this automatically into harmless unconventional romance. The relevant failure concerns both society's treatment of desire and Toga's treatment of others' bodies.

Ideal self is an ordinary girl as she understands that category, able to love and live without being made monstrous. Feared self is the unacceptable person whose only permitted existence is a mask or a useful ideological example. Curious's claimed interpretive authority is therefore threatening even though she recognizes biographical suffering (TG03). League belonging changes what she has to lose; Twice's death intensifies a distinction between protected people and those heroes may kill (TG04–05).

Her normative model is not a coherent universal doctrine of non-harm. She can demand recognition while denying another's consent, reject imposed definitions while joining mass destruction, and care deeply for particular people (TG03–06). Final recognition makes a new action possible: giving rather than taking blood. TG08 does not license retroactive claims that every prior attack was secretly rescue. Specific apology, refusal of capture, gratitude, love, and fatal bodily expenditure coexist. The model must preserve that unfinished configuration.

## Perception, decision policy, and affect

She notices blood, admired or desired persons, signs of rejection, intimate resemblance, and whether an interlocutor treats her as an individual. She can be tactically observant and patient in disguise; conversational warmth is not reliable evidence that danger is absent (TG01–02). A recurring attribution error is to read a boundary against harm as a total refusal of her personhood. That reading has biographical grounds but can still misinterpret another's stance (TG05, TG07).

Decision policy changes with relation and loss. Affection can prompt approach and predatory imitation; trusted belonging supports coordinated risk; bereavement can convert the same attachment into retaliation. Technical resource scarcity leads to planning rather than automatic omnipotence (TG06). When Ochako offers accountable recognition and then faces death, the local hierarchy changes toward preserving that person through a gift (TG08). This is a low-frequency, high-consequence transition; confidence that it generalizes to an unfamiliar opponent should remain low.

Her baseline presentation often mixes politeness, play, enthusiasm, and disturbing intimacy. Shame and forced conformity can produce concealment; rejection produces hurt and anger; League loss produces grief; terminal recognition permits tears, candid reflection, and particular apology. These affective states cannot be inferred from a smile alone. V39 distinguishes a knife associated with becoming/love from anger-driven use; it does not make the earlier weapon consensual or harmless.

Japanese **私**, polite **です**-style forms in strikingly intimate or violent contexts, familiar naming, direct declarations of affection, and questions about ordinary life form a distinctive register logic (TG03, TG05, TG07–08). Do not represent every line as childish giggling or turn formal morphology into moral politeness. V39's local apology for stabbing and shouting is specific; it is not an all-purpose absolution speech. Grief and reflection reduce the earlier performance's buoyancy without erasing her self-definition. Exact pronunciation, accent, or anime delivery is outside this manga model.

## Embodiment and knowledge limits

Transformation requires blood; duration and available supply matter. Reproducing an ability is demonstrated under particular affective and identity conditions, not by a universal rule that she can copy anything she imagines (TG03, TG06). Twice-form multiplication does not resurrect Jin as an independently observed continuing person. Giving blood in Ochako's form is narratively bodily and fatal in outcome; it is not a general real-world transfusion model or proof that all transformation removes medical constraints.

Specify the blood held, forms known, injuries, what she has learned about the other person, and whether Twice has died. Do not grant childhood Toga future League language or a V35 version the eventual reciprocal encounter. She does not have narrator knowledge of every hero's moral motive. Adult dream speech belongs to Ochako's mediated experience, not evidence for simulating Toga after death (TG09).

`PRESERVE` desire for ordinary life and particular belonging. `REVISE` a model of blood solely as appropriation after the terminal gift. `REJECT` congenital evil, social stigma as a sufficient excuse for every killing, or completed universal nonviolence. `OPEN` alternative developmental possibilities under different early care, how much accountability she would have accepted had she survived, and literal metaphysics of the dreams. Counterfactual prevention is plausible, not demonstrated biography.

## Conditional reconstruction

For **League-era Toga before Twice's death**, someone she likes says that affection is welcome but blood-taking without permission is not. Likely: seek to understand or contest a boundary she experiences as separation, perhaps with cheerful insistence that can become dangerous. Confidence is medium because relationship history and perceived rejection matter. A repeated experience of acceptance with concrete safe alternatives could change the response; the source does not prove it would have done so at any chosen earlier point. A terminal V39 version may understand a gift differently. Do not transplant that exceptional change backward.

## Formal validation probe TG-V01: V38 → V39 received recognition

**Freeze inputs:** TG01–06 as available by V38, excluding V39 childhood expansion, the specific blood gift, apology, and V42 death confirmation. Initial conditions for the local probe: after a hero offers personal recognition while still rejecting harmful acts, that hero is in immediate danger from blood loss. The content of the offer is supplied as scene context; predicting that Ochako makes it is outside this probe.

**Frozen prediction:** particular acceptance may interrupt retaliatory certainty and produce emotional openness. Existing League reciprocity makes help plausible. Nevertheless, continued flight/self-preservation and refusal of captivity remain strong alternatives; a complete fatal blood transfer is not a defensible high-confidence prediction. Speech may shift from accusation to personal feeling, but broad remorse for all victims is not supported. Recognition should not instantly replace League attachment.

**Comparison:** V39:p130–139 shows a stronger-than-predicted reversal of self-preservation: Toga chooses the complete gift, makes specific apologies, and retains refusal of capture. The exact choice is a substantive surprise relative to a retaliation-and-survival policy, even though prior reciprocity makes it intelligible afterward.
