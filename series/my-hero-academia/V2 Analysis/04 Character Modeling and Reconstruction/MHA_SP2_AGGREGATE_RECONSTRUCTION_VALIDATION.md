# MHA SP2 — Aggregate reconstruction validation

## Responsibility and evidence design

This report adjudicates the substance of all **30 formal probes**, not merely the presence of a heading. It reads each declared earlier freeze, later scene conditions, prediction, comparison, five-axis judgment and stated limitation against the dossier's selected evidence. Detailed transactions remain in their linked models. The [readiness index](MHA_SP2_CHARACTER_MODEL_READINESS_INDEX.md) owns tiers; the [corpus index](MHA_SP2_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_CORPUS_INDEX.md) owns routing.

All are **retrospective chronological evidence holdouts**. The analyst knew later manga; selected earlier evidence was frozen for a comparison, not under blind experimental conditions. Later scene initial conditions are supplied inputs, not predictions. Success on a broad conditional proposition is not exact foresight. There is no accuracy percentage, independent experimental sample or claim that every future domain is validated. Source observations are textual facts; model adequacy and axis judgments are interpretations constrained by those facts.

The inspected dossier/probe set is the published `b5ab760b6ed64fd203ae0dc5b16f64760c9b4d7b` tree. Earlier frozen sources retain their declared original local lock `2a5a604cd592bdce0d6d77cca34ac04122a5484b`; accessible published equivalent `7089f586ce3114e54a2c4954018f809b7c2cab09` has the identical tree and all 182 cited frozen references were checked. The [source audit](../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/MHA_SP2_PUBLICATION_AND_SOURCE_REVERIFICATION_AUDIT.md) preserves that equivalence and distinguishes inherited direct review from this continuation's actual inspections. No new frozen model is invented by this report.

## Five-axis inventory

Order is **action / motive / speech-register / emotional trajectory / relationship handling**. `S` means supported within the actual tested claim; `P` partial; `U` underdetermined; `M` a substantive miss. Qualifiers restrict support: `(c)` conditional, `(n)` narrow, `(scene)` local scene only. “Developing” means observed motivation is intelligible but its new form must not be smuggled into the earlier freeze. These are interpretive summaries of unequal tests and must not be summed into an accuracy statistic.

## Cross-corpus findings and validity limits

The useful common result is conditional coherence, especially when stable voice accompanies changed judgment. Bakugo's bluntness does not preserve his earlier ethics unchanged; Tsuyu can revise a classification in the same direct register; Tamaki can accept belief without becoming socially uninhibited. Models therefore require both a temporal state and a relationship-specific situation. Broad stable labels—kind, angry, anxious, loyal—do not specify behavior adequately.

The most informative non-confirmations remain visible: Toya's Shoto-directed apology breaks an invariant-hostility rule; Toga's fatal gift exceeds predicted magnitude; Aoyama's two plausible routes leave departure underdetermined; Spinner's altered body changes execution; Tamaki's new use of belief is development; Eri's horn route and Nagant's bodily cost are not inferred from generic gratitude. Hawks's corrected exercise remains contaminated. These are reasons to narrow or update models, not lower-confidence “hits” to hide in an aggregate success number.

Scene initial conditions account for substantial apparent foresight. Nighteye is given a surviving Midoriya; Chisaki is given a living boss and access; Shoji is given an award for work already done; Shinso is given continued post-control action; All Might is already confronting AFO with equipment; several final-volume tests are given survival and an available interlocutor. None may be reported as predicting those circumstances. Likewise observed outcome cannot reveal every private emotional trajectory. Under this report's five-axis discipline, a short public speech often supports voice and relationship but leaves affect underdetermined.

Current correction propagation excludes V37:p148 art, V36:p180 promotion, V38:p062–064 paratext and V42:p144–145 afterword from behavioral evidence; clarifies V31 raw +1 and V36 facing-page mapping; keeps V39 observer/actor intent distinct; and places Hawks extraction in V28.
