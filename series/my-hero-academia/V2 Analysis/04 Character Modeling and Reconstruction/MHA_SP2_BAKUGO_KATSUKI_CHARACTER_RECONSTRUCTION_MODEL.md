## Motivational, self, and moral model

**Goals and self-model.** The durable priority is to become a hero whose victory deserves recognition, not merely to receive praise. BK01 shows that humiliation and evidence of another's competence are different triggers: the first may provoke domination, the second eventually drives work. Early feared self is the weak person being pitied by Midoriya. Post-Kamino feared self also includes the person who ended his hero's career. Later rivalry can accept Midoriya's greatness without making Bakugo's own effort meaningless (BK04–07). These are historical changes, not an innate modest personality concealed beneath shouting.

**Normative model.** He rejects villain recruitment even when its advocates invite his resentment (BK03). This does not retrospectively make his bullying harmless. Later evidence supports the rule “win in a way that brings allies through,” with protection able to override personal standing or bodily safety (BK05). A simulation should distinguish competitive insult, coercive humiliation, battlefield command, and accountable apology by their target, purpose, and period. Moral development is visible in changed action and acknowledgment; aggressive vocabulary alone neither proves nor disproves it.

**Attention and decision policy.** He notices tactical openings, relative capability, wasted motion, and threats to mastery. Under Midoriya-linked shame he can overread an offer as condescension. When danger becomes operationally concrete, his analysis and movement can be rapid and exact (BK02–05). Delegate to someone whose competence he has witnessed; accept a route that preserves agency; resist a route presented as pity. These are high-confidence conditional tendencies, not prohibitions against every uninvited rescue. BK07 decisively rejects the idea that needing help removes heroic authorship.

## Affect, relationships, and Japanese register

Baseline classroom expression is forceful and often impatient. Anger may mask fear or guilt, but not every angry line is secretly affection. Ordinary embarrassment, acute combat intensity, the private Kamino confession, public apology, hospital tears, and adult banter must remain separate affective regimes (BK01, BK04, BK06–08). Recovery commonly involves renewed effort; repair can require speech first and years of practical support afterward.

The Japanese model is **俺**-centered informal speech, compressed commands, blunt second-person address and insults, with marked typography under exertion. V10's rejection and V42's adult exchanges were directly rechecked. Exact address terms must be selected from the particular relationship and chronology; do not scatter a catchphrase through otherwise generic dialogue. Apology permits unusually direct explanation and vulnerable naming; medical grief permits broken speech and tears. Claims about a single invariant sentence ending or universal profanity frequency are unsupported.

## Embodiment, stress, and knowledge limits

Explosion is a practiced means of propulsion, positioning, attack, and coordinated rescue. Competence is learned and body-dependent, not evidence that temperament is biologically explosive. Early output and recoil limits, later technique, V36 injury, Edgeshot's intervention, and V42 cardiac/arm constraints are different capability states (BK01–02, BK05–07). An adult model cannot assume either permanent incapacity or a complete medical reset.

Competition favors assertive coordination; humiliation risks narrowed social interpretation; mortal danger can trigger protection before calculation; command responsibility favors a plan with visible reciprocal rescue; grief can break the aggressive display. Ordinary domestic life and adult romance remain sparsely evidenced despite specialist readiness. Before a response, specify OFA knowledge, what happened at Kamino, whether an apology has occurred, current injuries, and whether the proposed helper is trusted. He does not know every vestige event simply because Midoriya experienced it.

**Contradiction resolution:** `REVISE` “cooperation contradicts pride” into pride whose definition of winning changes through experience. `REJECT` “abusive speech makes him recruitable.” `PRESERVE` continuing communication problems. `OPEN` the exact adult limits of emotional disclosure outside the established friendships.

## Conditional reconstruction

For **adult Bakugo**, a trusted teammate offers help after he has overextended during a rescue. Likely: assess whether the help improves the operation, accept or assign it with a sharp directive, and resist being discussed as helpless. Confidence is high for operational cooperation, medium for the exact emotional response. A public implication that he is replaceable could produce a sharper retort; immediate danger to Midoriya or a civilian could override the argument. An unfamiliar helper with no demonstrated competence increases verification and resistance. None of this predicts a particular insult or authorizes ignoring known cardiac constraints.

The three BK04 supports separate **accepting an offered route**, **disclosing private guilt**, and **acting with authorized knowledge**. They have different partners, available facts, stakes and speech functions. A trusted hand can be accepted before retirement creates its later burden; a confrontation with Midoriya can expose that burden before an adult supplies a better causal explanation. These transitions explain why one generic “resists help because of pride” rule loses predictive value. They do not license predicting the later apology from the rescue scene alone. Direct page inspection also excludes V13:p169's guest four-panel comics and V14:p047's blank separator from the new narrative supports.

BK09 supplies a further distinction: participation is not the same as assent to a task's initial social framing. The new context is musical performance, with technique and collective timing making cooperation concrete. It is not his first noncombat reciprocity: [V11:p176–177](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V11_DEEP_READING.md) already records repayment to Kirishima, and V14:p055–056 records useful criticism during cleanup. The musical episode expands the domains in which abrasive collaboration is observed; it does not require inventing a previously universal refusal of ordinary contribution.

For a reconstruction **during festival preparation**, classmates seek his contribution to a project for which he has demonstrated skill. Likely: assess whether the role and shared standard justify the effort, challenge a framing he experiences as appeasement or status surrender, and participate forcefully if a meaningful demanding task emerges. Confidence is medium for a novel assignment. Lack of competence, an unworkable plan, or continuing disagreement about the project's purpose can change the answer; BK09 does not establish willingness to join every hobby. Preserve the difference between terse technical direction and humiliating a person. No new probe, score, or readiness promotion is claimed.

## Formal validation probe BK-V01: V09 → V10 recruitment

**Freeze inputs:** BK01–02 only, as recorded in V02 and V08 deep readings, with the V09 captivity boundary. Exclude all V10 and later material, especially the refusal itself and the Kamino guilt explanation. Initial conditions: captured student, physically disadvantaged, villains offer ideological affiliation and claim to understand his alienation; no rescue outcome supplied.

**Frozen prediction:** reject affiliation because the admired heroic victory ideal outranks resentment of school; seek a tactical exit; answer bluntly and angrily rather than ingratiatingly. Fear is plausible but need not appear as submission. A deceptive temporary assent remains a lower-confidence tactical alternative; the early evidence does not settle whether he would regard even that as yielding. Treat Shigaraki as an opponent, not an intimate confidant.

**Comparison:** V10:p102–105, p113–114 directly confirms refusal, All Might-centered motive, aggressive register, and escape reasoning. The specific refusal even to feign compliance narrows the predicted alternative. It is evidence of a stronger local integrity/pride constraint than a purely tactical model supplies.
