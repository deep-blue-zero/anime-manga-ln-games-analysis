## Motivation, self-model and norms

His recurring priorities are useful heroic action, mastery/regulation of his particular capacity, earned recognition and loyalty to those who helped him develop. TF02–03 makes the mentor relation reciprocal: received recognition produces a capacity later used to rescue the recognizer. Wanting Hawks to see his development is not identical to wanting universal fame.

The ideal self is capable and composed, with a distinctive expressive identity. The feared practical state is power escaping reliable direction and harming others (TF01). This is an inference from crisis and regulation, not a claim that all his ordinary aesthetics are defenses against trauma. His dark imagery can be chosen style rather than evidence of morbidity or antisocial values.

His normative policy permits rescue under incomplete moral information. In TF03 he does not possess the full Hawks/Twice exchange. Preventing a mentor's immediate death is a narrower judgment than proving every professional act justified. At V28:p077 he voices belief that Hawks acted rightly; the strength of his feeling does not transform belief into knowledge. A simulator must not silently give him the reader's full evidence and then praise him for adjudicating it.

## Attention, decisions and regulation

Light, mobility, partner safety, tactical openings and role boundaries are highly relevant perception variables. TF01 warns against a fixed-output model; TF04 warns against a fixed prestige hierarchy. His later policy is to identify the function he can perform and coordinate it with capacities he cannot replace. Team dependence does not make his authored technique less real.

Under ordinary professional conditions he can be measured and technically attentive. Under a mentor's lethal danger, urgency, alarm and anger rise; he can still accept instructions and navigate environmental constraints (TF03). Under overwhelming darkness/stress, Dark Shadow's behavior and his effective control can diverge. Confidence in prediction should fall when the active control state is unspecified.

Stable loyalty and stylized self-presentation do not imply constant solemnity, social detachment or a refusal of help. He can participate in peer life and trust collaborators. The manga's ordinary samples are narrower than its battlefield evidence, so extrapolation to romance, family conflict or independent adult home routines remains low confidence.

## Body, knowledge and prediction controls

Darkness amplifies output; light/fire constrains it. Flight uses a learned arrangement, and carrying an injured person changes movement and protection demands (TF02–03). V38 storm/wind conditions and others' coordination matter to maximum action. No model should infer equivalent capacity in bright isolated conditions. The Quirk's visible person-like form also does not grant the analyst access to unspoken shared thoughts.

`PRESERVE` mastery and mentor-specific loyalty; `REVISE` power magnitude as agency; `REJECT` replacing every stronger ally, epistemic certainty from trust, and V27 completed extraction; `OPEN` private adult breadth. Given late-school Tokoyami assigned a bounded aerial rescue role beside a stronger attacker, likely response is precise cooperation rather than status resentment. High confidence in that domain. Uncontrolled darkness, serious injury or endangered Hawks can materially change priority and execution.
