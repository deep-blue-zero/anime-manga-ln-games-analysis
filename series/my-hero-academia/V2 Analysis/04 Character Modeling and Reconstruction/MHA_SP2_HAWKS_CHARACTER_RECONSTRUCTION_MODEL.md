## Goals, self-model, and moral policy

Persistent wants are to make rescue faster, prevent overwhelming catastrophe, and create a world less dependent on heroes' endless labor. His public lightness is compatible with intense work (HK01). The child possessed rescue ability and aspiration before the Commission shaped a controlled professional identity; V31 therefore rejects both “purely free self-made agent” and “empty state puppet” (HK05).

Ideal self is useful, mobile, and able to help people toward a less constrained life. Feared conditions include captivity, inability to protect, and a catastrophic threat reaching full deployment. His self-model can translate institutional training into personal purpose after institutional command becomes unavailable. This does not prove that every trained habit is freely chosen or ethically sound. Nor does friendliness establish transparency when he is operating under cover (HK03).

Normatively, he distinguishes a person's affiliative goodness from the danger their capability and commitments present. Twice is simultaneously someone he wants to help and someone he refuses to let deploy Double (HK04). The killing is not evidence that the preceding offer was wholly insincere; the offer is not evidence that the later killing becomes morally uncomplicated. His alternative future is individually framed and requires separation from the very belonging Twice prioritizes. The mismatch is partly substantive, not merely a misunderstanding correctable by nicer words.

V42 strengthens a reforming role: wider recognition, staffing, donations, and ministry coordination are specific changes, while allowing Nagant to decline release respects a boundary his former institution often denied (HK07). No scene proves that the entire commission's violent history is repaired or that every future policy will preserve that respect.

## Attention, decisions, and affect

He notices distributed movement, information channels, capabilities that can change the whole battle, and how a public signal will be received. Under surveillance he selects speech that can carry different meanings to different recipients (HK03). His sensor/control skill is bounded by feathers, fire, injury and what he can actually observe; it is not omniscient awareness. Dabi's identity disclosures and timing can destabilize him (HK02, HK05).

Decision policy: map the situation, establish containment, create an escape or rescue route for others, and escalate when a catastrophic capability appears about to become uncontrollable. Speed and preemption can compress deliberation. In mentorship, he recognizes an affordance and pushes the learner to discover its use; in public performance, a disruption may expose a stale reassurance; in postwar governance, a proposed change needs resources and procedure (HK01–02, HK04, HK07). These are different regimes of the same practical orientation.

Baseline social presentation is relaxed and conversational; covert work makes it instrumental as well as personal. Severe threat produces compact commands and calculation. Personal identity exposure can produce shock. He may express care without leaving the operational frame, and can remember someone positively after killing him (HK04–05). Do not harmonize that tension into either sentimental innocence or total emotional numbness. Detailed private grief remains less explicit than the chosen continued action.

## Embodiment, knowledge, and uncertainty

Fierce Wings permits precise sensing, rescue, transport and force, but feathers are finite and heat is costly (HK01). Injury and later Quirk loss change available action; becoming chair does not imply restored wings. A role can persist through other skills and institutional labor. Tactical expertise also does not make the correct moral choice mechanically computable from a threat label.

Knowledge is compartmentalized. Infiltration does not supply complete villain schedules; Dabi withholds information; a state-trained agent can be deceived. V27 cannot use the full V31 family account, while a V42 reconstruction should include the commission's collapse and his experience of identity beyond its command. `PRESERVE` speed, practical care, and audience awareness; `REVISE` institutional obedience as exhaustive motivation; `REJECT` omniscient spy and automatic moral purification by reform; `OPEN` private grief, future institutional safeguards, and the adequacy of alternatives in the Twice crisis.

## Conditional reconstruction

For **V42 chair Hawks**, a formerly exploited operative declines a public rehabilitation opportunity. Likely: offer concrete resources and a route without requiring immediate trust or gratitude, while asking what would make participation possible. High confidence for respecting this particular kind of refusal from HK07; medium for how he would handle conflicting operational emergencies. Evidence of imminent serious harm changes the policy toward containment. His past warns that recognizing a person does not guarantee he will prioritize that person's preferred affiliation over others' safety.

## Formal validation probe HK-V01: V27 → V31 freedom from command

**Freeze inputs:** HK01–04 plus V27 training disclosure and received rescue. Exclude V31 childhood details, explicit post-collapse choice, and positive Twice reference. Initial conditions: after severe injury and organizational collapse, the command structure that directed his life is nonfunctional; a decision about continued work is possible.

**Frozen prediction:** continued protective action is likely because public rescue and mentorship supply motives beyond command. Some relief or uncertainty is plausible; passive withdrawal is not impossible after injury. Endeavor remains a likely partner because of established trust. Predict conversational pragmatism rather than triumphant ideological denunciation. The exact childhood explanation and explicit selection of Twice as a moral exemplar are not predicted.

**Comparison:** V31:p079–085 confirms freedom from former constraint and chosen aid. p083 (raw0084.jpg) adds the unexpectedly explicit positive Twice comparison. Newly disclosed childhood supplies causal depth but cannot be credited to the earlier model's knowledge.
