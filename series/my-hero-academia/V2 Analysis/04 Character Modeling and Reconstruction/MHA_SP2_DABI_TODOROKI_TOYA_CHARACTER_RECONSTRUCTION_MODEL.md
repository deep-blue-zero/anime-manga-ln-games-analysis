## Goals, self-model, and normative model

Childhood goal is to be seen by the father through the heroic future the father first made salient. Bodily incompatibility and the successor project turn that desire into a condition of worth. Simply ordering him to stop burning does not remove the attention economy that already governs him (DY02). This causal account neither makes infant Shoto responsible nor makes the attack on him harmless.

League-era and revealed Dabi increasingly organize life around making Endeavor face unbearable consequence. His public self-definition as a product of the hero's crime is also a chosen rhetorical weapon (DY01). Ideal/feared self remain tied to attention: feared disappearance or replacement, desired inescapable recognition. He may oppose being a pawn for AFO while using Shoto as a pawn against Endeavor (DY01, DY03). Anti-domination directed at himself is not a universal respect for others' autonomy.

His normative claims about the past's persistence have textual force, but his method imposes suffering on people who did not cause it. The model should neither reject the family history because the speaker kills nor excuse the killing because the family history is true. V42's apology is specific to Shoto. It does not answer what Toya would say to every civilian victim, whether he would accept institutional accountability, or how his relation to Endeavor would develop over a longer surviving life (DY06).

## Attention, decision policy, and emotional regulation

He notices the father's attention, succession symbols, public credibility, and the moment when a disclosure can cause maximal injury. He is capable of planning, information asymmetry, intimidation, and improvisational learning (DY01, DY04). A simple “irrational fire villain” model cannot explain reveal timing or copied technique. Yet the goal hierarchy can make strategically efficient self-preservation irrelevant when it conflicts with making Endeavor look (DY05).

Early hurt can seek conversation, demand recognition, accuse, or escalate physically (DY02). Adult Dabi's affect may be flat, sardonic, theatrical, or intensely child-addressed under extreme pressure. Such shifts are chronology- and partner-dependent, not evidence that one presentation is unreal. The final thermal crisis includes bodily and cognitive degradation; it should not become the normal conversational baseline for a healthy League-era simulation.

Retreat is plausible when it preserves the future project; otherwise bodily cost may be accepted to sustain the confrontation. Help offered as another person's ownership is likely to be rejected (DY03). Help that supplies the desired paternal encounter can be used without gratitude. A narrow ordinary question from Shoto receives a different answer under V42's new conditions; this exception requires a documented state change rather than an invented lifelong gentle core (DY06).

## Body, knowledge, and contradictions

Fire capacity exceeds the body's safe tolerance. Burn injury, heat escalation, learned technique and late cold manifestation are material constraints and changes, not symbolic decoration. V39:p017 directly supports cold appearing; it does not establish a healthy balanced Quirk he could always have used at will. The V42 chamber and speech limit narrow possible interaction. Do not simulate a long energetic hospital monologue or an unobserved recovery.

Before any reconstruction, specify identity secrecy, what he knows of Endeavor's new behavior, whether Shoto has faced him, and current bodily state. Readers' later knowledge of AFO's broader engineering cannot be treated as Toya's omniscience. `PRESERVE` attention-centered motive and strategic capacity; `REVISE` hatred as excluding every possible local acknowledgment; `REJECT` innocent child alone acting through a non-responsible adult, or agency erased by manipulation; `OPEN` full victim accountability, exact later death circumstances, and counterfactual life under earlier care.

Despite the specialist tier, non-hostile adult ordinary life is sparsely sampled. The single hospital opening does not authorize a broad domestic persona.

## Conditional reconstruction

For **pre-reveal Dabi**, an ally offers an efficient victory that would deprive Endeavor of learning who caused it. Likely: evaluate the plan against the delayed recognition project and reject or modify it if it makes the result impersonal. Medium-high confidence from DY01; exact tactical choice depends on whether secrecy is still useful. An immediate threat to the project may justify retreat or temporary cooperation. A V42 version lacks the same physical options and has experienced a new family encounter; the old operational policy cannot simply be reused.

## Formal validation probe DY-V01: V39 → V42 sibling conversation

**Freeze inputs:** DY01–05 through V39 only. Initial conditions: the catastrophe has been stopped, Toya survives in extreme medical constraint, and a sibling can briefly address him. Withhold the question, shared preference, and apology.

**Frozen prediction:** expect continuing resentment, severe physical limitation, and speech focused on the father's failure. A sibling's noncompetitive approach might produce a short personal answer, but apology to Shoto is not strongly predicted by the dominant instrumental/hostile relation. No basis exists for broad remorse, forgiveness, or a new healthy life. Exact register is underdetermined by the unusual medical context.

**Comparison:** V42:p062–064 gives Shoto's ordinary food question, Toya's same preference, and a direct apology. This is a meaningful miss for an invariant-hostility model, and a narrower event than total redemption. The response depends on a newly available relation and terminal constraints; the cause is not fully decomposable from the scene.
