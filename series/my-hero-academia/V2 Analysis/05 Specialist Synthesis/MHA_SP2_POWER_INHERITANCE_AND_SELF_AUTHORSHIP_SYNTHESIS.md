# MHA SP2 — Power, inheritance and self-authorship

## Power is not one kind of possession

**Interpretation.** MHA distinguishes having an ability from knowing how to use it, surviving its use, having authority to use it on another, and making its history part of one's own identity. The [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md) is the primary owner, especially its capability/control/mastery/jurisdiction/authorship distinctions, `P-V08-1`–`P-V08-7`, and the OFA, Eri, final-war and V42 entries. Power is socially produced throughout the series; the critical ethical question is what claims that production permits over its user.

### Inheritance begins a task rather than completing a person

**Textual facts.** Midoriya's first decisive rescue impulse precedes OFA, but physical intervention by a trained hero is still needed (V01:p043–059). The offer is followed by bodily preparation, then injuries and adaptation rather than effortless mastery (V01:p064–085,p151–169). Later control methods and tactical learning give his movement a developing form; `P-V08-1`–`P-V08-7` retain the tension between finite capacity, coordination and judgment. Having received power therefore does not make his early body ready for every use or make All Might's movement the right permanent template. [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), V01–V08; [Class 1-A ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_CLASS_1A_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Midoriya training sequence.

Shoto supplies a parallel problem with different stakes. The fire is biologically his while its use has been captured by his father's project. Choosing how to use it can resist that project without denying its history. His later approach to Toya and adult ordinary preferences extend authorship beyond selecting the approved hero function. **Strong inference:** self-authorship is a changed relation to what one has received, not creation from nothing. V05–V06 Sports Festival; V26 internship; V42:p062–064,p167–168; [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), Shoto; [Family and civilians ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md), Todoroki family.

### Biology prevents a purely moral theory of succession

V31:p164–174 introduces a concrete compatibility problem. Hikage's long holding period and bodily deterioration, together with All Might's Quirkless history, support the holders' account that accumulated OFA overloads an already Quirk-bearing recipient. The evidence is not a universal actuarial table: other holders had shorter, battle-ended lives, and the explanation is reconstructed from a limited history. **Textual fact:** the vestige discussion makes suitability bodily as well as ethical. **Residual:** exact individual lifespans and all possible exceptional recipients remain unknown. [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), V31 succession; [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), All Might; [U.A. students and staff ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Mirio.

Mirio's refusal of Midoriya's hypothetical offer at V18:p088–090 demonstrates protective judgment without access to this later mechanism. He remains an ethically credible candidate in the earlier evaluative setting. It is nevertheless inaccurate, from the completed-series standpoint, to call him an uncomplicated excellent OFA successor without acknowledging that he already possesses Permeation. The earlier comparison did not become foolish because later evidence arrived; the final claim must become more qualified. This is a substantive V1/V2 revision handled in [S8](MHA_SP2_V1_V2_CLAIM_RECONCILIATION.md).

### Socially produced capacity does not confer ownership of the user

OFA carries contributions, advice and pressures from multiple people; it was never simply one isolated mentor's gift. U.A. training, peers, support equipment and judgment also make hero action possible. V42:p140–141 makes the later suit's international, engineering, financial and observational inputs explicit. **Interpretation:** collective enablement differs ethically from a collective entitlement to command Midoriya's whole future. His p159 preference to teach even in a retained-OFA counterfactual is important because it affirms an end not reducible to maximizing the supplied weapon's use. [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), OFA/V42; [Relationship state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), All Might–Midoriya and adult Class A.

The same principle applies more sharply to Eri. Her rescue does not create a debt payable through her body. V41:p157–159 shows her wish to help and Ectoplasm's assistance alongside Aizawa's concern about potential lasting damage; the intervention also has a limited rewind effect. Treating the scene as either pure passivity or uncomplicated child autonomy loses a depicted tension. **Interpretation:** valuable chosen contribution can coexist with a troubling asymmetry of age, authority and risk. Later creative desire cannot retroactively make every cost acceptable. [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), Eri; [U.A. students and staff ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), V41; [Family and civilians ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md), recovery and ordinary future.

### OFA and AFO are opposed without being simple substances of good and evil

OFA's preserved wills and AFO's appropriation establish a strong contrast, but neither biology nor the presence of internal voices directly supplies an ethical verdict. AFO understands vulnerabilities and relationships as instruments; V42:p014–015 reveals intense need for Yoichi while retaining possessive demands. He is not devoid of attachment or incapable of strategic social understanding. The failure is his refusal to let the other person's will limit his claim. [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), AFO/OFA; [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), final AFO; [Relationship state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), AFO–Yoichi.

Conversely, the final OFA transfer strategy is an intervention against a resisting recipient. Willing sacrifice by the transferring holders does not mean Tomura consents to receiving it. **Interpretation:** its protective/rescue aim must be evaluated as an exercise of power under catastrophic constraints, not purified by the label “gift.” It aims to break a destructive barrier and reach a person, yet does not grant the hero a guaranteed right to determine what that person will become. V40–V41 final transfer sequence; V41:p110,p116,p121–123; [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), final expenditure; [Recognition and failed rescue ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RECOGNITION_FAILED_RESCUE_LEDGER.md), final rescue.

### Expertise, mechanism and moral judgment remain distinct

Jiro's V36:p082–092 contribution works within a chain involving rebellious internal wills, weaker control of the copied AFO factor, sensory delay and allies' action. A sound-only explanation obscures this distributed causation. Shinso's V38:p155–156 distinction between command and the target's own grievance similarly refuses to infer lasting control of the heart from a temporary behavioral mechanism. **Strong inference:** the series repeatedly makes technical power less comprehensive than an observer might assume. This supports modest judgments about what a demonstrated ability can establish about personhood. [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), Jiro/Shinso; [Class 1-A ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_CLASS_1A_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), final-war entries.

The converse limit is that virtuous intent cannot conjure technical capacity. Gentle needs feasible means and La Brava's work; All Might's dangerous return depends on equipment and accumulated knowledge. At V39:p155 his win-directed intent is explicit, but does not guarantee victory. V38:p081–083; V39:p146,p154–155; [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Gentle/All Might. Dependence is not automatically domination; it becomes domination when enabling another is converted into an unlimited claim over them.

### Losing power can preserve identity while restricting practice

Midoriya's extinguished embers do not erase learned judgment, bonds or vocation. Teaching is meaningful in itself, and the suit later reopens a desired kind of field activity. Neither half should cancel the other. **Interpretation:** the ending rejects the identification of personhood with current power, while acknowledging that material access shapes what a person can do. It is therefore too weak to say power never mattered and too narrow to say the suit alone restores his worth. V42:p134,p140–141,p159; [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), V42; [U.A. students and staff ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), adult teacher.

**Residuals:** the suit's complete specifications and access model, a general metaphysics of vestiges, every possible OFA recipient and the moral balance of all emergency uses are not resolved. The [paired full-series adjudication](../06%20Full-Series%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_RESCUE_POWER_PERSONHOOD_ADJUDICATION.md) carries these limits into the comparisons with AFO, Shigaraki and Toga.

## The pedagogy of inheritance: supported authorship and managed initiative

### V03 constructs two successors before either is complete

The festival's early framing pairs All Might's public succession project with AFO's cultivation of Shigaraki after failure. All Might wants Midoriya to announce a presence capable of succeeding the Symbol; the invitation is generous and weighty at once. Midoriya receives not only capacity but a public measure against which to understand his effort. AFO, meanwhile, interprets an immature disciple's failure as something through which he can learn. His patience is real pedagogy in the instrumental sense: it can cultivate initiative. It is not evidence that the learner's independent limits will be respected. [V03](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V03_DEEP_READING.md), p070–090,p100–117.

**Interpretation:** the contrast is stronger when both mentors can teach than when one grants freedom and the other merely issues orders. The relevant question is who may revise the purpose of the inherited project. AFO can invite Tomura to think while monopolizing the resources, memory and dependency through which thought becomes action. All Might's model becomes more defensible as he accepts that Midoriya cannot and need not reproduce him. The distinction must be demonstrated longitudinally; V03 alone establishes two developing arrangements, not their final verdict.

### Midoriya learns to revise the model, and All Might learns to permit it

V05 begins loosening the demand for literal reproduction. Gran Torino then changes the bodily conception of OFA: an exceptional act concentrated at the moment of impact must become something Midoriya can inhabit and regulate. Later Shoot Style does not spring from pure individual inspiration. Teachers, peer observation and support engineering make a new organization of received capacity possible. These mediators matter because self-authorship occurs through selective uptake of help, rather than withdrawal from dependence. [V05](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V05_DEEP_READING.md), mentor response; [V06](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V06_DEEP_READING.md), Gran Torino; [V12](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V12_DEEP_READING.md), technique and equipment development.

All Might's V12 teaching principle is already learner-led: simply supplying the answer does not perform the work of education. V19 applies and refines that policy when the student's bodily mismatch and the teacher's easier historical control must be acknowledged. Air-pressure practice, the distinction between a tolerable limit and a controllable technique, and Hatsume's equipment show an iterative process. All Might can offer a problem and accumulated knowledge without possessing a complete embodied answer for this student. V19 therefore supplies continuity and elaboration, not his first conversion from imitation to education. [V12](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V12_DEEP_READING.md), teaching discussion and Shoot Style; [V19](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V19_DEEP_READING.md), p089–094,p117–120.

This history changes the meaning of the final loss and suit. Midoriya has spent the series assembling methods from inheritance, observation and assistance; the later equipment continues that social history under altered material conditions. What is new is not dependence itself. It is the explicit survival of valued identity and chosen teaching when OFA no longer supplies the central capacity, together with a new route to field action. Donors enable a desired practice without gaining the right to replace his answer about what he wants to do. [V42](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V42_DEEP_READING.md), p134,p140–141,p159.

### AFO's successor can acquire agency that AFO later violates

Shigaraki's changing strategies make AFO's pedagogy materially consequential. He learns to articulate a target of hatred, manage disagreement, postpone retaliation, delegate and acquire an organization. AFO's withdrawal predicts further independence, but that prediction is not itself evidence the student has already mastered leadership. Subsequent League action supplies the test. The bodily enhancement accepted later is also a different decision from consent to possession: accepting power under the terms available to Tomura does not authorize AFO to occupy his will. [V08](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V08_DEEP_READING.md), conviction; [V11](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V11_DEEP_READING.md), the predecessor's withdrawal; [V14](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V14_DEEP_READING.md)–[V18](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V18_DEEP_READING.md), coalition strategy; [V25](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V25_DEEP_READING.md); [V28](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V28_DEEP_READING.md), enhancement and emergence of control.

This is why AFO's eventual claim to have authored everything cannot be the analyst's final description. His engineering revises the causal history, but Tomura's formed bonds and destructive commitments are real enough to resist capture. AFO also miscalculates which relationships and memories can be treated as mere instruments. His strategic understanding is selective rather than nonexistent. At the endpoint, need for Yoichi reveals attachment that still cannot recognize departure as the other's right. **Interpretation:** ownership is the governing failure across his teaching, manipulation and intimacy; emotional intensity does not rescue it from that failure. [V38](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V38_DEEP_READING.md), contested interior identity; [V41](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V41_DEEP_READING.md), origin intervention and AFO's account; [V42](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V42_DEEP_READING.md), p014–018. [S5](MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_AND_ACCOUNTABILITY_SYNTHESIS.md) owns the organizational mechanisms.

### A limit on the gift analogy

The OFA line repeatedly makes contribution and recipient choice visible, but its final transfer deliberately acts against Shigaraki's resistance. Donor willingness, a rescue purpose and the immediate need to stop catastrophic harm support different parts of the justification; none manufactures receiver agreement. The invasion also has adverse consequences inside the contested identity, rather than functioning as a frictionless delivery of understanding. **Counterreading adjudication:** the opposition between entrusted power and appropriated power remains strong, provided it is an ethical comparison of practices under particular conditions. It cannot become a rule that every act performed with OFA automatically honors autonomy. The [paired adjudication](../06%20Full-Series%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_RESCUE_POWER_PERSONHOOD_ADJUDICATION.md) owns that unresolved emergency balance.

## Supplemental histories distinguish inheritance from complete knowledge

The books make the social production of capacity more concrete without turning every productive gift into an ethical one. UA's early AFO account joins granting power to domination and records the forced gift to his resistant brother; that act produces an unforeseen line of resistance. UAN describes well-paid civilian demand for electrical Quirks and regulated routes for equipment improvement. Capacity therefore has several possible occupations and material mediators before the ending. A bodily habit can also outlast a changed intention: Shoto's attributed account of habitually beginning with ice qualifies any instant conversion from rejecting fire to freely integrated technique. UAG's unsigned holder histories further distinguish the knowledge available to successive participants. Banjo's protective withholding means En learns about OFA only at the fatal encounter; Nana learns of her husband's secret struggle after his death and receives the inheritance because she is nearest to the dying En. Her later account of embers and capacity includes inference rather than a complete advance theory. Hikage's social exhaustion and conditional trust also prevent his isolation from becoming a timeless mandate that every successor should abandon relationships. This added exposition makes the chain less like a uniformly informed order. It does not supply missing manga scenes or a quantitative model of every lifespan, compatibility condition and unobserved alternative. The two late armors require separate treatment. All Might's privately financed, untested prototype is not adult Midoriya's later system. Horikoshi describes the latter's expanded and miniaturized functions, including elastic cables that emulate aspects of Blackwhip; UAG also names engineering collaboration and investment behind support development. Those specifics explain enabled practice more precisely. They do not prove output parity with OFA, affordable access for everyone, maintenance costs or long-term safety. Midoriya's recognized contribution before the armor and continuing teaching after it preserve identity across changing capacities.
