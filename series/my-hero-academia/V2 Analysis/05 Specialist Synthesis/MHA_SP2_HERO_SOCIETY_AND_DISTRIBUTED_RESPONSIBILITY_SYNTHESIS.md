# MHA SP2 — Hero society and distributed responsibility

## The endpoint redistributes responsibility through continuing institutions

**Interpretation.** MHA criticizes concentrated symbolic protection and specific institutional failures while retaining professional expertise, public organization and material support. Civilian responsibility supplements those structures and makes demands on them; it does not mean every civilian must become a combatant or that institutions should disappear. Primary owners are [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md) and [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), including `HS-V08-1`–`HS-V08-7`, licensing, HPSC, collapse, final-war coordination and V42 reform entries.

### A successful Symbol can create a brittle expectation

**Textual facts.** V01:p043–050 shows a dangerous emergency, constrained professionals and spectators waiting; All Might then acts at p052–059. His exceptional capacity saves lives. Later bodily limits and succession make the dependence on one visible guarantor precarious. **Strong inference:** the Symbol's success helps organize expectations that exceed any person's sustainable capacity. It is not the sole cause of every spectator's inaction, prejudice, family abuse or institutional secrecy. [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md), V01/Kamino; [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), All Might; [Family and civilians ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md), civilian bystanders.

The series therefore permits gratitude for All Might and criticism of concentration together. His later ordinary support, teaching relationships, equipment-enabled intervention and network facilitation do not reduce him to a failed old system. At V39:p146 an observer reads his risk as a deathward act; p155 gives his own determination to fight to win. The contrast reveals an individual whose purpose cannot be inferred from a public symbolic role alone. [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), All Might V32/V39/V42.

### Training and licensing are substantive, revisable institutions

U.A. and professional instruction supply judgment, technical specialization and coordination. The examination and licensing evidence (`HS-V08-1`–`HS-V08-7`; V13:p008–047) treats acting within a team and handling victims as learned practices, not incidental obstacles to authentic heroism. Momo's delegated emergency planning depends on information, materials and roles. The Big Three's V36:p173–179 coordination likewise cannot be replaced by goodwill alone. [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md), education/licensing; [U.A. students and staff ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), staff and Big Three; [Class 1-A ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_CLASS_1A_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Momo V28–V30.

Those benefits do not make the institutions infallible. Students are exposed to severe danger, evaluations can mistake surface appearance for disposition, and safety responsibilities require adaptation. Aizawa's V10:p110 defense of Bakugo offers a concrete better practice: judgment grounded in observed motivation rather than outsiders' stereotype. The institution's claim to educate must include revising its understanding of the person it serves. **Interpretation:** the narrative tests institutions by whether they enable and protect people, not by whether rules exist or whether a heroic individual can ignore them.

### Covert violence damages legitimacy beyond public relations

Nagant's HPSC history makes her violence an institutional problem with a specific causal owner; it should not be lazily included among AFO's original creations. AFO later exploits her disillusionment. Hawks's formation and V31:p079–085 choice after institutional command changes similarly distinguish assigned duty from chosen helping. His positive reference to Twice complicates any pure hero/villain sorting without automatically settling the earlier killing. [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Nagant V32 and Hawks V31; [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md), HPSC; [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), AFO recruitment.

**Interpretation:** public legitimacy cannot be repaired solely by maintaining a reassuring image. Concealed coercion, targeted people and the terms of professional obedience matter. Nor does exposing such wrongdoing establish that all prior rescues were fraudulent. Nagant's V38:p065–068 risky assistance draws on particular acts of trust and restored hope; her body remains injured and later institutional reserve remains intelligible. Reform cannot demand that a harmed person trust the organization merely because someone good now leads it.

### The public is plural and can participate badly

Civilian self-defense and professional withdrawal during collapse expose finite capacity, fear and fractured trust (V31:p087–095). Heteromorph persecution and the hospital mobilization show that “the public” includes victims, perpetrators, persuaded participants and dissenters. Shoji's anti-revenge position at V37:p140 and the rescued-child memory at p142 resist allowing violence to dictate his identity, but do not deny the harm or demand instant forgiveness. [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md), postwar collapse/heteromorph front; [Family and civilians ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md), rural persecution; [Class 1-A ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_CLASS_1A_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Shoji/Koda V37.

**Strong inference:** distributing action without improving judgment can also distribute harm. The positive endpoint therefore cannot mean spontaneous public involvement is always good. The hospital crowd's decision, later shared credit, and sustained regional work matter because they concern changed action under specific conditions. V42:p132 does not establish that all prejudice has disappeared. The V37:p148 author drawing is excluded and contributes no ordinary behavioral evidence.

### Media shifts from spectatorship toward accountable participation

Gentle's V38:p081–083 scene first makes rescue valuable to him without perceived applause, then reveals student cameras. It is inaccurate to call the whole event permanently unseen. The students' account of filming connects visibility to public responsibility, while Gentle's response loosens the dependence of value on immediate validation. Earlier hostile or reductive public images make that distinction consequential. [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md), media; [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Gentle; [Visual motifs ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VISUAL_MOTIF_LEDGER.md), spectatorship/camera motifs.

**Interpretation:** the critique concerns what visibility is used for. Attention can commodify, stereotype or sensationalize; it can also bear witness and help people understand shared action. The manga does not provide evidence that an entire media ecology has been corrected. The ending gives a changed possibility and selected practice, not comprehensive sector reform.

### Differentiation is the substance of distributed heroism

Engineers, teachers, counselors, funders, regional advocates, professional rescuers and attentive civilians do different things. The social whole is more resilient when capacities complement one another, but that is not an invitation to conscript every person's life into maximum usefulness. Mirio's V42:p042–043 positive-future argument and the adult ordinary scenes give the work an end: people can enjoy more than survival. Reform must make room for that enjoyment as well as reduce danger. [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md), V42; [U.A. students and staff ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Mirio; [Family and civilians ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md), ordinary life.

The resulting position is reformist and incomplete. It neither treats institutional continuation as proof nothing changed nor treats selected good outcomes as proof of structural resolution. [Social change and ordinary future](../06%20Full-Series%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_SOCIAL_CHANGE_AND_ORDINARY_FUTURE.md) integrates these findings with grief, family boundaries and prevention.

## The early institutional argument and the stages of redistribution

### Evaluation, recruitment and recognition are different mechanisms

The early school material already gives hero society more than a single ranking logic. U.A.'s entrance rescue points recognize conduct that the applicant does not know is being measured. Classroom assessment subsequently separates a team's nominal victory from the quality of its decisions: Momo can identify weaknesses in successful action. Thirteen's instruction makes dangerous capabilities a matter of trained jurisdiction, while Iida's evacuation role shows collective survival depending on someone leaving to obtain help. These are specific practices of evaluation and coordination, not incidental exceptions to an otherwise wholly individual system. [V01](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V01_DEEP_READING.md), entrance examination; [V02](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V02_DEEP_READING.md), battle assessment and USJ; [V03](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V03_DEEP_READING.md), evacuation and its consequences.

The Sports Festival then combines several markets and gateways. It displays prospective interns to professionals, offers movement between courses, advertises support technology, lets business students study promotion, and invites students to cultivate public identities. Ochako's financial motive belongs in that material setting. An institution can broaden opportunity while requiring candidates to become legible within competitive spectacles. Those two effects coexist. [V03](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V03_DEEP_READING.md), p091–099,p113–117,p150–159,p179–180; [V04](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V04_DEEP_READING.md), festival progression.

Shinso identifies a concrete limitation in the robot-based entrance examination. The festival supplies another opportunity, but one still conditioned by audience judgment, opposition and unequal preparation. This is evidence of both a blind spot and a revisable route, not proof either that merit is wholly fictitious or that a second chance removes structural disadvantage. Bakugo and Ochako's match adds professional misrecognition: apparently protective spectators underestimate her agency and read his serious resistance as improper cruelty. The victory podium then shows a school producing a reassuring image through coercive display. Evaluation, recruitment, public approval and the student's own measure of success can conflict. [V04](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V04_DEEP_READING.md), Shinso; [V05](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V05_DEEP_READING.md), Ochako–Bakugo and award ceremony.

### Professional differentiation was already part of the solution

Internships demonstrate divergent kinds of expertise and presentation rather than one universal heroic style. Hosu adds a jurisdiction problem: the students' contribution and the publicly assignable credit do not line up transparently under the law. The story can value lifesaving action while showing how institutional responsibility determines what may be officially acknowledged. That tension cannot be reduced to spectators misunderstanding good intentions. [V06](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V06_DEEP_READING.md); [V07](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V07_DEEP_READING.md), internships, Stain and police settlement.

Kamino's professional arrival is already plural, and the later licensing policy explicitly seeks reliability beyond one dominant figure through groups. Those earlier recognitions rule out a history in which the ending invents teamwork or first discovers the risks of concentration. Training, triage, interschool relations and remediation are attempted mechanisms of redistribution before collapse. Their existence, however, does not demonstrate adequate implementation or erase the gap between an institutional proposal and public expectations. [V10](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V10_DEEP_READING.md), coordinated professional operation; [V13](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V13_DEEP_READING.md), Commission reasoning and licensing/remediation; [V18](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V18_DEEP_READING.md), remedial teaching.

### What changes later is the distribution of obligation and access

The collapse is not explained by a shortage of cooperative sentiment. Simultaneous injury, prison releases, withdrawal, distrust and uneven safety overwhelm finite capacity. Civilian self-armament can increase danger, so simply multiplying actors is insufficient. U.A.'s refuge in V33 instead makes civilians participants in the terms of protection without requiring them to fight. Support, maintenance and documentation become increasingly visible as indispensable work; the final rescue makes the central hero's insufficiency public enough to invite contribution. [V31](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V31_DEEP_READING.md), collapse; [V33](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V33_DEEP_READING.md), refuge; [V34](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V34_DEEP_READING.md)–[V36](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V36_DEEP_READING.md), preparation and logistics; [V38](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V38_DEEP_READING.md), support and cameras; [V41](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V41_DEEP_READING.md), shared intervention.

The ending adds particular institutions and resources: expanded recognition, counseling support, sustained regional work and an engineered route for one former OFA holder to act. These change access, expectations and acknowledged obligations on different timescales. The suit demonstrates one effective enabling network. It is not evidence of universal equipment entitlement. The counseling program has specified support, but the text does not measure equal coverage or long-term prevention outcomes. **Interpretation:** redistribution is an uneven extension and public acknowledgment of dependence already present, joined to selected new arrangements. It is neither an instantaneous replacement of the old order nor a mere verbal relabeling. [V42](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V42_DEEP_READING.md), p118–123,p132,p140–141,p150.

### Organized plurality can reproduce domination

The liberation movement is a decisive negative control. It joins capital, media, political organization, consumer information and doctrine across many actors. Incorporation into the PLF expands scale without making those powers egalitarian or benign. Likewise, the hospital crowd's mobilization joins real experiences of discrimination to imposed symbolism and manipulable anger. Participants can revise a destructive method without thereby admitting that the grievance was false. Distributed resources and authentic injury do not guarantee accountable participation. [V24](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V24_DEEP_READING.md); [V25](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V25_DEEP_READING.md), MLA and PLF; [V37](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V37_DEEP_READING.md), hospital front; [S5](MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_AND_ACCOUNTABILITY_SYNTHESIS.md).

**Counterreading adjudication.** Institutional continuity does limit claims of revolution; it does not nullify every changed practice. Conversely, a personhood vocabulary cannot explain every causal mechanism named here. Licensing incentives, recruitment, administrative authority, geography, logistics and political mediation determine which choices are available. Respect for persons tests the legitimacy and effects of those arrangements, but is not a substitute for describing them. [F3](../06%20Full-Series%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_SOCIAL_CHANGE_AND_ORDINARY_FUTURE.md) integrates this qualified reform account and retains its unresolved coverage questions.

## The supplemental sequence makes institutions concrete without certifying them

UA's U.A. prospectus already describes four cooperating courses, distinct career routes, demanding timetables and organized care. Its claim that heroes are not the only people supporting citizens is direct earlier evidence for differentiation. Its superlative security promotion appears beside an image of a breached barrier, however; recruitment speech cannot certify the reliability it advertises. The same book's institutional report distributes USJ results among students and supporting professionals. A later account of shared responsibility therefore develops an existing organization of work rather than inventing collective action at the ending. A character's explanation is a fact that the character gives that explanation, not automatically a true account of the world.
