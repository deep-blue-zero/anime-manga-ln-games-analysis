# MHA SP2 — Villain formation and accountability

## Villain formation is a set of interacting pathways

**Interpretation.** MHA supplies neither a single “society made them do it” explanation nor a collection of isolated bad essences. Formation involves bodies, families, classification, neglect, political recruitment, affiliation, direct engineering and choices that continue afterward. The [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md) and [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md) are the primary owners: `VF-V08-1`–`VF-V08-6`, the segmented V25 origin analysis, and each antagonist's later state changes. Explanation can identify failures that invite prevention without transferring every later act to an impersonal cause.

### A comparative causal map

| Case | Mechanism the corpus supports | Simplification the case resists |
|---|---|---|
| Tenko / Shigaraki | Family violence and abandonment within AFO's deliberately engineered conditions; later League affiliation and resistance | Either untouched spontaneous childhood destiny or a person with no later will |
| Toya / Dabi | Instrumental parental ambition, Quirk/body mismatch, experienced rejection, later self-directed revenge | All family members equally responsible; all later violence mechanically compelled |
| Toga | Embodied desire under coercive normalization, concealment, violent choices and highly particular attachment | Quirk determinism, universal innocence, or universal benevolence after recognition |
| Twice | Precarity, identity disruption and a group that provides consequential belonging | Irrational refusal of an obviously equivalent safe future |
| Spinner | Stigma and isolation joined to affiliation, political instrumentalization and imposed bodily change | All mobilized heteromorphs share one motive or impairment was timeless personality |
| Nagant | Violence demanded and concealed by the HPSC, disillusionment, later AFO exploitation | AFO originally caused every institutional abuse |
| Gentle | Failed rescue, damaged aspiration and recognition-seeking, later feasible rehabilitation | Good intention equals competence; wrongdoing precludes later useful action |
| Chisaki | Boss-directed gratitude converted into coercive control and instrumental treatment of Eri | Attachment equals respect or apology to one person equals remorse toward all |
| Muscular | Explicit appetite for violence in the observed evidence | Every antagonist requires an invented hidden rescue-seeking motive |

These are **interpretive causal summaries**, grounded in [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md) and [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), corresponding named-character sections. They are not experimental estimates of causal weight. Early `VF-V08` distinctions, V25's stages, V31 family/Hawks material, V32 Nagant, V37 Spinner, V38 Gentle, and V41–V42 endpoints provide the longitudinal checks.

### Designed conditions do not exhaust continuing authorship

The V25 origin sequence establishes the child's environment and catastrophic development; V41 later revises how spontaneous that causal chain can be considered. AFO's p132 account claims extensive engineering and cultivation. It is a fact that he makes this claim and the narrative supplies major support for engineered conditions; his assertion that no choices belong to Tomura must still be tested against other depicted evidence. Nana's blocking at p110, Tenko's contradiction at p116, League-directed intent at p121–123 and Tomura's terminal re-emergence at V42:p018 prevent an unrestricted substitution of AFO's authorship for the person's own. [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md), V25/V41; [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Shigaraki/AFO; [Recognition and failed rescue ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RECOGNITION_FAILED_RESCUE_LEDGER.md), final rescue.

**Interpretation:** constrained authorship is still authorship, but it is not the same as freedom from manipulation. The manga makes it possible to condemn the engineering, recognize the harmed child, and hold later destructive choices in view. The strongest unresolved question is counterfactual: what could a surviving Tomura have chosen under safe, noncoercive conditions? The text supplies no rehabilitated adult sample. It cannot be solved either by assuming inevitable evil or by inventing certain acceptance of rescue.

### Bodies condition desire without dictating ethics

Toya's body frustrates the successor role imposed upon him. Toga's relation to blood and resemblance shapes desire and language. Spinner's enhancements alter cognition and performance. Those are not interchangeable mechanisms, and none licenses reading every action as the direct output of a Quirk. Shoto's history and Shinso's stigmatized ability provide negative controls against a one-step bodily destiny; their different opportunities and relationships also prevent treating them as perfectly matched experiments. [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md), Quirk/body and negative-control entries; [Class 1-A ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_CLASS_1A_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Shoto/Shinso; [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Toya/Toga/Spinner.

The Toga encounter particularly requires an ethical distinction. Ochako can acknowledge an ordinary desire and the person expressing it while refusing to affirm all harmful conduct (V39:p098–120). Toga's later fatal gift is directed to that person under exceptional conditions (p125–139), not proof of a timeless rule of universal altruism. Recognition changes possibilities; it does not reveal that killings were only misunderstood affection. **Residual:** the series does not show the full alternative life that an earlier safe intervention might have enabled.

### Belonging can sustain care and harmful commitment together

Twice's refusal of Hawks's restart offer is intelligible because the offered safety excludes the relationships that gave his life value. His concern for comrades is real, as is the danger of his power. V27:p149 locates the lethal strike; the final clone's rescue and gratitude do not establish that all League action was good. Spinner's postwar insistence on telling Shigaraki's story likewise preserves a counter-memory from within a harmful coalition, not a clean pacifist conversion. [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Twice V27 and Spinner V42; [Relationship state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), League belonging; [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md), affiliation.

**Strong inference:** affiliation is not merely ideological agreement. It can make costly behavior intelligible even when a group's public doctrine is unstable or externally assigned. Spinner's role as a heteromorph symbol exceeds his own elaborated program, and his bodily impairment changes execution. The hospital crowd includes participants capable of reconsidering; Shoji's later shared credit at V42:p132 makes their decision visible. Neither Spinner nor Shoji can stand in for every heteromorph person's motivation.

### Care without limits can become another form of domination

Chisaki's attempt to restore his boss's world overrides the boss and uses Eri. AFO's V42:p014–015 need for Yoichi remains expressed through ownership and refusal of departure. Garaki's enabling of destructive self-expression supplies another counterexample to the assumption that affirmation itself is care. The liberation movement's hierarchy complicates a doctrine that claims to free all Quirk expression. [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Chisaki/AFO/Garaki/Re-Destro; [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md), affirmation and hierarchy; [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), jurisdiction.

**Interpretation:** what matters is not only whether someone sees and encourages a desire, but whether they can accept another person's limits and independent ends. This keeps villain formation from becoming a demand for unconditional affirmation. It also prevents every villain's pathology from being described as lack of love: some have powerful attachments that are themselves possessive or selectively moral.

### Negative controls limit, rather than erase, social explanation

Shoto, Shinso, Kota and Bakugo encounter different forms of harm, stigma or misrecognition without following the same antagonist path. Their cases refute deterministic universal rules, but do not prove structural conditions irrelevant. Support, timing, resources and individual decisions differ. Muscular supplies a further limit: where the observed record emphasizes appetite for violence, inventing a universal wounded-child redemption mechanism exceeds evidence. [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md), negative controls and `VF-V08` entries; [Recognition and failed rescue ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RECOGNITION_FAILED_RESCUE_LEDGER.md), Stain/Muscular/Kota.

The preventive conclusion is therefore targeted. Family pressure calls for different interventions from institutional assassination, Quirk stigma, social isolation or deliberate criminal grooming. V42's new child receiving a civilian response and its supported counseling program depict possible changes in those pathways, not a guarantee that engineered harm, prejudice and violent appetite can never recur. V42:p119–123,p150; [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md), reform; [Family and civilians ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md), new child.

This synthesis supports understanding without acquittal, and prevention without omniscience. ## How formation becomes affiliation, strategy and organization

### Shigaraki's leadership develops through disagreements he cannot simply erase

The early League does not begin with a fully shared doctrine. AFO's patient response to failed action gives Shigaraki room to learn while keeping that room materially dependent on a predecessor. Stain's appearance then humiliates and instructs him without producing ideological agreement. The media's circulation of Stain's image brings recruits to a group whose leader resents the borrowed attraction. Recruitment can therefore be caused by an image that neither accurately represents the organization nor supplies its members with identical purposes. [V03](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V03_DEEP_READING.md), villain mentorship; [V06](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V06_DEEP_READING.md); [V07](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V07_DEEP_READING.md), Stain and recruitment.

The mall encounter with Midoriya is a consequential intermediate stage. Shigaraki presses an adversary to explain a difference he cannot initially articulate, then identifies a common symbolic target for his hatred. Midoriya's distinction between an intelligible ideal and acceptable conduct prevents that inquiry from becoming moral vindication. Later attack planning tests the new conviction through target selection and coordination. The failed attempt to recruit Bakugo also forces a limit: reading an angry public image does not reveal a willingness to abandon heroic victory. Shigaraki can ask for help after failure without yet possessing an independent organization. AFO's subsequent account of how absence will educate him remains a prediction, tested by later conduct. [V08](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V08_DEEP_READING.md), mall; [V09](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V09_DEEP_READING.md); [V10](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V10_DEEP_READING.md), attack and captivity; [V11](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V11_DEEP_READING.md), AFO's withdrawal.

Nor is the League a universal sanctuary. Dabi's destructive filtering of prospective members differs from Twice's attraction to a place where he can belong. Magne's defense of her chosen place then collides with Overhaul's demand for subordination. Shigaraki delays retaliation after her death because immediate expression of anger would endanger the group. This is an acquired capacity to manage grievance, not consent to the rival's hierarchy. It makes later delegation to Toga and Twice intelligible: their particular anger is allowed strategic scope, and their initiative helps turn imposed cooperation against the imposer. [V13](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V13_DEEP_READING.md), recruitment; [V14](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V14_DEEP_READING.md), Magne and Overhaul; [V16](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V16_DEEP_READING.md); [V17](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V17_DEEP_READING.md), delegated action; [V18](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V18_DEEP_READING.md), retaliation and seizure of technology.

**Interpretation:** destructive leadership matures when it can convert unlike motives into coordinated action. That development should not be praised as ethical growth merely because it involves trust, restraint and learning. The same abilities increase the reach of harmful purposes. AFO's later violation of Tomura's agency is more consequential because the intervening development has established something real to violate. [The Shigaraki literary study](MHA_SP2_SHIGARAKI_TOMURA_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) owns that whole trajectory and the changing disclosure of its conditions.

### Organizations require more than emotionally legible members

The Hassaikai supplies an internal comparison. Attachment to the old boss, loyalty to Chisaki, recognition received by a subordinate, friendship among fighters and appetite for combat are not one contract. Nemoto's investment in selected recognition can coexist with an abusive structure. Chisaki's claim to act for the boss does not grant permission to override that boss or use Eri. These distinctions explain why the organization can command costly acts without being held together by universal love or a uniform ideology. [V16](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V16_DEEP_READING.md), Hassaikai affiliations; [V17](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V17_DEEP_READING.md); [V18](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V18_DEEP_READING.md), boss, Eri and aftermath.

The League's later poverty and need for resources make a complementary point. Nomination as successor does not automatically transfer the loyalty of retainers; Giran's broker obligations, Spinner's questioning and Garaki's conditions mediate access. The MLA adds corporate capacity, political connections, media production and information about consumers. Curious's attempt to turn Toga into a usable biographical emblem is not simply misunderstanding her feelings: it is an organizational effort to appropriate the meaning of a person for a cause. [V23](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V23_DEEP_READING.md), resources, Giran and predecessor infrastructure; [V24](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V24_DEEP_READING.md), MLA and Curious.

Re-Destro subsequently interprets his defeat through his own doctrine, allowing an alliance in which Shigaraki's charisma and destruction, MLA infrastructure and AFO-derived technology remain distinguishable. The PLF is not evidence that every member adopted the same program or that Shigaraki became a faithful reader of liberation doctrine. Its practical capacity comes from combining unlike systems, then directing them toward destabilization. A causal explanation therefore needs resources, mediation, discipline and institutional transfer alongside wounds and belonging. [V25](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V25_DEEP_READING.md), recognition and incorporation; [V26](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V26_DEEP_READING.md), the larger program. [S4](MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_AND_DISTRIBUTED_RESPONSIBILITY_SYNTHESIS.md) retains the comparison with professional and civic networks.

### Toya's original attachment both resists capture and sustains revenge

The V35 disclosure alters the bridge between the childhood catastrophe and Dabi. After rescue and bodily reconstruction, Toya initially wants to return and apologize. He rejects the substitute family and power education offered within AFO's system. His attachment to his own family thus prevents one attempted appropriation; he is not simply manufactured into a willing replacement successor. The later secret return, however, makes the continued training of Shoto and the memorial legible to him as proof of replacement. The family does not knowingly reject a living child who has openly returned. That distinction matters: the interpretation through which Toya redirects his life is his response to what he sees, under conditions created by earlier family harm and the intervening absence. [V35](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V35_DEEP_READING.md), Toya's recovered history; villain formation and family ledgers, V35.

**Interpretation:** a surviving attachment can be protective against one controller while remaining organized around a destructive measure of worth. Dabi's self-directed revenge is not autonomy purified of dependence; it continues to demand recognition from the father whose project injured him. The later public spectacle addresses a national audience as well as that father, converting intimate history into an attack on heroic legitimacy. Explanation must preserve both the specific original authority imbalance and Toya's later selection of victims, means and audiences. [S6](MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CARE_AND_ORDINARY_LIFE_SYNTHESIS.md) owns the differentiated family responses; [V30](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V30_DEEP_READING.md); [V31](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V31_DEEP_READING.md), broadcast and family reckoning.

League relations also prevent reducing Dabi to a solitary function. Toga receives consequential care from comrades, and Dabi can convert that care and Twice's memory into battle utility. Strategic use does not establish that every affection in the group is false. The more disturbing mechanism is that real attachment can be recruited into harm. At the end, Toya's narrow Shoto-directed response supplies neither a general moral transformation nor evidence of a healthy future that the text does not show. [V35](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V35_DEEP_READING.md), Toga and Dabi; [V42](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V42_DEEP_READING.md), p062–064,p167.

### AFO and Spinner mark different limits on explanatory control

AFO's extensive engineering of Tenko's conditions does not prove he originates every social injury elsewhere in the work. Nagant's institutional formation has another causal owner; Toya rejects the offered substitute; the League's affiliations develop motives the predecessor does not fully control. His ability to exploit those histories is formidable without being universal authorship. Likewise, his attachment to Yoichi is not absent merely because it remains possessive. **Interpretation:** understanding and even needing another person can intensify domination when their refusal is treated as an error the controller is entitled to correct. [V32](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V32_DEEP_READING.md), Nagant; [V35](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V35_DEEP_READING.md), Toya; [V41](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V41_DEEP_READING.md); [V42](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V42_DEEP_READING.md), AFO's claims and endpoint. [S3](MHA_SP2_POWER_INHERITANCE_AND_SELF_AUTHORSHIP_SYNTHESIS.md) owns the pedagogy and inheritance comparison.

Spinner must be disaggregated differently. His experienced stigma, admiration for a particular companion, the symbolic role organizers impose, and the cognitive effects of enhancement belong to different explanatory levels. The hospital crowd is not merely his personality enlarged. Its members bring real grievances and can reconsider a method when confronted by the people it will harm. Postwar commitment to telling Shigaraki's story preserves a situated loyalty; it is neither proof of pacifism nor evidence that none of the crowd's choices mattered. [V37](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V37_DEEP_READING.md), hospital mobilization; [V42](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V42_DEEP_READING.md), Spinner and shared credit.

**Counterreading adjudication.** A generalized recognition failure does identify several moral wrongs, but cannot replace this organizational history. Selective recognition can recruit, genuine care can bind a harmful coalition, and successful coordination can amplify violence. Prevention must address the particular mechanism and available alternatives. The existing rescue/personhood and reform homes retain the cross-case judgment; no separate formation/accountability or adversarial file is required.

The three books disclose different amounts rather than three equally complete origins. UA's threat files leave Shigaraki's identity, Toga's blood mechanism and several causal relations unknown; UAN supplies more history but still cannot represent the later disclosure of AFO's engineering as knowledge those earlier accounts possessed. ## Evidence status and corpus role

This specialist owns the subject argument named above. Its primary ledger responsibilities are assigned in the [fourteen-ledger disposition](MHA_SP2_SPECIALIST_SYNTHESIS_INDEX.md). **Textual fact** identifies depicted action or attributed speech; **strong inference** identifies an implication supported by converging evidence; **interpretation** states the analytical judgment; **residual** marks what remains unshown or unsettled. A character's explanation is a fact that the character gives that explanation, not automatically a true account of the world.

Primary citations use the [logical-page locator](../07%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/MHA_SP2_PRIMARY_SOURCE_LOCATOR.md). The [source audit](../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/MHA_SP2_PUBLICATION_AND_SOURCE_REVERIFICATION_AUDIT.md) distinguishes this continuation's inspections from inherited direct review, locks source bytes and excludes paratext.
