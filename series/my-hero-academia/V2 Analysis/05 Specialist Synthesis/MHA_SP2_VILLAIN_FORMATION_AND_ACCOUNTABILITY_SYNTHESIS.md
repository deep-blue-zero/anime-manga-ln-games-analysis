# MHA SP2 — Villain formation and accountability

## Villain formation is a set of interacting pathways

**Interpretation.** MHA supplies neither a single “society made them do it” explanation nor a collection of isolated bad essences. Formation involves bodies, families, classification, neglect, political recruitment, affiliation, direct engineering and choices that continue afterward. The [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md) and [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md) are the primary owners: `VF-V08-1`–`VF-V08-6`, the segmented V25 origin analysis, and each antagonist's later state changes. Explanation can identify failures that invite prevention without transferring every later act to an impersonal cause.

### A comparative causal map

| Case | Mechanism the corpus supports | Simplification the case resists |
|---|---|---|
| Tenko / Shigaraki | Family violence and abandonment within AFO's deliberately engineered conditions; later League affiliation and resistance | Either untouched spontaneous childhood destiny or a person with no later will |
| Toya / Dabi | Instrumental parental ambition, Quirk/body mismatch, experienced rejection, later self-directed revenge | All family members equally responsible; all later violence mechanically compelled |
| Toga | Embodied desire under coercive normalization, concealment, violent choices and highly particular attachment | Quirk determinism, universal innocence, or universal benevolence after recognition |
| Twice | Precarity, identity disruption and a group that provides consequential belonging | Irrational refusal of an obviously equivalent safe future |
| Spinner | Stigma and isolation joined to affiliation, political instrumentalization and imposed bodily change | All mobilized heteromorphs share one motive or impairment was timeless personality |
| Nagant | Violence demanded and concealed by the HPSC, disillusionment, later AFO exploitation | AFO originally caused every institutional abuse |
| Gentle | Failed rescue, damaged aspiration and recognition-seeking, later feasible rehabilitation | Good intention equals competence; wrongdoing precludes later useful action |

### Designed conditions do not exhaust continuing authorship

The V25 origin sequence establishes the child's environment and catastrophic development; V41 later revises how spontaneous that causal chain can be considered. AFO's p132 account claims extensive engineering and cultivation. It is a fact that he makes this claim and the narrative supplies major support for engineered conditions; his assertion that no choices belong to Tomura must still be tested against other depicted evidence. Nana's blocking at p110, Tenko's contradiction at p116, League-directed intent at p121–123 and Tomura's terminal re-emergence at V42:p018 prevent an unrestricted substitution of AFO's authorship for the person's own. [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md), V25/V41; [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Shigaraki/AFO; [Recognition and failed rescue ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RECOGNITION_FAILED_RESCUE_LEDGER.md), final rescue.

**Interpretation:** constrained authorship is still authorship, but it is not the same as freedom from manipulation. The manga makes it possible to condemn the engineering, recognize the harmed child, and hold later destructive choices in view. The strongest unresolved question is counterfactual: what could a surviving Tomura have chosen under safe, noncoercive conditions? The text supplies no rehabilitated adult sample. It cannot be solved either by assuming inevitable evil or by inventing certain acceptance of rescue.

### Bodies condition desire without dictating ethics

Toya's body frustrates the successor role imposed upon him. Toga's relation to blood and resemblance shapes desire and language. Spinner's enhancements alter cognition and performance. Those are not interchangeable mechanisms, and none licenses reading every action as the direct output of a Quirk. Shoto's history and Shinso's stigmatized ability provide negative controls against a one-step bodily destiny; their different opportunities and relationships also prevent treating them as perfectly matched experiments. [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md), Quirk/body and negative-control entries; [Class 1-A ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_CLASS_1A_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Shoto/Shinso; [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Toya/Toga/Spinner.

The Toga encounter particularly requires an ethical distinction. Ochako can acknowledge an ordinary desire and the person expressing it while refusing to affirm all harmful conduct (V39:p098–120). Toga's later fatal gift is directed to that person under exceptional conditions (p125–139), not proof of a timeless rule of universal altruism. Recognition changes possibilities; it does not reveal that killings were only misunderstood affection. **Residual:** the series does not show the full alternative life that an earlier safe intervention might have enabled.

### Belonging can sustain care and harmful commitment together

Twice's refusal of Hawks's restart offer is intelligible because the offered safety excludes the relationships that gave his life value. His concern for comrades is real, as is the danger of his power. V27:p149 locates the lethal strike; the final clone's rescue and gratitude do not establish that all League action was good. Spinner's postwar insistence on telling Shigaraki's story likewise preserves a counter-memory from within a harmful coalition, not a clean pacifist conversion. [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Twice V27 and Spinner V42; [Relationship state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), League belonging; [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md), affiliation.

**Strong inference:** affiliation is not merely ideological agreement. It can make costly behavior intelligible even when a group's public doctrine is unstable or externally assigned. Spinner's role as a heteromorph symbol exceeds his own elaborated program, and his bodily impairment changes execution. The hospital crowd includes participants capable of reconsidering; Shoji's later shared credit at V42:p132 makes their decision visible. Neither Spinner nor Shoji can stand in for every heteromorph person's motivation.

### Care without limits can become another form of domination

Chisaki's attempt to restore his boss's world overrides the boss and uses Eri. AFO's V42:p014–015 need for Yoichi remains expressed through ownership and refusal of departure. Garaki's enabling of destructive self-expression supplies another counterexample to the assumption that affirmation itself is care. The liberation movement's hierarchy complicates a doctrine that claims to free all Quirk expression. [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Chisaki/AFO/Garaki/Re-Destro; [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md), affirmation and hierarchy; [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), jurisdiction.

**Interpretation:** what matters is not only whether someone sees and encourages a desire, but whether they can accept another person's limits and independent ends. This keeps villain formation from becoming a demand for unconditional affirmation. It also prevents every villain's pathology from being described as lack of love: some have powerful attachments that are themselves possessive or selectively moral.

### Negative controls limit, rather than erase, social explanation

Shoto, Shinso, Kota and Bakugo encounter different forms of harm, stigma or misrecognition without following the same antagonist path. Their cases refute deterministic universal rules, but do not prove structural conditions irrelevant. Support, timing, resources and individual decisions differ. Muscular supplies a further limit: where the observed record emphasizes appetite for violence, inventing a universal wounded-child redemption mechanism exceeds evidence. [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md), negative controls and `VF-V08` entries; [Recognition and failed rescue ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RECOGNITION_FAILED_RESCUE_LEDGER.md), Stain/Muscular/Kota.

The preventive conclusion is therefore targeted. Family pressure calls for different interventions from institutional assassination, Quirk stigma, social isolation or deliberate criminal grooming. V42's new child receiving a civilian response and its supported counseling program depict possible changes in those pathways, not a guarantee that engineered harm, prejudice and violent appetite can never recur. V42:p119–123,p150; [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md), reform; [Family and civilians ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md), new child.

This synthesis supports understanding without acquittal, and prevention without omniscience.
