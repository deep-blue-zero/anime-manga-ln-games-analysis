This document adjudicates V1 statements against the completed Japanese-primary V2 corpus. It does not rewrite the V1 files, invent convenient mistakes, or count every sharper locator as a new interpretation. The scope is a substantive reconciliation of the major claims bearing on the final questions, not a claim to have line-audited every V1 artifact.

**Survived** means the core claim remains defensible. **Strengthened** means more precise primary evidence gives an existing claim firmer or wider support. **Revised** means the proposition needs a changed scope, causal structure or qualification. **Rejected** means the stated version cannot stand, while a narrower proposition may survive. These are analytical judgments; textual facts and residuals are specified in each adjudication. Quoted snippets below identify actual wording, not fabricated strawmen.

## Claims that survived or strengthened

### CR01 — Heroism precedes overwhelming power: survived

V1-FS §1 already locates heroism in Midoriya's attention and movement before OFA; V1-42, “Deku losing One For All is not narrative punishment,” preserves identity after loss. V01:p043–059 and V42:p134,p159 support that continuity. V2 adds an important limit: early impulse still needs another's physical capacity, and the adult suit changes access to a desired practice. The core claim survives; “power is irrelevant” would not. [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), V01/V42; [Recognition and failed rescue ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RECOGNITION_FAILED_RESCUE_LEDGER.md), early rescue.

### CR02 — Reaching does not simply absolve: survived

V1-42 explicitly titles its section “saving without sentimental absolution,” and V1-FS already retains deaths and unequal rescue outcomes. V41:p116,p121–123 and V42:p018,p030–035 support recognition alongside continuing affiliation, harm and failed life-saving. V2 must not claim it newly discovered that Tenko dies or that recognizing him differs from acquittal. It strengthens the life/heart distinction while preserving grief and uncertainty about an unshown rehabilitated future. [Recognition and failed rescue ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RECOGNITION_FAILED_RESCUE_LEDGER.md), final rescue; [First-pass correction ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FIRST_PASS_CORRECTION_LEDGER.md), V42.

### CR03 — Public success cannot erase private injury: survived

V1-26/27 and V1-FS §5 already distinguish Endeavor's achievements from family accountability and preserve non-forgiveness. V26:p083–092, V39's family intervention and V42:p055–069 sharpen asymmetric responses. V2's added precision is relational: Shoto's initiative, Toya's narrow apology and Natsuo's boundary are separate acts. It is not a correction of an alleged V1 claim that everyone forgives, because that is not the claim these sources made. [Family and civilians ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md), Todoroki family; [Relationship state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), father/children.

### CR04 — Competence and courage are learned: survived, with limits

V1-08/09 already treats Full Cowl and training as movement toward usable power; V1-16/17 already distinguishes Kirishima's courage from fearlessness and gives Tamaki anxious competence. V2's longitudinal records and V36:p173–179,p181 extend that account to changed use of received belief. A grouped V1 volume title is not itself an event-location error. V1-08/09 states, “The final-exam arc completes the Full Cowl transition.” This is defensible as an arc-specific judgment; V2 scopes it to that step in bodily/tactical learning while preserving subsequent adaptations. [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), `P-V08` entries; [U.A. students and staff ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Tamaki; [Class 1-A ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_CLASS_1A_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Kirishima.

### CR05 — Teaching, enacted support and ordinary More: strengthened

V1-42 already covers teaching, Bakugo's support and the ordinary `More` chapter. V42:p140–141,p149,p151,p153,p159,p167–168,p180–183 now supplies differentiated evidence: the collective suit, Tamaki's care/correctability, teaching even with retained OFA, Shoto's ordinary interests and mutual approach. This is stronger grounding of an existing interpretation, not V2's invention of the coda's significance. [First-pass correction ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FIRST_PASS_CORRECTION_LEDGER.md), V42; [U.A. students and staff ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), adult life; [Family and civilians ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md), ordinary future.

## Claims whose final form changes

### CR06 — “Distributed inheritance replaces sacred inheritance”: revised

That phrase appears in an actual V1-42 heading; V1-FS §1 likewise contrasts the suit with inheritance from one chosen mentor. The opposition overstates a break. OFA already contains plural holders, transferred labor, instruction and relations; the suit makes a different material network and a broader kind of support explicit. V31:p164–174 and V42:p140–141,p159 support **a change in the architecture of enabling capacity**, not the first arrival of social production. The retained ethical question is whether contributors enable a user's chosen future rather than own it. [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), OFA/V42.

### CR07 — “It taught people to wait”: revised causal scope

V1-42's full-series thesis uses that sentence about All Might's age. The concentration of hope and expectation is defensible, but the formulation is too compressed if it makes All Might the sole teacher or cause of inaction. V01:p043–059 depicts actual technical constraints as well as spectatorship; family violence, stigma and HPSC secrecy have additional mechanisms. V42:p118–123 widens participation while retaining institutions. The replacement is a causal claim about **reinforced dependence within a wider system**, preserving All Might's real benefits and responsibility without assigning every failure to him. [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md), Symbol/collapse; [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md), heterogeneous pathways.

### CR08 — Rescue as a sequential ladder: revised

V1-FS §10 moves from physical rescue to psychological, moral, joy, reintegration and emotional recognition using repeated “then.” As a mnemonic for emphasis it is useful; as developmental replacement it is misleading. Emotional attention is already decisive in V01, and technical competence remains indispensable in V38–V42. V2 therefore treats these as overlapping dimensions with uneven outcomes, not successive stages the story leaves behind. V01:p043–059; V18:p088–090; V38:p054–059,p065–068; V42:p030–035. [Recognition and failed rescue ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RECOGNITION_FAILED_RESCUE_LEDGER.md), stage distinctions and final outcome entries.

### CR09 — “He probably would have been an excellent One For All successor”: rejected unqualified

V1-FS §12 applies that sentence to Mirio. His ethical and technical qualities remain credible, and the earlier V1-16/17 candidate discussion was written within a different knowledge boundary. But the completed-series claim omits V31:p164–174: already Quirk-bearing recipients face an accumulated-power compatibility problem. A final claim of uncomplicated suitability is rejected. The narrower statement is **ethically plausible candidate, biologically problematic under the later revealed mechanism**. Exact lifespan remains unknown. V18:p088–090 supports his protective judgment without canceling that distinction. [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), V31; [U.A. students and staff ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Mirio.

### CR10 — AFO “cannot understand relationships except as control”: revised

V1-FS §7 states this strongly. V42:p014–015 and the broader manipulation record support intense possessive need, but also strategic comprehension of others' attachments. The defect is not literal inability to recognize that relationships exist or that he needs someone. It is refusal to accept another as an independent center whose rejection can bind him. The final account distinguishes cognitive understanding, affective dependence and ethical recognition. No healthy reciprocal AFO relationship is invented. [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), AFO; [Relationship state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), Yoichi; [Recognition and failed rescue ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RECOGNITION_FAILED_RESCUE_LEDGER.md), manipulative recognition.

### CR11 — Nagant in the AFO instrumentalization list: revised causal attribution

V1-FS §2 places “Nagant is a hero turned into a covert state weapon” inside an account of AFO turning people into functions. The sentence correctly identifies instrumentalization but the surrounding list can misassign its original agent. The HPSC produced the covert role; AFO later exploited her disillusionment. V32's Nagant history and V38:p065–068 retain both stages. V2 separates similar instrumental logics from identical causal ownership. [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Nagant; [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md), HPSC; [Villain formation ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_FORMATION_LEDGER.md), institutional formation.

### CR12 — Final OFA “giving”: revised recipient boundary

V1-FS §2 calls the final transfer a meaningful extension of voluntary giving and already names it intervention. V2 sharpens the asymmetry: the transferring side can voluntarily expend itself while the recipient resists. The claim survives only if “voluntary” describes the holders' side, not mutual consent. V40–V41's final transfer and V41:p110,p116,p121–123 show an intervention aimed at reaching through conflict; they do not make the act ethically simple merely because power is given. [Power philosophy ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_POWER_PHILOSOPHY_LEDGER.md), final expenditure; [Recognition and failed rescue ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RECOGNITION_FAILED_RESCUE_LEDGER.md), jurisdiction.

### CR13 — Hawks “Strategically, he is probably right”: revised evidentiary strength

V1-FS §11 makes that actual judgment. The threat posed by Double and the immediate tactical predicament support a serious strategic case; Twice's particular belonging explains why the offered restart fails. V27:p149 confirms the strike, while V28:p060–077 gives the subsequent extraction and Tokoyami's belief. Neither proves that every alternative was impossible or that rescue of Hawks is a verdict exonerating the killing. The final claim is **a constrained tactical rationale with unresolved counterfactual alternatives**, not a completed necessity proof. [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Hawks; [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Twice; [Recognition and failed rescue ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_RECOGNITION_FAILED_RESCUE_LEDGER.md), failed rescue.

### CR14 — “Dabi survives but is ruined”: revised endpoint

V1-FS §10 accurately describes an immediate postwar state but is incomplete as the final V42 endpoint. The medically constrained p062–064 exchange is followed, in the adult setting, by Toya's altar at p167. Death by then is a strong inference. V2 must neither preserve survival as the final state without qualification nor invent an exact death scene/date. The particular apology remains distinct from death, forgiveness and broad reform. [Family and civilians ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md), V42; [Villains and antagonists ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Toya; [First-pass correction ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FIRST_PASS_CORRECTION_LEDGER.md), endpoint discipline.

### CR15 — Structural repair is mainly gestured toward: strengthened and narrowed

V1-FS §17 says the ending “gestures toward” institutions, policy, material support and accountability, and allows that some readers may find it more symbolic than concrete. The critical reservation remains legitimate, but V42:p118–119,p132,p140–141,p150,p159 supplies actual mechanisms and differentiated work. V2 should name staffing, ministry/sponsor support, regional practice, technical resources and teaching before judging their adequacy. The revised position is **concrete partial reform without complete outcome evidence**, rather than either symbolic-only repair or a solved society. [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md), V42; [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Hawks; [U.A. students and staff ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), teaching.

## V2 self-correction is a different category

The Hawks V27 extraction leak, Shoji's p148 kimono-behavior claim, vague Tamaki adult evidence, and archive/attribution problems are not assigned to V1 by convenience. They are documented corrections to V2's own evidence handling. Current notices preserve the older body text and exclude contaminated material from use. The Hawks exercise remains contaminated after correction; unchanged qualitative comparisons do not turn it into a clean experiment. [First-pass correction ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FIRST_PASS_CORRECTION_LEDGER.md), current addendum; source audit SR01–SR10; aggregate validation HK-V01.

V1-FS's character “philosophies” in §16 are interpretive condensations, not direct manga quotations. They may be compared as judgments but cannot become Japanese voice evidence. Likewise Nighteye's hypothesis at V18:p072 is not established physics merely because it fits a thematic account of many people changing a future. This discipline preserves the genuinely useful V1 inheritance while giving V2's source method practical force.

**Residual:** this adjudication does not settle every reasonable literary disagreement or verify every V1 sentence. It resolves the major actual claims that bear on the authorized full-series questions, identifies limits, and leaves historical files intact. [Form, revision and residuals](../06%20Full-Series%20Synthesis/MHA_SP2_FORM_REVISION_AND_RESIDUALS.md) explains how these changes affect the definitive interpretation.

A character's explanation is a fact that the character gives that explanation, not automatically a true account of the world.
