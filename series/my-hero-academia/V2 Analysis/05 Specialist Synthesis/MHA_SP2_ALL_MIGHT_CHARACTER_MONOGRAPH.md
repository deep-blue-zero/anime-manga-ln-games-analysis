# MHA SP2 — All Might: the person who must outlive indispensability

**Governing question:** How does a real public ideal become a teachable, dependent person and continuing mentor without becoming either an obsolete function or an innocent abstraction?

## The Symbol's reality is the condition of its problem

**Interpretation.** All Might is neither an empty reassuring image nor a solution whose moral purity excuses every consequence of concentration. His exceptional action genuinely saves people and expands what they can imagine. The same success helps organize expectations around a body that cannot indefinitely sustain them. His individual history asks how a person formed by that purpose can change his teaching, accept dependence and continue to live when being indispensable is no longer available.

V01 first gives Midoriya—and the reader—access to the damaged body beneath the admired form. All Might's initial advice is constrained by knowledge of professional danger; his later response to Midoriya's impulsive intervention revises what he recognizes as a possible beginning. The revision matters because the ideal is capable of being taught by someone who lacks its power. Yet the rescue still requires All Might's actual capacity. The scene joins responsive moral attention to material asymmetry rather than allowing either to explain the whole outcome. [V01](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V01_DEEP_READING.md), p022–085.

V02 then exposes a different limit: knowing how to perform an extraordinary act does not mean knowing how to teach it. His indulgence toward a cherished successor and willingness to exceed bodily limits complicate the role of educator. Recovery Girl's criticism matters because a good intention toward the student can still leave the student inadequately prepared and repeatedly injured. The public rescuer needs colleagues who can judge a practice he cannot evaluate simply by its emotional purpose. [V02](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V02_DEEP_READING.md), school assessment, injuries and USJ.

## Succession begins with reproduction and becomes more permissive

The V03 public succession demand is generous but exacting. All Might wants another presence that can reassure society after him, and the festival offers a stage on which the successor can become visible. The parallel with Shigaraki's mentor is structural: both understand that a successor has to be made socially legible. Their opposed aims do not mean that only one places a burden on a learner. Midoriya initially receives a model of public worth as well as power. [V03](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V03_DEEP_READING.md), successor framing.

Subsequent teaching begins to loosen literal reproduction. V05 permits divergence from the admired figure; Gran Torino supplies bodily understanding All Might cannot simply transmit. These changes are not a rejection of mentorship but a redistribution of its sources. Midoriya can be helped to become someone recognizable as his successor without being required to become the same body or method. [V05](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V05_DEEP_READING.md), response after the festival; [V06](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V06_DEEP_READING.md), Gran Torino.

All Might's emotional disclosure develops less smoothly. In V07 he gives historical information about OFA and AFO, then cannot tell the trusting student that he probably will not remain beside him when the inherited conflict arrives. Care here produces withholding without being identical to AFO's possessive control. It nevertheless limits the information with which Midoriya can prepare. **Interpretation:** the good mentor's failure must be described in its own terms, not made invisible because a worse mentor exists. [V07](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V07_DEEP_READING.md), p115–126.

V12 supplies an explicit pedagogical principle: diagnosing imitation and requiring the learner to think are part of education. The comically earnest teaching manual and the student's turn toward support expertise make his learning process visible. V19 then applies that policy to a new technical problem, while All Might admits that his own early ease with full power cannot answer Midoriya's bodily difficulty. He can help the student recombine experience, practice and equipment without claiming the resulting method as his own. [V12](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V12_DEEP_READING.md), p020–021,p044–050; [V19](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V19_DEEP_READING.md), p089–094,p117–120.

The qualification at V19:p120 also matters: he accepts equipment as a useful supplement while warning against overdependence. This is a mentor revising a practical relation to support, not suddenly discovering that every external device is liberating. The later armored return must be read against this history of considered, imperfectly transferable expertise.

## The promise to remain alive changes the measure of care

After Kamino, the loss of the Symbol's familiar function does not end responsibility toward the successor. Inko distinguishes support for her child's aspiration from consent to the conditions U.A. has supplied. All Might must answer an adult demand for continuing care, not simply offer a grand declaration of sacrifice. The promise to live and guide gives duration a value that spectacular protection alone cannot satisfy. [V11](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V11_DEEP_READING.md), Inko and school; [V15](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V15_DEEP_READING.md), the mentor's threatened future.

The Nighteye history sharpens the difficulty of acting under a predicted endpoint. All Might's desire to change that future is not proof that courage controls fate; the later overturned forecast and Nighteye's own explanation leave the mechanism uncertain. What is secure is the change in commitment: remaining with a student can be chosen as an obligation rather than experienced only as failure to die usefully. [V15](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V15_DEEP_READING.md); [V18](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V18_DEEP_READING.md), prediction and aftermath.

V26 allows that decision to coexist with continued pain. All Might researches predecessor history and produces a notebook for a student whose powers exceed his own experience. He also admits feeling unable to do what he once could. Aizawa's reassurance identifies his living presence as consequential in itself. Midoriya's pleasure that the mentor remains nearby supplies another perspective on the same value. **Strong inference:** personal significance survives the loss of the action that once made him nationally indispensable. The scene does not imply that he has nothing left to do; it prevents usefulness at the former scale from becoming the only measure of whether he should exist. [V26](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V26_DEEP_READING.md), p158–172.

## Care can fail even after the mentor has learned its value

The collapse puts ordinary support under conditions it cannot easily control. All Might's effort to remain with and care for Midoriya in V32 does not suffice to stop the student's flight into overextended responsibility. The failure is relational and practical, not proof that the care is insincere. It also reveals the limits of a mentor who helped form the aspiration now making the student difficult to reach. [V32](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V32_DEEP_READING.md), pursuit and separation.

The class's later intervention therefore does something All Might cannot accomplish alone. Accepting this wider field of help is part of the development of succession itself. The predecessor does not become irrelevant when peers perform the decisive relational work. Instead, the successor's life can acquire resources beyond the original pair. [V33](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V33_DEEP_READING.md), class intervention; [S1](MHA_SP2_CHARACTER_DEVELOPMENT_AND_RELATIONSHIPS_SYNTHESIS.md).

This middle-to-late history rules out a smooth progress story in which one good conversation resolves the problem of self-worth. All Might can sincerely choose life, learn to teach and still struggle when direct intervention is unavailable. A literary account should preserve that recurrence without calling it proof that nothing changed.

## The armored return distinguishes intention from an observer's reading

All Might's later fight reactivates a dangerous public role through prepared equipment and accumulated knowledge. It invites a serious counterreading: perhaps he has returned to the belief that only spectacular self-expenditure can justify his life. Tsukauchi's reading of the danger gives that concern a place inside the work. The analysis must nevertheless distinguish the observer's interpretation from All Might's own explicit intention to fight to win. V39:p146 and p155 are not interchangeable evidence of motive. [V39](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V39_DEEP_READING.md), p146,p154–155.

The equipment's relation to students' capacities also makes the action a record of having watched and learned. Dependence is materially present within the apparent return of the lone icon. Preparation does not make the risk safe or guarantee a favorable result, and a declared intention to win does not settle every ethical question about the choice. It does defeat a transparent reading in which the actor simply seeks death. The scene retains both purposeful agency and reasons for alarm.

The subsequent need for others continues that limit. The work does not let the retired Symbol reclaim a world in which one person's invincibility makes all assistance optional. **Interpretation:** his recognizable courage survives in a changed configuration of means, observation and support. The individual trajectory matters here because a purely institutional verdict—old Symbol either restored or abolished—would miss how the person acts within the new arrangement. [V40](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V40_DEEP_READING.md), threatened life and rescue.

## Enabling another future continues the vocation without owning it

The suit offered to adult Midoriya is assembled through an international, engineering, observational and financial network. All Might's role connects that history to the person it can enable. The offer does not erase Midoriya's teaching life or grant its contributors the authority to replace his chosen priorities. The later statement that teaching would still matter even with OFA prevents the gift from becoming a rescue from a supposedly worthless interim identity. [V42](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V42_DEEP_READING.md), p140–141,p159.

This is a mature form of the original offer because the recipient now brings an independently developed life to it. All Might can reopen a possibility without reproducing his original body or demanding that the beneficiary occupy a single social function. The persistence of his delight in enabling action is continuity; the changed terms of that enablement are development.

## Rival reading: a failed institution disguised as a kindly man

This reading correctly identifies brittle dependence, educational failures and the risk of turning self-expenditure into an ideal. It fails if those effects consume every instance of actual rescue, responsive learning or ordinary care. The opposite reading, in which good intentions make his guidance uniformly sound, fails on injury, withholding and his inability to reach Midoriya alone.

The more adequate judgment allows public achievement and personal fallibility to coexist. All Might revises what he can give: first power and an image, increasingly diagnosis, information, presence and access to other people's work. He does not fully cease struggling with lost capacity, and the text does not certify an untroubled retirement. His literary achievement lies in continuing as a person whose relations exceed his former function, while still wanting and sometimes finding consequential ways to act.
