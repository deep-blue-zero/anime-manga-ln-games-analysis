# MHA SP2 — Japanese language and longitudinal form

## Form controls what a claim can mean

**Interpretation.** Japanese wording, attribution, image sequence and recurring forms are part of the argument, not decoration added to a plot summary. They can support a reading while also limiting it: a name spoken in a mixed identity does not establish pure restoration, a visible child does not erase an adult's actions, and an offered hand can coerce as well as help. Primary owners are [Japanese vocabulary ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_JAPANESE_VOCABULARY_LEDGER.md), [Visual motifs ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_VISUAL_MOTIF_LEDGER.md) and [Callbacks and payoffs ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_CALLBACK_PAYOFF_LEDGER.md), including `VM-V08-1`–`VM-V08-8`, `CB-V08-1`–`CB-V08-11` and their longitudinal extensions.

### Vocabulary gains meaning through speakers and situations

The recurrent Japanese label `個性` connects an ability to socially interpreted difference, but it does not by itself assert that a Quirk determines moral character. Likewise `ヒーロー` moves among aspiration, occupation, recognition and a person's particular example. A single English equivalent cannot settle which function is active. **Interpretation:** the series expands uses of hero language while continuing to depict professional roles. Hawks's V42:p118 expanded recognition and Mirio's p042–043 account of a positive future make that plurality explicit without making every role technically identical. [Japanese vocabulary ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_JAPANESE_VOCABULARY_LEDGER.md), hero/Quirk vocabulary; [Hero society ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_LEDGER.md), V42.

Short, speaker-specific wording can be more decisive than an abstract label. Shoji's `俺は復讐者と思われたくない` at V37:p140 concerns how he refuses to be read and identified through revenge. It does not deny persecution or require universal forgiveness. Toya's `ごめんな` at V42:p064 is addressed to Shoto in a medically constrained exchange; the recipient and surrounding food question delimit the apology. The later `仏壇` context at p167 supports a strong inference of death, not an exact death scene. [Japanese vocabulary ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_JAPANESE_VOCABULARY_LEDGER.md), Shoji/Toya V37/V42; [Family and civilians ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md), terminal family.

### Attribution can reverse an apparent psychological conclusion

At V39:p146, Tsukauchi interprets All Might's extremely dangerous action. At p155, All Might states `私は過去一度たりとも負ける気で戦った事はない`. The latter supports an intention to fight to win; it does not establish that he always wins, is safe, or feels no fear. The former remains another person's reading of risk. **Textual fact:** two perspectives are present. **Interpretation:** conflating them manufactures a transparent suicidal motive that the actor's own statement complicates. [Japanese vocabulary ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_JAPANESE_VOCABULARY_LEDGER.md), All Might V39; [Professional heroes ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), corresponding comparison.

Other small attribution controls materially affect character models. Mirio apologizes aloud at V36:p170 and reconsiders internally at p171. Tamaki's reflection on receiving belief is p181, after coordinated action at p173–179; p180 is an author promotion. V41:p175's mutual-need statement belongs to Aoyama, whereas V42:p042–043's future/humor account belongs to Mirio. These are not interchangeable examples of a generic collective voice. [Japanese vocabulary ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_JAPANESE_VOCABULARY_LEDGER.md), final-war/V42; [U.A. students and staff ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/Character%20Group%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), Big Three and Aoyama.

Historical romanizations, escaped strings and broad quotation ranges in the cumulative ledger remain retrieval aids, not authority for exact glyphs. Direct quotation must return to the Japanese page and preserve whether the words are spoken, thought, narrated or authorial. Analytical shorthand such as “I am here becomes we are here” is a paraphrase of a proposed social transition, not an exact manga quotation.

### Stable register can carry changed ethics

Bakugo's abrasive speech persists through changes in rivalry, apology and enacted support. Tamaki retains hesitant social behavior alongside competence and a revised relation to praise. Tsuyu's directness can carry an explicit change of judgment. **Strong inference:** voice is neither a superficial costume nor an unchanging moral policy. A register prediction must be conditioned on interlocutor, public/private setting and bodily state. The [aggregate validation](../04%20Character%20Modeling%20and%20Reconstruction/MHA_SP2_AGGREGATE_RECONSTRUCTION_VALIDATION.md) therefore separates speech from motive and emotion, and preserves underdetermination rather than equating a familiar tone with a complete model. ### Hands and bodies have no automatic ethical meaning

Hands recur as rescue, imposed touch, inherited burden, violence and chosen contact. AFO's controlling relation to Tenko's history makes the visual grammar of reaching ethically ambiguous. V41:p110,p116,p121–123 combines intervention, contradiction and continuing affiliation; V42:p009–011 gives protection through a mixed Kurogiri relation. At V42:p182–183 mutual approach and hand contact belong to an ordinary future. The image of a hand cannot alone tell the reader whether the recipient is respected. **Interpretation:** recurrent form asks for comparison rather than mechanically supplying equivalence. Midoriya's early destructive use of his body, Eri's horn contribution and All Might's supported combat all bear bodily cost, yet age, consent, technical means and obligation differ. A visual resemblance does not make the cases ethically identical. Nighteye's framed future and its contradiction make foreknowledge appear authoritative before forcing revision. At V18:p038 the observed outcome differs; p072 proposes converging energy as an explanation. The narrative can stage uncertainty through a powerful visual device without certifying a new physical law. **Residual:** one disproven forecast and the character's hypothesis do not settle every mechanism of Foresight. ### Callbacks revise the meaning of earlier scenes

Repeated confrontation spaces, inherited names, rescue hands and the return of ordinary questions join the series longitudinally. Their function is often changed rather than merely completed: the same training ground can host a different relation; the admired Symbol becomes someone who can receive help; a rescuer becomes someone who must be reached; Shoto's self-authorship extends from using power to choosing tableware and enjoying food. `CB-V08-1`–`CB-V08-11`; [Callbacks and payoffs ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MHA_SP2_CALLBACK_PAYOFF_LEDGER.md), Ground Beta/All Might/Shoto; V42:p167–168.

**Interpretation:** these correspondences support a designed reading of the completed work. **Residual:** recurrence and payoff do not prove that each detail was planned from the beginning. AFO's later origin account can change the causal reading of earlier scenes without allowing an analyst to attribute all earlier uncertainty to deliberate authorial concealment. The method can describe what the finished sequence does without inventing its production history.

Both V42:p144 and p145 are afterword. They may be discussed as authorial framing, but they do not add in-world events or override narrative ambiguity. ## A longitudinal history of legibility, recurrence and withheld information

### Public images repeatedly separate action from the meaning assigned to it

The festival makes people legible through numbers, targets, names and performance before it shows how that legibility can fail. Midoriya's public success changes how others target him; the spectator account of Ochako and Bakugo's match conflicts with the tactical seriousness of the participants. Shoto's fire has a public competitive meaning and an intimate inherited meaning that the same visible act cannot collapse. The podium subsequently turns a nominal victory into an image that overrides the winner's own account of what winning requires. [V03](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V03_DEEP_READING.md)–[V05](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V05_DEEP_READING.md); visual and vocabulary ledgers, festival sections.

Stain's mediated image then gathers people without making their beliefs identical, and that borrowed image changes the League's opportunities. Later visibility can supply enemies with information, while Aoyama's beacon makes being seen a condition of rescue. The same formal family—display, attention, audience—therefore carries incompatible practical consequences. Meaning depends on who sees, what they know and what they can do with it. [V07](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V07_DEEP_READING.md), recruitment; [V09](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V09_DEEP_READING.md), exposed information; [V12](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V12_DEEP_READING.md), Aoyama's signal.

Festival spectators and the later professional ranking stage preserve this problem within legitimate institutions. Curious's attempted appropriation of Toga's story and Dabi's broadcast intensify it through an audience-directed claim about what a life proves. Those claims should be attributed to their makers before being adopted as the narrative's own causal verdict. Finally, student cameras around Gentle connect visibility to documentation and responsibility after his action has already acquired value without perceived applause. [V20](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V20_DEEP_READING.md); [V21](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V21_DEEP_READING.md), audiences and public hero image; [V24](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V24_DEEP_READING.md), Curious; [V30](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V30_DEEP_READING.md), Dabi; [V38](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V38_DEEP_READING.md), p081–083.

**Interpretation:** the sequence does not move from corrupt publicity to pure invisibility. It repeatedly contests who can author an image's meaning and what obligations seeing creates. **Counterreading:** public visibility can coordinate action, permit accountability and preserve testimony. That is positive evidence against an indiscriminate anti-media reading, not an exception requiring a separate theory. [S4](MHA_SP2_HERO_SOCIETY_AND_DISTRIBUTED_RESPONSIBILITY_SYNTHESIS.md) owns the institutional consequences.

### Repeated words require renewed attribution

Names and succession language organize relations rather than merely label fixed identities. The changed use of Midoriya's nickname depends on who speaks and how he takes it up; an insult's later chosen use does not retroactively remove the earlier insult. Likewise, inheritance and entrustment language can invite a future or load a recipient with a measure of adequacy. All Might's public succession demand, his later permission for a different method and AFO's claims over Tomura must be compared through their surrounding acts. Shared succession vocabulary cannot by itself establish equivalent pedagogy. [V01](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V01_DEEP_READING.md), naming; [V03](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V03_DEEP_READING.md), successor framing; [V05](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V05_DEEP_READING.md); [V12](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V12_DEEP_READING.md), teaching; [V41](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V41_DEEP_READING.md), claimed authorship; [S3](MHA_SP2_POWER_INHERITANCE_AND_SELF_AUTHORSHIP_SYNTHESIS.md).

The V11 next-person address demonstrates the effect concretely. All Might's gesture toward the camera can be received publicly as a warning to criminals and a continuing reassurance. Midoriya instead understands an exhausted predecessor's handoff: the private knowledge makes the same words carry loss and obligation. AFO then mirrors the address to his absent disciple after explaining how a mentor's removal is intended to cultivate independence through experience, hatred and grievance. The repeated address establishes a structural comparison; the surrounding actions make its ethical and emotional terms different. AFO's proposed result is still his prediction, not evidence that Shigaraki has already achieved it. [V11](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V11_DEEP_READING.md), p099–102,p105–107.

The persistence of the past receives an equally consequential reattribution in V30. Dabi uses the unerasable history to authorize a punitive collapse of heroic legitimacy. Midoriya accepts that the history does not disappear while insisting on observing Endeavor's present effort, and refuses Dabi's attempt to make his whole identity merely Endeavor's crime. The dispute is not between remembering and forgetting: it concerns what follows from the same retained past, who may prescribe the response, and whether a person's current actions can be reduced to another's sin. This difference would be lost if the recurring wording were treated as one stable thesis spoken by the work. [V30](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V30_DEEP_READING.md), p097,p150–151.

**Method:** each comparison must retain speaker, addressee, situation, speech/thought status and the point in disclosure. These are interpretive paraphrases of longitudinal usage, with the V11/V30 pages directly checked in the [maturation record](../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/MHA_SP2_MATURATION_IMPLEMENTATION_RECORD.md); exact Japanese wording still requires its individual page. Japanese vocabulary ledger, naming/succession/Todoroki entries; callbacks ledger, Kamino and family returns.

### Recurring places and bodies change the question being asked

Ground Beta first stages a contest in which Midoriya and Bakugo misunderstand or oppose one another's relation to ability and success. Its later return gives the rivalry a place to expose a different burden, followed by adult clarification rather than a magical settlement through fighting. Repetition makes the difference readable. The location is not proof that either confrontation solves everything, nor that the participants possess the same knowledge in both. [V02](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V02_DEEP_READING.md), battle training; [V13](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V13_DEEP_READING.md); [V14](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V14_DEEP_READING.md), duel and aftermath; callbacks ledger, Ground Beta.

The same principle governs hands, damaged bodies and child images. Visual recurrence invites comparison across Midoriya, Eri, Tenko and All Might, but cannot remove differences in power, age, coercion and the recipient's will. The image of an injured child can make earlier harm present within an adult conflict without turning every adult act into an innocent child's reflex. An ordinary hand at the endpoint can answer a history of rescue gestures without constituting an emergency rescue. The changed context is analytical content, not noise to be discarded in extracting a universal symbol.

### Disclosure order creates judgments that later evidence must revise, not erase

Vestige phenomena acquire changing explanatory status: an anomalous experience, a provisional account and later demonstrated internal agents are not equally available at every point. Nighteye's framed future gives a prediction apparent authority before the depicted outcome and a character's hypothesis force reconsideration. Neither the visual device nor the later explanation establishes an unrestricted law of fate. Power and interpretation develop together because readers and characters have different access to the mechanism. [V04](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V04_DEEP_READING.md), early anomaly; [V18](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V18_DEEP_READING.md), forecast and aftermath; [V21](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V21_DEEP_READING.md), vestige development; [V31](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/MHA_SP2_V31_DEEP_READING.md), holder deliberation.

The Tenko history is disclosed through successive revisions. Childhood catastrophe, later control and AFO's final engineering account change the causal interpretation of earlier material. They do not erase the actually depicted intermediate leadership, loyalties and chosen harm. Dabi's name is likewise withheld before the family history gives his public intervention a new weight. The finished sequence can be analyzed for the judgments it permits and overturns without claiming that every later detail was planned at the outset. Ochako's slowed attention to the person within a large power crisis supplies a complementary formal movement: the account changes scale without making the wider danger disappear. Later survivor memory belongs to her continuing interior life, not automatically to an independently acting dead character. **Interpretation:** language, image and temporal arrangement jointly control the same ethical claim about whose experience is being represented. Splitting their maintenance into separate specialist authorities would require repeated adjudication of that claim. ## Evidence status and corpus role

Its primary ledger responsibilities are assigned in the [fourteen-ledger disposition](MHA_SP2_SPECIALIST_SYNTHESIS_INDEX.md). **Textual fact** identifies depicted action or attributed speech; **strong inference** identifies an implication supported by converging evidence; **interpretation** states the analytical judgment; **residual** marks what remains unshown or unsettled.
