## Volume 1 major model change
































Volume 1 strengthens the first-pass thesis that heroism precedes power while shifting explanatory emphasis toward **recognition**: whether people and institutions can perceive heroic potential before conventional power makes it legible.
































## Volume 2 major model change
































Volume 2 shifts the early-series question from recognition to **judgment under power**.
































Key changes:
































- professional heroism is explicitly distinguished from raw victory or combat output;
- Thirteen defines Quirks as potentially lethal capacities that must be regulated, disciplined, and directed toward human life;
- Midoriya begins treating his body as a continuing operational resource and explicitly frames One For All as something he must make into `僕の力`;
- Bakugo is shown to absorb evidence against his self-concept and convert shame into competitive recommitment;
- Bakugo's Midoriya-specific rage is distinguished from substantially more tactical live-crisis behavior;
- Iida develops from procedural seriousness into functional crisis leadership;
- Aizawa's V01 anti-self-harm position is revised to anti-waste / meaningful protective sacrifice;
- All Might's finite body and social indispensability become an explicit system-level single-point-of-failure problem;
- Shigaraki's initial model must preserve both emotional immaturity and real tactical opponent modeling.
































## Volume 3 major model change
































Volume 3 shifts the question from judgment under power to **public legibility and succession**.
































Key changes:
































- All Might explicitly asks Midoriya to inherit not merely OFA but `私` and to make his arrival known to society;
- Shigaraki's unseen mentor constructs the opposing public project, asking Shigaraki to become a symbol of fear known to the world;
- the Sports Festival is revealed as spectacle, pro-hero recruitment market, course-mobility mechanism, Support-course exhibition, Business-course training surface, and personal-brand economy;
- Midoriya proves he can be intensely competitive when victory carries professional/relational meaning, and wins primarily through environmental recombination rather than raw OFA;
- Uraraka's income motive is grounded as reciprocal family care;
- Shigaraki is explicitly framed as developmentally immature but capable of growth under a skilled mentor;
- public recognition produces both opportunity and immediate vulnerability.
































## Volume 4 major model change
































Volume 4 shifts the question from public legibility to **self-authorship under unequal inheritance**.
































Key changes:
































- Todoroki reveals `個性婚`, Endeavor's deliberate successor-production project, maternal trauma, and his own attempt to reject toolhood by winning without fire;
- Endeavor becomes directly modelable as an upward-striving, status-comparative parent who calls Shoto a child `そうするべく つくった`;
- Midoriya explicitly acknowledges that he is now `恵まれた` despite his Quirkless past and defines himself as someone `誰かに救けられてここにいる`;
- OFA produces predecessor-like silhouettes/agency during Shinso's Brainwashing, strengthening relational-inheritance ontology while exact mechanics remain prospective OPEN;
- Shinso exposes an entrance-exam metric blind spot and serves as a non-villain control case against deterministic stigma→villain causation;
- Ojiro and Shoda voluntarily reject advancement they cannot regard as self-authored;
- Iida and Uraraka use competition to individuate from friendship-based reliance;
- the V04 source boundary ends at the Uraraka–Bakugo match introduction, so the substantive fight remains V05 evidence.
































## Volume 5 major model change
































Volume 5 shifts the question from self-authorship to **recognition without possession**.
































Key changes:
































- Uraraka's Bakugo match separates visible power asymmetry from agency: the crowd patronizes her, Bakugo treats her as a real threat, Aizawa defends that vigilance as recognition, and Midoriya later restores tactical authorship to Uraraka;
- Midoriya directly rejects Endeavor's successor-instrument logic through `僕はオールマイトじゃありません / 轟くんもあなたじゃない` and tells Todoroki the fire is `君の力`;
- Todoroki's fire use is prospectively defined as temporary de-centering of Endeavor (`お前を忘れた`), not reconciliation, and his original desire to become a hero is recovered beneath the anti-Endeavor strategy;
- Endeavor repeatedly recognizes Shoto's output while misrecognizing his agency through `義務`, `テストベッド`, `俺の野望`, and `上位互換`;
- All Might reveals he too was Quirkless and explicitly moves from autobiographical identification toward successor difference: Midoriya must derive something only Midoriya can;
- Bakugo's No.1 drive becomes an internally validated evidentiary standard: public first place is worthless when he does not recognize the causal route as legitimate;
- Recovery Girl and Inko shift Midoriya's sustainability problem from technical self-preservation to relational responsibility toward people who experience his injury as fear;
- Stain enters as a distinct ideological-purification villain route, further defeating any monocausal stigma/failed-recognition model of villain formation.
































































## Volume 6 major model change
































Volume 6 shifts the question from recognition without possession to **claimed identity versus enacted identity**.
































Key changes:
































- hero names are framed as future-self construction, with Midoriya reclaiming `デク`, Kirishima inheriting an admired heroic ideal, Bakugo exposing conquest-centered public identity, and Iida deferring `Ingenium` before later claiming it under revenge;
- Gran Torino identifies All Might admiration as a `足枷`, and Midoriya's `個性は体の一部` realization turns OFA from selected sacrificial discharge into continuous embodied Full Cowling;
- Iida's V05 family-harm OPEN state resolves into concealed private vengeance that overrides explicit legal/professional warning;
- Manual establishes that hero coercive authority is conditional on law, trust, and public role, explicitly rejecting `私怨` and `私刑`;
- Stain accurately diagnoses Iida's rescue-priority failure but converts moral perception into illegitimate lethal jurisdiction and treats people as `正しい社会への供物`;
- Todoroki uses his own resentment history to recognize Iida's danger and redirects him toward the person he wants to become;
- Midoriya operationalizes `余計なお世話` by inferring hidden Iida danger inside the wider Hosu spectacle;
- Stain advances to `moderate`; Shigaraki's direct ideological collision with Stain strengthens his developmental model without authorizing later ideology/origin backfill;
- professional internships reveal a heterogeneous hero ecology spanning public image, martial training, media labor, patrol, law, mentorship, and emergency response.
































































## Volume 7 major model change
































Volume 7 shifts the question from **claimed identity versus enacted identity** to **what the aftermath of revealed conduct becomes**.
































Key changes:
































- Iida's V06 reorientation develops into substantive repair: Tensei's low-status hero ideal centers helping a lost child; Iida apologizes, retains his damaged left hand as an accountability mnemonic, separates Stain's `信念` from the illegitimacy of `粛清`, and turns his own failure into prevention obligation;
- Midoriya explicitly identifies his earlier inability to see the depth of Iida's distress as a friendship failure (`友だちなのに`), strengthening the ordinary-intervention/recognition thesis while proving that Midoriya can notice distress without fully understanding it;
- Stain's physical defeat becomes symbolic amplification: Gran Torino compares the pressure of Stain's strong ideology/obsession to the same *kind* of mechanism as All Might's `平和の象徴観念`, while media circulation and the League as `受け皿` show how diffuse malice can aggregate into movement-scale villainy;
- the Hosu police aftermath gives hero coercive legitimacy a historical-institutional basis in `統率と規格`, accumulated trust, rule observance, supervision, and licensing, while the suppression of student causality proves official public hero narratives may diverge from actual rescue causality;
- OFA/AFO relational opposition receives explicit origin-level confirmation: All For One takes/possesses/allocates Quirks and builds dependency, while One For All develops into power whose identity depends on succession beyond the current bearer;
- All Might can transmit the history of OFA while still withholding painful personal truth: he internally knows he may not be beside Midoriya in the future but cannot tell him, revealing care-shaped epistemic failure in mentorship;
- U.A. redesigns the practical final from expected robots to paired students versus teachers because villain conditions have changed, providing positive evidence of institutional learning;
- Bakugo's `Origin` explicitly grounds his hero ideal in All Might as the hero who always wins from bad odds, while `負けた方がマシ` proves Midoriya-specific hierarchy injury can temporarily override that core victory value;
- Midoriya directly rejects Bakugo's shouting as a communication system (`会話にならない`) and calls Bakugo back to his own anti-surrender ideal; the volume ends with first coordinated power-sharing, not completed reconciliation.
































The strongest compact V07 formulation is:
































> **Aftermath is where an event becomes a future: recognition matters only if what has been recognized changes later action, relationship, institution, or inheritance.**
































































## Volume 8 major model change
































Volume 8 shifts the question from **what aftermath becomes** to **how correctly diagnosed resistance produces growth**.
































Key changes:
































- Yaoyorozu's final-exam problem is established as confidence-filtered access to her own judgment rather than lack of intelligence; Todoroki restores her epistemic standing by concretely reminding her that he voted for her as class representative because he judged her suited, after which she takes planning authority and solves the Aizawa problem;
- Todoroki gains an important relational correction: sincere respect does not guarantee that another person's judgment is functionally entering one's plan; he can recognize and repair that mismatch without becoming verbally effusive;
- Aizawa explicitly defines the practical final as calibrated diagnostic adversity: teachers preserve `勝ち筋` while observing `どう課題と向き合うか`, strengthening the model of U.A. as a high-demand but scaffolded educational institution rather than pure survival-of-the-fittest sorting;
- the V07 Midoriya/Bakugo cooperative breach becomes a successful shared-goal behavior pattern against All Might, while aggressive register, dependency discomfort, and lack of emotional reconciliation remain intact;
- Koda's insect-phobia → Jiro-injury → responsibility → action sequence gives one of the first substantial minor-Class-1-A conditional courage models, and Jiro gains injury/support/partner-trust evidence;
- Mineta gains independent aspiration (`かっけえ男`) and tactical problem-solving evidence without any sanitization of his explicit sexual-objectifying motive or bath-boundary violation;
- Toga and Dabi enter prospectively through Stain's symbolic afterlife; Toga's `生きにくい` is kept causally OPEN and Dabi's later identity is withheld;
- AFO teaches Shigaraki `排斥ではなく受容を`, revealing strategic incorporation and a mentorship style capable of cultivating subjective self-authorship inside structural dependency;
- the mall conversation produces Shigaraki's largest ideological transition so far: `全部 オールマイトだ` reorganizes diffuse grievance and `今日からそれを 信念と呼ぼう` marks the first explicit self-naming of a personal anti-All-Might conviction;
- All Might directly states that there are many people he could not save (`あるよ たくさん`) and `悔しいが 私も人だ`, proving that the Symbol's structural overconcentration is not grounded in Toshinori literally believing himself omnipotent;
- Kota introduces heroic sacrifice as a contested inheritance: his parents' publicly praised deaths protecting civilians are privately experienced as the event that took his parents away, making hero culture appear `理解できない 気持ち悪い` rather than inspiring;
- Midoriya does not answer Kota with hero propaganda, strengthening his model through epistemic restraint: recognition can mean acknowledging a grief-grounded worldview before having any honest way to change it;
- the camp closes by distinguishing mental/technical growth from Quirk-capacity growth and beginning explicit `個性を伸ばす` conditioning; Volume 8 ends here, **before** the forest-villain attack and Muscular/Kota payoff that the first pass had bundled with V09.
































The strongest compact V08 formulation is:
































> **A wall is useful when it reveals the real bottleneck. Growth comes not from applying more force to every obstacle, but from discovering whether the blocked route is confidence, communication, fear, motive, ideology, bodily capacity, institutional design, or the social meaning of heroism itself.**
































## Volume 9 major model change
































Volume 9 shifts the question from **diagnostic resistance** to **emergency enactment under broken guarantees**.
































Key changes:
































- the forest attack tests learned heroism without teacher-authored `勝ち筋`; adversarial reality, not pedagogy, determines difficulty and cost;
- Midoriya defeats Muscular only by accepting catastrophic bodily cost after concluding Kota's survival is directly contingent on him, revising self-damage from primarily technical immaturity toward **value-conditioned rescue expenditure**;
- `ワン・フォー・オール 1000000%` is preserved as the attack's direct name, while literal multiplier interpretation remains epistemically OPEN;
- Kota changes through experiential recognition rather than a better argument: being directly protected makes Mandalay's earlier explanation of Water Hose newly usable without erasing grief;
- Aizawa's `戦闘を許可する` establishes accountable emergency delegation — professional rules can change when the standing restriction would defeat its protective purpose;
- Shigaraki's V08 `信念` begins generating operational strategy through society-`ヒビ` logic, heterogeneous recruitment and Bakugo target selection;
- the Vanguard demonstrates motive pluralism rather than one villain psychology: Spinner's adopted ideology, Muscular's predatory enjoyment, Toga's affiliative/predatory intimacy, Mustard's resentment and other motives can be coordinated inside one operation;
- Bakugo's public aggression becomes adversary intelligence, proving public legibility can create recruitment/capture vulnerability as well as opportunity;
- Tokoyami, Shoji, Kendo and Tetsutetsu gain high-value crisis evidence showing distributed regulation, environmental inference and complementary team roles.
































The strongest compact V09 formulation is:
































> **When no one has designed a fair win condition, heroism becomes the problem of carrying recognition, restraint, coordination and responsibility into danger while deciding which costs can be borne without destroying the capacity to rescue again.**

## Volume 10 major model change
















Volume 10 shifts the question from **emergency enactment under broken guarantees** to **contested legibility, jurisdiction and protective authority after defeat**.
















Key changes:
















- the forest operation is explicitly accounted as `完全敗北`, proving that local heroic successes need not add up to institutional victory;
- public trust becomes a direct strategic casualty when U.A. states that the League took trust in heroes along with Bakugo;
- the `内通者` possibility enters as a rational restricted-information hypothesis while identity remains OPEN;
- Midoriya's `手が届く` rescue trigger is paired with a concrete medical warning that repeated comparable arm injuries may permanently remove future rescue capacity;
- Iida converts his own Hosu `私的暴走` into reciprocal prevention, joining the covert group as a boundary-holder rather than abandoning friends to repeat his failure;
- Momo's V08 confidence recovery persists under crisis: she trusts Todoroki while independently assigning herself the `ストッパー` role;
- Bakugo's V07 All Might-victory origin receives a strong prospective validation when League recruitment fails and he explicitly says `俺はオールマイトが勝つ姿に憧れた`;
- Aizawa accepts public responsibility for Bakugo's conduct while correctly rejecting the League's moral interpretation of that conduct;
- the professional raid demonstrates care translated into intelligence, multi-site coordination, specialist roles and bounded coercive authority;
- `我々が来た` pluralizes All Might's iconic arrival grammar inside a collective rescue operation;
- AFO tells Shigaraki `君は悪くない`, offers `僕がいる`, praises him for thinking/leading for himself, guarantees repeated retry, and closes with `全ては君の為にある`, making self-authorship-inside-dependency a directly evidenced mentorship mechanism;
- Best Jeanist becomes a power-philosophy control case when AFO attributes his exceptional effectiveness to training/experience and rejects the Quirk as temperamentally unsuitable for Shigaraki;
- V10 ends before the All Might/AFO battle resolves, sharply correcting the historical V1 V10–V11 bundle.
















The strongest compact V10 formulation is:
















> **Rescue after defeat becomes a problem of interpretation and jurisdiction: the person who reaches you may still misunderstand you, and the person who protects your agency may also become the structure on which that agency depends.**
















## Volume 11 major model change
















Volume 11 shifts the question from **contested legibility / protective authority** to **accountable succession / de-centered support**.
















Key changes:
















- Midoriya identifies that Bakugo is least likely to reject Kirishima's hand because Kirishima has built a `対等な関係` with him; rescue therefore succeeds through relational routing rather than by centering Midoriya as the physical savior.
- The V10 permanent-arm-risk warning receives a held-out behavioral PASS: Midoriya exits a lethal crisis through a distributed/no-combat rescue without new injury, and All Might explicitly recognizes this as his first injury-free escape from danger. Midoriya advances to `strong`.
- All Might's exposed weak body, final embers and `平和の象徴は死にました` end the combat-Symbol role without ending Toshinori's obligations; Gran Torino and Inko both redirect him toward staying alive and continuing as teacher/support. All Might advances to `strong`.
- Nana's smile instruction roots All Might's public reassurance in inherited rescue pedagogy rather than simple branding: `自分は大丈夫だ` must be performed so endangered people can believe survival remains possible.
- `次は君だ` becomes a dual succession signal: All Might's public warning privately tells Midoriya the Symbol's burden is passing, while AFO separately tells Shigaraki `次は君だ` after explaining that a teacher exists to make a student `一人立ち`。 The two mentorship systems now both include deliberate de-centering, though under radically different moral structures.
- AFO's model is revised from dependency engineering alone toward **engineered autonomy inside manipulated option-space**: he interprets defeat, preserves allies/resources, then deliberately withdraws so Shigaraki's hatred and self-direction can grow.
- Inko rejects All Might's first sacrificial promise (`この命に代えても`) and demands `命に代えないで / ちゃんと生きて / 守り育てて下さい`; legitimate mentorship is explicitly reframed as durable accompaniment rather than substitutive death.
- Kota's letter allows Midoriya to articulate that U.A. is not the source of his hero identity: `雄英でなくたって どこだって…いいよ`; All Might recognizes that Midoriya is no longer merely following his back.
- Mitsuki's trust in U.A. is grounded not in prestige but in accurate recognition: unlike superficial praise of Katsuki's visible ability, Aizawa's public statement convinces her the school actually `勝己を見てくれてる`.
- Aizawa refuses outcome-based exoneration of the unauthorized rescue. He distinguishes context-sensitive sanction from legitimacy itself and requires students to rebuild trust through `正規の手続きを踏み 正規の活躍をして`.
- Tsuyu's dorm-era disclosure converts failed prevention into relationship repair: she names both anger and self-directed inadequacy because unresolved feelings have made ordinary cheerful conversation impossible; the class actively rebuilds `日常`.
- V11 closes at `V11:p207` with the provisional-license preparation command `必殺技を作ってもらう!!!`; Volume 12 therefore begins by testing whether de-centered inherited purpose can become repeatable, legitimate skill.
















The strongest compact V11 formulation is:
















> **Succession becomes sustainable when the protector can step out of the center without abandoning responsibility: power, rescue, mentorship and trust must be redistributed through relationships and procedures that let other people act while keeping support alive.**

## Volume 12 major model change
