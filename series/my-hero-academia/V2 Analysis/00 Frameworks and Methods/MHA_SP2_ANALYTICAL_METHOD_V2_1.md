# My Hero Academia — Second-Pass Deep Reading Method v2.1
## Character-state and reconstruction amendment to v2.0

## 1. Status of this revision

Version 2.1 preserves the complete literary, linguistic, visual, institutional, and archival protocol of `MHA_SP2_ANALYTICAL_METHOD_V2.md` and adds a formal longitudinal character-modeling layer.

Nothing in this amendment reduces the priority of Japanese primary evidence, volume-by-volume close reading, epistemic classification, retrospective/prospective separation, thematic ledgers, source locators, or first-pass correction.

The new rule is:

> Every volume must now preserve not only what characters mean thematically, but also the source-grounded behavioral state necessary to reconstruct how they perceive, decide, speak, and relate under different conditions.

## 2. Why the original V2 method required amendment

The original character-state ledger captured:

- desire;
- fear;
- self-conception;
- relation to power;
- important relational changes;
- contradictions;
- irreversible decisions;
- voice changes.

That is sufficient for literary synthesis, but a reliable character reconstruction system additionally needs:

- context-specific behavior;
- decision heuristics;
- perception and blind spots;
- stress responses;
- relationship-conditioned variation;
- low-stakes behavior;
- Japanese register by partner and situation;
- knowledge boundaries;
- model confidence and contradiction tracking.

These additions are now mandatory where evidence exists.

## 3. New cumulative character ledgers

Replace the single undifferentiated character-trajectory concept with five group ledgers plus one relationship ledger:

1. `MHA_SP2_CLASS_1A_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md`
2. `MHA_SP2_UA_STUDENTS_STAFF_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md`
3. `MHA_SP2_PRO_HERO_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md`
4. `MHA_SP2_VILLAIN_ANTAGONIST_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md`
5. `MHA_SP2_FAMILY_CIVILIAN_SOCIAL_ACTOR_LEDGER.md`
6. `MHA_SP2_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md`

These coexist with the cross-cutting thematic ledgers defined in v2.0.

The group ledgers are character-centered. The thematic ledgers remain concept-centered.

## 4. Mandatory modeling fields

When a volume materially advances a character, capture as many of these fields as evidence supports:

- identity/current role;
- desire hierarchy;
- fears and vulnerabilities;
- self-conception;
- values and power philosophy;
- perception/attention tendencies;
- blind spots;
- decision heuristics;
- risk tolerance;
- response to authority;
- conflict/escalation behavior;
- care/help behavior;
- stress response;
- relationship-specific behavior;
- Japanese speech/register;
- embodiment and bodily-cost behavior;
- knowledge/epistemic limits;
- current contradictions;
- state transition from prior volume;
- evidence locators;
- confidence/readiness.

Do not fabricate fields merely to fill a template.

## 5. Behavioral evidence requirement

A modeling claim must be traceable to an observed scene.

Preferred compact form:

| Context | Trigger | Partner | Observed behavior | Interpretation | Locator | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Repeated evidence is more valuable than elaborate trait adjectives.

## 6. State/trait distinction

Every apparent personality feature should be treated as one of:

- stable tendency;
- current developmental state;
- relationship-specific tendency;
- role-specific behavior;
- stress-specific behavior;
- exceptional one-off.

The burden of proof rises when moving from a local observation to a stable trait.

## 7. Relationship conditioning

If a character behaves differently toward different people, preserve the difference rather than averaging it into one personality.

Examples likely to matter strongly across MHA include:

- Bakugo toward Midoriya versus peers versus adults;
- Midoriya toward victims versus rivals versus authority figures;
- Todoroki within and outside his family;
- Endeavor as hero, father, husband, and public symbol;
- Toga toward loved objects, League members, Ochako, and institutional enemies;
- Hawks in casual speech, undercover work, HPSC-linked contexts, and intimate trust.

## 8. Low-stakes evidence is first-class evidence

Do not model a character only from battles and dramatic speeches.

Actively preserve:

- classroom behavior;
- meals;
- dorm life;
- jokes;
- teasing;
- embarrassment;
- hobbies;
- mundane frustration;
- ordinary planning;
- small acts of care;
- how characters respond when nothing is at stake.

High-stakes scenes reveal values under pressure. Low-stakes scenes reveal baseline social personality.

## 9. Japanese voice amendment

The existing Japanese voice lens remains mandatory and now feeds a per-character speech model.

For modeling, record not only distinctive forms but their conditions:

- with whom does the character use them;
- under what emotional state;
- what changes when angry, frightened, embarrassed, professional, intimate, or exhausted;
- which forms are stable identity markers versus momentary effects.

Surface catchphrase imitation without register logic is considered a failed reconstruction.

## 10. Knowledge-state control

Any scenario reconstruction must lock the character's chronology and knowledge.

Analyst hindsight may inform interpretation, but simulated characters must not know:

- future reveals;
- private scenes they did not witness;
- hidden identities they have not learned;
- motives the manga has shown only to the reader.

## 11. New per-volume workflow pass

Add after the existing revision synthesis:

### Pass H — Character-model update

For each character with meaningful new evidence:

1. identify which group ledger owns the character;
2. update current state;
3. add new behavioral evidence atoms;
4. update relationship deltas;
5. update speech/register observations;
6. note contradictions or counterexamples;
7. update model-readiness status only if evidence breadth changes.

Then proceed to archival packaging.

## 12. New required section in each volume artifact

After the existing cumulative-ledger update section, add:

## Character-modeling updates

For each materially updated character:

- **State delta**
- **New context coverage**
- **New decision/behavior evidence**
- **Relationship-specific update**
- **Speech/register update**
- **Contradictions / uncertainty**
- **Model-readiness change**

If a volume adds no modeling-relevant evidence for a character, do not manufacture an entry.

## 13. Prediction is downstream, never evidence

A character dossier may eventually contain conditional predictions, but these are explicitly model outputs.

They must never be cited as evidence in:

- a later volume deep reading;
- a thematic synthesis;
- a character ledger;
- another prediction.

Only canonical source observations may update the evidence base.

## 14. Validation

At major arc checkpoints, perform held-out validation when feasible.

Freeze the model before a later scene, predict the response from prior evidence, then compare against canon.

Classify mismatch as:

- missing context;
- incorrect stable-trait inference;
- relationship-specific exception;
- stress-specific exception;
- character growth;
- genuine surprise;
- insufficient evidence.

Do not rewrite the prediction after seeing the result.

## 15. Modeling readiness

Use:

- `insufficient`
- `emerging`
- `moderate`
- `strong`
- `specialist_ready`

Readiness measures evidence breadth, not popularity, narrative importance, or confidence in a thematic interpretation.

## 16. Volume 1 retroactive application

`MHA_SP2_V01_DEEP_READING.md` remains canonical as written.

Its existing character-state, Japanese voice, relationship, embodiment, and source-locator findings should be used to seed the new longitudinal ledgers rather than rewriting the entire V01 artifact solely to match the new template.

Starting with Volume 2, the new modeling-update section is mandatory.

## 17. Governing modeling principle

The character model should answer:

> Given this chronology, knowledge state, relationship, stakes, and stress regime, what response is best supported by the character's observed behavioral history?

It should never answer:

> What response would be dramatically convenient or stereotypically in character?

The aim is evidence-grounded conditional reconstruction, not roleplay by vibe.


## 18. Official supplemental-material reconciliation amendment — 2026-09-10

This amendment governs a bounded continuation after the completed manga-only maturation pass. Sections 1–17 and all historical volume/checkpoint knowledge boundaries remain intact. The new admission boundary is the three owner-selected Japanese EPUB objects, **Ultra Archive (UA), Ultra Analysis (UAN), and Ultra Age (UAG)**, identified in the [supplemental source lock](../01%20Source%20Lock%20and%20Inventory/MHA_SP2_OFFICIAL_SUPPLEMENTAL_SOURCE_LOCK.md). The books are reviewed in that order; the current map records actual progress. Admission of a source object is not a claim that its contents have already been read or integrated.

### 18.1 Direct reading and complete accounting

Read every original spine image visually, at a resolution sufficient for its Japanese text and visual relations. Use original image bytes extracted in OPF spine order. OCR may assist retrieval but cannot substitute for visual verification or supply an unverified quotation. Record every page, including covers, profiles, gags, bonus fiction, interviews, design notes and publication matter, in that book's coverage table. Each row owns a spine locator, printed page only when actually verified, review method, content/disposition, and evidence-record links. Non-spine resources receive an explicit source-topology disposition. No category is dismissed merely because it is paratext.

Allowed page states are `REVIEWED_MATERIAL`, `REVIEWED_NO_MATERIAL_ADDITION`, `REVIEWED_DUPLICATE`, `EXCLUDED_WITH_REASON`, `UNRESOLVED_READABILITY`, and working-only `NOT_YET_REVIEWED`. A duplicate must name its comparator; a no-addition verdict must state what was reviewed and why it adds no material claim. Coverage is complete only when every page has an honest non-pending disposition. Unreadable text remains unresolved rather than being inferred from another edition. Individual claims may remain uncertain despite complete page coverage.

### 18.2 Claim authority, attribution and temporal state

The source object and the kind of statement are separate questions. Distinguish manga/bonus narrative, editorial synopsis, profile fact or preference, author comment, attributed self-report, another character's judgment, numerical rating, design material, and gag. Verify credited authorship and continuity for bonus fiction; official publication alone does not establish personal Horikoshi authorship or main-continuity event status. Creator intent and editorial clarification can enlarge characterization without demonstrating that the manga dramatized that material successfully.

Record book print/digital dates separately from package conversion metadata; an original interview/reprint date when established; editorial cutoff; depicted character/narrative state; and present applicability. Unknown fields remain explicit. Later publication does not make every statement an endpoint statement. Earlier-only, continuing-with-support, superseded, endpoint and unresolved uses are distinct. An interview answer, a character's conscious explanation, a concealed motive and behavior observed by another character have different epistemic force.

Use brief verified Japanese anchors and bounded English glosses for consequential claims. Preserve speaker, audience, relationship, occasion and uncertainty. First-person explanation is evidence of self-understanding, not automatic proof of underlying motive. Reprints, excerpted manga panels and paraphrases remain dependent evidence; the same fact appearing in three books is not three independent behavioral observations. Changing rating dimensions must be compared by their definitions before any claim of growth or decline.

### 18.3 Reconciliation and reconstruction

For every material record separate evidence class; analytical outcome (`CONFIRM`, `STRENGTHEN`, `COMPLICATE`, `NARROW`, `CONTRADICT`, `NEW`, `INSUFFICIENT`); temporal applicability; modeling effect; literary consequence; and source dependence. The [supplemental reconciliation ledger](../07%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/MHA_SP2_SUPPLEMENTAL_EVIDENCE_RECONCILIATION_LEDGER.md) owns those adjudications. Compare manga against book and book against book. Do not silently harmonize a discrepancy, count dependent summaries as fresh corroboration, or turn an absence of information into a negative character fact.

Preferences, aversions, routines and ordinary sociability are first-class characterization. A favorite food supports a stated preference; without contextual conduct it does not establish a restaurant decision policy, coping mechanism or relationship script. Changes can improve factual texture or domain breadth without promoting a readiness tier. All baseline/current readiness subjects require a named disposition, including no-change cases and historical distributed candidates outside the current 86-row snapshot. New book-only subjects require identity/discovery review, not automatic dossiers. Mineta specifically requires balanced treatment of misconduct, aspiration, competence, friendship and self-risk; rehabilitation would require acknowledged harm, changed boundary understanding and changed conduct, not a flattering profile.

Only current cumulative models, readiness and synthesis may absorb admitted supplemental findings. Give new supplemental atoms distinct IDs and exact locators, with dates and applicability. Preserve original atoms, historical checkpoints, manga source locks, formal probe inputs, predictions, outcomes and contamination notices byte-for-byte. Any subsequent validation is separately labeled retrospective/nonblind or explicitly withheld as appropriate; hypothetical behavior is not empirical validation. The readiness index remains the sole tier authority.

### 18.4 Literary use and stopping boundaries

Review all eight comparative specialists, eight literary character studies and four full-series documents. Revise a home only when evidence materially changes its responsibility; unchanged is a valid result. Distinguish expanded source characterization, a changed reading of the manga, and unresolved limits of the manga's execution. Keep serious counterreadings and source-specific tensions visible. Final integration follows evidence stabilization, all-character dispositions and reconstruction review, then specialist convergence and full-series revision.

Completion is **V01–V42 + these three named books reconciled**, with page coverage, traceable claims, current routes and preservation checks. It is not an exhaustive all-franchise supplemental audit. Volume-embedded author notes, profiles, design pages and omake remain a separately recorded coverage question; their prior exclusion from narrative sequence is not a finding of analytical worthlessness and does not authorize a new comprehensive volume reread.
