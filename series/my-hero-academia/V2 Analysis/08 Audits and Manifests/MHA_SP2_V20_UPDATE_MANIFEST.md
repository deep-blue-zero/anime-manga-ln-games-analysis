## Major V20 analytical propositions

1. V20 shifts from **ordinary joy as heroic labor / recognition as a contested social good** to **visibility under consequence / responsibility before multiple witnesses**.
2. La Brava's Love Quirk establishes that genuine relationship can generate real power without conferring moral legitimacy on the action it empowers.
3. La Brava's earlier rejection/withdrawal and first-fan recruitment are source-grounded; she is an active technical collaborator whose agency is narrowed around one dominant bond.
4. Gentle's criminal route is multicausal: poor hero-course results, four provisional-license failures, an unauthorized rescue causing serious harm, disgrace, dream abandonment and later terror of being forgotten precede criminal media performance.
5. Gentle/La Brava are a sincere reciprocal rescue structure whose relationship is morally compromised by criminal co-entanglement, not by fake intimacy.
6. Gentle's decisive failure transition is from preserving the public-memory project to protecting La Brava's future through surrender.
7. Midoriya recognizes Gentle's relational motive without yielding the protective/legal boundary; successful hero labor here can remain invisible so beneficiaries retain an ordinary day.
8. Class 1-A's performance makes communal affect and ordinary institutional life legitimate socially restorative labor.
9. Jiro integrates music and hero identity through self-authored social usefulness rather than forced vocational inheritance.
10. Eri's first spontaneous smile/laughter is distributed rescue causality; V20 adds positive affect and ordinary curiosity without claiming trauma is cured.
11. Gentle/La Brava's custody shows accountability can coexist with recognition of personhood and rehabilitative possibility.
12. Ragdoll's return strengthens the distinction between lost capability and lost identity/role.
13. Hero Billboard Chart JP quantifies visibility but cannot manufacture trust or All Might-like symbolic legitimacy.
14. Hawks treats media/public fluency and rumor diffusion as operational intelligence infrastructure and demonstrates distributed micro-rescue with Fierce Wings.
15. Endeavor's `俺を見ていてくれ` makes No.1 a future evidentiary burden rather than inherited trust.
16. The Todoroki family is a set of differentiated witnesses; there is no family-wide forgiveness state.
17. Rei's remembered-flower evidence supports attempted responsibility and attention, not reconciliation; direct contact remains frightening/inappropriate.
18. Endeavor's `俺の体は 熱が篭り続ける / だからおまえを / だからおまえたちを` directly reconnects his current body limit to earlier family/successor instrumentalization.
19. V20 ends before High-End resolution: apparent failure/public-family witness is the test point, not the final adjudication.
