## Core V34 adjudications

1. New Order requires touching/naming a target and imposing a rule; Star can maintain two rules simultaneously.
2. Star normally uses one rule for self-enhancement, but the source explicitly gives that enhancement a ceiling below All Might's physical level.
3. Star's Air Force pilots are active tactical partners and relationship nodes, not anonymous transport; Star's effective combat capacity is distributed across New Order, pilots, command/fire support and information.
4. New Order's application to a living target is identity/recognition sensitive rather than raw-strength gated.
5. Star's rule on `死柄木弔` fails because the AFO/Shigaraki-side identity no longer maps cleanly to that name.
6. V34 explicitly describes the AFO/Shigaraki state as advanced fusion: `僕らはもうじき1つとなる`, `融け合い新たな人格となりつつある`, and `AFOでもなく 死柄木弔でもない`.
7. The approximately 98% state is strong evidence of advanced merger, not proof of final permanent unity, later inseparability or extinction of prior identities.
8. Tiamat and the Air Force operation show that Star's battle cannot be reduced to an isolated one-body power contest.
9. AFO/Shigaraki succeeds in stealing New Order from Star.
10. Before transfer resolves, Star has imposed `新秩序は他の個性と反発する`; the stolen New Order attacks other Quirk factors from within.
11. Reflect is directly legible among the destroyed Quirk factors; V34 does not authorize a complete inventory of every destroyed Quirk from ambiguous imagery.
12. Star dies/disintegrates. Her death is a serious personal, national and international loss and should not be converted into a simple heroic-sacrifice ideal.
13. AFO/Shigaraki attempts to dump dangerous New Order into another nearby body, exemplifying externalization of cost into an expendable route.
14. New Order ultimately is not retained as a stable AFO asset.
15. Star's residual claim that `英雄の意志を継いだ誰か` will continue the struggle distinguishes transmitted will from AFO's possession-based inheritance model.
16. Star's pilots survive and preserve X-66 combat/biological/Quirk-response data; her legacy is informational and relational as well as memorial.
17. U.S./international hero dispatch contracts after Star's death because states prioritize domestic instability and remaining hero assets.
18. The V33 roughly-three-day enemy-completion estimate is superseded for current operations: after Star's damage, the hero side estimates Shigaraki will be unable to move properly for at least roughly one week.
19. That one-week window is `猶予` / reprieve, not proof of victory or guarantee of later exact timing.
20. Hero manpower remains heavily reduced; enemy force still includes original-body AFO, damaged AFO/Shigaraki, Dabi, Toga, approximately six Near High-End Nomu and remnant networks.
21. Class 1-A has continued training during the refuge period; Bakugo's Cluster and Shoto's new fire/ice technical development remain in progress.
22. All Might's `有精卵` metaphor returns as a plural-growth callback rather than a one-successor replacement model.
23. AFO explicitly states a long-horizon contingency doctrine: for one objective he prepares many routes/backups and connects whichever remains viable.
24. AFO's use of `友達` in that context describes an instrumental network of prepared pieces, not reciprocal friendship.
25. Aoyama was born Quirkless; his parents sought a way for him to live like other children and reached AFO, who supplied a Quirk.
26. AFO converted that real family vulnerability and granted power into coercive dependency under lethal threat.
27. Aoyama directly confesses active facilitation: `USJも 合宿も 僕が手引きした`.
28. Coercion materially mitigates/explains Aoyama's conduct without erasing the harmful acts or the need for security/legal accountability.
29. Hagakure independently notices Aoyama's anomaly, witnesses the disclosure, voices harmed-peer moral anger and refracts Aoyama's laser in a live danger context.
30. Hagakure's face becomes visibly revealed through the light-refraction event; the moment supports a bounded visibility/disclosure motif without universalizing invisibility into a truth symbol.
31. Authorities preserve adversarial security after disclosure; current evidence does not indicate an AFO-style explosive contingency in Aoyama's granted Quirk, but the family remains in custody/checking.
32. Midoriya does not declare Aoyama innocent. He recognizes the Quirkless-child parallel and refuses to let coerced wrongdoing totalize Aoyama's future personhood.
33. Midoriya proposes a practical restorative role: reverse the AFO-created information channel and use Aoyama to help deceive/lure AFO.
34. Iida defines friendship after betrayal as taking the hand of a classmate presently crying/despairing without falsifying the past facts.
35. Kirishima rejects permanent moral quarantine in this specific coercion context; this is not a blanket rule for all uncoerced betrayal.
36. Bakugo's abrasive register persists during future-oriented peer repair, validating V33's non-flattened post-apology model.
37. Aizawa says he currently has no intention of expelling Aoyama while preserving accountability/security and demanding an operational plan.
38. Hatsume/Power Loader are operating U.A. barrier/refuge and equipment infrastructure under wartime supply constraints.
39. Hatsume explicitly states `発明がサポート科のヒーロー活動なんです`; Support Course invention is hero activity while professional roles remain differentiated.
40. V34 ends with equipment/search/preparation for the second decisive battle assembled; the decisive battle itself has **not begun** within V34.
