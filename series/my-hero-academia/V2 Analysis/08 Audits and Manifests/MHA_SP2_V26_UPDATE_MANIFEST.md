## Major V26 analytical propositions
1. Endeavor's coaching resolves into a precise sequence: decompose complex action, repeat components until below-conscious execution, then layer concurrent tasks under real stakes.
2. Professional mastery is therefore partly a cognitive-bandwidth problem, not simply a raw-output problem.
3. Midoriya converts the method into controlled Blackwhip/multi-task rescue during the Ending incident rather than returning to catastrophic self-sacrifice.
4. Bakugo explicitly seeks his deficits and defines strong victory through a no-injury/no-casualty benchmark while retaining his abrasive competitive register.
5. Todoroki makes professional/familial separation explicit: he chooses to use Endeavor's hero expertise without treating that choice as restored fatherhood or completed forgiveness.
6. Endeavor correctly recognizes that he had misread Shoto's professional approach as relational opening and shifts into a hero-mentor frame.
7. The Ending incident is a relationship-conditioned stress test: Endeavor freezes because Natsuo is the hostage, while the students execute the method he taught them.
8. Natsuo's refusal to forgive remains legitimate; Endeavor explicitly distinguishes wanting forgiveness from wanting to atone.
9. Endeavor's material repair plan can include his own exclusion from the imagined repaired household, strengthening an atonement-without-entitlement model.
10. Kurogiri is established as an unusually coherent Nomu with a Shirakumo-linked corpse/substrate, but present Kurogiri identity and Shigaraki allegiance remain analytically real.
11. Aizawa/Present Mic's relationship-specific address produces the anomalous `hospital` response; intimate history becomes forensic capability without resolving layered identity into a simple equivalence.
12. Aizawa's severe survival-oriented pedagogy is explicitly linked to Shirakumo's premature death and a desire for students to live long enough to become heroes.
13. Distributed internship exposure produces visible heterogeneous student gains; montage evidence is not used to force individual readiness promotions.
14. All Might explicitly rejects a chosen-one model of OFA predecessors: they were people entrusted and who entrusted others amid repeated conflict.
15. Nana Shimura's Quirk is identified as `Float`; later training/mastery remains prospective.
16. Midoriya's low-stakes New Year reflection treats All Might's continued presence and ordinary Bakugo conversation as extraordinary good fortune, strengthening an ordinary relational baseline.
17. All Might's post-Symbol usefulness is reframed from combat productivity toward continued life, presence, research and mentorship.
18. Hawks attributes key intelligence/negotiation capacity to Public Safety training drilled into him from childhood, strengthening the coercive-institutional-development model.
19. Hawks recognizes Twice simultaneously as good-natured and as a catastrophic strategic force multiplier; V26 does not resolve that contradiction through later violence or death.
20. Twice's friendship-centered moral reasoning creates genuine affiliative trust and an exploitable infiltration surface at the same time.
21. The PLF's operational program includes coordinated urban attacks, weapons diffusion, liberation/self-defense framing, political entry and practical destruction of existing order.
22. Late-March deployment shifts hero society from distributed preparedness to preemptive operation, but visible students close the volume in rear evacuation support rather than equivalent front-line assault roles.
23. V1 grouped V26-V27 war claims are therefore split prospectively: V26 authorizes mobilization/deployment, not later battle outcomes.
