1. Infinite 100% is a coupled OFA/Rewind system, not independent 100% mastery; Aizawa's safe shutdown remains causally indispensable.
2. Nighteye directly recognizes at least one future deviation but explicitly leaves the branching mechanism unknown. Anti-fatalism becomes empirically grounded without becoming magical voluntarism.
3. Nighteye's death shows that altered future trajectory does not erase consequences already incurred.
4. Chisaki can experience gratitude and attachment while overriding the attached person's expressed authorship; his pathology is better modeled as attachment subordinated to project sovereignty.
5. Shigaraki converts grievance into delayed, symbolically tailored punishment and organizational acquisition rather than simple impulsive retaliation.
6. Mirio's `いらないです` resolves the OFA candidate challenge toward plural heroic legitimacy: another person's excellence and loss do not automatically delegitimize Midoriya's inheritance or entitle Mirio to it.
7. Eri's physical rescue does not complete recovery, and her Quirk does not become an immediately available therapeutic resource.
8. Remedial licensing establishes social/emotional leadership and participatory spectacle as professional hero competencies.
9. Endeavor's No.1 ranking becomes a starting-line problem; his future commitment to Shoto is not reconciliation, and `勝手にしろよ` preserves that boundary.
10. V18 ends with Aoyama intentionally unreadable. Later causal explanation of `本性` remains prospective evidence.
