## Core V33 adjudications

1. Class 1-A accepts the reality of AFO-linked danger but rejects Midoriya's unilateral right to decide that this danger makes reciprocal support inadmissible.
2. Midoriya explicitly recognizes that his classmates are worried about him, and Danger Sense does not flag them as hostile danger; his refusal of help is therefore a policy of self-exclusion rather than inability to perceive care.
3. Class 1-A's coordinated relay demonstrates distributed capability: independent actors can collectively reach a successor whose individual mobility exceeds any one classmate's.
4. Iida reaches Midoriya through `同じ意志で 同じ歩調で` and returns Midoriya's own `余計なお世話` rescue principle to him: intervention without invitation cannot coherently be reserved for other people only.
5. Iida advances **`strong -> specialist_ready`** on breadth of leadership, reciprocal rescue, relationship-conditioned behavior and Japanese speech evidence.
6. Bakugo explicitly explains his childhood mistreatment through his own insecurity and attempt to keep Midoriya down, bows and says `今まで ごめん` without demanding forgiveness or making Midoriya adjudicate the apology immediately.
7. Bakugo's subsequent abrasive/competitive register remains intact; accountability and relational repair do not flatten him into generic softness.
8. Midoriya apologizes for telling classmates they could not keep up, collapses physically and accepts their support, resolving V32's self-exclusion problem locally without proving it can never recur.
9. U.A. civilians' initial refusal of Midoriya is grounded in a real strategic concern that AFO/Shigaraki may target him; V33 does not reduce their fear to simple ingratitude.
10. Nezu's refuge architecture makes institutional protection an active engineering problem: U.A. is segmented, movable and linked with other evacuation infrastructure rather than functioning as an ordinary school campus.
11. Uraraka assumes public responsibility for the class's decision to return Midoriya and asks the crowd to see his exhausted body rather than only the threat attached to OFA.
12. Uraraka's `特別な力はあっても!! 特別な人なんていません!!` separates exceptional power from exceptional personhood/expendability.
13. Uraraka does not promise perfect safety; she acknowledges fear and uncertainty while asking civilians for bounded cooperation and a place where Midoriya can rest.
14. Uraraka advances **`strong -> specialist_ready`** through sustained public leadership, risk communication, personhood/capacity distinction, relational care and speech-register evidence.
15. Kota and the heteromorphic woman previously rescued by Midoriya physically move toward him, reversing the beneficiary direction and turning earlier rescue into later support capacity.
16. Narration states `みんなが最高のヒーローになるまでの物語だ`, pluralizing the greatest-hero frame at the V33 boundary without yet supplying its final-series definition.
17. Civilian `客` self-critique revises the old hero-society customer model toward bounded civic co-production; this does **not** imply identical roles, civilian vigilantism or abandonment of professional expertise.
18. Aizawa remains operationally engaged. Kurogiri/Shirakumo restoration remains uncertain but not abandoned; Toga countermeasures include blood/transform-duration analysis and an isolation period for evacuees before U.A. entry.
19. Stain confronts powerless All Might's self-erasure and argues that All Might's legacy is not the preservation of one Symbol but embers independently carried among other people.
20. The Kamino woman All Might last saved repeatedly cleans the vandalized statue, providing autonomous civilian evidence for Stain's ember claim rather than leaving it as ideology alone.
21. All Might recognizes his own failed self-classification, returns to Midoriya/class and commits to continue acting even if he must crawl; Midoriya simultaneously recognizes that he treated All Might cruelly by leaving him, moving their relationship into mutual repair.
22. Bathing, food, ordinary dorm conversation and sleep function as hero-support infrastructure; recovery labor is materially part of sustaining rescue capacity.
23. A dying Tartarus guard preserves a security recording; Stain recognizes the guard's conviction and entrusts the record specifically to All Might. This is constructive conduct without rehabilitation, pardon or exculpation of Stain's prior killings.
24. Tartarus data supports operational synchronization between original-body AFO and the consciousness operating through Shigaraki. The volume compares this with OFA but does not prove identical consciousness mechanics or unrestricted bidirectional thought-sharing.
25. The preserved signal is interpreted as `38日で完成させる`, and the current hero-side operational estimate at `V33:p183` is **roughly three days until completion**, superseding the earlier longer planning assumption for current operations.
26. International hero dispatch is constrained by domestic instability in other countries; U.S. No.1 hero **Star and Stripe** nevertheless initiates movement toward Japan because her mentor is in trouble. Arrival, combat and outcome remain V34+ OPEN.
27. V33's strongest social formulation is **special power does not create special expendability**. The hero exception collapses when a rescuer is treated as outside the class of people eligible to be rescued.

## Revision / claim transitions emphasized in V33

- **STRENGTHEN / REVISE:** Midoriya's self-sacrifice problem is not failure to notice care; he notices it and still denies its jurisdiction over him.
- **STRENGTHEN:** `余計なお世話` is reciprocal rather than one-directional; Midoriya's rescue ethic can authorize intervention into Midoriya himself.
- **STRENGTHEN / SPECIFY:** Bakugo's apology is an accountability act without a forgiveness demand and without personality flattening.
- **REVISE:** exceptional power does not entail exceptional personhood or exceptional expendability.
- **REVISE:** civilian participation is bounded co-production of public safety, not passive customer status and not equivalent professional/vigilante role collapse.
- **STRENGTHEN:** the greatest-hero frame becomes explicitly plural at V33, while its final meaning remains OPEN.
- **STRENGTHEN / SPECIFY:** All Might's post-Symbol legacy persists through independently acting people rather than through one irreplaceable body.
- **STRENGTHEN / BOUND:** Stain can perform constructive evidence-preserving/relay action without this functioning as rehabilitation or absolution.
- **REVISE:** current enemy-completion planning window is approximately three days based on Tartarus evidence; later accuracy/outcome remains OPEN.
- **OPEN:** final AFO/Shigaraki identity/control state, Star and Stripe arrival/combat/outcome, Kurogiri recovery, Toga countermeasure performance, later Toya-family confrontation and long-term civilian trust.
