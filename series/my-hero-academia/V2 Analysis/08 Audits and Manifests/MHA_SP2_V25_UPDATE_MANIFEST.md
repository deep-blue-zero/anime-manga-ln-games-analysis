## Major V25 analytical propositions

1. The Shimura catastrophe contains distinct intent states rather than one continuous moral category.
2. Mon and the early family deaths occur amid Tenko's confusion/panic and uncontrolled Decay propagation; Kotaro is later touched `明確な殺意をもって`.
3. Tenko experiences profound bodily relief after killing Kotaro and the recurrent itching ceases; this is strong reinforcement evidence but not retroactive proof that all earlier deaths were intended.
4. `誰か手を差し伸べてくれてたら` makes the post-catastrophe failed-rescue counterfactual canonical V2 evidence.
5. The public failure is **recognition without ownership of responsibility**: adults notice Tenko but do not convert perception into sustained intervention.
6. AFO performs the missing rescue-like hand extension and then exploits the resulting dependency.
7. AFO explicitly delegitimizes `善悪・道徳・倫理`, elevates Tenko's internal impulse, asks `君はどうしたい`, validates homicidal desire and affirms destructive enactment.
8. AFO gives Tenko his own surname `死柄木` and the new given name `弔`; the Shigaraki Tomura identity is directly authored inside the grooming relationship.
9. AFO's method preserves agency rather than replacing it: Tenko states real violent desire, but AFO selectively constructs a normative environment in which that desire is treated as the privileged authentic self.
10. Re-Destro's surrender is military, ideological and institutional: he recognizes Shigaraki as the realized freedom/liberation the MLA venerates and offers the army to follow him.
11. Shigaraki immediately asks about Re-Destro's corporate money/resources, preserving practical opportunism and ideological irreverence.
12. Re-Destro publicly describes succession as `必然の譲位` and Shigaraki as `真の解放者`.
13. `超常解放戦線` fuses League affective cohesion, MLA ideology/mass membership, corporate resources and specialized command nodes.
14. Shigaraki treats the organization's name as a `飾り`, showing that he uses inherited ideology/infrastructure without becoming doctrinally possessed by its vocabulary.
15. Ujiko's previous succession gate closes after Deika: demonstrated capacity, not AFO's designation alone, earns access to technical inheritance.
16. Midoriya achieves the first controlled V2 Blackwhip micro-output through a lock/unlock regulation model; combat-scale mastery remains later.
17. Bakugo's interview abrasiveness survives his rescue/team development, confirming domain-specific growth rather than global personality softening.
18. Best Jeanist's `名は願い` remains a privileged Bakugo self-authorship lesson; Bakugo withholds his chosen hero name while Jeanist is missing.
19. The Hero Public Safety Commission's abnormal mass internship request converts partial threat recognition into distributed preparedness across hero education.
20. Inko's `安心` response to Izuku's present face marks partial parental trust in visible maturation without erasing chronic protection anxiety.
21. Hawks operates under continuing Dabi/Skeptic suspicion and converts liberation literature plus deliberately anomalous speech into a partner-model-dependent covert channel.
22. Endeavor identifies Hawks's surface recommendation as `本心じゃない`, decodes the warning and becomes the intended recipient of the four-month intelligence.
23. Ujiko's Quirk singularity explanation frames generational power escalation as a body-governance problem in which capacity may outpace embodiment.
24. Shigaraki voluntarily accepts four months of extreme augmentation cost, adding future-oriented risk tolerance and successor self-authorship to the model.
25. The hero/villain timelines synchronize around the same four-month horizon: Ujiko prepares Shigaraki while Hawks/HPSC/Endeavor accelerate a distributed hero network.
