## Major V15 analytical propositions

1. V15 shifts from **earned legitimacy / reciprocal mastery** to **responsible action under uncertainty / contested futurity**.
2. Midoriya and Mirio correctly recognize Eri's distress before they possess enough evidence to complete a safe extraction; restraint preserves a later rescue path but produces genuine moral injury.
3. Eri's return to Chisaki is coercive compliance mediated through anticipated violence, not evidence that she is safe with him.
4. Failed rescue is relationally consequential: Eri later remembers Midoriya's hand as `優しかった`, so recognition can alter a victim's model of human contact before physical rescue succeeds.
5. Chisaki's public family claim functions as cover for private instrumentalization; Eri is named `計画の“核”` and her biological material is connected to prototype Quirk-destroying ammunition.
6. The prototype boundary remains explicit: V15 does not yet demonstrate a fully commercialized permanent Quirk-destroying round as an accomplished fact.
7. Nighteye's Foresight is constrained by a 24-hour interval, limited viewpoint and deliberate ethics of use; he subordinates prediction to ordinary investigation and probability-raising.
8. All Might reveals that he had internalized Nighteye's predicted death as an endpoint; Midoriya's demand that he live to witness `僕が来た` converts succession from replacement-through-death into intergenerational coexistence.
9. `僕も 一緒にねじ曲げます` is not proof fate can be changed; it is an ethical refusal to let uncertain inevitability erase agency.
10. Kirishima's V15 courage is constructed through training, self-command, named technique and mentor recognition; detailed later freeze/Crimson Riot causation remains OPEN.
11. Iida's cafeteria intervention demonstrates relationship learning: his own history of concealed crisis becomes the basis for recognizing and supporting Midoriya without demanding confidential details.
12. The Hassaikai operation is the institutional completion of V13's `群` logic: police, multiple agencies, teachers, sidekicks and licensed students convert distributed evidence into coordinated protective force.
13. Professional confidentiality has interpersonal costs; mature friendship may need to support a person without entitlement to case information.
14. The volume's temporal ethic is neither fatalism nor naive optimism: the past cannot be changed; whether the future can be changed is unknown; responsible action persists under that uncertainty.
