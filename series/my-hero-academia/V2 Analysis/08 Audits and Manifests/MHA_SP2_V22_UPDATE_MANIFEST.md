## Major V22 analytical propositions

1. Joint Training functions as epistemic infrastructure: it exposes team policy, Quirk affordances, planning assumptions, inherited craft, failure modes and rescue behavior under controlled but consequential pressure.
2. Match 2 resolves **4-0 for Class 1-B**, but Kendo's `勝った気しないな` separates scoreboard success from process quality. Yaoyorozu's plans continue to matter after her capture, so defeat does not negate strategic competence.
3. Yaoyorozu is strengthened as a planner whose decisions create downstream options for others rather than only as an on-field item generator.
4. Honenuki operates as a flexible systems/terrain thinker, creating enough grounded tactical evidence for a strong emerging model without prematurely promoting him to moderate.
5. Iida's Recipro Turbo is inherited family craft, not merely a new speed tier. Tensei offers the bodily modification conditionally - `おまえがもっと走りたいと思うなら` - and the technique requires `激痛と忍耐`; inheritance preserves recipient choice.
6. Todoroki's technical development is selective appropriation. Endeavor's `自覚しろ おまえは誰よりも強い力を秘めていると!!` can become useful embodied knowledge without implying relational reconciliation or forgiveness.
7. Match 3 ends in a draw. The result reinforces the distinction between character/power-model evidence and simple win-loss hierarchy.
8. Bakugo's Match 4 growth is procedural rather than cosmetic. His abrasive command register remains recognizable while his policy now incorporates teammate sensing, rescue, reciprocal cover and coordinated offense.
9. Class 1-A wins Match 4 **4-0**, but Bakugo is not a lone carry. The team architecture is what allows his aggression to become both rapid and rescue-compatible.
10. Midoriya explicitly reads Bakugo's development through the `勝って救ける` synthesis, strengthening the long-running reciprocal correction between All Might's two heirs.
11. Monoma's `誰もが 他人の人生の脇役であり / 自分の人生の主役なんだ` gives the series an explicit anti-protagonist-bias formulation: narrative centrality to one observer is not existential centrality to another person.
12. All For One's Tartarus line `弟の声がする` is source-grounded evidence of resonance with the OFA-side disturbance, but V22 does not yet establish a complete causal mechanism for that perception.
13. The inherited-power eruption is not deliberate Quirk selection. It appears as a control failure inside Match 5 and makes Midoriya temporarily dangerous to classmates.
14. Midoriya's repeated `止まれ` and concern that he does not want others to worry show that his first impulse remains to self-contain the problem. V22 then demonstrates that solitary containment is insufficient.
15. Uraraka's childhood motive is now much more specific than generalized kindness: she watched the faces around heroes because `人の喜ぶ顔が好きだった`, while helping people in difficulty felt ordinary rather than exceptional.
16. `ヒーローが辛い時 誰がヒーローを守ってあげられるだろう` establishes a major rescue-theory transition: heroes/rescuers are legitimate rescue subjects, not only providers of rescue.
17. Uraraka's intervention is not romantic decoration or passive concern. She physically enters a hazardous uncontrolled-power event, stabilizes Midoriya's body and recruits Shinso's unique control affordance.
18. Shinso's Brainwashing becomes emergency rescue/control infrastructure. `俺と戦おうぜ` produces a response and interrupts Midoriya's runaway state; a coercive-looking Quirk is made ethical through purpose, discipline and context.
19. Shinso explicitly recalls wanting to use his Quirk `人の為に`, strengthening the argument that Quirk affordance is not moral destiny.
20. The prior holder's V22 message establishes that an inherited predecessor power has manifested and is now too amplified to use safely with `雑念マシマシ`. Mental/emotional condition is therefore a meaningful control variable.
21. V22 does **not** establish a deterministic rule that anger alone causes the inherited power, nor does it establish the later complete multi-Quirk roster or controlled mastery.
22. The volume's larger power philosophy is anti-solitary without being anti-agency: teammates, mentors, predecessors and inherited craft enlarge what a hero can do, but ethical maturity includes deciding how to integrate those supports rather than surrendering authorship to them.
23. The rescuer-as-rescue-subject principle links Uraraka/Midoriya to the series' broader rejection of one-way heroic legitimacy. Needing intervention does not disqualify the person who ordinarily intervenes for others.
24. The V22 visual grammar repeatedly turns distributed competence into spatial form: battlefield division, rescue relays, team formations and the uncontrolled tendril spread make relational dependence visible rather than merely verbal.

## Character-model readiness changes

- **Midoriya Izuku:** remains `strong`; V22 adds loss-of-control behavior, concern about burdening others, receptivity to peer stabilization and first direct evidence of a newly manifested predecessor power.
- **Uraraka Ochaco:** **`moderate -> strong`**. V22 adds childhood motive structure, observer orientation toward others' affect, decisive hazardous intervention, rapid recruitment of another specialist and the explicit rescuer-of-heroes principle to her already substantial crisis/ordinary/relationship evidence.
- **Bakugo Katsuki:** remains `strong`; very strongly strengthened by reciprocal rescue policy and team-compatible command behavior without false surface softening.
- **Iida Tenya:** remains `strong`; strengthened by voluntary family-craft inheritance and high-cost Recipro Turbo control.
- **Todoroki Shoto:** remains `strong`; strengthened by selective technical appropriation from Endeavor without relational collapse.
- **Yaoyorozu Momo:** remains `moderate`, very strongly strengthened by forward planning that survives capture and by opponent recognition of her planning as the central threat.
- **Kendo Itsuka:** remains `moderate`; strengthened by victory, accurate opponent modeling and dissatisfaction with a result that did not feel like strategic superiority.
- **Shinso Hitoshi:** remains `moderate (very strongly grounded)`; strengthened by emergency rescue use of Brainwashing and explicit human-oriented purpose, but ordinary/noncompetitive and relationship breadth remain insufficient for `strong`.
- **Monoma Neito:** enters/advances `emerging (strongly grounded)` through explicit self/narrative philosophy, tactical Copy use and adversarial social behavior.
- **Honenuki Juzo:** enters `emerging (strongly grounded)` from flexible environmental control, team coordination and adaptive planning.
- **Tetsutetsu Tetsutetsu:** advances `emerging (strongly grounded)` through endurance, direct-pressure values and sustained Todoroki engagement.
- **Tokage Setsuna:** enters `emerging (strongly grounded)` from reconnaissance, decomposition/reassembly tactics and team-plan behavior.
- **Ashido Mina:** remains `insufficient/emerging (strongly grounded)`; V22 adds tactical/peer evidence but not enough cross-context breadth.
- **Mineta Minoru:** remains `emerging`; V22 adds bounded tactical evidence without changing tier.
- **Shoda Nirengeki:** remains `insufficient/emerging`; bounded tactical contribution only.
- **Pony Tsunotori, Kaibara Sen, Yanagi Reiko, Kodai Yui:** gain tactical baselines only; no premature readiness promotion.
