## Major V12 analytical propositions


1. V12 shifts from **accountable succession / de-centered support** to **operational self-authorship / reliable coordination**.
2. `必殺技` is defined through reliable form before spectacle: professional capability is capacity that can be repeatedly enacted under changing circumstances, and it need not be an attack.
3. Aizawa's explicit competency list prevents power-output reductionism: information, judgment, mobility, combat, communication, appeal and leadership coexist inside professional hero competence.
4. All Might enacts post-Symbol mentorship through answer-withholding. `答えを教えるだけでは 教育とは言えない` makes student discovery, not teacher reproduction, the pedagogical objective.
5. Shoot Style is not best modeled as rejection of All Might. Midoriya identifies an unconscious inherited-form assumption — OFA therefore fists — and redesigns the interface to fit his medically constrained body.
6. Support technology is part of agency when it translates limitations into reliable affordances. Hatsume's design culture helps Midoriya reconceive embodiment; Kaminari's gear converts indiscriminate output into ally-compatible precision.
7. Public legibility is operationally ambivalent. Sports-Festival exposure makes U.A. students tactically predictable to rivals, while Aoyama later converts conspicuous light into an ally beacon.
8. Midoriya's `僕の知ってる麗日さんじゃない` demonstrates that accumulated relationship knowledge includes behavioral prediction: visually plausible identity can fail when action policy is wrong.
9. Bakugo's `“借り物”… 自分のモンになったかよ` supplies external confirmation of the inheritance→authorship transition from one of Midoriya's most attentive antagonistic observers.
10. Iida's `俺の夢の形` gives the volume a second inheritance model: predecessor values can remain causally present while action becomes self-authored.
11. Aoyama's conspicuousness acquires a relational motive and cooperative function. `対等になりたかった` links self-presentation to equality/belonging rather than allowing flamboyance to stand as a complete personality explanation.
12. All 20 Class 1-A students clear Stage 1, but that success cannot be generalized into civilian-facing professional reliability. V12 ends before Stage 2 rescue evaluation begins.


The strongest compact formulation is:


> **A heroic value becomes professionally meaningful when it can survive translation into a repeatable technique, a teachable decision process, a cooperative protocol, or an institutionally legible form without losing the agency of the person who carries it.**
