## Core V31 adjudications

1. The war aftermath is neither simple hero victory nor simple defeat: large-scale PLF suppression coexists with catastrophic casualties, escapes and state-capacity collapse.
2. Midnight is confirmed dead; Bakugo, Aizawa and Shoto survive into hospital aftermath.
3. Tartarus falls; seven prisons are attacked and prisoners are released from six.
4. Shigaraki explicitly demands his body back and rejects pawn status; AFO defines him as `next me`, making replacement-self succession explicit.
5. Spinner distinguishes AFO from the Shigaraki he follows.
6. Hawks's HPSC command constraint effectively disappears; he still chooses helping, strongly validating autonomous motive without erasing institutional conditioning.
7. Hawks continues to recognize Twice/Bubaigawara as a good person trying to be useful after the lethal threat decision.
8. Hero resignations, civilian support-item self-defense and `I AM NOT HERE` establish a national capacity/legitimacy crisis rather than mere PR damage.
9. Todoroki-family causation is explicitly asymmetric: Shoto names Endeavor as initiator/cause before the family shares present repair responsibility.
10. Rei calls Shoto the Todoroki family's hero and credits his U.A. friendships with keeping the family connected.
11. Shoto's singular responsibility for Toya becomes a family `we will stop Toya`; final method remains OPEN.
12. Shinomori Hikage is identified as the fourth holder; Danger Sense and the 18-year/age-forty lifespan evidence establish OFA vessel overload in already-Quirked bearers.
13. All Might's forty-year tenure is explained by Quirkless compatibility; Midoriya may be the last successor.
14. OFA transfer is mechanically governed by holder will, sharpening the contrast with AFO's possessive succession.
15. The vestiges explicitly did not share Midoriya's V30 perception that Shigaraki looked as though asking to be saved.
16. Midoriya acknowledges Shigaraki's killings and that lethal stoppage may be necessary, but chooses wanting to save the crying child he perceives as his preferred aim.
17. OFA's inherited meaning is explicitly expanded from anti-AFO opposition toward saving through historical use.
18. All Might accepts that the changed threat environment requires broader OFA disclosure to necessary actors.
19. Endeavor publicly acknowledges family truth; apology is separated from atonement-as-work under scrutiny.
20. U.A. becomes civilian refuge infrastructure.
21. Midoriya discloses OFA to Class 1-A by letter and leaves U.A.
22. The endpoint deliberately preserves the self-exclusion problem: moral self-authorship strengthens while risk ownership remains unilateral.
