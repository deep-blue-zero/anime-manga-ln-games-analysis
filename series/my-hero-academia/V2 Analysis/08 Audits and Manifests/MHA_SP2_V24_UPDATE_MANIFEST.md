## Major V24 analytical propositions

1. Curious possesses real biographical information about Toga but converts it into MLA propaganda; factual observation is therefore not equivalent to reciprocal recognition.
2. Toga explicitly contests `普通の暮らし` and claims `普通` for her own life, while the manga refuses to treat that self-definition as moral exculpation.
3. Toga's childhood directly establishes Quirk-linked attraction to blood, parental fear/pathologization, normalization pressure, suppression and a constructed social mask.
4. `個性カウンセリング` is shown as care infrastructure whose implementation can slide into adaptation-to-normality rather than governable self-understanding.
5. While transformed as Uraraka, Toga directly uses Zero Gravity after observing Uraraka's use; the observed case is canonical, but complete later copied-Quirk mechanics remain OPEN.
6. Curious's death is immediately converted into martyr propaganda, showing that the MLA treats even its own adherents' biographies as movement resources.
7. Shigaraki's returning childhood material is initially fragmentary and intensely unpleasant; memory recovery increases autobiographical coherence without functioning as automatic healing.
8. Decay's field behavior expands under pressure, but V24 does not authorize a universal trauma-awakening law.
9. Twice's identity wound is a specific feared ontology: uncertainty over whether he is the original and whether sufficient injury would make him disappear.
10. V24 preserves both structural precarity and Twice's own bad choices in his formation history rather than making him a passive product of society.
11. Giran's `仲間` routing is a successful recognition intervention with a criminal destination, proving recognition itself is ethically underdetermined.
12. Severe injury without disappearance supplies Twice embodied falsification of the feared copy ontology; attachment to Toga and the League then converts restored certainty into Sad Man's Parade.
13. Twice's `連合の皆は俺の居場所なんだ!!` and `俺を受け入れてくれたおまえたちに報いたいんだ!!` make belonging an operational capability multiplier.
14. Geten explicitly states `異能の強さが社会的地位と直結する`, revealing a power-ranked hierarchy inside MLA liberation politics.
15. Re-Destro explicitly asserts `"個性"は人格に直結するものだ!`, making Quirk/personality essentialism an ideological claim rather than neutral narration.
16. Spinner accepts his status as a dependent `小市民` but rejects the shame hierarchy by pointing out that MLA followers likewise depend on Re-Destro.
17. V24 historicizes `異能` -> `〈個性〉`, showing humane terminology as a political achievement without treating it as proof that stigma or suppression has been solved.
18. Hawks's Best Jeanist-related action is an extreme covert trust test; V24 does not prove that Hawks killed Jeanist or that the supplied body is authentic.
19. AFO explicitly instructed Shigaraki to keep the family hands close so grief/anger would not fade, turning the hand ritual into direct anti-healing conditioning evidence.
20. `感情に経験が伴って往く` describes returning memory as experience attaching itself to already active affect.
21. Adult Shigaraki rejects Re-Destro's demand that destruction justify itself through creation: `俺は本当にただ 壊すだけだ` / `未来なんか要らないんだ`.
22. Child Tenko nevertheless still wants to become a hero and includes excluded peers in hero play; adult destructive identity cannot be projected backward as timeless essence.
23. Nana's separation letter confirms loving protective intent, while Kotaro's `いっそ嫌ってくれたらよかったよ` confirms the act's downstream experience as abandonment injury.
24. Kotaro converts that injury into an anti-hero household rule and later physical violence, but V24 preserves each choice as a distinct causal transition rather than deterministic inheritance.
25. The Shimura home is summarized by `僕を優しく否定する`: affection and structural invalidation coexist; household adults oppose Kotaro only after violence raises the intervention threshold.
26. Tenko's recurrent itching is strongly associated with domestic stress but remains physiologically/Quirk-mechanically underexplained at this boundary.
27. V24 deliberately cuts at the first visible cracking of Mon, before any completed death or intent sequence; later origin material is barred from V24 authority.
