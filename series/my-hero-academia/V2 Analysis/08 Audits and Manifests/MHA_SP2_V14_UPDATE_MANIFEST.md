## V14 readiness changes


- **Midoriya Izuku:** remains `strong`, strengthened. He explicitly self-authors the integrated standard `必ず勝って / 必ず助ける`; accepts that All Might's designation does not eliminate the burden of later justification; and enters Nighteye's professional test without treating inherited legitimacy as sufficient.
- **Bakugo Katsuki:** remains `strong`, very strongly strengthened. V13 guilt disclosure is resolved through All Might's adult assumption of responsibility; the OFA secret becomes a three-person trust structure; Bakugo then converts rivalry into useful technical critique of Midoriya's Shoot Style.
- **All Might / Yagi Toshinori:** remains `strong`, strengthened. He refuses a winner-take-all reading of his two student heirs, explicitly integrates victory and rescue, and reclaims causal responsibility for choices that Bakugo had internalized as personal guilt.
- **Shigaraki Tomura:** remains `moderate`, strengthened. He rejects Overhaul's coercive authority yet does not simply burn the organizational future for immediate retaliation; the `責任とらせろ!!!` demand coexists with delayed strategic adjudication.
- **Togata Mirio:** enters `emerging (strongly grounded)`. V14 grounds social facilitation, high professional competence, predictive timing, and mastery of a difficult Quirk through training rather than effortless giftedness.
- **Sir Nighteye:** enters `emerging (strongly grounded)`. He reveres All Might without treating All Might's successor choice as self-validating; hires Midoriya as an empirical legitimacy test while explicitly withholding recognition.
- **Overhaul / Chisaki Kai:** enters `emerging (strongly grounded)`. V14 grounds coercive hierarchy, controlled violence, instrumental organizational bargaining, contact-aversion behavior, and a ruler-oriented response to the post-All-Might/AFO vacuum. Later causal explanations remain OPEN.

## Major V14 analytical propositions


1. V14 shifts from **distributed professional trust / integrated responsibility** to **earned legitimacy / reciprocal mastery**.
2. Ground Beta converts the Midoriya/Bakugo rivalry from a zero-sum legitimacy contest into a dual inheritance problem: `救けて勝つ / 勝って救ける`. Neither rescue nor victory is sufficient alone.
3. Adult responsibility matters causally. All Might directly tells Bakugo that the OFA transfer was his own decision and that Kamino was not Bakugo's responsibility, redistributing guilt rather than merely praising resilience.
4. The OFA secret changes structure from a two-person secret to a three-person trust relationship. Bakugo's inclusion is not abstract information acquisition; it changes what can be said and learned among the three.
5. Rivalry becomes reciprocal mastery when Bakugo gives usable technical feedback on Shoot Style. Midoriya is no longer only extracting knowledge from Bakugo by observation; Bakugo intentionally contributes to Midoriya's development.
6. Mirio is a control against Quirk essentialism. Apparent invincibility is shown as difficult body-technique-experience integration rather than nominal power possession.
7. Midoriya's `必ず勝って / 必ず助ける` is a self-authored professional standard, not merely repetition of All Might's words.
8. Nighteye separates **designation** from **legitimacy**. OFA can be transferred by the holder's will while the social/professional claim that the choice was best remains contestable.
9. Nighteye's hiring logic is deliberately adversarial: `だが 認めたわけではない`; the work-study is an empirical test designed to expose Midoriya to professional reality and potentially make him abandon the successor claim.
10. Midoriya answers that challenge without appealing to authority alone: `絶対 認めさせてみせます!`. His claim becomes future-facing and performance-dependent.
11. Overhaul and the League reveal competing organizational legitimacy models. Overhaul demands `俺の傘下に入れ`; Magne asserts chosen `居場所`; Shigaraki refuses submission yet delays immediate retaliation enough to preserve organizational agency.
12. V14 begins the Eri problem but does not yet adjudicate it. Any claim about choosing investigation over immediate rescue belongs to V15 unless independently evidenced there.
