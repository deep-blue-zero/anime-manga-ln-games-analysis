## Major adjudications

- Toya's body model is revised: V39 establishes a latent Rei/Himura-side cold factor capable of death-edge manifestation. This does not establish stable routine dual-Quirk mastery.
- The Todoroki-family crisis is collective rather than Shoto-solitary: Iida transport + Rei/Fuyumi/Natsuo cooling + Shoto's decisive technique form the causal chain. Family-wide recognition (`みんな…見てる`) does not erase Toya's hatred.
- Shoto explicitly articulates a mature relational policy: conversation can create conflict, but conflict can sometimes increase understanding; he wants to become someone who can be angry/cry for others as his friends do.
- Endeavor's atonement advances from unilateral burden/self-punishment toward receptive listening and differentiated apology. He asks Toya to continue speaking hatred and does not demand forgiveness.
- Toga's childhood evidence strongly expands villain formation: atypical blood/love desire, parental dehumanization, counseling toward `普通`, shame, later violent enactment, League belonging, grief/anger and chosen enemy agency all matter. No monocausal innate-evil or society-made-her model survives.
- Tsuyu explicitly revises a rule-centered hero/villain ontology: she once equated rule compliance with hero status and deviation with enemy status; V39 shows her recognizing a harder relational rescue path without abolishing law/accountability.
- Toga's `私は トガヒミコとして 生きるのです` refuses a separate villain-self identity. Rescue cannot depend on pretending the dangerous present self is unreal.
- Uraraka's mature rescue doctrine is explicit: intentional killing cannot be made nonexistent, but Toga's smile cannot be made nonexistent either. Recognition and accountability coexist.
- Zero Gravity undergoes death-edge/contact-chain propagation while preserving Uraraka's non-harm power philosophy.
- Toga and Uraraka reach reciprocal recognition rather than one-way hero interpretation. Toga notices Uraraka's stable restraint and finally chooses to give rather than take blood: `私の血 全部あげる`.
- Toga's total transfusion is presented as knowingly fatal self-sacrifice and is accompanied by specific apology; formal post-act medical/death confirmation remains V40+ OPEN.
- Dabi's destruction of Toga's hated childhood house did not erase the internal wound; Toga explicitly says it remained in her heart. Uraraka's refusal to erase it makes the burden feel lighter. Destructive erasure and restorative recognition are therefore distinguished.
- Armored All Might is not a technology-equals-Quirk thesis. V39 explicitly states support gear breaks and has limits. Toshinori's effective action depends on engineering, behavioral knowledge, timing, pain tolerance and a support network.
- All Might explicitly fights to win rather than to die, despite knowing Nighteye's prophecy and accepting extreme lethal risk.
- All Might's suit literalizes received social inheritance: student-derived functions and `私が与えられてきたもの 全てをぶつける!!` frame power as what is given through relationships, in direct contrast to AFO's extractive ownership.
- All Might uses a longitudinal behavioral model of AFO: broad attacks, weakening, Quirk theft and All-Might-conditioned hatred/face-saving. AFO's high intelligence remains vulnerable to relationship-conditioned compulsion.
- Teenage Toshinori's pre-OFA Symbol-of-Peace theory is explicitly anti-extractive and anti-grievance: he rejects a world where takers profit and victims' sadness becomes hatred. `平和の象徴` is conceived as public assurance, not merely combat supremacy.
- `“無個性”には 役割が ないから` establishes Quirklessness as experienced social rolelessness. Toshinori self-assigns a society-wide role, preserving both heroic initiative and the causal seed of later overcentralization.
- `人の役に立てて 嬉しい` adds unusually clean evidence that being useful to others is intrinsically joyful for Toshinori.
