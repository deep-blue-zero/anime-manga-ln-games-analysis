## Major adjudications

- Armored All Might remains explicitly finite: equipment breaks and does not erase the Quirkless/embodiment problem. His tactical value comes from received engineering, behavioral knowledge of AFO, deliberate delay and willingness to use everything given to him.
- Stain returns as an All Might ally under his own ideology. `等しく赤い` grounds intervention in shared bodily materiality; `生きて勝て` strengthens his true-hero model toward survival plus victory rather than sacrificial purification alone. AFO had prepared a Bloodcurdle counter and takes `凝血`.
- Aoyama's reparative arc continues under uncertain institutional future; Hagakure converts Invisibility/refraction into active joint rescue/combat support and exhibits temporary stress-linked visibility.
- Shigaraki directly rejects AFO's attempted summons (`先生…邪魔するなよ`), materially strengthening present separability/agency.
- The Nighteye death point is explicitly reached and then `運命が捻じ曲がった`; V40 supplies accumulated relational/material intervention as the causal route rather than requiring a free-floating supernatural hope force.
- Bakugo is alive and conscious. Midoriya uses Gearshift to launch him; Bakugo rescues All Might. The action is co-authored without role collapse.
- Edgeshot is alive but severely depleted. His repair was indispensable, while `俺は…繋いだだけだ` and `お前が磨いてきた“個性”` preserve Bakugo's cultivated Quirk as causal in awakening.
- Bakugo accepts rescue/support without hierarchy injury: he thanks Edgeshot, accepts All Might's gear, rejects AFO's Kudo identity imposition, and states `俺たちの物語だ!!` plus `俺一人で…勝てるワケねーーンだよ`.
- AFO/Yoichi origin becomes direct evidence: severe birth deprivation and maternal death coexist with very early possession orientation. The same narrative culture produces Yoichi's hero aspiration and AFO's deliberately chosen Demon King dream.
- OFA's earliest mechanism is explicitly composite: Yoichi's tiny transfer factor merges with a power AFO forcibly gives him. Yoichi's `与えるも奪うも` formulation makes relation/consent/use, not abstract transfer mechanics, the ethical variable.
- AFO's manipulation heuristic becomes direct behavioral evidence: read others' hate/desire, use affective leverage, convert resulting vulnerability into control/possession.
- `全因解放` concentrates accumulated factors under one owner/objective. As Rewind youth and negative emotion intensify, `支配権` and factor integration weaken.
- AFO's final plan is to reach Shigaraki and reinforce/reinstall himself. He fails. The external Rewind body regresses to infancy and disappears; Shigaraki later states `先生が死んだな`.
- The sequence imagining stolen Quirks returning is not literal restitution; Hawks's vestige immediately rejects that imagined `サービス`.
- After AFO's external death, Shigaraki continues an independently stated destruction project aimed at leaving no `再興の芽`. He gives AFO bounded credit for making him stronger without reverting to obedience.
- The prospective endpoint is `V40:p199`: the Fourth bearer reacts and `危機感知` becomes the explicit unresolved state. Exact loss/theft/transfer/function remains V41+ OPEN.
