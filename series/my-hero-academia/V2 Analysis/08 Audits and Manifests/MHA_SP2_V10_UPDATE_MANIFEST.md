## V10 readiness changes




- **Bakugo Katsuki: `moderate → strong`.** V10 supplies a genuinely new hostile-recruitment/isolation regime and a clean held-out validation pass. His refusal is explicitly grounded in the previously established All Might-victory ideal rather than retrospective personality labeling.
- **Iida Tenya: `moderate → strong`.** His V06 failure and V07 repair now generalize prospectively: when classmates approach an unauthorized rescue resembling his own Hosu error, he recognizes the pattern, argues from responsibility and institutional externality, then joins specifically to prevent them from destroying themselves.
- **All For One: `emerging (strongly grounded) → moderate`.** V10 adds direct mentorship under subordinate failure, pain interpretation, absolution, availability, protected retry, praise of self-directed thought/leadership, ally recovery, and successor-specific Quirk curation.
- **Kirishima Eijiro: `insufficient/emerging → emerging (strongly grounded)`.** V10 establishes an explicit action threshold tied to heroic/manly self-conception and a preference for a no-combat rescue route after perceived failure to act.
- **Best Jeanist: `insufficient/emerging → emerging`.** The Kamino raid adds crisis triage, multiple-person protection, professional field mastery, and AFO's direct recognition that Jeanist's strength derives substantially from training and practical experience rather than Quirk possession alone.
- Midoriya remains `moderate (very strongly grounded)`. The explicit two-to-three-more-arm-injuries warning is a major new knowledge-state constraint, but V10 does not yet show how reliably he will act on it under a later rescue trigger.
- Shigaraki, Aizawa, All Might, Yaoyorozu and other established models remain at their prior major tiers while receiving material V10 strengthening where evidence supports it.




## Major V10 analytical propositions




1. Volume 10 shifts from **emergency enactment** to **contested legibility / protective authority**. After `完全敗北`, rescue is no longer only about willingness to act; it becomes a question of who has understood the person/crisis correctly and what authority follows from that understanding.
2. The League's Bakugo recruitment is a strong held-out falsification of surface-trait moral inference. Bakugo's public aggression is real; the inference from aggression to villain recruitability is wrong. His `俺は オールマイトが 勝つ姿に 憧れた` directly reactivates the V07 Origin model.
3. Bakugo refuses even strategic false compliance (`したくねーモンは嘘でもしねんだよ俺ァ`), strengthening a self-authorship rule that can override obvious short-term instrumental advantage.
4. Kirishima, Midoriya, Todoroki, Yaoyorozu and Iida do not represent one undifferentiated act of “student disobedience.” Their hospital debate is a conflict over rescue impulse, jurisdiction, covert/noncombat action, internal stopping rules, and responsibility for institutional externalities.
5. Iida's repair becomes longitudinally predictive. His Stain failure is no longer merely remembered guilt; it changes how he protects classmates approaching analogous self-authorized danger.
6. U.A. accepts public responsibility without epistemic surrender. Aizawa apologizes for his educational responsibility while explicitly rejecting the League's moral reading of Bakugo as `浅はか`.
7. The professional counter-operation contrasts with student rescue not by absence of emotion but by conversion of concern into intelligence, simultaneous targets, role differentiation, accountable coercion, and extraction priorities.
8. The raid's `我々が来た` pluralizes the reassurance grammar previously concentrated in All Might. Professional rescue is visually and grammatically staged as collective capacity even while the Symbol remains central.
9. AFO turns Shigaraki's defeat into protected retry. His mentorship combines failed-rescue interpretation, absolution (`君は悪くない`), availability (`僕がいる`), validation of asking for help, preservation of allies, praise of self-directed thought and leadership, and a promise that failure can be attempted again because he will remain the support infrastructure.
10. Subjective self-authorship and structural manipulation therefore remain compatible. Dependency may be strengthened by making another person's self-authored attempts survivable rather than by visibly dictating every choice.
11. Best Jeanist supplies a power-philosophy control case: stolen capacity does not automatically transfer accumulated practice, field experience, tactical judgment or temperament fit. AFO explicitly rejects Jeanist's Quirk for Shigaraki despite recognizing its effectiveness.
12. The volume ends before Kamino resolves. The final All Might/AFO outcome and the succession/public-symbol consequences belong prospectively to Volume 11.
