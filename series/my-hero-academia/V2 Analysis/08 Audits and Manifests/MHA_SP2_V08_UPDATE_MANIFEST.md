## Major V08 analytical propositions


1. Volume 8 is organized around **diagnostic resistance**: a wall is developmentally useful when it reveals the actual bottleneck rather than merely demanding greater force.
2. Yaoyorozu's final-exam difficulty is confidence-filtered access to her own judgment, not lack of intelligence. Todoroki restores her epistemic standing with concrete evidence of his prior trust, after which she resumes planning authority.
3. Todoroki learns that sincere respect for another person does not guarantee that their judgment is functionally entering a shared plan; recognition must alter coordination to matter.
4. Aizawa explicitly defines the practical final as calibrated diagnostic adversity. Teachers preserve `勝ち筋` while observing how students face their assigned problem, strengthening the model of U.A. as high-demand but scaffolded rather than pure survival-of-the-fittest sorting.
5. Midoriya/Bakugo functional cooperation is confirmed under extreme shared-goal pressure. Bakugo can materially enable Midoriya while retaining aggressive register and dependency discomfort; this is behavioral change without emotional reconciliation.
6. Koda's fear of insects is a context-specific block rather than evidence of global cowardice. Visible partner harm makes inaction feel causally his responsibility and crosses his action threshold.
7. Minor Class 1-A characters increasingly resist one-trait fandom reduction: Jiro, Koda, Mineta, Aoyama, Tsuyu, and others receive context-specific behavioral evidence beyond visual gimmick or comic function.
8. All For One's `排斥ではなく受容を` reveals a successor-making style capable of cultivating subjective self-authorship while preserving structural dependency.
9. Shigaraki's `全部 オールマイトだ` and `今日からそれを信念と呼ぼう` mark his largest ideological transition so far: the assigned future symbol of fear begins naming a conviction as his own.
10. All Might's `あるよ たくさん` / `悔しいが 私も人だ` proves that Toshinori knows he cannot save everyone. The Symbol-of-Peace critique must therefore distinguish his private awareness of limitation from society's public interpretation of concentrated reassurance.
11. Kota supplies a bereaved child's counter-reading of heroic sacrifice. Publicly praised rescue can be privately inherited as abandonment and loss.
12. Midoriya does not answer Kota with propaganda. His restraint establishes that recognition can mean acknowledging a grief-grounded worldview before having an honest argument capable of changing it.
13. The camp closes by distinguishing mental/technical development from Quirk-capacity development and beginning explicit `個性を伸ばす` conditioning.
14. The first-pass V08–V09 combined treatment is chronologically too coarse. V08 ends before the forest attack; the Muscular/Kota test belongs prospectively to V09.
