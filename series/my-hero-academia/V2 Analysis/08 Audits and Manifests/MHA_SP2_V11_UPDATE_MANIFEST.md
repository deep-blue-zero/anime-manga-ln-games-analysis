## V11 readiness changes




- **Midoriya Izuku: `moderate (very strongly grounded) → strong`.** V11 supplies the held-out result V10 explicitly requested: after learning that repeated comparable arm damage can permanently remove future rescue capacity, Midoriya helps execute Bakugo's lethal-context extraction through a distributed/no-combat plan without new injury. All Might directly marks it as Midoriya's first injury-free escape from a dangerous situation. Midoriya also states that even outside U.A. he will become a hero, widening identity beyond institution/mentor.
- **All Might / Yagi Toshinori: `moderate (very strongly strengthened) → strong`.** V11 resolves the V10 battle, extinguishes OFA embers, terminates the combat-Symbol role, adds public/private succession, colleague correction, post-power teaching adaptation, and parental accountability. The model is now sampled across substantially different capability/status regimes.
- **Asui Tsuyu: `emerging (strengthened) → moderate`.** V11 adds principled peer disagreement, failed-prevention pain, self-directed inadequacy, vulnerable disclosure and ordinary-life relationship repair.
- **Midoriya Inko: `insufficient/emerging (strengthened) → emerging (strongly grounded)`.** V11 supplies sustained institutional confrontation, explicit values, conditional consent and direct correction of All Might's sacrificial promise; broader ordinary-life and independent relationship breadth remains limited.
- **Bakugo Mitsuki:** enters `insufficient/emerging (strongly grounded)` through a narrow but high-information family scene distinguishing superficial praise from accurate recognition and grounding her conditional trust in U.A.
- Bakugo and Iida remain `strong`; Bakugo gains equality-compatible help acceptance and repayment, while Iida's institutional-accountability orientation survives the successful outcome of the rescue.
- Kirishima remains `emerging (strongly grounded)` and is strongly validated as an equality-compatible relational interface for Bakugo.
- All For One remains `moderate`, very strongly strengthened through explicit mentor-withdrawal / `一人立ち` theory and defeat-to-succession conversion.
- Shigaraki remains `moderate (very strongly grounded)`; V11 advances genealogy and succession framing, but post-AFO independent leadership remains prospective.




## Major V11 analytical propositions




1. Volume 11 shifts from **contested legibility / protective authority** to **accountable succession / de-centered support**. The central problem is how values and institutions survive when a former center can no longer carry them alone.
2. Bakugo's rescue succeeds because Midoriya models the recipient rather than centering the rescuer. He predicts that Kirishima's `対等な関係` makes Kirishima's hand more acceptable than his own.
3. The V10 bodily-sustainability question receives a positive held-out test: Midoriya solves a lethal crisis without fighting or further injury, proving that relational intelligence and explicit future-capacity knowledge can alter action selection under pressure.
4. All Might's exposed weak body does not instantly destroy public support; AFO tries to weaponize the body/role gap, but civilians' calls for All Might become reciprocal motivational infrastructure.
5. Nana's smile instruction establishes the Symbol's reassuring smile as inherited rescue pedagogy: the protector performs `自分は大丈夫だ` so frightened people can believe survival remains possible.
6. `次は君だ` is structurally doubled. All Might's public warning privately transfers succession burden to Midoriya; AFO independently tells Shigaraki `次は君だ` after explaining that a teacher exists to make a student `一人立ち`.
7. All For One's mentorship therefore cannot be modeled as dependency engineering alone. He deliberately removes the relied-upon mentor center so Shigaraki's hatred, alliances and self-directed leadership can grow: **engineered autonomy inside manipulated option-space**.
8. All Might's post-battle identity is not emptiness. Gran Torino redirects him toward unfinished duties at U.A.; he later tells Midoriya he will devote himself to the successor's development.
9. Inko rejects substitutive sacrifice as legitimate mentorship. `この命に代えても` fails; `命に代えないで / ちゃんと生きて / 守り育てて下さい` becomes the condition for consent.
10. Kota's letter allows Midoriya to articulate that U.A. is not the source of his hero identity. All Might recognizes that Midoriya is no longer merely following his back.
11. Mitsuki's U.A. trust depends on accurate recognition, not prestige. She distinguishes superficial praise of Katsuki's visible ability from Aizawa demonstrating that the school actually `勝己を見てくれてる`.
12. Aizawa refuses outcome-based exoneration. Successful covert rescue does not erase the breach of teacher trust; students must rebuild legitimacy through `正規の手続き` and legitimate hero work.
13. Tsuyu converts failed prevention into relationship repair rather than silent resentment. The class's return to `日常` is an actively reconstructed social achievement, not a reset button.
14. V11 closes before provisional-license technique development is observed. `必殺技を作ってもらう!!!` is a V12 handoff, not V11 evidence about the techniques themselves.




## First-pass transitions




- **PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN:** Kamino turns inheritance into burden, but V2 specifies the process through final embers, public/private succession, post-Symbol role loss and the requirement that the mentor remain alive as support.
- **PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN:** Midoriya can save without being the literal reaching hand. V11 now grounds the exact mechanism in relationship-conditioned routing: Kirishima's equality-compatible relationship with Bakugo makes his call the highest-probability interface.
- **REVISE:** “Midoriya's danger is martyrdom” is too coarse. V11 supplies a clean counterexample in which explicit bodily-risk knowledge changes his rescue method under lethal stakes.
- **STRENGTHEN / REVISE:** AFO/Shigaraki succession is not merely grooming through dependency. V11 directly establishes mentor withdrawal and `一人立ち` as part of AFO's design while leaving the option-space manipulatively structured.
- **REVISE:** Inko is not an anti-hero or anti-U.A. obstacle. Her objection is institution-specific and child-welfare grounded; Kota's letter and All Might's willingness to change the support contract materially alter her consent.
- **NEW / STRENGTHEN:** Aizawa's legitimacy model is outcome-insensitive at the rule level but context-sensitive at the sanction level; success does not legalize the rescue, while All Might's retirement crisis changes whether expulsion is administratively responsible.
- **NEW:** Tsuyu's conflict/repair sequence establishes ordinary relationship restoration as a modeled behavior domain rather than treating dorm life as tonal decompression only.
- **OPEN:** Shigaraki's post-AFO independent leadership; deeper Tenko/Shimura causation; AFO's ultimate motive; Dabi identity/motive; Toga causal biography; later Midoriya sustainability under another immediate rescue trigger; provisional-license techniques and exam outcomes.
