## Major new analytical propositions

1. V04 is organized around self-authorship under unequal inheritance: people must decide what to do with bodies, powers, opportunities, and relationships they did not fully choose.
2. All Might and Endeavor are rival successor architectures: entrustment allows the successor to author the inheritance; instrumentalization authors the successor for the elder's goal.
3. Todoroki's refusal to use fire is a negative authorship strategy: he attempts to deny Endeavor's ownership of his body and achievement, but remains organized around the father's terms.
4. Midoriya explicitly acknowledges present privilege despite past exclusion and defines his current self relationally through the people who saved and supported him.
5. Shinso demonstrates that Quirk stigma and institutional misrecognition can be real without mechanically producing villainy; alternate recognition routes matter.
6. Ojiro and Shoda show that procedural advancement can still feel illegitimate when the beneficiary cannot regard the result as self-authored.
7. Iida and Uraraka use competition as individuation rather than simple hostility toward friends.
8. OFA predecessor-like silhouettes become textually relevant under Brainwashing, but exact vestige ontology remains prospective OPEN.
