## Major adjudications

- Toga treats remaining Twice blood as a finite strategic resource rather than an unlimited army switch; Uraraka preserves an unfinished ordinary conversation (`恋バナ`) and later notices Toga's tears while the mass threat remains active.
- La Brava's hacking and Gentle's Elasticity return as hero-side capacities without personality purification. Gentle's most diagnostic held-out result is acting heroically while believing nobody is watching; later visibility makes the truth socially legible rather than motivating the action.
- Lady Nagant voluntarily re-enters under serious residual injury after medical warning, converting trained marksmanship into chosen protective action. Present action gives survival meaning without retroactively purifying HPSC violence.
- Shigaraki states that AFO genuinely fused with him but that a hidden `原点` recovered him from within. The recovered self explicitly joins Shigaraki/Tenko naming with a continuing destructive salvation wish. Midoriya answers with anti-erasure recognition: `泣いていた君を 見なかった事には しない`.
- Business Course students and reporters make recording, `繋ぐ`, process and outcome into hero-society infrastructure. Committed witnessing is compatible with transmitting defeat as well as victory.
- Inasa rejects AFO's attempt to monopolize historical knowledge through lived terror; inherited history can be learned and judged without inheriting submission. Shishikura similarly refuses to let personal hatred become AFO-controlled behavior.
- Toga's `好き` condition is shown not to be a mechanically transparent conscious switch for copied-Quirk use; sincere love and inability to reproduce loved persons' powers coexist.
- Repeated hero attacks accelerate AFO's finite self-erasing Rewind trajectory. As AFO becomes younger, raw power rises while negative affect becomes harder to suppress.
- Mina's Gigantomachia trauma remains active, but recurrence no longer dictates paralysis. `アシッドマン・ALMA`, deliberate post-failure training and `弱いから…寄り添い合っていたい` produce a mature vulnerability/interdependence model. Kirishima rejects reciprocal rescue as debt repayment.
- Shinso's Persona Chords/Brainwashing helps route Gigantomachia, but after control breaks Machia's anti-AFO abandonment grievance persists. `俺の“個性”で 心までは操れない!!` explicitly separates behavioral coercion from ownership of motive.
- Gigantomachia's loyalty is therefore narrow but genuine relationship-conditioned agency rather than permanent AFO property; AFO's affectionate/promissory conditioning creates reciprocal expectations whose betrayal becomes grievance.
- Mineta supplies a clean prosocial self-risk counterexample by trying to protect Tokoyami/Dark Shadow and offering Pop Off instead.
- Dabi/Toya becomes a projected mass-casualty thermal threat beyond Endeavor's fire-based ability to solve alone. Command identifies Iida's `Engine` + Shoto's `Half-Cold Half-Hot` as the necessary complementary response.
- Iida reactivates the inherited Ingenium service ideal through `迷子を導く為にあるんだから`, using his legs to carry the person whose distinct capacity is needed.
- No.386 explicitly returns to Midoriya's V01 Quirkless-hero question. Quirkless Toshinori places himself in AFO's path with support equipment and declares `私が / AM HERE`; tactical outcome and equipment limits remain V39+ OPEN.
