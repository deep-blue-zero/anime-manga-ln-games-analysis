## Major V06 analytical propositions

1. Volume 6 is organized around **claimed identity versus enacted identity**: names, ideals, inheritances, convictions, and powers become ethically meaningful only through conduct under stress.
2. Hero naming is substantive future-self construction. Iida's deferred and then revenge-conditioned adoption of `Ingenium` is the volume's sharpest symbol/conduct contradiction.
3. Full Cowling is not merely safer lower-output OFA; `個性は体の一部` marks embodiment and successor individuation, reducing All Might imitation as a technical and psychological shackle.
4. Manual gives the series' clearest early doctrine of legitimate hero coercion: exceptional power is conditionally entrusted through law, trust, and public role; `私怨` and `私刑` can turn a hero into a criminal.
5. Stain can correctly diagnose Iida's immediate rescue-priority and motive failure while remaining radically illegitimate. **Moral perception does not confer moral jurisdiction.**
6. Iida's pain initially narrows moral attention toward revenge, while Todoroki begins converting his own resentment history into prosocial recognition of another person's danger.
7. Midoriya operationalizes `余計なお世話` as inferential, embodied rescue: he reads Iida's abnormal affect and location inside the larger Hosu crisis and moves toward a hidden person-specific emergency.
8. Internships pluralize professional heroism across image discipline, martial training, media labor, patrol, law, mentorship, local civic work, and emergency response.
9. Villain formation is now explicitly causally plural: Shigaraki's developmental/grievance/mentorship route, Shinso's stigma without villainy, and Stain's moral-purification absolutism cannot be collapsed into one failed-recognition explanation.
10. Nomu becomes stronger personhood/toolhood evidence as an engineered body configured around externally assigned function; the exact creator and full mechanism remain prospectively OPEN at the V06 boundary.
