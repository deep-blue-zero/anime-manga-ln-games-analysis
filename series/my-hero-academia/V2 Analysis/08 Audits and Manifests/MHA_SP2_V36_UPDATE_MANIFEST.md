## Major adjudications

- Shoto's Phosphor integrates hot/cold circulation around the heart and explicitly reverses Endeavor's intended reproductive/body-design logic. He recognizes Toya's family-produced wound while rejecting causal fatalism; Great Glacial Aegir stops Toya's immediate offensive, but later Toya survival/remobilization/relationship state remains OPEN.
- Jiro experiences direct AFO-class terror, uses that experience to reinterpret the fear Midoriya and Aoyama carried, and acts because AFO made her friends cry. Her vibration catalyzes abnormal vestige resistance in an already damaged AFO Quirk-factor ecology; it is **not** raw-power defeat of AFO.
- Tokoyami explicitly rejects substitution: he cannot become Endeavor, but he can coordinate with Hawks. V36 makes non-substitution a positive model of distributed agency.
- AFO's current perception is a composite acquired vibration/infrared/etc. sensory architecture rather than intrinsic omniscience. His wound-cultivation method is clarified through the `soil`/seed metaphor, and he demonstrates a prearranged disposable-body contingency after apparently kill-level Endeavor damage.
- Endeavor explicitly rejects moral rebirth/purification, keeps `しつこい` persistence as continuous self-material, accepts that he damaged other people's futures, and defines adult responsibility through keeping adults' past from blocking young people's roads.
- Floating U.A. becomes visibly continuous maintenance rather than a static superweapon. Material replacement, power generation, Erasure, repair, combat and Business Course documentation are all necessary functions.
- Business Course recording is treated as hero-society mediation/social-memory infrastructure, not decorative comic relief.
- The fused Shigaraki/AFO entity explicitly frames difference -> incomprehension -> fear/exclusion and proposes universal exploitation beneath one hand as the solution. The hero coalition provides the countermodel: differentiated, non-interchangeable people coordinating without flattening each other.
- Mirio's “no friends” provocation triggers specific Shimura/Tenko childhood memories (Mikkun, Tomo-chan, Mon) and an explicit `志村` identity marker. This proves psychological nonuniformity; it does **not** settle separability/control/rescue.
- Nejire's curiosity receives a causal social history: peer distancing under unreciprocated questioning and stronger selfhood through reciprocal Big Three friendship.
- Tamaki converts the formerly crushing weight of other people's belief into usable support while anxiety remains; his large Manifest attack incorporates Nejire's energy and multiple consumed capacities.
- Bakugo explicitly understands Monoma/Aizawa/Kaminari/Momo/support labor as conditions for his own maximum action. Shigaraki weaponizes Bakugo's relationship to Midoriya; Bakugo's private rivalry becomes `俺まだ おまえに追いつけるかな 出久`, preserving competition while recognizing Midoriya's lead.
- Cluster strain produces full-body explosive discharge and increased speed; this is a bodily consequence of his existing Quirk, not a new Quirk.
- The All Might card/autograph wish adds a quiet childhood-fan layer to Bakugo's model.
- **V36 endpoint restraint:** Bakugo is in catastrophic, apparently terminal cardiac/chest condition and `心臓が—` is explicit, but the volume does not provide final medical/life-state adjudication. Definitive death/recovery remains **V37+ OPEN**.
