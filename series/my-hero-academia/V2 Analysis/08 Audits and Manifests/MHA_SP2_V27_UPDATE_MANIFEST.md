## Core V27 adjudications

1. Garaki's public medical/institutional role is real social camouflage over hidden Nomu/Quirk infrastructure; villain capacity is no longer spatially confined to visibly criminal institutions.
2. Hosu and Kamino are explicit hero-side trauma/institutional-memory inputs into the preemptive operation.
3. Quirk duplication is now admissible V2 evidence, while a universal mechanism for every repeated Quirk remains beyond the volume.
4. High-End production strengthens the person-to-function thesis: selected human material, Quirk inventory and combat disposition are technically optimized while agency is subordinated.
5. AFO's explicit friend-language toward Garaki strengthens their relationship beyond employer/technician and demonstrates malignant recognition as organizational glue.
6. Mirko learns through engagement, tracks stabilization, adapts under catastrophic injury and treats survival as instrumentally useful without allowing death risk to veto the mission.
7. Kaminari's fear is preserved. Midnight converts anonymous public duty into a concrete attachment anchor, after which he executes the specialist electrical counter for which he was selected.
8. Hero preventive ethics become explicit: organized trained combatants must not be allowed to escape because future harm is predictable; `protect` and `attack` are now joined inside the same institutional rationale.
9. Hawks initially restrains Twice, plans police handoff, offers atonement/restart help and personal assistance, and calls him a good person. V2 therefore rejects a sequence that begins with an immediate intent to execute him.
10. Twice rejects the offer because his own future is subordinate to League belonging. His value hierarchy is `self-preservation < repayment of belonging debt < League welfare`.
11. Dabi's intervention reopens the catastrophic Double threat. Hawks lethally neutralizes the original Twice after surrender/containment/rehabilitation have failed under active battle conditions.
12. Twice's last clone rescues Toga and Compress, returns Toga's handkerchief and dissolves in her arms. Toga explicitly thanks him for saving her.
13. Twice's terminal appraisal rejects the outsider description of mere bad luck: he regards the life in which he could remain with the League as genuinely happy.
14. Dabi knows Hawks's private name `Takami Keigo` and weaponizes identity information. He whispers another identity fact to Hawks, but V27 formally withholds the content from the reader.
15. Dabi says League/Shigaraki are not his ultimate commitments and invokes Stain's will. V27 therefore rejects a monolithic `League found family` model and distinguishes Dabi's more instrumental ideological affiliation from Twice/Toga reciprocal attachment.
16. Hawks's childhood HPSC-linked identity discontinuity and special hero training program are now canonical causal-biography evidence. His own rescue aspiration is also genuine, so institutional opportunity and institutional ownership coexist.
17. Tokoyami converts mentorship into reverse rescue by reaching/extracting the critically burned Hawks without possessing the reader's full Hawks/Twice knowledge.
18. The endpoint does **not** resolve Shigaraki's actual awakening/procedure result. Mirko remains injured and operational; Endeavor has reached the underground High-End fight; broader raid outcome remains future evidence.
