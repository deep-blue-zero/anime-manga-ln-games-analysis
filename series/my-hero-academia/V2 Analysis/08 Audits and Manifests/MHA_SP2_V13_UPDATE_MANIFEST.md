## V13 readiness changes


- **Midoriya Izuku:** remains `strong`, strengthened. Rescue-stage mistakes are corrected in-process; he demonstrates civilian reassurance, distributed rescue logic, and relationship-sensitive recognition of Bakugo's concealed burden.
- **Bakugo Katsuki:** remains `strong`, very strongly strengthened. V13 corrects the rescue model from bad triage to accurate physical triage plus professionally defective civilian-facing communication, then reveals a major hidden guilt/responsibility state around All Might's retirement.
- **Todoroki Shoto:** remains `strong`, strengthened. V13 directly validates that unresolved Endeavor-conditioned resentment can narrow interpersonal perception strongly enough to damage professional coordination, while also showing rapid corrective cooperation once recognized.
- **Uraraka Ochako:** remains `moderate`, strongly strengthened. V13 crosses the prior boundary from Ashido's external `恋だ` label into Uraraka's own explicit romantic self-recognition while preserving her effort to regulate that affect around professional work.
- **All Might / Yagi Toshinori:** remains `strong`, very strongly strengthened. `私は死なないぞ` is direct behavioral uptake of Inko's V11 anti-martyr correction and confirms a post-Symbol commitment to surviving as teacher/support rather than treating death as the natural completion of heroism.
- **Yoarashi Inasa:** `insufficient/emerging → emerging`. V13 explains his U.A. refusal, shows a sincere anti-coldness/anti-Endeavor value structure, and exposes the way that value can itself become tunnel vision.
- **Twice:** enters `emerging (strongly grounded)`. His mask functions as self-coherence infrastructure; duplicated-self trauma destabilizes identity certainty; the League is explicitly valued as `居場所`.
- **Toga Himiko:** remains `insufficient/emerging (strongly grounded)`, strengthened. V13 resolves the V12 presented-Camie impersonation within the source boundary and makes Transform's blood-input identity mechanism explicit without collapsing her broader psychology into Quirk mechanics.
- **All For One:** remains `moderate`, strongly strengthened. V13 clarifies succession through `託す`: he can preserve ideology/organization by deliberately handing future agency to Shigaraki rather than merely retaining direct command.


## Major V13 analytical propositions


1. V13 shifts from **operational self-authorship / reliable coordination** to **distributed professional trust / integrated responsibility**.
2. Rescue professionalism is recipient-facing. Correct intent and even correct triage are insufficient if the civilian cannot safely interpret or rely on the hero's conduct.
3. Bakugo's rescue failure is revised: HUC recognizes accurate low-priority triage, while deductions target his hostile communication. Judgment and recipient compatibility must therefore be modeled separately.
4. HUC formalizes rescue as a networked process: triage, stabilization, evacuation, reassessment and `橋渡し` into fire/police/emergency infrastructure.
5. Midoriya's All-Might inheritance is now selectively functional rather than bodily imitative: reassurance remains worth carrying forward even after combat-form imitation is rejected.
6. Todoroki/Inasa show that unresolved private history is a professional variable when it captures attention and degrades coordination. Integration is not emotional decoration; it is operational competence.
7. Provisional licensing is conditional delegated emergency authority. The remediation path for failed candidates shows an institution testing trustworthiness rather than demanding moral purity or permanent exclusion.
8. The Commission explicitly rejects waiting for another All Might-like `一強` and proposes a deliberately coordinated `群` of heroes as the post-Symbol institutional answer.
9. Twice provides a villain-side model of fractured self-certainty plus belonging: his mask is behavioral infrastructure and the League is `居場所`, strengthening motive pluralism rather than a universal grievance theory.
10. All For One's `託す` demonstrates that distributed succession is ethically neutral in itself; villain organizations can also build durable post-founder agency.
11. All Might's `私は死なないぞ` converts Inko's V11 demand from received criticism into action policy: survival becomes part of responsible mentorship.
12. Bakugo's final disclosure reveals that his strength drive has become partly guilt-regulatory. `俺は……オールマイトを終わらせちまってんだ` is not simple rivalry but a private causal self-accusation that Midoriya recognizes he is uniquely positioned to receive.


The strongest compact formulation is:


> **Heroic reliability is not isolated strength. It is the capacity to become trustworthy across persons, teams, institutions, and one's own inherited history. The post-All-Might world therefore moves from singular reassurance toward distributed professional trust, while individual characters learn that self-authorship requires integrating—not erasing—the relationships, wounds, and responsibilities that shaped them.**
