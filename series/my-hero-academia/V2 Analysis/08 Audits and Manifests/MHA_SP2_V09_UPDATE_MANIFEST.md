## Major V09 analytical propositions


1. Volume 9 shifts from **diagnostic resistance** to **emergency enactment under broken guarantees**. The lethal environment is not calibrated for student growth and does not preserve a teacher-authored `勝ち筋`.
2. Midoriya's Muscular rescue should not be reduced to either technical incompetence or generic martyrdom. He continues analyzing and chooses catastrophic bodily expenditure when he judges Kota's survival to be contingent on continued action.
3. Successful local rescue can reduce later distributed rescue capacity. The sustainability problem is now a command/resource-allocation problem, not merely an injury-avoidance lesson.
4. `ワン・フォー・オール 1000000%` is direct textual evidence for the attack name and emotional overdrive; exact literal multiplication beyond 100% remains OPEN.
5. Kota's shift is experiential rather than argumentative: being the protected person gives him access to the perspective of people saved by his parents without making his bereavement mistaken.
6. Aizawa's `戦闘を許可する` establishes accountable emergency delegation. It does not abolish the earlier licensing/supervision framework or authorize general private violence.
7. Shigaraki's V08 `信念` begins producing decision rules. The forest attack targets the credibility of protected hero space, uses Bakugo's public legibility as recruitment intelligence, and coordinates heterogeneous actors toward one operation.
8. The Vanguard Action Squad is not ideologically uniform. Muscular's predatory enjoyment, Spinner's Stain loyalty, Toga's intimacy/predation grammar, Mustard's status resentment, Dabi's still-OPEN personal motive, and other members' different drives require motive-plural modeling.
9. Muscular is a strong control case against monocausal social-victim explanations of villainy: voluntary domination and enjoyment of violence remain necessary causal categories in the MHA model.
10. Information routing becomes part of rescue. Midoriya, Mandalay, Kendo, Shoji, Aizawa, and others show that perception matters only when it reaches the people whose action can change the crisis.
11. Bakugo's public aggression has become villain-side selection data, but the League's interpretation of him remains an external inference. Volume 10 must test it against Bakugo's own behavior rather than treating recruitment as proof of recruitability.

- **REVISE / STRENGTHEN:** Kota is not converted by hero rhetoric; direct rescue supplies a protected-person perspective that can coexist with grief.
- **STRENGTHEN:** Full Cowling improves control but does not solve the ethics/sustainability problem of rescue-first self-expenditure.

- **OPEN:** exact `1000000%` mechanics, Bakugo's response to recruitment, final forest-camp capture outcome, Dabi's identity/motive, Toga's causal biography, and the wider public/institutional consequences of the attack.
