## Core V30 adjudications

1. Bakugo survives the V29 interception and immediately preserves the anti-solitary-victory critique: `一人で…勝とうと…してんじゃねェっ`; later medical outcome remains future evidence.
2. Midoriya's rage after Bakugo is injured shows that sincere care can degrade judgment; rescue motive does not immunize the rescuer against dysregulation.
3. Shigaraki explicitly asserts `俺の意志だ 俺の夢だ`, preserving self-authored destructive agency even as AFO pressure intensifies.
4. OFA vestiges explicitly choose continued co-presence with Midoriya (`僕たちはこの子の中にいる事を選んだ`; `この力はこの子についていく`) and reject AFO's seizure attempt.
5. V30 therefore makes the inheritance contrast source-explicit: OFA succession is voluntary relational co-presence, while AFO succession increasingly asserts possessive jurisdiction over the successor body.
6. AFO fails to take OFA in the vestige encounter. This is a local V30 outcome, not proof that later acquisition pressure disappears.
7. Toga exploits Uraraka's rescue reflex through disguise and then asks a genuine personal question about what heroes intend to do with people like her after Twice's death.
8. Uraraka answers with recognition plus accountability rather than annihilation or impunity: `やっちゃった事は消えない!!` and responsibility must be accepted.
9. Toga's quiet `ありがとう` / departure is a meaningful state change but not reconciliation, pardon or conversion.
10. Dabi publicly identifies himself as **Todoroki Toya**, resolving the V27 reader-withheld identity register.
11. Dabi/Toya's family grievance is substantially corroborated by the already-established Endeavor household record, but his national broadcast is a strategic rhetorical object rather than a neutral evidentiary package.
12. Best Jeanist's living return directly falsifies a central public implication in Dabi's framing without falsifying Endeavor's abuse history. Truthful grievance and truthful presentation must be analyzed separately.
13. `過去は消えない` becomes contested language: Dabi uses the past as an identity sentence and weapon, while Midoriya preserves the same past while attending to present repair effort.
14. Endeavor again freezes when family history invades professional action; Shoto must call him back to the present emergency.
15. Shoto treats Toya as both family member and current lethal threat while refusing to reduce Toya to Endeavor. The breadth now supports `strong -> specialist_ready`.
16. Midoriya's defense of present Endeavor is not victim-proxy forgiveness: he explicitly says the past does not disappear and speaks from his own mentor relationship/current observation.
17. Dabi/Toya explicitly instrumentalizes Shoto's prospective suffering/death as a route to Endeavor's suffering and defines himself as the crime of the self-described side of justice. Dabi/Toya advances `moderate -> strong`.
18. Best Jeanist returns alive, resumes active duty (`今日より活動復帰する!!`) and contributes decisive restraint/field leadership; he advances `emerging -> moderate`.
19. Bakugo finally reveals **`大・爆・殺・神ダイナマイト`** in Jeanist's presence, resolving the long-held hero-name register and confirming that the chosen audience mattered.
20. Yaoyorozu's V29 Gigantomachia anesthetic plan produces delayed but real physiological effect; good judgment is now joined by causal validation without pretending the plan was immediate or sole-cause.
21. Eri's supervised/trained Rewind restores Mirio's Permeation. This resolves the specific Mirio-restoration register but does not establish unrestricted Rewind mastery.
22. Mirio returns to battle using Permeation; his restored power does not erase the period of Quirk loss or Eri's role in restoration.
23. Mr. Compress supplies Harima-lineage causal/self-myth evidence, accepts severe bodily cost to preserve the coalition's escape chance, and frames himself as collaborator rather than final protagonist; current responsible readiness is `moderate`.
24. Compress entrusts Shigaraki specifically to Spinner because Spinner is the League member most centered on Shigaraki; coalition role knowledge is relationship-specific rather than generic camaraderie.
25. Spinner attempts to reach Shigaraki through Shigaraki-specific hand/family-object memory while AFO control is increasing; Spinner remains `moderate`, strongly strengthened.
26. Midoriya identifies and manifests the fourth inherited Quirk **`危機感知` / Danger Sense**. First manifestation is painful/uncontrolled, so mastery and the fourth holder's full biography remain OPEN.
27. AFO states that his coexisting consciousness strengthens and substantially operates Shigaraki's body to direct Nomu/escape by the endpoint. Successor agency violation is now explicit V2 evidence.
28. AFO's bodily control does not retroactively erase Shigaraki's independent choices, doctrine or culpability. V30 requires both self-authored harmful agency and later agency violation.
29. Midoriya explicitly remembers Shigaraki's killings and harm and does not simply forgive him, yet sees the AFO-overwritten Shigaraki as though he were asking to be saved (`救けを求めたように見えた`).
30. The endpoint perception is **Midoriya's inference**, not narrator-certified innocence or a proven conscious request by Shigaraki. It opens a rescue problem; it does not solve it.
31. The V01 rescue grammar therefore returns at maximum moral difficulty: perceiving an unspoken need can motivate rescue even when the rescue subject is also a responsible perpetrator.
32. V30's strongest ethical formulation is **rescue eligibility != innocence** and **saving != forgiving**.
