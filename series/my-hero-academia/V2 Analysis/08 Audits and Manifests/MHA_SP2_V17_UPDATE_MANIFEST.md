- **Togata Mirio:** `moderate -> strong`. V17 adds childhood/family context, long technical mastery, victim-facing promise behavior, catastrophic Quirk loss, severe injury and competent action without Permeation.
- **Eri:** `emerging -> moderate`. V17 adds origin, repeated coercive contexts, learned pain expectation, altruistic self-surrender, renewed movement toward rescue, Rewind mechanics and relationship-specific behavior. Ordinary baseline/language/post-trauma breadth remain too sparse for `strong`.

- **Toga Himiko:** remains `insufficient/emerging (strongly grounded)`, strengthened by trust-conditioned information withholding and League-first coalition behavior.

- **Shin Nemoto:** enters `emerging (strongly grounded)` from social isolation, trust hunger, selective-recognition loyalty and tactical behavior.

## Major V17 analytical propositions

1. V17 shifts from **role-bearing courage / strategic trust under pressure** to **identity beyond power / rescue as restored agency**.
2. Mirio's Permeation loss is catastrophic capability loss but not identity erasure. The acquired person - prediction, timing, protection, pain tolerance and role commitment - survives the mechanism.
3. Eri's surrender is prosocial in motive and coercively distorted in policy. Chisaki weaponizes her care for other people to make self-sacrifice appear morally obligatory.
4. Rescue advances from correct recognition and renewed pursuit toward restored victim agency: Eri begins moving toward rescue, and Midoriya's endpoint plan explicitly asks for her cooperation.
5. Rewind is a dangerous mutation returning living bodies toward earlier states; its danger does not morally contaminate Eri, and V17 does not grant her fine conscious control.
6. Infinite 100% is **initiated**, not completed, in V17. The endpoint is a coupled rate hypothesis: continuous 100% OFA damage countered by continuous Rewind.
7. Nighteye's Foresight remains credible evidence. V17 gives Midoriya an anti-fatalist commitment, not proof that the future has branched.
8. Chisaki must be modeled more precisely than “pure anti-care.” Nemoto demonstrates selective recognition capable of generating sincere loyalty while Eri remains subordinated to project utility.
9. League-Hassaikai cooperation remains grievance-bearing, politically divided and non-assimilative; Toga/Twice act as Shigaraki-trusted agents inside a hostile temporary coalition.
10. The completed Quirk-destroying projectile now has named-target field effect and explicit scarcity/refinement logistics.
