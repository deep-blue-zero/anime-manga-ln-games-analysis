## Major new analytical propositions

1. Hero and villain systems are already constructing **rival public successors**: All Might asks Midoriya to inherit him and make his arrival known, while Shigaraki's unseen mentor asks him to make fear known through a villain symbol.
2. The Sports Festival functions as spectacle, pro-hero recruitment market, internal mobility/reclassification mechanism, support-tech exhibition, business/branding training surface, and public status allocator.
3. Midoriya's rescue-first ethics do not imply competition aversion; he becomes intensely competitive when victory has professional/relational meaning.
4. Uraraka's economic motive is family-directed care and reciprocity.
5. V03 explicitly frames Shigaraki as developmentally immature but capable of growth under skilled guidance without yet revealing origin causation.
6. Public recognition creates opportunity and attack surface simultaneously.
