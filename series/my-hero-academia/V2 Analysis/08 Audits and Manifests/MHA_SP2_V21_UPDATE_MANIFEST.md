## Major V21 analytical propositions

1. V21 shifts from **visibility under consequence / responsibility before multiple witnesses** to **plural adjudication / legitimacy as situated trust**.
2. Endeavor defeats High-End, but Hawks is causally indispensable to the final acceleration; elite hero legitimacy is compatible with distributed dependence.
3. Public confidence in Endeavor forms through witnessed persistence rather than inherited All Might-like certainty.
4. Endeavor's raised fist quotes All Might's Kamino iconography without inheriting identical social or moral meaning.
5. Dabi's immediate post-victory intervention prevents High-End from becoming a clean symbolic reset.
6. Hawks is explicitly a Hero Public Safety Commission infiltrator: `敵連合に取り入れ ホークス`.
7. Dabi changes the High-End test conditions and withholds trust; infiltration is credibility under hostile verification, not control of the adversarial relationship.
8. Todoroki-family judgment remains decentralized. Natsuo can recognize attempted change while explicitly refusing forgiveness; Fuyumi and Shoto retain different repair/evaluation positions.
9. Professional hero achievement and fatherhood are separate moral jurisdictions; success in one cannot adjudicate the other.
10. OFA becomes source-grounded partial historical consciousness through a sustained vestige-space encounter.
11. All For One's historical taking/giving of Quirks is a dependency-producing social technology, not merely capacity accumulation.
12. Power-giving can be coercive when bodily/role authorship is absent.
13. `君は一人じゃない` makes inherited resistance relationally present to Midoriya; waking OFA discharge connects historical encounter to present embodiment.
14. V21 does not establish later full multi-Quirk mechanics; those remain prospectively locked.
15. Shinso's Persona Chords professionalizes Brainwashing by changing its interface while preserving the answer-trigger condition and ethical responsibility for coercive use.
16. Aizawa's Shinso mentorship is exacting sponsorship: access/support without lowered professional standard.
17. Class A's greater real-villain exposure is not automatic superiority over Class B's structured team development.
18. Match 1 is a distributed Class A + Shinso victory, not a singular Shinso rescue performance.
19. Asui manipulates identity/scent inference; Kaminari uses area-threat expectation as deliberate pressure; Joint Training repeatedly treats combat as information control.
20. Hawks mentors Tokoyami by identifying underused affordance rather than demanding imitation: `君はもっと自由に動ける` / `飛べる奴は飛ぶべきだよ`.
21. Tokoyami's `黒の堕天使` converts Dark Shadow from only a regulation-risk relation into a self-authored mobility partnership.
22. Kendo expresses respect for Yaoyorozu through accurate suppression priority: `考える時間は与えない`.
23. Yaoyorozu converts adversarial esteem into self-demand (`今の私を見せなさい!`), but the volume ends before the outcome is known.
24. V21 ends at `V21:p193` in the middle of Match 2; later match results, later Shinso status and later OFA expansion are not V21 authority.
