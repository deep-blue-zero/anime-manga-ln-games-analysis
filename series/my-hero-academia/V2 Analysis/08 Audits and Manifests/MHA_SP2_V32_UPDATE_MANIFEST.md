## Core V32 adjudications

1. Midoriya's rescue ethic does not assume every villain secretly wants rescue: Muscular rejects the alternate-path premise and Midoriya stops him.
2. Shindo's vibration materially contributes to Muscular's defeat; the rematch is not a pure solo-causation demonstration.
3. Midoriya's old catastrophic arm-damage mechanism is substantially improved, but the sustainability problem migrates into chronic sleep/food/wound/attention depletion.
4. Midoriya tells Inko he will not return to U.A. during the hunt and continues to assign risk unilaterally despite her objection.
5. Gran Torino survives and tells Midoriya that killing can sometimes be a form of salvation; this is a possibility/judgment frame, not a categorical kill order.
6. Midoriya protects a heteromorphic civilian from fear-driven civilian attack while acknowledging fear without validating the violence.
7. The second and third OFA holders cooperate after Yoichi identifies the second holder's offered hand as the act through which OFA began.
8. OFA predecessor Quirks are explicitly treated as tools; inherited capability is separated from predecessor imitation.
9. Fa Jin plus 45% OFA and movement composition creates `pseudo-100%`; this is not continuous ordinary 100% OFA output.
10. Lady Nagant is established as Hawks's HPSC predecessor/senior and former covert HPSC killer.
11. HPSC secret orders included extrajudicial killings concealed to preserve public legitimacy; Nagant's repeated compliance and later choices remain her own acts.
12. Nagant's exit from the HPSC was made unsafe through threat; institutional moral injury becomes a distinct formation route.
13. Nagant voluntarily accepts AFO's contract, but AFO secretly installs a lethal contingency for later defection.
14. Midoriya rescues Chisaki reflexively despite knowing his history, separating immediate rescue from moral innocence.
15. Midoriya infers Nagant's residual heroic restraint from what her precision allowed versus what she actually chose to do.
16. Midoriya asks Nagant to fight with the heroes; AFO detonates her when her heart changes. She survives the immediate event in critical condition and supplies the Haibori-forest lead.
17. Midoriya conditions future help to Chisaki on whether he can direct his desire to apologize toward Eri; rescue and accountability remain separate layers.
18. Hawks addresses Nagant as `senpai` after defection/escape and grounds his own retained orientation in support from others plus stable optimism.
19. AFO explicitly shifts primary interest from All Might to Midoriya and increasingly attacks Midoriya's compassion, urgency and burden model psychologically.
20. Desutegoro grounds at least one hero resignation in ordinary burnout under public hostility, fatigue and danger rather than simple cowardice/status hunger.
21. All Might's post-Symbol support becomes food, tracking, transport, warning and presence; Midoriya increasingly refuses that embodied care.
22. Urban rumor says the helping figure no longer looks like a hero, separating moral function from conventional heroic legibility.
23. Dictator exploits Midoriya's chosen isolation/exhaustion and uses controlled civilians as shields; Midoriya preserves their agency distinction and refuses to injure them.
24. The vestiges ask what could supplement Midoriya's present state; the volume structurally answers with Bakugo/Class 1-A's arrival rather than another power effect.
25. The V32 endpoint establishes Class 1-A contact and immediate tactical intervention only. The actual relational confrontation remains V33 evidence.
