## Core V28 adjudications

1. Shigaraki's procedure is catastrophically interrupted around the displayed **75%** state, but he nevertheless awakens operationally. V28 does not support a clean/full-completion shorthand.
2. Garaki makes `Quirk Singularity` an explicit in-world theory and supplies its social history: he was mocked/rejected for it decades earlier.
3. Garaki's longevity Quirk approximately doubles vitality/lifespan at the cost of motor ability; the Garaki currently present carries a duplicate.
4. AFO anticipated defeat and arranged succession by retaining/using a duplicate while transferring the **original All For One Quirk** to Shigaraki.
5. Shigaraki's internal succession sequence retains active Shimura-family memory while an AFO-associated voice says `Do not deny me`. V28 therefore supports neither `pure AFO puppet` nor `perfectly autonomous destroyer` as a sufficient model.
6. The first OFA holder warns Midoriya of the approaching transcendent threat before the physical-world information network has complete explanatory access.
7. Awakened Decay propagates through connected matter at city scale; geography becomes an attack surface and evacuation topology becomes a core hero responsibility.
8. Crust spends his final action preserving Aizawa during the Decay wave. The Erasure user is a legitimate rescue subject because his continued function protects the whole field.
9. Shigaraki calls Gigantomachia by the master's recognized scent and tells him to come with the others; the actual route/arrival/destruction remains V29+ OPEN.
10. Toga's grief over Twice becomes an explicit world judgment: heroes obstruct the world in which she can live among what/whom she loves. This is motive evidence, not moral exoneration.
11. Search is now used by Shigaraki for predatory target acquisition while Ragdoll remembers its rescue value. The same informational capability can be morally re-embedded by agent, purpose and institution.
12. Shigaraki experiences an AFO-linked drive to obtain One For All and uses Search to identify Midoriya as the target.
13. Midoriya reasons that public disclosure of himself as the target would divert scarce hero resources; he privately tells Endeavor he can lure Shigaraki toward a place without civilians.
14. Bakugo immediately understands the target problem and accompanies Midoriya, but explains the action through unique competence, Kamino/All Might debt, rivalry and `complete victory`, preserving prosocial conduct and abrasive self-description simultaneously.
15. Midoriya's current stated Full Cowl baseline is about **30%**, with about **45%** available at impact to reduce body load. Later Float/combat use remains outside V28.
16. Shigaraki combines multiple Quirks and disrupts communications, exposing information continuity as a structural dependency of hero coordination.
17. Gran Torino extracts Midoriya/Bakugo, rejects the idea that current Midoriya can safely contain Shigaraki alone, and treats OFA theft as a collective worst case rather than a private bearer cost.
18. Gran Torino returns to function as `Eraser's legs`, making support/mobility an explicit self-authored battlefield role.
19. Aizawa's Erasure suppresses Shigaraki's active Quirk use, but the modified body remains physically overwhelming without Quirks. Erasure is indispensable but not sufficient.
20. Manual's continuous eye support, Gran Torino's mobility, Crust's rescue, Endeavor/Ryukyu/offensive pressure and evacuation/communications make the containment state materially distributed.
21. Aizawa explicitly wants to survive to continue watching Class 1-A graduate and become heroes, converting earlier survival-oriented pedagogy into direct future-oriented attachment.
22. `V28:p180` ends with Midoriya and Bakugo re-entering the confrontation. The battle outcome is not V28 evidence.
