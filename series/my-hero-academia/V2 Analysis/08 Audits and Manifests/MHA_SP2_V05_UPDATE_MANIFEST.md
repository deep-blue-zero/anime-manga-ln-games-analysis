## Major V05 analytical propositions

1. Volume 5 is organized around **recognition without possession**: accurate recognition takes another person's capacity and agency seriously without patronizing, appropriating, or instrumentalizing them.
2. Uraraka–Bakugo is a recognition-quality scene: the crowd misreads visible asymmetry, Bakugo treats Uraraka as a dangerous opponent, Aizawa defends that seriousness, and Midoriya later restores tactical authorship to Uraraka.
3. Midoriya's `轟くんもあなたじゃない` and `君の力` make personhood/toolhood an explicit dialogic conflict with Endeavor's `義務`, `テストベッド`, `俺の野望`, and `上位互換` grammar.
4. Todoroki's fire use is not reconciliation with Endeavor; `お前を忘れた` defines the breakthrough as temporary de-centering of the father and recovery of Shoto's older heroic desire.
5. Bakugo's No.1 ambition is evidentiary and internally validated: public recognition and official victory are worthless when his own `絶対評価` rejects the causal route.
6. All Might's Quirkless past explains autobiographical recognition of Midoriya, but mature succession becomes generative rather than reproductive: Midoriya must derive a method only Midoriya can.
7. Recovery Girl and Inko move Midoriya's sustainability problem from technical self-preservation to relational responsibility; the heroic body is also a person others are allowed to worry about.
8. Stain adds a moral-purification/absolutist villain route, strengthening causal plurality and preventing a monocausal failed-recognition theory of villain formation.
9. V05 closes the Sports Festival by returning the students from national spectacle to intimate family spaces, reasserting that future heroes remain relationally embedded persons rather than only public assets.
