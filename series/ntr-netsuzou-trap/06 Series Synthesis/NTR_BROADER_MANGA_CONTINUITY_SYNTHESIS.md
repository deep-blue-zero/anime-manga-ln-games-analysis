# Attachment without a fully common world

The admitted manga continuity establishes a real, chosen Yuma–Hotaru attachment whose shared life remains less complete than its emotional legibility. The mainline changes a relation's ability to be named; LateWinter shows that naming still leaves a difference between caring for someone, directing them and being able to share the life one imagines for them. Direct specialness can coexist with independent social refusal. Tender private reciprocity can coexist with a withheld welfare hope. Public inference can persist without a voluntarily shared public account. The continuation extends the question of authority inside love rather than settling it with a successful couple outcome.

This is an additional current-continuity argument, justified by CLM-0047's several independent extensions. It does not replace [the frozen six-volume synthesis](NTR_FULL_SERIES_SYNTHESIS.md), whose question remains what V01–V06 established at its ending. [The LW integration](NTR_LATEWINTER_SUPPLEMENTAL_INTEGRATION.md) owns the exact delta and [the deep reading](../02%20Supplemental%20Readings/NTR_LATEWINTER_DEEP_READING.md) the complete new source. This argument instead connects the mature responsibilities across both boundaries. Mainline coordinates are **NTR-JP-VNN/SNNNN/I01**; LW coordinates are **NTR-JP-LW/SNNNN/I01**. Evidence is the exact Japanese manga witnesses, with embedded material individually routed and no imported adaptation, localization or commentary.

## Recognition changes what can be said without making another person fully known

The early relation obtains intimate proximity through explanations that do not adequately express its wanted meaning. Practical help and practice make Hotaru's initiative intelligible; friendship and gender allow Yuma to explain differential comfort. Yuma can desire and object at the same time. Their self-accounts change unevenly, and a shared act does not guarantee a shared account of why it occurred. Later private recipient recognition makes Yuma's continued practice cover more knowing. The mainline eventually gives direct naming, hurt/trust speech and chosen return; those are consequential changes in speech and participation rather than an inevitable outcome already explicit at the beginning. (V01 S0009–0016/0103; V02 S0031–0049/0146–0155; V04 S0134–0144; V06 S0058–0084/0148–0162; CLM-0005/0011/0021/0033/0037.)

LateWinter's direct Y-selective interest is now spoken inside the acknowledged relation. The vocabulary of interest rejects a universal inference of apathy while affirming one chosen person. This changes the available private account without turning it into a full relationship contract. Yuma's later partial-recognition narration supplies a stronger retrospective self-understanding, not proof that all earlier uncertainty was feigned. The chronology of knowledge remains part of the argument: what a later subject can say about herself cannot be silently assigned to the earlier prospective reader or to every earlier interlocutor. (LW S0012–0016; CLM-0040; REL-0039/0041, JPS-0032/0035.)

The closing hope makes the remaining inequality sharper. Yuma wants Hotaru to value others and anticipates her own loneliness, but the hope stays inward when Hotaru asks. The reader therefore possesses a consequential future proposition that the partner lacks. The earlier gap between actual contact and truthful naming has changed into a gap between present attachment and a shareable account of its desired development. This is why directness matters without solving transparency. It is also why Hotaru's attention to an expression cannot be promoted into knowledge of the thought. (LW S0019–0020; INF-0039, VIS-0038, CLM-0045.)

## Care becomes ethically adequate through its relation to choice, not its intensity

The mainline repeatedly distinguishes useful care from authority. Yuma's practical knowledge and childhood protector role make her concern causally intelligible, yet she recognizes that she has no guardian right and still refuses separation inwardly. Hotaru asserts work autonomy while overriding Yuma's labor choice and claiming her in other circumstances. Neither has one consistent permanent role. At the return, privacy circumvention, painful pulls and lost work remain real even when trust fear becomes intelligible and reciprocal staying follows. The hand motif supports both rescue and control; shared tenderness cannot erase the act-specific costs. (V03 S0073–0076/0099–0105/0125–0133; V06 S0116–0118/0137–0148/0157–0162; CON-0014/0016/0017/0037–0039; CLM-0016/0034/0035.)

LW moves the same question into a preference with less dramatic cost. Yuma asks Hotaru to try friendship; Hotaru answers that unwanted interest cannot simply be willed on request. No compelled group attendance follows. That positive difference matters: not every instance of care culminates in an override. The social-choice objection earns H-PC-04's bounded first-response support. It does not independently show Yuma's reliable response to private-address refusal, lost income or threatened abandonment. Care has encountered a preference and allowed the disagreement to remain spoken here; its portability remains untested. (LW S0007–0010; CON-0040–0041, AGY-0033–0034; CLM-0039/0044.)

Yuma's final wider-welfare wish adds an ethical capacity without proving its realization. She can conceive of a good that would make her less indispensable. The cost appears in her acknowledged loneliness. This defeats a wholly selfish account while preserving the significance of centrality to her care. The source does not show whether she later communicates the wish, asks what Hotaru wants or acts as though insight authorizes changing her. The difference between an imagined good and negotiated care is now observable in the form of a withheld thought. (LW S0019–0020; REL-0042, CON-0043, CLM-0045.)

The strongest dependency counterreading can accordingly accept love as real. It argues that the relation continues to reward a narrow world and may preserve unequal authority. LW adds selected-interest pleasure and ambivalent silence to the mainline's rescue/intrusion evidence. It simultaneously limits any version that requires total Hotaru passivity or complete Yuma indifference to wider welfare. The justified current claim is conditional: dependency and autonomous choice can coexist, and sustained responsive care has not been demonstrated. The competing favorable reading finds ordinary conversation, directness and modest restraint; those are actual local gains, with no repeated difficult boundary test to establish durability. (CLM-0045; [Yuma–Hotaru extension](../05%20Relationship%20Synthesis/NTR_YUMA_HOTARU_RELATIONSHIP_SYNTHESIS.md).)

## Social recognition does not transfer permission to the recognizer

The four-person system distinguishes knowledge from the right to intervene. Takeda's useful advice operates under a mistaken male-recipient assumption, yet can still direct Yuma toward the other person's answer. Fujiwara can possess better particulars and use them for conditional access, selective blame, retention and public exposure. Ordinary competence does not redeem harmful information use. His disclosure changes circumstances; it does not establish the girls' consent or the supposed beneficiary's desire for the service. Takeda's later rejection of that benefit matters independently of his own intrusion and violence. (V04 S0078–0085/0118–0126; V05 S0076/0091–0095/0155–0156; V06 S0003/0009–0011/0131–0132; INF-0016/0020/0024/0028/0029/0035; CLM-0031/0032/0036.)

LW strengthens attributed Fujiwara agency through Yuma's narration and adds persistent social consequences. The post's removal and the afterlife of knowledge remain different questions. Peer gaze and conversation do not authenticate another upload or prove that a surviving file is still circulating. Nor does the relation's continued pleasure ethically validate exposure. The new evidence extends responsibility and consequence without filling technical provenance or remorse. (LW S0006/0022; INF-0038/0040, CON-0044; CLM-0042.)

The named friends make the public more heterogeneous. Invitation can include Hotaru; tolerance of a girls' relation can coexist with disliking the particular partner; claimed noninterference can be contradicted by another peer. Those positions are neither universal acceptance nor uniform hostility. They are inferred through photographs, not a performed joint disclosure by Yuma and Hotaru. The girls do not hear the literal aside on the page. A public may know enough to classify while lacking the consent history, chosen meaning or right to define the relation for its subjects. (LW S0007–0008/0022; REL-0043, INF-0040, JPS-0036, VIS-0039; CLM-0043.)

Takeda's complete LW absence and Fujiwara's absence as a current actor are consequently substantive boundaries. The existing T recognition and F in-form/beneficiary-defense states remain last observed, without a new friendship settlement or formal H/F breakup. An analysis centered on the girls cannot erase the men's mature mainline trajectories, and it cannot fabricate their sequel responses. The current network has new peer nodes and family accounts, not a complete redistribution of knowledge to all four people. ([Ensemble extension](../05%20Relationship%20Synthesis/NTR_ENSEMBLE_RELATIONSHIP_AND_KNOWLEDGE_NETWORK.md); INF-0041, CAST-0038, CLM-0046.)

## Ordinary life is a real continuation and an unfinished material condition

The mainline's ordinary scenes make attachment more substantial than romance rhetoric: study, meals, notes, leisure, cooking, work, money and proposed rooms/chores supply activities and costs. They also make the future conditional. Yuma's admission condition, Hotaru's depleted funds and lost work resist a costless reunion account. Mainline fin gives actual return/rest; separately embedded gags give school and care/play. Neither provides a completed adult household. (V05 S0044–0046/0072–0073/0081–0088/0159–0162; V06 S0138–0144/0158–0162/0166–0167; REL-0030/0036–0037, AGY-0022/0031, CLM-0035.)

LW extends actual ordinary life through continued school, a cafeteria option, walking, a current-home visit and private repose. Its new paperwork explanation shows enrollment surviving partly through apparently unsubmitted withdrawal. A beneficial contingency is not reliable paternal care or secretly completed planning. Hotaru's reported family absence keeps emotional availability distinct from material/institutional effects. Graduation is still two or three months away, while new work, income, university and housing outcomes remain unshown. Hotaru's S0012 coat reception/hanging also makes reciprocal ordinary care concrete before private intimate initiative. It gives practical service without showing competence across tasks or a functioning household. An ordinary scene can establish real continuity without settling sustainability. (LW S0005–0012/0019–0020; INF-0036–0037, AGY-0032, CLM-0038/0046.)

The six-volume conditional future has therefore **not become actual**. A visit to an existing apartment is not cohabitation; current selective interest is not a lease or a lifetime contract; a school horizon is not admission. The claim is not that those futures fail or never happen, but that this witness does not establish them. Complete source coverage is compatible with open outcomes. This preserves the significance of chosen continuation without treating the analyst's desired closure as evidence.

## Form and written language retain the difference between closeness and a common account

Mainline formal recurrences distribute knowledge rather than merely illustrate psychology. Withheld faces, dream boundaries, phone interfaces, transfer/deletion sequences, private/public crossings, empty rooms and repeated hands create different routes for the reader and the subjects. The final recalled-speech montage can strengthen a retrospective interpretation without dating every past motive; Yuma's inward acceptance/repeated-help vow does not become a spoken mutual repair protocol. (VIS-0002/0004/0013/0027/0031–0035; JPS-0028–0030; CLM-0033/0035.)

LW extends that formal work independently of its events. Peer frames and small comic figures allow an ordinary invitation and refusal; the interest argument broadens Hotaru's answer before the private frame concentrates on the pair. Spacious inward hope briefly imagines other attachments, then the nonanswer restores closeness. The peer coda restores an external position and answers a self-description with contradiction. The ending is tender without making its focal thought common property. (LW S0005–0022; VIS-0036–0039.)

Written language establishes the same distinctions at another level. Practice, friendship, no-count, attributed protection and named love changed meaning as speakers and entering states changed. LW's interest sequence supplies a direct correction without a new orientation label. Temporal father/childhood phrasing separates present indifference from remembered need; familiar address continues without proving unchanged state; outward nonanswer omits inward hope; freedom rhetoric differs from noninterference. These are manga text and image relations, without acoustic voice or localization evidence. They support an argument about unevenly shared meaning rather than a generic claim of happier dialogue. (JPS-0032–0037; [mainline written-language specialist](NTR_JAPANESE_WRITTEN_VOICE_AND_RELATIONSHIP_CATEGORIES.md).)
