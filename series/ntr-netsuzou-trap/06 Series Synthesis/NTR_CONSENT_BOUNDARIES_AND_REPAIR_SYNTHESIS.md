# Consent, boundaries and repair: an attachment cannot give itself permission

The completed manga gives substantial evidence of Yuma and Hotaru wanting one another. It also gives substantial evidence of acts that exceed a stated boundary. These findings have different objects: attachment concerns what a person wants from a relation; permission concerns whether a particular action is accepted under the conditions in which it occurs. NTR's dramatic development becomes less intelligible if either finding cancels the other. Calling every encounter an expression of mutual love loses the objections, pressure, concealment and changed initiator. Calling every encounter identically imposed loses voluntary reapproach, requested cooperation, affirmative reception and eventual chosen proximity. The ethical trajectory is uneven development, with genuine local changes and incomplete repair.

The central mechanism is the substitution of a justification for an answer. Practice is supposed to help Yuma; practical competence is supposed to make assistance useful; protection is supposed to preserve her reputation; rescue is supposed to preserve Hotaru's future. An actor can sincerely value one of those ends while acting beyond the other person's permission. The story repeatedly distinguishes receiving a benefit from authorizing the method that produces it. Its ending does not abolish that distinction when its preferred relation becomes legible (CLM-0005, CLM-0011, CLM-0016, CLM-0022, CLM-0034, CLM-0035).

## The opening does not need a future verdict to be legible

Hotaru's earliest practice/help accounts occur alongside Yuma's requests to wait or stop, assertions that she can manage herself, and enacted pushing away. In V01 the restroom, balcony, school and bath encounters vary in audience and staging, but repeated continuation over voiced objection is clear. The later favorable comparison with Takeda's kiss is a new desire fact, not a reply that retroactively changes what Hotaru heard earlier. Likewise, Yuma's dream of Hotaru gives access to Yuma's conflict; dream-Hotaru's claim about her wishes is not the actual girl's knowledge or an actual agreement (V01 S0011–0016, S0030–0038, S0050–0054, S0085–0097, S0114–0117; CON-0001, VIS-0004, INF-0002).

The slope confrontation makes the disputed permission theory explicit. Following surprise contact and Yuma's slap, Hotaru argues that the physically stronger Yuma could stop her if she really wanted to. That assigns responsibility for preventing the act to its recipient. The evidence supports neither a requirement to overpower a valued friend nor an equation between strength and agreement. Yuma has already resisted, and she refuses Hotaru's reduction of the dispute to guilt about Takeda. Her exposure anxiety is a condition of the objection; it does not erase the objection. Her slap supplies agency and a harmful action of her own, without making the preceding initiation acceptable (V01 S0130–0138; CON-0002, AGY-0002, JPS-0002).

The distinction matters because a retrospective romance explanation can change an estimate of motive without changing a contemporaneous response. V06's attachment evidence makes an amusement-only account of Hotaru inadequate. It does not convert every early “stop” into a secretly affirmative answer, prove every initiative protective, or establish willingness in the V01 room encounter. That encounter's suggestive discovery leaves the act and its permission unshown; the earlier observed kick creates concern, not a warrant to invent an unseen event (V01 S0067–0068, S0139–0146; CON-0004, CON-0005; CLM-0006 → CLM-0033).

## Practical invitation is a narrow permission with real value

Repeated help scenes do not all share one structure. In V01's embedded pretrip fitting episode, useful advice becomes intrusive handling despite Yuma's self-sufficiency and objections. In V03 Yuma explicitly requests costume-fastening help and thanks Hotaru. The practical request is evidence of permission for that assistance. Desire triggered by remembered proximity is another fact. In V04 Yuma nods and says yes to fixing a fastening, then objects when Hotaru adds intimate contact. The assent remains valid for its actual object; it does not silently expand to the added conduct (V01 S0156–0160; V03 S0080–0084; V04 S0053–0060; CON-0006, CON-0015, CON-0020).

This comparison resists both an indiscriminate violation catalogue and a benevolent-help defense. Practical competence can be real. Gratitude can be real. The ability to distinguish the requested task from added contact is precisely what permits care to be evaluated rather than treated as inherently coercive or inherently self-authorizing. The useful task and its overreach need not be assigned one moral meaning.

The V05 classroom adds a temporal limit. Hotaru invokes Yuma's earlier practice request after Yuma objects to the present location. The remembered request is not a standing authorization independent of place, audience or current words. A curtain provides a partial privacy zone near classmates, rather than a fully secure room. Yuma's subsequent angry initiative is a new act; it cannot make Hotaru's earlier continuation retroactively permitted. Nor do Hotaru's surprise and blush establish explicit preceding assent to Yuma's pull (V05 S0023–0034; CON-0025, JPS-0018, VIS-0024).

## Chosen reapproach changes the participant, not the whole past

Yuma's emerging initiative is consequential ethical evidence. At the end of V02, Hotaru declines to supply an expected kiss and announces cessation; Yuma grasps her hand and returns to her lap while denying wanting a kiss. The gap between action and speech gives Hotaru grounds to criticize evasion, but not to treat every subsequent movement as preauthorized. Hotaru's substitute/blame explanation and forceful repositioning remain distinct from the chosen approach. A later permission question has no depicted affirmative verbal reply. Conflicted reception and attachment are supportable; a blanket agreement is not (V02 S0143–0155; CON-0011, AGY-0009, CLM-0011).

V03's infirmary encounter supplies another distinct structure: Yuma pulls Hotaru close, apologizes, and Hotaru smiles and reapproaches with accepting illness language. The source supports a local accepting response while hair obscures precise contact and genuine fever limits claims about a wider agreement. It does not support treating the entire scene as incapable of agency, nor as an unrestricted lasting declaration. Takeda's discovery changes who sees a position; it does not expose every prior step or certify permission (V03 S0143–0156; CON-0018, INF-0015, AGY-0014).

V04's chosen practice request is stronger evidence of express cooperation. Yuma asks, under an unnamed-beloved cover, and Hotaru says **いいよ** and embraces her. In V05 a home invitation is followed by Yuma's initiative and another local affirmative response. These responses count. To erase them because the relationship has a harmful history would make choice analytically unavailable to the people who experienced the harm. To treat them as permission for previous or subsequent unrelated acts would make choice equally unavailable by turning one answer into permanent access (V04 S0137–0141; V05 S0060–0068; CON-0023, CON-0026, JPS-0017, JPS-0019).

The relevant evolution is therefore granular. Some encounters become more requested and accepting. The people do not thereby acquire a consistently negotiated boundary practice. V05's homework objection produces a real pause and inquiry before teasing resumes; it is negative evidence against an always-uninterrupted model, but too narrow to prove durable correction. The source gives local improvements without supplying a general repair guarantee (V05 S0096–0101; CON-0028).

## Changing initiative preserves both people's accountability

Yuma's new agency includes coercive and violent possibilities. She pulls, strikes, investigates and presses for access as well as confessing and providing care. Her V05 slap follows hurtful no-count/identity language. That speech can inflict a real relational injury without authorizing the slap. In V06 she drags Hotaru into a private explanatory exchange, releases the grip after voiced pain, and later grabs her wrist again; Hotaru pushes and grips near her clothing/bag area before vulnerability becomes visible. Keeping actor and sequence precise prevents a simple reversal in which the former recipient of overreach becomes automatically justified as pursuer (V05 S0142–0149; V06 S0050–0053, S0073–0084; CON-0030, CON-0034, CON-0035).

The distinction is especially important at the ending. Hotaru's father withholds the address under her request. Yuma's false textbook purpose gains access to rental clues, while her eventual discovery comes by chance after a failed search. At reunion Hotaru breaks a pull, says she must work, voices lost income and asks Yuma to finish tea and leave. The beautifully repeated childhood hand does not eliminate the current work and privacy boundaries. Hotaru's later staying gesture can be chosen without authorizing the earlier forced pulls or repaying the income cost (V06 S0102, S0116–0118, S0126–0129, S0137–0141, S0157–0162; CON-0037, CON-0038, VIS-0034).

Yuma can be more candid about desire and still exceed authority. Hotaru can have violated Yuma's boundaries and still have a legitimate claim to decide work, departure and contact. Their harmful conduct differs by act and condition; identifying a contradiction in one domain does not cancel a valid claim in another. This is why the mature analysis needs both an agency specialist and a consent specialist rather than one fixed protector/victim taxonomy (CLM-0016, CLM-0034).

## The male relations require differentiated ethical analysis

Takeda's first sudden kiss has no depicted advance request; later tolerated hesitation and his V02 decision to stop and sleep on the floor supply stronger evidence of responsive restraint. His withdrawal over sincerity is a relationship boundary, not a demand to perform intimacy. Later aid and advice lack an explicit dating-repayment price, even while he wants renewed romance. His V05 comfort precedes a renewal bid at a vulnerable moment, so absence of an explicit bargain does not make timing frictionless. His covert following and V06 punch remain actual intrusions and force, rather than disappearing behind his valuable recognition of Yuma's choice (V01 S0021–0022; V02 S0011–0013, S0088–0092; V05 S0050–0056, S0150–0153; V06 S0009–0012, S0061–0063; CON-0003, CON-0007, CON-0029, CON-0030, CON-0033).

Fujiwara's kick after refusal, conditional access-for-silence demands and unpermitted recording have different mechanisms from Takeda's care and errors. The later historical mutual-request grammar does not establish equal power or permission for every request; pressure enforcement follows expressed reluctance. In V05 actual restraint is depicted while Hotaru outwardly reassures Yuma. These observations warrant serious ethical distinctions without making every unshown injury or encounter a proved act of violence (V01 S0067–0068; V02 S0110–0117, S0156–0158; V04 S0028–0029; V05 S0074–0076; CON-0004, CON-0010, CON-0012, CON-0021, CON-0027).

Nor does accurate information absolve the controller. Fujiwara can supply a useful account of the in-form relationship and still be responsible for coercion and exposure. His stated effort to wake Takeda up is rejected by its claimed beneficiary. A later romantic clarification produced in the wake of publication does not authorize the publication as a beneficial intervention (V06 S0011, S0109–0111, S0131–0132; INF-0034, INF-0035, CLM-0032).

## What repair occurred, and what remains absent

Repair is not reducible to whether someone says sorry or the final pair smiles. Several genuine steps exist. Hotaru apologizes for confusion, acknowledges jealousy and promises cessation in V03; the private truth/lie tactic makes the account incomplete without identifying every sincere clause as false. She later apologizes for excessive teasing at the aquarium. Yuma owns her irresponsible earlier dating and reports declining renewal in V06. Takeda recognizes the choice without claiming complete understanding. The publicly visible post disappears under pressure, corroborated by a peer screen check. These are different kinds of acknowledgment, restraint, recognition and practical harm limitation (V03 S0028–0035; V04 S0110; V06 S0031–0033, S0061–0071; CON-0013, CON-0024, CON-0039, INF-0031).

Their limits are also different. Broad apology does not specify earlier refused acts or produce a stable method for asking. Confession does not redress a slap or repeated painful pulling. Removal does not prove original-file destruction, eliminate earlier humiliation or establish remorse. No surviving screenshot is actually demonstrated, so persistence must remain possible rather than asserted. The ending does not show exhaustive mutual accounting, restitution for work loss, a negotiated privacy rule or an equal practical division of future care.

At S0159 Yuma inwardly accepts her inference that Hotaru still does not trust her completely. At S0160 the repeated-help commitment is likewise inward; it is not a spoken repair agreement Hotaru has explicitly accepted. Held hands, reciprocal smiles and shared train sleep show present comfort. The embedded school-return gag confirms a limited ordinary return, while the next caregiving joke exposes asymmetry. These observations support a changed relation without establishing durable trust or comprehensive repair (V06 S0157–0167; CON-0036, CON-0039, REL-0036, REL-0037, JPS-0030).

## Counterreadings and final ethical scope

The strongest romance-centered defense is that conventionally exaggerated teasing expresses mutual latent attraction, and the final vulnerability shows why both acted indirectly. It has real evidence: Yuma's anticipation and self-recognition, Hotaru's stated wound, locally affirmative reception and present staying. It explains why an exclusively predatory/wholly passive account fails. It cannot explain away communicated objections, imposed recording, the privacy restriction or voiced lost income by appealing to a future that had not yet happened. Its useful contribution is to desire and motive interpretation, with boundaries retained.

The strongest critical counterreading is that the ending beautifies control and substitutes dependency for repair. Painful pulls, circumvention, repeated rescue and mother-like care support it. Yet a wholly imposed ending loses Hotaru's direct fear speech, her coat grip and joint comfort, and treats her agency as impossible whenever her choices are conflicted. The better bounded conclusion holds these findings together: present reciprocal attachment is supported, while the story leaves substantial ethical work unfinished. That is a meaningful limitation of its resolution, not evidence that the attachment never existed.

This specialist's claims concern the six inspected Japanese witnesses and their separately routed embedded material. It offers no legal classification, clinical account, adaptation comparison or adult continuation. Its role is to make action-specific reasoning recoverable so that neither the ending nor a global label swallows the earlier sequence.
