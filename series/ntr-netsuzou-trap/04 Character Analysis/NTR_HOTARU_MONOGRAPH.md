# Hotaru: the difference between obtaining closeness and trusting someone to remain

Hotaru repeatedly makes closeness available while reducing what it is permitted to mean. Practice, special friendship, girls' contact that does not count, an adult-neighbor fantasy and an arrangement in form all allow company without a secure reciprocal claim. The completed manga makes this pattern more intelligible through her directly stated temporal hurt, wish not to burden anyone and fear of trusting someone. She wants more than detached amusement, yet receiving a wanted commitment does not automatically make that commitment believable. Her departure after accepted closeness is therefore central to her development: it breaks the expectation that naming the right beloved must resolve the relation.

This attachment argument has an ethical and epistemic limit. It cannot turn Hotaru's early boundary crossings, categorical ridicule or public humiliation into secretly benevolent acts. It cannot make work, money and privacy objections only disguises for needing rescue. Nor can it erase the practical competence, selective care, requests, refusals and small staying choices that keep her from being only the guarded child Yuma comes to see. Hotaru's later vulnerability changes the strongest account of her attachment, while leaving her a person whose current authority over particular acts and decisions survives both what she has done to others and what others have done to her.

## A bright account with a withheld face

The opening portrayal places Hotaru's familiar initiative beside visual interruptions of its apparent lightness. She can tease, provide help, insist on firstness and retract an exposure joke; she can also falter around Takeda's progress, wonder whether Yuma still needs her and retreat behind an averted or obscured face. The reader receives more than Yuma's immediate interpretation, but not a complete interior explanation. That combination is the basis for an attachment hypothesis, rather than proof that every initiating act expresses the same coherent plan. ([REL-0002], [VIS-0002], [JPS-0001]; V01 S0028–0037,0055,0069–0072,0105–0107,0133–0138.)

Practical familiarity is important to the ambiguity. Hotaru can help with washing, clothing or a slope task, so the help frame is not always empty. Yet she repeatedly extends an opportunity into unwanted contact. The practical result and the added act have different permissions. To call her help only fake would remove its ordinary credibility; to call it genuine and therefore harmless would remove Yuma's explicit objections. Her role depends on using a believable relationship resource in a way that can evade the harder question of what she wants and what Yuma has agreed to. ([CON-0001], [CON-0002], [CON-0006], [VIS-0004]; V01 S0011–0016,0085–0097,0127–0135,0155–0162.)

This makes her a source of interpretation as well as action. When she says that Yuma could resist if truly unwilling, she offers an account that transfers the burden to the person handled. That account is not the narrator's consent standard. It matters because it seeks to make contradiction disappear: protest and attraction would become a single hidden willingness. The manga supplies enough resistance and subsequent initiative to require a more exact explanation. Hotaru's later desire can be real; the responsibility-shifting words can still be wrong. ([CON-0002], [JPS-0002], [CLM-0005]; V01 S0131–0135.)

The bright surface is therefore neither a reliable window nor an intrinsically false mask. It is a way of acting in a relation where full need is hard to state. The withheld face supplies contrary evidence to an amusement-only account without fixing a unique motive. Later direct speech will narrow that uncertainty, but the early prospective reader's uncertainty remains legitimate. A mature interpretation should not convert hindsight into a claim that Hotaru's love and every tactic were obvious, conscious and settled from the beginning. ([CLM-0001], [CLM-0006], [CAST-0007].)

## The person who demands recognition also avoids it

Hotaru's V02 affair language challenges Yuma's friend/girl exemption. If the conduct is harmless, it should be tellable to Takeda; Yuma's actual lie exposes the inadequacy of her account. Hotaru has a real point about concealment and responsibility. But she presses it while continuing contact Yuma protests during the call. Correctly identifying another person's evasion does not make one's own method ethical. The sequence establishes Hotaru as someone who can reveal a truth and cross a boundary in the same interaction. ([INF-0005], [CON-0008], [JPS-0004]; V02 S0031–0049.)

Her situated description of liking similarly contains insight without complete self-disclosure. The beloved seems separated from everyone else; wanting that person's happiness can conflict with wanting exclusive possession. The account is more emotionally serious than simple teasing, but it names no recipient. Yuma privately assigns it to Fujiwara, while Hotaru's face remains withheld. Nothing shows Hotaru hearing that attribution. The scene dramatizes how words can disclose the structure of a feeling without giving the listener the proposition needed to understand this particular relation. ([JPS-0005], [INF-0009], [VIS-0008]; V02 S0099–0104.)

The cessation/reapproach sequence clarifies the asymmetry. Hotaru can announce that she will stop; Yuma grasps her hand and returns to the lap while denying kiss desire. Hotaru then calls the evasion unfair and frames the return through Takeda loneliness/substitution. She presses Yuma to own a wish while retaining an indirect account of her own. That is one diagnostic mechanism, not a general key to all Hotaru speech. Its first later test fails: in V03 she accepts the unnamed infirmary approach through an illness-inflected local response without Takeda projection. H-R03's bounded failure matters because a reconstruction that calls any indirect reply the same success would make Hotaru less specific than the source. ([CON-0011], [CLM-0008], [JPS-0011]; V02 S0143–0154; V03 S0152–0154.)

Her V03 apologies and special-friend explanation are real speech, not simply erased by the private thought about nine truths and one lie. The thought confirms strategic incompleteness but does not identify the false clause. A blanket false-confession account would assume what the manga withholds; a fully sincere transparent repair account would ignore that strategy and the absence of act-specific accounting. Hotaru can both care and manage the interpretation of that care. The danger lies in demanding that sincerity and strategy cancel rather than coexist. ([CON-0013], [INF-0011], [JPS-0008], [VIS-0014]; V03 S0028–0035.)

This pattern changes in severity after Yuma can name a recipient. Hotaru's adult-togetherness and special-friend wishes, shared rooms/chores and newlywed play offer a possible common life. Local **いいよ** responses to actual offered closeness matter. Yet when Yuma finally names Hotaru in V05, Hotaru says she noticed and then invokes girls/no-count language and an identity question. The categorical shelter now actively denies a claim Yuma has made explicit. Reported noticing has no dated onset and does not establish that Hotaru knew every earlier feeling. The cruelty is nonetheless present: the familiar childhood address does not soften away its consequence. ([REL-0019], [REL-0025], [CON-0023], [CON-0026], [JPS-0022]; V04 S0048–0051,0068–0069,0138–0141; V05 S0045–0046,0067,0085,0142–0143.)

## Fujiwara: a formally acknowledged relation with limited emotional claims

Hotaru's relation to Fujiwara must be read differently from her relation to Yuma. A public couple can be socially recognized while its acts are pressured and its emotional terms thin. V01 gives an explicit not-in-the-mood refusal followed by a kick. That is visible violence, known to the reader but not Yuma/Takeda. The later suggestive room discovery does not show the act or its permission. Continued dating and an eventual account of the arrangement cannot repair that evidentiary absence. ([CON-0004], [CON-0005], [INF-0003], [REL-0005]; V01 S0067–0068,0139–0145.)

Hotaru's explanation that good people unsettle her and she is not good either makes Fujiwara seem easier. Ease is not safety. It may concern reduced expectation, shame, cynical compatibility or practical company; it need not mean that she affirmatively chooses every harmful act. This self-account becomes more intelligible when later evidence describes the couple in form and as an exchange involving his access and her lonely time. But even that fuller account remains situated testimony and narrated history, rather than an objective total explanation of her. ([REL-0010], [JPS-0005], [REL-0035]; V02 S0120–0122; V06 S0109–0111.)

The historical mutual-request terms provide agency without proving equal power. No complaints, no unnecessary prying into each other and responding to the other's requests are grammatically mutual. Reluctance and coercive enforcement show why grammar alone cannot establish symmetry or act-specific assent. Hotaru's present invocation of the request term for her aquarium charade means she can use the arrangement too; it does not make the earlier pressured compliance freely equivalent. Her agency survives constraint, and constraint survives her agency. ([REL-0022], [CON-0021], [INF-0018], [JPS-0015]; V04 S0028–0031,0094.)

The V05 pregnancy sequence makes the difference between form and emotional truth painful. Hotaru reports a possibility and later says it did not occur; no clinical result is depicted. Her reproductive comparison gives the acknowledged male couple a consequence girls' contact cannot share, while Yuma feels that this disqualifies what happened between them. The analysis does not need objective pregnancy or paternity to understand the category injury. Nor can Fujiwara's responsibility denial prove other partners, actual ignorance or the medical outcome. Bodies become part of the characters' argument about what counts, and their statements retain their speakers. ([INF-0026], [JPS-0022], [JPS-0023], [REL-0028]; V05 S0110–0116,0122–0130.)

Fujiwara's restraint at S0076 also reminds the reader that Hotaru's outward reassurance is not proof of unconstrained participation. Yuma hears a declined visit and reassurance; the page turn gives the reader bodily control. The exact prior negotiations remain unshown. This is a specific constraint, not permission to invent an unseen act or use generic coercive resemblance as the missing conditional-demand forecast. By V06 Fujiwara's form-limited account helps clarify the attachment topology without redeeming his actions or establishing a fully depicted mutual breakup. ([CON-0027], [INF-0023], [INF-0034]; V05 S0074–0076; V06 S0107–0111.)

## Work and ordinary competence resist the helpless-child conclusion

Hotaru's working world is a material part of her characterization. She can perform a service role, shift to polite language, help with a requested fastening task and earn/save money. These acts reveal competence outside Yuma's childhood-protector image. They also reveal ethical contradictions: she represents Yuma as willing to try work without affirmative agreement, claims her publicly against client intrusion and does not want her continuing in the same environment. The source does not present one coherent autonomy philosophy. It presents a subject who can object validly to control while also controlling another person's options. ([CON-0014], [CON-0015], [CON-0017], [JPS-0010], [AGY-0012]; V03 S0065–0092,0125–0131,0146–0147.)

Her work objection is consequently not refuted by her other conduct. Yuma is not entitled to restrict her to approved work because she knows Hotaru well or has seen harms at the club. Work risks warrant analysis; no paid sexual-service fact or objective safety certification appears. Hotaru's distinction between herself and the bullied child Yuma remembers has force even when it also serves avoidance. A mature account must let an ethically compromised character make a valid argument about her current life. ([CON-0016], [REL-0013], [CLM-0016]; V03 S0099–0103,0130–0131.)

Ordinary skill is uneven. School difficulty contradicts the father's later good-grade assessment; poor cooking and reliance on simple food contradict general domestic competence. Yet these deficits do not cancel service work, practical assistance, relocation agency or useful hospitality. She can bring warmth to crying Yuma, show concern over cold lips, imagine chores, like sparklers and joke about being newlyweds. Those mundane choices make closeness a daily texture, not merely a succession of spectacular transgressions. They also keep her from being interpreted as exclusively an expert manipulator or exclusively a helpless dependent. ([CAST-0012], [VIS-0015], [REL-0030], [VIS-0028]; V02 S0095–0096; V03 S0049–0052,0080–0092; V04 S0062–0069; V05 S0063,0081–0088,0159–0162.)

The blank career survey adds uncertainty without certifying intention. She invokes a legitimate privacy claim, then allows Yuma alone to see that nothing is written. Blankness can mean indecision, incomplete schoolwork, withholding or something else; it is not by itself a calculated departure plan. Her imagined roomshare and adult-neighbor future similarly express possibilities without executing them. The subsequent "only for now" aside at V05 S0100 limits something unnamed; it is inward or faintly murmured, queried and denied, with its referent open. No mutually known plan or narrator-certified ending follows. ([INF-0022], [JPS-0018], [JPS-0021], [REL-0025].)

These ordinary details matter to the ending because relocation has a price. Hotaru reports depleted funds and explicitly objects that Yuma has cost her work income. The manga gives her practical reasons that cannot be replaced by a purely symbolic story of fleeing love. Her independent goal may be compromised by fear and limited resources; it need not therefore be inauthentic. Paid work and privacy can remain things she values even when she eventually chooses to return with Yuma. ([CON-0038], [AGY-0027], [AGY-0031]; V06 S0098–0103,0137–0140,0160.)

## Family history supplies context, not a universal causal verdict

The V06 household flashback finally gives the reader a care context the earlier volumes did not disclose. A prospective maternal partner refuses the role of mothering an unrelated child; Hotaru's father leaves and uses work explanations, money notes and offers to buy things in place of reliable accompaniment. Empty rooms and simple food make emotional absence tangible alongside provision. Hotaru appears fine while important school presence is missed. The distinction is not between a totally absent father and no support of any kind. It is between being materially provided for and being someone another person continues to remember and accompany. ([VIS-0032], [CAST-0030]; V06 S0093–0097.)

Yuma's accepted childhood hand enters this sequence as an alternate form of presence. The form strengthens the hypothesis that Hotaru's later wish for a privileged place is connected with loneliness and fragile expectation. It does not date the exact onset of romantic desire or show that every later act was produced by that history. A developmental explanation can make behavior intelligible without making it necessary, unconscious in a diagnosed way or exempt from responsibility. The source provides enough to reject an amusement-only account more strongly, not enough for a complete psychological biography. ([REL-0033], [CLM-0033]; V06 S0097,0078,0148.)

The later call supplies Hotaru's own explicit goal: **誰の負担にもなりたくないの**, not wanting to burden anyone. She asks for a rental guarantee, announces work/leaving and presents independence as freeing her father to live with a woman. An upbeat face and a nonburden statement can coexist with the visual memory of loneliness. The wish may regulate dependence as well as express practical independence. Its force comes from situated speech, not from an analyst diagnosing her or treating adulthood as a false pretense. ([JPS-0029], [AGY-0027], [INF-0033]; V06 S0098–0099.)

The father then describes an eighteen-year-old's choice as hers, assumes good grades and domestic competence, withholds the address as requested and expects later contact. His competence testimony is contradicted by admitted school/food evidence. His refusal to provide the address nevertheless communicates a real Hotaru boundary. Inaccuracy about one proposition does not invalidate every other proposition he conveys. If the analysis lets Yuma's superior practical knowledge automatically authorize bypassing the privacy condition, it repeats the very conflation between knowing and deciding that Hotaru objected to in V03. ([INF-0033], [CON-0037]; V06 S0100–0105.)

Yuma also does not receive the reader's complete family record. Her understanding draws on her own childhood memory, the present father account, Fujiwara's arrangement testimony and Hotaru's direct hurt/trust speech. The private anti-burden call and entire flashback are not shown fully disclosed to her. Her guarded-child interpretation can therefore be compassionate and well supported without being omniscient. The manga gives that interpretation formal emphasis; an analyst must retain its attribution, particularly when it competes with Hotaru's present work and privacy objections. ([REL-0033], [INF-0033], [INF-0034]; V06 S0079–0081,0093–0111,0148–0151.)

## Public protection that erases the protected person's attachment

The published photograph changes Hotaru's relation to control. Before circulation, she could help sustain small protected spaces, joke at exposure risk or manage audiences separately. The represented named account and peers' phones give the image an audience beyond the pair's control. It reveals a compromising moment while losing the information needed to understand consent, attachment and private definition. Hotaru responds by actively assigning an alternative story: she and Fujiwara were teasing Yuma, and the girls' romantic meaning should be denied. That public self-complicity is observed. An actual original joint scheme is not thereby proved. ([INF-0029], [INF-0030], [VIS-0029]; V06 S0003,0017–0021.)

The later protection explanation narrows one possible motive but does not certify pure benevolence. **かばってあげた** casts Hotaru as benefactor, while Yuma says the queer label would be preferable to the lie. The supposed protected person contests the protection's terms. This is not simply a private deception that accidentally hurts: it is an intervention in Yuma's public self-understanding that negates the attachment she wants counted. Peers' condemnation also means the manga does not provide a universally endorsing public audience. Rumor, taunt, care, criticism and ordinary classroom order coexist. ([REL-0031], [JPS-0025], [AGY-0026]; V06 S0021–0025,0033–0043,0052–0056.)

Hotaru's earlier no-count response is private categorical background; the V06 public denial is a single depicted public demonstration, followed by a private claimed rationale. There are not two independent public replications of a protective strategy. The literary continuity lies in reducing the claim of girls' intimacy, while the audience and stakes change. That continuity supports interpretation but remains weak evidence for predicting every future disclosure response. Public erasure cannot be called protective solely because it resembles earlier distancing. ([CLM-0027], [CLM-0030]; V05 S0142–0143; V06 S0018–0021,0053.)

Nor does later recipient clarity justify the exposure that forced the public crisis. Named attribution, pressure-conditioned post removal and possible screenshots are separate from repair. The image cannot authorize recording/distribution or certify the girls' prior permissions. Hotaru's hurtful response and Fujiwara's violation must remain distinct acts, rather than a combined plot instrument praised because the romance becomes clearer. The later attachment resolution gives no ethical credit backward to either method. ([CON-0031], [CON-0032], [INF-0031], [CLM-0036]; V05 S0155–0156; V06 S0031–0033.)

## A wanted confession can be difficult to believe

V06 finally gives Hotaru direct language stronger than a guarded face. She says that Yuma did not look at her for a long time and cannot believe a confession arriving now. The paired panels of love and disbelief, tears and child peer-image make that temporal wound visible. This does not establish the exact beginning of all romantic feeling. It establishes how Hotaru now explains the inadequacy of present words against an experienced past. Yuma's newly clear choice cannot simply overwrite the time Hotaru says she felt unregarded. ([REL-0033], [JPS-0028], [VIS-0031]; V06 S0077–0081.)

The exchange also contains bidirectional force before tenderness. Yuma's wrist grip elicits pain; Hotaru pushes and grips near clothing/bag before tears. Neither named desire nor visible vulnerability authorizes those preceding acts. Hotaru's claim that pursuit/withdrawal is a technique boys also fall for is her own explanation, not independently verified complete motive or dating history. A stronger attachment account becomes available through subsequent hurt speech; it does not require denying manipulation, and it does not make every technique a concealed request for rescue. ([CON-0035], [AGY-0026]; V06 S0072–0078.)

Her belief question and receptive closeness make the possibility of a chosen bond real. Yet the morning smile, shared waking and school plan are followed by absence, unanswered contact and relocation. This is the decisive contradiction to a resolved-by-confession account. The person who receives closeness can still move away. The anti-burden statement and later **誰かを信じるのは怖い**, fear of trusting someone, give convergence to a self-removal explanation. A practical independence wish remains compatible, and the source does not show every step of the decision inwardly. ([REL-0034], [AGY-0027], [JPS-0028], [JPS-0029]; V06 S0083–0103,0112,0148.)

At reunion, fear is not the first thing Hotaru gives Yuma. She breaks the pull, says she must work, voices lost income, serves tea and asks her to finish and leave, then questions how the address was obtained. These are current objections to current conduct. Yuma's argument about childhood togetherness and future life later reaches the attachment problem, but it does not retroactively turn the earlier pull into permission. The hand motif's resemblance to childhood invitation makes rescue compelling while the resisted movement gives it a different ethical structure. ([CON-0037], [CON-0038], [AGY-0029], [VIS-0034]; V06 S0133–0145.)

Only then does the explicit fear narrow the account of retreat. Yuma reads a guarded child beneath the adult-seeming performance and asks for trust. The source supports this response through direct testimony, remembered context and present vulnerability. It does not show a clinical cure or a comprehensive accounting of early harm. The old H-R04 autonomy prediction remains due-V04 NDO with uncertain later childhood-authority matching; H-R06 category-first support retains preceding-force safety uncertainty. The tenderness should not be used to remove those constraints simply because it seems to confirm a broader attachment explanation. ([CLM-0013], [CLM-0030], [CLM-0033], [CLM-0034]; V06 S0145–0154.)

## Written familiarity and changing visual authority

Hotaru's **由真ちゃん** persists across play, ridicule, apology, fear and need. That continuity gives the relation texture without proving a constant emotional state. Her common **私**, service politeness, casual evasions and short direct fear statements support written-register differentiation; they do not establish acoustic delivery or an imported anime performance. A reconstruction that makes her always coy or always clinically articulate would lose the source's audience- and state-dependent language. ([JPS-0001], [JPS-0010], [JPS-0022], [JPS-0028], [JPS-0031].)

Words that appear similar have different responsibilities. Special-friend naming can acknowledge real jealousy without plainly naming romance. Practice can frame an act, quote an old request or answer a fresh offer; only the latter supplies the precise H-R05 antecedent. A local yes supports acceptance of that encounter; it is not an agreement that all past and future contact is permitted. The referential or modal wording of a line limits what it establishes. Written voice is therefore not decorative linguistic trivia: it is part of how Hotaru lets a listener have something while withholding or disputing its consequence. ([JPS-0008], [JPS-0017], [JPS-0018], [JPS-0019], [CLM-0018].)

Visual recurrence eventually gives more authority to need while preserving uncertainty. Withheld faces become tearful paired panels; domestic rooms become empty family and relocated rooms; phone reassurance is cut against restraint; an accepted child hand becomes a city reach, resisted pull and later handhold. Dark-framed speech at S0150–0151 recalls earlier remarks rather than supplying new current accusations. Those images encourage an attachment interpretation without making the montage a complete explanation. They can reveal, intensify, repeat or beautify a claim; none alone certifies motive or consent. ([VIS-0002], [VIS-0025], [VIS-0031], [VIS-0032], [VIS-0034], [VIS-0035].)

## Counterreading: vulnerability can become an excuse and a smaller life

The strongest adverse reading is that the final disclosure sentimentalizes Hotaru's harmful acts and reduces a working, choosing young woman to a wounded child whose proper place is under Yuma's care. Early objection-deflecting conduct, truthful-but-strategic reassurance, private no-count cruelty and public humiliation give this concern weight. Hotaru herself calls pursuit/withdrawal a technique; the late family explanation could be taken as a device that changes judgment without showing accountability. The reunion's forced pulls and lost work, Yuma's repeated-help vow and maternal gag then risk making dependency appear to be the solution. This reading targets the ethics and form of resolution, not merely the unpleasantness of individual scenes. ([CON-0001], [INF-0011], [CON-0030], [CON-0038], [CLM-0035]; V06 S0072,0137–0140,0160,0167.)

The evidence weakens its strongest totalizing version. Hotaru really voices temporal hurt and trust fear; the anti-burden goal is hers rather than only Yuma's interpretation. Work competence, an enacted relocation, legitimate privacy/income objections and the choice to break a pull prevent generic helplessness. Her coat-grip, smiles, shared return and later direct playful need bid are small contributions to proximity; an account of pure imposition would erase them. Conversely, none proves that the prior harms were benevolent or that a chosen return validates the search method. The most defensible result is present reciprocal attachment with unresolved asymmetry and accountability. ([REL-0033], [REL-0034], [REL-0036], [CON-0036], [AGY-0031]; V06 S0099,0137–0140,0148,0157–0162,0167.)

There is a related caution about reading every absence as attachment fear. Hotaru has savings/work plans and an independence goal; she can legitimately want a room and a life not supervised by Yuma. The father is wrong about practical competence but conveys a real requested boundary. Her later stated fear strengthens one explanation of flight without making privacy itself pathological. Maintaining this distinction lets the monograph explain why retreat follows wanted closeness while still recognizing that autonomy need not be justified by emotional health or moral innocence. ([INF-0033], [AGY-0027], [CON-0037], [CON-0038].)

The same applies to sexuality and repair. Strong H→Y attachment is supported by the completed convergence, but exact onset, every early motive and a universal sexual identity are not established. V04's conditional genuine-love answer is not a definitive never-loved claim. V05 possible/negative pregnancy and old injury reports retain medical/causal uncertainty. No source proves V01 room permission. Actual apologies and local assent should count, but there is no exhaustive act-specific repair conversation or guaranteed durable trust. To preserve all these limits is not to refuse the emotional ending; it is to read the ending at the scale of what it accomplishes. ([CLM-0006], [CLM-0022], [CLM-0033], [CON-0039].)

## A small staying gesture is more secure than a complete future

Hotaru's coat-grip when Yuma offers to buy a drink is a modest staying initiative, followed by a denial that anything is wrong. It makes need visible without a full self-explanation. At S0158 **Yuma** proposes cohabitation conditional on university admission and maternal permission; Hotaru's startled, anxious reception is not her own proposal or an executed agreement. At S0159 Yuma interprets the anxious eyes as incomplete trust and inwardly accepts that condition; S0160's repeated-help vow is also Yuma interiority. Neither line is Hotaru's endpoint self-report or a spoken mutual repair contract. ([REL-0036], [JPS-0030], [AGY-0031].)

The train ending gives present shared comfort, not graduation, housing, work stability or permanent exclusivity. S0166 then shows actual return to school, apology for unauthorized absence and the teacher's admonition; S0167 separately stages notes, study, food and **抱っこ** play. A request for a hug can now be simple and direct, while the maternal joke keeps care asymmetry visible. Comic register prevents treating the scene as a diagnosis or proof that all future dependence is solved. The two pages are admitted embedded later school material, not an external sequel or independent forecast outcome. ([REL-0037], [JPS-0031], [CAST-0031].)

Hotaru finishes the bounded corpus neither transparently healed nor secretly unchanged. The relation has more acknowledged meaning, and she contributes to staying; fear and incomplete trust remain. Her central question is no longer only how to obtain a private moment without its full claim. It is whether she can let another person remain without requiring herself to be easy, unburdensome or already secure. The manga answers with a chosen return and rest beside Yuma, while preserving enough work, money, refusal and asymmetry to leave the practice of that answer unfinished.
