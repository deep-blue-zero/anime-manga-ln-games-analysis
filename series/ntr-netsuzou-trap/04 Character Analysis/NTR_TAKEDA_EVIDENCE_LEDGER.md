## Knowledge gate for retrieval

A reconstruction should request a volume state before selecting an action. V01 T has hesitation and friendship appearances. V02 adds anomalies and sincerity concern, still no actual girls' history. V03 changes reassurance, then ends with the first actual sight. V04 adds sustained uncertainty and an explicit male-recipient model. V05 adds following and mistaken workplace concern, without named H or shown photo receipt. V06 adds publication and Y's named H account. None gives him the reader's complete H family flashback, private anti-burden call, earlier permission history, brother-copy path or every hidden feeling. These gates explain both errors and revisions; they are not deficits filled by analyst omniscience.

T→Y desire/help/renewal must be retrieved separately from Y→T affection/gratitude/guilt/refusal. T→F friendship/advice-seeking changes into confrontation and rejection of F's claimed benefit. T→H public school acquaintance and use of her assurance do not demonstrate romantic interest or a fully informed independent H biography.

## Strong exclusions and open retrieval debt

Supported negative evidence includes sudden initiation, unauthorized following and punching: a universal careful/nonintrusive/nonviolent model is contradicted. Changing sincerity concern and boundary withdrawal contradict endless patience. Persistent failed first-love hope contradicts wholly disinterested aid. Mistaken male/workplace premises contradict omniscient interpretation. These are actual contrary observations.

Evidence gaps are different: no T career attainment, unfamiliar-client conduct, new romance, complete full-history confrontation, long-term friendship outcome or broad self-analysis is shown. Those gaps cannot be made into failings or virtues. The endpoint gag supplies school return and a particular feeling, not adult closure.
