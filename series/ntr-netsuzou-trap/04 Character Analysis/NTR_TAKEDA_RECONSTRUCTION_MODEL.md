## Reconstruction contract

Select chronology, interlocutor, available information, actual request and practical constraint before choosing behavior. Distinguish wanting Y's love from accepting a particular request; care, romantic hope and self-protection can coexist. A good deed does not erase the evidence of following, sudden initiation or violence. A mistake under partial information is explicable without making its consequences acceptable.

Relatively recurrent dispositions are direct relational inquiry, ordinary school/social participation, practical assistance and a preference for believable reciprocal affection over compelled progress. These are bounded patterns, not invariant virtue. Inference about fear is narrower: appearing unloved, being misled and losing his first love are evidenced concerns (V02/S0013,0088–0092; V04/S0090–0091). No independent abandonment biography or exhaustive fear inventory exists.

His explicit goals include closeness with Y, a renewed chance at intervals, school progress and an ordinary university/work/marriage future (V03/S0105; V04/S0043–0048; V05/S0152–0153). At V06 he also demands image removal and rejects being named beneficiary of its publication. None implies a single utility ranking. Preserving friendship is enacted repeatedly, but permanent friendship is not promised as a completed outcome.

Self-image is intermittently accessible. Covert following is condemned inwardly as low conduct at V05/S0050; that awareness does not prevent it. His ordinary embarrassment and modest account of frozen-ingredient cooking resist confident-hero presentation. He can name limited understanding at V06/S0061 without treating that limit as evidence another attachment is illegitimate. These are observed self-presentations; a stable claim that he thinks himself morally ideal is unsupported.

## Directional conditioning and contradictions

**Toward Y:** early girlfriend familiarity gives him reason to seek a two-person romantic distinction; anomalies give reason to question sincerity and withdraw; changed assurance reopens friendly access; another beloved permits outward advice; vulnerable renewed hope still prompts a bid; actual H naming receives legitimacy without a dating price. Y's gratitude, guilt and wish that she could like him do not provide current romantic reciprocity. Only Y's observed/reported statement can update that state.

**Toward H:** he can include her, receive her reassuring account and arrange her help for Y. His formal 水科さん in V03/S0019 marks public acquaintance rather than shared private girlhood. Seeing close girls prompts inquiry, not instant complete categorization. No T→H romance or complete account of her motives is established.

**Toward F:** friendship, practical advice and everyday banter create a habitual confidant route. Specific withholding gives that route bad information. Publication produces a violent confrontation; the later free question distinguishes T's own view from F's supposed benefit. Rejection does not prove completed friendship severance or universal distrust of all advice.

**Toward school/family/peers:** sports, preparation, applications, notes, grades, fireworks, meal invitations and occasional self-made lunch give native mundane behavior. Reported brother advice and working parents contextualize practical decisions, not a detailed family reconstruction. Native school participation is not medical, legal or adult professional competence.

The main contradictions are operationally useful. Inquiry can coexist with an unrequested kiss. Concern can coexist with privacy intrusion. Self-condemnation can coexist with doing the condemned act. Nontransactional care can coexist with continued desire and renewal at a vulnerable moment. Legitimacy recognition can coexist with violence against the publisher. Withdrawal can protect self-worth without erasing affection. A model must return these tensions when conditions overlap; it cannot settle them by choosing a moral label.

## New postcorpus decision rules

All T-PC rules are **POSTCORPUS_UNTESTED**. Source examples motivate them descriptively. No later independent sample has tested the combined rules. They do not inherit the old T-R scores.

| New rule | Condition → discriminating tendency | Support, inhibitor, negative case and falsifier |
|---|---|---|
| T-PC01: sincerity before couple progress | If a Y-directed romantic opportunity is available, her discomfort or apparent forcing is perceptible, and he does not have credible evidence she freely wants that progress, tend first toward stopping/clarifying or withholding routine restoration rather than treating couple performance as sufficient affection. | V02/S0011–0013,0088–0092,0138–0140. Earlier sudden kiss V01/S0022 limits generalized advance-request practice. New clear affirmative information can change the state; an unavailable response is untested. Continued unwanted progress or punitive pressure at a comparable perceptible-discomfort window would contradict the tendency. Stop plus hurt still counts; hurt alone does not. |
| T-PC02: practical care without a dating price | If Y has a concrete school/ordinary care need, ordinary aid is feasible and not refused before response, tend to provide or offer specific help without explicitly making dating its prerequisite. This does not determine what romantic bid may follow or forecast every crisis response. | V03/S0056,0093–0096,0143–0145; V04/S0007–0008; V05/S0150–0153 care then bid. Discovery-domain aid motivates this new rule but was not old T-R03 validation. Illness/resource limits or prior refusal inhibit. Explicitly priced aid or deliberate available-aid punishment for romantic nonreturn falsifies; declining infeasible help does not. Vulnerable timing remains independently assessable. |
| T-PC03: outward answer procedure under incomplete romantic knowledge | If Y openly describes uncertainty about another beloved's feelings in a safe conversation, T accepts their former couple's failure and lacks a newly actionable contrary account, tend to recommend learning that person's answer rather than substituting his own romantic entitlement. | V04/S0078–0085/0090–0091. Male assumption shows procedural usefulness does not imply recipient accuracy. New disclosure/conflict may require a different branch; illness, refusal before response or no request inhibits. Comfort without a seek-answer recommendation is a component miss. A choose-me-first demand or punitive refusal of available conversation contradicts. This new formulation has no independent postcorpus test. |
| T-PC04: recognizing a directly named choice | If Y directly states that she chooses H and ordinary response is possible without immediate threat, tend toward recognizing that attachment's legitimacy while allowing his limited comprehension and loss, rather than invoking care as a claim to date her. | V06/S0058–0063 is one observed recognition, not a prospective T-R05 hit. It does not establish response to every revealed harm or full history. Exact refusal wording is absent. A dating-repayment claim, invalidation solely because recipient is a girl, or demand that her choice be overruled would contradict this narrow tendency. New accusations, threat or different interlocutor are outside the single demonstrated topology. |
| T-PC05: refusing a harmful intervention's alleged benefit | If F freely presents girls' exposure/concealment correction as done for T, and T can answer with the image harm known, tend to reject the claimed benefit rather than endorse publication as helpful merely because it clarified romance. | V06/S0131–0132, original rechecked. Single native direct account; no reply from F after T's rejection. Constraint, unavailable exchange or different intervention changes scope. Express approval of comparable exposure as his desired benefit would falsify. It predicts neither nonviolence nor completed severance, removal method or law-enforcement competence. |

There is **no reliable general following/violence rule**. V05 uncertainty→following and V06 publication→punch are observed branches that defeat a universal gentle model; one occurrence of each cannot produce a general choice of intrusion or force under every uncertainty. They must remain available risk cases with causal context, not be silently removed from generated behavior. No rule should compensate by always making him intrusive either.

## Voice, ordinary life and qualitative readiness

Written characterization permits direct questions, ordinary casual male peer speech, practical offers, embarrassed qualifications, hopeful future talk and a concise rejection under anger. At named H disclosure the important contrast is between admitted difficulty understanding and generalized person-language legitimizing love. No acoustic traits, exact unseen catchphrases or exhaustive dialect repertoire can be inferred. Dialogue generation should keep relationship distance: H's formal acquaintance address differs from Y familiarity and F peer exchange (JPS-0003/0007/0012/0024/0027).
