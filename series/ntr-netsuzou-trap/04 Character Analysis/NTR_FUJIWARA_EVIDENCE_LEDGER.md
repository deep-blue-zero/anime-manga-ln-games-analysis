## Information belongs to specific holders

F gains evidence of girls' conduct before T and knows T uncertainty through confidant exchanges. He captures/forwards a photograph and can withhold specifics while girls believe deletion. This does not establish every hidden wish, every refused act or complete H psychology. His early H-work knowledge is his statement; his location ignorance later is testimony with no contrary positive evidence. Analysts cannot turn frequent strategic withholding into proof that every denial is a lie.

T supplies concern and distance to F; F returns general suspicion, insinuation or blame rather than complete helpful disclosure. Y hears injury denial, suffocation report, identity prescription, guilt pressure and the later in-form arrangement account. H receives demand/pressure and contests or invokes promise terms. The reader combines these scenes and private computer intent. No individual necessarily shares that composite view.

## Contradictions to preserve

F's useful school advice and reported cooking skill are affirmative evidence of ordinary competence. They defeat a flat portrayal in which he can only threaten. They do not contradict or mitigate the fact of violence, conditional access, image retention, physical control or publication. A person can provide useful information and use other information harmfully.

F sometimes withholds; he later supplies a relational account Y can use. A universal silence/refusal rule is therefore false. His description of H/F as in form is not an omniscient explanation of H, but it helps distinguish acknowledged public status from emotional reciprocity. His allowance of requested breakup and refusal to police H work are not proof of generally respecting bodily autonomy.

His claimed service to T is evidence of how he represents exposure, not independently sufficient motive. No clean remorse, victim-centered reparation or full helpful clarification follows the free question. Absence of depicted interior guilt is a gap; the present beneficiary defense is positive evidence of restricted accountability.
