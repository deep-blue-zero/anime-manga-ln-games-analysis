# Hotaru: regulating attachment claims while retaining agency in ordinary life

Hotaru is reconstructable as a bounded native decision system whose approach, categorization, concealment and withdrawal vary with audience, feared dependence and the state of Yuma's attention. The strongest completed-corpus explanation is defensive attachment: proximity can be wanted while a credible claim on another person remains frightening. That explanation is not exhaustive motive, a clinical diagnosis or an ethical defense. Work and privacy objections can be legitimate choices even when avoidance is also plausible. Her conduct toward Yuma includes initiative and harm; toward Fujiwara it includes refusal, pressure and a form-limited arrangement; toward her father it includes a competent-seeming facade and an explicit anti-burden goal.

## Stable tendencies versus states that must change

Repeated tendencies include audience management, familiar play, indirect or categorical management of an attachment claim, practical service/care competence in selected contexts, and sensitivity to a threatened special place. They coexist with real apologies, cessation, local assent and legitimate resistance. Hotaru's capacity for a bright presentation does not establish pleasure in every act; her concealed pain does not establish helplessness in every setting.

Goals become more directly evidenced over time. V01–V05 offers indirect privileged-place/togetherness clues; V06 supplies temporal hurt, belief/trust questions and an anti-burden statement. The later evidence strengthens a defensive-attachment model. It does not prove a settled lifelong romantic identity, exact onset or retrospective awareness of everything she felt. Her V02 bad-person self-description can make a low-expectation partner feel easier; it remains testimony compatible with cynicism, pragmatic use and conflicted shame. The model does not install a diagnostic explanation in place of that uncertainty.

School and cooking weakness are context-specific contrary evidence to her father's competence claim. Service work and relocation are context-specific contrary evidence to generic incapacity. A prediction of helplessness based on the ending gag would ignore these decisions; a prediction of universal independence based on paid work would ignore loneliness, material depletion and chosen proximity. ## Directional conditioning and information boundaries

**Hotaru → Yuma.** Childhood familiar address and practical closeness permit intimacy without naming its claim. Y's other friends/boyfriend or unknown wishes threaten a privileged place. H can press Y to admit desire while herself retaining a practice/friend/category explanation. V06 naming does not instantly yield trust; it elicits disbelief and later relocation. Direct temporal hurt/trust words strengthen the attachment account, while protests about work and address remain valid in their own domain. Y's interpretation of a guarded child can guide Y's care without certifying H's whole mind.

**Hotaru → Fujiwara.** Public couple form, low-emotional-demand testimony and physical access coexist with refusal/punishment, leverage and unequal compliance. H sometimes invokes the mutual request term for her own charade; that is agency within the arrangement, not equal power or permission for every act. V06 arrangement testimony characterizes it as in form and filling lonely time; it does not depict an exhaustive motive or completed mutual breakup. H's self-bad comfort account must not be translated into objective safety. F's reports are not automatically H's direct speech or entirely false.

**Hotaru → Takeda.** H can selectively reassure him, include him in an intelligible future and use his relation to Y as an explanatory frame. Her café account changes his beliefs; the private mixed-truth line leaves which part false open. A model cannot make her know his complete inner response or use his ignorance as an unlimited constant. H-R03's failure is important: she does not always project Y's unnamed approach through him.

**Hotaru → father/family.** Reader-visible childhood absence and material provision provide context for an appearance of being fine. Later H directly states work/leaving and not wanting to burden anyone. The father believes competence and withholds her address at her request; his mistaken assessment does not invalidate the privacy condition. These scenes support family-specific presentation and a nonburden goal, not a generic habit toward all authority figures. Yuma is not shown receiving the whole flashback or private phone line.

**Hotaru → workplace/peers/school.** Service language and role competence differ from familiar teasing, school underperformance and domestic skill. Bright politeness does not prove enthusiastic acceptance of every client liberty; paid work does not prove adult expertise or a purely liberating environment. Peers' public condemnation and rumor generate different social stakes. The school-return apology is genuine bounded ordinary accountability, not full repair for earlier intimacy or a proof of graduation.

## Mature conditional hypotheses — POSTCORPUS_UNTESTED

All `H-PC-##` rules are developed after seeing V06. They have **no new held-out validation**. A helpful explanatory mechanism is not a prediction that was made before its evidence.

### H-PC-01 — Proximity can be sought while its explicit claim is reduced

**Condition:** Yuma matters specially, H's desired relation is not mutually acknowledged or trusted, and familiar private access is available. **Expected response range:** approach/play with a practice, friendship or categorical account that limits overt demand. **Inhibitors:** a direct tolerable need bid, clear objection that she acknowledges, audience/physical constraints or a changed relationship can alter it. **Development evidence:** HEL-0001–0002,0006,0011,0017,0024. **Counterexamples:** cessation, apologies, local acceptance and V06 direct hurt/trust speech. **Falsifier:** repeated transparent need/relationship negotiation before indirect access under the same undeclared state. This does not revive H-R03's missing T substitution or claim all help is a pretext.

### H-PC-02 — Public category defense can claim protection without representing Y's preference

**Condition:** public exposure threatens a relation H has not safely acknowledged and she can intervene in its meaning. **Expected first response:** distancing or an alternative story may precede owning her attachment, potentially presenting herself as shielding Y. **Inhibitors:** secure prior agreement about disclosure or a changed audience might alter response; neither is established here as reliable competence. **Evidence:** V05 no-count is private categorical background; V06 supplies the single depicted public self-complicity/denial, followed by a private protection account (HEL-0024–0025). These are not two public replications. **Counterevidence:** Y explicitly prefers the queer label to erasure. **Falsifier:** accepting Y's account and openly owning the relation first in comparable conditions. Claimed protection is not pure motive, full prior joint scheme or ethical permission to humiliate. The single public demonstration makes this hypothesis particularly weak for portability.

### H-PC-03 — A credible wanted commitment may activate disbelief and self-removal

**Condition:** Y has plainly chosen H, H expects loss or cannot trust durability, and departure is materially possible. **Expected response range:** challenge the offer, retreat or reduce dependency alongside continuing proximity bids. **Inhibitors:** tolerable direct expression/continued availability might permit staying; this is a mechanism hypothesis, not a tested intervention. **Evidence:** temporal hurt, belief question, shared morning→absence/relocation, nonburden and trust fear (HEL-0026–0029). **Counterexamples:** actual receptive closeness, coat-grip and joint return. **Falsifier:** repeated stable direct need bids/staying without distancing under comparably stated disbelief. Work/privacy decisions need separate reasons; not every departure is abandonment fear.

### H-PC-04 — Nonemergency control of work or privacy invites a concrete objection

**Condition:** Y interferes with H's work/location/independent choice without a demonstrated immediate danger, and H has a response opportunity. **Expected first response:** identify a boundary, material cost or right to decide rather than quietly grant authority. **Evidence:** V03 work/overprotection and V06 address/work/income/leave requests (HEL-0014,0020,0029). **Inhibitors:** actual emergency, acknowledged permission or effective restraint changes conditions. **Counterexample:** eventually staying/returning after trust speech, which is a later choice rather than consent to the preceding pull. **Falsifier:** explicit acceptance of Y deciding before objection under comparable facts. This broader mature rule is new; it does not supply H-R04's uncertain childhood-authority trigger retroactively.

### H-PC-05 — Familiar care and selected competence coexist with uneven self-maintenance

**Condition:** ordinary native domestic/school/work task, known interlocutor and feasible resources. **Expected response range:** practical care/service or playful closeness, with competence depending on task rather than one global adult/child role. **Evidence:** hospitality/warmth, requested fastening help, service performance, poor cooking and school difficulty, missed notes/hug gag (HEL-0005,0008,0013,0021,0030). **Inhibitors:** role pressure, lack of skill, money/time or avoidance can alter action. **Counterexamples:** father overestimates grades/domestic ability; relocation depletes money by H report. **Falsifier of task-specificity:** a broad sustained competence or incapacity pattern contradicting these contrasts. No professional qualification, dietary judgment or childlike global identity is inferred.

### H-PC-06 — Low-expectation arrangements can seem tolerable without being safe

**Condition:** H experiences a person as not requiring goodness or trusted reciprocal emotional expectation, and loneliness or access needs remain. **Expected response:** tolerate/formulate a limited arrangement or describe it as easier, while specific refusal and harm remain possible. **Evidence:** self-bad comfort account, pressured mutual terms and V06 form/lonely-time testimony (HEL-0010,0016; [REL-0035]). **Inhibitors:** unwanted specific act, meaningful alternative attachment, changed expectations or force can produce resistance/exit. **Counterevidence:** explicit refusal/punishment and named trust fear expose the arrangement's costs. **Falsifier:** voluntarily insisting on a full emotional demand before such an arrangement under the same stated avoidance. ## Japanese written-register constraints

Hotaru's familiar **由真ちゃん** and common **私** do not have one emotional meaning. The address persists through help, jealousy, ridicule, vulnerable speech and play. It cannot prove that the relation stayed merely childhood friendship or that a later sentence is benevolent. Early practice/help terms manage a claim; **親友** and future-neighbor language can carry real attachment without naming romance. **いいよ** at an actual offer has local affirmative force without serving as a lifelong relationship agreement ([JPS-0001], [JPS-0008], [JPS-0013], [JPS-0017], [JPS-0019]).

Service politeness differs from familiar autonomy confrontation. A reconstruction must not make her workplace dialogue identical to bedroom teasing or convert a bright service manner into motive. No acoustic delivery, actor performance, unsupported dialect or generalized seductive repertoire is established by written speech.

V05 no-count/identity language is hers, not Yuma's self-label or authorial truth. V06 **かばってあげた** positions H as benefactor whose supposed protection Y contests. Later direct hurt and trust fear narrow the earlier uncertainty more strongly than another teasing sentence. The anti-burden line is spoken privately to her father; do not place it in a conversation with Yuma as if the manga depicted that disclosure. The S0100 V05 "only for now" aside remains inward/faint-mutter ambiguous, with referent open and no mutually known plan.
