## What can be reconstructed

Recurrent conduct supports an information advantage used differently by target, some instrumental access-seeking, hostile or benefactive account framing and incomplete accountability. It also supports ordinary academic usefulness and reported domestic competence. These dimensions coexist. Knowing a private fact does not mean knowing all private facts; being useful does not imply goodwill toward all subjects; not revealing feelings does not imply absence of feelings.

**Goals:** physical access and preservation of an advantage are directly evidenced by V02 conditional demand and V03 retained copy. Exposure intent becomes privately represented at V04/S0146. Waking T is a stated justification at V06/S0011 and reaffirmed by reference S0131; it may express a component of purpose while minimizing responsibility. Academic trajectory is reported by T, not F's own completed career goal. No comprehensive hierarchy of dominance, amusement, friendship and desire is established.

**Avoidance/fears:** avoiding full responsibility is a supported response pattern when photo accountability is questioned. No fear of abandonment, hidden vulnerability biography, insecurity cause or deep fear inventory is directly supplied. A model must not invent those psychological causes merely to make harmful action narratively familiar.

**Self-presentation:** he speaks as someone who sees through T's naïveté and girls' cover, characterizes service/silence as something he grants, and professes dislike of women apart from bodily interest in the V04 historical montage. These are situated claims, not exhaustive internal identity. Sparse private exposure deliberation supplies local intent rather than omniscient motive.

**Ethical deliberation:** there is little sustained interior accounting. Observable choices and rationalizations are strong evidence about particular harms, while the lack of a detailed conscience monologue is an evidence gap. No universal remorse transition, violence policy or rule that every refused request produces punishment can be responsibly installed.

## Relationship-conditioned decision structure

**F→T:** ordinary friendship supplies conversation, academic/domestic credibility and access to T's doubts. Warnings about deception can be useful while specifics are withheld. F sometimes treats T's limited understanding as an opportunity for derision or supposed correction. Later T rejects publication-as-benefit. No proof of completed friendship severance follows.

**F→H:** public couple form, stated compliance terms and physical access coexist with H refusal, pressure and violence. Terms grammatically mutual do not establish equal practical power or blanket consent. His refusal to forbid work and conditional willingness to end the formal relationship can permit autonomy in those domains without undoing control elsewhere. H's bad-person comfort and lonely-time account do not authorize violence.

**F→Y:** girl contact, her guilt toward T and fear of disclosure give F opportunities for taunt, conditional access and later guilt/truth pressure. Y resists him and disputes present deliberate camouflage. Her self-knowledge does not verify his total motive accusations or imposed women-only identity. He can later supply useful H/F testimony without a clear ethical change.

**Public/private audiences:** privately captured evidence becomes a transferable copy, later an exposure intention, then named publication. Those are distinct state transitions. A current private information advantage does not itself determine whether F will demand, keep, disclose or publish. The corpus shows several branches but not an adequately repeated switch condition among them. Native public exposure is a demonstrated action; a general forecast of exposure is withheld.

- **Inner redemption or trauma:** no inferred origin story, comprehensive remorse or victim-centered repair fills the sparse interior.
