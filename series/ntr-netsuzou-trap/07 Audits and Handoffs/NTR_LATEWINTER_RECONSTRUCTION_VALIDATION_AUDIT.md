## Selection and visibility contract

For each original rule, inspect the earliest candidate, establish its temporal state, information, interlocutor, constraint and response opportunity independently of the desired prediction, then score the first matched response. **NDO** includes an absent antecedent or absent decision opportunity, identified per row; it is not success. **NOT_APPLICABLE** records an explicitly native temporal/state scope that cannot simply be extended. **INSUFFICIENT_VISIBILITY** records a potentially relevant scene whose necessary condition or response is not shown. None is evidence of a predicted mechanism. A later compatible scene cannot repair an earlier matched miss. No such miss was found in the one valid test; all old mainline failures remain fixed.

The original Y/H risk ranges and task-specificity rules have deliberately weaker discrimination. A single compatible worry, touch or smile cannot establish them. The endpoint-specific Y-PC-07/H-PC-07 retain their literal V06 state language; treating later affection as an instant-trust test would both broaden the rule and infer an unshown anxiety antecedent. Comparison may inform development but receives no score here.

### Y-PC-01 — Threatened special access draws concrete pursuit before full clarification

**Condition:** at Y-V03 or later, she still wants H closeness, perceives credible access loss, lacks an intelligible negotiated alternative and has a real opportunity to approach. **Expected first response:** seek concrete contact/information before resolving all relationship meaning. **Inhibitors:** actual unavailability, clear practical barrier, chosen respite, or a credible agreed return/contact arrangement may redirect action; the last inhibitor is a hypothesis, not established competence. **Development evidence:** work following, brochure/practice, survey and search (YEL-0012,0018–0019,0028). **Counterexamples:** real summer withdrawal and her insight that she has no authority. **Falsifier:** sustained voluntary distance through a comparable available loss window, or candid negotiation before access-seeking. ### Y-PC-02 — Self-image conflict delays public truth relative to inward recognition

**Condition:** Y recognizes an attachment/act incompatible with her current good-friend/girlfriend account, while candid disclosure risks rejection or social cost. **Expected response:** an incomplete explanation, denial or privately self-critical account may precede full disclosure. **Inhibitors:** explicitly choosing responsibility can change order; after V06 named disclosure, mere continued familiarity is not a fresh secrecy trigger. **Evidence:** phone lie, jealousy admission, practice cover and public no-beloved claim (YEL-0007,0010,0018,0021). **Counterexample:** V06 direct H naming to T and H. **Falsifier:** immediate complete disclosure in a genuinely comparable pre-disclosure conflict. **Condition:** H-directed desire is active, Y experiences denial/humiliation or abrupt access frustration, the person is physically available and anger rises. **Expected response range:** forceful pursuit, grabbing or a retaliatory act becomes plausible alongside verbal confrontation. **Inhibitors:** physical distance, interruption, deliberation or respected exit can redirect; no reliable universal inhibitor is proved. **Evidence:** V05 classroom initiative/slap and V06 grips/pulls (YEL-0020,0023–0024,0026,0028). **Counterexamples:** chosen absence, ordinary requests and quiet T disclosure. **Falsifier of a stronger force tendency:** comparable accessible conflict handled consistently through nonphysical boundaries.
