## Authority of a test

A prospective test requires an already frozen literal condition, a later eligible opportunity and an observable specified response. Native scenes remain correlated, often repeated across chapter openings. Backstory newly revealed later is evidence for model development, not behavior occurring after a freeze. An unshown interval or a missing antecedent is not a success. Neither does every unmodeled new action count as a failed prediction.

The first matched response or explanatory window owns its score. A partial component must not be made whole by a later fitting sentence, unseen motive or larger consequence. **NDO** abbreviates NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY. A mechanism component miss can occur without the explicit reverse-order falsifier: no demand at a completed eligible window is a missing predicted component even when helpful relinquishment is also absent. That distinction is essential for F, and for Y's different cover explanations.

A later general defensive-attachment model can be useful without changing the failed earlier mechanism.

These errors matter because behavior has more dimensions than approach-before-disclosure. Replacing friendship/protection with any cover, or treating inner desire as a spoken request, would make a rule exclude too little. Y's later decisive choice cannot fix the first-window misses.

F's repeated conditional-demand rules overestimated how consistently one leverage mechanism would organize later handling. Secrecy-as-favor, physical control, guilt/truth pressure and public exposure are consequential but distinct. The missing-demand components survive V06 attribution. F-R04 supplies later bounded accountability support without making the earlier family of predictions universally correct.

T's design accumulated narrow information exclusions. Those exclusions correctly preserve history but yield few diagnostic opportunities after new discovery. This is a testing limitation, not evidence that T never changes or cannot be modeled. His named-recipient recognition is descriptively strong and was prospectively UNMODELED. A broader new care/recognition model requires its own future tests, not inherited T-R03/R05 successes.

Y-R06 and H-R06 retain different visibility/applicability limits. A refusal report can support represented action without proving its exact speech. A released grip permits a primary ordinary exchange reading without eliminating the safety counterreading. H-R04's late resemblance remains uncertain because childhood authority cannot simply be inferred from any request to stay.
