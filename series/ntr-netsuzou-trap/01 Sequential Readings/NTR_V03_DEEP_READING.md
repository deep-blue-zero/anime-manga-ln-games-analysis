## trap:9 — apparent repair keeps control over the account

**OBSERVED.** Fujiwara renews the demand and threatens to send the photograph to Takeda when Yuma demands deletion (S0006–0007). Hotaru takes the phone, a delete icon appears, and she says it is deleted (S0008). She sends Yuma home and allows Fujiwara to stay, recasting the girls' conduct as practice or a joke (S0009–0010). Yuma plugs her ears, listens to music, and considers the interruption next door (S0011–0014). Sounds and her imagination do not establish an exact subsequent act, its participants' permission, or what would otherwise have happened. Her knock complaining about noise and Hotaru's private thought of her make the neighboring wall an affective as well as acoustic division (S0015). Morning avoidance follows (S0016–0018).

The first new information-handling scene supports the prior Fujiwara forecast: renewed access/nondisclosure pressure occurs before phone surrender. It is a continuation of the cliffhanger, however, not an independent social setting or proof of a universal strategy. Later panels reveal more than the girls know. Fujiwara contacts his unnamed brother, recalls forwarding the image before deletion, and asks him to save it; the brother's computer displays the girls' photograph (S0038–0040). Transfer and retained third-party copy are now observed, unlike V02's mere captured-image uncertainty. Takeda is not its shown recipient. Hotaru's assurance of deletion proves neither complete erasure nor knowing deception about a copy she may not know exists.

Hotaru separately invites Takeda to a café (S0019–0020). His formal **水科さん** preserves the distance between their public school acquaintance and her access to intimate facts. He explains that lateness was a trigger, while forcing and concealment raised the possibility of another beloved (S0021–0022). Hotaru supplies a selectively reassuring account: someone persistently wanted Yuma, Yuma did not reciprocate, that person has given up, and secrecy reflected fear of misunderstanding; Yuma likes Takeda and he should reconsider (S0023–0025). This is her testimony, not a narrator's acquittal of Yuma or complete account of the girls' history. A nearby Fujiwara panel permits possible observation without proving he heard every word. Takeda then invites Yuma (S0026).

The girls' ensuing balcony conversation is a real change, not merely another identical tease. Hotaru apologizes for confusing conduct, admits jealousy over Yuma's first boyfriend, and recognizes that Yuma felt similarly about hers (S0028–0029). She calls Yuma a uniquely special best friend and says she did not want someone else taking her (S0030). The apology and jealousy testimony should be counted. They are also incomplete: the conduct is recast as joke/practice, individual earlier refusals are not addressed, and no agreed romantic relationship or act-specific repair is negotiated (S0031–0034). Hotaru promises to stop and asks that they remain best friends even with boyfriends. Yuma's assent supplies friendship acknowledgment rather than an explicit wish for intimacy.

S0035 then gives Hotaru private thought: embed one part lie in nine parts truth. Unlike an inferred expression, this verifies deliberate account management. It does not identify a particular false clause. Treating the friendship assertion, jealousy, cessation, or Takeda reassurance as individually disproved would exceed the evidence. The more precise reading is that sincere attachment and strategic representation coexist. School friendliness can therefore be achieved by an explanation whose adequacy the reader doubts. Yuma's private question whether this is good exposes that doubt without yet producing truthful speech (S0036–0038).

## trap:10 — an unknown life defeats the childhood protector's map

Spring school scenes widen the represented world. Yuma, Hotaru and Takeda share the new senior class; Fujiwara is in another class (S0043–0046). Group messages are friendly while Yuma feels a wall (S0047–0048). A teacher's account of Hotaru nearly failing promotion contrasts with Yuma's remembered excellent childhood marks (S0049–0050). Neither establishes a fixed intelligence level. Takeda proposes preparatory school; Hotaru refuses university and says she will marry a rich man, provoking classmates and Yuma's warning (S0051–0053). Her casual future claim could be a shield, an actual economic preference, or provocation. It is not a settled life plan.

Yuma's mother permits preparatory school; Takeda helps with the application (S0054–0057). Yuma's **別れて以来** now describes their breakup and improving ordinary conversation. This newly establishes how she represents the spring state; it does not retroactively rewrite the V02 freeze as already knowing a finalized breakup. Fujiwara's advanced science course and useful school advice demonstrate competence beside exploitative conduct (S0058–0059). His statement that he has not seen Hotaru recently breaks Yuma's assumption that late homecomings prove their meetings (S0060). The source allows him mundane usefulness without excusing violence or coercion.

At a city backdoor Yuma sees Hotaru with a rubbish bag, invents a mother-related excuse to leave the boys, follows, and discovers her hostess-bar work (S0061–0067). This is new work knowledge. A suggestive venue and costume do not prove paid sexual services. Yuma pulls Hotaru's wrist hard enough to elicit pain (S0068). A customer pulls Yuma toward him; she refuses and resists (S0069–0070). Hotaru and staff apologize to the customer, while the manager warns against disruption and recruits Yuma (S0071–0072). Service politeness normalizes customer expectations within the venue; it does not supply either girl's agreement to unwanted touch.

Yuma will not leave without Hotaru (S0073–0074). Hotaru tells the manager she wants trial work without obtaining her affirmative answer (S0075–0076). A protective refusal to depart is converted into a labor commitment. The practical option to go home exists, but attachment and imagined responsibility make it costly. Yuma's later participation cannot retroactively turn either the customer's handling or Hotaru's representation of her choice into permission. This also is not the predicted unnamed romantic reapproach: rescue, work, audience and immediate conflict supply different conditions.

## trap:11 — self-recognition does not immediately produce respect for autonomy

Yuma reads Hotaru's ultimatum as an expectation that she will give up and decides to persist (S0078–0079). Requested fastening help with the work costume differs from self-appointed help in earlier volumes: she asks, receives assistance, and thanks Hotaru (S0080–0084). Close practical help triggers memory and desire, without authorizing every future act. The contrast shows that competence-to-intimacy staging can carry more than one permission structure.

Hotaru switches into bright service speech while Yuma remains visibly awkward (S0085–0087). Yuma resists customer liberties, removes a man's arm from Hotaru, and ultimately punches him when he draws both close (S0088–0090). This is another directly enacted resistance, not a passive person's merely imagined refusal. It also causes workplace costs: both are scolded, not fired (S0091). Hotaru says the work pays well and she needs savings, withholding the reason (S0092). Her economic need is testimony supported by continued work; its ultimate purpose remains undisclosed.

Takeda notices missing notes and altered behavior, offers copies and consultation, and does not make assistance conditional on renewed dating (S0093–0097). Yuma appreciates his kindness and cries while thinking about her earlier harm to him. The boys' banter and meal invitation keep friendship and ordinary life present (S0098). Neither this care nor Takeda's continued wish to try again later proves restoration of the former couple. His changed assurance from Hotaru already prevents a literal T-R02 unchanged-information test.

Yuma waits outside the bar to avoid further disruption and asks Hotaru to quit (S0099). Hotaru distinguishes paid work from selling her body, then asks why she must do only work Yuma approves or arrange everything as Yuma wants (S0100–0101). Her point about authority is valid even if earlier intimacy conduct crossed boundaries. A person can wrong another in one domain and still have an autonomous life in another. An ice-cream treat returns them to familiar ordinary closeness (S0102), but Yuma privately acknowledges that she is neither guardian nor boyfriend and has no right to control Hotaru (S0103). Alternating boys' and girls' scenes contrast Takeda's first-love persistence with Yuma's wish that Hotaru abandon Fujiwara and her condemnation of her own self-interest (S0104–0105). Public kindness and private appropriation remain different facts.

Takeda's outing proposal includes both girls; Hotaru declines because of work (S0106–0108). Yuma learns that Fujiwara already knows about the job. He says he knew its contents from the start, declines any obligation to forbid it, and reports that Hotaru wants to leave home because living next to Yuma feels suffocating (S0109–0112). This reported exit motive wounds Yuma, whose own reading treats disclosure to Fujiwara as evidence of comparative intimacy (S0113–0116). It remains **Fujiwara's report**, not Hotaru's directly depicted private reason. Accuracy, partial truth, and strategic provocation are all possible. His refusal to police work differs from his coercive demand; no single good/bad label replaces those mechanisms.

## trap:12 — the public claim and the unnamed private approach

Color staging and a backward time cue arrange another performance of closeness (S0117–0122). Yuma returns, claiming that apology and earnest effort won the manager over; Hotaru doubts it. Yuma's agreeable service unsettles Hotaru (S0123–0124). When a client takes liberties, Hotaru intervenes with **この子は私のだから**, this girl is mine (S0125). It is a possessive public claim protecting or reserving access, not a mutually agreed girlfriend declaration. Customers ask about lesbian identity and demand a game demonstration (S0126–0127). Their category and audience pressure do not supply the girls' self-identification or consent. The Pocky breaks and Hotaru jokes about disappointment (S0128–0129); a completed kiss is not depicted.

Afterward Yuma explains that she wants to protect Hotaru. Hotaru replies that she is no longer the bullied child and objects to overprotection (S0130–0131). Yuma inwardly recognizes how easily she lies to Hotaru: the unknown part of Hotaru's life bothers her, and her supposed concern covers more than safety (S0132). She resolves not to let Hotaru leave even if hated (S0133). The black field, close face and imagined distant Hotaru give refusal of loss a visual firmness absent from her spoken account. This is evidence of Yuma's own resolve, not proof that Hotaru hates her or intends to escape her specifically. Recognition here is not yet a cure for appropriation: she understands a limit while retaining a wish to override separation.

Yuma skips preparatory school with a false cold excuse (S0134). The boys discuss recent helpfulness; Fujiwara warns Takeda that naïveté leaves him open to deception and exploitation (S0135–0136). The warning may be accurate and hypocritical together; its helpful or malicious ultimate motive is unconfirmed. Hotaru visits Yuma's mother and learns the school claim, then says she has seen Yuma with a boy in the city, apologizing that she may be mistaken (S0137–0138). The panel isolates Hotaru's smiling mouth, but cannot alone determine intention or certify the claim's accuracy. The mother later calls preparatory school and establishes absence (S0141). This is actual verification, not a mere omniscient parental guess.

Without Hotaru at work, Yuma cannot bear customer handling and leaves (S0139). Her city walk links guilt toward parents, Takeda, school and the manager before maternal confrontation over wasted fees (S0140–0142). Material obligations give concealment costs beyond romantic embarrassment. A later genuine fever, measured **38.5°C**, turns the earlier false illness into a represented fact (S0143–0144). It is not evidence of a medically established cause or proof that illness morally punishes her. Takeda arranges care and Hotaru brings her bag at his request (S0145).

Yuma says she will quit the work; Hotaru is glad and says she did not want Yuma there, while admitting inconsistency with her own choice (S0146–0147). This should not be flattened into a perfectly coherent autonomy position. She reserves difference between her life and Yuma's and can protect Yuma while exposing herself. Yuma feels a line forming between them (S0148). Hotaru leans close to check her temperature and wishes recovery (S0149). When she withdraws, Yuma pulls her back by the uniform into intimate closeness (S0150). The hand and initiative are clear; hair obscures precise contact, so a definite completed kiss is not claimed. Her desire remains unnamed, and she apologizes afterward (S0152).

Hotaru is surprised, then smiles and reapproaches. She frames the situation through fever, says **いいよ**, and invites Yuma to pass the cold to her (S0153–0154). She makes no Takeda-substitute explanation. This is a genuine negative case for the previously predicted framing under an undeclared-romantic-wish reading. The V02 freeze also used broad attachment/mutual-acknowledgment wording; their new special-friendship exchange permits a changed-condition reading. Both the mechanism failure and scope ambiguity must survive. Illness and joke language constrain any inference of full, durable romantic acknowledgment; they do not erase Yuma's observed initiative or Hotaru's response.

The sequence cuts to Takeda obtaining a drink and returning to help, then reaching the infirmary and seeing the girls close, with Yuma holding Hotaru's uniform (S0151,0155–0156). Unlike a threatened message or uncompleted approach to a door, this is actual visual access. It establishes that he sees the position, not that he now knows their complete history, all exact acts, or their agreed relationship. Both girls turn and notice him in the doorway S0155–0156; what they believe he saw or inferred is not established. His interpretation and next action remain future evidence.

## Embedded backstory and boundary argument

**少女リプレイス** identifies the girls' middle-school third year (S0159). Yuma notices Hotaru's makeup and maturity, hears about a boyfriend, and imagines adult experience (S0160). Those imagined acts are not proven. Hotaru offers makeup; Yuma hesitates but accepts practical help (S0161–0163). The resulting closeness and Yuma's feeling of being left behind even with the same makeup distinguish access to an appearance from shared development (S0164). This supports the long-running concern with unknown faces and lost priority. It is newly admitted backstory, not a prospective success for a rule awaiting V03 continuation.

The volume's strongest claim is that proximity does not guarantee knowledge or authority. Deleting a phone image does not erase a sent copy; a friendship acknowledgment does not settle romance; protection does not grant control of work or departure; illness assistance does not disclose all wishes. Yuma acquires more self-knowledge and independently pursues closeness, yet does not give the others a complete account. Hotaru offers an apology and autonomy argument worth taking seriously while keeping strategically incomplete representation and contradictory protection. Takeda's care persists outside a restored couple and finally places him where ordinary assistance grants painful visual access. Fujiwara has mundane competence and a potentially accurate warning without ceasing to exploit information.

The strongest counterreading remains mutual dependence and control rather than reciprocal romantic love. Yuma's refusal to allow departure, Hotaru's possessive public line, strategic reassurance and workplace maneuvers support it. Voluntary pursuit, real jealousy testimony, practical care and an accepting private response challenge an amusement-only or wholly passive account. They still do not establish an agreed romantic future, complete ethical repair, Hotaru's intended beloved, or a confirmed reason for leaving. Earlier communicated refusals retain their force.
