## trap:1 — private acts and public explanations

**OBSERVED.** The color karaoke opening places two apparent couples in a shared leisure space while intimacy between the girls is hidden from the boys (S0003–0005). The hush gesture anticipates a recurrent demand: maintain the ordinary group scene while privately doing something incompatible with its publicly legible pairings. Yuma and Hotaru are named at S0007–0008; Takeda is identified at S0008, Fujiwara at S0023. Hotaru's full name 水科蛍 appears at S0044. No external roster supplies additional names.

Yuma narrates childhood proximity, neighboring homes, Hotaru's vulnerability to bullying, and her own protective role (S0009–0010). She also recalls being pleased that Hotaru depended specifically on her. This is not merely a biography explaining why she tolerates a friend's teasing. It gives Yuma an investment in being uniquely needed. **INFERRED:** a protector identity can make intervention rewarding as well as caring. **OPEN:** the childhood scenes do not establish Hotaru's own retrospective understanding.

In the restroom, Hotaru frames unwanted intimate contact as practice for Yuma's boyfriend (S0011–0016). Yuma asks to wait, calls the situation strange, and says **やめて**. Hotaru continues until the boys' interruption. The word **予行練習** at S0014 reallocates the apparent purpose: an act initiated by Hotaru supposedly benefits Yuma's heterosexual relationship. It does not supply permission. The practical frame makes refusal harder to articulate without seeming inexperienced or frightened, but the depicted verbal objections remain clear. Hotaru then gives Takeda a makeup explanation for the delay (S0017), enabling a public reading that leaves the actual interaction invisible.

Takeda subsequently asks Yuma whether she avoids being alone with him (S0019–0022). She explains her nervousness; he kisses her suddenly. There is no explicit advance request and no depicted rejection in that scene. This particular ambiguity must not disappear inside an idealized contrast between a considerate boy and a manipulative girl. His direct inquiry is nevertheless a different mechanism from Hotaru's practice claim and continued contact after objection.

At home, Yuma's anticipated happy first romance is displaced by thinking about Hotaru (S0023–0024). A balcony conversation produces a playful apology followed by blame of Yuma's appealing reactions (S0025–0028). Hotaru's blank reaction to hearing about Takeda's kiss is visually consequential; jealousy is an inference, with control, amusement, or wounded priority as alternatives. Her subsequent question about what kind of kiss he gave leads to a false lip-cleaning pretext and another surprise kiss (S0029–0034). Yuma's mother interrupts; Hotaru muffles Yuma's response and maintains secrecy, then resumes after the mother leaves. Yuma eventually pushes her away. The available resistance does not make earlier continuation consensual.

Hotaru's claim to prepare Yuma before Takeda gets access is also a claim to firstness (S0035–0037). She invokes what might happen if Takeda found out, then retracts the implication as a joke and calls the interaction a **女の子同士の秘密**. This is exposure pressure, though not yet a formal ultimatum with a demanded exchange. Yuma thinks she could tell Takeda or refuse; her inability to make those thoughts effective is a question the narrative explicitly leaves alive. At S0038 she compares the girls' kiss favorably with Takeda's, acknowledging attraction. That admission is evidence of desire **after** the boundary crossings, not retroactive permission for them.
