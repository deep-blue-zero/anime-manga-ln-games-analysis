# LateWinter: selective attachment after return

LateWinter makes the returned relation ordinary enough to include school, friends, a visit and private closeness. It does not make that relation socially expansive or fully explained. Hotaru can say directly that Yuma matters to her, can refuse the social life Yuma recommends, and can ask about Yuma's expression without knowing her inward thought. Yuma wants Hotaru to value others and also admits that losing her uniquely necessary place would hurt. The after-story consequently extends the mainline's distinction between real attachment and adequate relationship practice. Its ending is a withheld concern, not a certificate of completed repair.

## Compact sequence and the consequential cuts

Hotaru apologizes to the teacher for unauthorized absence. Yuma explains inwardly that Hotaru had asked her father to submit a withdrawal notice, but he apparently forgot. Some students still look oddly at the girls after Fujiwara's photo dissemination. Yuma's friends invite her to the cafeteria and extend the invitation to Hotaru, who declines and says she will see Yuma later. On a subsequent outdoor walk Yuma asks whether Hotaru wants friends. Hotaru disputes the demand to manufacture interest. At Hotaru's apartment, Hotaru takes and hangs Yuma's outerwear while the father's renewed absence prompts an account of childhood loneliness. Hotaru rejects Yuma's conclusion that she cares about nobody: she cares about Yuma specifically. After private closeness, Yuma privately hopes Hotaru will someday value others, then says nothing is wrong when asked. The extra gives the friends' own discussion of the relationship and their different judgments.

The cut from S0008 to S0009 matters. Outdoor coats and bags replace the school invitation scene. It does **not** show Yuma abandoning lunch, immediately following Hotaru, eating with the friends, or accepting a negotiated separation. Any of those completions would fabricate the first response needed for an access or ordinary-care forecast. The closing private scene likewise depicts a visit and repose, not moving possessions, a lease or residence change.

## Return, institutional contingency and the father account

S0005 contains two different kinds of evidence. Hotaru's teacher apology is **POST_V06_STATE**: actual school participation continues. Yuma's account of the unsubmitted withdrawal is **RETROSPECTIVE_DISCLOSURE_ABOUT_EARLIER_STATE**, qualified by apparent/report-mediated knowledge. It explains how formal enrollment survived an intended withdrawal. It does not establish that Hotaru secretly arranged to preserve school, that her father deliberately protected her future, or that returning had no cost. Institutional continuity depends partly on another person's failure to act.

Hotaru tells Yuma that her father is not interested in her and that this is a relief, or rather…; the unfinished phrase leaves the affect less settled than a clean statement of emancipation. At S0011 she says he left again after her return and that his girlfriend apparently dislikes her. Yuma's thought that Hotaru was neglected for that reason is an interpretation of this testimony, not a direct scene inside the father's mind. His precise intention and the girlfriend's account remain unavailable.

At S0012 Hotaru distinguishes present indifference from childhood loneliness. This newly direct self-description strengthens the mainline's family/attachment reading without turning it into a diagnosis or the sole cause of every earlier action. It also shows that direct retrospective speech is possible now. The source supplies neither an apology for each earlier harm nor a full account of all motives. Yuma's worry and Hotaru's attempt to ease it are mutual attention; neither proves reliable repair over repeated conflicts.

S0012 also depicts practical hospitality independently of the family speech. In the right middle panel Hotaru holds a hanger and extends her free hand toward Yuma's coat; the left middle panel shows her handling/hanging the outerwear. This ordinary domestic task has a known visitor and feasible resources. It supplies practical care, not a demonstrated shared household or sustained competence pattern. The original scoring checkpoint overlooked this task and excluded H-PC-05 too strongly. The [fresh adversarial audit](../07%20Audits%20and%20Handoffs/NTR_LATEWINTER_ADVERSARIAL_INTEGRATION_AUDIT.md) preserves that original NDO and records the explicit current correction to **PARTIAL**: the care/service branch is observed, while uneven self-maintenance and task-dependent contrasts remain untested. S0005 return/apology and S0007–0008 participation choice do not show an earlier practical/academic task performance; the later private closeness cannot replace this first task window.

## A refusal that remains a refusal

The friends ask Yuma first at S0007. After their hesitation, the dark-haired friend includes **水科さん** at S0008. Hotaru's **私はいいや**, apology and **じゃ後でねー** are intelligible declining and later-contact language. She refuses a group activity without vanishing from the whole relationship. The scene does not require the analyst to diagnose hostility from facial staging, or to call the invitation a homophobic act.

Yuma's later **友達作ろうと思わないの？** and suggestion of effort at S0009 express concern but attempt to change Hotaru's independent social preference. Hotaru's first response questions why Yuma is asking this now and explicitly says she is not interested in friends. S0010 makes the argument concrete: does Yuma have interest in mathematics, politics or cricket? A preference cannot be produced merely because a partner judges it better. The exaggeration is comic, but its autonomy claim is real.

The reply does not establish that Hotaru can reject every request safely, or that Yuma has abandoned all rescue authority. No enforced attendance follows, which is important negative evidence against reading this exchange as another completed bodily override. Equally, the lack of compulsion in one conversation is too little to certify restraint under work, privacy, abandonment fear or a serious refusal. This is the first new response qualifying for the broad independent-choice condition of **H-PC-04**; the held-out audit bounds its support to social preference rather than silently promoting it to a work or housing test.

## Selective attachment and Yuma's recognition

Yuma moves from father indifference to **…誰にも興味ないんだね** at S0012. Hotaru answers **あるよ？** at S0013 and then **興味あるよ / 由真ちゃんにだけ** at S0014. The same vocabulary that rejected friendship now positively names Yuma. This is not apathy toward everybody, and it is not an indirect practice or unnamed-person cover. Hotaru can plainly locate special interest in her present partner.

The statement is narrower than a full relationship contract. It does not specify durable exclusivity, permissible relationships with others, how to disagree, how long to separate, or what to do when trust fails. Nor does it supply a categorical sexual identity. “Only Yuma” describes selective present investment; a jointly negotiated exclusivity agreement would require different evidence. Desire, emotional reliance and commitment overlap without becoming interchangeable.

Yuma's inward **ずっと / 心のどこかでは気付いてた** at S0015 is a later account of earlier partial awareness. S0016 elaborates her current sense that Hotaru reserves this look for her. **RETROSPECTIVE_DISCLOSURE_ABOUT_EARLIER_STATE** here concerns Yuma's later self-understanding. It can strengthen a reading of latent recognition, but does not make her earlier knowledge explicit, eliminate earlier uncertainty, reveal every Hotaru motive, or convert past objections into consent. The mainline's prospective freezes still record what was knowable then.

## Private initiative, desire and the limits of permission evidence

S0013–0018 give Hotaru initiative and Yuma visible/private attraction, culminating in reciprocal closeness. The relevant analytical facts are who initiates, what Yuma expresses, how the scene shifts from surprise to attraction, and what permission conversation is absent. No graphic description is needed. The source's confidence in the attraction should be accepted; an ethical reading need not deny the relation to preserve the distinction between desire and agreement.

Hotaru begins a bodily approach without a shown advance question. There is no explicit new refusal comparable to the mainline's earlier communicated objections. Consequently these pages cannot fairly be classified as a fresh demonstrated refusal violation; they also cannot fairly be called proof of an explicit negotiated intimacy protocol. Receptive affect is evidence of present desire, not comprehensive permission to every possible act. The scene's narrative reward for Hotaru's boldness remains available to the dependency/control counterreading even when the present closeness is accepted as real.

This asymmetric initiative complicates a simple story in which only Yuma has agency or only Hotaru is acted upon. It does not reverse the responsibility for earlier Yuma intrusion, Hotaru pressure or Fujiwara's exposure. The private scene is a new local event, not an ethical verdict on the six volumes.

## The inward wish that the ending leaves unspoken

At S0019 Yuma wishes Hotaru might someday value someone besides her—family or friends—and acknowledges she would feel a little lonely if that happened. That qualification is central. She can imagine Hotaru's welfare beyond exclusive dependence while remaining attracted to being uniquely important. The after-story does not solve one desire by erasing the other.

Hotaru notices something and asks **なに？**. Yuma responds **なんでもない** at S0020. Her smile and present closeness coexist with nondisclosure of a material hope about their future relation. This is ordinary private withholding, not a new public good-friend disguise or proof that every courtesy is a lie. It also prevents a strong claim of fully negotiated care: Hotaru has not heard or agreed to Yuma's wish to broaden her attachments. The outward proposal to try friendship at S0009 and the later inward hope have different epistemic status.

The strongest favorable reading finds a modest restraint: Yuma does not convert that final wish into a demand, and Hotaru need not make friends to retain her. The strongest contrary reading accepts that tenderness and still asks whether the rewarding centrality of “only Yuma” supports a closed dependency. The source gives both the pleasure and Yuma's awareness of its cost. Durable behavioral accommodation must remain open because no later difficult choice tests it.

It does not show an upload action, authenticate a technical channel, count all recipients, or prove that the removed post has no surviving copies. The extra's discussion demonstrates continuing social knowledge, not necessarily a newly circulating file.

S0022 names **葉々** (short-haired) and **砂羽子** (dark-haired), visually matching S0007–0008. Their school-friend role is established in this witness; no new standalone character model follows from one comic. They infer “that kind of relationship” from the photos. This is peer interpretation of exposure, not evidence that the girls deliberately disclosed a complete joint account.

葉々 says relations between girls are individual freedom and that she will not interfere, then objects specifically to **水科蛍** as the partner and asks what Yuma likes beyond her appearance. 砂羽子 answers that she is interfering a great deal. The comic puts a limit on the first friend's self-description through another peer's reply. It supports partial recognition of a girl's relationship alongside personal hostility and inconsistent noninterference. Neither universal queer acceptance nor universal homophobia describes it adequately. Hotaru and Yuma do not hear this private conversation on the page; their knowledge cannot be updated with its literal words.

Takeda never appears, speaks or is named in this witness. Fujiwara is named in the photo account but has no new depicted decision or interiority. Their post-V06 romantic, academic and friendship trajectories remain unobserved. The H/F relationship's formal disposition, Takeda's present feelings and T/F repair or severance cannot be settled by the girls' private continuity.

## Written register and visual argument

Hotaru's **由真ちゃん**, Yuma's intimate **蛍**, and the friends' **水科さん / 水科蛍** separate familiar intimacy from polite inclusion and evaluative third-person talk. Hotaru's **私**, Yuma's **あたし** in inward narration, casual endings and blunt questions continue native written voice; no performance or localization evidence is admitted. The semantic sequence **興味ない → 誰にも興味ない → 由真ちゃんにだけ** does more interpretive work than a generic claim that the girls now say romantic things. S0019's unspoken concern and S0020's outward nonanswer keep interior and audible language visibly distinct.

The early school panels place looking peers around the returned pair. Invitations shift to small embarrassed comic figures; the argument about interest expands Hotaru's reply and holds Yuma's reaction. Private closeness narrows the frame to the dyad and to Yuma's thought. The almost empty wish-space at S0019 makes a future beyond this closed focus imaginable, then S0020 returns to present proximity without communicating it. The separate three-panel extra restores a peer viewpoint and ends on contradiction rather than unanimity. Snowflake cover/separator design binds the short publication, but supplies no new fictional event.

This form extends the mainline's attention to who can see, speak, infer and answer. Interior access gives the reader Yuma's hope before Hotaru has it. The coda gives the reader the friends' positions before the girls hear them. Closeness is therefore legible without equal information or a fully common world.

## Adjudication boundary

**STRENGTHEN:** real reciprocal attachment; direct selective speech; Hotaru's ability to object to a nonemergency independent-choice prescription; childhood-loneliness reading through explicit self-report; persistent exposure consequences and attributed Fujiwara agency. **REVISE:** current school/public/knowledge state and the interpretation of withdrawal paperwork, not what V06 itself established. **PRESERVE:** the distinction between later desire and earlier consent; mainline historical prediction failures; actual return versus future household; truthfulness versus disclosure authority. **DOWNGRADE/REJECT:** an unqualified “nobody matters to Hotaru” or “all friends accept them” reading of this supplement; these are rejected candidate readings, not falsely attributed old canonical claims. **OPEN:** durable trust, full repair, negotiated autonomy in severe conflict, agreed exclusivity, post-school plans, work/money sustainability, housing, technical privacy and both boys' futures.
