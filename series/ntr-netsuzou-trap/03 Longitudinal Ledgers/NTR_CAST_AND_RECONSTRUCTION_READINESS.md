# Cast and reconstruction readiness

**Responsibility:** enroll subjects only when directly identified in an admitted Japanese manga witness, then track whether evidence supports an individual evidence ledger, model, monograph, or relationship specialist. Include repeated decisions, conditional state, written voice, negative evidence, alternative interpretations, mundane behavior, and transfer limits. Exclude public-summary cast prepopulation, assumed personality, A–E grades without eligible reviewed evidence, and automatic enrollment into the global character registry. The [project-local specification](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/NTR_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_SPEC.md) and live global reconstruction spec govern promotion.

**Record schema:** `CAST-#### | source-verified name/identity and VNN | continuity | locators | observed decisions/conditions | evidence coverage and gaps | conflicting observations | evidence-ledger trigger | model trigger and falsifier | monograph/specialist trigger | scenario-specific readiness | cross-series portability limits | linked REL/CON/INF/AGY/JPS/CLM IDs | review state`. A subject's ability to function in a particular story is not proof of a portable conversational or cross-scenario model.

**Locator/update rule:** source keys and verified VNN coordinates only. Append subject enrollment within a completed authorized volume; update readiness through targeted changes and retain the evidence/revision history. Global character curation is a separate authorized process. Coverage: `NO_NARRATIVE_EVIDENCE_INSPECTED_AT_BOOTSTRAP`; cast and evidence rows: **0**; grades: **none**.
