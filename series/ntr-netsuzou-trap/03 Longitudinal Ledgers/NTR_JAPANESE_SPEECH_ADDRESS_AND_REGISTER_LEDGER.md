### JPS-0001 — Yuma and Hotaru: familiarity with different evasions

Yuma commonly あたし in self-narration; Hotaru 私 and 由真ちゃん in familiar address (S0008–0014,0025–0029). Yuma's mock-polite anger 怒ってますよ S0025–0028 and direct やめて contrast Hotaru's ごめんごめん and joking retractions. Recurrence points to public play/private deflection, not dialect or acoustic voice. 予行練習 S0014 relocates purpose; 女の子同士の秘密 S0037 offers categorical shelter. Links CON-0001,REL-0002,CAST-0001/0002; high text, functional inference moderate.
