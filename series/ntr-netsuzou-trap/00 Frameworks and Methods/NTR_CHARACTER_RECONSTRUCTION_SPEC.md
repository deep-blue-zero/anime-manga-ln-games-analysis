# NTR — character reconstruction specification

This is the governing local evidence/validation contract, not a cast dossier. The [readiness register](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/NTR_CAST_AND_RECONSTRUCTION_READINESS.md) now source-enrolls Yuma, Hotaru, Takeda and Fujiwara, each promoted for separate evidence/model/monograph responsibility by the [post-V06 audit](../07%20Audits%20and%20Handoffs/NTR_POST_V06_ARCHITECTURE_ROLE_GAP_AND_INTEGRATION_AUDIT.md). No A–E grade or global capability record exists. Follow [`characters/RECONSTRUCTION_CAPABILITY_SPEC.md`](../../../characters/RECONSTRUCTION_CAPABILITY_SPEC.md) and its maintained schema when a real assessment becomes eligible; do not convert this project-local readiness instrument into the global character registry.

## What a model must explain

Separate a subject's stable dispositions from volume-specific knowledge, obligations, relationship state, pressure, opportunity, and recent injury/repair. Use repeated observed decisions, including decisions under changed conditions; mark unobserved alternatives as hypotheses. Track voiced intention against action; intimacy versus permission; affection versus ethical conduct; fear of loss versus pursuit/withdrawal; knowledge of another's feelings versus justification for acting on it. The model should generate bounded counterfactual predictions with falsifying cases, not a universal personality summary.

Written Japanese self-reference, address, sentence endings, politeness, teasing, indirection, and confrontation may support conditional dialogue constraints where repeated; lettering cannot establish acoustic voice. Visual poses and expressions are evidence with interpretive alternatives, not a direct readout of private motive. Differentiate behavior portable across settings from behavior caused by this specific relationship topology and information distribution.

## Promotion and validation

1. Identify a subject from admitted source evidence and create a dated readiness row with locators. No externally populated four-person roster.
2. Promote an individual evidence ledger only if recurring observations need a separate cumulative retrieval home beyond the ensemble ledgers.
3. Promote a reconstruction model only with repeated decisions, conditional rules, alternatives, failures, temporal specificity, negative evidence where observed, and held-out scene or later-unit validation where possible. A literary monograph has a different integration responsibility and is promoted separately.
4. Test dialogue, mundane social, ethical deliberation, romance/relationship, professional, and cross-scenario readiness against evidence appropriate to each setting. Novel scenarios must declare source-state timestamp, what the subject knows, interlocutor relationship and carried history, whether current commitments persist, whether native leverage/information structures exist, and unfamiliar setting constraints. Where evidence is absent, mark it `NOT_ASSESSED` or an evidence gap; do not infer grades from an effective narrative role.
5. Only the authorized character curation process can enroll a reviewed capability/evidence record in global character outputs. Preserve source/continuity scope and exact evidence references required by the live spec.

Ask during a later audit: which predicted behavior survives altered audience and information, which requires the actual dyad or group, what observation would falsify the rule, and what cannot be reconstructed from six manga volumes without adaptation voice or mundane-setting evidence? A tentative answer remains conditional rather than fictional certainty.

## Completed-mainline local model contract

Each `04 Character Analysis/NTR_<SUBJECT>_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md` binds the complete Japanese manga to six temporal snapshots and selective source-backed decision records. State variables must include available knowledge, relationship-specific expectations, immediate pressure, opportunity, desired outcome and avoidance target. Separate observed action, voiced account, reader-visible interiority and analyst inference. No model may give Yuma the private Hotaru phone statement or complete backstory merely because the reader sees it.

Conditional rules state antecedents, first eligible response, constraints, alternatives, falsifiers and supporting/counterexample locators. Dialogue rules are written-register constraints, not acoustic voice simulation. Mundane/school evidence is used where present; Hotaru's native work behavior does not certify adult career competence, and Fujiwara's utility does not establish ethical deliberation. Portability is qualitative and scenario-specific: dialogue, mundane social, relationship/romance, conflict, ethical deliberation, school/native work and materially unfamiliar scenarios. Use NOT_ASSESSED where appropriate; no global tiers or invented novel-scene successes.

The immutable V01–V05 forecasts and V02–V06 adjudications are the prospective validation set. Preserve H-R03's bounded failure, Y-R04/Y-R05 framing or request-component misses, Fujiwara's conditional-demand misses, changed-information NDO and the limited independence of a continued cliffhanger. Y-R06 is report-mediated bounded support; H-R06 has safety-applicability uncertainty; T-R05 is NDO despite observed nonpunitive recognition; F-R04 uses the later first unrestrained photo-account response, not the restrained opening. H-R04's historical NDO and uncertain later childhood-authority match remain unchanged. New mature rules are POSTCORPUS_UNTESTED, not retrospectively validated.

The separate `07 Audits and Handoffs/NTR_LOCAL_RECONSTRUCTION_VALIDATION_AUDIT.md` reconciles rule identities, scores, failures and readiness across the four models. A literary monograph may interpret motive more broadly with explicit uncertainty; it cannot silently expand a model's information state or prediction authority. The completed corpus supports bounded native decision reconstruction, not unconstrained impersonation or a guaranteed adult future.
