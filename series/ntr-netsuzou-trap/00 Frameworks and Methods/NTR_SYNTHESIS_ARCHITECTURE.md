## Evidence and claims

Stable record IDs use a domain prefix (`REL`, `CON`, `INF`, `AGY`, `VIS`, `JPS`, `CLM`, `CAST`) plus a monotonically assigned number, with `NTR-JP-VNN` and a verified spine/image/page/panel locator. The source key identifies edition and Drive object via the source map; no page coordinates are assumed before V01 packaging work. The deep reading owns local scene context and a compact synopsis. The ledgers own recurring dimensions. A later specialist cites both a locator and the relevant interpreted record/claim; exact Japanese phrasing belongs beside the specific interpretive question rather than as exhaustive transcription. Deterministic locator maps and source images belong in Drive; Git carries interpretation and locator identities.

An earlier prospective observation remains historically readable. Later evidence produces a revision row linked to old and new claim IDs, source boundary, reason, counterevidence, and transition (`PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT`, `OPEN`). Do not rewrite a frozen volume as if later information was known. Track audience and character beliefs separately from source truth and analyst inference. Model contradictions such as attachment plus manipulation without letting affect cancel permission or responsibility.

## Sequential closeout and promotion

A volume is closed only when exact source witness and locators are recorded, the complete volume is read, its deep reading exists, materially affected ledgers and claims are synchronized, predictions are adjudicated where due, source/entrypoint states advance, and the endpoint is frozen and validated. One file alone does not close the transaction. V01–V06 must close in order; their presence does not authorize a continuous run.

Promote a separate character evidence ledger when one verified subject's recurring observations exceed ensemble homes; a reconstruction model when repeated decisions support testable rules, alternatives, and failure conditions; a monograph when development, psychology, relations, speech, visual form, ethics, contradictions, and thematic role require integration; a relationship specialist when a pair or network has its own evidence and explanatory responsibility. These classes are distinct and require evidence. See the [reconstruction specification](NTR_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_SPEC.md). Do not assign names or grades from a public synopsis.

Potential specialist questions include desire/attachment/self-recognition; consent and later reciprocity; infidelity/deception/exposure; asymmetrical knowledge and leverage; exclusivity/jealousy; approach/withdrawal; melodramatic recurrence and visual erotic staging; gender/sexuality/relationship self-conception; mainline ending versus post-ending state; adaptation divergence; and creator/paratext framing. They are **candidate responsibilities**, not mandatory files or predetermined conclusions. After V06, audit which actually warrant independent synthesis.

Character reconstruction is a separate behavioral validation responsibility, ordinarily after sufficient literary evidence, and does not automatically follow a monograph.
4. Admit each verified supplement **after** mainline freeze, recording its confirmation/revision/contradiction and post-ending versus V06 distinctions; keep the anime adaptation separate unless a later scope admits comparison.
5. Reconcile affected specialists, claims, and chronology; then consider a full-series synthesis. Final validation/audit and explicit release freeze are separate from sequential completion, document creation, and branch publication.
