# SPY x FAMILY analytical method

## 1. Purpose and source restraint

This method adapts the [generic manga method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MANGA_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) for Japanese main V01–18 and a separate EYES ONLY fanbook. The [architecture](SXF_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) owns accumulation. No cast, setting, secret, relationship or plot may be imported from familiarity, adaptations, promotional summaries or another work by the creator.

The design keeps several questions independent: what is drawn/said; what each subject knows or believes; what they present to a particular audience; what the reader knows; and what conduct or relationship the evidence supports. A performed role need not be emotionally empty, and an expressed private motive need not exhaust an act's causes. Neither conclusion is presumed before reading.

Record prior exposure before an authorized run and exclude it. Keep chapter/movement disclosure frontiers inside a volume. Numeric source order is a candidate, verified before use; story time, publication order and reader disclosure are different coordinates.

## 2. Main witness and fanbook boundary

A future run requires an OPEN precondition plus explicit bounded authorization; neither exists now. Main keys `SXF-JP-Vnn` and companion key `SXF-EYES-ONLY` bind exact immutable bytes, edition/language and versioned spine/image maps. Distinguish presence, transport, verification, admission, inspection and analytical closure. A digest change creates a new witness until equivalence is shown.

Classify every main/companion component: narrative, illustration, cover, profile/data, editorial text, creator statement/interview, retrospective commentary, bonus, preview or unresolved. “Official” does not collapse source classes. A creator's stated intent is evidence of that statement, not decisive proof of depicted meaning. Data may answer a reference question without being information available to a character or prospective reader.

Fanbook publication and content disclosure need explicit boundaries. It cannot silently settle early identity, chronology, motives, relationships or later outcomes. Any companion comparison is separately scoped and labels main observation versus fanbook assertion, agreement/disagreement and authority limits.

Map actual chapters/images/extras; do not infer chapter numbers arithmetically. Covers, profiles and previews may spoil later material. A verified archive can still contain unreadable, missing or misordered pages.

## 3. Page inspection and locators

Inspect selected manga pages through STILL_IMAGE, including silent or OCR-resistant material. Page states are REVIEWED_MATERIAL, NO_MATERIAL_ADDITION, DUPLICATE, EXCLUDED and UNRESOLVED_READABILITY, with exact extent and exclusion reason. Search/OCR/contact sheets are navigation aids, not a full reading.

Use witness/version + verified chapter + one-based image occurrence including covers + visible printed label if any + panel/balloon ordinal. Bind to immutable digest, spine/member identity and map version. State reading-order conventions and uncertainty. Crops keep coordinates and full-page context; a spread retains both images and pairing basis. Verify facing layout before page-turn claims.

Reopen the actual page for central visual/text claims and exact Japanese quotations. Use EXACT_VERIFIED, WORKING_TRANSCRIPTION or PARAPHRASE, with labeled gloss. A failed route stays RETRIEVAL_UNRESOLVED and cannot support a settled central finding. Public Git contains sanitized keys, locators and brief eligible quotations, not source images, OCR dumps or private IDs.

## 4. Actor-specific knowledge, audience and disclosure

For each consequential proposition, record who knows, believes, suspects, repeats, conceals or lacks it, how the information arrived and what evidence establishes that state. Do not infer ignorance from missing depiction or deception from an incorrect statement alone. A subject may hold a sincere false belief; an analyst may be wrong about what the source implies.

Separate subject knowledge, narrator presentation, reader information and analyst inference. Where roles or self-presentations appear, record the actor, audience, intended impression if supported, actual observed presentation and what each audience member can infer. Do not presume that all people in a scene share knowledge or interpret the same sign alike.

Keep disclosure time distinct from event time and knowledge acquisition. A later revelation adds a version and may alter interpretation, but it cannot retroactively enter an earlier mental state. Preserve source-required anonymity and purposeful role references; later identity links retain the old boundary.

A matrix is not a complete explanation. Develop how information distribution shapes choices, misunderstandings, coordination, tension or comic effects only where pages support those functions. Include serious alternatives: apparent concealment may be omission, role convention, uncertainty or framing.

## 5. Causality, enacted roles and ordinary relationships

Segment by shifts in goal, configuration, location, information or form. Trace condition → perception → appraisal → affect → options/constraints → choice/non-action → consequence. Identify what is shown, asserted and inferred. Temporal succession, a successful outcome or a narrator's explanation does not uniquely settle motive.

Record source-established institutional rules, customs and enforcement separately from participant assumptions. A role creates opportunities and constraints; it does not alone prove sincerity or insincerity. Where daily conduct and declared intentions diverge, retain rival motives and contextual explanations rather than choose a moral label.

Ordinary routines, practical arrangements, low-stakes conversations, waiting, work or domestic activity are first-class evidence when present. They test whether dramatic responses generalize. A relationship can develop through small repeated actions; analysis must preserve their sequence and context rather than only memorable declarations.

Relations are A→B and B→A separately, with knowledge, expectation, access, trust/conflict, obligation, dependence and source-earned dimensions. Distinguish institutional/assumed tie, performed tie, observed practice and inferred affect. These may overlap without being identical. No numeric affection score or title label substitutes for an argument.

Character change causes are DISPOSITION, KNOWLEDGE, RELATIONSHIP, CONTEXT, ROLE, RESOURCE, REVEALED_NOT_NEW or UNRESOLVED. Keep stable tendency separate from temporary condition. A crisis response is not a trait; absence is negative evidence only with a real opportunity. Describe behavior before diagnosis.

## 6. Japanese expression and page form

Attribute speech/thought/narration by tail, placement and context, keeping uncertain attribution open. Preserve short consequential Japanese, address/self-reference, politeness, ellipsis, sentence endings and ruby/base distinctions. A gloss must expose ambiguity over agent, recipient, irony or implication.

Trace appraisal → objective → speech act → recipient/audience filter → register → words/lettering. Compare the same subject across recipients and ordinary/pressured situations. A register change may enact role, strategy, context or development; require controls and competing readings.

Read panel size/order, gutters, framing, reaction timing, silence, backgrounds, gaze, page turns and spread relationships. Explain a feature, its supported effect and an alternative. Comic, dramatic, affectionate or threatening tone requires page evidence; it is not inherited from genre familiarity. Rendering shifts are framing conventions unless the text supports literal consequences.

Sound effects are graphic Japanese sound/state/manner expressions with placement and scale. Lettering does not establish acoustic delivery; still panels do not show exact omitted motion. No anime music, timing or acting supplies the absent channel. Repeated compositions or verbal patterns require variation and counterexamples before thematic promotion.

## 7. Counterreading, values and revision

Classify claims as OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, CONTRADICTION, EXTERNAL_REFERENCE, EVALUATION or UNRESOLVED, with confidence and reason. Depiction, explanation, evaluation and endorsement remain distinct. Do not prefilter difficult material or treat later affection as proof of earlier consent or motive.

Every unit thesis has a serious rival and a disconfirmation condition. Preserve the most consequential causal, relational, linguistic and formal counterexamples. Contradictions may arise from source/witness, chronology, knowledge asymmetry, role/audience, report reliability, rendering convention, missing evidence or real inconsistency; distinguish before harmonizing.

Use PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT and OPEN, retaining earlier/new formulations, boundary, evidence, rivals and affected outputs. Frozen prospective readings and predictions are not rewritten. Main/fanbook disagreement remains explicitly source-layered.

## 8. Complete future output and acceptance

A future full reading contains receipt/component coverage; entering knowledge/role/relationship state and frozen expectations; developed thesis/rival; movement-by-movement causal/page analysis; subject and directed relationship changes; ordinary-life controls; source-earned institutional questions; Japanese/graphic voice; form/recurrence and counterexamples; claim transitions; five-register manifest; exit freeze; limits and completion decision.

Do not compress ordinary scenes into “bonding,” audience-dependent language into a catchphrase, or information asymmetry into a single secret column. Full reasoning must explain how pages, decisions, roles and relationships interact, including alternatives. No word ceiling or summary table replaces that duty.

Before semantic acceptance, promotion or integration, sample introduction, a substantial middle section, conclusion/exit, all major relationships and ambiguous actor passages. Check names, purposeful roles, pronouns, concealment, speaker/thinker/recipient and knowledge frontier. Classify JUSTIFIED_ROLE_REFERENCE, SOURCE_REQUIRED_ANONYMITY, KNOWLEDGE_BOUNDARY_REQUIRED, UNNECESSARY_GENERICIZATION, AMBIGUOUS_REFERENT or INCORRECT_IDENTITY_OR_ATTRIBUTION. Record scope/findings and repair supported defects only.

Accept the full reading and synchronized five registers atomically. Scoped no-change statements document actual inspection. Required unreadable pages, broken central locators or missing modality/update work remain holds.

### Current-unit story synopsis (initiation policy §5.3)

Each authorized plot-bearing volume reading must include a clearly labeled **Current-unit story synopsis** near the front of its canonical artifact, after receipt/component coverage and entering context, before the developed thesis/rival, extended interpretation and ledger inventories. Identify inherited developments briefly as inherited, then recount the current unit's new events and disclosures.

Write coherent, accessible prose tracing the opening situation, source-established participants and stakes, consequential actions and decisions, turning points/disclosures and the ending state. Preserve meaningful physical and scene mechanics and who acts toward whom, including ordinary practical conduct where consequential. Follow verified chapters, causal movements and panel presentation; distinguish succession, simultaneous viewpoints and omitted or inferred motion. Use supported names or purposeful role references while preserving source-required anonymity. A role, claimed relationship or label such as bonding cannot replace the actual sequence of events. Detail follows narrative density, without a fixed length or compulsory chapter template.

Use only the current admitted unit and explicitly marked inherited context. Separate depicted acts from speech/thought, narrator presentation, testimony, audience-directed presentation, conjecture, contested attribution and unresolved outcomes. A performed or claimed role does not establish motive, sincerity, deception or every audience member's knowledge. A unit-end disclosure may be recounted as new reader information but must not be assigned to an earlier actor or earlier prospective reading; newly disclosed earlier events are not new story-time events. Material events need retrievable witness/chapter/image/panel locators under §3, or stable evidence references resolving to them, while the account remains readable prose. Unadmitted EYES ONLY facts, later volumes, adaptations and creator familiarity cannot supply identities, motives or outcomes.

Compose or finalize this unit-end, spoiler-bearing orientation only after applicable prospective captures are frozen, and shield analysts assigned an earlier chapter, movement or volume horizon from the full spoiler-bearing reading. It is neither a prospective freeze nor independent evidence. Full causal, knowledge/role/audience, directed-relationship, ordinary-life, Japanese, page-form and counterreading arguments, five-register transaction and exit freeze remain required. A separately admitted narrative fanbook component receives the same bounded duty at its actual unit; only a genuinely non-narrative component/reference pass may record inapplicability with a reason, without exempting unclassified narrative content.

## 9. Reconstruction and transfer

The architecture separates literary monographs from operational models and independent tests. Policies need trigger/stakes/state, likely response family, inhibitors, exceptions, counterevidence, rivals and falsifiers. Distinguish disposition/temporary condition; trace appraisal → decision → audience-conditioned expression. Confidence, evidence distance and abstention/multiple-response rules bound projection.

Freeze genuinely held-out predictions before source exposure; exposed tests are RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST. Independent evaluators own outcomes/readiness; synthetic output never becomes primary evidence or canonical characterization. Exclude unadmitted fanbook facts from a main-lane model.

Preserve complete readings, state histories, counterarguments and source boundaries under the [handoff contract](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md) and [archive policy](../../governance/source-policies/ARCHIVE_AUTHORITY_AND_SUPERSESSION_POLICY.md). A fresh synthesis session receives full outputs, not a summary standing in for them.
