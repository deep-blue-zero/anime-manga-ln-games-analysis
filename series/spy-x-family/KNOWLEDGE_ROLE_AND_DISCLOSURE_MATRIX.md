# Knowledge, role and disclosure matrix

## Responsibility

Keep actor-specific information, audience-directed presentation, reader disclosure and causal choice separable. No subjects, secrets, roles, institutions or events are established at INITIAL.

## Operational schemas

| Register / key | Required fields |
|---|---|
| Proposition/knowledge `SXF-K-nnnn` | Source-established subject/narrator/reader position; proposition; known/believed/suspected/reported/unknown/withheld state; acquisition evidence/time; witness/frontier; who lacks it; uncertainty and alternatives |
| Role/audience `SXF-ROLE-nnnn` | Actor; audience; source-established role/claimed identity; observed presentation; stated versus inferred objective; audience's available evidence and response; private/public distinction only if supported; full argument |
| Disclosure/time `SXF-T-nnnn` | Event or proposition; main/fanbook lane; page disclosure; story-time constraint; subject acquisition; analyst admission; order/simultaneity alternatives; exact routes |
| Causal decision `SXF-A-nnnn` | Conditions; perception/appraisal; affect; options/resources/inhibitors; choice/non-action; consequences; information-dependent links; rival cause; evidence; full claim |
| Institutional condition `SXF-I-nnnn` | Source-established institution/role; asserted rule/speaker; custom versus observed enforcement; access/resources; exceptions; subject constraints; evidence and limits |

Common state is lane/witness/version + frontier + disclosure/story time + actor/proposition + audience/role + condition/resources + directed relationship. Modes: PROSPECTIVE_AT_BOUNDARY / RETROSPECTIVE_WITHIN_ADMITTED_BOUNDARY / FANBOOK_COMPARATIVE / UNRESOLVED.

Do not fill knowledge from reader omniscience. A wrong assertion can be sincere error; lack of an assertion is not proof of ignorance; a presented role does not alone establish motive or falsity. Keep actual behavior and its interpretation distinct. A later identity disclosure adds a relation without rewriting the earlier naming frontier.

## Update and analytical obligations

Develop the causal and epistemic argument in full reading prose before indexing it. Explain how information and audience differences matter; a “secret” label is not analysis. Preserve alternative causes, ordinary contexts and what the source withholds.

At every accepted reading append proposition/state/presentation changes and scoped no-change findings. Retain former beliefs, audience assumptions and disclosure positions. A later revelation may revise analyst interpretation without altering the earlier subject state.

Checkpoint review tests whether any actor has been given facts only the narrator, reader or fanbook supplies. Check audience asymmetries and institution assertions. Material changes propagate to directed ties, written voice, claims and future models. One sequential owner mutates this surface.
