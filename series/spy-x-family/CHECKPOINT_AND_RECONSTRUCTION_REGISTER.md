## Model responsibility and evidence firewall

A future operational model contains conditional trigger/stakes/state → response-family rules, inhibitors/overrides, exceptions, rivals/falsifiers and exact evidence. Distinguish disposition from temporary condition; trace appraisal → decision → recipient/audience-conditioned expression. Record confidence, distance from observed situations and abstention or multiple plausible responses when support is weak. Filter reader knowledge to what the modeled subject knows.

Models, monographs and independent evaluations are different outputs. Genuine held-out predictions freeze before source exposure; exposed exercises are RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST. Independent assessors compare diagnostic outcomes against baselines/controls with opportunities and confounds explicit. Synthetic output cannot enter primary evidence or canonical characterization. Global discovery/readiness registries remain with designated agents.

## Closeout and handoff

Accept complete readings and synchronized registers atomically. Review coverage, retrieval, actor/reader/audience boundaries, directed ties, ordinary behavior, full page/Japanese reasoning and the bounded name/reference check. A missing update or unread page is not no-change evidence.

The authorized run sets actual cadence; the four-volume proposal in architecture is not authorization or a story division. Checkpoints preserve complete earlier prose and state histories. Source-complete does not bypass role-gap or convergence review.

Follow the [handoff contract](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md): transfer full files, compare base/candidate/current, retrieve consequential evidence and inspect for lost routine, causal, linguistic, role/knowledge and counterreading detail. One mutable owner maintains this surface.
