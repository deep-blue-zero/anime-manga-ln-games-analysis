# Sound! Euphonium V2 — Supplemental Source Audit

## 1. Purpose

This audit prevents the V2 novel project from confusing three different questions:

1. **What belongs to the locked fourteen-book prose core?**
2. **What official Takeda-authored prose exists outside that core?**
3. **What official Kyoto Animation/adaptation material is useful but cannot govern novel continuity?**

The objective is not to acquire everything before Volume 1. The objective is to make every known source class explicit so later material can be admitted without silently changing the evidentiary boundary.

---

## 3. 2023 *Ensemble Contest* theatrical short stories

Official source:
`https://ensemble.anime-eupho.com/novelty/novel2/`

The film's official site identifies three **原作・武田綾乃 書き下ろし短編小説**, each distributed as an A6 12-page theatrical booklet:

1. **「休日、愛らしい友人と」** — 久美子＆麗奈
2. **「贈り物に愛を込めて」** — 葉月＆緑輝＆つばめ
3. **「偶発的再会と他愛ない会話について」** — 夏紀＆奏

### Corpus check

Exact-title checks against the locked `HIBIKE-V14` EPUB found none of the three titles. They are therefore **not silently absorbed by `みんなの話`** in the current digital edition.

### Classification

`official_author_written_adaptation_adjacent_supplemental_prose`

These are written by Takeda but distributed specifically as film-adjacent bonuses. Until continuity is individually audited, they must not be collapsed into the same authority tier as the locked book corpus.

### Analytical value

High, especially for character modeling:

- Kumiko/Reina low-stakes dyadic behavior;
- Hazuki/Midori/Tsubame ordinary social interaction;
- Natsuki/Kanade relationship-conditioned voice and teasing/conflict behavior.

### Disposition

**DEFERRED — desirable acquisition; not a Phase-1 blocker.**

If acquired, they require an explicit source-lock revision before being used as admitted supplemental evidence.

## 10. Admission policy after Phase 0

The initial `HIBIKE_SOURCE_LOCK.md` is treated as a frozen Phase-1 boundary.

A newly acquired supplemental item must not silently appear in evidence ledgers. Admission requires:

1. provenance and integrity audit;
2. exact title/source identification;
3. continuity/evidence-class decision;
4. duplication/republication check;
5. a superseding versioned source-lock artifact;
6. update to `CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md`.

Until then, the item remains `deferred`, `open`, or `adaptation_only`.

The core fourteen-book prose corpus is sufficient to begin the sequential V2 reread. The known supplemental gaps are explicit rather than invisible, and later acquisition can be incorporated through controlled source-lock revision.
