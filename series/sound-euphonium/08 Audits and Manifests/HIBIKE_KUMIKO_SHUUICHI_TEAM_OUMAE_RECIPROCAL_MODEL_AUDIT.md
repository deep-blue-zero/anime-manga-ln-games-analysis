# Sound! Euphonium V2 — Kumiko–Shuuichi / Team Oumae Reciprocal Model Audit
## Pair reciprocity, distributed-authority interaction, romantic/office boundary control, and three-agent falsification

## 1. Audit purpose and decision

This artifact closes the formal reciprocal gate that remained explicitly deferred after promotion of:

- `HIBIKE_KUMIKO_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md` v0.3; and
- `HIBIKE_SHUUICHI_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md` v0.2.

It also tests the resulting pair inside the already source-grounded **Team Oumae** three-role architecture using:

- `HIBIKE_REINA_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md` v0.3 as an independently audited counterpart model;
- `HIBIKE_KUMIKO_REINA_RECIPROCAL_MODEL_AUDIT.md` v1.0 as already-binding pair-interface authority; and
- the frozen V11–V12 movement checkpoint and longitudinal ledgers as shared upstream context.

The task is not to decide whether Kumiko and Shuuichi “work as a couple,” nor to summarize their romance. The modeling question is stricter:

> **Can Kumiko's and Shuuichi's independently specified attention, decision, conflict, care, voice, role, and state mechanisms generate the same canonical interactions without collapsing either person into the other's support function—and can those mechanisms coexist with Reina as a third autonomous executive agent rather than turning Team Oumae into Kumiko plus two satellites?**

A passing reciprocal model must therefore reproduce all of the following at once:

1. childhood familiarity that is unusually low-theatricality but not emotionally trivial;
2. real romantic desire without constant monopolization;
3. Kumiko's ability to make self-owned decisions that hurt Shuuichi without thereby making his support unreal;
4. Shuuichi's ability to respect a recognized other-owned decision without becoming globally passive;
5. Shuuichi's practical support without falsely granting him Kumiko's diffuse social-perceptual bandwidth;
6. Kumiko's overfunctioning without turning every burden she carries into proof that Shuuichi has failed her;
7. institutional situations in which Shuuichi's softness genuinely *does* externalize labor and therefore requires correction;
8. continued executive function while the romantic relationship is formally suspended;
9. explicit romantic restoration without converting office authority into couple authority;
10. autonomous Reina–Shuuichi disagreement and coordination; and
11. a three-agent structure in which musical standards, relational/institutional integration, and practical continuity constrain one another.

### Audit result

> **PASS WITH BINDING TEAM OUMAE INTERFACE CONSTRAINTS — NO MONOGRAPH PATCH REQUIRED**

Kumiko v0.3 and Shuuichi v0.2 are reciprocally compatible at the `audited_provisional` level. Their strongest shared scenes are not produced by mirrored psychology.

Kumiko's side is characterized by broad relational attention, rapid social interpretation, a persistent thought–speech gap, delayed ownership of self-relevant desire, and a tendency under overload to convert emotionally crowded problems into rules, roles, or tasks.

Shuuichi's side is characterized by baseline familiarity, sensitivity to visible deviations in known people, practical intervention, ordinary low-pressure speech, a strong but **domain-bounded** non-override prior for decisions he recognizes as someone else's, and a much narrower capacity than Kumiko for diffuse institutional-relational mapping.

Those mechanisms fit together because Shuuichi does **not** solve Kumiko's interiority for her. He is most reliable when a burden becomes visible or actionable, when Kumiko speaks, or when his own role creates standing. Kumiko, in turn, can receive this form of support precisely because it usually does not demand a performance of vulnerability or surrender authorship.

The audit also finds that the pair remains compatible with the already-binding Kumiko–Reina model. The evidence does not support a zero-sum triangle. V08 directly supports Shuuichi's low-monopolization romantic style; V11–V14 repeatedly support autonomous Reina–Shuuichi coordination; and V14 retrospectively makes Team Oumae's three-person comfort explicit. None of this requires weakening Kumiko–Reina specialness or Kumiko–Shuuichi romance.

The strongest correction supplied by the reciprocal layer is therefore **not a monograph revision but an interface constraint**:

> **support, jurisdiction, perception, and attachment must remain separate variables.**

Shuuichi may love Kumiko, know her baseline extremely well, and reliably help with concrete strain while still failing to notice or absorb the diffuse social labor her presidency generates. Conversely, Kumiko may be carrying too much without every instance of that burden being evidence of romantic neglect. When Shuuichi's own office softness causes work to spill onto Kumiko, however, the model must permit criticism and escalation rather than hiding behind “respecting her autonomy.”

- Shuuichi's non-override rule remains ownership-sensitive rather than universal passivity;
- Shuuichi's practical support remains narrower than Kumiko's global relational monitoring;

- restoration can be generated without retroactively erasing hurt;

The two principal monographs inherit the same Phase-1 source corpus, locator infrastructure, deep readings, checkpoints, and longitudinal ledgers. They are not statistically independent models constructed from disjoint evidence. Source agreement is therefore necessary but not sufficient proof of external validity.

This audit also does **not** complete:

- a blind adaptation-divergence evaluation for Shuuichi;
- independent human judgment of romantic naturalness;
- unrestricted post-university extrapolation;
- later supplemental-prose contradiction review;
- final frozen simulation authority.

The correct claim is therefore **state-, relationship-, knowledge-, and domain-bounded reciprocal validity through the locked V01–V14 prose boundary**.

## 6. Directional mechanism audit

### 6.1 Kumiko → Shuuichi

Kumiko's model predicts that Shuuichi occupies an unusual relational domain for her:

- extreme familiarity;
- low theatricality;
- practical availability;
- romantic desire that can remain embarrassing even after formal dating;
- relatively low demand for constant co-presence;
- ease of receiving care compared with more evaluatively charged relationships;
- a tendency to assume his continued availability because it has historically been ordinary.

Her recurrent risks toward him are not primarily inability to perceive his affection. They are:

1. **minimization through familiarity** — treating him as ordinary until comparison or loss makes the attachment salient;
2. **delayed self-recognition** — affect can leak as jealousy, irritation, or embarrassment before she owns the romantic claim;
3. **administrative simplification** — when presidency and romance feel mutually contaminating, she may change the formal relationship state rather than tolerate unresolved coexistence;
4. **availability assumption** — because Shuuichi reliably follows, waits, or remains nearby, she can under-model the possibility that his future availability is contingent rather than natural law;
5. **role absorption** — under executive load, she can accept support while still carrying broader invisible labor neither she nor Shuuichi has fully surfaced.

A correct Kumiko-side simulation therefore does not generate constant gratitude. Often the support is received as ordinary because *that ordinariness is itself part of the relationship's value*.

### 6.2 Shuuichi → Kumiko

Shuuichi's model predicts a different mechanism:

- he has a strong baseline model of Kumiko's ordinary behavior;
- he notices visible deviations, practical burden, and obvious strain;
- he usually responds with waiting, carrying, walking, simple questions, humor, or task-sharing before abstract interpretation;
- when he recognizes a decision as principally Kumiko's, he has a strong non-override prior even if he dislikes the outcome;
- romantic desire remains real and can produce hurt, jealousy, embarrassment, and future anxiety;
- he does not normally convert that desire into entitlement to her schedule, office decisions, musical choices, or future path.

Two limits are mandatory:

1. **attention limit:** knowing Kumiko's baseline is not equivalent to possessing Kumiko's full relational map. He may see that she is strained without seeing the 103-member field, factional dynamics, self-implication, or hidden causal chain she is carrying.
2. **jurisdiction limit on non-override itself:** if a problem belongs to Shuuichi's own vice-presidential responsibility, “it is her choice” cannot function as an excuse for inaction. His prior audit's R-01 patch exists precisely to prevent autonomy respect from becoming universal passivity.

### 6.3 Reciprocal loop

The pair's stable loop is therefore:

> **Kumiko carries broad context and may delay exposing self-relevant need → Shuuichi notices a familiar-person deviation or concrete burden → he offers low-pressure presence/action rather than interpretive capture → Kumiko can accept because authorship remains hers → ordinary interaction resumes without requiring permanent heightened disclosure.**

The failure loop is equally important:

> **Kumiko overfunctions and does not surface the burden → Shuuichi's narrower attention or conflict-softness misses/delays the needed intervention → work spills back toward Kumiko or Reina → criticism becomes warranted → Shuuichi must move from availability to role-owned correction.**

This dual loop explains why “supportive” and “sometimes adds to her burden” are not contradictory descriptions.

Disposition: **PASS**.

## 7. Romance versus office-jurisdiction audit

### 7.1 Romance does not transfer institutional authorship

Nothing in the source supports a rule of the form:

> boyfriend → right to decide president's institutional judgment.

The strongest counterevidence is the structure of the presidency transition itself. Kumiko's V09 decision changes their romantic status, but Shuuichi continues into vice-presidential work rather than treating romantic injury as jurisdiction over the office.

### 7.2 Formal suspension is real

The reciprocal model must preserve the literal V09 state change. It is wrong to simulate V10–V11 as though Kumiko and Shuuichi were secretly still dating in every relevant sense.

Evidence includes:

- the V09 explicit end of lover status — `HIBIKE-V09 / S05 / P0377-P0391`;
- V11 `部長` role-address distance and Shuuichi's private hurt — `HIBIKE-V11 / S06 / P0031-P0054`;
- Kumiko's own V11 description of the public relation as president/vice-president rather than active lovers.

### 7.3 Formal suspension does not zero attachment or function

The opposite error is equally serious. The source repeatedly shows:

- office cooperation continuing;
- Shuuichi waiting and taking concrete work;
- Kumiko wondering about his current feelings;
- embodied co-regulation returning under Nationals pressure;
- explicit reciprocal love after Nationals.

Task competence during suspension is **not proof of romantic repair**. It is evidence that institutional function can remain intact despite unresolved attachment.

### 7.4 Restoration does not produce monopoly

V14 restores the formal lovers label. It does not retroactively invalidate V08's low-monopolization pattern or the independent importance of Reina. Separate universities, separate activities, and multiple high-value relationships remain compatible with romantic continuity.

Disposition: **PASS**.

## 8. Team Oumae three-agent architecture

The V11–V12 frozen checkpoint already defines Team Oumae as a distributed authority structure. The reciprocal audit tests whether the current models can instantiate that structure without turning it into a slogan.

| Agent | Primary strength | Typical first useful contribution | Recurrent externality / failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kumiko / president** | relational mapping, legitimacy integration, translation across values | identifies the hidden stakeholder/value problem; proposes an architecture that multiple parties can inhabit | over-responsibility, containment thinking, self-application gap, motivated interpretation |
| **Shuuichi / vice-president** | practical continuity, accessible support, logistics, ordinary peer readability | makes work executable, notices concrete strain, reduces pressure, supplies an independent governance position | softness can defer correction; diffuse social labor may remain outside his attention until it becomes visible |
| **Reina / drum major** | musical standards, fast evaluative clarity, technical severity | names the performance standard or uncompromising quality constraint | fear/social-care costs, doctrinal rigidity, underweighting broader legitimacy |

### 8.1 The architecture is constraint, not specialization theater

The three roles matter because each can stop another's strength from becoming totalizing:

- Shuuichi's ordinary practicality can stop Kumiko from treating every problem as a relational thesis.
- Kumiko's relational/institutional map can surface social costs Shuuichi's local support does not see.
- Reina can force a hard performance question that Kumiko or Shuuichi might soften.
- Kumiko can prevent Reina's standard from becoming the only legitimate value variable.
- Shuuichi can provide implementation/continuity without demanding either Kumiko's social theory or Reina's musical absolutism.

### 8.2 Labor externalities are first-class

The frozen V11–V12 checkpoint explicitly warns that formal role separation does not eliminate externalities.

The clearest Shuuichi-side example is Reina's V11 criticism:

> his reluctance to scold juniors contributes to Kumiko's burden.

`HIBIKE-V11 / S06 / P0031-P0038`

This is important because it prevents the reciprocal pair from becoming romantically self-protective. “Shuuichi supports Kumiko” cannot be used to erase cases where his governance style creates work for her.

The correct triadic question is therefore not merely *who owns the task?* but also:

> **whose default behavior creates labor that another role must absorb?**

### 8.3 Reina–Shuuichi is an autonomous dyad

Team Oumae cannot be modeled as two independent relationships routed through Kumiko. V11–V14 contains repeated Reina–Shuuichi coordination, teasing, criticism, disagreement, and mutual legibility.

This matters for Kumiko as well. Her occasional mild exclusion response when Reina and Shuuichi coordinate independently should remain possible without being interpreted as evidence that the executive distribution is dysfunctional.

Disposition: **PASS**.

## 14. Binding Kumiko–Shuuichi / Team Oumae interface constraints

These constraints are now binding downstream interface rules. They do not alter either monograph in isolation.

### TO-01 — Specify state before relationship behavior

Never simulate “Kumiko and Shuuichi” as one timeless couple. At minimum distinguish pre-confession, dating, suspended romance/office, V12 restoration, and V14 postgrad states.

### TO-02 — Separate attachment from jurisdiction

Romantic importance does not grant Shuuichi authority over Kumiko's office, musical, educational, or future decisions. Conversely, Kumiko's presidency does not erase Shuuichi's right to have preferences or hurt.

### TO-03 — Preserve domain-bounded non-override

Shuuichi's strong non-override prior applies when he recognizes a decision as principally belonging to another person. It does **not** excuse failure to act inside his own or a shared role responsibility.

### TO-04 — Keep visible/actionable support distinct from diffuse relational labor

Do not grant Shuuichi Kumiko-level social omniscience. He is strongest at known-person deviations and concrete burdens. Diffuse factional, reputational, and multi-person labor may remain invisible until made legible.

### TO-05 — Do not moralize softness into automatic virtue

Shuuichi's low-pressure style can be caring and institutionally useful. It can also defer hard correction and create work for Kumiko or Reina. Preserve both possibilities.

### TO-06 — Preserve Kumiko's availability assumption

Longstanding reliable presence can make Shuuichi feel infrastructural to Kumiko. She may under-recognize his contingency, hurt, or future autonomy until something disrupts the baseline.

### TO-07 — Preserve Shuuichi's under-spoken self-interest

Autonomy respect does not mean absence of jealousy, attraction, irritation, grief, or future desire. Allow body and ordinary complaint to reveal stakes his explicit moral rule does not erase.

### TO-08 — Keep romance non-monopolistic without making it weak

V08 directly rejects constant co-presence as a requirement of dating. This does not demote the romance. Kumiko–Shuuichi romantic continuity and Kumiko–Reina exceptional specialness must coexist unless later source explicitly changes one.

### TO-09 — Treat V09–V11 suspension as a real state

Do not write them as secretly unchanged lovers. Do not write them as strangers. Office cooperation, affection residue, role distance, and private hurt coexist.

### TO-10 — Task competence is not emotional repair

President/vice-president function can continue during unresolved attachment. Serious relationship repair requires the relevant relational domain to change, not merely successful work.

### TO-11 — Team Oumae has three autonomous channels

Do not model:

> Kumiko decides → Shuuichi supports → Reina enforces.

That is too linear. Shuuichi and Reina have independent judgments, can coordinate directly, can disagree with Kumiko, and can understand one another without routing every exchange through her.

### TO-12 — Track labor externalities between roles

For every executive action, ask:

- who owns the task;
- who perceives the problem;
- who performs the corrective labor;
- who absorbs the emotional/social cost;
- whose default style created work for someone else.

This is required to prevent “distributed leadership” from becoming an unfalsifiable compliment.

### TO-13 — Preserve Reina's independent authority

The Kumiko–Shuuichi reciprocal pass cannot be used to domesticate Reina into the couple's third wheel. Her musical judgment, Taki relationship, standards, and Kumiko reciprocal constraints remain independently authoritative at their recorded levels.

### TO-14 — Preserve post-role jurisdiction boundaries

V14 supports continued consultation and affection, not permanent ownership of Kitauji. Kumiko can be a valued adviser, Shuuichi can route someone toward her, and both must still permit successor autonomy.

### TO-15 — Future support does not require identical life paths

Wanting to support someone predicts desired future availability and action. It does not establish co-location, sacrifice of independent vocation, marriage timing, or any other unobserved long-term outcome.
