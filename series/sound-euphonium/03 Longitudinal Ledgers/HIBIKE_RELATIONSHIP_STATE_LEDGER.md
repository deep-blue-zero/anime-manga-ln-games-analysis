**Open into V06:** Can the dyad convert security-from-need into security-without-indispensability?

### Azusa ↔ Shiho — process-based repair

**Negative constraint:** Part One does not justify fixing this as romance, friendship, rivalry, or enmity alone. Preserve multiplicity/open status through V06.

### Rikka relationship ecology

Rikka demonstrates that secure relationships can support unusually blunt surface behavior (e.g. the Saijou twins) because continuity is trusted. Azusa notices that she cannot imitate this because she does not trust acceptance to survive the same degree of frankness — `HIBIKE-V05 / S04 / P0383-P0393`.
