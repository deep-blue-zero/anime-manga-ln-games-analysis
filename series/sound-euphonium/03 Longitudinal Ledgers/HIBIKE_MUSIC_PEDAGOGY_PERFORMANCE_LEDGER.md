## V01 causal model

`declared goal → technical diagnosis → repeated embodied practice → audible improvement → increased self-efficacy → stronger desire → higher emotional stakes`

This chain is central to the novel's literary architecture and should be tested in every later volume rather than treating rehearsal as plot-neutral realism.

## HIBIKE-V05 music/pedagogy expansion — Rikka marching system

### Marching as compound embodied craft

Rikka requires simultaneous instrumental execution, memorization, step-size control, body alignment, spatial awareness, visual precision, stamina and ensemble timing. The novel therefore broadens the corpus's concept of musicianship beyond seated/concert execution.

### Distributed pedagogy

- Beginner instruction is substantially student-delivered; section leaders manage training schedules — `HIBIKE-V05 / S02 / P0366-P0369`.
- Student marching-design teams create formations before external specialist critique — `S02 / P0645-P0666`.
- Anna can teach weaker movers effectively partly because she remembers what not being able to do the skill felt like: **performance expertise and pedagogical expertise are not identical**.

### Amika's trumpet technique

Amika's improvement is represented through isolatable technique—mouthpiece/tonguing work, syllable articulation, slow repetition, concrete correction—rather than vague inspiration. Her later successful participation in `Sing` becomes embodied evidence that she can change — `HIBIKE-V05 / S03 / P0599-P0606`.

### Merit and replaceability

Rikka uses multiple performance authorities: Kumada's A-division audition, senior-led marching evaluation, section-level observation, and outside marching feedback. “Best” is task-relative; one rank does not answer every performance question.

### Intrinsic love remains present inside elite competition

Azusa enjoys fundamentals, repertoire, high-level contest work and the post-performance state. Rikka's pressure does not prove the students are merely extrinsically motivated. The first-year `Sing` experience also yields an explicit connection account: `音楽は楽しい。言葉がなくたって、つながっているような感じがする`.

### Repeatability/transmission extension

V01–V03 established that elite craft depends on repeatable performance and transmissible systems. V05 shows the organizational substrate of that repeatability: student teachers, scheduled correction, leadership apprenticeship, bodily standardization and role-specific selection.
