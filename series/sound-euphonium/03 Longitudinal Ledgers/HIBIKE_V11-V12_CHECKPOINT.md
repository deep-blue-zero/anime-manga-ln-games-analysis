## 2. Final-movement movement freeze

The canonical V11-V12 movement is:

> **role-entangled legitimacy -> repeated-evaluation precarity -> painful defensible outcome -> social personalization of procedure -> epistemic and relational rupture -> direct inquiry -> first-person institutional authorship -> plural trust -> shared performance -> transmission**

V11 makes Kumiko a participant in the same merit procedure she must defend. V12 makes that conflict real by selecting Mayu for the Kansai soli and then refusing to undermine the selection musically. The Mayu/Reina pair is excellent. Kumiko's pain therefore cannot be solved by discovering a hidden injustice. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0436-P0441`; `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0512-P0516`.

The final movement's core institutional principle is consequently:

> **A legitimate process may hurt. Its ethical task is not to eliminate all pain but to preserve agency, contestability, intelligibility, and ownership of effort and regret.**

Kumiko's pre-final-audition speech is the decisive formulation. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0792-P0818`.

## 3. Legitimacy model after V12

The V08-V09 four-layer model remains valid:

1. **procedural legitimacy** - are the stated rules fair enough?
2. **epistemic legitimacy** - do participants believe the judgment is competent and intelligible?
3. **social legitimacy** - can the result be lived with without informal retaliation or factional distortion?
4. **adaptive legitimacy** - can the institution revise itself when inherited arrangements no longer fit?

V11-V12 add two further controls that should not be collapsed into a fifth scalar layer.

### Contestability

Authority must be question-able without the question itself becoming disloyalty. Kumiko's direct conversation with Taki is the canonical positive case. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0958-P0983`.

### Participant-authored stake

A fair rule cannot decide what each person should value. Mayu may value shared time more than a soli. Kumiko may value the opportunity to risk failure. The institution can require a mode of participation, but it should not fabricate one hidden motive for everybody. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0350-P0394`; `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0792-P0818`.

## 4. Kumiko final-movement state

### V11

- president, mediator, competitor, and desired musical partner occupy the same person;
- she sincerely endorses merit while wanting the favorable result;
- she begins to doubt whether Taki's favorable judgment is musical or socially protective;
- her ordinary epistemic discipline degrades when her own worth is implicated.

### V12

- the feared unfavorable result occurs;
- she enforces the rule even while hurt by it;
- she becomes suspicious of Taki and the system;
- Reina's effort doctrine and Taki trust produce a major rupture;
- Kumiko finally questions Taki directly;
- external perspective from Asuka/Kaori humanizes rather than sanctifies Taki;
- Kumiko converts neutral-mediator posture into bounded first-person institutional authorship;
- she chooses teaching as an emergent future rather than a discovered destiny.

### Frozen predictive rule

`KUMIKO@V12` is especially likely to overinterpret ambiguous evidence when **self-worth + fairness + a valued relationship** are simultaneously at stake. Her recovery route is not generic reassurance. It is:

> **direct inquiry -> external perspective -> recognition of personal stake -> explicit first-person position -> action**.

Do not simulate her as an emotionally neutral proceduralist.

## 5. Mayu final-movement state

Mayu is now one of the corpus' clearest tests of whether the model can respect a value system different from the protagonist's.

Stable V11-V12 findings:

- repeated transfers produced strong adaptation competence;
- she can enjoy high-level performance without making result hierarchy her primary value;
- she wants shared time, friendship, and memory-making;
- she often reduces her own claim when competence threatens group harmony;
- withdrawal can pressure others even when manipulative intent is absent;
- she accepts full participation when Kitauji explicitly chooses that norm;
- winning a role does not convert her into a triumphalist rival;
- entering the group photograph marks visible co-belonging without proving total psychological transformation.

The source directly supports her unusual discomfort with being photographed, but not one settled cause. Keep that **OPEN**.

### Frozen predictive rule

When a future scenario places Mayu between personal distinction and relational harmony, default to **harm reduction by self-limitation** unless another value or explicit group norm makes withdrawal illegitimate. Do not automatically generate hidden competitive resentment.

## 6. Reina final-movement state

Reina remains a high-standard musician whose public musical register is low-hedge and result-oriented. V12 does not invalidate that state. It reveals the cost of turning a useful standard into a universal explanation.

The major rupture with Kumiko shows that Reina can become morally severe when Taki's legitimacy and effort doctrine are challenged. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0857-P0896`.

The Mayu soli demonstrates professional differentiation: Reina can perform excellently with a nonpreferred partner. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0512-P0516`.

The later repair demonstrates cognitive and relational flexibility: Reina can apologize and say Kumiko is also right without giving up her own core musical values. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0718-P0761`.

### Frozen predictive rule

Do not write Reina as either an unchanging merit automaton or a secretly soft character whose standards disappear under friendship. Her mature state is **severe standards + selective vulnerability + professional role discipline + capacity for explicit repair**.

## 8. Taki and authority freeze

Taki after V12 should be modeled as:

- high-skill expert;
- highly consequential evaluator;
- bounded and imperfect adult;
- weaker in human-relations transparency than in musical implementation;
- willing to explain reasoning when questioned;
- still learning from other educators;
- legitimately trusted because of demonstrated labor and competence, not because historical success makes him infallible.

The mature trust rule is:

> **imperfection is a reason to think with the expert, not automatically a reason either to obey or to reject him.**

## 9. Team Oumae and institutional reproduction

The distributed architecture succeeds relative to the earlier Asuka/Yuuko concentration problem, but V11-V12 prove that formal role separation does not eliminate externalities.

| Role | Primary strength | Recurrent externality |
|---|---|---|
| Kumiko / president | relational mapping, recognition, legitimacy work | over-responsibility, containment thinking, self-application gap |
| Shuuichi / vice-president | logistics, low-pressure support, social sustainability | can soften or defer hard correction |
| Reina / drum major | technical standards, rapid improvement, musical clarity | fear, crying, social-care labor, doctrinal rigidity |
| Taki / adviser | expert selection and ensemble design | opacity, founder mythology, interpersonal trust gaps |

Future institutional simulation should track not only **who owns a task** but **whose behavior creates labor for another role**.

## 10. Music and performance freeze

V11-V12 reinforce several durable music-model principles:

1. **ability is vector-valued** - role, repertoire, timbre, coordination, technique, and time matter;
2. **repeated auditions are state sampling**, not timeless ranking;
3. **mature ensemble excellence includes repeatability**, not only peak output;
4. **score literacy is relational**, requiring attention beyond one's own line;
5. **technical correctness and biographical meaning can coexist**;
6. **national gold matters**, but does not retroactively make all earlier choices morally correct;
7. **performance can be public, competitive, collective, and autobiographical at once**.

The main national-performance evidence is `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P1164-P1215`.

## 11. Future authorship freeze

Kumiko's teaching future must not be backported as an always-conscious destiny.

At the beginning of V12 she has no life-defining career desire. `HIBIKE-V12 / S02 / P0449-P0457`.

After leadership, family discussion, adult counseling, direct conflict with Taki, and observation of imperfect teaching, she chooses education. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0779-P0791`.

The epilogue confirms adult Kumiko as Kitauji assistant adviser. `HIBIKE-V12 / S05 / P0021-P0028`.

The correct longitudinal model is:

> **under-authored future -> action-generated preference information -> explicit vocation choice -> institutional transmission**.
