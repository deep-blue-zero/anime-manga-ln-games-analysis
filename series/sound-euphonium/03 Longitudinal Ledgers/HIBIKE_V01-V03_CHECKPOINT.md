## 2. Frozen opening-trilogy formulation

The opening trilogy's strongest current movement is:

> **deniable desire → plural and contested desire → accountable first-person agency under uncertainty → transmission**

This formulation preserves V1's insight that *Sound! Euphonium* is centrally about wanting while making the mechanism more precise.

### HIBIKE-V01 — desire becomes claimable

Kumiko's starting problem is not absence of feeling or absence of action. She is already perceptive, musically discriminating, jealous, attached, impulsive, and capable of care. Her defense is that she keeps motive and desired outcome **deniable**. Majority behavior, ambiguous smiles, internal judgments, and deferred commitments protect her from rejection and from responsibility for choosing.

Reina's middle-school question exposes the gap between a public goal and a goal allowed to define failure. Kitauji's institutional arc parallels Kumiko's: `全国大会出場` becomes meaningful only when the band allows the slogan to bind practice, selection, and disappointment.

### HIBIKE-V02 — desire becomes plural, asymmetric, and temporally unfair

Once wanting is owned, it does not become harmonious. Nozomi, Mizore, Yuuko, Reina, Kumiko, Taki, and the competitive institutions want different things and often assign different meanings to the same relationship or result.

The strongest new V02 principle is **non-retroactive justice**. A fairer present procedure can stop repeating an old wrong without restoring opportunities already lost under the previous order. The changed Kitauji cannot give Nozomi or the current seniors their lost first-year time back.

V02 also disciplines Kumiko's new intervention appetite: entering another person's problem is not the same thing as understanding it.

### HIBIKE-V03 — agency becomes accountable after certainty is surrendered

V03 refuses to let Kumiko solve the Asuka crisis by proving what everyone “really” wants. Asuka can defeat almost every institution-wide claim. Kumiko succeeds only when she stops speaking on behalf of the band and says what **she** wants.

The mature movement is therefore not from reason to irrationality. It is from falsely universalized reason to bounded responsibility:

> **I may not know what is best for everyone. I can still tell you what I want and accept that this is my claim.**

The graduation epilogue then converts private development into transmission: Asuka passes the notebook forward and instructs Kumiko to let future juniors hear the music.

## 3. Kumiko longitudinal state: what may now be treated as established

### Stable through V03

1. **Observation precedes self-knowledge.** Kumiko is usually better at noticing other people's behavior than naming her own motives.
2. **Her baseline spoken language is standard Japanese.** The Kyoto setting must not be used to default her to Kansai speech.
3. **Thought and speech are separate modeling layers.** Internal judgment is often sharper, pettier, more erotic, more skeptical, or more decisive than what she says aloud.
4. **Action can precede owned motive.** She may chase, touch, intervene, or protect before she consciously admits why.
5. **Social editing remains a default.** V03's Asuka speech proves capacity for directness, not permanent transformation into a blunt speaker.
6. **Care can become overprotection.** Her concealment of Taki's past from Reina shows a tendency to reduce immediate pain even when the recipient would prefer difficult truth.
7. **Intervention appetite is rising faster than epistemic certainty.** This is productive and dangerous.

### State transition

`KUMIKO@V01`:

> desire kept deniable; action frequently socially or impulsively displaced.

`KUMIKO@V02`:

> willingly enters other people's conflicts, but can overestimate her understanding.

`KUMIKO@V03`:

> capable of saying “I want” without claiming universal correctness; begins to imagine responsibility toward juniors.

### Negative constraints

Do not backport:

- later presidential competence;
- mature conflict mediation;
- generalized self-confidence;
- later relationship knowledge;
- or the assumption that because she can be direct in crisis, she normally prefers direct speech.

## 4. Reina longitudinal state

Reina remains a strong contrast to Kumiko but should no longer be modeled as simply “blunt, confident, intense.”

### Stable through V03

- exceptional musical seriousness;
- willingness to state competitive judgments others soften;
- explicit aspiration toward specialness;
- high tolerance for social isolation when musical principle is at stake;
- strong attraction toward people who do not perform socially approved niceness for her.

### Newly differentiated

V02–V03 establish **domain-conditioned directness**:

- musical/competitive propositions: categorical, compressed, often fearless;
- relational initiation: can be embarrassed, indirect, reassurance-seeking, physically close;
- trusted-intimate truth: prefers difficult truth to protective concealment;
- self-image: vulnerability can trigger shame because she expects unusual emotional strength from herself.

A simulation that makes Reina equally blunt under all emotional conditions is now contradicted by the source.

## 5. Asuka longitudinal state

Asuka is the strongest example so far of why V2 needs mechanism rather than adjectives.

### Established mechanisms

1. **Register control is social control.** Comedy, flirtation, mockery, cool rationalism, and quiet intimacy are selected according to situation.
2. **Competence is armor and bargaining power.** Excellence reduces the grounds on which others can constrain her.
3. **She manages environments even when personally endangered.** The maternal confrontation makes this explicit.
4. **She triages relationships functionally.** V02's Nozomi decision and V03's band crisis show willingness to withhold, block, or reframe according to ensemble risk.
5. **Functional models can underdescribe moral reality.** What protects the institution may fail to honor the person.
6. **She converts desire into objective-seeming reasoning.** This makes her own wants difficult for others to identify.
7. **Simple affirmation exists underneath the performance system.** `うちも` matters precisely because it is rare and low-defense.

### Institutional implication

Asuka is genuinely exceptional in ability. The error is not recognizing excellence; it is turning excellence into a category that suspends reciprocal care.

## 6. Institutional architecture through V03

### V01 — chosen objective

Kitauji moves from nominal aspiration to a goal whose consequences students must experience. Taki's authority is best described as **bounded student authorship under strong expert implementation**.

### V02 — historical injury persists

Procedural reform does not erase damage from the old institution. Present fairness is necessary and still insufficient.

### V03 — resilience requires redundancy

The Asuka crisis exposes charismatic dependence. Natsuki's substitute preparation and Haruka's leadership establish a crucial principle:

> **A humane institution should minimize functional indispensability because indispensability can make voluntary participation coercive.**

But functional redundancy must not be confused with emotional interchangeability.

### Frozen institutional propositions

- legitimacy is not identical to popularity;
- expertise can justify strong implementation without making authority absolute;
- rules operate inside unequal material histories;
- old harm can persist after rules improve;
- distributed labor is ethically and operationally superior to mystical reliance on one exceptional person;
- people still require recognition beyond their formal role.

## 7. Music and pedagogy through V03

The trilogy establishes music not merely as metaphor but as a causal practice.

### Technical reconstruction

V01 gives unusually concrete attention to:

- tuning;
- overtones;
- solfège;
- long tones;
- lip slurs;
- marching timing;
- practice scheduling;
- body/instrument interaction.

Improved competence expands what Kumiko can plausibly desire.

### Expression after technique

V02 adds Hashimoto and Niiyama's pressure toward expressive authorship. Technical cleanliness is not identical to alive performance.

### Repeatability

V02–V03 establish mature competitive craft as reduced variance. A developing band can produce a miracle; an elite institution can produce excellence reliably.

### Ranking without rank totalism

V03 bronze matters. Shindō's C matters. The project therefore rejects the claim that judging is emotionally painful and thus meaningless. But Shindō's separate praise of Asuka demonstrates that formal contest judgment does not exhaust musical or personal recognition.

### Transmission

The notebook's movement Shindō → Asuka → Kumiko gives musical inheritance a physical archive. The series title becomes an internal repertoire object whose future meaning depends on being played for another person.

## 8. Relationship principles frozen through V03

### Relational asymmetry is normal, not pathological by itself

Mizore/Nozomi shows that two people can sincerely inhabit different versions of the same relationship. Repair can correct false causal beliefs without equalizing attachment.

### Additional relationships do not necessarily replace the privileged one

Yuuko becomes a second anchor for Mizore. The story's answer is pluralization, not substitution.

### Intimacy does not guarantee epistemic access

Kaori's devotion to Asuka does not mean Kaori knows everything Asuka wants. Kumiko's closeness with Reina does not give her the right to decide which truths Reina should know.

### Ordinary intimacy is analytically important

Shuuichi's importance is structurally easy to underweight because his bond with Kumiko often uses routine familiarity rather than spectacular exceptionalism. V2 will continue tracking ordinary dyadic regulation rather than ranking relationships by dramatic staging alone.

## 10. What V04 must test

HIBIKE-V04 is not “bonus material” in the V2 methodology. Its anthology structure is a methodological stress test.

The opening trilogy has been overwhelmingly Kumiko-focalized. V04 must test:

1. which Kumiko-centered interpretations survive when other people become viewpoint centers;
2. how major characters sound when Kumiko is not mediating their language;
3. whether ordinary-life behavior supports or contradicts crisis-derived personality models;
4. how Aoi, Hazuki, Midori, Riko, Mizore, Reina, Shuuichi, Yuuko, Natsuki, and others describe themselves internally;
5. how much invisible labor and social support the mainline narrative leaves peripheral;
6. whether romantic, family, and friendship scripts change when the focalizer changes;
7. how early seeds for succession and second-year leadership appear outside the main crisis plot.

For character reconstruction, low-stakes stories receive **high evidentiary value** because a model built only from crises will systematically overproduce crisis behavior.

## 11. Checkpoint lock

The following opening-trilogy statement is canonical unless later V2 evidence explicitly revises it:

> *Sound! Euphonium* begins by asking whether a person can admit desire, then whether desire can survive a world of other minds and conflicting claims, and finally whether one can act responsibly without possessing certainty about everyone else. Its first durable answer is not self-assertion alone but **bounded first-person agency capable of becoming transmission**.
