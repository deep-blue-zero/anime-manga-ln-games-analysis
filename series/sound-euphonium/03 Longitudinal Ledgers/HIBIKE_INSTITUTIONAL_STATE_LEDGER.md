## Institutional interpretation

V01 does **not** establish a completed moral repair. It establishes a more functional order whose norms are increasingly aligned with declared excellence. The people driven out remain absent. Aoi's guilt is unresolved. Reina's social isolation demonstrates that performance-sensitive procedure does not automatically produce humane belonging.

### V01 governing distinction

`performance legitimacy ≠ total moral legitimacy`

The first is substantially improved; the second remains open.

## HIBIKE-V02 update

- Seniority allocation and “fun together” ideology protected weaker seniors from challenge.
- Serious younger students were accused of disrupting `秩序` and told to `空気読んで`: `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0149`.

- Yuuko identifies the midstream bargain problem: seniors who endured old seniority hierarchy reach senior year only when performance selection replaces it: `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0710`.

- Nozomi's claim is legitimate but Asuka blocks return during competition because Mizore is the only oboist and visibly destabilized.

### Leadership distribution

- Asuka performs atmosphere regulation and emotionally forceful rallying.

- V02 strengthens a distributed-leadership model rather than a single charismatic leader model.

### Material infrastructure

- Taki makes parent-funded activity costs explicit and links cost to responsibility for time: `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0227`.

### V08 legitimacy decomposition

Future HIBIKE institutional analysis should distinguish at least:

1. **procedural legitimacy** — what rule is actually used;
2. **epistemic legitimacy** — why members believe the rule;
3. **social legitimacy** — whether people can live with the correct result without informal punishment.

### V08 self-reproduction warning

Kitauji's movement toward self-reproduction is **not automatically moral progress**. It can transmit good standards and care, but also overwork, ritualized deference, popularity pressure and fear of visible failure. Institutional maturity therefore requires **pruning unauthorized/obsolete norms as well as transmitting valued ones**.


## HIBIKE-V09 institutional update — adaptive legitimacy and succession

### Legitimacy model after second year

Kitauji must now be modeled across four layers:

1. **procedural legitimacy** — selection/rules are defensible;
2. **epistemic legitimacy** — members have evidence rules are genuinely applied;
3. **social legitimacy** — correct outcomes can be lived without informal punishment;
4. **adaptive legitimacy** — structure can change when new capability, injury, role load, or competitive evidence invalidates an inherited fit.

### Adaptive ensemble response

Mizore's expanded sound remains score/tempo-correct but overwhelms the old balance. Taki reconfigures the ensemble around it rather than suppressing the individual — `HIBIKE-V09 / S04 / P0730-P0747`.

### Caregiver self-exemption failure

Yuuko's overwork while ill proves that a low-stress/caring culture is incomplete if the leader defines herself as outside the population entitled to care. Natsuki forces delegation — `S04 / P0489-P0547`.

### Manager role as infrastructure

Tomoe's nonperforming role proves administrative, novice-access, and grievance-routing work are institutionally productive. The band subsequently reasons that a large organization needs more formal role differentiation.

### Controlled Rikka transfer

Rikka/Tachibana's multi-role leadership model enters Kitauji's own redesign discussion. This is a legitimate comparative transfer now grounded by canonical HIBIKE-V05–V06 primary evidence, not a foil-only inference.

### Leadership redesign

By the epilogue:

- Kumiko = president;
- Shuuichi = vice-president;
- Reina = independent drum major;
- drum-major separation explicitly reduces burden — `S06 / P0027-P0037`.

Yuuko also reveals the Oumae Consultation Office was partly deliberate successor cultivation — `S05 / P0312-P0366`.

### Competitive institution state

Kitauji receives Kansai gold but no national berth. The institution is therefore **successful, credible, and non-invincible**. Relative competition prevents reform history from becoming permanent entitlement.

### Open structural vulnerability

Kumiko explicitly wonders what happens when Taki eventually leaves. Student governance is becoming self-reproducing; conductor/expert dependence remains an open institutional risk.

## HIBIKE-V11 institutional additions

### Kitauji third-year state — V11

1. **Distributed office architecture is real.** President/vice-president carry human-relational work; independent drum major concentrates on musical instruction. This is a structural response to earlier role concentration — `HIBIKE-V11 / S02 / P0002-P0051`.
2. **Externalities remain cross-role.** Reina's technically effective strictness produces social/care labor that Kumiko absorbs. Organizational separation does not automatically distribute costs.
3. **Scale is now a governing condition.** 103 members create instrument, A/B capacity, room, attention, and administrative scarcity — `S02 / P0609-P0707; P0860-P0875`.
4. **Prestige produces norm pressure.** Newcomers inherit a national-level identity whose founding struggle they did not witness.
5. **Goal authorization remains procedural but socially loaded.** National gold is unanimously reauthorized; Kumiko privately hopes unanimity will prevent later friction — `S02 / P0758-P0772`.
6. **Optional effort must not become moral debt.** Kumiko explicitly warns against converting self-imposed overtime into expectation for others — `S03 / P0703-P0727`.
7. **Repeated auditions are adaptive legitimacy with a cost.** Current ability can change selection at each stage, but positional security falls and continuous evaluation rises — `S03 / P1043-P1078; S04 / P0011-P0038`.
8. **Policy authorship correction:** Mayu supplies Seira comparative information; Team Oumae decides the repeated-audition policy. Do not assign policy agency directly to Mayu.
9. **Selection is contextual optimization.** Suzume over Satsuki is justified through ensemble/repertoire fit, not scalar total-musician rank — `S04 / P0834-P0851`.
10. **Founder legitimacy is generationally perishable.** Mirei explicitly distinguishes third-years who witnessed Taki's transformation from younger cohorts who inherited only the reputation — `S04 / P0819-P0821`.
11. **Recognition and containment overlap.** Kumiko listens to dissent and also directly thinks about preventing rebellion from growing — `S04 / P0796-P0833`. Mature leadership cannot be analyzed as morally innocent listening alone.
12. **Privacy can generate perceived conflict-of-interest risk without wrongdoing.** Motomu's hidden family relation changes Kumiko's perception but does not establish favoritism — `S03 / P1257-P1278`.
13. **Kyoto result:** gold + representative; Ryuusei and Rikka also advance. This is a progress state, not closure — `S05 / P0004-P0011`.

### Legitimacy model after V11

The V08-V09 four-layer model remains necessary:

- procedural legitimacy;
- epistemic legitimacy;
- social legitimacy;
- adaptive legitimacy.

V11 adds **self-implication** and **authority-inheritance** modifiers. A procedure can become harder to evaluate when the evaluator is personally affected, and a historically earned authority can lose epistemic force for cohorts who did not witness the earning process.

## HIBIKE-V14 institutional-state additions - succession without replication
