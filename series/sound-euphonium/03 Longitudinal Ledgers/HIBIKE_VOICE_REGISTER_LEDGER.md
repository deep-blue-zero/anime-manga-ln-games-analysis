### Mizore

### Nozomi

### Yuuko

- Direct-address attachment claim: `うちは？アンタにとって、うちはなんなん？` — `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0326`.

### Natsuki

- `罪滅ぼし` self-description supports blunt self-account rather than self-heroization: `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0098`.

### Asuka

- Highest register mobility remains intact: explanatory monologue, comic performance, blunt `無理や`, strategic compression, private `秘密`.
- V02 adds **information timing** as a voice variable: she not only chooses how to speak but whether a truth should be made available at all.

### Hashimoto

### Niiyama
