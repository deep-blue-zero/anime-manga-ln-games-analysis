# Sound! Euphonium V2 - Kaori Nakaseko Character Monograph
## Evidence-proportional Tier-B psychology, voice, behavior, relationships, and partial simulation model


## 1. Authority, purpose, and Tier-B boundary


This artifact is the first evidence-proportional Tier-B character monograph in the current *Sound! Euphonium* V2 Phase-2 modeling stack. It models Kaori Nakaseko not as a generic kind senior, not as a passive object of Yuuko's devotion, and not as a satellite whose only analytical purpose is to reveal Tanaka Asuka. The locked Japanese corpus supports a narrower but still generatively useful model: Kaori has recurring behavior across competition, mentorship, institutional frustration, attachment threat, post-graduation ordinary life, and mature retrospective advice, and V10 gives direct Kaori focalization sufficient to separate some of her own motives from other characters' interpretations.


The artifact is downstream of:


1. the immutable Japanese EPUB source lock for `HIBIKE-V01` through `HIBIKE-V14`;
2. all canonical V2 sequential readings and deterministic locator indexes;
3. `HIBIKE_CHARACTER_MODELING_METHOD.md`;
4. `HIBIKE_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md`, `HIBIKE_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md`, `HIBIKE_VOICE_REGISTER_LEDGER.md`, and `HIBIKE_BEHAVIOR_GESTURE_LEDGER.md`;
5. the audited-provisional Asuka model and its completed audits, used only to constrain reciprocal claims and never as a substitute for Kaori evidence.


The method explicitly places Kaori among likely Tier-B candidates: recurring figures with meaningful but narrower evidence who can support strong character descriptions and partial simulation capacity, but not the full context coverage required for Tier A. `HIBIKE_CHARACTER_MODELING_METHOD.md / Section 11`


The governing unit is therefore deliberately narrower than the Tier-A form:


> **Kaori state x relationship state x stake x perceived responsibility -> probable attention, persistence, care, speech, and boundary behavior.**


This model is `active_provisional`. It is current authority for Kaori reconstruction inside the initial locked V01-V14 source boundary, but it has not yet passed an independent character-monograph audit or a formal Asuka-Kaori reciprocal audit. Generated dialogue and predicted behavior are model output, not canonical evidence.


### 1.1 Epistemic notation


- **[A] Direct textual fact** - explicit Kaori speech/action, direct focalized thought when Kaori is the focalizer, chronology, or role fact.
- **[B] Focalized observation** - another viewpoint character's perception of Kaori; evidence of that perception, not automatic objective truth.
- **[C] Character interpretation** - another character's causal theory of Kaori or Kaori's theory of another person.
- **[D] Narrative-pattern inference** - a pattern supported across multiple independent scenes/states.
- **[E] Analytical inference** - a defensible extrapolation not directly stated.
- **[F] Paratextual support** - editorial/interview evidence; not needed for the present core model.
- **[G] Open / underdetermined** - evidence does not justify a settled conclusion.


Three ownership rules are especially important:


1. Asuka's claim that Kaori is not seeking pity and instead wants to reach a state of `nattoku` is privileged [C] relationship evidence, not direct Kaori self-report. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0589-P0604`
2. Natsuki's impression of Kaori as possessing overwhelming self-affirmation, compassion, and moral correctness is [B] Natsuki. V10 later supplies Kaori-owned evidence that she knows she is generally loved, but the two forms of evidence must remain distinct. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0519-P0522`; `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0093-P0095`
3. Kaori's theory that Asuka's harshness tests whether people will leave is [C] Kaori's model of Asuka. It may illuminate Kaori's attachment behavior, but must never be promoted into direct Asuka psychology. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0066-P0104`

## 2. Compact identity thesis


Kaori is best modeled as a **high-self-worth, prosocial continuity-preserver whose gentleness frequently masks persistence rather than weakness**.


Her baseline social presentation is warm, considerate, and low-force. She often reduces pressure, redirects attention away from herself, and gives others room to choose. But the same character repeatedly acts when something matters to her:


- she encourages a disengaged Natsuki not to quit while refusing to make her own opinion the center of the conversation; `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0515-P0544`
- she privately keeps practicing after losing the solo and publicly requests a second audition; `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0589-P0604`; `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0653-P0664`
- she stops Yuuko's attempt to turn devotion into an entitlement claim against Reina; `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0236-P0270`
- she presses Asuka for a private judgment even though she already knows the likely answer; `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0353-P0381`
- she runs after Asuka during the family crisis, inserts herself physically into the interaction, and later responds to Asuka's return with unguarded physical attachment; `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0093-P0170`; `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0718-P0727`
- she explicitly says she loves Asuka, wants relational primacy, and proposes living together; `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0076-P0116`
- once cohabiting, she can correct Asuka, warn Kumiko not to grant Asuka automatic authority, and independently formulate a practical response to Kumiko's problem. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0605-P0633`


The central generative rule is:


> **Kaori usually seeks continuity without coercion, but high personal value increases persistence, directness, and willingness to risk rejection.**


This differs from passivity. She is often non-dominating because she does not assume that wanting something gives her jurisdiction over the other person's choice. The V01 solo arc and V10-V12 Asuka material repeatedly show the same architecture in different domains: **want strongly -> pursue a legitimate avenue -> seek an answer/recognition -> do not erase the other's authorship when the answer is unfavorable or uncertain.**

## 4. Longitudinal state model


Kaori changes less through personality replacement than through **where she locates the authority to sustain what she values**.


### 4.1 `KAORI@EARLY_KITAUJI`


**Boundary:** V13 retrospective material before the main V01 year.


Kaori is already warm, morally serious, and continuity-oriented. When she overhears Natsuki wondering whether joining band was a mistake, she asks what happened and why Natsuki joined, then tells her not to quit. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0515-P0530`


Crucially, when Natsuki asks what Kaori herself thinks of the club, Kaori says her own view is not very important and redirects the question back to Natsuki. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0533`


That is not evidence that Kaori lacks opinions. In the same exchange she gives a clear account of Kitauji's seniority-based contest system and says a structure where children who work hard are properly rewarded would be better. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0540-P0544`


**State rule:** Kaori has a fairness ideal but does not yet treat possession of an ideal as authorization to dominate other people's decisions or reorganize the institution by force.


### 4.2 `KAORI@V01_SOLO`


**Boundary:** main V01 competition year.


This is the state in which Kaori's low-force presentation is most easily misread as weakness. She recognizes before the audition that Reina is better and expects Reina to receive the solo. She nevertheless says she will do what she can. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0378-P0387`


After losing, Kaori keeps practicing the solo privately. The source gives that fact through Asuka, who also says Kaori understands that Reina is better. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0589-P0604`


Kaori later raises her own hand and asks Taki for a second solo audition. Her playing has improved enough that Taki explicitly comments on it. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0653-P0670`


When given an opening to claim the solo, she refuses, cries, says she cannot do it, and tells Reina that Reina should play it. When Reina apologizes for her earlier blunt statement, Kaori answers that it was true. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0681-P0695`


**State rule:** wanting an earned chance and accepting a loss are compatible. Kaori's persistence should not be collapsed into entitlement, and her concession should not be rewritten as evidence that she never wanted the solo.


### 4.3 `KAORI@V03_ASUKA_CRISIS`


**Boundary:** Asuka's maternal coercion / withdrawal crisis.


Kaori's strongest attachment is threatened by blocked access. Early in the crisis she grips Asuka's uniform hem; Asuka removes the hand gently but definitively, and Kaori is visibly hurt when Asuka refuses to explain. `HIBIKE-V03 / S03 / P0036-P0039`


Later Kaori runs after Asuka and Kumiko, physically inserts herself between them, and allows accusatory affect to enter her otherwise gentle voice. `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0093-P0117`


The same state also contains low-ceremony care: food, practical help, gifts, and a shared birthday object. `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0121-P0170`


When Asuka finally returns, Kaori's composure fails: she approaches with trembling hands, clings to Asuka, cannot initially speak, and is held/stroked in return. `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0718-P0727`


**State rule:** attachment threat increases Kaori's physical/direct behavior and visible frustration, but she still does not become an Asuka-like rhetorical controller or a Yuuko-like public crusader.


### 4.4 `KAORI@V10_GRADUATION_RETROSPECTIVE`


**Boundary:** Kaori-focal V10 S03, combining earlier memory with graduation-letter authorship.


This is the richest Kaori-owned interior source. She worries whether the current club can face future motivated juniors with integrity, and asks Asuka why Asuka will not change the environment even though Kaori believes she could. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0052-P0067`


Kaori then states her own attachment directly. She tells Asuka that she likes her, that Asuka should understand she is loved, and when Asuka asks by whom, answers from herself. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0076-P0085`


Kaori says she has never wanted to become this close to another person and may never do so again. Direct focalization says she already knows that other people love her; Asuka is different. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0093-P0095`


However, when Asuka asks whether Kaori would stop liking her if Asuka were no longer special, Kaori cannot immediately answer. The question destabilizes her and Asuka notices. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0098-P0104`


The graduation letter then makes the continuity desire explicit: Kaori wants to remain together, wants to be Asuka's `ichiban`, fears post-school fading/replacement, and proposes living together, while adding a practical rationale and a no-force off-ramp. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0109-P0116`


**State rule:** Kaori's attachment is direct but not fully conceptually resolved. She owns her desire more easily than she can answer whether her love is independent of Asuka's exceptionalism.


### 4.5 `KAORI@POSTGRAD_V07_V09`


Kaori remains embedded with Asuka after graduation. V07 shows ordinary embarrassment, teasing, touch, and mild jealousy/possessive affect around Asuka's attention to others; Kaori also contributes a practical compromise when the group needs a concert-program solution. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0393-P0462`


V09 resolves a previously stale downstream locator: the coordinated visit and matching gold pinky rings are correctly located at `HIBIKE-V09 / S04 / P1369-P1394`, not V08. Asuka says she came because Kaori strongly wanted to support Kitauji, and Kaori later gently settles Asuka's theatrics. `HIBIKE-V09 / S04 / P1369-P1394`


**State rule:** postgraduation continuity is observable and durable. The rings and shared appearances are strong intimacy coding, but do not by themselves establish a formal relationship label.


### 4.6 `KAORI@POSTGRAD_V12`


By V12, Kaori and Asuka live together. Kaori now has enough distance from the V01 crisis to explain what it felt like when other people acted on her behalf until nobody could control the situation. She explicitly says everyone can be not-wrong and still produce a bad collective outcome. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0563-P0566`


Her theory of specialness has also changed. She still says Asuka is special to her, but can now formulate a broader model: everyone is special to someone, so Asuka can remain special to Kaori even if Kaori is not equivalently special to Asuka. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0577-P0590`


The narration cannot settle whether Kaori is fully happy in saying this or performing happiness. That ambiguity must remain open. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0584`


In domestic interaction Kaori handles groceries/tea, corrects Asuka's lazy responses, tells Asuka not to bully the junior, warns Kumiko not to take every persuasive Asuka statement as authoritative, and independently reframes Kumiko's problem into effort plus direct communication. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0591-P0633`


**State rule:** adult Kaori is warmer and more secure in her own valuation, but not omnisciently secure. She can tolerate uncertain reciprocity better, and her care includes interpretive correction rather than mere accommodation.

## 5. Core wants, values, fears, and self-claims


### 5.1 Continuity is a primary value


Across junior care, club participation, and Asuka attachment, Kaori repeatedly acts to preserve meaningful continuity:


- encourages Natsuki not to quit; `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0515-P0530`
- values a club environment future motivated juniors can face; `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0052-P0067`
- continues working after losing the solo rather than withdrawing; `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0589-P0604`
- wants Asuka relationship continuity after graduation; `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0109-P0116`
- returns as an alumna and remains engaged with Kitauji. `HIBIKE-V09 / S04 / P1369-P1394`


Continuity is not identical to stasis. Kaori does not insist that everyone remain in the same role or that the old institution stay unchanged. Her adult life includes nursing school and cohabitation, and V12 lets her look back critically on the social escalation surrounding her own solo.


### 5.2 Fairness matters, but she is not an absolutist reformer


Kaori says a setting where those who work hard are properly rewarded would be better. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0540-P0544`


She also worries about whether the existing Kitauji can face future serious students. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0052-P0067`


Yet she does not behave like Reina or Taki. She does not make pure merit a universal moral identity, and she does not claim that one correct criterion automatically solves social legitimacy. V12 explicitly reflects that a group can become uncontrollable even when individuals are not simply wrong. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0563-P0566`


### 5.3 Recognition matters most when it is voluntarily given


Kaori's V01 conversation with Asuka is especially diagnostic. She already knows Reina is likely better, yet still wants Asuka not to say so, even jokingly. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0367-P0381`


That suggests [D/E] that recognition from a valued person is not interchangeable with public status. Winning a role because others pity her would not solve the underlying problem; Asuka's [C] account of Kaori wanting `nattoku` is consistent with this. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0589-P0604`


### 5.4 Fear of relational replacement is explicit in the graduation letter


Kaori fears Asuka entering a new world, making new friends, and allowing high-school memory to fade. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0109-P0116`


This is one of the few places where Kaori's insecurity is directly self-authored rather than inferred from behavior. It should be weighted heavily in Asuka-related simulation, but **not generalized into a global abandonment pathology** unsupported by other relationships.


### 5.5 High self-worth and attachment insecurity coexist


Direct V10 focalization says Kaori knows she is loved by other people. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0093-P0095`


Natsuki independently perceives her as radiating unusually high self-affirmation. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0521`


Therefore the Asuka-specific fear of not being special should not be modeled as generic low self-esteem. Kaori can be socially secure and still experience high uncertainty around a uniquely valued bond.

## 6. Attention and perception model


### 6.1 Kaori notices participation and emotional continuity


Her early Natsuki intervention begins by noticing that a junior may be struggling and asking what happened. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0515-P0522`


She is attentive to whether people remain connected to the activity, not merely whether they perform well. This is also visible in V10's concern for future juniors and V12's concern for how group dynamics can spiral.


### 6.2 She is capable of accurate status perception even when the result hurts


Kaori predicts Reina is better before the audition and later accepts Reina's earlier blunt statement as fact. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0378-P0387`; `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0681-P0692`


Simulation consequence: **do not give Kaori motivated blindness as a default defense.** Her problem is more often what to do with a painful reality than inability to perceive it.


### 6.3 She reads Asuka closely, but not infallibly


Kaori recognizes Asuka's clowning/deflection behavior and can warn Kumiko not to treat Asuka's persuasive improvisation as final truth. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0098-P0104`; `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0605-P0633`


But Kaori's high-school belief that Asuka is categorically special is itself challenged by Asuka. The V12 maturation shows Kaori revising her framework rather than demonstrating that her original model was omniscient.


### 6.4 She often de-centers her own judgment when another person's authorship is at stake


The clearest early example is `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0533`: when asked what she thinks of Kitauji, Kaori says her own view is not very important and asks Natsuki what **she** thinks.


This is a useful predictive prior. Kaori can offer advice and moral judgment, but frequently tries to preserve the other person's ownership of the decision.

## 7. Decision policy: persistence without entitlement


A compact Kaori decision sequence is:


> **notice value -> preserve participation if possible -> assess reality -> pursue a legitimate avenue -> seek recognition/clarity -> accept the other's authorship -> maintain relation if possible.**


### 7.1 Competitive version


In V01:


1. Kaori knows Reina is probably better. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0378-P0387`
2. She keeps practicing. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0589-P0604`
3. She requests a second audition herself. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0653-P0664`
4. She improves enough to receive explicit praise. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0668-P0670`
5. After direct comparison, she refuses a solo she no longer believes she should take. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0681-P0695`


This is not passivity and not status hunger. It is **procedural persistence bounded by substantive recognition of merit**.


### 7.2 Relational version


In V10:


1. Kaori states love directly. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0076-P0085`
2. She asks to remain together and be Asuka's `ichiban`. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0109-P0115`
3. She proposes cohabitation.
4. She immediately provides a practical rationale and a no-force disclaimer. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0115-P0116`


The same architecture appears: strong ask, but not totalization of the other person's choice.


### 7.3 Adult advice version


In V12 Kaori reduces Kumiko's problem to two owned tasks: work hard enough to compete honestly and communicate what she actually feels. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0629-P0633`


This is highly consistent with Kaori's own historical pattern.

## 8. Conflict, escalation, and repair policy


### 8.1 Default conflict style: low-force boundary setting


Kaori often begins with de-escalation rather than domination. During Yuuko's public solo protest she reminds Yuuko that the audition decided the result, tells her to stop, apologizes to Reina, explains Yuuko's protective motive, and asks Yuuko not to say strange things. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0236-P0270`


The important point is that **Kaori does intervene**. She does not disappear behind politeness.


### 8.2 When low-force correction fails, she may persist privately rather than escalate publicly


Her conflict with Asuka over the solo judgment becomes emotionally direct but remains within the relationship: she asks for an answer, accuses Asuka of dodging, says she does not want Asuka to say Reina is better, and physically taps/hits her in frustrated play. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0353-P0381`


### 8.3 Attachment threat can increase physical insistence


During the V03 crisis she grasps Asuka's uniform, runs after her, inserts herself into the interaction, and later hugs/clings when Asuka returns. `HIBIKE-V03 / S03 / P0036-P0039`; `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0093-P0117`; `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0718-P0727`


This should not be generalized into coercive possessiveness. The same source also shows Asuka successfully setting a boundary and Kaori remaining hurt rather than overriding it.


### 8.4 Adult repair becomes more interpretive


V12 Kaori can explicitly explain how factional support escalated around her and warn Kumiko against treating persuasive Asuka talk as binding authority. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0563-P0566`; `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0605-P0633`


She has converted some painful experience into usable relational judgment.

## 9. Care and attachment behavior


### 9.1 Care is active, not merely receptive


Evidence includes:


- encouraging Natsuki to remain in band; `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0515-P0530`
- caring enough about future juniors to question the institution; `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0052-P0067`
- stopping Yuuko from attacking Reina on her behalf; `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0236-P0270`
- giving practical gifts/help during Asuka's crisis; `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0121-P0170`
- comforting Reina after the national result with touch and praise; `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P1378-P1382`
- adult hospitality, groceries, tea, cookies, and practical advice. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0591-P0633`


### 9.2 Kaori permits others to care for her, but may become embarrassed by overinvestment


Yuuko's devotion is explicitly something Kaori finds both pleasing and difficult. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0353-P0357`


A model that treats Kaori as either secretly enjoying all adoration or secretly resenting it is too simple. She appreciates the affection while recognizing its social costs.


### 9.3 Strong attachment raises directness


Kaori can be unusually literal with Asuka: she says she likes/loves her, wants to be first, wants to remain together, and proposes cohabitation. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0076-P0116`


This is not her generic conversational mode. It is relationship-conditioned high-stake directness.


### 9.4 Mature attachment tolerates asymmetry better


V12 is a meaningful development from V10. Kaori can now say that Asuka is special to her even if Kaori is not equivalently special to Asuka. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0577-P0590`


This does not prove complete emotional security because the narration explicitly leaves her affect ambiguous. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0584`

## 10. Musical competition and musicianship


### 10.1 Kaori is serious about trumpet, but the model should not exaggerate technical authority


External V01 descriptions present her as serious about practice and well-regarded. She is trumpet part leader. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0236-P0239`; `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0131-P0133`


Her V01 playing is described as delicate/soft, and Taki notices substantial improvement at the re-audition. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0668-P0670`


### 10.2 She can recognize a superior player without withdrawing effort


This is probably the most simulation-relevant musical trait. Kaori's internal/behavioral arc does not require convincing herself that she is better than Reina. She persists while holding the painful hierarchy in view.


### 10.3 She values fair opportunity partly because she lived under seniority exclusion


Yuuko recounts that Kaori and Haruka had been relegated to B under the old seniority system, and Kaori reaches third year only to face a new merit regime and a first-year soloist. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0708-P0712`


That history explains why the V01 solo dispute can be genuinely painful without making Kaori's eventual concession incoherent.


### 10.4 Negative technical constraint


Do not simulate Kaori as:


- a Reina-level virtuoso;
- an Asuka-level technical diagnostician;
- a Taki-level pedagogical designer;
- musically indifferent simply because she yields the solo.


The evidence supports a serious, improving, self-aware player with a distinct softer musical profile and high emotional stakes around earned opportunity.

## 11. Institutional and moral behavior


### 11.1 Kaori prefers earned participation over inherited status


Her early statement that hardworking students ought to be rewarded is directly sourced. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0540-P0544`


Her own V01 behavior eventually aligns with that principle even when it costs her the solo.


### 11.2 But fairness is relationally embedded


Kaori does not reduce legitimacy to a spreadsheet of merit. She understands why support becomes emotionally charged, why old institutional wounds matter, and by V12 can explain that people who are not individually wrong can still create an unmanageable group outcome. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0563-P0566`


### 11.3 She is more willing to ask a capable person to reform a system than to claim the reformer role herself


In V10 she tells Asuka she thinks Asuka could change Kitauji. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0066-P0067`


This is evidence of Kaori's reformist concern, but also of a limit: the corpus does not show Kaori taking on Asuka/Kumiko-scale institutional architecture herself.


### 11.4 Nursing-school fact is not a personality proof


V13 establishes that Kaori attends nursing school after graduation. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0064-P0066`


It is tempting to treat this as confirmation that a caring personality naturally selected a care profession. The source supplied here does not establish that causal motive. Preserve it as biography, not psychological proof.

## 12. Japanese voice model


Kaori's voice is less flamboyantly marked than Asuka's or Yuuko's, so fidelity depends more on **gentle directness, softening, and relationship-conditioned stakes** than on maximizing dialect tokens.


### 12.1 Baseline properties


High-confidence tendencies:


- first person commonly `私` (`watashi`);
- gentle sentence endings and questions;
- softeners and reflective particles rather than aggressive assertion;
- Standard Japanese and Kansai-compatible forms can coexist;

## 15. Developmental axis: from being chosen to owning choice


The most useful long-range Kaori development is not "shy -> confident" or "passive -> assertive." She is already capable of action in early material.


A stronger longitudinal formulation is:


> **external confirmation pressure -> legitimate pursuit -> painful reality acceptance -> direct attachment claim -> tolerance of uncertain reciprocity while preserving self-authored valuation.**


### 15.1 V01: proof before concession


Kaori wants enough process to know what the result means. She practices, re-auditions, improves, hears the comparison, and then yields. The process lets her own the loss rather than simply receive someone else's sacrifice.


### 15.2 V10: direct claim plus uncertainty


Kaori can own `I love you` and `I want to be first`, but Asuka's question about whether Kaori would still love an ordinary/non-special Asuka reveals an unresolved condition inside the admiration structure. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0093-P0104`


### 15.3 V12: valuation no longer requires symmetric proof


Adult Kaori can say Asuka remains special to her even if the reverse is uncertain. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0577-P0590`


That is a substantial shift toward **self-authored attachment**. It does not require claiming that all insecurity disappeared.

## 18. Scenario-simulation guidance


Tier-B simulations should produce a **distribution**, not a single deterministic answer.


### 18.1 Discouraged junior considers quitting


**High-confidence prior:** Kaori notices distress, asks what happened, asks why the junior joined, encourages continuation without declaring that Kaori's own view should decide the junior's life. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0515-P0544`


Likely output:


- first notice: affect/withdrawal rather than technical fault;
- first move: gentle question;
- pressure: low;
- advice: continuation-framed but not coercive;
- what remains unsaid: Kaori's own larger institutional judgment unless relevant.


### 18.2 Kaori loses a desirable role to someone demonstrably stronger


**High-confidence prior:** disappointment is real; private effort may increase; she seeks a legitimate route to test/improve if available; if direct comparison remains decisive, she is more likely to concede than demand symbolic compensation.


Important modifier: if procedure is visibly illegitimate rather than merely painful, Kaori's fairness concern may produce more sustained objection. That counterfactual is plausible but less directly sourced than the V01 case.


### 18.3 Yuuko attacks someone on Kaori's behalf


**High-confidence prior:** Kaori attempts to stop the escalation, acknowledges Yuuko's protective motive, and protects the target from being made responsible for Kaori's feelings. Do not give Kaori Yuuko's temperature.


### 18.4 Asuka refuses to disclose something painful


State matters:


- `KAORI@V03_ASUKA_CRISIS`: visible hurt, greater physical proximity, accusation can leak through soft voice, persistence rises;
- `KAORI@POSTGRAD_V12`: more likely to identify the deflection pattern, correct/limit Asuka's authority, and preserve the other person's decision rather than chase total disclosure.


### 18.5 Kaori wants a future commitment from Asuka


**High-confidence prior:** she can make a surprisingly direct emotional request, then reduce coercive pressure with practical framing and an explicit option to refuse. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0109-P0116`


### 18.6 Junior asks Kaori for advice on a relational/institutional dilemma


Adult Kaori is likely to:


1. disclose a bounded relevant experience;
2. acknowledge complexity without declaring one villain;
3. ask a clarifying relational question;
4. restate the junior's apparent goal;
5. return the action to the junior in concrete terms.


This is directly modeled by V12. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0563-P0633`


### 18.7 Counterfactual perturbation axes


For future audit, change one variable at a time:


- personal stake low vs high;
- stronger competitor vs weaker competitor;
- public crowd vs private one-to-one;
- Yuuko watching vs absent;
- Asuka high-school defensive state vs adult cohabiting state;
- procedural legitimacy intact vs compromised;
- written channel vs live speech;
- Kaori advising vs Kaori herself being the object of the decision.


A valid model should become more direct with attachment stakes, more procedural with fair competition, and more reflective in adult retrospective conditions without turning every state into the same gentle advice voice.
