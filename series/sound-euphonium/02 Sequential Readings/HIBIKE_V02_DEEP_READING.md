## 2. Executive local thesis — a changed present cannot repair the past

HIBIKE-V01 taught Kumiko to make desire claimable. HIBIKE-V02 asks what happens **after several people make incompatible desires claimable inside a world of scarcity, missed timing, unequal attachment, and irreversible history**.

The result is harsher than a simple meritocratic sequel. Current Kitauji is much fairer than the club Nozomi quit. Taki selects by present performance. Serious students are no longer punished merely for caring. Yet that improvement cannot retroactively give Nozomi the year she lost, return the opportunity denied to Kaori and Haruka as underclassmen, erase Natsuki's spectatorship guilt, or make Mizore's relationship with Nozomi as reciprocal as Mizore once imagined it to be. Yuuko captures the temporal injustice most explicitly when she notes that this year's seniors endured seniority-based unfairness only to reach their own senior year and discover that the rules had changed to performance-based selection. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0710 / 「そうそう。やからさ、今年の三年生が滝先生に反発した理由もよくわかんねんな。やっ`

The volume's central problem is therefore not just ranking. It is **non-retroactive justice**:

> **A better present can stop reproducing an old wrong, but it cannot make the people injured by the old order whole.**

That does not make reform meaningless. It makes repair a different task.

Nozomi's injury is temporal: she left one year before the institution became the kind of club she had wanted. Her recurring counterfactual is effectively “if I had been born one year later.” The students who stayed now receive the serious musical environment for which she had fought, including students who once opposed serious practice. Her wish to return is therefore musically sincere and psychologically entangled with the unbearable fact that history moved without her.

Mizore's injury is relational: a person she experienced as her singular best friend experienced her as one important friend among many. No fair institutional procedure can solve that asymmetry. Nozomi did not omit Mizore from the quitting plan because she despised her; she later explains that inviting the one person who kept practicing would have disrespected Mizore's effort. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0369 / 「どうしてって、みぞれはずっとこの部活で頑張ってたやん。わたしが腐ってたときも、` This explanation corrects Mizore's causal story, but it does **not** equalize their attachment. The text explicitly preserves the unequal emotional temperature between them. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0375 / 焦ったように告げる希美の声は、どこまでも軽やかで美しかった。互いに対する熱量が、`

Yuuko's role is therefore not to replace Nozomi or to prove Mizore's attachment irrational. She pluralizes Mizore's social world. Her furious question—`うちは？アンタにとって、うちはなんなん？`—forces Mizore to confront evidence that another person has been choosing her continuously. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0326 / 「うちは？アンタにとって、うちはなんなん？」` The repair is not “Nozomi mattered less than Mizore thought.” It is “Nozomi need not remain the only relation through which Mizore's existence becomes legible.”

Kumiko likewise develops beyond V01, but not by becoming a perfectly informed mediator. She now intervenes much more readily: she argues internally against Reina's harsh “quitting is escape” theory, promises Nozomi that she will investigate, questions Natsuki, Yuuko, Asuka, and Mizore, comforts Reina, and searches for Mizore during the crisis. Yet she still acts before she knows the whole situation. She makes the promise to Nozomi while significant facts are missing. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0201 / 「言ってないですよ。希美先輩は正しいです」` `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0208 / 逃げた、と麗奈は言った。部活を辞めるということは、逃げるということだと。だけど、` Her growth is therefore **increased willingness to enter other people's conflicts**, accompanied by a new risk: intervention can outrun knowledge.

This makes V02 a crucial bridge toward later Kumiko. Her defining question changes from “Do I want this?” to something closer to:

> **What do I do when everyone has reasons, nobody can receive everything they want, and refusing to choose is itself a choice?**

Competition provides the institutional analogue. Mizore hates it because musical judgment is irreducibly subjective. `音楽を正しく評価するのって、無理だと思う`; technique attracts her because it seems more legible than taste. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0385 / 「音楽を正しく評価するのって、無理だと思う」` `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0390 / 「だから、技術が重要」` Reina answers from the opposite direction: if one becomes overwhelmingly good, judgment becomes robust enough that complaint loses force. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0126 / 「もし圧倒的な上手さがあれば、コンクールで評価されへんなんてことはありえへんと思` Yuuko offers a third account: results can change how people experience years of work, but a bad result does not erase that work. Taki gives the most institutionally disciplined formulation before Kansai: a losing result would mean only that other schools received higher evaluations, not that Kitauji's music was ontologically inferior. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0547 / 「確かに、周りはどこも強豪校ばかりです。何年もこの舞台に立って、全国大会に進んで`

The novel does not choose one theory and discard the rest. The epilogue changes Mizore's feeling toward competition only after a lived experience of reconciliation, expressive awakening, successful performance, and advancement. Asked whether she still hates competition, she answers: `……たったいま、好きになった`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S05 / P0041 / 「……たったいま、好きになった」` This is not a philosophical proof that competition became fair. It is evidence that **meaning is experiential and revisable even when structure remains imperfect**.

The “hottest summer” is thus a summer in which inherited pain, current desire, judgment, friendship, rivalry, and artistic expression are forced into contact. The achievement is not harmonious resolution. It is that several characters acquire enough relational and musical support to keep moving **without requiring the past to become other than it was**.

## 3. Structural architecture

### 3.1 The instrument-titled movement

The three main chapters use instruments as structural centers:

- **フルートの来襲 / The Flute's Incursion** — Nozomi circulates around the edge of the club, socially mobile and impossible to contain as a merely absent former member.
- **トランペットの本心 / The Trumpet's True Feelings** — declared belief, private insecurity, and theories of competitive legitimacy become speakable.
- **オーボエの覚醒 / The Oboe's Awakening** — a technically controlled but affectively constricted voice acquires an addressee and changes sonically.

The titles should not be reduced to one-to-one allegory, but they correctly direct attention toward **how different instruments organize different modes of social presence**. The flute arrives from outside, the trumpet declares, and the oboe changes from within.

### 3.2 Volume-local movement

| Section | External movement | Internal / institutional movement |
|---|---|---|
| S01 | South Middle loses at competition | Mizore establishes competition as painful judgment and Nozomi as unanswered relational center |
| S02 | Kansai preparation; Nozomi asks to return | Past Kitauji becomes an active institutional problem; Reina/Kumiko intimacy deepens; Mizore's trauma becomes bodily visible |
| S03 | Pool, historical investigation, training camp | Competing theories of quitting, judgment, expression, and responsibility emerge; Kumiko moves from curiosity to intervention |
| S04 | Mizore crisis; Nozomi confrontation; Kansai competition | Relational asymmetry becomes explicit; Mizore's sound changes; Kitauji tests its new identity against established powers |
| S05 | Kansai result | Nationals becomes real; Mizore's relation to competition changes without erasing her earlier critique |

### 3.3 The prologue/epilogue frame

The prologue begins with `コンクールってなんなんだろう。` `HIBIKE-V02 / S01 / P0001 / コンクールってなんなんだろう。` The question is not posed by Kumiko but by Mizore, immediately decentralizing the series' epistemic field. South Middle practiced intensely, expected more, received silver, and left Mizore unable to extract stable meaning from the result. When Nozomi asks whether she likes competition, Mizore's true answer is `大嫌い`, but she pretends to sleep and withholds it. `HIBIKE-V02 / S01 / P0048 / 「大嫌い」`

The epilogue returns to the same question after Kitauji receives gold and is named a national representative. The answer changes to `……たったいま、好きになった`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S05 / P0041 / 「……たったいま、好きになった」` The framing is deliberately contingent: competition has not been philosophically purified; Mizore has had a different experience inside it.

### 3.4 Local truth and do-not-backport rule

**Local truth after V02:**

- Kumiko is more willing to intervene but remains epistemically incomplete and conflict-averse in her own most vulnerable relationships.
- Reina's severe musical ethic coexists with invitation anxiety, jealousy, and reassurance-seeking.
- Mizore's Nozomi attachment remains primary even after Yuuko becomes an acknowledged second anchor.
- Nozomi's care for Mizore is real but nonexclusive; asymmetry persists.
- Asuka's apparently detached management is revealed as strategic triage, not lack of perception.
- Taki's competition rhetoric is more nuanced than simple result absolutism.
- Kitauji's new system is fairer in procedure but cannot retroactively repair last year's victims.

**Do not backport:** later developments in any of these relationships must not be used to make this volume's tentative repairs look complete.

## 4. Scene-level close reading

### 4.1 Mizore's prologue — control without a stable reason to participate

Mizore's first sentence asks what competition is. `HIBIKE-V02 / S01 / P0001 / コンクールってなんなんだろう。` Her middle-school experience prevents a clean answer. The students had practiced as if nationals were a serious goal, yet the result could still be silver. The problem is not that Mizore lacked discipline. Her later characterization confirms the opposite: she can sustain practice even without social reward.

Nozomi's closing question—whether Mizore likes competition—produces a characteristic Mizore response. Internally the answer is categorical: `大嫌い`. Externally she says nothing and performs sleep. `HIBIKE-V02 / S01 / P0048 / 「大嫌い」` This becomes an important baseline for the character model: **Mizore can possess an intense evaluative judgment while withholding it almost completely from the person most implicated in it.** Her minimal speech should not be mistaken for minimal affect.

The prologue also establishes temporal scarcity. South Middle's seniors recognize that this is their last realistic chance to reach nationals. Their work becomes more intense precisely because time is closing. V02 repeatedly returns to this structure: opportunity is not infinitely recoverable.

### 4.2 Kansai means scarcity, not merely progress

The novel opens the main movement by specifying the competition architecture. Only three of twenty-three Kansai schools can advance, and Osaka's established powers dominate the region. This is not ornamental exposition. It makes the moral environment explicit: many serious, skilled ensembles can want the same outcome, but institutional design necessarily rejects most of them.

Azusa later states the point without romanticism: everyone wants to win, but feeling alone cannot produce the result. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0778 / 「勝ちたいって気持ちに変わりはない。でも、そんなん思ってるのはみんな同じや。気持` V02 therefore refuses two simplifying positions at once:

1. sincere desire does not entitle a person to success;
2. the absence of success does not prove insincerity or worthlessness.

This logic governs both competition and relationships.

### 4.3 Reina invites Kumiko — directness has relationship-specific limits

Reina's V01 public directness does not generalize to every domain. When she invites Kumiko to fireworks, the phrasing itself is ordinary—`じゃあさ、アタシと行かへん？`—but her body and follow-up reveal embarrassment. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0150 / 「じゃあさ、アタシと行かへん？」` After Kumiko laughs, Reina snaps that she is not used to inviting people. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0157 / 「あんまこういうの慣れてへんの！誘うのとか！」`

This is high-value simulation evidence. A model that produces Reina as uniformly fearless would fail. **Musical judgment is a domain of confidence; relational initiation can be a domain of adolescent uncertainty.** Her tendency is not generic bluntness but **low tolerance for falsifying a judgment she believes is real**, combined with much more conventional vulnerability when asking to be chosen.

Reina's very early practice schedule provides the same contrast through behavior. She is disciplined enough to arrive around six in the morning; Mizore is already there. Kumiko's experience of joining this routine becomes another form of intimacy—shared time organized around music rather than verbal confession.

### 4.4 Mizore's sound enters before her explanation

Mizore initially presents as an extraordinarily controlled player and a difficult social read. Asked whether she likes practicing, she answers only `さあ、知らない`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0345 / 「さあ、知らない」` The line is not coy playfulness. Across the volume, she repeatedly struggles to explain why she continues an activity associated with pain.

Her crisis is triggered not by an argument but by Nozomi's flute sound. `きもちわるい`; `吐きそう`; `この音、いや。聞きたくない`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0802 / 「……きもちわるい」` `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0815 / 「吐きそう」` `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0818 / 「この音、いや。聞きたくない」` The reaction is somatic before it is narratively understood. V2 should therefore model Nozomi-related threat for Mizore as **body-level conditioned distress**, not merely a conscious belief that Nozomi does not value her.

The text later reveals why music is such an efficient trigger: Nozomi is the person who brought Mizore into music. Instrumental identity and attachment history are fused.

### 4.5 Nozomi asks to return; Asuka refuses

Nozomi's return request creates the volume's central institutional triangle. She says plainly that she wants back into the club. Asuka's answer is equally plain: `無理や`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0548 / 「無理や」` She will not authorize the return before the competition.

On the surface, this resembles another gatekeeping act. The later information changes its meaning. Asuka knows that merely seeing or hearing Nozomi can destabilize the ensemble's only oboist. She therefore prioritizes present competitive continuity over Nozomi's legitimate desire to return.

This is where V01's “selective responsibility” model survives longitudinal pressure. Asuka is not neutral in the sense of refusing to act. She acts strongly and covertly when she believes a decision affects the ensemble's functioning. Her refusal to explain everything is part of the strategy.

At the same time, she repeatedly says Nozomi herself is not wrong. The decision is triage, not moral condemnation.

### 4.6 First-years, secrets, and distributed social intelligence

The old-year history is not immediately available to Kumiko, Hazuki, and Midori. Older members avoid discussing it, and the resulting exclusion frustrates Hazuki. Midori, however, responds with social redirection rather than frontal pressure: she proposes the pool and gives Kumiko signals that preserve the group's atmosphere while allowing information to surface elsewhere.

This is a useful correction to any model that makes Midori merely exuberant comic relief. Her high emotional expressiveness coexists with **considerable situational intelligence**. She often knows when continuing a line of questioning would make another person retreat.

### 4.7 Dust nobody cleans — Kumiko sees an institutional residue and does nothing

During Nozomi's appeal, Kumiko notices dust accumulated along the corridor. She alone attends to it, briefly considers wiping it away, and decides she does not need to dirty her hands. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0737 / 振り絞られた声は、確かに熱を帯びていた。あすか先輩は特別やから。夏紀が繰り返して`

The passage is unusually explicit as a visualized moral mechanism. **[D/E]** The dust functions as a provisional image of inherited institutional residue: something visible to an observer, ignored by others, and left in place because recognition does not yet generate responsibility.

This is not proof that “dust = last year's conflict” as an authorial cipher. But the recurrence becomes significant because later, after Kumiko physically enters the hidden Mizore/Nozomi crisis, she carries literal dust on her clothing and Reina quietly removes it. The motif makes intervention bodily: seeing dirt and remaining clean is no longer possible once Kumiko actually enters the abandoned room where the unresolved relation is hiding.

### 4.8 Fireworks — Reina's ethic of non-escape meets Kumiko's pluralism

Reina interprets quitting in severe terms: `辞めるっていうのは逃げるってことやと思う`; she says she herself would not run and would defeat an intolerable situation with ability. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0912 / 「辞めるっていうのは逃げるってことやと思う。それが嫌な先輩からか、同級生からか、` This is internally consistent with V01 Reina. Conflict should be answered by becoming strong enough that social pressure cannot override musical fact.

Kumiko rejects the universalization internally. Nozomi made what seemed to her the best choice available; retroactively calling that escape feels cruel. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0208 / 逃げた、と麗奈は言った。部活を辞めるということは、逃げるということだと。だけど、` Importantly, Kumiko does **not** immediately challenge Reina with the full argument. V02 therefore establishes a durable ethical difference between them: Reina prefers resistance through ability; Kumiko is more willing to recognize withdrawal as a legitimate survival choice.

The same scene exposes Kumiko's own thought–speech gap at a more intimate level. She becomes frightened by the idea that her current privileged closeness with Reina will someday become ordinary past tense. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0923 / 怖いと思った。いまこの瞬間が、過去になってしまうことが。襲ってきた動揺を隠すよう` The fear is high stakes and difficult to claim. Rather than state it, she reaches for a safer topic—the planned pool outing—and feels relief at having found the conversational exit. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0931 / 無邪気に笑う緑輝の顔が瞼の裏側に映り込む。そういえば、プールのことを忘れていた。` `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0932 / 「あのさ、今度葉月と緑と一緒にプール行く約束してたんだ。麗奈も──」`

This is not regression from V01. Kumiko can now say `全国に行きたい`; she still struggles to make **attachment fear** equally claimable.

### 4.9 “別格” and the expansion of Kumiko's competitive desire

At home, Kumiko watches a powerhouse school and hears it described as `別格`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P1041 / 「こういう学校は別格だものね」` The category bothers her. V01 Kumiko could treat nationals as a distant slogan. V02 Kumiko increasingly resents a worldview in which established powers occupy a categorically different plane.

Her desire is still often internal rather than performative. She does not deliver a manifesto. But the object has changed: she wants Kitauji to stand in the same evaluative arena rather than pre-concede difference. This is **competitive agency without Reina's rhetoric of specialness**.

### 4.10 Nozomi's history — procedural reform arrives one year too late

Nozomi's account of the old band is the institutional core of V02. Serious students wanted an environment where effort had consequences. The previous advisor and upperclass norm instead emphasized playing together and protecting established seniority. When younger students objected that diligent players such as Kaori and Haruka were left in B while weaker seniors occupied A slots, they were accused of disturbing `秩序` and told to `空気読んで`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0149 / 「香織先輩とか小笠原さんとかさ、真面目にコツコツ練習してた二年生がＢやってんな。`

This is V01's morally ambiguous `空気` in its coercive form. Group cohesion is invoked not to make better ensemble music but to suppress criticism of a hierarchy.

Nozomi leaves. The cruelty is temporal: the next year Taki arrives and creates almost exactly the performance-sensitive institution the quitters had wanted. Nozomi can therefore look at current Kitauji and reasonably experience both pride and injury. Her counterfactual is not “I deserved nationals because I cared.” It is “the institution became responsive just after my chance had passed.”

This is why current procedural fairness does not exhaust justice.

### 4.11 Why Asuka became “special” to Nozomi

Asuka tried to stop Nozomi from quitting. Her language was abrasive: sacrificing oneself because of people who would soon graduate was stupid. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0176 / 「ひどいよなあ？わたしもさすがにムッとしてさ。『べつにアホちゃいます』って言った` She predicted Nozomi would eventually want to return and warned that if Nozomi's presence later harmed the band, she would block her.

Nozomi later admits that being stopped mattered. Asuka became `特別` because intervention meant somebody had judged Nozomi worth retaining. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0736 / 「あすか先輩は、特別なんです」`

This is a recurring V02 mechanism: **being selected, recognized, invited, or stopped becomes evidence of value**. Mizore's insecurity is organized around whether Nozomi selects her. Yuuko's hurt is organized around whether Mizore recognizes her. Kaori mattered to Yuuko because she selected Yuuko for encouragement during a bad year. Competition itself is a selection system. V02 repeatedly asks what happens when people use selection as proof that their effort or existence has been seen.

### 4.12 Nozomi's blind spot toward Mizore

Nozomi's account initially looks devastating from Mizore's perspective. She did not invite Mizore into the quitting plan and can barely remember the exact conversation around it. `わたしが声かける必要ないでしょ`, she reconstructs. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0168 / 「ん？いや、なんも大したこと言うてないよ。『わたしが声かける必要ないでしょ』とか`

Kumiko privately suspects jealousy because Mizore had made A. That suspicion is **[B/E] Kumiko's unspoken inference**, not established motive. V2 must not promote it into Nozomi's psychology without qualification.

The later direct explanation is different. Nozomi says Mizore had continued to practice alone even when nobody rewarded her; asking such a person to quit would have insulted that effort. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0369 / 「どうしてって、みぞれはずっとこの部活で頑張ってたやん。わたしが腐ってたときも、` This substantially weakens a jealousy-as-cause reading.

The key blind spot remains. Nozomi assumes her respect would be legible without communication. Mizore interprets omission through attachment hierarchy. Both are psychologically coherent.

### 4.13 Kumiko promises before she knows — a new strength and a new danger

After hearing Nozomi, Kumiko decides she is right and says she will investigate. She explicitly rejects the idea that leaving must have been cowardly. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0201 / 「言ってないですよ。希美先輩は正しいです」` `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0208 / 逃げた、と麗奈は言った。部活を辞めるということは、逃げるということだと。だけど、`

The intervention matters because V01 Kumiko often preserved safety through nonchoice. Here she knowingly enters an older-student conflict.

But the sequence also prevents celebratory overreading. She does not yet know Mizore's trauma, Asuka's institutional calculation, or the full reason for Nozomi's exclusion. Her willingness to help has outrun her information.

This becomes a foundational later leadership problem in miniature:

> **Empathy can motivate intervention before understanding is complete.**

V02 rewards Kumiko for refusing detached spectatorship while also teaching her that good intentions do not make a social situation simple.

### 4.14 Training camp — ensemble attunement is made through repetition

Taki opens camp by making material support explicit: parents are paying the activity fee; every minute in the facility is purchased time and should produce value. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0227 / 「この三日間、ここのホールはうちが貸し切っています。お金のことをあまり細かくは言` The remark grounds artistic aspiration in family resources rather than treating practice as costless abstraction.

The ten-run rehearsal is physically punishing. Repeating the complete program produces fatigue but also progressively embodied synchronization: entrances, conducting cues, and awkward transitions become felt rather than merely intellectually counted. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0231 / 顧問の台詞に、部員たちの顔がわずかに強張った。十回通しとはその名のとおり、課題曲`

The afternoon work is deliberately unglamorous: pitch, rhythm, balance, dynamics, melodic shape, tone, repeated until stable. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0264 / 午後からの練習で滝が指導したことのなかに、特別なことはひとつもなかった。ただ不充` V02 therefore extends the V01 “embodied competence creates desire” thesis into a second principle:

> **Established excellence is repeatability, not the occasional miracle.**

That principle becomes decisive at Kansai.

### 4.15 Mizore's theory of competition — fairness as impossible objectivity

Mizore tells Kumiko directly: `私、コンクール嫌い`; when asked why, `不平等だから`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0381 / 「私、コンクール嫌い」` `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0383 / 「不平等だから」` Her reasoning is sophisticated rather than merely trauma projection. `音楽を正しく評価するのって、無理だと思う`; judges have preferences. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0385 / 「音楽を正しく評価するのって、無理だと思う」`

Technique attracts her because technical difficulty and visible error seem easier to score. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0390 / 「だから、技術が重要」` Her rhythm-game competence echoes this desire for legible criteria: if the system specifies the correct input, she can produce it.

Yet she cannot reduce musical judgment to such a system, and she cannot emotionally dismiss that fact as `仕方ない`. By P0408 she says she no longer knows why she continues. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0408 / 「もう、何もわからない」`

This is the exact point at which “technical perfection without reason to speak” becomes more than a metaphor. Technique has become both achievement and refuge.

### 4.16 Hashimoto — students are musicians, not practice machines

Hashimoto's criticism of Mizore is unusually direct. Her tone and pitch are excellent, but the solo bores him; it sounds robotic. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0461 / 「いや、問題っちゅー問題はないよ？音も綺麗やし、ピッチも安定してる。高校生にして` `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0463 / 「ぶっちゃけ、君のソロめちゃくちゃつまらんねん。ロボットが吹いてるみたいや」` He insists that if reproducing notes were sufficient, a machine could do it. Human performance requires an expressive remainder.

His larger critique of Kitauji is similar: technical progress has brought them near the established schools, but they need to understand what kind of music they are making rather than merely execute instructions. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0480 / 「君らには表現力が足りてへん。明工とか秀大附属とか大阪東照とか、そういう超強豪校`

This is not a rejection of technique. It is the next developmental layer after V01's technical reconstruction.

### 4.17 Niiyama — the adult corrects her own ceiling

Niiyama later apologizes to Mizore. She realizes she had unconsciously accepted “excellent for a high-school student” as a ceiling and thereby limited what she asked from Mizore. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0747 / 「あなたのソロを聞いたときにね、私も橋本先生と同じように少し物足りないなと感じた` This is one of the volume's strongest pedagogical moments because authority models fallibility rather than merely issuing correction.

Her final instruction is not “work harder”: playing is not an obligation; Mizore should allow herself to enjoy and love the oboe. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0753 / 「こんなこと言われても困っちゃうかもしれないけれど、これだけは言わせてほしい。楽`

Hashimoto and Niiyama therefore converge from different directions. One refuses technically perfect emptiness; the other refuses protective under-expectation. Both treat the student as an artist capable of authorship.

### 4.18 Asuka's private euphonium — hidden authorship

Asuka wakes at four in the morning even outside camp. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0725 / 「いや、うちは普段四時には起きてるから。合宿中は暇でしゃあなくてさ」` Kumiko encounters her playing a beautiful piece alone. Asked what it is, Asuka answers only `秘密`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0732 / 「秘密」`

V01 already suggested a gap between public theatrical Asuka and private musician. V02 strengthens it. The private music is not used to win a room, recruit a student, solve a dispute, or support the institution. It exists outside the social utility through which Asuka normally presents herself.

For simulation, this is important negative evidence against a caricature of Asuka as purely strategic. **She has a private aesthetic life she does not automatically convert into social capital.**

### 4.19 Reina's jealousy — declarative ethics do not eliminate uncertainty

Reina becomes deeply unsettled by the possibility that Taki and Niiyama may be romantically involved. She admits she has not organized her feelings yet. `まだ心の整理がついてへん`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0581 / 「まだ心の整理がついてへん」` She fears asking directly because the answer could reject the structure she wants to believe in.

This is almost a mirror of Kumiko's desire-deniability problem, but in a narrower domain. Reina can tell a room that she is better than Kaori; she cannot simply force herself to ask whether the adult she loves is available.

Kumiko's response is relationally active. She takes Reina's hand, listens, and encourages direct clarification. V02 therefore confirms that their bond is not merely Reina “awakening” Kumiko. Kumiko is increasingly able to regulate Reina in domains where Reina is less competent.

### 4.20 Taki's grief — information, not full causal explanation

Hashimoto reveals that Taki's wife died and that Taki subsequently withdrew from music for a period. He and Niiyama worried about him. When Taki later took the Kitauji position and invited them to teach, Hashimoto experienced it as evidence that he was returning. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0630 / 「滝クンね、吹奏楽の顧問経験はこの高校に来るまでなかったんやけど、補佐とか副顧問`

This is important context but must remain epistemically disciplined. Hashimoto explicitly says he does not know what triggered Taki's decision to take the job. V2 can therefore say:

- Taki suffered bereavement;
- he withdrew from music;
- friends interpret his Kitauji work as renewed engagement;

but should **not** claim that “teaching Kitauji is definitively his grief-recovery project” as a settled causal fact.

The earlier line in which Taki says he has neither wife nor children and therefore little besides work acquires painful context, but Kumiko's feeling that something sounded false remains her perception. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P1001 / 「私には妻も子供もいませんからね。仕事ぐらいしかやることがないんですよ」`

### 4.21 “Everyone is right” — Kumiko encounters irreducible moral plurality

After gathering competing accounts, Kumiko thinks: Nozomi is not wrong; Asuka is not wrong; probably everyone is right, which leaves her unable to know what to do. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0644 / 希美は間違っていないと久美子は思う。でも、あすかだって間違っていない。たぶん、き`

This is one of the series' clearest local statements of pluralism. It does **not** mean every factual belief is correct. Mizore's belief about why Nozomi omitted her will later be revised. It means several people can have morally intelligible claims that cannot all be satisfied simultaneously.

Kumiko's response is decisive: rather than retreat to neutrality, she decides she must finally ask Mizore. Her future social power begins in this willingness to tolerate the possibility that listening will not produce a simple winner.

### 4.22 Yuuko's theory of effort — results are psychologically powerful but not ontological

Yuuko initially approaches Kumiko with surprising ordinary kindness, buys her a drink, and explains why Kaori matters so intensely to her. During the old crisis, Kaori had encouraged Yuuko to stay. Yuuko's later “faith” in Kaori is therefore not arbitrary idolization; it is relational debt and memory.

Her account of competition is equally important. A good result can make years of effort feel meaningful; a bad result can make a person hate the system. Yet she rejects the proposition that failure means the effort never existed. She lists contingent factors—direction, repertoire, judgment—and asks whether unsuccessful labor should therefore be treated as unreal.

Her strongest institutional point concerns the seniors. They endured a seniority regime believing their own turn would eventually come; when it did, Taki changed the system to performance selection. Kaori consequently reached her final year only to lose the solo to a first-year. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0710 / 「そうそう。やからさ、今年の三年生が滝先生に反発した理由もよくわかんねんな。やっ`

Yuuko still concedes that if Kitauji is serious about nationals, Reina should play the solo. This combination makes her one of the volume's most useful ethical voices:

> **A decision can be procedurally correct and still wound someone for historically intelligible reasons.**

### 4.23 Natsuki's guilt — spectatorship is not morally empty

Natsuki reveals that she joined partly because the old club looked easy, yet she admired Nozomi precisely because Nozomi could care enough to suffer. In the prior-year conflict Natsuki sometimes joined the verbal fighting but ultimately remained while Nozomi's group left. She later calls her current effort to help Nozomi `罪滅ぼし`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0098 / 「罪滅ぼし、したかったから」`

This is a direct longitudinal pressure on Kumiko's V01 observer model. Natsuki is a future image of what passive observation can become: surviving the conflict does not mean one feels innocent afterward.

Her behavior toward Yuuko also shows her care style. Their interaction is abrasive, competitive, teasing, and physically energetic; yet when Yuuko despairs that Nozomi will always outrank her for Mizore, Natsuki reminds her that Mizore might not have endured the year without Yuuko. The reassurance is specific rather than sentimental.

### 4.24 Kumiko, Reina, and theories of judgment

Before Kansai, Kumiko asks Reina what she thinks of competition. Reina says that “competition doesn't matter” sounds legitimate from a winner but like sour grapes from someone who lost. Her answer to subjective judging is to become so good that recognition is overwhelming. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0126 / 「もし圧倒的な上手さがあれば、コンクールで評価されへんなんてことはありえへんと思`

This differs from Mizore without simply refuting her. Mizore seeks criteria that cannot betray her. Reina accepts the existence of preference but treats exceptional skill as a robustness strategy.

Kumiko says she basically agrees. Yet later she experiences ugly wishes that rival schools fail, and afterward empathizes with a substitute soloist from an opponent. Her own theory is therefore less doctrinal. She learns competition through morally mixed participation.

### 4.25 The Mizore crisis — fear of “reality”

When Nozomi appears unexpectedly, Mizore runs. In the abandoned film-club room, Kumiko finally asks the direct question: does she hate Nozomi?

`嫌いじゃない`. `全然、違う`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0267 / 「嫌いじゃない」`

Mizore says Nozomi is not at fault; the fault is hers. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0273 / 「違う。希美は、悪くない。悪いのは、全部私」` She is afraid to meet Nozomi because meeting her will make “reality” undeniable. The reality is relational asymmetry.

Nozomi was the person who invited her into music. Mizore reconstructs the bond as singular: Nozomi was her best friend, while she was only one of Nozomi's many friends. She joined high-school band partly because she feared their connection would disappear without the shared activity. Practice became relational insurance: if she remained technically necessary, perhaps Nozomi would continue to need her.

This is why Mizore's superb technique is psychologically double-sided. It is genuine ability and labor, but some of its compulsion is organized around preventing abandonment.

### 4.26 Yuuko names the relation Mizore has refused to count

Yuuko's entrance changes the crisis because she refuses Mizore's self-description. Mizore says she has only Nozomi and fears rejection. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0320 / 「だって、私には希美しかいない。あの子がいなきゃ、何もできない。だから、拒絶され` Yuuko asks whether the first person who saved her is really the only friend who counts. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0324 / 「なあ、アンタの友達はほんまに希美だけ？最初にアンタを救い出してくれたやつだけが` Then she asks the unavoidable question: `うちは？アンタにとって、うちはなんなん？` `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0326 / 「うちは？アンタにとって、うちはなんなん？」`

Mizore's defensive answer is that Yuuko is kind out of pity. Yuuko reacts angrily because this explanation erases her agency. She did not spend a year accompanying Mizore accidentally.

The exchange is one of the strongest character-model lessons in the volume: **Mizore's abandonment schema causes her to discount evidence of chosen attachment when that attachment does not come from Nozomi.** Yuuko's intervention makes alternative relational evidence impossible to ignore.

When Mizore admits that qualifying for Kansai did make her happy but also guilty toward the students who quit, Yuuko gives immediate permission: she is allowed to be happy. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0339 / 「……うれしかった、本当は。でも、それと同じくらい、辞めていった子たちに申し訳な` This does not erase historical guilt; it refuses to let guilt monopolize present experience.

### 4.27 Nozomi and Mizore — explanation repairs causality, not symmetry

The direct conversation supplies the correction Mizore could not generate alone. Asked why she was not invited to quit, Nozomi explains that Mizore had kept practicing even when nobody rewarded her; inviting her to leave would have been disrespectful. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0369 / 「どうしてって、みぞれはずっとこの部活で頑張ってたやん。わたしが腐ってたときも、`

This is a profound revision because the same event changes category:

- Mizore had read omission as **proof of dispensability**;
- Nozomi describes omission as **recognition of persistence**.

The relationship still remains asymmetric. The narration through Kumiko explicitly notes that the heat differs between them, and Nozomi's light apology can close an event that occupied far more psychic space for Mizore. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0375 / 焦ったように告げる希美の声は、どこまでも軽やかで美しかった。互いに対する熱量が、` Kumiko also suspects Nozomi may never fully know the scale of Mizore's feeling.

Yet Nozomi does provide something Mizore needs: she says she saw the Kyoto competition, admired Mizore's solo, and has liked Mizore's oboe sound since middle school. She expects to hear a good performance.

Mizore then asks Nozomi to listen.

The repair is therefore neither “equal love” nor “Mizore learns she was wrong to care.” It is **a usable relation rebuilt on more accurate information**.

### 4.28 The oboe awakens because technique receives an addressee

After the conversation, Mizore's playing changes audibly. Yuuko later summarizes the mechanism: Mizore had always been playing for Nozomi. Her prior inability to access that relation directly had left the sound constricted.

Before the Kansai performance, Mizore says: `私、希美のために吹く。だから、聞いてて`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0594 / 「私、希美のために吹く。だから、聞いてて」` The sentence is simple because Mizore's voice is simple. Its importance lies in explicit addressee assignment.

This is not an argument that artists must play “for someone else.” Reina immediately provides the countermodel: she generally plays for herself. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0491 / 「そんなん、考えたことない。上手くなるのが楽しいから吹いてるだけやし。まあ、強い` V02's stronger claim is plural:

> **Expression becomes possible when a performer knows what relation to the act is actually hers.**

For Mizore that relation is interpersonal. For Reina it is substantially self-directed. For Kumiko, ensemble belonging and the desire to be heard increasingly matter.

### 4.29 Asuka's “shock therapy” — adaptive management and moral reduction

After the accidental encounter makes concealment impossible, Asuka changes strategy and facilitates the direct confrontation. She later calls the result a kind of shock treatment and credits Yuuko heavily.

Her explanation of Mizore is deliberately colder than Kumiko's. She describes Mizore as afraid of being alone and Yuuko as a kind of `保険`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0459 / 「みぞれちゃんが希美ちゃんに固執してんのってさ、結局独りが怖いからってだけっぽい` Functionally, the model captures something real: Yuuko's reliable presence lowers the catastrophic stakes of Nozomi's possible rejection.

But Kumiko rejects the reduction because it turns a relationship into risk management. This disagreement matters. V2 should preserve both levels:

- **Asuka is often excellent at functional social prediction.**
- **Functional prediction can underdescribe what a relation means to the people living it.**

Asuka is therefore neither emotion-blind nor omniscient. She is a strategic interpreter whose compression can become ethically abrasive.

### 4.30 Reina after the crisis — intimate care is practical and bodily

Reina waits for Kumiko after the Mizore crisis. She notices dust on Kumiko's clothing and removes it. The gesture is minor and therefore highly useful for simulation: Reina's care does not always become a speech about care.

When Kumiko asks whom she plays for, Reina says she has never really thought about it; she plays because getting better is fun, and if forced to name someone, `アタシはアタシのために楽器吹いてるかな`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0491 / 「そんなん、考えたことない。上手くなるのが楽しいから吹いてるだけやし。まあ、強い`

Before the performance she playfully revises that answer: `アタシも、久美子のために吹こうかな`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0600 / 「アタシも、久美子のために吹こうかな」` She jokes that playing for Taki might make the music too emotionally sticky and settles on Kumiko.

This does not mean Kumiko replaces self-directed musicianship. It shows that Reina can temporarily designate an intimate addressee **without surrendering the underlying self-authored ethic**.

### 4.31 Taki at Kansai — judgment without ontological ranking

Before the performance, Taki explicitly tells the students not to be burdened by Kitauji's historical reputation. Treat this as the school's first Kansai appearance. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0545 / 「私は皆さんがそんな過去を気にする必要はないと思っています。関西大会、初出場。そ` The move is psychologically consistent with his broader program: current practice, not inherited status, should determine current action.

His account of evaluation is equally disciplined. If Kitauji loses, that does not mean its music is inferior in an absolute sense; it means other schools received higher evaluations from the judges. If Kitauji advances, it receives another opportunity to present its music. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0547 / 「確かに、周りはどこも強豪校ばかりです。何年もこの舞台に立って、全国大会に進んで`

This does not eliminate competition. It places the result in the correct epistemic category.

### 4.32 Asuka's rally — shadow leadership becomes public but still transfers ritual

Asuka uses humor to clear the pre-performance tension and then gives one of the volume's most emotionally explicit ensemble speeches. She acknowledges how improbable their progress has been, credits Taki, and refuses the comforting position that reaching Kansai is enough.

Yet she does not seize the formal closing ritual. She hands the final cheer back to Haruka.

The scene captures the V02 leadership division precisely:

- Asuka can diagnose and transform the emotional room;
- Haruka holds formal collective legitimacy;
- the band benefits because those functions can coexist rather than because one leader replaces the other.

### 4.33 Kitauji's “miracle” and the established powers' stability

During Kitauji's performance, Kumiko experiences a rare state in which individual parts, conductor cues, Mizore's changed oboe, and Reina's exceptional cornet playing cohere. She wants the performance not to end. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0640 / たぶん、いまのこの演奏は奇跡みたいなものだ、と久美子は思った。十回に一回とか、百` `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0641 / 百回に一回起こる演奏が、いままさに、この関西大会というふさわしい舞台で行われてい` The desire has expanded beyond winning: **she wants this shared sound to continue and to reach listeners.**

Then she hears an established powerhouse and identifies the difference: `安定感`. They do not need the rare perfect convergence Kitauji has just experienced. They can repeatedly produce excellence. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0711 / 強豪校とその他の学校の差はどこか。それを考えたとき、久美子はいつも同じ答えを導き`

This is one of the series' most valuable craft observations. High performance is not only ceiling; it is variance control.

### 4.34 The rival substitute — morally crowded competition becomes concrete

Kumiko privately wishes for rivals to fail, recognizes the ugliness of the wish, and still cannot simply abolish it. Only three schools advance. Owned desire under scarcity produces morally unattractive thoughts.

A rival ensemble then suffers a conspicuous solo error, but the group recovers immediately. Kumiko later overhears the substitute player's circumstances: an injured senior, a last-minute burden, guilt, and reassurance from the senior.

The encounter breaks any fantasy that competitors are anonymous obstacles. Every school arrives with injuries, labor, internal relationships, unequal circumstances, and the same desire to continue.

Kumiko does not become less competitive. She becomes better able to hold **competitive desire and recognition of the competitor's humanity at the same time**.

### 4.35 Daphnis becomes listenable

Nozomi, Yuuko, and Mizore hear material connected to the painful South Middle past. Earlier, the association had functioned as trauma. Now Yuuko can admire the music, and Mizore can agree.

This is an elegant formal example of the volume's central temporal claim. The past is not changed. The same musical object can acquire a new present relation because the people hearing it have changed.

### 4.36 The epilogue — loving competition “just now”

Kitauji receives gold. The final national-representative announcement names Kitauji, and Kumiko's body reacts before analysis: she screams; Reina embraces her. The result is not merely an institutional win. It confirms that the ensemble's rare performance survived external evaluation.

Kumiko then returns to the volume's opening question and asks Mizore whether she still hates competition. Mizore smiles and says she began liking it **just now**. `HIBIKE-V02 / S05 / P0041 / 「……たったいま、好きになった」`

The temporal adverb matters. Her answer is deliberately nonessentialist. Competition was not secretly good all along. The current experience has changed what it means to her.

## 5. Character-state extraction

### 5.1 Oumae Kumiko — `KUMIKO@V02`

V02 confirms the V01 model while adding a major new variable: **intervention appetite**.

Kumiko now wants outcomes strongly enough to enter other people's problems. She rejects Reina's reductive “escape” account of Nozomi, makes a promise to investigate, interrogates multiple perspectives, searches for Mizore, and remains physically present through the confrontation.

Her risk profile changes accordingly. In V01 the central danger was refusing ownership. In V02, the new danger is **acting on partial knowledge**.

Her thought–speech gap remains robust:

- she fears her special present with Reina becoming past, then changes the subject to the pool rather than naming the fear; `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0923 / 怖いと思った。いまこの瞬間が、過去になってしまうことが。襲ってきた動揺を隠すよう` `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0931 / 無邪気に笑う緑輝の顔が瞼の裏側に映り込む。そういえば、プールのことを忘れていた。`
- she privately resents the idea that powerhouse schools are categorically `別格`, but does not turn the thought into public bravado;
- she privately wishes competitors would fail and judges herself for it;
- she can suspect motives—such as jealousy in Nozomi—without presenting them as facts to the people involved.

Her moral development is therefore **not increased purity**. She becomes more capable of holding morally crowded states without escaping them: wanting Kitauji to win, sympathizing with rivals, believing Nozomi has a claim, believing Asuka has a reason, and recognizing that none of this produces a painless solution.

Musically, Kumiko's desire deepens from result orientation into public expression. During the Kansai performance she wants the sound to continue and be heard. Her relationship to music is increasingly collective and communicative rather than merely evaluative.

**Negative constraints after V02:**

- Do not write Kumiko as a fully transparent communicator; intimate fear still often becomes deflection.
- Do not write her as an impartial therapist; curiosity, self-interest, discomfort with unresolved tension, and affection all contribute to intervention.
- Do not write her as conflict-phobic to the point of passivity; she increasingly enters situations others avoid.
- Do not treat her focalized interpretations as objective character facts.

### 5.2 Kousaka Reina — `REINA@V02`

Reina's model becomes strongly domain-conditioned.

**Music / competition:** severe, declarative, self-authoring. She dislikes “escape,” believes overwhelming ability is the best answer to disputed judgment, and primarily plays because improvement itself is pleasurable. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0126 / 「もし圧倒的な上手さがあれば、コンクールで評価されへんなんてことはありえへんと思` `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0491 / 「そんなん、考えたことない。上手くなるのが楽しいから吹いてるだけやし。まあ、強い`

**Attachment / invitation:** vulnerable, embarrassed, capable of reassurance-seeking. Inviting Kumiko to fireworks flusters her. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0150 / 「じゃあさ、アタシと行かへん？」` `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0157 / 「あんまこういうの慣れてへんの！誘うのとか！」` Her uncertainty about Taki/Niiyama produces indecision she would find unacceptable in a musical claim.

**Care:** often practical and embodied rather than conventionally soothing. She waits for Kumiko, notices physical details, removes dust, offers touch, and can make a jokingly intimate promise to play for Kumiko.

**Negative constraint:** Reina's directness is not a generic conversational setting. She can become avoidant when the feared answer concerns her own attachment rather than an externally judgeable musical question.

### 5.3 Yoroizuka Mizore — `MIZORE@V02`

Mizore is one of V02's most information-dense models.

- **Speech:** sparse, standard Japanese, often exact and literal; intense affect can remain almost entirely internal.
- **Control strategy:** technique and repeatability provide criteria less vulnerable to subjective rejection.
- **Core relational threat:** being unnecessary to Nozomi / discovering the attachment is not reciprocal at the same scale.
- **Practice function:** genuine discipline plus relational security behavior; musical necessity becomes a way to preserve connection.
- **Competition theory:** rejects the fantasy of objective musical evaluation.
- **Embodied trauma:** Nozomi's flute can trigger nausea and flight before deliberate reasoning.
- **Repair condition:** accurate explanation + recognition + another dependable attachment + explicit addressee for performance.

Yuuko does not replace Nozomi. Nozomi remains first. The important change is that Mizore can no longer honestly claim she has nobody else.

**Negative constraints:** do not generate Mizore as verbally therapeutic or broadly sociable. Do not infer that `たったいま、好きになった` means her structural criticism of judging vanished.

### 5.4 Kasaki Nozomi — `NOZOMI@V02`

Nozomi is socially expansive, energetic, musically serious, and capable of direct institutional challenge. Her injury comes from **mistimed reform and frustrated recognition**, not from lack of resilience.

She has genuine blind spots. Because she possesses many social ties, she underestimates the degree to which Mizore organized her world around one bond. She assumes that respect can be obvious without being verbally specified.

Her failure to invite Mizore to quit should currently be modeled from her direct explanation—respect for Mizore's persistence—not from Kumiko's unspoken jealousy hypothesis. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0369 / 「どうしてって、みぞれはずっとこの部活で頑張ってたやん。わたしが腐ってたときも、`

Nozomi's affection is real. It is simply not exclusive at Mizore's scale.

### 5.5 Yoshikawa Yuuko — `YUUKO@V02`

V02 sharply expands Yuuko beyond V01's confrontational Kaori loyalist.

Her care is **high-intensity, action-first, and willing to be abrasive**. She runs toward the crisis, grabs Nozomi, confronts Mizore's self-erasing account, buys Kumiko a drink, and refuses to let people she loves narrate themselves as unimportant.

Her competition ethics are more flexible than her temperament suggests. She understands why results matter psychologically, refuses to equate failure with erased effort, recognizes historical unfairness toward seniors, and nevertheless accepts Reina's solo when nationals is the stated goal.

Her hostile banter with Natsuki is affiliative rather than evidence of simple dislike. V02 makes their relation legible as a form of care that often travels through insult, challenge, and mock competition.

### 5.6 Nakagawa Natsuki — `NATSUKI@V02`

Natsuki's self-understanding is organized by delayed responsibility. She once valued the old club partly because it was easy, yet admired Nozomi precisely for possessing the seriousness she lacked. She now interprets helping Nozomi as `罪滅ぼし`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0098 / 「罪滅ぼし、したかったから」`

Her care style remains unsentimental. She does not overstate her virtue and does not require Yuuko to stop being difficult before reassuring her.

**Simulation constraint:** a caring Natsuki should often still sound dry, teasing, or self-deprecating rather than suddenly becoming solemnly nurturing.

### 5.7 Tanaka Asuka — `ASUKA@V02`

V02 strengthens V01's “selective responsibility” model.

- She blocks Nozomi because she predicts real risk to Mizore and the ensemble.
- She withholds the cruelest explanation rather than weaponize it against Nozomi.
- Once the accidental encounter makes avoidance impossible, she changes strategy rapidly.
- She can reduce relationships to functional systems (`保険`) in ways Kumiko finds morally ugly. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0459 / 「みぞれちゃんが希美ちゃんに固執してんのってさ、結局独りが怖いからってだけっぽい`
- She can manage a room publicly and maintain a private musical self nobody else owns.
- She is willing to intervene when competence/ensemble survival is implicated while claiming indifference to conflicts she categorizes as irrelevant.

Her statement that she tried to stop Nozomi because Nozomi was useful/skilled should remain **open in tone**: it may be wholly sincere, strategically reductive, or both. Later evidence must adjudicate rather than V02 forcing a hidden-softness explanation.

### 5.8 Taki Noboru — `TAKI@V02`

Taki's public pedagogy becomes more epistemically careful as competition approaches. He distinguishes judges' evaluation from absolute musical worth. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0547 / 「確かに、周りはどこも強豪校ばかりです。何年もこの舞台に立って、全国大会に進んで` He also refuses inherited prestige mythology and tells Kitauji to treat Kansai as a first appearance. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0545 / 「私は皆さんがそんな過去を気にする必要はないと思っています。関西大会、初出場。そ`

His rehearsal model values expensive time, repetition, recording/playback, diagnostic precision, and stable execution.

His private history is partially exposed through Hashimoto: bereavement and withdrawal from music are direct reported facts within Hashimoto's testimony; the exact reason Taki returned through Kitauji remains unknown. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0630 / 「滝クンね、吹奏楽の顧問経験はこの高校に来るまでなかったんやけど、補佐とか副顧問`

### 5.9 Hashimoto Masahiro — `HASHIMOTO@V02`

Hashimoto uses humor and colloquial Kansai speech to lower social barriers while issuing unusually severe artistic judgments. His core pedagogical demand is authorship: students must decide what they are expressing rather than merely reproduce notation.

He also provides adult relational context the students cannot see, particularly around Taki.

### 5.10 Niiyama Satomi — `NIIYAMA@V02`

Niiyama models self-correcting authority. She notices that her own protective adult expectation—“excellent for a high-schooler”—became a ceiling. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0747 / 「あなたのソロを聞いたときにね、私も橋本先生と同じように少し物足りないなと感じた` She apologizes and asks more from Mizore while simultaneously reminding her that music is not obligation. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0753 / 「こんなこと言われても困っちゃうかもしれないけれど、これだけは言わせてほしい。楽`

### 5.11 Tsukamoto Shuuichi — `SHUUICHI@V02`

Shuuichi's V01 ordinary-intimacy role persists. He listens to competition repertoire privately, practices, notices Kumiko's anxiety, and responds with practical reassurance rather than elaborate emotional analysis. His low-drama familiarity gives Kumiko a relational mode distinct from the heightened symbolic intimacy she experiences with Reina.

### 5.12 Kawashima Midori and Katou Hazuki

Midori's emotional brightness continues to coexist with technical confidence and situational intelligence. She can admire excellent rivals without experiencing their quality as a personal threat.

Hazuki remains outside the competition ensemble but not outside the band. Her support foregrounds a crucial institutional fact: participation and contribution exceed stage selection.

## 6. Relationship-state extraction

### 6.1 Kumiko ↔ Reina — reciprocity becomes clearer

V01 established privileged disclosure. V02 adds evidence of **reciprocal regulation**.

Reina invites Kumiko despite embarrassment; shares insecurity about Taki; waits for her after the Mizore crisis; removes dust; plays with the idea of dedicating performance to her. Kumiko in turn holds Reina's hand, coaches her through uncertainty, and fears losing the relationship precisely because it has become unusually important.

The relationship is not symmetric in personality, but it is increasingly reciprocal in function: each can access a vulnerability the other's public persona does not advertise.

The category of the bond should remain textually disciplined. Romantic coding/intimacy can be analyzed; a final categorical label should not be forced beyond what the prose establishes at this point.

### 6.2 Mizore ↔ Nozomi — unequal centrality becomes explicit

This dyad must preserve two simultaneous truths:

- Nozomi genuinely values Mizore and her music.
- Mizore assigns Nozomi far greater exclusivity and existential centrality than Nozomi assigns Mizore.

The crisis is created by treating the second truth as evidence that the first is false. The reconciliation corrects that inference without eliminating the asymmetry.

### 6.3 Mizore ↔ Yuuko — from uncounted support to explicit anchor

Yuuko has been present for a year, but Mizore has psychologically discounted the bond because it is not Nozomi. Yuuko forces explicit recognition by asking what she is to Mizore. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0326 / 「うちは？アンタにとって、うちはなんなん？」`

Afterward Yuuko still knows she cannot “beat” Nozomi in Mizore's ranking. The achievement is not rank reversal. It is acknowledged nonzero relational security.

### 6.4 Natsuki ↔ Yuuko — antagonistic surface, affiliative mechanism

Their joking hostility is a stable interactional style. Competition and insult can coexist with accurate observation and support. A simulation should therefore avoid translating every insult into literal enmity or every caring moment into sudden softness.

### 6.5 Kumiko ↔ Asuka — observer begins to challenge the strategist

Kumiko increasingly seeks Asuka's reasoning rather than merely watching her. She remains fascinated by Asuka's competence but begins to reject some of Asuka's moral compressions, particularly the “Yuuko as insurance” formulation.

This is an important power shift. Kumiko is not Asuka's equal, but she is less content to let Asuka's framing settle the meaning of a relationship.

### 6.6 Kumiko ↔ Shuuichi — ordinary co-regulation

V02 maintains the mundane familiarity established in V01. Shuuichi's reassurance works because it is embedded in history and routine, not because he suddenly gains privileged access to every private fear. This relation should remain lower-theatricality than Kumiko/Reina.

### 6.7 Kitauji ↔ former members

The most important “relationship” may be institutional. Current Kitauji benefits from changes that former members wanted but did not receive. Nozomi's wish to return exposes the question of whether an institution owes anything beyond present fairness to people injured by its prior norms.

V02 provides no comprehensive restorative procedure. It makes the absence visible.

## 7. Japanese voice and register findings

### 7.1 Kumiko

V01's standard-Japanese baseline survives. V02 adds a clearer simulation rule: **Kumiko's most important information is frequently distributed across internal narration, involuntary action, and safer substitute speech rather than direct declaration.**

High-stakes attachment fear → internal elaboration → conversational pivot is directly observable in the fireworks/pool transition. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0923 / 怖いと思った。いまこの瞬間が、過去になってしまうことが。襲ってきた動揺を隠すよう` `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0931 / 無邪気に笑う緑輝の顔が瞼の裏側に映り込む。そういえば、プールのことを忘れていた。`

She can become direct when another person's crisis gives her a role—asking Mizore whether she hates Nozomi—but is substantially less direct when the vulnerable proposition concerns herself.

### 7.2 Reina

Reina continues to use `アタシ` and Kansai forms. Her syntax tends toward strong declaratives in evaluative domains. Yet invitation scenes include embarrassment, abrupt defensive exclamation, and visible loss of composure. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0150 / 「じゃあさ、アタシと行かへん？」` `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0157 / 「あんまこういうの慣れてへんの！誘うのとか！」`

Her voice should therefore be conditioned by **domain and addressee**, not reduced to “blunt Kansai girl.”

### 7.3 Mizore

Mizore uses standard Japanese despite the Kyoto environment. Her turns are often extremely short: `さあ、知らない`; `大嫌い`; `嫌いじゃない`; `全然、違う`. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0345 / 「さあ、知らない」` `HIBIKE-V02 / S01 / P0048 / 「大嫌い」` `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0267 / 「嫌いじゃない」`

When enough safety exists, she can produce longer explanatory speech, but her characteristic mode is compressed literal statement with long silence around it.

### 7.4 Nozomi

Nozomi's Kansai speech is fluid, socially expansive, and comparatively high-turn-length. She narrates institutional history readily, externalizes frustration, and uses casual language even when discussing emotionally consequential events. This linguistic lightness contributes to the perception gap with Mizore: Nozomi can sincerely apologize without reproducing Mizore's emotional weight.

### 7.5 Yuuko

Yuuko's Kansai speech is confrontational, affectively high-volume, and heavily oriented toward direct address. Her caring interventions do not necessarily soften the register. `うちは？アンタにとって、うちはなんなん？` is both accusation and attachment claim. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0326 / 「うちは？アンタにとって、うちはなんなん？」`

### 7.6 Natsuki

Natsuki's Kansai voice remains dry, ironic, and low-prestige in self-presentation. She often communicates care through teasing or blunt practical language rather than elevated sincerity.

### 7.7 Asuka

Asuka remains the most register-mobile major student. She can use theatrical brightness, technical explanation, blunt refusal (`無理や`), strategic understatement, and private minimalism (`秘密`) depending on function. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0548 / 「無理や」` `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0732 / 「秘密」`

The V02 addition is that **register mobility accompanies information control**. She not only changes tone; she controls which truth becomes available to which person and at what time.

### 7.8 Hashimoto and Niiyama

Hashimoto's colloquial Kansai and joking self-presentation lower hierarchy before severe critique. Niiyama's pedagogical language is gentler and self-reflective; her apology to Mizore openly assigns error to herself rather than hiding behind teacher authority. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0747 / 「あなたのソロを聞いたときにね、私も橋本先生と同じように少し物足りないなと感じた`

## 8. Focalization and epistemic audit

V02 uses perspective to demonstrate why relationship analysis cannot rely on one account.

### 8.1 Mizore's beliefs are evidence of Mizore

Mizore believes Nozomi's omission proves she was unimportant. That belief is psychologically decisive but factually incomplete. Nozomi later gives another reason. The novel therefore teaches the analysis not to equate subjective pain with accurate causal inference.

### 8.2 Kumiko's jealousy hypothesis about Nozomi is not established fact

Kumiko privately considers whether Nozomi excluded Mizore because Mizore made A. The clean text later supplies a direct competing explanation from Nozomi. Therefore jealousy must remain downgraded from “likely motive” to **possible Kumiko inference with weak corroboration**.

### 8.3 Asuka's social models are powerful but compressed

Asuka's “Yuuko as insurance” model predicts the stabilizing function of the relationship but does not exhaust its meaning. Kumiko's moral discomfort is itself evidence that the novel invites resistance to purely instrumental social explanation.

### 8.4 Hashimoto's Taki account is testimony, not omniscience

The wife's death and withdrawal from music are reported as known history; Hashimoto explicitly does not know what prompted Taki's return. V2 must preserve that boundary.

### 8.5 “Everyone is right” is Kumiko's pluralist realization, not proof every interpretation is true

Kumiko's thought at P0644 captures moral intelligibility, not factual equivalence. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0644 / 希美は間違っていないと久美子は思う。でも、あすかだって間違っていない。たぶん、き` This distinction becomes essential for later modeling: people can deserve empathy while still being mistaken.

## 9. Music, rehearsal, pedagogy, and performance

V02 makes musical craft even more causally central than V01.

### 9.1 Repetition produces embodied synchronization

The ten-run practice is not described as inspirational. It is exhausting, repetitive, and effective because the ensemble begins to feel entrances and cues collectively. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0231 / 顧問の台詞に、部員たちの顔がわずかに強張った。十回通しとはその名のとおり、課題曲`

### 9.2 Stability is the real marker of established excellence

Kumiko's “miracle” concept distinguishes a high ceiling from reliable mastery. Kitauji can produce an extraordinary performance under ideal convergence; elite schools can produce excellent performances repeatedly. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0640 / たぶん、いまのこの演奏は奇跡みたいなものだ、と久美子は思った。十回に一回とか、百` `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0711 / 強豪校とその他の学校の差はどこか。それを考えたとき、久美子はいつも同じ答えを導き`

This should feed future technical analysis: “stronger” is partly a distribution of probable performances, not just a single best sample.

### 9.3 Expression is not the opposite of technique

Hashimoto does not ask Mizore to become less technically excellent. He says technique alone is insufficient because humans are not playback machines. Niiyama likewise asks Mizore to enjoy the instrument after recognizing that she had unconsciously capped her potential. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0463 / 「ぶっちゃけ、君のソロめちゃくちゃつまらんねん。ロボットが吹いてるみたいや」` `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0753 / 「こんなこと言われても困っちゃうかもしれないけれど、これだけは言わせてほしい。楽`

### 9.4 “For whom do you play?” has plural valid answers

- Mizore: Nozomi. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0594 / 「私、希美のために吹く。だから、聞いてて」`
- Reina: primarily herself, with playful/intimate temporary dedication to Kumiko. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0491 / 「そんなん、考えたことない。上手くなるのが楽しいから吹いてるだけやし。まあ、強い` `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0600 / 「アタシも、久美子のために吹こうかな」`
- Kumiko: increasingly the ensemble/listener and continuation of shared performance.

V02 therefore rejects a single authentic motivation template.

### 9.5 Taki's result language disciplines competition

Results matter because they grant or deny further performance opportunities. They do not provide a metaphysical ranking of all musical worth. `HIBIKE-V02 / S04 / P0547 / 「確かに、周りはどこも強豪校ばかりです。何年もこの舞台に立って、全国大会に進んで` This principle coexists with strict selection and competitive ambition rather than abolishing them.

## 10. Institution, legitimacy, and non-retroactive justice

### 10.1 Old Kitauji confused seniority with social order

The old regime used `秩序` and `空気` to protect seniority allocation against merit-based criticism. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0149 / 「香織先輩とか小笠原さんとかさ、真面目にコツコツ練習してた二年生がＢやってんな。` This was not merely inefficient; it taught serious younger students that competence and effort could become socially punishable.

### 10.2 New Kitauji corrects procedure but changes the bargain midstream

Taki's performance selection is more defensible for a club that explicitly wants nationals. Yet current seniors had previously paid the cost of waiting their turn. Yuuko's account makes clear why their resentment can be morally intelligible even when Reina remains the correct soloist. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0710 / 「そうそう。やからさ、今年の三年生が滝先生に反発した理由もよくわかんねんな。やっ`

### 10.3 Former members create a restorative-justice problem

Nozomi's case asks whether present procedural fairness creates an obligation toward people harmed before reform. Asuka's answer is institution-first and prospective: do not destabilize the current competition ensemble. Nozomi's answer is relational/historical: she wants to return to the activity and institution from which she was pushed out.

The novel does not produce a general policy. It stages the tradeoff.

### 10.4 Selection cannot be removed from scarce competition

Only three schools advance. Even a perfectly benevolent institution cannot make every serious ensemble a national representative. V02 therefore separates **fairer process** from **guaranteed satisfaction**.

## 11. Ordinary life, humor, and embodiment

V02 supplies unusually valuable low-stakes character evidence.

- Reina is embarrassed by initiating a fireworks invitation. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0157 / 「あんまこういうの慣れてへんの！誘うのとか！」`
- Pool conversation reveals clothing/body self-consciousness and the group's casual teasing rather than crisis behavior.
- Midori enthusiastically redirects awkward social moments and enjoys attractive/interesting surroundings without shame.
- Asuka wakes at four and uses unwanted early-morning free time for private playing. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0725 / 「いや、うちは普段四時には起きてるから。合宿中は暇でしゃあなくてさ」`
- Yuuko can buy Kumiko a drink and speak normally despite prior confrontational history.
- Natsuki and Yuuko convert exercise/rivalry/insult into affiliative play.
- Reina removes dust from Kumiko after waiting for her, an intimate care behavior with almost no verbal ceremony.
- Mizore's distress appears as sweating, nausea, unstable gait, flight, closed eyes, and breathing changes before she can narrate it.

These details should carry high weight in later scenario simulation because they constrain how the characters behave outside dramatic speeches.

## 12. Family, material, regional, and social conditions

### 12.1 Musical access is materially structured

Nozomi owns a substantial flute because her middle-school environment expected serious players to have private instruments. This is evidence of a competitive culture with resource demands, not proof of a specific household class status.

Taki explicitly foregrounds parent-funded activity fees at camp. `HIBIKE-V02 / S03 / P0227 / 「この三日間、ここのホールはうちが貸し切っています。お金のことをあまり細かくは言` Time, facilities, travel, instructors, and instruments are not free inputs.

### 12.2 Regional competition matters

Kansai's structure places Kyoto schools against a region where Osaka's powers have accumulated substantial competitive legitimacy. The “three powers” operate as a social category students already know.

Kumiko's resistance to `別格` rhetoric shows how reputation can become psychologically self-fulfilling: categorizing another school as fundamentally different can pre-empt equal comparison before performance begins.

### 12.3 Language does not map cleanly onto geography

Mizore speaks standard Japanese despite local schooling, with family/relational linguistic background affecting her speech. This reinforces the V01 lesson from Kumiko: a Kyoto setting does not license automatic Kansai speech for every character.

## 13. Motifs and form

### 13.1 Time and the unrecoverable year

V02 is saturated with temporal counterfactuals:

- Nozomi's institution changes after she leaves;
- seniors wait for a turn that new rules remove;
- Kumiko fears present intimacy becoming past;
- Taki's life is divided by bereavement and return;
- Natsuki tries to atone for what she did not do last year;
- old musical material becomes listenable under new conditions.

This is stronger than a generic “past haunts present” motif. The volume repeatedly asks whether **later meaning can redeem earlier loss without pretending it never happened**.

### 13.2 Dust

Dust first appears as something Kumiko notices but declines to clean because she need not dirty her hands. `HIBIKE-V02 / S02 / P0737 / 振り絞られた声は、確かに熱を帯びていた。あすか先輩は特別やから。夏紀が繰り返して` Later she physically enters the abandoned room containing the hidden conflict and emerges carrying dust; Reina removes it.

**[D/E]** The recurrence plausibly tracks Kumiko's changing relation to inherited mess: observation without involvement gives way to contact, contamination, and relational care.

### 13.3 Heat

Summer heat is not merely atmospheric. It accompanies competition scarcity, bodily exhaustion, embarrassment, nausea, rehearsal labor, and emotionally overloaded conversations. “Hottest” is both calendar fact and a measure of how much relational/institutional material is being forced into explicit form.

### 13.4 “First” / “special” / selection

Nozomi calls Asuka special because Asuka tried to stop her. Mizore calls Nozomi her singular friend. Yuuko confronts being treated as secondary. Competition selects representatives. Auditions select performers.

V1's “asymmetrical first places” concept remains useful, but V2 should classify it as an analytical synthesis rather than treat every `一番` as a formal lexical system. The broader textual pattern is **recognition through selection under conditions where selections cannot be perfectly reciprocal**.

## 15. Simulation-facing behavioral baseline after V02

This section records predictive constraints, not invented canon.

### Kumiko

**Likely when confronted with another person's crisis:** observe first, ask targeted questions, become increasingly unable to leave the contradiction alone, offer practical presence, and sometimes commit before possessing the full map.

**Likely when her own attachment is threatened:** rich internal worry; weaker direct disclosure; conversational deflection to a safer adjacent topic remains plausible.

**Unlikely:** confident therapeutic monologue, perfect neutrality, or immediate articulation of her deepest relational fear.

### Reina

**Likely in musical dispute:** direct hierarchy claim, low tolerance for self-protective relativism, preference for proving worth through performance.

**Likely in private attachment uncertainty:** embarrassment, jealousy, reassurance-seeking, more physical closeness, and less decisive speech.

**Unlikely:** surrendering an honestly held musical judgment merely to protect atmosphere.

### Mizore

**Likely under evaluative/abandonment threat:** withdrawal, minimal speech, somatic distress, technical control, literal rather than socially lubricated answers.

**Likely after secure recognition:** greater expressive musical risk, still sparse speech.

**Unlikely:** broad extroversion, elaborate social manipulation, or casual emotional disclosure.

### Nozomi

**Likely:** direct social approach, high energy, broad network awareness, willingness to challenge institution, underestimation of how exclusively another person may organize around her.

**Unlikely:** calculated cruelty toward Mizore absent new evidence.

### Yuuko

**Likely:** confront the person she cares about, name the emotional issue directly, protect through action, combine possessiveness/jealousy with real insight.

**Unlikely:** neutral detachment when Kaori or Mizore is threatened.

### Natsuki

**Likely:** ironic self-presentation, practical help, teasing even during care, delayed responsibility rather than moral grandstanding.

### Asuka

**Likely:** model the system rapidly, control information, intervene strategically when ensemble function is at risk, change tactics when premises change, translate emotional relations into functional variables.

**Unlikely:** disclose her own private motive merely because someone asks; treat every conflict as deserving intervention.

## 16. Open questions after V02

1. Can Kumiko learn to intervene **without** needing to resolve ambiguity quickly?
2. Does her thought–speech gap narrow equally in musical, institutional, romantic, and family domains, or remain domain-specific?
3. Can Reina's “never escape” ethic survive situations where persistence causes harm rather than excellence?
4. Does Mizore's new relation to competition persist after a bad result, or is it strongly outcome-dependent?
5. How stable is Mizore's broader social world after Nozomi returns to proximity?
6. Will Nozomi ever understand the true scale of Mizore's attachment, and does she need to for the relation to remain viable?
7. Is Asuka's instrumental language primarily worldview, defense, performance, or some changing combination?
8. How does Kitauji treat people harmed by earlier institutional regimes once immediate competition pressure recedes?
9. Can current seniors reconcile performance-based fairness with the seniority bargain under which they previously endured?
10. Does Taki's distinction between evaluation and absolute worth remain stable when the stakes rise further?
