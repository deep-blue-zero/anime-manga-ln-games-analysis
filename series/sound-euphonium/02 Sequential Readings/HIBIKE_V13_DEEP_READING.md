### Focalization map

The principal novel is centered on **Nakagawa Natsuki**. Chapter titles name Nozomi, Mizore, and Yuuko, but those titles identify the relationship-specific lens through which Natsuki reconstructs herself rather than transferring interior authority to the named character. ## 2. Executive thesis - chosen attachment after role loss

V13 begins by removing the institutional identities that have made Natsuki legible to herself. She is no longer vice-president in any operational sense; the euphonium seat she occupied can be filled; the juniors can continue; the club can survive her retirement. The prologue makes the emotional problem explicit: what was once a burdensome title has become difficult to release, and Kumiko's past-tense gratitude confirms that a boundary has been crossed. Natsuki is frightened by the feeling that her place is disappearing. `HIBIKE-V13 / S01 / P0009-P0025`.

The volume does **not** solve that fear by discovering that Natsuki was secretly indispensable. It instead subjects the very idea of indispensability to sustained pressure. The club is functionally robust without her. Yuuko could, in principle, have other people beside her. Mizore's life is not organized around Natsuki alone. Nozomi's decisive choices cannot be owned by Natsuki simply because Natsuki once advised her. Even Natsuki's goodness cannot be stabilized by proving that every helpful act came from pure benevolence.

V13's deepest answer arrives through Yuuko: a person can be replaceable in functional terms and still be **specifically chosen**. Availability of substitutes does not make the actual relationship fungible. The volume therefore extends a principle already visible in the Rikka/Tachibana movement—functional redundancy without relational disposability—into explicit interpersonal ontology.

The governing V13 formulation is:

> **role loss exposes Natsuki's fear that replaceability means erasure; the volume resolves that fear not by proving her irreplaceable, but by distinguishing functional substitutability from chosen relational particularity.**

The volume-local movement is:

> **role loss / identity blank -> retrospective audit of low-coercion attachment -> guilt over Nozomi -> recognition of Mizore as autonomous friend -> exposure of Yuuko's hidden dependence -> self-accusation as "good only by indifference" -> chosen mutual attachment despite available substitutes -> bounded self-acceptance**

The epilogue closes the movement by returning to the graduation questionnaire that Natsuki could not initially answer. Early V13 stalls at `私は、`; the epilogue can finally write `中川夏紀は、身勝手だ`, followed by the private qualification that she does not dislike that self. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0019-P0021`; `HIBIKE-V13 / S05 / P0028-P0032`.

This is not purification and not absolution. Natsuki authors a stable self-description without requiring herself to be morally pure, universally kind, uniquely necessary, or emotionally transparent.

## 3. Structural architecture - three named mirrors around one focal self

The three main story titles can initially mislead because each names someone other than Natsuki:

1. `傘木希美はツキがない。`
2. `鎧塚みぞれは視野が狭い。`
3. `吉川優子は天邪鬼。`

Yet the structural center remains Natsuki. Each named person exposes a different part of her model of relationship.

**Nozomi** tests whether non-coercion can become moral evasion. Natsuki admires Nozomi's ability to draw people into collective action without making them feel conscripted, but later blames herself for refusing to tell Nozomi what she personally wanted when Nozomi asked whether to quit. The chapter forces Natsuki to separate respecting another person's agency from pretending her own stake does not exist.

**Mizore** tests whether hidden motive should dominate relational judgment. Mizore repeatedly evaluates Natsuki by what Natsuki actually does for her rather than by Natsuki's self-accusation about indifference, guilt, or impurity. Mizore's literalness strips away one of Natsuki's preferred defensive moves: if Natsuki claims that her kindness is invalid because the motive was imperfect, Mizore simply asks why that hidden theory should matter more than the experienced effect.

**Yuuko** tests replaceability most directly. Yuuko is socially capable, charismatic, surrounded by people, and not structurally helpless without Natsuki. Her answer is therefore stronger than an argument from necessity. She chooses Natsuki despite alternatives, and she identifies Natsuki as the person who repeatedly turns off the leader role Yuuko otherwise cannot stop performing.

The short supplementary story then changes focalization to Nozomi. This matters because the main text could otherwise leave the analyst trapped inside Natsuki's own self-critique. Nozomi sees Natsuki actively initiating future four-person travel and recognizes an embarrassment pattern similar to Natsuki's behavior around Yuuko. `HIBIKE-V13 / S06 / P0050-P0070`. The alternate POV therefore validates that Natsuki's attachment is behaviorally visible even when she narrates herself as detached.

Formally, V13 is a retrospective character monograph embedded inside fiction. It reconstructs a long Natsuki timeline while refusing the simplicity of a single causal origin. Her anti-coercion, laziness, competence, resentment, guilt, loyalty, attraction to improvement, delayed grief, and wish to be chosen coexist. The kaleidoscopic effect described in F01 is useful as paratextual corroboration, but the prose itself already establishes that contradictory states are the point. `[F] HIBIKE-V13 / F01 / P0012-P0014`.

## 4. Natsuki at retirement - role loss becomes wantedness threat

### 4.1 The title she disliked becomes difficult to surrender

The prologue is unusually compact and diagnostically dense. Natsuki has spent much of the series performing distance from institutional sentiment. Yet retirement destabilizes her not because she suddenly believes titles are sacred, but because titles had become **evidence of a place in other people's lives**.

She looks at Kumiko as an excellent junior and almost experiences that competence as suffocatingly complete. She wants to remain around the club and care for juniors, then mocks herself for sounding parental. The vice-president label, once experienced as a heavy thing, has become difficult to give up. `HIBIKE-V13 / S01 / P0005-P0010`.

The crucial mechanism appears when Natsuki jokes that the juniors must be happy to see the third years leave. Kumiko immediately denies it. The denial pleases Natsuki. `HIBIKE-V13 / S01 / P0018-P0021`.

That exchange gives a precise predictive rule:

> **Natsuki often probes wantedness through joking deniability rather than direct reassurance-seeking.**

She can create a socially cheap exit if the answer is bad: she was only joking. If the answer is good, she receives reassurance without having admitted she needed it.

The narration then states the threat directly: she is still afraid to feel her place disappear. `HIBIKE-V13 / S01 / P0021`. Kumiko's past-tense `ありがとうございました` makes the transition formal. `HIBIKE-V13 / S01 / P0024`. The prologue therefore reframes retirement as a test of identity after functional membership ends.

### 4.2 Replaceability is not the same thing as rejection

Later, Natsuki returns to the music room and sees her old euphonium role occupied by Kumiko and a junior. The ensemble continues. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0476-P0479`.

The fact is objectively benign and institutionally healthy: a school club should survive graduation. Subjectively, however, Natsuki experiences robustness as evidence that the world can continue without her. This becomes the volume's **replaceability threat**.

It should not be reduced to generic low self-esteem. Natsuki is often confident about competence, appearance, humor, and social judgment. What destabilizes her is more specific: if another person can perform the same function, what proves that *she* is personally wanted?

V13's answer will be that functional proof is the wrong proof.

## 5. The questionnaire and the blank self

The graduation questionnaire asks Natsuki for a self-description. She can easily write about others—her line on Nozomi comes quickly—but stalls when the sentence turns inward. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0016-P0021`.

The difficulty follows role loss. `副部長`, `ユーフォニアム担当`, older student, B/A status, and other institutional descriptors have provided temporary handles. Without them, `私は、` produces no stable continuation.

Natsuki explains that while people relied on her, the “real” self was desperately stretching to meet that expectation. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0021`. This is important but epistemically bounded: it is a **C-class self-model**, not omniscient diagnosis. Much of V13 exists precisely to show that Natsuki habitually interprets her own care in the least flattering way available.

The blank therefore functions at two levels:

- **identity uncertainty:** who is she outside a useful role?
- **moral uncertainty:** if usefulness was partly performance, does the helpful behavior still count as hers?

The final questionnaire answer will not discover an innocent “true Natsuki” hiding beneath performance. It will accept that performed responsibility, selfish desire, indifference, affection, and deliberate choice can all belong to the same self.

## 6. Pre-band Natsuki - autonomy as value, defense, and incomplete good

### 6.1 Anti-coercion is not simple laziness

Middle-school Natsuki dislikes the earnest school culture built around `熱血`, `根性`, and compulsory collective enthusiasm. Her criticism of group rope-jumping is especially revealing. She rejects the logic by which a collective task creates an atmosphere where the less capable individual becomes morally blameworthy for slowing everyone else. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0243`.

This is not mere refusal to participate. Natsuki still participates and does not sabotage the group. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0270`. Her position is better modeled as:

> **critique without defection: she resists moralized collective pressure while still carrying the minimum obligations of participation.**

Nozomi notices that the anger itself is relational. Someone wholly indifferent to the struggling students would not be angry on their behalf. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0271-P0275`.

This is an early example of the gap between Natsuki's **self-presentation as detached** and her **attention to imposed burden**. It anticipates her later institutional brake on collateral harm.

### 6.2 Personal autonomy can become empty autonomy

At middle-school graduation, Natsuki does not understand why classmates cry. If a relationship matters, she reasons, people can simply continue seeing one another. She does not feel regret—and is troubled by the fact that she does not.

The key statement is one of the best compact Natsuki formulations in the corpus:

`自分の時間を他人に盗られるのは嫌だ。だけど、すべての時間を自分に費やし続けるのはどこか虚しい。` `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0291`.

The opposition is not autonomy versus dependence. Natsuki wants both:

- control over her own time;
- relationships that make time matter beyond private consumption.

She quietly envies Nozomi's fuller school life and wants, at least once, to be someone who likes school. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0292-P0293`.

This yields a core character mechanism:

> **autonomy is both genuine value and defensive strategy; pure self-contained autonomy becomes hollow, so Natsuki seeks attachment that preserves low coercion.**

This principle explains why Nozomi's invitation succeeds where a stronger institutional pitch might fail.

## 7. Nozomi as low-coercion invitation

### 7.1 Why Nozomi can mobilize Natsuki

Nozomi's social effectiveness is not merely charisma. She makes participation feel like an available path rather than a moral debt.

She intends to join band because she likes music; Kitauji's weakness is not disqualifying because a weak group can be changed. She asks Natsuki to join and says that being together with Natsuki might be fun. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0341-P0360`.

Natsuki knows Nozomi is recruiting many people, but the specific invitation still matters. What persuades her is the combination of **personal invitation and low institutional pressure**. She would not have joined a tightly disciplined strong-school schedule. Kitauji appears weak, loose, and therefore survivable. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0381-P0382`.

This is an important correction to any model that treats Natsuki's later seriousness as evidence that she was always latently craving elite competition. She becomes invested **inside** a relationship and institution whose initial low-pressure character made entry possible.

### 7.2 Nozomi changes Natsuki without owning the change

At the current-state end of the volume, Natsuki can identify that Nozomi changed her life. But V13 strongly resists making influence equivalent to ownership.

When Natsuki later explains why she did not quit along with Nozomi, she says leaving would have made Nozomi's invitation “not have happened” in practical terms. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0867`. She wants to preserve the version of herself that exists because Nozomi invited her. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0870`.

That is a mature transmission model: **the relationship can remain causally important even when the two people do not make identical choices or remain constantly co-present**.

## 8. The Nozomi decision - non-coercion reaches its failure mode

The strongest guilt structure in Natsuki's history concerns the moment Nozomi asks whether she should quit.

Nozomi comes to Natsuki precisely because she expects a cool, neutral answer. Natsuki inwardly resents the assumption because Nozomi matters too much for Natsuki to be neutral. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0721-P0725`.

Yet Natsuki refuses to state the personal stake. She tells Nozomi to do what she wants and says the choice Nozomi believes in will probably be the right one. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0726-P0728`.

Natsuki physically sends her forward, and Nozomi later quits. Natsuki retrospectively calls this **her sin**. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0732`.

Two levels must remain separate:

- **[A/C]** Natsuki gave nonbinding advice and later feels guilty.
- **[G]** it is not established that a different answer from Natsuki would have prevented Nozomi's departure or produced a better outcome.

Natsuki's guilt reveals a recurring contradiction. She hates coercing another person's decision, but when she erases her own preference entirely she can later interpret the restraint as abandonment. The failure mode is therefore not “too much autonomy.” It is **pseudo-neutrality when the relationship itself creates standing to disclose a stake**.

By current graduation state, Natsuki can see a further danger in her guilt. Romanticizing the alternate history may insult the choices Nozomi and the others actually made. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P1024-P1034`.

This becomes a useful bridge to the V11-V12 final-movement principle of owned regret, but only as **retrospective thematic resonance**. It does not alter the frozen V12 causal model.

## 9. First-year Kitauji reconstructed - friendliness, seniority, and coalition imbalance

V13 supplies the most detailed retrospective view yet of the pre-Taki institution from Natsuki's side.

### 9.1 A friendly club can still have coercive structure

The previous adviser emphasizes a friendly club and relatively easy social distance. That softness is real. It does not prevent an inherited selection culture based heavily on seniority and memory-making rather than open performance comparison.

The defense of seniority is not intellectually empty. Older members argue that third years deserve a final stage and that unequal training and experience make supposedly pure skill comparison unfair. V2 should preserve this as a legitimate **argument** even where the resulting policy becomes exclusionary.

This matters because institutional conflict in *Hibike* is repeatedly strongest when competing positions contain real value rather than when one faction is simply malicious.

### 9.2 South Middle arrives as both competence and coalition

The South Middle students bring stronger performance expectations and substantial internal cohesion. Their prior relationships increase their ability to act collectively, but that same cohesion makes them legible to existing members as a faction capable of changing the club's order.

The old culture reads their demands as threat. Calls to “read the atmosphere” turn social peace into a veto over procedural change.

Natsuki's position is morally painful because she cannot simply identify with the reformers. Her actual preference at the time is closer to the passive seniors than to the activists. This is a **self-application conflict**: she can see unfairness while also benefiting from and preferring the low-pressure order that produces it.

The retrospective value of this material is high for simulation. Natsuki is not naturally an ideological reformer. She becomes willing to resist a system when its costs become personal and visible, but she retains aversion to moralized mobilization even when she agrees with the substantive complaint.

## 10. Natsuki's aggression has a collateral-harm brake

After the activist students leave, Natsuki eventually hears seniors mock them and snaps. She calls the seniors ugly in character and uses a middle finger. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0747-P0748`.

Her next line sharply separates personal autonomy from judgment of wrongdoing:

`うちが辞めるかどうかはうちが決めることなんで。それと先輩たちがクソなことは別問題` `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0754`.

This is Natsuki at unusually high directness: she refuses both loyalty coercion and the idea that staying makes her endorse the seniors.

Then the seniors introduce a different variable: what if retaliation against Natsuki pushes another low-brass student out? Natsuki goes quiet. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0766-P0774`.

The contrast gives a strong predictive constraint:

> **Natsuki accepts personal cost more readily than collateral harm. If her retaliation may externalize punishment onto an uninvolved person, her aggression brakes sharply.**

This helps explain why she can look unusually bold in one confrontation and suddenly cautious in another without inconsistency. The decision variable is not courage alone; it is **who bears the externality**.

## 11. Asuka retrospective expansion - selective responsibility has a domain boundary

Asuka's intervention after the senior conflict materially refines the model established in V01 and later volumes.

She enters an already open doorway, physically pushes Natsuki's head down, apologizes formally, and takes jurisdiction over the situation as a low-brass matter. She then attacks the seniors' framing—especially the use of “everyone” as if universal dislike were fact rather than a speaker's claim. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0797-P0812`.

Asuka later says directly that she would not have intervened if Natsuki were not low brass. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0812`.

This does **not** overturn V01's evidence that Asuka can call large social disputes irrelevant. Instead it identifies where her claimed indifference ends:

> **Asuka's protection is bounded by chosen jurisdiction. Once she owns a domain, she can combine formal deference, semantic/frame control, and direct protection without adopting a generalized duty to solve every conflict.**

Her style is characteristic:

- externally deferential enough to control escalation;
- internally dominant in defining what the dispute is allowed to mean;
- physically and verbally reassuring afterward without sentimentalizing the intervention.

This is a useful simulation distinction between **selective responsibility** and generic altruism.

## 12. Why Asuka designs Yuuko + Natsuki as a pair

V13 also provides unusually explicit evidence for leadership as **complementary system design** rather than selection of two individually superior leaders.

Asuka identifies Yuuko's likely failure mode: as president, Yuuko will hide damage and perform the leader role too completely. Natsuki is valuable because Yuuko cannot sustain that idealized performance around her. The reverse is also true: Natsuki cannot remain only the detached cynic around Yuuko.

Asuka reframes Natsuki's habitual `諦めぐせ` as a possible leadership asset. Natsuki accurately calibrates her own effort and skill; when Kumiko earns the A seat, Natsuki does not convert the result into status grievance. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0154-P0168`.

The key formulation is that Natsuki is the person in the current club capable of functioning as Yuuko's brake. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0168`.

Nozomi is not a simple substitute. She did not share the entire transition under Taki and remains, in that institutional-historical sense, partly an outsider. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0170-P0179`. Asuka also distinguishes playing skill from leadership aptitude; Natsuki's experience outside the elite A group can increase identification with non-elite members. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0182`.

The institutional system is therefore:

> **Yuuko supplies activation, confidence, emotional commitment, and public momentum; Natsuki supplies de-escalation, irreverence, permission to be imperfect, and lower-status identification.**

Their friction is not merely inefficiency. It is part of the architecture.

## 13. Natsuki and Mizore - literal recognition defeats motive-purity logic

Mizore repeatedly offers Natsuki a form of recognition that Natsuki finds difficult to accept because it is so literal.

### 13.1 `夏紀は夏紀`

When Natsuki talks about changing appearance, Mizore says she would recognize her regardless of hair color because Natsuki is Natsuki. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0454-P0471`.

The statement is almost too simple for Natsuki's defensive sophistication. Natsuki wants identity to require proof—role, usefulness, moral motive, uniqueness. Mizore treats identity as prior to those proofs.

### 13.2 Mizore judges effect, not hidden moral purity

Natsuki attempts to disqualify the “good person” label by appealing to her own motives. Mizore responds that Natsuki is a good person **to her**, and asks what else should matter. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0528-P0540`.

This is not a philosophical proof that motives never matter. It is a relational correction to Natsuki's specific self-punitive model. Natsuki cannot unilaterally invalidate another person's gratitude by producing a darker private motive.

The same argument will be made more forcefully by Yuuko later. Mizore supplies the cleanest minimalist version.

### 13.3 Mizore has more ordinary autonomy than crisis-only modeling suggests

V13 significantly expands Mizore's non-crisis model:

- she says her mother usually selects her clothes and she mostly does not care beyond wearability; `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0432-P0442`;
- she likes heights and repeated high-speed/drop rides and is unusually resistant to motion sickness; `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0395-P0408`;
- she expresses desire to fly and attraction to high places; `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0593-P0607`;
- by graduation she can request specific experiences and future return trips rather than merely follow;
- she tentatively rehearses the category `友達` for the four-person group. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0613-P0618`.

This prevents a simulation from overproducing passive, fragile Mizore behavior. Sparse speech remains real; passivity is not a total personality.

## 14. Mizore on Nozomi - category refusal without emotional diminishment

One of V13's most important relationship passages is Mizore's attempt to describe what Nozomi has meant to her.

Mizore says she never hated Nozomi and still loves her, but the self who loved Nozomi was painful to inhabit: `希美を好きな自分は嫌いだった`. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0561`.

She then says that if the feeling had been ordinary romance, it might have been easier because she could have imagined the conventional objectives—dating, marriage—and eventually given up. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0567-P0576`.

Her actual formulation is more basic and more difficult: she only wanted to be together, yet human beings do not simply stay together without reasons. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0576`.

This passage creates two strong negative constraints:

1. Do not reduce Mizore's attachment to a conventional romantic goal structure that she explicitly distinguishes from her own experience.
2. Do not use that distinction to claim the attachment is emotionally weak, casual, or categorically “nonromantic” in every possible sense.

The source establishes **category mismatch**, not a simple binary answer to fandom taxonomy.

For reconstruction, the safest formulation is:

> **Mizore experiences Nozomi as a profoundly prioritized attachment whose desired end is co-presence itself rather than a clearly articulated dating/marriage script.**

## 15. Nozomi and Mizore - asymmetry survives, but ignorance is revised

V13 strengthens the evidence that Nozomi and Mizore do not inhabit the relationship at identical intensity, while complicating any claim that Nozomi is oblivious.

### 15.1 Why Nozomi did not ask Mizore to quit

Nozomi says she did not invite Mizore to leave because Mizore kept practicing even when the club did not reward or value that effort. Asking her to quit would have disrespected what Mizore had continued to do. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0290-P0297`.

This revises a simplistic absence-of-care interpretation. The omission can be read as an attempt to respect Mizore's demonstrated commitment rather than as proof that Nozomi had forgotten her.

### 15.2 Natsuki refuses to force a disclosure

Natsuki recognizes that Mizore's emotional scale appears larger. She also suspects Nozomi is socially perceptive enough to understand more than she openly acknowledges. If Nozomi chooses not to name that knowledge, Natsuki declines to extract it. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0298-P0300`.

That restraint is important. Natsuki's mature care increasingly respects **disclosure jurisdiction**: noticing a hidden thing does not automatically authorize interrogation.

### 15.3 Nozomi's jealousy is direct; the full object is not

Nozomi later tells Natsuki that she is jealous of Mizore and sometimes wants to lash out despite properly liking her. The final continuation is lost to the wind. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0341-P0346`.

The missing phrase must remain **OPEN**. Jealousy/envy is direct; the exact full internal formulation is not recoverable from the prose.

Yuuko calls the bond friendship. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0349-P0354`. That is **Yuuko's interpretation**, not omniscient categorization.

In S06, Nozomi herself reaches a mature friend framing: she thinks that now the four can choose things together as ordinary friends, and experiences that freedom as both wonderful and slightly sad. `HIBIKE-V13 / S06 / P0074-P0075`.

The stable V13 model is therefore:

> **Nozomi/Mizore becomes more ordinary and mutually sustainable without becoming emotionally symmetrical or retrospectively simple.**

## 16. Nozomi's autonomous model - energetic pragmatism, effort dependence, and support without annexation

V13 adds a useful amount of ordinary and prospective Nozomi evidence.

She fills schedules, seeks `充実`, and becomes anxious when not trying hard at something. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0710-P0718`. Natsuki calls her a positive jack-of-all-trades; Nozomi accepts a similar self-description and likes that quality about herself. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0263-P0275`.

The wing hypothetical is especially diagnostic. Nozomi does not answer with pure fantasy. She asks about size, material, and reliability and says she would not risk her life on untested wings; her own feet are more trustworthy. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0689-P0696`.

Her projections onto the others are relationally revealing:

- Mizore would fly without imagining failure;
- Yuuko would train rigorously before attempting it;
- Natsuki could fly with Yuuko. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0697-P0702`.

This is not objective psychological truth, but it is high-value evidence of **Nozomi's model of the group**.

Nozomi also demonstrates a mature boundary around authored group identity. When another band already exists as a five-person unit, she offers support/staffing rather than inserting herself as a performer merely because she is capable. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P1000-P1008`.

That produces a useful rule:

> **Nozomi likes participation and action, but mature Nozomi can support a group without annexing it.**

## 17. Natsuki and Yuuko - chosen particularity despite substitutes

This dyad carries V13's strongest relational resolution.

### 17.1 A non-band channel becomes durable

Natsuki and Yuuko already possess high-frequency banter, mock politeness, teasing, and conflict. V13 shows how the relationship acquires a second channel not dependent on Kitauji roles.

Yuuko's “important-person points” joke turns into a guitar request, and Natsuki's recommendation starts a shared practice relationship. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0902-P0934`.

Yuuko buys the same Yamaha model in a different finish. Natsuki initially enjoys the asymmetry because teaching gives her superiority, but later finds equal playing more enjoyable. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0939-P0943`.

This is a small but important attachment transition:

> **being needed feels good; being met as an equal feels better.**

That distinction directly opposes any model that makes Natsuki's attachment security depend exclusively on usefulness.

### 17.2 Possessiveness exists without a formal romance label

Natsuki imagines Yuuko eventually having a boyfriend and becomes absurdly irritated by the hypothetical stranger, internally competing on the basis that she knows Yuuko far better. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0252-P0265`.

This is strong evidence of possessive attachment. It is not enough by itself to establish formal romance. The dyad is highly intimacy- and romance-compatible, but V13 supplies no explicit dating/confession label.

The canonical relation state should therefore preserve both facts:

- **highly prioritized chosen attachment with exclusivity/possessive cues**;
- **category remains textually underdetermined**.

### 17.3 Yuuko chooses redundancy because she fears collapse

Yuuko's reason for taking up guitar becomes one of the volume's strongest general principles. She wants multiple things and places to rely on so that losing one instrument, group, or home will not erase her. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0326-P0335`.

This is dependency diversification, not emotional shallowness:

> **resilient identity can use multiple supports without reducing the importance of any one chosen support.**

Her choice of guitar is not arbitrary. She picks it because Natsuki already plays.

### 17.4 Natsuki turns off the leader role

Yuuko's leadership strength contains an exhaustion risk: she can perform competence and responsibility beyond the point where others notice damage.

When she seeks solitude, Natsuki follows precisely because Yuuko wants to be alone. Natsuki frames the care as `嫌がらせ`, preserving Yuuko's pride and allowing the intervention to remain inside their ordinary banter grammar. She jokes about hand-holding, hugging, and lap pillows, then finally pulls Yuuko's head to her shoulder. Yuuko does not resist and admits that she is tired. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0441-P0449`.

Natsuki knows Yuuko could recover alone. That fact matters. She is not rescuing an incapable person. She is refusing to let someone she values remain fully trapped inside functional competence.

Yuuko's later letter names the mechanism explicitly: Natsuki repeatedly turned off her “president switch”; having someone stay after she told them to leave mattered, and Yuuko says she probably could not have kept going in the same way without Natsuki. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0532-P0536`.

This yields a relationship-conditioned care rule:

> **with Yuuko, Natsuki's care often takes the form of forced permission to become temporarily nonfunctional, delivered through teasing hostility rather than therapeutic language.**

### 17.5 Intent impurity cannot erase received care

Near the end, Natsuki attacks her own moral identity. She says she is ordinary, that her tolerance is partly indifference, that her kindness can be guilt or atonement, and that she may only look like a good person because she did not care enough to compete for certain things. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0719-P0734`.

Yuuko rejects the jurisdiction behind the argument. The person who received the kindness was helped, felt glad, and gained reason to continue. Natsuki cannot rewrite Yuuko's gratitude by announcing that her motive was impure. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0736-P0749`.

Yuuko then describes Natsuki with greater behavioral accuracy than Natsuki's own cynical self-summary: rude, combative, anti-group in self-presentation, yet endlessly meddling and thinking about other people.

This produces a durable V2 principle:

> **motive impurity does not invalidate relational effect; self-explanation does not monopolize the meaning of one's behavior.**

### 17.6 The anti-fungibility statement

Yuuko's decisive answer to Natsuki's replaceability fear is not “nobody else could do what you do.” It is the opposite. There are many possible alternatives, and Yuuko chooses Natsuki anyway. She wants music and ordinary time specifically with her. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0756-P0760`.

Natsuki reciprocates: if she is choosing, she cannot imagine choosing someone other than Yuuko. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0767-P0770`.

This is the volume's clearest relationship ontology:

> **relational particularity does not require functional irreplaceability. Chosen attachment is meaningful precisely because substitutes exist.**

The dyad does not need to prove that one person is cosmically unique. It needs only two agents who could do otherwise and continue choosing one another.

## 18. Natsuki's “good person” conflict - self-accusation versus experienced effect

The `いい人` problem recurs across V13 because Natsuki distrusts moral identities that can be achieved too cheaply.

Her self-model includes several claims:

- she did not resent Kumiko's audition success partly because she knew her own effort level;
- she can tolerate others because she is sometimes indifferent;
- Mizore's constancy makes her own opportunism uncomfortable;
- later caretaking may be atonement for first-year guilt;
- therefore praise for being kind may rest on a false picture.

This model contains truth. Natsuki is not maximally invested in every outcome, and lower status hunger really does reduce some conflicts. V13 does **not** replace the model with “Natsuki was secretly pure all along.”

What it rejects is the inference that mixed motive makes the effect unreal.

Kumiko supplies the strongest external evidence. At graduation, she tells Natsuki directly that Natsuki's ordinary, non-retaliatory treatment after the year-one audition mattered because Kumiko had been traumatized by senior retaliation in middle school. Natsuki's shake, meal, and continued normality changed the meaning of succeeding under a senior. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0495-P0518`.

Kumiko's statement is not generic gratitude; it identifies a specific causal experience: `夏紀先輩が先輩でよかった` and the claim that Natsuki saved her.

Natsuki still resists the “good person” identity. But she does something mature in response: she does **not** unload the full self-accusation onto Kumiko. She preserves the cool-senpai surface because making the junior manage Natsuki's moral uncertainty would itself shift burden downward. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0515-P0518`.

That adds another predictive rule:

> **Natsuki's intimacy is selective rather than indiscriminate; with juniors, she can suppress self-exposure when disclosure would convert their gratitude into a caretaking obligation.**

## 19. Delayed affect - public containment is not low attachment

Natsuki does not cry at graduation. Later, alone after getting ready to meet Yuuko, she suddenly experiences emptiness and cries uncontrollably. The narration explicitly identifies solitude as the condition that allowed the tears to arrive. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0559-P0562`.

This is simulation-critical negative evidence against a common flattening:

> **absence of public tears does not imply absence of grief or attachment for Natsuki.**

Her emotional processing can be delayed until there is no social task to perform and no observer to regulate around.

The pattern aligns with her broader defense style. She uses jokes, practical tasks, music, movement, sarcasm, and irritation as affect channels. Emotion often becomes explicit only after the external role has ended.

## 20. Music beyond competition - an alternate ecology of growth

V13 is especially valuable because it reconstructs musical identity outside the wind-band contest system.

### 20.1 Natsuki's private listening and taste

Natsuki prefers punk/rock and Antwerp Blue; she describes more orderly classical music as boring by default, even though band participation changed what she can hear in it. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0140-P0142`.

Her listening is materially ritualized. She reserves physical CDs, picks them up on release day, peels the wrapping, and treats anticipation as part of the event. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0128-P0137`.

This ordinary behavior matters for reconstruction because music is not merely institutional duty for Natsuki. She has a taste culture and material practice independent of Kitauji.

### 20.2 Guitar creates skill without rank

Natsuki's Yamaha Pacifica, inherited from an older cousin, becomes a second musical object around which she can experience improvement. She learns better from video demonstrations than from a paper manual and enjoys acquiring technique without needing an audience or competitive objective. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0939-P0947`; `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0458-P0463`.

Distorted pick-scratch / pick-scrape is especially important because the physical sensation and noise clear internal cognitive clutter. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0367-P0373`.

Music here functions as **affect regulation through embodied task**.

### 20.3 The Yuuko dyad moves from teacher/learner to equal playing

Yuuko initially gives Natsuki the pleasant superiority of being the teacher. Later, equality is more enjoyable. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0941-P0943`.

This is a direct counterexample to usefulness-secured attachment. Natsuki does not need Yuuko to remain inferior in order to keep the bond meaningful.

### 20.4 Small ensemble changes the ethics of error

A two-person band radically reduces externality. A mistake does not affect fifty-five contest members; it affects the two participants who have jointly chosen the activity. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0103-P0104`.

They slow tempo to coordinate singing and playing, divide lead/backing roles, use count-ins, and couple through eyes and ears. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0105-P0111`; `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0577-P0612`.

The practice is serious without becoming high-stakes institutional competition.

### 20.5 Performance becomes chosen continuity rather than adjudication

The final live performance is not scored. It is an opening act before a known audience and a shared social event. Yuuko speaks sentimentally about how music lets them reunite after everything; Natsuki rejects the sentimental register through sound and a rough exhortation to enjoy themselves fully. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0816-P0826`.

Different affect languages produce the same group purpose.

V13 therefore adds an important music-model constraint:

> **for these characters, serious musical commitment does not require competition; music can be mastery, regulation, hospitality, shared time, and continuity.**

## 21. Yuuko's dependency diversification - resilience without devaluation

Yuuko's guitar explanation deserves independent institutional and character-model status.

She has already experienced how one ensemble or instrument can become too total a home. Her solution is not detachment. She wants to like multiple things and possess multiple places of belonging so that loss of one does not erase the self. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0326-P0335`.

This creates a generalizable model:

> **resilience can be built through plural dependence rather than independence from dependence.**

That formulation is especially useful because it distinguishes redundancy from fungibility:

- multiple supports make collapse less likely;
- a specific support can still be deeply loved;
- choosing another support does not retroactively make the first unimportant.

This is continuous with the V05-V06 institutional insight that functional redundancy protects a system without making people relationally disposable. V13 supplies the person-level analogue.

## 22. Yuuko leadership - strategic opacity and visible imperfection

V13 adds two important corrections to any overly simple “emotionally transparent leader” model.

First, Yuuko can mediate through **benevolent information asymmetry**. In the junior conflict she already knows that the offended upperclassman wants reconciliation, but does not reveal every backstage fact. The junior then experiences the repair as safer and more self-authored, while Yuuko's charismatic authority rises. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0376-P0392`.

This is not lying for selfish gain. It is strategic staging of a social process. The episode shows that good leadership in the series is not identical to maximal transparency.

Second, Natsuki/Yuuko bickering exposes imperfection publicly. That imperfection can make leadership more credible and approachable rather than less legitimate. The pair therefore produces an important institutional paradox:

> **a leader may become more trustworthy when somebody close enough to puncture the role prevents the role from becoming total.**

Asuka's original pairing logic and Yuuko's later letter independently support this model.

## 25. Japanese voice and register findings

### 25.1 Natsuki - rough Kansai casualness with strategic deniability

Natsuki's baseline is casual Kansai Japanese with `うち` self-reference, clipped evaluative language, rougher lexical choices, and a high tolerance for teasing or mock hostility with close peers. Her linguistic model must not be reduced to “sarcastic.” Several social functions recur.

**Wantedness probe through joke**: she poses emotionally risky propositions as teasing claims so the addressee can contradict them without Natsuki having first confessed need. The retirement joke about juniors being glad seniors leave is the cleanest example. `HIBIKE-V13 / S01 / P0018-P0021`.

**Care as antagonistic framing**: with Yuuko, she can label staying close as `嫌がらせ`, converting care into a form Yuuko can receive without ceremonial vulnerability. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0441-P0449`.

**Direct anger at threshold**: when Natsuki believes an older student is behaving contemptibly and the cost is primarily hers, register can become unusually blunt and insulting. `HIBIKE-V13 / S02 / P0747-P0754`.

**Junior-protective editing**: with Kumiko at graduation, Natsuki does not dump her full self-accusation into the conversation. Her spoken output is kinder and more bounded than the internal argument. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0495-P0518`.

**Mizore adjustment**: Natsuki becomes more careful around Mizore because sarcasm and casual implication can be taken literally. This relationship requires more waiting, clarification, and self-correction than Yuuko banter.

**Yuuko dyadic velocity**: with Yuuko, rapid counters, mock politeness, challenge, insults, and shared routines are permissioned rather than signs of low trust. A generic “polite repair” simulation would be badly out of character for this dyad.

### 25.2 Yuuko - public activation, private compression

Yuuko remains high-affect and rhetorically active in public leadership. V13 adds a private mode in which exhaustion can collapse the leader surface into a few small admissions. The contrast between public competence and the quiet `……疲れた` moment is important. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0441-P0449`.

Her letter allows a more explicit affectionate register than live interaction and immediately attempts to revoke that vulnerability by demanding destruction. `HIBIKE-V13 / S04 / P0532-P0536`.

Thus:

> **Yuuko can disclose strongly in asynchronous/private form and then ritualistically attack the evidence of disclosure.**

### 25.3 Mizore - sparse literal language with growing request capacity

Mizore remains low-verbal and literal. Long turns retain high information weight. V13 adds a meaningful change: she can now state preferences, request experiences, identify friendship, and articulate the structural problem in her attachment to Nozomi.

The model should preserve sparse syntax without assuming sparse desire.

### 25.4 Nozomi - energetic social fluency plus pragmatic detail

Nozomi's speech is quick, socially easy, and action-oriented, but the wing hypothetical shows she is not simply dreamy or impulsive. She interrogates practical conditions before committing. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0689-P0696`.

Her mature friendship language can normalize previously dramatic relationships without pretending that the history disappears. `HIBIKE-V13 / S06 / P0074-P0075`.

## 27. Institutional synthesis - from place secured by role to belonging that survives role

V13's institutional contribution is retrospective but substantial.

It reconstructs a trajectory:

> **friendly seniority culture -> coalition conflict -> exclusion and exit -> bounded local protection -> Taki-era procedural transformation -> complementary peer leadership -> retirement -> functional succession without relational erasure**

Three durable institutional principles emerge.

### 27.1 Friendly atmosphere is not sufficient legitimacy

The pre-Taki club is capable of genuine friendliness while reproducing exclusionary seniority norms. Social warmth cannot substitute for procedural contestability.

### 27.2 Reform coalitions can themselves alter perceived power

The South Middle students are not merely isolated truth-tellers; they arrive with competence and preexisting cohesion. That cohesion gives them power and also makes incumbent members experience them as a threatening bloc. Institutional analysis must preserve both the substantive validity of reform and the social reality of coalition imbalance.

### 27.3 Leadership can be relationally engineered

Asuka's Yuuko/Natsuki pairing shows that leadership design can intentionally exploit complementary failure modes. The institution is not selecting a single ideal personality. It is building a system in which one person's excess is another person's brake.

The V13 end-state then completes the institutional distinction:

> **roles are replaceable by design; belonging need not therefore be erased.**

This is a necessary condition of healthy succession. If an institution can survive only by making former members indispensable, it has failed to reproduce itself. If succession means former members become meaningless, it has failed relationally. V13 holds both requirements simultaneously.

## 28. Motifs and form

### 28.1 Wings and flight

The title metaphor is distributed rather than assigned one meaning. Mizore imagines flight with relatively little failure projection; Nozomi interrogates the engineering; Yuuko would train; Nozomi imagines Natsuki flying with Yuuko. `HIBIKE-V13 / S03 / P0689-P0702`.

Flight therefore functions less as “freedom good / grounded bad” than as a test of **how each person authorizes risk and futurity**.

F01's commentary that both the one who flies and the one who remains grounded can be lonely is useful corroboration but remains paratext. `[F] HIBIKE-V13 / F01 / P0028`.

### 28.2 Blue / Antwerp Blue

Natsuki's musical taste, room aesthetics, and the Antwerp Blue reference give blue a character-specific rather than purely abstract function. F01 explicitly associates blue/Antwerp Blue with Natsuki, but the narrative evidence should remain primary. `[F] HIBIKE-V13 / F01 / P0012-P0014`.

### 28.3 The questionnaire

The questionnaire brackets the identity problem. At first, Natsuki can describe others but not herself. At the end, she can supply a morally imperfect self-description and privately accept it.

The formal movement is:

> **description of others -> blank self -> relational mirrors -> owned imperfect self-description**

### 28.4 Substitution and choice

Replacement appears at multiple scales:

- euphonium seat after graduation;
- vice-presidential succession;
- alternative friends/partners;
- multiple instruments/homes for Yuuko;
- the ability of institutions to continue.

The volume refuses the equation `replaceable = worthless`. Instead it makes choice visible precisely against the availability of alternatives.

### 28.5 Illumination / memory

`記憶のイルミネーション` turns memory from static nostalgia into a light under which current relationships can be seen differently. Nozomi's alternate focalization does not restore the old crisis. It recognizes that the four-person group can now choose ordinary friendship, and that ordinary freedom contains a small grief for the dramatic form that has passed.

## 34. Canonical V13 freeze

V13's major contribution is not a hidden fact about whether Natsuki is “really” kind, whether Nozomi/Mizore is “really” romantic, or whether Yuuko/Natsuki is “really” a couple. Its stronger achievement is to make those forced essence questions less useful.

Natsuki's mature problem is that she keeps trying to ground value in purity or necessity. If she was not perfectly benevolent, perhaps the care was false. If somebody else could have filled the role, perhaps she was not wanted. If she did not openly demand Nozomi stay, perhaps she abandoned her. If the club can continue without her, perhaps her place has vanished.

The people around her reject those equations in different ways.

- Mizore says Natsuki is Natsuki and judges the relation by what Natsuki actually is to her.
- Kumiko says Natsuki's ordinary senior behavior materially saved her from an old social-performance wound.
- Nozomi values Natsuki's change without requiring her to remain the person who first joined Kitauji.
- Yuuko gives the decisive answer: alternatives exist, and she chooses Natsuki anyway.

Natsuki can therefore stop demanding that the self be pure, irreplaceable, or role-certified before it becomes worth choosing.

Natsuki's development is the move from autonomy defended by detachment and self-worth secured through usefulness toward low-coercion, mutually chosen relation in which mixed motives, functional substitutes, and future separation do not erase present particularity.**
