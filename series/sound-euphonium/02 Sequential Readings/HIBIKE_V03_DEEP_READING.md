### Epistemic discipline

This volume is unusually vulnerable to hindsight contamination because later material makes Asuka, Taki, Kumiko, Reina, Shuuichi, and the graduating seniors look more settled than they are here. V03 therefore distinguishes:

- what Kumiko can know at the time;
- what other characters explicitly tell her;
- what behavior supports but does not prove;
- and what later books may retrospectively clarify without being backported.

## 2. Executive local thesis — agency becomes accountable only after certainty is surrendered

V1 correctly treated V03 as the volume in which desire becomes ownership. V2 **strengthens that arc but revises its mechanism**.

Kumiko does not solve the Asuka crisis by discovering an objectively superior argument. In fact, the clean text repeatedly teaches her that rational argument can become another way of hiding from personal responsibility. Asuka is better informed than Kumiko about the band, her mother, her exams, her own substitute, and the consequences of returning. Each time Kumiko argues from what is *best for Kitauji*, Asuka can dismantle the claim.

The breakthrough occurs only when Kumiko abandons the attempt to speak for everyone. She explicitly admits that Asuka is right and then says that correctness is beside the point:

> `正しいこととか、そんなの全部どうでもいいです。`  
> `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0563`

She does not prove that Asuka is indispensable. She says **I want to perform with you** and **I want to hear your euphonium on that stage**. `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0563` `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0571`

This is the decisive refinement to the opening trilogy:

> **V01:** desire becomes emotionally real.  
> **V02:** other people's desires remain partially unknowable and can conflict without one side being false.  
> **V03:** therefore mature agency cannot wait for total epistemic certainty; it must make bounded first-person claims and accept responsibility for them.

The volume's adult and near-adult characters repeatedly fail when they disguise choice as objective necessity. Mamiko obeyed the “correct” route so that if it failed, the responsibility would not be wholly hers. Asuka repeatedly converts a personal wish into a functional calculation about the band. Kumiko initially tries to save Asuka by proving that “the band needs her,” which still treats Asuka as a function. Taki himself worries that his technically effective leadership may be only the imposition of his own desire. `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P1431`

The novel does not answer these problems with romantic anti-rationalism. Academic deadlines, family costs, audition readiness, national judging, and institutional substitution remain real. Its stronger claim is:

> **A reason can be correct without being sufficient to choose a life for another person.**

V03 therefore closes the trilogy by transforming Kumiko from someone who protects herself through deniable desire into someone capable of saying **this is what I want, even if I cannot justify it as universally correct**.

## 3. Structural architecture — pressure, impact, resonance, transmission

### Prologue — inheritance before comprehension

A young Asuka receives a black case and a notebook from `進藤正和`, a father she barely knows. She first has to identify the silver instrument as a euphonium. `HIBIKE-V03 / S01 / P0003`

The prologue gives Asuka a musical lineage before she possesses a mature account of the relationship. Music arrives as:

- material evidence that an absent parent once thought of her;
- a relationship her mother cannot fully erase;
- a secret inheritance whose emotional meaning exceeds Asuka's vocabulary;
- and the beginning of a competence domain that will later become both refuge and armor.

The structural importance of the prologue is not merely that it “reveals Asuka's father.” It makes transmission physical: **case → instrument → notebook → repertoire**. The epilogue will repeat the same motion when Asuka passes the notebook to Kumiko.

### S02 — 「唸れ！ トランペット」: public declaration under pressure

Kitauji has reached nationals, but success exposes rather than eliminates fragility. Asuka's mother enters the school, brings a repeatedly torn and repaired withdrawal form, and demands that Taki accept it. `HIBIKE-V03 / S02 / P0391-P0402`

The chapter's dominant action is declaration:

- Asuka's mother declares what her daughter's future must be.
- Taki declares that he will not accept a withdrawal that does not express Asuka's own will.
- Asuka declares that she does not want to quit.
- Haruka finally articulates the band's complicity in mythologizing Asuka as beyond ordinary support.

Haruka's formulation is the institutional hinge. She says the band made Asuka “special,” drew a line around her, assumed she would be fine, and therefore failed to support someone carrying extraordinary academic and club responsibilities. `HIBIKE-V03 / S02 / P0548-P0552`

### S03 — 「轟け！ トロンボーン」: inherited routes and the violence of “correctness”

The second chapter braids Mamiko's abandoned trombone, Shuuichi's continuing trombone, educational choice, Taki's inherited dream, and Asuka's substitute problem.

Mamiko's conflict makes “correctness” bitter in Kumiko's mouth. Her parents insist that calm reflection will reveal that their route is correct; Kumiko recognizes that the same correctness structured Mamiko's earlier obedience. `HIBIKE-V03 / S03 / P0442-P0443`

This is the conceptual preparation for the Asuka confrontation. V03 does not ask Kumiko to become irrational. It asks her to notice that *correctness is often being asked to do work it cannot do*: decide whose desire counts, assign responsibility, and eliminate regret in advance.

### S04 — 「響け！ ユーフォニアム」: first-person claim and national judgment

The title finally moves from pressure and impact toward resonance. The central Asuka confrontation, Reina's response to concealed truth, nationals, Shindō's two different judgments, and Asuka's admission that she likes euphonium all occur here.

The chapter refuses one grand reconciliation. Instead, it allows multiple kinds of truth to coexist:

- Asuka can be strategically correct and personally self-denying.
- Natsuki can be ready enough to substitute and still not be Asuka.
- Kitauji can perform truthfully and receive bronze.
- Shindō can assign a C to the ensemble and personally praise Asuka's sound.
- Reina can understand why Kumiko concealed painful information and still say she wanted to be told. `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0797-P0803`

### Epilogue — sound becomes inheritance

At graduation, Asuka gives Kumiko the notebook she received through her father. Kumiko says she does not want goodbye; Asuka offers `またね` instead. `HIBIKE-V03 / S05 / P0047-P0053`

The crucial instruction is:

> `今度は久美子ちゃんが後輩に聞かせてあげて`  
> `HIBIKE-V03 / S05 / P0043`

Kumiko then learns the score's title for the first time: `『響け！ ユーフォニアム』`. `HIBIKE-V03 / S05 / P0056-P0063`

The trilogy therefore ends not at competition but at **transmission**. Desire becomes durable when it can be passed onward without requiring the successor to become the predecessor.

## 4. Kumiko Oumae — from intervention to bounded first-person agency

### 4.1 V02's intervention appetite survives, but its weakness becomes visible

V02 showed Kumiko increasingly willing to enter other people's conflicts. V03 does not simply reward that trait. Her first attempts to intervene with Asuka reveal a problem already implicit in V02: **Kumiko often knows less than the intensity of her intervention suggests**.

Asuka can counter each generalized claim:

- the band will collapse without her → Natsuki has been practicing as substitute;
- everyone needs her → institutions can and should continue without one person;
- her absence makes the band unhappy → her return could create different costs;
- she will regret quitting → Asuka claims to have evaluated the tradeoff.

Kumiko's growth therefore cannot be “be more forceful.” She must become more precise about what she actually has standing to claim.

### 4.2 Haruka gives Kumiko the missing conceptual distinction

Haruka's admission that the band itself made Asuka “special” gives Kumiko an ethical warning. `HIBIKE-V03 / S02 / P0549-P0552`

When Kumiko later hears Asuka's flawless rational objections, Haruka's words return: if Kumiko again treats Asuka as a uniquely capable object whose value lies in what she does for the band, she is repeating the same mistake. `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0559-P0560`

That is why Kumiko's final claim is first-person rather than institutional.

### 4.3 The confrontation succeeds because Kumiko gives up universal justification

Kumiko explicitly concedes Asuka's correctness:

> `先輩は正しいです。`  
> `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0561`

Then she separates correctness from desire. She says she wants Asuka in the performance. She calls Asuka's self-presentation as the fully informed adult a performance of maturity and insists that a high-school student has the right to be unfinished. `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0563-P0571`

For simulation purposes, this is a crucial state transition:

**KUMIKO@V03** can now make a claim that is explicitly:

- personal;
- emotionally costly;
- potentially selfish;
- not disguised as consensus;
- and not contingent on proving that the other person secretly agrees.

She is still uncomfortable with confrontation and still often seeks safer formulations first. But once those formulations fail, she is capable of direct first-person speech.

### 4.4 Her care remains vulnerable to overprotection

Kumiko's handling of Taki's late wife gives the complementary limit. She withholds the information from Reina because she does not want to hurt her. When Reina later learns the truth, she understands the motive and nevertheless says:

> `それでも、アタシは教えてほしかってん`  
> `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0801`

This creates a durable character-modeling rule:

> **Kumiko often treats reducing immediate pain as care; V03 teaches her that care must also respect the recipient's preference for truth and agency.**

This is not an abstract lesson she fully masters at once. It is a pressure placed on her future mediator identity.

### 4.5 Kumiko's speech is changing without becoming Reina-like

Her climactic Asuka speech is long, breathless, repetitive, emotionally explicit, and framed through `私`. That is not her default register. Its significance lies precisely in the breakdown of normal editing.

Outside crisis she still:

- uses standard Japanese as baseline;
- hedges;
- lets internal sarcasm remain internal;
- evades embarrassment through half-answers;
- and permits familiar people such as Shuuichi and Reina to see rougher edges than seniors or teachers do.

V03 therefore strengthens the modeling distinction between **capacity for directness** and **default directness**. Kumiko has acquired the former; she has not become the latter.

## 5. Tanaka Asuka — competence as an autonomy-preservation system

### 5.1 The public crisis reveals that Asuka regulates the person who is controlling her

The most disturbing feature of the confrontation with Asuka's mother is not simply that the mother is coercive. It is that Asuka becomes the emotional regulator of the adult who is coercing her.

As her mother escalates, Asuka repeatedly tries to:

- lower the temperature;
- move the discussion away from bystanders;
- protect the school from disruption;
- explain the mother's behavior sympathetically;
- and reassure her mother after the mother has struck and emotionally blamed her.

After the outburst, Asuka says `お母さんは悪ないから`. `HIBIKE-V03 / S02 / P0435-P0436`

For character reconstruction, this is stronger than the adjective “mature.” Asuka's maturity is partly **forced emotional labor**. She has learned that maintaining the environment requires her to anticipate and absorb another person's volatility.

### 5.2 Academic excellence is not merely another talent; it is a permission structure

V1 correctly emphasized Asuka's exceptional grades but V2 makes their functional role more explicit. Her mother permits continued band participation only under demanding academic expectations; Asuka's continued musical autonomy depends partly on proving that music has not made her academically vulnerable.

This yields a stronger causal model:

> **Asuka's competence is not only pride. Competence creates bargaining power.**

Being excellent at school, music, administration, and social performance minimizes the number of legitimate grounds on which others can constrain her. The cost is that success convinces observers that she has no support needs.

### 5.3 “Specialness” is simultaneously prestige and abandonment

Haruka's diagnosis is one of the trilogy's strongest institutional statements:

> `あすかは、特別なんかやなかった`  
> `HIBIKE-V03 / S02 / P0550`

The point is not that Asuka is ordinary in skill. She is clearly exceptional. The point is that the social category **special** was allowed to become an excuse for withdrawing reciprocal care.

This is an important negative constraint for later Asuka modeling. Her charisma should not be mistaken for effortless self-sufficiency. She is often most controlled precisely when she has the greatest reason not to be.

### 5.4 Functional replaceability is not personal replaceability

Natsuki's readiness to substitute for Asuka is essential to the ethics of the volume. A healthy institution must be capable of surviving a member's absence. Otherwise “we need you” becomes coercive.

But that institutional truth does not answer Kumiko's personal claim. Natsuki can cover the euphonium part; she cannot make Asuka's relationship to Kumiko, Kaori, Haruka, or the music interchangeable.

V03 therefore distinguishes:

> **function can be replaceable while personhood remains relationally irreplaceable.**

That distinction is more precise than V1's simple necessary/wanted binary and should remain central in later institutional synthesis.

### 5.5 Asuka's private music is not reducible to the band's objective

The father/notebook line explains why nationals matters to Asuka without turning her into a simple competition ideologue. She wants her father to hear the sound that came from what he sent her; nationals creates a plausible meeting point between private lineage and public achievement.

This makes her behavior toward the band morally complicated. She does use the institution partly as a vehicle for a personal hope. But so do many characters. The more important V2 point is that Asuka habitually **masks personal desire as functional professionalism**, making even her own motives hard for others to distinguish.

### 5.6 Shindō's double judgment finally gives her what the ranking cannot

After nationals, Taki shows that Shindō assigned Kitauji a C. `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P1452-P1454` The ensemble judgment is severe and professional.

Then comes a separate message to the euphonium player:

> `よくここまで続けてきたね。美しい音色だったよ`  
> `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P1470`

The two judgments are not contradictory. One evaluates the ensemble under contest criteria; the other recognizes a daughter's persistence and sound. V2 therefore resists framing the personal praise as a hidden correction of the C. Asuka needed **recognition from the person who began the chain**, not retroactive inflation of Kitauji's ranking.

Her response is unusually unguarded. She says simply that she is happy and later, when Kumiko answers instantly that she likes euphonium, Asuka replies `うちも`. `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P1476` `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P1480-P1485`

The moment matters for simulation because it shows the emotional endpoint underneath the performance architecture: Asuka is capable of simple affirmation, but usually only after the social risk has fallen dramatically.

## 6. Oumae Mamiko — obedience as outsourced responsibility

Mamiko's plot is not a detachable adult subplot. It supplies the conceptual grammar Kumiko needs for Asuka.

Kumiko grew up seeing Mamiko as the person who knew what the correct route looked like. Mamiko quit music, pursued the approved academic path, failed to reach her first-choice university, and now wants to leave university for vocational training.

The clean text repeatedly returns to `正しい`. Kumiko recognizes that her parents' arguments are reasonable and experiences the word itself as bitter. `HIBIKE-V03 / S03 / P0442-P0443`

Mamiko's critical admission is not merely “I chose the wrong major.” Her error is that she let obedience serve as insurance against ownership:

- if the approved route succeeded, she would be validated;
- if it failed, she could blame the route or the people who chose it;
- she would not have to bear the full terror of having chosen badly herself.

V03's ethic is therefore not “parents bad, dreams good.” The parents are often factually reasonable. The novel instead asks who must finally own the choice.

Mamiko becomes a mirror for Asuka because both women are surrounded by adults who define prudence on their behalf. The difference is that Asuka has become better at covertly preserving an autonomous domain, while Mamiko followed the approved track until the accumulated alienation became intolerable.

Her eventual repair with Kumiko is modest rather than melodramatic. The family does not become ideologically unified. What changes is that Mamiko begins to speak as the author of her own risk.

## 7. Ogasawara Haruka — leadership through demystification and distribution

V03 strongly upgrades Haruka's importance.

She is not a failed substitute for Asuka's charisma. Her leadership contribution is precisely the opposite: **she diagnoses why an institution built around charismatic exception is fragile**.

Haruka admits her insecurity, jealousy, and dependence on Asuka rather than pretending she was always a confident president. Then she identifies the band's failure: everyone treated Asuka's exceptional performance as proof that she required no support. `HIBIKE-V03 / S02 / P0548-P0552`

This makes Haruka an institutional thinker. Her strength is not omnipotent competence but distributed responsibility:

- ask who can substitute;
- make roles legible;
- admit dependency;
- prevent one person's brilliance from becoming a single point of failure;
- and continue the work when the charismatic center is absent.

Her baritone saxophone performance likewise matters because it allows Haruka to become visible through her own musical voice rather than only through the presidency. The volume's broader argument about leadership is therefore not anti-excellence; it is anti-mystification.

## 8. Nakagawa Natsuki and Nakaseko Kaori — support without converting love into entitlement

### Natsuki

Natsuki practices seriously as Asuka's substitute even though she wants Asuka to return. That combination is ethically important. She does not make institutional readiness conditional on getting the emotional outcome she wants.

For the character model, this confirms a recurring Natsuki pattern:

- low-theatricality care;
- practical preparation;
- generous response to someone else's success;
- resistance to turning disappointment into a claim on another person.

Her work means Asuka's return can be wanted rather than coerced by institutional desperation.

### Kaori

Kaori's attachment to Asuka remains intense, but V03 does not give her privileged access to Asuka's interior simply because she loves her. This is one of the series' recurring relational principles: **devotion does not automatically confer knowledge**.

Kaori can remain physically and emotionally devoted while Asuka keeps decisive parts of herself inaccessible. This qualifies romantic or yuri-coded readings without invalidating them: the intensity is real; symmetry and transparency are not guaranteed.

## 9. Kousaka Reina — direct in conviction, vulnerable in attachment

V03 continues to falsify any one-word model of Reina as merely “blunt.”

### 9.1 She is willing to state an unpleasant assessment of Asuka

When Kumiko wants reassurance, Reina can instead point to the practical case that Asuka may no longer be the only viable choice. Kumiko recognizes that Reina is not trying to hurt Asuka; she is naming a reality Kumiko does not want to face. `HIBIKE-V03 / S03 / P0161`

### 9.2 She rejects protection that removes her choice

The Taki-wife scene is more valuable for simulation than its romantic plot function. Reina's complaint is not “you hurt me.” It is that Kumiko decided unilaterally that she should not know.

`知ってる。それでも、アタシは教えてほしかってん。` `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0797-P0803`

Reina wants difficult truth if the alternative is being managed without consent. This creates a strong addressee-specific prediction: **when Reina trusts someone, concealment “for her own good” is likely to feel more violating than painful information delivered directly.**

### 9.3 Her self-image includes an expectation of unusual strength

Reina is surprised not simply that Taki had a wife, but that the knowledge destabilizes her. She says she thought she was stronger. `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P0804-P0807`

This matters because her exceptionalism is not only outward ambition. It is also a standard she applies to her own emotional regulation. Vulnerability can therefore produce secondary shame: she is upset, then upset that she was upset.

### 9.4 The public confession illustrates domain mismatch

Reina tells Taki that she likes him and chose Kitauji because he is there. Taki interprets the statement within the teacher-student frame and thanks her as a teacher. `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P1438-P1440`

The scene is comic, but analytically important: **direct wording does not guarantee shared framing**. Reina can be linguistically explicit while the relationship's institutional context determines what the addressee can hear.

## 10. Taki Noboru — public authority, private motive, and fear of imposition

V03 gives Taki a private reason for caring about national gold: the dream is tied to his late wife. The novel nevertheless does not collapse his public authority into that private motive.

Three layers should remain distinct:

1. **student-authored objective** — the band voted to aim for nationals;
2. **expert implementation** — Taki controls rehearsal, selection, interpretation, and performance preparation;
3. **private inherited motive** — nationals also carries personal meaning because of his wife.

The fact that layer 3 exists does not automatically delegitimize layers 1–2. But Taki himself worries about this exact problem. After nationals he admits uncertainty that he may simply have forced his own desired path upon the students. `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P1431-P1434`

That self-doubt significantly strengthens the institutional model from V01. Taki is not portrayed as a pure technocrat confident that expertise resolves legitimacy. He knows that effective leadership can still become paternalistic.

The interaction with Asuka's mother also clarifies his normative floor: he refuses to treat parental instruction as identical to the student's will. `HIBIKE-V03 / S02 / P0391-P0402`

## 11. Tsukamoto Shuuichi — ordinary intimacy becomes structurally consequential

V03 continues the V2 correction of Shuuichi's underweighting.

He is not narratively spectacular, but that is part of his function. His relationship with Kumiko is built from accumulated ordinary familiarity rather than exceptional staging. He knows family history, notices changes in her mood, practices within the same institutional routine, and participates in the kind of low-drama co-regulation that the more spectacular Kumiko-Reina bond does not replace.

The trombone chapter deliberately places him against Mamiko's history: Kumiko began music partly through admiration for Mamiko's trombone, and Shuuichi now occupies the continuing trombone line inside her present life. This does not make him a substitute for Mamiko; it makes music a medium through which childhood, family, and peer intimacy overlap.

The Italian White hair ornament also matters because the series repeatedly prefers small material tokens over abstract declarations. The relationship is not yet fully resolved here, but V03 establishes a more explicit romantic baseline that the anthology will later test from outside Kumiko's focalization.

## 12. Institution — resilience means surviving the absence of the exceptional

Kitauji's “greatest crisis” is not that Asuka may leave. It is that the band discovers how much organizational knowledge, emotional regulation, musical authority, and symbolic stability it has concentrated in one person.

The correct institutional response is therefore double:

- **build substitution capacity** so the organization can continue without Asuka;
- **refuse to confuse substitution capacity with proof that Asuka does not matter personally.**

Natsuki's preparation is not a betrayal of Asuka. It is institutional health. Haruka's ability to lead in the crisis is not proof that Asuka was never important. It is the distribution of responsibility that should have existed earlier.

V03 thus develops V01's bounded student authorship and V02's non-retroactive justice into a third principle:

> **A humane institution should not make any person functionally indispensable, because functional indispensability makes voluntary participation harder to distinguish from coercion.**

At the same time, the novel resists the bureaucratic opposite: people are not reducible to roles merely because roles are replaceable.

## 13. Music and pedagogy — from repeatability to truthful performance

### 13.1 Nationals exposes the difference between elite ceiling and elite stability

V02 established repeatability as the mark of mature competitive craft. V03 carries that lesson into nationals. Kitauji now stands among institutions for whom a “good day” is not enough.

The technical preparation is therefore no longer about proving that improvement is possible. It is about reducing the variance between what the ensemble can do and what it reliably does under maximum pressure.

### 13.2 The performer does not fully hear the performance the audience receives

During the national performance, Kumiko's experience is partial and embodied: breath, entries, neighboring timbres, muscle memory, fear of mistakes, the local sensation of the euphonium line. The novel repeatedly resists the fantasy that a performer possesses an objective God's-eye account of the ensemble while playing.

This is a useful epistemic parallel to the social plot. In music as in relationships, participation gives privileged access to some information and blocks other information.

### 13.3 Bronze does not falsify the performance

The bronze result is painful precisely because Kumiko now owns the goal. But the narrative refuses the opposite simplification that “the ranking doesn't matter.” The ranking matters; the band did not achieve what it wanted.

At the same time, a performance can be meaningful, disciplined, and personally true without receiving the desired external classification. Shindō's C and personal praise make the distinction explicit. `HIBIKE-V03 / S04 / P1452-P1477`

The volume therefore rejects both:

- **rank nihilism** — results are arbitrary so nothing can be learned;
- **rank totalism** — the result exhausts the value or truth of the performance.

## 14. Family, material conditions, and constrained agency

V03 deepens the socioeconomic/material layer without turning the novel into a total class thesis.

Asuka's academic excellence is supported and constrained by a household that treats prestigious education as protection. Reina's prior musical infrastructure remains exceptional. Mamiko's university and vocational choices carry real material stakes. Taki's professional network and inherited musical culture matter. Kumiko's own family can support long-term instrumental participation and educational experimentation in ways that are easy for the characters to treat as normal.

The key V2 question is not “who is privileged?” as a moral ranking. It is:

> **What resources, obligations, threats, and permissions make a particular choice realistically available to this person?**

Asuka is a particularly important example because material support and coercive control coexist. Access to education does not equal autonomy.

## 15. Voice and register findings

### Kumiko

- **Baseline:** standard Japanese, relationship-sensitive informality.
- **Crisis change:** long unedited turns, first-person `私` claims, repetition, rising intensity.
- **Key negative constraint:** V03 directness should not be generalized into a permanent default; it appears after safer formulations fail.
- **Care failure mode:** conceals painful information when she believes disclosure would cause avoidable harm.

### Asuka

- **Public mode:** lively Kansai-flavored performance, joking, rhetorical questions, teasing, rapid reframing.
- **Strategic mode:** cool, compressed, often frighteningly rational; can convert personal issues into system descriptions.
- **Family-crisis mode:** placating, managerial, minimizing of harm to herself.
- **Private mode:** capable of small, simple admissions (`うちも`) once risk is sufficiently reduced.
- **Negative constraint:** emotionally explicit self-disclosure without prior control of the scene is unlikely at this state.

### Reina

- **Conviction mode:** short, categorical, direct.
- **Attachment mode:** can become physically close, embarrassed, repetitive, reassurance-seeking, and self-critical.
- **Truth preference:** explicit evidence now supports that she prefers painful truth from trusted people over protective concealment.

### Mamiko

- Mature/older-sister authority remains in her ordinary speech, but under conflict she exposes resentment and uncertainty the family previously mistook for settled judgment.

### Haruka

- Her most important speech is not charismatic command but self-implicating diagnosis. She persuades by admitting weakness and naming distributed responsibility.

### Taki

- Controlled polite Japanese remains the default even under confrontation. His strongest assertions are often procedural rather than emotionally forceful, which increases rather than reduces their authority.

## 18. Opening-trilogy cumulative delta

Across V01–V03, the governing movement is now:

> **deniable desire → plural and contested desire → accountable first-person agency under uncertainty → transmission**

This is stronger than a simple “Kumiko learns to want” model because it identifies the changing epistemic burden of agency.

1. **V01:** Kumiko must admit that an outcome matters to her.
2. **V02:** she discovers that other people's interior worlds cannot be safely inferred from her own ranking of relationships or from institutional fairness.
3. **V03:** she must still act without possessing total knowledge, but the ethical form of action changes: speak from one's own desire, do not falsely ventriloquize everyone else's.
4. **Epilogue:** agency becomes inheritance when Asuka gives Kumiko not a command to imitate her but material to transmit onward.

The same movement occurs institutionally:

> slogan → chosen objective → contested legitimacy → distributed resilience → transmission of practice.

And musically:

> technical reconstruction → expressive ownership → repeatability under pressure → truthful performance despite imperfect ranking → repertoire passed onward.
