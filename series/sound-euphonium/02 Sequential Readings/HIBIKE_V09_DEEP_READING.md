## 2. Executive thesis: adaptive legitimacy after the promise of fairness

HIBIKE-V08 asked whether fair rules could become believable and socially livable. HIBIKE-V09 supplies a harder test: **what happens when fair procedure, genuine care, artistic excellence, and desired outcomes do not all point in the same direction?**

Kitauji performs well enough to receive gold at Kansai. Taki tells the ensemble that it is substantially better than at Kyoto and that he believes the students are the best performers that day. Yuuko frames all eighty-nine members as the people taking nationals together, regardless of which students physically appear on the A stage. Yet Kitauji does not receive one of the three national berths. `HIBIKE-V09 / S04 / P1400-P1410`; `P1460-P1498`

The result matters because the novel does not explain it through a moral accounting trick. There is no single catastrophic error whose correction would restore a just universe. The students can plausibly have done many things right and still lose to ensembles judged better. One other gold-but-not-advancing school is satisfied with its result; Kitauji is devastated because its goal was higher. `S04 / P1494-P1498`

That same non-equivalence structures the relationship plot.

Mizore can love Nozomi profoundly without Nozomi returning the same kind or degree of feeling. Nozomi can envy Mizore, feel inferior to her, want to remain significant to her, and still produce disciplined musical support. Mizore can accept Nozomi's divergent future without ceasing to love her. Nozomi can refuse a symmetric `大好き` while still making a real choice to stand beside Mizore musically. Their growth does not require sentimental equality.

The strongest V2 formulation is therefore:

> **The second-year movement matures from fairness-as-rule into adaptive legitimacy: excellence, care, and belonging cannot be collapsed into one criterion; leadership must create conditions in which people can risk honest performance, revise defenses, and survive outcomes without pretending that the outcome morally validates or invalidates them. Relational maturity likewise means differentiation without retroactively denying attachment.**

This extends V08 rather than replacing it. V08's recognition-before-correction model survives, but V09 adds an **intervention threshold and epistemic brake**. Kumiko briefly wants to shatter Mizore's idealization of Nozomi because she believes the truth would be better for her. After observing Natsuki and Yuuko, she recognizes that an outsider can mistake moral urgency for entitlement to restructure someone else's relationship. She later exposes contradiction without dictating Mizore's conclusion. `S04 / P0481-P0553`; `P0596-P0629`

The movement's durable sequence is:

> **credible rules → recognition of defended values → supported exposure → differentiated agency → relative defeat → adaptive redesign and transmission**

The endpoint is not victory. It is an institution and a set of people better able to continue after an outcome they did not want.

## 3. V08 stress test: which models survive?

V09 provides unusually clean longitudinal backtesting because V08 ended with multiple unsettled models.

### Kanade

The V08 model survives. Once her effort has been specifically recognized and her self-sabotage exposed, Kanade does not remain trapped in permanent crisis. She and Natsuki can practice in a recognizably reciprocal, still-prickly relationship. The earlier defense therefore should not be treated as a timeless essence. It was a high-threat strategy. V09's relative lack of crisis is itself evidence of state change.

### Mirei

Her self-authored experiment in approachability also survives. By Kansai, `みっちゃん` is no longer merely an externally imposed nickname; the hairpin interaction with Satsuki shows affectionate reciprocity and embarrassment without converting Mirei into an effusive person. `HIBIKE-V09 / S04 / P1358-P1366`

### Yume

V08 correctly identified visibility, not technical weakness, as the central problem. V09 strengthens the model with supported exposure. Blurring an audience through removed glasses is only a partial coping strategy. The stronger intervention is to make the audience relationally specific: Tomoe asks Yume to play *to her*, as one trusted listener whose response can be seen. `S05 / P0218-P0292`

### Yuuko and Natsuki

V08's overfunctioning warning becomes explicit structural evidence. Natsuki catches Yuuko working while sick, insists that one person carrying everything simply recreates the Asuka problem, and forces delegation. `S04 / P0489-P0547` V09 therefore validates the Rikka-derived lesson inside Kitauji: **a culture of care fails if the caregiver exempts herself from care**.

### Mizore and Nozomi

V08 correctly froze Mizore's future as non-autonomous. V09 changes that state. By the end, Mizore says she will continue oboe even without Nozomi because music is something Nozomi gave her. `S05 / P0192-P0200` This is not retroactive proof that V08 Mizore was already independent. It is the result of V09's work.

## 4. Structural architecture: admiration, silence, sonority, held time

### 4.1 Prologue — Nozomi finally becomes focalized

The prologue enters Nozomi's interiority directly. This is decisive because earlier books often made her the object of other people's readings.

Mizore's gaze produces in Nozomi an `曖昧な嫌悪`, a bodily, difficult-to-name aversion despite Nozomi's cheerful exterior. `HIBIKE-V09 / S01 / P0004` This does **not** establish hatred. It establishes that being looked at with Mizore's intensity is uncomfortable in a way Nozomi herself has difficulty integrating.

Nozomi also values having a place: seeing her bag where she expects it produces the thought `居場所がある`. `S01 / P0009` She wants to be the person capable of supporting Mizore's oboe in *Liz*. `S01 / P0012` Yet she simultaneously believes Mizore cannot understand how meaningful Nozomi's own solo opportunity feels because Mizore receives solos regularly. `S01 / P0015-P0016`

The key result is asymmetry without indifference. Nozomi cares about the relationship, but not from the same position as Mizore.

Her fairy-tale reading initially casts herself as the blue bird: if she once left, she can return, and tragedy need not remain tragedy. `S01 / P0021-P0024` The volume will overturn that role assignment.

### 4.2 「憧憬にアウフタクト」 — admiration starts motion but does not govern outcome

The Kyoto contest confirms Kitauji's competitive strength: the school receives gold and advances as a Kyoto representative. `S02 / P0390`

But the chapter also begins positioning another rising institution, Ryuusei, as a mirror. Kitauji once occupied the narrative role of underestimated school transformed by an exceptional conductor. Now it is an incumbent watching another school acquire the disruptive aura it once possessed. The series therefore denies permanent ownership of the underdog story.

### 4.3 「独白とタチェット」 — the decisive statements are not shared with the person who needs them

Nozomi tells Kumiko things she has not told Mizore. She distinguishes wanting to become a professional from simply wanting to continue playing: `プロになりたい` and `楽器を続けたい` are not the same thing. `S03 / P0675` She is uncertain which future she actually wants, has multiple possible schools in mind, and has not corrected Mizore's assumption because she does not think the uncertainty is worth a formal declaration. `S03 / P0679-P0682`

The ethical problem is therefore not reducible to one malicious lie. A once-plausible statement has been allowed to continue organizing another person's future after the speaker's certainty has dissolved.

### 4.4 「彼女のソノリテ」 — internal truth becomes audible, and the ensemble must adapt

The central chapter produces the artistic breakthrough. Niiyama does not simply demand more emotion from Mizore. She changes the representational role Mizore inhabits: if Mizore cannot become Liz because she cannot imagine letting a loved person go, she should imagine the blue bird instead. `S04 / P0668-P0709`

Mizore then finds an emotionally inhabitable model: the blue bird can accept Liz's choice because she loves Liz; sadness does not invalidate release. Her playing opens.

The crucial technical point follows. Mizore's expanded sonority overwhelms the ensemble, but the narration explicitly says she remains within the specified tempo and score. `S04 / P0730-P0747` Taki's response is not to suppress the individual back to the old balance. He rebalances the collective around the stronger reality.

This becomes one of V09's most important musical-institutional principles:

> **When legitimate individual capacity exceeds an inherited ensemble balance, adaptation may require the ensemble to change rather than the individual to shrink.**

### 4.5 「未来へフェルマータ」 — the held ending contains loss, redesign, and succession

The Kansai result stops Kitauji before nationals, but the volume refuses to make stopping equivalent to meaninglessness. Yuuko turns the result into the first day of the next attempt. Tomoe and Yume create a performance success outside the competition hierarchy. Leadership is redesigned. Mizore and Nozomi differentiate. Kumiko suspends romance. The fermata holds multiple futures at once.

### 4.6 Epilogue — Team Oumae is distributed authority, not a new Asuka

The new officers are Kumiko as president, Shuuichi as vice-president, and Reina as an independently constituted drum major. `S06 / P0027-P0037` The separation of drum-major authority is explicitly adopted to reduce role burden.

A strong **[D/E]** synthesis is that the new system answers the risk of excessive concentration embodied by Asuka and reenacted by Yuuko. But the text itself establishes the role split and burden reduction; it does not state an abstract “Asuka's curse” doctrine. V2 keeps those levels separate.

## 5. Nozomi: admiration, aversion, equality, and the need not to be left below

V09 makes Nozomi much harder to reduce to either “the socially healthy one” or “the person who failed Mizore.”

### 5.1 Her discomfort is real

Nozomi's bodily aversion to Mizore's gaze is direct interior evidence. `S01 / P0004` The significance is not that Mizore is bad for loving her. The significance is that the relationship asks Nozomi to occupy a degree of emotional centrality she does not experience symmetrically.

### 5.2 She also wants to remain special

At the pool invitation, Mizore asks whether Ririka can join. Nozomi drops or crumples what she is holding and her smile tightens. `S03 / P0198-P0241` Later Nozomi directly admits jealousy and competitive injury. The V2 model therefore cannot explain her discomfort solely as burden from Mizore's dependence. Mizore's expansion away from her can also hurt.

### 5.3 Her “not especially close” formulation is not emotional neutrality

Nozomi tells Kumiko that she and Mizore were never, in her own framing, especially close: Mizore is a friend she likes among other friends. She also says there are times she cannot approach Mizore and even wonders whether Mizore hates her. `S03 / P0635-P0704`

This is a much stronger model than “Nozomi does not care.” She wants a relationship with less totalizing centrality, while simultaneously wanting significance within it.

### 5.4 Talent converts admiration into threatened equality

When Mizore's full sound emerges, Nozomi cries not because she is simply moved by beauty, but because the gap between them becomes impossible to deny. `S04 / P0792-P0839`

Nozomi admits that she wanted to believe she should be better because she loved music more, worked harder, and suffered more. `S04 / P0808` That claim is **Nozomi's self-justifying belief [A/C]**, not an omniscient fact about how much either girl “really” loves music.

She also admits that Niiyama's attention to Mizore initially felt like favoritism until she had to accept that the adult was simply recognizing stronger talent. `S04 / P0810-P0814`

The music-college claim then becomes legible as a symbolic equality strategy: if Mizore is moving somewhere Nozomi might not be able to follow on ability, saying she will go there too protects a shared level.

### 5.5 Her answer to “do you like Mizore?” remains bounded

Kumiko asks directly. Nozomi answers: `嫌いじゃない。それだけはほんま`. `S04 / P0817-P0819`

This is not a reciprocal declaration of love. It is also not nothing. The response is characteristically bounded: Nozomi refuses to claim an emotion more expansive than she can honestly own.

### 5.6 Her musical support becomes resistance

After realizing Mizore is the blue bird, Nozomi decides she will support the oboe perfectly because it is her `唯一の抵抗`, her only resistance and her only way of maintaining a sense of equality. She explicitly says this is not reducible to liking or disliking. `S04 / P0824-P0829`

This gives the project a valuable new rule:

> **Negative emotion can be converted into disciplined prosocial performance without first becoming positive emotion.**

A model that requires envy to disappear before Nozomi can behave well would be false.

## 6. Mizore: from attachment-as-survival to gift-without-co-presence

### 6.1 Distrust remains at the center before growth

Mizore says she cannot trust Nozomi because Nozomi may disappear at any time: `信用、できるはずがない / だって、希美はいついなくなるかわからない`. `S04 / P0277-P0281`

She cannot identify with Liz because she cannot imagine letting the blue bird go. Her instinct is to keep the bird forever.

### 6.2 Generic intimacy scripts fail

Kumiko proposes the old `大好きのハグ` ritual as a possible bridge. Mizore strongly rejects it and explains that she hated the South Middle ritual and never participated, even with Nozomi. `S04 / P0288-P0305`

This is a high-value negative constraint. Mizore is **not** someone for whom induced group intimacy automatically creates closeness. Generic social ritual can feel coercive precisely when the relationship matters.

### 6.3 Kumiko's challenge creates contradiction, not a conclusion

Mizore says she wants Nozomi always beside her and that what Nozomi decides is what she decides. `S04 / P0596-P0629` Kumiko does not reveal Nozomi's hidden uncertainty. Instead, she asks why Mizore is selecting only one music school when Nozomi herself has multiple possible paths.

The intervention is effective because it does not say “stop depending on Nozomi.” It makes Mizore confront an inconsistency in her own model and leaves the interpretation to her.

### 6.4 Aesthetic attachment pluralizes

Mizore asks Kumiko to play with her again and says `その音、好き`. `S04 / P0635-P0639` This is small but important: Nozomi is no longer the only person whose sound can become personally meaningful.

### 6.5 Niiyama changes the role rather than forcing a feeling

Mizore cannot become Liz. Niiyama therefore asks her to become the blue bird. The lesson is not that love means painless separation. Mizore's breakthrough preserves sadness: the bird can be sad and still accept Liz's choice because it loves Liz. `S04 / P0668-P0709`

This is a deeper model of differentiation than detachment. Love does not require either captivity or emotional anesthesia.

### 6.6 Full sonority reveals agency

Mizore herself asks to play from the beginning to obtain the flow. `S04 / P0712-P0718` That proactive request matters because the character is often modeled as merely compliant.

Her sound then becomes so large that the ensemble shrinks around it. The score is not violated; the inherited balance is. `S04 / P0730-P0747`

### 6.7 The confrontation does not equalize the relationship

Before Kansai, Mizore says the old abandonment is not merely past: `昔じゃない。私にとっては、ずっといま`. `S04 / P1251-P1307` She explains that she practiced partly because she feared being abandoned again. When Nozomi argues that this diminishes Mizore's own effort, Mizore produces the cleanest formulation of their asymmetry:

> `オーボエが軽いんじゃない。希美が、重すぎるの。私にとって`

The oboe is not light; Nozomi is too heavy for her.

Mizore then initiates the very hug ritual she once hated. The difference is agency. The act is no longer imposed group convention; it is self-authored relational speech. She says `私、希美のこと、大好き`. Nozomi pauses and answers `私も、みぞれのオーボエ大好き`. The object changes from person to instrument. `S04 / P1251-P1307`

V09 refuses sentimental symmetry.

### 6.8 The durable endpoint is inheritance

After the competition, Mizore says she will continue oboe even without Nozomi because music is something Nozomi gave her. `S05 / P0192-P0200`

This is the second-year movement's mature relational logic:

> **what another person gave you can remain constitutive without requiring that person to remain physically beside you in the same form.**

The relation changes from chain to inheritance without pretending that the chain never existed.

## 7. Kumiko: recognition-first mediation acquires an epistemic brake

V08's Kumiko model survives but becomes less flattering and therefore more useful.

### 7.1 Her failure mode is moral urgency under incomplete information

After learning more about Nozomi's hidden future uncertainty, Kumiko briefly wants to break Mizore's worship and reset the relationship “for Mizore's sake.” `S04 / P0481-P0485`

This is exactly the kind of failure the project needs for simulation. Kumiko is not simply a wise listener. When she thinks harm is occurring and possesses enough partial information to feel morally certain, she can become interventionist before she knows enough.

### 7.2 Yuuko/Natsuki creates an epistemic brake

Kumiko then observes Natsuki stop an overworked, sick Yuuko. Natsuki recognizes both Yuuko's limits and her own right to intervene as a close counterpart inside that leadership relationship. `S04 / P0489-P0553`

Kumiko realizes that an outsider can mistake “I can see what is wrong” for “I have the standing to destroy the current balance.” She abandons the plan to force a Nozomi/Mizore rupture.

The durable V09 mediation sequence becomes:

1. distinguish immediate harm from difficult but partially known relationship;
2. identify whose knowledge and authority are missing;
3. recognize the value being defended;
4. expose contradiction or consequence;
5. preserve the actor's ability to decide;
6. refuse exoneration when conduct was harmful.

### 7.3 Recognition does not mean absolution

With Nozomi, Kumiko understands envy and insecurity without excusing the deception. She tells Nozomi that the lying was terrible and refuses to absolve it, while also saying she cannot hate her. `S04 / P0817-P0839`

That is a crucial refinement of “empathy.” V09's preferred category is **recognition without exoneration**.

### 7.4 Kumiko's own romantic problem mirrors her mediation problem

Kumiko is increasingly able to preserve other people's differentiated attachments, but she treats her own role conflict more rigidly. She suspends the relationship with Shuuichi after accepting the presidency, returning the white sunflower ornament and asking him to return it after the year if he still wants to. `S05 / P0377-P0391`

This is self-authored agency, but it is also defensive simplification. She assumes romance and leadership are incompatible before testing whether the relationship could adapt. Her discomfort with being comforted by Shuuichi—his kindness can make her feel dependent or indebted—helps explain the move. `S04 / P1178-P1199`

So `KUMIKO@V09` should not be modeled as “mature enough to solve everyone else.” She has become much better at preserving others' agency while still using preemptive role simplification on herself.

## 8. Yuuko and Natsuki: leadership, care, and the danger of making oneself the exception

### 8.1 Yuuko's care is real

Yuuko consistently tries to preserve an environment in which students can perform rather than break. Her decision to allow Yume to move away from the originally assigned first-trumpet position is made because she fears forcing the visibility-anxious first-year will produce worse playing and harm the student. `S04 / P0260-P0275`

Reina understands the rationale while fearing the accumulation of `少しずつの妥協`.

### 8.2 Humane decisions can remain competitively costly

The novel does not resolve the Reina/Yuuko tension by declaring one side morally correct. Mizore answers Reina with `無理やりは長持ちしない。いきなり壊れる`: forced performance does not last and can break suddenly. `S04 / P0260-P0275`

After the Kansai loss, Yuuko retrospectively wonders whether prioritizing cohesion and protection was self-satisfying care. `S05 / P0024-P0030` But the narration explicitly preserves uncertainty: a terrified Yume might not have performed better in first position. V2 therefore rejects the causal shortcut **“kindness caused the loss.”**

The stronger model is:

> **defeat reopens causal hypotheses without proving any one humane decision was wrong.**

### 8.3 Yuuko's self-exemption is the actual structural failure

Natsuki catches Yuuko ill and still working. Yuuko says she must push because she is president. Natsuki rejects the premise and invokes the earlier Asuka problem: if one person carries everything, the institution has learned the wrong lesson. `S04 / P0489-P0547`

Kumiko asks the question the leadership ethic requires: if Yuuko wants a club where people are not driven into intolerable stress, does that policy include Yuuko herself?

This yields a durable rule:

> **caregiving systems are unstable when the caregiver defines herself as outside the population entitled to care.**

### 8.4 Natsuki's strength is epistemic self-limitation

Natsuki also knows where *her* care becomes bias. She tells Kumiko that she will be too soft on Nozomi and that Yuuko is too soft on Mizore, so Kumiko should be the one to speak with Nozomi. `S04 / P0756-P0773`

That is a mature leadership behavior: not “I know best,” but “I know where my affection makes me a worse instrument for this task.”

## 9. Yume and Tomoe: supported exposure, not forced visibility

Yume's V08 model becomes one of the cleanest examples of relationship-conditioned performance psychology.

### 9.1 Avoidance is defensive, not laziness

Yume says directly that she is afraid of performing in front of people, afraid of real performance, and hates solos, visibility, and responsibility. A middle-school solo failure helped create the logic that if she does not attempt the exposed task, she cannot fail it. `S04 / P0962-P1136`

Her self-deprecating style is also focalized as genuine rather than a Kanade-like calculated social performance. `S04 / P1075-P1076`

### 9.2 Sensory occlusion is only partial coping

Tomoe first removes Yume's glasses so the observers blur. This reduces evaluative salience but does not create a durable performance model.

Kumiko offers a smaller exposure target: hold eye contact, then attempt that with Reina. Reina later deliberately meets Yume's gaze. `S04 / P1143-P1154`

### 9.3 Tomoe makes the audience one trusted person

For the ensemble concert, Yume again fails under observation. Pity and suggestions that Reina replace her worsen the anxiety. `S05 / P0218-P0238`

Tomoe does something more precise. She first establishes her own standing as a listener: she was proud of the Kansai performance and proud to contribute as manager. Then she asks whether she has ever abandoned Yume because of mistakes. Finally she gives Yume a concrete audience: `うちに向かって吹けばええ`; `最高の演奏を、うちにちょうだい`. `S05 / P0246-P0282`

Yume asks for her glasses back because playing to Tomoe is meaningless if she cannot see Tomoe's happy face. She then succeeds. `S05 / P0290-P0292`

This adds a simulation rule that should survive into later character modeling:

> **Performance anxiety can change when an anonymous evaluative crowd becomes a specific trusted addressee.**

This is not a universal cure. It is a mechanism documented in Yume's state.

## 10. Taki and Niiyama: expert authority as adaptive diagnosis

V09 provides unusually strong evidence that expertise is not merely enforcement of an existing ideal.

### 10.1 “Not wrong” is not yet “right”

Taki distinguishes technically permissible playing from a shared intended sound. `S04 / P0046` Ensemble truth requires more than absence of obvious error.

### 10.2 Technique can be blocked by representation

Mizore's problem is not simply that she lacks expressive capacity. Her interpretation of the story makes one role psychologically impossible. Niiyama solves this by changing the role from Liz to the blue bird. `S04 / P0668-P0709`

The lesson is pedagogically important: sometimes the bottleneck is **the representational frame through which technique is organized**, not a missing technical instruction.

### 10.3 Stronger truth can require changing the ensemble

Once Mizore produces her full sound, Taki adapts the ensemble around it rather than ordering her to return to the old equilibrium. `S04 / P0730-P0747`

Expert authority is thus capable of updating the target model when new capacity appears.

### 10.4 Time spent is not identical to effort

Taki later distinguishes simply spending time from effective practice and distinguishes limits of the instrument from shortcomings that the player can improve. `S04 / P1165-P1173`

This extends the V08 Kanade/Mirei problem. Visible time is neither meaningless nor sufficient. Practice has a learning mechanism and must produce retained change.

## 11. Competition: excellence is relative, and failure does not necessarily identify an error

### 11.1 Kitauji's performance is strongly validated

Taki tells the students before results that the performance is much better than Kyoto and that he believes they are the best players that day. `S04 / P1400` Yuuko frames all eighty-nine students as the best members taking the result together. `S04 / P1410`

Kumiko's required-piece solo is performed cleanly and the ensemble completes *Liz*. `S04 / P1436-P1444`

Kitauji receives gold. `S04 / P1460`

### 11.2 Gold is not the target

The third national berth goes to Ryuusei. `S04 / P1479` Reina breaks down: `悔しくって、死にそう`. `S04 / P1494`

Another gold-but-not-advancing school is satisfied. `S04 / P1497-P1498` The contrast makes the structure explicit: the emotional meaning of an external result depends partly on the goal against which the result is evaluated.

### 11.3 The novel does not omnisciently certify cross-year superiority

Yuuko later says she thinks this year's performance was better than the previous national-qualifying year's. `S05 / P0024-P0030` V1 treated the stronger-year comparison as settled fact. V2 revises this: **Yuuko's conviction is direct; objective cross-year superiority is not omnisciently established.**

### 11.4 Nothing obviously wrong can still end in defeat

Kumiko eventually grieves with Shuuichi and asks why effort did not pay when they did nothing wrong. `S05 / P0159-P0168` Shuuichi allows the possibility that there was no single moral or procedural error to identify.

This is one of the series' strongest anti-merit-entitlement claims:

> **effort can increase capacity without creating a debt the world must repay with victory.**

Evaluation is relative. Competitors also improve. The underdog can become the incumbent. The narrative does not reserve advancement for the protagonist institution.

## 12. Ryuusei as mirror: Kitauji no longer owns the underdog story

Ryuusei's rise is narratively important because it prevents Kitauji's transformation from becoming a permanent exceptional status.

Kitauji was once the school whose rapid improvement disrupted expectations. Ryuusei now occupies a similar role. The competitive field remembers no protagonist privilege. Institutions move relative to one another.

This strengthens the broader institutional model:

> **successful reform changes the baseline against which future success is judged; it does not freeze comparative advantage.**

That is why “we are better than last year” cannot by itself answer whether the band advances this year.

## 13. Motomu and Midori: recognition can include the right not to disclose

At Kansai, a Ryuusei student calls Motomu `月永` and invites him to greet people connected with “Gen-chan sensei.” Motomu refuses strongly. The Ryuusei conductor's full name is revealed as `月永源一郎`. `S04 / P1511-P1545`

The source deliberately stops short of specifying the exact family relationship in V09.

## 14. Kanade and Mirei after V08: repairs become ordinary life

V09 does not need another Kanade crisis to prove development. That absence itself matters.

Kanade and Natsuki can now practice in a reciprocal relationship. Kanade remains observant, socially strategic, competitive, and capable of polished self-presentation. The audition repair has not transformed her into an uncomplicated believer in people. ## 15. Kumiko and Shuuichi: comfort, debt, and defensive simplification

V09 gives the romance a more exact causal model before suspending it.

### 15.1 Labels are lossy

In conversation with Reina, Kumiko says she does not clearly understand the difference between `like` and `love`. Reina uses an overlapping-set model. Kumiko's most important response is that labels may make feelings begin conforming to the labels. `S03 / P0422-P0481`

This is not merely dialogue about romance. It is a methodological warning for the reconstruction project itself:

> **relationship labels are lossy compression and can become performative scripts.**

### 15.2 Romance is pleasant and constricting at once

Shuuichi tries to kiss Kumiko; she reflexively kicks him and bans kisses/embarrassing romantic behavior while club activity continues. `S03 / P0306-P0320` Their study date is comfortable, yet the lover role creates self-monitoring and guilt. `S03 / P0706-P0738`

### 15.3 His kindness can feel dangerous because it asks for nothing

After hard rehearsal, Shuuichi's unconditional kindness comforts Kumiko enough that she thinks `好きだ`, but the same kindness frightens her because she feels she has nothing adequate to return and may be weakened by relying on it. `S04 / P1178-P1199`

The relationship therefore challenges Kumiko's competence/self-sufficiency defense in a way overt demand would not.

### 15.4 The breakup is chosen, but not necessarily the only workable choice

After accepting the presidency, Kumiko returns the white sunflower ornament, asks Shuuichi to give it back after the year if he still wants to, and explicitly says she will accept it if he falls for someone else. `S05 / P0377-P0391`

The text then states that they ceased to be lovers. `S05 / P0391`

V2 preserves the agency of the choice while refusing to canonize its premise. Kumiko has **not demonstrated** that presidency and romance are actually incompatible. She simplifies the role conflict in advance.

That creates an important negative constraint for `KUMIKO@V09`: when responsibility rises, she can respond by removing a source of emotional complexity rather than testing whether the relationship can adapt.

## 16. Kumiko and Reina: intimacy survives the refusal of clean categories

## 18. Leadership redesign: institutions learn when burden becomes architecture

Tomoe's manager role proves highly successful. She organizes data, room allocation, beginner follow-up, and grievance access. `S04 / P0900-P0929`

The students then discuss the fact that a two-officer structure is too small for a large, ambitious band. But simply forcing a nonperformer into management would reproduce a different unfairness. Midori gives the key structural insight: an organization that only works because one person sacrifices herself is badly designed.

The discussion draws explicitly on Rikka/Tachibana's multi-role structure. This is the first clean case where the V05–V06 autonomous Rikka model becomes **comparative institutional infrastructure** rather than decorative foil.

By the epilogue, drum major is made an independent office specifically to reduce burden. `S06 / P0027-P0028`

The mature institutional lesson is not “find a better heroic leader.” It is:

> **redesign roles so ordinary capable people do not have to imitate an exceptional predecessor's total load.**

## 19. Kumiko's succession: from accidental listener to deliberately cultivated president

Yuuko and Natsuki ask Kumiko to become president. `S05 / P0312-P0366`

Crucially, Yuuko reveals that the “Oumae Consultation Office” was not merely an accident. It was partly a deliberate strategy to give Kumiko influence among younger members. `S05 / P0351`

Their selection criteria combine multiple legitimacy dimensions:

- workable relationships across the cohort;
- enough A-ensemble musical credibility to be respected;
- judgment under conflict;
- and nearby people capable of stopping or supporting her. `S05 / P0353-P0356`

This revises the old image of Kumiko as someone who somehow wandered into leadership. Her emergent skill was real, but senior leadership also **noticed, scaffolded, and trained it**.

Shuuichi becomes vice-president and Reina drum major, meaning the new structure distributes social/organizational, practical, and musical-standard authority rather than locating all of it in one Asuka-like figure. That functional interpretation is a strong **[D/E]** model, while the role assignment itself is [A].

## 22. Major conceptual findings

### 22.1 Fairness cannot answer every moral question

Fair selection answers who should occupy a role under an evaluative criterion. It does not answer whether the loser feels wanted, whether the winner can survive the social result, whether the organization has distributed care, or whether the competition outcome is deserved.

### 22.2 Recognition is not exoneration

Understanding Nozomi's envy does not make the deception harmless. Understanding Kanade's fear did not make self-sabotage acceptable. V09 stabilizes a mature ethical pattern: **recognize mechanism, preserve responsibility**.

### 22.3 Differentiation is not abandonment

Mizore can choose a future in which Nozomi is not physically present without declaring the attachment false. This directly parallels the Rikka duology's autonomy-inside-attachment model.

### 22.4 Negative emotion can be behaviorally productive

Nozomi's envy becomes disciplined support. Yuuko's regret becomes institutional redesign. Reina's frustration becomes renewed competitive drive. Emotions do not have to become morally pure before they can be converted into constructive action.

### 22.5 Care can become structurally self-defeating

Yuuko's care for everyone else becomes dangerous when she assumes presidency means she personally must absorb the load. The ethical target is therefore not maximal self-sacrifice but sustainable distributed care.

### 22.6 Failure does not necessarily reveal a mistake

Kitauji's loss is the strongest case so far. The ensemble can improve, perform well, receive gold, and still fail the specific goal. “What did we do wrong?” may have no simple answer.

### 22.7 Labels can reshape what they describe

Kumiko's warning that relational labels can make feelings conform to the categories is a metatheoretical warning for all later synthesis. Character-model tags should aid retrieval, not overwrite contradictory local evidence.

### 22.8 Privacy can be a legitimate boundary on understanding

Midori's response to Motomu establishes that care sometimes means refusing to investigate a hidden fact simply because one can infer that a secret exists.

## 25. Simulation-grade predictions from the V09 boundary

These are probabilistic derived-use rules, not canonical future events.

### If Kumiko believes two people are in a harmful but ambiguous relationship

Most likely sequence:

1. gather accounts and infer a causal model;
2. experience a strong impulse to intervene if one party seems exploited;
3. check whether she actually has standing/knowledge after V09's near-overreach;
4. prefer exposing contradiction or consequence over dictating the relationship's meaning;
5. preserve accountability even when she recognizes insecurity;
6. remain vulnerable to overreach if the perceived harm is acute.

### If Nozomi is surpassed by someone she helped introduce to an activity

Likely:

- outward competence and friendliness first;
- internal comparison, envy, and threatened equality;
- resistance to sentimental disclosure;
- attempt to preserve significance through a domain where she can still contribute;
- capable of disciplined support even while the envy remains.

Unlikely: immediate pure celebration with no self-comparison.

### If Mizore must separate from a person she loves after V09

More likely than at V08 to preserve the relationship internally while making an independent practical choice. She may still experience separation intensely and may not become socially expansive, but “the person is absent” is no longer identical to “the gift/relationship has ceased to exist.”

### If Yume must perform before strangers

High threat remains. Improvement is more probable when:

- exposure is graded rather than total;
- at least one trusted listener is made salient;
- success is framed as giving sound to that person rather than defending self-worth before an anonymous crowd.

### If Yuuko is responsible for a group in crisis

She is likely to overfunction before asking for help. A trusted counterpart with permission to override her is therefore protective infrastructure. Generic advice to “rest” from a distant person is less likely to succeed than direct intervention from Natsuki-like authority.

### If Midori notices a friend concealing family history

Likely to honor the stated identity boundary unless the hidden fact creates direct safety/ethical stakes. Curiosity alone is insufficient to justify probing.

### If Kumiko faces role conflict in her own life

V09 warns that she may solve ambiguity by removing one role before testing coexistence. Her mediation sophistication for others should not be assumed to transfer automatically to self-application.

## 27. Cumulative delta after HIBIKE-V09

V09 closes the second-year movement and promotes several provisional V08 concepts into more durable longitudinal rules.

### 27.1 Legitimacy is adaptive, not static

V08 decomposed legitimacy into procedural, epistemic, and social layers. V09 shows that even all three are not a final equilibrium. Institutions encounter new capacities, injuries, competitive fields, and role loads. Legitimate practice therefore includes **the ability to redesign itself when the premises change**.

Taki rebalances the ensemble around Mizore. Natsuki redistributes Yuuko's work. The students redesign leadership after the manager experiment. Tomoe changes the evaluative situation around Yume. None of these are simple enforcement of a fixed rule.

The cumulative model is now:

1. **procedural legitimacy** — rules and decisions are defensible;
2. **epistemic legitimacy** — members can reasonably believe the rules are real;
3. **social legitimacy** — outcomes can be lived without informal persecution;
4. **adaptive legitimacy** — the institution can revise structures when new evidence exposes a bad fit.

### 27.2 Recognition-before-correction needs an intervention threshold

V08 established that Kanade needed recognition before challenge. V09 prevents this from becoming a simplistic helper algorithm.

Kumiko's near-overreach with Mizore shows that even a correct diagnosis can be ethically dangerous when the mediator lacks standing or complete information. Future models should therefore ask before intervention:

- Is there immediate harm requiring action?
- Who owns the relationship/problem?
- What does the mediator actually know?
- Is the proposed intervention exposing a contradiction, or simply replacing another person's judgment?
- Can the actor remain the author of the next move?

### 27.3 Differentiation should replace “independence” as the preferred relationship term

Mizore does not become a person who no longer needs anyone. She becomes capable of carrying forward something Nozomi gave her while accepting that Nozomi's future can differ.

The mature relationship variable is therefore not dependence ↔ independence as a binary. It is:

> **fusion / asymmetrical dependence / differentiated attachment / negotiated interdependence**

This also fits Azusa–Amika and later institutional succession more accurately.

### 27.4 Supported exposure should be tracked separately from reassurance

Yume demonstrates that performance fear is changed neither by technical competence alone nor by generic encouragement. The intervention changes the social geometry of evaluation.

Future character modeling should track:

- anonymous versus known audience;
- trusted versus threatening evaluator;
- size of exposure step;
- whether the task is framed as self-defense or as giving something to a specific person;
- whether the person can perceive the recipient's response.

### 27.5 Receiving recognition and receiving care are now joined by receiving dependence

The project already tracks receiving-care strategy and receiving-recognition strategy. V09 shows another recurrent question: **what does a person do when someone else needs them intensely?**

Nozomi's discomfort with Mizore is partly about being made emotionally central. Asuka has historically regulated other people even while harmed. Azusa liked being needed but feared becoming unnecessary. These are different positions on a common axis.

A mature character model should therefore track:

- comfort with being depended upon;
- desire to be needed;
- fear of being responsible for another's stability;
- tendency to encourage autonomy, preserve dependence, flee, or regulate.

### 27.6 Competitive grief is not evidence of moral failure

Reina's devastation and Kumiko's grief should not be psychologized away as unhealthy merely because another gold-without-advance school is satisfied. Their pain follows from the goal they actually owned.

The relevant ethical distinction is whether failure produces destructive entitlement or usable revision. V09 ends with the latter: Yuuko and the successors convert grief into changed structure and renewed commitment.

## 28. Second-year movement synthesis — V08 + V09

The V08–V09 movement can now be summarized as:

> **credible rules → recognition of defended values → supported exposure → differentiated agency → relative defeat → adaptive institutional redesign and transmission**

This sequence is not limited to one subplot.

**Kanade** begins with a rule-credibility/social-punishment problem. She receives recognition, is forced into honest performance, and becomes capable of ordinary reciprocal senior-junior relation afterward.

**Mirei** begins with a recognition/social-legibility conflict. Once her competence grievance is seen, she can choose limited approachability without surrendering identity.

**Yume** begins with visibility avoidance. Supported exposure creates one successful path through a trusted addressee rather than erasing anxiety by force.

**Mizore** begins with future choice fused to Nozomi. Recognition and representational reframing permit stronger performance and later differentiated continuation.

**Nozomi** begins with socially fluent ambiguity. Direct confrontation with inferiority makes her capable of honest boundedness and disciplined support without requiring symmetric feeling.

**Yuuko** begins as caring president. Defeat and overwork expose the limits of self-sacrificial leadership; succession becomes redesign rather than imitation.

**Kumiko** begins V08 as an emergent mediator and ends V09 as president. The important growth is not increased certainty. It is better control over the relation between knowledge, recognition, intervention, and agency—while retaining visible self-application failures.

The second year therefore answers the first year's inheritance problem. Kitauji does not reproduce itself by creating another Asuka, another Haruka, or another Yuuko. It creates a structure in which **Kumiko, Shuuichi, and Reina can remain different people while distributing functions that had previously accumulated in exceptional individuals**.

That is institutional reproduction without identity cloning.
