## 2. Executive local thesis

HIBIKE-V01 is most precisely a novel about **the danger of making desire claimable**.

Kumiko does not begin as a girl without preferences, attachments, talent, jealousy, curiosity, or musical pleasure. She has all of them. Her defensive problem is that she habitually keeps them from becoming explicit commitments that another person—or reality itself—can reject. She lets a majority speak first, performs indifference, smiles ambiguously, tells herself that “it would be nice” rather than “I want,” and often moves before she has consciously admitted why she is moving.

That distinction matters. Calling her simply passive misses how often she acts: she chases Aoi, chases Reina, impulsively claims Reina as her festival companion, kicks Shuuichi, hugs Reina, practices seriously, and eventually shouts with the ensemble. What changes is not the acquisition of agency from zero. It is the **conversion of unowned or self-protective action into owned desire**.

The clean Japanese text therefore strengthens V1's formulation of “the difficulty of wanting,” but makes the mechanism sharper:

> **Kumiko protects herself by keeping desire deniable. The volume teaches her that music cannot become hers while her desire remains deniable.**

The institution undergoes the same conversion. Kitauji begins with a slogan—全国大会出場—that the students themselves admit previously had little operative force. Taki turns a nominal goal into a binding claim: if the band says it wants nationals, rehearsal, auditions, selection, and the emotional cost of exclusion must follow. His method is not pure democracy and not pure authoritarianism. Students choose the broad objective, but he controls much of the machinery by which that choice becomes real. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0410 / 「ほう、なるほど。ではこれはなかったことにしましょう」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0448 / 「いま決めた目標は、皆さんが自身の手で決めたものです。反対の人もいました`

Music is the bridge between the personal and institutional plots. The novel devotes surprising technical attention to tuning, overtones, solfège, temperature, breath, lip slurs, slowed practice, instrument weight, marching delay, and audition excerpts. Kumiko's desire becomes credible not because an inspirational speech awakens a hidden dream, but because **improved embodied competence changes what she can imagine wanting**. When she hears the ensemble begin to align, practices a passage until the impossible becomes possible, and feels the pleasure of playing well, “nationals” stops being a slogan and becomes an object of desire.

A second new V2 emphasis follows from the volume's repeated use of **空気 / atmosphere**. The previous band punished seriousness through social pressure. This band can punish Reina through the same mechanism. Taki and Asuka both manipulate atmosphere, while Kumiko is highly vulnerable to it. Yet ensemble music itself requires another kind of social attunement: listening, blending, timing, and responding to other bodies. Volume 1 therefore distinguishes neither individualism nor harmony as automatically virtuous. It asks a harder question:

> **When is responsiveness to other people coercive conformity, and when is it the condition of making something together?**

The prologue and epilogue answer the personal arc formally. Both begin from the same result-board situation. In S01, a non-advancing gold is “good enough” to Kumiko while Reina cries because the stated goal has failed. `HIBIKE-V01 / S01 / P0018 / 金は金でも関西大会には進めないダメ金だけど、まあでも金賞なら及第点だ。顧` `HIBIKE-V01 / S01 / P0025 / 「悔しい。悔しくって死にそう。なんでみんな金賞なんかで喜べんの？アタシら` In S06, Kumiko initially fears another failure because Reina is crying, then discovers those tears mean advancement; she loses strength, cries, and can say plainly that she is happy. `HIBIKE-V01 / S06 / P0012 / その表情を見た瞬間、心臓がギシリときしんだ。またダメだったのか。また、彼` `HIBIKE-V01 / S06 / P0027 / 「麗奈、私、ほんとにうれしい」` The image does not merely repeat. Its emotional grammar has changed because Kumiko has made herself vulnerable to the result.

## 3. Structural architecture

### 3.1 Greeting, return, and farewell

The four chapter titles form an emotional grammar:

- **よろしく** — establish a relation;
- **ただいま** — return home;
- **おかえり** — acknowledge the return;
- **さよなら** — leave something behind.

The sequence does not describe a literal linear journey. It describes Kumiko's changing relation to band music. She greets the euphonium again, returns to the pleasure of ensemble labor, is forced to welcome back the audition wound she wanted to escape, and finally says goodbye not to competition itself but to the posture from which competition could remain emotionally external.

### 3.2 Volume-local movement

| Movement | External action | Internal / institutional function |
|---|---|---|
| S01 | Middle-school result | Reina exposes the difference between nominal goal and lived desire |
| S02 | New school, club entry, goal vote | Kumiko attempts a reset but reproduces social drift; Kitauji converts slogan into choice |
| S03 | First rehearsal, technical reconstruction, SunFes | Competence becomes imaginable; collective labor begins producing pleasure |
| S04 | Aoi quits; audition announced; Reina mountain scene | Old institutional guilt surfaces; Kumiko's “good girl” defense becomes explicit |
| S05 | Auditions, old trauma, solo legitimacy crisis, competition | Merit, dignity, atmosphere, and personal desire collide; Kumiko consciously chooses victory |
| S06 | Result | The mirrored result-board scene validates the internal transformation |

### 3.3 Local truth versus retrospective significance

**Local truth at V01:** Kumiko has learned to own some desires—improvement, victory, euphonium attachment, privileged closeness with Reina—without becoming a consistently direct person. Asuka remains fundamentally unresolved. Taki's methods are effective but ethically incomplete. Kitauji has changed its operating norms but has not repaired its past.

**Retrospective significance permitted:** this volume establishes mechanisms—Kumiko's social editing, Asuka's selective detachment, Reina's exceptionalism, institutional memory, audition legitimacy—that later volumes can test.

**Do not backport:** later leadership capacity, later relationship knowledge, mature reconciliation, or later explanations of Asuka/Taki cannot be treated as though V01 Kumiko already possessed them.

## 4. Scene-level close reading

### 4.1 The prologue: a goal can be spoken without being believed

The prologue's most consequential contrast is not simply “Reina cares; Kumiko does not.” Both girls participated in a band that publicly claimed to aim higher. Kumiko is physically tense while awaiting the result, and she is relieved by gold. `HIBIKE-V01 / S01 / P0005 / 立て看板に並ぶシンプルな文字。中学校に入ってから、このホールに来るのは三` `HIBIKE-V01 / S01 / P0018 / 金は金でも関西大会には進めないダメ金だけど、まあでも金賞なら及第点だ。顧` What she lacks is the expectation that the formal objective could bind her emotionally.

Reina's language is brutally unbuffered: she is frustrated enough to feel she might die, cannot understand why a non-advancing gold satisfies everyone, and reiterates that they had aimed for nationals. `HIBIKE-V01 / S01 / P0025 / 「悔しい。悔しくって死にそう。なんでみんな金賞なんかで喜べんの？アタシら` Her follow-up—`アンタは悔しくないわけ？`—does not merely accuse Kumiko of apathy. It forces Kumiko to confront the distance between **saying a group aims at something** and **allowing that aim to define success or failure**. `HIBIKE-V01 / S01 / P0029 / 「アンタは悔しくないわけ？」`

Kumiko's reply, `……本気で全国行けると思ってたの？`, is important for V2 voice modeling because it is one of her characteristic failure points: a skeptical private judgment escapes before social editing can stop it. `HIBIKE-V01 / S01 / P0027 / 「……本気で全国行けると思ってたの？」` The line is not carefully cruel. Its force comes from being insufficiently mediated. Reina later identifies precisely this unvarnished quality as the reason Kumiko interested her.

The final line of the prologue makes the event a continuing avoidance object: remembering Reina's eyes makes Kumiko want to flee that summer. `HIBIKE-V01 / S01 / P0034 / そのときのことを考えると、久美子はいつだって彼女の瞳を思い出す。そしてそ` “Resetting” at Kitauji is therefore already defensive before the main story begins.

### 4.2 Entering Kitauji: the observer is not neutral

The first main chapter opens inside Kumiko's bodily and social attention. She notices legs, uniforms, her own chest, attractiveness, and the discrepancy between an imagined high-school self and the actual body wearing the uniform. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0002 / 膝上でそろえられた紺色のスカート。そこから伸びる白い脚が、体育館にずらり` Her school choice is partly aesthetic—the sailor uniform is cute—and she is frank internally about the “impure” motive. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0003 / 京都府立北宇治高校は、制服が可愛いことで有名だ。宇治市で唯一のセーラー服`

This matters for characterization. Kumiko's observational acuity is not an objective camera. It is saturated with insecurity, taste, vanity, humor, comparison, and social threat. The narration gives us a highly informative perceiver whose interpretations must still be audited.

The bad entrance-ceremony performance produces another involuntary leak: `……これはヒドイ`. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0007 / 「……これはヒドイ」` Before she can construct a socially safe response, musical experience makes the judgment audible. She immediately hears rhythmic and ensemble defects and concludes this is nowhere near competitive quality. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0008 / 無意識のうちに、言葉が口を衝いていた。耳に飛び込んできたのは、とんでもな` Kumiko is therefore never musically indifferent. Her detachment concerns **stakes**, not an inability to discriminate quality.

The clean text also resolves a central linguistic fact. Hazuki notices that Kumiko speaks 標準語. Kumiko explains that she formerly lived in Tokyo and that her family also uses standard Japanese. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0029 / 「っていうかさ、さっきから気になっててんけど、アンタなんで標準語なん？」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0032 / 「家族もみんな標準語だから、あんまりうつらないかな。あ、でも一緒にいた友` V2 must therefore resist treating Kumiko's ordinary spoken baseline as Kansai dialect simply because the story is set in Kyoto. She is locally knowledgeable but linguistically offset from many peers.

### 4.3 Joining the club: being carried is comfortable until it becomes revealing

Kumiko initially treats club choice as something that can be settled by social momentum. She is not strongly committed to another instrument and repeatedly lets other people's expectations make the next decision easier. This culminates in returning to euphonium even after fantasizing about a different high-school start.

The crucial point is that the instrument is not merely imposed. Her relation to it is already deep enough to shape attention. Seeing a euphonium at the entrance ceremony catches her breath. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0006 / ステージの下では吹奏楽部が真剣な表情で楽器を構えている。指揮棒を構えてい` Later, when choosing instruments, she has little articulated desire but is highly competent, and Asuka recognizes her usefulness almost immediately. Asuka's muttered `……この子、使えるな` exposes an instrumental logic beneath the playful recruitment surface. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0317 / 「……この子、使えるな」`

Asuka's recruitment is one of the earliest high-value behavioral samples for her model. She seizes Midori's hand, changes physical distance, lowers her voice to secure commitment, then immediately snaps back to bright celebration. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0265 / その姿を見た瞬間、あすかの目が爛々と輝いた。楽器を部長に押しつけ、ズカズ` Kumiko explicitly reads this as 人心掌握術. The point is not that Asuka is “fake.” It is that she can **select a register and bodily presentation suited to the desired social result**.

She uses the same logic on Kumiko: repeats an indirect cue until Kumiko responds, then points out that Kumiko was too slow to recognize the recruitment attempt. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0295 / 「うちのパートさ、さっきの子以外まだ一人も希望者来てないんやけど」` Asuka later exploits Midori's friendship with Hazuki because refusal becomes harder when a friend is already committed. Her charisma is tactical from the beginning, not merely decorative.

### 4.4 Shuuichi: mock conflict as familiarity

Kumiko's relationship with Shuuichi provides a useful counterexample to any model that treats her polite surface as universal. With him, childhood familiarity permits exaggerated mock-politeness—`過去のことを謝罪してください`, followed later by a breathless `もういいです近づかないでください`—before she drops into `もういいよ、めんどくさいし`. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0178 / 「そう言うなら過去のことを謝罪してください」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0190 / 「ああそうですかわかりましたもういいです近づかないでください」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0196 / 「もういいよ、めんどくさいし」`

This register switching is relational. The politeness is not respect; it is comic aggression and performed distance. Shuuichi, in turn, recognizes a pattern other new acquaintances do not: `またアレか？周りに流された？` and tells her she will have problems if she cannot state her own opinion. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0212 / 「もしかしてまたアレか？周りに流された？」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0214 / 「お前もいい加減その性格直したほうがええんちゃう？自分の意見言えんかった`

He is therefore not merely a romance token in V01. He is one of the few characters who can name Kumiko's default strategy from long exposure. His later role as practice partner and pre-performance regulator grows out of this ordinary familiarity rather than sudden romantic melodrama.

### 4.5 Taki's vote: autonomy as a mechanism of accountability

Taki's first major institutional act is rhetorically democratic but structurally more complicated than V1's shorthand “student choice” can imply. He discovers that 全国大会出場 had functioned as a largely empty slogan, crosses it out, and refuses to enforce an ambition that the group does not genuinely own. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0409 / 「それは目標というかアレですよ、単なるスローガンというかなんというか……` `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0412 / 「ですが、そういうのは困りますね。達成する気のない目標ほど無駄なものはあ` He says the level of strictness should follow what they decide they want.

Kumiko's response gives the scene its psychological counterpoint. She dislikes majority voting because a public choice means risking visible dissent. She empties her mind, watches others, and raises her hand with the dominant side. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0426 / 多数決。民主主義の構成原理でもある、集団で物を決めるときの例の方法のこと` The procedure is formally participatory, but its social “correct answer” is already becoming legible to her. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0435 / 小笠原の言葉に久美子は頬杖をついた。こういうとき、じつは何を選ぶべきかは`

Taki then binds the ensemble to its own declaration: this is the goal they chose, and effort cannot be outsourced to him. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0448 / 「いま決めた目標は、皆さんが自身の手で決めたものです。反対の人もいました` The pedagogical principle is powerful because it mirrors Kumiko's problem: **a stated desire creates obligations**.

The scene also establishes an important distinction for later institutional analysis. Taki lets students choose the *goal*. He does not democratize repertoire, auditions, technical standards, rehearsal method, or every allocation decision. The institutional order that follows is best described as **bounded student authorship inside strong expert control**, not simple democracy.

### 4.6 The first disastrous rehearsal: humiliation, expertise, and “air”

Taki's first ensemble rehearsal is deliberately uncomfortable. The band collapses; he asks what the performance even was, says it cannot yet be called ensemble playing, and notes that he heard chatting rather than instruments during section practice. `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0190 / 「なんですか？コレ」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0227 / 「私はこの一週間、皆さんがパート練習をしている教室を見て回りました。皆さ` He repeatedly demands a verbal response and makes the students inhabit the gap between their declared national goal and their actual habits.

Kumiko's body records the pressure. When she cannot produce `はい`, she grips her own wrist hard enough to leave a mark. `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0241 / 返事はなかった。皆、凍りついたように動かない。滝が笑顔のまま、もう一度繰` `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0244 / 滝は楽譜を抱えると、そのまま教室を出ていってしまった。誰も動かない。何も` V2 should preserve this as an embodied stress response rather than abstractly calling her “nervous.”

Afterward, the room erupts in complaint. Asuka—not Haruka—reorients the section toward practice. `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0252 / 「はいはいはい！愚痴はええから、とっととみんなパート連の部屋に戻ってー。` This scene establishes the asymmetry between formal leadership and affective command before later material complicates it.

Natsuki later interprets the rehearsal as strategic: Taki first forces the band to hear how bad it is, then demonstrates the possibility of rapid improvement, thereby changing what behavior the group considers normal. This is excellent evidence for an **atmosphere-engineering reading**, but epistemically it remains partly **[C] Natsuki's interpretation** and **[E] analyst inference**, not a narrated confession of Taki's secret plan. V2 therefore strengthens the pattern while downgrading any claim that Taki's exact intention is textually proven.

### 4.7 Technical pedagogy: desire becomes plausible through the body

The second rehearsal sequence is one of the volume's most important expansions beyond generic “hard work.” Taki asks students to sing pitches, explains solfège, produces a B-flat, and has them experience how aligned pitch generates overtones. `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0323 / 「ではまず最初に、チューニングの音を皆さんで歌ってほしいんです」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0355 / 音のなかに溶け込ませる。その言葉を意識して、久美子は周りの音に耳を澄ませ` Kumiko identifies the resulting unison as the most beautiful she has heard and, crucially, wants to test that sensation in ensemble. `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0357 / 滝の言葉に、久美子は構えていた楽器を下ろした。いままで聞いてきたなかで、` `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0362 / 滝の言葉に、久美子たちは勢いよく返事した。短時間の指導ではあったが、音程`

The instruction continues into temperature-dependent pitch, instrument behavior, detailed scheduling, the demand that every performance be treated seriously, long tones, lip slurs, mouthpiece scales, and individual technical diagnosis. `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0367 / 部長が前に立ち、皆で最後のチューニングをする。同時に同じ音を出し、その高` `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0411 / その言葉に釣られて、久美子もまた手のなかの紙に視線を落とす。そこには基礎` `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0434 / そしていま葉月が悪戦苦闘しているのが、リップスラーだ。ひとつの指使いでさ`

This material changes the causal interpretation of Kumiko's arc. She does not become ambitious merely because Reina possesses an inspiring philosophy. She becomes more ambitious while **hearing and producing new levels of control**. When her own mouthpiece sound compares poorly with Asuka's clean scale, she wants to sound beautiful. `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0452 / こんなふうに、とあすかはマウスピースだけで音階を奏でてみせた。その音があ` `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0456 / 音階が鳴るには鳴ったが、あすかのそれとはほど遠い。まず、音が汚い。息の吹` The novel links aspiration to sensory evidence.

### 4.8 SunFes: serious practice changes the meaning of nerves

SunFes is the first public proof that the new practice regime has changed the ensemble. Natsuki observes that they are nervous because they actually practiced seriously; Riko says this is the first time everyone has worked at this level. `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0648 / 「そりゃアレやろ、真面目に練習したからやろ」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0649 / 「皆でこんなに練習したの、この部活に入って初めてやもんねえ」`

Kumiko discovers that the parade is physically difficult but fun, and despite exhaustion she does not want it to end. `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0659 / パレードは楽しい。体力的にはキツイけれども、それでも楽しい。リズムに身を` She also likes the fact that low brass does not stand out. `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0665 / 音楽は終わらない。楽譜の終盤に来ると、最初に戻る。延々とその繰り返し。譜` This is not yet a rejection of excellence; it is an early clue that her preferred mode of musical value differs from Reina's. Kumiko is drawn to participation, texture, and support even while she increasingly wants the ensemble to win.

Reina's tuning note at SunFes cuts through a noisy field strongly enough that other schools notice; she continues without visibly responding to the attention. `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0620 / 夏紀がここまで言うのは珍しい。テレビ越しでその演奏を見ることは何度もあっ` Whether she truly does not notice or deliberately refuses to acknowledge the gaze remains open, but the event concretizes the social problem of exceptional ability: talent generates attention even before the character asks for it.

### 4.9 Aoi, Haruka, and the old band's moral injury

Aoi's resignation prevents the improvement plot from becoming a clean redemption story. Her academic reason is real—she is preparing for entrance exams—but she also says she never loved the club enough to make the current national goal hers and that she does not believe she has the right to proclaim renewed earnestness after the previous year. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0090 / 「私は、そこまで吹部が好きちゃう」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0098 / 「私はもう無理。耐えられへん。一生懸命頑張りますなんて、私には言う権利な`

Her earlier admission that voting against nationals created an “alibi” for quitting is structurally parallel to Kumiko's defensive language. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0480 / 「アリバイ作り、かな」` Both characters manage future pain by constructing a position from which failure or withdrawal will look less like betrayal of desire.

Taki respects Aoi's choice rather than coercing her to stay. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0065 / 「いいんですよ。あなたが自分でそう決めたなら、それを貫いてください」` This complicates the image of him as an authoritarian efficiency machine: his demanding accountability operates *after* a participant claims a goal, but he also accepts exit from the goal.

Haruka internalizes Aoi's departure as evidence that she should never have been president. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0124 / 「やっぱり、うちが部長なんて無理やったんや」` Her self-comparison to Asuka is severe. When Kumiko offers generic reassurance, Haruka forces her to specify what is actually good about her, and Kumiko can initially produce little beyond “kind.” `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0137 / 「小笠原部長だって、あすか先輩にはないいいところをたくさん持ってますよ。` `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0149 / 「優しいなんてなあ、褒めるとこがないやつに言う台詞やろ？うち、わかってん` This is a sharp test of Kumiko's observational intelligence: seeing other people does not mean she can always articulate value under social pressure.

Asuka restores Haruka not through formal leadership doctrine but through intimate teasing: she tells Haruka to correct her instability because she is president, then immediately adds that she likes that side of her. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0166 / 「あかんで晴香、その情緒不安定なところ直さんと。なんたって部長やねんから` `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0169 / 「でも、そんなところも好きやけど」` Kumiko simultaneously notices an unnervingly cold gaze behind the performance and retreats into `なんでもないです`. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0179 / あすかの言葉に小笠原が反論し、それを見てまたあすかが笑う。和やかな二人の` `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0181 / 久美子が立ち止まったのに気づいてか、あすかが振り返る。久美子は曖昧な笑み` This is precisely where V2 must preserve epistemic separation: **Kumiko directly sees the gaze; its meaning remains interpretation.**

### 4.10 Practice desire becomes speakable

Kumiko's technical history matters. She has played euphonium since fourth grade; Natsuki explicitly frames their difference as years of accumulated experience rather than innate moral deservingness. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0322 / 「小四からです。金管バンドに入れられて」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0324 / そりゃあ差もつくわなあ。そう言って、夏紀は自嘲じみた笑みを浮かべた。差と`

Kumiko practices deliberately, taking difficult material at a fraction of tempo and increasing speed, because she enjoys the transition from inability to ability. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0316 / 簡単そうな場面をひととおり吹いて確認してから、久美子は難関となるだろうメ` After SunFes makes improvement tangible, Shuuichi says they may be able to reach nationals; Kumiko responds, `うん、そうだね。全国行きたいね`, and shortly afterward says `もっと上手くなりたいなあ`. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0357 / 「全国、行けるかもな」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0365 / 「もっと上手くなりたいなあ」`

This is one of the cleanest state transitions in V01. The desire is still conversational and not yet fully tested by defeat, but it has crossed from private sensory pleasure into explicit speech.

### 4.11 Daikichiyama: Reina chooses the unedited Kumiko

The festival-night sequence is not merely “romantic framing” or a thematic monologue. It radically expands the behavioral model for both girls.

Kumiko's route to the outing is characteristically indirect. She is poised around Shuuichi/Hazuki tension, experiences jealousy, and impulsively claims that she is already going with Reina. She then performs a smile and avoids repairing Shuuichi's visible reaction. The act is socially defensive and emotionally consequential at once.

Reina takes the invitation literally. When Kumiko hesitates, Reina answers that of course they are going—Kumiko invited her—and reveals a grin Kumiko did not expect. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0440 / 「当たり前やろ。アンタが誘ってきたのに何言うてんの」` Reina dislikes crowds and bright noisy festivals, prefers Ujigami Shrine and the mountain, and admits that she sometimes wants irrational breaks from the repetitive school/club/study cycle. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0510 / 「いや、屋台に行くつもりはないで。アタシ、人混み嫌いやし」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0545 / 「でもさ、たまにこういうあほなことしたくなるねん」` These ordinary preferences are essential for simulation: public Reina's severity does not exhaust private Reina.

Most important, Reina identifies the split Kumiko works hardest to conceal. `久美子ってさ、結構性格悪いやん？` shocks Kumiko precisely because other people call her a good girl. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0560 / 「久美子ってさ、結構性格悪いやん？」` Reina says she wants to peel off the いい子ちゃんの皮 and frames her attraction as liking the parts Kumiko edits away. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0564 / 「そのいい子ちゃんの皮、ぺりぺりってめくりたいなあと思って」` `愛の告白` is joking language, but the relationship privilege is real: Reina is offering acceptance of an unattractive interior rather than asking Kumiko to become morally prettier.

Reina's own interior is also more plural than her public image. She says she wants to become 特別 and links trumpet to distinction and recognition. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0617 / 「アタシはさ、特別になりたい」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0625 / 「だからアタシは、吹奏楽をやってんねん。特別でありたいから」` Yet she also leans into Kumiko, enjoys bodily closeness, expresses dislike of crowds, and later requires reassurance after the solo conflict. Exceptionalism does not eliminate dependency.

### 4.12 Merit under unequal conditions

The audition system directly attacks seniority privilege. Taki says prior convention is irrelevant because he is the present advisor and that stronger players should be selected. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0036 / 「でも、いまの顧問は私ですよ？これまでのことなんて関係あります？」` The ethical force of the policy is real: Kumiko's old middle-school wound demonstrates what happens when a younger player's legitimate selection is socially denied.

But the clean text also prevents a simplistic “meritocracy = justice” reading. It makes unequal preconditions visible. Reina owns a distinctive high-end trumpet bought by her parents. `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0053 / あすかはそう言って教室を出ていく。久美子たちも慌ててそのあとを追った。音` Asuka owns a silver euphonium. `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0004 / あすかはそう言って、自身の楽器を指でなぞった。白銀のユーフォニアムは、あ` Midori arrives from a stronger environment. Kumiko has seven years of euphonium experience; beginners are competing inside the same institutional deadline. The narration later reflects explicitly on how training environments and conductors create unequal conditions even for students nominally in the same school system. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0753 / 十年前、北宇治高校がまだ強豪校だった時代、きっとその指導者は優秀な人間だ` `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0754 / 千五百以上ある高校のなかで、全国大会に残れるのはたった三十校足らず。京都`

The correct V2 formulation is therefore:

> **V01 endorses performance-sensitive selection over hereditary senior privilege, while simultaneously showing that measured performance emerges from unequal histories, resources, instruction, and social costs.**

Merit is treated as relevant evidence, not as metaphysical desert.

### 4.13 The old audition wound and Natsuki's refusal to repeat it

Kumiko's selection immediately reactivates a middle-school trauma. An older student whose place Kumiko had taken grabbed her wrist, kicked her euphonium, and told her that Kumiko's absence would have secured the senior's A-ensemble seat. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0056 / いつものように挨拶した久美子に、しかしいつものような返事は返ってこなかっ` `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0076 / 吐き捨てられた言葉が、久美子の心臓に突き刺さる。込み上げてきた嗚咽に、久`

Kumiko's retrospective formulation is severe: she loves music and instruments but does not trust “club” sociality because ugly feelings can hide beneath smiling surfaces. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0079 / あの日の、先輩の目。久美子はそれを忘れられない。久美子は音楽が好きだ。楽` This is central to her threat model. Her avoidance of visible ambition is not purely temperamental; she has learned that performing well can make her a social target.

Natsuki's response to losing her own seat therefore has outsized importance. Physical contact initially triggers the old memory, but Natsuki's grip is caring rather than punitive. She treats the outcome as intelligible given the gap in experience and tells Kumiko there will be another year. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0098 / そう言いながら、彼女は久美子の腕をつかんだ。あの先輩とは違う、相手を労る` `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0128 / 「気にせんでええって。だってさ、考えたら当たり前のことやろ？うちは高校入`

Natsuki also rejects Kumiko's tendency to sanctify her as “a good person.” She says she mainly pitied how much Kumiko was overthinking. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0132 / 「……先輩は、いい人ですね」` This matters because V01 repeatedly resists tidy moral labeling. Care can be real without requiring saintly self-description.

### 4.14 Natsuki's history lesson: atmosphere as institutional memory

Natsuki supplies the most direct internal account of the previous year's collapse. Serious students were ostracized; Asuka practiced but stayed neutral; Haruka's presidency later improved the tone; and Aoi remains unable to forgive her own relation to what happened. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0155 / 「上回生のやつらが、潰しちゃったから」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0167 / 「あすか先輩は怖いほど中立。誰にも肩入れせえへんかった。真面目に練習やり`

Her key term is `空気`: people are weak to the atmosphere around them, and only a few possess a self strong enough not to be carried by it. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0183 / 「空気」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0187 / 自分があるやつなんて、ほとんどおらん。` This diagnosis is doubly important because Kumiko's own most explicit problem is being carried by others.

Natsuki further interprets Taki's first rehearsals as deliberate atmosphere construction. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0195 / 「でも、滝先生はそうしいひんかった。多分、あの人は最初に見せつけたかった` That interpretation fits the observable sequence but remains a character explanation. V2 therefore records **Taki as highly effective at altering norms** with high confidence, while his exact private strategic intent remains moderate-confidence inference.

### 4.15 Reina, the solo, and the cost of public exceptionality

Reina's claim to the trumpet solo is musically straightforward and socially explosive. When Yuuko challenges her, Reina says the reason is obvious: she is better than Kaori. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0254 / 「香織先輩よりアタシのほうが上手いから、だからアタシがソロなんですよ」` She treats insults to Taki as more serious than damage to her own reputation and refuses the social demand that a talented first-year soften the fact of superior performance. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0265 / 「アタシのことはどうでもいいですけど、滝先生のことを悪く言うのはやめてく`

The scene is often summarized as “Reina is arrogant,” but V2 should retain the full mechanism. Her public language is compressed, declarative, and adversarial because she interprets the conflict as a challenge to musical truth and to Taki's integrity. Yet her clenched fist shows that the social hostility still hurts. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0277 / 吐き棄てるようにそう言って、彼女は音楽室をあとにした。その拳がぎゅうと握` She is not invulnerable; she refuses to let vulnerability change the claimed standard.

In private she explodes with repetitive anger, asks Kumiko whether she thinks Reina is wrong, accepts a hug, and makes Kumiko listen. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0291 / 「ウザいウザいウザい！もうなんなんアイツら！意味わからん、ほんま性格悪す` `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0303 / 「アタシ、間違ってると思う？」` The Kumiko–Reina dyad therefore functions as an **affect-processing channel** that public Reina does not offer the ensemble.

Her admission that she romantically loves Taki further complicates a purely meritocratic model. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0334 / 「アタシさ、滝先生のこと好きなの」` She chose Kitauji partly because of him, knows of his family history through her father, and is intensely protective of his reputation. Yet when Kumiko asks whether she would yield the solo, the answer is immediate: `ない`. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0342 / 「ない」` Love for Taki and commitment to her own performance claim reinforce rather than cancel each other.

### 4.16 Asuka's “neutrality” is selective disengagement, not lack of power

Asuka's V01 characterization becomes considerably sharper when her scenes are read together.

She actively recruits. She manipulates distance and vocal register. She steadies Haruka. She redirects the section after a failed rehearsal. She understands Kaori's emotional need. She can control the room's tone. None of this is political helplessness.

What she refuses is **normative investment in conflicts she does not recognize as valuable to her own musical project**. When Kumiko pushes her for a private view of the Kaori/Reina dispute, Asuka eventually says she genuinely does not care who has the solo and calls the dispute trivial. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0628 / 「正直言って、心の底からどうでもいいよ。誰がソロとか、そんなくだらないこ`

This does not prove every display of affection is fake. Nor does it prove the claim of indifference is a lie. The stronger V2 interpretation is that Asuka separates **attachment** from **obligation to let attachment govern institutional judgment**. She can care about Kaori and still refuse to turn that care into a position on who should play.

That distinction also explains why her previous-year neutrality is ethically troubling. She had the social capacity to intervene but did not treat the serious students' conflict as an obligation she had to own. Neutrality is therefore a value-laden policy of selective responsibility.

### 4.17 Midori and Hazuki: two ethical correctives to simple merit

Midori's cheerfulness should not obscure the sophistication of her competitive experience. She loves music, possesses strong skill, and can perform confidence without Reina's defensive severity. Before competition she effectively frames performance as an opportunity to display what one can do rather than merely avoid error. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0745 / 「いい？久美子ちゃんが緊張すんのは、失敗しちゃダメやって思うからやねん。`

But she has also seen elite environments where auditions and social rivalry damage people. During the solo dispute, she asks whether the best competition result is the only value that matters. Hazuki counters that asking Reina to yield would itself be unfair because Reina worked too. Their disagreement is not “kindness versus logic.” It is **two legitimate theories of fairness**:

- protect the human community from treating every nonmaximal choice as failure;
- do not make the strongest performer pay for other people's disappointment.

Kumiko refuses to settle the conflict. At V01 this is partly defensive avoidance, but the scene also establishes that her reluctance can sometimes track genuine moral plurality rather than mere weakness.

### 4.18 Kaori's second audition: truth plus agency

Kaori does not begin from technical delusion. Earlier she already recognizes that Reina is exceptionally good. The second audition matters because private knowledge has not produced public and emotional legitimacy.

Kaori requests the re-audition herself. Her improved performance is strong enough to receive natural applause, but Reina's performance audibly exceeds it. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0668 / 滝の指揮棒が振り下ろされる。香織の繊細で柔らかな音楽が、ホールいっぱいに` `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0674 / 最初の一音目がラッパから飛び出た瞬間、久美子の耳は明確に先ほどとの差異を` Taki then asks Kaori whether she will take the solo; Kaori refuses and says Reina should play. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0681 / 「中世古さん、あなたがソロを吹きますか？」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0688 / 「ソロは、あなたが吹くべきやと思う」`

V1's “dignity of losing” is therefore preserved, but one claim needs epistemic discipline. Kumiko interprets Kaori as having wanted to lose decisively enough that she could accept the outcome. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0695 / 不意にあすかの声が、耳元で蘇る。その言葉の意味を、久美子はここで初めて理` This is plausible and strongly supported, but it remains **[C/B] Kumiko's interpretation of Kaori's inner purpose**, not direct access to Kaori's mind.

The institutional lesson is broader: legitimacy is produced not only by the technically correct answer but by a process in which those affected can recognize the answer. The re-audition is musically redundant and socially productive.

### 4.19 The decisive conversion: from “nice if” to “I want”

The final rehearsal and competition sequences explicitly return to the prologue's question.

Kumiko realizes that in middle school she had participated largely through inertia. She had not reached the point where defeat could make her cry, and Reina's intensity frightened her because it exposed that absence. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0707 / 中学時代、吹奏楽部を選んだのは惰性みたいなものだった。運動ができるわけで` `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0709 / 久美子はあのとき、まったく悔しいと思わなかった。`

The volume then grounds the new state in bodily pleasure. The band plays; Taki calls the result perfect; Kumiko thinks simply that playing is fun. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0721 / 「……完璧です」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0722 / 滝は言った。彼の手放しの称賛など初めてのことで、部員たちは皆、驚いたよう` She recognizes how often she protected herself by using conditional language about nationals and arrives at the proposition that if a wish is never spoken, it cannot become real. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0727 / 全国に行けたらいいな。そう、久美子はずっと思っていた。中学生のころから、`

This is not naïve magical thinking. The preceding hundreds of paragraphs have made the opposite point: declared desire generates labor, exclusion, fear, technical repetition, and possible loss. Her declaration has force because she now understands its costs.

Before the performance itself, panic returns. Shuuichi notices despite her denial, tells her she has practiced enough, and steadies her while his own fingers tremble. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0812 / 「問題ないって顔じゃねえけど」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0820 / 「大丈夫だって、あんなに練習したんだからさ」` Their intertwined hands convert childhood familiarity into mutual regulation rather than grand confession. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0822 / 暗幕の向こう側から、割れんばかりの拍手が聞こえてくる。薄暗い視界のなかで`

At the threshold of performance, the narration gives the cleanest statement of the new state: `勝ちたいと、久美子は心の底から思う`. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0827 / しんと、静寂が満ちる。ざわめきが消滅し、人々の視線は舞台へと集中する。こ`

### 4.20 Epilogue: the same tears, new semantics

S06 deliberately reproduces S01's result-board situation. Reina again cries. Kumiko initially interprets the tears through the old wound and assumes they may have failed. `HIBIKE-V01 / S06 / P0012 / その表情を見た瞬間、心臓がギシリときしんだ。またダメだったのか。また、彼` This moment is important because transformation does not erase memory; the old schema activates first.

Then Reina says they advanced. Her language is ecstatic rather than controlled. `HIBIKE-V01 / S06 / P0019 / 「信じられへん！関西やで関西！久美子！」` Kumiko's body gives way, she cries, and she tells Reina directly that she is truly happy. `HIBIKE-V01 / S06 / P0024 / 喉が熱い。ぎゅっと締めつけられるような感覚は、久美子の唇から言葉を奪った` `HIBIKE-V01 / S06 / P0027 / 「麗奈、私、ほんとにうれしい」`

The volume ends not with final victory but with renewed obligation. Taki reminds them that advancement was not the stated endpoint; the next stage remains. `HIBIKE-V01 / S06 / P0032 / 「なんだかこれで大団円みたいな空気ですけど、明日から関西大会に向けての練` His eyes are slightly wet, and Kumiko urgently wants to play again. `HIBIKE-V01 / S06 / P0033 / 滝はそう言って、目を細めた。その瞳がわずかに潤んでいることに、久美子はそ` The ending therefore refuses closure: owning desire means accepting that success creates another exposure to possible failure.

## 5. Character-state extraction

### 5.1 Oumae Kumiko — `KUMIKO@V01`

**Primary wants**

1. belonging without becoming a social target;
2. freedom from an embarrassing or falsifiable self-definition;
3. continued access to music/euphonium before she can admit how much it matters;
4. intimate recognition from particular people without having to state every desire first;
5. increasingly, improvement and victory.

**Threat model**

- public rejection of a declared preference;
- social retaliation for outperforming others;
- being forced to choose between people she values;
- exposure of jealousy, desire, attraction, or ambition that conflicts with her “good girl” identity;
- being trapped by an irreversible declaration.

Her explanation to Azusa is unusually direct: she dislikes putting her thoughts into words because being denied after verbalizing them would hurt. `HIBIKE-V01 / S03 / P0718 / それだけ、と久美子は曖昧に微笑んだ。自分の考えを言葉にするのが、久美子は` This is the clean-text anchor for the whole defensive mechanism.

**Attention model**

Kumiko attends strongly to:

- changes in facial expression and gaze;
- the “air” of groups;
- bodies, clothes, attractiveness, and physical proximity;
- micro-contradictions between speech and gesture;
- musical quality and technical defects;
- who is excluded, embarrassed, or looking for a social opening.

Her attention is high-resolution but not impartial. It is conditioned by insecurity and her own unacknowledged desires.

**Default social strategy**

- ambiguous smile;
- soften or omit judgment;
- wait for others to declare first;
- use `べつに`, `なんでもない`, or equivalent dismissal when disclosure feels dangerous;
- perform indifference;
- convert serious conflict into teasing where relational history permits;
- move physically before fully admitting motive when avoidance fails.

**Conflict policy**

She initially avoids explicit alignment. Under enough emotional pressure she can become abrupt: blurting a musical judgment, chasing someone, hugging Reina, or physically teasing Shuuichi. These are not contradictions. They are failures of the editing layer.

**Care behavior**

Kumiko often cares through presence rather than advice: following Aoi, staying with Reina, listening, bodily contact, noticing distress, or allowing a lie to stand when she judges direct confrontation would wound the other person. Whether that last behavior is kindness or avoidance remains situation-dependent.

**Self-deception / blind spots**

- attachment to Shuuichi;
- jealousy toward Hazuki;
- degree of investment in euphonium;
- desire for competition success;
- the extent to which “neutrality” serves her own comfort.

**Negative constraints after V01**

A plausible V01 Kumiko should **not** routinely:

- deliver confident ideological speeches in group conflict;
- state a controversial preference before testing the room;
- confront another person's self-deception merely because she notices it;
- use Kansai dialect as her default speech;
- treat music as emotionally unimportant;
- behave as though her jealousy or attraction is already fully self-understood.

**State transition**

`defensive participation → competence-driven investment → privately recognized intimacy/desire → explicit improvement desire → acknowledged competitive desire`.

She has not become generally fearless. She has acquired specific desires she can now own.

### 5.2 Kousaka Reina — `REINA@V01`

**Primary wants:** exceptional trumpet performance, distinction (`特別`), the solo, Taki's esteem/protection, and a privileged relationship with Kumiko.

**Public strategy:** compressed directness, low concern for smoothing uncomfortable truth, confidence in performance hierarchy, willingness to accept social hostility rather than relinquish a claim she believes musically justified.

**Private strategy:** much more affectively expansive. She complains, repeats, jokes, seeks reassurance, admits romantic love, accepts touch, and shares irrational preferences. Public severity is therefore a register/state, not the whole personality.

**Threats:** being treated as ordinary, being told to surrender earned musical position for social harmony, insults to Taki, and possibly having exceptional ambition dismissed as socially illegible.

**Contradiction preserved:** she presents extraordinary confidence yet visibly trembles before the second audition and asks Kumiko whether she is wrong. Confidence is not absence of social pain.

**Negative constraints:** V01 Reina is unlikely to relinquish a musical claim merely to reduce tension; unlikely to hide her technical judgment once directly challenged; unlikely to give generic therapeutic reassurance in place of her actual evaluation.

### 5.3 Tanaka Asuka — `ASUKA@V01`

Asuka is not adequately modeled as “charismatic and neutral.” She is **high-agency and selectively noncommittal**.

She can:

- recruit strategically;
- change vocal/physical presentation on demand;
- read social pressure;
- stabilize Haruka;
- redirect practice;
- understand Kaori's needs;
- separate public office from private preference.

What she resists is allowing other people's emotional conflict to define her obligations. Her apparent neutrality is thus a conflict policy, not absence of preference or competence.

**Open:** how much of her coldness is stable value, defense, performance, or all three. V01 does not justify resolving this.

### 5.4 Taki Noboru — `TAKI@V01`

Taki combines procedural rhetoric, technical precision, emotional opacity, and bounded respect for autonomy.

He:

- asks students to choose the broad goal;
- holds them to it once declared;
- chooses repertoire and technical methods himself;
- rejects automatic seniority;
- corrects Reina publicly, undercutting favoritism claims;
- allows re-audition when legitimacy fractures;
- respects Aoi's decision to quit;
- speaks with controlled politeness even when sharply critical.

**Open:** the exact degree to which every early humiliation was consciously scripted as “atmosphere engineering.” The outcome is observable; Natsuki's account of intent is not omniscient proof.

### 5.5 Haruka Ogasawara — `HARUKA@V01`

Haruka begins by measuring herself against Asuka and finding herself deficient. Her leadership is emotionally porous rather than theatrically commanding. This looks like weakness in crisis, but the volume gradually shows another form of legitimacy: persistence, responsibility, accessibility, and shared fear.

Her pre-competition rally succeeds precisely because it does not pretend she is above the ensemble. V01 therefore establishes **ordinary leadership as an achieved relationship**, not merely a consolation prize for lacking charisma.

### 5.6 Aoi Saitou — `AOI@V01`

Aoi's exit has at least three simultaneous motives:

1. entrance-exam pressure [A];
2. lower attachment to band than the newly serious members [A];
3. moral injury/guilt concerning the prior year's treatment of serious students [A/C].

Her statement that she has no regrets conflicts with an embodied stress gesture, but the text does not grant objective access to whether the statement is “a lie.” Kumiko reads it that way and chooses to act as though she believes Aoi. **Disposition: preserve contradiction.**

### 5.7 Kaori Nakaseko — `KAORI@V01`

Kaori is courteous, socially loved, and technically realistic. She knows Reina is extraordinary, privately continues working, and requests a process that lets her confront the outcome directly. Her eventual refusal of the solo is not capitulation to humiliation; it is an exercise of agency after the musical comparison becomes public.

### 5.8 Natsuki Nakagawa — `NATSUKI@V01`

Natsuki begins with deliberately low investment: she joined partly because the club seemed easy. Yet she possesses clear institutional memory and moral perception. Her most consequential V01 act is losing without transferring humiliation to Kumiko. She also articulates the “air” theory that explains both prior collapse and current improvement.

Her care style is deliberately unsentimental: she rejects Kumiko's flattering “good person” label while still providing reassurance.

### 5.9 Kawashima Midori — `MIDORI@V01`

Midori combines high skill, conspicuous enthusiasm, strong aesthetic preferences, self-reference as `緑`, and experience of elite musical competition. She can be socially sunny while making sophisticated arguments about the human costs of ranking. Her confidence before performance resembles Reina's desire for recognition but lacks Reina's defensive severity.

### 5.10 Katou Hazuki — `HAZUKI@V01`

Hazuki is musically inexperienced but emotionally more direct than Kumiko. She can want, confess, be rejected, identify her own less flattering motive, and continue participating. Her beginnerhood also makes the physical labor of low brass visible rather than romanticized.

### 5.11 Tsukamoto Shuuichi — `SHUUICHI@V01`

Shuuichi knows Kumiko's historical pattern of social drift and challenges it directly. He wants trombone, improves technically, wants nationals, and serves as an ordinary-life regulator for Kumiko. His intimacy is built from history, teasing, irritation, practice, and bodily familiarity rather than rarefied exceptionalism.

### 5.12 Yoshikawa Yuuko — `YUUKO@V01`

Yuuko's loyalty to Kaori is factional, loud, and capable of unfair accusation. But she performs a necessary narrative function: she forces the band to confront a legitimacy problem that silent resentment would otherwise leave underground. Her behavior is not justified by that function; the institution nevertheless has to answer the distrust she voices.

## 6. Relationship-state extraction

### Kumiko ↔ Reina

**State:** rapidly privileged, asymmetric in explicitness, mutually regulating.

Reina sees more of Kumiko's edited interior than most characters and explicitly says she likes it. Kumiko, in turn, becomes a private witness for Reina's rage, romantic attachment, fear, and ambition. Physical contact is comfortable enough to include leaning, hugging, linked proximity, and sensuous attention.

**Speech conditioning:** Kumiko becomes more direct and playful with Reina than with most newer peers; Reina's public compression expands into longer, emotionally volatile private turns.

**Open:** V01 establishes intense intimacy but does not require one final categorical label for the relationship.

### Kumiko ↔ Shuuichi

**State:** high familiarity, low explicitness, reciprocal attention, concealed jealousy/attraction.

They permit mock-hostility and rough teasing that would be implausible with strangers. Shuuichi reads Kumiko's social habits and physical anxiety well. Kumiko suppresses or misnames the importance of his attention. The relationship's ordinary continuity is structurally different from the exceptional/private atmosphere of Kumiko–Reina.

### Kumiko ↔ Hazuki / Midori

These friendships give Kumiko a low-stakes everyday peer environment. Hazuki provides emotional directness and beginner perspective; Midori provides musical expertise without social severity. Both expose traits Kumiko lacks or disowns.

### Kumiko ↔ Asuka

Kumiko is attracted to Asuka's competence and social intelligence while increasingly disturbed by the possibility that the charm hides a radically different weighting of obligation. Asuka treats Kumiko as useful, interesting, and recruitable; later evidence is required before stronger intimacy claims.

### Asuka ↔ Haruka

Formal hierarchy understates actual power: Haruka is president, Asuka often commands attention more easily. Yet Asuka also knows how to restore Haruka rather than simply replace her. Their relationship contains both unequal charisma and genuine familiarity.

### Asuka ↔ Kaori

Asuka understands Kaori's private practice and emotional need but refuses to translate attachment into a public merit judgment. This dyad is key evidence that Asuka's “neutrality” is not emotional emptiness.

### Reina ↔ Taki

Reina's relation combines student respect, family-history knowledge, romantic love, and fierce concern for his professional legitimacy. Because her musical judgment and romantic attachment point in the same direction during V01, later material will be needed to test how she behaves when those commitments diverge.

### Ensemble ↔ Reina

Reina is musically central and socially precarious. Her exceptional performance earns institutional position while generating resentment. The second audition repairs some public legitimacy but does not imply universal affection.

## 7. Japanese voice and register findings

### 7.1 Kumiko: standard-Japanese baseline with relational switching

The clean source establishes directly that Kumiko's ordinary speech is **標準語**, explained by past residence in Tokyo and a family that also speaks standard Japanese. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0029 / 「っていうかさ、さっきから気になっててんけど、アンタなんで標準語なん？」` `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0032 / 「家族もみんな標準語だから、あんまりうつらないかな。あ、でも一緒にいた友` This is a foundational simulation constraint.

Her voice has several recurrent modes:

**Socially edited / new-acquaintance mode**
- polite or neutral standard Japanese;
- short acknowledgments;
- ambiguous smiles rather than explicit disagreement;
- thoughts that are sharper than what she says.

**Leakage mode**
- blunt judgment escapes before editing, e.g. the opening musical assessment and the middle-school question to Reina.

**Shuuichi mode**
- mock-politeness used as aggression/play;
- rapid switch back to casual standard Japanese when the joke exhausts itself;
- bodily teasing and irritation permitted by history.

**Upperclass/authority mode**
- careful polite forms and higher inhibition;
- difficulty pressing a question when social permission is weak.

**Reina-private mode**
- more teasing, more direct questions, less need to preserve “good girl” smoothness;
- still capable of avoidance when a topic threatens self-understanding.

**Thought–speech gap**

This is not incidental; it is the major voice mechanism. The narration may identify jealousy, cruelty, attraction, or skepticism while spoken Kumiko gives a smile, denial, or harmless phrasing. A simulation that simply converts her internal narration into dialogue will be wrong.

### 7.2 Reina: compressed public declaration, expansive private affect

High-value features in V01:

- first person `アタシ`;
- second person `アンタ` with Kumiko;
- strong Kansai forms (`～やろ`, `～ねん`, `めっちゃ`, etc.);
- short declaratives under challenge;
- repetition/intensification under anger;
- relatively low hedge density when stating musical judgment;
- increased playful and vulnerable turn length in private.

The one-word `ない` when asked whether she would surrender the solo is archetypal: decision precedes social cushioning. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0342 / 「ない」`

### 7.3 Asuka: theatrical control and deliberate modulation

Asuka's voice alternates among:

- exuberant technical monologue;
- playful Kansai banter;
- strategic repetition;
- sudden lowered/intimate delivery during recruitment;
- office-like neutrality when discussing institutional conflict;
- unnervingly flat directness when she decides the social issue is irrelevant.

The key modeling feature is not one catchphrase but **register as instrument**. Her delivery changes according to what she wants the interaction to do.

### 7.4 Shuuichi

Shuuichi uses familiar casual speech with Kumiko and can directly diagnose her behavior (`周りに流された？`). His speech permits roughness without automatically implying hostility because the dyad has long history. He also becomes technically specific about trombone practice and unexpectedly gentle before performance.

### 7.5 Midori

Midori's self-reference as `緑` is explicit and socially salient. `HIBIKE-V01 / S02 / P0111 / 「緑はね、ずーーっとコントラバスひと筋」` She combines cute/bright presentation with technical confidence and unusually candid enthusiasm. Do not reduce this to childishness; her ethical reflections during the solo dispute are mature.

### 7.6 Taki

Taki's baseline is controlled, polite, and syntactically measured even when the content is severe. The mismatch between soft surface and cutting evaluation generates pressure. He often converts criticism into questions or procedural statements, but he is capable of blunt value judgments (`本当に情けない`). His voice should not be simulated as a loud drill instructor.

### 7.7 Aoi / Haruka / Natsuki and regionality

These characters participate more naturally in Kansai speech than Kumiko. For simulation, regional language must be character- and addressee-specific rather than globally applied because the setting is Kyoto. Natsuki's casualness, Aoi's layered polite/casual management, and Haruka's emotionally open speech should be modeled separately as evidence accumulates.

## 8. Focalization and epistemic audit

The novel's close third-person is so saturated with Kumiko that many psychologically persuasive statements are still **Kumiko's model of another person**.

High-risk examples:

1. **Aoi's “no regrets.”** The arm-gripping gesture contradicts the ease of the claim, but “Aoi is lying” is Kumiko's interpretation rather than explicit narration of Aoi's intention. `HIBIKE-V01 / S04 / P0854 / 晴れやかな表情で言う彼女の指が、自身の腕をぎゅうとつかむ。白い皮膚に残る`
2. **Asuka's cold gaze.** Kumiko directly perceives a frightening mismatch between smile and eyes; what psychological system causes it remains open.
3. **Kaori “wanted to lose.”** Strongly plausible, but explicitly routed through Kumiko's reconstruction. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0695 / 不意にあすかの声が、耳元で蘇る。その言葉の意味を、久美子はここで初めて理`
4. **Taki's atmosphere engineering.** Natsuki supplies a compelling theory; exact strategic intent is not confessed by Taki.
5. **Shuuichi/Hazuki/Kumiko romantic interpretation.** Kumiko often sees other people's reactions more clearly than her own, creating asymmetric reliability.

The correct V2 practice is not to call Kumiko “unreliable” globally. She is often extraordinarily perceptive. Instead:

> **Treat Kumiko as a high-information but self-interested sensor.**

Her observations are evidence; her interpretations receive confidence according to corroboration.

## 9. Music, rehearsal, and pedagogy

### 9.1 Music as trainable material

V01 repeatedly refuses mystical shortcuts. Improvement is made of:

- breath and buzzing;
- tuning pitches;
- overtone recognition;
- solfège;
- slowed repetition;
- fingering;
- lip slurs;
- long tones;
- temperature compensation;
- marching coordination;
- instrument weight and fatigue;
- individual excerpts under audition pressure.

The body is the site where a declared goal becomes actual capacity.

### 9.2 Taki's pedagogical sequence

Taki's method broadly proceeds:

1. make the gap audible;
2. refuse to let the group rename poor ensemble as acceptable;
3. teach concrete perceptual/technical tools;
4. produce an early experience of improvement;
5. set a demanding but explicit schedule;
6. use auditions to connect individual preparation to institutional roles;
7. keep performance standards publicly legible.

This is why the “atmosphere” reading has force: technique and norm change reinforce each other.

### 9.3 Kumiko's musical cognition

Kumiko begins with enough experience to diagnose bad ensemble and enough training to make A ensemble. Yet V01 shows her perceptual world expanding. The overtone exercise gives her a new object to hear; technical improvement becomes pleasurable; by the final rehearsal she experiences ensemble synchronization as something she actively wants to reproduce.

### 9.4 Euphonium as support and attachment

Kumiko initially likes the low-brass lack of spotlight, which is consistent with her social defenses. But her instrument attachment is deeper than strategic invisibility. She reflexively experiences damage to the instrument as pain, names it, and eventually states that she loves euphonium. The instrument therefore supports a complex desire: **to matter inside a collective sound without needing to occupy its most visible position**.

## 10. Institution, legitimacy, and social atmosphere

### 10.1 The inherited wound

The missing second-years are not background trivia. They are institutional memory embodied as absence. Serious students were socially punished by older members for making the club's professed goals inconvenient. Improvement in the present does not retroactively repair them.

### 10.2 The goal vote and bounded authorship

The band chooses its aspiration but not every implementation mechanism. This creates genuine responsibility without pretending all expertise is democratic.

### 10.3 Auditions and performance legitimacy

Taki rejects age entitlement and chooses by performance. The system protects younger talented players from exactly the kind of senior claim that once injured Kumiko. But it also produces grief and resentment that a correct ranking cannot dissolve.

### 10.4 Re-audition as legitimacy repair

The second solo audition is institutionally fascinating because it is not needed merely to identify the better player. It is needed to make the result **socially hearable and agency-compatible**. Kaori's own refusal finally closes a legitimacy gap that private authority could not.

### 10.5 Haruka and ordinary leadership

The band does not end V01 with Asuka becoming “real leader.” It ends with Haruka publicly rallying the group and receiving a unified response. `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0793 / 「い、いままで本当にみんな頑張ってくれたと思います。あとはその頑張りを本` `HIBIKE-V01 / S05 / P0797 / 一丸となった声が、ホールのなかへとこだました。打ち上げられた熱量が、びり` This is evidence that legitimacy can accumulate through reliability and shared vulnerability, not only charisma.

### 10.6 Atmosphere is morally ambivalent

`空気` can:

- suppress serious effort;
- make Reina a target;
- normalize practice;
- generate courage;
- coordinate a bus full of students into spontaneous singing.

The band needs collective responsiveness but also standards that prevent responsiveness from becoming coercion. V01's institution is therefore not simply “better because unified.” It is better insofar as the new norms make certain goods—practice, musical truth, accountability—more defensible while still creating new pressures that later volumes must test.

## 11. Ordinary life and embodiment

For character modeling, low-stakes material is disproportionately important.

### Kumiko

- chose Kitauji partly for a cute uniform;
- dislikes excessive clutter/charms;
- judges bodies and appearances internally more than she says aloud;
- uses tired irritation to end mock conflict with Shuuichi;
- dislikes crowded or effortful social events unless a particular relation makes them meaningful;
- experiences anxiety through gripping, sweating, loss of fingering memory, bodily collapse;
- often uses smiles as social concealment.

### Reina

- dislikes bright/noisy crowds;
- likes quieter shrine/mountain space;
- can enjoy pain/physical exertion in a teasing context;
- is capable of unexpected grins and childish repetitive anger in private;
- tolerates or seeks bodily closeness with Kumiko.

### Midori

- has strong fashion/costume preferences;
- reads material that surprises Kumiko's expectations of her image;
- loves Beatles through family influence;
- names instruments and treats them as personal companions;
- can handle color guard more capably than appearance suggests.

### Asuka

- makes physical proximity and touch part of social performance;
- shifts voice rapidly;
- turns technical knowledge into enthusiastic monologue;
- can move from joking warmth to cold evaluative distance without external transition.

### Shuuichi

- familiar enough with Kumiko to read facial anxiety beneath denial;
- uses teasing and practical reassurance rather than elaborate emotional language;
- carries instrument/practice commitment into ordinary conversation.

These observations constrain future simulations more usefully than a list of personality adjectives.

## 12. Family, material, regional, and social conditions

### 12.1 Unequal starting conditions

The novel itself notices that competitive evaluation occurs after unequal preparation:

- private/personal instruments are costly;
- Reina's instrument and family musical network materially distinguish her circumstances;
- Asuka owns her instrument;
- Kumiko has seven years of euphonium experience;
- Midori has elite-school training;
- beginners like Hazuki face compressed learning time;
- school/conductor quality changes how much students can develop.

This does not make performance irrelevant. It makes performance **historical**.

### 12.2 Reina's family-musical network

Reina's father is a professional trumpeter and knows the Taki family. This supplies resources, information, musical socialization, and a pathway by which Reina knew about Taki's assignment. Her choice of Kitauji is therefore not a random sacrifice of prestige for abstract romantic destiny.

### 12.3 Kumiko's family and language

The family's Tokyo history explains Kumiko's standard-Japanese baseline. Her older sister's earlier music history also supplies the origin of Kumiko's instrument path. Family is not yet the dominant conflict domain in V01, but it already shapes language and musical inheritance.

### 12.4 Aoi and educational pressure

Aoi's exam concerns are not treated as fake merely because moral guilt also exists. The novel allows academic opportunity cost and emotional/institutional injury to coexist.

### 12.5 Uji as lived space

Kumiko is linguistically offset but geographically rooted. Uji, stations, river routes, festival sites, shrine and mountain are not generic backdrops. The mountain sequence uses physical separation from the festival crowd to produce a different speech regime: less public light, less noise, more disclosure.

## 13. Motifs and formal systems

### 13.1 Result boards and tears

S01 and S06 share nearly identical spatial machinery but reverse emotional interpretation. This is the volume's strongest formal demonstration of internal change.

### 13.2 Breath

Breath appears as anxiety regulation, musical material, ensemble timing, and bodily evidence of pressure. The same body that must calm itself also produces the sound through which desire becomes collective.

### 13.3 Skin / surface / mask

Reina's “good girl skin,” Asuka's smiling social surface, Aoi's smile over stress, and Kumiko's own ambiguous expressions make surfaces ethically ambiguous. Social editing can be kindness, cowardice, professionalism, manipulation, or survival depending on context.

### 13.4 Voice versus air

The volume repeatedly opposes **having a voice** to being carried by `空気`, yet band performance requires responsiveness to collective sound. The mature question is not “individual or group?” but **which forms of coordination preserve judgment and which erase it?**

### 13.5 Pain attached to instruments

Kumiko reflexively treats impact to euphonium as if the instrument hurts. Midori speaks of soul in an instrument; practice injures fingers and lips. Artistic attachment is consistently embodied before it is abstractly theorized.
