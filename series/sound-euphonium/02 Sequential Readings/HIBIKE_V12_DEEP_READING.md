## 2. Executive thesis - agency under painful legitimacy

V11 asked whether a leader can preserve legitimacy when she is also a competitor. V12 answers only after removing the most comforting escape route: **the procedure actually hurts Kumiko**.

At the second audition, Mayu receives the euphonium side of the third-movement soli. The novel then directly describes the Mayu/Reina pairing as beautiful and perfectly coordinated. There is no clean-text basis for rescuing Kumiko by declaring the decision obviously musically wrong. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0436-P0441`; `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0512-P0516`.

This produces the final movement's real legitimacy test. A fair or defensible process cannot guarantee emotionally acceptable outcomes. Nor can institutional care mean preventing every participant from experiencing loss. The deeper ethical requirement becomes **authorship of stake**: members must be allowed to decide what they are trying for, exert their own effort, receive the result, and retain ownership of the regret that follows.

The governing V12 formulation is therefore:

> **legitimacy becomes durable when participants can author their own stake, expose disagreement, preserve another person's right to choose, and continue acting together after disagreement becomes real.**

Kumiko eventually gives the strongest form of this principle before the final audition. She argues that the regret produced by losing belongs to the person who tried; nobody else has the right to take that regret away in advance by deciding that withdrawal is kinder. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0792-P0818`.

This does not turn full-throttle competition into a universal moral law. It is explicitly Kumiko's own institutional preference for the Kitauji she wants to lead. That boundedness is the point. Her leadership becomes stronger when she stops trying to speak from nowhere.

The final-movement arc can be compressed as:

> **inherited trust -> repeated-evaluation precarity -> painful legitimate outcome -> social personalization of procedure -> epistemic and relational rupture -> direct inquiry -> first-person institutional authorship -> plural trust -> shared performance -> transmission**

## 3. Why the Kansai soli matters: no hidden injustice is available

The second audition is the crucial held-out test because it denies a common narrative shortcut. Mayu is called for the soli; Kumiko's body immediately registers the result as threat. The narration records coldness, dizziness, and a compulsive opening and closing of the fist. Mayu silently apologizes. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0436-P0441`.

Kumiko's next behavior is analytically decisive. Mayu tries to preserve Kumiko's socially established seat beside the conductor. Kumiko refuses. At Kitauji, the better or currently preferred player sits closer to the conductor; if Mayu has the soli, the seating hierarchy should reflect that. Kumiko enforces the norm while being humiliated by it. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0449-P0460`.

This is a clean example of **principle surviving affect without eliminating affect**. Kumiko is neither serenely impartial nor secretly anti-merit. Her integrity consists partly in continuing to enact a rule that wounds her while remaining free to question whether the broader system is functioning as intended.

The rehearsal then removes the easy claim that Taki has committed an obvious technical error. Mayu's tone is described as beautiful; Reina answers it naturally; their coordination is called perfect. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0512-P0516`. The result can still be questioned institutionally, but not by pretending the selected pair is musically defective.

This distinction is central for later simulation work:

- **pain is not evidence of injustice;**
- **musical success is not evidence of social legitimacy;**
- **social cohesion is not evidence of musical correctness;**
- **one audition result is not a timeless scalar ranking of two whole people.**

The repeated-audition system makes the final point especially important. If evaluation can change as players and repertoire conditions change, then "A beat B" is a lossy summary. The relevant question is who fills a particular role best at a particular state boundary under a particular ensemble configuration.

## 4. Kumiko @ V12 - the self-application gap becomes explicit

Kumiko's V08-V09 growth gave her a powerful epistemic habit: distinguish observation, interpretation, and desire; validate a defended value before correcting the defense; intervene only when she has standing. V11 showed that these tools become less reliable when she is personally implicated. V12 turns that weakness into the main plot.

### 4.1 Fairness is no longer external to her

Kumiko can honestly say that the stronger performer should play. She can honestly want Mayu to participate. She can honestly want Kanade to improve. None of those statements cancels her own desire to stand beside Reina.

When the procedure selects Mayu, Kumiko begins searching for explanations that could make the outcome less threatening. Was Taki optimizing something else? Was the new low-brass configuration really his preferred sound? Is the policy itself creating unnecessary instability? These are legitimate questions, but the narrative makes their motivational entanglement visible. Kumiko is no longer merely analyzing an institution; she is trying to understand what the institution's judgment says about **her**.

This produces a useful simulation rule: `KUMIKO@V12` should become **less** certain and more prone to recursive interpretation when a fairness question is also a self-worth question. Mature mediation skill does not grant immunity to motivated reasoning.

### 4.2 The Reina rupture is ideological, institutional, and relational at once

The riverside argument is the strongest Kumiko/Reina conflict in the mainline novels. Reina's position is harsh but coherent: if a performer were overwhelmingly better, the decision would not be ambiguous; blaming Taki can substitute for acknowledging insufficient result; effort must show in performance. Kumiko rejects the totalization. Ability is comparative; opportunity and training are unequal; reducing every failed outcome to insufficient effort can be cruel; and a president cannot answer member doubt by commanding faith in the conductor. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0857-P0896`.

Reina's accusation that Kumiko is unfit to be president makes the conflict personal. The line hurts precisely because the presidency is one of the identities Kumiko has spent two years learning to inhabit.

Yet the next morning they still perform their institutional roles. They do not explode the executive structure or sabotage rehearsal. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P1038-P1053`. This is not reconciliation. It is evidence that a mature relationship can contain an unresolved ideological break while preserving task coordination.

### 4.3 Direct inquiry repairs epistemics before it repairs emotion

Kumiko's key corrective move is not to obtain reassurance from Reina. It is to ask Taki the question she had avoided in V11.

She asks why the low-brass configuration changed and whether it really reflects the music he wants. Taki explains his contest-oriented reasoning, ensemble balance, the students' own chosen national goal, and the pleasure he takes in hearing improvement. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0958-P0983`.

The answer does not prove that every selection is objectively correct. Instead, it turns an opaque authority into an inspectable reasoning process. Kumiko also recognizes that some of her fear had been displaced into suspicion of the institution.

This is a major V2 legitimacy advance:

> **epistemic trust is stronger when authority can be questioned without the question itself being treated as disloyalty.**

### 4.4 Kumiko stops trying to solve people from above

Midori eventually identifies a recurrent Kumiko problem: she is asking "what should I do?" when the deeper unresolved question is "what do I want?" `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0316-P0324`.

The same issue appears with Mayu. Kumiko keeps trying to uncover Mayu's supposedly deeper desire because she assumes a serious musician must secretly want the soli. Mayu's response breaks that model. She says that supporting Kumiko is itself her real preference and asks why that preference should be ignored merely because Kumiko dislikes the resulting asymmetry. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0350-P0394`.

Kumiko's mature leadership cannot consist of discovering everyone's true inner competitive self. Sometimes the person is telling the truth and the conflict remains.

### 4.5 The final speech is institutional authorship, not neutral mediation

Before the final audition, Kumiko no longer claims to have discovered the universal right answer. She says what kind of Kitauji she likes and what she wants the members to do. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0792-P0818`.

Her position contains several linked claims:

- status and year should not automatically decide opportunity;
- everyone should use all the strength they currently have;
- self-removal for harmony should not automatically be rewarded as morally superior;
- losing is allowed to hurt;
- regret belongs to the person who took the risk;
- effort cannot guarantee victory, but it can make the outcome one's own;
- shared performance does not require identical private motives.

The strongest improvement is epistemic. Kumiko labels this as **her wish**. She is no longer pretending that leadership means erasing herself from the statement.

## 5. Mayu @ V12 - a different value order must remain different

V12 is the volume that finally makes Mayu reconstructible without forcing her into either a saint/antagonist binary or a hidden-rival theory.

### 5.1 Transfer history produced adaptation competence, not emptiness

At the pool Mayu describes repeated moves as something she genuinely does not hate. New places create new acquaintances; she has friends around the country; she researches a new school before arriving. `HIBIKE-V12 / S02 / P0559-P0583`.

Her camera extends this social strategy. She keeps large archives of former classmates and contest memories, yet the pool scene ends by emphasizing that she herself is not in the photograph. `HIBIKE-V12 / S02 / P0572-P0599`.

The motif is strong. Its cause is not settled. V1 offered an elegant explanation in which remaining behind the camera makes later departure easier and protects Mayu from being fixed in a social image. V2 retains that as a plausible hypothesis but **downgrades causal certainty**. The clean text establishes the behavior, not one complete psychology.

### 5.2 Her Liz reading is a coherent attachment philosophy

Mayu calls Liz greedy for fastening onto one blue bird when other relationships were available and suggests that wanting less would make separation hurt less. `HIBIKE-V12 / S02 / P0585-P0593`.

This is one of the clearest statements of her relational logic. It coheres with broad rather than exclusive connection, high transfer adaptability, photographs of many groups, and reluctance to claim a unique place. But the model must resist over-teleology. We should not write: "Mayu was hurt by X, therefore she developed Y." The source gives a philosophy and a behavior pattern; it does not fully narrate the origin.

### 5.3 Withdrawal is sincere conflict management

Mayu repeatedly offers to yield the soli. In a more suspicious reading, this can look manipulative because the offer forces others to say they want her to compete. V12 does not support making conscious manipulation the default.

When Kumiko presses her directly, Mayu states a different priority order: she wants enjoyable shared time, friends, memories, and a good ensemble atmosphere; contest distinction is secondary. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0350-P0394`.

This does not make her unserious. She practices, can win the soli, and can perform at a high level. It means **effort and result-attachment are separate dimensions**.

### 5.4 Recognition can fail by demanding the wrong confession

Kumiko's recognition-first method reaches a limit here. She assumes Mayu is hiding the desire to win. Mayu asks, in effect, why her actual desire to support Kumiko is not allowed to count as a real desire.

This changes a core V2 operating rule:

> **recognition-before-correction requires accepting the possibility that the recognized value will remain different from the mediator's preferred value.**

Otherwise "recognition" becomes a soft interrogation technique aimed at producing ideological conformity.

### 5.5 Co-belonging replaces total-place competition

Kanade eventually makes explicit what Kumiko has feared: Mayu might take Kumiko's place. The final movement answers by splitting **function** from **person**. Mayu can take a soli. Kumiko remains Kumiko. A seat, part, office, or musical assignment is a function; person-specific relationships are not transferred by the same mechanism. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0711-P0715`.

The group photograph with Kaho, Kanade, Mayu, and Kumiko is therefore a useful endpoint. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0823-P0843`. Mayu, who so often records others, enters the visible group. The source supports inclusion. It does not prove that every distancing mechanism has vanished.

## 6. Reina @ V12 - doctrine can survive correction

V12 does not "soften" Reina by revealing her music-first position was fake. Her standards remain severe. What changes is her ability to admit that a different ethical position can also be right.

### 6.1 Professionalism and preference separate

The Mayu/Reina soli is excellent. Reina does not punish Mayu for being the selected euphonist, does not deliberately play worse, and does not refuse the role because she wanted Kumiko. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0512-P0516`.

This matters enormously for simulation. Reina's selective musical-partner desire is real; her professional execution is also real. The model must permit both.

### 6.2 Effort doctrine reaches its most dangerous form

In the argument with Kumiko, Reina treats insufficient result as insufficient effort and treats Taki's judgment as the authoritative musical resolution. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0857-P0896`.

The problem is not that hard work suddenly becomes bad. The problem is explanatory totalization. Once "try harder" becomes the answer to unequal opportunity, contextual comparison, institutional distrust, and every failed result, a useful discipline becomes a moral theory that cannot see its own blind spots.

### 6.3 Repair does not require conversion

After Kumiko has rethought Taki and her own position, the two girls explicitly repair. The scene uses verbal recognition and embodied closeness rather than their older assumption that special people should simply understand without words. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0718-P0761`.

Reina apologizes for the presidency insult and concedes that Kumiko is also right. This is not a conversion to Kumiko's entire worldview. Reina still reveres Taki and still values overwhelming musical ability. Mature relationship here means **capacity for principled difference without relational annihilation**.

### 6.4 Relationship plurality remains canonical

At Daikichiyama Reina says she plans to study music in the United States and still intends eventually to confess romantic love to Taki. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0998-P1017`.

The same sequence preserves the exceptional meaning of Kumiko. Kumiko understands their present co-presence as happiness and regards Reina as special beyond the immediate high-school period. The prose therefore does not permit a clean exclusive taxonomy in which one relationship must be declared false to protect another.

## 7. Shuuichi @ V12 - support without taking over

Early V12 explicitly describes Kumiko and Shuuichi's romantic state as suspended rather than currently ordinary dating. `HIBIKE-V12 / S02 / P0466-P0475`. That distinction matters because later support should not be misread as proof the suspension never existed.

Shuuichi's most characteristic scene occurs when he waits outside Kumiko's home. He does not offer superior musical analysis, force disclosure, or demand that the relationship be restored. He tells Kumiko she does not have to keep working harder and defines part of his vice-presidential role as being present when she cannot carry everything. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0675-P0705`.

This strengthens his longitudinal function: **permission without hierarchy** becomes **support without takeover**.

Kumiko's interiority directly establishes that she still loves him. After Nationals she tells him so, and he makes clear that he has carried the Italian-white hairpin through the year because his attachment persisted. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P1222-P1235`.

The correct state freeze is therefore:

- active dating status was suspended during the final year;
- reciprocal romantic attachment persists and is explicitly re-declared;
- the exact immediate post-Nationals administrative label is less explicit than the love itself;
- Shuuichi remains institutionally meaningful independently of romance.

## 8. Taki @ V12 - from founder myth to contestable expertise

V08-V11 progressively separated Taki's technical authority from unquestioned charismatic legitimacy. V12 completes that process.

### 8.1 The students can interrogate expert reasoning

Kumiko asks why he changed the low-brass balance and whether the result really reflects his preferred music. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0958-P0983`. Taki explains. He does not punish the question as insolence.

The important institutional shift is therefore not "Taki is right." It is **Taki can remain legitimate while being questioned**.

### 8.2 Asuka removes the god model

Asuka explicitly tells Kumiko that Taki is not a god and emphasizes his imperfect human-relations skill. At the same time, she points to his sustained work toward the goal the students themselves asked him to pursue. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0642-P0651`.

This yields a better authority model:

> **expertise + demonstrated labor + explainable reasoning + contestability + bounded fallibility**

rather than either blind obedience or generalized suspicion.

### 8.3 The teacher is still learning

Taki later describes himself as incomplete and continues learning from other educators. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0771-P0791`. This becomes relevant to Kumiko's vocational choice. She does not decide to become an infallible authority. She becomes interested in a profession where observation, guidance, transmission, and fallibility must coexist.

## 9. Team Oumae and institutional externalities

V11 established that distributed authority can still create externalities. V12 supplies the full case.

- Reina's severe instruction can raise musical standards while generating fear and peer-care labor.
- Shuuichi's softer style can protect sustainability while risking avoidance of hard correction.
- Kumiko's relational leadership can validate people while drifting into over-responsibility and political containment.
- repeated auditions can preserve developmental opportunity while producing chronic evaluative precarity.
- social loyalty can comfort Kumiko while marginalizing Mayu.

None of these observations requires identifying a villain. Midori's important formulation is that a problem can become large even when nobody is simply bad. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0814-P0856`.

This is a mature institutional claim. Conflict is sometimes the cost of keeping multiple real values alive rather than evidence that one malicious actor corrupted a harmonious system.

The final Kumiko speech is the governance answer because it makes the institution's normative choice explicit. Members do not have to want the same things. They do have to understand the rule of participation, exercise their own agency under it, and stop substituting unilateral self-erasure for the decisions of others.

## 10. Motomu - inherited identity versus chosen belonging

The prologue begins at the Tsukinaga family grave and directly identifies Motomu's dead sister Mitsuru. His parents are music educators; his grandfather Genichiro is a celebrated conductor; music has surrounded him from childhood. `HIBIKE-V12 / S01 / P0002-P0014`.

Later Higuchi discloses this history to Kumiko without Motomu's permission. Concern and privacy violation coexist. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0079-P0105`.

Motomu's own explanation is more important than anybody else's. He hates being treated as a route to his grandfather and hates praise whose hidden function is access to the famous family name. He understands that Genichiro cares, but care can still corner the recipient. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0197-P0223`.

His choice of Kitauji is therefore an identity decision. He hoped Taki - himself shaped by famous lineage and bereavement - would see him as one student rather than as a Tsukinaga. His double bass preserves a relation to Mitsuru; Midori's resemblance to his sister helps explain the intensity of their bond; but he explicitly states that he has never regretted choosing Kitauji even when another school achieved more competition success.

The endpoint is direct: Motomu identifies himself as a person of Kitauji. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0223`.

The Green/Motomu relationship should remain equally precise. His grief about her graduation is strong; her praise and wantedness are strong; their shared musical inheritance is strong. V12 still does not provide an explicit romance declaration. Differentiated attachment remains the safer model.

## 11. Future authorship - vocation emerges after action

V12 is unusually careful about Kumiko's future because it refuses the common coming-of-age shortcut in which the protagonist discovers an occupation that was secretly predetermined.

At home Kumiko admits she has not found something on which she wants to stake her life. Her mother responds that this is ordinary; work is shaped by income, holidays, opportunity, and discovery after entry as well as by passion. Mamiko represents a more passion-centered path, but the family no longer forces one model to defeat the other. `HIBIKE-V12 / S02 / P0412-P0478`.

Kumiko's eventual teaching decision is therefore not a recovered childhood essence. It becomes available only after years of:

- observing different leadership systems;
- noticing people in social detail;
- mediating conflict;
- learning the limits of mediation;
- teaching musical and social skills;
- carrying institutional memory;
- and seeing adults like Taki remain imperfect learners.

When asked what kind of adult she wants to become, Kumiko answers that she wants to become a teacher. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0779-P0791`.

The epilogue confirms the choice without turning it into a fairy-tale endpoint. Adult Kumiko is Kitauji's assistant adviser. Her file contains member lists, scores, logistics material, the handwritten piece notebook, and the hairpin. `HIBIKE-V12 / S05 / P0021-P0028`.

The objects matter because they collapse the false choice between "institution" and "personal life." Administrative memory, musical inheritance, and intimate relationship artifacts can inhabit the same adult work file.

## 12. Music as technical, relational, and autobiographical causality

The V2 project has consistently rejected using music as decorative metaphor. V12 strengthens the causal layer at every scale.

### 12.1 Full-score literacy

Kumiko reads beyond the euphonium line to determine whether she is foreground, support, or part of a low-brass composite with bassoon and bass trombone. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0157-P0158`. Mature musicianship is relational attention to the score, not just execution of one's own notes.

### 12.2 Mature Kitauji is repeatable

Earlier Kitauji could produce a "miracle" performance. By V12 the group is frightening precisely because strong execution can be reproduced. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P1157-P1164`. This fulfills the institutional development first visible in V02: elite performance is partly **reduced variance**.

### 12.3 Context changes hearing

Hashimoto tells the students that *Poem of a Year* was written after the composer's father died and argues that musicians should understand more than printed notes. `HIBIKE-V12 / S03 / P0748-P0775`.

This is not permission to replace technique with biography. It establishes that musical meaning is generated by technique, score, historical context, performer memory, ensemble interaction, and listener attention together.

### 12.4 Nationals becomes public autobiography

During the national performance, Kumiko's listening is saturated by the people and states accumulated across three years. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P1164-P1181`. The piece does not cease being a public contest work because it is autobiographical to her. Nor does personal memory excuse technical weakness. The series has reached a point where the same performance can be simultaneously:

- technically disciplined;
- competitively judged;
- institutionally collective;
- personally autobiographical;
- and publicly communicative.

## 13. National gold - meaningful precisely because it is not sufficient

Kitauji wins national gold. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P1204-P1207`. The correct V2 reading must avoid two opposite mistakes.

One mistake is result-monism: "they won, therefore the system and every choice were right." The other is anti-result flattening: "the themes taught us winning does not matter." Both are wrong.

The band wanted gold. It trained for gold. Taki and the students orient real parts of their lives toward gold. The result is joyous and consequential.

But the story has already secured value elsewhere before the award is announced:

- Mayu's shared time is real value;
- Motomu's chosen belonging is real value;
- Kumiko and Reina repair before the medal;
- Shuuichi supports Kumiko before the medal;
- Kumiko chooses a future before the medal;
- the final speech grants ownership of effort before the medal;
- the score already carries years of memory before the medal.

Gold therefore becomes **meaningful confirmation of a shared project rather than the sole source of that project's worth**.

Taki's tears fit the same architecture. The national goal was student-authored at the beginning of the series and technically implemented through his expertise. At the end, the result belongs to neither conductor nor students alone.

## 14. Relationship plurality at the final-movement boundary

V12 is unusually resistant to exclusive relationship sorting.

### Kumiko - Reina

They share extraordinary musical and emotional intimacy, suffer a severe ideological rupture, verbally repair, embrace, discuss future separation, and preserve explicit specialness. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0718-P0761`; `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0998-P1017`.

### Kumiko - Shuuichi

The romantic relationship had been suspended, but attachment remained. After Nationals Kumiko directly re-declares love and Shuuichi makes his continued love equally clear. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P1222-P1235`.

### Reina - Taki

Reina still describes her feeling as romantic love and plans a future confession. `HIBIKE-V12 / S04 / P0998-P1017`.

The corpus should therefore not "solve" ambiguity by falsifying one relationship. A simulation-grade model needs multiple relationship dimensions: romance, musical partnership, trust, shared history, bodily intimacy, disclosure access, authority, dependency, and future expectation do not collapse into one scalar closeness score.

## 16. Simulation-grade V12 state freeze

### `KUMIKO@V12`

- **Primary strength:** high social observation + recovered first-person authorship.
- **Failure mode under self-relevant evaluation:** recursive interpretation, somatic threat, suspicion of procedure, temptation to universalize the solution she wants.
- **Recovery route:** direct question -> external perspective -> explicit desire -> bounded public commitment.
- **Leadership rule:** can validate disagreement without promising that all legitimate values can be simultaneously satisfied.
- **Negative constraint:** do not write her as neutral under her own audition or as someone who has become indifferent to recognition.

### `MAYU@V12`

- **Primary strategy:** adapt, preserve social field, reduce exclusive claims, document shared time.
- **Positive desire:** enjoyable high-level ensemble life with friends and memories.
- **Competition:** capable and serious but lower result-attachment than Kumiko/Reina.
- **Threat response:** offer self-removal before imposing conflict on group.
- **Negative constraint:** do not invent secret ambition or conscious manipulation merely because withdrawal pressures others.
- **Open:** causal explanation for aversion to her own photographs.

### `REINA@V12`

- **Public music mode:** severe, result-oriented, high-trust in expert judgment.
- **Relational vulnerability:** selective possessiveness, fear of future distance, need for explicit repair after rupture.
- **Conflict risk:** can turn useful effort doctrine into an overly total explanation and can say cruel things while defending principle.
- **Growth:** can apologize and accept plural correctness without discarding standards.

### `SHUUICHI@V12`

- **Care mode:** availability, logistics, humor, physical/social co-regulation, permission to stop overfunctioning.
- **Relationship:** reciprocal romantic attachment explicit; exact immediate formal label remains less explicit.
- **Institutional value:** separate from romance; vice-presidential support is a genuine functional pillar.

### `TAKI@V12`

- **Authority:** expert, explainable, contestable, fallible.
- **Strength:** technical comparison, ensemble design, sustained work toward student-authored objective.
- **Weakness:** human-relations opacity and the institutional myth generated by prior success.
- **Negative constraint:** not omniscient and not a manipulative mastermind without evidence.

### `MOTOMU@V12`

- **Identity threat:** being reduced to famous lineage or instrumental access.
- **Attachment:** sister inheritance + strong Midori wantedness.
- **Belonging:** actively chooses Kitauji as person-level identity.
- **Negative constraint:** do not reduce Midori to sister substitution or assert romance absent explicit evidence.

## 17. Negative constraints after the final movement

1. Do not make **fairness** synonymous with emotional painless outcome.
2. Do not make **merit** a scalar whole-person ranking; keep role, repertoire, time, and ensemble context.
3. Do not make **Mayu** a secret manipulator, hidden Reina, or ambitionless saint.
4. Do not make **Reina** universally blunt or universally incapable of revising herself.
5. Do not make **Kumiko** an omniscient empath; her strongest failure mode is motivated interpretation under personal stake.
6. Do not make **Shuuichi** narratively disposable once the romance is suspended.
7. Do not make **Taki** either infallible or arbitrary.
8. Do not make **national gold** either everything or nothing.
9. Do not make **teacher Kumiko** a destiny that was always consciously present.
10. Do not collapse **Kumiko/Reina**, **Kumiko/Shuuichi**, and **Reina/Taki** into one exclusivity contest not supplied by the prose.
11. Do not infer the cause of **Mayu's photograph aversion** as settled.
12. Do not use cover or promotional imagery as governing prose evidence unless separately admitted as F-class visual evidence.

## 19. V12 canonical freeze

HIBIKE-V12 closes the main third-year movement with the national goal achieved, but the deeper closure occurs earlier: the characters have learned how to continue together without requiring identical motives, painless fairness, infallible authority, or permanent relational proximity.

The volume preserves the series' demanding merit ideal while making its ethical boundary clearer. People should be allowed to try fully and be judged honestly; the institution must also remain answerable to the people who bear the consequences. Care does not mean stealing another person's right to fail. Expertise does not mean immunity from questions. Relationship does not mean ideological sameness. Memory does not mean stasis.

> **Canonical V12 freeze: fair process cannot protect people from pain; durable legitimacy requires participant-authored stake, contestable expertise, explicit disagreement, ownership of effort and regret, and the ability to preserve relationship and institutional function without pretending difference has disappeared.**
