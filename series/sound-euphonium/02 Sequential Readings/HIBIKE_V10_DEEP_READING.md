This is the tenth canonical sequential reading of *Sound! Euphonium* V2 and the anthology/polyphonic calibration pass that follows the completed HIBIKE-V08–V09 second-year movement. HIBIKE-V08 and V09 froze high-pressure models of institutional reproduction, recognition, differentiated attachment, competitive defeat, and succession. ## 2. Executive thesis: the “true story” is conditional, plural, and larger than crisis

HIBIKE-V10 is easy to misclassify as a collection of useful extras around the main plot. That description understates what its form does to the analytical corpus.

The mainline volumes disproportionately observe people when something is at stake: auditions, competition, quitting, leadership breakdown, love, separation, or institutional conflict. A character model trained only on those scenes will learn crisis outputs and mistake them for personality. The anthology deliberately changes the observation conditions.

It gives us:

- Nozomi on graduation morning after the major Mizore conflict is already over;
- Hazuki, Kumiko, Reina, and Midori studying and eating sweets in a house;
- Kaori privately reconstructing first-year Asuka and writing a domestic proposal;
- Aoi in university after the act that once defined her—quitting—has receded into biography;
- Kanade cooking with Ririka;
- Serina resisting the word `友達` while behaving like a friend;
- Haruka imagining relationships that may remain real without becoming permanent or maximally intimate;
- Chihiro alive, joking, loving, and diagnosing Taki before bereavement turns her into memory;
- Mirei being drawn into silly hair play while retaining a touch boundary;
- Natsuki wondering whether the best musical experience of her life is already behind her;
- Riko answering physical separation with mobility rather than passive waiting;
- and Yuuko admitting in writing what her normal speech almost never allows her to say cleanly.

Then S12 returns to Kumiko and makes the same anti-overfitting move musically. A full ensemble with a conductor can hide both a player’s exceptional strength and a specific weakness. Tsubame appears generally weak under one set of conditions, yet may be Kitauji’s best marimba player. Her serious problem is not “bad musicianship” as a scalar property. It is a highly local coordination deficit: she does not instinctively read other musicians’ breath and visual preparation before sound begins. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P0873-P0957`

The collection’s deepest V2 proposition is therefore:

> **Character truth is conditional: person-state × relationship-state × task × stakes × environment produces behavior. “The true story” is not a secret essence behind performance; it is the larger distribution of outputs that becomes visible when perspective and situation change.**

That has a direct simulation consequence. A good model must explain both:

- Yuuko publicly holding a devastated band together **and** privately admitting that Natsuki was the person who switched her president-state off;
- Reina delivering severe musical correction **and** delaying an invitation because she is afraid Kumiko may reject her;
- Kanade using calculated charm defensively **and** enjoying calculated charm as an authentic shared language with Ririka;
- Mizore once organizing her future around Nozomi **and** later declining a Nozomi-linked ensemble invitation without hesitation;
- Aoi leaving Kitauji **and** later choosing music again.

The volume’s governing movement can be stated as:

> **crisis-defined identity → alternate focalization → ordinary/relational calibration → hidden capability and limitation → self-authored continuation → transmissible knowledge**

HIBIKE-V10 is consequently not a pause between “important” arcs. It is the volume that makes the later character-modeling project substantially safer.

## 3. Formal architecture: twelve alternate centers around one Kumiko institutional novella

S12 is the only large section that returns to Kumiko as the sustained main focalizer. The surrounding stories distribute interior access across Nozomi, Hazuki, Kaori, Aoi, Kanade, Serina, Haruka, Satomi, Mirei, Natsuki, Riko, and Yuuko.

That architecture resembles an ensemble more than a conventional protagonist-centered novel. Each focalizer contributes information unavailable to the others.

| Section | Primary focalizer | Principal modeling gain |
|---|---|---|
| S01 | Nozomi | jealousy, guilt, silence, graduation-state Mizore relationship |
| S02 | Hazuki | Kumiko’s over-consideration, Midori’s deliberate seniority, Reina’s pragmatic future logic |
| S03 | Kaori | early Asuka, usefulness/wantedness, specialness, primacy, domestic desire |
| S04 | Aoi | post-quitting agency, return to music, Haruka’s mature pride |
| S05 | Kanade | strategic self-presentation as authentic play, Ririka domestic friendship |
| S06 | Serina | label aversion, Azusa attraction, Midori as recognizer/motivator |
| S07 | Haruka | bounded friendship, post-office identity, external Asuka/Kaori perception |
| S08 | Satomi | Chihiro alive, Taki’s pre-bereavement relational baseline |
| S09 | Mirei | touch boundary, ordinary low-brass play, voluntary accommodation |
| S10 | Natsuki | burnout, adult ethical anxiety, unilluminated value |
| S11 | Riko | long-distance attachment, active waiting, musical repair vocation |
| S12 | Kumiko | governance, chamber pedagogy, relational selection, diagnostic leadership |
| S13 | Yuuko | gratitude, leadership co-regulation, Natsuki intimacy, written register |

The V2 consequence is methodological:

> **alternate focalization is not supplementary flavor; it is privileged cross-validation against Kumiko’s observational model.**

Where a prior volume contained only Kumiko’s interpretation of another person, V10 may raise or lower confidence. Where V10 itself gives one character’s interpretation of another, it remains [C]. More access improves epistemic resolution; it does not eliminate the need for labels.

## 4. Fine and D.C.: graduation as a change of access, not an erasure of relation

The collection is framed by two stories with the same title and different musical endings: Fine and D.C.

The first observes graduation through Nozomi. The last returns to the same threshold through Yuuko and reveals the contents of the letter already circulating in the social field.

This does not mean “nothing ends.” School routines really do end. Instrument sections change. Office titles expire. Daily access becomes harder. The book instead refuses to make **institutional ending equivalent to relational nonexistence**.

S01 already shows that the post-V09 Mizore/Nozomi state is neither old dependency nor emotionally purified equilibrium. Natsuki’s suggestion that Mizore may become a professional makes Nozomi reflexively resist; she envies Natsuki’s ability to speak about Mizore with uncomplicated confidence. `HIBIKE-V10 / S01 / P0037-P0058` Nozomi is also faintly irritated when Yuuko occupies a close place beside Mizore. The residual emotion matters because it backtests V09’s claim that maturation is greater control over action, not the deletion of jealousy.

Yet Nozomi and Mizore can now remain silent together without the silence automatically becoming threat. When Mizore notices that Nozomi had started to say something, she gives her attention back; Nozomi ultimately asks for a graduation photograph and experiences the resulting quiet as unexpectedly comfortable. `S01 / P0079-P0095`

Nozomi’s guilt about having hurt or disappointed Mizore is directly present [A], but V2 should not reduce the photo request to guilt alone. She also wants the image. She wants the relationship preserved in a form she can carry forward.

S13 then proves that graduation has widened rather than simply transferred Mizore’s relational world. Mizore runs because she sees Yuuko. Her “important errand” is to say good morning and, more importantly, to deliver long-deferred thanks. `HIBIKE-V10 / S13 / P0013-P0041`

Yuuko’s response is revealing. The tears come from discovering that the care **arrived**. Mizore says Yuuko helped her `ずっと`; Yuuko answers by thanking Mizore for remaining in band, continuing oboe, and enduring the same institutional history beside her. `S13 / P0031-P0059`

This is stronger than the old formulation of Yuuko as an emergency secondary anchor. It does not require erasing Nozomi’s unique weight. Mizore’s world has become plural enough that another attachment can be directly acknowledged on its own terms.

At the end Yuuko pushes Mizore’s back even while knowing Mizore can walk forward without her. `S13 / P0072-P0079` The gesture is self-aware. She does not believe Mizore requires propulsion. She wants one last embodied act in the old relation.

**Checkpoint consequence:** differentiation does not mean relational evacuation. People can stop being necessary without becoming meaningless.

## 5. S02 — study as future option, friendship as present value

「勉学は学生の義務ですから」 is a small story with disproportionate modeling value because nobody is in crisis.

Hazuki, Midori, Kumiko, and Reina study together at Midori’s house. The scene supplies domestic objects, sweets, pets, joking, unfinished homework, mild embarrassment, and future-talk. These are exactly the conditions under which a crisis-trained reconstruction system tends to become generic unless ordinary evidence has been preserved.

Hazuki observes a characteristic Kumiko behavior when Kumiko tries to comfort her about unfinished work and Reina casually reveals that she herself is nearly done. Hazuki’s judgment is direct: Kumiko’s consideration for others is both a good trait and a flaw. `HIBIKE-V10 / S02 / P0019-P0024` This external focalization strengthens a longitudinal mechanism already visible inside Kumiko: she often edits herself toward the affective needs of the room before checking whether that intervention is necessary.

Midori provides a more deliberate account of seniority than her cheerful manner might suggest. She studies because, as an upperclassman, she wants to become the kind of person who can reliably answer juniors’ questions—explicitly recalling Asuka as a model. `S02 / P0038-P0041` Care here is not warmth alone. It is competence-building in anticipation of another person’s need.

Reina’s account of academic work is characteristically pragmatic. Whether school subjects are useful depends on the future one chooses, but if the future is not yet known, current study protects later possibility from being narrowed by present neglect. `S02 / P0111-P0119` She does not romanticize study. She treats it as option preservation.

Hazuki’s answer is different. She can imagine that present happiness with friends is still worth something even if it produces future difficulty. The text does not force one of these positions to defeat the other.

This matters because the series repeatedly places characters between optimization and lived time. V10’s ordinary scenes say that future-capacity and present-value are different goods.

Reina’s behavior in the story also widens her register. She can be teasing, awkward in group encouragement, embarrassed by praise, and casually informative. `S02 / P0055-P0102` A model that renders every Reina line as a clipped hierarchy judgment is already wrong by V10.

## 6. S03 — Kaori and Asuka: usefulness, specialness, and the request for ordinary life

「だけど、あのとき」 is one of the most important Asuka documents in the corpus because its focalizer is Kaori and its temporal structure combines first-year memory with a graduation letter.

The flashback occurs before Kitauji’s later reform. Low-brass first-years are quitting for different reasons: disappointment, atmosphere, and the judgment that remaining has no value. Asuka does not stop them. Her stated position is that she needs only to play euphonium and sees little point in forcing someone to remain in an aimless club. `HIBIKE-V10 / S03 / P0029-P0036`

Kaori and Asuka also diagnose opposite institutional pathologies. Low brass can acquire an atmosphere in which quitting becomes easy, while other sections can make leaving socially difficult. `S03 / P0039-P0051` The correct V2 lesson is not “retention good” or “exit good.” A person’s ability to remain or leave should not be manufactured by coercive air.

When Kaori says Asuka could reform the club, Asuka refuses the hero role. But she says she would try to stop **Kaori** from quitting because Kaori is good and useful. `S03 / P0066-P0074`

This line is analytically dangerous if read in isolation. It supports usefulness as one register through which Asuka expresses value. It does **not** prove that utility exhausts her attachment. Kaori herself interprets Asuka’s harsh statements as tests—whether the other person will leave [C]. That interpretation is highly valuable because Kaori knows Asuka well, but it remains Kaori’s model rather than direct Asuka introspection.

Kaori then says what she can own directly:

- she likes Asuka;
- Asuka should understand that she is loved;
- and Kaori is willing to begin proving it from her side. `S03 / P0076-P0085`

The most revealing exchange comes later. Kaori describes Asuka as special. Asuka asks whether Kaori would stop liking her if she were no longer special. Kaori does not immediately deny it. Asuka’s eyes show disappointment before her public expression resets. `S03 / P0090-P0104`

This is a major refinement of the series’ specialness theme. For Asuka, being loved **because one is extraordinary** can reproduce insecurity rather than solve it. If the relationship depends on performance, what remains if the performance disappears?

Kaori’s letter attempts a delayed answer. She says she still loves Asuka, wants to be her `一番`, and proposes living together because their postsecondary schools will be near one another. `S03 / P0109-P0116`

That is not simply “admiration upgraded into romance” as a categorical fact. It is a move from aesthetic/institutional exceptionalism toward **domestic continuity**. Kaori wants access to the person after the performance ends.

Asuka’s answer is omitted. That omission is ethically important. Kaori’s directness gains authority without automatically authoring Asuka’s future for her.

## 7. S04 — Aoi: leaving an institution is not the same thing as leaving music

「そして、そのとき」 revisits Aoi after the act by which the mainline most strongly remembers her: quitting Kitauji.

At university, Aoi encounters another former band student and is invited toward a wind ensemble. The invitation is deliberately low-coercion: if the club is frightening or unpleasant, leave; music is a hobby and need not justify suffering. `HIBIKE-V10 / S04 / P0049-P0057`

Aoi then notices bodily memory. She misses the keys and weight of the saxophone. The desire is not produced by a lecture about regret. It appears as `吹きたい`. `S04 / P0060-P0062`

V2 must preserve two truths simultaneously:

1. leaving high-school Kitauji was a real, defensible choice under Aoi’s conditions;
2. that choice did not determine her permanent relationship with music.

The story therefore rejects identity foreclosure. “Quitter” was a state transition, not an essence.

Aoi’s more complicated unresolved feeling also becomes visible. She wanted forgiveness for leaving, but she did not want the event to become so trivial that everyone forgot it. `S04 / P0068-P0076` Moral relief and historical significance pull in opposite directions.

Haruka, encountered unexpectedly, supplies another longitudinal correction. Asked whether she ever felt glad to have been president, she answers immediately that there were many such moments. `S04 / P0093-P0095` The V01 Haruka model cannot be frozen at insecurity. Leadership became a source of earned pride.

Aoi’s route/map thought is a useful formal analogy [D/E]. A map can show the known way across a huge campus; whether to take that route or go elsewhere remains a choice. `S04 / P0087-P0088`

The volume’s mature autonomy model is therefore not “always know what you want.” It is **preserve the possibility of revising how you live once the conditions change**.

## 8. S05 — Kanade and Ririka: calculation is not the opposite of sincerity

「上質な休日の過ごし方」 is a direct corrective to simplistic “mask = false self” models.

Kanade and Ririka cook together in Ririka’s ordinary family home. Kanade notices that Ririka understands her own charm and deliberately uses it to enter other people’s space. Crucially, Kanade does not expose this as fraud. She likes it. `HIBIKE-V10 / S05 / P0037-P0038`

Ririka likewise openly values stylish naming, being seen as cute or sophisticated, and the pleasurable social effects of making ordinary life feel more elegant. Kanade participates rather than standing above the performance as the one person who sees through it. `S05 / P0055-P0061`

This creates an important simulation rule:

> **social performance can be mutually legible and still authentic.**

A character may consciously choose angle, voice, clothing, naming, or affect without thereby lying about the attachment being expressed.

The story also adds ordinary material evidence: cooking, weekend family work, household aesthetics, food, teasing, and plans for the next shared meal. Their relationship exists outside the audition wound that first made Kanade analytically visible.

Kanade’s V08 crisis remains real. It simply no longer deserves monopoly over the character model.

## 9. S06 — Serina and Midori: labels, praise, and the ethics of saying what one sees

Serina begins from active resistance to relational naming. She dislikes pity and distrusts labels such as “connection” or “friend,” even while her internal reactions to Azusa reveal attraction she cannot comfortably disown. `HIBIKE-V10 / S06 / P0001-P0010; P0040`

Midori does not solve this by forcing a label. She asks Serina to communicate an observation.

When Serina presses the hierarchy question—Kitauji or Rikka, which is better?—Midori resists turning all musical value into one rank. She can acknowledge contest indicators while insisting that listener, performance, and purpose matter. `S06 / P0054-P0078` This is not relativistic nihilism. Midori can still confidently call both ensembles top class.

More important is her theory of praise. `好き` and `最高` should be spoken because motivation can die when recognition remains unvoiced. `S06 / P0070-P0087`

This is the positive mirror of V08’s recognition-before-correction rule. Recognition is not only something to provide after crisis. It can be **preventive motivational infrastructure**.

Serina eventually finds the word `友達` less unpleasant when applied to Azusa. `S06 / P0098-P0101` The label follows behavior more safely than behavior being coerced to fit the label.

## 10. S07 — Haruka: a relationship can be finite in density and still be real

「未来を見つめて」 places Haruka, Kaori, and Asuka after the offices and crises that once defined them.

The trip contains a surprising amount of ordinary Asuka: physical play, boredom, jokes, hot-spring relaxation, and impulsive amusement. This is valuable because Asuka’s theatricality is often overinterpreted as only strategic mask. Some performance is simply play.

Haruka worries about Yuuko’s suitability for leadership. Asuka gives a more structural answer. Yuuko has too much charisma, in Asuka’s view, to function safely as a subordinate; Natsuki’s special value is that she can “operate” or manage Yuuko. `HIBIKE-V10 / S07 / P0052-P0074`

This is Asuka’s interpretation [C], but V09 and S13 provide later corroboration. The best leadership pair is not necessarily two independently strongest people. It may be two people whose interaction keeps each person functional.

Haruka also becomes an external witness to Asuka/Kaori. She cannot comfortably categorize them as ordinary friends, while `親友` also feels insufficient. `S07 / P0088` The observation supports unusual relational density without giving the project permission to invent a formal status.

Haruka’s own attachment to Asuka is more bounded. She does not expect the relationship to become progressively denser after graduation. She cannot dedicate herself to Asuka as Kaori can. Yet she wants a physical object that preserves the period in which they were friends and officers together. `S07 / P0121-P0123; P0139-P0175`

This is a crucial corrective to relationship models that measure “truth” by maximal permanence.

> **A relationship can be temporally bounded, role-conditioned, and still genuine.**

## 11. S08 — Chihiro and Taki before bereavement

「郷愁の夢」 is not valuable merely because it foreshadows tragedy. Its main function is to restore Chihiro from “dead wife who explains Taki” into a living person with her own voice, judgments, friendships, and humor.

Satomi’s focalization shows intense attachment to Chihiro, including jealousy of Taki. That intensity should not automatically be converted into a formal romantic category.

Chihiro offers an explicit plural theory of `好き`: affection can take family, friendship, romantic, and other forms, and the wish to remain together can matter without forcing one vocabulary. `HIBIKE-V10 / S08 / P0049-P0056`

That statement should govern later taxonomy. The series itself supplies a warning against making one word do more ontological work than the people using it.

Chihiro also provides high-value evidence on Taki. She says she likes all of him, finds him smart and kind, and worries because he is a person who cannot voice weakness when alone. She believes his apparently perfect strength is partly skillful presentation. `S08 / P0061-P0067`

This is [C], not direct Taki interiority. But it comes from an intimate partner in the pre-bereavement state and therefore has high modeling weight.

The longitudinal correction is important:

> **Taki’s controlled adult register is not evidence that he lacks vulnerability; it may be one of the ways he manages vulnerability.**

That distinction becomes particularly useful when later Taki admits fear about imposing his own goal or when he delegates evaluation rather than performing omniscient authority.

## 12. S09 — Mirei: voluntary accommodation without losing boundaries

「ツインテール推進計画」 is almost aggressively low-stakes. That is precisely why it matters.

The low-brass group plays with hairstyles. Reina teases Kumiko; Ririka recruits others into the joke; Takuya tells Riko she is cute regardless of styling. `HIBIKE-V10 / S09 / P0025-P0049`

Mirei’s case is more subtle. She does not like being touched. `S09 / P0062` Satsuki nevertheless styles her hair and uses a clover accessory. Mirei considers undoing it, then notices Satsuki’s disappointment and decides to keep it. `S09 / P0062-P0074`

V1 was right to read this as post-conflict trust, but “bodies without coercion” is too clean. There is social pressure. The important point is that Mirei can now **choose accommodation without interpreting accommodation as defeat**.

Her touch boundary remains. Her personality has not been rewritten into Satsuki’s. The relationship has become secure enough to permit small voluntary concessions.

This is the ordinary-life version of differentiated belonging.

## 13. S10 — Natsuki: unilluminated value, burnout, and the fear of adulthood

「真昼のイルミネーション」 contains one of the anthology’s strongest adulthood motifs.

Unlit illumination hardware looks ugly in daylight. Natsuki uses that sight to think about how people often value something only after it shines. Yuuko, in Natsuki’s view, is unusually good at seeing value before the lights turn on. `HIBIKE-V10 / S10 / P0028-P0035`

The metaphor later becomes darker. Natsuki worries that adults or outsiders can over-light, commercialize, optimize, or reinterpret something until they destroy what its original lovers valued. Her deeper fear is that **she herself** may become that adult—someone who steps on another person’s `好き` without even noticing. `S10 / P0112-P0123`

That is not generic career anxiety. It is a moral anxiety about perception.

The story also refuses to turn retirement into clean aspirational momentum. Natsuki feels empty. She does not immediately want to continue euphonium because Kitauji’s Kansai performance may feel like a peak she cannot recreate. `S10 / P0052-P0098`

Nozomi supplies a counter-model: instruments do not have to be lived only at elite school-band intensity. Adults play casually. People stop and restart. Continuation can change form.

Nozomi also admits her own uncertainty. Her optimism is not prophecy; it is a chosen form of care. She tells Natsuki she is a good person without being able to prove the future. Unsupported confidence can still give another person room to move.

This is a useful correction to over-rationalized dialogue simulation. Sometimes a character helps by saying something she cannot logically certify.

## 14. S11 — Riko and Takuya: separation without passivity

「木綿のハンカチ」 gives the corpus its clearest quiet long-distance relationship model.

Takuya leaves Kyoto for a specialist school in Tokyo to train as an instrument repair technician. This itself expands the musical ecology: a serious future in music need not mean professional performance. Repair is part of the infrastructure that makes every later performance possible.

The relationship is already integrated into family. Takuya’s mother privately thanks Riko for loving him and treats Riko’s continuing presence as something precious. `HIBIKE-V10 / S11 / P0032-P0041`

Takuya’s speech remains characteristically plain. In the confession flashback, ornament is minimal; even the difficult word `好き` comes out through eye avoidance rather than rhetorical flourish. `S11 / P0052-P0064`

At departure he says he cannot imagine someone other than Riko and asks her to wait. Riko’s answer is the more important modeling event:

`待てなくなったら、うちから会いに行くから`. `S11 / P0065-P0079`

She does not promise passive stillness. She converts waiting into the right to move.

> **stable attachment is not dependence on unchanged proximity.**

The dyad is useful as a contrast with Kumiko/Shuuichi, but V2 should not use it as a moral scoreboard. Different relationships solve distance, ambition, and duty with different mechanisms.

# Part II — 「アンサンブルコンテスト」

## 15. S12 as the volume’s institutional laboratory

The long S12 novella changes scale. The anthology has spent eleven stories showing what large public narratives omit. Now it applies the same principle to the band itself.

A seventy-one-member organization is difficult to perceive at individual resolution. Full ensemble rehearsals place many people behind section sound, conductor scaffolding, and established social reputation. Chamber ensembles strip those supports away.

The Ensemble Contest project therefore functions simultaneously as:

- performance preparation;
- hidden-skill detection;
- pedagogy;
- social-network exposure;
- leadership training;
- repertoire authorship;
- evaluation training;
- and succession infrastructure.

Taki explicitly wants students to form groups, choose music, and become judges. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P0090-P0108` He wants them to learn how difficult “objective” evaluation actually is and not confuse “I like this” with “this best satisfies the criterion I am judging.”

That is the institutional analogue of V10’s focalization method.

> **Different observation systems reveal different truths.**

---

## 16. Kumiko’s first presidential design problem: inclusion without labor concentration

Three weeks into the presidency, Kumiko is already dealing with a practical problem that did not exist in the same form under earlier leaders: how to make an internal ensemble system both serious and broadly participatory.

She refuses to let a student perform in multiple groups even though the internal event could technically allow it. The reason is not legalism. Doubling would concentrate burden and encourage divided commitment. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P0030-P0034`

This is a direct application of the V08–V09 anti-overfunctioning lesson. The institution should not solve every shortage by loading the most capable person more heavily.

At the same time, group formation inevitably creates leftovers. Some instruments are scarce; some repertoire requires specific combinations; some strong players receive multiple invitations; some socially timid students fear being compared with stars. `S12 / P0148-P0214; P0261-P0287; P0518-P0565`

Kumiko initially plans to wait and join whichever group needs her. `S12 / P0057-P0063` The instinct is prosocial and structurally useful—but also diagnostically familiar. The person who always fills the remaining hole does not have to state whom she wants.

V2 should preserve the risk without pretending the counterfactual is already proven. Kumiko does not in fact erase herself from the project. Reina interrupts that trajectory.

## 17. Team Oumae and plural evaluation: do not average distinct criteria into mush

The most important governance debate concerns voting.

Shuuichi wants retired third-years included because distance from current rivalry may produce a more objective view. Reina objects that retirees may still be biased by affection and that current students should own the official result. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P0112-P0120`

Both arguments are legitimate because they optimize different epistemic conditions.

Kumiko’s proposal is not a compromise in the weak sense of splitting the difference. She creates **two different votes with different jobs**:

1. active members choose the ensemble that will represent Kitauji;
2. the general audience separately recognizes the performance it most values. `S12 / P0123-P0143`

Taki immediately tests the failure mode: what if the outcomes diverge and the audience favorite feels robbed? Shuuichi argues that the divergence is itself educational. Reina comes to see that a parallel audience result can give real recognition to ensembles that do not fit official entry conditions.

This is an especially mature form of legitimacy because it refuses to force values into one scalar score.

> **When criteria answer different questions, preserve the criteria and expose the difference. Do not manufacture false consensus by averaging them.**

The later result vindicates the architecture without proving one judgment “true” and the other false. The clarinet quartet wins the active-member selection and later advances to Kansai. Kumiko’s *Ibuki* group wins the general-audience vote. `S12 / P1095-P1115`

Kumiko herself voted for the clarinet quartet. She can desire her own group’s success, enjoy the audience response, and still judge another ensemble more suitable to the contest.

This is a major development from V01’s simple merit dispute. Evaluation has become **purpose-explicit**.

## 18. Speech acts and leadership: Midori corrects Kumiko’s `ごめん`

One of S12’s smallest interactions should become a durable leadership-language rule.

Kumiko’s friends wait because her presidential work makes her late. She apologizes. Midori objects. The delay is not a selfish imposition; Kumiko is performing work for the band. If she wants to acknowledge the others for waiting, `ありがとう` is better than `ごめん`, and a leader’s apology can make juniors feel as though their own patience has burdened the leader. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P0288-P0306`

This does not mean leaders should never apologize. It means that **apology, thanks, request, and command distribute obligation differently**.

Kumiko later confronts the inverse problem when a junior calls her kind for solving a group-formation issue. Internally, she knows part of her motive is administrative: unresolved conflict creates later work. Being praised as pure kindness feels uncomfortable. `S12 / P0521-P0565`

The clean model is:

> **mixed motive does not invalidate benevolent action, but a leader should not confuse effective social design with moral purity.**

This is important for Kumiko, whose self-image is unusually sensitive to whether she is “really” being nice.

## 19. Reina’s invitation: selection, rejection fear, and the demand to be first

The *Ibuki* ensemble provides some of the strongest direct Kumiko/Reina evidence in the prose corpus.

The first correction is factual: Yume, not Reina, originally proposes playing the piece. Yume approaches Reina because she has wanted to perform it since middle school and now chooses to initiate the opportunity herself. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P0575-P0588`

Reina then recruits the rest. Her criterion is not merely “collect the statistically best players.” She says she seeks people with whom she can see the musical vision. `S12 / P0589-P0590`

Kumiko asks the question she previously might have concealed: did Reina invite her only because Kanade refused?

Reina says no. Kanade was never asked for the euphonium slot. She wanted Kumiko. `S12 / P0591-P0607`

Kumiko then makes the underlying relational demand explicit:

`いちばんがいいでしょ。麗奈に選ばれるなら`. `S12 / P0592-P0600`

She wants to be first when Reina chooses.

Reina’s answering vulnerability is equally direct. She delayed asking because Kumiko had said she might fill other groups, and `断られたら、嫌やん`. `S12 / P0603-P0610`

Reina’s musical confidence therefore coexists with fear of interpersonal rejection. Kumiko leans into her, says she wanted to play together too, and thanks her for asking. `S12 / P0611-P0616`

The scene should not be reduced to one taxonomy. It simultaneously contains:

- musical preference;
- relational priority;
- embarrassment;
- bodily ease;
- fear of rejection;
- and mutual selection.

Immediately afterward, Kumiko imagines a future better euphonist entering Kitauji. If Reina chooses on musical grounds, what happens to Kumiko’s specialness? `S12 / P0621-P0632`

That anxiety proves why V2 must keep two variables separate even when Kumiko does not:

> **musical specialness ≠ total relational specialness.**

Kumiko’s response is also revealing. She imagines peeling away a weak version of herself and discarding it. This is self-regulation through suppression, not proof the insecurity has been resolved.

## 20. Shuuichi after the breakup: functional proximity does not erase attachment

S12 quietly places Kumiko, Shuuichi, and Reina in the same working system after Kumiko’s role-based breakup.

Kumiko and Shuuichi continue familiar president/vice-president cooperation. He reassures her after public meetings and contributes practical judgment to the voting debate. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P0047-P0054; P0112-P0143`

Yet Kumiko’s emotional state is not neatly compartmentalized. She notices Hazuki looking at Shuuichi and imagines the possibility that the two might date. The thought produces jealousy even though Kumiko herself initiated the separation. `S12 / P0507-P0517`

The point is not that the breakup is “fake.” The status change is real. The emotional attachment also remains real.

This directly validates V09’s self-application-gap warning. Kumiko can advise others not to let labels flatten feeling, then attempt to simplify her own relational situation through a formal boundary that does not erase the underlying state.

## 21. Tsubame: why a scalar model of ability fails

Tsubame is the most important new simulation-grade case in HIBIKE-V10.

From the outside, she is easy to classify as weak. She has been a B member. She struggles with marching. She cannot coordinate independent drum-limb patterns well. Reina’s group rehearsals expose repeated timing problems. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P0813-P0831`

Then Junna asks Kumiko to hear Tsubame play marimba under a fixed metronome. The result is startlingly good. Junna says Tsubame may be the best marimba player in the band. `S12 / P0873-P0896`

The apparent contradiction disappears once ability is represented as a vector rather than a rank.

Tsubame is excellent at:

- fixed-tempo mallet execution;
- four-mallet control;
- accurate placement;
- and marimba sonority.

She struggles with:

- bodily independence on drum kit;
- flexible interpersonal timing;
- anticipating un-conducted entrances;
- and reading pre-sound cues.

She herself has never understood how an ensemble enters together without a conductor. She treats the first note almost like a surprise reaction test. `S12 / P0919-P0925`

Kumiko notices the missing mechanism. Tsubame does not need air to produce a marimba note. When concentrating, she can stop breathing. She also fixes her eyes on the keys and waits for audible sound from other players. Wind players, by contrast, coordinate through a movement **before** the note: eye contact, inhalation, posture, and the visible preparation of attack. `S12 / P0901-P0931`

Kumiko asks Tsubame to look and breathe with them.

The change is dramatic. Tsubame begins matching eye contact and imitating the breathing motion. Timing improves. `S12 / P0952-P0957`

This creates at least four durable principles.

### 21.1 Sound can be coordinated before sound exists

Hearing is not the only musical information channel. Bodies announce intention.

### 21.2 Instrument families create different perceptual habits

A percussionist can become technically strong without naturally internalizing a wind player’s respiratory grammar.

### 21.3 Full ensemble scaffolding can hide a local deficit

With conductor cues, Tsubame’s problem is less visible. Small ensemble removes the scaffold and makes the causal mechanism observable.

### 21.4 Self-evaluation is distorted by ease

Junna had earlier told Tsubame that what feels easy to her may be difficult for others and vice versa. Tsubame was ready to conclude she did not belong in percussion because her failures were salient and her marimba gift felt ordinary from inside. `S12 / P0934-P0944`

This is not merely Tsubame characterization. It is a reconstruction rule:

> **Do not infer global competence from A/B status, one audition, one instrument task, or one failure domain.**

## 22. Recognition changes Tsubame’s ambition

Tsubame’s arc also connects technical diagnosis to desire.

After learning to coordinate with the ensemble, she asks Kumiko whether A-stage performance is frightening. Then she says that she has always assumed being B was natural because she is bad—but wonders whether she is allowed to want A like Junna. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P1027-P1039`

Kumiko does not promise selection. She answers the more basic question: nobody gets to say Tsubame is forbidden from wanting it.

This closely resembles the series’ older desire architecture, but the mechanism is more mature:

> **accurate recognition of specific capacity → causal correction of specific deficit → increased self-efficacy → permission to own a higher ambition.**

The series is still about wanting. V10 shows how institutions can make wanting psychologically credible by improving the person’s model of her own capability.

## 23. Hazuki: responsive care can become musical lateness

Hazuki’s ensemble work produces another elegant personality-to-technique bridge.

She worries that she is holding the group back and wants Reina and Kumiko to feel that inviting her was worthwhile. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P0847-P0851`

Kumiko diagnoses a specific problem. Hazuki listens too completely—through the very edge of another player’s sound—before acting, and fails to account for the lag between deciding to play, breathing, engaging the instrument, and actual sound production. `S12 / P0852-P0863`

The social analogy is strong but should remain [D/E]: Hazuki’s gift is responsiveness, yet responsiveness can become hesitation when support requires stable anticipation.

Mirei’s contribution is equally important. She has been giving Hazuki precise basic-practice advice, and later directly notes Hazuki’s improvement. `S12 / P0961-P0975`

V08 Mirei once feared that competence was invisible. V10 shows her competence becoming **transmissive**. She can now use what she knows to alter another player’s development rather than merely defend her own standing.

## 24. Midori and Motomu: participation outside official eligibility

Midori and Motomu choose a contrabass duet even though a two-person group cannot become Kitauji’s official ensemble-contest representative under the rules. Midori’s reasons include direct teaching value for Motomu and the pleasure of playing together. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P0527-P0543`

Their performance later receives strong audience approval. `S12 / P1043-P1048`

This is an important institutional design result. The internal event is not built so that only official-eligibility value matters. Taki even gives coaching to ineligible configurations because the educational object is the act of serious ensemble making, not only tournament advancement. `S12 / P1001-P1004`

The series’ competitive ethic therefore remains non-totalizing:

> **a serious institution can maintain competitive standards while creating legitimate spaces whose value is not advancement.**

## 25. Mizore refuses Nozomi: V09 differentiation passes a held-out test

One of V10’s cleanest longitudinal stress tests occurs almost casually.

Retired Nozomi suggests that she, Yuuko, Natsuki, and perhaps Mizore could join stranded current students in an internal ensemble. Yuuko immediately worries that Mizore will accept merely because Nozomi asks. Nozomi says Mizore can now decide for herself. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P0670-P0708`

Kumiko asks Mizore directly and gives her an explicit no-pressure exit.

Mizore’s answer is immediate and firm: `私は出ない`. `S12 / P0712-P0725`

She is preparing for music-school examinations and does not want the added project. She is glad to have been invited and says she will thank Nozomi herself. `S12 / P0726-P0739`

This is not a rejection of Nozomi. It is evidence that Nozomi’s involvement no longer automatically determines Mizore’s choice.

V09’s “differentiated attachment” model therefore survives a held-out scenario.

## 26. Retired seniors: continuity without sovereignty

The same sequence refines institutional succession.

Yuuko, Nozomi, and Natsuki remain available. They coach younger groups. They can perform in the internal concert. Their expertise does not vanish merely because office and contest eligibility end.

But Yuuko insists on process:

- current students should ask the stranded students themselves;
- retired seniors should not pressure participants;
- Mizore should be free to decline;
- and the current president retains final decision authority. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P0634-P0788`

That is a mature form of institutional memory.

> **the old generation remains a resource without remaining sovereign.**

This closes the loop from V03’s concern about charismatic indispensability. The institution can retain people’s knowledge while letting the office move on.

## 27. The concert results: one performance can be first and fourth without contradiction

The internal event produces a beautifully designed empirical test of the voting theory.

The active students select the clarinet quartet. Kumiko’s group ranks fourth in that vote. The general audience selects Kumiko’s group first. Kanade’s group ranks second in both; Midori/Motomu place third with the audience. The clarinet quartet later advances to Kansai. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P1095-P1115`

The wrong lesson would be “audiences are emotional and musicians are objective.” The text is more disciplined.

Different groups answer different evaluative questions:

- Which performance is most immediately enjoyable or memorable to this mixed audience?
- Which ensemble is most likely to satisfy contest criteria and advance?
- Which group exhibits broad technical depth?
- Which group has developed most relative to its starting point?
- Which performance best serves its own participants’ purpose?

No single answer exhausts musical value.

Kanade’s double-second finish is particularly tempting to over-symbolize. It is factual that her group is broadly excellent without being first. But V2 downgrades V1’s stronger authorial-design claim that the result was “perfectly designed” as a named lesson for Kanade. The text supports the circumstance; it does not hand us the author’s private optimization objective.

## 28. Immediate musical pleasure is not dereliction of institutional duty

Kanade asks Kumiko which ensemble will win. Kumiko admits she is enjoying the event itself and wonders whether that indicates insufficient presidential seriousness. Kanade replies that concentrating on the music in front of them is normal. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P0985-P1004`

That exchange protects the institution from a subtle failure mode.

Once everything becomes “preparation for next summer,” present acts risk becoming purely instrumental. The band would then reproduce the same totalization that Natsuki fears in S10: value visible only after it is converted into external light.

A high-performance culture therefore needs both:

- longitudinal planning;
- and the ability to experience current music as an end rather than only a means.

## 29. Breath as musical mechanism and leadership metaphor

V1 correctly identified breath as the novella’s central metaphor. V2 preserves it with an epistemic label.

Musically, the breath mechanism is direct fact. Wind players coordinate attack through inhalation and bodily preparation; Tsubame has not been reading that signal. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P0901-P0959`

Institutionally, the analogy is [D/E]. Kumiko’s distinctive leadership increasingly consists of perceiving the condition that comes **before** visible failure:

- what value a person thinks is unrecognized;
- what audience she imagines;
- what burden is being silently concentrated;
- what cue a musician does not know exists;
- what social consequence makes a fair procedure unbelievable.

She is not simply better at telling people what result to produce. She is often better at identifying **why the other person cannot yet enter the shared action**.

That is the mature form of the Oumae Consultation Office.

# Part III — cross-volume character reconstruction

## 30. Kumiko @ V10: architectural leadership with persistent self-application gaps

HIBIKE-V10 materially strengthens `KUMIKO@V10`.

### Stable gains

**Relational diagnosis → causal mechanism.** With Tsubame, Kumiko does not stop at “timing is off.” She tests what the player is looking at, how she breathes, and what signal she expects. `S12 / P0869-P0959`

**Plural-value institutional design.** The two-vote system gives competing goods distinct channels rather than pretending one metric can answer both. `S12 / P0112-P0143`

**Bounded inclusion work.** Kumiko tries to ensure nobody is stranded, but accepts participant consent and official eligibility differences rather than forcing false equality.

**Improved desire speech.** She can directly tell Reina she wants to be first when Reina chooses. `S12 / P0592-P0600`

### Persistent failure modes

**Over-consideration.** Hazuki names it directly in S02. `S02 / P0019-P0024`

**Self-subordination.** Her first ensemble instinct is to fill whatever gap remains. `S12 / P0057-P0063`

**Mixed-motive discomfort.** She dislikes being called kind when she recognizes administrative self-interest in preventing future conflict. `S12 / P0521-P0565`

**Specialness anxiety.** She imagines a better euphonist displacing her in Reina’s musical choice and attempts to discipline the fear away. `S12 / P0621-P0632`

**Post-breakup jealousy.** Formal suspension of romance has not removed attachment to Shuuichi. `S12 / P0507-P0517`

> **Kumiko is increasingly good at creating conditions in which other people can choose and perform honestly; she remains less reliable at granting her own vulnerable states the same recognition-before-correction she gives others.**

## 36. Natsuki and Yuuko @ V10: co-regulation revealed from inside

## 42. Music and pedagogy: from conductor-centered correction to peer perceptual translation

S12 advances the series’ pedagogy model in three ways.

### 42.1 Chamber music removes cover

Large ensemble can hide local deficits. Chamber playing exposes timing, confidence, balance, and role understanding.

### 42.2 Better performer ≠ better teacher in every domain

Reina hears defects rapidly. Kumiko more readily finds the learner-specific bridge in Tsubame’s case. Junna knows Tsubame’s long-term skill profile. Mirei can give precise basic-practice feedback. Different forms of expertise are distributed.

### 42.3 The student must eventually perceive what the teacher perceives

Tsubame’s improvement is not a command-following trick. She learns a new information channel: gaze and inhalation. Hazuki learns to feel the delay between intention and sounded tuba. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P0847-P0863; P0869-P0957`

The strongest pedagogy sequence is:

> **observe output → isolate mechanism → make hidden signal perceptible → rehearse embodied correction → transfer self-monitoring to learner**

That is substantially more simulation-useful than “character X is a good/bad teacher.”

## 43. Material and socioeconomic world

V10 contains useful non-symbolic infrastructure that prevents the band from becoming an abstract social system.

S12 notes that school-owned instruments can be old but maintained, that replacement is constrained by budget, and that expensive percussion instruments may exceed ordinary school purchasing capacity. `HIBIKE-V10 / S12 / P0319-P0338; P1007-P1020`

The marimba weighs more than a hundred kilograms and costs well over a million yen; simply moving it down stairs requires coordinated labor. `S12 / P1007-P1020`

These details matter because “performance” rests on maintenance, storage, transport, adult funding, repair, schedules, and physical carrying work.

Takuya’s instrument-repair career in S11 extends the same insight beyond school. Musical culture contains technicians whose contribution is neither audience-visible nor competitive.

This supports the archive’s broader distinction between **visibility, functional necessity, replaceability, and relational wantedness**.

The anthology repeatedly answers through partial carriers rather than exhaustive confession.

## 49. V10 closing synthesis

HIBIKE-V10 changes the V2 project more by **calibration** than by overturning the second-year checkpoint.

V08–V09 asked whether Kitauji could reproduce legitimate excellence while respecting differentiated people. V10 asks whether our analytical model can do the same thing: can it preserve recognizable characters without forcing every future behavior to reproduce the crisis that first made them legible?

The answer is yes, if the model becomes conditional enough.

Nozomi can be jealous and gentle.

Kanade can be calculated and sincere.

Mirei can dislike touch and choose affection.

Aoi can quit and later play again.

Natsuki can burn out and leave open the possibility of return.

Yuuko can be a strong president because another person gives her permission not to be president every second.

Reina can be exacting enough to lead rehearsal and frightened enough to delay asking Kumiko to join it.

Kumiko can design a sophisticated plural evaluation system while still wanting, almost childishly, to be Reina’s first choice.

Tsubame can be weak under one metric and exceptional under another because the metric was never the person.

That last point is the book’s deepest convergence of character and music.

> **A large ensemble can hide the truth of one player. A large narrative can hide the truth of one person. Change the scale, change the perspective, and different capacities become audible.**

The “true story” is therefore not what remains after all performances are stripped away. Performance, styling, leadership, letters, jokes, and music are themselves real behaviors. Truth becomes more reconstructible when we know **under what conditions each behavior appears, what it protects, what it enables, and what changes when the conditions change.**

That is the V10 contribution the final-movement volumes must inherit.
