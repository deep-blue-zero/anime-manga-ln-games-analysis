## 2. Executive local thesis: authorship after inherited success

The V1 reading correctly saw HIBIKE-V07 as a post-competition volume in which the governing verb shifts from **being selected** toward **selecting**. Clean-text V2 keeps that insight but revises its mechanism.

The regular concert demonstrates almost immediately that **having preferences is not yet authorship**. Kumiko and Mizore try to respect everyone by ranking requested pieces according to majority vote. The result is musically incoherent: transitions are poor, difficulty is badly distributed, and the program lacks shape. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0293-P0296`

Asuka then supplies one possible philosophy of authority: other people's opinions matter, but merely skimming their surface produces something half-formed; the people entrusted with selection must decide what kind of event they want and what they refuse to surrender. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0354-P0367` Haruka offers a gentler version from her own history: excessive consideration can make a leader disappear from the decision, but she can now say of herself, `でも、いまは違うから`. `S01 / P0462-P0477`

Yet the novella does **not** culminate in Mizore becoming a miniature Asuka who simply imposes private taste. Her final program is relationally distributed. It includes her own preference, multiple Nozomi preferences, Kumiko's request, music that transforms Hazuki's erased wish into a stage-viable form, Yuuko and Natsuki's competing choices, student/instrument features, serious concert repertoire, and accessible entertainment. What makes that plurality authored rather than aggregated is the frame Mizore herself supplies: `……楽しい、こと`; she wants children, adults, and everyone who came to hear Kitauji to think the concert was enjoyable. `S01 / P0482-P0554`

The stronger V2 formulation is therefore:

> **Artistic authorship is bounded curatorial authority: a person or group takes responsibility for form while remaining answerable to performers, listeners, inherited memory, and the material demands of music.**

The second novella moves the same problem inside Kumiko. She has already learned to say that *she* wants Asuka back. Now she must confront a desire she had rendered illegitimate before it could become a goal: she wants to stand beside Reina **as a musical equal**.

Azusa is the disconfirming case that breaks Kumiko's old metaphysics of “specialness.” Kumiko had treated Reina's excellence as categorical—something almost species-like that explained why equal standing was not a reasonable desire for herself. When Azusa plays with Reina at a level Kumiko experiences as equal, jealousy reveals not betrayal but a suppressed ambition. The narration makes this explicit: the shock is that Azusa can stand beside Reina, while Kumiko had used Reina's specialness to make that possibility unavailable to herself. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0222-P0286`

The rest of the novella then **decomposes aspiration into trainable requirements**. Kumiko says `吹きたいです`; Asuka diagnoses a concrete performance mechanism—stress tightens Kumiko's lips, tangles the ring finger, leaves the third piston incompletely depressed, hardens the tone, and causes rushing; Kumiko practices under direct feedback; Taki later assigns the soli to Reina and Kumiko. `S02 / P0326-P0339`; `S02 / P0470-P0512`

The key longitudinal move is thus not merely:

> ordinary Kumiko → special Kumiko.

It is:

> **unlicensed desire → jealousy as diagnostic information → first-person claim → embodied technical work → prepared eligibility → selected co-presence.**

HIBIKE-V07 consequently separates two meanings of “specialness” that earlier Kumiko often fused:

1. **musical specialness** is produced, contestable, embodied, and at least partly trainable;
2. **relational specialness** is selective, affectively possessive, and not reducible to rank.

The illumination scene closes the volume by making the second category extraordinarily strong without cancelling the first or eliminating Kumiko's simultaneous relationship with Shuuichi. Reina knows Kumiko may later return with him and still wants this first experience with Kumiko: `久美子の初めては、アタシのやから`. They remain hand in hand, and Kumiko thinks that simply being together is happiness. `S02 / P0599-P0641`

HIBIKE-V07 is therefore best understood as a volume about **authorship, equality, and selective belonging after success has already been inherited**.

## 3. Structural architecture: winter rhapsody and star-colored serenade

The paired novellas operate at different scales.

### 3.1 S01: an institution must author itself after the crisis is over

The first trilogy gave Kitauji an obvious external objective: advance through competition. HIBIKE-V07 asks what happens after the intense first-year crisis no longer supplies every decision.

The older third-years are retired. Yuuko and Natsuki hold formal authority. Mizore receives responsibility for the regular concert. Kumiko has been named a future new-student instructor but has no freshmen yet, so she is drafted as Mizore's assistant. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0008-P0035`

This is a deliberate redistribution of narrative position. The first-year Kumiko entered structures built by seniors. Here she helps build the environment other students will perform inside.

The regular concert therefore tests whether Kitauji can move from **charisma-dependent recovery** toward **self-reproduction**.

### 3.2 S02: a person must author desire inside inherited relationships

The joint concert begins after the successful regular concert and reintroduces multiple older and external standards at once:

- Rikka/Tachibana as an already mature elite institution;
- retired Kitauji seniors;
- Azusa as an old friend whose growth occurred elsewhere;
- Reina as Kumiko's most intense musical/relational standard;
- Shuuichi as Kumiko's newly acknowledged boyfriend;
- Asuka as former section authority and personal model.

The novella's central musical problem—the opening `ノアの方舟` soli—compresses those relationships into a single question: **who is Kumiko trying to become capable of answering?**

### 3.3 The titles as interpretive, not factual, structure

V1 read “rhapsody” as plurality assembled into form and “serenade” as music addressed toward someone. That remains a useful **[D/E] pattern**, but V2 downgrades any implication that the titles provide an authorially fixed allegorical code. The textual architecture supports the association; the source does not provide a narrator's glossary equating each title to the analysis.

This is exactly the kind of claim where a strong literary reading should be retained **with its epistemic label visible**.

## 4. Post-nationals Kitauji: reputation becomes an inherited obligation

Nationals do not end institutional pressure. They change its form.

Taki explains that a regular concert is distinctive because the audience comes specifically to hear Kitauji. The band is no longer playing primarily before adjudicators or incidental public crowds; listeners have chosen the ensemble itself. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0094-P0102`

Kumiko immediately understands the reputational consequence. Kitauji's competitive result has changed what outsiders expect. The previous cohort's success becomes a condition the next regime inherits.

This is a useful institutional distinction:

- **competitive legitimacy** asks whether Kitauji can rank highly against others;
- **reputational legitimacy** asks whether the ensemble can continue to deserve the audience it has attracted.

The latter cannot be answered by repeating the competition program unchanged. A regular concert demands programming, pacing, presentation, audience modeling, promotion, staging, and student ownership.

The recruitment sequence makes the feedback loop explicit. Former middle-school teacher Fujishiro notes that many students now want Kitauji because it reached nationals and tells Kumiko she is glad Kumiko continued band. Kumiko can now answer that she too is glad she continued, without rewriting all of middle school as good. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0841-P0898`

Institutional success has thus begun to reproduce itself socially:

> effort → public result → reputation → audience/recruitment → stronger expectations → future institutional responsibility.

V2 should retain this as a material mechanism, not merely a metaphor for “legacy.” Reputation changes who applies, who attends, what the public expects, and what student leaders must organize.

## 5. Preference aggregation is not artistic authorship

HIBIKE-V07 supplies one of the corpus's clearest critiques of an overly simple democratic model of collective art.

### 5.1 Everyone's wish is real

The survey matters precisely because requests are not interchangeable data points. Riko's choice is linked to Takuya and the memory of hearing him play. Hazuki's erased wish is bound to her first low-brass ensemble experience. Other students choose pieces because of instrument identity, prior performances, favorite genres, or relationships. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0105-P0163`; `S01 / P0307-P0322`

A vote count strips those meanings down to frequency.

### 5.2 Majority rule produces a bad program

Kumiko and Mizore initially rank requested pieces by votes because it seems procedurally fair. The resulting order is plainly dysfunctional: flow and balance are poor, and difficult works cluster in impractical ways. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0293-P0338`

The lesson is not “democracy is bad.” It is narrower:

> **Preference aggregation answers which items many people want. It does not by itself answer how heterogeneous material becomes a coherent artistic event.**

That second problem requires judgment.

### 5.3 Asuka's answer: entrusted authority must actually decide

Asuka's advice is unusually explicit:

- cramming everyone's wishes together will not automatically create something good;
- merely taking the surface of other people's views creates a half-finished result;
- Mizore and Kumiko must ask what kind of performance they want and what they will not surrender;
- since they have been given authority, they can decide and the ensemble will follow. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0354-P0367`

This is strong evidence for Asuka's governing temperament: she treats unclear authority as a design failure and prefers identifiable decision ownership.

But it remains **[C] Asuka's normative philosophy**, not omniscient narration. V2 should not silently universalize it into “the novel proves leaders should impose their will.”

### 5.4 Haruka's answer: consideration without self-erasure

Haruka approaches the same issue through autobiographical failure. She used to attend so closely to others' expectations that her own judgment disappeared; she can now say that state no longer defines her. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0439-P0477`

This strengthens V1's reading of her arc but sharpens the ethical distinction. The problem is not caring what others want. It is **outsourcing authorship to anticipated reactions**.

### 5.5 Mizore's answer is different from Asuka's

Mizore then produces a program by “独断と偏見,” but its actual content is strikingly relational. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0482-P0554`

She chooses:

- something she herself wants;
- several pieces Nozomi requested;
- Kumiko's `ディスコ・キッド`;
- a transformed route for Hazuki's hidden `きらきら星` wish;
- music connected to both Yuuko and Natsuki;
- feature pieces organized by grade/instrument;
- known film music;
- more demanding concert repertoire;
- material that supports visual staging.

The unifying criterion is not “my favorites.” It is the audience-facing sentence `……楽しい、こと`.

Mizore's model can therefore be described as **distributed curation**:

> she does not erase other people's desire; she assumes responsibility for arranging those desires under a frame she can name as her own.

That is more precise than V1's already strong formulation “Mizore becomes curator.”

## 6. Mizore: from protected attachment to relationally distributed authorship

V02 made Mizore's emotional architecture legible. Her technical correctness partly functioned as control against subjective judgment; Nozomi had become an overwhelming attachment anchor; and reunion did not magically equalize that relationship.

V07 does not replace that person with a confident extrovert. It shows **new regulation inside recognizably Mizore-like behavior**.

### 6.1 Low verbal output no longer implies low initiative

Mizore proactively proposes a listening/viewing session, brings catalogs, retrieves prior-concert video, and prepares survey infrastructure. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0057-P0081`

Later, once a program exists, she proceeds with ordering music and logistics during the holiday. `S01 / P0556-P0558`

Simulation rule:

> do not infer passivity from compressed speech. Mizore can initiate concrete tasks decisively when she has a bounded object to work on.

### 6.2 She still hesitates before claiming personal preference

During the group meeting, Mizore incorporates Yuuko and Natsuki's competing suggestions but has not even filled in her own survey until directly asked. Only then does she name `日本おとぎ話ラプソディー`. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0232-P0292`

This preserves continuity: desire is more available than in V02, but first-person preference still does not automatically occupy the foreground.

### 6.3 Nozomi remains disproportionately important

The completed program explicitly includes multiple pieces because Nozomi wants them. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0517-P0523`

That matters because a simplistic “growth = independence from Nozomi” model would be false. Mizore's world has widened; Nozomi has not become unimportant.

The actual change is **pluralization**:

> Nozomi remains central without remaining the only meaningful listener, motive, or relational coordinate.

### 6.4 Mizore can now imagine unknown listeners

The decisive expansion is the theme itself. She wants children, adults, and the people who chose to attend to experience band as fun. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0538-P0545`

That is a genuine enlargement of relational imagination. V02's Mizore often organized music around technical control or one overwhelming attachment. V07's Mizore can author for people she does not personally know.

### 6.5 Play is part of the mature Mizore model

Once staging begins, Mizore unexpectedly volunteers to take the turtle role in `日本おとぎ話ラプソディー` and asks for a mirror ball. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0578-P0612`

This is not comic trivia to discard. It is high-value anti-overfitting evidence. A model trained only on V02 crisis scenes would predict “quiet, serious, emotionally dependent oboist” and miss that Mizore can have strong, specific, playful preferences once the social context is secure and task-bounded.

### 6.6 Receiving help becomes explicit

In S02, Mizore gives Kumiko the concert recording and says `私一人じゃ、無理だった`. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0168-P0193`

That statement is important after the Rikka checkpoint. V05–V06 established **receiving-care strategy** as its own character dimension. Mizore can now recognize that assistance does not invalidate authorship. The concert is hers in responsibility without being hers alone in causation.

## 7. Hazuki: from self-censored beginner to audibly necessary foundation

Hazuki receives one of the volume's most complete small-scale arcs.

### 7.1 The erased wish is pre-emptive status regulation

No authority rejects `きらきら星`. Hazuki erases it herself because a beginner-associated melody feels too childish or simple for a nationally recognized ensemble. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0105-P0163`

This is prestige functioning internally. The institution need not directly humiliate a beginner if the beginner has learned to disqualify her own memory in advance.

### 7.2 Good curation transforms rather than literally obeys the wish

Asuka notices the erased entry. Kaori identifies a musically viable solution, and `スター・パズル・マーチ` carries `きらきら星` material into a full concert work. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0410-P0467`; `S01 / P0559-P0577`

This is an excellent micro-example of the volume's authorship thesis:

> respecting a desire does not require reproducing its first literal form.

A curator can preserve the emotional truth while changing the artistic vehicle.

### 7.3 Hazuki's anxiety is relationally specific

V1 correctly emphasized her insecurity, but “performance anxiety” is too generic by itself. Hazuki can play alone and has experience in the B ensemble. The acute problem is that, in the small `トランペット吹きの休日` group, she fears her tuba will degrade Reina's much stronger sound. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0742-P0805`

Her anxiety is therefore:

> **ensemble responsibility under perceived hierarchy.**

She is not merely afraid of making a mistake. She is afraid that the weakest member will contaminate the strongest member's performance.

### 7.4 Reina's criticism is harsh because the role is structural

Reina points out Hazuki's unstable tempo and refuses the idea that apology is musically relevant. There is no percussion or conductor in the small ensemble, so the tuba is carrying the foundation. Reina also immediately criticizes Kumiko's fingering when Kumiko fails. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0633-P0664`

This matters for Reina modeling. Her bluntness here is **standards-triggered and role-sensitive**, not a targeted dislike of Hazuki.

### 7.5 Taki turns choice into accountable agency

When Hazuki struggles later, Taki offers the possibility of replacing her with Riko. Hazuki refuses. Taki does not answer with motivational reassurance. He makes the consequence explicit: since she chose to continue, she must take responsibility for the claim, and `この曲は、あなたが要です`. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0694-P0727`

The pedagogical sequence is:

> option → first-person commitment → accountability → structurally accurate recognition.

Hazuki is not protected from the standard; she is told why her contribution is necessary.

### 7.6 Recognition closes the loop

By rehearsal near the performance, Hazuki's tuba stabilizes the tempo strongly enough that the group follows her. Midori says she is proud to show Hazuki's improvement, and Reina—the most demanding critic in the immediate group—directly says `アタシも、上達したと思うよ`. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0900-P0969`

Because Reina's praise is sparse, it carries high evidentiary weight inside their local social system.

Hazuki's arc is consequently not “beginner becomes confident.” It is:

> **self-censored wish → exposed responsibility → fear of degrading others → accountable persistence → audible foundation → recognition by exacting peers.**

## 8. Yuuko and Natsuki: leadership as calibrated friction, not conflict elimination

V04 established that Yuuko/Natsuki conflict can function as a secure-return intimacy mode. V07 shows that the same dyadic friction can be institutionalized as governance.

### 8.1 Yuuko has become a real president

The opening itself presents Yuuko and Natsuki jointly assigning Kumiko to Mizore. Yuuko later runs meetings, manages the next event immediately after the regular concert, and speaks publicly to the band with clear institutional continuity. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0001-P0035`; `S02 / P0001-P0006`

Mizore explicitly says Yuuko is doing very well as president. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0398-P0408`

That matters because Yuuko's earlier intensity cannot be reduced to Kaori fandom. The same willingness to commit and confront now supports legitimate office.

### 8.2 Natsuki is not a passive “balance” personality

Natsuki counters Yuuko, redirects her, encourages juniors, and uses teasing or comic setup to move people into situations they might otherwise avoid. Her later “ghost” manipulation of Kumiko toward Asuka is ethically lighter than Asuka's own past strategic control but belongs to the same family of **indirect social engineering**. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0326-P0406`

V2 should not model vice-presidency as simply “calm person tempers fiery president.” Natsuki is capable of forceful intervention, rivalry, and deliberate nudging.

### 8.3 Conflict and play survive office

The regular-concert staging produces the comic Urashima/Otohime struggle rather than suddenly turning both leaders solemn. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0806-P0840`

This is important anti-overfitting evidence. Formal responsibility does not erase existing personality grammar.

### 8.4 Yuuko inherits Haruka without becoming Haruka

Before the concert, Yuuko tells the ensemble that last year's conflict helped produce the music they can make now; national gold remains the goal, but first they must show the audience in front of them that Kitauji is serious. Her raised fist and group response visually recall Haruka's earlier leadership. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0970-P1017`

The relevant institutional process is **differentiated reenactment**:

- Haruka's form persists;
- Yuuko reuses it;
- the emotional tone, history, and authority source are now Yuuko's own.

Institutional transmission does not require identity cloning.

## 9. Retired seniors: formal exit without relational disappearance

HIBIKE-V03 established that healthy institutions should reduce functional indispensability. HIBIKE-V07 shows what can remain once formal necessity has ended.

### 9.1 Haruka: continuity without professionalization

Haruka says she wants to continue saxophone as a hobby. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0970-P1017`

This is a valuable corrective to narratives that treat serious adolescent art as meaningful only if it becomes a profession. Continuation can mean altered intensity, different institutions, or private pleasure.

### 9.2 Asuka: authority survives as chosen mentorship, not office

Asuka no longer holds vice-presidential or section authority, but she remains a high-information mentor. In S01 she gives programming advice; in S02 she returns to the stage in a different role and later gives Kumiko technically exact one-on-one coaching. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0340-P0367`; `S02 / P0415-P0489`

Crucially, the social defense remains recognizable. When Kumiko says she is sincerely happy to share a stage again, Asuka deflects with jokes and slightly embarrassed physical affection rather than answering in matching emotional register. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0425-P0469`

V2 should therefore reject a simplistic “V03 healed Asuka into openness” trajectory. Her **availability** has changed more than her basic regulation style.

### 9.3 Kaori and Asuka remain strongly yuri-coded without explicit status

S01 and S02 repeatedly stage teasing physical/verbal intimacy. Kaori blushes when Asuka uses possessive or girlfriend-like language; Asuka touches, pets, and publicly jokes. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0410-P0467`; `S02 / P0487-P0489`

These are strong **[B/D] yuri-coded relational cues**. They do not establish a canonical dating relationship. The distinction matters because HIBIKE's relationship architecture repeatedly permits intensity that exceeds generic friendship without forcing a single legalistic label.

### 9.4 Functional absence makes structural change visible

Kumiko repeatedly experiences the post-graduation ensemble as familiar but altered. Former roles are being redistributed: Midori takes on Asuka-like knowledge work; Yuuko/Natsuki govern; Mizore curates; Kumiko moves toward instruction. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0007-P0049`

The institution persists precisely by allowing the old configuration to disappear.

## 10. Midori and the performative creation of tradition

One of V07's funniest scenes is also one of its most useful institutional observations.

Midori announces that Asuka entrusted her with the “tradition” of becoming the low-brass knowledge keeper. Takuya says he has never heard of such a tradition. Asuka has plainly theatricalized or invented the office. Midori nevertheless takes the mandate seriously, researches the new repertoire, and begins teaching the section. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0007-P0027`

This is stronger than ordinary inheritance.

> **Tradition can become real through successor uptake even when its alleged antiquity is fictional.**

Asuka creates a role rhetorically; Midori reauthorizes it behaviorally. If Midori later passes the practice onward, institutional memory will exist despite the origin story being partly a joke.

The sequence gives HIBIKE a useful four-part vocabulary for transmission:

1. **inheritance** — receiving a role, practice, artifact, phrase, or expectation;
2. **imitation** — reproducing a predecessor's form;
3. **reauthorization** — choosing to treat the inherited form as worth continuing;
4. **differentiation** — changing the form through one's own personality and needs.

Midori is not Asuka. Her instruction is more enthusiastic, associative, and openly delighted; Asuka's was more theatrical, teasing, and strategically controlling. The function survives through stylistic change.

This should remain a durable institutional-model concept for later volumes.

## 11. Controlled return to Rikka: V05–V06 changes what V07 can and cannot infer

HIBIKE-V07 was published in a book that also follows the Rikka duology, and V2 has now read those volumes directly. That creates an analytical advantage but also a contamination risk.

### 11.1 Rikka is no longer merely “another valid band culture”

From Kumiko's local perspective, Rikka is a famous marching powerhouse with athletic greeting culture, highly visible showmanship, and musicians who move with unusual confidence. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0050-P0089`

After V05–V06, the V2 corpus knows much more:

- distributed peer teaching;
- rigorous marching formation systems;
- leadership apprenticeship;
- pain/burnout risks;
- succession infrastructure;
- Azusa's overfunctioning mechanism;
- Amika's differentiation;
- Mirai's role in transmitting elite standards.

That knowledge should be used for **controlled comparison**, not retroactively placed inside Kumiko's mind.

### 11.2 Kumiko's view of Azusa and Amika demonstrates focalization limits

Near the illumination event, Kumiko sees Azusa and Amika together and concludes that they look naturally compatible and apparently untouched by quarrels or serious conflict. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0589-P0598`

V05–V06 has already shown this to be false as a history. Their relationship did contain dependence, resentment, withdrawal, confrontation, and later repair.

The important conclusion is not “Kumiko is foolish.” It is:

> **stable present behavior does not transparently expose the history that produced it.**

This is one of the best source-level demonstrations of why simulation models must preserve epistemic perspective. An external observer can correctly read present ease while being entirely wrong about whether the relationship was always easy.

### 11.3 Rikka comparison becomes diagnostic rather than normative

Rikka's presence reveals differences in institutional emphasis:

- Kitauji's strength is seated ensemble sound, conductor-led refinement, and a newly developing student governance culture;
- Rikka's identity is strongly associated with marching, visual integration, embodied discipline, and a long-established elite system.

Rikka euphonium players explicitly praise Kitauji's seated-sound quality, while Kumiko understands Rikka pride as centered partly on showmanship. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0050-P0089`

The joint event does not stage one culture as the true endpoint of the other. It allows mutually competent institutions to exchange standards without collapsing their identities.

## 12. Azusa's musical equality destabilizes Kumiko's metaphysics of “special”

The decisive personal crisis in S02 begins with a very ordinary conversation. Shuuichi comments that Azusa has improved enormously and may now be close to Reina's level. Kumiko immediately snaps back that this cannot be true because Reina is better, then denies that she is angry. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0140-P0167`

That reaction is diagnostically useful.

### 12.1 Loyalty defense precedes self-understanding

Kumiko does not first evaluate the proposition neutrally and then decide she disagrees. The defense of Reina arrives as a reflex.

This shows a recurring Kumiko mechanism:

> attachment-relevant threat can produce speech before introspection has caught up.

That mechanism differs from her more familiar pattern of thought preceding softened speech. Character models therefore need both directions of mismatch: **unspoken thought** and **spoken reaction whose motive is understood only afterward**.

### 12.2 The riverside scene turns abstract comparison into felt exclusion

Reina later finds Kumiko sad over Asuka's graduation and does not demand an explanation. She proposes practice. At the riverside, Reina says she loves playing with Kumiko and explicitly wants to perform the soli together. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0194-P0221`

Azusa then appears. Her performance is good enough to shock Kumiko, and Reina—musically excited rather than socially strategic—joins her. Kumiko hears the resulting exchange as more intense and equal than her own playing with Reina and leaves despite Azusa inviting her in. `S02 / P0222-P0279`

The narration then makes the internal structure unusually explicit. Kumiko is not simply jealous because Reina played with someone else. She is shaken because Azusa can occupy a position beside Reina that Kumiko had treated as inherently unavailable to herself. `S02 / P0280-P0286`

### 12.3 Idolization had become a defense against aspiration

This is one of the strongest V1 findings and survives V2.

If Reina is “special” in a categorical sense, Kumiko can admire her indefinitely without accepting the humiliation and risk of trying to become comparable. Azusa's existence makes that defense unstable.

The relevant mechanism is:

> **idealization can protect the admirer from competition by redefining equality as metaphysically impossible.**

This does not mean Kumiko's admiration is false. It means admiration has been doing two jobs simultaneously:

- accurately recognizing extraordinary skill;
- shielding Kumiko from the demand to test her own ceiling.

### 12.4 Musical specialness becomes produced rather than sacred

After the Rikka duology, V2 can see Azusa's improvement as the product of extreme embodied labor, institutional opportunity, mentoring, injury pressure, and accumulated competition—not spontaneous magical talent.

Kumiko cannot see all that history from her own focal position, but the corpus can.

The cross-volume consequence is important:

> **excellence remains real while “specialness” becomes materially and behaviorally analyzable.**

This prepares Kumiko to convert envy into work.

## 13. Shuuichi: permission without hierarchy

V1 called Shuuichi's role part of Kumiko's “musical awakening.” V2 revises that phrasing because it risks making him a causal author of a desire that the volume carefully returns to Kumiko.

### 13.1 The new romantic label changes surprisingly little at first

After their mutual confession, Kumiko and Shuuichi deliberately reduce visible school contact because they expect club gossip and friction. They still meet through ordinary night walks and brief private interactions. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0190-P0231`

The relationship's early form is therefore not dramatic couple performance. It is **continuity plus new acknowledgment**.

This fits the V04 state: the relationship is real but grounded in familiarity rather than spectacle.

### 13.2 Hazuki already knows—and does not demand repayment for pain

Hazuki later tells Kumiko that she knows about the relationship and that Kumiko does not need to worry about her. The scene preserves Hazuki's earlier hurt without turning that hurt into a permanent claim over the couple. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0742-P0805`

That is part of Hazuki's ethical maturity, but it does not prove the rejection became painless.

### 13.3 Shuuichi's key musical function is noncompetitive belief

After the Azusa/Reina crisis, Shuuichi finds Kumiko. He explains that he practices the relevant solo even though he does not expect to be selected because he wants to play it. When Kumiko asks whether she could do the euphonium soli, his answer is immediate: yes. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0287-P0325`

He does **not**:

- evaluate her against technical criteria;
- tell her she is already the best;
- define what “special” means;
- coach her mechanics;
- make the decision for her.

His relational function is narrower and important:

> **permission without hierarchy.**

He makes wanting the part feel legitimate before its probability is settled.

### 13.4 Physical reassurance is ordinary, not totalizing

Shuuichi's hand-taking/head-pat behavior in these scenes provides grounded physical comfort. It belongs to his relationship grammar: familiar, reassuring, lower-drama, and less verbally exalted than the Kumiko/Reina register.

This distinction should survive later modeling. Different relationships do not need to compete by using the same emotional vocabulary.

## 14. `吹きたいです`: desire becomes an accountable claim

Natsuki later asks Kumiko directly whether she wants the soli. Kumiko answers `はい。吹きたいです`. The force of her own answer surprises her. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0326-P0339`

This continues the opening trilogy but does not merely repeat V03.

### 14.1 V03 first-person agency was interpersonal

With Asuka, Kumiko learned to stop claiming to know what everyone needed and say what **she** wanted.

### 14.2 V07 first-person agency is competitive/artistic

Here the statement carries a different cost. Wanting the soli means accepting that:

- Natsuki wants it too;
- Rikka players may be stronger;
- selection may expose Kumiko's actual level;
- failure would become personally meaningful because the desire is owned.

The old defensive move would be to preserve dignity by never making the desire fully explicit.

### 14.3 Desire is not yet entitlement

Nothing in the scene implies that saying `吹きたい` creates a moral claim to the part. It creates a responsibility to prepare honestly for the possibility of selection.

That distinction becomes central in Asuka's coaching.

## 15. Asuka's coaching: musical psychology becomes embodied mechanics

The one-on-one lesson is one of the highest-value simulation passages in the first seven volumes because it connects internal state, body, instrument technique, and sound with unusual specificity.

### 15.1 The diagnosis is not “lack of confidence” in the abstract

Asuka says Kumiko's weakness is `ずばり、メンタル`, then immediately decomposes the word into observable behavior. When Kumiko thinks `失敗できない` or `上手くやらなきゃ`:

- her lips tighten;
- the sound becomes hard;
- her ring finger tangles;
- the third piston is not fully depressed;
- finger motion slips and the phrase rushes. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0470-P0477`

This is precisely the sort of evidence a simulation-grade model needs. “Kumiko gets nervous” is too coarse. The causal chain is:

> evaluative pressure → muscular tightening / finger-control degradation → timbral hardening + rushing → increased self-monitoring risk.

### 15.2 Euphonium pedagogy becomes an emotional regulation strategy

Asuka's advice is instrument-specific. She does not tell Kumiko to become more aggressive or louder. She tells her to stop forcing the instrument and concentrate on calm, beautiful tone. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0475-P0479`

This is a distinctly euphonium solution to a psychological problem: the technical demand itself provides the regulation target.

### 15.3 Kumiko's model of excellence changes from person to reproducible features

Kumiko thinks of Asuka's sound—soft, resonant, each note properly ringing—and wants to play like that. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0477-P0480`

Imitation remains present, but it is no longer only personality worship. Specific sound qualities and mechanical habits can be practiced.

### 15.4 Competition becomes ethically tolerable when procedure and desire are owned

Kumiko recognizes that Natsuki, Rikka players, and others are both companions and rivals. She explicitly contrasts this with the middle-school self that would have avoided the contest. Asuka says such a contest should be conducted fairly so no one is left with regret. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0481-P0486`

The ethical shift is not “competition is good.” It is:

> **competition becomes tolerable when desire is explicit, standards are legible, and rivals are not converted into enemies.**

### 15.5 The embodiment ledger should treat this passage as a primary causal anchor

For `KUMIKO@V07`, high-stakes performance prediction should now include:

- increased heart rate and anticipatory tension;
- risk of lip tightening;
- ring-finger/third-piston degradation;
- rushed passages when self-monitoring spikes;
- better regulation when attention is redirected toward calm tone rather than avoiding failure;
- improved performance under trusted one-on-one feedback.

This is much stronger than a generic trait label like “somewhat anxious.”

## 16. Kumiko is selected for the soli; the V1 phrase “wins the soli” is too strong

This is the clearest claim-level correction in HIBIKE-V07.

The source depicts full-group rehearsal in which all relevant trumpet/euphonium players initially play the passage. Taki looks at the score and says, almost matter-of-factly, that the trumpet part will be Reina and the euphonium part Kumiko. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0494-P0512`

There is **no depicted formal audition**, no explicit head-to-head result announcement, and no textual statement that Kumiko “won” against Natsuki or a Rikka euphonium player.

The correct V2 formulation is:

> **After sustained preparation, distributed rehearsal, and direct Asuka coaching, Kumiko is selected by Taki to perform the `ノアの方舟` soli with Reina.**

This revision matters analytically.

If the climax is described as “Kumiko defeats competitors,” the volume becomes a simple meritocratic victory story. The source places more weight on:

- owning the desire;
- accepting competition;
- correcting specific weaknesses;
- becoming prepared enough to be entrusted with the part;
- then experiencing co-presence with Reina as the reward.

Kumiko's first response is not triumph over a rival. It is overwhelming happiness that only she and Reina are producing the opening sound. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0507-P0512`

The event still has competitive meaning. It simply should not be embellished into a tournament structure the text does not depict.

## 17. Reina: exacting standards, activity-based care, and selective possession

V07 is exceptionally useful for resolving the false opposition between “severe Reina” and “soft private Reina.” Both are real, and the trigger conditions are clearer.

### 17.1 Performance-domain Reina is direct and impersonal

With Hazuki, Reina says apology is irrelevant to the sound and identifies tempo instability. She then criticizes Kumiko's fingering as well. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0633-P0664`

The pattern is:

> if the shared task has an objective performance requirement, Reina prioritizes accurate correction over face-saving.

That does not mean she is emotionally indifferent to people. It means performance discourse has its own register.

### 17.2 Praise is sparse enough to be high-value

Once Hazuki improves, Reina says so directly. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0900-P0969`

A character model should not make Reina endlessly critical. She is willing to update judgment when evidence changes. Because she does not flatter habitually, positive evaluation from her carries disproportionate social weight.

### 17.3 Reina's care can take the form of shared activity rather than interrogation

When Reina finds Kumiko crying or visibly sad after Asuka's graduation, she does not force verbal disclosure. She proposes practice. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0194-P0221`

This is a distinct care strategy:

> **presence through a mutually meaningful activity can precede or substitute for explicit emotional questioning.**

For this dyad, music is not merely a topic; it is a channel of regulation and intimacy.

### 17.4 Private Reina openly wants Kumiko as a musical partner

At the riverside she says playing with Kumiko is especially good and explicitly wants the soli with her. After selection, she later tugs Kumiko's sleeve and quietly says she is happy they can perform it together. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0194-P0221`; `S02 / P0513-P0540`

This is not generic teammate language. It is relationship-conditioned preference.

### 17.5 Possession is selective rather than globally exclusive

At the illumination, Reina knows Kumiko will probably come back with Shuuichi. She does not deny or attack that relationship. She nevertheless wants this first viewing for herself: `久美子の初めては、アタシのやから`. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0625-P0632`

That is a crucial model rule. Reina can desire **exclusive priority over a specific experience** without demanding exclusive ownership of Kumiko's entire social life.

### 17.6 Ordinary play remains part of the register

The scene begins with teasing, bodily guidance, and an almost conspiratorial `誰もいないところ`; Kumiko tells Reina she looks cute, Reina admits embarrassment, and both later laugh at Kumiko's deliberately eroticized joke. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0599-P0636`

The stable Reina model must therefore include:

- compressed public assertion;
- exacting musical correction;
- sparse but sincere praise;
- activity-based care;
- private teasing;
- embarrassment;
- physical initiation;
- selective possessiveness;
- comfort with intense dyadic silence.

“Blunt genius” is nowhere near sufficient.

## 18. The illumination scene: relational plurality without forced resolution

The final private Kumiko/Reina scene is among the strongest intimacy passages in the locked prose through V07.

### 18.1 The text explicitly acknowledges Shuuichi

Reina says that Kumiko will probably return to the illumination with Shuuichi. The dyadic intensity therefore does not depend on pretending Kumiko is unattached. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0625-P0632`

This directly supports the corpus rule that relationships must be modeled in parallel rather than forced into a single exclusive ladder.

### 18.2 The scene uses possessive language and embodied exclusivity

Reina claims Kumiko's “first,” places a hand on Kumiko's thigh, later takes her hand, and leads her into a deliberately isolated space. Kumiko's focalized narration says that in this moment she is Reina's and probably Reina is only hers. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0605-P0639`

Those details are too strong to flatten into “ordinary school friendship.”

### 18.3 The text still does not formally settle the relationship label

Kumiko is dating Shuuichi. Neither Kumiko nor Reina states that they are dating one another or explicitly identifies the bond as romantic love in this passage.

The correct evidentiary classification is therefore:

> **textually explicit mutual specialness, physical intimacy, selective possessiveness, and dyadic happiness; strongly yuri-coded / romance-compatible; formal romantic status [G] unestablished at this boundary.**

### 18.4 Wordlessness is itself relational evidence

Kumiko squeezes Reina's hand back and thinks she wants her happiness to transmit through touch. The narration concludes that words are unnecessary; simply being together is happiness. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0636-P0641`

This does not mean the relationship transcends language in some metaphysical sense. More concretely, it shows that this dyad can achieve mutual regulation and confirmation through:

- shared place;
- hand contact;
- visual beauty;
- practiced joke/reference;
- silence.

Those are actionable simulation parameters.

## 19. Music as hospitality: audience modeling without servility

V1's phrase **music as hospitality** remains valuable, but V2 makes the mechanism more exact.

### 19.1 The regular concert has a chosen audience

Taki emphasizes that these listeners came specifically for Kitauji. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0094-P0102`

That changes the students' responsibility. They are not merely demonstrating skill; they are designing an experience for people who have granted the ensemble time and expectation.

### 19.2 Mizore's `楽しい` is audience modeling

Mizore does not ask which program maximizes votes among performers. She asks what could allow adults and children to leave with an experience of fun. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0538-P0545`

Hospitality here is therefore a form of **audience imagination**.

### 19.3 Hospitality requires internal authority

The majority-vote failure is exactly why hospitality cannot mean obediently serving every preference. A host must choose a shape for the encounter.

The better formulation is:

> **musical hospitality = accountable authorship directed toward another person's possible experience.**

It includes listening, but not self-erasure.

### 19.4 Promotion labor matters

Students distribute publicity, contact schools and shops, build staging, prepare costumes, and design performance elements. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0578-P0612`; `S01 / P0841-P0898`

The concert is therefore an institutional product created by logistical labor as much as by the final sound.

This supports the V1 full-series thesis that musical institutions depend on work invisible in contest results.

### 19.5 The novella stops before the concert itself

S01's dramatic endpoint is preparation, confidence, and transfer of leadership rather than the audience's verdict.

That structural choice matters. The important proof of authorship is **having built the event**, not receiving applause as external confirmation.

## 20. Taki: bounded student authorship plus non-sentimental expert judgment

HIBIKE-V07 is useful because Taki occupies three distinct authority positions without collapsing them.

### 20.1 Regular concert: students author the experience

Taki frames the audience obligation and institutional stakes but does not personally dictate every programming choice. The student apparatus must decide what the concert becomes. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0094-P0102`

This extends the earlier model of **bounded student authorship under expert implementation**.

### 20.2 Hazuki: Taki offers an exit and then enforces responsibility

When Hazuki struggles, he presents replacement as a real option. Once she chooses to remain, he asks whether she can take responsibility for that statement and accurately tells her she is the musical keystone of the piece. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0694-P0727`

This is not therapeutic pedagogy. It is agency-respecting but demanding.

### 20.3 `ノアの方舟`: Taki makes the final assignment

The soli selection remains an expert decision. The source does not depict a student vote, a formal public audition, or self-assignment. Taki listens through rehearsal and names Reina/Kumiko. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0494-P0512`

The institutional architecture is therefore mixed rather than ideologically pure:

- students can author concert form;
- performers can own desires and compete;
- expert authority still decides some performance roles.

### 20.4 Paratext supports Taki's boundedness

Takeda describes Taki as technically compelling but poor at handling students and still incomplete as an advisor. `HIBIKE-V07 / F01 / P0025-P0027` **[F]**

That paratext supports—rather than creates—the novel-level distinction between musical expertise and total interpersonal competence.

## 21. Japanese voice and register: V07 expands state-conditioned speech rather than replacing earlier profiles

HIBIKE-V07 provides unusually useful ordinary and task-domain dialogue. The important question is not “what catchphrases does each character use?” but **how register shifts with role, addressee, and emotional load**.

### 21.1 Kumiko: spoken softness coexists with increasingly explicit first-person claims

The V01 baseline remains: Kumiko's spoken language is usually softer and more socially edited than her internal narration. V07 does not turn her into Reina.

Instead, the corpus now contains specific exceptions where desire crosses the threshold into direct speech:

- to Natsuki: `はい。吹きたいです`; `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0326-P0339`
- to Asuka: she directly says she is happy to stand on the same stage again and insists it is not flattery; `S02 / P0459-P0469`
- with Reina: she can give bluntly intimate praise (`可愛かったから見惚れてた`) and revive a shared eroticized joke; `S02 / P0605-P0636`

The simulation rule is not “mature Kumiko becomes direct.” It is:

> **directness increases when the desire has become sufficiently owned and the addressee relationship can tolerate exposure.**

Under uncertain group politics she still hedges, watches reactions, and mentally rehearses alternatives.

### 21.2 Mizore: compressed syntax can carry high agency

Mizore's dialogue remains short:

- `鑑賞会、しよ`
- `プログラム、作った`
- `……楽しい、こと`
- `うれしい`

Yet those compressed utterances can initiate large projects or state decisive criteria. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0057-P0081`; `S01 / P0482-P0554`

This strongly warns against using verbal length as a proxy for initiative or emotional intensity.

### 21.3 Reina: public standards language versus private attachment language

Performance register:

- short, evaluative, correction-first;
- apology does not substitute for technical reliability;
- praise is equally direct once earned. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0633-P0664`; `S01 / P0900-P0969`

Private Kumiko register:

- `やっぱ、久美子と吹くの、めっちゃいい`
- `アタシ、久美子と一緒にソリやりたい`
- `久美子の初めては、アタシのやから`

`HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0194-P0221`; `S02 / P0625-P0632`

The difference is not contradiction. It is **domain conditioning**.

### 21.4 Asuka: theatrical control survives increased warmth

Asuka still uses exaggerated entrances, mock-grand language, teasing labels, and strategic jokes. Even after genuine emotional exchange, she restores distance through comedy. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0340-P0367`; `S02 / P0425-P0469`

When teaching, however, the theatrics coexist with precise diagnostic language. She can move from teasing to technically exact causal description without losing her recognizable voice. `S02 / P0470-P0489`

### 21.5 Yuuko/Natsuki: office speech does not erase private conflict grammar

Yuuko can switch into formal leader mode for announcements and pre-concert framing while remaining explosive around Kaori and argumentative with Natsuki. Natsuki likewise can be managerial, teasing, or indirectly manipulative depending on task. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0970-P1017`; `S02 / P0001-P0006`

For simulation, **role register is a layer, not a replacement personality**.

## 22. Ordinary life, humor, and embodiment: anti-overfitting evidence after the crises

V04 established ordinary life as a required calibration layer. V07 continues that function inside a more institutionally consequential volume.

### 22.1 Kumiko/Shuuichi ordinary intimacy

The relationship is carried partly by low-drama walks, hand contact, familiar irritation, and reassurance. A model that produces constant confessional romance would be less accurate than one that often lets them simply share space.

### 22.2 Reina's physical initiation

Reina repeatedly uses bodily guidance with Kumiko:

- leaning against her at riverside practice;
- tugging a sleeve after soli selection;
- taking the wrist to lead her away;
- touching the thigh during the illumination conversation;
- taking and sustaining hand contact. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0194-P0221`; `S02 / P0513-P0540`; `S02 / P0599-P0641`

This is relationship-specific embodied evidence, not a general statement that Reina is touch-oriented with everyone.

### 22.3 Asuka uses touch as controlled affection

She messes Kumiko's hair, taps shoulders, moves physically close during advice, and uses joking touch with Kaori. This permits warmth while reducing the need for matching emotional disclosure.

### 22.4 Hazuki regulates fear through movement

The playground scene uses swinging as both action and metaphorically resonant bodily experience: what once felt safe can now feel frightening as the body and stakes change. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0742-P0805`

The important simulation point is concrete: Hazuki often processes difficult affect through movement, laughter, exaggerated reaction, or ordinary companionship rather than sustained abstract self-analysis.

### 22.5 Leadership still includes absurdity

The turtle/Urashima/Otohime staging argument, ghost hunt, Yuuko's Kaori fangirl behavior, and Midori's invented “tradition” presentation prevent institutional competence from becoming tonal solemnity. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0578-P0612`; `S01 / P0806-P0840`; `S02 / P0007-P0027`; `S02 / P0340-P0469`

This is reconstructively important. The future adult/leader versions of these characters should still be capable of ridiculousness.

## 23. Focalization and epistemic audit

HIBIKE-V07 is especially valuable for distinguishing direct knowledge from relational inference.

### 23.1 Kumiko can accurately read micro-affect without knowing everything

At the opening, Kumiko knows that Mizore's tiny facial change means genuine happiness and understands that Mizore rarely asks others for help. `HIBIKE-V07 / S01 / P0024-P0035`

That is evidence of accumulated dyadic familiarity. It is not evidence that Kumiko now has transparent access to all Mizore motives.

### 23.2 Kumiko's Azusa/Amika inference is locally plausible and historically false

As noted above, she observes present ease and infers an absence of serious conflict. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0589-P0598`

The V05–V06 focalization gives the corpus privileged correction. This should become a standing methodological example:

> **appearance can be accurate as present-state observation and inaccurate as historical inference.**

### 23.3 Kumiko's reading of Asuka embarrassment is still focalized

When Asuka's cheek reddens after Kumiko's direct affection, Kumiko thinks she may be embarrassed. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0428-P0431`

That is strong [B] evidence, not a direct statement from Asuka's interior.

### 23.4 Kumiko's relationship with Reina receives unusually strong interior confirmation

The illumination scene goes beyond observation because the narration gives Kumiko's own possessive and happiness states directly. `HIBIKE-V07 / S02 / P0631-P0639`

What remains unknown is Reina's full semantic label for the relationship—not whether she is deliberately seeking a special dyadic experience, which her speech establishes directly.

### 23.5 Paratext cannot be used as retroactive mind-reading

Takeda's interview says, for example, that characters were built in complementary pairs and that Azusa/Kumiko were intentionally differentiated. `HIBIKE-V07 / F01 / P0019-P0020`; `F01 / P0047-P0048`

That is valuable [F] construction evidence. It does not mean every dyadic scene should be read as a conscious symbolic “pair” inside the fiction.

## 24. Paratext audit: what the book's interview strengthens—and what it cannot settle

The embedded Takeda interview is significant enough to deserve a separate evidence layer.

### 24.1 Interior band life is an explicit authorial goal [F]

Takeda says she wanted to depict concert band from **inside** rather than from a teacher or distant overview, drawing on still-fresh memories of school band life. `HIBIKE-V07 / F01 / P0004`

This supports the corpus's emphasis on mundane labor, motivation gaps, social friction, and member-level perception. It does not make every fictional detail autobiographical.

### 24.2 Kumiko was created partly to prevent Reina's conflict from becoming a simple meritocratic conquest story [F]

Takeda says Reina was initially considered as protagonist, but that structure risked becoming a story in which a talented first-year defeats seniors and punishes unmotivated people. Kumiko's cooler, observer-like position was then created. `HIBIKE-V07 / F01 / P0014`

This is important support for the V2 refusal to reduce the series to “meritocracy wins.” The narrative viewpoint was designed partly to keep incompatible human stakes visible at once.

### 24.3 Character design is explicitly relational and parameterized [F]

Takeda describes building characters by varying parameters such as mental strength, musical skill, sociability, and assertiveness, and by constructing complementary pairs such as Kumiko/Reina and Midori/Hazuki. `HIBIKE-V07 / F01 / P0019-P0020`

This strongly supports the simulation project. It suggests that relational contrast is not merely incidental to characterization.

But V2 should avoid turning the author's “parameters” metaphor into a literal numeric ontology without source evidence. Characters still change state and behave contextually.

### 24.4 Taki's technical/interpersonal split is explicit [F]

Takeda describes him as attractive through technical specialization but poor at handling students and still incomplete as a club advisor. `HIBIKE-V07 / F01 / P0025-P0027`

This directly supports a distinction already recovered from narrative evidence.

### 24.5 V02 incomplete understanding is explicitly intended [F]

Takeda says Nozomi appears to understand Mizore but does not, connecting that to ordinary relationships in which people continue friendship or romance without fully understanding one another. `HIBIKE-V07 / F01 / P0030-P0032`

This is strong paratextual support for the V2 rule against assuming reconciliation produces symmetrical knowledge.

### 24.6 V03's “breaking the shell” is explicit but should remain mechanism-specific [F]

Takeda says Kumiko's action in the Asuka crisis allows Kumiko to break her own shell and grow. `HIBIKE-V07 / F01 / P0034`

That supports the V1/V2 arc but does not specify that every later direct act is simply “more shell-breaking.” V07 shows a new problem: **turning owned desire into trained capability**.

### 24.7 Rikka was consciously designed as a different drama [F]

Takeda says she felt the initial Kitauji material had reached a stopping point and used Rikka/marching to create another kind of story. `HIBIKE-V07 / F01 / P0043-P0045`

She also explicitly differentiates Azusa and Kumiko in prose behavior: Kumiko tends to make internal retorts whereas Azusa is more likely to say them aloud; even descriptive emphases differ. `HIBIKE-V07 / F01 / P0047-P0048`

This is excellent support for preserving the Rikka duology as an autonomous modeling domain rather than treating Azusa as “Kumiko but extroverted.”

### 24.8 `共依存` is useful author shorthand but not a substitute for the V05–V06 mechanism model [F]

Takeda describes the Azusa/Amika relationship as a softened version of an initially more extreme draft and uses `共依存` as shorthand. `HIBIKE-V07 / F01 / P0050-P0051`

The locked V05–V06 fiction provides a more granular current model:

- Azusa secures attachment partly through usefulness;
- Amika seeks autonomy without leaving the bond;
- Azusa overcorrects into withdrawal;
- reciprocity and non-instrumental wantedness become possible.

Paratext can therefore **support** the dependency reading without replacing novel-level causal analysis.

### 24.9 The afterword supports Kumiko/Shuuichi stability but does not rank relationships [F]

Takeda says she does like Shuuichi and describes writing the Kumiko/Shuuichi relationship as very stable. `HIBIKE-V07 / F03 / P0005`

This is relevant evidence against readings that treat the relationship as entirely false or accidental. It does not settle the semantic status of Kumiko/Reina intimacy or establish an exclusive hierarchy among attachments.

## 26. Simulation-grade character states after V07

### 26.1 `KUMIKO@V07`

**Current state**

Kumiko has crossed another threshold from first-person ownership into competitive/artistic self-claim. She remains socially observant and hedged, but “I want” is increasingly available when the desire is sufficiently owned.

**Stable mechanisms**

- tends to monitor atmosphere and anticipate reactions before speaking;
- can react verbally before self-understanding when attachment loyalty is threatened;
- under evaluative musical pressure, risks lip tightening, ring-finger/third-piston failure, hardened tone, and rushing;
- responds strongly to trusted technical feedback;
- converts jealousy into useful action when she can identify the disallowed desire beneath it;
- values ordinary continuity with Shuuichi and intense aesthetic/musical specialness with Reina without treating one as automatically cancelling the other.

**Likely behavior under ordinary strain**

- joke, complain internally, soften spoken refusal;
- seek familiar people without necessarily announcing emotional need;
- accept task responsibility after some reluctance if the relationship/institution makes refusal feel costly.

**Likely behavior under musical status threat**

- initial self-comparison and defensive idealization;
- possible sharp loyalty response;
- after V07, greater probability of converting comparison into practice rather than withdrawing.

**Negative constraints**

Do not model her as newly fearless, uniformly assertive, or technically equal to Reina by default. Do not erase performance anxiety simply because she was selected once. Do not make her romantic relationships mutually exclusive unless the state evidence requires it.

### 26.2 `MIZORE@V07`

**Current state:** low-verbal but increasingly initiative-capable curator whose world has widened beyond a single attachment without making Nozomi unimportant.

**Task rule:** given a bounded musical/logistical object, she can gather information, make selections, perform administrative follow-through, and state a simple governing criterion.

**Relationship rule:** still sensitive to burdening others; assistance can produce visible relief; Nozomi remains unusually influential.

**Play rule:** secure contexts permit unexpectedly concrete whimsical choices.

**Negative constraint:** do not equate quiet speech with passivity or treat growth as emotional independence from Nozomi.

### 26.3 `HAZUKI@V07`

**Current state:** beginner-derived identity still salient, but she now has direct evidence that her playing can become structurally necessary and publicly recognized.

**Threat mechanism:** strongest anxiety when her perceived lower skill could damage a stronger partner's performance.

**Negative constraint:** do not model her as carefree merely because she is socially energetic. Social brightness and musical insecurity coexist.

## 27. Longitudinal implications for the HIBIKE corpus

HIBIKE-V07 adds or strengthens several corpus-wide modeling principles.

### 27.1 Authorship is not the same as preference possession

Characters can know what they like and still fail to create form. Authorship requires selecting, arranging, refusing, transforming, and taking responsibility for consequences.

### 27.2 Listening to others and having a self are not opposites

Mizore's successful curation is neither mechanical democracy nor Asuka-style unilateralism. The corpus needs a **curatorial authority** axis distinct from simple assertiveness.

### 27.3 Jealousy can be diagnostic without being morally authoritative

Kumiko's jealousy accurately reveals a desire for equality; it does not automatically justify controlling Reina or resenting Azusa.

This distinction is useful for future character inference: affect can provide information about need without determining ethical action.

### 27.4 Musical desire should be modeled as embodied, not merely thematic

V07 gives a concrete bridge from wanting to technique. Later character models should ask:

- what bodily failure appears under stress?
- what attentional instruction helps?
- what kind of feedback is effective?
- what role of the instrument shapes emotional regulation?

### 27.5 Functional succession has multiple forms

The corpus now contains:

- Shindō → Asuka → Kumiko: musical/artistic transmission;
- Haruka → Yuuko: leadership reenactment with different personality;
- Asuka → Midori: rhetorically invented tradition becoming real through uptake;
- Mirai → Azusa → Erika: elite institutional succession through standards and artifacts.

These should not be collapsed into one generic “legacy” motif.

### 27.6 Relational specialness is not necessarily a single ranked ladder

V07 makes this impossible to ignore. Kumiko can have:

- stable romantic acknowledgment with Shuuichi;
- highly possessive, physically intimate, artistically central specialness with Reina;
- enduring mentor attachment to Asuka;
- ordinary friendship structures with Hazuki/Midori;

without the novel forcing all relations into one homogeneous priority measure.

Simulation therefore requires **relationship-specific grammars**, not a single “closeness score.”

### 27.7 Institutional memory can be performative

A tradition need not be ancient to shape behavior. If an origin story recruits sincere successor effort, the practice can become real after the fact.

This is especially relevant to schools, clubs, and organizations whose identity is continually narrated by members.

## 29. Volume synthesis

HIBIKE-V07 is the point where the first-year victory stops being an ending and becomes an inherited condition.

Kitauji must now do more than prove that it can be serious. It must decide what seriousness is **for** when a judge is not the only listener. The regular concert answers by showing that artistic community cannot be governed either by mechanical preference aggregation or by total self-erasure. Mizore's breakthrough is not sudden extroversion; it is the ability to receive many people's desires, accept assistance, and still name a form for which she is responsible.

At the individual scale, Kumiko confronts the parallel mistake she has made with Reina. She has listened so completely to the story of Reina's specialness that she removed herself from the category of people allowed to stand beside her. Azusa makes that story empirically untenable. Jealousy becomes evidence of a desire; Shuuichi makes the desire speakable; Natsuki forces a first-person answer; Asuka converts abstraction into bodily mechanics; Kumiko practices; Taki selects her.

The volume's strongest longitudinal formulation is therefore:

> **Success becomes authorship when inherited standards are no longer merely obeyed but reauthorized through first-person responsibility.**

That reauthorization happens at several scales:

- Mizore curates rather than aggregates;
- Yuuko inherits leadership without becoming Haruka;
- Midori makes Asuka's joke-tradition real;
- Hazuki converts beginner memory into present structural contribution;
- Kumiko converts admiration into trainable aspiration;
- Reina converts musical and relational preference into explicit requests for co-presence;
- retired seniors remain available without remaining institutionally indispensable.

HIBIKE-V07 also produces one of the corpus's clearest warnings against simplistic authenticity. People do not become themselves by stripping away relationships, inherited forms, or social performance. They become more authored by learning **which inheritances to keep, which desires to name, which standards to accept, and which forms to transform**.

The next sequential task is HIBIKE-V08, the first half of the turbulent second movement. V08 should test whether these first-year/post-first-year mechanisms survive a larger leadership and newcomer problem where Kumiko is no longer merely an observer of institutional reproduction but one of the people expected to carry it.
