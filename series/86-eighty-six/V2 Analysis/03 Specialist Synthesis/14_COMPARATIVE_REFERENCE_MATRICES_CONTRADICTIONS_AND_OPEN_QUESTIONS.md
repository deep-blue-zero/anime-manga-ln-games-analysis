# 14 — Comparative Reference Matrices, Contradictions, and Open Questions

## Scope and authority note

This document is the **compression, comparison, and adversarial retrieval layer** for the V2 corpus through Japanese Volume 14. It does not replace Documents 01–13, Document 17, the thematic retrieval dossiers, the chronological evidence ledger, or the Phase-5 locks. It does not introduce a new master interpretation whose argument has not already been established in those sources.

Its governing question is:

> **What are the strongest reusable conclusions, strongest counterarguments, unresolved tensions, and comparative formulations produced by the V2 corpus?**

The decisive comparison rule is:

> **Name the property or mechanism being compared before naming similarity, then state the unmatched features that prevent similarity from becoming equivalence.**

That rule applies at every scale. Giad can reproduce a Sector-like military relation without recreating San Magnolia's complete racial-genocidal system. A Sirin and a Shepherd can both be artificial or posthuman subjects without sharing origin, identity continuity, command structure, or capacity for refusal. Shin and Lena can both self-spend without doing so for the same reasons or through the same authority relation. A mission can be extremely dangerous without being designed as terminal disposal. A remembered face can be psychologically real without mechanically proving numerical identity.

The V14 boundary remains open. Every row marked `OPEN_FINAL_ARC` is a current analytical result, not a gap to be filled by plausible extrapolation.

# I. Executive reference thesis

The V2 corpus can be compressed into twelve interlocking propositions.

1. **Recognition must become standing.** Acknowledging humanity is indispensable, but it is politically weak unless law, medicine, records, residence, casualty recognition, refusal, and return respond to that humanity.
2. **Continuity is not the enemy.** Pride, vocation, citizenship, love, memory, family, promises, titles, and inherited objects can endure without becoming prisons.
3. **The recurring danger is exhaustive foreclosure.** A role or history becomes oppressive when it is made the sole condition under which a person may continue to count.
4. **Freedom requires both capability and answer-space.** Abandonment is not liberty; benevolent replacement of judgment is not care.
5. **Support is legitimate when it restores judgment.** Emergency interruption can be necessary, but its proper endpoint is the subject's renewed capacity to answer.
6. **Functions should be recoverable while persons remain singular.** Distributed command and institutional redundancy protect the collective without making individuals morally interchangeable.
7. **Standing, identity, agency, and flourishing are related but nonidentical.** No single biological, cognitive, memorial, or future-oriented property settles every personhood question.
8. **Memory can resist erasure and still become a form of capture.** Counter-archives are necessary; compulsory imitation, grievance monopoly, and weaponized preservation remain dangers.
9. **Ordinary life is capability infrastructure.** Food, school, hobbies, sleep, embarrassment, art, festivals, and play create repeatable time rather than merely decorating recovery.
10. **Love can be future-bearing without being cure.** Shin and Lena's relation is powerful because it becomes corrigible and return-oriented, not because it eliminates dependency, possessiveness, or asymmetry.
11. **Institutions are judged by corrigibility, burden distribution, and return—not by moral self-description.** Giad is genuinely better than San Magnolia and still capable of regression.
12. **Peace remains an unresolved institutional and developmental problem.** Ending Legion activity would not by itself settle citizenship, demobilization, artificial standing, memory, role exit, or the right to remain different.

The strongest counterargument to this framework is that it risks translating the whole series into one abstract language of foreclosure and answer-space. Document 14 therefore treats those phrases as **analytical glosses**, not hidden Japanese keywords or universal explanations. Every matrix preserves domain-specific differences: race, military command, disability, romance, artificial cognition, memory, and constitutional order do not become one phenomenon merely because each can produce enclosure.

# III. Comparison protocol

## 3.1 Property before analogy

A valid comparison states the shared property first: blocked return, concentrated risk, preserved memory, relation-bearing particularity, body replaceability, command constraint, or political scapegoating. Only then may it name the cases as similar.

## 3.2 Mechanism before genealogy

The same surface result can arise through different histories. San Magnolia's racial denaturalization, Sirin design, Fawn weaponization, a sacred inherited role, and a posthuman terminal command can each restrict exit. Their moral and political analysis cannot be merged until the mechanism is specified.

## 3.3 Scale before necessity

Military claims must name whether they are tactical, operational, strategic, or political-constitutional. An action may be tactically necessary and politically illegitimate, operationally sound and strategically self-defeating, or strategically useful while still assigning an unjust burden.

## 3.4 Standing before utility

A person or subject does not acquire standing by becoming useful. Care must precede the search for replacement function. This rule is clearest in Theo's disability, Fawn risk, Sirin casualties, and Shin's medically concealed sensory loss.

## 3.5 Ordinary and climactic evidence together

The seven core-major characters must retain both ordinary/control evidence and crisis evidence. A monograph that sees only a character's breakdown, command moment, romance, or battle performance produces a distorted psychology.

## 3.6 Lexical systems remain plural

`帰る`, `戻る`, `還る`, `帰還`, and `生還` are not one master metaphor. `人格`, `自我`, `自己同一性`, `意志`, and `意思` are not one philosophical word. Analytical English may compare them, but it may not invent source-language identity.

## 3.7 Evidence layers remain visible

A persistent object, repeated prose image, interior illustration, cover, map, technical plate, decorative graphic, narrator statement, focalized judgment, and character speech are different evidence classes. Convergence strengthens a claim; one layer cannot silently impersonate another.

## 3.8 Open means open

An open question may have strong directional evidence. Direction is not closure. No Face can be strongly directed toward Vaclav while the continuity mechanism remains unresolved; Frederica may publicly occupy a throne while her consent and actual power remain unknown.

# XXXIII. Final reference synthesis

Document 14 does not supply a smoother final answer than the specialist corpus. Its purpose is to preserve the friction that makes the V2 analysis trustworthy.

The series repeatedly offers goods that can become dangers without ceasing to be goods: pride, love, memory, duty, protection, citizenship, heroism, technology, continuity, and return. It also offers constraints that can become legitimate without ceasing to require scrutiny: command, medicine, emergency action, military necessity, and institutional care.

The correct comparative method is therefore neither celebration nor exposure by default. It asks:

- What property is present?
- What history produced it?
- Who benefits and who bears the risk?
- What can the subject refuse?
- What happens when function fails?
- Is return materially protected?
- Can the interpretation be corrected?
- Does the relation preserve a person who changes?
- Does it also preserve a person who chooses to remain durably committed?

At the V14 boundary, the strongest answer is not a universal demand for fluidity. It is a demand that no state, war, role, wound, body, memory, love, or inherited command acquire the right to become the only condition under which a life may continue to count.
