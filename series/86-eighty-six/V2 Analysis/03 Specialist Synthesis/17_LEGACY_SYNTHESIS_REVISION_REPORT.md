# 17 — Legacy Synthesis Revision Report

## Scope and authority note

This document is the canonical V2 audit of the **analytical transition from the historical first-pass / V1 discussion to the locked V2 corpus through Japanese Volume 14**. It does not replace the original conversation transcript as provenance. It does not rewrite old messages to make them appear more accurate in retrospect. Its function is to make the transition reconstructible:

> **legacy formulation → V2 evidence base → confirming evidence → counterevidence → revised formulation → disposition → current canonical home**

The historical transcript remains `historical_legacy`. Its export warning is preserved: the archived selected branch could not prove complete capture of the very first or final conversation boundary. The Phase-1 audit and `L11_LEGACY_V1_TO_V2_REVISION_LEDGER.md` therefore serve as the stable V2 extraction of the major inherited hypotheses. They are not primary-source substitutes; the locked Japanese reread, Phase-5 evidence infrastructure, T14 adversarial audit, and Documents 01–13 determine current authority.

The governing question is:

> **What did the first synthesis get right, underweight, overstate, miss, or misclassify?**

The answer is not that V1 was simplistic and V2 discovered a different series. V1 was already unusually strong at seeing the work's central political, relational, and psychological structures. The major V2 gain is **discipline of distinction**: it separates concepts V1 often allowed to collapse into elegant master metaphors, adds counterevidence and ordinary-scene controls, restores Japanese lexical and focalization boundaries, differentiates military levels and institutional mechanisms, and makes unresolved questions remain unresolved.

The final-arc boundary remains Volume 14. This report evaluates analytical generations, not unpublished outcomes.

# I. Executive judgment — V1 was directionally strong; V2 makes it falsifiable

The historical analysis correctly identified, very early, the series' unusual ability to connect state violence with intimate personhood. It recognized San Magnolia's atrocity as an **administrative system of nonpersonhood**, not merely personal prejudice. It understood that Eighty-Six pride was both a survival achievement and potentially dangerous when made the only available source of identity. It refused to make Giad morally equivalent to the Republic merely because Giad can paternalize or instrumentalize. It treated ordinary life as substantive rather than filler. It recognized that Shin is not emotionless, that Lena is not a simple savior, that romance is not a magical cure, and that artificial origin cannot settle the entire personhood question.

Those are not minor successes. They explain why so much of V1 survives.

The strongest inherited V1 sequence was approximately:

> **recognition of humanity → particular names and memory → rescue that proves insufficient → pride as dignity and possible enclosure → future desire → usable freedom → home, ordinary life, and interdependence → the right to remain capable of becoming something more than the role violence assigned.**

V2 retains most of that progression. It rejects its cleanest universalization.

The mature V2 thesis is no longer:

> healthy persons and societies remain endlessly revisable, while unhealthy ones are fixed.

It is:

> **The recurring danger is exhaustive foreclosure: one role, wound, category, command, relationship, body, institution, grievance, or inherited purpose becomes the condition under which a person is permitted to exist and closes every legitimate route of answer, refusal, correction, accommodation, return, or divergent future.**

That difference matters. V2 found that *86* positively values many forms of durability:

- Eighty-Six pride;
- military vocation;
- names and promises;
- long memory;
- citizenship;
- family and inherited relation;
- Shin and Lena's love;
- Undertaker as a real social office;
- chosen obligations;
- and objects whose history is deliberately retained.

The problem is not that these endure. The problem begins when endurance becomes **exclusive jurisdiction**.

The V2 corpus therefore bought four forms of analytical improvement.

1. **Conceptual precision.** Moral standing, identity continuity, agency, flourishing, citizenship, belonging, home, return, support, obedience, memory, and forgiveness are no longer allowed to substitute for one another.
2. **Adversarial durability.** Attractive claims are tested against counterexamples, particularly T14's forty-one claim pressures and thirty-two open final-arc questions.
3. **Source precision.** Japanese wording, focalization, visual layers, locked locators, and source-lineage defects can now constrain prose that once moved too quickly from theme to conclusion.
4. **Distributed causality.** Recovery, command, military effectiveness, and political repair are no longer organized primarily around one protagonist, one romance, one hero, or one institution.

The result is not a less ambitious interpretation. It is a more bounded one.

# III. Disposition grammar

Document 17 uses two related vocabularies.

## 3.1 Reader-facing disposition

| Disposition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **survives substantially intact** | The legacy formulation remains usable with only ordinary precision edits. |
| **survives but narrowed** | The core insight remains, but its scope, causality, or normative reach was too broad. |
| **strengthened** | V2 found substantially better primary-source, longitudinal, linguistic, ordinary-scene, or institutional evidence for the claim. |
| **materially complicated** | The claim remains useful only if a substantial counterforce or second variable is carried with it. |
| **partially overturned** | A meaningful component survives, but an important premise or consequence fails. |
| **fully overturned** | The current corpus supplies decisive counterevidence to the claim as formulated. |
| **unresolved** | V14 does not justify closure. |

## 3.2 Claim-transition grammar

For machine/retrieval crosswalks:

- `PRESERVE`
- `STRENGTHEN`
- `REVISE`
- `DOWNGRADE`
- `REJECT`
- `OPEN`

The two systems are not synonyms. A thesis can be **strengthened** at reader level while several component claims are `REVISE` or `REJECT`.

# IV. Master thesis transition

## 4.1 Legacy formulation

V1's strongest full-corpus idea was that recognition of humanity is only the beginning. The series expands toward the conditions that allow a recognized person to continue becoming rather than being frozen into:

- victim;
- weapon;
- hero;
- soldier;
- dead person's custodian;
- racial category;
- artificial function;
- lover;
- ruler;
- or symbol.

V1 sometimes condensed that into a stronger claim: *personhood itself* was aligned with the capacity to remain open to change.

## 4.2 Confirming V2 evidence

V2 strongly confirms the first half.

San Magnolia establishes that bare biological humanity is politically useless when law, language, space, medicine, casualty accounting, and archives refuse to let it create claims. Giad demonstrates that legal citizenship and material support are real improvements while still leaving questions of paternalism, conditioned vocation, and political agency. Theo's injury shows that standing must survive lost function. V13 shows a better state can make some citizens disposable again under fear. V14 gives direct vocabulary for change and possible return while simultaneously displaying reactionary restoration.

The whole corpus therefore continues to move from **recognition** toward the conditions under which recognition remains operative through crisis and change.

## 4.3 Counterevidence

V2 rejects the step from that observation to “open futurity is the minimum threshold of personhood.”

A subject may have moral standing while possessing limited future imagination, severe command constraint, disability, artificial construction, or a stable lifelong vocation. Conversely, a highly adaptive intelligence may lack evidence of autobiographical selfhood or independent ends. The Sirin, Fido, Phonix, Shepherd, and Fawn cases force standing, identity, agency, and flourishing apart.

The corpus also positively values durable identities. Pride does not need to disappear. Love can be lifelong. A soldier can remain a soldier. A scar can remain. An inherited name or object can remain. Citizenship is not inferior because it is institutional. Continuity itself is not captivity.

## 4.4 Revised formulation

> **Recognition must become material, legal, relational, bodily, and temporal conditions that prevent one category or function from exhausting the person's standing. The right to revise matters, but it is not a command to remain endlessly fluid and not a threshold below which moral standing disappears.**

**Disposition:** **strengthened but materially revised**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN + REVISE`.  
**Current homes:** Documents 01, 06, 08, 09, 11.

# V. Administrative dehumanization — one of V1's strongest intact findings

## 5.1 Legacy administrative-dehumanization formulation

V1's Volume 1 thesis called San Magnolia's weapon **administrative ontology**: the state changes the operative category of the Eighty-Six and then lets law, military doctrine, maps, records, public comfort, and casualty language follow from that classification.

## V2 evidence base

Document 05 greatly strengthens this. The Republic's atrocity is not merely racist belief plus battlefield exploitation. It is a coordinated sequence of racial classification, denaturalization, concentration, expropriation, coerced labor and service, technical euphemism, casualty erasure, blocked return, survivor elimination, and archival destruction.

Document 07 additionally shows that this moral crime is also military self-destruction: a state that refuses to count its soldiers also destroys institutional learning, veteran preservation, medicine, accurate casualty systems, and professional force generation.

## Counterevidence / narrowing

The existence of dissenting Alba, compromised resisters, children, later Republic refugees, and differentiated responsibility prevents “the Alba” from becoming a reversed racial essence. The state can be systemically genocidal without every individual carrying identical knowledge, agency, or guilt.

## 5.4 Revised administrative-dehumanization formulation

> **San Magnolia constructs administrative disappearance: it makes Colorata humanity unable to produce normal legal, military, archival, and political consequences. Individual prejudice matters, but the genocidal system works because institutions make exclusion ordinary and beneficial to people who need not experience constant personal hatred.**

**Disposition:** **strengthened**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN`.  
**Current home:** Document 05, with military consequences in 07.

# VI. Eighty-Six pride — V1 saw the tension; V2 corrects the direction of travel

## Legacy formulation

V1 repeatedly treated pride as two things at once: anti-dehumanization technology and a possible prison. The strongest early formulation was not that pride was false, but that “authorship over death is not yet authorship over life.” The Eighty-Six could choose how to stand in an imposed death world without yet possessing a developed basis for choosing among peaceful futures.

Some later V1 summaries became cleaner and riskier: pride and attachment “must change form, or become prisons,” and the series could sound as if healthy development meant progressively leaving warrior pride behind.

## V2 confirmation

V2 strongly confirms pride's anti-dehumanization function. It protects dignity, solidarity, refusal of pity, memory of the dead, and the right not to let persecutors author the victim's moral response. It remains meaningful in V14.

The Japanese restriction is decisive: the problem repeatedly crystallizes around **`誇りしか`**, not `誇り` in itself. The danger is having **nothing except pride** as a legitimate ground of worth.

## Counterevidence

The Eighty-Six can remain proud, remain soldiers, remain attached to Spearhead, and still expand ordinary life, love, study, curiosity, disability accommodation, and postwar possibility. Pride does not have to be transcended.

## Revised formulation

> **Eighty-Six pride is a durable good when it remains one source of identity and moral resistance. It becomes foreclosing when pride alone is allowed to determine worth, permissible role, or acceptable future.**

**Disposition:** **strengthened but narrowed**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN + REVISE`.  
**Current homes:** Documents 04, 09, 12.

# VII. Choice, reenlistment, and military vocation — V1's “free and constrained” formulation survives

V1 was already unusually careful here. It explicitly described the Five's return to military service as both **authentically chosen and historically constrained**. They receive citizenship, safety, education, property, and legal alternatives, yet their competencies, attachments, identity formation, and developmental history were all built in war.

V2 strengthens this rather than replacing it.

The correction is to resist using later civilian capability as retrospective proof that enlistment was fully unconditioned. Nor may conditioning erase agency and reduce every military preference to trauma. Document 06 calls the mature position **chosen vocation under historical gravity**.

The ethical test shifts from “do they leave the military?” to:

> **Could they leave, change role, become disabled, disagree, or desire something else without losing standing, livelihood, belonging, or intelligibility?**

V14 still cannot answer that postwar question.

**Disposition:** **survives substantially intact and strengthened**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN / OPEN`.  
**Current homes:** Documents 04, 06, 07, 09; T14-OQ-28 remains open.

# VIII. Rescue and paternalism — V1 correctly distinguished a better state from a perfect one

V1 correctly refused the false equivalence between San Magnolia and Giad. Ernst's citizenship grant, household, medical care, education, and refusal to use the Five as disposable test subjects are substantive moral improvements. The “cage of pity” critique never meant that care itself was domination.

V2 makes the boundary more precise.

Healthy scaffolding:

- enlarges usable options;
- supplies knowledge and material capacity;
- protects during genuine incapacity;
- makes errors survivable;
- and returns judgment.

Paternalism begins when the protector treats an image of recovery as authoritative over the protected person's own answer.

V13–V14 add a crucial institutional falsifier. Liberal constitutional structures do not immunize a state against fear, scapegoating, coerced exposure, or reaction. Their value lies partly in whether routes of correction, investigation, opposition, and restored judgment survive collapse.

**Disposition:** **strengthened and materially complicated**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN + REVISE`.  
**Current home:** Document 06.

# IX. Freedom and capability — V12 survives, but capability is not self-government

The V1 V12 synthesis argued that freedom is not merely absence of a ruler. People require education, information, material security, social practice, and institutions that make choice usable.

V2 strongly preserves this capability argument.

The correction is that capability **enables** self-government; it does not guarantee wise judgment. V13 shows citizens with legal standing and material institutions behaving fearfully and cruelly. Reactionaries then misuse that failure as proof that ordinary people should never have possessed freedom.

V2 rejects both directions:

> capability without agency is guardianship; agency without capability can become abandonment.

A humane order must support fallible judgment while preserving routes of correction rather than treating fallibility as a warrant for permanent rule by someone supposedly wiser.

**Disposition:** **strengthened but narrowed**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN + REVISE`.  
**Current home:** Document 06.

# X. Shin — Undertaker survives; “deepest wound” does not

## 10.1 What V1 got right

V1's “living grave” formulation remains powerful. Shin does not bring death so much as receive obligations a functioning society should have distributed across families, archives, medicine, burial systems, comrades, and public institutions. The name fragments make that abandoned social work physical.

V1 also correctly separated **carrying the dead** from **following the dead** and refused to define Shin as emotionally empty. His care is frequently behavioral: route planning, mercy, repair, remembrance, tactical protection, delegated trust, presence, and eventually concise requests.

The reading that Undertaker is a real social office rather than Shin's essence is strongly strengthened by Volume 10 and later delegation.

## 10.2 What V1 overstated

V1 sometimes called abandonment Shin's “deepest wound.” V2 finds abandonment indispensable but not sovereign. Rei's accusation and strangling, guilt, sensory burden, battlefield conditioning, Undertaker responsibility, repeated bereavement, fear of relational loss, and habituated bodily self-expenditure operate on partly independent axes.

The old “death wish” language also required segmentation. Shin has different states across the series:

- terminal responsibility toward Rei;
- fatalistic expectation;
- willingness to self-sacrifice;
- periods of active death-seeking;
- fear of duration;
- self-expendability without a wish for death;
- and later positive future desire coexisting with medical concealment.

These cannot be one stable diagnosis.

## 10.3 V14's decisive gain

Shin can want Lena, future travel, ordinary recurrence, and life after war while hiding total taste/smell loss because disclosure may remove him from frontline function. V1's tendency to align future orientation with recovery therefore breaks here.

> **A person can possess a future and still treat his own body as infrastructure.**

**Disposition:** Undertaker/care findings **strengthened**; “deepest wound” **materially complicated**; unified death-wish model **narrowed**; future-as-cure **partially overturned**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN + REVISE`.  
**Current home:** Document 02, with body/medicine in 08 and ordinary-life controls in 09.

# XI. Lena — V1's witness thesis survives; interpretive sovereignty becomes a central correction

## 11.1 Durable V1 finding

V1 correctly saw Lena as morally serious before she became practically competent. Her flaw was never secret racism that a names lesson magically cured. She already knows the Republic is wrong. What she lacks are particular knowledge, position-awareness, combat competence, practiced judgment, and institutional leverage.

Theo's rebuke changes her because correct universal belief becomes accountable relation. She learns names, accepts risk, develops command competence, disobeys criminal structures, and becomes capable of making decisions whose costs she cannot purify away.

V2 therefore rejects the simplistic “naive idealist becomes realist” arc.

## 11.2 The “moral narcissism” problem

V1 occasionally used “moral narcissism” to name Lena's tendency to make every death evidence against herself. That phrase is too diagnostic and totalizing. V2 retains the narrower insight: guilt can become another form of self-centering when it claims responsibility beyond one's causal or institutional share.

Volumes 11–12 materially strengthen Lena's capacity to distinguish guilt, grief, responsibility, and repair.

## 11.3 The underweighted problem: Lena can be right and still overclaim meaning

The largest V1 underweight is **interpretive authority**.

Lena often understands Shin unusually well. V2 refuses the inference that accuracy grants sovereignty over what his wound means or what his recovery should look like. V14 makes the issue explicit: her Rei interpretation produces genuine disclosure, yet she also interrupts Shin's own attempted formulation. T14-OQ-20 therefore remains open.

> **Accuracy does not produce ownership of another person's meaning.**

**Disposition:** witness/command thesis **strengthened**; global guilt pathology **narrowed**; interpretive-authority problem **substantially strengthened from underweighted V1 material**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN + REVISE + OPEN`.  
**Current home:** Document 03.

# XII. Shin and Lena — from “love as futurity” to interruptible interdependence

V1's most durable romantic insight was that Lena helps make continued life **imaginable** to Shin. Their relationship transforms waiting, return, travel, ordinary curiosity, and bodily presence into concrete future grammar. It also correctly rejected the idea that romance alone cures either protagonist.

The risk was elegance. Phrases such as “love as permission to imagine oneself alive after the war” or “anti-Legion temporality” can make the romance sound like the privileged metaphysical solution to the whole series.

V2 supplies four corrections.

1. **Recovery is distributed.** Rei, Raiden, Spearhead, Fido, Frederica, education, citizenship, military practice, medical systems, curiosity, ordinary life, and Shin's own decisions are causal, not background.
2. **Dependence is not impurity.** The relationship can be intensely reciprocal without being morally defective simply because each person can destabilize the other.
3. **Possession cannot be euphemized away.** Lena's V14 `その人はわたしのものだ！ 返せ！` is possessive rhetoric. Jealousy, fear, command asymmetry, hostage vulnerability, and bodily intervention are real.
4. **Intervention is judged by what happens to judgment afterward.** Acute interruption can be legitimate when it stops incapacity and returns the other person's ability to answer; it is not a standing license to decide the other's future.

The mature concept is therefore **interruptible interdependence**, not nonpossessive purity.

> **Love matters because it can become a return-address and a reason to remain answerable; it does not confer permanent jurisdiction over the other person's body, role, wound, or future.**

**Disposition:** **strengthened and materially complicated**; purity versions **rejected**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN + REVISE + REJECT`.  
**Current home:** Document 10, with character controls in 02–03.

# XIII. Home and return — V1 found a major structure and over-unified its vocabulary

V1's Volume 6 line that “home is the relationship in which return is possible now” captured a genuine relational transformation. It remains useful as one **Shin-centered interpretive formulation**.

It cannot remain the corpus's universal theory of home or return.

V2's Japanese-language work separates:

- `帰る` — usually homeward / recipient-oriented return;
- `戻る` — re-entry, restoration, recovery, or regression;
- `還る` — deathward / ontological return in relevant contexts;
- `帰還` — operational return;
- `生還` — return alive;
- `故郷` — lived place/origin home;
- `祖国` — political homeland.

V14 provides the decisive negative control: returning to an earlier political state can be reactionary. San Magnolia can remain birthplace and grave landscape without being a desirable future home. Giad citizenship can be genuine without replacing Spearhead, household, origin, or chosen destination.

Thus V1's “chosen home replaces inherited home” tendency is overturned as a binary.

> **Home in V2 is additive and plural; return is a family of movements whose moral value depends on destination, relation, and institutional conditions.**

**Disposition:** **survives but narrowed; binary partially overturned**.  
**Claim transition:** `REVISE`.  
**Current homes:** Documents 10 and 12, with institutional return in 06–07.

# XIV. Memory, names, and the dead — counter-archive survives; healthy memory needs an exit from monopoly

V1's emphasis on names, fragments, testimony, and the dead is one of its strongest contributions. It correctly understood Shin's portable memorial practice as a direct answer to Republic erasure and recognized memory as obligation rather than decorative remembrance.

V2 strengthens that archival reading across:

- San Magnolia's record destruction;
- the Legion as a violent archive;
- Aldrecht and Rito;
- photographs and fragments;
- Alter.1's ordinary archives;
- V14 recipes and radio;
- and the War History Compilation Room.

The correction is that **preservation is not inherently healthy**.

Memory can:

- demand imitation;
- become grievance purpose;
- make one survivor permanent custodian;
- convert a dead person's endpoint into the living person's duty;
- or turn a public victim into a politically convenient martyr.

The mature standard is not “remember or betray.” It is:

> **Preserve particular lives and crimes without granting the archive permanent jurisdiction over what the living must become.**

**Disposition:** **strengthened but narrowed**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN + REVISE`.  
**Current homes:** Documents 05, 11, 13.

# XV. Disability and the body — V1 had the right direction; V2 makes the loss material

V1's Volume 8 governing proposition—personhood must survive loss of military function—survives strongly.

V2 improves it by refusing to use Theo's amputation only as a thematic demonstration. The injury is real loss:

- bodily capacity changes;
- one mode of fighting closes;
- mobility and self-conception change;
- grief is justified;
- a prosthetic is not restoration of the prior body;
- adapted service is not cure.

The key evidentiary sequence is ethical: Theo retains membership and care **before** finding a new function. Later usefulness therefore cannot become the retroactive reason his life deserved preservation.

His adapted V14 role also remains open to a new risk: if every recovery must culminate in renewed usefulness, accommodation can become a second function trap.

The same body problem later reaches Shin through hidden sensory loss and the Fawns through engineered danger.

**Disposition:** **strengthened and materially complicated**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN + OPEN`.  
**Current homes:** Documents 04, 08, 09.

# XVI. Childhood and recovery — V1's anti-romanticization becomes stronger

V1 already warned that extraordinary combat competence should not be mistaken for completed adulthood. V2 turns that insight into a larger model of **asymmetrical development**.

War accelerates:

- tactical judgment;
- lethal responsibility;
- machinery;
- suppression;
- self-reliance;

while suppressing:

- schooling;
- safe experimentation;
- ordinary dependence;
- vocational exploration;
- family continuity;
- and recoverable mistakes.

The most important correction is that Giad does not create the Five's humanity or ordinary capacities. V1 sometimes rhetorically treated civilian life as the discovery of what had been absent. Alter.1 and the V1 reread make clear that cooking, art, teasing, games, books, fashion, care, and curiosity existed inside the Sector. Better institutions make them **repeatable and less expensive**.

Document 09 therefore replaces “return to childhood” with:

> **recovery as expansion of livable recurrence.**

**Disposition:** **strengthened**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN`.  
**Current home:** Document 09.

# XVII. Revenge, forgiveness, justice, and repair — one V14 legacy inference is directly rejected

V1 was already strong in Volume 11: refusing revenge does not require forgiving atrocity.

That distinction should have constrained the provisional V14 language more strictly. The legacy V14 synthesis suggested that Shin's continued pain could coexist with having forgiven Rei. The locked Japanese evidence and T14 audit instead preserve explicit nonforgiveness. V2 therefore rejects the inference that trauma resolution, nonrevenge, affection, or release must culminate in forgiveness.

The same correction scales politically.

Legal punishment may be legitimate and necessary, but punishment is not identical to:

- archival reconstruction;
- compensation;
- restored citizenship/property;
- education;
- memorialization;
- institutional reform;
- or survivor support.

Likewise, choosing not to avenge a wrong does not cancel accusation or legal responsibility.

> **Nonrevenge, nonforgiveness, grief, future orientation, and accountability can coexist.**

**Disposition:** V11 thesis **strengthened**; forgiveness inference **fully overturned**; repair theory **expanded**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN + REJECT`.  
**Current home:** Document 05, with developmental implications in 09.

# XVIII. War and heroism — “anti-war but not pacifist” survives; “anti-heroic” does not

V1 correctly suspected that the series' critique of war cannot be reduced to pacifism. The novels admire military competence, courageous rescue, disciplined command, loyalty, professional sacrifice, and the destruction of an enemy actively pursuing human extermination.

V2 strengthens this by enforcing four levels:

- tactical;
- operational;
- strategic;
- political-constitutional.

An action can be tactically correct and politically illegitimate. A campaign can be strategically necessary while still revealing institutional failure. Necessity can explain a choice without making its historical causes innocent.

The larger correction concerns heroism. V1 sometimes described *86* as anti-heroic. T14 rejects this. The novels admire heroes. What they reject is **hero dependence as an operating system**.

The institutional lesson is:

> **Preserve exceptional competence, but make functions recoverable so that collective survival does not require one singular person to remain permanently expendable.**

**Disposition:** anti-war/not-pacifist **strengthened**; anti-heroic shorthand **fully overturned**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN + REJECT`.

# XIX. The Federacy and “Sector reproduction” — a major V1 insight now has an asymmetric comparison rule

V13's legacy formulation that frightened people “created the Eighty-Sixth Sector again” captures a textually authorized structural rhyme. It remains powerful.

V2 requires that the phrase answer two separate questions.

## Matched mechanisms

A Sector relation can recur where the following converge:

- a marked or politically weak population;
- disproportionate exposure to danger;
- constrained refusal;
- blocked retreat/return;
- public distancing from the cost;
- and a function defined around continued exposure.

## Unmatched historical features

That does **not** automatically reproduce San Magnolia's:

- racial genealogy;
- denaturalization;
- mass expropriation;
- concentration geography;
- unmanned-war fiction;
- zero-casualty accounting;
- or planned survivor liquidation.

The current rule is therefore:

> **Structural analogy is not historical equivalence.**

This correction allows V13 to remain devastating without flattening the very historical specificity Document 05 worked to restore.

**Disposition:** **strengthened but narrowed**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN + REVISE`.  
**Current homes:** Documents 05, 06, 07.

# XX. Artificial subjects and personhood — V2's largest conceptual reconstruction

V1 correctly understood that the Sirins, Fido, Legion, and Phonix prevent biological origin from settling moral status. It also correctly saw the Sirins as a mirror for the Eighty-Six's relationship to expendability.

The main V1 weakness was treating **capacity for change** as too close to the central personhood criterion.

V2 replaces that with a property-by-property ontology.

Separate questions now include:

- biological origin;
- neural provenance;
- autobiographical continuity;
- current personality/self-model;
- singular identity integrity;
- embodied continuity;
- new desires;
- refusal and judgment;
- relation-bearing particularity;
- institutional replaceability;
- and purpose capture.

These properties can diverge.

A Phonix can learn without established personality. A Black Sheep can use human-derived neural structure without continuing the donor as a person. A Sirin can be a successor subject without being the donor resurrected. Fido can have designed purpose and still display contextual relation and memory. A Shepherd can preserve substantial personality while command constrains ultimate action.

Thus:

> **Moral standing, numerical identity, autonomy, and flourishing are related questions, not one personhood switch.**

**Disposition:** biological anti-essentialism **strengthened**; revisability-as-personhood **partially overturned**; ontology **substantially reconstructed**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN + REVISE`.  
**Current home:** Document 08.

# XXI. Legion temporality — “frozen dead” becomes purpose-captured agency

V1's metaphor of the Legion as dead minds frozen at a final command was productive. Rei, Kiriya, Aldrecht, grievance, and inherited final purposes support it.

It is mechanically insufficient.

V11–V14 establish that personality-bearing Legion can:

- interpret;
- strategize;
- conceal;
- negotiate;
- coordinate;
- adapt;

> **A subject may choose locally and remain psychologically complex while every route is ultimately enclosed by a terminal purpose the subject cannot fully revise.**

This preserves the philosophical insight behind V1 while correcting the literal mechanics.

No Face/Vaclav provides the strongest remaining continuity question: narrative identity direction is near-conclusive, but the incorporation and numerical-continuity mechanism remains open.

# XXIV. Motifs and visual form — a domain V1 underbuilt rather than simply got wrong

V1 used strong individual images:

- name fragments;
- the headless knight;
- walls;
- the sea;
- black/white/red contrasts;
- butterflies;
- photographs;
- uniforms;
- abandoned rooms.

But it lacked a consistent evidence-layer method. Symbolic readings could therefore become more stable than the corpus justified.

V2's formal synthesis makes two explicit rejections:

1. recurring visual forms do not establish a universal symbol dictionary;
2. visual evidence is not intrinsically more truthful than prose or testimony.

The replacement method tracks **object biographies** and **spatial transformations**:

> material form → inherited function → repeated use → changed custody/context → revised meaning

A sea can be destination, anticlimax, ecology, battlefield, and site of disability. A butterfly can participate in one associative field and later become a literal weapon mechanism. A wall can remain an atrocity structure while later becoming meeting geography or memorial space.

> **Motifs are historical verbs more often than symbolic nouns.**

**Disposition:** **legacy domain materially underdeveloped; fixed-equivalence tendencies rejected; major V2 expansion**.  
**Claim transition:** `STRENGTHEN + REJECT`.

# XXV. V14 — source redo, thematic gain, and the danger of “fixed meaning” as a new master key

The historical V14 reading was intellectually productive but methodologically provisional because of the Simplified-Chinese narrative body.

Its strongest formulation was that *Paint It Black* fights “fixed meaning”: trauma, propaganda, hatred, reaction, and No Face's posthuman proposal all try to turn one history into a permanent verdict.

That insight survives in revised form.

What V2 rejects is turning **change itself** into the definition of humanity. The final Japanese reread shows several things at once:

- Shin can say people may change and may be able to return;
- Lyudmila can form a new desire;
- Raiden can choose another response to rage;
- public communication can change function;
- political restoration can also be reactionary;
- Shin can still conceal bodily harm;
- Lena can restore judgment while speaking possessively;
- pride can remain legitimate;
- and stable commitments can remain goods.

The better concept is therefore not “fixed meaning bad / change good.” It is **whether inherited meaning becomes exhaustive and answerless**.

The old V14 suggestion that Shin's pain could coexist with having forgiven Rei is rejected by the locked nonforgiveness evidence. The old tendency to treat the volume as a synthetic culmination is also rejected: the afterword marks it as the opening chapter of the final arc.

**Disposition:** source lineage **fully rebuilt**; fixed-meaning thesis **survives but narrowed**; forgiveness inference **fully overturned**; endpoint reading **fully overturned**.  
**Claim transition:** `SOURCE REDO + REVISE + REJECT + OPEN`.  
**Current homes:** Documents 01–13, with unresolved synthesis routed to 14 and later 18.

# XXXII. Current revised master thesis

The V1 master thesis remains visible inside the V2 result, but no longer governs it alone.

A concise generational comparison is:

### V1

> **The series expands from recognition of persons toward the conditions that allow them to keep changing rather than being frozen into assigned functions.**

### V2

> **The series expands from the right to be counted toward the right not to be exhausted by the category under which one is counted. Recognition must become legal standing, material capability, bodily protection, answerable relationships, institutional corrigibility, memory without monopoly, and credible routes of refusal, accommodation, return, and divergent future. Durable identity and commitment remain legitimate when they do not close every other form of standing.**

The difference is not cosmetic.

V1's deepest positive term was **revisability**.

V2's deeper negative term is **exhaustive foreclosure**, while its positive term is **living continuity**: histories, promises, pride, offices, loves, memories, bodies, and institutions can endure while remaining answerable to persons they cannot completely contain.

# XXXVI. Legacy authority and retrieval rules after Document 17

The historical transcript remains valuable for four purposes:

1. **provenance** — reconstructing what the project believed before V2;
2. **hypothesis history** — identifying where a later strong claim originated;
3. **comparison** — demonstrating what evidence changed a formulation;
4. **conversation archive** — preserving materially distinct reasoning that structured artifacts later superseded.

It should **not** be used as current authority for:

- exact Japanese wording;
- locator coordinates;
- final V14 source-language claims;
- current master thesis;
- current personhood ontology;
- current return/home lexical distinctions;
- current Shin/Lena purity claims;
- current forgiveness claims;
- current Sector-equivalence claims;
- current visual-symbol readings;
- or final-arc outcome predictions.

For present retrieval, use the following route:

> **current corpus map → relevant specialist document 01–13 → T14 / relevant thematic dossier → Document 15 / Phase-5 lock → canonical volume deep reading → original Japanese source**

Use Document 17 when the question is specifically:

> “Did the earlier analysis say something different, and why did it change?”

# XXXVII. What V2 actually bought us

The cleanest measure of V2's value is not word count.

V1 already had many of the correct nouns:

- personhood;
- pride;
- future;
- home;
- memory;
- freedom;
- command;
- artificial life;
- war;
- love.

V2 improves the **relations among them**.

It can now say:

- citizenship is indispensable without being sufficient for home;
- recognition of humanity does not prevent disposability;
- future desire does not cure self-instrumentalization;
- nonrevenge is not forgiveness;
- disability does not need replacement utility to preserve standing;
- stable identity need not be enclosure;
- support can become domination even when motivated by care;
- a better republic can regress without having always been identical to a genocidal state;
- intelligence does not prove personality, and personality does not prove unconstrained agency;
- copied memory does not settle numerical identity;
- a machine-designed purpose can coexist with genuine relation;
- heroic action can be admirable while hero dependency remains institutional failure;
- a visual image can be authentic and causally misleading;
- a familiar title can be role, address, armor, joke, or political claim depending on speaker and context;
- return can mean homecoming, restoration, survival, operational extraction, or deathward release;
- and a durable love, promise, vocation, or pride can remain healthy when it does not erase every other claim the person can make.

That is the analytical gain.

V1 often asked:

> **Can this person change?**

V2 more often asks:

> **What still has the right to count if this person changes—and what still has the right to count if they do not?**

The second question is harder. It is also better suited to the series V14 has become.

# XXXVIII. Final disposition

The first-pass synthesis should be preserved as **historical legacy**, not discarded as error.

Its best claims survive because V2's stronger evidentiary system repeatedly confirmed them. Its weaker claims are valuable because they show where elegant interpretation outran textual differentiation. Its largest blind spots became architecture: the V2 project now has dedicated homes for body/disability, military levels, institutional comparison, Japanese voice, motifs/form, artificial-subject ontology, counterevidence, and open-question preservation precisely because V1 exposed the need for them.

The appropriate archival judgment is therefore:

> **V1 was a strong synthetic reading with insufficiently bounded master concepts. V2 does not replace its central insight so much as distribute it across more precise categories, stronger source controls, and explicit counterevidence.**

The mature transition can be summarized in one pair:

> **V1:** people must remain capable of changing beyond the roles violence gives them.  
> **V2:** people must remain more than any one role can exhaust, whether they change it, keep it, return to it, revise it, share it, or eventually leave it.

That second formulation is the current authority through Volume 14.
