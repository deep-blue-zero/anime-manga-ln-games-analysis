## 1. Master thesis

At the V14 boundary, the strongest series-wide formulation is:

> **『86』 is about the difference between being recognized as a person and living within material, institutional, relational, bodily, and temporal conditions that allow a person’s life to remain larger than the role, wound, category, command, grievance, body, institution, relationship, or inherited purpose that once organized survival.**

The negative structure is **exhaustive foreclosure**: one condition becomes final authority over the legitimate selves and futures available to a subject. The positive alternative is not limitless reinvention but **living continuity**—stable identity, obligation, memory, love, vocation, and inheritance that still preserve answer-space, revision, refusal, return, accommodation, and divergent futures.

Three dependent formulations organize the corpus:

1. **Political:** no society is humane if its safety depends on somebody else’s future being closed.
2. **Relational:** a mature bond may constrain, correct, protect, and sustain another person without acquiring permanent sovereignty over them.
3. **Military:** humane organizations protect irreplaceable people by making functions, knowledge, offices, and roles replaceable enough that individuals may rest, fail, withdraw, be injured, change role, or die without moral discount or institutional collapse.
