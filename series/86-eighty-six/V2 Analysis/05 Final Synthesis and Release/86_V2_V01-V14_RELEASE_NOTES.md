# 86-Eighty-Six V2 V01–V14 Release Notes

## Release identity

`86-V2-V01-V14-1.0` freezes the Japanese-primary V2 analytical corpus through Volume 14. It includes Alter.1 only as audited supplemental evidence and does not claim completion of the still-continuing novel series.

## Included analytical layers

- governing method and architecture v2.2;
- fifteen sequential readings;
- Phase-1 legacy audits and L01–L11 longitudinal ledgers;
- V01–V14 checkpoint synthesis;
- Documents 01–14, 16–18 and the Document-18 revision crosswalk;
- T01–T14 retrieval dossiers and their retained-evidence/source-search ledgers;
- Phase-5 evidence/locator locks;
- Phase-7 contradiction audit;
- Phase-8 Japanese-source verification;
- canonical CMR method, sixteen profiles, diagnostic index, directed matrix, crosswalk, and CMR-7/9 audits;
- final README, manifest, machine index, checksums, delivery audit, archival lock, and package.

## Principal analytical changes from V1

The new release replaces endpoint-heavy summary with chronological, locator-routed, Japanese-primary analysis. It strengthens secondary-character resolution, differentiates military scales, treats institutional legitimacy independently from personal benevolence, preserves final-arc uncertainty, formalizes V1→V2 claim transitions, and adds a character reconstruction layer disciplined by source and relationship specificity.

## CMR integration

CMR-9 promoted sixteen profiles and the shared retrieval infrastructure to canonical status. CMR-10 updated only administrative release metadata and the canonical first-read route. Characterization conclusions were not reopened.

## Exclusions

Copyrighted EPUBs, images, audio, adaptation video, and other source payloads are excluded. Redundant intermediate ZIPs, historical per-file checksum sidecars, and initial per-profile drafting validations remain available in Drive provenance but are not duplicated in the portable release.

## Future releases

A correction-only successor becomes v1.1 or later. Admission of V15+ changes the source boundary and requires a new source lock, sequential reading, ledger updates, specialist reconciliation, open-question revision, and versioned release.
