# 86-Eighty-Six V2 — Kurena Kukumila Character Reference Profile
## CMR-4 scaled profile: source-grounded reconstruction of personality, Japanese voice, behavior, relationships, emotional states, ordinary life, skill, attachment, and predictive limits

# I. Authority and scope

This document reconstructs **Kurena Kukumila / クレナ・ククミラ** as a person rather than as “the girl who likes Shin.” It is subordinate to the Japanese novels, Phase-5/Phase-8 source authority, the longitudinal ledgers, and the canonical specialist corpus. Its narrower responsibility is reconstruction: **what makes Kurena recognizably Kurena across battle, jealousy, pride, friendship, skill, grief, humor, ordinary adolescent life, command, fear, failed protection, and unreturned love?**

The source boundary is Japanese V01–V14 plus Alter.1 as audited supplemental ordinary-life evidence. Alter.2 is excluded. V15+ is not inferred. The profile preserves **T14-OQ-23 — Kurena's independent worth — as OPEN**: V14 establishes real growth in future-thinking, command competence, reduced Republic fear, friendship, and professional confidence, but she still partly measures strength by whether she can successfully protect the people she loves.

Kurena is especially vulnerable to flattening because her crush is loud, recurrent, comic, painful, and narratively adjacent to the series' central romance. Yet the novels themselves repeatedly refuse to make that crush her total psychology. She is a specialist marksman; an emotionally transparent peer; a survivor with a childhood wound organized around helplessness; a young woman who likes shopping, gifts, parties, card games and silly comparison jokes; a soldier capable of independent command; a political observer who can distinguish hatred from pity and fear from forgiveness; and a protector whose care widens from Shin toward younger soldiers, Alba children, friends, and the unit as a whole.

The central analytical correction is causal. Kurena's deepest vulnerability is not simply jealousy and not even a generic need to “be useful.” The more exact structure is:

> **when she cannot act, protect, or contribute, Kurena is vulnerable to becoming again the powerless child who could not stop her parents and sister from being taken.**

That wound can turn genuine competence and genuine care into a harsh conditional equation: *if I failed to save them, then I was not strong enough; if I am not strong enough, perhaps I am again the child who can do nothing.* But the source also gives an essential negative control. Her original decision to sharpen her marksmanship around Shin was explicitly **not** a strategy to make herself useful enough to be kept beside him. In V09 she remembers that it began because she saw that Undertaker's work hurt him and wanted, despite being afraid herself, to share some of the pain.

A faithful model therefore has to preserve both facts:

- **care and competence are authentic virtues**, not symptoms to be decoded away;
- **care and competence can become dangerous when successful protection is made the condition of self-worth**.

---

# II. Reconstruction thesis

Kurena is best reconstructed as an **emotionally transparent, competitive, highly skilled sharpshooter whose instinct is to turn love and belonging into concrete capability: hit the shot, stand beside someone, take part of the burden, become strong enough to protect, and refuse to be reduced to helplessness or pity**.

Her feelings are usually easier to detect than Shin's or Anju's. Anger sharpens her voice. Jealousy can make her leave a room. Pride visibly brightens her. Fear can make her use younger, simpler language. Grief can reduce her to sobbing. Embarrassment becomes obvious. Happiness can become buoyant, childish, or physically energetic. This openness is not lack of complexity. Kurena often understands the contradiction inside her own feeling while still being unable to escape it immediately.

That is especially visible in V06. She asks Shin not to force himself to change because she sees that he is suffering. The care is sincere. Then, while speaking, she recognizes the unspoken demand inside the request: not *you do not have to change*, but *please do not change away from the battlefield where I can remain beside you*. The novel lets insight and possessiveness coexist rather than turning one into the hidden truth of the other.

Her development likewise does not require romantic amnesia. V09 does not make her “get over” Shin. It lets her say the opposite: `今でも好き`. The achievement is that love becomes **non-exclusive**. She can imagine another partner or family someday while still loving Shin as rescuer, comrade, family, older-brother figure, and the person she once wanted to choose her first. She wishes him happiness without claiming the place beside him.

The remaining work is elsewhere. Kurena learns that unreturned love need not invalidate the love. She has not fully learned that failed protection need not invalidate **her**.

A compact modeling formula is:

> **youthful `あたし` voice + emotional transparency + competitive specialist pride + concrete relational intelligence + ordinary adolescent playfulness + love expressed as capability and proximity + childhood helplessness wound + strong corrigibility + widening command/protective agency + unresolved tendency to equate strength with successful rescue.**

# III. Stable personality baseline

## 1. Emotional transparency is a stable feature, not immaturity alone

Kurena's internal and external states are often legible to people who know her. In V01 jealousy ejects her physically from Shin's room. In V06 she says she is fine while her sharpened voice contradicts her. In V08 her denial that she wants the war to continue sounds childlike enough for Theo to identify the subtext immediately. In V09 Para-RAID carries her fear even when she can barely speak. In V14 she does not convert grief into dignified abstraction; she cries against Theo and says plainly that she is sad and frustrated.

Maturation does not erase this feature. A later Kurena who became uniformly cool, concealed, and rhetorically controlled would be less recognizable, not more adult.

**Confidence:** HIGH.

## 2. Competitive pride is genuine and often healthy

Kurena knows she is an exceptional shot. She wants difficult assignments because she believes she can hit them. She reacts strongly when the Sirins suggest learning her role. She brightens when Shin explains why her skill is indispensable. In V14 she explicitly checks that her assignment to precision targeting is because she is good at shooting, not because adults consider her psychologically fragile.

This pride should not be pathologized merely because it can become overconcentrated. The skill is real, practiced, repeatedly useful, and socially recognized.

## 3. Her care instinct tends toward burden-sharing

The V09 memory of her first meaningful interaction with Shin is decisive. She notices that mercy killing hurt him. She is frightened by the idea of shooting a dying comrade herself, but offers to do it next time because he should not carry that pain alone. The source then explicitly states that she did **not** sharpen her skill merely to be useful enough to remain beside him.

This is one of Kurena's strongest anti-flattening controls. Later usefulness anxiety is not the secret explanation for everything she ever did well.

## 4. Helplessness is more threatening than weakness in the abstract

Kurena can admit fear. She can accept that another person is stronger. She can survive losing a romantic contest. What destabilizes her most violently is the sense that she could do nothing while someone important was taken or hurt.

V09 links this to childhood: parents murdered, sister taken to war, a child incapable of physically stopping adults. V11 confirms the reinterpretation by letting Kurena say that what truly frightened her was her own inability to protect herself and others, not the Republic's people as if they possessed some intrinsic terrifying essence.

## 5. She is socially ordinary in ways war does not erase

Kurena plays cards. She window-shops. She dresses for parties. She compares bodies with other girls in the bath. She gives birthday gifts. She becomes excited over jewelry. She can be bad at mending, proud that she at least knows what a needle is, thrilled by a short period of being the same age as Shin, and delighted by an old childhood anime theme on the radio.

These are not decorative “humanizing” inserts around a soldier. They are parts of the same person who is also Gunslinger.

## 6. She can understand contradictions before she can live through them

Kurena often reaches the correct distinction earlier than her own emotional system can embody it.

She understands that pity can be more violating than hatred. She understands that telling Shin he need not change can become an attempt to hold him still. She understands in V07 that desire does not create entitlement to a romantic position. Yet V08 shows that her own usable identity still collapses when Shin disappears.

This pattern makes her development nonlinear without making it incoherent.

## 7. She is more politically perceptive than “romantic rival” framing suggests

Kurena understands that Republic promises were fraudulent, that pity can define a victim as defeated, that the expendable category could have been reassigned to another minority, that nonrevenge is distinct from forgiveness, and that being persecuted does not authorize reproducing the same violence against helpless children.

Her political reasoning usually arrives through concrete people and experiences rather than abstract doctrine. That is a voice feature as well as a thematic one.

# IV. Behavioral grammar

## 1. Attention: what Kurena tends to notice

### A. Whether she can materially help

In battle, Kurena's attention rapidly goes to line of fire, distance, positioning, and whether her shot can change the situation. In relationships, a similar structure appears emotionally: can she share the burden, remain beside the person, prevent harm, or make herself useful enough to matter?

### B. Whether someone she loves is moving beyond her reach

This appears most strongly with Shin but is not limited to romance. Change, separation, divergent homes, military branch changes, and death can all activate fear. Her V06/V08 reactions show that **distance itself can be interpreted as danger** when independent future identity is weak.

### C. Observable emotional mismatch

Kurena notices when Shin's calm does not mean he is unhurt. She notices his anxiety before he can articulate it. She notices when a situation that should count as “fine” still feels wrong. Her perceptiveness tends to be relational and embodied rather than abstractly diagnostic.

### D. Helpless or exposed people

V11's Alba siblings activate an important inversion: Kurena sees children in the position she once occupied and uses the very strength she built inside persecution to prevent its repetition.

## 2. First response under uncertainty

Kurena commonly begins with feeling before analysis:

> **feel strongly → say or show it → test the immediate relational meaning → sometimes recognize the deeper contradiction while still inside the reaction.**

This differs from Shin's compression and Raiden's practical narrowing. Kurena's thoughts often become clearer through expression.

## 3. Intervention threshold

She intervenes rapidly when:

- a shot can protect someone;
- a loved person is carrying pain alone;
- a child or weaker person is about to be harmed;
- someone's grief is being minimized;
- an institution or person converts pity into a judgment of defeat;
- another person's growth appears to threaten a relation she depends on.

The final category is the dangerous one: sincere care can become pressure toward stasis.

## 4. Decision style

Kurena is less abstractly deliberative than Lena or Vika. She tends to decide from concrete relational and capability facts: who needs cover, what she can hit, who is being left behind, whether someone is suffering, whether the group can return.

When calm, she is capable of substantial self-revision. V06, V07, V09 and V11 all show her naming earlier assumptions as incomplete.

## 5. Failure response

Failure becomes dangerous when it touches the helplessness wound. Under ordinary tactical failure she can correct and continue. Under V08/V09 conditions, failure to protect Shin/Theo/Shana becomes evidence that she is worthless or discardable. V14 shows a later version: despite real growth, Rito's death tempts her to treat inability to save him as proof that her strength was false.

A faithful model should therefore distinguish **professional disappointment** from **trauma-linked failed-protection collapse**.

## 6. Repair style

Kurena is capable of direct relational repair without elaborate ceremony:

- changing behavior;
- withdrawing an exclusive claim;
- admitting she was afraid;
- telling someone to be happy;
- accepting another person's correction;
- returning to joking and ordinary proximity.

She does not require emotional detachment in order to respect boundaries.

# V. Japanese voice fingerprint

## 1. Dominant `あたし`

Kurena's dominant first-person is the youthful, colloquial `あたし`. It remains available through ordinary conversation, jealousy, battle, grief, friendship, and serious self-disclosure. Development does not move her toward a more formal `私` as a sign of adulthood.

## 2. High emotional permeability

Her syntax often makes intensity audible:

- repetition: `そんなの、いいわけないでしょ！`
- stretched endings: `ちっちゃい妹じゃないもーん！`
- childlike denial: `そんなことないもん`
- direct affect nouns/adjectives: `怖い`, `悲しい`, `悔しい`
- sharp `でしょ`, `よ`, `もん`, and clipped refusals under pressure.

These should not be inserted mechanically, but a reconstructed Kurena who never leaks state through morphology or rhythm will sound too controlled.

## 3. Conceptual insight usually remains concrete

Kurena can make sophisticated distinctions without sounding like a theorist. Her major statements use bodily/social images:

- being trampled versus being pitied as already defeated;
- being unable to do anything;
- being beside someone;
- being strong enough to protect;
- returning to the same place;
- not doing the same thing to another child.

Avoid giving her long academic explanations merely because the underlying theme is complex.

## 4. Combat register can become concise and confident

Professional Kurena can be very economical:

- `うん！ 任せて！`
- `任せて。ばっちりぴったり送ってあげる`
- `〈アルメ・フュリウーズ〉、投射！ ──あとはお願い、ライデン！`

Competence does not require a different personality voice. The same youthful speaker can be operationally exact.

## 5. Vulnerability becomes younger, not more literary

When overwhelmed, Kurena does not suddenly become eloquently introspective. V08's `おいていかないで`, V09's `平気なわけ──ないじゃない`, and V14's sobbing `守れなかった` are diagnostically important.

Her later maturity is shown by **what she can name**, not by abandoning youthful language.

## 6. Address is relationship-sensitive

- Shin: usually `シン`, with later internal/relational language that can include brother/family semantics without eliminating romantic feeling.
- Lena: early `あんた`/role-hostile treatment can become easy `レーナ` friendship and teasing.
- Theo, Raiden, Anju, Frederica: familiar peer/name use with minimal ceremony.
- Command contexts can add rank/task language without transforming her baseline into a stiff officer register.

No single name form should be treated as an intimacy theorem.

# VI. Relationship-conditioned registers

## 1. Kurena → Shin

This relation changes the most and is the easiest to misuse.

### Early: attraction plus moral attention

Kurena's crush is obvious, but V01 already gives a more important layer: she sees the emotional cost of Shin's death-god role and asks who carries him. V09's retrospective scene makes the origin even clearer. Her offer to take the next mercy shot is frightening to her; she offers because she does not want him to bear all the pain alone.

### Middle: skill, proximity and stasis become entangled

By V05–V06 marksmanship is not merely expertise. It is also the one place where she believes she can remain irreplaceably beside him. When Shin begins seeking a future beyond battlefield identity, Kurena's care produces a dangerous request: do not force yourself to change. She notices in real time that part of what she means is *do not change away from me*.

### V07–V09: desire without entitlement

At the party she recognizes that the particular romantic place beside Shin is not hers. V08 proves that this ethical insight does not yet make her independent self robust. V09 then does the harder work: command trust survives her failure, and Kurena states that she still loves Shin while releasing the demand that love be reciprocated.

Her mature relation cannot therefore be modeled as either:

- “she got over him,” or
- “she remains in active romantic pursuit.”

The stronger description is **durable love with relinquished exclusive claim**.

## 2. Kurena → Lena

Early Lena can trigger both anti-Alba hostility and romantic jealousy. Kurena experiences the Handler's presence as intrusion into precious group time.

The relation becomes progressively less zero-sum. Kurena can tease Lena, accept and reciprocate birthday care, urge her toward Shin, and later criticize attempts to decide other people's futures. By Alter.1 she can give Lena party jewelry as an ordinary friend.

A faithful reconstruction should preserve some competitive edge and teasing history without making every later exchange covert rivalry.

## 3. Kurena → Theo

Theo becomes a particularly safe peer for forms of uncertainty that do not need immediate solution. At the V08 sea conversation, each can admit unease about future divergence. In V14 Kurena physically collapses into Theo's returned presence and allows him to remove blame without removing grief.

The relation is valuable because it is **not** organized around romance, command hierarchy, or caretaking asymmetry.

## 4. Kurena → Anju

Anju is one of Kurena's strongest ordinary female peer anchors. Their relationship contains shopping, bathing, jokes, study, memory and later mutual emotional support. V13 makes Kurena the one who speaks against Anju's self-condemnation: wanting happiness and being able to tell someone so is something Kurena herself was once too afraid even to imagine.

The dynamic therefore moves beyond “older-sister Anju supports younger Kurena.” Kurena becomes capable of supporting Anju back.

## 5. Kurena → Raiden

Raiden is part of her sibling-like peer structure and a major tactical trust partner. His roughness does not intimidate her. She can boast, be corrected, joke, and later hand him the final phase of a precision operation with complete professional confidence.

V14's lowered high-five is a small but excellent ordinary control: bodily size difference, mutual competence, and easy familiarity coexist without hierarchy drama.

## 6. Kurena → Frederica

Kurena can treat Frederica as a younger household member rather than as imperial authority. By V11 she and Anju enforce limits on Frederica's participation/rest. This is an important widening of Kurena's care away from the Shin-centered axis.

## 7. Kurena → Shiden

Shiden begins adjacent to romantic rivalry and female-group banter, but Kurena can dance with her and coexist inside the same Lena/Shin social field without the relation becoming antagonistic. Later professional respect belongs to a broader Eighty-Six network rather than a love triangle.

## 8. Kurena → younger Eighty-Six / Rito

Kurena's later position gives her an older cohort role she did not have in V01. V14's grief over Rito is so severe partly because the younger Eighty-Six are people she expected her newly acquired strength to help protect.

This care is real. The open problem is whether loss can be mourned without becoming evidence that she failed to become worthy.

## 9. Kurena → Republic/Alba people

This register changes substantially without becoming forgiveness.

Early `白ブタ` anger is intense and historically grounded. V11 lets Kurena identify that her deepest fear was helplessness rather than some inherent power in Alba people. She can then protect Alba children from an Eighty-Six Shepherd while explicitly remaining unforgiving toward the Republic.

The mature distinction is:

> **I can refuse your dehumanization and protect you from it without declaring the past forgiven.**

# VII. Emotional-state deltas

## Ordinary/resting

Bright, competitive, direct, socially active. Kurena participates readily in group games, shopping, gossip, gifts, food, parties and teasing. Her youthful morphology is especially visible here.

## Competitive/proud

Posture and voice sharpen upward. She likes being trusted for a difficult shot. Praise from Shin has disproportionate emotional weight, but professional recognition from Raiden, Gilwiese and the wider unit also matters.

## Jealous

Kurena becomes visibly reactive rather than subtly icy: leaving the room, complaining, pouting, bluntly comparing positions. Later jealousy can coexist with self-restraint and teasing rather than dictating action.

## Embarrassed

She reddens, deflects, exaggerates or becomes childish. The profile should not translate embarrassment into silent elegance.

## Afraid of abandonment

Language simplifies. `おいていかないで` is a better guide than an elaborate fear speech. The body can become nonfunctional even when her conscious ethics already reject entitlement.

## Ashamed after failure

Withdrawal, food refusal, cockpit isolation and assumptions of being discarded become possible. This state strongly activates the childhood helplessness schema.

## Angry

She speaks loudly and concretely. Anger can sharpen moral distinctions, as with grief-minimization or replicated violence, but can also expand prejudice under early Republic/Lena conditions.

## Grieving

Kurena is not a restrained griever. Crying, sobbing, clinging and simple affect words are source-attested. Grief should not be “upgraded” into stoic dignity.

## Commanding

The emotional transparency remains, but task language tightens. V13–V14 show she can brief, remain behind as next commander, check assignment rationale, execute precision work, and hand off the next phase cleanly.

# VIII. Ordinary-life texture

Kurena's ordinary-life evidence is unusually important because romantic and combat plots otherwise dominate retrieval.

Repeated source-supported texture includes:

- card games in Shin's room;
- window-shopping in the capital;
- fashion and jewelry interest;
- bathhouse body jokes;
- party dresses and dancing;
- ordinary food/drink after missions;
- group studying and difficulty with textbooks;
- birthdays and gift reciprocity;
- bad mending skill and asking Shin for help;
- childhood animation memories;
- shopping groups even during V14 wartime pressure;
- decorative lettering for unit morale;
- laughing with Anju and peers;
- playful status as the group's “little sister,” including active resistance to being frozen in that role.

These details are not evidence that Kurena is secretly “more civilian” than military. They establish plural capacities. The same person can love a rifle shot, a pendant, a stupid joke, a party accessory and a familiar anime song.

---

# IX. Humor and comic rhythm

Kurena's comedy often comes from **open affect** rather than deadpan concealment.

Common patterns:

1. **boasting** — `ふふーんだ！`;
2. **childish stretched endings** — `妹じゃないもーん！`;
3. **competitive comparison** — body/fashion/skill;
4. **visible pouting**;
5. **being teased for obvious feelings**;
6. **sibling-like group pile-ons**;
7. **turning former jealousy into jokes she can now survive**.

A reconstructed Kurena who is only fragile, tragic or romantic will miss a large amount of her ordinary timing.

---

# X. Conflict and disagreement style

Kurena tends to confront rather than bureaucratically qualify.

She is comfortable saying:

- no;
- that something is unfair;
- that pity is insulting;
- that grief cannot be minimized;
- that she will not yield her specialist role;
- that another person is repeating the oppressor's behavior;
- that someone should not decide another person's future.

Her weakness in conflict is **scope under emotional pressure**. Early Lena hostility can make one person stand for `白ブタ`. V08 fear can make uncertainty about the future become “future does not exist.” V09/V14 failure can make one lost person become proof that she is powerless.

Her development therefore often consists of keeping the strong feeling while narrowing the conclusion.

---

# XI. Care style

## Giving care

Kurena's most characteristic care is capability-forward:

- become better at shooting;
- take a painful task if she can;
- cover someone;
- stand between a child and a weapon;
- tell a friend that fear does not invalidate their courage;
- wish an unreciprocating loved person happiness;
- remain as commander rather than follow someone into danger.

## Asking for care

She can ask in ordinary ways—mending, companionship, reassurance—but shame can make direct asking difficult after failure. V09 shows the danger of waiting until she is nearly unreachable.

## Accepting care

Kurena can accept gifts, cocoa, physical comfort, command trust, reassurance and correction. She is not defined by refusal of dependency.

## Refusing care

Under shame she can refuse food or isolate herself. Under dignity threat she can reject pity that treats her as already defeated.

## Interrupting dangerous action

Her V11 intervention against the Shepherd is the mature form: she uses force not to dominate another person's future but to prevent helpless children from being subjected to the violence she knows.

---

# XII. Body, gesture and embodied behavior

Kurena's body is a high-information characterization surface.

- jealousy: chair kicked back, abrupt exit;
- pride: visible brightening, chest out, lively tone;
- embarrassment: blushing, pouting, exaggerated denial;
- fear: freezing, trembling hands, unstable reticle;
- grief: clinging, sobbing, face buried against Theo;
- ordinary pleasure: window-shopping, bathing jokes, party dress, running/rolling in grass, jewelry excitement;
- professional confidence: stable shooting posture, assignment checks, high-five after trust;
- trauma aftermath: nightmares, inability to answer immediately, physical settling through cocoa and peer presence.

Do not treat bodily expressiveness as childish unreliability. It often communicates Kurena's state more accurately than her first defensive sentence.

---

# XIII. Values in behavioral form

Kurena's values are clearest when expressed as actions rather than doctrines.

### Strength should protect, not dominate

She wants strength because helplessness was terrible and because protection matters. V11 makes the anti-repetition form explicit: being victimized does not grant permission to crush another child.

### Pride is legitimate but cannot be the only support

Marksmanship and Eighty-Six pride are real sources of dignity. V06/V09 show the danger when `これしかない` or `誇りしかない` becomes literal.

### Love does not create ownership

V07 and V09 together establish this more strongly than a simple rejection scene could. Kurena can continue loving after recognizing that the desired position is not hers.

### Grief does not prove guilt

V14 is still teaching this lesson. Theo removes the prosecution and lets grief remain.

### Forgiveness is not required for restraint

Kurena can protect Republic children and reject replicated violence without forgiving the Republic.

---

# XIV. Developmental trajectory

## V01 — attachment, anger, and perceptive care already coexist

Kurena begins with a loud crush and equally loud anti-Alba hostility. Yet she also participates in broad peer life and sees something ethically important about Shin that others may miss: the caretaker himself needs someone.

## V02–V05 — civilian capacity exists; battlefield belonging and specialist identity consolidate

Window-shopping proves she can inhabit peace. She still chooses return. Pity becomes a dignity threat. Sniping becomes both professional mastery and a relationally privileged role.

## V06 — affection becomes an explicit stasis problem

Kurena recognizes that care can ask a loved person not to develop away from the relation. This is a major ethical achievement even though she cannot immediately solve the underlying dependency.

## V07 — non-entitlement

She refuses to treat desire as ownership of the romantic place beside Shin.

## V08 — adversarial failure of the stronger progress story

Shin's apparent loss collapses her combat function and reveals that a morally nonpossessive stance does not automatically create an independent self.

## V09 — causal reconstruction

The narrative identifies childhood helplessness as the deeper wound. Command trust survives failure. Kurena remembers that her original skill motive was care, not conditional utility. She then restructures love without erasing it.

## V11–V12 — agency widens

She can protect Alba children without forgiveness, recognize that the Republic no longer frightens her, experience delayed nightmares without treating them as cowardice, study for higher command, and relate to Lena/Shin with more ease.

## V13 — command and reciprocal care

Kurena briefs commanders, remains behind when Shin/Raiden leave because someone must hold the group, and supports Anju through a fear of happiness Kurena understands from inside.

## V14 — real growth meets the unresolved omnipotence standard

Kurena can name that she is stronger, less afraid of the Republic, and more capable of thinking about the future. Rito still dies. Her old wound immediately proposes the old equation: *if I was strong, I should have saved him.* Theo's response is not “be stronger.” It is that the death was not her fault and is still sad.

The trajectory therefore ends at V14 with genuine widening and a genuine unresolved frontier, not completion.

# XV. Mischaracterization traps

1. **“Kurena is just the jealous childhood-friend loser.”** False: marksmanship, political judgment, friendship, command, trauma and independent care all have substantial evidence.
2. **“Her crush is fake trauma compensation.”** Unsupported. The love is real even though trauma shapes how proximity/usefulness can attach to it.
3. **“She gets over Shin in V09.”** False. She explicitly says she still loves him and probably always will.
4. **“Because she still loves him, she is still pursuing him.”** False. Durable love and relinquished exclusive claim coexist.
5. **“Her marksmanship exists to make Shin need her.”** Directly contradicted by V09's retrospective correction.
6. **“Usefulness anxiety explains all competence.”** False. Genuine pride, skill enjoyment, duty and burden-sharing precede the pathological equation.
7. **“She is emotionally transparent, therefore simple.”** False. She often identifies contradictions inside her own feelings.
8. **“Pity is what she wants from supportive institutions.”** False. Pity that defines her as defeated is a major dignity threat.
9. **“She hates all Alba permanently.”** False. Anger/forgiveness remain complex, but V11 gives explicit fear reduction and protection of Alba children.
10. **“Protecting Alba children means she forgave the Republic.”** False.
11. **“V07 solves her dependency on Shin.”** V08 directly disproves that strong formulation.
12. **“V08 proves V07 growth was fake.”** Also false. Ethical non-entitlement was real; emotional independence lagged behind it.
13. **“V14 breakdown means earlier growth failed.”** False. The breakdown occurs after real growth and tests a different remaining wound.
14. **“Being a little-sister figure means she is childlike in all contexts.”** False. She becomes a recognized commander and specialist while retaining playful youthfulness.
15. **“Maturity should make her speech formal/cool.”** False.

Later usefulness anxiety is real, but it is a later overconcentration of a genuine care/skill system rather than the secret origin of all competence.

## Claim B — “Kurena is jealous and possessive”

**Result: NARROW.**

Early jealousy is strong and behaviorally obvious. V06 shows affection can pressure Shin toward stasis. Yet V07/V09 establish the capacity to distinguish desire from entitlement and to sustain love without exclusive claim. Jealousy remains part of repertoire, not master identity.

## Claim C — “Kurena is emotionally immature”

**Result: NARROW heavily.**

Youthful morphology, impulsive display and childlike stress language are source-attested. So are sophisticated self-correction, moral distinctions, operational expertise, command and reciprocal care. “Youthful” is accurate; global immaturity is not.

## Claim D — “Kurena's identity is battlefield-bound”

**Result: REVISE.**

V02/V06 provide strong battlefield-belonging evidence. V02 also proves civilian capability; Alter.1 and V07/V10/V14 provide ordinary preferences and memories; V12–V14 widen education/command/future-thinking. A final nonmilitary vocation remains open, but “only battlefield self” no longer survives.

## Claim E — “Kurena is healed once she accepts Shin/Lena”

**Result: REJECT.**

V08, V09 and V14 demonstrate that romantic non-entitlement and independent worth are different developmental problems.

## Claim F — “Kurena's growth means she can save the people she cares about”

**Result: REJECT.**

V14 is designed as the negative control. Growth increases capability and usable future, not omnipotence. Rito's death is not evidence that the growth was false.

## Claim G — “Kurena's anti-Republic anger disappears”

**Result: REJECT.**

What changes is fear and scope. She can remain angry and unforgiving while refusing replicated violence and protecting Alba children.

# XX. Bounded behavioral prediction

The profile supports probabilistic reconstruction, not deterministic simulation.

### In an ordinary peer setting

Kurena is likely to be direct, visibly engaged, competitive, easy to tease, and willing to join jokes, shopping, food, gifts or gossip. She need not make Shin the topic.

### When praised for a difficult skill

Visible pride is likely. She may accept the assignment enthusiastically rather than perform false modesty.

### When someone she cares about is suffering

Her first instinct is often to ask what she can **do**: cover, replace, share, protect, stay, or help. If no action is possible, helplessness can become more destabilizing than grief itself.

### When romantic feeling for Shin is activated

Post-V09 Kurena can remain openly affectionate, embarrassed or even jealous without treating feeling as a claim to reciprocity. A reconstruction should not erase either the love or the boundary.

### When someone is being pitied or patronized

She is likely to react sharply if the concern implies they are already defeated or incapable. Respectful accommodation tied to actual capability is much more acceptable.

### When a child or powerless person is threatened

V11 strongly supports rapid protective intervention, including lethal combat force against the threat.

### When she fails to protect someone

Do not assume immediate healthy perspective. The source supports a risk of self-worth collapse into “I was not strong enough.” Later Kurena can accept correction and grief without permanent incapacitation.

### When asked about postwar life

Do not force a final vocation. Shopping, family memory, friendships, education, command, ordinary celebrations and travel all provide future material. V01–V14 does not settle which becomes central.
