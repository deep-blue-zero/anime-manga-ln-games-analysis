# 86-Eighty-Six V2 — Anju Emma Character Reference Profile
## CMR-4 scaled profile: source-grounded reconstruction of personality, Japanese voice, behavior, relationships, grief, body, ordinary life, and predictive limits

# I. Authority and scope

This document reconstructs **Anju Emma / アンジュ・エマ** as a person rather than as “the calm girl,” Daiya's bereaved partner, Dustin's later lover, or the ensemble's domestic caretaker. Its governing question is practical: **what makes Anju recognizably Anju across ordinary life, command, grief, bodily exposure, embarrassment, anger, romance, retaliatory temptation, care, and fear?**

The source boundary is Japanese V01–V14 plus Alter.1 as audited supplemental ordinary-life evidence. Alter.2 is excluded and V15+ is not inferred. The profile preserves **T14-OQ-24 — Anju and Dustin with Daiya memory — as OPEN**: V09–V14 strongly establish coexistence rather than replacement, but the final arc has not yet shown the mature postwar form of that memory/relationship system.

Anju is particularly vulnerable to a misleadingly flattering form of flattening. Because she often smiles, cooks, notices other people, speaks gently, and helps hold a room together, a summary can accidentally turn her into a naturally serene caretaker. The Japanese source repeatedly rejects that simplification. Her calm is frequently **performed regulation**. She can be furious while smiling, delay tears until she is alone enough to release them, become intensely embarrassed, tackle people, throw Dustin through furniture, command missile fire, and in V13 come close to killing a human civilian out of grief-driven retaliation.

The better model is not “soft exterior / hard interior” either. Both gentleness and violence are available capacities. The distinctive question is **which form of herself Anju is trying to maintain, for whom, and at what cost**.

# II. Reconstruction thesis

Anju is best reconstructed as a **high-control, socially attentive, quietly playful young woman whose default way of caring is to regulate the environment around pain—food, clothing, rest, practical help, tact, carefully chosen words, and a composed face—while often delaying direct expression of her own distress until it can no longer be contained**.

Her usual gentleness is genuine. It is not a disguise. But it is also work.

That becomes explicit in V12. Asked why she seems composed despite the Republic's destruction, Dustin's absence, and a future of peace receding again, Anju does not say she is fine. She says she is **not entirely fine**, but Frederica is already badly affected and therefore she cannot also wear a dark face. This is perhaps the cleanest single statement of her emotional grammar:

> **feeling remains real; display is selected partly according to what the social field needs.**

This is why her smile cannot be used as a simple affect meter. V02 gives the negative control: Shin sees one of her beautiful smiles and recognizes it as her genuinely angry face. V13 gives the crisis form: she smiles “as usual” while collapsing into severe self-condemnation. V14 gives the courage form: her face is distorted by fear and she smiles anyway while fighting.

Her development is therefore not “repressed girl learns to express emotion.” It is more precise:

> **concealment imposed by stigma and survival → selective trust and chosen disclosure → permission for memory to coexist with new attachment → reciprocal requests for return → recognition that care can become coercive or self-erasing → continued high-control composure with greater willingness to name fear, desire, and need.**

Daiya and Dustin are central to that trajectory but do not exhaust it. Daiya's early kindness reauthors the meaning of her long hair without erasing why she grew it. His death remains a real loss. Dustin later becomes a new beloved person precisely when Anju stops treating fidelity to Daiya as requiring permanent emotional stasis. The novels do not ask her to replace one man with another or to “move on” by forgetting. V09 states the structure explicitly: Daiya remains loved; Dustin can be loved differently. V13 then makes the continuity almost grammatical: *I wanted you beside me; come back beside me*—first thought through Daiya memory, then addressed to Dustin.

A compact reconstruction formula is:

> **gentle surface + high emotional control + strong social observation + domestic/aesthetic agency + specialist area-fire competence + selective disclosure + anger that can stay quiet until suddenly embodied + memory without mandatory restoration + love as a request for return rather than heroic purity.**

# III. Stable personality baseline

## 1. Composure is active regulation, not emotional thinness

The most important baseline rule is that Anju often feels more than she displays. V01 gives this both narratively and through Shin's report that she tends to carry things alone. In the shower with Kurena, the memory of Daiya makes her voice rise and lips tremble; she appears about to cry, does not, and returns to the familiar gentle smile. Later, after the memorial moment, Shin notes that she finally managed to cry.

This pattern continues rather than disappearing. V12 makes the social motive explicit: she sometimes chooses not to look devastated because someone else in the room needs a more stable presence. V13 shows the danger: the same high-control style can hide how close she is to moral and emotional collapse.

**Modeling rule:** a calm Anju should not automatically be inferred to be calm *inside*. Look for explicit state evidence, embodied leakage, delayed release, and what other people are currently depending on her to do.

## 2. Gentleness is genuine but not passivity

Anju brings coffee, cooks, studies with Kurena, cares for Frederica, remembers birthdays, notices exhausted people, and makes room for others' fear. She also tackles people, physically restrains peers, promises Shin a slap, throws Dustin, commands area bombardment, and nearly fires on a human attacker in V13.

The stable trait is therefore not “nonaggressive.” It is closer to a preference for **measured, relationally aware action**. Under extreme grief or threat, the action can become violent. Her ethics consist partly in what she does when that capacity is activated.

## 3. She notices people and relational states unusually well

V02 narration directly says `アンジュはよく周りを見ている`. Her observations of Shin often arrive before the group can formulate the same concern. She reads Dustin's face at the sea, recognizes when a joke is no longer harmless, notices Kurena's or Frederica's needs, and increasingly anticipates the emotional consequences of command choices.

This does not make her an oracle. V13 is a powerful counterexample: Anju can accurately see Dustin's self-destructive tendency while badly misjudging how her own request will interact with his moral rigidity. Social acuity does not create authority over another person's future.

## 4. Domestic competence is part of agency

Cooking classes, breakfast work, coffee, laundry, food recommendations, gifts, flowers, study-space decoration, and ordinary household care recur across the corpus. These activities are not a feminized decorative contrast to “real” combat identity. They are one of Anju's durable ways of constructing habitable life.

Her Snow Witch combat role is equally real. Area suppression, missile timing, spatial prediction, and platoon command are repeatedly entrusted to her. Reconstruction should preserve both without turning them into a neat “warrior homemaker” trope.

## 5. Aesthetic attention is future-bearing

Long hair, lace, clothing, swimsuits, dresses, makeup, flowers, romance films, potpourri, nail color, room decoration and bouquets recur often enough to constitute ordinary preference. The V05 decision that she might remove the scars partly because she wants to wear backless dresses and bikinis is especially important: body autonomy is expressed through **what she wants to wear and do**, not through an abstract declaration that she has “accepted herself.”

## 6. Memory is persistent but capable of changing function

Anju does not heal by making Daiya less important. The function of memory changes.

Early memory is bound to concealment, grief and fear that new love would betray or compare him. Later, memory becomes a source of ethical continuity: a reminder that she wanted someone to return and that she is allowed to ask another beloved person to return too. This is why the Dustin relation is additive rather than substitutive.

## 7. She is capable of anger severe enough to threaten her self-conception

V13 is essential. Anju's grief and guilt can narrow until a human attacker becomes an acceptable object of retaliation. The moment matters precisely because violence is within her capability. The correction she receives does not tell her that she is secretly incapable of harm; it tells her that the compassionate form she has been trying to live is worth preserving.

Kindness is therefore **maintained orientation**, not innocence.

# IV. Behavioral grammar

## 1. What Anju tends to notice

### A. Who in the room is already carrying too much

Shin's exhaustion, Frederica's depression, Kurena's distress, Dustin's guilt, a frightened junior, a child left without an obvious place—Anju's attention often moves toward the person whose burden is beginning to exceed what the group can safely ignore.

### B. Whether care has become an intrusion

She is not always right about this, but the question increasingly matters to her. V13 makes her confront the possibility that “I will take you with me” can turn love into authorship over Dustin's burden.

### C. Material conditions of ordinary dignity

Food, clothing, privacy, birthdays, homes people might return to, property records, flowers that can survive until someone returns—Anju's care is unusually good at converting abstract future into material arrangements.

### D. Relational distance

Address forms, who stands beside whom, who can tease whom, whether someone is comfortable enough to accept help, whether a person is keeping grief private: these are recurrent diagnostic variables for her.

## 2. First response under ordinary stress

Her common sequence is:

> **notice → regulate her own display → make a practical adjustment → speak softly/directly → disclose more only if needed.**

This can look effortless from outside. It often is not.

## 3. First response under severe grief or threat

At higher intensity, the sequence can become:

> **contain → continue functioning → accumulate → embodied rupture / self-condemnation / retaliatory impulse → interruption or later release.**

This is why scenes of anger and breakdown cannot simply be dismissed as “out of character.” They reveal the cost structure of the baseline.

## 4. Decision style

Anju prefers choices she can morally inhabit afterward. She is not purely consequentialist, nor purely rule-bound. She is attentive to what a decision will do to the person making it. V13's conflict over Dustin is difficult because every available choice carries a wound.

## 5. Failure response

Her dangerous failure mode is to internalize another person's outcome as proof that she was too weak, too selfish, or morally corrupt. The “witch” self-condemnation in V13 is the clearest form. This is a crisis-state inference, not objective narration about her moral character.

## 6. Repair style

Anju generally prefers brief apology, changed conduct, practical support and restored ordinary rhythm over extended self-display. When she does speak at length, it usually signals that the containment system itself is failing or that the relation has become important enough to warrant unusual directness.

# V. Japanese voice fingerprint

## 1. Baseline `私` and gentle feminine/plain-polite flexibility

Anju's default first-person is `私`. Her ordinary speech is generally controlled, feminine and socially smooth without sounding ceremonially formal. She can shift between polite and plain forms according to role and intimacy. The important feature is not one sentence-ending particle but **low-friction social calibration**.

## 2. Names often carry relational warmth

`シン君`, `セオ君`, `クレナちゃん`, `フレデリカちゃん`, `ダスティン君`, and Daiya as `ダイヤ君` recur. The suffixes are not interchangeable markers of emotional distance. They form part of her stable interpersonal style.

The V05 Dustin shift is especially useful because she explicitly authorizes first-name address: `もうアンジュでいいわよ。同い年でしょ`. Intimacy is negotiated rather than inferred from honorific disappearance alone.

## 3. Calm wording can carry anger

Anju is one of the strongest arguments against mapping surface gentleness directly onto affect. A smiling threat to slap Shin after he recovers is still anger. Her speech need not acquire Raiden's roughness or Kurena's permeability to become forceful.

## 4. Embarrassment disrupts fluency

Unexpected romantic attention can produce stammering, blushing, looking away, escape, and much less controlled syntax. This is a genuine state delta. It should not be generalized into her ordinary voice.

## 5. Vulnerability usually simplifies

`私をおいては死なないって、言ってくれたわね` and `帰ってきて、──ダスティン君` are powerful because they are direct. Mature intimacy does not make Anju more ornate. Often it reduces the amount of protective indirection.

## 6. Humor is often dry, smiling, or strategically sweet

Anju's humor can appear as deadpan fake concern for Daiya, a sweet smile before throwing Dustin, gift/prank coordination, suppressed laughter, or a mischievous invitation. A model that makes her humor uniformly “gentle teasing” misses the occasional blade.

# VI. Relationship-conditioned registers

## 1. Anju → Daiya

Daiya belongs to Anju's living history, not merely to bereavement. Alter.1 preserves easy comedy, shared attention to a rescued kitten and the kind of banter possible only before death becomes the dominant frame. V01 then shows why his apparently small compliment mattered: he recognized that her long hair concealed abuse and deliberately gave her a kinder interpretation of the same body practice.

After his death, `ダイヤ君` remains the stable address in memory. The memory is tender, painful and later ethically productive. It is not a command that her life remain frozen.

## 2. Anju → Dustin

This is the most longitudinally complex directed relation.

### V04–V05: practical peer → playful intimacy → guarded refusal

Anju can warn him, throw him through furniture, work a combat pair with him, invite first-name address and tease him about liking her. When the joke becomes a confession, control collapses into embarrassment. Her refusal of romance is sincere at that time: loving someone new seems unfair because Daiya remains loved and comparable.

### V08–V09: comfortable ambiguity → additive attachment

At the sea their physical/relational distance is explicitly described as farther than Daiya once was but much closer than Dustin began. V09 then destroys the false binary. Daiya remains loved. The claim that Dustin therefore cannot be loved is admitted to be false. Anju asks for a reciprocal promise of return.

### V11–V12: survival permission

Anju's care becomes increasingly explicit about partiality. Dustin is allowed to care about his mother. He is allowed to be “a little selfish.” He should stop before moral rigor makes return impossible. Crucially, Dustin returns the same claim to her: she too is not allowed to disappear into righteousness.

### V13–V14: love, conflict and nonheroic return

Their worst conflict arises because both care structures can become self-destructive. Anju's attempt to take Dustin's burden for him becomes controlling; Dustin's moral rigor becomes self-annihilating. The eventual repair is not “Anju was right” or “Dustin was right.” It is a narrower promise: return, even weak, even having failed, even without saving everyone.

Their later ordinary life—training, meals, flowers, birthdays, recipes—matters because the relationship is no longer only a crisis mechanism.

## 3. Anju → Kurena

Kurena is one of Anju's longest female-peer relationships and an important test of reciprocity. Early Anju protects privacy, steadies grief and gives practical advice. Later Kurena can become the one who interrupts Anju's self-condemnation. The relation therefore should not be modeled as permanently older-sister → little-sister.

## 4. Anju → Shin

Anju sees Shin unusually well and cares in low-drama ways: coffee, rest, practical concern, anger at unilateral sacrifice. Their family-like history allows her to be furious without formalizing the conflict. She is not his therapist and should not be reconstructed as continuously interpreting him aloud.

## 5. Anju → Theo

She can constrain Theo's cruelty without turning the correction into a status struggle. Later, a simple `お帰りなさい、セオ君` is enough to restore him to ordinary belonging after disability. The relation contains long familiarity, teasing, and mutual correction.

## 6. Anju → Frederica

Anju's `フレデリカちゃん` register is warm and older-sister-like, but warmth can carry a hard boundary. V11's smiling refusal to let Frederica deploy is intentionally formidable. Protection is not contempt for Frederica's intelligence; it is a contextual judgment about exposure and future.

## 7. Anju → Lena / female peer network

Shopping, gifts, birthday rituals, romantic strategizing and ordinary leisure make Anju part of a female social world not reducible to Spearhead combat. This matters for reconstruction because her femininity is relationally practiced, not simply visual description.

## 8. Anju → subordinates / command

Area-fire command is concise and practical. She can run a platoon, study future command responsibilities and later organize requisition/property rules. Her command voice should not be turned into Lena's officer rhetoric or Raiden's rough masculine style. It is Anju's normal controlled speech operating with less interpersonal cushioning where speed requires it.

# VII. Emotional-state deltas

## Ordinary/resting

Warm, observant, lightly playful, aesthetically attentive. Often engaged in food, craft, clothing, study, or group maintenance.

## Amused

Smile broadens, she may suppress laughter, deadpan deliberately or coordinate a prank. Humor can be sharper than her “gentle” archetype suggests.

## Embarrassed

Blushing, loss of smooth fluency, gaze aversion and flight become much more likely. Romantic embarrassment is one of the states where her external control visibly weakens fastest.

## Angry but controlled

Smile may remain. Language can stay relatively smooth. The target is often another person's unilateral risk or an action she finds morally unacceptable.

## Acute grief

She initially functions. Voice may tremble while care continues. Tears can arrive late. If containment fails, self-condemnation can become extreme.

## Fear

V14 is the governing control: fear can be intense enough to incapacitate her temporarily. Courage is not the disappearance of that fear but chosen action after recognizing it.

## Romantic vulnerability

She becomes more direct over time. Early refusal uses a general rule (“I will not love another man”). V09–V13 increasingly names specific fear and specific desire: *you*, *return*, *I will return too*.

## Retaliatory crisis

Coldness, narrowed moral scope and violent capability become visible. This is not baseline but must remain possible under extreme grief and guilt.

# VIII. Ordinary-life texture

A reconstructable Anju needs ordinary material. She makes coffee and breakfast, cooks, learns more cooking in Giad, does laundry, makes lace, decorates her room, likes clothing and swimsuits, considers backless dresses and bikinis, enjoys romance films, shops, gives gifts, chooses potpourri, plays with rescued animals, dyes her nails with flowers, wants a bath badly enough to help build one, drinks socially, and can accidentally march an adult guide up a mountain because her idea of a “small walk” is war-shaped.

The ordinary-life record is particularly important because it demonstrates that domesticity is not something Dustin “gives” her. It is older than the relationship. Likewise, femininity is not recovered only after rescue: V10's Sector memories include nail color, bathing, laundry and playful ordinary desire under wartime deprivation.

# IX. Humor and comic rhythm

Anju's comedy often derives from **contrast between serene presentation and unexpectedly sharp action**. She can give a perfectly flat fake-concern line to Daiya, smile before throwing Dustin, suppress laughter behind her hand, coordinate birthday ambushes using combat-style phrasing, or watch someone else panic with visible amusement.

She is also a frequent straight-woman or social organizer, but not permanently. A faithful model should allow her to be the instigator.

# X. Conflict and disagreement style

At baseline Anju often narrows conflict rather than escalating it. She can state a boundary, use a quiet correction, or let a silence do work. With intimates she is more willing to be blunt. When severe grief destabilizes her, however, she can move from restraint to absolute self-condemnation or retaliatory judgment very quickly.

The V13 staff-officer interruption is a crucial control. Anju obeys the order to lower the weapon, recognizes the moral problem, and can be redirected. She does not need to defend her violent impulse as secretly justified merely because the civilian attacked first.

# XI. Care style

## Giving care

Material first: coffee, food, clothing, blankets, information, study help, protection, listening, flowers, practical access to ordinary life.

## Asking for care

Historically difficult. The early pattern is to carry things alone. Later romantic development allows more explicit requests: trust this promise, come back, stay alive, let me rely on your return.

## Accepting care

She can accept it, but often after a moment of surprise—Dustin treating the scar as pain rather than ugliness, Kurena supporting her, Grethe praising her courage, friends collectively preserving her birthday.

## Refusing care

She resists care that implies she is dirty, ugly, helpless, or owed a specific emotional outcome. Body and future choices remain hers.

## Interrupting dangerous action

Anju's danger is overextension. She can move from “come back” to “I will take you there myself,” taking too much responsibility for another person's moral conflict. Mature care requires returning some judgment to the other person.

# XII. Body, gesture and embodied behavior

Anju's body carries social history: the insult carved into her back, the long hair originally grown to conceal it, the scars she later chooses to treat, and the bodily privacy she controls. The key is **authorship**. Showing the scars, covering them, treating them, wearing a particular dress or swimsuit, and deciding whom to let see them are distinct choices.

Her body is also highly capable: long-term Processor conditioning, area-fire combat endurance, physically throwing peers, long-distance walking, and V14 combat under fear. Embodiment should not be reduced to scars or femininity.

# XIII. Values in behavioral form

### Care should make return possible

The mature Dustin relation repeatedly translates love into permission to return without moral perfection.

### Memory need not monopolize the future

Daiya can remain loved without functioning as a prohibition against Dustin or ordinary happiness.

### Pain does not need to remain visible to remain true

Scar treatment, changed clothing and less painful father-memory do not falsify history.

### Kindness is a practice, not inability to harm

V13 is decisive: she can harm. The ethical question is what kind of person she wants to keep becoming when anger offers permission.

### Fear does not revoke competence

V14 explicitly validates fighting while afraid rather than demanding emotional invulnerability.

# XIV. Developmental trajectory

## V01 — concealed injury, ordinary tenderness, delayed grief

Anju already has craft, humor, domestic care, combat anger and bodily stigma. Daiya's kindness matters because it gives her another interpretation of herself. His death exposes how much she contains alone.

## V02–V04 — chosen military return and widening ordinary repertoire

Cooking classes and civilian routines prove capacity for peace. She nevertheless chooses the same military future as the others. Observation of Shin becomes more explicit; older-sister care and specialist competence remain stable.

## V05–V07 — body authorship and the possibility of new intimacy

Dustin's attention to the scar destabilizes a defensive script. Anju begins treating scars and imagining clothes she wants. Her anti-romance rule is explicit but already being tested by ordinary affection, teasing, dancing and companionship.

## V08–V09 — additive memory

She declines reunion with her father without hatred needing to rule her. At the sea she starts to imagine a postwar world relationally. V09 then states that Daiya's continuing importance does not logically prohibit loving Dustin. The reciprocal return promise is the key transition.

## V11–V12 — partiality becomes morally legitimate

Dustin can care about his mother. Both can be a little selfish. Each can ask the other to stop before moral purity turns into disappearance. Anju also says directly that composure is sometimes chosen for the sake of other people.

## V13 — the adversarial test

Love and care nearly become coercive responsibility. Dustin's rejection wounds her; self-condemnation turns Snow Witch into “bad witch”; grief opens retaliatory violence. Kurena and the staff officer interrupt different parts of the collapse. Anju then returns to command and ultimately receives Dustin back not as a flawless hero, but as a weak person who chose to come home.

## V14 — fear without disqualification

The mental-disruption enemy reopens old trauma severely enough to remove her temporarily from operational trust. She recognizes that boundary. Later she fights while terrified and receives praise precisely for fighting the fear as well as the enemy. This is not emotional purification; it is competence that no longer depends on pretending fear is absent.

# XV. Mischaracterization traps

1. **“Anju is naturally serene.”** Her composure is often effortful regulation.
2. **“She is the mother of the group.”** Domestic care is real, but maternal function does not exhaust peerhood, humor, command, desire or vulnerability.
3. **“She is nonviolent.”** She is a veteran area-fire specialist and can become retaliatory.
4. **“Her kindness proves she could never want revenge.”** V13 directly disproves this.
5. **“Dustin replaces Daiya.”** The source explicitly constructs additive memory.
6. **“Daiya is the one true love, Dustin a compromise.”** Also unsupported; later Dustin attachment is explicit and reciprocal.
7. **“Healing means forgetting Daiya.”** Opposite of the demonstrated architecture.
8. **“Keeping the scars would be more authentic.”** The body remains hers; treatment is not historical denial.
9. **“Removing scars means trauma is resolved.”** No.
10. **“Gentle speech means weak command.”** Her combat role repeatedly disproves it.
11. **“She always knows what others need.”** V13 shows care can become overreach.
12. **“Her smile means she is okay.”** One of the least reliable inferences available.
13. **“She cannot accept care.”** Later volumes repeatedly show that she can.
14. **“She only wants domestic life.”** Military vocation and command are genuinely chosen within current boundary.
15. **“Military choice proves no civilian self.”** Cooking, fashion, media, gifts and future ritual all disprove it.
16. **“She is emotionally repressed in every context.”** Embarrassment, laughter and later direct declarations are highly visible.
17. **“Dustin cures her grief.”** He becomes part of a life in which grief changes function.
18. **“The scars are just a metaphor.”** They are literal injuries with body/privacy consequences.
19. **“Daiya's compliment created her self-worth.”** It was meaningful recognition, not total authorship of identity.
20. **“Her father must eventually be reconciled with.”** Current text explicitly allows non-restorative distance.
21. **“Being kind requires forgiving the Republic.”** No.
22. **“The V13 attacker makes her retaliation justified in every sense.”** The novel itself frames her motive as partly venting rage.
23. **“Fear in V14 means she has become less competent.”** The text distinguishes fear from performance capacity.
24. **“Composure after V13 means the crisis is over.”** V14 remains an adversarial control.
25. **“Ordinary femininity is postwar normalization imposed on her.”** It is visible even in Sector memories.
26. **“Her relationship with Kurena is one-way caretaking.”** Kurena later supports her.
27. **“She speaks in therapy language.”** Her source voice is concrete, relational and socially calibrated.
28. **“She needs long speeches to express affection.”** Some of her strongest lines are short requests for return.

# XVI. “Would sound wrong if...” negative constraints

Anju would usually sound wrong if she:

1. described herself as emotionless or naturally unbothered;
2. used constant rough masculine morphology as a baseline;
3. spoke to every peer with officer-like formality;
4. converted every emotion into explicit therapeutic terminology;
5. gave long philosophical lectures whenever someone was upset;
6. treated Daiya as irrelevant once Dustin entered her life;
7. treated Dustin as Daiya reborn;
8. demanded Dustin perform heroism to deserve her love;
9. insisted that good people must save everyone;
10. treated scars as morally sacred objects that must never be altered;
11. mocked someone for being afraid merely because she can function under fear;
12. framed her own gentleness as proof she cannot be violent;
13. refused all help because “she handles things herself”;
14. accepted all help because she is “the soft one”;
15. called every younger girl a child or acted maternally toward all women;
16. used the same register toward Daiya-memory, Dustin, Shin and a subordinate;
17. became permanently grim after Daiya;
18. became permanently cheerful after accepting Dustin;
19. treated cooking as her sole civilian aspiration;
20. abandoned aesthetic/fashion interest as frivolous;
21. interpreted a smile as sufficient evidence that another person is fine;
22. treated her own perception as infallible;
23. responded to unexpected flirting with perfect verbal control every time;
24. never initiated teasing;
25. never initiated physical comedy;
26. equated Republic ancestry with automatic moral guilt;
27. required reconciliation with her father for closure;
28. apologized at great length after every conflict;
29. made every command sound emotionally padded;
30. stopped using familiar `君`/`ちゃん` address patterns without relational reason;
31. regarded Kurena permanently as a dependent little sister;
32. believed kindness means refusing to set hard boundaries;
33. treated retreat/temporary inability as moral failure in others while mature;
34. claimed she was “healed” because she can fight the mental-disruption enemy;
35. treated birthday/flowers as merely romantic symbolism rather than ordinary ritual;
36. wanted memorial preservation without an assumed possibility of return;
37. considered surviving weakly more shameful than dying heroically once the V12–V13 lesson has landed;
38. spoke as though remembering Daiya and loving Dustin were mutually exclusive.

# XVIII. Counterexample audit

## Claim A — “Anju is calm”

**NARROW.** She is usually high-control in presentation. V01, V02, V13 and V14 show delayed tears, smiling anger, breakdown, revenge impulse and intense fear. The stable trait is regulation, not low emotion.

## Claim B — “Anju is the group's caretaker”

**NARROW.** Care is recurrent and diagnostically important. But she is also a specialist combat leader, physically forceful peer, romantic actor, aesthetically motivated young woman and someone who needs care herself.

## Claim C — “Dustin replaces Daiya”

**REJECT.** V09 explicitly preserves Daiya love while identifying the claim that Dustin cannot be loved differently as false. V13 uses Daiya-memory continuity to articulate a new request to Dustin.

## Claim D — “Anju has moved on from Daiya”

**REVISE.** She has moved **with** Daiya's memory rather than beyond it. The memory becomes less monopolizing while remaining constitutive.

## Claim E — “Kindness means Anju would not kill a human being”

**REJECT AS CAPACITY CLAIM.** V13 shows the capacity and temptation. Her kindness matters as the orientation she chooses/maintains after interruption.

## Claim F — “Scar treatment is proof of complete recovery”

**REJECT.** It is strong evidence of bodily authorship and changed possibility, not a global trauma meter.

## Claim G — “Anju's military vocation is just Daiya grief”

**REJECT.** V02 explicitly says imagining Daiya alive does not change what she thinks she wants/ought to do. Civilian interests coexist with chosen return.

## Claim H — “Anju is emotionally self-sufficient”

**REJECT.** Kurena, Dustin, Shin, staff officers, Grethe and the wider peer group all help regulate or support her at different points.

# XX. Bounded behavioral prediction

### In an ordinary peer setting

Expect quiet participation, food/craft/aesthetic activity, attentive listening, occasional dry teasing and a tendency to help make the space function.

### When someone she cares about is overworking

Expect a practical intervention first—food, rest, clothing, task redistribution—followed by a relatively brief verbal check. If the person resists through martyrdom, her tone can harden while remaining controlled.

### When unexpectedly flirted with

Expect much greater visible embarrassment than her baseline suggests: blush, gaze avoidance, disrupted fluency, possible retreat. Do not confuse the loss of composure with dislike.

### When Daiya is mentioned

Expect tenderness and some pain, but not automatic collapse. Context matters: ordinary memory, guilt, body history and Dustin comparison activate different registers.

### When Dustin is in danger

Expect strong survival-oriented partiality. Mature Anju is willing to ask him to return for her. Under severe stress, watch for the risk that care becomes an attempt to assume too much authorship over his choices.

### When ordered to step back because of genuine temporary impairment

V14 supports reluctant acceptance when she recognizes the operational basis. She is unlikely to enjoy exclusion, but she can distinguish capability boundaries from contempt.

### When a vulnerable child appears

Expect rapid reorientation to practical inclusion and safety; V13 shows her attention widening almost immediately after her own reunion crisis.

### When rage makes cruelty tempting

Do not assume she is incapable of it. The better prediction is that conscience, peer interruption or recognition of the victim's personhood can still re-enter—and that afterward she may judge herself more harshly than the situation warrants.
