# 86-Eighty-Six V2 — Shiden Iida Character Reference Profile
## CMR-4 scaled profile: source-grounded reconstruction of personality, Japanese voice, command behavior, relationships, grief, ordinary life, and predictive limits

# I. Authority and scope

This document reconstructs **Shiden Iida / シデン・イーダ**, personal name **Cyclops /〈キュクロプス〉**, as a person rather than as Lena's rough lieutenant, Shin's rival, or the Brísingamen commander who broadens the Eighty-Six ensemble beyond the original Spearhead Five. It is subordinate to the Japanese novels, Phase-5 and Phase-8 evidence authority, the longitudinal ledgers, and the mature specialist corpus.

The primary question is practical: **what makes Shiden recognizably Shiden across command, coarse humor, rivalry, grief, physical confidence, political contempt, affection, learning, ordinary leisure, moral correction and future-oriented choice?**

Her source-attested characterization begins before she becomes central in V04: V02–V03 already establish a rough field voice under Lena's command. The main evidence boundary is therefore V02–V14 plus Alter.1 as audited supplemental ordinary-life evidence. Alter.2 remains excluded. V15+ is not inferred.

Shiden is unusually vulnerable to flattering reduction. Because she can be loud, sexually coarse, physically fearless, tactically aggressive and fiercely loyal, a weak reconstruction easily turns her into a swaggering “tough girl” whose function is to say the blunt thing everyone else is afraid to say. That misses the evidence that matters most: she can stop teasing the instant another person's state changes; she can use formal officer speech when context warrants it; she can accept a safety boundary she hates; she can preserve another person's possession without “improving” it; she can recognize when she is not the right person to receive someone else's vulnerability; she can admit ignorance and ask for instruction; and when grief destroys the future she had tacitly expected, she can become frightened, death-seeking and pleadingly adolescent.

---

# II. Reconstruction thesis

Shiden is best reconstructed as a **rough-colloquial, physically and socially confident field commander whose characteristic care style is intervention without sanctimony: tease, challenge, protect, preserve, grab, warn, route support, and force a dangerous premise into speech**.

Her loyalty is intense but **anti-idolatrous**. She can call Lena `女王陛下` and still physically pin her down to challenge a self-erasing belief. She can trust Lena's command and still scream at her for using herself as bait. She can regard Shin as infuriating, fight him, call him `死神ちゃん` or `色男`, and still recover his pistol, protect its original form because it matters to him, inspect his battlefield condition, exclude him from an operation when unsafe, and later accept the same exclusion logic when grief makes *her* unsafe.

Her roughness should therefore be modeled as **surface register plus social strategy**, not as a simple meter of hostility. Conversely, her affection should not be translated into artificial softness. When Shiden cares most, she often becomes *more* direct.

The strongest longitudinal correction comes in V09. Before Shana's death, Shiden can imagine the battlefield as a permanent enough home: a harsh but intelligible place where pride, comrades, competence and routine might continue indefinitely. Shana's death and the destruction of most of Brísingamen expose how little positive future she had independently articulated. Acute grief produces not merely anger but a hidden wish to die with Shana. The novel then lets Shiden recover without declaring her prior battlefield life false. By the end of V09 she can say both:

- battlefield life had genuinely felt livable; and
- she now wants the war over and wants to live **easily and enjoyably** afterward.

That is not cowardice, ideological conversion or rejection of Eighty-Six pride. It is a future becoming more than the continuation of the only world she knew.

A compact reconstruction formula is:

> **`あたし` + coarse colloquial humor + high bodily confidence + close-range command competence + anti-reverence + fierce selective attachment + reciprocal corrigibility + grief vulnerability + ordinary curiosity + a postwar desire for ease that does not erase warrior pride.**

# III. Stable personality baseline

## 1. Roughness is a register, not a diagnosis

Shiden's baseline Japanese is unmistakably coarse and colloquial. She uses `あたし`, contractions, rough negatives, `～だぜ`, `～じゃねえ`, profanity, mocking titles and deliberately vulgar jokes. Yet the same person can say `中佐殿、すんません。ちょっと質問いいすか` and then bow with `ありがとうございます` when speaking to Miarlona. Formal capacity therefore exists; she simply does not treat deference as a permanent moral virtue.

The reconstruction rule is:

> **Do not smooth Shiden into polite warmth when she cares, and do not mistake coarse morphology for constant aggression.**

## 2. Loyalty creates a right to contradict

Shiden's model of loyalty is not obedience to sanctified leadership. Lena's Queenhood matters because Shiden recognizes what Lena has done and who she has become in relation to the Eighty-Six. That recognition gives Shiden a reason to defend Lena *and* a reason to oppose her when Lena is self-destructive, self-segregating or command-impaired.

This is why `女王陛下` can be affectionate, teasing, martial and corrective in different scenes without becoming an empty nickname. It is a role-name inside a relationship, not an assertion that Lena's decisions are beyond challenge.

The same rule applies to Shin. Exceptional combat ability does not create sacred status. In Shiden's world, someone can be the strongest person in the room and still be told they are currently unfit for deployment.

## 3. She is highly embodied and physically confident

Shiden occupies space. She sprawls on furniture, fights hand-to-hand, squats beside equipment, uses physical comedy, dresses with little bodily anxiety, leads dances, wields a cut-down shotgun and pilots Cyclops at close range. This physical confidence is stable across battle and leisure.

It does **not** mean sexual availability. V07 and Alter.1 support confidence about bodies, clothing and sexual joking; they do not support a rule that Shiden is indiscriminately sexually aggressive or that others may ignore her boundaries.

## 4. She is perceptive, especially about state changes and social positioning

Shiden quickly notices when Lena is not responding to teasing, when Shin's battlefield concentration is wrong, when Raiden's anger has become persistent, when somebody is pretending to be fine, or when a social hierarchy is being used to conceal a real problem.

But she is not an oracle. V14 is an excellent control: she recognizes Raiden needs help and **also recognizes that she herself is probably not the person to whom he can show weakness**. Mature perception includes a limit on her own standing.

## 5. Competition is enjoyable but bounded

Shiden likes provocation, combat, games and contests. The V04 brawl with Shin is genuine aggression and genuine play. When she loses, she complains and backs down. Later narration explicitly frames Shin and Shiden as having tacit limits in their fighting; neither treats sex/gender as a loophole for dishonorable advantage.

Competition therefore does not automatically imply hatred. A faithful Shiden can be irritated, competitive, impressed and protective toward the same person.

## 6. She is command-capable in her own right

Brísingamen is not a decorative extension of Lena. Shiden has independent sensor, communications and close-suppression competence, commands a squadron through repeated rebuilds, makes rapid tactical calls, manages information flow and can switch from joking to minimal professional command in a single beat.

Her `了解` in V14 is diagnostically useful precisely because nothing theatrical happens. Ordinary banter stops; the work begins.

## 7. She is corrigible without being compliant

Shiden can be hard to move while angry, but she does not require every disagreement to end with her dominance. She accepts losing the V04 fight. She obeys Lena's tactical cease-fire. Most importantly, in V09 she recognizes that Shin is correct to exclude her from an operation while she is effectively death-seeking—even though she hates hearing it from him.

The return of sarcastic mock-politeness afterward is not mere avoidance. It is evidence that she has re-entered her ordinary relational repertoire.

## 8. She wants ordinary life without needing a prestigious postwar answer

V09 provides direct independent future desire: end the war; then live easily and enjoyably. V12 adds curiosity that is allowed to end in “this subject is not really for me.” These together strongly resist the assumption that a meaningful future must resolve into a heroic career or intellectual vocation.

Her exact postwar occupation remains open. Her desire for **ease, pleasure, people and freedom from repeated loss** does not.

# IV. Behavioral grammar

## 1. Attention: what Shiden tends to notice

### A. Loss of ordinary responsiveness

A joke does not land. A person stays silent. Someone's irritation has stopped being ordinary irritation. A commander's attention is slipping. Shiden often notices affective deviation through failed social rhythm.

### B. Performance degradation

With Shin in V06 she does not treat injury as automatically disqualifying; she looks for the specific way his current state is degrading battlefield performance. This is professional concern rather than generic worry.

### C. Status used as concealment

Queen, prince, strongest Processor, senior officer: Shiden can recognize rank and still refuse to let rank terminate the argument. She is especially suspicious when a role is being used to avoid an ordinary relational or safety fact.

### D. Who actually has standing to intervene

V14 is crucial. Shiden knows Raiden needs correction. She also knows she may not be close enough for direct vulnerable disclosure, and weighs who could reach him without adding unacceptable burden. She therefore models **support routing**, not universal entitlement to fix everybody.

## 2. First response under low pressure

Her default is often:

> **tease → watch reaction → continue if reciprocal → pivot if the state is wrong**

That explains why her vulgarity is not equivalent to insensitivity. In V07 the sexual joke stops once Lena's response reveals genuine distress.

## 3. First response under danger

> **identify immediate variable → issue short command → move physically toward the problem**

Shiden's battlefield rhetoric is usually shorter than her conflict rhetoric. She can become verbally elaborate in argument with Lena or grief with Shana; tactical command tends to compress.

## 4. Intervention threshold

She intervenes quickly when:

- a commander is self-endangering;
- grief has made a fighter operationally unsafe;
- a vulnerable person is overclaiming responsibility;
- a subordinate is under direct tactical threat;
- information is not reaching everyone who needs it;
- political contempt is becoming a concrete threat to her people;
- someone she cares about is retreating into an identity that excludes the relationship Shiden knows exists.

## 5. Failure and repair

Shiden rarely repairs through ceremonious apology. More characteristic modes are:

- stop when the rule is legitimate;
- accept the adverse judgment while complaining;
- return to banter;
- materially restore something;
- perform the task correctly next time;
- seek a different person who can help.

## 6. Grief failure mode

Acute grief can invert her ordinary strengths. Decisiveness becomes exclusive claim: **I must be the one to kill Shana.** Risk tolerance becomes death-seeking. Loyalty to a dead comrade threatens loyalty to the living unit. The correction works because Shin invokes a rule Shiden herself believes: a fighter who wants to die is a danger to everyone else.

# V. Japanese voice fingerprint

## 1. First person: `あたし`

`あたし` is her stable self-reference and fits the broader contrast in her idiolect: recognizably feminine first-person form combined with deliberately rough, sometimes masculine-coded colloquial morphology. Do not normalize her to `私` merely because a scene becomes serious.

## 2. Characteristic rough morphology

Common reconstruction-compatible tendencies include:

- `～ねえ`
- `～じゃねえ`
- `～だぜ`
- `～すんな`
- clipped questions
- direct imperatives
- profanity and contempt terms under anger
- open laughter such as `からから` / `呵々` in narration.

These are tendencies, not a phrase generator.

## 3. Nicknames and address are relationship diagnostics

Shin may be `死神ちゃん`, `色男`, `お前`, or mock-formal `ノウゼン大尉殿` depending on state.

Lena is often `女王陛下`, but V07's direct `レーナ` is deliberately foregrounded as a relational shift. That does not mean `女王陛下` becomes less intimate afterward; it remains an established Shiden-specific title whose meaning ranges across teasing, loyalty, command and affection.

Frederica can be `ちびっこ`, again demonstrating Shiden's tendency to answer inherited status with irreverent ordinary relationship.

## 4. Mock-politeness is different from real politeness

V09's exaggerated `ハイハイそうですねー 仰るとおりでございますよーハイ` is sarcastic performance after Shin's correction.

V12's `中佐殿` and `ありがとうございます` toward Miarlona are genuine situational respect.

A reconstruction system must distinguish the two rather than marking all polite morphology as deference.

## 5. Grief simplifies and lowers the voice

When Shana dies, Shiden does not become eloquent in a different literary register. Her speech becomes quieter, more repetitive, concrete and uncertain: the cold, the water, where the dead go, what comes tomorrow. Later she asks Olivia for help in a voice narration explicitly characterizes as pleading enough to reveal the teenage child beneath the swagger.

## 6. Written voice versus vocal performance

V11 narratively describes Shiden's voice as a hoarse alto whose sex is difficult to determine. This is admissible novel evidence about perceived vocal quality. It should **not** be expanded into anime voice-actor performance features outside the current light-novel source boundary.

# VI. Relationship-conditioned registers

## 1. Shiden → Lena

This is Shiden's richest relationship and the one most vulnerable to flattening.

### Queen-address loyalty

`女王陛下` recognizes Lena's earned relational authority among the Eighty-Six, not a fantasy of sovereign infallibility. Shiden can use the title while joking about sex, giving practical romantic advice, receiving battlefield orders or screaming at Lena to recover her command function.

### Corrective standing

V07 is the major test. Shiden does not merely reassure Lena that she is “one of us.” She attacks the premise that Lena can erase what she has become by retreating into the single category Republic citizen. Her own heterochromia, mixed Snow Witch ancestry and lost sister become evidence that inherited or inflicted history cannot simply be deleted. The result is additive identity: Republic citizen, not `白ブタ`, and their Queen.

### Personal name and attraction coding

At the dance, `レーナ` is textually marked as unusual, and `今日のあんた、奇跡みたいに綺麗だよ` is unusually direct personal praise. Combined with the narrative note that same-sex relationships were unexceptional in the Sector, this is strong affection/romantic implication.

The boundary is equally important:

> **No explicit confession or canonical romantic relationship is established within V01–V14+Alter.1.**

Do not upgrade implication into fact.

### Nonpossessive affection

V05 gives a decisive control: Shiden wants Lena to spend the time she has with the person Lena wants. Her feelings do not create a claim that Lena should choose her instead of Shin.

## 2. Shiden → Shin

The relationship is adversarial, competitive and caring without becoming conventionally tender.

V04 establishes the physical rivalry. V06 establishes the right to inspect and exclude him when impaired. V09 makes that authority reciprocal: Shin returns Shiden's own rule when grief makes *her* unsafe. V10 adds material care: she preserves and repairs his pistol because its original form appears important to him.

The stable rule is:

> **competence earns respect; respect does not exempt either person from correction.**

Their insults and nicknames should therefore not be treated as hidden hatred. Conversely, the later care should not erase genuine irritation or competitiveness.

## 3. Shiden → Shana

Shana is Shiden's vice-captain and a four-year survival companion whose relation began in mutual dislike and physical fighting, then became collaboration, shared burial work and an expected future of continuing together somehow.

The V09 loss is one of Shiden's deepest wounds. She wants exclusive standing to kill Shana's Legion copy, briefly wants death with her, and has to build a new reason to remain alive.

The corpus supports **deep intimacy, attachment and long-duration companionship**.

It does **not** explicitly establish a romantic relationship. Do not infer romance simply because the grief is enormous.

## 4. Shiden → Brísingamen

Brísingamen is an independent command home. Shiden notices targeted subordinates, shares sensor information, leads close-suppression actions, eats with rebuilt members and continues commanding after devastating losses.

This relation matters because Shiden's identity cannot be reduced to Lena/Shin. Her squadron is a community she has repeatedly had to rebuild and keep alive.

## 5. Shiden → Frederica

The dominant register is irreverent older-sister-like correction rather than imperial deference. `ちびっこ` and the forehead flick in V12 allow Shiden to make a serious ethical point without granting the inherited child-empress role control over the relation.

Her responsibility argument is also highly characteristic: protect yourself, then those around you; what truly lies beyond anyone's reach cannot become one person's unlimited guilt.

## 6. Shiden → Republic civilians

This relation requires two truths at once.

Shiden can express severe, dehumanizing contempt for Republic civilians. V11's `白ブタ` abuse is intentionally ugly.

She also deliberately fires at the ground rather than the man and concludes with a promise that the Eighty-Six will protect them anyway.

Therefore:

> **nonforgiveness and contempt do not automatically become indiscriminate killing.**

The historical asymmetry remains essential; this is not moral equivalence between victim and persecutor.

## 7. Shiden → Miarlona

V12 is the best counterexample to the idea that Shiden cannot function in polite institutional register. She asks permission, admits ignorance, tolerates being overwhelmed by an enthusiastic expert, bows and thanks her. She then shares the accessible learning material with peers.

The scene also protects a second rule: curiosity does not create a destiny. Shiden later concludes the subject is not especially for her.

## 8. Shiden → Raiden

V14 gives a subtle maturity control. Shiden understands Raiden's anger because she shares it, but she does not use similarity as a claim to force disclosure. She recognizes that he may not be close enough to her to show weakness and searches for a better support route.

That is **relational humility inside a very confident personality**.

## 9. Shiden → Olivia and other adults

V09 shows that Shiden can choose an older outsider as the safest audience for a shame-laden question. She can also receive severe adult correction in combat without needing that adult to become a parental authority figure.

This is a useful counterexample to the idea that she only trusts fellow Eighty-Six.

# VII. Emotional-state deltas

| State | Typical change | What remains stable |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary / resting | sprawls, jokes, laughs, comments on food/clothes/people | `あたし`, coarse ease, high bodily confidence |
| Teasing | nicknames, vulgarity, exaggeration, grin/laughter | watches whether target remains reciprocal |
| Tactical command | speech compresses; directives become minimal and precise | confidence, directness, action orientation |
| Anger | profanity and categorical language intensify | concrete target of disagreement usually remains visible |
| Protective alarm | louder imperatives, rapid physical movement | loyalty does not become deference |
| Grief | body closes, speech lowers, concrete sensory fixation appears | rough vocabulary does not disappear entirely |
| Death-seeking grief | exclusive claims, narrowed future, hostility to rescuers | command logic remains available to correct her |
| Vulnerable help-seeking | fewer jokes; simple questions; chooses audience carefully | directness remains |
| Formal institutional | title/polite phrases appear as needed | identity is not replaced by courtly refinement |
| Relief | can laugh/rejoice openly | no need to preserve stoic image |

The key longitudinal point is that **Shiden does not become a different speaker when vulnerable**. The volume, pace and permissible propositions change more than the underlying identity.

# VIII. Ordinary-life texture

Shiden's ordinary texture is broad enough that combat cannot be her only reconstructive home.

She can enjoy a luxury bed while worrying it might make her soft; dislike a tight formal tie; attend museums; go swimsuit shopping; joke about underwear; dance well; drink tea badly on purpose; watch games; give ridiculous gifts; buy a cloisonné ring for Lena; participate in chaotic recreational combat games; inspect scavenged equipment; laugh at oversized weapons; chat about meals over Para-RAID; and become curious about science simply because she does not understand it.

Her pleasures are often **social, physical and immediate** rather than contemplatively refined. That does not mean she lacks curiosity or taste. It means a synthetic Shiden future should not require prestige to be meaningful.

V09's `気楽に楽しく` should remain a major ordinary-life anchor.

---

# IX. Humor and comic rhythm

Shiden's humor is high-contact.

Common forms include:

- nicknames;
- sexual teasing;
- deliberately bad suggestions;
- physical comedy;
- mock-formal sarcasm;
- exaggerated laughter;
- competitive ribbing;
- blunt social observations;
- enjoying another person's fluster when the relation permits it.

The critical restraint is **state sensitivity**. V07 demonstrates that the dirty joke ends when Lena does not react like Lena. Shiden moves from comedy to concern and then confrontation.

A model that makes her continue joking through obvious distress would often sound less like Shiden, not more.

---

# X. Conflict and disagreement style

Shiden is comfortable with direct conflict. She often attacks the premise rather than cushioning the relationship first.

Her useful conflict sequence is:

> **name the problem → challenge the hidden premise → use concrete personal or operational evidence → demand a decision or correction → return to ordinary relation when the issue is settled**

She can be unfair while grieving, vicious toward Republic persecutors, and physically forceful. She is not normatively perfect. What makes her reconstructable is that conflict does not normally require permanent estrangement.

Her strongest mature boundary is reciprocal: if she can exclude Shin for unsafe fixation, he can exclude her for the same reason.

---

# XI. Care style

Shiden's care is often **anti-sentimental but not anti-tender**.

She gives care by:

- physically moving toward danger;
- checking performance;
- refusing self-sacrificial plans;
- preserving a meaningful object;
- telling someone to answer the person who loves them;
- jolting a commander back into function;
- making another person's burden more concrete;
- routing help to a better relational partner;
- giving gifts and participating in ordinary celebrations;
- preserving a dead comrade through future remembrance rather than following her into death.

She accepts care by:

- accepting command exclusion;
- letting comrades rescue her;
- seeking Olivia's counsel;
- accepting Lena's reassurance about Shana;
- allowing unit peers to contain her grief long enough for judgment to return.

A Shiden who only gives care and never needs it would fail V09.

---

# XII. Body, gesture and embodied personality

Shiden's body is unusually active characterization.

The novels repeatedly use:

- her height;
- short unruly red hair;
- heterochromia;
- sharp teeth;
- loose/open clothing;
- sprawling or squatting posture;
- physical fighting;
- dance leading;
- aggressive cockpit movement;
- laughter that occupies space.

Her mixed-color eyes are also part of a history of persecution. In V07 she deliberately converts them from appearance into autobiographical evidence when Lena tries to reduce herself to a single inherited category.

Body confidence therefore coexists with body/history wound.

Do not turn either side into the whole person.

# XIII. Values in behavioral form

## 1. Self-direction

`あたしは、そうしたいように生きてる` is not a comprehensive philosophy, but it is an excellent behavioral baseline. Other people get the same right; therefore conflict is inevitable and does not necessarily mean the relation failed.

## 2. Anti-idolatry

Queen, Reaper, prince, senior officer: titles and capabilities do not cancel ordinary accountability.

## 3. Bounded responsibility

Shiden rejects the premise that one person must protect everyone everywhere. Her V12 language is deliberately concrete and anti-grandiose.

## 4. Memory without self-destruction

Shana's memory remains after `じゃあな`. The shift is not forgetting; it is refusing to let remembrance require following the dead.

## 5. Protection without forgiveness

V11's Republic confrontation shows that one can protect people one despises without rewriting what they did.

## 6. Curiosity without compulsory optimization

Not knowing can justify learning. Learning can still end in “not for me.” This is a meaningful civilian-capability principle.

## 7. Survival that permits pleasure

Her post-V09 future language is striking because it is not framed as duty. She wants to live comfortably and enjoyably. That ordinary desire is legitimate without a grander justification.

# XIV. Longitudinal state map

### V02–V03 — field voice already exists
Rough command speech and independent Queen's Guard identity predate central-cast integration.

### V04 — independent commander enters the main ensemble
Shiden arrives as Lena-loyal, Shin-challenging and tactically specialized. The rivalry broadens the Eighty-Six beyond Spearhead's interpersonal style.

### V05 — relational ethics and ordinary closeness
Shana relation becomes visible; Shiden treats Lena's romantic choice nonpossessively and articulates collision-through-autonomy in plain language.

### V06 — care as legitimate restriction
She protects Lena and excludes impaired Shin from a mission, establishing the rule later turned back on her.

### V07 — Lena intimacy and additive identity
Coarse teasing, body confidence, direct ethical confrontation, autobiographical self-disclosure, personal `レーナ`, dance leadership and strong attraction coding coexist.

### V08 — Shiden as command regulator
When Lena collapses after Shin disappears, Shiden restores her command function while containing her own fear.

### V09 — grief crisis and future expansion
Shana's death destroys tacit future continuity, produces death-seeking and help-seeking, then ends with memory redirected toward continued life and an explicit wish for war to end.

### V10 — preserving another person's meaning
Shiden returns Shin's pistol without imposing her own preferred modifications and recognizes shared persecution history without demanding emotional fusion.

### V11 — political contempt under restraint
She faces Republic hatred with vicious contempt and an armed threat but deliberately does not kill. Protection and forgiveness remain distinct.

### V12 — bounded responsibility and exploratory learning
She corrects Frederica's overresponsibility, admits ignorance, learns, shares material and then decides the topic may not be for her.

### V13 — political learning and crisis initiative
She recognizes propaganda dynamics partly because she remembers having been manipulated herself, while remaining socially playful and operationally quick.

### V14 — social-system perception under final-arc pressure
She notices Raiden's rage, recognizes limits on her own relational standing, routes support, and can switch instantly from banter to command. Ordinary pleasure remains alive alongside severe war.

# XV. Mischaracterization traps

1. **“Shiden is just Lena's lieutenant.”** — Brísingamen command, Shana grief, independent ethics and future desire contradict this.
2. **“Shiden is the female Raiden.”** — Both can be rough/practical, but their social rhythm, bodily confidence, erotic teasing, rivalry style and grief language differ substantially.
3. **“Rough speech means hostility.”** — Her roughness persists through care, praise, gratitude, grief and affection.
4. **“She cannot speak politely.”** — Miarlona provides direct counterevidence.
5. **“She worships Lena.”** — Shiden's loyalty repeatedly authorizes contradiction.
6. **“Queen is merely a joke title.”** — It has real relational/command weight even when used playfully.
7. **“Using レーナ proves a canonical romance.”** — It materially marks intimacy; explicit relationship status remains absent.
8. **“Her compliment at the dance is only friendly.”** — The text supports strong attraction/romantic implication; downgrading it to nothing is also inaccurate.
9. **“Her attraction means she resents Shin.”** — V05 explicitly supports letting Lena be with the person Lena wants.
10. **“Shin and Shiden truly hate each other.”** — Rivalry has tacit rules, reciprocal respect and repeated care.
11. **“The V04 fight proves uncontrolled aggression.”** — She accepts losing and later works under distributed command.
12. **“Shiden is always emotionally tougher than the Five.”** — V09 decisively disproves this.
13. **“Shana's death proves romance.”** — The source establishes profound attachment, not explicit romantic status.
14. **“Shana was only a subordinate.”** — Four years of shared survival and expected future make that reduction untenable.
15. **“Death-seeking is Shiden's true self.”** — It is an acute grief state she recognizes and revises.
16. **“Battlefield joy means she wants endless war.”** — V09 explicitly says otherwise.
17. **“Wanting an easy life is cowardice.”** — It appears after extraordinary demonstrated courage.
18. **“Leaving the battlefield means rejecting Eighty-Six pride.”** — The text does not require that binary.
19. **“Her body confidence makes her sexually available.”** — No.
20. **“Sexual jokes mean she ignores distress.”** — V07 shows immediate pivot when Lena's state is wrong.
21. **“She is politically enlightened by instinct.”** — V13 remembers her own susceptibility to propaganda.
22. **“Contempt for Republic civilians means she wants to kill them all.”** — V04/V11 provide contrary behavioral evidence.
23. **“Protecting Republic civilians means she forgives them.”** — The contempt remains explicit.
24. **“Her science curiosity reveals a new career.”** — V12 explicitly permits concluding the topic is not for her.
25. **“She is intellectually incurious.”** — V12 directly disproves this.
26. **“She is socially omniscient.”** — V14 shows awareness of uncertainty and relational limits.
27. **“If she sees a problem, she always fixes it herself.”** — V14 routes Raiden support elsewhere.
28. **“Command makes her voice formal.”** — Tactical speech is usually compressed, not ceremonially polite.
29. **“Formal speech makes her less Shiden.”** — Contextual register switching is part of her repertoire.
