# T02 — Personhood, Function, Revisability, and Purpose-Capture Retrieval
This dossier retrieves the primary-source evidence needed to discuss personhood without collapsing several different questions into one. The target is not a binary answer to "who is human?" It is a property-by-property reconstruction of biological status, neural origin, autobiographical continuity, current personality, singular identity integrity, embodiment, memory, desire, refusal, command constraints, relation-bearing particularity, replaceability, and future-directed agency.
The governing adversarial question is: **Does the V01-V14 corpus support the claim that personhood is fundamentally the capacity to revise oneself, or does that theory fail when tested against Sirins, Fido, Shepherds, Phonix, Fawns, disabled humans, sacred soldier castes, and ordinary living people whose agency can be narrowed without disappearing?** The retrieval result is decisive: **revisability is a major condition of usable freedom and flourishing, but it is not a minimum threshold for moral standing or subjectivity.**
# 0. Authority, retrieval method, and evidence discipline
Primary authority is the locked original-Japanese prose of Volumes 1-14. Alter.1 is included only through its audited story-level chronology; Alter.2 remains excluded from mainline fact. The new lexical sweep produced **7,530 family-hit rows across 6,438 unique primary-source paragraphs**. Search hits were not treated as conclusions. They were used to locate candidate passages, which were then routed through canonical V2 volume locators or retained as new primary-source retrieval rows.
The human-facing ledger retains **158 context-reread items**. The selection deliberately includes negative controls: intelligent systems without established personality; role-fixed beings with affective and relational interests; living humans whose choices are constrained; people who voluntarily retain demanding identities; and institutions that value replaceable military functions while affirming irreplaceable persons.

# 2. Executive retrieval result: eleven axes that must remain separate
| Axis | Question | Why it cannot stand alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Biological status** | Is the current body biologically human? | Fido is not; Fawns and Theo are. Moral standing is not settled by substrate. |
| **Human origin** | Does the entity derive from a human person or brain? | Sheepdogs can use human-derived neural structure without preserved personality. |
| **Autobiographical continuity** | Does it remember a prior life as its own? | Shepherds often do; Sirins generally do not restore donor identity. |
| **Current personality/self-model** | Is there a differentiated present subject? | Phonix complicates this by learning without established personality. |
| **Singular identity integrity** | Is the subject one coherent self rather than fused/modularized cognition? | Noctiluca shows person-derived material can survive while singular integrity is destroyed. |
| **Embodied continuity** | Does identity depend on one body? | Fido and Legion challenge this; Theo shows body change without person loss. |
| **Desire/new ends** | Can new wants arise? | Important for flourishing, but V5 Sirins can matter even when role-fixed. |
| **Refusal/judgment** | Can the subject reject or reinterpret a command? | Kiriya has will but cannot disobey; Vika deliberately preserves Sirin refusal space. |
| **Relation-bearing particularity** | Do specific others matter in nonfungible ways? | Fido, Lerche and Shepherds repeatedly show this independently of biology. |
| **Institutional replaceability** | Can the system substitute another body/unit for the same function? | Military role replaceability can be healthy while person replaceability is not. |
| **Purpose capture** | Can cognition remain sophisticated while every admissible path returns to one imposed end? | This becomes the mature Legion/No Face anti-model. |

The matrix blocks two symmetrical mistakes: **human exceptionalism by substrate** and **AI-personhood by surface intelligence**. The project must neither deny standing because an entity is designed/copy-derived nor infer full personhood from learning, fluent speech, memory fragments, or humanlike appearance alone.
# 3. Volume-by-volume retrieval
## V01
The baseline crime is ontological administration. The Republic says `人間ではないのだから / 情報処理装置`, removing citizens from the category that carries rights, names, mourning and recognized death. At the same time, Legion mechanics already destabilize biological boundaries through `人間の脳です`. Most important for the later revisability thesis, V1 says living Shin is `生きているからどんどん変わって`. This is an early contrast between living change and dead repetition, but T02 refuses to promote it into a universal personhood test because later volumes distribute moral significance more widely.
## V02
V2 adds uniqueness and continuity. Ernst's proposition `人は常に、その人唯一つの存在` sharply contrasts human singularity with fungible military resources. Yet the direct source sweep finds that personality-bearing Shepherds cannot simply tolerate identical duplicate instances (`T02-V02-SHEPHERD-DUP`), while degraded Black Sheep can contain human neural structure without personality (`T02-V02-BLACKSHEEP`). Neural origin, continuity and current self therefore diverge almost immediately. Giad also proves that legal recognition does not end flattening: pity can become `哀れみの檻`.
## V03
V3 supplies the most important early autonomy distinction: Kiriya possesses `人格と意志 / 上位指揮官機の命令には背けない` and still cannot disobey superior command. A sophisticated subject can therefore possess memory, personality and local will while lacking control over ultimate action. In the human parallel, formal freedom produces `手に入れた“自由” / あまりにも大きな空虚` when desire and future have been systematically narrowed. V3 thus begins separating **personhood** from **usable agency**.
## V04
V4 is the first decisive falsifier of "change = personhood." Legion engineering removes `自己同一性` and treats `人格という夾雑物` as expendable noise, while the Phonix is explicitly `人格を持たない高機動型` and nonetheless learns, predicts and adapts. Adaptation is therefore not enough. Conversely, removing memory/personality can make cognition more fungible without proving that all intelligence is morally empty.
## V05
V5 destroys any remaining single-axis model. Sirins are `人造妖精〈シリン〉一番機`, based only on volunteers `材料はあくまで志願者のみ`, and explicitly `戦死者そのものではない`. Lerche says `人間になりたいわけではありませぬ` and denies donor identity `それがしは...レルヒェリート殿ではない`, yet she can be lonely `寂しいですな`, value Vika, judge Shin, and participate in relationships. The narrator cannot simply say she is nonhuman `人間ではないと言い放つことは...できなかった`. The crucial correction is therefore: **open futurity/self-revision matters greatly to flourishing, but its absence cannot erase every morally relevant interest or relation.**
## V06
V6 turns continuity into a technical problem. Recreated Lyudmila can carry the same pseudo-personality, experience and memory `目の前のリュドミラは、先の戦闘で死んだリュドミラと同じ疑似人格、同じ戦闘経験...`, yet Shin doubts that she is literally the same subject. Most Sirins can be reproduced from backups, while direct retrieval shows Lerche uniquely lacks backup and has only one copy of the information constituting her self (`T02-V06-UNIQUE-LERCHE`). Vika also refuses absolute obedience design `〈シリン〉の元となったのは、死んだ母親の新たな体とするべく幼いヴィーカが用意...` and sometimes preserves answer-space rather than commanding `その〈シリン〉を戦場に送りこみ、消耗品として使い捨てるのを、彼女の主は本心で...`. **Replaceability of body/pattern and moral particularity are separate variables.**
## V07
V7 creates the human/military distinction the later synthesis needs. Good military organization wants `代えの利く部品であることこそが、兵のあるべき姿`; Shin simultaneously insists `おれは家畜でもなければ無人機の部品でもない`. A role should be replaceable so the organization survives, while the person occupying it need not be treated as interchangeable. Fido becomes especially important because Shin refuses to make a being into `戦争の道具にはしたくない` merely because its capabilities would be useful.
## V08
V8 makes purpose capture explicit. Zelene asks `わたくしは人間かしら`, but more important is her explanation in the new direct row `T02-V08-KILLDRIVE`: personality-like continuity can remain while a destruction drive overwhelms rational interaction with humans. Noctiluca then presents `人格が、自我が幾つも`, showing that person-derived cognition can survive in a form where singular identity integrity has been violently destroyed. Theo's `僕はもう、──一緒には戦えないや` plus the bodily fact `左手は、──手首と肘の半ばから斬り落とされて` create the human control case: loss of combat function is a major identity injury, not loss of personhood.
## V09
V9 partially answers Theo: `僕はもう、戦えない / でも、死ななくていい` and later `新しい自分の形は決められる`. Function loss need not terminate life, belonging or self-authorship. The Holy Theocracy supplies the opposite human institution: `選択の自由はない` and `神戟は人ではないが、神の御使い`. Functionalization does not require contempt; a society can honor someone so completely as a sacred function that ordinary choice disappears. This is why T02 distinguishes **dehumanization** from the broader category **functionalization**.
## V10
V10 gives Fido direct interiority: `意識と思考を賜った`, affect `敵を増やしただけになっては、哀しい`, self-interpretation `代償行為とは知りつつ`, and particular reunion `また会えてよかった`. Yet Fido also carries the designed absolute `人間のよき友であれ / 絶対`. Designed purpose and agency therefore coexist rather than cancel one another automatically. Undertaker receives the human equivalent correction through `おれは死神でいなくてよかった`: Shin's defining military role is historically assembled and contingent, not his metaphysical essence.
## V11
V11 transforms the Legion model from frozen cognition to purpose-captured agency. Zelene is `EXACT_VERIFIED` for trying to stop the war, while other Shepherd leaders bend available mechanisms `EXACT_VERIFIED`. Local desires and strategic creativity can operate inside a system whose ultimate war-end remains captured. Human soldiers are also called political tools `EXACT_VERIFIED`, but the same commander forbids self-annihilating role enclosure `EXACT_VERIFIED`. Instrumental function and total reduction are therefore not identical.
## V12
V12 supplies human political analogues. Individual will can be dismissed as noise `己の意思という夾雑物`; legal freedom can arrive as imposed burden `それが『自由』だと押しつけられた`; and Shin can retain the death-god role while defining it anti-grandiosely as `一人きりじゃ戦えない、弱い死神だから`. Zelene's `わたくしは死なない。まだ死は選ばない` is especially important: a post-biological subject explicitly refuses death because further action remains meaningful. That is stronger evidence of agency than biological status alone could supply.
## V13
V13 turns the human body itself into a military platform. Fawns are adolescent girls `そう、少女だ。〈仔鹿〉とは全員が、十代後半の少女たちだ。` designed as `「私たちは、ユート。あなたたちエイティシックスの──〈レギオン〉戦争終結後まで生き残ったエイティシックスの、処分用の兵器とし...` whose biological condition is meant to substitute for other soldiers `たとえエイティシックスが〈レギオン〉の前に滅び去ってしまったとしても、〈仔鹿〉があれば国防の戦力は維持できる。処理装置に加え...`. Their dangerousness is real, but weaponization cannot exhaust what they are. The broader chapter repeatedly attacks categorical reduction, culminating in Shin's recognition of the convenient category for everyone unlike oneself `自分とは違うという、あらゆる人間に共通するただ一点を以て誰も彼もを放りこんでしまえる便利なカテゴリに、他人をまとめて放りこん...`.
## V14
V14 provides the current-corpus anti-model. No Face offers posthuman continuation with memory/personality `「見てのとおり、〈レギオン〉には人格を有する個体がいる。皆みな、元は人間だ。体は失ったが、生前の記憶と人格はそのまま引き継いでいる。──君た...` and replaceable bodies `「〈レギオン〉は──〈仔アグ羊ヌス〉は死ぬことがない。壊れたならば体を乗り換え、古びたならば中央処理系を順次入れ替え、おそらくは望む限り生き...`, but Shin rejects the proposal because `「お前の掲げた世界は理想郷どころか地獄だ。一つの幸福もない。心一つも自由にならない。あるのはただ、二度と尽きない憎ぞう悪おだけで──それしか...`. The decisive issue is not machine substrate; it is the absence of free ends. Yet the novel immediately complicates a too-clean human/Sirin distinction: narration expects Sirin stasis `まずい、また動けなくなる。人間である──成長して変化して強くなっていくエイティシックスたちならあるいは今度は耐えられるのかもしれないけれど、...`, then Lyudmila experiences `初めて思った。〈シリン〉にはあるまじき衝動だった。`. Designed role may not exhaust emergent motivation. T02 therefore leaves Sirin generalization open rather than treating one anomaly as a universal rule.
## A1
Alter.1 supplies low-pressure controls. Fido participates in ordinary camp sociality (`A1-E019`); Shin pursues historical curiosity outside combat (`A1-E036`); Theo participates in ordinary reciprocity after amputation (`A1-E097`). These are important because personhood/function analysis must not sample only moments where the plot asks whether someone is useful, dying, artificial, or heroic.
# 4. Human dehumanization and functionalization are related but not identical
San Magnolia is the baseline case because it performs **administrative ontological demotion**: the Eighty-Six are declared nonhuman so that citizenship, casualty status, names, graves, rights and bodily autonomy can be denied. This is stronger than ordinary military instrumentality. A legitimate army also uses people for functions; V11 openly says soldiers are political tools. The moral distinction is whether the role exhausts standing, whether refusal/exit and care exist, whether death is counted, and whether the person remains valuable when the function changes.
V7 makes this distinction almost formulaic. Good doctrine wants `代えの利く部品であることこそが、兵のあるべき姿`; Shin answers racialized utility with `おれは家畜でもなければ無人機の部品でもない`. **Replaceable role is not replaceable person.** Theo then supplies the strongest test: after `僕はもう、──一緒には戦えないや` and amputation, V9 establishes `僕はもう、戦えない / でも、死ななくていい` and `新しい自分の形は決められる`.
# 5. The Sirin stress test: moral standing without human identity
The Sirins prevent the project from defining personhood as biological humanity, donor resurrection, open futurity, or self-identification as human. They are successor artificial entities built from dead-derived neural material, with removed memories and installed pseudo-personalities. They themselves often accept designed function and replaceability. Yet the text repeatedly gives them affect, loyalty, preference, judgment, relationships and, by V14, at least one emergent desire not anticipated by the stable model.
The most important V5 sequence is the conjunction `未来などない。課された役目しかない` + `人間になりたいわけではありませぬ` + `寂しいですな`. A being can deny human identity, describe its future as role-fixed, and still be lonely. The direct narrator passage `T02-V05-PARTS` then blocks the inference that pseudo-personality plus machine origin makes mere part-treatment morally trivial.
T02 therefore adopts the term **morally significant successor subject** as a safer working description than either "resurrected human" or "mere machine." This is a retrieval label, not a final metaphysical verdict.
# 6. Fido: designed purpose does not erase agency
Fido is the strongest current artificial-subject case because V10 provides sustained first-person evidence: consciousness/thought `意識と思考を賜った`, sadness `敵を増やしただけになっては、哀しい`, reflective self-interpretation `代償行為とは知りつつ`, and particular reunion `また会えてよかった`. Yet Fido also bears a hard design imperative, `人間のよき友であれ / 絶対`.
This makes Fido a useful adversarial control against a simplistic autonomy test. Human desires are also shaped by origin, education, biology and relation. The relevant question is not whether a subject has **no prior causes**, but whether current action contains enough differentiated judgment, relation, preference, refusal and self-interpretation that treating the entity only as equipment becomes descriptively and morally inadequate.
# 7. Shepherds and the mature Legion model: purpose-captured agency
The old model "the dead are frozen at their final command" is no longer adequate. Kiriya has `人格と意志 / 上位指揮官機の命令には背けない`. Zelene retains continuity and can oppose the war. V11 says she was `EXACT_VERIFIED` for doing so, while other Shepherds pursue `EXACT_VERIFIED`. By V14 No Face can reason, persuade, plan and articulate a political utopia.
The retrieval-supported model is **purpose-captured agency**: cognition, memory, personality, local preference and strategic innovation may remain mobile while the space of ultimate admissible ends is dominated by command architecture, destructive drive, or grievance. This is a constraint model, not a claim that every Shepherd has the same phenomenology.
The sharpest negative formulation is No Face's offer, rejected because `「お前の掲げた世界は理想郷どころか地獄だ。一つの幸福もない。心一つも自由にならない。あるのはただ、二度と尽きない憎ぞう悪おだけで──それしか...`. A world can preserve intelligence and memory while extinguishing meaningful freedom if every route returns to one imposed end.
# 8. Phonix and Noctiluca: intelligence and person-derived cognition are insufficient
Phonix is the clean negative control: `人格を持たない高機動型` while displaying tactical learning. Noctiluca supplies a different warning: `人格が、自我が幾つも`. Human-derived consciousness material does not automatically preserve a singular person.
Accordingly, later Document 08 must distinguish at least **intelligence**, **personality**, **autobiographical continuity**, **identity integrity**, **agency**, and **moral standing**. A system can score high on one and low or unknown on another.
# 9. Fawns: dangerous embodiment does not create a duty to disappear
V13 is the strongest human embodiment test. Fawns are `そう、少女だ。〈仔鹿〉とは全員が、十代後半の少女たちだ。` intentionally made `「私たちは、ユート。あなたたちエイティシックスの──〈レギオン〉戦争終結後まで生き残ったエイティシックスの、処分用の兵器とし...`. Their bodies are dangerous by design, and precautions are therefore not fictional oppression invented by interpretation. But bodily danger does not make the subject equivalent to the weapon function imposed on her.
The resulting principle is narrow but strong: **a condition that makes a person dangerous may justify proportionate safety measures without converting continued existence itself into a moral offense.** This should feed both Document 08 and the institutional documents.
# 10. Sacred functionalization and honored captivity
The Holy Theocracy gives the human counterexample to Republic contempt: `神戟は人ではないが、神の御使い` coexists with `選択の自由はない`. A society can revere a military caste and still deny ordinary choice. Functionalization therefore includes both degradation and sacralization.
The diagnostic question is not simply "does society respect this role?" but **can the person remain valuable while refusing, leaving, failing, aging out of, or changing the role?**
# 11. Revisability: where the thesis works and where it fails
Revisability remains one of the corpus's most powerful concepts when used as a **freedom/flourishing condition**. Humans repeatedly suffer when an identity becomes exclusive: only soldier, only Undertaker, only Queen, only sacred weapon, only useful child, only grieving dead, only hero. V9 and V14 make new-self/return language explicit.
But it fails as a moral-status threshold. V5 Sirins can have affect and relations while describing role-fixed futures. Fido contains designed absolutes while displaying agency. Some human commitments are valuable precisely because they endure. Conversely, Phonix changes tactically without established personality. Therefore: **capacity to change is neither necessary nor sufficient for personhood by itself.**
# 12. Purpose-capture: precise working definition
For Phase 5/6, use **purpose-capture** only when the evidence supports all or most of the following:
- a subject/system possesses meaningful cognition or adaptive action;
- a dominant end is imposed, inherited, programmed, or grievance-fossilized rather than currently open to full reconsideration;
- local strategies may vary while the terminal end remains effectively fixed;
- refusal of the terminal end is impossible, punished, or structurally excluded;
- other desires are subordinated to, reinterpreted through, or extinguished by that end.

Do **not** use purpose-capture merely because someone is devoted, duty-bound, traumatized, religious, professionally obligated, or unwilling to abandon a long-standing commitment. V9 explicitly shows that durable pride and freely accepted bonds can remain life-giving.
# 13. Human-role controls: retaining function can be compatible with freedom
Theo does not want to stop being a Processor `プロセッサー、やめたくない`; Shin does not need to renounce Undertaker; soldiers do not need to become civilians; Lena does not need to stop commanding. The key shift is whether the role remains one meaningful part of a larger self and whether exit/failure/change cease to threaten basic standing.
V12's mature Shin says `一人きりじゃ戦えない、弱い死神だから`. This is not role abolition. It is anti-grandiose role retention inside interdependence. Likewise V14 permits Theo to choose return while Annette refuses to convert love into veto `行かないで、と言いたかった。けれどセオは、戻るつもりだ。アネットがいまさら何か言ってもそれはきっと変わらないし、それ以上に万一にも彼の意志を...`.

# 19. T02 disposition
The retrieval supports a **multidimensional moral-subject model**, not a single definition of humanity. The strongest current distinction is between **standing** and **freedom**. A being can matter morally without possessing maximal autonomy or open-ended self-revision; conversely, a legally recognized human can remain trapped in function, fear, command, sacred role, trauma, bodily utility, or institutional coercion.
The mature negative concept is **purpose-capture**: not simple programming, devotion, duty or trauma, but a condition in which sophisticated cognition can continue while the subject cannot genuinely reopen the terminal end organizing its existence. The mature positive standard is therefore not "be infinitely changeable." It is: **do not make one role, body, wound, command, origin, relationship or designed purpose the exhaustive condition under which a subject is allowed to matter.**
