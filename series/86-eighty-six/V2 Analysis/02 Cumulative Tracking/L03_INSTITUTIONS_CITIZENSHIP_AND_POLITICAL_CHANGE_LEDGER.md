## V01 institutional proposition

San Magnolia does not require citizens to literally believe an Eighty-Six body is nonhuman. It requires institutions that allow them to **act as though the person's humanity is irrelevant to law, casualty accounting, resource allocation, and memory**.

## V02 political correction

“Giad is better but imperfect” is now too weak. The important point is **how** it is better: its institutions can recognize persons, test claims, count dead, revise knowledge and preserve legal choice. Its failures arise not from absence of personhood law but from social hierarchy, pity, usefulness and wartime pressure **inside** a person-recognizing order.

## V03 political correction

The series now distinguishes **three dehumanizing pathways**: hostile exclusion (Republic), pity-script exclusion (V02 Giad society), and protective/utility exclusion (V03 Giad institutions). Moral progress is real because Giad has procedures capable of identifying and resisting the latter two; those risks do not disappear simply because law recognizes personhood.

# Volume 07 update

- **Giad care:** leave, school, mental-health observation, rotation and protected downtime are real institutional goods. V7 simultaneously refuses institutional self-congratulation: Willem calls part of the arrangement `所詮、こんなものはアリバイ作りだ`, acknowledging political insurance and reputational management alongside genuine care. **Working rule:** mixed motive does not nullify material benefit; humane outcomes must be evaluated separately from institutional self-image.
- **Republic restoration politics:** the visiting Republic officer uses Lena's command success as proof that the old system of Alba command over expendable Eighty-Six should be restored, while claiming concern for his own students. His logic preserves national/racial risk transfer rather than citizenship equality. **Status:** P004 Republic habits surviving collapse strongly strengthened.
- **Heroic public status:** the Strike Package's survival and symbolic value make it a morale object that must remain `英雄であり続けなければならぬ部隊`. Heroization becomes institutional pressure rather than merely admiration.
- **Frederica and sovereignty:** the Legion shutdown route requires genetic authorization from the old Giad imperial line, turning Frederica's inherited status into strategic infrastructure. The better republic therefore inherits a live constitutional/military problem from monarchy rather than cleanly superseding it.
- **Vika:** retaining a royal identity because he wants it (`そう在りたい`) provides a counterexample to the assumption that freedom always requires shedding inherited office/status.
- **Wald/Fleet Countries diplomacy:** the proposed offensive and intelligence-sharing framework show anti-Legion coalition politics operating through different state capacities rather than one hegemonic model. Track actual burden-sharing rather than regime labels.

# Volume 11 update — state collapse, evacuation hierarchy, and political rescue

## San Magnolia

- Evacuation reproduces elite/racial hierarchy even during extinction: senior elites and preferred phenotypes receive priority.
- Kurena's `エイティシックスじゃなくてもよかったんだ` establishes that the expendability mechanism can reproduce inside Alba categories; structural analogy must not become historical equivalence.
- Childhood montage traces scapegoating through preexisting `有色種` language + wartime uncertainty + visible casualty asymmetry + political explanation needs.
- Republic civilians are differentiated moral actors; state culpability does not imply equal personal culpability.

## Giad Federacy

- Strategic withdrawal, logistics, transparent bad-news reporting, medical support and command redundancy materially save lives.
- Richard explicitly treats humanitarian rescue as **political capital** (`正義の美名`) while personally paying the operational cost. Mixed motive does not nullify real duty.
- `軍も軍人も、政のための道具だ` confirms military instrumentality under legitimate politics; ethical distinction lies in limits, citizenship, accountability, and whether the whole life belongs to the role.
- Grethe is ordered to absorb some cruel command decisions rather than forcing child soldiers to own every moral burden.

**V11 institutional delta:** the mature comparison is no longer good-state/bad-state but whether political institutions preserve persons while necessarily using roles, incentives, coercion, and public narratives.

# Volume 12 update — formal liberty, usable agency, and institutional ambivalence

## Federacy strengths

- legal equality, public education and qualification replace hereditary role assignment;
- military education is adapted to actual learners (simplified manuals, staged instruction);
- mental-health care can remove even senior officers from duty;
- refugee volunteers are not supposed to become expendable screens (`施設は直せるけど、失われた命は取り返しがつかない`).

## Federacy complications

- an MP reduces Eighty-Six to `操縦士` and tries to exclude noncombat concerns;
- free reporting is temporarily suppressed during counterintelligence deception;
- racial backlash spreads from Republic guilt toward visible Alba generally;
- old imperial aristocratic contempt (`王`/`羊`) survives within democratic institutions;
- Ernst's self-erasing idealism makes political office itself vulnerable.

## Hail Mary / post-imperial transition

- formal equality arrives faster than civic skills/habits can be rebuilt after hereditary paternalism;
- some former subjects interpret required decision-making and education as imposed burden;
- same social background produces responsible soldiers when guidance is accessible and trust is warranted, defeating hereditary-incapacity explanations;
- Noelle demonstrates authority without sufficient epistemic/staff competence.

**V12 institutional correction:** formal rights are necessary but not self-executing. Institutions preserve freedom best when they cultivate usable judgment and recoverable error **without converting temporary support into permanent guardianship**.

# Alter.1 chronological supplemental update — infrastructure, archive, and ordinary capability

## Republic domestic order

- Domestic racism is visible at household scale: a mother treats an Eighty-Six-owned kitten as contaminated, showing racial ontology reproduced outside formal state administration.
- Material abundance inside the Eighty-Five Sectors includes the ability to keep pets and preserve civilian leisure while Eighty-Six children are denied medicine, graves and secure life.
- Recovered Processor personnel records restore names, family names, addresses and birth dates. **Archival recovery increases biographical option without compelling emotional homecoming:** Shin can know an old address and still experience it as an unfamiliar house.

## Federacy capability expansion

- Shin's early Giad behavior supplies a clean control for V12: he independently goes to a library because he found something interesting, while lacking the habit of using buses/trains and treating a three-hour walk as `徒歩圏内`.
- The issue is not lack of curiosity or judgment. **Infrastructure changes the practical cost and range of choices.**
- Birthdays, shopping, school-life, food, correspondence and leisure become repeatable because institutions provide safety, time, records and transport—not because the state manufactures humanity.

## Status and private action

- Vika notes that even an ordinary personal gift can acquire political meaning because he is royal. Formal status can constrain supposedly private choice.

**Alter.1 institutional correction:** ordinary agency predates rescue; better institutions enlarge its usable repertoire, reliability and horizon. Capability support should therefore not be narrated as civilization bestowed upon passive subjects.

# Volume 13 institutional update - regression, scapegoating, and the reproducible Sector relation

1. **P009 is now source-explicit but must remain narrow.** V13 directly states that when scatterable mines close the rear while Legion close the front, the resulting battlefield relation is no different from the battlefield San Magnolia called the Eighty-Sixth Sector (V13-L121-V13-L127). This confirms structural reproduction of **expendability + constrained refusal + blocked return**.
2. **Historical equivalence remains rejected.** San Magnolia's original system was deliberate racial denaturalization, citizenship stripping and extermination. Federacy degeneration proceeds through exhaustion, unresolved imperial cleavages, refugee politics, fear, manipulated information, desertion, coercive reserve action, rebellion and emergency authoritarian decisions.
3. **Fear is socially productive when narrativized.** V13 tracks self-deception, `we` pronoun amplification, insider/outsider line drawing, and moralization of scapegoating (V13-L052-V13-L060).
4. **The Legion conducts social-systems warfare.** No Face targets vulnerable formations and exploits existing fractures; enemy diagnosis is not omniscient narration, but subsequent collapse corroborates the operational logic (V13-L028-V13-L031, V13-L057-V13-L060).
5. **Federacy accountability does not vanish everywhere.** Rito's killer is investigated/punished, his command apologizes, and Shin refuses collective condemnation (V13-L108-V13-L111). Earlier institutional improvements remain real even amid regression.
6. **Refugee conscription reveals utility-to-desert slippage.** Real manpower scarcity is converted into the claim that politically weak `useless` people deserve greater exposure to death (V13-L043).
7. **The rebellion has antecedent coercion.** Reserve troops attempt to force refugee populations toward the front before armed resistance and the later New San Magnolia declaration (V13-L066-V13-L067).
8. **Lena's V13 civic minimum supplements V12 capabilities analysis.** Non-self-enforcing rights require institutional/behavioral maintenance, and weakness/difference must not be converted into a wish for erasure (V13-L047-V13-L051).
9. **Lena becomes hostage infrastructure.** The state plans to use her relationship with the Eighty-Six to compel continued service after trust collapses (V13-L119-V13-L120).

**V13 institutional conclusion:** the series no longer permits `Eighty-Sixth Sector` to be used only as the proper name of one historical zone, but neither does it permit it to become a vague synonym for any hardship. The comparison is strongest where a political community compels a marked group to absorb violence while materially blocking refusal and return.

# Volume 14 institutions / citizenship / political-change update

1. **Justice changes grammatical function.** `我ら、世界に誇る正義たらん` begins as an aspirational demand; the epilogue corrupts it into `我らはすなわち正義である`. Justice ceases to judge the community and becomes a title immunizing the community from judgment (V14-L001, V14-L206-V14-L209).
2. **The War History Compilation Room points the archive forward.** Its mission is `未来の戦史`: operational knowledge that may create a survivable future, not memorialization for its own sake (V14-L002-V14-L003).
3. **Willem's noblesse-oblige argument is not a regime endorsement.** Privileged houses are told to spend inherited resources because soldiers already spent their lives; fallen houses can rise again. The epilogue supplies aristocrats who instead convert crisis into hereditary entitlement (V14-L004-V14-L006, V14-L211-V14-L216).
4. **Democratic citizenship is framed as self-scrutiny.** Pirate radio ties the revolutionary slogan to a citizen's obligation not to close eyes to inconvenient facts (V14-L121-V14-L123).
5. **Civic repair occurs at kitchen scale.** Recipe exchanges between Eighty-Six and Federacy soldiers make injury and ordinary preference mutually legible without pretending structural betrayal is solved (V14-L124-V14-L131, V14-L185-V14-L190).
6. **The V12 capabilities thesis receives its strongest authoritarian falsifier.** Reactionaries argue that ordinary people are unfit for words, thought, freedom, and will and therefore should return to rule by `正当なる真紅の血統`. V14 presents this as ideological regression, not vindication of paternalism (V14-L206-V14-L216).
7. **The Federacy is neither secretly always San Magnolia nor guaranteed to remain better.** V13 demonstrated structural reproduction of the Sector; V14 demonstrates both repair attempts and authoritarian backsliding inside the same polity.
8. **Political regress remains possible after moral learning.** The final arc's human revisability thesis includes negative revision: people can `return` from cruelty, but societies can also return to empire.

**V14 institutional correction:** a just polity is not one whose members possess a morally superior essence. It is one whose institutions, language, and citizens remain capable of **being corrected by standards they do not own**.
