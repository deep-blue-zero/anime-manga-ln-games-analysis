- **Shin:** `別に死にたいわけじゃないからな` — V02 death-aversion remains linguistically stable.

- **Frederica:** `先に進めぬ、ではない。戻れぬ、であろ。` — archaic register carries sharp distinction between future and restoration.

## New terminology

- `自己同一性` — self-identity as obstacle to fungible replication.
- `人格` — personality/personhood-like individuality; must distinguish from raw intelligence.
- `夾雑物` — “impurity/admixture”; machine-production view of personality.
- `高機動型 / フォニクス` — adaptive combat AI explicitly without personality at V04 boundary.
- `牧羊犬 / シープドッグ` — mass-produced high-intelligence Legion through identity removal.
- `資産 / 全機` — Republic refugee language retaining Eighty-Six as inventory.
- `嗜好品の同情` — pity as consumable moral product.

## V04 voice principle

Shin's development remains **semantic rather than stylistic**: he does not become ornate or confessional; his existing plain register becomes capable of trust, delegation and request. Lena's opposite risk is that increasingly forceful language can make a strong interpretation sound more certain than the text establishes.

# Volume 06 Japanese voice / terminology update

## High-value Shin language

- `「今のままでは──いられない」` - change becomes relational necessity, not repudiation of pride.
- `『おいていかないでください』` - direct abandonment petition enters Shin's otherwise restrained register.
- `「貴女に海を見せたい。見たことのない景色を。戦争が終わらなければ、見られない景色を。だから、戦争...` - future moves from `show` toward a jointly spoken postwar destination.
- `「誰かと生きたい。どちらかなんて、選ばない。……おれはまだ、」` / `「生きてるから」` - additive `and` logic: retain pride and claim shared life because he is still alive.
- `「今日は、帰ります。……待っている人がいますから」` - ordinary return language closes the volume.

## Lena

- `「信じたいんです。この世界にはまだ、誰もが幸せに生きられる世界に、変われる可能性が残って...` - `believe/want to believe` marks normative commitment rather than empirical innocence.
- `「だから、帰ってきなさい。絶対に。わたしはおいていきませんから、──あなたも必ず、帰ってきなさい...` - command register becomes reciprocal return promise.

## New conceptual vocabulary

- pride/curse adjacency: identity gives meaning and can bind.
- total freedom is rejected; future analysis should focus on revisability and alternative possible selves.
- home/return language shifts from geographical origin toward a place/person where arrival is awaited.

# Alter.1 chronological supplemental update — low-pressure register corpus

Alter.1 materially improves the voice corpus because many scenes remove combat urgency.

- **Shin:** pre-Lena dry jokes, teasing and bad-cooking embarrassment directly weaken the “emotionless before Lena” caricature. `同じものが見られれば` during V1 gives an early plain-form shared-perception wish; later `おいていかれない` names relational safety without ornate confession.
- **Lena:** shopping/food speech with Annette, flustered jealousy, gift reactions and birthday embarrassment demonstrate a private adolescent register continuous with—but not reducible to—officer/Queen speech.
- **Frederica:** `言葉の選び方もわらわの一部じゃ` explicitly makes register an identity claim; her archaizing voice is not merely decorative characterization.
- **Shiden:** blunt colloquial diagnosis such as `ずいぶん気ィ許してんじゃねえか` continues to function as high-information social perception.
- **Gift language:** Shin's `おれが贈りたかったから、贈っただけです` is a mature agency formula: desire is neither debt nor command.

**Alter.1 language correction:** crisis dialogue systematically under-samples humor, embarrassment, mundane irritation and playful intimacy. The final voice index must treat low-pressure supplemental speech as necessary longitudinal evidence.
