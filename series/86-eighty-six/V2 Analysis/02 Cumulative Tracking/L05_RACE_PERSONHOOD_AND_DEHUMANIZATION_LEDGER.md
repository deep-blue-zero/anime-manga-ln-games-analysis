## V01 personhood baseline

Volume 1's strongest personhood lesson is negative:

> **No single administrative, biological, technological, or nominal category is adequate by itself.**

The state can deny humanity to biological humans; machines can contain copied human cognition; an AI can occupy a relationship without the novel yet settling its metaphysical status.

# Volume 05 personhood update — Sirin stress test

## Major methodological revision

V05 makes a **single-axis personhood model untenable**. Track these independently:

`biological life` · `human origin` · `original-person continuity` · `autobiographical memory` · `current personality` · `self-identification` · `autonomy` · `desire` · `relational standing` · `future orientation` · `embodiment` · `replaceability` · `capacity to refuse`.

| Entity | V05 evidence | Working status |
|---|---|---|
| **Sirin generally** | voluntary dead donors; artificial neural reconstruction; memories removed; pseudo-personality installed; self-identify nonhuman; differentiated relational behavior | **successor artificial entities**; donor identity explicitly denied; moral standing open but nontrivial |
| **Lerche** | not original Lercheritt; no original memory/personality; says she does not want human status; nevertheless sadness, loyalty, anger, preference and moral judgment | strongest evidence that nonhuman identity and relational/moral significance can coexist |
| **Original donor** | dies/unsalvageable before scanning; consent given in advance | donor consent governs procurement, not automatically every interest of successor entity |
| **Phönix** | adaptive, increasingly particular, apparent self-preserving behavior; no established personality | learning/optimization remains insufficient proof of selfhood |
| **Humans/Eighty-Six** | living future can be narrowed by role/pride without loss of humanity | capacity for self-revision is better treated as freedom/flourishing condition than threshold of moral status |

### V05 correction

**Self-revision/futurity is no longer usable as a minimum criterion for moral consideration.** A role-fixed being may still possess interests/relationships that matter. Conversely, high adaptive intelligence may exist without established personality.

# Volume 07 update

- **Shin's anti-objectification claim:** `おれは家畜でもなければ無人機の部品でもない` is an explicit first-person refusal of both animalization and component ontology. V7 thus moves from others recognizing his humanity toward Shin publicly asserting the category himself.
- **Dual identity and belonging:** Shin tells Lena that she is both `共和国人` and the Eighty-Six's Queen, refusing the idea that one stigmatized identity must be erased to validate another relation. This strengthens non-purification models of belonging.
- **Fido:** the possible autonomous-unmanned-war counterfactual sharpens the moral problem of artificial agents. Shin's reason for refusal is not a proof that Fido is human; it is that Fido was not made for war and is someone whose death he does not want to repeat. **Status:** moral consideration through relation/purpose strengthens without ontological collapse.
- **Zelene:** `我が生前はゼレーネ・ビルケンバウム` gives explicit continuity vocabulary across biological death and Legion existence. She preserves memory, purpose and self-reference while remaining under Legion technical constraints. **Status:** original-person continuity is stronger here than in Sirins and must be mapped separately from autonomy.
- **Frederica:** biological descent itself becomes machine-legible authorization. The body is again political/technological identity infrastructure, but here as sovereign credential rather than racial exclusion.
- **Working matrix after V07:** moral standing, memory continuity, personality, self-reference, refusal, purpose revision, embodiment, institutional status and relational recognition remain distinct dimensions. No single binary has become sufficient.

- Zelene reports exclusion from the Legion command network because she sought to stop the war.
- Current Shepherd commanders use machine architecture and logical evasions to pursue `彼ら自身の望み` beyond initial orders.
- Revenge Shepherds have memory/desire/strategy but remain **purpose-captured** by grievance; this further overturns the universal “static recording” model without proving flourishing autonomy.

**V11 personhood refinement:** agency is multidimensional. A mind may possess desire, strategic novelty and autobiographical relation while lacking sufficient capacity or willingness to revise the end around which its existence is organized.

# Volume 12 update — usable refusal, private atrocity, and will

- Republic dehumanization expands beyond public administration: Eighty-Six infants were privately purchased, tortured, sexually abused, used through Para-RAID and discarded.
- One rescued child remains unable even to conceptualize that the former master lacks authority (`今は主人の支配下にない...思い至ることすら...できない`). **Legal rescue and recognized humanity do not instantly create usable refusal.**
- Para-RAID again proves connection is not intrinsically ethical; consent and mutual standing determine whether synchronization becomes intimacy, command, surveillance or violation.
- Giad backlash after exposure of Republic crimes generalizes contempt toward Alba/white-haired people, showing category-substitution can recur without moral equivalence to San Magnolia genocide.
- Sirin epigraph calls independent will `夾雑物`; Legion Sheepdog material separately confirms human neural processing can remain after `人格と意思` are lost.
- Zelene's explicit refusal of death materially strengthens continuity, preference, future reasoning and revision capacity.

**V12 personhood correction:** moral/political freedom depends not merely on being recognized as a subject but on having a meaningful repertoire of refusal, preference and revision. Yet that does not license benevolent authorities to choose permanently on the person's behalf.
