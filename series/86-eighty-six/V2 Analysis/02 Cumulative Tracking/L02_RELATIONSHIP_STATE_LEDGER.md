## V03 relationship principle

Healthy relation in V03 is neither radical independence nor replacement-purpose dependence. ## V08 relationship principle

V08 makes **separation** the adversarial test of interdependence. Healthy relation is not demonstrated by never collapsing when someone disappears. # Volume 12 update — support versus obedience

- **Shin ↔ Lena:** Shin helps send Lena away for recovery despite missing her; after rest both directly say the other is `足りない`. Need can be verbalized without turning need into a veto on self-care.

- **Anju ↔ Dustin:** reciprocal permission to be “a little selfish” is really a mutual return contract; each asks the other not to reproduce earlier abandonment/death.

**V12 relationship principle:** support is healthiest when it expands the other's capacity and tolerates absence, limits and disagreement; obedience becomes dangerous when one person's judgment is treated as a permanent substitute for everyone else's.
