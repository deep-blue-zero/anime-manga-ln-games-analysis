## V01 doctrine correction

The V1 phrase “the Eighty-Six do not retreat” should never be treated as a cultural essence. Volume 1 establishes a **material history in which retreat was denied, punished, or meaningless**. Later reluctance to retreat must be tested as learned doctrine/habit rather than innate pride.

# Volume 07 update

- **Heroism as military pathology:** `英雄など今の世に、あるべきではない` is immediately explained as a force-design proposition: soldiers should be replaceable components because `一人の英雄で成り立つ軍` is an unacceptable institution. V7 therefore gives direct textual support to the V2 distinction between admirable individual excellence and dangerous organizational dependence.
- **Readiness/rest doctrine:** Strike Package personnel rotate school, training and leave; Shin notes that ordinary base leave cannot fully remove battlefield noise/sightlines. V7 makes recovery an operational readiness issue rather than sentimental reward.
- **Wald/Fleet Countries operational problem:** terrain, weather, visibility and local doctrine matter to the coming operation; V7's political leisure setting does not suspend military preparation.
- **Legion shutdown objective:** Zelene provides a possible whole-system termination code whose use requires imperial genetic authorization. This turns information exploitation into a potential strategic war-ending line of effort.
- **Frederica secrecy:** Shin withholds the shutdown-authority implication to protect Frederica. This is tactically understandable but creates a classic command/autonomy problem: strategic information about a person's body/status is being controlled without her participation.
- **Fido counterfactual:** Annette recognizes that completed autonomous Fido-type AI might have made genuine zero-casualty unmanned defense possible. Shin refuses to make current Fido a battlefield solution. V7 therefore distinguishes technical feasibility from morally permissible allocation of risk.

# Volume 11 update — deception, withdrawal, evacuation, and adult burden

- Legion executes continent-scale deception (`オペレーション・ネヴィル`) followed by orbital kinetic bombardment and a second Large-Scale Offensive.
- Giad's organized fallback demonstrates strategic resilience produced by doctrine, communications, logistics, rotations, rest, and supply—not heroic singularity.
- The 400-km Republic corridor uses alternating withdrawal, phased control lines, rearguard action, rail movement, and later truck substitution when rails are destroyed.
- Civilian bodily limits become operational facts; transport/logistics, not moral exhortation alone, are required.
- Richard's headquarters regiment undertakes a deliberate rearguard to buy evacuation time. Death is counted and mission-linked, not administratively erased.
- Richard explicitly prohibits `死に急ぐような戦い`, further institutionalizing survival as military requirement.
- Grethe is assigned future responsibility for cruel command decisions, distributing moral load upward.
- New Legion behavior includes sadistic anti-personnel configurations driven by revenge Shepherds, proving human-derived goals can distort ordinary machine optimization.

**Hero-dependence state after V11:** unique abilities remain strategically important, but coalition survival under D-DAY depends overwhelmingly on institutional systems rather than one irreplaceable combatant.

# Volume 12 update — the weak death god and distributed force competence

- Exact Shin formulation confirmed: `一人きりじゃ戦えない、弱い死神だから`.
- This rejects universal hero expectations rather than combat excellence.
- Dam operation demonstrates combined-arms distribution: Shin sensing/command, infantry anti-camouflage, Kurena/snipers timing windows, engineers demolition, other squadrons immobilization, logistics/recovery.
- Shin's democratic observation makes doctrine explicit: `自分以外まで支配し守護する、英雄など連邦軍には必要ない`.
- Simplified doctrine for low-literacy soldiers shows **usable knowledge** is a military capability produced institutionally.
- Hail Mary demonstrates the opposite: charismatic command + magical-weapon thinking + absent technical dissent + inability to admit uncertainty.
- Sirin epigraph identifies independent will as operational `夾雑物`; V12 as a whole shows why human judgment remains necessary despite added fog.
- Shin's sensory overload still creates a human single-point vulnerability; Grethe reframes reporting strain as commander duty.

**V12 heroism status:** C007/S009 strongly confirmed. Healthy forces value exceptional soldiers while actively refusing to let one exceptional person become the whole system.

# Alter.1 chronological supplemental update — forced self-reliance and adult redundancy

- A Claymore-squad lesson—`誰にも頼らず戦えないような子は生き残れないわ`—records **forced self-reliance under lethal institutional abandonment**, not the V5–V12 ideal of healthy distributed capability. “Independence” changes moral meaning depending on whether help is unavailable or deliberately nonexclusive.
- Eugene's astonishment at Shin's battlefield habituation reinforces V10's uneven-development model: equal age does not imply equal experiential burden.
- Immediately after the Morpho battle, adults explicitly say `次こそは、お前たちの手は必要ない`: the proper institutional aim is to make persecuted child exceptionalism unnecessary, not permanently celebrate dependence upon it.
- Low-pressure headquarters stories show command culture containing teasing, leave and peer life alongside military roles; military identity does not exhaust social identity.

**Alter.1 military correction:** C007 is strengthened from below. The opposite of unhealthy hero-dependence is not solitary competence; it is an organization whose support and redundancy make exceptional children progressively less necessary.

# Volume 13 military/doctrine update

- **Operation Overlord** is now the strategic war-termination project: seize Legion command infrastructure, establish new command authority through imperial blood, and issue stop/self-destruction orders. V13 does not yet prove the plan technically succeeds.
- **Manpower exhaustion becomes operationally decisive.** Raw emergency conscription can produce bodies able to fire weapons while predictably causing huge casualties; V13 explicitly frames such soldiers as disposable number-filling (V13-L012).
- **Fallback planning is initially professional doctrine.** Early V13 still constructs reserve defensive lines precisely because `no retreat` slogans are not strategy (V13-L010). This makes the final minefield a political-military degeneration, not ordinary defensive prudence.
- **Social cohesion is combat power.** No Face uses bombardment and targeted pressure to amplify fracture and cause formations to refuse mutual support (V13-L028-V13-L031, V13-L057-V13-L060).
- **Grethe owns ordered lethal force.** Her `hands/head/trigger responsibility` language prevents adult command from outsourcing the moral burden to junior officers, without erasing subordinate agency (V13-L069-V13-L070).
- **Irreplaceability remains dangerous.** Shin's ability and the Strike Package remain crucial while the same society increasingly resents/scapegoats the force it needs.
- **Final no-retreat doctrine is explicitly Sector-like.** Mines behind, Legion ahead, supply controlled from rear, and return denied leave continued combat as the soldier's only survival path (V13-L121-V13-L127).

**Correction:** distinguish *legitimate temporary defensive necessity* from a system in which command deliberately removes all exit/return so that troops cannot refuse. V13 itself supplies both forms in the same volume.

# Volume 14 military / doctrine / irreplaceability update

1. **Morale goods become explicit combat sustainment.** Shin demands sweets, cigarettes, coffee, current magazines, clean living space, and other non-minimum goods; the narration identifies morale maintenance as a legitimate component of combat power (V14-L009-V14-L012).
2. **Civilian property is treated as a future-return asset.** Requisition is logged for later repayment and avoidable damage to homes is discouraged because residents may return. Logistics is organized around postwar civic continuity rather than pure battlefield consumption.
3. **Vaneseed is a networked cognitive weapon, not magic.** It forces trauma-memory flashbacks, has field limits, interacts with Para-RAID architecture, and is less effective against Sirins whose relevant memories were deleted (V14-L093-V14-L102).
4. **Operation Wrath of Goddess operationalizes V7's anti-one-hero doctrine.** Shin is unconscious while Lena, Raiden, Anju, Michihi, Theo, Marcel, Kurena, Sirins, engineers, artillery, infantry, Grethe and allied formations solve differentiated problems (V14-L103-V14-L112).
5. **Shin remains exceptional without remaining a single point of organizational failure.** The force can continue, improvise, locate targets and conduct combined-arms action without his immediate sensory contribution.
6. **Theo's adapted engineering platform demonstrates role redesign rather than magical restoration.** Disability changes military function but does not require either total exclusion or return to the old Reginleif role (V14-L109).
7. **The Legion uses human social/neurological architecture against itself.** No Face says the butterfly line began as communication technology whose unintended trauma effect was later weaponized; treat exact origin as enemy-source CS while the observed weapon effect is PF/MI (V14-L133-V14-L136).
8. **War termination remains the strategic objective.** Shin explicitly reframes the enemy from one faction to the war that keeps generating frightened, morally degraded actors (V14-L043-V14-L046).

**V14 doctrine correction:** anti-irreplaceability is not mediocrity. It is **distributed competence, differentiated roles, recovery capacity, and an organization able to continue when its most exceptional member is absent**.
