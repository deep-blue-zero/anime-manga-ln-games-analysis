## V01 cumulative character correction

The most important baseline correction is that **Shin and Lena both begin more developed than simplified retrospective arcs suggest**.

- Shin already knows he is not rationally guilty for Rei's attack; later change is not merely acquisition of correct belief.
- Lena already knows the Republic's policy is racist and unconstitutional; later change is not merely acquisition of correct moral belief.

Both arcs therefore begin from a gap between **propositional knowledge and lived/relational capacity**.

## Character-model correction after V02

The Five are no longer analytically usable as one trauma unit. Their shared pride produces divergent futurity:

- **Raiden:** can extrapolate ordinary survival;
- **Shin:** cannot imagine beyond war except through small relational questions;
- **Theo/Anju/Kurena:** demonstrate real civilian capacities while still choosing military return.

Future synthesis must track **person-specific positive identity content**, not merely shared Eighty-Six background.

# Volume 06 update

- **Shin:** Future desire moves from outward `show Lena the sea` toward explicit reciprocal return, shared sea, her smile, and shared laughter. He confirms that old Eighty-Six pride was not wrong but cannot remain exclusive if he wants a differently constituted person beside him. Near death, survival desire is positive and relational, not merely instinctive. He voluntarily reopens family history by meeting his grandparents. **Status:** future-bearing selfhood strongly strengthened; broad postwar identity remains incomplete.
- **Lena:** Her world-trust is clarified as normative refusal to surrender the possibility of universal happiness, not ignorance of cruelty. - **Annette:** Explicitly identifies that children who never expected adulthood lack developmental models for adult desire. **Status:** future deficit reclassified partly as institutional deprivation.

# Volume 07 update

## V08 character-model correction

The phrase **“the Eighty-Six are recovering” is now analytically too coarse even when used positively**. V08 gives sharply different stress results: Shin's plural future consolidates; Rito's expands; Lena is destabilized but recoverable through relation; Kurena's identity remains Shin-centered; Theo reaches insight only to suffer involuntary bodily rupture. Future character analysis must track **resilience by domain and person**, not group-level recovery status.
