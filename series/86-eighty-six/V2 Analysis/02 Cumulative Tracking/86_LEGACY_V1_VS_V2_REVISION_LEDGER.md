# 『86―エイティシックス―』
# Legacy V1 → V2 Revision Ledger

## Governing use

This ledger converts inherited V1 claims into explicit V2 test items.

**Phase 1 assessment states are not truth-status labels.**

- `RETEST_STRONG` — legacy claim is well-motivated and should receive priority testing.
- `RETEST_WITH_NARROWING` — promising claim likely needs scope/wording correction.
- `ADVERSARIAL_PRIORITY` — elegant/high-impact claim that especially needs counterevidence.
- `SOURCE_LINEAGE_REDO` — old evidentiary route is invalid or insufficient; rebuild from primary source.
- `OPEN_QUESTION` — unresolved issue; no presumptive answer.

Only after Phase 2 primary-source rereading may a row receive the method’s actual correction states:

`CONFIRMED`, `STRENGTHENED`, `NARROWED`, `COMPLICATED`, `OVERTURNED`, or `STILL_UNRESOLVED`.

## Update protocol during Phase 2

For every completed volume:

1. Identify ledger rows materially touched by the volume.
2. Add exact primary-source locators in the corresponding `86_VXX_DEEP_READING.md`.
3. Do **not** change a broad series claim to `CONFIRMED` because one volume supports it.
4. Record counterevidence even when the final status remains supportive.
5. When a claim changes status, add:
   - volume(s);
   - locator(s);
   - reason;
   - revised wording;
   - downstream synthesis documents affected.
6. A claim that survives only after becoming narrower should be `NARROWED`, not `CONFIRMED`.
7. A claim that gains an important opposing truth while remaining broadly viable should be `COMPLICATED`.
8. A source-lineage row may move out of `SOURCE_LINEAGE_REDO` only after the new Japanese source is directly verified.

## Mandatory V14 rule

The previous V14 analysis used a Simplified-Chinese narrative body. The V2 source lock now contains a verified Japanese V14.

Therefore **all `V14xx` rows start from zero evidentiary credit**. They are retrieval prompts only. Even apparently identical plot claims must be re-established from Japanese before entering specialist synthesis.

## Phase 1 baseline

The V1 corpus is not discarded. Its claims are now explicit enough to be challenged.

The success condition for V2 is not “more confirmations than overturns.” It is that every important inherited conclusion acquires a visible evidentiary history.
