## V01 visual-form note

# Volume 07 motif update

- **Mist / haze / fog / starlight blue:** the chapter-color progression moves from obscured perception toward navigable light rather than total daylight/certainty. V7 resolves several desires while preserving war and relational incompletion.
- **Hero / cage:** heroic recognition becomes a public/institutional enclosure when the Strike Package must continue embodying national morale.
- **Line / boundary:** Lena repeatedly draws the Republic/Eighty-Six boundary to exile herself morally; Shiden insists that both sides have already crossed it relationally.
- **Dance / rhythm:** Shin and Lena coordinate separate rhythms, stumble, and continue. The image supports relation as adjustment rather than fusion.
- **Fireworks:** battlefield light is neither erased nor left exclusive; peaceful repetition adds a new future-facing association.
- **Glass / confession:** Zelene's meeting behind acrylic evokes confession without sacramental absolution; information and guilt cross a barrier while bodies/status remain separated.
- **Formal clothing / costume:** uniforms and combat gear no longer monopolize bodily identity; social dress temporarily makes alternative roles inhabitable.
- **Festival/calendar:** future becomes repeatable (`again`, next year, another sight) rather than a single impossible horizon.
- **Kiss / unfinished speech:** physical reciprocation precedes Lena's explicit verbal answer, preserving desire and relationship-status as distinct layers.

- **Blue sky:** beautiful weather remains indifferent to human misery; suffering is not cosmically validated.
- **Great Wall:** barrier of racial exclusion → failed fortification → first-meeting site → future chosen-return memorial.

# Volume 12 motif / object update

- **Mary / lamb / sheep / shepherd:** nursery-rhyme vocabulary turns obedience and guidance into a recurring formal problem across politics and Legion nomenclature.
- **Hail Mary / silver bullet:** desperate single solution substitutes for distributed learning and correction.
- **Blue bullet editorial line:** `生き抜くためには、愚かなままではいられない` foregrounds survival-through-learning at the paratext level.
- **doors:** educational fields imagined as doors one may open, inspect and decline; extends V7 capability-menu motif.
- **fog:** Sirins remove one form of operational fog by removing independent will; human politics must instead learn to judge inside uncertainty.

- **crown/title/name:** Vika/Frederica/Raiden scenes move inherited titles from fixed hierarchy toward negotiable relational language.

**V12 motif principle:** the volume repeatedly opposes the single perfect answer (bullet, princess, hero) to plural, revisable practices of learning and distributed competence.
