## 2. Governing proposition

> **In *To Be Hero X*, language does not merely express identities that already exist. It assigns roles, converts persons into public functions, carries Trust and Fear through stories, organizes hierarchy and care, and determines whether a bearer can answer the image imposed upon them. Mandarin dialogue repeatedly distinguishes being from becoming, names from addresses, persons from hero offices, reliance from popularity, and public usefulness from ownership. The Japanese track is most valuable when it exposes omission, ambiguity, or localization strategy; it becomes misleading when treated as a substitute for Mandarin lexical authority.**

The terminal linguistic principle is:

> **A person may be socially named, publicly represented, and materially empowered without becoming the property of the sentence others complete about them. Language remains humane when the named person can answer back.**

## 4. Stabilized linguistic findings

1. **The central conflict is unanswerable identity assignment versus answerable naming.** The season repeatedly asks who may complete the sentence “you are…” and whether the bearer can negate, reinterpret, or leave the result.
2. **Real name versus fake name is inadequate.** Hero names, inherited offices, stage identities, intimate addresses, and public roles can be socially real and ethically legitimate when the bearer retains answerability.
3. **`是 / 就是 / 不是 / 谁` forms a controlled identity sequence.** Imposed equivalence, present role adoption, relational negation, self-produced refusal, and totalized collapse remain grammatically distinct.
4. **Being and becoming must remain separate.** A-Hu’s `我要成为` names a future-oriented relational telos; Original Nice’s `我是奈斯` becomes fixed total equivalence. Future-directed language can still be coercive when institutions cultivate another person’s identity project.
5. **`普通人` is a positive dignity category.** Lin, Qīng, and X use ordinary-person language to preserve fallibility, privacy, limited obligation, non-performance, and exit.
6. **Commercial and constitutional management share one lexicon.** `品牌`, `包装`, `人设`, `附属品`, `枷锁`, `棋子`, `跳板`, and `经营` connect creative-industry production to institutional operation of heroes.
7. **Labor language becomes anti-apotheosis language.** `绝不加班` and `准点下班` reject the premise that exceptional capacity creates infinite availability.
8. **`信赖` is reliance before applause.** It spans confidence, role expectation, relational trust, social memory, mass adherence, and institutionally measured Trust Value.
9. **`恐惧` is not bad reputation.** It appears as affect, relation, value, contagion, engulfment, material intervention, and weaponized harm.
10. **Theological vocabulary marks active production.** `信徒`, `造神`, and `培育成为神` describe adherents, god-making, and cultivated apotheosis rather than ordinary celebrity alone.
11. **Governance language must preserve force.** `围剿`, `格杀勿论`, `宣战`, and `篡改历史` cannot be softened into neutral monitoring or routine administration when the source authorizes elimination, war, or historical falsification.
12. **Relational morphology is evidence.** `姐`, `叔`, `总`, `前辈`, diminutive `小`, familiar `阿`, and kinship forms carry hierarchy, familiarity, seniority, intimacy, or normative family standing.
13. **Executive titles are not legal names.** `尚总`, `扎总`, `老板`, and `社长` must not be back-formed into unsupported personal-name fields.
14. **Kinship language can be constitutive before classification is complete.** `就是我儿子` establishes a normative father-son relation without settling biology or formal legal status.
15. **Negative freedom does not require a complete alternative.** Xiao Yueqing’s `我很清楚自己不想要什么` and Qīng’s ordinary-person claim make refusal itself meaningful.
16. **Truth and authorship are separate.** God Eye can expose accurate facts while seizing interpretation; Lin’s confession restores his standing to narrate chronology, complicity, refusal, and subsequent action.
17. **Performance vocabulary must distinguish role, imitation, artifice, and falsity.** `演员`, `表演`, `模仿`, `人设`, and `假的` do not collapse into one claim that all performed identity is unreal.
18. **E24 causality grammar establishes antecedence without strict determinism.** Planted seeds, arranged encounter, and fate language preserve inherited causation and room for refusal.
19. **X and Zero form the terminal register contrast.** X speaks through ordinary, switchable, time-bounded role language; Zero’s language fuses hero, god, justice, sovereignty, and history.
20. **Yan Mo and Shang De engineer persons through different discourse.** Shang De imposes object/product value directly; Yan Mo speaks through cultivation, rules, opportunity, and field design. The stronger causal claim that Yan Mo planned every contingency remains unproven pending the required AV audit.

## 6. Japanese-reference audit

The aligned Japanese track is classified through four legitimate functions.

### Semantic completion

Japanese may recover a missing object or clarify broad event meaning when the Mandarin reconstruction is truncated. The recovered content must be labeled as cross-track evidence and must not be silently rewritten into a complete Mandarin quotation.

### Interpretive sharpening

Japanese may help select among plausible English paraphrases. For example, it can sharpen an ambiguous affection term toward reverential admiration or clarify the environmental/choice-architecture force of a Mandarin phrase. The Mandarin source remains primary.

### Independent localization

Japanese hero identities such as `トゥルーアイ`, `キング・デストラクション`, `マッドウルフ`, `スタンド`, `トラ`, and `黙殺` are edition-specific surfaces. They demonstrate active localization rather than a simple phonetic mirror of Mandarin.

### Source flattening or shift

Japanese sometimes changes metaphor, ontology, agency, or emphasis: the explicit right to make mistakes may become a general wish for ordinariness; Fear Value may become particle-oriented; a source weapon/ornament contrast may become machine/doll. These shifts are comparative evidence and must not be harmonized away.

The audit also preserves alignment exceptions, speaker-label uncertainty, Mandarin spoken-`ta` versus written-pronoun evidence, and the distinction between a plausible semantic reconstruction and quote-grade source text.
