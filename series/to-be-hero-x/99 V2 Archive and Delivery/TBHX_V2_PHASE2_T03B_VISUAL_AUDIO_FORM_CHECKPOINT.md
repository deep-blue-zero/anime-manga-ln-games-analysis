## Formal freeze

The current governing proposition is:

> **To Be Hero X treats representation as part of social and sometimes physical reality. Images, screens, costumes, poses, voices, stories, rankings, and animation modes do not merely display heroes after the fact: they help produce the conditions under which heroes can act, be believed, be governed, be exploited, or remain persons.**

The series opens by revealing heroic spectacle as editable production: office/client discourse interrupts the montage and orders its music stopped. It closes with production credits running across tournament hero-introduction machinery while X converts metaphysical defiance into the ordinary labor boundary `准点下班`.

The season's recurrent formal process is:

`person → role → image → circulation → Trust/Fear → bodily and institutional consequence`.

Counter-movements occur through private counter-images, testimony, recovered objects, revised recognition, and self-authored performance.

## Representational-level distinction

T03B stabilizes four categories that must remain separate.

### 1. Authorial/extradiegetic variation

Storybook memories, sketch ruptures, montage, lyric overlays, impact frames, openings, endings, credits, and symbolic deformation can carry literary meaning without constituting literal transformations inside the world.

### 2. Intradiegetic media representation

Broadcasts, phones, advertisements, rankings, FOCUS drones, recorders, radio, photos, and institutional screens are seen and used by characters. Because Trust and Fear are socially generated, media representation can alter action rather than merely document it.

### 3. Mechanic/affective visualization

Queen's gold rule field, Lucky Cyan's green protective field, Fear contamination, Original Nice's perfection force, Zero's apotheotic imagery, and Trust-linked transformations have local causal force. They do not form one universal color or rendering code.

### 4. Diegetic representational-state manipulation

X can place himself, opponents, selected objects, and environments into altered planar, image-object, graphic, spatial, and shared-history states. The stabilized term remains **representational sovereignty**.

The ledger rejects both extremes:

- treating every change of animation mode as an X mechanic;
- treating the E22–E24 planar/shared-world sequences as merely decorative style when characters inhabit and react to them.

## Media and public-history freeze

Screens and interfaces are a dual-use causal architecture.

They can:

- manufacture and assign hero images;
- quantify Trust and ranking movement;
- compress distributed causality into singular hero/villain stories;
- expose substitution or abuse;
- protect vulnerable people by increasing visibility;
- restore suppressed testimony;
- turn catastrophe into administrable public fact.

The current formal proposition is:

> **Public history is part of the battle, not an account written afterward.**

E20 crosscuts violence with plans for how the violence will be narrated. E24 opens by converting prior catastrophe into headlines, corporate images, rankings, and administrative continuity.

## Spatial-ethics freeze

The ledger stabilizes five recurrent spatial operations:

1. **verticality** — rank, monument, podium, strategic distance, godhood;
2. **enclosure** — apartment, laboratory, pod, vehicle, command room, closed door;
3. **interposition** — a body or field placed between a vulnerable being and violence;
4. **proximity/touch** — care, testimony, food, hands, hospital contact, domestic relation;
5. **exit/open direction** — portal, road, car, beach, bar, and clock-out boundary.

Protection is repeatedly made visible through costly interposition before verbal accountability is complete. That action can evidence change without erasing prior responsibility.

X's final movement is especially important: city-scale violence resolves through rooftop displacement, an ordinary car, a bar, and `准点下班`, not a sovereign domination tableau.

## Body, face, and image freeze

Masks, smiles, poses, costumes, faces, and ranking portraits function as repeatable hero signs. They can mediate vocation and recognition, but they can also become property managed by institutions or audiences.

Major conclusions include:

- a mask can coexist with genuine person-recognition;
- the Nice smile becomes both Trust surface and instrument of social engineering;
- Smile's pendant preserves private provenance after the public face collapses;
- A-Hu passes through nested imposed representations before choosing a hero relation;
- credits deformation does not establish literal bodily monster transformation;
- X demonstrates identity continuity across volumetric, planar, graphic, image-object, and shared-history substrates.

E20 also establishes a decisive animation contrast:

> **false perfection remains clean; genuine pain becomes ugly.**

Luo Li and Big Johnny's parallel rage sequence uses distorted faces, scratchier line, smear frames, and violent foreshortening. “Feral violence” is a formal/behavioral description, not proof that either loses personhood or shares one transformation mechanism.

## Montage, chronology, and convergence freeze
