## 2. Governing proposition

> **Dragon Boy and Smile form a paired study of suffering turned into social authority. Liang Long is taught that endurance proves worth and that failure makes a person disposable; his damage-return ability then gives retaliatory embodiment to that lesson, while Yan Mo converts it into a theory of godlike punitive sovereignty. Smile begins from a genuine capacity to create joy, but hero society, family expectation, and his own power condition make the public smile continuous labor until Fear occupies the same sign. Their collision produces punitive heroism: Liang stops a real threat while substantially helping manufacture the threat, then a causally cropped public narrative and Commission arbitration convert the result into rank-three legitimacy. The mature ethical distinction is that suffering may explain strength without granting jurisdiction over others, and protective efficacy may be real without erasing causal responsibility.**

## 3. Stabilized findings

1. **Punitive heroism is a compound structure.** It joins pain-as-credential, retaliation-as-repayment, causally cropped visibility, and institutional authorization.
2. **Broadcast order matters.** Liang first appears as publicly legible hero-content; the later episodes restore the coercive endurance history and hidden causal chain beneath that qualification.
3. **Liang's first public identity is platform-authored.** FOMO turns the group beating and apparent indestructibility into the joking `冬泳英雄` image before Yan Mo offers a top-down god project.
4. **`垃圾 / 清理` is the central punitive vocabulary.** The adult Liang identifies with the evaluator who called the suffering child useless trash and projects the verdict outward as a sanitation theory of violence.
5. **The childhood evaluator remains unidentified.** Season 1 establishes coercive endurance conditioning and humiliation, not a secure mother, coach, researcher, or institutional role.
6. **Yan Mo mistakes endurance for sovereign fitness.** `感受疼痛是必经的历练` treats pain as initiation into godhood rather than evidence requiring care or accountability.
7. **Liang's mechanic is damage-return, not a universal pain stat.** Received `伤害` can be returned at doubled magnitude; Fear locally qualifies as returnable damage. Exact storage, ceiling, cost, and modality rules remain open.
8. **Psychology and mechanic reinforce one another without collapsing.** The ability rewards a retaliatory worldview but does not cause the worldview or determine every use.
9. **Liang authors retaliation conditions.** He solicits violence, weaponizes Smile's private family wound, and later thanks Nice for supplying a reason to hit back.
10. **Smile's pedagogy is genuine.** He teaches Original Nice that a real smile should follow something genuinely worth happiness; the later public role does not retroactively falsify that gift.
11. **Authentic affect becomes compulsory labor.** Smile has distributed warmth for decades, forgotten fatigue, and requires the smile for full/100-percent output.
12. **Family evidence establishes asymmetry, not a total parenting verdict.** The unsmiling daughter photograph and matching pendant show a private wound beneath public abundance while leaving abuse, abandonment, divorce, death, and current family status unproved.
13. **The pendant is a counter-archive.** It moves from family object to Liang's weaponized evidence and then to memorial provenance recovered by X; final disposition remains open.
14. **Original Nice's attachment to Smile is genuine sustaining faith.** Shang De colonizes that aspiration rather than inventing it, and Smile's death becomes one major cause in Nice's later multicausal collapse.
15. **Nice acts with agency but without omniscience.** He violates Shang De's instruction and injects Liang with high-confidence protective motive, but uses an unethical Fear weapon without knowing Liang's return rule.
16. **Fear occupies Smile's existing identity axis.** Progressive corruption makes ridicule terror and the red grin more legible than the human face; this is bearer-specific evidence, not a universal affect-corruption equation.
17. **The crowd sees a real emergency through cropped causality.** Fear-Smile is genuinely dangerous; spectators lack Yan Mo's design, Liang's provocation, Nice's injection, the return mechanic, and the family-object manipulation.
18. **Liang kills a real threat that he substantially helped manufacture.** The act is neither imaginary protection nor sufficient moral vindication.
19. **The childhood endurance sequence and crowd cheering share one grammar.** Both command Liang to endure, rise, prove worth, and convert pain into public qualification.
20. **Nice's retaliatory grief carries the coercive script back toward Liang.** Deep recognition changes action but cannot by itself defeat institutional capture or revenge.
21. **X interrupts rather than balances retaliation.** Representational manipulation changes Liang's category instead of returning greater damage, while the crowd's `神` language transfers the problem from punishment to sovereignty.
22. **E24 performs institutional laundering.** The public account that Smile was consumed by Fear is not wholly false, but it compresses distributed authorship into an administratively usable crisis narrative.
23. **Rank three does not settle moral truth.** Commission arbitration gives Liang legal/ranking standing while exact procedure, evidence record, contestability, and later revision remain open.
24. **Smile's private provenance survives public collapse.** Family history, the pendant, Original Nice's grief, and X's recovery of the object prevent the corrupted public face from becoming the only surviving account.
25. **Liang's biography explains without absolving.** Coercive conditioning helps explain his pain-worth logic but does not excuse adult provocation, dehumanization, Fear redirection, killing, or aspiration to clean away unwanted people.
26. **The system extracts different kinds of value from both men.** Smile's affect becomes continuous public labor; Liang's endurance and violence become rankable punitive capital.
