## 2. Governing proposition

> **A-Hu's Season-1 arc shows that a myth becomes comparatively legitimate not when it stops being constructed, but when the being who must live inside it can answer it, revise it, and bind it to a concrete relation of care. Circus applause gives him power without recognition. Xinya's belief gives him an offered future, and his reciprocal belief turns that future into a chosen protective telos. Public media then singularizes distributed local causality into a ranked hero myth, while Episode 24 shows how quickly protection can be captured by catastrophic-risk logic and institutional management. A-Hu is therefore neither a fake hero made real by belief nor a pure local hero corrupted only afterward. He is a person learning to distinguish stories that enlarge his agency from stories that use his agency as material.**

32. **Survivable exit remains the decisive unresolved test.** Season 1 does not establish whether A-Hu can leave herohood while retaining speech, power, livelihood, family standing, medical autonomy, and public personhood.
