## Mechanics freeze for Tranche 01

Current full-series mechanics are organized around four principles:

1. **socially generated causality** — Trust/Fear arise through persons, publics, memory, media, institutions, and recognition;
2. **identity mediation** — powers and constraints attach to socially legible roles/images, not merely biological individuals;
3. **non-scalar exceptions** — rank/Trust quantity does not fully determine combat ability, freedom, legitimacy, or personhood;
4. **bounded ontology** — alien material and X demonstrate that ordinary Trust/Fear rules do not exhaust the world's possible mechanics.

Key rejected simplifications:
- Trust = popularity;
- Fear = bad reputation;
- self-belief = separate numeric Trust meter;
- Trust and Fear obey a universal zero-sum equation;
- one believer = one Trust point;
- X's snap is a universal prerequisite;
- X's coin is a binary fate selector.
