## 2. Governing proposition

> **Luo Li’s Season-1 arc is about acquiring authorship over the order in which the world reads her, then discovering that personhood cannot depend on permanent control of that reading. She refuses to erase the body and aesthetics others trivialize, builds technology that makes competence legible before stereotype can preempt it, inherits a father’s science without being obliged to inherit his theory of control, and finally survives a grief-state in which even her carefully authored image breaks apart. Her strongest form of self-authorship is therefore not perfect self-presentation. It is the capacity to remain an answerable person across beauty, strength, technology, dependence, rage, mourning, public recognition, and institutional recapture.**

## 3. Stabilized findings

1. **Appearance is an evidentiary override.** E13 repeatedly places competent action before observers allow Luo Li’s cuteness/smallness to displace that evidence.
2. **Cuteness is not treated as false.** The problem is category saturation: one accurate trait becomes socially overcomplete and is used to infer weakness, unseriousness, or decorative function.
3. **Refusal precedes reclamation.** The almost-haircut scene establishes a negative dignity claim—Luo Li will not erase long hair, smallness, or preferred presentation to satisfy a masculine-coded heroic expectation—before a fully positive “cute + strong” synthesis is secured.
4. **`英雄人设` is legitimate authored construction.** Luo Li’s suit/persona is counter-rhetoric, not a revelation of an unperformed essence.
5. **The helmet reveals by concealing.** It changes the order of legibility so heroic conduct can be registered before facial appearance activates stereotype.
6. **`力与美` is first solved as a technical/design problem.** Strength and beauty coexist materially; the series does not require a mystical claim that femininity itself is combat power.
7. **Nuonuo is a developmental recognizer.** She initially reproduces Luo Li’s cute-girl framing, later becomes a technical collaborator and witness, and remains fallible enough to misidentify Shindig.
8. **Private testimony has evidentiary value without monopolizing legitimacy.** Nuonuo’s “to me, Luo Li is a hero” follows witnessed conduct and technical relation before formal rank.
9. **Luo Tong is a mixed paternal case.** Real love and legitimate safety duties coexist with professional absence, obsolete knowledge of his daughter, proceduralized protection, and a later technocratic theory of affective governance.
10. **Science is infrastructure rather than purity.** Luo Li’s capacity is co-produced through Glimmer Lab, other engineers, alien materials, field logistics, and inherited access.
11. **Alien matter supplies the demonstrated local Trust/Fear exception.** Technology mediates the exception; it is not established as metaphysically independent by itself.
12. **Nullification is politically ambivalent.** It can protect personhood from unwanted social authorship or become a technology of detention, suppression, and monopoly.
13. **The E19 transmitter distinguishes care from surveillance by relational standing.** Open, consent-based tracking becomes a promise of response, unlike Ghostblade’s unilateral surveillance.
14. **Luo Tong’s technocracy is revised rather than simply rejected.** The transmitter and hostility sensor show science making relation/affect legible instead of eliminating subjectivity.
15. **E20 culminates the appearance/evidence problem.** Publicly perfected Nice is machine-read as intensely hostile (`敌意`), demonstrating the divergence between social hero legibility and current moral intent.
16. **Luo Li’s mature armor is organized around interposition.** It stands between danger and Luo Tong/Nuonuo, reducing or cancelling Original Nice’s power rather than establishing sovereign field control.
17. **Luo Tong’s death causality is fixed.** He dies shielding Nuonuo from Fear-created/augmented attackers; Original Nice does not directly deliver the fatal blow.
18. **The grief-to-rage chain is motion-confirmed.** Luo Li’s formal, emotional, and behavioral transformation follows discovery of Luo Tong’s sacrifice.
19. **The Big Johnny parallel is formal/narrative, not a shared mechanical equation.** Big Johnny changes morphologically; Luo Li does not. Direct Fear amplification of Luo Li remains open.
20. **“Feral” is a formal description.** Looser linework, distorted faces, smear frames, shadow, and violent foreshortening represent loss of self-control without implying literal animalization or lost personhood.
21. **Nice’s clean perfection versus Luo Li’s ugly grief is a major E20 contrast.** Formal polish is not moral truth; formal breakdown is not moral dehumanization.
22. **Queen/Cyan provide boundary and restoration.** Genuine feeling does not become sovereign merely because it is authentic; a person can be bounded in a dangerous state without being reduced to that state.
23. **The grave creates an inheritance problem.** Luo Li must inherit Luo Tong’s science, concern, and provenance without being obligated to reproduce his desire for unilateral control.
24. **FOMO captures the language of self-authorship.** The platform does not need to fabricate Luo Li’s authenticity; it can market a genuine resistance claim and thereby contest ownership of its circulation.
