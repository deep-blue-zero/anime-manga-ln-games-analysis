## 2. Governing proposition

> **The positive relational model of *To Be Hero X* is answerable interdependence. Persons become capable of heroism through other people, inherited roles, testimony, care, rivalry, material support, and public recognition; they are not self-created isolates. Yet no relation becomes legitimate merely because it is intimate, loving, protective, accurate, or causally powerful. A relation becomes comparatively person-preserving when both parties can answer it, revise it, fail within it, preserve provenance, maintain boundaries, and survive becoming someone the relation did not originally imagine.**

## 3. Stabilized findings

1. **Herohood is constitutively relational.** Every major hero identity depends on recognizers, witnesses, rivals, mentors, family, technical labor, institutions, and publics.
2. **Causal contribution does not create ownership.** A mentor, family member, office, public, or community may help produce a hero without acquiring title over the bearer.
3. **Relationship ethics operate across four scales.** Dyads, small groups, institutions, and publics can each preserve personhood or totalize it.
4. **Broadcast order converts anthology into relationship network.** Later episodes reveal that private bonds distribute roles, grief, obligation, and violence across arcs.
5. **Recognition differs from identification.** Identification assigns a socially usable entity/category; recognition preserves the categorized being's standing in what that category means.
6. **The positive recognition model has seven properties:** reciprocity, revisability, fallibility, provenance, consent/boundary, non-totalization, and survivable separation.
7. **Answerable interdependence replaces purity.** Persons may be made with others without being made for them.
8. **Recognition often precedes public legibility.** Yòuzi, Xia Qing, Luo, Nuonuo, A-Sheng/Xiao Qiang, Xinya, and Smile recognize conduct or personhood before institutions stabilize it.
9. **Early recognition is not automatically legitimate.** The orphanage director and Shang De show that accurate early insight can become high-resolution instrumentalization.
10. **Role-mediated recognition can be genuine.** E-Soul and A-Hu's hero role become comparatively legitimate because the bearer's answer can alter what the role means.
11. **Testimony is distributed personhood.** Witnesses carry names, conduct, family standing, and provenance into public fields where self-assertion alone would be erased or powerless.
12. **Witnessing does not create exclusive authorship.** Moon, Nuonuo, Xia Qing, Little Johnny, A-Hu, and X preserve evidence while remaining fallible and source-bounded.
13. **Public and private recognition correct one another.** Intimacy preserves fine detail; public scrutiny can challenge private domination; neither scale is morally innocent.
14. **Chosen kinship precedes classification.** Little Johnny/Big Johnny and Xinya/A-Hu establish relational standing before species, rank, speech, or legal category are settled.
15. **Family particularizes without automatically owning.** Kinship is person-preserving only while it remains answerable to the family member's future subjecthood.
16. **Protection, presence, and comfort are distinct goods.** Wang Yi's protection does not repair Nuonuo's abandoned ordinary life; Luo Tong's concern does not eliminate obsolete paternal knowledge.
17. **Parental repair must answer the specific deprivation.** Proof of love cannot substitute for missing presence, consent, comfort, truth, or reciprocal access.
18. **Guardianship creates responsibility, not permanent jurisdiction.** Rescue becomes illegitimate when continued dependence is required to validate the protector's role.
19. **Mentorship transfers possibility and danger.** Smile, Shang Chao, and Xinya open futures; Shang De, Yan Mo, and the orphanage director convert wounds/gifts into their own projects.
20. **Positive mentorship survives revision.** The learner may refuse, reinterpret, surpass, or become someone the mentor did not design.
21. **Institutional mentorship is ethically mixed.** Equipment, training, legal standing, medical care, and publicity are real goods that can become role-debt and capture when exit is absent.
22. **Affection does not create jurisdiction.** Lin/Moon, Yang/Xia Qing, and Qīng/Luo preserve genuine attachment without allowing attachment to retroactively legitimate coercion or settle labels unsupported by the source.
23. **Care plus separation is a positive achievement.** Xiao's departure, Qīng's escape, Wang Yi's hospital restraint, and X's repeated exits show that relation need not require possession.
24. **Friendship preserves non-instrumental remainder.** Wreck/Nice, Yang/Shang Chao, Luo Li/Nuonuo, and X/Smile keep persons legible outside public function while remaining materially insufficient to solve every crisis.
25. **Rivalry is mutual authorship.** Queen/Bowa, Yang/veteran E-Soul, Nice/Wreck, Lin/God Eye, and X/Commission contest which account of the other becomes socially operative.
26. **Adversarial recognition can be mature.** Truth and dissent need not culminate in affection.
27. **X/A-Hu remains adversarial trust.** Secrecy, threat, shared exposure, dissent, and forbidden testimony coexist without friendship, allegiance, or constitutional equality.
28. **Alliance is not recognition.** Shared objective can coordinate protection while denying standing to participants or targets.
29. **Differentiated contribution is a legitimate coalition principle.** Containment, restoration, testimony, engineering, interposition, and ordinary presence need not collapse into one final-blow metric.
30. **Queen/Qīng/Johnnies models distributed noncompetitive care.** The relation preserves boundary and differentiated function; long-term durability and complete audiovisual detail remain open.
31. **Distributed care counters savior sovereignty only when responsibility remains traceable.** Diffusion without provenance can become institutional non-accountability.
32. **Objects are relationship infrastructure.** Guitar, recorder, notebook, pendant, photographs, and coin preserve or contest provenance while remaining vulnerable to appropriation.
33. **Silence can be boundary or projection surface.** It ceases to be neutral when consent, decision, accountability, or revision require the silent person's answer.
34. **Relational language matters.** Names, kinship/address terms, `另一个你`, `我要成为`, `来看看`, and `准点下班` preserve relation, becoming, witness, and role boundary.
35. **Grief has two relational directions.** It may preserve provenance and permit new life or conscript survivors into revenge, succession, restoration, and frozen obligation.
36. **Recognition is necessary and materially insufficient.** Nice/Smile is the decisive case: genuine attachment changes action and produces grief without defeating institutional capture.
37. **Public recognition can correct and totalize.** It can restore attribution, expose coercion, create legal standing, or flatten persons into one function, myth, or god.
38. **Institutions are durable relationships.** They preserve commitments beyond individual goodwill and also harden mistaken categories beyond the people who created them.
39. **Hidden labor is hidden relationship.** Erasing co-producers removes witnesses, claimants, and possible limits on the visible hero's authority.
40. **Trust and Fear are relational multipliers, not moral scores.** Mechanical efficacy does not certify truth, consent, recognition, or relational health.
41. **Relational totalization is the recurring failure form.** One partial truth becomes the only socially actionable account of a person.
42. **The strongest positive relations preserve surprise.** The other person remains capable of becoming someone the founding relation did not predict.
