# To Be Hero X V2 — Phase 2 Tranche 02A Checkpoint

## Purpose

This checkpoint records stabilization of the two ledgers governing the project's central person/image problem:

1. `HERO_IMAGE_PERSONHOOD_AND_IDENTITY_LEDGER.md`
2. `PUBLIC_NARRATIVE_PRIVATE_EVENT_LEDGER.md`

The ledgers remain the canonical topical homes. This checkpoint is a state record, not a substitute synthesis.

## Personhood model stabilized

Current governing proposition:

> **A hero image becomes legitimate not because it is natural or unperformed, but because the bearer remains answerable: able to know provenance, revise meaning, fail without becoming disposable, accept responsibility, and leave without annihilation.**

The ledger now distinguishes:
- social/mechanical efficacy from moral legitimacy;
- public legibility from personhood;
- influence from ownership;
- danger from dignity;
- visibility from reciprocal recognition;
- role performance from totalization.

It also completes the episode-local sequence through E24 and provides full-series matrices for identity states, capture modes, and person-compatible roles.

## Public narrative model stabilized

Current governing proposition:

> **Public narrative is a causal institution that selects reality, distributes responsibility and visibility, and feeds back into Trust/Fear, bodies, rankings, contracts, markets, and violence.**

The ledger now distinguishes:
- falsehood from causal incompleteness;
- exposure from restored authorship;
- protective opacity from institutional concealment;
- strategic fiction from coercive substitution;
- public observation from full moral understanding;
- present chronology from prospective paratext.

## Joint conclusion

These two ledgers now share one normative test:

> **A socially useful story or role becomes dehumanizing when its usefulness depends on making the affected person unable to answer it.**

This test will govern later character, institutional, Trust/Fear, and full-series syntheses.
