## 3. Phase-4 corpus result

### 3.1 Narrative architecture

The season is not adequately described as merely nonlinear or anthology-like. Its broadcast order is a controlled sequence of provisional classifications. Viewers encounter public images, institutional facts, and visible consequences before the provenance that made them possible.

Broadcast order and diegetic order remain coequal analytical authorities. Recontextualization changes judgment without erasing the material consequences of earlier stories.

### 3.2 Social ontology

Trust and Fear form a layered social-material ontology rather than a popularity meter. Public expectation, bearer identity, relational provenance, institutions, socio-technical infrastructure, and material substrate interact. X adds an unresolved higher-order layer.

Representation can become materially real without becoming true, consensual, just, or complete.

### 3.3 Heroism, dignity, and legitimacy

Mechanical efficacy, protective effect, moral heroism, institutional legitimacy, and constitutional authority are independent axes.

Dignity survives usefulness, danger, non-human status, contamination, guilt, and failed self-control. It does not eliminate proportionate restraint or accountability.

The positive standard is power made answerable through proportionality, provenance, contestability, non-instrumentalization, revision, and survivable exit.

### 3.4 Institutions and political economy

Hero society is a polycentric constitutional market. Institutions perform necessary coordination while converting persons, relations, attention, labor, secrecy, and catastrophe into governable assets.

The central institutional danger is asset logic becoming sovereign. The answer is not anti-institutional purity but answerable organization capable of coordinating power without owning the person.

### 3.5 Public meaning

Public narrative is causal governance. Branding makes expectations durable; celebrity converts asymmetrical visibility into labor and obligation; myth compresses contested history; collective belief gives the result material force without creating informed democratic consent.

The positive standard is answerable public meaning: narratives preserve provenance, plural persons, legitimate privacy, contestability, revision, and a survivable difference between person and role.
