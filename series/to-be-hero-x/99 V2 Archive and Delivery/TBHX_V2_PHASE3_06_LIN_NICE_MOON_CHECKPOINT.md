## Governing thesis

> **The replacement hero becomes comparatively legitimate not when imitation disappears, but when succession becomes answerable: its provenance can be spoken, its inherited obligations can be owned without appropriating another person's history, its relationships can refuse or leave, and its current bearer can revise the role without ceasing to count as a person.**

The document rejects the simple reversal in which Original Nice is “fake” and Lin is “real.” Original Nice possessed genuine heroic aspiration and a sustaining faith in Smile before Shang De colonized that aspiration. Lin becomes more legitimate not through legal-name purity, but through public provenance, self-implicating confession, role refusal, chosen relation, responsibility for inherited obligations, and action before social validation returns.

Xiao Yueqing/Moon is treated as an independent autonomy-bearing character rather than a reward or authenticity device. Her public romance is labor; her wormhole ability makes direction and forced proximity literal; her boundary language permits refusal before a complete future plan; and the return of her movement device tests whether Lin's protection preserves rather than owns her direction.

## Major stabilized conclusions

1. **Nice has multiple simultaneous meanings:** original bearer, commercial brand, ranked social office, Trust-constituted body/behavioral program, and relational bundle.
2. **Original Nice's collapse is multicausal:** aspiration capture, Fear-engineered perfection, conditional public love, Smile's death, compromised agency, grief, Fear contamination, humiliation, and role totalization.
3. **Original Nice's suicide is an annihilating exit, not a completed autonomy model.** Choice is preserved without treating death as a person-compatible resignation mechanism.
4. **Lin's E01 endpoint remains role adoption:** `现在的我就是奈斯`; later self-naming must not be retrojected into the origin.
5. **The decisive V2 sequence is developmental:** imposed irrelevance → imposed/adopted equivalence → relational negation → self-produced refusal → public self-naming → contested role re-authoring.
6. **Lin/Xiao affection is genuine but does not legitimate coercive contract romance, forced proximity, or public marriage escalation.**
7. **Succession transfers operational responsibility more readily than it transfers memory, intimacy, consent, or moral authorship.** Wreck is the key relational test.
8. **God Eye's accurate exposé is not equivalent to restored authorship.** Lin's confession differs because the affected person speaks, admits complicity, and acts afterward.
9. **Lin's post-E04 state is partial emancipation, not clean liberation.** “The Commoner,” rank, contract, and E24 administrative regularization preserve recapture risk.
10. **The positive model is answerable interdependence:** role, public, institution, and relationship may matter without owning the whole bearer.
