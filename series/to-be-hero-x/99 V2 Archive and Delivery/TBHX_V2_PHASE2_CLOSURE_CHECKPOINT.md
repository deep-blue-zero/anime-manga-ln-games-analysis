## 4. Stabilized full-series model

### Trust and Fear

> **Trust and Fear are socially generated but materially consequential forces whose effects depend on expectation, identity, bearer receptivity, relational history, institutional mediation, and material exceptions.**

Phase 2 rejects the reductions:

- Trust = popularity;
- Fear = bad reputation;
- self-belief = a second numerical Trust meter;
- Trust/Fear = one universal zero-sum equation;
- one believer = one Trust point;
- every capability = direct Trust/Fear expression.

Exact equations, thresholds, persistence curves, and conversion rates remain open.

### Chronology and narrative form

Broadcast order and diegetic order remain separate authorities. E21–E23's Original Nice–Smile–Liang Long–A-Hu chain occurs before E01, making Original Nice's suicide the endpoint of a longer multicausal destabilization history rather than an isolated perfection collapse.

The E24 credits coda remains canonical supplemental future-facing opening-form paratext, not settled post-tournament chronology.

### Personhood and identity

> **Representation may be socially real, useful, and empowering without being entitled to own the represented person.**

The governing positive criterion is **answerability**. The terminal failure is **totalization**. A comparatively legitimate role preserves provenance, reply, revision, failure, responsibility, reciprocal relation, privacy, and survivable exit.

### Public narrative

> **Public narrative is a causal institution.**

The mature process is:

`event → selection → narration → circulation → uptake → material feedback → recontextualization`

Public and private accounts may both be partial. Exposure is not automatically ethical. Visibility can protect and capture simultaneously.

### Institutions

Hero society is a **polycentric constitutional market** whose institutions perform real coordination, research, allocation, verification, emergency, and anti-apotheosis functions while recurrently converting persons, relations, attention, risk, and crises into administrable value.

The Commission's fear of another Zero is historically rational. Its E24 preemptive anti-X procedure remains illegitimate under the stabilized proportionality, standing, contestability, provenance, non-instrumentalization, plurality, and revision test.

### Relationships

> **Recognition becomes personhood-preserving when the recognized person can answer, revise, fail, dissent, and survive changes in the relation.**

Care does not excuse coercion. Protection, presence, and comfort remain distinct. The positive standard is answerable interdependence rather than either isolation or total absorption.

### Language and naming

Mandarin lexical choice and address/register are current authority. Identity is repeatedly negotiated through being, becoming, naming, believing, managing, using, protecting, remembering, and leaving.

Current prose follows the audited crosswalk while frozen episode bodies preserve the terminology available at their original boundaries.

### Visual, audio, motif, and form

Representation is material social infrastructure. Authorial variation, intradiegetic media, mechanic/affective visualization, and X-centered representational-state manipulation remain distinct evidence levels.

X's bounded umbrella capability is **representational sovereignty**, not generic unlimited reality warping. Detailed season-wide acoustic analysis remains Phase-5 work.

### X and Zero

Zero is the limit case of hero image, public will, divinity, personal justice, and sovereignty totalizing into one identity.

X currently resists this through presentation plurality, ordinary labor, friendship, privacy, refusal of overtime, and role exit. Those practices are ethically meaningful but do not supply a durable constitutional settlement.
