## 3. Phase-3 corpus result

Phase 3 establishes a coherent character model without flattening differences among the arcs.

### 3.1 Personhood and hero roles

The specialist sequence rejects a pure authentic-self versus fake-role binary.

- Lin demonstrates the right to become without being replaced.
- Yang demonstrates inherited identity as covenant rather than permanent moral possession.
- Qīng demonstrates that entrusted hope differs from extractive public need.
- Luo Li demonstrates authored presentation without aesthetic self-erasure.
- A-Hu demonstrates answerable myth rather than role-free authenticity.
- X demonstrates anti-totalization as practiced plurality and exit rather than a permanent certificate of goodness.

### 3.2 Institutions and capture

The character arcs converge on institutions that provide real coordination, equipment, publicity, safety, research, and legal standing while repeatedly translating people into:

- brands;
- roles;
- rankings;
- risks;
- labor inputs;
- research objects;
- political symbols;
- replaceable offices.

The specialist corpus therefore supports Phase 4's institutional work without prematurely replacing it.

### 3.3 Recognition and answerable interdependence

The final relationship synthesis stabilizes the positive relational standard as **answerable interdependence**:

> persons are socially made through others while retaining dissent, revision, fallibility, provenance, boundary, non-totalization, and survivable separation.

This standard replaces both self-sufficient authenticity and romantic claims that being loved or accurately seen is enough.

### 3.4 Grief, inheritance, and posthumous obligation

The corpus distinguishes inheritance that preserves provenance from inheritance that conscripts survivors:

- Shang Chao/Yang;
- Luo/Qīng;
- Luo Tong/Luo Li;
- A-Sheng/Little Johnny;
- Smile/Nice/X;
- Zero/Yan Mo.

Death does not settle whether a role should continue. It makes the surviving person's answer more important and more difficult.

### 3.5 Power and legitimacy

The character specialists converge on a mature legitimacy distinction:

- power may be socially generated;
- recognition may be mechanically effective;
- protection may be real;
- institutional fear may be rational;
- none of those facts independently creates moral or constitutional authority.

Dragon Boy's punitive heroism, the Commission's anti-X operation, and Zero's apotheosis are the highest-pressure cases.

## 4. Specialist-by-specialist stabilized propositions

### Specialist 06 — Lin Ling / Original Nice / Moon

A person has the right to become without being replaced. E24's distinct Lin Ling/Nice tournament presentation and cracked Nice reappearance revise linear succession into forked continuity under unresolved ontology.

### Specialist 07 — Yang Cheng / E-Soul

Heroic inheritance is legitimate when it transmits practice and provenance without owning the successor. Legitimate origin does not immunize later conduct from judgment.

### Specialist 08 — Lucky Cyan / Qīng

A useful person remains entitled to fallibility. Hope becomes legitimate when entrusted to the survivor's future rather than extracted as continuing service.

### Specialist 09 — Queen

Queen's diagnosis of volatile hero society survives X; her confidence in solitary sovereign mastery does not. Her strongest later rule form is bounded, self-involving, revisable, and open to differentiated care.

### Specialist 10 — Luo Li

Self-authorship is control over the order of legibility, not permanent emotional or aesthetic control. Technology becomes ethical when it makes relation answerable rather than eliminating subjectivity.

### Specialist 11 — Ghostblade

Wang Yi changes what he refuses: from biography, disclosure, and moral complication toward refusal of corrupt orders and forced access. Protection remains short of confession and repair.

### Specialist 12 — the Johnnies

Recognition precedes classification. Big Johnny can be a person and a real danger simultaneously; dignity and risk governance are independent variables.

### Specialist 13 — Dragon Boy / Smile

Punitive heroism converts suffering into jurisdiction and causally cropped protection into rank. Authentic affect can become compulsory labor without becoming retrospectively fake.

### Specialist 14 — A-Hu

A myth becomes comparatively legitimate when the bearer can answer it. Local care and reciprocal belief create a real hero while remaining vulnerable to public singularization and preventive politics.

### Specialist 15 — X / Zero

Season 1 does not solve godhood by locating a good god. Zero is apotheotic totalization; X is a strong bearer-dependent anti-totalization practice without reciprocal constitutional guarantee.

### Specialist 16 — cross-arc relations

No hero is made alone, and no maker owns the hero. The relational endpoint is answerable interdependence rather than isolation, possession, worship, or sentimental recognition-as-salvation.
