## Institutional freeze

The current full-series institutional model is:

> **Hero society is a polycentric constitutional market built around socially generated power.**

Its institutions perform real public functions:

- catastrophe prevention;
- anti-apotheosis governance;
- coordination of dangerous bearers;
- scientific research;
- public verification;
- ranking succession;
- resource allocation;
- training and recovery;
- and technical/media infrastructure.

The same institutions convert:

- persons into brands;
- relationships into content;
- public attention into power and market value;
- crises into ranking opportunity;
- secrets into impunity;
- and catastrophe into administrative continuity.

The stabilized legitimacy test requires:

- proportionality;
- procedural standing;
- contestability;
- provenance;
- non-instrumentalization;
- recognition of private/role plurality;
- and revision under new evidence.

The Association's fear of another Zero is rational. Its E24 attack on X remains procedurally self-defeating because it treats unowned autonomy as evidence of sovereign intent and attacks the private boundary through which X presently limits role totalization.

## Ranking and tournament freeze

Rank is stabilized as:

- Trust-linked public office;
- market value;
- disciplinary metric;
- material-status allocator;
- anti-monopoly/anti-apotheosis mechanism;
- and entertainment spectacle.

The tournament is therefore neither merely corrupt nor merely fair. Its public constitutional purpose is genuine, while its operation is vulnerable to agenda-setting, assassination, preemption, and private faction strategy.

## Media and labor freeze

Media is an institutional layer that:

- makes heroes legible;
- allocates attention;
- exposes hidden abuse;
- compresses causal complexity;
- reprices legitimacy;
- and writes public memory.

Hidden creative, technical, performance, research, and infrastructure labor is constitutive of hero value.

## Relationship freeze

The current recognition model is:

> **Recognition is the social act through which another being becomes more than a function, image, wound, or use—but it is personhood-preserving only when the recognized person can answer and revise the relation.**

Seven positive properties are stabilized:

1. reciprocity;
2. revisability;
3. fallibility;
4. provenance;
5. consent/boundary;
6. non-totalization;
7. survivable separation.

## Major cross-series relationship conclusions

- Private recognition is causally important but not sufficient to defeat institutional capture.
- Hero names and inherited roles can mediate genuine recognition; legal-name purity is rejected.
- Care does not excuse coercion.
- Protection, presence, and comfort are distinct relational goods.
- Grief can preserve provenance or conscript the survivor into revenge/obligation.
- Testimony is one of the series' major mechanisms for carrying personhood into a hostile public field.
- X and A-Hu currently exemplify **adversarial trust**, not settled friendship.
- X and Zero form a philosophical rivalry between preserved relational plurality and totalized god-role identity.
