## Claim methodology freeze

The governing rule is:

> **A mature claim is the narrowest formulation that survives the strongest currently available evidence and counterevidence.**

The ledger now distinguishes:

- dialogue, visual, motion, audio, mechanic, institutional, and chronology facts;
- inference and interpretation;
- revision and alternative readings;
- unresolved propositions and source-control boundaries;
- local, cross-case, full-series, historical, prospective, and paratextual scope.

Confidence is attached to exact wording rather than general subject matter:

- **High** — source-secure or convergent proposition whose alternatives do not threaten its core wording;
- **Medium-High** — strong inference or interpretation with bounded remaining alternatives;
- **Medium** — useful but materially contestable;
- **Low** — hypothesis only;
- **Open** — the source does not currently discriminate among important alternatives.

The stabilized transition vocabulary is:

`PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN / REVISE / DOWNGRADE / REJECT / OPEN`

Older TBHX terms such as `CONFIRMED`, `STRENGTHENED`, `NARROWED`, `CORRECTED`, `OVERTURNED`, and `UNRESOLVED` remain visible in the preserved history but route into this current vocabulary.

## Load-bearing current claims

The most important stabilized propositions include:

### Trust and Fear

> **Trust and Fear are socially generated but materially consequential forces whose effects depend on expectation, identity, bearer receptivity, relational history, institutional mediation, and material exceptions.**

The ledger rejects the reductions:

- Trust = popularity;
- Fear = bad reputation;
- self-belief = a second numeric Trust meter;
- Trust/Fear = one universal zero-sum equation;
- one believer = one Trust point;
- X's snap = universal prerequisite;
- X's coin = binary fate selector.

### Chronology

The E21–E23 Original Nice–Smile–Liang–A-Hu chain is canonically placed before E01. Original Nice's suicide is therefore the endpoint of a longer multicausal destabilization history rather than an isolated consequence of perfection.

Broadcast and diegetic order remain separate authorities. Later truth does not erase the causal effect of earlier public stories or the reasonableness of frozen prospective readings.

### Personhood

> **Representation may be real, useful, and empowering without being entitled to own the person represented.**

The positive criterion is answerability. A comparatively legitimate role permits provenance, reply, reinterpretation, failure, responsibility, reciprocal relation, privacy, and survivable exit.

The terminal failure is totalization: one image, wound, utility, or office becomes the whole being and claims jurisdiction over the person's future.

### Public narrative

> **Public narrative is a causal institution.**

The mature process is:

`event → selection → narration → circulation → uptake → material feedback → recontextualization`

Exposure does not automatically restore authorship; private memory is not automatically omniscient; media can expose and protect as well as manufacture and capture.

### Institutions

> **Hero society is a polycentric constitutional market.**

Its institutions perform necessary coordination, research, allocation, verification, and emergency functions. They become illegitimate when brand, rank, research value, continuity, or catastrophic-risk classification outranks the bearer as an answerable person.

The Commission's fear of another Zero is historically rational. Its E24 preemptive anti-X method remains procedurally self-defeating and fails the stabilized proportionality/contestability test.

### Relationships

> **Recognition becomes personhood-preserving when the recognized person can answer, revise, fail, dissent, and survive changes in the relation.**

Private recognition is not inherently superior to public recognition. Care does not excuse coercion. Protection, presence, and comfort remain distinct. The positive standard is answerable interdependence rather than isolation.

### Language and form

Language assigns roles and can make or break the bearer's capacity to answer them. Representation is material social infrastructure. The corpus rejects:

- legal-name purity;
- performance versus authenticity;
- 2D truth versus 3D falsehood;
- one-color/one-mechanic coding;
- screens as intrinsically deceptive;
- every style shift as an X operation.

X's late-season capability is stabilized as **representational sovereignty**, not generic unlimited “reality warping.”

### X and Zero

Zero is the full-series limit case of role, public will, divinity, and personal justice totalizing into sovereignty.

X currently resists that structure through presentation plurality, ordinary work, friendship, privacy, refusal of overtime, and role exit. Those practices are ethically significant but do not constitute a durable constitutional safeguard.

The season does not resolve literal versus structural Zero persistence, exact X ontology, coin mechanics, or a legitimate reciprocal settlement between X and the Commission.
