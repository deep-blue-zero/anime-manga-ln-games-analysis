# To Be Hero X V2 — Specialist 06 E24 Nice-Return Revision Checkpoint

## 1. Revision trigger

The first canonical version of `06_LIN_LING_NICE_MOON_AND_THE_REPLACEMENT_HERO.md` correctly stabilized Lin Ling, Original Nice, Nice as office/brand/body, and Xiao Yueqing/Moon, but it treated the Season-1 endpoint too linearly: Original Nice dies, Lin differentiates himself from Nice, and E24 administratively regularizes Lin's succession.

A subsequent reinspection of the E24 tournament convergence exposed a material omission.

`TBHX_V2_E24_VISUAL_MICROSEQUENCE_LEDGER.md` already records **Lin Ling** and **Nice** as separately foregrounded entrant identities in the tournament sequence. Supplemental direct viewing identifies the Nice entrant as visually matching Original Nice while bearing conspicuous crack-like markings across the face/body.

The specialist document is therefore revised in place to version 1.1.

## 2. Evidence classification

### Frame-secure

- E24 separately foregrounds **Lin Ling** and **Nice** in the tournament convergence.
- The endpoint cannot be modeled as a simple one-body succession in which Lin is the sole present Nice bearer.

### High-confidence provisional visual observation

- The separate Nice entrant visually reads as an Original-Nice-like body with conspicuous cracks/fracture markings.
- The exact entrant frames have not yet been preserved in the targeted AV evidence layer, so the morphology remains explicitly marked as supplemental direct-view evidence rather than a fully packeted AV finding.

### Open

Season 1 does not establish:

- literal reincarnation;
- literal resurrection;
- whether consciousness or memory are continuous;
- Trust/Fear-mediated reconstitution;
- institutional reconstruction;
- duplication or role-body instantiation;
- the mechanical meaning of the cracks;
- or the exact legal/ranking relation between Lin Ling and the returned Nice figure.

Current bounded terminology is **apparent Original Nice reappearance** / **cracked Nice entrant**.

## 3. Canonical conceptual revision

The previous endpoint:

`Original Nice death → Lin succession → institutional regularization`

is replaced by:

`Original Nice death`
→ `Lin coerced replacement`
→ `Lin self-differentiation and public self-naming`
→ `institutional regularization`
→ **`Lin Ling / Nice public bifurcation`**
→ **`apparent cracked Nice reappearance / coexistence`**.

The mature Specialist-06 claim is now:

> **Nice continuity can fork rather than merely transfer. E24 makes Lin's differentiation from Nice externally legible by presenting Lin Ling and Nice as distinct entrant identities, while leaving the ontology of the cracked Nice figure unresolved.**

This strengthens the monograph's answerability thesis and changes the governance problem from succession alone to **coexistence under uncertain person continuity**.

## 4. Consequences for Original Nice

Original Nice's E01 death remains a secure event. The revision does not downgrade the suicide, erase its multicausal prehistory, or treat it as a temporary trick.

What changes is the proposition that death proves permanent absence.

If the E24 figure is personally continuous with Original Nice, the series radicalizes its exit problem: even bodily death may fail to guarantee release from a socially entrenched hero identity. If the figure is reconstructed or reconstituted rather than continuous, the ethical problem remains adjacent: Nice may have enough institutional, social, or metaphysical persistence to generate an embodied future beyond the dead bearer's consent.

The mechanism remains open.

## 5. Consequences for Lin Ling

Lin's E04 achievement is strengthened.

The system once imposed:

`Lin = Nice`.

E24 can now present:

`Lin Ling ≠ Nice`.

Lin does not need to erase the Nice identity in order to remain publicly legible as Lin Ling. The endpoint therefore validates differentiation more strongly than administrative contracting alone.

At the same time, coexistence reopens questions about rank, inherited obligations, contracts, public memory, and the division of responsibility between Lin and the returned Nice figure.
