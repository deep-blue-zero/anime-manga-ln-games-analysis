## 2. Governing proposition

> **Zero is the limit case in which hero identity, mass Trust, personal justice, divine status, and sovereign jurisdiction become one totalized person-world. X is the strongest current counter-model because he preserves multiple presentations, ordinary labor, friendship, privacy, refusal, and exit while exercising representational/world-state sovereignty. Yet X's ethics remain bearer-dependent rather than constitutionally guaranteed. Season 1 therefore does not solve the problem of godhood by finding a good god. It asks whether any public, institution, or hero may convert socially produced power into unanswerable authority—and answers that freedom begins with preserving the practical right to remain more than the role the world needs.**

## 3. Stabilized findings

1. **X is not a certified good god.** His current private-role plurality is the strongest anti-totalization practice in the source, but it can be abandoned and is not externally guaranteed.
2. **Broadcast order is central.** X first appears as an apex office without an administratively legible person, then as an ordinary worker/friend, then as a causally incomprehensible intervener, anonymous helper, targeted risk, forbidden historian, and tournament entrant.
3. **E11 establishes epistemic excess.** The system can recognize X before it can identify or explain the bearer; Queen's defeat destabilizes sovereign mastery rather than disproving her diagnosis of systemic volatility.
4. **Ordinary life is constitutive.** Work, home, glasses, sleepwear, coworkers, friendship, and refusal of overtime are practical alternative identities, not comic residue beneath the “real” hero.
5. **Smile supplies person-preserving recognition.** He knows both bearer and role, can ask for identity change, and can survive X's refusal.
6. **Godhood can be assigned against bearer desire.** Public attention, rank, believer concentration, and causal incomprehensibility can totalize a bearer who does not seek worship.
7. **`信徒` is theological vocabulary.** Extreme Trust turns support into mass adherence and makes the bearer socially legible as a god.
8. **Public will is causal without being morally sovereign.** It can create power, bodies, offices, and gods from incomplete or false stories while remaining fragmented, mediated, and reversible.
9. **The Commission's anti-apotheosis purpose is legitimate.** Zero supplies a rational basis for constraining extreme Trust concentration and recurring apex office.
10. **Tournament succession is constitutional technology.** It creates contestability and anti-monopoly discipline; the anti-X plot loses legitimacy by bypassing that procedure.
11. **Zero's heroic origin does not absolve him.** The forbidden history relocates the catastrophe inside hero society while preserving the judgment that Zero became a tyrant and killed dissenters.
12. **Zero is apotheotic totalization.** Person, hero, god, judge, justice, sovereign, and world order become one identity without external review.
13. **Trust-to-Fear reversal does not repair the public relation.** Mass Fear can overthrow or demonize a god without restoring due process, historical understanding, or the dead.
14. **Demon King Zero is a selective founding lie.** It preserves the catastrophe and hero coalition while concealing that mass Trust created the first tyrant.
15. **The founding lie has dual function.** It legitimizes the Commission politically and plausibly contains dangerous Trust/Fear memory metaphysically.
16. **Historical containment remains dangerous.** The same logic can make one institution the unreviewable sovereign of causal memory and can drive suppressed history into restoration cults.
17. **`Zero其实从未泯灭` is structurally secure first.** The Trust/Fear pattern, institution, myth, cult, and apotheosis possibility persist; bodily/conscious survival remains open.
18. **Yan Mo's late role is political-theological.** He converts suppressed memory into cultivation of future godhood, while exact resurrection/successor objectives remain unresolved.
19. **X's power is representational/world-state sovereignty.** Demonstrated operations span self-rendering, image-object, planar, spatial, object-state, multi-subject, shared-history, and cross-substrate continuity.
20. **Generic reality warping is rejected.** The source does not establish unlimited rewriting, fourth-wall authority, universal time travel, or control over every style change.
21. **The finger snap is recurrent but not necessary.** It functions as interface cue and role-switch sign without being a universal prerequisite.
22. **Identity persists across substrate.** X, A-Hu, Liang, and selected objects remain continuous across some representational transformations.
23. **World-state power can communicate.** X's “come look” history-space gives A-Hu material to answer rather than legislating a world according to X's justice.
24. **History-space is authored testimony.** It is diegetically shared and narratively authoritative but not proven to be an independently neutral archive.
25. **X exits Liang's retaliation grammar.** He changes the category of the encounter rather than returning greater harm, but the crowd's `神` response creates a new apotheosis risk.
26. **Anonymous E23 help is meaningful.** X acts without requiring public credit, though his anonymity contributes to the incomplete story that later ranks A-Hu.
27. **The pendant marks power's limit.** X can preserve Smile's private provenance but cannot restore the dead or repair Nice's grief through sovereignty.
28. **Anti-X fear is historically rational.** The operation remains illegitimate because capacity and suspicion become a secret preventive death sentence without proportionality or contestability.
29. **Autonomy is misread as domination.** The coalition treats refusal of institutional ownership as proof that X intends to own everything.
30. **The home invasion is self-defeating.** It attacks the private/role boundary that most clearly distinguishes current X from Zero.
31. **X uses power to leave.** Rooftop, car, bar, and scene transitions repeatedly serve escape, conversation, and termination rather than permanent rule.
32. **A constitutional settlement remains absent.** X's personal ethics cannot substitute for reciprocal limits, and the Commission's protective mission cannot authorize unanswerable classification or killing.
33. **X/A-Hu is adversarial trust.** Direct threat, preserved secrecy, dissent, shared exposure, and forbidden testimony make friendship too strong and mere enmity too weak.
34. **The coin is a false beginning.** X once treated it as the origin of his hero path; E24 places it inside already-sown antecedent causes and never uses the face as a selector.
35. **Dominoes make the omnibus causal.** Trust/Fear relations push lives into one another's worlds, but montage adjacency is not a literal one-edge graph.
36. **Seed causality is not strict determinism.** `都早已被种下` establishes antecedents while preserving variation, interruption, and choice.
37. **Fate resistance is non-repetition.** X does not escape causality; he refuses the expected outcome that causal inheritance appears to demand.
38. **`准点下班` is the terminal anti-apotheosis practice.** Literal labor, role boundary, anti-institutional limit, and anti-fatalism converge in the right to be finished.
39. **The credits overlay intensifies the labor argument.** Real production workers' names accompany X's refusal of infinite labor claims; this is formal rhyme, not proven fourth-wall awareness.
40. **The tournament is both constitution and flattening mechanism.** Heterogeneous lives become contestable in one field while trauma, locality, species, and private history become commensurable rank.
