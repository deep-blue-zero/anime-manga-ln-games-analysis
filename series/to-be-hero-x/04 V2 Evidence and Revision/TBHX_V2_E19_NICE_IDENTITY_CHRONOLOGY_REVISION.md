# To Be Hero X V2 — Episode 19 Nice Identity Chronology Revision

## 1. Reason for revision

The prospectively frozen E19 episode reading correctly preserved the **episode-local** observation:

> a figure recognized by Luo Tong and Wang Nuonuo as Hero Nice appears and attacks them, while E19 itself does not name the bearer.

That local-evidence statement remains historically valid.

However, once official creator chronology is admitted as project-level evidence, the broader identity claim can be resolved much further.

## 2. Official chronology evidence

Director Haolin's official Q&A states that Episodes 1–4 depict a **later portion of the overall story first**, effectively in reverse, and that subsequent arcs rewind through characters' pasts and different timelines before returning to the Nice timeline to tell the story **before Nice's jump**.

An Aniplex producer interview further states that the omnibus episodes are not isolated: incidents affect one another and are sometimes depicted from **different viewpoints**.

This establishes that broadcast order is intentionally distinct from diegetic chronology.

## 3. Consequence for Episode 19

Within E01:

1. Original Nice is alive at the beginning.
2. Original Nice jumps/dies.
3. only afterward is Lin Ling installed as the replacement Nice.

Episode 19 belongs to the pre-E01 chronological convergence created by the rewind structure.

Therefore Lin Ling has not yet inherited the Nice identity at the E19 diegetic point.

The hero publicly recognized as Nice in E19 should consequently be identified as:

> **Original Nice**

rather than Lin Ling.

## 5. What remains unresolved

Resolving the bearer does **not** resolve the scene's causal or psychological questions.

Still open at the E19 boundary:

- Why is Original Nice attempting to kill Luo Tong and Wang Nuonuo?
- Is he acting voluntarily?
- Is Fear involved?
- Is another actor controlling or manipulating him?
- Is this connected to the later destabilization that culminates in his E01 condition and suicide?
- How does this pre-E01 appearance connect to the creator-promised story of Nice before his jump and his later "darkened" state?
- What exact relation exists between Original Nice's public image and the lethal conduct shown here?

## 6. Methodological implication

Future TBHX analysis should preserve two clocks:

1. **broadcast revelation order**
2. **diegetic chronology**

A claim may be unresolved at the episode-local broadcast boundary while being resolvable at the project level through already-authorized chronology evidence.

The two states should not be collapsed.

For E19:

- episode-local epistemic state: Nice bearer not named;
- project-level chronological state: Original Nice.

This distinction should be carried into Episode 20 so that E20 is used to explain **what happened to Original Nice**, not to re-litigate whether the attacker is Lin Ling.
