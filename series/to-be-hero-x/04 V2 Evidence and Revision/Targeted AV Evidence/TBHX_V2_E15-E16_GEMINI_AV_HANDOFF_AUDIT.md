## 5. Literary integration

The incoming material is most useful when it makes a distinction more legible rather than announcing that all uncertainty has vanished. Wang Yi's fluent internal language and restricted external communication allow the audience access that Nuonuo lacks. That asymmetry can create sympathy for him while preserving her legitimate experience of threatening silence. An analyst must not use privileged narration to make her earlier fear unreasonable.

The notebook changes the evidence relation. Surveillance produces information held by the observer; the encountered notebook gives Nuonuo a trace she can read and interpret. That is a meaningful movement toward answerability. It still does not supply an ordinary conversation, a right to acceptance, or proof that she now feels comforted. `P2-CR-R05` and `R13` therefore survive the handoff's warmer framing.

The mission frame also preserves an ethical asymmetry. Ah Sheng is named in E15, but the father, family, former hero, caregiver, and institutional target are not yet equally available to the viewer. E17 supplies excluded biography. The error is not simply that the assassin lacks a name; it is that a numbered professional category lets him avoid the relational content of a person. The candidate's invented late order would flatten this broadcast structure and falsely make the commissioning motive explicit.

Music may give the audience a register unavailable to Wang Yi's ordinary speech, but that is a formal interpretation of score and lyric placement. It does not mean he literally sings to Nuonuo or that she hears every confession supplied to viewers. The titled-song question remains separable from the validity of that interpretation.
