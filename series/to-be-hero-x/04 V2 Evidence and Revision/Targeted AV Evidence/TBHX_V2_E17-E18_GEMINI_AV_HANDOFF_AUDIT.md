## 5. Analytical consequence

The distinction between local resistance and universal immunity is important to the Johnnies' personhood argument. Big Johnny does not acquire dignity by becoming physically invulnerable or harmless. Little Johnny's family recognition precedes the public emergency and survives the need to control real danger. The existing account can therefore preserve both kinship and legitimate safety questions without allowing research value or monster classification to exhaust the being.

The handoff's stronger immunity claim would undo that precision. Even if the living organism and the spacecraft share an extraterrestrial origin, a failed direct spatial operation does not prove equal responses to every Trust effect, Fear transmission, environmental attack, or material injury. The scientist's material theory, a local combat result, and the analyst's shared-substrate hypothesis remain different propositions.

The purported clone laboratory creates a parallel institutional risk. Micky, Liu Zhen, Shang De, Yan Mo, the Commission, and independent handlers have intersecting projects. A visually suggestive sample, a scientist's claim of control, and later clone violence cannot be compressed into one actor's fully demonstrated production chain without source evidence. `P2-CR-I13` is preserved precisely because the source resists one-mastermind compression.

Finally, the failure of the report's clocks does not erase its serious formal questions. A-Sheng's flowers, Little Johnny's inherited grief practice, the public conversion of kinship into a power label, and Fear making a past father perceptually present all merit AV review. Their analytical value does not depend on accepting false HUD precision or invented quotations.
