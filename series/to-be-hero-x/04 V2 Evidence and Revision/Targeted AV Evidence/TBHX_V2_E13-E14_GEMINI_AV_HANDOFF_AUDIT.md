## 5. What the source adds to the argument

The gym display gives a concrete, local embodiment to an argument already present in the mature corpus. The spectators do not discover a new lack of competence: their classification changes after Luo Li's appearance becomes legible. The visible meter and altered arm make that act of reading materially consequential. This strengthens the distinction between demonstrated conduct and the public's willingness to recognize it. It does not warrant assigning the one-unit change to one specific man or inferring a season-wide equation.

Luo Tong's briefing broadens the arc without dissolving its personal problem. Luo Li uses technical work to control how competence and cuteness are encountered; the same material becomes an institutional research opportunity. These are connected causal uses, not proof that her own authorship is reducible to her father's discovery. His proposal also makes research ambivalent: a potentially protective technology can support medical work and new forms of control. The proposal's conditional language matters.

Nuonuo's confrontation similarly holds two truths together. Her objection to objectifying judgments is legitimate; her identification of the immediate threat is mistaken. Later paternal provenance changes the audience's explanation without invalidating the fear she experienced. None of this establishes that paternal protection repairs a relationship without her participation.
