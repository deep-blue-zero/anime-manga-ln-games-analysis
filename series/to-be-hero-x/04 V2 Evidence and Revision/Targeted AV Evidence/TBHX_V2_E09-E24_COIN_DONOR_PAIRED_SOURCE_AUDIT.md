# E09/E24 coin-donor paired-source audit

## 1. Decision

**Evidence intake `TBHX-AV-E09-001` — confirmed retrospective identity.** The nighttime coin donor of E09 (WEB 11:44–12:03) is the person later known as X, in his black-haired, bespectacled, dark-suited civilian presentation. E24 (WEB 20:02–20:25) establishes this retrospectively. It re-stages the E09 encounter from the donor's side — the same night square, the same recipient, the same coin and the two distinctive E09 compositions of the coin landing and the donor's departing wave — shows the donor's face, carries the same departing figure directly into X's finger snap and his white-suited public presentation, and sets over it X's first-person narration `我本以为自己的英雄之路 / 是从那枚硬币开始的` (“I had thought my own hero path began with that coin”).

The identification is **retrospective**. E09 deliberately withholds the donor's face, and the frozen E09 reading's prospective record (“A coin drops”) remains the correct statement of what a viewer could know at that boundary.

The adjudication settles **who gave the coin**. It does not establish that the coin activates X's powers, chooses timelines, changes fate, transfers luck, or causes Qīng's (Lucky Cyan's) abilities or X's; it does not establish X's motive, the date on which he first acted as X, or that Qīng ever learns who the donor was.

Consequences in brief:

- `E08A-D2` (E08–E10 audit) and `E24-D03` (E24 audit) are **satisfied** for donor identity;
- the E08–E10 Gemini report's alternative attribution of the donor to **Liu Zhen** is **rejected**;
- the nighttime encounter remains **distinct** from the daytime tipper and from the later public busking montage;
- the reported wrist-counter “zero” is **rejected as a display reading** — the zero-shaped image belongs to Qīng's eyes, not the wrist — and the exact final display value remains **OPEN**, narrowed;
- the coin's **mechanism** remains a protected open question.

## 7. Adjudication

**Accepted formulation (`TBHX-AV-E09-001`, High):** *The nighttime donor of E09 WEB 11:44–12:03 is the person later known as X, in his black-haired, bespectacled, dark-suited civilian presentation. E24 WEB 20:02–20:25 re-stages that encounter, shows his face, carries the departing figure into X's snap and public presentation, and sets X's first-person narration over it. The identification is retrospective; E09 withholds the donor's face.*

**Why confirmed rather than “strong retrospective identification.”** Five channels converge: the revealed face matches the established civilian-X presentation; the montage repeats two distinctive E09 compositions with the same place and recipient; the coin design matches; an unbroken back-view figure becomes X on screen; and X claims the coin as his own remembered beginning in the first person. The remaining ambiguities are not material to the identity. E09's withheld face is a broadcast-order device that E24 resolves. The corpus's caution that X's history-space is authored testimony rather than a neutral archive bears on the completeness and framing of what X shows, not on whether X can identify an act he narrates as his own; nothing in the source contests the identification, and the visual correspondence is constructed by the work's editing against E09 footage presented without X's mediation.

## 8. Wrist display and eye image

At E09 12:37.58–12:38.96, sampled at 8 fps, Qīng's wrist readout — which E09 later identifies in dialogue as her Trust Value — cycles rapidly through distorted two-digit glyph states, shows a solid bar at 12:39.08, and holds a single stylized glyph at 12:39.21–12:39.58. The final E09 glyph is open on its left side, so it is not the closed zero form; it is more consistent with a non-zero digit, possibly the flagged one seen obliquely on the curved band, but the angle prevents archival transcription.

## 9. Symbol and mechanism

**Secure:** the coin is a recurring object associated with X — handled before his E20 civilian/public transition (`X-RS-01`), present in his E24 history-space, and featured as a gold coin in the opening sequence (paratext; no design identity is claimed for the opening) — and it is now also an object that passed from X to Qīng.

**Open:** whether the coin has any mechanical function. Nothing shown makes it activate powers, select timelines, alter fate, transfer luck, or cause Qīng's abilities or X's. X's own narration demotes it from origin (`本以为 … 但其实 … 都早已被种下`). The result of the toss is not consulted as a decision (the existing rejection of `CL-E24-M23` stands).

**Two lives, asymmetrically.** For X, the coin is the event he once took for the beginning of his hero path. For Qīng, as far as the source shows, it is one of the first coins of her post-escape self-support; E09 places her `在此之前 / 我要先学会自食其力` immediately after she picks it up. That adjacency is `F-DLG`/`F-VIS`; any claim that the coin prompted the resolve is inference (medium).

**Knowledge and relation.** Nothing shows Qīng learning who the donor was; her E09 state acts without that knowledge. The relation shown is directed and unilateral: X → Qīng, an anonymous, unclaimed gift followed by departure; no Qīng → X relation is shown.

**Chronology candidate.** X's self-narrated threshold falls after Qīng's escape from the orphanage and before her later daytime busking, her DOS recruitment and the E09 `联合36年` card, by which she is already publicly Lucky Cyan. The exact year, X's age, and whether he already acted as a hero before that night remain open. This candidate is recorded here for the chronology ledger's next review; it is not yet propagated there.
