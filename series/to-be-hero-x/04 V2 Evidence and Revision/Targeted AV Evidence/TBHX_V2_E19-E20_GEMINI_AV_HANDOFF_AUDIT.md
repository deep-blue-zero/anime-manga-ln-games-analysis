## 4. What the evidence adds

The ruins sequence is a convergence of incompatible projects rather than proof that every institution serves one concealed ruler. The Commission's suppression research, Luo Tong's scientific ambition, Micky's competitive sabotage, Shang De's Fear production, Yan Mo's Zero project, and the heroes' acts of protection share a field while retaining different goals. The restored clauses sharpen this plurality: suppression concerns both affects; the corporate plot anticipates public blame; the shrine makes restoration an explicit personal commitment. Collapsing them into one plot would discard the very causal distinctions the evidence supplies.

Likewise, local resistance remains compromised. Ghostblade's protective conduct does not retrospectively erase Nuonuo's abandonment. Luo Li's competence does not make every technical intervention morally neutral. Queen's containment and Cyan's aid do not turn Big Johnny into an object without standing. Yang's ranking ambition cannot be explained away by his earlier relational recognition. The handoff is most useful when it gives these tensions firmer source locators, not when its shorthand resolves them.
