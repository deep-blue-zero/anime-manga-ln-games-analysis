## 4. What the finale evidence does to interpretation

The cracked Nice presentation is consequential because it interrupts a simple succession story. Lin has fought to become nameable as Lin rather than merely another body carrying Nice. The finale foregrounds both identities within one public field. The fissures make that persistence visibly damaged, but they do not tell the viewer whether a dead bearer has returned, an image has been reconstructed, or another mechanism is operating. Preserving that uncertainty strengthens the ethical question: what standing, memory, consent and accountability would belong to each bearer if a role and a person can persist by different means? Calling the image a fully explained resurrection would close that question before the source does.

The distinction also disciplines the paratext boundary. Credits are already overlaid during the live introductions. The fact that a credit is visible does not erase diegetic action. Conversely, the rapid montage after the final exchange cannot automatically be mined as later event history. Source form must determine which boundary applies to a particular image; a blanket rule about all credit-bearing footage would misclassify the checked Nice sequence.

Zero and the coin operate through a different but related refusal of easy origin. X's statement that Zero has not perished is explicit, yet its conceptual surroundings concern the coexistence of Trust and Fear and the survival of causal formations. The coin appears amid antecedents rather than as an independently demonstrated first cause. These are robust full-series insights without requiring a claim that X can choose timelines or that Cyan's power retroactively created him. The historical model remains serious while its literal machinery stays open.

Finally, the twenty-first tournament matters as a small correction with broad retrieval consequences. The suspended twentieth event belongs to an earlier administrative history; the latest convergence is the twenty-first event already counted down in E04. Correcting that number does not revise the prospective readings' knowledge or create new institutional history. It makes the current chronology consistent with its own source witnesses.
