## 4. Analytical consequence

The corrected rank locator sharpens an existing distinction rather than creating a new hero-origin story. A-Hu is recognized by Xinya before the Commission ranks him. He chooses to protect her and the neighborhood before the public can compare him with Nice. Shang De then uses the rank to degrade another bearer, showing how institutional valuation can turn a genuine achievement into an instrument of conditional personhood. This is why the same eighth-place fact belongs in both local-heroism and political-economy retrieval.

Nice's crisis runs through that comparison but cannot be reduced to it. His insistence `我是奈斯` confronts a role that has become his only permitted answer, while A-Hu says `我要成为` and claims a future direction grounded in an existing relation. The form distinguishes fixed identity from chosen becoming. It does not establish a clinical diagnosis or prove that one drop, memory, insult or fear mathematically caused suicide. Those stronger claims would make the corpus less accurate even though the season clearly links the events.
