## 2. E24 terminal thesis

E24 reveals that hero society's founding catastrophe was internally produced: the first hero Zero accumulated effectively limitless Trust, became godlike, remade the world according to personal justice, killed dissenting heroes, and converted public Trust into Fear.

The Association's public `魔王Zero` history externalizes that contradiction while also plausibly serving affective/metaphysical containment by burying Zero in collective memory.

X is the principal counterexample to Zero not because he lacks comparable categorical power, but because he preserves boundaries between role and person.

Current governing X formulation:

> **X does not reject power. He rejects totality.**

Epigram:

> **“I am not your God. I am going home.”**
