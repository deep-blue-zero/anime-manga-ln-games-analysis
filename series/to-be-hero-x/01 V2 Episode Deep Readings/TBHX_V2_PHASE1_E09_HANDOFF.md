## Canonical E09 thesis

E09 is best understood as **chosen re-authoring under persistent mythology**.

Qīng successfully escapes the orphanage's direct control, but the Sacred Maiden social function follows her into public space. Her first post-escape ambition is not fame or hero ranking: it is `自食其力`, the ability to support herself, so that she can return for Luo. Busking begins as material survival and relational memory. The public gradually recognizes her, phones and network circulation amplify the encounter, Sacred Maiden language reappears, blessing seekers gather, visible Trust makes her legible to DOS, and she is eventually publicly named `幸运青` / Lucky Qīng.

The episode therefore distinguishes **physical exit** from **social exit**. A person can leave the institution that imposed a role without being able to make everyone else forget what that role meant.

At the same time, E09 is not a story of total recapture. Qīng tells blessing seekers not to pursue her. She conditions her DOS recruitment; the Japanese witness specifies a request to find someone, strongly pointing to Luo. Her hero decision is connected to Luo's own aspiration. Most importantly, she uses her first major concert to tell the public that an absent friend gave her the guitar, song, and determination that made the visible hero possible.

That act is best retained as **provenance resistance**: Qīng does not negate the new public identity; she makes it answerable to the relationship that produced it.

## Major state changes

### Qīng

- escaped direct orphanage possession;
- retains accumulated public Trust outside the orphanage;
- explicitly recognizes self-support as a prerequisite for freedom;
- rejects renewed Sacred Maiden blessing demand;
- enters DOS conditionally rather than through demonstrated force;
- becomes publicly known as `幸运青` / Lucky Qīng;
- publicly restores Luo to the origin story of her visible identity.

### Luo

- explicitly assists Qīng's escape;
- receives stronger evidence of a genuine bad-luck effect, though mechanics remain unresolved;
- remains physically separated from Qīng;
- is the source of the guitar, `My Color`, and hero aspiration that Qīng carries forward;
- hears her concert remotely by radio;
- is told by the director to forget Qīng “for her sake.”

### Orphanage director

- now explicitly tied to a hero-development theory for children through Luo's report;
- connects Qīng to his missed hero dream in his own words;
- converts prior opportunity and mass Trust into a debt/ownership claim;
- remains morally more complicated than a purely cynical exploiter because his original care and social-service motives are not erased.

### DOS / Ken / Mǐjī

- Ken becomes the direct recruiter who converts visible Trust into a hero-career offer;
- DOS occupies a double position: it has leverage over a materially vulnerable runaway but also possesses resources Qīng wants to use to find Luo;
- Mǐjī treats Qīng's luck/crash-survivor profile as recruitable value;
- institutional authorship remains important but has not yet erased Qīng's capacity to state purpose.

## Updated personhood criteria through E09

The cumulative corpus now supports several distinct safeguards against social-role capture:

- recognition of the person beneath/through the role;
- ability to refuse an imposed identity;
- ability to revise the role;
- ability to leave;
- ability to fail/disappoint the role;
- practical/material ability to survive after exit;
- ability to choose what obligations the public identity will serve;
- preservation of relational provenance rather than presenting a hero as self-created public property.

## Public/private and media findings

The strongest formal sequence is the busking montage:

`survival -> first listener/coin -> expanding crowd -> phones -> network circulation -> Sacred Maiden recognition -> blessing seekers -> visible Trust -> agency professionalization`.

The essential conclusion is that **social functions can reproduce without a central controller**. The public can rebuild the altar after Qīng leaves the institution that built the first one.

The final arena/radio crosscut adds the inverse media problem: Qīng becomes maximally present to a mass audience while remaining materially unable to reach Luo. Broadcast preserves symbolic intimacy while leaving physical domination unresolved.
