E03 freezes a distinction between:

- **externally imposed exposure**.

**E04 test:** does the episode preserve a moral/relational difference between God Eye revealing facts and Lin choosing to state them himself?

## 2.3 Trust as social authorship

- change bodies;
- redirect powers;
- enforce public-image consistency;
- rise through spectacle and recognition;
- imprison genuine heroes as well as manufactured replacements.

**E04 test:** if Lin rejects Nice publicly, what exactly happens to Trust Value, appearance, power, ranking, and the audience's object of belief?

## 2.4 Juān jiě (娟姐)

Through E03 she represents the institutional logic that treats crisis, relationship, and identity as convertible ranking/brand material.

**E04 test:** does Lin's truth actually defeat her operating logic, or does Treeman simply adapt and incorporate the new narrative?

## 2.5 “Paragon”

The song was used in E01 over Lin's first major heroic breakthrough while he remained inside Nice's image. Its lyrics combine sincere heroic aspiration with warnings about the price and commodification of being a paragon.

The prior project history reports that E04 reuses “Paragon” during the climax.

**E04 test:** what changes when the same song accompanies a Lin who may now be rejecting or revising the Nice identity?
