## Leading model through E04

The strongest cross-episode Trust/personhood model is now:

- **E01 — overwrite:** belief makes Lin into an already-valued identity.
- **E02 — alignment/override:** belief can align with or override Xiao's chosen direction.
- **E03 — essentialization:** belief can freeze a situational virtue into permanent bodily obligation.
- **E04 — responsive amplification:** Lin acts from a self-authored commitment first; afterward belief attaches to Lin and amplifies him without restoring Nice morphology.

This yields the current leading ethical hypothesis:

> **The crucial distinction is not individual self versus social belief. It is whether social authorship dictates the person's direction in advance or responds to a direction the person has already chosen.**

This is still a hypothesis to stress-test, not the final series thesis.
