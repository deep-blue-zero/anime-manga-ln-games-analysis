## Major E17 findings to carry into E18

### 1. A-Sheng identity resolution

E15 had frozen `阿生` only as Ghostblade/Wang Yi's 175th completed mission, with a dying plea and Union-Year-22 anchor. E17 directly has Vortex address Xiao Qiang's father as `阿生`.

Therefore, at the E17 prospective boundary:

- A-Sheng = Xiao Qiang's father;
- A-Sheng = husband of Xiao Qiang's deceased mother;
- A-Sheng = retired healing hero;
- A-Sheng = protector of Da Qiang/Big Johnny;
- A-Sheng = Wang Yi/Ghostblade's previously established 175th completed mission.

The killer can therefore be identified prospectively as **Ghostblade/Wang Yi** because that knowledge came from E15. The **orderer, motive, and higher-order conspiracy remain OPEN**.

### 2. Xiao Qiang's Vortex suspicion is character-level misattribution

Xiao Qiang sees a recent tense Vortex visit, finds battle traces and his father gravely wounded, and asks whether “that person” did it. He vows to investigate and avenge his father.

Do not convert this into narrator fact. The audience's E15 knowledge identifies Ghostblade as the killer. Vortex may still have an unknown higher-order relationship to the event, but E17 does not establish one.

### 3. Big Johnny's origin is now source-established

A-Sheng says:

- the baby is not a life-form from their planet;
- an extraterrestrial spacecraft crashed in an uninhabited area a few days earlier;
- he found the baby during the investigation;
- the baby was the only being he found;
- if others discovered him, he might be researched or harmed.

Do **not** yet equate this wreck with E14's alien Trust/Fear-nullifying material. Treat same-wreck/same-substrate identity as a strong cross-arc hypothesis only.

### 4. Chosen kinship is direct, not metaphorical

Xiao Qiang:

- calls Da Qiang `我的儿子`;
- names him;
- brings him into the household;
- defends him against village children;
- rejects `邪祟之物` / `怪物` classification;
- refers to A-Sheng as grandfather within Da Qiang's family genealogy;
- imagines Da Qiang becoming a great hero.

Do not overclaim symmetric verbal cognition: Da Qiang's own conceptual understanding of “son/father” is not verbally accessible.

### 5. Danger does not erase subjecthood

Da Qiang later transforms into a gigantic black/cyan-marked creature and creates a genuine village emergency. This makes safety/containment concerns legitimate.

It does **not** prove the villagers' earlier monster label was a complete description of the being. The episode deliberately establishes household personhood before the dangerous transformation.

Do not attribute the transformation to Fear, Trust, love, or rage yet. Mechanism is OPEN.

### 6. A-Sheng's hero identity fails the intimate test

A-Sheng tries and fails to save his wife, then says:

`英雄又有什么用 / 到头来 / 我还是没能救下她`

Public/child-facing hero material later describes his power as `治愈万物`—healing all things. Treat this as heroic/public absolutism, **not literal omnipotent mechanics**.

### 7. Trust mechanics now include a strong self-belief proposition

A-Sheng says that after the Downfall, people no longer grant heroes powers surpassing gods.

Vortex says residual social remembrance/belief means A-Sheng's healing ability still lives.

A-Sheng replies:

`如果自己不相信自己的话 / 信赖值也都只是一堆没用的数字而已`

Current bounded model:

> social memory can preserve hero power/identity after retirement, but an experienced former hero says external Trust may become functionally useless if the person no longer believes in himself.

Do not invent a self-Trust stat, formula, or universal threshold.

### 8. Grief philosophy

A-Sheng transmits his deceased wife's teaching:

- sorrow cannot heal wounds;
- grief can reveal what matters/what is needed;
- cry fully when it is time to cry;
- smiling sustains hope.

This is a direct countermodel to E16 Shindig's extraction of sorrow as contagious power. Sorrow has epistemic value without becoming curative, compulsory, or a resource economy.

### 9. Vortex

E17 directly establishes `旋涡` / Vortex as:

- current rank-one hero;
- current X in this historical period;
- a former associate/rival familiar with A-Sheng;
- someone who wants A-Sheng to return because A-Sheng previously defeated an unnamed opponent;
- a public rescuer during Da Qiang's transformation;
- someone who proposes capturing Da Qiang and returning him to the Association for research.

Do not merge Vortex with the visually distinct unnamed new X from E11–E12.

Do not flatten Vortex into villainy: he has legitimate emergency-containment grounds. His research language nevertheless reproduces the exact specimenization A-Sheng feared.
