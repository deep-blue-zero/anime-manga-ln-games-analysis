## Major additions

### 1. `事实` versus `真相`

Micky notes that much of the circulating material is factually true. Qīng later insists:

`这是属于我的真相 / 必须由我自己去拿回来`

Treat this as an epistemic-personhood claim, not relativism.

### 2. Entrusted inheritance versus extractive hope

The flight recorder establishes that dying passengers freely entrust wishes, messages, and continued life to child Qīng. This revises any simple V1 claim that hope itself is extractive.

### 3. Fear feedback loop

Zero-sum rumor -> resentment/fear -> physical Fear manifestations -> real harm -> apparent confirmation of rumor.

Qīng is explicitly a `契机`, not the sole origin.

### 4. `My Color` provenance

Luo's parents sing the family melody during the crash, explaining why both Luo and Qīng later experience it as familiar.

### 5. Queen recognition

Qīng addresses `女王前辈`. Queen says Qīng's song—not her luck—gave her strength and led her out of gloom. This is a healthy recognition route outside luck utility.

### 6. Curated authenticity

DOS openly constructs a new `人设` from real Qīng traits while suppressing her past. Do not reduce hero-brand capture to invented fake personality.

### 7. Fear institutionalization

Association formally names `恐惧粒子` after the outbreak. Liu Zhen and Micky already possess non-public knowledge and warn that others also seek/watch Fear.
