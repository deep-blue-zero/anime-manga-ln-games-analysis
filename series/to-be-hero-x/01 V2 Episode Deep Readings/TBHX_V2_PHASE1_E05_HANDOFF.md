## Updated five-case Trust/personhood model

1. **E01 — overwrite**: belief imposes an already-valued identity.
2. **E02 — alignment/override**: belief can align with or override chosen desire.
3. **E03 — essentialization**: belief can freeze a situational virtue into permanent obligation.
4. **E04 — responsive amplification**: person acts first; public Trust later attaches to the disclosed person.
5. **E05 — voluntary role mediation/inheritance**: person acts first; relational Trust can amplify a voluntarily inhabited role whose values the person has made his own.

Working ethical test is no longer mask/face or alias/legal-name. Track:

- imposed vs chosen role;
- role-as-erasure vs role-as-practice;
- action before vs after social compulsion;
- surface imitation vs enacted value;
- capacity to revise/refuse the role once others invest in it.
