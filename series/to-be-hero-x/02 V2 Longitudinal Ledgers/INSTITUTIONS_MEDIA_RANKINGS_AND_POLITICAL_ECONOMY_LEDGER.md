## Political-economy finding PE-E01-01 — Hero brand rests on hidden labor

The first episode makes advertising workers and subcontracting part of hero production. Nice's image is not spontaneous charisma; it is manufactured by teams whose labor is economically separable from the celebrity who benefits.

## PE-E01-02 — Risk is transferred downward

The former boss is squeezed by a powerful client and transfers insecurity to Lin and later to workers through layoffs/wage threats. Episode 1 therefore depicts hierarchy as a chain rather than a two-class tableau.

## PE-E01-03 — Publicity and power are coupled

Because belief can produce Trust, media work potentially has direct power consequences. This makes publicity an infrastructural function, not decorative PR.

## PE-E01-04 — Ranking produces material inequality

Rank is not merely symbolic prestige. It determines living elevation/space in Hero Tower, indicating that Trust-linked status is converted into material privilege.

## Adversarial cautions

- The former boss is a biased witness to Treeman's contracting conduct; his specific claims are credible evidence of grievance, not independently audited accounting.
- One exploited supplier does not prove the entire hero economy functions identically.
- Hero Tower's verticality is strongly symbolic, but later evidence is needed before building a complete class theory from one building.


# Episode 02 institutional update

## PE-E02-01 — Brand continuity outranks carrier continuity

Juān jiě explicitly defines Nice as the company's most important brand rather than merely an individual. The institution's continuity logic is now textual, not inferred.

## PE-E02-02 — Romantic intimacy is contractualized content

Xiao's public girlfriend role is governed by contracts; fan demand for escalation produces pressure toward engagement/marriage. The hero economy therefore commodifies intimate status as an expandable content product.

## PE-E02-03 — Coercion can be operational rather than exceptional

Juān jiě locks the pair together and repeatedly ignores Xiao's attempts to terminate the arrangement. “Producing chemistry” becomes a justification for constraining movement and consent.

## PE-E02-04 — Antagonism is also hero-production labor

Wreck's public nemesis identity was cultivated to strengthen Nice. Hero branding therefore requires not only supportive staff and romantic partners but authored enemies.

## PE-E02-05 — Imperfection itself can be commodified

Juān jiě's `不完美有时候往往比完美更完美` converts obstruction, tragedy, and even an apparent death into higher-value romantic narrative. The brand apparatus can metabolize contradiction rather than merely suppress it.

## PE-E02-06 — Public mourning becomes Trust intervention

The false death story is circulated at scale; Lin then uses the public's wishes to reconfigure Xiao's power. Narrative management can therefore function as direct infrastructure for ability governance.

## PE-E02-07 — Ranking can preempt private direction

The top-ten announcement arrives precisely as Lin is asked where his own heart wants to go. Whether he consented off-screen remains unresolved, but the formal placement shows the brand institution publicly assigning trajectory before the episode lets his private answer be heard.

## PE-E02-08 — Spotlight offers a rival identity-production system

Where Treeman builds hero identity through Trust, contracts, and brand continuity, Spotlight recruits the discredited True Eye through Fear-oriented epistemology and power, ending by declaring the old person-name gone.

## Adversarial cautions after E02

- Lin and Xiao develop genuine affection under coercive conditions; that does not retrospectively legitimate Juān jiě's confinement or forced-romance strategy.
- The fake-death operation produces liberation, but its success does not make broad public deception ethically neutral.
- Spotlight's claims about Fear are recruitment rhetoric until independently verified as universal mechanics.

## E04 institutional synthesis

E04 weakens any model in which "truth" sits outside the hero economy. Once Lin becomes publicly legible as Lin, the market immediately sees a recruitable asset. Institutions can therefore capture not only deception/perfection but **authenticity, ordinariness, confession, and visible vulnerability**.

# Phase 2 stabilization — canonical institutional, media, ranking, and political-economy model

## P2 authority rule

This section is the current **Phase-2 authority** for full-series institutional analysis.

The earlier episode-by-episode entries remain part of the project's prospective record. They preserve what each episode made legible at the time. When a later revelation changes the interpretation of an earlier institution, this section governs full-series retrieval while the earlier entry remains intact as historical analysis.

The stabilized model distinguishes:

- **institutional function** — what an organization, platform, ranking, office, or procedure does;
- **institutional justification** — the reason it gives for doing it;
- **material incentive** — the status, revenue, risk transfer, or strategic value produced;
- **personhood consequence** — what the arrangement permits or denies the people it governs;
- **constitutional effect** — how power is distributed, checked, concentrated, or made governable;
- **protected uncertainty** — what the 24-episode source does not establish.

The governing principle is:

> **Hero society is not a single conspiracy. It is a polycentric field in which public belief, private capital, platforms, offices, laboratories, rankings, media, and security institutions compete to govern socially generated power.**

This matters because the field can contain real checks and still produce coordinated coercion. It can solve genuine collective-action problems and still convert persons into managed assets.

## P2-IN-02 — the hero economy converts belief into governable assets

The series' political economy is not simply a celebrity economy with supernatural side effects. Belief is itself part of the productive base.

A recurrent conversion chain is:

`attention`
→ `legibility`
→ `public expectation`
→ `Trust/Fear`
→ `body/power/constraint`
→ `ranking`
→ `contracts/access/status`
→ `further attention`.

Institutions intervene at nearly every step.

They:

- design images;
- select names and costumes;
- arrange relationships and rivals;
- promote or suppress stories;
- create performance opportunities;
- manage scandals;
- classify crises;
- control access to research;
- allocate ranking and residence;
- and translate public affect into organizational value.

The economic and metaphysical systems therefore cannot be separated cleanly. Publicity is not decorative support for power. It helps produce the social conditions in which power becomes available, stable, contested, or lost.

### Consequence

> **The market does not merely price hero power after it appears. It helps constitute the social reality from which hero power emerges.**

This makes marketing, journalism, platform design, legal contracting, and technical infrastructure part of the world's power system.

## P2-IN-03 — rank is a constitutional office, a market price, and a disciplinary metric

The stabilized ranking model must preserve several functions at once.

### Constitutional function

After Zero, indefinite concentration of mass Trust is an existential risk. Ranking succession and tournament competition disperse attention and make continued top status contestable.

E21–E24 therefore support a serious anti-apotheosis purpose:

> **rank is one instrument for preventing public belief from hardening around a permanent god-sovereign.**

### Market function

Rank affects:

- employment value;
- sponsorship and office leverage;
- media access;
- residence/status in Hero Tower;
- investment;
- recruitment;
- contract credibility;
- and opportunities to convert crises into advancement.

### Disciplinary function

Rank makes bearer value continuously measurable. Managers can compare heroes, threaten replacement, and convert temporary underperformance into identity crisis.

Shang De's treatment of Original Nice after A-Hu reaches rank 8 is the clearest expression:

> the bearer is judged against the commodity value of the role, and failure becomes evidence that the person is worth less than the next usable asset.

### Spectacle function

The tournament packages constitutional succession and market competition as public entertainment.

### Strategic-violence function

Yan Mo, Shang De, and earlier E-Soul actors treat rival removal as ranking mobility.

### Stabilized conclusion

> **Rank is neither just popularity nor just combat power. It is the interface through which social belief becomes public office, market value, institutional control, and existential-risk governance.**

## P2-IN-07 — institutional legitimacy requires more than effective risk reduction

The full-series institutional test is not simply whether an intervention works.

A comparatively legitimate institution must satisfy at least seven criteria.

### 1. Proportionality

The threat and the response must be calibrated. Catastrophic-risk language cannot automatically license maximal force.

### 2. Procedural standing

The person governed must not lose all ordinary process merely because institutions classify them as exceptional.

### 3. Contestability

Affected persons and rival institutions must be able to challenge factual premises and classifications.

### 4. Provenance

The institution must preserve who produced the crisis, who benefits from the intervention, and which prior acts created the risk.

### 5. Non-instrumentalization

Bearers cannot be treated only as containers of Trust, brands, weapons, research subjects, or ranking slots.

### 6. Exit and role plurality

Institutions should recognize off-duty, private, familial, and non-hero aspects of bearer life as legitimate rather than as suspicious opacity.

### 7. Revision

Institutional measures must remain revisable when new evidence appears.

These conditions do not solve the X constitutional problem. They clarify why the existing response is inadequate.

In a world where public memory can generate Trust and Fear, burying hero-Zero may reduce the risk of renewed worship, cult continuity, or affective reconcentration.

These functions can coexist.

The lie is not trivial because it erases the system's own founding contradiction. It is not irrational because memory itself is potentially dangerous.

The Phase-2 judgment is:

> **The Association may have had a defensible containment objective while choosing a form of historical control that made institutional self-critique structurally difficult.**

Yan Mo's project exploits exactly what the founding myth cannot process publicly: Zero was not an external monster but the product of hero society's own deepest mechanism.

## P2-IN-20 — resolved earlier institutional hypotheses

### “The Association is simply the villain”

**Disposition:** `REJECT`.

The Zero catastrophe establishes real constitutional and public-safety functions. The critique concerns classification, procedure, historical control, and coercive overreach.

### “The ranking system is merely popularity”

**Disposition:** `REJECT`.

Rank combines Trust, status, market value, constitutional succession, institutional access, and disciplinary visibility.

### “FOMO democratizes heroism”

**Disposition:** `REVISE`.

It decentralizes access and visibility while creating new forms of attention power, rumor, pressure, and platform intervention.

### “Traditional offices are only exploitative”

**Disposition:** `REJECT`.

They provide real resources, legal/technical support, recovery management, and coordination. Their legitimacy fails when the role/asset outranks bearer answerability.

### “The tournament is only spectacle”

**Disposition:** `REJECT`.

It is also anti-apotheosis governance, Trust redistribution, office competition, and—under capture—an assassination market.

### “Secrecy is evidence of corruption”

**Disposition:** `REJECT as universal / PRESERVE as warning`.

Secrecy is sometimes necessary, but its recurring dual use demands procedural scrutiny.

### “Public exposure is inherently liberating”

**Disposition:** `REJECT`.

Exposure can correct attribution and constrain abuse, or it can seize disclosure, flatten causality, and intensify coercion.

### “X's refusal to join an office proves a desire to rule”

**Disposition:** `REJECT`.

The source supports a genuine institutional risk problem but not the inference that autonomy equals sovereign ambition.

## P2 institutional synthesis

The stabilized full-series model is:

> **Hero society is a constitutional market built around a socially generated power resource. It must coordinate dangerous bearers, prevent apotheosis, allocate attention, and respond to real crises; it does so through institutions that also convert persons, relationships, labor, and catastrophe into governable assets.**

The core institutional danger is not organization itself.

It is **asset logic becoming sovereign**:

- brand continuity outranks life;
- rank outranks private direction;
- risk classification outranks procedural standing;
- research value outranks subjecthood;
- public memory outranks historical provenance;
- and institutional ownership is mistaken for the only possible form of restraint.

The full-series normative requirement is therefore:

> **Institutions are comparatively legitimate when they coordinate power without making the bearer unanswerable to themselves, preserve contestable provenance, recognize private role boundaries, and remain subject to revision when the classification that justified coercion proves incomplete.**
