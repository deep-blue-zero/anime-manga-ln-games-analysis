## E01 provisional hypotheses

### TF-H01 — Self-belief as mechanical input

Lin's `我相信` sequence is narratively decisive, but the episode defines Trust socially. It is therefore **not yet safe** to say self-belief directly raises Trust Value.

**Status:** `UNR / HYP`  
**Confidence:** Low-Medium.

### TF-H02 — Trust is more than popularity

Episode 1 often routes Trust through popularity, publicity, and audience belief, but the chosen word is `信赖`. V2 should not reduce the mechanic to follower count unless later rules explicitly do so.

**Status:** `INF`  
**Confidence:** Medium-High.

### TF-H03 — Fear is more than bad reputation

The boss harvests concrete fear caused by job loss and violence. This suggests Fear Value depends on actual affect rather than merely people disliking a figure.

**Status:** `INF`  
**Confidence:** Medium.

### TF-H04 — Institution + belief feedback loop

E01 supports a provisional causal loop:

`packaging / staging → belief → Trust Value → bodily/power change → more credible image → more belief`.

This is strongly supported for Lin/Nice; generalization remains pending.

## Explicit non-findings after E01

- No source-backed proof that Fear spreads faster than Trust.
- No source-backed universal conversion formula between Trust/Fear and power output.
- No source-backed claim that all public attention counts as Trust.
- No source-backed claim that Fear automatically makes someone morally evil.
- No source-backed claim that Trust automatically makes someone morally good.
- No source-backed proof that the original identity is biologically destroyed when public transformation occurs.

## E04 cross-case synthesis

Episodes 1–4 now support a four-case model of social authorship:

1. **E01 — overwrite:** public belief can transform Lin toward a pre-existing Nice identity.
2. **E02 — relational constraint/alignment:** belief can override Xiao's desired mobility or be redirected toward it.

Leading interpretation: the mechanical system is not intrinsically dehumanizing. The ethical danger depends on whether collective expectation **precedes and dictates** the person's direction or **responds to and amplifies** a direction the person has already chosen.

## Phase-2 mechanics synthesis

The stabilized full-series model is:

> **Trust and Fear are socially generated but materially consequential forces whose effects depend not only on quantity, but on identity, recognition, expectation, bearer receptivity, institutional mediation, and exceptions in the world's physical substrate.**

The central error to avoid is treating Trust as a clean RPG statistic.

The series repeatedly shows that the same social force can:
- empower;
- constrain;
- deform;
- preserve;
- transfer;
- sanctify;
- commercialize;
- or totalize an identity.

The mature mechanics/ethics separation is therefore:

> **mechanical efficacy answers whether a social image can become real; legitimacy asks whether the person who bears that image can answer, revise, refuse, or leave it.**
