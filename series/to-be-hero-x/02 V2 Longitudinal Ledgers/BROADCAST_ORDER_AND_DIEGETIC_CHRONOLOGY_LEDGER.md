# Broadcast Order and Diegetic Chronology Ledger

## Rule

Broadcast revelation order and diegetic event order are separately preserved. Later episodes may insert earlier events; such additions must not erase what a first-time viewer could reasonably infer at the E01 boundary.

## Broadcast position

- `S01E01` is the first canonical episode analyzed.
- `下集预告` material after approximately source `00:24:38.500` is excluded from E01 diegesis.

## E01 known diegetic sequence

1. Lin already works in advertising and has created campaigns involving Nice/Xiao Yueqing.
2. Lin already admires Xiao Yueqing; her earlier words about dreams/self-belief predate his replacement.
3. Original Nice has suffered an unnamed prior destabilizing event (`自从那件事之后`).
4. Lin pitches the Nice/X advertisement and is fired after his boss's client-handling error.
5. Original Nice dies by suicide in Lin's presence.
6. Juān jiě conceals the death and begins Lin's replacement/training.
7. Public exposure raises Trust and changes Lin's body.
8. Xiao Yueqing sees “Nice” again after a gap attributed publicly to training.
9. `TRUE LOVE RECIPE` occurs; the former boss attacks using Fear.
10. Some diegetic time passes: news later refers to the disturbance as occurring “last week.”
11. Lin accepts his current identity as Nice and moves into Hero Tower.
12. He discovers the inherited cohabiting domestic relationship with Xiao Yueqing.

## Embedded past fragments

### CH-E01-A — Xiao Yueqing's encouragement of Lin

Shown as memory during the live-show confession and again during Lin's low point. Exact original date/context unknown.

### CH-E01-B — Original Nice's unnamed prior incident

Only referenced verbally. It is a placeholder, not a reconstructed event.

### CH-E01-C — Prior `探照灯` attacks

Known only from the news report that the same Fear amplifier appeared in multiple earlier attacks.

## Unresolved duration questions

- training duration between Nice's death and live-show reappearance;
- duration of Lin's prior ad career;
- exact timeline of Nice/Xiao Yueqing cohabitation;
- interval between unnamed Nice incident and suicide;
- timing of prior `探照灯` attacks;
- precise relation between Hero Tournament restart and Nice's death.

## Prospective-freeze note

If later episodes reveal pre-E01 Nice events, they belong in a retrospective column and may revise *causal understanding*, but the E01 viewer-state “motive unknown” remains historically valid.

## P2 chronology synthesis

The series' chronology is not merely “nonlinear.”

Its governing form is:

> **withhold causal antecedents until later perspectives force the viewer to reinterpret an already-known event.**

The most consequential example is Original Nice:

> **E01 gives the collapse; E20–E23 later disclose the social, institutional, relational, and Fear-mediated causal chain that made that collapse legible.**

Phase 2 therefore treats broadcast order as an interpretive variable rather than noise to be corrected.

The current full-series chronology should be used for causal synthesis, while frozen episode bodies remain the authority for what a first-time viewer could know at each point.
