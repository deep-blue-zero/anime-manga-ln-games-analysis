# Main/supplement synthesis architecture

## 1. Scope and authority

STR-G1 occupies `series/summer-time-rendering/`, with one [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) and paired [method](STR_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md). No prior architecture is superseded. Canonical status is INITIAL procedure only; no source admission, narrative result, owner run or model readiness is implied.

The inventory contains Japanese main manga V01–13 and a separately titled 2026 未然事故物件 supplement. Their actual contents and supplementary chronology/continuity are uninspected. The intended corpus explains causality, individual development, directed relationships, knowledge, Japanese expression and page form while preserving main-only and supplement-aware horizons.

Event/knowledge infrastructure is designed to distinguish presentation from event time and known from inferred facts. It can represent repeated/variant occurrences if the source establishes them. It must not seed a time-mechanism thesis from title familiarity.

## 2. Source and artifact model

The source/horizon map owns fourteen witness aliases, exact verification gaps and separate admission. Narrative, covers, notes/profiles, extras, jokes and previews need component classification. Supplement title/year is bibliographic identity, not event-time proof. Adaptation, translation and alternate editions are excluded.

Day-one homes: method, architecture, source/horizon map, event/knowledge ledger, person/relationship ledger, claim ledger, entrypoint, producer review and descriptor. Event/knowledge has an independent retrieval home to prevent future causal reconstruction from flattening information order. Ordinary/Japanese samples have a distinct typed section in the person ledger.

Future `readings/` contains substantial main and separately authorized supplement readings; `checkpoints/` preserves accepted main-only state; `studies/` holds earned studies; `reconstruction/` separates evidence/model/tests; `audits/` holds actual reviews. No mechanism specialist, cast dossier or source reading exists now.

## 5. Substantive interpretation and earned studies

Required mature responsibilities are causal/epistemic account, character development, directed relationships, ordinary/Japanese behavior and page form. They may be substantial integrated sections when separate specialists add no independent argument/retrieval value. The role-gap audit must identify exact carrying sections and explain that choice.

A causal specialist is earned when actual event complexity requires an independent sustained argument, not because the title is familiar. It must distinguish observations, inferred rules, exceptions and competing explanations, preserving uncertainty where the source does. A chronology chart or rule list cannot replace the prose explaining causation.

A character monograph is earned by a distinct developmental question and adequate state/context evidence. Relationship studies preserve each direction, differing knowledge and consequences. Ordinary evidence, counterexamples and source-layer distinctions remain necessary even when dramatic mechanisms dominate attention.

No universal word cap applies. Acceptance tests explanatory mechanisms, inferential warrants, representative and disconfirming evidence, named actors, temporal/occurrence specificity and source limits. Full synthesis integrates arguments rather than concatenating abstracts or announcing a solved chronology. Transfer review compares complete versions for lost reasoning.

## 6. Contradictions, dependencies and gates

First verify exact witness, source layer, attribution, physical locator and presentation horizon. Then ask whether accounts concern the same event/occurrence/frame. Similar imagery may be a report, recollection, formal echo or distinct event; identity needs evidence. Within a supported common scope test changed knowledge/state, self-report, mistaken belief, new disclosure, edition difference or genuine inconsistency. Keep competing interpretations OPEN where necessary.

Production:
`bound sources + authorized OPEN run → main readings with synchronized ledgers → source completion audit → longitudinal/event/claim reconciliation → role-gap/specialist readiness → studies → convergence → main-only synthesis readiness → validation/freeze`.
A separately authorized supplement then produces its own reading/deltas, cross-horizon reconciliation, affected-specialist revision, convergence and supplement-aware validation.

| Gate | Condition |
|---|---|
| Bootstrap | Source-specific paired foundation/controls; CLOSED/no run |
| Admission | Exact witness/maps, adequate image/Japanese route, owner scope and consistent OPEN fields |
| Unit completion | Full causal/counterreading prose, coverage, cumulative updates and exit freeze |
| Declared main completion | Actual authorized V01–13 coverage/exclusions accounted for; official/full-edition scope verified before claiming it |
| Longitudinal reconciliation | Event identity/time, person knowledge, states/edges/ordinary samples and claims aligned |
| Character/specialist readiness | Role-gap and exact evidence audit; no unsupported mechanism or trait |
| Specialist convergence | Competing causal/person/formal accounts reviewed; rationale if independent specialists unnecessary |
| Integrated-synthesis readiness | Required substantive inputs complete; evidence debts resolved or legitimately bounded |
| Supplement admission | Preserved main-only checkpoint plus separate scope/source verification; title/year not chronology |
| Supplement reconciliation | Event/state development versus newly disclosed facts distinguished; dependent arguments reviewed |

| Release/freeze | Accepted boundary and immutable history named through proper integration |

A completed source sequence does not automatically authorize synthesis or supplement reading. Owner scope remains recoverable for each stage.

## 7. Reconstruction, state specificity and independent testing

The [generic reconstruction method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md) §§9–12 and 14–18 are explicitly adopted for model structure, evidence-dimension crosswalk and validation. Local constraints below prevent future models from collapsing temporal/epistemic state. Present status NOT_READY; no subjects or models exist.

Evidence maps bind subject/state, actual source track and occurrence/frame where established, unit/disclosure horizon, situation/stakes, interlocutor, direct speech/behavior/interiority/report/observer/inference class, physical locator, full reading/claim route, Japanese status and counterexample. A hypothetical or alternate frame cannot supply biography without independent support in the subject's continuity. No such frame is presumed now.

Separate evidence map, operational model and validation artifact from literary monographs. Bind exact evidence-base commit, witness/continuity/temporal boundaries, exclusions and version. Disposition, developmental state and temporary conditions are separate layers. Change-cause tags include REVEALED_NOT_NEW; an analyst learning information is not automatically the person developing.

Rules specify trigger + perceived stakes + current state → likely response family with inhibitors, overrides, exceptions, counterevidence, rivals and falsifiers. A modeled person cannot use knowledge unavailable at that state. OBSERVED/CANDIDATE_POLICY/PROMOTED_POLICY require real support and validation.

Appraisal precedes decision objective, speech act, directed relationship filter, Japanese register and wording. Private thought is not public speech; source-language evidence governs voice, with decision and surface fidelity scored separately. Preserve ordinary situations, humor and negative-evidence opportunities so crises do not become the entire personality.

Use HIGH/MODERATE/LOW/OPEN confidence and D0 attested/D1 close-variant/D2 new within-world-state/D3 cross-scenario distance. D3 explicitly states chronology, knowledge, interlocutor and setting assumptions and abstains where unconstrained. A supplementary late state cannot silently supply an early model.

Genuine held-out evidence must be withheld from the model author before fitting. Freeze predictions, rule IDs, modeled state, assumptions, confidence/distance, test scope and falsifiers before release. A later source is not a clean test if its outcome was already known; label RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST after exposure. Supplementary state/context shifts may confound an early-state test and must be recorded.

An independent reviewer adjudicates diagnostic opportunity and exact evidence as SUPPORTED/PARTLY_SUPPORTED/CONTRADICTED/NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY/CONFOUNDED/UNRESOLVED, with reasoning. Use ordinary cases, changed-context/interlocutor perturbations, plausible decoys, abstention controls and a generic-personality baseline where suitable. Qualitative fidelity and counts/exclusions replace unsupported accuracy percentages.

Generated dialogue, scenes and forecasts are synthetic outputs, never canonical observations or literary evidence. No simulation is authorized. A model cannot supply missing events or mechanism evidence. Coverage makes twelve evaluator dimensions and six scenario types assessable without self-grading; discovery/evaluation registries remain designated-agent work.
