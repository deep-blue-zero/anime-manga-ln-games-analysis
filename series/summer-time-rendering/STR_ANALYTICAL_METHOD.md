# Main and supplementary manga method

## Preserve order without assuming the story's mechanics

The available Japanese sources are main サマータイムレンダ V01–13 and サマータイムレンダ2026 未然事故物件, creator metadata 田中靖規. No content was inspected at bootstrap. Neither the title nor genre familiarity establishes chronology, repeated events, special mechanisms, cast or plot.

The method preserves event time, presentation order and individual knowledge separately. If actual evidence later establishes repeated occurrences, frames or continuity branches, those receive explicit coordinates rather than being merged. Until then the structure remains a normal source-bounded event/knowledge record, not a seeded theory of the narrative.

[The source/horizon map](STR_SOURCE_AND_HORIZON_MAP.md) owns availability and unknowns; [the architecture](STR_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) owns cumulative and synthesis responsibility. This adapts the [generic manga method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MANGA_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md). Lock CLOSED, no run.

## Admission, units and source horizons

Before a future main-volume reading require an owner-defined run, verified immutable witness and ordered map, adequate STILL_IMAGE/Japanese route and consistent OPEN gate. Use actual collected-volume order, verified chapters and causal scene divisions; do not infer labels from another edition or split by processing batch.

Classify covers, contents, profiles, notes, extras, jokes and previews with spoiler boundaries. Historical package validity does not establish legibility or every page's presence. Preserve spreads and confirm facing-page layout.

The supplement is separately gated. Preserve the accepted main-only exit state and interpretive checkpoint before its authorized admission. Its title/year cannot determine event chronology or a privileged retrospective interpretation. Another source in the same folder is not automatically inside the run. Adaptations, translations, serialization and outside summaries remain excluded.

Disclose prior familiarity and quarantine it. Freeze entering state before each next unit; preserve scene-level disclosure inside volumes. A late main-volume revelation or supplementary explanation cannot become knowledge possessed earlier by a character or analyst. Source facts and revised interpretations need separate historical coordinates.

## Causal reasoning, identity and knowledge

Every consequential movement receives connected causal prose: entering conditions, named actors' perceived stakes/options, action or constraint, consequence and source-supported linkage. Distinguish causation, coincidence, misunderstanding, an asserted rule and an established rule. When a mechanism is hypothesized, state observed instances, conditions, exceptions and a possible falsifier; a useful theory must exclude outcomes.

Use source-established names within the relevant horizon. Preserve uncertain identity, aliases and purposeful anonymity. Similar appearance, role or recalled plot cannot resolve identity. Attribute actor, observer, thinker, speaker, information recipient and affected person clearly.

Evidence labels: OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, EVALUATION, UNRESOLVED; confidence HIGH/MODERATE/LOW/UNRESOLVED. Separate direct behavior, inner presentation, self-account and another's report. A character's mistaken or uncertain belief is not world fact.

Record event occurrence, event-time evidence, presentation position, narrative frame and character knowledge. If recurrence/branching is actually established, distinguish identical-looking depictions, recollection/report, a second occurrence and a variant state before assigning equivalence. Do not propagate knowledge across an assumed reset or frame without evidence. Use UNKNOWN rather than manufacture rules.

Character interpretation separates disposition candidates, developmental state and temporary situation. A new disclosure may be REVEALED_NOT_NEW. Relationships remain directed: A's knowledge, trust, desire or pressure toward B does not establish B's reverse state. Collective facts must retain who actually knows or acts.

## Ordinary behavior, Japanese expression and form

Preserve ordinary activity, humor, routine and low-stakes response where present alongside crises. A person's action under exceptional conditions is not a whole personality. Negative evidence requires an actual opportunity; absent situations are gaps.

Inspect Japanese speech, thought, narration, asides, diegetic writing, effects and ruby independently. Attribute with confidence. Brief verified forms and labeled glosses ground register/address/self-reference/endings/pragmatic claims; compare context/interlocutor and exceptions. Private thought is not public speech, and translation is not Japanese voice authority.

Read silent pages fully. Analyze composition, scale, gaze, framing, grouping, withholding, gutters, repeated images, backgrounds and rendering shifts where consequential. Repetition may be formal echo, recalled information, another occurrence or another function; prove the relation rather than infer temporal identity from resemblance. Explain feature → proposed effect → rival account.

Verify page-turn/spread layout. Panel endpoints do not establish unobserved motion, lettering/effects do not provide acoustic measurement and one expression does not uniquely reveal emotion. No adaptation acting, music or staging is admitted. For sensitive conduct if encountered, preserve distinctions among depiction, explanation, consent, consequences, framing and evaluation with minimal necessary detail.

## Locators, coverage and revisions

Use `STR-JP-M01-W01 / MAIN-V01 / Ch.<verified label or UNKNOWN> / i0001 / p<visible label or NONE> / panel 1 / balloon 1`; supplement uses `STR-JP-SUP2026-W01 / SUP2026 / <verified section>`. Image occurrence is one-based in actual spine order including apparatus. Panel/balloon reading order and ambiguity are stated. Crops retain parent and region.

L1 exact image/panel/balloon supports contested evidence/tests; L2 bounded scene; L3 unit navigation; L4 patterns cite representative L1 instances and inspected scope. Event IDs index interpretation; they do not replace physical source coordinates. OCR/search is retrieval, not reading.

The evidence plane retains per-image REVIEWED_MATERIAL/NO_MATERIAL_ADDITION/DUPLICATE/EXCLUDED/UNRESOLVED_READABILITY states and raw pages/derivatives. Git includes safe aliases, actual coverage/exclusions and brief quotations, not private IDs/paths, OCR dumps or bulk dialogue.

Every governing claim keeps support, inferential warrant, strongest counterreading and falsifier. Revisions use PRESERVE/STRENGTHEN/REVISE/DOWNGRADE/REJECT/OPEN, retaining prior formulations and prospective freezes. Reconstructed chronology is explicitly retrospective; it cannot overwrite the first-pass record.
