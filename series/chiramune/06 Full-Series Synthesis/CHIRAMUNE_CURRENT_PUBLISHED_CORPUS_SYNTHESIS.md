# Chiramune current published-corpus synthesis — through V06

## Purpose, authority, and limit

This is the analyzed-to-date integrator for the Japanese *Chitose Is in the Ramune Bottle* light-novel corpus. It routes a multi-document portfolio: V01–V06 main readings and freezes, separate V03/V05 supplemental checkpoints, five longitudinal ledgers, six warranted character monographs, the Fukui/locality specialist synthesis, and safely integrated bonus material.

It does not replace those documents' evidence or their source-bound historical states. The exact route begins at `../CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md`; source identity lives in `../01 Source Lock and Inventory/CHIRAMUNE_SOURCE_LOCK_AND_INVENTORY.md`; claim transitions live in `../03 Longitudinal Ledgers/CHIRAMUNE_REVISION_LEDGER.md`.

This synthesis is not terminal. V07–V09, V06.5, *Days of Endless Summer*, the V08 rough-illustration supplement, and V09.5 remain unanalyzed. Chiramune remains an open publication corpus at the current audit horizon.

## Current central synthesis

Across six main volumes, Chiramune treats youth as the problem of authoring lives from already social forms: talent, beauty, rank, friendship, family, sport, place, performance, memory, and other people's desire. Authenticity is neither an untouched interior nor the abandonment of style. It is retained capacity to recognize, revise, and answer for what a chosen form demands.

V05 adds a decisive limit to the V01–V04 ethic of recognition and distributed action:

> **Inclusion cannot abolish the exclusion built into singular romantic choice. A responsible relation must preserve both the right to request and the right to refuse, while treating the changed social world—not violence, self-punishment, personal-deficiency myths, or exact restoration—as the shared aftermath that remains to be faced.**

Recognition can enlarge another person's possible action without acquiring authority over goal, method, feeling, or consequence. But recognition is not enough if every difficult answer remains deferred. V05 makes truth an action with social cost: Yuko's request and Saku's refusal are legitimate, while the group's fracture shows that legitimate acts can still hurt and require repair.

The Special Edition historicizes that result. Saku's wish to preserve beautiful ambiguity, the Yuko hero/person asymmetry, Haru's challenge protocol, Yuzuki's fallible similarity theory, and the primary Yuko/Yua and Yuzuki/Haru friendships all predate the V05 crisis. Earlier roots do not predetermine the outcome; they show why the outcome reorganizes more than a set of isolated romantic bids.

V06 then revises the V05 aftermath model:

> **Responsibility does not include authority to decide which uncertainty, jealousy, refusal, or continued relation another person may bear. Repair begins when mixed motives become shareable, each participant recovers jurisdiction over their own desire, and continued connection becomes a renewed choice rather than an unchanged default.**

Saku discloses plural attachment rather than one identified beloved. Yuko and Yua reject self-erasure without denying friendship. The group reconnects, but explicit changes in distance and knowledge prevent a reset reading.

## I. Developmental sequence

### V01 — chosen form against imposed rank

Saku and Kenta expose status as real but not final. Saku's competence and authored social role are genuine; Kenta's development becomes durable when borrowed scripts become selectable tools and he ceases to be Saku's rehabilitation object. Helping begins as effective high-agency intervention with paternalistic risk.

### V02 — relationship creates standing, not unlimited permission

Yuzuki's crisis distinguishes self-authored performance from identity imposed by an observer. She requests help and names love, but Saku's trauma reenactment remains coercive at the method level. Friends establish legitimate standing over self-risk because consequences are relationally distributed.

### V03 — authorship of process and relationally formed selves

Asuka's Tokyo choice is between genuine goods. Her learned `Asuka-nee` form becomes authentic through continued selection and revision. Saku's hero persona is grounded in real care and defensive perfection. He supports a loved person's departure and accepts another person's edit of his self-story while continuing to violate procedural boundaries.

Ramune becomes co-authored: Saku's lonely marble and Asuka's loved moon are different readings of one object; the epilogue pluralizes orientation.

### V04 — shared heat, authored uptake, and distributed completion

Haru and Saku form a reciprocal circuit. Her intervention helps because it returns the baseball decision to him; her hidden basketball compact fails because leaders treat a shared goal as consent to their method. Both sports climaxes rely on differentiated people completing what exceptional individuals begin.

Saku authors one Fujishi retirement game rather than restoring the old role. Haru names love and transfers the next move. Injury romanticization remains unresolved.

### V05 main — finite form, truthful refusal, and redistributed care

Summer is built as something already ending: caramel pieces, stacked and edible triangles, fireworks, photographs, puzzle pieces, shells, a horizon cut, and bonfire residue. Retrieval preserves changed memory, not the original configuration.

Asuka deliberately makes a last local summer without revoking departure. Haru translates overflow into a future consent protocol. Yuzuki moves from deniable bid to a direct, bounded fireworks request. Yua's year-long domestic maintenance and final saxophone scene establish ordinary care that does not seize the recipient.

Yuko's public confession makes the group's suspended romantic incompatibility answerable. Saku refuses and says one other girl is in his heart; V05 main does not identify her. Kaito responds with unjustifiable violence. Saku converts guilt into self-punishment. Yua chooses the isolated Saku without requiring romantic priority and gives his grief privacy. The group fractures in fact; terminal destruction remains unproven.

### V05 Special Edition — retrospective depth without prospective overwrite

The appended booklet routes 18 stories across V01–V04 diegesis and first-year prehistory. It strengthens historical claims rather than extending post-refusal time: Saku repeatedly prefers relational stasis; Yuko can both sustain his hero image and see ordinary strain; Haru's future challenge has an earlier consent agreement; Yuzuki repeatedly mispredicts her own capacity to love a similar person; and Yuzuki/Haru partnership is primary before romantic rivalry.

The compilation also establishes an edition rule. Its regular V05 main is text-equivalent to the regular witness and counted once. Its `シャンプーにキャップ` is a revised witness to the already integrated V03 event: later wording specifies an overwrite of memory, but the shared event is not doubled. See the [V05 Special Edition supplemental checkpoint](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/CHIRAMUNE_V05_SPECIAL_EDITION_SUPPLEMENTAL_READING.md).

### V06 — autonomous burden and repaired, changed continuity

Yua rejects Saku's self-punishment and the group distributes support through interpretation, food, sport, confrontation, and ordinary maintenance. Kenta returns Saku's own agency lesson; Saku finally shares the unflattering reasons he prolonged Yuko's first confession.

The festival conversation clarifies the romance state. Yuko is in Saku's heart as a girl, but several other girls matter equally and he cannot name which feeling is love. He again refuses dating Yuko and offers no result promise. Yuko retains her love; Yua declares her wish to become more selfish and special.

Yuko's public confession is re-read as mixed: intended to release others from her veto, but rooted partly in jealousy and territorial protection. Yua's mediation is likewise both care and self-interested romantic action. Their first fight establishes friendship capable of conflict and accepted non-primacy.

Kaito directly confesses and accepts Yuko's refusal, replacing delegated sacrifice with answerable desire. The full group resumes festival practice through apology and renewed contact, while altered gaze, spacing, and voice mark a new topology rather than restored innocence. See the [V06 deep reading](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/CHIRAMUNE_V06_DEEP_READING.md) and [V06 freeze](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/CHIRAMUNE_V06_PROSPECTIVE_FREEZE.md).

## II. Current thematic architecture

### Self-authorship is retained revision capacity under consequence

No major self is origin-pure. Saku's hero form responds to praise and entitlement. Yuzuki's interface responds to visibility. Asuka turns imitation into practice. Haru makes heat into an ethic. Yuko turns correction into a romantic origin. Yua builds care through repeated domestic obligation.

V05 shows that a form may be sincere and still need to end. V06 shows that a truthful account need not contain one purified motive. Yuko's confession, Yua's mediation, and Saku's refusal each combine care, self-interest, fear, and responsibility. Authenticity lies in making those combinations answerable. See the [self-authorship ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/CHIRAMUNE_SELF_AUTHORSHIP_PERFORMANCE_AND_AUTHENTICITY_LEDGER.md).

### Care requires standing, method, consequence, and aftermath

The four-part intervention test remains:

1. standing to enter;
2. authority over the desired outcome;
3. authority over method and information;
4. ownership of ordinary consequences.

V05 adds aftermath. V06 adds burden jurisdiction: a legitimate refusal can cause pain without romantic debt, and the refuser cannot decide that another person is forbidden to wait, remain, or carry uncertainty. Yua supplies both non-extractive presence and a direct demand that Saku share what he withholds. See the [agency ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/CHIRAMUNE_AGENCY_INTERVENTION_AND_RESPONSIBILITY_LEDGER.md).

### Status is a network of permissions and distances

Beauty, popularity, competence, captaincy, and intimacy affect who may propose, refuse, intrude, or be believed. Yuko's first-year nomination shows that high status can coerce without intention. Her own experience shows that the same status can deprive its holder of ordinary opposition.

The group has independent ties, but Saku remains a romantic scarcity center. V05 creates a split; V06 first distributes survival across dyads and then reconnects the network. Yua, Kenta, Yuzuki, Haru, Asuka, and Kazuki carry distinct repair functions, reducing Saku's organizing monopoly. See the [social-status and group ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/CHIRAMUNE_SOCIAL_STATUS_INCLUSION_AND_GROUP_DYNAMICS_LEDGER.md).

### Recognition must coexist with refusal

Recognition survives demystification when it follows practice rather than ideal essence. It becomes dangerous when “I see you” turns into “I know your true interior” or “you owe the role I value.”

Yuko's case sharpens the boundary. Saku's first-year recognition changed her life; it did not create an obligation to return romantic love. Her year and a half of concrete observation shows that she recognizes more than a hero, while remaining compatible with Saku's refusal. Conversely, his right to refuse does not erase responsibility for how he prolonged her suspended bid.

### Directness is a structure, not an intensity

Yuko, Yuzuki, and Haru demonstrate three different direct forms:

- a completed public request for singular status;
- a finite present request with explicit bodily and relational limits;
- a future challenge protocol that owns unfinished work and refuses to demand waiting.

Directness is ethically valuable when it makes scope and answerability legible. It is not automatically private, low-pressure, or successful.

### Self-punishment is not accountability, and withholding is not always protection

Saku's V04 injury logic becomes explicit moral punishment in V05. V06 rejects it directly and uses symmetry to show why it fails: he wants Yuko supported after refusal, so he cannot coherently condemn himself for receiving support. He also learns that deciding which burdens others may carry can convert protection into control.

### Finite form preserves relation without stopping change

The developing material grammar includes Ramune, photographs, food, clothing, keys, shells, sport objects, and seasonal landscapes. V06 adds held hands, layered hands, handshake, welcoming/sending fire, and sparklers: connection is repeated action, not preservation of an unchanged group, role, or person.

## III. Current character models

### Saku Chitose

Saku is a genuinely capable, caring self-author whose defensive perfection protects against entitlement. V06 lets him reject self-punishment, disclose resentment and comfort that do not flatter the hero role, and admit multiple equally important girls without knowing which feeling is love. He commits to face relation but still does not clearly originate support. See the [Saku monograph](../04%20Character%20Analysis/Saku%20Chitose/CHIRAMUNE_SAKU_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md).

### Yuzuki Nanase

Yuzuki is an authored social optimizer whose performance carries rather than negates vulnerability. V06 shows safe form becoming false distance, then lets her redirect aesthetic discipline through ordinary sauce katsudon toward Saku's actual appetite and recovery. Her primary partnership with Haru contains rivalry pain; neither corrected intimacy nor Saku's jealousy is an answer. See the [Yuzuki monograph](../04%20Character%20Analysis/Yuzuki%20Nanase/CHIRAMUNE_YUZUKI_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md).

### Asuka Nishino

Asuka is a relational self-author who turns learned freedom into practice and chooses an editorial future through inquiry. V06 makes her ask not to be excluded from Saku's pain and diagnose his factual-without-emotional disclosure. Her private moon aside shows that she withholds her own romantic stake to preserve that role. Recognition remains intimate and non-neutral; selection and departure remain open. See the [Asuka monograph](../04%20Character%20Analysis/Asuka%20Nishino/CHIRAMUNE_ASUKA_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md).

### Haru Aomi

Haru is a high-temperature self-author whose directness can return or overrun jurisdiction. V06 makes her accept that romance lacks a fair start signal or score, while retaining sport as an authentic care language. She recruits Atomu, gives Saku embodied relief, and distributes burden through team ethics; her private aside exposes the romantic limit of that role. The promised challenge and team-governance questions remain open. See the [Haru monograph](../04%20Character%20Analysis/Haru%20Aomi/CHIRAMUNE_HARU_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md).

### Yuko Hiiragi

Yuko's social radiance both empowers and encloses. V06 gives her public confession mixed motive: liberation from her romantic veto alongside jealousy and territorial history. She refuses Kaito, demonstrates accumulated ordinary knowledge of Saku, accepts Saku's second refusal without demanding a promise, and repairs Yua friendship through conflict and non-primacy. See the [Yuko monograph](../04%20Character%20Analysis/Yuko%20Hiiragi/CHIRAMUNE_YUKO_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md).

### Yua Uchida

Yua's gentleness is a method for preserving face and agency, while social modeling, food, shopping, music, and improvisation show exact ordinary expertise. V06 grounds that competence in a post-abandonment survival contract, then makes her the repair architect and an interested romantic participant. She rejects replacing sacrifice “for family” with sacrifice “for Saku” and asks to become more selfish and special. Saku does not select her. See the [Yua monograph](../04%20Character%20Analysis/Yua%20Uchida/CHIRAMUNE_YUA_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md).

Kenta, Kazuki, Kaito, Nazuna, Atomu, Yusuke, the coaches, and family members remain recoverable through readings and ledgers until independent responsibility is earned.

## IV. Relationship state

The current model is directional and explicitly non-symmetrical:

- **Saku ↔ Yuko:** mutual gendered importance, Yuko's retained love, Saku's second refusal, a contingent future self-disclosure process, no promise or couple;
- **Saku ↔ Yua:** familylike domestic history, crisis care, shared-burden demand, Yua's avowed priority and desire for specialness, no Saku romantic selection;
- **Saku ↔ Haru:** reciprocal athletic partnership, direct Haru love, enacted team care, agreed future challenge still open;
- **Saku ↔ Yuzuki:** named Yuzuki love, bounded bids, sauce-katsudon care, Saku attraction/jealousy, no couple;
- **Saku ↔ Asuka:** named Asuka love, heart-level confrontation, Saku threatened-loss/admired-ideal language, mutual non-disclosure, no exclusivity;
- **Yuko ↔ Yua:** independently primary friendship, first direct fight, admitted non-primacy, enacted repair;
- **Yuko ↔ Yuzuki/Haru/Nazuna:** independent friendship and care under explicit romantic difference;
- **Yuzuki ↔ Haru:** independently primary first-year partnership, reciprocal formation, fair-contest norm, later romantic rivalry continuous with friendship;
- **Yuko ↔ Kaito:** Kaito's confession, explicit Yuko refusal, and preserved deep friendship without substitution;
- **Saku ↔ Kaito:** assault remains wrong; apology and contact are renewed in changed form;
- **Saku ↔ Kazuki/Kenta:** rival shame becomes discussable; Kenta's returned lesson catalyzes Saku's action;
- **friend group:** enacted repair and resumed practice inside a changed topology.

No exclusive couple is established. V06 does not identify one singular other girl; it revises the question into explicit plural importance and unresolved romantic naming. See the [relationship ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/CHIRAMUNE_RELATIONSHIP_RECOGNITION_AND_INTIMACY_LEDGER.md).

## V. Locality, departure, and ordinary youth

V05 crosses the specialist threshold for Fukui locality. Through V06, landscapes, trains, stores, food, household economy, sports sites, family routes, festivals, speech, and seasonal memory shape what characters can do and imagine. V06 makes Obon a bond theory, sauce katsudon recipient-directed care, park baseball a post-institutional practice, and the shrine–Yokokan–park route a passage from dyad to triad to group. Tokyo and Fukui remain rival bundles of goods and costs, not freedom and repression symbols.

The mature responsibility now lives in the [Fukui locality specialist synthesis](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/CHIRAMUNE_FUKUI_LOCALITY_DEPARTURE_AND_ORDINARY_YOUTH_SYNTHESIS.md). The key current result is that place becomes enabling when its practices remain usable without pre-authoring the whole future; memory permits return without exact restoration.

## VI. Ramune and finite-object grammar

The title complex now includes:

- enclosure and beautiful visibility;
- lens and mediated perception;
- co-authored interpretation;
- plural orientation;
- collaborative opening;
- marble retrieval;
- reversible sun/moon roles;
- eight bottles aligned with the group rather than one exclusive dyad;
- finite objects that retrieve transformed memory rather than an unchanged past.
- held and layered hands, handshake, seasonal fire, and sparklers as revisable connection.

No one function supersedes the others. V06 moves the bottle from group count to circulation through dyadic, triadic, and collective festival contact without supplying terminal meaning.

## VII. Counterevidence and unresolved tensions

### Honest acts can still be socially destructive and reparable

Yuko's request and Saku's refusal are both legitimate. Their legitimacy does not erase the group fracture; V06's repair does not erase the harm. Ethical analysis must retain jurisdiction, consequence, and changed continuity rather than designate one sole wrongdoer or declare a reset.

### Ordinary care may remain invisible or asymmetrical

Yua's maintenance is powerful precisely because it is repeatable and non-spectacular. V06 names what she wants and recovers its first-year reciprocal origin, but Saku still receives more support than he initiates.

### Direct bids remain below reciprocal commitment

Haru and Yuzuki make better-bounded claims; Asuka demands emotional access; Yua names priority and specialness; Yuko retains love. Saku values several girls as girls but has not supplied reciprocal romantic naming to any one of them.

### Friendship between rivals is real and burdened

The girls' independent relations cannot be dismissed as harem harmony. Yuko/Yua repair through anger and accepted non-primacy; Yuzuki/Haru regulate pain through partnership. Neither pattern is cost-free or reducible to competition for Saku.

### Team repair remains procedurally incomplete

Reported wins do not establish that Haru's team has repaired hidden governance, workload disagreement, or safety practice.
