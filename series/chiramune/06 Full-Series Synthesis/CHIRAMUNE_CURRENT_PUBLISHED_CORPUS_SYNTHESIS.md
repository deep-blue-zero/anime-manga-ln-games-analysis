# Chiramune current published-corpus synthesis — through Volume 04

## Purpose, authority, and limit

This is the current analyzed-to-date integrator for the Japanese *Chitose Is in the Ramune Bottle* light-novel corpus. It exists because the V01–V04 prospective readings now converge across five longitudinal ledgers and four warranted character monographs. It routes those documents; it does not replace their evidence, detail, or source-bound historical states.

The exact current route begins at `../CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md`. Source identity and integrity live in `../01 Source Lock and Inventory/CHIRAMUNE_SOURCE_LOCK_AND_INVENTORY.md`. Claim transitions live in `../03 Longitudinal Ledgers/CHIRAMUNE_REVISION_LEDGER.md`.

This synthesis is not terminal. Main V05–V09, V05 special-edition material, V06.5, *Days of Endless Summer*, the V08 rough-illustration supplement, and V09.5 remain unanalyzed. Chiramune remains an open publication corpus at the current audit horizon.

## Current central synthesis

Across four volumes, Chiramune treats youth not as discovery of an untouched “real self,” but as the problem of authoring a life from forms already shaped by talent, beauty, status, expectation, imitation, place, and other people. Its characters do not become authentic by removing performance. They become more responsible when they can recognize the form they are using, revise what it demands, and preserve other people's jurisdiction inside relationship.

The series' major ethical distinction is now:

> **Recognition can enlarge another person's possible action without becoming authority over their goal, method, interiority, or consequence. A relationship is most generative when influence can be received and translated while each participant remains able to refuse, revise, and answer.**

V01 establishes chosen social form and the danger of treating another person as a project. V02 distinguishes invitation to help from consent to method. V03 makes life-choice process and consequence ownership central. V04 tests the same structure in sports: Haru's challenge helps Saku when it returns the baseball decision to him, and damages her team when shared aspiration is treated as consent to concealed governance and one compulsory form of effort.

## I. Developmental sequence

### V01 — chosen form against imposed rank

Saku and Kenta expose status as real but not final. Saku's competence and authored social role are genuine; Kenta's development becomes durable only when he ceases to be Saku's aesthetic rehabilitation object and chooses participation for himself. The first Ramune function is beautiful enclosure: a visible, valued self can remain trapped inside the form that makes it visible.

### V02 — relationship creates standing, not unlimited permission

Yuzuki's crisis distinguishes self-authored performance from identity imposed by an observer. She requests help and ultimately names love, but Saku's effective trauma reenactment remains methodologically coercive. Friends also establish legitimate standing over Saku's self-risk: he cannot call injury exclusively his cost when others must bear its consequences.

### V03 — authorship of process and relationally formed selves

Asuka's Tokyo decision is a choice between genuine goods rather than freedom versus repression. Her learned “Asuka-nee” form becomes authentic through continued selection and revision. Saku's hero persona is grounded in childhood care, peer entitlement, sabotage, and defensive perfection. He can accept another person's edit of his self-story and support a loved person's departure while continuing to violate procedural boundaries.

The title image becomes co-authored. Saku's lonely marble and Asuka's loved, floating moon are different readings of one object; the epilogue pluralizes who may orient whom.

### V04 — shared heat, authored uptake, and distributed completion

Haru and Saku form a reciprocal circuit. Each has previously received the other's athletic intensity; neither is the sole source of the other's fire. Haru's strongest intervention combines full witness, anger, and rebuke with refusal to make Saku's final choice. He authors a one-game ending to Fujishi baseball rather than restoring the old role.

The parallel basketball plot tests the failure case. Haru and Yuzuki's hidden demon/Buddha compact converts a shared championship goal into presumed consent to their method. Team recovery begins when Yuzuki elicits direct accounts, Coach Misaki restores player jurisdiction, Haru names dependence, and distinct capacities become mutually enabling.

Both sports climaxes reject solitary completion. Saku's current friends create practice, his old teammates complete the winning sequence, and the basketball team makes Haru's breakthrough possible. Exceptional action matters because it changes what others can do—not because it makes them unnecessary.

## II. Current thematic architecture

### Self-authorship is retained jurisdiction over a relationally formed self

No major self in the first four volumes is origin-pure. Saku's hero form responds to teacher praise and peer entitlement. Yuzuki's interface responds to visibility and social claims. Asuka learns freedom from child Saku and turns imitation into practice. Haru makes her sun name and remembered defeat into an ethic of heat.

Influence is not the opposite of authenticity. The critical questions are whether the form remains revisable and whether the person and affected others may understand, refuse, and renegotiate its demands. See the [self-authorship ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/CHIRAMUNE_SELF_AUTHORSHIP_PERFORMANCE_AND_AUTHENTICITY_LEDGER.md).

### Care requires four separate jurisdictions

The current intervention test distinguishes:

1. standing to enter;
2. authority over the desired outcome;
3. authority over method and information;
4. ownership of ordinary consequences.

Saku improves at outcome and consequence recognition before method. V04 adds a collective form: team election and shared goals do not authorize hidden plans, compulsory sacrifice, or leaders' total access to teammates' interior effort. See the [agency ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/CHIRAMUNE_AGENCY_INTERVENTION_AND_RESPONSIBILITY_LEDGER.md).

### Status is a network of permissions, not only a rank ladder

Beauty, competence, popularity, seniority, captaincy, and intimacy affect who may intrude, joke, accuse, fail, or be believed. The group increasingly distributes authority—Kenta can lead through expertise, Yuzuki can redirect team process, and teammates can complete Saku's action—but Saku remains a central social and romantic connector. See the [status and group ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/CHIRAMUNE_SOCIAL_STATUS_INCLUSION_AND_GROUP_DYNAMICS_LEDGER.md).

### Recognition survives demystification when it tracks practice

Saku's regard for Asuka survives ordinary awkwardness and knowledge of imitation. Yuzuki's love survives disconfirming knowledge of Saku. Haru sees blood, dirt, repetition, and avoidance beneath beautiful play. Recognition becomes ethically dangerous when it changes from “I see this conduct and possibility” into “I therefore know your true interior and may choose the route.”

### Passion is not morally equivalent to maximum damage

V04 separates effort from guaranteed victory and gives bodily persistence enormous aesthetic force. It also repeatedly treats collapse and injury as sincerity. The current synthesis does not convert narrative efficacy into ethical validation. Sustainable effort, rest, medical dissent, and the right to choose a different intensity remain unresolved responsibilities.

## III. Current character models

### Saku Chitose

Saku is a genuinely capable and caring self-author whose defensive perfection protects against entitlement. By V04 he can disclose his formative baseball grief, accept an unscripted response, support another person's departure, choose a bounded ending, and let groups complete what he begins. He remains unreliable about procedural authority over his own body and about naming relationships that would constrain future options. See the [Saku monograph](../04%20Character%20Analysis/Saku%20Chitose/CHIRAMUNE_SAKU_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md).

### Yuzuki Nanase

Yuzuki is a highly self-authored social optimizer whose performance is a selective interface rather than a false mask. Her named love can act generously without becoming emotionally neutral. By V04 she moves from invisible complaint management into public corrective co-leadership, but direct romantic self-advocacy toward Saku remains deferred. See the [Yuzuki monograph](../04%20Character%20Analysis/Yuzuki%20Nanase/CHIRAMUNE_YUZUKI_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md).

### Asuka Nishino

Asuka is a relational self-author who turns learned freedom into her own practice and chooses an editorial future through inquiry rather than rescue. V04 does not test the durability of her independence or vocation. It does reopen the Saku-side category: V03's reciprocal love wording remains fact, while Saku later says he cannot tell whether to call his attraction romantic love. See the [Asuka monograph](../04%20Character%20Analysis/Asuka%20Nishino/CHIRAMUNE_ASUKA_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md).

### Haru Aomi

Haru is a high-temperature self-author whose directness can either restore another person's choice or overrun it. Her athletic discipline, love, and leadership are real; so are her fear of extinguished passion, hidden governance, and collapse-level self-demand. V04 makes her capable of naming dependence, receiving Yuzuki's correction, and seeking a side-by-side relation with Saku. See the [Haru monograph](../04%20Character%20Analysis/Haru%20Aomi/CHIRAMUNE_HARU_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md).

No other character yet has an independent monograph merely by virtue of appearance. Yua, Yuko, Kenta, Kazuki, Kaito, Nazuna, Atomu, Yusuke, the coaches, and family members remain recoverable through the readings and ledgers until further evidence creates an independent responsibility.

## IV. Relationship state

The current relationship model is directional and nonexclusive:

- **Saku ↔ Haru:** reciprocal sports formation, complete disclosure, direct Haru love, Saku's visible recognition/support, and an unanswered next move;
- **Saku ↔ Asuka:** Asuka's named love, Saku's attraction and importance, a historical reciprocal love utterance, chosen future separation, and reopened Saku-side categorization;
- **Yuzuki → Saku:** named love combining generosity, competition, ordinary home-like rhythm, and indirect bids; no reciprocal love naming;
- **Yuko → Saku:** direct specialness and self-authored competition, with her requested answer moratorium still procedurally asymmetric;
- **Yua → Saku:** practical/ordinary nearness and a future-performance bid whose meaning Saku receives without classifying;
- **Haru ↔ Yuzuki:** failed hidden co-leadership revised toward public correction, reciprocal partnership, and dual-domain rivalry;
- **Saku ↔ Kenta:** ordinary reciprocal friendship rather than client status;
- **Saku ↔ old baseball team/Wataya:** acknowledged harm and one-game cooperation without restored membership or erased history.

No exclusive couple is established through V04. See the [relationship ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/CHIRAMUNE_RELATIONSHIP_RECOGNITION_AND_INTIMACY_LEDGER.md).

## V. Ramune, sun, moon, and form

The title complex currently preserves several compatible functions:

- **enclosure:** beauty and visibility can coexist with bounded movement;
- **lens:** a removed marble mediates perception and intimacy;
- **co-authorship:** one object can carry opposed but relationally transmitted meanings;
- **plural orientation:** many people can be another person's moon;
- **collaborative opening:** Saku steadies the bottle while Haru pushes the marble;
- **retrievability:** the once-enclosed marble can be removed and raised to the sky;
- **reversible light:** sun and moon are positions of giving and receiving, not fixed essences.

No single function supersedes the others. The motif is a developing grammar for how a formed self can remain bounded, perceived, opened, and relationally reinterpreted.

## VI. Counterevidence and unresolved tensions

### Positive outcomes often obscure procedural harm

Saku's interventions work; Haru's pressure moves people; the team recovers; Wataya changes. None of those outcomes retroactively supplies consent to surveillance, physical coercion, hidden governance, or unsafe exertion.

### The series values distributed action while centering exceptional people

Teams repeatedly complete what protagonists cannot. Yet Saku's social, athletic, and romantic centrality—and Haru, Yuzuki, and Asuka's exceptional capacities—continue to organize access and recognition. The corpus has not shown that the same developmental routes are equally available without elite sponsorship.

### Direct love and reciprocal conduct remain below settled commitment

Several characters make serious, differentiated claims. Saku responds through attention, gifts, support, disclosure, or symbolic action more readily than through category and commitment. V04 makes this ambiguity more consequential but does not resolve whether it is epistemic care, optionality preservation, fear of self-binding, or a changing combination.

### Independence can become another role prison

Asuka's anti-dependence vow and Haru's inability to rest are different forms of the same risk: an authored identity can become compulsory when ordinary need feels like proof of failure.
