## I. Governing movement: public endings create answerable continuations

V09 completes the festival sequence without converting its spectacle into a romantic verdict. Performances make private histories publicly legible, but the strongest consequences occur when characters refuse the false equation between a bounded role and a whole relationship.

The festival does end the group's unchanged blue arrangement. It does not end its lateral relations. Yua addresses Saku through music while becoming publicly recognizable beyond him; Haru turns a paired race into reciprocal adjustment; Yuzuki's stage selection remains expressly theatrical; and Momiji's confession first receives refusal, then a more exact jurisdictional correction. She is not accepted as Saku's girlfriend. She is accepted as a claimant whose real words must be heard before a final answer is made.

> **Volume 09 converts deferred choice into answerable participation without producing singular selection. Public performance can carry authored truth, repair, and recognition, but cannot substitute for consent, direct disclosure, or mutually enacted relationship status. The old group form ends while its members deliberately retain rival, friendly, artistic, and ordinary routes into whatever comes next.**

## II. The V08 morning preserves violation, desire, and accountable continuity

Several days after the V08 apartment night, Saku and Yuzuki resume public roles while privately acknowledging that their relation has changed. They reject the temptation to call the encounter merely a mistake and instead treat the following morning as continuous with the night. A small daylight hand-touch becomes their present settlement `[p-0010#p0163-p0218]`.

The sequence preserves all necessary distinctions. Yuzuki's earlier remorse and Saku's desire do not retroactively authorize the ignored verbal limits. Continued intimacy does not erase the violation. Nor does preservation of the violation require treating Yuzuki as permanently reducible to it. V09's ethical advance lies in continuity with memory: both remain answerable to what happened while choosing present relation.

Yuzuki later apologizes more precisely for degrading the self Saku had protected and offers to become the mirror that returns him to his own standard `[p-0025#p0053-p0084]`. This is sustained accountability rather than a claim that one apology completes repair.

## III. Fukui's three-day festival makes finite youth materially public

The off-campus arts festival at Phoenix Plaza, sports festival, and culture festival create a three-day civic-school topology. Phoenix Plaza is not an abstract stage: it is a public performance venue linked to childhood and family memory, while school grounds, stalls, classrooms, the rooftop, and the closing bonfire distribute labor, spectatorship, and private speech `[p-0010#p0046-p0062]`.

Misaki's slogan `Shoot the blue moon` names a rare opportunity whose value comes from finitude rather than repeatability `[p-0010#p0450-p0472]`. The volume's theory of Fukui teenage life is similarly practical: a limited café map encourages young people to make situational places usable rather than consume endless interchangeable venues `[p-0012#p0138-p0148]`. Named food and vendors—including su_mu, Akiyoshi, Coppe-tei, and Orebo—function as provisioning and social infrastructure, not tourism inventory.

Teachers open the performance and adults supply instruments, contacts, memory, and attendance. School is therefore a cross-generational institution whose resources let students author an event; it is not merely a backdrop for romance.

## IV. Yua turns private music into a public address she owns

Yua directly asks Saku to listen and says that the coming performance is for him `[p-0012#p0417-p0447]`. Saku then tells Yuko about Yua's V05 riverbank saxophone rather than protect intimacy through concealment. Yuko hears Yua as crying in Saku's place, showing that entrusted knowledge can enlarge interpretation without transferring romantic authority `[p-0012#p0475-p0514]`.

Yua chooses wacci's `大丈夫`, proposes the final song, and appears as a recognized soloist whom peers and juniors already value `[p-0012#p0628-p0669]`. Her focalization refuses an all-or-nothing romantic economy. Even if Saku does not select her, the ordinary days she chose remain meaningful; she wants the closest place while also recognizing a sound distinct from her mother and a public identity that does not depend on being Saku's caretaker `[p-0012#p0690-p0829]`.

Saku cries openly. Yuko and Yuzuki recognize the same address. Yua runs to him, asks whether it reached him, and receives his promise to listen again `[p-0014#p0001-p0098]`. This is a direct Yua-side romantic expression and a major act of public self-authorship. It is not Saku's romantic selection, exclusivity, or couple status.

## V. Group-level repair with Momiji closes the apology cycle

At Ikku Park, Yuko, Yua, Haru, and Asuka make future plans with Momiji. Momiji says she will not forget the harm she caused the three women whose protected places she entered. Haru says the expiation period is over and that further apology is unnecessary; Momiji accepts the closure `[p-0014#p0130-p0195]`.

The scene materially resolves the V08 question of direct repair at group level. Every affected woman is present, the harm is named, and future practice replaces suspended penance. The source does not supply a long separate adjudication between Momiji and each of Asuka or Yua. The appropriate claim is therefore **group-level direct repair**, not three identical dyadic reconciliations or erasure of prior injury.

## VI. Yuzuki and Momiji build rivalry from private refusal to hate

Yuzuki escorts Momiji and discusses the V08 night. She does not present Saku's embrace as retroactive permission; she presents it as his protection of the whole person. She also warns Momiji against treating sexual display as a frictionless tactic.

More importantly, Yuzuki identifies Momiji's wish to be hated as a preventive strategy: becoming detestable now could make later rejection feel less vulnerable. Yuzuki refuses the assigned hatred. She will compete, and she would still hold an umbrella over Momiji in either outcome. Momiji apologizes for causing Yuzuki shame; Yuzuki recognizes that Saku prevented the feared contamination. Their `I will catch up / I will not wait` exchange makes rivalry and care mutually legible `[p-0014#p0255-p0396]`.

The pirate performance later embodies the relation. Yuzuki again says she likes Momiji, refuses hate, accepts future hurt and care, and seals the exchange as fellow fighters `[p-0020#p0190-p0357]`. Because the private conversation precedes the stage enactment, this is not merely fictional-role reconciliation.

## VII. Haru's paired race rehearses reciprocal adjustment

Haru initiates practice for the two-person race. Her disclosure about the Yuzuki crisis comes after Saku prompts her to let it out, so the scene strengthens bounded initiation without satisfying the long-running test for spontaneous support seeking `[p-0014#p0433-p0535]`.

During the race, Saku first matches Haru's rhythm and stride. After a stumble, Haru changes and matches Saku's pitch. The victory over Yuzuki and Kazuki is narrow, and Yuzuki adapts the same method during the contest `[p-0016#p0117-p0166]`, `[p-0016#p0266-p0421]`.

The event is less a rank proof than a model of relational technique: either partner can become the adjusting party. Haru privately names that she loved Yuzuki before Saku and can change for the woman she loves; she refuses to leave Yuzuki alone `[p-0016#p0404-p0417]`. This strengthens the primary Haru/Yuzuki partnership. It does not prove team basketball repair, durable athletic primacy, vocation, or an enacted romantic challenge to Saku.

## VIII. The blue-pirate performance distributes authorship

The blue team's pirate performance combines sword dance, acting, original music, band work, and multiple focal centers. Yuko and Momiji sing; Asuka plays guitar; Yuzuki plays bass; Haru drums; Yua moves between keyboard and saxophone. Saku and Kazuki stage combat, while Yuzuki and Momiji synchronize as opponents. Distinct parts remain recognizable inside a shared result.

Saku and Momiji's synchronization is real embodied compatibility `[p-0020#p0081-p0188]`. It is not evidence that Saku romantically chooses her. Kazuki says that he now has standing or qualification to protect, likely oriented toward Yuko, but the object is not named tightly enough to convert the line into settled couple evidence `[p-0020#p0107-p0134]`.

The performance becomes a public model of group form: coordination need not make every participant the same, and visible centrality need not create exclusive private status.

## IX. Tomoya's accountability gains a creative route without erasing harm

On the rooftop, Momiji reveals that Naruse Tomoya—the earlier aggressor—composed the performance's original song. Yuzuki had consented to see him, heard an apology, and chose forgiveness without forgetting. He has taken up band and music rather than demand restoration `[p-0022#p1624-p1743]`.

Yuzuki writes lyrics to his composition and the group performs it. This materially revises the earlier source-bound state from apology toward accountable creative reintegration. It does not erase stalking, create a right to renewed intimacy, or make artistic usefulness repayment sufficient by itself. Yuzuki retains the authority to define what forgiveness permits.

## X. The unchanged blue group ends while lateral relations survive

The festival formally ends the group's first and last school-festival form. Saku recognizes that the unchanged `blue` arrangement cannot continue `[p-0022#p0619-p0643]`.

This is neither a reset nor permanent social dissolution. The later bonfire gathers the group again, now with open declarations of rivalry, differentiated pairings, and explicit uncertainty about which threads will be retied or released `[p-0029#p0048-p0090]`. Momiji tells each woman that she is a competitor; they answer in their own registers and keep dancing.

The successor structure is therefore transparent contest inside preserved relation. It is more answerable than courtesy-as-moratorium and more complex than either unchanged harmony or total breakup.

## XI. Asuka authors a new domestic competence without choosing a domestic destiny

Asuka chooses the cat-ear-maid role because the festival lets her share an exceptional class event with Saku. With Yua's instruction, she makes her first omurice for him and adapts it to his preferences `[p-0022#p0782-p0934]`.

Yua's willingness to teach extends the V07–V08 pattern in which care knowledge circulates without losing provenance. Asuka's new practice is authored and collaborative rather than proof that one woman has seized another's office.

Asuka imagines a possible Tokyo domestic future with Saku. The focalized possibility is emotionally important but remains prospective imagination. V09 adds no housing, study, transport, employment, editorial placement, or communication logistics and does not establish cohabitation or a couple.

## XII. Yuko observes inclusive leadership and preserves a female future

Yuko recognizes a specific strength in Saku's public leadership: he notices people at the margin and makes room without requiring them to become copies of the central group `[p-0022#p0672-p0695]`. This corrects a model of Saku as merely charismatic center; his best public work redistributes visibility.

Yuko and Yuzuki also preserve their ten-year Kanazawa promise regardless of Saku's eventual answer `[p-0022#p0710-p0717]`. Neither the stage nor Yuko's continued love absorbs this independently primary female future.

## XIII. Saku chooses Yuzuki on stage and keeps romance undecided

Yuzuki apologizes for violating the self Saku protected and names herself as a truthful mirror. She promises to remain Yuzuki while Saku remains Saku; she will reflect his mistakes rather than preserve a flattering hero image `[p-0025#p0053-p0084]`.

Saku recognizes that Yuzuki has repeatedly enabled and corrected his choices and says she made his way of living appear beautiful to him `[p-0025#p0086-p0115]`. He then chooses the Dark Cloud Princess for `today, this stage only`, under the acting-only rule carried from V08 `[p-0025#p0120-p0147]`.

He calls her Moonlit Princess and understands her as mirror and moon, wanting to remain a man who will not disappoint her `[p-0027#p0001-p0024]`. Yuko returns to entrust their shared apple/heart; Yuzuki accepts it, and Saku and Yuzuki bite opposite sides of the red apple `[p-0027#p0031-p0045]`.

The image is deliberately intimate, but it is an apple-mediated stage gesture, not a lip kiss. The choice is theatrically and temporally bounded. It provides powerful independent recognition of Yuzuki's function in Saku's self-authorship while still not establishing a singular beloved, exclusive status, or couple.

## XIV. Yuzuki's mirror function becomes active support

At the afterparty, Yuzuki explicitly authorizes Saku to face Momiji as Saku. She does not ask him to accept or select Momiji; she demands that he stop protecting his hero-image through premature exclusion. Their reciprocal formula—Yuzuki as Saku's mirror and Saku as Yuzuki's moon—makes support a corrective relation, not unconditional affirmation `[p-0029#p0025-p0047]`.

This is the strongest current evidence that Saku can be supported by another person's independent judgment. He still does not originate the support request, so that developmental test remains open.

## XV. Momiji's confession changes refusal into admitted answerability

Before the public confession, Momiji asks Saku not to forget or exclude her after the festival. He promises continued memory if she continues to want it, without offering romance `[p-0022#p0001-p0079]`.

Momiji then publicly declares love `[p-0022#p1271-p1384]`. Saku immediately says it is impossible, refuses dating, and initially tries to return them to mere greetings because he will not accept another person while five already occupy his heart. The refusal is direct and remains a refusal to her immediate dating request.

Yuzuki sees that the method of expulsion contradicts Saku's own answerable heroism. She physically confronts him and insists not that he choose Momiji, but that he hear the real heart beneath the public tactic. The rest of the group independently tells him to go after Momiji `[p-0022#p1386-p1592]`.

On the rooftop, Saku hears the history of Tomoya's apology and song. Momiji then fully discloses her own history: love at first sight became a relation-specific love, and she seeks only the right to stand at the same start line until Saku names his final answer. She accepts likely loss and the disadvantage of arriving later `[p-0022#p1770-p1792]`, `[p-0024#p0001-p0011]`.

Saku recognizes that the time granted in V08 is for facing people rather than passively postponing them. He gives Momiji a third rooftop key and explicitly allows her to enter the answerable field `[p-0024#p0012-p0075]`.

The resulting state must retain both movements: **the immediate dating request is refused; the attempted social expulsion is rescinded**. Momiji becomes an openly known claimant whose bid Saku will face before the deadline. Saku recognizes that a different encounter order might have created another path, but does not directly name present romantic love for Momiji. There is no couple.

## XVI. The epilogue remains prospective despite exceptional Yuzuki recognition

The epilogue says that if the day comes when Saku names the heart Yuzuki saved, he would probably desire the way of continuing to be Saku `[p-0030#p0001-p0018]`.

The conditional and future grammar matter. Yuzuki is uniquely strong evidence for the self Saku wants to be in relation: a mirror who can expose and return him from error. The passage does not say that the heart has now received its final romantic name, retract the decision deadline, or enact an exclusive relationship.

V09 therefore differentiates Yuzuki without settling the field. Literary culmination, final focalization, stage centrality, and prospective desire are important evidence; none substitutes for the direct singular wording and mutual relationship status the corpus requires before a couple claim.
