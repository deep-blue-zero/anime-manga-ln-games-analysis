## I. Governing movement: truth makes exclusion real

Volume 05 takes the cooperative, nonexclusive group preserved through V04 and subjects it to the choice that structure had postponed. Yuko's public confession does not introduce romantic competition; it makes an already shared truth answerable. Saku's refusal is ethically necessary because acceptance would be false, but it is socially destructive because the group's warmth has depended partly on leaving incompatible claims unadjudicated.

The volume refuses two easy solutions. Inclusion cannot make singular romantic choice nonexclusive, and honesty cannot erase responsibility for the ambiguity that preceded it. Yuko's action removes Saku's preferred delay. His answer respects her request better than another moratorium would, yet the ensuing fracture exposes how his receptive intimacy, heroic availability, and refusal to name direction have distributed hope through the group.

The strongest current formulation is:

> **Volume 05 treats truthful choice as both a condition of responsible relation and an event that changes the social world. No amount of inclusive warmth can eliminate the exclusion built into a singular romantic request; the ethical question becomes whether people can bear that change without turning refusal into guilt, violence, self-punishment, or proof of personal insufficiency.**

## II. Yuko's `special`: from imposed difference to requested singularity

The prologue defines `special` first as the injury of being kept at admiring distance. Yuko is visible, praised, and accommodated, yet denied the ordinary friction through which equal friendship becomes possible. Saku's first-year intervention matters because he challenges her inadvertent coercion, lets Yua refuse, and treats Yuko as someone capable of correction rather than as an untouchable social object `[part0007#p0004-p0032]`, `[part0013#p0131-p0381]`.

Yuko then reverses the term. The category she hated becomes the position she wants from Saku alone. This is not inconsistency so much as a distinction between externally imposed exceptionalization and mutually chosen particularity. The danger lies in her reason for choosing him: Saku becomes heroic partly by taking the class role and blame onto himself. Her love recognizes real care while becoming attached to the same burden-bearing identity that constrains him.

The present volume gives Yuko independent reflective work. She tells Nazuna the origin of her love, fears that an ordinary beginning is weaker than the other girls' dramatic histories, and receives a necessary correction: love does not need narrative extremity to be real. Nazuna also normalizes jealousy as feeling without licensing harm and makes delay's cost explicit `[part0013#p0385-p0587]`.

## III. Finite summer and the forms that cannot be preserved unchanged

The volume's formal system begins with a caramel box whose changing sound makes pleasure countable. It continues through a composed restaurant photograph, stacked bath stools, triangular rice balls, fireworks, interlocking puzzle-piece keychains, paired shells, beach photographs, a horizon that cuts the group out of the day, and the residue of a shared bonfire `[part0008#p0004-p0033]`, `[part0008#p0292-p0312]`, `[part0008#p0490-p0544]`, `[part0018#p0810-p0900]`, `[part0026#p0143-p0192]`.

These objects promise retrieval but not exact return. A photograph may reactivate a day, a keychain may preserve a relation, and a shell may materialize cooperation, but later memory changes the color of what is recovered. The stacked triangle cannot be rebuilt as the same event once disturbed. This becomes the model for the group after confession: even successful reconciliation could not restore the precise unchosen form that existed beforehand.

## IV. Asuka: a bounded last summer and the request Saku disqualifies himself from making

Asuka treats the summer before departure as finite and therefore deliberately makes first and last experiences. The Ichijodani and Ichiho Falls excursion joins local transit, remembered school geography, playful matching clothes, a near-kiss, and food carrying regional and family memory. Her triangular `omusubi` explicitly binds the present meal to the childhood meeting that joined her fate to Saku `[part0008#p0371-p0820]`, `[part0010#p0001-p0008]`.

At the beach, Asuka comes alone after seeing the others, removes the rash guard so Saku will be the first to see her, and offers a bodily first without claiming a future category. Saku imagines another person's eventual touch and internally wants to ask her not to go or to wait. He does not speak because he believes he lacks standing `[part0022#p0206-p0263]`, `[part0024#p0001-p0018]`.

This materially clarifies the V04 uncertainty. Saku's desire and threatened-loss response are real; the text still does not supply a stable internal label or identify Asuka as the unnamed girl in his later refusal. Respect for Asuka's future and self-disqualification from making a request remain intertwined.

## V. Baseball after V04: institutional closure with chosen play still open

Saku explicitly calls the V04 game his Fujishi High retirement game. He considered returning and watched the team's later loss, but cannot simply resume the role from which he once fled. If he returns seriously, he wants to begin again from the uncomplicated pleasure of throwing and hitting. He commits to continuing his swings during high school `[part0010#p0276-p0310]`.

V05 therefore confirms the bounded reading of V04. Fujishi membership is closed; baseball itself is not. The important new possibility is a foundation selected for present pleasure rather than inherited entitlement, proof, or repayment.

## VI. Haru: direct protocol without a premature answer

Haru first repairs post-confession awkwardness through ordinary play, then asks to visit Saku's home. Yuzuki gives her room to choose how much aid she wants, and Haru acknowledges both desire and fear rather than hiding inside a tomboy role `[part0010#p0012-p0129]`.

During the later night run, Haru directly names the defect in her V04 action: she confessed and kissed Saku without making a request that established what answer, if any, was due. She refuses to retract her feeling. She asks to be seen as a girl and romantic possibility, says she must first complete an unspecified personal `kejime`, refuses to ask Saku to wait, and asks only that he not flee when she makes the genuine challenge. Saku agrees `[part0018#p0998-part0020#p0026]`.

This is a meaningful next move and a consent protocol, not a relationship resolution. Haru converts emotional overflow into a future accountable act while preserving Saku's freedom before it. The basketball team's post-V04 governance and safety practices are not materially retested; a report of wins and invitations does not answer the procedural question.

## VII. Yuzuki: bounded requests, jealousy, and shared burden

Yuzuki's apartment jealousy cannot be solved by dismissing Yua's domestic competence or the other girls' histories. On the balcony, she rehearses a hypothetical request to date. Saku promises that if she genuinely asks, he will genuinely consider it; she accepts that promise as sufficient for the day `[part0010#p0374-p0411]`, `[part0010#p0473-p0511]`.

At the fireworks festival, she makes a present request with explicit limits: ten fireworks, no hand because they are no longer lovers, only his sleeve. Saku complies. This is the clearest Yuzuki self-advocacy so far because desire, time, bodily boundary, and existing relationship status are stated together rather than hidden behind an `overwrite` alone `[part0016#p0242-p0326]`.

Saku later experiences unmistakable jealousy when she describes Kazuki's unperformed boyish smile. The feeling exposes his possessive assumption that only he should receive an unguarded Yuzuki. He recognizes the problem and then locks it away until after the trip, restoring ease through a mutually legible sexual joke rather than disclosure `[part0026#p0271-p0339]`. His attraction becomes more explicit while his deferral architecture remains intact.

## VIII. Yua: maintenance, priority, and non-extractive care

Yua's relationship with Saku is built from almost a year of shared shopping and food maintenance. After baseball withdrawal left him unable to feed himself adequately, she began cooking, taught workable recipes, and made food that would last. Saku knows the care exceeds convenience and consciously accepts it because she frames it as her own choice `[part0010#p0177-p0212]`.

This answers the support question narrowly. Saku can receive sustained care and name gratitude; he still does not initiate the request. Yua's expertise is not sentimental decoration. It integrates taste, season, economy, reuse, cleanup, and the long work of household obligation `[part0010#p0327-p0373]`.

The ending gives her an independent ethical rule. When actual separation forces a choice, she follows Saku and states that she will choose her own first priority. She does not demand to be the unnamed girl and explicitly allows that Saku's heart may hold Yuko, Yuzuki, Asuka, or Haru. She faces away and plays saxophone loudly enough to cover his sobbing `[part0029#p0104-p0154]`, `[part0031#p0001-p0012]`.

Her care supplies presence and acoustic privacy, not explanation, absolution, or romantic debt. This is the volume's strongest alternative to the hero model: another person's pain can be accompanied without being seized.

## IX. Girls' relations exceed the romantic ranking

Swimsuit shopping, cooking, dressing, water play, private love talk, and future plans make the girls' relations independently consequential. Yuzuki helps Haru prepare for a move toward Saku. Yuko and Yuzuki coordinate distinct forms of presentation. Yuko and Yua's paired blue obi flowers, embrace, and beach play carry a friendship that is not reducible to adjacency around Saku `[part0013#p0591-part0015#p0024]`.

The room's love question exposes the cost of that friendship. Yuko says she loves Saku; Yuzuki, Haru, and Yua protectively evade or deny their own directional claims. Yuko understands enough to feel both gratitude and apology. Cooperative rivalry remains behaviorally real, but protecting one another now requires concealment and anticipates exclusion.

## X. Yuko's public confession and Saku's explicit refusal

Yuko assembles the whole group in the empty classroom where their second-year network formed. She makes every rival a witness and removes the comic routine through which Saku first tries to restore ordinary rhythm `[part0027#p0032-p0129]`.

Her confession joins origin with accumulated daily love. Saku cannot remember the exact first-year wording that organized her life, while he recognizes his own dependence on her greetings, calls, companionship, and uncalculated attention. Yuko then asks to become his singular special `[part0027#p0131-p0284]`.

Saku wants the scheduled later reflection he had promised himself, but another person's completed choice eliminates that option. He considers the wider group cost and recognizes that his own pattern helped produce it. He refuses Yuko and says there is another girl in his heart `[part0027#p0292-p0377]`.

Three limits are mandatory:

1. the other girl is singular in his statement;
2. she is not named in the regular V05 main narrative;
3. the statement establishes neither her identity nor a formal couple.

Yuko's first cheerful recovery is another protective performance. She then lets it fail and says through tears that only Saku will do `[part0027#p0380-p0398]`. Truth is achieved; clean closure is not.

## XI. Kaito and Kazuki: concealed love does not authorize violence

Kaito punches and mounts Saku, revealing that he loves Yuko and had entrusted her happiness to Saku. His earlier withdrawal becomes legible as sacrifice organized around confidence in another man `[part0029#p0002-p0031]`.

The assault is not justified by love, grief, friendship, or Saku's prior ambiguity. Kaito misreads the quick spoken answer as quick internal disposal, but accurately exposes how little of Saku's deliberation is accessible from outside. Yuko stops him and correctly separates Saku's general kindness from romantic consent. Her further claim that she simply failed to become lovable individualizes a relational outcome into personal deficit and must not be adopted as the volume's ethical verdict `[part0029#p0034-p0047]`.

Kazuki prevents a second punch while refusing simple absolution. His earlier account of falling for a girl while seeing her love another, together with Saku's recognition, strongly implies Yuko; the exact direction remains inferential unless directly named. The boys therefore occupy distinct positions: concealed renunciation, violent entitlement, and culpability without romantic obligation.

## XII. Saku's self-punishment worsens

After leaving, Saku treats Kaito's blows as deserved and punches his already injured face for thinking of Asuka as a source of comfort. He tells himself that the person causing pain has no right to appear hurt or ask for support `[part0029#p0094-p0102]`.

This does not revise V04's self-disposability toward health. It broadens the failure from athletic injury to moral punishment. Accountability becomes a ban on receiving care, as though acknowledging his own pain would negate Yuko's. Yua interrupts isolation but does not repair the governing belief.

## XIII. Group fracture: enacted, not yet terminal

Saku leaves through the opposite classroom door and gives a second-term farewell rather than a next-day plan. Yua later follows him; the others remain with Yuko. The predicted exclusion becomes a material redistribution of presence `[part0029#p0058-p0064]`, `[part0029#p0104-p0144]`.

The prior configuration therefore does not survive unchanged. Saku imagines all ordinary meals and rhythms as permanently over, but that is focalized catastrophic expectation at the point of injury, not longitudinal proof of terminal destruction. The proper frozen state is **actual fracture with survival and repair open**.

## XIV. Fukui as developmental infrastructure

V05 makes locality independently retrievable. Ichijodani, Ichiho Falls, the Asuwa River, local rail, Genky, 100 Man Volt, AOSSA and Yutori Coffee, Takeda aburaage, simmered takuan, Phoenix fireworks, Tojinbo, the Echizen coast, Mikuni Sunset Beach, local speech, foods, and shopping routes are not decorative references. They mediate childhood memory, domestic competence, group logistics, first and last experiences, economic knowledge, family continuity, and the prospect of departure.

Tokyo is not simply freedom and Fukui is not simply enclosure. The most urgent departure scenes become meaningful because local life is dense enough to lose. V05 crosses the architecture's specialist threshold: locality now has a distinct evidence base and dependency route spanning V01–V05, independent retrieval value, and consequences for adulthood, romance, memory, and authorship.

## XV. Ramune, collective count, and form

The fireworks outing includes eight Ramune bottles, scaling the title object from privileged dyad to group count. No new bottle-opening climax supersedes V04's collaborative opening. Instead, the bottle enters the same finite-object system as photographs, triangles, shells, keys, and fireworks: bounded forms can hold shared time without preventing change.

The current motif model therefore adds **collective distribution** and **imperfect retrieval**. Enclosure, lens, co-authorship, collaborative opening, and reversible light remain available; no terminal meaning is established.

## XVII. Rival readings and hard abstentions

- **“Saku chose Asuka.”** Unsupported. His V05 refusal names one other girl but does not identify her.
- **“Yuko's confession was a mistake because it broke the group.”** Too simple. It creates harm and removes false stability, while answering the group's deferred ethical problem.
- **“Saku did nothing wrong because he answered honestly.”** Unsupported. Honest refusal is legitimate; his prior cultivated ambiguity and below-category reception of bids remain causally relevant.
- **“Saku owes Yuko acceptance because he helped her.”** Rejected. Care, formative impact, and mutual dependence do not create romantic obligation.
- **“Kaito's violence proves the strength of his love.”** Rejected as an ethical conclusion. It reveals suppressed attachment and entitlement while violating Saku and overriding Yuko.
- **“Yua's pursuit proves she is the unnamed girl.”** Unsupported. She explicitly refuses that condition.
- **“The group is permanently destroyed.”** Not established at the ending boundary. Fracture is fact; terminality is prediction.
- **“V05 repairs the injury ethic.”** Rejected. Saku converts responsibility into physical punishment and support refusal.
- **“Fukui is merely nostalgic scenery.”** Rejected by the density and causal work of place, transit, food, labor, speech, memory, and departure.

## XVIII. Visual evidence

The cover isolates Yuko as the focal volume character. The frontmatter group-at-fireworks spread distributes eight people across local interactions, while the bordered four-girl beach image anticipates preservation through photography. Narrative images foreground Asuka's offered rice ball, Yuko and Kotone's embodied family intimacy, Yuko helping Haru prepare, Yuzuki's sleeve-only fireworks boundary, Haru's clarified request, Asuka's deliberate swimsuit first, Yuko and Yua's independent play, Yuko's tearful post-refusal performance, and Yua's final saxophone protection.

The final horizontal image excludes Saku's body and lets Yua's instrument become the active line. It visually preserves her choice to create privacy rather than possess his grief.

## XIX. Prospective close

The regular main transaction freezes on five asymmetries:

1. Saku can tell the truth but still treats receiving comfort as moral theft.
2. Yuko can choose directness but cannot control the group cost or convert love into reciprocity.
3. Haru and Yuzuki can articulate bounded bids without yet receiving a romantic decision.
4. Yua can choose a person without requiring that he choose her in the same category.
5. the group can distribute care after fracture, but no current evidence shows the form of repair.
