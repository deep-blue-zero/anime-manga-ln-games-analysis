## 3. Component readings

### V065-01 — female friendship acquires a future independent of romantic outcome

Yuzuki focalizes the opening and outward Kanazawa movement. The narration explicitly changes to Yuko at `私、柊夕湖`, continues through the call and return meal, then explicitly changes back at `私、七瀬悠月` for the train close `[V065:p-0011#p0001-p0427]`, `[V065:p-0013#p0247-p0992]`, `[V065:p-0017#p0001-p0312]`.

Yuzuki identifies her mostly boyish wardrobe as a defense against the social cost of visible femininity. With Yuko's help she chooses to expose a protected register because she now wants Saku to see it. The act is authored, but its recipient-conditioned conquest frame prevents treating it as uncomplicated liberation `[V065:p-0011#p0397-p0427]`, `[V065:p-0013#p0001-p0012]`.

She also opens a real vocational question. Basketball remains her current commitment, but she wonders whether a first-discovered field deserves her whole life when her more general drive might produce excellence elsewhere. Haru's singular-seeming devotion becomes a contrast, not proof that Yuzuki is deficient `[V065:p-0011#p0213-p0248]`.

Yuko voluntarily recounts the V05/V06 crisis to Yuzuki and Nazuna. She owns the confession decision, rejects regret, and distinguishes a completed romantic attempt from continuing feeling: the summer love ended because Saku actually refused it, but she will keep thinking of him `[V065:p-0013#p0142-p0231]`. The source preserves the cost—she fears that only she has been refused and that another girl's confession might have received a different answer `[V065:p-0013#p0247-p0308]`.

Yuko nevertheless originates low-pressure reconnection. One local souvenir gives her a joking way to restart ordinary contact with Saku; a distinct parallel gift becomes her decision to speak to Kaito because she wants their friendship, not because she waits for his initiative `[V065:p-0013#p0627-p0685]`, `[V065:p-0013#p0807-p0927]`. Yuzuki helps through refusal symmetry: Yuko would not want Saku to avoid her, so she should allow that Kaito may likewise choose continued relation.

The group video call does not reverse Saku's refusal. Yuko turns sparse ordinary conversation into a renewed choice to love and replaces the V05 `bye-bye` with `see you in second term` `[V065:p-0015#p0115-p0175]`, `[V065:p-0017#p0001-p0033]`. Yuzuki, after learning that Yuko also knows Saku's miso soup, directly asks Saku to cook something he has never made. This is a differentiated shared-future request, not a dating request or reciprocal selection `[V065:p-0013#p0787-p0805]`, `[V065:p-0015#p0078-p0113]`.

Yuko, Yuzuki, and Nazuna then promise a Kanazawa return in ten years regardless of future partners. Shared travel, clothing, food, apology, humor, and name exchange make a female friendship with its own future rather than a staging device for Saku's ranking `[V065:p-0011#p0085-p0143]`, `[V065:p-0017#p0110-p0201]`. Yuzuki privately retains her romantic rivalry and resolves to become the most fitting woman `[V065:p-0017#p0206-p0312]`.

### V065-02 — editorial conviction is corrected by the ethics of silence

Asuka focalizes throughout. Her midnight radio and unsent-letter images search for co-present voices while acknowledging that even Saku cannot fully share the singular final summer of her third year `[V065:p-0019#p0001-p0046]`, `[V065:p-0021#p0001-p0034]`.

Her father now brings clumsy handmade rice balls, shares dreams, and arranges a visit to local magazine `URALA`. He explicitly supports rather than redirects her Tokyo plan. Their prior conflict has become imperfect practical care without transfer of decision authority `[V065:p-0021#p0039-p0141]`. Asuka still cannot ask Saku plainly to join; she conveys the opening and lets him volunteer, later admitting a direct invitation would have been legitimate `[V065:p-0021#p0145-p0190]`.

During a practice interview, Asuka's preparation and verbal fluency become an ethical failure. She fills each silence with a proposed answer until the interviewee can only agree. Saku asks simpler open questions and waits. The editor's correction is precise: Asuka made another person speak Asuka's words; silence is time in which the other searches within the self, and the interviewer must wait `[V065:p-0021#p0720-p0831]`.

Her bathroom collapse is the first failure in a vocation on which she has staked an authored future, not romantic humiliation. Hirayama does not annul the mistake. She recounts how nostalgia once made her overwrite a changed local bakery and says the capacity to remain dissatisfied with one's work is editorial fuel `[V065:p-0023#p0090-p0116]`. The editor-in-chief also supplies a non-catastrophic locality frame: Tokyo failure need not end Asuka's life because Fukui can remain a place of return, without becoming an order to stay `[V065:p-0023#p0120-p0154]`.

At `HOSHIDO`, Suzuki presents editing as care for another person's expression and as material preservation of a life. A book may support its author, transform suffering, create relation, and retain an afterimage after death `[V065:p-0023#p0231-p0285]`. When Asuka wants to disclose her failure, Saku notices and leaves the room. This targeted space-giving enables her self-originated disclosure to Suzuki `[V065:p-0023#p0287-p0311]`.

Suzuki reframes without excusing presumption. Because value is not universal, an editor may need the conviction that only she can find and carry a work; Asuka must pair that sustaining belief with the listening discipline learned at `URALA` `[V065:p-0023#p0314-p0359]`. The local editorial path is shown as authentic, which is precisely why Asuka refuses to appropriate it as a compromise that would let her remain near Saku while only nibbling the edge of her dream. Tokyo remains her choice `[V065:p-0023#p0413-p0440]`.

Saku recognizes that Asuka was so absorbed in her vocation that he scarcely existed in her attention. He calls the single-mindedness beautiful and reads failure as the reverse face of urgency `[V065:p-0023#p0443-p0467]`. Asked why he joined, he describes a post-V06 blank: he does not know whether to refill himself with baseball, study, love/friendship, or a new story `[V065:p-0023#p0468-p0498]`. The disclosure is unusually direct but still answers Asuka's question; it is not a self-originated support request. Their half-step of nearness and wish that their story continue sustain intimacy without creating a couple `[V065:p-0023#p0488-p0517]`.

### V065-03 — Yua asks for a date and receives a material place, not selection

Yua focalizes throughout and addresses her absent mother at both ends. Domestic competence, recovered home, and romantic longing remain connected without becoming interchangeable `[V065:p-0025#p0009-p0024]`, `[V065:p-0027#p1037-p1047]`.

The formerly safe shopping/cooking routine now feels precarious because Yua cannot hide inside familylike usefulness. She recognizes that sacrifice protected an idealized, convenient version of herself from jealousy, fear, and refusal `[V065:p-0025#p0047-p0084]`, `[V065:p-0025#p0135-p0163]`. Saku originates the ordinary shopping invitation; Yua refuses its default framing and asks directly, `朔くん、私とデートしよ？` `[V065:p-0025#p0090-p0179]`.

At Fukui's fresh-food market, Yua fears that her genuinely desired domestic outing cannot register as romantic. Saku accepts the date label, says this kind of date is possible only with her, and compliments her clothes `[V065:p-0027#p0022-p0136]`. He chooses her cooking over a popular restaurant and later asks her to make what she wants. The reciprocal ordinary practice includes shared money management and his kitchen help, although the greater labor remains hers `[V065:p-0027#p0140-p0186]`, `[V065:p-0027#p0323-p0335]`, `[V065:p-0027#p0909-p0936]`.

Yua's kitchen claim is newly exposed as contingent: if Saku chooses another partner, someone else will sustain his daily life. She concludes that ordinary proximity requires becoming special while refusing to make him carry her fear and jealousy `[V065:p-0027#p0290-p0326]`. Her concrete marriage-and-child scenes are private fantasy that she interrupts or withholds; they are desire evidence, not mutual future agreement `[V065:p-0027#p0247-p0277]`, `[V065:p-0027#p0498-p0502]`.

When Yua's father unexpectedly returns, Saku refuses to flee and originates a formal introduction. The father credits him as one cause of family change, but Yua immediately rejects transferring family damage into a duty that Saku never chose `[V065:p-0027#p0593-p0719]`. Asked not to hurt her, Saku refuses an impossible guarantee. He says continued relation makes hurt possible, admits some wounds cannot be healed by the person who caused them, and states that Yua taught him a relation can endure mutual hurt and that he wants to face her that way `[V065:p-0027#p0721-p0746]`.

This is explicit reciprocal relational commitment, not romantic selection. The father's courtship-coded farewell and Yua's imagined future introduction are bounded by her own counterstatement: there is no promise and the future opportunity may never come `[V065:p-0027#p0748-p0766]`. Saku later explains that his `大好き` meant Yua's cooking plus shopping and table time; Yua answers with a deliberately double-addressed `私も大好き` `[V065:p-0027#p0802-p0836]`.

Yua asks Saku to tell his family about her when contact arises and offers to meet them if requested. He thanks her and does not refuse, but the action is not shown; its outcome remains anticipated rather than completed `[V065:p-0027#p0861-p0903]`.

Saku then reveals an independently purchased antique stool for his kitchen. He noticed Yua standing through long preparations, frames it as gratitude for her repeated care and V06 labor, calls it `優空用`, and asks her to use it ordinarily rather than preserve it `[V065:p-0027#p0950-p1033]`. The chair materially reciprocates care and anticipates repeated future presence. It does not grant exclusive domestic ownership, cohabitation, romantic rank, or permanence. Yua herself says the underlying uncertainty remains before resolving to pursue first place `[V065:p-0027#p0997-p1047]`.

### V065-04 — Haru carries two loves into one expanding practice

Haru focalizes throughout. She experiences summer's end as possible displacement from a two-person season and recognizes that she and Saku have changed without knowing where the changed self goes next `[V065:p-0030#p0001-p0023]`.

The former Fujishi captain and ace who inspired her school choice, plus Ashiba ace Mai Todo, expose the scale of elite commitment. Ashiba's comparable hours but relentless fundamentals remove an environmental excuse, while its discipline sacrifices ordinary school life and romance to basketball `[V065:p-0030#p0091-p0127]`, `[V065:p-0030#p0211-p0279]`. Haru names the feared tradeoff: attraction has taken attention once given only to basketball, but a university/industrial-team path may take her away from Saku. Must the first love require abandoning the second `[V065:p-0030#p0282-p0313]`?

Her first half against the alumni is genuinely poor. She cannot turn bodily readiness into directed play, seeks the absent Saku even in thought, and is outplayed in passing, off-hand technique, reading, and adaptability `[V065:p-0030#p0425-p0478]`, `[V065:p-0030#p0481-p0593]`, `[V065:p-0030#p0596-p0699]`.

Mai—not Haru—uses Haru's phone to call Saku. Haru explicitly fears dependence and does not initiate the request. Saku returns Haru's own V06 injunction to smile, calls her his partner, and nearly says he prefers/likes her enjoying basketball; Haru preserves the ambiguity rather than convert the verbal stumble into a confession `[V065:p-0030#p0700-p0733]`, `[V065:p-0032#p0001-p0068]`.

The alumni's insult to Saku ignites Haru because it insults the summer and way of living they made together. The recovery is relationally catalyzed, but Haru converts the stimulus into self-authored proof and learning `[V065:p-0032#p0070-p0128]`. She expands her court map, uses passes as offensive weapons, learns from off-hand technique, retains her drive, and reveals a secretly practiced three-point shot `[V065:p-0032#p0181-p0259]`, `[V065:p-0032#p0263-p0436]`, `[V065:p-0034#p0001-p0049]`.

That shot explicitly addresses basketball and `ダーリン` as two current one-sided loves. Divided attachment does not require half-hearted action, but Saku's romantic reciprocity remains absent `[V065:p-0034#p0001-p0018]`.

The alumni's hostility is a coordinated intervention requested by Coach Misaki, although their technical correction is real. They recognize a chain of stewardship—Aki to Kei to Haru—and invite Haru and Mai to imagine university, corporate-league, and even Olympic continuation `[V065:p-0034#p0053-p0167]`. This creates a serious life-course option, not a decision.

The older players disclose that their lack of boyfriends was circumstance, not proof that serious basketball requires emotional purity. Misaki neither rejects Ashiba's policy nor universalizes it. Her answer is that Haru should embrace romance, friendship, success, failure, conflict, regret, and the beloved man, then put all of it into the shot `[V065:p-0034#p0180-p0260]`. Haru's `私の男` and Mai's teasing refusal to accept a gift are competitive language, not an established relationship or a new substantiated Mai bid `[V065:p-0036#p0007-p0037]`.

## 7. Rival readings and hard limits

### “The Yua date and kitchen chair make her Saku's girlfriend”

**Rejected.** Saku accepts the date framing, differentiates the outing, commits to honest continued relation, and creates a durable place for Yua. The text also explicitly withholds promise and romantic declaration. Material reciprocity is not synonymous with couple state.

### “Yuko has moved on because she calls the summer love finished”

**Rejected.** She says the attempt ended with actual refusal and then chooses to keep loving and to restart ordinary contact. Acceptance of refusal and persistence of feeling coexist.

### “Asuka's local editorial discoveries undermine her Tokyo choice”

**Rejected.** The local paths are valuable enough to tempt her. She then states that using one to remain near Saku would be her compromise, not those editors' compromise, and retains Tokyo.

### “Saku's words are the only reason Haru can play”

**Overstated.** His returned encouragement is an effective catalyst, but Haru's later performance depends on her own prior practice, observation, adaptation, female athletic network, and decision to integrate rather than purify her life.

### “The four stories establish a precise sequence”

**Unsupported.** Each has a secure late-August placement and internal transitions. No cross-reference proves the exact order of every outing; editorial order alone is insufficient.

### “A 6.5 volume can be absorbed into the V06 main freeze”

**Rejected by architecture.** It is separately published, differently structured, and first available after the V06 freeze. Current ledgers may change; the historical V06 epistemic record must not.
