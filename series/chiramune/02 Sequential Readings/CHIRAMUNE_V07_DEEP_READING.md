## I. Governing movement: protected places become unstable claims

V07 begins by permitting the repaired group a September pause. Each person values ordinary continuity, and each respects the distinctive place another woman appears to occupy beside Saku. That courtesy is real care. It is also the procedure by which nobody advances or withdraws.

Momiji enters after those norms formed. She requests access to Saku's dance, Asuka's riverbank, Haru's catch, and Yua's kitchen. Each concrete act is permitted; her romantic and destabilizing purpose is not disclosed. The resulting conflicts reveal that neither side can be reduced to a simple wrongdoer or owner.

> **Volume 07 distinguishes emotional reliance from romantic title. The five older girls have no proprietary right over Saku, public space, sport, or domestic competence, but their repeated practices carry real reliance and history. Momiji may pursue him and request entry; she remains responsible for strategically exploiting differently understood junior permission. Ending defensive stasis is valuable only when process, disclosure, consent, responsibility, and avoidable harm remain visible as separate axes.**

## II. September continuity is chosen, not restored

Saku and Yua resume the river route. He consciously names market food and future shopping because he refuses to let her care disappear into habit `[part0008#p0031-p0068]`. Kaito returns boisterously after mutual assault/apology; Atomu offers a minimal greeting; Nazuna is no longer treated as a mere classmate `[part0008#p0072-p0157]`.

Yuko cuts the long hair she had treasured and identifies the change as becoming a new self. Saku responds bodily and emotionally, repeats that the present Yuko suits her, and receives her request to keep watching `[part0008#p0297-p0388]`. This is renewed gendered attention after refusal, not a reversal of that refusal.

Four heroine focalizations explain why pause serves each of them. Haru fears romantic failure could destroy the athletic self linked to Saku; Yua knows she received the expected refusal vicariously without bidding; Asuka wants entry into the group's story before Tokyo; Yuzuki prefers unreasoned nearness to a claim that could be refused `[part0008#p0675-p1073]`.

The risk is therefore collective rather than only Saku's. Everyone can describe what delay protects.

## III. School-festival institutions redistribute visible roles

The class chooses a Fukui-adaptable *Snow White*: Yuko is Snow White, Yuzuki the queen/witch, and Saku the prince `[part0008#p1381-p1519]`. Performance roles are institutional assignments, not private relationship evidence.

Yuzuki volunteers as blue-team vice-captain beside Saku where Yuko might once have been expected. Yuko declines because cheer and play would overburden her. Nazuna manages class logistics; Atomu accepts backstage responsibility through her separate relation `[part0008#p0392-p0603]`, `[part0008#p1077-p1378]`.

Kazuki maneuvers Kenta into joining through anime performance and a pride challenge. Participation succeeds, and Kenta later works hard enough to teach others; the initial method nevertheless bypasses a direct refusal and should not be purified by the later result `[part0008#p0500-p0587]`, `[part0011#p0214-p0280]`.

The cheer camp creates a collaborative pirate sequence of departure, encounter, conflict, reconciliation, and feast. Women explicitly fight rather than occupy protected-only roles. Saku and Yuzuki divide teaching groups so weaker dancers are not marked as burdens `[part0011#p0085-p0144]`, `[part0011#p0710-p0766]`.

## IV. Momiji: authored juniorhood as entry strategy

First-year representative Nozomi Momiji is a 100-metre sprinter with Inter-High experience. She says she joined because she admires the conspicuous second-years as a group; first-year competition for blue-team places rose after their participation became known `[part0009#p0073-p0093]`, `[part0009#p0841-p0904]`.

Her direct requests contrast with the older girls' self-restraint. She seeks given-name address, Saku's dance partnership, and responsibility for his costume. Her innocent questions also expose external social optics: Saku/Yua look functionally domestic, and the former Saku/Yuzuki performance circulated as a real couple `[part0009#p1233-p1283]`.

Saku explicitly treats her junior status as safe. He says partnership with any of the other girls would bring the approaching decision into the festival; with Momiji he expects temporary innocence `[part0011#p0022-p0039]`.

Momiji later refuses simple circle membership because she wants to remain *their junior* `[part0019#p0573-p0604]`. Chapter 3 reveals this as both genuine relational preference and strategy: Saku would exclude a sixth romantic claimant but welcome apparently non-romantic junior affection `[part0026#p0286-p0316]`.

## V. The chair proves place exclusivity, not relationship exclusivity

During a dinner-planning visit, Yuzuki begins to use the stool marked `優空用`. Saku involuntarily stops her because it would feel disloyal and imagines Yua's sadness. Yuzuki immediately understands, replaces it, and moves to the sofa; Saku's apology stays internal `[part0009#p0331-p0356]`.

Yuzuki later reads the chair as a place Saku gave Yua rather than a nest she made for herself. Yua admits that the chair reassures her and that she imagined making Saku's costume from it `[part0015#p0456-p0486]`, `[part0017#p0141-p0155]`.

When Momiji reaches for it, Saku begins to intervene before Yua shouts. His repeated conduct and both women's interpretations establish strong symbolic reservation of the chair. They do not establish Yua as girlfriend, singular beloved, or exclusive domestic partner `[part0024#p0494-p0547]`.

## VI. Domestic memory becomes transmissible between women

Saku completes Yuzuki's V06.5 request with taco rice and labor-intensive edamame cold soup chosen for her tastes. Their private improvised dance is mutually coordinated and physically intimate, but he evades her mirror question rather than selecting her `[part0009#p0360-p0435]`, `[part0009#p0520-p0579]`.

At camp, Yua asks Yuzuki to teach her the katsudon recipe Saku praised. She refuses to outdo and overwrite a memory that belongs to Yuzuki and Saku. Yuzuki shares the recipe because Yua can preserve that history, and Saku's praise has already made her feel seen `[part0015#p0501-p0539]`, `[part0017#p0001-p0025]`.

This female-to-female transfer is not surrender or ranking. Practical knowledge becomes shared care while its history remains attributed.

## VII. Momiji's dance exposes five distinct jealousies

During the completed dance, Saku searches the silhouettes of other women rather than fully seeing Momiji. He imagines the pain of any one of them dancing with another man and recognizes that his senior smile is another postponement `[part0011#p0341-p0370]`.

Four brief internal fragments are later attributable. Yuzuki regrets yielding the model role; Haru resents losing the place beside her former `相棒`; Asuka wishes she could request as directly as Momiji; Yua wishes she had kept Saku's costume work from her chair `[part0011#p0374-p0392]`, corroborated by `[part0013#p0065-p0076]`, `[part0015#p0115-p0133]`, and `[part0017#p0063-p0073]`, `[part0017#p0141-p0155]`.

Yuko chooses a different response. She tells Momiji the dance was beautiful, admits that she too wants to dance with Saku, and asks the junior to watch him when he overextends. Yuzuki recognizes this as Yuko's ability to state love without either attacking a rival or masking herself `[part0011#p0401-p0425]`, `[part0017#p0029-p0066]`.

## VIII. Saku names five romantic stakes without choosing one

Yuko asks to remain beside Saku a little longer. He cannot say it aloud but internally wants her to stay `[part0011#p0882-p0916]`.

Later, imagining another man entering Yuko's room and receiving all of her produces pain Saku can only call love. He tests the same future with Yua, Yuzuki, Haru, and Asuka and says each hurts equally `[part0019#p0268-p0300]`.

This is direct Saku-side corroboration of the five-person romantic field, stronger than Yuzuki's earlier inference. It is not a rank, singular selection, couple declaration, or evidence that every form of care is interchangeable. Momiji is a sixth claimant from her own perspective but is not included in Saku's enumeration here.

## IX. Female relations carry truths the central dyads cannot

Asuka and Haru compare the looks Saku gives them. Haru calls hers teammate trust and Asuka's admiration; Asuka calls hers nostalgia. They acknowledge mutual frustration, then Asuka practices beside Saku rather than remain outside the sporting circle `[part0011#p0983-p1065]`, `[part0013#p0008-p0065]`.

Haru says cheer is part of becoming stronger. Asuka's decision to pursue Tokyo helps Haru articulate that a new athletic dream may require sacrifice of distance, time, or relation. Neither choice is completed here `[part0011#p0616-p0655]`, `[part0015#p0015-p0044]`.

Yua and Asuka exchange gratitude across time: Asuka accompanied Saku after baseball; Yua was beside him on the V06 rupture night and brought Asuka into the festival repair. They name mutual envy of the other's place, adopt more intimate given-name address, and explicitly propose friendship `[part0019#p0075-p0157]`.

The volume's most consequential relational work therefore travels among women as much as through Saku.

## X. Three crises distinguish permission, reliance, harm, and repair

### Asuka's riverbank

Saku recognizes Asuka's riverbank as bounded but rationalizes dance practice there because he and Momiji can wait for her. Momiji knows it is special and asks to share it. Asuka experiences a panic-like response, shouts at both, and flees while immediately recognizing her disproportionate jealousy `[part0020#p0162-p0218]`, `[part0020#p0222-p0418]`.

Saku discloses the event after Yuzuki detects distress on a work call. Yuzuki prevents him from pre-empting Asuka's apology. Asuka approaches both the next day; all three apologize and resume cheer work `[part0022#p0016-p0092]`, `[part0022#p0096-p0124]`.

### Haru's catch

Haru herself invites Momiji into the catch. When prior baseball experience lets the junior match Saku's tempo, Haru yanks back the glove hard enough to hurt her hand, lashes out, and flees `[part0022#p0147-p0217]`, `[part0022#p0225-p0467]`.

Haru realizes that being present in July was contingent and passive devotion cannot secure unique `相棒` status. She apologizes to both, brings Momiji outside, and plays catch with her through lunch. That enacted repair matters more than frictionless resumption `[part0024#p0038-p0052]`.

### Yua's kitchen

Yua invites Momiji home and voluntarily lets her cook. Momiji's comparable lifelong domestic competence, Saku's immediate praise, sleeve-rolling, tasting, and the sight of another cook destabilize Yua's `special ordinary` `[part0024#p0095-p0448]`.

Yua shouts when Momiji reaches for the chair. Momiji cries but hides it with an onion joke. Yua flees after an apology she knows sounds like a barb, then sends long messages and makes lunches for Saku, Yuzuki, and Momiji `[part0024#p0494-p0590]`, `[part0026#p0010-p0029]`. The source records behavioral repair but not a complete direct repair conversation.

The three older girls remain responsible for their outbursts. The emotional reliance behind each reaction explains but does not excuse harm.

## XI. Momiji's method: lawful access is not full disclosure

Yuzuki uses a false café meeting to test Momiji, then confronts her on the roof. The lie is Yuzuki's deliberate provocation, not neutral discovery `[part0026#p0033-p0130]`.

Momiji confirms love from before cheer squad and calls it love at first sight. She says she has committed no forbidden act: she asked to dance, practice, catch, cook, and enter spaces, and received permission `[part0026#p0238-p0279]`, `[part0026#p0320-p0343]`.

That defense is only partly sufficient. She did not fabricate concrete consent or coerce access. She did conceal the romantic/destabilizing purpose under a junior role that caused Saku and the others to evaluate her requests differently. Her process is strategically undisclosed even when each local act is permitted.

Momiji says admiration of the group and her tears are genuine, and that she did not want Asuka, Haru, or Yua to cry. She also says none owns a place merely by imagining it exclusive without a relationship title `[part0026#p0349-p0389]`.

She explicitly accepts the possibility of hurting Saku and prefers hatred to irrelevance. `傷つける覚悟` is harm tolerance, not self-validating courage. Later evidence must test whether she learns proportionate process or whether the story rewards destabilization without accountability `[part0026#p0470-p0535]`.

## XII. Her diagnosis is incisive and interested

Momiji calls the five women's courtesy beautiful and argues that it also lets them hold hands in stasis `[part0026#p0349-p0458]`. She attacks Yuzuki's fear that rescue is their only concrete bond. This repeats Yuzuki's own insecurity rather than establishing objective emptiness `[part0026#p0397-p0424]`.

Momiji compares her beauty with Yuzuki, cooking with Yua, athleticism with Haru, and listening ability with Asuka `[part0026#p0551-p0576]`. These are interested competitive claims. They do not make persons, histories, or functions substitutable.

She refuses friendship/circle membership because her desired outcome would hurt the five and because joining hands would reproduce pause. Her goal is to rewind spring and neutralize the accident of meeting order `[part0026#p0521-p0589]`.

The five have no title allowing them to bar another suitor. Momiji has no exemption from responsibility merely because her pursuit exposes their avoidance.

## XIII. Yuzuki converts romantic challenge into vocational release

Yuzuki stops trying to police Momiji and directs anger at her own defensive self-limitation. She realizes that keeping an unused reserve protects the fantasy that full effort would have succeeded `[part0028#p0003-p0088]`, `[part0028#p0473-p0538]`.

At team practice she asks Mai Todo for a 30-point one-on-one. Coach Misaki authorizes it as instruction. Yuzuki steals Mai's opening drive, scores from far beyond the three-point line, mirrors Mai's techniques, leads 27–24, and makes the final long three for an implied 30–24 practice win `[part0028#p0092-p0434]`, `[part0028#p0557-p0657]`.

This demonstrates an exceptional peak state in one practice format. It does not establish permanent regional ranking, team superiority, or repeatability.

Haru experiences the duel as another invasion of her athletic domain and recognizes she had reserved basketball primacy even from her trusted partner `[part0028#p0436-p0461]`. Yuzuki's empowerment has relational cost and does not erase the independent Yuzuki/Haru trajectory.

Yuzuki credits Haru's prior full-effort ethic and Nazuna's V06.5 challenge as well as Momiji. Her breakthrough is mediated by multiple women and enacted through an existing vocation, not delivered by Saku `[part0028#p0487-p0555]`.

## XIV. Yuzuki repudiates indefinite fake relation

Yuzuki rejects her earlier indefinite `いつか` and desire for the fake arrangement to continue. If Saku cannot name the relation, she will write a name into it; she wants the real rather than the fake `[part0028#p0631-p0643]`.

She closes by promising Haru, Mai, Momiji, and Saku that she will show them `七瀬悠月という、女` `[part0028#p0643-p0663]`. This is self-authored competitive intent. Saku does not hear or reciprocate it in this source, so no confession, selection, or couple is created.

## XV. Literal final prologue: Momiji becomes her own hero

The postposed `プロローグ　ヒーロー見参` uses Momiji's sprint imagery: starting blocks, gunshot, lost spring, a single wish, and a run toward the moon `[part0030#p0001-p0027]`.

It alludes to unreturned kindness and a casual hero who stopped cold tears but does not narrate the encounter. Do not invent a rescue chronology from the allusion `[part0030#p0004-p0029]`.

The final claim is self-authorship: Momiji will no longer wait for another hero but becomes her own, running to cut through stasis `[part0030#p0029-p0033]`. Her agency coexists with her willingness to hurt; neither cancels the other.

## XVI. Saku's support-request and baseball tests remain open

Saku tells Yuzuki about the Asuka conflict only after she hears distress in his voice, and he tells Momiji about stagnation only after she asks what troubled him `[part0022#p0016-p0056]`, `[part0019#p0630-p0653]`. Both are more candid than silence, but neither is a clearly self-originated support request.

Momiji promises to blow away the stagnation; Saku does not ask her to undertake that role. Accepting or failing to refuse volunteered intervention must not be misclassified as originating help.

Saku practices swings, remembers baseball without self-punishment, and catches with Haru and Momiji. These are repeated ordinary pleasures and relational media, not an educational, institutional, or vocational baseball choice `[part0011#p0977-p1055]`, `[part0022#p0147-p0386]`.

## XVII. Fukui locality and seasonal causality

Yuko's house, familiar park, Asuwa river route, Elpa, Sakura Chaya, Hachiban Ramen, Texas Hands, and Mister Donut organize provisioning, movement, secrets, and claims to place. Local food is social infrastructure: shared pickup economics, camp hospitality, family memories, unauthorized snacks, and late-night escape `[part0009#p0796-p0833]`, `[part0009#p0968-p1053]`, `[part0011#p0470-p0516]`, `[part0015#p0204-p0344]`.

Second-term school institutions are equally causal. Anonymous class decisions, vertical cheer organization, differentiated labor, full-school no-club days, and team-authorized one-on-one practice determine who can meet, lead, teach, and change.

September is not decorative. Dawn lets Momiji cast movement against pause; a season-changing shower frames her confrontation with Yuzuki; the final court sequence clears from rain toward blue. V07 makes transitional weather the temporal pressure that ordinary contentment cannot halt.

## XVIII. Visual evidence

The cover and color frontispiece make Momiji the threshold figure: uniform and Ramune on the cover, red track clothing at sunrise in the final color page. This brackets junior familiarity and time-restarting speed; it supplies no reciprocal Saku state.

A color group-night spread places a sleeping Saku within the camp ensemble rather than creating a couple portrait. Another isolates newly short-haired Yuko, supporting self-revision without relationship status.

Interior art repeatedly centers relations among women: Yuzuki/Haru in the bath, Yuzuki embracing Yua over the recipe, and Asuka touching Yua's hair. The three crises receive parallel embodied images—Asuka doubled over, Haru crying on her bicycle, and Yua collapsed after falling—distributing harm across distinct practices rather than comic rivalry.

The rain spread pairs tearful Yuzuki with composed Momiji, followed by Yuzuki alone in athletic focus. Visual progression runs from confrontation to self-directed activation, not Momiji/Saku couple iconography.

## XXII. Rival readings and hard abstentions

- **“Saku has six equal romantic interests.”** Unsupported. He enumerates five; Momiji is a sixth claimant from her perspective but remains junior in his current frame.
- **“Yua's chair makes her the chosen girl.”** Rejected. The chair is reserved; person and relationship exclusivity do not follow.
- **“Momiji simply innocently joins the group.”** Rejected. Her admiration is genuine, but her junior presentation and place entries are strategic.
- **“Momiji is a coercive villain.”** Overstated. She obtains concrete permission and has a right to pursue Saku; the ethical defect is concealed purpose and accepted avoidable harm, not fabricated consent.
- **“The five own Saku or their places.”** Rejected. Reliance and history merit recognition but do not create romantic title or authority to exclude another suitor.
- **“Jealous outbursts prove the relationships are romantic.”** Incomplete. They prove attachment and threatened identity; each romantic category requires its own direct evidence.
- **“Yuzuki defeats Mai and becomes the region's best player.”** Rejected. She wins one authorized 30-point practice one-on-one in a peak state; permanence and team context remain open.
- **“Yuzuki confesses to Saku.”** Rejected. She privately resolves to seek a real relation; Saku neither hears nor answers.
- **“Saku asks Momiji to save him from stagnation.”** Rejected. She volunteers that project after eliciting disclosure.
- **“Baseball is now Saku's chosen future.”** Unsupported. It remains repeated ordinary practice without durable institutional stakes.
- **“Asuka's Tokyo move is operational.”** Unsupported. Direction persists; concrete departure logistics remain absent.
- **“Conflict automatically makes the group healthier.”** Rejected. Some conflict reveals truth and prompts repair; avoidable injury remains injury.
- **“The afterword proves Momiji is ethically right.”** Rejected. Production testimony explains narrative construction; it does not settle diegetic ethics.

## XXIII. Prospective close

V07 freezes on six revised asymmetries:

1. Saku can name five simultaneous romantic pains but still cannot choose or originate a request that shares the decision burden.
2. Yua's chair is genuinely reserved as a place, while the person and relationship remain non-exclusive.
3. Asuka, Haru, and Yua learn that repeated access cannot guarantee permanent role ownership; each also remains responsible for harm done in panic.
4. Momiji has an autonomous right to enter the romantic field and an accountable strategy that combines permission with concealed purpose.
5. Yuzuki stops treating courtesy and unused capacity as proof of virtue, releasing both athletic and romantic ambition without yet receiving an answer.
6. the group's September peace is revealed as both needed recovery and a collective moratorium that can no longer hold.
