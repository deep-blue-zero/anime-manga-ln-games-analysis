# Chiramune V01 prospective freeze

## Responsibility

This file preserves the **epistemic state after completing Volume 01 and before opening Volume 02 for analysis**. It is intentionally shorter and more rigid than the deep reading. Its purpose is to prevent later knowledge from rewriting what V01 did and did not establish.

## Frozen source boundary

- Japanese Volume 01 EPUB only.
- Same-edition illustrations reviewed as visual witnesses.
- Author afterword reviewed as paratext only.
- No V02+ narrative, supplemental story, anime, wiki, reception, or external-character material used to settle the findings below.

## Preserved findings

### F01 — Saku's competence is real, not only narrated

**Class:** FACT + INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high

Saku's social and athletic competence is independently supported by peer behavior, teacher reliance, successful classroom regulation, preparation for Kenta's intervention, and the reactions of people who do not simply echo his self-description.

**Do not later rewrite as:** "V01 already revealed Saku's confidence was merely a facade." V01 supports a real competence / costly persona tension, not a fake-versus-real binary.

### F02 — Saku deliberately authors a public identity

**Class:** FACT
**Confidence:** high

He manages tone, messages, roles, expectations, humor, and presentation while explicitly describing the burden of continuing to be "Chitose Saku" as others imagine him `[part0017:p087]`.

### F03 — the authored identity is both authentic and constraining

**Class:** INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high within V01

The persona expresses values Saku genuinely endorses, but Asuka's over-regulated-park metaphor and Saku's difficulty showing weakness indicate that the same role narrows his available modes of being.

### F04 — Saku's rhetoric of absolute self-evaluation contains an unresolved contradiction

**Class:** INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high

He says his own pride matters more than others' ranking, yet his self-concept has incorporated the expectation that he remain the dependable Chitose Saku. This is not resolved in V01.

### F05 — social competence is treated as partly learnable practice

**Class:** FACT + INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high

Kenta improves through repeated action, grooming, exercise, conversation, broader reading, and social exposure. Saku explicitly grounds communication in wanting to know and be known rather than in tricks alone.

### F06 — V01 does not establish strong meritocratic universality

**Class:** OPEN / negative boundary
**Confidence:** high

Kenta receives exceptional mentoring, status sponsorship, and protection. V01 refutes Kenta's deterministic hopelessness in this case; it does not prove that effort alone overcomes all unequal starting conditions.

### F07 — Kenta's core development is recovery of authorship, not acquisition of caste rank

**Class:** INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high

His original desire to make Miki regret rejecting him fades. By Chapter 5 he acts to vindicate the direction he chose and the work he invested. His otaku interests are not purged.

### F08 — Saku's helping style has a paternalistic failure mode

**Class:** FACT + INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high

Haru demonstrates that Saku can erase Kenta's agency by taking total responsibility; Kenta demonstrates that Saku can universalize his own preference for participation. Saku accepts both corrections.

### F09 — Saku's protection is relational and partial

**Class:** FACT + INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high

He accepts harsh retaliation against people attacking a chosen friend and later recognizes that he cannot carry everyone without dropping something already precious. V01 does not support a universal-sacrificial-saint reading.

### F10 — popularity and status are not treated as identical to virtue

**Class:** FACT + INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high

The text distinguishes status as byproduct from status as objective and contrasts Saku's group with status-aggressive peers. At the same time, Saku's group materially benefits from high status and can lend it to Kenta.

### F11 — the Ramune marble is a V01 metaphor for beautiful enclosure

**Class:** INFERENCE
**Confidence:** medium-high

Saku explicitly ties his aesthetic to the marble inside the sealed Ramune bottle. In conjunction with Asuka's park image and his unreachable-height language, V01 strongly supports reading the marble as a self-image: beauty and distance preserved by a form that also traps.

**Limit:** Saku does not explicitly state "I am the marble." Later material may revise the referent or broaden it.

### F12 — Saku changes in V01, but only slightly

**Class:** INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high

He accepts corrective dependence from Haru, apologizes when his own inclusion model harms Kenta, recognizes responsibility limits, and is described by Asuka as a park where at least a sparkler may now be possible. V01 does not support a claim that his core self-regulation is solved.

## Frozen open questions for V02

1. What actually happened around Saku's departure from baseball?
2. What concrete history produced the rule that he must continue being the expected Chitose Saku?
3. Can he accept vulnerability without first converting it into a game, joke, wager, or aesthetic role?
4. What happens when another person's autonomous choice frustrates Saku's desire to produce a "beautiful" outcome?
5. Can his absolute-evaluation ideal survive romantic nonreciprocity?
6. Will Asuka become less of a projection surface and more of a mutually knowable person?
7. Which friends can reliably correct Saku, and which primarily reinforce the hero role?
8. Does Kenta remain a friend with independent trajectory rather than becoming a permanent rehabilitation object?
9. Will later cases expose stronger limits to the proposition that social competence is learnable?
10. Does the Ramune-bottle image remain primarily Saku-centered, or broaden into a series-level theory of self-fashioning and enclosure?

## Frozen predictions

These are deliberately falsifiable **PREDICTIONS**, not claims about unread material.

- **P01:** baseball history will materially explain Saku's relationship to expectation, talent, or withdrawal from a prior identity.
- **P02:** later intimacy will pressure Saku's ability to remain the invulnerable director of the scene.
- **P03:** at least one later conflict will arise because Saku's aesthetic/helping code and another person's autonomy point toward different outcomes.
- **P04:** the friend group will not function as a single undifferentiated "popular caste"; individual members will continue to reveal incompatible social philosophies.
- **P05:** Asuka's "park" diagnosis will remain a useful test of Saku's development even if the metaphor itself is not repeated.
