## I. Governing movement: performance expands capacity and threatens answerability

V08 makes performance do two contrary kinds of work. The class play can safely externalize unresolved choice because Nazuna explicitly removes romance, friendship, and sympathy from Saku's best-actress decision. Yuzuki's `Nana` persona, by contrast, treats night as a stage whose curtain could erase the consequences of action.

The text refuses that alibi. Acting can rehearse a choice, release a protected capacity, and reveal a suppressed desire; it cannot suspend consent, erase injury, or substitute a strategically effective self for the person who must answer afterward.

> **Volume 08 strengthens reciprocal romantic recognition between Saku and Yuzuki while refusing to let performance manufacture either consent or a couple. Yuzuki's released athletic and erotic capacity is real, but capacity becomes authentic only when the acting person remains accountable to teammates, boundaries, and the self who must live after the curtain. Saku supplies a time-bounded promise to decide by next spring, not the decision itself.**

## II. The class play separates public selection from romantic choice

Class 2-5 constructs `白雪姫と暗雲姫と優柔不断な王子さま`. Yuko is Snow White, Yuzuki the dark-cloud princess/queen/witch, and Saku the indecisive prince. The ending remains unwritten and will be settled by ad-lib performance `[p-0011#p0241-p0487]`.

Nazuna requires Saku to choose the best actress strictly as acting, excluding love, friendship, and sympathy. This institutional rule is analytically decisive: a later stage result will not itself establish romantic selection without independent direct evidence.

Yuko and Saku mutually accept the assignment with the increased trust created after V06. Yuzuki treats the role as rehearsal for choosing and refusing in life. V08 ends before the festival, so neither the play nor its evaluative choice occurs here.

## III. Mirrors identify persons but expose substitution

The prologue gives Yuzuki's account of Saku as a man hiding from mirrors, ashamed that heroism might want anything in return. She wants to become suitable beside him and imagines giving him her moon if real love cannot stay beautiful `[p-0010#p0003-p0032]`.

The refrain `鏡よ鏡` associates Yuko with the lake, Asuka with wind, Yua with sky, `Nana` with the poison-apple witch, and Yuzuki with the broad moon. These are not rank assignments. Mirrors expose the danger that a relational function, color, performance, or other woman's tactic could be copied while the person and history are treated as interchangeable.

White/black, day/night, stage/audience, blue/red, and real/fake make identity a temporal and ethical problem: who acts, who must answer, and which self remains when performance ends.

## IV. Yuzuki and Momiji preserve intimacy inside declared rivalry

Yuzuki takes Momiji to a café, calls her a girlfriend for the day, and gives her a canelé memory that does not pass through Saku. Momiji says the sweet junior and aggressive challenger are both authentic `[p-0013#p0269-p0391]`.

Momiji then describes a maximal romantic method: disclose tears directly, exploit sympathy, create opportunity, use femininity, and refuse aid to rivals. Her candor helps Yuzuki see the protective restraint in her own identity. It does not make Momiji's method neutral or ethically authoritative.

Momiji's request that Yuzuki hate her already records an unresolved cost. She wants both recognized intimacy and the freedom to use rivalry instrumentally.

Their later night conversation makes being outside the circle Momiji's competitive advantage. Yuzuki recognizes her standing to challenge meeting order; both accept that rivalry begins `[p-0020#p0181-p0480]`. Neither friendship nor competition is fake, and neither cancels responsibility for method.

## V. `Nana` produces athletic power and team injury

Yuzuki decides that friendship, sympathy, compassion, and even the name Nanase Yuzuki may have to be discarded, calling the result `Nana` `[p-0013#p0497-p0506]`.

Against Kanazawa Oboro, Coach Misaki gives her control. Yuzuki turns herself into scorer, organizer, and decoy-maker, producing a 65–51 victory. Haru's focalization registers the cost: the team cannot keep up, and she feels converted into a pawn or feint `[p-0013#p0640-p0794]`.

Misaki calls Yuzuki's earlier limits self-imposed aesthetic rules and identifies a new beauty without restraint. She nevertheless retains Haru as Fujishi's ace and says Yuzuki is waiting only while continuing forward; Haru must find her weapon and catch up `[p-0013#p0927-p1105]`, `[p-0015#p0002-p0187]`.

Yuzuki later judges the performance strategically optimal but personally wrong because she used teammates as pieces `[p-0015#p0191-p0266]`. V08 therefore strengthens her capacity while rejecting a clean individual-versus-team triumph. Neither permanent rank nor sustainable team form is established.

## VI. Haru must pursue a moving partner rather than defend a fixed place

Haru's V07 crises converge. Momiji showed that baseball partnership was contingent; Yuzuki now shows that Haru's basketball primacy cannot be guaranteed by affection or prior role.

Misaki refuses to console Haru by reducing Yuzuki. Waiting means advancing toward a partner who also moves, not asking her to stop. Haru renews her promise to challenge rather than remain a waiting woman.

This is independent athletic development. Haru still makes no present romantic request to Saku, and neither education nor career logistics settle her basketball future.

Misaki's history with former teammate Tominaga supplies an intergenerational parallel of being left behind and continuing. The male senior who helped Misaki is not named and must not be identified by conjecture.

## VII. Yua and Asuka complete mutual accountability, not Momiji repair

Yua and Asuka exchange their V07 conflict accounts and independently say that they themselves were at fault `[p-0016#p0434-p0495]`.

Yua tells Asuka that the riverbank belongs to nobody and that Saku used it with Momiji while waiting for her. Asuka tells Yua she is not Saku's wife, has no jurisdiction over kitchen access, and was herself the person who invited Momiji.

They infer that Saku did not chase either woman because doing so would transfer blame to Momiji. This interpretation deepens their understanding of his burden-taking, but remains their inference rather than proof of every motive `[p-0018#p0007-p0069]`.

The conversation supplies direct accountability and reciprocal correction absent from V07. It repairs Yua and Asuka's relation to their own conduct and to each other; it does not show either woman apologizing directly to Momiji. Their planned market outing strengthens an independent friendship. Asuka gains no new Tokyo logistics `[p-0018#p0104-p0221]`.

## VIII. Yuko restarts love without denying refusal

With Saku's consent, Yuko tells Momiji about her summer confession, refusal, and return to ordinary contact `[p-0018#p0509-p0606]`.

She says one summer love ended and a new love began. For Yuko, holding hands does not mean refusing to let go; it means entrusting the whole failed love to whoever eventually becomes happy `[p-0020#p0002-p0035]`.

This is neither withdrawal nor retroactive refusal-denial. The refused attempt remains complete; Yuko deliberately begins another love under changed knowledge.

Yuko and Saku both describe the coming festival as probably their last in the group's unchanged arrangement. The blue thread must eventually be retied as red `[p-0018#p0225-p0396]`. Their reduced need for explanatory speech shows relational trust, but no new couple or promise forms.

Momiji cries at the account and says she too let summer pass. Her sorrow is not presented as pure sympathy, and the volume still does not provide the full earlier rescue chronology.

## IX. Saku accepts a choice deadline without yet choosing

Kura-sensei frames selection as a life-form question: beside whom, and as what self, does Saku want to remain? Memories are evidence for that answer rather than a numerical ranking `[p-0022#p0134-p0321]`.

Saku opens the rooftop because he needs time to face his heart. Kura-sensei initiates the crucial supportive exchange, so the long-running test for a clearly Saku-originated request to another person remains open.

Saku states that once he names love he will never overwrite it. He promises to decide by the time the next cherry blossoms bloom and asks for more time `[p-0030#p0474-p0543]`.

This is a major answer-responsibility transition: uncertainty now has a stated limit. It is not a present choice, an exclusive promise to any one woman, or a couple.

## X. Saku directly reciprocates romantic language toward Yuzuki below exclusivity

During their repair, Saku says Yuzuki is in his heart and that he fell for the `Nanase Yuzuki` who keeps trying to be Nanase Yuzuki `[p-0030#p0380-p0494]`.

The language is direct and romantic. It materially strengthens the Saku/Yuzuki relation beyond V07's unheard Yuzuki resolve and confirms that his attraction is to her effortful whole-person identity rather than the `Nana` role alone.

It does not identify her as the only person in his heart, rank her above the other four women named in V07, retract Yuko's refusal, or establish an exclusive couple. Direct reciprocity and singular selection remain distinct tests.

## XI. The night is a consent violation followed by refusal and repair

Yuzuki comes to Saku's apartment and takes over weekend cooking with Yua's knowledge and consent. Because Yua already received Yuzuki's katsudon recipe, the sequence confirms that care knowledge and access can circulate without erasing attributed history `[p-0024#p0115-p0156]`, `[p-0026#p0062-p0142]`.

Yuzuki then stages a sexual and romantic `Nana` night. She repeatedly escalates after Saku tells her to stop or says the current point is enough, and she deliberately treats his rapid heartbeat as permission `[p-0028#p0175-p0270]`.

Saku gives a hard refusal. He will not accept her body without the named relation and whole person, and says accepting her then would disgrace Nanase Yuzuki.

Yuzuki immediately recognizes the action as one-sided and nonconsensual, compares herself to the aggressor who harmed her, and accepts responsibility. Her self-condemnation becomes excessive, but remorse does not erase the conduct.

Saku distinguishes her from that aggressor through their accumulated history, refuses her claim that her body and heart are dirty, prevents flight, and embraces `Nanase Yuzuki` rather than `Nana`. His desire and admission that restraint nearly failed are facts about attraction, not retroactive consent.

The source therefore establishes **boundary violation + refusal + remorse + negotiated repair**. It does not establish consent to the prior acts, consummation, ethical purification, or an uncomplicated romantic victory. Later reassurance cannot travel backward in time and authorize what Saku had refused.

Yuzuki returns to her name, accepts the decision horizon, and asks to share the festival before naming. The relation is materially strengthened and romantically reciprocal while remaining unselected and uncoupled.

## XII. Momiji gains recognition among women but no Saku reciprocity

Momiji invites the whole group to her class's festival event and obtains Saku's promise to attend. She may be unable to attend their play. Saku provides measurements already taken by Yua, closing the private measuring opportunity Momiji imagined; she says she is late again.

Yuko's disclosure gives Momiji a history that the other claimant chooses to share rather than protect as proprietary. Momiji's tears and admission that she let summer pass add conflicted loss but do not establish exact chronology or a Saku-side romantic response.

The volume continues to support both sides of Momiji's V07 model: her junior affection and admiration are genuine, and her competitive instrumentality is genuine. Her candid bridge with Yuzuki improves mutual knowledge without showing direct purpose disclosure to Saku or direct repair with Asuka, Haru, or Yua.

## XIII. Women's relationships remain independent causal infrastructure

V08's central changes are not exhausted by Saku's romantic answer:

- Yuzuki and Momiji create a private memory, recognize each other's divided authenticity, and enter rivalry without eliminating intimacy.
- Yua and Asuka correct one another and plan ordinary time together.
- Yuko entrusts her most consequential history to Momiji and offers a theory of love after refusal.
- Yua and Yuzuki coordinate domestic care through consent and transmitted knowledge.
- Yuzuki's athletic release changes Haru's self-concept and team future independently of romance.

The network now sustains challenge, knowledge transfer, and accountability that no central male adjudication supplies.
