## 4. Retrospective components: what changes and what does not

### Comics-era stories — early roles become self-conscious relationships

Haru reads Yuzuki's pre-stalking unease and proposes Saku because the two resemble constructed heroes. Yuzuki imagines that her eventual return may be needed precisely when Saku can no longer remain a hero `[DES:p-0012#p0023-p0054]`. This is early evidence for their later corrective mirror relation, not advance proof of the V09 stage outcome.

Yua's V01 rooftop lunch supplies an early care contract: presence can matter without solving a problem, and she promises to correct Saku if he loses his own way `[DES:p-0014#p0034-p0063]`. The promise deepens their long-term ordinary reciprocity without naming romance at that date.

Yuzuki's three V02/V03-adjacent pieces preserve a progression rather than a single timeless state. Before the fake relationship she prepares for uncertainty while insisting on her own terms `[DES:p-0016#p0027-p0048]`; after the crisis she explicitly wants the false relation to become real but declines to cross the bedroom boundary without naming her rescued heart `[DES:p-0018#p0022-p0048]`; after Saku's birthday she internally calls him the man she has fallen for while receiving a moon-marked gift `[DES:p-0019#p0031-p0076]`. These are directional Yuzuki-side facts. None is a present mutual commitment.

Haru and Yuzuki's practice-game aftermath makes their partnership a promise to continue reciprocal contest through the top `[DES:p-0017#p0042-p0054]`. Romantic rivalry grows inside that independently primary athletic relation.

### V05–V06 stories — scarcity, refusal, and chosen repair

Asuka refuses Okuno directly because she already loves Saku, while preserving their friendship only through graduation `[DES:p-0022#p0027-p0058]`. This supplies explicit Asuka-side exclusivity at that historical moment, not evidence that Saku selected her.

Yuko's mother warns that other forms of specialness exist, but Yuko maintains that no replacement of Saku and Yua is likely `[DES:p-0024#p0056-p0065]`. Yuzuki later hides her private fireworks interval from Yuko and experiences the conflict between friendship and competitive desire `[DES:p-0026#p0028-p0058]`. At the beach, Yuzuki wishes happiness could be shared; Haru answers that the first place cannot be divided `[DES:p-0028#p0034-p0059]`. The scene is a scarcity diagnosis, not a harem settlement.

Immediately after Yuko's refusal, Yuzuki and Haru recognize that Yua acted while they remained bound by friendship, style, and hesitation `[DES:p-0031#p0027-p0058]`. Haru then recruits Atomu because she wants to help Saku through the athletic language she believes he needs `[DES:p-0033#p0037-p0069]`. Kazuki separately refuses Kaito's attempt to blame Saku for a contest Kaito never entered `[DES:p-0035#p0050-p0075]`. Care and accountability become networked rather than hero-owned.

Yuko's experience of refusing Kaito lets her recognize the pain and possible incompleteness of Saku's answer, and she resolves to state that her own love is not mere first sight `[DES:p-0036#p0041-p0061]`. Yua invites Asuka to the festival despite recognizing Asuka's exceptional access to Saku; she names the act as her own choice and still claims the desired adjacent position `[DES:p-0038#p0049-p0069]`. Neither woman transfers answer authority away from Saku.

### V06.5–V07 stories — lateral institutions and future imagination

Yuko can feel jealousy and still value that her beloved friends love the same person `[DES:p-0040#p0048-p0063]`. Nazuna supports both Yuko and Yuzuki, then challenges Yuzuki to release the capacity she restrains in basketball and romance `[DES:p-0042#p0031-p0061]`. These are norms of rivalry built by women, not a reward granted by Saku.

Asuka studies her failed interview, accepts correction, and receives vocational guidance from her father `[DES:p-0044#p0002-p0058]`. At AKUSHU she uses literature to help a child encounter a difficult alternative rather than a reassuring answer `[DES:p-0050#p0044-p0079]`. Her independent authorship develops through listening, mediation, and local institutions.

Yua's late-August domestic story establishes repeated meal practice and covert Valentine's preparation `[DES:p-0046#p0002-p0053]`. Haru turns Mai's technical advice into a new compact three-point form, explicitly letting excellent rivals intensify athletic and romantic aspiration `[DES:p-0048#p0055-p0083]`. Domestic and athletic competence remain self-authored domains, not proofs of romantic entitlement.

At the V07 camp, Yua and Yuko hope their friendship survives any answer `[DES:p-0053#p0056-p0062]`; Yuko tells Asuka that loving Saku creates friendship rather than outsider status `[DES:p-0055#p0040-p0055]`; and Yuzuki and Asuka exchange a deliberately asymmetrical assessment—Yuzuki may uniquely understand Saku, while Asuka may be the person he uniquely wants to understand `[DES:p-0057#p0089-p0104]`. That distinction is strong relational evidence but not a ranking supplied by Saku.

Haru imagines marriage, children, basketball, and shared domestic labor while learning to make food from Yua `[DES:p-0058#p0027-p0058]`. The scene is an internal future fantasy, not an enacted future. Her next-day catch with Momiji establishes fair direct contest as their repair grammar `[DES:p-0060#p0043-p0055]`.

### V08–V09 stories — bounded privileges, distributed performance, and parallel witness control

After Yua's V08 apology, Saku deliberately restores their ordinary routine and says he is uncomfortable having Momiji repeat the same body measurement `[DES:p-0062#p0004-p0058]`. This is evidence of a specific relational boundary and dyadic ease. It does not erase Yua's earlier consent violation or make domestic access exclusive.

The girls' band becomes a relation that can proceed without Saku physically present; Asuka treats the one-time ensemble as a formative memory and the others refuse to imagine the same band without her `[DES:p-0064#p0030-p0050]`. Saku's encouragement of Haru's basketball self-contest `[DES:p-0065#p0033-p0049]` and his pre-festival dance with Asuka `[DES:p-0067#p0042-p0055]` remain distinct forms of recognition.

Yuko's white-dress wish and Yuzuki's black-dress vow articulate different self-authored romantic postures `[DES:p-0069#p0022-p0044]`. Saku and Yuzuki's shared meal shows her entering domestic practice and him noticing the resemblance to his routine with Yua; Yuzuki asks to cook breakfast next time, and he does not reject the future invitation `[DES:p-0070#p0025-p0058]`. It strengthens domestic possibility below couple status.

The sports-festival stories distribute achievement across Haru/Momiji coordination and the whole class rather than making Saku the sole author `[DES:p-0072#p0022-p0055]`, `[DES:p-0073#p0085-p0163]`. The culture-festival pieces preserve female skill transmission between Yua and Asuka and show Tomoya dedicating his final song to both Saku and Yuzuki `[DES:p-0074#p0037-p0056]`, `[DES:p-0076#p0027-p0038]`. Tomoya's concert is a parallel witness to V09's already-counted reintegration, not a second redemptive event.

## 5. The newly written story: current transition after Volume 09

`長く短い祭りのあと` begins the day after the culture festival and before the Okinawa class trip `[DES:p-0078#p0001-p0022]`. It is the collection's only newly written fiction and the only component that advances the current diegetic horizon beyond the festival-day action.

### Group continuity changes form rather than returning to stasis

The ten-person gathering at Takoku includes the preexisting circle, Asuka, and Momiji. Momiji publicly says she is happy to have entered the group while refusing to treat the resulting circle as a stopping point `[DES:p-0078#p0092-p0104]`. The group remembers the festival as jointly made, and the women recognize that their band produced an independent relation which the men can observe but do not organize `[DES:p-0078#p0128-p0163]`.

Saku asks Kura-sensei for another rooftop key and names Momiji the third-generation roof cleaner; Momiji accepts the responsibility `[DES:p-0078#p0191-p0204]`. This enacts the rooftop admission promised at V09. It confirms social access and entrusted maintenance, not romantic acceptance.

### Cross-grade asymmetry becomes a resource

Asuka and Momiji share the ache of being in different grades from Saku's class. Momiji argues that her junior position enabled this specific encounter and creates forms of approach unavailable to peers. Asuka in turn recognizes that being a senior enabled the `Saku-nii / Asu-nee` relation and invites Momiji to continue talking on the roof `[DES:p-0078#p0241-p0288]`.

This is a new lateral alliance with independent retrieval value. It converts status asymmetry from only a gatekeeping liability into a bounded resource while leaving the impending graduation and separation intact.

### Yua/Momiji repair preserves a real boundary

Yua apologizes that she could not eat Momiji's pasta. She accepts Momiji's meal labor and begins sharing recipes and future food experiments `[DES:p-0078#p0364-p0404]`. When Momiji asks to use Saku's kitchen stool, however, Yua immediately declines `[DES:p-0078#p0405-p0410]`.

The repair is therefore neither exclusion nor surrender. Yua admits Momiji as a culinary peer while retaining one symbolically charged domestic boundary. This supports a model of reconciliation that preserves differentiated relations.

### Haru refuses both passivity and inflated causal credit

Haru recognizes that Momiji helped ignite Yuzuki's unlocked performance but rejects the suggestion that Momiji created Yuzuki's capacity. She trusts her partner would eventually have opened the lock herself and says she runs in order to remain beside her `[DES:p-0078#p0564-p0608]`.

Haru then places basketball and romance inside the same fair-contest grammar without collapsing them into one prize. She and Momiji reaffirm direct contest, while Haru calls Saku the sportsman she respects most `[DES:p-0078#p0609-p0624]`. The story advances Haru/Momiji and Haru/Yuzuki relations; it does not settle athletic rank, vocation, or romance.

### The endpoint remains plural and finite

The group walks toward Hachiban Ramen as summer, blue, and age seventeen approach their ends. Yuko and Yuzuki flank Saku and answer his fear of forgetting, while the narration anticipates changing colors through graduation `[DES:p-0078#p0626-p0650]`.

This is a post-festival ensemble endpoint. It neither restores the old unchanged blue form nor replaces it with a couple. The operative movement is continued relation under known change.

## 6. Paratext and visual audit

The afterword states that the collection was originally planned as a special-edition booklet, became too large for that format, and was expanded into a standalone book by adding the newly written story `[DES:p-0079#p0002-p0005]`. That testimony explains why republished and new fiction coexist; it does not make every component contemporaneous.

The author also discusses co-writing all anime scripts with series composer Toshihisa Arakawa, the pressure of screen-time cuts, and substantial original-author supervision `[DES:p-0079#p0006-p0010]`. These are 2025 production statements. They do not admit the anime as evidence into the Japanese-light-novel continuity.

The 50 images comprise cover/frontmatter art, source-group dividers, character/story interstitials, the long-story divider, colophon/profile matter, and advertising. All were inspected in spine order. Repeated silhouettes and decorative character cards are not additional scenes; promotional backmatter creates no story state.
