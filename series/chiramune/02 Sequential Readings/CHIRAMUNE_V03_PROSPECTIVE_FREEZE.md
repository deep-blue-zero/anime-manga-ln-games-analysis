## Frozen V03 findings

### F27 — Asuka's “Asuka-nee” identity is learned, chosen, and authentically hers

**Class:** FACT + INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high

Asuka modeled her high-school role partly on the childhood Saku who once enlarged her world. She practiced that form for years and used it to help him. Saku explicitly rejects the inference that imitation makes the present person false `[p-0032#p0291-p0412]`.

**Do not later rewrite as:** Asuka's entire high-school personality was merely a deceptive mask.

### F28 — Saku's hero persona protects an earlier prosocial impulse against relational entitlement

**Class:** FACT + INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high

Saku's formation history shows genuine childhood care and effort followed by entitlement, sabotage, and the erasure of labor under “natural gift” language. His polished arrogance, concealed weakness, romantic unreliability, and self-sacrifice operate as defenses around rather than replacements for that impulse `[p-0031#p0203-p0301]`.

### F29 — V03 resolves the origin of Saku's defensive persona but not its present ethical legitimacy

**Class:** INFERENCE + VALUE JUDGMENT
**Confidence:** high

The history explains why he manages expectation and treats himself as disposable. It does not validate privacy violation, bodily coercion, sexualized feints, or unilateral method selection.

### F30 — Asuka chooses Tokyo as one genuine good over another

**Class:** FACT + INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high

She investigates campus, labor, scholarships, professional networks, danger, and city life before reaffirming editing. Fukui remains loved and protective as well as constraining. Tokyo remains enabling and dangerous as well as liberating `[p-0029]`, `[p-0032#p0442-p0502]`.

**Do not later rewrite as:** Tokyo objectively represents freedom while Fukui represents only repression.

### F31 — authorship of the decision process is more important than possession of the correct forecast

**Class:** FACT + INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high

Kenta and Kuranosuke articulate that adults can know likely failures without gaining authority to skip a young person's deliberation. A future becomes owned through inquiry, decision, and consequence-bearing, not merely through receiving the best prediction `[p-0024#p0010-p0025]`, `[p-0022#p0197-p0250]`.

### F32 — Asuka rejects transfer from paternal to romantic guardianship

**Class:** FACT
**Confidence:** high

When Saku begins promising to support her after failure, Asuka interrupts and claims both risk and vocation herself `[p-0032#p0643-p0676]`. Love supplies recognition and aid, not ownership of her future.

### F33 — Saku can receive another person's reconstruction of him

**Class:** FACT + INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high

He narrates his formation history; Asuka reorganizes it without denying its facts, locates the continuing child hero, and gives imperfection present value. He accepts her consensual witness rather than controlling the entire interpretive result `[p-0031#p0323-p0419]`.

### F34 — Saku still respects final jurisdiction more reliably than procedural jurisdiction

**Class:** FACT + VALUE JUDGMENT
**Confidence:** high

He supports Asuka's departure and accepts her responsibility claim, yet reveals private information, uses bodily shocks, organizes unsafe secrecy, and plants an open-call phone. Successful outcomes do not establish consent to those methods.

### F35 — Saku and Asuka mutually name love without establishing exclusivity

**Class:** FACT
**Confidence:** high

They repeat love language after disentangling childhood imitation and present recognition `[p-0032#p0348-p0412]`. They do not enter an exclusive dating relationship, and the story preserves multiple directional affections.

### F36 — Yuzuki's named love becomes costly, nonpossessive action

**Class:** FACT + INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high

Yuzuki urges Saku to act on his bond with Asuka despite her own desire, and the bonus's crescent-lamp gift asks for remembrance without a reciprocal demand `[p-0022#p0260-p0297]`, `[p-0024#p0144-p0166]`, `[p-0037]`.

### F37 — Kenta's group membership is durably ordinary at the V03 boundary

**Class:** FACT
**Confidence:** high

He participates in leisure, leads through specialist knowledge, eats with Saku and Kazuki, and receives frank present-tense recognition without renewed client status `[p-0022#p0379-p0465]`.

### F38 — baseball is a reopened relational field, not a resolved institutional return

**Class:** FACT + INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high

Haru restores Saku's throwing and coaching knowledge to present play; former teammates reactivate betrayal and return pressure. Whether Saku re-enters competitive baseball remains open `[p-0015#p0123-p0225]`, `[p-0017#p0002-p0031]`.

### F39 — the Ramune metaphor is co-authored and interpretively plural

**Class:** FACT + INFERENCE
**Confidence:** high

Child Saku calls the marble lonely; child Asuka calls it beautiful, loved, floating, and moonlike. Present Saku's enclosure metaphor therefore contains another person's older recognition. The epilogue expands one absolute moon into many relational moons `[p-0031#p0307-p0322]`, `[p-0032#p0270-p0287]`, `[p-0035]`.

### F40 — independence remains vulnerable to becoming another compulsory performance

**Class:** INFERENCE
**Confidence:** medium-high

Asuka's vow to expend a lifetime of tears so she never relies on anyone gives strength to departure but risks making ordinary need evidence of failure `[p-0034#p0002-p0004]`. Later sources must test durability rather than assume either pathology or resolution.
