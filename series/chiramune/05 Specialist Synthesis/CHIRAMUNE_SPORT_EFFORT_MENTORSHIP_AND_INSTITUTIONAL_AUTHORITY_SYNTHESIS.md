# Sport, effort, mentorship, and institutional authority — synthesis through Volume 09.5

## Responsibility and promotion decision

This specialist owns the cross-volume question of how Chiramune connects sport to effort, bodily difference, injury, rivalry, teaching, team governance, and life-course choice. It does not replace the source-bound readings, the agency and self-authorship ledgers, or the Misaki, Kuranosuke, Nazuna, Haru, Yuzuki, and Saku character models.

The responsibility is promoted at Volume 09.5. Before that source, basketball and baseball remained recoverable through the ledgers and character monographs. Volume 09.5 adds a pre-series coach/teacher genealogy, a second athlete's withdrawal testimony, serious independent Atomu practice, and an explicit cross-generational roof/mentorship chain. The domain now has:

1. an independently recurring evidence base across both major sports and several volumes;
2. a distinct dependency path through bodies, techniques, teams, coaches, schools, peers, and adult vocation;
3. contradictions—effort and injury, pressure and jurisdiction, title and moving capacity—that top-level synthesis cannot responsibly compress;
4. durable retrieval value for future main, supplemental, and adaptation comparison.

## Governing synthesis

> **Chiramune treats sport as a laboratory for authored relation. Effort matters because repeated practice enlarges what a person can choose, not because effort guarantees victory or moral rank. Coaches, teammates, rivals, and mentors can reveal a limit or make a repertoire available, but they cannot own the athlete's uptake. Institutions enable comparison, safety, memory, and vocation while repeatedly risking coercion, hidden governance, status enclosure, and romanticized injury.**

## I. Early participation already separates inclusion from benefit

V01's soccer dispute supplies the earliest governing limit. Saku distributes participation because he assumes involvement is good; Kenta explains that a person who dreads sport may reasonably prefer unobtrusive distance. Once the recipient names the unwanted effect, inclusive intent can become coercive `[V01:part0030:p451-p481]`.

This principle later scales from informal play to full teams. Participation, training, visibility, and improvement are not universal goods detached from the person who bears their costs.

## II. V04 makes baseball and basketball test the same authority problem

### Baseball: unfinished embodiment below institutional return

Saku continues swinging after leaving Fujishi baseball. The bought wood bat and daily practice show that an institution can be refused while embodied attachment survives `[V04:p-0015#p0079-p0118]`.

Haru's pressure helps him name grief but becomes legitimate only when she refuses to decide whether he returns. Atomu supplies repeated practice; the old team supplies one bounded game. Saku accepts the event, thanks the sport and team, and refuses continued participation `[V04:p-0025#p0387-p0027#p0273]`, `[V04:p-0030#p0748-p0032#p0027]`.

The result is closure of one institutional summer, not abandonment of all baseball and not a career decision.

### Basketball: a shared goal does not authorize a hidden method

Haru and Yuzuki increase training and divide themselves into coercive and consoling roles because they believe teammates secretly want the same sacrifice. Coach Misaki distinguishes outcome, method, and uptake: it is the players' basketball, so recognition of a limit and acceptance of a method must become theirs `[V04:p-0025#p0088-p0100]`.

The team's recovery distributes capacity among defenders, rebounders, passers, shooters, and a movement Haru translates from baseball `[V04:p-0032#p0356-p0510]`. Interdependence, not one exceptional athlete, becomes the unit of credible effort.

### Injury is not evidence of ethical seriousness

Haru collapses after inadequate food and hydration; Saku and Atomu train through damaged hands; Saku conceals a severe wrist injury and refuses medically prudent restraint `[V04:p-0023#p0004-p0127]`, `[V04:p-0030#p0023-p0323]`.

The narrative grants these acts heroic visibility while also supplying dissent. The specialist therefore preserves a hard non-equivalence: pain may reveal commitment, but injury does not validate method, rank effort, or authorize adults and peers to demand repetition.

## III. V06 reopens sport as ordinary, selectable pleasure

Haru and Atomu bring Saku into full-strength pitching and hitting outside school structure. The session is competitive, social, and enjoyable rather than organized as penance. Atomu leaves an available route if Saku chooses serious return `[V06:p-0022#p0428-p0539]`.

This matters because sport survives after formal closure as a practice that can be selected again. It remains noninstitutional, without schedule, roster, educational route, or vocation.

The episode also redistributes support. Haru chooses baseball because it is a care practice she understands; Atomu contributes peer technique; Saku receives bodily release without one helper owning the outcome `[V06:p-0022#p0391-p0573]`, `[V06:p-0024#p0001-p0057]`.

## IV. V06.5 separates elite opportunity from compulsory purity

Haru's alumni practice exposes real technical gaps in passing, off-hand use, reading, and adaptability. Her recovery expands the court map rather than simply increasing intensity, and a secretly practiced three-point shot becomes one new option `[V065:p-0030#p0481-p0699]`, `[V065:p-0032#p0181-p0436]`.

Misaki requested the alumni's hostile posture. The intervention yields learning, but secrecy and humiliation remain method costs. Correct technical content does not purify a coercive delivery `[V065:p-0034#p0053-p0167]`.

The alumni and coach make university, corporate-league, and Olympic continuation imaginable. They do not decide it. Misaki also rejects the claim that serious basketball requires emotional purity: romance, friendship, conflict, regret, success, and failure may all enter the shot `[V065:p-0034#p0180-p0260]`.

Yuzuki separately questions whether excellence in the first field she found should determine her whole life `[V065:p-0011#p0213-p0248]`. A talent can be real without becoming a lifelong obligation.

## V. V07–V08 make rank a moving institutional office

With Misaki's authorization, Yuzuki asks Mai for a 30-point one-on-one and wins an implied 30–24. The contest converts unused capacity into an observable result `[V07:part0028#p0092-p0434]`, `[V07:part0028#p0557-p0657]`.

The result neither crowns her permanently nor erases Haru. It destabilizes a relational assumption that Haru's position as ace guarantees athletic primacy.

In V08, Yuzuki controls a 65–51 game against Kanazawa Oboro while teammates cannot match her pace and Haru feels used as a piece `[V08:p-0013#p0640-p0794]`. Victory is strong capacity evidence and weak legitimacy evidence.

Misaki retains Haru as ace while refusing to stop Yuzuki's movement. The office becomes a developmental responsibility: Haru must find a weapon and catch a partner who is still advancing `[V08:p-0013#p0927-p1105]`, `[V08:p-0015#p0002-p0187]`.

Team reconstruction remains open. Neither one-on-one victory, match victory, coach title, nor emotional reconciliation proves sustainable workload, consent, coordination, or safety.

## VI. V09 shifts athletic technique toward reciprocal adjustment

In the paired race, Saku first matches Haru's rhythm; after disruption, Haru changes to match his. Yuzuki learns the same technique during competition `[V09:p-0016#p0117-p0166]`, `[V09:p-0016#p0266-p0421]`.

The event is not basketball rank proof. It offers a portable relational technique: either partner can become the adjusting party. Haru's declaration that she loved Yuzuki before Saku keeps their athletic partnership primary inside romantic rivalry `[V09:p-0016#p0404-p0417]`.

The festival also distributes performance across athletics, dance, sword work, music, acting, and technical labor. Embodied competence can build public authorship without one private winner, but performance remains outside this specialist except where it alters the sport/effort model.

## VII. Supplemental testimony shows skill moving laterally

The *Days of Endless Summer* stories show athletic knowledge circulating among women and peers. Nazuna challenges Yuzuki's self-limitation; Mai gives Haru shooting advice; Haru learns domestic skill from Yua without treating it as an athletic opposite; Momiji and Haru establish fair contest `[DES:p-0042#p0031-p0061]`, `[DES:p-0048#p0055-p0083]`, `[DES:p-0058#p0027-p0058]`, `[DES:p-0078#p0564-p0624]`.

These routes matter because no coach monopolizes development. Technical and ethical correction travel through rivals, friends, family, alumni, and juniors. Lateral guidance still requires consent and can carry interested motives.

## VIII. Volume 09.5 supplies the adult genealogy

### Misaki learns repertoire revision before she teaches it

Teenage Misaki treats stalled height and Tominaga's growth as a transparent ceiling. The material difference is real, but she has let one successful approach erase the ball handling, passing, shooting, footwork, speed, and defensive reading formed earlier `[V095:p-0012#p0271-p0349]`.

Kuranosuke's analogy and reading of Tominaga's trust make that repertoire legible; Misaki owns the uptake and recombines it in play. She reclassifies her 170-centimeter body as particularly compatible with a varied game `[V095:p-0022#p0001-p0125]`, `[V095:p-0022#p0172-p0419]`.

Her adolescent declaration that she will teach and coach establishes a durable vocation. She wants to transmit not only success but pain, failure, and regret `[V095:p-0018#p0083-p0107]`.

### Kuranosuke learns mentorship from bounded efficacy

Nishino-sensei rejects Kuranosuke's use of `employment` as anticipatory surrender while respecting skilled trades. At Akiyoshi he models an ordinary Fukui life positively chosen `[V095:p-0012#p0184-p0246]`, `[V095:p-0020#p0123-p0155]`.

Kuranosuke then sees that a well-timed interpretation may shorten another person's suffering without creating her capacity. Misaki's recovery makes teaching imaginable, and he asks how to enter the profession `[V095:p-0024#p0043-p0067]`.

The sequence is reciprocal: a teacher guides Kuranosuke; Kuranosuke helps Misaki retrieve sport; Misaki helps Kuranosuke imagine teaching. No role stays permanently upstream.

### Nazuna represents the athlete who stops

Nazuna's middle-school loss to Yuzuki and observation of the Yuzuki–Aomi–Todo chain expose a field where greater talent and greater work can coincide `[V095:p-0026#p0058-p0206]`, `[V095:p-0026#p0208-p0224]`.

She stops. The source neither condemns nor idealizes the decision. Her present regret shows that self-authored cessation can remain unfinished even when it was rational under the person's perceived costs.

By asking Atomu to teach a throw and catch, she briefly enters the embodied practice behind the people she evaluates. She chooses to remain a demanding witness rather than resume competitive sport `[V095:p-0030#p0017-p0066]`.

### Atomu strengthens practice without closing a life path

Atomu now works alone through a large supply of hardballs, repeats enough pitches to exhaust himself, and adds a sweeper because one fastball and one signature pitch are insufficient. His stated reason is to remain close enough to challenge Saku `[V095:p-0028#p0015-p0049]`, `[V095:p-0030#p0001-p0016]`.

This is durable-looking practice evidence: equipment, volume, technique acquisition, and repeated setting are present. Institutional affiliation, education, health plan, and vocation are not. The correct state is **serious independent practice, path open**.

## IX. Intergenerational mentorship is circular, not a ladder

The roof makes the circular model explicit. Nishino-sensei assigns teenage Kuranosuke to clean it; adult Kuranosuke begins a Fujishi line with Nishino's daughter Asuka; Saku inherits access; Momiji becomes third-generation cleaner `[V095:p-0032#p0066-p0085]`, `[DES:p-0078#p0191-p0204]`.

Each transfer joins permission to maintenance. The chain is neither a bloodline nor a sequence of romantic favorites. It is an informal institution in which a protected space survives because responsibility moves.

Mentorship across the corpus follows the same pattern:

- a mentor can make a repertoire visible;
- a rival can supply the pressure or evidence;
- a recipient decides whether and how to use it;
- the recipient may later become a mentor without reproducing the exact method;
- guidance can return to the prior generation as proof that its ordinary life mattered.

## X. Effort is capacity, not desert

The series repeatedly values practice: Saku's swings, Atomu's pitching, Haru's conditioning, Yuzuki's technical repertoire, Misaki's rebuilt game, and team members' role-specific work. Repetition creates available actions under pressure.

It does not establish that:

- the hardest worker must win;
- talent differences disappear;
- injury proves seriousness;
- a winner has governed teammates well;
- a coach's correct prediction authorizes any method;
- a skill must become a career;
- quitting invalidates prior effort.

The relevant outcome is enlarged authorship under real constraint, not meritocratic reward.

## XI. Sport and romance are mutually informative but not interchangeable

Misaki experiences basketball as first love and integrates attraction into her restored style. Haru names basketball and Saku as two loves. Yuzuki's athletic release and romantic bid share a problem of capacity without limits. Nazuna uses the Yuzuki/Aomi relation to imagine the road she abandoned.

These parallels are interpretive structures, not outcome shortcuts. Winning a game does not win a person; partnership does not establish romantic reciprocity; a coach's title does not grant private jurisdiction; carrying love into performance does not make injury, coercion, or consent irrelevant.

## XII. Character dependencies

### Misaki

Primary adult node for repertoire revision, player jurisdiction, moving rank, and sport-to-vocation continuity. See her monograph for the individual model.

### Kuranosuke

Primary node for bounded interpretive help, chosen teaching, and cross-generational infrastructure. He is not a technical coach and does not own Misaki's recovery.

### Haru

Tests singular athletic devotion, coercive leadership, bodily risk, expanded technique, rival partnership, and possible elite continuation. Her current educational/vocational path remains open.

### Yuzuki

Tests restrained versus released capacity, repertoire depth, team legitimacy, consent, and whether a first field must become a life. Her current basketball path remains open.

### Saku

Tests institutional closure versus embodied continuity in baseball. Recreational practice persists; no durable team, education, or vocation is selected.

### Atomu

Provides peer technique, competitive truth, and now serious independent practice. He has not yet chosen institutional return.

### Nazuna

Supplies the stopping athlete's account and converts regret into demanding witness. Her own sustained return remains absent.

## XIII. Institutional authority and its limits

Institutions are not uniformly oppressive. Schools provide coaches, teams, roofs, events, alumni networks, comparison, adult memory, and possible professions. Ashiba, Fujishi, Kanazawa opposition, school festivals, and local businesses make practice materially possible.

Institutional authority becomes legitimate when it:

1. makes options, resources, and consequences visible;
2. protects safety and access;
3. keeps roles revisable;
4. distinguishes advice from decision;
5. lets participants contest methods;
6. preserves credit and responsibility across a team.

It fails when outcome confidence substitutes for consent, hidden governors define others' seriousness, injury becomes proof, or status titles freeze moving capacity.

## XIV. Current hard limits

- Saku has not chosen institutional, educational, or vocational baseball.
- Atomu's serious private practice is not an institutional return or career choice.
- Haru remains Fujishi's ace, but the office does not prove permanent primacy or sustainable governance.
- Yuzuki's wins do not establish permanent rank, team repair, or lifelong vocation.
- Nazuna's cessation does not prove inability, and regret does not prove a duty to resume.
- Misaki's restored game does not erase the material advantage of height.
- Kuranosuke helps her recovery but does not create her repertoire.
- Alumni hostility produces learning without becoming ethically costless.
- Athletic partnership, coaching, and embodied support do not establish romantic selection.
- No current youth life-course route receives enough education, schedule, health, finance, roster, or employment logistics to count as settled.

## XV. Current specialist model

> **Through Volume 09.5, sport is Chiramune's most sustained material account of revisable capacity. Bodies and institutions impose real limits; repeated practice opens more responses; teams make individual skill meaningful; rivals and mentors reveal what a person cannot see alone. None of those relations transfers ownership of the decision. The adult lineage from Nishino through Kuranosuke and Misaki to current students proves that an ordinary vocation can grow from bounded intervention, while Nazuna and Atomu preserve two unresolved alternatives: stopping with regret and practicing seriously without institution. Effort earns neither victory nor moral title. It earns a larger, still answerable field of action.**
