# Chiramune analytical method

## Purpose

This method governs source-grounded analysis of Hiromu's *Chitose Is in the Ramune Bottle*. It is built for a first-person, socially dense light-novel series where characterization can be distributed across narration, dialogue, reputation, peer-group behavior, self-presentation, private vulnerability, romantic interaction, ordinary routine, and later retrospective information.

The method standardizes analytical responsibilities and evidentiary discipline. It does not predetermine what the series means.

## 1. Japanese source first

The acquired Japanese light novels are the current semantic anchor. Preserve the wording needed to settle a claim, but do not reproduce source text wholesale in Git. Record enough locator information to return to the exact volume and EPUB reading position/resource.

Official or source-supported name readings outrank generic character-by-character transliteration. Shared kanji/hanzi code points do not license Mandarin readings inside Japanese names or prose.

## 2. Prospective reading and freeze discipline

Read the main numbered series in order.

For each numbered volume:

1. begin from the frozen prior-volume analytical state;
2. record bounded expectations or open questions before reading the new volume;
3. perform the deep reading without using later unread material;
4. separate direct source findings from inference;
5. audit which prior expectations were preserved, strengthened, revised, rejected, or left open;
6. freeze that volume's reading before advancing.

Later information may revise the current model, but it must not erase what the earlier source boundary actually supported.

Supplemental stories are not automatically inserted into an earlier prospective pass simply because they occur earlier diegetically. Classify their publication and diegetic placement first, then integrate them without contaminating the historical prospective record.

## 3. Focalization is evidence, not omniscience

First-person narration provides privileged evidence about the narrator's perceptions, values, rhetoric, and self-conception. It does not automatically establish objective truth about other characters or the social world.

Track distinctions among:

- what the narrator observes;
- what the narrator claims about himself;
- what he claims about others;
- what dialogue and behavior independently support;
- what other characters contest;
- what the narrative later recontextualizes.

Narrative confidence, irony, exaggeration, aesthetic framing, and self-mythologizing are themselves analyzable phenomena rather than automatic reasons to distrust the narrator.

## 4. Do not collapse social performance into falseness

Use precise categories when the text supports them. Distinguish, for example:

- deliberate deception;
- ordinary politeness or norm competence;
- impression management;
- aspirational self-fashioning;
- protective concealment;
- masking or suppression;
- role performance;
- playful theatricality;
- manipulation;
- stable learned social skill.

The analytical question is what a performance does, what it costs, how voluntary it is, and how it relates to the person's values—not whether any performed behavior is therefore "fake."

## 5. Character state before trait generalization

A behavior may vary with:

- developmental period;
- relationship stage;
- public versus private setting;
- peer-group versus one-to-one interaction;
- romantic stakes;
- embarrassment, conflict, competition, grief, or aspiration;
- perceived responsibility for another person.

Before converting a scene into a durable trait, ask what state and recipient the behavior belongs to.

## 6. Ordinary life is analytical evidence

Food, exercise, clothing, study, hobbies, humor, boredom, texting, shopping, commuting, school routine, family interaction, leisure, embarrassment, celebrations, and preferred forms of spending time together can reveal stable values and social behavior more reliably than crisis scenes alone.

Record the source fact first, then the implication, then the extrapolation limit.

## 7. Relationship claims are directional and staged

Do not reduce a relationship to a ship label or endpoint. Track:

- what each party knows about the other;
- how they address and describe one another;
- bids for closeness and acts of refusal;
- trust and disclosure;
- dependence and reciprocity;
- jealousy, competition, status, and third-party mediation;
- asymmetries in emotional knowledge or social leverage;
- changes across volumes.

A character's affection does not by itself establish reciprocity, compatibility, or ethical health.

## 8. Separate epistemic classes

Use these labels in analytical prose when material:

- **FACT** — directly established by the source within the declared boundary.
- **INFERENCE** — best explanation of multiple source facts, but not explicitly stated.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — plausible model requiring later evidence.
- **PREDICTION** — prospective expectation about unread material.
- **VALUE JUDGMENT** — normative assessment rather than descriptive fact.
- **OPEN** — evidence is genuinely insufficient or conflicting.

Do not silently upgrade one class into another.

## 9. Counterevidence and rival readings

Every major character or thematic claim should survive an attempt to disprove it. Record contradictory scenes, scope limits, and a serious rival reading where one exists.

A contradiction should first prompt questions about state, recipient, motivation, or model inadequacy. Do not erase inconvenient evidence merely to preserve a clean thesis.

## 10. Source hierarchy

Within the current project boundary, prefer:

1. mainline Japanese light-novel text at the relevant chronological/publication stage;
2. same-edition or separately acquired official supplements once their relationship to the mainline is classified;
3. later official side-story collections and half-volumes, integrated with publication/diegetic provenance intact;
4. later anime material as an adaptation, audiovisual, and performance witness rather than an automatic replacement for prose authority;
5. creator interviews, official production material, and external reception research for questions they actually address;
6. fan summaries, wikis, and social-media discussion only as discovery/context surfaces, never as substitutes for available primary text.

## 11. Comparative analysis comes after reconstruction

Other project titles can sharpen a question after Chiramune has been reconstructed on its own terms. Comparison with Hachiman Hikigaya, Sakuta Azusagawa, Ayanokoji Kiyotaka, or other socially perceptive protagonists must not force Saku Chitose into their preexisting categories.

Comparative claims should identify both genuine structural overlap and the point where the analogy breaks.

## 12. Ethical/framework scoring is downstream

PACTRIH or other project-wide comparative frameworks may be applied only after enough source-grounded evidence exists for the relevant character and state. Scores are ordinal interpretive estimates, not a morality total, and should preserve uncertainty where the text does not support precision.

## 13. Artifact proportionality

Create a standalone artifact only when it has a clear responsibility that benefits from independent retrieval or revision. Do not create empty ledgers, monographs, or synthesis folders merely because another series has them.

The canonical current-state map must always tell a future session which artifacts actually exist, which are authoritative, and what operation comes next.
