# Nozomi Momiji — active-provisional character monograph through Volume 07

## Responsibility and promotion threshold

This monograph reconstructs Nozomi Momiji only from safely admitted Japanese light-novel evidence through Volume 07. V07 supplies sustained action across school, sport, romantic pursuit, group entry, conflict, direct self-account, and the postposed prologue; it also supplies substantial counterevidence about disclosure and harm. That combination crosses the independent-model threshold. Promotion is not a claim that prominence makes her central forever or that later sources cannot substantially revise this first model.

The governing V07 reading is `../../02 Sequential Readings/CHIRAMUNE_V07_DEEP_READING.md`. V08 and all later sources remain outside this boundary.

## 1. Entry begins with genuine group admiration

Momiji is a first-year class representative and an Inter-High-experienced 100-metre sprinter. She says she joins the blue cheer team because she admires the conspicuous second-years as a group, and first-year demand rises once their participation becomes known `[V07:part0009#p0073-p0093]`, `[V07:part0009#p0841-p0904]`.

Her admiration is not merely a cover story invented after exposure. She wants proximity to the group's brightness and later rejects ordinary membership because the distinct relation she values is being *their junior* `[V07:part0019#p0573-p0604]`.

## 2. Direct requests are her principal agency grammar

Momiji asks for given-name address, Saku's dance partnership, responsibility for his costume, access to Asuka's practice place, catch with Haru, and cooking with Yua. In each case she makes a bounded request and receives local permission.

This distinguishes her from the older women's mutual restraint. She does not accept their informal moratorium as a rule governing her desire.

## 3. Juniorhood is both authentic relation and strategic access

Saku treats partnership with Momiji as safe because he understands her as an innocent junior outside the romantic contest `[V07:part0011#p0022-p0039]`. Momiji later confirms that this asymmetry matters: a declared sixth romantic claimant would likely be excluded, while junior affection is welcomed `[V07:part0026#p0286-p0316]`.

Her performance is therefore neither pure mask nor transparent essence. She genuinely wants the junior relation and deliberately selects it because it grants access under materially different assumptions.

## 4. Love and competition predate the confrontation

Momiji says she fell in love with Saku at first sight before joining cheer squad. She evaluates her beauty against Yuzuki, domestic competence against Yua, speed and athleticism against Haru, and listening ability against Asuka `[V07:part0026#p0238-p0279]`, `[V07:part0026#p0551-p0576]`.

These comparisons show competitive self-confidence and a wish to neutralize meeting-order advantage. They do not prove that histories, competencies, or relationships are interchangeable.

## 5. Her diagnosis of the group is incisive and interested

Momiji calls the five women's courtesy beautiful and argues that holding one another back also preserves stasis `[V07:part0026#p0349-p0458]`. The diagnosis is substantially corroborated by the older women's own private accounts of what delay protects.

It is also interested. Her claim that rescue is the only concrete Yuzuki/Saku bond attacks Yuzuki's existing insecurity and understates later shared practice. Accuracy about the moratorium does not make every competitive interpretation neutral.

## 6. Concrete permission does not exhaust purpose disclosure

Momiji reasonably insists that she asked before dancing, practicing, catching, cooking, and entering spaces. She did not fabricate consent or force physical access `[V07:part0026#p0320-p0343]`.

She also withheld the romantic and destabilizing project that caused Saku and the others to evaluate those requests as safe junior acts. The ethical issue is not that she has no standing to pursue Saku. It is that permission to each act was obtained under deliberately incomplete information about the pattern and purpose.

## 7. She regrets tears while accepting avoidable hurt

Momiji says her admiration, affection, and tears are genuine and that she did not want Asuka, Haru, or Yua to cry. She nevertheless accepts the possibility of hurting Saku and prefers hatred to irrelevance `[V07:part0026#p0349-p0389]`, `[V07:part0026#p0470-p0535]`.

`傷つける覚悟` is evidence of consequence awareness, not a moral permission slip. Willingness to be blamed can coexist with insufficient effort to reduce foreseeable harm.

## 8. She is also the recipient of disproportionate gatekeeping

Asuka shouts at her on the riverbank, Haru pulls the glove hard enough to hurt her hand, and Yua shouts when she approaches the chair. Momiji's strategy helps create each collision, but the older women retain responsibility for their responses `[V07:part0020#p0162-p0418]`, `[V07:part0022#p0225-p0467]`, `[V07:part0024#p0494-p0590]`.

Haru later apologizes and deliberately plays catch with her. Asuka joins mutual apology. Yua sends messages and makes her lunch, while a complete direct repair conversation remains unshown. Momiji cannot be reduced to either invader or victim.

## 9. Sprinting supplies a self-authorship form

The final component is literally placed after Chapter 4 as `プロローグ　ヒーロー見参`. Starting blocks, a gunshot, lost spring, and a run toward the moon turn sprinting into her form of refusing suspended time `[V07:part0030#p0001-p0027]`.

An alluded casual kindness once stopped cold tears, but the scene does not provide a complete rescue chronology. Its present claim is that Momiji will stop waiting for another hero and become her own `[V07:part0030#p0029-p0033]`.

Athletic identity is therefore not decorative comparison with Haru. Sprint form organizes Momiji's temporality: one starting signal, no wish to remain held in spring, and movement whose direction she authors.

## 10. Relationship map at the V07 boundary

- **Saku:** he welcomes and trusts her as a junior, dances and practices with her, and does not yet classify her among the five women whose imagined futures cause romantic pain. Momiji loves him; reciprocity is unestablished.
- **Yuzuki:** strategic opponents who recognize one another's intelligence. Yuzuki uses a lie to test her; Momiji exposes Yuzuki's reserve and stasis. Neither is a neutral judge.
- **Asuka:** Momiji requests entry into the riverbank and receives a panic outburst followed by mutual apology; full boundary repair is unshown.
- **Haru:** comparable baseball access triggers harm, followed by Haru's direct apology and sustained inclusive catch.
- **Yua:** comparable domestic competence and the chair trigger crisis; behavioral repair begins, while complete direct repair is unshown.
- **Yuko:** Yuko praises the dance, names her own desire, and asks Momiji to watch Saku. This is the least gatekeeping initial response.
- **the five-woman group:** Momiji admires it but refuses circle membership so she can preserve junior identity and pursue change without accepting its moratorium.

## 11. Rival readings and hard limits

- **“Momiji is an innocent junior who accidentally causes jealousy.”** Rejected. Her affection and admiration are genuine, but the romantic entry pattern is deliberate.
- **“Momiji is a coercive villain.”** Overstated. She requests and receives concrete permission; the defect is strategically incomplete purpose disclosure and accepted harm, not fabricated consent.
- **“Because no one owns Saku, Momiji's method is fully justified.”** Rejected. Lack of ownership protects her standing to pursue; it does not remove process responsibility.
- **“Because three women hurt her, Momiji has no responsibility.”** Rejected. Recipient harm and initiating strategy must be attributed separately.
- **“Her comparisons prove she can replace the five.”** Unsupported. Competence similarity does not reproduce history, meaning, or relation.
- **“The final prologue proves the narrative endorses all her conduct.”** Rejected. Self-authorship and ethical assessment are separate axes.
- **“Saku now has six equal romantic interests.”** Unsupported. Momiji is a sixth claimant from her own perspective; Saku's direct enumeration contains five.
- **“The hero allusion supplies a complete love-at-first-sight rescue event.”** Unsupported. The source leaves the past encounter fragmentary.

## 12. Post-V07 current model

> **Nozomi Momiji is a high-agency junior who converts sprint temporality into a refusal of romantic and group stasis. Her admiration, junior relation, tears, competitive desire, and love can all be genuine at once. She also deliberately uses the safety of juniorhood to obtain access under incomplete information and accepts foreseeable hurt as the price of becoming relevant. She has standing to pursue Saku and no entitlement to ethical immunity. Her most promising trajectory is not simply whether she “wins,” but whether self-authored movement learns transparent process, proportionate responsibility, and repair without being absorbed into the moratorium she opposes. Saku does not yet reciprocally name her as romantic, and no couple forms.**

## V08 watchlist

- whether Momiji tells Saku directly that her pursuit preceded cheer squad and how he responds with full information;
- whether she revises her reliance on innocent-junior framing once that strategy is exposed;
- whether she apologizes, repairs, or changes conduct toward Asuka, Haru, Yua, and Yuzuki without surrendering autonomous pursuit;
- whether the group offers a relation other than exclusion or assimilation into stasis;
- whether sprinting acquires continuing educational, institutional, or vocational stakes;
- whether the fragmented hero encounter gains direct chronology without retrospective invention;
- direct reciprocal evidence only before any Saku/Momiji romance or couple claim.
