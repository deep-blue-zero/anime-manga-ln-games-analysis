# Yuzuki Nanase — V01–V03 character monograph

## Scope

Volume 02 supplies enough direct Yuzuki focalization, ordinary-life evidence, retrospective self-account, relationship contrast, crisis behavior, and counterevidence to justify a dedicated monograph. Volume 03 adds a bounded behavioral test of named love. This remains a V03-bounded model, not a final series interpretation.

# 1. Compressed model

Yuzuki Nanase is a highly self-authored adolescent whose competence began not as compensation for a single hidden wound but as a practical solution to the social costs of being conspicuously beautiful, capable, and visible.

She learned early that attention creates claims. Boys who gladly granted requests began seeking return; girls who clustered around her could also exclude and envy her. Her answer was not withdrawal. She became better at reading what people wanted, calibrated which version of herself each relationship could bear, and worked hard enough that criticism would have less purchase.

Her governing V02 paradox is:

> **Yuzuki possesses an unusually sophisticated social self but initially doubts whether anything remains hers when social skill cannot control the situation.**

The Yanagishita incident matters because brute force ignores the shared game in which her intelligence, beauty, humor, and calibration work. The deepest fear is not merely pain or male strength. It is the discovery that when those tools fail, she might immediately reorganize herself around the coercer's demands rather than find an inner principle that says no.

Volume 02 is therefore a story of **jurisdictional acquisition**. She does not discover a hidden "real Yuzuki" beneath the mask. She constructs a more durable self capable of saying: fear can be real, force can be overwhelming, another person can hurt my body, and none of those facts authorizes them to define who I am, whom I love, or what I consent to.

# 2. Self-fashioning before trauma

Yuzuki explicitly rejects a trauma-origin model `[p-0032:002-013]`.

She describes early social adaptation as a sequence of small choices: ask girls rather than boys for favors, defend girls in some situations, alter tone and behavior depending on the person, and invest heavily in competence so that others would have fewer grounds for resentment.

This is not proof of unconstrained freedom. Her choices are responses to gendered and status-based pressures. But within those pressures she experiences herself as an author and takes pride in the craft.

**FACT:** the persona predates Yanagishita.

**INFERENCE:** performance is therefore constitutive rather than merely defensive. Asking "what is Yuzuki like without the mask?" is partly malformed; there are multiple authored Yuzuki states, not one hidden essence waiting underneath.

# 3. The mask is selective interface

Yuzuki draws explicit lines around disclosure. She does not show every aspect of herself to every person, and she considers that normal rather than hypocritical.

Her attraction to Saku begins partly because he can detect the interface. She says she cannot suddenly show a bare face to someone who does not even know she is wearing a mask. This makes recognition a prerequisite for vulnerability.

The important distinction is therefore:

- **mask as authored interface:** Yuzuki chooses a presentation while retaining revision rights;
- **mask as imposed role:** Tomoya decides what "the real Yuzuki" must be and treats contradiction as evidence she has been corrupted by Saku.

Volume 02 strongly condemns the second, not the first.

# 4. Optimization as governing style

Yuzuki tends to solve life through calibration:

- read the field;
- minimize unnecessary social cost;
- wait for high-probability openings;
- preserve elegance and control;
- avoid spending force where precision works.

Basketball expresses the same structure. She defends carefully and shoots when the line is clean. The arcade air-hockey scene literalizes the contrast with Haru: Yuzuki aims for efficient, precise conversion; Haru launches repeated direct attacks and accepts waste.

This is why Yuzuki is fascinated by Haru and later Saku. They embody movement that is not reducible to optimization.

# 5. Haru as first developmental attractor

Yuzuki chose Fujishi after losing a middle-school basketball semifinal to Haru's team `[p-0032:024-035]`.

Technically Yuzuki's team was better. Haru's team won because her repeated forward motion changed the emotional and tactical field. Yuzuki remembers the moment Haru kept attacking even when victory looked unavailable.

Haru therefore matters before romance. She is the first person Yuzuki remembers as more dazzling than herself because she possesses something Yuzuki's calibrated excellence cannot manufacture: commitment that survives low probability.

Their high-school friendship preserves that dialectic. Haru can accuse Yuzuki of standing one step outside the contest; Yuzuki can criticize Haru's lack of precision. Neither simply replaces the other.

# 6. Yanagishita: failure of social tools

The middle-school coercion is a domain failure.

Yuzuki enters the encounter believing she can talk her way out as usual. Yanagishita responds by grabbing, pinning, and striking her. She describes her mind going blank and discovering that brute force can make her whole repertoire irrelevant `[p-0031:045-061]`.

Her later focalization identifies the deeper wound: she could not locate a principle that remained active once optimization failed. She imagines that if the encounter had gone further, she might have shifted immediately into "how to live well while being this man's girlfriend" rather than continue refusing `[p-0032:015-023]`.

This is not weakness in the ordinary sense. It is a precise failure mode of an identity built around successful adaptation.

# 7. Saku as mistaken mirror

Yuzuki first sees Saku as her one true social double: attractive, capable, controlled, popular, and occasionally bored in a way she interprets as the same thin darkness she carries.

The similarity is real at the tool level. The conclusion of sameness is not.

By Chapter 4 she understands that Saku repeatedly chooses the inefficient route when value is at stake. He gets dirty finding her shoes, accepts relational costs, runs into walls he could evade, and sometimes substitutes commitment for elegance.

Yuzuki's attraction increases as the mirror breaks.

That point is central to avoiding a shallow "perfect boy + perfect girl" reading. Their intimacy becomes stronger when perfection ceases to be symmetrical.

# 8. Specialness and the wound of non-uniqueness

Yuzuki initially takes pride in being one of the only people who can perceive Saku's structure. Asuka destabilizes this identity claim.

Seeing Asuka read Saku from an even deeper register produces jealousy-like pain before Yuzuki has named romantic love. Her own account frames this as a small defeat: she thought understanding a special person uniquely made her special.

This reveals that Yuzuki's self-authorship remains relationally vulnerable. She can move outside ordinary comparison socially, yet still build private rank around exclusive access to another person.

Saku recognizes the same pattern in himself, which deepens their mutuality.

# 9. Crisis does not erase fear; it adds action

Yuzuki's V02 growth should not be described as "she stops being afraid of violence."

In the final Yanagishita encounter she is still frightened. The change is procedural and jurisdictional:

- survey exits;
- assess public visibility;
- keep thinking;
- state nonconsent;
- distinguish bodily harm from ownership;
- plan to report;
- when Saku is endangered, identify escape/help as the action available to her.

Her statement that even rape would not make her Yanagishita's property is deliberately extreme `[p-0032:100-135]`. The novel locates selfhood in retained jurisdiction rather than physical invulnerability.

# 10. Saku's apartment intervention: gratitude does not erase coercion

Yuzuki's later account treats Saku's reenactment as transformative. She thanks him and says the most terrifying face in her memory has been replaced by Saku's deliberately frightening one, paired with his absurdly vulnerable reaction when she kicks him.

That is an important source fact.

It should not be converted into a claim that she prospectively consented to the method. She sought refuge and help; she did not know Saku would restrain and frighten her. Her terror during the scene is also a source fact.

A faithful Yuzuki model must preserve both:

- she experiences the result as empowering;
- the method exposed her to serious unchosen psychological risk.

Her gratitude is evidence about her interpretation, not a universal ethical warrant.

# 11. Apology to Nazu: self-authorship after ugly behavior

Under pressure Yuzuki falsely accuses Nazu using circumstantial evidence and almost says something crueler. Saku stops her and tells her plainly that she is in the wrong.

The next day Yuzuki apologizes publicly. She does not attempt to convert the apology into friendship, reputational advantage, or mutual absolution. When Nazu says she does not want to get along, Yuzuki says she does not either `[p-0031:349-372]`.

This is one of the strongest small scenes in the volume.

**INFERENCE:** a more durable Yuzuki is not one who never loses control. It is one who can incorporate an ugly action into self-authorship by naming it as hers and repairing what is repairable.

# 12. Love as chosen movement

Yuzuki's V02 romance is built around the prologue/epilogue distinction between projection and knowledge.

She begins by idealizing Saku as her mirror. She then sees:

- his foolishness;
- self-endangerment;
- frightening anger;
- domestic ordinariness;
- sexual interest;
- emotional evasiveness;
- other intimate relationships;
- difference from herself.

Her feeling strengthens through those discoveries. By the Tomoya confrontation she explicitly says she has named it.

This does not prove idealization has disappeared. She still calls Saku beautiful and heroic and may continue to romanticize his extremity. But she is no longer protecting an image from contradiction in Tomoya's manner.

**V02 endpoint:** love is a self-authored reason for Yuzuki to move toward Saku, not a claim that he already belongs to her.

# 13. Ordinary-life profile seeds

V02 supplies useful mundane evidence without yet requiring a separate preferences artifact:

- likes festival atmosphere and becomes visibly childlike about goldfish;
- previously kept festival goldfish and wants to keep the new pair;
- chooses vegetable salt ramen at Hachiban Ramen;
- is comfortable with spicy food;
- plays basketball at high level and ordinarily regulates herself for team optimization;
- uses carefully managed beauty/grooming routines but can also enjoy being seen in glasses and borrowed clothes;
- becomes jealous over indirect-intimacy details such as shared utensils, shampoo recommendations, and friend-given household items;
- can be sexually playful and direct when she feels safe;
- uses polished speech strategically but drops into more raw directness with trusted people and under emotional pressure.

These should be retained as ordinary-life evidence, not inflated into immutable traits.

# 14. Stable tendencies versus state effects

## More stable within V01–V02

- high social perception;
- deliberate presentation management;
- competitive pride;
- high effort / competence orientation;
- desire to remain agentic rather than pitied;
- preference for clean, controlled solutions;
- attraction to people who embody forms of commitment she lacks;
- capacity for direct repair after recognizing fault.

## State-dependent

- cold accusation under cumulative fear and harassment;
- acute freeze response around male violence;
- heightened jealousy/specialness injury around Saku;
- unusually reckless confrontation after deciding to build a stronger inner core;
- intensified romantic play after crisis disclosure.

# 15. Rival readings

### "Yuzuki is fake underneath"
Rejected. The text presents multiple consciously authored states, not a false shell over a singular opposite essence.

### "Her entire personality is trauma compensation"
Explicitly contradicted by her own chronology.

### "Saku saves her"
Incomplete. Saku catalyzes and protects, but Yuzuki's decisive development is self-authored and later exercised independently against both Yanagishita and Tomoya.

### "She falls for Saku because he is high-status and handsome"
Too weak. Those traits matter to initial recognition and the fake-boyfriend logic, but her attraction intensifies through difference, ordinary exposure, vulnerability, and value-laden action.

### "Her new confidence means the trauma is cured"
Unsupported. V02 gives a successful state transition, not longitudinal proof of permanent resolution.

# 16. V03 watchlist

- How stable is the new anti-coercion core outside the crisis frame?
- Does she preserve strategic competence while becoming more direct?
- How does named love alter behavior toward Saku and other girls?
- Does she continue idealizing Saku's dangerous interventionism?
- How does Haru function once romantic competition becomes more explicit?
- Can Yuzuki tolerate Saku's refusal to name his own feeling?
- Does she become capable of helping Saku in ways he did not script?

## V02 freeze model

> **Yuzuki Nanase is not a false girl learning to become real. She is an authored girl learning that adaptation alone is not enough: a self also needs claims it will not surrender simply because adaptation would be easier. Volume 02 gives her that beginning of a core, and romantic love becomes one of the names through which she chooses movement rather than possession.**

# V03 longitudinal revision

## Evidence limit

Volume 03 is Asuka-centered and does not supply new Yuzuki focalization or a sustained retest of trauma response. It should not be used to declare permanent recovery, a complete romantic philosophy, or a settled Haru/Yuzuki dialectic. Its material contribution is narrower and important: named love is tested against another person's competing importance.

## 1. Named love becomes costly action

Yuzuki recognizes Saku's distress and tells him to face the important feeling before it is lost. After learning the Asuka conflict, she identifies the direct method available to him and knowingly helps him move toward a romantic rival `[p-0022#p0260-p0297]`, `[p-0024#p0144-p0166]`.

This strengthens the V02 model of love as self-authorizing movement rather than possession. Yuzuki does not stop desiring Saku or become emotionally neutral. She acts from a value that can survive loss of competitive advantage.

## 2. The crescent lamp makes recognition ordinary

In the labeled pre-V03 `BONUS_FICTION`, Yuzuki gives Saku a crescent-moon desk lamp after the V02 events. The gift asks to remain within his bedside memory without claiming that only a full or exclusive light has value. It translates the crisis-born declaration of love into an object that can inhabit ordinary routine.

The evidence is official but class-bounded. It supplements relationship expression and material continuity; it does not become unmarked numbered-main chronology.

## 3. Rivalry and generosity coexist

Yuzuki's help should not be romanticized as pure self-erasure. She preserves desire, teasing, and her own claim while refusing to sabotage Asuka. This is healthier than possession, but it creates a new risk: nonpossessive generosity could itself become a role that prevents Yuzuki from expressing hurt or asking Saku for clarity.

## 4. V03 revision state

- named love as chosen movement → **STRENGTHEN**;
- love as possession → **REJECT as the best current account**;
- stability of the V02 anti-coercion core → **OPEN**, not substantially retested;
- Haru/Yuzuki optimization dialectic → **OPEN**, insufficient new dyadic evidence;
- idealization of Saku's interventionism → **OPEN**; V03 does not give Yuzuki a direct ethical audit of his methods toward Asuka.

## 5. V04 watchlist

- whether Yuzuki can remain generous without making self-suppression virtuous;
- whether she directly asks Saku for recognition or clarity;
- whether ordinary disappointment tests the V02 jurisdictional core;
- whether she contests Saku's invasive methods when she is not their recipient;
- whether Haru again functions as developmental rival/corrective;
- whether the crescent model—valuable without fullness—appears in her behavior outside the bonus.

## V03 current model

> **Yuzuki's V03 contribution is small in page share but large in diagnostic value: she can act from love in a way that protects Saku's authorship even when doing so helps another woman. The act strengthens nonpossession, but later volumes must test whether generosity remains chosen rather than becoming a new polished obligation.**
