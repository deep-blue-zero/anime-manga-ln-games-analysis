# Haru Aomi — cumulative character monograph through *Days of Endless Summer*

## Scope and promotion basis

Volume 04 crosses the independent-model threshold for Haru Aomi (青海陽): sustained prologue/epilogue focalization, athletic formation and practice, captaincy failure, ordinary-life behavior, direct romantic action, a major relationship with Saku, an independently important relationship with Yuzuki, group leadership, embodied risk, and substantial counterevidence. V01–V03 establish her as Saku's direct corrective and catch partner; V04 makes those functions testable against her own fear, methods, dependence, and change.

This is a V04-bounded current model, not a definitive series interpretation.

## 1. Compressed model

Haru is an intensely embodied self-author who treats effort as a way to remain answerable to possibility. Her directness, speed, appetite, competitive humor, and refusal of elegant resignation are not an uncomplicated lack of reflection. They are a practiced response to defeat and to the fear that her inner flame will become a resigned performance.

Her best relational act is **translation**. She receives another person's heat, returns it in a form they can use, and then leaves the decision with them. Her worst leadership act is **imposition**. She assumes that because she can see a teammate's avoidant conduct, she may define the teammate's inner seriousness and require her own form of sacrifice as proof.

Volume 04 does not transform Haru from reckless passion into balanced maturity. It makes her capable of naming dependence, recognizing Yuzuki as a rival/co-leader, asking teammates to supply what she lacks, and imagining relation as running beside rather than saving or orbiting another person. Sustainable effort, direct accountability, and Saku's answer remain open.

## 2. Evidence planes

### Haru focalization

The prologue and epilogue reveal her fear of extinguished passion, desire to prove that muddy effort is not shameful, initial projection of Saku as a moon, and later rejection of a savior reading `[p-0010]`, `[p-0035]`.

### Saku focalization

Most of V04 is filtered through Saku. His attraction, admiration, and identification are evidence about their relationship and his reading of her, not transparent access to Haru. Independent conduct—the training record, collapse, tactical play, team testimony, and Haru's own speech—must corroborate his aesthetics.

### Yuzuki and team witness

Yuzuki supplies the history of captain selection and the hidden leadership compact. Teammates contest Haru's account of their effort; their disagreement is evidence, not narrative noise. Coach Misaki supplies a procedurally important adult critique.

### Athletic outcomes

Haru's speed, point-guard vision, technical learning, and capacity to adapt movement are independently demonstrated. Loss to Mai and the later three-point team loss prevent competence from becoming omnipotence.

## 3. Passion as chosen identity

Haru's given-name association with the sun has become an ethical demand: she wants her exertion to reach someone and her future self to recognize that she did not abandon the attempt. The feared opposite is not ordinary defeat but the thin smile of someone who no longer burns `[p-0010#p0004-p0031]`.

This produces real virtues:

- unusually durable practice;
- willingness to be visibly uncool, sweaty, muddy, or losing;
- refusal to erase another athlete's labor as mere talent;
- capacity to convert shame into renewed action;
- readiness to speak feelings other people keep strategically deniable.

It also produces a compulsory edge. If heat becomes the criterion by which she knows herself, rest can resemble extinction and a different training choice can resemble moral failure.

## 4. Effort, talent, and structural limits

Mai's superiority matters because it cannot be explained away. Haru is fast, disciplined, and skilled; Mai's height, strength, speed, and technique still control the one-on-one `[p-0018#p0273-p0020#p0036]`. Haru rejects envy that makes another athlete's advantages costless, while Saku refuses the patronizing claim that she is impressive “despite” her height.

The current Haru ethic is strongest when it says:

> outcome is not guaranteed, structural difference is real, and only sustained attempt can disclose what a particular person or team may become.

It is weakest when it slides from that claim into the idea that maximal visible sacrifice is the only sincere relation to a goal.

## 5. Captaincy: legitimate aspiration, illegitimate interior authority

The team unanimously chooses Haru and names an Inter-High championship. Haru therefore has real mandate and a justified concern that the collective aspiration may be becoming decorative. She identifies concrete avoidant conduct, including Sen's decision not even to attempt a familiar block `[p-0023#p0237-p0313]`, `[p-0025#p0067-p0082]`.

Her failure lies in the route. The hidden “demon Umi / Buddha Nana” compact lets Haru absorb hatred and Yuzuki absorb complaints while teammates remain unable to evaluate the governance structure `[p-0023#p0264-p0025#p0063]`. Haru expands hours, treats one rate of correction as universal, and claims that serious strivers can see through teammates' accounts of their own best.

Coach Misaki's refusal to become an imposed villain returns basketball to the players and directly compares Haru's method to Wataya's `[p-0025#p0088-p0100]`. This is material counterevidence against treating Haru's accuracy or sacrifice as sufficient legitimacy.

## 6. Leadership revision through dependence

Haru's collapse makes visible that the captaincy form is consuming her. She skips food and hydration, apologizes while unconscious, and still tries to return before she can lift a drink `[p-0023#p0004-p0127]`.

After the team leaves, she names the proposition her role had hidden: she is incomplete and cannot fight without them `[p-0025#p0123-p0135]`. In the practice match she admits her athletic and captaincy weaknesses and asks teammates to supply the “missing twenty centimeters” `[p-0032#p0284-p0354]`.

This is a real shift from exemplary domination toward functional interdependence. The repair remains partial. Haru asks for help “if and when” teammates can give it and does not demand apologies, but she also does not directly account for the hidden compact or distinguish legitimate workload disagreement from the excuses teammates later admit.

## 7. Relationship with Saku

### Corrective equality and embodied ease

Across V01–V03, Haru is the peer most willing to puncture Saku's optimization, meet him through sport, and refuse the prestige surrounding him. The self-bought glove makes their catch partnership a shared practice rather than a gift or passive claim.

### Witness and disclosure

In V04 she protects him from Wataya's contempt, stops immediate self-harm, and refuses the old team's instrumental use of him. At Asuwayama she uses their tied wager to demand the complete account, listens without interruption, holds his hand, and rebukes his abandonment of baseball `[p-0017#p0063-p0199]`, `[p-0020#p0503-p0022#p0020]`.

Her rebuke is not neutral. Saku's old play materially sustained her, so she has a personal stake in what he abandoned. Yet she refuses to convert that stake into decision authority. She says what she loves and rejects in his account, then leaves the answer to him `[p-0022#p0024-p0049]`.

### Reciprocal formation without rescue

Saku's game once restored Haru's competitive heat; Haru's present accompaniment helps him choose a final game. She fears she caused self-betrayal, and he distinguishes action “thanks to” her from action “for” her `[p-0025#p0307-p0329]`. The epilogue confirms that she now sees his own fire rather than claiming to have supplied it.

### Love and unanswered jurisdiction

Haru names love repeatedly and directly. She imagines dating as exercise, food, and massage rather than only symbolic devotion. Saku calls the prospect attractive, directs his home-run wristband gesture to her, and promises support, but does not name matching love `[p-0027#p0027-p0074]`, `[p-0030#p0001-p0019]`.

Her final kiss is assertive but stops short of declaring reciprocity. The height difference redirects it to his throat; she leaves the remaining distance to his future move `[p-0034#p0001-p0025]`. Her V04 achievement is making her bid answerable without pretending it has already been answered.

## 8. Relationship with Yuzuki

Yuzuki is not merely Haru's calmer assistant. Their friendship begins from admiration and competition, then acquires a hidden division of labor that burdens both. Haru accepts public hatred; Yuzuki absorbs private complaint. The arrangement allows each to call the other indispensable while preventing open correction.

After collapse, Yuzuki asks the team directly, preserves voluntary practice, and uses play to make missing support visible. Haru accepts this intervention and names Yuzuki a genuine rival in basketball and love `[p-0023#p0131-p0166]`, `[p-0032#p0030-p0280]`.

The relationship becomes healthier when loyalty no longer requires agreement or invisibility. It remains unresolved whether they can discuss workload and hidden governance without needing a rupture first.

## 9. Love, rivalry, and gendered presentation

Haru refuses elegant romantic withdrawal. She wants to be the person who makes the beloved smile and remains when he cries `[p-0020#p0277-p0298]`. This is competitive, but not sabotage: she can recognize Yuzuki's claim and act publicly without demanding that the group erase every other bond.

Her athletic and feminine presentations are not opposites. The blue dress Yuko helped select coexists with game-day intensity, and Haru rejects the idea that relying on a man makes a woman lesser `[p-0028#p0004-p0057]`, `[p-0032#p0030-p0068]`. She can want to be seen as a girl without treating the athlete as a deficient pre-romantic self.

## 10. Ordinary-life profile

V01–V04 support a bounded mundane profile:

- hungry, physically expressive, and comfortable making food/exercise part of intimacy;
- highly observant of athletic movement and bodily condition;
- learns catch technique through repeated practice rather than immediate mastery;
- can sleep undefended around Saku after exhaustion;
- accepts playful styling and a special-day dress without abandoning sports identity;
- uses blunt humor, flirtation, physical competition, and nicknames as ordinary relationship language;
- returns to Asuwayama as a private place for defeat and recalibration;
- carries and transfers meaning through practical sports objects, especially glove and wristband.

## 11. Care and recognition style

Haru's care is high-temperature and embodied. She notices the hand about to hit concrete, the altered motion of an injured wrist, the athlete hidden inside refusal, and the teammate who has stopped trying a specific play. She is unusually willing to let care include anger.

At her best, she distinguishes recognition from command. She can say “I see what you are doing,” disclose why it matters to her, and return the decision. At her worst, confidence in bodily evidence becomes a claim to know the whole person better than their speech.

## 12. Stable tendencies versus state effects

### More stable through V04

- action-oriented directness;
- competitive joy and refusal of graceful resignation;
- learned athletic discipline and rapid bodily translation;
- readiness to express affection openly;
- sensitivity to hidden effort and contempt for “talent” as labor erasure;
- desire for relational equality expressed through challenge;
- practical loyalty under pressure.

### State- or role-conditioned

- treating rest as evidence that her flame may be dying;
- extending her personal effort standard into team-wide obligation;
- the demon-captain performance and willingness to absorb all blame;
- collapse-level self-neglect during leadership crisis;
- projecting Saku as a moon who must prove her way of living;
- intensified public romantic directness around the baseball answer.

## 13. Major contradictions and risks

### She returns Saku's jurisdiction but overclaims her teammates'

This is the central V04 contradiction. Haru can be a procedurally mature helper in one dyad and a coercive leader in another. The difference is not that she cares more about Saku. It is that the Asuwayama process culminates in his deliberation, while the hidden team plan was designed around eventual compliance.

### She rejects talent fatalism but can moralize effort

Haru correctly refuses accounts that make labor invisible. She risks making endurance itself a virtue independent of safety, method, or sustainable future action.

### She wants equality but can turn reciprocity into proof

Her epilogue rejects rescue and orbit. Earlier, she wanted Saku's return to validate her own way of living. The shift is significant but not yet tested outside the volume climax.

## 14. Rival readings

### “Haru is merely the honest tomboy corrective”

Rejected. Directness is real, but V04 shows concealment, role performance, romantic strategy, leadership overreach, and fear of inner extinction.

### “Her effort proves talent does not matter”

Rejected. Mai's body and skill remain decisive; the team's final loss preserves structural difference.

### “The team walkout proves Haru was wrong about their excuses”

Too simple. Some later admit avoidance, but their method objections were legitimate and Haru lacked authority over their interior self-descriptions.

### “Haru saves Saku”

Rejected in strong form. She supplies indispensable recognition and pressure, then refuses the decision; the epilogue attributes primary fire and answer to Saku.

### “Direct love makes her Saku's chosen partner”

Unsupported. He answers through support and a directed gesture, not reciprocal love or commitment. She explicitly leaves the next move open.

## 15. V04 current model

> **Haru Aomi is a high-temperature relational self-author whose deepest strength is making effort, desire, and affection visible without requiring elegance. Her V04 growth lies in discovering that another person's heat cannot be manufactured or owned: it can be witnessed, challenged, received, and returned. Her central failure is the opposite move—treating her own form of seriousness as authority over teammates' methods and inner limits. She ends capable of naming dependence and running beside another person, but sustainable effort, direct accountability, and reciprocal romantic choice remain unproven.**

# V05 regular-main longitudinal revision

## The transferred next move becomes a protocol

Haru begins V05 aware that her confession and kiss made feeling visible without establishing a request or answer. Her awkwardness with Saku shows that emotional directness can still leave procedural ambiguity `[part0010#p0012-p0030]`.

She first asks to visit his home, accepting Yuzuki's help at the level she chooses. During the later night run she names the problem directly. Haru refuses to withdraw her love, asks Saku to see her as a girl and romantic possibility, and says she must complete her own `kejime` before making the genuine challenge `[part0018#p0998-part0020#p0008]`.

She does not ask Saku to wait. Her bounded request is that he not flee when the later challenge comes, and he agrees `[part0020#p0010-p0026]`. This is substantial procedural growth: a dramatic declaration is translated into a future act with timing, responsibility, and non-entitlement.

## Running partnership is reciprocal without becoming a romantic answer

Saku explains that the V04 game closed Fujishi membership but left chosen baseball possible. Haru accepts the boundary and promises to watch without directing his next form. Each privately fears the other may become unreachable and uses the same running-partner language `[part0010#p0276-p0323]`.

The relation therefore gains explicit reciprocity around action and future aspiration. That does not automatically convert it into reciprocal romantic love. Haru herself preserves the distinction by reserving a genuine later request.

## Female friendship is part of her agency

Yuzuki first offers Haru private access, then helps only when Haru asks. Yuko and Yuzuki help her choose a swimsuit that presents the athletic body she actually has rather than treating it as a failed feminine form `[part0010#p0069-p0117]`, `[part0013#p0703-part0015#p0019]`.

Haru's romantic development is therefore not a solo conversion from tomboy to girl. It is supported through rival friendship while leaving sport identity intact. The V05 supplement's announced first-year Yuzuki/Haru story may later historicize this relationship, but it supplies no current evidence until opened and placed.

## Team-governance limits remain open

V05 mentions team success and Mai-related invitations but provides no sustained scene of training negotiation, workload accountability, injury protocol, or repair of the hidden compact. Outcome cannot stand in for method evidence. The central V04 leadership contradiction remains open rather than silently resolved.

## V05-main current model

> **Haru at V05 main retains her high-temperature directness while becoming more procedurally exact. She converts an unanswered confession into a future challenge that owns her unfinished work, preserves Saku's freedom not to wait, and asks only that he meet the eventual bid. Her equal running-partner relation with Saku is real but not a romantic answer. The largest unresolved gap remains collective: V05 shows no evidence that her improved dyadic consent practice has generalized to transparent, safe team leadership.**

# V05 special-edition longitudinal revision

## The challenge protocol has an earlier form

After Saku helps Yuzuki in a V02-associated story, Haru asks him to accept if she ever challenges him, and he agrees `[V05S:p-0053#p0053-p0059]`. The V05 future request is therefore not created from nothing by her V04 confession. It is a matured repetition of their shared competitive grammar, now made explicitly romantic and consent-sensitive.

This history strengthens rather than completes the protocol. Its content and Saku's eventual answer remain future matters.

## Attraction precedes the pool scene

Immediately after the pool-cleaning assignment, Yuko offers to take Haru's place. Haru refuses with a reason she knows is false, compares Yuko's conventional femininity with herself, and admits she was already anticipating time alone with Saku `[V05S:p-0067#p0012-p0039]`.

The pool encounter clarifies and intensifies attraction; it does not manufacture it. Haru's slow recognition is therefore genuine development rather than retroactive invention.

## The necktie is authentic without being exhaustive

Haru directly asks why Saku prefers her in a necktie. Their “male-friend” convention gives way to mutual acknowledgment that he sees her as attractive and as a girl `[V05S:p-0059#p0014-p0041]`. Her wish to remove the tie sometimes and experiment with a ribbon-coded gaze does not expose the tie as false `[V05S:p-0059#p0042-p0059]`.

Her gendered self-presentation is better modeled as selectable range: athletic peer style remains genuinely hers, while desire opens other forms she may try without treating her existing body or identity as deficient.

## Yuzuki is a primary partner, not merely rival or helper

First-year autumn shows Haru and Yuzuki already using one-on-one contest, teasing, teaching, and situational cover as a reciprocal system `[V05S:p-0074#p0113-p0200]`. Haru's imagined future partner resembles Yuzuki's cool, observant, self-containing care `[V05S:p-0074#p0260-p0317]`. Later, Haru and Yuzuki explicitly name their romantic contest while preserving the same fair-fight idiom `[V05S:p-0070#p0036-p0063]`.

Haru's relation with Saku develops partly through this partnership but is not reducible to Yuzuki's mediation. Likewise, Yuzuki/Haru is not reducible to competition over him.

## Post-special current model

> **Haru's directness is a learned range rather than an unchanging essence. Her future romantic protocol with Saku matures a long challenge idiom; her attraction predates the scene that makes it legible; and her athletic, peerlike presentation remains authentic while admitting gendered curiosity and desire. Yuzuki is an independently primary partner whose correction, teaching, and fair-contest norms help make Haru's later agency possible. No supplement turns running partnership or attraction into reciprocal romantic commitment.**

# V06 longitudinal revision

## Romance breaks the competition model before it produces a challenge

After Yuko's confession, Haru recognizes that romance has no shared start signal, rules, score, or repeatable training. Her prior future-challenge agreement does not guarantee that the field or participants will remain available `[V06:p-0012#p0187-p0351]`.

This is a material self-theory revision. Competitive grammar still gives Haru a way to act, but it cannot make love fair through procedure alone.

## Yuzuki remains the first container for scarcity pain

Haru and Yuzuki respond to the fracture by lying together, sharing a meal plan, and joining hands. Their partnership does not require either to deny desire or convert the other into an opponent to defeat `[V06:p-0012#p0354-p0366]`.

The dyad therefore survives the first explicit scarcity test. The promised contest itself remains unresolved.

## She authors care through embodied competence

Haru decides that conversation, letters, and conventional comfort would be inauthentic from her. She recruits Atomu to give Saku a full-strength baseball encounter, and Saku says it provides the first genuine relief of the week `[V06:p-0022#p0391-p0539]`.

Her method is imaginative rather than narrow: she uses her knowledge of Saku, another athlete's capacity, and play to create an experience she cannot provide alone.

## Team language both distributes burden and limits her bid

Haru tells Saku that everyone is a companion before being man or woman, and that he need not carry all clean and dirty feelings. The insight comes from team leadership and their history as partners `[V06:p-0024#p0001-p0049]`.

Her private aside—this is the only way she can be beside him—shows the cost. Team idiom enables support while keeping romantic desire below direct present request. Her V05 challenge protocol is not completed in V06.

## Post-V06 current model

> **Haru's V06 growth lies in accepting that romance cannot be made into a fair scored competition while still using athletic partnership to provide real, recipient-specific care. She mobilizes Atomu, gives Saku embodied relief, and distributes burden across the team rather than demanding a romantic answer. Yuzuki remains her independently primary partner and first container for rivalry pain. The unresolved tasks are the promised challenge, the meaning of her `kejime`, and whether dyadic consent growth reaches team governance.**

# V08 main longitudinal revision

## Yuzuki's team control makes the primacy shock concrete

Against Kanazawa Oboro, Yuzuki controls scoring and attention so thoroughly that Haru experiences herself and teammates as pieces in another player's proof. The team wins 65–51, but Haru cannot treat the result as collective flourishing `[V08:p-0013#p0640-p0794]`.

This is not mere jealousy or evidence that Haru is weak. It identifies a real coordination failure and reveals how much her athletic self still depends on comparative location beside Yuzuki.

## Ace status is retained as task rather than guarantee

Coach Misaki explicitly keeps Haru as Fujishi's ace. The reassurance carries a demand: Yuzuki will keep moving, and waiting means Haru must find her own weapon and catch up `[V08:p-0013#p0927-p1105]`, `[V08:p-0015#p0002-p0187]`.
