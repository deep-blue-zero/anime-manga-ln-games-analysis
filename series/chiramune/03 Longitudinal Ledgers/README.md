These are maintained cumulative documents through the current analyzed boundary. V03 and V04 append explicit boundary-labeled updates without rewriting earlier epistemic states preserved by their prospective readings and freezes. The separate V03 booklet materially updates only the revision, relationship, and self-authorship ledgers; agency and social-status remain at their main-V03 findings for that supplement because it supplies no independent transition for those responsibilities. V04 materially advances all five ledgers through sports institutions, team governance, relationship direction, disclosure, and effort/safety evidence.

Every entry must preserve the volume boundary that established it. Later recontextualization may revise the current interpretation through `PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN / REVISE / DOWNGRADE / REJECT / OPEN` without erasing the earlier formulation.

A new ledger earns a canonical file only when a recurring question accumulates independently and retrieving or revising it from the current ledgers and volume readings becomes unreliable. Candidate future responsibilities include aspiration/adulthood, ordinary-life/locality, prose/register, or motif/form tracking. These are possibilities, not predetermined theses. Combine sparse responsibilities and split only when independent retrieval becomes materially useful.
