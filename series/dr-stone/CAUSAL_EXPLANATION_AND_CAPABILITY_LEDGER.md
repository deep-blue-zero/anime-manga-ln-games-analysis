# Causal explanation and capability ledger

## Responsibility

Keep a source's explanation, depicted attempt/result, analyst mechanism and external truth claim separate. The same causal discipline applies to ordinary nontechnical decisions. No mechanism, process, resource or capability is established at INITIAL.

## Operational schemas

| Register / key | Required fields |
|---|---|
| Explanation `DS-EX-nnnn` | Exact witness/frontier; speaker/narrator/diagram; proposition or prediction; knowledge/authority basis as supported; conditions stated; omitted/uncertain links; evidence and full argument |
| Demonstration `DS-DEM-nnnn` | Exact pages; depicted attempt/action/result; what is directly visible; narrative assertion versus image; missing operations/time/scale; alternative cause; outcome limits |
| Capability/dependency `DS-CAP-nnnn` | Actor/group; time/frontier; task as source-established; resources/tools/knowledge/labor; dependency relation necessary/sufficient/enabling/correlated/unknown; evidence; failure/exception; rival; affected claims |
| Decision chain `DS-A-nnnn` | Conditions; perception/appraisal; affect; options/inhibitors; choice/non-action; consequence; supported versus missing causal links; full reading |
| External comparison `DS-EXT-nnnn` | Separately scoped question; exact external proposition/reference/date; fictional statement/page; agreement/disagreement and limits; actual verification performed; no claim to settle literary meaning |
| Causal dispute `DS-CD-nnnn` | Incompatible explanation/result/dependency; witness/knowledge boundary; possible omission/error/context/source-layer cause; needed evidence; current limitation; revision route |

Every record is keyed to lane/witness/version, disclosure and depicted-time constraints, actor/group knowledge, role/resources and interpretive mode. A group capability is not every member's personal knowledge. A diagram is not an experiment; a visible result does not reveal all omitted steps.

## Update and analytical discipline

Full readings own developed mechanisms and alternatives. Do not replace them with process names or dependency arrows. Explicitly mark where external expertise would be required to test a proposition; no experiment, calculation or fact-check may be claimed unless actually performed within scope.

After each accepted unit append explanation/demonstration/decision/dependency changes and scoped no-change findings. Preserve earlier beliefs and hypotheses. Later capability does not enter an earlier state, and a fanbook elaboration remains separately sourced.

At checkpoints compare explanation with actual depicted evidence; test alternative contributions, resources, coordination and failure conditions. Review whether an apparently technical conclusion displaced motives, relationships, ordinary activity or page form. Material revisions propagate to claims, character/group states, studies and models. One sequential owner maintains this ledger.
