# Character, group and relationship ledger

## Responsibility

Preserve individual states, group conditions, directed relationships, ordinary-life evidence and Japanese expression alongside causal/capability analysis. No cast, institution or relationship is populated from familiarity.

A contribution to a collective result does not alone prove affection, loyalty or consent. A task failure does not prove incompetence without context/resources. A facial expression is not a unique emotion. Preserve ordinary and contrary evidence rather than ranking characters by competence.

## Update and review

Create identities/groups only from admitted pages. Append subject, directed tie, group, language and opportunity evidence after each accepted reading. Scoped no-change findings show actual inspection. Maintain each person's knowledge separately from what the reader or group collectively knows.

Checkpoint review tests whether technical accomplishment has displaced motives, affect, mundane interaction, relationship asymmetry or expressive specificity. Explain how roles and resources condition behavior. New disclosures preserve earlier names/uncertainty and create revisions rather than overwrite prospective state.

Substantive changes notify causal dependencies, claims, studies and future model coverage. Full prose retains the arguments; this ledger provides durable retrieval. One active sequential owner updates the surface.
