# Dr. STONE synthesis architecture

## 1. Purpose, scope and INITIAL authority

This corpus must be able to distinguish **explanation, depicted capability, causal dependency and external truth** without allowing technical interpretation to replace character, relationship, ordinary-life, Japanese-language or graphic analysis. These are design responsibilities, not findings that any particular process or narrative event occurs.

The supplied planning boundary is Japanese main V01–27 plus separate 科学王国事典. No content is admitted. The [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) is the sole current route; the [method](DS_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) governs readings. Canonical INITIAL status covers rules and attributed metadata only; owner acceptance and literary/technical/model readiness are unclaimed.

Apply the [initiation policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md), [generic architecture](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MULTI_DOCUMENT_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE_TEMPLATE.md), [reconstruction method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md) and current archive/execution/handoff policies. No local gate grants source authorization.

## 2. Evidence classes and source ranking

Locked, readable and actually inspected Japanese main pages govern the depicted text/art of that witness. An in-world explanation, narrator assertion, diagram, demonstrated result and analyst causal inference are separate evidence categories. Metadata establishes reported inventory, not scientific or literary validity.

The fanbook has its own component taxonomy and publication/disclosure boundary: data, editorial/technical explanation, creator statement/interview, retrospective commentary, art, narrative/bonus or unresolved. “Official” does not make those categories interchangeable. A fanbook elaboration cannot silently fill a main-page omission or become an earlier subject's knowledge.

External references, if an explicit question warrants them, form a separate contextual/fact-check layer. They can assess a real-world proposition without settling fictional causality, intended meaning, motive or ethics. A calculation or experiment not actually performed is never reported as validation. No technical fact-check is performed in this bootstrap.

Adaptations, other editions, later acquisitions and unrelated works remain excluded pending identity/scope review. Derived analyses route sources, not independently corroborate them. Primary pages, raw extractions and private receipts remain in the evidence plane.

## 3. Existing structure and future outputs

Five registers are mandatory because each owns a distinct recurring responsibility across a long manga sequence:

| Existing home | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| [Source/fanbook register](SOURCE_AND_FANBOOK_REGISTER.md) | Witness versions, component/page coverage and separate companion authority |
| [Causal explanation/capability ledger](CAUSAL_EXPLANATION_AND_CAPABILITY_LEDGER.md) | Claimed mechanisms, shown actions/results, resources/knowledge/labor and causal dependencies |
| [Character/group/relationship ledger](CHARACTER_GROUP_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md) | Individual knowledge/state, group conditions, directed ties, ordinary controls and Japanese expression |
| [Page/diagram/claim register](PAGE_DIAGRAM_AND_CLAIM_REGISTER.md) | Deterministic image/text/diagram evidence, full arguments, rivals and revision |
| [Checkpoint/reconstruction register](CHECKPOINT_AND_RECONSTRUCTION_REGISTER.md) | Atomic transactions, freezes, dependencies, role gaps, model coverage/evaluation and handoff |

No actual mechanism, character or institution row is invented to justify the schemas. The causal register also serves nontechnical decisions; a specialist technical domain is earned later, not assumed mandatory now.

Future full readings use `readings/DS_JP_Vnn_READING.md`; full checkpoint reconciliations use `checkpoints/DS_CPnn.md`. Distinct evidence-earned character, relationship, group/institution, explanation/form or technical-context studies use topical homes under `studies/`. Fanbook comparison is separately scoped under `studies/paratext/`. Continuous synthesis uses `synthesis/DS_CONTINUOUS_SYNTHESIS.md`; reconstruction separates evidence/model/tests under `reconstruction/<subject-id>/`.

These are planned homes, not empty directories or completed artifacts. New files require distinct responsibility and an authority route. Do not create one technical appendix per concept or one dossier per name unless a real analytical role warrants it.

## 5. Sequential and checkpoint contract

Verify numbered order and actual chapters/page/spread maps before a run. A volume is the source unit with chapter/movement frontiers. Keep publication/edition order, depicted time, source disclosure, subject acquisition and analyst admission distinct.

The authorized run defines start, terminal scope, exclusions and checkpoint cadence. A provisional planning ceiling of four volumes between full reconciliations is not authorization or a story-arc claim; source density, a disputed mechanism or group/state discontinuity can require earlier review. The fanbook has no automatic sequential position.

A completed transaction includes full reading and synchronized five-register updates, with scoped no-change findings. Freeze entering/exit state and prospective predictions. Missing pages, unexplained dependency jumps or untested central routes remain debts.

At checkpoints reconcile capability claims with actual depicted evidence, temporal/knowledge states, individual/group/relationship history and page/language arguments. Preserve complete prior prose and quiet ordinary evidence. Final-volume arrival cannot substitute for longitudinal reconciliation or specialist readiness.

## 6. Temporal, epistemic and continuity states

State is keyed by lane/witness/version + admitted frontier + disclosure position + depicted-time constraint + actor/group knowledge + conditions/resources/roles + directed relationships + interpretive mode. Modes are PROSPECTIVE_AT_BOUNDARY, RETROSPECTIVE_WITHIN_ADMITTED_BOUNDARY, FANBOOK_COMPARATIVE, EXTERNAL_CONTEXTUAL and UNRESOLVED.

A statement of a mechanism is not an observed mechanism; a depicted result is not proof that every proposed dependency is sufficient or realistic. Store necessary/sufficient/enabling/correlated/unknown dependency types with reasons. Unknown steps and resources remain unknown instead of being filled from the analyst's expertise.

A group-level capability and each member's knowledge are different states. A later acquired resource, skill or explanation cannot be projected backward. Change causes distinguish DISPOSITION, KNOWLEDGE, RELATIONSHIP, CONTEXT, ROLE, RESOURCE, REVEALED_NOT_NEW and UNRESOLVED. Ordinary-life evidence and meaningful opportunities constrain trait inference.

Page order may depict succession, simultaneity, schematic decomposition or replay. Diagrams require exact observed conventions and may omit scale, time or operation. Neither a graphic endpoint nor a technical-looking label establishes omitted motion or actual experimental validation.

## 7. Contradictions, revision and preservation

Classify conflict as witness/page map, chronology, knowledge/report reliability, group/role/resource constraint, schematic omission, fanbook source layer, external-reference mismatch, interpretation, missing evidence or possible textual inconsistency. Preserve live alternatives; do not automatically correct fiction to match external science or ignore a fictional contradiction because a real-world procedure exists.

Use PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT and OPEN. Record old/new formulations, source/knowledge boundary, evidence, counterevidence, rival disposition, confidence reason and downstream dependencies. Current-view pointers may change after acceptance; prior state and full argument remain.

A change in causal interpretation reviews affected capability, character/group/relationship, visual/linguistic, specialist and model routes separately. Preserve unaffected supported findings. Frozen readings and forecasts are corrected through linked errata or current superseding work, not silent hindsight rewrites.

## 8. Monographs and specialist integration

Character monographs need adequate time, ordinary-context, relationship, knowledge and expressive coverage. They integrate behavior, motivation/rivals, development, language, graphic presentation and literary function. They cannot become competence rankings or accomplishment chronologies.

A relationship/group study earns an independent home when directed ties, distributed labor, obligation, authority or institutional conditions need sustained analysis beyond a single subject. Claims about contribution, loyalty or group purpose require evidence and contrary cases.

Technical/explanation, institution, diagram/form or ethical specialists remain anticipated, not established themes. Commission only after recurring evidence creates a material independent question, with a frozen source scope, complete readings, exact routes, counterexamples and explicit contribution. An evidence-based no-independent-specialist decision must name who owns the role.

Specialists return full arguments and evidence returns. Convergence reconciles or preserves conflicts before integrated-synthesis readiness. Continuous synthesis connects causal explanation, people, relationships, organization, language and form, including unresolved values; it does not concatenate process summaries or specialist abstracts.

## 9. Reconstruction and independent evaluation

Reconstruction is an intended later responsibility for sufficiently evidenced source-established subjects, distinct from monographs and technical simulations. No subject model, fictional capability benchmark or readiness grade exists. Designated agents own global discovery/capability registries.

Separate evidence/retrieval, operational model and independent tests. Identity is subject@lane/witness/frontier/state, with admitted evidence filtered to that subject's supported knowledge, excluded fanbook/adaptation facts and latent exposure recorded.

Twelve descriptive dimensions cover goals/values; appraisal/threat; motive conflicts; knowledge/errors; attention/reasoning; affect/regulation; disposition/condition; directed relations; role/audience; action repertoire/inhibitors; Japanese/graphic voice; development/exceptions. Do not infer a general problem-solving policy from a narrated success alone.

Each conditional rule specifies trigger/stakes/state → response family, inhibitors/overrides, exceptions, support, counterevidence, rival and falsifier. Trace appraisal → decision → communicative objective → recipient-conditioned expression. Record confidence, evidence distance and abstention/multiple-response criteria outside supported contexts, including unfamiliar technical situations. OBSERVED, CANDIDATE_POLICY and PROMOTED_POLICY remain separate.

Assess ordinary interaction, pressure/conflict, uncertain/false information, changed recipient/tie, role/audience shift and consequential choice where supported. Separate decision fidelity from Japanese/graphic voice fidelity; drawings do not certify motor or vocal performance.

Freeze true held-out predictions before source exposure and separate author/evaluator roles. Exposed exercises are RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST. Independent outcomes are SUPPORTED, PARTLY_SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY, CONFOUNDED and UNRESOLVED. Use meaningful baselines, diagnostic perturbations, decoys/negative controls and explicit opportunity denominators without invented accuracy metrics.

Synthetic dialogue, solutions and scenarios remain derived tests, never primary evidence, canonical quotes, technical validation or independent corroboration. A model's quality and a monograph's literary adequacy are separate gates.

| D7 Specialist convergence | Conflicts/dependencies handled; no-specialist alternative justified and owned |

Source-complete is not synthesis-ready. Main completion does not close the fanbook or prove official full-work completion. Interpretive uncertainty can remain; missing required inspection cannot be hidden as harmless uncertainty.

## 11. Execution, handoff and growth

SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS covers future readings/interpretive updates; DEEP_SYNTHESIS covers checkpoints, specialists, monographs and model/evaluator work; PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST covers architecture and continuous integration where incorrect dependencies would propagate. Stable indexing is BOUNDED_STANDARD and hashes ROUTINE_FAST. Resolve actual settings through the [reasoning policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_REASONING_AND_MODEL_ROUTING_POLICY.md).

The [long-series protocol](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_LONG_SERIES_HYBRID_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md) favors source-capable local reading/ledgers, fresh fully supplied synthesis, independent review and designated integration. Current access is metadata/documents only. Verify transport, processing, STILL_IMAGE/TEXT inspection, interpretation and persistence through [capability routing](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EXECUTION_TOPOLOGY_AND_CAPABILITY_ROUTING_POLICY.md).

[Handoff](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md) supplies base snapshot, full exact inputs/outputs/digests, authority, source/admission/knowledge boundary, owners and unresolved obligations. Returned work includes complete prose and change/evidence routes. The integrator compares base/candidate/current, retrieves central evidence and checks for lost causal steps, ordinary life, Japanese/diagram detail and counterarguments. One mutable owner per register applies.

New sources require identity/scope review and do not extend a run. Add schema only for a demonstrated recurring responsibility, record migration and proportional affected-evidence backfill. Preserve mutable register history and frozen accepted readings/checkpoints; archive genuine superseded arguments with reciprocal authority metadata, not compressed replacements.
