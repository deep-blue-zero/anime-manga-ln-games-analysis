# Dr. STONE analytical method

## 1. Scope and interpretive balance

Adapt the [generic manga method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MANGA_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) for reported Japanese main V01–27 and the separate 科学王国事典 fanbook. The [architecture](DS_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) governs accumulation. No primary narrative or technical assertion has been inspected; no familiar cast, invention, event or adaptation may supply evidence.

This design can preserve technical explanations if they recur, but it is a literary method. Practical or causal reasoning, character motives, ordinary routines, group organization, directed relationships, Japanese expression and page form all retain full analytical responsibility. Do not reduce people to competence ratings or a volume to a list of mechanisms.

Record prior exposure before a future authorized run. A fresh session is not necessarily blind. Keep chapter/movement disclosure frontiers; later knowledge cannot repair an earlier subject's ignorance or make a hypothesis certain in retrospect.

## 2. Source and component admission

No narrative is authorized now. Future admission needs an OPEN precondition and explicit bounded run. Main keys `DS-JP-Vnn` and fanbook key `DS-SCIENCE-FANBOOK` bind exact immutable bytes, language/edition receipt and versioned spine/image map. Presence, transport, verification, admission, inspection and closure remain distinct. New bytes create a distinct witness until equivalence is established.

Numeric main order is a proposed source order, verified before use; publication, depicted chronology, disclosure and analyst admission are separate. Map actual chapters, covers, title/insert pages, narrative, diagrams, bonus/omake, notes, colophon and previews without arithmetic assumptions. Extras may carry later information.

The fanbook is a separate publication and source class. Classify profile/data, editorial explanation, technical assertion, creator statement/interview, retrospective commentary, art, narrative/bonus or unresolved components. “Official” does not mean every assertion has the same evidentiary status. Fanbook information cannot silently enter earlier main readings or modeled subject knowledge.

Source metadata credits names; attribution of a particular image, idea or intention needs specific evidence. Other editions and adaptations remain outside scope until separately identified/admitted.

## 3. Page inspection and evidence retrieval

Inspect selected pages in STILL_IMAGE, including silent pages, small lettering and diagrams. OCR/search is a retrieval aid, never coverage or quotation certification. Record REVIEWED_MATERIAL, NO_MATERIAL_ADDITION, DUPLICATE, EXCLUDED or UNRESOLVED_READABILITY with exact extent/reason. Container validity is not legibility or page completeness.

The locator pairs witness/version, verified chapter, one-based image occurrence including covers, visible printed page label if any, panel/balloon or diagram-region ordinal with immutable digest, spine/member and map version. State analyst ordering conventions and ambiguity. Crops retain coordinates and full-page context; spreads keep both images and verified pairing. Page-turn claims need actual facing layout.

Reopen exact pages for central visual, Japanese or diagram claims. Use EXACT_VERIFIED, WORKING_TRANSCRIPTION or PARAPHRASE; label glosses and interpretation. Preserve base/ruby, address, ellipsis, agency, modality and consequential terminology. Technical-looking words still require source transcription rather than memory.

Test central retrieval. A missing/failed route remains RETRIEVAL_UNRESOLVED and cannot support a settled core claim. Public Git holds eligible short quotations and sanitized locators; pages, bulk OCR, private IDs and acquisition details stay in evidence/working storage.

## 4. Explanation, demonstration and external truth

For a source-established mechanism or practical task, separate four layers:

1. A speaker/narrator's explanation or prediction.
2. What the panels actually depict as attempted action and result.
3. An analyst's inference about the fictional mechanism and its causal sufficiency.
4. Any independently sourced external fact-check, only if separately relevant and authorized.

Agreement between explanation and depicted success does not establish real-world validity or reveal every omitted step. A diagram may be schematic, pedagogical or metaphorical; identify its arrows, labels, scale and omissions before inferring a process. Do not reconstruct missing operations as if shown.

Track conditions, resources, tools, knowledge, labor, sequence and failure alternatives where source-established. Distinguish necessary, sufficient, enabling, correlated and unknown dependency relations. A character's confidence, a later success or a fanbook elaboration does not automatically validate an earlier causal claim.

If external checking is warranted, state the exact question, use appropriate primary references and retain EXTERNAL_REFERENCE status. External correctness does not settle a scene's literary function, motivation or fictional world rule. This bootstrap performs no scientific fact-check, experiment or procedural replication.

## 5. Causal action and subject knowledge

For every movement develop conditions → perception → appraisal → affect → options/constraints → choice/non-action → consequence, with unsupported links marked. A technical explanation is only one possible part of that chain. Consider rival causes, unintended consequences, incomplete information and who can act or refuse.

Keep narrator presentation, reader information and each subject's knowledge/belief separate. Track acquisition and disclosure time, mistakes, testimony and withheld information. A later capability cannot be projected backward as earlier competence. An assertion about a group's knowledge does not mean every member knows it.

A success can depend on coordination, resources, others' labor or context; failure can reflect constraints rather than stable incapacity. Preserve those alternative accounts. A useful mechanism may still have uncertain consequences or contested values; practical success does not prove endorsement.

## 6. Character, group, relationship and ordinary life

Create named subjects and groups only from admitted source evidence. Separate speech, visible behavior, represented thought, others' reports and role presentation. A drawn expression does not uniquely identify emotion; describe before diagnosing. Stable disposition, temporary condition, role, resources and acquired knowledge are distinct.

Ordinary work, rest, routine conversation, practical needs, play and low-stakes decisions are first-class evidence where present. They prevent dramatic accomplishment or crisis from becoming the whole character account. Negative evidence requires an actual opportunity, not merely an absent scene.

Relationships are directed A→B and B→A. Preserve knowledge, expectations, dependence, recognition, trust/conflict, power and obligation with source-earned dimensions. Contributions to a group do not alone establish affection, loyalty or consent. Group/institutional states distinguish stated rule, custom, observed practice/enforcement and participants' assumptions.

A proposed change uses DISPOSITION, KNOWLEDGE, RELATIONSHIP, CONTEXT, ROLE, RESOURCE, REVEALED_NOT_NEW or UNRESOLVED, with prior/new evidence and rivals. Retain relational and ordinary counterexamples rather than writing a uniformly improving competence narrative.

## 7. Japanese expression and graphic form

Attribute speaker, thinker, narrator and recipient from balloons, tails, placement and context. Distinguish technical explanation, joke, aside, thought, narrated statement and diegetic text. Preserve consequential Japanese wording and gloss alternatives, including whether a term is defined by the source or merely familiar to the analyst.

Written voice traces appraisal → objective → speech act → recipient/audience filter → register → words/lettering. Compare explanatory, ordinary and pressured contexts where present. Expertise does not erase relational register. Sound effects are drawn sound/state/manner conventions, not measured audio.

Analyze panel/page/spread organization, gutters, framing, reactions, silence, diagram-text integration, background detail and rendering changes. Explain feature → effect → alternative. A diagram's presence does not make its claim more reliable than surrounding testimony. Still images omit motion; page succession can be simultaneous viewpoints or schematic sequence.

Recurrence, tone, symbolism and thematic claims require variation and counterexamples. Preserve the interaction of page form and character/causal interpretation; do not treat visual analysis as an optional illustration to a technical summary.

## 8. Counterreadings, revision and ethics

Claims use OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, CONTRADICTION, EXTERNAL_REFERENCE, EVALUATION or UNRESOLVED, with confidence/reason. Each unit's developed thesis has a serious rival and disconfirmation condition.

Distinguish depiction, explanation, evaluation and endorsement, including where practical success and ethical judgment diverge. Do not prefilter contentious material or treat a successful outcome as retrospective justification. Describe only what the sourced analysis needs.

Classify apparent conflict by witness/page map, chronology, knowledge/report reliability, role/group constraints, schematic omission, fanbook layer, external-reference disagreement, interpretation, missing evidence or possible textual inconsistency. Preserve open alternatives. Revision uses PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT and OPEN, retaining old/new formulations, boundary, evidence, rival disposition and dependencies.

## 9. Complete future output and acceptance

Each accepted volume needs receipt/coverage; entering state and frozen expectations; developed thesis/rival; movement-level causal/page analysis; explanation-versus-demonstration checks where earned; subject/group/directed relationship changes; ordinary-life controls; Japanese/graphic language; page/diagram form; recurrence/counterexamples; claim transitions; synchronized five-register manifest; exit freeze; limits and completion decision.

No list of discoveries, mechanisms, plot outcomes or character traits substitutes for full reasoning. Preserve uncertainty, causal alternatives, linguistic detail and mundane relational evidence at the length needed. Technical expertise is not a reason to shorten literary analysis.

Before semantic acceptance, promotion or integration, sample introduction, a substantial middle section, conclusion/exit, all major relationships and ambiguous actor passages. Check names, purposeful roles, pronouns, concealment, speaker/thinker/recipient and knowledge boundary. Classify JUSTIFIED_ROLE_REFERENCE, SOURCE_REQUIRED_ANONYMITY, KNOWLEDGE_BOUNDARY_REQUIRED, UNNECESSARY_GENERICIZATION, AMBIGUOUS_REFERENT or INCORRECT_IDENTITY_OR_ATTRIBUTION. Record sample/findings and repair supported defects only.

Accept full prose and register changes atomically. Scoped no-change findings require actual inspection. Missing required pages, unreadable diagrams, broken central routes or absent updates remain holds.

## 10. Models and preserved history

Reconstruction is a later conditional model, separate from literary monographs and evidence. Policies include trigger/stakes/state, response family, inhibitors, exceptions, counterevidence, rivals and falsifiers. Distinguish disposition/temporary condition; trace appraisal → decision → recipient-conditioned expression. Confidence, evidence distance and abstention/multiple-response rules prevent unsupported extrapolation, including unfamiliar technical situations.

Freeze true held-out predictions before exposure; exposed work is RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST. Independent evaluation uses actual diagnostic evidence and baselines. Synthetic outputs never become primary evidence or canonical characterization.

Frozen readings preserve their historical knowledge boundary; later corrections use linked revisions/errata under [archive authority](../../governance/source-policies/ARCHIVE_AUTHORITY_AND_SUPERSESSION_POLICY.md). The [handoff contract](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md) requires complete outputs, causal/linguistic arguments, counterevidence and limits, not compressed substitutes.
