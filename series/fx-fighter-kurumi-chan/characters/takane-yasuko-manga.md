## Orientation

**Who.** 高根やす子 (Takane Yasuko) is a small, rabbit-obsessed, foul-mouthed young woman who lost money at FX before the story's present begins, hid it, warned her friends from that experience, left the market, and became a manga artist. She is the next-door neighbour and protective friend of 山師芽吹 (Mebuki), a wary and later candid acquaintance of 小金萌智子 (Mochiko), a worried friend of the protagonist 福賀くるみ (Kurumi), and in 2017 the old schoolmate and stock-market mentor of 善波なな (Nana). She is a supporting character: one night as an anonymous blogger in V01, a member of the four-woman circle in V02–V07, a voice in a chat in V08, and a recurring figure in V09–V10, where a flashback to 2013 finally explains her.

**Place in the story.** She is the series' person who learns from losing. The page shows her trading badly, then trading under rules, then breaking a rule once, cutting, abstaining, and leaving FX on the evidence of her own record; it shows her using her losses as the authority for warnings that her friends ignore; and it shows her moving the same appetite for a big bet into her art, where the downside is bounded. V10 then reveals that her caution has a biography: a forced liquidation of her savings in December 2013, out of which she invented the blogger persona the reader met first.

**Boundary and spoilers.** This portrait covers the accepted main narrative of V01–V10, ending at V10 i0156 (Monday 24 April 2017), including the V10 flashback to November–December 2013. It contains spoilers to the end of V10. Nothing later is used. Covers, flaps, afterwords, bonus strips and under-jacket pieces (one of which labels her handle) are paratext and are not evidence for her. Real market history and any knowledge of the title outside the admitted pages are excluded.

### The portrait in brief

Yasuko is blunt, loyal, poor, proud and quick to act for others. She speaks in a rough, masculine-leaning casual register with あたし, swears freely, mocks herself and pays for other people's food when she has almost none. She hides her own failures (her trading past, her hurt) and demands openness from others. Her most consistent act across ten volumes is going to a friend in danger and telling her the truth, at cost to herself. Her most consistent mental habit is to trust observed results over her own confidence ("信用すべきは… "現実"だ…!", V04 i0046). Her own decision record moves, within story time, from being liquidated (2013) to cutting fast (V03), leaving (V04) and holding unleveraged shares (2017). Her limits: she freezes when unwelcome news arrives with no time to think (V04 i0142–i0143); her softest warnings are dropped as soon as they are spoken (V06 i0147); she conceals more about herself than she ever asks of others. Her romantic and intimate life is never tested by the page.

## 1. Identity and circumstances

**Age and status.** "(20)" on both V01 (i0144) and V02 (i0074) captions; 23 in April 2017 (V09 i0142). In 2014 she is a 【フリーター】 with a part-time job (V02 i0074 "今日は仕事休みなん?" — "まぁね"; V03 i0096), not a student ("暇な大学生と違って", V03 i0139). She studied at a design 専門学校 — 新都デザイン専門学校 on the 2013 sign (V10 i0066) — and is a graduate ("専門卒だっけ?" — "そうだけど", V09 i0109). Nana was her younger schoolmate there (V10 i0066; V09 i0150). Her family is never shown or mentioned.

**Home.** A flat in an apartment block (the V01 establishing shot is a tall building at night, V01 i0144), next door to Mebuki's ("お隣同士", V02 i0074). Her room is a shrine to her own brand: YASUKO banners and signs, rabbits on every surface, a pink heart-backed chair (V01 i0144; V09 i0109–i0113), later a drawing board and tablet (V04 i0076; V06 i0100). In 2017 she works at Mochiko's company office as an outsider because construction next door makes her flat too noisy ("家だと隣の工事がうるさくてよぉ〜", V09 i0148).

**Appearance (embodied presentation).** Very small — taken for a primary-school pupil (V02 i0139) and, with no ID on her, for a schoolchild by the police (V04 i0125–i0126). Twin tails with rabbit hair clips and an X clip, a choker, a rabbit pendant, fingerless gloves, striped over-knee stockings, plaid mini-skirts, platform boots (V01 i0144; V02 i0074; V04 §H; V09 i0142). The kawaii styling and the gutter voice are a deliberate contrast that the page plays for comedy from the first page (V01 §K).

**Occupation over time.** 2013: design student who fails document screening for jobs (V10 i0067). 2013–2014: FX blogger with affiliate links nobody clicks (V02 i0003, i0009–i0010; V10 i0078–i0079). 2014: freeter; designer of "YASUKO" goods that did not sell (V02 i0151–i0152; V03 i0082); amateur manga for social media, begun by her own count about two months before 23 November 2014 (V05 i0123 "2カ月前から"; drawing at night with a how-to book, V03 i0139); a viral rabbit comic and a circle, "Rabbits" (V04 i0042, i0148). November 2014: sells 372 of 1000 printed copies at コミチア and receives an editor's card (V05 i0124, i0126). Winter 2014–2015: comic sales, a publisher's editor, illustration commissions including Mochiko's game art; "色々込みで100万円くらい" (V07 i0023–i0024, i0041). 2015: serial storyboards rejected, assistant work valued for speed, no debut (V09 i0086, i0110–i0111). April 2017: 【漫画家】; serialization of 「重力ウサギ」 in 「プラッパー」 decided (V09 i0142; V10 i0080, i0092). The magazine spread at V10 i0092 carries a small credit under the title lettered "YA好こ" (crop), which reads as a byline playing on her name (INFERENCE MODERATE; no page names her pen name, and the run records the pen name as unknown, V10-U15).

**Money.** In 2014 she lives on very little: a near-empty fridge and 「いつもカツカツ 梅干し茶漬け」 (V03 i0007, i0022), outings only where "お金がかからないとこ" (V03 i0080), "貸せるだけのお金もねーけど…!" (V04 i0093). Her FX account holds 76,573円 at the contest base (box, V02 i0159), 71,052円 after her V03 losses (box, V03 i0155; V04 i0047), all withdrawn by Christmas 2014 ("全額出金済み", V07 i0011). By 2017 she has held shares since the previous autumn ("去年の秋から", V10 i0091), among them shares of her own publisher, with no figure printed (V10 i0092). The reconciliation of her figures is in the [source-completion and longitudinal reconciliation](../audits/FX_KURUMI_SOURCE_COMPLETION_AND_LONGITUDINAL_RECONCILIATION.md), chain E (§4.6).

## 2. Personal history: what happened, when the page showed it, and who knew

The series shows her before it names her, and names her before it explains her. Her history therefore has to be read in two orders: story time, and the order in which the reader learns it.

**Story time.**
1. **5 November 2013 (shown in V10, i0066–i0069).** At a job fair at her 専門学校 she says she was cut at document screening ("書類選考の時点で落とされてる", "世間に需要ないんだろうな…", i0067). She decides on the spot: "あたし / 今日から FX始めるわ!" (i0068). Her reasons are fear and appetite in plain words — without a design job she may be "一生フリーターで 貧乏人", while another income would give her "健康で 文化的な生活" and "美味しい ご飯が / 毎日" (i0068–i0069) (SELF_REPORT HIGH; FXK-V10-E031). Nana, invited to join, declines (i0069).
2. **14 November 2013 (V10 i0070–i0073).** "1週間で / 3万円 稼げた…!" after a plan of 1万 a month (i0070); the pound at "ちょうど 160円" looks like a ceiling, "天井タッチ!" (i0071); "3枚売り 発射!" with a planned take-profit at 158 for "6万円" (i0072); she treats Nana to fries before any take-profit on the short is shown, though she had already made 3万 that week (i0073) (FXK-V10-E032; anticipatory spending INFERENCE LOW-MODERATE, V10 §G.4).
3. **3 December 2013 (V10 i0074–i0079).** The pound rises about nine yen to about 169 against her short; 「強制ロスカットのお知らせ」 closes it; "あたしの / 全財産…" (i0075); a long black candle falls just after the liquidation: "あたしを… / 殺すためだけに 上げたのか…!?" (i0076). The loss figure is not printed and the lot unit of her 2013 broker is not established (FXK-V10-E033; [FXK-V10-C022](../FX_KURUMI_DECISION_AND_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md)). In the cafeteria she curses the "殺人通貨!!", admits weeks of chart-watching instead of job-hunting (i0077), and hits on the blog: affiliate income from FX posts. Haloed and winged in her own fantasy, she names herself "FX堕天使 あふぃちゃん" (i0079) (FXK-V10-E034).
4. **December 2013 – spring 2014 (gap).** Unshown. She keeps trading as a blogger: the V01 night and the V02 blog prove it. That she funded an account again after the December liquidation is INFERENCE HIGH, not shown; the pages print the 2013 "全財産" and the 2014 balance of 76,573円, not whether the 2014 account is a new one, and the origin of the 76,573 is never printed (reconciliation §4.6; V02-U05 ADVANCED, not resolved). (The ledger's V10 note states the re-funding as shown; this portrait follows the reconciliation's grade.)
5. **V01, one night (i0144–i0152; caption 「少し時は遡る」).** As あふぃちゃん she holds an AUD/JPY long bought high, in the 95円 range, through a fall ("豪ドル円を取引する投資家", narrator, i0144; FXK-V01-D029), cuts on fear ("これは本気で 逃げないと死ぬ", i0148) — the narrator names the cut as her difference from Kurumi, who did not cut (NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, FXK-V01-D029) — and then announces a "全力" reversal into a short — "豪ドル円 ドテン売り! 全力だッ!" — framed as "取り返す チャンス" and drawn as her own winged, haloed vision of the future, striking the keys as she says it (i0149), with a vow to retire if it fails ("これが失敗したら もうFXは引退!", i0150; V01 §H.3). No fill is drawn: that the short was executed is INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH (V01 §F.2, FXK-V01-E033). The market reverses: "上げンなら 下げンなやァ! クソがァ!" (i0152). The result of the short is not shown (FXK-V01-D029; V01 §H.3).
6. **Late May 2014 (V02 i0003, i0009–i0011).** Her blog post 「大損〜豪ドル編〜」 announces "もうFXは引退だぁ(泣)" (i0003); she drinks alone among cans and laments that nobody clicks her links (i0009–i0010). Unknown to her, Mochiko reads the blog for amusement and posts an encouraging comment under the false name "ともこ" to keep her trading (i0010–i0011; FXK-V02-C008). No page shows Yasuko learning who "ともこ" was.
7. **June – 15 July 2014 (V02).** Named (i0074); warns Mebuki against FX in exact terms while denying she knows or does it (i0075–i0083, i0147); stung by a comparison, announces aloud that she will trade FX under stated rules (i0156), privately resolves to watch over Mebuki (i0157), and joins the contest Mebuki then proposes while privately refusing to compete (i0157–i0160); her account stands at 76,573円 (i0159). *The contest*, which recurs through V07: Mebuki's idea (V02 i0157–i0160), with rules written by Kurumi (sheet "作 くるみ", V03 i0086–i0088) — it runs to 25 December, is decided by percentage growth from each FX account's starting balance, and the winner treats everyone; withdrawals count, open positions are valued at market and false reports are banned (V06 i0141–i0144). Yasuko ends 7.2% down on FX but is crowned on Mochiko's reading of the clause "投資で増やすこと" and pays (V07 i0011–i0026; reconciliation §4.9).
8. **Late July – October 2014 (V03).** "また戻ってきてしまった…" (i0003): she returns to her trading app; one trade, a fast cut, an announced doten not taken, small further losses, then abstention (§4).
9. **November 2014 onward.** Leaves FX (V04 i0046–i0047; executed by V07 i0011), becomes a manga artist (V05–V10), and in 2017 holds shares (V10 i0091–i0092).

**Reading order, and what each disclosure changed.**
- V01 shows the blogger with no past and no name: she is the narrator's foil ("彼女も くるみと同様に", V01 i0144) and the illustrated subject of a prospect-theory explainer (V01 i0146–i0147).
- V02 names her and shows a person who talks like an expert and claims to be a non-trader. Mochiko privately reads her as a "相場経験者" from her phrasing (V02 i0156). The reader can identify her with the blogger from the caption; the other characters cannot.
- V03–V04 let her own words leak the past. Freezing over a doten, she thinks "前と同じで / ここが底に なるのでは…?" over a faint overlay of her V01 lines (V03 i0019; the reading records the overlay as a memory of her V01 doten). To Mebuki she starts "実はあたし ずっと前から FXやってて——" and is cut off (V03 i0142). Her memory collage includes the blogger's V01 trades among her own (V04 i0046; [FXK-V04-C018](../FX_KURUMI_DECISION_AND_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md), OBSERVATION HIGH on the crop). She tells Mebuki outright "そもそも本当に 前からFX やっていたし / 再開も最終的に あたしが 決めたんだ" (V04 i0136). Mebuki's uptake is not shown.
- V05 breaks the secret in public: a fan at her コミチア booth asks "「FX堕天使 あふぃちゃん」 ですよね!!", having matched her blog and social-media photos, and says the blog has not been updated lately (V05 i0110). She does not deny it ("な…なぜ それを…"). Mochiko, standing beside her, drives the fan off; whether Mochiko registers the identity is never shown (V05-U22 OPEN; FXK-V05-C025, C057).
- V10 supplies the origin (2013). Nothing in it changes what she did in V01–V09; it gives existing traits a history. It is disclosure, not development (qualification audit §1.5). Three earlier words now have a source: her V02 slang "ポンドって 殺人通貨じゃねーか!" (V02 i0082) repeats her 2013 curse at the pound (V10 i0077) (INFERENCE HIGH by identical wording); "これは経験者からの忠告——" (V03 i0141) and "芽吹には あたしと同じ 失敗をさせたくない" (V03 i0142) now have a dated failure behind them. The reconciliation notes that the flashback supplies a possible content for her "同じ失敗", not a recorded one (§7, V03-U08).

**Who knew her past.** Nana witnessed the 2013 liquidation ("あんな やす子ちゃん 見たくなかったな…", Nana's inner line, V10 i0080; her aversion to FX comes from that sight, INFERENCE HIGH, FXK-V10-E035) and knows her school days, which Nana in turn asks her to keep quiet (V09 i0150). Mebuki was told in V03–V04 that she had traded "ずっと前から", first disbelieved ("そういう ウソはいいよ", V03 i0142). Mochiko suspected (V02 i0156), witnessed the outing (V05 i0110), and by Christmas 2014 jokes "どう? やす子も もう一度?" (V06 i0147). Kurumi is never shown learning of the blog or of 2013. No friend is shown hearing the 2013 story told; apart from Nana, who saw it happen, only the reader holds it, and Study B ("Obligations, relationships and where the cost of a choice lands", [studies/FX_KURUMI_OBLIGATIONS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COST.md](../studies/FX_KURUMI_OBLIGATIONS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COST.md)) draws the consequence that her warnings rested on an experience the people she warned never learned the extent of.

## 3. Personality and psychology

**Values.** Three values recur in her own words and acts.
- *Friendship as something you show up for.* "友達がアホしてるのに 止めに来ないやつがいるかよ!" (V04 i0132); "友達は 大切だぞ?", "あたしは 芽吹を 失いたくねぇ", "芽吹は この世に ひとりしか いねーんだよ" (V05 i0138). The page backs the last line with a collage of her memories of Mebuki (V05 i0138), as it backed her review of her trades with a collage of her losses (V04 i0046).
- *Owning one's choices, without letting that end care.* She refuses Mebuki's apology for drawing her into FX: "あたしの 責任だよ" (V04 i0136). She concedes that Mebuki's borrowing is "芽吹の 自己責任" (V05 i0133) and in the same breath claims the right to know the lender as a person because Mebuki is "大切な 友達" (V05 i0134). Responsibility, for her, is a reason not to blame others, never a reason not to help ([FXK-V04-C015](../FX_KURUMI_DECISION_AND_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md), revised by FXK-V05-C041; latest transition FXK-SYN-C013, the meanings of responsibility).
- *Openness between friends.* Her grievance against Mochiko is not the loan but the concealment: "お前は 自分の考えは 話しても / 自分のことは いっさい 話さないよな" (V05 i0135). The irony that she herself had hidden her trading past for three volumes is on the page and is not remarked on by anyone (§13).

**Goals.** Durable and concrete. To be a designer with her own brand (V02 i0151–i0152; "あたし本当は デザイナー志望 なんです…", V05 i0123); then, on an editor's advice, a professional manga artist (V05 i0124; "今は もうプロの 漫画描きを 目指してるんだ", V06 i0147); and throughout, enough money for decent food and a life that is not poor (V10 i0068–i0069; V05 i0121 "お金を… 稼ぎたい… から…です…"). Food is the unit in which she imagines money: a 1枚 trade will "grade up" her 梅干し茶漬け to a tempura bowl (V03 i0007); +322円 becomes dessert (V03 i0009).

**Fears.** Poverty first: the earliest motive the page gives her is fear of being "一生フリーターで 貧乏人" (V10 i0068). Then the fear of repeating her own failure — the arm frozen in mid-click over "前と同じで" (V03 i0019) — and the fear of losing a friend to FX, which Kurumi's account of her mother's death sharpened ("くるみの 話を聞いてから 改めて思ったよ", V05 i0138). In V09 she imagines Kurumi, in debt, drifting into illicit work (an explainer-video list headed 「闇バイト TOP10」, V09 i0090).

**Vulnerabilities.** Pride wounded by comparison: Mebuki's tactless "儲かってないのやす子ちゃんだけ" (V02 i0154) produces a private spiral — "ほんと… なんで あたしだけ 負けてん だろうな…", "あたしは 違うのに", "ムカつくぜ…!" (V02 i0155) — while aloud she says only "わかったよ…"; on the next page she announces that she will trade FX, under rules (i0156) (SELF_REPORT HIGH in thought). The contest is Mebuki's idea, proposed after that (i0157). Self-doubt about her own competence: "私がヘタクソなだけで 本当は勝てる ゲームなのか…?" (V03 i0093, thought). A need for encouragement she does not voice: alone after Mochiko pushes her to see editors, "優しさが ほしい… 一言 だけでも…!" (V05 i0113). Being dismissed hurts her visibly when no one is watching: after Mebuki calls her a liar, she sits curled in her chair gripping her wrist, "バカが…" (V03 i0143).

**Contradictions the page keeps.**
- The rule-maker who calls the others gamblers ("どうせ全員 ハイレバで ギャンブルしてるんだろ?", V02 i0155; "あたしは ギャンブルなんて しねーぞ", V02 i0156) is the blogger who, in V01, announced a full-size reversal to win back a loss and struck the keys for it (execution INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH; the announced intent alone makes the contrast) (FXK-V02-C017, INFERENCE MODERATE; revised as mixed by FXK-V03-C030 because her V03 conduct is genuinely restrained).
- The person who demands disclosure (V05 i0135) concealed her own past from the same circle (V02 i0076, i0083, i0147).
- The sniper who fights "勝てるときだけ" (V03 i0005) opens her first V03 trade at about 17× and says so herself ("たった1枚でも ハイレバ取引に なっちまう", V03 i0007) — her account is too small for any size below the broker's minimum lot (box: 76,573円; 25× maximum; 1枚 ≈1,370,250円).
- The kawaii brand and the gutter mouth (V01 §K) — a contrast of presentation, not of values.

**Coping.** Four habits. Self-mockery and hyperbole that make her ordeal a joke ("このペンだこちゃんが 悲しみの咆哮をあげてるぜ…", V04 i0040; comic asides during the night search, V04 i0133). Action rather than rumination when someone else is in danger (V04 i0077–i0094). Food, drink and company: drinking alone among cans after the AUD loss (as あふぃちゃん, V02 i0010), ringing Mebuki's bell for a shopping trip after her V03 cut (V03 i0023). Re-targeting after rejection: two editors fail her and she says "次は 4コマ系を 攻めるぞ!" on the spot (V05 i0116).

**Self-conception.** In V02 she sees herself as "違う" — not a gambler, a rule-keeper (V02 i0155–i0156). By V03 she speaks as an "経験者" (V03 i0141). In V04 she decides that her own confidence is the least trustworthy thing she has (V04 i0046). At Christmas 2014 she draws a line under a former self: "FXやってる あたしとは バイバイだよ" (V06 i0147). In 2017 she will not be called 先生 (V10 i0081, reported by Kurumi), and in 2013 she had cast herself, half in jest, as a fallen angel (V10 i0079). Across all of these she presents herself as someone who has lost and says so; what she will not present is the size of the loss.

## 4. Cognition and decision-making

**What she notices.** Other people's danger, early and specifically. From Mebuki's claim of a 15万 gain on a 20万 start she infers high leverage ("てめー ハイレバで 運ゲーしてやがんな…?", V02 i0078–i0079); from "held since September" she infers that Mebuki held through the rise ("上がる前からじゃねーか…!", V03 i0141); she sees "冷静じゃなくなってる" in her (V03 i0141); she notices that Mebuki has grown "闇深く" in a group chat (V06 i0099) and that "あいつも変わったよなぁ" (V06 i0140); she remembers that Kurumi held Swiss-franc exposure when the franc news breaks (V07 i0135). She also notices what Mochiko does not say about herself (V05 i0135). What she notices least is a slow, structural risk in a friend's arrangement when it is told to her quickly: the terms of Mebuki's loan pass her by in the moment (V04 i0139–i0142).

**How she reasons.** Concretely and from cases, mostly her own. Her V02 warnings are a compact theory of the game drawn from experience: leverage turns trading into luck ("運ゲー"); winners cannot see it ("勝ってるうちはわかんねーよ"); one loss creates the urge to win it back, stakes grow, and that is "破産RTAのスタート" (V02 i0079–i0081). Her V04 decision is an explicit inference from her own record: "今まで自信がある トレードで 成功したこと あったか…!?" → "あたしの"自信"なんて… 一番信用しちゃ いけないんじゃ ないか…!?" → "信用 すべきは この漫画に 反応がある という"現実"だ…!" (V04 i0046) (SELF_REPORT HIGH; FXK-V04-E024, [FXK-V04-C017](../FX_KURUMI_DECISION_AND_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md)). Her heuristics are simple and visible: a round number as a ceiling in 2013 ("ちょうど 160円か…", "天井タッチ!", V10 i0071); minimum size and low-volatility pairs in 2014 (V02 i0156; V03 i0006–i0007); in 2017, for herself, shares bought in cash to support something she is part of, held without leverage and without a profit target (V10 i0092), and, for a friend, shares chosen by affection — "気持ち的に 買いやすい" (V10 i0093). The two are kept apart below: her own purchase is support, superstition and luck; the affinity rule is advice to Nana.

**How she prioritizes.** A friend's safety over her own comfort and standing (V03 i0141–i0142; V04 i0077–i0132); survival over pride when she trades ("これ絶対 買値まで 戻らないやつじゃん! 損切りだよ 損切り!", V03 i0016); art over FX once the evidence turns ("FXで死ぬよりマシだろ!", V04 i0047). Her own interest in money is real but modest in scale and tied to food and stability.

**Under uncertainty.** Three responses appear, each conditioned.
- *With her own money at stake after a loss*: an announced chase ("次で 取り返す…", "相場を ATMに したい…!", "全力! ドテン売…!", V03 i0017–i0018) that she does not execute — the arm stays raised and frozen as "前と同じで" surfaces (V03 i0019); she then watches the price fall without her and curses (V03 i0021) ([FXK-V03-C014](../FX_KURUMI_DECISION_AND_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md): OBSERVATION HIGH for the cut and the abstention, INFERENCE HIGH that the doten was not executed). The reading's judgment is "learned fear, not learned discipline" (EVALUATION MODERATE) — at this stage.
- *With a view but no position*: abstention. In October 2014 she thinks a rise likely and says so ("底から1円以上 反発ってるし 新規売りの ほうが怖くないか?", V03 i0139), yet holds nothing (V03 i0155–i0156).
- *With unwelcome news delivered fast*: silence, then self-reproach ("…なにも 言えなかった…", V04 i0143).

**Conflicting obligations.** The page tests her once, hard. On the train, after a night spent finding Mebuki, she hears that Mochiko is lending Mebuki 2,000,000円 so that she can keep trading (V04 i0139). She knows the lender and the sum; she does not know the rate, the conditions, the family security, the salon work or that Mochiko holds the opposite position ([FXK-V04-C024](../FX_KURUMI_DECISION_AND_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md)). Mebuki has just been reconciled with her; the train is moving; she starts "あ… あのさ…" and is cut off as the train comes into the station (i0141); she says only "面接がんばってな", Mebuki gets off, and Yasuko, still aboard, thanks her from the doorway (i0142). Only after the doors close, alone on the train, does the judgment form — "…なにも 言えなかった…", "借りる 芽吹も アホだけど… / 貸すのは 違うだろ…", and, fist on the door, "そこは 止めろよ…! / 萌智子…!" (V04 i0143) (FXK-V04-C014; V04 E078–E079) — said to no one. The reading's account — a failure to act in the moment on partial information, under time pressure, not indifference (V04 §I) — fits everything she does next: she arranges to meet Mochiko within days (V04 i0147–i0149) and confronts her (V05 i0129–i0139). A rival account — that she held back out of respect for Mebuki's right to decide — has some support in her later concession of Mebuki's 自己責任 (V05 i0133), but she never says so, and her thought at i0143 is self-reproach, not principle (§13).

**Her decision record, in story order.** The decision episodes run: 2013 — enter from fear of poverty; escalate after an early win; short a round-number "ceiling"; hold through about nine yen to a broker's forced close of everything (V10 i0068–i0076). V01 (as あふぃちゃん) — cut a losing long on fear, then reverse at full size to win it back (V01 i0148–i0149; announced, execution INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH). V02 — state rules aloud: low leverage, low-volatility currencies (V02 i0156); privately, no competing (i0160). V03 — break the leverage rule by necessity on one minimum lot, cut by hand at −2,892円 (box, V03 i0016), announce a chase and freeze, lose a little more off-page (to 71,052円 by 19 September; −2,629 by ANALYST CALCULATION from the boxes), then stay out (V03 i0155–i0156). V04 — review her record and leave FX, putting her balance "全額" into her comic (V04 i0046–i0047; withdrawal reported at V07 i0011). V05 — print 1000 copies of a comic where "30部売れたら いいほう", calling it "あたしの 全力投資" because "爆死しても FXと違って モノが残るし" (V05 i0106) ([FXK-V05-C023](../FX_KURUMI_DECISION_AND_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md)). V09 — "FX再開しようとか思ったりしないの?" — "まったく 思わんけど?", but "まったく 投資を しないのも / 違う気が してるん だよなぁ… / FXは やらんけど!" (V09 i0112–i0113). 2017 — in shares "去年の秋から" (since autumn 2016, her answer to Nana, V10 i0091); cash shares of her publisher bought when her serial was decided (whether that was her first purchase is not stated), "応援の 気持ちでな!", with a superstition that a shareholder will not be cancelled, profit "ラッキー程度", "現物取引だから 強制ロスカも ないし / 気長にやる感じで" (V10 i0092) ([FXK-V10-C026](../FX_KURUMI_DECISION_AND_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md)).

**What Study C's argument means for her.** Study C ("Risk, loss and financial self-destruction across persons", [studies/FX_KURUMI_RISK_LOSS_AND_FINANCIAL_SELF_DESTRUCTION.md](../studies/FX_KURUMI_RISK_LOSS_AND_FINANCIAL_SELF_DESTRUCTION.md)) reads her record against the series' other traders. Its arguments that bear on her are summarized here; cross-person comparisons are Study C's, and the page anchors are this portrait's:
- *She belongs to the series' ruin pattern before she leaves it.* Her 2013 short — leveraged, without a stop, held past where she could afford it, ended by the broker, taking all her savings — is, on Study C's account, one of four instances of the same visible sequence (with Kurumi, Mebuki and Kozue (梢), Kurumi's mother, named from V06 i0065, whose ruin V06 depicts). Study C argues that the drivers differ by person and that hers is the least differentiated of the four, because 2013 is shown briefly and late. On the page: a modest plan overtaken by an early win and a round-number ceiling (V10 i0070–i0072), with no specific danger named before the rise — her only word on risk is a general "リスクは あるけどさ…" when she decides to start (V10 i0068). The monograph takes from this only that her 2013 motive should not be over-read: fear of poverty, early reward and a ceiling judgment are what the page shows.
- *Her decision rule changes, within story time, toward realizing losses* — liquidated (2013), cutting but chasing (V01), cutting and freezing (V03), leaving on evidence (V04), unleveraged shares (2017). Study C argues that hers is the only such change among the series' traders. In reading order the sequence is inverted: the reader meets the cautious Yasuko first and the reckless one last, so V10 gives her caution a history after the fact.
- *Her restraint is learned, not temperament.* Study C weighs the rival that she is simply a cautious type and finds it contradicted by 2013, where the same person is shown without restraint. On her own record the restraint follows losses and is carried by evidence she reads off that record (V04 i0046): the rules she stated aloud in V02, before her next trade (i0156), broke on that trade by the necessity of a minimum lot (V03 i0007); what held afterwards was abstention after the V03 cut, the V04 exit and cash-only shares in 2017.
- *Exhaustion, not judgment, ended her 2013 chain.* "あたしの / 全財産…" (V10 i0075) is the end of that funding chain; nothing beyond it is shown. What changes afterwards is not a funding constraint but her rule.
These are Study C's arguments; this monograph neither decides its general claims nor restates its tests, and where the published study's wording differs, the study governs.

## 5. Behavioural patterns: ordinary conduct, cooperation, conflict, stress, mistakes

**Ordinary conduct.** Most of her pages are not about money. She works part-time, draws at night, designs goods and hair clips (V03 i0082), plays arcade dance games well (V02 i0075–i0079), drinks beer at dinner (V05 i0133–i0136; V06 i0142), hosts and cooks — at the Christmas yakiniku she runs the grill with garlic prepped in olive oil and serves with "喰ってみろい" (V06 i0145–i0146; FXK-V06-C025). Her flat is where the group gathers whether she likes it or not ("なんでウチが タコパ会場に なってんだよっ!!" — "だってここが大学から一番近いんだもん", V09 i0109). She studies what her comic needs, even general relativity from videos (V09 i0087). In 2017 she camps at Mochiko's office, is told she is an outsider staff may ignore, and pleads "冷たいこと 言わないでくれよ" (V09 i0148).

**Generosity on little money.** She treats Nana to fries in 2013 before any take-profit on her short is shown, with 3万 already made that week ("今日は あたしが / 奢って やる からさ!", V10 i0073); offers to pay for Mebuki at the game centre (V04 i0061); pays for dinner with Mochiko from the day's sales, "今日は あたしの 奢りだ", before confronting her (V05 i0128) — the poorer friend paying first, a claim to standing (EVALUATION MODERATE, V05 E043). At the same time she expects never to be the one who treats when winners pay: "あたしは 絶対 おごられる ほうだしなー!" (V06 i0141), and the joke "私は負けてる!" beside her confident pose at the contest meeting (V03 i0088) turns on the same rule. When the contest (rules in §2, item 7) makes her "winner" on Mochiko's reading of "投資で増やすこと", she pays the whole Christmas bill, protesting, "カネ…ナイヨ…" (V07 i0021–i0026).

**Cooperation.** She helps in kind and in person: she relays Mebuki's message to the group ("来るってさ", V06), sells at her own booth with Mochiko's help (V05 i0104–i0112), delivers commissioned illustrations fast enough that Mochiko remarks on it (V07 i0041), and in 2017 brings Nana into Mochiko's company ("やす子ちゃんに もちもち企画を 紹介されて / 脱ニートした", V10 i0088). She takes advice when it is specific and honest (the editor 毒島☆寿美, V05 i0121–i0124) and rejects advice that ignores what she loves (a critic who tells her to draw cats rather than rabbits, V03 i0156; she vetoes a cat hero again in V06 i0099).

**Conflict.** She fights for someone else more readily than for herself. With Mebuki she argues directly and loses the argument (V03 i0141–i0142). With Mochiko she plans the confrontation — a pretext of selling at コミチア and an explicit wish to talk in person (V04 i0147–i0149) — thanks her first for the day's help (V05 i0127), pours her beer, admits she is being "イジワル" on purpose because she is angry about Mebuki, and presses until Mochiko drops her soft register: "いらないんだよ… 友達とか なれあいとか…!" (V05 i0136–i0137) (FXK-V05-C028). She ends with a threat on Mebuki's behalf ("芽吹に もしもが あったら 絶対 許さねぇ からな", V05 i0139) and a concession of goodwill ("萌智子のこと 少しは知れて よかったよ"). On her own behalf she is quicker to bristle than to fight: to a flattering editor who rejects her, a private "下げんなら 上げんなや クソがぁ…!" (V05 i0116); to Mochiko's needle about her "投資経験", a private "こいつ…!" (V04 i0053).

**Stress responses.** Under fear for someone else: movement. Unable to reach Mebuki after Kurumi's warning, she pounds on her door, calls, and searches the city through the night (V04 i0077–i0130). Under her own losses: comic rage at the market (V01 i0151–i0152; V10 i0076–i0077), food and drink (as あふぃちゃん, V02 i0010; V03 i0022–i0023), then a pause. Under social hurt: retreat into private speech ("ムカつくぜ…!", V02 i0155; "バカが…", V03 i0143). Under fear of an adult stranger: stammered politeness (V09 i0095).

**Mistakes and corrections.** Her mistakes are of three kinds. Trading errors she corrects by rule: the V01 full-size reversal (announced; execution INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH) is followed by the V02 rules; the V03 leverage breach by a fast cut and abstention (V03 i0016, i0156); her whole record by exit (V04 i0046–i0047). A moral failure in the moment — silence when the loan is disclosed — corrected within days by a confrontation (V04 i0142–i0149; V05 i0129–i0139). And misjudgments of people: she assumes "さすがに 萌智子も 貸さんだろ…" (V04 i0093) and is wrong; she takes Kurumi's father, seen with Kurumi at a restaurant, for a paid "パパ活" partner and confronts him together with Mebuki, then apologizes when he says "父ですが… なにか?" (V09 i0095–i0097; FXK-V09-C020).

**Meaningful exceptions.** She keeps a declared exit from FX for the whole of V04–V10, after declared exits that did not hold (the V01 vow, the V02 blog retirement, "また戻ってきてしまった…", V03 i0003). Her softest act of care is also her weakest: at Christmas, told that Kurumi has made "1000は 超えてる" this year, she asks whether that is not enough and says only "なんか色々 あったみたい だし? / 引き際… とかさ…" (V06 i0147); the suggestion is dropped at once (V06 §H.4), and when Kurumi's 300-lot position comes up at the same table a little later (V06 i0148–i0153) she is not shown returning to it. It is the only spoken prompt to Kurumi to stop at that table (qualification record), and it is unpressed. Under the contest's no-lies rule she dodges the question of her comic income until Mochiko makes her own it (V07 i0021–i0024).

## 6. Speech and communication

**Self-reference.** あたし, from the first page to the last and in every register: in the blog days ("あたしは この損切りと 引き換えに…", V01 i0149), in 2013 ("あたし / 今日から FX始めるわ!", V10 i0068), in polite speech to an editor ("あたし本当は デザイナー志望 なんです…", V05 i0123) and to the police ("あたし大人なので…", V04 i0126), and in 2017 (V10 i0091–i0092).
- *The four lettered 私.* The kanji 私 is lettered for her four times, and every instance sits outside an ordinary speech balloon: unballooned bold lettering beside her at the contest meeting, "私は負けてる!" (V03 i0088), on a page that uses the same lettering for Mebuki's inner lines; a thought balloon with a bubble tail, "私がヘタクソなだけで…" (V03 i0093); a jagged mutter-burst while she stomps alone down a corridor, "私のメッセージは 既読スルー しやがって!" (V04 i0077); and a small hand-lettered aside beside her spoken encouragement to Mebuki, "私もそう だったぜ?" (V04 i0137). None carries furigana. Her balloon speech on the same pages uses no other self-reference, and her polite balloon speech keeps あたし. The pattern is verified on all four pages; what it means is UNRESOLVED (V04-U10). The likeliest reading is an orthographic variant of the same pronoun in non-balloon lettering, or an unguarded inner form (HYPOTHESIS LOW-MODERATE); nothing supports a shift of register in her public speech.
- *ウチ.* The ledger and the V09 reading list ウチ as a V09 self-reference. On the page, "なんでウチが タコパ会場に なってんだよっ!!" (V09 i0109) uses ウチ for her home — the place that has become the party venue (INFERENCE HIGH from the sentence); a later "まぁうちは 別に いいけどよぉ" on the same page can be read as "my place" or "I". This portrait does not count ウチ as an established first-person form.

**Register.** A rough, masculine-leaning plain style, constant from 2013 to 2017: sentence-final ねー / ねぇ, だろ, じゃねーか, ぜ; verb forms like しやがって, 切っちまえ, しねーし, 喰ってみろい; vocatives おい and あのなぁ; expletives クソ, くっそ, ちくしょう, バカが, どアホ, バカタレ (V01 §K; V02 §H.4; V04 §H.4; V06 §H.4; V09 §K; V10 §K). In V01 the lettering adds katakana emphasis and stretched vowels ("上げンなら 下げンなやァ! クソがァ!", "勘弁して くれよぉ…!", V01 i0144, i0152). The roughness sits against her kawaii styling, and the V04 reading notes that form and content run inverted between her and Mochiko: her rough voice carries care, Mochiko's soft voice carries pressure (V04 §K, INFERENCE MODERATE).

**Shifts by audience and pressure.**
- *Polite under evaluation, authority or fear.* To two seniors she has come to confront: "や… やす子です…", "FXは しませんので お手柔らかに…" (V02 i0147), while privately "超怖ぇン ですけど…!". To police who take her for a child: "いや…あたし大人なので…", "今は持ってないです…" (V04 i0126). To an editor: です/ます, honest and apologetic, ending "迷惑ですよね! … 失礼しました!" (V05 i0121–i0123). To Kurumi's father, believed a stranger: "し…しししし 失礼ですが / くく…くるみさんと どどのような ごごご関係ですか?" (V09 i0095) — the stammer and the honorific くるみさん appear only here.
- *Bare and rough with intimates.* "芽吹", "お前", "てめー" to Mebuki (V02 i0075–i0083); "あいつ" of her (V06 i0140); "萌智子" in thought by V04 (i0093, i0143) and to her face from V05 (i0134); "くるみ" (V06 i0147; V07 i0135; V09 i0119); "ばなな" for Nana, in 2013 and 2017 (V10 i0066; V09 i0150). "社長〜!!" for Mochiko in 2017 (V09 i0143).
- *Cowed once by a junior.* When Nana asks her not to talk about their school days at the office, she answers only "お…おう…" (V09 i0150).

**Vocabulary and rhetorical habits.**
- *Trader and gamer jargon, used precisely*: ハイレバ, 低レバ, ボラ, ドテン, リバウンド, 新規売り, 発射 for placing an order (V03 i0008; in 2013, "3枚売り 発射!", V10 i0072), 強制ロスカ (V10 i0092); クソゲー, 運ゲー, 破産RTA (V02 i0077–i0081).
- *Death idiom for money*: "逃げないと死ぬ" (V01 i0148), "殺人通貨" (V02 i0082; V10 i0077), "ははは… 死んだ…", "殺すためだけに 上げたのか…!?" (V10 i0076).
- *FX words moved into art and food*: "全力投資", "爆死" for a print run (V05 i0106), "爆益" (V05), "うまさに レバレッジ… 5倍" for garlic (V06 i0146).
- *Mock-heroic*: 風林火山 and "動かざること あたしでないし" (V01 i0149); "FX堕天使", "ポチポチ革命だ!!" (V10 i0079).
- *Hyperbole as affection and self-mockery*: "超スーパーウルトラ グレートデリシャス ワンダフルウルトラ ボンバーどアホ…!" on finding Mebuki safe (V04 i0131); her "ペンだこちゃん" (V04 i0040).
- *A formula she reuses*: her V01 curse at the market, "上げンなら 下げンなやァ!" (V01 i0152), returns inverted at an editor who praised and then rejected her, "下げんなら 上げんなや クソがぁ…!" (V05 i0116) (OBSERVATION of the wording; INFERENCE MODERATE that the echo is designed).

**Humour and silence.** Most of her humour is at her own expense, and much of it is drawn rather than said (tiny figure, gag faces, asides). Her silences are few and telling: "…" when she resolves to watch over Mebuki (V02 i0157); no word at all when the loan is disclosed (V04 i0142); trailing sentences when what she means is serious ("引き際… とかさ…", V06 i0147); a confession cut off mid-line ("ずっと前から FXやってて——", V03 i0142).

**Inner and outer speech.** Her outward speech is direct about other people's danger and about her opinions; what she keeps inside is about herself. Wounded pride is in thought while she says only "わかったよ…" (V02 i0155); the decision to watch over Mebuki is in thought (V02 i0157); self-doubt (V03 i0093), hurt (V03 i0143), self-reproach and moral anger (V04 i0143) and the wish for "優しさ" (V05 i0113) are all unspoken. The reverse also occurs: her retort to a vain editor, "わかってる からだろ…", is private (V05 i0118). What she thinks about other people she mostly also says to them; the gap between inner and outer speech opens over herself (EVALUATION MODERATE).

**Written voice.** The blog title and post — 「†FX堕天使† あふぃちゃんのFX日記」, 「大損〜豪ドル編〜」, "もうFXは引退だぁ(泣)" (V02 i0003) — show a performed, self-dramatizing online persona, with a self-coined title from 2013 (V10 i0079). Her chat messages are short and plain: "くるみって たしか スイス触ってたよな? 大丈夫か…?" (V07 i0135); 「くるみに つながったか?」, 「もうちょっと 連絡してみる」 (V08 i0107).

**Limits of the medium.** This is lettering, not voice: tone, volume and accent are inferred from type, balloon shape and drawing. Unballooned lettering can be thought, mutter or aside, and attribution then rests on placement (the "4桁万円" and "簡単" lines squeezed out at V06 i0147 are not counted as hers; their speaker is UNRESOLVED). Two western-dialect-like negatives in V05 (i0106, i0116) are a one-off colouring, not evidence of a dialect (V05 §K, HYPOTHESIS LOW).

## 7. Relationships

Each state of a tie is dated to the volume that shows it; both directions are kept apart.

### Yasuko and Mebuki (V02–V09): the protector who is not believed

*Yasuko → Mebuki.* The steadiest tie of her life on the page and the series' most consistent line of care toward one person.
- **V02.** Neighbour and friend ("お隣同士なのに なんか久しぶり!", i0074). She had told Mebuki not to start FX ("あれだけ やめとけって 言ったのに", i0075), and when Mebuki starts she warns her exactly (i0078–i0082) while denying any knowledge of FX herself (i0083). Her private judgment is resigned, not spiteful: "芽吹は もう手遅れだ… / 一回痛い目見ないと 治らないだろうな…", and her resolution is to stay close: "ならせめて あたしが 一番近いところで 見守ってやる…" (i0157, thought). That resolution, together with wounded pride (i0155), is why she announces she will trade (i0156) and joins the contest Mebuki then proposes (i0157–i0160).
- **V03.** She phones after Mebuki's August loss (i0048), is told a protective lie ("10万円くらい", i0049–i0050), offers her a way out of reporting her result to the group (i0094), and in October, learning that Mebuki has held a short since early September through the rise, tells her to cut it now, "これは 経験者からの 忠告——" (i0141). ### What Study B's argument means for her

Study B ("Obligations, relationships and where the cost of a choice lands", [studies/FX_KURUMI_OBLIGATIONS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COST.md](../studies/FX_KURUMI_OBLIGATIONS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COST.md)) asks who pays for each consequential choice. Its arguments that bear on her are summarized here; cross-person comparisons are Study B's, and the page anchors are this portrait's:
- *The duty to warn is hers, and so is its price.* In Study B's register of obligations (its entry O9) she is the friend who carries the duty to warn and to intervene, toward Mebuki (V02–V05, V09) and toward Kurumi (V06–V08). Study B observes that the immediate cost of a warning falls on the warner — she is insulted for hers (V03 i0142) — while withheld warnings cost the withholder no money on the page. Her conduct is what that pattern looks like from the warner's side.
- *Her responsibility is the healthy kind.* Study B separates four shapes of responsibility in the series. Hers — accepting her own choices ("あたしの責任だよ", V04 i0136) while keeping a duty to intervene in a friend's — is the one shape among friends that does not end care, and she fails it once, in the moment (V04 i0143). Study B also reads her V05 concession of Mebuki's 自己責任 (V05 i0133) as the point where the contrast with Mochiko moves from whether Mebuki is responsible to whether responsibility ends care.
- *Her view of lending.* Study B cites her lines as the norm behind its thesis B2 — between friends, credit for trading is harm, and care is not money (INFERENCE MODERATE; FXK-SYN-C022) — a norm she states, not a judgement of Mochiko alone. On the page the view takes three forms: alone on the train she thinks that lending to a friend to trade is wrong ("借りる 芽吹も アホだけど… / 貸すのは 違うだろ…", V04 i0143, said to no one); to Mochiko she objects to interest between friends ("おいおい 友達から 利息 取ってんのか!?", V05 i0131); and, having conceded Mebuki's 自己責任 (i0133), she frames "「安易に お金貸すなよ」 とか 「まずFX やめさせろよ」" as "あたしの個人的な 意見" (V05 i0134). She holds the view firmly and voices it as hers, not as a rule she imposes. Her own transfers are treats and payment for work.
- *Friends pay in time and standing.* She spends a night, her comfort and her pride on Mebuki (V04; V03), and in V08 she is one of the two friends who tried to reach Kurumi without knowing the figure of her debt, while Mochiko, who knew it, had not tried ("萌智子先輩は 連絡して みました?" — "…いや", V08 i0107; FXK-V08-E038).
- *Her own concealment cost no one on the page.* Study B notes as a contrary case that she hid her trading past from Mebuki (V02 i0076, i0083) with no cost shown. Concealment of her losses hurt only her credibility as a warner (V03 i0142).
These are Study B's arguments, which this monograph neither decides nor restates; their fuller form is in that study, and where the published study's wording differs, the study governs.

## 10. Capabilities and limitations

**Artistic capability.** Speed above all: twenty pages in about three days after one day's post (V05 i0123); the editor's "一番の武器は 筆の早さです" (V05 i0124); fast delivery of commissions (V07 i0041); assistant work valued because "筆が 早くて 助かる" (V09 i0110–i0111). Character and goods design that sells: her goods sold out at コミチア and 372 of 1000 books sold at an event where, by her own estimate, thirty would have been good (V05 i0106, i0111, i0126). Limits named by a professional: animals well drawn, people poorly; weak ネーム (story construction) — "その場の テンション だけで 進む" (V05 i0122). Those limits persist: serial storyboards rejected through early 2015 (V09 i0086), an editor's "1ミクロンも 面白くない" in 2017 and her "明日までに 直します" (V10 i0010–i0011, caller INFERENCE MODERATE), confinement at the publisher over storyboards (V10 i0086), and then a decided serialization (V10 i0080, i0092). Whether 【漫画家】 in V09 marks a debut is not stated (V09-U10, resolved in part by V10).

**Financial knowledge.** Accurate vocabulary and a clear account of how leverage, luck and loss-chasing destroy small traders (V02 i0079–i0082). She can read a chart well enough to judge a short dangerous in October 2014 (V03 i0139), and the rise that followed bore her out, though she held no position. Her own trading results are poor: apart from the first week of 2013 ("1週間で / 3万円 稼げた…!", V10 i0070), every trade of hers that the page closes ends in a loss, from the 2013 liquidation and the AUD long cut at a "大損" in V01 (the reversal short that followed has no result on the page) to the −7.2% of the contest (V07 i0011; "前と変わらず 5000円負けの マイナス7%!"); Mebuki's "一回も 稼いだ ことないくせに!" gets her answer "それは そうだけど…!" (V03 i0142). The distinction between capacity and willingness is sharp here: her analysis is good and her execution historically poor, and from V04 on she chooses not to execute at all. Of her knowledge of shares in 2017 the page shows only one item, a unit rule Nana relays from her ("やす子ちゃんが 言うには", V10 i0104), which did not hold for the stock Nana chose (i0105); the narrator explains that 100- and 1000-share units coexisted in 2017 (i0106).

**Physical and social capacities.** Stamina and persistence (the night search, V04 i0077–i0130); good at dance games (V02 i0075–i0079). Her small size and youthful look cost her credibility with strangers (V02 i0139; V04 i0126). Socially she can confront (V05) and can be polite under pressure (V05 i0121–i0123), but she cannot always speak when it matters most (V04 i0142).

**Capacity versus willingness.** She could have objected to the loan and did not, in the moment (V04 i0142); she could have pressed Kurumi and did not (V06 i0147); she could trade and does not (V06–V10). The first two are limits of the moment; the third is a choice she states and keeps.

## 11. Longitudinal development: what changed, what was only revealed

**Real change, dated.**
1. *Her rule about trading* — the clearest development she has. V01 (as あふぃちゃん): cuts on fear, then sets out to chase at full size (V01 i0148–i0149; execution INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH). V02: the blog announces あふぃちゃん's retirement (V02 i0003); named, she then states rules for a return aloud — low leverage, low volatility (V02 i0156) — and privately resolves not to compete (i0160). V03: comes back ("また戻ってきてしまった…", V03 i0003), breaks the leverage rule by necessity, cuts fast, freezes before a chase she recognizes from before, and stays out (V03 i0007–i0021, i0156). V04: decides on her own record that her confidence is untrustworthy and quits (V04 i0046–i0047). V06: says goodbye to "FXやってる あたし" (V06 i0147). V07: the account is empty, withdrawn by her (V07 i0011). V09: "FXは やらんけど!", but not against investing (V09 i0112–i0113; [FXK-V09-C027](../FX_KURUMI_DECISION_AND_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md)). 2017: shares since autumn 2016 (V10 i0091), among them cash shares of her own publisher held as support, with a superstition and a hope of luck, unleveraged and without a forced exit (V10 i0092; FXK-V10-C056 STRENGTHEN). *Why*: the page gives the reason in her own words at the turning point — repeated losses on trades she was sure of, set against the one thing that had a measurable response, her rabbit comic (V04 i0046). Fear of repetition (V03 i0019) comes first; evidence-based choice (V04) second. Learning took more than one ruin: she was liquidated in 2013 and still set out to reverse at full size in V01.
2. *Her vocation.* Designer with an unsold brand (V02) → manga as a means to visibility (V03–V05) → professional manga as the aim after an editor's advice (V05–V06) → paid commissions and a publisher's editor (V07) → assistant work and rejected storyboards (2015, V09) → 【漫画家】 with a serial decided (2017, V09–V10). *Why*: an honest critique that took her seriously (V05 i0121–i0124) and her own speed.
3. *Disclosure of her past.* Concealed and denied (V02) → leaked and half-told (V03–V04) → outed in public (V05) → spoken of openly as a former self (V06 i0147). She is never shown telling anyone the 2013 story.
4. *Her tie to Mochiko.* Fear (V02) → suspicion and anger (V04) → a frank confrontation that asks for friendship (V05) → commerce and banter (V07) → an easy, teasing familiarity in which she calls Mochiko shrewd (2017).
5. *Her role toward others.* From object lesson (V01, the narrator's foil) to warner (V02–V05) to mentor (2017, toward Nana).

**What does not change.** Her voice (あたし and the rough register, 2013–2017); rabbits and her brand; her care toward the people near her; her habit of measuring money in food; her readiness to spend on others when she has little; her reluctance to speak about her own hurt.

**Late revelation, not change.** V10's 2013 flashback (V10 i0066–i0079) is disclosure. It explains where the blogger came from, why her warnings sound like an expert's, why she calls the pound a "殺人通貨" and what her "同じ失敗" could refer to; it does not alter anything she did in V01–V09 ([FXK-V10-C062](../FX_KURUMI_DECISION_AND_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md) relates the flashback to her 2014 account as continuity, not contradiction). Read in story order, her development runs from reckless (2013) to restrained (2017); read in reading order, the reader meets the restrained person first, so her caution acquires its history only at the end. The two orders should not be merged: the V01–V04 learning is development the page shows as it happens; the 2013 origin is a fact supplied afterwards. **Tested once, and held.** Her exit survived a friend's spectacular success in FX: at Christmas 2014, hearing that Kurumi had made over 1000万 and invited by Mochiko to try again, she refuses at once ("やめい!", V06 i0147).

**Untested within the boundary.** Whether her exit from FX would survive a large gain in her shares or a cancelled serial; whether she would ever tell the friends about 2013; how she would respond to learning of Kurumi's V08 crisis (no page shows her learning of it).

## 13. Interpretive uncertainty

**Alternative explanations kept open.**
- *Why she resumed trading and joined the contest in V02.* Two motives are on the page and neither displaces the other: protection of Mebuki ("一番近いところで 見守ってやる…", V02 i0157) and wounded pride ("ムカつくぜ…!", V02 i0155). Her own account in V04 adds that she had traded all along and that the return was her decision ("再開も 最終的に あたしが 決めたんだ", V04 i0136). The page does not rank them.
- *Her silence when the loan is disclosed (V04 i0142).* The accepted reading is a failure to act in the moment on partial information under time pressure (V04 §I). A rival — deference to Mebuki's right to choose — draws on her later concession of 自己責任 (V05 i0133) but is not supported by her thought at the time, which is self-reproach (V04 i0143).
- *Why she hid her trading past.* The page gives no stated motive. Shame (her pride is visibly sensitive to being the loser, V02 i0155), protection of a blogger persona, and the wish to warn as a neutral party all fit; none is shown. The V02 reading calls the concealment "protective of herself" (V02 §I), an interpretation.
- *Whether her caution is learned or temperamental.* Learned, on the evidence: 2013 and the V01 night show the same person without restraint, and her V04 decision states its evidence. A temperament reading needs the 2013 conduct to be an aberration, which the page does not suggest.
- *Her 2013 motive.* Fear of poverty, early reinforcement and a round-number ceiling are what the page shows in a short flashback; anything richer is speculation (Study C grades her 2013 driver the least differentiated of the four ruin sequences).

**Counterexamples to the main portrait.** The V01 full-size reversal, announced and probably placed (execution INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH; the announced intent alone tells against "non-gambler"); the V03 17× trade and the announced chase (against her rules); her concealment of her past (against her demand for openness); the silence of V04 i0142 (against "acts when a friend is in danger"); the dropped "引き際" (against "presses warnings"); the V07 dodge of her income question under a no-lies rule (V07 i0022–i0024); and in 2017 an investment recommendation made by affinity, not analysis (V10 i0093), which within V10 moves a friend into shares — her relayed unit rule failing for the stock chosen, the first holding 8,000円 down (V10 i0104–i0105, i0145) — with no page showing her learn of it (against her role as the circle's warner).
