## Orientation

**Who he is.** Subject FXK-P002 is the father of 福賀くるみ (Fukuga Kurumi), the protagonist of the Japanese manga **FX戦士くるみちゃん** (story でむにゃん, art 炭酸だいすき). His surname 福賀 is printed for him; his given name 郁夫 (Ikuo) is an inference (INFERENCE HIGH; the basis is given under "Name forms" below). He is a salaried man in glasses whose work repeatedly posts him away from home. His wife, Kurumi's mother 福賀梢 (Kozue; unnamed in V01–V05 and named from V06 i0065), lost about 2000万 of household money trading FX in 2008 while he was posted away, kept it from him, and died by suicide on 13 February 2009, the day he was due home (V01 i0014, i0024). From April 2009 he lives with his daughter. In 2014 she secretly takes the 300万 he has been saving at home and deposits it behind a losing position that she then enlarges (V01 i0109–i0127; her purpose in taking it is an open question), and he finds out (V02). In January 2015, after he has left on another transfer, a forced liquidation leaves her trading account at −101,534,644円, a debt she cannot pay (V07 i0118, i0130); she hides it, and a crisis follows that she also hides from him (V07–V08). In V09 she tells him, and he answers.

**Where he is on the page.** Two concentrated sequences carry most of the evidence: the discovery of V02 (V02 i0014, i0021–i0038) and the confession, offer and settlement of V09 (V09 i0062–i0098, i0122–i0123). Between and before them are short traces: in V01 his scenes with Kurumi on the day of his wife's death (V01 i0031–i0033), a gentle warning as he moves back home (V01 i0038–i0039) and one glimpse of him at home in May 2014 (V01 i0105); the announcement of his transfer (V05 i0069), his departure (V07 i0030–i0031) and one unanswered night call (V08 i0035–i0036). He is mentioned but not drawn in present time in V03, V04 and V06. In V10 he is neither drawn nor named; one line of Kurumi's may allude to him (V10 i0151; INFERENCE LOW). Much of what the page says about him is said by others: by Kurumi's memories and narration (V01, V05, V08), by his wife's account inside the V06 flashback, and once by the narrator (V08 i0035). He never trades.

**Boundary and spoilers.** The accepted V01–V10 main narrative, ending at V10 i0156 (Monday 24 April 2017). This portrait contains spoilers through V10. Nothing after V10 is known or used; paratext (afterwords, flaps, jackets, bonus pieces) is never evidence; nothing outside the admitted pages is used. Kurumi's mother's death is described here only as a suicide. The crisis of V08–V09 is discussed only as a confession, a request and a plan, without any method.

**The portrait in brief.** A reserved, plain-spoken provider who, by his own late account, ran his marriage on the rule that he handled the money and his wife the house (V09 i0072), and who after her death arranged to commute from home, put cash aside at home every month — for Kurumi, he says only in V09 — and warned Kurumi once, gently, against FX (V01 i0038–i0039, i0108–i0109; V09 i0074). His two large scenes mirror each other. When he discovers a concealment that she then justifies by its success and by a claim to be an adult (V02), he slaps her once, names her motive and her process exactly, denies that she is an adult and bows to beg her to stop. When she confesses ruin to him herself, before he can discover it (V09), he promises not to be angry, says he is glad she is alive, blames himself for having made it hard to confide in him, gives her the 300万 he had saved for her, asks for a vow, withdraws the demand when she refuses it, and keeps his opposition "親として". What holds across the series is his wish to be relied on, his judgment of process before outcome and a short, concessive, plain masculine speech; what changes, between V02 and V09, is his acknowledgment of her adulthood. Whether that change is development in him or a different answer to a different act is open (section 11).

## 1. Identity and circumstances

**Family.** Husband of Kurumi's mother — unnamed in V01–V05, named 梢 (Kozue) from V06 i0065 — until her death on 13 February 2009; father of Kurumi, who is 15 in October 2008 (V01 i0008), 20 in February 2014 (V01 i0040) and 23 in April 2017 (V09 i0152). No other child exists; a second child appears only as a wish in Kozue's 2005 daydream (V06 i0065). His own parents are only mentioned ("父さんも / 親に反抗して / よく喧嘩したもんだ", V09 i0083), beside a comic inset of his youth in which they are not drawn. Kozue's parents, whose cash she took in 2008 without their knowledge (V06 i0077, "実家の… お金…"; INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH, V06 reading §I), are never shown with him.

**Work and movements.** His occupation is never shown or named. The page gives a company (Kozue's remembered "会社は知ってるんでしょ?" and his "そのぶん給料はもらえるんだ", V09 i0072), suits and ties (V08 i0036; V09 i0062), a colleague, and repeated transfers (転勤). The sequence, with its sources:

- When Kurumi was small, a transfer that his wife protested ("また転勤なの?"); he answered "しょうがないだろ 仕事なんだから" (his V09 memory, V09 i0072; OBSERVATION of a memory inset, HIGH).
- 2008: posted away from home (単身赴任), absent even at the New Year (V01 i0014, i0024; Kurumi's retrospective narration).
- April 2009: "父は仕事の担当先を変えてもらったらしく家から通勤することになった" (V01 i0038, caption in Kurumi's narration, hedged with らしく). That he asked for the change in order to live with his daughter is INFERENCE MODERATE; the caption gives no reason.
- Autumn 2014: he comes home late (Kurumi: "お父さん最近遅いな…", V04 i0115); the reason is never given (V04-U07, ADVANCED only by the transfer announced in V05; the lateness itself is never explained).
- From the New Year 2015: a transfer, announced with an apology (V05 i0069); he leaves at the end of December 2014 with a suitcase (V07 i0030–i0031).
- Early 2015: on the posting, driven by a younger colleague, he says long postings separate one from family and that occasional business trips are the most fun (V09 i0062–i0063); after the confession he comes home often (V09 i0123).

**Home.** From April 2009 he shares a flat with Kurumi (V01 i0038; V05 i0069; V09 i0085). He keeps a cash reserve in a chest drawer among clothes (V01 i0107–i0109). The furnishings of his scenes are a sofa, a low table and newspaper, and a kotatsu in winter (V09 i0070–i0074, i0122–i0123).

**Appearance.** Glasses in every present-time scene; a sweater and stubble in V01 (V01 i0105); house clothes in V02; a suit, tie and sweater vest in V09. A younger man without glasses stands in the family photograph (V08 i0065, by the V08 reading's identification). His age is never printed (UNKNOWN).

**Place in the story.** He is the unknowing owner of the money that saves Kurumi's first position (V01), the discoverer and first critic of her trading (V02), the absent conscience she remembers at moments of temptation and ruin (V04, V05, V08), the person from whom she hides her debt (V07–V08), and the person to whom her decisive disclosure is made (V09). His offered savings and the lawyer he introduces open the bankruptcy procedure she believed she could not afford (V08 i0032–i0034; V09 i0074, i0098; that the 300万 actually paid the costs is INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH, V09-U14). His V02 judgment is the one Kurumi later adopts, alone, as her own principle after refusing to lend to Mebuki (V04 i0074; FXK-V04-C007).

## 2. Personal history: what is depicted, what is disclosed, and when

His past reaches the reader in three layers held by three people, and they are kept apart here.

**Kurumi's memory and narration (V01).** In 2008 he is away; his wife begs their fifteen-year-old to keep her losses from him, and Kurumi agrees for fear the family will split (V01 i0014). He is due home on 13 February 2009, the day of the death (V01 i0024, i0031–i0033). A flashback page shows him standing with bowed head before his wife's funeral portrait (V01 i0108, calendar 「2009 2月」). In April 2009 he moves back to commuting from home (V01 i0038) and, unpacking, warns his daughter against FX (V01 i0039). Kurumi's narration says that since her mother's death he has added to a cash reserve at home at every month-end (V01 i0108–i0109: "お父さんは毎月月末に / タンス預金をしていることを!"), illustrated by flashback calendars and an image of him at the drawer. Her caption reads the money as "きっと…福賀家再興の証し" (V01 i0109): that is her surmise, not his statement.

**Kozue's account (V06, a late revelation of 2005–2008).** In the flashback of 第28話, the father is seen through his wife's eyes: he tells her she need not work ("郁夫さん 私には働かなくていいって言うけど", V06 i0062); she thinks him "古い人" who would not know that money can be earned on the internet (V06 i0064); she imagines his amazed praise if she makes money (V06 i0064–i0065); and when her account has fallen to 1,005,144円 she fears that "郁夫さんにバレたら一家離散よ…" (V06 i0082). These are her views and fears about him. The chapter's authority is mixed: its captions are a narrator's, it is framed by Kurumi reading the journal, and some of its scenes go beyond what a journal could contain (V06 reading, counterreading 7). No page shows him learning of the losses while she lived (FXK-V06-C013).

**His own account (V09, also a late revelation).** Told of his daughter's ruin, he sets it against his wife's silence: "母さんのときは / 相談もなかったからな…" (V09 i0072). A static-textured memory then shows an earlier exchange: his wife protests another transfer while their small child draws on the floor, and he answers that it cannot be helped, that the pay makes up for it, and "お金のことは全部俺に任せて / お前は家のことだけやってたらいいんだ" (V09 i0072; OBSERVATION HIGH of the inset; the page re-opened for this portrait). In the present he draws the conclusion himself: "…父さんが相談しづらくしてたんだろう / 情けない話だ" (V09 i0073; SELF_REPORT HIGH). He also tells his daughter that the 300万 she once found had been saved for her from the start ("もともとくるみのために貯めてたお金だったからな", V09 i0074), and that as a young man he defied his parents and fought with them; a comic inset shows a contrarian literary youth skipping school (V09 i0083).

## 3. Personality and psychology

The page supports a modest but consistent inner profile. Most of it rests on what he says in two scenes and on how he acts in them; it is inference from outside, and is labelled as such.

**Values.** Five recur.
- *Ordinary sufficiency over speculation.* "投資が悪いとは言わん… / だがそんなことしなくても / 十分幸せになれるんだからな?" (V01 i0039). He does not condemn investment as such; he denies that happiness needs it (SELF_REPORT HIGH).
- *Being relied on.* "親をもっと頼ってくれ…!" (V02 i0027); "本当によく言ってくれた…" (V09 i0073). In both crises the first thing he asks of his daughter is that she tell him (OBSERVATION HIGH).
- *Process before outcome.* "お金借りないと勝てない状況になってる時点でダメだろ" (V02 i0031): in his words, a win that needed borrowed money is already a failure. (He adopts her framing of the taking as borrowing here; in the analysis's own terms her act was an unconsented, concealed use of family savings with an intent to repay.) A ledger claim records this as the story itself voicing the critique the V01 reading made from outside (FXK-V02-C016).
- *A parent's duty to say stop.* "もうやめろ" (V02 i0031) and, after he has conceded her freedom, "それでも俺はやめろと言うぞ / 親・と・し・て・な" (V09 i0084). The duty survives her refusal and her adulthood (SELF_REPORT HIGH).
- *Character over circumstance.* Meeting her friends, he cuts off "FXがきっかけで——" with "そんなことはいい / いい子たちなのか?" and ends "友達は大切にな" (V09 i0096–i0097).

**Goals and fears.** His evident goals are her safety and the family's continuance: support offered before any explanation (V02 i0027), "生きていてくれて" as his first substantive answer to ruin (V09 i0072). His fear that she will repeat her mother is INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH: in V02 his rebuke goes straight to "母さんの失敗を取り返す必要がある?" (V02 i0030), and in V09 he sets her survival against his wife's silence (V09 i0072); when she says it was "ギリギリ", his widened eyes are cut against the mother's memorial portrait (V09 i0080).

**Vulnerabilities.** Absence by work, which twice leaves his family alone at the moment of its crisis (2008; January 2015). Dependence on others' disclosure: in both generations the money is lost or taken where he cannot see it, and he learns only by discovery or confession. By his own late verdict, a manner that made confiding in him hard (V09 i0073).

**Contradictions the page leaves standing.**
- He asks to be relied on (V02 i0027), yet by his own later account he made reliance difficult (V09 i0073), and within the V02 scene the request for trust and the slap are four pages apart (V02 i0027, i0030).
- The husband who told his wife to leave all money matters to him (V09 i0072, remembered) tells his adult daughter she is free to do what she wants with her life (V09 i0083).
- The man who said that needing borrowed money to win is failure (V02 i0031) offers his own 300万 (V09 i0074). This one resolves on inspection: the V02 judgment concerns money taken to trade, the V09 offer money given openly to end a debt through a court procedure (INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH).
- "怒らないから" (V09 i0069) after the V02 slap: his anger is visible once in each crisis, but only V02 shows force.

**Coping.** Silence and short pauses ("……", "そうか…", "そう…だな…"); a physical gesture of strain repeated across volumes — he takes off or lifts his glasses and presses his face (V02 i0030, i0032; V09 i0071); sitting down heavily (V02 i0030); food and outings as the frame for hard talk ("食いながら話を聞こうか…?", V09 i0069; hamburg steak, V09 i0085); deadpan jokes ("まさか こんな使い道になるとはな", V09 i0075). Under strain he does not rage at length: in V02 one blow is followed within a page by collapse into a plea.

**Self-conception.** He speaks of himself first as a parent: 父さん is his ordinary self-reference to her, and his stance is grounded "親として" (V09 i0084). He judges himself plainly ("情けない話だ", V09 i0073) and remembers his youth as a time of defiance and quarrels with his parents (V09 i0083); the word "ひねくれてた" beside his line there is small unballooned lettering, read here as his own aside. The page offers no other view of how he sees himself (UNKNOWN beyond this).

## 4. Cognition and decision-making

**What he notices.** Money and motives, not markets. He notices at once that the home cash is gone (V02 i0022; why he looked that night is not stated, and the month-end of V01's "タンス預金更新日" is only a possibility, UNRESOLVED). In the same exchange in which she gives her account, he names her motive exactly — "なんでくるみがお金を—— / 母さんの失敗を取り返す必要がある?" (V02 i0030) — and the flaw in her process (V02 i0031). In both crises he registers how hard it was for her to speak: "言いにくいのはわかる" (V02 i0026), "くるみも言いにくかっただろ" (V09 i0073). Meeting her friends, he looks for their character (V09 i0096). What he does not notice is as telling: between V02 and V09 nothing on the page shows him checking on her trading (the V02 reading's forecast that he would check or restrict her FX again was disconfirmed, FXK-V02-F030), and while he is away she loses everything and plans her death without his knowing (V07–V08).

**How he reasons.** In principles and categories rather than figures. He never asks a number: the "1億" of his V09 summary is what he has been told (V09 i0070; the V09 reading notes no balloon in which he asks the amount), and the 300万 is "300万円入ってる" with no balance shown (V09 i0074). His judgments are about what an act shows ("そんな考え「大人」とは言えないな", V02 i0030) and what a role requires ("親として", V09 i0084). His reading of a situation revises as information arrives. On the V02 night it moves in five steps: an outside theft to be reported ("家のお金が盗まれた! / 今から警察に通報する!", V02 i0022); relief when the culprit is his daughter ("くるみが犯人でよかった…"); accountability ("でもそれですむ話じゃないよな?", "理由を言いなさい", V02 i0025); a protective hypothesis that she is in trouble not of her making ("なにか事件にでも巻き込まれてるなら助けてやる…!", V02 i0027); and, only after her boast, critique (V02 i0030–i0031) (OBSERVATION HIGH of the sequence; pages re-opened for this portrait).

**Priorities.** Life over money: his first substantive response to a debt of "1億" is that he is glad she is alive (V09 i0072). Disclosure over obedience: when she refuses the vow because she cannot be sure of keeping it, he frowns ("…よく平気でそんなこと言えるな", V09 i0079), hears her out, and accepts the refusal (V09 i0083). Process over outcome: in his words, being in a position where she could not win without borrowing money is already "ダメ", whatever the result ("お金借りないと勝てない状況になってる時点でダメだろ", V02 i0031).

**Uncertainty.** When he does not know what to say, he says so — "現実離れしすぎて / もうなんて言ったらいいのか…" (V09 i0071) — and falls silent or stares. When a decision of his own troubles him, the page shows hesitation rather than argument: told by his daughter not to worry about the transfer, he sweats and answers "そう…だな…" (V05 i0069; unease or something unsaid, INFERENCE LOW-MODERATE).

**Conflicting obligations.** Work against family is his recurring conflict, and his answer changes. Earlier in the marriage, by his own memory, the job came first ("しょうがないだろ 仕事なんだから", V09 i0072). After his wife's death he had his assignment changed so as to commute from home (V01 i0038, by Kurumi's narration hedged with らしく; reason unstated). In 2014 he accepts a new transfer but apologizes for it ("スマン…", V05 i0069) and, after the confession, comes home "しょっちゅう" while still posted away (V09 i0123). Control against autonomy is the other: in V02 he demands and begs; in V09 he concedes her freedom and keeps his stance in words only (section 11).

**His decisions on the page.** He trades nothing; his consequential choices are about money, his daughter and his own conduct.

His voice in V08 arrives only after the money is gone. Only when his role becomes an arrangement she has agreed to (V09 i0107) does it shape what she can do with money. The same study treats that arrangement as one reason her abstention after V09 cannot be called purely chosen, and reads her refusal of his vow as a choice about how any future choice will be made — openly (Study C, section 7). Those are the study's questions; for him, the point is that the vow he asked for was refused, while the control he holds is one she reports having settled with him ("お父さんと相談して決めたんだ", V09 i0107).

## 5. Behavioural patterns

**Ordinary conduct.** At home he greets his daughter and asks whether she has eaten ("おかえり / ご飯は?" — she answers "食べた!", V02 i0014), and on another evening whether she has already made dinner ("もう夜ご飯作ったのか?", V09 i0085). Kurumi does the household cooking — curry when he is late (V04 i0115), drying the dishes in an apron as they talk (V05 i0069), a remembered evening carrying dishes to the table and laughing together (V08 i0072, her memory) — and his V09 question presumes that she cooks the evening meal (INFERENCE MODERATE that he expects it as a household routine; it rests on that one line). He reads the newspaper on the sofa (V09 i0122–i0123), takes tea at the kotatsu (V09 i0070–i0074), brings souvenir manjū back from his posting (V09 i0069, i0073), and texts briefly — 「今駅降りた」「もうすぐ着く」 (V09 i0067). Leaving on the transfer, he says only "じゃあ行ってくる" and "戸締まりはちゃんとするんだぞ?" (V07 i0031). In a colleague's car he sits silent until spoken to (V09 i0062). When she has not made dinner, he pauses, says "…いや / いいんだ", and takes her out for hamburg steak, telling her "好きなの頼みなさい" (V09 i0085, i0092).

**Cooperation.** He offers help before he is asked and before he knows the facts: "なにか事件にでも巻き込まれてるなら助けてやる…!" (V02 i0027); the bankbook slid across the table without a request (V09 i0074); the lawyer (V09 i0098). He accepts her decisions about her own life when she makes them openly: her reassurance about living alone (V05 i0069), her refusal of the vow (V09 i0083), her choice of job, which she says they decided together (V09 i0107).

**Conflict.** The one full escalation is V02: command ("理由を言いなさい", i0025), then — after her outcome justification and "だってもう「大人」だもん!" (i0029) — one slap, "なにを言ってるんだ / お前は…" and "頭冷やせ" (i0030), a heavy sit, a critique and a denial ("無理だよ くるみには", i0031), and a bow from his chair with his glasses in his clasped hands, "頼むから… / FXなんてやめてくれ…", "頼む…" (i0032). The slap is his act toward an adult daughter; it is recorded, not explained away (V02 reading, E008). In V09 the conflict is reduced to one frown and one sentence (V09 i0079).

**Stress responses.** Glasses removed or lifted, a hand pressed to his face or eyes (V02 i0030–i0032; V09 i0071); a heavy sigh ("ハア…", V09 i0071); a hard, silent stare that makes his daughter flinch before he softens (V09 i0071); sweating silence (V05 i0069); bowing (V02 i0032). In comic register, a menacing aura and "無理だ… / お前じゃ扱いきれん…!" when his young colleague, unaware of the call, offers himself as a match for his daughter (V09 i0065–i0066) — a gag that also shows the call's effect, since the next line is "駅へ行ってくれ" (INFERENCE HIGH).

**Mistakes and corrections.** The page does not call the slap a mistake; V09 sets "怒らないから" against it (V09 i0069), and Kurumi's own expectation of "ビンタ" (V09 i0040) shows how she remembered it. The mistake the page does name is his own, in his own words: a manner that made his wife, and then his daughter, unable to consult him (V09 i0073). His corrections in V09 are a withdrawn demand (the vow) and, more faintly, a revised domestic expectation ("…いや / いいんだ" after his remembered "家のことだけやってたらいいんだ"; INFERENCE LOW-MODERATE, offered in the V09 reading and not adopted there).

**Meaningful exceptions.** The slap is the only physical force he uses in ten volumes. The gag menace toward the colleague is the only time he menaces anyone, and it is played for comedy.

## 6. Speech and communication

**Register.** Plain, masculine, short. Negative and verb forms of casual male speech: "言わん" (V01 i0039), "扱いきれん" (V09 i0066), "食いながら" (V09 i0069). Sentence endings carry most of his tone: ぞ for warning and stance ("するんじゃないぞ", V01 i0039; "わかるぞ?", V02 i0025; "言うぞ", V09 i0084), な for settled statements ("だからな", V01 i0039; "からな…", V09 i0072; "よかったな", V09 i0123; "大切にな", V09 i0097), だろ for appeals to what she must know ("ダメだろ", V02 i0031; "言いにくかっただろ", V09 i0073). The parental 〜なさい marks command and generosity alike: "理由を言いなさい" (V02 i0025), "好きなの頼みなさい" (V09 i0092).

**Self-reference and address.**
- 父さん to his daughter as a parent: "父さんはなにがあってもくるみの味方なんだ…!" (V02 i0027), "父さんが相談しづらくしてたんだろう" (V09 i0073).
- 俺 for his own stance and in remembered speech to his wife: "お金のことは全部俺に任せて" (V09 i0072, memory); "それでも俺はやめろと言うぞ" (V09 i0084). Within one V09 scene he shifts from 父さん to 俺 and back as he moves from care to stance and back to care ("父さんにとっては可愛い子供だからな", V09 i0084; FXK-V09-E035).
- くるみ to his daughter ("なあ…くるみ…", V01 i0039; "くるみ!", V02 i0027); お前 to her only in anger ("お前は…", V02 i0030); お前 to his wife in memory (V09 i0072) and to his colleague (V09 i0066).
- 母さん for his wife, the family-role word seen from the child (V01 i0032; V02 i0030; V09 i0072).
- To his daughter's friends, a deadpan polite "父ですが… なにか?" (V09 i0095).

**Rhetorical habits.**
- *Concession, then a firm clause.* "投資が悪いとは言わん… / だが…" (V01 i0039); "やったことは滅茶苦茶だが / 本当によく言ってくれた…" (V09 i0073); "…わかった / くるみももう大人なんだ… / それでも俺はやめろと言うぞ" (V09 i0083–i0084). The pattern is his most stable verbal signature across the series (OBSERVATION HIGH for the instances; EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH as a habit).
- *Confirming questions.* "…電話の件 / 本当なんだな?" (V09 i0069); "……学校の友達か?", "いい子たちなのか?" (V09 i0096); "わかってくれたか…?" (V02 i0038).
- *Verdicts with 無理.* "無理だよ / くるみには" (V02 i0031); in the gag, "無理だ…" (V09 i0066).
- *Pleas.* "頼ってくれ" (V02 i0027), "頼むから… / やめてくれ…", "頼む…" (V02 i0032): when command fails, his speech becomes supplication rather than louder command.
- *Apology.* "スマン…" for the transfer (V05 i0069) and for a crooked tie his daughter straightens (V09 i0091).
- *Emphasis.* In "親・と・し・て・な" the word 親として carries emphasis dots and the final な does not (V09 i0084): the lettering marks a slow, stressed delivery of the role he speaks from.
- *Framing words.* In V02 he calls the taking theft ("盗まれた", i0022; "お金を盗むなんて", i0025), against her "借りた"; in his critique he then speaks of the money as borrowed ("お金借りないと勝てない状況", i0031) — he argues within her framing once he is criticising her process (OBSERVATION HIGH; the reading's §K records the contested words).

**Humour and silence.** His humour is dry and brief: "まさか こんな使い道になるとはな" over the 300万 (V09 i0075); "運がいいのか悪いのかわからないが…" when she has drawn the one hot takoyaki five times running (V09 i0122); "父ですが… なにか?" (V09 i0095). The broader comedy around him (the colleague's matchmaking, the friends' suspicion that he is a paying "パパ") is the series' comic register, in which he is the straight man. Silence is a large part of his speech: "……" in the car (V09 i0062) and in the hallway (V09 i0068), "そうか…" (V01 i0039), "そう…だな…" (V05 i0069).

**Generational marker.** He calls chat messages "メール" ("メールは終わったのか?", V09 i0094), which fits his wife's 2005 view of him as "古い人" who knows little of the internet (V06 i0064) (INFERENCE LOW-MODERATE as a trait).

**Shifts by listener and pressure.** With his daughter at ease: affectionate brevity and practical care (V07 i0031; V09 i0092, i0123). Under her concealment and boast: お前, imperatives, a verdict, then a plea (V02 i0030–i0032). Under her confession: confirmation, a promise, an invitation to talk, and the concessive structure (V09 i0069–i0073). With her friends: questions and a maxim (V09 i0096–i0097). With a younger colleague: reserve, then comic menace (V09 i0062–i0066). With his wife, as he remembers it: 俺 and お前 and the language of a provider (V09 i0072).

**Inner and outer speech; the medium's limits.** No interior speech of his appears, so the difference between what he thinks and what he says cannot be tested; the narrator's one statement about him is a prediction ("少なくとも彼女の父は / 早期の解決に向けて / 協力は 惜しまない はずである", V08 i0035; NARRATOR_PRESENTATION), which V09 bears out. What survives is written register: balloon shape, ellipses and emphasis dots, not a performed voice.

### 7.5 What the specialist study of obligations means for him

The study of obligations and cost — "Obligations, relationships and where the cost of a choice lands" (Study B, [studies/FX_KURUMI_OBLIGATIONS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COST.md](../studies/FX_KURUMI_OBLIGATIONS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COST.md)) — places him at the centre of its strand on concealment in the Fukuga family. Three of its consequences belong to his portrait.

- **He is the unknowing party in both generations.** In 2008 Kozue stakes and loses the household money, the cancelled insurance and the home down-payment behind his back (V06 i0071–i0077); in 2014 Kurumi puts his savings behind a position she enlarges to 300 lots without his knowledge (V01 i0109–i0127); in 2015 she hides the debt and the crisis from him (V07–V08). The study's account of where cost lands shows why this matters: in V01 the risk his savings carried landed on no one only because the trade won (V01 i0157); in V02 its cost was his peace and his trust; in V08 the plan addressed to him would have handed him the discovery, the grief and a legal act; after V09 the costs he bears — his savings, his journeys, the management of her wages — he bears by his own offer and her agreement. The same 300万 is taken in concealment in V01 and given openly in V09 (V09 i0074; FXK-V09-C071).
- **His own role is part of the problem the study traces.** He supplies the evidence himself: a marriage in which money matters were his ("全部俺に任せて") and the house hers (V09 i0072), and a manner that made consultation hard (V09 i0073). The study keeps two rivals open that bear directly on him — that his change between V02 and V09 is situational rather than a change of stance, and that the family's money roles were the structural cause of the 2008 concealment, so that V09 shows a change in him rather than only the effect of a disclosure. It does not decide between them, and neither does this portrait (section 11).
- **He is one pole of the word 大人.** In the study's reading the word moves from a licence to act alone (Kurumi, V02 i0130; V05 i0069) to a capacity to disclose (Mochiko, V09 i0043); he denies her adulthood in V02 ("そんな考え「大人」とは言えないな", V02 i0030) and grants it in V09 ("くるみももう大人なんだ", V09 i0083) while keeping his opposition.

## 10. Capabilities and limitations

- **Saving and provision.** The reserve his daughter finds in the drawer in May 2014 — "300万円" in three bands — is, by her narration, what his month-end saving since 2009 has built (V01 i0108–i0110), and in V09 he has it in a bank account to give (V09 i0074; V09-U13 is NARROWED: the cash returned to him in V02, and only when it was banked is not shown). Whether it paid the bankruptcy costs is not shown (V09-U14 OPEN; INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH that it did, reconciliation audit §4.3).
- **Practical standing.** He can have his work assignment changed (V01 i0038, by Kurumi's hedged narration), and he has a lawyer to introduce (V09 i0098). Kurumi treats him as the person who handles her official status as a dependant (V02 i0018, i0029).
- **Judgment without market knowledge.** He reads motive and process accurately (V02 i0030–i0031) though, by his wife's account, he knows little of internet earning (V06 i0064). Nothing shows him knowing how FX works beyond its danger.
- **Limits.** He cannot see what is hidden from him, and he is away when both great losses happen (2008; January 2015). He cannot make his daughter confide: in V02 his request for trust is followed by her assent and relapse, and in V08 his call goes unanswered. His one use of force does not change her course.
- **Capacity and willingness.** He has the standing to insist — the money, the household, the vow he asks for — and in V09 he chooses not to enforce the vow, keeping only his stated opposition (V09 i0083–i0084). The control he exercises afterwards is over her wages, and it rests, by her account, on an agreement (V09 i0107).

## 11. Longitudinal development: change, constancy and late revelation

**What stays the same, V01–V09.** His opposition to FX (V01 i0039; V02 i0031–i0032; V09 i0084). His wish to be relied on (V02 i0027; V09 i0073). His habit of judging process rather than outcome (V02 i0031; in V09 the outcome is a ruin, and he judges the act "滅茶苦茶" and the telling "よく言ってくれた", V09 i0073). His care shown in practical acts — a home to come back to, cash set aside, food, a lawyer. His speech: 父さん, plain masculine endings, concession followed by a firm clause.

**What changes, dated.**

**Is the V02 → V09 difference development?** The project's character qualification audit, in its record for this subject (FXK-P002; linked in section 14), names this "a real change of stance … with the rival that the change is situational". Four readings stay live, and the page does not decide between them.
1. *Development.* He has revised his view of what a parent may impose on an adult child; the vow's withdrawal and "親に人生を決められたくはないだろう" (V09 i0083), backed by memories of his own rebellion, say something V02 never offered (EVALUATION MODERATE).
2. *A different answer to a different act.* In V02 the disclosure was forced by discovery. Before her boast he had already rebuked the taking and demanded her reasons ("でもそれですむ話じゃないよな?", "理由を言いなさい", V02 i0025), then acknowledged that it was hard to say and offered support without conditions (i0026–i0027); the slap followed only her outcome justification and her claim to adulthood, "私のことは放っておいていいよ!" and "だってもう「大人」だもん!" (i0029–i0030) (OBSERVATION HIGH for the order; INFERENCE MODERATE that the boast, not the taking, triggered the slap). In V09 the disclosure is her own act (though made under Mochiko's job condition, V09 i0050), made prostrate and without excuse, and he again judges the act ("やったことは滅茶苦茶だが", V09 i0073) while helping the person. One rule — rebuke for the act and help for the person in both scenes, force only for a concealment justified by success — would produce both scenes.
3. *Situational scale.* A recovered win in V02, a ruin and a near-death in V09, set against his wife's death (V09 i0072, i0080): the stakes, not his beliefs, may have changed (the rival kept by the qualification record and by the obligations study).
4. *Narrative function.* V02 needs an in-story voice for the process critique (FXK-V02-C016) and an obstacle to Kurumi's trading; V09 needs a recipient whose answer proves the narrator's V08 verdict that telling him was the best course (V08 i0035; FXK-V09-C046, STRENGTHEN). His independent life on the page is thin enough that a change in what the story needs from him cannot be excluded.

Against reading 2 alone: the V09 demand for a vow shows the same impulse to control as V02, and only its withdrawal, with the words about her adulthood, is new. Against reading 1 alone: no page between V02 and V09 shows him reflecting, so the change cannot be dated within that interval. What would settle the question — his response to a later concealment, or to an open return to the market — lies beyond V10.

## 13. Interpretive uncertainty

**Open questions the page leaves.**
- Why he checked the cash on the night of discovery (V02 reading E006, UNRESOLVED).
- What he knew of his wife's losses, and when (V06-U09, ADVANCED for him only by his own "相談もなかった", V09 i0072, which says she did not consult him; whether he learned otherwise before her death is not shown). That he knew she traded FX at all rests on an inference from his warning (V01 i0039; INFERENCE HIGH).
- Why he came home late in autumn 2014 (V04-U07, ADVANCED only by the transfer announced in V05; the lateness itself is never explained), and what his sweating silence over the transfer meant (V05 i0069; INFERENCE LOW-MODERATE of unease).
- Why he called in January 2015 and what he would have said (V08 i0035–i0036; not shown).
- Whether he ever learns of the letter and the plan (V09-U07 OPEN); Mochiko, who read it, is not shown telling him.
- When the 300万 went into the bank (V09-U13, NARROWED: the cash returned to him in V02) and whether it paid the costs (V09-U14 OPEN); whether the vow was meant as a condition (FXK-V09-E032: not stated).
- Whether the wage arrangement was carried out as Kurumi describes it, and whether it still held in April 2017, when her national tax was still unpaid (V10 i0045) and her residence is not shown (V10-U09). The arrangement is known only from her own report (V09 i0107).
- Whether the phone wallpaper shows him and his wife (V09 i0085; INFERENCE LOW-MODERATE).
- His age, his occupation and his life on his postings: never shown.

**Alternative explanations and counterexamples kept visible.**
- The slap (V02 i0030) is the only physical force he uses; whether it is characteristic or exceptional cannot be decided from one instance.
- The remembered husband who kept all money matters in his own hands (V09 i0072) against the father who gives his savings openly and grants his daughter freedom (V09 i0074, i0083).
- His V02 judgment that needing borrowed money is failure (V02 i0031) against his own gift of 300万 (V09 i0074) — reconciled above as money to trade against money to end a debt, by INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH.
- His wife's picture of him (V06) comes through a chapter of mixed authority; it converges with his own V09 memory on one point (he wanted her not to work, or to keep to the house) and is otherwise unconfirmed.
- The narrative-function rival (section 11): much of his conduct can be read as the story's need for a critic in V02 and a vindicating listener in V09.

**Limits of the evidence.** Two concentrated scenes carry most of the portrait; everything else is a trace, a memory or another person's view. No interior speech of his is ever shown. Generalizations beyond the conditional profile above are extrapolation and should be labelled as such.
