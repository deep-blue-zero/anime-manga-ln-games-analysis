## Orientation

**Who she is.** 月森きずな (Tsukimori Kizuna), 27, works in sales (【営業】) at (株)もちもち企画, the small company that 小金萌智子 (Kogane Mochiko) runs in April 2017 and where 福賀くるみ (Fukuga Kurumi), the series' protagonist, is now a clerk. She is one of three named colleagues introduced at the end of V09, beside the new hire 酒田いなご (Sakata Inago) and the creator 善波なな (Zenba Nana). She is the richest person at the welcome-party table by the figures the page prints, the office's most confident trader, and the colleague who does not know that Kurumi, the quiet clerk who serves her soy latte, was once left owing more than a hundred million yen by her own currency trading. Much of V10's irony runs through that gap.

**Place in the story.** She does not appear in V01–V08; the qualification record dates her first drawing to V09 i0152. She appears on the last seven story pages of V09 (第45話, V09 i0152–i0158; Monday 3 April 2017) and in every chapter of V10 (第46–50話, 3–24 April 2017), including one flashback to 24 June 2016 at her former employer (V10 i0118). V10 gives her a passage of first-person narration, lettered freely on the panels without boxes or balloons, alone at home (i0109–i0110), the only currency trade entered in present time in the volume (i0119–i0124) and its last spoken line, "え… / なんで?" (i0156).

**Spoiler boundary.** This portrait uses everything up to V10 i0156, the last page of the accepted main narrative (evening of Monday 24 April 2017). Nothing after it exists for this document. The outcome of her euro short, whether she buys カリフラワー shares and how she answers Nana's plea all lie after V10; they are stated as unknown and never predicted (the ledger records the related forecasts FXK-V10-F107, F132, F133 and F141 as NOT SCOREABLE IN THIS RUN). Paratext (covers, flaps, the V09 character-design roughs that include a 「きずなラフ」, afterwords, bonus pages) is never used as evidence about her.

**The portrait in brief.** Kizuna performs ease and measures herself in private. In company she is sing-song, affectionate, blunt and generous with invitations — lengthened vowels, hearts, cheek-squeezing, a slogan for her ten-billion-yen dream ("自・由・億!", i0022), a modest boast about her ten million ("珍しくもないよ〜♡", i0050). Alone, in cool first-person narration, she opens her asset app each morning as the way to "確かめる" her own existence and judges a gain of 166,670 yen not good enough to lift her out of the ordinary ("ただの凡人止まりだわ", i0110). She reads people and even a fictional man by money, treats markets as stimulation and opportunity, decides quickly and frames her bets so that every branch looks acceptable, and dismisses study and doubt. She is not shown exploiting anyone: her daily recruiting brings her no shown gain, she accepts refusal without escalation and she disclaims mockery. Her private contempt for Kurumi's skill and her stated urge to tease Inago stay inside her head. What the pages never show is the test that would most define her: a large loss of her own.

## 1. Identity, circumstances and personal history

**Position and work.** Kizuna has been at もちもち企画 for three months on 3 April 2017 by her own count, and introduces herself as both the oldest and the lowest: "みんなより少〜しお姉さんだけど / 社員歴は3カ月だから下っ端だよ〜" (SR; i0016, re-opened). Her title is sales; what she sells and to whom is never shown. The only scene of her work is her departure with Mochiko on 7 April, at Mochiko's polite "月森さん そろそろ出発しましょうか?" (i0063, re-opened; INF MOD a sales visit). Mochiko calls the company "零細" in the same scene. Nana, with eighteen months' tenure, has been there longest of the staff at the party (i0016).

**Former employer.** Before もちもち企画 she worked at 「講報堂」, captioned 「日本一の広告代理店」 (i0062, re-opened); Inago calls it an "ウルトラ大企業". Her only stated memory of it is that nobody there played the market and she was lonely ("全然相場やってる人いなくて / 寂しかったんだも〜ん", SR, i0062). Why she left a leading agency for a small firm is asked by Inago ("なんでこんな小さな会社に…") and never answered (i0063): UNRESOLVED.

**Money.** The pages give her more figures than anyone in V10 except Nana.
- Total assets ¥10,011,927 on her phone app on 3 April, 「目標達成率100%」 and 「達成!」 on the screen, with the caption 「きずな 保有金融資産 10,011,927円」 (OBS HIGH; i0047, re-opened). She says she passed ten million "ちょうど今日" (SR). Of the four people given such captions that evening (Inago 42,175, Nana 834,877, Kurumi 22,963 and Kizuna) she holds about 91.75%: 10,011,927 / 10,911,942 = 0.9175 (ANALYST CALCULATION; the reconciliation audit §4.10 prints 91.7%).
- ¥10,178,597 on 21 April with 「前回+¥166,670」 (OBS HIGH; i0108, re-opened). The difference from 3 April is exactly 166,670 (ANALYST CALCULATION), but whether the gain came from markets or a deposit, and over what period, is UNRESOLVED (V10 reading §F.8).
- 「きずな FX口座残高 3,972,290円」 on 21 April (caption; i0122, re-opened); that this balance is part of the app total is INF, and if it is, it is 39.0% of it (ANALYST CALCULATION).
- Three money words are kept as printed: 総資産 on the screen, 純資産 in her speech, 保有金融資産 in the caption (i0047).

**Home.** On 21 April the page shows her in a tidy flat in a mid-rise building, with a sofa, a small table and laptop, photo frames, a plant, a full-length mirror and a raccoon plush (OBS HIGH; i0108–i0110, re-opened). She is shown alone. Whether she lives alone is not stated; the V10 checkpoint qualified a stronger claim to that effect (V10 checkpoint adjudication D-6). She goes home from the party by taxi (i0055).

**Appearance.** Light hair in a bun, a beauty mark beneath one eye, sleeveless knits and heels (V09 i0153, i0158); on most pages a long fitted skirt (V09 i0158; V10 i0006, i0049, i0053, i0109), and once, seated cross-legged at her desk, black trousers (V09 i0153, re-opened). In the 2016 flashback she wears a suit jacket at an open-plan desk (i0118, re-opened).

**Personal history, and when each piece is disclosed.** The pages give three facts about her past, all disclosed in V10 and all from her own mouth or her own image:
1. She has invested for five years in profit every year without studying: "私全然勉強してなかったけど / 投資始めてから5年連続で収支プラスだし〜" (SR; i0039, re-opened). That she began in 2012 is Kurumi's calculation ("きずなさんは5年前にデビューか…", i0039), not hers. No figure supports the record; it is unverified self-report (V10 reading §F.8). By ANALYST CALCULATION from the name box, she would have been about twenty-two at the start.
2. On 24 June 2016, the day the narrator's exposition gives for the British referendum result, she sat at her desk at 講報堂 smiling flushed at her phone, 「にやぁ…」「んふ〜♡」 (OBS HIGH; i0118, re-opened), and in 2017 she recalls "あのときは大儲けさせてもらったなぁ〜". Kurumi infers a pound short ("きずなさんポンド売りしてたんですか!?") and Kizuna answers "まぁねぇ〜" (i0119, re-opened): a large 2016 profit by self-report and assent, the instrument by Kurumi's inference, size and result unprinted (FXK-V10-C032).
3. No one at her former workplace traded (SR; i0062).

Nothing is shown of her family, birthplace, schooling, first money, the start of her investing or any formative event. No past is given that would explain her present conduct; the history that exists indicates that what V10 shows (pleasure in a fall, a large profit on a political event by her account, attention to markets during the working day) predates the story. The 2016 evidence is one dated scene: she is at her desk with her phone, smiling, on 24 June 2016; that she was following the market then is INF HIGH from her "大儲け" and the narrator's chart, not an observed screen (i0118–i0119). By her own account her investing goes back five years, to about 2012. This is disclosure, not change (section 9).

**Knowledge state at V10's end.** She knows that Kurumi traded FX hard as a student, was wiped out ("退場"), lost more than the others imagine and still owes national tax (i0042–i0045). That the tax is from 2014 rests on Kurumi's "2014年のやつが…!" (i0045) being spoken, as the accepted reading holds at INF MOD; the V10 checkpoint (adjudication C-02, open item V10-U11) keeps a dissenting reading of it as thought. She does not know the scale (the 1億), the bankruptcy or any figure. She believes Kurumi lives with her family ("でもくるみちゃんも実家暮らしだったよね?", i0045, re-opened; her belief, which the page does not confirm). She knows that Nana has bought shares of "the company that makes the ダンぷり app" (i0146, re-opened) and that Nana loves the character メイ; she apparently believes Nana holds カリフラワー (INF MOD; section 3). She does not know that Nana resents her, wished her a large loss, planned to watch the vote for it and on Monday looked up the euro chart (i0124–i0125, i0138–i0141). She knows her own position; no one else, and not the reader, is shown its result (V10 reading §I, FXK-V10-C043).

## 2. Personality and psychology: ease performed, rank measured

**Two temperatures, one subject.** The clearest fact about Kizuna is that the pages show her in two registers that never meet in front of others. In company she gushes: the hug for a newcomer she has just laid eyes on ("わぁ〜! もしかして新人の子お〜? かわいい〜!!", V09 i0157), the slogan, the hearts, the modest boast. Alone, the narration in her own voice is cool and literate: "私にとって / 資産管理アプリを / 開ぐことは / 出勤前に / 顔を見るのと / 同じ" — "自分の存在を / 確かめるための / ルーティン——" (SR in first-person narration lettered freely on the panels; i0109, re-opened). Then, before a mirror in a burst of drawn shards, "上位1%には / 届いていない——", and, aloud with no one to hear, "こんな成績じゃ / ただの凡人止まりだわ" (i0110, re-opened). The content of the two registers is the same — money and rank — but the temperature differs: delight and display outward, dissatisfaction inward (EVAL MOD-HIGH; FXK-V10-C030, E045). V10's governing thesis names her use of money as "net worth as existence" (FXK-V10-C001).

**Values and goals.** She values wealth as both identity and freedom. Having a concrete goal is what makes a person "何者か"; not being able to name one means not knowing "海に行くのか 山に行くのか" (SR; i0021, re-opened). Her goal is 金融資産10億円, "通称—— / 自・由・億!" (i0021–i0022), and the realistic interim is 1億, reachable "学校のクラスで1番になる程度の努力で" (i0031–i0032, re-opened): "現実的だよ / 1億円は". Her app's ten-million goal was reached the day of the party (i0047). The ladder moves as it is climbed: ten million reached on 3 April, a further gain on 21 April, and at once a higher reference, the top 1% of her generation, which she has not reached (i0109–i0110). INF MOD: a goal gradient in which each reached figure is replaced by the next comparison; this is the shape of her dissatisfaction, not a diagnosis.

**Motives, stated and inferred.**
- *Stated.* Financial freedom as what everyone wants ("「経済的自由を手に入れたい」って", i0024); becoming someone through a goal (i0021); companionship in the market as the reason for her recruiting ("寂しかったんだも〜ん", i0062) and joy at a new "仲間" (i0146, re-opened).
- *Inferred.* Self-confirmation through rank (her own word is "存在", i0109; INF MOD-HIGH that rank, not consumption, is what the figure gives her: in her narration she names no purchase, only percentages). Pleasure in volatility and in falls: the accepted transition of her V09 scene reads the leer at a falling chart as the pleasure of a short-seller and of a thrill (FXK-V10-C054, REVISE to INF MOD-HIGH).
- *Unresolved.* Why falls please her beyond those two candidates; why she wants to tease Inago; why she left 講報堂.

**Fears and vulnerabilities.** The one fear she names is to be ordinary — "凡人止まり" — and she names it only to herself (i0110). She expresses no fear of loss ("今回の売り全然怖くないもん", i0123, re-opened) and calls a 1億 investment loss impossible (i0035). Candidate model (V10 reading §G.2): contingent self-worth indexed to rank — a self that is confirmed each morning by a number. If that model is right, her vulnerability is the number itself; the pages never test it, because no loss of hers is shown.

**Contradictions the page sets up.**
- Public modesty and private dissatisfaction: "1000万円なんてそんな珍しくもないよ〜♡ / まぁ同世代だとちょっっとだけ上のほうかなって感じだけど〜♡" (i0050, re-opened) against "凡人止まり" (i0110). The public line is itself a status display (EVAL MOD-HIGH; E024).
- A double standard about money questions: she asks Kurumi "いくら負けたの〜!?" (i0043), then, asked her own assets, calls Kurumi odd for asking — "…やっぱりくるみちゃんは変わってるよね〜 / ……そういうこと / 聞けちゃうんだもん" (i0046, re-opened) — and shows the figure anyway ("別に減るもんじゃないし〜", i0047).
- "下っ端" by tenure (i0016), yet she takes the inner seat that Kurumi offered the new hire, leaving Inago, who had studied a manners book, defeated (V10 reading E006).
- "かんたんよ〜" about investing (i0039) beside a daily ritual of self-measurement (i0109).
- Affection and a stated urge to torment in one breath: "ほんといなごちゃんはかわいいな / 存在してるだけで癒やされちゃうし…" then "ちょっと / いじめたくなっちゃう♡" (inner; i0112, re-opened).
- An apparent contradiction that is not one in her own frame: "私もするとは思ってないよ" about France leaving the EU (i0119) and a bet that the exit camp wins the vote and the euro falls (i0121). She bets on sentiment, not on the exit (V10 reading E048; the qualification audit records the point in this form after correction).

**Coping and self-conception.** Under adversity she is not shown, so her coping is unknown. The nearest test is the morning after the vote, when, on Nana's screen, the euro has gapped against the short she was last shown holding: she arrives early, flushed and laughing behind her hand, then types with a neutral face (i0138, i0141, re-opened). That mood cannot be read as profit or loss (V10 checkpoint open item V10-U01). She sees herself as an investor: she claims five profitable years (i0039) and welcomes Nana as a fellow trader, "仲間が増えて / 嬉しいよ〜" (i0146). (Her "投資家として / アンテナ広いのは偉いと思うし", i0149, is praise of Nana; whether "投資家として" names Nana's new role or her own standpoint is not decided by the page, and the ledger records the line without deciding it, FXK-V10-C040.) She also sees herself as an older sister to the juniors ("お姉さん", i0016), as a teacher of markets ("私が相場のこと / 色々教えてあげられるから", i0036), as someone above most of her generation ("周りより / 持っている / 自覚はあるけれど", i0109) and as one who has "a more realistic method" than the lottery or founding a company (i0027).

## 3. Cognition and decision-making

**What she notices.** Money and position first. She reads others' finances aloud — Nana's comfort as someone living with her parents ("実家暮らしだと余裕なんだね〜 / 生活費親持ちなら…", i0042), Kurumi's presumed savings (i0045) — and reads a fictional man first by wealth: asked by Kurumi whether メイ is her "type", she answers "ん〜 タイプっていうか〜…" and then "この顔・だよ〜? / 絶対この中で一番お金持ってるでしょ〜" (i0152, re-opened; FXK-V10-C041). She is right in substance, as Nana's inner concession shows (i0153). She notices falling prices (V09 i0154–i0156), political stories with market consequences (i0119–i0121), cuteness (V09 i0157; i0112) and someone staring at her (V09 i0156, "…ん?"). What she does not notice is as consistent: Nana's resentment and possessiveness (i0156), the emotional ownership in Nana's fandom, and the scale of what Kurumi lived through.

**How she reasons.**
- *By narrative and analogy.* A goal is a destination (i0021); wealth is a class ranking reached by effort (i0032); a drama's debt-ridden investor is a "間違ったイメージ" spread by television (i0035, re-opened).
- *With statistics as rhetoric.* Household survey shares, the rising share of the wealthy, pensions and the falling birth rate (i0031–i0032, i0038, re-opened) — cited, never sourced, and used to argue that not investing is itself a risk ("全く投資やらないのもリスクあるから", i0037).
- *By scenario trees that end in acceptable branches* (i0123, re-opened): if the exit camp wins, "100万円くらいは利益出ると思うし〜"; if the remain camp wins, "チャートはすでに残留派勝利を織り込み済みだから / 上昇余地は小さく損失は少ない"; and perhaps "事実売り" will turn even that into profit.
- *Against study and against trend.* To Nana's "せめて勉強して十分準備してからのほうが…" she answers "そんなのいらないよ〜" (i0038, re-opened); to Kurumi's chart-based doubt, "くるみちゃん浅いな〜 / だからこそ売りなのにぃ〜" (i0121, re-opened). On the first evening she had already found that Inago "smelled" of someone who had only studied ("なんかまじめに勉強してきただけの人間の匂いがする〜", i0019, re-opened).
- *With certainty markers.* "全然怖くない" (i0123), "これ絶対当たってるから!" (i0152), "大暴落する" (i0121).
- *By generalizing from herself.* "いなごちゃんも頑張ればこれくらい余裕だよ〜!" (i0051), "かんたんよ〜" (i0039).

She concedes facts when they are put to her — "私もするとは思ってないよ / フランス的にもリスクが大きすぎるし" (i0119) — and keeps her conclusion. INF MOD: concessions are absorbed into the frame rather than changing it.

**Uncertainty and priority.** Uncertainty becomes, for her, a reason to act: "こんな割のいい勝負 / やらないほうがおかしい", "悩んでるヒマがあるなら / さっさと行動しなきゃ" (i0124, re-opened; FXK-V10-C034). Between work and trading she chooses trading at her desk during the working day (i0122; Nana, inwardly, "いや仕事をしろよっ…!!!"), and she browses personal matters on the office PC (i0147). No reprimand is shown, and Mochiko is absent from the office after i0063. Toward company money she is easy: "会社のお金なんだから / もっとお高い店にすればいいのに〜" (i0012, re-opened) and "タダ飯食べないとね〜" (i0051); a past outing remembered as "30万はやばかったね〜" is hers by placement (INF MOD), and who incurred it is not shown. The V10 checkpoint adopts an "other-people's-money attitude" only as HYPOTHESIS LOW-MOD (adjudication B-15).

**Typical and exceptional decisions.** Her typical decisions are small and social and recur: to invite a colleague to invest and, when refused, to ask again the next day without pressing (i0041, i0062); to answer a question about her money by showing it (i0046–i0047); to propose a drink and a pricier venue (i0008, i0012). Her exceptional decision — the only trade she enters in present time — is the euro short below. INF MOD: the style is continuous — quick commitment inside a frame in which the choice looks easy — but in the typical decisions, which concern people, she yields when refused, while in the exceptional one, which concerns a market, she waves doubt off.

**Her one fully shown decision: the euro short (Friday 21 April 2017, i0119–i0124).** Kurumi, serving her soy latte, asks whether she is watching the currency market and whether there is a "hot" market now (i0113–i0114). Kizuna recalls her 2016 profit, predicts that the euro area will still be shaken by the theme of France leaving the EU, concedes that the exit itself is unlikely, argues that after Britain's exit the market is hypersensitive ("敏感のビンビン", i0120) so that even a tilt of opinion would make the euro "大暴落" (i0121), dismisses Kurumi's observation that the chart has turned up, and names the weekend's presidential vote as the contest: "私はここでEU離脱派が勝つほうに賭ける…!" (i0121). Then "ってことでユーロドル30枚売り / 発射ぁ〜♪" with a sweep of the hand; the captions read 「EUR/USD 1.07230 30枚売り」 and 「きずな FX口座残高 3,972,290円」 (i0122, re-opened; FXK-V10-C033).
- *Action status.* Executed (INF HIGH from the caption's rate); size 30枚; the currency units per 枚 for her account are not printed, so no notional or yen exposure is computed (V10 checkpoint adjudication B-12). No stop is mentioned. Entered on a Friday before a weekend vote, so held over the weekend (INF from i0121).
- *Alternatives.* The page shows only the sell. The accepted reading records the alternatives open to her as not trading or a smaller trade (V10 reading E048); the risk study (Study C, below) adds waiting for the vote's result.
- *Information held.* A political narrative and her own 2016 experience against a chart Kurumi reads as rising.
- *Outcome.* NOT SHOWN. On Monday, Nana's search shows the euro gapped up from about 1.07 to about 1.086–1.091 (i0140; an adverse move for a short, direction HIGH, size MOD by ANALYST CALCULATION about +0.017). No P/L, exit, balance or margin notice of Kizuna's appears through i0156 (FXK-V10-C038). The page states no result of the vote in words, and neither does this portrait.
- *Failure kinds kept apart* (V10 reading §G.2). Whether her forecast was poor cannot be judged within V10; her risk management is undisclosed rather than shown to be poor; an unfavourable outcome would not by itself be wrongdoing.

**Two other decisions.** On 3 April she shows her total when asked (i0046–i0047), after a visible pause. ## 5. Speech and communication

**Medium.** Everything here is lettering. Lengthened vowels, small kana, hearts and musical notes are written marks of a manner, not a performed voice; balloon tails and placement decide attribution. Four of her lines rest on placement alone: the "30万" remark (i0012), "タヨタ自動車とか…" (i0146) and "ちなみにどのキャラが好きなの?" (i0149), each INF MOD; and "は〜い" (i0063), a reply whose tail runs out of a panel in which she is not drawn, INF HIGH hers because it answers Mochiko's request to her by name. The exclamation "わ! 一気にいった!" at the moment of her trade (i0122) has no fixed speaker (V10 checkpoint open item V10-U11) and is not used as hers.

**Self-reference and address.**
- She calls herself 私 throughout, in speech and in narration (i0021, i0028, i0035, i0039, i0109, i0119, i0121, i0154). She also casts herself by role: "お姉さん" by age and "下っ端" by tenure (i0016).

## 11. Interpretive uncertainty

**Rival readings the pages leave open.**
1. *Competent trader or the next victim?* For competence: five profitable years by her account, a real 2016 profit, a written downside branch, calm on the Monday (V10 reading §L.1). Against, only by analogy or by one instance: Yasuko's 2013 short liquidated after a "ceiling" call (i0071–i0076), Kurumi's uptrend warning borne out by the Monday gap (i0140), the volume's pattern of adverse developments after each "発射" (FXK-V10-C072). The pages do not decide, and neither does this portrait.
2. *Comic trait or a streak of cruelty?* The V10 reading treats her flushes and "いじめたくなっちゃう♡" as comic, with no harm following (§L.6). Against a purely comic reading stand the delight at Kurumi's loss (i0043) and the private contempt (i0114), both observed. This portrait's position (EVAL MOD): she takes pleasure in others' discomfort at the level of curiosity, teasing and private judgment; no act of harm is shown, and reading sadism into it would exceed the page.
3. *Why she recruits.* Her own reason is loneliness for fellow traders (SR, i0062); Kurumi calls it good will (i0064); Nana calls it a religious pitch (i0064). This portrait adds a HYPOTHESIS (LOW-MOD): that others joining confirms the frame in which she measures herself, resting only on the match between her narration of net worth as existence and her daily invitations. It would be overturned by a page where she recruits indifferent to whether anyone joins, or where her loneliness is shown concretely. Against all motive readings that imply self-interest: no gain to her is shown, she accepts refusal, and her joy at Nana's debut matches her stated reason (i0146).
4. *Net worth as existence.* Her words make "existence" explicit (SR HIGH for the words); contingent self-worth is a candidate model only, and a playful morning ritual is a live rival given her "はぁ〜♡" (i0108). No loss tests either; the V10 checkpoint records money-as-identity as supported and her response to loss as untestable (adjudication D-14).
5. *"私も" at i0154.* A mistaken belief that Nana holds カリフラワー (INF MOD) or a general "I too will buy" (V10 reading §L.9).
6. *The Monday morning.* The early arrival, the flushed laugh and the calm typing (i0138–i0141) fit a profit, a loss well absorbed, an exit already made, indifference or simply not having looked. UNRESOLVED (V10 checkpoint open item V10-U01).
7. *Other people's money.* "会社のお金なんだから…" (i0012) and "タダ飯" (i0051) support only a HYPOTHESIS LOW-MOD (V10 checkpoint adjudication B-15); ordinary party banter is the rival, and the "30万" line is hers by placement only.
8. *The V09 chart.* A short-seller's pleasure or a thrill in volatility: the accepted transition keeps both (FXK-V10-C054). The chart is a price display without a pair or ticker; its axis level is not used to identify an instrument (the V09 reading's reconciliation summary).
