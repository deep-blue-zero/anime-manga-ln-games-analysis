## Orientation

**Who she is.** 善波なな (Zenba Nana; ruby ぜんば) is 23 in April 2017 and works as a クリエイター at (株)もちもち企画, the small company that Kogane Mochiko runs as 社長 and where Fukuga Kurumi now works as a clerk. She attended a design 専門学校 with Takane Yasuko, spent the years after it without work, joined the company through Yasuko, and has been a devoted fan of the game character 綺羅メイ since 2010. In the series' final admitted volume she becomes the newest investor in the cast: she opens a securities account in April 2017 and buys her first shares on the morning of Monday 24 April.

## Portrait in brief

Nana is a careful, formal, slightly severe young office worker whose formality is a costume she put on deliberately after years without work, modelled on her employer, Mochiko; underneath it is an anxious, self-deprecating, intensely invested fan who polices other fans in private, talks to a wall scroll of her favourite character in his voice and her own, and measures people by the depth and fidelity of their devotion. She fears investing because she watched her friend Yasuko lose everything in 2013, and in April 2017 she refuses her colleague Kizuna's daily invitations to invest. Within three weeks she becomes a shareholder, not because the fear was argued away but because investing was re-described in the terms of her fandom — owning shares as a higher rank of support — and endorsed by Yasuko, the friend she trusts. She then behaves like a cautious novice inside an incautious story: she researches, computes, keeps about half her savings in her bank account, outside the market, and buys unlevered shares of a proxy company — though that one stock still takes about 44% of her printed assets — while telling herself a ×3 fantasy and an imagined idol's approval. She resents Kizuna for pressing her to invest and enjoys the thought of Kizuna's loss, yet absorbs her advice; she resolves to respect other fans' tastes and breaks the resolution within two pages when her own favourite is touched. When caught out — by her mother, by Kurumi, by colleagues who discover her fandom — she flinches, denies and deflects, and then often says more than she meant to. V10 leaves her holding a small paper loss and pleading with Kizuna, a far richer colleague, not to buy the stock she had marked as hers.

## 3. Personality and psychology

The portrait rests on one structure that the page shows from several sides: a deliberately built public self and an anxious, invested private self, held apart by effort and bridged, in V10, by money.

**The built public self.** Nana says outright that her office manner is a project. Having "脱ニート" thanks to Yasuko, she wants to be "しっかりした社会人… / それこそ社長みたいな" (i0088; SELF_REPORT HIGH; FXK-V10-C025). Yasuko describes the result as "職場だと謎にキリッとしてる" and attributes it to Mochiko's influence, which Nana confirms ("うん…", i0087), and warns her: "形から入るのはいいけど / 無理するなよー?" (i0088). The manner is visible from her first scene: "おはようございます" as she opens the office door (V09 i0148), then "善波です / よろしくお願いします", "了解です", "昼食後にでもいただきます" (V09 i0149), and a sharp correction of colleagues ("福賀さんタオル! / そして寝癖!", V09 i0152). Its maintenance costs her: she fears that the new hire's diligence will strip her of "先輩の威厳" and expose that she is "本当はダメ人間" (i0087; SELF_REPORT HIGH), and she says she is under stress from other things (the recruiting; "ほかのことでストレス溜まっている", i0088).

**The private self.** At home the tone drops to "仕方ないじゃん!", "突然湧いてくるんだもん" (i0056); she talks to a life-size 綺羅メイ scroll and answers herself in his voice ("どうしたんだいななちゃん?", "ななちゃんには俺がいるだろ?", i0059; INFERENCE HIGH that she voices him herself), and asks him "投資なんて怖いこと / しなくていいよね?" (i0060). On social media she presents a welcoming fan ("同担◎他担も大歓迎です", i0082) while privately adding a fan's account to a locked 「同担監視リスト」 of 617 accounts after reading her gift post as a status display ("これってマウントですよね?", "ニワカめ…", i0083–i0084; FXK-V10-C024). Her narration names that habit as making her "不健康な心" (i0100; SELF_REPORT).

**Values and goals.** (a) Fidelity and depth of devotion: she judges fans by whether their understanding is deep and faithful ("解釈違い", "記号的解釈", "語るなら最低限原作を触って——", i0100, i0153). (b) Rank among fans: she resents fans who "お金だけかけてマウントしてくる" (i0100) and wants support of a higher order — "〝格〟が違う! / 応援の〝質〟が違う! / 愛の〝深さ〟が違う!" (i0101; SELF_REPORT HIGH). (c) Competence and respectability at work (above). (d) Loyalty to Yasuko. Money for its own sake is not among them: she says she is "そこまでお金に困ってない" and, asked what she wants, blushes and says "別に…" (i0041); the blush has an object, the merchandise her mother calls "おもちゃ" (i0057; FXK-V10-E026, EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH).

**Fears.** Investing, privately and strongly ("怖いこと", i0060), stronger than the reasoned caution she voices in public (i0038; FXK-V10-E016). Exposure: of her real competence (i0087), of her fandom at work ("こっ… 殺してくれ…っ!" as colleagues open the character list, i0148, a comic hyperbole in thought), of her chart-watching (i0145), of her household contribution (i0057). Loss of what she treats as hers: "お金の暴力で / 私のメイくんを / 取らないで…!" (i0155).

**Vulnerabilities.** Envy, recurrent and denied: "羨ましいなぁ" in 2013 (i0072), "羨ましくなんかないですっ…!" in 2017 when Kizuna teases her about Kizuna's ten million (i0051). Upward comparison with Yasuko, silent outside and sharp inside: when Kurumi announces Yasuko's serialization, Nana says nothing ("……", i0080–i0081) and thinks "そう… / やす子ちゃんは特別…!" and "それに引きかえ / 私は——" (i0081; FXK-V10-C023). Susceptibility to the voices of people she trusts (section 4).

**Contradictions, each shown on the page.** Public caution and private certainty (i0038 against "3000%成功する!", i0103). Contempt for Kizuna's trading during work ("いや仕事を / しろよっ…!!!", inner, i0122) against her own watching of the pre-open board at her desk from 8:50 (i0136–i0141; working hours are not printed, so the irony is the reader's, not the page's; FXK-V10-E055). A public welcome to fellow fans against a surveillance list. "決してオタクなどではありませんので" (i0148) against a breathless lecture on the franchise that follows almost at once ("ええもちろん! / 知らないほうが非常識まであります!", i0148; i0149). A resolve to respect another fan's taste (i0151) broken within two pages when Kizuna characterises 綺羅メイ (i0153). Resentment of Kizuna's recruiting against adopting Kizuna's own maxim ("月森さんの「さっさと行動」は / 一理ある…", i0126). These are not inconsistencies of the record but the character's own; the page usually stages them as comedy.

**Coping.** Four habits recur. *Withdrawing to the idol*, whose voice she supplies and who soothes or authorises her (i0059–i0060; "賢い選択をしようぜ / ななちゃん", i0127; a fist bump over the plan, i0131). *Watching and listing*: the 617-account list, a plan to "「監視」" Kizuna's loss on the day of the French vote (i0125), and later "つまんないカニの監視に戻ろう" (i0141) — one vocabulary for fandom, rival and position (INFERENCE MODERATE that it is one habit). *The persona*: composure, surnames, glasses. *Deflection and denial* when exposed (section 5).

**Self-conception.** In her own words she is a "ダメ人間" who makes "結構ミス" (i0087), someone set against the "特別" Yasuko (i0081), and a fan whose surveillance makes her heart "不健康" (i0100). Shareholding briefly gives her a higher image of herself: "私がメイくんの世界を支えるんだ…!" (i0102) and "これで投資家デビュー…" (i0143). EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH: her low self-estimate and her high estimate of her devotion coexist, and the purchase draws on the second to answer the first.

## 4. Cognition and decision-making

Nana's one consequential decision within the boundary — from refusal to a first purchase — is traced on the page in unusual detail, almost day by day. The account below gives what she noticed, how she reasoned and what the page leaves open. Its money figures are those of the reconciliation audit's chain for V10's new figures (§4.10), which closes exactly.

**Before the decision: caution with an origin.** At the welcome party on 3 April (the date is inferred; the first printed V10 date is 7 April, i0061), when Kizuna presses the newcomer Inago to invest, Nana reasons in public: "素人が投資に手を出しても / 失敗する気がします", "せめて勉強して / 十分準備してからのほうが…" (i0038); Kizuna answers "そんなのいらないよ〜". At home that night the caution is fear (i0060). On 7 April she refuses Kizuna's daily question ("始めてないです", "毎日聞かれても困ります", "なんでそんな / やらせたがるんですか", "だからって巻き込まないでください", i0062), calls the recruiting "宗教の勧誘" and asks Kurumi, who defends Kizuna's good faith, "福賀さんも敵なんですか?" (i0064; FXK-V10-C021). Her caution is real and rests on a witnessed instance; it is also undifferentiated: her fear is of "投資" in general (i0060) though her evidence concerns FX.

**The unlocking: an instrument separated, cues assembled (8 April).** Asked how to stop Kizuna, Yasuko says FX is "ただのストレス発生装置" and Nana agrees eagerly ("だよねー / 危ないよね!?"), but Yasuko adds that stocks are "アリ", that she holds shares of her own publisher bought "応援の気持ちで", that cash shares have no forced liquidation, and that Nana could buy shares related to 「ダンぷり」 (i0090–i0093; FXK-V10-E039–E040). The page then draws Nana's decision as a jigsaw of remembered cues — Kurumi's "善意", Kizuna's "経済的自由" and "投資すればいいのに", Yasuko's "別にやってもいいんじゃないか?" and support-buying, her own 2013 refusal, her surveillance list — whose last piece fits (i0094–i0096), and she says: "やす子ちゃん / 株のこと / 詳しく教えて" (i0096; FXK-V10-C027). EVALUATION HIGH (the reading's): the decision is social assembly; every voice except her mother's and her own fear points one way. INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH (this portrait's): the separation of stocks from FX by the very person whose ruin grounded her fear is what made entry thinkable.

**The reframing: shares as fandom rank.** In her own narration she turns from the "不健康な心" of fan surveillance to an account just opened ("これなら…!", "でもそんなことで悩むのはもう終わり!", i0100), and the purchase becomes "高次元の / 推し活" (i0101): owning the franchise's company is support of a higher "格", "質" and "深さ" than buying goods to show off, "キャラを超越し / 母体そのものを応援!" (i0101). On news of a new 「ダンぷり」 app she is certain: "長年ダンぷりを見てきた私にはわかる!", "3000%成功する!", "セールスランキングトップに貼りつきだ!" (i0102–i0103), grins ("にまぁ…") and deposits 100,000円 (SCR i0103; 0.12 of her printed assets, CALC). Candidate models (labels, from the V10 reading §G.3 and Study C): status seeking and social comparison within the fandom; money entangled with identity; an illusion of knowledge in which fan expertise reads as market foresight; certainty language.

**An information error, and the first limit.** Relying on Yasuko's hearsay that Japanese shares trade in 100-share units, she submits "カリフラワー100株 / 推し買い発射…!" and is refused: 「売買単位:1000株」 (SCR i0105; FXK-V10-E044). The error is one of information, not of judgment under loss.

**21 April: contempt, a borrowed maxim and a sound doubt.** When Kizuna sells 30 lots of EUR/USD at her desk before a weekend vote, Nana thinks "いや仕事を / しろよっ…!!!" (i0122), "大損すればいいのに…!" and imagines Kizuna ruined (i0125; FXK-V10-C035). Yet the same day she grants that Kizuna's "さっさと行動" has a point and that she should "今すぐ動くべき" if she wants カリフラワー (i0126). **22 April: a plan with a rule inside a fantasy.** She anchors on the chart's past highs (">2000円を何度か", i0129), takes another franchise's hit as a template, aims at "株価3倍" (i0130) and lays out a two-stage plan: profit on Kani, then buy "本命の銘柄 / カリフラワー" (i0130), agreed in a fist bump with the imagined メイ (i0131). Candidate models (labels): anchoring on past highs; representativeness; anticipatory pleasure; motivated reasoning through a self-voiced authority. Counter-observations: research, a sizing rule, a cash instrument and a self-correction.

**24 April: execution and first loss.** She watches the pre-open board from 8:50 ("これが寄前気配ってやつか…", "ドキドキしてきた…", i0136–i0137). Her first thoughts are pride and a pang: "これで投資家デビュー…", "やっぱり初めての株は / カリフラワーがよかったな…", then "さて仕事仕事…" (i0143). She is deflated ("含み損8000円かぁ…", i0145) and says nothing of selling or buying more (FXK-V10-E058; V10 checkpoint D-12: deflation, no chasing).

**How she reasons, in sum.** (a) She notices social signals first — who is mounting whom, who is a "ニワカ", who is singling her out ("なんで私ばっかり投資に誘われるんですかね…", i0064). (b) She reasons from a domain she knows deeply (the franchise) into one she does not (the market), with high certainty about the thesis. (c) She is careful about size and instrument and can compute, though her reserve rule still leaves one stock at about 44% of her printed assets; her rules are simple and executed at the moment of funding. (d) She takes advice selectively: from Yasuko readily, from Kizuna grudgingly and unacknowledged, from her mother not at all. (e) Under conflicting pulls she personifies the choice and has メイ resolve it. EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH: confident about why, careful about how much — "prudence within desire", in the V10 reading's phrase (§G.3).

**Response to uncertainty.** She converts uncertainty about the product into certainty language and treats uncertainty about money with a reserve. About other people's uncertainty she is curious in a way she disowns in others: on the Monday, seeing the euro gapped against Kizuna's short, she wonders "いくら負けたんだろう…? / どんな気持ちなんだろう…?" (i0140) — EVALUATION MODERATE (this portrait's): the same curiosity about another's loss that Kizuna showed toward Kurumi ("いくら負けたの〜!? / 気になる〜!!", i0043). When Kizuna stays calm, the pleasure turns to "なんか悔しい…" (i0141). The page shows no result of Kizuna's position (FXK-V10-C038).

**What the specialist studies imply for her decisions.** Study C (*Risk, loss and financial self-destruction across persons*, [studies/FX_KURUMI_RISK_LOSS_AND_FINANCIAL_SELF_DESTRUCTION.md](../studies/FX_KURUMI_RISK_LOSS_AND_FINANCIAL_SELF_DESTRUCTION.md)) reads every depicted trader's decision episodes against explanatory models. Its consequences for Nana, put in this portrait's words: by that study's count she is one of two people in the series who set a rule before money was at stake and whose rule held within the boundary. The other is Yasuko after 2013. Her restraint is not the rule she stated before her 2014 trading — "低レバでボラが小さい通貨しか触んねーからな" (V02 i0156; FXK-V02-D029) — which her first V03 trade breached (about 17×, V03 i0006–i0008; V03 reading E001); what held, in Study C's account, is her V03 abstention, her V04 exit after reviewing her own record (V04 i0046) and her cash shares of 2017 (Study C §2.7), while her FX account went from 76,573円 to 71,052円 before it was shown fully withdrawn by V07 ("全額出金済み"; reconciliation audit §4.6). The accepted records anchor both persons; the count itself is Study C's. The kind of restraint that holds in the series is the kind executed at once and changing where the money sits — her transfer, which moved 300,000円 to the securities account and left about half her savings (434,877円) in her bank account, outside the market, and her purchase of shares without leverage are of that kind, while her in-the-moment doubts are routed through fantasy; her chain of funding never starts, but the test is weak, because no loss arrives that she wants to chase. That study also reads V10's two new traders as the series' two poles restaged — Kizuna's framing in which every branch pays and a bias to act, against Nana's allocation rule — with both outcomes withheld. Nothing in it, or here, decides what her rule would do under a large loss.

## 5. Behavioural patterns

**Stress responses.** A physical flinch when a self-presentation is exposed — 「ギクッ」 at her mother's demand (i0057) and again when Kurumi says she watched charts all day (i0145) — followed by denial ("きゅ… 休憩中に / ちょっと見ただけですよっ!", i0145) or deflection: cornered over her living costs, she turns the party's attention to Kurumi ("それより福賀さんは / どうなんですか?", i0042). **Mistakes and corrections.** The unit error (i0105), corrected by computing the cost and declining to stake everything (i0107). ## 6. Speech and communication

**The medium's limits.** Everything here is written register on a still page: balloon text, balloon shapes (rounded for speech; dark, spiky or boxed for thought and narration), katakana and rubies used as typographic voice, and the speaker fixed by tails and placement. No performed voice exists. Attributions that rest on placement are flagged where they matter (section 13).

**Self-reference.** 私 in every register: at home ("私にも色々お金が必要なの!", i0057), at work ("私は知ってるんですよ…!", i0065), with Yasuko ("私って結構ミス多いし", i0087), in narration ("私がメイくんの世界を支えるんだ…!", i0102), in thought ("私のメイくんを", i0155). In the voice she gives 綺羅メイ she becomes "ななちゃん" (i0059, i0127). No other first-person form is shown.

**Forms of address.** At work she uses surname + さん for every colleague — "月森さん", "酒田さん" (i0037), "福賀さん" (i0042, i0064). She refers to Mochiko as 萌智子社長 or 社長 when speaking of her to others (to colleagues at the party, i0026; to Yasuko, i0088); no direct address of Mochiko by Nana is printed (she answers Mochiko's instruction with a bare "了解です", V09 i0149). The one exception is her self-introduction, where the new hire is "いなごさん" (i0016); from the party on, Inago is "酒田さん". She addresses no one shown as 先輩 (she uses the word only of her own standing, "先輩の威厳", i0087), so her "月森さん" for the older Kizuna, who joined after her, is her uniform workplace form rather than a slight (INFERENCE MODERATE). For Yasuko her form tracks the setting and her guard: "高根さん" at the office when Yasuko starts to talk about their school days (V09 i0150); "やす子さん" at the office in V10 as the reading attributes the line (i0010); "やす子ちゃん" in private and in thought (i0067, i0080–i0081, i0096). How others address her is listed in the orientation table: her employer calls her by her surname (善波さん, V09 i0149); Yasuko uses the bare nickname ばなな, which Nana herself offers to the newcomer (i0016); Kizuna and Kurumi add ちゃん to it, and Inago 先輩.

**Registers.** Five can be distinguished.
- *Office*: です/ます, short and crisp, with nominal verdicts ("自由度を間違えた働き方改革の敗北です", i0007) and inverted emphasis ("宗教の勧誘ですよ / あれは", i0064); reasoned objections hedged with "気がします" and "せめて…" (i0038).
- *Home*: plain and petulant — "仕方ないじゃん!", "〜もん" (i0056), "必要なの!", "おもちゃじゃない!" (i0057), "…もうっ" (i0057).
- *With Yasuko*: plain, warm and candid — "だよねー / 危ないよね!?" (i0090), "うん…" (i0087–i0088), a direct request ("株のこと / 詳しく教えて", i0096); in 2013, the same plainness with "へへへ…" and teasing (i0066, i0079).
- *Online and inner (fandom)*: public politeness with emoji and hashtags ("気軽に絡んで下さい", i0082) over a private vocabulary of fandom — "同担", "夢女", "マウント", "ニワカ", "解釈違い", "推し活", "推し買い", "母体" (V10 reading §K) — and crude contempt ("きっしょ…!", "クッソどうでもいい", i0084); under extreme provocation a katakana-tinged menace, "浅すギる理解デ / メイくんヲ語るナ…!" (i0153).
- *Narration*: in 第49話 her first-person narration is literate and mock-epic about the franchise and her plan (i0097–i0102), and writes 「メイ君」 where her speech says "メイくん" (i0099; V10 checkpoint D-11).

**Rhetorical habits.** (a) *The cornering polite question*: "これってマウントですよね?", "違うなら鍵アカでやってますよね?" (to a screen, i0083); "福賀さんも敵なんですか?" (i0064); "…ダンぷりの / アプリ作ってる / 会社ですけど" followed by "なにか?" (i0146). EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH: her aggression takes the form of courteous interrogation; it rarely becomes an outright statement. (b) *The lecture* when her domain is touched (i0149). (c) *Hyperbole*, in thought ("3000%", i0103; "殺してくれ", i0148). (d) *The launch idiom* "発射" for submitting an order ("推し買い発射…!", i0105; "買い発射…!!", i0135), a word Yasuko used in her presence in 2013 ("3枚売り発射!", i0072). Her own use precedes Kizuna's "発射ぁ〜♪" (i0122), so she did not borrow it from Kizuna (V10 checkpoint D-5; FXK-V10-D036), and the page shows no borrowing in either direction (V10 reading §K).

**Shifts under pressure.** Composure breaks first into stammer — "きゅ… 休憩中に" (i0145), "わっ私は一般常識として" (i0148), "えっええ… / 株ですが…" (i0146) — then into one of two exits: over-disclosure (the lecture) or an emotional appeal. Even the appeal keeps its formality and apology: "月森さん / すみません…", "それだけは / やめてください…!" (i0156). The single break of office register before her seniors is "だめっ…!" (i0147).

**Humour and silence.** Her humour is deadpan judgment (i0007) and friendly needling (i0079); most comedy around her is at her own expense (gag faces, the kneeling "財務に確認します!" before an imagined mother, i0132). Silence is a recurring move: "……" before a cutting thought or instead of a reaction (i0080–i0081, i0083, i0146). Strategic silence also governs information: she tells colleagues only that she bought "the company that makes the 「ダンぷり」 app", not the name, size or loss (i0146; FXK-V10-C043), and lets Kizuna's apparent misunderstanding stand (section 7).

**Inner versus outer.** The difference is of content as well as politeness. Outwardly: caution, composure, denial of fandom, refusal. Inwardly: certainty, contempt ("ニワカめ…"), wishes for a rival's ruin ("大損すればいいのに…!", i0125), fear ("やだ… / やめて…", i0155). The page shows the inner voice often and lets it reach speech only once, as a plea.

**What Study B's argument means for her.** In the series' accounting of obligations, Nana's choice is one in which the chooser bears the risk openly: the money is her own savings, she bounds the exposure herself, and the outcome is not graded within the boundary. The two people in her life with the most relevant experience do not protect her from the market — Yasuko, whose ruin taught her fear, advises her into stocks as an act of support, and Kurumi, whose loss is far larger than Nana knows, watches her trade with friendly curiosity (FXK-V10-C039) — which, in Study B's account, places her in the series' recurring pattern of withheld or absent warnings, here without blame, since neither is shown foreseeing harm. The one obligation attached to her money is the household's claim, which the page sets beside her transfer and leaves open. And the cost that V10 makes her feel at its close is not money at all but a threatened sense of possession, priced by a richer colleague's casual intention. ## 13. Interpretive uncertainty

**Rival readings.**
1. *Investing as recovery* (V10 reading §L.4): she names her surveillance unhealthy and turns to an activity with rules (half savings, cash, research). Against it: the status motive and certainty language (i0101, i0103) and the surveillance turned on Kizuna (i0125). Both are on the page; the boundary ends before either prevails.
2. *Caution as principle or as untested anxiety.* Her public caution is reasoned (i0038) but her fear generalises from one witnessed FX liquidation to "投資" as such (i0060) and dissolves once the instrument is separated; this portrait reads it as anxiety with a real origin (EVALUATION MODERATE).
3. *Her rule as temperament or as borrowed doctrine.* It may be her own prudence or Yasuko's doctrine (cash shares, no forced liquidation, i0092) applied; her desire is large and coexists with the rule (i0103, i0132–i0133), which weighs against a cautious-temperament reading. UNRESOLVED.
4. *Her objection to Kizuna: moral or rivalrous.* Both are shown (i0122; i0155); their weight is EVALUATION, not fact.
5. *Comedy or psychology.* V10's live competing thesis reads the volume as a workplace-and-fandom comedy that keeps consequences small (FXK-V10-C002). Much of Nana's interior is drawn in gag form (the kneeling, the katakana menace, the wedding fantasy). This portrait treats the gags as evidence of disposition but not of severity: they show what she wants and fears, not how strongly she would act.

**Fragile attributions and guards.** The thoughts about a 10万 loss and a lost passbook and seal at i0044 are Inago's, not Nana's (V10 checkpoint C-01). "今日ずっとチャート見てたでしょ?" (i0145) is Kurumi's; "株はまだまだ勉強中だし!" (i0146) is Kurumi's by placement (INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH). Kizuna's belief that Nana holds カリフラワー is INFERENCE MODERATE with an alternative kept (FXK-V10-C070).

**Open questions (none answerable within V10).** Her household contribution (V10-U08); the "前作のアプリ" bitter memory (V10-U05); whether the 300,000円 transfer was made on 22 April and the type of her accepted order (V10-U04); her Kani position after its first day and her two-stage plan (V10-U04; NOT SCOREABLE); Kizuna's purchase and answer (V10-U03; NOT SCOREABLE); Mochiko's knowledge of her staff's investing (V10-U13).

**Limits of extrapolation.** One decision path, about three weeks and a flashback. Her rule has met a loss of 8,000円, about 2% of the position's cost (CALC), and nothing larger.
