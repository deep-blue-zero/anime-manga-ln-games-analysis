## 1. Orientation

**Who.** 福賀梢 (Fukuga Kozue) is the mother of the series' protagonist, 福賀くるみ (Fukuga Kurumi), and the wife of Kurumi's father. She was a homemaker who began trading foreign exchange from home in 2006, lost what she and her family had — about 2000万円 gathered from her own savings, the household's money, a cancelled insurance policy, the down-payment saved for a house and cash taken from her parents — in the autumn of 2008, concealed the loss from her husband, and died by suicide on 13 February 2009. Her death is stated, never shown, and this portrait describes it no further.

**Place in the story.** She is alive on the page only in the past. V01's first chapter (October 2008 – February 2009) gives her last months through her daughter's retrospective narration; V06's 第28話 (1998 and May 2006 – October 2008) gives the rise and fall of her trading through a flashback that includes her own trading journal. Everywhere else she is a memory, a photograph, a voice or an image in someone else's mind, and a sum of money: the 2000万円 that her fifteen-year-old daughter resolves to win back, and whose pursuit drives the series. She is therefore both a person with her own evidence and the origin of the protagonist's project; this portrait treats her as the first and does not reduce her to the second.

**Boundary and spoilers.** The portrait covers the accepted V01–V10 main narrative (to V10 i0156, Monday 24 April 2017) and includes V10 end-of-unit knowledge. Nothing after V10, no paratext (afterwords, flaps, jackets, bonus pages) and nothing from outside the admitted pages is used. All evidence about her after V01 i0033 is retrospective or mediated.

**Name forms and where each is established.** In V01–V05 she is never named: the door sign of her and her husband's room reads 「ぱぱ・ままのへや♡」 (V01 i0010, the first identification), Kurumi calls her "お母さん" and, as a small child in memory, "ママ" (V02 i0111; V05 i0160–i0161), and her husband calls her "母さん" (V01 i0032; V02 i0030). V06 names her: her husband in her own daydream says 「梢(こずえ)」 (V06 i0065), the narrator says "くるみの母・梢" (i0066), and her passbook reads 「福賀 梢 様」 (i0075). V08 labels her 「母(故人)」 in Kurumi's imagined family tree (V08 i0047). This portrait follows the project's knowledge-boundary rule: where it narrates V01–V05 events it says "Kurumi's mother" or "the mother", because the reader of those volumes does not yet know her name; where it narrates V06 and later, and where it speaks of the whole person, it says Kozue, except where a passage is seen through the child's eyes (section 7's account of September – October 2008, where she is "the mother"). The two are the same person (OBSERVATION HIGH from V06's household, journal and door sign). Her husband is "the father" or "her husband"; his given name 郁夫 appears in this portrait only inside her quoted words, because it is printed for Kozue's husband (V06 i0062, i0064, i0071, i0082) and identified with Kurumi's father only by INFERENCE HIGH.

**The portrait in one paragraph.** A warm, teasing, food-giving mother who wanted something of her own and a little more money for her family, who tried a "passive" trade she had first called too good to be true, and who, when it turned against her, read the fall as a bargain, poured in first her own money and then everyone else's, hid the loss from everyone, flipped from patient income-seeking to a plan of 400× leverage for an instant recovery and then a 100-lot short whose size and leverage are not printed, and ended with a journal that resents the stop-loss rule, feels watched by the market, does not know how to apologize to her family and wishes to disappear. In her last months, as her daughter tells it, she took the burden on herself in words, refused her daughter's help, worked long hours, kept the secret from her husband, and, pressed to confess, turned on her daughter for a moment before apologizing. V09 adds her husband's late account: she objected to his transfers, he had told her to leave all money matters to him, and she never consulted him; he now thinks he made it hard for her to.

## 2. How the portrait reaches the reader

Kozue's evidence arrives in three layers held by three different people, each disclosed later than the last. The layers do not have the same authority, and this portrait keeps them apart.

1. **V01 (2008–09), through her daughter.** Chapter 1 is narrated by Kurumi looking back on her fifteenth year: captions ("家のお金 約2000万円を 勝手に使い込んでいたと知った", V01 i0014), scenes in which the mother speaks, and Kurumi's thoughts. What the mother says on these pages is reported speech inside a grieving child's account (OBSERVATION of the speech; the framing is Kurumi's). Kurumi's judgments of her — "ばか", too poor at maths for middle-school problems (V01 i0035–i0036) — are another character's interpretation, made in anger after her funeral.
2. **V06 第28話 (1998, 2006–2008), through a flashback that is partly her own journal.** Dated journal entries in a plain hand are her written SELF_REPORT. Captions are a narrator's (NARRATOR_PRESENTATION), including the line that she "believed" (彼女は信じていた, V06 i0067) and two first-person captions in her voice at the liquidation (i0080). Some scenes — her thoughts, the things she says aloud when alone, her daydreams, her visit to her parents' safe — go beyond anything a journal could contain. The chapter ends on the adult Kurumi weeping over the open journal (i0090), so it is framed as Kurumi's reading. The project records this chapter's authority as mixed: partly omniscient narration, partly the journal's self-report ([V06 reading](../readings/FX_KURUMI_V06_DEEP_READING.md) §L item 7). Her figures carry a reliability flag (§F.10): her claim on 2 September 2008 that the rate had gone "back and forth between 100 and 90 for a year" is contradicted by her own entry of 18 July, "103円突破!" (i0068, i0070; V06 checkpoint B-7).
3. **V09 (memory), through her husband.** The father tells Kurumi that "母さんのときは 相談もなかったからな…" over a remembered quarrel about his transfers, in which he tells her to leave all money matters to him (V09 i0072–i0073). This is his SELF_REPORT and his memory, offered in early 2015, about six years after her death and many years after the scene it recalls (Kurumi is drawn as a small child with crayons, i0072).

Between these layers she appears as image and echo: a smiling woman in a framed family photograph (V01 i0014; V04 i0072; V05 i0154; V07 i0037, i0112–i0113; V08 i0065), a straw-hatted mother in a beach memory (V05 i0159–i0161), a face and a voice in Kurumi's crisis (V05 i0073; V07 i0080, i0147), an inset of her 2008 plan inside Kurumi's 2015 plan (V07 i0145), a crossed-out face in Kurumi's imagined family tree (V08 i0047), a memorial portrait (V09 i0080) and a grave, 「福賀家之墓」 (V09 i0055–i0061). These are evidence about how others hold her, and only rarely about her.

Three consequences follow for everything below. First, there is no present-time interiority after October 2008 except as Kurumi reports it, and none at all for the last four months of her life beyond what Kurumi saw. Second, the journal is the only place where she speaks without an audience, and it is written by a person under strain about her own losses; it is evidence of what she thought and felt, not a reliable ledger. Third, V06 and V09 are **late revelations**: they change what the reader knows about her, not who she was. No development of hers happens after February 2009; what develops is the picture.

## 3. Identity, household and the marriage's money roles

**Circumstances.** Kozue is a married homemaker ("主婦業" in Kurumi's account, V01 i0009; "主婦の彼女", narrator, V06 i0064) living with her husband and their only child in an apartment whose parents' room carries the child-drawn sign 「ぱぱ・ままのへや♡」 (V01 i0010–i0011; V06 i0061, i0081). Her age is never printed. The family owns a desktop PC from 1998 that the small Kurumi is scolded for touching (V06 i0058), later a laptop on a low round table where she trades (i0066, i0084). Her own parents are alive in 2008: an elderly couple whose home, her 実家, holds cash in a safe (i0077). She had savings from before her marriage, "独身時代の貯金" (i0071), which implies money of her own from her single years (INFERENCE MODERATE; her pre-marriage work, if any, is never shown). The family was saving toward a house: the "マイホームの頭金" is among the money she later commits (i0077), and "広い庭付きのマイホーム" is in her dream (i0069).

**The marriage's money roles.** Two holders, speaking from opposite sides and years apart, describe the same arrangement. In May 2006, alone at home, she says to herself, "郁夫さん 私には働かなくていいって言うけど / くるみも もう中学生よ…?" (V06 i0062), and imagines surprising him because "古い人だから インターネットで お金稼げること 知らなそうだし" (i0064). In V09 her husband remembers telling her, during a quarrel about one of his transfers, "お金のことは全部俺に任せて / お前は家のことだけやってたらいいんだ" (V09 i0072). Each statement is OBSERVATION HIGH as a printed line. That they describe one durable arrangement — he earned, claimed authority over money matters and did not expect her to earn, while she kept the home — is INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH from their convergence. It was an arrangement of authority and expectation, not of access. The household passbook she later drains to zero is in her own name (「福賀 梢 様 … 総合口座通帳」, V06 i0075), her journal resolves to put "家のお金" into her trading account with no one shown knowing (i0072), and the V06 reading counts that passbook as the household's money in the composition of the 2000万 (V06 reading §F.7). Day-to-day access to the household's savings was hers. The arrangement matters to every later section: it is the ground on which her wish to "do something" grew, the reason a profit would have been a surprise, and, in her husband's own later reading, part of why she did not tell him when it went wrong (V09 i0073).

**His absences.** The father's work moves him. She objects to it in his memory ("また転勤なの?", "子供が小さいこと 会社は知ってるんでしょ?", V09 i0072), and by autumn 2008 he is on a single posting away from home (単身赴任, V01 i0014), absent even at New Year (V01 reading §H.5, i0024). The 2008 loss and its concealment take place while she is effectively alone with a fifteen-year-old.

**Appearance and presentation.** Long dark hair, drawn shoulder-length in Kurumi's early-childhood memory (V02 i0111) and long thereafter; an apron and a long dress at home (V06 i0061, i0068), a basket bag and a fringe clip outdoors (i0060); a white dress and a wide straw hat at the beach (V05 i0160); in V01's last months, office clothes — blouse, skirt and jacket — for the paid work she took on (V01 i0016). The art marks her states on her face: closed-eye smiles in the good years (V06 i0061, i0068), ringed eyes and dark-edged thought balloons when she decides to stake everything (i0071, i0075, i0082), spiral pupils at the liquidation (i0080), a hidden, haggard face in her last months (V01 i0023, i0026).

## 5. Personality and psychology

**What she wanted.** Her stated goals at the start are modest and centred on others and on a self of her own. In May 2006, with her daughter now a middle-schooler, she wants "なにか やっときたい のよねー", observes that "お金はあるに 越したことはないし", and finds it wearing that her working mum-friends keep asking about her hobbies (V06 i0062; SELF_REPORT, said aloud to herself when alone). The first gains she imagines are lunch money (i0064) and her husband's astonishment; the narrator's caption over her first journal page is "ちょっと豊かな暮らしを目指して…!" (i0065). As profits accumulate the goal grows into a family picture — a house with a garden, a second child, a Sheltie — summed up as "私たちは もっと幸せな家族に なれる——" (i0069), and at the moment she stakes her savings it becomes "ここで買えば みんな幸せになれる" (i0071). The constant is the family as the beneficiary and herself as the one who brings it the gift: "私が稼いだの!" in her daydream (i0065), "郁夫さん、くるみちゃん 私、頑張るからね!" in her journal (i0071). Within the boundary no page marks this family ideal as a pretext for something else (Study C's overturn condition for her case), so the portrait takes it as her real stated motive, with the qualification that it comes through a chapter of mixed authority.

**What else the page shows driving her.** Alongside the stated motive the art draws appetite: three rising cries of "買いたい…!", ringed eyes and panting fill one panel, and in the next the dream family ("夢が… 叶う…!") is drawn in a thought cloud over the same ringed eyes (V06 i0075). The project records this as a craving "as drawn", a visual presentation, not her self-description and not a diagnosis. The two are not alternatives on the page: craving and dream are drawn together, in consecutive panels over the same eyes. A third element is social: the boom on television ("主婦がFXで3000万", i0059; "FX長者出現!?", i0067), the bestseller with a housewife's testimonial (i0062), her mum-friends at a café (i0067). A fourth is pride in competence: "だいぶFXのことが分かって来た。 そろそろ初心者卒業かも!" (journal, 2 September 2008, i0070), written as the fall was already under way.

**What she feared.** Discovery and the break-up of the family. "郁夫さんにバレたら 一家離散よ…" (V06 i0082) is her own line; in V01 the fifteen-year-old's stated reason for keeping the secret is the same fear in her own words — "家族がバラバラに なるのは嫌だった" (V01 i0014) — which shows the fear was shared in the household, whoever voiced it first. Beyond discovery, the journal shows shame and isolation: "こんなこと誰にも言えない…。 息が苦しい…。" (16 September, i0073) and, after the ruin, "家族になんて謝ればいいかわからない。" (20 October, i0087).

**Vulnerabilities and contradictions.** Five tensions recur, and none is resolved on the page.
- *The sceptic who buys.* Her first reaction to the boom is ridicule (i0060); she buys because buying "is not difficult" (i0064). Her scepticism is not abandoned but overridden by ease.
- *Responsibility in words, concealment in deed.* "子供はそんなこと 考えなくてもいいの! これは私の 責任なんだから…" (V01 i0016) is an explicit acceptance of responsibility in words — the V01 reading records it as such (§I) — and it is spoken by the person who is hiding the loss from her husband and has just asked her child to help hide it (i0014). The same reading concludes that in V01 no one accepts responsibility toward another after harm (§I); the two records are compatible if, as this portrait reads it (EVALUATION), the words took the burden without disclosure or repair. The words take the burden; the secret spreads it.
- *Protective and burdening at once.* She refuses her daughter's labour and tells her to study (i0016), yet the secret she lays on a fifteen-year-old is a burden whose weight the page never shows her grasping (V01 §I; V01 checkpoint C-16: "a plea only").
- *Self-knowledge against her acts.* She names the danger before it arrives — "強制ロスカットされたらどうしよう" (i0072) — and in the same entry resolves to put in the household's money; the household passbook is at zero by late September (i0075). She writes the rule she breaks — "損切りしなければ勝っていた。 でも損切りしないとダメらしい。" (i0087) — holding it as hearsay ("らしい") and resenting it in the same sentence.
- *A wish to surprise her husband, and a fear that he will find out.* The same man is the imagined admirer of her success (i0065) and the feared discoverer of her failure (i0082).

**Coping.** In order of appearance: hope and prayer ("あとはもう、祈るだけだ", i0077); averaging down and adding margin; concealment through small lies ("ごめんね 今日はちょっと体調悪くて…" to Kurumi, i0073); withdrawal (the unanswered door, i0081; silence before her daughter's reproach, i0074); a sudden switch to fast recovery, a 400× plan and a 100-lot short (i0082–i0084); and, after the October entries, paid work to repay (V01 i0016–i0017, i0023). The beer cans of 24 October suggest drinking during the crisis (V01 i0010–i0013; INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH for her drinking). The journal's last entries record a wish to disappear, "消えたい。。。" (i0087), and a page scribbled black and torn (i0088–i0089). These are recorded as what she wrote and what the page shows; this portrait gives them no clinical name.

**Self-conception.** In the good years she sees herself as a capable home trader and a contributor (the journal's exclamation marks, "すごい…! FXすごい…!", i0068). In the bad weeks she sees herself as wronged by the market — "なんで私を苦しめるの!? 世界に監視されている としか思えない!!" (16 October, i0087) — and then as someone who has failed her family and cannot face them. In V01 she presents herself to her daughter as the responsible adult who will fix it alone (i0016) and, when pressed, as someone for whom confession is impossible ("無理よ", i0026). Whether she believed she could repay by work is not stated; her "大丈夫よ?" (i0023) is said to reassure.

## 6. Cognition and decision-making

**What she notices and how she reasons.** Her reasoning is accessible on the page mainly through V06's thoughts and journal (mixed authority; section 2). It runs on a few recurring moves.
- *Ease and passivity as safety.* "買うだけなら 難しくないし…" (i0064); the yen-carry method is presented by the narrator as appealing to a housewife precisely because it is passive income (i0063–i0064). She perceives risk as low because she does not have to act.
- *Rate and trend as belief.* She tracks the interest differential and the weak yen (the 5.5% → 7.25% graphic beside "金利は どんどん上がるし / 円安も続いて", i0068); the narrator calls her expectation a belief (i0067).
- *A range that her own record contradicts.* On 2 September 2008 she reasons that the pair has gone "back and forth between 100 and 90 for a year", so the fall is a bargain (i0070) — six weeks after writing that it had broken 103 (i0068). The bargain frame is her reading, not the narrator's fact (reliability flag, V06 checkpoint B-7).
- *Certainty under loss.* "豪ドルは ぜったい100円まで もどる…!" (i0075); "わかってたのに…!" (i0076) — regret for the dip she could not buy.
- *A regime flip after ruin.* With 1,005,144円 left she abandons the method she had believed in — "もう悠長に 金利狙ってる場合じゃないわ…" (i0082) — and the direction too: "100枚 売り", "もう円安の時代は 終わったのよ" (i0084).
- *Counterfactual and personal attribution.* "損切りしなければ勝っていた" (14 October); "買うと下がる。売ると上がる。 … 世界に監視されている としか思えない!!" (16 October, i0087).

**Her decisions, as Study C's episode record sets them out.** The financial specialist study (Study C, *Risk, loss and financial self-destruction across persons*, [studies/FX_KURUMI_RISK_LOSS_AND_FINANCIAL_SELF_DESTRUCTION.md](../studies/FX_KURUMI_RISK_LOSS_AND_FINANCIAL_SELF_DESTRUCTION.md)) treats her 2008 as one arc of decisions with the journal's authority marked. What it establishes about her, in this portrait's words:
- *One visible sequence, her own door into it.* Kozue's 2008 follows a sequence that Study C finds repeated in other traders of the series: a leveraged position held without a stop past a danger she had herself named, funded from money labelled as protected, and ended by the broker's forced liquidation (6 October 2008, i0080) rather than by her own order. What is hers is the entrance and the reason for enlarging the stake: a family ideal, a belief presented as belief, a bargain read from a range her own journal contradicts, and a drawn craving. Her daughter's door, by contrast, is a sum fixed by her mother's loss — the 2000万 that Kurumi resolves to win back (V01 i0015). The study's point for this portrait is that her ruin cannot be explained as "the same thing as her daughter's" — the end is shared, the motive is not.
- *Sizing against a reference point.* After the liquidation she plans a trade at 400× leverage — 「100万円×400倍 = 4億円」 beside her "4億円の 取引だって できるのよ…" (i0083) — to recover "4桁万円" in an instant (i0084), and her journal sets "必ず2000万円取り返さなきゃ・・・。" as the goal (13 October, i0087). The plan's figures are printed (OBSERVATION HIGH); that its size was set by the distance to what she had lost, not by the 1,005,144円 she had, is INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH from those lines. The order she then places is "100枚 売り" (i0084), whose unit size and leverage are not printed. The page prints the regime that allowed the plan — 「※2008年当時日本にはFXレバレッジ制限なし」 (i0083) — and she planned to use it.
- *Accurate insight, inert at the moment.* She writes down the danger ("強制ロスカットされたらどうしよう", i0072) and the rule ("損切りしないとダメらしい", i0087); neither changes the next act. In her, the gap between knowing and doing is visible in her own hand.
- *A funding chain that ends by exhaustion.* Pre-marriage savings → household money → cancelled insurance and the house down-payment → her parents' cash → the broker's close → the remaining million put behind a 400× plan and a 100-lot short → 498,302円 (i0071–i0086). Each pot was labelled for something else; none was kept back by her judgment that the position was wrong. The chain stops where the money stops.
- *Her rule toward cutting.* None at first; then hearsay, resented ("損切りしないとダメらしい"); then refusal ("もう損切りしたくない", i0087). Her journal implies that she did cut after the liquidation and experienced the cut as loss (INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH; the cuts themselves are not shown). Kurumi later voices nearly the same counterfactual ("やっぱ損切りいらなかったじゃん", V06 i0098), but no page shows her reading that entry or taking a rule from it; the echo is form, not a shown cause (V06 checkpoint C-02).

**Conflicting obligations.** The money she committed belonged, in the page's terms, to different people and purposes: her own savings, the household's, the family's future home, her parents. She resolved each conflict the same way, by taking and not telling. Toward her daughter the conflict is between protection and need: she protects Kurumi from labour and knowledge of the scale, and needs her silence. Toward her husband the conflict is between honesty and the family's survival as she perceives it ("一家離散"). The page never shows her weighing these aloud; it shows the outcome of each.

**Under uncertainty.** She meets uncertainty first with belief, then with hope ("祈るだけ"), then with acceleration ("短期間で 何度も 売買して", i0083) and finally with a sense of being watched and targeted by the market ("世界に監視されている としか思えない!!", i0087). She shows none of the responses that Study C finds elsewhere in the series under the same pressure: an exit set in advance, a cut taken early and kept, or a disclosure to the person owed — the way her daughter ends her own concealment in V09 (V09 i0072–i0073). Study C keeps open what her inner states were beyond what the journal records; this portrait does the same.

**Rivals to this account.** (a) *Timing, not process.* Kurumi's own V05 view — "お母さんだって やる時期が よかったら…" (V05 i0153) — makes her mother a victim of 2008. V06 supports timing (the 6 October day of "80円から71円まで", i0079) and also shows process — the range belief, the escalation, other people's money, the 400× plan — and gives far more space to process (V06 reading §L item 8). (b) *Institutional co-production.* A regime with no leverage limit made the 400× plan possible. That is Study C's stated rival, and the institutional question belongs to the specialist study *Screens, institutions and the allocation of responsibility* ([studies/FX_KURUMI_SCREENS_INSTITUTIONS_AND_RESPONSIBILITY.md](../studies/FX_KURUMI_SCREENS_INSTITUTIONS_AND_RESPONSIBILITY.md)); this portrait records only that she planned to use that leverage and why (the leverage of the short she then placed is not printed). (c) *Design.* The chapter re-stages a template the series repeats; the design reading explains the recurrence but not her particular words.

## 7. Behaviour: ordinary conduct, strain and crisis

**Ordinary conduct (the good years).** The page gives her more ordinary life than most adults in the series. She sees her daughter off at the door in the mornings and keeps doing so when the middle-schooler finds it embarrassing: "くるみちゃん いってらっしゃい", "車に気をつけてね", and, to "いちいち親と挨拶するのなんかダサい!", an amused "あらあら 反抗期♡ ふふっ" (V06 i0061, May 2006). Two years later the girl waves back "いってきます!" (i0068). She bakes a tiered cake (2007, i0066), makes a lunchbox luxurious on a good trading day and wonders whether her daughter will notice ("気付くかな??", i0068), does the laundry (a shirt marked 「PAPA」, i0067) and vacuums in a good mood (i0068–i0069). She meets her mum-friends at a café (i0067). In Kurumi's memories she praises — a drawing ("ネコ上手に描けてるね! さすが小学1年生!!", V02 i0111), a dress ("お姫様みたい"), help in the kitchen, a graduation (V01 i0037) — and she waits on a beach far longer than she threatens to (V05 i0160–i0161). Her trading, in these years, is fitted into the housework: the laptop sits on the low table among the household things (V06 i0066).

**Under strain (September – October 2008).** The household comes apart in the child's sight while the mother hides the cause. Kurumi washes the dishes "again" and asks what her mother did all day; the answer is a lie with a smile, "ごめんね 今日はちょっと体調悪くて…" (V06 i0073). The flat fills with laundry; when Kurumi warns that her father will complain, the mother says nothing and, a moment later, buys again (i0074). She does not answer the door to her daughter's "お母さん ごはんまだー?", and the child cooks for herself and brings her a bowl (i0081, i0085). Tied rubbish bags stand on the floor beside her as she reads her balance (i0082). The project records this as negligence toward the household under escalating concealment, rendered from the child's side and comically, without the narrator's blame and without cruelty (V06 reading §I; FXK-V06-E028).

**After exposure (late 2008 – February 2009).** Her conduct changes again once her daughter knows. She refuses the girl's offer to share the work and sends her back to her studies (V01 i0016), takes paid work and keeps long hours, leaving meals under covers (i0017; whether she held more than one job is Kurumi's guess), and visibly wears down — Kurumi lists dry hair, dark circles and sunken cheeks (i0022). Asked whether work is hard, she answers about secrecy: "大丈夫よ? お父さんには バレないように 働けてるし…" (i0023). The narrator: "このときから 母は少しずつ 変になっていった" (i0023).

**In confrontation.** On the day her husband is due home, her daughter urges her to confess and offers to apologize with her. She refuses, and the page turns her uncanny for one panel — dark, fuzzy-edged balloons, a haggard face: "無理よ", "くるみちゃん… あなた舐めてる でしょ?" (V01 i0026). When Kurumi shouts "誰のせいで こんなことに なってンだっけ!?" she yields at once: "…そうね ごめんなさい", and then, ordinarily, "私 買い物 行ってくるわ" (i0027). Kurumi goes instead and orders her to write out what she will say to her husband; she leaves a sheet with only "すみませんでした" (i0028–i0030). This is her last act on the page before her death, which is stated and not shown (i0031–i0033).

**Mistakes and corrections.** Her mistakes are on the page in her own hand: the over-buying she names on 5 September (i0072), the size she could not carry, the household money she resolved to put in and the passbook she drained (i0072, i0075), the lies. Her corrections are few and come late: the cuts her journal implies after the liquidation (INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH), which she then repudiates; and, after October, paid work in place of trading. Her journal's last dated entry is 23 October 2008 (i0089), but the last trading the page shows is the next night, 24 October, when Kurumi finds her pleading with a chart as it breaks a level — "お願いよ! ここ突破されたら 終わるの!", "最後に一回くらい 勝たせなさいよ!" (V01 i0010–i0011; that she held an open position is INFERENCE HIGH). After that night no page shows her trading, and none shows her deciding to stop. She never corrects the concealment.

**Meaningful exceptions.** Three acts cut against the general pattern and keep the portrait from collapsing into "the reckless mother". She refuses to let her child work to repay her (V01 i0016). She concedes to her daughter's accusation at once and without counter-attack (i0027), after a single moment of turning on her. And she offers, in the middle of that scene, to go and do the shopping (i0027) — the homemaker's ordinary duty, kept up to the last.

## 8. Speech and communication

**The medium's limits.** Everything below is written register: lettering in balloons, captions and a journal styled as handwriting. Her voice, pitch and accent are not available. V07's "voice" is an imagined recurrence of her V01 words in Kurumi's mind (section 9), not new speech.

**Register.** Her ordinary speech is feminine standard Japanese with soft sentence endings: "〜よ" ("無理よ", "大丈夫よ?"), "〜わ" ("この下落は チャンスだわ", V06 i0070; "買い物 行ってくるわ", V01 i0027), "〜のよね" ("なにか やっときたい のよねー", V06 i0062), "〜でしょ?" ("ふたり目の子供も つくれるでしょ?", i0065). To her child she uses the gentle imperative "〜なさい" ("今まで通り ちゃんと 勉強しときなさい", V01 i0016) and playful stretched vowels ("もう置いて 帰るわよ〜?", "パパに 言いつけるよ〜", V05 i0160). She laughs in print ("ふふっ", "ふふふ") and teases ("あらあら 反抗期♡"). No dialect is marked.

**Self-reference and address.** To her daughter she calls herself "お母さん" in the parental third person ("お母さん うれしいな", "お母さん算数 苦手だから 教えてね", V01 i0037) and "私" when she speaks as an adult taking a position ("これは私の 責任なんだから", V01 i0016; "私が稼いだの!", V06 i0065). She addresses her daughter as "くるみちゃん" throughout — in the remembered early years (V01 i0037), in 2006 (V06 i0061), in the journal's one direct address ("郁夫さん、くるみちゃん", V06 i0071) and at the moment of confrontation. Speaking of her to herself, or writing of her in the journal, she uses plain "くるみ": "くるみも もう中学生よ…?" (V06 i0062), "くるみのお弁当豪華にしたけど気付くかな??" (i0068). Once, and only then, she adds the distancing "あなた": "くるみちゃん… あなた舐めてる でしょ?" (V01 i0026), the name and the pronoun in one breath, affection and accusation together. She calls her husband "郁夫さん" (given name plus さん) in what she says aloud when alone (V06 i0062, i0064, i0082) and in the journal (i0071) and, in his remembered quarrel, "あなた" ("ちょっと あなた…!", V09 i0073). Her child calls her "ママ" when small (V02 i0111; V05 i0161; V06 i0058) and "お母さん" from middle school (V06 i0073, i0081; V01).

**How her register shifts with listener and pressure.**
- *With her child, at ease:* praise, teasing, small endearments; questions that invite the child to talk ("なにを 描いているの?", "将来は お絵描きさんかな?", V02 i0111).
- *With her child, covering:* the same soft forms carrying lies or deflection ("ごめんね 今日はちょっと体調悪くて…", V06 i0073; "大丈夫よ?", V01 i0023).
- *With her child, pressed:* the refusal and the uncanny "あなた" (V01 i0026), then a plain apology, "…そうね ごめんなさい" (i0027).
- *Alone, at the market:* the only rough language she is given — "最後に一回くらい 勝たせなさいよ!", "ふざけんな!! 誰よ上がるって 言った人!!", "2000万円 返せぇぇぇ!!" (V01 i0010–i0011), shouted behind a closed door at a chart and an unnamed forecaster. She never addresses a person on the page this way. Her "ふざけんな" is later heard from Kurumi (V01 i0100) and from the blogger あふぃちゃん (i0151) (V01 reading §K).
- *Alone, aloud:* in 2006 she muses and plans in ordinary speech balloons with no listener ("なにか やっときたい のよねー", V06 i0062; "もし儲かったら 郁夫さん びっくりするかな?", i0064), and in October 2008 she laments and fears the same way ("かき集めた 2000万円が たったの 100万円に…", "郁夫さんにバレたら 一家離散よ…", i0082).
- *Inwardly, staking:* short, incantatory lines, either in dark-edged balloons ("ここで買えば / みんな幸せになれる", V06 i0071) or lettered free round her face ("買いたい…!" ×3 with panting, i0075; "取り返さなきゃ" six times, i0082).

**The journal's voice.** Ruled pages with NO./DATE, each entry headed by a date with the weekday in brackets. Plain forms, no keigo, short declaratives with exclamation marks in the good weeks ("本日豪ドル円、103円突破!", "夢を叶えるには 行動しなきゃ!!") and, as the losses deepen, questions and trailing "。。。" and "・・・" ("あとはもう、祈るだけだ。。。", i0077; "消えたい。。。", i0087). It addresses her family directly in the second person once, as if writing to them: "郁夫さん、くるみちゃん 私、頑張るからね!" (i0071). The last pages turn from words to scribble and a torn sheet (i0088–i0089). Inner lines and journal differ in one telling way: the inward lines crave and urge; the journal records, reasons, blames and finally despairs.

**Apology, in three registers.** To her daughter, covering: "ごめんね" (V06 i0073). To her daughter, conceding: "ごめんなさい" (V01 i0027). On the sheet Kurumi had handed her to write what she would tell her husband: "すみませんでした" (V01 i0030), the most formal of the three and the only one that is written. That the formality marks her husband, or the family, as its addressee is INFERENCE MODERATE; the page gives no addressee, and the narrator says only that she left this and nothing more.

**Silence.** She is silent before her daughter's reproach in the heaped laundry (V06 i0074) and behind the closed door at dinner time (i0081). Her largest silence is the one her husband names: she did not consult him (V09 i0072). The page never gives her a scene of telling anyone.

## 9. Relationships

Each tie is directed and dated. Her own conduct toward a person (A → B) is kept apart from how that person holds her (B → A), and no state is carried forward or back.

**Kozue → Kurumi.** *1998:* a scolding for touching the new PC (V06 i0058). *Early childhood, undated (memory):* warm, teasing, praising, attentive to the first-grader's drawing and dream (V02 i0110–i0112); patient on the beach, with threats she does not carry out (V05 i0160–i0161). *2006–July 2008:* the morning ritual kept up through the child's "反抗期" (i0061, i0068); food as care — cake, the lunchbox she hopes will be noticed (i0066, i0068); her daughter in her dream of a happier family (i0069, i0075). *September–October 2008:* lies ("体調悪くて", i0073), silence (i0074), an unanswered door (i0081); the child carries the household. *From 24 October 2008:* the secret laid on her — a plea to keep it from Kurumi's father (V01 i0014) — and, at once, protection from its cost: no labour, study instead (i0016). *13 February 2009:* refusal of her daughter's offer to confess together, a moment of turning on her ("あなた舐めてる でしょ?"), immediate concession (i0026–i0027). Across all states, the page shows affection and dependence together, and no manipulation of the child for gain. Whether she understood what the secret asked of a fifteen-year-old is not shown (V01 §I; V01 checkpoint C-16). The project's grade for the 2008 household is negligence under escalating concealment, not cruelty (V06 §I).

**Kozue → her husband.** *Early years (his memory):* she objects to his transfers on the child's behalf and follows him in protest ("また転勤なの?", "ちょっと あなた…!", V09 i0072–i0073). *2006:* she resents, mildly, being told she need not work, calls him, to herself, "古い人" who would not know one can earn online, and wants to surprise him (V06 i0062–i0064); in a daydream she imagines his amazement and his delight at a second child, and laughs it off herself ("なんてねー", i0065). *2008:* he is the person who must not find out, for fear of "一家離散" (i0082); she conceals throughout. *2009:* she works, and tells Kurumi that she manages it without his finding out (V01 i0023); on the day he is due home she refuses to confess (i0026). No page shows the two of them discussing money except his remembered "お金のことは全部俺に任せて" (V09 i0072), and his own account is that she did not consult him (i0072).

**Kozue → her parents.** One scene: she visits cheerfully ("ちょっと来たよー"), and the inset shows her taking banknote bundles from their safe, sweating, behind their turned backs (V06 i0077). That this was without their knowledge is INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH. They are never shown again, and what they learned is never addressed (V06-U09).

**Kozue → her mum-friends and the world of the boom.** A source of pressure and comparison ("働いてるママ友に 趣味趣味言われるのも しんどいし", V06 i0062), and the setting in which the boom becomes talk (i0067). Behind the V01 cry "誰よ上がるって 言った人!!" (V01 i0011) is someone whose forecast she relied on; who it was — a friend, a book, a broadcast — is never stated (INFERENCE MODERATE that she relied on someone's forecast).

**How others hold her.** These are other characters' interpretations, kept apart from evidence about her.
- *Kurumi* holds her successively as the person she must rescue and keep the secret for (V01 i0014–i0015); the one she blames herself for and then blames ("私が殺した…?!", then "最初から私を頼ってれば…", V01 i0034–i0035); a beloved figure of childhood (V02; V05); the victim of FX, in her first disclosure to friends ("私のお母さん FXが原因で 自殺しちゃったから", V04 i0054 — Kurumi's own causal attribution); a victim of timing (V05 i0153); the owner of a debt to be repaid, "母が失った2000万円" (V06 i0095); a mirror she cannot escape ("これじゃ私も お母さんとおんなじ——", V07 i0147); someone she now understands ("お母さんが 家事しなくなった 気持ち わかっちゃった かも", V08 i0044); and finally someone she resembles and is not: "私とお母さん 似てるけど 違うんだ" (V09 i0061). In V09 she also re-reads her own project: she had used "お母さんの失敗" as a "大義名分" for greed of her own (V09 i0032). These are Kurumi's states and belong to [her monograph](fukuga-kurumi-manga.md); for this portrait they matter because almost everything the reader is first told about Kozue is filtered through them.
- *Her husband* calls her loss "母さんの失敗" in 2014 (V02 i0030) and, in 2015, sets her silence beside his own conduct: "母さんのときは 相談もなかったからな…", "…父さんが 相談しづらく してたんだろう / 情けない話だ" (V09 i0072–i0073). The project grades this an accepted share of responsibility, not a confession of cause (V09 §I).
- *Mochiko*, Kurumi's friend, doubts the story when she first hears it ("ドラマじゃあるまいし…", V04 i0080), imagines Kurumi and herself as two daughters "母親に呪われている" (V07 i0053, an unvoiced thought over ghost figures identified by likeness only), and in V09 suggests to Kurumi that her mother's influence was real ("母親の影響も あったんじゃないかな", V09 i0033). No page shows Mochiko, who knows Kurumi from university, meeting her.

**Study B's consequences for her.** The relationship specialist study (Study B, *Obligations, relationships and where the cost of a choice lands*, [studies/FX_KURUMI_OBLIGATIONS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COST.md](../studies/FX_KURUMI_OBLIGATIONS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COST.md)) follows the chain of concealment and money in the Fukuga family. Its findings bear on her in four ways.
- *Where her cost landed.* On the page, the whole of her ≈1900万円 loss came out of persons' money — her own, the household's, the family's future house, her parents' — and its last cost fell on her daughter: the secret, the neglect and her death. None of it landed on an institution. Her account is printed at 1,005,144円 and then 498,302円, with no shortfall shown (V06 i0082, i0086). The study sets this against Kurumi's 2015 loss, which left a negative balance owed to a broker; why the two differ is a question for the institutional study, not this portrait.
- *Concealment as the active element.* Her case is the study's instance (claim B3) that accepting responsibility in words does not protect anyone if the act is hidden: she said "これは私の責任" and kept the secret, and the cost travelled to the people who did not know. In the study's terms her concealment is the first link in a chain: Kurumi, who kept her secret, goes on to conceal from the same man — the journal (V01 i0039), his savings (V01), her trading, debt and plan (V07–V08) — and breaks the chain in V09 by a chosen disclosure.
- *The secret laid on a child.* The study grades it a burden without manipulation for gain; her grasp of it is not shown.
- *A live rival about cause.* The family's money roles — the husband's "お金のことは全部俺に任せて / お前は家のことだけ" (V09 i0072) — may be the structural cause of her silence, of which the concealment is the symptom. The husband himself proposes a version of this; the page does not decide it. The roles were of authority and expectation, not of access: the household passbook she emptied was in her name (V06 i0075; section 3).

**How her ties develop.** Within her life, only her tie to her daughter is shown changing: from easy warmth (to mid-2008), to concealment and neglect (September–October 2008), to a shared secret with protection and refusal (late 2008), to the last confrontation. Her tie to her husband is shown only in fixed states (his memory; her daydream; her fear). After her death nothing of hers changes; what changes is how Kurumi and her husband hold her (section 13).

## 10. Preferences and ordinary life

What the page supports, with the distinction between stable preference and situational choice:
- **Cooking and feeding others** (stable across layers): cake (V06 i0066), luxurious lunchboxes (i0068), meals left covered even at her lowest (V01 i0017). In her first daydream of profit she pictures a lavish lunch — "ランチ代 くらいには なるのかな?", "おいしそ〜♥" (V06 i0064).
- **Home and family rituals** (stable): seeing her daughter off at the door (i0061, i0068); praise and small celebrations in Kurumi's memories (V01 i0037; V02 i0111).
- **A wish for a house with a garden, a second child and a Sheltie** (stated dream, 2008; i0069, i0075). The dog is a wish, not a pet the page shows.
- **Social life among mum-friends** (situational evidence; café, i0067), with some fatigue at their questions (i0062).
- **Australian dollar / yen** as her currency (V01 i0062 in Kurumi's account; V06 throughout). This is a trading choice made for its interest rate ("選んだ通貨は豪ドルで大正解!", i0068), not a taste.
- **Aversions.** Being idle at home while her daughter grows up (i0062); being thought hobby-less by working friends (i0062); and, at the end, the stop-loss cut ("もう損切りしたくない", i0087).
- **Leisure and habits otherwise** are not shown: no hobby, reading habit (beyond the one bestseller, i0062), music, food dislike or comfort routine is evidenced. Whether she drank before October 2008 is unknown.

## 11. Romantic and intimate psychology

The page tests this dimension only lightly. What it shows: she wants her husband's admiration and imagines his delight at her earnings; she wants a second child and ties it to money ("これなら ふたり目の子供も つくれるでしょ?", V06 i0065); the daydream ends with a comic flourish from the imagined husband that she herself waves away, blushing — "なんてねー" (i0065). In his memory she quarrels with him about his transfers and their small child (V09 i0072–i0073). Beyond her stated wishes about the marriage, the dimension is **UNTESTED**.

## 12. Capabilities and limitations

**Domestic competence.** She ran a household well for years — cooking, cakes, laundry, cleaning, morning routines (V06 i0061–i0069) — and kept doing some of it at her lowest (V01 i0017, i0027). Its collapse in September–October 2008 is shown as a consequence of her trading crisis (V06 i0073–i0085), not as an incapacity.

**Paid work.** After October 2008 she took paid work and long hours (V01 i0016–i0017, i0023), in office clothes. What the work was is not stated.

**Trading knowledge.** She learned enough to run a yen-carry position profitably for about two years (narrator, V06 i0066; amounts unprinted), to track the interest differential (i0068), to understand forced liquidation and margin well enough to fear it and to resolve to add money to avoid it (i0072), to know of the stop-loss rule (i0087) and to know that the 2008 regime let her trade at up to 400× ("FXは やろうと思えば レバ400倍…", with the printed 「100万円×400倍 = 4億円」, i0083). Her limits are equally on the page: a range belief contradicted by her own record (i0068, i0070), a position too large for what she had ("買い過ぎてしまった", i0072), no exit rule, and a counterfactual reading of a cut as the cause of a loss (i0087). Her lot sizes (before the liquidation and in the October short), the amounts of her 2006–2008 profits and the sum of her pre-marriage savings are never printed (reconciliation audit §4.11).

**Arithmetic.** Kurumi's outburst after the funeral calls her too stupid for middle-school maths (V01 i0036), and a remembered line has her ask the child to teach her ("お母さん算数 苦手だから 教えてね", i0037). The accepted V01 reading takes that memory at face value: within Kurumi's memory it confirms the mother as the less numerate parent (FXK-V01-E006; V01 reading §H.4). The same memory panel draws her smiling, chin on hand, with "ふふふ", while the child eagerly explains; it can also be read as a mother's game of letting a child teach her (HYPOTHESIS, LOW-MODERATE). The play reading is this portrait's hypothesis and decides nothing. The page gives no independent test of her arithmetic, and the claim that she was weak at it rests on a grieving child's anger and on a memory that may be play; this portrait neither adopts nor rejects it.

**Capacity against willingness.** She could have confessed — her daughter offered to stand with her (V01 i0025–i0026) — and would not ("無理よ"). She could cut — her journal implies that she did after the liquidation (INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH) — but came not to want to ("もう損切りしたくない", i0087). She could stop the household's slide, and in the weeks of the crisis did not; after October she worked hard to repay. In each case the page shows that the obstacle was not ability.

## 13. Development and late revelation

**What changed in her (all within 2005 – February 2009).**
1. *From sceptic to trader (2005 → May 2006).* The ridicule of i0060 gives way to "FX… 始めてみよう!" (i0065). The change is small and practical: she does not come to believe the poster; she decides that buying is easy and that the downside is small.
2. *From entrant to believer (2006 → July 2008).* Two years of carry income turn caution into confidence — "選んだ通貨は豪ドルで大正解!", "すごい…! FXすごい…!" (i0068), "そろそろ 初心者卒業かも!" (i0070) — and the goal grows from lunch money to a new family life (i0069).
3. *From open to hidden, and from her money to everyone's (September 2008).* In four weeks she moves from her own savings to the household's, the family's future and her parents', and from a mother who sees her daughter off to one who lies to her and does not answer the door (i0071–i0081).
4. *From patience to haste, and from belief to a sense of being watched by the market (October 2008).* After the liquidation she drops the method she trusted for a 400× plan and a 100-lot short (i0082–i0084); the journal moves from "まずは第一歩" to "世界に監視されている としか思えない!!" and "消えたい。。。" (i0087).
5. *From secrecy against everyone to a secret shared with one child (24 October 2008 →).* Exposed by her daughter, she accepts responsibility in words, refuses the child's help, gives up trading as far as the page shows (no trading is shown after that night), and works (V01 i0014–i0023).
6. *The last day (13 February 2009).* She refuses to confess, turns on her daughter for one moment, concedes and apologizes, and leaves a one-line written apology (V01 i0026–i0030).

These are real changes of conduct, priorities and self-understanding inside a person's life, and they are dated. Their causes are given on the page only as her own and the narrator's accounts (sections 5–6).

**What was only revealed later.** V06 supplies her motives, her journal, the composition of the 2000万, the 400× plan and her last entries; V09 supplies the marriage's money roles and the fact that she did not consult her husband; V02 and V05 supply memories of her warmth in Kurumi's early childhood. None of this changes her: it changes what the reader knows. Its effect is to re-read V01. In V01 the reader meets her through a child's eyes — a frightening woman shouting at a chart, a mother who takes the blame and then cannot face confession, and, after her funeral, someone her daughter calls stupid. After V06 and V09 she is a person with ordinary reasons, a constraining marriage and her own record of what happened. The project keeps these as disclosure, not development (qualification audit §1.5).

## 15. Interpretive uncertainty

**Rival readings of the person.**
- *Family ideal or appetite.* Her words give a family ideal; the art draws a craving and, in the next panel, the dream over the same ringed eyes (V06 i0075). The portrait keeps both because the page does: no page marks the ideal as a pretext, and no page denies the craving. A later page showing the family motive as a cover would overturn the first half; none exists within V10.
- *Victim of 2008 or author of her ruin.* Kurumi's V05 "やる時期が よかったら…" (V05 i0153) against V06's long account of process. The page gives more space to process, but timing is also printed (V06 i0079). Both are kept (V06 reading §L item 8).
- *Responsible adult or concealer.* "これは私の 責任なんだから" (V01 i0016) and the secret (i0014) are both hers. The portrait reads them as simultaneous, not as one hiding the other; a reading that the words were only a way to keep her daughter quiet has no support beyond their effect.
- *The uncanny turn of 13 February.* The one panel in which she turns on her daughter (V01 i0026) is drawn in a chapter narrated by Kurumi looking back after the death. Whether the dark balloons and haggard face render the mother's intent, her despair, or the child's remembered horror cannot be decided (INFERENCE: all three are compatible with the page). The words themselves are hers; their recurrence in V07 shows how they stayed with her daughter.
- *A person or a function.* She is the origin of the protagonist's project, and the series uses her as a mirror (re-enactment, the 1,005,144, the inset, the voice). The design reading is strong for the afterlife (EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH) and weak for her own pages, where V06 gives her motives, a household, a marriage and a voice of her own. The qualification record treats her as evidence in her own right; so does this portrait.
- *Her husband's account.* "相談もなかった" and "父さんが 相談しづらく してたんだろう" (V09 i0072–i0073) are his self-report. They may be accurate, self-blaming or self-protective; Study B keeps live both that his money roles caused her silence and that his V09 response is situational.
