## Orientation

**Who she is.** 福賀くるみ (Fukuga Kurumi) is the protagonist of the Japanese manga **FX戦士くるみちゃん** (story でむにゃん, art 炭酸だいすき) and is on nearly every main page of its ten collected volumes. At fifteen (October 2008) she keeps her mother's secret trading losses of household money; her mother dies by suicide in February 2009; Kurumi resolves to win back the lost "2000万円" by foreign-exchange (FX) trading, waits five years until she is twenty, and begins in February 2014 with 300,000 yen of her own savings. Across 2014 she reaches a realized peak of 21,213,915 yen (one night in V01 survived only by her father's cash, taken without his consent and returned to him in V02), loses everything on a single unprotected position in January 2015, owes the broker 101,534,644 yen, passes through a suicidal crisis, is brought back by her friend 小金萌智子 (Kogane Mochiko) and her father, goes bankrupt, and in April 2017 works as a clerk in Mochiko's company, not trading, still paying her 2014 tax.

**Boundary.** This portrait covers the accepted V01–V10 main narrative (witnesses FXK-JP-V01-W01 to FXK-JP-V10-W01), ending at V10 i0156 (Monday 24 April 2017). It contains every spoiler up to that page and nothing after it. Paratext (afterwords, bonus strips and essays, flaps, jackets, under-jacket boards, blurbs) is never used as evidence of who she is. Nothing outside the admitted pages is used: no real market history, no adaptation, no later volume.

## 1. Identity and circumstances

Kurumi is an only child in the household the pages show. At fifteen she is in her third year of junior high (V01 i0008); in April 2012 she enters 新都大学 経済学部 (the 平成24年度 orientation, V05 i0042–i0044), on a half-tuition scholarship (特待生, V01 i0041) at a university chosen so she could commute and spare her father the cost. She is a second-year in February 2014 and a third-year from April 2014 (FXK-V05-C064), so in V04 she shops for a job-hunting suit with friends of the same year (V04 i0035, i0065). She is twenty on 14 February 2014, "20 or 21" between April and late October 2014, twenty-one from 25 October 2014, and twenty-three in April 2017 (V09 i0152; age rule from the reconciliation audit).

She lives with her father from April 2009 (V01 i0038) in a flat in a small two-storey block with an outside staircase, unit 「201」 with the nameplate 「福賀」 (V04 i0065; V06 i0045; V08 i0115). V01 once draws the home as a detached house; the accepted record treats that as an art inconsistency, not a second home. Her door still carries the sign she made in first grade, 「くるみのへや」 with a cat doodle (V01 i0014; V02 i0114; V09 i0123), and her parents' door the child's sign 「ぱぱ・ままのへや♡」 (V08 i0043). From New Year 2015 her father is posted away and she lives alone (V07 i0031). Where she lives in 2017 is not established: she sleeps at the office by choice (V10 i0007), and when Kizuna, an older colleague, assumes she lives with family she does not correct it (V10 i0045); her residence remains an open obligation (V10-U09).

Her work is ordinary and continuous. Through 2014 she has a part-time job at a convenience store (V01 i0047; V02 i0012–i0013, net pay 52,487 yen after extra shifts; V03 i0154); she quits in V05 citing job-hunting (V05 i0083), returns to the same store after the crisis (V09 i0107), and in April 2017 is a clerk in her first year ("入社1年目"; the joining date is not printed), "事務と雑用", at (株)もちもち企画, the company of which Mochiko is president (V10 i0016; V09 i0143, i0151–i0157). She wrote a graduation thesis titled "爆益を狙った場合における投資行動の一考察" (V09 i0103); her graduation itself is not shown.

Her money is the spine of the story, and its main points are printed (reconciliation audit §4.2, Chain A):

## 2. Personal history, and the clock of disclosure

### 2.1 The founding events (2008–2009, told first)

The founding events are given at the start, in V01's opening chapter, largely through Kurumi's own retrospective first-person captions (FXK-V01-D001–D006). In 2008, while her father was posted away, her mother lost about 2000万 of household money in FX and asked fifteen-year-old Kurumi to keep it from him (V01 i0014). The daughter carried the secret, watched her mother's decline (i0022), offered to share the apology ("私も一緒に 謝って", i0026) and took over the shopping (i0027). In a confrontation she lashed out at her mother, "誰のせいで" (i0027). Her mother left a sheet reading 「すみませんでした」 (i0030) and died by suicide on 13 February 2009 (i0030–i0033). The captions record what followed as an oscillation: self-accusation ("私が殺した…?!") and blame of her mother's incompetence, ending in a posthumous "ばぁか" (i0034–i0036) (SELF_REPORT HIGH, retrospective). One reproach in that outburst returns to her years later: "最初から 私を頼ってれば 死なずに すんだんだよ!" (i0035; FXK-V01-D006). She blamed her mother for not relying on her; in V08, hiding her own ruin from everyone, she hears the same words again at her old school (V08 i0086, where the page letters the verb 済んだ; each quotation in this portrait follows its own page). That the page sets her own conduct against her own reproach is OBSERVATION; that the reproach names the pattern she repeats — not asking for help — is INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH.

At fifteen she adopted the lost sum as her own debt (i0015) and resolved on FX. She did not begin at once. She studied for five years until the legal age (i0020) and, on 14 February 2014, framed what she was about to do in two sentences that the rest of the series keeps testing: "多分これは 自己満足の 戦いだ" and "でも私が 頑張れば お母さんが 死ぬことは なかった / その証明が したい" (V01 i0040, re-verified). The proof she wanted was not only of skill. It was a counterfactual about a death: that competent trading would have saved her mother (INFERENCE HIGH from the caption's wording).

### 2.2 History filled in later, and what each layer changes

Later volumes add history by flashback, each at a moment when it bears on a present choice. These are late revelations: they re-read earlier pages and do not change Kurumi.

- **High school and childhood (V02).** A high school spent only studying ("遊ぶことを知らずに生きてしまった", V02 i0093) and, in childhood, a drawing habit and the dream of becoming a kindergarten teacher (i0111–i0112). Both are shown at the moment of her relapse into trading, as the life she is setting aside.
- **The 2012 friendship (V05).** In April 2012, at the start of university, Kurumi offered Mochiko friendship first ("これから一緒に頑張ろうね!", V05 i0049, re-verified), already set on FX and the 2000万 at eighteen (i0047). The same page shows the eighteen-year-old balking at the stop rule Mochiko describes: "えっ! そのあと上がるかもしれないよ?" (i0049). The reluctance to cut is older than her first trade. Mochiko's inner record of the same meeting, that Kurumi would make "いいサンプル" whose failure she wanted to watch (i0048), is something Kurumi never learns.
- **Her mother's process (V06).** 第28話 shows Kozue's 2008 trading through the 「FXトレード日誌」 that Kurumi found in V01 (FXK-SYN-C001) and through scenes beyond the journal. Its authority is mixed: part journal self-report, part narrator, framed as Kurumi's reading (V06 reading §L.7). It adds Kurumi's childhood: poking the family PC at about five (1998, i0058), washing dishes her mother left (i0073), cooking a "プリン雑炊" ("まっず!") and then a plain one for her (i0085). What Kurumi takes from reading it is an emotional charge, not a method: tears, "次は絶対勝つ" (i0090), and "母が失った2000万円を… 奪い返す" (i0095). No page shows her adopting a rule from the journal (V06 checkpoint C-02).
- **Her father's side (V09).** The 3,000,000 she took in V01 had been "もともとくるみのために貯めてた" (V09 i0074); he says his wife never consulted him and that he made consultation hard (i0072–i0073). This re-reads V01's "福賀家再興の証し" (V01 i0109) without changing what Kurumi knew then.
- **Her own account of herself (V09).** After the crisis she re-reads her whole history as appetite: "根っからの欲張り" since childhood (V09 i0031), the mother's failure only a "大義名分" (i0032, re-verified). The page stages this by re-presenting a childhood beach memory, first shown in V05 without any such figure (V05 i0161), now with a small winged girl beside the shell-gathering child (V09 i0031); that the two are the same memory is INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH. This is self-understanding offered late, a SELF_REPORT about her past, not a change in her disposition (reconciliation audit §5.5 row 2; qualification audit §1.5).

Through V10 she never learns Mochiko's 2012 "sample" thought, Mochiko's V07 inner account of why the friendship began (section 7.3), Mochiko's V03 passing of her V01 win to Mebuki as borrowed-money gambling (V03 i0104–i0105), the V04 label 狂人, Mochiko's withheld V06–V07 warnings, or Mebuki's 2,000,000 loan from Mochiko (V05-U20 OPEN). ## 3. Personality and psychology

### 3.1 Values

Four values recur in her words and acts across the series, and each costs her something.

- **The family kept whole.** At fifteen she kept her mother's secret because "家族がバラバラになるのは嫌だった" (V01 i0014). The same value, turned inward, drives every later concealment from her father.
- **Not being a burden (迷惑).** It shapes her refusals and her silences alike: she will not borrow from Mochiko because "全部溶かしたらどうすんの…? 困るのは萌智子でしょ?" (V01 i0066); after the ruin the rule becomes "誰にも迷惑かけちゃダメだ" (V07 i0149) and an inner chorus orders 「親ニ迷惑カケルナ」 (V07 i0128); her letter apologizes 「迷惑をかけてごめんなさい。」 (V08 i0070). The consequence for her, argued across the family by the specialist study [Obligations, relationships and where the cost of a choice lands](../studies/FX_KURUMI_OBLIGATIONS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COST.md) ("Study B" below) and registered as FXK-SYN-C021 (INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH): concealment carries the first cost of a choice to people who do not know of it, and defers and enlarges it. By hiding what would let her father help, so as not to burden him, she enlarges the burden she fears.
- **Being an adult (大人).** "だってもう「大人」だもん!" she tells her father while confessing (V02 i0029, re-verified); "子供じゃないんだよ〜?" (V05 i0069); "大人だよ" while still refusing to tell him (V09 i0042). Adulthood, for her, means carrying cost alone. The series answers through Mochiko: "大人って言われたいならちゃんとお父さんに話しなよ" (V09 i0043).
- **Honesty among friends, unevenly applied.** She calls out Mochiko's lie cheerfully, "嘘はよくないよ!" (V05 i0144), and writes a no-lies rule for the group (V06 i0143). She herself lies once about her position from shame (V01 i0116) and hides her whole ruin for weeks (V07–V08).

### 3.2 Goals and motives: a moving target, two accounts of why

Her stated goal moves at almost every volume, and the movement is more telling than any single statement:

1. Restitution and proof: "奪われた 2000万円は 私が 取り返す" (V01 i0055); the proof of the counterfactual (V01 i0040).
2. Self-making: "誰のためでもない / 自分の人生を 始めるため / 2000万円を 稼ぐため", under "自己責任の 名のもとに" (V02 i0131), as she returns to trading after promising to stop.
3. A gross figure: "税金考えると2500万円が本当の目標になる…" (V03 i0054).
4. The target questioned at the moment it is nearly reached: "お母さんの負け額だけ稼いで満足? お母さんは死んでるのに?", "2000万円ぽっちで済ませていいの…?" (V05 i0154).
5. Restitution renewed by her mother's journal: "母が失った2000万円を… 奪い返す" (V06 i0095).
6. After ruin, a new figure of the same kind: "今から1億円を稼げばいいんだ" (V07 i0143).
7. After the crisis, an open, conditional future: "次に投資を やりたくなった そのときは / 堂々と やりたいんだ" (V09 i0079, re-verified) and "いつかは 戻ってきたいと 思ってるんだ… / だって私 / 相場が / 大好きだから" (V09 i0119–i0121; FXK-V09-C030).
8. In 2017, a revaluation and a question: "失ってから 気づいた" about the ten million she once held (V10 i0048), and "もう一度 資産を作る ことは できる だろうか…?" (V10 i0049–i0050).

Why she trades has two accounts on the page, and this portrait keeps them apart. **Restitution** is her account through V08: a debt adopted from her dead mother. **Appetite** is present from V02 as a personified figure that speaks in her first person ("私はFXをやめられない…!", V02 i0100, the tempter's line by the drawn mouths, INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH; "脳汁", also the tempter's, i0109) and becomes her own account in V09: "根っからの欲張り" (V09 i0031) and the mother's failure as a "大義名分" (i0032), a judgment she reaches by adopting the epithet Mochiko had thrown at her ("強欲の雌豚… 本当その通りの人間だったんだなって", V09 i0032, re-verified). Mochiko immediately qualifies it: "母親の影響も あったんじゃないかな" (i0032). The V09 account is a SELF_REPORT made after the crisis, not a settled cause. What can be said firmly is narrower: from V04 to V08 the restitution figure and its successors, whatever their motivational weight, worked as the reference point that set the size of her bets (FXK-SYN-C026, EVALUATION HIGH for her; scope and limits in section 4.3).

### 3.3 Fears and vulnerabilities

- **Contempt from those she loves.** She refuses her father's call because "軽蔑…されたくない…" (V08 i0036–i0037), greets Mochiko with "笑いに来たなら帰ってほしい" (V08 i0118), and at her lowest asks "軽蔑するよね!?" (V08 i0139, re-verified).
- **Being found a child.** See 大人 above; she covers the fear with cheerful performance to her father ("子供じゃないんだよ〜?" and "私のことは気にせず行ってきて♪", V05 i0069).
- **A grey life.** At the moment of her largest win she foresees "灰色の人生" without trading (V05 i0155), having spent her adolescence "中学からずっと相場相場で" (i0155).
- **Her mother's road.** She sees the parallel herself, twice in the same words: "これじゃ私も… お母さんとおんなじ——" at the V01 nadir (i0140) and again before staking her last 1,000,000 (V07 i0147).

Her structural vulnerabilities are not fears she names: the guilt of a counterfactual about her mother's death (V01 i0040); a first success that, by the narrator's account, became "一生消えることのない呪い" (V02 i0132–i0134; FXK-V02-C001); protective labels on money that are her own and can be removed by her (section 4.4); and an isolation of knowledge, since she holds the fewest facts about the people around her (section 2.3).

### 3.4 Contradictions

The pages build her out of visible contradictions, and the portrait should not resolve them prematurely.

- She presents as a strategist and rule-keeper ("準備は整った", V01 i0040; "容赦なく損切りする", i0112; "絶対条件", i0124) and breaks or reinterprets each rule under pressure (i0088, i0125, i0135; V06 i0009–i0013, i0098–i0099).
- She says "勝ち逃げ" (quit while ahead) as a plan (V01 i0044–i0045; V04 i0036) and never quits while ahead.
- She refuses to borrow from a friend (V01 i0064–i0066) and to lend a friend money for FX (V04 i0071), yet used her father's savings without consent (V01 i0109–i0111). The boundary she keeps is drawn round friends, not round protected family money; for the friend she names the lender's risk (V01 i0066), and the page never says why the line falls where it does.
- She tells Mebuki that investment is "自己責任" (V02 i0066) and later refuses Mebuki money because it would not be "for her sake" (V04 i0074) — care and disclaimer from the same mouth.
- She says she does not want money (V02 i0046) and calls the 2000万 what she has lived for (V02 i0124).
- Outward cheer and inward panic run together: "大丈夫だよ!" with a cutesy 「にゃんっ♪」 at the Christmas table while she holds 300 unprotected lots (V06 i0148, against her panting at i0118); "全然平気!" typed into the friends' group chat after the shock that left her unable to breathe (V07 i0120, i0136).

### 3.5 Coping

- **Avoidance of what she cannot face** (OBSERVATION HIGH): not watching at the V01 nadir (i0141–i0143; the narrator calls it "ある意味では勇気"); the trading window closed, her mother's photograph turned face down, the broker's mail deleted (V07 i0106, i0113, i0127); her phone switched off and her father's call refused (V08 i0036, i0056).
- **Fantasy staging**: storms, bridges and knights for a decision (V06 i0012, i0121–i0137), pet names for imagined figures ("ニンニクさん", i0137); fairy-tale self-narration at the worst moment ("私はスイスに殺されちゃいましたとさ", V08 i0053).
- **War framing** throughout: "軍資金", "戦場", "発射", "撤退" (V01), "戦う" for trading (V02 i0017, i0131), orders announced as launches ("300枚発射…!", V05 i0158; "発射ッ!!!", V08).
- **Talking to her mother**: to the photograph after her largest win (V05 i0159), on leaving the flat ("じゃあお母さん 行ってきます", V07 i0073), and at the grave (V09 i0055–i0061).
- **Small consolations and plans of escape**: a book binge topped by a guide to becoming a fortune-teller (V02 i0094–i0096); unread self-discovery hobby books, among them a VTuber primer (V07 i0049).
- **Self-commands**: "がんばれ私 逃げるな私", "よし…やろう…" (V07), and brisk commands over grave acts in V08.

### 3.6 Self-conception

Her view of her own competence is unstable and context-driven (FXK-V02-C003, INFERENCE HIGH). She believes study and mathematics decide FX (V01 i0019, i0036) until the nadir, when she reverses, "私にFXの才能なんてなかった" (V01 i0139). To her father and when accused she credits luck (V02 i0033, i0041; V04 i0073 "…それは 運がよかっただけ…で…"); after two wins she claims "私の実力" (V08 i0008–i0012); in 2015 she counts herself among the "下手くそ" next to Mochiko, "投資がうまい" (V09 i0022); in 2017 her V01 success was "たまたま ハイレバFXで" (V10 i0049; FXK-V10-C064). The one stable element of her self-conception after V09 is not about skill but about appetite and acceptance: "いろいろ 間違ってると 思うけど / これが私" (V09 i0061, re-verified).

## 4. Cognition and decision-making

### 4.1 What she notices

She notices danger accurately, and early. In V01 she names her own error mid-trade, "完全に 欲張った" (i0084). In V02 she predicts herself: "このまま続けたら 負ける人間" (i0101). In V06 she names the cluster of stop orders under EUR/CHF 1.20 that could break the floor (i0128–i0132), and puts the scheduled report on her calendar (i0011). In V07 she names the ECB risk and its date (i0072). She reads macro context aloud (geopolitical risk receding, the post-QE3 dollar adjustment, V03 i0121–i0122) and she computes exactly: "あと566万…とんで85円" is right to the yen (V06 i0096; ANALYST CALCULATION 25,000,000 − 18,339,915 − 1,000,000 = 5,660,085), and her V08 reckoning of tax and bankruptcy costs is lucid (V08 i0030–i0034).

### 4.2 How she reasons

When she is flat or recently chastened she reasons in explicit scenarios, drawn as diagrams to herself (Patterns A–C, V05 i0078–i0079; Patterns A–D, V06 i0008–i0011), checks procedures before her first order ("新規発注及び 決済注文方法 確認よし", V01 i0045), and hand-makes rule sheets with hearts and doodles (V03 i0086; V06 i0143). She can refuse a trade on grounds: "根拠がなさすぎる…" (V04 i0115) and EUR/USD "for lack of a guarantee" (V06 i0106).

The same mind works differently by condition. This is among the best-supported regularities in her record, and it is conditional, not a trait label:

- **After a gain**, she escalates in size and target and minimizes in words: "次はもっと欲張ろう" (V03 i0053), "軽く30枚くらいだよ?" (V03 i0055), "レバ20倍は低レバかなぁ…" (V03 i0012), an all-in add of 120 lots at a new high (V04 i0006), "冷静になる必要なんて なかったじゃん" about her earlier restraint (V05 i0070), 300 → 600 → 1000 lots after two wins in one night (V08 i0008–i0015).
- **Under a moderate open loss**, she rationalizes and refuses or delays the stop: "そんな気がする", "騙し下げ" (V01 i0089–i0094), "やっぱ損切りは なしッ!!" (V01 i0088), "やっぱり 損切りはナシ…!" (V06 i0009).
- **At catastrophe**, she first avoids and her body collapses (V07 i0106–i0121), then she denies ("あれは「バグ」だ", V07 i0125), then her reasoning becomes exact and turns against herself (V08 i0029–i0047).
- **At the nadir of V01**, her self-judgment becomes global ("私にFXの才能なんてなかった", i0139).

### 4.3 The rule that sets her size

The specialist study [Risk, loss and financial self-destruction across persons](../studies/FX_KURUMI_RISK_LOSS_AND_FINANCIAL_SELF_DESTRUCTION.md) ("Study C" below) argues, across the traders of the series, that what turns a loss into the end of an account is decided before the broker acts, by size and funding set against a reference point, not only by the failure to cut (registered as FXK-SYN-C026: EVALUATION HIGH for Kurumi, MODERATE-HIGH across persons). For Kurumi, from V04 to V08, this is the most consistent decision rule in her record, and it explains those decisions better than any single trait; as the motive of the size it is graded EVALUATION HIGH for V05 and V07–V08 and MODERATE-HIGH for the V06 franc position (FXK-SYN-C010). The rule has a beginning. In V01 her enlargement to 300 lots was sized to wipe out an open loss by month-end ("この含み損を 月末までに 消して", V01 i0122), not to the restitution figure. By V04 the target stands beside the size: she adds 120 lots at a new high with "税引き後 2000万円" as her stated aim (V04 i0006).

- In V05, minutes after reaching her realized peak, she computes "税金分考えて あと300万円だけ" (V05 i0153; the count, 2,786,085 by ANALYST CALCULATION, fits the rule — 2500万 gross less the balance and the 1,000,000 withdrawn in V03 — that her V06 count matches to the yen, V06 i0096; FXK-SYN-C002) and opens 300 lots short with a take-profit sized so that "たった1円の 値動きで 約300万円の利益" closes the gap (i0158).
- In V06 she drops the stop rule not because cutting hurt but because "私はあと数回勝てばゴールなんだから" and "これから何年もFXで戦い続けるわけじゃないし" (V06 i0099): the goal is near and the horizon short.
- In V07, with the debt in front of her, the reference jumps to 1億 and the instrument changes with it: "海外口座ならレバレッジ無制限", seven doublings of 1,000,000 (V07 i0143–i0148; 100万 × 2⁷ = 1億2800万, FXK-V07-C025).
- On the V08 night the rule and the stakes are stated before the first click: "読みが外れても 損切りとか 考えない", "この100万円が… ゼロになるか 1億円になるか" (V08 i0006).

The rule has counterexamples that limit it to the decisions that enlarged risk: she declines a second add with the gap still open (V04 i0113–i0115), withdraws 1,000,000 while raising her target (V03 i0054, i0124), and brackets her V05 long with a protective stop (V05 i0077). In V10 the reference itself is re-valued: "1000万円あれば レバレッジを かけなくても" (V10 i0049).

### 4.4 Insight that does not govern, and what restrains her

Her insight is usually accurate and usually does not govern the decision it concerns (Study C's cross-person claim, registered as FXK-SYN-C027: EVALUATION HIGH as a pattern; mechanism UNRESOLVED). The clearest case is V07: "これじゃ私も お母さんと おんなじ——", then, on the same page, "あはは…" and "私…まったく成長してないなー…", and, lettered beside her prone figure, "でも 仕方ないじゃん" (V07 i0147). Promises of a future act fail in her hands: she breaks the promise to cut at the break of 94 (V01 i0112 → i0135) and the V02 retirement (V02 i0040 → i0102). Two conditional resolutions never came due, because she moved the goal or sized a trade to reach it first: the V01 plan to quit at 2000万 (i0044–i0045), re-set to 2500万 gross before she reached it (V03 i0054), and "目標達成したら ちゃんと FX引退しなきゃ!" (V05 i0155), followed within minutes by a 300-lot short sized to reach the goal (i0158; FXK-SYN-C027).

What has restrained her came from outside the trading moment:

- **An act that changes the structure of the money at once**: the 1,000,000 withdrawn in V03 "無茶して 失敗しないように" (i0124). It held for four months, until she relabelled it "無傷の100万円" (V07 i0144) and, by INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH, deposited it (V08 i0003).
- **Another person, present or remembered**: her refusals in V01 were partly on Mochiko's behalf (i0066); the memory of her father's slap checked the "億の世界" thought (V05 i0154); Mochiko's question about what she wants, not an argument about money, turned the V08 crisis (V08 i0131–i0139). A remembered person can push the other way: in V05, with the cursor on 「建玉全決算」 and "利益確定は ひとつの正義…!" in her head, her mother's face appears and she pulls back, "違う!" (V05 i0073, re-verified), and holds on for the full sum, calling the 14 million "はした金" (i0071–i0076) before bracketing the position with a stop and a limit (i0077, i0080).
- **Institutions and family control**: the bankruptcy discharge, payment "地道に", and her father's management of her wages "税金払い終わるまでは" (V09 i0104–i0107).

For a reader predicting her, this is the practical consequence: a stated intention of hers to stop, quit or cut later is weak evidence; a structural act already taken, or a person whose regard she fears losing, is strong.

### 4.5 The failure to cut changes kind

Her inability to take a loss is not one thing across the series (reconciliation audit §5.5 row 3; FXK-V01-C010 → FXK-V06-C063 → FXK-V08-C067):

- **V01: felt incapacity**, once her father's money is in the account: "家のお金が溶け始めてる", "動けない… 損切り できない…" (i0131); the narrator: "もはや不可能であった" (i0135).
- **V06: capacity shown, then refused.** She cuts twice by hand, at 18:27 on 5 December (−1,743,000, i0013) after arguing that "普通は… そうしないといけないんだ" (i0012), and again that night on a new short (−1,131,000, i0046). No stop order is shown for either position. Days later (on or after 8 December, INFERENCE MODERATE), when the rate has fallen back to "現在118円" (i0098), she repudiates the rule ("勝率重視で含み損耐えるべきだったよ", i0099), never registering that holding the first short to the rate printed later on the night of the cut (121.597, i0045–i0046) would have lost 5,898,000 instead of 1,743,000, a saving of about 4.2 million (counterfactual ANALYST CALCULATION). She then holds EUR/CHF with no stop "あえて" (V06 i0152).
- **V07–V08: a doctrine stated in advance** (V08 i0006).

A rival reading stays live: that the V06 repudiation relabels an unchanged inability to bear losses. On the V08 night she sits through nine open-loss displays without acting (V08 i0016–i0019), and there policy and incapacity cannot be told apart (UNRESOLVED). Against the rival: her V06 cuts were executed by argument and the repudiation cites goal and horizon, not pain.

### 4.6 Luck, skill and certainty

Her attribution of outcomes swings with audience and result (section 3.6). Under uncertainty she seeks certainty rather than tolerating it: "必勝の通貨" (V06 i0103), "スイス国家が 守ってくれている" (V06 i0115), "さっすがスイス" (V07 i0071), and a named risk dismissed as "織・り・込・み済みのはず!" (V07 i0073). The forecast behind the franc position rested on an official promise; the accepted reading judges it "not irrational; it failed" (V07 §G.1). Not every failure of hers is a failure of forecasting: her V01 support held, two of her three V08 reads paid, and her V10 warning to Kizuna about an uptrend is borne out once on the next chart (V10 i0121, i0140).

### 4.7 Conflicting obligations

When obligations collide she tends to choose the one that keeps others from knowing or paying, and to take the cost herself.

- **V01, survival against her father's savings.** Facing liquidation she reasons "90%の確率で 死ぬより 50%の確率で 生き残る" (i0108), names the money as untouchable ("手を付けちゃいけない", i0110), deposits it (i0111), and plans secret restitution (i0113). Whether she took it to survive or to attack is OPEN (FXK-V01-C015): the narrator gives a defensive account (i0107), while her own later thought says the cash was taken "そのため", to attack (i0122).
- **V04, a friend's plea against her own principle.** She offers Mebuki food money and refuses 1,000,000 for FX, alone afterwards citing her father's maxim that needing to borrow to win means you have already lost, and "お金を貸すのは 芽吹ちゃんの ためにならない…!" (V04 i0067–i0074, re-verified), then doubts having taught her at all (i0075).
- **V08, responsibility against living.** She converts responsibility into a duty to disappear, reasoning that if her father renounced the inheritance "清算完了だ" (V08 i0047); when Mochiko asks what she wants, she cries "死にたくないよ!" and calls that wish "無責任" (V08 i0139, re-verified). The page answers her accounting not with a counter-calculation but with a question about wanting (FXK-V08-C001).
- **V09, honesty against a promise.** Asked by her father to vow never to trade again, she refuses rather than give a promise she might break: "それは誓えない…!", "絶対に しないとは 言い切れない" (V09 i0078, re-verified), and commits instead to openness, "堂々と" (i0079).

### 4.8 Choice and desire

Were her returns to the market choices? The accepted record now answers in part. The agency strand of FXK-V02-C002, held OPEN at the reconciliation (FXK-SYN-C004), was revised on Study C's argument (FXK-SYN-C007, EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH): her returns are depicted as choices owned in her own words — 自己責任 in V02 (i0131), "でも 仕方ないじゃん" in V07 (i0147), 堂々と in V09 (i0079) — made against her own accurate prediction and, in V09, declared openly; the desire behind them is presented as given, by the narrator as a curse (V02 i0132–i0134) and by Kurumi as innate greed (V09 i0031). Whether the returns were also free of compulsion stays OPEN in that row, and the page gives reasons on both sides. For: she is not shown executing a trade she had just decided against at the moment of decision (V04 i0115; V06 i0106), and from V09 to the end of V10 she does not trade. Against: after promising to retire she did not close her trading account (the tempter: "どうして FX口座を解約しなかったの?", V02 i0101), and a domestic account is shown already funded with 2,100,000 while she is "retired" (V02 i0102; the deposit undated); her assent to her father ("…うん", V02 i0038) is followed by her return (i0130–i0131); she cries out "誰か私を止めて…!" (V02 i0109); and in V01 her own felt incapacity to cut is on the page (i0131, i0135). For the portrait the consequence is this: she is shown deciding, repeatedly, against her own accurate prediction, and asking, once, to be stopped; how far the deciding was free is not settled by the page. Her abstention after V09 is confounded with constraint (discharge, her father's control of her wages, 22,963 yen in April 2017), so it cannot by itself show what she would choose free of them.

## 5. Behavioural patterns

### 5.1 Ordinary conduct

Most of her days are not crisis days, and the page shows them steadily. She is a conscientious worker: a week after a 1.31-million-yen night she is back at the register, pleased with "よし今日も ミスなし…!" (V02 i0012), taking extra shifts (i0013). She pays her own share in coins (V03 i0124). She keeps house for her father: curry cooked and eaten alone when he is late (V04 i0115), dishes dried in an apron (V05 i0069) and soaked before her bath (V05 i0147), rubbish sorted by the posted schedule (V08 i0044); in V09 she is expected to have made dinner (i0085, INFERENCE MODERATE) and walks arm in arm with him, straightening his tie (i0091). In 2017 she serves Kizuna's soy latte in the right mug because she knows the order (V10 i0113), sweeps the floor (V09 i0157), minds the office (V10 i0063), picks the restaurant and leads the toast at a welcome party (V10 i0012–i0015) and eats and drinks with open pleasure (i0052–i0053).

Her one persistent habit is **repeated chart-checking**: on the commute, at the shop counter and on the walk home ("相場見ながら 帰ってたら 遅くなっちゃった…", V01 i0056, i0071, i0078), on her phone in class (V03 i0055), while brushing her teeth over the news (V07 i0071), with an octopus speaker set to sound her FX alerts (V07 i0055) and a finger tapping "トントン" while the rate goes against her (V06 i0007). In 2017, not trading, she still watches her colleagues' screens and asks the figures (Kizuna's, V10 i0046, i0155; Nana's, i0141, i0143).

### 5.2 Cooperation and generosity

In ordinary times she is open with her friends about her positions, sizes and wins, announcing orders to the group ("60枚発射です!", V03 i0138; the EUR/CHF position at the Christmas table, V06 i0148). She invites Mebuki into the group's meetings (V03 i0084), hosts Yasuko kindly and steers talk away from FX for her sake (V02 i0150–i0152), and chooses Yasuko as the safe channel for a warning about Mebuki (V04 i0076). She is generous in small things: after announcing her own victory in the friends' contest and then losing it on a rule, she protests nothing and offers to pay anyway (V07 i0020, i0024); she brings food to Mebuki after Mebuki's loss (V07 i0012); in 2017 she offers the new hire the honoured seat and tells her she may refuse the party (V10 i0008, i0014).

### 5.3 Conflict

She rarely confronts, and when she does it is either sharp and brief or turned into comedy. The sharp cases are few and dated: "誰のせいで" to her mother (V01 i0027); in V08 she charges Mochiko with having steered her back into FX in V02 (V08 i0121–i0122) and apologizes within a page (i0123). Accused by Mebuki of hypocrisy, she does not defend her win at all: "…それは 運がよかっただけ…で…" (V04 i0073). The comic mode is "下手くその 気持ちを 考えて 発言してください!" (V09 i0022). With Kizuna, an older colleague, in 2017 she assents to a moral lesson about greed, "…そんなところです", while a fist clenches under the table (V10 i0045, re-verified; the fist hers at INFERENCE MODERATE, V10 E021).

### 5.4 Stress

Under acute stress her responses run in a recognizable order: avoidance first, then the body, then words. In V07 she closes the trading window, turns her mother's photograph down, pulls the duvet over and deletes the broker's mail (i0106, i0113, i0119, i0127), and her breathing and legs fail (i0109–i0121); asked by Yasuko in the friends' group chat, she types only "お金ない", then a flood of reassurance — "ぜ…全然平気!", "生きてるから!", and, panicked into politeness, "死んでないです!" (i0135–i0136). In V08 her body dissents before her words do: a trembling pen as she writes, vomiting, halting at dusk, a fist against a utility pole (i0066, i0078, i0084, i0088), and days without food, sleep or a bath (i0130, i0145). Her words, spoken or written, meanwhile stay brisk and orderly. The gap between calm words and a body in revolt is one of the most reliable signs, across V06–V08, that she is in more danger than she says.

### 5.5 Mistakes and corrections

She corrects some mistakes quickly and others only when forced. The V01 lie to Mochiko about her position (i0116) is followed the next morning by candour ("死にそうに なったから 寝た / 寝てないけど", i0154). The taken cash goes back to her father at discovery (V02 i0024–i0026). The blame of Mochiko becomes an apology (V08 i0123). Her father's journeys home draw an apology (V09 i0123). But the central concealment of her trading from her father is corrected only after a friend makes it a condition (V09 i0050, i0063), and her V06 lesson about cutting losses is reversed within days by the market's next move (V06 i0098–i0099).

### 5.6 Meaningful exceptions

Several acts break the pattern of escalation and concealment and must be kept in any account of her: the five-year wait to the legal age (V01 i0020); trading only her own savings at first (V01 i0041); the refusals of all-in, 100 lots and a loan (V01 i0064–i0066); a domestic account chosen for its smaller "即死リスク" (V02 i0017); the 1,000,000 withdrawn in V03 (i0124); the declined second add (V04 i0115); two losses cut by her own hand (V06 i0013, i0046); a feel trade refused for lack of a guarantee (V06 i0106); the refusal of a vow she might break (V09 i0078); and, from V09 to the end of V10, no trade at all.

### 6.2 Baseline register and humour

Her baseline with peers is cheerful casual: sentence-final じゃん, もん, よね, 〜てるんだ; hearts and humming in the lettering ("うふ♡", "えへ♡", "秘密…っ♥", V03–V04). Her humour is affectionate and verbal: puns ("お・餅・持ってきたよ! 萌・智・子だけに!", V07 i0037), sing-song coinages ("ユロスイチェ〜ック!", V07), emphasis dots for comic stress ("織・り・込・み済み", V07 i0073), English "ノープロブレム!!", mock-formality, and in 2017 comic armour under exposure: "最高機密! トップシークレット です!" (V10 i0044).

### 6.3 Shifts by listener

- **To her father** she performs: in April 2009, two months after her mother's death, she hides her mother's trading journal from him with "ううん なんにも" and a giggle (「えへへ」), then "わかってるよ…" to his warning against FX (V01 i0038–i0039); "だってもう「大人」だもん!" while confessing (V02 i0029); "子供じゃないんだよ〜?" (V05 i0069); minimal assent under his pressure, "…うん", "う…ん", "え…うん" (V02 i0038, i0055, i0087). In V09 the performance drops and her speech to him becomes plain and direct: "それは 誓えない…!" (i0078).
- **To friends** she can be firm without leaving the casual register: "うん それは 穏やかじゃないね?" and "だめ… 貸さない…" to Mebuki (V04 i0067, i0071), or switch to a menacing play-teacher ("いいよ! 教えて あげるよ!", V02 i0065). She uses です/ます in a factual group-chat report ("共有です", "注意です", V04 i0076).
- **To strangers and at work** she uses the service register ("いらっしゃいませー", V01 i0047), polite apology to a co-worker ("就活って 言ったじゃないですか", "すいません…", V05 i0083) and stock politeness in distress ("だ… 大丈夫です", V08 i0082).

### 6.4 Shifts under pressure

- **Self-commands, masculine-leaning**: "いくぞ…", "行けっ", "上がれ!" (V01); "日和ってなんか いられない…!" (V05); "がんばれ私 逃げるな私" (V07).
- **Coarse katakana under anger**: "なってンだっけ", "ふざ けんな!", "ばぁか!" (V01).

### 6.5 Inner versus outer speech

The gap between what she says and what she thinks is widest exactly where she is most exposed. At the Christmas dinner she says "大丈夫だよ!" with a cutesy 「にゃんっ♪」 and fists up (V06 i0148) after panting alone at her desk (i0118). After the shock that left her unable to breathe she writes "全然平気!" to her friends in the group chat (V07 i0120, i0136): the gap holds in writing as in speech. In V02 a black-balloon tempter speaks inside her in her own first person, "私はFXを やめられない…!" In V07 an inner chorus of debt-collector phrases, 「カネ払エ」「逃ゲルナ」「親ニ迷惑カケルナ」 with 「自己責任」 among them, fills her head (i0128). In V10 she tells colleagues only that her 2014 national tax is unpaid, while an inset shows her inner reckoning of 3,807,983 and "自己破産 出来たとしても" (i0045, re-verified).

- **The war lexicon** of V01 ("軍資金", "戦場", "戦略", "発射", "撤退", "天王山") survives into V08's trading cries ("発射ッ!!!", "りカく!!!"), and she uses "戦う" for trading (V02 i0017, i0131).

### 6.7 Silence and the medium's limits

Her silences are as characteristic as her speech: the "……" when she cannot answer, the minimal "…うん" to her father, "…そんなところです" to Kizuna, and in V10 the role of silent witness to her colleagues' investing, warning twice and then assenting (i0033, i0121, i0124). Tone, volume and timing are inferred from balloon shape, font, placement and drawn expression, and attribution where tails are ambiguous follows the accepted readings' grades. Nothing here describes a performed voice.

## 7. Relationships

### 7.1 Her father

Her father is the person she most loves and most hides from. **V01.** The concealment begins in April 2009, two months after her mother's death, when he moves home: she finds her mother's trading journal while unpacking and hides it from him ("ううん なんにも", with a giggle), then answers his warning against FX with "わかってるよ…" (i0038–i0039), her first active deception of him (V01 E007). She chose a commuting university and a scholarship for his sake (i0041), and on the morning of the worst night she waves him off cheerfully ("うん! いってらっしゃい!", attribution MODERATE) while resolving "やるしかない…!" (i0105). On the worst night she takes his 300万 タンス預金 knowing what it means ("手を付けちゃいけない", i0110), deposits it, and plans to put it back without his knowing (i0113). **V02.** He discovers it, slaps her and denies her adulthood (i0022–i0032). Her answer is a confession framed by outcome ("勝手に 借りたのは 悪かったと 思ってるけど / おかげで 勝てたしさ") and a claim to adulthood ("だってもう「大人」だもん!", i0029, re-verified); she gives assent without commitment ("…うん", i0038) and, by the volume's end, returns to trading with his worry acknowledged in thought and overridden (i0130–i0131). **V03–V05.** He is mostly off the page: her home is off limits to the group because he is there (V03 i0080); she adopts his maxim about borrowing to justify refusing Mebuki (V04 i0074); she cooks for him and notices he is late (V04 i0115); she encourages his new posting while he hesitates (V05 i0069); his remembered slap checks her "億の世界" thought (V05 i0154). **V07.** He leaves on a posting at New Year and she lives alone (i0031); after the ruin the rule in her head is 「親ニ迷惑カケルナ」 (i0128). **V08.** She refuses his one call because "軽蔑…されたくない…" (i0036–i0037), and writes him a letter, meant to be read after her death, that asks him to renounce his inheritance so her debt will not pass to him (i0066–i0076). The narrator states that telling him would be the best course and that "罪悪感と 幼稚な 保身" bind her (i0035–i0036). Childhood images of meals with him run behind her words (i0072). **V09.** She first wants to settle everything "お父さんに バレずに" and fears "ビンタ されちゃう" (i0040); after Mochiko makes a job conditional on telling him (i0050), she calls him from her mother's grave (i0063), panic-polite: "ちょっと 帰ってきて ください!" Afterwards she walks arm in arm with him (i0091), agrees that he manage her wages until the tax is paid (i0107) and apologizes for his frequent journeys home (i0123).

What the relationship shows about her: the cost she tries hardest to spare him is knowledge, and it is the concealment that leaves him carrying risks he does not know of. Study B traces the family chain (registered as FXK-SYN-C021): her concealment from her father repeats her mother's concealment from the same man for the same stated reason, not to burden the family; V09 is the first disclosure in the family that is chosen rather than discovered, and authority over money passes by agreement to the person who in V01 was the unknowing owner of the money she took. Her V08 plan was right that the money need not land on him and wrong that her absence would cost him nothing. Whether the V09 change belongs to her or to him (a living daughter after a dead wife; his own admission about the family's money roles) is left open by the page.

## 10. Capabilities and limitations

**Capacities.** She is a disciplined student: five years of self-study before her first trade (V01 i0020), a half-tuition scholarship (V01 i0041), an economics degree course with a summer TOEIC course (V03 i0052) and a graduation thesis on investment behaviour aimed at large gains (V09 i0103). As a trader she reasons in scenarios, gives macro reasons, makes correct calls (a top call before her V05 short, i0157), names scheduled risks (V07 i0072) and computes exactly (V06 i0096; V08 i0030–i0034, where her reckoning of tax and bankruptcy costs is lucid). Through V05 no realized loss of hers is printed: every position she closes ends in profit (V03 reading §H.2), although inside the V01 300-lot position the first 50 lots lost and the averaging-down adds made the whole profit (FXK-V01-C014). By December 2014 she held 18,339,915 in the account plus the 1,000,000 set aside, about 64 times her February stake (ANALYST CALCULATION, conditional on no unshown flows). Socially she is warm, funny, hospitable and well liked; in 2017 she is a reliable clerk.

**Limitations.** Her limits are of willingness more than of knowledge. She states the stop-loss rule as a "normal" duty (V06 i0012) and suspends it; she knows "勝ち逃げ" and does not quit; she knows the floor can break and holds without a stop (V06 i0152). The pages show her capable of cutting (V06 i0013, i0046) and choosing not to: capacity and willingness are visibly different in her. Other limits are of information and of trust: she does not read Mochiko's lies or private contempt (V03 i0090; V05 i0144), does not understand why an order is rejected after her account is emptied (V08 i0023), and cannot ask for help until someone forces the door (V08 i0116–i0123; V09 i0050). The rules of her broker that would bound her risk — a stop-out level or maintenance threshold — are never printed (V01 U-06 OPEN), so it cannot be said whether she knew one and ignored it. She has no physical or special abilities; her body matters in the story as a sign of what she will not say (section 5.4).

### 11.2 What really changes

- **Her decision rule about losses** changes in V06, and for the worse: she learns she can cut and decides not to (FXK-V06-C063; reconciliation §5.5 row 3). This is development in the strict sense, not the revelation of a fixed incapacity, though the rival reading is kept (section 4.5).
- **Her meaning of responsibility** changes twice. It moves from a disclaimer and a licence (V02) to a solitary duty that forbids sharing the cost (V07 i0140, i0149) and then turns toward self-erasure (V08 i0042–i0047, analysed without method); in V09 it becomes disclosure, payment by instalments and accepted control ("地道に", i0106; i0107), and the word 自己責任 disappears from her speech (FXK-V09-C065; Study B's argument that responsibility borne alone and hidden disables help while shared responsibility becomes payable, registered as FXK-SYN-C013: EVALUATION HIGH as motif, INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH as argument). This change was tested once, under maximal pressure, and is not tested again within V10.
- **Her stance toward concealment** changes from hiding until discovered (V01–V08) to a chosen confession (V09 i0063) and a promise of openness about any future investing (V09 i0079). It is only partly kept in 2017: she tells colleagues she was wiped out and owes tax, and withholds the amount and the bankruptcy (V10 i0042–i0045).
- **Her self-understanding** changes: she owns pleasure in the market ("楽しかった 自分が いるんだ", V09 i0058, re-verified; "相場が 大好きだから", i0121) and separates herself from her mother (i0061). Study C's reading, which this portrait adopts, is that the pleasure was present from V02 (the tempter's "私はFXを やめられない…!" and "脳汁", V02 i0100, i0109; the desire presented as given, FXK-SYN-C007) and that what develops is her knowledge of it, not the disposition.
- **Her relationships** change in structure: her father from deceived owner of the money to the agreed manager of her wages (V09 i0107); Mochiko from friend to employer and friend (V09–V10).

### 11.3 What does not change

Her insight into her own errors is accurate from V01 and stays so; what moves is her reference point (2000万 → 2500万 gross → 1億 → a revalued 1000万) and who carries her restraint (no one she could not override, through V08; family and institutions from V09). Her self-reference, her cheerfulness, her domestic habits, her warmth to friends and her reluctance to burden anyone persist through ruin. The appetite persists too: in V09 the winged figure returns with "おかえり" / "ただいま", she expects it to remain "私が 死ぬまで" (i0124–i0126), and she resolves not to banish it but to "支配して / コントロールして みせる…!" (i0128, re-verified), drawn holding its hands. That resolution is untested within V10 (FXK-V09-C032, OPEN).

### 11.4 Late revelations, kept apart from change

Five disclosures re-read earlier pages without changing her: the 2012 friendship and her early balk at the stop rule (V05 i0048–i0049); her mother's process and her own childhood care-taking (V06, mixed authority); the purpose of her father's 300万 (V09 i0074); her account of greed since childhood, staged on a re-presented memory (V09 i0031); and her 2017 admission that she had "実際に 意識したこともある" the 億 world (V10 i0049), which is a retrospective statement, not a V05 goal (the V05 temptation is checked on the page, i0154–i0155).

## 13. Interpretive uncertainty

### 13.1 Rival explanations kept open

- **Restitution or appetite.** Her V01–V08 account (a debt to her dead mother) and her V09 account (innate greed, the mother's failure a pretext) are both on the page. The V09 account is a SELF_REPORT reached after a crisis and by adopting a friend's insult; Mochiko qualifies it at once (V09 i0032). The narrator's third account, a lucky win turned into a lifelong curse (V02 i0132–i0134; FXK-V02-C001, last STRENGTHENED by FXK-V10-C064), competes with both. What would settle the weight among them is not in the boundary. What is settled is narrower: from V04 to V08 the restitution figure and its successors set her size (FXK-SYN-C026; section 4.3).
- **"相場が 大好きだから": self-knowledge or licence.** It may be the truthful naming of a long-present pleasure, or a post-crisis reframing that licenses a return (INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH for the first; the second is not excluded). No trade follows it within V10.
- **Doctrine or relabelled incapacity.** Her V06 repudiation of the stop rule is argued from goal and horizon, not pain, but on the V08 night policy and incapacity cannot be told apart (UNRESOLVED).
- **Luck or skill.** The accepted readings keep her wins' attribution open (V01 §L.1; V05 §L.4); she herself attributes them differently by audience and year.
- **Choice or constraint after V09.** Her abstention coincides with the discharge, her father's control of her wages and 22,963 yen in assets; it cannot by itself show what she would choose free of them.
- **Design or psychology.** Much of her course is visibly designed: her balance equals her mother's to the yen (V07 i0144), the 「すみませんでした」 sheet redraws a composition (V08 i0042), the beach memory is re-presented with a new figure (V09 i0031). A reading of the series as a black comedy of chance, in which outcomes are not earned (FXK-SYN-C006, registered OPEN at G2 and restricted by FXK-SYN-C029), would treat parts of her "psychology" as the plot's instrument. This portrait describes what she does and says; where the page's design does the work, it says so.
- **Reticence in 2017.** Her silence about the amount and the bankruptcy (V10 i0042–i0045) may be shame, prudence or ordinary privacy; the page does not say.
