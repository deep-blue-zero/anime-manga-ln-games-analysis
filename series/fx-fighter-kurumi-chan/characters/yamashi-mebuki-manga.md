## Orientation

**Who she is.** 山師芽吹 (Yamashi Mebuki) is a twenty-year-old third-year student at 新都大学 from a Kansai-speaking family, living alone in the university town on a scholarship and food parcels from home. In the Japanese collected manga **FX戦士くるみちゃん** (story でむにゃん, art 炭酸だいすき) she is the novice whom the protagonist Fukuga Kurumi and Kurumi's friend Kogane Mochiko bring into foreign-exchange margin trading in 2014. She is the centre of V03 and V04, a principal thread of V05 and V06, and a supporting presence in V07–V09. Across those volumes she turns a 200,000円 scholarship stake into 613,200円, then loses every yen she deposits in FX — 3,600,000円 in all (ANALYST CALCULATION on the printed deposits), including her fourth-year tuition, card credit, a student loan and 2,000,000円 borrowed from Mochiko at 109.5% a year — takes leave from university, works at an esthetic salon under the name 若葉, and by autumn 2015 has nearly repaid Mochiko and opened a new FX account. She does not appear in the 2017 office of the V09 coda or anywhere in V10.

**Why she matters.** She is a complete case of financial self-destruction, run beside Kurumi's and ahead of it: the same leveraged position held without a stop past a point she had named, funded from money she had labelled as protected, and ended by the broker. She is also the person on whom the series' clearest interpersonal wrong lands: the trusted senior who coached her, lied to her about her own position, lent to her at 109.5% and held her parents' address as security is Mochiko. And she is one of the few people in the series who comes out of ruin into ordinary work and says that she likes it.

**Boundary and spoilers.** This portrait covers the accepted V01–V10 main narrative and uses V10 unit-end knowledge (present time Monday 24 April 2017). It contains full spoilers through V10. Nothing outside the admitted pages is used: no real market history, no knowledge of the title beyond V10, no adaptation. Paratext (covers, flaps, afterwords, under-jacket notes such as the V03 "芽吹一家初期案") is classified only and is never evidence for her character.

## 1. Identity and circumstances

**Background.** Her family lives in a Kansai-speaking region: a loud, practical mother who calls her to dinner with a pot and ladle, a lenient father, and a younger brother, 健斗, who is "about to go on to school" (V03 i0024–i0030, i0074). By her own account the family still has twenty years left on the house loan (V03 i0074). She had promised to pay her own fourth-year tuition from savings, and her mother holds her to it; the mother's "学業以上に有効活用できるかアホ!" answers her plan to put the money to "better use" (V03 i0028–i0029). The family's praise "やればできる子" is the phrase she lives inside (V03 i0058). None of the family is named except 健斗; the parents are "her mother" and "her father" throughout.

**Circumstances in 2014.** She lives on the third floor of a block next door to Yasuko, whom she treats as her closest everyday friend (V02 i0073–i0074). Her flat has a loft bed with a ladder, a star lamp, rabbit posters and figures, a teddy bear, bunting and a sofa (V03 i0071, i0082; V04 i0010). She has a scholarship and no part-time job, and lives on food parcels from home: "余裕で生きられます!" (V02 i0060). She met Mochiko in a language class in April 2014 (V05 i0051), and through Mochiko she meets Kurumi.

**Her place in the story.** V02 introduces her as the exuberant beginner who delegates every decision to Kurumi's calls and multiplies her stake. V03 is her volume: a wipe-out, the tuition gamble, a long losing short, rivalry with Kurumi and the lie that keeps her in. V04 follows the harm into debt and work. V05 shows her "rescue" trading on borrowed money and the moment she overhears the truth about Mochiko's position. V06 ends her trading by the broker's forced liquidation and turns her to wage work. V07 is her partial disclosure and her reckoning with Mochiko. V08 shows her, in a flashback, as the person who pushes Mochiko to help Kurumi. V09 shows her repaying, protective of Kurumi, and trading again. She is absent from the 2017 coda (V09) and from V10.

**Circumstances after the crisis.** By 25 December 2014 she is on leave from university (休学), studying esthetics, working at the salon she calls "癒やし" and dreaming of a shop of her own (V07 i0007–i0010). In January 2015 she mentions a バイト shift in a chat (V08 i0107), which the V08 reading takes as a separate part-time job. In autumn 2015 she is close to repaying Mochiko and holds a South African rand position in a new domestic account (V09 i0114–i0117). Whether she returned to university, where she works in autumn 2015 and where she is in 2017 are not shown.

## 3. Personal history, and what was disclosed when

Most of what the reader learns about her past arrives late, and it re-reads earlier scenes rather than changing her. Such late revelation is kept apart from development throughout this portrait, as the [character qualification](../audits/FX_KURUMI_CHARACTER_QUALIFICATION.md) requires of every monograph (its section 1.5).

- **The family promise (disclosed V03, at the time of depiction).** The tuition money is hers by saving and her family's by promise: she said she would pay her fourth year herself, and she put the money into FX in September against her mother's explicit refusal (V03 i0028–i0029, i0058). Her mother's later parcel note, "学費の貯金大変やろ? ご飯は送ったるから 根性入れて頑張るように! 母より" (V04 i0010; packed in V03 i0159), shows that the family still believes she is saving it. The narrator asks, over that note, whether she had forgotten what the money was (V03 i0159; NARRATOR_PRESENTATION).
- **Why she started (disclosed in two stages).** In V02 she says it in one cheerful phrase: "働きたくないから ですねっ!" (V02 i0056), with no wish to be a "社会の歯車" (i0057) and a plan to reach 1億 in "一発" (i0058–i0059). In V04, on a night train after her ruin, she gives Yasuko a different and deeper reason: "FXってもっとキラキラしてて 楽しい世界だと思ってたんだ…", "みんなで楽しくお金を儲けられたら 素敵だろうなって…" (V04 i0135; FXK-V04-C019). V05's flashback to April 2014 adds the first scene: "個人投資家って響きがかっこいい", money without work, "億万長者" (V05 i0051). The V04 and V05 material is late revelation of a motive that was already acting in V02 — the recruiting of Yasuko, the group contest she proposes, her pleasure in trading "together" — not a new motive (INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH).
- **How Mochiko saw her from the start (disclosed V05, to the reader only).** The April 2014 flashback shows Mochiko's private contempt behind her assent (V05 i0051–i0052; FXK-V05-C008), and in November Mochiko privately resents her as a girl who "has everything" (V05 i0053; FXK-V05-C009). Mebuki never learns these thoughts.
- **What she knew about Mochiko's position, and when.** From October 2014 she believes Mochiko is short with her and suffering the same loss (V03 i0148; affirmed V04 i0087). She learns otherwise only by overhearing it in a café in early December (V05 i0144–i0146). She hears Mochiko's own account of using her only on 25 December (V07 i0017).
- **What she never learns on the page.** How Kurumi's mother died (she is absent when Kurumi tells it in V04, and her accusation of Kurumi is made in ignorance of it: V04 i0072, INFERENCE HIGH); the full extent of Kurumi's debt and crisis beyond what Mochiko tells her in January 2015 (V08 i0105–i0106); Yasuko's 2013 history.
- **Her parents' knowledge.** No page shows them learning of the FX, the tuition, the debt or the salon. She lies to her mother about school on 5 December (V06 i0005), then that night calls home and asks for her father because she has something to tell him (V06 i0044); the content of that talk is never shown (V06-U03). At Christmas she reports that her parents opposed her leaving university ("とりあえず大学は卒業しとけって", V07 i0008). That the Christmas report reflects the December call is INFERENCE MODERATE; what they were told of the money is UNRESOLVED.

## 4. Personality and psychology

**Values and goals.** What she wants most consistently is to belong to a bright, winning group and to be seen as capable inside it. Money matters, but in her own words it matters as shared fun ("みんなで楽しく", V04 i0135) and as proof that she is the "やればできる子" her family praised (V03 i0058). She brings friends in (she recruits Yasuko, V02 i0062–i0063, i0148–i0155; invites her again to the rand in V09 i0117), invents the group contest (V02 i0157–i0160), calls the circle "仲間" (V03 i0152) and, at her lowest, grieves that she had wanted "萌智子先輩と一緒に勝ちたかったな——" (V07 i0014–i0015). Early, she also wants money without work ("働きたくないから", V02 i0056). After her ruin her goals become a craft and a shop of her own (V07 i0009).

**Self-conception, and how it moves.** In V03 trading is her identity: "ポンドで育った私" (i0060), "最強のポジションを持っとんねん" at −559,500 (i0095), "私は戦うよ" (i0094). In V04 it splits into bravado — "私は無敵なんやから…" (i0062) — and its opposite, "アホでしょ? 私…" (i0131). In V05 she names herself prey in the traders' slang, "どうせ私は養分で〜す" (V05 i0101; FXK-V05-C014; the first "養分" in the corpus, by search of the accepted readings). In V07 she judges herself without excuse: "私は勝手に盛り上がって自滅したマヌケ" (i0014, unballooned thought, re-opened). By V08 she speaks of herself modestly to Mochiko — Kurumi is "私と違って 頭いい" (V08 i0105), and, having asked whether FX can leave a trader in debt, "すみません 勉強不足で…" (i0106) — while acting with more initiative than ever (section 8). These are SELF_REPORTs; the series does not endorse the harshest of them, and Kurumi and Mochiko see her as grown, not as a fool (V07 i0006, i0009; V08 i0110).

**Fears.** Her governing fear is that her parents will be burdened and will know. When Mochiko takes her family's address and number she begs "親には言わないでください!" (V04 i0118), and at the ticket machine afterwards she thinks "返済滞ったら親に迷惑かかってまう…", then corrects herself: "いや…もう…親が知らんだけで… ずっと迷惑かけっぱなしや…" (V04 i0128, re-opened; FXK-V04-C021). That self-correction is the clearest sign that she knows concealment does not spare them; she conceals anyway. Next to it run a fear of being the only loser ("もしかして負けてるの私だけか…!?", V03 i0088, re-opened) and a fear of being an "アホ" — the word she hears Mochiko use of anyone still short (V05 i0145) and repeats of herself (V07 i0015).

**Vulnerabilities.** (1) Dependence on a voice that licenses what she already wants: Kurumi's calls in V02, Mochiko as "相場のプロ" in V03–V04 (V03 i0153), with whom she checks even when she has decided ("萌智子先輩… 私でも… 勝てるでしょうか…?", V04 i0107, re-opened). (2) A reference point that moves to her own suffering: after weeks of loss, getting back to zero is "割に合わん" (V03 i0128–i0129), and the planned +229,700 exit is "しょぼい" beside Kurumi's millions (i0130–i0131). (3) The fusion of her worth with trading results, which makes cutting a loss feel like a verdict on herself. (4) Shame, which makes her hide exactly what would let others help (her parents throughout; her wipe-out from Yasuko in V03, "10万円くらい", i0049; her workplace, "はずかしいから内緒", V04 i0137; her job, "なんか言うの恥ずかしくて", V07 i0009).

**Contradictions.** "私貯金めっちゃうまいねんで?" (V03 i0028) beside tuition put into a short; "これ借りてもきっとうまくいかへん…" thought in the same moment she asks whether she can win (V04 i0107); a disowned superstition (V03 i0114) beside bracelet, haircut and look-away rituals (V03 i0075, i0105–i0113); care for Yasuko — a protective lie so she will not worry (V03 i0049–i0050) — beside contempt for her ("一回も稼いだことないくせに!", V03 i0142); dependence on Kurumi beside private ill-wishes ("全額突っ込んで失敗しちゃえばいいのに…", V03 i0124, re-opened). None is resolved by a choice of one side; each pair is held.

**Coping strategies.** She turns money into a game ("死亡ライン", "残り寿命(ライフ)", "Alive / Dead", "対戦よろしくお願いします", V03 i0072–i0073, i0109) and her plans into heroic fantasy (the "救い玉作戦", announced in a mage's costume, V05 i0063, with samurai and archer figures in the same fantasy, V05 §H.2; "防御力30万の盾", V04 i0056). Under loss she ritualizes (V03 i0106–i0113), prays to and scolds the market (V05 i0092–i0097), personifies it as a killer who speaks her own Kansai (V04 i0018) and mocks herself. Food is her comfort and her barometer: one meal a day planned (V03 i0076), nine hours without food (i0114), a cheap doria as a revelation (i0120–i0121), and later a draft beer and "私は遠慮なく食べるけどね〜" (V07 i0006; V09 i0088). From V06 her coping becomes work.

**Responsibility.** In private she first treats responsibility as a duty to win the money back: "取り返さなきゃ… 絶対に…" (V03 i0074, re-opened), reasoning from her brother's schooling and the house loan. With others she takes responsibility readily, even where it is not hers: she apologizes to Yasuko for getting her into FX ("FXなんかはじめさせて… 大事なお金使わせちゃってごめんね…", V04 i0136; Yasuko refuses the apology, "あたしの責任だよ"), warns a customer that her hunch is only a hunch and apologizes in advance ("えっでも私の勘ですよ…?", "損させたら申し訳ないです…!", V06 i0035, re-opened), owns the debt ("借金返してからにします", V06 i0053, re-opened) and blames herself, not Mochiko, at Christmas (V07 i0014). She never says 自己責任 of herself; others say it of her (Mochiko, V04–V05; Yasuko's concession, V05 i0133).

## 5. Cognition and decision-making

This section explains, for her, the decision models argued across persons in Study C, "Risk, loss and financial self-destruction across persons" ([studies/FX_KURUMI_RISK_LOSS_AND_FINANCIAL_SELF_DESTRUCTION.md](../studies/FX_KURUMI_RISK_LOSS_AND_FINANCIAL_SELF_DESTRUCTION.md)), sections 2.2, 4 and 6.2. The models are proposed explanations of what the page shows; they are not diagnoses, and the page supplies behaviour more securely than mechanism.

**What she notices.** When calm she is numerate and exact. She states the risk of an add correctly (V03 i0072), computes her own "death line" correctly three times (i0073, i0078, i0110) and again after re-entry ("1.5 yen", V04 i0021). She notices standing — who is winning, who is losing, how her 200,000 looks beside Kurumi's millions (V03 i0088, i0131) — and she notices endorsements ("暴落予想は萌智子先輩のお墨付きなんやぞ…?", V04 i0018). She does not notice what she trades in V02 (i0070) or what an indicator is in V03 (i0041). By V08 she notices people: she sees through Mochiko's cover story for visiting the salon and reads her hidden reasoning about not messaging Kurumi (V08 i0103, i0108).

**How she reasons.** Her early reasoning learns from outcomes only ("勝ちかただけ", V02 i0064), extrapolates linearly ("来月には100万円", V02 i0121), turns one win into a doctrine of delegation (V02 i0088–i0089) and believes cooperation removes risk ("みんなで協力すれば負ける要素ない", V02 i0148). Under loss she builds arguments that license holding: a "完璧な論理" the next day undoes (V03 i0062); the gambler's fallacy said aloud ("なら くるみ先輩は負けますね", V03 i0124–i0125); Kurumi's survival reread as a "cheat method" of infinite borrowing (V04 i0023–i0026; FXK-V04-C005); a card deposit as a "魔法の入金方法" that the narrator corrects to "一種の借金" (V04 i0015–i0016); a student loan as "正義の借金" and "くるみ先輩も通った道" (V04 i0032); a "救い玉" against a "クソポジ" that the narrator dismantles as "とどのつまり ただのポジション" (V05 i0066–i0067, i0090). She explains others' success and her own losses by luck ("運だけウーマン", V03 i0103; i0105, i0125; FXK-V03-C016).

**How she prioritizes.** Two references govern her choices. The first is break-even: her exit rule is "+20万で即撤退" to recover her lost stake (V03 i0059), her V05 goal "プラマイゼロでFX引退" (i0061). The second is the family's ignorance, which she ranks above her solvency (V04 i0118, i0128). On the night of 15 October two exits on her own break-even terms arrive, one after the other, and she takes neither, because the reference has moved to what she has suffered and to Kurumi's gains. At 21:30 the account is back above her deposits (+8,900: her break-even goal, "原資奪還", reached), and she weighs that against her ordeal: "あの地獄の日々を耐え抜いて それで得た利益が数千円か…!?", "割に合わんやろ…!" (V03 i0127–i0129). At 22:28 her pre-set target is met (+229,700, "ようやく最初の目標利益達成…!"), and the 200,000 that would remain after withdrawing the tuition and living money is "かなりしょぼくないか…?" beside Kurumi's "何百万" (i0130–i0131). The second moment is the one the V03 reading calls the clearest error signal of the volume (EVALUATION HIGH), the one the narrator later names "最後の逃げ場" (i0136; NARRATOR_PRESENTATION), and the one where the competing claim FXK-V03-C002 places the decisive failure (its "hers alone" since downgraded, FXK-SYN-C009). Study C reads this as reference-point sizing: her position grows with the distance to break-even ("あとたった18回" → "多くない?" → 25 more lots, V05 i0085–i0087), not with the money at risk (Study C, SC-2).

**Under uncertainty.** She looks for a voice to decide for her: Kurumi's calls in V02 (i0089), Mochiko's in V03–V04. Her question to Mochiko in V03 is direct and her exit is conditional on the answer ("撤退しようかな…", i0147); Mochiko lies (i0148). In V04 her question is not whether to borrow but whether she can win (i0107). Her dependence is selective: she rejects Kurumi's authority once she resents her ("騙されんで", V03 i0122; "全然説得力ないです!", V04 i0072) and Yasuko's as a "初心者" (V03 i0142), and keeps the voice that licenses holding. This selective deference corresponds to one of the models in Study C's model table (its section 3); the page evidence for it is the run of rejections and retentions just cited. In V06, where her own hunch is wanted by a customer, she marks it as only a hunch (i0035).

**Under conflicting obligations.** Her obligations to her parents (the tuition promise; not to burden them) collide with her losses, and she resolves the collision by concealment and more borrowing, each step justified as the way to make the parents whole. Study B calls 迷惑 the hinge of this pattern across the series: each daughter hides what would let her parents help, so as not to be a burden, and so enlarges the burden she fears (Study B, "Obligations, relationships and where the cost of a choice lands", [studies/FX_KURUMI_OBLIGATIONS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COST.md](../studies/FX_KURUMI_OBLIGATIONS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COST.md), section 8.2).

**Insight that does not govern.** Her failures are not ignorance of risk (FXK-V03-C005). She knows, and the knowing does not decide: "アホか私は…!" and then the next deposit (V04 i0027–i0028); "借りない選択はないんでしょうか…?" (V04 i0105); and in one inner breath "ほんまはもうやめたいねん!", "これ借りてもきっとうまくいかへん…", "でも…", "逆転できるって言われたら… もう——" (V04 i0107, re-opened). The V04 reading calls this reconsideration voiced and overridden — alone, under the lender's answers, and by deference to a promise of reversal (FXK-V04-C023). Study C places her in its cross-person claim that insight is usually accurate and inert in the moment, and that restraint which holds is carried from outside (SC-3). In her case the restraint came from the broker. The one stop she made herself while money remained — refusing to put her wages into the account — was resignation about the market, not a judgement of her position: "入金してもどうせ全部溶かす", "動けば動くほど損するだけや", "そういう仕組みになっとんや FXってやつは——" (V05 i0102, re-opened; FXK-V05-C014), and she kept the position. Study C's summary is that her change from holding to letting go between V03 and V06 was "done for her" (section 6.2).

**What drove the sequence.** Study C (SC-1) gives the series' shared sequence of self-destruction a different driver in each person; for her it is belonging and a borrowed authority — "キラキラ", "みんなで楽しく", Mochiko as "プロ", and the title "立派なFX戦士" conferred by Mochiko at the moment she borrows (V04 i0110) — with money also wanted and the tuition as the stake that turns a loss into panic ("来年の学費なんです", V03 i0099). Her funding followed a chain of money first labelled as protected and then made stakeable: scholarship, tuition, living money, card credit, a student loan, Mochiko's loan (Study C, SC-4). It ended not by her judgement but by exhaustion and the broker's close (V04 i0012; V06 i0039).

**Fitting models, and where they stop.** The page supports, for her: realization aversion ("まだ負けてないんやから", V03 i0111; "損切りしなきゃ幻の数字やし", i0158; a cut made conditional and then refused, "こんな膨らみきった損失で確定するのはアホや", V05 i0087–i0091); loss chasing (same-night re-entry, "取・り・返・さ・ん・と マジでやばい…!", V04 i0013); sunk cost and escalating commitment ("もう後戻りできない…", V04 i0028; "一緒にピンチを乗り越えてきたこのポジション", V03 i0140); perceived control ("強制ロスカットされんかったら絶対勝つ", V03 i0077); avoidance and magical thinking (look-away, prayer); social comparison; and family-directed concealment. Two counterexamples keep the account from being a type: in V05 she takes modest exits on her first two rescue trades ("これ以上欲張らん", i0065–i0066), and she refuses to deposit her wages. Her own model, given after the fact, is the sharpest in the series: "なんか負けてるときって 頭が悪くなる気がするんです…" (V08 i0107, re-opened; SELF_REPORT).

**After the debt.** In autumn 2015 she has opened a domestic account and holds a rand position because "ちょっとお金に余裕出てきたから やりたくなっちゃって" — before her loan is repaid and after asking Mochiko where her debt stands (V09 i0114–i0115, re-opened). Her grasp of the carry is hearsay-thin ("金利がめっちゃ高いっぽいから", i0117, re-opened; "金利の差額? が いい感じで…", i0116), her plan for a fall is unspecified ("下がったときの作戦も考えてるから大丈夫!", i0117), and the page's own diagram sets a short rising line of interest against a long falling line of loss (i0117). Size, direction and outcome are never printed (V09-U06). Whether her rule changed is not established (Study C, section 6.2); the V09 reading treats the re-entry before repayment as a reliability flag on her self-report, not as a shown error.

## 6. Behavioural patterns

**Ordinary conduct.** Sociable, physical and comic: arcade dance games with Yasuko (V02 i0075–i0079), a lavish dinner on her gains (V02 i0085), arriving late to the Christmas dinner and staging a grievance about the eaten meat (V07 i0003–i0005). She hoards snacks from hungry guests (V03 i0083) and plugs her laptop in the wrong way every time (V02 i0061). She shares food and treats with Yasuko (V09 i0088). At home with her family she is quick-tempered and childish: told off about the tuition, she storms from the table — "この話終わりや! ご飯が不味くなったわ!" (V03 i0029, re-opened).

**At work.** She is conscientious and procedural: pointing-and-calling checklists before a session (V06 i0016–i0017), exact timing ("時間ぴったりです", announced when a customer asks for more, V06 i0042, re-opened), self-coaching ("まずは 愛想よくして 好印象!", V08 i0097), "お金を もらってる以上 ちゃんとやりますよ" (V08 i0100), an option sold (i0101) and salon sales lore — 営業メール, リピート率 (i0109). She hides her FX knowledge from a customer who lectures her (V06 i0019) and, when he follows her hunch, warns and apologizes in advance (i0035). In the V05 night shift a customer and a senior worker humiliate her and she gets through it (V05 i0057–i0059; what passed is not shown).

**Cooperation and conflict.** She cooperates by joining and by following: she adopts Kurumi's "戦う" and the circle's "発射" (V03 i0032, i0069, i0094), trades on others' calls, and keeps faith with Mochiko against Kurumi (hiding her repayment from Kurumi, V05 i0143). Her conflicts are mostly inward. To Kurumi she shows rivalry and devaluation only in thought until V04, when a refused loan turns her polite supplication — a premium offered, "くるみ先輩なら私の気持ちがわかるはずです…!" — into an accusation, "自分は借金して爆益出したくせに…" (V04 i0069–i0072), and then into a polite withdrawal, "無理言ってすみませんでした…" (i0073). To Yasuko she says the cruellest thing she says to anyone (V03 i0142) and then thanks and apologizes as she leaves (i0143). With Mochiko she never quarrels; she defers "色々言いたいこと" until the debt is paid (V06 i0053) and, on the page, never says it.

**Stress responses.** Bodily: hyperventilation and "やばい!" ×4 at the forced close (V04 i0013), nausea and vomiting while holding (i0059), a thrown and taped laptop (V03 i0070, i0108), desk-stabbing and leg-jiggling (V05 i0082, i0088–i0089). Behavioural: repeated phone-checking (V03 i0084), the look-away ritual, repetition ("耐えて耐えて耐えて耐えて…", V04 i0060), prayer. Social: concealment and avoidance (Yasuko's invitation unanswered and a call probably cut by her, V04 i0061, i0079, INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH). The page several times makes a danger to her person salient to the reader — a solitary night page with "こんな世界 知らないほうがよかった——" (V04 i0122), Yasuko's fear for her life and Mochiko's contemptuous dismissal of that fear (V05 i0138), and her vision of the account's death, "私はここで死ぬんやな…" (V06 i0025). No page gives her a stated intention, a plan or an act; the accepted readings keep these as reader dread and trader's idiom (the V04 reading's counterreading L.9; the V06 reading's counterreading 2), and so does this portrait. She is calm and working on the page after the V06 vision (i0040–i0044).

**Mistakes and corrections.** She corrects herself in words often and in action rarely while she trades. The corrections in action are few and specific: modest exits on two rescue trades (V05 i0065–i0066), the refusal to deposit her wages (V05 i0102), no wage deposit to save the short (V06), and the move to disclosure (most of her story to Yasuko, V04 i0137–i0139; the wipe-out to her creditor, V06 i0051). After V06 her corrections are of conduct: she apologizes for the café confrontation of Kurumi's father (V09 i0096–i0097), pays on schedule (V09 i0114).

**Meaningful exceptions.** On 3 November, on the way to the loan office in the middle of her crisis, she rises to give her seat to an elderly woman, who declines by gesture (V04 i0031, re-opened; wordless). She holds one boundary under her lender's pressure: she refuses night work ("むむ無理ですよ…!", V04 i0103) and chooses a salon whose listing excludes sexual services — "これが限界です…!" (V04 i0116, re-opened). She cares about a stranger's money more than he does (V06 i0035). These exceptions show that her decency was intact throughout the period of her worst decisions.

## 7. Speech and communication

**The main rule: Kansai inside, standard outside.** Her inner voice and her speech at home are Kansai-type (ねん, やろ, ほんま, あかん, 〜へん, 〜てまう, ちゃう, せぇへん, アホタレ, やんけ); her speech to seniors, customers and strangers is polite standard (です/ます, 先輩). The switch first reads as concealment: in one scene she thinks "すでに今月分の奨学金(生活費)はFX口座に入れた…!" and says "お茶置きますね" (V03 i0083), says "すみません ちょっと …メール打たなきゃなんで…" and thinks "お話し中に相場動いたら大変やろが!" (V03 i0084, re-opened; FXK-V03-C021). V04 refines it: Kansai stays her inner voice even when she tells Yasuko her story, and the confession itself is in standard ("私ね…", "これが最後の勝負だよ…", V04 i0135, i0139, both re-opened), so the switch tracks inner against outer voice as well as what she hides (FXK-V04-C027).

The words are those of Mochiko's soft public formula ("うん そうだね", V03 i0152) used the same way, as a surface over a contrary inner voice — a structural echo (INFERENCE MODERATE); no borrowing is claimed.

V08: service and business katakana — "新規さん", "オプション", "リピート率", "エビデンス", which Mochiko teases — and no Kansai lettered at all. V09: "てへへ" (i0115), a fierce polite "絶対許さないんですけど!" (i0095). She sometimes uses trading verbs loosely ("こんなしょっぱい利益で売ってたまるか" for closing a short, which is a buy-back, V03 i0129).

**Humour and silence.** She is funny about herself (comic grievance, V07 i0003–i0005; "400万円の… 焼き肉やね…" about her −3,400,000, V07 i0012) and, by V08, about her creditor: warning that Kurumi might turn to a loan shark, she names Mochiko as the type, to Mochiko's face — "闇の金融屋さんに 飛び込んでも おかしく ないんですよ! 萌智子先輩 みたいな!" (V08 i0108). Her silences carry weight: the "……" when a customer tells her "責任は僕にあるから" (V06 i0035, re-opened); thanks to Mochiko held in thought, "まだ直接ありがとうとは言えないけど" (V07 i0018–i0019); the reproach she defers and never voices; her ill-wishes toward Kurumi, which exist only in unballooned thought (V03 i0108, i0124).

**The medium's limits.** This is written register, not performed voice: "Kansai" means lettered dialect forms, and nothing about accent, pitch or delivery is recoverable. Some attributions rest on balloon placement (for example V03 i0120). The absence of Kansai in V08 may reflect the scenes (work and a senior's visit) or lettering rather than a change in her; one volume does not settle it. Her V09 speech is a small sample.

## 8. Relationships

Each tie is directed (A → B and B → A are different facts) and dated. The account of who pays for what draws on Study B, "Obligations, relationships and where the cost of a choice lands" ([studies/FX_KURUMI_OBLIGATIONS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COST.md](../studies/FX_KURUMI_OBLIGATIONS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COST.md)), sections 3–6 and 9, whose consequences for her are explained here; its separate questions are left to it.

### 8.1 Mochiko (小金萌智子)

**Mebuki → Mochiko.** Idolization from the first months: "萌智子先輩に出会って人生変わりましたよ〜" (V02 i0059). In V03 she confesses her tuition loss to Mochiko as to a "プロ" (i0097–i0099), ties a half-formed exit to Mochiko's view and asks her directly whether the rate will rise (i0144–i0147), and after the lie that Mochiko is short too, declares "私たちは仲間ですよ!" (i0152). In V04 she is a grateful debtor who asks her creditor whether she can win (i0107, i0109) and believes Mochiko shares her loss (i0087). In V05 she keeps faith — hides her repayment from Kurumi and pities Mochiko's supposed loss ("そんなん聞かんでええのに", i0143–i0144) — until she overhears the truth (i0145–i0146). In V06, having already transferred 30,000, she reports the wipe-out to her creditor on the Monday morning after the close — "借りたお金全部溶かしました 退場です" — and defers her reproach until she owes nothing (i0051–i0053, i0053 re-opened). At Christmas her first thought on Mochiko's +74% is admiration ("やっぱりすごいなぁ 萌智子先輩は…", V07 i0014, re-opened); after Mochiko's admission she holds two things together — "……騙されていたのは事実で" and gratitude for the hard lessons, not yet spoken (i0018–i0019). In V08 she is warm, teasing and clear-eyed: she reads Mochiko's cover story, jokes about the loan, and pushes her to act for Kurumi (i0103–i0110). In V09 she is a debtor near clear who has to whisper to her creditor to learn where she stands (i0114).

**Mochiko → Mebuki (another character's conduct, as it bears on her).** Contempt behind assent at the first meeting (V05 i0051–i0052); a novice set at 500× and taught the "一発" (V02 i0007, i0059); in V03 knowledge that the money is tuition (i0099), hold-supporting advice that names her parents as a backstop ("失敗しても親が払ってくれる", i0100), a lie about her own position in answer to Mebuki's direct question (i0147–i0148) and the plan to keep her short as a "canary" until "その鳴き声が止むまでは" (i0149–i0150) — graded manipulation for informational gain with private enjoyment (V03 reading §I, EVALUATION HIGH). In V04 the 109.5% loan, the exits answered with a doubled sum, the parents' address and number taken as security, "親を悲しませちゃだめだよ?" (i0103–i0119) — manipulation (INFERENCE HIGH on the acts and words), with small kindnesses beside it (fare and a coat, i0118, i0120). In V05 the loan described to Yasuko as a hedge of her own long and no duty of kindness owed (i0130–i0133). From V06 no new exploitation: lenient words, a flat private "そっか 芽吹ちゃん死んじゃったか", repayment worry, and approval of her turn to work, "やっと人間(まとも)になったじゃん" (V06 i0051–i0054). In V07 the admission without remorse: "私は芽吹を利用して勝ちに行った", "悪いことしたとは思ってない", and release offered once the debt is repaid (V07 i0017; FXK-SYN-C005). In V08 she pays full price at Mebuki's salon (i0102) and thinks "本当 たくましくなったな——" (i0110, re-opened; Mochiko's thought by placement, after her smile, lettered over Mebuki's face).

**What the tie shows about her.** She gave Mochiko more trust than anyone and was the person in the series most harmed by trusting. Her response to learning it was not anger, revenge or exposure: she disclosed to the person who had deceived her, worked, paid, deferred judgement until she owed nothing, and then, given the admission, chose to hold deception and gratitude together. By V08 the dependence is gone and the warmth remains; she treats Mochiko as an equal she can tease and press. No page shows her receiving an apology, a refund of interest or an account of the lies, and none shows her asking for one (Study B, section 6.2).

**Where the cost landed (Study B's account, explained for her).** On the money, Study B's answer is unambiguous: Mebuki paid all of it — her 3,600,000円 of FX deposits, interest whose total is never printed, and a year of study — while Mochiko paid nothing in money and realized +7,691,000 on her own long in the scene in which she reads Mebuki's report of ruin (V06 i0051–i0052; the time of the close is unprinted). Her family carried an exposure they never knew of: their address and number held as security (V04 i0118–i0119). Study B counts the loan as the only loan between friends in ten volumes and the series' clearest exploitation, and finds that every other proposed transfer of credit between friends is refused (its thesis B2). For Mebuki this means that the one person who agreed to lend to her is the one whose lending harmed her, while Kurumi, who refused her 1,000,000 on protective grounds (V04 i0071), did her no harm by refusing — though the refusal cost Kurumi an accusation.

**Agency: what is settled and what is not.** Four accepted claims frame the question how much of her V03 holding and V04 borrowing the page charges to Mochiko: FXK-V03-C001 (her harm self-authored but socially aggravated) against FXK-V03-C002 (the decisive failure hers alone, on 15 October), and FXK-V04-C001 (the decisive escalation relational) against FXK-V04-C002 (her course set before Mochiko spoke). At this portrait's snapshot all four were OPEN in the ledger pending their argument in G3 (reconciliation audit, section 5.4 c); they have since been written as rows FXK-SYN-C008 and C009 (REVISE), C011 (STRENGTHEN) and C012 (REVISE). Study B's section 9 argues them together and proposes a combined finding rather than a winner: every order, deposit, add and refusal was hers, and she says so herself (V07 i0014); Mochiko's deception and exploitation were real, deliberate and admitted (V05 i0131, i0145; V07 i0017); and the page declines to fix how much the lie changed the outcome — when Mebuki learned the truth she still did not close (V05 i0145 → V06 i0039), which weakens, without settling, the reading that the lie removed her one exit, since December is not 15 October (V03-U05). For the portrait the consequence is this: she should be described as the author of her trades and as the object of a confirmed wrong, both at once, which is exactly how she describes herself at Christmas. Those rows write the transitions Study B proposed — for V03, self-authorship strengthened and the wrong confirmed and admitted with its causal weight undetermined (FXK-SYN-C008), and "hers alone" downgraded (FXK-SYN-C009); for V04, the relational mechanism confirmed by the lender's own account (FXK-SYN-C011) and Mochiko's stated hedge superseding "motive unstated" (FXK-SYN-C012); this monograph decides none of them.

### 8.2 Kurumi (福賀くるみ)

**Mebuki → Kurumi.** In V02 admiring dependence: "FXの天才", "くるみ先輩を信じて", daily urgent messages and total delegation (i0089). In V03, after Kurumi's stay-out answer about the pound, rivalry ("軽く超えてやる", i0032, as she enters the 246× trade); during her wipe-out, a reproach said alone ("知ってたなら教えてくださいよ…!", i0042); afterwards devaluation — "運だけウーマン" (i0103), "落ち目" (i0153) — and ill-wishes she keeps to herself (i0108, i0124, re-opened), all seeded in part by Mochiko's inference that Kurumi won with borrowed money (FXK-V03-C016). In V04 a supplicant, then an accuser who throws Mochiko's inference at her (i0069–i0072), in ignorance of how Kurumi's mother died. In V05 she hides the loan from Kurumi (i0143). From V07 the resentment is gone: polite warmth at Christmas, worry and attempted contact in January (V08 i0107), a support message she says she will send at once (V08 i0110, re-opened; the sending is not shown), relief at Kurumi's reply and a fierce, mistaken defence of her against an older man who turns out to be Kurumi's father — "私たちの先輩を傷つけるなら 絶対許さないんですけど!" (V09 i0095, re-opened). She is the one who asks Kurumi whether she will trade again (V09 i0119) and beams at the answer (i0120–i0121).

**Kurumi → Mebuki (as it bears on her).** In V02 Kurumi gives advice under a disclaimer ("自己責任原則", i0066), a hedged call that triggers Mebuki's debut, a soft unheard attempt to stop (i0115), and then, by 15 July, uses Mebuki's views as a contrary indicator without telling her (i0141; V02 reading, INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH) — with no harm to Mebuki shown, since her balance rose. In V04 she refuses the 1,000,000 on protective grounds and doubts having taught her (i0067–i0075). At Christmas she admires her plan: "芽吹ちゃんはすごいなぁ… ちゃんと先のことまで考えてて" (V07 i0009, re-opened).

**What the tie shows.** Mebuki's attitude to Kurumi tracks her own fortunes and her source of authority: dependence while Kurumi's calls pay, resentment once Mochiko becomes her authority and Kurumi's success sets off her own losses ("負けてるの私だけか…!?", V03 i0088), goodwill once she has stopped trading (INFERENCE MODERATE for the link to her fortunes). Neither ever learns the other's full history on the page: Mebuki is not shown hearing about Kurumi's mother; Kurumi is not shown learning the loan's terms, the rate or the family security (Study B, section 4). No money passes between them; the emotional cost of the V04 accusation falls on Kurumi.

### 8.3 Yasuko (高根やす子)

**Mebuki → Yasuko.** Affection and recruitment in V02, with a tactless "儲かってないのやす子ちゃんだけ" (i0154). In V03 a protective lie so she will not worry ("10万円くらい", i0049–i0050), then, when Yasuko is the only one to tell her to cut, contempt: "私よりあとから始めた FX初心者のくせに!", "一回も稼いだことないくせに!" (V03 i0142, re-opened) — followed by thanks and an apology as she leaves (i0143). In V04 avoidance, then regret, tears, "ありがとう…" and most of her story: her money history as drawn on a chart of her losses, that the short is still open, that Mochiko is lending her 2,000,000 ("萌智子先輩から200万円借りることにした…", i0139) and that she has an interview that night (i0129–i0139). She keeps back the rate, the income condition, her parents' address and telephone held as security, the IOU and where she will work ("はずかしいから内緒…", i0137; FXK-V04-C024). From V07 the tie is easy again — Kansai banter at the Christmas table ("やす子ちゃん それは褒めすぎやで", V07 i0007, re-opened), treats together in V09 (i0088), the invitation "やす子ちゃんも 一緒にランドしない?" (V09 i0117, re-opened).

**Yasuko → Mebuki.** The series' most consistent care toward her: warnings in V02, the call after the wipe-out (V03 i0048), the cut-it-now warning from her own experience (V03 i0139–i0142), the night search and "友達がアホしてるのに 止めに来ないやつがいるかよ!" (V04 i0132), and advocacy to Mochiko on her behalf (V05 i0134–i0139). She fails once, by her own account: told of the loan's lender and sum but not its terms or security, she says nothing in the moment (V04 i0140–i0142) and reproaches herself afterwards, "…なにも言えなかった…" (i0143); her silence was formed on that partial information. She refuses the rand flatly: "絶対やらねー!" (V09 i0117).

**What the tie shows.** The status order Mebuki built in V03 — Mochiko the pro, Yasuko the beginner — decided whose warning counted, and it was the wrong order. Her readiness to apologize, thank and confide when Yasuko comes for her at night (V04) is the first point at which she chooses disclosure over concealment — a partial disclosure that still withholds the terms, the security and the job (shame is her stated reason only for the job, "はずかしいから内緒…"; that she held back the rest so as not to alarm Yasuko is INFERENCE MODERATE).

### 8.4 Her family

**Mebuki → her parents and brother.** Concealment throughout the trading: "普通やし" to her mother (V03 i0064); tuition used against her mother's refusal; lies about school on 5 December (V06 i0005). Her family is also her deepest stake: their praise, their finances (her brother's schooling, the house loan, V03 i0074), and the fear of being a burden (V04 i0128). Her vision at the moment of borrowing is of her family cheering her university admission, "芽吹えらい" (V04 i0107, re-opened), and her inner voice speaks to them in Kansai: "あのな… ちゃうねん…". The first turn toward them is the late call of 5 December asking for her father, the lenient parent of the V03 dinner (V06 i0044, re-opened).

**Her parents → Mebuki.** Her mother is strict about tuition and generous with food and money — a 10,000円 note captioned "困ったら使うこと!!" (V03 i0096), "ちゃんとご飯食べて頑張るんやでー!" (V03 i0064) — and believes she is saving (V03 i0159). Her father is lenient ("学費くらい出したったらえぇがな…", V03 i0029). By her report they opposed her leaving university (V07 i0008).

**Where the cost lands.** Study B records her parents as paying in money they were told was being saved and in trust, without their knowledge on any page (the entry on the tuition and her parents' trust in its section 3.1), and treats her case as part of its thesis B1: in each family-money episode the first cost falls on someone who did not know, and the one who conceals calls the concealment protection. Her own words at the ticket machine (V04 i0128) show that she knew this while she did it.

### 8.5 Work, customers and strangers

At the salon she is polite, procedural and honest in a way she was not with her family: she tells a lecturing customer that her market view is only a hunch and apologizes in advance for any loss; he answers "大丈夫 責任は僕にあるから" and profits on it (V06 i0035–i0042; FXK-V06-C008). Study B counts this exchange as the cleanest allocation of responsibility in the series — a paid stranger who states her uncertainty and a customer who owns his choice. Her irritation at his profit dance (V06 i0040, re-opened) and the manga premise she proposes in the group chat — "接客のお仕事のお話見たい!", "延々とキモイ客を相手にするやつ", which Yasuko answers with "なんか芽吹闇深くなってないか?" (V06 i0099; FXK-V06-C018) — suggest that politeness at work does not mean she likes everyone she serves (INFERENCE MODERATE: the premise is a story idea, plausibly drawn from her work, not a statement about her customers).

## 9. Preferences and ordinary life

**Stable preferences (seen across volumes).** *Food*: parcels from home greeted with "実家から食料キタ~", "ほんま助かるわ~", "カレー多めや…" (V04 i0009); retort curry (V03 i0077); a snack hoard she calls "非常食" (V03 i0083); grilled meat and a draft beer at Christmas (V07 i0006); "私は遠慮なく食べるけどね〜" at a restaurant (V09 i0088); a takoyaki party with the circle in Yasuko's flat (V09 i0108–i0117). *Games*: arcade dance games with Yasuko (V02 i0075–i0079), a gamer's frame of reference (V02 i0062), gacha as her scale of value (V05 i0060), a headset at home (V02 i0114; V03). As the contest's originator she rules gleefully for Mochiko's loophole at Christmas — "ルールの抜け穴を突いた人が勝ちなんですよ!", "漫画で見ました!" (V07 i0025): a pleasure in loopholes that she credits to manga. *Cute animal things*: rabbit posters and figures, a teddy bear, phone cases with round, rabbit or bear ears (V02 i0073; V03 i0158; V04 i0060; V06 i0003–i0006, i0043–i0044). *Clothes*: a beret, a ribboned or white blouse, a short black skirt and thigh-highs are her outdoor look (V04 i0031–i0032; V06 i0006; V07 i0005); yellow clogs at home (V06 i0003).

**Habits.** Wakes at 14:00 when working nights (V06 i0003); keeps her wages in cash in an envelope (V05 i0102, re-opened); plugs in her laptop the wrong way (V02 i0061); reads her horoscope (うお座, V03 i0075); made three credit cards "ポイント目当て" and never used them until her crisis (V04 i0016); usually has her bangs cut at a salon for 500円 and, when she cannot afford it, cuts them herself (V03 i0105–i0106); skipped weeks of classes while trading, then went back to lectures (V03 i0103, i0120, i0157–i0158).

**Situational choices, not preferences.** Living on one meal a day, a bank balance of 871円 and a wallet of coupons (V03 i0076; V04 i0014) are poverty, not taste. Her dislike of work in V02 ("働きたくないから", i0056, re-opened) is a stated attitude that the page later reverses (section 12). The salon was chosen under pressure, as the limit of what she would accept (V04 i0116), and only afterwards became work she likes (V07 i0009).

**Aversions.** Being thought a fool ("アホ"); being the only one losing; her family's knowing. Night work, which she refuses even under her lender's pressure (V04 i0103, i0116). Nothing on the page shows her disliking a food or a pastime.

## 11. Capabilities and limitations

**Capabilities.** Calm arithmetic: she computes margins and death lines correctly (V03 i0072–i0073, i0078, i0110; V04 i0021). Quick uptake of jargon and of the circle's idiom. Work skill she values and practises procedurally (checklists, timing, an option sold, repeat-customer lore; V06 i0016–i0017, i0042; V08 i0097–i0109). Reading people, from V08: she sees through Mochiko's pretext and reasoning (V08 i0103, i0108). Social energy: she convenes, recruits and confronts (V02 i0157–i0160; V09 i0095). Persistence in repayment (V09 i0114).

**Limitations.** Thin knowledge of what she trades and why (V02 i0070; V03 i0041; V09 i0116–i0117); impulse ("考えてるうちに下がったら嫌", V02 i0069); dependence on a licensing authority; an exit rule tied to break-even. Her FX practice never includes stops: no stop order is shown in any of her positions; the two account-ending closes whose mechanism is shown were made by the broker (V04 i0012; V06 i0039), and the mechanism of the 13 August zero is unshown (V03-U01).

**Capacity against willingness.** The gap is the centre of her portrait. She has the capacity to see the danger and say so, and the willingness to act on that sight fails while she has money to defend with and a voice to license holding. She can cut when a gain pays for it (V05 i0065–i0066) and cannot realize a loss. She has the capacity to tell the truth and chooses much of it when someone comes for her (Yasuko, V04, still without the loan's terms or security) or when she owes it (the wipe-out to her creditor, V06), and withholds it from those whose knowledge would hurt most (her parents).

**What did not change, or came back.** In autumn 2015 she is trading again, in a new domestic account, before her loan is repaid, on a rationale she half understands, with an unspecified plan for a fall, and inviting Yasuko to join her (V09 i0114–i0117, re-opened). The page leaves her here: neither a closed cautionary tale nor a cured one.

**What was revealed late, without being change.** The April 2014 origin and her first motive (V05 i0051); the "キラキラ", "みんなで楽しく" motive she voices only in V04 (i0135), which re-reads her V02 recruiting and contest; Mochiko's private contempt at their first meeting (V05 i0051–i0052); her parents' opposition to her leaving university, reported in V07 (i0008); her own statement that she was force-liquidated to 0円 "何度か" (V08 i0105), consistent with the closes already shown.

**Why the change happened, and the rival.** The page's own account is that the broker's close lifted a weight (V06 i0040) and that work turned out to be good (V06 i0042). Study C reads her change from holding to letting go as "done for her" by the broker, not chosen. Study B notes that she bore her debt alone but reported the wipe-out to her creditor without delay (the first Monday morning after the close, V06 i0051) and told her friends part of it (V07 i0007–i0009), and came through; it reads her case as separating solitude from concealment, a refinement of its thesis B3 (responsibility borne alone and hidden disables help; shared, it becomes payable). The rival reading is that she was released rather than changed, and the V09 re-entry supports it in part; the change in her stance toward work and in her two main ties, which the V09 scenes keep (i0088, i0095, i0114), supports real development. Both are kept: her circumstances changed first, and some of her changed with them.

## 14. Interpretive uncertainty

**Rival readings of her agency.** (1) *Self-authored*: every order and refusal was hers, she knew the risks, and her own V07 verdict says so; the lie may have changed nothing, since before asking she had restated "私はまだ下がると思います" and her exit thought was unfinished (the V03 reading's counterreading 9). (2) *Relational*: the trusted authority she asked lied in answer to a direct question while holding the opposite position, then lent her the means to stay in at 109.5% and answered her voiced exits with more money (V03 i0147–i0150; V04 i0103–i0119). (3) *Combined*: the page shows both and declines to weigh them (Study B, section 9). The four accepted claims on the question, OPEN at this portrait's snapshot, are now written as FXK-SYN-C008, C009, C011 and C012, which keep this combined reading; what would most change the portrait is a page showing what she would have done on 15 October had she known the truth, and none exists (V03-U05).

**Other counterreadings recorded in the readings.** A deliberate last-chance gambler in V04 who went in informed ("最後の勝負", V04 i0139) — against it, her own "これ借りてもきっとうまくいかへん…" (V04 i0107) and "なんで私… こんなことしてんやろ…" (i0120) (the V04 reading's counterreading L.3). Reasonable mean-reversion trading that met bad luck in V05 — against it, the narrator's verdict (i0090), risk added on borrowed margin into a rising market, and a cut made conditional on a favourable move (the V05 reading's counterreading L.3). The market as her enemy in V03 — against it, 246× with no stop into an event she did not know of, and tuition as margin (the V03 reading's counterreading 3).

**Development or release?** See section 12. The evidence supports both a real change in her relation to work and to her two main ties and a persistence of appetite; it does not settle whether her trading process changed.

**Person or device?** She can be read as Kurumi's cautionary double, run ahead of her: the same sequence (a stopless leveraged position, protected money relabelled, a broker's close) arrives in Mebuki first. Study C's differentiation of her driver from Kurumi's (belonging and borrowed authority, not restitution or appetite), her counterexamples (the seat, the limit, the customer's money) and her initiative in V08–V09 argue that she is drawn as a person as well as a parallel.

**Known gaps (UNRESOLVED or never printed).** The mechanism of her 13 August zero (V03-U01). What she told her parents on 5 December and what they know (V06-U03). The identity of the voice that calls "芽・吹・" (V06 i0055). The total she repaid and the interest she paid (reconciliation audit, section 4.11). Her card terms and student-loan rate (V04-U08, U12). The rand position's size, direction and outcome (V09-U06). Whether she returned to university, and where she works and lives in 2017. Why Kansai is absent from her V08 lettering. The nature of her salon treatments (V05-U19).

**Limits of extrapolation.** Her V09 position and any later trading face events after V10 and are not predicted here. The danger to her person that some pages make salient (V04 i0122; V05 i0138; V06 i0025) is reader dread on the accepted readings, with no stated intention, plan or act; it is not to be built into a claim about her.
