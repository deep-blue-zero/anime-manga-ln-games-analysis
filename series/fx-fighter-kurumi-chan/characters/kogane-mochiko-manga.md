## 1. Orientation

**Who she is.** Mochiko is the friend Kurumi made in her first year at 新都大学 (April 2012), an economics student on a tuition-exempt scholarship who has traded since her teens and who, alone among the circle, trades with discipline. She is the one person tied to every other principal — Kurumi's confidante and watcher, Mebuki's idol, adviser, concealed counterparty and creditor, Yasuko's sparring partner and client — and the one who holds everyone's figures. By April 2017 she is the 代表取締役社長 of a small company that employs Kurumi.

**Her place in the story.** She appears in every volume. V01–V04 show her mostly from two sides at once: a soft public voice and a coarse private one. V05 (第21話, about seventy pages) gives her childhood and is the first biography in the series. V06–V07 make her the silent witness of Kurumi's road to ruin; V08 makes her the person who comes; V09 makes her Kurumi's employer; V10 shows her in two short office scenes. Both specialists of the synthesis treat her tie with Kurumi as the spine of the series (qualification record, notes). The story it turns on, in one sentence: Kurumi, the protagonist, trades FX to win back the 2000万円 her mother lost (V07 i0051); her 300-lot EUR/CHF long without a stop, announced to the friends at Christmas 2014 (V06 i0148–i0153), is caught by the franc shock of 15 January 2015 and leaves her owing 101,534,644円 (V07 i0118, i0130); V08 depicts her suicidal crisis, and V09 her bankruptcy and a new start as Mochiko's employee.

**Boundary and spoilers.** The portrait covers the V01–V10 main narrative up to V10 i0156 (Monday 24 April 2017) and discloses everything in that span, including her V05 history, her V07 inner account of how the friendship began, the V08 intervention and the V09 employment. Nothing after V10 is known or assumed. Paratext (jackets, under-jacket boards, afterwords, bonus strips) is never used as evidence about her; an under-jacket label for the man in her flashback is excluded on that ground.

This monograph calls her **Mochiko**. Others are named as the pages name them: Kurumi (福賀くるみ); Mebuki (山師芽吹); Yasuko (高根やす子), who is only the blogger **あふぃちゃん** until V02 i0074 prints her name — Mochiko's comment of V02 i0011 is addressed to the blogger, and no page shows Mochiko linking the two; Kurumi's mother, who is "Kurumi's mother" for V01–V05 events and Kozue (梢) from V06 i0065; Kurumi's father ("the father"; his given name 郁夫 only at INFERENCE HIGH); and Nana, Kizuna and Inago, her 2017 staff. Mochiko's own mother, her paternal grandmother and the bespectacled man of her flashback are never named on the page and stay unnamed here.

**Age and year.** 20 at V01 i0058 (May 2014); 12 in spring 2006 (V05 i0005); 23 in April 2017 (V09 i0143). Kurumi, Mochiko and Mebuki are all third-years in 2014 (V05 i0041–i0044; reconciliation usage rule), so Mebuki's 先輩 marks investment seniority, not age.

**In brief.** Between 2006, when at twelve she left school and her mother's home, and 2010, when she began to trade, Mochiko learned to answer people with a pleasant formula and to think something cruder underneath, to count on no one's charity, and to treat the market as the one fair contest. With her own money she is careful: she cuts losses by rule and keeps her size modest. With other people's money and weakness she was, in V03–V05, cold and calculating: she lied to an admirer about her position, used that admirer's panic as a market signal, and lent her money at 109.5% a year with her family as security. She chose Kurumi in 2012 as a "sample" whose failure she would watch at close range, and in V06–V07 she saw Kurumi's ruin coming more exactly than anyone and said nothing. In V08 she came, by force and with care; in V09 she gave Kurumi a job on condition that she tell her father. Her stated doctrine never softens; her conduct does. Why she changed is the largest open question about her (section 13).

## 2. Circumstances and personal history

### 2.1 What the present-time pages show (May 2014 – April 2017)

- **Student and trader, 2014.** She skips class to watch the market ("いや 相場見てた", V01 i0058) and lives alone in a high flat with a four-monitor trading desk, a wall of books and a white cat (V01 i0116–i0118; V02 i0005–i0006). Kurumi believes her assets are "少なくとも 1000万円以上" (V01 i0066; Kurumi's narration, belief only). Her own figures are first printed on 15 July 2014: a domestic account of 12,014,110円 (BOX, V02 i0158). On 19 September the box prints 13,302,630円, a gain of 10.7% while she tells the group she is down 10% (V03 i0090). She closes all her longs on Monday 8 December 2014 for +7,691,000円, leaving 20,993,630円 (BOX, V06 i0052, re-opened) — a 74.7% gain in the friends' contest, which she reports truthfully (V07 i0014). (The contest: begun among the four friends on 15 July 2014 (V02 i0157–i0159) and given rules on Kurumi's hand-drawn sheet at their 19 September meeting — each ranked by the percentage growth of her FX account to 25 December, the winner to treat the others to a meal (V03 i0086–i0088). At the Christmas results dinner Kurumi added a rule against false reports, 「嘘の申告は禁止!!!」, written "萌智子用に" — "中間発表のとき嘘ついてたでしょ?" (V06 i0143), the interim report being the false −10% of 19 September; Mochiko accepted it, "…了解 嘘はつかないよ" (V06 i0144).) The reconciliation audit holds the whole chain (its section 4.4).
- **Creditor, November 2014 onward.** She lends Mebuki 2,000,000円 at 109.5% a year against an IOU with a thumbprint (V04 i0086–i0120, i0145); in autumn 2015 she tells Mebuki the repayment is on course to finish "来月" (V09 i0114). The total repaid and any interest are never printed.
- **Founder, by January 2015 (founding date unprinted).** By 7 January 2015 she has set up a company ("法人", "見切り発車", V07 i0041) and made a first game, 「CATFORCE ~Money or Hell~」, with art commissioned from Yasuko (V07 i0038–i0041). She is still a student then: she sits an econometrics final (V07 i0133), and Kurumi later thanks her for coming during her exam period (V09 i0051).
- **Employer, 2015–2017.** In autumn 2015 she says one person can no longer manage the workload and that she cannot wait for Kurumi's graduation (V09 i0110). By April 2017 she runs (株)もちもち企画 (phone card and mailbox, V09 i0136, i0140) as 代表取締役社長, with Kurumi as clerk, Nana and Inago as creators and Kizuna in sales; she hires in person and leaves on business trips (V09 i0143–i0151; V10 i0063). Asked about the firm's size she says "いや 零細だけど?" (V10 i0063, re-opened).
- **Never printed.** Where her starting capital came from; what her company does; its capital; her stop levels; whether she graduated. These are unknown, and nothing in this monograph supplies them.

**What she thought of Kurumi in 2012.** In her own thought, on the day they talk at the student café: "この子はいいサンプルになりそうだ", "泳がせて観察してみるのも悪くないな…", and "その挑戦がどう成功するかよりも / どう失敗するのか真横で見るのが望ましい / それが私が勝つための一番の糧になるはずだ…" (V05 i0048, re-opened; SELF_REPORT HIGH as her thought). **How the history bears on the adult.** The V05 reading records three verbal bridges between the childhood and 2014 (FXK-V05-E061; OBSERVATION HIGH for the words, INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH that they are designed echoes, her own awareness UNRESOLVED): the man's "他人の施し" returns as her charge against Mebuki ("これ以上 他人からの 施しを与えて どうするんだ?", V05 i0053); his "友達" lines return when Yasuko asks whether she ever had friends (V05 i0136) and Mochiko answers, after draining a beer, "いらないんだよ… / 友達とかなれあいとか / そういうの…!" The public formula of 2014 has its origin on the EAON page of February 2010, with a coarse private word under it ("ばーか"); the coarse inner voice itself is already present at twelve.

This is **context, not cause** (V05 reading, counterreading L.1: "biography explains, it does not excuse"). No page links a childhood event to a later act as its reason, and the friends know only fragments: Yasuko learns a little at the sushi bar and says she is glad to know her better, to which Mochiko thinks "全然よくないわ" (V05 i0139, re-opened); on 7 January 2015 Kurumi says she knows little about her (Mochiko thinks "言ってないからね") and asks about high school; Mochiko says "…言いたくない" and meets the next question with silence (V07 i0045, re-opened), then, as Kurumi keeps asking, tells her aloud — "ほんとうは 私 / 高校なんて 行ってない から", "高認取って 大学に入った / ただの 中卒ってこと" — closes the subject ("はい 満足した? / この話は おしまい") and apologises, "嘘ついてて 悪かったね" (V07 i0046, re-opened). The lie, by INFERENCE HIGH from Kurumi's "株やってたって聞いたけど" (V07 i0045), is her April 2012 remark that she had traded stocks "高校のときから" (V05 i0047, re-opened). Kurumi answers with admiration, "すごい!" (V07 i0047, re-opened). She gives Kurumi the fact, not the history behind it. Earlier in V07 she says she will "probably never" go home ("たぶん一生帰らないよ", V07 i0028, then retracted). In V07 she pictures herself and Kurumi as two daughters "母親に 呪われているんだ——" (V07 i0053, re-opened); the frilled woman clinging to her in that image is by INFERENCE MODERATE her mother. Neither V09 nor V10 mentions her family. In V10 Kurumi remembers her building her assets around the end of 2012 and early 2013, with a white cat beside her (V10 i0040).

## 3. Personality and psychology

Mochiko is one of the best-evidenced minds in the series because the page gives her a private voice from her first chapter: thought balloons, burst balloons and narration boxes in her own words. Three layers can be kept apart: a creed she states, conduct that sometimes departs from it, and a few moments when she questions both.

### 3.1 The creed (SELF_REPORT, consistent from V02 to V09)

- **Only results count.** "結果を出した奴だけがすごい" (V02 i0151). Traders are "敵同士", and friendship between them is a fair exchange of use ("おあいこ", a "健全な関係", V02 i0142–i0144). When Kurumi asks whether Mochiko wants her back in the market so as to use her, the answer is "…半分正解", and when Kurumi says she will use Mochiko in return, "いいよ… / やってみな…?" (V02 i0145, re-opened).
- **Each answers for her own losses.** 自己責任 is her word for the friends' rule: "知ったことではない" (V04 i0052); "全部自分の責任だろうが!" in thought (V05 i0132); "全部 くるみが決めたことだろう?" (V06 i0154, re-opened).
- **The market is the one fair contest.** Already her view in the EAON period, 2010 (V05 i0033), and recalled in V07 as "所詮この世は資本主義で経済至上主義…" with the aim "勝ち組になる…" (V07 i0138).
- **Trust is a weakness; concealment and dominance are the rules of contact** (V05 i0045).
- **Charity is neither to be expected nor given without terms.** The man's "他人の施し" (V05 i0027) becomes her charge against Mebuki (V05 i0053) and, in V08, the name of her own help, "打算付き" (V08 i0114).
- **Others cannot be helped.** "他人になにかできるわけないのに" (V07 i0135).

### 3.2 What she wants, and what she cannot say she wants

She wants to win and to owe no one. Her goal beyond winning is never stated before V07, when, in the week of Kurumi's ruin, she objects that earning money is for doing something ("稼いだお金でなにかしたいのが普通じゃないのか!?", V07 i0137) and then asks herself "私はいったい / なにがしたかったんだろう…?" (V07 i0139, re-opened). The page stages the question with the adult facing a girl in school uniform — by INFERENCE MODERATE her younger self. Her company and first game already exist by then (V07 i0038–i0041), so the question is about what the winning was for, not about a lack of projects. In V09 she names two wants of her own toward Kurumi: someone to talk markets with ("…話相手が欲しいという意味では / そう…かも?", V09 i0022, re-opened) and Kurumi's real "知識と経験" (V09 i0034, re-opened).

Under the competent surface she doubts her own skill: alone in April 2014, at twenty, she thinks the capital she has built may be luck of timing ("たまたまタイミングが合っただけ", V05 i0050), and asked by Mebuki how she came to win she says "さぁ…? 運じゃない?" (V02 i0011).

### 3.3 Vulnerabilities and coping

- **Being known.** She resents insight into herself more than any insult: when Yasuko says she is glad to know her a little better, Mochiko thinks "全然よくないわ" (V05 i0139, re-opened), and after Yasuko passes her secret to Kurumi, "やす子の野郎…" (V05 i0145). Asked about her school years she refuses first ("…言いたくない", then silence, V07 i0045); pressed by Kurumi, she gives the bare fact aloud, apologises for her old lie and ends the subject ("この話は おしまい", V07 i0046). What she resists is being read; a fact told briefly and on her own terms she can give.
- **Closeness and praise.** In V07 she turns aside praise or closeness at least eight times while accepting the closeness in action ("褒めてんのか…?", "大袈裟", "いや… 普通でしょ", "絶対いや!!"; FXK-V07-C074); in V08 she blushes and stammers "べ… 別に… / お礼を言われることなんて してないし" (V08 i0151).
- **Coping strategies** (OBSERVATION for the acts, INFERENCE MODERATE for their function): the public formula that ends a conversation ("うん そうだね"); sitting apart (V06 i0142, i0154); the cat as the one listener who hears her candidly ("ねぇ聞いた?", "面倒だよね… 人間関係って…", V04 i0150); converting a moral question into a question of price (元金回収, 投資, 打算); and doctrine — 自己責任 — as a reason not to act when acting would cost her something (V06–V07).

### 3.4 Contradictions

- **V01–V05: her private conduct is harsher than her public speech.** She says "いいと思うよ! 多分上がる!" and thinks "買い煽ったの私だけど" (V01 i0069–i0070); she says "うん とてもそう思う" and thinks "バッカじゃねーの!?" (V03 i0126, re-opened).
- **V07–V09: her stated beliefs are harsher than her conduct.** "いらないんだよ… 友達とかなれあいとか" (V05 i0137) against "私の友達なんだから" (V08 i0142, re-opened); "他人になにかできるわけないのに" (V07 i0135) against the night of V08; "私の施しは 打算付きだ" against a bath run, laundry done and a night spent awake beside Kurumi (V08 i0144–i0157). Study B describes this as an inversion of the earlier relation (Study B §6.6).
- **Contempt with respect inside it.** On the page where she privately mocks Mebuki's reasoning she also defends Kurumi against Mebuki's "運だけ": "さすがにくるみを / ナメすぎだ…" (V03 i0126, re-opened). Her private voice was never uniformly contemptuous of Kurumi.

### 3.5 Self-conception

She describes herself as unsentimental and self-interested: "私… / こういう性格だから…" (V07 i0017, re-opened); "私がそんな お人好しに 見える?", "私自身の ためだよ…", "ただの 気まぐれだよ" (V08 i0095, i0096, i0099). The page repeatedly shows her acting against this description from V07 on, and never shows her revising it in words.

### 3.6 Enjoyment of misfortune, arrogance, cruelty: what the pages support

The project's owner asked that these areas be tested, with disconfirming evidence weighted equally, and the readings test them volume by volume (for example the V02 reading §E, Movement I, on the blogger as "owner objective C (enjoyment of others' losses)"; the V06 reading §H.3, "owner hypothesis C tested"; and each reading's §H entries "care vs callousness" and "counterexamples"); Study B §6.6 draws the cross-volume result. The result is time-dependent.

- **Enjoyment of others' financial misfortune** is evidenced as a *private attitude* in V01–V03 and nowhere voiced to its object. Its objects are an anticipated ruin (Kurumi: the inner verdict "強欲の雌豚——!" with its LOSER/WINNER tableau and "このタイプに 中途半端な 終わりはない…", V01 i0156, re-opened; and, when Kurumi closes in profit, "面白くない… / こんなんで 利益出してん じゃねーよ… / そこは 死んどけや——", V01 i0159, re-opened), a stranger's losses (the blogger's, "下手糞すぎて面白い", V02 i0010) and a friend's plight while it is still recoverable (Mebuki as "焼豚", V03 i0099; "面白い——", V03 i0151). It thins in V04 (an eerie smile as Mebuki gives in, V04 i0109; a smile as she corrects "芽吹ちゃん" to Mebuki's salon name "若葉ちゃん", V04 i0145–i0146) and is absent from V05 on, including when ruin is real and a friend's: Mebuki's liquidation draws the flat trader's idiom "そっか芽吹ちゃん死んじゃったか" (V06 i0051), and Kurumi's ruin draws disgust at a forum gloating over the ruined, with the admission "私もどちらかといえば蹴りたい側" (V07 i0134–i0135). A weak exception: in autumn 2015 she reads the gazette's list of recent bankrupts as entertainment and gives Kurumi the page as a joke they share (V09 i0104–i0105). Claim state: FXK-V03-C008, revised to "absent" and preserved through FXK-V08-C063.

## 4. Cognition and decision-making

### 4.1 What she notices

She is the series' most accurate reader of risk, of charts and of people, and the page tests the accuracy.

- **Kurumi's type.** After "3カ月くるみを観察してきて分かった" (V01 i0155) she concludes that Kurumi is the kind of trader for whom there is no middle outcome — total ruin or a fortune (V01 i0156, re-opened). As a risk reading this is borne out across ten volumes; as an outcome forecast it was wrong twice the same morning (V01 i0154, i0157).
- **Markets.** A structural read of the October 2014 chart — panic selling, weak longs flushed, "上値が軽い", "どちらかといえば 買い…だな…" (V03 i0146); trend and floor reads in V07 (i0055–i0057); at Christmas 2014 a chart of Kurumi's position that she sees as a monster, "こんなチャート / 存在しては / いけない…!" (V06 i0151, re-opened).
- **Exposure.** After the franc shock she works out Kurumi's loss at her own desk and the counterfactual on a stop, "た・ぶ・ん助かってる" (V07 i0115–i0118); her estimate "1億2千万" is her labelled estimate, not the debt, which is 101,534,644円 (V07 i0118, i0130).
- **People's weak points.** Mebuki's tuition and the second wipe-out at stake (V03 i0099); her fear of her family learning (V04 i0118); her ignorance of how domestic accounts work (V08 i0106); what Kurumi was carrying (V08 i0117) and her state ("思ったより 重症じゃん", i0118).

### 4.3 Reasoning with other people's weakness (V03–V05)

- **The lie and the canary (V03).** Mebuki, frightened for her tuition, says she might close her short if Mochiko agrees, and asks her directly what she holds (V03 i0147). Mochiko answers "下がるね…! / 私は芽吹ちゃんに 乗った…!" and "私・も・売・り・を / 入・れ・た・よ…!" while the box prints "50枚買い" (V03 i0148, re-opened), and then, in her own interior: "芽吹ちゃんを / カナリアとして / 連れていく——", Mebuki will keep her short as the dollar rises, "いつか芽吹ちゃんの 心が折れて / その鳴き声が 止むまでは" (V03 i0150, re-opened). Mebuki drops her conditional exit (whether she would have closed is not shown). The V03 reading grades this as manipulation for informational gain with private enjoyment (EVALUATION HIGH); intentional cruelty is not established.
- **The loan (V04).** Her own words chart the route: "知ったことではない" (i0052); "こいつに してあげることなんか なにもない… / あるとすれば——" (i0084); "芽吹ちゃん困ってるんでしょ…?" (i0086); the shared-short lie affirmed ("……そうなんだよねぇ…", i0087); 109.5% a year offered in a soft request form (i0088). When Mebuki voices stopping, the sum doubles to 2,000,000 with "私だって 無理に貸そうとは してないし" (i0103–i0105), the waste of her suffering ("苦労が ぜーんぶ無駄", i0106) and her mother as levers; the parents' address and number are taken as security, and, smiling: "万が一 芽吹ちゃんが 怪我したり / 病気になって 返済不能に なったら 困るでしょ?" and "親を 悲しませちゃ / だめだよ?" (V04 i0118–i0119, re-opened). The V04 reading: manipulation, INFERENCE HIGH from acts and words; enjoyment INFERENCE LOW-MODERATE.
- **The hedge (V05).** To Yasuko she lays the logic out: if the dollar rises her long gains; if it falls she still collects interest; "ほぼ 負けることは なくなる", "いい リスクヘッジ でしょう?" (V05 i0130–i0131, re-opened). The reconciliation audit finds the hedge coherent in direction and overstated in size (its section 4.4). Asked to keep Mebuki from harm, she promises to — for a financial reason: "…もちろん ライン超え させないよう 注意する つもりだよ / 最悪なことに なったら さすがに元金回収 無理だし" (V05 i0139, re-opened).
- **Pattern** (INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH): faced with a person's weakness, she asks first what it means for her book. A friend's panic is a signal, a friend's need is a loan with a rate, a friend's possible death is a risk to the principal. In V06 she closes her own longs in the scene of Mebuki's "退場" message (V06 i0051–i0052); that the canary logic was enacted is INFERENCE MODERATE, and the rival — the dollar at a new high was reason enough — is kept (V06 reading, counterreading 3). The conflict of interest toward Mebuki is established and admitted (FXK-SYN-C005); none toward Kurumi is recorded.

### 4.4 Reasoning toward silence (V06–V07)

- **Christmas 2014.** She judges Kurumi's 300-lot EUR/CHF long without a stop baseless (V06 i0153), sees the chart as something that should not exist, asks one question aloud — "さすがに / 損切り注文くらい / してるよね?" - **January 2015.** Four interventions are begun and withheld (FXK-V07-C009): an invitation broken off at "私と一緒に…" (i0054); a warning withdrawn as "やっぱなんでもない…おやすみ" (i0065); the message 「くるみ、大丈夫?」 typed and not sent — "…こんなの送って / なんの意味が あるんだよ…", the phone slammed face down and her cat licking her hand (V07 i0099–i0100, re-opened); and Kurumi's "大丈夫" accepted against her own knowledge, "まぁ本人が言ってるし" (i0136). Respect for Kurumi's autonomy, self-protection and realism about what warnings achieve are all live readings; none is asserted (V06 reading counterreading 4; V07 reading counterreading 2). ### 4.5 Reasoning toward action (V08–V09)

- **The trigger.** A few days before Kurumi's crisis night, at the salon, Mebuki tells her that she and Yasuko tried and failed to reach Kurumi and asks "萌智子先輩は / 連絡して みました?"; Mochiko, caught, says "…いや" (V08 i0107, re-opened). Mebuki then teases her and pushes her to go (i0108–i0110).
- **The decision, in her own narration.** A fifth message withheld (i0111–i0112) on the ground that her worry changes nothing ("私が心配して 気を使ったところで… 現実はなにも 好転しない", i0112); then: "祈りを捧げる / 千羽鶴は / 必要ない / だけど / 投資することなら できる" (i0113, re-opened); "くるみ"; "私の施しは / 打算付きだ" (i0114, re-opened). The content of the "investment" is never stated (V08-U07).
- **At Kurumi's flat**, her reasoning is a creditor's and a pragmatist's. She will not pay the debt: "仮に私が 10億円持ってても 絶対しないね / 立て替えた瞬間 自己破産されたら 泣き寝入りだし" (V08 i0119, re-opened). Bankruptcy is the route, and telling the parents the first step (i0120). She refuses false empathy ("私はくるみじゃない", i0131). She separates wanting from obligation: when Kurumi answers that she *must* die, Mochiko says that is no answer and demands "どっちですか?と / 聞いてるの!!" until Kurumi says she does not want to (i0133–i0138). Counterfactuals are "不健全" (i0150); the franc move was an "事故" (i0153); privately she expects Kurumi to aim at a hundred million next and "結局 死にそう" (i0154).
- **In V09**, the same register: a debt is manageable, bankruptcy the plain route, a life worth more than a debt (i0017, i0020); "それで1億チャラに なるなら 安いでしょ" (i0040); being an adult means telling one's father (i0043); and the job offer is conditional on that call — "明日確認 するからね?", "もし連絡 してなかったら / 会社の話も なかったことに してもらうから / いや本当に!" (V09 i0050, re-opened).
- **Study C's consequence for her.** Study C's third claim is that across persons insight is usually accurate and usually inert at the moment it concerns, and that what governs is carried from outside the moment — an act that changes structure at once, another person, an institution. Mochiko's conduct toward Kurumi fits the shape though its object is a friend, not her own position: her insight was exact from V06 and inert until another person named her inaction; and the restraint she then imposed on Kurumi (the condition of V09 i0050) is itself an outside carrier. Study C lists her V08 question about wanting, rather than an argument about arithmetic, among the restraints another person supplied.
- **Pattern** (INFERENCE MODERATE): when she acts against her doctrine she names the act in the doctrine's own vocabulary — 投資, 打算, 施し, 元金. Rival: the vocabulary is literal, and the rescue was the acquisition of an employee (V09 reading, counterreading 2: "recruiter rather than rescuer"); against it, she made the job conditional on honesty with the father, privately doubted Kurumi's fitness ("爆弾", "ずっと子供のまま", V09 i0042–i0043, thought), and refused to pay the debt.

## 5. Behavioural patterns

### 5.1 Ordinary conduct

The page shows her in ordinary life as often as in crisis. She watches the market instead of attending class and whispers about it in lectures (V01 i0058, re-opened); drinks lounge coffee (V01 i0095); helps Mebuki open an account (V02 i0007); reads investor blogs for fun (V02 i0008); lectures with a raised finger (V04 i0099); goes suit shopping with the others (V04 i0035); browses bookstores (V03 i0155); cooks a lavish dinner for Kurumi (V07 i0058) and okayu for her after the crisis (V09 i0013–i0016); makes a game (V07); hires staff herself (V09 i0144). In the 2017 office she types furiously through a crunch and asks colleagues going out only to bring back receipts ("ごめん 無理! / 行くなら 領収書だけ お願い", V10 i0010), and leaves on a business trip with Kizuna, asking Kurumi to mind the office ("くるみ 留守番 お願いね", V10 i0063, re-opened).

### 5.2 Help, on her own terms

Her help is practical, exact, and either priced or denied. Train fare and a coat for Mebuki on the night of the loan (V04 i0118, i0120); steering Mebuki away from a robbery-prone convenience-store night shift (V04 i0100); a whole day of protection and real help for Yasuko at her event, including a polite but firm dismissal of the fan who outs Yasuko before the queue ("冷やかしなら 帰って いただけます?", V05 i0110) — when the same fan later approaches Mochiko herself ("あのー ツイッタとか…"), she is blunt ("してねぇよ!", "もう帰れ…", V05 i0118, re-opened); a full-price salon booking, taking only foot pressure points, to see Mebuki and "芽吹の売り上げに貢献" (V08 i0097–i0102); a night of care for Kurumi — bath, cold towel, laundry, sleeping in her bed, keeping watch (V08 i0144–i0157); okayu and coaching (V09); a job (V09). Each is given in act and disowned in word (section 3.5). The early kindnesses sit beside exploitation of the same person (V04), so care is real and early but not, before V08, governing.

### 5.3 Taking a step for someone else

When she decides, she takes the other person's agency into her own hands: she dials the salon on Mebuki's phone and drafts the IOU herself (V04 i0117); she waits at Kurumi's door, then uses insult, a grip on the collar and jaw and pinning to force a question (V08 i0125–i0136); she sets a deadline and says she will check (V09 i0050).

### 5.4 Conflict

In V01–V05 she avoids open conflict with the assent formula ("まぁ… ここは適当に 合わせるか…", V03 i0126). Open conflict comes only under drink and provocation (V05 i0137) or in anger ("笑ってんじゃねーよ ちっ", V07 i0135). In V08 force is deliberate and in service of a question; the V08 reading keeps both "coercion" and "care" as readings of it (counterreading L.2), and the page does not judge her means.

### 5.5 Lies, and their end

- In April 2012, on the day they meet, she tells Kurumi she has traded stocks "高校のときから" (V05 i0047, re-opened), though she never attended high school; the phrase could pass for "since high-school age", but she later treats it as a lie (by INFERENCE HIGH, section 2.2). She ends this lie herself, on 7 January 2015: "嘘ついてて 悪かったね" (V07 i0046, re-opened; section 2.2). It is the first lie she ends by her own word, and with an apology.
- Under the false name "ともこ" she posts on the blogger's page that she too lost heavily the day before ("私も昨日豪円で 大損しちゃいました"), urges her not to give up, and rates the post five stars, with the stated aim of keeping a stranger trading ("引退しないようにコメント残してやるか…", V02 i0010, thought; the comment form, V02 i0011, re-opened). The claimed loss is never verified and was probably invented (INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH, V02 reading, given her stated motive).
- She reports −10% when the box prints +10.7%, thinking "こんなの正直に言う必要ないし" (V03 i0090).
- She tells Mebuki she is short while long (V03 i0148), affirms it (V04 i0087) and repeats it to Kurumi (V05 i0144). It collapses through friends, not by confession: Yasuko passes the fact to Kurumi (V05 i0144, INFERENCE HIGH), Kurumi says cheerfully "嘘はよくないよ!", and Mebuki overhears Mochiko add "アホしかいないでしょ" (V05 i0144–i0146). Mochiko shrugs.
- **After V06 no deception is shown.** Her "ノーポジ" to the group is true, though silent about her profit and about Mebuki (V06 i0100); she promises "嘘はつかないよ" (V06 i0144) and reports her contest result exactly under the no-lies rule (V07 i0014; the contest and the rule are set out in section 2.1); she tells Mebuki to her face what she did (V07 i0017); she ends her oldest lie to Kurumi herself (V07 i0046); she confesses reading Kurumi's letter (V09 i0044, i0052).

### 5.6 Mistakes, corrections and what is never corrected

She misses her V01 entry (i0092), mis-forecasts (V01 i0154, i0157; V04 i0145), apologises to Kurumi for her old lie about high school, "嘘ついてて 悪かったね" (V07 i0046), admits she never tried to call Kurumi (V08 i0107), and apologises for reading the letter "つい出来心で" (V09 i0044, i0052). ### 5.7 Under stress

Sweat, shivering and a monster vision at a dangerous chart (V06 i0151–i0153); the phone slammed and her hair clutched (V07 i0100); days of agitation (V07 i0137); withdrawal to the edge of the table (V06 i0142, i0154). Her first response to pressure is silence; action, when it comes, is sudden and total.

### 5.8 Meaningful exceptions

In V01 she says her contrary wish aloud to Kurumi on the phone, the one moment that volume lets her private view be spoken (V01 i0117–i0118). In V03, at the contest's interim meeting of 19 September, minutes after her false −10% and as Kurumi's +73.5% is revealed, she says she would rather forfeit than play on — "こんなのくるみちゃんの優勝でいいでしょ とっとと棄権したいわ" (i0093); whether this is boredom, cover for her own gain or a wish not to compete in the open is not shown. In V07 she is disgusted by a forum gloating over the ruined, while admitting she is on the side that wants to kick (i0134).

### 6.4 Inner speech, outer speech, and their crossing

In V01–V05 the page pairs what she says with what she thinks, systematically: "いいと思うよ! 多分上がる!" / "買い煽ったの私だけど" (V01 i0069–i0070); "うん そうだといいね" / "知らねー" (V01 i0094); "…へぇ… お上手だね" / "下手クソかよ" (V01 i0095); "うん とても そう思う" / "バッカじゃねーの!?" (V03 i0126). V05 shows where the habit began (V05 i0031).

The split then crosses in three steps (Study B §6.4, verified here on the page). The private voice tells itself how the tie with Kurumi began, in a jagged burst, with "言ってないからね" in a thought cloud beside it (V07 i0045, re-opened); on the next page, pressed, she says aloud the plain fact about her schooling that she had hidden (V07 i0046, re-opened). The public voice turns plain and candid to Mebuki and drops the honorific (V07 i0017, re-opened). And in V08 the epithet of her V01 inner verdict is shouted to Kurumi's face in a jagged balloon — "くるみは / 強欲の雌豚(メスブタ)でしょうが!" (V08 i0126) — not as contempt but as an appeal against self-erasure: you are greedy, so you want to live (EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH; the V08 reading keeps the rival that it re-arms the appetite that ruined Kurumi, counterreading L.4). The coarse register that carried contempt behind a mask now carries care in the open. This is the strongest single sign that her development is drawn rather than asserted; it shows what her register does, not why it changed.

### 6.5 The medium's limits

This is written lettering, not a performed voice: tone is read from faces, balloon shapes and lettering, and attribution depends on balloon conventions — tailed balloons for speech, clouds and bursts for thought, narration boxes in her own voice, dashed balloons for whispers (V01 i0058). Several attributions are graded rather than certain: the i0159 lines of V01 are a mutter or a thought (a burst balloon without a tail, re-opened); "全部 くるみが 決めたこと だろう?" (V06 i0154) is inner voice at MODERATE-HIGH; the V07 i0045 account is unspoken at INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH; 「まあ い[っ?]か…」 (V07 i0066) is contested; "私面倒なのは嫌なんだけど" (V03 i0086) is hers at MODERATE; a 「は?」 in the 2017 office (V09 i0146) is not attributed to her (UNRESOLVED). ## 10. Capabilities and limitations

- **Trading.** The most disciplined process in the series: loss-cuts by rule since 2010, a stop, moderate leverage, profit taken (section 4.2). Limitation: forecasting — she misjudges outcomes as often as anyone and says so ("まったくわからない…!", V04 i0145).
- **Reading risk and people.** Precise and repeatedly borne out (section 4.1). Limitation: until V08 her reads change no one's decision but her own.
- **Self-education and making.** 高卒認定 by self-study, a scholarship to university, a shelf of textbooks; a company founded and a game made while still a student (V07 i0038–i0041); by 2017 an office with staff.
- **Practical money sense as the page presents it.** The logic of a creditor (V08 i0119), bankruptcy as a route (V08 i0120; V09 i0017–i0020, including a comic explainer of the alternatives), a suggestion of welfare to escape the tax that Kurumi refuses (V09 i0106). - **Social performance.** She can perform agreement, handle a fan, protect a friend, interview and hire. By her own account she made no friends at school and wants none ("いらないんだよ…", V05 i0137).
- **Physical.** Enough force to collar and pin Kurumi (V08 i0125–i0136); nothing else is tested.
- **Capacity against willingness.** Four cases show the difference. She could have warned Kurumi in V06–V07 — she had the read, the figure and the counterfactual on a stop — and did not until V08. She refuses to pay Kurumi's debt as a matter of principle, not means ("仮に私が 10億円持ってても", V08 i0119). She could have lent to Mebuki on easy terms and chose 109.5% with the family as security.
