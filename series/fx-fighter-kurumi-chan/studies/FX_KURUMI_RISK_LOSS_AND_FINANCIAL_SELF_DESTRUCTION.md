This study is the third specialist synthesis of FXK-SYN-20261123-V01-V10. It asks how the traders of FX戦士くるみちゃん interpret losses, risk, probability and their own competence, and which explanatory models fit which decision episodes when the same mechanism is compared across persons. The [architecture and role-gap audit](../audits/FX_KURUMI_ARCHITECTURE_ROLE_GAP_AUDIT.md) (§3.3) fixes the question, the scope and the acceptance criteria; the [source-completion and longitudinal reconciliation](../audits/FX_KURUMI_SOURCE_COMPLETION_AND_LONGITUDINAL_RECONCILIATION.md) supplies the reconciled figure chains (§4), the usage rules (§4.11, §9) and the consolidated claim wordings (§5.5) from which every cross-volume claim here starts; the [character qualification](../audits/FX_KURUMI_CHARACTER_QUALIFICATION.md) fixes names and knowledge boundaries (§1.4–1.5). The evidence is the ten accepted readings (`../readings/FX_KURUMI_Vnn_DEEP_READING.md`, sections D, E, F, G, L and M), the [decision and evidence ledger](../FX_KURUMI_DECISION_AND_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md) and fifteen pages re-opened for this study (section 12).

Three limits govern everything below. The psychological terms are explanatory models tested against the page; none is a diagnosis, and no clinical language is used. The suicidal crisis of V08–V09, and the death of Kurumi's mother (V01; named Kozue from V06 i0065; accepted date 13 February 2009), are treated analytically, without any method, method step or item. The run's forecast record is not evidence about the characters: no forecast row is cited for or against a person, and no exposure-sensitive forecast is cited as having predicted a market event.

The study argues one central finding, in four cross-person claims (section 4): **the series shows financial self-destruction as a convergent behaviour with divergent motives, whose decisive step is the sizing and funding of a position against a reference point — a target, a break-even, a sum owed to the dead — rather than the failure to cut alone; the traders' insight into their own errors is usually accurate and usually inert, and restraint holds only when something outside the trading moment carries it.** Its stated rival is institutional co-production (section 5). The agency strand of FXK-V02-C002 is argued in section 7.

## 1. Question, evidence and method

### 1.1 The question and the persons

The question (role-gap audit §3.3): how do the manga's traders interpret losses, risk, probability and their own competence, and which explanatory models — loss aversion, loss chasing, sunk cost, escalating commitment, motivated reasoning, perceived control, intermittent reinforcement, social comparison, rationalization, money entangled with identity — fit which episodes, with the same behaviour shown to arise from different motives where the page shows it?

The persons are those whose trading decisions the pages show: Kurumi (V01–V08 trading; V09–V10 abstaining), Mebuki (V02–V06; one position in V09), Kurumi's mother Kozue (her 2008 trading as V06 第28話 shows it), Yasuko (as the blogger あふぃちゃん in V01, identified as Yasuko from V02 i0074; V03–V04; the 2013 flashback of V10), Mochiko (stops, concealment, credit, the canary) and the V10 staff, Kizuna and Nana (Inago as a non-trading contrast). The father trades nothing; his acts enter only as constraints on others.

### 1.2 Evidence, labels and status words

Claims carry the run's labels (OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, EVALUATION, UNRESOLVED) and grades (HIGH / MODERATE / LOW). Every consequential claim cites an accepted ID (FXK-Vnn-Ennn observation, -Dnnn episode, -Cnnn claim) and/or a page locator (Vnn iNNNN). Numbers keep sign, unit and provenance (box, tile, screen, speech, document, narrator); arithmetic on printed operands is labelled ANALYST CALCULATION (CALC) and is taken from the reconciliation audit §4 unless stated.

Action status follows the readings: **intended** (a stated plan), **submitted** (an order or request entered on the page), **executed** (a fill or deposit confirmed by a box or screen), **realized** (P/L booked) — and, because it matters for this study, **realized by the broker** where a forced liquidation closes the position without its holder's order.

The reconciliation audit's usage rules are followed throughout: the debt owed after 15 January 2015 is 101,534,644 (V07 i0118, i0130), never "1億2千万"; 3,807,983 is Kurumi's 2014 tax computation (V08 i0034), never a 2017 balance; the V08 stake is "the 1,000,000, by INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH the V03 tax reserve"; Mochiko is "by her own account, flat from her December close (time unprinted)"; Kurumi's V06 cuts are hand cuts, and no stop order is shown for the V05 short; the V01 use of the father's cash is, in this study's own voice, an unconsented, concealed use of family savings with repayment intent (借金 appears only as a named speaker's word); the domestic cap "証拠金25倍まで" is cited from V01 i0075 and V03 i0007 and never asserted as Kurumi's broker's rule.

### 1.3 How the models are tested

Behaviour (what the page shows a person do or say) is kept apart from mechanism (why). A model is credited with an episode only where the page supplies behaviour the model predicts and the rival models predict less well; it is debited where the page shows the predicted behaviour absent under the model's own conditions. The page often states its own models — the narrator names prospect theory over Kurumi's refusal to cut (V01 i0147), a memory that keeps the reward and drops the fear (V02 i0107, i0133), heuristic first instincts and bias (V02 i0116–i0118), and "とどのつまりただのポジション" against Mebuki's labelled lots (V05 i0090). These are NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, a theory the work holds about itself, and are tested like any other model. Characters' own models (Mebuki's "負けてるときって頭が悪くなる気がするんです", V08 i0107; Yasuko's "信用すべきは…現実だ", V04 i0046) are SELF_REPORT.

Four failure kinds are kept apart, as every reading's section G kept them: poor forecasting, poor risk management, inability to realize a loss, and refusal to reconsider. An unfavourable market outcome is not treated as a failure of reasoning.

### 1.4 What this study does not do

It does not decide who pays for a choice (the question of [Study B](./FX_KURUMI_OBLIGATIONS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COST.md)) or how screens and institutional text distribute apparent responsibility (the question of [Study A](./FX_KURUMI_SCREENS_INSTITUTIONS_AND_RESPONSIBILITY.md)); it reads neither study. It does not score or resolve any forecast. It does not supply real market history, real outcomes of depicted events, or law beyond the page; drawn charts are illustrative on internal evidence (FXK-SYN-C003). Paratext is not evidence. Nothing after V10 is predicted: the questions whether Kurumi returns to the market (FXK-V09-C008) and whether she can "control" her desire (FXK-V09-C032) stay OPEN.

## 4. Four cross-person claims

The models of section 3 are tools; the claims below are what the comparison across persons shows. Each states its label and grade, the evidence that carries it, the strongest contrary evidence, its rivals, and what would overturn it. They start from the reconciliation audit's consolidated wordings (§5.5 rows 1–5, 11), not from the V01 wording of FXK-V01-C011.

### 4.1 SC-1 — One behaviour, several doors

**Claim.** The series repeats one visible sequence of financial self-destruction — a leveraged position held without a stop past a point the holder had named, funded from money labelled as protected, and ended by the broker — in four persons: Kurumi (to the brink in V01; to ruin in V07 and V08), Mebuki (V03–V06), Kozue (2008) and Yasuko (2013). It gives the sequence a different driver in each. OBSERVATION HIGH for each sequence (section 2.7); EVALUATION HIGH for the convergence; EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH for the differentiation of drivers.

**Evidence for the differentiation.** The page spends words on why each person entered and why each enlarged the stake, and the words differ.
- *Kurumi*: a sum fixed by her mother's loss and adopted as her own debt (V01 i0015–i0020; FXK-V01-E002), and a proof ("その証明が したい", i0040). In V05 the reference is explicitly the dead: "お母さんの負け額だけ稼いで満足? お母さんは死んでるのに?" (i0154, re-opened). In V09 she re-reads the whole as appetite: "根っからの欲張り" (i0031) and the mother's failure as a "大義名分" (i0032) — a SELF_REPORT that is a late re-reading, not a change of her earlier conduct (section 6.1).
- *Mebuki*: belonging and a borrowed authority — "キラキラ", "みんなで楽しく お金を儲けられたら" (V04 i0135), Mochiko as "プロ" (V03 i0025, i0153), the title "立派なFX戦士" conferred at the moment she borrows (V04 i0110) — with money also wanted ("お金持ち目指して", V04 i0121) and tuition as the stake that turns loss into panic ("来年の学費なんです", V03 i0099).
- *Kozue*: a family ideal and a belief presented as belief ("私たちは もっと幸せな家族に なれる", V06 i0069; "彼女は信じていた", NP i0067), a bargain read from a range her own journal contradicts (i0068, i0070), then a craving drawn as such ("買いたい…!" ×3, i0075). Her authority is mixed (journal SR; narrator; scenes beyond the journal), and the chapter is framed as Kurumi's reading (V06 reading §L item 7).
- *Yasuko*: a modest plan overtaken by an early win ("最初は堅実に 1カ月 1万円稼ぐ 目標だったん だけどな / ポンド触ったら 一気に増えた!", V10 i0070), a round-number ceiling ("天井タッチ!", i0071), and a persona born of the loss ("FX堕天使 あふぃちゃん", i0079).

**Contrary evidence and limits.** The drivers differ most at entry and at the step that enlarges the stake; they converge at the end, where all four chase (model 2: "取り返す" in Kurumi, Mebuki, Kozue, and あふぃちゃん's V01 doten). Kozue's motives come through a channel of mixed authority; Yasuko's 2013 is a short late revelation (V10 i0066–i0079), so her driver is the least differentiated of the four. Kurumi's own driver is contested between restitution and appetite (section 8); the claim needs only that hers differs from the others', which holds under either reading.

**Rivals.** (a) *One mechanism suffices*: realization aversion and loss chasing explain every end, and the motives are decoration. They explain the ends; they do not explain the entries, the sizes, or why Yasuko and Mochiko, under the same pressure, cut (V03 i0016; V05 i0034–i0035). (b) *Institutional co-production*: the same broker mechanics produce the same end, whatever the motive (section 5). (c) *Design*: the sequence recurs because the work re-stages a template (V10 stages Yasuko's 2013 as the template, FXK-V10-C065). The design reading explains the recurrence; it does not erase the differentiation, which is equally designed. Read together, the design says that one behaviour has several doors.

**Overturn.** A page giving two of these persons the same stated driver under the same pressure (for example Mebuki's chase framed as restoring a family loss), or a page marking a stated driver as a cover contradicted by conduct (for example Kozue's family ideal shown as a pretext). Within V10 the only such re-reading is Kurumi's own (V09 i0031–i0032), and it concerns her alone.

### 4.2 SC-2 — The hinge is sizing against a reference point, not only the failure to cut

**Claim.** Across persons, what turns a loss into the end of an account is decided before the broker acts, by the size of the position and the money behind it; and that size is set by the distance to a reference point — Kurumi's target, Mebuki's break-even, Kozue's 2000万, Yasuko's tripled goal — rather than by the capital at risk. Realization aversion explains why the ends are broker closes; reference-point sizing explains why the close ends the account. In Kurumi the failure to cut changes kind across the series, so that her V07 ruin follows a sizing doctrine adopted after she had shown she could cut. EVALUATION HIGH for Kurumi; EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH across persons.

**Evidence (Kurumi).** The sequence of her reference point and her sizing is printed at each step:

### 4.4 SC-4 — Funding chains end by exhaustion or by outside hands

In these chains it never ends on the page because the trader judges, with money still available, that the position or its size was wrong. EVALUATION HIGH as a description of the four chains; INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH for the general form.

**The chains.**
- *Kurumi*: her own savings → the father's cash, named as untouchable and then deposited ("手を付けちゃいけない", V01 i0110 → "+3,000,000入金", i0111; returned to him in V02) → the reserve she created against her own excess (V03 i0124) → "無傷の100万円" (V07 i0144) → the overseas deposit (V08 i0003; by INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH the same money) → 0円 (i0020) → a rejected order (i0023) → no further funds sought; from V09 her wages are managed by her father (i0107). Her boundary is drawn round borrowing from friends, not round protected family money: she refuses Mochiko's loan, naming the lender's risk (V01 i0066), and refuses to lend to Mebuki ("だめ… 貸さない…", V04 i0071; D010–D011).

## 5. The stated rival: institutional co-production

The role-gap audit names institutional co-production as this study's rival, and assigns to Study A the finding that the size of each ruin depends more on the account regime than on the trader's last decision, to be argued there against this study's psychological reading and a design reading, and adjudicated at convergence. This section does not argue Study A's finding. It states what a psychological reading of the episodes must concede to the institutional one, what it holds against it, and where the two can be made to meet.

**What the psychological reading concedes.**
- *Magnitude.* The same stopless holding ends at 0円 in some accounts and in a shortfall in another. Mebuki's account (500×, set up in V02, i0007) ends at 0円 twice (V04 i0012; V06 i0039), as does Kurumi's V08 overseas deposit (V08 i0020); Kurumi's domestic account ends at −101,534,644 (V07 i0118, i0130), the joint product of the gap ("この区間取引成立せず", i0114; i0116–i0117) and that account. No stop-out level is printed for Kurumi's or Mebuki's broker (U-06 OPEN); Yasuko's 2013 broker's 100% threshold is printed in the V10 flashback (i0076); that a domestic account can end below zero is stated only in Mochiko's unspoken thought (V08 i0106). The difference is therefore observed in outcomes and in one character's knowledge, not in a printed rule for the accounts concerned (corrected at convergence).
- *Timing.* In every account-ending loss the broker, not the trader, fixes the moment of realization (section 2.7).
- *Leverage on offer.* The page prints what each regime allowed: the domestic cap "証拠金25倍まで" (V01 i0075; V03 i0007), the 2008 footnote that no leverage limit then applied (V06 i0083), and, in Kurumi's own words, "海外口座ならレバレッジ無制限" (V07 i0145). Kozue's 400× plan and Kurumi's V08 sizes are possible only where those regimes allow them.
- *Equal shock, unequal exposure.* The narrator insists the January shock came to everyone equally (V07 i0082) and draws four friends in the same instant (i0081); what it did depended on what each held.

**What it holds.**
- *Exposure precedes the regime's action in every ruin.* Size, the absence of a stop and the funding are set by the person before the broker acts (sections 2.1–2.4; SC-2, SC-4). The regime prices an exposure it did not choose.
- *Choosing the regime is itself a decision, and the reference-point rule explains it.* Kurumi chooses a domestic account in V02 for its smaller "即死リスク" (i0017), a risk-reducing choice made while she was still restraining herself; after the ruin she plans to stake in the overseas account she had opened on 7 January, because its leverage is unlimited (V07 i0060, i0145), a choice sized to a 1億 reference. Kozue's 400× is chosen to retrieve "4桁万円" (V06 i0083–i0084). Mebuki's 500× account is chosen through the person she trusts (V02 i0007; model 12). The regime is in part endogenous to the persons' reference points.
- *The regime's irony cuts both ways.* The account Kurumi chose to reduce sudden-death risk is the one that turned the gap into a shortfall, while the unlimited overseas accounts ended at zero. A purely psychological reading cannot explain that asymmetry; a purely institutional one cannot explain why she was holding 300枚 without a stop in the account where the asymmetry mattered.

**Where the two readings meet, and the question for convergence.** The readings answer different questions — how large a ruin is and who appears to cause it (Study A), why the exposure was there (this study). They are compatible if the account-regime finding is stated as: the person sets exposure against a reference point; the regime prices that exposure, sometimes discontinuously (zero floor against shortfall). They conflict only if the finding is taken to mean that the exposure, or the decisions that produced it, were not decisive. The test this study proposes for convergence: is there a ruin episode within V01–V10 in which a person's exposure was modest and the regime alone made the ruin? The tables of section 2 contain none — Mebuki's 13 August zero, whose mechanism is unshown, came from ≈246× with no stop into a release (V03). A *design* reading (institutions as plot devices that deliver what the theses require, as the black box states the outcome before Kurumi reads it, V07 i0118) is compatible with both, and shares with this study the premise that the variable the plot tests is the person's exposure.

## 6. What changes over the series, and why

Her motives are Study B's question.

### 6.6 Why the record moves as it does

Three things move across the series, and one does not. The reference points move (section 6.1); the page's schedule of outcomes moves from reward (V01–V05) to ruin (V07–V08), training first escalation and then a chase (model 7); and the carriers of restraint move from absent or removable to held by others (SC-3). Insight does not move: it is accurate in V01 and remains so. The series' psychology of self-destruction is therefore not a story of ignorance cured; it is a story of where restraint can be lodged.

## 7. The agency strand of FXK-V02-C002

**Rivals.**

**Assessment.** "Chosen" holds: every return the page shows is preceded by named alternatives and owned in her own words (自己責任, V02 i0131; 堂々と, V09 i0079), and the V09 refusal of the vow is itself a choice about how future choices will be made — openly, not in concealment, the reverse of V01 and of "お父さんに バレずに チャラにしたい" (V09 i0040). "Not a curse" fails: the page keeps the curse as the narrator's presentation and adds greed as Kurumi's own account; the desire is presented as given, by both voices. The page's position, read whole, is that the desire is not chosen and the return is.

**Proposed disposition.** REVISE the agency strand: *Kurumi's returns to the market are depicted as choices — alternatives named, reasons her own, responsibility claimed in her own words — made against her own accurate prediction (V02, V07) and, in V09, declared openly as a conditional future ("堂々と", i0079); the desire that motivates them is presented as given, by the narrator as a curse (V02 i0132–i0134) and by Kurumi as innate greed (V09 i0031), so the strand's "not a curse" does not survive; its "not a compulsion" stays OPEN.* EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH. Whether any return occurs, and whether she can control it, lies beyond V10 (FXK-V09-C008 and FXK-V09-C032 stay OPEN; nothing here scores or anticipates them).

**What would overturn it.** Within V10: a page showing her trading after the V09 refusal (there is none), a page showing her unable to refrain from a trade she had decided against, or a page in which the narrator withdraws the curse.

## 9. Consequences for the integrated synthesis

1. **Why the actor chose as the page shows.** The study's answer for the integrated synthesis's interaction of the three accounts: the decisive choices are sizing and funding against a reference point (SC-2, SC-4), made with accurate knowledge that does not govern them unless something outside the trading moment carries restraint (SC-3); the same behaviour enters through different doors in different persons (SC-1).

4. **"Information as capital".** Mochiko is the one person shown using another trader's capitulation as her signal — Mebuki's, the canary followed "その鳴き声が止むまでは" (V03 i0149–i0150) — while keeping her own losses small by rule (V05 i0034–i0035; V03 i0152). 5. **Convergence.** The account-regime finding and SC-2 should be adjudicated on the test proposed in section 5: a ruin with modest exposure made by the regime alone. None appears in section 2.
6. **The V10 workplace.** Kizuna and Nana restage the series' two poles in a new key — a framing in which every branch pays, with action bias (V10 i0123–i0124), against an allocation rule executed at funding, in cash (i0132–i0135).
