**V06's narrative authority.** The 2008 chapter of V06 (第28話) mixes channels: dated journal entries are Kozue's written self-report, captions are a narrator's, some scenes go beyond what a journal could contain, and the chapter is framed as Kurumi's reading (V06 reading §L item 7). **What "agency" and "responsibility" mean here.** The study separates four things the page can attribute: who *decided* (an order, a deposit, a refusal); who *executed* (a fill, a forced close, a refusal of an order); who is the *grammatical agent* of the page's statement of the outcome; and who *bears* the loss (whose money, whose debt, whose labour). The page often gives these four to different parties. The study's claims concern how it distributes them.

## 2. Findings in brief

1. **Institutions realize, persons bear (claim A1).** Of the seven account-ending losses in V01–V10, six are printed as forced liquidations and the seventh, Mebuki's first zero (V03 i0047), has no shown mechanism; none is closed by its holder's own order. Yet no market institution is ever the grammatical agent of a decision. Its text comes after the fact, in nominal, passive or automatic grammar (「強制ロスカット」; 「保有ポジションはありません」; 「自動ロスカットが執…」; 「エラー:証拠金不足です」), and the only time it addresses a person by name it states her obligation (V07 i0130). The persons supply the agency, either by owning the choice ("私が選んだ結果だし", V07 i0140) or by turning the market into a killer ("あたしを… 殺すためだけに 上げたのか…!?", V10 i0076). EVALUATION HIGH.
2. **Exact institutions, unreliable persons, and a reader who holds the ledger (claim A2).** The narrator's boxes and the drawn screens are exact wherever their operands are printed; error and deceit live in what characters say about figures and in the documents they draw for themselves. Page form gives the reader the institution's figure before, or instead of, the character: the black box of V07 i0118, the white box over a sinking figure at V08 i0020, the census boxes, the 「保有金融資産」 captions; at four nadirs the figure breaks for the character. EVALUATION HIGH.
3. **The account regime decides who carries a loss that outruns the stake, not whether a ruin happens (claim A3, the account-regime finding argued against two rivals; qualified at convergence).** The accounts labelled 海外口座 that are shown ending end at 0円; the domestic account — labelled in V06, but not on the decisive V07 box (V07 i0118) — ends in a printed debt. The strong form of the finding — that the size of each ruin depends more on the account regime than on the trader's last decision — holds for none of the seven account-ending losses: it is joint with the person's decisions for V07 and V08, fails for Mebuki's two forced closes and Kozue's ruin, whose size is the sum of their own funding decisions, and is undecidable for Mebuki's first zero and Yasuko's 2013 (V01 and the V06 cuts end no account and stand outside the tally). What survives is narrower: the person's sizing, stop policy and funding decide the stake and whether an adverse move can end the account; that exposure, with a market gap, decides whether a loss outruns the stake; the account's terms decide who carries the excess (V07: of the printed 120,284,479, 101,534,644 became her debt; CALC). QUALIFIED; EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH.
4. **Law as lecture: maxima that are used, exits that are priced (claim A4, restricted at convergence).** Law and market rules reach the page as narrator lecture, footnote, a friend's hedged menu or a machine's refusal. They set maxima that characters then use (25×, and an overseas broker beyond it; 109.5% as the highest rate without criminal penalty; no leverage limit in 2008) and price the exits (the deposit for bankruptcy; the surviving tax). No market or legal institution warns or stops a trader before a loss: the platform refuses an order on an empty account, the discharge and a father's control of wages come after the ruin, and the equity market's unit rule is friction on a novice's below-unit order, after which her own half rule governs. The V03 tax reserve is Kurumi's own act. EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH as a pattern.
5. **Who speaks for the institution (claim A5).** The narrator calls forced liquidation mechanical mercy (V01 i0101), the market an impartial scale (V07 i0080), the trader's act gambling or an impulse buy, and the root of investing desire (V10 i0052–i0053); the characters, in turn, personify the dealer or the market as hunter and killer. The narrator's irony (V01 i0143; V02 i0132–i0134) loosens its verdicts without ever moving responsibility onto an institution. EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH.
6. **The household is the source of last resort, and the state's claim outlives the debt (section 10).** Family money enters the market without its owners' consent in 2008 and 2014 and is offered with consent for the exit in 2015 (its use for the bankruptcy costs INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH); the tax reserve, a protective pot Kurumi made herself, becomes by inference the last stake, and the 2014 tax is the one obligation the page shows surviving bankruptcy (V09 i0106; V10 i0045).

**What the inventory shows before any argument.** Three distributions are plain from the table. First, by voice: the institution's own words (3.3, the rejection line of 3.2, and the liquidation boxes of 3.4) are few, late and agentless, while the narrator's voice (3.1, 3.10) carries almost every figure and every verdict. ## 5. Claim A1 — Institutions realize, persons bear: the grammar of the notice

**Claim.** Across V01–V10 market institutions execute every printed account-ending loss and are never the grammatical agent of a decision. The page states outcomes as nouns, states, absences, passives or automatic processes; the broker addresses a named person only once, to state her obligation; the only institutional decision printed in ten volumes is a court's grant of relief. The agency the institutions' text leaves empty is filled by persons, who either own the choice or personify the market as a hostile force, and by the narrator, who describes the institution as mechanism. EVALUATION HIGH (the grammar is OBSERVATION HIGH at each locator; the reading of it as an allocation is EVALUATION).

**Evidence across the boundary.**
- *The notices.* Mebuki's first forced close is 「0円 (−800,000円) 強制ロスカット」 above a screen that says only 「保有ポジションはありません」 (V04 i0012). An equivalent position panel on Kurumi's platform, with the same heading and a differently worded sentence, announces her best day: 「現在お持ちの建玉はありません」 under 「21,213,915円 (+17,602,200円)」 (V05 i0152). The institution's sentence is indifferent to outcome; it records that a position no longer exists. Mebuki's second close puts the noun in parentheses (V06 i0039); Kurumi's two put it at the foot of a box (V07 i0118; V08 i0020). Yasuko's 2013 notice, the only one that prints a rule, uses an automatic process as its subject: 「自動ロスカットが執…」 (V10 i0076). The platform's one sentence on V08's night is 「エラー:証拠金不足です」, six times (V08 i0023): an error state whose subject is the customer's insufficient margin.
- *The one address.* After the shock the broker speaks, as 「たのしいFX事業部」, in a polite procedural register: 「[重要]不足金の入金が必要です。」 (V07 i0122), then 「【不足金】-101,534,644円」, "お預かり資産以上の損失", a deadline and 「年率14.6%」 (i0130–i0131). Its grammatical subject is the payment the customer owes. It is addressed to her; it says nothing about what the institution did.
- *Persons' own grammar.* Kozue's first-person caption in the journal-framed flashback (V06; mixed authority) puts the institution's act in the passive and the loss in the first person: 「すべてのポジションが強制ロスカットされた。私は1900万円もの大金を失ってしまった。」 (V06 i0080). Kurumi, after the shock: "負けて借金になっちゃったけど 仕方ないよね 私が選んだ結果だし" and "自己責任だもん" (V07 i0140). In V09 she pays what survives "地道に" (i0106). In V02 the age of majority licenses the relapse under 自己責任 (i0130–i0131). The vocabulary of responsibility belongs to Study B; what matters here is that the persons' sentences supply the agent the institutional sentences omit.
- *Personification.* When characters give the market or the dealer agency, it is hostile and unanswerable: "奴らは 問答無用で 狩りにくる!" with a reaper (V01 i0100); "損切り注文してたら狩られて負けるだけ" (V06 i0153); "あたしを… 殺すためだけに 上げたのか…!?" (V10 i0076) and "殺人通貨!!" (i0077). The hunter and the killer are forces of nature, not parties that could be held to account.
- *The narrator's grammar.* The narrator's institution is a mechanism: "ただ機械的に執行する" (V01 i0101). Its one sentence with brokers as subject states an incapacity, not an act: "一部のFX業者は 適切なレートを 提示できず", "取引不可能な状態に陥っていた" (V07 i0114).

**Contrary evidence.**
- *An institutional decision is printed once* — and it releases the person: 「破産手続き終結及び免責許可決定」, "破産者について免責を許可する" (V09 i0104). A legal institution is shown deciding, in the person's favour. The claim is therefore stated for market institutions; the law's only decision on the page is relief.
- *The page does assign causal weight to institutions*, through characters and the narrator: Mochiko reasons that the forced close executed after trading resumed (V07 i0116–i0117) and names a product specification, "国内口座の仕様" (V08 i0106); the narrator explains the gap (V07 i0114); the V04 lecture makes the law the thing that allows 109.5% (i0096–i0097). These are attributions of cause, as mechanism, rule or permission. None is an attribution of fault or choice.
- *An institution acts for a person in a gain*: the V05 limit order is filled while Kurumi is away (i0151–i0152). This shows the institution executing a decision the person delegated, and it is stated in the same neutral grammar as the losses.

**Rivals.**
- *Point of view.* The broker is silent because the pages follow persons who never contact it; the grammar is a by-product of focalization, not an allocation. Against: the narrator's boxes are extra-diegetic and unfocalized, and they use the same nominal grammar (V04 i0012; V07 i0118); the narrator explains the gap without any character present (V07 i0114) and still gives the brokers only an incapacity.
- *Design.* A silent broker keeps the moral focus on persons, as an instructional comedy needs. This explains *why* the page allocates as it does; it does not contradict *that* it does. The page could print a rule when it wished (V10 i0076, for a secondary person in a flashback), which makes the protagonists' unprinted rule a choice of the page.

**What would overturn it.** A page on which a market institution warns before a loss (a margin call, a message before the close), or is the grammatical subject of the close, or is shown choosing a rate or a time against a person. None exists in V01–V10.

**What changes over the series, and why.** V01–V06: no institution speaks to anyone; liquidation is a concept, a monster or a noun. V07: the institution speaks, as a creditor. V08: it refuses. V09: the law decides, and its decision is relief. V10: the market's rule is printed, retrospectively and for someone else, and the institutions on the page are the slow, rule-dense ones of the equity market. The institutional voice grows as the protagonist moves from trader to debtor to employee: institutions become audible when they collect and when they release, never while a position is being chosen.

## 6. Claim A2 — Exact institutions, unreliable persons: the reader's ledger

**Claim.** Wherever their operands are printed, the narrator's boxes and the drawn screens reconcile exactly; error and deceit live in what characters say about figures and in the documents they draw for themselves. Page form gives the reader the institution's figure before, beside or instead of the character, and at a nadir withdraws the figure from the character. The consequence for responsibility is that no ruin in V01–V10 can be explained by an institution's false or missing figure about the person's own account; what is missing is the institution's *rule*, not its *number*. OBSERVATION HIGH for the exactness; EVALUATION HIGH for the form's distribution.

**Evidence across the boundary.**
- *Exactness.* Each Kurumi box follows exactly from the previous one wherever a trade and its operands are printed (V01; V03 from 1 September; V04–V08), and the cross-character checks close (two tiles fixing one rate in V03; Mebuki's deposits composing her contest figure in V07; Nana's passbook in V10) (reconciliation audit, section 4.12). - *Small residues in institutional figures*: the +10円 on every 300-lot value (FXK-V01-C013, OPEN), the 11,000 gap at Mebuki's liquidation (V06 i0039), the twin boxes +24,200 and +24,000 (V01 i0049–i0050). Each is small; none changes an outcome.

- *The narrator's lectures are less exact than its boxes*: the tax lecture prints "×0.45%" where 45% is meant and applies a rate flat (V08 i0030); the trading-hours dial prints 午後 for the morning session (V10 i0104). The claim therefore holds for the boxes and screens, not for the lectures.

Against: the page uses the exactness argumentatively — it sets the true box beside the lie (V03 i0090) and the figure beside the face that will not read it (V07 i0118; V08 i0020). (b) *Dramatic irony only.* The reader-character gap is a suspense device. Irony is how the page allocates here: the reader, holding the exact figure, is put in the position of the judge, and the judge is shown a person, not an institution.

**What would overturn it.** A box or institutional screen later shown wrong by a printed figure, or a ruin traced on the page to an institution's false figure. None exists in V01–V10.

**What changes, and why.** The one-number-per-person format widens from trading accounts (the V02 census) to a contest of persons (V03–V07) to whole persons' holdings at a welcome party (V10). The ledger stops measuring trades and starts measuring standing, as the volume's subject moves from trading to the workplace (FXK-V10-C001, C014).

## 7. Claim A3 — The account regime decides the reach of a ruin, not whether it happens

### 7.1 The finding under test

The role-gap review (section 3.1, criterion 4; section 7) records a finding offered to it from the financial side: *the size of each ruin depends more on the account regime than on the trader's last decision.* It did not adopt the finding; it required that it be argued against two stated rivals — the psychological reading, whose full account is Study C's, and a design reading in which institutions are plot devices — and not presumed. This section tests it episode by episode and against both rivals. "Account regime" means what the page prints about the account in which the loss occurs (overseas or domestic; its leverage; whether it can end below zero) and, where the page shows it, the funding regime that put money into it (cards, a student loan, a private loan, family money).

### 7.2 What the page prints about the regimes

- *Overseas.* Kurumi's V01 broker is "海外のFX業者", beyond the domestic "証拠金25倍まで" (NP, V01 i0075); her V01 position ran at 59× on the narrator's figure (i0074) and the account at 1:500 by calculation (FXK-V02-C013). Mebuki's form chooses 500倍 on Mochiko's advice (V02 i0007). The census labels each balance 国内口座 or 海外口座 (V02 i0158–i0159). Every account labelled overseas that is shown ending, ends at 0円: Mebuki at V04 i0012 and V06 i0039 (the latter labelled 海外口座), Kurumi at V08 i0020 (labelled 海外口座, with no 不足金 stated). Mebuki's V03 zero (i0047) shows no mechanism.
- *Domestic.* Kurumi moves to a domestic account in V02 because it carries "即死リスクが小さい" and because "税金のことを考えると 海外口座は不利" (V02 i0017). That account ends at −101,534,644 with a demand for payment (V07 i0118, i0130). The general rule — "国内口座の仕様" — is printed only as Mochiko's unspoken thought, when Mebuki, who has been liquidated to 0円 "何度か", assumes the same of Kurumi ("でも私みたいに 借金してないなら きっとすぐに 復活——", V08 i0105) and asks "FXで借金しちゃう パターンも あるって ことですか…?" (i0106).
- *The gap.* "一部のFX業者は 適切なレートを 提示できず… 取引不可能な状態に陥っていた", 「この区間 取引成立せず」 (NP, V07 i0114); Mochiko's inference that the forced close executed at the rate after trading resumed (i0116–i0117). The domestic account's negative balance is the joint product of the gap and the account.
- *Other regimes.* Kozue's 2008 account ends positive, at 1,005,144, after a forced close (V06 i0080–i0082); the footnote that Japan had no leverage limit in 2008 is printed for her plan after the ruin (i0083). Yasuko's 2013 broker is the only one whose threshold is printed (100%, V10 i0076). ### 7.4 The within-person comparison, V07 against V08

The page's sharpest test is its own. The same person, on the same pair, with the same declared refusal of a stop ("あえてしてない!", V06 i0152; "読みが外れても 損切りとか 考えない", V08 i0006), at a larger size in V08 (1000 lots against 300), ends in a debt in the domestic account and at 0円 in the overseas one. The page then stages the comparison in dialogue: Mebuki generalizes her own floor to Kurumi, and Mochiko corrects her aloud and names the product's specification only in thought (V08 i0105–i0106). Two limits keep this from settling the matter. The V08 fills, rates and leverage are not printed (FXK-V08-C004), so whether V08 also met a gap is unknown; and the "仕様" is Mochiko's inference, not a printed rule. Behaviour does not explain the difference; the residual rivals are the regime and the gap, which the page does not separate (no gap is printed for V08, and the one forced close shown without a halt, Kozue's in 2008, left a positive balance, V06 i0082). INFERENCE MODERATE (lowered at convergence from MODERATE-HIGH) that the domestic specification, rather than the gap alone, explains why the one ruin produced a debt and the other did not.

### 7.5 First rival: the psychological reading

*Statement (constructed here from the accepted readings' section G and the reconciliation audit's consolidated wordings, not from Study C, whose own account is adjudicated at convergence).* The size of every ruin follows from the person: Kurumi sizes to the distance from her target rather than to the risk and escalates after success (FXK-V03-C012; reconciliation audit §5.5 row 4); her inability to cut changes into a chosen refusal of the stop (§5.5 row 3); Mebuki refunds after each zero despite voiced reconsideration (V04 i0027, i0103, i0105, i0107); Kozue drains pot after pot. Institutions are the stage on which these mechanisms play out.

*What it explains better than the regime.* The stake and the occurrence of every ruin. Kurumi chose the domestic regime herself and for her own reasons — less instant-death risk and tax (V02 i0017) — and after the ruin planned to stake in the overseas account she had opened on 7 January, because its leverage is unlimited ("海外口座ならレバレッジ無制限", V07 i0060, i0145); the regime was itself a decision. Mochiko's counterfactual puts the avoidable part of R7 in Kurumi's December choice: a stop just under 1.20 "た・ぶ・ん助かってるはず" (V07 i0115). Mebuki's and Kozue's ruins are as large as their funding decisions made them (section 7.3).

*What it cannot explain.* Why 101,534,644 of the R7 loss became debt rather than stopping at the 18,749,835 of equity; why Mebuki's three zeros produced no broker debt; why the same behaviour in V08 produced a floor. Nor does it explain the distribution of *knowledge* about the regime, which is an information-state fact, not a mechanism of risk-taking: Mebuki did not know her own account's rule when she first lost ("まさかそのまま借金!?", V03 i0046), learned the floor by losing, and in V08 still did not know the domestic rule (i0106). No page shows any trader told the rule by the institution.

*Adjudication.* The psychological reading governs whether a ruin occurs and how much the person stakes; the regime governs how far the ruin reaches beyond the stake and on whom it lands. For the series' largest figure the regime decides who carries the part of the loss that outran the stake (84.4% of the printed parenthetical, an accounting split, not a causal share; FXK-SYN-C018); size and the gap decided that it outran the stake. For the series' recurring ruins (Mebuki's chain, Kozue's 2008) the person's funding decides the size. Psychological mechanisms are candidate models here, not diagnoses.

### 7.6 Second rival: the design reading

*Statement.* The regimes are plot devices, not claims about the world. The protagonist needs a debt to drive V08–V09's crisis and bankruptcy; the comic friend needs repeatable zeros; so the protagonist gets the domestic account and the friend the overseas one.

*Support on the page.* The stop-out rule is never printed for either protagonist's broker (U-06) and is printed, once, for a secondary person in a flashback (V10 i0076): the page withholds the rule where it would constrain the plot. The identical 1,005,144 across two generations (V06 i0082; V07 i0144) is a designer's signature (reconciliation audit §4.11, "design"; FXK-V07-C025). After V07 the exact debt is never reprinted; it becomes the round "1億" (V08–V09). The broker is named 「たのしいFX」 (V07 i0122). The unit rule arrives exactly when a novice is about to stake her savings (V10 i0105–i0107). The narrator's lectures arrive when the plot needs them (the interest law before the loan, V04 i0096–i0097; tax and bankruptcy at the reckoning, V08 i0030–i0034).

*Where it fails as a rival.* It explains why the regimes fall where they do; it does not dissolve their causal role inside the fiction. The page builds a consistent regime logic and has characters reason it out from three viewpoints (Kurumi's V02 choice, i0017; Mochiko's V07 reasoning about the gap, i0115–i0117, and her V08 thought, i0106; Mebuki's movement from not knowing, V03 i0046, to a wrong generalization, V08 i0105). The boxes label the regime in the census and at two ends (V02 i0158–i0159; V06 i0039; V08 i0020), but not on the decisive box, which prints 「くるみ FX口座残高 -1億153万4644円 (-1億2028万4479円) 強制ロスカット」 with no regime label (V07 i0118); the account's domestic identity there rests on the V06 boxes and continuity (V06 i0013, i0046, i0095; INFERENCE HIGH). A device that the page explains three times and tests against a within-person contrast is one the work asks the reader to understand as a cause (EVALUATION MODERATE, lowered at convergence). The page's omissions — the label on the decisive box, the broker's rule, any statement of the specification before V08 — are evidence the design reading may use ([convergence audit](../audits/FX_KURUMI_V01_V10_CONVERGENCE.md), section 4.3). Whether it is also a claim about the real world cannot be assessed here: that comparison is EXTERNAL_REFERENCE.

*Adjudication.* Design and regime are compatible: design answers why the protagonist carries the debt; the regime answers, inside the fiction, how the debt arose. The design reading is a rival to the finding's standing as a statement about markets, which this study does not make; it is not a rival to its standing as a statement about the page.

### 7.7 Disposition

The strong form — the size of *each* ruin depends more on the account regime than on the trader's last decision — is **not adopted**: it holds for none of the seven account-ending losses (section 7.3, tally as corrected at convergence). What survives is narrower and better supported:

> **A3.** In V01–V10 the account regime decides the *reach* of a ruin — whether it stops at the stake or runs into debt, and who becomes the creditor — while the person's sizing, stop policy and funding decide the stake and whether a ruin happens. The accounts labelled overseas that are shown ending end at 0円 and the domestic account ends in a printed debt; in that one ruin, 84.4% of the printed parenthetical lay beyond her equity and became her debt (CALC) — size and gap decided that a loss outran the equity, the account's terms that the excess was hers. A floor does not stop debt; it moves it to whoever funded the refunding (Mebuki's cards, student loan and private lender). Knowledge of the regime is unequally held and is learned by losing, never from the institution.

Label: OBSERVATION HIGH for the printed ends of each account (their regime labels printed at V06 i0039 and V08 i0020, inferred for V07 i0118); INFERENCE MODERATE (lowered at convergence) for the domestic specification, rather than the gap alone, as the explanation of the V07/V08 difference; EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH for the claim as a whole. The adjudicated wording of A3 is in the [convergence audit](../audits/FX_KURUMI_V01_V10_CONVERGENCE.md), section 4.4 (ledger row FXK-SYN-C018). What would overturn it: an overseas account on the page ending below zero, or a domestic account stopping at 0円 after a gap; a printed stop-out rule for Kurumi's domestic broker showing that the V07 debt arose without a gap; a page showing that Kurumi knew the domestic account's tail when she chose it in V02 (which would move the reach, too, into her decision). None exists in V01–V10.

## 8. Claim A4 — Law as lecture: maxima that are used, exits that are priced, protection that comes late

**Claim.** Law and market rules reach the page as narrator lecture, footnote, a friend's hedged speech or a machine's refusal. In the ruin episodes they appear as *maxima* that the characters then use or route around, and as *prices* on the exits. No market or legal institution warns or stops a trader before a loss: the platform refuses an order on an empty account, the discharge and a father's control of wages come after the ruin, and the equity market's unit rule is friction on a novice's below-unit order, after which her own rule governs. The restraints that held before a loss were self-made (the V03 tax reserve; Nana's half rule; Study C, SC-3). The market's own protective device, the forced close, is drawn as execution and narrated as mercy; it protects where it can execute and fails where it cannot. EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH as a pattern (restricted at convergence).

**Evidence across the boundary.**
- *Maxima, used.* The domestic "証拠金25倍まで" is printed in the same panel as the fact that Kurumi's dealer is overseas, "そこには日本の規制は及ばない" (V01 i0075). Mebuki's form takes the 500倍 on offer (V02 i0007). The interest lecture ends on the rate as a ceiling — "これが刑事罰を受けない最大の金利になるのだ" — and Mochiko lends at exactly that rate (V04 i0088, i0097). The 2008 footnote supplies the means of Kozue's 400× plan (V06 i0083). After the shock Kurumi thinks of the overseas account as the escape from domestic limits: "海外口座ならレバレッジ無制限" (V07 i0145). In each case the rule is printed as the edge of what is allowed, and the person goes to the edge or past it.
- *Exits, priced.* Bankruptcy is first blocked by its entry cost, the 予納金 "150万" (V08 i0034), while the narrator, who knows more than she does, says discharge is likely (V08 i0033). In V09 the legal routes come as a menu with prices — 任意整理 at "月々166万円以上", 個人再生 only below "5000万円" — and the price selects the route (Mochiko's speech, hedged "確か", "らしい", V09 i0018–i0019). The tax is undischargeable (V08 i0034; V09 i0106).
- *Protection, late, and restraint that is self-made.* The V03 reserve is Kurumi's own act, made before any ruin of hers, with tax as its reason (V03 i0124; counter-case C1), and the reserve becomes, by inference, the V08 stake. The platform refuses an order on an empty account (V08 i0023; C2). The discharge closes the broker debt and the father holds her wages (V09 i0104, i0107; C3). The unit rule rejects a novice's 100-share order as below the 1,000-share unit, raising the minimum stake to about 705,000 (CALC 705 × 1,000; V10 i0105–i0107; C4); her own half rule then governs her purchase (i0132). The first institution in the series that spends on the friends rather than taking from them is Mochiko's company (V10 i0010–i0012).
- *The forced close's two images.* Kurumi draws it as dealers who hunt and a reaper (V01 i0100); the narrator answers with guillotines and "ただ機械的に執行する—— むしろそれは温情ではないだろうか?" (i0101), and she replies "…え?". The argument recurs in practice: Mebuki is relieved when the close comes ("肩の荷が下りて", V06 i0040); the device fails when trading stops (V07 i0114–i0117); it works, and the account is empty anyway, on V08's night (i0020); Yasuko calls it a killing (V10 i0076).

**Contrary evidence.**
- *The market offers protection that persons decline.* The stop order exists from V03 (Mochiko's 逆指値, i0152), Kurumi's one bracket realizes the series' largest gain (V05 i0077–i0080, i0152), and Mochiko's counterfactual says a stop would probably have saved her in V07 (i0115). The claim "protection comes from outside the market" holds for what *stopped* a trader; the market's own instruments were available and were refused ("あえてしてない!", V06 i0152).
- *Law as a limit that would protect.* The 25× cap is a protective rule; the page shows it bypassed, not absent (V01 i0075). Whether Kurumi's domestic broker enforced it is not shown: her V04 add runs at about 29.9× against it (CALC; V04-U02, a tension, OPEN).
- *One law stops an action before any loss*: the FSA footnote that ends Kurumi's idea of an advisory fee (V02 i0091–i0092). It stops a scheme for earning from others, not a trade.

**Rivals.** (a) *Instructional genre*: the lectures teach the reader financial literacy and carry no view of institutions. The content argues otherwise: across volumes the lectures present the law as the edge of the permitted (V01 i0075; V04 i0097; V06 i0083) and as the price of an exit (V08 i0034; V09 i0018–i0019), never as a protection that operated. (b) *Realism of youth*: the characters do not know the law and the page shows them learning it. This is compatible and is part of the claim: the knowledge arrives after the ruin, from a web page, a narrator and a friend.

**What would overturn it.** A page on which a law or market rule warns a trader or stops a position before a loss (other than the platform's refusal on an empty account and the unit rule's friction on a below-unit order), or on which a lecture presents a rule as having protected someone.

**What changes, and why.** Law is a footnote in V01–V06, the subject of V08–V09, and administrative friction in V10. The shift follows the protagonist from trading to reckoning: the page shows law where her situation makes it binding.

## 9. Claim A5 — Who speaks for the institution: the narrator as moral authority and as ironist

**Claim.** The narrator assigns the cause of ruin to the person and describes institutions as rule, mechanism or impartial force; characters, by contrast, personify the market or the dealer as a hostile agent. The narrator's irony loosens the authority of its verdicts in V01–V02 but never moves responsibility onto an institution, and after V07 its verdicts and the outcomes agree. EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH.

**Evidence across the boundary.**
- *Verdicts on persons.* "誤解を恐れずに言えば 彼女がやっていることの本質は——" "ただの博打(ギャンブル)なのだ" (V01 i0075–i0077); "ただの衝動買い" (i0128); "とどのつまりただのポジション" against Mebuki's "救い玉" (V05 i0090); "くるみの目はもう なにも見えなくなっていた" (V07 i0150); "人が その行為に 踏み出すとき / その 根底には / 必ず / 欲望が 存在する" (V10 i0052–i0053).
- *Institutions as mechanism or impartial force.* "ただ機械的に執行する" and "温情" (V01 i0101); "FXは平等である", "平等に機会を与え / 平等に殺す", "投資家それぞれの事情など 決して考慮されることはない", drawn as a pair of scales (V07 i0079–i0080); the brokers' incapacity in the gap (V07 i0114); law stated as fact (V04 i0096–i0097; V08 i0032–i0033).
- *Characters' personification* (section 5): hunters, a reaper, a killer currency (V01 i0100; V06 i0153; V10 i0076–i0077).
- *The equality maxim and unequal exposure.* "この日起きた出来事は 平等に訪れた現実なのだ" is set over a montage of four friends in one instant, only one of whom is shown holding a position (V07 i0081–i0082; FXK-V07-E031). The shock was equal; the exposure, decided by the person, was not. The maxim is formally true and, for responsibility, decisive: it removes the market as a party and leaves the position.

**Contrary evidence.**
- *The narrator also personifies*: "平等に殺す" makes FX an executioner (V07 i0080). The difference from the characters is that the narrator's executioner is impartial — drawn as a balance — and the characters' is malicious.
- *The page's form personifies the danger at the moment of choice*: a wordless full page sets a skeletal Death figure with lantern and scythe behind Kurumi as she places the EUR/CHF order (V06 i0116–i0117), and Mochiko's own vision turns the chart into a monster before her single question (i0151–i0153; V06 reading E041, §D). The V06 reading treats both as genre staging, not authorial verdicts; like the narrator's executioner, they figure the risk without assigning fault to an institution.
- *The narrator explains structural facts that partly exculpate the person*: the gap made exit impossible for minutes to tens of minutes (V07 i0114); the 2008 absence of a limit (V06 i0083). These are the closest the narrator comes to assigning a share of a ruin to an institution, and they are stated as conditions, not faults.
- *The narrator's own causal model can be exculpatory*: the lucky win became "一生消えることのない呪い" (V02 i0132–i0134), which locates the later danger in an outcome acting on her rather than in her character; Kurumi's V09 account, innate greed (i0031–i0032), competes with it (reconciliation audit §5.5 row 2).
- *Irony*: the narrator calls her refusal to look "ある意味では 勇気があった" just before the rescue (V01 i0142–i0143) and jokes about the manga's own cancellation (i0086). V01's outcome rewards what the narrator called gambling (reconciliation audit §5.5 row 1). This is the material of V01's competing thesis, registered by FXK-SYN-C006 and argued in the integrated synthesis.

**Rivals.** (a) *The narrator as pedagogue*: its verdicts instruct and allocate nothing. The verdicts are moral in vocabulary (博打, 衝動買い, 正当化, 欲望) and are never pointed at an institution. (b) *The narrator as unreliable ironist* (the V01 competing thesis): outcomes mock the verdicts. True of V01–V02; after V07 the outcomes and the verdicts align, and the V10 sentence on desire is unironic.

**What would overturn it.** A narrator verdict that blames an institution — a broker's practice called unfair, a law called inadequate, a product called a trap. None exists in V01–V10; the nearest is the stated incapacity of some brokers in the gap (V07 i0114).

**What changes, and why.** V01–V02: didactic and ironic. V05–V06: corrective of persons' labels. V07: tragic and impartial. V08: an informant on law who knows more than the person. V10: anthropological, rooting all investing in desire. The narrator moves from judging trades to judging motives as the series moves from one trader to a workplace of four.

OBSERVATION HIGH for each transfer as printed; EVALUATION HIGH for the pattern. The V01 act is described in this study's own voice as the V01 reading classifies it — an unconsented, concealed use of family savings with an intent to repay; 借金 is a named speaker's word (reconciliation audit §9 item 11).
2. *Institutions label pots; the broker's account erases the labels; persons relabel.* The household's institutions name what the money is for (タンス預金 with its 更新日; 税金分; a bank book "for Kurumi"). The broker's box records only 「入金」. The persons supply new labels that make protected money stakeable ("借りた"; "無傷の100万円"). The labels are the page's evidence of what was protected; the box is its evidence that the market does not read them.
3. *The state's claim outlives the debt.* Tax is the reason for the one protective pot Kurumi made (V03), the reason she chose the domestic account (V02 i0017), and, by inference, the money she staked last (V08). Its claim is the one obligation the page shows surviving bankruptcy, paid "地道に" from wages another person holds (V09 i0106–i0107; V10 i0045).
4. *A figure as a family's signature.* The identical 1,005,144 (V06 i0082; V07 i0144) ties the daughter's bank balance to the mother's post-liquidation balance. It is design (INFERENCE HIGH; reconciliation audit §4.11); whether Kurumi notices is not stated (V07-U12). It is the clearest place where the design reading (section 7.6) is right: the page, not the market, put that number there.

**Contrary evidence.** The household also protects: the father's money comes back intact in V02, funds the exit in V09 and holds her wages after it. The V09 revelation that the 300万 was "for Kurumi" all along re-reads V01 without making the V01 use consented. And the tax reserve was set aside by Kurumi herself: the protective pot was her own institution before it was the state's.

## 13. Consequences for the integrated synthesis

1. **Who appears to act.** In every ruin the decision-maker and the grammatical bearer is a person; institutions execute and are described as mechanism, rule or impartial force; the one institutional decision printed is a release (claims A1, A5). The integrated synthesis should describe no ruin as an institution's act and none as the person's alone where the page shows a regime fixing the reach (A3): the V07 debt is a joint product of a December decision, a gap and an account whose excess fell on the customer.
2. **The interface with Study C.** This study proposes the qualified A3, not the strong form. Psychological mechanisms explain the stake and the occurrence of a ruin; the regime explains its reach and its creditor. The convergence review adjudicates Study C's reading against A3 and the design reading (role-gap audit, section 3.4); section 7.3's episode table is offered as the common ground for that adjudication.
3. **The interface with Study B.** The map (section 4) and the household chain (section 10) identify bearers as printed: Mebuki's debt arises from her funding, not from her broker; the household's money enters the market without consent and leaves it by disclosure; the broker's own loss is never shown; the state's claim survives. What each transfer cost the persons is Study B's question.
4. **"Information as capital."** Mochiko holds knowledge of rules as well as of figures: the gap mechanism (V07 i0115–i0117), the domestic specification (V08 i0106), the legal menu (V09 i0018–i0019), the gazette she buys and reads (V09 i0104), the family backstop she invokes to Mebuki ("失敗しても親が払ってくれる", V03 i0100). The required section should treat knowledge of institutions as part of that capital; motive questions stay with Study B.
5. **Manga form.** The load-bearing formal uses — the black and white boxes, the census and captions, the broken figures at nadirs, the scales of V07 i0080, the guillotines of V01 i0101 — are inventoried in section 3 and argued in sections 5–6 and 9.
6. **V01's competing thesis (FXK-SYN-C006).** Two findings bear on it: the narrator's irony in V01 (section 9) and the finding (INFERENCE HIGH on the narrator's statement of the cap, V01 i0075, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION; R1, CALC) that the V01 reward of "the worst process" was available only outside the printed domestic rule. Chance rewarded Kurumi in a regime that let chance operate at 59× and 1:500.
7. **Cross-volume wordings.** The reconciliation audit's §5.5 row 1 ("an arc of early reward setting up later ruin") keeps its grade. The mechanism decided at convergence: the early reward needed leverage beyond the narrator's stated domestic cap (R1); the V07 ruin's reach needed a gap and an account whose excess fell on her; the V08 ruin, in the reward's own overseas regime, ended at 0円 ([convergence audit](../audits/FX_KURUMI_V01_V10_CONVERGENCE.md), section 4).
