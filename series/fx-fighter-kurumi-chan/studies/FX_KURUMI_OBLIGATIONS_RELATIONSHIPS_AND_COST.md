## 1. Question, boundary and conventions

**Question.** Across V01–V10, which obligations (family money, loans, debts, tax, tuition, promises, friendship duties, the duty to warn or to intervene) bind whom; how directed relationships change after harm; and on whom the cost of each consequential choice actually lands — the chooser, a parent, a friend, a lender, or no one?

**Boundary.** The V01–V10 main narrative, ending at V10 i0156 (Monday 24 April 2017). Paratext is classified only and is never evidence. Nothing outside the admitted pages is used: no real market or legal history, and no knowledge of the title beyond V10. Forecasts that face events after V10 are NOT SCOREABLE IN THIS RUN and are not touched; neither are the outcomes of positions open at V10 i0156.

**Evidence and labels.** OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, EVALUATION, UNRESOLVED, with confidence HIGH, MODERATE or LOW. Locators are `Vnn iNNNN` (page occurrence in the accepted witness), with panel or balloon where attribution matters; accepted IDs are FXK-Vnn-Ennn (observations), -Dnnn (episodes), -Cnnn (claims), FXK-SYN-Cnnn (synthesis transitions) and the readings' obligation IDs (Vnn-Unn). Short Japanese anchors are quoted exactly with their locator; " / " marks a break between lines or balloons where it matters, and spacing inside a balloon is not reproduced.

**Ethical vocabulary.** The readings' scale is kept: *ignorance* (not knowing), *negligence* (failing to attend to what one should), *recklessness* (knowingly running a risk that falls on another), *manipulation* (steering another by deception or pressure toward one's own end) and *intentional cruelty* (wanting another to suffer); and, separately, *understanding* a vulnerability, *exploiting* it and *enjoying* its consequences. An unfavourable market outcome is not wrongdoing: a loss is an outcome, and only conduct toward a person is graded. A grade describes conduct on the page, never a person's nature.

**What "cost" means here.** What the page shows a person losing or bearing because of a choice: money (with sign, unit and provenance), labour and time, control over one's own money, standing and trust, knowledge forced on someone, and risk carried without consent. A cost "lands on no one" when the page shows no person bearing it. "Who pays" is asked of the page, not of law or practice outside it.

**Names and knowledge boundaries.** Names are the default reference (handoff contract §15; qualification audit §1.4–1.5). The blogger of V01 is あふぃちゃん until V02 i0074 prints her name, 高根やす子 (Yasuko). ## 2. Theses in brief

The argument is carried in sections 3–9 and stated with its rivals and overturn conditions in section 10; section 14 proposes the transitions.

- **B1 — Concealment carries cost to people who do not know.** In each family-money episode the first cost of a choice falls on someone who did not know of it, and the one who conceals describes the concealment as protecting them. The page shows that concealment does not spare them; it defers and enlarges the cost and threatens to hand them its worst form. The series turns when a disclosure is chosen (V09), and the same 300万 that was taken without consent in V01 is then given (V09 i0074).
- **B2 — Between friends, credit for trading is harm, and care is not money.** The only loan between friends in ten volumes is the corpus's clearest exploitation (V04–V05); every other proposed transfer of credit between friends is refused, and the help the series validates is presence, time, a question and a job. Commerce and treats between friends do no harm. Family money is harm when taken in concealment and help when offered openly.
- **B3 — Responsibility borne alone and hidden disables help; shared, it becomes payable.** 自己責任 changes object and function across the series, from a disclaimer to a licence, a reason to owe no care, a reason for a friend's silence, an inner judge and a duty turned toward self-erasure; in V09 it is replaced by disclosure, payment "地道に" and accepted control. What disables help is not self-responsibility as such but self-responsibility joined to concealment.
- **B4 — The largest sum lands on no one the page depicts.** The 101,534,644円 debt ends in a court notice (V09 i0104); by the page's own creditor's logic (V08 i0119) a discharged debt is the creditor's loss, and the creditor never appears as a person. The costs the page dwells on are not money, and they land on the nearest people.
- **B5 — Mochiko changes as a person, and her doctrine does not.** Her change toward Kurumi (V07–V09) is drawn through her interior before it is drawn in action, with antecedents in V04–V05 and a verified crossing of her two voices; it is also a change of narrative function. Her doctrine, her refusal to pay and her framing of help as interest do not change, and her motive stays open.
- **Strand (iv).** Mebuki's harm is self-authored throughout; Mochiko's conduct toward her is a confirmed and admitted wrong whose causal weight on the outcome the page declines to fix (section 9).

### 3.2 Obligations refused

Five refusals mark where the series will not let an obligation form:

- Mochiko's offer to lend Kurumi 300万 for trading (V01 i0065), refused partly for Mochiko's sake: "困るのは萌智子でしょ?" (i0066).
- Mebuki's request that Kurumi lend her 1,000,000 for trading (V04 i0067–i0071), refused on protective grounds — loss upon loss, and people who have died (i0071–i0072) — and afterwards grounded in her father's maxim (i0074).
- Kurumi's guess that Mochiko came to pay the debt, refused on a creditor's logic: "仮に私が 10億円持ってても絶対しないね / 立て替えた瞬間自己破産されたら泣き寝入りだし" (V08 i0119, re-opened).
- The father's demand for a vow never to trade again, refused because she might break it (V09 i0076–i0078).
- Mochiko's suggestion of welfare to escape the tax, refused: "体は健康だし! / 私は地道に払っていくよ!" (V09 i0106).

### 3.3 What the register shows

Three patterns hold across the ten rows; sections 5–10 argue them.

First, an obligation's fate follows knowledge more than amount. The same 300万 is a concealed breach in V01 and a gift in V09 (O1); Kozue's 2000万 and Mebuki's tuition are lost behind the backs of the people they belonged to (O2, O3); the debt that ends by discharge is the one Kurumi finally discloses (O6), and the reserve she once set aside for the tax is, by INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH, the stake she lost alone and unseen in V08 (O7).

Second, obligations between friends end by repayment or by an offer of severance, never by forgiveness. The one loan between friends is repaid on a schedule its lender calls "順調" while she offers release once the debt is paid (O5); no interest is shown returned and no apology for the lies is shown made. Mebuki defers her reproach until she owes nothing (V06 i0053).

Third, promises that bind only by being known fail, and those that are written down or made a condition hold: the V02 assent is broken within weeks; the no-lies rule, the contest prize and Mochiko's condition are kept; the father's vow is withdrawn rather than broken. The obligations the series honours are the ones that can be checked.

### 5.2 Kurumi's plan as an account of who pays

Kurumi's V08 plan is itself a theory of where cost lands. With the debt "1億円を超える", her 2014 tax and no affordable bankruptcy, she reasons that as she is single and childless her father is her heir, and that if he renounces the inheritance "1億円を超える借金も私の去年の税金もお父さんは一切を支払わずに済む" — "清算完了だ" (V08 i0047; SR HIGH; the logic as lettered, not compared with real law). Her accounting makes the money land on no one. The page shows its error in two registers. The letter that carries the plan would hand her father the discovery, the grief and the legal act she asks of him — "相続放棄を / してください——" across a two-page close-up (i0074–i0075) — and the childhood images drawn behind her words contradict the request (FXK-V08-E028). The narrator names the alternative and the obstacle: telling her father is the best course (i0035), and "罪悪感と幼稚な保身" bind her (i0036). V09 carries out the alternative: the debt ends at an institution, the tax stays with her, and the father offers to pay for the procedure. Her plan was right that the money need not land on her father, and wrong that her absence would cost him nothing.

### 5.3 The creditor no one sees

The broker that holds the largest debt in the series appears as a balance box (V07 i0118), an e-mail with a deadline and a rate (i0130), a wall of imperatives in her head — 「カネ払エ」「借金カエセ」「親ニ迷惑カケルナ」 (i0128, re-opened) — a supposition that it must be calling ("FX会社からも連絡きてるんだろうけどさ", Mochiko, V09 i0048) and, last, a court notice that names only the 破産者 and records that the 債権者集会 "は終結した" (V09 i0104, re-opened). The page twice makes the procedure legible — a narrator box on discretionary discharge (V08 i0032–i0033) and Mochiko's gag explainer of the alternatives (V09 i0017–i0019) — and states once whom a discharge costs: Mochiko will not pay Kurumi's debt because, if she did and Kurumi then went bankrupt, she would be left "泣き寝入り" (V08 i0119, re-opened). By that logic, which is a character's and is the only one the page gives, the unpaid balance of the discharged debt is the creditor's. No page shows the creditor as a person, a loss or a voice of its own; after i0104 the debt never returns, while the tax does (V09 i0106; V10 i0045, re-opened).

Kozue's 2008 loss is the contrast. Her account is printed at 1,005,144 and then 498,302 (V06 i0082, i0086), with no shortfall printed; on the page the whole of the ≈1900万 she lost (caption, V06 i0080; ANALYST CALCULATION 20,000,000 − 1,005,144 = 18,994,856) came out of persons' money — the household's, her parents' — and its last cost fell on her daughter. Kurumi's loss left her account at −101,534,644円, and that excess beyond anything she had left the persons' world. Whether that difference is the effect of an account regime is Study A's question; this study records only where the page puts the cost.

### 5.4 Patterns

- **The chooser pays when she bears the risk openly.** Mebuki in V05–V06 pays with her own money, then borrowed money, then wages; Yasuko in 2013 pays with her savings; Kurumi's two V06 hand cuts are paid by her alone. None of these exposes anyone who did not know.
- **Unknowing family pays when the chooser conceals.** Kozue's husband, her parents and her daughter; Mebuki's parents (their tuition money and their trust); Kurumi's father in V01, whose savings carried nearly all of a 300-lot position's risk (about 94% of the equity at the deposit; ANALYST CALCULATION 3,000,000 ÷ (3,811,600 − 629,000), conditional on the open loss at that moment) without his knowledge, and lost nothing only because the market turned.
- **Friends pay in time and standing, not money.** Yasuko spends nights and comfort on Mebuki and is insulted (V03 i0142; V04); Kurumi is accused for refusing a loan (V04 i0072); Mochiko spends nights in an exam period (V08–V09); Mebuki sends a support message and pushes her creditor to act (V08 i0108–i0110).
- **Lenders.** The friend who lent hedged herself so that she would "almost never lose" (V05 i0131) and is repaid; the institution that lent through the account absorbs, by the page's own logic, a debt it is never shown to feel.
- **No one.** Kurumi's V01 use of her father's cash, Mochiko's V02 steering until V07, Kurumi's contrarian use of Mebuki in V02 and the salon customer's leveraged trade on Mebuki's hunch (he profits, V06 i0040) land on no one because their outcomes were favourable. The early volumes let risk land on no one; the later ones show who had been carrying it. This is the obligation-side face of the consolidated V01 wording — an arc of early reward setting up later ruin (reconciliation audit §5.5 row 1): early reward postponed the question of who pays.

### 6.4 Two voices and their crossing

The observable spine of strand (i) is register, verified on the page. In V01–V05 Mochiko speaks softly and agrees in public and thinks coarsely in private. The private voice of V01 is masculine and contemptuous ("面白くない… / こんなんで利益出してんじゃねーよ… / そこは死んどけや——", V01 i0159, re-opened, in thought); the public formula of V03 is "うんとてもそう思う", spoken over a private "バッカじゃねーの!?" and a calculated "まぁ…ここは適当に合わせるか…" (V03 i0126, re-opened). V05 shows the formula's origin in a twelve-year-old at a shop counter: "うんそうだね / そっちも勉強がんばってね", and, when the schoolgirls have gone, "ばーか" (V05 i0031, re-opened) — a late revelation of a practice, not evidence of a cause.

Three things then happen in order. The private voice describes the tie's origin to itself (V07 i0045, re-opened, in a jagged burst; "言ってないからね" in a thought cloud beside it). The public voice turns candid and plain to Mebuki ("私は芽吹を利用して勝ちに行った", V07 i0017, re-opened), dropping the honorific Mebuki hears as closeness (i0018). And in V08 the V01 inner epithet is spoken aloud in a jagged balloon to Kurumi's face — "くるみは強欲の雌豚でしょうが!" (V08 i0126, re-opened) — as an appeal against self-erasure. The same coarse register that carried contempt behind a mask now carries care in the open (EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH).

Two qualifications keep the spine honest. The private voice was never uniformly contemptuous of Kurumi: on the same V03 page it defends her ("さすがにくるみをナメすぎだ…", i0126). And register is lettering, not performed voice: the crossing shows a change in what her coarse register does, not why. It is evidence that her development is drawn, not asserted; it is not evidence of motive.

### 6.5 A person's development, or a change of narrative function?

**For development of a person.** (1) Interior precedes action: V07 gives her the account of origin (i0045), kinship (i0053), an unfinished invitation (i0054), four withheld messages with her reasons (i0099–i0100) and her own question "私はいったいなにがしたかったんだろう…?" (i0137–i0139) before V08 gives her any act. A change of function alone would not need that record. (2) The change has antecedents: care-like acts toward Mebuki (the fare and coat, V04 i0118, i0120), toward Yasuko (V05 i0108–i0112) and toward Kurumi (V01 i0116) precede the turn, and relish at a friend's plight is absent from V05 on even when ruin is real and intimate (FXK-V08-C063, PRESERVE as revised). (3) The register crossing of section 6.4 is a change in how she speaks, not only in what she does.

**For a change of narrative function.** (1) After V07 the series needs a rescuer, and Mochiko is the one person who holds Kurumi's figure. (2) The turn is triggered from outside: Mebuki's push (V08 i0108–i0110) and a business rationale Mochiko accepts ("リピート率", i0109). (3) The rescue arrives framed as "投資" and "打算付き" (i0113–i0114), and its content in V09 is a job whose benefit to her company she names (V09 i0034, i0110). (4) Her role sequence — tempter (V01–V02), concealed counterparty (V03–V05), silent witness (V06–V07), rescuer (V08), employer (V09–V10) — is the kind of sequence a plot assigns.

**What does not change.** Her doctrine ("他人になにかできるわけないのに", V07 i0135; the refusal to pay, V08 i0119); her framing of help as interest; no apology to Mebuki for the lies and no interest returned on any page; no answer to Kurumi's V02 charge (V08 i0122).

**Finding (INFERENCE MODERATE).** Both readings are true and do not compete: her narrative function changes, and the page renders the change through her interior, with antecedents and a register change, as a person's change of conduct and attachment toward Kurumi. What changes is conduct and attachment; what persists is doctrine. Her stated beliefs, harsher than her conduct from V07 on, invert the V01–V05 relation in which her private conduct was harsher than her public speech. The motive under the change stays UNRESOLVED (FXK-V01-C005).

### 7.2 The re-enactment of V07–V08

The parallels are on the page (OBSERVATION HIGH): Kurumi's bank balance of 1,005,144 equals her mother's 2008 balance to the yen (V07 i0144; V06 i0082); she says "これじゃ私もお母さんとおんなじ——" (V07 i0147); a 「すみませんでした」 sheet in V08 redraws the composition of the note of V01 i0030 (V08 i0042; recall of her mother's note favoured at INFERENCE MODERATE, the status UNRESOLVED, and no claim is made that Kurumi wrote the words); she speaks to her mother's photograph, "待っててね…お母さん…" (V08 i0063–i0065), and writes "お母さんのところへ" (i0067). ### 7.3 The chosen disclosure, set against 2008

V02's disclosure was a discovery; V09's is the first chosen one in the family. The page supplies three conditions and does not rank them: a friend's ultimatum and her reading of the letter ("大人って言われたいならちゃんとお父さんに話しなよ / あんな遺書書くよりよっぽど簡単でしょ", V09 i0043; i0050); the narrator's verdict a volume earlier (V08 i0035); and Kurumi's own act, made after she separates herself from her mother at the grave ("私とお母さん似てるけど違うんだ", V09 i0061). The 300万's purpose, "もともとくるみのために貯めてた" (i0074), is a late revelation that re-reads V01; it is not a change in him.

### 7.4 What the strand shows about cost

In both generations the money is family money; what differs is consent. In 2008 the cost travelled away from the chooser to people who did not know; in 2015, after disclosure, it was carried by the person who knew and agreed. The clearest emblem is the father's management of Kurumi's wages "税金払い終わるまでは" (V09 i0107, re-opened): authority over money passes, by agreement, to the person who in V01 was the unknowing owner of the money she took (EVALUATION HIGH, FXK-V09-E043). Two rivals stay live. The change may be situational rather than a change of stance — a living daughter after a dead wife, the V02 slap and the V09 "怒らないから" being one man's answers to different stakes. And the family's money roles, which the father himself names ("家のことだけ"), may be the structural cause of the 2008 concealment, so that V09 shows a change in him rather than an effect of disclosure. The page does not decide between them. Open at V10: Kozue's parents are never addressed; whether the father learned of her losses otherwise before her death (V06-U09); whether he learns of the letter (V09-U07); whether the 300万 paid the costs (V09-U14).

### 8.2 責任, 迷惑 and 大人

**責任.** Read across persons, the series keeps four shapes of responsibility: a disclaimer that moves cost away from an adviser (Kurumi in V02, Mochiko in V04–V07); an acceptance of one's own choices that does not end care for others (Yasuko, V04–V05); the customer's acceptance of having followed advice (V06); and a duty converted into self-erasure (V08). The cleanest allocation in ten volumes is commercial: a paid stranger who states her uncertainty and a customer who owns his choice. Among friends, responsibility is disclaimed to end care or claimed to forbid help; only Yasuko joins acceptance of her own choices to a duty to intervene in another's, and she fails it once, in the moment (V04 i0143).

**迷惑.** The word of concealment toward parents. Mebuki: "ずっと迷惑かけっぱなしや…" and her reading of the security, "返済滞ったら親に迷惑かかってまう…" (V04 i0128); Kurumi: 「親ニ迷惑カケルナ」 (V07 i0128) and "誰にも迷惑かけちゃダメだ" (V07 i0149); the letter: 「迷惑をかけてごめんなさい。」 (V08 i0070). Each daughter hides what would let her parents help, so as not to be a burden, and so enlarges the burden she fears. 迷惑 is the hinge of thesis B1.

**大人.** Claimed as licence ("私はもう子供じゃない", V02 i0130; "子供じゃないんだよ〜?", V05 i0069); denied by the father, "そんな考え「大人」とは言えないな" (V02 i0030, re-opened); redefined by Mochiko as the capacity to tell one's father (V09 i0043); granted by the father with his opposition kept, "くるみももう大人なんだ" (i0083) and "それでも俺はやめろと言うぞ / 親・と・し・て・な" (i0084, re-opened). Adulthood moves from a licence to act alone to a capacity to disclose. The father's 俺 for his stance and 父さん for his care mark the same division in his own speech (FXK-V09-E035).

### 8.3 What the vocabulary shows

The series does not reject 自己責任; it tests what it is joined to. Joined to a disclaimer, it lets an adviser walk away (V02, V04); joined to care, it coexists with help (Yasuko, V05); joined to a correct allocation between strangers, it works (V06); joined to concealment, it forbids help and turns into a duty to disappear (V07–V08). V09 replaces the word with acts that share the cost, and keeps the one obligation that cannot be shared or discharged, the tax, as Kurumi's own. This is thesis B3 (section 10).

### 9.3 Argument

The pairs were framed as rivals about how much of Mebuki's holding (V03) and borrowing (V04) the page charges to Mochiko. V05–V09 do not move the agency finding: every order, deposit, add and refusal remains Mebuki's, and she ends by owning the outcome in her own words. On the other side they confirm that the aggravating conduct was real, deliberate and structural: the position lie was a lie (V05 i0145), the loan was built as the lender's hedge (V05 i0131), and the lender admits use without remorse (V07 i0017). On causation they run the other way: when Mebuki learned the truth she still did not close (V05 i0145 to V06 i0039), which weakens the strongest causal reading of the aggravation — that the lie removed her one exit — without settling it, since the situation of early December is not that of 15 October.

The two pairs therefore converge on a combined finding rather than a winner. The harm is self-authored (OBSERVATION HIGH on the acts; SELF_REPORT HIGH in V07). Mochiko's conduct toward Mebuki is a wrong of deception and of exploiting a known position, confirmed by the page and admitted by her (V03 §I EVALUATION HIGH; V04 §I INFERENCE HIGH; V05; V07), and the page declines to fix its causal weight on the outcome. Mebuki's own V07 sentence holds both at once, and that is the page's last word on the question. Her market losses are not graded as wrongs; the wrongs graded are Mochiko's deception and exploitation and Mebuki's concealment from her parents.

On this study's question the pairs give a clear answer. Mebuki paid all of it in money (her 3,600,000 of FX deposits, then interest whose total is never printed) and in a year of study; her parents paid in trust and in money they were told was being saved (V03 i0159); Mochiko paid nothing in money, realized +7,691,000 on her long in the scene of Mebuki's report of ruin, after her reply (V06 i0050–i0052; clock time unprinted), and offered release only once repaid (V07 i0017).

## 10. Cross-volume claims

Each claim names its downstream and contrary evidence, its rivals and what would overturn it. None rests on a market outcome treated as a wrong.

### B1 — Concealment carries the cost of a choice to people who do not know it; the series turns when a disclosure is chosen

**Claim.** In each family-money episode, the first cost of a choice falls on someone who did not know of it, and the one who conceals describes the concealment as protecting that person. The page shows that concealment does not protect them: it defers and enlarges the cost and threatens to hand them its worst form. When a disclosure is chosen (V09), the cost is carried by people who know and agree, and is bounded. **Label:** INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH as a cross-volume pattern on OBSERVATION HIGH bases; EVALUATION MODERATE as design.

**Evidence.** 2008: household money, insurance, a down-payment and her parents' cash lost behind their backs, with the secret laid on a child (V01 i0014; V06 i0071–i0082), for fear of "一家離散" (V06 i0082). V01: a 300-lot position carried on the father's savings without his knowledge (V01 i0109–i0111). V03–V07: Mebuki's tuition lost under her mother's "学費の貯金大変やろ?" (V03 i0159), her family's contact held as security on condition of silence (V04 i0118), "迷惑かけっぱなしや…" (V04 i0128), lies about school (V06 i0005). V07–V08: Kurumi's concealment from her father in the name of not burdening him (V07 i0128, i0149; V08 i0036–i0037), ending in a plan that would hand him discovery, grief and a legal act (V08 i0047, i0066–i0076). V09: disclosure (i0063) and a father who answers "怒らないから" (i0069), offers his savings (i0074) and takes on the management of her wages (i0107); the costs are then his by agreement. Outside the family, Mochiko's concealed long made Mebuki decide on false information (V03 i0147–i0148; V04 i0087). [Study A](./FX_KURUMI_SCREENS_INSTITUTIONS_AND_RESPONSIBILITY.md) (section 10) adds the institutional strand of the same transfers, merged with this claim at convergence: the broker's box records 「入金」, not whose money it is, and the persons relabel protected money as stakeable.

**Contrary.** (1) In V01 the concealment cost the father nothing, because the trade won (V01 i0157); the cost is risk made visible only by later outcomes. (2) Mebuki's parents are never shown learning (V06-U03); their cost is inferred from her own words and the narrator's, not shown in a scene. (3) V09's disclosure is pressed by a friend's ultimatum and her reading of the letter (i0043, i0050), so "chosen" is qualified. (4) The father's own account shares the blame for the 2008 silence (i0072–i0073): concealment was co-produced by the unknowing party's role. (5) Yasuko conceals her trading past from Mebuki (V02 i0076, i0083) with no cost shown.

**Rivals.** Concealment as a suspense engine rather than an argument (design); the family's money roles as the cause, of which concealment is a symptom (the father's "家のことだけ"); outcome luck, by which concealment is costly only when the market turns.

**Overturn.** A page on which a concealment of family money, through a bad outcome, spares its unknowing owner at no cost; or a disclosure that brings the feared collapse (contempt, a family broken). V09 shows the opposite.

### B2 — Between friends, credit for trading is harm, and care is not money

**Claim.** The only loan between friends in V01–V10 is the corpus's clearest exploitation, built so that the lender almost cannot lose and secured on the borrower's family. Every other proposed transfer of credit between friends is refused, by the lender, the borrower or a third friend who names the norm. The help the series validates between friends is presence, time, a question and work. Family money follows a different rule: harm when taken in concealment, help when offered openly. **Label:** INFERENCE MODERATE.

**Evidence.** The loan (V04 i0086–i0119; V05 i0131; V07 i0017). Refusals: of Mochiko's 300万 by Kurumi (V01 i0065–i0066), of Mebuki's request by Kurumi (V04 i0067–i0076), of paying Kurumi's debt by Mochiko (V08 i0119). Yasuko's norm, "借りる芽吹もアホだけど…貸すのは違うだろ…" (V04 i0143), and "おいおい友達から利息取ってんのか!?" (V05 i0131). Help as non-money: Yasuko's night search (V04 i0077–i0136), Mochiko's nights (V08), okayu and coaching (V09 i0013–i0052), a job (V09 i0034–i0035). Mebuki's joke at her creditor, "闇の金融屋さん" (V08 i0108). Family money: taken in V01, given in V09 (O1).

**Contrary.** Money passes between friends without harm in commerce and treats: Mochiko makes Yasuko pay as a client (V07 i0024) and pays full price at Mebuki's salon (V08 i0102); the contest prize is paid (V07 i0021–i0026); the friends feed Mebuki after her result (V07 i0012); the job's wages flow from Mochiko to Kurumi (V09–V10). Mebuki's loan is repaid and the tie survives (V09 i0114). The claim therefore holds for credit for trading, not for money as such.

**Rivals.** Prudential realism, a common norm rather than an argument of the work; Mochiko's character as the source of the harm (the loan is harmful because she made it). Against the second: the norm is held by Kurumi in both directions and stated by Yasuko as a norm, not as a judgement of Mochiko alone.

**Overturn.** A page on which a friend's loan for trading, or a friend's payment of another's trading debt, is rendered as care without harm.

### B3 — Responsibility borne alone and hidden disables help; shared, it becomes payable

**Claim.** The series tests 自己責任 by what it is joined to (section 8). Joined to concealment it forbids help and turns, in V08, into a duty to disappear; in V09 the word is absent, and responsibility becomes disclosure, payment "地道に" and accepted control, with the cost shared by family and an institution and the one obligation that cannot be shared or discharged, the tax, kept by Kurumi. **Label:** EVALUATION HIGH as a motif (every occurrence lettered); INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH as an argument.

**Evidence.** Section 8.1, every row; V09 i0063, i0079–i0082, i0106–i0107; V10 i0045.

**Contrary.** Mebuki bears her debt alone and comes through (V06–V09) — but she disclosed to her creditor at once (V06 i0051) and partly to her friends (V07 i0007–i0009), so her case separates solitude from concealment and supports the refinement. Yasuko's solitary "あたしの責任だよ" (V04 i0136) is healthy. Kurumi's mother accepted responsibility in words while concealing (V01 i0016), and her concealment was as costly, which shows that the active element is concealment (B1), with 自己責任 as the name Kurumi gives her own version of it. Mochiko never recants her doctrine in words (V07 i0135; V08 i0119). V10 lets a colleague re-describe the loss as greed (V10 i0045), and Kurumi lets the description stand: the series' last word on the vocabulary is not a vindication of sharing.

**Rivals.** The motif as a didactic device; the psychological reading of self-blame and shame (Study C's question, not decided here); the institutional reading, in which what makes the debt payable is the bankruptcy procedure, not the sharing (Study A's question).

**Overturn.** A page on which Kurumi's solitary concealment ends well without disclosure, or on which disclosure fails to bring help.

### B4 — The largest sum lands on no one the page depicts; the costs it dwells on are not money and land on the nearest

**Claim.** The 101,534,644円 debt ends in a court notice that names only the bankrupt and a creditors' meeting (V09 i0104). **Evidence.** Section 5.3; the bankruptcy drawn as comedy, the 官報 given as a present and "破産おめでと" (V09 i0103–i0105); the tax that "survives" (V09 i0106; V10 i0045).

**Contrary.** A 配当 is recorded with no assets stated (V09 i0104; UNRESOLVED): something reached the creditors. The creditor's logic is a character's reason for refusing, not a narrated fact. The father's 300万, if used (V09-U14), is a cost to a depicted person. The state, as creditor for the tax, is depicted through Kurumi's instalments.

**Rivals.** Design: the bankruptcy clears the plot quickly (FXK-V09-C002, a restoration comedy that dissolves the crisis too fast, STRENGTHENED by FXK-V10-C049). ### B5 — Mochiko changes as a person, and her doctrine does not

**Claim.** Mochiko's conduct and attachment toward Kurumi change between V07 and V09, and the page draws the change through her interior before her acts, with antecedents and a verified crossing of her two voices (sections 6.4–6.5). **Overturn.** A page showing a concealed interest beyond the stated one — a gain from Kurumi's loss, or a position against her on 15 January (V07-U15) — would turn the change into function plus concealment.

## 11. What changes over the series, and why

**The shape of change.** In V01–V02 the costs of concealed and disclaimed obligations are postponed by favourable outcomes: the father's savings and Mebuki's first stake land on no one. In V03–V05 they land on Mebuki and her family, through the only loan between friends and the only concealed adverse position, while Yasuko carries the duty to warn and is insulted for it. In V06–V07 knowledge without action (Mochiko) meets responsibility without disclosure (Kurumi), and the largest cost in the series falls on the person who will tell no one. In V08 the solitary accounting reaches its limit, and a friend pays in time rather than money. In V09 disclosure shares and bounds the cost: an institution extinguishes the debt, the father offers his savings and manages her wages, the tax stays hers. In V10 obligations are those of work; the veteran does not warn the newcomers, a newcomer bounds her own cost by a rule (Nana, V10 i0132), and every outcome lies beyond V10.

**Real change and late revelation, dated.** Real changes: Mochiko's conduct and attachment toward Kurumi (V07 inner record, V08 action, V09 offer); Kurumi's disclosure to her father (V09 i0063); the father's stance from denial of her adulthood (V02 i0030) to its recognition with opposition kept (V09 i0083–i0084), with the situational rival kept (section 7.4); Mebuki's turn from trading to wages (V06) and back to trading before repayment ends (V09). Late revelations, which re-read earlier obligations without changing them: Mochiko's 2012 "sample" thought and childhood (V05); Kozue's journal and the composition of the 2008 money (V06); the 300万's purpose (V09 i0074); the father's account of 2008 (V09 i0072–i0073); Yasuko's 2013 start and liquidation (V10 i0066–i0079), which gives her V03 warning "経験者からの忠告" (V03 i0141–i0142) a history the others never learn.

**Why, as the page states it.** The narrator names guilt and childish self-protection as what binds Kurumi (V08 i0036) and confession as the best course (i0035). The father names his own past paternalism as what made consultation hard (V09 i0073). Mochiko names adulthood as the capacity to tell one's father (V09 i0043). Kurumi names her separation from her mother (V09 i0061) and her wish not to have her life undone (i0082). These are the page's explanations, given by narrator and characters (NARRATOR_PRESENTATION and SELF_REPORT); this study adopts none as settled cause.
