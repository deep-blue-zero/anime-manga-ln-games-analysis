Her case: the central bank's unlimited intervention at 1.20 makes a fall below it temporary and possibly the trigger of an intervention rally; resting stops would be "hunted". She named a danger (clustered stop orders at 1.20 in the thin early-morning hours, a cascade, "買い方全滅") and reasoned it away; she never treated the policy itself as able to end. Her stated exits: retire "if the currency rises" ("今勝負してる通貨が上がれば 引退予定"), or a discretionary switch if a clearer currency appears in about a month. Last printed balance 18,339,915円 after two realized V06 losses on 300-lot USD/JPY shorts (−1,743,000 at 18:27 on 5 December, then a re-short at 121.220 cut at 121.597 for −1,131,000), the first printed realized Kurumi losses in the corpus. By conditional calculation the long is about 24× her balance and about 3.7 million yen per 0.01 of EUR/CHF (unit convention and cross rate from V06's own board; illustrative). She has read her mother's 2008 journal, vowed "次は絶対勝つからね", refused to "withdraw half-way", and recast her two stops as mistakes ("やっぱ損切りいらなかったじゃん", "含み損耐えるべきだった"), though the same night's 121.597 vindicated the first cut. At Christmas: cheerful convenor; wrote a no-lies rule against Mochiko's V03 interim lie; teased Yasuko ("思ったより低レバだね"); burnt the kalbi. She knows Mebuki quit FX and has a job she is working at on Christmas (source unshown); she does not know of the wipe-out, the loan or that the job is the salon.
