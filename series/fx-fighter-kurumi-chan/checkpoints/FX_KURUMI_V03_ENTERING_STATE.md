- **福賀くるみ (Kurumi), 20, second-year at 新都大学.** Her project rests on her mother's 2009 suicide after an FX loss of about 2000万円 of household money; her target is 2000万円, now framed as "自分の人生を始めるため", earned "in the name of self-responsibility". In V02 her father discovered the missing money and took it back; he slapped her once, rejected her claim to be an adult, said that needing borrowed money to win was already failure, and begged her to stop; she said "…うん". She announced her retirement, attributed her win to luck, became Mebuki's unpaid market forecaster at Mochiko's request, kept her FX account open, funded a domestic account with 2,100,000円 (the deposit is undated), and relapsed on 30 June 2014 after an allegorical inner struggle in which she cast off her gentle child-self and licensed herself by adulthood ("大人", ruby "20歳") and 自己責任, vowing to fight "たとえこの身が滅ぶことになろうとも". She is kind to Yasuko and finds the contest "ちょっと面白そう". She still works a convenience-store job (last seen early in V02). Evidence sufficiency HIGH.
