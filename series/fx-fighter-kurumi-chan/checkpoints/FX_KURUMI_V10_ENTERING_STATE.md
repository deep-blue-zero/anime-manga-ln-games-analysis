- **The story so far, in brief.** Kurumi's mother (福賀梢) lost about 2000万 of household money in FX and died by suicide on 13 February 2009, when Kurumi was 15 (V01). At 20, in 2014, Kurumi began trading FX to win the money back, secretly using her father's 300万 savings (returned to him on discovery, V02), and became close to 小金萌智子, a wealthy, market-watching student with a private contempt for her and real care in action, to 山師芽吹 and to 高根やす子 (V01–V06). On 15 January 2015 a franc move left her owing −101,534,644円 to たのしいFX (V07); she reached the brink of suicide and was stopped by Mochiko (V08). In V09 she chose bankruptcy, confessed to her father, refused his demand that she never trade again ("次に投資をやりたくなったそのときは 堂々とやりたいんだ"), was discharged (gazette, decision dated 平成27年11月), and declared to her friends that she wants to come back to the market someday "相場が大好きだから".
- **Date and frame.** V09 (strip 「第9集 共生」) ran 第41話–第45話, from the morning after V08's last night (undated, early 2015) through 「2015年2月上旬」 and 「2015年秋」 to 「2017年4月3日 月曜日」. Its last chapter changed viewpoint to a newcomer, 酒田いなご, on her first day at (株)もちもち企画 (room 201, 「新都ビルヂング」, 大都市大都区大都町○○○). No account, order, position or chart of hers is shown anywhere in V09.
