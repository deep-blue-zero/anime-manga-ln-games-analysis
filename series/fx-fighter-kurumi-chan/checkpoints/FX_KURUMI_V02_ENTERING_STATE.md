## Known states entering V02 (accepted through V01)

Every state below comes from the [V01 reading's exit freeze](../readings/FX_KURUMI_V01_DEEP_READING.md#p-exit-freeze-state-at-v01-end-i0162) and its observations.

- **Position and money (end of May 2014 trading, V01 i0162).** 福賀くるみ (Fukuga Kurumi), 20, a second-year student at 新都大学, is flat after closing an AUD/JPY long of 300枚 (average 94.434) at 94.872 for a realized +1,311,510円. Her account balance is 5,123,110円. Of that, 3,000,000円 is her father's タンス預金, which she took from a drawer without his consent and deposited as margin. It is not shown returned. His month-end "タンス預金更新日" in May 2014 is still ahead, and she had planned to return the money "without being noticed" by month-end. By calculation her own share is 2,123,110円, from a 300,000円 start in February 2014. Her stated target is 2000万円, and her stated rule was to quit FX once she had won ("勝ち逃げ"). Neither has been tested: she has not reached the target and has not invoked the rule. No stop-loss order has ever been shown in her trading. Her overseas account has very high leverage (about 59× on 50枚 by the narrator's own figure); the broker's stop-out level and margin rate are never printed.
- **Kurumi's inner state at exit.** At the nadir she called her past wins luck, said she had no talent for FX, identified herself with her dead mother ("これじゃ私も… お母さんとおんなじ——") and stopped watching the screen. After the win she cried, laughed and embraced Mochiko. She drew no lesson on the page. Whether the win taught caution or reinforced holding and averaging down is open. Her project rests on her mother's 2009 suicide after an FX loss of about 2000万円 of household money, and on her wish to prove that the outcome was avoidable.
- **小金萌智子 (Kogane Mochiko).** A 20-year-old third-year student and stock investor since high school, believed by Kurumi to hold at least 1000万円. She lives in a high-rise flat with a multi-monitor rig and a white cat. In public she is a teasing, congratulating friend who tipped AUD/JPY, urged an all-in buy, offered a 300万円 loan and later urged Kurumi to look at her position. In thoughts the reader sees and Kurumi does not, she has "observed" Kurumi for three months, calls her "強欲の雌豚", had written her off as "戦死" and "いい肥やし", and resented her profit ("面白くない… そこは死んどけや", mutter or thought). She does not know of the father's money or the 300-lot size; Kurumi told her a lie about a partial cut. No position of hers that would gain from Kurumi's loss has been shown.
- **あふぃちゃん.** A 20-year-old blogger trader in a high-rise flat, known only by the handle あふぃちゃん. Her possessions are lettered "Yasuko"; that this is her given name is an exposure-capped inference (MODERATE-HIGH). She cut an AUD/JPY long at a large loss, then reversed into a full-size short ("全力"), vowing to retire from FX if it failed. The market reversed against her; the result is not shown. She has had no contact with Kurumi or Mochiko.
- **The father (unnamed).** Unaware of Kurumi's trading and of the withdrawal. He warned her against FX in 2009 and knew his wife had done FX (inference).
- **The mother (unnamed).** Dead since 13 February 2009. Present as motive, as the presumed owner of the hidden "FXトレード日誌", and as a spectre at the nadir. AUD/JPY was her main currency, by Kurumi's account.
- **Minor.** 優子 (Yūko), a friend offstage (the 2008 and 2014 Yūko identity is an inference). 芽吹 (Mebuki) has been named once, offstage, as someone who cannot work out how to open an account; she has never appeared on the page. 山口さん is a co-worker named once in comparison.
- **Relationships.** Kurumi → Mochiko: trust, gratitude, affection, one lie. Mochiko → Kurumi: public warmth and help in effect; private contempt and enjoyment of anticipated ruin; understanding of Kurumi's risk profile high; exploitation not shown. Kurumi → father: love, concealment and an unreturned, unconsented use of his savings.
