## C. Current-volume story synopsis

**Cold open (2014年5月).** In colour, a bob-haired figure hunches at a desk in a dark room. A chart of falling blue
candles fills the screen, with clicking SFX "カチ". She says "神様 お願いします…" and "このお金は ダメなんです…". A
platform field reads **評価損益 −3,251,490円**, beside large emphasis-dotted lettering: "このお金は 絶対 溶かせないん
です…!" (i0003). The title spread follows: "叶うなら / 時間を巻き戻してください——". Narrator boxes ask why people
cannot see they stand at the edge of despair until the moment comes (i0004+i0005). The volume then explains how
she got here.

**2008–2009: the mother's loss.** Over 2008 news headlines (サブプライムローン問題, パリバショック, リーマンショック;
i0006), **福賀くるみ (Fukuga Kurumi), 15, 中学三年生**, comes home in the rain on Friday 24 October 2008 (i0008). The
house is dark and neglected. Behind a door marked "ぱぱとままのへや♡", her mother screams at a chart: "最後に一回
くらい 勝たせなさいよ!", "誰よ上がるって 言った人!!", "2000万円 返せぇぇぇ!!" (i0010–i0011). The parents' room is
strewn with beer cans (i0012+i0013). Kurumi's retrospective narration explains that her mother had been doing "FX"
and had used about 2000万円 of household money without permission. The mother begged her to keep it from the father,
who was posted away from home. Kurumi agreed, fearing a divorce (i0014). Alone, Kurumi resolves to win back the
2000万円 before her father finds out (i0015). She asks to work. Her mother refuses ("これは私の責任なんだから"),
tells her to keep studying, and starts working long hours herself (i0016–i0017). Kurumi reads FX self-help books
aimed at housewives. She reasons that anything that can easily melt 2000万円 can just as easily earn it (i0018–i0019).
Finding that FX needs her to be 20 (18 with parental consent), she decides to study until then (i0020). In December
2008 her classmate **優子 (Yūko)** warns her that many people who fail at FX kill themselves (i0021). Kurumi starts
watching her worn-out mother and silently wonders whether she is thinking of suicide (i0022–i0023).

On Friday 13 February 2009, the day her father is due home, Kurumi urges her mother to confess. She promises she
will not let them divorce and that she herself will "definitely win the money back" (i0024–i0025). Her mother calls
this naïve ("あなた舐めてるでしょ?"). Kurumi shouts "誰のせいで こんなことに なってンだっけ!?", slams down paper and
pen, and orders her mother to write out what she will say to the father (i0026–i0028). Kurumi goes out. When she
returns, the paper bears only "すみませんでした", and her mother is gone (i0029–i0030). The father arrives. The
police telephone: **the mother has died by suicide, jumping** (i0031–i0033). At the funeral Kurumi thinks "私が
殺した…?!" (i0034). Then she beats a stuffed bear, screaming that her mother was too stupid for middle-school maths
and that it would not have happened if she had relied on Kurumi, "if I weren't a child" (i0035–i0036). A collage of
tender memories of her mother follows (i0037). In April 2009 the father moves to commuting from home. While
unpacking, Kurumi finds a notebook, "FXトレード日誌", and hides it. When her father says "FXなんて するんじゃない
ぞ…", she answers "わかってるよ…" with the diary behind her back (i0038–i0039).

**February 2014: the plan and the first trade.** Five years on, Kurumi calls her project "多分これは自己満足の戦い
だ": she wants to prove that if she had tried hard her mother would not have died (i0040). On Friday 14 February
2014 she is 20, a second-year student at a commuting university she chose to reassure her father, and a
half-tuition scholarship student (特待生). She has saved 30万円 from part-time work (i0040–i0041). Her strategy is
explicit: FX has no sure-win method; her 2000万円 target needs risk; long-term winning is impossible, so she will
force a short decisive campaign, take risk early while losses are small, use an overseas dealer, and above all
**quit** once she has won ("FXをやめること——!!", "勝ち逃げだ!") (i0042–i0045). Her account box shows 300,000円 (i0044).
She sells USD/JPY 10枚 at 102.131 (i0046). She watches the unrealized gain beat a day's wage from her part-time job,
fantasizes about "秒給1000円", and closes on a rebound about five minutes later (i0047–i0048): +24,200円 at
101.889, balance 324,200円 (i0050; a box on i0049 says +24,000). She ranks herself above a co-worker (山口さん) and
her father ("もう…なにもできない子供じゃないんだよ…?") (i0049). She ends Chapter 1 laughing and crying, "お母さん——",
"絶対!! 取り返してやる!" (i0050).

**May 2014: the streak, the friend and the mother's currency (第2話).** About three months later (i0051), a montage
shows a winning streak, "累計50万円以上の利益" (i0052). Kurumi's own primer diagrams show only profitable outcomes
(i0053–i0054). She calls the 2000万円 "奪われた" (stolen) (i0055). In May 2014 her balance is 811,600円, but the
market is quiet (i0056). At 新都大学 she meets **小金萌智子 (Kogane Mochiko), 20, 大学三年生**, an investing friend
since Kurumi's first year, who has traded stocks since high school and, Kurumi believes, holds at least 1000万円
(i0058, i0066). Kurumi privately wants 2000万円 "今年中に" and wishes for a "大事件" to move the market (i0059). She
recasts the Lehman shock, the event that ruined her mother, as a chance others profited from, and plans to raise her
lot size (i0060–i0061). At lunch ("WcDon…") Mochiko tips **AUD/JPY** (i0061–i0062). Kurumi silently recognizes it
as her mother's main currency, "the currency that killed her", and pictures the diary (i0062). Mochiko urges an
all-in buy of 100枚 for "ワンチャン100円台" (i0064). She also offers a **300万円 loan** in a sinister panel; Kurumi
refuses: "借りるわけないじゃん", and asks who would be hurt if it failed: "困るのは萌智子でしょ?" (i0065–i0066). During the lunch Mochiko takes a call from **芽吹ちゃん (Mebuki)**, who
cannot work out how to open an account (i0066). Kurumi forms her own, more modest view (a 96円 ceiling) and **buys
AUD/JPY 50枚 at 95.610** on her phone, expecting about +20万 (i0067–i0068). Mochiko asks "マジで買ったの?", stifles a
laugh, and says "いいと思うよ! 多分上がる!", with a lettered aside "あははっ ウケるっ" (i0069). Alone, Mochiko thinks
she herself would wait and SELL at 96 with a stop above, that buying in size here is pointless, and "まぁ買い煽ったの
私だけど…" (i0070).

That evening, at her convenience-store job, Kurumi sneaks a look: 95.889, unrealized +139,500円, equity 951,100円
(i0072). She rings up customers on a high of "ミリオネアパワー" (i0073). An editorial narrator explains that her
500,000-unit position is worth about 47.8M円, about 59× her funds; that Japan caps leverage at 25×; and that her
overseas dealer is outside that cap (i0074–i0075). Across a page turn the narrator concludes: "彼女がやっていること
の本質は—— ただの 博打(ギャンブル)なのだ" (i0075–i0077). At home she declines to take about +10万, wanting at least
20万 (i0078). Then the position turns: −57,500 (i0079), then 95.422 (i0080–i0081). A box reads "評価損失 −94,000円",
captioned "初めてくるみはその重さを知る——" (i0082).

**Holding the loss (第3話).** The loss deepens to −105,000 (i0083). Kurumi admits "完全に欲張った", "浮かれてた"
(i0084). She considers a textbook loss-cut (i0085). A narrator explainer defines 損切り and jokes about manga
cancellation (i0086). The narrator says a cut must be quick and calm; she dithers ("決める!…次で!", "…いや 待て…")
(i0087) and refuses: "**やっぱ損切りは なしッ…!!**". The narrator concedes "それができたら 相場は どれだけ簡単か"
(i0088). She talks herself into holding: a feeling it will "get interesting", "長い目で見たら", a crouch before a jump
to 96円 (i0089–i0090). She holds overnight as a "勝負" (i0091). Three days later in the student lounge, AUD/JPY is
95.065 and Kurumi is down −272,500. Mochiko privately laments missing her own planned short and thinks she is still
"better off than Kurumi" (i0092–i0093). Kurumi asks for reassurance that this is a "騙し下げ". Mochiko says "うん
そうだといいね", thinking "知らねー…" (i0094). Mochiko privately thinks "損切りすりゃいいのに…下手クソかよ" and asks
aloud "もしかして 強制ロスカット されそうなの?". Kurumi says she will cut before that. Mochiko: "…へぇ… お上手だね"
(i0095).

The next night AUD/JPY is 94.619, −495,500 (i0096). Kurumi builds a thesis of "鉄板" buy support at 94円, "我慢の
とき", "損切りさえしなければ" (i0097). Then she sees her margin ratio at 330.4% and realizes her own forced-liquidation
line lies "around 94" (i0098). The narrator explains forced liquidation as investor protection (i0099). Kurumi
pictures the dealer as executioner ("奴らは 問答無用で 狩りにくる!", ¥811,600 cut by a reaper) (i0100). The narrator
retorts that mechanical execution is "むしろ…温情", and Kurumi says "…え?" (i0101). At −629,000 she screams "やだやだ"
(i0102), then "死にたくない…っ!". Clutching a photo of her mother: "親子揃って… 絶対負けるわけに いかない…"
(i0103). "お金さえ あれば…!" brings back Mochiko's loan offer (i0104). The next morning she sees her father off
cheerfully (balloon attribution MODERATE) and decides "やるしかない…!" (i0105). As she pulls open a drawer in the chest
by the landline, unballooned display lettering shouts "最強の一手 借金っ!" (i0107; speaker uncertain). The narrator says
she meant to solve the imminent problem by depositing more, which lowers the liquidation line. She needs no loan from
Mochiko, she thinks, "だって知ってるから", and takes her **father's タンス預金**, cash he has added
at every month-end since the mother's death. She holds three bundles: "きっとこの「300万円」は 福賀家再興の証し——"
(i0108–i0109). In tears she says "手を付けちゃいけないお金…!", "失敗したら完全に終わり…!", "…でも これだけあれば…
負けようがない…っ!" (i0110).

**The household money (第4話).** Under a storm, the narrator says "家のお金に 手をつけてしまった——". A box shows the
balance at **3,811,600円 (+3,000,000入金)** (i0111). Kurumi makes one promise: if 94 breaks, "容赦なく損切りする"

と思ってね…", "生きてる?" (i0116). Kurumi imagines saying she put in the household money, but lies: "一部損切り…して
凌いでるよ…?" (i0116). Mochiko calls it "五分五分" and openly wants 94 to break so the "クソ相場" ends: "安定した相場
なんて トレーダーの敵じゃん?". Kurumi answers "そんな相場 いりません…っ!" (i0117–i0118). The price bounces;
Mochiko says "さすがにここは反発するかぁ… おめでと"; Kurumi hangs up wishing for a "平和な相場" (i0119).

## D. Governing thesis and live competing thesis

**Governing thesis (EVALUATION, MODERATE-HIGH).** V01 is an education in loss whose plot overturns its own lessons.
Kurumi's money is never just money. From i0040 every trade tests a counterfactual about her dead mother ("if I had
tried, she would not have died"). From i0062 the instrument is the mother's own currency. From i0109 the stake is
the father's savings, which Kurumi takes to be (きっと) "福賀家再興の証し". A didactic narrator states the rules three
times (cut losses quickly and calmly, i0086–i0088; liquidation is mercy, i0101; prospect theory explains why people
cannot cut, i0145–i0147). The narrator condemns each escalation ("ただの博打", i0077; "ただの衝動買い", i0128). The
plot then pays out on the worst process in the volume: an unconsented deposit of household money, a broken stop
promise, two rounds of averaging down to six times her first AUD size, prayer and avoidance. The arithmetic of the
close makes the point more sharply than any verdict. At 94.872 the original 50 lots, the position she refused to cut,
lost 369,000円. The two averaging-down adds made 560,000円 and 1,120,500円, and the 150-lot add that the narrator called
an impulse buy produced about 85% of the net (E043; ANALYST CALCULATION, outcome arithmetic only). The foil runs the
other way: あふぃちゃん's textbook cut realizes a 大損, and her immediate full-size doten is then caught by the reversal,
with no result shown. The page aligns the textbook act with a bad night and the rule-breaking hold with a windfall. So
the volume's main effect is not ruin but **reinforcement in structure**: it ends on a win that rewards the very
behaviour the page has called gambling. Whether Kurumi takes the win as confirmation is not shown; that is a V02
question. The volume leaves the moral debt (the father's 300万) and the epistemic debt (no lesson drawn) open under "TO
BE CONTINUED…". The psychological core is not ignorance. Kurumi can state every rule (i0042–i0045, i0085, i0112). What the page shows
is how grief-bound identity, a deadline and paper-wealth euphoria turn knowledge into rationalization, and then
into a felt incapacity to realize a loss (i0131, i0135).

**Live competing thesis (HYPOTHESIS, MODERATE).** V01 is a black comedy of chance in which competence is not what
decides outcomes, and the moral reading over-reads it. Mochiko, the experienced trader, misses her own planned
short entry (i0092), judges Kurumi "dead" twice (i0154, i0157), and is wrong. あふぃちゃん does what the narrator
prescribes, realizes a 大損, and is then caught on the short she re-risked. Kurumi wins by not looking. The narrator is
unreliable or ironic as a moralist: it calls her aversion "in a sense courageous" (i0143) just before the rescue, and
it jokes about the manga's own cancellation (i0086). On this reading the market is an indifferent trickster
("あざ笑うかのように", i0150). The volume's subject is spectatorship: the reader's and Mochiko's pleasure in watching
someone stand on the edge. It is not a lesson about process. The flap (i0002) and the publisher's blurb (i0166) are apparatus,
classified only. They carry no weight in choosing between the theses. The story author's afterword (i0165) is an authorial extra and is not cited.

**Why the governing thesis is preferred, provisionally.** The page repeatedly ties the trades to the mother (i0050,
i0062, i0103, i0139–i0140) and the father (i0109–i0113, i0124, i0138). It frames the win with unresolved debt and a
withheld lesson, and it lets Mochiko voice the outcome-versus-process gap herself ("こんなんで利益出してんじゃねーよ",
i0159). The competing thesis stays live because V01 never confirms that the win will be punished or reinforced. That
is a V02 question (MAIN_EXIT FX-3).

   counter to it). あふぃちゃん, with the same model applied to her by the narrator, does cut (i0145). So the model
   describes a tendency, not a determinism. *Status*: page-asserted, strongly fitting.
2. **Risk-seeking in the loss domain; break-even chasing.** *Behaviour*: "90%の確率で 死ぬより 50%の確率で 生き残る"

## J. Page form

**J.1 How facing pages were determined.** See §A.4: rtl progression from the OPF, folio parity (p.8 = i0010 right,
p.9 = i0011 left), and art continuity for spreads. Odd/even pairing was not assumed to make a spread.

**J.2 Page turns and openings as dramaturgy.**
- *Reveals after a turn* (odd → even):
  - the note "すみませんでした" (i0029 → i0030);
  - the quitting condition "FXをやめること——!!" (i0043 → i0044);
  - "お母さん——" over the WIN box (i0049 → i0050);
  - the narrator's verdict "ただの 博打なのだ" (i0075 → i0076+i0077);
  - "評価損失 −94,000円" (i0081 → i0082);
  - the retrace after "やったか!?" (i0121 → i0122);
  - the break of 94 after the wordless spike (i0133 → i0134);
  - the prayer tableau (i0135 → i0136);
  - the result boxes after the wordless embrace (i0161 → i0162).
- *Reveals on a facing page*: the phone rings (i0032) facing the police call and the death (i0033). The book puts
  the dread and its answer in one glance.
- *Chapter boundaries inside openings* (FXK-V01-E038): each chapter ends on a right page facing the next chapter's
  first page. In book form, magazine cliffs become juxtapositions: the kneeling "神様——" (i0136) beside the
  −3,251,490 screen and "ほんと どうしよう…" (i0137). Prayer and the unanswered number sit side by side.
- *Mid-scene chapter break*: 第3話 begins with only an edge tab (i0083) and continues the second-by-second loss.
  Chapter structure yields to market time.

**J.3 Screens vs faces; numbers vs distress (owner objective F).**
- Small, crisp numbers face huge, sweating faces: "利益 どこ いった?" (i0079); the three identical flicker tiers
  over a trembling body (i0130); the panting balloons that wallpaper i0137.
- The numbers are always exact and internally consistent. The faces show what the numbers mean to her.
- At the nadir the number itself breaks up: the glitched "−3,[?]4[?],[4?]90" (i0141). Form enacts "見てられない".
  The objective value exists, but the subject can no longer read it.
- Conversely, the paper gain of +139,500 is felt as "ミリオネアパワー" and a cherry-blossom rapture (i0072–i0073).
  Subjective value runs far ahead of objective value.

**J.4 Register and tonal dissonance.** Three registers alternate, often on one page:
- *gag and chibi* (USA!! USA!!, i0045; "わーい 近くなったよー", i0123; space-blank "なにも考えていない", i0128; the
  editor-in-chief cancellation joke, i0086; "〜ポジ" drones);
- *shōnen and battle* (aura SFX, "発射", "天王山", the RPG pillar "この先、すごいお宝。", i0112; the 風林火山 doten,
  i0149);
- *horror* (the mother's uncanny dark balloons, i0026; the reaper slicing ¥811,600, i0100; the body-horror cavity
  "内臓を掻き回される", i0131; the looming spectre, i0140).

The comic register often carries the harshest verdicts (i0128). The horror register arrives exactly where family
money and the mother converge (i0131, i0140). あふぃちゃん lives mostly in the comic register (a gag slump, desk-trashing)
while Kurumi is in horror. The page grades their stakes (INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH).

**J.5 Does the page endorse or question the character's interpretation?**
- *Questioned*:
  - her elation (verdict over the skipping, i0076+i0077);
  - her hunter frame for liquidation (narrator "温情", "…え?", i0101);
  - her "not a strange move" (a plunging candle drawn beside the words, i0125);
  - the "just averaging" justification ("衝動買い… 呑気に正当化", i0128);
  - the "bottom" belief (light imagery at i0132 undone by the wick at i0133);
  - the bullish spike (devil wings, i0121);
  - the loan offer (vines, slurp SFX, i0065).
- *Endorsed or ambiguous*:
  - the 94 support "holds" twice (i0119–i0120);
  - the narrator states the margin arithmetic of the deposit as "事実" (i0107);
  - the narrator calls the aversion "ある意味では勇気" (i0143);
  - the plot then rewards the hold (i0157).

The volume's form condemns the process and its plot rewards the outcome. That split is the volume's main formal
tension (§D).

**J.6 Dread devices.**
- Ticking-clock SFX ("チッ") across i0113, i0120, i0129, i0137 and i0152.
- Heartbeat SFX ("ドクン", "ドッ ドッ ドッ").
- Storm and lightning at the deposit (i0111).
- Silent or near-silent pages: i0012, i0133 and i0160+i0161 are wordless; i0136 is a single-panel tableau with minimal
  lettering ("神様——" and one caption).
- Callbacks: "時間を巻き戻してください——" (i0005) → "時間は 巻き戻せない——" (i0032). The Lehman headlines (i0006)
  return as Kurumi's opportunity sketch (i0060).
- Melt and sinking imagery (E041).
- The cold open as prolepsis. The reader carries −3,251,490 from p.1 and watches the arithmetic close in (i0130
  residue → i0137).

**J.7 Reader alignment.** Chapter 1 is narrated in Kurumi's first person (私), which aligns the reader with her
grief. From Chapter 2 a third-person editorial narrator (くるみが, 彼女) explains, judges and addresses the reader
("あなたなら", i0146). The reader is given privileged access to Mochiko's thoughts (i0070, i0093–i0095,
i0155–i0159). The reader therefore knows more than Kurumi about her friend. The final lyrical spread (i0160+i0161)
shows the friendship from Kurumi's side, with Mochiko's face withheld. The last page's dark screentone and Mochiko's
flat "よかったね…" (i0162) keep the reader's knowledge in play.

## L. Counterreadings (mandatory)

1. **"The win is earned, not lucky."** Kurumi's 94-support and retest thesis (i0097, i0105, i0124) was roughly right.
   Her averaged positions bought near the eventual low, and holding through the spike may be read as conviction. The
   page resists this reading. The narrator calls the adds "なにも考えていない" (i0128). Her own promise to cut below 94
   was broken (i0135). She herself attributes past wins to luck at the nadir (i0139). Mochiko's frame ("運よく流れに
   乗って", i0156) treats any win as luck. The counterreading stays live only as a character-level possibility for
   V02.
2. **"The narrator's 'courage' line endorses avoidance."** "ある意味では勇気があった" (i0143) can be read straight:
   by not watching she avoided a panic close at the low. It can also be read as irony, or as a set-up for あふぃちゃん's
   contrast (she watched, cut, re-risked and lost). The hedge "ある意味では" and the earlier condemnations favour
   irony or set-up. The plot's reward complicates both.
3. **"Mochiko is not malicious; she is a blunt, competitive friend."** Evidence: the check-in call (i0116), the
   liquidation question (i0095), candour about her contrary wish (i0117), "おめでと" (i0119), the urging that led Kurumi
   to look (i0155), the returned hug (i0162). Her private coarseness may be in-group trader humour. "肥やし" can mean
   a lesson to learn from. "死んどけや" may be hyperbolic frustration at a contradicted theory. Against this: the
   dehumanizing "強欲の雌豚", the instrumental "肥やし", the systematic public/private split, and the undisclosed
   contrary view when urging the trade (i0070). The reading keeps "enjoyment of anticipated ruin" as a stated private
   attitude and does not claim intentional harm.
4. **"Mochiko's Chapter 2 push was a test or a joke, not encouragement."** Her "…だよね" on Kurumi's refusal (i0065),
   her surprise "マジで買ったの?" (i0069), and her laughter fit a teasing or testing reading. Under this reading
   Kurumi took a joke seriously. The admission "買い煽ったの私だけど" (i0070) shows she knew she had pushed. So
   negligence is still the minimum classification.
5. **"Kurumi's use of the father's money was a loan, not theft."** The display lettering at i0107 calls it "借金" (speaker uncertain), she plans to return it by
   month-end (i0113), and the money ends intact inside 5,123,110 (i0162). Against this: no lender is asked and no consent was sought, the
   act was concealed by design ("気付かれずに"), and she knew it was "手を付けちゃいけない" (i0110). The reading
   classifies it as an unconsented, concealed use of family savings with repayment intent. "Theft" and "loan" are
   both rejected as labels.
6. **"The mother's death is not caused by Kurumi's confrontation."** The page orders the events (demand, accusation,
   errand, note, death) and gives Kurumi's guilt, but never states causation. Yūko's warning (i0021) and the
   mother's prior deterioration (i0022–i0023) show a pre-existing trajectory. The reading treats Kurumi's
   responsibility as her belief, not as a narrative finding.
7. **"The volume is pure entertainment; the didactics are parody."** Explainer gags undercut the narrator (i0086). This
   supports the competing thesis (§D). The light publisher blurb ("ちょっとキケン(!?)", i0166) points the same way, but
   it is apparatus and, as §D says, carries no weight between the theses. The suicide plot, the body horror and the
   father's savings weigh against a purely comic reading. (Version 1.1 removes the story author's afterword, i0165, as
   support: it is an authorial extra and is not cited.)
8. **"The foil teaches that cutting is futile."** あふぃちゃん cut, realized a 大損, and was then caught by the reversal.
   But the reversal hit her "全力" doten, not the cut as such (i0149–i0151), and the result of the short is not shown.
   The page does not show the cut alone as harmful. The governing thesis (§D) now says the same.
