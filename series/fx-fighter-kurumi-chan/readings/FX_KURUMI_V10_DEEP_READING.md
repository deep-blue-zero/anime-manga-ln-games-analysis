## C. Current-volume story synopsis

## D. Governing thesis and live competing thesis

### D.1 Governing thesis (C001)

**V10 moves the series' market from Kurumi's body to her workplace and makes her its silent witness: on one evening the narrator sows
"欲望の種" in four women, and the volume watches the seeds open as three different uses of money for selfhood — Kizuna's net worth as
existence, Nana's shares as fandom rank, Inago's inherited suspicion — while Kurumi, the only one at that table who has lived the outcome, conceals the
scale of it, rehearses the old question ("もう一度 相場の世界で 戦えるだろうか?") inwardly, and is left asking the price of someone else's bet.**

The thesis rests on four page-level facts.

1. **The narrator names the mechanism and distributes it.** 資産運用 is defined as mere money-handling, "ただし / 人が その行為に 踏み出す
   とき / その 根底には / 必ず / 欲望が 存在する" (NP, i0052–i0053), and the seeds sown "この日" wait "それぞれ違う形で" (i0053–i0056) over four
   departures (i0054–i0055). The captions 「〇〇 保有金融資産 …円」 (i0036, i0041, i0043, i0047) give each woman a number in the same impartial
   format, so the volume is staged as a comparison of persons through money.
2. **The three new characters' desires are explicitly about identity, not consumption.** Kizuna: the asset app is "自分の存在を確かめるための
   ルーティン", the top 1% the standard, "凡人止まり" the fear (i0109–i0110); she reads a fictional man first by wealth (i0152). Nana: investing
   in the franchise's parent is "高次元の推し活", a rank above fans who "mount" with goods (i0100–i0101); the stock becomes "私のメイくん", to be
   defended against "お金の暴力" (i0155–i0156). Inago: wants to "become someone" and has not (i0019), but carries her mother's "騙された
   いなごは 喰われて おしまいさ" (i0034), admires money from a distance ("いっせんまんえん…", i0050) and stays out of the market.
3. **Kurumi is the volume's knowing silence.** She is the first to name 投資 (i0028) and the first to warn of the reverse (i0033); she tells
   the table she traded FX hard and was wiped out (i0042–i0043) and still owes 2014 national tax, but keeps the amount "最高機密" and the bankruptcy inside
   her own head (i0044–i0045), while Kizuna calls a 1億 loss impossible (i0035) and later privately calls her "よっぽど 下手クソ" (i0114).
   Her inner lines are the volume's most self-critical: the ten million "それが大金 だったと / 失ってから 気づいた" (i0048), her success
   "たまたま ハイレバFXで" (i0049). Her desire is visible only as envy ("羨ましいな…", i0040), questions ("今って なにか アツい相場 あります?",
   i0114; "それって いくらくらい です?", i0155) and an inferred line "株はまだまだ 勉強中だし!" (i0146; INF MOD-HIGH hers). She places no order.
4. **The series' founding mechanisms are re-staged on others, with Kurumi as spectator.** Yasuko's 2013 story re-stages several of them
   — a fear-driven start, early reinforcement ("1週間で 3万円"), a ceiling call ("天井タッチ!"), a position held without a stop until a forced
   liquidation at the top — and adds the cruel reversal after the stop-out (i0068–i0076); Kurumi's own ruin was a long caught by a collapse
   (accepted V07), not a short. Kizuna's euro short repeats the short's shape (a contrarian sell
   against Kurumi's "上昇モード" warning, i0121–i0122) with its outcome withheld — the drawn Monday gap bears out Kurumi's trend reading, not Kizuna's story (i0140–i0141). Nana's cash purchase, sized by a rule ("貯金は
   半分くらい 残しておけば", i0132), is the counter-model the volume's strip (「株式取引」) and Yasuko's doctrine ("現物取引だから 強制ロスカも
   ないし", i0092) propose.

EVAL MOD-HIGH (design): V10 tests V09's coexistence thesis only by displacement — the desire Kurumi resolved to "control" is seen in others,
and her own control is never tested because she does not act.

### D.2 Live competing thesis (C002)

**V10 is a workplace-and-fandom comedy that uses the market as a source of gags and lessons and keeps its consequences small or offstage: it
domesticates investing as a safe, cash-only pastime (the "株式取引" turn), pauses Kurumi's arc, and defers every real outcome to a later
volume.** Evidence: the only realized market harm in present time is an unrealized −8,000円 (i0145); Kizuna's bet is never resolved and she
shows no distress (i0141, i0144); the volume's emotional climax is a fandom possessiveness gag ("お金の暴力で 私のメイくんを 取らないで…!",
wedding fantasy, i0155); its expository pages (unit shares, Brexit; i0106–i0107, i0115–i0118) and its many parody names make it read as a
light didactic comedy; Kurumi's one dark moment (blood-vomiting vision, i0033) is in gag-horror register; and the narrator's "欲望の種" frames
seeds that have not yet sprouted. On this reading the "seeds" sentence is a serial's promise, not a thesis the volume carries out.

The two theses agree on the facts and differ on weight: D.1 reads the comic surface as the vehicle of a design that aligns the reader with
Kurumi's knowledge against the others' confidence; D.2 reads it as the substance. The withholding at the end (i0140–i0141, i0156) fits both.

## G. Psychology of the decisions

Behaviour is kept separate from mechanism; every mechanism below is a labelled candidate model with counterevidence. Four kinds of failure are
distinguished: poor forecasting, poor risk management, inability to realize a loss, refusal to reconsider.

### G.1 Kurumi — the abstainer who rehearses

- **Behaviour.** No trade; discloses the fact of her FX loss and her exit, conceals size and bankruptcy, names the unpaid 2014 tax aloud while its old figure returns only in an inset (i0042–i0045);
  envies the timing she missed (i0040); asks whether she could fight again (i0050); probes Kizuna for a hot market (i0114); warns once each about the
  reverse (i0033) and about the uptrend (i0121), then assents ("そうかもですね〜…", i0124; "アリな気がしますね", i0154); asks a cost (i0155); is (INF
  MOD-HIGH) studying stocks (i0146).
- **Candidate models.** *Hindsight re-evaluation of a reference point* (the V05 "はした金" now "大金", i0048) — a revision of the loss-chasing frame
  of V05, not a continuation of it. *Attribution to luck* ("たまたま ハイレバFXで", i0049) — counter to the overconfidence of V03–V07. *Social
  comparison* (envy of Kizuna and Mochiko's timing, i0040) and *approach motivation under abstinence* (the vortex question, i0050). *Shame-based
  concealment* (the "最高機密" gag, the assent to Kizuna's "欲張ったら") — counterevidence: she volunteers the FX fact unprompted at Nana's question
  and jokes about it; prudence or kindness toward a new hire are equally available motives.
- **Failure kinds.** None in action. Her warnings show sound forecasting of risk, and her one trend call is borne out by the drawn Monday move
  (i0121 → i0140; one instance, a chart reading rather than an election call, and she says nothing of it); her assents show unwillingness to press a warning on a senior
  colleague (deference, not refusal to reconsider).

### G.2 Kizuna — the contrarian whose net worth is her face

- **Behaviour.** Daily recruiting (i0062); wealth goals as identity ("何者か", i0021); a ritual of checking net worth (i0109); dissatisfaction at a
  gain (i0110); a large sentiment trade held over an event weekend against a trend (i0121–i0122); a scenario in which both branches pay (i0123);
  "さっさと行動" (i0124); calm on Monday (i0141); reading people by wealth (i0152); proposes a stock purchase on a colleague's fandom as an edge (i0154).
- **Candidate models.** *Contingent self-worth on rank* ("自分の存在を確かめる", "凡人止まり"; upward comparison to the top 1%). *Overconfidence /
  one-sided framing* ("全然怖くない", "これ絶対当たってる", "織り込み済み") — counterevidence: five profitable years by her report, a stated downside
  branch, a 2016 profit; *action bias* ("悩んでるヒマがあるなら"). *Narrative-driven speculation* (a political story traded as a probability tilt).
  *Thrill / arousal in markets* (the flushed register shared with her affection for Inago) — a visual pattern, not a diagnosis.
- **Failure kinds.** Whether her euro forecast was poor cannot be judged from V10 (outcome unshown); her risk management is undisclosed (no stop,
  size in 枚 only); an unfavourable outcome would not by itself be wrongdoing.

## J. Page form

- **Spreads and openings.** One drawn spread (i0004+i0005) by art continuity; openings are (even | odd) by the package's page sides, so turns reveal
  even occurrences (§A.3). Without separators, chapter ends and beginnings share an opening (i0028 | i0029, i0060 | i0061, i0096 | i0097, i0128 | i0129):
  a reader sees the next chapter's label beside the last beat of the previous one.
- **Turns as reveals.** "わかります" (i0027) → "投資 / ですよね?" (i0028); "投資なんて怖いこと / しなくていいよね?" lands overleaf (i0060); the rising
  chart against Yasuko's short (i0074) and the liquidation notice (i0076) come after turns; "株のこと / 詳しく教えて" (i0096); the Brexit crash (i0118)
  and Kizuna's "発射ぁ〜♪" (i0122) after turns; the mirror "上位1%には / 届いていない——" (i0110); Monday's caption (i0136); the EUR/USD gap (i0140)
  after three silent back views (i0139); the 9:01 fill (i0142); "私もカリフラワー買ってみようかな〜" (i0154); the plea and 「TO BE CONTINUED...」
  (i0156). The disclosure "…学生時代は / がっつりFX" (i0042) and "へぇ〜 FX!" (i0043) face each other in one opening — no turn separates the
  confession from its reception.
- **Screens and faces.** Screens carry the money (Kizuna's app, Nana's bank and order screens, the board, the fill), faces carry the weight:
  Kurumi's shadowed eye under "1億円 負けなんて ありえない" (i0035), Kizuna's flush at a loss story (i0043) and at Brexit (i0118), Nana's collapse at
  −8,000円 (i0145), Kizuna's neutral face where a loss would show (i0141). The page refuses to let the decisive screen (Kizuna's account) be seen.
- **Captions as a ledger.** The 「保有金融資産」 boxes (i0036, i0041, i0043, i0047) are the narrator's impartial accounting of four persons; later
  caption types track Nana's and Kizuna's accounts (i0103, i0122, i0143, i0145). Kurumi's tax never gets a caption — she names it aloud only as unpaid, and its old figure returns only in her inset.
- **Registers.** Gag (seating chart, crab boat, wedding fantasy, "殺してくれ"), gag-horror (blood-vomiting vision, i0033; Yasuko's swirl eyes, i0077),
  didactic (asset vehicles, wealth pyramid, unit shares, Brexit), and quiet dread (the shadowed eye; the vortex question, i0050). The comic register is
  framing, not literal event; it lowers the volume of harm without erasing it.
- **Endorsement and question.** The page questions Kizuna's "ありえない" by Kurumi's silence (i0035) and her "全然怖くない" by staging Yasuko's
  identical structure a chapter earlier (i0071–i0076); it questions Nana's certainty by the unit error (i0105) and the first-day dip (i0145); it
  endorses Yasuko's cash-stock doctrine at least to the extent that no one in cash is ruined. Kurumi's chart reading (i0121) is borne out by the drawn Monday move (i0140) — one instance, a trend call
  rather than an election call, and Kurumi says nothing about it; Kizuna's own outcome stays unshown, so the page neither refutes Kurumi's
  warning nor settles it in a result.
- **Objective vs subjective.** §G.6. The withheld outcome at i0140–i0141 is the volume's purest instance: an objective move the reader can see, a subjective
  state the reader cannot read.

## L. Counterreadings (mandatory)

1. **Kizuna as a competent trader.** Five profitable years by her account (i0039), a real 2016 profit (i0119), a written downside scenario (i0123), calm on
   Monday (i0141): the page may be setting her up as the market-literate foil who wins, not the next victim. The volume does not decide; the Yasuko
   template (i0071–i0076) argues the other way only by analogy.
2. **Kurumi's silence as kindness or prudence, not shame.** She volunteers the FX fact at a junior's question (i0042), jokes about the amount (i0044) and
   keeps a new hire's first evening light; telling the 1億 and the bankruptcy at a welcome party would centre herself. Her assent to Kizuna's lecture
   (i0045) can be politeness to a senior.
3. **Kurumi's questions as collegial curiosity, not relapse.** "アツい相場" (i0114) and "いくらくらい" (i0155) are what a former trader asks in a room of
   traders; "株はまだまだ勉強中" (i0146), if hers, may be study for its own sake (her thesis subject in V09). Nothing commits her.
4. **Nana's investing as recovery.** She names her fan surveillance "不健康な心" and turns to an activity with rules (half savings, cash, research)
   (i0100, i0132); her first stock is a sober proxy. Against it: status motive and certainty language (i0101, i0103), and the surveillance turned on Kizuna
   (i0125).
5. **The volume as comedy that domesticates the market** (D.2): the strip, the cash-stock doctrine, the small numbers and the gags.
6. **Kizuna's "いじめたくなっちゃう♡" and her flushes as a comic trait, not sadism.** The register is light and no harm follows; reading cruelty into it
   would exceed the page.
7. **The narrator's endorsement.** The didactic pages (unit shares, Brexit) explain rather than judge; the "欲望" sentence names a root, not a fault. One
   may read V10 as neutral about investing as such and critical only of FX.
8. **Mochiko's absence as design, not neglect.** The V09 alternative C075 (共生 as workplace symbiosis) receives almost no material; V10's office is run
   without her presence, which may be the point (a small firm where the boss is out selling).
9. **Kizuna's "私も" as general, not a mistaken belief.** "I too will buy" may mean only that she, like Nana, will buy a stock — choosing
   カリフラワー as the franchise's owner she has just found — rather than that she thinks Nana holds it; Nana's inner "私はまだ…" would then be
   Nana's own reading. The page does not decide (E062; C070).
