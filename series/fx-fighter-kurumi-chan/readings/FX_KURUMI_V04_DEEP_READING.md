## C. Current-volume story synopsis

The volume runs from the night of Friday 31 October 2014, hours after the Bank of Japan's surprise easing that closed
V03, to "数日後" (a few days after 5 November). Its spine is how Mebuki finds money. The easing wipes out her account
(0円, −800,000円, "強制ロスカット"); the same night she re-enters on three credit cards and sells USD/JPY again; three days
later she takes a 300,000円 student loan and deposits it; on 5 November, sick with fear, she asks Kurumi for 1,000,000円 and
is refused, then prostrates herself before Mochiko, who lends her 2,000,000円 at 109.5% a year on two stated conditions —
the rate, and employment paying about three times the loan a year — and, once the job is chosen, a further demand for her
parents' address and telephone number. Mebuki chooses an esthetic salon, is hired as the therapist "若葉", and deposits all
2,000,000 into her FX account, calling it "最後の勝負"; by inference from the 2,600,000円 balance and Mochiko's "入金が間に合って
しまった", the losing short is most probably still open (INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH), though no position or P/L is printed after
i0068. Kurumi, for her part, adds 120枚
all-in on the night of the easing; on 3 November (by inference) she tells Mochiko and Yasuko for the first time that her mother killed
herself because of FX; on 5 November she refuses Mebuki's request on protective grounds and only afterwards, alone, grounds
the refusal in her father's maxim; and, probably the same night, she finds herself +7,812,000円 open and decides not to add
again. Yasuko's rabbit comic goes viral, she resolves to leave FX, searches for Mebuki through the night and reaches her on a
station platform, hears Mebuki's money history and that Mochiko is lending her 2,000,000 (not the rate, the conditions or the
kind of work), cannot bring herself to object, and ends by arranging a face-to-face talk with Mochiko. The last pages belong to Mochiko: long and in profit, she has the IOU on her desk and her
debtor's salon profile on her screen, and she tells her cat that human relationships are a nuisance.

### Cold open and 第16話 (i0003–i0032): the add, the zero, the cards, the loan office

  "日が暮れてもその勢いは止まらず".
- **Kurumi's 120枚 (i0004–i0008).** At home at night, Kurumi sees her 60枚 long at +3,132,600円 ("まさかの追加緩和…?") with
  USD/JPY "1ドル112円を超えていた——" (narrator, i0005). She judges the rally "加熱しすぎ" and admits "いつもなら利益確定
  したくなるところだけど…", then decides "むしろ飛び込むのが正解…!" (i0005): "全力 買い(ロング)…!", "120枚発射…!!"; box
  "USD/JPY 112.012円 120枚追加買い (計180枚：平均買値110.271円)" (i0006). Her stated aim is "税引き後2000万円" — "一気に取り
  返せる…!". The price breaks up through 112 (i0007); the tile reads +3,427,200円 and she cannot stop laughing behind the
  door marked "くるみのへや" (i0008).
- **Mebuki's zero (i0009–i0014).** Mebuki receives a food parcel from home with her mother's note "学費の貯金大変やろ? …根性
  入れて頑張るように!" ("チクッ…", i0010), then Mochiko's message "ドル円やばいよ". She hopes "いい意味で" (i0011), opens her

  (i0012). Panic: "取・り・返・さ・ん・と マジでやばい…!" (i0013). Her bank shows 871円 and her wallet only coupons and coins

- **The cards (i0015–i0021).** She finds a credit card; the narrator explains that a card deposit uses the shopping limit,
  "一時的に立て替えてもらうだけの 一種の借金——" (i0016). She has three cards, "合計 ショッピング枠 30万円…!", "これだけあれば
  戦える…!"; she hesitates ("ほんまにええんか…?", "さすがにちょっと危ないんちゃうか…?", i0017) but reasons that the fall is

  20枚売り" — "一番高いところで売れた…!" (i0020). Her own arithmetic: a 10-yen fall would pay "200万円", and her death line
  is "1.5円上の114円…!", "さすがにこれ以上上がるわけがない…!" (i0021).
- **The "cheat method" and the burned conscience (i0022–i0030).** The price goes on rising past "112,2" ("また私を殺す気や…",
  "萌智子先輩… 私… どうしたら…", i0022). She recalls Mochiko's V03 account of how Kurumi won ("どこからか借金して… 大量に
  お金を突っ込んで…", i0023) and turns it into a doctrine: "入金し続ける力さえあれば いつか絶対勝つ", "私も勝つまで無限に借金

  then: "やっぱり…やめられない…", "溶かしたらあかんお金溶かして… クレジットカードまで使って…! もう後戻りできない…", "絶対
  勝つしかない…" — and the image burns away (i0028). Narrator: "灼熱の階段——", "それでも芽吹は 上るしかなかった" (i0029);
  "最後まで乗りきりさえすれば これまでの損失すべてを取り返せる 暴落があるかもしれないのだから" (i0030).
- **3 November (i0031–i0032).** Caption "11月3日 月曜日——": on a train Mebuki offers her seat to an elderly woman, who seems
  to decline cheerfully, then browses part-time jobs (an izakaya at "時給880円~", i0031), then walks from 新都駅 to a building with a giant "学生ローン" sign: "勝ったら返す…", "これはくるみ先輩も通った道…",

## D. Governing thesis and live competing thesis

### D.1 Governing thesis (C001)

V04 moves Mebuki's harm from trading losses into debt and labour, and the decisive escalation is relational. Each new
source of money is costlier and more personal than the last — three credit cards (the narrator's "一種の借金", i0016), a
student lender at about 4,200円 a month on 300,000 (i0056), a friend who refuses (i0071), and a senior who lends 2,000,000 at
109.5% a year, secured on Mebuki's fear of her parents learning (i0088, i0118–i0119). At that last step the only person
whose money she takes answers the first exit Mebuki voices in the flat ("これ以上傷つかないうちにFXやめて——", i0103) by
doubling the loan ("違う違う そうじゃない"; "200万円 融資しよう…", i0103–i0104), and the second, which comes after the doubling
("借りない選択はないんでしょうか…?", i0105), by permitting refusal in words while recasting it as waste ("全然アリだよ?" →
"もったいないんじゃないかなぁ…?", i0105) and then arguing sunk cost, scarcity, rationality and Mebuki's mother's grief (i0106). Mochiko
uses what she knows of Mebuki's vulnerabilities — the tuition already lost, her mother's trust, the shame of exposure (i0106,
i0119) — while affirming to Mebuki's face the lie that she too is short (i0087) and holding a long position whose logic, by
her own V04 thought, was to stay long while Mebuki's heart had not broken (i0037). The page judges the lending through its
form (an angelic frame for "いいよ 貸してあげる!!" against a horror close-up for "年利109・5%", i0086/i0088; thorns, i0106; a
didactic page that places "個人間融資" outside the commercial caps, i0096–i0097) and through Yasuko's verdict "借りる芽吹も
アホだけど… 貸すのは違うだろ…" (i0143).

Mebuki's agency is intact at every step and is part of the thesis, not an exception to it: she deposits the card money and
re-shorts the same night, alone (i0015–i0020); she takes the student loan (i0055); she chooses the salon (i0116); she deposits
all 2,000,000 (i0146); and she does each against an insight she states herself — "アホか私は…!" (i0027), "わかっとんねん…",
"これ借りてもきっとうまくいかへん…" (i0107). Her failure in V04 is less ignorance or bad forecasting than an inability to
realize a loss that has grown into an inability to stop borrowing (V03 C005 strengthened; §G.1).

Kurumi is the volume's counterweight and its irony. She urges withdrawal (i0068) and refuses on protective grounds — loss
upon loss, and people who have died (i0071–i0072) — and only afterwards, alone, grounds the refusal in her father's maxim
(i0074); but she cannot speak with authority: her own borrowed-money win is thrown back at her ("自分は借金して爆益出した
くせに…", i0072) and she can only call it luck (i0073); she pays in self-doubt ("最初からFXを教えた…?", i0075). Meanwhile her
own escalation — 120枚 all-in at a new extreme, ≈29.9× by calculation — is rewarded with +7,812,000 open (i0112), and she
then declines to add again (i0115). The outcome again rewards the riskier process for Kurumi and punishes the more
desperate one for Mebuki (V01 C011 strengthened). Across all four, the volume opposes two meanings of responsibility:
Mochiko's 自己責任 as a disclaimer of any duty of care ("私らみんな自己責任でやってるし", "知ったことではない", i0052) and
Yasuko's "あたしの責任だよ" as acceptance of her own choices (i0136) joined to a duty to intervene ("友達がアホしてるのに 止めに
来ないやつがいるかよ!", i0132) — a duty Yasuko herself then fails to discharge in the moment (i0143).

### D.2 Live competing thesis (C002)

Mebuki's course was set before Mochiko said a word. On the night of the liquidation, alone, she found the cards, re-entered
the same short and built a doctrine of "無限に借金" (i0015–i0026); three days later she borrowed from a lender of her own
choosing (i0055); on 5 November she asked Kurumi for 1,000,000 before going to Mochiko (i0067); she arrived at Mochiko's door
with "大事な話" and a dogeza, already resolved to borrow ("お金さえ調達できれば… 私は無敵なんやから…", i0062), and agreed to
"金利" before hearing a number because she had always meant to pay a premium (i0087). On this reading Mochiko supplies terms
rather than motive. Had she refused, Mebuki would have looked elsewhere (Yasuko's fear of a "街の金融屋", i0094, shows the
alternative the page itself imagines). The doubling to 2,000,000 changes the size of the harm, not its direction, and what
keeps Mebuki in the game is a market that keeps rising against her. The page never states why Mochiko lends — to earn
109.5%, to keep her "canary" alive, to watch, or for another reason — and her own words frame every step as Mebuki's choice
("借りるかは芽吹ちゃんが決めていいけど", i0106; "私だって無理に貸そうとはしてないし", i0105).

What tells against C002 as a full account: in Mochiko's flat Mebuki twice voices stopping (i0103, i0105); Mochiko answers
the first with more money (i0104) and the second with the language of waste, scarcity and Mebuki's mother (i0105–i0106); the family lever is Mochiko's addition (i0118–i0119); and the
lie that Mochiko shares her loss (i0087) is part of the information on which Mebuki decides. C002 remains live because the
page never shows what Mebuki would have done with a refusal.

### D.3 What the volume does not settle

- Mochiko's motive for lending (return, canary, curiosity, enjoyment, or a mixture); the page shows acts, knowledge and
  private remarks, not a stated reason (§I, §L.2).
- Whether Mebuki's 20枚 short survives the continued rise; no open figure is printed after i0068 (§F.1).
- What "ある意味運が悪かったのかもね…" (i0145) means for Mochiko: most naturally foresight that the rescued short exposes Mebuki to
  a larger loss (INF MOD-HIGH); irony and regret are weaker alternatives, the drawn smile at "芽吹ちゃん… いや…" telling against
  regret (§H.3).
- Whether Kurumi's restraint (i0115; probably the night of 5 November) holds, and whether her father's lateness matters (i0115).
- What Yasuko will say to Mochiko and what Mochiko will answer (i0149–i0150).

## G. Psychology of the decisions

Every mechanism below is a candidate explanatory model, labelled as such, with the page behaviour it would explain and the
evidence that tells against it. None is a diagnosis. Behaviour (what the page shows) is kept apart from mechanism (why).

### G.1 Four kinds of failure (owner objective D)

- **Mebuki.** *Forecasting:* she expected a fall at every print ("さすがにこれ以上上がるわけがない…!", i0021; "暴落を待つ…!",
  i0060) and was wrong at every print (112.102 → 114.719 → ≈114.82 → 115.840); but a wrong forecast was not the decisive failure.
  *Risk management:* ≈74.7× on borrowed money with no stop (i0020), then every new yen used to widen the distance to zero rather
  than to reduce size; she computes that distance correctly (1.5 yen, i0021; CALC F.6-5) — the risk is known, not ignored.
  *Inability to realize a loss:* the broker realizes her loss on 31 October (i0012); the same evening it is discovered (the
  liquidation time is unprinted) she re-enters the same direction to "取り返す" (i0013, i0020), and she never closes the new short herself ("耐えて耐えて…", "それしか道はないんや…!",
  i0060). *Refusal to reconsider:* this is the term V04 most complicates. Mebuki does reconsider — "アホか私は…!" (i0027), "これ
  以上傷つかないうちにFXやめて——" (i0103), "借りない選択はないんでしょうか…?" (i0105), "ほんまはもうやめたいねん!" (i0107) — and
  each time does not act on it: once alone (i0028), twice under Mochiko's answers (i0103–i0106), once by deferring to the promise of
  reversal (i0107). The V04 pattern is reconsideration voiced and overridden, not reconsideration refused (V03 C005 STRENGTHEN with
  this qualification).
- **Kurumi.** *Forecasting:* bullish and so far right (i0007, i0112). *Risk management:* the 31 October add is all-in at a new
  extreme ("全力", CALC ≈29.9×, no stop, i0006); on 5 November she models exactly that risk (i0113) and declines a second add for
  want of grounds (i0115) — the same person, two processes five days apart (if the second scene is 5 November). *Realizing a loss:* untested (no Kurumi loss in V04;
  V01 C010 still OPEN for her). *Realizing a gain:* she names profit-taking and declines it (i0005), says she plans to "勝ち逃げ"
  (i0036), and realizes nothing; she holds a winner, the reverse of a disposition effect.
- **Mochiko.** Admits a forecast error ("115円付近でちょっとは下がると思ってたけど", "まったくわからない…!", i0144–i0145) while her

## I. Relationships and interpersonal ethics (directional)

The categories kept apart: ignorance, negligence, recklessness, manipulation, intentional cruelty; understanding a
vulnerability, exploiting it, enjoying its consequences. An unfavourable market outcome is not wrongdoing; what is assessed
below is conduct toward a person.

- **Mochiko → Mebuki: creditor, concealed counterparty, steerer.** *Vulnerability recognized:* yes — the tuition (i0106), the
  student loan (i0098), her mother's trust (i0106), the fear of the family learning (i0118). *Consequences understood:* yes —
  "余計に傷口広げるだけ" of 1,000,000 (i0103), and of the deposit she made possible: "入金が間に合ってしまったのは ある意味運が悪かった
  のかもね…" (i0145). *Conflict concealed:* yes — long and in profit (i0037, i0144), affirming the lie that she is short (i0087),
  holding her long because Mebuki's heart has not broken (i0037), and now creditor at 109.5% (i0145). *Responsibility:* disclaimed
  in advance (自己責任, i0052; "借りるかは芽吹ちゃんが決めていいけど", i0106). *Classification:* not ignorance or negligence; her
  conduct in the flat — escalating the amount after an exit is voiced, closing the exit, using Mebuki's mother and family as levers,
  dialling for her — fits **manipulation** (INF HIGH on the acts and words; motive unstated). Intentional cruelty — a wish to harm —
  is not established on the page (no line states it; V04's enjoyment cues are an eerie smile and a smile at the self-correction, INF MOD). She
  **understands** the vulnerability, **exploits** it (INF HIGH), and may **enjoy** its consequences (INF LOW-MOD in V04).
- **Mebuki → Mochiko: grateful debtor.** Trust intact: she asks her creditor whether she can win (i0107, i0109), believes Mochiko
  shares her loss (i0087), thanks her for the coat (i0120). No suspicion is shown at the volume's end.
- **Kurumi → Mebuki: care with a limit, refusal, self-reproach.** She recognizes danger (Yasuko's report, i0067), understands what a
  loan for FX can lead to (her mother, i0072), refuses (i0071), and questions her own responsibility for teaching Mebuki (i0075).
  *Ignorance:* she does not know of the tuition, the cards, the student loan or Mochiko's loan (i0051, i0067). She does not contact
  Mebuki again; she passes the warning to the friend she judges caring (i0076).
- **Mebuki → Kurumi: supplicant, then accuser.** Polite appeal, a premium offered, identification as leverage ("くるみ先輩なら私の
  気持ちがわかるはずです…!", i0069), then "自分は借金して爆益出したくせに…" (i0072) — an accusation built on Mochiko's V03 inference,
  thrown in ignorance of Kurumi's mother (INF HIGH).

## L. Counterreadings (mandatory)

- **L.1 Mochiko as the only rescuer.** She gave Mebuki money no one else would, refused to let her take a dangerous night shift
  (i0100), insisted on income before disbursing (i0098), paid her fare and lent her a coat (i0118, i0120), and charged what the page
  itself presents as the maximum rate free of criminal penalty for a private loan (i0097). Mebuki asked, accepted and chose the
  job. *Against:* the affirmed lie (i0087), the exit closed (i0103), the amount doubled (i0104), the family as security (i0118–i0119),
  and her own "運が悪かったのかもね…" (i0145). Kept as a minority reading of motive, not of conduct.
- **L.2 Which motive.** (a) Return: 109.5% on 2,000,000 is ≈180,000 a month (i0105). (b) The canary: lending keeps Mebuki short, the
  signal she used to hold her long (i0037) — though a funded canary is a poorer signal, since it sings longer whatever the market
  does (INF). (c) Curiosity or enjoyment (eerie smile, i0109; the "若葉ちゃん" line, i0146). (d) Something like care in her own terms
  ("私が芽吹ちゃんの立場なら間違いなくやるけどね", i0103). The page supports (a)–(c) in part and states none.
- **L.3 Mebuki as a deliberate last-chance gambler.** With the tuition gone and repayment due, a long-shot recovery may look like the
  only way back; she calls it "最後の勝負" (i0139), sets a boundary on the work (i0116), and goes in informed. *Against:* her own
  "これ借りてもきっとうまくいかへん…" (i0107) and "なんで私… こんなことしてんやろ…" (i0120).
- **L.4 Kurumi's refusal as self-protection.** The refusal also protects her money; "運がよかっただけ" deflects the charge of
  hypocrisy; she does not call Mebuki again and delegates care to Yasuko. *Against:* the loss and the dead (her mother unnamed) as the grounds stated at the time (i0071–i0072), with
  the father's maxim afterwards (i0074), and her self-reproach (i0075).
- **L.5 Yasuko's silence as respect.** Mebuki is an adult who has just been told she is cared for; Yasuko wishes her luck and turns
  her effort on the lender (i0142–i0149). *Against:* her own "…なにも言えなかった…" (i0143) names it a failure.
- **L.6 Comedy over judgment.** The series' gag register (police farce, chibi law page, "どアホ") could make the lending a dark joke
  rather than a moral indictment. *Against:* the horror framing (i0088), the thorns (i0106) and Yasuko's verdict (i0143) are not comic.
- **L.7 Kurumi's add as skill.** She read the "狂乱のパーティー" and judged "これは違う" (i0005), was right, and later showed restraint
  (i0115) — process, not luck. *Against:* her own word for her V01 survival is luck (i0073); the add exceeded by calculation the 25× maximum
  printed for Yasuko's broker, which counts only conditionally because Kurumi's own rule is unprinted (F.7).
- **L.8 The コミチア panel as flash-forward.** The elf panel could show the event to come (i0148). *Against:* the frame-breaking giant
  figure, Mochiko's "なんで私が…?" and the event still "今度" (i0147).
- **L.9 The railing as melodrama, not threat.** Mebuki's railing page (i0122) can be read as a regret tableau only; the reading keeps
  dread as a reader effect and claims nothing about her intent.
