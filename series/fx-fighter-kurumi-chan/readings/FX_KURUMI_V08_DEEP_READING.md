## D. Governing thesis and live competing thesis

### D.1 Governing thesis (C001)

V08 turns a debt into a question of what responsibility may demand of a life, and answers it with a friend's question rather than
with money. In one night Kurumi spends the last 1,000,000 — the V03 tax reserve, the money she calls "最後の 命の灯火" — on the same
stopless, escalating certainty that ruined her: 300 lots, then 600, then 1000, two wins read as "私の実力", a third called "勝ち確",
and 0円 (i0003–i0020). Her reckoning is not blind; it is exact and self-condemning — even a 1億 recovery would create a 5500万 tax,
bankruptcy costs money she lacks, the 2014 tax survives bankruptcy (i0029–i0034) — and it converts the 自己責任 she accepted in V07 into
a duty to die: 責任 → 清算 → 相続放棄, a death that would leave her father nothing to pay (i0042–i0047). She walks her mother's road with her mother's words — 「すみませんでした」, the words of Kozue's last note (V01 i0030), on a sheet before her (i0042; memory or new note, the page does not say), "お母さんも同じ気持ちだったのかも" (i0041), "待って
てね… お母さん…" (i0065) — while her body and her delays argue against the plan (i0067, i0078, i0084, i0090). The narrator names the
road not taken, telling her father (i0035), and the motive that blocks it, "罪悪感と 幼稚な 保身" (i0036). Mochiko, the friend who had withheld every word in V07, is pushed by Mebuki to send even a few words (i0108–i0110), withholds once more even so (i0111–i0112), resolves instead on an "investment" with "calculation" attached ("打算付き", i0113–i0114), and comes in person (her arrival follows the flashback by placement, INF MOD). She refuses both pity and the debt (i0119, i0131), and asks the one question Kurumi's responsibility
logic had shut out — not whether death is the best settlement, but whether she wants to die (i0133–i0136). "死にたくないよ!" (i0139) and
"私の友達なんだから" (i0142, the strip's title word) move the volume from settlement to care: a towel, a bath, laundry, a shared bed,
sleep (i0144–i0158). The money is untouched; the volume ends on sleep, not on a solution.

Confidence: OBS HIGH for every element; EVAL MOD-HIGH that the volume's design sets "must" against "want" (the page states the
distinction twice: "私は死ななきゃいけない" / "答えになってない!", i0135; "死ぬしかないって言いながら死にたくないなんて!!", i0139); EVAL MOD-HIGH
that the Kozue re-enactment is designed (the note's wording on the page; the cordon at a tall building; the photograph; the V01 outburst
recalled at i0086).

### D.2 Live competing thesis (C002)

V08 is a rescue melodrama whose resolution is emotional, late and ethically ambiguous, and which leaves the financial facts and the
friend's interest exactly where they were. Mochiko arrives only after five withheld interventions, the fifth made after Mebuki's prodding; she
describes her help as an investment with calculation attached and never says what the investment is (i0113–i0114, i0156); she
refuses material help on a creditor's logic (i0119); her method is insult and force — "アホらしい", "強欲の雌豚", the collar, the jaw,
pinning Kurumi to the bed (i0125–i0136) — and the "greedy sow" she invokes is her own V01 private verdict of contempt (accepted V01
E035), now used to re-enlist the appetite that drove the ruin (i0126, i0154). She calls the franc move an "事故" (i0153), which
exonerates Kurumi's process and leaves unanswered Kurumi's charge that Mochiko's V02 flattery restarted her (i0121–i0122). The page
treats hospital care and the purchased means as gags (i0150) and frames the bath as fan-service comedy (i0145–i0146). The father, the
shortfall, the tax, the deadline, the means on the floor and the letter are unresolved; the crisis is interrupted, not ended (no
statement of intention to live; "これからどうしよう…", i0155). Live for the next admitted volume: whether "投資" proves to be care, a
stake, or both.

## G. Psychology of the decisions

Candidate models are labels for patterns, never diagnoses. Kurumi's self-reported distress (the scene that will not leave her eyes, breathlessness, no sleep, no appetite) and what the page shows (vomiting; days without a bath, by Mochiko's remark) are recorded as SELF_REPORT and OBSERVATION; no clinical condition is inferred.

### G.1 Four kinds of failure, kept apart (第36話)

- **Poor forecasting.** Not the decisive failure on the page: two of three reads paid (i0009, i0012); the third, a dip entry on a
  perceived rhythm ("勝ち確", i0013), failed, and the page answers it after entry (i0016–i0019). Her post-liquidation read ("あ…これ まだ
  下がる", i0022) is untested.
- **Poor risk management.** Decisive and stated in advance: no stop "読みが外れても" (i0006); size raised from 300 to 600 to 1000 lots
  on a balance that rose from 1,000,000 to 4,519,175 (i0008–i0015) — each trade sized to the account's whole capacity, with the outcome
  space she had named as "ゼロになるか 1億円になるか" (i0006).
- **Inability to realize a loss.** Not separable from refusal here: across nine open-loss boxes she takes no action and says only "?"
  (i0016–i0019). The pre-stated rule (no stop) means there is no decision point at which incapacity could be shown apart from policy.
  V01-C010 (as revised: repudiation of the stop rule) holds.
- **Refusal to reconsider.** Not shown at the 100-lot order: after the liquidation she orders again (i0023), and "なんで 売れないの?"
  shows she has not grasped that the account is empty — information lag or a reflexive retry, not a knowing refusal (INF MOD; reviewer

  bankruptcy reckoning (i0029–i0034).

### G.3 Uncertainty, success and failure; objective value and subjective value

- **The binary.** "ゼロになるか 1億円になるか" (i0006) names zero as one outcome of the new trading, staked on a fresh overseas balance while
  the domestic shortfall already exists; "こんなに負けるなんて 考えもして なかった" (i0152) is said of the V07 franc trade. The two statements
  concern different trades and moments and are not combined here (reviewer C-11).
- **Objective value vs experience.** The page prints exact figures (2,545,501; 4,519,175; −2,460,296; 0円) beside a silent face growing
  larger (i0017–i0019), and a deliberately unreadable figure at the height of her distress (i0130): the number she cannot bear is drawn as
  noise. "1億の数字" is experienced bodily (breath, sleep, appetite).
- **Response to success.** Competence attribution and size escalation within minutes (E004–E005).
- **Response to failure.** A rejected order she does not understand (i0023), then an exact, self-condemning reckoning (i0029–i0034), then a moral conversion of the

- **What answers the logic.** Not a counter-calculation: Mochiko refuses to argue the arithmetic ("なんでそうなる", i0131) and asks about
  the wish (i0133–i0136). The page distinguishes reasoning from wanting; the resolution is affective and relational, not financial.

- **L.1 The rescue as contrivance.** The friend arrives on the exact night of the plan, after a flashback that supplies motive. Against:

  suggest an interest not yet disclosed. Against: "私の友達なんだから" and the night's care are non-transactional; the content is OPEN.

  目指す… 結局死にそう", i0154). Against: the line provokes, it does not propose trading; the volume ends with no trade and with
  "これからどうしよう…".
- **L.5 Responsibility logic as care, not pathology.** Kurumi's plan aims to free her father from the debt (i0047); her criteria spare
  others (i0049, i0081). Against: the page shows the harm the plan would do him (the letter's request; the narrator's verdict), and her own
  body and fear dissent.
- **L.6 "事故" exonerates.** Mochiko's framing (i0153) supports V07-C002 and blurs the stopless sizing the page shows again on 第36話.
  Against: Mochiko's next line ("次は1億円… 結局死にそう") restores the character-based reading — she frames the event as an accident and
  the trajectory as Kurumi's.
- **L.7 The comic register trivializes.** Gags around the means and the clinic (i0150), a doll-chibi at the purchase (i0081), fan-service
  in the bath. Against: the gags arrive after the crisis has turned, or as a distancing device inside it (INF MOD), and the volume

- **L.8 The narrator's harshness.** "幼稚な保身" (i0036) judges a young woman in crisis. Against: the same narrator names the better course
