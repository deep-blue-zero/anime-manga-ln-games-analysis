## C. Current-volume story synopsis

V09 (strip 「第9集 共生」, "coexistence", i0003) runs 第41話–第45話. It begins on the morning after V08's last night, carries Kurumi
through a confession to her father and the start of bankruptcy in early February 2015, jumps to autumn 2015 for the discharge, a
reunion of the four friends and Kurumi's declared love of the market, and ends with a further jump to Monday 3 April 2017, seen through
a newcomer hired by Mochiko's company, where Kurumi works as an office clerk. No trade by Kurumi is shown anywhere in the volume.

### 第41話 (i0003–i0044): the dream, the "life meeting", the offer, the letter

In a dark void a small winged, unclothed girl with Kurumi's face and low twin tails taunts her — "今どんな気持ち?", "でも楽しかった
でしょ?" — until Kurumi, calling her "この悪魔!", orders her out ("早く私から出てってよ"); the girl asks not to be denied so easily
("…簡単に私を否定しないでほしいな") and fades (i0003–i0009). Kurumi wakes at 10:58 having slept ten hours; the sky seems unusually bright
(i0010–i0012). Mochiko, still in the flat, has made rice porridge; Kurumi, who had vomited a convenience-store bento, empties the pot
(i0013–i0016).

Mochiko then opens "くるみの人生会議" (i0017). On the debt she says "自己破産一択だと思うけどね" and, in a gag explainer, rules out the
alternatives: 任意整理 would cut "年間1400万円以上" of late damages but leave 1億 to repay in a few years ("月々…166万円以上"); 個人再生 is
closed to debts above "5000万円" (i0017–i0019). Kurumi concludes "…やっぱり自己破産しかないと" (i0019). What matters more, Mochiko says,
is "これからくるみがどうやって生きていくのか" (i0020). Kurumi has no wish to win back her 2000万 — "心が枯れちゃった"; anyone who still
invested after this would be "頭おかしい" (i0021). Mochiko disagrees: "勝てばいいのでは?"; trying again "for your own sake" is not strange;
she would like "a conversation partner", though "generally one ought to stop you" (i0022). She predicts Kurumi will come back to the
market because she chose high-risk FX rather than any way of earning, and asks "本当に母親のためだけだったの?" (i0025–i0027). Kurumi
re-examines her founding premise (i0028–i0029: she could have saved 2000万 as an ordinary office worker), sees the winged figure rise from
beneath her (i0030) and recognizes it: it always appeared small "because I was greedy to the core long before I started FX" (i0031, over
a memory of herself as a small child gathering every shell on a beach). She accepts Mochiko's V08 epithet "強欲の雌豚" as true and says
she used her mother's failure as a pretext ("大義名分", i0032). Mochiko: "くるみは強欲だし / ヘンテコな人生だと思うけど 私は否定しないよ"
(i0033). Her knowledge and experience are real, Mochiko says, and that is also why she is helping; then, standing, blushing, she asks
"私の会社に来ない?" (i0034–i0035). A box of the purchased means stands labelled 「返品する!!」 (i0035).

Kurumi returns the purchases at the home centre's service counter, coached by Mochiko, who calls them by a blunt category word that is
played as comedy (i0036–i0037). Passing a law office's sign in the city (「きしかいせい 法律事務所」), Mochiko says bankruptcy is a system
for people without money and the easiest route is to have a parent bear the cost; Kurumi refuses — she will be despised, slapped — and
wants to clear the debt "お父さんにバレずに" (i0038–i0040). After a gag in which a queue of men offers their cheeks for a banknote slap,
Mochiko tells her not to regress into a child (i0041). In thought Mochiko now fears that the present Kurumi would bring only "爆弾" to
her company and that "ずっと子供のまま時が止まっていたんだな" (i0042–i0043). Aloud: "大人って言われたいなら ちゃんとお父さんに話しなよ /
あんな遺書書くよりよっぽど簡単でしょ". She has read the letter: "ごめん つい出来心で… お父さんがかわいそうすぎて泣けた" (i0043–i0044).

### 第42話 (i0047–i0066): the phone, the condition, the grave, the call

At the 大都南駅 plaza Kurumi dares to look at her phone: a missed-call badge reads 「99」, the display limit; Mochiko supposes the FX company

## D. Governing thesis and live competing thesis

### D.1 Governing thesis (C001)

V09 answers V08's question — what may responsibility demand of a life? — by separating the debt from the desire that produced it and
treating them differently. The debt is closed through institutions and family: bankruptcy named as "一択" (i0017), confessed to the
father (i0063, i0070), met by his offer of the 300万 he had saved for her (i0074; accepted in thanks, i0075; its use is not shown, INF
MOD-HIGH that the costs came from it), carried by a lawyer he introduced (i0098) and ended in a discharge printed under her name (i0104); what bankruptcy cannot clear, the 2014 tax, she pays "地道に" with wages her father manages (i0106–i0107). The
desire is not cured but owned: Kurumi names the winged figure as her innate greed (i0031), admits she used her mother's failure as a
pretext (i0032), tells her mother's grave that part of her enjoyed the market (i0058) and that she and her mother are "似てるけど違う"
(i0061), refuses her father's vow (i0078) and tells her friends she will come back "いつか" because she loves the market (i0119–i0121); in
her room she greets the figure ("ただいま", i0126) and resolves to "支配して コントロールしてみせる" (i0128). The volume's title word 共生
(i0003) names that settlement: coexistence with one's greed, conducted openly ("堂々と", i0079) rather than in secret (EVAL MOD-HIGH; a live
alternative, raised by the character specialist, reads 共生 also or instead as the symbiosis of Kurumi and Mochiko that ends in a shared
workplace, i0035, i0110, i0152 — C075; the cover's grouping of the four friends is paratext and is not used for either). Two relationships
make it possible — Mochiko's refusal to deny Kurumi ("私は否定しないよ", i0033), which answers the figure's own plea ("簡単に私を否定しない
でほしいな", i0009), and the father's refusal of anger ("怒らないから", i0069) combined with his refusal to approve ("それでも俺はやめろと言う
ぞ 親としてな", i0084). The last chapter tests the settlement only by displacement: in 2017 Kurumi is a clerk in Mochiko's company, the market
is on another employee's screen (i0154), and whether Kurumi trades is not shown. EVAL on OBS HIGH; design EVAL MOD-HIGH.

### D.2 Live competing thesis (C002)

V09 is a restoration comedy that dissolves V08's crisis faster than its premises allow. The debt that V07–V08 made the measure of a
life disappears off-page in nine months between two captions (i0098, i0104) with no creditor, no broker contact and no figure; a
parent's savings are offered for the cost; every confrontation Kurumi feared (contempt, a slap, i0040) is converted into relief, sweets and a meal; the
friends' concern becomes a パパ活 gag; and the volume then leaps to a new cast and a new workplace in which Kurumi, the protagonist, is
decentred. On this reading "共生" is less an ethic than a narrative licence: the story keeps its market engine by letting Kurumi
declare her love of it (i0121) and fly into it (i0130) without showing what an honest, controlled return would cost, while the newly
introduced Kizuna (i0154–i0156) and Mebuki's rand (i0115–i0117) carry the risk that Kurumi's arc has set aside. Evidence for it: the
off-page legal process; the absence of any broker contact (only Mochiko's supposition, i0048); the comic register around every serious
beat (the gazette as a gift, i0104; "破産おめでと", i0105; death-sauce luck, i0122); the time skips. Evidence against: the volume does show
costs (the tax survives, wages controlled by her father, the convenience-store job; the father's opposition kept, i0084); it states the
moral turn explicitly and in Kurumi's own words; and it ends with her in subordinate, unglamorous work. OPEN (live).

### D.3 What the volume does not settle

Whether Kurumi has traded by April 2017, and with whose money; what (株)もちもち企画 does (a creator's client "retake", a sales employee,
a live chart; no statement in words); why Inago was hired; whether Kurumi graduated, where she lives and why she sleeps at the office; the
status of the 2014 tax; Mebuki's rand position and her "作戦"; the fate of the physical letter (Mochiko read it; the father is not shown
told of it); the campus name 「大都大学」 against the accepted 新都大学.

## L. Counterreadings (mandatory)

1. **Restoration comedy (C002).** The debt dissolves off-page between two captions; a parent's money is offered; every feared consequence becomes relief;
   Kurumi, the protagonist, is decentred by a new cast. The governing thesis's "coexistence" may be a licence to keep the market engine without cost.
   (Counter: the tax survives, the wages are controlled, the father's opposition stands, the work is menial.)
2. **Mochiko as recruiter rather than rescuer.** Her help has a stated asset motive (knowledge and experience, i0034), a workload need
   (i0110) and ends with Kurumi as her clerk (i0152); she encouraged a return to investing (i0022). On this reading the V08 "投資" is literal
   — an employee acquired. (Counter: she made the job conditional on honesty with the father (i0050), privately doubted Kurumi's fitness
   (i0042), disclosed her interest, and refused to pay the debt; the clerk's title is not a trading role.)
3. **Greed relabelled as identity.** Kurumi's "根っからの欲張り" (i0031) and "相場が大好き" (i0121) may be a new self-justification
   replacing the old one (the mother) — the same desire under a more flattering name, with "control" as its alibi (i0128). (Counter: she
   states present non-intention, studies rather than trades, and accepts external control of her money.)
4. **The father's leniency as enabling.** He pays, praises and withdraws his demand; his "やめろ" has no sanction (i0084). (Counter: he
   controls her wages (i0107) and keeps his stance in words; the page frames his response as repair of the failure that preceded his wife's
   death.)
5. **Kizuna as the displaced risk.** The volume moves the market's appetite onto a new character (i0154–i0156) while Kurumi, the protagonist, is
   shown clerical and clumsy; the risk is deferred, not resolved.
6. **The campus and building names.** 「大都大学」 and 「新都ビルヂング」 may signal a deliberate re-mapping of the setting rather than an error;
   no reading is adopted.
