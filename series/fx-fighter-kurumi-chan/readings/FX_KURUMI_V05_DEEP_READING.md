## C. Current-volume story synopsis

V05 covers two stretches of time. 第21話 is almost entirely Mochiko's past, from her first day of junior high in spring 2006
to April 2014; it returns to the present on its last two pages. 第22–25話 then run from Monday 17 November 2014 to an evening
some time after Tuesday 2 December 2014. The volume strip is titled "第5集 糸" ("Thread", i0003).

### 第21話 (i0003–i0054): Mochiko, 2006–2014

**Junior high and the break with home (i0003–i0021).** At the 平成18年度 entrance ceremony of 市立第二中学校 a twelve-year-old
girl is seen off by an elderly woman, who says her mother might at least have come today; the girl answers that she doesn't
care (i0003). The title page names her: "小金萌智子 12歳——" (i0005). She lives in a run-down flat with her mother, who drinks
at work and wheedles; a polite bespectacled man who manages the mother's work is with her there — he calls Mochiko "娘さん", and
his job depends on her mother's "売り上げ" (i0006–i0008; no line says he lives in the flat). Alone in her room she presses her hands to her ears: "大っ嫌い…" (i0008). She wants to leave home and live alone,
and studies as preparation (i0009). She scores 499/500 at the May midterms and answers classmates' praise by calling them
"low" (i0009–i0010); she refuses to share homework and rebukes two girls who gossip about a teacher (i0011). The class turns on
her: flowers and a mock apology note on her desk, a footprinted gym shirt, a desk scrawled "学校来るな", her English textbook in
the bin (i0012–i0014). In the girls' toilet she rules out hitting back (it would only feed the laughter) and telling a teacher
(the school would contact "あの親") (i0015). She stops going to class. On a riverbank she shares a rice ball with a white stray
kitten and tells it, in tears, that she wanted an ordinary school life, that she failed at it, and that she doesn't want to go
to school and wants even less to go home (i0017–i0018). "よし 決めた" (i0019). That night she turns up with the kitten at her
grandmother's house, a long way off: she will not go to school from tomorrow; let her live here until she is eighteen
(i0020). Home and school "produce nothing but 「損」"; "そんなもの 切り捨てて やる…!" (i0021).

**Self-made (i0022–i0031).** September 2008: studying alone for the 高卒認定 at her grandmother's, with the radio reporting the
US House's rejection of a financial stabilization bill and a record fall in the Dow (page text), she resolves to be one of
society's "勝ち組", faster than anyone (i0022). Her plan: skip high school, pass the equivalency exam at sixteen, aim at a
tuition-exempt university place, use the freed time to make money "with her head", and spare her grandmother further burden
(i0023). September 2009: she has passed; her grandmother celebrates with sushi and the kitten, now grown, tries to steal
the shellfish (i0024–i0025). She contacts the bespectacled man and asks him to introduce her to well-paid work. At the café
"すわっぷぽいんと" he laughs at her ("人生なめすぎ"), offers work in "うちの系列", tells her she is running from hardship and
expecting "他人の施し" — "お母さん以下だよ 君は" — pats her head, thanks her for "面白いもの", and pays the bill (i0026–i0027).
She walks home furious, then concedes she had been naive and that she is lucky to have her grandmother (i0028). Asked whether
she might go home, she says her mother cannot possibly be worried about her (i0029). February 2010: stocking shelves at the
supermarket EAON, she meets two of her old bullies, now in high-school uniform, who sneer "中卒かぁ〜!"; she answers with a
radiant smile, "うん そうだね そっちも勉強がんばってね", and when they have fled, "ばーか" (i0030–i0031).

**The market (i0032–i0038).** June 2010: a laptop bought with her wages; she dismisses the web's "誰でも簡単" schemes as
already crowded and turns to investment, which seems "the fairest" field — amateurs and experts, poor and rich, all on one
pitch, "強い奴が勝って 弱い奴が負ける" (i0032–i0033). October 2010: a stock she holds (500 shares; name unprinted; previous
close 375円 on 2010/10/4) opens under special-sell quotes at 357 despite good results; she weighs averaging down and hoping
for a recovery, rejects hope "私の都合で", and cuts: "損切り…!", "現物売り500株約定しまし[た]" (i0034–i0035). She concludes
that with so little capital, earning more and depositing ("入金力") would be more efficient, and that studying markets, unlike
studying for exams, has no clear answers (i0036). Then, in an allegory of fog, she senses a "temperature" in it — "ヒトの
意識… 欲望… 絶望… 本能…" — and a realization whose content is withheld; her grandmother calls her for mochi (i0037–i0038).

**The funeral and the university (i0039–i0049).** A wordless montage of a warm year at the grandmother's ends at the gate of
the 平成24年度 新都大学 entrance exam (i0039). At the grandmother's funeral (date unprinted) mourners whisper that Mochiko is a
"引きこもり"; her mother, in black, says she is the one who is ashamed, accuses Mochiko of living off her mother-in-law's
pension ("義母さん" — so the grandmother was her father's mother), and orders her to work and bring money home: she'll be back
when she can't feed herself (i0040). Mochiko answers "んなわけ ねーだろ" and shows her notice of admission to 新都大学 as a
tuition-exempt 特待生; her mother leaves: "勝手にしたら!?" (i0041). April 2012, at the 新都大学 経済学部 orientation, Mochiko
resolves not to repeat her junior-high failure and to manage contact strategically (i0042–i0043). A short-haired girl asks if
the seat next to her is free: "私 福賀くるみ!" (i0044). In the campus café Kurumi wants to use her first name; Mochiko refuses
"ちゃん" from a peer, and privately sets out her creed: never be looked down on, keep the initiative, hide information about

## D. Governing thesis and live competing thesis

### D.1 Governing thesis (C001)

V05, "第5集 糸", is about threads that bind the four friends without their consent — lines of money, of concealed information
and of learned habit — and about knowing when to stop. Its first chapter gives Mochiko's adult creed a biography: concealment,
dominance, the market as a "fair" arena where the strong win, and loss-cutting as an ethic were each learned under neglect,
humiliation and isolation (OBS HIGH for the scenes, i0006–i0045; their causal force INF MOD-HIGH, never deterministic). The page
binds that biography to the present in its own words: the charge the bespectacled man levelled at the fifteen-year-old, "他人の
施しに 期待しちゃってさ" (i0027), is the charge she now levels at Mebuki in thought, "これ以上 他人からの 施しを与えて どうするんだ?"
(i0053), and his "学校で友達も 作れなかった 君" (i0027) returns in Yasuko's "萌智子って 友達 いなかったん じゃねーの?" and in
Mochiko's "いらないんだよ… 友達とか なれあいとか" (i0136–i0137) (OBS HIGH for the words; INF MOD-HIGH as designed echoes; her
awareness UNRESOLVED; E061). The same chapter shows her, in her own thought, taking Kurumi up in 2012 as a "sample" whose failure
she would watch at close range, and taking Mebuki up in 2014 behind a mask of assent and a private "大っ嫌い" (SR HIGH, i0048,
i0051–i0052). In the present that creed meets three tests and holds against each while losing ground: Yasuko's claim to
reciprocity at the sushi bar draws from Mochiko an open statement of self-interest (the loan as a hedge of her long), a rehearsed
自己責任, a dismissal of Mebuki's suicide risk and, after a beer, the refusal of friendship (i0129–i0139); Yasuko passes the fact of
Mochiko's long to Kurumi, and Kurumi exposes the shared-short lie in Mebuki's hearing, where Mochiko adds that "only idiots" are
still short (i0144–i0146). Against this the two traders act out opposite relations to the same 117 "ceiling". Mebuki, seeking only
to get back to zero and quit, escalates "rescue" shorts (10 → 10 → 25 lots) that the narrator calls "ただのポジション"; she admits her
error yet will not realize the loss, and collapses into "どうせ私は養分で〜す" (i0060–i0102). Kurumi embraces greed ("欲張ってなにが
悪い") but brackets her long with a stop and a limit; the limit fills at 120.05 while she is away from the screen, paying +17,602,200
and a pre-tax balance of 21,213,915円, which by her own after-tax reckoning still leaves her "あと300万円だけ" short of her target
(i0076–i0080, i0151–i0153). On the evening of her win she attributes it to luck and extends that luck to her dead mother; tempted by
"億の世界", she is checked by the memory of her father's slap and rejects it ("ダメダメ!"); she talks of retiring once the target is
met, then opens a 300-lot short — her V01 stake — sized to close exactly that shortfall, with a take-profit at 118.6 and no stop
shown, calls it the end of her battle and switches off the screen; the volume ends on a candle against her and a child's bucket of
shells spilled by the tide (i0153–i0162). Two meanings of responsibility are now refined: Yasuko concedes Mebuki's 自己責任 in
principle and moves her claim from the loan to the relationship — friendship, disclosure, "芽吹はこの世にひとりしかいねー"
(i0133–i0138) — while Mochiko keeps 自己責任 as a reason to owe nothing.

The thesis is an EVALUATION built on observations graded HIGH (scenes, boxes, lettered lines) and on two formal facts: the
narrator's open verdicts on Mebuki (i0090, i0094–i0095) and the volume's final pairing of chart and spilled bucket (i0162).

### D.2 Live competing thesis (C002)

V05 is chiefly a sympathetic origin story that relocates the volume's moral centre. Mochiko's coldness is self-protection
learned in a home and a school that gave her nothing; her loan to Mebuki is a disclosed, rational hedge that Mebuki chose and
that Yasuko herself accepts as Mebuki's 自己責任 (i0133); she protects Yasuko at the booth, helps her sell 372 copies and pushes
her toward the editor who gives her a path (i0110, i0112, i0126); she promises to keep Mebuki from "crossing the line" (i0139).
On this reading the cruelty of the café scene is accidental (she does not see Mebuki), and the real danger in V05 is Kurumi's
grief and restitution drive ("お母さんの負け額", "全然割に合わない", i0154), which turns a nearly reached target into a new, larger bet.

What supports it: the flashback's weight and sympathy (tears with the kitten, i0018; gratitude to her grandmother, i0028;
mochi, i0038); real help to Yasuko; the hedge argument is coherent in direction, though overstated in size (§F.3); Yasuko's concession. What weighs against
it: her own 2012 thought makes failure-watching her purpose toward Kurumi (i0048); her stated motive for the loan is profit,
her stated motive for restraint is recovering the principal (i0130–i0131, i0139); she still hides her long from the borrower
and repeats the lie to Kurumi (i0144); she meets Yasuko's fear of a suicide with contempt for Mebuki's "courage" (i0138); and
her private thoughts about Mebuki across V05 are resentment, contempt and self-justification, never concern (i0051, i0053,
i0132, i0139); and the doctrine she applies to Mebuki exempts the family support she herself received and acknowledged
("私には おばーちゃんが いる時点で 恵まれている", i0028). The competing thesis stays live for Kurumi's half: the page itself turns from Mochiko to Kurumi in 第25話.

### D.3 What the volume does not settle

Whether Mebuki's 43-lot short survived the move to 120 (no figure after i0091; §F.1); whether Mochiko's restraint is concern or
calculation (she gives only the calculation, i0139); whether Kurumi's new short has a stop (none shown, i0158); who says
"なにそれ" at i0145; what Mochiko realized at i0038; whether Mebuki will confront Mochiko. Real exchange-rate history
was not consulted on any of these and is not evidence.

## L. Counterreadings (mandatory)

- **L.1 Mochiko as survivor rather than predator.** For: the flashback's sympathy and her care for her grandmother, the cat and
  Yasuko (i0018–i0039, i0110–i0126); her disclosure of the loan's logic is honest; she promises restraint (i0139). Against: the 2012
  "sample" purpose (i0048); the hedge stated as profit (i0130–i0131); the lie repeated to Kurumi (i0144); contempt for Mebuki's
  "courage" (i0138). Disposition: biography explains, it does not excuse; both readings stay in §D.
- **L.2 The loan as a disclosed business arrangement.** For: Mebuki chose it; Yasuko concedes 自己責任 (i0133); the hedge is
  coherent (§F.3). Against: the counterparty's adverse position was hidden from the borrower and lied about (V04; i0144); the rate
  and the family security (V04) are not ordinary terms between friends; the "hedge" is a friend's solvency.
- **L.3 Mebuki's rescues as reasonable mean-reversion trading that met bad luck.** For: two rescues worked (i0066, i0085); the market
  did dip. Against: the narrator's verdict (i0090); each rescue added risk on borrowed margin into a rising market; the cut was made
  conditional on a favourable move (i0091).
- **L.4 Kurumi as disciplined.** For: the bracket (i0077–i0080) and a correct top call (i0157); the win came from a pre-set order.
  Against: the greed creed (i0076), the 億 temptation (i0154, checked), 300 lots with no stop shown and the PC off (i0158–i0159).
- **L.5 The ending as staging.** The final candle is schematic, and the bonus strip says short-term charts are altered for drama
  (i0165): the spike may be a wick; the short may profit. The reading claims only that the page ends on a move against her and on
  the spilled-bucket image, not an outcome.
- **L.6 Yasuko's concession as complicity or as respect.** Her "芽吹の自己責任" (i0133) could license Mochiko; it can also be read as
  refusing to treat Mebuki as incapable, while insisting on care. Her threat (i0139) and fear (i0138) weigh toward the second.
- **L.7 "あの子にそんな勇気は——" as cruelty or as defensive reassurance.** It may be meant to calm Yasuko (Mebuki will not kill
  herself) as much as to belittle Mebuki; the word "勇気" and Yasuko's interruption make contempt the stronger reading (MOD).
- **L.8 Kurumi's "お母さんだってやる時期がよかったら…".** Rationalization that keeps FX fair, or grief and humility; both supported
  (E054).
- **L.9 Comic register as undercutting.** The bath and the neighbours' dogs could trivialize the win and the stakes; they also make
  the following escalation and the closing image sharper by contrast.
