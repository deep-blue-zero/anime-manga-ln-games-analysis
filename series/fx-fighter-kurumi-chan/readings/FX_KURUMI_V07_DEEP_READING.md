## D. Governing thesis and live competing thesis

### D.1 Governing thesis (C001)

V07 prices the certainty V06 built. It does so in three movements that the volume keeps in contact.

First, **everyone else moves on and says what they hid**. At the Christmas dinner Mebuki reports a −654.4% result, lost tuition, debts, a
leave of absence and a job she likes (i0007–i0012); Mochiko reports +74% exactly as printed and, in private, tells Mebuki "私は芽吹を利用して
勝ちに行った… 悪いことしたとは思ってない" (i0017); Yasuko is crowned on a loophole for turning her FX money into a comic (i0021–i0025).
Kurumi's 643.4% (i0020), the best FX result, is set aside by rule-play and she does not protest. On 7 January Mochiko discloses her missing
schooling and Kurumi her mother's 2000万円 (i0046, i0051); Mochiko sees them as two daughters "cursed by their mothers" (i0053). Kurumi reads
her friends' exits as progress she is not making ("みんなFXやめてどんどん先に進んじゃうなぁ", i0027) and asks for "normal" job counsel
(i0042), while her only position is the 300-lot long.

Second, **the market event is presented as indifferent, and the page also shows what made it ruinous for her**. The narrator frames
15 January as equal fate — "平等に機会を与え 平等に殺す", "投資家それぞれの事情など決して考慮されることはない" (i0080–i0082) — and the SNB
official had publicly reaffirmed the cap three days earlier (i0070). Yet the page gives the mechanism of Kurumi's particular ruin to
Mochiko's reasoning: about 24× (i0115), no stop by choice (i0116), a gap in which no trade was possible ("この区間取引成立せず", i0114), so that
the forced loss-cut executed after trading resumed (i0117); a stop just under 1.20 would "た・ぶ・ん" have saved her (i0115). The black box
then states the outcome to the reader — 「-1億153万4644円 (-1億2028万4479円) 強制ロスカット」 (i0118) — before Kurumi is shown reading any
figure. Kurumi's own forecast had named the risk (ECB easing could make Swiss intervention "しんどくなる", i0072) and dismissed it as
"織り込み済みのはず!" (i0073), and the narrator answered at once: "相場を大きく動かすのはいつも衝撃である" (i0074).

Third, **the aftermath re-enacts the mother**. Kurumi closes the window by reflex (i0106), turns her mother's photograph face down ("見ちゃ
だめ…", i0113), cannot breathe (i0120), calls the figure a "bug" (i0125–i0129), reads the demand — 101,534,644円 by 19 January 15:00, 14.6%
a year after (i0130) — and accepts "借金" as "私が選んだ結果… 自己責任" (i0140). Then the childhood inference of V01 returns ("簡単に2000万円
溶かす事が出来る何か… 稼げる何かとも…", i0142) and becomes a plan: her untouched tax money, 1,005,144円 — the identical figure of Kozue's 2008
balance in the accepted V06 reading — short-term trading, unlimited leverage, "取り返すなんて一瞬", seven doublings to 1億2800万 (i0144–i0148).
She recognises the script ("これじゃ私もお母さんとおんなじ——", i0147) and goes on, framing responsibility as isolation ("誰にも迷惑かけちゃ
ダメだ", i0149). The narrator, unusually direct, calls her thinking skewed and her eyes blind (i0149–i0150). Mochiko, who understood the risk
exactly and estimated the loss within a few percent (i0118), never says a word of it to Kurumi: she withholds an invitation (i0054), a

The governing claim, then: V07 shows a catastrophe that the narrator calls blind to circumstance and that the page nevertheless makes
legible as the product of a sized, stopless, certainty-driven position; and it shows Kurumi respond by adopting her mother's ruinous
plan in full knowledge of the parallel, while Mochiko, the one friend who knew, stays silent. EVAL on OBS HIGH; the "re-enactment" is EVAL MOD-HIGH
on form (the identical 1,005,144 and the inset are the page's; Kurumi's awareness of the identical figure is not stated).

### D.2 Live competing thesis (C002)

V07 is chiefly the story of an exogenous disaster and of a resilient young woman. A central bank broke its word three days after promising
(i0070, i0097–i0098; Mochiko: "舌の根も乾かないうちに… ダメでしょ 道徳的に…!"); the narrator insists the event struck everyone equally and
ignored circumstances (i0080–i0082); Kurumi's thesis was the official policy (i0069–i0070), she watched the right risk and the right date
(i0072), and even a stop might have slipped in a market where no trade was possible ("この区間取引成立せず", i0114; Mochiko's hedged
"た・ぶ・ん", i0115). Her collapse is a normal shock response; her acceptance of "自己責任" (i0140) and her refusal to burden anyone (i0149) are
responsibility, not denial; her plan at the end is the genre's comeback set-up ("私の命まだ尽きてないかも…!", i0143). Mochiko's silence
reflects a sober judgment that nothing could be done ("他人になにかできるわけないのに", i0135) and respect for an adult's choice.
Evidence for: i0069–i0074, i0080–i0082, i0097–i0098, i0114–i0115, i0135, i0140, i0143. Against: the narrator's own verdict on her plan
(i0149–i0150); Kurumi's recognition of repetition (i0147); the 24× sizing and the deliberate absence of a stop (i0115–i0116); her dismissal
of the risk she named (i0073) under the narrator's rebuttal (i0074); Mochiko's contrary counterfactual that a stop would probably have saved
her (i0115); the doubling ladder (i0148) as an image of fantasy rather than plan. OPEN (live).

## G. Psychology of the decisions

Candidate mechanisms are labelled models, not diagnoses; each is set against counterevidence. Four kinds of failure are kept apart.

### G.1 Four kinds of failure, kept apart

- **Poor forecasting.** Kurumi forecast that the cap would hold and that an ECB move was "織り込み済み" (i0073). She was wrong about the
  policy's persistence — the official's own promise (i0070) was broken three days later (i0097–i0098). A forecast resting on an official's
  word is not irrational; it failed. The narrator's maxim ("相場を大きく動かすのはいつも衝撃である", i0074) frames this as the predictable
  unpredictability of shocks, not as stupidity.
- **Poor risk management.** About 24× (Mochiko's estimate, i0115; consistent with the accepted conditional calculation) and no stop by choice
  (i0116; V06). The page makes this the decisive difference through Mochiko's counterfactual: a stop just under 1.20 would "probably" have
  filled between 1.20 and 1.17 (i0115); without one, the forced loss-cut met a gap and executed after trading resumed (i0116–i0117), turning a
  loss into a shortfall of 101,534,644 (i0118, i0130). The hedge "た・ぶ・ん" and "この区間取引成立せず" (i0114) keep open that a stop too might
  have slipped (§L.1). The size of the 8 January gamble (30 lots on 100,000 yen; "火遊びすぎる", i0061) repeats the pattern in miniature.
- **Inability to realize a loss.** Not tested in V07: after the shock there was no decision point at which she could cut — the broker
  liquidated in the gap. Before it, she held a profitable long (≈+40万, i0019) at a "defended" level; her holding was conviction, not
  paralysis.
- **Refusal to reconsider.** On 13 January she named the threat to the floor and dismissed it in one morning (i0072–i0073); asked about the
  franc on 7 January she said "それは問題なし!" (i0056). The second opening (i0065) never reached her: Kurumi's "なぁに〜?" invited it and
  Mochiko withdrew it before the warning, so it is not evidence of Kurumi's refusal (reviewer C-05). After the shock, she reconsiders the trade's premise
  ("最初から無理があった", i0141) but not her method: the new plan raises leverage to "unlimited" and seeks seven doublings (i0145, i0148). The
  refusal now concerns the whole project (FX as the only remedy, "FXの負けはFXで取り返すしかない!", i0148).

### G.3 Uncertainty, success and failure; objective vs subjective value

- **Objective value against experience.** The reader is given −101,534,644 and −120,284,479 in a black box (i0118) while Kurumi has seen only
  "あんな数字" (i0110) and calls it "1億" without meaning ("1億ってなに…", i0121). Her subjective sequence — frozen heart, breathlessness,
  "bug", fear wall, "払えるわけない", "自己責任", "取り返す" — is drawn against a figure that never changes.
- **Equal fate, unequal exposure.** The narrator insists the event "came to everyone equally" (i0082); the montage puts four friends in
  the same instant (i0081), and only Kurumi is shown exposed. The maxim and the montage together say that the shock is indifferent and that
  what it does depends on what one holds.
- **Success before failure.** The volume begins with Kurumi's best number (643.4%, i0020) and with a small leveraged win (+90,000, i0063);
  her last stated feeling about the long is "凪々チャート" (i0101).
- **Mochiko's uncertainty about herself.** Her certainty about markets (i0055, i0057, i0115–i0117) contrasts with her uncertainty about her
  own goal ("それから…", i0138; "私はいったいなにがしたかったんだろう…?", i0139) — the volume's only explicit self-doubt from her.

## L. Counterreadings (mandatory)

1. **Indifferent catastrophe, not a verdict on process.** The narrator's "平等" maxim (i0080–i0082) and the stopped feeds (i0098, i0114) support
   reading the shock as a disaster that no ordinary prudence could have survived at that size; Mochiko's stop counterfactual is hedged
   ("た・ぶ・ん", i0115). Response: the page grants the indifference but gives the size (24×), the absence of a stop and the gap mechanism a
   decisive role in Mochiko's reasoning (i0115–i0117), and the narrator reserves its explicit judgment for the plan, not the trade.
2. **Mochiko's silence as respect, or as realism.** "他人になにかできるわけないのに" (i0135) and "まぁ本人が言ってるし" (i0136) can be read as a
   sober view that warnings do not change traders and that Kurumi is an adult. Response: the page shows her unable to send rather than
   unwilling (anguish, i0100; agitation, i0137), and lets Yasuko ask what she would not; respect and self-protection are both live.
3. **Kurumi's end as responsibility, not blindness.** "自己責任だもん" (i0140), "責任取らなきゃ…", "誰にも迷惑かけちゃダメだ" (i0149) are the
   language of someone accepting a debt; a loss-chasing plan is one of few she can see. Response: the narrator calls it blindness (i0149–i0150);
   the voice she hears as her mother's calls it "無理" (i0147, INF MOD); she herself calls it repetition. The responsibility is real; its form (solitary, by FX) is what
   the page questions.
4. **The winner dispute as care.** Mochiko's rules-lawyering could spare Kurumi the bill or honour Yasuko's real investment. Response: the
   motive is UNRESOLVED; the comic register and Yasuko's "カネ…ナイヨ…" favour mischief, but Kurumi offers to pay anyway and nobody disputes her
   643.4%.
5. **CATFORCE as play.** The game may be Mochiko's first creative product shown with pride ("どう?面白い?初めて作ったんだけど", i0040), not a
   warning. Response: both; no line makes it a warning; its content ("札束無視すれば進めるから", "ロスカットエンド") is a parable on the page
   regardless of intent.
6. **"Bug" as ordinary shock.** The denial of i0125–i0129 can be read as the normal first hours of disbelief rather than motivated reasoning.
   Response: agreed that no pathology is implied; the page marks it as denial by refuting it in writing (i0130) and by her own abandonment of
   it (i0132).
7. **The Kozue parallel as authorial design.** The identical 1,005,144 and the inset may be the creators' signal to the reader, not Kurumi's
   psychology. Response: agreed for the figure (her notice is not stated); but she does recognise "お母さんとおんなじ" (i0147), so the parallel
   is also hers.
8. **Mochiko's motive as superseded.** "利用できると…思ったから付き合っただけだ" (i0045) describes how it began; cooking, staying, the near-
   invitation and the post-shock agitation show attachment since. Response: both are on the page in the same chapter; the volume does not
   say which governs her silence.
9. **The fall pages as spectacle.** The twelve-page fall (i0084–i0095) can be read as formal play, not dread: the spread across i0084 | i0085,
   the page turn before the break (i0086), the repeated falling labels (i0087–i0094) and the comic 「ピョーン」 under the dated caption
   (i0095) display the device as a device.
   Response: the register clash ("ピョーン") is real; the caption's naming and the following chapters make the spectacle a prelude to a human
   consequence.
