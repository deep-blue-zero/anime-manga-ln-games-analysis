## C. Current-volume story synopsis

The volume covers about seven weeks, from late May to 15 July 2014, in five chapters. Disputed or unshown figures are
flagged; all money is in yen.

**第6話 — The money comes home (i0003–i0040).** "一週間後" (one week after V01's night), Mochiko sits at her four-monitor
rig reading the blog "†FX堕天使† あふぃちゃんのFX日記": the blogger reports a crushing loss on the Australian dollar and
announces her retirement ("もうFXは引退だぁ(泣)", i0003). A visitor arrives: 山師芽吹 (Yamashi Mebuki), captioned
"(20)【大学三年生】", a cheerful blonde who calls Mochiko "萌智子先輩" (i0004). She fusses over the white cat Mochiko feeds
but denies keeping (i0006), and opens a yen-denominated overseas FX account on her laptop; on Mochiko's advice she picks
the maximum leverage, "500倍" (i0007). Asked what she did today, Mochiko says she only browsed investor blogs. She shows
Mebuki あふぃちゃん's blog, jokes that the author is a middle-aged man posing as a girl, privately finds the blogger's
losses entertaining ("下手糞すぎて面白い", "FX芸人"), and posts a comment under the false name "ともこ", claiming an AUD
loss of her own and urging the blogger not to give up (i0009–i0011). Cut-ins show あふぃちゃん drunk among cans, lamenting
that nobody clicks her affiliate links and quitting "everything" (i0009–i0010). Asked how she became a winner, Mochiko
says "運じゃない?" and points Mebuki to a "school acquaintance" she calls a "戦士" (i0011–i0012).

That acquaintance is Kurumi, leaving her convenience-store shift. Walking home she savours having no open loss
("含み損がないって最高〜!"); her payslip for a month of extra shifts reads 52,487円 (i0013). Tonight she must secretly
put back the 3,000,000円 she calls "借りた300万円" (i0014). In her room she counts the cash bundles and reviews her
results: about 1,800,000円 cumulative profit, own capital about 2,100,000円 (a diagram: "2月 自己資金 30万円スタート" →
"5月 +300万円(借入) +約130万円(利益) −300万円(返済)" → "現在 自己資金 約210万円" → "目標 2000万円"). She weighs "勝ち逃げ"
and rejects it because the 2000万 target is unmet (i0016). She decides to "reflect on" the trade and move to a domestic
account with a smaller "instant-death" risk, which 2,100,000円 is enough to fight with and which is better for tax
(i0017). Tax reminds her that filing would drop her from her father's dependants and reveal non-wage income (a gag
fantasy of tax officials, i0018). She considers confessing her FX to her father, hoping he might approve of a daughter
"who can earn, unlike her mother", and dismisses the idea as wishful (i0019).

Before she can act, her father pounds on her door: the household money has been stolen and he is calling the police
(i0021–i0022). Kurumi, the bundles stuffed inside her dress, tries to stage a "finding" of the money; he grabs her arm
and the cash falls to the floor ("終わっ…た…", i0023–i0024). In the living room he is first relieved that the culprit is
she, then demands a reason, and tells her he is on her side whatever trouble she is in (i0025–i0027). She confesses,
with a fixed, unsettling smile, that the money was needed to win at FX (i0028), then brightly explains: she trades to win
back the 2000万 her mother "melted"; she is sorry for borrowing without asking, "but thanks to it I won"; she has
1,800,000円 profit, will probably leave his dependants, would he do the paperwork; she will never borrow again, can take
responsibility herself, and is an "大人" now (i0029). He slaps her once, rejects the idea that she must recover her
mother's failure and her claim to be an adult, says that needing borrowed money to win is already failure, "無理だよ
くるみには", orders her to stop, and then bows and begs: "頼むから… FXなんてやめてくれ…" (i0030–i0032). In a white
dream-space Kurumi judges that she survived by luck and that wanting to go on is "狂ってる"; she resolves to quit and to
earn slowly like anyone else (i0033–i0035). But the barefoot, sailor-uniformed girl who led her there, a figure of Kurumi's own mind, turns into a winged tempter
who whispers that earning is a drag, that FX makes a fortune in an instant, and "気持ちよかったでしょ…♥" (i0035–i0037).
Drenched in sweat, Kurumi tells her father "…うん" (i0038). That night, awake, she thinks "やめなきゃ——". An editorial
narrator comments that her survival can be called lucky but that, seen paradoxically, she was "運が悪かった" (i0039–i0040).

**第7話 — Retirement and a pupil (i0040–i0072).** The next week, in a café at 新都大学, Kurumi tells Mochiko she is retiring
from FX because she has no talent (i0040). Mochiko counters each reason in turn: Kurumi has the makings of a billionaire;
FX is a "no-risk battlefield" because you lose only money; only people with families are really hurt; why cherish "たかが
数十万円" of part-time savings; saving is for idiots; "ギャンブル上等"; Kurumi's holding through the drawdown showed a
gambler's instinct that a normal person lacks (i0040–i0045). Kurumi, flustered, says she does not want money as such and
refuses to tell Mochiko why she wants 2000万 ("萌智子には関係ないよ", i0046). Mochiko accepts the retirement, then asks a
favour (i0047): at the university gate she introduces "FX見習い" Mebuki, who calls Kurumi "くるみ先輩" and repeats
Mochiko's boast that Kurumi turned 300,000円 into 2,100,000円 in three months, "FXの天才" (i0048–i0049). Mochiko asks the
retired Kurumi to be Mebuki's FX teacher, flatters her knowledge, and whispers in her ear, staged exactly like the
winged tempter, "人助けだと思ってさ" (i0050–i0053). Kurumi tells herself that teaching is not trading and agrees,
"知ってること教えるだけなら"; Mochiko leaves at once for a lecture (i0054–i0055).

In a study room Mebuki explains her motive: she does not want to work or be a "cog in society"; Mochiko told her that
"一発当てる" with FX is the shortcut to 100 million yen (i0056–i0059). Kurumi privately questions Mochiko's advice, then
admits it is no different from what she herself did (i0059). Mebuki's stake is 200,000円 from her scholarship (narrator
box, overseas account, i0060). Asked how much is in her own account, Kurumi says she has retired and has put no money in
any more; a narrator box shows "くるみ FX口座残高 0円", and she flinches when Mebuki notes she can restart any time
(i0061–i0062). Mebuki wants only to be told which way the market will move (i0064). Kurumi first declares the
"自己責任原則": whatever loss follows her advice is Mebuki's own (a chibi gag of the adviser disowning the result, i0066).
Looking at Mebuki's GBP/JPY chart (axis 170.9–171.2), she gives her "第一感", rendered as a power-up: "下がる… …かな"
(i0067–i0068). Over Kurumi's warning that she has seen only a short-term chart, Mebuki sells at once: "芽吹 VS GBP/JPY
170,897円 10枚売り" (i0069–i0070). The price falls toward 170.7; Mebuki squeals that her money is increasing, while
Kurumi, panting, itches with the thought "私も売っていれば儲かったのに…" (i0071–i0072).

**第8話 — The adviser who does not trade (i0073–i0102).** "2週間後", "2014年6月中旬": Mebuki phones Mochiko to thank her;
thanks to Kurumi she is winning "連戦連勝" (i0073). In her apartment corridor she meets her next-door neighbour and friend,
captioned "あふぃちゃん改め 高根やす子(20)【フリーター】" (i0074). At an arcade dance machine Yasuko is furious that Mebuki
started FX after all her warnings; she calls FX a rigged "クソゲー"; hearing that Mebuki is up "15万くらい" from
200,000円 she diagnoses "ハイレバで運ゲー"; she warns that Mebuki will be "sunk in a bath" and that winners cannot see the
danger, describes the "magic" that makes losers chase their losses until the stakes grow into a "破産RTA", and calls the
pound — the currency Mebuki says a "先輩" recommended (which senpai is not shown; Kurumi had been puzzled that Mebuki
was watching it, i0067) — a "殺人通貨": "お前騙されてるぞ!?" (i0075–i0082). Mebuki notices how much Yasuko knows; Yasuko
denies knowing or doing FX and tells Mebuki to reconsider her company; Mebuki announces that she has devised a "必勝法"
(i0082–i0083).

At night Kurumi reads savings books and dreads every message from Mebuki because it is usually about FX (i0084–i0085). A
black-guttered flashback returns to the debut trade: as the unrealized gain reached +21,900円, Kurumi taught Mebuki how to
close by market or limit order while thinking "もし私も売っていたら…"; she told herself the fall was chance and that she
must not trade; Mebuki closed at "170,702円(+19.5pips)" for "10枚利益確定(+19,500円)" (i0086–i0087). Mebuki concluded that

by financial regulators (a footnote states that paid investment advice requires FSA registration); watching a rising
chart she starts to plan a "様子見程度に5枚くらい" long and stops herself (i0091–i0092). Deciding she is tempted because she
has nothing but FX, and remembering a high-school life of nothing but study, she goes into town to find a hobby
(i0093–i0094). She buys a heavy stack of books, passes a pachinko parlour, comes back, rationalizes learning "大人の遊び",
plays, and loses 5,000円 without understanding or enjoying it (i0095–i0099). At once she thinks "ちょろっとFXやれば
取り返せる", catches herself — "やめるって決めたじゃん…!" — and the winged tempter returns: "私はFXをやめられない…!"

## D. Governing thesis and live competing thesis

**Governing thesis (EVALUATION, supported by NARRATOR_PRESENTATION HIGH).** V02 answers V01's open question about how
Kurumi would take her rewarded catastrophe (V01 C011), and its answer is that the *lesson is learned and then overruled*.
Every authority the volume offers names the V01 win as luck: Kurumi herself (i0033 "運よく生き延びた", i0041 "運がよかった
だけ", i0087–i0088 "偶然", "たまたま", i0101), the narrator (i0039–i0040 "運がよかった… 運が悪かったのだ"; i0116–i0118 on 第一感 and バイアス), and
Yasuko about Mebuki (i0079 "ハイレバで運ゲー"); her father judges the process instead ("お金借りないと勝てない状況に
なってる時点でダメだろ", i0031). None of this governs action, for three reasons the pages show. First, memory
keeps the reward and drops the fear (i0105–i0107: "あの恐怖が思い出せない", only "光り輝く数字と安堵を超えた至福"). Second,
the people around her keep re-presenting the outcome as competence: Mochiko re-describes holding a loss as "勝負勘" and
talent (i0045), retails "30万円を210万円" as "FXの天才" (i0049), and converts Kurumi's retirement into a teaching role
(i0050–i0055); Mebuki's luck-made streak is credited to Kurumi ("さっすが", i0088) and keeps proving her calls right in
front of her (i0072, i0120). Third, she licenses herself in the very vocabulary the volume had earlier discredited:
"大人" (claimed at i0029, rebutted by her father at i0030, lamented by her at i0113, reclaimed at i0130 with the ruby
"20歳") and "自己責任" (first a disclaimer of her responsibility to Mebuki, i0066, then her own banner, i0131). The
narrator names the result: the "運だけの勝利" became "一生消えることのない呪い", and she "could not" imagine losing
(i0132–i0134). V02 thus extends V01's structural point — the outcome rewarded the worst process — into a psychological
one: a rewarded catastrophe is remembered as a comeback story, and the story authorizes the next risk.

**A second strand of the same thesis.** The volume spreads the pattern from one person to a network. Mochiko sets a
novice at 500× and tells her that "一発当てる" is the shortcut (i0007, i0059); Kurumi's hedged call becomes Mebuki's trade
(i0068–i0070); Mebuki's luck becomes her doctrine (i0088–i0089) and her recruitment pitch (i0148–i0155); the volume ends by
formalizing social comparison as a contest with no rules (i0157–i0160). The voices against FX are the people who have
lost: the father, whose wife died after an FX loss (accepted V01), Yasuko, the blogger who lost big (i0155), and Kurumi
herself at her lowest.

**Live competing thesis (agency and adaptation).** The pages also support reading V02 as a *chosen* return, not a
compulsion or a curse. The allegory shows Kurumi's own arm throwing the child-self into the pool, in tears (i0126): the
act is hers. Her stated purpose is coherent and her own — "誰のためでもない / 自分の人生を始めるため / 2000万円を稼ぐため"
(i0131). She makes risk-reducing changes the governing thesis must discount: she moves from the overseas account to a
domestic one with a smaller "即死リスク" (i0017, i0102, i0129), resists two impulses (i0092, i0116), and devises a method
(Mebuki's views as a contrary indicator, i0141). And the depicted market keeps validating her: every call of hers shown
in V02 is right (i0068→i0072, i0119→i0120; "当たりまくってる", i0091), and her balance rises by +556,690円 between the
domestic deposit and 15 July (CALC, i0102, i0158). On this reading the "curse" is the narrator's didactic frame laid over
outcomes the story keeps paying out, and V02 is a coming-of-age into a dangerous vocation. **What separates the two
theses within V02** is only the narrator's explicit verdict and prolepsis (i0132–i0134, i0161–i0162) and the form that
undercuts her "liberation" (the broken chains of i0131–i0132 bind her to Mochiko and Mebuki at i0144). The competing
thesis must set the narrator aside to stand; it is kept live because the pages' own outcomes do not yet contradict it.

## G. Psychology of the decisions

Candidate models are labelled as such; none is imposed as a conclusion, and no clinical condition is diagnosed. The
volume is unusual in that its own narrator states explanatory models (heuristic first instinct, pattern-seeking in
randomness, bias, memory converting an ordeal into a good memory, overconfidence: i0116–i0118, i0132–i0134). These are
recorded as NARRATOR_PRESENTATION — the page's own theory — and tested against the pages like any other model.

### G.1 Four kinds of failure (owner objective D)
- **Kurumi.** *Poor forecasting*: not shown — every call she makes in V02 is depicted as right (i0072, i0120; "当たりまくって
  る", i0091); the narrator nevertheless calls the basis heuristic and biased (i0117–i0118), and she agrees ("ただの妄想",
  i0118). *Poor risk management*: the structural choice is risk-reducing (domestic account, i0017); but no size, stop or
  exit plan for her renewed trading is ever shown, and she keeps a funded account while "retired" (i0102). *Inability to
  realize a loss*: not tested — no loss of hers occurs in V02 (V01 C010 stays untested). *Refusal to reconsider*: the
  opposite is shown — she reconsiders repeatedly and accurately (luck, i0033, i0041, i0101; "このまま続けたら負ける人間",
  i0101) — and the reconsideration does not govern action. V02's specific failure is **precommitment failure under
  craving**, ending in self-licensing: a correct belief that loses to a remembered reward.
- **Mebuki.** *Poor forecasting*: her own forecasts are "mostly wrong" by Kurumi's account (i0141). *Poor risk
  management*: 500× (i0007); about 85× effective on her first trade (F.2); she does not know what she is trading ("多分
  ポンド?", i0070); she enters before thinking ("考えてるうちに下がったら嫌", i0069). *Inability to realize a loss* and
  *refusal to reconsider*: untested, because she has not lost; she dismisses warnings (i0075–i0083, i0148).
- **Yasuko.** In V02 she trades nothing; her stated rules (low leverage, low volatility, no competing; i0156, i0160) are a
  precommitment. Her self-image "あたしは違う" (i0155) is contradicted by her V01 full-size doten (accepted V01 F.2).
- **Mochiko.** No trade shown; her stated contest strategy is passive ("現状維持", i0160).

## I. Relationships and interpersonal ethics (directional)

The categories are those of the method: ignorance, negligence, recklessness, manipulation, intentional cruelty;
understanding a vulnerability vs exploiting it vs enjoying its consequences.

- **Kurumi → father.** Concealment (planned secret return, i0014–i0020), then a staged deception when cornered (i0023),
  then confession (i0028) framed by outcome justification and a request to be left alone (i0029); verbal assent to quit
  under inner arousal (i0038). By late June at the latest she keeps a funded account (the deposit date is not shown), and she relapses on 30 June; in thought she acknowledges his
  worry and overrides it ("お父さんが心配するのはわかるけど 私はもう子供じゃない", i0130). Responsibility: partly accepted in

  disconfirmed, §M).

**Screens vs faces.** V02 has few trading screens and no screen of Kurumi's renewed position: her relapse is given only
as a narrator box (i0129) and as a face lit from below (i0128). The one detailed screen is a *memory* (i0107), glowing in

phone thrust into Yasuko's face (i0149). Objective value is everywhere mediated by the subjective: the narrator's box
reports a number; the drawing shows what the number does to a face.

**Registers.** Three registers alternate and comment on each other. (1) *Gag* — tax officials (i0018), FSA officials
(i0092), chibi hypotheticals (i0066), the arcade (i0075–i0083), Yasuko at the gate (i0138–i0139). Gag scenes carry real
information (tax exposure, the legal limit on paid advice, the 自己責任 escape route) while defusing it. (2) *Horror and
gambling-manga* — "ざわ" (i0102), "ゴゴゴ" (i0065, i0116, i0146, i0161), shrunken pupils and dark bands across eyes (i0028,
i0100, i0128), tendrils and dark pools (i0108–i0109, i0124–i0126). (3) *Allegory* — the psychic stage of 第6話 and 第9話 with
two younger selves, wings, straps, chains, a table of money and a sign "ロンガーの皆さん 爆益おめでとう♥". The allegory
externalizes a decision that the realistic pages show only as clicks.

**Does the page endorse her interpretation?** It does both, deliberately. Visually, the self-licensing pages grant her
liberation — chains shatter, wings spread, she soars (i0131–i0132). Verbally, the narrator undercuts the same pages

re-binding chains of i0144 (E040) answer the image. In 第6話 the narrator's verdict (luck, bad luck) sides with the father
against Kurumi's outcome claim (E011). The narrator's didactic interventions (i0039–i0040, i0116–i0118, i0132–i0134,
i0161–i0162) are a new device relative to V01's balance boxes and explainer captions, and they make the volume's thesis
unusually explicit.

**Formal rhymes.** Two whispers (E037); "無理だよ" (E038); "…うん" / "う…ん" / "え…うん" — Kurumi's minimal assent to her
father (i0038), to Mochiko (i0055) and to Mebuki (i0087) in the same form; three readings of one episode (E039); chains
(E040); the "くるみのへや" sign drawn in childhood (i0111) and still on her door on the night she relapses (i0114).

**The closing image.** The last page sets the most carefree figure of the volume — Mebuki's eyes-closed double peace — over
an abyss with chart gridlines, and gives the caption "ひとりの少女の人生を変えるような" beside her face (i0162). The page
designates the girl by juxtaposition while never naming her, and leaves the direction of the change unstated (claim
C011). After the four-balance spread, the reader holds
the exact numbers at risk; the abyss gives no number.

**Comic register vs consequence.** The volume never lets gag erase consequence: the one slap (i0030) and the father's bowed
plea (i0032) are drawn realistically; the relapse is wordless and tearful (i0126). Conversely, the most consequential
ethical exchange — "利用" (i0142–i0146) — is played as banter with laughter, framed by "ゴゴ". The page leaves the tone of
that exchange open.

## L. Counterreadings (mandatory)

**L.1 Choice, not compulsion.** The governing thesis stresses the remembered reward and the self-licensing; the allegory
also shows Kurumi's own arm casting off the child (i0126), and her stated purpose is her own (i0131). On this reading she
is an adult choosing a dangerous vocation with eyes open, and the tempter is the page's way of dramatizing a choice, not
a force that removes it. *Assessment*: kept as the live competing thesis (§D). The page supports both; the narrator sides
with the curse reading.

**L.2 Mochiko as a sincere trader friend.** Her creed (only money is at risk; saving is for idiots; talent is holding
where others cut) could be her honest worldview, offered to a friend she admires ("見直した", i0142) and misses on the market
("嬉しい", i0144); her request that Kurumi teach Mebuki could be meant to protect a "危なっかしい" novice; her "半分正解"
could be candid friendship in a competitive frame. *Assessment*: possible for the café and teaching scenes taken alone. It
does not fit her deception of あふぃちゃん for amusement (i0010–i0011), her private contempt (i0151) or her expectation that
her friends will drop out (i0160), and the page's staging (E037) and dark framing argue against it. Kept as a minority
reading of motive; the effect of her persuasion is not in doubt.

**L.3 Kurumi's luck attribution as self-protective modesty.** "才能ないから" and "運がよかっただけ" (i0040–i0041) could be a way
to end Mochiko's pressure rather than a belief. *Assessment*: her private captions say the same thing (i0033, i0087–i0088,
i0101), so the belief is real; but its social use is also visible.

**L.4 The domestic account as prudence.** Moving her whole capital into a lower-leverage domestic account (i0017, i0102)
could be read as a reasonable, risk-reducing plan for a future she had not yet abandoned, not as preparation for relapse.
*Assessment*: plausible; the page presents it through the tempter's accusation ("また…お金が入ってるの?") and the narrator
does not comment; because the deposit is undated, the volume does not settle whether the account was already funded
when she told Mebuki she had put nothing in (i0061). Both readings stay live (claim C004).

**L.5 The market vindicates her intuition.** Every call Kurumi makes in V02 is right on the page, Mebuki's balance triples,
and Kurumi's rises by +556,690. The narrator's debunking (i0116–i0118) is didactic; the depicted outcomes run the other
way. *Assessment*: a real tension in the volume. The governing thesis reads the outcomes as the reinforcement that
deepens the curse (and the narrator's prolepsis points forward); a reader may instead take them as the story's evidence of
skill. No outcome in V02 decides between these.

**L.6 The contrarian use as harmless.** Using Mebuki's views as a signal costs Mebuki nothing on the page — she keeps
winning — and Mebuki had extracted daily unpaid advice. *Assessment*: Mochiko's "おあいこ" argues this; the page frames it
as ominous ("ゴゴ"; the chains of i0144). Harm is not shown; the ethical point is the undisclosed use of a dependent
friend's information, not damage.

**L.7 The "girl" of the final caption is Kurumi or no one.** The series is Kurumi's; the narrator's sentence could be
generic or point to the protagonist. *Assessment*: the juxtaposition with Mebuki's image is deliberate; INFERENCE
MODERATE-HIGH for Mebuki, alternatives kept.

**L.8 The father as controlling.** His slap, his "無理だよくるみには" and his refusal of her adulthood can be read as
paternal control over an adult, which her later "私はもう子供じゃない" (i0130) resists legitimately. *Assessment*: the page
shows his plea as love and grief (i0032) and his critique as correct in substance (aligned with the narrator); his force
is recorded. The tempter's echo of his "無理だよ" (E038) shows how his denial of competence can be turned.

**L.9 Yasuko's motive as pride and envy.** Her announcement follows a humiliation (i0154–i0155) and envy ("羨ましくなっちゃう",
i0149). *Assessment*: both motives are on the page; the private care motive (i0157) comes after and is the one she tells
herself. Mixed motive.

**L.10 Kurumi's "誰か私を止めて…!" as rhetoric.** The plea could be the allegory's drama rather than a real request.
*Assessment*: her real-world attempt to stop the advising (i0115) shows a matching impulse; no one hears either.
