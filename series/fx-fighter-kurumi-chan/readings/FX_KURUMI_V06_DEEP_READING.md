tiles read −1,941,000 and −1,833,000 (i0011, crop). At 18:25 a
calendar alert on her phone reads 12月5日 「米雇用統計」 (i0011, crop). In an imagined storm she argues herself round: she cannot

## D. Governing thesis and live competing thesis

### D.1 Governing thesis (C001)

V06 ("第6集 必勝") turns the volume's losses into an inheritance and then into a certainty. On one Friday the two USD/JPY shorts
end in opposite ways: Kurumi, who in V01 could not realize a loss, cuts twice (−1,743,000 at 18:27 and −1,131,000 after the
report; balance 18,339,915円, i0013, i0046), while Mebuki, who never cut, is force-liquidated to 0円 at the 22:30 release
(i0039) and finds relief in wage work (i0040–i0042, i0053). The page judges Kurumi's discipline by two clocks. Minutes after her
first cut the rate dips (i0015) and days later it stands at "118円" (i0098); but on the same night it passes 121.597 (i0044–i0046),
where the original short would have lost 5,898,000 (CALC) instead of 1,743,000. Kurumi takes her lesson only from the later clock
(i0098–i0099). 第28話 then sets beside Kurumi's goal and her recent conclusions their counterparts in her mother Kozue's 2006–2008
journal and trading — the notebook itself (i0065), the "2000万" (i0082, i0087), a carry trade "for the family", a "バーゲンセール"
averaging-down, household and parents' money, forced liquidation of "1900万円", 400× loss-chasing, and the sentence
"損切りしなければ勝っていた。でも損切りしないとダメらしい。" (i0070–i0089) — and frames the chapter as Kurumi reading that journal
(i0090); no page shows her taking any rule from it. After it Kurumi vows "次は絶対勝つ" (i0090), refuses to "withdraw half-way"
(i0094), recasts her two cuts as the error ("やっぱ損切りいらなかったじゃん", "含み損耐えるべきだった", i0098–i0099) and seeks a
"理詰め" "必勝の通貨" (i0103). She finds it in a central bank's promise, announces a "必勝全力の300枚買い" of EUR/CHF (i0115), feels
dread (i0116–i0118), refuses to "cross" until she can name it (i0122), names a real-seeming danger (crowded stops at 1.20 in thin
hours, i0127–i0132), reasons it away and decides "ユロスイは「買い」だ" (i0137); on 25 December she holds 300 lots with no stop order
("あえてしてない!", i0148, i0152). The volume's form refuses her certainty — the Death figure (i0116–i0117), her own "重大な見落とし"
(i0118), the dam already spilling (i0121), Mochiko's monster vision and unspoken "万が一" (i0151–i0153) — while its dialogue records
it. Mochiko, who closed her own longs in the same scene, right after reading Mebuki's message (i0051–i0052; the time of the close
is unprinted; the link INF MOD), recognizes Kurumi's exposure and withholds the warning as "くるみが決めたこと" (i0154). Responsibility appears in three shapes: the customer's "責任は僕にある"
(i0035), Mochiko's 自己責任 extended from Mebuki to Kurumi (i0154), and Yasuko's gentle "引き際" (i0147). The ending withholds the
outcome.

Basis: OBS HIGH for the positions, figures and lines cited; EVAL MOD-HIGH for "inheritance" as a reading of form (the echoes are
designed and adjacent); the page does not show Kurumi drawing her stop-loss conclusion from the journal, and no causal derivation is
claimed (reconciliation, reviewer C-01 to C-03); EVAL for the rest of the reading of form.

### D.2 Live competing thesis (C002)

V06 is chiefly a story of two young traders learning to take losses and of Mebuki's release, with Kurumi's EUR/CHF trade a reasoned
position rather than a repetition. On this reading: both Kurumi and Mebuki stop holding losers (one by stops, one by liquidation and
exit); Kurumi's post-flashback method is genuinely different from her mother's — she rejects her own mean-reversion "第一感" on
EUR/USD because "今回もそうなる保証はどこにもない" (i0106), seeks an institutional floor instead of a pattern, pauses on a bad feeling
and refuses to "cross" until she can name it (i0122), identifies crowding and stop-cascade risk (i0127–i0132), and then omits a stop
for a stated microstructure reason (stop-hunting, i0152–i0153) while treating a dip as an intervention opportunity; her retirement is
planned (i0148). The Death figure and Mochiko's monster are genre staging, not authorial verdicts, and Mochiko's silence respects an
adult's autonomy after she asked the one question that matters.

For it: i0106, i0122, i0127–i0132, i0148, i0152–i0153; Mebuki's relief (i0040–i0042). Against it: the size ("全力", ≈24× effective by
conditional CALC, §F.2), the certainty vocabulary ("絶対下がらない", "必勝", "勝った!!", i0115), the self-reassurance sequence that turns a
named danger into a "bad dream" (i0133–i0136), the close echo of the mother's anti-stop sentence (i0098–i0099 against i0087), the
absence of any stop order — her only stated exits are discretionary (switch if a clearer currency appears, i0123–i0124) or
conditional on success ("上がれば引退", i0148) — and the page form (§J). For it, also: her first cut was vindicated the same night
(i0044–i0046; −5,898,000 avoided by CALC), so the volume does not simply punish discipline. The thesis stays live because V06 never shows the
position's fate.

### G.2 Candidate mechanisms, with counterevidence (labels, not diagnoses)

- **Loss chasing.** Kurumi: "絶対ここで取り返す" and a full-size re-short into the report (i0031–i0034, i0046); "次に大きく勝てばなにも
  問題ない" (i0049). Kozue: "取り返さなきゃ" ×6 and a 400× plan (i0082–i0083). Counter: Kurumi's EUR/CHF search begins with a refusal
  of a "feel" trade (i0106) and is framed as a long-horizon floor, not a quick recovery.
- **Hindsight and outcome bias; reversal of a rule.** "やっぱ損切りいらなかったじゃん", "勝率重視で含み損耐えるべきだった" (i0098–i0099);
  the page's own sequence (i0015, i0098) supplies the outcomes that train it, and she ignores the outcome that vindicates the first cut
  the same night (121.597, i0045–i0046; −5,898,000 avoided by CALC). Counter: her stated reason is a short horizon ("何年も戦い続ける
  わけじゃない"), and a rule judged by two outcomes is still a rule she chose to drop, not a loss she could not take.
- **Counterfactual regret aimed at the one decision that paid.** "1760万円程度の利益で喜ぶんじゃなかった", "あのとき利確しなければ"
  (i0046–i0047); Kozue's "わかってたのに…!" (i0076). Counter: Kurumi also tells herself she is "still rich" (i0048).
- **Sunk cost / goal gradient.** "こんな中途半端なところで 撤退できるわけがない" (i0094); "あと566万" (i0096); "あと数回勝てばゴール"
  (i0099). Counter: she does plan retirement (i0148).
- **Certainty-seeking and illusion of control; authority reliance.** "理詰めで解けるような 期待値MAXの 必勝の通貨" (i0102–i0103), "もっと
  確実ななにか" (i0107), "スイス国家が守ってくれている", "絶対下がらない" (i0115), "結局誰も無限には敵わない" (i0134). Counter: she names
  "no guarantee" for EUR/USD (i0106) and pauses on a feeling (i0122).
- **Motivated reasoning / rationalization (sequence).** Worry named (i0128–i0132) → "いや… それはない… のでは…" (i0133) → the SNB
  repairs everything → the knight's terror becomes "a bad dream" → "霧は晴れた" (i0134–i0137); later the cascade insight becomes the
  reason to hold no stop (i0152–i0153). Counter: her reasoning about crowding is coherent on its own terms.
- **Escalation of commitment.** Kozue: savings → household → insurance and down-payment → parents' cash → "全て投入" (i0071–i0077).
  Kurumi: a "全力" 300枚 buy announced after a 2.87M loss (i0115), decided at i0137 and held on 25 December (i0148). Counter: Kurumi does
  not add margin or borrow in V06.
- **Intermittent reinforcement.** Kozue's tap followed by a bounce (i0074) and one win before four losses (i0087); Kurumi's every guess
  followed by the opposite move (i0097). Counter: neither is shown as the reason for continuing.
- **Social comparison and isolation.** "もしかして FXやってるの 私だけ?" (i0101); the long knights as "同類" and "邪魔" (i0125–i0126);
  Kozue's mum-friends (i0062, i0067). Counter: Kurumi joins the crowd she distrusts ("渡ろう みんなで", i0137).
- **Fatalism or contrary self-forecast (Mebuki).** "私はここで死ぬんやな…" and "上がると思います" against her own short (i0025–i0028)
  without acting. Counter: relief afterwards ("肩の荷が下りて", i0040) suggests the end of a held loss, not despair.
- **Money and identity.** "私はまだお金持ち…!" (i0048), "FX戦士" self-framing ("通貨(戦場)", i0049), "母が失った2000万円を…奪い返す"
  (i0095); Kozue's "私たちはもっと幸せな家族になれる" (i0069). Counter: Kurumi's goal is explicitly bounded (2500万 gross).
