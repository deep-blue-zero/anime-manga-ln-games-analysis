## C. Current-volume story synopsis

The volume runs from late July to 31 October 2014. Its spine is Mebuki: in eleven weeks she wipes out her account on
the pound, re-deposits her fourth-year tuition money, shorts USD/JPY against a rising market, doubles the short in anger,
feeds it with her living money, survives a near-death touch of 110 yen, refuses to take the profit that the market then
hands her, and is last shown in late October still holding the losing short; on the final page a surprise Bank of Japan
easing, drawn by the narrator as a "pillar of fire", runs against it, and her account after it is not shown. Around her,
every Kurumi trade the page closes is profitable — her two USD/JPY longs on the side opposite Mebuki's short, without her
knowing it — and her last long is in profit, still open; Mochiko lies about her own result and about her own position,
assents to what she privately calls nonsense, and privately treats Mebuki as a "canary"; Yasuko, the poorest and most
experienced loser, is the only one who tells Mebuki to cut.

## D. Governing thesis and live competing thesis

### D.1 Governing thesis (C001)

**V03 transfers the series' warning from Kurumi to Mebuki and turns it into harm with a social cause. Mebuki's harm —
the 8/13 wipe-out, then tuition and living money exposed in a losing short — is self-authored: every order, deposit and
refusal is hers, and the narrator says so ("決断するのは彼女自身なのだ", i0134). But it is socially aggravated: the one true
warning is dismissed because of who gives it (Yasuko, i0141–i0142), and the authority she trusts lies to her about her own
result and her own position and coaches her to hold (Mochiko, i0090, i0100–i0101, i0148–i0152), for a purpose the page
discloses to the reader only for the canary (i0149–i0150). The pattern V01 diagnosed in Kurumi — an inability to realize
a loss rather than ignorance of risk (V01 C010) — reappears in Mebuki in her own words ("損切りしなきゃ幻の数字やし", i0158),
together with the V02 mechanism of a lucky survival read as vindication (the narrator's "神懸かった奇跡", i0135).
Meanwhile Kurumi, the model Mebuki abandoned, closes every trade the page closes in profit — her USD/JPY longs on the side
opposite Mebuki's short, without knowing it.** Kurumi's stay-out answer on 13 August (i0025–i0026), given without the
reason she later shows she had (i0042), is context for the first loss, not one of its social causes: Mebuki overrode it.

Support:
- Agency: Mebuki chooses 246× on her own call (i0030–i0033); deposits the tuition knowing what it is (i0059); sells against
  the narrator-certified breakout (i0060–i0063); adds in anger (i0069); states the risk asymmetry of the add herself
  (i0072); refuses the +229,700 profit and her own pre-set exit plan (i0129–i0131); rejects Yasuko's warning (i0142).
- Social aggravation, shown and narrated: Mochiko's "失敗しても親が払ってくれる" (i0100) returns at the moment of maximum
  danger (i0110); Mochiko's lie (i0148) answers a direct question and removes the one exit Mebuki had voiced, hedged as it
  was (i0145 "そのときは——"; i0147 "撤退しようかな… とも思ってます"); whether Mebuki would actually have closed is not shown;
  the narrator attributes her late-October calm, with a "からか" hedge, to "the reassurance of Mochiko being on her side"
  (i0158).
- The page judges: the narrator's prolepsis (i0136), its "had she forgotten what the money was?" over her mother's parcel
  (i0159), and its framing of Mochiko's forecast as the majority error (i0161).

### D.2 Live competing thesis (C002)

**The decisive failure is Mebuki's alone and came before Mochiko's lie; the final blow is an exogenous shock that no one
on the page forecast. On this reading V03 is a tale of process and timing more than of exploitation: the narrator places
the "最後の逃げ場" on the night of 15 October (i0136), when Mebuki had +229,700 and a written plan to withdraw the tuition
(i0130–i0131) and abandoned it unprompted; she had already refused Yasuko's advice before phoning Mochiko (i0141–i0145);
Mochiko's lie is a move inside a contest framed by her own "敵同士" doctrine (V02) and aggravates a commitment Mebuki had
made; and the "サプライズ緩和" of 31 October was, by the narrator's own words, unexpected by "多く" market participants,
including Mochiko herself (i0161).** Mochiko's role would then be an aggravation and a moral wrong, not the cause. One
complication cuts against "no one forecast": six days earlier an in-world news column on Yasuko's screen shows the phrase
"追加金融緩[…]" (stamp "2014年10月25日", i0156), its content cut off; it does not contradict the narrator's "多く", but the
page did put the words on a screen before the event (MODERATE, truncated).

Why it stays live: it fits the chronology exactly, it respects the narrator's double stance (responsibility placed on
Mebuki at i0134; the concession that steadfast belief sometimes wins, i0135), and the volume withholds Mebuki's result
(i0162). Why it does not govern: the page goes further than chronology. It has Mebuki voice a hedged willingness to exit
if Mochiko too recommends buying and ask her directly "やっぱりこれ… 上がりますか…?" (i0147), gives the lie its own page
turn and dotted emphasis (i0147→i0148), spells out the canary purpose (i0149–i0150) and names Mochiko's reassurance,
hedged "からか", as the cause of Mebuki's desensitization (i0158). Both theses agree that Mebuki, not the market, made the
decisions; they disagree about how much of her late-October holding the page charges to Mochiko.

### D.3 What the volume does not settle

- Whether Mebuki's account survives the 31 October move (withheld; CALC only).
- Mochiko's motive beyond the stated instrumental one (informational use of Mebuki as a contrary indicator, i0150): the
  page also shows relish ("焼豚", i0099; "面白い", i0151), contempt (i0126, i0147) and a flicker of reassessment ("まともな
  親いるじゃん…", i0096); none of these is reduced to a single motive.

### G.3 Responses to uncertainty, success and failure

- **Mebuki.** Under uncertainty she reaches for narrative ("105は今年の最高値"), authority (Mochiko) and ritual; under

## J. Page form

- **Dread by prolepsis.** The narrator tells the reader the shape of the outcome before it happens: "即死する" over the 246×
  order (i0033); "もっともその状態が続いたのは——" across the page turn to "20秒間だけであった" (i0037→i0038); "半年以上続いていた
  1ドル100円~104円の相場は もう終わっていたのだ" right after her short (i0063); "最後の逃げ場であったと——" (i0136). Suspense is
  therefore not about what happens but about when and how she will see it.
- **Screens against faces.** Tiles and boxes are set beside Mebuki's face in nearly every crisis panel (i0034, i0039, i0045,
  i0074, i0078, i0109, i0158); the number is the event, the face its reception. The 8/13 sequence renders money as a
  spectacle — digits rolling by the second with the narrator's "純粋な好奇心" (i0036–i0037) — then as silence: six near-
  identical frozen profiles after "口座残高 0円" (i0047).
- **Objective value vs subjective experience.** A −2,892 loss becomes hunger (i0022); +600,000 becomes "学費余裕" (i0037);
  +8,900 becomes "あの死の淵から" (i0127) and then "数千円か" (i0128); +229,700 becomes "しょぼい" against Kurumi's "何百万"
  (i0131); a 500円 haircut becomes unaffordable (i0106); −258,100 becomes "幻の数字" (i0158). The page repeatedly shows that
  the same yen weigh differently by reference point.
- **Page turns and facing pages.** Spread determination is by inventory spread properties and art continuity (§A.4). The
  turns used for reveals and the facing-page landings are listed at E066. The two most consequential turns are
  i0147→i0148 (Mochiko's shadowed smile → the lie with dotted emphasis) and i0161→i0162 ("ドル円相場には——" → the pillar).
- **Comic register vs horror.** Gag fantasies carry the characters' models of the market (E069); horror is reserved for
  faces of pleasure — Kurumi's delight through Mochiko's eyes (i0056), Mochiko's aura over "焼豚" (i0099) and her smile before
  the lie (i0147). The canary mine (i0149–i0150) fuses the two registers: a fable drawn as a choking, collapsing Mebuki.
- **Does the page endorse a character's interpretation?** It questions Mebuki's at every turn: "しかし翌日" after "完璧な論理"
  (i0062); the narrator's verdict on the range (i0063); Mochiko's flat, darkened face against her coin argument (i0125–i0126);
  the narrator's "思い込んでいた" (i0135) and "鈍感になっていた" (i0158). It half-endorses Kurumi's (her bullish case is borne
  out by i0154–i0155 and i0162) and Yasuko's (her caution is the only advice the page rewards). It exposes Mochiko's
  public words through boxes (E067) while making her private reading of the market correct (i0146, i0154).
- **The narrator's double stance.** It concedes that "愚直に信じる心を持つ者だけが真に大きな勝利を得られるのかもしれない"
  (i0135) and that from outside her swings "may look like confusion" (i0134), but it insists "決断するのは彼女自身" (i0134) and
  ends by asking whether she had forgotten what the money was (i0159). Sympathy and judgement coexist.
- **Documentary close.** The last three pages leave the characters: place labels, a policy explainer and a single candle
  (i0160–i0162). Mebuki's fate is withheld by form — the volume ends on the event, not the person — and the title strip of
  i0003 ("第3集 火柱") is paid off.
- **Colour.** Only the cold open, the title spread and the page after it are in colour (i0003–i0006); the story's crises are all grayscale.

## L. Counterreadings (mandatory)

1. **Mochiko as a fair competitor, not a manipulator.** She holds that traders are "敵同士" (V02); the contest makes the
   others her opponents; she never promised advice; "誰も相場は予測できない" (i0101) is literally true; her first lie may
   simply avoid becoming the paying winner (i0090, i0093), or console a junior who has just said "やっぱり私だけ…!" (i0089). Against: the page gives her the stated purpose of keeping
   Mebuki in a losing short "until her heart breaks" (i0150), knowledge that the money is tuition (i0099), and pleasure
   ("面白い", i0151). The reading survives only for the first lie.
2. **Mochiko's advice as kindness.** At night with a weeping junior she calms her ("まぁまぁ 落ちついて"), tells her the
   deposit was not a mistake and that expulsion is not immediate (i0099–i0100). Against: the private "焼豚" line comes first
   (i0099), and the advice supports exactly the holding she privately thinks hopeless (i0098). Partial: comfort and
   steering can coexist.
3. **Mebuki as the market's victim.** Three scheduled events (BoE, FOMC, BoJ) move the market against her, and the last is
   a surprise to "多く" participants (i0161). Against: 246× with no stop into an event she did not know of (i0032, i0041);
   tuition as margin; the refused +229,700 and her abandoned plan (i0130–i0131). The shocks are the occasion; the
   exposure is hers.
4. **Kurumi's reticence as responsibility.** Her stay-out answer on the pound (i0025–i0026) was the protective stance, it
   ends a dependence the V02 reading called unhealthy, and her "様子見" may be honest uncertainty. Against: she knew of the
   8/13 release and gave no reason for waiting (i0042), and in V02 she used Mebuki's views without telling her. The page
   leaves open whether she owed Mebuki the reason; it does not make her stance a cause of the loss, since Mebuki overrode it.
5. **Yasuko's warning as projection.** She never profited (i0142) and may be speaking from fear. Against: her reasoning is
   specific (a one-yen rebound, Mebuki's loss of calm, i0139–i0141) and the page bears it out (i0155–i0158).
6. **The narrator as unreliable or merely tonal.** The didactic voice is part of the comedy, and it concedes that steadfast
   belief can win big (i0135). Against: its prolepses come true within the volume (i0136 → i0137–i0158) and its judgement on
   Mochiko's role (i0158) is consistent with what the reader has seen.
7. **"Ruin" may be premature.** The volume never prints Mebuki's result after 10/31; at i0158 her equity is ≈541,900 by CALC;
   the drawn candle reaches ≈110.4 on an axis that may not be to scale. The governing thesis therefore speaks of harm done
   (the 8/13 wipe-out, tuition and food money at risk, weeks of skipped classes, a friendship strained) and of a ceiling
   broken, not of a final loss.
8. **Kurumi's skill vs luck.** Mebuki's "運だけ" and Mochiko's "なかなかのギャンブラー" vs Mochiko's own "ナメすぎだ" (i0126),
   Kurumi's macro reasoning (i0121–i0122) and her withdrawal (i0124). V03 cannot separate skill from luck for Kurumi; it shows
   that each character's verdict on it serves her own needs.
9. **Mebuki would have held anyway.** Before the call she restated "私はまだ下がると思います" (i0147), her exit thought was
