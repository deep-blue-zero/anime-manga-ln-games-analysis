## 5. Monographs: self-containment and fidelity

For FXK-P001 and FXK-P005 a probe in a fresh context answers the same questions from the monograph alone, and the reviewer compares each probe answer with the source (FAITHFUL, PARTLY, WRONG). *Fidelity*: the reviewer lists the load-bearing claims (identity and name forms, history and disclosure timing, decisive decisions, relationships, speech, development, the behavioural profile's support) and checks each against its locator, the readings, the ledger's current claim state and, for decisive points, the page, classifying SUPPORTED, OVERSTATED, UNSUPPORTED, MISATTRIBUTED or BOUNDARY_BREACH; it also checks the G3 rules and the five single-document usability failures. The author revises; *the principal* checks that the revision answers every finding and probe defect (each revision-log line traced to the text), verifies a sample of decisive corrections on the page (section 8), checks every restatement of Study B and Study C against the published studies, and decides the two results separately.
