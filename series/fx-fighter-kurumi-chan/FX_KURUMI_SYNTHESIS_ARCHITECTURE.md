# FX Fighter Kurumi-chan — Synthesis Architecture

## A cumulative account of depicted decisions

[Current State and Corpus Map](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) is the canonical entrypoint. The [source register](FX_KURUMI_SOURCE_REGISTER.md) fixes the candidate Japanese collected-manga lane. The [method](FX_KURUMI_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) supplies disclosure, page inspection and evidence standards. This foundation asks how the manga makes its characters inhabit changing uncertainty and how different kinds of visible value, information and obligation change the action that seems possible to them.

| Need | Capture from a volume | Canonical cumulative home and future responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A recoverable account of what changed | Perceived alternatives and available information, a depicted choice, changes and human consequences with separate times and actors | [Decision and evidence ledger](FX_KURUMI_DECISION_AND_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md) tracks material recurrences. Supports cross-volume interpretation of agency under uncertainty. |
| Evidence of individual and relational change | Distinctive habits and speech, ordinary preferences, reliance, knowledge gaps, reversals, accountability and attempted or accepted aid | Ledger records directed, horizon-specific changes and qualification evidence. Supports person and relationship synthesis. |
| How the manga renders risk | Screen framing, sign and figure provenance, distance from action, reaction panels and the time created by layout | Owning reading supplies direct-page argument. Recurring visual cases use the same ledger evidence IDs. |
| Whether a governing explanation survives a counterexample | Material contrary case, claim transition, separately attributed prospect and later disposition | Ledger’s claim and forecast history. Supports review and revision of the main argument. |

One initialized cumulative ledger connects the figure or word, what a character could know, the choice and the later narrative consequence. Separate balance sheets or panel logs would break that retrieval path. Preserve narrowly local numerical displays and minor formal observations in the volume reading. Add a separate home only if a material recurrent argument cannot be retrieved through the same transaction evidence.

## Future independent reading

The principal analyst and integrator inspects the entire admitted volume and owns the canonical reading. An independent specialist follows the source’s information and quantities through its interfaces and narrative transitions, including what each depicted figure could actually mean. On especially dense units add a specialist on interpersonal accountability, ordinary-life preferences, speech and affective staging. A distinct independent reviewer directly opens the decisive pages and challenges numeric attribution, scene order, unshown intervening causes, character or reader exposure, and significant counterexamples. The standing configuration is principal, independent specialist and distinct independent reviewer. No reduced category is designated.

Begin those contexts with the same authorized source frontier and role-specific assignment. Let specialists develop evidence independently of the principal’s draft and prefer prior diagnostic-source inspection for reviewers. Record actual exposure, any contamination, and the remaining scope of independent or prospective credit. The principal reconciles in `checkpoints/FX_KURUMI_Vnn_CHECKPOINT.md` with each assignment, pages inspected, independent observations, conflicting explanations, pressure tests, unresolved obligations and attributed disposition. Publish the material findings in their canonical evidence and claim homes. The integrator alone updates the shared ledgers in the unit transaction. A capability gap keeps the unit pending without high-water advance. Follow [topology §4.1](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EXECUTION_TOPOLOGY_AND_CAPABILITY_ROUTING_POLICY.md#multi-agent-source-reading) and the [handoff contract](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md).

## From volumes to synthesis

Use `readings/FX_KURUMI_Vnn_DEEP_READING.md` for each accepted unit. Its receipt, freezes, near-front synopsis completed after prospective capture, direct reading, revision manifest and checkpoint support source completion. Keep boundaries for market/action time, disclosure, private knowledge and present interpretation in both the reading and ledger. Compare character report, page-rendered display, third-party statement, inferred calculation and later correction separately. When numerical or causal tensions matter to the thesis, reopen the verified page, test which edition and time the display represents, preserve rivals and record unresolved differences. Stabilize a claim only when both downstream and contrary evidence are retrievable.

After accepted source completion, reconcile state, evidence locators, calculation provenance and claim history. A role-gap review then decides which anticipated syntheses deserve separate space. The work’s treatment of institutional language and rendered screens can clarify who appears to have agency. A longitudinal account of obligations and relationships can show where the cost of a choice lands. Let those accounts interact in integrated synthesis when the authorized evidence supports it. Distinct gates govern state reconciliation, specialist readiness, whole-boundary synthesis, semantic validation, and approved release/freeze. Use fresh, scope-bounded retrospective contexts and recover the exact evidence and authorizations through the handoff contract.

Keep person qualification decisions in the decision ledger. Include ordinary conduct and linguistic behavior as well as crises, compare relevant counterexamples, and assess each adequately evidenced person within the declared period. Qualified portraits live in `characters/<subject>-manga.md` and satisfy the [self-contained monograph standard](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md#self-contained-character-monograph-standard). Review portrait-alone retrieval and source fidelity separately. Record missing pieces and reassessment conditions for other candidates. Observe the handoff contract’s named-subject check. Reconstruction depends on separate scope, evidence and validation.

Keep the map, source register and current ledger views mutable. Preserve frozen forecasts and exit records; revise a meaningful interpreted claim by append-only transition and link to the revised authority. Use targeted errata or supersession for frozen material. If an admitted component or edition changes, update scope explicitly rather than treating volume numbers as equivalence. Production may use local inspection and fresh grounded synthesis with designated repository integration under the [long-series protocol](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_LONG_SERIES_HYBRID_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md). Assign BOUNDED_STANDARD to metadata, ROUTINE_FAST to deterministic checks, SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS to units, DEEP_SYNTHESIS to cross-volume work, and PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST to high-propagation architecture and contradiction/integration review. Revisit the architecture at a natural evidence boundary; add a durable record only to address a demonstrated coverage or retrieval gap.

### Claim transitions made by a synthesis

Synthesis transitions of accepted claims are append-only rows `FXK-SYN-Cnnn` at the end of the ledger's claim-and-forecast table, with its columns. Each row names its synthesis ID and the claim's last explicit transition, and points to the audit or study section that carries the argument. The series is global for the project: a later synthesis continues the numbering. A transition whose evidence on both sides is already accepted and which needs no study's argument is decided at G2; one that needs a study's argument, and every new cross-volume claim, is argued in G3 and written at G3 integration; G4 decides none.

### Person qualification and the monograph naming rule

Qualification outcomes use the initiation policy's vocabulary and are kept in the ledger's qualification table, with the arguments in the qualification audit; subject IDs (FXK-P001 onward) are permanent and a later boundary appends new ones. At the V01–V10 boundary FXK-P001, FXK-P002, FXK-P003, FXK-P005, FXK-P006, FXK-P008, FXK-P020 and FXK-P021 are QUALIFIED_PENDING, each an obligation for G3. A monograph's path is `characters/<subject>-manga.md`, where `<subject>` is the lowercase ASCII kebab-case romanization of the fullest name the pages establish for the person within the boundary, in printed order (family name first for Japanese names), in modified Hepburn without macrons or honorifics; a name component known only by inference is used only when the pages print it for a person the ledger identifies with the subject at INFERENCE HIGH or above, and the monograph states the inference. An intended path is written as plain text until an accepted monograph exists; self-containment and fidelity are decided and recorded separately.

### Ledger size rule

The ledger stays under 1,048,576 bytes. A synthesis adds compact records only (one sentence per cell; IDs and locators rather than quotation), and the full arguments live in audits and studies; FXK-SYN-20261123-V01-V10 caps its additions at 60,000 bytes across G2–G5. No transaction may bring the file above 1,000,000 bytes: such a transaction opens a successor ledger instead, linked from the entrypoint, with the existing file frozen for V01–V10 and every ID preserved. The navigation index lives in the reconciliation audit and is regenerated from row IDs, never kept as a second home.
