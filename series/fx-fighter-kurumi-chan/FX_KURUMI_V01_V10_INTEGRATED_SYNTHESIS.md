## 1. Three accounts, one boundary: the common ground and the thesis in brief

### 1.1 What the three accounts separate

Ordinary talk about a financial ruin fuses four things that FX戦士くるみちゃん keeps apart. The page shows separately **who decides** (an order, a deposit, a refusal), **who executes** (a fill, a forced close, a refused order), **who knows** (the figure, the rule, the other person's position) and **who pays** (whose money, whose debt, whose labour, whose grief). Study A separated deciding, executing, the grammatical agent of the page's notice and bearing; Study C tabulated, for every decision episode, the information the person held and the action's status from intention to realization; Study B mapped who knew what and when and where each cost landed. Read together, the three accounts show the same thing from three sides: in V01–V10 these four roles almost never fall to the same party, and the series' argument is carried by the way it pulls them apart and, once, puts them back together. The exception proves the pattern: Kurumi's two V06 hand cuts, in which she decides, orders, knows and pays, are the only realized losses of hers that she executes herself, and they end nothing (V06 i0013, i0046; Study A, R6).

### 1.2 Common ground adopted from convergence

The convergence audit verified sixteen agreements among the studies (AG-01 to AG-16) and four further convergences (its section 3). They are premises here, not conclusions:

- **Seven account-ending losses, none closed by its holder.** Six are realized by a broker (Mebuki on 31 October and 5 December 2014; Kozue on 6 October 2008; Kurumi on 15 January 2015 and on the V08 night; Yasuko in 2013) and one has no shown mechanism (Mebuki, 13 August 2014) (AG-01; V04 i0012; V06 i0039, i0080; V07 i0118; V08 i0020; V10 i0076; V03 i0047).
- **The person sets the exposure before any institution acts; no ruin shows an institution deciding** (AG-03; FXK-SYN-C016, C018, C026).
- **The strong form of the account-regime finding is refused**: that a ruin's size depends more on the account regime than on the trader's decisions holds for none of the seven (AG-02; FXK-SYN-C018).
- **Kurumi chose the domestic account herself**, in V02, for less "即死リスク" and for tax (V02 i0017); the debt arose in the account chosen to lower risk (AG-04; that the V07 account is that domestic account is INFERENCE HIGH, V06 i0013, i0046, i0095; FXK-SYN-C014).
- **A floor does not bound a refunded ruin**: Mebuki's and Kozue's ruins are as large as their own funding made them, and the 0円 floor moved Mebuki's losses onto her lenders (AG-05; FXK-SYN-C022, C028).
- **What stopped Kurumi after the ruin was held by others and by an institution**: the discharge, payment "地道に", her father's management of her wages (AG-06; V09 i0104, i0106–i0107).
- **Kurumi's V06 losses were hand cuts, followed by a repudiation of the stop rule argued from goal proximity** (AG-07; V06 i0013, i0046, i0099).
- **Mebuki's harm is self-authored; Mochiko's deception and the hedged loan are a confirmed, admitted wrong whose causal weight the page leaves undetermined** (AG-08; FXK-SYN-C008, C009).
- **The household money's path, the late revelation of the 300万's purpose and the identical 1,005,144 as design** (AG-09; FXK-SYN-C021).
- **The 2014 tax is the one obligation shown surviving the bankruptcy**, partly unpaid in April 2017 (AG-10; V09 i0106; V10 i0045).
- **Restraint lodged outside the trading moment; early reward postpones who pays; Mochiko's information position; the funding regime** — the four convergences of the audit's section 3, which sections 5–7 below develop.

### 1.3 The integrated thesis in brief

The synthesis argues six claims. Each is stated with its evidence, contrary evidence, rivals and overturn condition in the section named.

1. **Agency (section 2).** The person decides the exposure; an institution executes the end; the market supplies the discontinuity; the account's terms route what outruns the stake. Inside the person, the desire is presented as given and the return as chosen, and insight into one's own error is accurate and inert. Between persons, the page makes concealment and withholding into acts.
2. **Uncertainty (section 3).** The traders are rarely uncertain about themselves. The uncertainty that ruins them is about rules the page withholds, about a discontinuity no one can trade through, and about other people's figures and intentions; it is held unequally, and the page gives the reader the ledger the persons lack. Certainty is manufactured, or a binary embraced, exactly where exposure is largest; the protective response the page rewards is to bound exposure, not to forecast better.
3. **Responsibility (section 4).** The page keeps apart who brought a loss about, to whom the page's own voice assigns it, and who answers for it. Institutions' text assigns nothing; the narrator assigns cause to persons; persons fill the empty agent's place with their own 自己責任 or with a hostile market. The series does not reject self-responsibility; it tests it by what it is joined to and condemns its union with concealment.
4. **Cost (section 5).** Cost travels by knowledge. Concealment sends the first cost of a choice to people who do not know of it; early reward postpones the question of who pays; refunding moves a floored loss to the funders; an account's terms route the excess beyond the stake; and the largest sum leaves the depicted world while the costs the page dwells on fall on the nearest people.
5. **The integrating claim (section 6).** Restraint and cost are carried in the same place: outside the trading moment, by structures and by other people. Concealment severs the trader from both — the people who bear her cost cannot restrain her because they do not know — and the one chosen disclosure of the series (V09) reconnects them, so that the people who bear the cost by consent become the holders of restraint.
6. **Information as capital (section 7).** Information is the one capital on the page that compounds without exposure. Mochiko holds every party's figures, the rules and the signal of a friend's capitulation, and withholds judgment; the reader is given the same ledger without the power to act; the trader who carries the largest cost holds the least of it; and giving information away costs the giver first.

## 2. Agency: who acts, when, and inside whom

### 2.1 Between the person and the institution

**Claim I-1.** In V01–V10 the person decides the exposure — size, stop policy and funding — and so decides whether an adverse move can end the account and how large the stake is; an institution executes the end, after the fact and in agentless grammar; the market supplies the discontinuity; and the account's terms decide who carries a loss that outruns the stake. The page also displaces agency in time: the decisive choices are made in calm scenes, and the losses arrive while the person is not deciding anything. EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH; the printed ends and the grammar of the notices are OBSERVATION HIGH.

**Evidence.** The adjudicated wording of FXK-SYN-C018 (convergence audit §4.4) is the premise: exposure with a market gap decides whether a loss outruns the stake, and the account's terms decide who carries the excess — in the one case the page shows, the customer, as 101,534,644円 (V07 i0118, i0130). Study A's claim A1 (FXK-SYN-C016) supplies the grammar: every printed end is a noun, a state, an absence or an automatic process — 「強制ロスカット」, 「0円」, 「保有ポジションはありません」, 「自動ロスカットが執…」 (V04 i0012; V06 i0039; V07 i0118; V08 i0020; V10 i0076) — and the one time the broker addresses a named person it states her obligation (V07 i0130). Study C's SC-2 (FXK-SYN-C026) supplies the decision: what turns a loss into the end of an account is set before the broker acts, by size and funding against a reference point.

The two accounts meet at a point neither states alone. The page's grammar matches its causal structure *in time*. The decisions that fix the V07 figure were taken in December, in a scene of reasoning and bravado: "勝った!! 必勝全力の 300枚買い!!!" (V06 i0115) and "あえて してない!" (V06 i0152). On 15 January Kurumi shops and eats (V07 i0075–i0079); there is no decision on the day (Study A, R7). On the V08 night the rule is stated before the first click — "読みが外れても 損切りとか 考えない" (V08 i0006) — and the end comes through nine open-loss boxes in which she does nothing (i0016–i0019). The series' largest realized gain is executed the same way, by a limit order filled while she was away (V05 i0151–i0152), and V01's win arrives after she stops looking (V01 i0141–i0143, i0157). Agency on these pages is exercised in calm and paid for in absence. That is why the institution can be the executor of every end without ever being the agent of a decision: by the time it acts, nothing is left to decide.

The V07 figure shows how the three parties divide one outcome. Exposure is the person's (300 lots without a stop, chosen in December; about 24.4× on 18,339,915, conditional on the lot convention, FXK-V06-C033). Discontinuity is the market's: "一部のFX業者は 適切なレートを 提示できず", 「この区間 取引成立せず」 (V07 i0114, narrator). The terms are the account's. The 84.4% of the printed parenthetical that became debt (101,534,644 ÷ 120,284,479, ANALYST CALCULATION) is an accounting split, not a causal share: the convergence audit's conditional calculation shows that a position of 46 lots or fewer, filled as the 300 were, would have ended at or above zero (18,749,835 ÷ (120,284,479 ÷ 300) = 46.8, ANALYST CALCULATION, conditional on a loss linear in lot count at the same unprinted fill). Size and gap decide *whether* a loss outruns the stake; terms decide *who carries* what outruns it.

**Contrary evidence.** (1) One institutional decision is printed, and it releases the person: 「破産手続き終結及び免責許可決定」 (V09 i0104). The claim holds for market institutions; the law's one printed decision is relief. (2) The regime was itself chosen. Kurumi moved to the domestic account in V02 because it carried less "即死リスク" and for tax (V02 i0017); the choice was hers, but no page shows that she knew the account could end below zero, so the tail was not chosen knowingly on any page (convergence audit §4.3 (6)). (3) The platform once executes by refusal: 「エラー:証拠金不足です」, six times (V08 i0023); its effect is protective, and Kurumi experiences it as obstruction ("なんで売れないの?").

**Rivals.** *The account-regime finding in its strong form* — that a ruin's size depends more on the regime than on the trader's decisions — is refused for all seven account-ending losses (FXK-SYN-C018). *A purely psychological reading* explains the stake and the occurrence of every ruin but not why V07 ended in a debt and V08, at a larger size and with the same declared refusal of a stop, at 0円 (V06 i0152 against V08 i0006; 300 against 1000 lots); the residual rivals are the regime and the gap, which the page does not separate (D-3, OPEN; the specification over the gap at INFERENCE MODERATE, FXK-SYN-C014). *The design reading* — institutions as plot devices — is compatible inside the fiction and stronger than Study A first allowed: the decisive box carries no regime label (V07 i0118), no stop-out rule is printed for either protagonist's broker (U-06), and the domestic specification arrives only as Mochiko's unspoken thought a volume later (V08 i0106). Its intent stays OPEN (D-2); it cannot remove the exposure, which every ruin prints and its holder reasons out in advance.

**What would overturn it.** A page on which a market institution decides a ruin — warns, chooses a rate or a time against a person, or is the grammatical subject of a close; an overseas account below zero after a gap, or a domestic account stopping at or above zero after one; a page showing that Kurumi knew the domestic tail when she chose it. None exists within V10.

### 2.2 Between persons: the wrong and the loss kept apart

The page assigns agency for a loss and agency for a wrong separately, and it refuses to convert the one into a share of the other. Mebuki's case is the test, argued in Study B's strand (iv) and written as FXK-SYN-C008, C009, C011 and C012. Every order, deposit, add and refusal is hers (V03 i0058–i0131; V04 i0013–i0032; V05 i0060–i0091). When Mebuki learned the truth she still did not close (V05 i0145 → V06 i0039), which weakens the strongest causal reading of the lie — that it removed her one exit — without settling it, since early December is not 15 October (V03-U05). Her own V07 sentences hold both at once: "私は勝手に盛り上がって自滅したマヌケ" (V07 i0014–i0015) and "騙されていたのは事実で" (i0018). The page's last word is that the harm is self-authored and the wrong is real, and that the second does not buy a share of the first. Two other kinds of act between persons carry agency the market never has. **Concealment** is an act, not an absence: Kurumi takes her father's cash and books it as a deposit (V01 i0109–i0111); Kozue hides her losses from her husband, by her own account (V06 i0082, mixed authority); Kurumi hides the debt so as not to trouble her father (「親ニ迷惑カケルナ」 among the voices of V07 i0128). **Withholding judgment** is an act when the withholder knows: Mochiko reads the danger of Kurumi's franc position exactly, asks one question — "さすがに 損切り注文くらい してるよね?" (V06 i0151) — and then decides, inside, that it is not her concern: "全部くるみが決めたことだろう?" (V06 i0154). Four further interventions are withheld in V07 (FXK-V07-C009) and a fifth in V08 (i0111–i0112). Whether that silence was negligence or respect is left open by the page (D-15). What the page settles is that it was a choice, because Mochiko held the knowledge that would have made speech possible (Study B §4).

### 2.3 Inside the person: the desire given, the return chosen

**Claim (adopted from FXK-SYN-C007, with its OPEN strand).** Kurumi's returns to the market are depicted as choices — alternatives named, reasons her own, responsibility claimed in her own words — made against her own accurate prediction, and in V09 declared openly as a conditional future; the desire that motivates them is presented as given, by the narrator as a curse and by Kurumi as innate greed. "Not a compulsion" stays OPEN. EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH.

The two voices that make the desire given are on the page: the narrator's "一生消えることのない呪い" (V02 i0132–i0134; FXK-V02-C001, the run's most consistently confirmed claim, last strengthened by Kurumi's own "たまたま ハイレバFXで 上手くいったから", V10 i0049) and Kurumi's "根っからの 欲張り" (V09 i0031), staged by re-presenting a childhood beach memory with the winged figure added (the V05 memory at i0161 has none; INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH that it is the same memory) — a late re-reading, not a change of her earlier conduct. The choices are on the page too: the V02 licence "自己責任の 名のもとに" (V02 i0131); "でも 仕方ない じゃん" after she names the re-enactment (V07 i0147); the refusal of her father's demanded vow, "それは誓えない…!" (V09 i0078), and its terms, "次に投資を やりたくなった そのときは / 堂々と やりたいんだ" (i0079). Against "not a compulsion" stand a retirement reversed within weeks (V02 i0040 → i0102), an assent to her father broken by the relapse (V02 i0038 → i0130) and a decided cut she could not execute (V01 i0131, i0135); her abstention after V09 is confounded with constraint — the discharge, her father's management of her wages and 保有金融資産 22,963円 (V09 i0104–i0107; V10 i0043). The V05 resolution to retire at the target is conditional on a target never reached within V10 (V05 i0155; V06 i0096) and is evidence neither way (convergence audit, P-01).

The integrated point is where the series puts Kurumi's agency. It is not in what she wants, which both voices treat as given, nor in what she knows, which is accurate from V01 (section 3.1). It is in the *manner* of acting on a want she cannot choose: concealed or open. "堂々と" (V09 i0079) is a choice about how future choices will be made, the reverse of "お父さんに バレずに チャラにしたい" (V09 i0040). Section 6 argues that this is the variable on which the series hangs both where cost lands and whether restraint can hold.

## 3. Uncertainty: what the traders know, what they cannot know, and what the page knows for them

**Claim I-2.** The traders of V01–V10 are rarely uncertain about themselves: their insight into their own errors is usually accurate. The uncertainty that ruins them lies in three places — rules the page withholds, a discontinuity no one can trade through, and other people's figures and intentions — and it is distributed unequally among the persons, while the page gives the reader the exact ledger they lack. Faced with uncertainty, the persons manufacture certainty or embrace a binary exactly where their exposure is largest; the response the page shows holding is to bound exposure at the moment of funding, not to forecast better. EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH.

### 3.1 About themselves: accurate and inert

Study C's SC-3 (FXK-SYN-C027) is the premise. Kurumi names her own error as she commits it — "完全に 欲張った" (V01 i0084) — and her own type before the relapse: "このまま続けたら 負ける人間" (V02 i0101). She names the stop cluster at 1.20 that will ruin her and reasons it away (V06 i0128–i0137). After the shock she names the repetition of her mother's course and proceeds: "これじゃ私も お母さんと おんなじ——" / "でも 仕方ない じゃん" (V07 i0147). Mebuki computes her own death lines correctly (V03 i0073, i0078, i0110; V04 i0021) and, in V08, states a model of the gap between knowing and doing: "なんか 負けてる ときって 頭が 悪くなる気が するんです…" (V08 i0107, re-opened). Kozue's journal records the danger before she meets it: "強制ロスカットされたらどうしよう" (V06 i0072, journal; mixed authority). Every holder of an account-ending position named the danger beforehand, except Yasuko in 2013, whom the page gives no stated awareness before the rise (Study C §2.7). The form figures the dread at the moment it is reasoned away: a wordless page sets a skeletal Death figure behind Kurumi as she places the franc order (V06 i0116–i0117), and Mochiko's vision turns the chart into a monster before her single question (i0151–i0153). The V06 reading treats both figures as genre staging, not authorial verdicts; either way, the page shows the danger as known.

### 3.2 About rules, discontinuities and other people: withheld and unequal

- **Rules.** No stop-out level is printed for Kurumi's or Mebuki's broker in ten volumes (U-06, OPEN). The one threshold printed belongs to Yasuko's broker in a 2013 flashback: 「証拠金維持率が100%…」 (V10 i0076). The only sentence on any page that a domestic account can end in debt is Mochiko's unspoken thought, after Mebuki assumes Kurumi's loss stopped at zero: 「そういえば 芽吹は 国内口座の仕様を 知らないんだっけか」 (V08 i0106). Mebuki did not know her own account's rule when she first lost — "まさかそのまま借金!?" (V03 i0046) — and learned the floor by losing. Knowledge of the regime is held unequally and learned by losing, never from the institution (FXK-SYN-C018). Study A's claim A2 (FXK-SYN-C017) names the shape of the gap: the boxes and screens are exact wherever their operands are printed, and what is missing is the rule, not the number.
- **Discontinuity.** The gap is narrated as a time in which no trade was possible: 「この区間 取引成立せず」 (V07 i0114). Even the protection a stop would have given is uncertain on the page; Mochiko's counterfactual is "た・ぶ・ん助かってるはず" (V07 i0115), a friend's estimate on schematic charts. Whether a stop near 1.20 would have filled is UNRESOLVED.
- **Other people.** Mochiko reports a loss while her box prints a gain — "こんなの正直に言う必要ないし" (V03 i0090) — and tells Mebuki she is short while long (V03 i0148; V04 i0087). Kurumi is never shown learning of Mebuki's loan or its lender within V10 (V05-U20). In 2017 Kurumi keeps the scale of her own loss from her colleagues: "最高機密! トップシークレットです!" (V10 i0044, re-opened); Inago imagines something like 10万 (the attribution the V10 reading fixed). Section 7 treats this distribution as the page's economy of information.

### 3.3 Forecasting is not where the series puts failure

The page separates the quality of a call from the size of a ruin. Kurumi's V02 calls are right (V02 i0072, i0120); two of her three V08 trades pay before the third ends at 0円 (V08 i0009, i0012, i0020); her V07 forecast rested on an official's reaffirmation of the floor (V07 i0070–i0073) and was, in the V07 reading's words as Study C cites them, "not irrational; it failed". Mochiko, the series' most disciplined trader, admits she cannot read the move that is paying her — "まったくわからない…!" (V04 i0144–i0145) — and profits because her size is modest and her losses are cut by rule (Study C §6.5). Where reasoning fails, the page marks it as reasoning, not luck: Kozue reads a one-year range that her own July entry contradicts (V06 i0068, i0070; mixed authority). The narrator states the series' theory of market uncertainty in one image: "平等に機会を与え / 平等に殺す", drawn as a pair of scales, then four friends in the same instant, only one of whom holds a position (V07 i0079–i0082). The shock is equal; what it does depends on what each person holds (Study A §9; Study C §5).

### 3.4 Certainty manufactured where exposure is largest; bounding where restraint holds

The largest exposures are taken under manufactured certainty or an embraced binary. The franc trade is "必勝" and guarded by a state — "スイス国家が 守ってくれている" (V06 i0115); the V08 night is "簡単すぎる…", "勝ち確…!" (V08 i0011–i0013) after "ゼロになるか 1億円になるか" (i0006). In V10 the same register returns in others: Nana's "3000%", "間違いない" (V10 i0103) and Kizuna's "全然怖くない" (V10 i0123). Perceived control on these pages is a state that alternates with luck attributions as outcomes turn, not a belief a person holds (Study C, model 6).

The responses that hold are acts that bound exposure when the money is placed, not better forecasts: the bracket of stop and limit (V05 i0077, i0080), filled while Kurumi was away for the run's realized peak, +17,602,200 (V05 i0152); the 1,000,000 withdrawn "無茶して 失敗しないように" (V03 i0124); Mochiko's stop (V03 i0152) and her 2010 cut against her own hope (V05 i0034–i0035); Yasuko's exit after reviewing her own record (V04 i0046); Nana's transfer that leaves about half her savings in her bank account, outside the market — "貯金は 半分くらい 残しておけば 急な出費が あっても 問題ないと 思うし…" — executed in a chain of boxes from bank to securities account to a planned purchase of 736円 × 500株 = 368,000円 (「Kani購入予定金額」, V10 i0132, re-opened), bought two days later at 741 (V10 i0142). The series' schedule of outcomes then trains its traders the other way: the V01 win pays the worst process (FXK-V01-C011; the 150枚 add made about 85% of the profit, ANALYST CALCULATION 1,120,500 ÷ 1,311,500 on the printed legs of FXK-V01-C014); the V06 hand cuts saved about 4.2 million円 the same night (ANALYST CALCULATION from V06 E008: 5,898,000 − 1,743,000) and were repudiated on the later fall, "やっぱ 損切り いらなかったじゃん" (V06 i0098; Study C, model 7). Outcome learning, not ignorance, is what the page shows undoing restraint.

**Contrary evidence.** Novices are genuinely ignorant and the page lectures them: Mebuki did not know what an indicator was (V03 i0041); Nana's first order is refused for being below the trading unit (V10 i0105). Overconfidence is a failure of self-estimate (V01 i0073, i0110; V04 i0062). Kozue's misread range is a reasoning error about the market, not about herself.

**Rivals.** *The instructional reading*: the work cures ignorance, and its lectures are the cure. True for rules, which the narrator and Mochiko teach (V04 i0096–i0097; V08 i0030–i0034; V09 i0017–i0019); but the lectures arrive after the ruin they explain (Study A, claim A4, FXK-SYN-C019), and the restraints that held were structural, not informational. *The forecasting reading*: the ruins are bad calls. The page separates the two (section 3.3).

**What would overturn it.** A ruin traced on the page to a trader's ignorance of her own position or risk, which better self-knowledge would have prevented without any outside carrier; or a page on which a better forecast, rather than a bound on exposure, saves a trader from ruin. None exists within V10.

## 4. Responsibility: what the page assigns, and what persons answer for

**Claim I-3.** The page keeps three allocations apart: who brought a loss about (causal responsibility), to whom the page's own voices assign it (the institutions' notices, the narrator), and who answers for it in word and act. The institutions' text assigns nothing. The narrator assigns the cause of ruin to the person and describes institutions as mechanism or impartial force, and is silent on the one institutional term that decided the reach of the largest ruin. The persons fill the agent's place the notices leave empty, either with their own 自己責任 or with a hostile, personified market. The series does not reject self-responsibility; it tests it by what it is joined to, and the union it shows failing is self-responsibility joined to concealment. EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH; the vocabulary as a motif EVALUATION HIGH (every occurrence lettered).

### 4.1 The page's own assignment, and where it falls silent

Study A's claims A1 and A5 (FXK-SYN-C016, C020) fix the page's voices. The narrator's verdicts are on persons and their acts: "ただの博打(ギャンブル)なのだ" (V01 i0075–i0077), "ただの衝動買い" (V01 i0128), "とどのつまりただのポジション" against Mebuki's labelled lots (V05 i0090), "くるみの目はもう なにも見えなくなっていた" (V07 i0150), and, in V10, investing rooted in desire: "その 根底には / 必ず / 欲望が 存在する" (V10 i0052–i0053). The institutions it describes are mechanism or impartial force: liquidation executed "ただ機械的に" and called "温情" (V01 i0101); the market as scales that "平等に殺す" (V07 i0079–i0080); the brokers, once, as an incapacity — "適切なレートを 提示できず" (V07 i0114). The characters, by contrast, personify the market or the dealer as hunter and killer: "奴らは 問答無用で 狩りにくる!" (V01 i0100); "あたしを… 殺すためだけに 上げたのか…!?" (V10 i0076). The narrator's irony loosens its verdicts in V01–V02 — her refusal to look is "ある意味では 勇気があった" just before the win (V01 i0142–i0143) — but it never moves responsibility onto an institution, and after V07 verdict and outcome agree.

Set against the convergence ruling, the narrator is right about who decided and silent about who routed the excess. Study C's finding that the person sets the exposure (FXK-SYN-C026) is what the narrator says, volume after volume. The account's terms, which decided that the V07 excess became Kurumi's debt (FXK-SYN-C018), are never named by the narrator; they appear only in Mochiko's unspoken thought a volume later (V08 i0106). The page's moral voice and its causal structure diverge exactly there. Whether the silence is a design choice that keeps the moral focus on the person (Study A §7.6; convergence D-2) is OPEN as to intent; what it does is plain: the reader is given the verdict on the person and the figure of the debt (V07 i0118, i0130), and must reconstruct for herself the term that turned the one into the other.

### 4.2 What persons answer for: the vocabulary tested

Study B's strand (iii), written as the extension FXK-SYN-C013, follows 自己責任 by speaker and function through every lettered occurrence. It moves away from an adviser as a disclaimer — "投資は自己責任だからね!?" (Kurumi to Mebuki, V02 i0066) and Mochiko's "知・っ・た・こ・と・で・は・な・い" (V04 i0052). It becomes a licence for Kurumi's return, "自己責任の 名のもとに" (V02 i0131). Yasuko joins it to care: "あたしの責任だよ" with a friend's duty to intervene (V04 i0132, i0136), and in V05 both she and Mochiko accept that Mebuki's case is "芽吹の自己責任" while disagreeing whether responsibility ends care (V05 i0132–i0133). A salon customer gives it a third, clean shape between strangers: "大丈夫責任は僕にあるから" (V06 i0035). It becomes a reason for silence (V06 i0154), an accusing chorus (V07 i0128), and solitary acceptance — "私が選んだ結果だし / 自己責任だもん" (V07 i0140). In V08 it is a duty turned toward self-erasure (V08 i0046, i0068, i0130, i0139; analysed without method). In V09 the word is absent, and in its place stand disclosure (V09 i0063, re-opened), "堂々と" (i0079), "私は地道に払っていくよ!" (i0106) and an agreed control of her wages (i0107, re-opened). The father takes a share of the 2008 silence on himself — "父さんが相談しづらくしてたんだろう / 情けない話だ" (V09 i0073) — an accepted share of responsibility, not a confession of cause.

The grades of conduct toward persons stay on the readings' scale and never touch market outcomes: Kozue's taking of household and parents' money is deception in pursuit of recovery, without cruelty (V06 §I, mixed authority); Kurumi's V01 use of her father's cash is a knowing, concealed breach with repayment intent, unchanged by its favourable outcome (V01 §I, §L.5); Mochiko's V03 lies and canary are manipulation for informational gain with private enjoyment (EVALUATION HIGH), her V04 loan manipulation (INFERENCE HIGH), her V06–V07 silence understanding without exploitation, negligence or respect left open, and her V08 intervention rough means with no exploitation shown (Study B §5.1, §6).

### 4.3 The finding: answerability shared becomes payable, and the institution is reached through it

The integrated finding joins Study B's B3 to Study A's account of institutions. Solitary, hidden answerability failed twice on the page: in V08 it converted the debt into a duty to disappear, and the bankruptcy that would have released her was judged closed to her — the 予納金 "150万" (V08 i0034), "お金ないからできない" (V08 i0120). The one institutional decision in ten volumes, the court's release (V09 i0104), was reached only through shared answerability: a disclosure to her father (V09 i0063), his savings offered for the costs (i0074; their use INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH, V09-U14), a lawyer retained in early February 2015 (i0098). What Study A shows as an institution that speaks only when it collects or releases (claim A1), Study B shows as an institution that a person concealing alone cannot reach. And the one obligation the institutions refuse to release — the 2014 tax (V08 i0034; V09 i0106; V10 i0045) — stays with the person who incurred it, paid from wages another person holds by agreement. Responsibility on these pages ends where it can be shared, and stays where it cannot. INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH.

**Contrary evidence.** Kurumi's mother accepted responsibility in words while concealing — "これは私の 責任なんだから…" (V01 i0016) — and her concealment was as costly as her daughter's, which shows that the active element is concealment, with 自己責任 the name Kurumi later gives her own version of it. Mebuki bore her debt alone and came through (V06–V09), but she disclosed to her creditor at once (V06 i0051) and in part to her friends (V07 i0007–i0009): her case separates solitude from concealment. Mochiko never recants her doctrine (V07 i0135; V08 i0119). In V10 Kizuna, who does not know the size of the loss, re-describes it as greed — "だめだよ〜あんまり欲張ったら〜" — and Kurumi lets the description stand (V10 i0045): the series' last word on the vocabulary is not a vindication of sharing.

**Rivals.** *A didactic motif* with no argument beyond the lesson; *a psychology of shame* (Study C, model 13), in which concealment is a symptom, not a choice; *the institutional reading*, in which the procedure, not the sharing, made the debt payable. The last is answered above: on the page the procedure was reachable only through the sharing.

**What would overturn it.** A page on which Kurumi's solitary concealment ends well without disclosure, or on which disclosure fails to bring help; a narrator verdict that blames an institution's practice. None exists within V10.

## 5. Cost: where the cost of a choice lands

**Claim I-4.** Cost on these pages travels by knowledge. Concealment sends the first cost of a choice to people who do not know of it, defers it and enlarges it. Early reward postpones the question of who pays. A floored account that is refunded after each zero moves the loss to whoever funded the refunding. An account's terms route the excess beyond the stake. And the largest sum in the series passes through one person's life and then leaves the depicted world, while the costs the page dwells on are not money and fall on the people nearest to her. INFERENCE MODERATE as a cross-volume pattern, by the weakest-link rule of section 13: the concealment strand rests on FXK-SYN-C021 (INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH), the funding-floor and largest-sum strands on FXK-SYN-C022 and C023 (INFERENCE MODERATE); the transfers themselves are OBSERVATION HIGH as printed.

### 5.1 Concealment, and the people who do not know

Study B's B1, merged with Study A's household chain as FXK-SYN-C021, is the spine. In 2008 Kozue's household money, a cancelled insurance policy, the home down-payment and her parents' cash went into the market behind the backs of the people they belonged to (V06 i0071–i0077; mixed authority; the parents' ignorance INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH), and the secret was laid on a fifteen-year-old at her mother's plea (V01 i0014). In V01 Kurumi's father's 300万 carried nearly all of a 300-lot position's risk without his knowledge — about 94% of the equity at the deposit (ANALYST CALCULATION, 3,000,000 ÷ (3,811,600 − 629,000), conditional on the open loss at that moment; Study B §5.4). Mebuki's tuition was lost under her mother's note, "学費の貯金大変やろ?" (V03 i0159), and her parents' address and telephone were held as security on condition of her silence: "親を悲しませちゃだめだよ?" (V04 i0118–i0119). In V07–V08 Kurumi hid the debt from her father, and her plan, carried by a letter, would have handed him discovery, grief and a legal act it asked of him (V08 i0047, i0066–i0076). The word that hinges each concealment is 迷惑: "ずっと迷惑かけっぱなしや…" (V04 i0128); 「親ニ迷惑カケルナ」 (V07 i0128); 「迷惑をかけてごめんなさい。」 (V08 i0070). Each daughter hides what would let her parents help, so as not to be a burden, and so enlarges the burden she fears (Study B §8.2). The broker's box records only 「入金」, never whose money it is (V01 i0111); the persons relabel protected money as stakeable — "借りた", "無傷の100万円" (V02 i0014; V07 i0144) — and the box is the page's evidence that the market does not read the labels (Study A §10).

### 5.2 Early reward postpones who pays

The early volumes let risk land on no one because outcomes were favourable: Kurumi's V01 use of her father's cash (the trade closed at +1,311,510, V01 i0157), Mochiko's V02 steering, Kurumi's silent use of Mebuki's views as a contrary indicator, and the salon customer's trade on Mebuki's hunch all cost no one money (Study B §5.4). The later volumes show who had been carrying the risk. This is the obligation-side face of the consolidated wording of FXK-V01-C011, "an arc of early reward setting up later ruin" (reconciliation audit §5.5 row 1), whose mechanism section 8.2 states from all three sides.

### 5.3 Funding regimes: the floor that moves the loss

The convergence audit's shared finding (its §3 item 1; FXK-SYN-C022, C028) is that Mebuki's 0円 floor did not bound her ruin, because each zero was refunded — from a scholarship, tuition, living money, card cash called "魔法の入金方法", a student loan and Mochiko's 2,000,000 at 109.5% (V04 i0015–i0020, i0056, i0088, i0145) — until all 3,600,000 of her FX deposits were lost (ANALYST CALCULATION, reconciliation audit §4.5). The floor moved the loss to whoever funded the refunding. Credit between friends is the one channel the page renders as harm without exception (FXK-SYN-C022): the only loan between friends was built so that its lender "almost never loses" — "ほぼ負けることはなくなる / いいリスクヘッジでしょう?" (V05 i0131) — and was secured on the borrower's family; every other proposed credit between friends is refused (V01 i0065–i0066; V04 i0067–i0076; V08 i0119), and Yasuko states the norm: "借りる芽吹もアホだけど…貸すのは違うだろ…" (V04 i0143). Funding chains end by exhaustion or by outside hands, never on the page because the trader judged her position wrong with money still available — except Yasuko's V04 exit, which no protected-money chain lies behind (FXK-SYN-C028).

### 5.4 Terms route the excess, and the largest sum leaves the depicted world

The convergence audit's ruling on who pays (its §4.3 (8); FXK-SYN-C023) joins Study A's account terms to Study B's B4. The domestic terms made the V07 excess pass *through a person*: as Kurumi's debt of 101,534,644円 (V07 i0118, i0130), the V08 reckoning, the V08–V09 crisis and a bankruptcy whose costs came, by INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH, from her father's 300万 (V09 i0074–i0075; V09-U14). The discharge then moved it, by the only creditor's logic the page states — Mochiko's "立て替えた瞬間自己破産されたら泣き寝入りだし" (V08 i0119) — onto a creditor that never appears as a person, a voice or a loss. Kozue's 2008 account is the contrast: it printed no shortfall (V06 i0082, i0086), so the whole of her ≈1900万 came from persons' money and its last cost fell on her daughter.

The integrated point is that the regime's practical reach is the *passage* of the excess through a life, not its final resting place, and that the passage is what the page spends two volumes on. The money leaves the depicted world in a court notice that names only the bankrupt (V09 i0104). What stays, and what the page dwells on, are costs that are not money and that land on the nearest: the father's journeys and the savings he offers (V09 i0074, i0123); Mochiko's nights in an exam period (V09 i0051); the friends who tried to call when Mochiko had not (V08 i0107, re-opened); and, in V10, Nana, who had watched Yasuko's 2013 liquidation and says of it in the present "あんな やす子ちゃん 見たくなかったな…" (V10 i0080), her aversion to FX dating from it (FXK-V10-E035, INFERENCE HIGH). And the state's claim outlives the debt: the 2014 tax is paid "地道に" from wages her father holds (V09 i0106–i0107) and is still partly unpaid in April 2017 (V10 i0045).

**Contrary evidence.** In V01 the concealment cost the father nothing, because the trade won: the cost is a risk made visible only by later outcomes. A 配当 is recorded with no assets stated (V09 i0104; UNRESOLVED), so something reached the creditors. If the father's 300万 paid the costs (V09-U14), a depicted person bore part of the debt's passage in money. Mebuki's parents are never shown learning (V06-U03); their cost is inferred from her words and the narrator's. Yasuko conceals her trading past from her friends (V02 i0076, i0083) with no cost shown.

**Rivals.** *Outcome luck*: concealment is costly only when the market turns. The page shows both — concealment that cost nothing (V01) and concealment that cost everything (2008) — and the difference is the outcome; but the risk carried without consent is a cost the page grades as conduct (Study B §5.1), whatever the outcome. *A restoration comedy* (FXK-V09-C002, strengthened by FXK-V10-C049): the debt vanishes between two captions so that the plot can move on; this explains how quickly the money leaves the page, not where the page says the cost of the passage fell. *Family money roles as the cause*, of which concealment is a symptom: the father's own "お金のことは全部俺に任せて / お前は家のことだけやってたらいいんだ" (V09 i0072); the page does not decide between this and concealment as a choice (Study B §7.4).

**What would overturn it.** A page on which a concealment of family money, through a bad outcome, spares its unknowing owner at no cost; a disclosure that brings the feared collapse; a later page showing the creditor as a harmed party, or the father's money or Kurumi's wages paying the broker. None exists within V10.

## 6. The integrating claim: restraint and cost carried in the same place

**Claim I-5.** On these pages the restraint that held and the cost that fell were both carried outside the trading moment — by a structure executed at once, by other people, or by an institution's control. Concealment severs the trader from both carriers: the people who bear her cost do not know of it, so they cannot restrain her, and the restraint she builds alone she can undo alone. Knowledge without control of the money did not restrain; control without knowledge was impossible. The one chosen disclosure of the series (V09) is the first point at which the person who bears the cost both knows and holds control, by agreement. INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH as the series' argument within V01–V10; EVALUATION MODERATE as design; the arrangement of V09 is untested after it is made, because Kurumi places no order in V10.

This claim joins Study C's SC-3 (restraint carried from outside the trading moment, FXK-SYN-C027) to Study B's B1 and B3 (cost carried to the unknowing by concealment; responsibility payable when shared, FXK-SYN-C021, C013) and to Study A's counter-cases (institutions that stop or release only after a ruin, FXK-SYN-C019). Each study states its half; none states that the halves are carried by the same parties, or that the series aligns them only once.

### 6.2 The other chains

- **Mebuki.** Her parents bore the first cost unknowing — tuition and trust (V03 i0159; V04 i0012) — and were then made into the lever of her silence: their address and number held as security on condition that she repay, "ちゃんと返済してくれたら言わないって" (V04 i0118). - **Mochiko's question.** The crisis of V08 was answered by another person's presence and a question about wanting, not by money: Mochiko refuses to pay the debt (V08 i0119) and asks whether Kurumi wants to die (V08 i0131–i0136); the V08 reading records the answer as "affective and relational, not financial" (Study C §4.4). For the crisis, presence carried the restraint; for the money, control did.

### 6.3 What the alignment shows

Under concealment the series shows cost exported to people who cannot restrain, and restraint either absent or self-made and self-undone. Under disclosure it shows cost borne by people who know, consent and hold control. It never shows a third combination — a trader restrained by someone who does not know — and the combination of disclosure, consent and control appears once, in V09. That is why the vocabulary of responsibility changes when it does (section 4.2), why the institution's release becomes reachable when it does (section 4.3), and why the agency the series grants Kurumi is a choice of manner, open or concealed (section 2.3): the manner decides where both restraint and cost will lie.

**Contrary evidence.** (1) The disclosure was pressed: Mochiko made the job conditional on it and used the letter she had read to press for it — "大人って言われたいならちゃんとお父さんに話しなよ / あんな遺書書くよりよっぽど簡単でしょ" (V09 i0043; i0050). "Chosen" is qualified. (2) The V09 control is untested: Kurumi places no order in V10, and her abstention is confounded with having 22,963円 (V10 i0043); her resolution to "control" her desire (FXK-V09-C032) faces events after V10 and stays OPEN. (3) Self-made restraints that held without others exist: Nana's half rule, executed at funding (V10 i0132), and Yasuko's exit on the evidence of her own record (V04 i0046). Each qualifies rather than overturns the claim — Nana's rule is never tested by a loss she wants to chase (she stands at −8,000 open, V10 i0145), and Yasuko's restraint was learned from a ruin she bore alone (V10 i0075; a late revelation). (4) Mebuki's chain was ended by an institution, not by a disclosure; her parents are never shown learning of it (V06-U03, advanced only by inference).

**Rivals.** *A restoration comedy* (FXK-V09-C002, strengthened by FXK-V10-C049): the V09 arrangement is how a plot closes a crisis quickly. It explains the speed, not the alignment, which is built across nine volumes before it is closed. *A situational father*: the V02 slap and the V09 "怒らないから" are one man's answers to different stakes, a living daughter after a dead wife (Study B §7.4); the page does not decide. *The institutional reading*: the discharge and the surviving tax, not the family, carry the restraint. They carry part of it (section 4.3); the wage control is the family's. *The psychological reading* of precommitment (SC-3) is compatible; this claim adds the side of cost.

**What would overturn it.** A page on which a trader is restrained by someone who does not know of her trading; or a page on which the person who knows, consents and holds control nonetheless fails to restrain — which, for Kurumi after V09, could only lie after V10 and is not anticipated here.

## 7. Information as capital

**Claim I-6.** Information is the one capital on these pages that compounds without exposure. The person who holds other parties' figures, the rules and the signal of another trader's capitulation can profit, hedge and survive at modest size; the series makes Mochiko that holder, and makes withholding — of figures by lies, of judgment by silence, of rules by unspoken thought — the way the capital is kept. The trader who carries the largest cost holds the least of it. The page gives the reader the same ledger Mochiko holds, without the power to act. Giving information away costs the giver first. OBSERVATION HIGH for the holdings and withholdings; INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH for the pattern; motive questions are referred to Study B (section 6) and stay OPEN (FXK-V01-C005; FXK-SYN-C024).

### 7.1 What Mochiko holds

The required inputs come from all three studies (convergence audit §8.6).

Mebuki's ruin and debt, as her creditor (V04 i0086–i0119; V06 i0051). - **The rules** (Study A §13 item 4). ### 7.2 How the capital is kept and converted

She converts information at modest size and with losses cut by rule: about 4.0× in V03 (ANALYST CALCULATION on 13,302,630, Study C §2.5), a stop entered (V03 i0152), a small cut reported (V07 i0063), a currency refused (V09 i0117). The canary is kept singing by a lie (V03 i0148). The loan to Mebuki is, by her own account to Yasuko, a hedge of her own long — "ほぼ負けることはなくなる" (V05 i0131) — a self-reported rationale coherent in direction and overstated in size (FXK-SYN-C012). She does not need to forecast: she admits she cannot read the move that is paying her (V04 i0144–i0145). Her company makes the capital ordinary (【代表取締役 社長】, V09 i0143), and her offer of a job to Kurumi names the information it buys: "使い方は ともかく / 身につけた 知識と経験は 本物じゃん", "私が こうやって 手を貸してる 理由でもある" (V09 i0034, re-opened). Kurumi's experience of ruin is itself, on Mochiko's account, a capital worth employing.

**Withholding judgment.** With Kurumi, Mochiko holds the figure and withholds the verdict. She asks one question at the Christmas table — "さすがに 損切り注文くらい してるよね?" (V06 i0151) — and then, inside, "私が気にすることじゃない", "全部くるみが決めたことだろう?" (V06 i0154). In V07 she withholds four interventions (FXK-V07-C009), the unsent 「くるみ大丈夫?」 drawn with anguish (V07 i0099–i0100); in V08 a fifth (i0111–i0112). When Mebuki asks whether she has tried to reach Kurumi, as Mebuki and Yasuko have, she answers "…いや" (V08 i0107, re-opened): the one friend who held the figure did not call, and the two who did not hold it did. Her help, when it comes, is framed as interest — "投資することならできる", "私の施しは打算付きだ" (V08 i0113–i0114). No exploitation of Kurumi's vulnerability and no gain from her loss is shown (FXK-V07-C075; her own position on 15 January is not shown, V07-U15; by her own account she was flat from her December close, time unprinted). Whether the withholding was negligence or respect, and what motive lies under the change toward Kurumi in V07–V09, are Study B's questions and stay OPEN (FXK-V01-C005; FXK-SYN-C024: her conduct and attachment change, her doctrine does not).

### 7.3 Not hers alone: the counter-case

Mochiko is not the only person shown using another trader's behaviour as information. In V02 Kurumi silently uses Mebuki's views as a contrary indicator after advising her under a disclaimer (V02 i0066–i0141; Study B §5.1, graded negligence moving toward exploitation of information, INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH). What is Mochiko's alone is the scale of the practice, its stability from V03 to V08, and its pairing with concealment and credit (convergence audit, K-10).

### 7.4 Who holds least, and the reader who holds all

Kurumi holds the least about others and carries the largest single cost (Study B §4). She is never shown learning of Mebuki's loan or its lender within V10 (V05-U20); she learns of Mochiko's lies only through Yasuko (V05 i0144, INFERENCE HIGH); she is never shown learning of the 2012 "sample" thought; she holds no rule of her own account (V02 i0017; V08 i0106). In V10 the relation inverts in miniature: she holds an experience her colleagues lack and keeps its scale from them — "最高機密! トップシークレットです!" (V10 i0044, re-opened) — warns gently and then assents (V10 i0033, i0121, i0124). The silent witness of FXK-V10-C001 withholds, in a milder register, as she was once withheld from.

The reader holds everything. The narrator's boxes and the drawn screens are exact wherever their operands are printed and give the reader each friend's balance, regime and deposit while the friends hide them from one another (V02 i0158–i0159; FXK-SYN-C017); the black box gives two nine-digit figures before Kurumi reads any (V07 i0118); the V10 captions price each newcomer for the reader alone (V10 i0036–i0047; FXK-SYN-C015), and the boxes follow even a novice's unlevered transfer to the yen (V10 i0132). The reader therefore stands where Mochiko stands — holding the figures — without the power to act or the duty to warn. The page asks the reader for a judgment and, through the narrator's verdicts, points it at the person, not at the institution (section 4.1).

### 7.5 Giving information away

The page charges the giver first. Yasuko's warning to Mebuki, the one true warning of V03, is dismissed with an insult (V03 i0141–i0142). Kurumi's refusal to lend, on protective grounds, is answered with an accusation made in ignorance of how her mother died (V04 i0071–i0072). Mochiko's eventual intervention costs her nights in an exam period (V09 i0051). The friends' one institution for information — the no-lies rule written into the contest against Mochiko's V03 lie (V06 i0141–i0144) — is kept to the letter (V07 i0014).

**Contrary evidence.** (1) Information did not protect Mochiko from forecasting error; her edge is in sizing and in reading people, not in prediction (V04 i0144–i0145). (2) Her information about Kurumi produced no gain on any page (FXK-V07-C075): knowledge of a friend's exposure was not converted. (3) Information about oneself is not capital: the traders' self-knowledge is accurate and inert (section 3.1). (4) Information received too late does not change a course: Mebuki learned the truth and still did not close (V05 i0145 → V06 i0039). (5) Yasuko's experience of 2013 is used protectively, as warning — "FXはやめとけ" to Nana (V10 i0090–i0092) — not as capital.

**Rivals.** *Temperament*: Mochiko is disciplined and cold, and the information is incidental. Against it: the page gives her method a dated origin in a turn from study to reading people (V05 i0036–i0037), and the canary is information-seeking in its own words. *Narrative function*: the plot needs one holder of everyone's figures, first as tempter and concealed counterparty, then as rescuer and employer (Study B §6.5). Both readings are compatible with the claim, which describes what the page makes information do, not why Mochiko is as she is.

**What would overturn it.** A page on which Mochiko loses through her use of others' information, or gains from Kurumi's loss (which would also turn the change of FXK-SYN-C024 into function plus concealment); a page on which the trader holding the least information fares best because of it. None exists within V10.

### 8.2 The arc of FXK-V01-C011, with its mechanism

The cross-volume claim starts from the reconciliation audit's consolidated wording (§5.5 row 1): true of V01–V04; mixed in V05–V06 (a bracketed exit rewarded, V05 i0152; escalation punished, V05 i0091; cuts judged by two clocks, V06 i0015, i0044–i0046, i0098); the no-stop, high-leverage process punished catastrophically in V07–V08 (V07 i0118; V08 i0009–i0020); displaced onto Yasuko's 2013 in V10 (i0070–i0076). What survives is an arc of early reward setting up later ruin, not a moral that the worst process pays. EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH as an arc.

The convergence audit (its §3 item 4 and §8.6) reconciled the arc's mechanism from three sides, and the synthesis adopts it:

1. **The reference point was fixed by a loss that was not hers.** Her mother's 2000万 became Kurumi's target at fifteen (V01 i0015–i0020; FXK-V01-E002), and her own sizing was set against it — "母が失った2000万円を… 奪い返す" (V06 i0095) — through every later revision of the target (Study C, model 4; FXK-SYN-C010).
2. **The early rewards trained the rule of sizing to that reference, at leverage beyond the narrator's stated domestic cap.** The V01 position that made both the nadir and the win exceeded "証拠金25倍まで" as the narrator states it, at about 59× and 74× (V01 i0074–i0075; Study A R1; INFERENCE HIGH on the narrator's statement, with the V04 tension V04-U02 noted). After success, the size stepped up: "次はもっと欲張ろう" (V03 i0053); "冷静になる必要なんて なかったじゃん" (V05 i0070) (Study C, model 7).
3. **The V07 ruin's reach needed a gap and an account whose excess fell on her** (FXK-SYN-C018); the V08 ruin, in the reward's own overseas regime, ended at 0円 (V08 i0020).
4. **The early reward postponed who pays** (Study B §5.4): in V01–V02 risk landed on no one because outcomes were favourable; the later volumes showed who had been carrying it (section 5.2).

The synthesis adds one thing to the arc: it is the series' structure of *training*. Chance on these pages supplies rewards in an order that teaches a sizing rule; exposure, which the person sets, turns the lesson into a ruin. That is the sense in which "the outcome rewards the worst process" was true of V01 and is false of the series. Counterexamples keep the arc from being a law: the V03 withdrawal (V03 i0124), the V04 declined add (V04 i0115) and the V05 memory of the slap (V05 i0154) are restraints inside the arc, and the V05 bracket shows a protective process rewarded (V05 i0152). What would overturn the arc: a page on which an early reward is followed by a sizing rule set by capital at risk rather than by distance to a reference. None exists within V10. The added mechanism extends the claim's scope; a row is proposed in section 14.

### 8.3 V01's competing thesis: standing at V10 (FXK-SYN-C006)

FXK-SYN-C006 registered V01's competing thesis — "V01 is a black comedy of chance in which competence is not what the market rewards" (V01 §D, HYPOTHESIS MODERATE) — and left its standing to this synthesis.

**For it, across the boundary.** In V01 the textbook act is punished and the forbidden hold rewarded: あふぃちゃん cuts as the narrator prescribes and realizes a 大損, then is caught by the reversal (V01 i0145–i0152); Mochiko, the experienced trader, misses her own entry and judges Kurumi "dead" twice, wrongly (V01 i0092, i0154, i0157); the narrator is ironic about its own moralism (V01 i0143, i0086). Later volumes keep the separation of call from outcome (section 3.3): right calls ruined anyway (V08 i0009, i0012, i0020), the most disciplined trader unable to read the move that pays her (V04 i0144–i0145), cuts judged by two clocks (V06 i0015, i0098). The exogenous strand survives in a character's voice — "事故" (V08 i0153) — and the comic register persists: the bankruptcy is drawn as comedy and the gazette given as a present (V09 i0103–i0105), and the comedy theses of V09 and V10 are live (FXK-V09-C002; FXK-V10-C002).

**Against it.** Every account-ending loss follows an exposure its holder set and, but for Yasuko in 2013, named in advance (FXK-SYN-C025, C026, C018). Those who bounded their exposure are not ruined within V10: Mochiko (rule-cut losses, modest size), Yasuko after 2013 (V04 i0046; cash shares in 2017, V10 i0090–i0092), Nana (her half rule; −8,000 open at the boundary, V10 i0145). A protective process — the bracket — produced the run's largest realized gain (V05 i0152). After V07 the narrator's verdicts and the outcomes agree, and the V10 sentence on desire is unironic (Study A §9).

**Standing.** The thesis divides along the line section 3 draws. *Competence at reading the market is not what decides outcomes* survives across V01–V10 (EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH). *Competence is not what the market rewards* fails as the series' thesis if competence includes bounding exposure: the series' counter-claim is that survival is decided by exposure, which persons set, while chance decides the sign and timing of single outcomes and the order in which rewards arrive. Chance is the series' trainer, not its subject (section 8.2). "Black comedy" names a register that persists into V09–V10 and is carried by the live comedy theses; as a causal claim about V01–V10 it is restricted. EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH. The V01 reading's own reason for preferring its governing thesis — the trades are tied to the mother and the father, and the win is framed by unresolved debt (V01 §D) — is borne out by the later volumes. A transition (REVISE, restricting the claim's scope) is proposed in section 14 (P-1) and is written, as proposed, as ledger row FXK-SYN-C029 (EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH), which records this standing in place of the OPEN registration of FXK-SYN-C006 (semantic validation audit, finding V-02 and section 11). What would overturn the standing: a page within V10 on which a trader who had bounded her exposure is ruined by chance, or a ruin without an exposure its holder set. None exists; the open positions of V10 face events after the boundary and are not anticipated (section 11).

## 9. Manga form: how the page distributes knowledge and judgment

The role-gap audit gave manga form no separate study and assigned its load-bearing uses to Study A, with figural staging to be treated where it changes an argument (role-gap §3.6); the convergence audit found the one formal gap, the staging of the franc order, and placed it under A5 and SC-3 (D-10). This section states what form does for the integrated argument. Its claim: **the page's form gives the reader the exact figure and the narrator's verdict on the person, withdraws the figure from the character at the nadir, puts the decisive rule and the decisive motive in unspoken thought, and keeps a comic register over exact accounting — so that the reader is placed as a judge of persons who holds the ledger, and the institution's term is the one thing the reader must reconstruct.** EVALUATION MODERATE-HIGH.

1. **The reader's ledger.** The narrator's boxes and the drawn screens are exact wherever their operands are printed (FXK-SYN-C017; reconciliation audit §4.12): each Kurumi box follows from the previous one, and the cross-character checks close. The cold open prints the nadir before the story begins (V01 i0003, the −3,251,490 of i0137). The census boxes give every friend's balance and account regime while the friends hide them from one another (V02 i0158–i0159). The black box prints two nine-digit figures beside Mochiko's private estimate, "マイナス 1億2千万円!!!", before Kurumi reads any (V07 i0118; FXK-V07-E040); the white box states 0円 over a sinking figure who registers only "れ…?" (V08 i0020). In V10 the 「保有金融資産」 captions price each newcomer for the reader alone (V10 i0036–i0047), and the boxes follow a novice's transfer from bank to securities account to the yen (V10 i0132, re-opened). The residues are small and kept as printed (the +10円 on every 300-lot value, FXK-V01-C013; the 11,000 gap at Mebuki's liquidation, V06 i0039).
2. **Withdrawal at the nadir.** The figure breaks for the character exactly when she most needs it: glitched digits at V01 i0141 (UNREADABLE), V02 i0105–i0106, V07 i0125 and V08 i0130 (UNREADABLE); a hoped-for "-101,534円" with the true figure struck through (V07 i0129). The form gives the number to the reader and takes it from the person.
3. **The institution's grammar.** The position panel that announces Mebuki's ruin, 「保有ポジションはありません」 (V04 i0012), announces Kurumi's best day in an equivalent panel, 「現在お持ちの建玉はありません」 (V05 i0152): the institution's sentence records that a position no longer exists and is indifferent to whether it was a ruin or a peak (Study A, claim A1).
4. **Thought as the place of the decisive rule and motive.** The page puts its most consequential knowledge in unspoken thought: the domestic specification (V08 i0106), the canary (V03 i0150), the silence at Christmas (V06 i0154), the 2012 "sample" (V05 i0048), the inner account of how the friendship began, "利用できると…思ったから 付き合っただけだ" (V07 i0045; INFERENCE MODERATE-HIGH that it is unspoken). Mochiko's two voices — soft agreement in public, coarse contempt in private (V01 i0159; V03 i0126; the formula's origin at a shop counter, V05 i0031) — cross in V07–V08: the public voice turns candid to Mebuki (V07 i0017), and the V01 inner epithet is spoken aloud to Kurumi as an appeal against self-erasure, "くるみは強欲の雌豚でしょうが!" (V08 i0126). The crossing shows that her change is drawn, not asserted; register is lettering, and it is not evidence of motive (Study B §6.4).
5. **Figural staging.** The dealers as hunters with a reaper and the narrator's answering guillotines (V01 i0100–i0101); the canary drawn as a walk into a mine (V03 i0150, re-opened); a wordless page with a skeletal Death figure behind Kurumi at the franc order (V06 i0116–i0117) and Mochiko's vision of the chart as a monster (i0151–i0153); the market as scales (V07 i0080); the winged figure of appetite — "脳汁" (V02 i0109), and in V09, by inference the same figure, "でも楽しかったでしょ?" (V09 i0006) — which V09 adds to a re-presented childhood memory that V05 drew without it (V09 i0031; V05 i0161). The V06 reading's rival, for the figures of the franc order, is kept: they are genre staging, not authorial verdicts. On either reading the figures figure danger and desire and never assign fault to an institution (Study A, A5 contrary evidence; Study C, SC-3).
6. **Repetition as design.** The identical 1,005,144 ties the daughter's bank balance to the mother's post-liquidation balance (V06 i0082; V07 i0144; INFERENCE HIGH that it is design; whether Kurumi notices is not stated, V07-U12). A 「すみませんでした」 sheet in V08 redraws the composition of the note of V01 i0030 (V08 i0042; recall of the mother's note favoured at INFERENCE MODERATE, status UNRESOLVED; no claim is made that Kurumi wrote the words). "やっぱ損切りは なしッ!!" (V01 i0088) returns as "やっぱり 損切りはナシ…!" (V06 i0009), and Kozue's "損切りしなければ勝っていた" (V06 i0087, journal) is echoed by Kurumi's "やっぱ 損切り いらなかったじゃん" (V06 i0098); no page shows Kurumi reading that entry or taking a rule from it (V06 checkpoint C-02). Yasuko's 2013 re-stages the founding sequence on another person (FXK-V10-C065). Repetition makes the re-enactment legible to the reader without making it a cause in the person (reconciliation audit §5.5 row 5).
7. **Time and disclosure.** Biography arrives after judgment: Mochiko's childhood and 2010 method (V05 i0003–i0049), Kozue's 2008 (V06 第28話), the purpose of the father's 300万 (V09 i0074) and Yasuko's 2013 (V10 i0066–i0079) each re-read pages the reader has already judged, complicating the judgment without changing the conduct. The narrator knows more than the character when it matters: it says discharge is likely while Kurumi judges bankruptcy closed to her (V08 i0033–i0034).
8. **Mixed authority.** V06's 2008 chapter is Kozue's journal, a narrator's captions and scenes no journal could hold, framed as Kurumi's reading (V06 i0090); its two sentences at i0080 — 「すべてのポジションが強制ロスカットされた。私は1900万円もの大金を失ってしまった。」 — are a first-person caption in white type on black bands (convergence audit, K-08). The mother's ruin reaches the reader as the daughter's reading of it.
9. **A comic register over exact accounting.** Liquidation as a gag monster (V03 i0077–i0078), the legal routes as a gag explainer (V09 i0017–i0019), the bankruptcy drawn as comedy and "破産おめでと" (V09 i0103–i0105): the comedy never softens a figure. The distance it gives is the distance from which judgment is possible.

The synthesis found no further formal question on which its claims depend.
