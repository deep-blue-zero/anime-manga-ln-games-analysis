# NANA — Multi-Document Synthesis Architecture v1
## Architecture for the definitive extant-text synthesis after completion of the sequential Japanese deep reading

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the recommended architecture for the eventual multi-document synthesis of Ai Yazawa's 『NANA』 after the sequential Japanese-original deep reading is complete.

It is designed to work with:

- `NANA_ANALYTICAL_METHOD_V2.md`;
- the canonical per-volume files `NANA_V01_DEEP_READING.md` through `NANA_V21_DEEP_READING.md`;
- any subsequently analyzed continuation chapters through Chapter 84;
- the per-volume Evidence Classification Ledgers;
- the Primary-Source Locator Ledgers;
- the cumulative character, relationship, material-life, voice, narration, motif, and correction streams generated by the volume analyses;
- and later official paratext such as *NANA 7.8*, analyzed only after the relevant main narrative has stabilized.

The architecture is intended to solve four problems simultaneously:

1. produce a readable full-series literary synthesis rather than a concatenation of volume summaries;
2. preserve the evidentiary traceability established by the v2 analytical method;
3. prevent duplication across character, relationship, ethical, material, industry, and formal analyses;
4. preserve the unfinished status of the manga by distinguishing trajectory from destination.

The governing architectural principle is:

> **Every major analytical question should have one primary documentary home.**

Other documents may invoke the same scene or conclusion, but they should cross-reference rather than independently re-explain the entire argument.

The governing synthesis principle is:

> **The volume deep readings preserve local complexity; the synthesis should compress, connect, compare, and test them rather than recreate them.**

# 2. Recommended final scope and title

If the synthesis begins after the complete numbered tankōbon run through Volume 21 but before Chapters 81–84 have been supplied and analyzed, the package should use a title such as:

```text
NANA: Definitive Synthesis Through Volume 21
```

Recommended directory/archive prefix:

```text
NANA_Definitive_Synthesis_Through_Volume_21
```

If Chapters 81–84 are supplied in Japanese and analyzed before synthesis, the preferred title becomes:

```text
NANA: Full-Series Synthesis Through Chapter 84
```

Recommended directory/archive prefix:

```text
NANA_Full_Series_Synthesis_Through_Chapter_84
```

Do **not** call the work a "complete-series analysis" unless the primary narrative is actually completed.

The correct distinction remains:

> **trajectory ≠ destination**

The hiatus limits what can responsibly be said about final outcomes, but it does not prevent strong conclusions about the architecture, relationships, ethics, motifs, and historical trajectories of the extant text.

# 3. Architectural model

The recommended package contains:

- **15 reader-facing analytical documents**, numbered `00`–`14`;
- **2 archival/reference documents**, numbered `15`–`16`;
- the governing analytical method;
- this architecture document;
- a corpus manifest;
- a checksum manifest;
- and a final duplication/provenance audit.

The reader-facing corpus should be approximately:

> **105,000–130,000 words**

The evidence/reference layer should add approximately:

> **20,000–40,000 words or structured-entry equivalents**

The complete package will therefore likely occupy approximately:

> **130,000–165,000 words / structured-entry equivalents**

This is intentionally much shorter than simply recombining the per-volume analyses. The volume documents already preserve exhaustive local reading. The synthesis should provide the higher-order structure.

# 7. Descriptive relationship analysis versus ethical analysis

Documents 05 and 07 must remain distinct.

## Document 05 asks

- Who is this person?
- What do they want?
- How do they understand love?
- What does this relationship provide?
- How does the relationship change?
- What does the character believe they are doing?
- How does the character's voice reveal their model of intimacy?

## Document 07 asks

- Was the action consensual?
- Was power used coercively?
- Did care become control?
- Did protection erase autonomy?
- What responsibility survives sincere affection?
- What harms occurred?
- What does apology repair?
- What remains ethically unresolved?

This distinction prevents moral judgment from flattening psychology.

It also prevents psychological understanding from becoming moral exoneration.

# 8. Why the temporal document requires independent status

`09_NARRATION_MEMORY_TIME_ABSENCE_AND_THE_UNFINISHED_FUTURE.md` should not be subordinated to the character documents.

By the middle volumes, the future narration already does more than deliver retrospective commentary.

It changes the tense of the entire story.

The manga repeatedly makes:

- happiness feel remembered while it is still occurring;
- domestic spaces feel like future archives;
- ordinary objects feel like relics;
- present speech feel like possible "last words";
- absence feel structurally real before its cause is known;
- waiting become a form of home;
- memory become a second narrative timeline.

This deserves series-level formal treatment.

The central methodological rule remains:

> **Future narration establishes only what it actually establishes.**

If it proves that someone is absent, it does not automatically prove why.

If it calls something a final statement, it does not automatically establish death.

If Hachi speaks retrospectively, her interpretation remains retrospective character testimony rather than omniscient narration.

# 11. Duplication-control rules

Long synthesis corpora fail when every document retells the same scenes.

Use these rules.

## Rule 1 — One primary home

Every major analytical question gets one primary document.

Examples:

- Nana psychology → Document 02
- Hachi psychology → Document 03
- Nana/Hachi → Document 04
- secondary cast → Document 05
- material adulthood → Document 06
- consent/coercion → Document 07
- industry/media → Document 08
- future narration → Document 09
- Japanese voice → Document 10
- manga form/fashion → Document 11

## Rule 2 — Cross-reference rather than reproduce

A document may summarize another document's conclusion in one or two sentences, then refer the reader to the primary home.

## Rule 3 — Reuse evidence; do not rewrite argument

The same evidence ID may support several arguments.

That does not require repeating the same full scene analysis.

## Rule 4 — Allow deliberate overlap only when the analytical question changes

Example:

A Takumi/Hachi scene can appear in:

- Document 03 as evidence about Hachi's psychology;
- Document 05 as evidence about Takumi's relationship model;
- Document 06 as evidence about material dependency;
- Document 07 as evidence about coercion.

Each use must answer a different question.

# 13. Evidence confidence and synthesis prose

The final corpus should preserve the v2 distinction among:

- **TF — Textual fact**
- **SI — Strong inference**
- **TI — Thematic interpretation**
- **CB — Character belief**
- **UA — Unresolved ambiguity**
- **FP — Prospective/foreshadowing signal**
- **RC — Retrospective correction**
- **VJ — Value judgment**

And the A–E confidence scale:

- **A — directly established**
- **B — strongly established**
- **C — strongly implied**
- **D — plausible inference**
- **E — speculation/theory**

Reader-facing prose does not need to label every sentence mechanically.

But the archival layer must retain enough classification that a contested synthesis claim can be traced back to its evidentiary status.

# 14. Counterreading standard

Every major document should contain a serious adversarial section.

The purpose is not performative balance.

It is to prevent the mature synthesis from becoming an unfalsifiable theory.

Examples:

## Nana/Hachi

Preferred reading might emphasize romance-shaped narrative centrality.

Counterreadings should test:

- intense female friendship;
- chosen family;
- displaced attachment;
- queer romantic coding;
- deliberately uncategorizable intimacy.

## Hachi/Takumi

Preferred reading may emphasize both genuine attachment and coercive power.

Counterreadings should test:

- captivity-only reading;
- conventional marriage reading;
- material-security reading;
- attachment/fear-of-abandonment reading;
- evolving mutual commitment.

## Nana independence

Preferred reading may see independence as both authorship and armor.

Counterreadings should test:

- simple self-reliance;
- trauma-driven avoidance;
- artistic vocation;
- dependency concealed by pride.

## Future narration

Preferred reading may emphasize elegiac temporal contamination.

Counterreadings should test:

- melodramatic foreshadowing;
- narrator self-fashioning;
- editorial suspense;
- limited retrospective knowledge.

# 16. Why this length is justified

The synthesis should be substantially shorter than the aggregate volume analyses.

However, 『NANA』 genuinely supports a large final corpus because its complexity is longitudinal rather than encyclopedic.

The principal drivers of length are:

- two major protagonists with independent full-series arcs;
- a central relationship that must be analyzed separately from both;
- multiple substantial romantic/sexual relationships;
- pregnancy, marriage, parenthood, home, money, work, and class;
- two major bands and professional music-industry structures;
- celebrity, fandom, paparazzi, privacy, and media narration;
- fashion and visual self-authorship;
- highly meaningful Japanese voice/address distinctions;
- a second temporal plane created by retrospective narration;
- an unfinished future that requires unusually careful epistemic boundaries;
- recurring objects and spaces whose meaning changes over many volumes.

The comparison with other large synthesis projects should be understood structurally:

- *Legend of the Galactic Heroes* requires length because of politics, institutions, military operations, and ensemble scale.
- *Revue Starlight* requires length because performance, music, visual dramaturgy, and intertext operate simultaneously.
- 『NANA』 requires length because **small relational, linguistic, domestic, and temporal changes accumulate across a very long psychological narrative**.

The synthesis should therefore be long enough to preserve this accumulation but short enough to avoid recreating the per-volume close readings.

# 19. Final architectural principle

The final synthesis should not merely answer:

> **What happens in 『NANA』?**

It should make possible a more durable set of questions:

> **Who do Nana and Hachi become?**

> **What kinds of love can preserve another person's personhood?**

> **When does care become control?**

> **What does home mean once cohabitation ends?**

> **How do money, pregnancy, work, fame, and gender alter supposedly private choices?**

> **How does music move from personal expression into institutional commodity?**

> **How does memory change the meaning of the present?**

> **What does the manga establish about its future, and what does the hiatus prevent us from knowing?**

The architecture succeeds if it preserves two things at once:

1. **the contradictory humanity of the characters**, and
2. **the evidentiary path back to the Japanese pages that made those contradictions legible.**

That is the intended end-state of the `NANA_` analytical corpus.
