# Mapped-omnibus analytical method

## One witness, multiple prospective units

This method governs the Japanese manga witness 彼方のアストラ 全巻合本版. Metadata identifies 篠原健太 and 集英社 and calls the source a complete omnibus. Internal units and all narrative content remain unexamined. The method must preserve natural narrative boundaries without pretending the container's size determines an adequate reading.

[The source/unit map](ALS_SOURCE_AND_UNIT_MAP.md) must identify actual internal volume or chapter boundaries before the first run is defined. Prefer explicit collected-volume units where the witness supports them; otherwise use verified chapters with a documented scope decision. Never guess the published volume count, derive chapters arithmetically or divide at a file-processing limit.

The [architecture](ALS_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) routes cumulative evidence and synthesis. This adapts the [generic manga method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MANGA_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md). The lock is CLOSED. A future reader needs owner-defined mapped units, source binding, adequate STILL_IMAGE/Japanese access and a consistent OPEN gate. The entire file being accessible does not authorize later internal units.

## Witness mapping and exposure

Bind immutable source identity and global one-based image occurrences in actual spine order, including apparatus and repeated items. Map internal page-number resets and chapter labels rather than using them as globally unique coordinates. Preserve spreads and verify facing-page layout; no synthetic split EPUB becomes an original witness.

Classify covers, internal title/contents pages, profiles, notes, omake and previews. They may reveal later information. Quarantine beyond-horizon apparatus before a prospective pass. Historical ZIP/container/resource checks do not establish visual legibility, every page's presence or actual reading.

Disclose prior plot/adaptation familiarity and exclude it. Freeze entering state before each authorized internal unit; keep scene disclosure frontiers within it. A later section of the same file is later evidence, not “already available” knowledge. A full-omnibus retrospective synthesis is a separate authorized stage after sequential coverage and reconciliation.

## Causality and knowledge

For each movement explain entering conditions, source-established named actors, perceived goals/options, decision or constraint, consequence and inferential warrant. Distinguish actual causation from coincidence, misunderstanding, claimed explanation or temporal proximity. The work's title is not permission to invent situations or a specific plot mechanism.

Record presentation order, event chronology, each person's knowledge and reader disclosure separately. A new disclosure can change interpretation without depicting development; use REVEALED_NOT_NEW where supported. Do not rewrite an early claim or entering freeze with end-of-omnibus knowledge. If distinct narrative frames or identity uncertainty arise, document the source signals and competing explanations rather than force harmony.

Use names once known within the local horizon. Preserve deliberate anonymity/aliases and avoid habitual role substitutions. Keep who acts, speaks, thinks, observes, receives information and suffers consequences clear. Character states distinguish disposition candidates, developmental states and transient conditions. Another person's report is not direct access to the described subject's motive.

Relationship evidence is directed: A toward B does not establish B toward A. Track knowledge, trust, pressure, cooperation/conflict and reciprocity only when evidenced. If collective action or institutions become material, distinguish individual decisions, group rules and a speaker's claims about authority; those are conditional lenses, not findings about unread content.

## Ordinary life, Japanese expression and page form

Deliberately preserve ordinary interactions, humor, routine and low-stakes behavior where shown. A crisis response is not a complete personality; absent opportunities remain gaps. Compare behavior across contexts and interlocutors before proposing a general rule.

Separate speech, private thought, narration, captions, asides, effects and diegetic text. Attribute with confidence and inspect Japanese address, self-reference, endings, politeness, omissions and ruby where consequential. Use brief source-verified forms with labeled glosses. Translation does not establish Japanese voice, and private thought cannot automatically become public speech.

Read silent pages and graphic transitions as fully as dialogue. For composition, panel size, gutters, framing, gaze, grouping, repetition, withheld reactions, tone/colour or symbolic rendering where present, describe the feature, proposed effect and alternative. Verify page turns/spreads in the omnibus layout. Visual endpoints do not establish unobserved movement, graphic sound effects do not supply acoustics and one expression does not uniquely disclose emotion.

Use OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, EVALUATION or UNRESOLVED with HIGH/MODERATE/LOW/UNRESOLVED confidence. Sensitive acts if encountered require minimal necessary description and distinctions between depiction, explanation, consent, framing, consequence and judgment. Neither sympathy nor later outcome establishes earlier consent or endorsement.

## Locators, review and claims

Locator grammar: `ALS-JP-OMNI-W01 / <verified unit key> / Ch.<actual label or UNKNOWN> / i0001 / p<visible label or NONE> / panel 1 / balloon 1`. The global image occurrence never resets at an internal volume. Printed-page reset is an additional coordinate. Panel/balloon ordering and ambiguity are documented; crops retain parent and region.

L1 exact image/panel/balloon anchors disputed evidence and tests; L2 is bounded scene; L3 unit navigation; L4 pattern has representative L1 instances and checked scope. Pending mappings cannot support mature claims. OCR/search is a retrieval aid; reopen images for quotations or contested/formal claims.

Evidence-plane page states are REVIEWED_MATERIAL, NO_MATERIAL_ADDITION, DUPLICATE, EXCLUDED or UNRESOLVED_READABILITY. Git records actual reviewed boundary/exclusions and safe receipt aliases, not raw images, OCR dumps, full dialogue, private IDs or paths.

Every governing interpretation retains its support, inferential step, strongest counterreading and falsifier. New evidence uses PRESERVE/STRENGTHEN/REVISE/DOWNGRADE/REJECT/OPEN, keeping original wording and freezes. A clean map or high page count cannot substitute for argument.

## Complete future unit and acceptance

A future `readings/ALS_<VERIFIED_UNIT>_DEEP_READING.md` has `artifact_type: deep_reading` only within an authorized run. Required sections: bound witness/unit and actual inspection; exposure/entering state; thesis/rival; full movement-level causal prose; named state and directed relationships; ordinary/Japanese evidence; visual form/recurrence; counterreadings and claim/forecast changes; exact ledger-update manifest; exit state, debt and completion decision.

Synchronize source/unit coverage, state/evidence and claim ledgers with every accepted unit. “No material change” is justified in the manifest, not invented rows. A unit with prose but missing cumulative state—or tables without prose—is incomplete. No universal word ceiling constrains warranted reasoning.

Perform handoff §15.6 review on opening, substantive middle, exit synthesis and material relationships: accurate names/attribution, purposeful roles, preserved concealment and clear causal agents. Record findings and repair supported defects. Essential unread pages, unclear mapping or materially unsupported claims hold the affected completion state. Repository success is not semantic acceptance.

### Current-unit story synopsis

*Prospective amendment, 2026-11-13, under initiation policy v1.11 §5.3.*

Each completed plot-bearing unit reading includes a clearly labeled **Current-unit story synopsis** near its front, after brief witness/unit coverage and entering context, before thesis and extended analysis. Its boundary is the verified internal collected-volume unit or, under the documented scope decision, verified chapter unit authorized for that reading. The accessible omnibus is a container, not permission to summarize through its ending or to invent internal volume counts.

Write connected, accessible prose explaining the unit's opening situation, named participants and stakes, consequential actions and decisions, causal turns, disclosures and ending state. Preserve concrete physical and scene mechanics established by the inspected pages. Identify inherited developments briefly and distinguish them from what happens here. Keep shown/narrated events separate from testimony, belief, analyst conjecture and unresolved outcomes; preserve any source-established identity or frame uncertainty. Material event claims have retrievable source locators or stable observation references, with global image coordinates and the verified unit mapping where needed. The account remains prose, scaled to actual complexity without a fixed word quota.

Compose or finalize this unit-end, spoiler-bearing account only after all entering and within-unit prospective captures are frozen. Shield it and the completed reading from earlier-horizon analysts. It may disclose every outcome established in the current authorized internal unit, but later internal units, their apparatus, adaptations and outside familiarity remain excluded. A full-omnibus retrospective retains its separate authorization and readiness gates. The synopsis supplements all existing movement-level causal, visual, linguistic, character, relationship and counterreading work and synchronized state/claim updates. Acceptance requires both; no frozen record is rewritten and the CLOSED analysis lock remains governing.
