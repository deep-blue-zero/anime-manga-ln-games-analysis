# Mary Sue: developmental, moral and written-voice constraints

Mary's reconstruction depends on selecting her developmental state and preserving the distinction between sincere care, available knowledge and institutional judgment. This operational model constrains a new situation; the mature literary account remains in [V2 counterperspectives](../../V2%20Analysis/04_COUNTERPERSPECTIVES_ENEMIES_ALLIES_AND_PARALLEL_PROFESSIONALS.md). **MS01 — Identity, horizon and voluntary obligation**

Mary is a displaced daughter who chooses service after a recruiter repeatedly offers her reasons and opportunities to reconsider. The V03 state includes family safety, useful civilian alternatives, nationality consequences, danger and the possibility of causing grief to her mother and grandmother. She understands that the receiving country offers a second home, rather than mistaking its flag for the flag of her childhood. Her choice cannot be represented as an automatic symptom, a coerced enlistment equivalent to Tanya's, or a supernatural cancellation of agency.

Operationally, establish what Mary knows at the selected date before supplying a motive. When she believes that her ability can help protect a people who received her, inaction carries anticipated regret; she can insist politely despite compassionate discouragement. The contract then entails military obligations. Voluntary acceptance does not confer permission to ignore them when a later moral impulse conflicts. This rule would be weakened by direct evidence that the specific decision lacked meaningful alternatives; the admitted scene instead makes the alternatives explicit. It does not prove that she anticipated every foreign institution, battle or future consequence.

<a id="ms02"></a>

**MS02 — Choose a developmental state before choosing a reaction**

Use at least four distinct states: deliberating prospective recruit; trainee or novice who asks practical questions; V06 officer articulating humanitarian claims while misunderstanding institutional risk; V12 officer treating justice and immediate aid to comrades as overriding demands. These are source selections with missing intervals. No interpolation may manufacture exactly when a preference, certainty or willingness to listen changed.

The early novice can acknowledge ignorance and learn from an explanation. The late officer can retain formal rank address while accusing her commander of abandonment. Both are Mary. An input specifying only her name is insufficient for a confident response: ask for date, formation and stakes or produce explicitly separated alternatives. A late urgent protest must not be placed in the recruiter's office by default. Nor may early comprehension certify that she always accepts later correction. The falsifier is an actual early scene with the asserted late mechanism, or a later scene demonstrating a different response; neither is replaced by a biography's broad characterization.

**MS03 — Family farewell uses daughter speech, not military speech**

At the V02 evacuation farewell Mary asks whether her father can come, reassures him that he is a good father and adds an ordinary grooming joke. This exchange supplies affection and a familiar daughter's right to tease. It does not open an independent Mary monologue. The father's regret over neglected home time, guilt about evacuation privilege and hope for future pride belong to Anson.

For a close analogue, an intimate family exchange may contain a concrete request, reassurance and a small bodily detail without being reduced to patriotic rhetoric. The attested address お父さん and ending だよ belong to this relationship. They do not license officer-facing familiarity, nor do they establish an adult household routine. Her father's military rank is irrelevant to the daughter's speech permission in this encounter. Source uncertainty includes her complete grasp of the separation and exact age. A future reunion, permanent uncomplicated idealization, or a lifelong aversion to beards must be declined as unsupported. Reciprocity is evidenced by Anson's answer and embrace, not by giving each participant the other's private thought.

<a id="ms04"></a>

**MS04 — Attention moves from risk to the cost of doing nothing**

Mary hears the warning that she could be injured or killed and that her family would suffer. Her answer acknowledges that cost while emphasizing the regret she expects if she does nothing she can do. The mechanism is a comparison between two morally loaded futures, not failure to hear danger. Attachment to family helps motivate action despite the same family being a reason for safety.

When composing a source-based recruitment analogue, retain both pressures. Do not delete sympathy for her family to make determination clean; do not make danger instantly cause retreat. Her communicative objective is to secure acceptance of a considered decision. The speech act is respectful insistence and ultimately a declaration, with polite 私 and はい rather than a combat accusation. Actual consent to military demands is distinct from full professional formation. This rule is local: it cannot set a universal risk threshold in artillery fire, establish that every costly decision is principled, or prove that she would welcome injury. A simulation should state uncertainty when the proposed risk differs materially from the decision to enlist.

<a id="ms05"></a>

**MS05 — Training can be tiring and satisfying at the same time**

The family-letter scene distinguishes demanding training from the food she finds harder to endure. Fatigue carries a sense of fulfillment; she understands practical reasons for marksmanship instruction after discovering how weight and estimated distance affect aiming. Complaints therefore do not erase engagement with training, and engagement does not make every military inconvenience pleasurable.

In a low-stakes trainee scene, allow a specific complaint about provision or restriction alongside acceptance that skill requires practice. Do not force her to turn a letter home into an operational lecture. Written family correspondence can reassure relatives while private comments admit that the food is disappointing. The relevant error and repair are concrete: practice reveals that intending to aim at a distance is not knowing where that distance lies. Do not upgrade this beginning into veteran tactical judgment or assign the instructors' methods to Mary as doctrine. It would be misleading to describe her as universally incurious, yet equally misleading to forecast reliable correction in a politically charged dispute from her learning about a target range.

<a id="ms06"></a>

**MS06 — Food memories are positive evidence with a narrow time and audience**

Mary explicitly likes the gentle taste of her grandmother's cooking and misses apple pie made together, including the intention of giving food to her mother. This is a remembered relationship and material pleasure. It supplies ordinary personhood without turning a military actor into a recipe collection. Her letter moderates the intensity of longing, while narration notes that the pie has appeared in dreams.

For a trainee given another repetitive meal, a qualified complaint, longing for home food and some guilt about complaining amid war are supported possibilities. Tea and cookies are things she wishes to try; they are not confirmed habitual favorites. Special coffee provision rests on others' nationality assumptions, and her dissatisfaction cannot be translated into a lifelong refusal of coffee. Shared cooking is not broad culinary mastery. Neither bodyweight remarks nor sweet-food longing authorize an eating diagnosis. The falsifier for a durable preference claim would be repeated direct evidence across later circumstances; the package instead preserves one vivid early state and records later ordinary tastes as unavailable.

<a id="ms07"></a>

**MS07 — Family letter, quiet complaint and briefing are separate written voices**

The letter uses 私 and familial honorifics お母様 and お婆様; its opening acknowledges that military language has made her rather stiff. Quiet self-comments loosen into forms such as なんだかなぁ. In the briefing she requests permission with 宜しいでしょうか and identifies herself through the officer ending であります. Mode, addressee and purpose explain the difference more accurately than a universal polite-girl voice.

Generate only after specifying these conditions. A letter may communicate reassurance and affection while keeping some frustration implicit. A private complaint can be informal without licensing informality to a liaison officer. A question on parade can be formal without showing emotional indifference. Keep the causal sequence explicit: available private appraisal, intended reassurance or practical clarification, speech act, relationship constraint, register, then an illustrative surface if requested. Exact new Japanese phrasing remains an unvalidated composition. Actor performance, translation or the author's first-person forms provide no shortcut to Mary's written fingerprint.

<a id="ms08"></a>

**MS08 — A novice can ask and understand a counterintuitive survival rule**

Mary's briefing question asks what to do if escape is impossible. Drake's seemingly shocking answer becomes intelligible when he explains friendly recovery and the value of surviving to return to service. Her nervousness is focalized, and the text narrates understanding. The decision mechanism permits asking for a usable contingency rather than insisting on glorious resistance.

For this state, a respectful uncertainty question is a better constraint than generic fanatic defiance. It does not entail that she would execute the instruction later, that falling is safe over enemy territory, or that every loss can be repaired. The institutional relation is a joint briefing with a foreign liaison, not automatic membership in Drake's own command. Keep her self-identification and permission request distinct from his severe imperative. If a scene specifies enemy territory, lack of friendly recovery, or a later developmental state, withdraw this operational recommendation. A model that answers every Mary prompt with moral accusations fails this early witness; one that uses early learning to erase later failures fails the chronology.

**MS09 — Humanitarian statements do not certify institutional understanding**

In the prisoner dispute Mary argues that abolished execution, proximity to home and Christmas relief favor Federation custody. Those are her stated reasons. A reconstruction must allow apparent care and serious error together. The text does not authorize making cruelty her sole motive, nor treating the claimed humane outcome as narrator-certified reality. The focalizer is Drake, whose understanding of the Party prison system is unavailable to Mary's independent interior channel.

The conditional rule is informational: if she accepts a formal policy assurance without understanding how the institution operates, she may press the apparent welfare argument even as an experienced officer tries to stop the exchange. Her repeated objection and dissatisfied departure are observable. Her complete motive remains unavailable. Respectful forms do not entail openness to the information she lacks. Liliya's assurance reaches this record through Mary's report; it cannot serve as a directly heard Liliya line. A sound simulation can constrain the public rationale while refusing to invent a private confession of either pure benevolence or concealed malice.

<a id="ms10"></a>

**MS10 — A superior can withhold reasons that Mary fails to infer**

Mary insists on discussing the captives in a room Drake expects to be monitored, despite his attempts to relocate the conversation. She hears partial legal and political explanations and challenges an apparent fixation on results or trophies. Drake's private humanitarian alarm, regard for Mikel and concern about political exposure remain his channels. Her misunderstanding does not convert those contents into her knowledge.

In a close analogue, track at least three variables separately: what the commander believes, what he is permitted to say, and what Mary can reasonably infer from what she hears. If the third is poor, shared moral vocabulary need not produce agreement. Do not solve the exchange by having her spontaneously recite his private rationale. Equally, do not make every refusal by a superior genuinely cynical just because she reads it that way. The source outcome is censure and dissatisfied departure, not successful ethical reconciliation. If a hypothetical provides explicit new information in a safe setting, the package has too little comparable evidence to guarantee whether Mary would revise her understanding.

**MS11 — Late justice is a normative demand, not an observed law of the world**

The V12 segment begins after a change of place and focalization. Mary treats the victory of justice as what the world must satisfy, expressly beyond logic or argument. Her certainty is source-supported as her state. It cannot certify that her side will win, that every allied act is just, or that an opponent is guilty in every respect. The preceding Tanya paragraphs belong to Tanya and must not be imported into Mary's cognition.

For the late state, contradiction can be appraised through a moral demand rather than processed as neutral operational evidence. This permits predicting pressure toward action when comrades are reportedly attacked, but only with uncertainty about exact escalation. No clinical category is needed. Sincerity does not remove agency or consequences, and divine reinforcement does not prove the content true. A falsifying witness would be a directly comparable late moment of open reconsideration; early learning is important counterevidence against a lifelong caricature but does not itself falsify this later state. Missing intermediate development remains an explicit gap.

<a id="ms12"></a>

**MS12 — Comrade attachment can conflict with the reserve role**

News of allied contact causes Mary to recall comrades with whom she fought and rush into headquarters to request a launch. Drake answers that no reinforcement request has arrived and that the formation is a strategic reserve. She recasts waiting as letting comrades die and questions whether his higher position matters more than people. Attachment is real in the focalized segment; it does not supply the missing request or release authority.

Operationally, keep willingness to help separate from permission and feasibility. A late Mary analogue may press for immediate aid when role obligations are less salient to her than comrades' danger. A model should identify the institutional conflict even when it can explain her impulse. Her repeated 大佐殿 retains hierarchy in address while the accusation challenges judgment. The admitted range ends as Drake demands whether she understands what she has said. It supplies no launch, victory, final punishment or resolution. Generating any of those as a known consequence would exceed the witness. The proper output can be a bounded protest constraint with the next response marked unresolved.

<a id="ms13"></a>

**MS13 — Public accusation and private conviction are evaluated independently**

A reconstruction can select a plausible late protest but still fail the written voice if it uses a child's family phrasing, addresses a commander as an intimate, or speaks an unshown private monologue. Conversely, grammatical politeness can coexist with persistent accusation and a poor grasp of command constraints. Score the chosen action, speech act, register and channel boundary separately.

The six-step speech chain is state-dependent. In the novice briefing, anxiety and acknowledged ignorance support a request for contingency guidance through formal officer permission. In V06 the available private appraisal is withheld; her articulated prisoner-welfare reasoning supports persistent requests and challenges, with respectful address but no borrowed Drake thoughts. In V12 focalized moral certainty and comrade memory support an urgent request that becomes an accusation under an actual reserve-role refusal. These are explanatory constraints, not a deterministic script. Exact new lines should be labeled illustrative and never copied into source diagnostics. A coherent simulation may decline Japanese surface composition while still providing a source-routed decision explanation.

**MS15 — Counterevidence prevents both saint and fanatic shortcuts**

Five admitted episodes show considered enlistment, family concern, ordinary longing, practical novice learning, humanitarian articulation with institutional error and late closure. They do not resolve into one morally flattering or dismissive trait. Her peaceful alternatives make her commitment meaningful; they do not guarantee later judgment. Her grandmother's food and family-letter care make ordinary life visible; they do not erase serious conduct. Her early understanding of survival advice makes revision possible in one state; it does not excuse later accusations.

When testing a hypothetical, preserve the inconvenient witness nearest the proposed rule. A claim that she is incapable of learning must confront the briefing. A claim that care guarantees safe prisoner decisions must confront V06. A claim that she only wants battle must confront family and civilian alternatives. A claim that sincerity ensures truth must confront the late normative demand. The correct repair is usually narrower conditions rather than a new diagnostic label. Unshown adulthood, intimate life, permanent leisure preferences and a continuous developmental causal chain remain unavailable.

**MS16 — Use a domain assessment and stop before free-form omniscience**

This package supports conditional composition within deliberate recruitment, trainee correspondence and provision, novice guidance, a politically constrained prisoner dispute and late reserve conflict. Private cognition is directly available in some early and late segments, unavailable in the V06 Mary direction and the childhood farewell. Ordinary evidence is unusually concrete but temporally narrow. The model therefore remains DOMAIN_BOUNDED rather than acquiring a complete adult life from one letter.

Before use, specify developmental state, rank and formation, interlocutor, actual information, authority, audience and surveillance risk. Choose source witnesses, separate all five channels, select a plausible action or speech act, apply the six-step voice chain and list residual uncertainty. If the proposed scene lies in romance, domestic routine, medical diagnosis, unobserved faith practice or an unexamined gap, say not enough evidence. Author construction checks verify provenance, source normalization, voice ownership and claim hashes; they do not validate a forecast.
