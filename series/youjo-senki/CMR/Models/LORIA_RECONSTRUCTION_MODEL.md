**LO01 — A narrow but morally consequential domain**

Loria's admitted package contains two sharply different states: a predatory fixation during an air raid and a competent political-information explanation before the Party leadership. HIGH confidence concerns the source attribution and bounded findings. It does not establish a harmless ordinary voice, broad private life or a probability that every policy follows the same motive. The model preserves both states without converting competence into reform or predation into affection.

Select the live situation before forecasting. An official intelligence discussion can activate coherent analysis and calculated deference. A target-fixation state can redirect attention away from the punitive response subordinates fear. Neither licenses a sexualized reconstruction or a reciprocal relationship with Tanya. The original source's disturbing content is treated only as a non-graphic analytical fact. A new scene outside coercive official interaction or observed fixation requires explicit uncertainty. The package is a bounded mechanism register, not permission to improvise private social behavior.

The rule is compatible with some sincere Party patriots and some real material improvements, both admitted in the scene. It is false to simplify the explanation into nobody ever supports the Party.
