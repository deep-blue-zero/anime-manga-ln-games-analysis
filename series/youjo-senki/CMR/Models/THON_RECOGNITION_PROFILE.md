## Five channels identify what is actually available

The private-cognition channel is unavailable. The public-speech channel is also unavailable: speakers who name him do not supply his first-person wording. Reported conduct includes his absence from the meeting and officer reconnaissance, conveyed by Tospan rather than directly embodied as a Thon-focalized scene.

External interpretation carries much of the evidence. Weiss defends practical infantry self-sufficiency; Tanya criticizes readiness and later recognizes excess emotion in her appraisal; Tospan defends doctrine and officer initiative; Grantz doubts that a stubborn officer would abandon duty. These remain independently attributed judgments.

Institutional consequence is clearer than private motive: command is transferred and an MIA treatment is imposed for command purposes; later command remains elsewhere while search resources are constrained. Neither consequence proves what he believes, why he is absent, or what ultimately happened to him.

<a id="n03"></a>

## Early appraisal is disputed and developmental

The initial assessment establishes the full source name and captain's infantry role. Weiss sees practical self-reliance in infantry's need to handle its own problems. Tanya responds with harsh criticism of independence beyond readiness, then recognizes her own emotional excess.

This supports recognition of a field-adaptation problem as viewed by commanders. It cannot establish objective all-context incompetence, a complete private doctrine, or a timeless character essence. The disagreement itself matters: one derogatory summary cannot replace the other appraisal or the focalizer's self-correction.

No ordinary positive activity, household relation, taste, leisure, or intimate attachment is opened by this assessment. That is a limit of the admitted audit, not a claim that he has no such life anywhere in the novels.

<a id="n04"></a>

## Reconnaissance report and authority dispute retain their owners

During the defensive crisis Tospan reports that Thon independently left on officer reconnaissance and conveys a rationale about officers watching the front. Tospan then argues for multiple information sources, doctrinal discretion, and an analogy with Weiss's urgent outpost withdrawal.

Those are Tospan's report and defense. They are not an on-page Thon confession or exact original utterance. Tanya acknowledges that reconnaissance can be a plausible method under other force conditions, while rejecting it here and distinguishing unauthorized absence from immediate outpost withdrawal.

The competing considerations are legible in the dispute: information gathering and officer initiative versus force disparity, immediate defensive preparation, available consultation, and command concentration. They cannot be assigned as a verified complete set of Thon's private objectives. The source records contested doctrine under authority, not neutral legal adjudication.

Tanya transfers command to Grantz, threatens continued disruption, and directs Thon to be treated as MIA for command purposes, with an instruction to report if he returns. This establishes administrative handling rather than confirmed death, capture, or desertion.

<a id="n05"></a>

## Later absence remains unresolved

Later staff discussion has no sighting report. Grantz explicitly doubts that Thon, though stubborn, is the type to abandon duty. Tanya declines a separate search while battle still requires scarce personnel, considers possibilities privately, and ultimately advises against prejudgment.

The positive counterclaim concerns duty consistency as Grantz sees it. It is meaningful counterevidence to a settled deserter or coward label, but it is not direct Thon self-testimony or proof of survival. Tanya's death, capture, and desertion possibilities remain possibilities rather than an outcome record.

Use the most precise supported state: **absent; whereabouts and fate unresolved in the admitted window**. MIA does not equal death. Failure to witness return in this bounded passage does not demonstrate he never returns anywhere in the series.

## Falsifiers and recognition protocol

Resolve the source speaker, reporting layer, time, role, command consequence, and uncertainty before making even a recognition statement. A request for a full private or spoken scene, detailed tastes, intimate life, a moral diagnosis, or settled fate exceeds this profile; abstain or label unsupported extrapolation.
