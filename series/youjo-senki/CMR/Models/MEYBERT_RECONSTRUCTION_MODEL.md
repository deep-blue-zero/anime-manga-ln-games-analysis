# Meybert reconstruction model

<a id="m01"></a>

## Narrow domain and conditional person

Meybert supports a narrowly promoted **DOMAIN_BOUNDED, MODERATE** model: artillery, combined-arms colleagues, field/garrison friction, responsibility under impossible orders, and emergency reporting. Five diagnostics and six directed episodes support those contexts. They do not supply a complete civilian or intimate baseline.

The recoverable officer can intend strict conformity while overlooking his own cap violation, laugh with a junior colleague, adapt fire to a practical need, expose an impossible command through conditional pressure, and still report abnormally fragmentary speech in acute shock. He is neither perfect rigor nor timeless incompetence. His tendency to privilege a field task remains bounded by actual means and differentiated roles.

The [CMR method](../Methods/YOUJO_SENKI_CHARACTER_MODELING_REFERENCE_METHOD.md) governs this model; section VIII of the [mature V2 command-culture study](../../V2%20Analysis/03_IMPERIAL_PROFESSIONALS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COMMAND_CULTURE.md) remains the literary home. Its larger synthesis is not a substitute for this model's admitted source coordinates. Independent QA is pending at the original construction snapshot and remains separately versioned.

## Development and five channels

V05 supplies Tanya and Weiss's criticism of a newly integrated artillery captain's ammunition assumptions. His quoted exercise complaint is reported by another officer; it is not live Meybert dialogue or direct private explanation. The changed Eastern supply condition is the identified problem.

V09 directly exposes his rule intentions, embarrassment, field-derived appraisals, anger, and decision to prepare defense. The uniform scene begins with Tospan's discomfort, then shifts to Meybert's intentions and reflections. Do not merge their interiors. V14 radio reporting is direct speech observed through Tanya, and the later combined-arms convergence is principally Ahrens-focalized. Those scenes do not expose a full Meybert private monologue.

Private cognition, action, public speech, external interpretation, and institutional consequence remain separate. Reported early weakness does not invalidate later competence; later competence does not erase early difficulty. An offered shelling alternative is an act of communication, not an executed attack. Beginning relief is an action, not a guaranteed final rescue result.

## Attention: what can the available branch actually do?

The early reported failure concerns a mismatch between inherited artillery expectations and scarce supply. Later direct evidence shows a more discriminating account of means: skilled mages require friendly mage response; artillery supports infantry against boarders; destroyed fortification and friendly-fire immobilization make nominal rescue impossible; nearby troops and paper reserves cannot be treated as delivered strength.

The conditional composition is **identify the actual threat and means → distribute tasks by branch capability → make the feasible contribution → challenge a demand that assumes unavailable means → preserve immediate defense or relay an honest limit**. This is bounded field judgment, rather than an omniscient strategy engine.

He competes with more than one obligation. Regulations and command hierarchy matter, but so do surviving the engagement, supporting colleagues, avoiding immobilization by replaceable property, and keeping responsibility attached to an extreme order. His bluntness is not evidence that every legal constraint is meaningless to him. His recognition of limits prevents artillery enthusiasm from becoming a claim that guns alone solve every threat.

<a id="m04"></a>

## Ordinary positive texture and rule error

Meybert initially welcomes apparently quiet rear work. Actual garrison life then feels constricting after field habits. He admits difficulty readapting to regular schedules without claiming total incapacity. His cap, embarrassment, rueful laughter, and familiar talk with Tospan make the conflict ordinary and concrete.

When the junior complains about crisp uniform rules, Meybert sincerely instructs him to follow regulations. Tospan courteously points out the crushed cap. Meybert touches it, recognizes the overlooked contradiction, and acknowledges field-unit influence. He does not punish this bounded correction or pretend his initial self-image was infallible.

The repair is **concrete inconsistency presented respectfully → revise intended rule compliance in light of the object → admit drift and discuss the shared difficulty**. This supports corrigibility in this collegial setting, not a guarantee that every subordinate may challenge him without constraint. His comments on Tanya's patriotism remain observer readings.

Actual collegial laughter and desire for usable rest are positive evidence. Neither the cap nor envy of armored exercise conditions supplies a favorite fashion, hobby, domestic routine, or permanent civilian alienation.

<a id="m05"></a>

## Tospan: reciprocal correction and differentiated work

Meybert addresses a lieutenant and can initially enforce a rule, while Tospan preserves respect and nonetheless corrects a specific inconsistency. The captain then asks for his view of the rear environment. The junior qualifies his own ability to articulate it rather than becoming an echo.

Meybert's stronger outsider description is his own. Tospan's hesitancy must not inherit that confident formulation or the captain's private appraisal. Their shared jokes and experience permit a working relation without establishing unrestricted intimacy.

At the port Meybert expects the infantry colleague to do the necessary deployment while complaining. That expectation demonstrates Meybert's trust and interpretation; it is not itself direct observation of every Tospan action. Role differentiation matters: he can offer fire support while relying on infantry and mages for their own tasks. Removing those limits would falsely turn the relation into solitary artillery mastery.

<a id="m06"></a>

## Property tradeoff is specific to combat need

During the port attack Meybert delegates enemy mage response to friendly mages, keeps artillery useful against boarders, and changes one gun to high-explosive ammunition. He recognizes that not every attacker can be stopped before landing.

He rejects a subordinate's objection that friendly facilities may be damaged. His direct appraisal treats replaceable facility damage as secondary to the combat task and accepts writing the necessary explanation afterward. This supports task-oriented initiative and accountability in the sampled engagement.

It does not license arbitrary destruction, establish indifference to all law, or imply that every friendly-person risk is equivalent to property damage. The branch is **active boarding threat with limited stopping capacity → prioritize useful support over preserving facilities intact → direct ammunition/fire orders → captain commanding gun personnel → concise imperative and blunt refusal → source-owned controls**. The actual threat and ammunition distinction are causal; without them, the harsh phrase becomes a caricature.

<a id="m07"></a>

## Impossible rescue: formal pressure changes the public act

When a destroyed bunker is ordered relieved despite friendly-fire immobilization and enemy mages, Meybert privately recognizes the contradiction and becomes angry. He takes the receiver, breathes once, and transforms that anger into an upward report.

He states the shortage and conditionally asks for a responsibility-bearing order to shell everyone present. The extreme alternative exposes what the demand would mean with the available means. It is communicative pressure, not an actual indiscriminate bombardment or private wish to kill indiscriminately.

His public courtesy is therefore consequential. The six-stage chain is **direct impossibility appraisal and anger → force the superior to confront feasibility and authorize responsibility explicitly → formal limit report and conditional extreme alternative → subordinate addressing command → controlled honorific qualification → actor-owned written forms**. Internal insult and receiver-slamming are distinct from the carefully phrased call.

Afterward he orders all personnel to prepare close defense. Challenging the infeasible implication does not make him abandon the obligation to fight. A reconstruction must retain both resistance to nonsense and continued local responsibility.

<a id="m08"></a>

## Crisis delta: fragmentary speech still carries honest information

V14's decapitation assault produces an unusually agitated call. Tanya explicitly treats the distress as uncharacteristic. Meybert's repetition and broken onset are state-dependent; they are not a permanent voice tic or proof that earlier competence was imaginary.

He identifies the threat, says the surrounding facts are unknown, asks about abandoning the base, confirms the “any sacrifice” condition, and requests mage support. These are meaningful clarification acts despite shock. The source does not make him invent a complete tactical picture.

Tanya orders all available personnel committed and states the physical limit on timely support. Her attempted calming does not fully calm him, and no reassuring wording can abolish distance. Her private prioritization and enemy-cost hypothesis are unavailable to him except for what she discloses. His commitment to begin relief does not prove a completed rescue.

<a id="m09"></a>

## Ahrens: usable scarcity replaces comforting paper strength

At the later convergence Meybert arrives with hauled guns and joins the provisional combined-arms posture. Ahrens asks for rescue capacity and strategic reserves. Meybert answers that few nearby troops are available and the reserve exists on paper.

This clipped peer report differs from his earlier stammered superior-facing call. It supports functional participation after acute shock, but not a complete private recovery account. Ahrens's joy, warmth, or internal reinforcement desire cannot become Meybert's private state.

The useful orientation is **report delivered capacity rather than nominal allocation**. His uncomfortable realism remains cooperative: it informs what the assembled forces can actually do. Neither the grim answer nor provisional role coordination establishes omniscience about every reserve or the ultimate battle outcome.

## Written Japanese retains audience and state

| Act and audience | Verified short forms | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Rule instruction and collegial reflection | 規則だ; 私; 中尉; 貴官 | Senior/junior garrison conversation |
| Gun personnel instruction | 知るか; 弾種変更 | Blunt local combat orders |
| Upward infeasibility report | 小官; ないかと存じます; 幸いです | Polite conditional pressure, not agreement |
| Urgent radio report | 中佐殿; 全てが不明です; 期待できますか | Shock with retained uncertainty |
| Peer scarcity report | 紙の上だ | Clipped capacity correction |

The index binds each control to an exact primary occurrence and actor assignment. The early exercise complaint is another officer's report and supplies no literal live-voice rule. Tospan's courteous correction and Ahrens's exuberant welcome cannot be borrowed.

Written narration can establish delivery cues but supplies no performed accent, pitch, or frequency measure. A stammer belongs to its crisis; an honorific belongs to its upward act; blunt gun orders do not define all ordinary speech.

<a id="m11"></a>

## Speech must follow the six-stage chain

Apply **private appraisal → communicative objective → speech act → relationship/role constraint → register → surface wording**. Direct V09 access permits tracking a deliberate transformation from private anger to formal pressure. Where V14 private access is absent, stop at supported objectives and visible distress.

The same captain can admit a cap error, reassure an infantry colleague, order a gun crew sharply, condition an impossible superior demand, or request help in shock. A generic loud artillery voice erases these differences. A polite surface also fails if it turns resistance into assent or emits the extreme alternative as an executed decision.

Resolve what he knows at that moment. Unknown surroundings stay unknown; delayed mage support stays delayed; paper reserves cannot be made physically present. Generated dialogue is an extrapolation from conditions and controls, never new source evidence.

<a id="m12"></a>

## Repair, falsifiers, and use ceiling

The admitted error-repair evidence is strong for the mundane cap inconsistency and bounded for developmental learning. Earlier supply assumptions are reported critically; later task discrimination is directly shown. The intervening complete teaching history, general apology habit, and every private reaction to failure are not supplied.

Source falsifiers are concrete: perfect rule compliance loses the cap lapse; timeless ineptitude loses later initiative; lawless destruction loses the specific property/combat tradeoff and accountability; indiscriminate enthusiasm loses the conditional speech act; permanent stammer loses the later clipped report; limitless artillery mastery loses branch dependence and real scarcity.

Resolve era, role, interlocutor, task, known threat, available means, physical state, and authority before use. The model remains narrow. Detailed leisure, family, intimacy, tastes, broad civilian philosophy, and a full psychological endpoint require abstention or labeled extrapolation; they are unknown in this focused scope rather than absent from all novels.

All five diagnostics and six directions retain their original channels and ceilings in one source catalog. New source reads were checked against all seven reserved ranges; no QA input, expected file, or outcome was consumed. The frozen original has no claimed independent QA pass.
