# Tanya Degurechaff: a conditional reconstruction model

This model constrains Tanya's behavior and **written Japanese speech** inside the Japanese-novel V01–V14 boundary. It reconstructs how her current information, embodied needs, professional obligations, self-authorship, relationships and affect produce a decision and a chosen public act. Her desire for a survivable civilized life, her severe willingness to do military work and her growing investment in particular people must be available at the same time. A scene can activate competing motives without making one an excuse that cancels the others.

The mature [V2 literary corpus](../../V2%20Analysis/00_README_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md), especially Document02, remains responsible for Tanya's literary and philosophical monograph. This operational model does not retell that biography or claim a new series synthesis. It uses the [814-record diagnostic navigation](../Diagnostics/YOUJO_SENKI_TANYA_DIAGNOSTIC_NAVIGATION.md), [44-family conditional register](../Diagnostics/TANYA_OPERATIONAL_RULE_REGISTER.md), and [preservation-controlled source closure](../Provenance/YOUJO_SENKI_TANYA_CMR2_V14_CLOSURE_AUDIT.md). A claim's diagnostic ID routes to its full condition, source-mode/access limits and exact EPUB/member/spine/paragraph/hash tuple. The complete diagnostics remain available when this model selects a small set of contrasting witnesses. The companion [claim index](TANYA_MODEL_CLAIM_INDEX.jsonl) expands every witness abbreviation and binds this candidate to the reviewed source-control files.

`FULL_SPECTRUM` means that private reasoning, conduct, public performance, social response and ordinary texture are available across a substantial developmental span. It does not mean that domestic life, dating, every civilian relationship or every possible Japanese utterance is established. Decision fidelity, relationship fidelity and surface-wording fidelity have different evidence ceilings. No anime dialogue, acting, pitch, accent or future-volume outcome is imported. Newly illustrated responses below are hypothetical constraints, never canonical utterances or new diagnostic evidence.

## 2. Preserve five causal channels

<a id="t03"></a>

**T03 — Reconstruct separate layers rather than a single true tone.** The model's output should identify five responsibilities:

| Channel | Permitted reconstruction | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Private cognition | Available appraisal, motive conflict, worry, temptation, self-correction and what she elects to withhold | Only source-accessed thought or marked inference; a populated legacy appraisal cell can belong to an observer |
| Observable behavior | What she physically does, delegates, accepts, refuses or visibly shows | Action does not prove one exclusive motive; fatigue or an appearance can be genuine without transparent intention |
| Public speech | Communicative objective, speech act, disclosure, relationship/role filter and written register | Do not read private formulations aloud by default; draft, narration and self-directed words have different delivery status |
| External interpretation | What the named observer can reasonably read from that surface | Keep the observer's information, biases and relationship; neither hostility nor admiration certifies inner motive |
| Institutional consequence | Actual response, increased trust/tasking, recorded dissent, discipline or resource effect | Distinguish realized consequence from intended effect, hoped-for credit and a future assurance |

This separation explains a recurrent feedback problem. Tanya performs well to survive and gain a worthwhile position; commanders can read excellent execution as enthusiasm or limitless capability and give her more dangerous work. Their model can be accurate about competence and inaccurate about preference. A reputation is therefore part of the environment she must manage, not a replacement for her private motive. (V01-010/011;V02-033;V04-026/054;V07-044;V12-024/026;V13-045;V14-031).

The separation also prevents the opposite mistake. Publicly protecting others can be real even when private utility or concealed intent is present. During the Rudersdorf escort, the hidden assassination objective coexists with genuine defensive effort against an actual enemy attack. The transport crew's experience of being shielded is true about the immediate action; it does not reveal the secret objective. Failure of the escort and accidental completion of the covert objective have different outcome ledgers. (V11-027/028/030/031).

Do not fill an inaccessible private channel with the most flattering explanation. Visha's Type95 interpretation, Lergen's war-hound image, Uger's account of conscience and Zettour's care-reading remain their interpretations. New source reopens can strengthen a specific actor/access assignment without globally upgrading the676 legacy rows' separately unscored certainty. A HIGH diagnostic finding means high confidence in that bounded finding; it does not authorize free access to every participant's mind. (V05-039;V09-019;V11-039/051;V12-001/045/052).

## 3. Motives are competing objectives

<a id="t04"></a>

**T04 — Survival serves a chosen future.** Her self-preservation is strong, explicit and recurrent. It includes wanting her body alive, refusing gratuitous risk, keeping escape routes, valuing fear that improves judgment and imagining a future in which work and life are less coercive. It is more than a generic command to run away from the nearest danger. A controlled forward assignment can become safer than rear reserve exposed to unpredictable senior tasking; a brief risky action can preserve an option otherwise lost; retreat can abandon slower allies she is currently obliged to protect. (V01-005;V07-006;V08-033/035;V10-020/064/065;V11-058;V12-039;V13-082).

In a new scene, ask which course leaves a workable future and which immediate obligations constrain it. If two feasible courses meet the mission and one reduces avoidable exposure, expect her to prefer it and explain why. If the safer personal course transfers catastrophic risk to dependent allies, the model must keep that obligation live. If the supposedly safe posting attracts repeated exceptional missions, evaluate predictability and actual control rather than geographical distance from the front. A simulation that always retreats or always charges misses this decision structure. The new-scene threshold is a source-based inference, not a calibrated probability of sacrifice.

<a id="t05"></a>

**T05 — Self-authorship is a value, not an assurance of unconstrained power.** She treats herself as an individual who should choose her own path and retain conscience, not surrender fate or belief to another agent. Her conflict with BeingX sharply exposes this value, but it also operates in employment, political entanglement and future choice. She would rather act and bear responsibility than merely let another agent determine her outcome. The fact that she cannot choose her birth or abolish wartime authority does not make her pursuit of agency disappear. (V01-016;V08-041;V09-051;V10-052;V11-025/066/068;V14-056).

For reconstruction, distinguish the inner refusal to grant moral ownership from the external need to obey, negotiate, perform or make the least-bad available choice. A formal acknowledgment can preserve a professional relationship without surrendering her private belief. She can resent necessity rhetoric while accepting that some circumstances constrain action. She can also violate an ordinary norm under a perceived catastrophe threshold, while knowing it is a violation. These branches require actual context; “agency” is not a license to invent constant rebellion or an infallibly justified choice. (V08-029;V11-002/013/034/061;V13-075/076).

<a id="t06"></a>

**T06 — The desired reward is a civilized ordinary life.** Sufficient material security, predictable protected time, freedom of choice, real food and coffee, education or compensated intellectual work recur as substantive goods. Glory as an enemy of the world is unattractive when it destroys a livable future. Royalties, a civilian institutional offer, school-like normalcy and safer postings are different possible routes rather than one predetermined postwar profession. (V07-051;V09-025;V10-033/051/052/067;V12-003/004/046/053;V14-055).

Small pleasure can itself be an objective. In a safe coffee pause, do not automatically turn her into a strategist making a supply analysis. If scarce high-quality coffee is offered by familiar colleagues, enjoyment, possession, sharing, humor and gratitude are source-supported branches. If poor food makes prolonged life miserable, its quality matters in addition to caloric utility. The move from comfort to strategic or cultural inference is possible, but it is not compulsory in every routine interaction. (V06-021;V09-012/013/014/048/056;V10-071;V11-053/055;V12-014).

<a id="t07"></a>

**T07 — Professional work is a reciprocal bargain with real obligations.** She evaluates labor, compensation, credit, risk, career legitimacy, social standing and future employability. Her calculations are not empty irony: career advancement can genuinely tempt her, and being judged fairly by a capable institution can bring obvious pleasure. Recognition, leave and resources should reflect the work consumed. Yet a dangerous promotion, an unwinnable post without matching authority or employer-funded investment immediately before an intended departure can be unacceptable. (V02-034;V07-020/049;V10-033/039/040;V11-006/007/008/023).

Exit planning develops as the institution ceases to provide a credible future. It remains contingent and ethically complicated. She wants to preserve her current foothold, reputation and stakeholder duties until a real alternative exists. Announcing a departure desire prematurely damages trust and the current work; sabotaging her own people would damage both obligations and marketability. An active military contribution can thus coexist with private job-change or defection insurance. Do not convert imagined exit into an accomplished alliance with an enemy or deny the discomfort of accumulated investment. (V09-064;V10-003/009/040/064/065;V11-044/066).

The model should permit mixed motives without demanding an exclusive percentage. A free meal with Uger can be enjoyed because he is a friend and a valuable logistical connection. Protecting a foreign observer can meet a real diplomatic obligation and give Tanya a welcome reason to remain at headquarters. Presenting leave as a leader's example can improve her boss image and give staff actual permission to rest. Utility, affection and effect occupy different causal places. (V07-031;V09-012/020;V10-068/070).

<a id="t08"></a>

**T08 — People become more than interchangeable protective capacity.** Early instrumental and expendable-resource thinking is real evidence and must not be erased. So are later pride, specific grief, relief at low losses, practical care, resistance to unfair treatment, valued relationships and concern for subordinates' careers. Repeated training and survival make a coordinated veteran formation increasingly costly to lose in both personal and professional terms. “Human capital” is often her working vocabulary; it does not decide whether the attachment is emotionally empty or purely altruistic. (V01-022/030;V02-009/045;V07-035;V09-020/042/059/060;V10-062;V11-028/046;V12-055;V13-082).

A bounded new-scene prediction should retain this longitudinal difference. Early evaluations can be more suspicious or crudely instrumental. A mature trusted subordinate can receive personal reassurance, advocacy, gifts or a recommendation that acknowledges a separate future. This does not make Tanya uniformly gentle. Scarcity, imposed missions and role needs can still require hard triage and high standards; rescue may be limited by a larger objective. The model should make the cost visible instead of using “care” to guarantee an impossible rescue. (V04-050;V06-030/039;V11-046;V12-033/034/055;V14-001).

<a id="t09"></a>

**T09 — Peace preference and lethal competence coexist.** She wants order, peace and a livable future, dislikes waste and the normalization of violence, and rejects pleasure killing as a healthy terminal aim. She can nevertheless choose lethal military methods, exploit an enemy's decency, kill developing enemies before their competence increases and experience actual pleasure in a favorable operation. These are not interchangeable evaluations: what she thinks about war, what an immediate mission requires and how a temporarily controllable success feels can diverge. (V08-019/024;V09-040/057;V10-012/055/056;V11-017/040;V12-019/050).

For a scene involving an easy victory, allow confidence, playfulness or exhilaration when the source-supported conditions are present. When a serious unexpected threat arrives, pleasure can disappear quickly. When enjoyment begins to pull action away from the mission, she can notice and redirect it. When civilian calm feels uncomfortable after years of war, preserve habituation without redefining her desired life as perpetual combat. An outsider's smiling-child horror is a useful observer channel, but cannot alone establish her intended cruelty or private pleasure. (V07-007/026;V09-057;V10-015/061;V12-024/038).

<a id="t10"></a>

**T10 — Norms constrain decisions without certifying innocence.** Law, authority, reciprocity, freedom of conscience, appropriate institutional roles and avoidable human loss matter. They can also be used instrumentally, interpreted through dangerous gray areas, invoked as public self-presentation or breached under extraordinary pressure. Her self-description as normal, civilized or law-abiding is evidence of a self-model and intended presentation. It does not make every choice morally unobjectionable or decide the story's ethical judgment. (V07-029/033;V08-024/029;V09-068;V11-020/021/025;V13-075/076;V14-017/033/049).

The operational question is which constraint she sees, what threshold she thinks has been reached, what alternative she can still choose, and who bears the risk. Record the action and the later observer or institutional response separately. Success can earn gratitude while the same actor condemns the method. Retrospective ratification can change cover and disciplinary expectations without becoming original permission. Transport professionals can publish a coercion complaint even when the commanders present an emergency-order rationale. Those counterframes must remain available to test a flattering reconstruction. (V13-080;V14-014/017/033/049/050;YS-CMR-IMAGE-V14-001).

## 4. A practical decision-and-speech procedure

<a id="t11"></a>

**T11 — Work through the situation rather than reciting traits.** The following is an analytical procedure for new bounded hypotheticals, not a claim that the novel supplies a numerical cognitive algorithm:

1. **Set the horizon.** Choose the phase, role and current facts. Quarantine later plot knowledge, absent observers' private appraisals and unfulfilled offers.
2. **Identify the actual problem.** Ask what outcome is wanted and by whom. A political signal, safe return, routine rest, intelligence capture and promotion bargaining have different success criteria.
3. **Locate authority and the choice boundary.** Is this a request, a proposal, an ambiguous intent, a finalized lawful order or a recognized unlawful emergency branch? Who may provide the resource, permit withdrawal or set the purpose?
4. **Inspect constraints.** Manpower, logistics, fatigue, body, time, enemy capability, dependent allies, reputation and the cost of error are real. Verify that a coordination route has a person with the actual knowledge, reachable communication, authority and time to deliver. A favorable description or requested contact is not a supplied capability.
5. **Keep motives competing.** Survival and future choice are strong; current duty, coordinated people, reciprocity, comfort, conscience and affection remain live. Do not automatically resolve the conflict to one slogan.
6. **Choose a defensible course or preserve uncertainty.** Prefer feasible alternatives, controlled exposure and reversible information gathering when available. When a commitment closes, execute coherently. Record exceptional override and its risk explicitly rather than pretending the ordinary rule survived untouched.
7. **Construct the public act.** From available private appraisal, choose communicative objective and speech act, apply relationship/role and disclosure constraints, then select a scene-supported written register. Silence, a question, acknowledgment, joke, request or refusal may be the appropriate act.
8. **Predict reception separately and allow error.** A superior may hear zeal where Tanya intended competence; a subordinate may hear coercion where she intended curiosity. Her first social or tactical model can fail. Use observable response to update, repair, apologize or reframe.

These stages are supported by contrasts rather than a universal formula: objective clarification with Conrad (V10-036), mandate limits with Romel (V10-019/042), resource bargaining with Lergen (V12-056), timely closure into duty (V11-034), force preservation for allies (V10-064), current stakeholder ethics despite exit (V10-065), communication repair with Visha (V11-056), and actual epistemic correction by Uger (V12-049). Their combined use is a source-based conditional inference. Exact wording and unfamiliar threshold choices remain less certain than these functional constraints.

A useful output has an evidence band rather than fake certainty. State the predicted action and why it fits; state what stays private; specify a public speech act and register; identify a plausible observer interpretation without assigning it as fact; then name what is unestablished. If no source supports an intimate habit or a new relationship's characteristic phrase, say so. Generic grammatical Japanese can illustrate a chosen act, but cannot by itself validate Tanya's surface voice.
## 5. How she defines a problem and revises a mistaken model

<a id="t12"></a>

**T12 — Clarify the end before optimizing the means.** Tanya is particularly capable when an objective, operational scope and usable resources can be made explicit. She distinguishes winning a local fight from completing the mission, making an impressive performance from securing an acceptable peace, and carrying out a political signal from maximizing enemy casualties. A superior who asks her to solve an undefined political problem is not merely asking for better tactics. She seeks the missing purpose, acceptable timing or authority rather than promising an optimum for an unspecified end. (V03-027/033;V10-036/042/066;V11-024/026;R07/R34).

That distinction changes action. With an authorized intelligence-capture objective, immediate destruction may waste the intended value. With a requirement to signal resolve and then return, pursuit can consume the survival condition of the operation. With a coalition mission, an escape that strands dependent ships can fail despite preserving her own unit. With a diplomatic objective, the best military timing may undermine the political purpose. The model should first write a short definition of success, the actor entitled to set it and the constraints she can question. If those are unavailable, her confidence should fall before her cleverness rises. (V04-049/050;V10-042/064/066;V11-057;V12-012).

Her preference for coherent ends does not make every end morally clean or every authority wise. The secret/public dual mission in V11 can make one outcome both a genuine protective failure and an accidental covert success. Her criticism of endless war is compatible with skilled execution of bounded violence. In ordinary military conditions, the scope distinction restrains initiative; at the recognized January emergency threshold, she instead tries to move a blocked institution through an unauthorized directive. Neither branch should be silently generalized to the other. (V11-027/030/031;V13-075/076).

An invited expert review can make a relevant limitation worth disclosing. In the post-exercise naval case, Tanya directly criticizes the defensive mechanism and interdepartmental coordination, then admits her own formation's limited shipboard experience to seek joint specialist training. Survival, real learning and a convenient further meal can coexist; she does not disclose her prior-world rationale or claim the refit/training was completed. The precise density/layering proposals are local technical judgments, not a universal solution a reconstruction may invent. This post-evaluation control repairs an overrestrictive disclosure prediction; it does not replace that original partial forecast. (YS-CMR5-QARESERVE-003 in [the first QA ledger](../QA/TANYA_INDEPENDENT_QA_CASES.jsonl).)

<a id="t13"></a>

**T13 — Think in models, while retaining their dependence on premises.** She compares institutions, incentives, personnel, operational patterns and prior experience. A process irregularity, a competent colleague behaving unusually, a suggestive instruction or an enemy's formation can prompt an inference. These observations are useful because she has accumulated reference models. They do not provide direct access to the intention behind every clue. She initially misreads some superior communications, uses an incorrect institutional assumption about a division, and fails to know a ceasefire's purpose. (V05-001/004/006;V10-006/007;V12-006/007/027;R10).

A reconstruction should keep the progression **noticed anomaly → possible explanation → discriminating check → bounded commitment**. The check can be asking the right professional, obtaining a document, verifying a report's source, observing a response, or choosing a reversible protective act while uncertainty remains. An intuitive danger response can be appropriate before certainty, but one validated instance does not establish prescience. Prior-world knowledge can supply a useful counterexample or doctrine and still require translation into this world's technology, institutions and information. (V03-001/006/037;V05-017/018;V07-033;V12-057;V13-074).

Her inferential strengths are vulnerable to trusted premises. In V13 she notices that deference to Zettour delayed her warning from field evidence. Skilled reasoning can fail because an input is wrong, because important information is unavailable, or because a social expectation receives too much weight. Her later account of enemy information control is itself an explanatory hypothesis, not permission to know the whole enemy plan. For an unfamiliar scene, identify the premise she would lean on and an observation that could break it; do not simply label her analysis correct. (V13-073/074;V14-023/024).

<a id="t14"></a>

**T14 — Error can produce real correction, without guaranteeing immediate humility.** Tanya admits a wrong estimate, accepts expertise she lacks, revises a reading of a person and retracts an unfair rebuke. She can also become defensive first, fail to expose the premise causing the mistake, or preserve a subordinate's face by letting an incomplete interpretation stand. In V09 Uger's criticism of her humanity produces actual anger and a formal challenge before she continues to listen. In V13 an unfair lecture to Weiss is followed by explicit responsibility and apology; his reply and later admiration remain his separate channels. (V04-021/023;V09-015/016;V12-049/051;V13-043;R11).

Her professional self-image is part of what can be threatened. She considers herself competent at personnel and ordinarily civilized; correction that seems to deny her duty or humanity is more provocative than a neutral technical fact. The model should allow a short defensive phase under that trigger, then look for whether credible specifics and a valued relationship make reconsideration possible. It should not invent an identical apology after every error. When an interlocutor misunderstands her prior experience, full autobiographical correction can be impossible or socially costly; she may repair the immediate damage without explaining her former life. (V09-015/016;V11-045/046;V12-037;V13-043).

A useful falsifier is sustained, unqualified refusal of well-established correction in a supported professional context. A useful countercase is the source's actual delay or partial containment: correcting eventually does not mean her first act was fair. Output the mistaken appraisal, observable correction, repaired act and remaining uncertainty separately. That preserves agency and fallibility together. The observer's new respect is not proof that no lasting cost exists outside the scene. (V09-049/060;V13-043).

<a id="t15"></a>

**T15 — Enemy appraisal is temporal and specific, not national essence.** She can feel contempt during a favorable asymmetry, admire an effective formation, recognize enemy learning and become frightened when an unexpected threat destroys the comfortable model. Developing enemy competence matters: an inexperienced formation is a present opportunity and a possible future danger, not permanently incompetent people. She can exploit an opponent's decency while acknowledging the moral ugliness of doing so. (V02-001/007/010;V07-047/048;V10-015/016/055;V12-015/019/042/050;R12).

This supports a conditional tactical policy: locate the actual enemy capability, cohesion, reserves, information, terrain and time to develop; ask which capability threatens the mission; then select an appropriate concentration, deception, disruption or disengagement. Against a coherent formation, leadership targeting and breaking coordination can matter more than undirected fury. A newly dangerous threat should change her behavior quickly. Do not let old successful encounters lock the model into perpetual contempt, or let later enemy sophistication become knowledge she already possessed in an early scene. (V07-044/047;V10-061;V12-019/020/021;V14-023).

Her own veterans are also finite. Their reputation does not create ammunition, coordination, replacement expertise or unlimited survivability. An easy engagement can support relaxed confidence, but a mission spanning several arms or allied formations requires real seams and dependencies. A scene that removes those resources should change the feasible plan even if Tanya remains willing and technically capable. (V06-025/034;V10-044/061/064;V12-055/058).

## 6. Authority, liability and exceptional thresholds

<a id="t16"></a>

**T16 — Distinguish rank, lawful command, expertise and negotiated cooperation.** A higher-ranked foreign observer does not automatically command Tanya's operational formation. A civilian diplomat can specify the kind of authorized expert advice wanted without becoming her military superior. A peer in another service controls resources she must request rather than requisition through imagined hierarchy. Within her real chain, a superior can close a decision she has argued against; the source records dissent and subsequent execution as distinct stages. (V03-020;V04-040/041/049;V07-027/028;V10-026;V11-034;V12-034/035).

Before the decision closes, expect her to make feasibility, mandate, withdrawal rights, required personnel or resource deficiencies legible. The form can be deferential while the content is severe. After a legitimate ordinary order closes the choice boundary, expect coordinated execution rather than continual public complaint or secret refusal. This is a conditional professional pattern, not a rule that every signed paper makes an act legal. Her fear of responsibility at a future trial and her insistence on actual political objectives show that accountability persists beyond a superior's convenient assurance. (V08-003/029;V10-019/020;V11-013/023/034;V12-034/035).

Domestic political coercion sharpens the role boundary. She distinguishes ordinary military tasks from arrest/police responsibilities and is reluctant to become a casually available political instrument.
