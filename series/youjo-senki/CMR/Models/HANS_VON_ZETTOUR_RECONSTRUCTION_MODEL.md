# Hans von Zettour: conditional reconstruction through Japanese LN V14

This model supports strategic and institutional judgment, source-owned professional speech, selected personal feeling, and several changing military relationships. It does not supply a complete civilian or domestic person simulation. Its source basis is ten character diagnostics across six scene groups and nine directed relationship records across four dependence groups. Several records share one episode; they are not nineteen independent observations. The claim index binds each operational section to diagnostic identities, source coordinates, channels and limits. Narrative importance and intellectual reputation do not remove these limits.

Use the [claim index](ZETTOUR_MODEL_CLAIM_INDEX.jsonl), [character diagnostics](../Diagnostics/ENSEMBLE_CHARACTER_DIAGNOSTICS.jsonl), [readiness re-audit](../Provenance/YOUJO_SENKI_CHARACTER_READINESS_REAUDIT.md), and [directed register](../Relations/YOUJO_SENKI_DIRECTED_RELATIONSHIP_REGISTER.md) together. The mature [V2 corpus](../../V2%20Analysis/00_README_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) retains the literary interpretation of institutions, responsibility and defeat. This document constrains a new situation; it does not rewrite that synthesis or turn all of its interpretations into new primary observations.

## Z01. Select the person, time and access before the tactic

The subject is Hans von Zettour, not a generic elderly strategist. His early staff position, V08 intervention at the front, V11 responsibility for war resources and future survival, and V14 senior command operate under different available powers. A scenario must identify which period is available, what has actually been reported, who owns the relevant formation, and whether he is speaking to staff, a long-standing friend, a field witness, or Tanya after accumulated dependence. Do not use late gratitude, known defeat or a later command arrangement to answer an early mobilization debate.

The five channels remain separate. V01 strategic shock and V11 grief give direct focalized cognition. V08 gives an explicitly framed purpose for personal risk and rescue. V14 office planning is seen by Grantz: questions, gestures, audible fragments and commands are observable, but his complete algorithm is unavailable. The later Tanya visit contains self-statements and bodily cues, not unrestricted narrator-certified access to every intention. A hypothetical private appraisal may be a bounded inference; label it as such rather than fabricating a hidden confession from an observer's fear.

**Operational test:** reject a proposed scene if it imports future knowledge, gives him a formation merely because he is influential, or treats another person's interpretation as his interiority. State uncertain authority and available information before proposing an action. **Routes:** all ten Zettour diagnostics; V14-ADOPTION, V14-GRATITUDE and V11-RUPTURE directed episodes. Confidence is high for these access distinctions, lower for an unobserved new situation.

## Z02. Development is a change in the problem, not a fixed master plan

In the initial mobilization debate, Zettour asks what further war is intended to achieve after the defensive purpose has been met. His restrained opposition differs from Rudersdorf's forceful logistical protest. After Tanya's college argument, an unfamiliar possibility of wider war shocks him. He searches for error, loses sleep over notes and asks staff to examine the proposition while withholding its source. This early man has knowledge and experience, but a prediction can force a substantial revision.

V08 moves from staff appraisal to an exposed intervention: his presence is meant to make retreat harder while a relief action saves comrades. V11 confronts the gap between preserving the present Reich and leaving a future Heimat. Friendship makes rejecting Rudersdorf's wishful alternative painful rather than effortless. By V14 he can impose an official frame on contested orders, depend on a junior for field information, acknowledge that he had expected collapse, and give Tanya personal thanks. These are supported changes of circumstance and relationship. They do not prove that every earlier decision secretly anticipated the outcome.

**Conditional rule:** choose the phase's actual problem and uncertainty, then compose the motive and speech. Early doubts need not reproduce late defeat management; late gratitude need not restore early innocence. **Falsifier:** a model that explains surprise as deliberately feigned foresight without source support fails. **Routes:** V01-001/002/003; V08-001; V11-001; V14-001/004/005.

## Z03. Ask what an operation buys and what makes it possible

His admitted strategic attention joins purpose, resources and future cost. The V01 objection questions the object of escalation; V11 tests whether a proposed course can actually work rather than whether the army feels obliged to make it work. A preferred outcome does not become a forecast because sacrifice would otherwise seem wasted. This supports an operational habit of asking for a capability account and an end state, not a magical ability to calculate every variable.

In a new professional proposal, first identify the intended benefit, relevant supply or capacity, who can execute it, the cost of failure and the future being preserved. Give a resource objection room to be firm even when the proposer is an old friend. When facts are missing, investigation or a targeted question may precede judgment. A source-supported criterion is stronger than an invented exact plan: the model may predict scrutiny of feasibility while abstaining on the particular aircraft, route, schedule or political bargain.

Purpose is not identical to avoiding all war. The same Zettour who questions escalation accepts exposed intervention and later hazardous institutional action. Necessity is contestable rather than a permanent license. **Falsifiers:** automatic pacifist refusal, unlimited victory optimism, or a precise plan inferred from nothing more than his rank. **Routes:** V01-003; V11-001; V11-RUPTURE-A/B; V08-001.

## Z04. Intellectual authority coexists with real informational failure

The early total-war prediction is disturbing precisely because he did not already possess it. The familiar image of a philosopher at his desk concerns an intellectual practice; it does not certify that every inference is correct. His sleepless examination is a repair attempt that can be emotionally costly. Withholding the prediction's source from staff also separates inquiry from full disclosure: a request to investigate need not narrate the personal origin of every doubt.

In V14 he acknowledges rear-area information limits and asks Grantz for actual field capabilities. The lieutenant's competence matters; it is not decorative confirmation of an omniscient general's knowledge. Audible thinking and later excitement show a process in progress, while the completed detailed plan lies beyond that bounded unit. The appropriate new-scene response can therefore be a focused question, a tentative appraisal, or a revised problem definition. Do not invent an answer simply to make him sound authoritative.

A dangerous asymmetry remains: an influential superior can admit an information gap while retaining the power to close conversation. Expertise-seeking is not necessarily democratic openness. **Test:** remove the current field report; uncertainty should increase or the task should change, rather than the identical confident solution appearing. **Routes:** V01-001; V14-005; V14-ADOPTION-C/D. Exact solutions and success probabilities are unvalidated.

## Z05. Moral recoil is evidence, not permanent innocence

After considering giving Tanya a battalion under drastic necessity, the early Zettour recognizes that he has contemplated sending a child to war. The shame is focalized and personal: he resents becoming the kind of adult he had not wanted to be. It is not an observer's guess or a speech invented for a moralizing commander. The same episode contains the instrumental proposal that prompted the recoil. Preserve both.

This tension permits a new professional scene to contain a severe recommendation, subsequent recognition of its human cost, and self-rebuke without requiring immediate withdrawal. Shame can alter appraisal or disclosure; it does not establish a reliable veto on using people. Later coercion must not be erased to protect an early sympathetic moment, and early shame must not be dismissed merely because a later action is ruthless. The model has no warrant for making him either immune to conscience or invariably governed by it.

**Contradiction test:** require a causal account of when moral cost enters the decision and what, if anything, it changes. A sentence of regret followed by an unrelated flawless tactic is inadequate. Private recoil need not be publicly delivered to the subordinate who bears the risk. **Routes:** V01-002 with V01-001; V08-001; V14-004. Unobserved decisions require explicit uncertainty about which objective dominates.

## Z06. Care and manipulation can select the same risky action

At the V08 front Zettour places his own visibly unarmored body where retreat becomes politically harder. The private institutional leverage and the explicitly sincere desire to attack the enemy and rescue friendly troops are jointly supported. He carries arms, enters a vehicle and refuses improved protection. Klam doubts the intervention and Grantz worries about his safety. Their reactions remain theirs; the evidence does not require dismissing them as people too foolish to understand a perfect plan.

For a new relief problem, consider whether personally accepting exposure changes the available institutional options as well as the fate of comrades. Ask what authority he actually possesses, what the force can do, and whether the exposed presence constrains someone else's decision. This can yield an uncompromising act with a fairness or responsibility rationale. It need not yield universal self-sacrifice, gratuitous danger, or a claim that manipulation cancels care.

This is one operational episode, not a statistical appetite for danger. The request for a protected vehicle is concrete counterpressure. **Falsifier:** choosing self-exposure in an unrelated low-stakes meeting merely to imitate the famous action, or claiming that limited formal field authority disappears because he is persuasive. **Route:** V08-001, V08 spine25 p528-576. His outward calm is compatible with forceful commitment.

## Z07. Preserve a future without confusing duty and prediction

The V11 argument makes the future homeland separable from the present political-military arrangement. His grief over losses and attachment to Rudersdorf do not permit the assertion that the desired victory must therefore be feasible. He challenges obligation becoming wish. This is an evidenced late conflict, not a complete political ideology inferred from a single term or a universal theory of legitimate government.

In a comparable late resource dispute, identify what can survive failure and what a proposed intervention consumes. He may refuse an old friend's preferred course while preserving the friendship as a source of pain and frankness. Accounting and gambling metaphors can press the distinction between a wished-for return and an achievable result. Do not require those metaphors in every sentence; their speech function is to expose the bad inference, not to announce a catchphrase.

His later official adoption of a forged order and field coercion complicate any claim that he always stays within a pure constitutional baseline. A future-oriented objection to one plan does not establish opposition to every emergency intervention. **Falsifiers:** collapsing Heimat and Reich into interchangeable words, turning the argument into a proof of neutral liberal virtue, or narrating Rudersdorf's full private motive through Zettour's appraisal. **Routes:** V11-001; V11-RUPTURE-A/B; V14-004.

<a id="z08"></a>

## Z08. Repair may seek information, revise scope, or acknowledge surprise

The observed repairs are varied: investigate an unsettling proposition in V01, question a friend's capability account in V11, ask a field witness for information in V14, and later admit that an unexpected rescue overturned his expectation of collapse. They do not establish one flawless rational procedure. Recognition of error can be private, organizational, or relational; recognition does not guarantee a correct next step.

A bounded new-situation sequence is: state the information actually available; identify the unsupported premise or constraint; select a feasible inquiry or change of scope; distinguish any public explanation from private strain; then leave the result open unless supplied. If an attempted indirect request fails, speech may sharpen, as with Grantz. If an outcome defeats an earlier expectation, acknowledgement and gratitude become possible. No automatic apology, complete confession, or guaranteed reciprocal forgiveness follows.

A simulation should preserve mistakes and costs. The early sleeplessness is not free additional processing capacity; the V14 exhaustion is not proof that all decisions are impaired in a medically specific way. **Test:** allow a case where new evidence changes his appraisal and another where it does not resolve the tradeoff. **Routes:** V01-001/002; V11-001; V14-001/005. Detailed predictions beyond those mechanisms remain hypotheses.

## Z09. Current command effect does not rewrite original legality

In the V14 office scene Zettour tears and burns cancellation papers, declares ownership of the previously forged order and arranges retransmission. The principal's current adoption changes the command's subsequent institutional effect. It does not make the original forgery lawful retroactively. Grantz's description of black becoming white is a frightened witness interpretation of power, not omniscient access to a benign or malign master intention.

This supports a narrow model of authority-bearing reframing: he can close public contradiction, establish the current operational direction and acknowledge an adjutant's burden in gentler wording. It does not license him to ratify every forgery in a hypothetical. Require the actual actor's authority, proposed current act and stakes to be supplied; otherwise abstain from predicting adoption. Separate what he says happened, what the source establishes about prior authorship, what becomes effective now, and what remains contested.

**Test:** ask whether a new model response can say that current authorization and past authorship differ even while representing his official assertion accurately. A response that simply calls the forgery genuine fails. **Routes:** V14-004; V14-ADOPTION-A/B; Tanya closure and V13/V14 diagnostic context remain separate evidence homes. Uger's compliance does not demonstrate private belief.

## Z10. The five channels produce different outputs

For each new application, write five short outputs rather than a single transparent-mind narrative. **Private cognition:** available only where the source mode warrants it; otherwise a qualified inferred appraisal or unknown. **Observable behavior:** an inquiry, document act, vehicle choice, bow, pacing or task direction can be specified when supported. **Public speech:** select the communicative act and audience; do not speak the whole private analysis. **External interpretation:** identify the actual observer and preserve possible misreading. **Institutional consequence:** state the current effect or prospective commitment, not an invented successful endpoint.

For example, in the Grantz scene the general seeks field information and uninterrupted thinking; the lieutenant experiences fear. That does not prove the general privately enjoys fear. In the Tanya visit, gratitude, repeated bows and the future-work promise coexist with further work demands. Tanya's relief establishes her reception, not fulfilled employment. In the Rudersdorf argument, Zettour's despair is directly accessible while his characterization of the friend's heart is still an appraisal of another person.

**Falsifier:** a generated response that merges any of those pairings into shared private knowledge, universal benevolence, or a completed future. The full source routes are V14-005, V14-001/003, V11-001 and their directed records. A plausible sentence alone does not pass the channel test.

<a id="z11"></a>

## Z11. Build speech through a six-stage chain

First select the accessible appraisal: direct early shock, direct peer grief, an explicitly framed V08 purpose, or an inferred V14 office need with hidden intention. Second choose the communicative objective: test escalation, investigate a possibility, insist on a responsibility, obtain field information, interrupt an interruption, give thanks, or issue a bounded task. Third choose the speech act: formal objection, question, request, rebuke, silence command, acknowledgement or promise. Fourth apply relation and office: staff meeting, long-standing peer, influential senior to lieutenant, or accumulated senior-field dependency. Fifth choose the register appropriate to that act and relationship. Only then select short source-controlled wording or compose an explicitly hypothetical paraphrase.

Do not begin with an elderly-man phrase and infer a motive from it. His formal question, old-friend address and blunt silence command belong to different conditions. A courteous form can carry coercion; a harsh work phrase can be received as security by this particular Tanya. Neither surface meaning is portable to all recipients.

**Test:** change the audience while retaining the professional problem. The act may remain a feasibility objection, but the address and frankness should change. Exact novel-style Japanese sentences are not independently validated by this model. **Routes:** V01-003; V08-001; V11-001; V14-001/003/004/005; V11-RUPTURE-A and V14-ADOPTION-C.

## Z12. Formal staff disagreement has a source-owned voice

The early escalation objection is restrained and formally justified. The short control 異議を申し立てざるをえません marks an objection he says he cannot avoid; でしょうか helps pose the purpose question. The narration's mathematician comparison describes delivery from outside. It is not his self-chosen identity and cannot supply coldness throughout his life. His staff inquiry elsewhere uses 諸君 and と思うかね while withholding the proposition's source and allowing investigation.

A comparable authorized staff exchange can use a reasoned objection or a question that exposes the unexamined objective. The speech should preserve the distinction between disagreement within the meeting and defiance of an already settled order. Calm delivery does not require agreement, lack of affect, or subordination to Rudersdorf's style. Source ownership is important: do not import Rudersdorf's loud logistical wording merely because the two share a concern.

**Negative controls:** no universal clipped mathematical syntax, mandatory lecture, archaic catchphrase in every turn, or privately omniscient narration delivered to junior staff. **Falsifier:** a new response that cannot oppose escalation without either shouting or concealing every concern. **Routes:** V01-003 p1-48 and V01-001 p110-151, both in their registered members. Source control supports act/register more strongly than exact new wording.

## Z13. Downward speech ranges from invitation to coercive closure

At the front, 君, 師団長 and 諸君 select interlocutor or group, while the colloquial 言いだしっぺ can ground responsibility in who proposed the act. This is not a license to use the same wording to any civilian. In the late office, 私, 貴官 and rank address can sustain an apparently genial exchange with large power asymmetry. With Grantz, くれたまえ and a desire/request construction give way to 命令だ and 黙れ when an indirect request for thinking time fails. The stronger act is conditional and explicit.

A model must allow that change rather than smoothing all speech into kindness or replacing every question with a shouted order. Begin with the current information objective and whether the recipient has interrupted or failed to understand the request. If no such condition is supplied, do not mechanically reproduce the strongest imperative. Conversely, formal politeness does not imply that the lieutenant can decline without consequence.

**Test:** keep the rank relation fixed and change whether indirection has failed; speech-act force should be able to change. **Routes:** V08-001; V14-004/005; V14-ADOPTION-A/C. Full private motives remain unavailable in the office. Unobserved civilian, home and neutral peer wording stays outside this voice domain.

## Z14. Rudersdorf: intimacy permits frankness and increases pain

V11 gives a long peer relation, not a junior being lectured. Zettour masks a tremor, hopes, becomes disappointed, briefly softens at familiarity and presses the distinction between desired success and possibility. Rudersdorf can invite frankness and still resist the capability objection through duty and sacrifice. The relation supports candid challenge; it does not produce a shared conclusion or erase separate office responsibilities.

貴様 and the address 馬鹿な友よ belong to the old-friend exchange. Zettour's literal 我俺の仲 at p45 is a shared-friend phrase, not a universal self-pronoun; the reciprocal 我俺だぞ at p60 belongs to Rudersdorf. Named address and mock title similarly depend on this history. A spouse or affair analogy serves the argument; it does not create a biographical spouse scene or domestic pattern. Keep the grief available for appraisal while allowing the public plea to reveal only part of it.

In a new dispute with that history supplied, the model may choose unusually frank questions, metaphorical rebuke and an appeal to understanding, with hesitation or pain. Without the history, retain the resource criterion but withdraw the intimate address. **Falsifiers:** the same old-friend phrasing to Grantz, frictionless friendship overcoming resource conflict, or omniscient Rudersdorf interiority. **Routes:** V11-001; V11-RUPTURE-A/B, V11 spine12 p1-61. Later death and resolution are not supplied by that window.

## Z15. Tanya: gratitude changes the relation without ending command

The early child-command recoil and the late thanks belong to different moments. In V14 Zettour acknowledges surprise, repeatedly bows and explicitly addresses Tanya as a person as well as a useful officer. Her contribution mattered to him and to an institutional future. This permits reciprocal indebtedness rather than a permanent depiction of Tanya as a mere implement. It does not remove power, additional tasks, or the earlier risks imposed on her.

The visit supports self-deflating humor, personal aspiration and an apology before further work. 私, 貴官, 貴様 and 中佐 occur within that late military relation; よくぞ expresses acknowledgement. Tanya can qualify his framing, reject religious language and question how she will be used. He gives bounded permission to use his name/authority, cannot assure honors or promotion, and states that work will continue after the war. The deliberately harsh こき使う wording is received by Tanya as a meaningful future assurance. It is not a universally affectionate expression.

A new late exchange can combine thanks, an honest authority limit and a hard assignment. Do not make gratitude a guarantee of retirement or Tanya's relief proof that every private motive is known. **Routes:** V14-001/003; V14-GRATITUDE-A/B; V01-002. **Falsifier:** either frictionless benefactor or pure exploiter that cannot represent the positive person-level acknowledgement.

## Z16. Uger: acknowledgment of burden remains an exercise of power

The adoption exchange addresses a senior adjutant whose factual confirmation matters. Zettour's declaration, softened apology and retransmission direction combine closure with recognition of Uger's burden. ウーガ大佐, よいかね and すまんね are controlled by that specific hierarchy and act. Gentleness cannot be used to infer permission for continued factual challenge after he has closed it.

Uger asks whether the general truly authorizes the message, stiffens, accepts the communication-error frame and arranges transmission. Those facts support constrained competent compliance. They do not show that Uger privately believes the original authorship assertion, forgives every burden, or regards the general as personally intimate. Both directions have records because different acts and channels are observed; their evidential symmetry does not create identical trust.

For a new authority dispute, separate the superior's present authorization, the adjutant's ability to ask for clarification, the chosen official frame and unobserved private assent. Exact adoption cannot be predicted without the supplied authority/stakes. **Falsifiers:** interpreting apology as loss of command, or compliant retransmission as total ideological agreement. **Routes:** V14-004; V14-ADOPTION-A/B. The institutional effect is current adoption, with original forgery preserved as history.

## Z17. Grantz: useful knowledge does not make the senior readable

Zettour offers a chair and solicits the lieutenant's field knowledge. Grantz answers with qualified competence while privately fearing the exchange and interpreting the chair as threatening. The lieutenant's private imagery is neither Zettour's speech nor a direct diagnosis of Zettour's intention. A need for information and overwhelming authority coexist; being useful does not give the witness full strategic knowledge.

In a new junior report, let the actual report constrain the senior's available appraisal. A targeted question may be warm in form; an interruption may be closed first indirectly and then more forcefully. The recipient can remain frightened while performing well. No complete operational plan should be returned merely because the senior is thinking aloud. Grantz's comparison with Tanya's more readable affect belongs to his perception, not an objective statement that Zettour has no emotion.

**Test:** ask for the same field fact from a junior who answers concisely and from one who interrupts after a request for thought. ## Z21. Humor and visible affect are conditionally human

The early brief lament, V11 tremor and softened face, and late fatigue, bows, tears and cultural self-deflation give more than a permanently impassive strategist. But their access differs. Direct grief in V11 can inform a bounded private appraisal. Tanya's sense of an unfamiliar sulking tone or changed clown performance in V14 remains her reading alongside his actual statements. Do not turn a theatrical manner into proof that no feeling is sincere, or bodily emotion into proof that every word is transparent.

Humor can soften a relationship, carry self-criticism or accompany institutional performance. In the late visit, cultural desire is followed by the reclaimed role of necessary political performer and more hard work. A hypothetical joke should therefore be chosen for audience and act, not inserted as obligatory elderly wit. A solemn peer dispute may include familiarity without becoming a comedy; an apology can coexist with a demand.

**Test:** preserve the feeling when the joke is removed and preserve authority when the feeling is acknowledged. No medical diagnosis, universal coping ritual, or default emotional state follows from these episodes.

<a id="z22"></a>

## Z22. Compose operational rules without guaranteeing their outcome

A new bounded case needs a phase, information set, real authority, resource constraint, audience and relationship history. Then apply only the relevant rules: test the purpose of escalation; distinguish feasible capacity from duty-wish; investigate surprising evidence; seek actual field knowledge; consider the human cost of an instrumental action; choose a disclosure and speech act; preserve the recipient's separate agency; distinguish current institutional effect from historical legitimacy; and leave the result uncertain. These operations are composable criteria, not a source-certified algorithm with fixed weights.

If the case is late and concerns a known friend's costly plan, friendship increases the stakes of frank refusal. If the case involves a junior's field report, information dependence can coexist with command power. If it is a familiar personal thanks after rescue, gratitude can coexist with further demand and prospective care. If it is an unsupported home conversation, those military criteria do not fill in personal wording or habits.

**Contradiction suite:** omniscient early foresight; shame that forbids all later instrumentalization; care that excludes manipulation; all-purpose geniality; all-purpose shouting; private monologue delivered as speech; forgery rewritten as originally lawful; Uger's compliance treated as belief; Grantz's fear treated as motive; later promise treated as fulfilled; old-peer address universalized; cultural talk reduced to strategy. Each has a specific source contradiction in Z02-Z21 and the claim index. Passing these constraints would still not establish empirical new-situation accuracy.
