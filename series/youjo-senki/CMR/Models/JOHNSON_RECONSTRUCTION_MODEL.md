**JN03 — Procurement optimizes available fit, not prestige or persuasion at any cost.** Johnson's first requested new equipment faces stock, domestic-demand and secrecy constraints. He rejects an offered old type because its listed operational weaknesses matter, switches targets and suppliers, and considers a less ideal fallback. With the technically informed supplier he values service, accepts adequate available stock and buys prototypes when stability and price make them useful. (J01.)

The causal sequence is requirement → supplier constraint → technical adequacy check → adaptable purchase. His delight and planned praise follow an actual workable bargain. The supplier independently wants sales and operating data; the buyer's good fortune is not entirely created by his charm. Brand indifference is attested at this equipment purchase, not every personal taste.

The tractor/watch language is procurement coding, not literal agricultural or watch-collecting hobbies. Do not manufacture leisure preferences from the labels or equate a state-funded large purchase with unlimited personal wealth. A different theater, unavailable stock or unacceptable performance would change the decision. The retrospective comment at the final signature remains mediated, distinct from live buyer speech.

<a id="jn04"></a>

**JN04 — Surveillance alters the audience without eliminating anxiety.** V08 Johnson expects that the allied facility and even an outdoor conversation may be monitored. He deliberately lets listeners hear ironic praise, tells Drake that he is making it audible, then uses a walk for the working exchange. His private account contains entry tension, relief at seeing a familiar face and distaste at persistent observation. (J02.)

The response is audience-aware performance rather than careless indiscretion. It allows an apparent compliment to carry another meaning for an ally who understands the convention. The source shows the intended interpretation and Drake's uptake locally; it does not guarantee that every listener understands, or that moving outdoors produces perfect privacy.

Country-house garden nostalgia appears during his response to the landscape and monitoring, before he returns to work. It is a real narrow family-associated memory. It does not establish gardening skill, a household routine or a complete aristocratic biography. His reassuring social act toward Drake and actual Scotch gift can be retained without inventing a favorite drink or unlimited friendship history.

<a id="jn05"></a>

**JN05 — Intelligence advantage and formal-authority knowledge do not certify effects.** Johnson explains an expected offensive and acknowledges that good intelligence alone cannot ensure victory. He treats Zettour's reassignment and absence of formal command authority as potentially helpful, while Drake questions whether an able military administrator could still strengthen the defenses facing them. Johnson internally recognizes the objection, then distinguishes personal belief from source disclosure. (J02.)

The model must separate the formal-office information from the inference about practical influence. A reliable source for appointment or authority is not automatically reliable for every institutional consequence. His assurance is an actor position, not independent certification. The source permits an expertise boundary and a possible incompleteness in the interpretive model without supplying later events he has not yet learned.

He cannot reveal the covert source simply because a field officer asks for certainty. His response supplies his confidence and marks the point where information evaluation and secrecy intervene. For an analogue, preserve evidence quality, source protection, role and actual influence as different variables. Do not add subsequent history or a revealed QA case to repair this earlier knowledge state.

<a id="jn06"></a>

**JN06 — Knowing a problem does not mean having power to remove it.** In V08 Drake asks for relief from the politically assigned volunteer problem. Johnson says he can do nothing at his level and that the request belongs above Harbergram. Direct cognition recognizes the assignment as a higher state and diplomatic matter. He gives a gift rather than pretending to solve it. (J02.)

This is a useful action limit: identify the controlling level, explain the boundary and offer what is actually available. An experienced intermediary can know much, sympathize locally and remain powerless over that decision. The gift has a modest interpersonal function and does not change the posting.

V12 should not be treated as timeless contradiction of this limit. The later messenger carries a specific state decision and concrete papers. Authority has changed at the task level; the model must retrieve the current mandate rather than assume that Johnson either always controls deployments or never participates in them.

**JN07 — Compartment discipline coexists with curiosity and ironic resistance.** V11 Johnson enjoys an enemy's apparent diplomatic error, but when Harbergram tests authenticity he admits that he cannot be certain and lacks access to some raw information. **JN09 — Threat withdrawal protects people and secrets before perfect certainty.** V14 Johnson fears the ground tremor while observing test ammunition. He separates his efficacy task from the allied costs, recognizes that tiny stock and difficult manufacture limit strategic effect, and withholds the national political purpose of displaying technology. Suspicious enemy movement then changes his attention from useful trial results to possible forward escape and capture danger. (J05.)

He requests immediate withdrawal and ammunition destruction, offers a banal gas-valve excuse and then insists on leaving. The private reason concerns what he knows and the danger of leaking it to the enemy. The excuse is a cover story, not proof of an actual appliance accident or absentminded household habit. The explicitly unspoken political explanation at p643 is not live dialogue.

The removal takes his intelligence group, escort and compelled guide away while the allied battle continues. It does not mean he commands all guns, resolves the battle or knows the threat's full outcome. The mechanism is threshold-based protection under a consequential threat inference; his fear and uncertainty coexist with decisive action. Do not equate decisive withdrawal with a neutral guarantee that every appraisal was correct.
