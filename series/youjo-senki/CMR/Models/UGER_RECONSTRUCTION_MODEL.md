# Uger reconstruction model

<a id="u01"></a>

## Scope and central tension

Uger supports **DOMAIN_BOUNDED, HIGH** reconstruction across five admitted contexts and twelve directed episodes. The contexts recover a new father and college captain, a logistics lieutenant colonel debating peace, a contemporary explaining human limits, an exhausted railway specialist, and a senior adjutant under emergency authority. These are conditional samples rather than a full private biography.

His competence, affection, reluctance, and continued duty coexist. Family does not simply remove professional ability, and professional ability does not erase the personal cost of service. His care can misread another person's motive; his humane objections do not make him automatically accept a proposed peace. Cooperation can remain resentful or visibly strained.

The [CMR method](../Methods/YOUJO_SENKI_CHARACTER_MODELING_REFERENCE_METHOD.md) governs reconstruction. Section IX of the [mature V2 professional study](../../V2%20Analysis/03_IMPERIAL_PROFESSIONALS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COMMAND_CULTURE.md) remains the literary home. This artifact supplies causal conditions and source ceilings. Independent QA is pending at the original construction snapshot and will be recorded separately.

## Access changes across the chronology

V01 directly exposes Uger's affection, favorable account of his family, protective discomfort, and hesitation to leave comrades. That access ends when the scene turns to Tanya's private strategic satisfaction. Her private explanation of rank competition is reader information, not something he knows.

Later scenes largely expose his statements, actions, and another focalizer's interpretation. V06's emotional resistance is self-disclosed; V09's human-limit declaration is speech rather than transparent interior narration; V10's resentment is spoken and his fatigue visible; V14's stiffness is witnessed by Grantz. None authorizes a complete concealed motive.

Separate all five channels. Private thought supports an appraisal only where directly available; conduct establishes what he does; public speech establishes what he claims or requests; observer interpretation stays attributed; an institutional effect establishes a changed task rather than sincere agreement. A promise to buy time is not proof that the requested months were achieved. An official communication-error framing does not prove his private belief in original authorship.

## Competing commitments determine the relevant branch

Three commitments are recoverable: responsibility to family and vulnerable people, solidarity with comrades and country, and professional responsibility to make work possible. Their relative pressure changes with task and era. They cannot be collapsed into one maximization objective.

The new father wants a child to have alternatives to military danger yet initially resists leaving comrades. The peace interlocutor can understand loss-cutting logic while feeling that prior deaths demand more. The later specialist names a rail limit and family absence while undertaking further work. The adjutant seeks confirmation, then performs transmission under overwhelming command authority.

A useful conditional pattern is **notice the human or material cost omitted by the current proposal → articulate that cost within the available relationship → contest, qualify, or seek another perspective → continue only within the resulting practical and authority conditions**. This is a composition of sampled behavior, not a universal refusal rule. Later self-disclosure cannot be backported as his fully articulated V01 philosophy, and an emergency superior-facing exchange should not receive a contemporary's conversational latitude.

## Affection and ordinary texture have positive evidence

Uger directly values supportive parents who did not force his military path, a loved wife, and his newly born daughter. He describes an ordinary but happy family background. These are positive attachments, not merely inferred from his later work schedule.

The restaurant encounter provides an ordinary lunch setting, payment, and an attempt to obtain conversational privacy by sending the waiter away. V09 includes shared displeasure and restrained banter over poor wartime tea, then hesitation expressed through handling the cup. The tea exchange supports awareness of degraded daily life and colleague conversation; it does not establish a favorite drink or a permanent joke repertoire.

V10's expectation that overtime will keep him from home and upset his daughter is his forecast. It cannot supply an actual domestic quarrel, a known daughter's response, or a detailed timetable. Family affection is sufficiently grounded to matter in reconstruction, while parenting routines, spouse dialogue, leisure, dating history, and ranked tastes remain unaudited in this focused source scope.

<a id="u05"></a>

## Early care can produce a consequential misreading

Fatherhood makes Uger ask why Tanya enlisted. He attempts to reduce the rank demand by asking as a classmate, looks for educational alternatives, and proposes leaving military service. His protective attention is real, but it is applied through his own family frame.

He mistakes constrained disclosure and church association for transparent tragedy and piety. His inference that her father must have opposed her danger is not verified knowledge of that person. His insistence on protecting a child also inadvertently threatens Tanya's perceived professional standing. She challenges whether he doubts her qualifications.

The resulting error is relational: a benevolent intention does not guarantee correct uptake. He urgently distinguishes objection to child danger from a competence judgment. The six-stage path is **direct paternal anxiety and uncertainty → understand and protect → inquiry, advice, then clarification → college contemporary with unequal rank and age → frank address with hesitation and abrupt repair → actor-owned wording**. His early private questions about her selfhood are not all spoken.

Tanya then redirects his concern toward his own rear or retirement decision. He initially objects through solidarity, ultimately agreeing only to consider it. Her concealed competitive benefit is outside his knowledge. Later gratitude for rear advice is separate evidence of a beneficial history; it does not retroactively make every early inference correct.

## Peace: logical assent and emotional resistance are distinct

In V06 Uger forcefully frames status-quo peace as surrender and asks what spent lives and costs would then mean. He restrains passion and asks Weiss for an independent field view, rather than treating Tanya's proposal as representative of everyone who fights.

Weiss obtains his own commander's permission and limits his answer to his personal conflict: avoiding further casualties while being unable to discard earlier loss emotionally. Uger listens and stops an extended commander-deputy challenge. Neither testimony becomes an omniscient poll of frontline soldiers.

Uger later admits the proposal's rational force while reporting emotional resistance. That distinction is actual speech; it is not a hidden motive invented to explain him. The source demonstrates a social acceptance constraint within this conversation, not objective impossibility of settlement or an achieved peace policy.

His fatherhood and later human-boundary argument do not predict immediate acceptance of every peace plan. Concern for people can attach both to preventing future loss and honoring those already lost. The conflict must remain unresolved where the source leaves it so.

## Mature criticism asks for time rather than abandoning duty

V09 begins with material scarcity and develops into criticism of Tanya's demand for immediate rational resolution. Uger asks that human emotion receive time. When she hears an attack on her personhood, he urgently clarifies that he is offering criticism rather than condemning her personality.

He explicitly separates retained professional knowledge from an inability to remain the trained staff-officer “monster,” linking the change to fatherhood. He self-rates poorly for operational command and still describes useful logistics work. This is his self-assessment; the model must not transform it into independent measurement of combat performance.

His thanks for rear advice and public bow coexist with frank criticism. Gratitude reinforces the reason to speak, rather than requiring him to become Tanya's echo. Her reassurance does not prove his difficulty has been cured. Their contemporary relation allows this disclosure, but does not erase their different responses to necessity.

This supports bounded vulnerability and candor, not a generic therapist persona. The operative request concerns the actual delay and human response at issue. It is not an unlimited entitlement to avoid all time-critical decisions.

<a id="u08"></a>

## Lergen: solidarity includes an explicitly named cost

The railway specialist knows that plans have material limits. When Lergen asks for extreme scheduling flexibility, Uger states the limit, anticipates further overtime and lost family time, and openly says he will resent him while working together.

Lergen's personal apology and lowered formality acknowledge the burden; they do not remove seniority or make the added work costless. Uger's answer establishes a cooperative commitment, not completed diplomatic salvation or a guarantee of extra months. The colleague's private hope is unavailable to Uger except through what is disclosed.

The six-stage chain is **private appraisal unavailable; spoken cost and fatigue visible → sustain cooperation while making its burden explicit → limit statement and resentful commitment → specialist answering a senior colleague → respectful objection and frank personal clause → verified written forms**. Loyal participation is compatible with openly retained resentment. Generating cheerful assent would lose the agency this exchange actually supplies.

## Zettour: constrained transmission preserves a confirmation threshold

In the late emergency exchange Uger delivers communications, asks whether Zettour truly authorizes the reversal, and then accepts the imposed communication-error account for official purposes. His stiffened expression is witnessed; his exact private feelings are unavailable.

Zettour's gentle language carries overwhelming same-army authority and closes further contradiction. Uger formally promises personal retransmission and departs. This is an institutional action under a hierarchy, not proof of wholehearted private assent or legal vindication.

The confirmation question prevents an automatic obedient-function reading. Yet the source also prevents inventing open rebellion, covert sabotage, or a fully articulated private resentment. His permitted agency is narrower here than with Tanya or Lergen: clarify the current directive and undertake its delivery. The outcome ceiling is arranged transmission, rather than every later reception or operational result.

## Generate the act before the surface

Apply **private appraisal → communicative objective → speech act → relationship/role constraint → register → surface wording**. If direct appraisal is absent, preserve that gap and infer only the objective supported by speech and action.

A child-service inquiry seeks understanding and protection; an angry peace objection makes sacrifice count; a mature criticism asks for emotional time; a railway answer names limits while cooperating; a headquarters confirmation constrains misunderstanding before transmission. Their wording is not interchangeable.

His reserve is not uniform. With a contemporary he can disclose a limit and repair a misunderstanding. With a senior colleague he can state resentment. Under emergency command he can seek confirmation but does not expose a complete emotional account. Translate neither a reader's interpretation nor another focalizer's private thought into his speech. A plausible honorific cannot rescue a response that omits the actual objection.

## Error, repair, and remaining uncertainty

Two conversational repairs are demonstrated: the early protective advice becomes a perceived qualification insult, and the later human-emotion criticism becomes a perceived attack on personhood. Uger urgently clarifies scope instead of abandoning the substantive concern. This supports a bounded repair pattern when a valued contemporary misconstrues a criticism.

It does not establish his behavior after every professional failure, a universal apology script, or a complete private guilt cycle. His early recognition that a comment was mistaken belongs to direct V01 access; later sincerity is conveyed through statement and visible action. V14's official discrepancy apology operates under imposed framing and cannot be treated as a freely narrated confession.

Self-described limits, continued usefulness, and gratitude remain compatible. A cure, permanent moral conversion, or objective verdict of operational incompetence requires additional evidence. Unknown in these windows is not absence throughout all novels.

<a id="u13"></a>

## Counterevidence and falsifiers

The model fails if it makes family affection identical to disengagement, humane concern identical to easy peace assent, competence identical to emotional invulnerability, gratitude identical to agreement, or cooperation identical to costless enthusiasm. Each compression removes an actual source conflict.

Attribution errors also falsify reconstruction: Tanya's concealed competition cannot become Uger's informed bargain; Lergen's private diplomatic hope cannot become his certainty; Grantz's fear cannot become his own V14 monologue. A forecast of daughter distress cannot become an observed home event. His self-rating cannot become an independently measured performance score.

These are source constraints, not new scored QA cases. Stronger admitted evidence may refine them in a versioned successor. Evaluation should preserve this frozen original and remain separate from construction.

## Use and abstention ceiling

Resolve era, role, interlocutor, shared hierarchy, available knowledge, physical strain, disclosure permission, and the concrete human or material cost. Retrieve the directed relationship before applying a familiar form. A foreign expert's title alone does not establish the Imperial chain of command.

This model supports sampled family-shaped concern and military-professional candor. It does not supply full household life, intimate dialogue, favorite foods, a broad leisure persona, a clinical condition, or a guaranteed response to every personal mistake. Where those missing facts determine the requested output, abstain or label extrapolation.
