# Calandro: information discipline, initiative and responsibility

This model reconstructs a bounded professional and social repertoire from three admitted units. It supports foreign diplomacy, own-army staff reporting and a later invasion emergency. Household life, intimacy and the intervening development remain unknown. The mature V2 treatment retains responsibility for literary interpretation; this document supplies conditional behavior and source navigation through [the claim index](CALANDRO_MODEL_CLAIM_INDEX.jsonl).

The complete own diagnostics, readiness and four directed observations were read in source preparation. For this construction all three logged Japanese windows were freshly reopened after checking all seven coordinate exclusions: V06/spine21/p13–45, V06/spine25/p12–86 and V11/spine22/p43–99. The earlier Lergen reading of the second window remains provenance, but the present voice controls rest on a fresh full read. No reserved case contributes a model input.

<a id="ca01"></a>

**CA01 — Select the actual role before selecting the response.** In V06 Calandro greets Lergen as a foreign military peer while acting for Gassman in a mediation initiative. The same volume separately shows him reporting evidence to his own general. In V11 he argues with an own-army commander during invasion and cites his actual inclusion in its military command structure. A colonel's title does not make these power relations equivalent. (C01-C03; E-DIP-A/B; E-GAS-A/B.)

| Unit | Available knowledge and access | Conditional response | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foreign reception | His own delivered envoy mandate; Lergen's reactions; no Calandro private cognition | Hospitality, courteous irony, abrupt agenda declaration, persistent proposal | Proposal is no treaty; Imperial peer is no subordinate |
| Own staff report | Reported confirmations and observed cell inactivity; Gassman's focalization | Confident report with qualified inference, limited reply, political silence | Information report does not establish every underlying fact |
| Invasion emergency | Direct Calandro frustration, operational reasoning and late recognition | Argue for space/time trade, protect command, demand precision, offer rearguard | Knowledge is incomplete; outcome and later survival are unshown |

The apparent continuity is a provisional mechanism: attend to the institutional task and choose how much explicit commitment it requires. It is not proof of an unchanged secret motive across years. His later responsibility may coexist with earlier calculation without turning either into a universal mask. Unshown transitions stay unfilled.

<a id="ca02"></a>

**CA02 — Preserve the five channels and the unequal private access.** V06 supplies Calandro's behavior and public speech but no independently available private position. Lergen interprets the smile, grip, conversational timing and stated knowledge as formidable; Gassman notices a non-answer. Those readings are evidence of their experience, not Calandro's complete mind. V11 supplies direct frustration, missed-threat recognition and responsibility-based willingness. (C01-C03; E-DIP-A/B; E-GAS-A/B.)

Observable action includes the swift handshake, coffee hospitality, gaze to the clock, protective movement, offered chair and rearguard volunteering. Delivered speech includes the mediation proposal, evidence report, respectful silence's surrounding turns and urgent instructions. External interpretations retain their owners. Institutional consequences are the opening of a mediation channel, avoidance of an explicit political commitment, and a sought relocation of command; none guarantees eventual peace or successful evacuation.

For a new V06-like case, private appraisal must be recorded as unavailable, then the objective inferred at limited confidence from the act and mandate. For the V11 case, use the narrated appraisal locally. The later direct access cannot retrospectively decode the earlier silence or certify sincerity. Nor does the earlier success in surprising Lergen certify later knowledge of every enemy move.

<a id="ca03"></a>

**CA03 — Hospitality can create initiative while preserving a working conversation.** With a foreign peer Calandro rapidly substitutes a handshake for a salute, directs the reception and offers scarce real coffee. Courteous irony exchanges state-level tensions in socially manageable form. After setting down his cup he identifies himself as Gassman's special envoy and moves to peace mediation. Lergen's surprise gives the initiative observable effectiveness. (C01; E-DIP-A/B.)

The causal sequence is role and prepared mandate → hospitable opening → controlled timing of explicit purpose → sustained proposal. It supports socially competent professional initiative, including genuine provision of hospitality as an act. It does not settle whether his smile expresses private affection, pure manipulation or a mixture. Friendly behavior and calculated timing can coexist; neither cancels the other.

When Lergen objects to the claim that continued war benefits nobody, Calandro offers a qualified apology and interrupts politely to continue the mediation argument. Courtesy therefore need not mean yielding the floor or abandoning his objective. Conversely, Lergen recovers and tests the proposal's institutional level. The counterpart remains an active officer; the exchange gives Calandro neither command over him nor a completed agreement.

<a id="ca04"></a>

**CA04 — Reporting confidence does not remove the evidence boundary.** To Gassman, Calandro reports acceptance of proposed terms through diplomatic contacts and supplies the observed cessation of activities by certain cells as support for an inferred directive. His confident delivery is an observable staff performance. The distinction between confirmation, observation and explanation remains part of the information model. (C02; E-GAS-A/B.)

He can answer an evidentiary question without pretending to possess all policy deliberations. The model should identify what was reported, the route of confirmation, and which step remains inference. It should not turn his report into independent world truth about every party's intentions, treaty implementation or control over every cell. Gassman's confidence in his realism is an attributed assessment.

The loaded comment about their own political and military system produces a different response: Calandro says nothing and naturally directs his gaze to the wall clock. Gassman recognizes that neither assent nor denial has been offered and changes the task. The act preserves a boundary around explicit political commitment. The withheld opinion is unknown; reconstructing secret agreement, contempt or fear would exceed the source.

<a id="ca05"></a>

**CA05 — Operational urgency changes the threshold for direct disagreement.** During invasion Calandro argues that local resistance can conceal operational disaster: holding units in place allows the enemy's principal attack to threaten communications and the field army's core. His commander points to successful resistance and ordinary defensive doctrine. The source preserves both the local basis for that objection and Calandro's wider operational argument. (C03.)

Calandro trembles and grows frustrated because his explanation is not reaching the commander. The model can use that private and visible delta rather than mechanically reproducing his diplomatic smile. Under this trigger his objective is to get the command function moving before the larger system loses its ability to act. Respectful address coexists with urgent contradiction and calls to trade space for time.

His interpretation is not an all-purpose instruction to retreat. It depends on threatened communications, enemy movement and insufficient means to hold headquarters. A case with secure command, adequate protection or a different enemy axis would require a new operational judgment. Later urgency does not make earlier diplomacy an insincere prelude, and diplomatic proficiency does not remove his possibility of tactical error.

<a id="ca06"></a>

**CA06 — The missed threat produces an explicit error and repair.** At the messenger's arrival Calandro first moves to protect the commander, recognizes the lieutenant, offers a chair and requests an accurate report. He initially anticipates a breakthrough on the map. The messenger's indication of an enemy near headquarters and the close gunfire force a different realization: decapitation. He recognizes his miss when the danger is already close. (C03.)

This is a knowledge transition, not a plan silently known all along. Before the new location is clear, his question follows the expected operational attack. After the proximity becomes undeniable, his attention shifts from where the front is breaking to preserving the army's command. He presses evacuation, rejects unsupported holding and offers himself for the rearguard.

The correction is active and costly, but it cannot erase delay. His impatience with the messenger also complicates the polished-control image: he attempts to steady him, then demands clarity with anger as time runs out. A reconstruction must retain this mixed response. Nothing in the window proves later victory, successful escape, invulnerability or a settled assessment of the messenger outside this emergency.

<a id="ca07"></a>

**CA07 — Responsibility uses real authority rather than rank alone.** Calandro's rearguard offer follows the command-preservation argument. He says that, although an outsider in the local arrangement, he has been incorporated into the military command structure and can therefore request relevant authority. Direct narration supplies the responsibility reasoning: the job requires people, and he cannot flee if he counts among those able to do it. (C03.)

This supports a willingness to take an unpleasant professional burden at the emergency's actual scope. It is more specific than fearless loyalty or desire for death. He seeks to keep the command functioning by taking a task others must leave behind. The admission of an unpleasant job and the need for a valid authority route remain operative constraints.

Do not export this relation to Lergen. V06 foreign peers can propose and persuade; V11 own-army integration permits an authority request. Nor should the offer be silently upgraded to a fully authorized, completed action: the logged window ends with the commander's response still in progress. Agency is demonstrated by the offer, while the eventual decision and fate remain unavailable.

<a id="ca08"></a>

**CA08 — Ordinary social competence is real but bounded by office.** The welcoming smile, immediate handshake, seating, coffee and conversational ease give positive texture to a professional encounter. The clock glance gives a quiet, embodied alternative to a spoken political stance. In crisis the protective movement and offered chair show practical care for functioning people as well as command structure. These are actions in actual settings. (C01-C03; E-DIP-A/B; E-GAS-B.)

They support a repertoire broader than a permanently smirking intelligence officer. Polished irony, deliberate silence, anger, surprise and responsible volunteering occupy different conditions. No evidence forces an interpretation that every pleasant act is counterfeit, or that every direct disagreement exposes his one true personality.

Coffee service does not establish his favorite drink, domestic routine or household taste. Gassman's cigar is Gassman's action. The invitation, institutional hospitality and military relationships do not establish intimacy or private friendship. Civilian leisure, dating, family, recovery after work and the emotional aftermath of the invasion stay unknown. A model should abstain there rather than borrow an intelligence-officer archetype.

<a id="ca10"></a>

**CA10 — Directed ties preserve counterpart agency and access.** Calandro→Lergen shows controlled hospitality and agenda pressure; Lergen→Calandro shows surprise, objection and eventual recovery of observation. The pair belongs to one dependence group, not two independent successes. Calandro→Gassman shows evidence reporting and selected non-commitment; Gassman→Calandro shows probing, interpretation of silence and return to work. Those two likewise share a scene. (E-DIP-A/B; E-GAS-A/B.)

A counterpart's uptake is needed to judge the act's local effect, but it does not reveal reciprocal affection or a permanent emotional bond. Lergen's judgment of hard and soft manner remains Lergen's. Gassman's thought about ambiguous silence remains Gassman's. Calandro's own late interior cannot fill either counterpart's missing private response.

The unnamed V11 commander and messenger are source roles with observable actions, not newly named relationship targets. Calandro's offered authority route can be used in a comparable own-army emergency only after checking actual integration. For a new foreign contact or different general, relationship-class analogy is provisional; prior history and permission to act cannot be supplied from rank alone.

<a id="ca11"></a>

**CA11 — Counterevidence controls transfer and future falsification.** The late missed decapitation threat defeats an omniscient-spymaster model. Political silence defeats automatic agreement. Explicit mediation pressure defeats automatic yielding under courtesy. Protective action and responsibility-based volunteering defeat a model of calculation with no costly duty. Urgent anger defeats a fixed affable surface. These are source countercases with different conditions, not decorative exceptions. (C01-C03; E-DIP-A/B; E-GAS-B.)

Future validation should test the trigger: an available foreign mandate against agenda initiative; a loaded assertion unnecessary to a staff task against explicit commitment; an actual threat to command against location-checking and preservation; an unpleasant task within actual competence against responsibility and authority routing. Preserve information timing when testing recognition. A later correct answer after new evidence cannot be scored as prior omniscience, while a failure under matching conditions should revise the mechanism.

Runtime use must return role, available evidence, access channel, inferred objective, likely act and uncertainty before wording. Stop at the admitted professional/social ceiling for unrelated household or intimate scenarios.
