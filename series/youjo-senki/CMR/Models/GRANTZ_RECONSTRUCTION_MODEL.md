# Grantz reconstruction model

<a id="g01"></a>

## Scope: development without a fabricated recovery endpoint

Grantz supports high-confidence **DOMAIN_BOUNDED** reconstruction with explicit developmental states. Six diagnostics cover five logged contexts: new-officer travel, Arene, a quiet watch, desert adaptation, and late headquarters reporting. Six directed episodes add the Weiss rescue relation and preserve two-way Tanya and Zettour interactions. These records share dependencies and are not a set of independent proofs of every tendency.

The recoverable person has early heroic ambition, fear, ordinary needs, moral perception, limits on attention, adaptive competence, and later institutional anxiety. He continues functioning without the source demonstrating that the cost of earlier events disappears. “Inexperienced forever,” “fully healed veteran,” and “willingly brutal professional” are all inadequate compressions.

The [CMR method](../Methods/YOUJO_SENKI_CHARACTER_MODELING_REFERENCE_METHOD.md) controls this model. The [mature V2 professional-network study](../../V2%20Analysis/03_IMPERIAL_PROFESSIONALS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COMMAND_CULTURE.md), section VII, remains the literary home for moral injury and veteran manufacture. The reconstruction formalizes conditions, information, action, and speech at the admitted ceiling. Independent QA is pending at the original construction freeze and belongs to separate versioned records.

## Five channels and segmented access

Direct Grantz private access exists in the novice travel scene, Arene distress, quiet watch, the bounded desert passage, and late headquarters focalization. It can show fear, causal recognition, self-justification, relief, admiration, or attention limits. Access belongs to the actual segment: the desert Grantz scene ends before separate de Lugo headquarters thought, and Arene later changes to Tanya's private reflection.

At rescue, Grantz's warning and unfinished objection are directly attributed, while Weiss supplies the interpretation of his silence and look. That reading cannot become a transparent Grantz monologue. Tanya's private motives and Zettour's complete strategic reasoning are likewise unavailable to him merely because the reader has other access.

Keep private cognition, action, public speech, external interpretation, and consequence separate. His competent report is compatible with private fear. His compliance after bodily force is compatible with resentment. His quiet coffee respite is compatible with coming work and unresolved injury. A command's successful enforcement supplies an institutional effect, rather than voluntary moral agreement. A source anchor may be narration rather than an utterance; source mode and nearby quotation marks do not identify the speaker by themselves.

## State, rank, and knowledge before personality shorthand

Early V02 is a newly commissioned second lieutenant whose hoped-for heroism meets the damaged front. Arene then presents a specific moral crisis. A subsequent quiet night permits bodily comfort and reflection. The V03 desert scene still identifies a second lieutenant learning to translate equipment and repeated preparation into action; later rescue and headquarters episodes identify a lieutenant with greater experience.

These states do not simply replace one another. Later reporting competence cannot be backported into the travel newcomer, nor does it erase the Arene protest. Fear before overwhelming senior political power is not a reversal to total battlefield incompetence. The person is different in the relevant task and relationship even when the emotion has the same English label.

Resolve what he has already experienced and what the source makes known. Do not give the novice later operational habits or the courier Zettour's full plan. The admitted chronology provides meaningful samples rather than a continuous inner history. A permanent diagnosis, complete healing, and every missing promotion date remain outside the model.

## Attention and appraisal: immediate reality can break a reassuring frame

Early aspiration does not make him indifferent to evidence. The train approach makes damage and danger concrete; rumors and witnessed school conduct shape his apprehension about Tanya. At Arene, bodies and sensory horror defeat an account that he is merely rescuing comrades or fighting a limited military component. In the desert, light and sand make an unpopular piece of equipment intelligible. At headquarters, known transported material conflicts with a powerful officer's official framing.

The compositional tendency is **attend to the immediate condition → test the available reassuring or doctrinal account against what follows from action → recognize a limit or consequence → seek a workable response within authority**. This is an analytical pattern, not a universal decision algorithm.

The competition changes with state. Ambition conflicts with fear and need for rest; direct tactical function conflicts with known civilian consequence; practical adaptation conflicts with self-recognized attention limits; accurate field reporting conflicts with wishing to avoid dangerous notice. He is not merely an optimizing instrument or a fixed conscience slogan. The concrete discrepancy must be retained before assigning a general trait.

<a id="g05"></a>

## Ordinary life: state-specific comfort remains positive evidence

The newcomer eats tolerable rations quickly and passes travel through talk and naps, hoping for early sleep while worried about collective punishment. These are actual deployment needs and actions. They establish neither a favorite meal nor enduring avoidance of duty.

On the quiet watch he notices the warmth of a lined coat, the relief of sitting, and the quality of unusually available real coffee. Calm gives ordinary attention room to return. He remains on duty and knows an advance is imminent; his hope that the coming operation could end war is tentative.

Recover these particulars without converting them into a civilian routine. Appreciating good coffee in scarcity is not a favorite-drink ranking. Warmth and rest are not proof of permanent laziness. A temporary respite is not a completed psychological recovery. The model should preserve a person who can experience relief and quality amid work, rather than making every post-Arene moment continuous horror or hiding all ordinary texture behind “unknown.”

## Arene: causal knowledge defeats clean-hands rationalization

Grantz's horror is sensory and causal. The source provides nausea and recognition of human bodies, while his earlier self-exculpation tries to keep his role limited to rescue or mage combat. He then understands that removing the defending shield exposes people behind it to artillery. The indirectness of his role does not prevent him from knowing the consequence.

This does not make him a fully articulated moral philosopher or establish a permanent pacifist program. It supplies this young officer's conflict between a military role and the human result he can foresee. He remains able to comprehend the tactical sequence even when emotionally overwhelmed.

Preserve the knowledge boundary. He can understand the immediate consequence without understanding every command motive, legal theory, or civilian fate. Tanya's separate private reason for rescue does not become his. Nor should the institutional classification of “enemy” replace his recognition that people are present. A reconstruction that treats the civilian consequence as information he has failed to notice would miss the trigger for his actual protest.

## Protest: moral urgency can break through rank fear

Grantz addresses the battalion commander respectfully and asks reconsideration. Repetition and an unfinished conditional mark disruption; he does not deliver a polished denunciation. He knows the danger of protesting an order, is startled by his own intervention, and briefly continues despite it.

The six-stage chain is **explicit distress, consequence knowledge, and institutional fear → seek reconsideration to prevent that consequence → repeated appeal and interrupted objection → second lieutenant facing commander under extreme military authority → rank retained but sentence completion disrupted → source-owned short forms**. His private anger is not fully verbalized.

The intervention threshold is specific: the known killing consequence becomes urgent enough to produce protest despite fear. This does not establish an all-context capacity to refuse orders, nor does hesitation show that concern is unreal. The public speech's incompleteness matters. Generating a fluent moral speech from his private questioning would change both the act and the extent of disclosure. The source proves this objection occurred, not that every future conflict will reproduce it.

<a id="g08"></a>

## Tanya → Grantz: compulsion is not persuasion

Tanya answers through enemy-threat and order arguments, applies a sweep, and directs him to take his weapon. Grantz understands the requirement to comply while retaining private anger and conflict. The recorded effect is restored ordered participation after a suppressed objection.

Keep the reciprocal relation distinct. The commander controls task and discipline; the junior offers an alternative and is physically compelled. Command success does not certify moral legitimacy or prove that he has accepted a complete ethical explanation. His reading of her calm as indifference is also a reading, rather than full access to her contemporaneous mind.

Adjacent Tanya aftermath supplies a separate perspective to the reader; it cannot resolve Grantz's knowledge or erase what occurred to him. Later competence should never retrospectively turn this event into freely chosen enthusiastic brutality. Equally, the event alone cannot supply a universal private account of their later relationship. The actual hierarchy and coercion must remain visible wherever this episode is used as an analogue.

## Desert adaptation: enforce protection while recognizing a limit

V03 shows practiced preparation and environmental understanding. Grantz recognizes why large, unpopular goggles are needed against light and sand and insists his subordinates use them. He translates a commander's equipment decision into local safety discipline despite complaints.

The response is **recognize a concrete hazard and protective function → prevent avoidable injury → direct warning and equipment check → junior officer addressing subordinates → brief imperative → source-owned control**. It supplies subordinate-facing directness quite different from Arene's upward appeal.

At the same time he admires Tanya's ability to combine navigation and command and privately recognizes that his own attention could not manage that combination. Adaptability and a self-recognized limit coexist. Do not turn protective-equipment competence into universal mastery, a clothing preference, or proof of recovery. The claim stops with Grantz's scene; de Lugo's subsequent headquarters thought and an unattributed radio reply are not his cognition or voice.

<a id="g10"></a>

## Grantz ↔ Weiss: report danger within an incomplete objection

At rescue, Grantz warns Weiss of enemy approach and asks about following footprints with the battalion's limited means. He cannot finish the objection. The speech directly supplies a qualified field concern, while his precise private motive is unavailable in this directed record.

Weiss gives a quiet shoulder tap and retains his own feasibility judgment. The support belongs to Weiss, and his reading of Grantz's silence stays attributed. The junior's silence is neither free assent nor proof of cowardice or healed moral injury. He subsequently responds to the updated task.

The operational pattern is useful but bounded: **when field conditions worsen, he can report the concrete threat and raise a role-aware limit, even if full opposition is not spoken**. The relation has a senior and junior role inside a shared mission. It does not establish an intimate confidant relationship, a universal consolation response, or every later dialogue between them. New intercepted information changes what is workable; he must not know it before the source supplies it.

## Headquarters fear has an institutional object

Late Grantz is an experienced courier who knows what he carried. He watches Zettour adopt and reframe authority, and privately experiences the power involved as frightening despite genial manners. He would prefer not to receive the general's attention and finds the offered chair alarming. This is specific fear of senior institutional power, not evidence of general battlefield incapacity.

His comparison with Tanya's more readable affect is his own appraisal. It does not give him Zettour's complete motive or prove that apparent warmth is always deceptive. It identifies how this encounter changes his attention and disclosure.

A useful branch is **known material and observed authority maneuver → recognize dangerous uncertainty about the senior's intent → suppress exposed distress while fulfilling the courier/officer task → carefully qualified report → silence when explicitly ordered**. The private slang and imagery remain internal. His attempts at self-control and distorted sense of time are source observations in this state, not a clinical diagnosis or complete account of all anxiety.

## Competent reporting does not require fearless trust

Despite fear, Grantz compares staff maps with his field information, reports airborne experience, and offers qualified capability estimates. He can answer accurately about his domain without understanding the senior's emerging plan. Expertise and unequal authority coexist.

Zettour solicits information, offers apparent ease, and then imposes explicit silence when Grantz fails to understand the desired pause. The senior's spoken thought fragments do not reveal the entire strategic account. Grantz's questions and time guesses remain uncertain; he cannot solve the plan simply because the reader sees later events.

The six-stage path is **direct fear and desire to avoid notice → fulfill the task without attracting danger → acknowledgment, qualified report, clarification, then silence → overwhelming superior relationship → formal ranked language → source-owned forms**. Silence is an actual institutional constraint rather than a missing line to invent. Reliable reporting is not evidence that earlier moral injury has healed, that fear is absent, or that he can speak equally freely to every authority.

## Speech is downstream of state and communicative purpose

Use **private appraisal → communicative objective → speech act → relationship/role constraint → register → surface wording**. Grantz's source often exposes more private material than he can say. Early fear and desire for rest do not create a verified travel-conversation voice. Moral distress yields a broken upward appeal; field hazard yields a direct safety imperative; late institutional fear yields a precise report and withheld distress.

Where the private channel is absent, such as the rescue-directed record, preserve absence of access. An objective can be inferred from warning and inquiry without creating a complete hidden emotion. Select the act and disclosure level before applying a familiar Japanese form.

This protects both decision and surface fidelity. A respectful sentence can still falsely agree with an objection's object; a plausible internal curse can still be inappropriate public speech. A late qualified report must not sound like an early panicked novice, and a novice must not receive the later polished capability account. Generated wording never becomes evidence or fills an unassigned source turn.

## Function, learning, and repair have different endpoints

The corpus supports increasing usability: practiced preparation, equipment judgment, qualified reporting, and continued participation. It also supports ordinary relief after acute horror. These are meaningful developments, but they are not a demonstrated psychiatric recovery endpoint.

At Arene the immediate repair is institutional and coercive: the order becomes executable again. That is not proof of a freely resolved conscience. The desert safety action prevents a concrete hazard without proving all prior distress gone. Quiet night-watch attention shows restored margin, not permanent absence of suffering. Headquarters fear has a different object from frontline horror and should not be explained solely by the earlier event.

General apology, self-blame, help-seeking, later moral reconciliation, and long-term failure responses remain insufficiently sampled. Use the actual learning and task endpoints while abstaining from a total therapeutic story. Unknown here does not mean absent everywhere in the novels. Continued competence and emotional cost can remain simultaneously represented without forcing a verdict that one must invalidate the other.

<a id="g16"></a>

## Falsifiers and reconstruction hazards

The source falsifies several tempting shortcuts: later competence does not erase early protest; early fear does not make him permanently incapable; ordered participation after force is not enthusiastic assent; a quiet coffee break is not full healing; a tactical safety judgment is not universal mastery; late formal accuracy is not fearless political trust.

Attribution failures are equally concrete. Do not assign Tanya's radio orders or later inner reflection to him, de Lugo's headquarters cognition to his desert scene, Weiss's shoulder tap or inferred feelings to Grantz's own private channel, or Uger's earlier headquarters wording to his report. A private slang expression is not public canon, and the unassigned newcomer rush line remains unassigned.

These are qualitative source constraints and falsification targets, rather than a new scored adversarial suite. Stronger source evidence could narrow or revise a conditional rule. Independent evaluation must preserve the frozen original and record changed claims in a successor if needed; it must not fit expected continuations back into the evidence.

## Scenario packet and abstention

Resolve era, rank/task, prior experiences, present audience, institutional pressure, immediate knowledge, physical state, and stake level. Retrieve relationship and state before lexical overlap. A battalion commander suppressing an objection, a deputy listening to a field warning, and a general soliciting a courier's report are materially different authority conditions.

The model is strongest for the sampled military-developmental states. Detailed civilian/intimate adulthood, permanent taste and leisure, a broad polished conversational repertoire, assured recovery, and the response to every kind of personal error remain outside the ceiling. No-shared-hierarchy encounters do not acquire command obedience merely from a foreign professional's title. Where missing evidence determines the requested output, use bounded recognition or label extrapolation.
