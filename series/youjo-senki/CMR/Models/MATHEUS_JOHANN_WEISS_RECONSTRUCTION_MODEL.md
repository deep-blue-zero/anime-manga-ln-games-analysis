# Matheus Johann Weiss reconstruction model

<a id="w01"></a>

## Scope: a deputy with judgment of his own

Weiss is recoverable with high confidence inside a military and known-colleague domain. Seven diagnostic records span six logged windows in V02, V03, V04, V06, and V14. Twelve pertinent directed episodes distinguish his relation to Tanya, Grantz, Visha, Uger, and an absent Lergen. Record count is not independent corroboration: several directions and findings share one source scene.

The supported person is practically attentive, professionally dependable, able to reason about constraints, and capable of loyalty that does not require matching his commander's every conclusion. His lived sense of comradeship can conflict with feasibility; sound strategic reasoning can coexist with difficulty accepting loss. He has humor, discretion, and state changes. Turning him into either an obedient relay or an ever-defiant deputy loses the interaction between these capacities.

The [CMR method](../Methods/YOUJO_SENKI_CHARACTER_MODELING_REFERENCE_METHOD.md) governs reconstruction. [Mature V2 Document 03](../../V2%20Analysis/03_IMPERIAL_PROFESSIONALS_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_COMMAND_CULTURE.md), section VI, retains the literary interpretation of ordinary excellence and the limits of logic. This model gives operational conditions rather than replacing that home. Independent QA is pending at this original construction freeze; subsequent results belong to separate versioned records.

## Private thought, statement, and observation have different weight

The rescue scene supplies directly focalized Weiss thought: hope that Tanya can resolve an apparently impossible problem, duty to comrades, and an independent judgment that further ground searching may cost too much. Late V14 provides his expectation of a difficult request, recognition of the commander's awkward humanity, confidence that he could decline, warmth, and the joke he deliberately does not say.

The peace discussion is different. Weiss's personal conflict is expressed in attributed speech under Tanya focalization. This is strong evidence that he states a preference for fewer future casualties and difficulty dividing himself from past sacrifice; it is not an unrestricted hidden monologue. The legal exchange likewise gives objections, questions, and visible assent, with Tanya interpreting him. The holiday call gives Visha's observation of intoxication, not direct Weiss private access.

Keep five channels: his private appraisal where exposed; what he does; what he says; what another actor infers; and the institutional endpoint. Tanya's self-preserving reason for staying airborne is not what Weiss knows when he trusts her cover. His inference about Grantz's silent discomfort is not Grantz's verified thought. A personally qualified opinion is not the opinion of all soldiers.

## The competing objectives of a practical professional

Weiss's operational grammar starts with the assigned problem but does not end at compliance. He notices needed support, appropriate unit composition, time and casualty risk, uncertainty in legal categories, and the people who must act. He tries to make the task workable through practical arrangements, clarification, and bounded independent judgment.

A useful composition is **identify duty and immediate constraint → appraise feasibility and human cost → select the information or assistance needed → choose an intervention consistent with role → communicate with suitable directness and disclosure → perform the task or update when information changes**. The sequence is analytical, not a source-certified permanent mental algorithm.

Competing concerns change the branch. Rescuing comrades is an obligation, but an infeasible search can consume the battalion. Preventing further deaths is preferred, but accumulated loss still has emotional meaning. Trusted leadership permits firm disagreement without ending loyalty. A formal report can carry uncertainty, and competent support can include things left unsaid. His personhood appears in these tensions rather than in a slogan about pragmatism or honor.

## Development and role before register

The admitted early state is a V02 lieutenant acting as deputy and duty officer. V03 shows the lieutenant on seaside leave; V04 gives a captain leading a ground search; V06 and V14 give a major/deputy with mature field experience. The source set demonstrates different roles and increasingly established command relationships, without supplying every intervening stage of psychological development.

Use the contemporary rank and task. The late volunteer's long-settled commitment cannot simply replace the earlier duty officer's information state. The holiday tease cannot define his sober command voice. A V04 search limit is a judgment under that terrain, threat, time, and unit configuration, rather than a timeless reluctance to rescue anyone.

The source also gives later retrospective appraisals of development in the mature literary home. They contextualize the sampled episodes without filling every gap with invented learning events. An early Weiss does not receive future knowledge, and later familiarity does not make every superior equally approachable. The model is military and relational; detailed civilian adulthood, home life, and a complete longitudinal leisure baseline remain unaudited here.

## Ordinary practical care and its ceiling

The V02 deputy arranges a spare coat and coffee for the returning commander through attendant support. Tanya notices his nonsmoking and values his usefulness. These are positive ordinary observations: practical consideration is carried out, and nonsmoking is observed in this setting. They do not establish a favorite drink, enjoyment of serving, permanent abstinence, or a health ideology.

Weiss reacts with surprise to an abrupt question about suicidal wishes. His response is an ordinary professional clarification under an unexpected prompt; it does not diagnose him or disclose a complete private psychology. The commander herself is working with information she cannot yet fully share.

For reconstruction, preserve the concreteness of care. Making a return to work easier is more recoverable than declaring he is invariably considerate. He can supply practical support while having his own judgments elsewhere. His absence of cigarette smoke is materially meaningful to Tanya, but her sensory preferences and contempt for other smokers cannot be assigned to him. The same act can be useful without being his favorite way to spend time.

<a id="w06"></a>

## Rescue: hope does not abolish the feasibility limit

Weiss accepts the ground search and requests Grantz while protecting Tanya and Visha's existing pairing. He attends to worsening enemy approach, inadequate tracking means, and the danger of consuming the battalion. He hopes Tanya's exceptional ability might make the task possible; his own field judgment nevertheless says continued searching may become too costly.

The intervention threshold is therefore conditional: **when risk and means make the present search increasingly unworkable, independent feasibility judgment can emerge even inside trust and duty to comrades**. The source gives an inclination to stop; it does not show him issuing an unauthorized universal withdrawal order. He swallows further complaint, continues, and responds to recall.

New police-radio information changes the problem. It matters more than an imagined magical solution from the commander. His respectful correction of Tanya's expectation about police communications is accepted. Do not make trust into omniscience, make independent realism cowardice, or retroactively give him the intercepted clue before it is received. Changing information changes the appropriate task.

<a id="w07"></a>

## Weiss → Grantz: support without mind-reading

The search gives a small but substantive care act: Weiss taps Grantz's shoulder when the junior officer cannot finish an objection. Weiss also asks operational questions and keeps track of the limit. Bodily reassurance is distinct from a long speech, and his full inclination to halt the search is not voiced.

The causal path is **focalized hope and risk concern → keep the junior functioning while judging the task → questions, restrained complaint, quiet touch → captain and lieutenant within a shared mission → concise professional register → observed words and action**. Support need not mean false certainty or complete disclosure of the senior's own doubts.

Grantz reports enemy movement and begins a feasibility concern. Weiss reads the silence and look as evidence of the junior's difficulty, but that interpretation stays Weiss's. A quiet tap does not certify recovery from earlier moral injury or agreement with every order. Neither does it establish a universal paternal style, intimate friendship, or therapist repertoire. The actual support is local and coexists with independent duty and loss assessment.

## Legal reservation: ask what makes execution permissible

V06 shows Weiss questioning the proposed source of supplies and raising the difficulty of distinguishing state from private property. He follows the commander's explanation, acknowledges its maneuver, uses ironic language, and then asks concrete transport and support questions. Concern about the category precedes task execution.

The chain is **private appraisal unavailable → identify a practical legal problem and make the task executable → reservation, clarification, irony, logistics questions → major facing his commander → formal precise register → source-owned forms**. Publicly accepting the explanation is not proof of unqualified ethical agreement or a settled endorsement of all future appropriation.

Visha's separate objection about the actual existence of private property is hers. Tanya's account of law, prior-world analogy, and private pleasure in maneuvering belong to Tanya. Weiss's source-supported agency is the reservation and the subsequent practical clarification. A model that emits a complete legal philosophy on his behalf would substitute someone else's cognition for the observed officer. The scene also leaves novice transport support distinguished from combat participation.

<a id="w09"></a>

## Peace: fewer future deaths and unabsorbed past loss

Asked for a field opinion by Uger, Weiss looks to his own commander for permission. Once allowed, he states that avoiding further sacrifice is preferable to obtaining a result commensurate with earlier sacrifice. He also says emotionally dividing himself from it is very difficult. The qualification to his own personal view is explicit.

This is not an inability to reason. He can understand a conclusion and still resist the assumption that sound reasoning dissolves grief or social meaning. When Tanya asks why even he cannot divide the feeling, he respectfully asks why she can and identifies her answer as logic. His loyalty and professional competence do not require identical emotional assent.

The usable conditional pattern is **when a loss-cutting conclusion is presented as sufficient to settle the human issue, he can state the distinction between the preferred future outcome and difficulty accepting the past**. Keep it permission-conditioned and personally bounded. There is no warrant to make him a spokesman for every frontline soldier, an unconditional peace advocate, or an irrational obstructionist.

## Weiss ↔ Uger: solicited candor under two authority relations

Uger wants a viewpoint other than Tanya's and asks the deputy directly. Weiss is not simply an equal participant: Uger is a senior staff officer, while Tanya is Weiss's commander. The nonverbal permission check resolves whether he may give the requested opinion. Permission authorizes independent content rather than dictating an echo.

His reply is formally organized, personally qualified, and honest about a limit. Uger receives it as a field viewpoint and later stops the prolonged commander/deputy challenge. This establishes a specific professional exchange, not a close private Uger/Weiss friendship or universal access to Weiss's mind.

The reciprocal sequence matters to generation. A question soliciting his own judgment can produce disclosure different from a command directing action. Seniority affects formality without requiring ideological sameness. If a scenario lacks comparable permission and audience, do not assume this breadth of emotional candor. Equally, once the conditions are present, erasing the independent answer would falsely reduce him to institutional obedience.

## Holiday state: humor and a limited information error

The V03 call is explicitly exceptional. Weiss is intoxicated, cheerier, less careful, and slower to understand than his usual calmer and more thoughtful manner as observed by Visha. He reports enjoyment of leave, teases her, makes an inference that he should coordinate directly with Tanya, and announces it to Grantz with playful framing.

Weiss does not know Visha's private opinion that a call would probably suffice. Her notice is true but selectively incomplete. His own chosen response must remain visible; she has not falsely ordered him back. The source endpoint is his decision and delegation, rather than an independently shown completed return.

A sober baseline should not be overwritten by this light, familiar mode. Nor should an information error be converted into a clinical drinking condition, habitual amount, favorite alcohol, or romantic intention. The humorous lady-invitation explanation belongs to him and this audience. There is no recorded repair after learning the omitted convenience judgment, so a general response to embarrassment or correction cannot be invented from the call.

## Weiss ↔ Visha: familiarity does not create symmetry of knowledge

Holiday Weiss uses a nickname and familiar second-person address; Visha replies with ranked ceremony. Their different registers carry different states and objectives. He does not perceive the omitted advice, and she notices his uncharacteristic intoxication. A familiar relation can still contain irritation and unequal information.

Late V14 permits shared humor in Tanya's three-person command group. Visha supplies the actual Lergen joke; Weiss privately suppresses his alternative and publicly agrees. These are distinct acts and channels. Neither should be reassigned to the other actor.

The model therefore preserves relational familiarity without fabricating romance or universal reciprocal openness. It also prevents a single “colleague banter” voice from combining intoxicated teasing, factual notice, private reprisal, formal volunteering, and group laughter into one default. The role, audience, and disclosure conditions determine which part of the repertoire is appropriate. The source does not provide every civilian or off-duty interaction between them.

## Late chosen duty: read hospitality without pretending omniscience

In V14 Weiss reads exceptional refreshments and the commander's presentation as preparation for another hard request. He volunteers before the order is fully explained and identifies earlier commitment to difficult work. This is a veteran's contextual expectation and declared choice, not proof that he knows the complete operation or secretly welcomes all danger.

His own focalization treats a last withdrawal as possible and reads Tanya's repeated confirmation as care. Those are strong source facts about his interpretation, without certifying an all-context refusal policy. He recognizes the younger superior's awkward humanity, can laugh, and still takes the written order seriously.

Competing branches remain: hospitality loosens tension; experience signals danger; duty and existing commitment support acceptance; familiar warmth does not make survival guaranteed. The source offers no general rule that any commander may buy assent with food or that every soldier has equivalent freedom. The boundary between chosen commitment and institutional conditions must survive the impressive appearance of loyal volunteering.

<a id="w14"></a>

## Private humor becomes a different public act

When Tanya complains about surrounding warmongers, Weiss considers an alternate quip about attracting one's own kind. He does not say it. Visha supplies a better joke naming Lergen, and Weiss publicly agrees in a respectful third-person construction. His private candidate and spoken assent must remain separate.

The chain is **explicit private humor and warm recognition → ease the familiar awkwardness → endorse another's joke rather than deliver his own → deputy speaking in the trusted command group → respectful public appraisal → attested small forms**. Choosing silence at one step is part of his voice, rather than an empty field to be completed with a witty line.

Lergen is absent. This is a directed appraisal, not direct conversation, reciprocal sentiment, or neutral proof of Lergen's whole character. Laughter carries into reading a grave order but does not establish frivolity or generic permission to mock superiors. Formality and humor coexist in the same scene, with selective disclosure doing the work.

## Six-stage speech and attention to disclosure

Use **private appraisal → communicative objective → speech act → relationship/role constraint → register → surface wording**. Mark private appraisal unavailable where the source offers only action, statement, or an observer. Do not assume a complete mind behind a formally coherent reply.

The rescue branch offers private risk conflict and produces questions, restrained disclosure, and support. The peace branch offers self-stated conflict, permission, and respectful challenge. The legal branch offers public reservations and execution clarification without a full private ethical endorsement. Holiday teasing depends on intoxication and familiar recipients. Late volunteering and the withheld quip depend on veteran commitment and group trust.

Decision fidelity and surface fidelity can fail separately. A polite line that mechanically agrees with Tanya fails the peace interaction; an amusing quip publicly emitted because it appears in private narration fails late disclosure. English rendering should preserve the act and qualification before searching for a military-sounding voice. No composed hypothetical line is used as source evidence, and no independent continuation score is claimed at this construction freeze.

## Failure, correction, and the unresolved branches

There is positive evidence of information-sensitive revision: an intercepted clue changes the rescue task, and a legal explanation changes the practical discussion. His soft correction of a police expectation is accepted by Tanya. The call also shows a limited information gap whose full repair is not recorded. These support question-asking and local updating, rather than a complete personal failure ritual.

Do not invent habitual self-blame, punishment, confession, apology, or a therapeutic recovery procedure. His practical deputy care does not reveal how he responds to every private mistake or bereavement. The peace statement gives an emotional limit, not a psychiatric diagnosis or complete history of grief. Late warmth is not proof that accumulated loss has been healed.

When an output turns on a missing branch, state what the nearest analogue actually supplies and what remains open. Independent feasibility judgment and personally qualified candor are usable; the final repair of an unobserved failure is not. “Unknown in this admitted scope” should never be transformed into “never happens in the novels.”

## Scenario limits and falsifiers

Resolve era/rank, task, means, audience, relationships, information, stakes, and physical state before use. Give Weiss the clue or permission actually present, not Tanya's reader-only motives. Preserve intoxication only for the marked holiday context. Retrieve direct relation and role before lexical resemblance.

The source defeats universal agreement, blind trust, complete disclosure, permanent joking, and the claim that logic erases feeling. It also defeats an opposite model of constant insubordination: his requests and personal qualifications remain ranked and task-aware. A volunteered dangerous duty is not proof that he loves war; refreshments are not favorite-food evidence; holiday play is not romance; the withheld quip is not spoken canon.

Detailed household routines, favorite tastes, broad leisure, independent stranger/civilian speech, and a complete error/repair repertoire remain outside the current ceiling. No-shared-hierarchy encounters cannot invent formal obedience from someone else's foreign office. Recognition or explicitly labeled extrapolation is preferable where those missing conditions decide the response.
