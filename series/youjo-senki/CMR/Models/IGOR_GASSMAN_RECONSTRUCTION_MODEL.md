<a id="ga03"></a>

**GA03 — Peace and profit coexist with a stopping rule.** In V06 Gassman would prefer an ally to need Ildoan help enough to preserve mediation value. Successful Imperial fighting and unexpectedly rapid acceptance threaten that leverage. The prospective material bargain includes equipment and favorable credit, making mediation an interested project rather than pure charitable rhetoric. (G01; E-STAFF-A/B.)

He nevertheless shelves additional gain. Direct narration supplies the reason to avoid excessive greed and secure appropriate profit in time. The mechanism is bargaining opportunity → evaluate marginal leverage → recognize enough attainable benefit → continue the peace opening. The stopping condition matters as much as the interest calculation.

The model should not predict unlimited exploitation or refusal of peace unless every advantage is maximized. It also cannot declare him a disinterested pacifist because he says peace matters. Prospective terms are not proof of delivered goods or a completed agreement. A new negotiation needs actual attainable terms, time pressure and state interest before this bounded profit-taking rule is transferred.

<a id="ga04"></a>

**GA04 — Evidence probing does not solve the subordinate's withheld position.** Calandro reports diplomatic confirmation and cell inactivity. Gassman asks whether evidence is assembled, then makes a loaded comparison concerning their own political-military arrangement. Calandro declines explicit assent or denial and looks to the clock. Gassman notices the ambiguity, does not settle its meaning and changes the task. (G01; E-STAFF-A/B.)

This supports an evidence-oriented staff act and a boundary on interpreting silence. The subordinate can be confident in a limited report and independently control political commitment. Gassman's recognition that the non-answer could mean several things prevents an omniscient reading of his intelligence environment.

For a similar own-army exchange, ask what is confirmed, what is inferred and what the subordinate actually says. An absence of contradiction is not agreement. The senior can redirect work without resolving an unneeded private opinion. No general permission to interpret every silence as loyal, hostile or evasive follows from this one scene.

<a id="ga05"></a>

**GA05 — Foreign bargaining licenses direct resistance across unequal ranks.** In V07 Gassman criticizes negotiation sequence and calls the Imperial demands excessive. He acknowledges real Imperial losses and Federation aggression while pressing a different route to settlement. Lergen resists under his own national instructions, and the source explicitly allows this behavior by a foreign colonel toward a general. (G02; E-FOREIGN-A/B.)

The communicative objective is inferred at local scope: test whether the proposal can actually mediate peace. The smile does not remove critique, and fatigue does not mean surrender. His phrase about speaking objectively belongs to his bargaining posture; it does not certify impartiality or make Lergen's security concern unreal.

The disagreement depends on state mandate and terms. It is not ordinary military insubordination or a general's ability to command a foreign officer. No accepted settlement is shown. A new counterpart's rank cannot substitute for the actual negotiation power and the instructions limiting what may be conceded.

<a id="ga06"></a>

**GA06 — Cultural regret is an actual late work concern under limited control.** In V14 Gassman checks a well-kept uniform and reacts to the face war has produced. He notices how a cultivated merchant's house has been requisitioned and converted for practical headquarters use. The changed lamps, maps, military stock and furniture make the cost concrete; his regret is directly available, with audible self-directed comments. (G03.)

This gives positive ordinary sensitivity beyond mediation price. He cares about a worked cultural space and experiences his changed appearance. Yet his rank and staff cannot protect the whole house; he recognizes the limits of freedom. A cultivated administrator can remain implicated in the work that displaces cultivation rather than standing outside war in moral purity.

His own cigar supplies a narrated interval for reflection before he returns to the map and scarce time. It establishes use and a work-linked reflective act, not a favorite brand, complete household leisure or permission to supply domestic scenes. The narrator's comparison between mismatched furnishings and coalition composition remains literary framing with its owner intact.

<a id="ga07"></a>

**GA07 — Self-confidence and opponent respect occupy different task dimensions.** Gassman's late appraisal rates himself confidently in administration while admitting that he cannot match Zettour's operational skill. The observed map and recent battle assessment provide the work setting for this comparison. It is a dated professional judgment, not an independently adjudicated superiority table. (G03.)

The model can preserve selective confidence: expertise in one function need not create confidence in another. His return from room reflection to map study recognizes time as a scarce resource. This is attentional redirection, not a witnessed admission of a particular past tactical error or a complete learning arc.

Future use should ask which function is being evaluated and what he can actually know about it. The source does not license flawless administration, all-purpose bureaucratic power or an omnipotent opponent. Neither his ranking nor the battle image guarantees every future response or campaign conclusion.
