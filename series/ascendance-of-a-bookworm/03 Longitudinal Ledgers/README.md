# Ascendance of a Bookworm longitudinal-ledger router

No longitudinal ledger is instantiated at bootstrap. This file records when one should be created and which responsibilities are likely to recur in a 33-volume Japanese-primary reading.

A ledger earns a canonical file when the same analytical question must be updated across multiple volume freezes and retrieving it only from individual deep readings becomes unreliable.

Candidate responsibilities include:

- character state, self-concept, identity continuity, names/titles/roles, and developmental change;
- family, friendship, service, mentorship, patronage, rivalry, intimacy, separation, reciprocity, and obligation;
- institutional position, status/class, legal/customary constraint, practical autonomy, and coercive leverage;
- knowledge transfer, production, technology/process change, labor, education, commerce, diffusion, and unintended consequences;
- information asymmetry: who knows what, when, and how disclosure or secrecy changes available action;
- world-model evidence for religion, law, politics, history, economics, education, and other setting systems, with belief separated from corroborated mechanism;
- bodily constraints, risk, competence, resources, and the interaction between capacity and choice;
- ordinary life, preferences, habits, etiquette, gifts, work rhythm, leisure, and recipient-conditioned behavior;
- recurring imagery, metaphors, formal patterns, distinctive Japanese terminology, and focalization effects.

These are **candidate ledger responsibilities, not predetermined theses**. Combine responsibilities when evidence is sparse; split them only when independent revision/retrieval becomes useful.

Every longitudinal entry should preserve the source boundary and volume freeze that established it. Later recontextualization may revise the current interpretation without erasing the earlier prospective state.
