# Ascendance of a Bookworm full-series-synthesis router

This directory is the canonical future home for the integrated full-series synthesis of the declared Bookworm source boundary.

At the pre-V01 boundary, **no full-series synthesis exists or is authorized by source completion**. This router exists to make the completion gate explicit before sequential analysis accumulates architectural debt.

## Intended canonical artifact

When all gates pass, the expected integrated artifact is:

`BOOKWORM_FULL_SERIES_SYNTHESIS.md`

Its exact title or decomposition may be amended by a later stabilized architecture if the completed source proves that one integrated artifact would obscure independently necessary responsibilities. The default is still one clear integrated synthesis, not a proliferation of near-final documents.

## Eligibility gate

Sequential completion at V33 is necessary but not sufficient. Full-series integration becomes eligible only after:

1. V01-V33 are all frozen under the prospective method;
2. the analytical placement and legitimate retrospective scope of *Royal Academy Stories: First Year* are explicitly resolved for the synthesis boundary;
3. `../03 Longitudinal Ledgers/BOOKWORM_MASTER_LONGITUDINAL_LEDGER.md` and any later split ledgers are reconciled through the final covered boundary;
4. a post-sequential architecture/role-gap audit confirms that material specialist responsibilities have been satisfied or explicitly waived with rationale;
5. character/relationship models required by specialist or final claims are mature enough for their downstream use;
6. major claim-revision state, rival readings, and contradictions are stabilized enough to state uncertainty honestly;
7. required specialist syntheses have converged without unresolved routing conflicts;
8. evidence routes are sufficient to recover the basis of major mature claims;
9. the current corpus map explicitly records `FULL-SERIES READY` or an equivalent recoverable gate state.

## Authority behavior

A mature full-series synthesis may become the preferred integrated interpretation for its declared source boundary. It does **not** erase or supersede the frozen sequential readings as historical records of what the prospective source state supported at each volume boundary.

If a later source-lock revision adds a materially new witness, state whether the full-series synthesis remains current for its old boundary, is revised in place while mutable, or receives an explicit successor. Do not silently expand its source claim.

## Reasoning class

The default reasoning class is `PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST` because final integration is corpus-wide and propagation-sensitive. A later architecture revision may downgrade that class only if the mature corpus is sufficiently bounded and the reliability case is explicit.
