## 3. Volume-level analytical synthesis

V02 can be summarized as a movement from **private improvisation to institutionalized production, commerce, legal constraint, and class-mediated survival**.

V01 established that remembered information is not equivalent to practical agency. V02 turns that observation into a recurring system rule. Myne's paper project succeeds only when knowledge is translated through Lutz's body and local familiarity, Marc's procurement and organization, Benno's financing and market access, specialist artisans, suitable wood and binder, repeated measurement, error correction, and enough time to learn what the remembered concept did not specify. The same rule appears in miniature when a professional attempt to reproduce Myne's hair-cleaning mixture initially fails: filtering the mixture more finely removes tiny plant solids that had been contributing abrasive/scrubbing function. A seemingly cleaner process destroys an unrecognized functional component.

The larger implication is methodological as much as technological:

> **Imported knowledge enters the local world first as a hypothesis. Its usefulness must be discovered again under local materials, skills, institutions, incentives, and hidden variables.**

At the same time, V02 transforms Myne's economic situation. Paper becomes an actual product; hair ornaments become a luxury commission and then a scalable household product; children and relatives can become paid suppliers; merchant education becomes a concrete human-capital project; and income becomes relevant not merely to books but potentially to survival from `身食い`.

The identity problem also becomes interpersonal rather than purely internal. Lutz directly notices that current Myne cannot plausibly be continuous in behavior and knowledge with the child he remembers. His challenge forces Myne to articulate her uncertainty about what happened to the prior Myne. Yet the scene does not resolve metaphysics. Its durable importance is relational: after confronting the possibility of replacement, Lutz deliberately chooses the current person as “his Myne.” Recognition by another person gives the Urano/Myne identity a social anchor that self-narration alone did not supply.

Finally, `身食い` shifts from a speculative reader-side hypothesis to a strongly corroborated recognized condition. Frieda reports analogous internal heat; the Guild Master has already spent wealth and connections obtaining magical tools for her; and Benno treats Myne's earning capacity as potential survival capital. But the volume remains careful enough that the project should do the same: the observed syndrome is now strongly established, whereas the complete magic model, exact prognosis, treatment mechanics, and V02 emergency outcome remain partly or wholly open.

## 4. Source structure and focalization

### 4.1 Myne's main focalization

The main body remains closely filtered through Myne. Her prior-world knowledge continues to produce useful conceptual shortcuts but also hidden assumptions. This is especially visible in production work: she knows what “paper” or a cleaning product should accomplish, but local implementation exposes missing process knowledge. Her strongest competence is increasingly not omniscient technical memory but **iterative problem framing**: compare outcomes, isolate variables, modify process, recruit expertise, and keep records.

Myne's account must also be distinguished from the commercial models held by Benno, Marc, the Guild Master, and Frieda. She often thinks first in terms of making something she wants. Adults with market experience immediately think about scarcity, price discrimination, exclusivity, imitation, contracts, political exposure, and leverage.

### 4.2 Prologue: household model of “changed Myne”

The prologue places the reader with Tuuli and Effa. Their evidence matters because the family does perceive a before/after discontinuity. Current Myne is more active, persistent, useful, inventive, and goal-driven than the child they remember. However, they interpret the change through ordinary family categories—recovery, maturation, stubbornness, resemblance to Gunther—rather than supernatural replacement.

This is important counterweight to both extremes. The household is not simply blind to change, but neither does V02 show them independently inferring Urano's identity.

### 4.3 Lutz and the identity confrontation

Lutz's accumulated observations cross a different threshold. In `ルッツのマイン`, he asks who she actually is because the old Myne could not have possessed the current Myne's knowledge and capabilities. His inference is grounded in remembered behavior rather than abstract metaphysics.

Myne cannot answer with certainty. She explains her own remembered prior life and her speculation that the original Myne may have disappeared into the internal heat. Lutz initially imagined that removing the current Myne might allow the old one to return, then recognizes that current Myne's disappearance may simply leave no Myne at all.

He estimates that almost all of the Myne he has personally interacted with—the hairpin-making, cooking, paper-seeking, bargaining person—is the current consciousness. His acceptance therefore rests on **relationship continuity as experienced by him**, not proof of ontological continuity.

### 4.4 `エピローグ`: crisis through Lutz

The epilogue gives Lutz's perspective on Myne's final collapse. It externally confirms abrupt heat severe enough for him to feel physically, his fear and helplessness, and an unusual yellowish vapor-like phenomenon around her. Benno immediately treats the condition as the anticipated `身食い` emergency and redirects them toward the Guild Master's resources.

This is stronger corroboration of an abnormal magic-linked syndrome than V01 possessed, but the scene still ends before the intervention's result.

### 4.5 `コリンナの結婚事情`

Corinna's story substantially broadens the institutional model around the Gilberta Company. It shows a business with female-line inheritance history, Benno acting as manager while Corinna is the designated heir, craftmaster requirements that include sustained orders and noble clientele, and marriage negotiations shaped by occupational compatibility, family verification, guild power, and market pressure.

Otto's decision to purchase Ehrenfest citizenship and enter soldier work is also reframed as a marriage strategy. Status, occupation, guild politics, and intimate choice are not separable domains in this social world.

### 4.6 `洗濯中の井戸端会議`

Effa's viewpoint supplies the most important new evidence about pre-awakening Myne. During severe fevers, the earlier child spoke of a dream world where she could run freely, eat wonderful food, and perhaps remain asleep forever. That dream-talk later stopped around the broader behavioral change.

This evidence does **not** solve the identity question. It does, however, weaken an overly clean “Urano arrived at one instant into a wholly unrelated blank vessel” model. Some form of prior dream/memory permeability is possible within V02 evidence. Effa herself interprets the current goal-driven Myne as resembling Gunther, which gives the family an ordinary kinship narrative through which to absorb otherwise unusual behavior.

## 5. Identity: from private uncertainty to social recognition

V02 materially revises the identity model without closing it.

### 5.1 What is now stronger

The following are independently supported:

- Urano's first-person continuity remains direct experience;
- family members remember a behaviorally different earlier Myne;
- Lutz independently recognizes the knowledge/behavior discontinuity strongly enough to confront her;
- Myne herself lacks a definitive account of the transition;
- the earlier Myne's fever dreams may have contained imagery or experiences resembling Urano's world;
- current Myne can disclose the prior-life identity to Lutz and be accepted without the old Myne returning.

### 5.2 Lutz's choice is social, not metaphysical adjudication

Lutz's acceptance is analytically important precisely because it does not require a solved ontology. He chooses the person with whom he has actually formed a relationship. This converts identity from “what entity is inside this body?” into a second question: **who is recognized as the continuing participant in present relationships?**

That social question has a clearer V02 answer than the metaphysical one. Lutz recognizes current Myne.

### 5.3 Current freeze

The simple replacement model should be **downgraded**, not rejected outright. A merger, gradual transition, memory permeability, or other model remains possible. V02 does not establish the mechanism that links Urano, prior Myne, the fevers, or the abnormal heat.

## 6. Desire to live: a major developmental shift

V01 ended with survival beginning to acquire non-book motives. V02 strengthens that shift substantially.

Myne now explicitly says she does not want to die. Her reasons include:

- paper has become feasible;
- a future route toward books is becoming concrete;
- she has adapted through a substantial seasonal cycle;
- she is useful to other people;
- family relationships are functioning;
- work and exchange have become meaningful;
- she is enjoying her current life.

This is not evidence that book obsession has weakened into an ordinary hobby. Rather, the project that began as “obtain books at any cost” has generated relationships, competence, obligations, and social belonging that now make life itself more valuable.

There remains a tension: during the Lutz identity confrontation Myne can still conceptualize surrendering to the heat and disappearing. The V02 model is therefore not “suicidality resolved,” but **survival motivation has become substantially broader and stronger**.

## 7. Paper: from remembered concept to production system

### 7.1 Success is distributed

The paper breakthrough should not be attributed to Myne alone. Causal contributions include:

- Myne: remembers the broad plant-fiber concept, organizes experiments, compares variables, records outcomes, proposes process changes;
- Lutz: performs much of the physical work, gathers and processes materials, maintains pace/safety, remembers scarce instruction, and translates local conditions;
- Benno: provides capital, market access, commercial testing, procurement authority, and strategic protection;
- Marc: coordinates purchasing, tools, contacts, and production logistics;
- local artisans and suppliers: provide equipment and embodied craft Myne cannot manufacture herself;
- the environment: constrains wood, water, season, binder, Trombe availability, and feasible work rhythms.

This is V02's strongest evidence for the project's anti-“technology tree unlock” model.

### 7.2 Failure remains evidence

Paper quality changes with wood species and age, binder composition, degree of beating, steaming, sheet formation, and other local parameters. Trombe fiber produces especially good results and is easier to strip, but its material is dangerous, unpredictable, and ecologically costly to obtain. Ordinary wood remains viable with enough process tuning.

The correct causal story is not that Myne “knows papermaking.” She knows enough to formulate a search space and recognize success, then learns the actual local process through experiment.

### 7.3 Product form follows local demand

Sheet size is adapted to local document and contract use rather than copied from Japanese convention. This is a small but important example of successful transfer occurring through **functional localization**, not cultural replication.

### 7.4 Scaling is not yet solved

V02 reaches functional production and commercial acceptance, not industrial maturity. Winter constrains water/wood processing, larger tools require financing, labor organization is still forming, and material supply remains uncertain. Scaling becomes a V03-facing institutional question.

## 8. Tacit knowledge: the hair-cleaning mixture as a control case

The hair-cleaning product is analytically useful because it reverses the usual knowledge direction. The household method works, but a more professional replication initially fails despite apparently superior processing.

The reason is hidden function: fine filtering removes tiny plant solids that had been acting as scrub. A process improvement defined as “remove impurities” destroys an unrecognized useful variable.

This is strong evidence for three claims:

1. users can possess effective tacit knowledge without a correct mechanistic theory;
2. professionalization can fail if it optimizes the wrong variable;
3. replication requires understanding what each process step *does*, not merely copying visible ingredients.

The episode therefore strengthens V01-C01 independently of papermaking.

## 9. Contract magic and the limits of enforcement

`契約魔術` is V02's clearest demonstration that magic is institutional infrastructure rather than only creature/illness phenomenology.

Benno describes it as historically associated with binding especially powerful or tyrannical nobles. The contract uses restricted magical paper and ink, signatures and blood, and becomes strongly bilateral: neither party can simply cancel it. Breach is described as potentially life-threatening.

The document disappears when activated. That creates a striking administrative problem: magical enforcement does not preserve an ordinary readable copy of the terms. Benno later tests Myne and Lutz on the investment agreement. Myne initially forgets a term and recovers it only by carefully reconstructing/re-reading what she had written before activation.

The durable institutional lesson is:

> **Perfectly strong enforcement does not eliminate the need for literacy, memory, records, or bargaining competence.**

Magic can enforce an agreement while leaving the human participants vulnerable to misunderstanding what they agreed to.

## 10. Commercial Guild: identity, permission, and finance

The Commercial Guild expands the city's institutional model substantially.

Observed functions include:

- registration and authorization to trade;
- blood-linked magical guild cards;
- access control within the guild building;
- storage/transfer functions analogous to banking or deposit infrastructure;
- merchant-family norms for pre-baptism temporary registration;
- discretionary/gatekeeping power in unusual cases;
- status-coded access and spatial differentiation.

Myne and Lutz's non-kin pre-baptism registration is unusual enough to require negotiation. The Guild Master attempts to capture the value represented by the children and their products. Benno responds strategically: he conceals paper while exposing hair ornaments, then uses Frieda's urgent demand for an ornament as leverage to secure registration.

The episode shows that market access is neither anonymous nor automatic. **Institutions mediate whether technical competence can become legal commerce.**

## 11. Money, pricing, and merchant pedagogy

V02 supplies a usable but still local money ladder, with units increasing roughly by factors of ten from small copper upward. The important analytical point is less the memorized denomination table than Myne's changing relationship to price.

Benno repeatedly teaches her that price is not just material cost plus a modest margin. It reflects scarcity, urgency, exclusivity, buyer wealth, bargaining position, and strategic market creation. Frieda's bespoke hair ornament is a luxury commission, not a household craft sold at replacement cost.

Myne nevertheless retains a distributional preference: she imagines a cheaper general-market version for poorer girls alongside premium products. Her pricing instincts therefore combine commercial learning with a persistent desire for broad access.

The paper deal similarly formalizes contribution and risk. Benno allocates costs/tooling, his own fee, and a residual share to Myne/Lutz. Whatever later contracts do, V02 establishes that ideas become economically legible only through negotiated ownership, capital, and distribution.

## 12. `身食い`: from hypothesis to class-mediated survival crisis

### 12.1 Independent recognition

Frieda independently recognizes Myne's symptoms as `身食い` and describes an analogous internally moving heat. The Guild Master has already expended substantial wealth and noble connections to obtain magical tools for Frieda. This materially strengthens the diagnosis beyond Benno's V01 speculation.

At the V02 boundary, the most defensible formulation is:

- `身食い` is a recognized local condition strongly matching Myne's symptoms;
- the condition is associated by informed actors with excess `魔力`;
- magical tools can apparently absorb/discharge enough power to prolong life;
- access to those tools is scarce, expensive, and tied to noble networks;
- exact underlying metaphysics and all treatment mechanics remain incompletely demonstrated.

### 12.2 Survival is political-economic

Frieda's survival is not merely a medical fact. It is made possible by household wealth and the Guild Master's connections to nobles. Benno therefore reframes Myne's product knowledge as survival capital: money and commercial value may buy time or bargaining leverage.

This makes `身食い` a direct bridge between body, class, magic, and patronage. A person can possess the same physiological/magical problem but face radically different survival probabilities depending on wealth and access.

### 12.3 The crisis accelerates

Myne increasingly distinguishes ordinary fatigue-related fever from the internally moving `身食い` heat. Emotional triggers become smaller; suppression takes more time and energy; and eventually even positive completion/relief is associated with an episode. At the final shop collapse she can no longer contain it.

Lutz feels the heat externally and perceives an unusual yellowish vapor-like phenomenon. This is strong corroboration that the event is not only subjective distress.

### 12.4 V02 does not show the treatment result

Benno has negotiated emergency access to a nearly exhausted one-use magical tool held by the Guild Master. Its cost and remaining capacity are described as severe, and the expected benefit is temporary—on the order of months rather than a definitive cure.

But the source ends before the tool is used on-page. Therefore V02 cannot canonically say:

- that the intervention succeeds;
- exactly how much time it buys Myne;
- what side effects occur;
- what arrangement follows;
- whether a different mechanism intervenes.

Those are V03-or-later questions.

## 13. Magic doctrine contradiction: nobles and commoners

V02 creates an important unresolved world-model tension. Marc presents `魔力` as a power associated exclusively with nobles in ordinary social understanding. Benno, Frieda, and the Guild Master simultaneously describe `身食い` commoners as possessing excess magic.

Possible explanations include restricted social definitions, rare exceptions, class ideology, incomplete common knowledge, terminology differences, or another mechanism. V02 does not adjudicate among them.

The correct freeze is therefore **OPEN**: the apparent doctrine “only nobles have magic” cannot simply be promoted to objective fact, but neither should it be discarded without a source-grounded explanation.

## 14. Myne and Lutz: reciprocity becomes identity-bearing

V02 strengthens the Myne/Lutz partnership in four distinct dimensions.

### 14.1 Production complementarity

Myne supplies conceptual models, writing/calculation, experiment design, and market-facing ideas. Lutz supplies strength, local material knowledge, field execution, pacing, risk management, and unusually strong memory under conditions where written reference is scarce.

### 14.2 Career reciprocity

Myne continues to defend Lutz's right to choose his own occupation. She teaches or helps coordinate the literacy, calculation, and merchant knowledge that his family cannot supply. His career becomes partly a joint human-capital project.

### 14.3 Identity recognition

Lutz is the first local peer to receive Myne's direct disclosure of Urano's prior-life identity. His deliberate acceptance of current Myne gives their relationship a unique information state: he knows something her family does not, while also having the strongest firsthand memory of the behavioral discontinuity.

### 14.4 Survival motivation

During the final crisis Lutz carries Myne, panics at the possibility of losing her, and seeks intervention. Benno redirects that fear toward action and earning power. Lutz's desire to find profitable products thus becomes partly linked to buying Myne survival time.

The relationship is therefore materially deeper than “helper and inventor,” but V02 still does not require an independent dyadic synthesis. The V03 Part 1 boundary is the appropriate first mandatory promotion review.

## 15. Benno: protector, investor, gatekeeper, and extractor

V02 makes a one-dimensional reading of Benno increasingly untenable.

He:

- finances tools and production;
- provides market access;
- buys paper and treats it as commercially important;
- controls disclosure to competitors;
- uses contract magic to bind terms;
- tests Myne and Lutz's memory and commercial competence;
- seeks access to Myne's product knowledge;
- recognizes the survival implications of `身食い`;
- negotiates emergency access to a magic tool;
- teaches both children to think in terms of leverage, price, and earning capacity.

His protective and extractive motives are not mutually exclusive. His incentives often align with Myne's survival because a living, productive Myne is also commercially valuable. The analysis should preserve that mixed structure rather than convert it into either pure altruism or pure exploitation.

## 16. Lutz's family, occupational choice, and education

Lutz's conflict with family expectations becomes concrete in V02.

His father favors a craft path; paper success can actually make “become a papermaker” more plausible from that perspective. Lutz, however, wants the broader merchant/travel route. His mother worries about uncertainty and the educational gap between him and children raised in merchant households.

Myne's response is normatively clear within the scene: the decision about what Lutz wants to become belongs to Lutz. Yet desire alone is insufficient. He needs literacy, arithmetic, vocabulary, etiquette, money knowledge, product knowledge, proper clothing, and a sponsor willing to admit him.

Winter work therefore functions as education and capital formation. Lutz's brothers become paid workers once wages are made explicit; Lutz tracks production and money; Marc insists even family labor be accounted for. Household activity becomes a proto-enterprise rather than invisible kin labor.

## 17. Hair ornaments: luxury, diffusion, and household industry

Frieda's hair ornament commission reveals how a small aesthetic technique becomes a product category.

Frieda identifies the dimensional crocheted flower as locally novel. The absence of an established artificial-flower market may partly reflect elite access to magically preserved real flowers, although V02 does not warrant turning that observation into a complete fashion history.

Benno teaches Myne to price the bespoke commission as luxury. Myne simultaneously imagines a cheaper version for ordinary girls. The Guild Master's enthusiasm then creates demand before deliberate market launch, forcing a rush-production problem.

Myne herself is not the best producer. Effa and Tuuli are much faster; Lutz's brothers can contribute for wages. The scalable unit is therefore not “Myne's hands” but a **household and neighborhood production network organized around Myne's design/market knowledge**.

Income also feeds back into household welfare, including winter preparation. Commercialization is becoming socially embedded rather than separate from domestic life.

## 18. Education and the merchant competence stack

V02 makes visible a broad merchant-apprentice competence stack:

- grooming and presentation;
- greetings and polite speech;
- local literacy;
- numbers and calculation;
- use of local calculation devices;
- money denominations;
- product and trader knowledge;
- contract literacy;
- memory under scarce reference access;
- bargaining and pricing;
- awareness of status and institutional procedure.

Myne and Lutz have complementary deficits. Myne possesses strong writing and written arithmetic but lacks local vocabulary, etiquette, units, tools, and market assumptions. Lutz has weaker formal literacy but unusually disciplined retention because he cannot assume information will be available later.

Myne's old-world habit of relying on future lookup is therefore exposed as an environmental adaptation, not a universal cognitive advantage. Scarcity of books and references changes what “good memory” is worth.

## 19. Family, gender, occupation, and status

The appended viewpoints show that household expectations are neither purely individual nor purely gendered; they are entangled with trade inheritance, marriage markets, guild relations, and family strategy.

Effa expected a soldier's daughter to move toward a household role compatible with soldier life. Current Myne's insistence on an unconventional occupation feels disruptive, but Effa can interpret it through Gunther's own history of rejecting an inherited woodworker route to become a soldier for a personally important goal.

Corinna's story complicates any assumption that commerce simply follows male inheritance. The Gilberta Company has a female-line history, Corinna is the designated heir, and Benno manages within that structure. At the same time, marriage remains economically strategic and subject to family/guild pressure. Otto's relative insulation from the Guild Master's market power is part of what makes him an attractive partner for Corinna.

The durable model is one of **role-constrained agency**, not either unrestricted choice or a single rigid gender rule.

## 20. Calendar and religious-social time

V02 gives Myne a more formal model of local weekly time:

- `水の日`
- `芽の日`
- `火の日`
- `葉の日`
- `風の日`
- `実の日`
- `土の日`

`土の日` functions as the rest day in the observed routine. Baptisms remain season-linked social thresholds.

It is significant that Myne learns formal day names relatively late. Ordinary household survival did not require her to master the full calendrical vocabulary immediately. This reinforces the broader epistemic rule that social knowledge is distributed by role and necessity rather than automatically shared by everyone in the city.

## 21. Maps and illustrations

The two reviewed maps provide rough spatial corroboration for the institutional geography used in the prose: Myne's home, Lutz's home, the workshop, South Gate and forest, temple, central commercial area, Gilberta Company, Commercial Guild, market, artisan streets, and other city landmarks.

They support the distinction between household/peripheral, gate/administrative, craft, commercial-guild, and temple spaces. They do not by themselves establish exact distances, class boundaries, or travel times beyond what the prose demonstrates.

The nine reviewed narrative illustrations support scene identity, clothing/material culture, character groupings, and the visual presentation of key production/commercial moments. No illustration independently resolves the metaphysics of `身食い`, reincarnation, or magic doctrine.
