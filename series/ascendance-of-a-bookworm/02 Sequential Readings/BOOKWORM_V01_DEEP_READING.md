## 2. Volume-level analytical synthesis

V01 is not principally a story in which a modern adult simply applies superior knowledge to a primitive environment. Its more durable structure is a repeated confrontation between **desire, knowledge, embodiment, material conditions, and social cooperation**.

Urano Motosu enters Myne's life with an unusually stable obsession—books—and with memories of a society in which literacy, paper, writing implements, sanitation, food distribution, transport, and institutional services are largely invisible infrastructure. V01 strips that infrastructure away. Again and again, Urano knows that a thing *exists* without possessing the embodied craft knowledge, raw materials, tools, labor, money, local vocabulary, social permission, or physical strength necessary to reproduce it. Her repeated failures with papyrus-like material, clay tablets, wooden slips, ink, Japanese food, and ordinary household labor are not incidental comedy. They establish a governing causal rule for the series at this boundary:

> **Information becomes practical agency only when it can be translated through local materials, embodied skill, labor, institutions, trust, and resources.**

The volume then changes Myne's strategy. She begins by trying to recreate a readable world almost entirely through her own effort. By the final merchant meeting, she has learned to treat division of labor and exchange as technologies in their own right: if her body cannot do the work, someone else can; if she cannot pay conventional wages, she can provide recipes, information, product concepts, literacy, introductions, or future commercial value. Lutz becomes the first sustained embodiment of that complementarity. Otto supplies literacy, administrative access, and the conceptual move from self-production to organized labor. Benno supplies a market test and converts paper from a private coping project into a potentially disruptive commodity.

That practical arc is inseparable from an identity arc. At awakening, Urano treats Myne's body, family, and memories as circumstances that have happened *to her*. She can access Myne's memories, but some of the associated feelings initially register as belonging to someone else. Her attachment to life is correspondingly weak: a body that is always sick and a world without books do not yet feel fully hers. The first completed clay-tablet story briefly creates a path on which she can imagine living here. After later failures, a more important shift occurs: when the strange internal heat threatens to consume her, **an obligation to Lutz** becomes one reason to resist. Books remain her dominant desire, but they are no longer the only thing with motivational force.

The appended viewpoints prevent the reader from accepting Myne's self-description at face value. Myne repeatedly calls herself useless because she cannot perform ordinary physical labor. Otto and Benno instead see an anomalously valuable child: literate, numerate, administratively competent, strategically guarded with information, able to negotiate, and socially composed beyond her apparent age. Lutz sees both the burden and the exchange value of caring for her. Fey sees something more disturbing: when Myne becomes truly angry, her eyes change and he experiences a frightening pressure. V01 therefore establishes a persistent distinction between **Myne's internal model, her family's familiarized model, and the increasingly alarmed or commercially interested models of outsiders**.

Finally, V01 ends with a major information asymmetry. Myne recognizes that her fevers are behaving unlike ordinary illness, but she does not know why. Benno, in a private conversation unavailable to her, identifies `身食い` as a possible explanation and describes it as excess `魔力` consuming the person from within. His identification is explicitly uncertain. The correct V01 freeze is therefore not “Myne has the Devouring” as a settled fact. It is:

- the abnormal heat is directly experienced by Myne and externally associated with anomalous eye/pressure effects;
- Benno privately proposes `身食い` as a plausible explanation;
- the reader receives a magic-linked causal hypothesis unavailable to Myne;
- confirmation, exact mechanism, prognosis, and available alternatives remain **OPEN**.

## 3. Source structure and focalization

### 3.1 Main focalization

Most of V01 is tightly focalized through Urano/Myne. That matters because her narration continually uses Japanese historical, technological, and social analogies as interpretive shortcuts. Terms such as “European-looking,” comparisons to Ekiden processions, references to Mesopotamian/Yellow River civilization, modern cooking categories, and Japanese institutional expectations are **evidence about her cognitive translation process**, not automatically objective descriptions of the setting.

The volume repeatedly demonstrates that her first analogies can be useful but incomplete:

- an object can resemble a familiar vegetable while behaving in an impossible way;
- “paper” knowledge proves insufficient without process knowledge and materials;
- familiar-looking fish do not taste or cook as expected;
- common cooking steps differ, sometimes because local ingredients behave differently;
- the apparently ordinary “fever” does not obey ordinary illness logic;
- assumptions about work, apprenticeship, citizenship, and travel merchants must be corrected by local adults.

V01 therefore requires three levels to be kept separate throughout the corpus:

1. **what Myne directly observes**;
2. **what Myne infers by analogy to Earth/Japan**;
3. **what local actors with relevant experience report or demonstrate**.

### 3.2 `エピローグ`: Otto focalization

The epilogue relocates the reader to Otto and Corinna's home and is analytically indispensable. It establishes that Myne's abnormality is not merely a joke produced by her own internal narration. Otto and Benno independently notice mismatches between her class position and her appearance, posture, speech, calculation, literacy, composure, and negotiation behavior.

It also reveals information withheld from Myne:

- Benno already thinks her product knowledge may be commercially dangerous or extraordinarily valuable;
- Otto and Benno compete over access to her labor;
- Benno privately suspects `身食い`;
- Benno describes a severe institutional choice if that suspicion is correct: death without a way to discharge magic, or survival through dependence on a noble contract and access to a magical tool.

These remain viewpoint-bound claims, but they materially widen the reader's model.

### 3.3 `マインのいない日常`: Lutz focalization

Lutz's viewpoint corrects any tendency to idealize his assistance as purely selfless devotion. He explicitly values the food Myne shares, the recipes she provides, the possibility of introductions, and the motivational effect of seeing someone weaker than himself pursue an ambition relentlessly. His help is genuine and often generous, but it is also reciprocal and intelligible within his own needs.

This chapter also externally corroborates Myne's frightening anger state. Lutz reports that her golden eyes become complex/rainbow-like when she is genuinely angry; Fey reports that being glared at left him feeling as though he could not breathe and made Myne seem uncanny. This does not establish the mechanism, but it shows that the phenomenon is not confined to Myne's subjective fever narrative.

### 3.4 `変わらぬ日常`: Shuu focalization

The Shuu story supplies a prior-life control sample. Urano's book fixation, tunnel attention, tendency to neglect surroundings, love of unfamiliar libraries/bookstores, and need for another person to pull her away from reading all predate reincarnation. This strongly supports **continuity of a stable book-centered tendency** rather than treating it as a reaction created by Myne's circumstances.

The same story supplies counterevidence against the simplistic reading that she is incapable of learning from consequences. After being accidentally locked in a library as a child, Urano becomes sensitive to darkness/closing conditions and changes her behavior enough to avoid an exact repeat. The stable trait is not “cannot learn”; it is closer to **extreme attentional capture by books, with learning possible when consequences become salient enough**.

## 4. Identity continuity, reincarnation, and acceptance of life as Myne

### 4.1 What V01 directly supports

The awakened consciousness identifies herself as Urano Motosu, remembers being twenty-two, remembers her Japanese family and Shuu, and remembers dying under falling books during an earthquake. She then wakes in a feverish child's body and receives access to Myne's linguistic and autobiographical memories as local stimuli trigger them.

The text makes a meaningful distinction between **memory access** and **felt ownership**. Early in the volume, Myne can know who Effa, Gunther, or Tuuli are through the body's memories while simultaneously experiencing their emotional significance as strange or not fully hers. Prior Myne's jealousy of Tuuli and attachment to Effa can surface as something Urano notices rather than something she straightforwardly recognizes as her own longstanding emotion.

V01 therefore supports personal continuity of Urano's first-person self-conception while leaving the deeper ontology open.

### 4.2 What V01 does not settle

Do **not** resolve from later lore whether:

- original Myne died before Urano's awakening;
- Urano replaced her;
- the two consciousnesses merged;
- Myne's memories are a substrate, inheritance, or integrated identity;
- the abnormal heat caused the transition;
- Urano's arrival and the prior Myne's disappearance are causally identical events.

Myne herself later hypothesizes that the original Myne may have been swallowed by the same heat now threatening her. That is a psychologically important inference, but it is not independent confirmation.

### 4.3 From alien body to provisional life project

At first, Myne's weak body has little subjective value to Urano. She repeatedly behaves with a degree of recklessness that is easier to understand once the narration admits that death initially does not seem like an unacceptable outcome. A world with no accessible books and a body that fails constantly do not yet feel like a life worth protecting.

The clay-tablet sequence in `粘土板はダメだ` marks the first explicit turn. After producing a complete written story, she realizes that reading and record-making are possible here and thinks that she may be able to live in this world after all. She links her planned “mother's story collection” to a desire to receive the love that previously felt as though it were directed toward someone else. The would-be book is thus not only a substitute for commercial books; it is a **bridge between Urano's old identity and Myne's new relational world**.

The later destruction of the wooden slips and bamboo attempt drives her toward the opposite pole. She becomes willing to let the abnormal heat consume her. Crucially, the reason she first refuses complete surrender is not a book but an unresolved obligation to Lutz: he helped her in advance and she promised to introduce him to Otto. The comic memory of unhandled “black history” from Japan then adds a second reason to resist. At the V01 boundary, this is the clearest evidence that Myne's motivational system is broadening even while books remain dominant.

**V01 claim:** Urano's self-conception remains continuous, but her willingness to invest in *Myne's* life develops through created meaning, reciprocal obligation, and emerging attachment. The precise metaphysics remain OPEN.

## 5. Books as stable desire, regulating object, and source of risk

Books are not merely Myne's hobby. V01 presents them as:

- the most stable continuity between Urano and Myne;
- a central regulator of hope/despair;
- a framework through which she evaluates occupations and institutions;
- a source of practical innovation;
- a cause of tunnel vision and dangerous rule-breaking;
- a basis for exchange with other people;
- a route through which she begins to accept her new family and world.

The prologue establishes the extremity of the trait before reincarnation. Shuu's later account independently confirms it. In the new world, book deprivation converts that stable desire into repeated production attempts.

However, “book obsession” should not be flattened into a single comic trait. Its behavioral consequences vary:

- **constructive:** literacy learning, numerical work, material experimentation, cooking/exchange relationships, willingness to rehabilitate physically, commercial planning;
- **self-endangering:** violating explicit rest instructions on the first forest trip, working until fever, attempting hazardous firing without process knowledge, escalating emotionally after destruction;
- **socially imposing:** recruiting Tuuli, Lutz, Fey, and others into labor; frightening other children during anger; repeatedly creating cleanup or supervision costs for family;
- **relationally integrative:** preserving Effa's stories, helping Lutz pursue his own ambition, finding ways to repay assistance, creating gifts/food that matter to family.

V01 therefore supports a stable drive with mixed effects, not a morally self-interpreting virtue.

## 6. Embodiment, illness, rehabilitation, and practical agency

### 6.1 Two bodily problems must not be collapsed

V01 presents at least two analytically separable constraints:

1. **ordinary physical weakness/deconditioning:** low strength, short stature, poor endurance, inability to carry water, use heavy knives, walk long distances, or sustain ordinary child labor;
2. **the abnormal internal heat:** recurrent episodes whose onset/intensity can correlate with emotion and whose felt behavior is subject to concentration/will in a way Myne recognizes as unlike ordinary illness.

The first improves through training. The second remains a potentially lethal unresolved system problem.

### 6.2 Rehabilitation is real

The volume gives a useful longitudinal progression rather than a static “sickly child” label. Myne initially cannot descend stairs or walk to the gate without severe exhaustion. Over roughly three months of repeated gate trips, better food/hygiene, activity, and deliberate exercise, her recovery interval improves from several days after an outing to increasingly frequent consecutive trips. By late spring/early summer she can reach the forest under supervision and later perform limited gathering.

The change matters because it shows that some of her weakness is trainable and that family restrictions are responsive to observed capacity rather than pure overprotection.

### 6.3 Her remaining limits are economically decisive

Even after improvement, she cannot substitute for Tuuli in ordinary household labor:

- water hauling is vastly slower than Effa's;
- fire-making with flint frightens her and exceeds her practiced competence;
- heavy knives and full-scale butchery are beyond her strength;
- she cannot safely reach or stir large cooking pots without help;
- sustained cleaning or chimney work can still trigger collapse;
- forest work requires pacing and Lutz's supervision.

This is why “knowledgeable” and “useful” cannot be treated as synonyms. Myne's local value is increasingly cognitive/organizational while her environment expects children to contribute substantial embodied labor.

### 6.4 The abnormal heat

`木簡と不思議な熱` supplies the clearest first-person phenomenology. After the destruction of her wooden slips, Myne experiences a heat that seems to surge from inside, numb her limbs, consume consciousness, expand when she yields to despair, and contract when she concentrates or finds a reason to resist. She explicitly distinguishes it from ordinary sickness.

The Lutz side story adds external evidence: anger can coincide with visibly altered/rainbow-like eyes and a frightening pressure reported by Fey.

The Otto epilogue adds a private explanatory hypothesis. Benno calls the possible condition `身食い` and says it is not an ordinary disease but a case in which growing `魔力` consumes the person. He also says magical tools are expensive and generally inaccessible to commoners, and that noble patronage may provide access at the cost of lifelong dependence. Benno explicitly lacks confirmation.

**Frozen V01 state:**

- abnormal heat phenomenon: **DIRECT**;
- externally visible anger anomaly: **DIRECT from Lutz/Fey viewpoint**;
- Benno's `身食い` explanation: **DIRECT as Benno's private belief/testimony**;
- Myne actually having `身食い`: **CORROBORATED INFERENCE / OPEN**, not DIRECT;
- exact relationship between the heat and original Myne's disappearance: **OPEN**.

## 7. Family system: alienation, care, competence, and communication gaps

### 7.1 Family care is structurally central

Myne survives V01 because family members repeatedly supply labor she cannot perform: carrying, water, food, sewing, supervision, medical care at the household level, winter preparation, tool-making, and access to work/social networks. Their care is not an abstract background. It is part of the causal infrastructure that makes every later innovation possible.

### 7.2 Effa

Effa is simultaneously a source of local expertise and a recurring site of epistemic mismatch.

She is skilled in textile work, sewing, food preparation, winter household management, and dye-related labor. Myne can improve or modify some processes, but often only because Effa provides the materials, heavy work, heat, cutting, or local ingredient knowledge.

The destruction of the wooden slips is especially important because it is **not malicious opposition to literacy**. Effa interprets them through the household category available to her: prepared bits of wood separated by a child who has recently begun gathering fuel. She thinks she is honoring Myne's contribution by using them. Myne, meanwhile, has not successfully communicated that they are irreplaceable records. The disaster is therefore a clean example of how shared affection does not eliminate category mismatch or information asymmetry.

### 7.3 Gunther

Gunther's most important V01 function is not comic possessiveness. When Myne despairs at being useless, he reveals that the family's baseline expectation was not productivity but **survival**. They had lived with the possibility that another collapse might kill her; watching her become stronger is already enough.

That statement materially recontextualizes Myne's self-criticism. She judges herself against Tuuli and Urano's adult competence, while Gunther judges her against the possibility of losing a chronically ill child.

Gunther also supplies institutional access through the South Gate and tolerates Otto's literacy training because it creates a plausible work path compatible with Myne's body.

### 7.4 Tuuli

Tuuli is the household competence benchmark Myne repeatedly fails to match. She can carry, gather, use tools, manage younger children, prepare for winter, and later begin a sewing apprenticeship. Her competence initially triggers inherited jealousy from prior Myne's memories, but Urano increasingly interprets it as evidence of skill and care.

The relationship becomes more reciprocal over V01. Myne improves Tuuli's hygiene, makes/stages the baptism hair ornament with family help, styles her hair, provides celebratory food, and tries to give her advantages Myne actually can provide. Tuuli in turn remains a caregiver, supervisor, and bridge to neighborhood norms.

Tuuli's apprenticeship is also an institutional shock to Myne: once Tuuli becomes a recognized working child, Myne can no longer rely on her constant presence. The resulting redistribution of household labor exposes Myne's bodily limits and pushes her toward greater autonomy.

## 8. Myne and Lutz: reciprocity becomes partnership infrastructure

The Lutz relationship is the most important new non-family relationship in V01, but its development should be represented without hindsight or romantic inflation.

### 8.1 Initial exchange logic

Lutz is physically capable, socially embedded, and hungry. Myne has food knowledge, recipes, unusual ideas, and later literacy/access. The parue-cake sequence gives Lutz a concrete reason to value her knowledge: a waste/byproduct can become filling food in a household where older brothers compete for portions.

Lutz later states from his own viewpoint that food is a major reason he helps. This is not evidence that the relationship is shallow. It establishes that reciprocity begins in a materially meaningful need rather than idealized altruism.

### 8.2 Care becomes specialized knowledge

Through repeated gate walks, Lutz learns Myne's safe pace. He becomes better than Gunther at detecting when she is planning to overexert herself and refuses to be deflected by affection or excuses. On the first forest trip, he correctly infers that the supposedly improvised digging attempt was premeditated because she brought a tool.

This creates a distinctive recipient effect: Myne can manipulate or distract some family members more easily than Lutz. He functions as both collaborator and constraint on her self-endangering drive.

### 8.3 Mutual aspiration

Myne's intervention in Lutz's career is not a simple favor. She recognizes that becoming a travel merchant is structurally difficult, asks Otto for a realistic account, arranges a meeting, improves Lutz's presentation, and pushes him to articulate what he actually wants. In doing so, she applies knowledge from her prior-life interview/social experience to a local apprenticeship system.

Lutz, in turn, accepts responsibility for making what Myne conceptualizes. At the Benno meeting, under pressure to name something he can sell/do, he chooses the paper project and states that he will make the things Myne thinks up because her body cannot safely do the work.

By V01 close, the relationship has therefore moved from ad hoc exchange toward an emergent division of labor:

- Myne: concept generation, literacy, calculation, planning, commercial framing, introductions;
- Lutz: strength, field execution, craft attempts, mobility, local social/physical competence;
- shared: willingness to exchange effort for each other's ambitions.

This is not yet a formal business partnership. It is a **credible partnership substrate**.

### 8.4 Obligation as a reason to live

The most consequential relationship evidence appears during Myne's abnormal fever. When she considers letting herself disappear into the heat, she remembers that Lutz provided help in advance and that she still owes him the promised Otto introduction. That obligation becomes a reason to resist annihilation.

This is stronger evidence of relational integration than Myne's explicit affectionate labels because it changes her behavior under an existential limit state.

## 9. Otto, Benno, and the transition from household problem-solving to market coordination

### 9.1 Otto as literacy mentor and opportunistic employer

Otto's role is mutually beneficial from the beginning. He teaches letters and relevant administrative vocabulary, provides a slate, exposes Myne to paperwork, and uses her exceptional arithmetic to reduce his own workload. He recognizes that clerical work may fit a child who cannot perform normal physical labor.

Myne's competence advances from copying/learning toward real administrative judgment. During Otto's absence, she reads a noble introduction, identifies the required authority, places the merchant in an appropriate waiting space, and explains the delay in a way that avoids insulting the visitor. Otto's epilogue later evaluates this response as essentially ideal.

The relationship therefore demonstrates an early labor-market mismatch: Myne experiences herself as nearly useless because she cannot haul water; Otto experiences her as capable of performing a large fraction of valued paperwork.

### 9.2 Otto's key conceptual intervention: labor can be organized

When Myne asks what to do if she knows how to make something but lacks the body to make it, Otto responds that she can employ someone who can. When she says she lacks money, he adds the more manipulative commercial version: arrange incentives so the other person wants to do it voluntarily.

Myne recognizes that Otto himself has used this logic on her. This is a critical cognitive shift. From this point forward, **coordination and incentive design join tools/materials as part of Myne's production system**.

### 9.3 The merchant meeting

The meeting corrects Myne and Lutz's understanding of travel commerce. Otto explains `市民権` as city residence/legal identity tied to temple registration at baptism and relevant to work, marriage, and housing. He describes the difficulty and cost of obtaining it as an outsider. Travel merchants, he says, generally transmit routes/knowledge within mobile families; there is no ordinary apprenticeship slot for a city child, and eventual city settlement/citizenship is itself a common aspiration.

This turns Lutz's romanticized mobility goal into an institutional choice with costs.

Benno then demands a commercial reason to take Lutz on. Myne converts her private paper obsession into a business proposition: a non-animal-skin writing material that could be cheaper than parchment. Benno grants a conditional test—produce a usable prototype by spring before the children's next summer baptism period.

The volume closes this thread not with employment but with **a falsifiable commercial gate**.

### 9.4 Benno's private model of Myne

Benno sees several potential products immediately:

- the hair stick (`簪`);
- the hair-cleaning treatment;
- non-parchment paper;
- an unknown reserve of additional ideas.

His reaction is not benevolent mentorship. He wants to prevent competitors from acquiring her. This is important prospective state: Myne sees Benno as the person who gave them a chance; Benno already sees Myne as potentially market-disruptive intellectual/product capital.

## 10. Knowledge transfer and production: what works, what fails, and why

V01 provides unusually clean evidence against a “modern knowledge solves everything” model.

### 10.1 Successful transfer tends to have embodied or low-dependency support

#### Hair-cleaning mixture

Myne combines locally available oil/herbs/salt in a shampoo-like treatment. The visible result—cleaner, glossier hair—creates adoption pressure. Tuuli and Effa use it; later outsiders notice the difference immediately. The key is not only the formula but demonstrable value with ingredients already in household reach.

#### Basket-making

Urano had actual hands-on craft exposure through her mother. That embodied memory transfers better than vague historical knowledge. She can reproduce weaving technique and even risks looking unnaturally skilled compared with Tuuli.

#### Crochet/lace-like flower ornament

Again, practical prior experience plus local thread/tool fabrication yields a visible product. The ornament attracts strong neighborhood interest and demonstrates that novel craft knowledge can create status/aesthetic value before Myne has any formal commercial position.

#### Cooking

Myne's cooking succeeds most often when she treats local ingredients as ingredients rather than insisting on exact Japanese reproduction: parue cakes/burgers, broth, pomé-based dishes, gnocchi, steaming, and other techniques. Family approval is strong even when Myne herself is dissatisfied because the result is not “Japanese food.”

### 10.2 Failed transfer reveals missing layers

#### Papyrus imitation

Myne remembers the civilizational association and rough appearance, not a reliable scalable process. Labor is enormous and outcome poor. This is remembered concept without sufficient procedural knowledge.

#### Clay tablets

The material is locally obtainable and inscription succeeds, but she lacks firing/process knowledge. Rain destroys one set; firing an inadequately prepared tablet produces an explosion. The concept is correct while production control is not.

#### Wooden/bamboo slips

Technically workable as writing surfaces once soot-pencil succeeds, but they fail socially/logistically: household members classify them as fuel, and peeled bamboo resembles another fuel material. The missing prerequisite is not chemistry but **shared category/ownership signaling and safe storage**.

#### Ink

Commercial ink is far beyond Myne's income. Water plus soot does not bind well. Oil is a scarce household input. She eventually makes a brittle soot/clay writing stick that works adequately, again by accepting a lower-tech substitute rather than reproducing modern ink.

#### Japanese food

The failure is mostly supply-chain and ecological rather than intellectual. Rice, soy sauce, miso, kōji, kombu, bonito, familiar fish, sugar, spices, and reliable fermentation conditions are unavailable or uncertain. She correctly refuses unsafe spontaneous mold cultivation. This is a useful counterexample to reckless innovation: Myne *does* abstain when risk is legible to her.

### 10.3 Local expertise repeatedly outranks abstract memory

Lutz knows where clay-bearing ground is likely to be and can dig it efficiently. Effa knows which household materials are economically unavailable for experiments. Tuuli knows how local plants/foods must be handled. Local children know hunting, forest routes, dangerous animal parts, and tool use. Otto knows employment, citizenship, trade, and administration. Benno knows commercial incentives and noble-linked markets.

The volume's strongest innovation model is therefore **combinatorial**, not protagonist-solitary.

## 11. Literacy, writing materials, and information as class infrastructure

Books are inaccessible to Myne not because “people do not value knowledge” in any simple sense but because writing infrastructure is expensive and stratified.

V01 establishes:

- Myne's home contains no books or ordinary writing materials;
- many city signs communicate pictorially;
- market numerals are more accessible than broad literacy;
- gate soldiers require varying amounts of literacy, with some unable to comfortably read formal documents;
- Otto possesses unusually high literacy because of his merchant background;
- parchment and ink are controlled, expensive administrative materials;
- a single sheet of parchment is reported by Gunther/Otto context as comparable to extremely large household expense, and ink is priced far beyond Myne's child wages;
- nobles, large merchants, officials, and administrative offices are the main writing-material users discussed in V01;
- commoner children can learn on reusable slates rather than disposable paper;
- formal letters of introduction mediate access to noble destinations and require seal/authority handling.

Literacy therefore functions as both **cultural capital and employability infrastructure**. It immediately gives Myne a non-physical form of practical agency that is scarce in her class environment.

The volume also demonstrates the difference between knowing a script and knowing its domain vocabulary. Myne's learning initially focuses on administrative terms useful to Otto; she can read noble-introduction formulas before she can comfortably write ordinary household words. Literacy is task-embedded, not an all-or-nothing trait.

## 12. Baptism, apprenticeship, citizenship, and the social production of adulthood

V01 makes age seven a major institutional boundary.

### 12.1 Baptism

The city collectively celebrates seasonal baptism ceremonies. Children wear white ceremonial clothing, join a procession toward the temple, and are then able to begin apprenticeships. Myne understands this as becoming countable as a member of the city; Otto later gives the stronger institutional formulation that baptism registers the child with the temple as a person of the city and anchors `市民権`.

Nobles are reported to receive clergy at home rather than participating in the same public route. This is evidence of class-differentiated ritual practice, but V01 does not yet provide an internal temple viewpoint.

### 12.2 Apprenticeship and kin/network routing

Work is not chosen from an open anonymous labor market. Parents and relatives typically introduce children into related occupations through trusted networks. Tuuli's sewing apprenticeship is routed through Effa's professional/social connections. Lutz's desire for a career opposed by his family is therefore structurally difficult because he lacks the normal sponsorship chain.

This context explains why Myne's connection to Otto matters far beyond casual advice: an introduction can substitute, imperfectly, for family occupational capital.

### 12.3 Citizenship

Otto's account gives `市民権` concrete consequences for:

- residence;
- identity/status in the city;
- employment;
- marriage;
- housing;
- the cost of settlement for outsiders.

His own biography demonstrates that the institution is not abstract. He spent essentially his accumulated capital to obtain city status so he could remain and marry Corinna, then changed occupations when he could not also finance a shop.

At V01, this is the first strong evidence that legal/social status can redirect life trajectories even when personal skill and money exist.

## 13. Household economy, child labor, and material scarcity

V01's domestic scenes establish a survival economy in which many objects that Urano initially treats as disposable or trivial have competing uses.

Examples:

- ash is used for soap, snow management, dyeing, and sale;
- plant and animal fats are food, hygiene, candle, and soap inputs;
- cloth and thread are expensive enough that sewing competence materially affects household presentation;
- fuel collection is recurring child labor;
- winter preparation requires coordinated processing/preservation and neighborhood labor;
- gathered forest resources materially supplement food and household stores;
- a gifted bird is economically important enough to structure multiple meals;
- Lutz's household food competition makes increased volume from parue residue meaningful, not merely tasty.

This context is essential for evaluating Myne's experiments. A failed test can consume a resource with a real opportunity cost. Her family's resistance is often rational under scarcity even when she experiences it as obstruction.

Children are economically active before formal apprenticeship: gathering, cleaning, food work, supervision, craft practice, and household tasks are normal. Baptism changes their formal work status rather than creating responsibility from nothing.

## 14. Ordinary life and acculturation

V01 repeatedly uses low-stakes behavior to show that Urano has not simply become culturally local because she possesses Myne's memories.

### 14.1 Hygiene and disgust

Myne has strong prior-life standards for bathing, hair, teeth, cleanliness, waste, and food preparation. These produce useful innovations but also disgust toward people and practices around her. She refuses, for example, to promise hair styling for unknown girls partly because she does not want to handle poorly maintained hair.

This matters for character reconstruction: compassion and disgust coexist. Her desire to help is recipient- and cost-dependent.

### 14.2 Meat and food processing

She initially struggles with slaughter, plucking, entrails, and unfamiliar ingredients. Over time she can remain present and work despite revulsion. This is genuine acculturation/skill change, not disappearance of prior sensibility.

### 14.3 Household contribution and self-worth

Myne equates usefulness with performing the labor Tuuli performs and is devastated when she cannot. Other people value different capabilities. The mismatch between **local expected child labor**, **Myne's embodied limits**, and **her emerging cognitive niche** is one of the strongest ordinary-life sources of character development in V01.

## 15. Social perception: Myne is becoming legibly anomalous

By V01 close, Myne's unusualness is no longer contained inside the family.

### 15.1 Family normalization

Gunther sees a sick daughter who is getting healthier. Tuuli sees a strange but beloved younger sister. Effa often interprets Myne's projects using ordinary household categories. Familiarity and affection reduce the likelihood that they will systematize how unusual her behavior is.

### 15.2 Otto's model

Otto notices too-fast literacy acquisition, calculation beyond what her stated learning history explains, adult-like administrative competence, and a large gap between appearance and capability. He estimates privately that she can already handle much of his document work despite limited vocabulary.

### 15.3 Benno's model

Benno adds commercial/social diagnostics: posture, speech, ability to withstand his stare, information withholding, bluff/commitment under uncertainty, and negotiation. He treats these not as “smart child” traits but as behavior inconsistent with an untrained pre-baptism soldier's daughter.

### 15.4 Lutz and Fey

Lutz does not primarily respond with metaphysical suspicion; he classifies Myne as someone whose mind works differently and whose behavior is difficult to predict. Fey's reaction is more explicitly uncanny/fearful after the clay-tablet incident.

**Prospective implication:** visibility risk is increasing. V01 does not yet show institutional consequences, but Myne can no longer assume her differences are invisible.

## 16. World-model evidence and unresolved system claims

### 16.1 Parue

The winter `パルゥ` sequence supplies an early non-Earth regularity. Tuuli reports special gathering conditions, including warming the branches by hand; the tree later changes dramatically around noon and disperses fruit. The household receives and processes the resulting material. The phenomenon is clearly treated by locals as normal enough to exploit.

V01 does not explain the mechanism. Keep it as observed/reported world-model evidence rather than forcing an Earth botanical analogy.

### 16.2 Unfamiliar food behavior

Some ingredients scream, change color, “dance,” or otherwise behave unlike their familiar-looking analogues. Local family members possess practical handling rules. These scenes are evidence that visual resemblance to Earth ingredients is not sufficient for material inference.

### 16.3 `魔石` and small magical creatures

The Lutz side story provides direct local-world testimony/practice around `シュミル`, described as a small `魔獣`. Children hunt them; incorrect disassembly can cause the body to dissolve into dark material when the blade contacts the internal `魔石`; the stone can be sold to a specialist shop. This is the first robust, non-Myne-focalized evidence that “magic-like” categories have ordinary economic consequences even among commoner children.

### 16.4 `魔力` and nobility

In the epilogue, Benno describes `魔力` as a force usually associated with nobles, says some non-nobles possess it, and says magical tools are required to discharge/use it safely in the suspected `身食い` case. Otto is surprised by the commoner-specific detail but not by the existence of magic itself.

These are informed local testimonies, not yet directly demonstrated mechanisms. V01 should therefore distinguish:

- existence of a social category called magic/magic power: strongly supported;
- operational role in governance/nobility: reported local doctrine/common knowledge;
- exact physics/metaphysics: unresolved;
- Myne's condition being magic-linked: plausible but unconfirmed.

## 20. Rival readings and falsification targets

### 20.1 “Myne is simply selfish and uses everyone for books”

There is real support: she deceives, withholds, breaks rest promises, recruits others into labor, and evaluates many opportunities through book access. However, V01 also shows repayment behavior, concern for Tuuli/Lutz, distribution of credit, effort to improve others' outcomes, guilt, and an obligation to Lutz strong enough to motivate survival. The one-dimensional selfishness model is therefore too coarse.

**Would strengthen later if:** she consistently externalizes costs while refusing reciprocal sacrifice when stakes rise.  
**Would weaken later if:** she repeatedly accepts meaningful costs for others independent of book goals.

### 20.2 “Modern knowledge makes Myne functionally adult-superior to locals”

V01 directly undermines this. Her adult memories produce cognitive advantages while her physical incompetence, cultural ignorance, missing procedural knowledge, and resource dependence repeatedly make children and adults around her more competent in the relevant task.

**Would strengthen later if:** imported concepts routinely work without local expertise or institutional adaptation.  
**Would weaken further if:** successful innovations increasingly depend on specialized local collaborators and negotiated institutions.

### 20.3 “The family obstructs Myne because they are backward”

V01 does not support this as a general model. Many restrictions are rational responses to her illness, scarcity, fire/tool risk, and lack of communication. Family members also adopt innovations once benefits are demonstrated.

**Would strengthen later if:** they knowingly reject safe, beneficial changes for status/ideological reasons.  
**Would weaken if:** resistance continues to track concrete risk/resource constraints.

### 20.4 “Lutz is purely altruistic toward Myne”

His own viewpoint rejects the pure version. Food, recipes, ambition, introductions, pride, and reciprocity all matter. This does not negate affection/care; it makes the relationship more causally specific.

### 20.5 “Benno has diagnosed Myne with certainty”

False at V01. His wording and private discussion explicitly retain uncertainty. Treating `身食い` as confirmed would be hindsight collapse.
