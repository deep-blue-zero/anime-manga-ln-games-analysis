# Ascendance of a Bookworm specialist-synthesis router

This directory is the canonical home for cross-volume analytical objects that acquire an **independent synthesis responsibility** beyond sequential readings and cumulative ledgers.

At the pre-V01 boundary, **no specialist synthesis is yet canonical**. The directory exists because the project-initiation architecture must define where mature cross-volume responsibilities converge; it does not assert that every anticipated domain will eventually deserve a separate document.

## Promotion threshold

Create a specialist synthesis when a question:

- recurs across multiple frozen source units;
- draws on more than one cumulative dimension or ledger;
- materially affects later full-series integration;
- has enough evidence and counterevidence to support independent adjudication;
- would otherwise be repeatedly reconstructed from scattered readings;
- cannot be maintained cleanly inside a character monograph, relationship profile, or longitudinal ledger.

Importance alone is insufficient. The artifact must own a stable retrieval/revision responsibility.

## Anticipated domains to test

The architecture currently anticipates, without prejudging their findings or eventual file count:

- institutions, status, class, law/custom, patronage, coercion, and practical autonomy;
- literacy, books, education, knowledge transfer, production, labor, commerce, and diffusion;
- religion, doctrine, magic/system mechanics, metaphysical evidence, and world-model revision;
- identity, names/titles/roles, focalization, information asymmetry, and social recognition;
- family, service, mentorship, friendship, patronage, intimacy, obligation, separation, and network change;
- bodily constraint, illness/risk, competence, resources, ordinary life, and material conditions of agency;
- Japanese prose voice, speech register, titles/forms of address, terminology, and translation-sensitive interpretation;
- part/macrostructure synthesis if the five-part design develops independent analytical responsibility.

Later evidence may combine, narrow, add, or eliminate these responsibilities.

## Part-boundary syntheses

After V03, V07, V12, V21, or V33, a checkpoint may be created when it improves retrieval. Suggested naming:

`BOOKWORM_PARTN_BOUNDARY_SYNTHESIS.md`

A part-boundary synthesis should summarize the model reached at that source boundary, adjudicate material claims/predictions, identify architecture gaps, and preserve rather than replace the underlying volume freezes.

A checkpoint is not mandatory merely because a part ended.

## Dependency rule

A specialist synthesis should normally depend on:

1. the relevant frozen sequential readings;
2. reconciled cumulative state through its declared source boundary;
3. promoted character/relationship work when the specialist's thesis depends materially on those models;
4. adequate evidence routing for its major claims.

Specialist syntheses may inform one another, but circular authority should be avoided: each artifact should make clear which evidence/model responsibilities it consumes and what distinct synthesis responsibility it produces.

## Reasoning class

The default reasoning class for mature cross-volume specialist synthesis is `DEEP_SYNTHESIS`. A propagation-sensitive contradiction or claim-revision artifact may justify `PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST` under the corpus-wide reasoning policy.
