## Related evidence and single source home

The cross-title digital artbook 正反対な君と僕×氷の城壁 is held once with You and I Are Polar Opposites. This project references that shared paratext relationship without duplicating its primary source or making it an admitted narrative witness. It cannot fill missing scenes or merge the two casts/continuities. Adaptation and serialization witnesses are excluded.

Private source IDs, hashes, paths and receipts stay in the governed evidence plane. An authorized operator binds aliases to actual immutable witnesses before use. Provenance records owner-supplied Calibre material and an owner report that almost all ebooks came from Rakuten Kobo; individual purchase receipts were not inspected.
