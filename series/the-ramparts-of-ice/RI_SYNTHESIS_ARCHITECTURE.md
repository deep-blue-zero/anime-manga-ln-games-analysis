## 5. Substantive studies

A person earns a monograph through a distinct developmental question with sufficient state/context/relationship evidence, not fame or appearance count. A directed relationship study needs independent evidence for each side, consequential change and an explanatory question. A small case may remain a bounded section instead of an empty standalone document.

The mature corpus must account for character/causality, relationships, ordinary/contextual behavior, Japanese expression and colour/page form. The colour/form responsibility is mandatory because the supplied edition is the evidence object; it does not require an isolated document if substantial integrated sections adequately explain it. A specialist becomes necessary where independent recurring questions cannot be responsibly absorbed. Role-gap review records exact carrying sections and a reason when separate specialists are unnecessary.

Formal interpretation must connect observed colour/layout features to narrative effect while testing lighting, routine palette, viewpoint and edition-design alternatives. The character argument must retain self-report versus conduct, ordinary versus crisis context, actual development versus new disclosure and counterexamples. An integrated synthesis reconciles these arguments; it cannot simply append a visual appendix to a psychology summary.

No universal word cap applies. Accept depth by preserved causal mechanisms, inferential warrants, significant evidence, competing explanations, names, directionality, temporal state and limits. Compare full transferred versions for lost substance. Ledgers are retrieval apparatus, not miniature substitutes for mature prose.

## 6. Reconciliation and completion gates

Check witness/edition/render basis before interpreting a discrepancy in colour or layout. Then verify source layer, wording, attribution, chronology and frame. Distinguish different context, reader disclosure, self-presentation, translation/edition differences or real inconsistency. Do not “correct” tankobon analysis using an unadmitted scroll/adaptation memory.

Production order: source binding and authorized OPEN run → ordered volume readings and synchronized person/visual/claim state → semantic checkpoints → source completion audit → longitudinal/evidence reconciliation → role-gap/specialist readiness → studies → convergence → full declared-scope synthesis → validation → freeze.

## 7. Reconstruction pathway and evaluation

The [generic reconstruction method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md) §§9–12 and 14–18 are explicitly adopted for model structure, dimension crosswalk and validation. Local constraints below are additional. The present state is NOT_READY; no subject or model exists.

Capture per subject/state: exact volume/witness/scene horizon, situation and stakes, interlocutor, DS speech/DB behavior/NI inner presentation/RB report/OS observer-role/INF inference, source locator, full reading/claim route, Japanese status, counterevidence and limits. Colour/expression is an observation channel, not direct mind access. Ordinary contexts, negative-evidence opportunity and directional conditioning must be represented or identified as gaps.

Keep separate evidence map, operational model and validation artifacts; monographs remain literary interpretation. Bind exact evidence-base commit, source/temporal/continuity boundary, exclusions and version. Separate disposition, developmental state and temporary conditions, with change-cause tags including REVEALED_NOT_NEW.

Rules use trigger + perceived stakes + current state → response family with inhibitors, overrides, exceptions, rival explanations and falsifiers. OBSERVED, CANDIDATE_POLICY and PROMOTED_POLICY require actual support/testing. Appraisal leads to decision objective, speech act, relationship filter, Japanese register and wording. Distinguish private thought from speech and decision fidelity from surface fluency. No acoustic voice is modeled.

Confidence is HIGH/MODERATE/LOW/OPEN; distance D0 attested, D1 near variant, D2 new in-world/state, D3 cross-scenario with explicit chronology/relationship/setting assumptions. Abstain where evidence cannot constrain the response. Shared-artbook or cross-title material cannot supply missing narrative psychology.

Genuine held-out evaluation requires evidence withheld from the model author before fitting, with prior exposure disclosed. Freeze predictions/rule IDs/state/assumptions/confidence/distance/falsifiers before test release. If material was already seen, label RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST; later adjudication cannot restore blindness. Later volumes require authorized admission and state-mismatch checks.

An independent reviewer records diagnostic opportunity, exact observed evidence and SUPPORTED/PARTLY_SUPPORTED/CONTRADICTED/NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY/CONFOUNDED/UNRESOLVED with reasoning. Include ordinary cases, interlocutor/context perturbations, plausible decoys, abstention controls and generic-personality baseline as useful. Report qualitative fidelity and counts/limits rather than unsupported accuracy percentages.

Generated dialogue/scenes/predictions remain synthetic outputs, never source observations or canonical evidence. No simulation is authorized. A model cannot fill unseen pages or use another title's characters as evidence. Coverage maps the twelve evaluator dimensions and six scenario types without self-grading; discovery and capability registries remain designated-agent responsibilities.
