## Edition and reasoning safeguards

A colour/layout inference names the actual witness and links visual instances plus the full argument. A strong association is not direct emotion evidence; preserve alternative accounts. Shared-artbook or adaptation observations cannot silently enter manga claims.

Append material changes with the accepted volume, never automatic confirmations. A warrant summary is retrieval, not replacement prose. Before synthesis reconcile names, knowledge, directionality, witness/form limits and counterexamples. A changed central premise requires dependent argument review, while earlier freezes retain their historical scope.
