## Causal prose and individual behavior

Explain each meaningful movement in connected prose: entering conditions, named actors' perceived stakes/options, choice or constraint, consequence and inferential warrant. Distinguish causes from coincidence, misunderstandings, individual assertions and institutional/customary rules. The title does not establish a psychological metaphor before reading.

Use source-established names within the appropriate disclosure horizon. Preserve deliberate anonymity and aliases, and keep actor/observer/thinker/speaker/recipient clear. Role labels can explain social position but cannot habitually replace known people.

Evidence labels are OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, EVALUATION and UNRESOLVED, with HIGH/MODERATE/LOW/UNRESOLVED confidence. Distinguish thought, spoken account and shown behavior. Another person's account of a motive is their belief until supported independently.

Separate stable disposition candidates, developmental state and temporary conditions. Preserve ordinary activity, routine, humor and low-stakes interaction where present alongside conflict. An absent opportunity is a gap, not negative evidence. Relationships are directed and conditional: desire, recognition, trust, pressure or reciprocity in one direction does not establish the reverse. Explain what changes and what remains asymmetric.

Where source evidence makes intimacy, harm, consent or ethics material, separate behavior, framing, motive, consequence and judgment. Use the minimum detail needed with locators. Neither sympathetic presentation nor later affection establishes endorsement or earlier consent.

## Japanese register and visual colour/form

Inspect Japanese speech/thought/narration, small asides, diegetic writing, effects and ruby separately. Attribute with confidence. Address, self-reference, politeness, endings, omissions and pragmatic purpose need brief source-verified examples, labeled glosses and interlocutor/context comparison. Private thoughts cannot become public speech habits; translations cannot establish Japanese voice.

Read silent pages fully. Analyze panel arrangement, scale, framing, gaze, distance, grouping/isolation, backgrounds, gutters, repeated composition and withheld reaction when consequential. Describe feature → proposed effect → plausible rival account. A face is not unique inner-state evidence, a symbolic rendering is not automatically a literal event, and gutters do not show continuous motion.

Treat colour as an edition-specific graphic channel. Record what colour/tone actually appears, where, under what represented lighting/context and against what local baseline. Test whether a shift aligns with point of view, scene, recurring object, editorial design or routine rendering before interpreting it emotionally or symbolically. A palette resemblance across scenes needs representative exact instances and counterexamples. Do not assert monochrome equivalence or scroll-format effects without another admitted witness.

Confirm facing-page relationships before page-turn/spread claims. Sound/state effects are graphic language, not measured acoustics. No acting, vocal delivery, music or animated motion is admitted.
