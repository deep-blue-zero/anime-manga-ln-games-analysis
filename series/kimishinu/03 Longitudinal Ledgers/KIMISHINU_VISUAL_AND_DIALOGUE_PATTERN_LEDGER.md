## Dialogue patterns

### VD-SHEENA-V01-01 — hesitant internalizing voice
- **Transition:** OPEN
- **Evidence:** recurrent across V01, especially `CH01 p_0011–p_0020`, `CH03 p_0077–p_0088`, `CH04 p_0115–p_0130`.
- **Features:** ellipsis, tentative questions, self-directed uncertainty, polite school register, emotionally important internal monologue.
- **Pragmatics:** more likely to think a strong judgment than state it aggressively to another person.
- **Stress transformation:** narrows toward immediate fear, bodily state, and modest practical goals.
- **Negative constraint:** high-confidence blunt ideological speeches would be out of baseline at V01.

### VD-MIMI-V01-01 — childlike directness
- **Transition:** OPEN
- **Evidence:** `CH01 p_0024–p_0035`; `CH02 p_0043–p_0074`; `CH04 p_0123–p_0130`; `CH05 p_0153–p_0160`.
- **Features:** third-person self-reference `ミミ`; short wants; direct requests; elongated/playful phonology; attention-getting repetition; low social hedging.
- **Representative forms:** `ちょーだい`, `ねーねー`, energetic self-introduction, simple appetite/sleep statements.
- **Pragmatics:** bodily proximity and care are enacted before explanation.
- **Stress transformation:** V01 does not show a wholesale shift into formal/ideological language even around severe injury.
- **Negative constraint:** elaborate political justification for her combat behavior is unsupported.

### VD-FRAN-V01-01 — familiar directive/scolding register
- **Transition:** OPEN

- **Confidence:** medium-high.

## Visual patterns
