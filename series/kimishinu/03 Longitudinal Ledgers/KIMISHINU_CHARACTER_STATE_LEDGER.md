## SHEENA — Totsuki Sheena / トツキ・シーナ

### SHEENA-V01-01 — death non-normalization
- **Transition:** OPEN → current initial model
- **Scope:** V01
- **Evidence:** `CH01 p_0001–p_0020`; `CH03 p_0063, p_0083–p_0100`; `CH04 p_0115–p_0117`; `CH05 p_0137–p_0143`
