## V05 durable distinctions

1. **Fewer casualties != less violence.** Violence may be concentrated in a recoverable body.
2. **Recoverable != disposable.** Mimi's ability to survive changes arithmetic, not moral status.
3. **Protective motive != harmless policy.** Mimi's substitution is caring and self-erasing at the same time.
4. **Ordinary weakness != worthlessness.** Non-combat capacities have direct relational value.
5. **Care for survival != authority over another's life plan.** Reciprocal agency is now part of personhood.
6. **Visibility of injury matters.** Concealed cost enables instrumental normalization.
7. **Sacrifice can become self-reinforcing social expectation.** Successful substitution generates demand for more substitution.
