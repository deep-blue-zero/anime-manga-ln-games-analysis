## Relationship-comparison model at V03

V03 establishes two different attachment-to-war transformations:

**Sheena -> Mimi:**  
`care -> injury remains morally salient -> waiting/return -> possible restraint/reassurance pressure`

**Seiran -> Ari:**  
`care -> Ari's injury becomes intolerable -> self chosen as substitute -> more fighting/self-risk`

**Ari -> Seiran:**  
`care -> affirm chosen strength + preserve moral humanity + secretly wish to stop departure`

The corpus must therefore avoid any one-direction rule that intimacy simply makes characters more pacifist or more sacrificial. Relationship state modifies the target and weighting of risk.
