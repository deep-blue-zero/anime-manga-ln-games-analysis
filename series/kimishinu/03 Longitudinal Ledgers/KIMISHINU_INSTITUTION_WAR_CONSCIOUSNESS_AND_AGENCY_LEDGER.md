## INST-V01-01 — dual orphanage / weapon-training institution
- **Transition:** OPEN
- **Evidence:** `CH01 p_0005–p_0009`
- **Fact:** the facility is an orphanage and magical military-weapon training institution. Children roughly ten to seventeen live/study there.
- **Fact:** innate magic is explicitly trained for the capacity to kill people.
- **Confidence:** high.

## INST-V01-02 — normalization language
- **Transition:** OPEN
- **Evidence:** `CH01 p_0005–p_0011`; `CH03 p_0083–p_0091`; `CH05 p_0140–p_0143`.
- **Pattern:** death announcement -> exhortation to continue training; abrupt mock-battle scheduling; real deployment summons; prayer after casualties.
- **Model:** the institution converts war participation into school rhythm rather than treating it as a separate emergency mode.
- **Confidence:** high.

## INST-V01-03 — student knowledge is operational, not geopolitical
- **Transition:** OPEN
- **Evidence:** V01 as a whole.
- **Known by students:** combat occurs; enemies can kill them; certain training/medical procedures; summons; casualties; Mimi rumors.
- **Not established:** developed understanding of war aims, enemy politics, state ideology, diplomacy, strategic history.
- **Rule:** retain bottom-up perspective; do not manufacture macro-politics.

## INST-V01-04 — mock battle as dangerous institutional training
- **Transition:** OPEN
- **Evidence:** `CH03 p_0083–p_0100`; `CH04 p_0103–p_0117`.
- **Fact:** approximately one hundred targets are prepared; ordinary expectation is around three per student; severe injury is possible.

## INST-V01-05 — real deployment attrition
