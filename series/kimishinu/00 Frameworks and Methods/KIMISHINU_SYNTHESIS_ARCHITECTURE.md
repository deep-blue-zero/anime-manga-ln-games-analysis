# KIMISHINU_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md

## 1. Purpose, authority, and architectural status

This document is the canonical **synthesis/corpus architecture** for the current KimiShinu analytical project. It governs what the already-completed sequential readings and longitudinal infrastructure are building toward, how specialist authorities feed final integration, how evidence and claim revision route, what temporal distinctions must remain intact, and what gates must be satisfied before the current-publication corpus can be treated as fully synthesized.

It is deliberately distinct from `KIMISHINU_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md`.

- The analytical method governs **how source units are read**.
- `KIMISHINU_LONGITUDINAL_LEDGER_ARCHITECTURE.md` governs **what cumulative state is preserved while reading**.
- This architecture governs **how the preserved state converges into mature specialist and full-series authority**.

The project predates the corpus-wide `MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md`. It is therefore a grandfathered mature project rather than a newly initiated one. The absence of a dedicated synthesis architecture during V01-V09 does not invalidate the completed work because the project already accumulated strong prospective deep readings, five differentiated ledgers, canonical specialist syntheses, and a role-gap audit. The purpose of this document is to repair the genuinely missing governance layer **without restructuring the mature corpus or inventing retrospective work for symmetry**.

### 1.1 Current architectural boundary

At the time this architecture is established:

- numbered mainline deep reading is complete and frozen through V09;
- the V05 special booklet is complete/frozen supplemental evidence;
- the standalone Side Stories collection SS01-SS06 is complete/frozen supplemental evidence;
- the integrated analytical boundary is `V09+SIDE_STORIES`;

### 1.2 Architecture lifecycle state

This architecture begins at **STABILIZED**, not INITIAL.

That status is justified because the currently available source corpus has already been read sequentially, the mature analytical dimensions are known, longitudinal evidence has accumulated, and the pre-full-series specialist role-gap audit has already tested the corpus for missing responsibilities.

`STABILIZED` does not mean immutable. This architecture may still be revised if a later numbered volume, new canonical source class, material contradiction, or full-series validation failure exposes a genuinely new responsibility.

### 1.3 No mandatory retrospective reread

Creating this architecture does **not** trigger a V01-V09 reread.

The existing deep readings and ledgers already preserve the dimensions the architecture needs: character state, relationships, mortality/personhood, institution/war/agency, and visual/dialogue form. Specialist synthesis has already tested those layers against the mature boundary.

Targeted primary-source reinspection remains permitted and expected when exact Japanese wording, page composition, locator verification, chronology, or a contested claim requires it. That is evidence verification, not an architectural backfill project.

# 2. Architectural thesis

KimiShinu should converge through a **compact layered architecture**, not through one giant document that tries to reproduce every character model, every relationship policy, every ledger transition, and every page-level observation.

The canonical analytical stack is:

`Japanese manga source`

`-> prospective sequential deep readings`

`-> five longitudinal ledgers`

`-> specialist character / relationship / institutional syntheses`

`-> full-series integrative synthesis`

`-> validation / release audit`

The responsibilities are intentionally non-identical.

- **Sequential readings** preserve source-local chronology, prospective uncertainty, page-level interpretation, and volume-specific evidence.
- **Ledgers** preserve cumulative state transitions and cross-volume evidence without rewriting every volume.
- **Specialist syntheses** convert dense recurring domains into canonical models with their own internal logic.
- **Full-series synthesis** integrates across those specialist systems to answer questions that no single specialist can answer alone.
- **Validation/release audit** tests authority, temporal integrity, evidence traceability, contradiction handling, and completion before the synthesis is treated as the current reader-facing whole-corpus authority.

The governing anti-duplication rule is:

> **The full-series synthesis should integrate specialist authorities, not paraphrase them in full.**

Detailed behavioral-policy reconstruction remains in character and relationship monographs. Detailed chronological state transitions remain in ledgers. Exact volume-local evidence remains in the deep readings. The full-series synthesis should quote or summarize only what is required to support genuinely cross-domain conclusions.

# 3. Source model and continuity

## 3.1 Primary continuity

The present architecture governs one primary Japanese manga continuity.

Main numbered source:

- V01-V09 Japanese tankobon/digital fixed-layout manga.

Canonical supplemental source classes already inside the project boundary:

- V02/V04/V07 drawn bonus material where included in the corresponding deep readings;
- V03 in-volume extra narrative;
- V05 special booklet;
- V08 in-volume supplemental narratives;
- standalone Side Stories SS01-SS06.

The project does not presently integrate an anime adaptation, translation edition, external interview corpus, fandom interpretation layer, or creator-commentary layer into this analytical generation.

## 3.2 Source-language precedence

Original Japanese manga pages remain primary evidence.

For exact dialogue, pronouns, address terms, sentence endings, ambiguity, handwritten text, sound effects, or visual-text relationships, the original Japanese page outranks paraphrase in a synthesis or ledger.

Translated wording may be supplied for reader comprehension but should not become evidence independent of the Japanese source.

## 3.3 Mainline versus supplemental evidence

Supplemental material is canonical evidence but retains a distinct epistemic role.

The architecture preserves:

`numbered prospective history != mature integrated interpretation`.

A Side Story or bonus narrative may securely deepen a pre-existing relationship, reveal an earlier event, or refine a mature character model. It may **not** be used to rewrite what a prospective V01-V09 reader was entitled to know before that supplemental source was opened.

The current project therefore carries two simultaneous boundaries:

- `numbered_prospective_boundary: V09`
- `integrated_boundary: V09+SIDE_STORIES`

Both must remain visible in full-series synthesis metadata and in any claim whose force depends on retrospective knowledge.

## 3.4 Chronology uncertainty

If supplemental material can be placed only within a range rather than at an exact chapter boundary, preserve the range.

Do not invent exact chronological placement merely to make the final synthesis cleaner.

Use formulations such as:

- securely pre-V03;
- after CH23 reconciliation but exact order relative to CH24 unresolved;
- retrospective pre-death evidence;
- exact internal placement OPEN.

## 3.5 Future numbered releases

A new numbered KimiShinu volume immediately becomes the highest-priority source operation.

When a new volume appears:

1. do not continue retrospective full-series drafting past the new source boundary as though V09 were still current;
2. resume prospective reading from the frozen V09 state;
3. update affected ledgers;
4. revise specialist authorities only where the new evidence materially changes their responsibility;
5. perform a focused architecture impact check;
6. only then refresh or supersede the full-series synthesis as appropriate.

A future volume does not require a parallel corpus or a new root.

# 6. Sequential-reading architectural contract

The V01-V09 sequential pass is complete, but this architecture governs future volumes as well.

A future numbered volume is not architecturally complete merely when `KIMISHINU_VNN_DEEP_READING.md` exists.

A completed future sequential operation must:

1. verify the source object and source lock;
2. read prospectively from the prior numbered freeze;
3. produce the deep-reading artifact;
4. adjudicate any genuinely diagnostic frozen expectations;
5. update every materially affected ledger;
6. preserve claim transitions using `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT`, or `OPEN`;
7. identify whether any specialist authority now requires amendment or a new generation;
8. identify whether this architecture requires revision;
9. update `CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md`;
10. update the master Drive index without overwriting concurrent corpus work.

A supplemental source follows the same principle but may not move the numbered prospective boundary unless it is itself a numbered mainline source.

# 7. Longitudinal infrastructure

## 7.1 The five-ledger design remains canonical

No sixth ledger is created by this architecture.

The present five-ledger system already covers the recurring causal dimensions required for full-series synthesis and character reconstruction. Creating additional ledgers merely because the new corpus-wide policy lists examples would add administrative duplication rather than analytical value.

## 7.2 No mandatory dedicated claim-revision ledger at V1

A separate `KIMISHINU_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md` is **not currently required**.

Reason:

- claim transitions already live in deep readings and cumulative ledgers;
- major mature claims have already been stabilized through specialist synthesis;
- the role-gap audit did not identify revision retrieval as a blocking problem;
- the current corpus has one main continuity and a manageable source size.

Create a dedicated claim-revision ledger only if future releases or a V2 reread produce enough cross-artifact revisions that the current transition history becomes difficult to recover deterministically.

## 7.3 No mandatory evidence matrix or locator index at V1

A separate evidence matrix or locator index is also **not a precondition** for the first full-series synthesis.

The existing chain is sufficient:

`full-series claim -> specialist/ledger -> sequential deep reading -> Japanese page`.

If the full-series validation audit finds that important mature claims cannot be traced efficiently, create the narrowest evidence artifact needed at that point rather than creating a broad index speculatively.

## 7.4 Mutable versus frozen state

Mutable while active:

- `CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md`;
- the five ledgers;
- this architecture while `STABILIZED` and not release-locked.

Frozen or historically stable:

- completed prospective V01-V09 deep readings;
- completed supplemental deep readings;
- canonical specialist syntheses unless superseded by a later generation;
- completed audits once their decision boundary is fixed.

Do not silently rewrite a frozen prospective reading because mature interpretation changed.

A character or relationship earns a separate canonical specialist home when it has:

- meaningful evidence across multiple source units or a major before/after state transition;
- interacting behavioral policies or contradictions that would be flattened in a full-series paragraph;

# 10. The full-series synthesis object

## 10.1 Canonical target

Primary target filename:

`KIMISHINU_FULL_SERIES_SYNTHESIS.md`

Canonical home when created:

`05 Full-Series Synthesis`

The folder should be created only when the synthesis itself is created. Do not create an empty `05` folder for symmetry.

## 10.2 Current-publication meaning of “full series”

The manga is still treated as an active project. Therefore `FULL_SERIES_SYNTHESIS` means:

> **full integration of the currently completed canonical source boundary**, not an assertion that the publication is permanently finished.

Its front matter must state at least:

- `series: KIMISHINU`
- `artifact_type: full_series_synthesis`
- `generation: V1`

- `numbered_prospective_boundary: V09`
- `supersedes`
- `superseded_by`
- `do_not_use_as_current_authority`

## 10.3 What the full-series synthesis must do

The final synthesis should answer questions that require several existing authorities at once.

Examples:

- How does intimate attachment alter the meaning of danger without simply making characters safer?
- How do recoverability, pain, mortality, and military utility interact?
- How does the series distinguish being useful from being disposable?
- How do Sheena, Mimi, Haru, Ari, and Seiran represent different forms of agency under constraint?
- How can care expand another person's life while also becoming controlling, self-sacrificial, or institutionally complicit?
- What does ordinary life do analytically inside a war-school setting?
- How do resurrection, aging, memory, grief, and futurity change what “always” can mean?
- How do adults humanize a coercive system without necessarily undoing it?
- What does the manga page itself contribute through touch, distance, ordinary/combat juxtaposition, negative space, visual silence, and return/home imagery?
- What does the source actually establish about the war, and what does it deliberately or simply leave outside the children's horizon?

## 10.4 What the full-series synthesis must not do

It must not:

- silently replace specialist monographs;
- replay nine volume summaries sequentially;
- erase prospective uncertainty by writing mature truths backward;
- treat supplemental evidence as though it had been available to the V01-V09 prospective reader;
- invent post-V03 living Seiran behavior;
- assign Haru a categorical gender identity or sexual orientation the source has not supplied;
- infer that Fran or Omi possess policy sovereignty the manga has not established;
- equate adult care with structural innocence;
- equate institutional participation with absence of personal care;
- equate Mimi's recoverability with painless invulnerability or complete indifference to injury;
- treat Sheena's maturation as simple fearlessness;
- manufacture LOGH/86-style macro-politics from sparse strategic evidence;
- use an external adaptation or fandom framing as evidence without explicitly extending the source boundary;
- create a separate “final thoughts” artifact that duplicates the full synthesis.

# 12. Evidence routing and locator responsibility

## 12.1 Default escalation path

For a mature full-series claim:

`full-series synthesis`

`-> relevant specialist synthesis and/or ledger`

`-> sequential deep reading`

`-> original Japanese page`.

Use the narrowest layer that can verify the claim.

## 12.2 Exact Japanese wording

If a full-series argument turns on wording, preserve the Japanese line and verify it against the primary page or a deep reading that already records it accurately.

Do not quote a remembered English gloss as though it were source-language evidence.

## 12.3 Visual claims

Any material claim about panel composition, gaze, body distance, negative space, page-turn structure, visual silence, or recurring motif should route to:

- the Visual & Dialogue Pattern Ledger when the pattern is longitudinal;
- the relevant volume deep reading for scene context;
- the source page for final verification when the formal claim is central.

## 12.4 Specialist-derived claims

The full synthesis may rely on a canonical specialist for a mature formulation, but if that formulation becomes a **load-bearing series-level thesis**, verify that its source spine remains traceable through ledgers/deep readings.

## 12.5 Evidence-crosswalk trigger

Create a dedicated `CROSSWALK`, `EVIDENCE_MATRIX`, or `LOCATOR_INDEX` only if one of the following becomes true:

- the validation audit cannot efficiently trace multiple load-bearing claims;
- the full synthesis introduces many cross-domain claims whose evidence is distributed across several specialists;
- later releases create competing generations that make retrieval ambiguous;
- exact source-language/visual locators become too costly to recover ad hoc.

No such artifact is mandatory at architecture revision 1.0.

# 13. Claim revision, contradiction, and supersession

## 13.1 Transition vocabulary

Continue using:

- `PRESERVE`
- `STRENGTHEN`
- `REVISE`
- `DOWNGRADE`
- `REJECT`
- `OPEN`

## 13.2 Do not resolve contradictions silently in the full synthesis

If two canonical inputs appear to conflict:

1. identify whether the conflict is actually temporal state change;
2. check prospective versus mature knowledge state;
3. check whether one source is supplemental retrospective evidence;
4. check whether one statement is character belief and another is reader-level fact;
5. inspect the relevant deep reading and primary page;
6. determine whether a canonical topical home requires revision;
7. only then integrate the current formulation.

The full synthesis should not become a hidden repair layer where earlier authorities are silently overwritten.

## 13.3 Common KimiShinu contradiction classes

### Prospective versus retrospective

Example pattern:

`romantic status OPEN at early boundary -> later explicit confirmation`.

Both historical states can be correct.

### Living versus posthumous

Ari-Seiran requires a hard distinction between:

- living reciprocal behavior through V03/secure retrospective pre-death evidence;
- Ari's V04+ posthumous relationship state.

### Capability versus personhood

Statements such as “Mimi can recover” do not entail “injury is irrelevant” or “Mimi is ethically disposable.”

### Choice language versus structural freedom

A character can make a meaningful choice inside a constrained menu. Adult respect for local choice does not prove structural autonomy.

### Presentation versus categorical identity

Haru's clothing, pronoun, hairstyle, tolerated address, or future attraction questions do not independently settle a modern identity label.

## 13.4 Supersession behavior

If full-series drafting exposes an outdated specialist formulation:

- update or supersede the specialist first if the change is material to that specialist's semantic responsibility;
- record the transition;
- then use the corrected authority in full integration.

Do not preserve a known-wrong specialist merely because full-series drafting has begun.

# 14. Temporal, developmental, and epistemic state rules

The architecture requires four simultaneous state dimensions.

## 14.1 Source-time state

What is true or knowable at V01, V02, ... V09?

## 14.2 Mature integrated state

What is the best current interpretation after `V09+SIDE_STORIES`?

## 14.3 Character knowledge state

What does a particular character know, believe, misunderstand, conceal, or infer at that time?

## 14.4 Evidence-publication state

Was an insight available in mainline sequence, an in-volume bonus, V05 booklet, or standalone Side Story opened only after V09?

The full synthesis may write from mature integrated authority, but whenever a conclusion depends on later clarification it should avoid language implying that the earlier state was always transparent.

### 14.5 Character development is not trait replacement

Where possible, preserve chains such as:

`earlier regulatory policy -> pressure/relationship change -> revised policy`.

Examples already stabilized in specialists include:

- Sheena: helplessness avoidance -> bounded action while fear remains;
- Mimi: recoverability-conditioned self-expenditure -> relational cost becomes more salient without proving generalized tactical caution;
- Haru: compensatory competence -> sustainable self-authored viability;
- Ari: composure-as-loyalty -> grief permission -> posthumous non-ownership -> later self-authored divergence.

The full synthesis should preserve development without pretending early states were simply false personalities.

- supplemental evidence does not rewrite prospective certainty;

- every load-bearing series-level claim routes to specialist/ledger/deep-reading evidence;

- character names and romanization stable;
- source-boundary language consistent;
- authority states consistent with corpus map;

- no stale “next operation” survives inside the release state.

- the full synthesis integrates rather than reproduces specialist documents;
