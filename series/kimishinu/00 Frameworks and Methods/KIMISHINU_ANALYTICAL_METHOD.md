\# KIMISHINU\_ANALYTICAL\_METHOD.md

\#\# Purpose

This document governs the prospective deep-reading pass for Aono Nachi's きみが死ぬまで恋をしたい. The project treats the Japanese manga page as the primary analytical object and is designed for sequential reading from V01 forward without importing later-volume knowledge into earlier readings.

The method is intentionally proportional to the corpus. It does not copy the architecture of larger projects merely for symmetry. Its central analytical problem is the interaction of intimate attachment, institutionalized warfare, constrained agency, mortality, expendability, and manga visual form.

\#\# Source priority

1\. Original Japanese tankobon pages are primary evidence.  
2\. Tankobon navigation and metadata are structural aids, not substitutes for page reading.  
3\. V05 special booklet and Side Stories are supplemental canonical evidence and must remain separately labeled from mainline chronology.  
4\. External summaries, adaptations, reviews, interviews, and fandom material are excluded from the sequential deep-reading evidentiary layer unless a later specialist study explicitly introduces them.  
5\. OCR is a last-resort aid only. Japanese dialogue, narration, furigana, handwritten text, SFX, and visual placement should be read from the page itself whenever legible.

\#\# Prospective-reading rule

Each numbered volume is read at the knowledge boundary established by the immediately preceding completed volume.

Before opening the next volume analytically:  
\- freeze the prior volume's character-state, relationship-state, institutional, mortality, and thematic claims;  
\- record genuinely diagnostic predictions or open questions only when they arise naturally from evidence;  
\- do not revise prior notes silently after later information arrives;  
\- route changed interpretations through PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT, or OPEN transitions.

Later volumes may explain earlier ambiguity, but they do not retroactively erase what an attentive reader could and could not know at the earlier boundary.

\#\# Reading unit and locators

The main reading unit is the tankobon volume. Within a volume, chapter boundaries should be recovered from EPUB navigation when available and checked against the page sequence.

Preferred locator grammar:  
\`VNN / CHNN / EPUB-image-or-page-position\`

If printed page numbers are present and stable, record them secondarily. Never invent printed pagination where the digital edition does not expose it.

\#\# Required analytical passes

\#\#\# 1\. Object verification  
Confirm filename, volume identity, Japanese language, page count/image count, chapter navigation, bonus pages, and obvious packaging anomalies before analysis.

\#\#\# 2\. First-order narrative reading  
Establish what happens, who knows what, who acts, what changes, and which uncertainties remain unresolved. Avoid thematic inflation before the event structure is stable.

\#\#\# 3\. Japanese dialogue and voice reading  
Track character-specific diction, pronouns, address terms, sentence endings, politeness shifts, ellipsis, hesitation, repetition, bluntness, evasiveness, joking register, and emotionally diagnostic departures from baseline.

For important lines, preserve the Japanese wording and provide only as much translation/gloss as needed to support the analytical claim.

\#\#\# 4\. Manga-form reading  
Treat page design as evidence. Track panel size, page turns, negative space, gaze direction, bodily distance, touch, framing, repeated compositions, background suppression, screentone, visual silence, SFX placement, and transitions between domestic/school space and combat/death imagery.

Do not reduce visual evidence to plot illustration. Ask what the page makes the reader experience that a prose transcript would lose.

\#\#\# 5\. Character-state reconstruction  
For every materially affected character, distinguish:  
\- baseline temperament;  
\- current emotional state;  
\- beliefs and misbeliefs;  
\- desires and aversions;  
\- coping behavior;  
\- attachment strategy;  
\- conflict behavior;  
\- relationship-specific behavior;  
\- self-conception;  
\- degree and form of agency;  
\- speech/behavioral invariants and exceptions.

Do not infer a stable trait from a single crisis behavior unless repeated evidence supports it.

\#\#\# 6\. Relationship and intimacy reading  
Track relationship change as a state transition rather than a shipping verdict. Relevant dimensions include trust, disclosure, dependency, reciprocity, jealousy, caretaking, physical proximity, exclusivity, rupture/repair, promises, future orientation, and whether intimacy expands or constrains each person's agency.

Because the central romance is explicit, analytical effort should focus on how love functions rather than repeatedly proving that romantic coding exists.

\#\#\# 7\. Mortality and expendability reading  
Track how characters understand death, injury, return, survivability, replacement, sacrifice, and the possibility or impossibility of a shared future. Distinguish ordinary fear of death from habituation, dissociation, fatalism, instrumental self-conception, and institutionally imposed expendability.

Mimi's anomalous relationship to death must not be generalized into an interpretation until the manga establishes its rules and her own understanding of them.

\#\#\# 8\. Institution and war-consciousness reading  
Track the school and war primarily at the level the manga supplies. Record:  
\- what students are told;  
\- what they know or do not know about the war;  
\- how deployment is normalized;  
\- how duty is taught or assumed;  
\- how discipline, training, punishment, rewards, and peer norms operate;  
\- how orphans/children are rendered militarily usable;  
\- evidence of resistance, adaptation, normalization, or endorsement.

Do not force 86- or LOGH-style macro-political analysis onto a source that does not provide it. If later volumes introduce substantial state, ideological, strategic, or geopolitical material, expand the architecture then.

\#\#\# 9\. Ethical/agency reading  
Distinguish external freedom from moral agency. A character may be institutionally trapped yet retain meaningful choices about care, truth, refusal, loyalty, violence, or self-definition.

Where useful, distinguish coercion \-\> adaptation \-\> normalization \-\> endorsement rather than treating participation in the system as a single ethical state.

\#\#\# 10\. Volume synthesis and forward freeze  
Close each volume with:  
\- major state changes;  
\- relationship transitions;  
\- institutional/mortality developments;  
\- visual-form findings that recur or intensify;  
\- claims strengthened or weakened;  
\- unresolved questions;  
\- only genuinely diagnostic prospective expectations for the next boundary.

\#\# Evidence discipline

Separate:  
\- FACT: directly observable source content;  
\- INFERENCE: interpretation strongly supported by source pattern;  
\- HYPOTHESIS: plausible but underdetermined model;  
\- VALUE JUDGMENT: ethical or aesthetic evaluation.

Use cautious wording where the manga is intentionally withholding information. Absence of explanation is not evidence of absence.

\#\# Supplemental-material protocol

\#\#\# V05 special booklet  
Read after V05 unless internal chronology clearly requires a narrower placement. Mark every extracted claim as supplemental evidence.

\#\#\# Side Stories  
Do not open prospectively until the mainline reading reaches the latest main-series point needed to avoid chronology contamination. Before analysis, inspect the collection's internal chronology and publication framing. Route each story to the relevant character/relationship state while preserving its supplemental status.

\#\# Deep-reading output contract

Sequential files should use:  
\`KIMISHINU\_VNN\_DEEP\_READING.md\`

Each file should contain authority front matter, source verification, chapter map, narrative reading, Japanese dialogue/voice evidence, visual-form analysis, character-state changes, relationship changes, institution/war-consciousness evidence, mortality/expendability evidence, claim transitions, ledger updates, unresolved questions, and prospective freeze.

\#\# Method-evolution rule

Modify this method only when recurring evidence demonstrates that a missing analytical dimension spans multiple volumes or materially improves later synthesis. Record material method changes in the current corpus map. Do not create new ledgers merely because a theme appears once.
