## V06 freeze - governing thesis

V06 is a disclosure/reclassification volume. It converts several hidden, ambiguous, or externally legible states into directly usable evidence without flattening them into premature endpoints.

The V05 transparent-return agreement is behaviorally confirmed: when Mimi returns seriously injured, Sheena encounters the real damaged body in medical space and uses healing magic rather than receiving a cleaned-up performance. This is a transparency change, not evidence of tactical caution. Mimi's protective-substitution logic remains active, and the corpus must keep those two variables separate.

The volume also supplies the first real causal account of the war. A powerful grimoire containing forbidden magic, including resurrection magic, is held and protected by the country; its existence leaked outside; enemies seek to obtain it; and the children are largely denied this causal knowledge. Authority rhetoric now explicitly invokes national defense and victory. The macro-politics gate therefore changes **CLOSED -> AJAR / ACTIVE WATCH**. This is enough to track recurring political-war evidence more actively, but still not enough to justify a standalone political-warfare ledger.

On the relationship side, Sheena's attraction crosses another evidentiary threshold. Jealousy/exclusivity, the desire to be Mimi's number one, a sustained kiss explicitly distinguished from magic, and the final confrontation with the category `lover` make a friendship-only model untenable. Formal mutual dating/confession nevertheless remains OPEN at V06.

V06 also deepens mortality through memory: Mimi's long life has eroded clear autobiographical memory of her mother while sensory traces remain. Immortality therefore threatens not only endless injury and repeated bereavement, but gradual loss of personal detail. Sheena answers that asymmetry with finite memory-making rather than a fantasy of permanence.
