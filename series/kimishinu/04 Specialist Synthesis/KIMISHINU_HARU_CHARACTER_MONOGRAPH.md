# KIMISHINU_HARU_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md

## 1. Purpose and authority

This is the canonical current character monograph for **Haru / ハル** at the KimiShinu analytical boundary `V09+SIDE_STORIES`.

Its responsibility is to reconstruct Haru as an individual behavioral system rather than treating Haru as a bundle of secondary-character functions: “the sickly classmate,” “the blunt romance adviser,” “the student in the boys' uniform,” or “Claire's crush.” All of those descriptions touch real evidence. None is sufficient by itself.

The mature Haru model is organized by one recurring problem:

> **How can Haru remain the author of a life when body, institution, comparison, social expectation, and other people's interpretations all constrain the available field?**

That question appears in different forms across V04-V09 and the Side Stories:

- a chronically weak body limits practical participation;
- academic effort becomes compensation for falling behind;
- stronger or healthier peers activate comparison and inferiority ranking;
- exceptional ability does not justify entitlement over another person's body;
- clothing and presentation become sites of self-choice after earlier gendered sorting;
- future work becomes a problem of material self-support;
- help is acceptable when it expands viable options but threatening when it authors Haru's future from outside;
- romantic possibility becomes imaginable before partner gender, orientation, or final identity category is settled;
- forms of address such as `ハルくん` can be tolerated casually when they do not seize authorship;
- by V09, bodily limits are not defeated but incorporated into a more sustainable agency policy.

The strongest stable principle is therefore **self-authorship under constraint**, not independence-as-isolation and not resistance to all outside influence.

### Source-boundary rule

The numbered prospective boundary remains V09. The standalone Side Stories are supplemental/retrospective evidence and refine the mature model without changing what was knowable during the frozen V04-V09 sequential reads.

### Identity-boundary rule

This monograph deliberately preserves an unresolved source state.

The current corpus establishes that Haru:

- uses `ボク`;
- chooses short hair and the boys' uniform / trousers;
- has a history of resenting externally imposed gendered sorting;
- values the realization `自分で選べるんだ`;
- wonders whether a future loved person might be male or female;
- explicitly struggles with `自分が何者なのか`;
- tolerates Claire's `ハルくん` when asked;
- does **not** supply a final self-chosen modern gender identity or sexual-orientation label at the current boundary.

The simulation-ready model must therefore represent the **behavioral meaning of those facts** while keeping categorical outcome open.

# 2. Executive character thesis — authorship under constraint

Haru is best understood as a character whose development moves from **compensation for constrained capacity** toward **authorship of a viable life that can include limits, help, uncertainty, and attachment without becoming controlled by them**.

The earliest visible problem is bodily.

Haru is frequently ill, develops fever/cough, spends substantial time in the infirmary, and cannot participate in practical training as consistently as peers. In the school's environment, this is not merely an inconvenience. Practical capacity has social and institutional value. Children are trained for combat, competence is visible, and being unable to keep pace can mean being positioned as less useful.

Haru initially compensates in the domain still available: study.

`bodily limitation -> missed practical participation -> fear/risk of falling behind -> intensified academic effort`.

This is not yet a complete autonomy philosophy. In V04, the deep reading correctly leaves several motives open: pride, belonging, fear of exclusion, fear of dependence, or some combination. What later volumes clarify is that **comparison** is a major regulator.

By V06, Haru can name the tendency to organize differences through superiority and inferiority. Mimi becomes interesting to Haru because Mimi does not seem to look down on people weaker than herself. Haru admires precisely the trait that exposes Haru's own painful ranking habit.

That produces an important distinction:

> Haru is comparison-sensitive without being hierarchy-committed.

Haru may automatically notice who is stronger, healthier, more capable, or better positioned. But Haru does not conclude that the stronger person owns the weaker person's body or that capability creates moral entitlement. When classmates casually speak as though Mimi should function as their shield or absorb death in their place, Haru objects directly.

This is a major personhood signal.

Haru's own experience of being disadvantaged by the body does **not** become a claim that exceptionally advantaged bodies are fair game for others.

The same architecture later appears in questions of presentation and identity.

V08's `ゆらめき` establishes that Haru has a history of being sorted by others through gendered expectations. The wound is not simply “Haru dislikes feminine clothes.” The important complaint is externally imposed classification: other people decide which category Haru belongs to and then use that decision to constrain what Haru should wear or be.

The discovery `自分で選べるんだ` therefore matters more than any individual garment.

Haru's boys' uniform, short hair, and `ボク` belong to a self-authorship history. They are meaningful. They are not a license for an external analyst to finish the identity question that Haru explicitly has not finished.

The same principle governs work.

In V08, Haru says `一人で 生きていけるように なりたい`. Abstracted carelessly, that can sound like a rejection of dependence. The surrounding evidence instead shows a material goal: Haru wants enough capacity, work, and resources to sustain a life that is not wholly determined by a weak body or another person's protection.

Friends can suggest indoor or home-based work. Haru does not reject the suggestions merely because they come from someone else. The key variable is whether help **expands the option set** or **takes authorship away**.

That distinction becomes behaviorally explicit in V09.

Haru attends more classes, keeps the self-support goal, plans to stop before physical collapse, accepts a peer's hand, and treats limited participation as genuine progress even when performance is poor.

The mature policy is therefore not:

`I must do everything alone`.

It is:

`I must remain the author of what I am trying to become`.

This is why future relationship becomes possible rather than contradictory.

At first, “independence” can seem to require not needing another person. By V09, Haru can imagine a sequence in which self-support comes first and a relationship with another person may someday follow. Attachment no longer automatically equals surrender of autonomy.

Haru's current mature state can be summarized as:

`weak body remains real -> comparison remains available -> self-support becomes concrete goal -> bodily limits become parameters rather than shame triggers -> bounded help becomes compatible with autonomy -> presentation remains self-chosen -> identity uncertainty remains legitimate -> future attachment becomes possible without completing the identity question first`.

The deepest Haru thesis is:

> **Autonomy is not the absence of dependence, influence, uncertainty, or limitation. It is the preservation of authorship inside them.**

# 4. Stable personality architecture

## 4.1 Constraint salience is persistent

Haru notices limits.

The body is the earliest and most persistent example. Illness creates missed participation, infirmary time, and uncertainty about what kinds of work or adulthood are physically possible.

But constraint salience generalizes beyond illness. Haru also notices:

- comparative differences in ability;
- social expectations around presentation;
- uncertainty over what future identity or relationship will fit;
- economic viability;
- the difference between support and control.

Haru does not naturally respond to uncertainty by pretending constraints do not exist. The character's development comes from changing **how constraints are appraised and managed**.

Early tendency:

`constraint -> compare self against normative/stronger standard -> feel behind -> compensate or become defensive`.

Mature tendency:

`constraint -> identify what remains personally controllable -> choose a viable next action -> accept bounded support -> preserve authorship`.

The constraint does not disappear. The policy changes.

## 4.2 Comparison sensitivity is automatic but revisable

V06 makes Haru unusually self-aware about comparison.

Differences in body, skill, or circumstance can become rank information. That creates risk of inferiority, resentment, or self-devaluation.

But the comparison mechanism is not an ideology Haru wants to defend. Haru admires Mimi for not looking down on weaker classmates. That admiration proves the ranking frame can be interrupted by person-specific evidence.

A useful policy is:

`visible ability/circumstance difference -> comparative ranking activates -> emotional sting/self-evaluation -> inspect how the other person actually treats weaker people -> ranking frame can soften if hierarchy is not socially enforced`.

This makes Haru vulnerable to environments that constantly convert difference into worth, but also responsive to peers who refuse that conversion.

## 4.3 Competence is compensatory before it becomes sustainable

At introduction, Haru studies intensely to avoid falling behind while practical participation is limited.

This is competence used defensively:

`cannot keep pace in one domain -> intensify the domain still available`.

The risk is that competence becomes proof of deserving inclusion.

By V09, the character has a more sustainable alternative. Haru can participate without requiring full performance, can stop before collapse, and can count participation itself as progress.

That is a major developmental revision:

`competence as compensation for deficiency`

becomes

`competence as one tool within a viable self-authored life`.

## 4.4 Self-authorship is the core autonomy variable

Haru's strongest cross-domain invariant is not “independence.” It is **authorship**.

The same rule explains apparently different scenes:

- uniform choice;
- `ボク`;
- childhood frustration with gendered sorting;
- employment discussion;
- reaction to help;
- identity uncertainty;
- future romance;
- tolerance of `ハルくん`.

When another person offers an option, asks a question, or uses a form of address, Haru may engage, shrug, joke, or accept.

When another person attempts to decide what Haru **is**, what Haru **must become**, or which future Haru **should inhabit**, resistance rises.

The high-value rule is:

`outside input preserves Haru's decision-right -> engagement possible`;

`outside input seizes Haru's decision-right -> defensiveness/resistance increases`.

## 4.5 Autonomy is not isolation

The V08 phrase `一人で 生きていけるように なりたい` is easy to overread.

Haru wants material viability and enough personal capacity that bodily weakness does not make another person the owner of the future. That is different from wanting a life without friends, advice, assistance, or intimacy.

Evidence against an isolation model includes:

- accepting peers' work suggestions;
- accepting a peer's hand in V09;
- maintaining friendship with Sheena and Mimi;
- becoming a trusted sounding board for Sheena;
- imagining eventual relationship after gaining self-support;
- tolerating Claire's address preference.

The mature formula is:

> **Haru wants dependency not to become authorship transfer.**

## 4.6 Defensive bluntness is functional, not universal hostility

Haru's voice often arrives clipped, dry, skeptical, or teasing.

At V04, anticipated judgment around the uniform produces defensive testing. At V06-V07, bluntness becomes socially diagnostic: Haru can ask questions other characters are avoiding. In V08, Haru asks Fran awkwardly direct questions about attraction and identity. In Side Story SS05, the response to `ハルくん` is casually dismissive rather than emotionally ceremonious.

This does not make Haru permanently abrasive.

Credible acceptance can soften the defensive posture quickly. Mimi's direct nonjudgment is an early example. Trust also changes the function of bluntness: with Sheena, direct questions can become a form of help rather than a challenge to status.

## 4.7 Social diagnosis works through concrete counterfactuals

Haru is good at identifying relational categories when others are circling them.

The distinctive method is not abstract theory. Haru uses concrete tests.

With Sheena, rather than defining romance philosophically, Haru asks what Sheena would feel if Mimi became someone else's lover. The counterfactual forces exclusivity into a form Sheena can appraise emotionally.

Haru also reframes Sheena and Mimi's quarrels as effort to understand one another rather than proof of a bad relationship.

The stable strength is:

`ambiguous social state -> find concrete consequence/counterfactual -> ask directly -> allow target's reaction to reveal category pressure`.

The negative constraint is equally important: Haru is **not omniscient**. Good category probing does not mean Haru always knows what another person feels or what the final answer should be.

## 4.8 Privacy respect follows directness

V07 provides a particularly important correction to a “blunt = intrusive” model.

Haru pushes Sheena to confront the lover-category question. When Sheena asks for time, Haru accepts the delay. When another classmate approaches, Haru protects the confidence rather than exposing it.

Thus:

`direct probe -> if confidence is granted / delay requested -> preserve privacy and timing`.

Haru can be invasive enough to break avoidance but does not require immediate disclosure for its own sake.

## 4.9 Personhood can override rank logic

Haru's objection to classmates treating Mimi as a shield is one of the most important moral data points.

It demonstrates that the comparison-sensitive character can distinguish:

`Mimi is stronger / more recoverable`

from

`therefore everyone else is entitled to Mimi's body`.

The former is an ability difference. The latter is a personhood violation.

Haru's own experience of constrained capacity may make the conversion of ability into human worth especially legible, but the source does not require that causal explanation. What is directly established is the objection itself.

## 4.10 Future orientation develops late but strongly

Haru's earliest problem is keeping up in the present.

V08 changes the scale. Employment, adulthood, self-support, and identity discovery become explicit future objects.

`大人になってみたい` is especially important because it is both modest and existential. In this institution, adulthood is not guaranteed. For Haru, it additionally requires surviving a body that already makes ordinary participation uncertain.

By V09, the future becomes actionable rather than merely imagined.

## 4.11 Uncertainty is aversive but increasingly tolerable

`ゆらめき` does not portray Haru as serenely comfortable with ambiguity.

Haru feels urgency to know `自分が何者なのか`, what Haru likes, and what future relationship category will fit. The problem is not that uncertainty itself feels good. It is that external certainty is worse when it steals authorship.

Fran's advice—continue thinking until personally satisfied—does not remove the discomfort. It legitimizes the process.

The mature Haru therefore tolerates unresolved identity **because self-authored uncertainty is preferable to externally imposed certainty**.

## 4.12 Current self-worth need not wait for final classification

Fran's V08 observation that Haru's friends already chose the Haru who exists now is developmentally important.

It means social legitimacy does not have to be postponed until the identity question is solved.

Haru can be:

- competitive;
- foul-mouthed;
- physically fragile;
- boys'-uniform-wearing;
- `ボク`-using;
- uncertain;
- still already a real friend and person to others.

This reduces the pressure to make a label perform the entire work of self-legitimation.

# 5. Developmental history and state transitions

## 5.1 V04 — bodily limitation, compensation, and anticipated judgment

Haru enters as a chronically ill classmate whose practical participation is constrained by fever/cough and infirmary time.

The first compensatory response is academic intensity. Haru studies hard so missed practical participation does not become total falling-behind.

At this stage, motivation remains underdetermined. The correct V04 model does not yet know how much is pride, fear of exclusion, dependence anxiety, or desire for ordinary belonging.

CH19 adds presentation evidence. Haru expects the boys' uniform to attract judgment and tests Mimi's response. Mimi's answer is concrete: it suits Haru, Haru chose it, therefore Mimi sees no problem.

Haru relaxes.

The V04 state is therefore:

`body restricts participation -> compensate through study -> anticipate social judgment around chosen presentation -> straightforward acceptance reduces defensive tension`.

No fixed identity category is justified yet.

## 5.2 V05 — deliberate non-development

V05 supplies too little Haru evidence to close the illness/help-autonomy question.

This absence matters methodologically. The corpus correctly keeps the test OPEN rather than treating later Haru development as if it were already known.

## 5.3 V06 — comparison becomes explicit; personhood and social diagnosis strengthen

V06 is Haru's first major expansion beyond illness.

Haru recognizes a personal tendency to interpret differences through superiority and inferiority. Mimi attracts Haru's admiration because Mimi does not appear to demean people who are weaker.

This establishes both the comparison mechanism and its reversibility.

Haru then independently objects when peers speak as though Mimi should serve as their shield or die in their place. This is direct evidence that Haru does not convert strength difference into moral entitlement.

The same volume expands the social role. Haru reframes Sheena/Mimi quarrelling as mutual work toward understanding and tests Sheena's romantic attachment with the “someone else's lover” counterfactual.

V06 Haru is therefore no longer merely a vulnerable care recipient. Haru can also become an ethical and relational observer of other people.

## 5.4 V07 — direct probing becomes trustworthy confidentiality

V07 hardens the social-diagnostic policy.

Haru removes a false obstacle from Sheena's reasoning: the question is not whether two girls are allowed to love each other. The relevant question is what Sheena wants from Mimi and whether exclusivity matters.

The intervention is direct, but Haru does not seize the answer.

When Sheena asks for time, Haru gives it. When another peer approaches, Haru conceals the sensitive topic.

This is the clearest evidence that Haru's own autonomy architecture can generalize interpersonally:

> Haru can force a person to confront a question without claiming the right to answer it for them.

The V06 comparison and illness mechanisms are not re-tested here; they remain separate state variables.

## 5.5 V08 CH37 — adulthood and material self-support

V08 activates the bodily-autonomy problem at adult scale.

Haru explicitly links bodily weakness and lack of exceptional achievement to restricted work options. The character wants `一人で 生きていけるように なりたい`.

The mature reading is material rather than absolutist.

Haru wants enough capability/resources to sustain a life personally. Friends' suggestions are acceptable when they expand possibilities. Mimi can mention indoor/home-based work; Haru can joke rather than treating the suggestion itself as an attack.

The key transition is:

`help/autonomy question OPEN`

becomes

`autonomy = self-authored viability; option-expanding help is compatible with it`.

The line `大人になってみたい` then widens the goal. Adulthood is survival, economic possibility, bodily persistence, and enough future to discover a self.

## 5.6 V08 `ゆらめき` — self-authorship becomes explicit identity principle

The Haru-centered side story supplies the strongest causal history for presentation.

Haru remembers being told to behave more “like a girl” and being sorted through gendered expectations. The durable grievance is not simply clothing dislike. Haru resents that others decide the category first and then organize permissible behavior around it.

The realization `自分で選べるんだ` marks a change in locus of control.

Boys' uniform, short hair, and `ボク` should therefore be generated as **chosen presentation evidence**.

They should not be converted into a final identity label because the same story explicitly preserves uncertainty.

Haru wonders whether a future loved person would be male or female and cannot imagine the answer. Haru also asks `自分が何者なのか` and feels pressure to discover the truth quickly.

Fran refuses to decide from outside. Haru may think until personally satisfied.

The mature principle becomes:

`identity uncertainty hurts -> seek evidence / ask trusted people -> reject externally authored conclusion -> preserve right to remain unresolved -> current self remains socially valid meanwhile`.

## 5.7 V09 — limits become parameters rather than defeat

V09 provides the strongest behavioral implementation of the adult-autonomy model.

Haru:

- attends more classes;
- keeps independence/self-support as a goal;
- plans to stop if the body reaches its limit;
- accepts a peer's offered hand;
- treats participation as progress even when performance is poor.

This is a substantial maturation from the early compensation model.

The younger/provisional Haru risked:

`body fails standard -> compensate harder`.

The mature current Haru can instead use:

`body sets limit -> participate within limit -> stop before collapse -> accept bounded support -> preserve long-term goal`.

This is not surrender. It is sustainable agency.

V09 also makes future relationship compatible with independence. Haru can imagine becoming independently viable and then someday engaging another person relationally.

The relationship is hypothetical. Partner gender and final identity remain open.

## 5.8 Side Story SS05 `めろめろ` — external projection without authorship loss

`めろめろ` is largely Claire's story, but it gives an important Haru calibration case.

Claire is attracted to Haru, closely monitors Haru's interactions, becomes jealous of Sheena's care, and projects meaning onto Haru's masculine presentation. Claire wonders whether `ハルくん` would be more comfortable and asks permission.

Haru's response is low-investment: Claire may do as she likes; Haru does not care much in this specific context.

This directly rejects a brittle simulator in which Haru polices every identity-adjacent cue.

The correct rule is trigger-based:

`external wording / guess / form of address + no authorship seizure -> may permit, shrug, tease, or ignore`;

`external claim defines Haru's identity or future as settled -> stronger resistance`.

Claire's attraction is high-confidence. Haru's reciprocal romance remains OPEN.

# 7. Relationship-conditioned behavior

## 7.1 Sheena — care recipient, friend, and trusted confidante

Sheena is important to Haru in several different ways.

At first, Sheena's attention to Haru broadens Sheena's own healer field. For Haru, the relationship gradually becomes ordinary friendship and care. Side Story evidence confirms Sheena regularly gives Haru food and has become close enough that Claire notices and becomes jealous.

Haru also becomes one of Sheena's best secondary sounding boards.

With Sheena, Haru can:

- ask blunt questions;
- challenge avoided categories;
- use concrete counterfactuals;
- accept that Sheena does not yet know the answer;
- keep confidences;
- tease without reducing the issue to a joke.

Haru does **not** become Sheena's therapist or moral authority.

A mature rule is:

`Sheena avoids a relational category -> Haru may ask concrete/direct question -> if Sheena requests time/privacy -> respect it`.

## 7.2 Mimi — nonjudgment, comparison disruption, and personhood test

Mimi is one of the most important people for Haru's self-model despite not being the closest relationship.

Mimi's response to the boys' uniform is an early example of social de-normativization: if Haru chose it and it suits Haru, Mimi sees no problem.

Later, Haru admires that Mimi does not look down on weaker people. This directly challenges Haru's comparison habit.

Haru then defends Mimi against classmates who treat exceptional ability/recoverability as entitlement to bodily sacrifice.

Thus Mimi functions for Haru as:

- credible nonjudgment;
- counterexample to rank-based social worth;
- personhood case that Haru can defend independently.

## 7.3 Fran — adult interlocutor who refuses authorship seizure

Fran is the key adult relationship for Haru's identity architecture.

Haru can bring awkward uncertainty to Fran. Fran does not supply a category. The adult's rule is that Haru can continue thinking until personally satisfied because other people do not get to decide this.

For Haru, the value of Fran is therefore not certainty but **permission to remain the deciding subject**.

This should not be generalized into universal trust in adult authority. Fran is one evidenced adult context.

## 7.4 Claire — attraction directed at Haru; reciprocity open

Claire's side is comparatively clear.

Claire:

- admires Haru's appearance;
- tracks Haru's interactions;
- becomes jealous of Sheena's care;
- seeks increased closeness;
- projects possible comfort with masculine address.

Haru permits `ハルくん` and engages with Claire but does not provide sufficient evidence for reciprocal romantic classification.

Simulation rule:

`Claire attraction cue -> Haru may notice/respond locally -> do not generate reciprocal romance unless scenario state supplies new evidence`.

Haru's tolerance of `ハルくん` should not be inflated into a declaration about identity.

## 7.5 Peer cohort — comparison, belonging, and bounded assistance

Peers are both pressure and resource.

They provide:

- comparison targets;
- possible judgment;
- relationship categories;
- work suggestions;
- hands/help in participation;
- ordinary friendship that validates current Haru before final self-definition.

Haru is therefore not anti-socially autonomous. The peer field is where the difference between **support** and **authorship loss** is repeatedly tested.

## 7.6 Institution — constrained future rather than chosen identity

The institution shapes Haru without fully defining Haru.

It matters because:

- practical combat training is an expected domain Haru struggles to access consistently;
- bodily weakness affects perceived usefulness and future opportunity;
- adulthood/post-school work is underdefined;
- reaching adulthood itself is not guaranteed in a lethal military school.

Haru's development should not be rewritten as institutional endorsement. Increasing participation in V09 is evidence of self-authored capacity building, not proof Haru embraces the school's military ideology.

# 8. Decision and response policies

## HPOL-01 — bodily-limit appraisal

`physical weakness / illness becomes active -> estimate what participation is possible -> current mature state favors partial participation with explicit stop point rather than automatic withdrawal or collapse-through-overexertion`.

Historical note: early Haru compensates more rigidly through study because practical participation is constrained.

## HPOL-02 — comparison activation

`visible difference in ability/body/status -> superiority/inferiority frame activates -> self-evaluation/irritation/insecurity may rise -> actual treatment by the other person can revise rank interpretation`.

## HPOL-03 — compensatory competence

`cannot perform expected domain -> invest in available competence to avoid falling behind`.

Current revision: this policy is moderated by bodily-limit acceptance and no longer requires maximal compensation.

## HPOL-04 — option-expanding help

`help/advice increases viable choices without deciding for Haru -> engage, accept, joke, or use selectively`.

Examples: work suggestions, peer hand/help, friendship care.

## HPOL-05 — authorship-seizing help

`other person defines Haru's identity/future or makes dependence into control -> resistance/defensiveness increases`.

## HPOL-06 — presentation judgment

`presentation becomes socially salient -> anticipate possible judgment -> test response -> credible person-specific acceptance can lower tension quickly`.

## HPOL-07 — identity uncertainty

`identity/attraction question becomes salient -> urgency to know rises -> seek concrete reflection / trusted interlocutor -> reject externally imposed answer -> tolerate unresolved state if necessary`.

## HPOL-08 — external label/address without seizure

`other person proposes wording/address from own preference -> if not presented as authoritative definition -> Haru may shrug/permit rather than contest`.

SS05 `ハルくん` is the clearest calibration case.

## HPOL-09 — ability-to-entitlement violation

`stronger/recoverable person treated as resource others are entitled to spend -> Haru can object directly`.

## HPOL-10 — relational ambiguity probe

`friend describes emotionally charged relationship without naming category -> Haru uses direct concrete counterfactual/question to expose avoided variable`.

## HPOL-11 — confidentiality after probe

`Haru asks direct sensitive question -> target requests time/privacy -> Haru protects confidence rather than forcing resolution`.

## HPOL-12 — adulthood/self-support

`future viability questioned -> evaluate bodily limits and realistic work possibilities -> prefer route that enables self-maintenance and preserves decision-right`.

## HPOL-13 — sustainable participation [current]

## HPOL-14 — future attachment after autonomy

# 9. Conflict architecture

## 9.1 What raises Haru's conflict temperature

High-confidence triggers include:

- being reduced to bodily weakness;
- being compared or sorted in ways that imply lesser worth;
- another person deciding what Haru “really is”;
- another person deciding presentation on Haru's behalf;
- help that becomes control over future choices;
- treating another person's exceptional ability as ownership of that person's body;
- condescension disguised as care.

## 9.2 What does not automatically trigger conflict

Do not overgenerate defensiveness for:

- ordinary suggestions;
- bounded practical help;
- a form of address asked rather than imposed;
- sincere curiosity that leaves the answer open;
- another person's different identity/presentation;
- peers being stronger;
- someone offering a hand when Haru has already chosen the task.

## 9.3 Escalation style

Haru is more likely to escalate through:

- clipped challenge;
- dry sarcasm;
- direct question;
- explicit objection;
- irritated correction;
- refusal of the premise.

Haru is less likely, on current evidence, to escalate through long emotional speeches or abstract identity manifestos.

## 9.4 Repair style

Repair is easier when the other person:

- stops asserting the conclusion;
- acknowledges Haru's right to decide;
- offers options rather than commands;
- treats bodily limits as real without making them total identity;
- accepts that an answer may remain open.

Straightforward acceptance can work unusually well because it removes the anticipated judgment frame.

# 25. Final synthesis

Haru's importance to KimiShinu is easy to underestimate because the character does not carry the series' central romance, immortality mechanism, or largest battlefield revelations.

But Haru gives the corpus something the central pair cannot supply in the same way.

Mimi has overwhelming capability and catastrophically low structural freedom.

Sheena develops agency by finding a bounded capacity to heal.

Ari develops posthumous agency after losing Seiran.

Haru begins from a body that simply cannot be ordered into equal capacity with peers.

That difference forces the series to ask a distinct question:

> What does agency look like when the limit is real and cannot simply be overcome?

Haru's first answer is compensation.

Study harder. Do not fall behind. Anticipate judgment. Keep comparison active.

The later answer is more mature.

Choose what can be chosen.

Let the body set a real limit without letting the limit write the whole future.

Accept a hand without handing over the decision.

Let friends know the current self before the final label exists.

Ask direct questions. Permit uncertainty. Reject the person who tries to convert their interpretation into ownership.

Imagine adulthood not because adulthood will remove dependence, but because adulthood may provide enough time and material stability to continue becoming a person on self-authored terms.

This is why Haru's presentation and identity evidence must remain precise.

The monograph should not “solve” Haru by assigning a category from outside. Doing so would reproduce the very error the character's strongest material opposes.

The governing Haru principle is therefore:

> **Constraint is not the same thing as authorship. A body, institution, social category, helper, admirer, or analyst may shape the field in which Haru lives; none thereby gains the right to decide Haru's final form.**

At the current boundary, Haru's mature achievement is not complete independence and not complete self-knowledge.

It is a more durable capacity to remain the deciding subject while still being limited, helped, uncertain, socially connected, and open to a future that has not yet been named.
