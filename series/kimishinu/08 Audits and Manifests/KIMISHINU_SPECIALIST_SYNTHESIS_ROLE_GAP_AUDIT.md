# KIMISHINU_SPECIALIST_SYNTHESIS_ROLE_GAP_AUDIT.md

## 1. Purpose

This audit determines whether the current KimiShinu analytical corpus is ready to advance directly to full-series synthesis or whether additional specialist artifacts have distinct analytical responsibilities that should be completed first.

The audit follows the project-wide rule that **architecture should be proportional to the source**. A recurring character, relationship, or theme does not automatically deserve a standalone specialist file. A new artifact is justified only when it has a responsibility that would otherwise be flattened, duplicated, or become difficult to retrieve inside existing monographs, ledgers, or the eventual full-series synthesis.

The current source boundary is the complete available Japanese manga corpus through V09 plus the V05 special booklet and standalone Side Stories. The current numbered prospective freeze remains V09; supplemental material does not alter earlier prospective knowledge states.

This audit is administrative and architectural. It does not supersede the sequential readings, ledgers, character monographs, or relationship synthesis.

---

## 2. Existing specialist coverage

The corpus already has three strong specialist authorities:

1. `KIMISHINU_SHEENA_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md`
   - canonical, simulation-ready individual model for Totsuki Sheena;
   - covers anti-disposability, bounded courage, healer formation, recipient-sensitive care, semantic caution, future orientation, mortality fear, and relationship-specific behavior.

2. `KIMISHINU_MIMI_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md`
   - canonical, simulation-ready individual model for Gagari Mimi;
   - covers asynchronous development, resurrection/recoverability, protective substitution, ordinary-life novelty, concrete cognition, memory erosion, institutional weaponization, jealousy, and relationship-specific behavior.

3. `KIMISHINU_SHEENA_MIMI_RELATIONSHIP_SPECIALIST_SYNTHESIS.md`
   - canonical, simulation-ready dyadic model;
   - treats the relationship as a history-dependent system rather than adding the two individual models together;
   - synthesizes formation, waiting/return, mutual non-disposability, V05 protection-versus-agency conflict, reconciliation without consensus, romantic primacy, healer/protector differentiation, bodily and temporal asymmetry, memory, mortality, joint behavioral policies, and future falsification tests.

The five longitudinal ledgers remain the authoritative cumulative record for character states, relationship transitions, mortality/personhood, institution/war/agency, and visual/dialogue patterns.

The question is therefore not whether more material exists. It is whether any remaining material needs an independent **specialist synthesis layer** before full-series integration.

---

## 3. Audit criteria

Candidates were tested against six criteria.

### 3.1 Distinct analytical responsibility

Would the artifact answer a question not already answered adequately by an existing specialist artifact or ledger?

### 3.2 Longitudinal density

Does the candidate accumulate meaningful evidence across multiple volumes or across a major before/after state transition rather than a single isolated episode?

### 3.3 Model complexity

Does the material contain interacting policies, contradictions, or state transitions that are likely to be flattened by a shorter full-series section?

### 3.4 Retrieval value

Would future character reconstruction, comparison, simulation, or thematic analysis benefit from having one canonical topical home?

### 3.5 Non-duplication

Can the artifact avoid simply rewriting material already organized well in another specialist file?

### 3.6 Full-series dependency

Would completing the artifact materially improve the accuracy or architecture of the eventual full-series synthesis?

Verdicts used here:

- **CREATE BEFORE FULL-SERIES** — clear gap; specialist artifact should be completed first.
- **DEFER / REASSESS AFTER NEXT RELEASE** — plausible future artifact, but current evidence or distinctness is insufficient.
- **DO NOT CREATE SEPARATELY** — existing canonical homes are sufficient; a new artifact would mostly duplicate them.

# 4. Candidate 1 — Ari–Seiran relationship specialist synthesis

## Verdict: CREATE BEFORE FULL-SERIES — Priority 1

### Recommended filename

`KIMISHINU_ARI_SEIRAN_RELATIONSHIP_SPECIALIST_SYNTHESIS.md`

### Recommended reasoning level

**Extra High.**

The challenge is not source volume alone. The synthesis must preserve a split temporal object:

- a living reciprocal dyad through V03;
- a posthumous relationship that continues to organize Ari's grief, ethics, identity, and later agency through V09;
- retrospective Side Stories that deepen the pre-death relationship without rewriting prospective certainty backward.

## 4.1 Why Ari–Seiran has crossed the specialist threshold

Ari and Seiran are no longer adequately described as a tragic foil to Sheena and Mimi.

The corpus now supports a distinct dyadic system with its own causal structure.

### A. Love increases Seiran's battlefield self-risk

Seiran's major protective policy is not merely institutional obedience. She wants to become stronger and take more battlefield burden because Ari's injury is intolerable to her.

The relationship therefore supplies a crucial counterexample to any simple model in which attachment makes characters more cautious:

`Ari becomes more precious -> Ari's exposure becomes less acceptable -> Seiran chooses herself as substitute -> Seiran seeks more danger`.

This is especially important because Seiran is mortal. Her substitution policy cannot be explained by Mimi's resurrection/recoverability.

### B. Ari performs moral repair after combat

After Seiran's first kill, Seiran interprets horror and hesitation as proof that she is weak and useless. Ari rejects that institutionalized shame frame, validates the reluctance to kill, and recenters value around Seiran surviving and returning.

Ari therefore functions not only as a beloved person but as a **protector of Seiran's moral self-conception**.

### C. Ari is a genuine return target, but return fails

Seiran's catastrophic final march is organized by `帰らなきゃ` with Ari salient. The attachment has real behavioral force. Yet Seiran dies before reunion.

This makes Ari–Seiran analytically different from a simple doomed-romance trope. Their arc demonstrates:

> attachment can motivate survival without guaranteeing survival.

This is a direct but nonredundant comparison to the Sheena–Mimi `departure -> waiting -> return -> reception` grammar.

### D. Ari's suppressed veto creates a distinct agency conflict

Ari supports Seiran's chosen strength and tells her she is proud while privately wishing she had been able to say `行かないで`.

This is not the same structure as Sheena and Mimi's V05 quarrel.

Ari's problem is that **respect for a loved person's chosen protective identity can suppress the survivor's own attachment-protective preference**.

After Seiran's death, Ari cannot know whether her support helped Seiran endure or helped sustain the path that killed her.

That uncertainty remains ethically live.

### E. Posthumous love becomes an autonomy problem

When resurrection becomes available, Ari refuses it despite desperately wanting Seiran back. Her reason is not indifference but ownership/consent: Seiran's life belongs to Seiran.

This is one of the corpus's strongest formulations of love as refusal to appropriate the beloved.

The act deserves analysis in its own relational system rather than appearing only as supporting evidence for Sheena–Mimi or the mortality ledger.

### F. Ari's grief develops rather than merely persists

Ari initially performs composure partly because she believes understanding Seiran's choice obligates her not to grieve visibly. Mimi's intervention permits sadness without requiring Ari to retroactively revoke Seiran's agency.

Later, Ari chooses to continue living as `the self Seiran loved`.

This is not “moving on” through replacement. The posthumous relationship becomes a continuity structure:

`Seiran-linked grief -> sadness remains legitimate -> remembered valuation of Ari's living self -> return to ordinary/social life`.

### G. V09 prevents the posthumous relationship from becoming static command

Ari eventually chooses the combat proficiency exam despite believing Seiran would be hurt by that decision.

This is a major mature development:

> continued love does not require the survivor to freeze her future at the dead partner's last known preference.

Ari can remain faithful to the relationship while becoming newly self-authoring in a direction Seiran may not have chosen for her.

### H. Side Stories provide a positive intimacy substrate

`SS06 ぴかぴか` prevents the pair from being reconstructed only through sacrifice and death.

Seiran closely observes Ari's expressions, habits, body, and differences between socially managed and spontaneous smiles. Ari actively asks Seiran to look at her and wants Seiran specifically to find her cute.

The relationship is therefore built around **reciprocal seeing and being seen** before its protector logic becomes catastrophic.

## 4.2 Required scope of the specialist synthesis

The file should include:

- formation and pre-death ordinary intimacy;
- reciprocal gaze / social smile versus spontaneous affect;
- Seiran's protective self-substitution;
- Ari's support-versus-veto contradiction;
- first-kill moral repair;
- portable Ari / pendant / letter / safe-return structure;
- failed reunion;
- medicine sacrifice and generalized self-expenditure;
- grief permission;
- resurrection refusal as posthumous autonomy protection;
- grief integration without replacement;
- Ari's later relational role for Sheena;
- V09 combat-exam divergence from the dead partner's presumed preference;
- comparison with Sheena–Mimi without reducing Ari–Seiran to a foil;
- temporal simulation constraints.

## 4.3 Simulation constraint

The artifact can be **relationship-model-ready**, but must explicitly distinguish two modes:

1. **Living-dyad simulation:** only at source-consistent states through V03 or securely placed retrospective material.
2. **Posthumous relationship simulation:** V04 onward models Ari's memory, grief, ethical reasoning, and Seiran-referential behavior. It must not fabricate new post-V03 Seiran choices or dialogue as though she survived.

## 4.4 Separate Ari or Seiran monographs?

**Do not create them before the full-series synthesis.**

Ari has enough material that an individual monograph could eventually become justified, especially if later volumes substantially expand her V09 combat-agency arc. At the current boundary, however, most of her highest-value evidence is inseparable from Seiran, bereavement, and posthumous relationship continuation.

Seiran has an unusually rich V03 and Side Story model but a terminal chronological boundary. A separate character monograph would substantially duplicate the relationship synthesis while offering little additional current-state modeling.

**Reassessment trigger:** a later volume gives Ari a major independent arc not primarily organized by Seiran, or supplies substantial new retrospective Seiran evidence outside the relationship.

# 5. Candidate 2 — Adult institutional mediation specialist synthesis

## Verdict: CREATE BEFORE FULL-SERIES — Priority 2

The user's intuition that “the teachers” deserve attention is correct, but the most useful artifact is **not** a set of teacher character monographs.

### Recommended filename

`KIMISHINU_ADULT_INSTITUTIONAL_MEDIATION_SPECIALIST_SYNTHESIS.md`

### Recommended reasoning level

**Extra High.**

The difficult problem is separating personal care, professional responsibility, institutional realism, coercion, and actual policy power without collapsing the adults into either benevolent protectors or villains.

## 5.1 Why the correct scope is adult institutional mediation

The source repeatedly shows adults as **human interfaces through which the militarized institution becomes actionable**.

Their analytical importance lies less in private biography than in how they:

- train children;
- manage injuries;
- allocate or discuss risk;
- preserve secrets;
- interpret capability;
- regulate information;
- define or defend local choice;
- continue institutional schedules after death;
- normalize deployment while sometimes recognizing its human cost.

This is already a coherent specialist problem.

## 5.2 Fran is the central case

Fran alone now supports a complex model that should not remain dispersed across several ledgers.

She is simultaneously:

- an exasperated but genuinely protective caretaker toward Mimi;
- a medical manager;
- custodian of Mimi's resurrection secret;
- an institutional realist who explains that removing Mimi from battle simply transfers danger to someone else under present conditions;
- a developmental mentor who forces Sheena to confront displaced costs rather than offering moral comfort;
- the adult who recognizes Sheena's healing aptitude and helps develop it;
- a person who can defend Haru's right to self-definition;
- a practical manager of Mimi's non-aging secrecy problem;
- a mortality realist who accepts Sheena and Mimi's relationship while testing “always” against actual death and sacrifice;
- historically, an active participant in the cycle of retrieving Mimi from underground confinement, sending her to battle, washing/restoring her, and returning her underground.

The last point prevents an uncomplicated caretaker reading.

The correct formulation is not:

`Fran cares about Mimi -> Fran is outside the coercive system`.

Nor is it:

`Fran participates in coercion -> all observed care is false`.

The source supports:

> **personal care and institutional complicity can be simultaneously real.**

That is one of the series' strongest adult-level ethical problems.

## 5.3 Omi provides differentiation, not enough for a standalone monograph

Omi's evidence is narrower but analytically useful.

The Side Stories establish a teacher whose care is expressed through:

- preparation;
- realistic training design;
- procedural responsibility;
- willingness to be less liked if that is perceived as necessary for competence or safety.

Fran's teasing familiarity and Omi's more restrained structure-oriented style demonstrate that the institution does not have one adult psychological voice.

Omi therefore belongs in the specialist synthesis as a **contrastive adult mode**, not as an independent monograph.

## 5.4 The unnamed adult decision layer also matters

V09 expands the adult field beyond interpersonal teachers.

Adults explicitly discuss:

- decreasing enemy force;
- possible supply-line or strategic explanations;
- friendly force distribution across bases;
- student combat-readiness as deployable supply;
- upcoming summons;
- whether Mimi should remain assigned to the highest-intensity sector;
- whether students should be kept unaware of strategic uncertainty.

This matters because the series now directly shows a layer where people who may sincerely claim not to want to waste student lives still manage children as combat resources.

The adult specialist synthesis should therefore study **mediated coercion**, not search for a single villain.

## 5.5 The adult autonomy contradiction

The corpus contains genuine adult statements and behaviors supporting child agency:

- opportunities may be an adult responsibility while choice belongs to the child;
- Fran refuses to decide Haru's identity from outside;
- Sheena's self-authored healer path receives instruction rather than suppression.

At the same time:

- student education is tied to combat-readiness production;
- information is deliberately withheld;
- Mimi's historical confinement was extreme;
- current deployment and possible post-school retention remain outside Mimi's demonstrated control;
- grief recognition does not halt the training schedule.

A specialist synthesis is justified precisely because the source refuses the easy equation:

`adult care = structural freedom`.

It also refuses:

`institutional participation = absence of care`.

## 5.6 Required scope

The artifact should analyze:

- Fran as caretaker / manager / mentor / secret custodian / institutional participant;
- Omi as structure-oriented professional care;
- adult recognition of grief versus institutional continuity;
- capability identification and conversion into role;
- child-choice language versus constrained choice in practice;
- information asymmetry and strategic secrecy;
- the student-to-combat pipeline;
- Mimi's underground history and current exceptional-asset treatment;
- “no intention to waste lives” versus actual resource-management logic;
- differentiated adult psychology inside one coercive structure;
- limits of adult policy autonomy where the source is silent;
- how adult mediation shapes Sheena, Mimi, Haru, Ari, Laura, and the broader student body.

## 5.7 What this artifact must not become

It should **not** become:

- a macro-political war history;
- a speculative theory of unnamed governments;
- a “teachers are evil” prosecution brief;
- a defense of the institution because individual adults care;
- separate pseudo-monographs padded from thin evidence;
- a claim that Fran has policy authority the manga has not shown.

The existing institution/war/agency ledger explicitly finds that a standalone macro-politics ledger remains unjustified. This audit preserves that judgment.

## 5.8 Separate Fran or Omi monographs?

**Do not create before the full-series synthesis.**

Fran has enough material for a substantial section inside the adult institutional synthesis and could eventually support her own character monograph if later volumes add more independent biography, private goals, or policy conflict.

Omi does not currently have enough independent evidence for a monograph.

# 6. Candidate 3 — Haru character monograph

## Verdict: CREATE BEFORE FULL-SERIES — Priority 3

### Recommended filename

`KIMISHINU_HARU_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md`

### Recommended reasoning level

**High to Extra High; prefer Extra High for first canonical synthesis.**

The evidence quantity is lower than Sheena or Mimi, but the interpretive risk is high because the source deliberately leaves some identity categories open. The synthesis must preserve underdetermination rather than converting presentation, pronouns, clothing, address terms, or attraction into a label the character has not chosen.

## 6.1 Why Haru is a genuine role gap

Haru has accumulated a multi-volume arc from V04 through V09 plus Side Stories, and the arc addresses a domain not reducible to Sheena/Mimi or Ari/Seiran.

The core system is:

`chronic bodily limitation -> risk of being left behind / dependence -> compensatory competence and social defensiveness -> comparison with more capable peers -> search for viable adulthood -> self-authored presentation and identity boundaries -> acceptance of bounded help -> independence becomes compatible with future attachment`.

This is already enough for a coherent character model.

## 6.2 Distinct analytical contributions

### A. Disability/bodily limitation without pure passivity

Haru's chronic illness constrains practical participation and produces comparison pressure, but the character does not remain at “sick student” status.

The arc repeatedly asks how to build agency when the body cannot simply be disciplined into matching peers.

### B. Competence as compensation

Academic effort and later practical self-support are partly shaped by a desire not to fall behind or become dependent.

This provides another route into the series' usefulness problem, distinct from Laura's militarized achievement and Mimi's battlefield utility.

### C. Self-authorship without premature identity closure

Haru's boys' uniform, `ボク`, tolerated `くん`, and conversations about adulthood/presentation all matter, but the source repeatedly refuses to collapse them into a fixed external classification.

Fran's advice is especially important: other people cannot decide this for Haru, and Haru may continue thinking until personally satisfied.

The monograph should preserve **self-authorship as the stable principle while category outcome remains open**.

### D. Bounded help rather than autonomy-as-isolation

Haru increasingly imagines self-support while also learning that accepting limited help does not erase autonomy.

By V09, independence and future relationship are no longer mutually exclusive in Haru's imagination.

That is a mature developmental change that deserves its own canonical home.

### E. Haru is useful for future simulation and comparison

Haru offers a strong comparative model for:

- bodily constraint;
- agency without physical dominance;
- identity/presentation under social projection;
- defensive humor/directness;
- help acceptance;
- future planning;
- romantic openness without premature target or identity assignment.

## 6.3 Why this should be a monograph rather than a thematic synthesis

Unlike the adult institutional problem, Haru's evidence has a clear individual center and multiple behavioral policies.

A standalone character monograph can therefore produce a reconstructable model without inventing a broader theme-only container.

## 6.4 What the monograph must not claim

It must not:

- assign a gender identity the source has not explicitly fixed;
- infer sexual orientation from current evidence;
- treat `ボク`, trousers, or tolerated `ハルくん` as dispositive;
- model chronic illness as the whole personality;
- equate self-support with rejection of care;
- treat compensatory effort as proof that Haru accepts the institution's military ideology.

# 7. Candidates that do not currently justify standalone specialists

## 7.1 Esta–Rika relationship

### Verdict: DO NOT CREATE SEPARATELY

The pair supplies valuable evidence for:

- reciprocal protector impulses among mortal characters;
- grief self-deprioritization;
- findability;
- non-coercive survival care;
- refusal to demand forgetting/replacement.

This is analytically useful but currently too narrow for a dedicated specialist artifact. Keep the material in the relationship ledger and use it as comparative evidence in the full-series synthesis.

**Reassessment trigger:** substantial new mainline focus on Esta/Rika or a larger post-loss relationship arc.

## 7.2 Laura

### Verdict: DO NOT CREATE SEPARATELY

Laura is important as:

- Sheena's pre-V01 relationship history;
- a case of achievement becoming usefulness obligation;
- evidence that institutional pressure can generate peer resentment through unequal capacity;
- a demonstration that remembered relationships can remain morally significant without blocking new primacy.

Her current evidence is concentrated in retrospective supplemental material. She belongs in the adult/institutional specialist and full-series synthesis rather than a standalone monograph.

## 7.3 Claire–Haru

### Verdict: DO NOT CREATE SEPARATELY

Claire's attraction is clear; reciprocity remains open. The relationship is useful for Haru's self-authorship model but not yet dense enough for a dyadic specialist.

## 7.4 Fran–Omi relationship

### Verdict: DO NOT CREATE SEPARATELY

The Side Story establishes adult familiarity and teasing/proximity with an asymmetrical expressive style. Romantic/sexual classification remains open. Its primary current value is demonstrating differentiated adult personalities inside the institution.

## 7.5 Macro-political / war-strategy specialist

### Verdict: DO NOT CREATE SEPARATELY

V09 activates meaningful strategic tracking, but the corpus still lacks the density required for a LOGH/86-style political-war artifact:

- no sustained named faction/government architecture;
- little political leadership;
- little diplomacy;
- little public opinion or propaganda;
- underdeveloped enemy ideology;
- insufficient strategic chronology.

Keep this material in `KIMISHINU_INSTITUTION_WAR_CONSCIOUSNESS_AND_AGENCY_LEDGER.md` and the adult institutional synthesis.

## 7.6 Separate mortality/personhood specialist

### Verdict: DO NOT CREATE BEFORE FULL-SERIES

Mortality, recoverability, memory, disposability, restoration, and sacrifice are central enough to support a thematic specialist in principle. At the current boundary, however, the decisive material is already distributed coherently across:

- the mortality/personhood ledger;
- Sheena monograph;
- Mimi monograph;
- Sheena–Mimi relationship synthesis;
- proposed Ari–Seiran relationship synthesis;
- proposed adult institutional mediation synthesis.

A separate specialist would currently duplicate these homes more than it would solve a retrieval problem. The full-series synthesis is the appropriate next integration layer.

## 7.7 Separate visual-form specialist

### Verdict: DO NOT CREATE BEFORE FULL-SERIES

The visual/dialogue ledger is sufficient as the evidence store. Recurring visual structures—distance, touch, domestic/combat transitions, injury visibility, bodily framing, ordinary objects as memory carriers—should be integrated into the full-series synthesis rather than extracted into another standalone artifact unless later evidence demonstrates a stronger independent formal problem.

# 9. Revised synthesis sequence

The corpus is **not yet ready to move directly to full-series synthesis** if the goal is to preserve the strongest available character/system modeling before integration.

The recommended sequence is:

### Priority 1

`KIMISHINU_ARI_SEIRAN_RELATIONSHIP_SPECIALIST_SYNTHESIS.md`

Reason: the strongest remaining dyadic gap; analytically independent from Sheena–Mimi; already mature enough for canonical synthesis.

### Priority 2

`KIMISHINU_ADULT_INSTITUTIONAL_MEDIATION_SPECIALIST_SYNTHESIS.md`

Reason: prevents the full-series treatment of the school from flattening adults into a monolithic “system”; centralizes Fran's care/complicity problem, Omi's contrasting professional style, strategic information control, and the gap between child-choice rhetoric and constrained institutional reality.

### Priority 3

`KIMISHINU_HARU_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md`

Reason: strongest remaining individual-model gap; preserves bodily limitation, self-authorship, presentation, adulthood, help, and future relationship without premature identity closure.

### Then

Open the gate to a **full-series synthesis at the V09+SIDE_STORIES boundary**.

No fourth specialist is currently required before that synthesis.

# 10. Why this is still proportional architecture

Adding these three artifacts does not reproduce the complexity of much larger projects.

Each solves a distinct problem:

- **Ari–Seiran:** a second major relationship system with a fundamentally different love/risk/death trajectory.
- **Adult institutional mediation:** how human care and coercive institutional function coexist at the adult interface.
- **Haru:** a secondary individual arc about body, autonomy, identity self-authorship, and viable adulthood.

The audit explicitly rejects several plausible but redundant artifacts. That is the architectural control mechanism.

The completed specialist set would therefore be:

1. Sheena character model;
2. Mimi character model;
3. Sheena–Mimi relationship system;
4. Ari–Seiran relationship system;
5. adult institutional mediation;
6. Haru character model.

For a nine-volume work whose central concerns include romance, death, child soldiers, bodily asymmetry, institutional coercion, grief, healing, identity, and survival, this remains proportionate.

# 11. Full-series readiness criterion after the three specialists

After the three recommended artifacts are complete, the full-series synthesis should be able to integrate rather than invent the following major layers:

- Sheena individual architecture;
- Mimi individual architecture;
- central relationship dynamics;
- alternate love/protection/death model through Ari–Seiran;
- adult/institutional mediation and complicity;
- Haru's body/autonomy/identity/adulthood model;
- mortality, recoverability, expendability, restoration, and memory from the canonical ledger;
- institutional/war-consciousness evidence without overclaiming macro-politics;
- manga-form and dialogue patterns from the visual/dialogue ledger;
- secondary comparison cases such as Esta/Rika, Laura, Claire, and others without forcing unnecessary standalone syntheses.

At that point, another role-gap audit is not required unless one of the three specialist syntheses itself reveals a new dependency.

If V10 or another numbered mainline volume becomes available before these retrospective specialist tasks are complete, the new numbered volume takes precedence under the prospective-reading protocol.

The current numbered freeze remains V09.

The specialist artifacts may resume afterward using the expanded integrated boundary, but they must preserve the historical V09+SIDE_STORIES state rather than silently rewriting what this audit decided at the present boundary.

# 13. Final audit decision

**KimiShinu is not yet at the optimal full-series synthesis point.**

Three specialist gaps remain worth filling:

1. **Ari–Seiran relationship specialist synthesis — required.**
2. **Adult institutional mediation specialist synthesis — required.**
3. **Haru character monograph — required for the current character-reconstruction standard.**

The user's two proposed areas are therefore both supported, with one refinement:

- Ari and Seiran deserve a dedicated relationship synthesis.
- The teachers/adults deserve a synthesis of their **institutional mediation roles**, not separate character writeups.

Haru is the additional gap identified by the audit.

Everything else currently belongs in existing ledgers, these specialists, or the eventual full-series synthesis rather than new standalone artifacts.
