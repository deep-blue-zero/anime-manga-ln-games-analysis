## 2. Volume-level thesis — what was hidden becomes nameable

V05 was organized around concealment. Mimi hid the actual state of her body so Sheena could experience a clean return; the school benefited from casualty reduction while much of the cost was concentrated in a child whose recoverability made that cost easier to ignore; Sheena herself had only recently learned to name passivity as a defense against helplessness. V06 repeatedly takes things that had been hidden, misclassified, or only indirectly legible and brings them into explicit recognition.

Five disclosures organize the volume.

1. **The wounded body becomes visible.** Mimi's next meaningfully injured return occurs in medical space, without the sanitized homecoming that produced V05's epistemic rupture. Sheena is allowed to see the damage and uses healing magic to reduce pain while Mimi's own body recovers.
2. **The war receives a concrete causal object.** The vague childhood stories about a national `treasure` are revealed as cover stories surrounding an indestructible grimoire containing forbidden magic, including resurrection. The book's leaked existence motivates enemy attempts to seize it; the school and country are protecting it while withholding the truth from children.
3. **Mimi becomes more legible as a person to peers.** Haru and other classmates begin separating the rumored secret weapon from the ordinary girl who likes simple things, does not rank weaker people contemptuously, and can be addressed by name.
4. **The past becomes biographically and sensorially explicit.** Sheena reveals the family accident that left her alone. Mimi admits that most concrete memories of her mother have faded, while the mother's smell persists strongly enough to have structured Mimi's first attachment response to Sheena.
5. **Sheena's desire approaches a name.** Jealousy, exclusivity, bodily heat, the wish to be Mimi's `一番`, a sustained mouth-to-mouth kiss explicitly distinguished from magic, and finally Haru's `恋人` question push Sheena beyond a merely external romantic reading. V06 ends at the category threshold rather than crossing into a formal mutual couple label.

The governing V06 transition is therefore:

`hidden wound -> transparent care`  
`mythic treasure -> forbidden-magic grimoire / war cause`  
`secret weapon rumor -> ordinary peer recognition`  
`orphan present -> named family-loss history`  
`maternal cue -> persistent sensory memory under long-term forgetting`  
`friendship jealousy -> relational primacy desire -> explicitly non-magical kiss -> "lover?" category threshold`.

This is not simply a volume of exposition. Each disclosure changes the decision model.

- Transparent injury allows Sheena's care to operate on truth rather than a managed appearance.
- War-cause knowledge changes what the children can believe they are defending without yet granting them meaningful strategic agency.
- Humanizing knowledge of Mimi makes pure weapon categorization less socially stable.
- Family-loss and memory-loss knowledge make separation and remembrance active relationship variables.
- Romantic self-recognition changes the meaning of Sheena's prior exclusivity, waiting, touch, and fear of being forgotten without retroactively pretending that V01 Sheena already possessed the V06 category.

## 3. CH25 — `ありがとう`: grief without self-erasure

### 3.1 Ari's grief remains active after ordinary function resumes

CH25 returns to Ari rather than immediately advancing the main-pair romance. This is analytically important because V05 had already shown Ari capable of eating, studying, talking, and functioning while still being susceptible to Seiran-linked cues. V06 does not turn that partial recovery into closure.

Walking with Esta in the rain, Ari becomes emotionally exposed again (`V06 / CH25 / p_0021-p_0031`). Rain still belongs to the Ari-Seiran grief grammar established in V03-V05, but its function is now less about involuntary memory alone and more about what Ari will do with the person grief is making her become.

Ari describes Seiran with increasing differentiation. Seiran's words could be hard or strong, but Ari understands that forcefulness as emerging from concern for others. Seiran put herself last and could be vulnerable or awkward underneath the disciplined exterior. This preserves V03-V04's correction against flattening Seiran into the serious soldier identity she publicly performed.

### 3.2 `すごく情けなくて` — Ari judges her own grief performance

Ari calls herself pathetic / miserable for how she has been handling the loss. She has avoided friends, tried to act as though she should already have accepted what happened, and struggled with the fact that a loved person is being converted into `the past` (`p_0023-p_0028`).

Esta does not supply a political answer or a philosophical proof that Seiran's choice was correct. She normalizes the temporal difficulty of bereavement: being unable to immediately accept that someone important now belongs to the past is painful but not shameful.

This is a useful correction to Ari's V04 tendency to treat composure as loyalty. Ari is learning that grief does not insult Seiran's agency.

### 3.3 Identity continuity becomes Ari's chosen grief ethic

Ari's strongest CH25 formulation is not `I will stop being sad`. It is that living as **the self Seiran loved** matters to her:

`あの子が好きになってくれた 私のままで生きることが 私にとっては大切なんです`

The important mechanism is identity continuity.

Ari is not trying to preserve herself because grief is weak or inconvenient. She refuses to let bereavement erase the person who participated in the relationship. This produces a new policy:

`Seiran cue -> grief reactivates -> temptation toward withdrawal/self-devaluation -> remember the self Seiran loved -> choose continued participation without requiring grief to disappear`.

That is psychologically distinct from `moving on`.

### 3.4 Re-entry into friendship is behavioral evidence

After the rain conversation Ari returns to the other girls instead of continuing to isolate (`p_0029-p_0034`). The action matters more than a generic statement that she is healing. Ordinary social re-entry now coexists with tears, memory, and unresolved longing.

**V05 prospective test #6:** strongly supported.

## 4. CH26 — `傷口`: transparent injury and the first real causal politics of the war

### 4.1 The V05 transparent-return agreement generalizes

CH26 opens with exactly the sort of event required to test V05's revised waiting policy. Mimi returns meaningfully injured. Her boots and clothing are bloodied; she is physically depleted; Sheena is with her in medical/recovery space (`V06 / CH26 / p_0035-p_0040`).

The key difference from V05 CH20-CH21 is epistemic. Mimi does **not** first transform the body into a reassuring clean-room version before Sheena can see it.

Sheena's internal framing recalls the V05 promise that Mimi can return in whatever condition and still be received. The V06 result is therefore not `Mimi now avoids being hurt`. It is:

`injured return -> actual condition visible -> Sheena present -> healing/care occurs before cost is erased`.

This is the exact distinction frozen in prospective test #1.

### 4.2 Sheena's healing has become operational care

Sheena uses `治癒魔法` to ease Mimi's pain while Mimi's own body performs the deeper restorative work (`p_0035-p_0038`). The text maintains the magic-category distinction developed since V04:

- Sheena's `治癒魔法` supports healing / eases pain and living recovery;
- Mimi's extraordinary `修復` capacity restores catastrophic bodily damage;
- `蘇生魔法` remains the forbidden resurrection category.

Sheena's thought that she would like to share or divide even Mimi's pain should not be literalized into a new magical transfer mechanism. Its evidentiary force is relational: she no longer wants the cost of Mimi's protective labor to be privately borne and socially invisible.

### 4.3 Measurable skill growth without fear-erasure

The semiannual health examination measures height, weight, vision, and magical output (`p_0041-p_0046`). Sheena hopes her magic value has increased; the result indicates that it has. Her classmates recognize that she has been practicing.

This is unusually clean evidence for V05's `bounded capability -> train -> improve` policy. Sheena does not become a different temperament. She acquires demonstrable capacity while remaining the same cautious person.

### 4.4 Haru articulates inferiority/comparison rather than simple illness resentment

Haru's V04-V05 provisional model becomes much more useful here. Haru notices that Mimi does not look down on classmates weaker than herself and does not organize every difference through superiority/inferiority (`p_0057-p_0062`). Haru contrasts that with a more comparison-sensitive way of experiencing the world.

The important formulation is not `Haru hates strong people`. Haru is able to admire exactly what exposes Haru's own painful comparison habit.

Provisional policy:

`visible difference in ability/body -> automatic comparative ranking becomes salient -> risk of resentment/self-devaluation -> person-specific evidence can interrupt the rank frame`.

Haru also begins naturally calling Mimi by name. Sheena notices and is pleased that classmates are seeing `Mimi` rather than only the weapon rumor.

This is a social-personhood transition, not merely a friendship beat.

### 4.5 `なんで戦ってんの？` — the children finally ask the causal question

V01-V05 repeatedly showed children living inside war while possessing almost no meaningful account of why the war existed. CH26 changes that boundary.

A student bluntly asks why they are fighting (`p_0047-p_0054`). The answers reveal an information ecology built from incompatible stories about a `treasure`:

- some were told it is a priceless treasure;
- others heard natural resource / jewel / ancient weapon / magical-tool variants;
- no ordinary student possesses a stable causal account.

The inconsistency is itself evidence. The children are not simply ignorant because they never cared; authoritative adults have supplied simplified, contradictory stories.

### 4.6 The `treasure` is an indestructible grimoire of forbidden magic

The adult/authority-side conversation reveals the actual protected object (`p_0051-p_0054`). It is a grimoire containing numerous forbidden magics. `蘇生魔法` is explicitly among them. Powerful magic prevents the book from simply being destroyed, leaving the country in the position of continuing to possess and protect it.

The book's existence became known outside the country. Enemy forces seek to obtain it. The authorities fear what could happen if such magic entered the hands of a militaristic power.

This is the first genuinely causal war-structure disclosure in the sequential corpus:

`forbidden-magic grimoire exists -> country possesses/cannot destroy it -> existence leaks abroad -> external actors seek acquisition -> repeated invasion/defense -> children are trained/deployed inside the resulting war`.

### 4.7 Secrecy is now a political variable, not only a plot convenience

The truth is treated as classified / too dangerous for students to know. This matters because the children are asked to risk their bodies while receiving incompatible substitutes for the real reason.

The analytical category therefore changes from:

**V01-V05: war consciousness / institutional normalization with macro-politics gate CLOSED**

to:

**V06: causal-political infrastructure present; macro-politics gate AJAR / ACTIVE WATCH.**

A separate heavyweight political-warfare ledger is **not yet warranted**. We still lack sustained evidence about named governments, diplomatic history, enemy ideology, factional politics, leadership competition, resource allocation, or strategy comparable to *86* or *LOGH*. But it would now be incorrect to say the manga supplies no war-cause politics. It has begun to do so.

### 4.8 Child simplification recreates ideology immediately

Once fragments of the truth circulate, students quickly compress them again into simple narratives: `bad countries` want the book; therefore they are heroes fighting for peace. Another child punctures the glamorized framing.

This is a useful institutional-psychology observation. Political information does not move directly from fact to mature interpretation. Children convert complex strategic risk into morally legible stories that make compulsory participation easier to inhabit.

## 5. CH27 — `甘い熱病`: jealousy becomes diagnostic self-evidence

### 5.1 The bodily metaphor arrives before the category

CH27 opens with Sheena observing Mimi's easy physical/social interaction with other classmates and experiencing an unpleasant bodily reaction — a prickling/needle-like or feverish sensation she cannot immediately classify (`V06 / CH27 / p_0065-p_0073`).

This is important for simulation. Sheena's romantic awareness does not begin with a clean verbal thought such as `I am in love.` It begins as:

`Mimi closeness with another -> somatic discomfort -> monitoring -> comparison -> embarrassment -> self-questioning`.

The chapter title `甘い熱病` prepares a romantic reading, but the internal character model still has to earn it through Sheena's cognition.

### 5.2 Maternal-cue anxiety becomes an exclusivity question

Sheena remembers that Mimi's first attachment cue involved the smell of Mimi's mother. She begins wondering whether Mimi's special affection toward her is only a maternal-type association.

This does **not** support collapsing Mimi's attachment to Sheena into mother substitution. It shows Sheena herself worrying about what category she occupies for Mimi.

That distinction becomes especially important later in CH28 when the maternal sensory memory is explained more precisely.

### 5.3 Cookies become an involuntary competition test

While baking with Mimi, Sheena hears that Mimi liked Ari's rusk and abruptly asks which Mimi likes better — Ari's rusk or Sheena's cookies (`p_0077-p_0082`).

Mimi answers that Sheena's cookies are her favorite.

Sheena is immediately embarrassed by having asked. The content is trivial; the decision mechanism is not. Sheena has created a ranking test in response to attachment insecurity.

Candidate policy:

`Mimi directs pleasure/affection toward another -> exclusivity threat appraisal -> competitive comparison question -> desired reassurance obtained -> embarrassment/self-inquiry follows`.

This should not become a global `jealous personality` trait. The evidence is relationship-specific and newly emergent.

### 5.4 Sheena takes the problem to Ari rather than solving it privately

Sheena later confides in Ari (`p_0085-p_0092`). This is a meaningful agency shift from early-volume rumination. She still internalizes first, but she can now seek a trusted relational interpreter.

Ari frames Sheena as someone changed by accumulated time with Mimi and reminds her that Sheena became Mimi's first friend.

Sheena then offers the crucial self-hypothesis: perhaps what hurts is that she is no longer Mimi's **only** friend.

Ari does not accept friendship as a sufficient description. She says Sheena's gaze does not look like one directed merely at a friend:

`友だちに向ける眼差しには 見えないけれど`

Ari begins asking `あなた ミミちゃんのこと—`, and Sheena prevents the sentence from being completed.

The boundary is deliberate. CH27 establishes that the friendship-only model is failing. It does not yet provide Sheena's finished romantic label.

## 6. CH28 — `泣かないで`: origin loss, memory asymmetry, and a kiss that is not magic

### 6.1 Grooming opens the past

The chapter begins from one of the project's established ordinary-intimacy motifs: hair care (`V06 / CH28 / p_0093-p_0100`). Mimi attempts grooming; Sheena explains that her mother taught her how to care for herself.

The mundane act becomes an autobiographical route into Sheena's childhood.

### 6.2 Sheena's orphan status acquires a concrete causal event

V01 established that the school is an orphanage/weapon-training institution. V06 finally establishes Sheena's specific loss history.

Sheena remembers being fearful, clumsy, and strongly dependent on her mother. Her family then suffered an accident while traveling, and Sheena was left alone (`p_0097-p_0100`).

This materially deepens—but must not reductively explain—several existing traits:

- separation sensitivity;
- preference for routine and dependable domestic care;
- fear of sudden irreversible loss;
- difficulty with institutional normalization of death;
- the significance of a reliable `welcome home` relationship.

The correct formulation is **contextual enrichment**, not `all current attachment behavior is caused by childhood trauma.` The manga has shown multiple later mechanisms, especially Seiran's death and Mimi's repeated deployments, that independently strengthen these variables.

### 6.3 Mimi invites dependence without contempt

Mimi responds to Sheena's history by telling her that she can rely on / be dependent on Mimi too. This is consistent with Mimi's long-standing valuation of Sheena's ordinary, non-combat self.

The exchange again distinguishes weakness from worthlessness. Being someone who needs another person is not presented as moral failure.

### 6.4 Mimi remembers her mother through smell after biography has faded

When Sheena asks about Mimi's mother, Mimi cannot supply the ordinary autobiographical material one would expect (`p_0103-p_0109`). The face and many concrete details have faded across Mimi's unnaturally long life. Mimi knows some things secondhand — that her mother was exceptionally capable — but admits that old memories disappear.

What persists is sensory memory.

Mimi remembers the smell associated with her mother: warmth, food, sunlight, water, flowers. That is the memory Sheena activated when they first met.

This is the first precise resolution of V01's maternal cue.

The relationship model should now say:

`Sheena's smell -> ancient sensory mother-memory activation -> initial attachment/proximity cue`

not:

`Sheena = substitute mother`.

Everything after V01 has accumulated independent peer, friendship, conflict, trust, care, desire, and romantic/intimate evidence. The maternal cue is origin information, not an exhaustive category for the present bond.

### 6.5 Immortality becomes memory asymmetry

Mimi's fading memory introduces a new cost of indefinite life. V02-V04 emphasized accumulated bereavement; V06 adds erosion of autobiographical continuity.

Sheena asks whether Mimi will remember this place / these moments. Mimi answers that even if she forgets, seeing flowers may make her remember again (`p_0109-p_0113`). Objects and sensory cues can therefore act as retrieval bridges.

Mimi herself then says she does not want a future in which she no longer knows Sheena — Sheena's voice, eye color, warmth. Sheena responds internally that she does not want to forget any of it and will remember until she dies (`p_0113-p_0116`).

The temporal asymmetry is severe:

- Sheena's memory horizon is bounded by mortality;
- Mimi's life may continue beyond ordinary mnemonic continuity.

### 6.6 Wanting to be remembered is not a resurrection demand

Sheena privately admits that she does not want Mimi to forget her, but also judges that desire selfish enough that she cannot simply impose it as a demand. She therefore converts it into something enactable: leave more moments, more memories, more things that can remain (`p_0116-p_0118`).

This parallels V04's finite-presence ethic.

Sheena repeatedly answers uncontrollable permanence with **finite accumulation**:

`cannot guarantee eternal presence -> make the available time dense with recognizable care / memory`.

### 6.7 The kiss ontology is explicitly resolved

V01's early kisses could not be used as stand-alone romantic proof because direct magic transfer was a healing mechanism. V06 removes that ambiguity.

Immediately before deployment, Sheena initiates a sustained mouth-to-mouth kiss with Mimi (`p_0118-p_0119`). Afterward the scene explicitly states:

`…これも 魔法じゃないよ`

This is not healing magic.

That sentence is one of the highest-value evidence points in the relationship corpus because it is a source-controlled disambiguation. The kiss is intentionally intimate action outside the magical mechanism that complicated earlier interpretation.

Mimi is shocked and visibly flushed. She does not reject Sheena. Sheena then flees in acute embarrassment (`p_0120-p_0122`).

The correct V06 claim is therefore:

**deliberate reciprocal-presence intimate kiss / romantic evidence: strongly established**

while:

**formal mutual dating label or mutual verbal confession: still OPEN.**

## 7. CH29 — `好きな人`: Sheena reaches the lover-category threshold

### 7.1 After the kiss, ordinary routine is affectively transformed

CH29 begins with Sheena unable to sleep because of the kiss (`V06 / CH29 / p_0123-p_0127`). Ordinary shared actions that were already familiar — waking in the same bed, touching, moving through school together — now produce a heat/fire-like bodily awareness.

The important change is not that the actions themselves suddenly become romantic. It is that **Sheena's appraisal of them changes**.

Simulation-state update:

`familiar Mimi cue -> previously ordinary/comforting action -> post-kiss romantic salience -> autonomic heat/embarrassment -> self-monitoring`.

### 7.2 `はやく ミミと二人きりになりたい / ミミの一番になりたい`

At `p_0127`, Sheena's internal desire becomes much less ambiguous:

- she wants to be alone with Mimi soon;
- she wants to be Mimi's `一番` — Mimi's number one / most special person;
- a following `ミミに…` thought is interrupted rather than completed.

The first two propositions are explicit evidence. The incomplete third phrase must remain incomplete.

This is stronger than V05 external romantic legibility and stronger than CH27 jealousy. Sheena now consciously wants relational primacy.

### 7.3 Protective substitution remains active and socially rewarded

Class discussion returns the volume from romance to the war system (`p_0131-p_0136`). Mimi tells Sheena:

`シーナは ミミが死なせないもん`

Mimi will not let Sheena die.

Classmates immediately generalize the protection: if they remain near Mimi they will be safer; Mimi can defeat enemies on their behalf. Mimi cheerfully answers that she will fight for everyone.

This directly confirms that V05's protective-substitution motive remains active. Transparency has improved; tactical self-expenditure has **not** been shown to decrease.

The social reinforcement is especially important:

`Mimi can protect us -> classmates rely on her -> Mimi enjoys being useful -> substitution policy receives interpersonal reward`.

A policy can be loving, voluntary at the immediate level, and still be structurally reinforced in ways that deepen self-instrumentalization.

### 7.4 Sheena's anti-substitution response now includes shared exposure

Sheena answers that when the time comes, she will fight too (`p_0133`). She tells Mimi not to shoulder everything alone.

This requires careful classification. It is **not** evidence that Sheena has become combat enthusiastic or abandoned her non-normalization of killing. It is evidence that healer/support agency is moving toward shared battlefield responsibility rather than accepting Mimi as the sole shield.

Candidate transition:

`Mimi offered as sole protector -> Sheena rejects one-body burden concentration -> intends to enter shared risk/support role`.

Future volumes must test what `fight together` actually means operationally for a healer with Sheena's temperament.

### 7.5 The teacher both acknowledges Mimi's utility and condemns passive dependency

The classroom teacher explicitly says Kagari/Mimi's activity has reduced school damage and that enemy incursions are currently milder (`p_0134`). Yet the teacher refuses to let the class treat that as a guarantee of safety.

The teacher's language is overtly national/military:

`そんな態度で 国が守れますか？ 勝利できるんですか？`

Can the country be protected / can victory be achieved with that attitude?

This adds another V06 macro-political data point. The institution does not only train children to survive. It frames their obligation through **national defense and victory**.

The school therefore simultaneously:

- benefits from Mimi's exceptional substitution;
- refuses the rest of the children permission to become passive dependents;
- invokes national defense to justify continued competence and exposure.

### 7.6 Haru rejects using Mimi as a shield

Afterward Haru directly criticizes classmates for saying, in front of Mimi, that she should function as their shield / die in their place (`p_0137-p_0140`). Haru recognizes the personhood problem without Sheena having to supply it.

This strongly deepens Haru's V06 model. Haru is comparison-sensitive, but not therefore committed to hierarchy. Haru can identify when ability difference is being converted into entitlement over another person's body.

### 7.7 Quarrel is reframed as mutual work, not relationship failure

Sheena explains that she and Mimi have fought about these issues and that Sheena promised not simply to stop Mimi from fighting. She remains troubled.

Haru asks whether quarrelling means they are actually on bad terms. The conversation arrives at a more mature formulation: they are making an effort to understand each other (`p_0139-p_0141`).

That directly supports V05's new relationship policy:

`disagreement -> conflict -> mutual understanding effort -> relationship can persist without consensus`.

The significance is that a third party can now see the relationship's conflict not as evidence of failed attachment but as evidence that the relationship contains enough trust to sustain difference.

### 7.8 Sheena can describe why Mimi is lovable as a particular person

Asked about Mimi, Sheena produces one of the corpus's richest person-specific descriptions (`p_0141-p_0144`). She talks about Mimi liking to do things together, enjoying matching/shared routines, delighting in morning preparation, communal baths, gathering the same flowers, and reacting honestly to small experiences.

Sheena concludes that Mimi is `すごく可愛い` and that they became close because Mimi remained `ミミらしく` — herself.

This is crucial against two reductive readings:

- Sheena does not love Mimi merely because Mimi protects her.
- Sheena does not love Mimi merely as the sensory stand-in for Mimi's mother.

Sheena's affect is directed toward accumulated ordinary particularity.

### 7.9 Haru supplies the `恋人` counterfactual

Haru hears Sheena's description as `のろけ`-like gushing and tests the category. Sheena reflexively denies that she and Mimi are `そういうの` (`p_0143-p_0146`). The denial is not enough to close the question because it occurs alongside intense embarrassment and the explicit desires already established at `p_0127`.

Haru then asks the decisive counterfactual:

`ミミが キミ以外の恋人になっちゃってもいいの？`

Would Sheena be fine if Mimi became someone else's lover?

The final narrative page answers only with Sheena's stunned:

`恋人……？`

V06 ends there (`p_0147`).

That is an analytical boundary, not an invitation to import V07.

### 7.10 V06 romantic state at freeze

At V06, the safest high-confidence state is:

**Sheena romantic self-awareness: strongly established but still undergoing explicit category formation.**

Evidence now includes:

- jealousy / social-comparison discomfort;
- concern about no longer being Mimi's only friend;
- Ari's observation that Sheena's gaze is not merely friendly;
- deliberate non-healing mouth kiss;
- post-kiss romantic heat and preoccupation;
- explicit desire to be alone with Mimi;
- explicit desire to be Mimi's `一番`;
- describing Mimi as very cute and lovable in person-specific ordinary terms;
- shock when confronted with `恋人` as the relational category.

Still OPEN:

- a first-person explicit `I love Mimi romantically` declaration;
- a mutual dating agreement;
- Mimi's own explicit romantic self-label in symmetrical terms.

## 9. Character-state transitions at V06

### Sheena

## 11. Mortality, expendability, and personhood

V06 adds four durable distinctions.

### 11.1 Transparency does not equal safety
Mimi's wounded body becomes visible to Sheena, but her willingness to act as substitute remains. Epistemic improvement must not be mistaken for tactical risk reduction.

### 11.2 Immortality threatens memory continuity
An immortal body can outlast ordinary autobiographical detail. Personhood risk therefore includes not only repeated physical destruction and bereavement, but erosion of remembered relationships.

### 11.3 Memory objects/sensory cues can resist erasure without guaranteeing permanence
Flowers and smell function as retrieval cues. The series increasingly treats ordinary sensory life as a way of carrying relationships across absence without claiming that memory is infallible.

### 11.4 Humanization can occur socially while instrumentalization persists structurally
Peers can learn Mimi's ordinary preferences and call her by name while simultaneously relying on her to kill enemies and shield them. `being recognized as a person` and `being used as military infrastructure` are not mutually exclusive states.

## 12. Institution, war consciousness, agency, and the macro-politics gate

### 12.1 War cause — current source-grounded model

At the V06 boundary the strongest supported causal account is:

1. an exceptionally dangerous grimoire containing forbidden magic exists;
2. resurrection magic is among its contents;
3. powerful magic prevents easy destruction of the book;
4. the country in which the school exists possesses/protects it;
5. knowledge of its existence leaked externally;
6. enemy forces seek to obtain it;
7. authorities fear militaristic misuse;
8. recurring enemy incursions and national defense are therefore tied to possession of the book;
9. children are trained/deployed inside that defensive structure while much of the actual cause is kept secret from them.

### 12.2 What remains unknown

V06 still does **not** establish:

- the enemy country's name or complete political structure;
- whether all enemy combatants share the same motive;
- the original provenance of the grimoire;
- whether the current possessor has itself used or intends to use all forbidden contents;
- diplomatic attempts, treaties, bargaining, deterrence policy, or strategic factions;
- a complete history of how the war escalated;
- whether `militaristic country` refers to the current principal attacker in every case or a broader risk category.

Do not fill these gaps from genre expectation.

### 12.3 Gate transition

**Macro-politics gate: CLOSED → AJAR / ACTIVE WATCH.**

Reason: V06 supplies direct war-origin/cause information, secrecy/information-control evidence, national-defense rhetoric, and an enemy acquisition objective. That is enough to require longitudinal tracking inside the existing Institution/War/Agency ledger.

### 12.4 Agency implication

Knowledge increases without equal power increase.

Sheena and the others can now understand more of what they are protecting, but they still do not decide possession policy, negotiate with enemies, choose war aims, or control deployments.
