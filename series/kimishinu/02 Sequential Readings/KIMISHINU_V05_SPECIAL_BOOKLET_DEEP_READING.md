## 2. Chronology determination

The booklet is titled `けんかのあとは` - "after the quarrel" - and its internal state independently confirms a post-reconciliation placement.

The clearest anchor is the grooming routine. In V05 CH22, Ari notices that Mimi's hair has not been combed in the familiar way because Sheena and Mimi are still estranged. At the opening of the booklet, Sheena is again brushing/arranging Mimi's hair (`V05 SPECIAL / p_0001-p_0003`). Their ordinary morning movement is comfortable and coordinated rather than avoidant.

This establishes:

**earliest secure placement:** after the CH23 reconciliation.

The booklet does not contain a sufficiently diagnostic event to prove whether it occurs before or after every scene of CH24. Therefore the chronology should remain:

`post-CH23 reconciliation; relative ordering against CH24 unresolved`.

Do not force false precision merely because it was packaged with Volume 5.

## 3. Governing supplemental thesis - repair is proven by restored ordinary life, not by a second argument

The mainline V05 conflict is philosophically large: substitution, agency, weakness, concealment, and the right to disagree. The booklet deliberately does not reopen that debate. Instead it asks what reconciliation looks like in ordinary embodied time.

The answer is a sequence of resumed and expanded routines:

`hair grooming -> shared morning -> tending plants -> Sheena uses healing magic in low-stakes care -> bathing -> eating/studying -> shared sleep and physical closeness -> rain conversation -> Sheena invites Mimi into a formerly solitary post-rain ritual`.

The significance is not that the conflict vanished. Mainline CH23 explicitly established that it did not. The booklet shows that **unresolved disagreement no longer blocks domestic trust**.

This strengthens the V05 relationship model in a very specific way: repair becomes credible because the pair can return to the mundane behaviors that had functioned as state indicators before the quarrel.

## 4. Grooming returns as a relational-state marker

The opening restores Sheena brushing/arranging Mimi's hair (`p_0001-p_0003`). This is high-value longitudinal evidence because V05 mainline had already taught the reader to interpret the routine diagnostically.

Before quarrel:
`Sheena grooming Mimi -> ordinary closeness / dependable care`.

During quarrel:
`grooming absent -> Ari notices relational disturbance`.

After reconciliation:
`grooming restored -> embodied care resumes without need for verbal re-litigation`.

For simulation, this supports the rule that Sheena and Mimi often repair security through routine touch and co-presence after explicit conflict processing has finished.

## 5. Healing magic moves into ordinary domestic care

Sheena and Mimi visit the plants. Mimi is distressed because a pot/flower has been damaged after she knocked/dropped it, and Sheena reassures her (`p_0004-p_0006`). Sheena uses her developing healing/growth magic on the injured plant. New healthy growth visibly appears; Mimi responds with delighted praise (`p_0006-p_0008`).

This is supplemental but important evidence for Sheena's healer identity.

Mainline V05 demonstrated that Sheena trains because she wants practical capacity to relieve pain and avoid future regret. The booklet shows that the attribute is not psychologically reserved for battlefield or medical usefulness. It naturally enters **ordinary caretaking**.

That lowers the risk of modeling Sheena's healing development as a simple militarized skill-acquisition arc. Her magic belongs equally to:

- preserving small living things;
- repairing ordinary accidents;
- reassuring Mimi;
- making shared life more livable.

The moment also produces reciprocal warmth. Mimi's praise is immediate and uncomplicated; Sheena does not respond with grand self-importance. The value is in the restored life in front of them.

## 6. Domestic intimacy after the quarrel

The middle pages compress multiple ordinary activities (`p_0008-p_0014`):

- bathing together;
- drying/wrapping after the bath;
- eating;
- studying/reading;
- moving back toward bed;
- sleeping in close physical contact.

These are not new categories of intimacy for the pair, but their **post-conflict timing** changes their evidentiary value.

The booklet supports:

`major unresolved values conflict -> explicit repair -> ordinary bodily intimacy can resume -> relationship security does not require ideological consensus`.

The shared-bed material is especially useful as a negative constraint against simulating the quarrel as lingering emotional coldness after CH23. Whatever disagreement remains, the pair's attachment system has re-stabilized enough for familiar physical co-regulation.

## 7. Rain conversation - Sheena turns a solitary ritual into shared future

Rain keeps the girls awake/attentive in bed (`p_0012-p_0016`). The conversation remains low-temperature and sensory: humidity, the sound of water, physical closeness.

Sheena then discloses a small personal routine. After rainy days she used to walk alone to look at wild plants and the daisies in the back garden. Flowers that have received plenty of water shine and look especially beautiful.

The key transition is her final desire:

`「雨が上がったら… ミミと見たい……」`

This is modest but highly diagnostic relational evidence.

The ritual was previously solitary. Sheena does not discard it when attachment deepens; she **invites Mimi into it**. This is a healthy form of relationship-conditioned change: expansion of an existing self rather than replacement of self by relationship.

Mimi answers with uncomplicated anticipation (`「楽しみ～」`).

The booklet therefore adds a small but durable future-oriented state:

`rain -> tomorrow's wet flowers -> shared walk / shared seeing`.

That future is ordinary, near, and chosen. In a series organized around interrupted returns and uncertain lifespans, such small planned continuities carry disproportionate relational weight.

## 8. Character-model additions

### Sheena

**SUPPLEMENTAL STRENGTHEN - healer identity in non-emergency context**  
Sheena uses healing/growth magic on a damaged plant without battlefield pressure. Healing is becoming a general mode of care rather than only preparation for future casualties.

**SUPPLEMENTAL STRENGTHEN - relational expansion without self-erasure**  
A formerly solitary post-rain flower-viewing habit is not abandoned; Sheena wants to share it with Mimi. This supports the V05 mainline distinction between chosen growth and losing "the self as she is."

### Mimi

**SUPPLEMENTAL STRENGTHEN - ordinary praise and receptivity**  
Mimi reacts enthusiastically to Sheena's healing success. She can value Sheena's developing capacity in practice even though mainline V05 showed fear that training might endanger/change her.

This should not be overread as proof that Mimi's objection to dangerous training has vanished. The booklet concerns a low-risk domestic use of healing magic.

### Sheena-Mimi

**SUPPLEMENTAL STRENGTHEN - repair robustness**  
Post-quarrel grooming, bathing, eating, studying, sleep, and future planning show that explicit disagreement can coexist with restored ordinary intimacy.

## 9. Visual/dialogue evidence

### 9.1 Hair as relationship-state continuity
The first pages visually reverse the missing-grooming cue from CH22. No explanatory caption is required; the repeated bodily routine carries the state transition.

### 9.2 Plant repair as miniature thematic echo
A damaged living thing is not discarded or treated as irrelevant because it can recover. Sheena tends it. The scene is appropriately small: it echoes the larger personhood/healing problem without turning a houseplant into a one-to-one symbol for Mimi.

### 9.3 Rain as a different affective register from Ari's grief
Mainline CH24 uses rain as an Ari/Seiran grief trigger. The booklet uses rain inside Sheena-Mimi shared sleep and future planning. The same weather therefore supports distinct relationship-conditioned meanings rather than one universal "rain = sadness" code.

### 9.4 Language remains concrete
The final exchange is about flowers after rain, seeing them together, and looking forward to it. The booklet strengthens the series' tendency to encode large attachment states through ordinary sensory plans rather than abstract romantic speeches.
