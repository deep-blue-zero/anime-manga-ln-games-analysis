## 2. Volume thesis - love becomes a finite choice rather than a promise of symmetry

V08 made the future visible. Sheena could imagine adulthood, medical work, continued partnership, and the possibility that only her body would mature while Mimi remained physically fixed. She and Mimi verbally affirmed that bodily mismatch did not have to invalidate their relationship.

V09 asks whether that affirmation remains meaningful once the cost stops being theoretical.

The answer is not that Sheena becomes unafraid. V09 does almost the opposite. It reveals that she has been frightened beneath the repeated reassurance: afraid of becoming the only adult, afraid of separation, afraid of dying first, afraid of leaving Mimi behind, and afraid that Mimi may eventually lose even the memory of her face. She is also afraid of telling Mimi how frightened she is.

What changes is the *quality* of commitment. Sheena no longer needs the future to be painless or symmetrical in order to choose the relationship. When a hypothetical form of immortality is raised, she rejects it if it requires another person's sacrifice. When Fran points out that the future may repeatedly hurt her, Sheena still concludes that letting go of Mimi's hand now would hurt more.

The governing V09 transition is therefore:

`future asymmetry accepted conceptually -> survival desire becomes explicit -> healer identity becomes battlefield mission -> old Mimi weapon-confinement becomes direct fact -> lover identity explicitly separated from maternal association -> strategic-war management becomes explicit -> Haru turns adulthood into concrete practice -> Sheena turns healing vocation into portable battlefield preparation -> "always" is tested against mortality -> Sheena admits fear of aging/separation/forgetting -> sacrifice-based sameness rejected -> finite chosen presence reaffirmed -> major future-anxiety disclosure to Mimi remains open`.

V09 also changes the institutional layer. The school is no longer only a child-weapon institution inferred through training, casualty processing, and Mimi's repeated deployment. Fran directly remembers a period in which Mimi was kept underground as a secret weapon, retrieved for summons, washed/restored after return, and sent back underground. At the strategic level, adults discuss enemy force trends, supply-line versus strategy explanations, base allocation, combat-ready student production, information concealment, and whether Mimi should continue to be assigned to the predicted fiercest sector.

The politics/war gate therefore moves from **AJAR / ACTIVE WATCH** to **OPEN / ACTIVE STRATEGIC-WAR TRACKING**, while still remaining inside the existing institution ledger. V09 supplies operational strategy and force-management density, but not yet enough sustained named-government, diplomacy, ideological, factional, or political-leadership material to justify a standalone politics ledger.

## 3. CH39 - survival becomes part of Sheena's healer ethic

### 3.1 School progression and military progression become nearly inseparable

CH39 opens into an upperclass horizon in which ordinary advancement means increasingly combat-realistic training. The students can see that only a small number of steps remain before school progression becomes battlefield availability.

The manga does not present this transition as a single militarizing ceremony. Instead, the lethal future is embedded in ordinary class advancement, exercises, peer interaction, minor injury, treatment, and continued school routine. That continuity matters: institutional normalization works partly because battlefield preparation is experienced as the next grade of school rather than a separate social world.

### 3.2 Sheena's desire to live becomes direct and positive

Earlier volumes established Sheena's fear of death and her resistance to normalizing harm. V09 gives that fear a new positive formulation. During the chapter's combat-facing material, her internal language strips down to the desire to live, return alive, and survive.

This is not cowardice replacing moral agency. It is the first time the mature Sheena model can confidently say:

**Sheena positively wants her own continued life.**

That distinction matters because the corpus has repeatedly shown characters who discount themselves for different reasons: Mimi through recoverability and protective substitution, Seiran through relationship-protective self-expenditure, and Esta through grief-driven loss of future weight. Sheena now supplies a counter-model in which self-preservation is morally productive rather than selfish.

### 3.3 The healer role gives survival instrumental and relational meaning

Sheena treats a classmate's injured foot and carefully distinguishes pain suppression from actual complete resolution: the classmate should still go to the infirmary while the effect is useful. This continues the V07-V08 procedural healer model. Sheena is not becoming a magical cure-all. She assesses, reduces harm, and directs onward care.

Her key V09 reasoning is that **if she can return alive, she can treat the injured**. She frames her movement toward the battlefield through the purpose of saving lives.

That revises the relationship between fear and usefulness:

`battlefield proximity -> fear of dying -> desire to return alive -> living body retains healing agency -> survival becomes condition for helping others`.

Sheena's self-preservation is therefore no longer merely an aversion. It is becoming part of her public ethic of care.

### 3.4 Simulation implication

Do not simulate mature Sheena as someone who proves moral seriousness by accepting death. When danger rises, she may be frightened, but the mature V09 policy increasingly supports **survive in order to continue helping**. Her anti-disposability ethic now applies to herself more clearly than before.

## 4. CH40 - `mama`: the origin cue is not the current relationship

### 4.1 Mimi's direct care remains stable

The chapter opens with Sheena becoming physically unwell in the bath. Mimi reacts immediately and practically, getting her to medical care. This preserves a long-standing policy: visible Sheena vulnerability produces rapid Mimi intervention.

The important development in CH40 is not a new rescue mechanism, however. It is the historical institutional context Fran finally supplies.

### 4.2 The school once literally kept Mimi underground as a secret weapon

While grooming/drying Mimi, Fran remembers the period when the school still kept its secret weapon underground. Her description is repetitive and procedural:

- a summons occurs;
- Fran goes to retrieve Mimi;
- Mimi fights;
- on return Fran washes and restores her body;
- Mimi is sent back underground;
- the cycle repeats.

Mimi sometimes escaped and wandered the school on her own, causing Fran to be reprimanded when teachers found her.

This is a major institutional/personhood transition because it turns a previously inferred history of isolation and weapon management into direct fact.

The correct agency model is dual:

- **structural autonomy:** extremely low; Mimi was literally confined and cycled through combat use;
- **behavioral/tactical agency:** unusually high; she escaped, wandered, and resisted containment in small embodied ways.

Power never equaled freedom. V09 makes that rule materially undeniable.

### 4.3 Fran's care and institutional management coexist

Fran's retrospective also hardens her character model. She has long cared about Mimi bodily and personally, but she also participated in the retrieve/wash/restore/return cycle that made Mimi usable as an asset.

That should not be simplified into either:

- "Fran was merely an abusive jailer," or
- "Fran's personal care means she stood outside the institution."

The evidence supports a more uncomfortable dual role: **caregiver inside a dehumanizing management system**.

Fran's later recognition that Mimi no longer has to return to the underground room gives the current ordinary room/class/relationship life much greater historical weight. Ordinary life is not just pleasant contrast. It is an actual expansion of Mimi's social personhood and living space.

### 4.4 The mother-substitution hypothesis is explicitly closed

The V01 maternal smell cue has remained carefully constrained throughout the project. V06 established that the cue could be understood through degraded autobiographical memory and preserved sensory traces. V07-V08 independently established romantic primacy and mutual lover status.

CH40 now closes the remaining weak interpretation directly.

Fran teases Mimi about Sheena being mother-like. Mimi explicitly rejects the equivalence: Sheena is not her mother. Mimi acknowledges the original maternal-smell attraction but says that is no longer the whole reason; Sheena is her **lover**.

Current mature claim:

`initial maternal sensory association -> early approach cue -> independently accumulated friendship/care/specialness/desire -> mutual lover identity -> mother substitution rejected explicitly`.

This is a **REVISE / CLOSE** current mature claim, not a rewrite of V01. The V01 reader was correct to treat the smell cue as unresolved attachment evidence because the later distinction did not yet exist.

### 4.5 Mimi's romantic ontology becomes explicit in her own terms

Mimi explains that friendship stopped being enough because the kind of love between them needed a special, exclusive one-person status. She describes the pair as having made each other "special."

The formulation is highly consistent with her established voice:

- concrete rather than theoretical;
- person-specific;
- organized through specialness, one-person exclusivity, wanting, touch, and behavior;
- emotionally direct.

This does **not** mean Mimi requires Sheena to abandon friendships or distributed care. V07 already demonstrated that she can preserve Sheena's healer role while claiming a unique dyadic place.

Her simplest current formulation is also the most characteristically Mimi-like: she loves the parts of Sheena that are simply **Sheena**.

## 5. CH41 - `usotsuki`: war strategy becomes explicit while three girls turn futurehood into action

### 5.1 Adult strategic discussion is now recurring source evidence

V09 supplies the densest strategic conversation in the corpus so far. Adults discuss:

- a declining trend in enemy deployed strength;
- uncertainty over whether that decline reflects supply-line problems or a strategic shift;
- inability to read the enemy's intention confidently;
- whether force distributions across bases should be reviewed;
- whether military strength should be adjusted merely because visible enemy numbers have fallen;
- the danger of making decisions from numbers alone;
- student combat-readiness and exam throughput;
- whether newly qualified students can meet the next summons;
- the claim that the institution does not intend to let students die pointlessly;
- deliberate concealment of strategic uncertainty from students;
- whether Mimi should continue to be placed in the region expected to see the fiercest fighting.

This is qualitatively different from V06's first causal war explanation. We now have **active operational interpretation and resource allocation**, not merely knowledge that a forbidden grimoire caused foreign acquisition pressure.

### 5.2 Politics/war gate transition

**V08 state:** AJAR / ACTIVE WATCH.  
**V09 state:** **OPEN / ACTIVE STRATEGIC-WAR TRACKING.**

The gate opens because the source now repeatedly exposes:

`enemy force data -> competing strategic interpretation -> uncertainty -> allocation decision -> student combat pipeline -> information control -> exceptional-asset placement`.

Still, a new standalone political-warfare ledger is **not yet justified**. V09 remains thin on:

- named national leadership;
- internal political factions;
- diplomacy;
- developed enemy ideology;
- public opinion / propaganda architecture;
- legislative/legal structure;
- sustained national strategy history.

The existing institution/war-consciousness/agency ledger remains the correct canonical home, now with an active strategic sublayer.

### 5.3 The information asymmetry is deliberate, not accidental

Adults explicitly prefer that the students not notice the strategic uncertainty. This strengthens a long-running institutional pattern: children are required to become militarily competent faster than they are allowed to become politically informed.

They are given enough information to obey, train, deploy, and survive tactically, while causal/strategic interpretation remains concentrated above them.

That matters to agency analysis. The students' choices occur inside **structured epistemic deprivation**, not merely lack of curiosity.

### 5.4 Mimi remains an allocation problem as well as a person

The adult question of whether Mimi should remain assigned to the predicted fiercest sector shows that V05's protective-substitution problem has not disappeared after relationship formation.

Institutionally, Mimi's recoverability still creates an incentive to concentrate danger in her body.

Critically, V09 does **not** supply a clean battlefield sequence demonstrating that her future with Sheena has changed tactical self-risk. The frozen test must therefore remain indeterminate rather than being declared successful because the couple now has more future language.

### 5.5 Ari chooses the combat proficiency exam despite knowing Seiran would object

Ari tells Esta she will take the combat proficiency exam. Her reasoning is conflicted. She knows Seiran is no longer waiting in the battlefield world and believes Seiran would be hurt to know Ari is moving toward it. Yet Ari also admits that somewhere inside she had been waiting for the day she would be called.

This is not evidence of a simple ideological conversion or an active death wish.

The stronger model is:

`grief integrated enough for ordinary life -> combat world remains emotionally charged because Seiran inhabited it -> opportunity for direct participation appears -> Ari knowingly moves toward it despite anticipating Seiran's disapproval -> motive remains mixed/open`.

Esta understands rather than moralizes, which reinforces the corpus-wide distinction between recognizing dangerous motives and flattening them into a single pathology.

### 5.6 Haru turns adulthood from aspiration into practice

V08 gave Haru the desire to become an adult without yet knowing what adulthood would contain. V09 produces the first strong behavioral implementation.

Haru:

- attends more classes than before;
- preserves the goal of becoming able to live by personal capacity;
- explicitly plans to stop partway if the body reaches its limit rather than pushing until collapse;
- accepts a peer's offered hand/help;
- frames participation itself as meaningful even when performance is poor;
- begins to imagine that after achieving independence, a future relationship with another person might be something worth trying.

This strongly revises the early autonomy hypothesis. Haru is **not** defined by refusal of help. The mature mechanism is:

`support expands viable self-authored options -> can accept`;

versus:

`support decides identity/future on Haru's behalf -> resist`.

### 5.7 Haru's self-definition remains deliberately unresolved

V09 does **not** provide a self-chosen gender, sexuality, or fixed attraction label. Haru can imagine someday facing another person relationally without knowing who that person will be or what category will ultimately fit.

Therefore V08's self-definition constraint is PRESERVED. Do not infer a label from pronoun, uniform, presentation, or openness to future relationship.

### 5.8 Sheena begins engineering battlefield medicine

Sheena experiments with putting healing magic into a portable bottle/container and explicitly intends to bring it to the battlefield.

This is a major operationalization of the V08 adult vocation. Her medical future is no longer only career imagination. It is changing present training behavior:

`healing aptitude -> infirmary role -> adult medical vocation -> battlefield portability problem -> experiment with stored/portable healing`.

The attempt does not need to work perfectly yet to matter. It proves that the future vocation has become a present design problem.

### 5.9 "Always" becomes a claim the story is preparing to interrogate

The chapter returns to Sheena and Mimi in ordinary intimacy. They want to remain together and verbally reaffirm continued presence.

The emotional truth is real. But CH41 intentionally places "always" beside a future in which only Sheena ordinarily grows older. That prepares CH42's correction: **sincere forever-language is not the same thing as literal eternity.**

## 6. CH42 - `watashi no koi`: finite love becomes the chosen answer

### 6.1 The nightmare unifies death, separation, and forgetting

CH42 opens from intimate ordinary life and then fractures into a nightmare/imagined combat sequence.

Sheena confronts catastrophic injury and the possibility of her own death. Her fear is now explicitly relational as well as self-preservational: she does not want to die and leave Mimi behind.

The dream then converts death into a second horror: Mimi searching and eventually being unable to remember Sheena's face.

The mature Sheena fear system therefore contains at least three linked but distinct threats:

1. **death:** Sheena's own existence ends;
2. **separation:** Mimi continues without her;
3. **memory erosion:** even the relationship's personal specificity may eventually fade for Mimi.

V06-V08 had already supplied the factual architecture for this fear. V09 finally shows how deeply Sheena has internalized it.

### 6.2 Ordinary school life continues around deployment absence

After the nightmare material, the manga returns to homework, class absence, studying, notes, and make-up work. Mimi has academic obligations because summons overlapped with school.

The school adjusts expectations around war absence rather than treating military interruption as exceptional. This is another example of institutional simultaneity:

`child/student life + military asset use` are administratively made compatible.

Sheena notices and praises Mimi's real effort in studying. That detail matters because mature Mimi must not be reduced to immortal weapon + lover. Ordinary developmental/social competence remains part of the personhood model even when biological maturation is fixed.

### 6.3 Sheena asks the institutional future question directly

In conversation with Fran, Sheena asks whether Mimi can now graduate together with the others.

The question itself is significant because V08's future problem was mostly dyadic and bodily. V09 adds an institutional uncertainty:

**Even if Sheena and Mimi accept different bodily timelines, will the institution release Mimi into the same social future?**

The answer remains unresolved. Sheena therefore builds a contingency: if Mimi remains at the school, Sheena will work toward employment at a nearby medical facility.

This operationalizes "stay together" without pretending she can control Mimi's institutional status.

### 6.4 "Always" is explicitly separated from literal eternity

Fran points out that saying one will always stay with someone does not literally mean endless immortal coexistence. Parting comes to all lives; Mimi is different partly because she will encounter that fact more often than most people.

This is an important semantic correction. The relationship's promise should be modeled as:

`continue choosing proximity across the future they actually have`

not:

`guarantee metaphysical endlessness`.

### 6.5 V08 reassurance is revised: Sheena was afraid all along

V08 established that Sheena could affirm love despite bodily mismatch. V09 reveals the internal cost behind that affirmation.

Sheena admits that although she repeatedly told herself and others that they could remain lovers and each other's special person even as their ages and appearances diverged, she has actually been frightened by the prospect of becoming the only one who grows up.

This does **not** reject V08. It refines it:

**V08 commitment was genuine, but commitment did not equal emotional ease.**

The mature model is stronger because it can hold both states simultaneously:

`I believe our relationship can continue` + `the asymmetry terrifies me`.

### 6.6 The temptation to stop Sheena's time is morally diagnostic

Sheena briefly wishes that her own time could stop too. Fran then raises the hypothetical possibility of resurrection or some path to a body like Mimi's.

This provides an unusually clean values-under-pressure test. Sheena is not rejecting immortality because she has no desire for synchronization. She *does* desire it.

She rejects the option because magic that produces another sacrifice is unacceptable.

This is one of the strongest confirmations in the corpus of her personhood ethic:

`private anguish + desired solution -> discover solution requires another person's sacrifice -> reject solution despite personal benefit`.

The anti-disposability principle survives direct self-interest.

### 6.7 Marriage and equal time remain unresolved, not denied away

Sheena explicitly confronts that she and Mimi cannot live the same amount of time and says even marriage may not be available to them in the future she imagines. The text does not provide enough legal/social infrastructure here to convert that into a broad doctrine about law. The evidentiary claim is narrower: **Sheena presently understands their future as lacking ordinary symmetric adult milestones.**

That subjective understanding is psychologically important even if later volumes change the external conditions.

### 6.8 Future forgetting becomes an explicit relationship fear

Sheena now directly fears that Mimi will eventually forget her too.

This strengthens the memory problem substantially but still does **not** establish that Mimi's diary, flower crown, objects, or routines form a consciously designed anti-forgetting system.

The correct transition is:

`memory erosion fact -> relationship-specific future forgetting fear becomes explicit`

while

`deliberate memory-preservation program -> OPEN`.

### 6.9 The final choice: letting go now would hurt more

Fran is not trying to deny the relationship. She is warning Sheena that the future may repeatedly hurt and that seeing Sheena cry makes Fran want to tell her to stop before the pain deepens.

Sheena's response supplies V09's final ethical/romantic thesis:

**Even knowing the future may hurt, letting go of Mimi's hand now would hurt more.**

This is not naive optimism. It is not faith that death, aging, institutional retention, or memory loss will conveniently disappear.

It is a consciously finite choice:

`future pain acknowledged -> no sacrificial escape -> no guarantee of symmetry -> present person remains worth choosing -> continue relationship`.

### 6.10 The relationship still contains a disclosure gap

The final note prevents V09 from being mistaken for perfect transparency. Sheena is afraid of Mimi knowing the depth of these feelings and fears.

Thus the current relationship state is:

- trust: very high;
- love/lover status: secure;
- shared future desire: explicit;
- acceptance of bodily asymmetry: principled but emotionally difficult;
- full disclosure of Sheena's future anxiety to Mimi: **OPEN**.

This is a valuable simulation distinction. High trust does not imply immediate disclosure of every fear.

## 9. Mortality, expendability, and personhood

### 9.1 Sheena's own life is now explicitly claimable

The series' anti-disposability ethic began with Sheena resisting the treatment of Mimi's recoverable body as expendable. It has since generalized to enemies, peers, Seiran, Esta, and Haru. V09 makes the inward application much clearer: **Sheena's own survival matters because Sheena herself is a person with future desires and because her continued agency can preserve others.**

### 9.2 Immortality is not only survival; it is repeated asymmetry

Mimi's anomaly now includes:

- extreme recoverability;
- inability to die in the ordinary sense established by the resurrection condition;
- fixed bodily development;
- repeated exposure to others' deaths;
- autobiographical memory erosion;
- possible future retention by the institution;
- repeated need to navigate social time while the body remains visibly unchanged.

V09 therefore strengthens the distinction:

`continued existence != shared time != ordinary adulthood != freedom != remembered continuity`.

### 9.3 Sheena refuses to solve mortality by exporting its cost

The resurrection hypothetical is ethically diagnostic precisely because Sheena wants what it offers. Her refusal is meaningful because the solution addresses a genuine fear.

The decision rule is:

`desired escape from mortality/asymmetry -> identify nonconsensual sacrifice cost -> reject escape -> continue finite relationship`.

### 9.4 Old underground confinement is direct personhood deprivation

Mimi's historical treatment is no longer describable only as social isolation. Literal underground confinement, deployment retrieval, bodily restoration, and return after use instantiate the weapon/person distinction materially.

Her current room, classmates, lover, ordinary meals, schoolwork, and ability to wander openly are therefore not sentimental decoration. They represent concrete enlargement of lived personhood relative to the previous regime.

### 9.5 Memory fear rises; deliberate anti-forgetting strategy remains unproven
