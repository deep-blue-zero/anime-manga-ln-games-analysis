## Standalone study decisions

The two lead monographs have independent questions and repeated opportunities to test them. The partnership study needs the V05–V07 civilian and professional channels as well as baseline and midpoint evidence. A case-local counterexample matters where a specialist argument would otherwise generalize. Ayane’s intervention, Mitsuki’s mediated agency, Shichijō’s choice of secrecy and Shiobara’s relevance after clearance can be treated substantively in their volume readings and in the studies they constrain. Their evidence is concentrated in a small number of scenes. A dedicated individual model or longitudinal monograph for any of them would need a separate, narrower question and account of opportunities.

The mystery study should compare when a reader can identify a meaningful anomaly, discriminate alternatives and allocate acts. The policing and form study should show how authority, access, time and trained perception constrain those allocations. Place, visual media and Japanese register belong there or in the relevant character study when they change the reasoning. A separate evidence matrix would currently repeat stable, compact routes already supplied by the ledgers. No additional source tranche is required for the literary claims identified at this gate. The terminal reconciliation preserves the bounded uncertainties that additional reading of the same admitted material cannot resolve. The convergence review must check whether any interpretation exceeds those limits.
