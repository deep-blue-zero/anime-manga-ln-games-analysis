### KCMP-V07-001 — Kawamura and Aonuma were fellow apprentices, not teacher and pupil

- **Volume and source locator:** V07 C01 p0384; C03 p0331; C04 p0465 (context C02 p0252–0257).
- **Subject:** character backstory (Kawamura, Aonuma Kazushige).
- **GPT claim:** `readings/KSOA_V07_READING_INTRODUCTION.md` §3 "Current-volume story synopsis", ¶2 (l.38): Ugetsuan was "run by Kawamura's former teacher Aonuma". The deep reading's `#ksoa-v07-h-0003` says only "a former apprenticeship tie between the chefs", and no ledger row states the relation.
- **CB1 claim:** `CB1_V07_READING.md` §C ("senior fellow-apprentice at a Ginza 料亭"); `CB1_V07_EXIT_STATE.md` §1 row 2; `CB1_V07_CHARACTER_EVIDENCE.md` §2.3 (Kawamura is "青沼's 弟弟子").
- **First-pass source check:** C01 p0384: Tomonaga says the two were 兄弟弟子 who worked at a Ginza ryōtei as disciples of the same chef (key wording: 同じ料理人の弟子). C03 p0331: Hashio repeats 兄弟弟子. C04 p0465: Shiobara reports that Kawamura called Aonuma his 兄弟子 and said he was bullied by him when young. Aonuma is a teacher (師匠 or 板長) only to Izumida, Eguchi and Nagasaka (C02 p0255–0257; C03 p0191, p0531).
- **Difference type:** fact.
- **Counterevidence and alternatives:** none in the text. The slip probably carries over the pupils' relation to Aonuma.
- **Disposition:** `GPT_ERROR`. **Confidence:** high.
- **Proposed destination:** GPT reading erratum (V07 introduction §3 ¶2); a new V07 character-ledger PERSON/REL row for Kawamura → Aonuma (senior fellow apprentice; reported rivalry).

## 7. Compression note (V07)

The GPT V07 deep reading is 25.7 KB. The CB1 V07 set is about 400 KB: a 65 KB reading, 139 KB of section notes and 93 KB of character evidence, plus freezes and an exit state. Much of GPT's brevity is genuine economy. Its prose is accurate where it speaks; it keeps the key epistemic distinctions (borrowing against causation, attribution against warrant, spoken against private commitment); and its checkpoints keep prospective knowledge bounded.

The compression nonetheless **lost genuinely useful evidence**:

- **Case mechanics:** the weapon, and the custody of the recording.
- **The victim's motive:** Kawamura's complex toward Aonuma.
- **Chronology:** the inquiry calendar.
- **Japanese-language analysis:** no V07 VOICE rows at all.

In the absence of this material, unchecked phrasing could slip into the reader-facing introduction: "teacher", "tells Motoya", "evening interval" and the pronoun "He".
