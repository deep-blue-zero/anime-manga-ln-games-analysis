## 7. Compression note

V02 is not one of the late, short GPT readings: its deep reading is 45.8 KB. Even so, its compression loses useful evidence rather than just achieving economy. It deliberately avoids naming objects, times and Japanese forms. That keeps the frozen horizon records clean of later knowledge. But the habit carries into the retrospective sections, the case ledger and the introduction. A user of the canonical corpus cannot learn what fell, from which light, how the alarm was handled, or how anyone speaks.

The losses fall in three places:

- **Operational reconstruction** (KCMP-V02-001, 013, 014).
- **Japanese-language and ordinary-life evidence for character reconstruction** (KCMP-V02-008, 010).
- **Clue analysis**, where the corpus states detectability conclusions without the paragraph pair that grounds them (KCMP-V02-005, 006).

The abstraction also produced two outright errors in the derived introduction (KCMP-V02-002, 003). Where GPT's economy works is its inference discipline: horizons, the separation of testimony from inference, and its refusal to promote Motoya's view (KCMP-V02-011). That should be kept while the missing facts are restored through ledger rows rather than by rewriting the frozen reading.
