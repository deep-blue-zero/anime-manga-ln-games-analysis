### KCMP-SERIES-001 — Extortion and silencing as the recurring case structure

- **GPT claim or omission.** No series artifact names extortion or the silencing of a would-be discloser as a recurring structure. In the whole GPT corpus "blackmail" occurs once, as a negative (`KSOA_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v04-cl-0001-r02`: "without independently establishing parentage, blackmail or a motive"). Extortion is named for V04 (`KSOA_CASE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v04-case-0002`, "Reported extortion") and V07. V05 is euphemized: `readings/KSOA_V05_READING_INTRODUCTION.md` says Hani met Wakao to "press him about the hotel design", and `readings/KSOA_V05_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v05-h-0014` speaks of a "financial demand". For V01 the motive is reduced to "pressure on household staff" (`readings/KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v01-h-0012`). The integrated synthesis files V05's assault under "The community's competing ways of seeking redress" (`studies/KSOA_INTEGRATED_SERIES_SYNTHESIS.md#the-work-people-leave-behind`).
- **CB1 claim.** LS §2 (Concern 1) and §3.3; `CB1-CL-045`, `CB1-CL-046`, `CB1-CL-047`. Five killings silence a would-be discloser. In V05 and V07 the extortionist kills the person who refused to pay. The killers in V05 and V07 meant to threaten.
- **Primary Japanese evidence (verified).**
  - V01 C03 p0977: 和枝 quotes 富田 on a man who would bleed them for life, 「一生食いつかれて強請られるんだ。殺すしかない」.
  - V04 C03 p0920: 宮崎 paid once and was asked again, 「何度でも金をせびってくるに違いない」.

  - V07 C04 p0598: 「何度も何度もあなたから金を脅し取られる」.
- **Difference type.** Evidence coverage; interpretation (thematic); presentation (euphemism).
- **Counterevidence and alternatives.** V01's central death is a suicide. 圭子's blow in V03 is partly rage at a sneer [V03 C03 p0421]. V02's motive stays plural [V02 C03 p1031, p1037]. In V05 the extortion and the route are Asumi's reconstruction; 羽仁 admits only the blow [C03 p0954], although the prologue's "demand" about "my past" narrows the alternatives. GPT's care, protection and credit thesis is compatible with this pattern and does not replace it.
- **Disposition.** `GPT_OMISSION` (series theme). Secondary: `GPT_COMPRESSION` (V01 motive; V05 extortion euphemized).
- **Confidence.** High that the pattern is absent from GPT and supported by the source; moderate on how much thematic weight it carries.
- **Proposed correction and destination.** Record the extortion and silencing structure as a series claim and qualify the integrated synthesis's V05 "redress" framing. Destinations: new `KSOA-SER-CL` row in the claim revision ledger; integrated synthesis (successor revision); V01 case ledger (motive note against `KSOA-V01-RES-0003`); V05 case ledger and reading erratum.

## 7. Compression note

The GPT series layer is compact: three studies of about 13 KB each, against CB1's 114 KB synthesis. Most of that compactness is economy, not loss. The detectability distinctions, the closure taxonomy and the variable institutional model are stated more precisely in fewer words than CB1's equivalents, and on those points CB1's extra length adds illustration rather than new claims.

The losses cluster in V05–V07 and in cross-volume patterns, and they come from the late readings feeding the studies:
- **V05.** Hani's extortion is reduced to "financial demand" or "press him about the hotel design", so the integrated synthesis files the assault under "redress" (001).
- **V06.** The drugged host and the shouting test never reach the case ledger, so the mystery study explains the alibi abstractly ("a common unsupported middle") without its mechanism (004).
- **V07.** The finale's intoxication and addressee are dropped, which inflates the series partnership claim to "High" (003).

These are real evidential losses, not stylistic economy, because each changes a series-level claim or its confidence. CB1's length also carries cost: one misread line becomes the "strongest counterevidence" to an attribution (002), and one developmental arc is stated more strongly than its own table supports (005).
