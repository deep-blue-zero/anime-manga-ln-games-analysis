## 2A. The self-contained monograph standard (§5.5.1, in force) and qualification

### 2A.1 Applicability

Policy v1.12 (dated 2026-11-14) added §5.5.1. GPT's three character studies are dated 2026-11-13. Under §10 they are grandfathered: their nonconformance "is not by itself a correction duty", but the standard binds any general revision or superseding monograph, and an established project "records qualification decisions for the characters within its scope when it next enters character or specialist synthesis, or at its next architecture review checkpoint". CB1's lead monographs were written to the pending text of the standard as a nonbinding design reference.
