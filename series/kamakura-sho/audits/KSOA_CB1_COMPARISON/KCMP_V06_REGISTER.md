H-0010: "The scene cites additional circumstantial support while deferring forensic collection." Neither the drug, nor the prescription, nor the doctor's statement, nor the champagne appears anywhere in the GPT corpus.
- **CB1 claim:** In `CB1_V06_READING.md` §C (gathering), Asumi "charges 久米 with putting his prescribed sleep-inducer into the master's champagne (investigators already have his doctor's statement)". §F2 row "Sleep-inducer". §F4: "off-page finding disclosed at the reveal… decisive… depends on a newly introduced fact". `CB1_V06_EXIT_STATE.md` rows 28 and 36 and CB1-CL-031 follow it. - **First-pass source check (verified):**
  - C03 p0711 (Yakushiji): 「八時頃から眠くて眠くて仕方なくなった」.
  - p0717 (Asumi): 「久米さん、あなた、薬師寺先生のシャンパンに睡眠導入剤を混入させましたね。」

- **Disposition:** `GPT_OMISSION`.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Canonical destination:** V06 reading erratum. Case ledger: a new EV row for the reported prescription and doctor's statement, and append-only revisions of EV-0010 and ACT-0002. ## 7. Compression note

The GPT V06 reading is 25.6 KB and the introduction 7.6 KB, with 52 V06 ledger rows (count per the source-completion audit). CB1's V06 reading alone is 67.6 KB, and its V06 records total about 390 KB with the supporting files. Some of GPT's shortness is economy. Its prospective records, causal-role distinctions and disclosure states are accurate, and in one place (KCMP-V06-004) more careful than CB1's fuller account.

The compression has nonetheless lost evidence that is useful, in four kinds:
- **Case mechanics.** The drug and doctor's statement (001) and the distance and soundproofing proof (002) are absent, so a GPT user cannot explain how the alibi failed. GPT's abstractions ("tests the house and accounts") cannot be expanded back into what happened.
- **Character reconstruction.** The address dispute (005), the evidence on the hostility motive (006), Yakushiji's arc (007) and the register inventory are gone.
- **Setting.** No V06 place or institution is named (010).
- **Residual ambiguity.** The open threads are not listed (011).

A structural risk compounds this. GPT's paragraph ranges are broad enough to include material their row text never mentions (EV-0010 spans p0717–0723). Locator coverage therefore gives false assurance that the evidence was considered.

Conversely, GPT's compression produced no wrong case facts in the material I checked. Its two accuracy defects are a softened paraphrase (003) and a misattribution (009).

On balance, the shorter V06 reading is economical in its interpretive framing. It is lossy on evidence for case mechanics, character and setting, and those losses should be recovered through errata and ledger revisions rather than accepted as economy.
