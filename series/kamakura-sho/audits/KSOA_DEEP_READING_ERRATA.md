# Deep reading errata

**Spoilers for all seven volumes.**

The seven frozen deep readings, `readings/KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md` through `readings/KSOA_V07_DEEP_READING.md`, remain byte-unchanged. This carrier governs their correction. Where an erratum and a frozen reading differ, the erratum gives the current reading of the source, and the frozen text remains the record of what was written at its horizon. Read each erratum alongside the record or section it names.

**Basis.** Each erratum implements a type (a) integration action of the Phase 4 adjudications of the KSOA-CB1 comparison: `audits/KSOA_CB1_COMPARISON/ADJ_A.md` (V01–V03), `audits/KSOA_CB1_COMPARISON/ADJ_B.md` (V04–V05), `audits/KSOA_CB1_COMPARISON/ADJ_C.md` (V06–V07) and `audits/KSOA_CB1_COMPARISON/ADJ_D.md` (series and character records). Every adjudication was decided against the Japanese witness, never by agreement between the two generations. KSOA-CB1 (manifest SHA-256 `35f7a16eddf07a6dffb0750e1146c34874d227a486886db36ca7bf9248113040`) is an independently authored, noncurrent generation; its findings enter here only through adjudication. Each row cites its adjudication ID. Where two adjudication groups proposed the same correction, it appears once, with both IDs.

**How to read the tables.**
- The native locators column uses `SEC pppp–pppp` in the volume's witness `KSOA-JP-Vnn-W01`. A bracketed `[SEC pNNNN]` inside a correction refers to the same volume unless another volume is named.
- Japanese wording is quoted only in short phrases verified against the source line.
- Most narration is focalized through Motoya Kitsukawa. The corrections keep observed conduct, a character's own account, narratorial interpretation, inference and open questions distinct.
- An erratum that adds evidence available before a reveal records reader-side availability. It does not turn a frozen prospective record into knowledge held at its horizon. Unless the erratum says the frozen statement was wrong when written, what the record says was reasonable to infer at its horizon stands.

| Transition | Use in this carrier |
|---|---|
| `STRENGTHEN` | Supplies omitted evidence or specifics within the frozen statement's scope |
| `REVISE` | Changes wording, attribution, sequence, scope or mechanism |
| `DOWNGRADE` | Narrows the support or lowers the weight of a frozen statement |
| `REJECT` | The evidence defeats the frozen statement |

Interpretive consequences are versioned in the [claim revision ledger](../KSOA_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md). Evidence additions are rows in the [case](../KSOA_CASE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md), [disclosure](../KSOA_DISCLOSURE_AND_HYPOTHESIS_LEDGER.md) and [character](../KSOA_CHARACTER_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md) ledgers marked "Reconciliation 2026-11-15".
