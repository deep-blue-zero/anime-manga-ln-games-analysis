# Character reconstruction readiness

This is a producer assessment of the available evidence for Asumi Ogasawara and Motoya Kitsukawa. It assesses interpretive sufficiency for specific uses within the admitted Japanese continuity. The [reconstruction specification](../KSOA_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_SPEC.md) governs candidate evidence and independent tests. The [Asumi](../studies/KSOA_ASUMI_OGASAWARA.md) and [Motoya](../studies/KSOA_MOTOYA_KITSUKAWA.md) monographs and the [directed relationship study](../studies/KSOA_PARTNERSHIP.md) supply the literary basis. Every passage in the admitted corpus has been exposed to the producer. No operational validation result or capability assignment follows from this assessment.

## Sufficiency by use

| Intended use | Interpretive assessment |
|---|---|
| `DIALOGUE` | Both leads have sufficient original-language evidence for source-near speech with familiar partners, colleagues and witnesses, scoped by role and state. Motoya’s extensive thought is a different register and should have a separate evidence route. Evidence for rare interlocutors, sustained intimate dialogue and unrestricted stylistic imitation is insufficient. |
| `PROFESSIONAL_CONTEXT` | Both have enough observed decisions and counterexamples for source-near appraisals with attested permissions. Asumi’s action changes with local knowledge, evidentiary leverage and supervision. Motoya’s choices change with available corroboration and the audience. Authority outside the depicted hierarchy requires a stated assumption. |
| `MUNDANE_SOCIAL` | Both support situated interpretations of meals, travel, familiar talk and some off-duty interactions. The books repeatedly show these activities next to work. Continuous domestic routines or unfamiliar social groups require abstention or a visibly weaker inference. |
| `ETHICAL_DELIBERATION` | Both have diagnostic scenes involving confidence, pressure, and transmission or withholding. Motoya’s narrated appraisal supports a wider scope of interior analysis. Asumi’s evidence is stronger for enacted or stated judgments than the route by which she reaches them privately. |
| `ROMANCE_RELATIONSHIP` | The directed work relationship and bounded emotional episodes support interpretation. Motoya’s hopeful inference about Emiri has a decisive correction. The text supplies insufficient evidence for a stable mutual romantic relationship between the leads or for an extended romantic behavioral model. |
| `CROSS_SCENARIO` | Source-near counterfactual questions can be formulated by changing one item of knowledge, authority or relationship at a time. The seven novels offer no direct comparison continuity and no reliable validation for cross-series or distant scenarios. |

The use judgments establish enough evidence to prepare a bounded candidate for routine work, register and several kinds of interaction. They also identify uses that would need new evidence or abstention. A case-local person may support a narrow reconstruction of one decision or interaction. The evidence for a general-purpose reconstruction of a case-local subject has not been established here.

## What an independent evaluator would need

An authorized evaluator would pin the model version, same-subject discovery references, evidence basis, opportunity set, access cutoff and exclusions. The useful discriminators already visible are the ability to differentiate withholding from deception, response to a qualified authority, an ordinary interaction with a familiar person, and dialogue before and after a change in evidentiary pressure. The cited scenes can be used in retrospective replay. A custodian would need to supply genuinely unexposed and diagnostic material for independent testing and retain a separate retest reserve. Testing should keep factual leakage, implausible powers, unsupported interiority and incorrect Japanese register separately diagnosable. Reviewed discovery and registry actions remain with the designated maintainers. [Independent test protocol](../KSOA_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_SPEC.md#interpretive-readiness-and-operational-validation).
