# Analytical method

Apply this adaptation of the [generic novel method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_NOVEL_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) to the authorized Japanese witnesses in publication order. The [witness map](KSOA_SOURCE_AND_WITNESS_MAP.md) governs exact locators and coverage. The [architecture](KSOA_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) governs cumulative destinations and closeout. The [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) owns the analysis lock and run state.

## Start at the authorized frontier

V01 through V07 are the atomic repository reading units. Within each volume, read prologue, numbered chapters and epilogue in witnessed order. Take finer scene or passage freezes immediately before a major disclosure where the distinction matters. Start with an entering record that identifies the volume, witness, authorized endpoint, previous accepted freeze, reader exposure and entering knowledge for the relevant characters. Load the prior frozen state and only prior authorized text and records. Restrict previews, search, OCR, opening links and extraction to the current interval. Use bibliographic titles for witness identity. Keep publisher summaries, adaptations, prior memories, later navigation headings, spoiler-bearing paratext and retrospective files out of the prospective working view. Log unavoidable material exposure with its affected scope. A contaminated view may support explicitly qualified retrospective work. An independent, unexposed pass is needed for a genuinely blind result.

## Track three orders

| Order | What to record |
|---|---|
| Fictional events | Actual, alleged or inferred occurrence, relative or absolute time, participants and causal dependencies. Use ranges and competing sequences for disputed chronology. Maintain a provisional sequence through the reading and a separately labeled, endpoint-bounded reconstruction when warranted. |
| Investigation | Who encountered which object, testimony or inference, in what order and through which action. Track collection, interpretation, circulation, withholding, error and consequences. Discovery by one person establishes access only for that person unless transmission is evidenced. |
| Narration | When the reader receives information, with whose focalization and framing, at what level of explicitness and emphasis. Track gaps, reordered scenes, reported memories, withheld facts, apparent salience and information known to the reader alone. |

Tie the three orders through the same evidence IDs. A later recollection may concern an earlier event while entering the investigation and the reader's knowledge at separate points. Give an uncertainty and source locator to each asserted time or dependency.

## Assess evidence and hypotheses

Record presentation and judgment independently. Classify the source as a directly represented event or observation, narratorial assertion, character testimony, or character belief, with speaker, focalizer, receiver and access route. Then identify the analytical proposition as an investigative hypothesis, source-supported inference, unverified speculation, contradicted or revised claim, or retrospectively established explanation when applicable. This permits a directly represented utterance to establish that a character spoke while its content remains testimony or belief. A narrator may report someone's inference or limited perception. Assign scope, degree of support, known conflicts and plausible alternatives to each consequential proposition.

Test claims of intent, lying and investigative error against opportunity, knowledge at the time, possible memory error, misunderstanding, limited access, withholding and affirmative evidence of deception. Treat an accusation as an attributed investigative claim and track how its basis holds up. Require corroboration or a sufficiently warranted textual convergence before labeling an explanation established, and record residual ambiguity even if a novel closes an investigation. Preserve a source locator for every material link in the causal chain and evidence for viable alternatives.

## Freeze before looking ahead

At each section close, record the exact exclusive predecessor endpoint (or member opening) and inclusive end of the disclosed span, its predecessor, reader and investigator knowledge, knowledge of relevant other characters, unresolved alternatives and counterevidence. Use the [disclosure ledger](KSOA_DISCLOSURE_AND_HYPOTHESIS_LEDGER.md) to record what the prior horizon allowed a reader or investigator to infer. Save the exact snapshot in the authorized working environment and record its capture time and SHA-256 before opening the next span. Carry the same contents into the accepted volume transaction. Freeze hypothesis wording, supporting and weakening evidence, predicted discriminators and any material exposure limit. Record subsequent revelations as new rows with their actual disclosure horizon. Keep the original snapshot and its confidence recoverable from the volume's committed bytes.

At case close, audit each load-bearing inference at the last relevant pre-reveal horizon. Ask whether the diagnostic clue was already available, legible, emphasized or obscured, available to which party, and how well the clue discriminated among the recorded alternatives. Separate textual availability, reasonable inferability and uniqueness of inference. Identify explanations that depend materially on newly introduced facts. An explicit reveal can clarify an earlier clue without proving that the earlier clue uniquely determined the outcome. Apply a fair-play question where the novel invites it, and record when its narrative design serves a different purpose.

## Read the prose and the institution

- Examine who narrates, whose attention and interiority govern each passage, how transitions and pacing control uncertainty, and how descriptive selection makes a place, object or utterance salient. Verify original Japanese wording, ruby, orthography, punctuation, forms of address, politeness, indirect speech, and register in context. Record a gloss as a gloss and identify what it loses. Treat translation differences and alternative readings as claims with their own basis. Label quotations as verified exact, working transcription or paraphrase. Verify load-bearing wording against the native witness, using minimal excerpts only where permitted by publication rules.
- Record depicted evidence handling, interview or witness interactions, reporting and corroboration, division of responsibility, jurisdiction and discretion, cooperation and constraint, and ethical judgment. Attribute assertions about authority to their speakers or narrators. Trace how a rule, resource, delay or discretionary choice changed the investigation when the evidence supports the connection. Claims about actual Japanese law or police practice belong to separately sourced context with a jurisdiction, date, primary authority and scope of comparison. Fictional conduct alone establishes a portrayal.
- Record choices and pressures outside the investigation as well as case conduct. Distinguish internal intention from attributed or observed behavior, expressed emotion from inferred motivation, professional role from private disposition and situational response from a demonstrated tendency. Follow directed changes in familiarity, intimacy, operational and interpersonal trust, obligation, conflict and attachment. Evaluate the central partnership across actual choices and disclosures. Any romantic interpretation requires affirmative evidence and plausible counterreadings. Retain case-local subjects' goals, decisions, constraints and causal consequences in their own right.
- Test whether Kamakura geography, local history, cultural institutions or social relationships affect access, movement, evidence, motive, decisions or interpretive structure. Distinguish atmosphere, causal function and thematic significance by naming the mechanism and testing a competing reading. Accumulate repeated language or motifs with variation, counterexamples and opportunity. Recurrence starts a question about theme.

## Close the volume transaction

The reading contains an entering boundary, complete source coverage and apparatus dispositions, a section-by-section literary argument with exact evidence references, focalization and a plausible counterreading, frozen prospective snapshots, retrospective case reconstruction and unresolved matters, character/relationship/institutional changes and warranted revisions. The owning reading records the source locators of material non-case passages. Use the [case evidence ledger](KSOA_CASE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md) for case evidence and investigation, the [character and relationship ledger](KSOA_CHARACTER_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md) for state, and the [claim revision ledger](KSOA_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md) for interpretive conclusions that need longitudinal retrieval. Include needed counterevidence and plausible alternative readings. Review important Japanese references and named subjects at their disclosure boundary.

Check the entire authorized span against the witness map, link ledger deltas or explain why no delta is warranted, verify consequential locators and forward-reference boundaries, and freeze the exiting state. Update the coverage and current high-water mark with the reading and ledger changes in the same accepted unit transaction. The next volume requires a new authorization or an existing continuous run whose explicit boundary includes it.
