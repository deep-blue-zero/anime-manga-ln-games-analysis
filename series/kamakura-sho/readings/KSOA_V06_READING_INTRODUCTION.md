# Kamakura Police Station — V06 佐助ヶ谷の銀雫

**Spoilers through the end of V06.** For cutoff-bounded work, use the [disclosure ledger](../KSOA_DISCLOSURE_AND_HYPOTHESIS_LEDGER.md#v06-disclosure-history) to retrieve the permitted freeze.

## 1. Identity, authorization, and source contract

*佐助ヶ谷の銀雫* is the V06 Japanese novel by Kyōichi Narukami, published by Bungeishunjū under the Bunshun Bunko imprint. The completed KSOA-G1 run read witness `KSOA-JP-V06-W01` from the prologue through epilogue p[0043], ending at [KSOA-V06-H-0011](KSOA_V06_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v06-h-0011). The [witness map](../KSOA_SOURCE_AND_WITNESS_MAP.md#v06-source-coverage) and [coverage receipt](KSOA_V06_DEEP_READING.md#witness-and-coverage) give the source identity, section ranges and apparatus limits.

## 2. Prior-volume developments

V05 ended with Naoto Hani admitting that he struck Masafumi Wakao. The dispute over Atobe's designs and the architects' efforts to expose appropriation had separate histories. Motoya's work with Sayaka Takigawa brought him into contact with Emiri Hatano, an illustrator and friend of Yuka Wakao. When Emiri appeared during the case, Asumi questioned Motoya about the two women. Her suspicions about Emiri remained unsupported. The later meeting on the beach made the friction between Asumi and Sayaka visible to Motoya and left him uneasy about his own position. ## 3. Current-volume story synopsis

In the dark, a person approaches a park shelter for a meeting. He remembers Mitsuki and a night in Otaru, thinks a deception kept them apart from her family, and hopes to ask Kuniaki Yakushiji to forgive him and restore his life in Sasuke-ga-yatsu. He sees the figure of the person he holds responsible for their misfortune and resolves to make that person tell the truth. The novel cuts away before the two speak. [Prologue](KSOA_V06_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v06-h-0001)

At a shelter on the Kajiwara side of Genjiyama Park the police have an unidentified man, an uncertain time of death and no clear reason he was there. The cliff makes nearby homes harder to reach than they look on a map. Emiri and Sayaka, there for a magazine shoot at the matchmaking stone (縁結び石) of nearby Kuzuharaoka Shrine, are at the cordon. Emiri's information and parallel police inquiries lead to glass artist Shigeyuki Nonaka, who had worked in Otaru. Asumi limits what she tells the women, and Motoya gives them a qualified account of the death. He persuades Masaki to defer checking their whereabouts and keeps Emiri's tangential contact in the V05 investigation to himself. Asumi takes Motoya to Makiko Shinagawa's house below the shelter. Shinagawa places a cry just before the end of the nine o'clock news. Other households heard it but cannot place it as precisely. The briefing confirms Nonaka's identification by his sister and his apprenticeship under Yakushiji. [Identification and disclosure](KSOA_V06_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v06-h-0003) · [Shinagawa and the briefing](KSOA_V06_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v06-h-0004)

Asumi secures the assignment to interview glass artists. Katsuya Mizutani describes Nonaka's influence on his craft and offers a checkable account of his evening in Ginza. Yoshio Kume says Mizutani was expelled soon after Nonaka left Yakushiji's workshop. Kume puts himself and fellow pupil Noritaka Kasuya together with Yakushiji at a birthday gathering. Kasuya recalls Nonaka's ruined *Silver Drops* exhibition piece and says he saw Mizutani near the preparation room. The police have a witness to someone's presence near the room and a suggestion about what that person did there. The team gives priority to financially pressed commercial contacts while Asumi continues to see the old workshop as a possible source of the present grievance. [Mizutani](KSOA_V06_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v06-h-0005) · [The workshop and party accounts](KSOA_V06_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v06-h-0006) · [Kasuya's account](KSOA_V06_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v06-h-0007)

Emiri reaches Motoya through Sayaka and explains why she understated her connection with Nonaka. She had been close to Mitsuki at university and deliberately sought out Nonaka at the Ginza exhibition. Mitsuki was his wife and Yakushiji's daughter. Emiri describes their elopement, letters about their life in Otaru, and a story of stalking that may have been fabricated. Mitsuki worked to support the household and helped conceive Nonaka's ice-inspired work. Her death and Nonaka's wish to make peace with Yakushiji give the prologue a likely speaker and an intelligible reason for the meeting. Emiri permits Motoya to use her name. [Emiri's correction](KSOA_V06_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v06-h-0008)

Chikako Kosugi and taxi records corroborate the endpoints of the birthday gathering. Kosugi spent the important interval upstairs. Yakushiji confirms the marriage rupture and repeats the old accusations against Mizutani. Mizutani denies them and acknowledges recent contact he had earlier withheld. According to Mizutani, Nonaka told him about the frame on the eve of the killing and intended to find proof before speaking to Yakushiji. This gives anyone implicated in the old deception an immediate reason to fear Nonaka's return. Asumi arranges to bring the workshop circle together. [The gap in the alibi](KSOA_V06_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v06-h-0009)

At the confrontation, Kume says Kasuya spoiled the exhibition work and admits they planned it together. Yakushiji names them as the sources of the stalking story. The two men do not answer that accusation. Asumi shows that a shout downstairs does not reach Kosugi's room upstairs and that the shelter is under 700 m away, within a half-hour round trip. Yakushiji also withdraws his account of their continuous presence at the party. He slept during the relevant interval and heard what they had done after they returned. Asumi then accuses Kume of putting his prescribed sleep-inducer in Yakushiji's champagne, citing a doctor's statement the investigators have obtained; Kume deflects and Kasuya calls the case circumstantial. The new account assigns the attack to Kume and the arranged meeting and knowing participation to Kasuya. It also accounts for Yakushiji's earlier protection of the men after the event. Asumi leads the questioning and Motoya asks all three to accompany them. [Confrontation](KSOA_V06_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v06-h-0010) · [Acts and dispositions](../KSOA_CASE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#v06-case-evidence)

The epilogue reports full confessions and detention for Kasuya and Kume, and release on bail for Yakushiji. Motoya hopes Yakushiji will avoid prosecution, which remains unresolved. Over drinks Sayaka asks whether this is a date. Asumi, already well into her drink, says it is and challenges Sayaka's right to call him 元哉くん. Sayaka retorts that Asumi is only his work partner, and Asumi answers that they are buddies. When Sayaka suggests that rank forces him to drink with her, Asumi insists he came gladly. Motoya privately disputes her account of how willingly he came and tries to slip away. The exchange gives her chosen description of their partnership and his separate response. [Epilogue](KSOA_V06_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v06-h-0011)
