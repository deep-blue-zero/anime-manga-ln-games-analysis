## 1. Identity, authorization, and source contract

*竹寺の雪* is the V04 Japanese novel by Kyōichi Narukami, published by Bungeishunjū under the Bunshun Bunko imprint. The completed KSOA-G1 run read witness `KSOA-JP-V04-W01` from the prologue through epilogue p[0137], ending at [KSOA-V04-H-0015](KSOA_V04_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v04-h-0015). ## 2. Prior-volume developments

The cases through V03 have taught Asumi and Motoya that a first account can explain part of an event and still leave a second person's contribution unaccounted for. In V03 Keiko Yuhara's admission explained the appointment and the first blow. Maya Hino's later account explained the remaining injuries and the moved stone. The recovered writing also changed the police's understanding of Mariko Wakao's much older death. Asumi's eye for color helped open that inquiry. She later told Motoya how others' assumption that she could manage alone had hurt her, and he stayed with her after a resident called an officer. Their cooperation and the meaning each assigns to the other's care enter V04 at different levels of certainty. ## 3. Current-volume story synopsis

The prologue begins with a couple's last decision in a snowy bamboo garden. An end title reveals that the scene belongs to an old film, *Snow at the Bamboo Temple*. Nonfiction writer Fuyuhiko Akao rewinds the image in a private screening room and stops on the left wrist of the woman on screen. He says he means to show what he has seen. Someone comes up behind him and attacks him. [Prologue](KSOA_V04_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v04-h-0001)

Akao is found dead in the screening room at the Sekigawa memorial on April 10. Weeks later the police have a homicide, accounts placing the dinner party at the main house and little help from the gate camera. Yoshio Satake sends Asumi and Motoya to question the family again. Echika (恵智花, rendered “Kechika” in the linked deep reading) says Akao was writing a centenary biography of actor Yōsuke Sekigawa with the support of her aunt Yūko, who is in hospital. Akao interviewed director Hongō at dinner and asked to check something in Sekigawa's film. Teranishi set up the enhanced Blu-ray. A recording places Akao alive a little after seven. When he stops responding, the group retrieves the usual key from a drawer and finds him behind the locked door. The discovery fixes when the group entered and leaves open who could get in earlier. [Renewed inquiry](KSOA_V04_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v04-h-0002) · [Screening and discovery](KSOA_V04_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v04-h-0003)

At the memorial Asumi notices jewelry in the photographs. The film turns out to be the final picture of Akiko Shichijō, who played Michiyo Mochizuki. Asumi and Motoya watch a streaming version without spotting the clue. Tadashi Torii, the editor from V01, says Akao's unfinished biography broke off at this film. Satake lets them follow that thread while the team continues its inquiries into Akao. Hongō describes Sekigawa's work and how the film made its snow and bamboo garden, and supplies a route to Shichijō. A hospital restriction keeps the detectives from interviewing Yūko. Actor Teiichi Hachiya tells them what she has said about Sekigawa as a father and gives a Tokyo party as his alibi. [The memorial and film](KSOA_V04_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v04-h-0004) · [Torii and the manuscript](KSOA_V04_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v04-h-0005) · [Hongō](KSOA_V04_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v04-h-0006) · [Hachiya](KSOA_V04_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v04-h-0007)

At Shichijō's house Takayuki Miyazaki bars the visitors until she invites them in. She says she never met Akao and had no contact with Sekigawa after her retirement, though she attended his wake. Mariko Utsunomiya remembers the two as close screen partners and refers the detectives to writer Katsuo Yamashina. He tells them about his former relationship with Saori Yashiro, who later became Sekigawa's second wife. Yamashina recalls helping Shichijō move to Inatori after she retired. A teenage Miyazaki helped her too and spent his working life in her service. Asumi thinks his longstanding loyalty and opportunity merit a closer look. Motoya questions the basis and helps test it. [Shichijō](KSOA_V04_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v04-h-0008) · [Yamashina](KSOA_V04_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v04-h-0010)

The pair recheck the road-camera footage for a car like the one they saw at Shichijō's house. Following Motoya's suggestion to look at the return direction supplies a clearer profile without a readable plate. The times and profile give Satake grounds for further comparison and questioning. Miyazaki accounts for the trip as shopping. Asumi challenges the timing. When he produces a passbook showing a large withdrawal, he says Akao took the money in a fraudulent investment and that Akao had called while Shichijō was out. The withdrawal establishes movement of money. Miyazaki is the source for what he says the payment was for. [Camera recheck](KSOA_V04_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v04-h-0011) · [First account of the money](KSOA_V04_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v04-h-0012)

Shichijō comes to the police herself. She says Yūko is her child by Sekigawa, born after she withdrew and raised as the daughter of Sekigawa and his first wife Machiko. Motoya explains the limits of confidentiality as she asks what the police will do with that information. Miyazaki then says he paid Akao under pressure. According to Miyazaki, Akao noticed Shichijō's own bracelet in the film and traced a private history that people had kept out of public view. Miyazaki describes protecting her as a matter of faith and admits the attack. When the detectives ask how he obtained access he begins to explain. The narration withholds that part of his account and moves to a warrant arrest and search. [Maternity, the bracelet and the arrest](KSOA_V04_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v04-h-0013)

The arrest leaves the key unexplained. Hachiya's physical alibi checks out, but the detectives find a family register that shows he and Yūko were married and learn of substantial debts. Asumi proposes that Akao also threatened Hachiya and that exposure of Yūko's maternity could affect what Hachiya expected to inherit. Hachiya admits giving Miyazaki the key and explains the spouses' reasons for keeping their marriage private. Police pursue alleged instigation and assistance. The admitted transfer, corroborated absence and allegation of encouragement carry different kinds of support. [The key and Hachiya's role](KSOA_V04_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v04-h-0014) · [Case disposition](../KSOA_CASE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v04-res-0002)

In the epilogue Yūko has died and Motoya is finishing the paperwork. He and Asumi hear that Hachiya intends to renounce inheritance and that Echika wants the foundation to sustain the house and memorial. The transfers and legal outcome remain ahead. Asumi invites Motoya to share a walk and a meal, and speaks of being overlooked because of appearance. He is more willing to follow her plans than he initially intended. When she becomes unwell near Jufukuji and local residents intervene, he starts looking for a taxi. [Epilogue](KSOA_V04_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v04-h-0015)
