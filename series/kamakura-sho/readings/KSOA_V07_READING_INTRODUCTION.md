## 3. Current-volume story synopsis

In the garden of Yamabe Saryō, an unnamed person injures Shigeyoshi Kawamura near a well. He considers seeking help and leaves. The moonlit scene gives the reader the encounter and the person's attempt to justify leaving. His identity and the exchange that led to the attack remain out of view. In the morning manager Mihara and a colleague find Kawamura, the restaurant's chef, at the well. The evening interval and recent computer activity fit an encounter after other staff left. The footage covers only some entrances, and cash is still there. Mihara says the restaurant plans to close and that its signature *Flower Dance* course depended on Kawamura. [Prologue](KSOA_V07_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v07-h-0001) · [Discovery and scene](KSOA_V07_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v07-h-0002)

Asumi checks the grounds and speaks to Tomonaga, a restaurateur she knows. He has heard that *Flower Dance* resembles the *Snow Blossom* course at Ugetsuan, run by Kawamura's former teacher Aonuma. Tomonaga has tried neither. Yasushige Aonuma rejects a single explanation for his father's illness and business decline and points the detectives to people more closely involved with the former restaurant. Nikaidō and Yoshida assign them to follow the old staff and acquaintances without treating the rumor as proof. [The two courses and the old staff](KSOA_V07_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v07-h-0003) · [Yasushige](KSOA_V07_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v07-h-0004)

At Izumida's restaurant the detectives unexpectedly meet Sayaka and Emiri. Sayaka has eaten both courses. She found the presentation similar and the flavor very different, and she doubts the new course ruined the older restaurant. Motoya elicits her comparison while keeping case details private. Izumida describes a borrowed idea, different execution and struggling conditions in the industry. He admits contempt for Kawamura but denies revenge. Eguchi rejects derivation and points to venue, pricing and the expense of Aonuma's cooking. Hashio says imitation is part of the trade while allowing that the presentation could violate a local rival's sense of courtesy. Nagasaka, whose training and plans were disrupted by the closure, voices the strongest anger while describing a new possible start abroad. The interviews give the detectives motives to test and no established link between the former pupils and the well. [Sayaka and Izumida](KSOA_V07_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v07-h-0005) · [Eguchi and Hashio](KSOA_V07_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v07-h-0007) · [Nagasaka](KSOA_V07_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v07-h-0009)

Asumi proposes checking whether staff worked in both kitchens and whether their later businesses are in trouble. Nikaidō obtains records and diverts people to those inquiries. Mihara and Yukari Shiobara already have corroboration for their whereabouts during the death interval. Separately, Emiri asks Motoya about the case and vouches for Izumida from her experience as his customer. Motoya sticks to the public account and asks for evidence. He hopes her attention might be personal and keeps their private meeting from Asumi. [The change of inquiry](KSOA_V07_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v07-h-0010) · [Emiri and Motoya](KSOA_V07_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v07-h-0011)

Asumi puts two dated staff photographs together. Motoya recognizes Shiobara in both. She worked at Yamabe Saryō, then Ugetsuan, then returned to Yamabe Saryō, with her name absent from Ugetsuan's final roster. The dates undermine her claim that she returned when Ugetsuan closed. She admits collecting information for Kawamura in return for money she needed while caring for her mother, and names work practices he adopted. The admission establishes a history of deliberate borrowing. It leaves the extent of the older restaurant's losses and the causes of Aonuma's illness open. Shiobara's past access also gives the detectives a way to ask who knew enough to pressure Kawamura. [Photographs](KSOA_V07_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v07-h-0012) · [Shiobara's account](KSOA_V07_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v07-h-0013)

Shiobara supplies recordings linking Eguchi to extortion. Eguchi acknowledges his voice and presents his demands as retaliation for what Kawamura did. Nishina reports money trouble at Eguchi's Fleur restaurant. Motoya takes the financial need as a stronger explanation, while Eguchi's own account foregrounds revenge. Asumi reconstructs a dispute over payment at the well and attributes the attack to Eguchi. He disputes her account. Yoshida arrests Eguchi on suspicion of extortion and announces a separate homicide search. The book gives an evidentiary basis for the extortion arrest and a supported homicide attribution, with the results of the search and any homicide admission or verdict still open. Asumi and Motoya agree to celebrate with a meal. [The recordings and arrest](KSOA_V07_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v07-h-0014) · [Formal steps and remaining uncertainty](../KSOA_CASE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v07-res-0001)

At Izumida's counter, Sayaka brings Emiri and Izumida forward to announce their marriage plans, and Asumi congratulates them. Sayaka then says that Izumida was with Emiri on the night in question. Motoya sees why Emiri pressed him to clear Izumida and why she found it hard to tell him. The book shows no independent check of the alibi. Asumi and Sayaka shake hands. She tells Motoya that she can do the work because he is there. He hears her and privately resolves to keep supporting her. The finale ends with the detectives and their companions sharing a place and a meal while the legal process remains outside the story.
