## 1. Identity, authorization, and source contract

*西御門の館* is the V05 Japanese novel by Kyōichi Narukami, published by Bungeishunjū under the Bunshun Bunko imprint. The completed KSOA-G1 run read witness `KSOA-JP-V05-W01` from the prologue through epilogue p[0044], ending at [KSOA-V05-H-0015](KSOA_V05_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v05-h-0015). ## 2. Prior-volume developments

In V04 Akao's attention to a bracelet led to a hidden family history. The camera recheck, Shichijō's own disclosure and Miyazaki's revised account led to his arrest. Hachiya's alibi held even as his admission established a separate route by which Miyazaki obtained the key. Yūko died before any estate outcome was shown. After the investigation Asumi invited Motoya to spend time with her. He stayed through more of the evening than he planned and looked for a taxi when she became unwell. The two enter V05 with proven working reliance and a private relationship whose meaning each still reads differently. ## 3. Current-volume story synopsis

An unnamed architect confronts Masafumi Wakao by the sea. They argue about a demand and something that happened in their professional past. The speaker strikes Wakao and sees him fall. The speaker says he meant to intimidate Wakao and chooses concealment over seeking help. The scene gives the reader the confrontation and the blow while withholding the speaker's name and the full circumstances of the death. [Prologue](KSOA_V05_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v05-h-0001)

The next morning Motoya is off duty helping feature writer Sayaka Takigawa interview her longtime friend Emiri Hatano, an illustrator who collects sea glass. He asks to keep his name out of the piece. He sees police cordoning the beach. Asumi tries to draw him over and his own supervisor Masaki formally assigns him to the scene. Wakao's license identifies the body, and his wallet and phone are still with him. Watanabe and the responding officers discuss the injury, estimated timing and tide. Where the body was found leaves several possible places for the assault. The pair's neighborhood inquiries yield no witness to it. A homicide investigation begins with Wakao's work and relationships. [The beach feature](KSOA_V05_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v05-h-0002) · [Body identification](KSOA_V05_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v05-h-0003) · [Neighborhood inquiry](KSOA_V05_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v05-h-0004)

Wakao's wife Yuka describes his training with Shintarō Atobe and the houses Wakao went on to design. The house they live in shows his interest in moving air. She was visiting family in Kitami and says she spoke with Wakao at home around nine. She and Wakao first met through a university photography circle. Naoto Hani visits to support her. He remembers Wakao as his senior at university and his mentor at Atobe's office. Yuka mentions Masayuki Tanaka, Takanori Kodera and Kenzō Enomoto, Wakao's colleagues from the Atobe office, and Hani describes them as having joined the office after him. Hani says he was staying at a hotel and ordered room service. That account gives the police a potential observation at one time during the evening. [Yuka and the house](KSOA_V05_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v05-h-0005) · [Hani's visit](KSOA_V05_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v05-h-0007)

Emiri arrives as Yuka's friend and fellow illustrator. She explains that Yuka came to the coast through their friendship and persuaded Wakao to move. She also mentions the interview with Sayaka, putting Motoya's recent off-duty company into Asumi's hearing. Outside, Asumi asks him to explain those contacts and raises possibilities about leaked plans and a private relationship between Emiri and Wakao. Those ideas remain unsupported. Motoya defends Emiri and catches himself letting his feelings and impressions influence his judgment. Asumi arranges to interview Atobe's widow before the partners meet again. [Emiri's visit](KSOA_V05_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v05-h-0008) · [Conversation outside](KSOA_V05_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v05-h-0009)

Emiko Atobe recalls how the younger architects shared work, how she supported them and how her husband Shintarō tried to explain the movement he sought in a design. Tanaka remembers Wakao growing dismissive after the hotel's success. He remembers going out of obligation to the housewarming that Yuka and Hani recall fondly. Kodera tells the detectives about struggling for commissions and missing what a client wanted. A witness corroborates his whereabouts. Enomoto returns from France for the funeral and quietly relays Kodera's story about Atobe searching for drawings and calculations that went missing in 2005 and later deciding to forgive. The inquiry now has a specific old grievance as well as the original set of professional relationships. [Emiko](KSOA_V05_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v05-h-0010) · [Tanaka's recollection](KSOA_V05_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v05-h-0011) · [Kodera's account](KSOA_V05_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v05-h-0012) · [Enomoto's confidence](KSOA_V05_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v05-h-0013)

Before the wake, Emiko confirms the loss of the drawings and says Atobe recognized his own work in the 2007 winning hotel design. Tanaka supports that attribution from his engineering knowledge. Enomoto and Kodera acknowledge their separate effort to collect evidence and expose the appropriation. The story gives that effort and Atobe's willingness to forgive their own history. Asumi then turns to Hani. Former classmates place him in an earlier relationship with Yuka, and his practice is in financial trouble. Asumi argues that the room-service timing left him free to leave the hotel by way of a nearby marina, meet Wakao near Koshigoe and threaten to expose the secret of the hotel design unless Wakao paid him. Her account of the demand and the route is a reconstruction. Hani admits striking Wakao after an insult and says he did not intend to kill him. Motoya makes an emergency arrest on suspicion of assault resulting in death. The precise passage on the water and final legal outcome remain unverified at the ending. [The gathering and arrest](KSOA_V05_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v05-h-0014) · [Design history](../KSOA_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v05-cl-0002-r01) · [Case disposition](../KSOA_CASE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v05-res-0002)

Motoya and Sayaka meet for another feature in the epilogue. Emiri declines to join for a reason Motoya cannot determine. Asumi and Sayaka meet again, their competitive exchange making Motoya uncomfortable. Asumi leaves on duty after a pointed gesture in his direction. The investigation has established an admitted blow and several distinct attempts to deal with an old wrong. Motoya is left to make sense of their exchange. ## Revision note (2026-11-15)

Version 1.2 corrects the synopsis against the Japanese witness, following the adjudicated KSOA-CB1 reconciliation (`audits/KSOA_CB1_COMPARISON/ADJ_B.md`, `ADJ_D.md`). Corrections to the frozen reading are in the [deep reading errata](../audits/KSOA_DEEP_READING_ERRATA.md#v05-errata).
- Yuka, not Hani, names Tanaka, Kodera and Enomoto, as Wakao's colleagues from the Atobe office. They are not described as student friends, and Tanaka and Enomoto studied at other universities [V05 C02 p0414–p0422; C03 p0308] (KCMP-V05-012).
- Asumi's reconstruction is extortion: Hani threatened to expose the secret of the hotel design for money, which Wakao refused [V05 C03 p0947, p0952]. It remains a reconstruction; Hani admits only the blow [p0954] (KCMP-SERIES-001).
