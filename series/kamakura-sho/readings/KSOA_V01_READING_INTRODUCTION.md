## 3. Current-volume story synopsis

An unnamed writer struggles to hold onto his present as images of wartime departure and a girl by the coast come back to him. He believes he has left his manuscript, *Sunset at Inamuragasaki*, with someone who will take it to his editor. The handoff is part of his own uncertain recollection. By the end of the prologue he is in distress and unable to move. [Prologue](KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v01-h-0001)

At the Ashina estate, prefectural detective Motoya Kitsukawa works the death of novelist Morio Ashina under Asumi Ogasawara, a childhood acquaintance who now leads their pair. Household manager Minoru Tomita describes the discovery. Toshio Kanagami found the study latched from inside, broke in and found Ashina collapsed. The staff recount Ashina's routine and the estate's security, and Marina Matsumoto says she had seen him reading, with his manuscript tray empty. Editor Tadashi Torii says Ashina had called to arrange pickup of the finished work. The manuscript has disappeared, and Torii's knowledge ends at the call. Motoya is inclined to accept a suicide and to doubt there was a finished work. Asumi takes the absent manuscript seriously and credits Motoya for pursuing Ashina's health and medication. [The estate](KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v01-h-0002) · [Torii and the promised manuscript](KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v01-h-0003)

The autopsy finds poisoning. Ashina's doctor reports a likely advanced brain malignancy, Ashina's decision to forgo treatment and his request for confidentiality. A line in a notebook about disappearing into the sea at Inamuragasaki also fits the police's suicide ruling, and headquarters dissolves the inquiry. Asumi has read another of Ashina's novels and doubts her partner's theory of a dying prank. She takes Motoya to the funeral, where the staff's expected benefits and ongoing work complicate his assumptions about their incentives. Ashina's longtime friend Gamō acknowledges a difficult shared past and declines to discuss it. [Medical evidence and closure](KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v01-h-0004) · [Funeral](KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v01-h-0005)

A new investigation starts when freelance writer Shōichi Uehara is found dead at Meotoike Park. The medical finding supports homicide. His valuables are still there and his phone is missing. The last entry in his notebook, `UBAGAYA 6/6`, leads Asumi and Motoya to the former Ubagaya Hotel, now a university site. A researcher tells them about the hotel's wartime use. Torii then supplies the interview in which Ashina called his planned work a fictional confession and said he wanted Gamō to read it. Motoya spots Uehara's byline. The police establish that Uehara had been Ashina's student as well as his interviewer, and the hotel and its later university use help explain their connection. [Uehara's discovery](KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v01-h-0006) · [The notebook and hotel site](KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v01-h-0007) · [The interview](KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v01-h-0008) · [The former student](KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v01-h-0010)

Gamō tells the detectives about the families' ties and points them toward Father Alain Carpentier. The priest initially refuses to divulge a confession. After seeing the interview and hearing the circumstances of Ashina's illness, he decides to tell them what he knows. He says Ashina and Takako Satomi, Gamō's wife, met at Ubagaya Hotel in 1945. Their son Hidesato was raised as Gamō's son. Carpentier says Hidesato learned the secret from Takako and told Ashina before Hidesato's death. That history puts Hidesato's son Hideyuki in a position to lose standing in his family and company if the manuscript comes out. Police corroborate Hideyuki's travel in both relevant periods. On a visit to the cape, Asumi tells Motoya her alternative and proposes a test. The reader hears his reservations while her plan is still withheld. [Gamō's account](KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v01-h-0009) · [The priest and the alibis](KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v01-h-0010)

At Ashina's memorial, officer Nishina plays a synthetic version of Ashina's voice under Asumi's direction. Marina's response gives the detectives a lead. In a separate voluntary interview she says Ashina privately entrusted her with the manuscript for Torii and a new notarized will for his lawyer. She explains that Ashina disclosed his condition and his fear of losing control of his own decisions. Marina read and withheld both documents because she was involved with Hideyuki and feared what his descent would cost him. She says she made that decision herself. She lets the police retrieve the documents, and their recovery corroborates her custody account. Ashina's death remains a suicide under the fuller account of his plans and circumstances. [Memorial and custody](KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v01-h-0011) · [Recovery](../KSOA_CASE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v01-res-0002)

Marina also recalls Uehara's call about the delivery and alleged financial misconduct. Her appointment with him was set for after the day he died. Asumi proposes a second reading of his notebook clue, an Okinawan wordplay pointing to Kazue Mimura as well as the Kamakura place. At the confrontation, Asumi bluffs about recovering the phone. Kazue admits her own participation and names Tomita as the one who assaulted Uehara. Tomita defends taking the money, and both are taken away. No recovery of the phone or court outcome is shown. The returned manuscript and will let the family hear what Ashina intended. Hideyuki asks to keep his work and keep calling Gamō his grandfather, Gamō accepts both bonds, and Torii gains assent to publish. [The two accounts of the deaths and the family disclosure](KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v01-h-0012)

In Fujisawa, Motoya offers Asumi real praise in a teasingly formal address. They differ over Hideyuki's public declaration of love. Asumi, who has been drinking, says she wants to love someone seriously and embraces Motoya. He is comforted and embarrassed, then tries to wake her for the train. The ending gives the reader his reaction and leaves the object of Asumi's wish open. [Epilogue](KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v01-h-0013)
