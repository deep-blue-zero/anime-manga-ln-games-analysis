## A single assault amid several projects of redress

The old studio case enters as a claim of absence and only then as a possible taking. 榎本 relays 小寺's account and tries to withdraw its significance. 恵美子 confirms the absence and dates the later recognition to the winning design. Technical comparison gives the attribution force inside the story. The old drawings and the engineering comparison are not reproduced for the reader. No legal case establishes infringement or ownership. Atobe's reported willingness to forgive and the junior architects' determination to prove appropriation show real disagreement about who can decide whether a historical wrong is over. Tanaka feels unable to challenge the mentor's indulgence while Enomoto and Kodera try to assemble proof for a later public intervention. Those actions cannot be treated as forms of assistance to the homicide simply because all three have grievances. [Design-history revision](../KSOA_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v05-cl-0002-r01) (C03 0691–0755, 0842–0927).

羽仁's disclosure is narrower than the detective's reconstruction. He admits striking Wakao after a perceived insult and says he did not intend death. His speech matches the prologue's shock and resentment. The early story of professional mentorship and assistance in grief can remain part of their history alongside antagonism. Asumi reports financial strain, independent witnesses to his old relationship, and an alternative to a continuous hotel stay. The facts support a specific occasion for questioning and the novel's identification of the attacker. The exact agreed route, demand amount, disposition of belongings and forensic confirmation remain open in the final text. PRO describes a fall. ## Design, recognition and care

A deceased architect's work and daily practice become legible through people who did not occupy the same position. 結花 describes the work pattern, the ocean view and her husband's commitment to wind. 羽仁 makes the teacher's idea of flow central. 恵美子 qualifies that expression as a public and pedagogical shorthand, and accounts for seasonal sounds, choice of site and the work of keeping an old house. The house without displayed honors, set against the one full of Wakao's awards, gives those different positions something to look at. Her help with food and Nanako's present assistance show care as part of making the atelier function. The account of creative inheritance includes client judgment, working conditions and mentorship as well as style and intellectual property (C02 0068–0077, 0137–0163, 0319–0368; C03 0007–0262).

The three interviews offer different answers to what a promising career is. 田中 aims for the part of design he finds most difficult. 小寺's staircase story demonstrates that giving a correct technical answer can miss the practical wish underneath it. 榎本 finds a role by choosing the scale at which he can work and revises the account of professional admiration when he admits trying to expose Wakao. Equally, a remembered occasion of friendship can be a celebration for one guest and humiliation for another. Their eulogies combine genuine loss, still-live competition and regard. Asumi uses a gathering of grief to expose those interests. Motoya wonders how much needed to be made public. The confession has dramatic force while the act of testing alternative accusations leaves a cost to witnesses with already corroborated alibis (C03 0297–0397, 0484–0585, 0629–0755, 0783–0927).

The vocabulary of taking carries from drawings to a former partner during the accusation. An object can be removed without its author's consent. A person changing partners exercises choices that the accounts here do not independently reconstruct. Asumi labels part of the story her own speculation. Yuka describes her own work, her shared time and her role in choosing where to live. Her support for the dead man in the interviews and the others' relationship histories are evidence of different things. Preserve her part in making the home rather than equating the transfer of credit, loss of a person and loss of a competition (C02 0137–0229, 0581–0652; C03 0811–0824, 0928–0946).

## Work and private obligations collide

This volume begins with one detective in an off-duty social and professional setting whose rules are different from those of questioning a suspect. Sayaka asks Motoya to frame an interview for readers who do not know the craft. His simple questions are productive and his request to keep his name out marks a professional boundary. Masaki's call, based on what Motoya told a colleague, independently orders the response that Asumi first demanded. She volunteers to continue the work together and brings local familiarity to it. Motoya completes the scene handoff and explicitly credits her judgment of the forensic officer. Their friction has practical and institutional conditions beyond his private dislike (C01 0023–0064, 0113–0230, 0313–0437, 0471–0583, 0632–0735).

The later interview with Emiri is a stronger test of their ordinary habits than the teasing in V04. The inquiry can legitimately establish how a witness knows the household. Asumi turns to what Motoya and Sayaka did together, pursues a leakage suspicion without corroboration and makes unsupported character accusations outside the house. Emiri redirects the personal question and names her own corroborators. Motoya sees excess, then notices that his confidence in her may be influenced by attraction. The detectives nevertheless return to productive questioning and he performs the arrest. Neither private motive has the authority of the observed exchange. The final scene gives Sayaka her own pointed reply to Asumi and leaves Emiri's absence unexplained. Their friendship and work merit independent treatment. [Partnership revision](../KSOA_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v05-cl-0004-r01) (C02 0519–0902; C03 0399–0422, 0954–0977; EPI 0004–0044).

This volume's narratives of glass, architectural design, drawings, photographs and hearing witnesses supply different access to the past. The ending leaves the merits of a professional claim, the full route of the assault, the eventual legal disposition and the detectives' private intentions open. It establishes the principal act and a sustained professional connection with a sharper, audible conflict about its reach.
