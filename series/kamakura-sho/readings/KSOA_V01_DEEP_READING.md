## Interpretation at the end of V01

The novel uses three institutions that authorize different ways of knowing a person. The police classify the death, the church hears a confession, and the publisher waits for a manuscript whose subject its own editor does not know. Each institution has an account that is coherent at its own boundary. The investigation advances when 亜澄 and 元哉 test where those accounts fail to meet. The danger of their method appears when a private disclosure travels farther than its original speaker could control. The withheld theory at the cape and the public disclosures in the church make the reader feel the force of crossing that boundary.

### A death known from the inside and from the file

The prologue alternates momentary physical certainty with repeated loss of intention. The expectation marker はず recurs around delivery, completion and preparation. The voice can recognize a substance and a bodily impediment, yet fails to keep its own name and times straight. The military term 出撃 and the religious language of ascent allow 1945 and the evening of the death to coexist in the consciousness before the reader can assign them to separate dates. In C01 the focus shifts to 元哉, whose attention begins with architecture and rank rather than the author's final acts. The staff's account locates the body and the examiner's estimate, while the prose withholds the pending manuscript and diagnosis from the investigator-reader alignment. The title reaches the pair through 鳥居 considerably later than it reaches the reader. Thus the same initial event enters the two orders through different channels. The ruling of suicide survives the ending. What changes is its basis. The first official account relies on a medically established poisoning, inferred terminal despair, a secured study, and a notebook line interpreted as a note. It cannot explain the source of the substance or the fate of the manuscript. The doctor's assertion of confidentiality describes what he withheld, rather than every route through which 蘆名 might tell someone. 麻里奈 later reports a private handoff and an articulated fear of losing the ability to decide and protect his intentions. The novel gives that fear its sharpest form by showing just how unreliable his recall becomes after the planned handoff. A reading centered solely on pain misses his stated concern about agency. A reading centered solely on agency misses how strongly the narrated pain and the religious date shape his decision. His final theological intention remains an open inference.
