**Character, method, and form.** Asumi handles the arrest and determines the rhythm of questioning, including a shift from confrontation to waiting. Motoya obtains the hall employment record, alerts the institutional team to the confession and shares the arrest. One arrest, one evidentiary seizure, an admission, and the end of the special team are separate actions. The chapter's apparent cadence is broken by an off-duty call marked by a film score. Asumi invokes a childhood episode of having protected Motoya from bullying and draws him back to the hall. Their friendship history is also a means of pressing him (C03 0593–0617, 0633–0715, 0772–0839).

**Open.** What can the venue reconstruction establish about the second interval? Is the secondary actor an account of opportunity, a proven person, or a defensive invention? What did the attack affect other than its victim?

## A case with two kinds of authorship

The first uninterrupted musical movement in the novel belongs to someone who expects a precisely placed cymbal entry. 高梨秀夫 takes the theater as a place to listen, remembers where he is in the symphony, then has to act as station chief when an unanticipated sound interrupts it. His attention was already directed elsewhere. An emergency is witnessed before its source or its causal roles are understood. Treating the cymbal's coincidence as a causal link imposes a single score on several events. The book gives 由良貞人 space to describe what that mistake cost him. See [initial hypothesis](../KSOA_DISCLOSURE_AND_HYPOTHESIS_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v02-hyp-0001) (C01 0007–0169, 0174–0224; C02 0023–0114).

The performance and the death are best reconstructed with two distinct intentions. 石川宗佑 has learned that 里見義尚 noticed the diverted funds and is pressing him to turn himself in. He prepares to kill 里見 on rehearsal night, then initiates the act during the concert. 椎名慶一 says he came back because he was concerned about tuning, saw 石川's preparations and privately redirected their effect toward 三浦倫人. His account lets the intermediate security interval, the observed placement and 石川's otherwise unsupported account of a different target explain each other. The two actors share a causal chain without a shared plan. Labeling the first as the perpetrator supplies physical action but leaves the choice of victim unexplained. Labeling the second as the perpetrator would leave the initiating act and its motive unaccounted for. See [two-actor resolution](../KSOA_CASE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v02-res-0002) (C03 0716–0779, 1004–1026).

## How fair is the second interval?

The early reader can reject a cymbal-trigger story, ask about stage access, distinguish a witness's professional knowledge from knowledge of someone at home, and posit more than one actor after the security events. The second piece has a visible job before it ever sounds. Its piano changes where people sit for the first piece. The title's concerto, suppressed by the death, is therefore present through preparation and absence. Likewise, 相木's discussion of tuning makes a routine available early, but the explicit comparison between ordinary practice and the omission that morning becomes clear when 亜澄 states it during the reveal. The reader also learns the undisclosed key, the chance overlap and the changed target from attributed late accounts. The available clues support a family of second-actor hypotheses ahead of time. A fully specified solution depends on evidence disclosed near the end (C01 0533–0578, 0643–0649, 0757–0849, 0994–0998; C03 0721–0768, 1004–1026).

There are three levels of closure. Purchases, footage, the old personnel register and both accounts support parts of the reconstruction with different independence. The special investigation team initially accepts a one-actor explanation. Its reasons for discounting the intermediate record and treating an inferred motive as sufficient are themselves available to the reader (C03 0653–0675, 0720–0746, 0772–0792). The second confession undercuts that account. The rehearsal provocation alone is weak evidence and puts the person under pressure in public. The best support is that the later account explains independently known facts that the earlier institutional story left over. The aftermath does not narrate forensic testing of the whole second account or judicial outcomes. See [closure versus attribution](../KSOA_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v02-cl-0001-r03).

## Conducting, listening and keeping a secret

The same pattern recurs at lower stakes. 由良 sees the concertmaster's bow carrying 里見's poetic instructions into common action. The secret is less that one person controlled all performances than that visible prestige and practical direction diverged. 里見 describes his own contributions within what he calls a collaboration. His stated reason for using abstract rehearsal directions long predates his hearing loss. Treating all abstraction as concealment would erase an aesthetic commitment and much of 三浦's labor. In the house, 彩音 similarly converts speech into a form her father can use. She controls turn-taking, protects his ability to answer and restricts what outsiders learn. Her role changes as her father stops insisting on confidentiality and she insists on making a complaint herself. The failed listening test in the study and the interrupted performance are deliberately inequivalent scenes of mediation and surprise. See [operational dependence](../KSOA_CHARACTER_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v02-rel-0004) (C02 0115–0249, 0467–0646; C03 0309–0531).

壬生's account gives professional rank a material cost across generations. He treats one conductor's refusal to qualify a judgment as effectively ending another musician's career and interprets his own abrupt silence as a refusal to repeat that harm. 彩音 hears the same silence as contempt for her ensemble. 亜澄 and 元哉 in turn disagree over whether generosity explains the sustained support that followed. 椎名's account supplies grounds to reconsider the history but comes from someone defending his teacher and himself. 里見's supposed atonement remains 元哉's inference. Responsibility for a remark, intention to bring about a dismissal, and causal responsibility for everything that followed call for different evidence (C03 0107–0164, 0212–0258, 0321–0349, 0972–1002).

椎名 calls his allegiance national and artistic, describing 里見 as a source of Japan's cultural standing and of his own formation. 彩音 identifies an incentive tied to 里見's role in selecting a prize and its possible effect on 椎名's career. The first interview already contains both concern for his teacher's standing and the prestige economy that gives the concern force. The novel gives the rival motive to a witness who has her own investment in who is blamed. It allows conviction, self-interest and self-justification to coexist. The narrative supplies an incentive and a confession, with room for overlap between them. See [contested motive](../KSOA_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v02-cl-0003-r02) (C02 0852–0900; C03 0956–1002, 1028–1055).

## The partnership and its methods

亜澄 distinguishes voluntary contact from earned cooperation in her apology to 由良. She uses local knowledge and a learned eye for color, which 元哉 did not know she had despite their childhood connection. She can admit that an interview went wrong, reconstruct its turn-taking later, and return with a test. She also withholds her plans, frames the outcome as a professional necessity and uses familiarity to demand off-duty time. The test on 里見, the limited agreement about his medical privacy and the rehearsal provocation create an accumulating ethical question about discretion. The novel shows 彩音 naming an affront and 椎名 shaken in front of colleagues. Motoya witnesses, supports and sometimes objects. His contributions include noticing the first interview's pattern, obtaining the old employment record and offering a plausible skeptical reading of 壬生's story. He is willing to accept institutional closure before 亜澄. Their distinct errors and contributions are part of the investigative division of labor (C01 0249–0355; C02 0023–0114, 0642–0714; C03 0212–0286, 0351–0528, 0650–0715, 0787–0959).

Both direct the language of attraction toward a third person and read the other's behavior. 亜澄 later admits that taking 椎名 in was painful using an image drawn from ordinary taste and touch. 元哉 refuses emotional engagement, tries to leave and returns after civilians misread their relationship. This case adds explicit evidence of childhood protection and long-standing leverage to the V01 baseline of familiarity and professionalism. Mutual care can be intermittent and costly. The civilians' romantic label, the teasing and the continued joint work support different directed claims with different confidence (C02 0642–0714, 0935–0973; C03 0794–0839; EPI 0019–0087). See [V01 baseline](KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#ksoa-v01-h-0013) and [V02 relationship revision](../KSOA_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v02-cl-0004-r02).

## Place, pace and handoff

The hall, rehearsal rooms, hillside homes, hospital, office and shore have distinct regimes of permission and observation. A yato and the neighborhood's heat affect what the detectives can physically sustain, while 相模湾, the hill view and the shared Hiratsuka past give them a common language outside work. The old hotel's association with the hall is a materially located callback to V01. The epilogue insists on the shore's changed orientation. It takes a site of self-exculpation and public violence and ends on ordinary vulnerability, dusk and civilian watchfulness. C03's polyphony works through sequences of differently positioned testimony, reframings of silence and the concerto's voices coming together before one more rupture (C01 0249–0355, 0965–0994; C02 0715–0851; C03 0290–0310, 0902–0938, 1046–1059; EPI 0005–0087).

The main causal inquiry closes at the level of the novel. The funds investigation has its own case record and procedural endpoint at the cutoff. The novel leaves the ultimate prosecutions, the hearing loss's public status, the ensemble's long-term succession, and full motive weighting open. Carry forward the claim that institutional closure can be provisional, the asymmetric operational trust, the costs of professional mediation and the possibility of independent causal contributions. Carry no assumptions about V03's case.
