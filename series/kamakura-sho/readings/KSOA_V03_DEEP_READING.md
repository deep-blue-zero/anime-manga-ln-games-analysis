Interest in the picture is less discriminatory than ability to act and knowledge of what a study might reveal. The two calls have their identity fixed late, but by combining an independent record with footage rather than treating a lie about location as a confession. The autopsy and recovery site press on the first account. A restaurant visited as ordinary background supplies a later witness to the second actor's movement. Asumi uses a claim about trace evidence that Motoya recognizes as a bluff. The work can therefore dramatize inference and expose the reach of pressure in the same sequence. That bluff has no evidentiary weight, and the contradiction between counts and the stone's location has force conditional on what the first speaker actually did. The second account is strengthened where it explains them. Maya's undisclosed relation and private reasoning become available in her speech, giving the volume stronger retrospective integration than advance identifiability of a precise solution. V03 gives the second person an opportunity only after the first assault, while the evidence for the first attack and the evidence for completion come through different speakers. ## Who gets to interpret, own and disclose the work?

一遥 values art as expression by a person and resists placing it in curatorial categories. His objects of attachment and judgments about how the market rewards him nonetheless coexist with a reliance on those institutions. He prizes 宗二郎's work despite a remembered rivalry, and he changes his disposition of the picture when the study has made its hidden writing legible. 貴遥 is most present through other speakers. The son describes a distant routine, his father dislikes his academic manner and respects his care for art, 真矢 reports a mentor before supplying a harsher account, and 圭子 portrays his pursuit of disclosure as harsh to her family. Hold open the weight of scientific curiosity, prestige and family rivalry. The object can bear beauty, ownership, price, guilt and permission to speak in the same scene because those claims come from different positions (C01 0335–0418, 0825–0899, 0902–0987; C02 0076–0194, 0360–0412; C03 0487–0527, 0544–0558).

The observed tonal difference is a model of bounded expertise. A kimono shop trained 亜澄 to discriminate shades, as she told 元哉 in V02. One experienced painter initially missed the difference and then corroborates it. He supplies an ordinary explanation before they formulate a test for underlying material. The specialist study derives its authority from his permission, the laboratory route and the reported preliminary output. He offers funding that the police would have struggled to allocate. He also chooses who can hear his private account. Asumi had already told Maya she would keep the plan from him. The text gives Maya a protest when that assurance is breached, even as consent improves the investigative route. A promise to limit police publication at the disclosure meeting leaves all later social circulation open. [Test and consent](../KSOA_CASE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v03-ev-0009) (C02 0164–0177; C03 0141–0258).

The daughters inherit different knowledge and burdens. 圭子 values her mother's labor and draws professional opportunities from a father she despises. 真矢 attributes her education to her own mother's work and recounts an acknowledgment that she planned to use publicly. Each mother raised a daughter after being denied durable support, through different means. The accounts of that labor make the men's reputation and the daughters' actions intelligible without excusing them. A comparison to V02's prestige dispute must preserve who reports what. Here both women articulate their own interest and both have a material stake. The sisters' different valid times of learning and different reasons for shielding the work prevent their motives being collapsed into family loyalty. [Competing motives](../KSOA_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v03-cl-0004) (C02 0555–0599, 0651–0668; C03 0399–0433, 0487–0533).

## Walking, watching and caring

Asumi's walking route first elicits resistance and then earns an acknowledgment of its investigative value. The yato are place-name memories, obstacles to movement, social spaces and constraints on who can see whom. The victim's commute and probable meeting site become a shared topography instead of a dot on a map. The detectives' trip also distinguishes an actual temple gate from an idealized landscape. On a different kind of walk, Ichiyō's Hagoromo analogy oscillates between a disappearing woman and the observer's wish to keep her in reach. His account can be read as aesthetic attention and as a projection that displaces the young woman's creative gifts and material choices. The prologue gives the reader access his later interpretation cannot supply. Observe also the absent wife who gave up painting and the two mothers who raised the women now facing him. The myth explains how he speaks and remembers. The reports of material acts sustain the case account (C01 0007–0256, 0661–0703; C03 0020–0099, 0121–0168).

The pair's professional cooperation becomes stronger as the case exposes limits of seeing. Motoya asks the economic question and helps preserve the technical display and record. He misses the tonal difference, criticizes interrogation pressure and can be distracted by Keiko. Asumi both courts assistance and openly puts propositions under pressure. The two use eye-signals and share knowledge of social class and place without drawing the same conclusions about either. Her intoxicated account of being denied attention for her perceived strength gives the reader a stated preference for care and a remembered experience of its withdrawal. Motoya's reaction gives it an unresponsive audience, partly bound by public and institutional exposure. As in V02, an outsider's intervention prompts his staying, while the outsider's role differs. Repeated frustration gives the relationship more texture without yielding a stable joint commitment (C01 0278–0334, 0481–0508, 1004–1058; C02 0687–0752; C03 0257–0273, 0314–0325; EPI 0024–0128).

The novel closes on two attributed admissions, a proposed voluntary transfer and stated intentions for the artwork and estate. The later disposition of the investigations and property is beyond the cutoff. Carry forward V03's use of visual attention and private writing, the distinction between what an admission covers and what remains unexplained, and Asumi's explicit experience of strength being treated as a reason to withhold care. No claim about V04 follows.
