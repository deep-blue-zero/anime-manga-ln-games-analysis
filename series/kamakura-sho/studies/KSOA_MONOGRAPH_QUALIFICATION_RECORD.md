# Character monograph qualification record

This record holds the project's qualification decisions under the [self-contained character monograph standard](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md#self-contained-character-monograph-standard). The [synthesis architecture](../KSOA_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md#character-monograph-qualification) names it as the qualification home. Monographs live under `studies/` and are named `KSOA_<GIVEN>_<FAMILY>.md`.

The project entered character synthesis again on 2026-11-15 with the KSOA-CB1 independent comparison and reconciliation. That review is recorded in the [comparison and adjudication report](../audits/KSOA_CB1_COMPARISON/KSOA_CB1_COMPARISON_AND_ADJUDICATION_REPORT.md). Every character for whom the cumulative homes or the independent KSOA-CB1 baseline hold differentiated evidence was considered.

The test applied to each character has three parts:
- convergent evidence;
- meaningful variation across contexts or interlocutors;
- constraints or counterexamples that rule out a generic sketch.

Prominence does not decide qualification.

## Outcome vocabulary

`QUALIFIED_PENDING`, `COMPLETE_AT_BOUNDARY`, `DEFERRED_EVIDENCE` and `REASSESS_AFTER_REVISION`, as defined in §5.5.1. `NOT_ELIGIBLE_INSUFFICIENT` marks a character whose evidence is trace-level or confined to a single function.

## Acceptance review of the v2.0 lead monographs

Each lead monograph was reviewed on 2026-11-15 by an independent reviewer who did not write it.

The review had two stages:
1. **Self-containment probe.** The reviewer read only the monograph and answered ten fixed questions. The answers were written and hashed before any other file was opened. The questions covered personality, typical and exceptional decisions, reasoning, speech and register, relationship differences, ordinary habits, development, a novel comparable situation, the limits of extrapolation, and what the document cannot answer.
2. **Fidelity check.** The reviewer checked load-bearing claims against the Japanese witnesses, confirmed that the adjudicated corrections were present, and ran the §15.6 name-and-reference check.

The monograph authors corrected the confirmed defects, and a non-author verified each MAJOR fix against the source. The two properties are recorded separately below.
