# Motoya Kitsukawa

*Spoilers for all seven volumes, including every solution and the final epilogue.*

## Orientation

**Who he is.** Motoya Kitsukawa (吉川元哉) is a 巡査長 in the sixth violent-crime unit (強行六係) of the First Investigation Division of Kanagawa Prefectural Police headquarters [V01 C01 p0014]. The witness glosses his surname きつかわ, not the common よしかわ [V01 C01 p0008]. In each of the seven novels a murder headquarters is set up at Kamakura station and he is paired with Asumi Ogasawara (小笠原亜澄), a 巡査部長 in the station's violent-crime section. She is two years younger than he is and grew up on the same Hiratsuka shopping street. She outranks him because she passed the sergeant's examination they sat together and he failed it [V01 C01 p0060]. He is about thirty in V01 and says he will turn thirty-one in spring 2023 [V04 C02 p0043].

**Why he matters.** Outside the prologues and one opening sequence of V02, which is told from the vantage of Kamakura's station chief Takanashi [V02 C01 p0014–0170], the series is narrated in close third person through Motoya. Every case, every view of Kamakura and every reading of Asumi reaches the reader through his perceptions, judgments, memories and mistakes. Asumi's interior is reached only through his inferences (KCMP-SERIES-008). He is the series' consciousness rather than its detective. In every volume the decisive solution is Asumi's, and he says so in his own thought: 「すべては亜澄の功績だ。」 [V07 C04 p0645]. He is nevertheless a focalizer with a job of his own. His work has its firmest basis when it takes the form of locating a record, asking a concrete follow-up or marking what can be said publicly. Examples are the victim's health in V01, the interviewer's byline that joins two cases, the concert hall's staff ledger in V02, the reverse-direction camera check and the request to question the suspect in V04, and the departure-timing point and the alibi endpoints in V06 (§13).

**The portrait in brief.** Motoya is a conscientious, procedure-minded and habitually self-deprecating man from a modest shopkeeping and civil-service family. He places himself as a commoner and measures the wealthy against his salary, his car loan and his off-the-rack suit. He respects hierarchy and rarely tells a superior what he wants. He resents Asumi's big-sister manner and keeps a running private ledger of her faults. Yet from the first case he privately rates her intuition, and by the last he credits her with everything and privately resolves to keep supporting her. He swallows retorts. His thoughts about her are both harsher and kinder than anything he says, and he says what he feels mainly when cornered or drunk. He reads people from their demeanour. That makes him good at sensing that someone is withholding something and unreliable about why people act, above all with a likeable performer or a gentle woman he finds attractive. He distrusts flattering, selfless explanations of motive. He can notice when a proposed explanation goes beyond the evidence while making similarly hopeful leaps about people he likes. In V05–V07 he builds hopes around two women from his private life, Takigawa Sayaka and Hatano Emiri. Each hope comes to nothing, and he is ashamed of having had it. Off duty he likes coffee, which he judges with some precision, cheap filling food, sake, sea air and films on a subscription service. He drinks with Asumi after most cases and usually ends up looking after her. In the last volume he is the one who passes out. The distance between his visible competence and his sense of his own usefulness runs through the whole series.

**Change and revelation in one paragraph.** What changes across the seven novels is the scope, frequency and consequence of his credit to Asumi. His voice with her changes too: the おまえ of V01–V03 disappears, apart from one angry use in V05. A softer 僕 self-presentation appears from V05 onward, and he becomes more aware of his own bias. Almost everything else new about him is the late disclosure of a condition that predates the series: his family, his schools, past girlfriends and an old wound (§14).

**Evidence strength.** His evidence is the densest in the series, because the narration runs through his interior. Professional conduct, pressure, ordinary meals, private hopes and self-reproach are all heavily attested. The main gaps: no scene with his family; almost no stated career ambition; an "old wound" from a past relationship that is never explained [V05 C01 p0061]; no physical description beyond other people's compliments; and no scene in which he conducts an interview alone while a superior watches.

**How to use this document.** §1 sets the evidence conventions and §1a identifies every case figure named here. §2–§13 give the portrait by responsibility. §7 and §8 analyse the gaps between what he thinks, says, does and misjudges. §14 separates development from late revelation. §15 is a conditional behavioural profile, not a tested model. §16 lists what remains uncertain. §17 records what changed from v1.0 and why, and §18 gives source traceability.

## 1. Boundary, sources and evidence conventions

**Boundary.** The portrait covers V01 稲村ヶ崎の落日 through V07 長谷の花舞, ending at V07 EPI p0151. Narrated time runs from late spring 2022 (V01) to the first week of August 2023 (V07). V02's year (2022) and V05's (2023) can be derived from dates in the text (KCMP-V02-013; KCMP-V05-008). V07 opens on Monday 31 July [V07 C03 p0440]; its year is not printed. Nothing about Motoya before or after this span is narrated, except as memory or exposition.

**The focalization rule.** Outside the prologues, the narration is Motoya's close third person, with one exception: V02 opens through Takanashi Hideo, Kamakura's station chief [V02 C01 p0014–0170], and returns to Motoya at [V02 C01 p0174]. The prologues belong to other voices, often a culprit's or a victim's. Asumi's interior is never narrated as such. The narration does sometimes state her intentions without a hedge (for example [V01 C03 p0612]); these are best read as Motoya's confident inferences (KCMP-V01-016). Everything the reader knows about other people's minds therefore passes through his reading, and his reading can be wrong.

**Evidence classes.** Tags are used where the class is not clear from the wording.
- **OBS**: a directly represented act or utterance. That Motoya said something is OBS. What he says about himself is his self-report; what he says about others is his testimony.
- **INT**: Motoya's narrated interiority, meaning his belief, feeling, self-appraisal or inference as the focalized narration gives it. It is evidence of his mind, not of the world. A judgment voiced through him ("this woman is insolent") is his, not the novel's verdict.
- **NARR**: narratorial assertion, mostly background exposition consistent with what he knows.
- **TEST**: another character's statement, report or appraisal.
- **INF**: this monograph's source-supported inference.
- **SPEC**: unverified speculation, flagged where used.
- **UNK**: not shown, or not decidable at this boundary.

An untagged report of what Motoya thinks or feels is INT. An untagged quotation of his speech is OBS.

**Medium.** These are written novels. The text records Japanese register, pronouns, sentence-final particles, stammers (「そ、捜査一課の吉川です」 [V02 C02 p0546]), elongations and narrated manner (あきれ声, 「つばを飛ばして」 [V05 C02 p0812]). It records no voice, accent or timing, and no illustration of Motoya is part of the evidence. "Tone" below always means narrated tone.

**Locators and links.** `[Vnn SEC pNNNN]` gives the paragraph ordinal in the accepted witness `KSOA-JP-Vnn-W01`; SEC is PRO, C01–C04 or EPI. Links to the ledgers, claim records and readings let a reader verify a statement; they do not carry the portrait. Adjudication IDs (`KCMP-…`) resolve to the records in `audits/KSOA_CB1_COMPARISON/`. Every Japanese quotation in this document was checked against its source line during the v2.0 revision.

**Inputs and authority.** This version integrates the KSOA-G1 monograph v1.0 (2026-11-13) with the independently written KSOA-CB1 monograph `05_monographs/CB1_MOTOYA_KITSUKAWA_MONOGRAPH.md` v2.0 (manifest SHA-256 `35f7a16eddf07a6dffb0750e1146c34874d227a486886db36ca7bf9248113040`), as decided by the four adjudications in [`audits/KSOA_CB1_COMPARISON/`](../audits/KSOA_CB1_COMPARISON/ADJ_D.md). KSOA-CB1 is a noncurrent baseline of limited authority, not a second portrait. Where the inputs disagreed, the Japanese text decided. Corrections to the frozen deep readings live in the [deep reading errata](../audits/KSOA_DEEP_READING_ERRATA.md).

## 2. Identity and circumstances

**Names and forms of reference.** 吉川元哉, read Kitsukawa Motoya. Superiors call him 吉川 and civilians 吉川さん. Asumi calls him 吉川くん, キミ, sometimes 元哉くん and, when she is angry, あんた (§9). Her teasing names come from his grandparents' shop: 「吉川紙店」 [V01 C02 p0042] and 「吉川紙店の孫」 [V01 C01 p0995]. The narration calls him 元哉 throughout.

**Rank, unit and chain of command.** He is a 巡査長 in 神奈川県警刑事部捜査一課強行六係 [V01 C01 p0014]. His immediate superior is Masaki Tokio (正木時夫). Masaki is 主任 in V01–V02 [V01 C02 p0032; V02 C01 p0240] and, by V05–V06, 警部補 and head of the unit's third squad (第三班長) [V05 C01 p0381–0382; V06 C01 p0034]. Masaki does not appear in V03, V04 or V07. The headquarters Motoya joins are run by 管理官 Nikaidō, except in V04, where 管理官 Satake commands (KCMP-SERIES-005). Kamakura's violent-crime chief Yoshida speaks for the station side.

**How he lives during a case.** As a headquarters detective he joins investigation HQs anywhere in the prefecture. For the length of each case he sleeps on rented futons in the station judo hall [V03 C02 p0691; V05 C03 p0591; V07 C04 p0007]. Asumi, a Kamakura officer with an apartment in Zaimokuza, sleeps at home [V03 C02 p0689–0690; V04 C03 p0025].

**His standing with Asumi.** They belong to different units and have no line relationship. 管理官s keep pairing them, and she behaves as though she were his instructor [V04 C01 p0022]. He knows the formal position: a 巡査 or 巡査長 commonly pairs with a 巡査部長, who leads, but she is neither his superior nor, strictly, his 指導係 [V05 C02 p0556–0558] (INT). His private theory is that the upper ranks enjoy the inverted childhood pairing [V03 C01 p0028; V04 C01 p0027] (INT). By V06 the pairing has become 「宿命的なもの」 to him [V06 C01 p0932]. The Kamakura forensics chief Watanabe greets him as 「いつも小笠原に引きずり回されている青年」 (TEST). Motoya wonders whether that is how Kamakura station sees him and thinks it 「事実だが、不名誉なことだ」 [V05 C01 p0482–0484].

**Age and appearance.** He is two school years older than Asumi [V01 C01 p0060; V07 C01 p0017]. She is 28 in V01–V03 and 29 in V04 [V04 C02 p0032]. In spring 2023 he tells a witness 「はい、わたしも三一歳になります」 [V04 C02 p0043]. The text never describes his appearance. Others call him 「イケメンの刑事さん」 (Emiri [V05 C01 p0083]) and 「素敵な殿方」 (Kosugi [V06 C03 p0362]), while Asumi tells him 「だからモテないんだよ」 [V04 C01 p0029] (TEST in each case).

**Origins.** He was born and raised on the shopping street at the north exit of Hiratsuka station, the 銀座通り商店街, since renamed スターモール [V01 C01 p0824, p1000]. His grandparents ran the stationer 《吉川紙店》, a paper shop since his great-grandparents' time. His father watched them, chose Hiratsuka city hall instead, and married a city-hall colleague [V01 C01 p0824; V06 C02 p0513–0514] (NARR). From V02 onward the shop is always in the past tense [V02 C01 p0234; V04 C03 p0017; V06 C01 p0107], and V04 records the street's decline [V04 C03 p0017]. Whether the shop still trades is not stated (INF: probably not). He went to the same elementary and middle schools as Asumi, 崇善小学校 and 江陽中学校 [V06 C01 p0108]. He studied for university entrance exams [V03 C01 p0690] and took a liberal-arts course in the history of political thought [V06 C02 p0168]. No siblings are mentioned. The family temple is 高野山真言宗, and he holds no personal religion [V01 C03 p0561].

**Present circumstances.** He lives in Yokohama [V02 C02 p0708; V04 EPI p0074], in a small apartment like an ordinary twenty-year-old block [V03 C02 p0470] (INT). He owns a domestic car worth under ¥3 million on a loan of over ¥70,000 a month, which is 「ラクではない」 [V03 C01 p0491]. At home he uses a coffee server for lack of time [V04 C01 p0735]. He drinks at the counter of a bar near his home [V05 C02 p0764]. He has no girlfriend in V01: when Asumi teases 「あれぇ、キミに彼女なんていたっけぇ？」, he snaps back that she has no man either [V01 C01 p0776–0778].

**Place in the story.** He is the series' focal consciousness, Asumi's partner and foil, and the voice of institutional procedure: warrants, admissibility and the chain of command. From V05 he is also a private man whose off-duty life keeps colliding with the cases.

**What he knew when.** Motoya carries these memories throughout the narrated time. The reader receives them late and in pieces, and nothing suggests he learns anything new about his own past. The bullying story is the instructive case. It first appears as Asumi's leverage (V01–V02). It returns in V07 as Motoya's own acknowledged debt. The shift belongs to his relationship with Asumi, which is development; it is not new knowledge.

**Undocumented.** His parents' present life; whether the stationery shop still trades; his age on joining; any posting other than police-box duty and violent crime; who the women above were and whether the "old wound" belongs to any of them; and why he failed the sergeant's examination and whether he has sat it again.

## 4. Personality and psychology

### 4.1 Values and self-conception

**A commoner's self-placement.** Motoya measures the world from below in every volume (INT). He gapes at mansions and their gates, beginning with 「すげぇ門だ……」 [V01 C01 p0007]. He cannot settle on a magnate's sofa [V01 C03 p0015]. As a shopping-street boy he cannot imagine how the heirs of a business group spend their days [V01 C03 p0182]. A ¥3 million painting is 「大変な金額」 to people like him [V03 C01 p0970], and a ¥30,000 taxi fare is beyond his sense of money [V05 C03 p0390–0391]. He accepts an expensive hamburg lunch only because there is nothing else nearby [V06 C01 p0899], and he would rather not even know what Ginza 料亭 charge [V07 C03 p0147]. When the wealthy Gamō family reconciles with generous grace, something does not sit right with him. He concludes 「やっぱり自分は庶民として生まれ育ったんだな」 and adds 「俺は彼らほど純粋にはなれないな……」, and the thought leaves him feeling pathetic [V01 C03 p1100–1108]. The self-placement wavers in detail: he "grew up comfortably" [V03 C03 p0055], yet is "glad to be a poor man's child" [V04 C03 p1119]. The constant is relative: wherever he stands, the people in the case stand above him (INF).

**Public-servant integrity.** He refuses a magnate's reward outright: 「いえ、失礼ながらそれはお断りします。わたしたちは公務員ですので」 [V01 C03 p1145]. He silently agrees when Asumi turns down a cash envelope [V01 C01 p0962]. After the V05 magazine interview he asks Sayaka to keep his name out of it, because it would be trouble if his superiors heard [V05 C01 p0227–0229]. When Emiri mentions the interview in front of Asumi, he runs through the public-servant side-job rule and dismisses it in a sentence, since no fee was paid. His real worry is Asumi's reaction, and he tells himself, and later her, that Sayaka tricked him into it (「俺はだまされたんだよ」) [V05 C02 p0538–0540, p0746].

**Information discipline.** From V01 he checks whether something has been released before saying it. He is alarmed when Asumi tells the editor Torii about an unreleased clue [V01 C02 p0645–0647]. When she names the victim to Torii, he notes that it has already been reported and so is acceptable: 「すでに報道されている内容なので問題ない」 [V01 C02 p0621]. The discipline holds through V07 (§8).

**Moral and civic reflection, nearly all in thought.**
- Standing above the reservoir at the V03 scene, he reflects that unseen labour supplies water as the police supply safety: 「水と安全はタダではない」 [V03 C01 p0115].
- Intruding on a frightened witness makes police work 「嫌な稼業」 [V03 C02 p0497].
- He feels fate's irony when Keiko and her mother, who hate her father Yuhara Sōjirō (湯原宗二郎) for his violence, must protect his name for their own sake [V03 C03 p0414–0416].
- He admits that a stage-IV diagnosis would unhinge him, and marvels at the old painter's self-command [V03 EPI p0030–0040].
- He cannot understand Miyazaki pitying Shichijō, whom he served, when she was pregnant by Sekigawa. He grants that Miyazaki's love is peculiar and concludes that love differs from person to person, so that 「これが愛のかたちだ、などというものは存在するはずもないのだ」 [V04 C03 p0949–0955]. He judges the murdered Akao a worthless man all the same [V04 C03 p0924].
- He thinks Asumi 「意地が悪い」 for exposing everyone's secrets at the V05 gathering [V05 C03 p0926].
- Marriage, he thinks, is decided by the will of the two people alone; a doting father's possessiveness is 「歪」 [V06 C03 p0421].
- He hopes the bailed Yakushiji will not be indicted [V06 EPI p0007].

His moral sense is decent and conventional, and it is voiced almost entirely inwardly.

**What sustains him.** The single explicit statement comes in V05 (INT). A detective's hours barely exist, he lives under tension and bosses nag, but the feeling that he is 「世の中から犯罪を減らすために力を尽くしている」 sustains him; it may be, he adds wryly, 「やりがい搾取のようなブラックな職場かもしれない」 [V05 C01 p0038–0041]. To Sayaka he deflects with 「いえ、しょせんはサラリーマンですから……」, and the narration marks it as another evasion [V05 C01 p0042–0043]. Her reply, that he is a detective who lives for his work, goes unanswered [V05 C01 p0048].

**Salaryman ethic.** 「刑事がどんなに大変でも、仕事してりゃ給料はもらえるからな」 [V06 C02 p0511]. He has always meant never to be self-employed, a family precept [V06 C02 p0512–0515]. He is glad not to have chosen a creative profession, "not that he had the ability" [V05 C03 p0662–0663].

**Self-assessment: modest, often deflating.**
- He sums up his own instinct aloud: 「まぁ、俺の直感なんてたいしたことないけどな」 [V04 C02 p0796]. He says this just after sensing that Shichijō's answers about Sekigawa sounded like a model answer [V04 C02 p0708–0710, p0788]. In the same breath he judged that she was not lying, 「晶子さんはウソはついていないと思った」, yet she had lied in that interview about not meeting Sekigawa after her retirement [V04 C02 p0698, p0788; V04 C03 p0733] (§7.4).
- Asumi's associative leaps are 「元哉にはできない思考法だ」 [V01 C03 p0940].
- He supposes he lives in a world of colour a tenth as rich as hers [V03 C03 p0183].
- He "only tagged along" [V07 C04 p0645].

Against this, the narration states that only capable detectives reach the First Investigation Division: 「いちおう優秀な刑事でなければ、捜査一課には配属されない」 [V04 C02 p0833] (NARR). He cannot answer to the label "elite" because he is ordered about by a precinct officer [V02 C03 p0081]. The evidence shows a competent man who habitually ranks himself below the people around him (INF).

### 4.2 Motives and goals

- **Stated.** Reducing crime; detective work since joining (§4.1).
- **Situational and openly admitted.** Sleep and release. He hopes a death will prove an accident so that no headquarters is set up, which 「強行犯の刑事なら誰しも願うことだ」 [V05 C01 p0509–0510]. He welcomes the V02 headquarters' conclusion that Ishikawa misremembered the alarm, partly because 「元哉たちは本件から解放されるのだ」 [V02 C03 p0790]. He dreads the damp judo-hall futons [V02 C02 p0967] and treasures a free Saturday night as 「久しぶりのパラダイス」 [V02 C03 p0795].
- **Engagement that grows inside a case.** In V01 his "detective instinct began to burn a little" [V01 C02 p0733], and for the first time he wanted to recover the manuscript himself [V01 C03 p0124]. In V03 「元哉も楽しくなってきた。」 [V03 C03 p0228]. He wants to keep pursuing the painting but knows command must approve [V03 C02 p0771].
- **Counterweight.** On hot and cold days he often longs for indoor work such as 警務課 [V02 C03 p0298]. It is passing envy, not a plan.
- **Not evidenced.** Ambition for promotion, a plan to retake the sergeant's examination, or any wish to leave the First Investigation Division. The text never tests these, so they remain unknown rather than absent.

### 4.3 Fears, vulnerabilities and coping

- **Asumi's displeasure.** He fears reprisal and future pairings if he abandons her drunk [V01 EPI p0060–0061]. He fears renewed blackmail whenever she mentions his grandfather's shop [V01 C02 p0045]. He dreads her moods, which may turn sour again at any time [V05 C03 p0589]. He hides Emiri's V07 visit because the previous month Asumi stopped speaking to him for a while [V07 C04 p0238–0240].
- **Embarrassment and exposure.** He fears that superiors will learn of his magazine interview [V05 C01 p0229]; that a waitress takes them for a quarrelling couple [V05 C02 p0733]; that he looks like a shabby detective in a hotel lounge, in a 「吊るしのスーツ」 ironed that morning [V05 C03 p0615–0619]; that returning to headquarters early would look like slacking [V03 C02 p0459]; and that Asumi will end up in the drunk tank and shame Kamakura's detectives [V03 EPI p0115].
- **A romantic hurt** that he protects by keeping his distance [V05 C01 p0060–0061].
- **Coping.**
  - He swallows retorts because quarrelling would spoil the day: 「だが、元哉も大人だ。」 [V02 C01 p0293].
  - He ignores her teasing, because getting angry 「亜澄に娯楽を与えるだけのことだ」 [V03 C02 p0705].
  - He lets things pass to avoid trouble [V05 C02 p0885].
  - He curses himself aloud: 「俺はバカだっ」 [V01 C01 p0843]; 「俺はバカだ……」 [V03 EPI p0006; V04 EPI p0134]; 「俺はやっぱりバカだっ」 [V03 EPI p0125].
  - He escapes [V06 EPI p0036–0042], or falls silent when cornered [V07 EPI p0130].

### 4.4 Contradictions

- He resents Asumi's condescension and condescends himself. He wishes she could speak more sweetly [V01 C01 p0587], thinks her "cute as a woman, perhaps, but a failure as a detective" [V01 C02 p0416] and calls her a child in thought [V02 C01 p0294]. Before a witness he volunteers 「失礼ですが、こいつはもう三十路に手が届くんですがね」 [V04 C02 p0035].
- He calls her insolent, yet from her first case he privately values her intuition: 「亜澄の直感力のよさを実は評価している。」 [V01 C02 p0316].
- He insists on procedure, yet keeps information from superiors when it protects people he likes or spares him trouble. He withholds Emiri's link to the V05 case from Masaki [V06 C01 p0591]. He joins Asumi's V02 secrecy promise to Ayane after calculating that no reprimand is likely [V02 C03 p0467–0469].
- He objects to Asumi's bluffs, then reflects that detectives bluff too, for example with 「すべて調べはついているんだ」 [V05 C02 p0759–0760].
- He thinks the work blunts feeling: 「事件が起きるたびに感情移入をしていては身がもたない」 [V06 C01 p0081]. Yet he looks away from Yakushiji's tears [V06 C03 p0741], cannot answer a weeping widow [V05 C02 p0382], and prays that Yakushiji will not be indicted [V06 EPI p0007].

## 5. Cognition and decision-making

### 5.1 What he notices

- **Practical, concrete facts.**
  - The dead novelist's health and medication: 「あの、ひとつだけ伺いたいんですけど、蘆名さんの健康状態はいかがだったんでしょうか」 [V01 C01 p0219]. Asumi later calls it a 「ナイスプレイ」 [V01 C01 p0580].
  - The byline at the end of a magazine interview with Ashina. While Asumi and Torii talk about publication, Motoya glances at the last page and finds that the interviewer was Uehara, the murdered writer [V01 C02 p0604–0613]. It is the first documented tie between Ashina and Uehara.
  - Who holds the key to the lighting panel: 「この端子盤の鍵は誰が持っているのですか？」 [V02 C01 p0438].
  - Whether an expensive car fits a part-timer's income: 「失礼ですが、あのクルマ高いでしょ？」. He adds in thought that 「こうした違和感を放っておけないのが、刑事という職業だ」 [V03 C01 p0486, p0494].
  - The price and posthumous value of a painting, and whether the victim expected it to rise [V03 C01 p0966–0980].
  - That the V04 victim was 「多少は金には汚い」, though no money dispute serious enough for murder had been found [V04 C01 p0646]; later, the ¥5 million withdrawal in Miyazaki's passbook [V04 C03 p0578].
  - Whom to pursue once robbery is ruled out: 「そうなると、鑑が濃い者を追いかけるべきだな」 [V07 C01 p0258].
- **Money and class.** Always (§4.1).
- **Faces and demeanour.** These are his main instrument for reading people. He judges Haruto's alibi by his 「自信たっぷりな口調と浮かれ方」 and the seven witnesses he names [V03 C01 p0477–0478]. Of Tanaka: 「表情をずっと観察していたけど、大きなウソをついているようには見えなかったな」 [V05 C03 p0403]. Of Mizutani: 「ただ、水谷さんはなにか大きなことを隠している」 [V06 C02 p0253].
- **What he does not notice.**
  - Colour and pictorial nuance. He sees no difference in the hedge colour that Asumi and Ichiyō see: 「だが、元哉にはどう見ても同じ色にしか見えなかった。」 [V03 C03 p0165].
  - Kamakura's geography. He relies on Asumi and on map apps [V05 C02 p0020; V06 C01 p0622–0625].
  - The meaning of Asumi's emotional talk when she is drunk: 「なにを言っているのか理解できないが、理解する気もない。」 [V02 EPI p0053].

### 5.2 How he reasons

**(a) A conventional prior, defended firmly.** He reaches for the most ordinary explanation and argues it with conviction.
- V01: the dying novelist set a last prank for his editor, 「イタズラだよ」 [V01 C01 p0726]. The narration adds that 「言葉にしているうちに、事実はこれしかないだろう、そんな気がしてきた。」 [V01 C01 p0731].
- V03: the victim must have known his killer, so the motive must be a grudge [V03 C01 p0151–0155]. Asumi calls the second step 「それは予断じゃん」; when she points out that something more important than a wallet might have been taken, he 「旗を巻かざるを得なかった」 [V03 C01 p0157–0165]. The motive did prove personal, so what he conceded was the procedural point rather than the substance.
- V04: against Asumi's interest in the dead film star's history, 「しかし、関川洋介は昭和四八年つまり五〇年前に死んでいる。」 [V04 C01 p0655].
- V07: copying a dish could hardly be a motive for murder, and the rival chef died two years ago [V07 C01 p0403–0405].

When the prior is structural, he is often right: that the V03 killer knew the victim; that contempt does not make Izumida a murderer [V07 C02 p0416]; that flexibility does not prove Eguchi bore no grudge [V07 C03 p0244]. When the prior concerns a particular person's inner life, his record is mixed (§7.4).

**(b) The institutional frame.** He thinks in procedural categories.
- 「警察は上意下達の組織である。」 [V01 C02 p0273].
- A search warrant would be refused while no suspect is identified [V01 C03 p0516].
- A confession provoked at a memorial mass cannot be used as evidence [V01 C03 p0612].
- 「初動捜査は捜一の仕事じゃないぞ」 [V05 C01 p0361].
- A canvass loses value as memories fade [V05 C01 p0681–0682].
- He supplies the law in the scene: 刑訴法一四九条 [V01 C03 p0276, p0279]; a suit to deny filiation backed by DNA [V01 C03 p0404]; 刑法一九九条 [V04 C03 p0690]; 傷害致死 against murder, with excessive self-defence only a discretionary mitigation [V06 C02 p0694]; 犯人隠避罪 under 刑法一〇三条 [V06 C03 p0735]; lying to a detective as distinct from perjury [V06 C03 p0244]; and the limited weight of a lover's alibi [V07 EPI p0121].

This frame makes him the pair's brake. It also leads him to underweight hunches that have no stated basis.

**(c) Doubt Asumi's hunch, then yield and follow through.** The pattern recurs in five volumes.
- V01: 「おいおい、蘆名は自殺で片づいてるじゃないか」, then he decides he 「亜澄の言葉にも耳を傾けるべきだ」 [V01 C02 p0213–0221].
- V03: 「根拠はないよな」, said to test her, then 「俺もそのことには気づいてた……」 [V03 C02 p0252–0256].
- V04: 「根拠はよ？」, she answers 「ない」, he objects that Sekigawa died fifty years ago, and then: 「小笠原の勘に期待しよう」 [V04 C01 p0617–0659].
- V06: 「うーん、どうかな」, beside his own thought that 「亜澄の勘はあなどれない」 [V06 C02 p0533–0534].
- V07: 「おい、本当に行くのか。あんまり関係ないんじゃないか」, then 「わかったよ。小笠原の勘に従うよ」 [V07 C01 p0423–0427].

Each time the narration adds his private record of her past successes [V04 C01 p0642–0644; V07 C01 p0429–0430]. In V07 it goes further and entertains the idea that her intuition is 「言語による思考よりも高次元の発想」 [V07 C01 p0430] (INT).

The record divides by kind of question.
- **Lines of inquiry.** When he resisted a line she proposed to pursue (V01, V03, V04, V06, V07), the line proved productive every time.
- **Readings of motive.** When he resisted her reading of a person's motive, he was sometimes right.
  - In V02 he would not accept her "beautiful story" about the conductor Satomi's decades of support for Miura (5.2f). At the solution he concludes that the support was atonement. That conclusion is his own free indirect judgment, resting on Shiina's uncorroborated report; Satomi never confirms it (KCMP-V02-011).
  - In V07 he cautioned that Izumida's contempt for the victim did not make him a murderer [V07 C02 p0416]. Eguchi was arrested; Izumida's late alibi came through Sayaka, uncorroborated, and contradicted Izumida's own earlier account (KCMP-V07-012).

**(d) Sharp leaps when facts converge.**
- He names Satomi Takako before Father Carpentier does, biting the inside of his mouth as he asks: 「いままでにわかっていた事実からほかにないと思ったのです」 [V01 C03 p0344–0348]. Asumi supplies the hotel and Gamō Takasato's name in the same scene (KCMP-V01-004).
- He spots the byline that ties the two V01 cases together [V01 C02 p0604–0613].
- He sees the pattern in the V02 interview correctly: Satomi pauses and Ayane jumps in. He attributes it to temperament, but the cause was Satomi's deafness [V02 C02 p0623–0625, p0670–0672] (KCMP-V02-007).
- He senses that whoever stole Atobe's drawings 「若尾の事件の重要な鍵を握っている」 [V05 C03 p0736]. He is right: by Emiko's and Enomoto's accounts the thief was the victim [V05 C03 p0876, p0918–0922]. Asumi charges that the secret was Hani's lever for extortion; Hani admits only the blow [V05 C03 p0952–0954] (KCMP-V05-010).
- In V06 he points out that two pupils left Yakushiji at about the same time, which strictness cannot explain [V06 C02 p0525] (KCMP-V06-009).
- In V07 Asumi finds the old 《雨月庵》 staff photograph, sets it before him and tells him 「もっとちゃんと見なさいよ」. Only then does he recognize Shiobara, despite her different hairstyle [V07 C04 p0302–0316]. The discovery is hers; the recognition, prompted, is his. He then draws the inference himself: 「どう考えても塩原はなにかの役割を果たしている。」 [V07 C04 p0323].

What he cannot do is assemble a case's structure: 「しかし、若尾事件の構図は、元哉には少しも見えてこなかった。」 and 「亜澄には違う脳内回路があるのだな」 [V05 C03 p0766–0767].

**(e) Initiative, when the facts are in hand or a step is agreed.**
- V02: the call to the concert hall, whose topic he had agreed with Asumi beforehand, establishes that the suspect Ishikawa had worked there until thirteen years earlier and retired as 管理課長 in March 2009. Neither the headquarters nor Motoya had known that the old staff ledgers survived [V02 C03 p0650–0667]. The call broke Ishikawa's denial on the device. The murder of Miura was solved by Asumi, who exposed Shiina [V02 C03 p1017–1020]. It was a real initiative with a bounded effect.
- V04: he proposes meeting the biography's editor at the publisher [V04 C01 p0675]. Once Asumi has found the car in the camera footage, he proposes checking the return direction, and the narration gives the route reasoning as his own [V04 C03 p0426–0431]. He then tells 管理官 Satake, 「毅然とした態度で」, 「まずは、わたしと小笠原で直接、宮崎の事情聴取をしたいと思います」, and predicts that Miyazaki will not flee because flight would trouble Shichijō [V04 C03 p0473–0484]. Earlier in the same volume, stating a wish to a 管理官 had been 「元哉にはとてもできない業だ」 [V04 C01 p0885].
- V06: 「薬師寺という大作家には、どうしても会ってみる必要があるな」 [V06 C02 p0529]. Then come the housekeeper call and the taxi check that fix the suspects' arrival and departure times [V06 C03 p0270–0305].

**(f) Scepticism of selfless motives (V02).** On hearing that Satomi supported Miura from the age of twelve, at a cost he reckons in the tens of millions of yen, he says 「俺さ、小笠原ほどは思い入れできないんだよな」 and 「そうかなぁ、俺は美談は信じないんだよなぁ」 [V02 C03 p0224–0232]. He suspects 「もっと人間的な理由」 that he cannot name [V02 C03 p0236–0237]. Asumi retorts 「キミのこころは汚れておる」, and he hears her real opinion under the joke: 「亜澄の本音のような気がした」 [V02 C03 p0234–0235]. At the solution he thinks 「やはり美談とは言えなかったのだ。」 [V02 C03 p1001]. As noted, the conclusion is his, on Shiina's word. The disposition behind it is sustained and genuine.

### 5.3 Uncertainty and conflicting obligations

- **Uncertainty.** He hopes for something to turn up, 「瓢箪から駒」 [V04 C01 p0897], and that tomorrow brings progress [V05 C03 p0586]. He is patient about wasted legwork: 「無駄足に終わる聞き込みは刑事の常である。」 [V05 C01 p0732]. To Asumi's 「だいたい刑事の仕事は九〇パーセント以上が無駄なんだから」 he answers 「まぁ、そりゃあそうなんだけどさ」 [V07 C01 p0579–0581].
- **Duty against private wish.**
  - He leaves the two women and his day off the moment Masaki orders it [V05 C01 p0409–0414].
  - When Emiri pleads for Izumida at night, 「元哉の心は乱れた」, but 「元哉は自分が答えるべきことはわかっていた」: 「残念ですが、僕は刑事です」 [V07 C04 p0187–0189].
  - He keeps case information from Sayaka and Emiri [V07 C02 p0184–0185; V07 C04 p0107–0126].
  - In V06 he tells Emiri honestly that her disclosure cannot be kept from Asumi or the headquarters [V06 C03 p0240].
- **Loyalty to friends against thoroughness.** In V06 he tells Masaki that neither Emiri nor Sayaka needs an alibi check, and keeps back Emiri's link to the V05 case (§8).
- **Partner against superiors.** On the V04 beach, alone with Asumi, he finds her theory that Miyazaki killed out of conviction incredible: 「信じられんな」 [V04 C03 p0303–0321]. When Yoshida calls it a fantasy and Satake asks his view, he concedes 「たしかに突飛だとは思いますが」 and adds that Miyazaki's love and service toward Shichijō 「尋常なものとは思われません」. The narration adds 「元哉としては、亜澄への援護射撃を行うしかなかった。」 [V04 C03 p0370–0375]. In V02 he corroborates her test before a hostile witness, 「残念ながらわたしも見ていました」 [V02 C03 p0374–0375], and backs her story to another [V02 C02 p0095–0096]. In public he backs her; his objections come in private, afterwards.

## 6. Behavioural patterns: conduct, mistakes, corrections and exceptions

### 6.1 Ordinary conduct and cooperation

- **Reluctant compliance (the core pattern).** He grumbles, pouts or groans, then does what is asked:
  - 「うへっ」 [V01 C02 p0033]; 「結局、今回も小笠原と組むのかよ」 [V01 C02 p0281]; 「また、おまえかよ」 [V02 C01 p0227];
  - 「は……小笠原とですか」, feigning surprise though he knows it is useless [V06 C01 p0224–0225];
  - 「わかったよ」 [V07 C04 p0305].
- **Weekend summonses (V01–V02).** Asumi opens with the same line in both volumes, 「ねぇ、明日の日曜日ヒマでしょ？」 [V01 C01 p0756; V02 C03 p0806]. The narration calls it a 枕詞 by which she makes him act against his will [V01 C01 p0760; V02 C03 p0809] (KCMP-V02-009). She adds the bullying debt [V01 C01 p0815–0831; V02 C03 p0820–0827]. He refuses loudly (「忙しい、忙しい、忙しい」 [V02 C03 p0811]) and gives in. In V01 he tells himself he came because he could not ignore how invested she was in the case, not because of the threat [V01 C01 p0841–0842] (INT; it may be self-justification, §16).
- **Courtesy to witnesses.**
  - He softens the hall manager Endō's mood with an idle question. Detectives need not care about moods, he reasons, but he wants Endō's continued cooperation [V02 C01 p0461–0464].
  - He offers to talk at the door when the witness Keiko is worried about her neighbours [V03 C02 p0498–0502].
  - 「大丈夫ですか」 to a widow weeping in her kitchen, and then silence [V05 C02 p0375–0382].
  - He lets Miyazaki brief the housekeeper before going with him: 「わかりました。もちろんかまいません」 [V04 C03 p0596–0598].
  - He relays Emiri's praise of Mitsuki to soften her father [V06 C03 p0414–0415].
- **Managing up.** He voices what he senses Masaki privately thinks [V06 C01 p0206–0207]. He feigns compliance: 「いえ、了解しました」 [V01 C02 p0037–0038].
- **Silent coordination with Asumi.** He asks by a look whether he may question a witness, and she gives a slight nod [V03 C01 p0482]. He runs the laptop and slides at the V03 presentation [V03 C03 p0238]. Faced with a talkative director, 「期せずして二人は同じことを考えたようだ。」 [V04 C02 p0049]. By V07 she asks his view first after interviews: 「吉川くんは、どう思う？」 [V07 C03 p0660].

### 6.2 Conflict and stress

- **Answering Asumi's teasing.**
  - Short jabs: 「生意気な女だな」; 「おい、ほめてないぞ。日本語勉強しろよ」 [V01 C01 p0050–0053]; 「おまえ長生きするよ」 [V03 C01 p0619].
  - Revenge teasing about her weakness for handsome men: 「おまえ、イケメンに甘過ぎるんだよ」 [V01 C03 p0408]; 「小笠原、なんだよ、デレデレしちゃって」 [V02 C02 p0938]; 「イケメンにはすぐにデレデレする小笠原に言われたくないよ」 [V03 C02 p0723]. The trait is his narratorial view of her, which she denies (KCMP-CHAR-005).
  - Most often, silence (§4.3).
- **Genuine anger, rare and diagnostic.** In the V05 café quarrel Asumi suggests he told a civilian where the investigation was going: 「元哉は本気で腹を立てた。」 He answers 「おい、小笠原、おまえ頭大丈夫か？」 [V05 C02 p0785–0789]. The trigger was an attack on his professional integrity, not teasing. A month later in V06 she makes the same insinuation, 「あんた、捜査情報を漏らしてんじゃないでしょうね？」. This time he answers 「そんなはずないだろ」 with an account of where he had been, 「いささかムカつきながらも、元哉は理詰めに説明した」 [V06 C01 p0650–0654]. The same trigger now draws irritation and argument rather than anger and おまえ (INF).
- **Panic and fluster.** When Sayaka jokes that he can vouch for her whereabouts the night before: 「お、おい。なに言ってんだよ。俺は昨日は仕事だ。」 [V06 C01 p0501]. Asked how he knows Emiri, in front of Asumi: 「はい……友人です……」, in a vanishing voice [V05 C02 p0531].
- **Escape.** He tiptoes out of the V06 bar as Sayaka and Asumi trade insults, 「トイレですっ」, then 「しまった。カバン忘れた」 [V06 EPI p0036–0042]. He plans to slip away from a drunk Asumi in V02 and V04 [V02 EPI p0058; V04 EPI p0108].
- **Concealment to avoid friction.** He keeps Emiri's night visit from Asumi [V07 C04 p0238–0240]. Caught out in the epilogue, he tells the truth in one sentence and then declines to answer: 「面倒なので元哉は返事をしなかった。」 [V07 EPI p0126–0130].

### 6.3 Caretaking after the case (V01–V04; altered in V06–V07)

At the end of a case Asumi treats or lures him to drinks, gets drunk and quarrelsome, and he is left with her. His motives shift from volume to volume.
- **V01.** He thinks of leaving her asleep, but 「だが、報復が怖い。またいつ亜澄と組まされるかわからないのだ。」 [V01 EPI p0060–0061]. He also tries practically to get her to the last train: 「終電なくなるぞ。起きろよ」 [V01 EPI p0072]. Fear of reprisal is the reason the narration gives; the care is shown in what he does (KCMP-V01-012).
- **V02.** Asumi is heavily drunk [V02 EPI p0018–0025] (KCMP-V02-008). He decides to leave her asleep on a seafront bench, since 「別に危険な場所でもない」 [V02 EPI p0058–0061]. Two elderly crime-prevention volunteers scold him for abandoning 「大切な彼女」 [V02 EPI p0064–0067]. He swallows his anger, claims to have been looking for a toilet, and goes back to her [V02 EPI p0069–0078].
- **V03.** Having shown his badge to a patrol officer, he 「亜澄をここに捨てて帰るわけにはいかない」 [V03 EPI p0124].
- **V04.** He plans to slip away; neighbours complain; he looks up a taxi company [V04 EPI p0108–0136].
- **V05.** No drinking epilogue.
- **V06.** He escapes the bar instead [V06 EPI p0036–0042].
- **V07.** He is the one who blacks out [V07 EPI p0131].

The caretaking is grudging, practical and driven by accountability. The one tender moment is V01's, and he disowns it (§12). The private vow at the end of V07 follows more than one occasion on which he has already looked after her, so it cannot mark the first instance of his care (§14).

### 6.4 Mistakes and corrections

His recurrent errors fall into four families.
1. **Settling early on the obvious about someone's inner life.** The novelist's "prank" [V01 C01 p0726–0731]. Hideyuki as the manuscript thief [V01 C03 p0404–0408]; Asumi's doubt, which he mocks as partiality to a handsome man, proves right. Miyazaki's motive as an investment fraud, 「これが真の動機だったか。」 [V04 C03 p0582]; the real lever was Akao's extortion over Shichijō's secret.
2. **Liking or attraction possibly lowering his demand for verification.** In V03 he registers the critic Keiko's beauty and suppresses 「刑事が事件関係者に持つべきでない感情の発露」 [V03 C02 p0478, p0533–0534, p0596]. Later in the same interview she volunteers that she was in Tokyo bookshops from three to five [V03 C02 p0646]. That span begins before the window the police asked about and covers the times of the calls to the victim [V03 C02 p0762] (KCMP-V03-001). He thinks the bookshops' cameras might prove it, then: 「もっともそんな捜査は必要ないはずだ。」 [V03 C02 p0648–0650]. The narration gives no reason. That the attraction shaped the thought is an inference (INF), not a narrated fact. Separately, in V05 he judges the chance that Hani is the culprit 「皆無」 and his alibi unproblematic [V05 C02 p0370, p0397] (KCMP-V05-003). Hani is a likeable performer whose demeanour he trusted; no attraction is involved.
3. **Underweighting Asumi's lines of inquiry that lack a stated basis** (five volumes; 5.2c).
4. **Misreading women's intentions,** Asumi's and others' (§7.4).

A "reasoning lapse" sometimes alleged in V02 is withdrawn here. When the musician Yura gives an izakaya visit for the evening, Motoya concludes that Yura has no alibi [V02 C02 p0265]. Yura has said the same himself, 「僕にはアリバイはありません」 [V02 C02 p0258], and an unverified self-report is not an alibi (KCMP-V02-015).

**Corrections and self-awareness.**
- He corrects small misreadings on his own. The sound leaking from Asumi's phone was due to how she held it [V02 C02 p0440]. She was not advertising her family's shop after all [V04 C02 p0639].
- In V05 he turns reflexive: 「英美里を好意的に見るあまり、自分の判断が間違っているのだろうか。」 [V05 C02 p0876].
- In V06 he hears himself making excuses: 「自分の言葉が言い訳めいているな」 [V06 C03 p0318]; 「自分はそこまで英美里をかばう必要はないような気もする」 [V06 C03 p0328].
- He admits ignorance readily: art (「わたしは美術関係には非常に暗くて……」 [V03 C01 p0510]); sea glass [V05 C01 p0139]; the word 奸譎 [V06 EPI p0030].

### 6.5 Meaningful exceptions

- **Overriding Asumi on principle.** Against her secrecy at the V06 cordon, he releases harmless facts in order to draw more out: 「まぁ、いずれわかることだ。規制線のなかのことを話してもいいんじゃないか」 [V06 C01 p0417–0418].
- **Objecting to her ethics, to her face.** After she questions Emiri about her alibi at the victim's house, which he thinks inwardly is 「職権濫用ではないか」 [V05 C02 p0674], he says in the café: 「波多野さんのアリバイなんぞ訊く必要はなかっただろ」; 「だって動機がないじゃないか」; 「そんな女性じゃないことは見てりゃわかるだろっ」 [V05 C02 p0850–0866]. It is his strongest confrontation with her, and it comes where her conduct touches his private life.
- **Siding with Asumi against a civilian friend.** When Sayaka jokes at Asumi's expense: 「ここばかりは亜澄に味方したくなった。」; Sayaka is 「悪趣味」 [V06 C01 p0507, p0512].
- **Leading a questioning.** He leads the questioning of Miyazaki [V04 C03 p0495–0598] (§13).
- **Full credit to Asumi, unprompted** [V07 C04 p0645].

The V05 arrest is not an exception. He makes it on Asumi's explicit order, 「吉川、手錠掛けて、宣告して」, and his inner reaction is 「なんでこいつは上司面をするんだ」 [V05 C03 p0959–0962].

## 7. Private interpretation, utterance, professional action and misjudgment

Because the narration sits inside Motoya, the text keeps four layers distinct: what he privately concludes, what he says, what he does professionally, and where his judgments of other people fail. The divergence between these layers is a large part of his character. His hesitation often belongs to his internal response. A source-grounded account has to distinguish that hesitation from the calm phrasing another character hears.

Two regularities follow.
1. His interior is harsher about Asumi than his speech, and kinder about her than anything he would say aloud. Both the insults and the admiration are mostly withheld.
2. The narration certifies his sincerity when speech and thought coincide: 本音 [V01 C01 p0979; V04 C02 p0088; V04 EPI p0030; V05 C01 p0399; V05 C03 p0404; V06 C03 p0068; V07 C02 p0078]; ほぼ本音 and そのまま本音 [V02 EPI p0042, p0046]; 「お世辞ではなく」 [V02 C02 p0486]; 「まんざらお世辞でもなく」 [V02 C03 p0249]. These markers imply that his words are guarded by default (INF).

A small echo across the series shows the same restraint. In V01, drunk and among friends, he says aloud of Hideyuki's public declaration of love, 「よくもヌケヌケとあんな大勢の前で言えるよな」 [V01 EPI p0049]. In V07 the same idiom, about Emiri and Izumida, stays in his head.

### 7.2 Spoken response and professional action

His voiced objections rarely stop him acting.
- **V01.** The brass will not listen, he says, and a warrant would be refused, so the line must wait for evidence [V01 C03 p0508–0517]. He then helps carry out Asumi's unorthodox trap at the memorial mass.
- **V03.** 「関係者に対して、亜澄はいつも大胆な方法で質問をする。」 He often feels mortified, sweats and suppresses a jiggling leg, but never interrupts her [V03 C02 p0327–0334].
- **V04.** 「信じられんな」 to her theory in private [V04 C03 p0320], then public supporting fire for it before Satake [V04 C03 p0374–0375]. It is the series' sharpest pairing of private doubt with public backing.
- **V07.** 「結局、《雨月庵》関係にまわることになったじゃないか。無駄足が続くな」 [V07 C01 p0577], and he walks the whole 《雨月庵》 line.

The one point where objection becomes refusal is off duty: 「ご苦労さん。俺はオフだよ」 and 「初動捜査は捜一の仕事じゃないぞ」 [V05 C01 p0345–0361]. Even then he goes the moment Masaki orders it [V05 C01 p0387–0414].

### 7.3 Private interpretation and professional action

- **V03.** He registers and suppresses his attraction to Keiko, and later in the same interview privately waives a camera check of her alibi, with no reason narrated [V03 C02 p0533–0534, p0596, p0648–0650]. That the attraction steered the judgment is an inference (INF). It is the most suggestive such case in the series, but it is not narrated.
- **V06.** He likes Emiri and Sayaka. He vouches for them to Masaki and keeps back Emiri's link to the earlier case [V06 C01 p0591–0607] (§8).
- **V07.** He hopes Emiri likes him [V07 C04 p0076], but his professional answers to her stay curt and correct: 「その質問には答えられないです」 [V07 C04 p0151]; 「残念ですが、僕は刑事です」 [V07 C04 p0189]. Here the private wish does not steer the action. He even explains the alibi logic 「少し意地悪な調子かもしれないなと思いながら」 [V07 C04 p0181].

Read as a sequence, V03, V06 and V07 suggest that attraction influences his professional action less over time; by V07 he separates the two explicitly. Because the V03 link is itself inferred, the trend is tentative (INF).

**Correct readings, for calibration.**
- Haruto is no killer: 「あいつは人殺しができるようなタマじゃないと思う。気のちいさな男だよ」. The narration notes it is 「単なる元哉の直感なので根拠はない」, and Asumi voiced the feeling first [V03 C01 p0646–0649].
- Tanaka is not telling a big lie [V05 C03 p0403], and Kodera is speaking the truth [V05 C03 p0567].
- The drawings thief holds the key [V05 C03 p0736].
- Mizutani "hid something big". It proved to be what Nonaka had told him the day before the killing about why he had been expelled [V06 C03 p0499–0503, p0523–0533]. Asumi agrees at the time, 「吉川くんが言うとおりだと思う」 [V06 C02 p0253–0263].
- Yakushiji is not the killer [V06 C03 p0422–0423]; he was a concealer [V06 C03 p0735].
- Nagasaka is an unlikely culprit: 「犯人とは考えにくいな」 [V07 C03 p0662].
- Shiobara had no part in the killing: 「元哉は、塩原は河村重良殺しには関与していないことを確信した。」 [V07 C04 p0487].
- Contempt does not make Izumida a murderer [V07 C02 p0416].
- Eguchi's talk of justice is self-justification over money [V07 C04 p0638].
- Satomi's benefaction is not a 美談 but something "more human": right on the terms he accepted, namely Shiina's report (§5.2f).

**Mixed.** Shichijō's answers about Sekigawa sound to him like a model answer, 「教科書的な答えしか聞けなかった気がするんだ」 [V04 C02 p0710, p0788], and she was indeed concealing Yūko's parentage. In the same sentence he judges that she is not lying, yet she had lied about not meeting Sekigawa after her 1961 retirement: 「あれは偽りだった。」 [V04 C02 p0698, p0713; V04 C03 p0733]. He sensed the flatness and missed the lie, then disparaged his own instinct [V04 C02 p0796].

**Pattern (INF).** Motoya is good at detecting that someone is withholding something. His modelling of why people act is mixed. He is right when he distrusts a flattering explanation or refuses to infer murder from dislike, and when the hidden thing is a fact (Mizutani, Haruto; Shichijō only in part, since he sensed her rehearsed answers but judged that she was not lying). He is often wrong where the motive is love, shame, rivalry or attachment (Keiko, Miyazaki, Emiri). He is weakest with women whose warmth or hostility he takes personally, Asumi included. He can notice when a proposed explanation goes beyond the evidence while making similarly hopeful leaps about people he likes.

## 8. Discretion and the risk of being accommodating

**Keeping a witness's channel open.** Motoya can make a witness's channel safer by keeping a question small and leaving space to answer.
- He softens the hall manager with an idle question so that cooperation continues [V02 C01 p0461–0464].
- He offers to talk at Keiko's door, 「ここでもかまいませんよ」, because nobody wants the neighbours to know a detective has called [V03 C02 p0498–0502].
- He repeats his question to the first officer at the V05 scene because 「些細なことを言い忘れている場合もあるからだ」 [V05 C01 p0616–0618].
- He coaxes Yakushiji's housekeeper with flattery about her cooking, 「調子のよい声で」, until she gives the times he needs [V06 C03 p0274–0296].

In V02 he backs Asumi's sincerity before a resistant witness [V02 C02 p0095–0096]. He also joins the restaurant check Asumi proposes, which confirms Yura's visit but not its time [V02 C02 p0960, p0977–0979] (KCMP-V02-007).

**What may be said publicly.** His discipline about release is consistent.
- In V01 he is alarmed when Asumi shares an unreleased clue with Torii [V01 C02 p0645–0647]. When she names the murdered man to Torii, he notes that the fact is already public [V01 C02 p0619–0621].
- In V02 Ayane offers a trade: the name of a criminal in exchange for silence about her father's deafness. Asumi promises silence unless the investigation or a court requires otherwise, and Motoya joins: 「わたしも右に同じです」. His reasoning is about reprimand, not principle: since the Satomis are unlikely culprits, 「捜査本部に伝えなくてもおとがめを受けることはないだろう」 [V02 C03 p0463–0469]. The promise buys Ayane's information about Ishikawa's embezzlement and her 被害届, which she makes conditional on it; no complaint against Asumi is filed [V02 C03 p0474–0482, p0525] (KCMP-V02-002; KCMP-CHAR-013).
- In V04 he assures Shichijō, in textbook terms, that the police will never leak her account to the press [V04 C03 p0696–0699].
- In V05 he avoids talking shop with Sayaka at all, for fear of saying something he should not [V05 C01 p0053].
- In V06 he releases harmless facts at the cordon over Asumi's objection, reasoning that 「隠す意味がない情報はいくらでもある」 and that he may learn something from Emiri in return [V06 C01 p0417–0418]. It is a calculated release, not a leak.
- In V07 he tells Emiri only what the press release says [V07 C04 p0116–0123].

**Friends and the institution (V06).** His discretion toward people he likes is less disciplined than his discretion about information. When Masaki asks about the two women at the cordon, Motoya does three things [V06 C01 p0591–0607].
1. He passes on Emiri's account that she barely knew the victim [V06 C01 p0595].
2. He says that both women seem to have solid alibis, though he has only their own accounts: 「元哉は自信のある声を出すしかなかった。」 [V06 C01 p0599–0600]. When Masaki asks about checking, he insists the connection is too thin [V06 C01 p0603].
3. He keeps back Emiri's link to the V05 case, because raising it 「面倒なことになりそう」 [V06 C01 p0591].

Masaki cites limited manpower, accepts Motoya's insistence (「おまえがそこまで言うなら、とりあえず後回しだ」), and puts the check off; Motoya is grateful to be trusted [V06 C01 p0605–0606]. The resource reason is Masaki's. Motoya's own contribution is an overstated assurance, and the omission is a separate act with a separate basis, convenience (ADJ_C §5, sample A8). Emiri's dawn correction later shows how readily he had accepted her account of distance from Nonaka. Once she corrects it, he tells her it cannot be kept from Asumi or the headquarters [V06 C03 p0240]. He reports it, and Asumi stops speaking to him [V06 C03 p0264]. When she objects that Emiri went around her, his defence sounds to him like an excuse [V06 C03 p0315–0328].

**Candour as evidence.** He reads Yakushiji's open hatred of Nonaka, declared to detectives, as 「犯人ではない証拠のようなものだよ」. To Asumi's 「でも、芝居しているだけかもしれない」 he answers deadpan, 「だとしたら、薬師寺さんは役者になったほうがいい」 [V06 C03 p0466–0468]. He was right that Yakushiji had not killed. He was wrong to treat candour as proof, because Yakushiji was hiding what he had learned.

**V07 places the accommodation hypothesis under pressure.** He wants Emiri's night visit to have a personal purpose: 「もしかすると、英美里は自分に好意を持っているのかもしれない」 [V07 C04 p0076]. He still limits his answer to the authorized release [V07 C04 p0116–0123]. He does not let a fan's assurance exclude a witness, and he states what would count, an alibi [V07 C04 p0174–0184]. He keeps the meeting from Asumi because he anticipates friction [V07 C04 p0238–0240]. His private decision about whether to tell her has different consequences from his refusal to release case information. In the epilogue Sayaka, drunk, reports why Emiri could have given evidence and did not: Izumida had been with her that night, and she was too embarrassed to say so [V07 EPI p0114–0123]. Izumida's small bow and Asumi's laugh accept the account; nothing else corroborates it (KCMP-V07-012). His projection fails even though his immediate answer about what would count as evidence remains sound. On Sayaka's account, the alibi he asked for was exactly what Emiri was carrying and could not bring herself to give. When Sayaka tells it, he reconstructs Emiri's dilemma at once and adds, characteristically, 「恋人のアリバイ証言がどれほど信用されるかは別として」 [V07 EPI p0121].

**Summary (INF).** The risk is not that he leaks; he almost never does. It is that liking lowers his demand for verification about the person he likes, and that avoiding friction governs what he tells Asumi.

## 9. Speech and communication

This section covers register, self-reference, address in both directions, characteristic expressions, humour, silence, and the gap between his thought and his speech. His ordinary habits are in §11. **The arrival of 僕.** Every dialogue 僕 in V01–V04 belongs to another speaker. The one exception is a parody in V03, where Motoya mock-praises Asumi with 「僕はあの人の観察力、分析力、推理力に嫉妬していました」 [V03 C01 p1033]. 僕 arrives with Sayaka and Emiri in V05, on a day when the narration says 「今日の自分は不自然にいい子を演じている。」 [V05 C01 p0091]. It then spreads to light on-duty exchanges. His self-introduction to Nagasaka in V07 combines the official 吉川です with 僕 in one utterance (KCMP-CHAR-003). He never uses 僕 to Asumi. Two readings are possible, and the text does not decide between them (§16). One is a character-level self-presentation: a softer persona first adopted for women he wanted to please and then generalized. The other is simple exposure: the register was always available, but V01–V04 gave him few civilian peers to use it with.

### 9.2 Forms of address, in both directions, by interlocutor and over time

**Motoya to Asumi.**
- **小笠原** in speech, in every volume. It is also how he refers to her to others: 「は……小笠原とですか」 [V06 C01 p0224].
- **おまえ** is frequent in V01, where the KSOA-CB1 census counts about fourteen uses, and continues in V02 and V03 (four and three uses). Examples: 「おまえ、蘆名さんの小説読んだことあるのかよ」 [V01 C01 p0040]; 「うるせぇな。おまえだってオトコなんていないくせに」 [V01 C01 p0778]; 「おまえ、ほんと酒癖悪いな」 [V01 EPI p0017]; 「また、おまえかよ」 [V02 C01 p0227]; 「おまえ長生きするよ」 [V03 C01 p0619]; 「だって、おまえ、か弱くないじゃんか」 [V03 EPI p0058]. It is **absent in V04, used once in V05 in genuine anger** (「おい、小笠原、おまえ頭大丈夫か？」 [V05 C02 p0789]), and **absent in V06 and V07**. In those volumes every おまえ belongs to a superior addressing the pair (KCMP-CHAR-002). The text never comments on the change; possible causes are listed in §16.
- **デカチョウ**, sulking, 「わかりましたよ。デカチョウ」 [V01 C01 p0057]. **デカチョウどの**, mock-formal praise, 「今回の事件では、名探偵ぶりを遺憾なく発揮しましたな。デカチョウどの」 [V01 EPI p0029]. The ledger records this shift as a rank-based address that moves from sulking compliance to mock praise ([V01 VOICE-0002](../KSOA_CHARACTER_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v01-voice-0002)). Later, a mock 「承知しました。部長」 with a salute [V05 C03 p0417].
- **こいつ** before third parties: 「こいつは性格の悪い嘘つきだ、信じるな」 [V01 C02 p0095]; 「失礼ですが、こいつはもう三十路に手が届くんですがね」 [V04 C02 p0035]. And in thought: 「なんでこいつは上司面をするんだ」 [V05 C03 p0961]; 「こいつは本当に優秀な刑事だ。」 [V07 EPI p0147]. Contemptuous in the earlier uses, admiring in the last.
- **Never 亜澄 aloud.** The narration calls her 亜澄 throughout; that is the narrator's naming, not his speech.

**Asumi to Motoya.**
- **吉川くん**, in all volumes except V04.
- **キミ**, her teasing-senior address. Dialogue lines containing it run 11/10/5/1/0/0/2 by volume (KCMP-CHAR-001). Examples: 「勉強不足なんだよ、キミは」 [V01 C01 p0047]; 「あれぇ、キミに彼女なんていたっけぇ？」 [V01 C01 p0776]; 「ごあいさつね。あたしはキミの指導役なんだよ」 [V02 C01 p0236]; 「キミのこころは汚れておる」 [V02 C03 p0234]. It returns in V07: 「ここにキミと来るのは久し振りだね」 [V07 C01 p0012]; 「えっ、キミはいつ英美里さんと会ったのよ？」 [V07 EPI p0124].
- **元哉くん**, occasional: 「あたし別に元哉くんの教官じゃないんだから」 [V01 C01 p0584]; 「元哉くん、よだれ」 [V02 C01 p0327]; 「元哉くん、報国寺に来たことあるんだ？」 [V04 C01 p0046]. Her last direct use is the sharp 「元哉くんっ」 at the V05 cordon [V05 C01 p0313–0314]. In V06 she disputes Sayaka's right to the form [V06 EPI p0015].
- **あんた**, when she is very angry. Motoya knows the rule: 「亜澄が元哉をあんた呼ばわりするときは、相当に機嫌が悪いのだとわかっている。」 [V06 C01 p0652]. It occurs once in V01 (「いい？　吉川くん。あたしは主任だよ。あんたを指導する立場なんだよ」 [V01 C01 p0055]), seven times in the V05 café quarrel, and five times in V06. In V07 she uses it only to Sayaka (KCMP-V05-007; KCMP-V07-008).

**Motoya and juniors.** Plain authority to a young patrol officer: 「本部の捜査一課、吉川だ」 [V03 EPI p0093].

### 9.3 Register shifts with audience and pressure

- **Rough casual with Asumi.** すげぇ, うるせぇな, 痛ぇな, 〜じゃんか, 〜だぜ, and sentence-final よ, な and ぞ: 「すげぇ門だ……」 [V01 C01 p0007]; 「痛ぇな。で、次はどうするつもりなんだ？」 [V01 C02 p0737]; 「俺はなんでもいいぜ。牛丼チェーンでもラーメン屋でも」 [V06 C01 p0872].
- **Officialese when Asumi fumbles before the important.** As she simpers before the Gamō heirs, he takes over: 「わざわざのお言葉恐縮です。今後とも警察の活動にご理解とご協力をお願い致します」. The narration: 「仕方がないので元哉が役人臭い言葉を返した。」 [V01 C03 p0194–0195].
- **Textbook law for the anxious.** He cites the article, then promises that the police will never leak to the press; the narration twice calls the answers 教科書的 [V04 C03 p0690–0699].
- **Stammer and fragmentation under pressure:**
  - 「そ、捜査一課の吉川です」, before the beautiful Ayane [V02 C02 p0546];
  - 「じょ、冗談はやめてください」, when Ichiyō suggests Asumi as a love interest [V03 C03 p0070];
  - 「い、いや……そういうわけでは……」, to Emiri's "a date?" [V05 C01 p0099];
  - 「お、おいっ。この人はもしかして、一昨日《山辺茶寮》で会った塩原さんじゃないか」, on recognizing Shiobara [V07 C04 p0309];
  - the fragments to Masaki about his day off [V05 C01 p0674].
- **Spluttering in self-defence:** 「つばを飛ばして」 [V05 C02 p0747, p0812].
- **A politeness repair** before women he wants to please: 「ちょっと腹が減って……いえ、お腹が空いてきたところなんです」 [V05 C01 p0271].
- **Deadpan understatement:**
  - 「ああ、いつまでも待っていると蚊に喰われてつらいな」, at a dawn stake-out [V02 C03 p0550];
  - 「わかった。帰るから、逮捕しないでくれよ」, to the patrol officer who questions them in a park [V03 EPI p0110];
  - 「だとしたら、薬師寺さんは役者になったほうがいい」 [V06 C03 p0468].

### 9.4 Characteristic expressions

- 「俺はバカだっ」 [V01 C01 p0843]; 「俺はバカだ……」 [V03 EPI p0006; V04 EPI p0134]; 「俺はやっぱりバカだっ」 [V03 EPI p0125]. Self-reproach, murmured aloud, when Asumi has roped him in again.
- 「おいおい」, as an opener of objection [V01 C01 p0990; V01 C02 p0213; V04 C01 p0621; V04 EPI p0106; V07 C01 p0288].
- 「根拠はよ？」 [V04 C01 p0617] and 「根拠はないよな」 [V03 C02 p0252], used to test Asumi.
- 「わかったよ」, reluctant surrender [V02 C03 p0828; V07 C01 p0427; V07 C04 p0305].
- 「小笠原の勘に期待しよう」 [V04 C01 p0659] and 「小笠原の勘に従うよ」 [V07 C01 p0427].
- **Praise in a teasing register, sincere in content.** The デカチョウどの line comes after the narration says that 「今回は同じ刑事としてちょっと尊敬できる部分もあった」, and he adds 「俺はいいものは素直にいいって言うよ」 [V01 EPI p0026–0032]. Conversely, a compliment can sound sarcastic without his meaning it: 「へぇ、意外と教養あるんだな」, and 「からかうつもりはなかったが、皮肉に聞こえたかもしれない」 [V04 C01 p0035–0036].
- **Blunt, honest compliments:** 「小笠原は鎌倉のこと、ほんとによく知ってるな。すげーな」, with the narration's 「お世辞ではなく」 [V02 C02 p0485–0486]; 「ま、小笠原は頭いいからな」, followed at once by 「性格は悪いけどな」 [V02 C03 p0248–0252].
- **Appreciation of food:** 「こりゃあ美味いな」 [V01 C01 p1034]; 「美味いな……」 [V04 C01 p0732]; 「あの店はうまかったな」 [V07 C01 p0021].

### 9.5 Humour and silence

His humour is mostly deadpan or retaliatory. He tells Asumi she will live long [V03 C01 p0619]. His mock-praise of her as a genius backfires when she accepts it, 「本気にするか？　ふつう」 [V03 C01 p1033–1036]. Silence is a frequent choice:
- before Asumi's condescension [V01 C03 p0483];
- before a witness, when voicing doubt would be improper [V07 C01 p0406];
- before a grieving widow [V05 C02 p0382];
- before Ayane's denunciation of Shiina after his confession, 「なんと返事していいかわからず、元哉は黙って頭を下げた。」 [V02 C03 p1043];
- before Asumi's 「なんであたしに言わないのよ」 [V07 EPI p0128–0130].

### 9.6 Thought against speech

The four-layer analysis is in §7. For speech it implies three practical rules.
1. What he thinks about Asumi is sharper, and sometimes warmer, than what he says.
2. He usually swallows the sharpest line, and the narration marks the rare moments when speech and thought coincide (本音).
3. His hesitation is mostly interior. Those around him hear a calm or casual phrasing, or a deadpan joke.

The exceptions are anger at an attack on his integrity (§6.2), fluster before an attractive woman (§9.3), and what he says when drunk.

### 9.7 What the medium cannot show

There is no voice, accent or pacing. The ruby gives only the reading of his name. "Tone" is the narrator's word (あきれ声, 不機嫌な声, 弾んだ声, 冴えない声). All register analysis concerns written Japanese and narrated manner.

## 10. Relationships

Each subsection describes Motoya's own conduct in the relationship. Where the other person's conduct matters, it is given as he perceives it, since only his interior is narrated. The independent relational questions, and the civilian women's stakes in their own right, belong to the [partnership study](KSOA_PARTNERSHIP.md).

### 10.1 Motoya toward Asumi Ogasawara

**Baseline (V01).** He resents her rank and her manner. Her face is 「明るく愛らしい顔立ちとも言えるが、生意気この上ない女だ」, and she is 「残念ながら」 his partner [V01 C01 p0024–0025]. He wishes she could speak more sweetly [V01 C01 p0587]. Yet from the second chapter: 「亜澄の直感力のよさを実は評価している。」 [V01 C02 p0316].

**The private ledger.** By V05 he states it in full (INT): he grants that she is capable (「彼女が優秀であることは認める。」) and that, sharp-tempered as she is, she can be candid and sweet, but finds her constant condescension wearing and sums her up as 「苦手な女だ」 [V05 C01 p0026–0029]. A few lines later he credits her intuition and 「元哉には想像もつかない脳内回路の働き」, which her cheeky way of talking spoils [V05 C01 p0059].

The positive column grows across the series:
- 「亜澄はいつもどこか楽天的だ。そこが彼女のつよさになっているような気がする。」 [V02 C02 p0437];
- her friendliness and un-detective-like looks get them welcomed into houses, so perhaps he should thank her [V03 C01 p0296–0301];
- even he, who suffers most from her, cannot deny her excellent brain [V04 C01 p0043];
- her capacity for enthusiasm is her good point: 「このあたりが亜澄のいいところだ。」 [V06 C02 p0252];
- her intuition 「あなどれない」 [V06 C02 p0533];
- 「あらためて、亜澄はたのもしい女だなと元哉は思った。」 [V07 C04 p0655];
- 「こいつは本当に優秀な刑事だ。」 [V07 EPI p0147].

The negative column holds steady: her big-sister tone 「鼻につく」 [V07 C03 p0796–0797]; 「なんでこいつは上司面をするんだ」 [V05 C03 p0961]; 「なんでこういう可愛くない物言いをするのだろう。」 [V07 C04 p0304]. In V07 he even wishes she would take a lesson from Emiri's softness: 「彼女の爪の垢でも煎じて、亜澄に飲ませたいくらいだ。」 [V07 C04 p0216] (KCMP-V07-010).

**What he does toward her.**
- **Public backing.** He corroborates her test before Ayane [V02 C03 p0375] and gives supporting fire before Satake [V04 C03 p0374–0375].
- **Private objection.** The V05 café quarrel [V05 C02 p0846–0876].
- **Grudging caretaking** when she is drunk (§6.3).
- **Concealment** of his own private meetings, to avoid her displeasure [V07 C04 p0238–0240].
- **Occasional rescue.** He extracts her from a garrulous old man, 「小笠原、そろそろ」, because 「たまには助け船を出したほうがいいだろう」 [V02 C03 p0198–0199].
- **Defending her to himself.** When she is swept along by Shiina's charm, he thinks 「弁護するわけではないが、亜澄がやに下がっているせいではない」 [V02 C02 p0840].
- **Siding with her** against Sayaka's joke [V06 C01 p0507].
- **Giving her the credit**, silently and entirely, at the V07 arrest [V07 C04 p0645].

**The childhood debt.** In V01 he concedes it but caps it: 「たしかに恩はある。だが、それは一〇歳くらいまでの話に過ぎない。」 [V01 C01 p0830]. In V07, irritated by her big-sister tone, he thinks: 「小さい頃に弱かった元哉を亜澄がいじめっ子から守ってくれたことは忘れてはいない。」 He adds that he is no longer 《吉川紙店》's grandson, she is no longer 《かつらや》's daughter, and he is two years older [V07 C03 p0797–0800]. The debt has moved from leverage she uses against him to a memory he holds himself. The family past helps explain why her protective tone can feel intrusive to him as an adult.

**Where it ends.** In the last scene of the series Motoya has blacked out over drinks at Izumida's counter [V07 EPI p0131]. He comes to after eleven [V07 EPI p0132]. Izumida and Emiri are asleep. Asumi and Sayaka, both drunk, are making a truce and shake hands [V07 EPI p0135–0143]. Asumi says to Sayaka, with a serious face, about him in the third person: 「吉川くん、いいとこいっぱいあるんだよ。あたし、こいつがいるから捜査やれるんだ」 [V07 EPI p0144–0145]. Whether she knows he is awake is not stated. 「その言葉は元哉の心に染み入った。」 He thinks 「こいつは本当に優秀な刑事だ。何度感じ入ったことかわからない。」 and resolves 「これからも自分は亜澄をサポートしてゆこう。」. In the next instant he blacks out again [V07 EPI p0146–0149] (KCMP-SERIES-003; KCMP-CHAR-007; KCMP-V07-002).

The internal pledge supplies evidence of a changed way of describing his future role. It follows more than one occasion on which he has already supported her, so it cannot mark the first instance of care. It is private, made drunk, and immediately followed by a blackout. Whether he tells her, or acts on it sober, lies outside the seven novels. The arc runs from a resented assignment ("unfortunately", V01), through resignation (「宿命的なもの」, V06 [V06 C01 p0932]), to a chosen supporting role.

## 12. Romantic and intimate psychology

**Past relationships.** Several separate references imply several past relationships, which may overlap: a woman he dated about three years before V01, with whom he drove to the Shōnan coast [V01 C01 p0499]; 「その頃つきあっていた彼女」 at Enoshima [V03 C03 p0039]; an ex with whom he visited 報国寺 about ten years before V04 [V04 C01 p0048, p0055]; and 「連れの女性」 on the Enoshima エスカー [V07 C01 p0455]. Girlfriends have stopped speaking to him when call-outs kept spoiling dates, which he has 「何度も経験している」 [V05 C01 p0418]. An old wound from a woman has not healed in years [V05 C01 p0061]. Its event and content are never disclosed.

**Self-report.** Asked by Ichiyō whether a woman has ever taken over his whole heart: 「残念ながら、まだそういう女性には出会っていません」, and 「正直に答えるしかなかった」 [V03 C03 p0064–0067].

**What attracts him.** Asumi extracts his own list with a leading question: 「品がよくて、たおやかで、女らしくて、やさしそうで……」 and, fatally, 「そうそう、図々しさなんてカケラもなくて……」 [V02 C02 p0679–0684]. He is drawn to softness of speech: 「こうした英美里の物言いのやわらかさには、元哉は心惹かれる。」 [V07 C04 p0215]. He is also drawn to a hidden sadness. In the painted model of 『極楽寺逍遙』 he is caught by 「切ない思いを内包したような両の瞳」 [V03 C01 p0944]. Asumi teases him for being 「かわいそう好き」, which he denies [V03 C02 p0713–0716]. He admits to himself that he instantly thinks receptionists cute [V03 C02 p0153].

**Assessment.** No romantic desire, intention or sustained attraction toward Asumi is narrated in seven volumes. There is one disowned moment of physical pleasure and many qualified appraisals of her looks. His narrated romantic hopes in V05–V07 go to other women. Because his interior is visible throughout, this absence is affirmative counterevidence for his side of the relationship. On Asumi's side the question stays open (KCMP-CHAR-009). The reading most consistent with the text is a partnership coloured by a sibling-like history, 「姉さんぶってみたいのだ」 [V02 C01 p0294], in which romance is neither excluded nor pursued. His stammer at Ichiyō's suggestion is equally readable as embarrassment at being teased by an elder in front of a colleague (INF).

**Jealousy.** None is narrated on his side. He meets Asumi's admiration of handsome men with teasing only.

**Boundaries.** He keeps civilians, including women he hopes for, at arm's length on case information (§8). No intimate preferences beyond his "type" list are evidenced.

## 13. A focalizer with a job of his own: capabilities and limitations

**Weighting his contribution.** In every volume the decisive solution is Asumi's. Motoya's own moves are real but bounded, and he habitually rates them lower than his superiors do. The V04 direction proposal, the request to question Miyazaki and the V06 checks originate with him. The V04 car identification and the V07 photograph recognition were prompted by Asumi's finds. The ledger call in V02 broke a denial but did not solve the murder (KCMP-CHAR-008, resolved toward the KSOA-G1 weighting; KCMP-V04-015).

V04 provides a particularly clear test of the claim that he only accompanies Asumi. Earlier in the volume he thinks stating a wish to a 管理官 beyond him [V04 C01 p0885]. The contrast is between the evaluation of one case and his broader estimate of his own capacity.

**Professional capabilities, as demonstrated.**
- **Procedural and legal fluency.** He supplies the correct article or procedural limit, often for Asumi or a witness (§5.2b).
- **Interview craft.**
  - He repeats "anything you noticed?" because people forget details [V05 C01 p0616–0618].
  - He keeps quiet while a suspect is quiet: 「事情聴取では相手が黙っているときには、刑事も口をつぐんでいるべきである。」 [V04 C03 p0567].
  - He takes handwritten notes, knowing they put pressure on interviewees [V03 C01 p0332–0333].
  - He uses softening questions [V02 C01 p0461–0464] and flattery [V06 C03 p0274–0279].
  - He handles evasion without forcing it: sensing that Hashio is avoiding a question, he judges 「無理に喋らせるのは得策ではない」 [V07 C03 p0383–0384].
- **Reading demeanour.** Strong for detecting concealment, weaker for motive (§7.4).
- **A sense for records.** The hall's staff ledger [V02 C03 p0650–0667]; the passbook withdrawal [V04 C03 p0578]; the taxi company's times [V06 C03 p0303–0304].
- **Recognition, when prompted.** The silver Alto, once Asumi had stopped the frame [V04 C03 p0402–0410]; Shiobara, once Asumi had found the photograph and told him to look harder [V07 C04 p0302–0316].
- **Arrest and custody.** He calls the time while Asumi arrests Ishikawa [V02 C03 p0603–0608]. He makes the emergency arrest of Hani on Asumi's order and expects it to become a murder charge [V05 C03 p0959–0966]. He calls for patrol cars and gives Yakushiji a legal gloss [V06 C03 p0729–0735].
- **Initiative.** Clear in V02, V04 and V06 (§5.2e).
- **Credibility as a partner.** His supporting fire before Satake [V04 C03 p0374]; his vouching accepted by Masaki [V06 C01 p0605].

**Limitations, demonstrated or self-acknowledged.**
- **Assembling a case's structure.** He senses links but cannot see the shape [V05 C03 p0765–0767]; Asumi's leaps are 「元哉にはできない思考法だ」 [V01 C03 p0940].
- **Kamakura's geography and transport.** He is unfamiliar with the 横須賀線 side's transport [V02 C02 p0707–0708]; he is still weak on Kamakura's detailed geography [V05 C02 p0020]; he cannot find his way without Asumi [V06 C01 p0622–0625].
- **Cultural domains.** Literature [V01 C01 p0041]; classical music [V02 C02 p0344–0347]; painting (he does not know Ichiyō's name) [V03 C01 p0505–0510]; glass (Gallé and Argy-Rousseau mean nothing to him) [V06 C02 p0167]; high-end Japanese cuisine [V07 C02 p0166–0171].
- **Colour.** He cannot see the hedge difference [V03 C03 p0165].
- **Stating wishes to superiors.** 「元哉にはとてもできない業だ」 in V04's first chapter [V04 C01 p0885], overcome later in the same volume.
- **Physical.** Heat [V02 C03 p0293–0297]. No other physical limitation is shown. Asumi, not Motoya, is credited with judo and arrest technique [V03 EPI p0065–0067], and in the one arrest that turns into a scuffle his help is not needed: 「元哉が加勢するまでもなかった。」 [V02 C03 p0609]. No physical struggle involving him is narrated.
- **Interpersonal.** He misjudges motives rooted in love and shame (§7.4), and he has empathy gaps with Asumi's personal grievances, such as the boyfriend story that is 「元哉にとっては興味のない話だった」 [V03 EPI p0077].

**Capacity and willingness.** He has the legal knowledge to stop Asumi's bluffs, but never contradicts her in front of others. He whispers a correction [V01 C03 p0279] or frets inwardly [V01 C03 p0970–0972]; he recognizes her bluffs as bluffs [V01 C03 p0970; V03 C03 p0482; V04 C03 p0549; V06 C03 p0681] (KCMP-SERIES-006). He has the standing to refuse weekend summonses, but gives in. He has the means to report Emiri's V05 link, but chooses not to [V06 C01 p0591].

## 14. Development and late revelation

Each volume line separates **development** (a change in disposition, behaviour or self-positioning within narrated time) from **revelation** (a new fact about an existing condition) and from **stable** features.

**V01 稲村ヶ崎の落日.**
- *Baseline:* resentment of the pairing and of her rank; insults in thought; おまえ; conventional reasoning (suicide, prank); the institutional voice; a commoner's unease.
- *Development within the volume (INT):* he privately values her intuition [V01 C02 p0316]; he reconsiders and decides he should listen [V01 C02 p0221]. 「からむなよ。ここまで来たんだ。俺だってこの筋を追いかけたいよ」, and his 「刑事としての本能が少しだけ燃え始めた」 [V01 C02 p0728–0733]. For the first time he wants to recover the manuscript [V01 C03 p0124]. At the end comes open respect, 「今回は同じ刑事としてちょっと尊敬できる部分もあった」, and praise [V01 EPI p0026–0032]. There is also the disowned moment of pleasure [V01 EPI p0067].
- *Revelations:* family and shop, the schoolyard bullying, an ex-girlfriend, religion, reading habits.

**V02 由比ヶ浜協奏曲.**
- *Stable:* irritation; finding her hard going; おまえ.
- *Development:* small private upgrades of her (her optimism; "clever", said aloud, 「まんざらお世辞でもなく」 [V02 C03 p0249]); his first clear initiative, the ledger call; his first narrated attraction to a woman in a case (Ayane). At the end of the epilogue: 「亜澄などと二度と飲むかと思っていた」 [V02 EPI p0084].
- *Revelations:* lives in Yokohama; has aimed at detective work since joining; the Totoro DVD; subscription films; Asumi's ringtone is the 『ジョーズ』 theme [V02 C03 p0799].
- *Errors:* Satomi's pauses; accepting the "memory error"; Ayane's prize motive.
- *Against Asumi's reading:* his scepticism of the "beautiful story", concluded as atonement on Shiina's report [V02 C03 p0232 → p1001].

**V03 極楽寺逍遙.**
- *Development:* wordless coordination with Asumi [V03 C01 p0482]; he begins to enjoy the case [V03 C03 p0228]; empathy with an old man's love [V03 C03 p0137–0140]; and a professional judgment that his attraction may have coloured (Keiko), the most suggestive such case, by inference.
- *Revelations:* the car loan; the small apartment; police box to violent crime; "not yet met" a woman who ruled his heart; strong attachment to Hiratsuka; entrance-exam study.

**V04 竹寺の雪.**
- *Development:* おまえ disappears from his speech to Asumi; the cause is unstated. Within one volume he moves from "cannot state wishes to a 管理官" to proposing and leading Miyazaki's questioning [V04 C01 p0885 → C03 p0473–0598]. He gives public supporting fire for her outlandish idea [V04 C03 p0374–0375]. Privately he grants her some credit: 「少しくらいは功績を認めてやってもいい。」 [V04 EPI p0052–0054].
- *Revelations:* age thirty-one; the ex at 報国寺; "particular about coffee".
- *Errors:* the fraud motive; 「小笠原のロマンだろ。そりゃ」 [V04 C03 p0635].

**V05 西御門の館.**
- *Development:* his private life enters the narrative. Sayaka, the "good boy" self-presentation and 僕 appear. He objects to Asumi's conduct to her face ("abuse of authority" inwardly, "third-rate drama" aloud) and uses おまえ once in real anger. He becomes reflexive about his own bias. He makes an emergency arrest, on her order.
- *Revelations:* the old wound; girlfriends lost to call-outs; what sustains him in the work; the bar near home.
- *Errors:* Hani; misreading Asumi's silence.

**V06 佐助ヶ谷の銀雫.**
- *Development:* the pairing becomes "fated" in his own framing [V06 C01 p0932]. He shows protective discretion toward his civilian friends (the vouching and the omission), then reports Emiri's correction honestly despite the cost with Asumi. He notices his own rationalizing. He answers Asumi's renewed leak insinuation with argument rather than anger [V06 C01 p0650–0654] (INF). He escapes the Asumi–Sayaka quarrel.
- *Revelations:* the schools; the family precept of salaried work; the university course.
- *Correct readings:* Mizutani; Yakushiji; the departure timing.

**V07 長谷の花舞.**
- *Development:* role over wish with Emiri, 「残念ですが、僕は刑事です」 [V07 C04 p0189]. He credits Asumi with the whole solution [V07 C04 p0645] and thinks her dependable [V07 C04 p0655]. The bullying debt is now a memory he holds himself [V07 C03 p0798]. He makes the drunk private vow 「これからも自分は亜澄をサポートしてゆこう。」 [V07 EPI p0148]. The drunk coda is reversed: he is the one who blacks out.
- *Revelations:* the エスカー companion; his liking for sake.
- *Errors:* Emiri's intentions; doubting the 《雨月庵》 line.
- *Correct:* "contempt is not murder" for Izumida; caution about Eguchi; Shiobara uninvolved.

**What changed (supported).**
1. **The explicitness and weight of his credit to Asumi.** His private valuation of her intuition is present from V01, so his basic esteem is partly constant. What changes is its scope (from "intuition" to "the whole solution"), its frequency, and its consequence (a stated commitment to support her). This is development of self-positioning: from a resented pairing (V01), through fate (V06), to chosen support (V07). The V07 vow is private, made drunk and never voiced.
2. **Address.** おまえ fades after V03, with one angry use in V05. This is a change in sampled behaviour, without a stated cause.
3. **Separation of attraction from professional judgment (INF).** V03 (possibly fused, by inference), then V05 (he doubts his own bias), then V07 (explicitly separated). Three points, the first of them inferred: suggestive, not proven.
4. **Initiative and voice with superiors.** These appear strongly in V04 and continue in V06's self-directed checks. V07 shows plain obedience again: 「元哉は不満だったが、管理官の命令に反することはできない。」 [V07 C01 p0575].
5. **Response to attacks on his integrity.** Anger and おまえ in V05; irritated argument in V06 (INF, two points).

**What stayed the same (supported).** His commoner self-placement; swallowed retorts; doubt-then-yield toward Asumi's hunches; demeanour-based reading of people; susceptibility to gentle women; coffee and cheap food; conflict avoidance; and grudging caretaking, until V07's reversal.

**What only appears to change (revelation, not development).** His private life (V05) is new to the narrative, not to him: the old wound and the past girlfriends predate the series. 僕 may be a register that was always available to him and is only now shown, since V01–V04 never set him among civilian peers of his own age (§16).

**Development in Asumi, or revelation through Motoya?** Asumi's V07 statement of dependence is new to the reader. Whether it is new to her cannot be known, because her interior is never narrated, and it is said drunk to Sayaka. The narrated change is Motoya's response: 「その言葉は元哉の心に染み入った。」 [V07 EPI p0146].

## 15. Operational behavioural profile

This is a conditional interpretive summary built from the evidence above. It is not a reconstruction model, it has not been tested or validated, and it carries no reconstruction capability grade. Each rule gives the trigger, how he is likely to perceive and evaluate it, his likely response, the counterconditions, documented counterexamples, the support, and when to abstain. Support is **strong** for a pattern shown in three or more volumes, **moderate** for two volumes or one strong sequence, and **weak** for a single occasion or an inference. All rules assume his 2022–2023 situation: a headquarters detective paired with Asumi. His earlier life is unevidenced.

**R1. Asumi proposes a line of inquiry without a stated basis.**
- *Perceives:* the risk of wasted legwork, of looking foolish before the headquarters, and of her being right again.
- *Response:* asks for the basis (「根拠はよ？」 [V04 C01 p0617]), voices one structural objection (an old motive, a thin link, someone else's turf), then yields with a stock phrase (「わかったよ」, 「小笠原の勘に期待しよう」 or 「小笠原の勘に従うよ」 [V07 C01 p0427; V04 C01 p0659]) and follows through fully. Privately he recalls her past successes.
- *Counterconditions:* a direct order from a 管理官 settles it either way.
- *Counterexamples:* he never refuses outright on duty; sometimes he agrees at once (「俺もそう思う」 [V02 C01 p0920]).
- *Support:* strong (V01, V03, V04, V06, V07).
- *Abstain:* from predicting whether he is right. Against a line of inquiry she wanted to pursue he has always proved wrong; against her readings of motive he has sometimes been right (V02, on his own terms; V07).

**R2. Asumi bluffs, deceives or questions aggressively in front of others.**
- *Perceives:* risks to legality, admissibility and their reputation.
- *Response:* inner alarm (ハラハラ, sweat, a jiggling leg he suppresses); at most a whispered correction or a tug at her sleeve; no public contradiction; any objection voiced later and in private. He identifies the bluff as a bluff in his own mind.
- *Counterconditions:* the presence of witnesses keeps him quiet.
- *Counterexample:* when her questioning touches his private life (Emiri's alibi; "from whose home did you set out?"), he objects strongly afterwards and thinks 「職権濫用ではないか」 [V05 C02 p0674, p0850–0866].
- *Support:* strong.
- *Abstain:* where she breaks the law outright. The novels never show it, so his limit there is unknown.

**R3. Asumi teases or condescends to him.**
- *Perceives:* a threat to face; rank resentment.
- *Response:* a short jab back (「小笠原に言われたくないわ」 [V04 C01 p0031]), or silence because anger would only entertain her. His judgment in thought is harsher. Sometimes he teases her back about her weakness for handsome men.
- *Counterconditions:* a superior or a witness present.
- *Counterexample:* an insinuation that he leaked case information to a civilian, which attacks his integrity rather than his pride, produced real anger and おまえ in V05 [V05 C02 p0788–0789] and reasoned irritation in V06 [V06 C01 p0650–0654].
- *Support:* strong.
- *Abstain:* from exact wording beyond the documented repertoire.

**R4. A superior gives him an order he dislikes, or teases him about the pairing.**
- *Perceives:* hierarchy and career prudence.
- *Response:* feigned compliance (「いえ、了解しました」 [V01 C02 p0037]; 「は……小笠原とですか」 [V06 C01 p0224]), tongue-tied protest at most, then obedience. He grumbles privately and manages up by voicing what the superior thinks.
- *Counterconditions:* none observed.
- *Counterexample:* one strong initiative, proposing to question Miyazaki to Satake [V04 C03 p0473], made once the evidence was in hand.
- *Support:* strong for obedience; moderate for initiative when facts are in hand.
- *Abstain:* from predicting promotion behaviour or open confrontation with a superior. Neither is shown.

**R5. A witness or suspect is distressed or under suspicion.**
- *Perceives:* cooperation and decency.
- *Response:* courteous, softening questions; banal but sincere consolation; silence before tears; procedural gentleness in custody (letting a suspect brief the household); bluntness only with a suspect already exposed (「ごまかそうたって無駄なんだよ」 [V02 C03 p0665]).
- *Counterconditions:* a performance he finds convincing lowers his suspicion.
- *Counterexample:* he finds alibi questions distasteful but asks or attends them: 「訊かれるほうはもっと嫌だろうが、訊くほうの刑事だって嫌なのだ。」 [V07 C03 p0433].
- *Support:* strong.
- *Abstain:* from violent confrontation. He makes arrests, but no physical struggle involving him is narrated.

**R6. A person's demeanour seems open, earnest or likeable.**
- *Perceives:* someone to trust.
- *Response:* judges them truthful or "not the type", often aloud to Asumi ("too small-hearted to kill"; open hatred proves innocence; 「実に謙虚な人だな」 [V07 C02 p0412]). He is good at sensing an omission.
- *Counterconditions:* Asumi pushes to check anyway.
- *Counterexamples:* he was wrong about the charming Hani [V05 C02 p0370] and the attractive Keiko [V03 C02 p0650]; right about Haruto, Tanaka, Kodera, Mizutani, Yakushiji (as non-killer), Nagasaka, Shiobara and Izumida; half right about Shichijō, whose answers he found rehearsed but whom he judged not to be lying [V04 C02 p0788; V04 C03 p0733].
- *Support:* strong for the tendency; moderate for accuracy on concealment; mixed on motive. He is right to distrust flattering or dislike-based explanations, and often wrong where love, shame or attraction is involved.
- *Abstain:* from treating his judgment as evidence of innocence.

**R7. An attractive, gentle or soft-spoken woman shows him attention.**
- *Perceives:* hope, and a chance to present himself as a "good boy".
- *Response:* a quickened heart, a stammer, a softer register (僕, です/ます, 「お腹が空いて」 [V05 C01 p0271]); he offers help beyond need, defends her against Asumi and quickly builds hope ("maybe she likes me"). If the hope fails: burning shame and silent sarcasm, with outward compliance (he claps).
- *Counterconditions:* his professional role (V07: 「残念ですが、僕は刑事です」 [V07 C04 p0189]); the old wound (he keeps some distance from Sayaka); fear of Asumi's displeasure (he conceals meetings).
- *Counterexamples:* Ayane's coldness cools him quickly [V02 EPI p0030–0032]; he sides with Asumi when Sayaka is unkind [V06 C01 p0507].
- *Support:* strong (V02–V07).
- *Abstain:* from predicting that any such hope succeeds; none does in seven volumes.

**R8. A civilian friend asks for case information.**
- *Perceives:* the risk of a leak, against the friend's feelings.
- *Response:* deflects (「さあね」 [V07 C04 p0103]), cites the press release, gives only released facts, and withholds the case even from friends.
- *Counterconditions:* none observed.
- *Counterexample:* he shares harmless facts to draw more out [V06 C01 p0417–0422], a calculated exception made over Asumi's objection.
- *Support:* strong (V01, V05–V07). The discipline is already present in V01, when he is alarmed that Asumi shares an unreleased clue with Torii [V01 C02 p0645–0647].
- *Abstain:* no notable zone.

**R9. Information from a friend could cause friction with the institution or with Asumi.**
- *Perceives:* friction, and the friend's exposure.
- *Response:* withholds it from the party who would make trouble (from Masaki [V06 C01 p0591]; from Asumi [V07 C04 p0238]). He reports evidential content honestly when it matters [V06 C03 p0240, p0264]. When caught, he tells the truth briefly and then goes silent.
- *Counterconditions:* a direct question.
- *Counterexamples:* none.
- *Support:* moderate (V06–V07).
- *Abstain:* from high-stakes concealment, such as hiding a suspect. Nothing of that order is shown.

**R10. Asumi is drunk, quarrelsome or self-pitying off duty.**
- *Perceives:* embarrassment, the neighbours, her safety and reputation, and his own accountability.
- *Response:* tells her to keep her voice down; answers her grievances literally ("you're not frail") or with indifference; considers leaving; stays and arranges her way home once he is accountable (badge shown, neighbours or volunteers watching); calls himself an idiot.
- *Counterconditions:* a third party present.
- *Counterexamples:* V06, he escapes the bar; V07, he is the drunk one. Tenderness appears only once, and he disowns it [V01 EPI p0067].
- *Support:* strong (V01–V04).
- *Abstain:* from predicting emotional support. He does not provide it in the record.

**R11. He is offered money, a reward or a favour.**
- *Perceives:* public-servant integrity.
- *Response:* a polite, firm refusal citing his status.
- *Counterconditions:* none.
- *Counterexamples:* none. He reasoned that unpaid help with Sayaka's interview was permissible.
- *Support:* moderate (V01 twice; the V05 reasoning).
- *Abstain:* no notable zone.

**R12. He receives praise or credit.**
- *Perceives:* a call for modesty, and his rank.
- *Response:* deflects ("hardly elite"; "I only tagged along"), credits Asumi, and disparages his own intuition, even straight after it has partly worked [V04 C02 p0788–0796].
- *Counterconditions:* none.
- *Counterexample:* 「俺はいいものは素直にいいって言うよ」 [V01 EPI p0032]. He gives praise readily when he thinks it deserved.
- *Support:* strong.
- *Abstain:* no notable zone.

**R13. Food choice.**
- *Response:* defers to Asumi; asks for something cheap and filling; enjoys and appraises what comes; orders coffee and judges it; avoids luxury prices.
- *Support:* strong.
- *Abstain:* from specific dishes beyond those listed.

**R14. Rank, his failed examination, or Asumi's "leader" claim comes up.**
- *Perceives:* wounded pride.
- *Response:* concedes the fact silently or with an inward tongue-click; declines to correct inaccuracies because doing so would be childish; resents her 上司面 inwardly.
- *Support:* strong.
- *Abstain:* from whether he will sit the examination again. It is never addressed.

**R15. An interviewee evades or lies.**
- *Perceives:* the truth at stake, against continued cooperation and his own read of the person.
- *Response:*
  - Without facts in hand, he notices the evasion and does not force it [V07 C03 p0383–0384]. He reports his read to Asumi afterwards [V06 C02 p0253].
  - With documents in hand, he confronts bluntly [V02 C03 p0656–0665].
  - Otherwise he uses a calm truth-seeking register and keeps silent while the other is silent [V04 C03 p0537–0541, p0567].
  - To a liked civilian who confesses having lied to the police, he offers reassurance and a legal gloss: not perjury, not 犯人隠避 [V06 C03 p0244].
- *Counterconditions:* liking and hope. He read Emiri's night visit as possible interest in him [V07 C04 p0076]; by Sayaka's later report it concealed her role as Izumida's alibi [V07 EPI p0114–0123]. Charm also misleads him (Hani).

- *Support:* moderate (V02, V04, V06, V07).
- *Abstain:* from interviews held under a superior's direct observation. No such scene exists, so his behaviour there is untested.

**R16. He has made a judgment that later proves partial or wrong.**
- *Perceives:* embarrassment, sometimes shame.
- *Response:* small errors he corrects silently. Errors bound up with his own liking or hope produce self-questioning (「自分の判断が間違っているのだろうか」 [V05 C02 p0876]; 「言い訳めいているな」 [V06 C03 p0318]) or a burst of shame (V07 epilogue). He does not announce corrections to Asumi.
- *Support:* moderate (V05–V07).
- *Abstain:* from predicting a sober, spoken self-criticism to Asumi; none occurs.

**General abstention zones.**
- His family life.
- His conduct in physical danger, never shown at length.
- His sexuality beyond the attractions listed.
- Any sober, explicit conversation about his relationship with Asumi, which never occurs.
- His behaviour before 2022 or after August 2023.

**Choosing speech after behaviour.** When rendering him, choose the action family first, then the register:
- 俺, 小笠原 and plain speech with Asumi, with おまえ only before V04 or in anger;
- わたし and です/ます with witnesses and superiors;
- 僕 and a soft polite register with women he likes and some civilians, from V05 on;
- blunt plain speech with exposed suspects.

Keep his thought harsher, and sometimes kinder, than his speech, and let him swallow the sharpest line.

## 16. Interpretive uncertainty

1. **Why おまえ disappears from V04 on.** Candidates: growing respect for Asumi, consistent with his rising private credit; a move toward mature restraint; or authorial register drift. No text marks the change. Unresolved.
2. **Whether 僕 is development or exposure.** See §9.1 and §14. Unresolved.
3. **The "old wound."** Its event, the partner involved and any effect beyond "keeping some distance" are never disclosed [V05 C01 p0061]. Connecting it to any of the named past girlfriends would be speculation (SPEC).
4. **Romance with Asumi.** Two readings.
   - *Suppressed attraction.* Evidence: the V01 embrace [V01 EPI p0067], the stammer at Ichiyō's suggestion [V03 C03 p0070], the repeated "cute, if…" appraisals, and the V07 vow. Counterevidence: his narrated hopes repeatedly go elsewhere; he finds the "couple play" idea nightmarish; and the narration, with free access to his interior, never voices desire for her.
   - *Sibling-like partnership.* Evidence: 「姉さんぶってみたいのだ」 [V02 C01 p0294], the childhood debt, and the professional framing of the vow ("support"). This reading is better supported, but the source keeps the question open.
5. **The weight of the V07 vow.** It is made drunk and privately, after he comes to from one blackout and just before another [V07 EPI p0131–0149]. Asumi's statement that prompts it is also drunk and addressed to Sayaka, and whether she knew he was awake is unstated (KCMP-SERIES-003). The serious face and the content support sincerity in the moment. Whether either commitment holds sober is untested; the series ends there.
6. **Poor or comfortable?** "Grew up comfortably" [V03 C03 p0055] against "glad to be a poor man's child" [V04 C03 p1119]. Both are situational. The stable element is his sense of relative inferiority to the people in the cases.
7. **His V01 claim that he came for Asumi's sake, "not because of the threat"** [V01 C01 p0841–0842]. It may be self-justification; the text gives no outside check.
8. **His accuracy about people.** His judgments of concealment are often right; his judgments of motive are mixed (§7.4). The split is suggestive, not statistical. The best-known case of him being right against Asumi, the V02 atonement, is right only on Shiina's uncorroborated report (KCMP-V02-011).
9. **What Asumi's hostility to Sayaka and Emiri means to him.** The narration's recap states that she has suspected 「元哉と沙也香の仲」 since V05 [V06 C01 p0297]. Asumi gives dislike as her reason, and he accepts it [V06 C03 p0333–0334]. He sees the rivalry as a puzzle about the two women, 「この二人はどうしてこんなに折り合いが悪いのだろう」 [V06 C01 p0416]. The text never shows him asking what her suspicion implies about her feelings for him, though it offers repeated opportunities (V05–V07). That unasked question is itself evidence about his obtuseness in this one area (INF). Jealousy on her side remains a reader's hypothesis (KCMP-V06-006).
10. **The V03 camera thought.** That his attraction to Keiko steered his passing judgment that a camera check was unnecessary is inference only [V03 C02 p0648–0650].
11. **Speaker anomaly.** At [V05 C02 p0862–0863] the line 「そんな三文ドラマみたいなことがあるわけないだろ」 is followed by the tag 「亜澄はあきれた声を出した」, though context gives the line to Motoya. This portrait attributes it to Motoya.
12. **Gaps.** No family scene; no stated career plan; no physical description; no religion practised; no hobbies beyond viewing and walking; and his drinking capacity is inconsistent (V03 against V07).
