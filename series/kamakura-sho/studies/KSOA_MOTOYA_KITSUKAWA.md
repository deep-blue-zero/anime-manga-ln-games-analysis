# Motoya Kitsukawa

吉川元哉, with the witness ruby `きつかわ もとや`, is a prefectural investigator and 巡査長. The novels frequently place the reader inside the subordinate partner’s assessment of the person who leads their joint work. He can notice when a proposed explanation goes beyond the evidence while making similarly hopeful leaps about people he likes. His own work has a firmer basis when it takes the form of locating a record, asking a concrete follow-up or marking what can be said publicly. The distance between his visible competence and his sense of his usefulness is part of the series’ development. ## A focalizer with a job of his own

The first house makes him attentive to wealth and to his place in the police hierarchy. His injured pride can make an invitation from Asumi sound like condescension, and the same chapter gives him independent questions about Ashina’s health. Later he catches a connection in Torii’s article while Asumi and the editor attend to publication. In V02 he verifies workplace and personnel material and gives the arrest time. He is willing to live with an unresolved security anomaly after the first institutional closure, a case where Asumi’s persistence is corrective. In V03 his eye goes to spending, class and the market where Asumi sees differences in paint. He also helps record and present the evidence and privately questions the pressure applied to witnesses. These scenes give him defined contributions with different stakes. [V01 STATE-0001 and STATE-0003](../KSOA_CHARACTER_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v01-state-0001) · [V01 byline and place](../readings/KSOA_V01_DEEP_READING.md#the-town-as-a-way-of-reading) · [V02 CH-0005](../KSOA_CHARACTER_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v02-ch-0005) · [V03 CH-0002–CH-0004](../KSOA_CHARACTER_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v03-ch-0002).

V04 provides a particularly clear test of the claim that he only accompanies Asumi. She identifies a plausible travel lead and he proposes checking the other direction for a clearer view. He asks to lead a later questioning and credits what the footage can support without a readable plate. In V05, outside duty, he deliberately asks basic questions on behalf of readers unfamiliar with Emiri’s craft and asks that his name stay out of the piece. Once ordered into the investigation, he follows up on what the first team learned and articulates why he trusts Asumi’s estimate of the medical examiner. His resentment at losing his private time explains the mood without erasing those acts. [V04 CH-0004](../KSOA_CHARACTER_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v04-ch-0004) · [V05 CH-0001–CH-0002](../KSOA_CHARACTER_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v05-ch-0001).

In V06 he recognizes an omission about Mizutani before either detective can specify it. His call with Kosugi and check with the taxi firm produce the limits of the party evidence. Asumi uses those findings to confront the witness whose account is decisive. Motoya contacts headquarters after the confrontation and interprets Yakushiji’s possible responsibility as a distinct post-event matter. In V07 he accepts Asumi’s restaurant line while repeatedly asking for a bridge between grievance and action. At the photograph comparison she has found it already and he independently recognizes the face when prompted. Yoshida praises both, while Motoya judges his contribution smaller. The contrast is between the evaluation of one case and his broader estimate of his own capacity. ## Discretion and the risk of being accommodating

Motoya can make a witness’s channel safer by keeping a question small and leaving space to answer. In V02 he supports a credible apology and tests what a restaurant worker can actually corroborate. In V05 his interview for Sayaka models an ability to ask ordinary questions without pretending expertise. In V06 he gives a qualified fact about the scene when Sayaka challenges the police’s asymmetrical access. His later recommendation to defer independent checks follows a resource judgment, but his omission of Emiri’s peripheral connection to the previous case is another act with another basis. Her correction reveals how readily he accepted her account of distance from Nonaka. His favorable assessment of Yakushiji’s candor is also vulnerable to Asumi’s suggestion that the man could be performing it. [V02 ACT-0002](../KSOA_CASE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v02-act-0002) · [V05 CH-0001](../KSOA_CHARACTER_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v05-ch-0001) · [V06 CH-0001 and CH-0004](../KSOA_CHARACTER_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v06-ch-0001).

V07 places the accommodation hypothesis under pressure. He wants the meeting with Emiri to have a personal purpose. He still limits his answer to the authorized release, does not let a fan’s assurance exclude a witness and asks for a corroborator. He keeps the meeting from Asumi because he anticipates conflict. His private decision about whether to report the encounter has different consequences from his refusal to release case information. The epilogue discloses why Emiri could have given evidence and did not. His projection fails even though his immediate answer about what would count as evidence remains sound. ## Registers, habits and the final resolve

Motoya’s speech tracks audience. At the initial scene he follows formal identification, later using rank as a mock honorific with Asumi. His ordinary talk with her can use `俺` and surname address. In V07 his official introduction uses `吉川です`, the coffee conversation with Nagasaka uses `僕`, and his private meeting has both courteous and more familiar turns. His hesitation often belongs to his internal response. Source-grounded characterization needs to distinguish that hesitation from the calm phrasing someone else hears. [V01 VOICE-0002](../KSOA_CHARACTER_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md#ksoa-v01-voice-0002) · [V07 C03 p[0481]–p[0506] and C04 p[0095]–p[0126]](../readings/KSOA_V07_DEEP_READING.md).

The ordinary scenes give him preferences and thresholds beyond case work. The familiar food he asks for when tired in V06 differs from what Asumi can find in the neighborhood. He develops a taste for a new combination at the V07 counter and can put attention on Izumida’s practiced competence even after the difficulty of the interview. His family narrative in V06 explains why salaried work feels more secure than running a small business, with his parents’ choices as well as the grandparents’ shop visible in that decision. The family past also helps explain why Asumi’s protector tone can feel intrusive as an adult. [V06 C01 p[0870]–p[0899] and C02 p[0509]–p[0515]](../readings/KSOA_V06_DEEP_READING.md) · [V07 EPI p[0033]–p[0068]](../readings/KSOA_V07_DEEP_READING.md).

Across the earlier epilogues his care for Asumi has several constraints. He can stay after local officers intervene while citing what being identified makes him responsible for. At the finale, he makes the decision internally after hearing her explicit appreciation. The internal pledge supplies evidence of a changed way of describing his future role. It follows more than one occasion on which he has already provided support, so it cannot mark the first instance of care. Whether he tells her or acts on the pledge later is outside the seven novels.
