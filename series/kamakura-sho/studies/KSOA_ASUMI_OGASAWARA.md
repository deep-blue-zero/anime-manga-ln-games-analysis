# Asumi Ogasawara

小笠原亜澄 (おがさわら あすみ) is a 巡査部長 in the violent-crimes unit (強行犯係) of the Kamakura station's criminal section. Her local position and rank give her authority in a pairing with an older childhood acquaintance from prefectural headquarters, Motoya Kitsukawa (吉川元哉). The series also shows that other officers, witnesses and experts can grant, deny or extend the authority she needs. Her investigative character is legible in how she makes a neglected discrepancy available to those people, and in what happens when her familiarity with another person, or her dislike of one, impedes that effort. Outside the prologues, the narration travels through Motoya. This portrait therefore keeps her acts, her explanations and his readings of her distinct throughout.

This is version 2.0, a general revision written to the self-contained character monograph standard. It integrates the earlier KSOA-G1 study (v1.0) with the fuller coverage of the independent KSOA-CB1 baseline and applies every adjudicated correction to either. A reader should be able to understand Asumi from this document alone. The links and locators exist to verify claims; they do not carry the portrait. The [revision record](#revision-record-v20) lists what changed and why.

## Orientation

- **Who.** 小笠原亜澄, 巡査部長 in the 強行犯係 of the 刑事課 at 鎌倉警察署. She is 28 in 2022 and 29 in 2023. She transferred to Kamakura in spring 2022 after a posting at prefectural headquarters (本部) and earlier service as a theft-squad (盗犯係) detective at 厚木署.
- **Where from.** She grew up in the station-front shopping street of 平塚 as the only daughter (一人娘) of the kimono shop 《かつらや》. The shop is three generations old and her father still runs it; he is also well known as a maker of cosplay costumes. Her mother died of illness while Asumi was a student.
- **The partnership.** Whenever a joint investigation headquarters is set up at Kamakura, she is paired with Motoya. He is two school years older, a 巡査長 in the prefecture's First Investigation Division, and he failed the sergeant examination she passed when they sat it together. As a small girl she protected him from a bully. In V01–V02 she uses that debt to recruit him on his days off for lines the inquiry had closed; the bully card is not played after V02, and when she next needs him off duty (V05) she simply insists. She invites him to a case-end celebration from V01 on, in every volume except V05. She leads, teaches, teases and relies on him. In the V07 epilogue, drunk and speaking to Sayaka Takigawa about him, she says she can do investigations because he is there.
- **How we know her.** Only from outside. She never narrates and her thoughts are never rendered. Her motives reach the reader through her own words, through what she does, and through Motoya's guesses and judgments, which are often uncertain and sometimes wrong.
- **Professional signature.** She fixes on anomalies others have stopped seeing, admits openly when a hunch has "no basis", and pursues it anyway, often past official closure. She turns the hunch into something others can test or grant: a prediction about camera footage, a colour comparison, a document obtained off the page, a request framed as a gap in what is known. She stages the disclosure at a gathering or confrontation. She bluffs about evidence (the narration labels bluffs in V01, V03, V04 and V06) and presses liars hard. She states her limits: nothing illegal, no perjury, no publication of what an investigation uncovers. Promised discretion is a weaker limit: in several volumes she later uses what she was told in confidence.
- **Reception.** Her authority is not self-executing. Commanders sometimes back her lines, sometimes ignore them, and in V07 adopt both her proposals. Witnesses and experts decide whether her questions become evidence.
- **Manner and speech.** Her emotions show plainly: she cries at stories, flushes, trembles and bares her teeth. She shifts register fluently: あたし and plain bossy speech with Motoya, わたし and deferential です/ます with witnesses, わたくし on formal calls, a sugary elongated register with handsome men and flattering elders in V01–V03, and plain harsh speech with lying suspects. She calls Motoya キミ (often in V01–V03 and again in V07), 吉川くん, occasionally 元哉くん, and あんた when angry (V05–V06).
- **Off duty.** She is a Kamakura enthusiast and an unpaid guide to its history, paths and eateries, and she chooses where to eat in every volume. She drinks coffee and wine. In six of the seven volumes she drinks heavily at the case-end celebration, usually becomes quarrelsome, and in five of those evenings laments men or her own lot.
- **Romance.** She has no partner in the series and mentions one relationship from her student days, told while drunk. In V01–V03 the narration presents her as weak for handsome men. From V04 she reacts against Motoya's interest in other women: mildly in V04, with open hostility toward the writer Sayaka Takigawa (滝川沙也香) in V05–V07, and in a milder, partly investigative way toward the sea-glass artist Emiri Hatano (波多野英美里). The feud with Sayaka ends in a drunken truce. Whether Asumi has romantic feelings for Motoya is open at the end of the series.
- **Unresolved.** In V01 she says she has a fear of hospitals and promises to explain it "someday". She never does.

## Boundaries, evidence and the perspective problem

### Source, continuity and time

The portrait covers the seven accepted Japanese EPUB witnesses of KSOA-G1, through V07 EPI p[0151]. There is one continuity and one Asumi. Story time runs from V01 稲村ヶ崎の落日 (May–June 2022) to V07 長谷の花舞 (31 July to 5 August; the volume states no year, and the series sequence places it in summer 2023). The portrait discusses every solution and the final epilogue. Where a fact is disclosed late, its disclosure point is stated with it; a fact that first appears in a later volume is never projected back into an earlier scene as something a character knew then.

Locators take the form `[Vnn SEC pNNNN]`, where SEC is PRO, C01–C04 or EPI and pNNNN is the paragraph ordinal of the witness. Japanese quotations are short and were checked against those lines. English glosses lose register: あたし, わたし and わたくし are all "I", and キミ, あんた, 吉川くん and 元哉くん are all "you" or a name.

**Reading of 吉川.** In this series Motoya's surname is read きつかわ, as the author's ruby shows at its first appearance, ｜吉《きつ》｜川《かわ》 [V01 C01 p0008], and again at [V05 C01 p0010]. Asumi's 吉川くん is therefore "Kitsukawa-kun", never "Yoshikawa-kun".

### Evidence classes

Every interpretive statement below is tied to one of these classes:

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| observed | A represented act or utterance of Asumi's |
| self-report | Asumi's own statement about herself; sober and drunk self-reports are kept apart |
| Motoya's view | Motoya's narrated perception, inference, memory or judgment about her |
| narration | A narratorial statement without a named perceiver; in this series it always sits within Motoya's knowledge horizon |
| testimony | Another character's statement about her |
| inference | This portrait's analytical proposition, graded *supported*, *tentative* or *speculative* |
| unknown | The source gives no evidence, or gives an opportunity and leaves the question open |

### What the narration lets us know

**The focalization rule.** Outside the reader-only prologues, every chapter is narrated in close third person through Motoya, with one exception: the opening scene of V02 C01, a concert at which the concertmaster dies, is focalized through the station chief Takanashi Hideo (高梨秀夫) [V02 C01 p0014–0170], and Asumi does not appear in it. No passage in any volume is focalized through Asumi. A search of all seven volumes for constructions that would render her thought from inside (亜澄は思った, 亜澄の胸, 亜澄の心, 亜澄は考えた, 亜澄は感じた) finds none. The rule is also a device of the mystery form: her reasoning is withheld from the reader until she discloses it (KCMP-SERIES-008).

**Opacity is marked.** Motoya often cannot tell why she does something, and the text says so. 「なぜか亜澄は」 recurs [V01 C01 p0713; V05 C01 p0427; V05 C02 p0620; V05 EPI p0025], and after an unexplained grin 「元哉には亜澄の感情が掴めなかった」 [V06 C01 p0070]. Hedges such as らしい, ようだ and のだろう attach to her states in every volume.

**Motoya's confident readings.** Several times the narration states her intention or feeling without a hedge:
- 「亜澄はＵＢＡＧＡＹＡについて触れるつもりだ」 [V01 C02 p0644];
- 「最初から亜澄はこの場で証拠にできる言葉を得ようとは思っていないのだ」 [V01 C03 p0612];
- 「亜澄はわざとふたりを挑発している」 [V01 C03 p0926];
- 「亜澄は隆鄕の許可を得ようとしている」 [V01 C03 p1024];
- 「姉さんぶってみたいのだ」 [V02 C01 p0294];
- 「自分の考えが突飛だということがわかっているのだ」 [V04 C03 p0327];
- 「亜澄は本音で言っている」 [V04 C03 p0923];
- 「亜澄は沙也香や英美里に対する敵意を内心に抱え込んでいたのだ」 [V05 C02 p0844];
- 「亜澄は元哉と沙也香の仲を疑い」, in a recap paragraph [V06 C01 p0297].

These are Motoya's inferences in free indirect form, not access to her mind, and this portrait classes them uniformly as **Motoya's view**. The V05 instance shows the risk: the line before it admits that he had misread her the previous day, 「勘違いしていた」 [V05 C02 p0843].

**Narratorial trait statements.** The narration also states some traits as facts: she has a good memory [V01 C02 p0116; V03 C01 p0327]; 「基本的に亜澄はのんきだ」 [V03 C02 p0461]; 「亜澄はイケメンに弱い」 [V01 C01 p0899]; her drinking habits are remarkably bad [V04 EPI p0031]; she very much likes cafés where the customer chooses the beans [V07 C03 p0481]. All of these are things a childhood acquaintance could know, and they read as his knowledge and judgment.

**Motoya as evaluator.** Motoya is reliable about what happened and fallible about why. The cases prove him wrong repeatedly: the prank theory he offers in V01 [V01 C01 p0726–0731], and the zero chance he gives the eventual culprit Hani in V05 [V05 C02 p0370]. His evaluations of Asumi carry his own measures. He wishes she would speak "more cutely" [V01 C01 p0587]; he judges her open emotions charming in a woman and a failing in a detective [V01 C02 p0416]; by V07 he thinks she would be cute if she kept quiet, 「おとなしくしていれば、亜澄は可愛いし」 [V07 EPI p0019]. His judgments of her intelligence are corroborated by the outcomes. His judgments of her manner are his own and are evidence about the partnership as well as about her.

**What this leaves.** We can establish what she does and says; what she says about herself, sober (often teasing or performative) and drunk (more confessional, but drunk); what Motoya makes of her; and what others say. Her motives are always inference.

### Rank, unit and career

- **Rank and unit** (narration and observed): 巡査部長, 鎌倉署刑事課強行犯係 [V01 C01 p0022]. Her direct superior is the section chief Yoshida Yasutaka (吉田康隆) [V01 C02 p0450].
- **Her claimed authority over Motoya.** 「あたしは主任だよ。あんたを指導する立場なんだよ」 [V01 C01 p0055]. The narration notes more than once that she is not formally his supervisor: they belong to different organizations, and headquarters simply keeps pairing them [V03 C01 p0075; V04 C01 p0022; V05 C02 p0556].
- **Career, as far as the text discloses it.** She joined the prefectural police two years after Motoya [V01 C01 p0060]. She served as a detective in the theft squad of 厚木署, 「かつて亜澄は厚木署の盗犯係で刑事をしていた」 [V01 C01 p0569; V02 C01 p0284]. She came to Kamakura from 本部 "a little over a year" before V04's spring 2023 [V04 C01 p0070], arriving the month before V01 [V01 C01 p0498]. What she did at headquarters, and when she left Atsugi, is unknown.
- **The examination.** She and Motoya sat the sergeant examination together; she passed and he failed [V01 C01 p0060; V03 C01 p0074]. For women officers, eligibility requires a grade in arrest technique (逮捕術) and first aid (narration, [V03 EPI p0066]).

### Age and life stage

She is 28 in V01–V03 [V01 C01 p0023; V02 C01 p0230; V03 C01 p0299] and 「二九歳」 in V04 [V04 C02 p0032]. Motoya says she is two school years below him [V01 C01 p0829; V06 C01 p0108]. She was a 学生 around July 2014, at about twenty [V03 C02 p0036], which supports a *tentative inference* that she attended some post-secondary school before joining the police; no institution is named. She calls herself the middle of the ゆとり generation [V04 C03 p0080] and remembers a television drama from her middle-school years [V04 C02 p0388].

### Appearance and presentation

- **Face and build** (Motoya's view, repeated in four volumes): a small face with a small nose and mouth and eyes too large for it, looking younger than her age [V01 C01 p0023; V02 C01 p0230; V03 C01 p0299; V04 C02 p0032]. He calls her face bright and lovable and, in the same breath, 「生意気この上ない女」 [V01 C01 p0024]. She was small as a child [V01 C01 p0829]. She walks fast, swinging her arms, slightly bow-legged [V05 EPI p0022].
- **Dress.** Light grey recurs in her suits [V01 C02 p0025; V02 C01 p0226; V05 C03 p0792]. In summer 2023 the narration calls a white shirt with a tight light-grey skirt her usual outfit, 「いつもの白シャツにタイトなライトグレーのスカート姿」 [V06 C01 p0060], and the outfit appears twice in V05 [V05 C01 p0318; V05 EPI p0020]. Variations include a black one-piece for a funeral [V01 C01 p0846], high-cut sneakers on a beach [V05 C01 p0462] and a dress shirt with black trousers [V07 C04 p0234]. She wears no perfume on duty, because a detective must not be remembered by scent [V07 C04 p0259–0261].
- **Kit.** A silver ballpoint [V01 C02 p0420], a notebook, an IC recorder [V03 C01 p0334], a daypack and a notebook PC [V07 C04 p0345].

The medium is prose; there is no illustration of her in the admitted evidence and no performed voice.

### Home and routine

She rents an apartment in 材木座, close to the station: 「亜澄は、鎌倉署に至近の材木座にアパートを借りている」 [V03 C02 p0689; V06 C03 p0063; V07 C03 p0801]. Motoya has never been inside. During investigations she sleeps at home rather than at the station [V03 C02 p0690; V04 C03 p0025]. Her usual arrival time is known only from Motoya's hedged estimate to Sayaka, around eight and probably not before 7:30 [V06 C03 p0060] (Motoya's view). She has furnished the apartment with Nordic-design items (self-report, [V05 C03 p0150]). She has a bar she frequents in Kamakura [V04 EPI p0073], and in V07 she is a self-described regular at the restaurant 《さんばれー》 in 長谷: 「あたしそこの常連なの」 [V07 C01 p0290].

### Family

- **Father.** He runs 《かつらや》 in the 平塚 shopping street [V01 C01 p0824; V05 C03 p0512]. She calls it a third-generation kimono shop: 「平塚の駅前商店街で三代続く呉服屋の娘なんです」 [V03 C03 p0175]. He is in his sixties, self-employed and talks of retiring (self-report, [V05 C03 p0511]). He is also well known as a cosplay-costume maker (narration, [V05 C03 p0512; V06 C01 p0106; V06 C02 p0179]). She mentions the shop proudly to the kimono-wearing former film star Shichijō Akiko (七条晶子), but waves away Shichijō's offer to visit it, 「鎌倉からは遠いですから、いいです」; that naming it advertises the shop is Motoya's irritated thought [V04 C02 p0628–0638].
- **Mother.** She died of illness during Asumi's student days. The fact is stated once, in Motoya-focalized narration, when Asumi's eyes moisten as the critic Yuhara Keiko (湯原圭子) speaks of her own mother: 「亜澄は学生時代に母親を病気で失っている」 [V03 C02 p0577]. The next sentence is Motoya's guess that she may be remembering that grief. Asumi herself never speaks of her mother.
- **Grandmother.** One memory: travelling Toyama medicine sellers came round when her grandmother was a child [V06 C02 p0443].
- **Siblings.** The narration calls her the shop's 一人娘 [V01 C01 p0824]. No sibling is mentioned.
- **Religion.** The family is 日蓮宗, 「うちは日蓮宗だよ」 [V01 C01 p0794]. At a Catholic memorial mass she sings the hymns and joins the readings boldly, which Motoya thinks shows no delicacy [V01 C03 p0549–0560].

### Position in the stories

In every volume she is the investigator who solves the case. She gathers the decisive facts, often off the page; stages the disclosure; and extracts the admission or makes the arrest, or orders Motoya to make it [V05 C03 p0959]. Motoya is her partner, the focal consciousness and often the voice of procedural caution, and he makes independent contributions of his own (the V04 reverse-direction footage proposal, the V06 taxi check). The cases come from the worlds of literature, music, painting, film, architecture, glass and cuisine, and range across Kamakura's neighbourhoods. Her local knowledge is part of how she leads.

## Personal history: event, disclosure and bearing

Asumi's past is never narrated as a flashback from her side. ## Personality and psychology

Because her mind is never narrated, this section separates what she does, what she says about herself, what Motoya and others think, and what can reasonably be inferred.

### Values and professional identity

**Truth-finding by any lawful means.** Her clearest statement of values comes in V02, after she has exposed the conductor Satomi's concealed deafness with a sudden blast of orchestral sound and his daughter Ayane has called the test contemptible, 「卑劣だわ」 [V02 C03 p0376]. Asumi answers, in a voice the narration calls 凜と [V02 C03 p0379], that it may be contemptible, but 「わたしは刑事なのです」; her job is to bring out the truth; she will use 「違法でない手段はなんでも使います」; even for 「人倫にもとるような行為であってもわたしは迷いません」; and 「話を訊いた人が隠しごとをしているのを黙っているわけにはいかないのです」 [V02 C03 p0378]. The credo has three parts: a self-definition as a detective, a limit (legality) and a trigger (concealment by the people she questions). The clause accepting acts against common decency matters, because it is the licence her later bluffs and stunts rely on (KCMP-SERIES-006, KCMP-CHAR-006). Her conduct in every volume is consistent with it.

**She likes being a detective.** When Emiri jokingly suggests she might try art school, she answers 「刑事としての自分が気に入ってますから」, which the narration says she delivers 妙にくそまじめに, oddly dead-earnest [V05 C02 p0625–0626]. She does not want to leave Kamakura: 「あたしも鎌倉署から動きたくない」 [V03 C02 p0040].

**Her stated limits, and how far she keeps them.**
- *No perjury.* To Ayane she promises silence about the deafness unless the investigation requires otherwise, and says she cannot lie if called to testify [V02 C03 p0465]. No breach of the perjury limit is shown.
- *No publication.* Before the V03 group disclosure she states that the painting analysis was done only for the murder investigation and that the police will not make it public [V03 C03 p0252–0253]. This limit is kept where it is stated.
- *No gifts.* She declines Marina's envelopes with a smile, 「あ、公務員なんで、それはご遠慮します」 [V01 C01 p0960]. When the magnate Gamō offers a reward, Motoya refuses first, 「わたしたちは公務員ですので」, and she seconds him; the narration calls her line a 模範解答 [V01 C03 p1145–1148].
- *Discretion.* This is the limit she states most readily and keeps least reliably. The record across volumes:
  - **V01.** Dr Ōura asks whether his dead patient's diagnosis will be used only for the investigation. After a slow 「令状が必要とお考えでしたら、また伺いますが」 [V01 C01 p0643], she assures him 「よそへ漏らすことはありません」 [V01 C01 p0647]. She later tells the priest of the malignant brain tumour [V01 C03 p0318] and states the diagnosis to the assembled family and staff in the chapel [V01 C03 p1038]. The text does not judge whether either disclosure breaches the promise (KCMP-V01-013).
  - **V02.** The conditional pledge to Ayane (above), with its explicit exception for investigative necessity.
  - **V03.** She promises the curator Maya silence, 「お約束します。刑事は口が堅いんです」 [V03 C02 p0167]. Later she tells the painter Ichiyō that Maya had asked her to keep it quiet, asks him in turn to keep it to himself, 「先生のお胸にだけ留めて頂けますか」, and discloses what Maya had confided, with a hint at its significance [V03 C03 p0186–0198]. The breach is not merely a later cost of her method: it is the instrument by which she leads Ichiyō to state the overpainting hypothesis himself (KCMP-V03-002; see [the V03 scene](#making-a-discrepancy-testable-by-others)).
  - **V04.** To the screenwriter Yamashina she promises conditional silence, 「事件解決に必要な範囲でなければ、誰にも言いません」 [V04 C03 p0195]. At the reveal the heiress Echika hears the secret parentage, which decides the inheritance, so this use arguably falls within the condition.
  - **V05.** The architect Enomoto asks 「ここだけの話にしてくれますか」 and she answers without conditions, 「もちろんです」 [V05 C03 p0691–0693]. At the end of the same interview Enomoto restates the request conditionally: keep it to yourselves 「今回の事件に関係がないならば」 [V05 C03 p0754]. At the memorial gathering she makes the eighteen-year-old rumour the frame of her disclosure, 「一八年前の黒い噂が事件の根幹にあると思います」 [V05 C03 p0842], confronts Kodera with what he overheard [V05 C03 p0900], and turns on Enomoto himself with possible hatred [V05 C03 p0910]. She does not name her source. Whether this breaches the confidence is analytical judgment: the case relevance Enomoto later set as his condition was arguably met, while her own promise had been unconditional (KCMP-CHAR-006, KCMP-V05-013).
  - **V06.** Before the disciple Kasuya tells his story of the colourant switch, she promises that no one in the police will repeat it, 「わたしをはじめ警察の者は誰にも言わないことをお約束します」, while warning that a prosecutor may call him [V06 C02 p0593–0596]. He then says he saw Mizutani entering the preparation room the night before the switch [V06 C02 p0646]. She later tells Mizutani, without naming her source, that she has heard he made the switch [V06 C03 p0486–0488], and at the Yakushiji gathering, when Yakushiji asks who did it, she turns to Kasuya and confronts him with the sighting he had claimed [V06 C03 p0587–0596].

  Promised discretion is therefore not a reliable inhibitor. The text never comments on these uses, and her public apologies address the disorder she caused, not the confidences.

**Moral indignation.** At a man's violence toward a woman: 「最低っ」, her voice edged with anger [V03 C03 p0117–0118]. At a murdered blackmailer: 「ひどい男ね」, followed by the unhedged narration 「亜澄は本音で言っている」 [V04 C03 p0922–0923], which is Motoya's confident reading. At a craftsman sabotaged by fellow disciples, she says that in Nonaka's place she would not want to work beside people trying to bring him down by foul means [V06 C02 p0690].

**Sympathy.** She cries at an old film's lovers [V04 C01 p0920–0922]. Her voice trembles when the dying Ichiyō speaks calmly of his cancer [V03 C01 p0878–0879] and when the old conductor Mibu tells of Satomi's secret support for a dead friend's son [V02 C03 p0159–0160, p0222–0223]. Her voice cracks at the details of wartime human-mine training [V01 C02 p0568], and she wipes her eyes repeatedly at a family reconciliation [V01 C03 p1099].

### Drives (observed pattern; motive inferred)

Two drives recur in what she does: solving the case, and refusing to let an anomaly go. Her own words for the second are 「でも、あたしあきらめたくないんだ」 [V01 C03 p0518], 「あたし、どうしてもアラームが気になるんだよね」 [V02 C03 p0787] and 「なんか、この事件はこれで終わりじゃない気がするんだ」 [V04 C03 p0830]. The text supplies no deeper motive. She never says why she became a detective, and no event in her history is linked to the vocation. Readings that tie her persistence to her mother's death, her childhood role as protector or the student-days rejection are **speculative**.

### Self-conception: sober and drunk

**Sober**, her self-presentation is confident and often comically self-flattering: 「そりゃああたしはかわいいけどさ」, hands on hips, then 「だってほんとのことだもん」 [V03 C01 p0613–0617]; 「あたしがかわいいからに決まってんじゃん」 [V03 C01 p1008]; and, when Motoya mockingly calls her a genius, 「やっとわかったじゃん」 [V03 C01 p1034]. She accepts a director's compliment on her looks with an unembarrassed 「えへへ」 [V04 C02 p0033]. She describes herself, lightly, as the opposite of the refined, gentle type Motoya admires: 「だよね、あたしとは真逆だもんね」 [V02 C02 p0682]. She owns her candour as a fault, 「つい本音が口から漏れちゃうんですよ」 [V02 C02 p0099], and is cheerful about her own nastiness: 「そう、あたし性格悪い嘘つきなの」 [V01 C02 p0100].

The theme of looks also appears **sober**, aimed at Motoya rather than at herself: 「ずいぶんかばうんだね。あの人の見た目がきれいだからって」, said of Emiri [V05 C02 p0868]. On gender she speaks seriously and sober in V04. When the old actress Utsunomiya marvels at a female senior as a sign of 「女権時代」, Asumi answers 「いえ……警察はまだまだ男性中心の組織ですから」, with a serious face [V04 C02 p0848–0852].

**Drunk**, the self-image turns to grievance about how men and the world treat a woman like her:
- 「こんなにいい女なのにさぁ」 [V01 EPI p0023] and 「あたしだってね。本気で人を好きになりたいよ」 [V01 EPI p0064];
- 「いいオトコっていないもんだね」 [V02 EPI p0047];
- 「あたしなんて誰一人からも、か弱いなんて言われたことないんだよ」 [V03 EPI p0053];
- 「顔がいい女はみんなから大切にされる。あたしはそうじゃないから」 [V04 EPI p0092];
- 「あんたとかあたしみたいな女には用はないってわけか」, said cackling [V07 EPI p0137–0138].

Each grievance has a different content: no good men, being taken for strong, looks, type. What repeats across V01, V03, V04 and V07 is a pattern: drunk, she voices a sense of being overlooked or unprotected because she is taken to be strong or unappealing. That repetition is a *supported* inference. That it reflects a stable private belief rather than drink-talk is *tentative*. In V04 she denies that it is aimed at anyone: 「別に誰ってことはないよ」 [V04 EPI p0096]. The sober V05 jab shows the theme is not confined to drink, though sober she aims it at men's preferences, not at her own appearance. The sober-drunk split is a tendency, not a rule.

The sober "of course I'm cute" and the drunk "I'm not pretty, so I'm left alone" contradict each other. On one reading the boasting is performance and the drunk complaint closer to sincere; on another both are banter, the drunk version self-pity licensed by alcohol. The text does not decide.

### Vulnerabilities, fears and soft spots

- **The fear of hospitals.** After a clinic visit leaves her listless, she says with a frown 「最近はずいぶんよくなったけど、あたし病院恐怖症なんだよ」 [V01 C01 p0740–0741]. Motoya's teasing guess about a big injury draws only a clouded face, and she defers the explanation with a dead-serious 「そのうち話すよ」 [V01 C01 p0742–0745]. The listlessness may also reflect the case being closed as suicide that day. In V02 she proposes visiting Mibu before either of them knows he is in hospital, and is stiff-faced at his door, which Motoya puts down to nerves about an eccentric patient [V02 C03 p0008–0010, p0021–0022]. In V04 she herself proposes, 「あのさ、病院に行ってみない？」 [V04 C02 p0295], with no reaction narrated. Only V04 shows her willingly going to a hospital, which fits "much better recently".
- **Being moved by art, sober.** She tells Keiko 「わたし一遥画伯のお宅で『極楽寺逍遙』を拝見したときに、涙が出そうになりました」, says she felt the anguish of its painter, Yuhara Sōjirō (湯原宗二郎), Keiko's late father, then blushes and looks down [V03 C02 p0618–0619; V03 C02 p0535]. Motoya sees no such anguish in the painting [V03 C02 p0620]. It is one of the very few sober reports of an inner response.
- **Loss of confidence.** After a strained interview she says it was the hardest questioning ever and that she has lost some confidence [V02 C02 p0665]. With Motoya, when her theory is unusual, she becomes diffident in a way the narration marks as unlike her: 「ちょっと気弱な声音」 and 「亜澄にしては慎重な物言い」 [V01 C03 p0493–0496]; 「単なる推察に過ぎないよ。そのつもりで聞いてね」, said 珍しくしおらしい [V04 C03 p0290–0291].
- **"No one to drink with."** Asked by the disciple Kume whether a buyer needs more than one expensive wine glass, she answers in the hypothetical, 「わたしの場合なら一緒に飲む相手もいないから、ひとつあればじゅうぶんです」, and imagines pouring wine into a favourite glass while listening to music, all in a happy voice [V06 C02 p0363–0364]. Motoya stifles a laugh at her confessing to being a 「ぼっち」 [V06 C02 p0365]. The premise, no one to drink with, is a present-tense statement about her life; the evening alone is imagined, not a reported habit. Whether the premise is confession or casual self-deprecation is undecidable.
- **Susceptibility to charm** (V01–V03; see [handsome men](#handsome-men-v01v03)).

### Coping and recovery

- **Optimism.** Motoya, privately: 「亜澄はいつもどこか楽天的だ」, and that seems to be her strength [V02 C02 p0437].
- **Reframing.** 「仕方ないじゃん。だいたい刑事の仕事は九〇パーセント以上が無駄なんだから」, then 「でも、あたしは無駄だとは思っていない」 [V07 C01 p0579–0583].

## Cognition: from noticing a difference to making it testable

### What she notices

- **Absences and anomalies in conduct and objects.** In V01, the novelist Ashina's announced and then missing last manuscript [V01 C01 p0720]. In V02, the conductor Satomi answers only after Ayane repeats each question in announcer-perfect diction [V02 C03 p0390], and the pianist Shiina, for once, skipped his tuning check [V02 C03 p1014]. In V04, the victim Akao watched only one film at the memorial hall [V04 C01 p0631–0640]. In V06, Emiri's phrase 『そんな亡くなり方』 betrays knowledge she should not have had [V06 C01 p0454–0458]. In V07, the waitress Shiobara appears in an old staff photograph from a defunct rival restaurant [V07 C04 p0298–0326].
- **Colour, texture and material.** In V03 she identifies a painting as another artist's by colour and touch [V03 C01 p0911] and sees that one hedge in the oil painting 『極楽寺逍遙』 uses more blue and black than its pair, 「右のほうが青と黒の絵の具を多く使っているように見えます」 [V03 C03 p0161]. In V04 she names the fabric and dyes of Shichijō's kimono [V04 C02 p0622–0642] and, in the memorial exhibition, singles out one thin diamond chain bracelet in a dead actor's private photographs, naming the maker as probably Van Cleef & Arpels [V04 C01 p0534–0538]. That identification becomes a reader-side clue when the bracelet is matched at the reveal [V04 C03 p0885–0902] (KCMP-V04-005). In V06 the narration deflates one of her colour answers as what most people would say [V06 C02 p0604].
- **Topography and jurisdiction.** She knows the seven approaches to 源氏山公園 and that houses fifty metres away as the crow flies are far longer by road [V06 C01 p0140–0169], which streets belong to Kamakura and which to 大船 [V06 C01 p0684], and the lanes of 長谷 [V07 C01 p0296–0310].
- **Words and slips.** When the V02 suspect Ishikawa claims the hall's security is nothing like it was in his day, she pounces: 「なんでいまの態勢を知ってるのよ」 [V02 C03 p0672].
- **Memory.** She gives a complete scene briefing without notes [V01 C02 p0115–0116] and reconstructs an unrecorded interview from memory [V02 C03 p0390]. She also falsely claims a bad memory to justify recording an interview [V03 C01 p0325–0327].

### How she reasons

**Intuition first, admitted as such.** Asked for her basis, she concedes there is none and goes on: 「そう、たしかに根拠はない。だけどね」 [V03 C02 p0252–0254]; 「根拠はよ？」, 「ない」, said with an unruffled face [V04 C01 p0617–0620]. Motoya's considered view is that what guides her is 「一種の勘なのだ」 rather than logic, an intuition some regard as higher than verbal thought [V07 C01 p0430], and that she is excellent at logic too, 「なによりも勘がいい」 [V06 C02 p0533]. Her 勘 has more texture when set beside what the novels show of her learning. In V02 she explains that the family kimono trade trained her eye for colour [V02 C01 p0300–0312]; in V03 that eye becomes decisive. Her local knowledge was built over years of visiting Kamakura before she was posted there.

**But not prejudgement.** When Motoya declares a grudge motive in V03, she answers 「それは予断じゃん」, and the narration, noting that criminal police dislike anything but established fact, judges that 「亜澄のいまの思考法も、その意味では刑事らしい」 [V03 C01 p0155–0176]. In V02 she argues against fixing on the stage manager Aiki for lack of motive [V02 C01 p0854–0918]. Her hunches are about where to look, held alongside a stated caution about whom to accuse. She does not always honour the caution: with Sayaka and Emiri she breaks it (see [mistakes](#mistakes-and-failures)).

**Structural and associative moves.**
- V01: decoding ＵＢＡＧＡＹＡ as the place name 姥ヶ谷 [V01 C02 p0257–0262], and later through a pun learned from an Okinawa television programme; Motoya thinks 「元哉にはできない思考法だ」 [V01 C03 p0940].
- V03: inferring that Ichiyō disclosed his illness and inheritance plans so that police recordings would serve as evidence in a future lawsuit [V03 C01 p1016–1025].
- V04: building a hypothesis of a crime of conviction through the idea of a normative barrier [V04 C03 p0294–0318].
- V05: seeing a decades-old theft of drawings as the root of the case [V05 C03 p0768].
- V06: 「恨みのベクトルが逆だよ」, the vector of resentment points the wrong way [V06 C02 p0692].
- V07: sorting the possible contacts between two restaurants into persons, methods, ingredients and suppliers [V07 C03 p0694–0736].

**Base rates and place.** 「鎌倉なんてとこは滅多に事件は起きない場所だよ」, so two deaths 500 metres apart are probably linked [V01 C02 p0215]. Robbers work the beaches, not 源氏山 [V06 C01 p0894].

**Legal fluency.** She cites the attempt provision of 刑法43条 [V02 C03 p0704] and inheritance by representation [V03 C01 p0881], and sketches the civil and unfair-competition questions raised by industrial espionage [V07 C04 p0471–0473]. Against this stands a false claim about the law, made for tactical reasons, in V01 (see [deception](#deception-and-its-limits)).

**Hedged reconstruction.** At reveals she can mark her own conjecture: in V05 she prefaces her account of the victim's marriage and motive with 「これはわたしの想像ですから、間違っているかもしれません」 [V05 C03 p0943]. That same account dates the marriage after the 2008 award, which three witnesses contradict [V05 C03 p0224; V05 C02 p0424–0428, p0639]. Whether this is the detective's error or an authorial slip is unresolved; it does not touch the culprit (KCMP-V05-005).

### Making a discrepancy testable by others

Her method is not only private insight. Again and again she converts a neglected discrepancy into something other people can check, fund or authorize, and the series makes those other people's choices part of her effectiveness.

- **V01.** She pursues the unwritten part of a case the team believes it has classified. A book can contain a statement about a death without being the official note. Her way into the question is a title, a missing manuscript and the local significance of a place. At the headquarters meeting she volunteers her surfing knowledge of the coast in front of the 管理官 Nikaidō, who looks annoyed and then assigns her the line: 「いいだろう、小笠原は姥ヶ谷という言葉がなにを意味するのか徹底的に調べろ」 [V01 C02 p0270–0276]. When she plans the decisive confrontation, she admits to Motoya that she cannot move the brass with her idea alone, 「あたしの考えで上を動かすのは無理」 [V01 C03 p0510], and acknowledges uncertainty about the plan [V01 C03 p0493–0496].
- **V03.** She notices the colour difference in the hedge and asks Ichiyō to look [V03 C03 p0155–0161]. He confirms it, 「君は正しい」, and first explains it as careless paint-mixing by Sōjirō [V03 C03 p0163–0168]. She then breaks Maya's request for silence, telling Ichiyō of his murdered son Takaharu's (鰐淵貴遥) plan to have the painting analysed, and hints that the son had noticed the same difference; the narration marks her words as 含みを残した [V03 C03 p0186–0198]. Ichiyō exclaims that he understands, and states 断定的に that something lies painted beneath the hedge; she answers 「わたしもそう思っています」 [V03 C03 p0199–0205]. His consent and funding turn the observation into a laboratory test [V03 C03 p0207–0213]. The order matters: the breach of Maya's confidence is the instrument that elicits the hypothesis, not only a cost of obtaining access (KCMP-V03-002). Earlier, the line itself had survived only because the division chief Fukushima argued for keeping weak lines open and Nikaidō grudgingly let the two of them continue, 「ふたりだけですから、好きにさせましょうか」 [V03 C02 p0769–0775] (KCMP-V03-007).
- **V04.** The 管理官 Satake allows her a line outside the usual victim-centred route, partly on her record: 「小笠原が読んだ筋から事件が解決したことは一回ではない」 [V04 C01 p0889–0893]. She predicts that the suspect's car will appear on the 金沢街道 cameras, stops the footage herself, 「見つけた！」, and points to the car; Motoya identifies the model he had seen at Shichijō's carport [V04 C03 p0402–0409]. He then proposes, from his own route reasoning, that the return journey should appear in the opposite direction [V04 C03 p0426–0432]. Her recognition of a possible vehicle, his suggestion to check a clearer direction, and the supervisory decision to resource the review are separate contributions (KCMP-V04-015, KCMP-CHAR-008).
- **V06.** Her local knowledge becomes practical access. The map's nearest house to the scene is hard to approach, and she gets the detectives there [V06 C01 p0657–0688]. She asks the elderly resident, Shinagawa Makiko (品川眞喜子), whose card names her a little later [V06 C01 p0839], only when the cry was heard, 「何時頃のことか覚えていますか」 [V06 C01 p0812]; Shinagawa anchors it herself to the end of a regular news programme [V06 C01 p0814]. Neither skill by itself guarantees the answer. At the evening briefing, her account of the disciples interests almost no one, and headquarters turns to commercial leads [V06 C02 p0718–0719].
- **V07.** The finale gives the clearest view of her reasoning in dialogue. She first pursues the rumour from her regular restaurant, then recognizes that shared emotion has yet to supply a link. She proposes cross-employment and financial inquiries. At the briefing she presents the lack of a bridge as a gap in what the inquiry has established rather than as a request of her own, and Motoya notes 「亜澄は報告の要領を心得ている」 [V07 C03 p0754–0757]. Nikaidō takes up her idea [V07 C03 p0763–0764]. On the second point she puts on 「とぼけた顔」 and lets Nikaidō discover for himself that nobody has checked it [V07 C03 p0781–0787]. 「結果として、亜澄の考えは二つとも実現した」 [V07 C03 p0792]. A staff photograph, kept by Aonuma Yasushige (青沼泰茂), then shows the waitress Shiobara, whose alibi for the fatal interval the first inquiry had confirmed [V07 C03 p0750], among the staff of the defunct 《雨月庵》 [V07 C04 p0290–0326]. Asumi treats evidence of contact and evidence of direct action as different tasks. This pursuit makes Nikaidō's allocation of people and Yasushige's choice to supply the photograph part of her effectiveness.

Reception of her lines therefore varies by commander and by case: backed in V01, V03 and V04, ignored at the V06 briefing, adopted in V07. It is not a steady rise in institutional licence (KCMP-SERIES-005).

### How she handles uncertainty

She shows two faces. **With Motoya** she hedges and asks. From V04 on she regularly asks his impression first after an interview: 「ねぇ、どう思った？」 [V04 C03 p0262]; 「どう思う？」 [V05 C03 p0400]; 「どう思った？」 [V06 C02 p0246; V07 C01 p0278; V07 C02 p0410]; 「吉川くんは、どう思う？」 [V07 C03 p0660]. That is six debriefs from V04 to V07; it is not established as a habit in V01–V03. She labels guesses as guesses [V01 C03 p0495; V04 C03 p0290], and once adopts his reading outright, 「吉川くんが言うとおりだと思う」 [V06 C02 p0263].

**With suspects** she projects certainty she does not have (see [deception](#deception-and-its-limits)). **With superiors** she usually frames carefully: she tells Satake the line is probably wasted effort [V04 C01 p0907]; she reports gaps rather than requests; she uses a feigned-innocent face to let Nikaidō discover a gap. That is strategic framing, not a false statement; no false statement to a superior is recorded. Not every superior meets the careful face, as the next section shows.

### Conflicting obligations

**Hierarchy against initiative.** Initiative usually wins, and she usually covers it with sanction. She speaks out of turn at headquarters meetings [V01 C02 p0257–0259; V02 C01 p0190–0198], views a crime scene when told only to visit the family [V03 C01 p0047–0051], and volunteers for lines [V01 C02 p0270; V05 C01 p0685; V06 C01 p0920]. She usually obtains sanction before pursuing them: from Nikaidō in V01 and V07, from Satake in V04, from Yoshida in V06–V07. When rebuked she apologizes at once, 「あ、すみません、ひとり言です」 [V02 C01 p0197]. Three items show the limits of the careful face, the second only in its institutional cost:
- **Blurting before senior officers.** Asumi tells the cymbalist Yura, a little embarrassed, that she blurted 「映画の見過ぎ」 at an investigation meeting and was scolded by the brass [V02 C02 p0091–0092]. Motoya adds that being near her makes him nervous because 「えらい人がそろっている場面で言わなきゃいいこと言っちゃう」, and the narration confirms 「この話は事実だ」 [V02 C02 p0095–0096] (her own account, testimony and narration agree). It is the only direct evidence on how she behaves under senior eyes.
- **Bypassing the headquarters.** With Ayane's information against Ishikawa she goes straight to her own station's criminal section, the competent desk for an embezzlement report, without stopping at the headquarters, 「捜査本部には寄らずにまっすぐに刑事課に向かった」. The report is accepted and the pair are lent to the fraud squad; Nikaidō is 渋い顔 at the loan and the 刑事課長 Ōno pushes it through, 「二階堂管理官は渋い顔だったが、大野課長が頑張ってくれた」 [V02 C03 p0524–0527]. The text does not call the route a breach.
- **Devaluing an HQ meeting.** On the 江ノ電 in V01 she proposes watching the 稲村ヶ崎 sunset rather than hurrying to the evening meeting: 「そんなもん遅れて行ったっていいよ。どうせロクな話出てこないんだもん」 [V01 C03 p0455–0457].

No scene in the corpus shows her conducting an interview while a superior watches.

**Decency against truth.** Her credo resolves this in favour of truth, within legality.

**Confidentiality against the case.** She keeps the public silent (no publication) and treats private confidences as instruments when the case needs them (V03; V05; V06; V01 and V04 less clearly).

**Personal feeling against duty.** In V05–V06 her animus toward Sayaka and Emiri leaks into questioning. Motoya judges that she normally knows better but is 「あきらかに調子が狂っている」 around them [V06 C03 p0139]. Emiri says she came to fear the police after the previous month's case [V06 C03 p0129–0134]; Sayaka puts it as fear of Asumi [V06 C03 p0057]; Motoya blames Asumi's interrogating tone [V06 C03 p0138]. These are the reasons Emiri brings her corrected account to Motoya instead.

### Interview and interrogation style

- **Correct form when it matters.** In V01 she gives a voluntary-interview caution: what the person says can be used as evidence, and they need not say what they do not wish to [V01 C03 p0664]. Motoya: 「亜澄のような正しい説明をする刑事は少ない」 [V01 C03 p0666].
- **Implied consequences from the start.** When Dr Ōura hesitates over a dead patient's records she says slowly 「令状が必要とお考えでしたら、また伺いますが」, then assures him firmly that 「我々が秘密を漏洩する罪はお医者さまより重いのです」 [V01 C01 p0643–0648]. He discloses.
- **Graded escalation against obstruction.** When Shichijō's household man Miyazaki blocks access to her in V04, Asumi moves through firm, irritated, softer, then cool and resolute: 「わたしたちは七条晶子さんにお目に掛かりたいと申しております」, said いくらか強い声 [V04 C02 p0549–0550]; irritably, that it is Shichijō they want to talk to, not him [V04 C02 p0554–0555]; 「だから、七条さんに会わせてください」, a little softer [V04 C02 p0558–0559]; and 「ご本人ならともかく、あなたには断る資格はないですよ」, 冷静だが、毅然とした口調 [V04 C02 p0562–0563].
- **Frontal pressure on weak suspects.** To the fleeing administrator Ishikawa: 「なんで逃げたのよ」; 「いい加減なこと言うんじゃない」; 「石川宗佑さん、あなた、三浦倫人さんを殺したんでしょ」 [V02 C03 p0633–0638]; 「わたしの目を見なさいっ」 [V02 C03 p0678].
- **Pressure when lied to.** To Shiobara: 「噓ですね？」, then that she had better not give the police sloppy explanations, with a hard glare [V07 C04 p0418–0425]; she then softens with condolences and sympathy for Shiobara's sick mother. The tag 「噓ですね？」 occurs only here. The same move appears as 「先生、あなたはわたしにウソをついていますね」 to Yakushiji [V06 C03 p0699] and 「宮崎さん、あなたは投資詐欺の被害なんかに遭っていないでしょ」 to Miyazaki [V04 C03 p0843].
- **Patience and a still face.** 「今度は亜澄は辛抱強く待った」 [V02 C03 p0695]. She hides surprise, and Motoya concedes 「彼女もさすがはプロの刑事だ」 [V02 C03 p0701–0702].
- **Gentleness with the broken.** 「楽屋へ戻りましょうか」, gently, to the humiliated Shiina [V02 C03 p0936]; no handcuffs for the confessing Keiko [V03 C03 p0436–0437]; advice to the confessing Shichijō to find a good lawyer (V04 C03).
- **Leverage through what a person protects.** She uses Shichijō's coming to the station to move Miyazaki [V04 C03 p0847–0848]. She presses the reluctant disciple Kasuya in a soft voice, 「やわらかい声で亜澄は恫喝した」, and then tells him honestly that a prosecutor may still call him to testify [V06 C02 p0587–0596].

**Limits she keeps.** No false statement to a court or to a superior is recorded. She refuses perjury explicitly [V02 C03 p0465]. She tells witnesses honestly that they may have to testify [V06 C02 p0595]. Promised discretion is not on this list (see [values](#values-and-professional-identity)).

**What her apologies cover.** After staged disclosures she apologizes formally: 「皆さま、聖なる追悼ミサにこのような混乱を招いて申し訳ありません」 [V01 C03 p0628]; 「この結果を得るために、いろいろと失礼なことを申しあげました」 [V05 C03 p0973]. The apologies address the disorder and rudeness, never the lies.

### Witness costs and repair

Asumi can repair a damaged investigative relationship, and the repair is itself a tool.
- **Yura (V02).** The cymbalist is angry at having been taken in for questioning and refuses to meet. She phones him in a nasal, wheedling voice, 鼻へ抜けるように猫なで声 [V02 C01 p0943–0944], takes the blame in humble form, 「わたくしどもの手落ちです」 [V02 C01 p0949], and gets the meeting. At the café she bows deeply and pays out of her own pocket [V02 C02 p0040–0042]. When Motoya insinuates that she wanted to apologize only after the Satomi household refused her, she answers, unbothered, 「えへへ、お詫びするなら早いほうがいいと思ってさ」 [V02 C01 p0958–0961], which is neither admission nor denial. Yura ends by trusting her, 「小笠原さんの言葉は信じられる気がします」 [V02 C02 p0113], and the exchange becomes a source of professional evidence. That the apology was instrumental is a *supported inference* from its timing and Motoya's insinuation, not her admission.
- **Ayane (V02).** Asumi protects the case by applying pressure around Satomi's hearing, and places boundaries on her promise of discretion [V02 C03 p0465]. Ayane objects to the means and then decides how to act within that pressure: she agrees to file a victim's report (被害届) of Ishikawa's embezzlement on condition that the detectives keep their promise, 「お約束を守ってくださるなら、被害届はもちろん出します」 [V02 C03 p0480–0482], and the station's fraud squad takes it up [V02 C03 p0524–0525]. She files no complaint about Asumi (KCMP-CHAR-013).
- **Maya (V03).** Ichiyō's consent as owner authorizes examination of the painting and its circulation to a defined group. That permission does not resolve the earlier understanding with Maya, whose confidence is the instrument that elicits the owner's hypothesis [V03 C03 p0186–0205].
- **The V05 memorial.** The confrontation gives a related problem a different shape. Asumi apologizes to the room [V05 C03 p0973], but Motoya, privately, finds her spiteful and wonders whether everything needed exposing in that company, 「ここまですべてを明らかにする必要があるのだろうか」 [V05 C03 p0926–0927]. She does not apologize to Motoya for their quarrel that week; the quarrel simply ends, and by the afternoon she is herself again [V05 C03 p0411] (KCMP-V05-014).

**The counterexample to a consistently adaptive interviewer is specific.** Asumi uses Emiri's V05 interview partly to pursue questions about Motoya and Sayaka, and asks Emiri for an alibi in a barbed tone; Motoya wonders whether she has lost her senses [V05 C02 p0682–0689]. In V06 Emiri gives fear of the police as her own reason for having lied, 「警察は怖いという印象を抱いてしまったのです」 [V06 C03 p0134]; Sayaka, bringing her to Motoya at dawn, puts it more bluntly, 「英美里は亜澄ちゃんが怖いの」 [V06 C03 p0057]. Asumi reacts sharply to being bypassed, but the correction becomes central to her questions of Yakushiji and Mizutani. Her favourable response to craft expertise offers an internal comparison. In V06 she asks the glass artist Mizutani a generous set of questions about his working life and materials before returning explicitly to the unpleasant procedural question, with an apology for asking [V06 C02 p0145–0200]. In V07 she lets Sayaka's useful firsthand comparison of two restaurants proceed during another hostile encounter, and says afterwards that she would not have let the women talk if it were useless, 「まったく役に立たないなら、二人に話させないよ」 [V07 C02 p0215]. Both later actions matter to her professional competence, alongside the costs of the earlier behaviour.

### Mistakes and failures

- **Information risk.** She shows the editor Torii unreleased evidence on personal trust, over Motoya's alarm [V01 C02 p0643–0662]. No harm follows in the text.
- **Groundless suspicion driven by dislike.** The barbed alibi question to Emiri [V05 C02 p0682–0689]; an affair-and-rejection motive for Emiri that proves irrelevant and that Motoya calls cheap melodrama [V05 C02 p0860–0862]; and a demand for Sayaka's alibi with no link to the case, which collapses when Sayaka says Motoya can vouch for her: 「いっ」, then 「け、刑事に向かって冗談はやめなさい」 [V06 C01 p0488–0505]. Motoya: 「どうも亜澄は常軌を逸している」 [V06 C01 p0495].
- **Consequences of her prickliness.** Her interrogating manner is one reason Emiri lies to her and later brings the truth to Motoya alone [V06 C03 p0057, p0134–0139].
- **Charmed by a killer.** She softens before the pianist Shiina [V02 C02 p0751–0757], who is the murderer. She still traps him, and afterwards says, drunk, that arresting him felt like crushing a pretty decorated cookie in her own palm [V02 EPI p0052].
- **Public slips.** Her muttered 「映画の見過ぎじゃないの」 at a meeting draws Nikaidō's rebuke [V02 C01 p0190–0198]. Her sugary, sloppy reply to Gamō's grandsons leaves Motoya to supply the official answer [V01 C03 p0185–0195].
- **A hunch that was not decisive.** She keeps the chef Izumida in view because he has no alibi, 「アリバイはないし……」 [V07 C02 p0418]. He had told her he went home alone to 常盤 [V07 C02 p0403]; Sayaka later reports, and his small bow tacitly accepts, that he spent the night at the flat of Emiri, his fiancée [V07 EPI p0110–0122] (KCMP-V07-012).
- **A reconstruction slip.** Her V05 dating of the victim's marriage contradicts three witnesses (see [cognition](#how-she-reasons)).

### Meaningful exceptions

- **Meekness.** After a wasted day off: 「ごめんね。せっかくの休みを無駄にしちゃって……」, eyes on the pavement; Motoya finds her 珍しくしおらしい [V01 C01 p0975–0977].
- **Explicit deference** to Motoya's reading of a witness [V06 C02 p0263].
- **Stunned silence during her own reveal.** Speechlessness at surprising information is common for her: 言葉を失った, 絶句 and ぼう然 recur in six volumes [V01 C02 p0563; V02 C02 p0162, p0498; V02 C03 p0131, p0140, p0426; V04 C02 p0908; V04 C03 p0656, p0708, p0747; V05 C02 p0523; V05 C03 p0171; V06 C02 p0416, p0641]. What is distinctive in V03 is the framing, at a confession during a disclosure she is running: 「亜澄ですら黙ったまま、ぼう然と真矢を見ている」 [V03 C03 p0531].
- **No triumph at an arrest.** 「亜澄は静かに目を伏せていた」 as Shiina is driven away [V02 C03 p1056]. She nods silently at Eguchi's self-justification rather than berating him [V07 C04 p0637–0639].
- **No staged gathering in V07.** The arrest is made by Yoshida's team, who arrive with warrants after her confrontation [V07 C04 p0610–0616].

## Speech and communication

### The medium's limits

This is written prose. Voice quality reaches the reader only through narrated descriptors, almost all of them Motoya's: a voice "from the top of her head" (頭のてっぺんから声) [V01 C03 p0163; V03 C01 p0279]; a nasal, wheedling 猫なで声 [V02 C01 p0944]; a company voice, よそゆき声 [V01 C02 p0473]; a sugary 甘ったるい声 [V02 C03 p0846]; a ringing 凜と [V02 C03 p0379]; a cracking voice, 「亜澄の声は裏返った」 [V02 C03 p0462; V05 EPI p0034; V07 C01 p0362]. There is no acoustic evidence and no performance. The register contrasts that carry much of her characterization are flattened in English, so any reconstruction of her voice should work in Japanese register terms.

- キミ is her default teasing-senior form, frequent in V01–V03;
- 吉川くん can frame collegial support or invoke seniority;
- 元哉くん is affectionate or mocking and becomes rare; by V06 she polices who else may use it;
- あんた is the anger register, concentrated in V05–V06;
- in V07 キミ returns and あんた disappears.

### Silence and withholding

- **Withholding her reasoning from Motoya** until a reveal: in V01 she whispers her theory into his ear and the narration omits its content [V01 C03 p0503, p0523]; in V02, 「いまのところは秘密にしとく」 [V02 C03 p0260].

- **The freeze-out.** In V06, Sayaka and Emiri come to Motoya alone at dawn with Emiri's corrected story. He tells Asumi as soon as she arrives, and she stops speaking to him: 「出勤してきた亜澄に、英美里から聞いた話をかいつまんで話すと口を利いてくれなくなった」 [V06 C03 p0264]. She walks ahead in silence [V06 C03 p0313]. Her stated grievance, when it comes, is that the women went to him alone (see [Asumi and Motoya](#asumi-toward-motoya-kitsukawa)). He concealed nothing in V06; concealment occurs only in V07.

### Thought and speech

Her inner speech is never given, so the usual comparison between private thought and spoken word cannot be made directly. Three substitutes exist.
1. **Motoya's reading against her words.** He supplies her motives, often uncertainly: 「助け船のつもりか」 when she changes the subject after his gaffe [V01 C01 p0239]; his reading of her diffidence as awareness that her idea is outlandish [V04 C03 p0327]; his reading of her V05 outburst as long-held hostility [V05 C02 p0844]; his V06 recap that she suspects a relationship between him and Sayaka [V06 C01 p0297].
2. **Sober against drunk speech.** Sober she teases and boasts; drunk she grieves about how men treat strong or plain women. Her single serious drunk utterance about Motoya is a professional credit: 「吉川くん、いいとこいっぱいあるんだよ。あたし、こいつがいるから捜査やれるんだ」, said まじめな顔で [V07 EPI p0144–0145].
3. **Avowal against conduct.** "I've given up", "I've never gone soppy", "I'm not picking a fight", "nobody in particular" (see [contradictions](#contradictions-the-text-leaves-standing)). Each may be denial, joke or truth; the text does not decide.

## Relationships

Each subsection describes Asumi's own conduct toward the person, then what is known of the other side. The separate [partnership study](KSOA_PARTNERSHIP.md) analyses the relationship as a two-way system; this portrait does not depend on it.

### Asumi toward Motoya Kitsukawa

**Structure.** Four facts shape the relationship.
- They are childhood acquaintances from the same 平塚 shopping street, two school years apart. As a girl she protected the small, frail Motoya from the bully Ogura. He acknowledges the debt but limits it to "until about age ten" [V01 C01 p0825–0830].
- They were out of contact from his middle-school graduation until they met again as police officers [V01 C01 p0746].
- She outranks him, having passed the examination he failed [V01 C01 p0060], though as members of different organizations she is not formally his superior [V05 C02 p0556].
- Investigation headquarters at Kamakura pair them every time. Nikaidō and others seem to enjoy it [V03 C01 p0028]; Yoshida tells Masaki 「吉川とはいつも組んでるじゃないですか」 [V06 C01 p0220]; she tells Masaki herself, 「鎌倉署に捜査本部が設置されるといつも組んでいます」 [V05 C01 p0693]. Motoya comes to regard the pairing as fated, 宿命的 [V06 C01 p0932]. She calls them 「バディ」 [V06 EPI p0019].

**Her conduct toward him** (observed; motives inferred):

1. **Leading and instructing.** In V01 she claims to be his 主任 with a duty to guide him [V01 C01 p0055]. She calls herself his instructor before others: 「これは捜一の吉川くん。あたしが指導している巡査長だよ」 [V03 C01 p0072]; 「吉川の指導係です」 [V05 C02 p0549]. When the old actress Utsunomiya takes her for Motoya's attendant, she states 「わたしがリーダーなんです」 and then commends him, 「わたしはよい後輩に恵まれてますから」 [V04 C02 p0840–0856]. She orders the arrest, 「吉川、手錠掛けて、宣告して」, hands on hips, and Motoya wonders 「なんでこいつは上司面をするんだ」 [V05 C03 p0959–0961]. In V07 she still sends him off with 「しっかり休んでね」 [V07 C03 p0795]. Motoya reads all this as a wish to play big sister, 「姉さんぶってみたいのだ」 [V02 C01 p0294], and finds it grating, 「鼻につく」 [V07 C03 p0797].
2. **Teasing and coercion.** The Sunday summons and the bully card (V01–V02). Taunts about his having no girlfriend [V01 C01 p0776]. "Couple play" at a café table, twice: 「たまにはカップルごっこもいいじゃない」, then 「バカ、本気にするんじゃないよ」 with a practical excuse [V01 C02 p0508–0512]; 「カップルごっこしようよ」 [V04 C01 p0726]. Mockery when she catches him admiring women: bikini-clad girls [V02 C01 p0327–0331], Ayane [V02 C02 p0675–0694], Keiko [V03 C02 p0692–0721], Echika [V04 EPI p0068].
3. **Reliance.** It is enacted from V01, long before it is stated. She asks his impression first after interviews from V04 (six debriefs). She coordinates with him wordlessly. She has him phone the hall in an interrogation they planned together [V02 C03 p0650–0659], run the computer at the V03 disclosure [V03 C03 p0238], and make the V05 arrest. She asks him before a witness whether he has questions, 「吉川くん、なにかお伺いすることありますか」 [V02 C02 p0924], and openly adopts his reading of Mizutani [V06 C02 p0263]. In V06 she cites his friendship with a local artist to win the workshop assignment from Yoshida [V06 C01 p0924].
4. **Praise, rationed.** Praise for his first-day questioning [V01 C01 p0580]; 「たまにはいいこと言うじゃん」 with a hard slap on the shoulder [V01 C02 p0734–0736]; she had told the junior Nishina in advance that Motoya was an excellent detective, 「吉川さんは優秀な刑事だって、小笠原先輩が」 (testimony, [V01 C02 p0093]); "a good junior" to Utsunomiya [V04 C02 p0855]; and the V07 credit, discussed below.
5. **Care and apology.** She apologizes for wasting his day off [V01 C01 p0975] and treats him to lunch, 「あたしがおごるよ」 [V01 C01 p1009]. She asks seriously whether he is short of sleep, then needles him [V04 C03 p0019–0026]. When he balks at more coffee: 「仕方ないじゃん。オレンジジュースでも飲みなよ」 [V07 C03 p0643].
6. **Physical familiarity**, concentrated in V04: she pokes his arm [V04 C01 p0169], kicks his calf right after he praises Echika's looks [V04 C02 p0087–0090], takes his hand and dances at the footage breakthrough [V04 C03 p0442–0444], and tugs his sleeve and jacket hem [V04 EPI p0024, p0046]. Elsewhere she grabs his arm to lead him [V06 C01 p0627], sits right beside him [V07 C04 p0258], and in V01, drunk, hugs him [V01 EPI p0065].
7. **Displeasure at his contact with other women** (V04–V07). Two configurations must be kept apart.
   - *(a) The women go to Motoya directly* (V05–V06). In V06 Sayaka and Emiri come to him alone at dawn with Emiri's corrected story. He reports it to Asumi as soon as she arrives, and she freezes him out [V06 C03 p0264]. Her stated grievance is the bypass, not secrecy: 「だから、なんで吉川くん一人を呼び出してそんな重要なことをあんただけに話しに来るのよ」 [V06 C03 p0315]; 「罪の意識があるならなおのこと、あたしに直接、ほんとのことを話すべきよ」 [V06 C03 p0320]; and it was Sayaka who called him out, 「性悪よ。結局、あんたが甘いから」 [V06 C03 p0329]. She then takes up the evidence and both accept a further interview with Mizutani, so joint work survives the disagreement.
   - *(b) Motoya withholds a contact* (V07 only, a single instance). He keeps Emiri's evening visit to the station from Asumi, explicitly to avoid another freeze-out like the previous month's [V07 C04 p0238–0240]. When Sayaka lets it slip, Asumi demands 「えっ、キミはいつ英美里さんと会ったのよ？」 and 「なんであたしに言わないのよ」; he does not answer [V07 EPI p0124–0130].
8. **Celebration invitations.** She proposes the case-end meal or drink in V01 (Thai dinner) [V01 EPI p0008], V02 [V02 EPI p0019], V03 ("I'll treat you to celebrate") [V03 EPI p0008], V04 (soba, then "just one bar") [V04 EPI p0028–0047], V06 [V06 EPI p0023] and V07, where she proposes and books 《月の路》 [V07 C04 p0648–0653]. In V06 she tells Sayaka he came gladly; Motoya inwardly denies it, 「別に元哉は「喜んで」ここへ来ているわけではない」 [V06 EPI p0023–0025].

**The V06 address contest.** In the V06 bar, Sayaka asks whether this is a date, 「あらぁ、デートぉ？」 [V06 EPI p0008]. Asumi, already well into her drink, accepts the word as a taunt, 「そうよぉ、うらやましい？」 [V06 EPI p0011–0012]. When Sayaka switches from her usual 吉川さん to 元哉くん, Asumi challenges her: 「なによ、あんたに元哉くんなんて言う資格あるわけ？」 [V06 EPI p0013–0015]. Sayaka counters that Asumi is only his work partner; Asumi answers 「あたしたちはバディだもの、あたりまえじゃないの」, and Sayaka then insinuates that rank compels him to drink with her [V06 EPI p0017–0021]. She treats familiarity with Motoya as hers to police. Intoxication and the feud bound what this shows about affection (KCMP-V06-005).

**The V07 credit.** Late in the final evening, Motoya has passed out over drinks, 「そのうちに元哉の意識はなくなっていた」 [V07 EPI p0131], and comes to after eleven [V07 EPI p0132]. Sayaka, slurring, tells Asumi that Motoya likes gentle girls like Emiri; Asumi cackles that he has no use for women like the two of them, and they shake hands, 「酔った二人は楽しそうに握手している」 [V07 EPI p0135–0143]. Then Asumi says, with a serious face, 「吉川くん、いいとこいっぱいあるんだよ。あたし、こいつがいるから捜査やれるんだ」 [V07 EPI p0144–0145]. The line is addressed to Sayaka, about Motoya in the third person (こいつ), by a drunk speaker. Motoya overhears it; whether Asumi knew he was awake is not stated. His private vow follows, 「これからも自分は亜澄をサポートしてゆこう」 [V07 EPI p0148], and he blacks out again [V07 EPI p0149]. The serious face and the professional content support sincerity at the moment. The intoxicated setting limits projection to her later conduct, as the series' first epilogue does (KCMP-SERIES-003, KCMP-CHAR-007).

**Motoya's side** (narrated interiority), so that her conduct can be read against it:
- *Irritation and difficulty.* Her rank and her 上から目線 annoy him; she is 生意気; he finds her a hard woman to deal with, 「苦手な女」 [V01 C01 p0024; V02 C01 p0193; V05 C01 p0029].
- *Admiration.* He respects her brain and her intuition [V06 C02 p0533; V07 C01 p0429–0430]; 「すべては亜澄の功績だ」 [V07 C04 p0645]; 「あらためて、亜澄はたのもしい女だなと元哉は思った」 [V07 C04 p0655].
- *A mixed ledger.* 「気はきついが、素直でかわいらしいところもある」, alongside condescending, tiresome and 酒乱気味 [V05 C01 p0027–0030].
- *Remembered debt.* In V07 he remembers that she protected him, and reflects that he is now two years older and no longer the boy she rescued [V07 C03 p0798–0800].
- *Attraction.* One moment of pleasure at her drunken hug, which he curses, 「心地よく感じてしまった自分を元哉は呪った」 [V01 EPI p0067]. Otherwise no narrated attraction to her in seven volumes; his interior interest goes to other women (Ayane, Keiko, Echika, Sayaka, Emiri), and his appraisals of her looks are always qualified [V01 C01 p0024; V07 EPI p0019].

**Reading.** As Asumi enacts it, the relationship is senior-junior leadership laid over a childhood hierarchy of protector and protected, with teasing as its usual idiom. Her reliance on him is shown from V01 on, but she states it only once, drunk, to a third party, in V07.

### Police superiors and colleagues

- **Nikaidō (管理官).** In V01 he rebukes her uninvited interjection, then grants the lead she volunteers for [V01 C02 p0257–0276]. In V02 he hears her muttered sarcasm and she apologizes [V02 C01 p0195–0198]; later he is sour-faced when, after she takes Ayane's report to her own station rather than to his headquarters, the pair are lent to the fraud squad, a loan the 刑事課長 Ōno pushes through [V02 C03 p0524–0527]. In V03 he grudgingly lets her line continue [V03 C02 p0769–0775]. In V07 he adopts both her ideas [V07 C03 p0763–0792]. Toward him she is usually formal; she apologizes when caught; she steers him with tact, including a feigned-innocent face [V07 C03 p0782].
- **Senior officers generally.** Motoya, to Yura, says she says things she should not when the top brass are present, and the narration confirms 「この話は事実だ」 [V02 C02 p0095–0096].
- **Yoshida (her section chief).** She grumbles about him to Motoya [V05 C01 p0647], requests and gets the V06 workshop assignment from him [V06 C01 p0920–0925], and pre-clears skipping a morning assembly with him [V07 C04 p0341]. He calls her あいつ approvingly when recommending her local knowledge [V06 C01 p0220] and praises both detectives at the V07 arrest, 「小笠原、吉川、ご苦労だった」 [V07 C04 p0641].
- **Satake (管理官, V04).** She states her wish to him plainly, as Motoya never could [V04 C01 p0880–0885], frames her prospects modestly [V04 C01 p0907], and receives a bounded licence on the strength of her record [V04 C01 p0893].
- **Masaki (HQ squad chief).** In V05 he knows her as 「よく吉川と組んでるヤツ」 [V05 C01 p0691]. In V06 he calls her 「鎌倉署の切れ者」 while unable to recall her name; she answers with a straight back and a cheerful self-introduction, and Motoya reads her as pleased at 切れ者 [V06 C01 p0097–0102].
- **Watanabe (Kamakura forensic chief).** She is proud of him: 「うちの鑑識係長、ベテランですごいんだ」 [V01 C02 p0164]. With him she plays meek, 「いやだ。わたしおとなしいじゃないですかぁ」, and the narration calls it 猫をかぶっている [V05 C01 p0491–0492]. His view of the pair: 「いつも小笠原に引きずり回されている青年だな」 [V05 C01 p0482].
- **Nishina (junior).** She makes him her accomplice for the V01 memorial device [V01 C03 p0617–0618]. Introducing Motoya to him, she jokes 「そう、あたし性格悪い嘘つきなの。仁科くんも気をつけてね」 [V01 C02 p0087–0100].

### Sayaka Takigawa (V05–V07)

- **V05.** Asumi meets Motoya off duty with Sayaka and Emiri and pulls him away by the wrist: 「あなたに関係のないことです」; 「吉川は警察官です。事件を優先します」, baring her teeth [V05 C01 p0372–0377]. In the quarrel she calls freelance writing 「怪しい稼業」 [V05 C02 p0752] and suggests Sayaka spilled a drink on him deliberately, 「いやらしい」 [V05 C02 p0766]. In the epilogue she pretends not to know her and makes an あっかんべー at Motoya [V05 EPI p0029–0041].
- **V06.** The misnaming and her sharp correction [V06 C01 p0291–0295]. Hearing Sayaka's magazine assignment, she sneers 「へぇ、そんなヒマ記事を書いてるんですか」, and Sayaka retorts that only women like Asumi need such articles [V06 C01 p0314–0320]. She asks Sayaka's alibi with no grounds and is wrong-footed [V06 C01 p0488–0505]. She calls her 性悪 [V06 C03 p0329] and explains her hostility only as taste, 「人間には好き嫌いがあるんだよ」 [V06 C03 p0333]. The bar contest follows (above); Sayaka calls her 「奸譎な女」 and 「教養のない女」, and she turns crimson [V06 EPI p0026–0034].
- **V07.** 「なんであんたらがいるのよ」 and 「あたしは胸がムカムカしてくる」 at the restaurant [V07 C02 p0033–0042]; 「なんだ、またあんたたちか」 and 「新参者で悪かったね」 in the epilogue [V07 EPI p0009–0015]. Then the truce: 「そうかぁ、つまり、あんたとかあたしみたいな女には用はないってわけか」, and 「じゃあ握手だ」 [V07 EPI p0137–0141].

Hostility does not stop her using the two women as sources. In V07 she lets Sayaka and Emiri talk at length, and when Motoya says their talk proved useful she answers sharply that she let them talk on purpose [V07 C02 p0213–0216]. Sayaka provokes as much as she is provoked: she coins the "date" and makes the 元哉くん provocation in V06. In V07 it is Sayaka who says their feud is pointless, and Asumi who offers the handshake.

### Emiri Hatano (V05–V07)

- **V05.** Asumi introduces herself as Motoya's instructor and stares at Emiri [V05 C02 p0549]; she is stiff-faced when Emiri talks of art school [V05 C02 p0619–0627]; she asks a barbed alibi question [V05 C02 p0682–0689].
- **V06.** 「あの英美里って女は、なんでこんなに早く、あたしたちの目の前にしゃしゃり出てくるのよ」 [V06 C01 p0646]. She presents Emiri's information to the disciple Kume as coming from a friend, which Motoya calls brazen, 「どこまで、面の皮が厚い女なのだ」 [V06 C02 p0456–0459]. She sneers at the dawn visit, 「いい大人なのに付添が必要なわけ？」 [V06 C03 p0324]. Sayaka says Emiri is afraid of her [V06 C03 p0057]; Emiri herself speaks of fearing the police [V06 C03 p0134].
- **V07.** When Motoya says aloud 「波多野さんのお料理、いっぺん食べてみたいな」, Asumi turns away in displeasure [V07 C02 p0077–0080]; his further thought that eating it would be happiness is unspoken and not something she could hear [V07 C02 p0079]. She starts the applause at the engagement to Izumida, 「おめでとう！」 [V07 EPI p0105–0106], and laughs at Izumida's concealed alibi, 「そっか！　それなら言ってくれればいいのに」 [V07 EPI p0118–0119]. She is stern that Motoya concealed meeting Emiri [V07 EPI p0124–0128].

Her suspicion that Emiri was "pushing in" was half vindicated: in V06 Emiri had lied about her ties to the victim, though not about the crime. Toward Emiri the hostility is milder than toward Sayaka and partly investigative in form. By the V07 epilogue it has given way to applause.

### Elders, eminences and witnesses

- **Gamō (V01, magnate).** Humble formality, わたくしども [V01 C03 p0047]. She asks his consent before disclosing a will that may hurt him; the narration says she chooses her words carefully to obtain his permission [V01 C03 p1023–1024].
- **Ichiyō (V03, painter).** Mutual admiration. He wants to paint her eyes, 「賢く力強く輝くその瞳で真実を見抜こうとしている」 [V03 C01 p0797], and tells her that she is too straight, that he would like to paint her but would never fall in love with her; she grins and laughs 「うふふふ」 [V03 C03 p0027–0029]. Drunk afterwards, she praises his humility and composure [V03 EPI p0028]. She also uses his trust to elicit the overpainting hypothesis (see [cognition](#making-a-discrepancy-testable-by-others)).
- **Mibu (V02, old conductor).** Flustered by his flirting, she still steers back to the case, finds him fun, and leaves politely [V02 C03 p0207–0216].
- **Shichijō (V04, retired film star).** Kimono rapport [V04 C02 p0622–0645]; persistent questioning; sincere comfort after the confession. She is a different woman from the old actress Utsunomiya of the rank exchange.
- **Yakushiji (V06, master glass artist).** Reverent greeting, cool contradiction, and at the reveal the accusation of lying (see [address](#address-with-other-people-both-directions)).
- **Tomonaga (V07, restaurant owner).** A regular's warmth and plain register.
- **Torii (V01, V04, editor).** In V01 she trusts him with unreleased evidence [V01 C02 p0648]; in V04 she phones him as an old acquaintance.
- **Yura, Ayane, Maya, Emiri.** See [witness costs and repair](#witness-costs-and-repair).

### Handsome men (V01–V03)

Her softening is visible each time: before the magnate's grandson Hideyuki, 「素敵……」, eyes fixed [V01 C01 p0892–0899]; before the pianist Shiina, tongue-tied, 「目尻を下げてぼーっとしている」 [V02 C02 p0756]; before Ichiyō's grandson Haruto, a voice from the top of her head [V03 C01 p0278–0280]; before a young patrol officer, drunk, 「あたしの顔、覚えておいて。小笠原亜澄だよ」 [V03 EPI p0101].

The trait itself is Motoya's narratorial view: 「亜澄はイケメンに弱い」 [V01 C01 p0899]; 「この女はとかくイケメンに弱い」 [V03 C01 p0280]; 「相手がイケメンだとやに下がるのが亜澄の悪いクセだ」 [V03 EPI p0103]. She denies it: 「あの人はね、こころがイケメンなんだよ」 [V01 EPI p0044]; 「デレデレしたことなんて一回もないし」 [V03 C02 p0724]. It should be recorded as his recurring view, not as an established trait of hers (KCMP-CHAR-005).

The one diagnostic case cuts against the idea that attraction biases her judgment. In V01 she doubts that Hideyuki stole the manuscript, 「あの秀之さんが原稿盗むなんてちょっとピンとこない」, and Motoya mocks her, 「おまえ、イケメンに甘過ぎるんだよ」 [V01 C03 p0406–0408]. Her doubt proves right (KCMP-V01-008). The susceptibility never stops her suspecting or arresting a man: she traps Shiina, and she asks Haruto to write his number in her notebook rather than exchange phones, which Motoya guesses is because she does not want him to have hers [V03 C01 p0535–0541]. A whole-corpus search finds the narration's イケメン remarks about her only in V01–V03; from V04 the observed pattern shifts to her reactions to women around Motoya.

### Family

Pride in her father and the shop [V04 C02 p0628–0633]; empathy through him, when she mentions his talk of retiring to a witness [V05 C03 p0511]. She never mentions her mother; one scene of moistened eyes is tied to her only by Motoya's knowledge [V03 C02 p0575–0577]. One memory of her grandmother [V06 C02 p0443]. The text gives no scene with any family member.

## Preferences and ordinary life

"Repeated" means two or more independent occasions. A single choice is not a habit.

### Kamakura and 平塚

- **Kamakura enthusiasm, repeated across V01–V07.** 「あたし、むかしから鎌倉ファンなんだよ」 [V02 C01 p0269]; she visited often before her transfer [V04 C01 p0073]. She explains it as longing for a world unlike the one she grew up in: 「自分の育った環境とすごく違うから憧れるんだよね」 [V02 C02 p0487]; 「なにもかもが違うから鎌倉に惹かれるんだと思うな」 [V03 C01 p0697]. She lectures fluently on local history and topography in every volume: villas, 谷戸 and the literary residents of the 1930s (V02); reservoirs and 極楽寺 (V03); the 旧華頂宮邸 (V04); the coast at 七里ヶ浜, where she prefers the 七里ヶ浜 side (V05) [V05 C02 p0015]; the seven paths up 源氏山 (V06); the lanes of 長谷 (V07). She regrets how fast favourite shops close: 「気に入っているお店とかがなくなると淋しいよ」 [V04 C01 p0061]. With her, Motoya never needs a map [V04 C01 p0071; V06 C01 p0172]. Familiar places for food and walking affect what she can offer a colleague as well as what she knows about a locality.
- **平塚, repeated.** 「あの街が大好きだよ」 [V03 C01 p0697]; unpretentious, its people cheerful and friendly [V03 C02 p0040]. She shares hometown banter with Motoya [V01 C01 p0990–1002; V03 C02 p0019–0041].
- **Wishes, not habits.** To stay once at a famous seaside hotel, though it is expensive [V02 C01 p0260]; to eat once at the soba house 《松原庵》 [V02 C01 p0264]; to see the 江の島 lantern festival, 「今年こそ見にいきたいです」 [V07 C01 p0451].
- **Scenery over routine: the 稲村ヶ崎 sunset (the V01 title scene).** On the 江ノ電 she announces that sunset is about seven [V01 C03 p0444] and proposes 「稲村ヶ崎の落日見に行こうよ」 [V01 C03 p0448]. When Motoya objects that the headquarters meeting is at eight, she says it does not matter if they are late [V01 C03 p0455–0457]. At the summit his first reply is curt; she demands 「もっとちゃんと言って」, and when he admits it is beautiful she grades him, 「そう、よくできました」 [V01 C03 p0477–0482]. The narration: for a while the two of them gaze at the 稲村ヶ崎 sunset, which neither had seen before [V01 C03 p0484]. She then uses the summit's arbour for a private and unusually diffident exposition of her theory [V01 C03 p0485–0527].

- **Colour and kimono.** A childhood and adolescent study that she calls only a hobby [V03 C03 p0178]. It is decisive in V03, social in V04, and displayed in V06. To Shichijō she says she has long wanted to become 「紬が似合うような大人」 [V04 C02 p0622].

- **Fiction and film.** She watched a film adaptation as a fan of the actress [V01 C01 p0043]; read a novel and cried at its ending [V01 C01 p0798]; watched an old film on DVD as preparation and liked it very much [V04 C01 p0249]. - **Being moved by art and stories.** Near tears at 『極楽寺逍遙』 [V03 C02 p0618]; tears at a novel [V01 C01 p0798] and an old film [V04 C01 p0920–0922].

Being lied to; indoor work [V03 C02 p0246]; Sayaka, "likes and dislikes" [V06 C03 p0333]; hospitals (the V01 fear, "much better"). Whether she dislikes tourist pricing is only a weak inference: her own remark is that not every shop is touristy, and the complaint is Motoya's.

## Romantic and intimate psychology (as evidenced, never extrapolated)

**What the text establishes.**
- **No partner** at any point. Motoya knows she has no boyfriend [V01 C01 p0780]; her own line is 「あたしはいい男をセレクト中だもん」 [V01 C01 p0781].
- **One past relationship, a drunk self-report.** Late in her student days she dated a good-looking supermarket manager about seven years older, who went back to a dependent former girlfriend and told Asumi 「おまえは強いから一人でも大丈夫だろ」 [V03 EPI p0074]. She is still angry, and shouts at Motoya to listen seriously [V03 EPI p0078].
- **Longing, drunk.** 「あたしだってね。本気で人を好きになりたいよ」, followed by a hug [V01 EPI p0064–0065], while the background song 「むなしく響き続けていた」 [V01 EPI p0075]. Laments that good men are taken by good women, and that there are no good men [V01 EPI p0036, p0055–0058; V02 EPI p0047, p0055]. These evenings supply no specific addressee for the desire.
- **Weak for handsome men** in V01–V03, by Motoya's narratorial view, which she denies. She gushes to Motoya over a declaration of love she witnessed, 「『はい、わたしは麻里奈を愛しています』」 [V01 EPI p0048].
- **The "frail woman" theory.** Sober, on a bus after meeting the beautiful Keiko, she says 「本当にかわいそうな女は他人に甘えられない人なんだよ」, 変につよい調子で, and calls it advice for him [V03 C02 p0718–0721]. Her account of being treated as someone who can manage without care is therefore an affectively charged self-report made largely while drunk, and Motoya's reluctance to engage with it is a distinct observation about the relationship [V03 EPI p0041–0080]. Drunk again in V04, the lookism grievance [V04 EPI p0092–0105].

**Competing readings.**
1. *Attachment or unacknowledged romance.* The V04–V07 sequence consistently targets women who show interest in Motoya or in whom he shows interest. The narration's V06 recap says she suspects a relationship with Sayaka [V06 C01 p0297], though that is Motoya's view. In V06 she claims a special status (バディ; the right to 元哉くん). The V07 truce line, accepting that she and Sayaka were in effect rivals for a man who prefers gentle girls, is the most romance-compatible statement in the corpus.
2. *Proprietorial partnership.* She guards a working relationship and her senior position, and dislikes outsiders who distract her partner and pull information through him. The professional complaint is explicit: "are you leaking investigation information?", 「あんた、捜査情報を漏らしてんじゃないでしょうね？」 [V06 C01 p0650]; "why tell only you?"; "she should tell me directly" [V06 C03 p0315, p0320]. She also uses the two women as sources while disliking them [V07 C02 p0215]. Her one serious statement about him is professional.
3. *Personality clash.* She dislikes Sayaka, by her own account, 「人間には好き嫌いがあるんだよ」, and the narration takes that as the clear reason, 「まさにこれがはっきりした理由だろう」 [V06 C03 p0333–0334]. Sayaka provokes deliberately. The hostility toward Emiri is mild by comparison, partly investigative (Emiri had lied in V06), and fades in V07 (KCMP-V06-006).
4. *Drunk banter.* Every intimate-sounding line is drunk or adversarial (the V01 hug; V06 "date" and "buddies"; the V07 truce), or immediately retracted (V01 "couple play").

**Status.** Romantic intent toward Motoya is **not established** and **not excluded**; it is open at the series' end. Motoya shows no narrated romantic interest in her after V01's disowned moment; for his direction, the counterevidence is affirmative, since his interior attention visibly goes elsewhere (KCMP-CHAR-009). Nothing about her intimate preferences beyond these statements is evidenced, and nothing further should be supplied.

## Capabilities and limitations

### Demonstrated capabilities

- **Investigative intuition with follow-through.** She solves the case in all seven volumes. Motoya, who often doubts her hunches at first, repeatedly credits them [V06 C02 p0533; V07 C01 p0429–0430; V07 C04 p0645].
- **Interviewing and interrogation.** Calibrated soft and hard registers, patience, a still face, correct cautions [V01 C03 p0664–0666; V02 C03 p0695–0702].
- **Memory.** Briefings without notes [V01 C02 p0116]; reconstruction of an unrecorded interview [V02 C03 p0390].
- **Local knowledge and navigation** [V04 C01 p0071; V06 C01 p0140–0172].
- **Visual discrimination.** Colour, fabric, dye and jewellery [V03 C03 p0155–0165; V04 C01 p0534–0538; V04 C02 p0622–0642]. Ichiyō confirms her colour reading, 「君は正しい」 [V03 C03 p0163].
- **Legal knowledge**, with the V01 tactical falsehood as a counterpoint.
- **Physical skill.** She holds the arrest-technique grade [V03 EPI p0066]. In V02 she dodges the fleeing Ishikawa's punch, twists his wrist, cuffs him one-handed and pronounces the arrest, 「石川宗佑、公務執行妨害罪で現行犯逮捕する」 [V02 C03 p0593–0610]. Judo is Motoya's claim only [V03 EPI p0065].
- **Institutional craft, uneven.** She usually obtains sanction for her lines, reports concisely, frames requests as gaps [V07 C03 p0754–0757] and pre-clears absences [V07 C04 p0341]. Against this: she blurts what she should not before senior officers [V02 C02 p0095–0096]; she mutters sarcasm at a meeting [V02 C01 p0190]; she takes a lead to her own station rather than to the headquarters, which costs the headquarters two investigators for a time [V02 C03 p0524–0527]; she dismisses a meeting as not worth arriving on time for [V01 C03 p0457]. The craft is real but not constant, and its reception depends on the commander.
- **Social disarming.** Motoya attributes the good treatment they often receive from interviewees to her amiability, youthful looks and un-detective-like appearance, and thinks he may owe her thanks for it [V03 C01 p0294–0301].

### Limitations, self-reported or shown

- **English.** 「わたしは英語はダメなので、外国人対応には回されないんですよ」 [V07 C03 p0517].
- **Gaps in cultural knowledge.** History before her generation (V04 C01); classical music (V02); ceramics, 「わたしなんにも知らなくて」 [V01 C03 p0827]; art, by her own modest account (V03 C01); 『宴のあと』; Fellini (V07).
- **Emotional legibility.** Motoya counts it a professional weakness, telling Yura 「でも、警察官としては失格なんですよ」; she answers 「えへへ」 and then styles herself, with some exaggeration, 「同僚からも失格と言われるわたし」 [V02 C02 p0103–0108]. Interviewees sometimes trust her for that very candour, as Yura does [V02 C02 p0101–0113].
- **Animus leaking into work** in V05–V06.
- **Susceptibility to charm**, V01–V03, by Motoya's view, without proven effect on her judgment.
- **Holding drink.** Poor, by the narration's repeated account.

**Capacity and willingness.** She can be meticulously correct, in cautions, consent and legal limits, and chooses not to be when she judges that a lie or a stunt will work. Her willingness to deceive is a choice governed by her credo, not a gap in knowledge. In V01 Motoya takes her claimed legal ignorance for an act [V01 C03 p0283].

## Development and late revelation, volume by volume

**Ground rule.** Every biographical fact about Asumi that first appears in a later volume is the late revelation of a prior condition: her mother's death, her apartment, the three-generation shop, the student romance, her father's costume work, the Nordic furnishings, the school names. These are not development. What can change across volumes is her observed behaviour. Because her inner life is never narrated, even behavioural change cannot be proved to be inner development. It may reflect a change of situation, of narrative emphasis, or of what Motoya happens to notice.

### V01 稲村ヶ崎の落日 (May–June 2022): baseline

- **Situation.** One month at Kamakura [V01 C01 p0498].
- **Revealed.** Her family, the shop and the family religion [V01 C01 p0794, p0824]; the childhood rescue of Motoya [V01 C01 p0825–0830]; her Atsugi past [V01 C01 p0569] and surfing [V01 C02 p0270]; the fear of hospitals [V01 C01 p0740]; that she has no boyfriend [V01 C01 p0780].
- **Conduct.** She persists after the suicide ruling: reads the dead novelist's book, attends his funeral on her day off, then pursues a linked murder [V01 C01 p0790–0835; V01 C02 p0205–0221]. She breaks meeting etiquette to win a lead [V01 C02 p0257–0278]. She makes a false legal claim to Father Carpentier and stages Ashina's synthesized voice at his memorial mass to break Marina [V01 C03 p0273–0283, p0587–0629]. She proposes arriving late to a meeting to watch the sunset [V01 C03 p0444–0484]. She gives a model caution [V01 C03 p0664] and seeks Gamō's consent before a disclosure [V01 C03 p1023–1024]. ### Stable and changing

The evidence sustains a particular kind of continuity. Asumi's willingness to ask for more persists from the first book; the person who asks and the conditions under which others will answer vary. The V06 claim of being buddies, made drunk and in front of Sayaka, still draws on seniority and familiarity. In the V07 epilogue her statement 「こいつがいるから」 [V07 EPI p0144] grounds her effectiveness in what Motoya supplies. It is direct evidence about her estimate of their work, qualified by the drinking and by its audience. The short scene supplies little access to sustained private feeling, and any extension into later conduct would require new evidence.

## Interpretive uncertainty

1. **Three global readings of the person.** (a) *The confident professional*: the drunk grievances are drink-talk, and her identity is the work she likes herself in [V05 C02 p0625]. (b) *Confidence over an unprotected core*: the student-days rejection, the lifelong role as the strong one, perhaps her mother's loss, together producing the recurring drunk complaint of being left alone; this is *speculative* beyond what she says. (c) *Series convention*: the drunk coda, the summons and the handsome-man gag are recurring comic set pieces, and psychologizing them over-reads genre machinery. The evidence supports elements of all three. Reading (b) has the most texture, but the text never connects her history to her present conduct.
2. **The hospital fear.** Its cause is never disclosed [V01 C01 p0740–0745]. Motoya's teasing guess (a serious injury) is rejected only by her expression. A link to her mother's illness is pure speculation; the text never makes it. V04 alone shows her willingly proposing a hospital visit [V04 C02 p0295].
3. **Hostility to women around Motoya.** Jealousy, proprietorship, personality clash and investigative suspicion compete. Her own account is "likes and dislikes", which Motoya accepts in a hedged sentence [V06 C03 p0333–0334]; his earlier reading is long-held hostility [V05 C02 p0844]; the recap narration is that she suspects a relationship [V06 C01 p0297]. None of these is from inside her (KCMP-V06-006).
4. **The ethics of her methods.** The narration frames her deceptions as effective, and the outcomes vindicate them. The counter-voices are present but muted: Motoya's alarm [V01 C03 p0274; V01 C03 p0970] and his doubts about her questioning [V03 C03 p0270; V05 C03 p0926–0927]; the priest's anger [V01 C03 p0277–0278]; Ayane's 「卑劣だわ」 [V02 C03 p0376]. Her handling of confidences belongs here: she promises discretion conditionally (V02, V04, and V06 with a testimony caveat) or without conditions (V03, V05) and later uses what she was told in V03, V05 and V06, in V05 and V06 turning it against its source; whether the V05 use falls within Enomoto's later condition is a judgment the text leaves open. Her V01 disclosure of Ashina's diagnosis after promising Ōura discretion is not judged by the text either. Her apologies address disorder, not lies or confidences. A reader can see her as an admirable investigator or an ethically loose one; the text leans to the first without excluding the second.
5. **Development or variation.** The address and behaviour shifts may reflect changes in her, in the cases' social configurations (Sayaka and Emiri enter only in V05), or in Motoya's attention.
6. **Motoya as evaluator.** He is wrong about cases repeatedly and holds gendered expectations ("cuter", "cute if she kept quiet"). His positive judgments of her intelligence are corroborated by outcomes; his negative judgments of her manner are his own.
7. **The weight of drunk speech.** The V07 credit is said seriously, but drunk, to Sayaka, about Motoya; he hears it after coming to; whether she knew he was awake is **unknown** [V07 EPI p0131–0146]. It is the best evidence of her regard for him and it is not conclusive. The V01 and V02 epilogue appeals and the V03 account of denied care carry the same discount for drink.
8. **Gaps the source never fills.** Her education beyond being a student around 2014; her career between joining the police and 2022, beyond the Atsugi theft squad and a headquarters posting; friends outside work; hobbies beyond Kamakura walks, food, drink, films and crafts; her relationship with her father beyond pride; siblings; ambitions in rank; what she thinks of Motoya's failure in the examination.
9. **A slip in her own reconstruction.** Her V05 dating of the victim's marriage contradicts three witnesses; whether this is characterization or an authorial slip is unresolved (KCMP-V05-005).
10. **A source slip.** At [V05 C02 p0862–0863] a line plainly spoken by Motoya is followed by a tag attributing an exasperated voice to 亜澄. It is not used as evidence here.

**Nature of the change.** Version 2.0 is a general revision of the v1.0 study (Git blob `fcce3dbf50210b83521d150de62fde386fdb352c`), written in place to the self-contained character monograph standard (initiation policy §5.5.1).
