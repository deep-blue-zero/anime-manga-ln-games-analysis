# Character and relationship ledger

**Analytical rows at initialization: 0.** Name and bind an actor only when supported at the active disclosure boundary. Preserve aliases, role labels and unresolved identities with their respective horizons. Use stable IDs of the form `KSOA-Vnn-{kind}-NNNN`, with Vnn keyed to first observation. If evidence later establishes shared or distinct identity, append a crosswalk with its own disclosure horizon and retain the original records.

All rows carry generation, ID, valid story interval or scene, source horizon and locator/evidence IDs, predecessor when relevant, observation/inference type, support and counterevidence or limits. Record whether a difference reflects development, knowledge, role, relationship, situational pressure, a condition disclosed later, or an unresolved cause.

| Record | Additional fields |
|---|---|
| Actor identity `PERSON` | Names and readings as attested, disclosed role(s), case associations, ambiguity, and crosswalks to any later verified alias or separate identity |
| Actor state `STATE` | Actor, observed conduct, presented interiority and viewpoint, self-report or reports by others, appraisal/affect, available alternatives, situational or role constraints, motivation if inferred, prior state and consequence |
| Language sample `VOICE` | Speaker, addressee/audience, situation, intended or observed speech act, Japanese register features, exact locator, quote status and any translation-sensitive uncertainty |
| Directed relationship `REL` | Source actor, target actor, prior edge, event/trigger, observed conduct, attributed and inferred attitudes, familiarity, emotional intimacy, operational trust, interpersonal trust, cooperation, obligation, conflict and attachment where supported, uncertainty and consequence |

Attach ordinary life, workplace routine and stress contexts to each sample. Confidence in a stable disposition requires recurrence across appropriate situations or strong affirmative evidence, with alternatives and limits. Separate outward expression from internal intention or affect. A case-local actor's choices, motives and causal effects can remain substantial even when no further appearances occur. Relationship direction and conditions matter. Record reverse edges only with their own evidence, and label absence of evidence for change as a gap unless the scene offered a diagnostic opportunity.

## Longitudinal use

Append deltas with their evidence and preserve earlier states. An earlier disposition may remain provisional even after a later state is well attested. A newly disclosed condition has an earlier valid time only as supported by the source, with its later disclosure plainly recorded. A retrospective update can inform a monograph without changing a model built from the earlier prospective horizon. Select rows through the relevant horizon and use their linked passages and disconfirming examples in the [reconstruction specification](KSOA_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_SPEC.md).
