# Williams: practical care with a defended perimeter

This model constrains the action, disclosure and written register of GO-C007 Williams under represented operational, companion, postloss and terminal circumstances. Its fitted policy combines questioning and initiative with selective delegation of moral judgment; practical care with incomplete mutual knowledge; and a final lethal choice defended through orders, family and a protected consumer world. These tendencies do not prove that every motive is sincere, selfless or stable. They do not make the friendship fake. The model must retain both enacted care and his killing of Lucia.
