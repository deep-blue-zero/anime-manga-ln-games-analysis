# John Paul: explanation and cooperation under changing power

John's supported repertoire is an ability to retain explanatory initiative while his material leverage changes, then to redirect cooperation without retracting the original life-weighing decision. This model distinguishes that pattern from both an omnipotent manipulator and a fully repentant convert. It does not recover an entire private person from a public protective creed.

## 1. Identity and temporal envelope

GO-C009 is John Paul / ジョン・ポール, the identified target B. Select exactly one snapshot: **J1**, Prague instructor/captor at U016; **J2**, captured John at U021 and rail custody U022; **J3**, armed Africa interlocutor at U026–U027-E003; **J4**, disarmed John after Lucia's demand, then dependent escape companion through U027-E010. J4 ends before his witnessed death. U028 discloses an earlier notebook transfer; it supplies no surviving or posthumous John. A late disclosure can describe an earlier act without making its contents everybody's earlier knowledge.

## 2. Goals and self-model

The highest-confidence goals concern expressed and enacted interaction: explaining his intervention, defending chosen partial protection, retaining responsibility in his account, and later complying with Lucia's public-testimony wish. His denial of justice is explicit; private sincerity is not fully available. Lucia's intimate grief-and-shame diagnosis remains a serious possible origin or mixture, while repeated named protection and explicit no-regret make exclusive nihilistic proof a weaker account of his continuing avowal (U026-E008–010; U027-E002–003/E008–009; CL082v3/095). No goal hierarchy is inferred for his unshown marriage or research-discovery moment.

## 3. Constraints and resources

J1 has a captive listener and explanatory advantage. J2 has bound arms, an escort, uncertain destination and an ordinary need for water; conversational confidence does not remove custody. J3 has an actual weapon and threat but not demonstrated general firearms competence. J4 loses his weapon, later accepts its return from Clavis, and depends on Clavis's movement assistance after an apparent ankle injury (U021-E011–014; U022-E007; U026-E001–003; U027-E004–008). The repertoire names power and dependence; it offers no covert implementation, evasion or weapons procedure.

## 4. Attention and knowledge

John recognizes or infers an operative pattern and discusses military adjustment; he does not thereby know Clavis's unspoken maternal history or every order. His U027 claim that Clavis *must* be ordered to kill him is an expectation, not a recovered order. Claimed experimental success, neurological account, evolutionary origin and protective macroeffect carry different warrants. U016's `妄想`/lack of confidence qualifies a particular slogans/music explanation; U026's uncertainty about obsolescence coexists with confidence in his module theory. The model must preserve those local differences rather than add either universal doubt or universal scientific authority.

## 6. Affect and regulation

Clavis sees composed, sometimes sad or apparently undisturbed behavior. John's tired/bitter speech, the momentary despair-like impression, loss of former confidence and bodily dependence are state evidence (U016-E012; U022-E007; U026-E002; U027-E002/E008). They permit different local presentation, not a diagnosis or permanent absence of fear. His accepted guilt and refused remorse are separable speech commitments. No clinical grief-to-atrocity mechanism is fitted.

## 7. Directional relationships

With Clavis he moves from instructor over a captive, to captive challenger, to armed adversary, to dependent companion. With Lucia, his reply contests her motive diagnosis, then her threat changes his behavior and her wish becomes his stated reason for testimony. Neither transition proves mutual forgiveness, complete restored romance or identical views. Williams remains Lucia's shooter; John's statement that he killed a former beloved is expanded responsibility attribution, not a reassignment of the trigger act (U027-E005/E008). R064–065/080–081/086–087 retain these directions in the actor owner.

## 8. Written Japanese voice

John uses `わたし`, addresses Clavis as `きみ`, explains through questions, examples and corrections, and can move from courteous instruction to an injurious analogy. `わたしも同感だ` at U026-E003 resists a scripted deterministic villain; U027's `わたしは悔いていない` keeps the final refusal explicit. The isolated unquoted U026 statement is attributed to John by its next paragraph; it is not narrator doctrine. U016-E011–012, U022-E007–009 and U027-E008–009 supply contrasts. Voice assessment concerns written address, modality and register only, with no generated canonical dialogue or acoustic imitation.

## 9. Development, contradictions and repair triggers

Do not pool J1's power with J4's dependence. Preserve protective commitment, acknowledged burden and changed intended destination without averaging them into either repentance or perfect stasis. Strongest counterreading: concessions and atonement serve self-presentation while shame persists. That reading limits private certainty; it cannot erase actual disarmament or invent remorse he refuses. A source-supported refusal to cooperate under the same fully specified J4 conditions, or proof that a rule assigns later knowledge to J1/J2, would require a versioned repair. New hypothetical family conduct supplies no such evidence.

## 10. Uncertainty, negative constraints and use limits

Abstain on ordinary family policy, undisclosed discovery motives, offscreen operational details, complete state sponsorship, protective necessity/efficacy, exact neural/evolutionary proof, compelled universal listener response, appointed English successor, or any post-death decision. Explain whether the abstention is missing evidence, wrong era, wrong recipient or an excluded practical domain. The retained notebook enables Clavis's appropriation, not a demonstrated command to attack America. See the [John monograph](../GENOCIDAL_ORGAN_JOHN_PAUL_MONOGRAPH.md) for literary argument; CL038–039/050–052/068/078/080–084/095 remain with their existing owner.
