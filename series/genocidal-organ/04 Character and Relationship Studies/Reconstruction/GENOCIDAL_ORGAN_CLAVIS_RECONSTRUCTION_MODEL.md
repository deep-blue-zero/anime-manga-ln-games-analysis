# Clavis Shepherd: a time-bounded action policy

This model returns constrained ranges of Clavis's actions, disclosures and written registers under specified circumstances. It does not return a complete private mind, a moral verdict, new canonical dialogue or real-world operational advice. Its fitted proposition is that Clavis can exercise substantial discretion while withholding information and wanting someone else's judgment to certify ownership of his acts. That proposition allows care, hesitation, obedience, cruelty, genuine revision and deliberate harmful authorship under different conditions. It forbids the shortcut from acknowledged guilt to improved conduct.

The G2 designation MULTICONTEXT_CANDIDATE supplies eligibility, and GO-CL091 supplies a literary dependency; neither is a validation result. Every rule below is a model-local inference fitted retrospectively from specified evidence. Rule labels C01–C13 are internal addresses, not new GO claims or relationship IDs. The complete v1 bytes and the evaluation input contract are frozen by an external SHA-256 manifest; the file contains no self-hash. Validation was NOT_PERFORMED at the dated construction freeze. Exact v1 bytes, including this construction record, stay unchanged after testing. Current independent acceptance, errors, abstentions and validation state belong to `GENOCIDAL_ORGAN_G5_VALIDATION_AND_ERROR_ANALYSIS.md` in this directory and the existing actor/readiness owner, keyed to this model's exact full-file SHA-256. Any repair creates a new version with explicit changes and affected independent reruns.

## 2. Goals, self-model, constraints and attention

## 5. Written Japanese register constraints

This is a written-register filter, not a dialogue generator or an acoustic model. The frequent narrative ぼく, technical/ruby vocabulary, film reference and long analytic explanation belong to retrospective prose; do not put them automatically into immediate speech. Direct dialogue has local variation: C-S to Williams can be clipped, teasing or skeptical (U002-E011; U005-E015 uses 俺 in dialogue; U021-E003), whereas C-S as cover student uses polite questions to Lucia (U008-E002/E008). C-B asks the doctor for an answer and declines counseling with 結構です (U014-E010/E012). C-S leader gives compact imperatives/checks (U021-E003/E008–009). C-F's actual self-identification states full name/rank, and after Lucia's killing the exchange moves from familiar お前 to hostile 貴様 (U027-E004/E005–006). These are recipient- and event-specific forms, not interchangeable signatures.

Short diagnostic sequences also distinguish narrative functions: 想像してから、殺す is professional self-description (U002-E004–005); 罪から逃れたいのではない and the wanted accusation are unquoted inner narration (U019-E011–012); いや repairs a claim about past choice (U025-E003); repeated ことにした claims the epilogue decision (U028-E009). They must not be emitted as universal spoken catchphrases. The bedside はい is institutionally effective assent in the recalled procedure (U002-E008), not proof of certainty or a general response style. No accent, timbre, actual prosody, English translation cadence or unshown linguistic fluency is inferred.

Required input, supplied as a record or equivalent explicit prose:


Scene/disclosure cutoffs must identify the event within a unit, not merely “after U027.” A fact stipulated in a counterfactual is marked STIPULATED, and the evaluator must assess whether changing it remains within the rule's domain. Absent recipient, state or consequential knowledge yields ABSTAIN with the missing field. “UNKNOWN” is never filled by what later narration tells the reader.

Execution order is fixed: (1) identity/source/scope check; (2) temporal and knowledge admissibility; (3) bodily/order/recipient gating; (4) select applicable rules; (5) resolve by the most specific later **available** event transition, never by later analyst knowledge; (6) return only the supported intersection/range. A rule with an unsatisfied premise is inactive. If two equally applicable rules require incompatible actions and no stated discriminator resolves them, return PARTIAL or ABSTAIN for the contested choice, keeping the conflict visible. Do not add generic personality to break a tie. C13 never overrides C01–C12 outside C-R. A knowledge violation is a material failure even if the resulting action resembles the source outcome.

Required output:

```text
case_id / model_id / version;
admissibility = SUPPORTED | PARTIAL | ABSTAIN;
state_and_cutoff_used; rules_applied;
preferred_supported_range; secondary_range_with_conditions;
excluded_or_unlicensed_actions;
knowledge_and_attribution_guard;
confidence = HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW, with scope-specific reason;
discriminating_observations_or_missing_input;
evidence_ids; source_route;
register_constraints [only if requested].
```

PARTIAL means a named dimension is supported and another is expressly withheld; it cannot conceal a confident unsupported action. BOUNDED_COUNTERFACTUAL outcomes are expectations, not invented source events. Do not produce exact probabilities, new canonical dialogue, unobserved other minds, hidden command documents, future causal success, cross-era knowledge or real coercive/violence instructions. Prefer action categories and disclosure limits over scene choreography.

Confidence calibration is qualitative: HIGH for exact observable/reported source distinctions or repeated narrowly matched patterns; MEDIUM for a close variation preserving era, recipient, stakes and constraint; LOW for an analogy too weak to decide, normally PARTIAL/ABSTAIN. Known source replay must identify the actual outcome separately from the model prediction range. A correct abstention is an outcome, and a hard identity/knowledge/continuity error fails the affected scope. Preserve all material failures. Repairs require a new immutable version, change history and affected independent reruns.
