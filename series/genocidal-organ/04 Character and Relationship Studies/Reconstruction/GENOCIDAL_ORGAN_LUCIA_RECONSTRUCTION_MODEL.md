# Lucia Schkroup: expertise, boundaries and a public obligation

Lucia's model connects actual acts of address while retaining their different informational grounds. A teacher's correction, a negotiated disclosure and an armed demand for public judgment cannot become one timeless response of an infallible conscience. The model's useful commitment is conditional: she can maintain a relationship while correcting its terms, and later acts to transfer a private lethal encounter into accountable public custody. Clavis's desire for her judgment supplies neither her motive nor her verdict.

## 1. Identity and temporal envelope

GO-C028 is Lucia Schkroup / ルツィア・シュクロウプ. **L1** is the Prague teacher/host addressing an apparent student called Bishop (U008–009); **L2** is her chosen outing, club affiliation, confession and maternal discussion (U011–015); **L3** is U017 captivity, with separate cutoffs before and after the U017-E008 operative disclosure; **L4** is the witnessed Africa accusation and public-arrest intervention (U026–027-E004). In L3, witnessed pain/own surveillance become available before the full operative disclosure; do not pool those moments. No continuous private policy is fitted across the unshown Prague–Africa interval. The title Madam Paul and a husband's mention in U025 do not verify legal marriage. The model ends at her actual death, not an imagined later judgment.

## 2. Goals and self-model

Professional communication, control over personal disclosure, understanding responsibility and a public duty to know are supported through her choices. She chooses a club and interlocutor for testimony, calls the place safe, speaks of her own guilt, rejects genes/memes as an absolving warrant and later demands public accounting (U013-E001–004; U015-E003–007; U027-E004). These do not establish an exhaustive hierarchy or a secret preplanned prosecution. Her longing, guilt and fear in the affair history are her testimony. Clavis's longing and preferred image of her remain his.

## 3. Constraints and resources

L1 has linguistic expertise, a teaching livelihood and home hospitality, but an authenticated false identity conceals the visitor's mission. L2 has social affiliations and choice of place/listener; its privacy is claimed rather than guaranteed. L3 can plead, challenge Lucius and ask for help but does not control the captors. L4 has an immediate weapon threat and succeeds in obtaining disarmament and Clavis's assent, without a proven safe trial route or general combat capability. The frozen U015 ending leaves her immobilized before John; urged flight is not a shown escape (U015-E008–012). Agency and physical constraint coexist.

## 4. Attention and knowledge

Her early teaching and confession do not imply knowledge of Clavis's military acts. His edited maternal narrative and unspoken appeals for punishment remain outside what she was told (U015-E001–003). She says she does not know John's classified research contents; her team/wife estimates are qualified testimony (U009-E013; U011-E012–016). L3 adds operative/surveillance exposure. U025's imagined apology is Clavis's thought; L4's spoken accusation establishes awareness then, not its onset. Actual-name receipt follows her U027 question; do not give L1 the name Clavis.

## 6. Affect and regulation

Clavis observes enjoyment in teaching, while its sincerity is his interpretation. Her confession reports happiness that submerged guilt, then conflicting desire, shame and fear. Her witnessed distress in captivity and tears during accusation coexist with a controlled demand at U027 (U008-E004/E008–009; U013-E003–004; U017-E006; U026-E008–010; U027-E004). These are distinguishable local states, not evidence of emotional instability, total self-command or a diagnosis. The model must not turn tears into incapacity or calm address into general weapons competence.

## 7. Directional relationships

Lucia is teacher/host toward Bishop, participant in her own club network, former lover and accuser toward John, and petitioner/critic toward Lucius. Her later actual-name question and Clavis-directed arrest request change the basis of address. Her concern after deception is real behavior; its private meaning remains open. John's response, Clavis's felt rescue and his desired forgiveness are separate directional outcomes, not reciprocal confirmation. Tyron's missing-her feeling is Lucius's report. No verdict from Lucia can be recovered after Williams kills her.

## 8. Written Japanese voice

The source distinguishes customer politeness, conversational `わたし` and `あなた`, question/correction, `わ`/`の`/`ね` endings, self-deprecating wordplay, chosen first-person testimony and later direct imperative. U008-E004/E008–012 shows the register shift, U009-E012 the intrusive-question challenge, and U027-E004 the purposeful move from `ビショップ` to `クラヴィス` after his answer. `知る責任` is a public obligation, not a promise to pardon him. U013's unquoted testimony remains hers through its framing; U025's unspoken apology remains his. No acoustic timbre, invented canonical utterance or imitated performance is provided.

## 9. Development, contradictions and repair triggers

The model permits coherent responsibility talk without making every claim correct. Her initial consolation overreaches; her biological/free-choice accounts are not demonstrated theorems; her diagnosis of an exclusive continuing motive is less well supported than John's repeated protective avowal, while remaining possible origin/mixed motive. Her final threat uses coercion to seek public judgment. These countercases constrain idealization. A correct repair would narrow the affected rule if source showed recipient/knowledge mismatch; it would not erase her actual interventions because a private intention is uncertain. The missing interval remains missing.

## 10. Uncertainty, negative constraints and use limits

Abstain on reciprocal romance, marital status, earlier complicity/onset, unshown travel/reunion choices, broad combat behavior, Clavis's pardon, universal anti-surveillance or nonviolent ideology, private maternal knowledge and posthumous endorsement of either man's later acts. A literary account can discuss her effect without simulating those gaps. CL031–032/034/040–047/078/082/084–085/096 remain with the existing claims owner; the [Lucia monograph](../GENOCIDAL_ORGAN_LUCIA_MONOGRAPH.md) supplies the broader literary argument.
