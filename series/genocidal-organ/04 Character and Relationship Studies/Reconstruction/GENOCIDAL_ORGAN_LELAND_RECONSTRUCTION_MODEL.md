# Leland: professional questions and bounded reports under pressure

Leland supports a conditional model of team communication across briefing, uncertain exposure and severe bodily injury. It is narrower than a doctrine for a universal ideal soldier. He can question a superior's framing, decline an ungrounded gamble and report what he knows while explicitly withholding what he cannot observe.

## 1. Identity and temporal envelope

GO-C013 is リーランド, addressed as BlueBoy / ブルーボーイ in the operational link. **B1** covers Part I team membership and consultation; **B2** the India briefing/preparation and operation; **B3** the rail attack while he remains alive, ending before Clavis explicitly addresses his corpse (U022-E017). The alias is a link address, not another person. No posthumous response is modeled; Clavis's final answer cannot be received by the dead Leland.

## 2. Goals and self-model

Supported priorities are accomplishing an assigned role, clarifying professional status and avoiding an unsupported risk estimate while maintaining communication. His questions about proxy/contractor status and John's custody are actual professional address (U018-E007/E010). A nearby social/religious question and the Moses simile supply limited wit, not a full ideology. No global loyalty ordering, ordinary home role, racial doctrine or comprehensive political view is fitted. Clavis's claim of shared room sentiment cannot supply each person's private goal.

## 3. Constraints and resources

Leland is one member of a hierarchy, with locally situated sight and communication. In B2 his position can constrain what route/risk he is willing to endorse. In B3 he is trapped, wounded, relying on bodily support and a communication link; knowing pain without its painful quality does not mean invulnerability (U021-E008; U022-E013–017). This model represents reply options, knowledge limits and willingness to question. It contains no assault plan, weapon use or medical intervention recipe.

## 4. Attention and knowledge

He distinguishes present access from inference. A reported engine explosion is qualified; he does not know the adjacent prisoner carriage's state, while nonresponse and medical status supply a narrower report about guards (U022-E013). His refusal to gamble on sniper numbers protects an unknown rather than guessing. The final repeated question about the outside remains unanswered before he dies. Clavis's consciousness/pain explanations supplement but cannot replace Leland's own report. Barry/Nelson coreference and the loose limb's ownership remain unresolved.

## 6. Affect and regulation

The source describes a surprisingly unchanged lively delivery while Leland reports injury, then weaker speech and a faint bitter smile before death (U022-E013–014/E017). Those observations support a changing capacity envelope, not complete private emotional access. His reported pain distinction is corroborative for local bodily technology, not proof that all feelings are removed. Religious/social wit and the earlier questioning matter as countercases to a merely mechanical team instrument.

## 7. Directional relationships

With Clavis he gives role/status replies and questions; Clavis's rank matters without eliminating Leland's judgment. With Rockwell he asks for mission clarification and receives correction. With Alex he asks about heaven; it does not authenticate Clavis's conjecture about habitual church attendance. Williams/Leland's reported shared laughter at Alex's jokes establishes received speech, not identical beliefs. The final failed bodily assistance belongs to Clavis's action and limited knowledge, not Leland's consent to a substitute limb (U004-E010; U005-E012; U018-E010; U022-E017).

## 8. Written Japanese voice

B2/B3 mix link compression, professional acknowledgments and colloquial first-person forms (`俺ら`, `ぼくら`, `俺`), with respectful endings toward Clavis. U021-E008's unwilling-to-gamble phrasing is dry, conditional professional resistance. U022-E013's `わかりません` marks an access limit; U022-E014 expressly separates `認識` from the quality of pain. The final repeated outside-status question is fragmented by failing capacity. The prose's voice descriptions are attributed narrator observations, not an audio track or permission to invent last words.

## 9. Development, contradictions and repair triggers

The record changes situations and material capacity rather than providing a full private development arc. Earlier readiness for dangerous work coexists with a particular refusal of an ungrounded gamble. B3's report narrows as the injury worsens; later corpse status cannot be backdated to the earlier live report. Any rule that confidently assigns the prisoners' fate, makes every joke a global worldview, or produces a reply after death fails this model's envelope and requires versioned repair.

## 10. Uncertainty, negative constraints and use limits

Abstain on home life, comprehensive doctrine, private religious commitment, general racial ideology, all unobserved actions, hidden final feelings, exact Barry/Nelson identity, limb ownership and post-death response. The viable model is a narrow professional communication repertoire, not a whole soldier. CL061/066/068/070 and the [relationship specialist](../../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/GENOCIDAL_ORGAN_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_RESPONSIBILITY.md) preserve pain/agency limits; no new monograph or discovery record follows automatically.
