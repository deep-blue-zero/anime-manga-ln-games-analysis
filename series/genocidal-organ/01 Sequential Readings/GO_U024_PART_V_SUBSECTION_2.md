# GO-U024 — returning the manufactured body to its lake

The narrator enters a landscape that supplies the flesh carrying him. A briefing calls the lake's conversion an industrial revival; Williams denies that it is a revival at all. The narrator's earlier search through a product's history reaches the factory and stops, while military tracking finds a target by looking for similarly interrupted human records. ## A future investigation inside a present descent

**これが最後のシーウィードになるだろうな** begins as the narrator's thought, retaining its conjectural **だろう** (E001). The next paragraph supplies a different kind of knowledge: this mission occurs three months before the scandal that draws him, Rockwell, the retired Senate leader and other officials into twelve investigative committees. The count includes individual operations and incidents. It does not mean twelve committees devoted solely to John, and these future investigations are not knowledge every participant possesses during the flight.

That retrospective framing gives the earlier report of eventual disruption a more definite institutional form. It does not supply the committees' findings or turn the present descent into a completed successful mission. The retrospective label “last operation” does not disclose its result.

The men are already enclosed in pods at the hangar. Williams speaks on the wire, so his current presence is direct; the other teammates are not individually named. The decreasing count recalls the preceding subsection's counted coffins, but now measures release into action. The narrator expressly denies that combat excitement causes his anticipation. His thought supplies another reason: **これから行く場所には、ルツィア・シュクロウプがいる**. This is his confident expectation, which the later fear of her departure will qualify rather than independently verify.

## A release taken from the pilot

Radar illumination and a reported missile launch interrupt the procedure (E002). The copilot resists releasing the payload, while the narrator repeatedly demands it and initiates a pod-side **優先権〔オーヴアライド〕**. A system voice announces the override; the organic hooks release. This is an actual consequential choice and implemented mechanism, not merely a plan or a metaphor for autonomy.

The subsequent blast violently moves his pod, but **シーウィードにミサイルが当たったのかもしれない** preserves the uncertainty about a hit. Cutting the physical wire and entering radio silence removes access to the pilot and Williams. Their silence is not a casualty report. The copilot's apparent agitation and the narrator's explanation about expecting a third-rate African army also have an identifiable perspective; technical surprise is not an independent measure of the enemy's actual sophistication or identity.

The narrator then turns a repeated military procedure into a ritual of birth before an audience with a sorcerer (E003). His account concerns John-related infiltration; Prague is its stated exception. It does not claim that every transport leg, including the earlier rail escort, was itself an airborne insertion. The **まるで儀式だ** makes the comparison explicit. Real tactical practice can carry this imaginative significance without establishing supernatural power.

The pod computes course corrections from a bad release position and the shock wave. A wide lake still does not guarantee a safe landing. His absence of prayer for luck is stated, but neither technical calculation nor confidence has eliminated the hazard. The narrative now interrupts the fall with the briefing that explains what kind of place and body this operation joins.

## What counts as a restored lake

Rockwell begins with Lake Victoria's former diversity and the introduction of Nile perch in 1954 (E004). The voices within the account need separating. N32 and N34 are his direct speech; N33's account of violent ecological displacement, exports and local destitution is unquoted narratorial exposition inside the remembered briefing. These are represented historical explanations. The reading need not authenticate or silently correct them as external ecological history to examine their force here.

The story moves through successive substitutions. An introduced species destroys the old food relation; export value does not feed the local people; the disappearance of small fish permits excess algae; oxygen loss kills the perch too. The sweeping alternatives offered to residents in N33 belong to this stark exposition, rather than an independently exhaustive census of their possible lives.

Industrial operators then clear algae with nanomachines and build facilities that restore the lake's **生存性**. Rockwell describes the early neural-link technology precisely: control of muscular contraction is practical, while the transmission of vision, thought and sensation remains unreproduced in his account. This is a claimed technical distinction, not a demonstration of total mental access or evidence that every neural function has the same mechanism.

The eventual product is not strictly synthetic muscle. It is tissue from genetically modified whales and dolphins adapted to fresh water, raised, slaughtered and exported (E005–006). Calling the lake a vast aquarium makes the transformation intelligible as a productive system. Williams's **それぜんぜん再生してないですよ** directly contests its description as restoration. The original biodiversity has not been brought back in the account; a new industrial viability has replaced it.

Rockwell's further story connects this resource to a coastal independence movement, a ring-shaped state and recognition by developed countries whose industrial base depends on muscle (E006). His **利権剝き出し** assesses the movement through interests. Its political-economic explanation is strongly stated speech, without supplying the private purposes of every resident or proving that each participant shared one motive. The new state's existence, recognition and forces are briefing claims, not an independently inspected constitutional or military record.

## The product history stops where attention does

The narrator recalls his own investigation between Prague and India (E005). He follows the catalogue history of a porter through constituent materials, recycled parts and control software. This supplies a concrete practice behind the preceding subsection's general exposition of metadata. It also gives that practice a limit: the muscle's record ends at a Victoria factory with “produced here.” It does not lead him back through the tissue's prior living history.

No forum, lobby or alpha consumer concerned with the origin appears in his search. His explanation is that institutional purchase leaves this industrial material outside private consumers' felt concerns, which center on food and household devices. **人間は、見たいものしか見ない** generalizes sharply. The next paragraph immediately implicates him: until Lucius told him, he had not known either. He cannot simply laugh at Williams's disbelief from a superior position of transparent knowledge.

The breadth of that consumer explanation should not flatten the source's own wording. N44 places porters in offices and wealthy homes, while N46 says only sufficiently large companies and government agencies buy them. Placement and purchase need not be the same relation, but no particular procurement explanation is supplied. The tension remains in his account; inventing an intermediary purchase would repair more than the evidence permits. Likewise, the absence of a forum in his search is not an independent proof that no concerned person exists anywhere.

The cheap substitute caviar and seaweed-derived lotion provide analogies for ignorance about familiar materials. They are the narrator's private comparisons, rather than things he actually says to Williams. Together with his self-implication, they make restricted attention and unexamined ordinary use part of the problem, without forcing a single ethical score onto every consumer or industrial user.

## Tracking the break instead of the person

Rockwell explains how the Prague pheromone trail reached the airport (E007). He attributes the tag on Lucia to Clavis. This is newly admitted reporting of the earlier operation, not permission to insert its details retrospectively into an earlier knowledge freeze.

The direct track cannot continue through presumed multiple false IDs. The reported method instead checks everyone boarding from the identified gate and excludes IDs whose later footprints remain rationally consistent. The remaining discontinuities become candidates. This is a specific inverse filter, described as laborious and slow. It is distinct from simply saying that SNDGA supplied an answer; that algorithm is not named in this account, nor is the actual dataset or a unique selection result shown.

The method also differs from the product search. A factory endpoint limits the narrator's commodity inquiry; an odd break in authentication becomes a military lead. The shared formal feature is interrupted legibility, not proof that the same technology governs both systems or that a break necessarily identifies a culprit.

Rockwell's report places John in a guesthouse and in a position overseeing domestic media. The sentence ends **いるらしい**, retaining its character as reported intelligence. A screen actually displays armed boys, but it does not show the guesthouse's current occupants or demonstrate that John's language has caused a local massacre. The location is operational intelligence with narrative consequences, rather than a present reunion the reader has witnessed.

## The judgment he wants to reach

The briefing does name antiaircraft missiles and a navy (E007–008). The later radar warning therefore cannot simply be explained as total absence of intelligence about defenses. Williams's incredulous joke about Africans using boats expresses his stereotype; it is not the analysis's description of African ability. Rockwell's account of corvettes and a former Japanese missile boat supplies the specific force estimate.

The narrator's attention is elsewhere. He recalls Lucia's face and running eyeliner, and specifically hopes **彼女がぼくを罰してくれる**. The preceding wish for someone to punish him now has a named addressee. This advances his current motive beyond a generic need for accountability, while preserving both the earlier desire for forgiveness and his wish for genuine guilt as historically admitted states. It does not establish Lucia's present intention, willingness, moral verdict or even her continued presence.

## Reassembled flesh, apparent concealment and disposal

Back in the descent, the pod's aquatic form is described through flying fish, mermaid and dolphin comparisons (E009–010). The narrator explains its muscular rather than mechanical movement as reducing detection. The comparisons give the represented engineering a living, fluid shape.

The shallow landing and gradual underwater deceleration actually occur. He hears marine-mammal calls beneath the quiet lake while water jets and active sonar pass above. The navy **見つけることができないようだった**: apparent non-discovery is the observation, not verified universal invisibility. The narrator attributes it to a body assembled from former residents' flesh. The source does not independently trace this particular pod's cells to particular animals or isolate tissue composition as the only possible cause.

The bodily return gives the earlier language of restoration an unsettling material counterpart. A lake's inhabitants become industrial muscle and return as an instrument carrying an armed operator through the reconstructed habitat. This is a strongly supported formal relation among the stated production history, his pod and his own reflection; it need not settle the truth of every ecological claim or assign an unspoken doctrine to the novel's author.

His friends' unknown fate hurts him, yet his deeper worry is that John and Lucia have already left after sowing massacre (E011). The text asserts both levels of concern. Priority is not complete absence of care. The feared departure is not an actual event supplied to the reader. It makes him shudder at his own reckless release decision: if Lucia is absent, the risk becomes meaningless to him. That judgment identifies a personal criterion of meaning, not a retrospective erasure of the declared military task.

After roughly an hour, the pod announces the programmed destination, recommends gear checks and disposal, and he complies. He exits into the water, cuts the necessary enzyme supply and watches gradual decay. This is an actual ending for the used body, with functioning instructions and his actions distinguished. Shoreline arrival, recovered companions, a meeting with John or Lucia and complete disappearance of every trace remain unshown. The next source operation remains outside this unit.
