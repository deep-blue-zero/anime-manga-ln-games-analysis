## Deep reading

### A protective coffin is also disposable flesh

The new subsection does not abandon the earlier coffin image when descent becomes technically explicable. It begins with that figure and repeatedly carries it through guidance, bodily passivity and landing. The narrator’s comparison to a munition with a person in place of its explosive charge makes the operator a delivered payload. Yet the apparatus protects him rather than literally burying him: the exit opens onto the stars. It would be too simple to turn the figure into a statement that the men are already physically dead (E001).

The technical vocabulary also changes what “coffin” encloses. The pod’s muscles and tissues make it organic material; the narrator qualifies his own description before calling most of it flesh. Nothing here establishes a subject that feels or chooses inside that tissue. The important observed relation is dependence: a sustaining supply is interrupted, decay follows, and some remaining components must be removed. In GO-U002, withdrawal of molecular support and maternal death were narrated together. The recurring support/withdrawal pattern is now materially legible in equipment, without making pod tissue and a person morally equivalent (E002; GO-CL012).

The narrator expects the residue eventually to fertilize the grassland. The unit shows degradation beginning, not the eventual completed ecological outcome. His efficient disposal account is simultaneously an erasure-of-traces account; the apparently benign return to soil serves clandestine violence. The campfire-cleanup simile briefly gives the group adolescent normality. Its qualification—that their own festival is still to begin—turns casual leisure toward impending action. This recurring fire/leisure vocabulary supports an unsettling correspondence; a deliberate allusion designed by the author remains an inference, not an independently established intention.

### Precision changes vulnerability without removing choice

The narrator voices great confidence in guidance accuracy, but the subsection devotes substantial space to walking. His technology can deliver, conceal, display information and mitigate bodily demands; it cannot make distance or fatigue disappear. The repeated walking language, account of selection attrition and two-hour exhaustion are meaningful form and bodily context, not expendable transit between plot events. Precision has not made the men disembodied. The route is selected through consultation and available information, and later demands further judgment (E003–04).

This matters for the lethal decision. The text gives an operational explanation for urgency: discovery could alert the targets, and dawn threatens completion. It also distinguishes survival from mission success; the narrator believes the group might escape a larger force yet fail its assignment. Thus the decisive pressure is not narrated as immediate inability to survive an attack. The camouflage works well enough to leave them unseen, while the newly arrived patrol prevents easy movement. A technical advantage becomes a situation that still requires a human choice (E006).
