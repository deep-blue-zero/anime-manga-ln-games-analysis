# GO-U021 — the perfect operation and the return to the dream

The capture operation succeeds in the sense the narrator reports: the team takes its prisoners, boards the recovery aircraft and begins returning. Within that success, the subsection makes the prepared soldier's efficacy disturbing through precise distinctions. He knows pain without feeling it; he kills children without hesitation while recognizing that his own protected body resembles theirs; he announces and performs John's arrest and participates in devices that move prisoners against their will. When the violence recedes, ordinary life becomes the dream he must reenter. His desired meeting with ルツィア has not occurred.

## An apparatus that carries bodies

The aircraft arrives through a grotesque description rather than a clean engineering diagram (E001). Its rectangular body appears aerodynamically impossible to the narrator, while small moving flaps resemble fingers or soft hair. The satellite view in which it would resemble a monolith is imagined: **見えたに違いない** does not introduce an actual satellite feed.

The bomber carries insertion pods **爆弾のかわりに**. Later use of an emergency bomb prevents this local description of its principal present cargo from becoming an absolute statement that no bomb is aboard. Human bodies now occupy the place of munitions, although keeping those bodies intact requires a long series of checks. The narrator's deliberate inspection, continued despite Williams's impatience, matters: readiness is performed through attention and choice as well as through pharmacology.

The later descent extends the aircraft's bodily imagery (E004–005). The pod has actual artificial-muscle legs; **「肉々」しかった** makes the sight excessive flesh through a pun. Its guns act before the narrator reaches the ground. He first sees nearby dead militia through the pod's exterior vision and later looks out directly. Those channels differ, even as both support his account of killings already accomplished by guns and firing software.

After touchdown the other seven land, establishing eight team members here. Within fifteen seconds all pods **begin** biological decomposition. The text does not say every trace has already disappeared. Acid destroys electronics and the muscles lose their sustaining enzymes. Beside the people it has killed, a pod itself is described as **「死んで」**. The quotation marks and biological construction support this deliberate analogy; they do not establish a conscious machine's experience of death.

## Friendship and an interpretation of friendship

The nasal procedure is actual, and Williams jokingly calls it **『友情の素』** (E002). His affectionate clowning leads クラヴィス to wonder whether his colleague is trying to ease his anxiety. The questions remain explicit: **だろうか** marks the reading of Williams's intention, and **だとしたら** marks the fear that even interpreting that care might be an effect of induced cooperation.

The passage therefore supplies a real intervention and a real epistemic doubt. It does not prove that the friendship is wholly false. The disturbing possibility is that the narrator cannot cleanly separate a chemical condition of trust from the recognition of another person's concern. His dismissal of his own existential worry before landing postpones the question rather than solving it.

Combat Medical is now expressly named as the contractor for almost all the group's medical provision, including the counselor who performed his emotional adjustment. The narrator places that service among specialized suppliers of weapons, intelligence, satellite reconnaissance, food and water. **戦争という巨大な「流通」** continues the prior unit's logistics, but the circulating goods now include the soldier's ability to function.

His institutional argument is stronger than a generic observation that contractors exist. Fragmentation and mutual dependence make the image of one private army overthrowing G9 governments unpersuasive to him; official forces share dependence on private support. This is his explanation of the market and its restraints, not a demonstrated proof that every private threat has disappeared or that a named contractor controls the entire state.

## Another reality is made on the eye

The alternative display is applied and checked on the body (E003). Insulating cream confines the layer-forming liquid; the eye's electrical potential organizes a thin display. The technical account is narrated, while the application, test patterns and exchange of a towel are present actions.

**もうひとつの〔オルタナテイヴ〕現実** joins the ordinary Japanese phrase to the foreign reading through ruby. The typography presents another reality as an overlay, rather than replacing the visible cargo bay with an entirely separate world. This matters to the later combat: maps, gun views, remote cameras and direct perception repeatedly inhabit the same field.

Williams's widened eyes and grin are compared to a drug user's appearance. That simile and his joking description of the test pattern as a trip do not establish addiction or an independent medical condition. The panda joke then brings the sophisticated interface back to cream on a colleague's face. Technical mediation and intimate banter share the same preparation scene.

## The protection that makes the enemy familiar

The team and the narrator explicitly kill armed children (E006). His argument against merely wounding them is presented as battlefield reasoning about danger and the lack of rescue resources. The analysis can state that reasoning clearly without adopting its generalizations about everyone in a place where life is cheap.

The drug account has a narrower grammar than the killings. Commanders sometimes supply drugs, and **そういう状態にあったとしたら** makes the present application conditional. Actual armed child combatants are shown; intoxication of every current opponent is not established.

The decisive recognition turns toward his own side. He and Williams also have pain masked, so an opponent cannot rely on a painful wound to stop them. **ぼくらとて、あの子供らと一緒なのだ。** breaks the distance his efficiency argument has constructed. The similarity concerns bodies that may remain dangerous despite injury; it does not make their resources, conscription or responsibility identical.

**ぼくはぞっとした** is equally consequential. Emotional adjustment has not erased every affect. The narrator can feel a chill at the analogy while continuing the work of killing. Claims that he now feels nothing would erase an explicitly narrated response.

## What “perfect” means here

An additional encounter supplies individually narrated shootings by クラヴィス (E007). A child's armed appearance and an adult's presence are shown. His conjecture about sexual exploitation and the adult's superior role remains conjecture; it is not an observed act whose details the reader independently knows. The scene establishes lethal action without requiring a graphic repetition of the child's body.

**このときのぼくは、完璧だった。** immediately defines its own criterion: he can shoot children without hesitation. The prose does not leave “perfect” as an analyst's loose moral judgment. It identifies operational consistency, then argues that even a well-trained soldier cannot guarantee that ethics or affection will never interrupt judgment.

The narrator calls a species capable of preferring love or morality to its own survival **歪んだ**. That reversal makes the preservation of life the local instrumental standard and moral refusal the possible malfunction. Lucia's evolutionary explanation enters conditionally, as something she has said, rather than as a newly demonstrated law of the brain.

The final **カウンセリングと化学物質は、完璧に機能していた。** is a strong causal self-account supported by the performance just narrated. It should be stated confidently at that level. The scene does not separately isolate the contribution of training, chemicals, counseling and personal motive through a controlled test. Nor does this distinction justify retreating into doubt about whether he actually kills without the hesitation he describes.

His explanation gives the suppression a temporal limit: emotions and morals are restrained during battle, **社会から切り離された祝祭**. Later boredom puts pressure on that tidy division. For this narrator, the festival increasingly supplies the felt reality against which everyday social life seems insubstantial.

## Pain remains information

A bullet burns his cheek (E008). He perceives pain, ducks and receives Williams's covering response, while explicitly not feeling painfulness. This is a field enactment of the sensory distinction made during the earlier needle procedure. The narration explains why total suppression of the signal would prevent this protective response; masking is useful because information survives the removed aversion.

The dead child whom he identifies as accidentally shot by her own side extends his account of danger. It is his attribution of the death, not a displayed view of the particular shooter. The two propositions—his own cheek event and the other body's condition—must retain their respective channels.

The team also encounters a practical limit. Williams proposes smoke; クラヴィス considers and rejects it because it would impair them inside. Leland reports risk from the opposite approach. Technical competence here consists partly in deliberation and exchanged information, rather than the elimination of all decisions by a device.

## Asking a human pilot for a god's intervention

Williams's suggestion to ask God becomes a link to the aircraft whose pilot earlier wished them Godspeed (E008–009). クラヴィス asks for the obstructing building to be destroyed; Williams supplies laser designation and coordinates. The actual result is a bomb and a collapsing building, after which the team advances. No casualty total inside that building is provided.

The repeated joke does not make the event immaterial. A light exchange joins the killing apparatus to an available human operator, a camera and a specific request. The aircraft that principally carried their bodies also retains emergency explosive force. The episode exposes the reach of a coordinated system while preserving the actors' choices and the narration's limits on what deaths are directly seen.

## The joke is also a claim to jurisdiction

The room's seizure is narrated through swift team action, with a boy shot and another young girl **無力化** (E010). That latter word does not independently certify death. The narrator guesses that the girl was lover and bodyguard; she should neither be merged with the earlier child nor assigned a verified relationship on the strength of **であろう**.

Williams announces the Spanish Inquisition, continuing the Python repertoire. An English-speaking official asks who they are. Williams answers that they are the International Criminal Court's enforcement unit; the man then accuses them of being mercenaries. Internally クラヴィス concedes the formal mercenary description: although American regulars, in this mission they act as Japan's military agents. The joke therefore sits beside a substantial gap among identity, contract and asserted arrest authority.

The official's anti-Muslim holy-war declaration is his speech. Neither his sectarian certainty nor Williams's disgust establishes a universal account of Hindu belief. The narrator's generalization about intellectual elites with overseas experience becoming such leaders is likewise his interpretation, not independently established biography.

## A private purpose becomes audible

John is actually present and recognizes the former spy in a new operational role (E011). Daylight reveals a scholarly appearance, and the narrator says that, as before, his eyes show no madness. This outward demeanor is not a clinical adjudication of his claims or responsibility.

**ルツィアはどこだ** is now spoken aloud. This changes the access state from U020, where クラヴィス concealed his motive from Williams and disclosed it only through narration. John replies **ここにはいないよ**. That is his statement of local absence, not a verified account of her whole present location or fate.

John's inference that the narrator's purpose lies elsewhere than the state's is well grounded in the question he has just heard. It need not be inflated into prior omniscience. Williams's presence in the broader operation also does not by itself establish that he hears and understands the entire exchange or the private wish for forgiveness.

クラヴィス states that he will arrest John and actually tapes his arms; John submits. The changed order from the briefing is thus performed in a bounded present event. The scene does not yet settle final custody, the promised handover arrangements or the reason for retaining him alive. The personal destination and the state's capture order intersect without becoming identical.

## The walking body and the recovered package

The radio idiom makes prisoners goods in a shopping basket, ready for settlement (E012). After the preceding unit's furniture warehouse and delivery network, this language transfers efficient handling from equipment to human captives.

The walking device makes that handling coercive in a specific way. The group of prisoners walks through signals that interfere with locomotion despite its members' intentions. Their astonishment supports the loss of voluntary control. The narration explicitly says no one in the detachment knows the device's formal name; **バカ歩きデバイス〔シリー・ウオーク・デバイス〕** supplies a comic nickname rather than complete technical understanding.

This is distinct from the narrator's pain masking, his emotional preparation, and the later sedating stickers. A forced walking signal does not prove that counseling operates by the same mechanism or that the system can freely read and replace any thought. Taped arms, group device application and later recovery are narrated, but a separate patch application to John is not individually shown.

The trap's explosion is heard as the team climbs. A complete collapse of the stairs remains the narrator's conditional expectation. Similarly, difficulty for pursuing children is an anticipated advantage, not a view proving that every possible route has closed.

## Seeing the mirror from the machine

The approaching helicopter supplies a new camera view (E013). The narrator can see his own team through the machine's eye: **異様な鏡だな**. This is materially different from the earlier imagined satellite view. The current inset is an operational feed inside the eye's overlay.

The description asks whether the machine trembles with its privilege of killing. The question personifies violent hardware; it does not establish machine consciousness. The surrounding fire, approach and loading give the rhetoric a concrete occasion while keeping the attribution figurative.

Williams loads the prisoners and applies knockout stickers. Their sedation and earlier compelled walking are separate steps. The helicopter's approach and subsequent gunfire help extraction, while RPG impacts and a damaged roof preserve the possibility of failure. A colleague's thumbs-up and the narrator's impression that no one seems hurt do not make him an omniscient examiner of all bodies.

## The operation ends before its prisoner reaches a destination

The group boards, and Williams takes the helicopter out of autonomous mode into manual control (E014). Agency passes between human operators and machines through explicit acts, rather than disappearing into a single omnipotent system. The actual end of this subsection is departure from the street, not arrival at the base or completion of the planned rail escort.

The narrator overlays the helicopter gun's view on the satisfied men's faces (E015). The violence beneath them shares a visual field with the expression of successful work. As the landscape recedes, adrenaline withdraws like a tide from the body's shore.

Then the ordering of reality reverses: **日常という夢、生活という夢に。次の戦場までの、長い待ち時間。** The account of war as a temporary exception does not contain his attachment to it. He must return to daily life while waiting for the next battle. Lucia remains part of this future-directed longing: he hopes to meet her on some battlefield, rather than reporting reunion or forgiveness.

His final report says all packages and the valuable item are secured, with **負傷者なし**. The earlier cheek graze remains an actual narrated event. The report may employ an operational injury threshold, but the source does not explicitly explain that threshold; neither event should be silently erased to harmonize the record. Similarly, “the valuable item” strongly points to John within the mission's covert priority, while the code noun alone supplies no new information about his eventual use.

Successful capture has therefore closed a concrete action without closing the operation's institutional or personal purposes. He can perform a command precisely and still leave unanswered why the state needs this prisoner, where Lucia is, and what sort of life will feel real outside combat.
