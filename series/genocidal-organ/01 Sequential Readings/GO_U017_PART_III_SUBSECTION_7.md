## The familiar place becomes a place of custody

Fifteen minutes after John leaves, an assailant walks クラヴィス through a graffiti-marked corridor at gunpoint. The graffiti seems recent: what he had thought eliminated by personal tracking still exists. The club's projected abyss supplies recognition before its owner explains anything (E001).

ルーシャス appears with ルツィア and armed men. He rejects “collaborator,” then immediately admits assistance under another description: John is a customer and their cooperation protected themselves. The disagreement concerns the meaning and justification of the relationship, rather than whether assistance occurred.

ルツィア's confusion about “we,” her attention to the bound man and her later questions constrain any claim that she already knows this organization or its surveillance. They do not exhaust everything she knows about John. クラヴィス's alternatives—perhaps she was not on their side, perhaps she helped unknowingly—remain his questions.

## Names that refuse to be counted

The archaic census quotation gives **〈計数されざる者たち〉** a history through its negation (E002). ルーシャス identifies the census at the opening of Luke chapter 2 as evidence that subjects have long been counted. This is his political analogy, not an independently established institutional continuity. クラヴィス's **無宗教** answers a question about biblical familiarity; lack of religious affiliation need not contradict his earlier denial that he is an atheist.

The group's anonymity is not explained as breaking an otherwise perfect central database (E003). ルーシャス largely agrees that intrusion or forgery is impossible. He instead describes mapping heterogeneous sensors, finding relatively passable routes and presenting different bodily identifiers to different devices. The narrator recalls the young follower's mismatched finger and eye records. That actual earlier mismatch now receives a coherent account; it does not independently verify every route or the archive's size.

The identities reportedly include babies who died soon after birth, missing travelers, contractors missing or not confirmed dead, and especially Sarajevo losses. The narrator then isolates the names of people not confirmed alive or dead into short paragraphs; ルーシャス then describes making selected records **「生きた」ＩＤ**. Administrative life is detached from bodily survival. Borrowed names let living people evade a system that still requires credentials.

Their freedom therefore depends on maintained information, selection and an archive. Its speaker says they do not assist terrorism or grave crime, yet will do anything to protect that archive. The source does not establish that he knows John's complete project or reconcile the prohibition with every customer's conduct.

## Available information and desired belief

クラヴィス recalls the owner's earlier claim that freedom is a transaction. ルーシャス preserves the premise: his objection is that privacy supplied and safety received are grossly unequal (E004). His later coercion cannot simply expose a secret belief that all tradeoffs are impossible.

He asserts that intensified tracking coincided with rising terrorism, and that cessation followed proliferating civil wars and ethnic conflicts. Official graphs are invoked without being displayed. This is a definite causal argument in his speech, not an independently inspected statistical demonstration. The narrator's short paragraphs about seeing and believing what one wants strongly take up selective attention; they are not a separate audit proving security had no possible effect.

The artificial-muscle account makes selection bodily (E005). ルーシャス says modified whales and dolphins are farmed in Lake Victoria, dissected and packed through low-paid children's labor. The narrator recognizes the consequence for vehicles he has used: the apparently technical surface becomes **生々しい、本物の肉**. Particular production history remains the owner's report; the narrator's visceral recognition and shock are directly represented.

The ruby chain—**事物 / オブジエクト**, space, time, **時空 / スパイム**, **物歴 / メタヒストリー**—makes traceability a material infrastructure. It can answer where products came from without making anyone ask. Food safety and household materials interest consumers while distant suffering does not. クラヴィス includes himself through CNN clips, Domino's and the repeated free fifteen-minute film preview. Narrowed attention is explicitly his own practice, not an accusation confined to civilians.

## Protecting their freedom by watching hers

John reportedly noticed CIA surveillance and notified the organization as an ID customer's duty. ルーシャス says the threat concerned exposure of the whole archive, not merely his arrest. This supplies a specific stated reason for watching ルツィア's home, without showing the database or complete command chain.

Her question uses their own terms: people rejecting dependence on managed information have watched another person for their freedom (E007). **哀しいジレンマ** concedes the contradiction while claiming necessity. The narrator's *Animal Farm* reference and comparisons with murderous rulers sharpen the objection. They concern how necessity licenses unequal freedom; they do not prove identical histories or scales of power.

The irony returns to him. Asked when his own crimes will be punished, ルーシャス points out that he too watched her. The symmetry is real in surveillance, without making every motive and consequence identical. Identifying the captive's wrongdoing does not answer whether the captors' violence is justified.

## The confession he cannot choose

ルツィア hears that her visitor watched her to capture John and is an American military operative (E008). Her access changes without an assumed earlier private suspicion. The repeated **ぼくは……知っています** is unquoted interior address: meals, coffee and purchased condoms turn intimate knowledge into an inventory of surveillance. These are not additional spoken confessions.

He wants the device switched on. First physical pain could scatter awareness and interrupt shame; then its spectacle could make him pitiable to her. His apparent reading of ツヴィ's eyes, including the taunting “speech,” remains his projection. No actual taunt is quoted from the youth. **地獄はここにある。頭のなかに。苦痛すら必要ない。** states that inward suffering no longer needs the device.

Earlier pain remains real; its desired use has changed. The man coerced through suffering now seeks it as distraction, a visible claim on compassion and, finally, punishment. Earlier relief through taking responsibility is not retrospectively erased. Newly exposed betrayal alters his present need.

Her gaze remains explicitly unreadable—confusion or contempt—and he still wants her to stay (E009). He imagines warning her that John is a mass murderer but judges himself unqualified to speak. The warning stays unspoken. His shame restricts communication without proving the warning false or already known to her.

Her request that ルーシャス help him is an actual act of concern. She then leaves after hearing “I dislike murderers.” No private verdict behind her departure, reunion with John or confirmed further destination is shown. His exhausted interpretation cannot make her mind transparent.

## Sorrow does not cancel what is about to be done

The captors look down at the man over their artificial abyss (E010). He understands that he is about to be killed to protect freedom and resist management. Their shared inward preparation is his interpretation; no execution occurs. The owner's sincere-looking sadness and admission that this is painful receive no exculpatory value from him. He immediately includes his own guilt toward ルツィア.

The point is sharper than discovering a captor without conscience. Sorrow can coexist with prospective violence; feeling it does not repair the injury. Comparisons with earlier perpetrators retain their rhetorical levels, without supplying a generic diagnosis or equal numerical guilt score.

## A moth interrupts the abyss

A moth lands on the middle finger that had been releasing pheromones; ルーシャス recognizes a **追跡犬 / トレース・ドツグ** (E011). クラヴィス covers his ears as far as bound hands and shoulder permit and opens his mouth before the southern wall explodes. This physical preparation recalls the preceding ears-without-eyelids account while providing limited protection against a blast. It neither disproves nor demonstrates John's claimed linguistic effect.

Tracking and breach give the earlier emergency signal an effective rescue sequel. Exact insect technology and every communications link remain unexplained. The blast incapacitates the group in the room; damaged eardrums are the narrator's inference, his own ringing actual sensation.

Training now tells him to keep down (E012). Rotating attacker, captor and hostage roles explain why competence requires refusing to watch. He explicitly does not see who is shot or secured. The operation ends in about three minutes and Williams appears in special-operations equipment; individual deaths, arrests, John's capture and ルツィア's recovery are not provided. He requests release of his wrists; the release itself is not narrated.

The hole reveals nighttime Prague without restoring command of its “maze” (E013). He does not know where she went. Williams's dusting, banter, concern and question are supportive acts. They fail to meet the narrator's urgent wish for punishment. **ような気がした** qualifies the hope that pain could escape numbness; **切実に罰を必要としていた** strongly states his need. The rescue reaches him; kindness cannot supply the moral sentence he wants.
