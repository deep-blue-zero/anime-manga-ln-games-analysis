# GO-U026 — the explanation that does not yet explain the purpose

At gunpoint, the narrator receives a more explicit account of the grammar's supposed adaptive function. Yet explaining how an inherited capacity could work does not answer why John continues to use it. Clavis asserts choice and responsibility; John agrees. Lucia then appears with another gun and offers a psychological explanation that John rejects. The final answer invokes protection of loved people. The subsection makes that answer audible without yet identifying those people or explaining how the killings would protect them.

## A reversed window and a gun without credentials

The opening explicitly reverses the Prague encounter's positions: Clavis now stands with the moonlit window behind him (E001). John's face remains apparently sane and sad. This is a formal reprise with changed spatial power, rather than a medical finding about sanity or innocence. The narrator compares the seated man's dignity to a charismatic savior, then denies the shining insistence, arrogance and intrusive benevolence he associates with that type. The qualification matters: John cannot be explained simply by the visible manner of a conventional fanatic.

The old Browning carries no ID authentication. **誰でも使えて、誰でも殺せる** recalls the earlier encounters in which credentials constrained a weapon's operation. Here an apparently obsolete object presents lethal availability without that credential barrier. No shot tests it in this unit. John keeps it pointed at Clavis while sitting and later standing; his report that this is his first night handling a gun remains testimony, albeit testimony given beside the unfamiliar-seeming attention he pays to the object (E002).

He says he thought the rescue assault might have killed Clavis, says the experiments ended years ago, and calls the present regional **種播き** only just begun. The first is his earlier uncertainty about another person's survival; the last two are his accounts of the history and current phase of his work. They give the narrator a sharper explanation of the present encounter, without independently dating every experiment or demonstrating what these speeches have caused.

## Discovery, responsibility and an agreement that interrupts the expected argument

Clavis interprets John's gaze at the gun through a possible question about personally holding a concrete instrument of killing. The wording remains **まるで…かのように** and **どう受け止めているのだろうか**: he cannot inspect John's thoughts. His accusation that John kills without dirtying his own hands draws a substantive distinction between direct violence and distributed effects.

John answers **これはわたしの力じゃないよ**, printed as its own paragraph without dialogue brackets, then attributed to him by **そう言って**. Its typographic isolation does not turn it into the narrator's view. His anatomist analogy recasts the genocide language as something found in the brain, rather than a personal power invented by its finder. Clavis's Einstein comparison challenges the attempted separation of discovery from responsibility. Neither historical comparison is independently adjudicated by the scene.

John's account of pre-existing cruelty invokes theft, rape, killing, war and an attack on Margaret Mead's Samoa. Those claims belong to this speaker's polemic; the narrator's question about peaceful “primitive” societies is also a represented question, not an anthropological result (E003). The civilizational vocabulary and asserted follow-up research cannot be silently adopted as externally verified knowledge.

Clavis then states the argument that the preceding subsection developed internally: **人間は、選ぶことができる**, he bears guilt and can answer for choices, and evolutionary explanation cannot justify violence. John's **わたしも同感だ** interrupts the anticipated opposition (E003–004). The narrator reports being disconcerted. This is not a debate in which John simply asserts that biology abolishes responsibility while Clavis alone believes in choice. Their disagreement has to be located more precisely.

## Competing capacities and a hypothesized ancestral emergency

John includes care, love and self-sacrifice alongside violence as products of survival needs. He describes competing emotional modules and uses a sweet preference becoming inconvenient in abundance as an example of a capacity's changed context. When asked whether the violent modules are obsolete, he says **それはわからない**: obsolescence depends on the present cultural situation. He nevertheless confidently identifies the genocide-inducing grammar as one such module. His uncertainty about whether it is obsolete and confidence about classification are distinct statements.

The moonlit window frames the lakeside landscape **タブローのように** before he proposes a drought in a pre-agricultural community (E004–005). The narrator's present poverty description and John's ancestral hypothetical occupy adjoining space, but they are not the same event or an actual observed historical experiment. John's explanation leaves genetic versus cultural or memetic evolution undecided while asserting adaptation to survival needs.

Clavis grasps the proposed answer: massacre as an adaptation to food shortage. John affirms it. His accompanying story makes language the available one-to-many medium after the human sense of smell had diminished. These are specific claims about the alleged function and transmission of the capacity, stronger in detail than an unexplained magical command; no particular prehistoric record or controlled intervention is shown.

## A social threshold in the narrator's own explanation

The short paragraphs **啓発された残虐行為。** and **生存のための大量殺人。** interrupt the longer account before Clavis's horrified synthesis (E005–006). The contraction of the prose makes the explanatory promise difficult to separate from what it explains: organized communication and altruism can belong to a society capable of mass killing.

N44 is an important increase in explicitness. The narrator now says the grammar does not simply increase an individual's aggression. Drawing on John's earlier claim that Jews also spoke it in Nazi Germany, he describes a capacity that functions socially once it has “infected” enough individuals: value judgments bend, an anticipatory mood of massacre forms, and at a social threshold people with particular conscience-related modules suppressed carry it out. This is a confidently articulated mechanism within the narrator's adopted explanation. It should not be weakened into a mere vague suspicion, or promoted into an independently demonstrated causal result. The unit supplies neither a numerical threshold nor an exact neural module, a decoded rule, a literal current broadcast or an isolated test.

Clavis does not merely accept the evolutionary story without objection. He repeats Williams's earlier individual-selection challenge and the rejection of the lemming-suicide picture (E007). John responds that reducing population can stabilize food and favor individual survival: permission for massacre and conscience masking would thus not be self-destruction. The reply addresses the objection within his argument; it is not a displayed evolutionary model proving the trait's actual origin. The persistent difference between an explanatory account and a reason for choosing to activate it prepares the next interruption.

## Lucia's actual accusation replaces imagined access

Clavis asks whether proving humanity's essential cruelty is John's purpose. **わたしのせいね** answers from another speaker. Lucia extends a Luger toward John's head, steps into the moonlight and presses it to the back of his head (E008). Her reappearance supplies ordinary embodied presence after the earlier empty-room appearance; where she had been hidden remains unprovided. Her cold or corpse-like beauty is a simile, while her tears are visibly narrated.

This time her words are actual speech. She says John was sleeping with her when Sarajevo disappeared and his wife died, could not forgive the betrayal of wife and child, and now spends terrible numbers of lives proving that cruelty and betrayal are unavoidable human nature. The affair's timing is her testimony; the guilt-to-experiment explanation is her explicit interpretation of him. It is more substantial evidence than Clavis's earlier projected apology, while still not transparent access to John's private motive.

Her knowledgeable accusation changes the present evidential balance against Clavis's preceding preference for her ignorance. It does not establish when she learned what, whether she earlier collaborated, or that the imagined U025 apology was secretly audible or true. That earlier freeze remains exact. Nor is this yet the reciprocal conversation, punishment or pardon Clavis had sought from her: she addresses John, with a gun.

## The rejected motive and the answer left at the boundary

John explicitly denies continuing in order to prove something (E009). His reply does not separately deny every factual element of Lucia's account of the earlier affair. He insists that neighbor-love is biologically grounded as strongly as, or more strongly than, cruelty, and that discovering the **虐殺器官** does not make him despair over human nature. Clavis now notices the absence of despair in his manner and interprets his apparent sanity through it. The recognition revises an available psychological explanation; it remains the narrator's reading of a visible and audible person.

Clavis steps forward despite the Browning and asks for the other reason. No disarming, firing or resolution follows. Silence and Lucia's breathing slow the exchange; after what feels like endless hesitation, the narrator names the speaker **虐殺の王〔ロード・オブ・ジエノサイド〕**. The supplied ruby is retained literally, including full-size **エ**, rather than regularized into a familiar spelling (E010).

**愛する人々を守るためだ** is the actual answer. It returns to the capacity for love that John has just defended, making affection available as the claimed motive for atrocity instead of a simple antidote to it. The earlier guard's hopeful account of a common future also remains relevant as already admitted speech, without proving the hidden mechanism. This final claim does not itself settle sincerity, justification, the identity of the loved people, the causal plan for their protection or the encounter's outcome. The natural subsection ends with those questions still open, not with permission to supply their answers from a later unit.
