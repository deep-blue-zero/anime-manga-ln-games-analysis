## Integrated argument and strongest challenges

**ACCEPT / PRESERVE the original G4-P01 proposition, reserved by AGENT00 as GO-CL097, as a supported, open literary inference.** The synthesis supplies a common explanatory problem rather than four specialist summaries: an account can enable competent action without authorizing the observer to settle another person's meaning or costs. The argument develops that problem through bodily recognition, operational division, intimate testimony, records, felt ownership, material geography, public judgment and retrospective narrative. These are compared without making surrogate care morally identical to patrol murder or every useful service a disguise for domination.

The strongest counterexample is the bedside: someone must act without recovering the mother's wishes. The candidate gives this case enough force to reject a general rule that incomplete knowledge itself makes action wrongful. It locates differences in purpose, alternatives, expertise, authority and burden. Functioning authentication, useful provenance, genuine Williams care and competent tactical initiative likewise change the conclusion: the world is not globally fraudulent or devoid of agency. The argument consequently remains discriminating rather than explaining every event by one word such as control.

The constructive horizon also has source support, with a necessary ceiling. Lucia's demand actually changes the armed plan into arrest; John subsequently intends public disclosure; Mugabe voices collective civic hopes; provenance supports criticism; publicity can address real neglect. These support the possibility of another person's demand changing what happens. They do not demonstrate a safe disclosure regime, general public wisdom, universal benevolence or a completed institutional alternative. Lucia's gun, her overinformed reassurance, uncertain motive diagnosis and acceptance of possible public ridicule remain consequential countercases. The candidate retains them and calls its horizon interpretive rather than a complete social program. **QUALIFIED_OPEN / PRESERVE** this bounded conclusion; no manufactured institutional blueprint is required.

John's acceptance of responsibility and explicit refusal to claim justice are especially important. They prevent the conclusion from reducing his catastrophe to failure to understand that explanation is not justification. The candidate recognizes that he can acknowledge the distinction, accept a burden and still choose other people's deaths. Lucia's guilt-based interpretation has real evidence, but the repeated protection and beneficiary language gives the avowed protective perimeter stronger support than an exclusively nihilistic motive. Hidden mixtures remain open. This is calibrated conviction, not a claim to his whole private mind.

The ending resolves the resulting literary problem: explicit ownership does not complete public answerability. Clavis's declared decisions, deliberate composition and current harms permit judgment without importing future massacre or worldwide rescue. His solitary pizza, defended supply and imagined external quiet show a selective protective experience persisting within his inversion of the old protected exception. The connection to the opening's unavailable reply is a formal interpretation, adequately grounded by the asymmetry between intimate access to the narrator and the audience's exposure. It need not prove that every event literally instantiates a single ethical law. The candidate states the required limits.
