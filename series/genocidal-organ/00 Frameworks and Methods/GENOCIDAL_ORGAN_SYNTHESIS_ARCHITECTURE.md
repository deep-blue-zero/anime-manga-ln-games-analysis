# Genocidal Organ / 虐殺器官 — synthesis and corpus architecture

## 1. Responsibility, authority and source model

This INITIAL architecture defines how future readings accumulate into a mature analysis of **one prose novel**. It pairs with the [analytical method](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/GENOCIDAL_ORGAN_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) and [source/witness map](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/GENOCIDAL_ORGAN_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE_MAP.md) under [the sole entrypoint](../CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md). Generation `GENOCIDAL_ORGAN_BOOTSTRAP_V1` remains active provisional guidance, substantively reviewed and accepted under the 2026-10-16 pilot instruction, then retained for the separately owner-authorized sequential continuation; no prior architecture is displaced. The entrypoint and source map own the current admitted boundary; G0 alone closes no narrative unit.

The source model separates the original Japanese work, the verified reading witness to be selected, alternate editions, translation witnesses, paratext and adaptations. Shared packaging and authorship do not establish shared continuity. Conflicts are classified as edition/translation difference, focalizer knowledge, memory, deception, institutional claim, framing level, chronology or unresolved inconsistency before any harmonization. Uncertainty survives as an explicit claim state.

## 3. Sequential contribution and retrieval

Every closed unit contributes its complete reading/evidence table, entry/exit frontier, coverage, ledger updates or no-change dispositions, claim/prediction adjudications, readiness limits and verified transaction. The method owns exact closeout steps. Reading prose without cumulative state is incomplete.

Future readings live in `01 Sequential Readings/` only when authored. A complete novel coverage audit must account for narrative framing, every admitted natural section, epilogue and each ancillary component's explicit disposition. Parts or sections can be checkpoints; EPUB file count is never the completion metric.

Retrieval starts at the entrypoint, then the responsible ledger/current synthesis, the exact frozen reading/evidence row, and finally the hash-bound Japanese witness. No later synthesis erases the earlier reading's historical knowledge frontier. Source keys, observation IDs and all subject/claim histories are private to this novel's namespace `GO`.

## 4. Character and relationship responsibility

A character monograph explains development and literary function. A reconstruction constrains behavior at a specified time and knowledge boundary. A relationship synthesis explains directional change and interaction. None automatically supplies the others.

Create a dedicated character home only when evidence spans distinct decisions/contexts or development states and repeated retrieval cannot be adequately handled in the ledger. Minor or mediated figures may warrant recognition-only records or abstention. A relationship gets an independent synthesis only when a distinct argument with evidence on both direction and change warrants it; one actor's report alone cannot establish the other's private state.

Future monographs belong in `04 Character and Relationship Studies/`; reconstruction models and their validation belong in `04 Character and Relationship Studies/Reconstruction/`. These are proposed roles, not extant empty folders. The complete specification below is the canonical reconstruction contract; no second spec is created.

## 5. Full character-reconstruction specification

### 5.1 Evidence thresholds and scope

Assess evidence dimension by dimension. Use `UNASSESSED`, `INSUFFICIENT`, `RECOGNITION_ONLY`, `DOMAIN_BOUNDED`, or `MULTICONTEXT_CANDIDATE` as **project-local readiness**, not global capability grades. At bootstrap there are zero assessed subjects. Repeated mentions and narrative importance do not satisfy a threshold.

A behavioral candidate needs several independently diagnostic decisions or responses across materially different stakes, an attributable knowledge/perception boundary, relationship-conditioned evidence, and support for both a proposed tendency and its limitations. A broad candidate additionally needs ordinary-life as well as stress evidence, temporal development or a reason stability is supportable, speech/register evidence and meaningful countercases. If interior access or domain breadth is missing, narrow the model; do not infer private psychology from a public creed. Recognition-only and explicit abstention are valid completion dispositions.

Every consequential rule cites evidence IDs, confidence, conditions, strongest alternative and counterexample. Distinguish behavior observed, self-report, another person's report, narrator access, private-belief inference and authorial interpretation. Contradictory evidence can lower readiness; source completion cannot raise it by itself.

### 5.2 Required recoverable model dimensions

| Dimension | Required content and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Identity / temporal envelope | Verified names/aliases; source witness; scene-era and disclosure cutoff; relevant pre/post-transition snapshots; no pooled timeless person |
| Goals and self-model | Prioritized goals, conflicts, ideals/fears, self-presentation versus evidenced private belief; unknowns remain unknown |
| Constraints and resources | Bodily, social, institutional and informational limits; capacities versus intentions; alternatives actually available |
| Attention and knowledge | Salient cues, blind spots, beliefs, uncertainty, deception, who knows what; no narrator omniscience assigned to the actor |
| Decision policy and agency | Conditional choice rules, tradeoffs, commands versus discretion, consent/refusal and consequences; reasons separated from justification |
| Affect and regulation | Emotion evidence, coping and stress regimes; distinguish transient states from durable tendencies; no unsupported clinical diagnosis |
| Social / relationship policy | Directional trust, obligation, power, intimacy, antagonism and recipient-conditioned change; no automatic reciprocity |
| Speech and voice | Japanese address, register, syntax, lexical repertoire, silences and context shifts; narrated prose versus dialogue; no acoustic timbre claim from written prose |
| Development / contradictions | State transitions, competing accounts, failures and revision conditions; preserve unresolved alternatives rather than average them |
| Uncertainty and use limits | Unsupported situations, negative constraints, refusal/abstention behavior and known failure modes |

### 5.3 Counterfactual and validation contract

Before evaluation, freeze model version, source boundary, training evidence, scenario conditions, expected behavior ranges and explicit prohibited responses. Counterfactuals vary a meaningful constraint, relationship, knowledge item or stake within the supported domain; they do not fabricate a factual scene. Predictions should state plausible alternatives and discriminating observations, not one inevitable line of dialogue. Generated dialogue, if later separately requested, is a labeled noncanonical test output and never source evidence; none is generated here.

Prefer prospective later-source tests: freeze the earlier model/prediction before admitting the later section under separate source authorization. Alternatively reserve source cases from model fitting and record actual access separation. If the author/evaluator has already read the supposed holdout, classify it as **retrospective consistency QA**, never blind or held-out predictive validation. A dedicated evaluator may use a frozen evidence packet only when that later work is authorized; no delegation is initiated by this specification.

Each test records case ID, model/version, evidence-exposure history, era/recipient/knowledge inputs, predicted range, source outcome, decisive evidence, error type and repair. Challenge wrong-era knowledge, narrator-to-character leakage, public/private conflation, relationship reversal, institutional-command confusion, overgeneralization from crisis, formal framing confusion, Japanese register drift and unsupported domain extrapolation. Include counterevidence and cases where the correct response is abstention.

Assess behavioral fit, epistemic restraint, speech consistency, condition sensitivity and calibration separately. A hard identity/knowledge/continuity violation fails the affected scope even if other cases pass. Repair rules with explicit evidence and rerun affected cases; keep the failed prediction and prior model recoverable. No universal numerical pass rate substitutes for material-error adjudication. Validation remains `NOT_PERFORMED`, `RETROSPECTIVE_ONLY`, or a precisely bounded prospective/held-out result; never label an entire person validated without scope.

### 5.4 Coverage and acceptance

The post-reading readiness audit must dispose of every materially represented subject: supported dedicated model, domain-bounded treatment, recognition only, or insufficient evidence with rationale. Owner review sets the desired reconstruction breadth; the evidence sets the ceiling. Reconstruction completion requires actual scoped models, evidence/constraint review, test outcomes and limitations, independently of monograph and novel-synthesis completion. Do not create global character discovery records here; the designated curator owns enrollment.

## 6. Specialist work and novel-level synthesis

Mandatory **responsibilities**, once relevant evidence is admitted, are: narration/disclosure and formal construction; character/relationship development and agency; institutional/causal and normative reasoning; and a bounded account of the ending's relation to prior evidence. These may be integrated sections for a compact novel, with an explicit role-gap review; they are not mandatory file counts.

Prospective emphasis for this novel: **Military/institutional terminology, command versus agency, evidentiary access, narrated causality and linguistic/cognitive mechanism claims**. Other anticipated lenses are personhood, consent/coercion, surveillance, violence, responsibility, collective welfare and biotechnology. They are questions to test, not findings or preset chapters. A specialist becomes required only when recurring independent evidence and downstream dependence justify it; record exclusions/consolidations with reasons.

Future specialist arguments may live in `05 Specialist Synthesis/`. The future integrated novel synthesis belongs in `06 Novel Synthesis/`. It must advance a coherent literary argument, discriminate narrator/character/institution/novel stances, reconcile specialists and strong alternatives, and state unresolved questions. It cannot be a concatenation of abstracts, a plot summary or a default ethical ranking. Specialist inputs and character monographs remain evidence-bearing dependencies; synthesis does not silently complete models.
