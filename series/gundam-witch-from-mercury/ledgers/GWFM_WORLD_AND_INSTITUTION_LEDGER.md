# The Witch from Mercury — World and institution ledger

Preserve organizational rules, technical constraints and chronology where they shape choice and consequence. Institutional statements, demonstrated mechanisms and ethical evaluations remain different kinds of records.

## Shared record discipline

The principal/integrator is the single mutable owner. Add records only from an accepted or explicitly provisional source transaction inside its authorized horizon. Every record binds a stable ID, unit and witness, source frontier, evidence IDs, evidence class, confidence, current claim references and review disposition. Use exact source names with stable subject keys once verified; preserve uncertain attribution rather than guessing.

Append event/revision history. Update a clearly identified current-state projection only from that history; preserve earlier boundaries and frozen checkpoints. Never overwrite an old belief with later knowledge. One locator ID may support several dimensions without duplicating raw source material.

At bootstrap there are **zero narrative records**. Header definitions below are contracts, not synthetic examples or findings. This file is current authority for its schema only; no narrative claim is eligible as evidence.

## Institution and rule record

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| entity/rule_id | Verified organization, office, rule or practice at this horizon |
| scope / authority_claim | Jurisdiction, stated legitimacy, issuing actor and source of the statement |
| incentives / resources | Who controls resources and what compliance or defiance costs |
| enforcement / exceptions | What actually happens, who benefits and who bears the cost |
| actor_options / accountability | Demonstrated alternatives and attribution of responsibility |
| information / concealment | Who knows the rule, exception or consequence |
| evidence / counterexample | Locator IDs, rival explanations and current claim state |

Corporate, school, familial and military authority are not interchangeable. Compare them only through observed practices. Fictional legality, institutional legitimacy and the analyst's moral judgment receive distinct labels.

## Technology and embodiment record

Record system/body/machine identity, attributed controller or speaker, demonstrated function, asserted function, conditions, limits, costs, failure modes, uncertainty and evidence IDs. Use distinct records for a technical mechanism, identity attribution and a personhood/ethical claim. Do not infer universal technological laws from one success, or infer consciousness solely from an interface label.

## Chronology and disclosure record

Record event ID, shown event time or bounded relative order, disclosure unit, witness, participating subjects, audience knowledge, each character's knowledge, reported versus observed basis, and unresolved ordering. Relative timing is valid when absolute dates are absent. Do not fill a chronology gap from a companion manga or later episode outside admission.

At a revelation, append the new disclosure and link affected older claims; never rewrite earlier character knowledge to include it. Distinguish reclassification of an old event from a new event.
