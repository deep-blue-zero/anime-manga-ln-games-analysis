# The Witch from Mercury — Form and performance ledger

## Shared record discipline

The principal/integrator is the single mutable owner. Add records only from an accepted or explicitly provisional source transaction inside its authorized horizon. Every record binds a stable ID, unit and witness, source frontier, evidence IDs, evidence class, confidence, current claim references and review disposition. Use exact source names with stable subject keys once verified; preserve uncertain attribution rather than guessing.

Append event/revision history. Update a clearly identified current-state projection only from that history; preserve earlier boundaries and frozen checkpoints. Never overwrite an old belief with later knowledge. One locator ID may support several dimensions without duplicating raw source material.

At bootstrap there are **zero narrative records**. Header definitions below are contracts, not synthetic examples or findings. This file is current authority for its schema only; no narrative claim is eligible as evidence.

A score cue title is catalogue metadata, not proof of its in-scene meaning. Do not transcribe long lyrics or dialogue. Do not infer exact synchronization from subtitle timing or emotional truth from delivery. OP/ED variants and their episode placement receive witness-specific observations when inspected.

## AV verification queue

For each real issue, record queue ID, neutral observation question, channel, interval/anchors, affected claim, disconfirming observation, required/optional status, escalation state, object/clock, observer coverage, finding and disposition. Keep the interpretation withheld from an independent observation pass until its log is frozen.

Use the [transaction protocol](../integration/GWFM_READING_TRANSACTION_PROTOCOL.md#av-reconciliation) and global reconciliation rules. Preserve REPORTED versus ANCHORED observations, disputed premises and unknown sampling. A required unresolved channel blocks the related completion claim. An explicitly narrowed endpoint claim may stand on stills when that scope is adequate.
