# The Witch from Mercury — Directed relationship ledger

Track how interactions alter trust, dependence, attachment, authority and available action. A→B and B→A are distinct records joined by an interaction ID, not an assumed symmetric relationship.

## Shared record discipline

The principal/integrator is the single mutable owner. Add records only from an accepted or explicitly provisional source transaction inside its authorized horizon. Every record binds a stable ID, unit and witness, source frontier, evidence IDs, evidence class, confidence, current claim references and review disposition. Use exact source names with stable subject keys once verified; preserve uncertain attribution rather than guessing.

Append event/revision history. Update a clearly identified current-state projection only from that history; preserve earlier boundaries and frozen checkpoints. Never overwrite an old belief with later knowledge. One locator ID may support several dimensions without duplicating raw source material.

At bootstrap there are **zero narrative records**. Header definitions below are contracts, not synthetic examples or findings. This file is current authority for its schema only; no narrative claim is eligible as evidence.

## Directed interaction record

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| relationship_id / interaction_id | Stable directed pair and shared event |
| subject / other / witnesses | Who acts toward whom and who observes |
| event/disclosure/state horizon | Timeline and knowledge bounds for each participant |
| public_status / private_account | Formal title/agreement and each person's account; unknown if unspoken |
| initiating_act / response | Request, refusal, offer, concealment, disclosure, intervention, repair or withdrawal |
| resources / options / constraints | Leverage, realistic alternatives, costs of refusal and asymmetry |
| trust / attachment / obligation | Dimension-specific change and its evidence; no invented numerical score |
| communication | Speech act, register, silence/body/voice evidence and channel limits |
| consequence / revision | Immediate outcome, later reinterpretation and current claim link |
| counterreading / opportunity | Rival explanation and whether absence of conduct had diagnostic opportunity |
| evidence / review | Locator IDs, confidence and acceptance disposition |

Romantic, familial, collegial, adversarial and corporate relationships may require different dimensions; do not force them onto one intimacy scale. Public “bride,” “groom,” “holder” or other labels, when actually admitted, are recorded as source terms with speakers and consequences rather than automatically translated into private feelings or consent.

A material relationship claim should identify the particular action or pattern, direction and boundary. Conflict is not automatic absence of care; care is not automatic freedom to refuse. Preserve the evidence needed to test both explanations.

## Specialist routing

At an accepted checkpoint, identify a dyad or ensemble whose reciprocal structure warrants a separate argument under the architecture. Link its real document once produced. Each person's monograph still explains their own behavior without requiring that study for basic comprehension.
