## Prospective capture and adjudication

A real prediction records prediction ID, freeze artifact/commit, exposure boundary, underlying rule, expected response family, alternatives, disconfirmation criterion, diagnostic opportunity, distance and confidence. Store the original freeze as a separate immutable checkpoint before exposure; this mutable ledger indexes it and later adjudication.

After authorized diagnostic exposure, append SUPPORTED, PARTLY_SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY, CONFOUNDED or UNRESOLVED, with source locators and reasons. Later-known outcomes and search-exposed details cannot be backdated as predictions. Prior knowledge or compromised independence is recorded, not hidden.

## Conflict and dependency handling

Classify disagreement as reporting/deception, knowledge difference, later revelation, changed condition, subtitle/translation error, edition revision, adaptation/continuity difference, paratext conflict or unresolved inconsistency. Record competing formulations rather than a compulsory harmonization.

For a load-bearing revision, identify every affected current portrait, specialist, model and final argument. Apply targeted corrections and preserve earlier freezes through errata or bounded successors. Current retrieval must surface the qualification while the correction is incomplete.
