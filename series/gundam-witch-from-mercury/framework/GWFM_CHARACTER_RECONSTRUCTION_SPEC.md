# The Witch from Mercury — Character reconstruction specification

## Responsibility and prerequisites

This specification defines how future models derive from accepted anime evidence. It creates no model, portrait, test result, readiness finding or global capability grade. The [method](GWFM_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md), [architecture](GWFM_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md), source frontier and current claim state govern all use. The [generic reconstruction method](../../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md) supplies the evidence/model/testing distinctions adapted here.

A model supports a declared use: for example, a reaction to a familiar interpersonal situation, a decision under comparable institutional pressure, or Japanese dialogue within an attested relationship. A plausible scene is not a test result. Generated dialogue, simulated scenes and previous model outputs never become source evidence.

## Subject and temporal contract

Bind every model to:

- stable subject key and any existing curated subject ID, otherwise null;
- anime continuity, exact edition/witness and admitted source units;
- developmental boundary `<subject>@P00` or `<subject>@E##`;
- what the person knows, believes, can do and can reasonably perceive at that boundary;
- material event/disclosure differences, aliases and unresolved identity links;
- immutable evidence basis commit, current claims, excluded supplements and gaps;
- actual available inspection lanes and any performance limitations.

Never merge people because they share a face, name, role or machine association. Conversely, a verified identity link does not erase earlier uncertainty. Pilot, machine/system, attributed speaker and institutional representative are distinct attribution roles until evidence resolves their relation. Do not import novel interiority or a parallel-setting relationship history into the anime subject.

## Evidence capture and conditional model

Retrieve observations from the character and relationship ledgers and their locator IDs, including ordinary-life samples. Keep directly shown speech/action, self-report, other-person report, institutional characterization, performed presentation and analyst inference distinct. External demeanor does not grant direct interior access.

A model contains:

| Layer | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Stable dispositions | Recurrence across contexts, strongest counterexamples and range of validity |
| Current state | Knowledge, goals, bodily/emotional condition, resources, roles and relevant relationships |
| Appraisal and priorities | What attracts attention, perceived stakes, competing obligations, likely errors |
| Conditional policy | Trigger + perceived stakes + state → response family; inhibitors, overrides, alternative explanation and falsifier |
| Directed relationships | Interlocutor-specific trust, candor, attachment, formality, conflict and repair |
| Speech/register | Japanese self-reference, address, discourse patterns and speech acts; public/private and ordinary/stress variation |
| Performance | Only from verified auditory/AV evidence, with calibrated locators and sampling limits |
| Counter-structure | Misleading stereotypes, decoy responses, failures, abstention conditions and evidence gaps |

Change tags distinguish `DISPOSITION`, `KNOWLEDGE`, `RELATIONSHIP`, `CONTEXT`, `ROLE`, `RESOURCE`, `REVEALED_NOT_NEW` and `UNRESOLVED`. A later revelation need not be a newly acquired trait.

Each candidate behavioral rule cites supporting and contrary evidence, the situations where it applies, and what would defeat it. Statuses: `OBSERVED` for an attested instance, `CANDIDATE_POLICY` for a supported but untested pattern, `PROMOTED_POLICY` only after actual validation. A description compatible with every action is not a predictive rule.

Especially test whether a crisis response transfers to ordinary school/work life, whether deference is specific to an authority or close relationship, and whether capacity differs from willingness. These are testing dimensions, not established traits of any cast member. Negative evidence requires a genuine opportunity to behave otherwise.

## Voice as a consequence of intention

Generate dialogue through appraisal → objective → speech act → relationship filter → register → wording. Maintain attested Japanese patterns separately from plausible but unattested phrasing. Translation-only evidence supports a model of the translation, not a Japanese voice model.

Assess decision fidelity and surface/register fidelity separately. Fluent dialogue fails if the person would not choose that speech act. Mimicking a repeated catchphrase cannot substitute for cognition or interlocutor sensitivity. Vocal stress, hesitation and emotion need inspected performance evidence; text punctuation alone does not establish their sound.

## Distance, uncertainty and counterfactuals

Use qualitative confidence HIGH / MODERATE / LOW / OPEN, with reasons and known gaps.

| Distance | Use |
| --- | --- |
| D0 | Attested situation; retrieval/reconstruction |
| D1 | Close variant of an observed situation |
| D2 | Novel situation within the same world and developmental boundary |
| D3 | Crossovers, altered worlds or other material counterfactuals |

For D3, declare assumed age, time, powers/resources, institutions, interlocutor familiarity and changes to biography. Adult counterfactuals do not invent intervening maturation, romantic experience or healed trauma. Separate retained tendencies from speculative adaptation. When the scenario requires unsupported knowledge or a radical new relationship, abstain or present bounded alternatives rather than a single canonical reaction.

Source-supported romantic psychology may be modeled within the actual evidence. Neither relationship labels nor audience shipping preferences justify inventing intimate preferences. Minors' source coverage remains limited to what the work depicts.

## Qualification is not validation

The character ledger owns monograph qualification and the actual paths of completed portraits. The architecture requires a self-contained portrait for every qualified character, but a portrait's short operational profile does not establish a separate model.

Model readiness is task- and boundary-specific. Record the requested use, available evidence, missing contexts/channels and next test. An unassessed subject is not ready by default and is not rejected by default. Keep these decisions inside the series; the designated evaluator owns global reconstruction capability records.

## Independent testing and records

For genuine prospective tests, freeze rules, predictions, alternatives, confidence, distance and exposure boundary **before** seeing the diagnostic source. Preserve the freeze; adjudicate in a separate record. No later unadmitted unit may be read merely to obtain a test. If the analyst already knows the outcome, label it retrospective fit. A fresh reviewer may conduct a separate prospective test only within an authorized frontier and genuinely uncontaminated context.

Validation should include:

- source-anchored scene reconstruction and held-out evidence where truly available;
- ordinary-life and crisis contrasts;
- changed-interlocutor and one-condition perturbations;
- plausible decoys that violate a named constraint;
- negative controls where abstention is correct;
- separate decision and Japanese-register judgments;
- comparison with a generic trait sketch to identify what the model actually adds.

Adjudication vocabulary: SUPPORTED, PARTLY_SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY, CONFOUNDED, UNRESOLVED. Record all outcomes, test counts and exclusions, not an unsupported accuracy percentage. No diagnostic opportunity is not a success.

A mature-use audit is rule-specific: PASS, PASS_WITH_BOUND, NEEDS_TARGETED_SOURCE_REVIEW or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. Name the independent reviewer, actual inspected evidence, disputed results and revisions. Each actual model receives a separate validation artifact under the architecture's naming contract.

## Revision and acceptance

Resolve literary disagreements through the claim ledger first; propagate accepted changes into models and affected tests. Preserve historical boundary models and their test records. A newly admitted unit may change knowledge, relationships or scope without disproving an earlier model at its own frontier.

Before a model is offered for use, verify source identity, boundary fidelity, evidence retrieval, conditional specificity, ordinary-life coverage or its absence, speech limits, independent tests and abstention. Remaining limits travel with every simulation request. No model is created during this bootstrap.
