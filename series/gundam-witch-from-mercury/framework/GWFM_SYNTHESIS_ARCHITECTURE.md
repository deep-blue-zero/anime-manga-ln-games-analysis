# The Witch from Mercury — Synthesis and corpus architecture

## Purpose and source model

This INITIAL architecture builds an anime-grounded account of narrative causality, character development, relationships, institutions, embodiment and audiovisual form, capable of supporting later bounded reconstruction. It depends on the [analytical method](GWFM_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md), the [reconstruction specification](GWFM_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_SPEC.md) and the global [initiation contract](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md). There is no predecessor corpus or superseded generation.

Proposed core: Japanese PROLOGUE and E01–E24, in disclosure order. Accepted source boundary: NONE. Later source families are described in the source register and remain outside admission. A same-franchise label does not merge novel, manga, music-video, commentary or parallel-world evidence into the anime. The main anime continuity uses separate witness records for edition variants; a genuine continuity change receives its own subject/state scope.

Each artifact has one identity, primary responsibility, source horizon and canonical home. The [entrypoint](../CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) is the sole current retrieval surface. Branch history preserves proposals; integrated main governs current analytical authority.

## Character and relationship responsibilities

Person-level synthesis is in scope. The character ledger is the canonical home for monograph qualification decisions and model readiness notes; it contains no invented qualification outcomes. At the first major synthesis checkpoint and no later than specialist-readiness review, examine **every character with differentiated evidence**, including supporting characters, adults and antagonists. Prominence and a fixed cast list do not decide eligibility.

Apply initiation section 5.5.1: convergent evidence, meaningful variation across situations/interlocutors, and constraints/counterexamples must support a differentiated portrait. A brief but concentrated sequence may qualify; repeated appearances may not. Record identity, source boundary, rationale, largest gaps, next review trigger and actual monograph path when it exists. Outcomes are `QUALIFIED_PENDING`, `COMPLETE_AT_BOUNDARY`, `DEFERRED_EVIDENCE` or `REASSESS_AFTER_REVISION`. Qualification creates a production obligation; no character-layer completion claim is valid while a qualified portrait is missing.

Each qualifying monograph must be readable alone and address, proportionally: identity/circumstances; personal history with event/disclosure distinctions; psychology; cognition and decisions; ordinary/crisis behavior; Japanese speech and performed communication; directed relationships; ordinary preferences; source-supported romance and boundaries; capability and limits; development; conditional operational profile; uncertainty; and traceability. Unknown dimensions stay unknown. A relationship section explains the person's conduct rather than sending the reader away to a linked study.

Review **self-containment** and **fidelity** separately before acceptance, record both results in an acceptance receipt and link it from the qualification decision. Ask representative questions from the portrait alone; verify load-bearing answers against admitted evidence. A portrait is not a validated model or a capability grade. The reconstruction specification governs that distinct layer.

A dyad earns an independent study when its reciprocal structure, changing terms, causal influence or unresolved tension cannot be adequately explained by one person's portrait. Track A→B and B→A separately. A public status is not equivalent to mutual attachment, and asymmetry is not automatically proof of coercion: analyze the particular options, power and conduct evidenced. Ensemble studies are justified by recurring group dynamics, not by a roster.

## Specialist plan without predetermined conclusions

The following **responsibilities** must be assessed before final convergence. Separate documents are anticipated only when the evidence supports sustained independent arguments; related responsibilities may share a document, with the mapping recorded at readiness.

| Responsibility | Questions and evidence required |
| --- | --- |
| Agency, family, care and dependence | Character choices, refusal opportunities, obligations, trust changes and counterexamples; determine where explanations compete |
| Corporate institutions and organized violence | Declared rules versus actual enforcement, incentives, material constraints, inequalities and downstream effects; avoid treating the setting as a generic real-world allegory |
| Technology, embodiment and personhood | Demonstrated mechanics, identity attributions, control, costs and limits; distinguish factual mechanism from ethical status |
| Relationship development and intimacy | Directed actions, speech, consent, public/private categories and repair; separate the anime's evidence from later paratext |
| Audiovisual form and music | Verified staging/voice/editing/sound evidence and recurrence changes; explain dramatic function rather than list motifs |
| Ending, causality and accountability | Only after the relevant final units are admitted: which causal problems are resolved, which social/ethical questions remain open, and what the ending actually shows |

Cross-franchise or theatrical-literary comparisons are optional later studies requiring their own source basis. Familiar parallels do not supply a hidden interpretive template. A proposed specialist can be merged, deferred or removed with a reason; actual recurring evidence can require a new one.

## Dependencies and completion gates

1. **Foundation gate:** current eligible method/architecture, source register, necessary ledgers, standing independent-reading configuration, verified required capabilities, authorized bounded run and explicit OPEN lock. This bootstrap establishes the documents but leaves source/run/capability prerequisites unresolved.
2. **Unit acceptance:** complete required inspection, independent source-aware contribution and review, reconciled disputes, retrievable evidence and synchronized deltas. A polished reading alone is insufficient.
3. **Sequential boundary completion:** every unit in the authorized run is accepted; omissions and edition exceptions reconciled. Record run completion and no next admission; do not automatically open supplements.
4. **Longitudinal and architecture audit:** reconcile character/relationship/world states, claim history and chronology; resolve or bound required AV debt. Review at P00 pilot, E06, E12, E18 and E24 where those units fall within the authorized run, or earlier on a material discovery. These are review targets, never admission permissions.
5. **Specialist readiness:** freeze a retrievable evidence snapshot, qualify all evidenced character candidates, identify real specialist obligations and gaps. Source completion alone does not satisfy this gate.
6. **Literary convergence:** produce required portraits and specialists, reconcile shared claims and counterarguments, then write an integrated argument rather than concatenate summaries. Full-series readiness lists every outstanding portrait and required evidence obligation.
7. **Reconstruction validation:** models may be developed at an already accepted bounded checkpoint without waiting for the full series, but mature-use claims require the specified independent tests. Modeling is an output branch of the literary corpus, not authority over it. Character-layer completion still requires qualified portraits.
8. **Release:** verify traceability, temporal/continuity boundaries, portrait self-containment/fidelity, unresolved limitations, authority metadata and repository audit. Freeze only what is actually complete at the named boundary. A bootstrap or E24 catalogue entry is no release.

## Contradictions, revision and memory

Classify apparent conflict before resolving it: mistaken belief, deception, later disclosure, changed condition, translation or subtitle error, edition revision, adaptation difference, paratext dispute or genuine unresolved inconsistency. Keep competing supported readings; never harmonize merely because all sources share a title.

The locator index owns evidence identity. The claim ledger owns material proposition/revision history. Character, relationship, institutional and formal ledgers own their cumulative dimensions; a changed claim triggers targeted updates to dependent syntheses and models. Log old/new formulations, evidence, transition and affected paths. Retain frozen earlier readings/checkpoints and append corrections or successors with reciprocal supersession metadata.

Entrypoint and ledger current projections are mutable; factual history and adjudication records are appended, not erased. Freeze prospective captures before exposure and accepted checkpoints at their declared horizon. Move dated operational history to a linked changelog when it grows; avoid workflow IDs or publication-status copies in the current map.

Compare base, returned candidate and current target before integration; do not replace an existing artifact from a summary.
