# The Witch from Mercury — Analytical method

## Scope and authority

This V1 method governs future source-bounded readings of the Japanese anime PROLOGUE and E01–E24. It is paired with the [synthesis architecture](GWFM_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md), the [reconstruction specification](GWFM_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_SPEC.md) and the sole [entrypoint](../CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md). It establishes no story findings and admits no source.

The [initiation policy](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md), [inspection-lane policy](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_MEDIUM_AND_INSPECTION_LANE_ROUTING_POLICY.md), [bundle specification](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EPISODE_BUNDLE_SPECIFICATION.md), [topology policy](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EXECUTION_TOPOLOGY_AND_CAPABILITY_ROUTING_POLICY.md) and [continuation policy](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_SEQUENTIAL_EXECUTION_SCOPE_AND_CONTINUATION_POLICY.md) govern their respective responsibilities. This adapts the generic anime method's source seal and channel distinctions; it does not adopt a generic template as a substitute for series-specific practice.

## Witnesses and reading order

Default proposed order: PROLOGUE, E01 through E24 in numbered broadcast order. PROLOGUE is a distinct unit, never a second E01. Story chronology and disclosure order are separate. No chronological rearrangement based on future revelations is permitted.

The locked Japanese audiovisual witness is the narrative basis. Japanese subtitle/transcript evidence governs verified wording; record whether it is an official track, OCR, ASR, listening transcription or corrected derivative. A text–audio disagreement requires verification against the retained source rather than automatic deference to either derivative. English localization supports navigation and translation comparison, not Japanese voice modeling. Broadcast, streaming and disc revisions have separate witness IDs and clocks. Alternate soundtracks, recaps and previews are not silently spliced into a preferred cut.

Full OP/ED sequences and post-credit narrative belong to the inspected episode if present in its locked witness. Record any preview separately: later-unit material accidentally disclosed by previews, menus, title listings or search results is exposure, not admitted episode evidence. Deduplicated OP/ED references must still be checked for changed imagery, sound, credits, placement or episode framing.

Cradle Planet, the prose adaptation, Vanadis Heart and Seishun Frontier require a separate admission decision and medium-specific method extension. Their identity is established in the [source register](../source-inventory/GWFM_SOURCE_REGISTER.md), but their narrative is excluded. Other Gundam timelines, stock franchise rules and fan terminology cannot fill unexplained world mechanics.

## Knowledge and claim discipline

At unit N, admitted evidence ends at N. Keep separate:

- event time; disclosure time; each character's demonstrated knowledge;
- what happened; what a person reports; what an institution claims;
- a change in behavior; a change in circumstances; later disclosure of an existing condition;
- personal choice; compliance under threat; strategic performance; inability to act;
- shown embodied conduct; inferred feeling or motive; the analyst's ethical judgment.

Use `OBS` for inspected observation, `REPORT` for a character/institutional statement, `INF` for supported inference, `HYP` for a contestable explanation, `VALUE` for a stated evaluative judgment, and `OPEN` for unresolved matters. Each material claim also names its evidence channel and confidence. An observed utterance establishes that it was said, not that its content is true.

Before exposure to a new unit, preserve inherited hypotheses and any predictions with disconfirmation criteria. Do not label known endings, remembered identities or supplemental revelations as prospective insight. Record known prior exposure honestly; a source-bounded rereading can still be valuable without being blind. Future-aware aliases must not collapse initially distinct identities. Add identity links when the admitted source establishes them, preserving earlier labels and attribution uncertainty.

## Episode procedure

1. **Verify entry state.** Confirm current scope, witness, source receipt, capable participants and last accepted unit. Resolve missing scenes, tracks and clock mapping before claims depend on them.
2. **Freeze entering hypotheses.** Record exposure limits and the state being tested. Freeze any genuinely prospective prediction before seeing its test.
3. **Inspect the complete admitted unit.** Use bundle coverage to traverse all narrative scenes, including quiet transitions and ending material. A contact sheet is navigation, not proof of frame or audio inspection. Record coverage by channel and actual intervals.
4. **Segment the causal sequence.** Give scenes stable local IDs. Capture location, participants, opportunity, information, decision, intervention, consequence and unresolved outcome. Distinguish an asserted mechanism from a demonstrated one.
5. **Read character and relationship changes.** Retain ordinary conversation, humor, competence, habits, preferences and care alongside duels, confrontation or crisis. Identify who initiates, refuses, yields, conceals, repairs or changes the terms of an exchange.
6. **Test institutional and technological claims.** Record declared rules, incentives, enforcement, exceptions, beneficiaries and costs; separate authority to command from ability to comply. Track what interfaces, bodies and machines visibly permit without reverse-engineering unsupported lore.
7. **Inspect formal evidence.** Follow the channel contract below. Queue unresolved load-bearing motion, vocal, music or editing claims for an adequate route.
8. **Obtain independent contributions and critical review.** Use the transaction protocol. Reconcile substantial disagreements and preserve alternatives with their locators.
9. **Write the readable unit-end account.** After prospective freezes, finalize the synopsis and extended analysis. Do not expose that spoiler-bearing document to an earlier-horizon participant.
10. **Close the transaction.** Update the required cumulative homes, source/coverage state, claim dispositions and entrypoint together. Advance the accepted high-water mark only after semantic acceptance; partial channel completion is explicit.

## Series-specific analytical questions

These are questions to test, not predetermined conclusions or a checklist of findings.

| Dimension | Questions the admitted episode can answer |
| --- | --- |
| Agency and dependency | What options does each person recognize? Who defines the available choices? Can care, protection, gratitude or obligation constrain refusal? What would distinguish conviction from compliance? |
| Relationships and consent | How do public titles, formal agreements and private conduct relate? Are trust, attachment, bargaining power and knowledge reciprocal? Whose account of the relationship is available? |
| Family and succession | Which expectations are inherited, rejected or reinterpreted? Does a character act from loyalty, fear, ambition, affection or an unresolved combination? |
| School and corporate order | How do competitive rules, technical education, resources, ownership and public status shape choices? When do stated rules diverge from enforcement? |
| Earth/space and organized violence | What inequalities and claims of legitimacy are actually demonstrated? Keep the responsibility of individuals, corporate actors and political institutions distinct; group membership alone proves no motive. |
| Technology, embodiment and personhood | What capacities, costs and limits are demonstrated? Who speaks for or controls a body or system? Keep identity, agency, technical function and others' descriptions separate. |
| Ethical deliberation and aftermath | Distinguish intention, foreseeable harm, available alternatives, responsibility, remorse, repair and forgiveness. Tactical success does not settle moral legitimacy; sympathy does not erase consequences. |
| Domestic life and belonging | What does routine school/work life reveal that crisis scenes do not? Record activities and preferences only with the choice context and opportunity for alternatives. |
| Dramatic form | How do scene order, reaction shots, distance, framing, sound and changes in tone affect what the audience can know? Is a recurrence structural, lexical, visual or musical, and what counterexamples exist? |

Investigate romance and queer characterization through the depicted relationship, language, staging and actions; neither promotional labels nor external controversy determines the reading. Creator statements are dated paratext if later admitted. Interpretive uncertainty must name the uncertain proposition rather than erase clearly supported observations.

## Governing bundle lane and AV companion

The default is `BUNDLE_MEDIATED` with Japanese text verification. A complete bundle must provide provenance, calibrated timestamps, clean visual samples and selection manifest, available primary-language text, dialogue/frame linkage, aligned audio access, extraction/QA history and disclosed missing layers. A screenshot ZIP or subtitle file alone is a partial derivative.

| Channel | Required discipline |
| --- | --- |
| Static composition and expression | Cite inspected clean frames. Specify the moment; endpoints cannot prove the gesture connecting them. |
| Wording and register | Cite the verified Japanese cue and speaker. Preserve uncertainty in attribution and distinguish translated wording. |
| Voice, silence, sound and music | Delivery claims need verified listening; an ASR result or waveform alone establishes no timbre, intent or emotion. Measurements are labeled measurements. |
| Motion, action geography and microacting | Escalate when a material claim depends on the continuous path. Still frames can establish endpoints only. |
| Editing and audiovisual synchronization | A cut list can establish measured durations; expressive rhythm and music–image relations require suitable continuous inspection. |
| Identity and interiority | Performance is observable; motive and personhood arguments remain evidence-qualified interpretation. Recognizing an actor is not speaker attribution. |

The AV companion covers load-bearing character reactions, combat/duel geography, agency at action boundaries, musical cue/image relationships and editing transitions. Use `VIDEO_NOT_REQUIRED` where the actual question is answered by available layers, `VIDEO_TARGETED_ESCALATION` for a diagnostic interval, or `VIDEO_FULL_EPISODE_ESCALATION` where whole-episode form or distributed evidence is material. No universal full-video requirement is inferred from the genre.

For each escalation, record neutral question, claim ID, channel, interval, required/optional status, source object, result and open debt. The continuous reviewer verifies combined perception and effective visual sampling; extraction with ffmpeg is not perception. Calibrate beginning/middle/end anchors for full episodes and enough independent anchors for clips, documenting offset, drift and precision. The ordinary ±0.5-second navigation tolerance never licenses finer timing claims. Use higher-rate inspection or measurement for brief events.

The [transaction protocol](../integration/GWFM_READING_TRANSACTION_PROTOCOL.md#av-reconciliation) adapts the corpus reconciliation route. Required unresolved evidence blocks the associated claim and relevant unit acceptance unless the claim/scope is legitimately narrowed and the reason preserved. Optional enhancements may remain open; they never become positive evidence through omission.

## Canonical reading structure and evidence

A real reading is created only after authorized source inspection, with `artifact_type: deep_reading` and accurate authority metadata. Its structure is flexible, but these responsibilities must be recoverable:

- short witness/boundary/entering-state orientation;
- **current-unit story synopsis** near the front, in coherent prose describing concrete actions, causal turns, disclosures and the ending state;
- source-bounded episode argument and scene-level evidence;
- character, relationship, institution and formal changes proportional to the episode;
- Japanese wording and performance distinctions;
- strongest counterevidence, competing explanations and unresolved questions;
- prospective end-state captured before later exposure;
- cumulative deltas and participation/review/acceptance record.

The synopsis may spoil the current unit, never a later unadmitted one. A report or suspicion stays attributed. Cite evidence IDs for consequential events without making every sentence a table.

Public evidence IDs follow `GWFM-EV-<unit>-<NNN>`; canonical unit keys are `P00` and `E01`–`E24`. Witness-qualified source IDs and clock/derivative links live in the locator index. Do not invent hashes, cue IDs, timestamps or episode findings from catalogue metadata.

## Revision and stop rules

Mutable ledgers preserve event history and current-state projections. Frozen readings, predictions and checkpoints are not rewritten after new exposure; append claim revisions or attributable errata with `PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN / REVISE / DOWNGRADE / REJECT / OPEN`. A new scene does not automatically invalidate earlier interpretation at its own frontier.

Stop on absent authorization, wrong witness, missing required channel, contaminated prospective assignment, unverified independent execution, unresolved identity affecting evidence, or conflicting state. Preserve valid work and record the exact blocker; do not repair it with remembered plot or silently advance the frontier. Source completion, narrative acceptance, synthesis readiness and repository publication remain different statuses.
