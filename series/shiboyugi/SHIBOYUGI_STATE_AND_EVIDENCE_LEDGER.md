# State and evidence ledger

## Responsibility and current baseline

This is the canonical cumulative home for future subject identities, directed relationships, epistemic state, disclosure/chronology distinctions and material constraints supported by admitted Shiboyugi prose. It links these changes to full readings and exact evidence; it does not replace them with summaries.

The [architecture](SHIBOYUGI_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) justifies a combined home initially because an action can change a person's knowledge, available choices and relationships at once. It does not assume a cast, institution, game structure or economy. Separate these into new files only when an evidence-grounded retrieval burden warrants an explicit amendment and preserved crosswalk.

**Baseline SBY-STATE-BASE-000, 2026-11-13:** no narrative admitted; source high-water mark NONE; zero subjects, relationship records, event/knowledge records, constraint observations, voice observations and narrative evidence IDs. There are no entering/exit freezes or completed unit transactions. All ten available witness slots remain unadmitted. This baseline records an absence of project evidence, not an absence of characters or relationships in the work.

No empty character table or fabricated sample observation is created. The operating contracts below initialize the required preservation responsibility. First real records must arise in a future owner-authorized unit transaction.

## Record identity and evidence route

Assign stable IDs when evidence first warrants a record: SBY-P followed by a stable numeric identity for subjects, SBY-R for directed relationship histories, SBY-K for knowledge/disclosure records and SBY-S for other state or constraint histories. Never renumber an ID because a subject becomes more important. These prefixes are allocation grammar, not current subject assignments.

A real observation receives a unit-qualified evidence ID in its full reading. Each ledger entry names the current factual or interpretive formulation, evidence class, confidence, actual source component, disclosure frontier, full-reading path/anchor and associated claim ID when an interpretive argument exists. A locator resolves through the [source register](SHIBOYUGI_SOURCE_REGISTER.md) to exact witness/extraction identity.

Use a readable entry title and enough explanatory prose to say what changed and why the record matters. A list of evidence IDs alone is not a state history. Long arguments remain in the reading or current specialist with a verified full-argument link; this ledger preserves the material delta, qualifications and dependency route.

The public ledger does not contain private source object IDs, raw receipts, local paths, source dumps or acquisition filenames. Those remain retrievable through authorized evidence-plane records.

## Named subject identity

A subject entry begins only when the admitted source establishes an identifiable person or a deliberately unnamed figure needing a stable reference. Record the source-established name, Japanese form, meaningful aliases, boundary at which each identity relation is established and unresolved identity alternatives. Do not assign an invented personal name or conflate people because they occupy similar roles.

Keep actor, observer, thinker, speaker, addressee and affected person explicit where attribution could matter. A scene-bound entry can preserve a concealed identity while a later unit-end note records its disclosure; neither rewrites the earlier knowledge state. Narrative-function labels are explanatory tools, not habitual substitutes for known names.

A subject's current view links the full historical sequence of changes. For each supported change, preserve:

| Responsibility | What a real record must explain |
|---|---|
| Situation | Witness/component, scene, interlocutor/audience, ordinary or crisis context and available alternatives |
| Evidence access | Direct action/speech, represented thought, narrator presentation, another person's report or inference |
| Before and after | What changed in state and what remained stable; valid boundary and confidence |
| Mechanism | Trigger, perceived stakes, appraisal, choice and consequence, with explicit versus inferred links |
| Competing explanation | Role, temporary condition, knowledge, resources or relationship context that could explain the same act |
| Counterevidence | Diagnostic contrary cases or a stated absence of opportunity; not merely a missing search hit |
| Retrieval | Exact observation route, full explanatory prose and affected claim/model/synthesis anchors |

Distinguish revealed-not-new information from actual development. Stable dispositions require recurrence or strong explicit support; a single unusual act stays a situated observation. A later interpretation of a prior act is a retrospective annotation linked to claim history.

## Directed relationships

A relationship history is ordered by subject and counterpart. A toward B and B toward A are separate views even when they share an event. Record attested recognition, expectations, trust, dependency, power, conflict, reciprocity, desire or other source-earned dimensions; do not require unsupported dimensions to be filled.

Each change explains what one party knows or believes about the other, what the other party can know of that state, what action or disclosure demonstrates change and what alternatives remain. Mutual language such as “they trust each other” needs support from both directions. A third party's characterization is evidence of that observer's account before it is evidence of the relation.

Link a relationship-conditioned behavior or register shift to the relevant subject state. Do not globalize behavior seen with one interlocutor. Preserve asymmetry and unresolved disagreement rather than forcing each dyad into a single score or stage label.

A relationship specialist becomes justified only when its cumulative explanatory problem exceeds a straightforward set of unit deltas. Its output must recover the underlying directed histories, not replace them with a label.

## Knowledge, disclosure and event time

For each material epistemic change, identify who holds the knowledge or belief, what proposition they hold, how they learned it, confidence in its attribution, the source/disclosure point and any established event time. Separate known fact, belief, rumor, deception, conjecture and reader inference.

Record whether a fact is newly true, newly known to a character, newly disclosed to the reader or newly inferred by the analyst. These are different changes. If event chronology is uncertain, retain relative order or UNKNOWN rather than manufacturing dates.

Prospective entering/exit states and any within-unit freezes remain immutable. The mutable current view may point to a revised retrospective account but must preserve the earlier state and its argument route. Later bonus or adaptation evidence does not silently enter a main-volume knowledge record.

## Rule-like assertions, constraints and material choices

This lane activates only where admitted prose supplies relevant evidence. It does not presume an actual game system or recurring livelihood mechanism.

For a rule-like statement, preserve who asserts it, who receives it, exact bounded wording if load-bearing, purported scope, participant belief, observed enforcement or exception, and unresolved alternatives. Link assertion and enforcement as separate observations. A successful or failed act is evidence about its circumstances; general scope requires further support.

For a material constraint, distinguish actual resources, perceived availability, stated costs, costs borne, institutional or interpersonal pressure and alternatives considered. Explain how the constraint affects a specific decision instead of assigning a universal motive. A title-prompted question is a design question until evidence is admitted.

When an interpretation becomes a recurring rule, policy or thematic claim, register it in the claim ledger with its full argument and counterevidence. This ledger preserves observed state and asserted constraints; the claim ledger owns the changing interpretation.

## Written voice and supporting visual evidence

Preserve Japanese speech observations with speaker, interlocutor, circumstance, speech act, knowledge boundary, exact quotation status and source locator. Link repeated self-reference, address, politeness, endings or diction to state and relationship rather than treating a memorable phrase as a personality.

Narrated thought, free indirect wording, quoted speech and actual dialogue are distinct channels. A private thought is not an attested spoken register. Voice evidence here concerns written expression; performed delivery, timbre and timing are outside the current source scope.

An illustration observation requires actual visual inspection and a component-qualified locator. Distinguish image content from the prose claim it frames or supports. Keep spoiler placement, inspected coverage and unresolved image-text questions visible. An audit's image-decode count cannot populate this lane.

## Updating and preserving the ledger

For each accepted source unit, append a transaction entry naming the reading, entering and exit boundaries, records created or changed, evidence and claim links, unchanged required dimensions and outstanding obligations. State “no material change” with a reason when appropriate; do not append empty records for symmetry.

Append substantive deltas; update the current routing view only after its supporting record is accepted. Preserve earlier formulations and validity intervals. A later amendment names its predecessor and the claim transition that justifies it. Never rewrite an earlier record to make its reader appear to possess later knowledge.

A large reclassification must name affected relationships, chronology, constraints, voice records, specialists and models. Apply supported changes to those actual homes and record remaining work. If an exact source check is unavailable, keep the affected record bounded or open; a confident prose rewrite is not verification.

## Review, freeze and handoff

At a meaningful checkpoint, test identity consistency, directionality, temporal validity, evidence retrieval and whether major recurring observations are orphaned. Sample ordinary and crisis contexts where both exist, favorable and contrary cases, and records whose conclusions changed. A table's completeness is not evidentiary completeness.

The checkpoint routes to a frozen evidence snapshot with full readings and arguments. Its dispatch list can be compact, but recipients must retrieve the actual relevant prose. Keep one mutable owner for this ledger; a specialist returns proposed deltas against the frozen base rather than overwriting the live file.

Future subject readiness records can be maintained here when evidence warrants them, under the [reconstruction contract](SHIBOYUGI_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_CONTRACT.md). They state coverage and gaps, not evaluator grades or owner permission. There are no such records at this baseline.

## Completion and recovery

A unit's cumulative work is complete only when source coverage, full reading, state changes, claim/forecast changes and exit state agree. A missing required change blocks that unit's transaction even if the essay is polished. Recover the last accepted transaction and current source boundary before continuing; never infer progress from an unaccepted draft.

The present recoverable state remains SBY-STATE-BASE-000 with no narrative. Resume through the [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md), not by adding records from title familiarity or another project's analysis.
