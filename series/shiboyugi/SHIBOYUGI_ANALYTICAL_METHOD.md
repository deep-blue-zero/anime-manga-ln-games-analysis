# Analytical method

## 1. Purpose and governing question

This method governs future source-bounded reading of **Playing Death Games to Put Food on the Table / 死亡遊戯で飯を食う。** from the supplied Japanese novel witnesses. It adapts the [generic novel method v1.1](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_NOVEL_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) and the [initiation policy v1.11](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md). The [architecture](SHIBOYUGI_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) owns cumulative destinations and completion gates; the [source register](SHIBOYUGI_SOURCE_REGISTER.md) owns current witness facts.

The central reading question is: what does the admitted prose let a reader establish at this disclosure boundary, through whose perception and language, and what changes in actions, understanding, relationships or available choices? A completed analysis must explain its answer in sustained prose with retrievable evidence, rival explanations and limits. Tables organize retrieval; they do not replace close reading or an argument.

No narrative has been read for this INITIAL foundation. The title motivates possible questions about asserted rules, enforced constraints and material livelihood, but it establishes none of their narrative forms. Do not assume a protagonist, game cycle, institution, debt, economy, cast, narrator, chronology or recurring ontology from the title. The future source can earn, alter or retire these design questions.

The lock is CLOSED. This method neither authorizes admission nor certifies owner approval, narrative understanding or reconstruction readiness.

## 2. Unit and source contract

The initial candidate backbone is the supplied numbered volumes 01–10. The normal future closure unit is one admitted volume, with chapter or dramatic-movement captures inside it. Internal segmentation follows actual headings, changes of setting or participants, goal changes, disclosure shifts and narrative form. It must not follow arbitrary extraction chunks or an assumed one-game-per-volume pattern.

Before reading, bind the selected witness to its exact source receipt and component map. Keep distinct the state of a file being present, transport-verified, byte-bound, admitted, inspected and closed. All current files are merely available with metadata matched to the supplied audit. Ten available units are not a continuous-run authorization.

Electronic bonuses require special care: every recorded title includes an electronic-bonus designation, yet the components are not mapped. Separate main narrative, bonus narrative, illustrations, creator statements, advertisements and previews before exposing their content to the reader. Only explicitly admitted components enter the reading. A bonus may be later-disclosing or independent; do not classify it by its filename alone. Record the actual release position, narrative position if established and admission order independently.

Japanese prose governs narration, register, wording and speech claims. A gloss is labeled as an analyst's translation. A translation or adaptation can be a separately admitted comparison witness, never an invisible substitute. Textual variants require separate witnesses and a map of additions, deletions, substitutions or relocations before explanations are proposed.

No repair of V03, V06 or V07 is presumed necessary merely because the audit records non-first mimetype entries. A future access failure is a technical/source obligation. Preserve original bytes; any authorized derivative must retain a recoverable relationship to the original and its own identity.

## 3. Prospective knowledge discipline

At entry, record what the analyst has already encountered: source units, cover or bonus exposure, later summaries, adaptation familiarity, existing analyses and recalled franchise knowledge. A fresh session is not automatically a blind reader. Exclude external knowledge from source-derived claims and record material contamination honestly; do not manufacture a prospective status after exposure.

Maintain five separate orders: observed numbered/publication order, source disclosure order, inferred story chronology, each person's knowledge acquisition and project admission order. Publication or story chronology may remain unresolved. A later-disclosed explanation can change the current retrospective account without changing what the earlier reader or character could know.

Before V01, the entering freeze records no admitted story facts. Before later units, freeze prior-unit developments, character and directed-relationship states, open claims, eligible forecasts and disclosure limits from the last accepted boundary. Within a unit, preserve a chapter or movement freeze wherever a consequential inference or prediction will later be tested. Freeze before exposure to the testing passage, not merely before writing the completed reading.

Each freeze identifies its boundary, permitted inputs and exact retained artifact or snapshot. Forecast wording and rationale stay immutable; outcomes go in separate adjudications. If the testing material was already read, label the exercise retrospective. A prediction without a diagnostic opportunity is not a successful prediction or a failure.

Do not feed a finished unit reading, its synopsis, an exit ledger or a later reconstruction model to an analyst assigned an earlier horizon. Supply a filtered packet with provenance and permitted inputs. Identity minimization or pseudonyms require an explicitly scoped benchmark; ordinary readings use established names within their actual boundary.

## 4. Coverage before selective interpretation

At an authorized preparation stage, map actual package components and the admitted narrative sections. The reading receipt records which narrative sections were read, which illustrations were inspected, which components were excluded and why, and what could not be accessed. Attribute a future focalizer per section only after source inspection.

Read the whole admitted unit. Search and extracted snippets aid retrieval but do not establish coverage. Select close-reading passages for causal, formal or character importance and for disconfirming power, not only because they support a favored hypothesis. Attend to ordinary exchanges and low-stakes decisions where present; an exclusively memorable-crisis sample cannot establish a behavioral baseline.

Required prose access is tested on the selected witness. Verify Japanese text fidelity, section order, ruby base/reading distinction and meaningful formatting. Record normalizations used for search; preserve exact source form in checked quotations. A successful parser or inherited audit is not evidence that the analyst has inspected the narrative.

## 5. Current-unit story synopsis

Every future plot-bearing unit reading has a clearly labeled **Current-unit story synopsis — unit-end spoilers** near the front. Place it after the brief witness receipt and entering context, before the governing thesis, extended diagnostic interpretation or ledger inventories.

Write or finalize it only after applicable prospective captures have been frozen. The synopsis orients an independent human reader to what happens: opening situation, identifiable participants and stakes, consequential actions and decisions, concrete scene and physical-event mechanics where established, causal turning points, major disclosures and the state at the ending. Scale its length to narrative density. It may describe the unit's climax or resolution, but never import a later unadmitted revelation.

Distinguish witnessed events, a narrator's framing, participant testimony, conjecture, disputed attribution and unresolved outcomes in ordinary prose. A report that someone won, failed, suffered or intended something remains a report until its evidentiary status warrants more. Do not replace what happened with abstract thematic significance. Orient inherited prior-unit events briefly and label them as entering context.

Use unobtrusive source locators or stable observation IDs for material events. The synopsis is not an independent evidence source, a prospective freeze, a causal map or a substitute for full analysis. Its event account and the detailed causal interpretation should agree without duplicating every paragraph.

A non-narrative component audit may mark this section inapplicable with a concrete reason. The presence of metadata-only work does not create a fictional story synopsis.

## 6. Causal and formal reading

Reconstruct dramatic movements in the order disclosed. For each movement, identify the entering conditions, active named participants, available information, choices actually shown, changes and leaving conditions. Test whether the apparent connection is explicit causation, a decision, institutional or material constraint, misinformation, coincidence, delayed disclosure or unresolved.

Build an evidence-linked causal account separate from the synopsis. Distinguish an event's occurrence from a character's explanation of its cause. Retain rival causal paths when the prose does not decide between them. Explain why a turning point matters through its consequences for alternatives and later actions within the admitted boundary, not by invoking a remembered ending.

If the prose presents rule-like statements, capture the speaker or textual source, addressee, wording, scope, apparent exceptions and evidence of enforcement. Keep **rule assertion**, **witnessed enforcement**, **participant belief** and **analyst inference** separate. A participant's successful action does not alone prove a universal rule. An unexplained exception does not alone prove deception. These are conditional analytical distinctions, not a claim that the work has any particular game system.

If resources, compensation, subsistence, work, risk or dependency become material, ask what is actually available, who believes it available, what cost is stated, what cost is borne and how this changes choice. Do not infer a coherent economy, occupational identity or motive from the English title. Keep formal terms, customary expectations, personal preferences and coercive circumstances distinct.

Genre or ethical interpretations must be earned by the source. Depiction, explanatory power, sympathy and successful action do not equal endorsement. Describe morally complex actions precisely without substituting condemnation or celebration for causal, relational or formal analysis.

## 7. Narration, focalization and reliability

Identify the narrative mode actually encountered, including shifts: first-person present or retrospective, close or omniscient third person, free indirect discourse, embedded testimony or other attested forms. Do not preassign one mode to the series.

For a consequential statement, distinguish narrator assertion, focalizer perception, another person's report, reader synthesis from admitted evidence and analyst inference. A focalized description first establishes a perception. It does not automatically establish an external fact or another person's interior state.

Evaluate reliability per epistemic act. Ask what the speaker or narrator can observe, remember, understand or conceal at that point; what competing evidence exists; and what remains untested. A person can report an event accurately but misread a motive. Do not use “unreliable narrator” as a substitute for locating a specific discrepancy.

When interiority is represented, trace only the supported links among circumstance, perceived evidence, appraisal, affect, considered alternatives, choice, consequence and later revision. Mark explicit links, inferred links and missing links. Thought, speech and behavior can diverge without establishing hypocrisy or an enduring disposition. Later revelation of an already operative motive is disclosure, not necessarily developmental change.

## 8. Character and relationship evidence

Use names or unambiguous natural short forms once identity is established within the relevant boundary. Preserve meaningful aliases and deliberate anonymity; do not invent names or conflate people with similar roles. Keep actor, observer, thinker, speaker, recipient and affected person identifiable in causal prose.

For materially active subjects, separate stable disposition, current developmental state, situational condition, knowledge, role, resources and relationship-conditioned behavior. Capture the trigger, perceived stakes, options, action, result and counterevidence. A crisis act is not automatically a trait; a performed or learned response is not automatically false.

Relationships are directed. A's trust in B is not B's trust in A. Record asymmetries of information, power, dependency, expectation and reciprocity when the source supports them. Test whether a change is stable, situational, merely claimed, or observed by only one party. Explain what actions or disclosures become newly possible between named people.

Ordinary-life behavior, hesitation, humor, refusal, care and omissions matter when actually present. Absence is evidence only if a diagnostic opportunity existed. No opportunity means a gap. Unsupported psychological or clinical diagnoses are excluded.

The [state ledger](SHIBOYUGI_STATE_AND_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md) preserves changes and evidence routes. The [reconstruction contract](SHIBOYUGI_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_CONTRACT.md) defines the later, separately evaluated route to bounded models. A reading does not silently become an operational model.

## 9. Japanese wording, written voice and visual evidence

For load-bearing wording, record a brief EXACT_VERIFIED quotation, deterministic locator and labeled gloss. WORKING_TRANSCRIPTION and PARAPHRASE are distinct states and cannot prove exact linguistic features. Preserve meaningful ruby distinctions, self-reference, address, sentence endings, politeness, lexical choices and shifts from the attested baseline.

Written voice requires recurring evidence across contexts and interlocutors. Explain speech acts and pragmatic aims before imitating surface patterns. A phrase can be routine, strategic, quoted, narrated or unusual; do not generalize from one line. Narration borrowing a subject's diction is not automatically direct speech. A character's private thought is not a line they would say aloud.

Illustrations may frame, corroborate or anticipate prose; they do not overrule it. Inventory inspected images and their source placement. A cover or early insert can disclose later information, so state its exposure boundary. Claims about an image require STILL_IMAGE inspection; decoding 208 raster images in a prior audit is not visual analysis.

A prose-only reading can be complete for an expressly text-scoped responsibility while its illustration coverage remains pending. If a material claim depends on an image, the missing inspection blocks that claim's completion unless the authorized scope is explicitly narrowed. No vocal delivery, music, acting or continuous movement is inferred from prose or stills. Audiovisual work would require separately admitted sources and a method extension.

## 10. Evidence and argument retrieval

Assign immutable local observation IDs such as SBY-V01-E001 only when a real observation exists. Keep a human-readable chapter/scene label beside a deterministic route consisting of witness slot, actual spine/member, anchor or paragraph and bounded character range when needed. The private receipt pins the exact file/extraction identity. Reflowed pages never masquerade as print pages.

Every load-bearing claim identifies its source layer, disclosure horizon, evidence class, confidence, alternatives and a route to its complete argument. Evidence classes preserve OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, CONTRADICTION, EVALUATION and UNRESOLVED. External material remains EXCLUDED_EXTERNAL unless separately admitted. Confidence is HIGH, MODERATE, LOW or OPEN, with reasons rather than numeric pseudo-precision.

The full reading owns detailed observation and interpretation. Ledgers index it with stable heading anchors and preserve the premise-to-conclusion reasoning necessary to understand a revision. Do not replace a substantial existing argument with a row containing only “supported” and a locator. Retrieve and inspect the actual passage before accepting disputed wording, a high-impact reinterpretation or a suspect citation.

## 11. Counterreading and revision

Each completed unit thesis engages at least one credible rival explanation and the strongest available counterevidence. Where narration matters, include a reading that takes the narrator's appraisal less on trust. A rival must explain evidence, not act as a straw opponent.

Classify claims as supported, supported but contestable, provisional, contradicted or open. Revision uses PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT or OPEN. Retain prior formulation, prior full-argument route, new evidence, counterevidence, reason, authority boundary and dependent artifacts. Later evidence may change current interpretation without altering the earlier freeze.

Apparent contradictions are first classified: identity or locator error, chronology, viewpoint/knowledge difference, assertion versus enforcement, genuine textual variant, bonus/paratext boundary, adaptation difference or unresolved inconsistency. Check the minimum diagnostic evidence. Do not harmonize incompatible accounts merely to simplify a model, and do not declare a deliberate mystery where only uncertainty is established.

## 12. Completed-unit artifact and transaction

Use a stable planned path such as readings/SHIBOYUGI_V01_DEEP_READING.md only when an authorized real reading exists. Its structured artifact type is deep_reading. Its source boundary, inspected components and actual knowledge horizon must be explicit.

The completed artifact contains, in order suited to the actual source:

1. Identity, exact witness/admission receipt, coverage and exposure disclosure.
2. Prior-unit orientation and the already frozen entering state.
3. Current-unit story synopsis with unit-end spoiler label.
4. Governing thesis and live competing interpretation.
5. Sustained movement-by-movement causal and formal reading.
6. Narration, focalization and reliability; character, relationship and knowledge changes.
7. Source-earned constraints, material choices or other domain modules, with Japanese wording and image/paratext status.
8. Recurrence and counterreadings; claim transitions and forecast adjudications.
9. Exact cumulative-update manifest, exit freeze, unresolved obligations and scoped completion decision.

This is a responsibility contract, not an order to compress every volume into eighteen short boxes. Related responsibilities may be integrated in substantial prose when they remain independently retrievable. Preserve distinctive scenes, physical mechanics, names, motives supported by the text, counterarguments and ambiguity.

Close the source-unit transaction only when the reading, required cumulative deltas or explicit no-material-change decisions, source coverage, locators, claim revisions, forecast outcomes, exit freeze and current high-water state agree. Persist and review the complete transaction before admitting a next unit. If interrupted, retain the last verified boundary and separate unfinished work; do not advance from an outline or the final chat sentence.

## 13. Acceptance and named-reference check

Acceptance asks whether the reading fulfills its source-bounded analytical responsibility, not whether it has the right headings or length. Test consequential source claims, exact quotations, material causal links, credible counterevidence, state distinctions and required inspection channels. Confirm that an independent reader can recover what happened and why the interpretation is warranted.

Before semantic acceptance, sample the synopsis, a substantial middle section and the concluding synthesis/exit state. Extend the sample to each major relationship and confusing attribution. Classify material issues as JUSTIFIED_ROLE_REFERENCE, SOURCE_REQUIRED_ANONYMITY, KNOWLEDGE_BOUNDARY_REQUIRED, UNNECESSARY_GENERICIZATION, AMBIGUOUS_REFERENT or INCORRECT_IDENTITY_OR_ATTRIBUTION. Correct only supported defects, record sampled scope, and preserve source concealment and historical knowledge.

Editorial clarification cannot silently change who acted, thought or knew something. A changed attribution or claim uses substantive revision. Frozen readings and prospective records remain unchanged; use an authorized erratum or successor. Passing repository checks does not certify literary quality.
