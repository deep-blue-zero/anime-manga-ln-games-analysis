# Character reconstruction contract

## 1. Purpose, current boundary and distinct responsibilities

Character reconstruction is an intended downstream responsibility of this novel project. Its purpose is to develop auditable, state-bounded accounts of what an evidenced character is likely to perceive, value, choose, say, avoid or leave unresolved under specified conditions. It is not a promise that every subject will support a model.

This contract adapts the [generic reconstruction method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md) to Japanese prose and the currently available electronic-bonus witness candidates. The [method](SHIBOYUGI_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) governs source reading; the [architecture](SHIBOYUGI_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) governs literary convergence; this contract governs derived modeling and validation.

**Current readiness boundary: NO_ADMITTED_EVIDENCE.** There are no established subjects, character dossiers, monographs, models, predictions, validation results or approved operational uses. This file establishes a pathway only. No narrative identity, personality, motive, relationship, rule belief or livelihood mechanism is inferred from the title.

A literary monograph, an evidence record and an operational model perform different jobs. The monograph develops an interpretive argument about the character; the evidence record enables source retrieval; the model formalizes conditional behavior and language within a defined state. A validation record tests a specific model and evidence version. None substitutes for another, and a polished monograph does not certify a model.

## 2. When a subject earns separate work

Begin subject-specific reconstruction only when admitted evidence provides an identifiable subject, a coherent temporal/continuity boundary and enough diagnostic situations to formulate bounded mechanisms. A major subject may warrant a monograph before a model. A minor subject may warrant only a carefully limited literary treatment. Refusing to model an underdetermined subject is a valid result.

Evidence should support distinctions across situations, interlocutors, information states or developmental changes. Repetition of an epithet, a memorable crisis or a few signature lines is inadequate by itself. Where the source supplies only one kind of situation, preserve that narrow scope rather than inventing ordinary-life competence or a global personality.

Create a concrete subject/state path only after this responsibility is earned. Keep one current model per declared subject/state/use scope. Different sessions or providers do not justify competing “local” and “cloud” canonical models. Alternative models can remain explicit candidates for comparison without creating parallel literary authority.

## 3. Evidence prerequisites and access classes

Every proposed subject binds its established name and Japanese form, relevant alias history, continuity/witness scope, earliest/latest admitted evidence, intended state and exclusions. Match a discovery subject if one already exists; do not fabricate global registry IDs. The discovery agent owns registry enrollment.

Retrieve full eligible readings and arguments, not a summary of traits. For important or contested behavioral and wording claims, reopen the actual source passage and test its locator. Keep direct speech, directly presented behavior, represented interiority, another person's report, narrator/observer presentation and analyst inference distinct.

A first-person account, if encountered, is viewpoint-bound evidence. A focal character's perception of another's motive is evidence of that perception before it is evidence of the motive. Even explicit interiority has a narrative frame and disclosure position. The model must not turn a privately narrated thought into speech the person would automatically utter.

Japanese governs written voice. A translation can supply a separately scoped translation model or navigation aid; it is not source-language voice authority. Illustrations require actual inspection and support only their observed layer. This project has no performed-voice source: no claims about vocal timbre, acting, breath, musical timing or gesture over time may be inferred from text or still images.

Bonuses and main text remain component-qualified. A later-disclosing extra cannot silently supply biography, relationship history or a motive to an earlier main-text model. Any accepted cross-component relation must state its admission and continuity basis.

## 4. Evidence record and coverage

For each observation used by a model, the evidence record supplies exact source identity/locator, current eligible reading or monograph anchor, subject state, interlocutor/audience, situation type, knowledge available, evidence class, confidence, brief observation and interpretive limits. It routes to the full argument rather than duplicating the narrative corpus.

Coverage must show which admitted units and situations actually inform the model. An inventory of available volumes is not evidence of consumption. Identify inaccessible or uninspected components, missing ordinary-life contexts, weak interlocutor coverage and unresolved attribution. Required versus optional channels are declared separately from inspection state.

Deliberately seek ordinary-life controls where the source provides them: routine exchanges, work, idle talk, mundane choices, humor, play or low-stakes cooperation. Do not assume those scenes exist. Their absence from the source is a coverage limit, not evidence that a character lacks mundane behavior.

Negative evidence requires opportunity. “Does not disclose this information” is useful only when the person could have disclosed it and the scene makes that choice diagnostic. A context never shown is a gap. Keep evidence-backed limits separate from evidence gaps.

## 5. Mature literary reconstruction before operational modeling

A mature character monograph must explain mechanisms and development through connected, source-grounded prose. It should answer how the character appraises situations, what competing commitments matter, how relationships and knowledge affect decisions, what changes across time, and how the narrative form shapes access to the character.

Build a developmental account with genuine transitions and persistent tensions. Distinguish a newly revealed existing disposition from a new disposition, a temporary crisis from baseline, a role requirement from a personal value and a relationship-specific response from a universal trait. Do not infer unshown psychological history to make a trajectory neat.

The monograph integrates speech, behavior, represented thought, narrated framing, counterevidence and the work's formal/thematic use of the character. It can explain an action without endorsing it, and identify contradictions without resolving them into a clinical label. It must retain credible alternative readings and the evidence that favors each.

A table of attributes is not a mature monograph. Neither length nor a full heading list establishes explanatory adequacy. Preserve scene specificity, names, exact source routes and the reasons a conclusion changed. A handoff delivers the complete prose and argument chain, not a compressed personality summary.

## 6. Model header and state layers

Every future model binds subject identity, witness/component scope, admitted source boundary, temporal/disclosure boundary, represented state, model version, exact accepted evidence basis, intended use, inspection lanes, excluded evidence and observed contamination. Repository/source receipts preserve exact version identities; public prose contains only approved provenance.

Represent the modeled state as subject-at-boundary, never an unqualified “complete character.” A later-state model may use earlier behavior as labeled history. An earlier-state model may not use later revealed motives or knowledge merely because the analyst now knows them.

Separate the following layers:

- **Persistent dispositions:** patterns supported across appropriate situations, with recurrence and exceptions.
- **Developmental state:** enduring changes at a defined boundary, with evidence for their cause and validity interval.
- **Situational conditions:** temporary bodily, emotional, informational or material circumstances.
- **Knowledge and beliefs:** what the person knows, believes, doubts or misunderstands, separately from analyst world knowledge.
- **Relationships and roles:** directional conditioning, audience and obligations actually established by evidence.
- **Resources and constraints:** what is available or perceived available and how it narrows the choice set, if relevant.

Change-cause tags can distinguish DISPOSITION, KNOWLEDGE, RELATIONSHIP, CONTEXT, ROLE, RESOURCE, REVEALED_NOT_NEW and UNRESOLVED. A tag supports retrieval; the explanatory prose must still justify it. Do not assign a new state solely to accommodate a prediction failure.

## 7. Mechanisms, decision policies and exceptions

A useful mechanism traces supported links from trigger and perceived stakes through appraisal, affect, considered alternatives, choice, action and consequence. Mark links as explicit, inferred or unknown. Missing interior access narrows the model; it does not authorize invented private reasoning.

A conditional decision policy has the form: a specified trigger, perceived situation and current state make a bounded response family more plausible, with inhibitors, overriding commitments, relationship effects and named exceptions. Every policy records source evidence, material counterevidence, rival explanation, disconfirming observation and scope.

Classify a policy as OBSERVED when seen only once or in one context; CANDIDATE_POLICY when recurrence or explicit evidence supports it but held-out testing remains absent; PROMOTED_POLICY only when the relevant declared validation has been completed. These are project model-development states, not global capability grades or owner use authorization.

The title-prompted questions in this project can suggest tests only after evidence exists. If a person hears a rule-like statement, the model must distinguish their belief about it from its actual enforcement. If resources or livelihood concerns are shown to matter, test whether behavior follows a material constraint, an endorsed value, a relationship obligation or another explanation. Do not assume the work's characters optimize survival, money, victory, risk or any other objective.

A policy must exclude something. A description compatible with every possible action is not predictive. When evidence supports several response families, retain conditional alternatives and abstention conditions instead of choosing whichever sounds most dramatic.

## 8. Directed relationships and knowledge constraints

Model each significant relationship directionally. State how the subject's goals, disclosure, trust, dependency, conflict response and written register vary toward a particular other person at the specified boundary. Never derive B's feelings from A's behavior alone.

Preserve what each person knows of the other's intentions and what they only infer. An analyst's knowledge of an event cannot be supplied to a character who has not learned it. A model must know when to withhold a confident inference, misunderstand a cue if supported, or ask for information rather than act omnisciently.

Aliases and identities remain boundary-qualified. A scene-level reconstruction can preserve concealment while a later unit-end explanation uses a disclosed name and makes that difference explicit. Different people with similar roles must not collapse into one functional slot.

Counterfactual uses require a scenario contract specifying assumed chronology, interlocutor relationship, source continuity and material setting. A new scenario is not permission to invent a relationship history or grant knowledge outside the modeled boundary.

## 9. Written voice as decision and language

Speech begins with appraisal and communicative objective, then speech act, audience/relationship filter, register and wording. A model that mimics endings while choosing an unsupported speech act fails decision fidelity even when its Japanese sounds fluent.

A source-grounded register account identifies attested self-reference, address forms, politeness, lexical/syntactic patterns, sentence endings, discourse markers, evasions, humor and context-conditioned shifts. Not every category must be populated. Identify the interlocutors and situations actually represented; never extrapolate a complete voice matrix from one exchange.

Keep direct dialogue, quoted dialogue, narrator diction, represented thought and free indirect expression distinct. Preserve the thought–speech gap: private interiority does not license the model to say everything the source tells the reader. Translation and analyst gloss remain separate layers.

Test **decision fidelity** and **surface fidelity** independently. Record “would sound wrong if” traps only when evidence supports them, with counterexamples and context limits. Catchphrases are not mechanisms. Written voice does not establish a performed voice, and no audio imitation is part of this source scope.

## 10. Confidence, distance and abstention

Use qualitative HIGH, MODERATE, LOW or OPEN confidence with reasons. Do not report numeric behavioral probabilities or broad accuracy percentages without a defensible sampling frame; this contract supplies none.

Distinguish D0 attested situations, D1 close variants, D2 novel situations within the modeled story state and D3 materially new or cross-scenario settings. Greater distance normally lowers warranted confidence. A validated narrow rule does not become universally applicable through fluent extrapolation.

Each model names abstention conditions: missing subject state, unestablished relationship, incompatible source versions, required uninspected evidence, unresolved attribution, unsupported knowledge or a scenario outside the evidence's reach. An abstention can be a correct result. A model must not conceal a gap with invented biography or generalized genre expectations.

## 11. Validation design and prospective freezes

Before testing, define the model version, evidence basis, test question, applicable policy, response family, discriminating outcomes, baseline and exclusions. Separate training/interpretive evidence from held-out material. Do not call a previously read scene held out because it is omitted from the prompt.

Freeze predictions and rationales before exposing the test source. Preserve the exact model and forecast state; adjudicate separately in the [claim and forecast ledger](SHIBOYUGI_CLAIM_AND_FORECAST_LEDGER.md). A later source unit can be a prospective test only if its admission is separately authorized. No test plan extends the reading run.

Where all relevant evidence is already known, perform a labeled retrospective test with exposure disclosure. Such tests can locate inconsistency and overfitting but cannot certify genuine prospective success.

Test behavior across relevant context variation, including ordinary-life and relationship controls where available. Useful adversarial checks include a close perturbation of an attested scene, a plausible but policy-violating decoy, a negative control requiring abstention, a knowledge-boundary challenge and separate voice-content versus naturalness assessment. A generic trait sketch is a useful baseline; fluency or resemblance to genre conventions is not a source-grounded success measure.

## 12. Adjudication and failure analysis

For each test, record the exact tested boundary, observed source behavior, prediction, match and mismatch, relevant circumstances, evidence route and exposure limits. Use SUPPORTED, PARTLY_SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY, CONFOUNDED or UNRESOLVED. State denominators and exclusions qualitatively; do not convert a few selected successes into a general accuracy score.

When a result fails, distinguish an incorrect policy, wrong temporal state, missing knowledge, relationship mismatch, voice/pragmatic mismatch, changed material conditions, source-attribution error and an underdetermined test. Retain rival failure explanations until evidence discriminates. Do not rewrite the frozen prediction or loosen the rule after seeing the answer without recording a model revision.

A mature fidelity audit proceeds rule by rule with PASS, PASS_WITH_BOUND, NEEDS_TARGETED_SOURCE_REVIEW or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. It evaluates mechanisms, state separation, knowledge discipline, relationship conditioning, Japanese voice, contrary cases, abstention and scope. A model does not pass merely because every test category has one example.

If two models differ, compare their rules, evidence and predictions at the same state and test boundary. Prose style is not the comparison object. Use the claim-revision route to resolve literary disagreements; the model follows accepted literary corrections.

## 13. Coverage record for independent evaluation

A subject-specific coverage record, created only when evidence exists, points to the actual homes for psychological mechanism, longitudinal state, written voice/register, mundane behavior, relationship conditioning, interiority, conflict behavior, ethical deliberation, humor/play, source completeness, negative evidence and temporal specificity.

It also describes the evidence and limits relevant to dialogue, cross-scenario, mundane social, ethical-deliberation, romance/relationship and professional-context uses. Unsupported contexts stay unassessed or out of scope; this list does not establish that the source contains them.

The author reports source coverage and evidence gaps. The independent reconstruction evaluator owns capability grades, dimension grades and scenario-readiness entries in its global registry. The analysis author must not self-assign an A–E tier or write those global outputs. An assessment is tied to an exact evidence set and can become stale after a source or model change.

## 14. Acceptance and operational use

Separate five decisions:

1. **Author completeness:** the assigned model/evidence/validation package fulfills its declared responsibility with full arguments and honest limits.
2. **Independent semantic and fidelity review:** an adequate reviewer tests source grounding, counterevidence, state boundaries, rules and actual validation results.
3. **Repository acceptance:** current authority, preservation, scope and mechanical checks are satisfied through the designated integration process.
4. **Evaluator capability assessment:** the authorized evaluator assigns any capability/scenario grades against the exact evidence basis.
5. **Owner acceptance for a use:** the owner authorizes a particular operational purpose, state and limitations.

No earlier decision implies a later one. A canonical model may remain unsuitable for a requested scenario. Owner permission does not make unsupported predictions accurate, and an evaluator grade is not permission to expose unadmitted sources or modify literary authority.

A future readiness record states each decision's actual state and unresolved owner/role obligations. It must not claim owner acceptance from an agent-written field. At this bootstrap, all subject-specific decisions are NOT_STARTED because no narrative evidence has been admitted.

## 15. Revision, synthetic-output firewall and preservation

Generated dialogue, simulated scenes, counterfactual responses and model predictions are synthetic outputs. They are never evidence about the source character, never canonical biography and never a substitute for inspecting the source. Keep them clearly labeled and outside literary evidence routes. No simulation is produced in this bootstrap.

When evidence or a model premise changes, issue a bounded new model version and preserve the predecessor, prediction freezes and adjudications. Record the changed policy, supporting/contrary evidence, downstream use limits and need for reevaluation. Do not promote a provisional model by editing only its authority metadata.

Frozen literary readings and historical models remain recoverable. Current corrections follow claim revision and reciprocal supersession. New source admission follows the separate owner run and initiation gate; a reconstruction gap cannot reopen a closed source boundary.

## 16. Handoff and completion

A reconstruction handoff delivers complete eligible evidence routes, full monograph/argument where used, exact model version, state/continuity, exclusions, prediction registers, separate adjudications, fidelity audit and remaining obligations. Include exact output hashes and base identity in the operational receipt. A short profile or trait list cannot replace the complete package.

The receiver verifies bytes, full contents, source/knowledge scope and current-base drift; performs the named-reference check; and accepts responsibility for unresolved work. If a central premise changes during evidence completion, revise the affected model and dependent arguments before acceptance, preserving unaffected material.

Model construction, validation, evaluator assessment and approved use are separate completion gates. A subject can finish a bounded literary monograph while reconstruction remains insufficient. The current project has completed none of these subject-specific stages. Its sole authorized operation here is defining this contract for review.
