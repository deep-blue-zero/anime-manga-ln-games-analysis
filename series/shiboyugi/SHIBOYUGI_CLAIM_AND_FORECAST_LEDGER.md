# Claim and forecast ledger

## Responsibility and zero-admission baseline

This ledger preserves why an interpretation is held, what could weaken it, how it changes and where the full argument can be retrieved. It also preserves prospective forecasts separately from later adjudications. It does not replace full readings or specialist prose.

**Baseline SBY-CLAIM-BASE-000, 2026-11-13:** no admitted narrative, no literary claims, no predictions, no adjudications, no revised interpretations and no operational model. Source facts and unresolved audit obligations belong to the [source register](SHIBOYUGI_SOURCE_REGISTER.md). Title-prompted design questions in the method are not hypotheses pretending to arise from source reading.

The current narrative boundary and high-water mark are NONE. No record is created merely to demonstrate a schema. The contracts below initialize the required day-one responsibility without inventing content.

## Claim identity and scope

Allocate a stable SBY-C identifier to a real material claim when its argument is first written. Keep the ID across revision; changes to wording or confidence do not make the prior argument disappear. If one claim splits into genuinely different propositions, preserve the predecessor and name each successor's distinct scope.

A claim's header binds its exact proposition, subject(s), source witness/components, temporal/disclosure boundary, evidence class, confidence, current authority home and status. Distinguish direct observation, self-report, narrator presentation, inference, hypothesis and value judgment. A narrator's assertion and an analyst's endorsement of it are different propositions.

The claim's current route is a mutable index, not the whole intellectual object. Every material entry must expose a stable full-argument path and heading anchor. If the full argument remains in a reading, preserve that prose and point to it. If a specialist becomes current authority, retain the original argument route as historical basis and identify the accepted successor.

## Full argument contract

For every load-bearing claim, the canonical argument explains:

1. What precisely is asserted, at what source and knowledge boundary, and what is not asserted.
2. The relevant observations and why each supports the claim; who perceived, said, thought or acted.
3. The inferential steps or causal mechanism, with gaps and assumptions identified.
4. The strongest credible competing explanation and the evidence it accounts for.
5. Material counterevidence, negative controls or exceptions, and whether a diagnostic opportunity existed.
6. What new evidence could change the conclusion and which uncertainty remains.
7. The current conclusion's consequences for downstream character, relationship, thematic or model work.

Do not accept a table containing “supported,” a quotation and a locator as an adequate argument. Nor should a mature monograph be reduced to several ledger rows during a handoff. The ledger preserves retrieval and revision; the full prose retains reasoning, scene specificity and counterargument.

A claim that no behavior occurs requires a defensible coverage and opportunity boundary. Failure to find a keyword is not negative evidence. A claim about a rule, incentive or livelihood mechanism must distinguish assertion, belief, observed consequence and interpretive generalization. The title supplies no premises.

## Current status and revision history

Use separate fields for epistemic status, qualitative confidence and revision operation. A claim can be supported but contestable at MODERATE confidence and undergo PRESERVE; those are different judgments.

| Transition | Required explanation |
|---|---|
| PRESERVE | The bounded formulation survives the stated new evidence; explain why it remains adequate |
| STRENGTHEN | Additional diagnostic support improves confidence or scope without hiding the prior limit |
| REVISE | Formulation, mechanism, scope, attribution or validity interval changes; preserve both versions |
| DOWNGRADE | Support weakens or an assumption becomes doubtful; state which dependency is affected |
| REJECT | The claim is contradicted or no longer supportable; preserve its former argument and rejection basis |
| OPEN | Evidence is insufficient or competing interpretations remain live; state what could discriminate |

Each substantive revision is appended with date or accepted transaction identity, earlier formulation, transition, proposed/current formulation, new evidence, contrary evidence, reason, knowledge boundary, full-argument routes and affected downstream homes. The previous version remains recoverable even after rejection.

A material correction must demonstrate evidence, a source distinction or an analytical defect. An unfamiliar style, different model or preferred author is not a reason to replace sound analysis. Supported competing readings may remain side by side; coherence is not purchased by deleting disagreement.

## Prospective forecasts

Allocate a stable SBY-F identifier only to an actual bounded forecast. Before the test evidence is exposed, freeze the exact forecast wording, rationale, governing claim or model rule, allowed evidence, predicted response family or event, discriminating observation, alternatives, confidence, intended test boundary and contamination disclosure.

Forecasts must exclude some outcomes. “Something will change” is not diagnostic. Avoid false precision and percentage probabilities; use qualitative confidence with reasons. Record whether the forecast is about a close variant of observed behavior, an unobserved situation in the same state, or a more distant counterfactual.

Freeze the record in recoverable persisted state before inspecting the testing material. A declaration added after reading is retrospective regardless of its label. If later material is merely available to the session, control exposure and record what was actually seen. A finished unit synopsis or exit ledger must not leak into an earlier-horizon test packet.

Forecasting never authorizes the test source. A later unit, bonus, adaptation or other witness can be tested only within its own authorized admission boundary. Unavailable or unauthorized tests remain unperformed.

## Separate adjudication

Append a new adjudication after the permitted test is inspected. Preserve the frozen forecast unchanged. Bind the outcome to the exact test source, observed result, relevant context, evidence locator, exposure history, alternative explanations and impact on the governing claim or model.

Use SUPPORTED, PARTLY_SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY, CONFOUNDED or UNRESOLVED. Explain the fit rather than treating a broad prediction as success after the fact. NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY is neither support nor failure; a source may simply not test the proposition.

A retrospective fit is labeled RETROSPECTIVE_TEST and weighted below a genuinely prospective result. It can reveal constraints but cannot become evidence of forecasting success. Later discoveries may revise a current adjudication through an appended correction while preserving the original evaluation history.

## Dependency and contradiction routing

For every material claim, list downstream full readings, current specialist sections, state records, reconstruction rules and synthesis arguments that actually depend on it. This is a semantic dependency route, not an invitation to create empty files.

When a premise changes, identify each affected home, required correction, responsible role and acceptance condition. A central premise cannot be closed by adding a footnote while leaving the dependent conclusion unqualified. Review only affected dependencies and preserve adequate unaffected argument.

For apparent contradiction, check source identity, locator, edition/component, chronology and attribution first. Then test knowledge differences, narrator presentation, assertion versus enforcement, context, material constraint or genuine inconsistency as warranted. Unknown does not mean secretly coherent. If additional evidence is out of scope, retain OPEN and the specific missing observation.

If the source is closed to new admission, revisions within accepted evidence remain possible through the governing correction route. A frozen reading or forecast is not edited; a claim revision or separate erratum supplies the current correction. No closure bypass is inferred from interpretive urgency.

## Claim retrieval and version preservation

A future current index identifies the live formulation and its full-argument route, then links the complete revision chain. Do not delete earlier evidence routes or reuse a stable anchor for a different proposition. When artifacts are superseded, use reciprocal authority metadata and preserve a single current retrieval route under the archive policy.

For an evidence check, retrieve the full relevant passage and context from the exact witness, not only the excerpt carried in a summary. Test suspect locators, disputed quotations, high-impact reinterpretations and representative mature claims. Report the actual review sample and unresolved limits; a bounded audit is not a full reread.

Public records use brief necessary quotations and public-safe source locators. Full text, raw receipts, private IDs and acquisition filenames stay in the evidence plane. Synthetic examples or model outputs are never entered as source evidence.

## Unit closeout and synthesis dispatch

Each unit transaction records claims added, preserved, revised or left open; forecasts frozen or adjudicated; material no-change decisions; dependency repairs; and full-reading/exit-state anchors. It must agree with the [state ledger](SHIBOYUGI_STATE_AND_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md) and current source boundary. An unresolved required revision blocks acceptance of the affected conclusion, even if other work is complete.

Before specialist dispatch, select a frozen scope and supply the claim index, complete arguments, relevant readings, evidence routes, counterreadings, revision history and unresolved obligations. A receiver must be able to recover why a conclusion was held, not merely that it was held. Compact navigation is welcome; intellectual compression that erases evidence or alternatives is not.

At convergence, compare specialists' source boundaries, premises and counterarguments. Resolve real conflicts through evidence or record the remaining divergence. A future full-series argument must integrate the work rather than concatenate summaries of this ledger.

## Editorial and recovery discipline

Apply the named-reference check to substantive argument prose: synopsis/introduction, a middle section and conclusion, expanded across major relationships where needed. Preserve meaningful aliases and concealment; distinguish justified role reference from unnecessary generic labels or incorrect attribution. A changed speaker or knowledge holder is a substantive revision, not cosmetic replacement.

One mutable owner maintains current routing and appends accepted history. Recover from the last accepted transaction and exact authority surface after interruption. The current state remains SBY-CLAIM-BASE-000; no narrative claim or forecast can be inferred from these initialized protocols.
