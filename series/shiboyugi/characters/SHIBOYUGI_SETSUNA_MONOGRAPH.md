# Setsuna

雪名, Setsuna, is the playing name of the former 雪乃七見, Yukino Nanami. After surviving Maiden Race she takes employment with the games and volunteers to be assigned to Yūki, the woman who inadvertently changed her mind about living. Her patient knows the service and the worker who supplies it. She has no shown recognition that the worker is the person she helped beyond the finish line of her first game. Setsuna can appear unobtrusive while acting quickly about transport, housing, medical access, a shot under grave uncertainty or a private decision about the fate of the player’s possessions. That range grows over the story. The central contradiction concerns someone who protects a player’s capacity to continue while knowing that her own support has made it possible to keep undergoing serious harm. By the final hospital encounter both practical devotion and self-accusation are direct evidence. The first cannot settle the truth or completeness of the second. This portrait covers the Japanese narrative through V10’s open ending. V01 S15 P0052–0061, V06 S18 P0679–0796, V10 S16 P0378–0409 and S26 P0029–0069.

## Maiden Race and a life she chooses to keep

Her life before the game comes in a few particulars. She had pulled away from ordinary society and was out of work. She disliked and avoided conflict and accepted the premise that she might die. Kanade and Shizuku, two sisters she meets on the platform, have differently informed reasons to be there. At what turns out to be a real mortal obstacle, Setsuna hears an urgent warning and wants to go but initially remains frightened. She elects to wake a late sleeper, Yūki, and helps her get started. The player who can move quickly despite almost no understanding of the real stakes seems admirable and exasperating. Setsuna can switch to a safer route where Yūki can make a more demanding jump. Her line 文脈でわかれよ expresses annoyance that Yūki does not understand what she means, even while the two cooperate. Aptitude and a desire to risk actual death travel independently. V06 S18 P0007–0074/P0079–0173/P0183–0223/P0234–0269. [Before knowing the stakes](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V06_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v06-e002--the-novices-who-start-together).

Yūki pushes Kanade off the last bridge when time is running out. Setsuna is horrified. She reads the shocked witnesses’ later avoidance as a wish to forget what happened. She has no access to each spectator’s reasons. She can consider stealing a key and rejects that option while she can search for her own. After she finds one, Shizuku blames her for waking Yūki and injures her. Now in danger, Setsuna nearly yields because Shizuku had been drawn in against her will. She then imagines the determined Yūki on the bridge pushing her off it. She borrows from an earlier sight she still finds terrible and kills Shizuku. After recognizing the death, she goes on striking because she is afraid she will become passive if she lets herself stop. This aftermath and her later distress carry a more specific understanding of her than the result of a fight. The threat is real, the model briefly present to her is imagined, and the action is her own. V06 S18 P0311–0367/P0381–0469/P0538–0576/P0613–0675.

Through the finished gate, Setsuna asks for a shoulder and acknowledges what she has just done. She has to name the unpleasant fact to someone who does not understand how anyone could have died. Yūki has survived while believing they were acting in a stage-managed attraction. After recognizing her own omission in not making the actual danger plain, Setsuna laughs and wants to live. The absurdity and help come from the same person as the earlier shock. It would be an overstrong inference to call the laughter forgiveness for the death of Kanade. It does change the options Setsuna imagines for herself. She attends no subsequent game as a player in the admitted evidence. She accepts another role in the industry as a way to make a livelihood out of known circumstances. The narration supplies loss of civil registration and a changed face; she retains the memory of her original name. This sequence has no demonstrated identity operation with the terms of other characters' elective memory erasures. V06 S18 P0679–0796. [What the ending changed](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V06_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v06-e005--two-incompatible-examples-in-one-person).

## Help the recipient sees, work she cannot see

When Setsuna learns that the other woman continued playing, she wants to see where she will go and actively requests the assignment. This motive is not visible from her largely quiet manner after routine outcomes. In the earlier #30 car trip she offers the greeting and congratulates Yūki, with little expression to insist on an answer to a further question. Yūki likes the light conversational demand that comes with an apparently hands-off agent. Asked about an apparent penalty, Setsuna uses ご想像にお任せします. She explains why she believes the organization could not leave a threat to itself unaddressed and apologizes for the cost to Yūki. She also declares her own investment. She is among the people wanting to see players stake everything on victory and promises to remove whatever interferes. Yūki hears unusual conviction and finds that response more appalling than Kinko’s father’s appeal. The later conviction that she has helped make harm possible already has this stronger ground than the agent’s ordinary willingness to transport and repair a player. The exchange is partial knowledge given in a formal register. It does not identify who intercepted an object, who decided the timing or whether the subsequent invitation implemented a particular penalty. She arranges later access when asked to take Yūki to the artisan, relays a rumor with explicit uncertainty and provides progressively more evidence about how she thinks staff should and can act. V02 S18 P0025–0072, V03 S12 P0070–0085/P0103–0113/S23 P0097–0107, V06 S18 P0765–0796. [The early case around an official refusal](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V02_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v02-e013--from-the-fathers-polemic-to-the-agents-peculiar-allegiance).

A suspected follower puts her more directly in the field. She tries to identify the person and gets hurt. Her warning that a public hospital would create a problem for an agent without ordinary registration marks a practical limit on the help she can accept. She apologizes for a poor outcome, reports what she herself saw and distinguishes an educated guess about the attacker's experience from the identity she failed to see. Yūki offers her a place for the night. Later Setsuna can support the continuance of school once the person who acquired private information turns out to have been initiated before. At a different point she connects a referral from the artisan to actual diagnosis and adjusts what she asks of Yūki about a new invitation. Helping after games is real work and can extend to what it takes for the player to sustain school or to recover away from other observers. Her account of institutional constraints does not show that she personally speaks for every arm of the system. V04 S17 P0037–0056/P0188–0224/P0339–0373/P0396–0411 and S19 P0363–0376/P0603–0618. [Support where two lives meet](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e003--the-private-scope-of-protection).

## When the request needs an answer she must provide

V06 shows two occasions that can look like obedient service from outside. After #62 Setsuna sees conditions for worry about Yūki’s treatment of Tamamo’s death. The initial sign could be forgetting or an effort to pretend forgetting, and she does not know which. She answers the urgent call, has been asked to put Yūki to sleep and finds that the originally requested means will not work when the player cannot use it. She gives an injection on her own judgment when the requested capsule is unusable. The visible cause of her own injury is a blow from Yūki. She accepts that the violence was inadvertent and can fit what she has heard, while the two do not share a view of a second physical attacker. Her professional language acknowledges that she has acted on her own. She then conveys Yūki’s proposed competition to Rinrin and helps give it actual organization and costs. A narrator's access to her recognition of pain and urgent conduct adds evidence that Yūki herself cannot fully see. V06 S18 P0777–0796, S20 P0103–0124/P0133–0205/P0320–0357.

In Snow Room, Rinrin has withheld aspects of the design from her. During recruitment, Setsuna gives Hakushi the former handle 雪名 as a pseudonym and keeps the assigned player's identity private. During play Hakushi recognizes her voice, draws her to the microphone and asks about a theory of the other side. Setsuna begins asking what Hakushi would do if it were true. Setsuna sees on a monitor that Yūki has stopped unexpectedly in the marked area, and believes an apparition is involved because that is how Yūki understands her obstruction. She can only really see the player, and actual hair under tension does not disclose the visible body of anyone else. Setsuna prepares to act and hesitates at the risk to the woman she is responsible for. Rinrin asks what she wants to do for her and offers to take over. Setsuna refuses and says 私が、やります. She operates the room’s penalty guns where Yūki thinks the apparition is holding her, wounds Yūki and sees her move again after the perceived attacker disappears in Yūki’s account. It also leaves Setsuna trying to explain that hair movement and later hearing someone in an apparently empty place use her former playing name. That sequence supports her sensory experience and a problem of attribution, not identified agency for the voice. Her ability to exercise control under a request here does not equate her knowledge, authorship or authority with that of the other people arranging the trial. V06 S20 P0712–0833/P1112–1168/P1267–1358. [The choice to use force](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V06_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v06-e013--who-takes-the-final-action).

The same person can operate an expense account, patch a tracksuit and cut hair because she is openly asked. Her capacity to use the game funds does not mean she paid personally for Snow Room. She says the burden of the arrangement comes out of Yūki’s money and advises the degree of economy that might require. She checks whether Yūki wants the damaged hair restored, hears that the shorter cut means something, and agrees to do a job she openly has not done before. With the blade behind her, Yūki values familiarity more than experience. A request made with that trust is an unusual practical intimacy. So is Yūki describing her first game and an otherwise forgettable companion to that very person. Setsuna privately marks the discrepancy and does not choose to end it. Her keeping quiet in that conversation may protect the past, the assignment or the other's ability to speak freely. The narrative gives a choice of silence and remembered feeling but no authority to fix all its purposes. V06 S22 P0005–0057. [The haircut and the surviving gap in knowledge](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V06_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v06-e014--the-work-of-repair).

## A material form of attachment

Setsuna sometimes estimates the other residents of Tochinoki will keep separate from Yūki’s private life. She knows Edomoto through work, while proximity turns out to conceal more than she expects. The later security crisis repeatedly puts her in position to help. She dispatches appropriate aid after the violence around Awahime, helps Yūki occupy a new address, selects and supplies a house under actual delegation and arrives after a further exposure to move her to a hotel. She delivers food and takes part in repeated maintenance arrangements. These acts create options for her patient and can fail to produce safety when somebody else discovers the address. A later hospital awakening supplies observable relief in excess of a tally of routings. Her directly exercised authority here is arranging circumstances under delegation and adapting to new instructions. The terms of the player’s career and professional medical decisions are made across other relationships. V07 S19 P0449–0476/S23 P0174–0178/P0258–0259, V08 S18 P0021–0045/S20 P0815–0816/P0887–0889/P0935–0939/P1013–1015/S22 P0015–0024/P0041–0055/P0095–0097, V09 S21 P0146–0218/P0410–0418/S26 P0262–0279. [Shelter and what is delegated](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e005--the-visitor-the-bird-and-the-room-selected-for-safety).

The hospital-adjacent house brings delivery into a repeated medical regime. Setsuna brings frozen food the hospital has planned, supplies Yūki’s #86 result after a missing interval and sees the wardrobe crisis near #90 at the door. She agrees aloud to collect and dispose of boxed outfits. Yūki assumes a particular businesslike answer will follow, and when it does not, she apologizes for the waste of deliveries rather than asking what else could be wrong. Setsuna collects the boxes and privately plans to keep them. Her action secures temporary possession. A suitable store, her later location of the boxes and a reconsideration by the owner are not supplied. Her response has more force than silent and dutiful compliance. It conceals substantive disagreement in a relationship with a patient whose small number of attachments she has watched closely. V10 S16 P0182–0198/P0344–0408. [The person who hears the request differently](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e005--the-costumes-in-the-boxes).

Inside her car she considers a pause, counseling and the part of the hospital that could give access. She then accuses herself as 加害者 and part of a 人でなしの体制. Suddenly worrying about mental welfare strikes her as belated hypocrisy after years of encouragement. She tells herself to accompany Yūki to the end. The fact that she looks for alternatives expresses a recognizable criterion of harm, and the reproof expresses both guilt and willingness to keep assisting. The narration does not show her making the proposal aloud, actual counseling, or a jointly changed professional decision after the disagreement. In the last scene she sees enough of the game feed to go to hospital instead of the harbor. Once told her patient is alive she asks whether she will still be able to play. The service role and personal concern hence show up in sequence instead of one settling the other. The artisan's prognosis, his information about previous experience and his access to the room are distinct from hers. Busutake says she can keep Yūki able to fight, claims that she will take her and signals her companions to attack. Setsuna recognizes a player she thought had taken a headshot and is surprised she is alive. An earlier monitoring account called that shot fatal. The clinician dispute starts in particular witnessed acts and ends before Setsuna could respond or a transfer of care occurs. V10 S16 P0393–0409, S23 P0304–0309 and S26 P0029–0071. [The terminal clinical dispute](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e016--a-second-custodian-for-a-damaged-body).

## What she says, withholds and can be expected to do

Setsuna's formal address includes 幽鬼さん, repeated apologies and access-specific professional disclosures. The routine courtesy can carry decisions of her own. 僭越ながら and 独断でやらせていただきました follow a concrete urgent choice. In the firing scene the small, plainly first-person 私が、やります refuses delegation. In the car her private use of second-person reproof, rougher syntax and びびってやがる would surprise somebody relying on the public forms. The register difference precedes no audible confession. Compare the young Setsuna’s 文脈でわかれよ and an unusually open form of “please listen” after Shizuku’s death. The written source permits these comparisons among interlocutors and knows an inflection in specific scenes. It supplies no canonical audio profile or externally measurable accent. V02 S18 P0025–0057, V06 S18 P0160–0173/P0679–0716/S20 P0149–0163/P1267–1294, V10 S16 P0380–0408.

Historically she executes a solvable request with little demand for conversation. If the means fails and the risk is immediate, she can choose a different means, warn about a gap in authority or take an action herself rather than let an untrusted party decide. If a request seems to destroy something the other person has long valued, the actual wardrobe evidence supports protective retention and silence as plausible responses. It does not warrant extending secret intervention to every request about danger. At #1 Setsuna initially freezes and nearly yields, at #62 she has obsolete information and hesitates, against the follower she is physically overmatched, and at the hospital she has to depend on information from a craftsperson. Knowledge, delegated remit, practical means and desire to help are all independent. The story offers fairly exact clues about how she makes a familiar trip and very few about how she would respond to an ordinary personal invitation or a job outside the game organization. She can offer and accept quiet domestic care. Her routine includes alert driving, supply errands and sustained private attention to one person. Her hobbies, ordinary tastes, family life and any romance are not established. The strength of this relationship comes from the specific favor, work and hidden history that its scenes show. V04 S17 P0188–0224, V06 S18 P0111–0173/P0613–0675/S20 P0149–0163/P1267–1294, V10 S16 P0378–0409 and S26 P0029–0069.
