# Edomoto Tōhei

江戸元東平, Edomoto Tōhei, is the named supporter from a branch of the Fuchidori family. He makes assignments practicable for its young successor, Yayoi, by obtaining relevant information, preparing her portion of the work and handling what comes after it. He can be polite while using a title as teasing, serious elsewhere in defending the importance of preparation and unconvincing while he deliberately plays an outsider. Discrepancies between the parts of an assignment he and others actually see are especially useful for understanding him. His effectiveness in winning concrete assistance does not give him control over what people want or why they act. The substantive evidence is concentrated in V07, with V08 retaining his actual professional relation across the household assault. V07 S19 P0103–0142/P0224–0229/P0264–0280/P0449–0476, S21 P0446–0484, S23 P0794–0844 and S25 P0038–0053, V08 S20 P0762–0788.

## What a supporter allows others to know

He is young according to the narration that identifies him and has worked with Yayoi long enough that both are at ease sitting in silence. His role makes him aware of risks which can be shared while facts about the person and cause of an assignment remain separate. In one work car he hands over paper she must return after memorizing it. It gives immediate facts useful to the assigned person, including history directly relevant to what they can do, and lacks a name and reason for the commission. When she asks why the man is to be killed he says he does not know. That answer may arise from which information can legitimately be used, from actual ignorance or from established workplace restraint. It occurs often enough for Yayoi to recognize the form. It would be too strong to assume that he privately withholds a reason he knows on every occasion. He registers her unusual declaration that the country is sick without prolonging the talk. Restraint could make possible exactly the amount of cooperation that follows. V07 S19 P0103–0142. [What each person gets in the car](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e003--an-inherited-profession-and-an-apparent-peer).

The responsibility of his role continues if the vulnerable person is still alive at handover. After a detailed act of incapacitation his partner believes the man is alive. The transfer is completed and Edomoto drives away. In a different assignment the two are together longer and he glances at the enclosed target with a question about whether death has now occurred. He can precisely estimate time and how difficult a route is without having confirmed the end of a bodily process. This distinction matters to a relationship that looks from outside like a pair who each knows everything necessary. Yayoi likes his ご幸運を, the simple recognition that one remaining condition for success cannot be planned. His caring effect on her here is evidenced in her response. The short greeting does not establish that he feels the same sense of luck, responsibility or futility. V07 S19 P0132–0142/P0217–0239/P0257–0280, S21 P0446–0465 and S25 P0038–0048.

## Formal familiarity and a poor performance

With Yayoi he treats 八代目 as a form the heir is entitled to and says that a plain name would be too familiar. His private awareness that the title irritates her sometimes makes using it attractive. It is her from whom his restraint receives an actual answer. She asks him to use 縁鳥 or 八代衣, notices when his attempt to play a victim goes wrong and faults somebody she usually thinks can do anything. He admits with a half-joke that it may be overacted. An unguarded comment about the other person's manner can take place in a working pair that does not otherwise chatter. This is an ordinary part of their relationship available inside scenes governed by a violent duty. It is unhelpful to force the formality into either devotion or hostility. The work both gains from and creates ways to tease without changing its terms. V07 S21 P0451–0465 and S23 P0828–0844. [Title distance and teasing](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e013--address-politeness-and-what-registers-conceal).

The work on Kōen has genuine unknowns because the target rarely goes to her reported address and has no dependable schedule. Edomoto anticipates a means of making the public conflict end sooner, then provides useful information to someone who can actually take part. He intended at first to provide it to someone associated with the current chief. He chooses Maguma, whose return he has reason to expect might accomplish some of the same purpose. He presents himself as a victim of extortion and justifies his possession of the information implausibly. Maguma picks up the gap. He instantly stops acting so agitated and simply says it is useful. She doubts his story and accepts the proffered access on its own merits. That is a case of observable negotiation working despite failed role-play. The usefulness of what he has given someone remains distinct from her attitude to her old group or a willingness to support his ultimate purpose. The text later links this intervention to the target coming home and his preparation for Yayoi’s assignment. V07 S21 P0465–0476, S23 P0794–0844 and S25 P0040–0048. [The remaining stage of a funded job](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e010--the-commissions-close-after-the-fighters-leave).

## Whose purpose his work serves

Different employers can commission their practice. The game organizers commission a job on a military figure probing the preservation treatment. An individual police officer commissions the separate job on Kōen and the fee is raised by crowdfunding. The narration identifies Hiyori as making the final payment, with no showing that Edomoto knows this. Edomoto's work familiarity with Setsuna and the indirect institution crossing supply a reason two private occupations can know more about one another than the tenants of one house. He accepts a precise part of a project, waits for sufficient payment and assesses whether people he can reach can enact an immediate purpose. He prefers being able to finish the practical problem rather than maintaining that everyone involved understands its cause. Yayoi can recognize the reason an outcome was requested and still be deeply unsure what doing the job is for. Evidence of the supporter addressing completion and payment is stronger than a claim about his own moral interpretation of each person who commissioned a death. At a later case of actual assault she still turns to her supporter and the subsequent apportionment is largely out of view. V07 S19 P0449–0476, S21 P0446–0484/S23 P0794–0844/S25 P0003–0015/P0040–0053, V08 S20 P0762–0788. [Work outside the residents’ view](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e012--the-city-as-an-arrangement-of-disclosures).

Ordinary evidence independent of work is spare. He favors quiet company, can enjoy the confidence of a counterpart and uses an unconvincing but adaptable performed social role to get something done. The timing, wish of luck and shared turn toward another task show distinct capacities. The routine at a car and the real transition into another setting are evidence of sustained attention, separate from owning the whole family role. We have no reliable childhood story, detailed civilian occupation, diet, romantic tie or bond with other members of the family beyond the approved relationship material. The written shift from polite brief delivery, to emphatic requests in the role of victim, to まあまあ、いいじゃないですか when the attempt fails can be read without inferring a stable vocal accent. He makes a commitment believable in part by changing what he asks once the other person sees through a story. Future independent work with a different organization remains uncertain. V07 S19 P0112–0142/P0264–0280, S21 P0451–0476, S23 P0794–0844.

A comparable prepared role can be expected to produce selection and supply of immediately relevant information, limited revelation of the larger plan and adjustment when another person proves the best available route. In close business with a known family member the politeness may carry teasing rather than an order about social position. A stranger testing the exact underlying story may force a practical concession. The evidence also shows an unmeasured share of luck, uncertain outcomes after handoff and a genuinely inadequate attempt to simulate an aggrieved outsider. Making something happen in the assigned role can be predicted more securely than any particular private justification for his profession. V07 S19 P0112–0142, S21 P0451–0476, S23 P0794–0844 and S25 P0038–0048.
