# Tamamo

玉藻, read タマモ, starts playing in Halloween Night. Yūki thinks Tamamo looks like a minor. Tamamo later uses that age class herself. No exact age or civil name is supplied. She can take the initiative to enter a relationship because she wants the person she is to disappear into it. She first hires a stranger’s help to stay alive, pursues her as a teacher and puts more into the resulting household than Yūki expects. She later chooses to face that teacher as someone distinct enough to defeat her. By the end of the admitted Japanese novels through V10, Tamamo is dead. The fight is depicted in V05, the later interpretation of the act that killed her develops in V06, and Shirō’s account in V07 adds a consequence for their team. The figure who takes Tamamo’s form in Snow Room belongs to Yūki’s later experience. It does not give Tamamo a new scene of her own. V04 S21 P0395–0495, V05 S19 P0083–0095 and S21 P0909–0935/P1089–1100, V06 S16 P0018–0032 and S22 P0125–0135, V07 S23 P0152–0178.

## What the first encounter gives her

When several other players pursue Tamamo, she finds Yūki and offers her half of the sweets she has, before learning the name Yūki. The share is something Tamamo controls. She judges that an appealing bargain is more useful than a plea alone. Yūki takes the arrangement on terms she finds good and wins the confrontation. Tamamo notices the competence and asks to become her student. An agent had recommended finding a teacher if she wanted to live, and Tamamo needs no famous name or tally to make the first request. When Yūki looks for a reason to reject her, Tamamo even offers to change the way she wears her hair. Yūki tells her to slim down and catch her, then runs away in the expectation of avoiding the request. When Tamamo reaches her apartment, she appears much slimmer and asks for the next lesson. Yūki treats the conditions as fulfilled. The story leaves the means of her bodily transformation largely unstated. Both the striking difference from her earlier appearance and her work in tracking Yūki are part of that return. V04 S21 P0395–0449/P0453–0495/P0692–0706, S23 P0065–0087, V05 S19 P0011–0076. [The apparently impossible prerequisite](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e014--two-returns-after-a-win).

Tamamo first calls this a professional request. In her later private account she describes the personal feeling with 「惚れた」 and says the rescuer seemed to offer a last chance to change her life. She does not know how long she has hated herself. She thinks that mistreatment by a primary-school piano teacher or trouble with peers in middle school might matter. She cannot certify a cause and does not want to retrace it. Self-dislike is present even without an immediate affront and makes her own existence painful. Teaching seems to offer the removal of something painful rather than only the chance to become skilled. This fuller explanation is disclosed during the final game, in Tamamo’s reflection. It was not available to Yūki just because Tamamo asked for instruction at first sight. The choice to take classes with someone was in keeping with the trade. The hope that an admired person’s words and ways could replace her was much more particular. V05 S21 P0791–0811/P0909–0935.

## Closeness in the apartment

The first time Yūki really takes her as a pupil, Tamamo says 「おそばにいさせてください」 when continued training has been postponed. She can make a deferential request and sustain it as a practical imposition. She follows when Yūki tries to lose her, accepts a run at the cost of a night’s comfort, and offers actual domestic help. She washes, cleans, does laundry and cooks in space Yūki usually leaves untended. Work in the household is readily visible evidence of skill and care. There are no grounds to infer which work she privately enjoys or which food she would choose for herself. When Yūki asks whether anyone at home will worry, Tamamo says 「心配する人は、おりませんから」. That leaves the distinction between being alone, being estranged, and not wanting to answer intact. Yūki sees something of herself in it and lets the subject rest. V05 S19 P0044–0076/P0080–0136/P0138–0193.

Tamamo’s wish to remain close does not authorize every form that proximity takes. On the first night she hesitates at the use of Yūki’s bedding and offers to sleep on the floor. She tries to withdraw and Yūki presses the physical closeness after that resistance. The narrator explicitly keeps the absence of further resistance from amounting to assent. The factual distinction matters even where Tamamo wants instruction and company and even if Yūki jokes about them resembling a couple. Later bedding, distinct homes and meetings chosen for practice make the forms they use for closeness more varied. Tamamo respects a later request that Yūki be left alone for a call. The evidence for Tamamo’s attraction supports the specific private attachment she describes. It does not show a bilaterally declared relationship, mutual agreement on physical boundaries or an intimate history beyond those scenes. V05 S19 P0138–0193, S21 P0071–0111/P0917–0929.

Clothing gives Tamamo something she can change directly. She later wears an unassuming tracksuit and lets her hair fall in a style borrowed from Yūki. She explicitly wants to reduce the attention her face otherwise draws. Admiration and a useful disguise are compatible. She reveals her face when hair inconveniently obscures her eyes in competition, and later mildly rebukes Shirō’s suggestion she ought simply to show it off. 「人の勝手でしょ。私は隠すの」 is an explicit decision over her own presentation. Shirō’s failure to recognize her when she first takes a position in the room is evidence the effect can work. It does not establish how she transformed herself in the months before they met again. V05 S21 P0690–0724/P0795–0811/P0827–0844.

## Learning more than she had asked to learn

Tamamo often catches things Yūki misses. Clothes give her readable information, and she is alert to what unfamiliar uses of a device can imply. On the island she generally knows whose judgment she ought to seek without giving up all of her own. When Yūki begins to treat Rinrin’s challenge as if it were an ordinary contest, Tamamo keeps the original teaching purpose in view. She first obeys a direction to leave danger and later returns on hearing enough to believe Yūki needs help, intervenes and actually gets her away. Her willingness to make herself useful is not confined to domestic service. Yūki has to acknowledge receiving aid from the person she means to keep safe. At other times Tamamo gives real disagreement or waits without forcing a confidence. She can be dependent on a teacher for direction while taking a better local reading than her teacher. V05 S19 P0258–0285/P0431–0439/P0461–0519/P0574–0608/P0617–0625/P0653–0747.

Yūki makes training more deliberate after the island by giving knowledge, designing less harmful practical exercises and talking about the assumptions under which each style is useful. Tamamo attempts the beginner’s tasks, accepts correction and can give a characteristically polite account of a failure within an exercise: 「すいません、死にました」. The line is a training acknowledgment. It signifies neither actual death nor satisfaction with failure. She would like to fight as the woman she admires fights. Repeated difficulty with a first attacking move leads Yūki to recognize patiently held defense as a better match to Tamamo’s stamina. Tamamo asks why this differs from the teacher’s own way. The developed skill is evidence that she can take a useful provision without duplicating the person offering it. She later sets up practice for Yūki’s visual difficulty, checks whether her partner is hurt, asks about continuing and can herself surprise Yūki. The extent attributable to solitary work, instruction and additional games cannot be disentangled. V05 S19 P0187–0216/P0967–0976, S21 P0003–0028/P0212–0253/P0329–0362/P0494–0519/P0656–0669/P0769–0811.

Her counts should stay attached to their disclosure times. Halloween Night is her first appearance by her later statement. In an earlier stage of practice she says it is her only prior game. About four months into the apprenticeship she reports her eighth clean clear, after reporting seven at the preceding meeting. V05 does not establish her precise tally at Royal Palace. Continued practice and professional success are more secure evidence than a hypothetical complete record. V05 S21 P0092–0106/P0169–0175/P0212–0220/P0917–0924.

## A separation neither of them understands completely

Yūki says that her own teacher could not reach the objective she inherited. Following the same way of operating can only go so far because a student is a different person. She gives Tamamo the advice to find her own style and ends the apprenticeship outright. Tamamo does not volunteer to leave or answer in agreement. Her later memory preserves the pain and the teacher’s last useful words, while considerable detail of what she did afterward is absent. Yūki had pressing concerns about her sight and a real belief in practical independence. Later she finds in herself a less disinterested motive. She wanted to avoid responsibility for a possible death and the knowledge of it. Tamamo interprets the abrupt separation as Yūki having seen her wish to erase herself. Her belief has a plausible basis in her repeated imitation and the actual advice she receives. Silent suffering cannot prove that Yūki fully understood the private reason. V05 S21 P0164–0208/P0925–0935/P1070–1080. [The separation and its motives](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V05_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v05-e008--the-choice-to-leave).

For two more months she continues in games and describes having to live as herself. Learning to depend on her own decisions is partly the effect of a suggestion she still values. It is a change in practice. She does not lose all wish for her teacher’s gaze, and thinks of their coming confrontation as an occasion to show her a particular self. Her ability to take direction from an admired person, her discomfort at the end of the relationship and the chosen work after it all require time to explain. They allow a bounded account of development without asserting a completed recovery from self-dislike. V05 S21 P0909–0935.

## Shirō and the team she stands with

Tamamo remembers Shirō from the group that pursued her in Halloween Night. She recognizes a loss she saw and says simply 「任せられない」 when asked to trust an apparently unreliable teammate. Shirō initially fails to place her. Seeing what Shirō can currently do supplies more evidence than the old encounter, and Tamamo adjusts enough to tell her something useful about Yūki’s weakened right eye. She also aids Shirō after being asked, and later puts the changing team standing before her. The initial objection and subsequent competent help belong to different evidence at different times. Helping Shirō with the match does not assign Shirō’s specific decisions in the duel to Tamamo. Their relationship combines reluctance, corrective evidence, useful information and work on the same side. When asked how she knew, Tamamo says only that there was a relation for a time. The brief answer leaves its exact character and emotional investment outside Shirō’s knowledge. She then volunteers to take the final round if Yūki wins the preceding one. V05 S21 P0426–0439/P0560–0631/P0690–0724/P0827–0844. [The actual recognition between teammates](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V05_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v05-e010--being-recognized-in-another-teams-room).

The team accepts her proposal. She is relatively intact, already has a win and thinks the others have less chance. She believes she can do something her old teacher will not expect because she knows the habits they practiced, and she wants her to see 「ほかの誰でもない〈私〉」. Both the group’s remaining option and the personal demand belong in the explanation. At the fight she keeps the familiar degree of distance at first, uses the patience Yūki identified and varies the pattern that Yūki expects. 「ご無沙汰でございます、幽鬼さん」 is a remarkable greeting with an actual purpose. It reaches a barely sighted woman who had not realized who she was fighting, and its form is recognizable from ordinary apprenticeship. The recognition unsettles the opponent and gives Tamamo a moment without making the heart of the declaration insincere. She wounds Yūki severely and temporarily has much more practical authority than the nominal difference in experience would suggest. V05 S21 P0909–0913/P0939–1050.

Yūki beats Tamamo until she is unresponsive, then believes she is unconscious and alive. Yūki decides separately to stab her. The act is shown before the loss of the surrounding details and actual confirmation of Yūki’s #62 clear. An independent check of Tamamo's vital signs is unavailable within the scene. V06 supplies Yūki’s recovered understanding that she killed her former pupil and the fact of death independently used in conversation. V07 adds Shirō’s account that losing by Tamamo’s death rather than surrender gave Team 8 the needed advantage on a tiebreak. Shirō’s own later living presence is independently secure. Her statement does not expose Tamamo to some new choice, prove that Tamamo aimed to die, or establish the entire team’s official result. Treat a late hallucination, the earlier actual pupil and the teammate’s retrospect as separate evidence. ## Written speech and choices under comparison

With Yūki, Tamamo uses 私 and the respectful 幽鬼さん, accepts instruction in formulas such as 「かしこまりました」, can combine very careful politeness with persistent refusal to retreat, and becomes candid slowly. 「……自分が嫌いだから、でしょうか……」 in response to the question about becoming a player is a partial truth, tentative enough to leave her reasons inaccessible. Her fuller account of falling for Yūki and wanting to become like her remains private. Around Shirō the pace and formulations change. 「任せられない」 is a judgment offered to a peer. 「人の勝手でしょ」 makes an immediate boundary, and 「一時期、関係を持ったことがある」 supplies just enough explanation while refusing its details. The later formal greeting to Yūki does not signal return to student obedience. One recognizable register can reach a former teacher during competitive action. The silence at the separation is its own evidence of shock, not acceptance. All such effects come from dialogue and narration, without a performed Japanese voice. V05 S19 P0086–0109, S21 P0105–0111/P0164–0208/P0426–0439/P0693–0713/P0791–0811/P0827–0844/P0987–1005.

Where an inexperienced Tamamo encounters useful ability and thinks the person can change her future, she is likely to make a concrete offer and persist across marked refusal. Where information and responsibility turn more immediate, she can stop deferring, check equipment or an opponent’s answer, act to preserve a partner and change a tactical prediction. Neither pattern predicts a surrender of her own judgment across contexts. Early closeness gives no warrant for broad inference about assent; training provides strong bounded evidence of patience and revised practice; a team duty supplies a new audience, costs and independent decisions. In the final phase she can use familiar forms while pursuing a new purpose. Family history, independent details of school, long-term romantic preferences, authority over future teammates and reliable answers to novel decisions outside those known roles remain unavailable.
