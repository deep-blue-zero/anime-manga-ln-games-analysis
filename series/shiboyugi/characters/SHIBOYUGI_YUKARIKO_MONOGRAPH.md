# Yukariko

紫子 is read Yukariko from 紫=ゆかり and 子=こ. She is an older organizer archivist and a former undercover officer in public security. In V10 she hands the player Shiro a disc that she says contains footage of a past ninety-ninth game, then protects Shiro when another employee questions her about restricted information. Her practices for handling confidential material are exacting. The choice to protect a confidante occurs after a much earlier decision to keep herself safe. Neither the date of her birth nor a surname is given. This portrait includes spoilers through the admitted Japanese V10 ending. V10 S16 P0033–0097/P0202–0277.

## The investigator who stayed

As a young public-security officer Yukariko went undercover to investigate the organizers of lethal games. She remembers disgust at players and staff taking pride in the work. She was nevertheless able to share the staff's wavelength on the surface and says she gathered enough evidence to expect a crackdown. Her immediate superior told her the inquiry was finished and hinted that somebody had made a deal over the heads of those performing it. An organizer representative soon revealed that they already knew her identity. The representative said the organizers sought only to run their games, claimed they had no intention of harming the public interest and made good treatment contingent on Yukariko's compliance. She remembers the order and encounter directly. She could only infer the level and terms of any deal, and memory of her apparent readiness to bring the case does not establish what prosecutors could have proved. V10 S16 P0212–0224.

The furious 「クソ犯罪者どもが」 belongs to the protest Yukariko still wishes she had made. She actually remembers fear, gratitude at being spared and surrender of the purpose she had found in the work. Her security career remained an option in her own account, but she elected to stay with the institution and let its archive work occupy her. She can imagine ways of challenging it again without police support. The fact she imagines them makes her persistent inaction more informative about the loss she calls 青春, a youth she associates with the ability to act on conviction. It does not make her past compliance wholehearted. V10 S16 P0225–0236. [The recalled investigation and accommodation](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e003--an-archive-out-of-view).

The present archive is another test of what she means by aging. Her office is six floors up with no elevator. After contemplating the steps every morning, she finds that unpleasant thoughts now pass quickly enough to make the effort bearable. The institution keeps rooms of old tapes most employees do not know about, while its archivist finds forgetting personally comfortable. Her actual readiness to spend days reviewing material shows she can sustain effort when a task matters. Her former police self, her judgment that ordinary concerns dissolve with age and her work over repeated days belong to different kinds of evidence. There is no disclosed family life, leisure pattern or intimate history. V10 S16 P0202–0214/P0053–0065.

## What she will share with Shiro

Shiro approached her in the street more than a year before their shown exchange. Yukariko wonders how Shiro found her and whether Shiro knows her former job. By the time of the exchange Shiro has given her a key to a vacant family property where they meet regularly. Yukariko checks that Shiro carries no transmitting device and rules out email because a record can survive and a communication might be intercepted. She tells a skeptical Shiro 「相手は運営よ」. The device check and the agreed contact routine are facts. The organization's capacity to plant devices inside players is Yukariko's warning. Much later in the volume, an actual call by another organizer employee is interrupted, which independently makes some internal monitoring plausible without validating the specific means Yukariko imagines. V10 S16 P0033–0052/P0231/P0307–0336.

Yukariko tells Shiro that she searched the archive, found the claimed recording and dubbed an original videotape. She sets a DVD in front of her, asks that it be guarded and reminds her about the payment. Shiro leaves with the disc. The terms under discussion do not confirm payment or viewing, and the story does not show anyone authenticate its contents. Yukariko also adds an unsolicited warning of a game larger than Candle Woods, using unusually vigorous recruitment as one sign. Takami has separately alerted Shiro to the number of newcomers. A large event is later enacted, but the advance warning does not deliver its name, exact size or rules or let Shiro avoid it. The resources that let Yukariko help Shiro are specific. Her position grants access to a historical archive and some knowledge of the organization while leaving the projects compartmentalized. V10 S16 P0053–0097, S19 P0001–0007/P0089–0131. [The handover and the coming project](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e003--an-archive-out-of-view).

Yukariko considers the practical explanation that an ally in a senior position could make the organization easier to change. Her private answer is 「彼女の青春がうらやましい」. She likes being near the energy she has lost, and makes its continued existence a stake of her own. Her help also has a price, and Shiro needs real access to old records. Both women behave as people with something to gain. Yukariko asks 「尸狼さんのほうは、具合はどう？」 and tells Shiro to avoid getting killed before she reaches the end, while Shiro keeps a warning of her own unease at their parting to herself. The novel shows practical trust, a desire for another person's company and consequential loyalty. It gives no basis for a claim about erotic interest or intimacy. V10 S16 P0068–0097/P0231–0236.

## When caution means keeping quiet

Yukariko calls her position 〈社史編纂室〉 and the later questioner identifies her with the 資料整理室. Both descriptions belong to the record, which does not lay out the formal relation between the two offices. The armed woman addresses her as 「紫子さん」 in an apparently procedural opening and shifts to asserting extensive questioning authority. Yukariko quickly works through the possible reasons for being followed. She admits policing and unauthorized inquiry but twice denies passing information. Her inner account is that she might save herself by giving the questioner Shiro, whose ability to play might then be taken away. She chooses to retain the risk. When ordered to continue upstairs she even tells her questioner to be careful lest she expire before she can answer. The effect comes partly from a decorous surface in 「慎重におやりなさいね」 applied to a threat whose outcome the narrative withholds. V10 S16 P0243–0277.

Yukariko usually notices what someone would leave behind by speaking and tries to move contacts onto terms she can control. A recognized authority leaves her capable of assessing evidence and feigning agreement. Fear of being killed once made personal safety decisive. When silence offers a concrete chance to keep someone else from suffering what Yukariko thinks will close her future, her later composure lets her act differently. Her report of satisfaction at the second chance is better evidence for the choice than a general claim that she has returned to policing or resistance. The direct warm and firm forms with Shiro, the unspoken younger anger and the lightly insolent politeness toward the questioner are written dialogue and interior narration. Any conclusion about how she would act during a different interrogation would need the stakes, the target, and the protection a disclosure could buy. The next scene of a staffer describing an unnamed punished pair cannot settle the archivist's ultimate treatment or Shiro's connection to that particular leak.
