# Mononobe

物部 carries the ruby モノノ／ベ and appears as Mononobe in the Japanese edition's Latin lettering. She tells Takami that she is sixteen during her fourth outing as a player, Gimmicky Mansion. She describes herself as a runaway falsely accused at a prestigious school and says she now plays to support herself. Her agent's investigation gives a sharply different account of her conduct. Mononobe can notice practical details, coordinate people and keep a courteous surface while rejecting even an arguable share of responsibility for harm. Long after she and Yūki fight over an exit, she acts on a grudge the other player does not know she has kept. The Japanese witness through V10 leaves any other civil name and exact family circumstances unknown. Her personal appearances are concentrated in V09. V09 S05-I01, S18 P0185–0222/P0405–0433/P0491–0537, S24 P0551–0625.

## The account she keeps giving

Mononobe tells Takami 「はめられたんですよ」, that she was set up, before explaining the rival groups in her school and the accusation that supposedly got her expelled. She says she could not tell her parents and left home. She recognizes that she easily makes enemies and says 「私は私ですからね」. This self-recognition has a limit. She presents her disposition as what she has had to accept and the decisive wrong as something others did to her. When Takami affably suggests she is suited to the work, Mononobe says 「それはどうも」 coldly and asks her to return to searching. The polite ending allows a rebuke without familiarity. V09 S18 P0185–0222. [Mononobe's spoken grievance](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e004--the-name-mononobe-gives-to-defeat).

The agent heard much the same story when sounding her out over tea about two months before this game. The agent recalls evidence of conduct so severe that “bullying” scarcely described it and says witnesses mostly agreed. The reported camera evidence remains secondhand. After Mononobe dismissed the contrary accounts as malicious fabrication, the agent chose not to ask about the camera and signed her up. Mononobe's age, presentation of family circumstances and role as a runaway come through situated sources. The evidence of harm is stronger than her account allows, while the exact sequence, who saw what and the school's handling of its decision stay unresolved. In V09's later attack, Mononobe herself remembers an occasion on which she meant to slap someone as a form of correction and then went further. She places the cause on the other's expression. The remembered act fits the agent's account of deliberate harm and persistent denial without letting the reader identify the victim or independently prove all the school allegations. V09 S18 P0522–0537, S24 P0598–0606.

The agent sees disposition in her readiness to take up each invitation then offered, roughly one every half month in the early relationship, and her tendency to become animated in play. The agent moves from calling her outward posture affected innocence to believing she takes the account of necessity seriously. That judgment is not an independent diagnosis. Mononobe's own later thought supplies evidence compatible with some degree of self-belief when there is no interlocutor to convince. Her body reacts with excitement as she is attacking Yūki and she still tells herself 「私のせいじゃない。私ははめられたのだ」. The act, the excitement and the rejection of responsibility coexist. No witness supplies a complete prior life of deception or pleasure in violence. A childhood outdoor school memory gives one unusual ordinary detail, the frustration of having to prepare a raw fish from an older man's hard-to-follow instructions. It is recalled because of what Mononobe is doing in V09, so gives scant grounds for generalization about her tastes. Her pastimes, ongoing family contact, romantic and intimate history are otherwise unknown. V09 S18 P0529–0537, S24 P0595–0606.

## The competent teammate who gives up a clear

After three of the twelve are no longer working with the group, the remaining nine find an opened ceiling and evidence of a route someone may already have used. Mononobe takes the risk of investigating it and finds it blocked. Other players locate the numbered map. She combines their finding with her own to infer eight routes that each close after one use, and urges the others to pursue two participants already seeking exits. Her reasoning about the used route taking Takami is plausible from the scene, and the actual later escape through Yūki's route verifies single transit there. Mononobe's judgment and quick leadership are real evidence of work under shared pressure. V09 S18 P0405–0433/P0585–0610. [What the opened and blocked routes reveal](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e002--a-communal-search-becomes-a-scarcity-problem).

Hinoe has raised an alarm and Sana is crawling toward an opened hatch when Mononobe arrives. Mononobe shoots Sana, then deliberately kills her while privately thinking 「ざまあみろ」. She tells the players who follow that the route is still available and says 「私は使いませんので、誰かどうぞ」. The offer leaves her precise reasons unstated. Asked whether she means it, she says she has business left. When someone reports Yūki has been found she visibly brightens and runs. Neither a grudge about injured sight nor repayment of an earlier debt yet explains this turn. Those belong to what happens next. This first costly choice shows that pursuing a target can matter to her more than using a secure-looking competitive advantage. V09 S18 P0462–0518.

She fights Yūki as one of four opponents and sustains a wound through the right side of her face. She renews the attack rather than breaking off. When an exit opens, Yūki disengages and beats her through, and the closing barrier crushes Mononobe's arm. She shouts 「逃げるな！」 and 「必ず殺してやる！」 at the retreating woman. Her anger is outward here whereas the contempt at Sana's fall was private. The narration marks some of the outburst as failing to form words, so the actual threats and loss of composure are stronger evidence than any reconstruction of the missing words. Mononobe nevertheless clears the game by a route not shown. In the hospital she learns the facial wound has permanently disabled her right sight, which is distinct from the arm injury inflicted by the gate. She had assumed the institution could fix anything she suffered in a competition. Her doctor suggests treating the damage as a lesson in whom she chooses to fight. She hears an accusation and resolves to retaliate, regardless of the usual terms of competition. V09 S18 P0553–0610, S24 P0576–0585.

## What she gets to do with the grievance

Mononobe tells herself reprisal is what she has always done. She enters as many invited games as possible in the ensuing search, and by her fifteenth she has become fairly accomplished. The narrative gives the search and result, while leaving the opponents, purposes and dates of each intervening event unspecified. In Phantom Thief a message identifies Yūki as a guard. Mononobe takes an exhibit that could be useful for a clear and stays to find her. Yūki is already injured from an earlier, independent fight. Mononobe shoots and deliberately maims her, thinking about how to inflict a harm repair will not undo and recognizing the pleasure this gives her as something she resists naming. Busutake interrupts and kills Mononobe before she finishes. The revenge produces permanent harm while falling short of the death Mononobe planned. V09 S24 P0522–0567/P0576–0625, S26 P0262–0288. [The interruption and its consequences](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e011--interrupted-revenge-and-the-late-hospital-awakening).

She uses 「私」 in her deliberate accounts of herself and retains it when admitting to herself how much her actions affect her. The polite 「ちゃんとしてください」 that redirects a colleague, the outward command to Yūki and the inward denial of blame have different work. In matters she treats as shared and practical, Mononobe tends to look closely and can direct coordinated action. When an antagonist becomes a personally important target, she has repeatedly set aside a secure option and pursued the target. Recognition of injury or arousal can sharpen her resolve without making her accept her own part in originating the conflict. The record supports these conditions and a substantial change from first encounter to sustained revenge. It supplies no all-purpose explanation of her earlier school conduct, how she would respond to care from someone she trusts, or whether she could put aside an injury she considered just. The evidence for tone is written dialogue, descriptions of delivery and interior language. V09 S18 P0185–0222/P0405–0433/P0510–0537/P0573–0609, S24 P0576–0606.
