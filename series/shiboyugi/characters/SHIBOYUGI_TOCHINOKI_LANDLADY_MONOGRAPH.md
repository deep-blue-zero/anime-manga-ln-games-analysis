# The Tochinoki landlady

The older woman who owns Tochinoki apartments also lives there, alone, in room 101. She is addressed as 大家さん. Yūki thinks she must have seen a legal name when she took her lease and cannot recall it. The woman can administer bills carefully, ignore what a resident wants to be called and put herself between a threatening crowd and the other tenants. Near the end of the shared residence she pays better attention to the lived constraints of some tenants than to their line of work. Her attempts to keep people around and her ability to let someone leave speak to each other. This portrait's latest direct evidence is Yūki’s move out in V08. Through V10 the tenant's plan to return and the older woman's promised tenure have no observed fulfillment. V07 S16 P0021–0074/S21 P0047–0075/S23 P0342–0369, V08 S20 P0288–0345 and S22 P0026–0097, V10 S16 P0344–0408.

## Rent, water and company

All four young occupants tolerate the monthly talk. Yūki speculates that they put up with it in return for rents lower than elsewhere. She knocks without warning, can order Yūki to follow and interprets the assembled faces as evidence of aimlessness. One lecture's example of planning is footballer Fudō Ryōei, whom she regards as having understood early what is needed to make a career. Her belief that other people need more sustained planning runs against private knowledge of both Yūki’s life goal and Yayoi’s inherited job. The lecture is an occasion on which the landlady can give company an explicit purpose, with no independent confirmation that seeing other people is the motive. She notices Yūki’s haircut and generally attends to the duration of her tenants’ presence. These are more concrete sources of interest than a supposition by others that someone who has infrequent visitors has no relatives. V07 S16 P0021–0074, V08 S20 P0288–0345/S22 P0026–0097. [The usual threshold](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e001--apartment-thresholds-and-making-a-break).

Her care over the water fee is ordinary and consequential. The total is shared evenly and is collected personally. When Ramona overpays she goes to return the difference, bangs on the door and demands entry with レモン. Ramona asks her to say ラモーナ and later tells Yūki what an unwanted English connotation the alternate name carries. The recipient shows hurt and the proprietor keeps using it. She may consider the sounds functionally equal. Whether she knows that she is conveying contempt is uncertain. What she can choose to pay back is a precise form of consideration that does not make her socially accommodating in the same scene. A portrait that takes her apparent hostility as sufficient evidence of either ill will or secret generosity loses half the exchange. V07 S21 P0047–0075. [Written names and actual small payments](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e005--ramonas-refusal-and-the-cost-of-useful-information).

## What she tries to protect

When bikers surround the building looking for Akane and Yūki, the landlady acts according to her own account of what is about to happen. She treats the encroachment as a threat to her place and its occupants. She rushes the crowd with two kitchen knives, creating the opening the two women use to get away. She is not privy to the whole dispute, and Akane cannot tell whether her shouted apology and explanation are understood before they go. Yūki knows enough about the actual effect to express private thanks and worry about what reprisal might follow. The next encounter at home carries different danger and shows the landlady in need of protection herself. Yayoi can perceive, act and speak usefully about the threat. Across the two disturbances authority over an ordinary domestic space and the ability to read the current danger come apart. The second scene is neither proof that the first action was naïve nor permission for her to intervene successfully in every future case. V07 S23 P0342–0369, V08 S20 P0529–0622. [Who helps at the apartment](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V08_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v08-e009--four-views-of-the-same-address).

After Akane moves out the landlady begins cooking for the other three. Dinner is at ten, an actual accommodation for school. Yūki and the others infer she wants them to stay. What the older woman states is that it is becoming harder to keep managing the place and she considers having somebody else do it. There are relatives who might eventually inherit. These claims qualify the earlier interpretation that nobody is left. They give no names or evidence that an owner has actually changed. Her preparing meals provides a new means of company irrespective of her private reason for leaving the lectures aside. The actual room tenancy, shared food and separate management discussion have different histories. V08 S20 P0288–0345 and S22 P0026–0097.

## A tenant's future

As Yūki takes a temporary place after the further violence, the landlady knows that something difficult has been concealed. She distinguishes the tenant's earlier presentation from looking considerably more alive. She says she worries more about youth passing without undertaking a chosen direction than about a career it is not her business to identify. A specific refusal to tell gives her a chance to press her formal authority as a property owner. She gives advice without forcing disclosure, and says she will continue as landlady until the tenant comes back. The result is a mutual forecast under existing circumstances. Yūki retains the lease and thinks of wiping mold from her room when she comes back. It is neither a verified repair of the relationship's last known arrangement nor a license to infer why the landlady never previously asked. She describes sleeping from midnight to four after the crisis in keeping with her own view of age. Whatever the scope of the habit, it places her at the door early enough to have the exchange. V08 S22 P0026–0097. [A specific agreement under an indefinite separation](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V08_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v08-e011--a-furnished-place-and-a-retained-lease).

The writing gives an older voice described by another person as gravelly, with the landlady’s own おれ and plain imperatives. おら and a frequently mispronounced name accompany a mundane refund. ついてきな introduces a group lecture. 気が済むまでやることだな follows an actual withheld explanation and changes the weight that Yūki gives her advice. A tenant reciprocates with a rough joke about returning before she dies, and the seriousness of the answer does not prove it was heard as a joke. The source warrants specific audience distinctions and described effects, with voice quality limited to what the written scene reports. V07 S16 P0021–0055/S21 P0047–0075, V08 S22 P0063–0097.

In a familiar dispute about money or a resident’s ordinary needs, the evidence supports direct personal action and considerable roughness of address. Whether she would protect someone who says little depends on what she sees and what kind of threat she can understand. The rooftop or city outside the property, the next stage of Yūki’s absence and motives for any longstanding habits beyond the household remain weakly evidenced. Her tenant has no trustworthy access to her romantic life. Concrete hospitality and practical vigilance are enough to explain why the younger people without obvious means of intimacy will give her some things she asks for and keep others private. V07 S16 P0021–0074/S23 P0342–0369, V08 S20 P0288–0345 and S22 P0026–0097.
