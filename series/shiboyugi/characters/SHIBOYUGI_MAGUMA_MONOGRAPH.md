# Maguma

真熊, Maguma, is the playing name of 赭熊早百合, Shaguma Sayuri. She was the founding chief of the riding group Red Bear before becoming known for exceptional size, trained strength and her preference for working alone in the lethal games. Other people declare a solitary fighting manner a form of identity. Maguma sometimes does this herself. She also remembers the names and dispositions of a crowd she has not seen for years, intervenes on an old subordinate's behalf and honors one person’s apparent willingness to risk injury before later choosing to rescue her. By the time she says she might look for ordinary employment, keeping away from any group has begun to sound to her like something she deliberately made into a posture. The change does not make her willing to join Shiro's organization or to offer help in every kind of emergency. This portrait runs through the last depicted moment with Airi on Jamboree Ship. ## What a solitary player actually does

At One Fine Day, Yūki's fortieth game, Maguma's own number is forty-one. A later joint invitation to Cloudy Beach is her forty-third. She can be lively and relatively generous in giving information about what she has previously seen, including the winter game Airi was hurt leaving. That sociability has a limit. She does not want to be persistently guarded or watched by people whose roles she does not know. She separates from the other players' lodgings and makes access to her working area difficult. Bandages make the rest suspect she has been attacked or played a part in wrongdoing. She is reluctant to make her preparations available just because her absence attracts suspicion. The strangeness of her marks also helps Airi work out that the organizers put tracking devices into the players. The guarded answer is compatible with tactical intelligence and concern about what a group could do to her. It also deprives others of information they could use until they make independent discoveries. When a person wrapped in improvised bandages comes to Maguma after she has removed the devices, the risk of encountering her without the prior advantage changes that person's immediate choice. The isolated player still has a directed preference. In the V04 opening she goes aboard after waiting for the island’s eight-day pickup, hears the agent's report that Etsusei died and asks about Yūki. When her agent calls the other woman a favorite she denies being invested in a particular player and offers affinity between styles as an alternative. Her own thought preserves a preference for Yūki over an opponent whose way of using her body she disliked. She has also independently noticed that Yūki's right eye whitened and wonders what practical difference it made. The effect of her preferred term, 唯我独尊, is partially ironic. A professed tenet of autonomy coexists with paying attention to this opponent's health and feeling relief. The friendship or result she claims is no more general than the actual preference narrated there. V04 S15 P0001–0023. The conditions link their ability to succeed without turning them into a pair that finds each other congenial. Maguma's responses to scares and loose physical control look more appropriate than Yūki's. She refers to her prior history as a biker and resents being leaned on. The sources stage her steadiness where her younger colleague remains startled despite experience. It shows a comparison in this room and does not establish a general difference in courage. ## An old group and the person who leaves it

Before her career as a player, she begins Red Bear with people who want to ride together after a conflict with the existing local group. She regards the ongoing practice of gathering and riding as more important than imposing an elaborate purpose. Other people want a name, code or hierarchy and soon use the group for incompatible expectations. The red of the bikes and the banner gives social identity to people who share little else. Maguma tires of the increasing apparatus and leaves. She repaints her large bike white and black and lives in an isolated house on a high site outside the built-up area. A trip to a large supermarket is a point of comparison with her urgent return. The narrator records that she dislikes swearing as a gesture and finds herself doing it as soon as the need to help feels real. She retains selective attachments and thoroughly ordinary routines alongside the strong language with which other people identify her. Civil biography apart from this period, her possessions and her work plans is limited. V07 S23 P0670–0740/P0844–0876, V09 S21 P0013–0058.

What brings her back is a phone call from a wounded Botan, who worries that asking an unaffiliated person for help is humiliating and might be dangerous. Maguma recognizes her voice without recent contact, corrects the old 総長 address and tells her that speaking up was valuable. She decides the call has made known a problem she has actual responsibility to stop. She then deliberately approaches a much larger group because she wants contact and can judge who is likely to support Kōen. She remembers names and personalities although she is capable of brushing off a different visitor on the pretext or basis of not remembering names. The exact operative contrast is a preference to honor relations and practical knowledge from this past, rather than a universal memory trait. She rejects the factions’ effort to use her name by saying the banner must end and that people are free to gather separately. Similar acts in each direction are to prevent a false assumption that the founder has endorsed either. On deciding to intervene, Maguma remembers retaliating against the local gang that repeatedly picked fights with her and thinks her decision to leave without guiding or dissolving Red Bear helped bring about the current crisis. Later, when Yūki expects regret about ending the group now, Maguma says the end felt natural and reports no conflict. Her initial urgent response and the attention she still gives people keep the outcome from looking effortless in every sense. V07 S23 P0670–0740 (especially P0732–0740)/P0746–0792/P0921–0979/P1152–1191, V09 S21 P0035–0058. [Conflicting meanings of a group she helped form](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e008--three-ways-to-explain-a-groups-purpose).

She recognizes that the actual capacities of the younger people in the confrontation differ from what she can handle. At the first roadside encounter she tries to cause enough hurt to stop their movements while avoiding deaths and lasting injuries. After the different large encounter inside the hotel, she says she intended no killing but cannot guarantee every person's survival. Local intention and checked outcome are distinct. She and Akane and Yūki work effectively in this second conflict despite different causes for being there. Maguma asks them to use her civil name, has a concrete warning about Kōen’s danger, and takes the part of the operation she is strong enough to perform. When Akane wants her to make literal the earlier promise to strike everyone, she mutters that the proposal was figurative. She tries to dissuade an already injured person, then does what the other requests. That exchange treats an unfamiliar practice as something other people may choose to value. An earlier forced act, voluntarily asking to accept another and use of ordinary professional courtesy still have different status. V07 S23 P0746–0792/P0921–0979/P1048–1053/P1152–1191.

## A boundary with Shiro

By V09 Shiro's own #8 and #23 meetings have made Maguma someone the organizer-seeking player can approach. As her own estimate puts the veteran at around sixty clears, she thinks Maguma might furnish the rare route to #99. She is also aware of a residential address obtained through earlier surveillance. Maguma actually stops an unknown visitor before allowing the conversation. She says her work gives her reason to treat surprises at home as potentially hostile, and Shiro has to negotiate from there. The prospective club member declines. The refusal is stronger than the loose stage-estimate that had suggested her availability. No qualifying intent follows from her strength, professional experience or ability to deal with a hidden threat. In Jamboree Maguma can still accept Shiro's instructions for a narrow shared objective without reversing the earlier membership choice. She tells herself that her old decision to avoid all teams after Red Bear is becoming tiresome. Actual short-term needs and renewed sociability can coexist with a considered refusal of long-term control. V08 S18 P0167–0174, V09 S21 P0013–0079, V10 S19 P0394–0456. [The visitor who reaches her door](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e005--the-visitor-the-bird-and-the-room-selected-for-safety).

At the new game Maguma avoids the crowd at the roll call because it makes her dizzy, and trusts her unusual size to make her countable in absentia. She calls Airi's survival a surprise and asks uncharacteristically many conversational questions. Airi tells her the real #95. Maguma says the games happened to suit her, while entertaining a future other than solving their largest challenge. Doctors have described cumulative wear and a danger of grave loss of function if she keeps playing. She has no current symptoms and tests her working hand in front of her interlocutor. そろそろ潮時か describes a plausible stopping point, and she suggests she might make this her last entry in view of meeting Airi. An ordinary job might involve belonging to an organization. This attracts her in part because it was unappealing to a previous version of herself. One cannot infer a specific new profession, rehabilitation or exact registration from this decision. V10 S19 P0143–0192. [How she talks of stopping](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e007--maguma-finds-a-stopping-point).

## Two ways to cooperate with Airi

In keeping with more localized cooperation, she follows instructions relevant to their side’s immediate task and pursues a dangerous person she already knows as a formidable player. She sees a partly injured Airi holding Yūki and concludes Airi is volunteering to be hit if it will stop Yūki. The narration leaves whether Maguma directly heard her request uncertain. Her 望み通りにしてやる is a construal presented as what Airi reads in Maguma's eyes. The next scene directly shows Maguma explaining her own inference after she fires. She makes another judgment from situation and group access than Shiro, who decides to preserve Airi, prohibits the others from firing and shoots toward Maguma. To make either woman answer for the group as a whole would erase real conflict in what loyalty practically demands. Maguma uses a view of the wider situation, not certainty about what the endangered person could later do. V10 S19 P0394–0456, S23 P0133–0178/P0268–0277. [Taking a colleague's risk seriously](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e012--airi-sets-a-term-of-her-own).

When Yūki incapacitates Airi in water, Maguma stops the chase and carries Airi up to a safer spot. Airi recognizes this as behavior different from her model of the other woman. Maguma tells her she could always look after friends, invokes being entangled in a 腐れ縁 and considers whether the injured survivor can still make plans and fight beside her. Help and judgment that renewed cooperation is useful are both expressed. Airi’s own expectations also change, and in the next crisis they can reach the same hypothesis about where Yūki has managed to go without agreeing to take the same risks. Shelter on Deck 17 supplies the last actual extent of their common action. The collective result does not fix their individual trajectories. Maguma can accept that cooperation is useful, act to ensure someone's survival, and stop obeying someone whose appraisal of larger danger she no longer trusts. V10 S23 P0182–0216/P0268–0277/P0384–0459 and S26 P0026–0028.

## A rough manner with many different functions

The written あたし, あんた, よう and the various clipped forms make it easy to recognize Maguma in blunt scenes. They can acknowledge uncertainty or soften a stated limit as well as express command. Responding to Botan she rejects a title with 普通に名前で呼びな and follows it with a specific concern about her voice and condition. The recognition and thanks carry as much relationship evidence as the title refusal. Her 何者だ when she finds Shiro at the door demands practical identification under urgent uncertainty. She confronts Shiro's protection strategy with 阿呆が and “you can't tell friend from foe” once she is herself fired at, with entirely different aims than the question she had asked alone at her door. On the high deck she announces the idea of an enduring troublesome tie while pretending to take offense at an injured friend’s surprise. Compare the reasoned private forecast of adversaries' motives to the directness of a colloquial group exit. Speech need not become gentle in order to permit affection, service or injury appraisal. The original writing occasionally characterizes the strength of the sound and how a familiar person recognizes it. These are audience-dependent written cues with no performed voice here. V07 S23 P0684–0709/P0748–0784/P1153–1191, V09 S21 P0033–0058, V10 S19 P0146–0192/S23 P0151–0178/P0384–0422.

When known partners can combine different capabilities, she can take on the physically demanding role, give plainly difficult advice and re-evaluate the rest as the practical limits change. She is more likely to offer assistance on seeing a person she considers worth the work, or a responsibility she treats as her own, than on accepting titles or the first principle offered by a group. Her help to Botan and Airi supports those specific response ranges. The disabling risk she describes, the uncertainty about future secular work, and her ability to miscalculate who would willingly maintain their cooperation set limits on carrying those responses into a new setting. The last game offers no medical proof about when her body will fail. Neither her rough defense of privacy nor carrying and sheltering someone grounds a romantic partner history. Her bike, solitude, ordinary trips and aversion to crowds are directly usable parts of the portrait. Ordinary diet, family, hobbies separate from riding and attitude to intimacy remain open.
