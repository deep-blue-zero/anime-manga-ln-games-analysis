# Shinozaki Sakura

Sakura thinks a group of people who have been badly treated can have a place to recover without setting out to do the same to other people. She also thinks mutual pain can be a language and enjoys the kind of fight she finds expressive. She has welcomed a person who needed the group's company and hoped to find her own reason to leave, yet when someone tries to strip her of the group's title she insists on it and personally fights to keep the banner out of their hands. Care, loyalty, force and a precise idea of whom the force may be used against make her conduct more specific than a story of an old chief resisting change. V07 S23 P0187–0250/P0598–0633.

## The person who remained

篠崎紗九良, Shinozaki Sakura, is the second chief and longest-standing member of Red Bear at the time of the events. She is already there when the high-school-aged Kōen joins. Maguma, known in this part of her life as Shaguma, founded it and left. Later people still use the same title for the founder and the present chief. Sakura's precise age, family situation, education, non-group livelihood and reason for first joining are unknown. Her own continuity is visible in what she does with the group, how she remembers it and what she hoped she and other people might find outside it. V07 S23 P0187–0206/P0623–0633/P0675–0709/P0877–0882/P0929–0940. [The distinct purposes and histories of the leaders](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e008--three-ways-to-explain-a-groups-purpose).

At a regular gathering Akane finds her looking at her phone and asks whether everybody is there. Sakura has a practical estimate, knows some are yet to arrive and expresses the severity of the decline without dwelling on it to the people waiting. She briefs the group on the ride, including practical contingencies. Akane remembers rides improving her mood and now feels chased even before anybody stops them. This is another person's independent description of a difference in atmosphere. Sakura's regular presence and leadership give the group a familiar way to spend time. The memory of the earlier fellowship includes riding, reckless games, fighting and intoxication, as well as projects she regarded as useful. It supplies tastes and social circumstances within that life. How often she engaged in each activity outside the remembered group, what she chose on a quiet day alone and any food or personal leisure not associated with it are unestablished. V07 S23 P0187–0211/P0623–0627. [Gathering and fellowship](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e008--three-ways-to-explain-a-groups-purpose).

Her recollected Red Bear also punishes outsiders for acts its members consider predatory and reprimands conduct within the group they consider base. Sakura does not present it as behavior generally approved by society. She sees it as having limits that members actually discussed and sometimes applied. Those limits distinguish reciprocal fighting and action against people she regards as aggressors from intentionally recreating the group's own suffering at random. The remembered intimidation, attacks and shared recklessness give serious counterexamples to treating every former practice as benevolent or proportionate. They also show why the later disagreement is about which conduct is allowed and whom the existing community protects, rather than simply whether its members should ever act by force. V07 S23 P0233–0250/P0598–0608/P0623–0633. [Practice within a claimed ethic](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e008--three-ways-to-explain-a-groups-purpose).

## Kōen before she was an enemy

Sakura remembers an unusual newcomer from a prestigious private school who could not make herself at home there. In her account the group accepts Kōen because outsiders and people who have been hurt are precisely the people it is for. They take part in a variety of activities together. Eventually Kōen seems to accommodate the school better, does well and gains admission to the place she wanted. The group's custom permits her to leave at graduation when she no longer has the same reason to belong. Sakura accepts the departure, remembers the smile and hopes some day she too can find a reason to leave. That memory shows the acceptance of an independent future for someone she could have wanted to keep. It also suggests that her own authority and personal fulfillment are separate, without establishing a specific career or private life she hoped for at the time. V07 S23 P0623–0627/P0632. [The past that Sakura takes to be shared](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e008--three-ways-to-explain-a-groups-purpose).

Kōen comes back with deeper despair in Sakura's judgment and wants to use a larger, stronger organization to inflict suffering and force recognition. Sakura tries to stop her new business. She also sees charisma, intelligence and practical leadership she did not encounter in the student. Once the conflict grows, she privately admits that Kōen has the ability to win and that she herself may lose. She wants to know why those abilities are being used this way. The question of whether the group's earlier care helped temporarily or the smile had concealed failure remains hers. Kōen's own account of the work incident supplies an independent grievance and the subsequent purpose. It gives no reason for Sakura to have understood all of that while their earlier relationship was working. Their later opposition and Sakura's changed appraisal of Kōen are depicted developments. The account of a past care relation is later disclosure to the reader. V07 S23 P0468–0474/P0613–0633/P0983–0998. [Disappointment as well as rivalry](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e008--three-ways-to-explain-a-groups-purpose).

## When the banner is contested

During a ride the people who stayed away from the gathering have positioned themselves in the road. A speaker from Kōen's side calls Sakura by name instead of title and says she no longer holds the office. Sakura protests and asks what the people are doing. The speaker identifies Akane as someone who interfered in work their faction had approved. Akane had in fact helped Yūki reach Ramona during the earlier abduction. Sakura answers that this kind of work ought to be interfered with. The recognition people around Akane give her in that moment is a practical sign of what Sakura's response permits. It is not evidence that Sakura directed or knew in advance about the rescue. When told that each person must accept the changes or leave, Sakura refuses the offered alternatives. She says the other faction must stop using the group's banner, threatens future interference and tells them she will drive them out. The emphasis on the title is genuine. So is the defense of someone's ability to intervene against an abuse of nominally shared authority. They do not give every dissenter room to keep doing as they please under Sakura. V07 S21 P0319–0373/P0414–0436, S23 P0187–0250. [An order and the two people who decline its terms](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e008--three-ways-to-explain-a-groups-purpose).

She can retreat when outmatched, regroup and summon support. She also fights directly and persists despite attrition. In her own account the headbutt is her favored attack because its sensation reaches the person giving it as well as the person receiving it. The ability to give and take pain in a fight seems to her a way to communicate. It is something she enjoys in addition to regarding it as expressive. A person knocked unconscious by her has not thereby agreed to the meaning. A more bounded observation is that she fights with force, stops when the opponent is no longer conscious and walks around asking after her own injured people. Her care has limits in the situation. After the third clash she chooses to take the people still able to move and leave those who cannot. Botan is among those left. She wakes, leaves separately and eventually calls the founder. That is an actual effect of a wounded follower making a decision after Sakura made hers. No source shows Sakura asking her to call, approving her choice, or being able to foresee the founder's decision to end both factions. V07 S23 P0598–0616/P0675–0709/P0725–0740. [The fighting, loss of numbers and founders' different decisions](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e008--three-ways-to-explain-a-groups-purpose).

## The photograph and the last observation

Akane receives a message saying that the chief has been beaten. Yūki initially thinks of the founder she sees alongside them. The title and the shock get across before the referent is fully clear to that observer. Kōen independently sees Sakura bound, injured and unresponsive. Kōen already knew through a report with a photo, which had made it to Sakura's faction. The photograph is evidence that a captured chief was displayed and the fact of defeat conveyed. It does not give Sakura's prior intent, a medical verdict or the full means of capture. Kōen kicks her and gets no response. The story does not show Sakura waking, speaking after this, being rescued or dying. When Maguma, Akane and Yūki cooperate, the objective they choose together is to overcome Kōen and end the coercive course. This is a separate operation from the earlier rescue of Ramona. The closing aggregate view of injured members and Kōen's later words to those conscious provide no individual outcome for Sakura. V07 S23 P0877–0882/P0890–0946/P1152–1171, S25 P0019–0026. [Capture, the founder's return and the different last sights](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e008--three-ways-to-explain-a-groups-purpose), [The conflict's closure](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e010--the-commissions-close-after-the-fighters-leave).

## Register, ordinary attachment and conditional appraisal

Sakura's 「おう」 to Akane is unembellished and familiar. She communicates business before commenting 「ここまで減っちまうとはな」, with contraction making the dismay immediate. The protest 「〈総長〉だろうが」 comes when name replaces office in an enemy's address. Her 〈掟〉 in the following exchange is a shared rule to which she tries to hold another person, using 「私たち」 to include both sides as historically affected. Once she judges that the other person has chosen the opposition, 「だがうちの看板は使うな」 redraws the group boundary. The prose describes the change of voice and identifies who listens, while private doubt about Kōen's superiority remains unspoken to her followers. The Japanese supplies plain address, rough imperatives and the force of shifts. It gives no acoustically performed voice. The closeness of shared riding and fighting is evidenced in the group, without evidence of attraction, romance or intimate preferences. V07 S23 P0195–0206/P0212–0250/P0610–0633.

Around loyal members in familiar conditions, Sakura is likely to offer straightforward direction, attend to changes in participation and value doing something together. Around people who have not been well received elsewhere, the prior reception of Kōen supports letting them in and allowing a real departure. When former allies claim that outsiders deserve the same cruelty they received, Sakura may use her authority and force to obstruct them. When the group is already under pressure, she can choose retreat or leave people who cannot move rather than insist the collective stays together at any cost. This is shown once in a particularly severe crisis. The earlier fights and her own pleasure in reciprocal pain mean she is not uniformly restrained even where she opposes a specific kind of cruelty. Broader claims about conduct under lawful supervision, private love, a current life outside Red Bear, or what became of her after the capture require independent evidence. V07 S23 P0187–0250/P0598–0633/P0675–0709/P0908–0917.
