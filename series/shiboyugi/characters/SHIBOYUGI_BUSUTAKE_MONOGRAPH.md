# Busutake

毒茸 is read ブス／タケ and spelled Busutake in the admitted Japanese edition's Latin-letter character plates. She is a player, an early member of Shiro's Mikkai and a daughter of the prosthetics artisan who has treated several of the players. She enjoys getting mechanisms to work, including ones she can use to change her own body. She can care for an injured opponent, treat a clubmate as an opposing player, and say that another person's treatment should be hers to control. Her decisions turn on the immediate job, the kind of relationship she wants, and what bodies might be made capable of. Her civil name, age, exact tally and Yūki's eventual treatment are unknown. This portrait includes the Japanese novel through the V10 hospital confrontation. V08 S08-I01, S18 P0149–0166, V09 S08-I01, S24 P0610–0626, S26 P0035–0111, V10 S07-I01 and S26 P0046–0069.

## A craft she was taught and a place she chose

Busutake says she learned the family work from her earliest years. When an older sibling was picked to inherit, she was considered 「不適格」 and decided to play instead. Her father is the artisan Airi already visits for maintenance. Busutake can cover for him, examines Airi's replacement toes and establishes that the signals she is checking appear normal. This is practical substitute skill. The reason she was considered unsuitable, and her ability to perform any of the more advanced procedures associated with her father, remain unknown. She allows that people move from playing into the ancillary trades and sees no reason the reverse should be impossible for her. Her precise maintenance speech to Airi is part of the family legacy she still applies on different terms. V09 S26 P0027–0071. [Busutake and the family workshop](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e012--airis-affirmative-answer-without-shiros-ambition).

She tells Airi that Shiro praised her technique without being repelled by it. She jokes that she may have been taken in by a gifted flatterer and says she has little interest in the prize for ninety-nine clears. She was already living in Mikkai when Awahime joined. She wants company for her particular body practice as well as an exchange of knowledge. These two accounts of what draws her to Mikkai are compatible. One describes a welcome she found unusual; the other a practical want the group has failed to fulfill. Takami works with her on surveillance and game arrangements and later thinks of her as the person with whom she best gets along. There is no identified romance or intimate history. V08 S18 P0149–0166, S20 P0245–0249/P0713–0720, V09 S26 P0073–0089, V10 S21 P0058–0078 and S23 P0463–0474.

At a shared dinner she eats a tortilla with salt and no filling. Her room is full of equipment. When Takami tells her to tidy it, Busutake calls her equipment “accumulated” rather than strewn about and the joke fails to land. Kokone describes a quick, relatively high voice, nearly always smiling features and the impression of a science student, and allows for Busutake being a good person on the qualified terms of the games. Busutake supplies the cameras on Takami's crows and relays their footage to hired pursuers. When she sees Yūki at the place she assumed was Awahime's home, she spits her tea out, recognizes her mistake and gets an image to Shiro. Her concern about an uninvolved person coexists with her own participation and prior assumption that the job was proceeding as expected. She suspects the reason the feed and telephone fail, but the account shows Shiro attempting the recall and Busutake trying to make sense of the failed connections. The episode supplies a visible case of technological competence, complacent inference, correction and care about a newly recognized risk. V08 S18 P0149–0166, S20 P0245–0249/P0437–0478. ## When the relationship is useful to both sides

Just after a game, Busutake reports that a player who described herself as having a much smaller count appears to have cleared around eighty. She notices the discrepancy through actual play and asks. She tells Shiro how little weight a self-report would have without the skill she observed, offering the woman as a plausible replacement for Yūki. The narrative temporarily leaves the name unavailable. Airi is the candidate, and Busutake later identifies eighty-two as the total when they met. At the workshop she follows up while performing maintenance and asks why Airi has stayed in the games. Busutake's combination of curiosity and personal observation brings a durable answer from a person who has her own reason for cooperating and her own reservations about Shiro. Airi asks Busutake to relay her decision after a long delay. She later joins, but Airi's reasons remain her own, and she continues to distinguish her curiosity about the games from enjoyment. Busutake also distinguishes Shiro's sincere belief about the ninety-ninth game from confirmed rule knowledge, saying her own agent is unaware of the provision. V09 S21 P0241–0271, S26 P0072–0111, V10 S16 P0101–0155/P0281–0307.

During Phantom Thief, Busutake cooperates with Yūki as a fellow guard while knowing them to be enemies in another sense. She looks closely at how the map works, praises the tools put into the game and qualifies what she can infer about their design. She rapidly seizes Chiyo, obtains needed information and kills her after Yūki declines further questioning. To Busutake, the change can be read as a machine stopping. She then checks a disturbing curiosity while watching Yūki move away. Later she kills Mononobe and finds Yūki still breathing after a deliberate attack. She covers her to discourage further attention and wants the contest to end early so she can get to medical care. Her stated reasons include the chance of later reconciliation, the rarity of a player who could reach ninety-nine and the possibility of 「私の被験体」. The rescue is real. The language of a possible research subject belongs to her interior, and Yūki authorizes no such future work here. V09 S24 P0145–0183/P0191–0231/P0595–0626. ## What her own body seems to prove

Busutake says she chose to replace more than half of herself and wants to go further. She accepts limits at the brain and certain organs. Her account to Kuromatsu ends in cheerful disgust at biological flesh and a tentative label, 肉体恐怖症, with ruby サルコフオビア. Her use of ナマモノ and comparison to a familiar food aversion make the feeling communicable. The source gives a self-description, witnessed adaptations and a suggested cost. Kuromatsu has heard that Busutake has shortened her life to make particular injuries survivable and wants a longer life after gambling for enough money. Busutake seeks others who will share her choice, accepts Kuromatsu's refusal in the conversation and jokes about a possible defection to Yūki. Her reports of refusal and disappointment do not establish that she can offer everyone the same consequences. V10 S21 P0058–0078. [Busutake's proposed alternatives and their limits](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e016--a-second-custodian-for-a-damaged-body).

In game her familiarity can shape the harm she chooses. Early on the ship she cheerfully takes equipment from the immobilized Takeda and decides to select someone else for questioning because she dislikes the idea of an acquaintance being tormented. Takeda inwardly hopes to be selected as a captive and cannot get the words out. Busutake later counts Takeda and Umesada as dead. The depicted decision is consequential and the scene leaves Takeda's exact final act outside view. Later Busutake sees an opportunity to anticipate Yūki's path through the failing ship, follows and finds herself in Yūki's sights. A staff viewpoint reports the shooting in language that says Yūki killed her. Takami sees the apparent body and qualifies that impression. At the hospital Setsuna sees Busutake alive with a dressed head wound, speaking to her father. This overturns that provisional death, and nothing in the scene establishes all she can survive or how the specific survival worked. V10 S19 P0269–0289, S21 P0069–0078, S23 P0219–0248/P0304–0317/P0463–0474, S26 P0046–0058.

Her comparatively warm field and workshop speech carries 「ですな」, lengthened endings and the technical politeness of 「人差し指お願いします」 while doing actual work. She calls Yūki 「幽鬼氏」 in analytical, recruitment and later clinical settings yet offers the injured woman a conventional 「幽鬼さん」 in direct encouragement. She answers Takami's instructions with 「はいな」. Calling her father 「お父さん」 persists as the familiar greeting becomes an openly challenging exchange. At the hospital the relaxed 「や」 becomes 「お父さんの技術じゃ、今の彼女は助けられない」. She says Yūki must be restored as a fighter and that her own 「刹那的な力」 is required for this. The exact meaning of that power remains uncertain. She says she will take charge of Yūki and signals her companions, who attack. This goes beyond independently tending Airi or keeping an option alive for Yūki and confronts other carers in Yūki's absence. No treatment, change of custody, source of the group's allegiance or consent is shown. V08 S20 P0461–0478, V09 S24 P0621–0626/S26 P0054–0060, V10 S19 P0235–0242 and S26 P0046–0071.

Busutake typically asks what a material system can do, and she is inclined to notice interesting capabilities before agreeing with a familiar person about what has happened. She can be reassuring when a client's request sets the task and give meaningful assistance to an opponent when their survival serves ends she values.
