# Airi’s former assigned agent

Airi's first assigned agent is an unnamed woman who has moved into the organizers' game execution division by the time she appears directly in V10. Airi is then working with a second agent. The former agent still calls her independently and later meets her while employed aboard the cruise ship used for Jamboree Ship. She likes getting close to the work and wants to share that enthusiasm with someone she knows from her old work. The conversation that shows their familiarity also gives her a chance to limit what she actually shares. This portrait covers the admitted Japanese novels through the V10 ending. V09 S26 P0017–0027, V10 S16 P0307–0336 and S19 P0066–0088.

## Calling a former charge

When Airi has completed her eighty-fifth game, she has had her then current agent only for a short time and understands that her predecessor probably transferred into game management and execution. The second agent's joking suggestion that Airi try for a hundred and the slight embarrassment that follows belong to this newer relationship. The former agent becomes directly visible after Airi reaches ninety. A caller says 「お久しぶりです！　藍里さん」. The excitement is recognizable from the writing, though Airi takes a moment to identify the familiar voice. The woman asks after her and says helping Airi was stimulating, while her present work is even more exciting. Airi gives a specific and newly reached clear count and reciprocates by asking about the transfer. The scene shows continuing personal interest in both directions without revealing how well they got along during a particular earlier assignment. V09 S26 P0017–0027, V10 S16 P0307–0318.

The line breaks into noise when the agent starts describing a project. The drop and restoration are directly narrated. The woman attributes it to 「検閲」 inside the execution division and warns Airi that even an outsider on the call could be affected. She tells of an employee who disappeared from the project after passing on restricted information and says the recipient seems to have been dealt with as well. She gives no names, and the threatened archivist in the neighboring section and the reported punished person remain separately evidenced. The account shows that the agent expects colleagues to have reasons to fear leaks. It gives no independent proof of the punishment or of the exact way the interruption works. V10 S16 P0243–0277/P0318–0326. [The interrupted conversation](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e003--an-archive-out-of-view).

Airi's objection is that she should avoid a call that endangers either person. The former agent says 「だって、話したかったんですもの」 and then checks whether Airi actually has time. Airi agrees. The former agent goes on about the enthusiasm of the team and how seeing the whole game has given her a broader view of the organization, without giving the particulars that triggered the interruption. She thus accepts the risk of contact while adapting the substance of the conversation. Airi keeps thinking of her as エージェント because the generic 職員 feels distant. Familiarity survives a role change without restoring the earlier employment tie. Airi hears the pleasure clearly and cannot share it. She feels left behind by people who have found something to be excited about, and the agent's talk contributes to that reaction whether or not the agent realizes it. V10 S16 P0321–0336.

## In the lounge

Airi next finds her across the bar aboard the cruise ship. The former agent identifies the place as the project from the call and says her primary responsibility was setting up the stage. She has bartending experience and was pressed into that role. She speaks with pride about the actual work and regrets that she cannot answer Airi's questions about how the organization secured and deployed a ship. The invitation 「未成年にも提供しますよ」 is mildly provocative in a bar setting and shows that this employee is comfortable signaling a permissive boundary. Airi drinks oolong tea. The scene contains no actual service of alcohol to a minor. The next day Airi sees the empty bar and hears that staff may have left by an obscured connection, whose traffic the watcher did not see. Her former agent's exact duties and whereabouts after the bar scene remain open. V10 S19 P0066–0088/P0198–0209. [The stage and its staff](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e006--the-invitation-nobody-could-match).

Her outward address remains 「藍里さん」 across the two work settings. She combines polite endings with colloquial compression such as 「働いちゃったみたいですね」 when she explains the interruption. The private-sounding 「ですもの」 accompanies her insistence on personal contact, and 「話せないのが惜しいなあ」 brings friendly regret to the actual service encounter. The narrative describes laughter and a jaunty greeting. The novel supplies written register and descriptions of delivery, with no performed voice for comparison. Prior bar work is self-reported and the observed role is actual. The source leaves her name, age, home life, outside preferences and romantic or intimate life undisclosed. V10 S16 P0309–0332 and S19 P0068–0086.

In the two encountered situations the former agent starts from a real attraction to technically demanding work and chooses Airi as someone she can tell. When secrecy becomes salient, she gives a frank account of the risk and continues in a form she regards as permissible. When Airi encounters the project in person, the agent can confirm her role but still withholds details. This conditional candor can coexist with personal consideration and a willingness to expose a former charge to risk. One plausible reading is that she mainly wanted an enthusiastic listener, while another takes seriously the opening inquiry about how Airi is doing. Her actions support both an independent pleasure in work and a maintained attachment. They do not show how much danger she understood before she dialed, how she would choose in a situation of actual sanctions, or what she expected Airi to feel. V10 S16 P0307–0336 and S19 P0066–0088.
