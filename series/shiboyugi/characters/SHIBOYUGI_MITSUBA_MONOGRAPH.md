# Mitsuba

Mitsuba can spend an afternoon in beautiful water, object to treating a whole life as a contest and still take a physical test of another person much further than that person can bear. She joined a teacher to improve her odds of living. She left rather than make the teacher's resentment her own, yet misses something about the pupils who could devote themselves to it. The novel gives her recreational ease, invasive curiosity and a real wish for something that would hold her attention. None of these suffices to explain the others. V03 S17 P0309–0348/P0372–0420/P0435–0449.

## Her own thirtieth game

蜜羽, read ミツバ, introduces herself as a player at her own thirtieth, in the game that Yūki plays as her forty-fourth. By the time the others look at her, she has already got a bottle of ramune and warmed a portion of takoyaki in the cottage. She makes them wait, finishes both, introduces herself and puts a second portion in the microwave. In the time it takes to warm she raises a practical question about the game's apparent lack of an objective. The situation supports self-directed enjoyment in public and a willingness to make use of what is at hand while others observe her. Her plain 「よろしくです」 keeps the social obligation small. Near the beach she makes use of a float and its view of the water. The placed picture emphasizes her horizontally floating figure and Yūki's other position. The image allows a reader to see the ease with which she uses the setting without knowing what she privately wants out of the game. V03 S15 P0159–0181, S16-I01/S05-I01, S17 P0309–0334. [The group on arrival](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e003--a-boundary-discovered-by-the-participants), [What the placed plate shows](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e013--where-the-plates-put-the-reader).

She likes the water enough to keep taking time there in an emergency. She figures she should play while she can, because injury or death would stop her, then makes a literal joke about salt in a wound. She refuses Yūki's offered formula about enjoyment being a victory. What bothers her is the assumption that a life can be sorted into wins and losses, even by someone trying to praise enjoyment. In private after a long afternoon nap she is awake in an illuminated cottage, getting bored of the ramune and remembering the daily tasks in a phone game she currently favors. The ordinary engagement is real. So is the ease with which a pleasant thing falls out of her attention by the next day. A currently favored game, a food choice and her present use of the beach establish concrete pleasures with distinct durations. They do not establish a lifelong lack of interests or a single diet and schedule. Her civilian name, exact age, family or education, and non-game work are not given. V03 S17 P0313–0335/P0437–0448. [The familiar pleasures and their limits](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e005--more-than-one-succession).

## An apprenticeship she could leave

About a year earlier by her own estimate, when she was new to the games, Mitsuba met 御城, Mishiro. She says Mishiro was looking for recruits empty enough to do what they were told. Mitsuba calls herself empty and declines the other adjective. She wanted to stay alive and saw a teacher as a way to learn how. The recruitment thus establishes an offer and a recipient with her own reason for accepting, independently of the recruiter's possible ulterior aim. The text gives the teacher's habitual focus on Yūki and the shared conditional direction to defeat her through Mitsuba's firsthand report. V02 independently shows what one follower, Riko, did with her own instruction. It gives no reason to assume the same unconditional motive or exactly the same wording for Mitsuba. The remembered 「打ち負かせ」 is defeat. Riko's enacted attack and Mitsuba's decision to leave are distinct responses. V02 S16 P1024–1034/P1115–1116, V03 S17 P0372–0420/P0439–0448. [More than one succession](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e005--more-than-one-succession).

Mitsuba still calls Mishiro her teacher in her own thoughts, recalls what she was taught indirectly through so many descriptions of Yūki, and considers that obsessive estimation badly inflated. The fact that she left sets a limit to Mishiro's practical control. The wish to find an absorbing commitment outlasts it. She privately sees something lively in Mishiro's followers, including Riko, and envies their capacity to give themselves to a person and purpose she considers foolish. She criticizes herself more harshly than the devoted pupils. Her public account to Yūki gives an inability to empathize with a grievance enough to participate in it. Her private account makes the same independence feel like an inability to become bound even when she wants to be. That is an articulated contradiction, and neither claim by itself describes her actual morality or every relationship she might have been capable of. The evidence gives no romantic relationship, attraction or intimate preference. Her comparative attachment to teacher and pupils concerns loyalty and conviction. V03 S17 P0406–0418/P0437–0449. [The pupil who left](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e005--more-than-one-succession).

Mitsuba thinks of Mishiro and of all the other pupils as dead. The thoughts follow her memory of seeing or hearing from them in the past. No witness chain or exact last moment underlies the comprehensive outcome in her account. The much later disclosure to readers is of what an insider then believed. It does not supply new witnessed events for the endpoint of Mishiro's own V02 action or show when any report reached Mitsuba. V03 also gives Yūki's use of Riko's name in remembering the earlier event, without showing how she acquired it. Those knowledge histories matter because a reader who already knows the names can easily overestimate what two people on their first encounter know about each other's sources. V02 S16 P1024–1034/P1115–1116, V03 S17 P0380–0408/P0439–0449. [Yūki, Mishiro and the two pupils](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e005--more-than-one-succession).

## What the test does to another person

Mitsuba offers Yūki both information and a challenge. During the discussion of the apparent body she first draws a plausible conclusion about two players and accepts Yūki's correction about time available to move it. She jokes that Mozuku's written name suits Cloudy Beach. In the same scene it is possible to see how much weight she chooses to give a playful association and how readily she follows a firmer constraint. Her knowledge of Mishiro's account then gives her a separate interest in what Yūki is capable of. She says they have never met directly, invites her close with a confidence and lets Mishiro's name do the work of surprise. While holding Yūki in the water she explains why she joined and why she will not pursue the old instruction. Yūki perceives no killing intent and still knows she could be killed. Mitsuba physically prevails, releases her voluntarily and concludes that the teacher exaggerated. No controlled test of either person's full capability has taken place. It is a real test of surprise and position and of Mitsuba's willingness to subject a new acquaintance to serious risk to satisfy her curiosity. V03 S17 P0336–0365/P0372–0420/P0439–0441. [Concealed motives and the test of Yūki](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e005--more-than-one-succession).

Yūki later tells the group about their conversation while withholding the near-drowning because it could generate a misleading suspicion and because she resents being beaten. Finding the dead Mitsuba produces an independent feeling of loss. Yūki privately misses the conduct that irritated or hurt her and does not give the group this reaction either. Mitsuba elicited interest, indignation and real affection in someone whose reputation she wanted to test. There is no evidence that she understood the last feeling. Their tie does not need to have been a friendship or a conscious rivalry from both sides to have this effect. V03 S19 P0017–0044/P0088–0105. [The first real body and its reception](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e007--the-hunter-the-first-body-and-the-altered-archive).

## Agency and limits at the wall

Mitsuba has continued to accept invitations through her agent and privately hopes the thirtieth will present something she cannot simply leave. She does not panic when an unknown person comes into her room. She has already removed the planted devices that she thinks would otherwise control her. Her preparation lets her gain the upper hand. The hidden face remains a question she chooses to answer. On discovering it is Etsusei she freezes briefly and is struck. The discovery of her body and Etsusei's description of an initial strong target establish the death, the reason the assigned killer selected her and the actual opportunity her surprise afforded. Mitsuba is the first actual victim. Her performance supplies straightforward evidence of detection, self-defense and motor control against an opponent who expected surprise to favor her. It also shows one way in which a mismatched model of whom she could meet can interrupt practiced competence. Her wish to find an inescapable demand does not establish a wish to die or a refusal to defend herself. V03 S17 P0435–0492, S19 P0017–0044, S21 P0014–0018. [Etsusei's own account](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e007--the-hunter-the-first-body-and-the-altered-archive).

## Speech and a conditional profile

With strangers Mitsuba's 「ゲームは三十回目です。よろしくです」 combines a polite announcement with clipped informality. She calls Yūki 「幽鬼さん」 and uses 「お耳、貸してください」 to make a confidential disclosure seem socially safe. The respectful request is the means of getting close. The same easy polite endings continue in 「心配しなくても、殺すつもりはありませんよ」 while she physically constrains her audience. “Carefree” can describe the manner that Yūki hears without describing the risk her body is in. Later 「お顔拝見しちゃいましょうか」 keeps a playful honorific turn for an intruder she thinks she has defeated. 「……え？」 loses the stance when she recognizes the face. The first-person 私 also carries different self-representations through speech and interior. She says she is not suited to another person's vengeance, and thinks much harder things about how little can hold her. The novel provides these linguistic choices and reactions from people in the scenes. It gives no acoustic or uniformly repeated voice pattern in other relationships. V03 S15 P0159–0167, S17 P0313–0327/P0380–0418/P0439–0448/P0477–0492.

In a dangerous setting with accessible pleasures and no immediate demand, Mitsuba is likely to find something she can enjoy and set its end herself. A concrete problem can draw practical attention at the same time. If a person's reputation has dominated a relationship important to her, she may seek firsthand examination and treat the cost to the other person less seriously than the opportunity. If handed a belief or grievance as an obligation, she is more likely to decide afresh whether it interests her, especially once organizational membership can be left. She is capable of planning and surprise rather than an undifferentiated appetite for excitement. Her own vulnerability to a consequential surprise and the unresolved strength of any long-term commitment constrain how far those predictions go. A continuing relationship she chose and sustained would be more informative about the latter than another brief display of enjoyment or tactical ease. V03 S15 P0159–0181, S17 P0309–0365/P0372–0420/P0435–0492, S21 P0014–0018.
