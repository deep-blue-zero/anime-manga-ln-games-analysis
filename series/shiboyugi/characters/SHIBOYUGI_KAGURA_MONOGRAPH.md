# Kagura

花楽彩芽 is Kagura Ayame, with 花楽=かぐら, 彩=あや and 芽=め. She plays as 神楽, also read Kagura. The admitted Japanese portraits print Ayame Kagura for her civilian identity and Kagura for her playing identity. She starts as an irregular worker with a fondness for manga and spending, and enters the games through an employer who withholds their nature. Friendship with Sana gives her a purpose beyond the next payout. By the end she has succeeded in doing something for the friend and still cannot answer what to do with her own life. The Japanese V01–V10 corpus contains her direct appearances in V09. Her exact career tally, age and later formal status remain open. V09 S06-I01/S09-I01, S21 P0105–0137/P0275–0407 and S26 P0236–0257.

## Accepting an offer and coming back on her own

Kagura has been working at Kotono Shiori's secondhand bookshop when Kotono offers her lucrative work. She knows Kotono as a shopkeeper, and has yet to learn that she was a player called Kotoha, lost both legs and earns a commission for introducing recruits. The source lets a player later choose the job she would not have understood at the first invitation. Kagura clears Abandoned Condo in a combination of terror and fortunate circumstances. Back at the shop with around three million yen, she says 「ありがとうございます。私を紹介してくれて」. She remembers the weight of the prize and pleasure at achieving something taking the place of fear, together with unease at her own readiness to feel pleased. She spends the money in weeks and goes back to Kotono for another introduction. The eventual decision to continue is hers, while the first risk was concealed from her. V02 S12 P0924–0970 for Kotoha's history, V09 S21 P0275–0311. [The novice's actual path into the games](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e006--what-training-gives-kagura-and-what-it-cannot-settle).

The publication places this account just after Busutake reports a veteran of around eighty clears and Shiro asks for a name. That veteran eventually proves to be Airi. The cut to Kagura is a misleading invitation to connect two accounts. The visible history actually establishes Kagura's earliest games and later ability without giving her a comparable tally or a role in Shiro's recruitment. The same attention to timing helps with Kotono's bookshop. Kagura says she has done no ordinary work since starting the games, and the prose shows her using the shop as a meeting place. An admitted civilian caption still gives the part-time affiliation. The two can reflect different periods, whose exact relation is unavailable. Training becomes part of her later life without a settled ordinary occupation. V09 S06-I01/S09-I01, S21 P0105–0137/P0241–0286, S24 P0239–0249 and S26 P0072–0111. [The appearance that changes referent](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e013--the-auspices-of-waking).

Manga is the only kind of book Kagura reads. She likes the shape of a smart watch and impulsively buys the highest-priced model, using less than half its functions. She also takes up the offer of a free drink when Kotono wants company after a bad customer encounter. The idleness the narrator attributes to her belongs alongside these lived choices. She tells Sana that normal paid work looks pointless against a single game's reward and that she has no vivid sense of why dying should matter to her. She feels embarrassed next to someone who must play to survive. Comparisons can be painful in other settings as well. Advertising images of celebrities make her feel she is being asked how much work she puts into a life. When she actually sees the veteran Yūki, she feels the same kind of resentment and refuses to become someone else's incidental casualty. The street posters are an analogy from her ordinary life. The sources give few details of family or intimate life. Sana's objection when Kagura calls her wish to be scattered at sea a 性癖 is familiar joking. Kagura privately mentions that books could reveal her tastes after death, but the contents of those tastes are unspecified. V09 S21 P0105–0109/P0362–0368, S24 P0239–0252/P0326–0340/P0420–0433/P0496–0509.

## Sana and a reason to spend differently

By Kagura's third game her own agent has taken over invitations. Sana saves her near the end of Undead Mall. Kagura repays her with two nights at an amusement park and discovers the conditions Sana lives under. An escort follows them and Sana explains the bargain she made for treatment. They continue to see one another away from play. Sana challenges her casualness at lunch, then helps her learn to keep herself alive. They speak with the familiarity of people who can disagree and go on helping each other. When Sana says she wants her ashes scattered at sea so she can travel the world after death, Kagura teases her and readily offers to do it. The exchange occurs by the mock harbor on the third day of their amusement-park trip. The reader receives it as a memory when Kagura thinks her later encounter with Yūki is lost. The original ease of the promise gives the later financial decision an object beyond trying to sustain the career. V09 S21 P0308–0317, S24 P0239–0269/P0496–0514. [The relationship behind the planned return](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e010--what-the-dress-carried-through-the-fight).

Kagura arrives at the shop expecting to meet Sana. Kotono tells her Sana will never come and puts her in contact with Sana's agent. Kagura hears that the organization is likely to process and sell the body. She feels shame that a wasteful person should outlive someone with powerful reasons to live. Looking at the expensive watch while thinking about her spending habits helps bring the other possible buyer to mind. She asks whether players can bid and decides to enter more games to raise enough. The agent asks whether Sana would wish for that. Kagura admits to herself she probably would not, and wonders if this is another impulsive purchase. Her own words, 「友人なんです」, come out with a force that surprises her. She insists that she wants to give Sana a proper farewell, and the agent agrees to help open a route to the sale. Kotono also notices that Kagura has never seemed like someone to form and pursue such a determinate wish. The feeling can coexist with guilt and a still imperfect grasp of cost. V09 S21 P0105–0137/P0323–0407.

## The things luck and training give her

Kagura counts six entries in about a month and a half as she seeks to fund her bid. Phantom Thief is the last depicted opportunity before the auction. On the way in she makes a definite choice to work alone, despite a coherent suggestion from someone with a nearby target. The proposal assumes a distribution of guards that the thieves cannot confirm. Kagura's more immediate reason is her own impression. She attributes that kind of choice to her intuition and says she cannot reason things through well. In play she quickly dispatches opponents at first contact, identifies the possibility that the guards know more than her side expects and still fails against the defenders of her intended brooch. Training from Sana has a real effect. It does not guarantee the success of Kagura's plan or establish what taking the offered partner would have changed. V09 S24 P0110–0137/P0239–0284/P0344–0352. [What practice does and what chance changes](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e006--what-training-gives-kagura-and-what-it-cannot-settle).

At the staircase another thief, Kudan, throws her a dress and is killed. Kagura takes the gift and sees correctly that it is an unlikely opportunity. She cannot know everything the reader soon learns about Kudan's initial success, a weapon failing to fire and the deliberate disposition of the dress. She then has to carry it past Yūki. Kagura exploits the defender's concern that damaging an exhibit can carry a penalty. She recovers from a serious wound, uses the dress to conceal what she is doing and presses the attack. Yūki notices real signs of training and quick perception. At the end she gains a timing advantage, but her impaired left hand cannot fire, and Kagura's shot incapacitates her. The separate perspectives distinguish the earlier nonfire during Kudan's pursuit from the failure of Yūki's hand in the later duel. Kagura gets out with the damaged dress and the organization confirms a clear. Victory requires Kudan's gift, acquired skill, usable chances and errors on both sides. The clearing announcement verifies this damaged garment as acceptable in this case. V09 S24 P0359–0416/P0420–0491/P0518–0547/P0630–0652. [The dress changes hands](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e010--what-the-dress-carried-through-the-fight).

## The price of keeping the promise

Kagura judges in advance that her money should exceed an ordinary price. She still has to get herself into the auction and needs to borrow a computer at the last moment, since she has not tested access on her phone early enough. During the sale she stops when another buyer tops her bid, then commits far above the amount she can pay on her own. She intends to play again to cover the shortfall despite the chest wound she is still recovering from. Kotono decides to lend her the difference out of recruiting commissions, expressly on repayment terms. This answers one danger created by their earlier relation. Kotono pays further costs the bidder failed to budget and asks to accompany her. After receiving the body, Kagura uses the available funeral arrangements, attends with Kotono and Sana's agent and travels with Kotono to scatter the ashes at sea. She carries out the promise instead of keeping the body or turning back from the expense. V09 S26 P0115–0156/P0172–0247. [What Kotono pays for and why it matters to Kagura](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e007--what-kotono-owes-the-people-she-recruits).

When Kotono asks what comes next, Kagura says she intends to quit and must make her wait for repayment. She will look for ordinary work. These are new aims whose formal or financial completion the source does not show. The last interior question, 「私はこれから、どうすればいいのだ？」, belongs to a person who feels unsettled by her own survival in a way she could not have explained during her first rush of pleasure. Her previously relaxed 「っすね」 with Kotono and casual banter with Sana contrast with the earnest, conventional politeness she uses to commit herself to mourning in the conversation with the agent. The Japanese allows comparison of written register and of what Kagura says and thinks. Performance remains outside this witness. V09 S21 P0379–0407, S24 P0496–0514, S26 P0207–0211/P0244–0257.

Kagura is receptive to tangible opportunities and uses initial impressions when she has little time to choose. In an arena she can carry out rehearsed actions despite calling herself lucky. A specific debt to a friend can turn the person who spent the first prize in weeks into someone who trains, saves and endures risk for one purpose. An urgent last step can also bring back weak preparation or willingness to spend above her means. She accepts help if it makes the promise possible, even though she rejected tactical help from a stranger in the gallery. Expecting stable self-sacrifice, permanent imprudence or an already settled life outside play would exceed the comparisons the novel supplies.
