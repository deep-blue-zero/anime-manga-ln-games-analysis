# Maya

摩耶, with ruby マ／ヤ, is a resident in Shiro's shared house and plays in the organized lethal games. Shiro brings her into Mikkai to see how a novice develops when given the group's training. By Mossygrove she is recognizably dangerous, and by Jamboree Ship she knows enough to take responsibility for a team. She also wants Shiro's affection to be hers alone. She can resent an order, carry it out, and then knowingly give an order of her own when leaving the choice to individual pride would damage the group. Those are different responses to people and stakes she can name. Her civil name, age and earlier home life are unknown. This portrait uses the material admitted through the Japanese V10 ending, including Maya's death during Jamboree Ship. V08 S16 P0083–0118, S18 P0414–0458/P0693–0729, V10 S19 P0226–0242 and S21 P0142–0157/P0215–0275.

## Inside Shiro's house

Maya runs out in a nightgown and hugs the nearest figure at the entrance, warmly saying 「おかえりなさい！　ダーリン！」. When Kokone explains who is in her arms, she lets go and repeats the greeting just as eagerly to Shiro. The term has a precise intended recipient. Maya openly wants company and tells Shiro 「やだあっ、一緒にいるんだもん」 when asked to wait. Away from the doorway she sometimes takes up different work in the relationship. After Shiro's attempt to enlist Yūki, Maya recognizes signs of disappointment behind an ordinary face, sits close and asks if it went badly. When Shiro comes home after being turned down by Maguma, she pulls Maya with her onto the sofa and embraces her. Maya strokes her head and says that a better way will come. The two scenes show recognized intimacy, some pleasure in one another and actual comfort. They leave the whole private relationship and its founding terms uncertain. V08 S16 P0083–0118/P0253–0267, V09 S21 P0065–0087. [The household and its different addresses](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V08_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v08-e002--domestic-division-of-access).

Around others, affection recedes audibly. Maya calls Kokone's reminder about dinner irritating, with 「うざ。お母さんかよ」, while taking strongly flavored chips and café au lait. Kokone has learned that a straightforward favor can get done through the brusqueness. Asked to summon the others, Maya remains silent and actually brings them in. She makes her taco hotter with extra salsa and drinks her café au lait with the meal. In the woods she throws off headgear she thinks makes her hair lie flat and takes chewing gum from a dead teammate. Those preferences show what she cares about on an uneventful day. The record gives little about her life beyond the household and games. In V09 she checks the boundary of the relationship the moment Takami borrows 「ダーリン」. 「ダーリンって呼ぶな」 is spoken to Takami, not a line Shiro has to say herself. V08 S18 P0126–0160/P0436–0453, V09 S21 P0222–0240.

## What a trial member learns

At Mossygrove the hunters' numerical assignment favors coordination against a larger elven side. Iidate asks the group to stick together and has already insisted on keeping helmets on. Maya shoots her side's de facto leader and sends away the hunters who try to follow. Her thought that she wants to do things her own way is borne out in the choice. The result also supplies an early correction. In her own review Maya calls the attack unnecessary and judges herself unusually irritable. She stops herself from another irritated gesture because she knows it will aggravate her state. The phrase 「しなければならない」 names what she hates in collective constraints and occurs again in what she believes about Shiro. Maya thinks Shiro's affection must be directed entirely toward her, and blames her heightened anger on what she reads as Shiro's interest in Yūki. She recognizes that protest could disrupt a plan Shiro values. This shifts the anger onto people sharing her field rather than forcing a conversation with Shiro. V08 S18 P0381–0408/P0414–0458/P0529–0543.

Actual skill develops alongside these costs. Maya learns of the elves' marker code, recognizes it as a Mikkai tool developed by Busutake, and uses its shared meanings to draw opponents to predictable places. Her own account credits her with nine elves before Keito. Iidate is a different death on her own side. Keito evades her attempt to claim a tenth; blood, the unchanged count and the field response give different kinds of evidence for survival. Maya also leaves resources Yūki later uses to approach unseen. Yūki experiences real danger, sees the newcomer's errors, and comes away thinking Maya strong with conspicuous unevenness. Shiro afterwards calls Maya a 「サンプル」 and cites this as her fourth game and a success of a few months of instruction. Shiro is making a recruitment case for Mikkai and Maya herself says later that she was initially a test subject rather than a regular member. Actual skill transfer is demonstrable here. The exact fraction to credit to training, Maya's improvisation or the match circumstances is open. V08 S18 P0414–0459/P0501–0559/P0590–0616/P0693–0729, V10 S21 P0142–0152. [Maya's result and Shiro's use of it](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V08_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v08-e006--mayas-result-and-shiros-product-claim).

Maya is excited when she recognizes Yūki, despite a serious wound. Yūki can be attacked under game conditions and Shiro need never know that Maya chose to pursue her on personal grounds. Maya speaks to Yūki in openly hostile, taunting terms. Her reliance on a larger group's eventual success survives her refusal to act as part of it. The quota reaches twenty, an announcement marks the end and Yūki's weapon does not fire when she is trying to shoot Maya. Yūki's inference of an automated lock remains distinct from the enacted finish. Maya shows Yūki the count with a sardonic 「終わりだってよ」 and staff take her out alive. V09 shows her recovered enough to greet Shiro and live at home, without disclosing the course of medical recovery or a precise new game tally. V08 S18 P0598–0686, V09 S21 P0065–0087.

## Affection on terms she accepts

On the ship Maya thinks Shiro is losing interest once the training trial has served its purpose. She has taken part in Mikkai while privately preferring to be alone with Shiro. V10 adds that she chose an attachment she believes is conditional. 「彼女との愛は、取引なのだ」 is her understanding of the relationship, and 「承知の上で」 qualifies how consciously she remains. She finds professed unconditional giving suspect because it can become a demand for a much greater return. The internal snippets of promise allow a history of experience or a generalization based on what she has learned; no earlier partner or event is identified. She is attracted to what she considers Shiro's cold clarity and still frightened of finding herself disposable like Awahime. The wish to preserve intimacy and the wish to be of use become reasons to fight together. By her own reckoning the motive at Mossygrove had been different. There an apparent romantic rival could be privately removed. Here she hopes defeating Yūki will make Shiro turn toward her again. Neither motive settles how Shiro valued the actual person. V08 S18 P0537–0543/P0622–0625, V10 S21 P0142–0157.

Maya is again quick to oppose authority. She tells Takami 「命令すんな」 and goes with her to help anyway. She challenges Maguma's presence when she already knows the other player rejected Mikkai. Later, in the engine space, she pushes a weapon exchange that exposes a teammate at a vulnerable moment and derides how the woman handled it. Maya also reasons about the room, competing shots and Yūki's use of the unfamiliar space, and recognizes that the scattered survivors are being worn down. When a messenger has gone for help, Maya would still like to defeat Yūki herself. She instead says 「じゃ、出口固めるぞ」 and sets the group to await reinforcements. Before the plan can be established Yūki interrupts it and kills Maya. This choice at the exit defeats a portrait built wholly out of rashness or contempt for coordination. V10 S19 P0226–0242/P0402–0413, S21 P0161–0207/P0208–0275. [The last search and its failed turn](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e010--a-city-with-a-machinery-room).

Dying, Maya sees Yūki's gaze as brutally hopeful and starts an unfinished comparison, 「これは、まるで」. Its last referent is withheld. Yūki wonders afterward whether Maya was acting for Shiro, and uses her body to intimidate the larger side. Shiro visibly strikes a duct and promises revenge. Takami considers the anger a practiced display and pities Maya, expressly comparing Shiro's emotional habits to her own. Shiro's earlier reciprocated comfort and her later unexplained anger in a separate confrontation make a simple verdict about all of her feeling untenable. Those scenes still cannot tell the reader exactly what Shiro felt at Maya's body. The record confirms Maya's death, her vulnerability in a relationship she knowingly chose, and the difference between the anger an observer sees and the attachment Maya experienced. V10 S21 P0253–0275/P0315–0382 and S23 P0511–0542. [The marked body and its interpreters](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e011--the-two-ways-to-read-the-marked-body).

Maya is likely to protect private initiative when an apparent superior gives a blanket instruction or when a rival seems to threaten a scarce affection. She can accept ordinary requests, follow a plan she objects to and exert restraint when she can see the shared tactical cost of proceeding alone. A favor done in silence, blunt protest, possessive address and a practical field command carry different evidence for that range. Interior blame and outward help can run together. The novel supplies conversational text and descriptions of its delivery, with performance beyond its boundary. Extrapolation about whom Maya would trust outside these people, how she became a recruit or whether she would leave a clearly broken bargain needs more evidence than this career supplies.
