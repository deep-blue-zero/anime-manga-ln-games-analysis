# Koyomi

Koyomi (古詠, コヨミ; SBY-P035) has outlasted the division in the profession that Candle Woods produced. She says Cloudy Beach is her twentieth appearance, and later says she is twenty-eight. Her career has crossed that historical divide without accumulating anything like her friend Hakushi’s tally. She avoids the earlier disaster by declining an invitation, helps a frightened group keep track of one another without sharing everything, and decides to stop after escaping Cloudy Beach. That pattern supports a distinctive kind of caution. The same woman who keeps Yūki at her threshold when there are no other witnesses will offer to put her in contact with an old friend, then take care to leave them alone. The scene, the people, and the likely consequences determine how much distance she wants. This portrait has spoilers through V10 of the original Japanese novels. Her active portrayal is in V03, with later uses of her example and status that do not give her new conduct.

## A career with the right to refuse

Koyomi speaks first for the other cottage group at introductions. Yūki hears a hoarse quality and reads an oddly elderly air in a youthful face. Koyomi names her own twentieth appearance, then focuses with curiosity on a person she has never met. She knows what Hakushi has said of Yūki, says “お前の〈お師匠〉からね,” and enjoys Yūki's reaction long enough for Airi to supply context. Airi says Koyomi was already playing before Candle Woods. Yūki infers that the information about her therefore comes from before the death she thinks she witnessed. The early conversation allows that inference without showing it to be what Koyomi means. The original V03 portrait, with the patterned jacket and the chosen line about the teacher, also makes the first effect of apparent familiarity visible. It does not date the conversation from which the familiarity comes. V03 S15 P0116–0151 and S05-I01. [V03, the gathering](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e003--a-boundary-discovered-by-the-participants) · [V03, the cover portraits](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e014--cover-composite-portraits-and-an-unsigned-handover-note)

When invited to Candle Woods, Koyomi says she sensed something unpleasant and decided to sit it out. She credits that “嗅覚” with getting her this far. Hakushi privately remembers the decision after comparing Koyomi's sense for danger with her own. To Hakushi it almost resembles seeing the future directly. The source supplies a meaningful correct refusal and the respect of a very successful player, no demonstration of literal precognition, and no log of other invitations she accepted or declined. Her tally, smaller than some younger players’ tallies, does not permit a reconstruction of how frequently she played through that whole period. When Yūki later asks her age she says “二十八だよ” and observes what that age means in their profession. Her willingness to call herself a grandmother or older woman in front of younger people plays with their expectations and a professional context in which she really is a veteran. It need not establish frailty or a literal chronological discrepancy. V03 S15 P0135–0146/S17 P0519–0527/S23 P0072–0077. ## Attention without full exposure

After Etsusei's apparent death, Maguma and Hizumi prefer to work alone. Koyomi sees no gain in requiring them to spend whole days with somebody who could be the offender, yet she sees what they lose if nobody regularly accounts for who is still alive. She proposes a morning gathering at her cottage with individual freedom for the rest of the day. When Maguma asks whether everything must be disclosed, Koyomi answers “黙っときたいことは黙っときなよ.” Both innocent and guilty people have reasons to withhold things. Openness can reduce suspicion, so silence is a real choice with a cost. The group can observe attendance without believing everything an attendee says. That arrangement preserves the option to cooperate and the right to protect some information. Koyomi’s formulation of an obligation belongs to an agreement among players. It has no force as an official rule of Cloudy Beach. Some players prefer less proximity because they are stronger alone. Koyomi can run a different balance without treating their strengths as errors. V03 S17 P0073–0109. [V03, the meeting](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e004--detecting-a-rule-without-seeing-it)

Koyomi is willing to accompany Airi to inspect the scene after insisting that Mitsuba make good the food already taken from the cottage. Her sarcasm about the supplies and use of “お前” tell Mitsuba she cannot treat another person's allotment as hospitality. Food is consequential where they cannot establish how long it will last. When Yūki later heats and offers food from the unclaimed supplies, Koyomi eats easily enough beside her. She does not share every other person's concern about coming close to the consumption of a former occupant’s provisions. Neither instance establishes a favored meal. Her participation in inspection shows readiness to do some work under uncertain circumstances. She can take a practical step and hold off on certainty about its explanation. She already allows for an outside offender before inspecting the room. She disputes Mitsuba's casual account of a simple assassination, given the apparent victim's experience. Once the body is found missing, she says the theory of an outside offender has revived. These are views formed from markedly incomplete access. Neither qualifies as identification of the actual culprit. V03 S17 P0066–0071/P0097–0098/P0113–0165/P0198–0246/P0261–0277. [V03, looking for what supports an explanation](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e004--detecting-a-rule-without-seeing-it)

Listening comes closest here to a documented ability. The cottage is adjacent to Etsusei’s, but the cottages are far enough apart for Koyomi to distinguish between “聞こえなかった” and “聞かなかった.” She did not listen through the hours she slept, so she cannot say the whole night was quiet. She qualifies the observation about the light because she was not deliberately tracking it. Later she says she sleeps lightly enough to notice a visitor and that wild camping or going without sleep is hard on her. Her actions at dawn match her willingness to protect the space in which she can use that faculty. She will talk with Yūki, yet has her wait by the door until someone else arrives. A trained caution can lower exposure without giving her better information than the source of a sound or its timing warrants. The historical refusal, her precisely limited report and the specific instruction at the door are instances of three things often attributed to the same faculty. They involve a bad expectation, an honest report, and a simple policy under uncertainty. V03 S17 P0143–0148/P0232–0242/P0503–0532. ## A route back to an old friend

On the first day Etsusei asks what Hakushi thinks of her. Koyomi says “悪い意味での天才,” then explains why the teacher applies it. Koyomi is herself curious about the other pupil, and says Hakushi expects something of Yūki. The look of distress and explicit question she gets from Etsusei take time to register. Once she notices it she offers comfort in the idea that a higher tally would produce higher expectations. That suggestion has little relation to what Etsusei actually wanted to hear, a limit of Koyomi's social appraisal. She sees enough familiarity to disclose one person's words but cannot control their weight for the listener. The reader gets the conversation through Etsusei late in V03. In represented history it precedes much of what Koyomi and Yūki later work out. V03 S21 P0364–0397. [V03, what Hakushi is said to expect](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e010--two-readings-of-a-teachers-practice)

Yūki asks about Hakushi after a gathering. Koyomi reacts to the proposal to speak alone with visible caution. Yūki accepts the company of Airi and Mozuku, so the request for particular knowledge goes ahead with witnesses. Koyomi can report “馬鹿弟子” and the less flattering detail behind it, joke about the question, and note that Etsusei asked the same thing. When Yūki reveals she thought Hakushi was dead, Koyomi says she drank with her a week earlier at her usual bar. She knew Hakushi had retired after Candle Woods. The degree of injury is news to her. Her information about current life and Yūki's specific physical observation from Candle Woods fit together. They simultaneously begin to see that Etsusei may have staged a death. Koyomi has made a genuine discovery by putting one source of knowledge alongside another. The delay in telling Yūki that Hakushi survives is explicable from her never having known Yūki doubted it. The earlier grin could equally contain a taste for teasing someone who knew none of what the old player knows. V03 S19 P0437–0512. [V03, the information they can combine](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e010--two-readings-of-a-teachers-practice)

Koyomi gives Yūki a place and time to see the teacher again. She arrives a few minutes after Yūki has left to let the two of them speak privately and admits it, laughing. Hakushi treats that as characteristic enough to have spotted the intention. The two women exchange the short greeting of familiars, and Koyomi can ask whether Yūki is still the “馬鹿弟子” and why the desired total is ninety-nine. Her questions are personal in a way that a demand to divulge information about an ongoing game would be dangerous. She can tease Hakushi and invite a more honest account of what she hopes to see, then tolerate a deflection when she asks what the number means. The direct interaction corroborates a longstanding ease and willingness to offer practical assistance. It does not establish Koyomi’s entire acquaintance with Hakushi or an intimate relationship in any more specific sense. The bar and the social drink she describes are genuine ordinary-life evidence. Her own current drink, favored food, employment away from the games, other hobbies and romantic life remain unknown. V03 S23 P0010–0015/P0035–0043/P0060–0093. ## The exact end to what is known

“もう、今回でやめにするよ,” Koyomi says when she and Hakushi are alone. It is her own decision, supported by knowing she escaped and noticing things she was unable to read. She says she had come back to play after a while and could not keep up with the level of the field. The devices and Etsusei both exceeded her understanding. She attributes escaping to luck. There is a relevant distinction between that appraisal and her earlier sure withdrawal from Candle Woods. In one case she judges before entry. In the other she has enough evidence of the limits of her performance only after she has escaped. The source establishes Cloudy Beach as her twentieth appearance, her own account that she got away, and her physically present bar conversation. It separately establishes her decision to retire. It does not give a checkable individual announcement of a twentieth clear or a formal retirement procedure. When Yūki advises an apprentice in V05, she uses Koyomi as the unusual exception to the idea that excessive wariness prevents enough experience from accumulating. In V06 she thinks of Koyomi as retired while considering a range of potential opponents. Those passages establish later judgments by Yūki, with no shown return by Koyomi or additional result. V03 S15 P0120–0124 and S23 P0060–0079. V05 S21 P0014–0017. V06 S20 P0864–0873. [V03, the decision to stop](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e011--the-answer-from-someone-at-the-bar) · [V05, later reflection on an exception](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V05_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v05-e006--inheritance-in-the-practice-rooms) · [V06](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V06_DEEP_READING.md)

Her written “私,” “わたしゃ,” “ねえ,” “かい,” and “お前” help explain the elderly familiarity the narrative comments on. She uses a directive before a stranger can enter too far, laughs with a peer, and puts careful qualification into apparently easy conversational forms. She can tease Yūki or Hakushi without restoring the public role of host or superior in every exchange. The reported roughness of her voice is an impression given in writing. There is no performance record here. Hakushi calls her “臆病者” in agreement with her judgment about stopping. That identifies a strength in a tradition where one's choices about when to take a risk matter more than a joke about bravery.

Given an uncertain invitation and freedom to decline, Koyomi is unusually ready to trust a bad feeling before other people compute the stakes. During an active collective danger, she is likely to support regular contact if it can be combined with distance and a plausible right to silence. She is capable of investigating a serious possibility together with others, asking after the evaluation of people she has met, and intervening to arrange a reunion when nobody faces the same danger. Her credible early hunch does not protect her from adopting false premises when she has incomplete evidence. She may even leave room for a temporarily useful mistake by somebody she trusts. The relative steadiness of those judgments is established in her portrayed situations. Starting over with her resources after retirement or facing a new institutional demand would be outside the surviving evidence.
