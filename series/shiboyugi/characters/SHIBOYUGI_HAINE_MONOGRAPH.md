# Haine

Haine's training as a household employee gives her a way to stay composed with visitors and a way to greet someone she intends to kill. Her strongest attachment grows out of a home where she and her sister can live without constantly playing for survival. An attack on that home sends her back to the work she had largely left. Her resolve to retaliate changes which dangers she will accept and which rules she will spend time learning. Even after she says to herself that she would give her life for the result, she tries to escape and preserve it. V04 S19 P0226–0244/P0547–0573, S21 P0178–0216/P0293–0357.

## The household that furnished an alternative

灰音, read ハイネ, is the older twin. 心音, Kokone, is her younger sister. Haine identifies the relation when Yūki cannot tell whom she has met at Halloween Night. The twins had left home before completing compulsory education. Haine expressly will not tell why, even if the person asking were Kirihara. Her private rejection of universal appeals to gratitude to parents is sustained and severe. It gives important evidence of what appeals to family obligation mean to her. It does not disclose the precipitating events, parental intentions or everything her sister thinks. The earlier support they can get as active players includes food and shelter. Both need those things and both are young enough that Haine sees little ordinary way to make it alone. She understands the games as the cost of relying on that provision. The remembered chronology does not give the twins' exact age then or current clear totals. V04 S21 P0121–0134/P0178–0190. [Household protection and how it ends](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e011--haine-acts-kokone-watches).

Former player Kirihara Kanami offers them jobs in her house. They can live and work together and largely leave the games. By Haine's account she has served Kirihara for years. Day-to-day competence is visible when the twins stand at the gate, distinguish what they can do for visitors from what requires the proprietor, explain the inaccessible front-door key and accompany the inquiry when calls get no answer. They first answer strangers in evenly alternating lines. They also separately confirm the identity of a body they know. Their similar appearance and training do not mean they have the same motives or even the same next action. Haine asked why Kirihara took them in and remembers a response about needing help. She hears the possible brush-off, considering modesty, utility and whim. Whatever Kirihara wanted, the offer stands in Haine's mind as the act of someone dearer than her original family. Her appreciation is compatible with still keeping an old secret from the person who sheltered her. Tattoo work can bring dangerous connections into the house, and Haine knows enough to imagine a killing arising from the past. She finds this particular silencing intolerable because the originating game was none of Kirihara's doing. V04 S19 P0226–0244/P0273–0292/P0405–0408, S21 P0178–0197. [The discovery and the separate account of motive](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e007--red-blood-death-and-affiliation-after-retirement).

## Acting with her sister and then without her

Both sisters want to join the investigation of Kirihara's killing and work with Keito. When Shion is nearly caught, the latter's agent invokes the protection attached to new participation. Haine takes the change as information she can use. She phones her own agent, asks if there is an immediate invitation and accepts the one on offer. Keito learns after working with them that both had been players. Haine's inference is based on two players accepting at very nearly the same time, not privileged knowledge of the lists. Kokone tries her own contact and gets no invitation. Haine taps her shoulder and says 「任せて」 followed by a promise to catch Shion. Kokone gives the short answer 「うん」 after an unspoken hesitation. Haine offers to bear the action the two of them had pursued together. She receives no expressed agreement that she should put all her own chances of survival behind it. Kokone later gets access as a spectator, out of a wish to know how both lives finish. The modes of involvement and the two kinds of relief they might have wanted stay distinct. V04 S19 P0336–0345/P0547–0573, S21 P0126–0148, S23 P0016–0029. [The point at which invitation changes what people can do](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e008--when-an-invitation-makes-someone-protectable).

Haine has not played for about half a year. In the new game she can discuss several plausible explanations of the rules and warns that the survival interpretation would make the contest hardest. She plainly marks that part as her opinion. While speaking to Yūki she identifies herself and the purpose of her arrival, tells her the likely name and appearance and asks what she has seen. She has heard through Keito of Yūki's victory over Kyara and recognizes the advantage of another capable person coming across Shion. She privately cannot tell whether she would feel glad or resent being preempted. She wants to act herself. Her ability to weigh information and speak usefully survives the decision to pay less attention to the requirements of staying in the game. She walks toward the encounter, passing out sweets and largely foregoing the usual effort to accumulate them. What she wants done matters more to her at that point than improving her own position. V04 S21 P0121–0174/P0193–0216. [The private priorities behind the return](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e011--haine-acts-kokone-watches).

## Pressed conduct and the difference between a wish and a result

Haine recognizes the exposed face and build of Shion even without seeing the identifying tattoos. Shion met her during the earlier action without seeing Haine's face. Haine understands and uses that uneven knowledge. She is nervous enough to tell herself to stay calm, then gives the common 「こんばんは」 with the practiced manner of a servant. She closes the social distance before Shion understands why she has been sought. Once she discloses her name and employment relation, she can strike, press and inflict a serious leg injury in a way the smaller opponent cannot readily contain. Her plan continuously depends on the time the game allows and her estimate of what another person will do. Shion survives the encounter. Chance disruption and the attentions of the costumed enforcer defeat Haine's advantage. V04 S21 P0201–0248/P0254–0363. [Haine confronts Shion](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e011--haine-acts-kokone-watches).

When remaining to finish becomes incompatible with escape, Haine actually decides to escape and try again. She underestimates the enforcer, then desperately seeks and offers one last sweet. Her desire for survival in that moment and earlier willingness to die securing her aim both have direct support. The new offer comes too late under the rule as it operates in the scene. The enforcer kills her. Haine's last assessment draws on the very difference between how seriously she thinks she has lived and how unfairly the odds have favored her target. It shows the personal meaning she assigns to the outcome. An earned right to live or actual discrimination by the game does not follow from it. Yūki later finds her dead and considers several possible sequences. Shion claims to have retaliated successfully. The reader has seen what Shion contributes, including resisting her, and what actually kills Haine. Kokone's later observation and gratitude for Shion's defeat have different knowledge and a distinct purpose. V04 S21 P0293–0357/P0735–0742/P0791–0798, S23 P0016–0029. [Haine's death and its later interpretations](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e011--haine-acts-kokone-watches).

## Register, ordinary scope and conditional appraisal

As a working attendant Haine uses わたくし and deference such as 「わたくしのエージェントに」 when telling Keito what she has done. To Yūki in the game she can still say 「落ち着いて聞いてください」 and 「またお会いできることを、祈っております」 before taking her own route. The unceremonious 「任せて」 addresses her sister rather than a guest or senior. To Shion, 「──こんばんは」 initially borrows the professional manner for concealment. Plain identification as 「灰音。桐原佳奈美の使用人だ」 now announces hostility and an allegiance. The formal outward ability and the underlying anger are both real. The inward 私 of her last judgment is also more direct than the greeting register. The Japanese print gives hesitation, remembered practice and a controlled enactment. It does not establish a consistent performed accent or pitch. Work at a household, willingness to preserve private boundaries, and the ways she and Kokone divide help are identifiable ordinary circumstances. Favorite foods, leisure, and romantic or intimate history are unestablished. V04 S19 P0233–0244/P0553–0573, S21 P0134–0164/P0201–0240/P0351–0353.

Where people come to the household with a practical request, Haine can cooperate politely within the role's limits and try to solve a problem without overriding control she understands someone else to reserve. If a person she regards as family is harmed and a feasible route to a perpetrator appears, she is willing to take the lead and reason about access, timing and what the opponent still does not see. The pursuit gives strong evidence of commitment and plausible but fallible inferences, not of disregard for every survival consideration. Pressure to honor an assumed parental obligation, to wait for a more capable ally or to accept loss without an explanation each engages a different relationship. The material can ground those distinctions without predicting what she would do under a trusted person's explicit refusal, after risk to a bystander is shown, or under an ordinary conflict outside the household. Evidence of choices with her sister earlier, a separate view of the household or a scene she chose to describe in calmer retrospect would narrow those limits. V04 S19 P0226–0244/P0547–0573, S21 P0121–0197/P0201–0357.
