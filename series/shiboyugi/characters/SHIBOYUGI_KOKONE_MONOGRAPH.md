# Kokone

Kokone offers people access as part of her work and as a way of acting on obligations she has chosen herself. She ushers Yūki toward an employer whose plan she privately hopes Yūki will refuse. She regards Yūki as a benefactor despite barely knowing her and uses a former employer’s contacts to offer her a route to useful instruction. The difference matters to her. An even greeting and competent assistance can carry an opinion, gratitude or doubt that a visitor has too little information to read. V04 S23 P0010–0049/P0065–0067, V08 S16 P0056–0078/P0210–0212/P0239–0248, S18 P0175–0194.

## The work that gave her and her sister a home

心音, read ココネ, is the younger twin of 灰音, Haine. No other civil name or exact age is established. The sisters left home before completing compulsory education, turned to the games as a way of securing food and shelter and were taken into Kirihara Kanami's house as employees. They were close to retirement while continuing to have industry contacts. These formative circumstances come chiefly through Haine's recollection. Haine refuses to give the reason for leaving and her striking inward rejection of parental gratitude belongs to her own account. The two shared the move, the trade and the material opportunity to stop playing, without proof that they gave identical meanings to each. V04 S19 P0226–0244/P0553–0573, S21 P0126–0129/P0178–0197. [The shared household and two separate choices](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e011--haine-acts-kokone-watches).

Kokone's training appears with her sister at the gate, where they can offer to hear an inquiry but cannot let the visitors through the main door. She makes a call herself, fails to reach Kirihara and helps investigate. On seeing her employer's body she acknowledges whom it is without speaking. Later, when a call intended for Kirihara comes from the former pupil Hitomi, Kokone recognizes her game-name 一巳, checks what the caller now remembers and supplies the death and the fact it was a murder in measured stages. She offers to tell the rest. This action takes place later than the discovery of the body in story time, although the reader encounters the telephone call first. At the earlier point Kokone knows household routines and has no evidence why Kirihara does not answer. At the later time she has seen the body and is able to distinguish what the caller wanted from the answer she has to give. The chronology gives no exact account of the scene in which Kokone first came to know Hitomi or every detail of what she subsequently told her. V04 S17 P0442–0469, S19 P0226–0244/P0273–0292. [Death, working knowledge and notification](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e007--red-blood-death-and-affiliation-after-retirement).

Both twins are willing to help pursue Shion outside the games. When Haine takes the possibility of an available invitation as a route to continuing the pursuit, Kokone calls her own agent. No invitation is available to her at that moment. Haine promises to act and Kokone has the visible air of someone with more to say before she answers with a single word. The brevity is actual. Whether she wanted to protest, caution or help plan remains unknown. She subsequently reaches the organizers through another capacity and sees the outcomes as a spectator. She had wanted to know what happened to her sister and Shion. This gives Kokone a course of action she undertakes separately from her sister and outside the combat, after the route she first tried is closed. It does not show a preference for spectatorship over intervention when both options are available. V04 S19 P0336–0345/P0547–0573, S23 P0016–0029. [The different routes available to the two twins](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e011--haine-acts-kokone-watches).

## Gratitude she turns into an offer

Kokone finds Yūki after the game and thanks her for Shion's defeat. Yūki corrects the frame. She had not been pursuing a punishment and had not delivered the final blow. Kokone maintains its importance to her. Yūki sees no easy relief in her face. The exchange joins grief, a reason to give thanks and a refusal to pretend that the two people acted with the same purpose. Kokone asks whether Yūki’s right eye is impaired and whether she means to continue. Whether it will deteriorate remains a separate question. Because she does, Kokone offers to reach a former player acquainted with Kirihara, who had continued to play with no sight. A later message makes the opening real and the encounter with Rinrin follows. Kokone brings knowledge of a person, a connection and an applicable question. She does not prescribe how Yūki should live with the impairment. Nor does later evidence that Rinrin chooses a coercive lesson give Kokone advance knowledge of that private plan or make her its coauthor. V04 S23 P0010–0049/P0065–0067, V05 S19 P0234–0241/P0264–0273/P0301–0328/P0390–0414. ## Service at Shiro's house

After Kirihara's death Kokone finds she has to make a place for herself again. She decides to establish herself as a domestic employee specializing in players and gets her first client, Shiro. When an old acquaintance comes to the house, Kokone serves tea and sweets and returns later to work in a kitchen suited to preparing dinner for several people. At the taco dinner she prepares part in advance and leaves ingredients for others to assemble according to their own taste. She has a view about how snacking fits a real meal and restrains her disagreement with Maya. Even faced with an unkind reply, she uses what a few months of actual work have taught her. Maya's manner toward most people is abrasive, yet a practical request to call the others may be effective. It is. Kokone waits and watches until Shiro invites her to take the spare chair, then takes a taco when prompted to eat. Ordinary skill here involves timing, observation and a way to accommodate variation. The meal does not prove a personal favorite dish or that she participates as an equal resident member of Mikkai. Her position at the table is specifically that of someone separately invited to sit. V08 S16 P0108–0117, S18 P0126–0194. [Domestic division of access](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V08_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v08-e002--domestic-division-of-access).

Shiro makes use of Kokone's existing tie to Yūki when asking the latter to come and hear an offer. Kokone guarantees against bad intent in the initial approach and says the purpose will be given on arrival. Yūki suspects recruitment by small concessions and sees why it is harder to refuse a person she recognizes. Her own interest in Shiro also plays a part. Later when Shiro requests an unspecified future favor, Yūki reads Kokone's slight bow as ambiguous and cannot ascertain whether she was briefed. The reader subsequently learns that Kokone was acting on an instruction and would have liked Yūki to refuse. Kokone thinks both women are formidable, sees traits they may share, and fears a collision affecting them and herself. She knows Shiro has something to gain and lacks the full meaning of the offer. She privately finds performance and opportunism in her employer without treating the latter as simply unfamiliar or withdrawing her labor. She is unsettled by Shiro's request to hand over her own contact. She follows through after Yūki agrees. Her opinion of the bid, her outward work and the subsequent practical use of her number remain independently evidenced. V08 S16 P0056–0078/P0182–0248, S18 P0163–0194. [The bid and the person asked to facilitate it](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V08_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v08-e001--the-invitation-offered-as-friendship).

## Where her access goes, and where the reader's stops

Awahime recalls Kokone talking about Yūki as an odd, unpretentious, skilled player still living in a particular deteriorating apartment house. In Awahime's account the conversation supplied the building name, which let her find it herself and try to make contact. This is evidence of an apparently casual disclosure with consequences. It does not by itself show that Kokone gave an exact flat number, knew of a later use of the location, identified the address to Shiro or helped commission the attack. When the harm is already under way Shiro asks to use Kokone's phone. Kokone checks whether Shiro means to ring Yūki. After the retort she stops asking, unlocks it and hands it over. Shiro makes the call. The narrative gives Kokone a place in one communication and an independently attributed source role in another, not one undifferentiated conspiracy. V08 S20 P0263–0284/P0441–0478/P0834–0871. [Information and the apartment](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V08_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v08-e009--four-views-of-the-same-address).

In V09 Yūki deliberately uses that same stored number when she wants Takami instead of Shiro. Kokone is surprised and checks which person is intended, then makes the connection. Later she retrieves the phone, attends to the entry when a member returns and continues to serve the house. Takami's actual observation and choices over what to do with Yūki's address are separate from the person who connected a call after the bird was detected. In V10 Kokone is cooking when introduced to Airi as the housekeeper and gives a slight bow without speaking. Airi interprets the quiet as avoiding saliva near the food. This remains a minor inference. The later route and the scene with Airi confirm stable civil work and an ability to be useful to more than one person. There is no evidence there of fieldwork, an enrolled #93 appearance, shared control over surveillance or a voice in who will claim the reward. V09 S21 P0166–0178/P0222–0244, V10 S16 P0118–0121. [Changed uses of the same contact](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e005--the-visitor-the-bird-and-the-room-selected-for-safety), [The later household introductions](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e004--what-they-ask-of-airi).

## Speech, relationships and a conditional profile

With acquaintances and clients Kokone often uses わたくし and formal directions of respect. 「深く感謝いたします」 is an actual thanks, where 「拝見しておりました」 with its emphasized verb gives Yūki notice that she watched. In 「それでも、わたくしにとっては重要なことです」 she registers disagreement without raising the social temperature. With Shiro's visitor, 「どうぞ、ゆるりとおくつろぎください」 provides hospitality while another conversation is withheld. 「まもなくお夕飯ですゆえ」 to Maya mildly reproves by invoking the practical household schedule. Later she treats giving Maya a task as the useful remedy, rather than pressing a point of culinary classification. The 「うん」 she finally gives her sister has little of the register she uses in employment. There is no need to decide from that single sound what she wanted to say. Her inner 「自分の雇い主」「自分の恩人」 in proximity reflects two real obligations. The Japanese print and narrated expressions give effects and restraint in specific settings. They do not furnish a performed voice. V04 S19 P0567–0573, S23 P0013–0049, V08 S16 P0108–0117/P0210–0212/P0239–0247, S18 P0136–0148/P0175–0189.

In a familiar practical setting Kokone is likely to observe, offer concrete help and use mild questions or an intermediary route when an instruction leaves her uncertain. When some duties or ties conflict, she may perform the request while withholding her own reservations. Those reservations may remain visible in hesitation or a check of what is intended. When given a meaningful opportunity to arrange help on her own, she sometimes pursues it decisively, as with Rinrin or the spectator access. The strongest comparative evidence is in domestic care and communication; one explanation of her silence will fail across those settings. It is uncertain what would make her refuse an employer outright, what circumstances could make her want to fight again, and whether she would continue to disclose a particular kind of location once the stakes were known. The books establish professional cooking and work, considered opinions about guests and dinner and reasons to respect the two principal women. They leave her favorite foods and leisure outside work, her independent account of the early home and her romantic or intimate life unestablished. V04 S19 P0547–0573, S23 P0013–0049, V08 S18 P0126–0194, S20 P0834–0871, V09 S21 P0166–0178.
