# Hiyori

At her school table Hiyori is close enough to her older twin to share a sentence and separate enough to argue for a different explanation. When their friend is injured after intervening on their behalf, both sisters offer practical care. A later scene reveals Hiyori, by herself, using money she has carefully saved to fund a retaliatory commission. The change in what she is willing to do is severe, and she feels it. No one in the earlier classroom scene can infer the full extent of it. V04 S17 P0068–0109/P0415–0438, V07 S21 P0082–0114, S25 P0010–0015.

## One person at a shared table

天野日和, Amano Hiyori, attends the evening division of a high school and is a classmate and longstanding friend of Hitomi. 天野風見, Amano Kazami, is her older identical twin. They present themselves similarly enough that even their rate of eating looks the same in one scene. Hitomi recognizes Hiyori facing her on the left and Kazami on the right. Their acquaintance goes back at least to an earlier school year, when their shared class first encountered Sorimachi Yūki. There is no specified age, home history, or evidence of a separate relationship of either sister to Yūki outside school at this point. The plate in V07 gives a visual route to the familiar company around walking home. The prose establishes how much of their speech, voice and trust is shared on any one occasion. V04 S17 P0068–0088/P0415–0438, V07 S19 P0160–0174, S05-I01. [Hitomi at the table](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e004--hitomi-and-the-knowledge-in-the-body), [Hitomi hears a difference](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e006--hitomi-hears-a-difference).

Hiyori starts 「あの女──」 and Kazami finishes 「──って？」 when Hitomi raises a question about the quiet student. Their guesses about the classmate belong to ordinary talk prompted by her appearance and demeanor. The lines for particular guesses are not individually attributed. Both sisters are taken aback when Hitomi describes searching the other girl's things and announces that she will follow her. The joint protest mixes curiosity with judgments about what is an acceptable means of finding something out. Later Hitomi checks with the pair about the classmate's apparent attendance, and they do provide what they believe is useful. Yūki has arranged a stand-in. They report an appearance without recognizing the substitution, and the text gives no access to a suspicion or further inquiry from Hiyori at that point. Choosing to answer a trusted friend is compatible with recoiling at the purpose or failing to know how she will act on the answer. V04 S17 P0073–0109/P0261–0263/P0285–0292. [The difference between attendance and identity](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e004--hitomi-and-the-knowledge-in-the-body).

The cafeteria argument about cola supports a more individual account of her reasoning. Kazami says a bottle's small quantity ought to be offset by an improved flavor. Hiyori says 「そんなわけないよ」 and asks what that would imply about two-liter bottles. She offers common manufacture and a change in perceived taste caused by a different container as a better explanation. She chooses a new comparative case to test the other person's inference and gives her own cause. The argument does not establish who would be right on a real blind tasting or a stable drink preference. Both call Hitomi into the debate and follow an aside about another kind of cola before moving onto ice cream and getting sick. After her own encounter with Yūki, Hitomi finds just this sort of ordinary company reassuring. Later Hitomi avoids walking home with them on mathematics and science test days because she expects them to show off. This is good evidence that ordinary friction and security coexist in the friendship, while which sister has what degree of interest or ability in science remains unmeasured. V04 S17 P0415–0438, V07 S19 P0160–0174. [Ordinary companionship and separate views](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e004--hitomi-and-the-knowledge-in-the-body).

## Care and a private act

In V07 Hitomi sees an ambiguous text from the twins and infers a request for help, then chooses to go in person. The sender is not assigned as either twin. Hitomi later says she drove away the people bothering them and that a larger group subsequently came after her. Yūki observes Hitomi's injuries and sees both twins trying to cover the wounds with bandages. The initial twin exchange sounds apologetic and solicitous. As Yūki approaches they take their own distance, and Hitomi looks toward them before deciding how to tell her account. The sisters receive protection and give care, while Hitomi chooses how much of her work to expose. Their presence does not mean that they know what she and Yūki are able to do or why. Their startle and withdrawal give more evidence about their actual comfort with this classmate than the guesses they made over lunch. V07 S19 P0204–0215, S21 P0082–0114. [The relief that each person can offer](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e006--hitomi-hears-a-difference).

Hiyori's later contribution is her own. She hears of the site, finds an existing request close to meeting its target and puts in the entire fund she has built up for a computer with a good GPU. Her stated reason is revenge on the organization that harmed her friend. Hitomi is the strongly supported friend from this run of scenes, though she is unnamed in Hiyori's interior. The saved money gives the decision a specific cost and an ordinary aspiration it interrupts. It leaves the intended uses of the computer open. Hiyori calls this the cruelest she has ever felt. The inner language of 天誅 makes retaliation something she wants to see imposed, with the terse 「死んじまえ」 making the severity unmistakable. She does not address the words to her sister or friend. There is no shown assent from either. Kazami's joint care remains factual but does not travel with Hiyori into this decision.

Hiyori is the final contributor to a commission opened and funded in parts by people acting for different reasons. Her contribution meets the threshold. The request targets Kōen, the established leader. Other people determine the carrying out of the job. Hiyori's private anger and consequential payment do not tell us that she chose the target in the first place, controlled what followed or learned the exact outcome. The coda calls the completed commission an assassination. Its direct depiction ends at loss of consciousness and removal at the hands of the people doing the job. How much Hiyori expected or understood of those events, what happened to her feelings afterward and whether she ever told Hitomi are unanswered. V07 S25 P0003–0053, especially P0013–0015. [The commission's separate contributors and result](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V07_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v07-e010--the-commissions-close-after-the-fighters-leave).

## Speech, ordinary knowledge and conditional appraisal

The split question with Kazami uses a family ease that leaves the twins' identities recoverable in context. Hiyori's individual 「そんなわけないよ」 and reasoning about the size of containers show colloquial dissent with a person she regularly talks with. The group can also speak together, genuinely object together, or be unsettled enough to stop speaking. Only consistently marked lines belong to one person. The much harsher contraction 「死んじまえ」 and the isolated first person in the funding passage are printed interior language, without a listener or evidence of a spoken voice. They support a single strong difference in setting and feeling. There is little basis for Hiyori's usual private forms of address, an exact acoustic performance, or how she would speak directly to a person she holds responsible. School meals, debate with her sister, mutual care, and patient saving for a wanted computer support her ordinary life. Personal favorites, established hobbies beyond that intended purchase, and romance or intimate preference are not evidenced. V04 S17 P0068–0109/P0415–0438, V07 S21 P0082–0106, S25 P0013–0015.

Around trusted peers and a shared everyday question, Hiyori is likely to participate playfully and can offer a distinct reason when she disagrees. She may offer limited support while expressing discomfort at an intrusive plan. When harm to the friend is immediate she can help practically alongside Kazami. When the damage has already been done, she has once interpreted decisive retaliation as something she can bring about at a real personal cost. The closest comparable trigger involves a friend actually hurt after a protective act, a target Hiyori blames and a ready mechanism requiring one more contribution. That is a well-supported reaction in one specific case. An answer about how she would act without such a mechanism, with clear danger to bystanders, after direct objection from the friend, or in a more ordinary disagreement about loyalty would exceed the evidence. The retaliation changes the reader's knowledge of her and, by her own report, how cruel she has felt. It does not establish how long any resulting change lasts. V04 S17 P0091–0109/P0415–0438, V07 S21 P0082–0114, S25 P0013–0015.
