# Mozuku

Mozuku hesitates at an introduction among older hands, keeps a private project from them and survives one encounter because she can tell which kind of mistake a confident opponent might make. Her fear of that opponent is real. So is the exhilaration when she finds that what she had taken for inadequate skill can give her an option. Those experiences remain side by side after the danger recedes. V03 S15 P0154–0158, S17 P0243–0258, S21 P0281–0340.

## A new acquaintance with a private project

海雲, read モズク, is the player name she gives during the introductions at Cloudy Beach. This is her tenth game. It is Yūki's forty-fourth and includes several players with substantial experience. Mozuku visibly hesitates, gives her count and says 「えっと、その、十回目です。よろしくお願いします」. She uses a conventional polite greeting, then nervously passes the turn to Mitsuba. Yūki reads her as intimidated by the other players. The character plate depicts the same closed-in presentation. Her civil name and exact age are unknown. The written name gives Mitsuba a chance to joke about sea and cloud in a game called Cloudy Beach. Yūki identifies the superficial resemblance and says even the meanings differ. The joke can expose what kinds of things players start reading as signs. Mozuku's actual position is a contestant facing the attack of the person assigned as killer. V03 S15 P0154–0158, S17 P0347–0365, S21 P0003–0023/P0281–0340, S05-I01. [The visual introductions](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e014--cover-composite-portraits-and-an-unsigned-handover-note).

When players review the first apparent death, Mozuku draws a practical implication from the condition of the body and asks about a weapon. When Airi proposes checking the cottages, Mozuku hesitates and offers an alternative. Someone hiding evidence might put it somewhere else. The suggestion leaves her own room private, and it does not have to be wrong to serve that interest. Koyomi independently objects to readily inviting room searches and gives Airi another reason to drop the proposal. Yūki privately detects that Mozuku may be concealing something and uses her tenth appearance to counter the surface impression of helplessness. She remains without grounds to identify her as the culprit. Mozuku's later report of staying in her cottage is compatible with the observed isolation and leaves the reason undisclosed. The reader sees the preparation's effect later. The timing of its individual stages goes beyond what the other contestants can verify, including Etsusei, who tracks Mozuku's movements and fills the gaps with inference. V03 S17 P0243–0258/P0347–0365, S19 P0088–0097, S21 P0305–0336. [Detecting an unseen rule](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e004--detecting-a-rule-without-seeing-it), [The case of Mozuku's cottage](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e009--mozukus-decision-under-a-bad-comparison).

She can reserve information and still invest in real company. When four players share Koyomi's cottage, she brings a relatively large load from her own and covers it. She is part of the arrangement in which residents take turns keeping watch and stay with one another. Yūki is curious about what she has packed and decides to respect the cover, so the contents remain unseen. On the eighth morning Mozuku shakes Yūki awake with word that help has arrived. A quick defense from the sleeping player startles her into an immediate protest, then she recovers the reason she is there and tells her what has happened. She hesitates over the introductions. Here she initiates a small, useful act for one of the experienced players. Her reluctance to have a room examined has consequences for what she reveals. It is a poor rule for how much she will cooperate when action and disclosure can be separated. These scenes establish situational habits of packing, working in private and passing along important news. They leave usual food, favorite leisure, home routines and any preference for solitude across ordinary settings unknown. V03 S21 P0081–0085/P0123–0144. [The shared cottage and the disputed end condition](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e008--seven-days-three-killings-and-an-undecided-death).

## What she does with incomplete skill

Mozuku studied in an electrical field at university and left during her second year. She deeply regrets not having studied more when she finds herself wanting a capability she cannot make work. She describes an earlier disengagement from both university and society and a subsequent arrival in this line of work. These are her own retrospective and current judgments. They do not disclose what caused her to leave school or her first invitation. She can make part of a technically plausible idea function and recognizes the limit of what her education supports. She builds her decision around the limit. If she can persuade the person pursuing her that the incomplete object will do what that person already fears, she does not need it to have that capacity. V03 S21 P0281–0340. [Mozuku's decision](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e009--mozukus-decision-under-a-bad-comparison).

Etsusei chooses Mozuku expecting the least experienced of the four players to be the easiest to kill. She actually wounds her in the leg. Mozuku then confronts her with a credible display while still in danger. Etsusei reasons from what she can see, Mozuku's earlier time in the cottage and the effects of a device she knows from her own actions. She misreads the object and revises her judgment of the person. From Etsusei's side, retreat is the result of taking a plausible danger and personal stakes seriously. From Mozuku's side, it is an attempt to make a much stronger opponent carry uncertainty. Only after the point of view changes is the shortfall in the object's capability disclosed. No evidence shows Etsusei learning it in time. The contrast gives Mozuku a result worth crediting without turning another person's reasonable fear into proof she had designed the imagined device. The violent relation lasts through the risk and the retreat. The effect on each person's knowledge is directional. Mozuku learns that even a veteran can misread her signals, while Etsusei walks away with an altered and still mistaken appraisal.

Mozuku lies down and murmurs 「……助かったあ……」. She is frightened enough to feel her heart and excited enough to look for a word for what worked. She settles on 〈詭道〉 as a possible style and experiences this as the first time she has thought she might have a talent. It is genuine change in self-appraisal, disclosed through the same viewpoint that gives her older disappointment. It is also one demonstrable success under an unusually favorable misapprehension. She gets moving on the hurt leg toward the rescue boat. Her individual boarding or later use of the style remains unshown. V03 S21 P0286–0345, S23 P0005–0006. [Retreat, aftermath and the new idea of talent](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e009--mozukus-decision-under-a-bad-comparison).

## Speech, relationships and conditions for extending the portrait

In group introductions Mozuku uses polite endings and audible hesitations that make her junior position easy to read. In 「そんなの、コテージに置いてはおかないんじゃない、ですか……？」 the hesitations soften an objection that still changes the direction of the discussion. She uses neither the same strategy nor the same degree of speech in the next two important encounters. Around Yūki on the eighth morning, 「な、なにするんですかっ」 protests a physical reflex. Her decisive 「迎えが！」 follows when she remembers the reason to wake her. In the forest, much of her communication is what she lets Etsusei see and supply for herself. The stretched ending of 「助かったあ」 is spoken for herself after Etsusei has gone. 〈詭道〉 is a much more self-possessed formulation in her private assessment. The Japanese print gives hesitations, endings, and their audiences. It leaves ordinary voice quality, favored private address and the way she would speak in a secure lifelong relationship unestablished. V03 S15 P0154–0158, S17 P0243–0258, S21 P0123–0144/P0289–0340.

In a group of unfamiliar, more experienced players, the observed pattern is polite, hesitant speech and useful support in specific tasks. A process that would expose something important to her may get a hesitant objection and a plausible alternative. What she is hiding has to be established independently. If a stronger opponent is visibly acting on a convention and Mozuku can make a credible contrary possibility apparent, this encounter supports trying to turn the opponent's caution into an opening. It is weaker evidence for choosing to deceive helpful people without a cause, for functioning without fear or for surviving an equally skilled opponent who can test the supposed advantage. Participation in Koyomi's group and giving Yūki useful news are countercases to a portrait based solely on private ruses. The novels give no attested attraction, romantic history or intimate preference. Reliable extensions into friendship outside the group, work outside the games, actual training sustained over time or judgment after a comparable bluff fails would require new evidence. V03 S17 P0243–0258, S21 P0081–0085/P0123–0144/P0286–0345.
