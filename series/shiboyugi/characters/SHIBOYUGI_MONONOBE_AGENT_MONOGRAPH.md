# Mononobe’s assigned agent

Mononobe's assigned agent recruits her into the lethal games, continues to offer her work and watches her compete. The unnamed organizer employee finds reason to doubt the history Mononobe tells and makes that doubt part of the decision to recruit her. The same mind later watches her give up a clear to pursue another player and alternates between amused recognition and revulsion. This portrait is limited to the agent's work with one player and the agent's interpretation of it. The admitted Japanese continuity through V10 gives the directly attributable material in V09. V09 S18 P0515–0537.

## An inquiry becomes recruitment

About two months before Gimmicky Mansion, the agent notices someone whose clothes and movements suggest a girl from a well-off family walking in an area where the agent would not readily expect her. Mononobe's look makes the agent uneasy. Over tea the agent raises her circumstances indirectly and she rapidly begins telling a story of a hostile faction at school, a false allegation and forced withdrawal. The agent recalls that the delivery invited sympathy. A recorded voice or independent tape of that interview is unavailable. Mononobe later tells Takami a materially similar story. The conversation with Takami corroborates the existence of a public account she continues to offer, while her description of the school requires its own evidence. V09 S18 P0185–0222/P0529–0533. [The recruitment and the recurrent explanation](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e004--the-name-mononobe-gives-to-defeat).

The agent goes to the trouble of checking. The people consulted mostly agree, in the agent's account, that Mononobe engaged in behavior more severe than the word bullying conveys. The agent questions her again. Mononobe calls the accounts malicious and explains that she has enemies. The agent has heard about school surveillance evidence and chooses to leave the obvious further question unspoken. The conclusion is a personnel judgment. The reader is told that the agent is now sure Mononobe is suitable for play and makes the offer. Independent inquiry is an actual part of the agent's judgment process. The camera record itself remains a reported object and the novel does not establish the identities of the people consulted, the terms of their testimony, or any official agreement to investigate or recruit on that basis. V09 S18 P0531–0534.

## Watching the result of that choice

The agents outside Gimmicky Mansion wait for their players to emerge. Mononobe's agent chooses the broadcast over talking with colleagues. A tablet whose size makes it easy to handle and adequately readable is a rare ordinary working detail. The agent sees Mononobe pass up the available route and head for Yūki, thinks 「あいかわらず愉快な人だ」 and relates the choice to her enthusiasm in earlier cases. According to this account the agent has brought offers at about half-month intervals and Mononobe has accepted readily. The agent thinks Mononobe continues to cast herself as a person compelled to earn a living. The analysis shifts from simple pretense toward the idea that she actually believes her description of herself. 「どうしてこんな人間が生きてるんだろうな？」 is the agent's last private response in this scene. The contrast between fascination and condemnation is direct evidence of the observer's reaction. It does not disclose what the agent would do about the harm or even whether the agent believes intervention is part of the job. V09 S18 P0495–0537. [The actual decision shown to the monitor](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e004--the-name-mononobe-gives-to-defeat).

Later Mononobe remembers harm she inflicted as supposed correction, locates the fault in the other person's response and again tells herself she has been set up. This occurs during an attack she knowingly pursues. It gives the reader independent grounds for taking seriously part of what the agent inferred about her capacity to disown responsibility. The words do not give the agent independent knowledge of the later act, validate every detail of the school report or turn a hostile description of Mononobe into a clinical category. The later revenge and its interruption belong to other perspectives. V09 S24 P0576–0625. [Whose knowledge the later account supplies](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e011--interrupted-revenge-and-the-late-hospital-awakening).

The agent's strongest evidenced pattern is to draw a person out gently, compare the account with other information and act on the features judged useful to the institution. Here understanding the evidence makes the agent withhold a challenge and recruit the person anyway. Continued invitation and scrutiny can coexist with a harsh private appraisal. The plausible alternatives are fascination with an unusual charge, a compartmentalized job or a mixture of both. An isolated working detail and professional contact are the available ordinary circumstances. The agent's civil identity, age, gender, household preferences and intimate relationships are unspecified. The novel reports the questions at tea and the follow-up with little verbatim outward register or self-address. The two quoted judgements show interior diction in writing. Predicting the agent's independent reporting, refusal, behavior toward another recruit or response under authority would require a separate episode. V09 S18 P0522–0537.
