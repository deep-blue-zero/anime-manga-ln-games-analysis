# Takami

鷹三 carries the ruby タカ／ミ. Takami lives with other members of Mikkai, scouts for people who can contribute unusual skills and uses trained crows in work for the group and on her own initiative. She takes a cynical interest in other people's mistakes and has a strong practical investment in preparing for them. The novel eventually links those qualities to a mother whose preparations taught Takami how to live and ended the mother's own life. It also shows that selective loyalty, sympathy and satisfaction make a difference to how Takami acts. Her civil name, exact age and career count remain unknown. The admitted Japanese account through V10 leaves her facing an attack aboard Jamboree Ship. V08 S18 P0149–0166, S20 P0219–0259/P0279–0284, V09 S18 P0133–0222, V10 S23 P0463–0507.

## The preparation she inherited

The story gives Takami's early life late, when she thinks she has seen enough of the games. Her mother expected disaster and believed a conflict was already under way out of public view. She trained Takami in practical survival. An actual earthquake killed her when her own shelving and supplies collapsed, before she had drawn on the stockpile. Takami remembers she did not cry at her funeral. She associates the apparent waste with her sense of death and with the cold amusement she later takes in people getting things wrong. She believes her absent father fled her mother, though she never knew his face. The material consequences of the earthquake and instruction have a narrower warrant than Takami's conviction that they explain her personality. V10 S23 P0475–0485. [The late account of how Takami came to play](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e014--who-is-left-to-blame).

Picked up by a recruiter while drifting, she found that the skills were useful and that watching others flounder could feel like a ridiculous film. The source shows actual consequences of her preparedness. Among the people waiting aboard an apparently comfortable cruise ship, she actively anticipates a fight and prepares with what she can find. She has already taught the use of natural materials and is just as ready to use manufactured ones. She will tell others how she prepared if they turn out to be allies. The conditional aside is an ordinary part of her teaching, and actual cooperation follows when Shiro authorizes an existing plan and she asks Busutake and Maya to help. The account supplies no general guarantee that a prepared person will recognize every source of danger. V10 S19 P0198–0244. [Different kinds of preparation on the ship](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e006--the-invitation-nobody-could-match).

## What she owes the people she likes

At dinner Takami leaves meat out of her taco. She feeds the crows, including while discussing hard terms with Shiro. At Airi's welcome she is already drinking, slings an arm around the newcomer and makes her sit down for conversation. Airi expects the forceful hostess to press a drink on her. Takami asks her age and pours ginger ale when she learns Airi is eighteen. She then openly tells her that Shiro first pursued Yūki, a fact Airi had not heard. The combination is characteristic of the shown social range. Takami can be intrusive and unsparing with information while distinguishing what she is willing to give someone under the circumstances. The source supplies no stable romantic preference, lover or intimate history. V08 S18 P0149–0166/S20 P0222–0232, V10 S16 P0118–0150.

She calls Shiro 「大将」 and tells her she likes her despite what she calls a longstanding dislike of humanity. The sting is 「人でなし」 as a joking reason for making an exception. Shiro takes offense at the perspective even though their temperaments often suit each other. The talk is also a dispute over what to do with Awahime. Takami advises paying enough to settle, while Shiro has already set force in motion. Takami arranges the birds and asks Busutake for cameras. The equipment transmits automatically, and Busutake monitors the footage and passes the relevant image to Shiro. Her criticism and service persist together. In another setting Takami calls Awahime 「お嬢」, tells Yūki that publicizing the walk was Shiro's mistake, and asks Yūki to keep the criticism secret. The evidence supports closeness and professional loyalty as distinct from blanket approval. The declarations of liking and teasing supply no specific romantic history. V08 S20 P0219–0259/P0279–0284/P0438–0441/P0453–0463, V09 S18 P0051–0088. [Her dissent and continued work for Shiro](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V08_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v08-e008--the-skill-whose-owner-could-still-feel-its-loss).

The forms she uses with other players change with the setting and how publicly she is willing to speak. After speaking somewhat politely to Yūki about the conflict, she refers to Mikkai through 〈密会〉 with the ruby あれ, and Yūki matches it, so a group name available to the reader can pass in public as an indeterminate reference. 「幽鬼さん」 is audible in the conversation, while 「幽鬼の旦那」 is useful when referring to her around Shiro or among teammates. She repeatedly uses 「大将」 for Shiro, tries 「ダーリン」 once when offering reassurance and stops when Maya objects. That exchange gives the pet name a social boundary. With Nanase and others she uses the familiarity of 「ちゃん」 and a bright approach. Airi gets 「藍里ちゃん」 even at a welcome. The written dialogue combines these vocatives with chummy teasing and shifts back to politeness when exact knowledge matters. Those are prose cues and reported delivery. The evidence cannot establish the sound of a performance. V08 S20 P0222–0259, V09 S18 P0051–0096/P0133–0159/P0213–0222, S21 P0222–0240, V10 S16 P0136–0150 and S21 P0283–0329.

## A cooperative scout who withholds the means

In Gimmicky Mansion Takami proposes a sensible first search and is bored by how uninteresting it proves. She converts the time into active recruiting. The familiarity she uses to startle Nanase also gives her a rough test of recognition and a cover for finding out why people enter the games. She sympathizes with Sana's precarious contract and sees signs of usefulness in Mononobe as a player. Neither offers a distinct skill that meets her ideal for Mikkai. Her contemptuous thought that incompetence in one area generalizes is an inference of her own. It reveals how aggressively she screens and leaves open whether she misses capability in people she would quickly dismiss. She leaves without gaining a recruit and returns alive to the house. Her particular exit route and individual formal score are missing. V09 S18 P0128–0222, S21 P0065–0096. [The search, recruiting and actual return](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e002--a-communal-search-becomes-a-scarcity-problem).

V08 shows her birds furnishing information in an organized pursuit, with cameras obtained through Busutake. V09 establishes a more independent intervention. Takami says she took the opportunity to set her crows to follow Yūki after their encounter. Exactly where the birds waited is part of Yūki's reconstruction. Yūki later catches a suspicious crow at a furnished house thought to be safe. The captured bird carries no visible device. Takami laughs and says 「バレましたか」 when challenged, then evades the claim that the birds were actually brought into the game. She praises Yūki for catching one and offers the possibility of Yūki keeping it. Yūki declines, relocates and insists future conflict occur in play. Takami actually repeats Yūki's messages to Shiro, despite thinking that Shiro hardly absorbs them. The material result establishes surveillance and a reason to rethink a housing choice. The source supplies no actual game ruling on animals or comprehensive account of how Takami acquired, trained or deployed them. V08 S20 P0240–0249/P0279–0284/P0409–0446, V09 S21 P0085–0096/P0146–0240. [What the captured crow actually proves](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e005--the-visitor-the-bird-and-the-room-selected-for-safety).

## Pleasure, sympathy and the last challenge

On the ship Takami sees Yūki disappear over a rail and observes a ripple. She never sees a body sink. She points out the physical problems with one suggested return and tests several reasons why the result might remain unsettled, including mistaken information and a second stage. She carries out Shiro's decision to work on the possibility Yūki is alive. Later Shiro visibly strikes a duct and threatens revenge over the discovery of Maya's body. Takami has watched for the reaction. She sees a brief blankness before anger, takes it as almost automatic performance and explains that she recognizes a similar tendency in herself. The inference is framed by a bias she names. Her pity, 「あわれだな」, is directed toward Maya, whose apparent hope of being able to move Shiro she thinks has failed. Her amusement and concern both belong to her. The narrated exterior reaction and Shiro's independently shown capacity for anger in a later, different situation prevent Takami's diagnosis from deciding what the other woman felt then. V10 S19 P0587–0624, S21 P0016–0054/P0318–0334, S23 P0511–0542. [What she reads in Shiro's response](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e011--the-two-ways-to-read-the-marked-body).

In V09 Takami could scarcely imagine Busutake dead. On the ship she sees what looks like Busutake's body in the water and thinks she must surely be dead, though those modifications make any such judgment uncertain. Busutake later appears alive at the hospital. Takami has no access to that correction when she calls Busutake the person she got on with best. Her attention to the tense of 「だった」 turns into a moment of self-scrutiny. She calls what she feels 「満足」, believing that Yūki's feat has given her the last large spectacle she needs, and starts to consider leaving the games and taking ordinary life more seriously. This is a change in what she finds sufficient. She still refuses to yield a place to those who blame her and demands they carry out their own verdict. Her last perspective ends with an object coming from behind, apparently thrown by the players she has just noticed. No enacted resignation, surrender or final personal outcome is recorded. V10 S23 P0463–0507 and S26 P0046–0069.

Takami tends to prepare independently, share her advantage selectively and meet strong uncertainty with hypotheses and ridicule. A leader whose project she respects can obtain help despite disagreement. A person who surprises her may earn admiration where an ordinary applicant would be dismissed. Her actual treatment of Airi and feeling for Maya are counterexamples to taking her announced dislike of people as a complete guide. When suddenly made responsible for other people's collective failure, she challenges them openly, but the continuation of that decision is outside her shown range. Predicting accurate judgments of a close associate, automatic betrayal, or how she would act after leaving would outrun the evidence she herself distinguishes from what she thinks she sees.
