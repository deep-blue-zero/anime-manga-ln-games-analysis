# Mishiro's assigned agent

The unnamed agent assigned to Mishiro (御城のエージェント; SBY-P025) provides a strikingly personal service inside a deliberately uniform occupation. Before Mishiro entered, the agent offered her a way to become exceptional. After Scrap Building, the adult who picks her up listens to the consequences of failing to be the best and treats the proposed repair as a compromised return rather than vindication. Their register shifts from soliciting a candidate’s faith in an institution to negotiating with a participant who can address the agent as the adult assigned to help her. The continuity is their attention to what she wants. The change is how frankly the two can admit what service will achieve. This portrait uses the original Japanese novels through V10, with spoilers. P025 has no disclosed personal name, biography or independent viewpoint. This is the character who recruits and later transports Mishiro, distinct from the assigned agents in Yūki's and Etsusei's separate scenes.

## The remembered offer

After the loss of her right arm, Mishiro cannot stop asking whose decisions put her there. Her thoughts arrive at the agent who brought her into the games. She recalls two sentences she says she can remember word for word. “ここなら、あなたの先をゆく人は誰もいません” offers her a field where nobody will be ahead. “あなたの望む地位を、私たちは提供いたします” presents her desired status as something the agent’s group can supply. The second statement claims a collective ability to provide a position. The personal choice of promise is effective because the candidate has an intense wish to find a rank that nobody else already occupies. That wish and the depth of its connection to the mother's hopes become accessible in the same recollection to the reader. The agent's knowledge of the exact family history is not shown.

Mishiro recalls that she found the offer compelling and reasons that very few people would play often enough to compete with her. Now she is encountering an evidently superior player and has lost confidence along with the arm. In her thoughts she belittles the distinction the recruiter once made so attractive. Her memory supplies evidence of a pitch that found a vulnerability and shaped her choice. It also comes from an injured person looking for responsibility and follows her accusation in thought against Yūki. Her remembered sentences establish a meaningful appearance in her own decision history. They do not independently establish the recruiter’s knowledge of the whole field or a literal guarantee enforceable by the prospective participant. In represented chronology this is the agent’s earliest intervention. The prose discloses it much later, at the point when the offer stops describing what matters to Mishiro in the immediate game. ## A private audience with a limit

The pickup follows Mishiro's admission to Yūki that she now knows who is superior and her apology. The narrator explains the common assignment of personal agents to regular players after surviving a second game and distinguishes those who merely provide transport from those who speak, become involved in daily concerns and help a player progress. Mishiro's agent belongs to the latter category. It is a specific contrast in behavior within a described job. The recruitment was earlier than the ordinary threshold for a dedicated assignment. The agent could approach one potential participant and later serve as her assigned contact. The story does not explain the administrative steps between the two activities.

The agent says they saw the apology and jokes about wishing they had recorded it. “お嬢様” gives Mishiro a title that continues to acknowledge the identity she presents. The teasing puts pressure on the distance normally suggested by that title. She asks with practiced courtesy if she can let herself go. “勘弁してほしい” displays the driver's objection to this happening in the car, and she appeals to the agent’s supposed duty of メンタルケア before kicking the seat and shouting. The agent continues driving. Once she has exhausted herself, the adult is amused and changes the angle of the previous joke. They say it is good the words were not recorded and joke that anyone else who heard her would cut ties. This actually makes the agent her only audience in the scene and gives that audience a distinctive tolerance. It also leaves open how much the agent likes the outburst and whether there is any recognized care duty as extensive as Mishiro claims. There is neither a formal permission nor an agreement that she has used the car appropriately. What the scene offers is a tolerated exception, sheltered from the social effect the driver jokes about. V02 S12 P1125–1152. [V02, the pickup after the ballot](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V02_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v02-e005--taking-a-secret-ballot-at-its-word)

As she settles the agent asks why she apologized if she resents it so much. This invites her to explain, and Mishiro distinguishes resentment from a necessary acknowledgment. The agent registers surprise when that explanation includes continuing to play. The interaction is ordinary work in an extraordinary occupation: driving, objecting to damage or disruption, engaging a withdrawn person and learning how she intends to handle a serious bodily loss. The narrator does not give the driver's thoughts on whether she should retire. Their question can express surprise without revealing a wish about her career. The adult's reply to her actual choice goes directly to what is feasible. V02 S12 P1153–1158. ## What a service can provide

Mishiro wants to take up an earlier offer. The agent makes an exaggerated guess about what it was, then says weapons brought into the game are out when Mishiro asks whether that idea would violate the rules. Mishiro requests an ordinary artificial hand. The driver says even the person they have in mind cannot restore things perfectly. This is a technical limit specified by the intermediary, without an account of how well they know the treatment. Their distinction between accommodation and an unauthorized weapon is a rule assertion in conversation. The scene supplies no equipment inspection or encounter where the agent actually enforces it. The specialist is only “〈あの人〉” here. Mishiro's use of a prosthesis in later evidence does not identify the person in this conversation or establish that the driver made or fitted it.

The agent first offered to provide Mishiro with “地位.” Now they say they cannot put her exactly back at “ゼロ,” the starting point from which she wants to try again. Mishiro replies that improving her skill will have to compensate. The agent makes a pun about “腕前,” arms and proficiency, and explains it in case the silence means it did not land. Mishiro tells them to watch the road. “仰せのままに” closes the exchange with ritual deference that plays along with the rebuke. The agent's frankness about a practical loss invites the client to work out her own role, whatever was rhetorically implied by the early pitch. V02 S12 P1156–1169. ## An appraisal from the one relationship

The recruiter’s attributed “私たち” and honorific “いたします” introduce institutional backing and deference. The observed driver often uses 私, “お嬢様,” a mixture of honorifics, contractions and “ですぜ.” The latter turn the formality into a recurring joke the driver can adjust as the client’s patience diminishes. They tease when Mishiro is exhausted, respect practical boundaries when she proposes a treatment, and finally follow a straightforward instruction. It is a register supported by the writing and the reader's access to Mishiro's interactions. It gives no performed voice and no general basis for assigning age, gender, accent, or a habitual register with colleagues, a partner, or a different client.

Within the only sustained relationship, a client whose public dignity has just been strained can expect this agent to recognize the need for privacy, respond to recognizable rank-seeking language and draw a reasonably plain technical line. The answer can include a flippant joke even when the question is serious. The likely response to a client who has given up on play or to a violation involving another agent cannot be inferred from this. Continuing to provide actual help and calling her お嬢様 can reflect active investment in Mishiro's progress or a job done with a particular style of familiarity. Care, irritation and professional instrumental interest could coexist. There is no independent romantic, intimate, domestic or leisure evidence. The ordinary conduct actually available is bound to pickup and care around play. Other unnamed agents, including Etsusei’s independently depicted agent, have their own institutional relationships and diagnoses of their clients. V02 S12 P1134–1169. V03 S21 P0180–0235.
