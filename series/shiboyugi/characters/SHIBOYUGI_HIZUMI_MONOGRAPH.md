# Hizumi

Hizumi can join a group by nodding and walking with it, then decide in her own words that she would prefer to be alone. The quieter actions matter to a portrait that otherwise seems to begin with the public effects of one terrible teacher. When Airi asks about Kyara, Hizumi recognizes an accusation that has not yet been spoken. She claims the right to act for herself and then gives Airi a real reason to be afraid of her. Later readers learn that a second person has independently assumed that the pupil might reproduce the teacher's actions, with a consequence Hizumi could not foresee. V03 S15 P0048–0058/P0116–0118, S17 P0089–0107, S19 P0237–0319, S21 P0017–0018.

## A former pupil in other people's accounts

日澄, ヒズミ, is Hizumi's player name. The game at Cloudy Beach is Yūki's #44. Hizumi only gives her name when other players give their counts. No exact age or career tally is established. Yūki thinks a young-looking player with her distant manner fits one of the recognizable kinds of misfit who come into the trade. That is the first observer's shorthand. V04 locates Hizumi among the teenage girls living at Kyara's home in Shion's remembered period. The short V03 responses do not disclose that history to Yūki. V03 S15 P0048–0058/P0116–0118/P0134–0137, S05-I01, V04 S21 P0628–0634. [The island group](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e003--a-boundary-discovered-by-the-participants), [The earlier household](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e012--shions-ways-of-making-and-losing-a-home).

Hizumi herself brings the words 弟子 and 師匠 into her exchange with Airi. Etsusei independently knows she is apparently a pupil of Kyara, and Shion later supplies a remembered address in the same household, a time when Kyara was less publicly notorious, and what living together actually meant to her. These accounts converge on the historic affiliation. The specific lessons Kyara gave Hizumi, the form of her joining and the ways she felt indebted remain out of view. Shion says she herself learned extensively without having thought of the relationship as formally becoming a pupil. Her account cannot furnish Hizumi's own chosen definition of apprenticeship.

Shion remembers talking readily to Moegi and speaking very little with Hizumi. She also remembers trying to provoke her with small household pranks, at which point disengaged quiet could turn into an angry, dangerous pursuit. Neither response explains the relationship alone. Shion learns practical skills under this pressure and jokes to herself that the two were closely communicating. That is a particular beneficiary's recollection of a game she helped create. It does not tell us whether Hizumi found the pranks fun, liked that kind of proximity or consciously intended to teach. They continued to contact one another after Kyara's disappearance and the breakup of the house. The continuing contact is positive evidence of a tie that cannot be reduced to their temperaments being compatible or incompatible. Shion recalls occasional contact. Its medium and what Hizumi meant by keeping in touch remain open. Shion hears of the later death from an agent after their contact stops. The reader already has a much more specific source for it in V03. V03 S19 P0282–0319, S21 P0017, V04 S21 P0628–0642. [The disputed implications of apprenticeship](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e006--airis-reluctant-scrutiny), [Shion's relationship memory](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e012--shions-ways-of-making-and-losing-a-home).

## Choosing access on Cloudy Beach

In the first encounter she gives a name softly and says little, but comes along when asked to meet the group. When others later choose their own paths, she says 「わたしも、ひとりがいい」. Solitary action is a choice she can communicate, in a setting where others make it too. It is compatible with showing up to a common meeting. It does not give another player a reliable way to infer her assignment. The illustrated warning and closed presentation on the V03 character plate are consistent with her guarded exchanges. They do not explain why she keeps the group at a distance. V03 S15 P0048–0058/P0116–0118, S17 P0089–0107, S19 P0088–0098, S05-I01. [Portraits and their selected lines](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e014--cover-composite-portraits-and-an-unsigned-handover-note).

After the deaths, Airi thinks the staging could point to a disciple reliving Candle Woods. She recognizes that the theory is a chain of assumptions. She has independent evidence that contestants can carry planted means of coercion, however, and wants to check Hizumi's body. Hizumi asks for a reason and refuses. Airi's offer to show she is unarmed does not relax the objection. Hizumi means the request to stay away seriously enough to escalate if approached, but does not know all the motives Airi is balancing. Airi has already considered suspecting a killer and now offers a verbal question instead of the body check. Hizumi answers the implied indictment and gains a real physical advantage. Airi retaliates and escapes. She knows more about the likely historical connection after the encounter, but remains uncertain that Hizumi caused the murders. The speed with which a request about a body can turn into a fight is grounded in Hizumi's conduct, even though the accusation that drew Airi there is wrong. V03 S19 P0237–0319. [Airi's competing inferences](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e006--airis-reluctant-scrutiny).

## Speech, violence and the last change in what others know

「日澄」 followed by silence is enough for an introduction. 「わたしも、ひとりがいい」 adds a plain personal preference to a group discussion without giving a reason or trying to direct others. To Airi, the repeated, clipped 「なに？」 and 「なんで？」 demand a purpose. 「いやだ」 and 「近づくな」 set an unmitigated boundary. Her question about what being a pupil is supposed to mean gives way to 「私は私だ！」 and 「勝手なイメージを貼り付けるな！」. That passage asserts her free will and her body in the same argument. It uses direct and rough accusation rather than the cooperative silence of the first encounter. Airi hears her adjusting the volume and takes it as a sign that she is unused to shouting. Hizumi then says she will kill all of them and live. The threats make Airi defend herself. As a matter of evidence, a threat can expose a capacity or a wish in that moment while leaving the assigned role, her general purposes and the truth of older judgments open. Printed Japanese and the narrated change in volume give the rhythm and local effect. There is insufficient evidence for a stable voice performance or a usual inner monologue. V03 S15 P0051–0058/P0118, S17 P0089–0107, S19 P0250–0277/P0302–0319.

Etsusei, who actually received the hidden role, chooses Hizumi after deciding against an attack on Maguma. Etsusei acts on her own prior fear of another Candle Woods. Hizumi already seems to have found and removed some of the controls in her body. She has overlooked the implants at her soles, which leaves the attacker an advantage. The discovery of Hizumi's body and Etsusei's account confirm that Hizumi was the second actual victim. Her practical defenses have more to show for them than either the placidity at first meeting or Airi's suspicion alone would suggest. The anonymous early viewer of a Candle Woods recording is never securely identified as Hizumi. This sequence establishes a correction in what the reader and other people can know, as distinct from a new commitment by Hizumi to continue or abandon Kyara's work. V03 S19 P0003–0013/P0362–0398/P0415–0430, S21 P0014–0023. ## Ordinary limits and a conditional profile

In ordinary company she is shown keeping her speech short, accepting limited cooperation and choosing personal space. Within the old house there are baths, beds, visits and the uneven coexistence of three younger people and a host. The richest everyday account of her is from Shion, who deliberately changes conditions and finds the reactions informative. The pranks give evidence about a setting in which Hizumi was pushed, with too little basis for stating favorite foods, hobbies, relaxed habits or preferences across homes. No romance or intimate history is evidenced, and exact family circumstances and her route into the industry remain unknown.

A stranger making a bounded request about a shared problem may receive brief assent or a few functional questions. An attempt to examine her body or read her in advance through Kyara is likely to meet abrupt refusals and may prompt force if the person approaches anyway. This has strong support in the island exchange, and the domestic recollection offers a narrower comparison when she believes someone is needling her. Her attendance at first and subsequent communication with Shion are meaningful controls on treating distance as absolute. She can take practical danger seriously without sharing what she has learned, and she can miss an obscure source of vulnerability. There is too little access to her own account of understanding, settled aims and long-term preferences to predict a deeply trusted or explicitly consensual encounter, her position on another pupil's choices, or her interior life under no pressure. Earlier direct evidence of individual instruction or a calm exchange chosen by her would narrow those uncertainties. V03 S15 P0048–0058, S19 P0237–0319, S21 P0017, V04 S21 P0631–0640.
