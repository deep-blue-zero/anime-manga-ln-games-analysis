# Etsusei

Etsusei (永世, エツセイ; SBY-P034) is a player who treats the games as problems she can learn to solve from other people's lives. Her own assigned agent is confident enough to credit her with a fiftieth clear too early. She readily corrects that mistake. Her reading of other contestants is usually just as deliberate, but selective confidence becomes her vulnerability when Mozuku surprises her and Yūki asks her to act against the apparent direction of the hunt. Etsusei wants to be the strongest practitioner of what she believes the proper way to learn. It matters to her that the teacher who exemplified that approach should judge her superior. The story of Cloudy Beach allows her to succeed at its longest deception and fail at its last decision. This portrait covers the original Japanese novel continuity through V10 and contains spoilers. Most of her evidence is in V03, with the immediate outcome reported at the opening of V04.

## What a competitor can see

When she appears at the amusement park, Yūki is at her fortieth game, Maguma at her forty-first, and Maguma says this is reportedly Etsusei's forty-fifth. Yūki has encountered Etsusei twice before, knows her as a regular and marks her separate from the group, examining a piece of the costume. Yūki sees a woman with soft hair and the grave appearance of someone forever thinking, a comparison to a literary figure she uses again around the beach. They acknowledge each other with a nod, without talk. It is possible to be a familiar high performer without sharing any actual personal knowledge. The Cloudy Beach participants later introduce themselves. Etsusei explicitly says the current game is her fiftieth appearance. She has accumulated forty-nine successful games before this entry. The book often uses milestone shorthand. At the actual point when the agent first congratulates her on fifty completed games, Etsusei expressly says that she has yet to clear this one. The delayed original V03 portrait gives the Latin “Essay” and prints her fiftieth-appearance introduction beside a white outer garment. The Japanese prose ruby is the basis of “Etsusei” here. Neither the name display nor her scholarly appearance establishes an occupation outside play. V03 S12 P0045–0053 and S15 P0092–0100/P0147–0156, S04-I01, S21 P0007–0011/P0214–0218. [V03, earlier encounter](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e001--a-wall-that-selects-the-company-one-keeps) · [V03, the original portraits](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e014--cover-composite-portraits-and-an-unsigned-handover-note)

Etsusei asks the tallest competitor, Maguma, to compare what she can see and help her see whether anything lies beyond the coast. She turns the increased viewing height into an approximate lower bound on a direct escape. She also asks whether anyone saw land from the beach. “おっしゃる通りです” concedes Maguma’s practical objection that the inferred distance makes a blind departure impractical. Having extracted what the finding is good for, Etsusei gives more than one explanation for the givens and asks the group to keep searching. Her conclusion is intelligible and useful to them. Her actual information is very different. The organizers had already given her special instructions. She can do informative work in the open and use genuine collaboration to sustain an effect of uncertainty she does not share. The gradual disclosure allows a reader to take her technical competence seriously and later revise the assumption that the conversation made her trustworthy. V03 S15 P0193–0222 and S21 P0008–0014. ## Seeking a complete lesson

V03 eventually lets the reader look back at her formation with more information than the other players had. “〈学習〉” names her strongest ability. Mistakes in these games can end a career immediately, so she prefers studying other people's attempts to making her own unnecessary errors. The apprentice chooses Hakushi as the best existing example. She uses direct instruction, the teacher's everyday conduct and recorded games obtained through another route, including Candle Woods. She also investigates the bodily alterations that made an apparent death survivable and pursues an adaptation requiring the loss of much of her original body. The source establishes willingness and a real extraordinary result. It never gives a complete account of who did the work. Hakushi later tells Yūki “やつが勝手に調べただけだよ” in answer to a question about this method. Learning that Etsusei also received actual tutelage gives no warrant for treating the particular operation as assigned by the master. V03 S21 P0003–0008 and S23 P0038–0042. [V03, the hunter and her source of learning](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e007--the-hunter-the-first-body-and-the-altered-archive) · [V03, Hakushi's direct account](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e011--the-answer-from-someone-at-the-bar)

Etsusei gives the preparation a value beyond survival alone. Greater achievement calls for a greater sacrifice. Getting through the perceived Wall of Thirty and reaching the threshold of fifty give her excellent personal reasons to think that this use of past experience is justified. In Cloudy Beach she has one week to commit at least three killings that can be recognized by the others, with her own execution stipulated if she fails. She makes herself look like the first victim and stays hidden. That disappearance removes her from the others’ working set of suspects. It also lowers her physical readiness. She chooses her early targets in light of a temporary informational advantage, recognizing that the others will be able to take it away. Mitsuba's earlier preparations make the initial assumption about her wrong, but Etsusei still prevails. After finding that Maguma has disposed of the means she was counting on, she switches targets. She already has a reason of her own to want to stop Hizumi, whom she takes to be a pupil of Kyara and a risk of another Candle Woods. That view is her appraisal, not evidence that Hizumi intends to repeat her teacher’s conduct. With Mitsuba and Hizumi dead, Etsusei has two actual killings. She has also made independent preparations for a later move if the original advantage disappears. The same strategies with which she studies a precedent and estimates how quickly a device will be discovered allow her to plan for a loss of immediate tactical power. V03 S19 P0516–0529 and S21 P0003–0023/P0214–0218. [V03, the real count and the rival explanations](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e008--seven-days-three-killings-and-an-undecided-death)

Her assigned agent, a different character from Mishiro's agent, sees armed staff on the rescue boat and is privately certain they won't be needed. Admitted only to the agent’s side of the relationship, the reader sees confidence so great the fine point of a tally has escaped the adult. Etsusei makes that fine point clear to the one person who is already disposed to believe in her. She wants the boat's resources, calls arriving there before evacuation a legitimate way of obtaining them, and treats its proximity as inclusion in the area of play. The agent points out the danger and raises the seizure from staff as a limit. Etsusei answers precisely and maintains her courtesy, although her assessment of where the game extends is her position rather than a formal proclamation on the spot. She does obtain equipment and bring it back into play without an immediate penalty being shown. Both the action and the ordinary politeness she accords the agent make “ぎりぎりのラインまで攻めるのが私の流儀” more informative than her cultivated appearance. She considers the feasible edge of a rule part of her job. Later V10 presents a comparable but separately adjudicated question of an organizer's vessel in a different game. Its adjudication does not operate retroactively as formal certification of Etsusei's move. V03 S21 P0180–0235/P0281–0285. V10 S23 P0278–0376. ## What becomes personal

When Etsusei takes up the hunt again she chooses Mozuku as the least experienced available opponent and injures her. Mozuku produces a device Etsusei has reason to mistake for a functioning threat. Etsusei updates quickly, accepts that a hit of that kind could be especially damaging to an altered body, and withdraws while there is still some time to find another contestant. The reader subsequently learns that much of the device's perceived capability was a bluff. Her assessment of uncertainty is sensible on her own premises. The episode also shows how her reliance on known ranks and a comprehensible model of an opponent’s resources makes her susceptible to a risk that somebody with modest skills can simulate. Withdrawal under those conditions is a consequential exception to the image of a player who advances regardless of cost. V03 S21 P0281–0340. [V03, the person she underestimates](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e009--mozukus-decision-under-a-bad-comparison)

Etsusei does stay with the next opportunity when Yūki proves difficult to hit. A change of target would now cost precious time and either alternative is a substantial competitor. Continuation has a rational basis. Her interior thought adds a quite different reason. On the first day, she asked Koyomi what Hakushi had said about her. Koyomi delivered a warning about a brilliant person whose striking misunderstandings could bring her down. Etsusei answered politely. Then she learned Yūki was also a pupil and that Hakushi expected something of her. “なぜです？” came before time for calculation, and she could not speak for a while afterward. Koyomi's suggestion that the higher tally must imply the greater expectation does not help. By the time Etsusei has Yūki in front of her, the recollection is an injury she has repeatedly rehearsed. Her wish to prove she is on the right path and her ability to take an available third victim now point at the same person. She experiences the encounter as if fate had invited her to eliminate the comparison. The reader learns the earlier conversation after seeing much of Etsusei’s actual preparation. Etsusei had the information from day one. Yūki learned of the relationship considerably later, from the same intermediary. The strength of Etsusei's response says what recognition means to her. It cannot establish Hakushi’s internal ranking as a settled fact. V03 S19 P0443–0482 and S21 P0364–0397. [V03, rival meanings of a shared teacher](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e010--two-readings-of-a-teachers-practice)

Yūki reads their teacher's physical decline as a lesson against actively incurring the removal of parts for a tactical effect. She uses that contrary lesson to lower her estimation of Etsusei's invulnerability. She has to infer that the outcome requires an actual killing by her opponent, rather than the mere fact of one more death. By making the prospect of her own death appear immediate, she takes advantage of Etsusei's need to confirm the circumstances. Etsusei approaches, loses the upper hand and is eventually motionless after an extreme fight. Yūki waits, sees no further motion, leaves and reaches the rescue boat. She leaves without learning whether Etsusei is dead. Later, at the bar, Hakushi challenges Yūki’s request to know which pupil should have won, reminding her that she is no longer a pupil bound by the judgment of someone outside. After all that investment in copying and being measured, the question the teacher actually answers is who will exercise the authority to judge. V03 S21 P0398–0455 and S23 P0003–0006/P0038–0056. [V03, Yūki's reversal and the bar](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V03_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v03-e011--the-answer-from-someone-at-the-bar)

V04 reopens the aftermath from Maguma's boat cabin. Her own agent says directly, “永世さんが死亡しました” at S15 P0007. “幽鬼さんを襲い、返り討ちにあったようです” at P0009 qualifies the account of how she came to die, rather than putting the death itself in the same grammatical doubt. This is meaningful evidence of the outcome in the mouth of an assigned agent who is briefing another survivor. It is a different degree of access from seeing the final body after the passage or receiving an official fiftieth-game tally. It gives no transmission of that report to Yūki, who ended the previous account with the result still uncertain. The secure literary record contains Etsusei's entry at fifty, two confirmed kills before the final chase, her defeat and the later report of her death. V04 S15 P0001–0017. ## Speech, preferences and the reach of the portrait

Etsusei's public requests use 私 and “真熊さん,” with honorifics that solicit willing cooperation and with questions that let practical disagreement enter the discussion. Her instructions to her agent can switch to the curt “静かに” and then return to “少し、先乗りさせていただきました” and “失礼します” while she overrules the adult's judgment. She keeps “そうですか” outwardly level when hearing a memorable criticism. Her privately represented “ふざけるな” and “幽霊女” show what that composure can contain. Nothing securely shows how Etsusei addresses an intimate, uses this register while off duty, or talks in settings with no competition at stake. These differences are written words, punctuation and focal access. They give no performed voice. Studying records and observing a teacher appear as pursuits with an explicit professional purpose. Hair, clothing and pose in the original art have no independent force as evidence of leisure, romance or a private aesthetic. Her civilian identity, intimacies, family, ordinary food and leisure preferences remain unknown.

When she has a record of another person’s competence and a bounded objective, Etsusei is likely to compare opportunities, turn information into a concrete test, exploit an edge she can justify and change targets if the expected loss changes. She is capable of careful caution about an immediate threat alongside a very high tolerance of long-term or bodily cost. A personal challenge to her place in a lineage can add rigidity precisely where she sees signs of an ordained comparison. The direct evidence warrants giving that accelerator weight next to scarcity of time. It cannot make all earlier competence into performance for her teacher or all tactical misjudgments into wounded pride. Her original habits, mastery and mistakes permit a portrait of a learner whose method delivers enormous advantages. The ending exposes the cost of using another person's authority as the final test of the method.
