# Character reconstruction readiness

A usable literary portrait supports understanding a person in the novels. A conditional model needs enough comparable choices to make inquiry, silence, different responses to the same person and abstention more plausible under specified conditions. Finishing a contest, choosing to leave and losing eligibility to enter prompt different choices. A model needs to identify which of those conditions applies before it can use behavior from a similar scene. This readiness judgment uses only the ten admitted Japanese main narratives and their recorded original visual strata. It reflects evidence supporting individual states rather than a measured ability for new scenario simulation. [Reconstruction contract](SHIBOYUGI_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_CONTRACT.md). [Qualification and source routes](audits/SHIBOYUGI_RETROSPECTIVE_QUALIFICATION.md).

## Where bounded candidates are already useful

Six private candidates make explicit state-based mechanisms, knowledge and audience filters, direct sources, alternatives and disconfirming conditions. Five retain their independently reviewed V1. Maguma’s V2 revises the single rule the independent review flagged, and retains V1 and its original tests. The authorized literary work supplies the basis. The candidate papers, exact basis and tests are retained together under the retrospective working record. The visible source and reviewed portraits keep their own authority. The candidates label descriptions of attested conduct as OBSERVED and conditional extensions as CANDIDATE_POLICY. The completed source was known to the author and reviewer. All tests here are retrospective. A future prospective test would require separate admission and a freeze before anyone in the tested lane reads the new evidence. The exact historical state and fictional knowledge change what a model can answer. The candidate questions range from D0 (attested), to a D1 perturbation where the relationship and material stakes can be preserved. D2 requires an established novel state and specific available information, with a bounded inquiry or refusal when crucial context is absent. In D3 (a substantially new setting or life stage) the evidence favors abstention. An actual aptitude under steady conditions does not supply ability with a changed body. No amount of suitable sentence endings would show a correctly chosen speech act under opaque conditions. [Relevant cases in the claim ledger](SHIBOYUGI_CLAIM_AND_FORECAST_LEDGER.md).
