# Yūki and Setsuna

The exceptional intimacy of an assigned agent’s work is usually built on keeping information separate. In V06 one person remembers the first game as a case of not knowing what she was doing. The person cutting her hair remembers the same encounter as a reason for being in the room. They can recognize and address immediate practical needs while misunderstanding a shared past or the other’s present interpretation of their work. In V10 Setsuna can cause Yūki's beloved costumes to remain potentially available by letting her think she will get rid of them. The act extends an earlier habit of filling in missing means. Here the missing agreement is about the value of what will be lost. V06 S18 P0679–0796/S22 P0025–0047, V10 S16 P0344–0409. The individual's histories are recovered in [Yūki](../characters/SHIBOYUGI_YUUKI_MONOGRAPH.md) and [Setsuna](../characters/SHIBOYUGI_SETSUNA_MONOGRAPH.md).

## The shared event and the half-shared relation

At their first game Setsuna knows entering might mean dying and struggles to take the first required action. Waking a stranger is one way she can move. To Setsuna, bringing Yūki into action then has an unintended connection to Kanade’s death. The sister who pursues Setsuna explicitly makes it a source of blame. Later Yūki is physically ready to assist an injured Setsuna, and the admitted reasons for each woman’s conduct dislodge the latter's understanding of what she has witnessed. Yūki did not recognize a lethal venue or the exact harm she had done. When Setsuna wants to live, her relief follows this evidence plus a practical act of aid and a shared walk. It is not simply an opinion about how successfully the competition ended. Both know the exchange and its bodily assistance. Yūki does not see how thoroughly it changes the older participant's assessment of her next options. V06 S18 P0079–0223/P0381–0469/P0538–0604/P0679–0761. [The two firsthand positions](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V06_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v06-e005--two-incompatible-examples-in-one-person).

Setsuna enters agent work when unemployed and looking for a way to stay near the games after leaving play. After she becomes an agent, she learns that Yūki is still playing and volunteers for the assignment so she can see what happens to the woman she credits with changing her life. To Yūki this is an agent arrived through work, with the valuable habit of letting her be quiet. Setsuna’s altered face and lost civil register make physical recognition in ordinary contact much less available. She had retained memories, chooses proximity and can allow the relationship to operate without independently proving the past to her beneficiary. At the haircut Yūki remembers a player and her poor explanation of what the first contest was. She has forgotten the player’s name. Setsuna has already used the old encounter to decide to keep making aid possible. A model that interprets current consciousness as an exclusive personal or professional purpose loses this demonstrated split. It also misreads the relationship's development. The connection is old. The reader learns it later and each of them adds real everyday labor to the shared history. V01 S15 P0052–0061, V02 S18 P0025–0057, V06 S18 P0765–0796/S22 P0025–0047. [The reader’s access to the assignment choice](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V06_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v06-e006--an-agent-who-remembers-being-addressed-by-name).

## What is actually delegated

After Golden Bath, Setsuna’s formal replies about the possibility of an intervention let Yūki know agents can deliberately limit information. Setsuna speaks frankly about the operator’s interest in preservation and apologizes for the injury. She also identifies herself with spectators or workers who want to see players stake everything, and promises to remove interference. The recipient hears unexpected conviction. This gives concrete antecedent force to the later agent’s description of herself as causing harm. Late at night by the roadside the direction of dependency briefly changes when Setsuna is physically overmatched while trying to learn the follower's identity. Yūki offers temporary household care and takes seriously the civil constraint on using a public hospital. Setsuna then connects ways to continue attending class and accepted medical referral. Shared trust here consists of actual help evaluated case by case. Their relationship would look less reciprocal if following the apparent informer and arranging care counted as service, but the invitation into a small room and a borrowed futon disappeared into an undifferentiated account of ordinary life. V02 S18 P0025–0072, V04 S17 P0037–0056/P0188–0224/P0339–0411 and S19 P0363–0376. [Where their privacy needs interact](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V04_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v04-e003--the-private-scope-of-protection).

The work leading to Snow Room already tests whether Yūki lets Setsuna make a consequential decision. Before Yūki asks Rinrin to organize the exercise, she asks Setsuna to make her sleep. Setsuna sees the requested method fail and finds another. Yūki wants to acknowledge that competence. Later, during the test itself, Setsuna has independent control of the usable machine and must judge Yūki's urgent order against the obvious risk to her. Rinrin, who withheld a design change, can offer to relieve her, but Setsuna says she will act. The person in danger takes the risk in setting the situation and asking. The worker supplies an independent decision in applying a different person's apparatus. The effect depends on both. This jointly necessary act at #62.5 is an important precedent and closely delimited by an emergency. It does not settle whether a later choice about the design or pace of Yūki’s career changes through an expressly heard disagreement. Neither the two roles changing nor the permitted means being dangerous substitutes for evidence of that later decision. V06 S20 P0133–0205/P1112–1168/P1267–1358. [The agent's two choices](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V06_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v06-e013--who-takes-the-final-action).

Retaining a certain amount of privacy is part of Yūki’s own active cooperation. She asks to control what she can about the medical aftermath and allows the agent to cut her hair precisely because an expert stranger would be harder to trust. Trust has an ordinary cost only one of them sees. When she moves out of room 107, Yūki delegates furnishing and location selection and on later evidence again asks for help when the new address is unsafe. Setsuna supplies genuinely new options, some of which outside observation defeats. Knowing another person’s location through trust and delegation gives a different relationship to the space than knowing it through surveillance. Harm to that privacy produces the next decision without requiring the two women to disagree about whether to move. V06 S22 P0005–0057, V08 S20 P0887–0942/S22 P0015–0097, V09 S18 P0630–0636 and S21 P0146–0218/P0410–0418. [Care under the exposed address](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V09_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v09-e005--the-visitor-the-bird-and-the-room-selected-for-safety).

## Disagreement at ninety and the division of hospital authority

After her ninetieth clear, Yūki thinks her notes can replace commemorative objects and that she is unlikely to finish school. That disposition differs from her after #9, when she took small and highly deliberate steps to give an ordinary life significance. Setsuna knows that Yūki holds very few things tightly. She sees new loss where Yūki explains a lot of unnecessary storage and what she infers is an agent’s disappointment in hours wasted. Her verbal acceptance ends the exchange. Physical transfer takes place and private intention is unmistakably different. There is no corresponding witnessed choice by Yūki after being told the boxes will be kept. The narrative has enough evidence for an authentic material disagreement without the follow-through and mutual recognition the stronger relationship claim would require. V01 S17 P0127–0145, V10 S16 P0344–0409.

Setsuna considers using the available institution to provide rest and psychological assistance, and sharply reproaches herself for thinking she is suddenly an outsider to the harm done. From Yūki’s nearest perspective, needs and signs at this point are concrete. She receives the food, keeps her regimen and continues planning how to work. Private recognition of care and complicity by someone else is not her assent to new treatment or retirement. After the ninety-third game she cannot make a discussed choice in the last scene. Setsuna directs herself toward the hospital, expresses relief that Yūki survived and asks about return to play. The artisan can go farther into the facility, report his own prognosis and offer a precedent. Busutake then claims a different treatment will make a different outcome possible. Actual listening, disputes before the patient's door and an attack establish real care authority in contention. Only an actual choice by Yūki informed by those relations could answer how much each now shapes her will to continue. V10 S16 P0377–0409/S26 P0029–0069. [The handover and its cost](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e005--the-costumes-in-the-boxes), [the hospital dispute](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e016--a-second-custodian-for-a-damaged-body).

Two rival readings deserve preservation. Setsuna may be enabling a destructive practice in the only form of aid she has helped build. Her own side of the last wardrobe section is the strongest evidence. A less bleak reading sees a worker protecting specific practical options available to somebody she respects, after having once received such protection herself. The capacity to judge the risk personally, put resources behind the other woman's decision and acknowledge the likelihood of deterioration supports that reading. The last authorized evidence leaves those possibilities joined. It permits documenting R035/R036’s actual reciprocity, F094’s more exact unsettled choice and the difference between a recipient and a witness. It also leaves marriage, family status and romantic motives unattested. Directional help, genuine professional intimacy and material control can all be argued without resolving that silence.
