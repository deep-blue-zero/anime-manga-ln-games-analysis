# Airi, Maguma and Yūki

The three veterans at the center of this study know each other well enough to shape plans and badly enough to make confident errors. Cloudy Beach gives them differently documented exits. Yūki leaves without checking the full boat roster. Maguma’s later embarkation is separately shown. Airi hears something in Yūki's answer long before she can say what attracts her to the games. Maguma sometimes prefers a player she says she does not favor. Yūki benefits from both people’s work while remaining quite willing to pursue her own advantages. When the three meet in opposing roles at sea, a particularly useful form of knowledge is being able to infer what the other person is willing to do. The sharpest failure of this approach would be to think that conditions once understood create permanent mutual wishes. The deployments of help become apparent only after opponent, ally and observer are allowed to mean different things in the same contest. ## The table of debts is incomplete before they meet again

At Candle Woods Airi can disarm once she believes she has met the Stumps' quota, with no immediate need to turn on the other survivor. The visible result is distinct from what each gives the conversation. V10 contains Airi’s account of asking why Yūki enjoys the games and hearing a refusal of exactly that explanation. “That is why I am here” hangs over her own continuing interest. At the time, readers see the end of a candidate lethal encounter and later recognize bodies and survivors. Only much later do they learn the reason Airi sometimes comes back to the other woman’s example. The effect does not require a completed relationship in which each can rely on the other's preference or a wish to tell her private aspirations. In the later near-fatal exchange Yūki’s interest in whether Airi's leg still works makes a particular form of regard observable. Earlier initiating speech and that regard are grounds to treat Yūki as someone Airi knows. They are weak grounds to suppose she knows what her own conduct has meant to Airi. V01 S15 P1162–1181, V10 S16 P0147–0155 and S23 P0068–0092/P0116–0129. [A late disclosure about earlier interest](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e012--airi-sets-a-term-of-her-own).

At the workshop before Cloudy Beach, Airi and Yūki can talk of exact consequences of a former visit while she is meeting the need for skilled maintenance. Airi has experience of a storm delaying her postgame pickup, and Maguma was there and has a different sort of memory of the same event. In Cloudy Beach Airi first gets Yūki to come out and take part in a practical inquiry. She can teach a way to know the sky and use that knowledge together. She maintains real informational advantage about bodily devices for a time, partly because she can see that sharing may reduce her protection relative to the others. Maguma has figured out something relevant about her own body in private. She can say an intruder should try a different target and can stay outside a collective inquiry. Her influence on the terrain and the clues seen on her body nonetheless shape how the other women can act. Yūki first misjudges both their motives and means and changes course when the material observations support it. The survivors’ arrangement has active reciprocity in part because they permit local truths about both protection and distrust. Automatic rescue, complete candor and being all in the same faction are different conditions. V03 S12 P0139–0207, S15 P0007–0088, S19 P0130–0358/P0362–0442, S21 P0095–0108/P0402–0455. Maguma’s assigned agent later asks whether Yūki is her favorite. Maguma privately preferred that Yūki survive the confrontation with Etsusei and is concerned about her right eye. She resists the agent’s more general characterization. The distinction fits her claim to act independently. There is enough prior practical regard to permit herself a concern without thereby making her a passive admirer or an attached person whose help requires no terms. Yūki and Maguma’s later #65 pairing makes differences in fear and irritation observable under shared game conditions. The first approach to the hotel is Yūki and Akane’s visit to rescue Ramona. Maguma takes part when they return to that site during the Red Bear conflict. Akane has already supplied her civilian name. Maguma asks Yūki to call her Shaguma there. Maguma uses the old advertising banner to clear the entrance for the group. It is also distinct from turning every relationship with a veteran into enlisted membership. Maguma refuses Shiro’s group when she is approached and chooses temporary cooperation at sea. Airi’s assent to the group comes with a different reason and an explicit view of what she agreed to try. ## What an alliance binds

Airi has new work near Shiro without becoming the sort of player who wants office. At sea it is possible for Airi to recognize the skill of an officer and decline the leadership post suggested to her. She and Maguma can coordinate more closely than people who share a formal group by depending on what they can each do. Maguma hears a carefully truthful count. She knows how the bodies of experienced competitors show gradual loss and explains why she might leave. Airi finds that a surprisingly personal conversation from somebody known for ignoring routine social needs. The conversation changes the career's future as they picture it. No group loyalty alone could have supplied that change or guaranteed the degree of cooperation that follows. V10 S19 P0101–0118/P0146–0192/P0394–0456/P0557–0603.

The affiliation at sea makes Yūki an enemy to both. Airi’s immediate, increasingly irrational-seeming desire to find her comes paired with her own exceptionally careful way of anticipating how another fighter sees a scene. Help from Maguma and actual withdrawal after injury have distinct capacities. Airi cannot simply follow one movement Maguma manages, and Yūki manages things neither one predicted. Against a more derived state of attention that gives Yūki a great practical advantage in the ensuing conflict, Airi wants to know if she has broken. She hears a voice that sounds, to her, more affectionate than a reply to an enemy need be. Then Airi judges there is no longer anything to say. A friend in the sentimental sense cannot generally be deduced from the choice either makes about following the other into danger. People who have observed each other's habits for years can grasp a dangerous action without gaining mutual consent to the new conditions. V10 S19 P0479–0603 and S23 P0068–0092/P0116–0149/P0182–0216.

## The same risk receives three responses

Airi chooses to tell people to fire while she keeps her hold. Shiro blocks the plan, which Airi interprets in part through the difference between a risky collaborator and someone Mikkai needs for a future challenge. Maguma makes an independent reading from the held body's arrangement and the danger they know about. She shoots, afterwards says aloud that Airi's conduct could hardly mean anything else, and treats her as a person entitled to make an appalling choice. The original still leaves hearing the exact request uncertain. Shiro returns gunfire toward Maguma to protect the current ally. Yūki acts in accordance with what she repeatedly says she wants, to live. Her next physical response puts Airi in danger of death. Distinguishing the three acts avoids making actual regard, allegiance and enforcement into synonyms. V10 S23 P0068–0092/P0133–0178/P0182–0216/P0268–0277. [Independent decisions about Airi’s bid](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e012--airi-sets-a-term-of-her-own).

The previous choice does not give Maguma a continuing warrant to take every risk with the same woman. When Airi is incapacitated, Maguma decides to carry her to a higher part of the ship instead of extending the pursuit. Airi subsequently has reason to revise her estimate of someone who had looked like a fixed solitary competitor. Maguma asks for cooperation against a new danger and says caring for companions is something she could previously do. Persistent ability and changed local practice explain more than assuming she suddenly develops a general friendliness. She can share Airi’s hypothesis about Yūki's dangerous destination without subsequently undertaking the same chase. Airi then interprets Yūki's actual apparent success as a kind of accomplished career. It gives her a reason to stop, although there is no shared description or later response from Yūki. Maguma has her own medical and social reasons for stopping and has articulated them before this event. The parallel leaves space for a person to come to a similar decision via a different account of what matters. V10 S19 P0171–0192, S23 P0182–0216/P0268–0277/P0384–0459, S26 P0026–0028. [Individual appetites and exits](../readings/SHIBOYUGI_V10_DEEP_READING.md#sby-v10-e015--the-several-meanings-of-leaving).

The comparison supplies no future bond or confirmed individual departures. It supports a less general result. Intermittent acquaintance allows anticipation, local aid and the recognition of limits. Magnifying a person's example can give a life independent shape while leaving that person ignorant of the effect. Making the risk physical forces each party to find out which duties she will actually exercise. The relational evidence is richer than a static set of alliances because the judges of risk change in each scene. These results are expected in the relation's case material, with no need to invent long periods of unreported intimacy to explain how a few actual exchanges matter.
