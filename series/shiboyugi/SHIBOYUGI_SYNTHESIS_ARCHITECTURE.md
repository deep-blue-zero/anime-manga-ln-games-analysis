# Synthesis architecture

## 1. Purpose, authority and proportionality

This architecture governs how future readings of **Playing Death Games to Put Food on the Table / 死亡遊戯で飯を食う。** accumulate into a recoverable literary corpus. It belongs to SBY-INITIAL, depends on the [method](SHIBOYUGI_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md), and is routed by the sole [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md). No earlier project architecture is superseded.

It satisfies the sixteen responsibilities of the [initiation policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md) through the numbered sections below. Its authority is bootstrap design and attributed source metadata, not interpretation of unread prose. Architectural availability, analytical readiness, validation, publication and owner run authorization are distinct.

Ten available Japanese novel volumes justify preserving state and arguments from the first future unit, before later evidence could erase earlier uncertainty. They do not justify a separate file for every possible theme, person, institution or game. Two cumulative ledgers and one source register are enough initially. A dedicated reconstruction contract is justified by the campaign's intended future character responsibility, not by a claim that any character is already modelable.

## 2. Source model

The [source register](SHIBOYUGI_SOURCE_REGISTER.md) defines ten supplied witness slots, SBY-JP-V01-W01 through SBY-JP-V10-W01. It records electronic-bonus title labels, live name/size agreement, inherited audit results and package warnings, while keeping all narrative unadmitted. Numbered packaging suggests a candidate main-volume backbone; verified publication order, story chronology and internal section structure remain open.

The source topology is initially one proposed Japanese novel track with unclassified internal components. Main prose, bonus narrative, illustrations, afterwords and previews receive distinct component and admission classes when inspected under an authorized scope. A future bonus is not treated as another main volume or automatically read after the file's final main chapter. Its disclosure frontier must be established.

Other editions, languages and adaptations are outside this boundary. A later comparison track requires witness identity, explicit admission, source precedence and a recoverable alignment; it cannot silently become a second canonical root or overwrite the novel's account. A shared title or apparent parallel scene does not establish identical continuity. Narration and participant assertions remain source-layered evidence, not automatically world facts.

Technical audit claims and actual inspection are separate. The supplied audit reports language and integrity results; this project matched metadata only. The inherited audit cannot close a later reader's source-access, language, illustration or coverage obligation.

## 3. Sequential contribution contract

A future volume reading contributes more than an isolated essay. It supplies a complete narrative account within its boundary, sustained causal/formal interpretation, exact evidence routes, directed state changes, material claim transitions, forecast dispositions, coverage closure and an immutable exit state.

A near-front **Current-unit story synopsis — unit-end spoilers** answers what actually happens, including the unit's ending where established. It follows concise witness and entering-context material and precedes extended interpretation. Finalize it after all applicable prospective captures are frozen. It may not disclose a later unadmitted unit or silently turn testimony into fact. Concrete scenes and physical mechanics remain visible; thematic abstractions do not replace the event sequence. Stable observation references provide unobtrusive retrieval.

The synopsis is distinct from the causal account, the claim register and the cumulative ledgers. The causal account explains changes and alternative explanations; the synopsis enables independent human comprehension. Neither licenses a future or earlier-horizon reader to cross their assigned knowledge boundary.

The closure transaction consists of the full reading plus source coverage, state/evidence deltas or reasoned no-change decisions, claim and forecast changes, exit freeze and entrypoint/high-water synchronization. No next source is admitted merely because the essay is drafted. The method owns the detailed unit acceptance procedure.

## 4. Day-one cumulative homes

The **source register** owns witness identity, availability versus admission, component scope, source obligations and the route to private exact-byte receipts. It contains meaningful current metadata and open obligations, not empty source-lock placeholders.

The **state and evidence ledger** owns cumulative subject identity, character/relationship/knowledge changes, event-versus-disclosure position, and recurring constraints when the source earns them. These dimensions share one home initially because a single action can change what a person knows, how they relate to another person and what choices are available. The ledger indexes full readings and retains short explanatory deltas; it does not replace their arguments.

The **claim and forecast ledger** owns claim history, competing explanations, evidentiary revisions, downstream dependency routing and immutable forecasts with separate adjudications. Its responsibility differs from describing current state: it explains why the current interpretation differs from an earlier one and where the complete argument survives.

The **reconstruction contract** governs later derived use and records today's zero-evidence readiness boundary. It does not create per-character models, evidence dossiers or performance claims now. Named subject records are created only from actual admitted evidence.

No standalone rulebook, economic ledger, timeline, voice ledger, motif index or readiness spreadsheet is initialized. If a recurring dimension cannot be retrieved faithfully through the two ledgers and full readings, an architecture amendment can split a bounded home with a migration crosswalk and preserved IDs. This avoids both premature ontology and later loss of longitudinal evidence.

## 5. Character and relationship synthesis

Character development and relationships are intended analytical responsibilities. The readings must distinguish dispositions, states, knowledge, situational pressures, roles and relationship conditioning, including ordinary-life evidence where the source supplies it. Current plans do not presume any particular subject or cast hierarchy.

An independent character monograph is earned when evidence across multiple situations or developmental boundaries supports a sustained explanatory problem that would be lost in a general synthesis. It must integrate mechanisms, diachrony, interiority access, Japanese voice, directed relationships, formal presentation, material counterevidence and rival readings. A short appearance or an attractive archetype alone does not earn a monograph. Supporting subjects can remain in source-bounded ensemble or relationship analysis.

A relationship specialist is earned when a directed relationship has independently accumulating changes or asymmetries that cannot be understood by placing two monographs side by side. It explains each person's knowledge, expectations, dependencies and choices, the mechanisms of change and persistent disagreement. An ensemble synthesis becomes appropriate only when group-level configuration adds an explanatory responsibility beyond dyads.

The [reconstruction contract](SHIBOYUGI_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_CONTRACT.md) separates literary monograph, evidence record, operational model, validation record and use acceptance. A persuasive literary account does not certify predictive validity. A model never becomes evidence for its own source character.

## 6. Specialist responsibilities: required by evidence, anticipated by design

Narration/disclosure and character/relationship integration are required responsibilities of the eventual literary synthesis, because this project is a prose-reading and character-oriented corpus. They may be fulfilled within a proportionate integrated work or by independently justified specialists. Responsibility does not imply an empty specialist file today.

Three additional domains are **anticipated design questions**, not established themes:

- If repeated rule-like assertions and consequential enforcement appear, examine who states a rule, who believes it, how it is tested, its scope and exceptions, and what uncertainty does to decisions. Do not assume a game-system ontology, cycles or a stable institution.
- If livelihood, resources, work, reward, risk or dependency recur, examine actual constraints and reported constraints, alternatives and costs, and how these interact with character choices. The title does not establish economic mechanisms.
- If repeated tensions between moral judgment, survival, responsibility or narrative framing emerge, analyze the competing reasons and formal treatment without equating depiction or successful action with endorsement.

A domain earns mandatory specialist treatment when it recurs, carries independent evidence, materially changes other conclusions and cannot be handled adequately inside existing homes. Record why, its admitted boundary, exact responsibility, dependencies, output path, evidence gaps and acceptance test. A planned domain that the source does not support is retired explicitly. No unit is forced to populate every anticipated category.

Illustration/prose relations, textual variants or adaptation divergence can become specialists only after the relevant evidence is admitted and inspected. There is no mandatory music, acted voice or audiovisual layer for this prose boundary.

## 7. Evidence routing and full argument retrieval

The deterministic route is: current entrypoint → current specialist or reading → stable claim/evidence anchor → witness/component locator → exact evidence-plane receipt. Search hits must pass an authority and source-boundary check before use.

Full readings own detailed observations and interpretations. Stable evidence IDs identify real source-backed records; stable claim IDs identify bounded propositions and their history. The state ledger links a change to evidence and full explanatory prose. The claim ledger links every current formulation to its prior formulation, supporting and contrary evidence, current argument and downstream dependents.

A mature claim cannot be represented solely by a confidence label, a table row or an isolated quotation. Its argument must retain premises, causal or interpretive steps, material alternative explanations and limits. A compact synthesis-dispatch index is a router into the complete corpus, not a replacement for it.

Japanese quotations remain brief and exact where load-bearing; working transcriptions and glosses are labeled. Source media, full prose extractions, private acquisition names, object IDs and raw receipts remain outside Git. A retrieval failure is recorded against affected claims; a missing locator is not invented to fill the table.

## 8. Claim history, correction and supersession

Use PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT and OPEN with an attributable revision record. Each material change preserves the earlier formulation and full argument route, states the new evidence and strongest counterevidence, explains the change, marks its source/knowledge boundary and identifies affected downstream homes.

The [claim ledger](SHIBOYUGI_CLAIM_AND_FORECAST_LEDGER.md) contains a mutable current routing view and append-only substantive history. Revising the current view never deletes a rejected interpretation, its reasons or its historical validity boundary. Competing supported readings may coexist without a fabricated consensus.

Accepted sequential readings, entering/exit freezes and forecasts remain historical evidence. Correct them through a labeled erratum or current claim revision; do not rewrite them as if the reader always knew the later answer. Supersession between artifacts uses reciprocal repository paths and one current sink under the [archive policy](../../governance/source-policies/ARCHIVE_AUTHORITY_AND_SUPERSESSION_POLICY.md). A version increment is not permission to alter sealed bytes.

A closure of source admission does not forbid legitimate correction within already admitted evidence. It does forbid acquiring new narrative authority through an erratum. Exact closure and run rules remain owned by the current corpus policies.

## 9. Time, development, knowledge and continuity

Every material state record separates source position, disclosed event time where established, reader frontier, subject knowledge and current retrospective boundary. Unknown event chronology stays unknown. Do not turn late disclosure of an old disposition into a new development or import a current retrospective account into an earlier freeze.

Directed relations, aliases, roles, resources and temporary conditions are state-qualified. A relationship mode observed with one person does not automatically apply to another. A reported rule may be known to a narrator but not to a participant. A belief can motivate action while remaining false or unresolved.

If a later admitted source establishes alternate frames, branches, resets or other distinct states, qualify records by the actual evidence and amend the architecture only as needed. None of those devices is assumed at bootstrap. An adaptation or translated witness cannot resolve the novel's uncertainty without a declared cross-source claim.

## 10. Contradiction and cross-source handling

First test identity, source version, locator and scope. Then distinguish epistemic disagreement, chronology, unreliable testimony, narration, material exceptions, edition variants, component boundaries and genuine inconsistency. Use the smallest diagnostic source review that can resolve the disputed claim within current authorization.

Do not harmonize every disagreement into one coherent hidden rule, and do not treat a conflict as proof of authorial intent. Retain incompatible readings and the evidence each explains when resolution is unavailable. Mark whether the conflict blocks a central argument, one specialist, a model use or only optional enrichment.

A newly verified source variant does not rewrite the original witness. It receives a separate record; findings are scoped to the version that supports them. If resolving a contradiction needs an unadmitted unit, leave the claim open and retain the source obligation rather than crossing the boundary.

## 11. Dependency and convergence order

The operative dependency sequence is:

    reviewed foundation and authorized source scope
      → exact witness/component binding and capability verification
      → ordered unit readings with synchronized cumulative state
      → authorized-boundary completion and architecture/role-gap audit
      → claim/evidence stabilization and frozen synthesis snapshot
      → earned monographs, relationship studies and specialists
      → cross-specialist convergence and required evidence completion
      → integrated literary synthesis
      → scoped validation, acceptance and any authorized release

Character reconstruction branches from an adequate state-qualified literary evidence base. A model can be developed at an explicitly bounded intermediate state, but it does not replace later literary synthesis or certify untested states. Operational use remains separately gated. Later source admission requires its own authorization even if a model's test plan names it.

A specialist may challenge another specialist's premise. Record the evidence and revision dependency, reopen only affected arguments and review dependent outputs before acceptance. Do not append a contradictory note while retaining an unchanged central conclusion. Preserve unaffected substantial prose and historical freezes.

## 12. Architecture amendment and review

Review after the first complete authorized unit, after an actual early tranche or major disclosure boundary, when a new source class is proposed, when repeated observations lack a sufficient home, before specialist dispatch and before integrated synthesis. The first review tests whether the proposed unit, combined ledger and design questions fit the actual source.

An amendment records the problem, evidence, changed responsibility, exact homes, preserved identifiers, affected claims, required backfill and authority consequence. Split a ledger only when its independent retrieval burden warrants it. Add a specialist only when it has a distinct question and evidentiary dependency. Do not add a new directory because another series has one.

INITIAL becomes EVOLVING when source-grounded findings justify adaptation. STABILIZED requires evidence that the mature responsibilities are substantially understood. A release-frozen architecture requires a successor version for material change. Neither source inventory size nor a completed first draft changes lifecycle automatically.

Backfill is bounded to material gaps. Try complete existing readings and retained evidence routes first; reread only the necessary authorized source. Do not rerun the corpus merely to adopt a cleaner template.

## 13. Completion gates

**Foundation review.** Method, architecture, source distinctions, day-one homes, responsibility matrix and retrieval routes are substantively adequate; current authority metadata is valid; independent review addresses material defects. This is not narrative authorization.

**Single-unit closure.** Complete admitted coverage, synopsis, analysis, source locators, cumulative transaction, claim and forecast disposition, exit freeze and acceptance checks agree. Unresolved required evidence blocks the affected closure claim; optional enrichment remains visibly optional.

**Authorized source-boundary completion.** The specified run reaches its actual terminal unit, every unit transaction is closed, the committed high-water state is consistent and no next candidate is authorized. Completion of an acquired V01–10 tranche does not establish the official series endpoint.

**Longitudinal reconciliation.** Subject identities, directional relations, chronology/disclosure, knowledge, constraint evidence, revision history and unresolved disagreements can be traced through the boundary. No important recurrent observation is orphaned and no ledger quietly overwrites an earlier state.

**Specialist readiness.** Conduct the architecture/role-gap audit; define earned specialists and explicit non-specialist destinations; retrieve complete readings and arguments; identify required versus optional channels; stabilize material claims enough for each assigned question. Open claims can be carried honestly; unresolved necessary evidence cannot be hidden.

**Integrated-synthesis readiness.** The needed specialist responsibilities are complete for their scopes, dependencies and contradictions are reconciled or bounded, counterarguments remain available and source channels supporting central claims are inspected. Sequential completion alone never satisfies this gate.

**Validation and acceptance.** Review source-boundary fidelity, full arguments, representative locators and contested/high-impact evidence, named-reference clarity, preservation, and required-channel debts. Reconstruction validation and independent evaluator capability assessment remain separate from literary acceptance and owner-authorized use.

**Freeze/release.** Confirm scope, stable authority routes, exact retained artifacts, approved limitations and appropriate archival metadata. Apply current repository run-stop/terminal-closure rules when relevant; do not create an empty closure record for this zero-reading bootstrap. Release of a foundation does not open source admission.

## 14. Reasoning, stage ownership and handoff

Use BOUNDED_STANDARD for stable source mapping, SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS for future unit readings and interpretive deltas, DEEP_SYNTHESIS for checkpoints, mature monographs, specialists and models, and PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST for load-bearing architecture, difficult contradiction adjudication and final convergence. Mechanical hashing uses tools. A class is not a model name, runtime capability or approval.

Prefer local source-capable sequential execution and cumulative maintenance; fresh fully supplied sessions for substantial synthesis; then local evidence completion and designated repository integration. Either adequately grounded environment may author architecture. This bootstrap is local because its current metadata, policies and private evidence are available here. Future capabilities must be observed per stage; no model label certifies Japanese text or image access.

A substantial handoff follows the [contract](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md): one operational start-here packet, canonical artifact identities/target paths, permitted changes, recoverable authorization, exact base, complete output files and hashes, full required inputs, admitted and inspected boundaries, unresolved obligations with owners, preservation rules and next action. Transfer the full argument, not a summary of it.

The receiver verifies exact bytes, reads full outputs, compares base/candidate/current state, classifies drift and accepts named remaining work before altering a live target. Receipt acceptance, semantic acceptance, repository validation and authority promotion are separate. A fresh session may not overwrite newer ledgers or replace developed prose with a thin recap. Publication remains with the authorized integrator; CI or the owner merges, and specialized registries remain with their designated agents.

## 15. Mutable state and historical retention

The entrypoint, source register and current ledger routing views are mutable within their declared scope. Substantive ledger history is append-only. Forecasts, prospective captures and accepted sequential artifacts retain their historical bytes and knowledge boundaries; corrections attach through explicit errata or successors.

A mature monograph or model has a bounded version and evidence basis. Preserve the predecessor when revising it, with reciprocal supersession and a visible current route. Do not delete materially distinct interpretation because it is older, and do not let a historical “definitive” title override current authority metadata.

Keep operational campaign status, commit IDs and workflow IDs in their actual receipts/history, not in this entrypoint's prose. Private transfer packages and primary evidence never become parallel public analytical authority.
