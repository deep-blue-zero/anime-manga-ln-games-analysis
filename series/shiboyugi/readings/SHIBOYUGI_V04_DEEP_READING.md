## Current-unit story synopsis

On the eighth day of Cloudy Beach, Maguma boards the rescue vessel and sleeps in a cabin. Her agent says the game has ended and reports that Etsusei is dead after going up against Yūki. Yūki herself has arrived without obvious major injuries. Maguma is relieved, although she does not want her agent to describe Yūki as a favorite. She remarks that one of Yūki's eyes has become whiter since they last met.

After forty-seven clears, Yūki tells her agent someone at evening school has been watching her and looking through her belongings. At school she is Sorimachi Yūki, a student whose absences, injuries and effort to hide three artificial fingers attract attention. The agent claims the operator can make someone forget the discovery, but warns that Yūki would have to leave school. Honezuka Hitomi, the curious classmate, enlists casual information from her twin friends, Hiyori and Kazami Amano, then follows Yūki home. Hitomi unexpectedly throws the agent who comes to identify her and discovers the route to Yūki's apartment. Yūki takes her injured agent home and then has a stand-in attend school in her place. When Hitomi returns to search the apartment, Yūki is waiting.

Yūki asks Hitomi to stop and offers to let her go. Hitomi feigns agreement, fights Yūki and opens the closet. The stored game costumes unlock recollections of her own career. She had stopped near thirty after a friend's death and had asked the operator to change her memory. Practical skills and a lingering attraction survived. Yūki accepts the explanation and lets Hitomi leave. The agent says Yūki can probably keep attending, subject to verification. Hitomi decides to keep her memories and her student life. When she calls her old teacher Kirihara, Kokone recognizes her under the old alias 一巳 (ヒトミ) and reports that someone killed Kirihara the month before.

The story jumps back to after #44 and Yūki's meeting with Hakushi. Her agent has told her a story circulating through other agents about a recent game with eighty entrants and three survivors. Yūki asks the artisan, who refers her to a retired player and tattooist, Kanami Kirihara. Keito, whom Yūki needs to be reminded she played with in Scrap Building, has independently heard from a survivor. The game was Garbage Prison and a player with tattoos on both arms devastated it near the end. Outside Kirihara's home, their questions and the lack of a reply lead them and the household attendants Haine and Kokone to enter. They find Kirihara dead. Keito calls people connected with the operator, proposes finding and killing the suspect outside a game, and enlists the twins. Yūki declines. Her agent, contacted by the artisan, awaits her with an arranged eye examination.

The tattooed player is Shion, fourteen years old. Once a member of Kyara's household, Shion killed repeatedly to prevent discovery in Garbage Prison and later silenced people who could identify her, including Kirihara. Keito's group tracks her down and wounds her. Shion tries to invoke her retirement. Her agent, who earlier refused help for that reason, returns with an invitation to Halloween Night. Shion accepts and is carried away under the operator's protection. Haine obtains an invitation by calling her own agent, while Kokone receives no invitation. When Yūki is independently invited, she already knows her right eye is deteriorating and says she wants to keep her usual pace.

In the pumpkin field, costumed figures ask each player for sweets. Those unable to give an intact sweet in time face lethal pursuit. Ordinary players discover the condition through encounters, and some begin taking other players' sweets. Haine finds Shion, attacks her and breaks her leg. When they lose their baskets, Shion happens upon one sweet in her sleeve and avoids the costumed child's first attack. A late sweet fails to stop the child once it has declared Trick, and Haine dies. Shion fights successive groups until she is severely injured and entirely without sweets. Yūki accepts half a fleeing player's supplies in exchange for help against eight pursuers. After the rescue, Tamamo asks to be Yūki's pupil. Yūki gives the pretense of a first lesson and runs away.

Near dawn, Shion and Yūki meet beside Haine's body and discuss revenge and the cost of following a purpose. Shion tells Yūki her career aim looks like a slow suicide. Yūki sees the possible ruin and chooses to continue. Shion takes comfort from the reply and attacks to get her sweets. She exploits Yūki's weak right-side vision. A gathering of costumed pursuers interrupts their fight, and neither can satisfy the full demand with the sweets at hand. Yūki calls a temporary truce and both make for the same exit. Shion attempts to sacrifice Yūki as a distraction, which Yūki anticipates. The counter knocks Shion toward the pursuers. Yūki hears bones break but does not see the actual finish. She herself is pinned until recorded birdsong announces morning and the pursuer releases her. She has cleared #45.

Kokone finds Yūki after the game and thanks her for Shion's defeat. She obtained access as a spectator and watched the outcome her sister could not report. She also offers to arrange contact with a retired player who once competed while blind. Over about a month Yūki clears #46 and #47 without a further reported decline in the eye. While she is preparing to address the surveillance at school, Tamamo appears outside her apartment, transformed in appearance and ready for the second lesson she took Yūki to have promised.

## The two lives in Schoolmate

### SBY-V04-E001 — Who knows how Cloudy Beach ended

The epigraph says there are people who cannot fit in even within a world of outsiders. The opening first shows a comparatively settled outsider among people she rarely wants to rely on. The previous book left Maguma unverified in Yūki's closing knowledge. Here P0003 directly narrates her boarding. Maguma's agent then reports Etsusei's death and qualifies the attribution that she was killed in her attack on Yūki with ようです. This is materially stronger than Yūki's earlier uncertainty. The reporter's inspection route and the whole evacuation roster remain undescribed. The initial announcement of the game's end fixes much less than all the ancillary results. S13 P0004; S15 P0001–0011; V03 S23 P0003–0006.

The agent lightly calls Yūki Maguma's お気に入り. Maguma says shared style and some acquaintance account for the affinity and names her motto 唯我独尊. The following interior sentence admits a specific preference between Yūki and Etsusei, based in part on what she disliked about the latter's revealed practice. Her concern about the eye shows she had attended to an apparently peripheral change while there was still combat to do. The affection is neither the agent's phrase alone nor an unrestricted pledge to favor any acquaintance. The comparison of whiteness has its own later corroboration. S15 P0012–0023.

### SBY-V04-E002 — Sorimachi Yūki, an exposed routine

The car scene introduces gratitude phrased as a general reflection on individuals and their circumstances. Yūki's trouble from school is also hard to ask about, and her agent spots the transition. Yūki started attending after Candle Woods and fought to gain basic education. She is still struggling to keep up with ordinary schoolwork. Monthly absences, minor cuts outside the scope of medical restoration, inability to take routine health screenings after the operator's treatment, and conscientious concealment of her fingers accumulate into a visible pattern. The same firm privacy intended to protect the school helps Hitomi read her as someone belonging elsewhere. Yūki responds and avoids taking the initiative socially. The shift between 幽鬼 and 反町友樹 accompanies changes in focal access and addressee. It keeps the same person visible under real competing practices. S17 P0003–0038/P0060–0088.

S19 opens the apartment to another kind of witness. Yūki has enough savings to move, but likes the shabby room she knows. School books and sports clothes remain out. She has become alert to garbage days and keeps valuables accessible. She stores more than forty game outfits, with some early sets already discarded. The closet which she reads as a personal record and safeguard is precisely the threshold Hitomi's desire will cross. The routine is laborious. A week absent in one setting and an extra day to see Hakushi put Yūki behind in several subjects. Later she experiences catching up through deliberate review as genuine relief. The prose supplies grounds for care and attachment to ordinary life alongside the long game aim. The limited “one day” marketing schedule provides a visually distinct occasion with mathematics frustrations, sitcoms, park practice, errands and a late return to sleep. S19 P0007–0019/P0596–0606; S02-I01.

### SBY-V04-E003 — The private scope of protection

The operator's secrecy norm is stated directly. Yūki is allowed to deal with people in adjacent work such as her artisan, and says the evidence is kept away from school. “Have them forget” first produces an inference of killing. Her agent explicitly corrects it to memory processing and adds removal from school to prevent renewed exposure. In this scene these are capabilities and consequences asserted about a hypothetical further event. The agent's immediate reply to the late request and failed attempt to identify the follower then mark a different use of staff authority. An absent public identity is also a practical limitation: after the agent's injury, she stops Yūki from calling an ordinary hospital because she says agents have no family-register existence. Yūki's decision to offer a spare guest futon treats this as the recovery of a familiar person as well as an occupational problem. A third party occupies the school seat in the subsequent deception. Yūki says she has arranged that stand-in and the twins mistake the stand-in for her. Its precise identity or means of disguise remains outside the narration. S17 P0037–0056/P0132–0140/P0158–0224/P0285–0292.

### SBY-V04-E004 — Hitomi and the knowledge in the body

Hitomi's lunch conversation initially presents an apparent member of an ordinary social group. She can reliably distinguish Hiyori and Kazami and has strong shared familiarity. Their tendency to finish one another's phrases is comic and they treat her announced pursuit as alarming, while still serving as her limited later school information channel. Their speculative labels for Yūki are the extremes to which unexplained but plain details lead them. The transition from a palpably racing heart to an unexpected calm changes the value of Hitomi's claim of purely ordinary curiosity. The first trained movement comes as a shock to her. She can act with an ability she does not recognize as hers and later ask whether she has a natural aptitude for behaviors she has already learned. The reader receives the act before either the agent or Yūki can reconstruct it. S17 P0068–0109/P0113–0156/P0158–0184/P0190–0218.

Hitomi crosses further limits than attempting to identify her distant peer. She judges her own proposed entry into the apartment illegal, recognizes that continuing defeats all obvious interests, and cannot make herself stop. Her prior public self, the unremarkable disgraced child of affluent parents in the memory now available to her, and her attachment to the neglected school goal put this compulsion in contrast. The narration supports the existence of that available autobiographical account. It gives no definite verdict about which family facts were fabricated or by whom. Yūki, who anticipated the final move, tells Hitomi she will overlook the intrusion but will not let her open the closet. Hitomi genuinely welcomes an opportunity to relinquish the pursuit, then consciously speaks its apparent acceptance and acts otherwise. Her internal discrepancy matters as much as her physical control. S17 P0235–0257/P0261–0307/P0309–0344.

The costumes are recognizable because Hitomi used to compete. That shift to recalled training and a formerly useful role permits recovery of a damaged self-image and her fear of returning to the game. The prior loss of a friend at #30 originated in an attributed report. The friend brought her into the trade when she needed work. According to Hitomi they initially profited and regarded success as talent and contingency. In her own account she chose removal of her memories to counter survivor guilt and a lingering financial and personal attachment. Her injury in the present gives a visually independent material clue, only a little white in the blood after treatment gradually wore off. Kokone's recognition of the former name 一巳 (ヒトミ) and acknowledgment of the erase corroborate the action indirectly. Prospective diagnosis of retained skill now has much stronger support than a general reading of obsessive curiosity. S17 P0342–0375/P0396–0411/P0442–0469.

Yūki openly calls the ability to give up a talent. Hitomi will keep both past and schooling, without replaying the medical or professional decision. Later lunch talk turns from varieties of cola to wanting ice cream while ill, with explicit relief that she can still sit with the twins. Confrontation with an old purpose permits her to name a needed present relationship. She briefly considers paying for the damage she did, then decides not to bring it up. Her ordinary claims and behavior contain kindness, evasion and a feeling of safety independent of the earlier physical accomplishment. No permanent promise that her decision will hold follows from that comfort. S17 P0376–0392/P0415–0438.

## City Scenario and the route to Garbage Prison

### SBY-V04-E005 — The labor of getting an account

S19's return to an earlier point puts a one-sentence homecoming ahead of its explanation. The phone call at V03 close is expanded from the same assigned agent's side, explicitly a chain of agents without corroboration. Average survival around 70 percent is an expectation in the exchange, powerful enough to frame eighty/three as an anomaly but unaccompanied by any population or calculation register. The possible reasons include design difficulty, poorly coordinated players and an offender like Kyara. The latter initially belongs to Yūki's fear and experience. She wants to investigate and does not know contract, market, volume of games, roster, institutional history or broad audience scale. She has a reputation and game acquaintances without a player address book. She can consider two retirees and is reluctant to rely on them. Her functional link to her own agent and her left fingers actually gives her a new route. S19 P0003–0019/P0023–0068.

The artisan is a hub for clients' questions and expressly declines to perform insider expertise. Yūki's newly sharpened expectation of a second prank leads to a minor cascade of mess in his workshop. He laughs and accepts shared responsibility. That lived retest supplies the counterweight to the similarity of the two arrivals. The visit opens a route into another trade even though the artisan can verify no survivor. An independent meeting by the gate involves a former participant Yūki barely remembers. Keito recognizes Yūki, has socially accumulated contacts and reached a survivor. The combined referral and report produce the case, whereas no single guide plans or controls all the discovery. S19 P0072–0134/P0161–0226.

### SBY-V04-E006 — One known escalation and an unknown tally

Keito gives the specific name Garbage Prison and the claimed account of evading guards in an ordinary exit game. She records how much doubt belongs even to the apparently clearest cue. The arms might have been marked before participation, by an outside route, in a recognizable trade, or by means that only resemble tattooing. Her ability to find a survivor changes the source chain. She is still relating the account to Yūki, with no direct listening inside that first interview. Later close access to Shion supplies the narrator's independent past for this same game. Shion describes exposure of a “bad habit,” killings to hide the earlier one, new witnesses and a rapid attempt to eliminate everyone else. This supplies an actionable cause by one offender. The exact first conduct, tally and motives of the guards are absent. “A few got away” establishes an escape plurality in Shion's account. The particular 80/3 still belongs to report. The off-game retaliation cannot be counted as an in-game collective cause. S19 P0205–0224/P0405–0416/P0450–0462.

The volume preserves likeness to Candle Woods while deliberately reopening provenance. Keito later obtains footage through an unnamed route, enough to associate a name and face and direct her coalition toward a geographic lead. Etsusei's access in V03 provides a comparable separate case. These disclosures establish that independently motivated players can reach recordings. They still reveal little about general spectator terms or a single route common to them. Kokone's later admission as a spectator is an independently voiced case with a different aim. S19 P0444–0462; S23 P0016–0033; V03 S19 P0003–0013 and S21 P0003–0009.

### SBY-V04-E007 — Red blood, death, and affiliation after retirement

Haine and Kokone identify themselves at the gate in alternating formal lines. Haine initially stands at the viewer's left and Kokone at the right. To Yūki they first look indistinguishable. S17 P0075 gives the initial cafeteria arrangement separately: Hiyori is at Hitomi's left and her older sister Kazami at the right. The household attendants can open the gate. Only Kirihara holds the front-door key. At the house Keito notices a window and the twins supply access. Familiarity with the proprietor, her previously documented artificial legs and the new sensory shock independently converge on the death. Yūki's certainty that even a badly damaged body would not disturb her fails at the unfamiliar experience of smell and red blood. Keito offers a conditional biological chronology in this fictional world: ordinary renewal has had years to remove the effects of prep treatment. Hitomi's only partial change at a different horizon is useful independent support for decay. No uniform duration or general biology follows. S19 P0226–0295/P0306–0313; S17 P0367–0370.

The search is structured through rival institutions and personal assumptions. Keito uses an operator-linked contact that promptly arrives. Yūki's uncertain question about ordinary police first sounds like a joke to her. The early conjecture that a killer is destroying traces gets later corroboration from Shion's own action and independently remembered work by Kirihara. Kirihara once liked tattooing as a hobby and adopted it after severe game injury near thirty. Hitomi received her as a mentor. Haine sees her as the older person who took two runaway minors into her house, permitting them to stop regular participation. Kirihara herself describes this as wanting help around the house, which leaves gratitude, care and hiring compatible without certifying the inaccessible motive. Those continuing ties explain why the same dead body sends some people back to games. S17 P0442–0469; S19 P0161–0165/P0299–0345/P0405–0408; S21 P0178–0197/P0237–0240.

### SBY-V04-E008 — When an invitation makes someone protectable

Keito's V02 talent for backing a perceived winner acquires a different shape in V04. Her contacts, interviewing, later recordings, material resources, leadership and independent proposal give her leverage. Her language can switch from convivial promotion to prepared defense and a clear decision to kill. That competence is not success by itself. She believes the players' own reality outside games permits a level of prearrangement a usual game with supposedly shared initial conditions would not. Shion’s choice of destination and injuries limit her ability to evade the group, and off-game protection changes the relevant costs again. The retrospective assertion at P0462 expresses Keito's understanding of game fairness, not a general guarantee of equal conditions. When given a face that might escape, Keito considers Shion's claim to stop sincere but cannot rely on it. The twins need something different. Keito can judge threats reduced enough despite losing the target, while the twins cannot recover Kirihara. S19 P0314–0354/P0444–0462/P0489–0494/P0514–0573.

Shion's agent has helped her maintain a life outside school or a facility as a fourteen-year-old player. She responds by phone with deliberate distance once her charge says she has retired. Then she arrives in person with an invitation and stakes her authority on the fact that Shion has just accepted orally. Her declared duty is safe passage for her entrant, and Keito's group treats interfering with this as opposing the operator. This is an exceptional opportunity to see an apparently abstract protection take effect off game. The specific invitation must be available, as the twins' independent calls show. Haine can join and accepts the foreseeable risk. Kokone receives no opening. In the car Shion and her agent return to the offer of a meal and the agent's claim of a financial benefit if her player clears thirty. The agent expressly invokes the bonus as her reason. Shion takes her near-thirty position as relevant and even offers to pay for the proposed meal, which the agent denies was sincere. Payment policy and actual emotional motive have separate standing; neither explains how she timed her arrival. S19 P0417–0440/P0500–0510/P0530–0592.

### SBY-V04-E009 — The eye becomes an actual limit

The artisan is experienced enough with physical recovery to treat a light iris as a changed condition. Earlier the apparent state was an established injury with vision restored. No particular diagnosis comes from his remark alone. He contacts the agent, and the agent arranges a hospital assessment. A retrospective aside had already described an irreversible loss beginning earlier and a deadline for the protagonist. The examination is then held back until the middle of Halloween Night, after Yūki's practical attempts to see with the right eye alone. The hospital identifies ongoing loss. Yūki links it to the old injury inflicted by Kyara and takes delayed poisoning as the explanation. The worst-case blindness is still conditional. Her medical team's and agent's report is enough for her to reconsider what her workshop mishap and dropped coins may mean. The causal contribution of sight to any such isolated lapse is a later self-attribution. S19 P0138–0157/P0359–0376/P0605–0618; S21 P0111–0113/P0681–0700.

At the first new invitation she says she will maintain two or three games a month because it balances physical recovery with the danger of losing her edge. Her hands already contain marked replacements, but she regards eye loss as a different material problem. Attempts to verify her function with one eye seem adequate in a static field, and beating the eight gives a more vigorous but still limited performance check. A single highly attentive opponent then identifies the impairment under harder conditions. The blow Yūki fails to perceive is the crucial demonstrated effect. When she finally recognizes the right side as a true limitation, she tries first to choose an approach that leaves use of her left eye. Later she expects a particular movement and uses its probability without seeing it. This is a specific adaptation made in the field by someone who has learned to mistrust her own unchanged sensation. It does not establish that the defect has improved. S21 P0427–0433/P0681–0706/P0872–0908/P0923–0948.

## Halloween Night

### SBY-V04-E010 — Trick, timing, and constructed scarcity

The repeated opening uses opponent comparison before the two meet. Shion has time to find and bite her cookie before first contact. Yūki wakes to footsteps and finds the same sort of basket when she follows the performers' eyes. Her private name for typically trustworthy game meals, ゲーム飯, expresses a habitual small pleasure. Both interpret objects and voices through the conventions of the trade. The unpleasant sweets are a local prop and finite expenditure. Early encounters demonstrate how to satisfy demands before either knows the termination condition. Costumed figures accept a sweet apiece and leave, while direct witnesses later see lethal pursuit when someone cannot comply. Haine finds another sweet after Trick is called and the attack does not stop. Shion discovers unwrapped or badly damaged sweets do not qualify in her own encounters. Participants try transfers, raids and recovery of previously dropped items. No complete account establishes how the costumed figures find targets, what modifications or selection they received, or the absence of every possible supply cache. S21 P0007–0076/P0084–0113/P0215–0219/P0288–0357/P0503–0512.

Yūki continues to consider the possibility of escape and finds no visible boundary exit. Dawn is a reasoned stopping hypothesis based on title, environment and a broad industry motif. Timekeeping in Cloudy Beach depended on help from Airi. Without her, Yūki can see gradual change and cannot turn it into a precise deadline. The supply's apparent necessity creates an incentive to accumulate even without an announced rule to plunder. Her projections from a usual survival rate are conjectural. The story tests their more useful qualitative component. Visible injuries make a player inviting as a source of scarce sweets. Continued fighting compounds the injury and therefore the chance of being selected again. Shion's specialist ability can defeat multiple attackers but cannot break this loop. The eight with fewer sweets after losing to Yūki also have reason to take the chance. At the end, artificial birdsong announces the start of morning and a pursuing child releases Yūki. This establishes the actual stop for this witnessed chase alongside earlier demonstrated local enforcement. S21 P0395–0433/P0511–0566/P0570–0604/P0620–0654/P0673–0682/P0907–0925/P0959–0977.

### SBY-V04-E011 — Haine acts, Kokone watches

Haine's ordinary register can function as professional composure and tactical performance. She has actual ties with her sister and the woman who took them in. The account refuses to specify the reason she and Kokone left home, while giving Haine's severe inward rejection of general parental gratitude. Independent support for food and shelter had seemed to require lifesaving industry participation. Kirihara provides a private household alternative. When Haine follows an invitation specifically to kill Shion, she is accepting mutually opposed practical ends. She wants to do it with her own hands and is prepared to die. This moves her away from preserving sweets, seeking information, or saving enough effort to return. The switch from her deferential introduction to a harsher accusation in her first contact with Shion is possible because she has been practicing both styles. She makes real initial gains, but punctuating the fight with claims about earned or appropriate death supplies no privilege under its actual rule. Her intense final thought that she has lived more sincerely cannot substitute for the missed return window. S21 P0121–0174/P0178–0219/P0221–0278/P0288–0357.

The loss has a different shape for Kokone. Her brief hesitation when Haine promises to catch their target leaves her facing uncertainty without an invitation of her own. The later encounter supplies an active alternative. Kokone approaches the organization as a spectator because she wants to see both outcomes. Showing Yūki the formal respect associated with the house does not make her grief go away. Her repeated gratitude makes Yūki reconsider Shion's difficult assertion about revenge and the large amount of uncertainty bundled into a promised success. She offers a helpful contact whose authority and provenance are separate from her witness to the game. The sisters' loyalty is mutual in the present evidence, but their actual routes after loss and the weight they give particular outcomes remain distinct. S19 P0552–0573; S23 P0015–0050.

### SBY-V04-E012 — Shion's ways of making and losing a home

Before any confrontation with Yūki, Shion is seen in a familiar shopping street with a favorite inexpensive croquette stall and memories of coming there since childhood. The price and ordinary repetition are part of a life whose appearance is deceptively inconspicuous to her. Anger at a stranger brings her close to acting and she suppresses it because public violence would expose her. Her position as a minor also cuts down what she can do despite her earnings. She relies on agent support for housing and ordinary legitimacy, worries that she cannot book a place to stay and tries to present herself as a pupil on her way to class. This independent access gives the later claim of social exile real costs without explaining every detail of why she kills. S19 P0380–0401/P0412–0440/P0468–0484.

The attack at Halloween Night prompts a past "first" different from the public event that made her known. Shion says her parents could greatly hurt her with words and gives no precise incident to outsiders. Killing them is described in her own interior as the first achievement that felt like freedom. The thing which subsequently persists is the drive to recapture that feeling. A game could promise sanction and normality for a hidden habit. She comes to see an organized society even where life is treated lightly and remains compelled to hide her conduct. Shion calls this a curse. What she says about universal rejection is strong evidence of her present worldview and immediate alternatives within her history. It is less strong as an unqualified judgment about every other person. The volume does not have to exonerate her to take that view seriously. S21 P0608–0616/P0635–0648.

Kyara has an actual household with disparate residents. Through Shion the reader sees Moegi trying to change an ordinary tendency to obey others, and Hizumi responding to Shion's pranks with alarming anger. Her nickname for the interplay and her valuation of what it teaches her belong to her own history. The existence of the relationship does not give everyone who lived there Shion's goals or knowledge. Kyara taught Shion practical lessons and received discussion of her life. Shion treats her as a teacher in effect while distinguishing formal submission. She remembers the household as the happiest period she had known and needs no direct future promise for the loss of it to affect her. Moegi and Kyara's reported ends precede a more recent report about Hizumi. The final prediction that a putatively improving society will abolish people like herself expresses what defeat has made believable to her. The reader can recover Hizumi's actual death elsewhere without borrowing that confidence. S21 P0628–0648; V03 S21 P0018–0023.

### SBY-V04-E013 — Recognition and the false truce

The physically depleted Shion encounters Yūki after exercising the little control she still has. The group that stripped her of sweets had stopped short of killing her. Her recognition of the starting place comes from the cookie she discarded earlier. The same unpleasantness becomes a final ability to move when it can no longer buy safety. She intends to hit the body of her former opponent. Yūki notices a resemblance to Moegi's determined face before Shion exposes her arms. In the exchange, Yūki hears Shion claim Haine's death as a victory. The reader knows the costumed figure delivered the fatal attack and how Shion contributed to the outcome. That gap matters because the final mutual acknowledgment occurs between people who cannot know everything the book has let the reader see. S21 P0652–0669/P0735–0744/P0748–0798.

Shion's claim that moral advocates dwell on what revenge will feel like comes from years spent deciding whom she could kill and the experience of running from two pursuers with different authority. Yūki can understand someone wanting revenge while finding Shion's premise hard to accept. Shion then asks the more personal question about wanting 99 and calls it a slow suicide. Yūki's prior conversation with Hakushi, retold moments earlier, has made the acquisition of something to do the source of a steadier life. She nevertheless recognizes in Shion what it may look like if she pursues it to ruin. The same words are repeated from Shion's side. Shion sees the mirror too, recovers a feeling of the earlier household's acceptance and treats the next choice as fully alive even under looming death. These are emotionally effective exchanges and neither person has agreed to protect the other. See the distinct internal addresses at S21 P0712–0731/P0799–0843/P0847–0866.

After a struggle neither has provided enough sweets to avert enforcement. Yūki calls for a temporary stop and tries the partly blocked escape lane. Shion privately rejects the proposal and intends to use Yūki as a substitute target, even while feeling gratitude. Knowing the pattern from her fight with Kyara, Yūki deliberately presents that opportunity and prepares to counter without relying on a clear right-side view. It is a different use of recognition from Shion's emotional attachment. The same professional who treated a given aim as worth the risk can still find a way to escape and actually choose it. The pursuers take over where her counter leaves Shion, with only the blow's sound available to Yūki. Another reaches and almost kills Yūki. ## Growth, harm, and the limits of the numbered setting

The fractional labels share a result across the two nonnumbered encounters. The title can take actions as intense and consequential as a numbered night without classifying them as formal clears. Yūki chooses not to enlist with Keito's group, although she considers the tactics intelligible. She permits a recovered former player to keep what she voluntarily decided to remember, after a confrontation that is costly outside a prescribed setting. Shion's operator temporarily refuses the person and protects the invited entrant. Haine and Kokone illustrate the reach of that boundary on the other side. The two can both act through the game system even when only one is eligible to enter one game. Contacts, viewers and workers around the game have leverage with distinct institutional and social costs.

A central comparison carries more than the literal likeness of two faces. Yūki can accept the risk of ruin in her own voice without needing to trust the person whose ruin gives her words new force. Shion feels gratitude while planning to sacrifice the person who helped her make sense of her life. Haine accepts consequences for her own actions without much reckoning with how they may touch her sister after the fight. Hitomi's real choice to resume an unglamorous education, with more knowledge about why she kept looking, offers another outcome. The declared 99 has helped make Yūki able to live at all, and can constrain what forms of living she will recognize as intelligible. Tests of her later form of resolve need the hospital, closet, school and novice no less than the staged confrontation.
