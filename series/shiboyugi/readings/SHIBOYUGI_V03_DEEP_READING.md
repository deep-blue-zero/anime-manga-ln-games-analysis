## Current-unit story synopsis

Yūki opens her eyes at an amusement park dressed for a school outing she would never have chosen. She chose the lethal game itself. This is One Fine Day, her fortieth appearance. Three participants with more than thirty clears, Yūki, Maguma and Etsusei, are present in a game designed to have its players survive pursuit by costumed executioners. The players fight back, eliminate those threats before half the nominal time, and clear. Yūki is troubled by finding the game so easy. She asks her agent to drive her to the artisan who replaced the three left fingers she lost in Golden Bath.

In the artisan's forest workshop Yūki unexpectedly meets Airi, the other still-active survivor she knows from Candle Woods. Airi has lost her toes after her already-cleared thirtieth game because a storm prevented extraction. She has carried on playing despite wanting little to do with it, and has grown her hair on the advice that popularity affects payouts. The artisan welcomes the industry recovering from Candle Woods. Airi and Yūki each wonder whether that recovery makes another catastrophic game more likely.

A month later, in Cloudy Beach, Yūki wakes in one of eight cottages over shallow water. Airi wakes her and helps unite the entrants. Maguma is on her forty-third appearance, Airi her thirty-third, and Etsusei her fiftieth. Koyomi, on her twentieth, mentions hearing of Yūki from her teacher, but Yūki still believes Hakushi died. Mozuku looks frightened and says she is on her tenth. Hizumi gives only her name. Mitsuba announces her thirtieth after helping herself to food. Seven explore and find the island has no visible mainland or evident route out. Mitsuba chooses to swim. Yūki sleeps lightly in her own cottage after moving a wardrobe to obstruct her unlocked doorway. At the next morning's gathering, Etsusei is missing. Her body is found apparently dismembered in her cottage, reminding the two Candle Woods survivors of Kyara's work.

The participants consider an outside assailant or a specially assigned murderer among them. Yūki infers that remaining alive may suffice for most of them, and uses the pack count to estimate seven days. Koyomi proposes a daily meeting while allowing each participant to operate independently and withhold information. When Airi, Yūki, Koyomi and Mozuku revisit the scene, the body has disappeared. It is hard to reconcile this with the movements of the two people who left the meeting earlier. Airi and Yūki consider Kyara's disciples. At the beach, Mitsuba privately tells Yūki that she herself was a disciple of Mishiro and has left her. She subjects Yūki to a dangerous bout in the shallows as a test of the reputation she inherited. That night a masked visitor comes to Mitsuba. Mitsuba has removed hidden devices from her own body and initially prevails, then looks at the visitor's face and is struck. Her body is found dismembered the following morning.

Yūki gets nowhere approaching Maguma's base and its traps. Airi examines the injuries on the bodies, suspects the meaning of the patterned cuts on Maguma and removes the twelve objects she finds, including those in her soles. She suspects a player can be located and temporarily disabled through them. Airi tries to test Hizumi, who rejects her and attacks when asked about Kyara. Hizumi indirectly concedes the affiliation and resents Airi casting her as a proxy for Kyara. Yūki independently finds her own implants after seeing Airi bandaged the same way as Maguma. At night the masked assailant approaches Maguma, who has already removed the twelve devices, and leaves after a short conversation. The next morning Hizumi is found dead. The survivors check their own implants and apparent extraction injuries. Koyomi tells Yūki that Etsusei was also Hakushi's student and that she met Hakushi for a drink the previous week. An alternative solution becomes credible. Etsusei survived her own display. The narration then shows her alive and identifies her as the murderer.

Etsusei has tried to imitate Hakushi through instruction, study of movement and archived games, and her own drastic bodily alteration. Etsusei was told to make at least three killings manifest to the others within a week. At evacuation, failure means execution. Other players have to board the rescue boat after the interval. Etsusei disabled the group's ability to identify her by becoming its first apparent victim. She killed Mitsuba after the latter relaxed on recognizing her face, then switched from Maguma to Hizumi when she learned Maguma had removed the devices. She needs one further killing. The four in Koyomi's cabin cooperate despite the thought that sacrificing one of them would leave the others safe. At the end of a week, Etsusei boards the incoming rescue vessel ahead of them, obtains a gun and impersonates a rescuer. The four scatter. She wounds Mozuku, who survives the attack by persuading Etsusei that the homemade props she carries are dangerous equipment.

Etsusei next targets Yūki. An earlier conversation with Koyomi showed Etsusei how unfavorably Hakushi had described her, and that Yūki was a fellow pupil in whom Hakushi had hope. Etsusei has not added to her required total by the time they fight. Yūki sees Etsusei's self-dismemberment as an error in how to learn from Hakushi. She goes back to Maguma's traps and threatens to die by them, a loss that would not meet Etsusei's rule. Etsusei approaches the pit. Yūki reverses the fight, using the weapons she gets from Etsusei, and leaves when Etsusei stops moving. Yūki reaches the rescue boat but does not know if Etsusei died or who else boarded.

At a magic bar Yūki finally meets Hakushi, whom she last saw apparently dead eighteen months earlier. Hakushi confirms Etsusei found her method independently. Asked which pupil she would rather have seen there, Hakushi challenges Yūki to say whether she would give up the place and tells her that she is no longer a pupil who has to care about an outsider's judgment. Koyomi, who deliberately left them alone, arrives afterward. She intends to retire. Hakushi privately says she continues to hope to see Yūki reach 99 because only Yūki among her pupils actually declared that aim, and would accept a later change of mind. After Yūki leaves the bar, her agent calls to relay an unverified account of a separate game run around the same time as Cloudy Beach. According to the report, eighty entered and only three survived.

## Leave the Frontline

### SBY-V03-E011 — The answer from someone at the bar

Yūki enters a small magic bar rather than the exclusive place she had expected. She has to find words for someone she thought dead for about a year and a half. The precise question she opts to ask is which former pupil Hakushi would have liked to find there. ## Themes that change under the new disclosure

Yūki and Etsusei both learn from conduct they did not fully understand at Candle Woods. Etsusei chooses reproduction of a particular condition and employs it for long life and for a local ruse. Yūki chooses an aim and later reasons from the material and practical cost that made it impossible for Hakushi herself to keep pursuing it. Both have acquired some fabricated body parts. Both also have reasons to think that a way of learning can be sustained only up to a point. The book makes the effect and handover of actual changes visible with different epistemic conditions. For that reason a credible mentor portrait or subsequent simulation of Yūki would require the relevant earlier belief in Hakushi's death, current practice of passing on or concealing information, shifts in desired approval and what she now thinks a decision is for. A timeless label for a loyal disciple would obscure most of those causes.

The island revisions put evidence of ordinary interests and strong game abilities into the same person. Airi's astronomy lesson and economic hardship, Maguma's condition and secrecy, Mitsuba's preferred leisure and difficulty caring beyond it, Mozuku's regrets about study, and Koyomi's apprehension of out-of-game loss all have usable lower-pressure controls. The attraction of competence and the affect of being left out of another person's story raise risks an attack-centered account would miss. The volume also changes what a successful professional result means. The exhausted Yūki sleeps through the opportunity to verify who made it. Koyomi is sufficiently dissatisfied to choose retirement despite escaping. Some rival relationships end in ignorance. The agent's report opens pressure beyond any one person’s game.
