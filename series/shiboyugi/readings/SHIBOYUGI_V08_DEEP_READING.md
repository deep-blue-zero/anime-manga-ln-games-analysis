# Shiboyugi V08 — Japanese primary deep reading

The opening observation that ability attracts trouble acquires force when a practiced movement becomes something a rival can learn, an achievement becomes a qualification to govern, and an address becomes known beyond the household. Each gain gives someone else a choice. By following the hosts, the players, the organizer and the neighbors in turn, the volume shows how much those choices depend on who knows the terms of an exchange and who can still walk away. Yūki finishes a game with an accommodation she deliberately kept and later leaves her apartment with a lease she deliberately retains. Shiro ends the volume preparing to continue her bid after the person she wanted refuses. S14 P0004, S16 P0213–0265, S18 P0275–0303/P0342–0345, S20 P0952–1006, S22 P0041–0097.
