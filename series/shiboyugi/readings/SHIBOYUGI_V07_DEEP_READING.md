# Shiboyugi V07 — Japanese primary deep reading

The acts that most change a scene in V07 often have a different meaning to each person who sees them. A neighbor reads a greeting as surveillance, an experienced player hears an ordinary-world abduction as an interruption before recognizing it as a crisis, and members who once defended the same group make different choices about giving up its name. The book makes this consequential by giving characters private work, public obligations and imperfect access to one another. Yūki's increased range of practical perception makes the limits on that access especially legible.
