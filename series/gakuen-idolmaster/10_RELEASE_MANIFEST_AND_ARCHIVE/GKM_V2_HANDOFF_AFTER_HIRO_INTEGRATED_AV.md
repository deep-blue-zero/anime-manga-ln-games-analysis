## Governing formulation

## Voice-performance model

Reject descriptions of Hiro as merely sleepy, flat, emotionless, or low-energy. Her stable acting identity is better described as **low-friction micro-affective delivery**:

- economical expenditure;
- deliberate pauses;
- short/fragmented phrase units;
- little overmarking of irony;
- calm sincerity even when the content is bizarre;
- small changes in pace, breath, hesitation, or endings carrying large weight because the baseline is restrained.

Observed states now include ordinary analytical Hiro, difficulty-delighted Hiro, bodily exhaustion, first jealousy/competitive ignition, friend-facing Hiro, cute/embarrassed Hiro, fear-of-loss Hiro, intimate Producer teasing, crisis-concentrated Hiro, public `神格`, Prima-Stella peak, and post-peak ordinary Hiro.

Her comedy often arises because the acting does not announce that she is joking. The gap among alarming content, other characters' reactions, and Hiro's calm evaluation produces the humor.

### Quietness changes function

Early quietness often accompanies consequence-light curiosity: difficulty is interesting because it resists her. Later quietness contains investment. Dear 008's `悔しいかもしれない` is important because Hiro must first classify a feeling that reveals idolhood now matters enough for another person's success to hurt.

Her development is not greater loudness. It is increasing emotional consequence inside the same low-friction expressive system.

## Crisis performance and ethics

AV strengthens the factual claim that acute stakes can improve Hiro's performance by producing narrowed organization:

- distraction falls away;
- gaze and phrase direction sharpen;
- stillness gathers attention;
- economical movement becomes intentional;
- the body appears to spend exactly what it has.

Do not convert effectiveness into ethical closure. Producer controls information, schedule, opportunity, exposure, and career interpretation; Hiro has real physical and reputational risk.

Current relational verdict:

> **productive mutuality — confirmed; ethical closure — open.**

## `神格`

Reject the model that goddess-like Hiro is her hidden essential self.

It is real as reception, not proof of stable conventional technical supremacy. The route's `瞬間最大風速` language remains the correct limiter for her maximum state.

## Cute Hiro

The `ガラクタロード` commu is a major correction to reducing Hiro to challenge-seeking eccentricity. When Producer presses beyond her prepared explanation that idolhood attracted her because it was badly suited to her, Hiro eventually admits:

> `…………かわいくなりたかった。`

Crucial distinction:
- `神格` is substantially something spectators perceive under extraordinary conditions;
- `かわいい` is partly something Hiro herself wanted idolhood to let her become.

Cute Hiro and goddess Hiro are not fake and real selves. `ハッピーミルフィーユ`, `仮装狂騒曲`, `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！` and similar material show ordinary cuteness, comedy, costume play, and social pleasure becoming public Hiro performance modes.

## Musical identity

Use **experimental pop** or **avant-garde-adjacent pop** as careful corpus-level descriptors rather than forcing every track into a strict genre.

Character-owned repertoire repeatedly resists frictionless idol-pop regularity through abrupt textural/dynamic shifts, unstable or asymmetric motion, unusual harmonic coloration, intimate vocal presentation against discontinuous surroundings, fragmented graphic/editorial language, and structures that require active listener reorientation.

The deeper parallel is:

> **Hiro's body does not fit inherited idol expectations, and her character-specific music often does not fit inherited idol-pop smoothness. In both cases, apparent bad fit becomes the source of another form.**

### Principal developmental musical sequence

`光景 → コントラスト → サンフェーデッド`

- `光景` — nonfit becomes perceivable form.
- `コントラスト` — form becomes relational difference; contradiction becomes organized rather than merely present.
- `サンフェーデッド` — relational difference becomes future-bearing identity; experimental form remains after Hiro has acquired real stakes and attachment.

Do not narrate this as strange beginner → increasingly normal professional. Prefer:

> **private nonfit → publicly legible difference → experimental identity capable of sustaining attachment.**

### `メクルメ`

Broadens Hiro's experimental identity beyond the principal three-song sequence and one composer relationship. The supplied object was full audio/lyric presentation, not a choreographed 3DMV; do not invent choreography.

### `コンテンポラリのダンス`

Makes the body itself an experimental object. It demonstrates that Hiro's body can become conceptually expressive before becoming conventionally virtuosic.

### Common-song controls

Common/inherited works inspected include `Campus mode!!`, `初`, `がむしゃらに行こう！`, `Howling over the World`, `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！`, and `ENDLESS DANCE`.

These prevent the overclaim that every Hiro performance becomes avant-garde. Portable traits are instead:

- economical movement;
- precise but idiosyncratic timing;
- restrained/intimate center inside larger arrangements;
- ability to filter effort, aggression, comedy, or cuteness through Hiro rather than merely adopting generic affect.

`Campus mode!!` is an important institutional control: its bright school-idol grammar is badly suited to early Hiro, so successful performance demonstrates adaptation of inherited repertoire to a nonstandard performer rather than simple assimilation into an ideal idol body.

## Visible improvement

Best formulation:
