# GKM UNIT STORY PLACEMENT AND CONTINUITY TRACKS

## 0. Conclusion

`unit_01` is a complete, internally longitudinal ensemble continuity. It begins on the entrance-ceremony day and ends at the old-rules summer H.I.F., where Re;IRIS wins the unit division and Saki becomes the summer Prima Stella.

It should be designated:

> **TRACK U1 — Re;IRIS / Begrazia summer-H.I.F. continuity**

U1 is not disposable side material, but it also cannot be silently merged into every individual Produce route.

# 2. Episode-by-episode placement

## 2.1 Episode 1 — entrance and unit formation

Opening source:

`adv_unit_01-01_01.txt`

The Producer attends the entrance ceremony, meets Ume, and learns that she wants to defeat someone. The following parts identify Saki as Ume's sister/rival and establish the Producer's plan to assemble Saki, Temari, and Kotone.

By `adv_unit_01-01_03.txt`, the Producer explicitly proposes that the three form a unit and aim at H.I.F. Episode 1 then follows the persuasion and competitive tests required to make the unit possible.

Endpoint:

`adv_unit_01-01_14.txt`

Saki, Temari, and Kotone report that they have agreed to form the unit.

Classification:

- entrance ceremony: `C1 @ U1`;
- unit formation: `C1 @ U1`;
- incompatible with the premise that the same Producer is exclusively assigned to one idol in an individual Produce route unless a source supplies a bridge.

## 2.2 Episode 2 — April/May Selection and domestic formation

`adv_unit_01-02_01.txt` establishes:

- Selection at the end of April and end of May;
- participation as a unit;
- strong results in both as the road toward H.I.F.;
- the first Selection as the unit's first live.

The rest of Episode 2 develops:

- leader and center questions;
- cohabitation;
- food, training, sleep, and bodily management;
- Saki, Temari, and Kotone learning one another's ordinary habits;
- conflict becoming a workable unit practice.

Classification:

- April/May calendar: `C0-C1 @ U1`;
- domestic and relational developments: `C1 @ U1`;
- transfer to solo P-track biography: not automatic.

## 2.3 Episode 3 — parallel units and challenge structure

Episode 3 introduces or consolidates:

- Ume, China, and Hiro as the low-ranked “Worst Three” ecology;
- Ume seeking Sena's production;
- Begrazia formation around Sena, Ume, and Misuzu;
- Re;IRIS receiving its name;
- Re;IRIS's first live;
- Begrazia challenging Re;IRIS at the following month's Selection.

This episode turns the initial unit into a two-unit competitive system. It is not merely a cameo collection; it establishes the rival structure that drives Episode 4.

Classification:

- Re;IRIS/Begrazia history: `C1 @ U1`;
- use in other tracks: relationship possibility-space unless explicitly imported.

## 2.4 Episode 4 — Selection defeat, recovery, summer H.I.F.

The sequence is explicit:

1. Re;IRIS and Begrazia meet at Selection.
2. Re;IRIS loses.
3. Each member confronts the defeat and the unit recalibrates.
4. The Producer establishes the H.I.F. plan.
5. Both units prepare for the summer event.
6. Re;IRIS wins the unit division.
7. Saki becomes summer Prima Stella.

Important anchors:

- `adv_unit_01-04_11.txt`: first early-morning meeting after Selection, now aimed at H.I.F.;
- `adv_unit_01-04_14.txt`: H.I.F. is approaching and the venue will be outdoors in the summer heat;
- `adv_unit_01-04_16.txt`: Saki says she has been enrolled only roughly half a year;
- `adv_unit_01-04_20.txt`: Re;IRIS wins the unit division;
- `adv_unit_01-04_24.txt`: the unit victory is followed by selection of one Re;IRIS member and the solo winner for the final Prima Stella stage;
- `adv_unit_01-04_25.txt`: Saki is announced as `夏のプリマステラ`.

Classification:

- ordered sequence: `C1 @ U1`;
- Re;IRIS unit victory: track-exclusive biography;
- Saki summer Prima Stella: `C0 @ U1 endpoint`, not global.

# 3. Old H.I.F. rules versus Series 3 reform

## 3.1 U1 old-rules structure

U1 explicitly has:

- a unit division;
- a solo division;
- a later final selection for Prima Stella.

## 3.2 Series 3 new-rules structure

Series 3 explicitly says the winter H.I.F. will:

- abolish the solo/unit division;
- select participants more strictly;
- use three Selection exams;
- judge prescribed and free songs together;
- name the overall winner Prima Stella.

## 3.3 Institutional ordering

The rule change establishes a strong institutional chronology:

```text
old-rules summer H.I.F. (U1-type system)
    -> reform decision
    -> new-rules winter H.I.F. (P3 system)
```

This does **not** establish that U1's exact winner history is the predecessor of every P3 character route.

Possible explanations—seasonal title changes, intervening competitions, or deliberate route alternatives—remain hypotheses. Phase 1 does not choose among them without evidence.

Therefore:

> **Champion succession must be recorded per track, not converted into a single inferred list.**

# 5. Producer-continuity comparison

## U1 Producer

- produces Saki, Temari, and Kotone as Re;IRIS;
- later becomes involved with Ume's development;
- operates in an ensemble/unit structure;
- is explicitly part of the unit's collective victory.

## Individual P-route Producer

- begins from one selected idol's route;
- develops character-specific one-to-one pedagogy and intimacy;
- reaches route-conditioned competition outcomes.

The two versions may share stable Producer tendencies, but their biographies are not additive by default.

Safe use:

> Across both Saki's individual route and U1, the Producer repeatedly frames defeat as evidence to be analyzed rather than a final judgment.

Unsafe use:

> The Producer completed Saki's solo route, then Temari's solo route, then Kotone's solo route, and afterward formed Re;IRIS.

# 6. Relationship-evidence policy

U1 is exceptionally strong evidence for:

- Saki/Temari/Kotone group dynamics;
- Saki/Ume rivalry and sisterhood;
- Temari/Misuzu history and renewed competition;
- Ume/Sena and Ume/Misuzu;
- Re;IRIS/Begrazia competitive identity;
- first-year school ecology;
- bodily and domestic practices of unit formation.

However, later character monographs must label whether a relationship fact is:

- explicit pre-route history;
- U1-specific development;
- reproduced elsewhere and therefore likely invariant;
- incompatible with another route's state;
- merely thematically analogous.

# 8. Placement verdict

U1 is best understood as:

> **a parallel ensemble route that preserves an old-rules summer-H.I.F. institutional stage and supplies one complete possible first-year history.**

Its institutional structure helps explain the reform described in Series 3. Its personal outcome history remains track-specific.
