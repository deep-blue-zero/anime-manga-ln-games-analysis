## Principal findings

1. The intended Produce macro-order is Series 1 (`初`) → Series 2 (`N.I.A.`) → Series 3 (reformed winter `H.I.F.`).
2. Series 2 directly presupposes a successful Series 1 regular-performance state, but not one uniquely identified result grade.
3. Series 3 has mutually exclusive reigning-champion world states: common routes use Sena; Saki's route uses Ume and makes Sena the former champion.
4. `true` remains a source label rather than a universal canon declaration.
5. Numeric suffixes have family-local meanings; Series 1 uses some as result grades, while Series 3 also uses them as technical composition positions.
6. Unit Story 01 is an internally fixed parallel ensemble track from entrance ceremony to old-rules summer H.I.F., ending with Re;IRIS's unit victory and Saki as summer Prima Stella.
7. The old-rules summer H.I.F. institutionally precedes the reformed winter H.I.F., but U1's personal result history cannot be merged into every Produce route.
8. Dear Idol, communications, events, support cards, Produce events, seasonal, and live material now have default C0-C4 placement rules and promotion criteria.
