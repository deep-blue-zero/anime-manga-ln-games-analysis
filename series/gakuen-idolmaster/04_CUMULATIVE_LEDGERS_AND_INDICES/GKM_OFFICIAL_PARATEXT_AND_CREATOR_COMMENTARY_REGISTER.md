# GKM OFFICIAL PARATEXT AND CREATOR COMMENTARY REGISTER

## Purpose

This register implements the governing rule:

> **Primary-source priority does not mean primary-source exclusivity.**

S1 game material remains authoritative for enacted narrative reality. This register supplies formal setting metadata and production/design commentary that dramatic dialogue has no obligation to state exhaustively.

## Source classes

- `S1` narrative primary source — game ADV and directly presented narrative/system material.
- `S2` official canonical paratext — first-party setting/profile/reference material.
- `S3` creator/staff commentary — credited production rationale/history.
- `S4` reliable external secondary source — edited reporting/context.
- `S5` fan/discovery reference — discovery only unless independently corroborated.

Claim types: `A` textual fact; `B` official setting fact; `C` creator-stated rationale; `D` secondary-source report; `E` analytical inference; `F` open/unresolved.

## Hypothesis routing

### H1 — Structured liminality
**Status:** STRENGTHENED (`E`, high confidence).

The Producer is simultaneously student, trainee, evaluator, career gatekeeper, manager, and intimate developmental partner. S1 establishes the dual student/authority condition; S3 explains that the distance was intentionally engineered.

### H2 — Authority while still educated
**Status:** STRENGTHENED (`E`, high confidence).

Hatsuboshi evaluates the Producer while giving him consequential authority over idol schedules, bodies, opportunities, and careers. This should remain central to Producer ethics.

### H3 — Vertically integrated formation system
**Status:** STRENGTHENED (`E`, high confidence).

The game already shows a school/production/labor ecosystem; S3 adds a stated history in which idol and producer pipelines are institutionally co-developed.

### H4 — Deliberately engineered relational distance
**Status:** CONFIRMED AS CREATOR RATIONALE (`S3/C`), OPEN AS UNIVERSAL SCENE EFFECT.

The teacher/classmate/specialized-university design choice is explicitly creator-stated. Whether each individual route successfully produces the intended balance remains a textual question.

## Negative-evidence / audit note

As of 2026-08-15, this audit recovered official first-party `プロデューサー科` terminology but **did not recover a standalone S2 page whose parsed text independently spells the complete `初星学園専門大学` compound**. The compound is therefore not mislabeled S2 merely because it appears on a Bandai Namco-hosted creator interview page; the proposition there is attributed to Komino as `S3/C`.

## Future use

Future paratext claims should be appended by semantic responsibility, not collected indiscriminately. Priority targets are formal institutional taxonomy, official character facts absent from ADV, credited music/visual production rationale, and creator statements that materially sharpen or challenge an existing textual hypothesis. Creator intent never overrides contrary S1 behavior.
