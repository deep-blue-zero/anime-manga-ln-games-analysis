## Planned Phase 6 — Kaya Rinha / 賀陽燐羽 distributed-character reconstruction

**Architectural status:** mandatory Phase-6 specialist branch; not a fourteenth playable-character core.

Rinha's evidence is distributed across other characters' story objects rather than contained in a dedicated playable-character bundle. The future profile must therefore preserve provenance by host character and distinguish Rinha's direct behavior from how Temari, Misuzu, Saki, and others remember or interpret her.

### Planned artifact chain

1. `GKM_KAYA_RINHA_SOURCE_CROSSWALK.md` — unique-script inventory and continuity/viewpoint/AV routing.
2. `GKM_KAYA_RINHA_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md` — direct versus mediated claims, contradictions, confidence, revision state.
3. `GKM_KAYA_RINHA_CHARACTER_DOSSIER.md` — active-provisional distributed-source synthesis.

Create a separate AV request register only if the mined scene set is dense enough to justify a distinct acquisition responsibility.

### Evidence-origin vocabulary

- `R-DIRECT` — Rinha explicitly states a proposition.
- `R-ACTION` — Rinha demonstrably acts in a way relevant to the claim.
- `R-LING` — Japanese speech/register evidence from Rinha herself.
- `R-AV` — directly inspected voice, facial, body, or timing evidence.
- `OTHER-REPORT` — another character describes/interprets Rinha.
- `MEMORY` — retrospective reconstruction of Rinha/SyngUp! by another character.
- `REL-INFERENCE` — pattern inferred across interactions rather than explicitly stated.
- `META` — official profile/paratext evidence.
- `OPEN` — plausible but insufficiently triangulated.

### Required observer separation

Maintain distinct evidence columns for:

- Rinha herself;
- Temari's perception of Rinha;
- Misuzu's perception of Rinha;
- Saki's perception of Rinha;
- other observers / institutional framing.

Do not average disagreements away. A contradiction between observers may itself reveal relationship-specific dynamics or unreliable retrospective framing.

### Scheduling rule

The crosswalk may be started opportunistically, but the dossier should not be stabilized until Phase 4 numbered events and Phase 5 support cards are substantially read. It should be substantially complete before definitive Phase-7 Temari and Misuzu monographs.

### Governing principle

> **Reconstruct Rinha from intersections, not reflections.**
