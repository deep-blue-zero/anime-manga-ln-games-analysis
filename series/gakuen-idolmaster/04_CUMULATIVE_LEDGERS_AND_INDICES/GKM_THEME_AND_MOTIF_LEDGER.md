## China thematic delta

- **Accountable inheritance:** inherited wealth, name, love, manners, networks and office are neither denied nor treated as morally self-justifying; legitimacy depends on governance, noncoercion and public answerability.
- **Relational legitimacy:** support becomes part of achievement without eliminating the need for acquired craft or nondelegable acts.
- **Self-authored stakes:** idolhood gives a sheltered girl experiences money cannot purchase directly—serious frustration, jealousy, completion, risk, gratitude and a dream she refuses to abandon.
- **Atomized merit critique:** China challenges the fiction that achievement can be isolated from support, while still valuing skill, fair comparison, consent and accountability.
- **Love → obligation:** `愛された責任` makes gratitude generative but potentially burdensome; later phases must test whether China can receive care without compulsory repayment.
- **Mask as authentic practice:** `仮面`, `ふにゃ`, and `きり` are not arranged as fake/public versus real/private selves; authoritative performance becomes one chosen part of China.
- **Fluke language and self-credit:** `まぐれ勝ち` protects against overclaiming until trusted others make persistent under-crediting itself inaccurate.
- **Summit succession:** Sena's `一番星` rhetoric is inherited and transformed into China's own public `わたくしを目指しなさい`, making succession an obligation to become visible enough for others to orient toward.

## Phase-3 Tsubame thematic delta — 2026-08-16

- **Second place as shelter:** a high rank can protect self-esteem while freezing the hierarchy it appears to contest.
- **Effort versus development:** enormous effort is not automatically growth when the method preserves the framework that explains failure.
- **Heroism / non-abandonment:** Tsubame's mature vocation says an idol should not abandon people; care is enacted through confrontation and renewed possibility rather than soothing alone.
- **Responsible victory:** winning means inheriting some of the defeated side's disappointed expectations; private rivalry must become publicly answerable.
- **Individuation through survivable separation:** `貴様がいない世界で生きる覚悟` makes continued attachment more rather than less meaningful.
- **Renewable rivalry:** a rival is not a ladder discarded after victory but a relation capable of repeatedly reopening the future.
- **Technique / `スター性`:** late Tsubame joins the corpus-wide critique of reducing idol value to legible parameters without turning that critique into anti-skill romanticism.
- **Shell / swallow / sky / stars:** the stronger trajectory is not No.2 → No.1 but closed ladder → plural open field.

## Phase 3 AV — Misuzu thematic deltas

### Tempo is not morality

Misuzu can perform high-motion and effort-forward material. Her ethical claim is narrower: exertion is a means, and no outside observer may infer seriousness solely from visible strain. Dear 023–026 shows that effort can become alienated when defeat seizes control of tempo.

### Softness as gravity

Softness is not the opposite of power. Misuzu uses low-friction attention to create a centripetal field in which care, intimacy, and possession become persuasive.

### Vulnerability and office

Prima Stella does not require emotional invulnerability. Tears, dependence, bodily exhaustion, and the final yawn make the crown inhabitable rather than destroying it.

### Night and scale

`ツキノカメ`, `ヨルニテ`, `Superlative`, and `VEIL` differentiate intimate night, orbit, dream possession, concealment, and cosmic public sovereignty. Night is a temporal/relational system, not merely a color palette.

### Plurality versus incorporation

The mature night sky may host independent stars or claim them as contents of itself. This remains a governing open question for later relationship and institutional synthesis.

## Phase 3 Hiro AV thematic additions

### Resistant form / mismatch composed

Hiro supplies a new thematic formulation:

> **Mismatch is not removed. It is composed.**

- bodily unsuitability → developmental choreography and visible effort;
- low-force speech → micro-affective deadpan and close attention;
- musical nonconformity → curated experimental repertoire;
- crisis response → bounded concentration rather than stable mastery;
- hobby → dream only after people, obligations, and loss accumulate.

This revises the simplistic “overcome weakness” motif. Improvement creates expressive surplus but does not require Hiro to become a conventionally effortless idol.

## Phase 4 — Events 013–020 thematic additions

## 10.1 Continuity-bearing witness as a third Phase-4 social mechanism

The first three event tranches now support a cumulative model:

1. **Events 001–005 — peer relation as production technology.** Characters develop through rivalry, class structure, work, seniority, and shared institutional tasks.
2. **Events 006–012 — peers as error-correction systems for aspiration.** Other people identify bad method, misdirected effort, hidden fear, and recipient-blind creativity.
3. **Events 013–020 — peers as continuity-bearing witnesses.** Other people preserve information about who a character was, who they are when unobserved, and what changes without declaring one state the only authentic self.

These functions overlap but should not be collapsed.

A person can correct method without knowing history. A person can remember history without having authority to correct. A durable close relation often does both.

The third function becomes especially important in a live-service corpus because the same character appears across branch variants, seasonal events, professional contexts, and radically different emotional states. The stories themselves repeatedly resist the idea that one surface should become definitive.

### Examples

- Rinami can remember Sena as a classmate before the current perfect presidency and still respect the public crown.
- Tsubame can remember Sena's long rivalry and therefore identify producing juniors as insufficient replacement for being challenged herself.
- Mao can remember the lonely Prima Stella while seeing the present institutional leader.
- Temari can reproduce Rinha's old instruction even though the current relation is no longer physically present in the scene.
- Lilja and Sumika know one another before idol school and can therefore read current bright/timid surfaces as genuine but incomplete.
- Misuzu knows Hiro's chosen philosophy of hardship well enough to notice when this fear is different.

This is not an argument for privileged “true selves” known only by intimates. The better formulation is:

> **close relations hold multiple valid states in memory and therefore reduce the pressure to make any one state total.**

## 10.2 Maturity means governing dependence, not eliminating it

Across the tranche, the most mature outcomes are not solitary.

Sena needs rivals if she is to escape developmental stasis. Rinami needs juniors capable of carrying a live when she freezes. Hiro needs friends to teach her that difficulty has external moral costs. Misuzu needs a reason she personally endorses before effort becomes worth spending. Sumika needs people who can know fear without turning it into a verdict. Lilja needs Sumika but also needs the ability to act without Sumika. Temari needs Misuzu's packing list while also needing to prove she can travel without Misuzu. Saki needs Ume to have a life beyond her and also needs permission to experience loneliness about it.

The governing distinction is therefore not dependence versus independence. It is:

- **enclosed dependence** — the relation reduces alternative action, alternative attachments, or the ability to leave;
- **governed dependence** — care remains available, refusal remains possible, the person can act elsewhere, and support can reverse direction.

Events 013–020 repeatedly reward the second form.

## 10.3 “Ordinary life” is productive material, not leftover non-idol time

Events 016, 017, 019, and 020 are especially important for behavioral reconstruction because the dramatic stakes are often low.

Characters shop, eat, tease, sleep, budget, babysit, play sports, talk about hobbies, worry about siblings, and fill out questionnaires. None of these scenes suspends idol identity.

Event 017 explicitly theorizes why. Sena argues that idols sing ordinary people's feelings and therefore need ordinary experience. Rinami concludes that dream-pursuing daily life can itself shine. Event 019 converts embarrassing sleep behavior into stage MC. Event 020 turns private fandom/cosplay into a possible public identity asset.

For reconstruction, this means:

> **do not simulate Gakumas characters as if their “real characterization” appears only under H.I.F.-level pressure.**

Their low-stakes conduct contains core mechanisms: who notices what, who accepts help, how quickly teasing escalates, where embarrassment appears, how professional language enters play, what kind of leisure restores them, and how public role bleeds into private habit.

## 10.4 Role authenticity is repeatedly additive rather than subtractive

This tranche adds several more anti-binary cases:

- Rinami can be soft and commanding.
- Sena can be perfect and ridiculous at a hamburger shop.
- Mao can enjoy princehood while being visibly bad at bitter coffee and roller coasters.
- Misuzu can dislike effort and still choose extreme exertion for a friend.
- Hiro can enjoy mismatch and still care about recipient satisfaction.
- Sumika's bright mask can be chosen and emotionally real while still protecting fear.
- Lilja can use an anime quotation and still mean the value she is expressing.
- Temari can assert independence and still sleep-seek warmth.

A useful Phase-7 guardrail is therefore:

> **When a scene exposes contradiction, first test whether the corpus is expanding the character's repertoire before concluding that one side is false.**

## 10.5 Rivalry has at least four distinct functions in the current corpus

Events 013–020 help separate rivalry mechanisms that should not be merged into one trait.

1. **Epistemic rivalry** — Saki uses competition to learn what another person can do.
2. **Pedagogical rivalry** — Saki constructs challenges that cause peers/juniors to develop.
3. **Identity rivalry** — Ume/Saki and Tsubame/Sena use the other person as a durable reference point while differentiating.
4. **Anti-stagnation rivalry** — Sena needs an equal opponent not to prove current superiority but to make further growth possible.

The fourth is especially important. It explains why succession cannot satisfy Sena's full problem. A successor may preserve the institution after her; only a rival can threaten the present self enough to create a new one.

## 10.6 Friendship becomes operational when friction becomes legible

Event 019 gives the clearest formulation, but the pattern spans the tranche.

Close groups do not become effective because conflict disappears. They become effective because members know how to interpret it.

- Saki knows Temari's limits and gives her a song-heavy role.
- Temari knows Saki's recklessness and corrects it without treating the relationship as broken.
- Kotone knows when to mock, when to work, and when to let Saki lead.
- Rinami knows Tsubame's hostility can contain care.
- Hiro knows Misuzu's low-effort preference is not the same as indifference.
- Sumika knows Lilja's dramatic quotation habits well enough to test whether a line is borrowed without dismissing the conviction.

For later relationship synthesis, **predictability of friction** should be tracked as a form of intimacy distinct from emotional warmth.
