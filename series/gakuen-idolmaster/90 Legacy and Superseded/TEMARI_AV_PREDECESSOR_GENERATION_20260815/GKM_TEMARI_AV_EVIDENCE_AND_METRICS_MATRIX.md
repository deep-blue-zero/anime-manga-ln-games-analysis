## 0. Purpose

This appendix separates direct observation, source alignment, derived metrics, and interpretation. It is designed to prevent a full-mix measurement or one dramatic frame from being promoted into a stronger claim than the evidence supports.

## 5.1 Interpretation rule

These windows are useful for identifying candidates for closer inspection. They are not speaker-isolated measurements. A numerical contrast is promoted only when exact dialogue, visible acting, and scene structure point in the same direction.

The Rinha confession is the clearest example: the proxy indicates unusually high/volatile energy, but the decisive evidence is Temari's visible sobbing, folded posture, elongated pleading, and explicit imitation confession.
