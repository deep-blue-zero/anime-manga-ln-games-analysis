# TSUKIMURA TEMARI — COMPLETE AUDIOVISUAL BASELINE

## 0. Executive finding

The audiovisual corpus strongly confirms the Phase-3 textual account of Tsukimura Temari as the **idol of authored combustion**, but it also prevents that phrase from hardening into a single sonic genre, a permanently severe public face, or an opposition between a “fake cool idol” and a “real needy girl.”

Temari's most stable audiovisual identity is a procedure for placing emotional pressure under form:

> **feeling is not reduced; it is concentrated, selected, timed, and made publicly legible.**

Her characteristic coolness is therefore not low temperature. It is the visible discipline of high temperature. The Producer's phrase from the `ガラクタロード` communication—`冷ややかな氷ではなく、超高温で完全燃焼する青い炎`, “not chilly ice, but a blue flame undergoing complete combustion at extreme temperature”—is vindicated as an organizing metaphor. It accurately describes the relation between restraint and force in Temari's speaking performance, singing, and stage body.

It is not an exhaustive definition. Several works deliberately exceed or disrupt it:

- `Unhappy Light` centers sleeping, crying, domestic vulnerability, a child image, and ordinary desire rather than preserving an aloof star;
- `Campus mode!!` permits bright institutional optimism and open smiling;
- `がむしゃらに行こう！` makes awkward effort and school comedy part of her public appeal;
- `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！` proves that her image can survive hyper-cute, meme-like, streaming-era play;
- `雨上がりのアイリス` places her inside distributed group focus, mutual gaze, and shared lines;
- the post-H.I.F. scenes let the newly crowned Prima Stella lend the Producer a handkerchief, discuss ramen, and speak present-tense love without abandoning ambition.

These are not counterexamples showing that the cool persona was false. They reveal what Temari's mature persona can contain.

The core musical sequence is especially coherent:

1. **`Luna say maybe`** makes indirect confession the condition of performance. Temari denies resemblance to the song's twisted girl while continuing to hum and inhabit her. The official MV fragments and multiplies Temari through cyan-blue interfaces, repeated portraits, running figures, tears, and typography. The 3DMV places her alone at a microphone before an enormous luminous audience. Emotion exceeds direct speech and finds a more exact public form in song.
2. **`アイヴイ`** turns multiplicity into deliberate curation. The song commu gives the Producer permission to decide what “Temari-like” means. His answer includes the crying, spoiled, unreasonable, strict, solitary, beautiful, and cool Temaris, while the stage selects the especially compelling elements because she is an idol. The 3DMV's dark blue/teal geometry, black costume, and more physically expansive silhouette embody authorship rather than concealment.
3. **`一体いつから`** makes historical self-interrogation public. Its white-gray costume, violet-white geometry, moments of near-monochrome isolation, reaching and kneeling body, and moonlit ending enlarge an inward question into arena dramaturgy. Its commu also shows Temari learning that emotional communication has multiple techniques: China's gentleness, Misuzu's deliberate audience control, and Temari's own total-force projection.
4. **`ガラクタロード`** converts private self-theory into category authorship. Temari and the Producer decide that their “cool” need not match the conventional category. The performance uses common prescribed material, neon color, open-arm address, teasing confidence, and visible bodily expenditure to make a public claim: `いつか、こっちを本物にしてやります`, “Someday I will make *this* the real thing.”

The sequence is not a march from falsehood to authenticity. It is a development in authorship:

> disowned resemblance → curated plurality → learned expressive plurality → public redefinition.

The speaking-performance corpus confirms the same movement. Temari can occupy at least six recurring acted states:

- compact professional control;
- dry or abrasive directness;
- childlike need, panic, and food-transparent pleasure;
- high-pressure anger or sobbing in which articulation remains surprisingly clear;
- lowered intimate trust;
- formal stage declaration.

Growth consists less in eliminating any state than in becoming able to recognize, select, and move among them without treating the needy or childish state as proof that the idol is counterfeit.

The greatest audiovisual correction concerns dependence. The Producer does not merely discover a hidden authentic Temari. He becomes one of the people through whom she authors herself:

- `アイヴイ` grants him visual-image authorship;
- `ガラクタロード` grants him conceptual language for her desired category;
- Dear Idol 035 asks him to send her onto the final stage by calling her simply `手毬`;
- Dear Idol 037 reverses care when she lends him a handkerchief and accurately reads his tears;
- Re;IRIS material shows that she can promise to listen while still wanting the exclusivity of “her” Producer.

This strengthens reciprocity. It does not resolve enclosure. The same Producer who understands her is also the person who organizes food, stamina, rest, repertoire, publicity, and the conditions under which she may continue burning. The rendered `ガラクタロード` aftermath—Temari panting after treating rehearsal as if it were the real performance—keeps the physical cost of “complete combustion” visible. The partnership can be mutually desired and still enable dangerous overextension.

The audiovisual evidence therefore confirms, strengthens, and narrows the textual thesis:

> **Tsukimura Temari is the idol of authored combustion. She does not become truthful by discarding performance, imitation, dependence, or her cool persona. She becomes more truthful by acknowledging their sources, learning additional ways to communicate feeling, and making a chosen public form capable of containing the many Temaris she once ranked as real or false. Her blue flame is not one sound or mood; it is the disciplined conversion of emotional excess into an image, voice, and song for which she increasingly accepts authorship.**

## 1.4 Limitations

- No studio vocal stems were supplied. A computational source-separation attempt was not used because the required model weights were unavailable offline. The analysis therefore does not claim singer-only formants, precise tessitura, isolated breath noise, or vocal compression behavior.
- Full-mix metrics include arrangement and mastering. They help distinguish broad relative profiles; they do not function as a score transcription.
- Dialogue pitch proxies are 40-second rendered windows that may include BGM and other speakers. They are corroborative, not decisive.
- The `叶えたい、ことばかり` file is a full-song source with static cover imagery, not an authored MV. No visual dramaturgy is inferred from it.
- Shared-song authorship cannot be fully separated from composition and choreography until equivalent performances by additional idols are compared. Saki provides one partial comparison; a systematic multi-idol pass remains future work.
- `雨上がりのアイリス` belongs to the `U1` ensemble track and does not become an event in every solo-route biography.

# 2. The audiovisual thesis: authored combustion

## 2.1 Why “cool beauty with a gap” is inadequate

Temari's popular surface can be summarized as a cool, technically gifted idol whose private self is needy, gluttonous, lazy, childish, and emotionally explosive. The AV corpus makes that description too shallow in two ways.

First, the “gap” is not merely comic contrast. Different voice states and visual modes correspond to different relational and ethical conditions. The childlike register may express pain, trust, manipulation, panic, appetite, or relief. The controlled register may express professionalism, concealment, considered tenderness, defeat acceptance, or dangerous self-suppression. Neither register has one fixed truth value.

Second, stage Temari is not produced by deleting the offstage states. Her image songs repeatedly translate them:

- `Luna say maybe` translates denial and twisted attachment;
- `アイヴイ` explicitly selects from many contradictory Temaris;
- `Unhappy Light` foregrounds the domestic and childlike self;
- `一体いつから` turns self-accusation and historical uncertainty into public scale;
- `ガラクタロード` includes cute, rock, exciting, and comic elements inside a newly authored cool category.

The stage is where contradiction receives form, not where contradiction vanishes.

## 2.2 Blue flame as relation between pressure and form

The metaphor works because Temari's audiovisual presence often combines:

- cool blue, teal, violet, black, or white visual fields;
- comparatively contained or geometrically disciplined framing;
- emotionally pressurized material;
- sharp changes in projection, posture, or scale;
- a body shown working hard enough that control cannot be mistaken for effortlessness.

`Luna say maybe`'s giant blue arena and microphone isolation, `アイヴイ`'s dark geometric stage, `一体いつから`'s white-violet light architecture, and `ガラクタロード`'s neon-black public swagger all use different surfaces. Their common function is not “icy aesthetics.” It is to make high affect appear deliberate.

The metaphor fails if it becomes a genre rule. `Unhappy Light` has the darkest relative spectral profile in the supplied full-mix measurements yet uses a pastel domestic visual world. `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！` is the brightest measured signal and the most hyper-cute visual performance. Both remain Temari works because the stable identity lies in the management and public ownership of a state, not in one color, tempo, or arrangement.

## 2.3 Multiplicity: from shame to selection

The official `Luna say maybe` MV multiplies Temari through portraits, panels, running figures, interfaces, typography, and emotionally inconsistent images. The effect is not a stable idol portrait but a self trying to define itself amid media saturation and internal contradiction.

`アイヴイ`'s commu supplies the interpretive next step. Temari asks the Producer to make something “Temari-like.” He imagines the crying, spoiled, irrational, angry Temari as well as the strict, cool, solitary, beautiful one. The final definition is not that only the attractive states are real. Rather, an idol performance is a deliberate selection from a person who has many states.

By `ガラクタロード`, Temari no longer asks whether the constructed category is counterfeit. She intends to alter the category until her construction becomes a public reference point.

## 2.4 The body remains the bill that performance must pay

Temari's route theory can sound almost idealist: imitation becomes inheritance, artificial wings become aircraft, and a produced self becomes real through authorship. The rendered body prevents that argument from becoming disembodied.

The `ガラクタロード` commu ends with Temari audibly and visibly spent after singing rehearsal as if it were the actual performance. She jokes, but the Producer reacts with fear. This is not generic “idol effort.” It is the exact danger identified throughout her textual route: a peak ability whose cost exceeds the body's current sustainability.

The AV corpus therefore supports two simultaneous claims:

1. Temari's full-force performance is not fake intensity. It has a material cost.
2. That cost does not automatically justify the method used to produce it.

The Producer's accomplishment is partly technical: he tries to keep the peak without destroying the performer. The unresolved risk is that he and Temari may admire the peak enough to normalize the danger.

## 2.5 Public/private is not truth/mask

The private and public modes overlap.

- The most childish sobbing appears in an extremely serious confrontation with Rinha.
- One of the most mature scenes occurs after victory, when Temari reads an adult's tears and lends him a handkerchief.
- The cool stage image is explicitly built from a private relational gaze.
- The domestic `Unhappy Light` self becomes official image-song material.
- Re;IRIS lets the competitive soloist participate in mutual gaze and distributed focus without ceasing to be herself.

The mature model is therefore not “be the private self in public.” It is “stop using publicness and privacy to rank selves as real and false.”

# 3. Speaking voice and acted personhood

## 3.1 Voice-state inventory

The full commu corpus supports the following provisional state model:

| state | performed features | common function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `T-V0` compact professional control | measured pace, clear articulation, reduced outward volatility | evaluation, planning, post-loss containment, formal conversation |
| `T-V1` abrasive/direct Temari | clipped replies, dry attack, audible impatience, fast correction | boundary setting, rivalry, resistance to management |
| `T-V2` childlike need and panic | higher mobile pitch, elongated vowels, sobbing, pleading, food-transparent delight | pain, trust, fear, attachment, comic exposure |
| `T-V3` combustive protest | strong projection, sharp amplitude expansion, clear words inside anger or tears | refusal, demand, competitive reactivation |
| `T-V4` lowered intimate trust | softer projection, longer pauses, reduced social armor | self-hatred, dependence, first-name intimacy, receiving care |
| `T-V5` formal stage/public address | disciplined projection, declarative cadence, audience-facing clarity | idol identity, title acceptance, public promise |
| `T-V6` playful/cute performance | deliberately exaggerated brightness, comic timing, embarrassment accepted as entertainment | novelty songs, food/comedy, flexible public image |

These states are not mutually exclusive. Temari can begin in `T-V0`, rupture into `T-V2/T-V3`, and reconstruct a `T-V5` conclusion within one scene.

## 3.2 Early overwork: permission as intimacy and danger

In the early STEP1 scene, Temari begins relatively contained. The rendered model then shows flushed strain, downward gaze, clenched hands, and a quick return to determined posture once the Producer authorizes harder work.

The acting changes the ethical meaning of the exchange. Permission to approach the limit is not received as cold instruction. It produces relief. Temari hears it as evidence that the Producer understands the person she is trying to become.

That explains his appeal and intensifies the risk. A boundary-crossing plan can feel like recognition precisely because restraint previously felt like contempt or containment.

## 3.3 Artificial wings: shame spoken in a lowered register

Dear Idol 010 gives the phrase `私の翼は造り物で、本物じゃない` in a scene where Temari is not simply performing cool disdain. The visual and vocal state is comparatively vulnerable and searching. She asks whether the Producer truly chooses her over Misuzu, then protects herself through dry humor and a command to follow her to the highest place.

The sequence shows how ambition functions defensively:

> disclose inferiority → request selection → receive selection → turn the answer into a future task.

The future-directed command is not proof that the vulnerability disappeared. It is how Temari makes the vulnerability livable.

## 3.4 Rinha confession: the child state is filial truth, not comic residue

The Rinha confrontation produces the widest visibly and audibly volatile state in the selected corpus. Temari folds forward, cries openly, clenches and raises her hands, and erupts into elongated `ヤダヤダヤダヤダ` pleading. The derived window also has the highest pitch proxy and one of the largest rendered dynamic ranges among the selected scenes; because the metric includes the full mix, the image and exact dialogue remain the decisive evidence.

What she confesses is not merely fear of retirement. She tells Rinha that singing, dancing, and even her cool `キャラ作り` were formed through continued respect and imitation.

The childlike register is appropriate because the relationship is partly filial. Rinha is an origin figure. Temari's apparently least composed voice communicates a structural truth that the composed idol could not say.

This is not the revelation that child-Temari is the only authentic one. It is the moment when the authored idol acknowledges one of her authors.

## 3.5 Misuzu: from managed child to rival speech

Temari's voice with Misuzu moves among irritation, familiarity, panic, admiration, and challenge. The most important development is that rivalry can be spoken directly.

When Misuzu becomes capable of producing a performance that makes Temari a fan, Temari's response is not only humiliation. She can remain in relation while recognizing a world that is not organized around caretaking her. Later, the airplane/rocket scene permits both intimacy and competitive escalation:

- Misuzu validates constructed wings;
- Temari accepts the metaphor;
- each immediately claims the higher flight.

The alternating warmth and provocation is a healthier grammar than silent management and accumulated resentment.

## 3.6 Loss to Sena: low containment without emotional erasure

After summer H.I.F., Temari says `負けは負けです`, “a loss is a loss.” The performance is comparatively contained. That does not mean the loss is emotionally shallow. Her restraint lets her separate two claims:

- she materially changed the competition and performed extraordinarily;
- she did not win.

The Producer supplies the angry, partial refusal that Temari chooses not to perform. Temari then recognizes his frustration as relational support. Like Saki's audiovisual pass, this scene distinguishes behavioral direction from completed affective recovery, but Temari's characteristic strategy is different: she compresses the response into judgment and reorients through another person's visible excess.

## 3.7 First-name sendoff: chosen intimacy as performance preparation

Before the winter final, Temari asks the Producer to stop using `月村さん` and send her out by calling her simply `手毬`. The request is small in physical scale and enormous in relational meaning.

The name change does not dissolve professionalism. It is used to prepare professional action. Temari chooses a more intimate address as the form of recognition she wants immediately before public performance.

The scene therefore condenses the whole route:

> dependence is not eliminated; it is named, requested, and converted into authored action.

## 3.8 Prima Stella and post-victory self-love

The title scenes do not present a permanently majestic Temari. Public declaration and private disorientation coexist.

She can accept the title, credit fans/rivals/Producer, refuse to protect the summit passively, and insist that the next task begins immediately. She can also admit that her emotions have not caught up, observe the Producer sobbing, lend him a handkerchief, joke about ramen, and state:

> `私は、今日の私が一番好き！　いまのあなたが一番好き！`

The scene is warm and reciprocal. Its temporal grammar matters. Temari loves **today's** self and **the present** Producer, then immediately seeks the next horizon. The AV evidence strengthens present-tense self-love; it does not establish self-love independent of motion, success, or the partnership.

## 3.9 Cross-scene acting rule

Temari's higher register cannot be read generically as immaturity. It may indicate panic, pain, joy, anger, filial attachment, or deliberate comic play. Her lower register cannot be read generically as coolness. It may indicate control, shame, reflection, tenderness, exhaustion, or self-suppression.

Interpretation requires the full configuration:

> pitch/projection + pacing + body + interlocutor + BGM/staging + what Temari does after the line.

# 4. Singing identity and musical profile

## 4.1 A stable expressive procedure, not one genre

Temari's personal catalog has a recognizable center: modern, dense production; clear foregrounding of a solo voice; strong contrast between contained passages and large emotional release; cool or dark visual environments that can carry very hot material.

But the wider corpus disproves a one-genre identity. She remains legible in school pop, prescribed competition material, theatrical/gothic abstraction, digital aggression, hyper-cute novelty, domestic introspection, and unit song.

Her portable signature is better described through performance decisions:

- precise attack and intelligibility under pressure;
- phrases treated as addressed statements rather than decorative melody;
- an ability to make contained posture feel pressurized rather than passive;
- sudden expansion that reads as release of accumulated force;
- tension between solitary centrality and an audible need for an interlocutor;
- willingness to let embarrassment, anger, need, or comedy become part of the idol image.

Without isolated stems, these remain full-performance observations rather than claims about studio vocal anatomy.

## 4.2 Comparative full-mix signal profile

The derived measurements are deliberately limited. They do show useful relative tendencies:

- `Unhappy Light` has the lowest mean spectral centroid and lowest flatness in the supplied set, matching its comparatively softer/darker full-mix field without proving any single instrument choice.
- `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！` has the highest centroid and flatness, matching its visually hyper-bright, high-information novelty presentation.
- `ENDLESS DANCE` and `Howling over the World` occupy brighter full-mix profiles than the foundational `Luna say maybe` official source.
- the core songs do not collapse into one measured surface; the character identity must therefore be sought in form and delivery as well as spectrum.

Exact numerical results are preserved in the evidence matrix. Tempo/key estimates are not promoted into interpretive claims because compressed video sources, silence padding, and half/double-time ambiguity make them less reliable than the relative spectral/dynamic values.

## 4.3 `Luna say maybe`: disowned resemblance becomes song

### Official MV

The authored MV is a visual argument about unstable self-definition. It uses cyan, blue, black, white, lime, and occasional warm accents; repeated Temari portraits; interfaces; circular markers; running profile; close-up tears; oversized typography; and changes between flat graphic space and emotionally saturated portraiture.

Temari is both the object being examined and the person trying to outrun the examination.

### 3DMV

The 3DMV strips away much of that collage and places Temari in a vast dark arena at a microphone stand. Movement is comparatively restrained; scale is supplied by the luminous audience and large environmental light changes. The body reads as contained because the song's pressure is being organized through voice and direct gaze.

### Commu relation

Temari initially says the song's girl is too different from her, yet cannot stop humming and performing the material. The contradiction is the point. The song permits resemblance without requiring direct self-identification.

### Character conclusion

`Luna say maybe` establishes song as **indirect truth technology**. Temari can sing a resemblance she cannot yet admit in ordinary speech.

## 4.4 `アイヴイ`: selection and the Producer's gaze

The commu stages several rejected reductions:

- childish Temari alone is not the answer;
- angry Temari alone is not the answer;
- cool Temari alone is also incomplete.

The final PV is “Temari-like” because the Producer understands the plurality and knows what to carry onto the stage. Temari's delighted inner response matters: she discovers that she appears cool and beautiful in a trusted observer's gaze.

The 3DMV's dark blue/teal/purple architecture, black costume, triangular/wave panels, back-arching silhouette, and more expansive bodily movement make coolness feel authored and sensuous rather than emotionally absent.

The ethical ambiguity also sharpens. Temari asks for harsher training so that the promoted image will be deserved by the fans. Image authorship immediately becomes bodily demand.

## 4.5 `Unhappy Light`: vulnerability becomes official image

The MV centers the space Temari's cool persona might be expected to exclude:

- bed and couch;
- alarm clock;
- bare or casual domestic posture;
- crying child imagery;
- sunset transition;
- a cat approached with an open hand;
- waking and leaving.

Its pastel cyan world is visually gentle without denying sadness. The relative full-mix signal is also the least bright/noise-like in the measured set.

The song therefore supplies crucial counterevidence to a narrow blue-flame theory. Temari's authored identity can make low energy, private need, and ordinary longing publicly meaningful. Combustion is not always an explosion; it can be the quiet conversion of an unhappy interior into a form someone else can recognize.

## 4.6 `一体いつから`: historical self-interrogation and learned plurality

The 3DMV stages Temari alone in a large white-violet arena. Geometric light lines, distant audience fields, near-monochrome passages, reaching gestures, kneeling or contracted postures, and the moon near the end make the song feel like private historical questioning projected onto public scale.

The related commu matters because Temari does not simply discover another intense confession. She trains with Misuzu and China and recognizes several ways of putting feeling into song:

- Misuzu's deliberate control of audience response;
- Temari's own full-force emotional projection.

This is technical and ethical development. She can learn another person's method without treating imitation as humiliation, and she can rely on Misuzu while naming that reliance rather than disguising it as solitary genius.

The visible and audible panting afterward prevents the declaration from becoming mere branding. The category is being produced through bodily expenditure.

## 5.1 `Luna say maybe`: resemblance without admission

The self is encountered as someone else's lyric/persona. Temari can perform the feeling before she can own the resemblance.

## 5.2 `アイヴイ`: plurality curated through a trusted gaze

Temari studies other people's affective methods, names dependence without humiliation, and makes historical self-questioning the material of a solo performance.

## 5.4 `ガラクタロード`: the constructed category becomes a public project

Temari no longer asks whether she is naturally cool. She and the Producer define the kind of cool they intend to make socially real.

## 5.5 What changes

- denial becomes acknowledgment;
- imitation becomes cited inheritance;
- singular full-force expression becomes a repertoire of methods;
- private image anxiety becomes public category authorship;
- dependence becomes something that can be requested and named.

## 5.6 What does not change

- self-regard remains highly directional;
- the body still pays for total-force performance;
- competition remains a central language of intimacy;
- the Producer remains an unusually powerful witness and co-author;
- stillness remains difficult to value for its own sake.

# 8. Revision to the Phase-3 character thesis

## 8.1 Revised compact thesis

> **Tsukimura Temari is the idol of authored combustion: a person who converts emotional excess, imitation, bodily need, dependency, and self-disgust into chosen public form. Her development is not the discovery of a natural self beneath performance, but increasing authorship over which Temari appears, how different states relate, and what her constructed coolness will mean to other people.**

## 8.2 Revised account of coolness

Coolness is neither low affect nor simple technical precision. It is the capacity to make pressure legible without losing form. It can include tears, anger, cute comedy, domestic sadness, group belonging, and visible exhaustion.

## 8.3 Revised account of song

Song begins as indirect confession, becomes curated image, then becomes a learnable social technique and finally a vehicle for redefining a public category.

## 8.4 Revised account of dependence

Temari's healthier state is not self-sufficiency. It is dependence that can be named, contested, reciprocated, and converted into action without automatically fixing her in the role of the weak child. The ethical question is whether that dependence remains revisable when Temari wants something dangerous.

## 8.5 Revised account of self-love

The late declaration is genuine and significant. Its strength lies in being present-tense rather than metaphysically unconditional. Temari can love the self she has made today. Future phases must test whether she can continue loving herself when movement is impossible or when her chosen direction changes.
