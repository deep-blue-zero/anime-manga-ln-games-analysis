# TSUKIMURA TEMARI — MUSIC, MV, AND PERFORMANCE CLOSE READING

## 0. Purpose and conclusion

This document isolates the music/MV portion of Temari's Phase-3 AV baseline. Its central conclusion is that Temari possesses a recognizable **performance grammar** rather than a single genre identity.

That grammar combines pressure, clarity, direct address, and deliberate selection. It can inhabit severe electronic spectacle, domestic introspection, school pop, prescribed repertoire, gothic abstraction, novelty comedy, and ensemble performance. The widely useful `青い炎` metaphor describes the disciplined relation between affect and form, not a requirement that every Temari work sound or look cold, dark, or aggressive.

The strongest developmental sequence is:

> `Luna say maybe` → `アイヴイ` → `一体いつから` → `ガラクタロード`

It moves from disowned resemblance, through curated plurality and learned emotional technique, toward public category authorship.

# 1. Method

## 1.1 Objects inspected

Fifteen supplied files represent thirteen musical works, with both official/2D and 3DMV forms available for `Luna say maybe` and both full-song and 3DMV forms available for `アイヴイ`.

## 1.2 Visual method

Visual claims are based on:

- full-motion inspection;
- keyframe and uniform contact sheets;
- camera distance and angle;
- costume and body silhouette;
- color and lighting fields;
- spatial relation to crowd, screen, and other performers;
- editing and graphic multiplication where the official MV supplies it.

An official authored MV and a game 3DMV are treated as different layers of authorship. The former can build a song-specific symbolic argument; the latter tests how Temari embodies the material on the standardized game stage.

## 1.3 Sonic method

Sonic conclusions combine direct full-mix inspection and conservative derived measurements. No isolated vocal stems are available. Spectral centroid, flatness, onset density, and percentile dynamic range are used only for relative comparison among the supplied files.

# 2. `Luna say maybe`

## 2.1 Narrative position

The song is Temari's foundational image-song statement because its commu begins with denial. She insists that the girl described is too different from her, yet continues humming and eventually performing the material. Song precedes self-admission.

## 2.2 Official MV: fragmented self-definition

The authored MV uses a cyan/blue/black/white system punctured by lime, pink, and orange. Temari appears as portrait, running figure, interface object, repeated panel, tearful close-up, and typography-framed silhouette. Circular markers and UI-like elements make the self look observed, indexed, and edited.

The repeated figures do not yet form a peaceful plurality. They suggest a person moving through several incompatible readings of herself.

## 2.3 3DMV: confession under containment

The game performance removes most of the graphic collage. Temari stands alone with a microphone in a huge dark blue-purple arena. The body is comparatively contained; the audience and environmental light supply scale.

That staging makes her voice the main vector of expansion. The performance does not need constant choreography to read as intense. Stillness becomes pressure.

## 2.4 Sonic profile

The official source sits near the middle of the corpus in spectral brightness. The 3DMV source has a broader measured percentile range, partly because of stage lead-in/out. Neither measurement alone defines the song. The important formal contrast is between a dense authored image field and a physically restrained arena performance.

## 2.5 Character conclusion

`Luna say maybe` establishes **indirect self-recognition**. Temari can inhabit a confession before she can name it as hers.

# 3. `アイヴイ`

## 3.1 Narrative position

`アイヴイ` is the first work in the supplied corpus whose accompanying story explicitly asks what a “Temari-like” public image should contain.

## 3.2 Rejected reductions

The Producer imagines several possible PVs:

- the crying, spoiled child;
- the unreasonable angry girl;
- the severe cool idol.

Each becomes false when isolated. The final answer is plural: Temari contains all of them, while the stage carries especially compelling elements because performance is selective by design.

## 3.3 3DMV visual field

The stage uses dark blue, teal, and purple; black costume; luminous triangular or wave-like panels; and a more expansive bodily silhouette than `Luna say maybe`. Back-arching and longer limb lines make coolness sensuous and physically authored rather than simply remote.

## 3.4 Image, fans, and bodily demand

Temari's response to promotion is to demand more severe training so she can show fans something worthy. This joins image construction to bodily risk. She does not treat the PV as a substitute for ability; she treats visibility as an obligation to intensify ability.

## 3.5 Character conclusion

`アイヴイ` changes the question from “Which Temari is real?” to “Which Temari is being deliberately placed on this stage, by whom, and why?”

# 4. `Unhappy Light`

## 4.1 Why it matters

This is the strongest counterexample to a narrow cool-image account. It centers the domestic, sleepy, crying, ordinary, and childlike Temari without treating those states as a scandal.

## 4.2 MV architecture

The MV uses a pastel cyan room, bed, couch, alarm clock, casual body, crying child, sunset, and cat. The visual rhythm moves through waking, avoidance, memory, contact, and departure.

The cat encounter is particularly useful. An open hand replaces command. Recognition becomes quiet and noncompetitive.

## 4.3 Signal profile

Among the supplied works, `Unhappy Light` has the lowest mean spectral centroid and the lowest flatness. That does not prove a specific arrangement, but it corroborates the perception of a comparatively less bright and less noise-like full-mix field.

## 4.4 Character conclusion

Mature Temari's image can contain low energy and private need. The public persona does not have to erase the person on the bed in order to remain authored.

# 5. `一体いつから`

## 5.1 STEP3 developmental center

The work belongs to the period in which Temari can acknowledge Misuzu and Rinha as continuing sources rather than embarrassing dependencies.

## 5.2 3DMV visual grammar

A white-gray costume stands against violet-white geometry and a vast audience field. Temari is frequently isolated in long shot. Reaching, contracted posture, kneeling, near-monochrome passages, and the final moon image turn historical self-questioning into arena-scale form.

## 5.3 Technical plurality in the commu

Training with Misuzu and China lets Temari compare affective techniques. China's gentleness and Misuzu's audience control are not inferior because they are less explosively “Temari-like.” Temari can learn without framing learning as proof of counterfeit identity.

## 5.4 Character conclusion

`一体いつから` turns introspection into **studied expressive practice**. The singer who once treated total force as the only honest method begins to acquire a repertoire.

# 6. `Campus mode!! [月村手毬]`

## 6.1 Common material and institutional brightness

The performance uses bright multicolor staging, balloons/confetti, broad smiles, and a sporty-cute costume. Temari does not darken the song to preserve identity.

## 6.2 Inheritance

The song commu places performance in relation to Rinha and Hatsuboshi tradition. Temari's interest in comparison turns common repertoire into a field of lineage and rivalry.

## 6.3 Counterevidence

This performance weakens any claim that Temari's signature depends on aloofness, darkness, or solitary staging. Her precision and directness survive open cheerfulness.

---

# 7. `ガラクタロード [月村手毬]`

## 7.1 Prescribed material

Because `ガラクタロード` is common H.I.F. material, it provides a better test of performed individuality than an exclusive image song.

## 7.2 Visual field

The black costume, neon multicolor architecture, open-arm gestures, direct gaze, and teasing facial expression create a performance that is simultaneously cool, cute, rock-oriented, comic, and exciting.

## 7.3 The blue-flame argument

The commu defines Temari's coolness as high-temperature complete combustion rather than ice. The rehearsal then leaves her panting. Form and cost are inseparable.

## 7.4 Public category authorship

Temari's conclusion is not “I have proven I naturally belong to the category.” It is “I will make this version the real one.” Authenticity becomes prospective, social, and performative.

## 7.5 Comparative limit

A complete multi-idol comparison is still required before attributing every difference to Temari rather than costume, camera, or shared choreography.

# 8. `がむしゃらに行こう！ [月村手毬]`

## 8.1 Comic effort

The classroom/school stage, warm pastel-green palette, cute athletic costume, and kinetic choreography make effort public without requiring perfect dignity.

## 8.2 Relation to peers

The commu places Saki and Kotone inside the same performance field. Temari's comparison drive becomes a social and aesthetic practice rather than only a response to existential threat.

## 8.3 Character consequence

The image survives embarrassment. This is evidence of greater persona resilience.

---

# 9. `初 [月村手毬]`

The white costume, ornate stage, lightstick audience, and open-hand address place Temari inside foundational Hatsuboshi imagery. Her identity remains legible through disciplined directness and controlled expansion rather than song-specific trauma content.

The work is evidence of institutional belonging without self-erasure.

---

# 10. `ENDLESS DANCE [月村手毬]`

The gothic interior, throne/stairs, black costume, magenta-green palette, playing-card and heart motifs, and manga-like inserts emphasize theatrical artificiality.

Temari adapts readily because her character already treats persona construction as a legitimate artistic practice. The work extends authored image rather than threatening authenticity.

---

# 11. `Howling over the World [月村手毬]`

Digital glitch, duplicated Temaris, screens, and cyan-red neon turn multiplicity outward. The repeated self no longer reads primarily as uncertainty; it becomes attack and broadcast.

This forms a useful formal contrast with `Luna say maybe`.

---

# 12. `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！ [月村手毬]`

The performance uses hyper-bright pastel/neon color, a green cute costume, chat/streaming/recording-booth motifs, plushies, and meme-like play. It also has the highest measured spectral centroid and flatness in the supplied set.

Its analytical value is adversarial: if Temari were coherent only while severe, the song would dissolve her. Instead it exposes an already real comic and childlike capacity and turns it into deliberate entertainment.

---

# 13. `叶えたい、ことばかり`

The supplied source is audio with static cover imagery. It broadens the sonic and affective sample toward ordinary desire and softness. No motion/camera argument is made.

This work is a reminder that not every meaningful Temari song needs to dramatize maximal crisis.

---

# 14. `雨上がりのアイリス`

## 14.1 Group form

Re;IRIS uses rotating focus, mutual gaze, shared lines, hand gestures, and warm white-purple staging. Temari can leave the visual center without disappearing.

## 14.2 Listening and jealousy

The commu lets Temari promise to listen to the Producer while also revealing jealousy and exclusive attachment. Distributed artistic focus and relational possessiveness can coexist.

## 14.3 Continuity caution

This is `U1` evidence. It proves capacity, not an event common to every solo biography.

# 16. Final music/MV thesis

Temari's music does not reveal a natural singer beneath a manufactured idol. It reveals that manufacture is one of her most truthful practices.

Her stable signature is the capacity to make a chosen form carry more feeling than its surface seems able to contain. As her repertoire expands, authorship becomes less defensive. She can cite influence, use another person's gaze, learn another singer's technique, perform common material, become cute or comic, and share the frame without treating those acts as evidence that “Tsukimura Temari” has disappeared.

`青い炎` is therefore valid as a dramaturgical principle:

> **not coolness as absence of heat, but heat disciplined into a recognizable public shape.**
