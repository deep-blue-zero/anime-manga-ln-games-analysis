# TSUKIMURA TEMARI CORE READING — AUDIOVISUAL REVISION ADDENDUM

## 0. Revision status

The parent text-first reading remains canonical as the Phase-3 textual artifact. This addendum records what the supplied audiovisual evidence confirms, strengthens, narrows, or leaves unresolved. It should be imported into the later definitive Temari monograph after events and support-card evidence are processed.

---

# 1. Revised executive formulation

## Parent claim

Temari is the idol of authored combustion. Her coolness is an authored truth, not a fake mask; imitation, dependence, and constructed performance can become valid parts of personhood.

## AV revision

**Strongly confirmed.** The stable audiovisual feature is not coldness, darkness, or one genre. It is emotional pressure shaped into public form. The blue-flame metaphor accurately describes the relation between control and force, while `Unhappy Light`, `ミラクルナナウ`, and Re;IRIS prove that the mature image can contain softness, comedy, and distributed attention.

## Revised formulation

> Temari's authored combustion is a **performance procedure**, not a fixed aesthetic: she selects, concentrates, and directs contradictory states without requiring the unselected states to become unreal.

---

# 2. Coolness

## Parent claim

`クール` matures from borrowed image into `青い炎`.

## AV revision

**Confirmed and narrowed.** Luna/Ivy/Ittai/Garakuta repeatedly pair controlled staging or cool visual fields with high emotional pressure. But blue flame cannot be treated as a requirement that every song sound aggressive. Its validity lies in disciplined intensity, which may be quiet in `Unhappy Light` or comic in `ミラクルナナウ`.

---

# 3. Public persona and authenticity

## Parent claim

The cool persona is an authored truth rather than fraud.

## AV revision

**Strongly confirmed.** `アイヴイ` explicitly stages several recognizable but insufficient reductions of Temari, then lets the Producer curate a public image from the plurality. `Unhappy Light` makes the vulnerable/private self official image material. `ガラクタロード` shifts from seeking admission to a category toward intending to redefine the category.

## Corrective rule

Do not write the final monograph as though offstage leakage disproves stage authenticity. Ask instead which state is being selected, who selects it, and what material cost sustains it.

---

# 4. Song as honest communication

## Parent claim

Song communicates what Temari cannot say directly.

## AV revision

**Confirmed and expanded.** `Luna say maybe` is indirect confession. `アイヴイ` is visual/persona selection. `一体いつから` is learned expressive plurality. `ガラクタロード` is public category authorship. Song does not merely reveal interiority; it trains Temari to organize and address it.

---

# 5. Artificial wings and inheritance

## Parent claim

Imitation and socially produced ability need not be counterfeit.

## AV revision

**Confirmed.** The Rinha confrontation's acted collapse gives the imitation confession filial force. The Ittai commu then shows influence becoming technique that can be named and learned. The airplane/rocket exchange turns constructed flight into competitive pride.

## Remaining limit

Achievement remains the main proof that constructed wings are worthy. Later phases must test whether construction can be valued during incapacity or failure.

---

# 6. Body, food, and sustainability

## Parent claim

The body is a central site of shame, care, pleasure, and control.

## AV revision

**Strengthened.** Early overwork is acted as relief when harder effort is authorized. Garakuta rehearsal ends in visible/audible exhaustion. The Producer's matching strategy preserves peak intensity rather than merely calming Temari.

## Ethical revision

The concern is no longer only whether others restrain Temari too much. It is also whether accurate recognition becomes an enabling relationship in which dangerous expenditure feels like love.

---

# 7. Producer relationship

## Parent claim

The Producer replaces containment with chosen dangerous partnership; the relationship is reciprocal but asymmetric.

## AV revision

**Reciprocity strengthened; asymmetry unresolved.**

- Ivy grants him image authorship.
- Garakuta grants him conceptual authorship.
- Dear 035 lets Temari request the intimate name used to launch performance.
- Dear 037 reverses care when she reads his tears and lends the handkerchief.
- ReIRIS shows listening alongside jealousy/exclusivity.

The final monograph should describe the pair as reciprocal co-authors without implying equal institutional power.

---

# 8. Misuzu

## Parent claim

Misuzu becomes viable as rival rather than caretaker.

## AV revision

**Strengthened.** Temari can be made into Misuzu's fan, study Misuzu's expressive method, receive the airplane metaphor, and return warmth as rivalry. The relation becomes more speakable rather than less intense.

## Remaining limit

Misuzu's own core pass may substantially revise whether she experiences this form as freedom.

---

# 9. Rinha

## Parent claim

Rinha is teacher, model, rival, and one of the authors of Temari.

## AV revision

**Strongly confirmed.** The visible sobbing and vocal rupture show that Rinha's departure threatens Temari's continuity of self, not merely competitive opportunity.

---

# 10. Unit capacity

## Parent claim

U1 independently shows Temari can inhabit direct conflict, distributed leadership, and nonfused relationships.

## AV revision

**Confirmed within U1.** `雨上がりのアイリス` uses rotating focus and mutual gaze. Temari can recede and return without loss of identity. The commu preserves jealousy, so distributed stage focus does not equal fully horizontal attachment.

---

# 11. Self-love

## Parent claim

`今日の私が一番好き` is genuine but not proven independent of achievement.

## AV revision

**Confirmed exactly.** The post-victory scene is warm, reciprocal, and emotionally real, but the grammar remains present-tense and immediately future-oriented. Do not upgrade it to unconditional self-acceptance.

---

# 12. Final import instruction for the definitive monograph

The definitive Temari monograph should inherit the following AV-backed propositions:

1. coolness is disciplined intensity, not emotional absence;
2. public/private does not map onto false/true;
3. song progresses from indirect confession to public category authorship;
4. imitation is affective lineage and learnable technique;
5. bodily cost remains visible inside the ideal of complete combustion;
6. Producer reciprocity is real, while concentration of authorship power remains ethically open;
7. Misuzu can become rival and teacher without restoring SyngUp!;
8. Re;IRIS proves distributed-center capacity within U1;
9. late self-love is real, present-tense, and still directional.

These claims must still be tested against numbered events, support cards, Misuzu's core pass, and ordinary-life evidence.
