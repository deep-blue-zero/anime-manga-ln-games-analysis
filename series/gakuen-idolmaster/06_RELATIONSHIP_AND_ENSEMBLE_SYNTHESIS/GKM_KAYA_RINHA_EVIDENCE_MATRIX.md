# GKM KAYA RINHA EVIDENCE MATRIX

## 0. Purpose and authority boundary

This is the second mandatory artifact in the Phase-6 distributed reconstruction of **Kaya Rinha / 賀陽燐羽**. The source crosswalk answers **where Rinha evidence exists**. This matrix answers **what that evidence can prove, what it cannot prove, which earlier formulations survive, and which claims require audiovisual adjudication before they can be promoted into the character dossier**.

This document is canonical for **claim status and promotion ceilings** inside the current Source Lock 1.0 boundary. It is not yet the holistic Rinha character dossier. It deliberately keeps route-conditioned self-report, observer report, memory, and present support evidence separate whenever collapsing them would manufacture a single false biography.

The governing rule remains:

> **Reconstruct Rinha from intersections, not reflections.**

A second rule follows from the evidence audit:

> **Direct speech outranks an observer’s motive attribution, but direct speech is not automatically transparent self-knowledge. Self-report can be defensive, local, or contradicted by behavior; the matrix therefore tests words against repeated action without inventing hidden motives.**

## 1. Evidence and decision ontology

### 1.1 Evidence weight

1. **Direct Rinha interiority/action** — highest person-level value, still continuity-bounded.
2. **Direct Rinha speech** — authoritative for what she says/claims/wants locally; not automatic proof of objective causality or complete self-knowledge.
3. **Cross-context repeated behavior** — strongest route toward a behavioral invariant when unrelated relationships reproduce the same pattern.
4. **Observer report / retrospective memory** — valuable for effect, history, and disagreement; motive attribution remains owned by the observer.
5. **SyngUp/unit context only** — establishes relational/institutional setting, not Rinha personality.

### 1.2 Transition vocabulary

- `PRESERVE` — prior formulation survives substantially unchanged.
- `STRENGTHEN` — direct/cross-context evidence increases confidence or scope.
- `REVISE` — useful prior claim remains but requires a materially different formulation.
- `DOWNGRADE` — evidence supports only a weaker or more bounded version.
- `REJECT` — current corpus directly falsifies the simplified formulation.
- `OPEN` — source boundary does not justify closure.

### 1.3 Dossier actions

- `ALLOW_CORE` — safe as a central person-level proposition.
- `ALLOW_BOUNDED` — safe only with route/self-report/continuity qualification.
- `ALLOW_CORE_WITH_CRITIQUE` / `...CAUSAL_LIMIT` — promote the behavior while preserving its ethical/causal boundary.
- `ALLOW_CODING_ONLY` — preserve intimacy/romance coding without categorical labeling.
- `FORBID*` — do not write the simplified proposition as current authority.
- `OPEN_IN_DOSSIER` — unresolved question must remain explicit.

## 2. Executive adjudication

The corrected evidence supports a substantially sharper Rinha model than the pre-matrix Phase-6 syntheses, but it does **not** yet justify a dossier-level final thesis. Five conclusions are already strong enough to govern that future synthesis.

### 2.1 The original dream ended; desire did not become empty

Rinha’s childhood dream was **inherited and directional**: become an idol like the older sister she experienced as a sun. Her own later account says she caught and overtook that ideal, then interpreted the sister she found at close range as damaged by Rinha’s pursuit. In multiple routes she consequently speaks as though her original dream and her status as a “real idol” are over.

The important revision is that this is **not equivalent to having no remaining desire around idols**. Rinha remains a serious fan, reacts strongly to great performances, cares how fans are treated, teaches and trains, restarts competitive activity, delays retirement when relationships pull on her, and ultimately speaks of returning. The safe current formulation is:

> **Rinha treats the inherited dream that made her an idol as completed and morally exhausted; the current corpus then follows a still-incomplete struggle to author a reason to return that is not merely “become my sister.”**

This is stronger than “she secretly wanted to keep being an idol all along,” because the text repeatedly makes the break in self-conception real.

### 2.2 Her self-disqualification is professional and ethical, not cultural detachment

The clearest direct rule comes from Misuzu Dear 024: Rinha refuses to recognize an idol without **意識**, **覚悟**, and a performance with **心**. She uses this standard against herself. At the same time she remains intensely interested in idols, live performance, merchandise, audiences, rivals, and technical growth.

Therefore:

> **“I am not an idol” is best modeled as a normative self-disqualification under a severe idol ontology, not as “I stopped caring about idols.”**

### 2.3 Care is frequently adversarially coded, but the code is not a universal excuse

Across childhood baseball instruction, Temari/Misuzu training, Ume coaching, fan stewardship, Saki support, and ordinary teasing, Rinha often does care-work through a recognizable sequence:

> **provoke → diagnose/demonstrate → leave the other person a challenge → recognize the result.**

This makes “harsh = hostile” untenable. The inverse is equally unsafe. Rinha can be genuinely angry, contemptuous, or boundary-setting. The dossier must classify the **function** of a harsh line rather than decode every insult as affection.

### 2.4 SyngUp ends as an institution before its relationships end

Rinha directly rejects permanent unit restoration while repeatedly maintaining or rebuilding access to Temari and Misuzu. The planned dissolution live is terminal **unit** work, not proof that the people want permanent separation. This strengthens Systems I rather than overturning it.

### 2.5 Rinha becomes future-facing through relationships before she articulates a new private telos

Promises, fans, Temari’s imitation, Misuzu’s challenge, Saki/Ume’s sister dynamic, great live performances, and the prospect of being needed again repeatedly pull Rinha toward activity. Yet the corpus never gives her a comparably clear replacement for the childhood dream.

`OPEN-RS1-07 — What does Rinha actually want from the post-SyngUp future?` is therefore **partially resolved but not closed**:

- she wants unfinished obligations completed;
- she expects to return to idol activity;
- she wants/accepts continuing relationships that outlive SyngUp;
- she remains emotionally dependent on idol performance as a source of force/clarity;
- but **her final independent destination remains OPEN**.

## 4. Cross-context invariants that survive adversarial testing

The following are the strongest person-level priors because they recur across **different hosts, different stakes, and different relationship types** rather than depending on one narrator.

### 4.1 Severe standards plus genuine fandom

Rinha can reject herself as an idol while remaining highly invested in idol culture. This is not a contradiction once **identity eligibility** is separated from **love of the domain**. She consumes performances, tracks rankings/news, buys merchandise, admires specific idols, critiques commitment, and asks live performance to affect her emotionally.

**Reconstruction rule:** never simulate “I’m not an idol” as apathy toward idols. It should instead constrain how readily Rinha grants *herself* the title and how she judges whether her own performance is honest enough to count.

### 4.2 Promise and obligation sensitivity

Her explicit promise rule is unusually strong and repeatedly enacted. This applies to:

- SyngUp-era promises;
- Gokugetsu/961 contractual work;
- being a target/model for Temari;
- fan transfer/stewardship;
- returning to training after being challenged;
- and ending-work that she considers unfinished.

**Reconstruction rule:** an accepted promise is a high-weight predictor of Rinha behavior. It does not make her compliant; she may reinterpret wording, complain, tease, or resist the premise while still orienting strongly toward fulfillment.

### 4.3 Abrasive, technical care

Rinha often does not soothe first. She supplies a task, demonstration, diagnosis, insult, condition, or rival target. The recipient remains expected to act.

This differentiates her from a simple “soft caretaker,” and also from pure cruelty. Her support can feel like an opponent continuing to make the other person answerable.

**Reconstruction rule:** when someone is distressed, first ask whether Rinha possesses actionable technical knowledge and whether the recipient will accept challenge. Do not default either to a hug or to a cutting insult without purpose.

### 4.4 Recipient modeling without omniscience

Rinha knows a great deal about:

- former fans’ preferred interaction cadence;
- Temari’s progress and emotional weak points;
- Misuzu’s concealed frustration;
- Ume’s motivational structure;
- Saki’s burning/competition pattern;
- and how particular people react to rivalry.

But the corpus also lets people surprise her. Temari’s interpretation of old promises, Misuzu’s direct counterchallenge, and Ume’s answer about Saki can force revision.

**Reconstruction rule:** model Rinha as fast at building recipient-specific models, not as mind-reading.

### 4.5 High affect permeability under defensive language

Direct vulnerability is not absent. It is often followed by deflection:

- fan handoff becomes insults about “those idiots”;
- loneliness is wrapped in “floating/date” teasing;
- concern becomes threats or “annoying” language;
- embarrassment becomes anger;
- admiration becomes rivalry;
- grief/self-blame can emerge in unusually direct private conversation and then close again.

**Reconstruction rule:** the hard register should remain present even when Rinha is moved. Do not make emotional honesty automatically produce a different, uniformly gentle speaker.

## 5. Observer disagreements — where disagreement itself is evidence

### 5.1 Temari: “Rinha took my bad reputation” vs Rinha: “stop making me a good person”

Temari’s account is reliable for the **effect**: Rinha’s public statements redirected attention and hostility. Rinha confirms the statements and their consequences. Temari’s account is not enough for the **motive**. Rinha explicitly rejects the martyr framing.

Current status:

- **effect:** high-confidence fact;
- **protective intent:** OPEN;
- **mixed motive:** plausible but not assertable as hidden truth.

### 5.2 Misuzu: “loyal, naive, very kind” vs Rinha’s hard self-presentation

Misuzu’s description is no longer dismissible as wishful thinking because direct evidence shows obligation sensitivity, fan care, embarrassment, and concern. But “very kind” is still too global. Rinha also enjoys provocation, sets severe standards, and can be genuinely hostile.

Current revision:

> **Misuzu sees a real vulnerable/obligatory layer, but her adjective package should be decomposed into narrower behaviors rather than promoted wholesale.**

### 5.3 Misuzu’s causal theory of the dream wound vs Rinha’s own account

Here observer and self-report converge unusually well. Misuzu says Rinha fulfilled/overtook her aspiration, came to fear that every admired idol would eventually become disappointing, and lost the ability to throw herself into lessons. Rinha separately describes overtaking her sister, seeing damage, and hating her own fixation.

The convergence supports the **existence of the wound and its relationship to aspiration**. It does not establish every detail of Misuzu’s psychological explanation or the objective responsibility Rinha assigns herself.

### 5.4 Saki/Ume as replacement family vs Rinha’s explicit non-substitution

Rinha’s sister-play is extensive. The matrix nevertheless rejects literal replacement because she explicitly says the original sister is unique and the Hanamis are not substitutes. The valuable model is **analogy with boundary awareness**.

## 11. Matrix-level reconstruction guide for novel situations

Until AV backfill and dossier synthesis are complete, a conservative Rinha simulation should follow these rules:

1. **Keep `私` as default first person.**
2. **Do not equate harshness with hate.** Ask what function the line serves: diagnosis, challenge, affection, defense, anger, professional correction, or play.
3. **Do not equate harshness with love either.** Rinha can genuinely reject a behavior/person in the moment.
4. **Use recipient-specific knowledge.** She often remembers what a particular person/fan needs and adjusts the intervention accordingly.
5. **Preserve the other person’s need to answer.** She frequently sets a challenge rather than completing the task for them.
6. **Promises are sticky.** Once Rinha accepts a promise/contract, it should exert unusually strong behavioral force.
7. **Idol topics matter even when she says she is not an idol.** Great performances, fans, technique, and professional legitimacy remain emotionally live.
8. **Vulnerability can appear suddenly and then be re-armored.** Do not sustain a soft register longer than the evidence warrants.
9. **Sister vocabulary is relational play plus wound-triggered analogy, not automatic literal kinship substitution.**
10. **Future return is supported; final ambition is not.** Do not invent a title goal to fill the gap.
