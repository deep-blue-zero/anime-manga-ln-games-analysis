# GKM RELATIONSHIP SYSTEMS I — HANAMI, REVERSI, AND SYNGUP

## 0. Purpose, authority, and principal thesis

This is the first canonical Phase-6 relationship-system synthesis for the Gakuen Idolmaster V2 corpus. Its architecture-defined scope is intentionally narrow:

1. **Hanami Saki / Hanami Ume**;
2. **Katsuragi Lilja / Shiun Sumika / REVERSI**;
3. **Tsukimura Temari / Hataya Misuzu / Kaya Rinha / SyngUp!**.

The document does **not** attempt to absorb every important relation around those characters. Saki/Temari/Kotone, China/Ume/Hiro, Sena/Tsubame, class networks, senior/junior systems, succession beyond these three systems, and Producer-as-relational-node belong primarily to `GKM_RELATIONSHIP_SYSTEMS_II_RIVALRY_FRIENDSHIP_CLASS_AND_SUCCESSION.md`. They appear here only when they are necessary counterevidence or when they show that an originally closed relationship system has become permeable.

Likewise, this is **not** Kaya Rinha's definitive reconstruction. Architecture v2.4 requires a separate distributed-character branch:

- `GKM_KAYA_RINHA_SOURCE_CROSSWALK.md`;
- `GKM_KAYA_RINHA_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md`;
- `GKM_KAYA_RINHA_CHARACTER_DOSSIER.md`.

Rinha claims here are therefore bounded to what is necessary to understand SyngUp as a relationship system. Direct Rinha evidence, Temari/Misuzu observations, retrospective memory, and inference are kept conceptually separate.

### 0.1 Principal thesis — the relationship must become larger than its founding role

The three systems look very different on the surface. One is a sibling rivalry, one is a best-friend/unit partnership, and one is a broken middle-school idol unit whose members remain relationally entangled after dissolution. Yet the completed V2 source-facing pass reveals a common maturation problem:

> **A close relationship becomes sustainable when the people inside it can preserve attachment while reducing the founding role's right to define the other's whole future.**

The relevant founding roles are different:

- **Saki / Ume:** older sister who stays ahead ↔ younger sister who chases;
- **Lilja / Sumika:** believer who follows / receives courage ↔ strong friend who opens the world and must remain strong;
- **SyngUp:** center who needs support ↔ caretaker who regulates ↔ leader/model who coordinates and absorbs responsibility.

None of those roles is wholly false. Each grew from real love, real competence, real history, or real need. The problem appears when a useful relation becomes **totalizing**.

Saki's older-sister role becomes dangerous if Ume must remain behind so that Saki can remain necessary. Ume's pursuit becomes dangerous if following Saki is the only structure through which she can want a future.

Lilja's admiration becomes dangerous when Sumika experiences it as a requirement to remain the old prodigy. Sumika's protectiveness becomes dangerous when weakness must be hidden so Lilja can retain the strong friend she believes in. REVERSI's shared dream becomes dangerous if separate desire is experienced as betrayal.

Misuzu's care becomes dangerous when accurate knowledge of Temari's body becomes jurisdiction over how Temari is allowed to live. Temari's dependence becomes dangerous when receiving help proves, in her own mind, that she is permanently inferior. Rinha's leadership/model function becomes dangerous when the unit's asymmetry and public fallout allow her to carry blame and ending-work for everyone else.

The mature movement across all three systems is therefore **de-totalization without emotional evacuation**.

The characters do not become healthy by caring less.

They become healthier when:

- one person can arrive without the relation ending;
- one person can want something different without the bond becoming fraudulent;
- one person can refuse care without denying that the care was loving;
- one person can receive care without accepting the giver's whole theory of what they should be;
- one person can leave a unit role while remaining a friend, rival, witness, or future collaborator;
- and an old relationship can survive by changing its operating rules instead of restoring the earlier form.

### 0.2 The Phase-5 correction — relationship is not only access

The completed support-card pass adds a second governing layer. By Phase 5, a relationship is no longer adequately modeled as "who is close to whom." The evidence repeatedly distinguishes:

1. **access permission** — what this person may say, touch, ask, correct, or decide;
2. **recipient model** — what they know about the other person's body, preferences, fears, habits, and thresholds;
3. **brokerage** — whether they can carry care or information across a blocked edge;
4. **internalized model** — whether the other's likely response has become part of one's own decision process;
5. **succession function** — whether the relation teaches a practice that can later be reproduced for someone else.

These dimensions matter directly here. Saki's care for Ume is highly permissioned and deeply individualized but can misfire when exported to China. Sumika and Lilja carry internal models of each other's "I'm fine" behavior and fear responses. Temari/Misuzu care persists even when direct delivery is blocked, creating room for Hiro or Kotone to broker access. Rinha's coaching language can survive inside Temari's later teaching even when Rinha is absent.

> **The mature relationship system is therefore not a static tie. It is a learned repertoire of permissions, predictions, refusals, techniques, memories, and future obligations.**

# 2. Comparative overview — three relationships, three failures of totalization

| system | founding role | what the role gives | what becomes dangerous | mature correction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Saki ↔ Ume** | older sister ahead / younger sister chasing | discipline, aspiration, body culture, winning ethics, emotional certainty | Saki preserves distance; Ume borrows vocation from pursuit; burnout can become proof of love | **stable shared field**: either may lead; Ume self-authors idolhood; rivalry becomes renewable rather than terminal |
| **Lilja ↔ Sumika / REVERSI** | admired strong friend / believer; shared dream | courage, social translation, dream access, technical teaching, companionship | belief becomes obligation; weakness becomes concealment; shared dream threatens separate desire | **synchronized differentiation**: mutual reliance, rivalry inside partnership, separate dreams after shared fulfillment |
| **Temari ↔ Misuzu ↔ Rinha / SyngUp** | center / caretaker / leader-model | performance completion, bodily regulation, technical formation, belonging | care becomes containment; dependence becomes shame; asymmetry becomes unspeakable; blame concentrates | **post-unit plural relation**: unit can remain dissolved while care, rivalry, memory, ordinary access, and future collaboration survive |

The important contrast is not "healthy versus unhealthy relationship." All three systems contain real care from the beginning. The question is whether care can survive the loss of monopoly.

A mature relation in Gakumas repeatedly asks:

> **Can I remain important to you without being the only person, role, pace, dream, or future through which you are allowed to exist?**

The answer is not always fully secure, but all three systems move toward "yes."

# 3. The Hanami system — from chase and flight to a shared field

## 3.1 The sister relation is constitutive, not ornamental

Saki and Ume cannot be modeled as two independently formed characters who happen to be sisters and rivals. Each supplies part of the other's operating system.

From Saki's side, `お姉ちゃん` is not decorative family characterization. It means:

- remain admirable;
- remain useful;
- remain ahead enough to provide direction;
- feed, train, correct, and prepare;
- celebrate Ume's growth;
- and convert Ume's belief into an obligation to become worthy of it.

Saki's "world's best" ambition is explicitly connected to Ume's childhood statement that her older sister was the best in the world. Winning therefore has moral force inside the relationship: Saki wants Ume's belief to have been true.

From Ume's side, Saki is likewise more than motivation. Saki materially shapes:

- Ume's body and training habits;
- meal planning;
- winning etiquette;
- how loss should be acknowledged;
- how competition produces joy;
- how technical learning should be approached;
- and what an idol initially looks like.

Ume can say that her body is practically something Saki made. The support pass independently validates the technical side of this claim: a professional trainer sets a load for Ume roughly convergent with Saki's training notes. Saki's knowledge of Ume is not merely controlling confidence; it is often highly accurate person-specific expertise.

### Claim status

**PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN:** the Hanami bond is a form of mutual construction. "Ume imitates Saki" is too weak, and "Saki merely uses Ume as motivation" is equally inadequate.

## 3.2 The founding asymmetry: Saki stays ahead partly by changing the race

The most important V2 correction to the mythology of the unbeatable older sister is `勝ち逃げ`.

Saki admits that she repeatedly changed fields before Ume fully caught her. This does not erase Saki's real excellence. She is a rapid learner, disciplined all-rounder, strategist, and unusually capable self-manager. But it reveals how the relationship's hierarchy was protected.

The problem was not simply:

> Saki is stronger; Ume wants to catch her.

It was also:

> **Saki needs to remain the back worth chasing, and one way to preserve that identity is to leave before arrival can occur.**

Ume's slow-start learning profile magnifies the wound. In older competitions, by the time Ume learned how to move, Saki could already be gone. Pursuit therefore contains an abandonment structure. Ume does not only want to win; she wants to become fast enough that Saki cannot disappear into another field before the contest becomes real.

This is why the mature Hanami problem is not "can Ume defeat Saki?" It is:

> **Can Ume arrive without destroying Saki's identity, and can Saki remain when arrival happens?**

## 3.3 The first stable contest changes the relationship's ontology

Saki's first genuinely stable head-to-head contest with Ume is frightening because the old escape route is gone. Her hands shake. She already recognizes that Ume can surpass her in measurable singing and dance dimensions.

Saki wins, but the significance is not proof that she is secretly more talented in every domain. It is that both sisters remain in the same field afterward.

The relationship becomes durable because competition no longer requires disappearance.

This is the first major move from **protected hierarchy** toward **shared field**.

## 3.4 Ume's victory is not independence from Saki; it is freedom from compulsory pursuit

Ume's mature development is often misdescribed as "becoming independent of Saki." V2 rejects that formulation.

After beating Saki, Ume can finally say that if Saki changed paths again, she would not automatically follow: `もう、追いかけない。`

The sentence is not emotional severance.

It means Ume now possesses:

- a reason to be an idol that is not reducible to Saki's path;
- friends and rivals whose lives matter independently;
- fan obligations;
- artistic and professional choices;
- and a future that remains meaningful even if the sister race temporarily changes shape.

Saki remains irreplaceable.

What disappears is **monopoly over vocation**.

### Transition

**REJECT / REFRAME:** "Ume must become independent of Saki."

Current formulation:

> **Ume differentiates without severing. The sister relation remains constitutive while ceasing to be the sole author of her future.**

## 3.5 Saki's defeat is the harder horizontalization test

Ume's arrival is not only Ume's developmental victory. It is Saki's identity crisis.

In the summer H.I.F. state where Ume becomes Prima Stella, Saki performs older-sister dignity publicly. She praises Ume, recognizes her as the best idol she has encountered, welcomes the birth of "idol Hanami Ume," and does not make her private devastation Ume's burden.

Privately, the loss breaks her far more deeply than the earlier "recover quickly" image would predict. She cries repeatedly, loses confidence, and needs reconstruction through the Producer, Project Stardust, rivals, songs, and accumulated relationships.

This is one of the most important bilateral findings:

> **Saki can celebrate Ume without pretending not to hurt, and Ume can win without needing Saki's pain to prove the victory mattered.**

The emotions are not mutually exclusive.

Love does not cancel grief.

Grief does not falsify love.

## 3.6 The mature sister relation: arrival becomes repeatable

The winter rematch and Ume-side Dear material support the same structural correction even when exact result states remain track-sensitive.

The sisters can now occupy a stable competitive field where:

- either may lead;
- either may lose;
- neither needs to leave the field merely to preserve identity;
- victory creates the next race rather than final ownership;
- and the relation can survive periods when other rivals, friends, producers, or professional obligations matter more locally.

Ume's mature rivalry formulation becomes **renewable propulsion** rather than finite combustion.

Saki's mature formulation becomes **indomitability** rather than invincibility.

These are complementary changes.

The invincible older sister must not be caught.

The indomitable older sister may be caught, broken, helped, and still run again.

The pursuing younger sister must reach the back.

The self-authored younger sister can remain in the race even after the back is no longer the only destination.

## 3.7 Care grammar — recipient-specific, physically literate, and still vulnerable to overreach

Phase 5 greatly sharpens the Hanami care model.

### Saki's care for Ume

Saki's normal intervention sequence is:

> diagnose → optimize → prepare → prescribe → begin now.

With Ume, this is unusually accurate because years of shared body history give Saki privileged calibration. She knows food, recovery, training loads, fatigue patterns, and competition rhythms well enough that professional trainers can converge with her conclusions.

But the China support scene establishes the crucial boundary: **Ume is not a universal human baseline**.

Saki initially imports Ume's tolerance into China's training and has to be explicitly told that China is not Ume.

The final support tranche then gives Saki a more mature formulation through cooking: good care is **love + chemistry**. Correct technique is necessary but insufficient; the giver must model the recipient's actual body and preferences.

### Ume's care for Saki

Ume's care is more immediate and physical. It appears as:

- proximity;
- challenge;
- morale;
- spontaneous help;
- insistence that Saki rest emotionally as well as physically;
- and the willingness to become strong enough to threaten the person she loves.

The tackle-hug support scene is especially useful. Saki does not reject Ume's affection; she redirects the delivery into a safer channel she can receive. Ume, for her part, accepts the boundary without needing the affection itself to be repudiated.

### Relationship permission

This pair has unusually broad access permission:

- bodily correction;
- food governance;
- intense teasing;
- competitive provocation;
- explicit affection;
- physical contact;
- direct criticism;
- and emotionally loaded comparison.

But mature modeling must not confuse **broad permission with unlimited permission**. Ume can self-author vocation. Saki cannot simply decide what Ume's future should mean because she once designed her training.

## 3.8 Jealousy and social expansion — attachment without enclosure

Events and support stories repeatedly test whether the sister bond can tolerate external friendship.

Saki experiences real jealousy as Ume becomes close to Lilja, student-council peers, and others. Yet the dominant behavior is not enclosure. She often supports the new relation, obtains information about Ume's independent life, or converts loneliness into rivalry, teasing, or renewed contact.

Ume likewise acquires friends and models who do not route through Saki. Rinha becomes a non-family rival/teacher. China and Hiro become ordinary friends and later technical/philosophical equals. Misuzu and Sena provide alternative idol logics.

The correct mature rule is:

> **Saki remains Ume's constitutive sister and rival without being the administrator of Ume's entire social world.**

## 3.9 Burnout remains the principal unresolved danger

The Hanami system becomes more sustainable, but it does not become noncompetitive.

Both sisters are attracted to escalation. Saki can turn care into optimization. Ume can treat challenge as the default way to make life vivid. The route explicitly confronts romanticized burnout, but does not remove the possibility that renewable rivalry becomes compulsive escalation.

The ethical question for later monographs is not whether competition is secretly bad.

It is:

> **Can either sister recognize value, rest, intimacy, or self-worth during periods when nobody is winning, chasing, or being chased?**

That remains OPEN.

## 3.10 Hanami synthesis claim transitions

| earlier / simplified claim | transition | current V2 relationship formulation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saki is naturally superior and Ume chases | **REVISE** | Saki is genuinely excellent but historically protected hierarchy partly through `勝ち逃げ`; Ume's ceiling and slow-start pattern make pursuit structurally complex |
| Ume must escape Saki to mature | **REJECT** | maturation is differentiation without severance |
| Saki's care is controlling | **REVISE / STRENGTHEN WITH BOUNDARY** | Saki has unusually accurate Ume-specific body knowledge; overreach appears when that model is exported or when care claims authority over vocation |

## 3.11 Hanami reconstruction rules

For novel-situation modeling:

1. Do not ask only "does Saki love Ume?" Ask **what does Saki believe Ume's current condition requires, and is that belief actually calibrated to present Ume?**
2. Do not make Ume passive before Saki. Ume can refuse, choose another path, seek other rivals, and still love Saki intensely.
3. Preserve simultaneous affect. A scene may contain pride, jealousy, anger, affection, and competition at once.
4. Saki usually converts concern into something actionable: food, schedule, technique, correction, challenge, or preparation.
5. Ume usually converts attachment into presence, physical action, invitation, morale, or direct challenge.
6. Their rivalry becomes unhealthy when **distance itself** must be preserved rather than the contest enjoyed.
7. Their mature bond allows other people to matter without downgrading the sister relation.

# 4. REVERSI — from borrowed courage and protector pressure to synchronized differentiation

## 4.1 The relationship begins by making a world imaginable

Lilja and Sumika's relation is causal before it is professional.

From Lilja's side, Sumika is the person who brings her out of an inwardly closed world. Sumika is:

- best friend;
- admired person;
- social translator;
- guide to idol culture;
- source of courage;
- and the person who helps make a shared idol future imaginable.

Lilja explicitly describes Sumika as someone who brings her outside the world she would otherwise close around herself.

The later support pass makes this origin even stronger. Sumika directly invites Lilja toward idolhood, the two study toward Hatsuboshi together, and their shared future remains imaginable even under the hypothetical that they might not attend the same school.

This is important because V2 refuses an autonomy model in which a dream is invalid if someone else first made it thinkable.

> **Relationally received desire can still become genuinely self-authored.**

## 4.2 Sumika's side reveals the cost of being the admired one

Lilja's Phase-3 corpus begins with gratitude. Sumika's Phase-3 corpus reveals why the same relation can hurt.

Sumika's childhood ballet talent was real. Injury destroys the future around which she had organized herself. She then uses unseriousness, social brightness, and strategic distance to avoid the demands attached to the old prodigy identity.

Lilja's belief is loving.

But Sumika experiences belief through the wound of expectation.

The central danger is therefore not:

> Lilja expects too much because she is cruel.

It is:

> **Sumika turns Lilja's faith into an obligation to remain the strong person Lilja believes in.**

This distinction is critical for simulation. Sumika can become defensive even when Lilja has not actually imposed the judgment Sumika fears.

## 4.3 The concealment crisis — protection becomes disconnection

Sumika's old care strategy is to protect Lilja by hiding weakness.

She can reason:

- Lilja needs the strong Sumika;
- showing fear will hurt Lilja;
- therefore a good friend should perform "I'm fine."

The strategy fails.

Lilja's route identifies Sumika's core relational fault not as weakness but concealment: `清夏ちゃんは、嘘つきなんです！` and `わたしを頼ってくれないんです……！`

The mature correction is not that Lilja stops believing in Sumika.

It is:

> **Sumika stops treating reliance as betrayal of the role she occupies in Lilja's imagination, and Lilja stops accepting secrecy as a condition of closeness.**

Lilja can say directly that Sumika is not alone and should rely on her.

This is one of the clearest examples in the corpus of intimacy deepening through increased burden-sharing rather than through ever more competent unilateral care.

## 4.4 Care reverses direction without erasing origin

The relationship becomes truly bilateral when Lilja can recognize Sumika's false "I'm fine" state and act.

Phase 5 strengthens this through an injury-related distress scene. Lilja reproduces a strategy Sumika once used for her: rather than force immediate confession, she removes Sumika from the ordinary routine and creates room for the state to change.

This is not role replacement.

Sumika does not become the weak one and Lilja the permanent protector.

It is **care-direction reversibility**.

The founding history remains true: Sumika once opened the world for Lilja.

Maturity adds a second truth: Lilja has become capable of carrying Sumika when Sumika cannot carry herself.

## 4.5 Ordinary pleasure is not filler; it is the substrate of high-stakes trust

Support cards make the relationship much harder to over-theorize.

They share:

- lotteries and the micro-myth that Lilja is good at chance;
- ice cream;
- karaoke;
- anime and fandom;
- photography;
- shopping;
- dorm domesticity;
- food;
- study;
- games;
- late-return monitoring;
- moving-in history;
- teasing;
- and small reciprocal expertise.

This matters because REVERSI does not emerge from pure trauma processing or symbolic complementarity.

It emerges from a relationship that is enjoyable.

The low-stakes scenes establish a simple but important reconstruction prior:

> **Lilja and Sumika often want to spend time together even when no developmental crisis is being solved.**

That fact should not be lost beneath the H.I.F./REVERSI argument.

## 4.6 Jealousy becomes non-enclosing

Events repeatedly show Sumika noticing Lilja's social expansion.

Sumika can feel jealous when Lilja becomes closer to Saki, Kotone, Ume, or others. She can joke possessively, including `うちのリーリヤ` or jokes about not "giving" Lilja away.

But the operative behavior is usually non-enclosure.

She:

- waits rather than interrupting Lilja's new friendship;
- supports Lilja accepting difficult social work;
- celebrates new connections afterward;
- and allows Lilja's world to become larger than the friendship.

This parallels the Hanami system in an important way. Attachment becomes more secure not because jealousy disappears, but because jealousy loses the authority to block expansion.

## 4.7 REVERSI is genuine intimacy and production technology at the same time

REVERSI is not only "best friends finally sing together."

It is a deliberately produced competitive object.

The Producer recognizes that their history can make performance more legible and emotionally powerful. Childhood photos, shared history, rehearsal/public narrative, and the story behind the unit are selected and framed so audiences and judges can understand what the performance means.

The key ethical fact is dual:

- the relationship history is real;
- the public presentation of that history is constructed.

> **Authentic intimacy becomes competitive/public capital without becoming fabricated.**

Both members materially participate in this process. Consent is present. Yet the system should not treat consent as making publicity analytically neutral.

Later thematic synthesis should continue to ask:

- who decides which part of the relationship becomes public;
- whether either member can later withdraw access;
- whether fans begin to demand the relationship performance itself;
- and whether the unit's commercial legibility can pressure the pair toward an identity they have already outgrown privately.

## 4.8 Shared dream must permit selfish desire

The decisive REVERSI maturation occurs when the shared dream collides with a singular title.

Lilja initially experiences personal desire for Prima Stella as possible betrayal. Sumika gives her permission to say that she wants what she wants: `欲しいものは欲しいって、そう言っていいんだよ`.

Lilja can then hold:

- I want REVERSI to win;
- I want to beat Sumika for the individual title.

This is not a contradiction to be solved.

It is the relationship's mature form.

`仲間でライバル` means the bond can contain conflicting desire without requiring emotional withdrawal.

### Transition

**REVISE:** REVERSI as "two inseparable halves."

Current formulation:

> **REVERSI is strongest when the two people remain legible as separate centers of desire whose coordination creates an additional shared object.**

## 4.9 The unit's mature endpoint is not fusion but separable futures

Dear 037 provides the strongest anti-fusion evidence.

After fulfillment of the shared dream, Lilja experiences grief because the old telos has ended. Sumika answers that even if they can no longer dream exactly the same dream, they can continue helping one another: `もう同じ夢は見れなくても、助け合うことはできるでしょ？`

This is the correct mature REVERSI proposition:

> **The bond survives the loss of a shared telos.**

The relationship is therefore not proven by permanent dream identity.

It is proven by the capacity to remain emotionally and practically available while futures diverge.

## 4.10 Attraction / romance coding — preserve, do not force

The corpus contains attraction-coded and possessive material:

- `好きな人` teasing around Lilja's natural smile;
- Sumika's `うちのリーリヤ`;
- jealousy;
- domestic proximity;
- intense trait knowledge;
- emotionally privileged access;
- and repeated two-person future imagery.

This is meaningful texture.

It is not, by itself, an instruction to collapse the relationship into a single romance category.

For current authority:

- **intimacy: very high**;
- **best-friend identification: direct**;
- **mutual emotional centrality: very high**;
- **attraction/romantic coding: present**;

# 5. SyngUp — when the unit dies but the relationship does not

## 5.1 SyngUp is not adequately described as a "failed unit"

SyngUp is a formative childhood/middle-school structure for Temari, Misuzu, and Kaya Rinha.

It contains:

- shared idol revelation;
- training;
- bodily regulation;
- technical modeling;
- ordinary food and leisure memories;
- public performance;
- hierarchy;
- jealousy;
- protection;
- competition;
- and eventually rupture.

Calling it a "failed unit" is factually insufficient because the unit succeeds at producing skills, identities, memories, aspirations, and relationship grammars that continue long after dissolution.

The correct question is:

> **Which SyngUp functions remain valuable after the permanent-unit form becomes unsafe or undesirable?**

## 5.2 The founding triangle is relationally asymmetrical

Temari's core remembers SyngUp through inferiority.

She occupies a visible center that she believes she can sustain partly because:

- Rinha improvises, models, teaches, and coordinates;
- Misuzu watches, regulates, feeds, rests, prepares, and compensates;
- the unit can complete performances Temari's current body could not sustain alone.

This creates an unbearable interpretation for Temari:

> the others' care is proof that she is the defective component.

Misuzu's side shows a different history. Temari is the child who breaks Misuzu's cold self-sufficiency and teaches her that an uncontrolled other person can make life more interesting. Misuzu's idol origin itself is triangular: she sees Temari captivated by an idol and wants that gaze.

Rinha, through currently distributed evidence, is likewise not merely "the leader." She becomes:

- model;
- teacher;
- technical reference;
- mediator of old aspirations;
- someone who later absorbs public hostility;
- rival;
- and a person whose own desire becomes harder to reconstruct once the unit's collapse is narrated through the other two.

These three positions are not interchangeable.

## 5.3 Misuzu's care is accurate enough to become dangerous

One of the strongest SyngUp findings is that Temari's grievance does **not** require Misuzu's care to have been fake.

Misuzu often knows what Temari needs.

She can anticipate:

- food;
- calories;
- skin condition;
- sleep;
- towels;
- drinks;
- pace;
- emotional state;
- and the exact practical condition under which Temari can enjoy something without spiraling into guilt.

That precision is part of the problem.

Temari experiences care as infantilizing when Misuzu can know and act on a need before Temari has authored the act of needing.

> **The care is accurate, loving, and capable of becoming containment at the same time.**

V2 therefore rejects both easy extremes:

- Misuzu was a manipulative captor whose care was never care;
- Misuzu was simply correct and Temari irrationally rejected good help.

The ethical failure lies in **jurisdiction**.

Misuzu's model of Temari sometimes becomes a claim about what Temari is allowed to choose.

## 5.4 Temari's independence is partly a dependency-transfer problem

Temari wants to stop being dependent on SyngUp.

Yet her solo development is not self-sufficiency. The Producer assumes a huge regulatory role in training, food, stamina, schedule, publicity, and competitive planning.

Temari experiences this differently because the Producer's intervention feels oriented toward enabling her chosen combustion rather than proving that combustion itself is illegitimate.

The contrast is psychologically real.

It is not ethically self-validating.

For relationship synthesis, the important conclusion is:

> **Temari's problem was never dependence in the abstract. It was dependence interpreted as permanent incapacity and loss of authorship.**

That is why she can later accept help from Misuzu, Rinha, Hiro, China, Kotone, or the Producer when the help is framed as compatible with continued agency.

## 5.5 Rinha's importance survives imitation

Temari's relation to Rinha is unusually layered.

Rinha is one of the people from whom Temari builds her public idol form. Temari imitates aspects of Rinha's song, dance, coolness, and persona. Mature authorship therefore requires not repudiating imitation but overtaking it.

Event 016 adds a crucial transmission point: Rinha's historical coaching language survives inside Temari's own later pedagogy.

Phase 5 adds attachment/safety evidence. During thunder panic Temari calls for Rinha. Food memories reveal Rinha's teasing may have carried body-regulation care. Ramen memories restore the three-person ordinary-life triad rather than only the performance system.

This is why Rinha cannot be reduced to:

- traitor;
- absent leader;
- technical benchmark;
- or blame absorber.

The dedicated Rinha dossier must still decide how these roles fit from her side.

## 5.6 The breakup is produced by unspeakable asymmetry, not insufficient affection

The unit's central failure is not that the members did not care.

They cared intensely.

The problem is that their interpretations of care, work, pace, and legitimacy became difficult to state in forms the others could answer.

Temari reads support as evidence of inferiority.

Misuzu reads intervention as obvious care and is slow to make anger explicit.

Rinha's exact interiority remains distributed, but current evidence shows responsibility, coaching, withdrawal, later blame, and a continuing relation to the old members.

When disagreement is handled through quiet management, overfunctioning, self-punishment, or disappearance, the unit can continue functioning while the relationship becomes less speakable.

V2's mature SyngUp insight is therefore:

> **Conflict can be more ethical than containment when it restores the other person's right to answer.**

Misuzu's route treats direct anger as acquired relational competence. She later tells Rinha they should properly quarrel. Temari likewise becomes capable of demanding rivalry rather than passive support.

## 5.7 Post-rupture residue — care persists after permission is revoked

Support Series 1 provides one of the best small-scale SyngUp scenes.

After the rupture, Misuzu no longer directly hands Temari the drink and towel she prepared.

She still prepares them.

This distinction should govern the entire repair model:

> **separation can revoke delivery permission without erasing the care habit.**

The pair's grievance likewise contains active defense. Temari complains about Misuzu, but resists an outsider flattening Misuzu into a bad person. Misuzu can describe the training incompatibility without retroactively denying affection.

The bond survives as witnesshood before it survives as direct access.

## 5.8 Estrangement-era co-regulation — the relation keeps operating indirectly

Later support evidence shows Temari and Misuzu continuing to monitor one another while direct access is damaged.

They can:

- notice new friends;
- infer what the other would say;
- route correction through other people;
- continue caring about effort, rest, training, and emotional state;
- and respond to fear with temporary truce.

This is not reconciliation yet.

It is evidence that the relationship remains cognitively active.

Each woman still carries an internal model of the other.

That makes later repair possible because the bond has not become informationally empty.

## 5.9 Reconciliation is a new operating agreement, not SyngUp restoration

This is the most important Phase-5 correction.

Temari and Misuzu explicitly repair their friendship while rejecting a return to the old structure.

Misuzu does not need to become Temari's wings again.

Temari does not need care to disappear.

They can instead negotiate:

- rivalry;
- separate pace;
- food care;
- outings;
- evaluation;
- shared lessons;
- new friends;
- and chosen moments of dependence.

The line of development is not:

> breakup → misunderstanding cleared → old good relationship restored.

It is:

> **old relationship → unsustainable role structure → rupture → continuing witnesshood → bounded indirect care → explicit conflict → reconciliation → new access under revised terms.**

This is a stronger model of repair because it does not require the past to have been secretly fine.

## 5.10 New memories matter more than reenactment

The final support tranche provides the mature-state indicator.

Temari and Misuzu take private trips, share food, negotiate future outings, and deliberately create experiences that did not exist before the rupture.

A prior Rinha memory can be activated by food without forcing the present to become a reenactment of the old triangle.

Temari and Misuzu can value the past and still want a memory that belongs to the repaired relation.

> **Repair becomes durable when the pair can generate new relational history rather than living only through postmortem analysis of SyngUp.**

# 6. Cross-system synthesis — relationship maturity as de-totalization

## 6.1 The founding role is usually real

A recurring analytical mistake is to assume that development reveals the old role as fake.

That is not what happens in any of these systems.

Saki really is an older sister whose training, care, and example materially build Ume.

Sumika really does open the world for Lilja and supply courage.

Lilja really does believe deeply in Sumika.

Misuzu really does know Temari's body and needs unusually well.

Rinha really does shape Temari technically and aspirationally.

The route does not "debunk" those truths.

It limits their jurisdiction.

## 6.2 The dangerous step is monopoly

Each system becomes unstable when one relation acquires monopoly over meaning.

### Hanami monopoly

- Saki must stay ahead.
- Ume must chase.
- winning proves older-sister legitimacy.

### REVERSI monopoly

- Sumika must stay strong for Lilja.
- Lilja must share the same dream.
- the unit must express inseparability.

### SyngUp monopoly

- Misuzu must regulate Temari.
- Temari's supported success proves inferiority.
- Rinha must lead/absorb the ending.
- the unit must remain the privileged container for the relationship.

Maturation repeatedly involves adding alternative routes.

## 6.3 Relationship health is not low intensity

None of the three systems becomes calm in the generic sense.

The Hanami sisters remain intensely competitive.

Lilja and Sumika can remain jealous, emotionally central, ambitious, and publicly paired.

Temari/Misuzu/Rinha can remain possessive, angry, rivalrous, and historically wounded.

The better distinction is:

> **Can intensity coexist with refusal, differentiation, and alternate support channels?**

That is a more useful GKM relationship criterion than "do they stop fighting?"

## 6.4 The mature relation permits arrival

All three systems contain an arrival problem.

Ume arrives at Saki.

Lilja arrives beside Sumika as someone who can support, challenge, and rival her.

Temari/Misuzu/Rinha arrive at a point where the old unit can be named, remembered, inherited, and ended without forcing reunion.

The founding relation often needs asymmetry to begin.

The mature relation has to survive its reduction.

## 6.5 The mature relation permits other people

The support/event pass repeatedly tests whether external ties are experienced as betrayal.

Successful development looks like permeability:

- Ume gains China, Hiro, Rinha, Misuzu, Sena, Lilja, and broader professional networks.
- Lilja gains Saki, Kotone, Ume, China, Rinami, Tsubame, and independent confidence.
- Sumika can observe those expansions without enclosing Lilja.
- Temari develops Kotone, Hiro, China, Saki, Producer, and other ties.
- Misuzu develops Ume, Hiro, China, Sena/Tsubame council relations, and others.
- Rinha's distributed evidence increasingly shows connections outside SyngUp.

A useful Phase-6 principle follows:

> **Relationship depth should not be measured by how completely the relationship excludes alternative ties. Mature depth is often demonstrated by surviving them.**

## 6.6 Care becomes more ethical when it becomes recipient-relative

Across all three systems, mature care moves away from universalized solutions.

Saki learns that Ume's baseline cannot simply be applied to China.

Sumika learns that helping Lilja does not always mean pushing; sometimes it means preserving the exit and waiting for Lilja to choose.

Lilja learns that supporting Sumika may mean reading the false "I'm fine" rather than demanding immediate explanation.

Misuzu learns that her own sustainable pace is not automatically Temari's correct pace.

Temari learns that old SyngUp knowledge can be given to other performers without reproducing the old unit.

The common rule is:

> **Care is not validated only by benevolent intention or technical accuracy. It is validated by whether the recipient can remain an author inside it.**

## 6.7 Brokerage is not relational failure

A mature network does not require the closest person to handle every need.

Hiro helping Temari repair Misuzu is not evidence that Temari/Misuzu are less close.

Kotone carrying Misuzu's care ethically is not evidence that the dyad failed to reconcile.

Saki receiving information from Rinami about Ume can reduce the pressure to monitor Ume directly.

REVERSI members can receive support from other peers without threatening the unit.

This gives Phase 6 a strong network-level correction:

> **Intermediaries can protect authorship by preventing a historically overloaded relationship from having to perform every function itself.**

## 6.8 Internalized others are the deepest evidence of relationship effect

The final Phase-5 tranche literalizes a general phenomenon through Hiro/China, but it applies conceptually here as well.

A relationship can become part of action selection.

Saki can ask what Ume's body can actually sustain because years of Ume-specific knowledge live inside her routines.

Ume carries Saki's winning ethics and training grammar even while choosing against automatic pursuit.

Lilja can recognize Sumika's concealment without waiting for a confession.

Sumika can model how Lilja will experience expectation and social exposure.

Temari carries Rinha's coaching language.

Temari and Misuzu can infer one another's likely reactions even while estranged.

This is more important than whether they are physically together in a given scene.

> **The relationship has become durable when the other person remains cognitively present without needing to be physically or institutionally dominant.**

## 6.9 Succession is relationship reproduced at institutional scale

The completed support pass also reveals a bridge from private relation to institution.

Saki's older-sister model becomes public leadership.

REVERSI turns received courage into performance that audiences can receive.

Temari transfers SyngUp experience into unit advice.

Misuzu/Rinha techniques persist after unit dissolution.

The school itself repeatedly reproduces care through people who remember how they were treated and later pass a useful function forward.

Relationship synthesis is therefore not separate from institutional synthesis.

It explains one mechanism by which Hatsuboshi reproduces culture.

# 7. A Phase-6 relationship-system schema

The evidence from these three systems suggests that later relationship dossiers should not use a single closeness score.

A more useful schema is:

| dimension | question |
| --- | --- |
| **founding role** | What original asymmetry or shared purpose made the relation legible? |
| **care language** | What does each person actually do when the other matters? |
| **accepted access** | What interventions are normally permitted? |
| **blocked access** | What actions are currently refused or unsafe? |
| **recipient model** | What does each person know about the other's body, fears, preferences, habits, and goals? |
| **dependence interpretation** | Does help feel like support, debt, inferiority, control, partnership, or something else? |
| **competition grammar** | Does rivalry threaten attachment, sustain it, or become a communication channel? |
| **jealousy / exclusivity** | What triggers possessive affect, and does it produce enclosure? |
| **brokerage** | Who can carry care/information across a blocked edge? |
| **internalized model** | Whose likely reaction has become part of decision-making while absent? |
| **public role** | How does the relation become institution, brand, unit, family mythology, or audience narrative? |
| **continuity mode** | What must remain, what may change, and what must be allowed to die? |
| **succession function** | What practice learned here gets reproduced for later people? |
| **principal failure mode** | What useful relation becomes totalizing when overextended? |
