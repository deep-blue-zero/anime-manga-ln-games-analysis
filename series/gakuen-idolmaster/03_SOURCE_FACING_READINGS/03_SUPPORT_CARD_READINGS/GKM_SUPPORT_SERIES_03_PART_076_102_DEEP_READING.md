# GKM SUPPORT SERIES 03 PART 076–102 DEEP READING

## 0. Scope, method, and principal finding

This is the eighth and final architecture-defined Phase-5 support-card reading for the Gakuen Idolmaster V2 corpus. It closes Support Series 3 and, under **GAKUMAS V2 Source Lock 1.0**, closes the complete support-card pass.

The routing bundle is named `support_series_3_part_076-102.dialogue.txt`, but its literal story interval is non-contiguous. The bytes contain **90 unique scripts / 1,797 messages**, organized as **30 three-part support stories** with the following IDs:

`story_0076`–`story_0098`, `story_0100`–`story_0105`, and `story_0108`.

`story_0099`, `story_0106`, and `story_0107` are not present in this analysis bundle. Conversely, `story_0103`–`story_0105` and `story_0108` extend beyond the filename's apparent upper boundary. This is a routing-label mismatch, not an uncovered Source-Lock gap: the tranche contributes exactly the **90 scripts / 1,797 messages** required to reconcile cumulative Phase-5 coverage from **408/498 scripts / 7,980/9,777 messages** to **498/498 scripts / 9,777/9,777 messages**.

As throughout Phase 5, story IDs are not promoted into one global chronology. Three parts under one support-story ID are read as a related evidence envelope. Stronger placement is used only where school year, season, graduation state, relationship state, remembered history, institutional role, H.I.F. framing, or another internal anchor supports it. Retrospective material can establish earlier relational grammar without forcing every modular support story into one single branch calendar.

A2 dialogue extraction is the reading surface; A1 raw-script paths remain exact wording authority. Text-only reading does not infer uninspected card art, facial animation, camera direction, costume rendering, or vocal nuance. Performance-sensitive distinctions are routed to the AV verification queue.

### Principal tranche thesis — relationships become part of action selection

Earlier Phase-5 tranches progressively established default care languages, behavioral intimacy, procedural care, interpretive literacy, social infrastructure, continuity through change, and brokerage across blocked relationship boundaries. The final tranche supplies the missing internal layer:

> **A mature relationship does not only change what another person does for a character. It changes what the character can think, notice, choose, and attempt even when the other person is absent.**

`story_0088` literalizes this proposition. Hiro says that, after spending enough time with China, a “China” has appeared inside her mind: when Hiro is uncertain, the internal China asks what she would do and urges Hiro toward becoming a proper idol. China answers that she has an internal Hiro as well: during painful training, imagining that Hiro would probably be smiling at the difficulty helps China regain a forward-looking attitude. They then decide that an internal Ume would also be useful.

This is unusually direct evidence for the project's reconstruction goal. The relationship ledger should therefore model not only **access** (“who can this person ask?”) and **brokerage** (“who can translate care across a blocked edge?”), but also **internalized relational priors** (“whose likely response has become part of this person's own decision process?”).

The same mechanism appears less literally elsewhere:

- Saki's cooking incorporates feedback from other people and becomes an explicit theory of “love + chemistry”: correct technique plus a model of the recipient's preferences and body.
- Tsubame studies Ume's athletic movement because Ume exceeds her in one physical domain; authority does not prevent internalizing a junior as technical evidence.
- Saki and Tsubame combine modern/scientific and traditional training methods rather than treating either person's system as complete.
- Misuzu is explicitly asked to support China and Ume after the third-years leave; senior practice becomes a successor's future behavioral responsibility.
- Mao explains that she became dorm leader to return to juniors the care that earlier dorm seniors once gave her.
- Sena and Misuzu imagine carrying an old candy-shop experience into future school/community events, even after Sena graduates.
- the three trainers recognize that rising student ability requires *their* pedagogy to change; the institution is also capable of internalizing feedback from the people it trains.

The modeling consequence is:

> **When reconstructing a character in a novel situation, ask not only “what would this character normally do?” but “which people have become part of the character's learned repertoire for deciding what to do?”**

A Hiro who has become close to China can plausibly hear a China-like objection in her own reasoning. A China facing painful effort can plausibly stabilize herself by imagining Hiro's delight in difficulty. A Saki designing care for someone new will not merely repeat Ume's regimen; she has learned to model recipient differences. A Tsubame encountering a movement problem may use evidence from a junior without surrendering senior authority.

### Secondary thesis — succession is internalized relationship at institutional scale

The personal and institutional layers converge in this tranche.

Mao's dorm leadership is not an abstract office: she says earlier dorm seniors watched over a stubborn, difficult junior Mao, and she volunteered to become dorm leader because she wanted to return that received care to the next cohort. Tsubame tells Misuzu that, after the senior class is gone, Misuzu should support China and Ume. Sena is invited to continue helping school events as a graduate. The sports-festival closing live is explicitly framed as the third-years' encouragement to the students who will carry Hatsuboshi next. The trainers decide that the students' rising level obligates the adults to revise the training system.

> **Institutional memory in Gakumas is frequently a person remembering how someone treated them, then reproducing the useful function for a later recipient.**

That makes Phase 5 an essential precursor to Phase 6 rather than decorative slice-of-life. Relationships are one of the mechanisms by which the school reproduces itself.

# 2. `story_0076` — Sena, Temari, and Misuzu: reconciliation creates new access, not restored simplicity

The first part establishes a clean post-reconciliation Temari/Misuzu state. Temari explicitly wants to spend time with Misuzu *because* they have reconciled, but the bond is still awkward enough that she hesitates to ask for lunch. Misuzu immediately accepts and has prepared enough sandwiches to share. Sena, who had been waiting for Misuzu for student-council work, silently urges Misuzu to go and then says openly that Misuzu had wanted reconciliation for a long time.

That combination matters. Repair is neither instantaneous restoration nor merely declarative. Temari's new access has to be exercised again; Misuzu's long-standing desire to reconcile can coexist with the earlier control problems and conflict documented elsewhere.

Part 2 creates a new Sena/Temari axis. Temari preemptively rejects being produced by Sena, but agrees to let the current `一番星` evaluate her singing. Sena recognizes Temari's middle-school `歌姫` reputation, then asks to sing together. Temari's outward challenge is competitive; her inner reaction is delighted and honored. Sena says she is glad that Temari was the person with whom she sang after a long interval and proposes a future live together.

Part 3 shows Misuzu entering this new relation without pretending indifference. She redirects Sena's desire to evaluate Temari into a dance-game contest and then adds a wager: if Misuzu wins against Temari, Temari joins the student council. The scene is comic, but it keeps possession, rivalry, and institutional recruitment in the same social field.

### Claim transitions

- **Temari ↔ Misuzu — STRENGTHEN repaired-state access:** reconciliation permits lunch, invitation, shared food, and playful rivalry while residual awkwardness remains.
- **Misuzu — STRENGTHEN:** Sena confirms that reconciliation was something Misuzu had wanted for a long time.
- **Sena ↔ Temari — PROMOTE:** Sena evaluates Temari as a serious first-year talent; Temari accepts professional evaluation while refusing producer capture; duet pleasure and future-live interest are reciprocal.
- **Temari — STRENGTHEN:** admiration for elite seniors can produce intense private delight without canceling her public competitive register.

### Phase-6 rule

Do not reconstruct post-reconciliation Temari/Misuzu as if the rupture never occurred. New warmth is evidence of successful repair; hesitation and rivalry are evidence of the new terms under which that repair operates.

# 3. `story_0077` — Saki, China, and Mashiro Yu: recipient modeling becomes explicit product design

Saki's recovery system continues to expand beyond Ume. She performs post-training cool-down and stretching for China, explains the physiological purpose, and supplies SSD. China reports that her condition has improved since drinking it. This is now ordinary enough that China knows the expected sequence and treats Saki's care as part of routine peer life.

The crucial refinement is Mashiro Yu. Saki has not merely offered Yu the same SSD that she gives physically intensive idol-course peers. She redesigns it for someone with lower exercise volume: an herbal-tea base, flavor balance, and intended effects chosen for Yu's likely use case. Yu loves the result; China immediately names the new variant `SST`.

Part 3 prevents Saki's helper role from becoming unilateral hierarchy. China thanks Saki for morning training, but Saki says she trains multiple peers because they asked for help and because she enjoys making food and drinks that recipients appreciate. Then Saki calls China the “boss of Class 1-2” and says *she* has much to learn from China. China, in turn, refuses a taste-softened SSD for herself and explicitly asks for the most effective version.

### Claim transitions

- **Saki — STRENGTHEN:** care is recipient-specific engineering, not ritual repetition. She adjusts an intervention to exercise level and recipient profile.
- **Saki ↔ China — STRENGTHEN reciprocal learning:** Saki is trainer-like in physical domains while explicitly recognizing China's social/leadership qualities as something she can learn from.
- **Mashiro Yu — STRENGTHEN ordinary-course integration:** Yu is becoming a recipient of peer-designed idol-course care without being absorbed into idol training.
- **China — STRENGTHEN:** she accepts intensive support without treating dependence as status loss and selects efficacy over comfort when the tradeoff is explicit.

### Modeling rule

Saki should not be simulated as applying “the Ume solution” universally. Her mature care behavior increasingly begins with *who is this for?*

# 4. `story_0078` — Ume and Mao: strength, study, private taste, and the sibling-shaped senior

Part 1 converts physical hierarchy into learned technique. Ume dominates an arm-wrestling chain until Mao uses an aikido-style method learned from Saki to redirect Ume's force. This does not suddenly make Mao stronger than Ume. It demonstrates that Ume's overwhelming power can be locally countered by technique and that Saki's knowledge has already traveled to another senior.

Part 2 finds Ume in the library because she has concluded that defeating Saki requires more than physical training; she needs to study idol expression and posing. Mao praises the decision, then reveals a private embarrassment of her own: she still loves a children's fantasy-novel series. Ume rejects the premise that such affection is uncool. Her own model is uncomplicated: saying openly that she loves Saki does not diminish her, so saying one loves a book should not diminish Mao.

Part 3 deepens Mao's sibling relation to the juniors. Ume is surprised that Mao is an only child because Mao behaves so much like an older sister. Mao then distinguishes Ume's situation from her own aspirational history. Mao has continued to “play the prince” partly because of an admired person she wanted to resemble; she envies Ume because Ume's admired person remains physically present, continuously available as loved one, rival, and goal.

### Claim transitions

- **Ume — STRENGTHEN:** rivalry motivates explicit intellectual study, not only more training.
- **Mao — STRENGTHEN:** private taste includes enduring attachment to child-oriented fantasy; shame about it is image-consciousness, not absence of genuine affection.
- **Mao — STRENGTHEN role continuity:** princehood remains linked to an admired predecessor without being reducible to imitation.
- **Mao ↔ Ume — PROMOTE/STRENGTHEN:** Ume reads Mao as sibling-like senior; Mao uses Ume's sibling relation as a mirror for her own aspiration history.

# 5. `story_0079` — Tsubame, Sena, and Misuzu: succession requires disagreement about how people grow

Misuzu's first scene is an efficient summary of her institutional problem: she can skip, sleep, and frustrate senior authority while still completing assigned student-council work before anyone can accuse her of incompetence. Tsubame is angered because ability does not erase obligation; Sena is more amused because she already understands the pattern.

Part 2 turns the trio into a pedagogy dispute. Misuzu attends a serious lesson and demonstrates exceptional first-year completion. Sena immediately pushes toward a higher-level issue—more dynamism—because she believes Misuzu's fundamentals are already beyond ordinary freshman needs. Tsubame objects that Sena is demanding a level inappropriate to a first-year and argues for patient foundations. Sena replies from a producer orientation: a superior producer should be able to obtain results quickly. Misuzu accepts Sena's note and then disappears for a nap before the adults can resolve the argument.

The story does not crown either pedagogy universally correct. It reveals a meaningful difference in developmental philosophy: **Sena accelerates from observed ceiling; Tsubame protects the base that makes durable growth possible.**

Part 3 adds a major succession marker. Misuzu returns after helping with juniors' training, triages urgent council paperwork without being asked, prepares material Tsubame needs, and volunteers because she anticipates the seniors' workload. Tsubame responds by telling Misuzu that, when the seniors are gone, she should support China and Ume. Misuzu accepts the care function gladly.

### Claim transitions

- **Misuzu — STRENGTHEN:** selective effort is not inability. When she authors participation, she can be exceptionally efficient, anticipatory, and institutionally useful.
- **Sena ↔ Tsubame — STRENGTHEN pedagogical contrast:** acceleration/results versus foundations/patience is a real philosophy difference inside their larger rivalry.
- **Tsubame ↔ Misuzu — PROMOTE:** Tsubame recognizes Misuzu as a future support node for the next council generation.
- **Next-council cluster — PROMOTE:** Misuzu's future function includes supporting China and Ume after third-year departure, not merely occupying a title.

### Phase-8 implication

The student council reproduces itself through disputed but complementary models of development. Sena selects and accelerates; Tsubame disciplines and transmits duty; Misuzu can become a low-tempo continuity layer between outgoing seniors and incoming officeholders.

# 6. `story_0080` — Sumika and Lilja: being known includes the impractical and obsessive parts

Sumika and Lilja's arcade day is a compact map of reciprocal domain expertise. Lilja is bad at the crane game and persists until the cost becomes absurd; Sumika is good at it and simply retrieves the desired plush for her. Sumika's competence comes from ordinary leisure repetition, not idol training. Lilja immediately translates the experience into “practice,” and Sumika has to remind her that some forms of practice are financially self-defeating.

Part 2 returns to Lilja's hidden fighting-game ability. Public players recognize her old handle, `氷の魔女`, and an experienced opponent identifies her as a high-level player. Sumika already knows this history and is unsurprised. This decisively rejects any global “Lilja is clumsy/inexperienced” model: her novice status is domain-bounded.

Part 3 reverses the performance hierarchy again. Sumika has loved karaoke since before aspiring to idolhood and repeatedly beats Lilja in scoring. Lilja specifically wants to demonstrate how much vocal training has improved her. Sumika then invites her to use an anime song Lilja loves, despite previously complaining about marathon viewing of the series. The key line is not that Sumika has become a fan of the show; it is that she likes seeing Lilja talk about what Lilja loves.

### Claim transitions

- **Lilja — PROMOTE hidden-domain expertise:** elite fighting-game competence is robust and socially recognized.
- **Sumika — STRENGTHEN:** karaoke enjoyment and singing confidence predate idol aspiration; ordinary leisure competence contributes to performance repertoire.
- **Sumika ↔ Lilja — STRENGTHEN:** intimacy includes tolerating and even facilitating interests one does not personally share because the partner's enthusiasm is itself pleasurable.
- **Romantic/affection coding — OPEN:** the line about liking Lilja when she talks about things she loves is intimate but does not independently establish a romantic declaration.

# 7. `story_0081` — Saki and Ume: sibling care is historical ritual, present infrastructure, and competitive fuel

The Christmas story gives unusually dense longitudinal sibling evidence. Ume sneaks into Saki's room to leave a present and remembers watching Saki sleep when they were children. Saki, still asleep, says she loves Ume; Ume answers without hesitation. This is not a new confession but the ordinary language of a relationship whose emotional primacy predates idolhood.

Part 2 shows Saki's Christmas care as an annual system. She gives training equipment, skincare, household-efficiency goods, and a hand-knitted scarf. The knitting itself has years of history: Saki began when they were small, remembers early work as technically poor, and Ume rejects the quality-based downgrade because the earlier gifts carried the same affection. Saki's care is therefore both optimization and ritual continuity.

Part 3 keeps the Hanami household linked to school life. The sisters prepare gifts for family and grandparents; dorm residents have messages thanking the grandparents for shared vegetables. Then they encounter a public broadcast of Sena's Christmas live. Both are fans enough to stop and watch, but Saki immediately also converts the performance into competitive learning and motivation toward `一番星`.

### Claim transitions

- **Saki ↔ Ume — STRENGTHEN longitudinal ritual:** Christmas gifting and handcraft are durable pre-Hatsuboshi practices.
- **Saki — STRENGTHEN:** material care combines training, appearance, household efficiency, handcraft, and emotion rather than belonging to one “optimization” category.
- **Hanami family/dorm network — STRENGTHEN:** family produce and dorm gratitude connect the sisters' home ecology to their school residence.
- **Saki ↔ Sena — STRENGTHEN fan/rival duality:** admiration is actively converted into competitive study rather than treated as incompatible with rivalry.

# 8. `story_0082` — Lilja and Tsubame: public image does not exhaust private fandom

Lilja and Tsubame unexpectedly discover one another in an anime-merchandise space. Both initially attempt face-saving explanations. Tsubame is disguised; Lilja is holding a large number of capsules from repeated gacha attempts. The embarrassment disappears as soon as they identify shared knowledge.

Part 2 clarifies that Tsubame's public profile is curated rather than false. Her listed interests—calligraphy, Japanese history, reading—are real. Anime is simply omitted because it conflicts with her image strategy. She can discuss favorite titles, adaptation style, character deaths, and genre expectations with specificity. Lilja's anime knowledge partly comes through her Japanese father, adding another route by which Japanese cultural material entered her pre-Hatsuboshi life.

The two exchange recommendations and explicitly call themselves `同士`. Part 3 then shows them deliberately meeting for a film release, analyzing adaptation choices together, and buying merchandise. When Mao and Rinami see them, both lie badly about having met intentionally or watched the film.

### Claim transitions

- **Tsubame — PROMOTE private fandom repertoire:** anime interest is genuine, technically informed, and deliberately excluded from public image strategy.
- **Lilja ↔ Tsubame — PROMOTE:** shared fandom creates cross-year intimacy that is neither idol training nor student-council obligation.
- **Role-permeability model — STRENGTHEN:** public-image curation is selective disclosure, not proof that the disclosed public traits are fake.
- **Lilja family context — STRENGTHEN:** her Japanese father is an explicit source for some anime/cultural exposure.

# 9. `story_0083` — Misuzu and Lilja: rest can be taught without making rest the whole person

Lilja initially interprets Misuzu through an anime-inflected “delinquent” stereotype because Misuzu sleeps through school assembly. Temari immediately distinguishes Misuzu's conduct from her own self-image and warns Lilja not to over-romanticize it. Misuzu nevertheless plays along enough to let Lilja follow her.

Part 2 is more important than the delinquent joke. Lilja actually skips class with Misuzu and is anxious about it. Misuzu reads that anxiety as evidence that Lilja may be too rigidly serious, recommends occasional breathing room, and creates a situation in which Lilja falls asleep. When Lilja wakes feeling physically lighter, she asks whether Misuzu was worried about her. Misuzu refuses to make the care declarative.

Part 3 shows that Misuzu's low-effort philosophy coexists with high technical dance competence. She diagnoses Lilja's stiffness, suggests whole-body softness and more stretching, and then ends the lesson according to her own pace. Lilja says she enjoys training with Misuzu; Misuzu extends the lesson slightly and remarks that this aspect of Lilja resembles “that girl,” without identifying the referent in this source.

### Claim transitions

- **Misuzu ↔ Lilja — PROMOTE:** Misuzu becomes both rest-permission figure and concrete dance mentor.
- **Misuzu — STRENGTHEN:** pacing is an authored principle; it can produce useful recovery advice rather than functioning only as avoidance.
- **Lilja — STRENGTHEN:** seriousness can become overcontrol, and she is capable of accepting a peer's permission to rest.
- **“that girl” referent — OPEN:** likely relationally meaningful, but this artifact does not name the person and does not force identification.

# 10. `story_0084` — Temari and the Class-1-2 trio: people can know your care language before you admit it

- **Ume–China–Hiro cluster — STRENGTHEN:** ordinary competence can improve collectively through practice and culminate in seeking trusted external evaluation.

# 11. `story_0085` — Tsubame and Saki: reciprocal rigor creates methodological synthesis

Tsubame teaches Saki and Ume seated Zen practice as a means of mental stability and concentration. Saki immediately notices a possible contradiction—if she uses Zen in order to improve lessons, is that instrumental motive itself a distracting thought? Tsubame laughs at the seriousness of the objection and explains that the initial motive matters less than actual practice. Saki has already incorporated ten minutes of daily Zen and considers using it before lives.

Part 2 moves to calligraphy. Saki chooses `不撓不屈`, a phrase that aligns cleanly with her established self-concept, and asks for precise technical correction. Ume accidentally writes `七転八倒` when she means `七転八起`; the scene is a bounded comic language error, not evidence for broad intellectual incapacity.

Part 3 reverses the teaching direction. Tsubame evaluates Saki's equipment-heavy, scientific training as rational and says she wants to learn from it because she tends to cling to old methods. Saki refuses the false binary: Zen helped her, so the right solution is to combine useful elements from each system. Their joint session becomes overpacked, but both report real growth and mutual motivational benefit.

# 32. Cross-story synthesis

## 32.1 Internalized others are a first-class behavioral variable

The strongest new result is the literal “person inside my heart” language of `story_0088`. It gives a source-grounded basis for something that had previously been inferred indirectly across the corpus: important relationships alter a character's own action-selection process.

This can be modeled at three levels:

1. **Direct access** — the person is present and can act. Misuzu can split food with Temari; Saki can stretch China; Hiro can coach Temari around the cat.
2. **Learned procedural transfer** — a technique survives its original teacher. Mao uses Saki's force-redirection lesson; Saki uses Tsubame's Zen; Tsubame studies Ume's athletics; Sena uses Tsubame's dashi instruction.
3. **Internalized counterfactual model** — the person is absent but the character can still ask what that person would do. Hiro explicitly carries China this way; China explicitly carries Hiro.

The third category matters enormously for reconstruction. It predicts why character behavior can change without trait replacement. A person does not need to become “more like” a friend globally. She may import one friend's decision rule into one kind of problem.

### Reconstruction rule

When generating a plausible novel situation, ask:

- Who would this character think of even if that person is absent?
- What specific rule or contrast has been learned from that person?
- Is the internalized model supportive, corrective, aspirational, or cautionary?
- Does the character imitate surface style, or translate the other's function into her own repertoire?

The source strongly favors **functional translation over cosmetic imitation**.

## 32.2 Care is best modeled as recipient-relative optimization

Saki's “love + chemistry” formulation consolidates the entire support-card pass. Technical competence alone does not define successful care. The intervention has to fit the recipient's preferences, constitution, current state, access permissions, and goals.

This is why:

- Saki redesigns SSD for Mashiro Yu instead of giving her China's version;
- Misuzu splits one chosen food with Temari rather than either forbidding the pleasure or encouraging unrestricted eating;
- Tsubame can be strict with an able student and still stop unsafe training elsewhere;
- Sumika encourages Lilja's fandom even when she personally finds marathon viewing exhausting;
- Mao translates China's imitation into safer practice in earlier tranches rather than demanding exact copying;
- Sena's developmental insight can still become consent-unsafe when she bypasses refusal, proving that accurate diagnosis is not sufficient.

Phase 5 therefore rejects both “care = softness” and “care = optimization.” The more accurate model is:

> **care = a chosen intervention built from a model of what this specific recipient can receive and use.**

## 32.3 Rank and expertise form a mesh, not a ladder

This tranche repeatedly sends knowledge upward, sideways, and outside formal status:

- Ume's physical ability becomes evidence for Tsubame;
- Sumika teaches Tsubame social-media photography;
- Hiro teaches Temari a puzzle system;
- Lilja's gaming skill far exceeds many peers despite being an idol technical beginner;
- Saki and Tsubame combine training systems;
- Yu becomes a communication specialist whose work elite idols need;
- the trainers themselves recognize that student growth requires teacher growth.

A mature GKM model should therefore separate at least:

- institutional rank;
- performance rank;
- domain expertise;
- embodied skill;
- social access;
- practical household competence;
- interpretive knowledge of a specific person.

Collapsing these into “more competent / less competent” destroys too much of the observed behavior.

## 32.4 Repair is demonstrated by future creation, not historical erasure

Temari/Misuzu evidence now spans active estrangement, indirect care, reluctant monitoring, explicit reconciliation, awkward re-entry, and private travel. The final support tranche provides the decisive mature-state indicator: they deliberately create experiences that did not exist before the rupture and explicitly value them as **new memories**.

Rinha's material reinforces the same principle at triadic scale. The old three-person unit can share a dissolution live and ordinary memories without requiring the old unit arrangement to become desirable again.

> **Repair is strongest when the characters can build future-tense behavior without falsifying what previously went wrong.**

This should govern the Phase-6 SyngUp dossier.

## 32.5 Succession is relational before it is bureaucratic

Phase 5 closes with multiple examples of institutions reproducing themselves through interpersonal memory:

- Mao becomes dorm leader because prior residents once sustained her.
- Tsubame designates Misuzu as someone who should support China and Ume.
- Sena can be imagined returning as an alumna to help school/community events.
- the third-years frame the sports-festival live as an encouragement to successors.
- trainers revise teaching because the next cohort has changed.
- Yu's broadcast work becomes meaningful because Sena repeatedly gives her opportunities and she discovers its value.

Formal titles matter, but the actual transmission unit is often: **someone remembers what support felt like and chooses to reproduce its function for the next person.**

## 32.6 Private leisure is not noise; it is part of voice and action reconstruction

The final tranche adds unusually rich low-stakes priors:

- Tsubame is an anime fan whose public profile omits that interest strategically.
- Sena has little experience with ordinary teenage leisure and can overcompensate enthusiastically.
- Lilja is a high-level fighting-game player and still bad at crane games.

These are exactly the kinds of variables that distinguish a plausible mundane simulation from a generic “core personality” imitation.

# 33. Phase-5 completion synthesis — from care language to relational decision machinery

Phase 5 began with a deliberately modest question: what do support stories reveal that main routes cannot? Across **498 support scripts / 9,777 messages**, the answer became progressively more structural.

## 33.1 Eight layers established by the complete support pass

1. **Default care language** — low-stakes scenes expose what a character reaches for first: optimization, food, correction, invitation, logistics, play, observation, opportunity, or endurance.
2. **Behavioral intimacy before declarative intimacy** — relationships often become close in practice before the characters agree on `友達`, family-like, rival, or other labels.
3. **Care as technique** — durable care becomes procedural: diet design, taping, tutoring, cooking, scheduling, staged invitations, body regulation, social framing.
4. **Interpretive literacy** — close people learn how to read another person's denials, complaints, pride, pacing, and indirect bids without reducing them to one “true self.”
5. **Social infrastructure** — care circulates through groups, dorms, councils, trainers, jobs, family, classes, and ordinary-course peers rather than remaining dyadic.
6. **Continuity without stasis** — relationships survive by carrying forward useful functions while changing the terms under which those functions operate.
7. **Translation and brokerage** — mature networks contain people who can route needs and care across relationship boundaries when direct access is blocked.
8. **Internalized relational priors** — important others become part of the character's own repertoire for deciding what to do.

These layers should not become eight independent trait lists. They form a single action model.

## 33.2 Phase-5 reconstruction heuristic

For downstream character simulation, a plausible response should be conditioned by:

> **character prior + current state + domain competence + recipient model + relationship history + access permission + network position + institutional affordance + internalized others.**

This is not a mathematical scoring system. It is a guardrail against flattening.

For example, “Temari is blunt but kind” is insufficient. A better reconstruction asks whether the recipient already understands Temari's reluctant-care register, whether Temari possesses relevant expertise, whether the request touches pride or dependency, whether another person is brokering the interaction, and whether Temari has reason to interpret the situation through Misuzu, Hiro, China, Kotone, Rinha, or another internalized relationship history.

Likewise, “Sena is a great leader” is insufficient. The source distinguishes president, `一番星`, rival, childhood friend, producer aspirant, Kotone admirer, institutional representative, and ordinary high-school girl with missing leisure experience. Which mode activates depends on context.

## 33.3 What Phase 5 rejects

The complete support pass provides enough counterevidence to reject or strongly downgrade several simplistic reconstructions:

- **REJECT:** “one stable competence level per character.”
- **REJECT:** “care is proven by softness.”
- **REJECT:** “friendship labels determine relationship depth.”
- **REJECT:** “hierarchy determines the direction of teaching.”
- **REJECT:** “post-conflict repair means returning to the old relationship.”
- **REJECT:** “private-role inconsistency reveals the public self as fake.”
- **REJECT:** “wealth or hardship alone determines social capability.”
- **REJECT:** “the closest relationship should handle every need.”
- **DOWNGRADE:** “body, training, or diet advice can be modeled without recipient-specific physiology and consent.”
- **DOWNGRADE:** “institutional continuity is mainly bureaucratic.”

## 33.4 Phase-5 exit condition

The architecture-defined exit condition is satisfied under Source Lock 1.0:

- **all 498 support-card scripts read;**
- **all 9,777 support-card messages covered;**
- support-story participants indexed;
- A1 source locators routed;
- durable character/relationship/institution/theme/register/NPC/song deltas integrated;
- performance-sensitive ambiguities routed to AV verification rather than silently resolved from text;
- Phase-6 relationship/ensemble priorities materially sharpened.

Phase 5 can therefore move from **IN PROGRESS** to **COMPLETE**.
