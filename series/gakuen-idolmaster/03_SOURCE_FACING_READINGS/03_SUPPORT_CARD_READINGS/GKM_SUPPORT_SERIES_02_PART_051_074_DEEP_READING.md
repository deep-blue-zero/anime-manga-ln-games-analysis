### Principal tranche thesis — care becomes shared interpretation

The first three Phase-5 readings built a sequence:

1. **Support Series 1:** low-stakes scenes expose default care languages and default miscalibrations.
2. **Series 2 Part 001–025:** intimacy often becomes behavioral before either person agrees on the label.
3. **Series 2 Part 026–050:** care becomes durable when translated into technique.

The present tranche adds a fourth layer:

> **Close relationships become reconstructively stable when people learn how to interpret one another's behavior without demanding that the other person become simpler.**

The key development is not merely more care. It is **better reading**.

- Ume correctly reads Temari's desire to approach a dog, then later recognizes exactly how far she can tease Temari about friendship before retreating.
- Rinami reads Kotone's exhaustion as older-sister overload and temporarily offers her permission to stop performing that role.
- Mao and Rinami can disagree about a dance, remember earlier disagreements, and treat friction as part of a durable relation rather than evidence that the relation has failed.
- Tsubame recognizes that Hiro's request for greater severity is precisely when severity must be constrained by physical reality.
- Kotone reads Tsubame's intrusive training check as concern and thanks her even while complaining about the intrusion.
- China and Misuzu exchange cultural expertise without either reducing the other to “wealthy beginner” or “lazy girl.”
- Lilja and Hiro discover that superficially similar physical weakness rests on very different motivational grammars, then design a low-intensity activity that can accommodate both.
- Sena's peers and juniors increasingly see the person behind the institutional role without needing to declare the role false.
- Temari repeatedly experiences people whose care is legible to her even when its delivery is irritating, embarrassing, or physically overpowered.

The resulting modeling rule is:

> **Do not reconstruct intimacy by smoothing disagreement away. Reconstruct the specific misunderstandings, tolerances, repair patterns, and interpretive shortcuts that a relationship has learned to survive.**

A second tranche-wide finding is **ordinary-role reversibility**. Several characters who are normally teachers become students, people who are normally protected become caregivers, and people who appear socially effortless reveal domains of embarrassment or dependence:

- China learns tea posture from Misuzu and then teaches her afternoon-tea practice.
- Sumika helps China with flexibility while China draws Sumika into an enthusiastic ballet conversation Sumika had initially tried to keep casual.
- Mao teaches Hiro martial-arts form, while Hiro's game-design expertise later causes Mao to overtrain through a badly calibrated reward loop.
- Rinami cares for exhausted Kotone, then receives Kotone's handmade gift and admiration.
- Tsubame mentors Hiro physically, but Hiro's description of Tsubame as another person who does not fit the stereotypical idol image forces Tsubame to concede a point about herself.
- Sena is the top idol and president, yet Ume becomes the person who teaches her how to perform an ordinary summer game.

> **Competence remains domain-specific. Rank does not abolish local dependence.**

This is especially important for downstream character simulation because it prevents static role assignment. A strong model must know not only who normally leads, but **what kinds of situations cause that person to accept instruction, become embarrassed, reveal ignorance, or let another character define the activity.**

# 2. `story_0051` — China and Misuzu: calmness is partly technique, partly temperament

**Sources:** `story_0051/part_01` — 20 messages; `part_02` — 20 messages  
**Participants:** China, Misuzu, Tsubame  
**Functions:** tea practice, embodied instruction, admiration, humor, student-council work

China enters the school's tea room for the first time and admits that she abandoned tea practice because sitting formally made her legs go numb. Misuzu does not moralize the failure. She gives concrete body instructions: distribute weight differently, imagine being suspended upward, lengthen the spine, breathe, and avoid overthinking the image.

China improves quickly and attributes the success to Misuzu's teaching.

The scene does two things at once. First, it extends Misuzu's practical competence. Her low-effort orientation is not lack of embodied knowledge; she can explain a traditional practice with precise, recipient-readable cues. Second, it shows China as a receptive learner who does not defend status by pretending prior competence.

Part 2 turns China's admiration into a comic misunderstanding. China says she has long envied Misuzu's composure and asks what habit produces it. Misuzu answers: napping. China almost converts that into a self-improvement protocol before Tsubame interrupts and identifies the obvious problem: Misuzu is using a flattering explanation for something she often simply wants to do.

This should not be read as evidence that Misuzu's calmness is fake. It establishes instead that **Misuzu can fold self-serving humor into advice without China's literal admiration immediately detecting the joke**.

### Claim transitions

- **Misuzu — STRENGTHEN:** embodied/domestic/traditional competence remains high even where formal exertion motivation is low.
- **Misuzu — STRENGTHEN:** teaching style is gentle, image-based, and non-shaming.
- **China — STRENGTHEN:** admiration can make her unusually literal and eager to convert another person's trait into a reproducible practice.
- **China ↔ Misuzu — PROMOTE:** reciprocal respect is now grounded in direct teaching rather than only council proximity.
- **Tsubame — STRENGTHEN:** senior oversight can function as comic reality-checking rather than only severe correction.

### Behavioral modeling note

A plausible Misuzu response to a struggling peer often begins by reducing effort-friction through a small technique. A plausible China response to admired competence is to ask how it can be learned, sometimes with insufficient skepticism about whether the answer is serious.

# 3. `story_0052` — China and Sumika: admiration reopens ballet as positive language

**Sources:** 20 + 19 messages  
**Participants:** China, Sumika  
**Functions:** flexibility, physical assistance, admiration, ballet interest, hidden continuity

Sumika finds China working on flexibility and immediately offers practical help: assisted stretching, breath timing, and recommendations for equipment. China then unexpectedly gives Sumika a long, specific compliment. She admires Sumika's willingness to help while managing her own training, her sensitivity to whether others feel intimidated, her fashion, and the beauty of individual dance movements.

Sumika is sufficiently flustered that she accidentally presses too hard during the stretch.

This matters because China's admiration is not generic “Sumika is cool.” She names **social ease plus technical beauty**. That combination is a good Phase-7 retrieval target for Sumika's public attractiveness: brightness is not merely affect; it includes competent regulation of other people's comfort.

The second part is more consequential. Sumika casually recommends ballet stretching. China infers that she likes ballet and begins discussing `白鳥の湖`, large-ensemble synchronization, emotional expression, and the visual force of the prima. Sumika initially tries to remain noncommittal, then answers with obvious fluency and enthusiasm before realizing China has drawn her out.

This does **not** erase Sumika's painful ballet history. It does establish that ballet remains available to her as aesthetic knowledge and pleasure. The safe revision is:

> **A painful ballet career does not imply hatred of ballet as an art form.**

### Claim transitions

- **Sumika — STRENGTHEN:** physical-help language is casual, upbeat, and immediately practical.
- **Sumika — REVISE:** former-ballet pain should not be generalized into emotional rejection of ballet itself; aesthetic attachment and expert attention remain accessible.
- **China — STRENGTHEN:** sustained admiration can draw out another person's concealed or underplayed enthusiasm without confrontation.
- **China ↔ Sumika — STRENGTHEN:** their relation supports both body-level instruction and aesthetic conversation.

### AV request

The moment Sumika shifts from evasive casualness into genuine ballet enthusiasm is performance-sensitive and worth voice verification. The textual transition is clear; the degree of self-consciousness is not.

# 4. `story_0053` — Lilja and Hiro: similar bodies, different theories of difficulty

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** Lilja, Hiro  
**Functions:** shared training, physical limits, motivational contrast, Swedish continuity, ordinary-room intimacy

Lilja and Hiro train together because both occupy the low-physical-capacity end of the cast. Superficially, this might look like simple solidarity among weak students. The dialogue makes the difference between them explicit.

Hiro says training with Lilja is `苦しくて、楽しい`. Lilja recognizes the danger because she herself sometimes collapses, but explains her own persistence differently: she believes she lacks talent and therefore has to work harder than others to catch up.

Hiro responds that liking something matters more than talent.

This is a useful contrast because the same physical behavior can arise from different motivational structures:

- Hiro approaches difficulty because resistance itself is interesting and because idolhood is the unlikely path she chose.
- Lilja accepts difficulty because she wants an idol future strongly enough to compensate for a perceived deficit.

Neither should be simulated as “the weak girl who overtrains.”

Part 2 places Hiro in Lilja's room. The social access is ordinary and unforced: anime collector's boxes, photographs of Sweden sent by Lilja's father, unfamiliar Japanese vocabulary, and a training book. Lilja has found `プロギング`, the Swedish-origin practice of jogging while collecting litter, and specifically thinks it might suit **both** of them because it builds stamina without requiring extreme intensity.

This is relationally important. Lilja does not merely receive motivation from Hiro. She researches an activity calibrated to Hiro's body as well as her own.

### Claim transitions

- **Lilja — STRENGTHEN:** effort is increasingly self-calibrated rather than synonymous with maximum intensity.
- **Hiro — STRENGTHEN:** she can validate another person's aspiration without converting that person's motive into her own resistance-seeking philosophy.
- **Lilja ↔ Hiro — PROMOTE:** shared physical limitation becomes reciprocal pacing and ordinary private-space friendship.
- **Lilja family context — STRENGTHEN:** her father actively maintains Swedish-home continuity by sending landscape photographs.
- **Lilja — STRENGTHEN:** cultural identity can feed practical idol training rather than existing only as biography.

### Modeling implication

The pair is a useful falsification test for character simulators. Given the same exhausting exercise, Hiro should tend to describe the resistance as interesting; Lilja should tend to connect persistence to catching up, fulfilling a dream, or making herself capable of continuing.

# 5. `story_0054` — Sumika and Mao: mutual styling and comedy as adaptive performance

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** Sumika, Mao  
**Functions:** clothing, reciprocal gaze, humor, institutional outreach, performance rehearsal

Mao asks Sumika for help choosing clothing. Sumika happily turns the task into a dress-up session until Mao notices. Instead of ending the play, Mao reverses it: a jacket that does not fit him suits Sumika, and he begins styling *her* with equal enthusiasm.

The important point is reciprocal gaze. Mao is not simply the prince who evaluates others or the senior who receives junior fashion knowledge. He notices Sumika's height, legs, and model-like proportions and becomes excited by how clothing works on her body.

Part 2 reveals a different kind of performance adaptation. Mao and Sumika have been assigned a comedy routine for prospective Hatsuboshi applicants because the student council wants the school to feel approachable. Sumika thinks the demand is unreasonable. Mao treats it as a job and studies how to make it work, even deliberately feeding Sumika a false claim about Idol Course students walking through halls in dance steps so that her natural `なんでやねーん` becomes the desired straight-man response.

### Claim transitions

- **Mao — STRENGTHEN:** performance authorship extends into humor and outreach work, not only prince acting or dramatic roles.
- **Mao — STRENGTHEN:** he can engineer another person's spontaneous register instead of demanding polished acting.
- **Sumika — STRENGTHEN:** fashion competence is both technical and playful; she accepts role reversal when Mao becomes stylist.
- **Mao ↔ Sumika — STRENGTHEN:** reciprocal teasing/styling has become low-stakes social play rather than solely production collaboration.
- **Institution — STRENGTHEN:** student-council recruitment strategy includes making Idol Course life legible and approachable to prospective students.

### Japanese-register note

Mao's attempt to sharpen `なんでやねん` and Sumika's eventual natural retort are useful comic-register anchors. The humor is not generic Kansai identity; it is a rehearsed performance problem solved by provoking authentic timing.

# 6. `story_0056` — Tsubame and Hiro: severity becomes care when it knows where to stop

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** Tsubame, Hiro  
**Functions:** training safety, body assessment, professional severity, nonstandard idol identity

Tsubame is helping supervise first-year running. She begins in her normal severe register: straighten the arm, look forward, do not quit because it hurts. Hiro falls far behind. Tsubame initially interprets continued effort as admirable and pushes harder.

Then she notices Hiro's actual physical state.

The shift is immediate. Tsubame orders rest, water, and safety. Hiro, barely functional, asks her to be *more* severe. Tsubame reacts with genuine alarm.

Part 2 makes the principle explicit. Tsubame describes Hiro's body without euphemism: poor base stamina, little muscle or fat, slight skeletal build, small appetite, and therefore a body not naturally suited to idol work. Hiro calls her kind.

The kindness is not softness of wording. It is **accurate constraint**.

When Hiro asks Tsubame to train her because she cannot become an idol alone, Tsubame asks why she insists on idolhood despite other outstanding abilities. Hiro answers: because it seems the thing least suited to her.

Then Hiro turns the comparison back on Tsubame: Tsubame also does not resemble the stereotypical public image of an idol. Tsubame resists, then concedes that the point is not entirely wrong.

### Claim transitions

- **Tsubame — STRENGTHEN:** severity is condition-sensitive; once physical danger is legible, pushing harder stops being virtuous.
- **Tsubame — STRENGTHEN:** care often appears as precise negative judgment plus actionable limits, not reassurance.
- **Hiro — STRENGTHEN:** she can experience accurate severity as kindness when it treats her chosen goal seriously.
- **Hiro ↔ Tsubame — PROMOTE:** mutual recognition forms around nonconformity to ordinary idol expectations.
- **Tsubame — STRENGTHEN:** she can accept a destabilizing observation about her own public fit without abandoning pride.

### Phase-7 behavioral rule

A reconstructed Tsubame should not simply maximize intensity. Her credibility depends on distinguishing **discipline from preventable physical collapse**.

# 7. `story_0058` — Rinami and Kotone: care can temporarily suspend the older-sister role

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** Rinami, Kotone  
**Functions:** summer fatigue, sibling labor, permission to depend, reciprocal gifts

Rinami sees Kotone visibly depleted during summer. Kotone's exhaustion is not reducible to training: tests, homework, lessons, work, and the emotional/logistical burden of thinking about where to take her younger siblings during school vacation are all accumulating.

Rinami understands immediately because she is also an older sister. The similarity matters more than socioeconomic symmetry. Their circumstances differ, but they share the experience of being a child who is routinely expected to think about younger children.

Rinami buys Kotone ice cream, listens, validates the strain, and then makes an explicitly temporary offer: for this moment, Kotone can stop being the older sister and become Rinami's younger sister instead.

Kotone accepts with exaggerated sweetness rather than rejecting the dependency as humiliating.

This is a strong addition to both characters. Kotone's resistance to being controlled does not imply resistance to receiving care. She accepts care when it is **bounded, freely offered, and does not create debt or claim authority over her future**.

Part 2 reverses the flow. Kotone brings homemade cookies to thank Rinami, accepts an invitation into her room, admires her diffuser and high “feminine competence,” and calls her an ideal woman. Rinami responds by praising Kotone's warmth, popularity, kindness, and the way even a greeting from her can energize other people.

Kotone's internal response—Rinami is “unfair” when she talks like this—shows that praise from Rinami gets through her defensive comic surface unusually efficiently.

### Claim transitions

- **Kotone — STRENGTHEN:** receiving care is acceptable when it does not threaten autonomy, finances, or future obligation.
- **Rinami — STRENGTHEN:** reversible hospitality includes allowing another caretaker to become the cared-for person without stripping her competence.
- **Rinami ↔ Kotone — PROMOTE:** shared older-sister identity supports unusually fast emotional recognition across year/status difference.
- **Kotone — STRENGTHEN:** handmade gifts are part of her reciprocity repertoire despite limited money.
- **Rinami — STRENGTHEN:** praise often names social impact rather than abstract virtue.

### Modeling implication

Kotone should not be treated as globally suspicious of nurturing senior women. The trigger is not “care” but **care that carries hidden cost, leverage, or loss of self-direction**.

# 8. `story_0059` — Hiro and Mao: expertise can become a badly designed incentive

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** Hiro, Mao, dance trainer  
**Functions:** martial-arts training, childhood play, software/game expertise, gamification risk

Hiro decides that martial-arts training might strengthen body and mind and invents a self-imposed challenge: catch a falling leaf before it reaches the ground. Mao recognizes the structure immediately because he invented similar “training” games as a child.

Mao does not mock the impulse. He redirects it toward fundamentals and offers to teach basic forms.

This is a small but useful overlap between them: both can turn development into stylized challenge, but Mao has enough embodied training to know where fantasy needs technique.

Part 2 reverses the competence hierarchy. Hiro has been asked by the ordinary-course game club to advise on an app that rewards completed lessons with collectible items. Mao agrees to alpha-test it, becomes hooked on obtaining a rare character, and extends dance practice beyond the trainer's limit to pursue the reward.

Hiro's internal conclusion is not “the app works.” It is that the engagement system has room for improvement.

This is analytically valuable because it shows **Hiro recognizing an unintended behavioral consequence of system design**. She is not merely a programmer or clever observer. She can evaluate how a designed incentive changes another person's choices.

### Claim transitions

- **Hiro — STRENGTHEN:** technical/software competence includes product-behavior observation and iterative design judgment.
- **Mao — STRENGTHEN:** challenge/reward structures can pull his disciplined personality into overinvestment.
- **Mao — STRENGTHEN:** childhood imagination and current training discipline are continuous rather than opposed.
- **Hiro ↔ Mao — STRENGTHEN:** expertise is strongly bidirectional—Mao supplies embodied technique; Hiro supplies systems/design expertise.
- **Institution — STRENGTHEN:** Idol Course students participate in ordinary-course club projects across school subcultures.

### Counterexample to a simple thesis

“Care becomes technique” does not mean technical intervention is automatically beneficial. Hiro's app creates a motivation loop powerful enough to distort Mao's rest boundary. **Good intentions require feedback.**

# 9. `story_0060` — Hiro and Rinami: ordinary firsts as relationship material

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** Hiro, Rinami  
**Functions:** volunteer labor, student-council community work, puns, cooking instruction

Rinami has been asked to help collect fallen chestnuts as part of neighborhood cleanup, but the student council is busy. Hiro volunteers because she has never picked chestnuts before.

The scene is useful because Hiro's novelty-seeking appears at very low stakes. She is interested in thorns, insects, and the physical object itself. Rinami repeatedly warns her about injury but does not overtake the task.

Their accidental pun sequence—`広ちゃん拾って` / `広い`—also establishes a shared comic register that does not depend on Hiro being bizarre or Rinami being maternal. They can simply be silly together.

Part 2 converts the volunteer material into cooking. Hiro has never prepared chestnuts. She damages them while peeling and Rinami immediately changes the evaluation criterion: appearance does not matter because the chestnuts will become paste.

That response is a compact example of good instruction. Rinami does not tell Hiro the failure did not happen; she tells her why the failure is **non-consequential for the actual goal**.

They turn all the chestnuts into Mont Blanc, and Hiro makes an absurdly large one. The closing wordplay around “Rinami Mont Blanc” keeps the scene in low-pressure shared play.

### Claim transitions

- **Rinami — STRENGTHEN:** teaching often reduces anxiety by reclassifying which errors actually matter.
- **Hiro — STRENGTHEN:** ordinary novelty can be intrinsically motivating even when no extreme resistance is involved.
- **Hiro ↔ Rinami — PROMOTE:** the pair sustains practical work, instruction, humor, and food-making without needing a crisis frame.
- **Institution — STRENGTHEN:** student council/community obligations include mundane neighborhood maintenance and resource redistribution.

# 10. `story_0061` — Rinami and Temari: body anxiety meets non-punitive beauty labor

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** Rinami, Temari  
**Functions:** stretching, body perception, praise response, beauty-care routine

Temari and Rinami do flexibility work together. Rinami praises Temari's knowledge and then comments that Temari has a strong balance of muscle and fat for stage appearance.

Temari's immediate internal reaction is not pride. It is fear that she has gained weight.

Rinami clarifies that she means the opposite of criticism: Temari's body reads well in dance and looks ideal for an idol. Temari then internally melts under the praise.

This is high-value modeling evidence because it demonstrates the speed of Temari's body-anxiety trigger. A technically neutral or positive observation involving fat/weight can be parsed first as threat, especially before clarification.

Part 2 shows Rinami sharing body oil, lemon water, skin-care knowledge, cold-prevention routines, and diet-adjacent advice. Temari stares at Rinami and realizes that her polished hair and skin are products of repeated small effort.

The scene helps distinguish two kinds of “effort” Temari values:

1. obvious performance/training exertion;
2. hidden maintenance labor that makes a professional presentation possible.

She is willing to incorporate the second once it becomes legible.

### Claim transitions

- **Temari — STRENGTHEN:** weight/body sensitivity can activate even inside praise; do not simulate it only in explicitly diet-focused scenes.
- **Temari — STRENGTHEN:** praise from a respected senior penetrates defensiveness very quickly.
- **Rinami — STRENGTHEN:** beauty competence is practical maintenance knowledge, not only aesthetic polish.
- **Temari ↔ Rinami — PROMOTE:** Rinami can mentor Temari in body care without triggering the oppositional response that some other forms of intervention produce.
- **Theme — STRENGTHEN:** professional presentation is supported by invisible maintenance labor.

### AV request

Temari's internal shift from weight panic to delighted reception of Rinami's praise is a strong voice-performance target.

# 11. `story_0062` — Kotone and Mao: teasing tests the permeability of princehood

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** Kotone, Mao  
**Functions:** Halloween play, fear response, remembered preference, sweet coffee identity

Mao is choosing a Halloween costume and remembers that Rinami teased him the previous year by proposing a bunny outfit. He has come alone this time because he wants to choose something “cool.”

Kotone hides, appears as a zombie, and scares him badly enough that his composed senior image collapses into a startled cry. Kotone immediately recognizes that she may have gone too far.

The point is not “Mao is secretly cowardly.” The point is role permeability. Kotone has enough relational access to create a scene in which Mao's unguarded reaction appears without making the relationship unsafe.

Part 2 is subtler. Kotone makes coffee jelly because Mao previously said he likes coffee. Mao's private reaction shows anxiety about the coffee bitterness, but Kotone adds a very sweet condensed-milk sauce because that is how *she* likes to make the dessert. Mao is relieved, genuinely enjoys it, and considers trying the combination again.

This should not be overread as Kotone deliberately exposing the black-coffee pose. The text does not establish that she knows. What it does show is that an ordinary gift accidentally creates a bridge between **Mao's stated coffee identity and his actual sweet preference**.

### Claim transitions

This is health-sensitive mentorship in a harder register than Rinami's hospitality or Misuzu's anticipatory environmental design.

### Claim transitions

- **Tsubame — STRENGTHEN:** fandom and rivalry are not opposites; she can openly admire Sena's growth while treating it as competitive data.
- **Tsubame → Kotone — PROMOTE:** mentorship includes technical correction plus willingness to override a junior's training preference when health appears at risk.

- **Kotone ↔ Tsubame — PROMOTE:** complaint, trust, correction, and gratitude can coexist in one short interaction.
- **Tsubame — STRENGTHEN:** health is explicitly framed as professional capital whose loss imposes costs on others.

### Modeling distinction

Tsubame's intervention is more directive than Rinami's. A simulator should not flatten all “senior care” into one style:

- Rinami tends to offer and reopen choice.
- Tsubame tends to assess, decide, and justify the decision through professional obligation.

Both can be caring.

# 34. Final tranche synthesis

Support Series 2 closes with a much richer ordinary-life model than it began with.

The support pass now supplies four nested levels of reconstruction:

1. **default care language** — what a character reaches for first;
2. **behavioral intimacy** — what kinds of access and imposition a relationship can survive;
3. **care as technique** — how affection becomes practical intervention;
4. **shared interpretation** — how people learn to read one another's motives, limits, jokes, defenses, and failures without demanding simplification.

This final Series-2 tranche also demonstrates why support-card evidence is indispensable for later character simulation. The core/event corpus can tell us that Sena is a producer-minded institutional leader, Tsubame a heroic rival, Mao a refractive role-author, Kotone an autonomy-centered worker-idol, Rinami a reversible host, Ume a renewable rival, and Hiro a resistance-seeking experimentalist.

The support stories tell us what those abstractions *do* at 4 p.m. in a hallway, dorm room, tea room, clothing store, summer event, practice room, or grocery-adjacent social situation.

They tell us:

- who notices cold hands;
- who buys ice cream;
- who turns a pillow into a capital-investment argument;
- who thinks a 3D printer is an ordinary chocolate-making tool;
- who reads a friend's facial expression before the friend speaks;
- who gets embarrassed by being called cute;
- who treats a fight as an intimacy experiment;
- who can praise a rival while disguised in the rival's audience;
- who makes a training app too engaging;
- who realizes that “failure” does not matter when the chestnut will become paste;
- who says “you are already cool” before the other person can believe it.

The strongest tranche-level conclusion is therefore:

> **Gakumas intimacy is not the disappearance of friction. It is the accumulation of accurate enough interpretations that friction becomes survivable, useful, funny, or reparable.**

That principle should govern Phase 6 relationship synthesis. The next phase should not rank bonds only by confessions, explicit labels, or dramatic sacrifices. It should ask:

- What does each person reliably notice about the other?
- What kinds of correction can pass without rupture?
- What kinds of help are welcomed, tolerated, or rejected?
- What does each person chronically misread?
- How does the dyad repair those errors?
- Which private or mundane self-states are available only inside that relationship?

Series 2 has now supplied enough ordinary-life evidence to answer those questions for a large fraction of the central cast.
