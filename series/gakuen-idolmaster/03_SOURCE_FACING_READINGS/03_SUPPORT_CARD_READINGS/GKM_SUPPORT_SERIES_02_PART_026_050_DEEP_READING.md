# GKM SUPPORT SERIES 02 PART 026–050 DEEP READING

## 0. Scope, method, and principal finding

This is the third canonical Phase-5 support-card reading for the Gakuen Idolmaster V2 corpus, following `GKM_SUPPORT_SERIES_01_DEEP_READING.md` and `GKM_SUPPORT_SERIES_02_PART_001_025_DEEP_READING.md`.

The source boundary is the complete `support_series_2_part_026-050.dialogue.txt` analysis bundle: **50 unique support-card scripts / 995 messages**, organized as **25 support stories, `story_0026` through `story_0050`, with two parts each**. Unlike the preceding Series-2 bundle, the filename range and actual byte boundary agree exactly.

As throughout Phase 5, support-story IDs are not treated as chronology. Paired parts under one support-story number are read as related evidence surfaces, not automatically as adjacent scenes. Most scenes remain modular C2/C4-compatible evidence unless an explicit memory, seasonal anchor, institutional role, or relationship-state reference earns stronger placement.

The A2 dialogue extraction is the reading surface. A1 raw-script paths remain the source authority for wording and are retained in the locator section. No facial animation, card illustration, camera state, costume rendering, or vocal nuance is promoted from text alone. Performance-sensitive questions are routed to the audiovisual queue.

### Principal tranche thesis — care becomes technique

Support Series 1 established that low-stakes scenes reveal each character's default care language and default miscalibration. Series 2 Part 001–025 showed that intimacy often exists behaviorally before the characters agree on a label. The present tranche adds a third layer:

> **In ordinary life, care becomes durable when it is translated into technique.**

Characters repeatedly show attachment by learning how to *do something* for, with, or around another person:

- Rinami and Misuzu discover one another through cooking competence rather than confession.
- Saki's love for Ume is embedded in nutrition design, SSD preparation, seasonal lunches, and recipient-specific chocolate planning.
- Misuzu's care for Temari becomes meal planning around Valentine's chocolate rather than a generic declaration of concern.
- Sena converts admiration into active scouting, technical feedback, institutional succession, and escalating attempts to understand Kotone's needs.
- Mao's dorm leadership becomes bug removal, snake trapping, rule maintenance, and the willingness to accept junior help when the work is genuinely shared.
- Hiro turns social play into a designed environment: she finds games, creates contests, and works to create situations where friends will enjoy themselves.
- Sumika's social intelligence becomes food-sharing, fashion translation, digital publicity, and invitations that lower the cost of joining a group.
- Trainers operationalize care through structured correction, deliberately chosen fundamentals, audience imagination, and workload-specific advice.
- Tsubame and Sena treat teaching the next student council as part of succession rather than as clerical inconvenience.

The tranche therefore supports a stronger behavioral-modeling proposition:

> **To reconstruct a Gakumas character plausibly, do not ask only what she feels. Ask what practical intervention she reaches for when she decides another person matters.**

This also sharpens an existing caveat. Technique can misfire. Saki can over-optimize; Sena can escalate support into pursuit; Ume can turn help into impact force; Hiro can turn ordinary objects into danger because difficulty attracts her. Care is not automatically correct because it is sincere. But care becomes legible through the *method* chosen, and those methods are highly character-specific.

A second tranche-wide finding concerns **reciprocal expertise**. Seniority, rank, wealth, and talent do not produce one-way competence hierarchies. Rinami is the senior but asks Misuzu to teach her Japanese cooking. Sena knows fish preparation intellectually but respects China's practiced skill. Mao can model a prince persona while Sumika teaches her social-media performance. Kotone is economically constrained but can teach Sumika cost-efficient shopping. Lilja is technically behind Saki in dance yet can still become a source of motivation for Saki. Ordinary life repeatedly redistributes expertise.

> **Gakumas social hierarchy is therefore porous: people can remain unequal in rank while still becoming locally dependent on one another's knowledge.**

# 2. `story_0026` — Rinami and Misuzu: reciprocal domestic competence

**Sources:** `story_0026/part_01` — 20 messages; `part_02` — 20 messages  
**Participants:** Rinami, Misuzu  
**Functions:** food culture, reciprocal teaching, senior/junior ease, council context

Rinami is preparing stewed hamburger steak and potato salad. Misuzu enters only to make tea, but her attention is caught because her family usually ate Japanese food. Rinami invites her to cook together while immediately emphasizing that she should not feel obligated.

The invitation is characteristically Rinami: **offer access, remove pressure, leave the choice intact**.

The second part reverses the apparent expertise hierarchy. Misuzu peels potatoes quickly and correctly removes the eyes before Rinami can explain. Rinami is skilled at Western-style home cooking; Misuzu has substantial Japanese-cooking experience from home. Rinami therefore asks Misuzu to teach *her* next time.

This is a clean example of reciprocal expertise across seniority. Rinami does not preserve authority by pretending competence. She enjoys being able to receive instruction from the junior she was initially going to guide.

### Claim transitions

- **Rinami — STRENGTHEN:** low-stakes hospitality follows the same consent-sensitive structure observed in higher-stakes help scenes.
- **Rinami — STRENGTHEN:** receptive authority includes openly becoming the learner when another person's domain knowledge is stronger.
- **Misuzu — STRENGTHEN:** her low-effort orientation coexists with substantial practical domestic competence; Japanese cooking is a familiar household skill rather than an exceptional effort display.
- **Rinami ↔ Misuzu — PROMOTE:** ordinary mutual respect extends beyond student-council coordination into domestic peer/senior life.

### Continuity note

The closing reference to a student-council meeting “tomorrow” places the vignette inside a period where both are already active in the council ecology, but does not independently determine an exact calendar point.

# 3. `story_0027` — third-year familiarity and Sena's unselfconscious intensity

**Sources:** 20 + 20 messages  
**Participants:** Sena, Mao, Rinami; Sena, Mao, Kotone  
**Functions:** third-year familiarity, hidden taste management, Kotone pursuit, peer witnessing

Part 1 quietly deepens Mao/Rinami. Mao is drinking black coffee. Rinami notices, asks to exchange it for her own sweetened coffee because she suddenly “wants black,” and Mao thanks *her* for making the trade. Sena immediately notices the unusually soft expression on Mao's face and asks why the person doing the favor is the one expressing gratitude.

The implication is bounded but strong: Rinami understands Mao well enough to create an excuse that saves Mao from openly admitting a preference or difficulty, and Mao understands what she has done.

Part 2 shifts to Sena's fixation on Kotone. Sena calls Kotone a giant diamond in the rough, offers to buy her tea, and declares them “partners and friends” so they can discuss future production. Kotone's answer is revealingly double-negative: she does not *entirely* deny friendship, and free food tempts her, but she still escapes the production conversation.

Mao's reaction is useful observer triangulation. He has known Sena as a high-functioning president and top idol but had not realized this “intense” side existed.

### Claim transitions

- **Mao ↔ Rinami — STRENGTHEN:** reciprocal care can operate through face-saving indirection; Rinami knows when to solve a problem without naming it.
- **Mao — STRENGTHEN:** his composed prince/senior surface is observably more permeable around Rinami.
- **Sena — STRENGTHEN:** Kotone pursuit is experienced by established peers as a genuine departure in intensity, not merely normal presidential enthusiasm.
- **Kotone ↔ Sena — STRENGTHEN:** Kotone permits some friendship language while still rejecting Sena's attempt to convert relational access into production agreement.

### Behavioral modeling note

Sena does not understand herself as “being intense.” `私はいつも事実を述べているだけよ` is a strong reconstruction cue: her internal model may classify highly charged declarations as simply accurate description.

# 4. `story_0028` — Kotone and Ume: sibling history makes care legible

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** Kotone, Ume  
**Functions:** play, Hanami household history, sibling comparison, Saki's early caregiving

Ume is earnestly selecting the “best” pampas grass so she can show off to Saki. She gives Kotone one too; Kotone briefly accepts the gift before protesting the absurdity of treating it like a valuable present. The humor works because Ume's generosity is completely sincere.

Part 2 is biographically important. Ume explains that Saki has cooked snacks and meals for her since childhood. Their parents were often busy and away from home, and an initially failed pancake attempt triggered Saki's familiar response: practice until she would not fail again. Eventually Saki was preparing Ume's meals routinely.

Kotone answers from her own sibling experience, referring to her younger siblings as `チビども`. The scene creates a quiet bridge between two girls whose socioeconomic circumstances differ substantially but who both understand the burden and affection of older-sibling life.

### Claim transitions

- **Saki — STRENGTHEN:** optimization-through-care has a household developmental origin; failed caregiving tasks are converted into training projects.
- **Saki ↔ Ume — STRENGTHEN:** routine meal preparation is longstanding family infrastructure, not an idol-era invention.
- **Hanami family biography — PROMOTE:** the sisters' parents were often absent/busy enough that Saki's practical caregiving became regular.
- **Kotone — STRENGTHEN:** older-sibling identity is part of her ordinary social understanding even when she uses dismissive/comic labels for the younger children.

### Caution

The scene establishes frequent parental absence due to busyness; it does **not** establish neglect, motive, occupation, or a complete household-care arrangement.

# 5. `story_0029` — China and Temari: harshness interpreted inside trust

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** China, Temari; China, Temari, Hiro  
**Functions:** acting help, reciprocity, friendship-label negotiation, care-through-severity

Temari is struggling with an acting assignment because she cannot understand the mentality of a twisted character. China notices her hesitation, offers to listen despite openly doubting her usefulness, then gives concrete research suggestions: study the original work and ask the scriptwriter about the role. Temari immediately asks for more detail.

China is delighted because this is a chance to repay Temari's previous kindness.

After the performance, Temari credits China. Hiro asks whether the two are very close friends. China answers enthusiastically; Temari initially says “not *that* close,” sees China's disappointment, and backs down: `別に、いいよ。すごくでも、なんでも。`

China then says she has gradually come to understand Temari: frightening and mean on the surface, but fundamentally kind. Temari resists the flattering simplification—if she were truly kind, she would not speak that way, and she has no intention of apologizing. China nevertheless asks Temari to keep being strict because she understands the intent.

### Claim transitions

- **China ↔ Temari — PROMOTE/STRENGTHEN:** the relation is now explicitly reciprocal; China contributes expertise and sees herself as returning prior help.
- **Temari — STRENGTHEN:** she resists having harshness erased by benevolent interpretation. Care does not make every delivery choice “nice.”
- **China — STRENGTHEN:** receptive trust includes accepting difficult feedback without requiring the giver to perform softness.
- **Temari — STRENGTHEN:** friendship-label resistance can yield when she recognizes that the label itself matters to the other person.

### Japanese-register note

Temari's `別に、いいよ。すごくでも、なんでも。` is a useful low-stakes intimacy signature: concession is often delivered as reluctant permission rather than positive declaration.

# 6. `story_0030` — Saki and Hiro: care, rank, and the discovery of competitiveness

**Sources:** 18 + 20  
**Participants:** Saki, Hiro  
**Functions:** physical care, SSD, school-entry comparison, academic competition

Saki finds Hiro exhausted after a “walk to her limit” and offers her homemade SSD. Hiro likes the strange taste enough to ask for the recipe. Saki responds by deciding to prepare an extra portion every day and tells Hiro to take it freely from the refrigerator.

The care grammar is unmistakable: Saki sees recurring depletion and builds recurring infrastructure.

Saki proudly states she was overall entrance-exam valedictorian; Hiro says she had a perfect score on the written examination and concludes that Saki must be “the second-place person.” Saki explodes. Hiro then realizes, with apparent pleasure, that perhaps she herself is competitive.

### Claim transitions

- **Hiro — STRENGTHEN:** competition is not limited to physical impossibility. Intellectual comparison can produce genuine amusement and self-recognition.
- **Saki ↔ Hiro — STRENGTHEN:** their relationship can combine care infrastructure and ranking friction without either cancelling the other.

### Continuity note

The entrance-exam discussion is biographical evidence, but the support scene itself does not establish an exact current date.

# 7. `story_0031` — Ume, Rinami, and the secret ingredient in Saki's system

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** Ume, Rinami, Saki  
**Functions:** SSD recipe, sibling mythology, surveillance/play, Rinami interpretation

Ume describes Saki's SSD as a secret recipe. Her account of the ingredients is grotesque enough to alarm Rinami—egg, dried fish, liver, natto, and more. In the next part they spy on Saki making it and discover ordinary vegetables and fruit instead.

Saki has anticipated Ume's surveillance and leaves a note giving a hint: the secret ingredient begins with `あ`.

Rinami's private interpretation is almost certainly `愛情`.

The point is less the literal recipe than the relational system. Saki has turned nutrition into sibling folklore: the drink is practical care, performance preparation, an object of teasing, and a vehicle through which Ume experiences herself as specially cared for.

### Claim transitions

- **Saki ↔ Ume — STRENGTHEN:** care rituals gain symbolic value through secrecy, repetition, and play.
- **Rinami — STRENGTHEN:** she reads relational subtext rapidly without needing to expose it aloud.
- **Saki — STRENGTHEN:** her care can be technically elaborate and emotionally theatrical at the same time.

### Evidence caution

The text does not explicitly spell out `愛情`; Rinami's inner line marks it as her inference, and the analysis preserves that status.

# 9. `story_0033` — Sumika and Mao: the junior can teach the prince how to perform publicity

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** Sumika, Mao  
**Functions:** posing, social media, publicity, prince performance, flirtation/performance permeability

Sumika directs Mao through pose work and asks for a “prince-like” version. Mao gives her a full `子猫ちゃん♪` line, then discovers Sumika was also recording video for publicity rather than only taking the assignment photograph. Mao is mortified and tries to stop its use.

- **Mao — STRENGTHEN:** embarrassment at publicity does not negate the role; control over context remains important.
- **Sumika — STRENGTHEN:** social-media literacy is concrete production expertise that seniors can depend upon.
- **Mao ↔ Sumika — PROMOTE:** junior/senior relation supports reciprocal expertise and playful performance intimacy.

### AV target

The shift from Mao's embarrassment to voluntary prince improvisation, and Sumika's surprise, is strongly performance-sensitive.

# 10. `story_0034` — Saki and Ume: childhood competition survives inside domestic memory

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** Saki, Ume  
**Functions:** pose training, embodied absurdity, seasonal food, childhood rural memory

Saki trains the ability to hold a final pose so perfectly still that a dragonfly lands on her finger. She assumes this is specialized mastery—until a dragonfly casually lands in Ume's hair, which Saki interprets as possible unconscious attainment of the same “extreme.”

The joke restates a fundamental sister problem: Saki turns ability into method; Ume can sometimes arrive at similar outcomes through embodied ease.

A dragonfly triggers memories of playing near their grandmother's rice fields, getting muddy together, eating unknown berries, and competing at dragonfly catching. - **Hanami sisters — PROMOTE:** shared childhood includes recurring time around their grandmother's rice fields and outdoor play.

- **Ume — STRENGTHEN:** unusual bodily resilience is represented as longstanding rather than purely idol-era athletic development.
- **Saki ↔ Ume — STRENGTHEN:** rivalry predates idol competition and can attach to trivial childhood games without becoming emotionally hostile.

### Caution

Do not infer the grandmother's residence, profession beyond the rice-field association, or frequency of visits beyond what the memory supports.

# 11. `story_0035` — Ume and Hiro: the inability to stop can become a gift

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** Ume, Hiro  
**Functions:** craft, Christmas, difficulty attraction, dream-giving, semantic reframing

Hiro has learned knitting to make ugly Christmas sweaters for friends. The difficult part was not knitting; it was stopping. Without a satisfying endpoint she continued until the sweater reached an absurd `XXXXXXXXXXL` size. She concludes that knitting may not suit her precisely because it permits indefinite continuation.

This is a highly specific extension of Hiro's resistance model. A task can attract her not because it hurts but because it refuses to supply a natural stopping rule.

Ume reacts not by mocking the failure but by proposing more decorations and helping turn the object into a social joke for China.

Part 2 begins with Ume's childhood wish to become Santa because she wanted to make people happy. Hiro gives her factual information about professional Santa certification, then offers a conceptual bridge: Santa and idols both give people dreams. The childhood wish is therefore not discarded; it can be fulfilled in transformed form.

### Claim transitions

- **Hiro — PROMOTE/STRENGTHEN:** attraction to resistance includes open-ended tasks where the challenge is deciding when completion is enough.
- **Hiro — STRENGTHEN:** she can provide emotionally useful reframing through literal/factual reasoning rather than conventional reassurance.
- **Ume — STRENGTHEN:** “make people happy” is a childhood aspiration that can be integrated into her current idol goal.
- **Hiro ↔ Ume — STRENGTHEN:** friendship includes collaborative absurdity and surprisingly effective conceptual support.

# 12. `story_0036` — Lilja, Kotone, and Sumika: spontaneous affect defeats manufactured smiling

**Functions:** smile training, camera self-review, Sumika-centered spontaneity, teasing, attraction-coded play

Lilja asks Kotone for help because the trainer says her smile looks stiff. Kotone initially disclaims expertise but agrees after Lilja sincerely praises her smile. The first attempt is comically frightening.

- **Kotone — STRENGTHEN:** coaching skill often works through engineered social conditions rather than explicit technical instruction.
- **Sumika — STRENGTHEN:** `うちのリーリヤ` is playful possessive language that signals ease and protective intimacy.

### Caution

The scene supports romantic/attraction **coding and teasing**, not a definitive canon declaration of romance. Lilja explicitly rejects Kotone's implied reading while affirming liking and respect.

# 14. `story_0038` — Kotone and the dance trainer: professionalism means using limited time well

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** Kotone, dance trainer  
**Functions:** pedagogy, work ethic, scarce time, audience imagination

The dance trainer is strict but brings Kotone a sports drink because she has been working hard. Kotone's stated reason for effort is revealing: lesson time is valuable, available time is limited, so she wants to raise its quality as much as possible.

Kotone accepts the argument immediately.

### Claim transitions

- **Kotone — STRENGTHEN:** optimization is not exclusive to Saki; Kotone's version is scarcity-driven and explicitly time-economic.
- **Kotone — STRENGTHEN:** she is highly receptive to practical instruction when its purpose is explained.
- **Trainer system — STRENGTHEN:** technical instruction includes performance orientation and individualized encouragement rather than raw discipline alone.
- **Theme — STRENGTHEN:** effort is productive when connected to the actual recipient/context of performance.

# 15. `story_0039` — Saki and the visual trainer: every competence is convertible into idol development

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** Saki, visual trainer  
**Functions:** yoga, rest, teachability, visual identity, “trainer” disposition

The visual trainer introduces yoga as a method for regulating mental condition. Saki immediately asks for the functional effect, tries it, identifies possible use before performances and on rest days, and decides to teach it to Ume.

The trainer says Saki would be well suited to being a trainer. Saki does not reject the implication as a diversion from idolhood. She says she will use *that quality too* to become a top idol.

### Claim transitions

- **Saki — STRENGTHEN:** pedagogical aptitude is recognized externally and accepted as useful identity, not denied as non-idol labor.

### Register/cultural note

# 29. Institutional and everyday-life promotions

## 29.1 Trainers as relational error-correction infrastructure

Three trainer-focused stories sharpen the institutional model:

- Dance trainer → Kotone: limited lesson time must be connected to audience/performance purpose.
- Visual trainer → Saki: mental recovery, rest, and appearance are legitimate performance infrastructure, not “soft” distractions.
- Vocal trainer → Temari: advanced students need deliberately renewed fundamentals because competence itself creates neglect risk.

The school therefore does not merely maximize workload. Its trainer system repeatedly tries to correct **how** effort is allocated.

## 29.2 Dorm life as social production infrastructure

This tranche adds:

- shared kitchens supporting cross-year cooking;
- dorm leaders managing safety threats;
- inherited board games producing intergenerational communal culture;
- residents conducting errands for cleaning and food supplies;
- shared refrigerators and routine food infrastructure.

The dorm is not only accommodation; it is a major low-stakes relationship engine.

## 29.3 Student-council succession is skill transmission

`story_0049` makes explicit that next year's council members will be taught current administrative workflows. Tsubame's expertise becomes part of succession infrastructure. This strengthens the Phase-4 conclusion that institutional inheritance is not title replacement.

This matters for simulation because the likely advice-giver depends on the domain, not the global status hierarchy.

# 35. Closing synthesis

Support Series 2 Part 026–050 substantially improves downstream character reconstruction because it supplies **procedural personality**.

The most useful question this tranche answers is not simply “who cares?” It is:

> **When this person cares, what does she actually do next?**

Saki prepares something, measures something, teaches something, or turns the problem into a training plan. Rinami invites, de-pressurizes, and restores information. Misuzu anticipates secondary consequences and changes the surrounding environment. Sumika lowers participation costs through pleasure and casualness. Kotone protects autonomy and solves resource constraints. Hiro makes difficulty into an experience people can share. Mao quietly absorbs responsibility until another person's help becomes genuinely useful. Sena models, recruits, and mobilizes resources, sometimes faster than consent can keep up. Tsubame teaches through severe precision. Temari corrects bluntly and may deny the sentiment while accepting the obligation. China receives care gratefully and increasingly learns to return it through concrete contribution. Lilja turns reassurance into another attempt. Ume expresses affection physically and immediately, sometimes before calibration catches up.

Those are not decorative traits. They are **action-selection rules**.

The tranche also reinforces that relationship modeling should track not only declared closeness but **access permissions**: who can tease whom, correct whom, touch whom in a care context, see whom frightened, choose clothes for whom, interrupt whose diet, ask whose training advice, or be invited into whose private ritual. These permissions often provide more predictive value than friendship labels.

Finally, the support corpus continues to reject a single hierarchy of competence. The best adviser changes by domain. This is crucial for Phase 6 and Phase 7: a strong character model must know not merely who is “better,” but **who each person would realistically seek out for a given kind of problem—and why that particular relationship makes the advice acceptable.**
