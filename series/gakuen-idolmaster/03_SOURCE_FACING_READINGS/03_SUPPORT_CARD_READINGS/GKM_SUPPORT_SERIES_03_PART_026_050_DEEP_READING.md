# GKM SUPPORT SERIES 03 PART 026–050 DEEP READING

## 0. Scope, method, and principal finding

This is the sixth canonical Phase-5 support-card reading for the Gakuen Idolmaster V2 corpus and the second reading of Support Series 3.

The source boundary is the complete `support_series_3_part_026-050.dialogue.txt` bundle: **75 unique support-card scripts / 1,489 messages**, organized as **25 three-part support stories, `story_0026` through `story_0050` inclusive**. Unlike the preceding Series-3 bundle, the filename and actual story interval agree exactly.

As throughout Phase 5, support-story numbering is not converted into a global chronology. Three parts grouped under a story ID are related evidence surfaces, not automatically consecutive moments. Stronger placement is used only when school year, season, institutional role, remembered history, explicit relationship state, or another internal anchor supports it.

The A2 dialogue extraction is the reading surface. A1 raw-script paths remain wording authority. The text does not license claims about uninspected card art, facial animation, camera emphasis, costume rendering, or vocal nuance. Performance-sensitive distinctions are routed to the audiovisual queue.

### Principal tranche thesis — continuity without stasis

The first half of Series 3 established that care can become **social infrastructure**: needs circulate through friends, siblings, dorm roles, student council, teachers, trainers, and school systems rather than being solved through one signature dyad. The present tranche asks what makes those systems durable.

> **Gakumas relationships and institutions persist not by returning to an earlier form, but by carrying forward selected functions while changing the terms under which those functions operate.**

This is continuity without stasis.

The tranche repeats this structure at multiple scales:

- Sumika and Lilja carry a childhood bond from Sweden/Hokkaido-linked memories into Japanese school life, but their relation changes from Sumika giving courage to reciprocal domestic and professional support.
- Temari and Misuzu reconcile without restoring SyngUp. Misuzu explicitly abandons the old aspiration to become Temari's “wings,” while retaining a commitment to help Temari shine by a different route.
- Saki and Ume preserve the sister/rivalry system while Ume develops friendships and professional worlds that no longer orbit Saki alone.
- Ume, China, and Hiro move from repeatedly asking whether they are friends to treating friendship as a stable fact that can survive busier careers and future physical distance.
- Rinami passes to China a festival viewing place once given to her by an older student-council member, turning remembered kindness into a future obligation of care.
- Mao teaches Lilja a dorm cooking tradition passed down by previous residents; the dorm becomes an institution with inherited memory rather than merely housing.
- `Campus mode!!` is explicitly understood as a school-wide song whose technique, emotional weight, and opportunity structure pass from upperclassmen to juniors. Mao and Rinami describe themselves as receiving a baton from earlier seniors and becoming responsible for handing it forward.
- Sena's producer identity develops through recognition that talent perception alone does not prepare her for the personalities she recruits. Her institutional role survives by becoming more complex, not by abandoning the original ambition.

The modeling consequence is substantial:

> **A plausible character model needs transition rules for what a person preserves when a relationship changes.**

The question is not simply “are Temari and Misuzu friends again?” It is: which old care functions return, which are rejected, which are rerouted, and which remain unresolved? The question is not simply “are Ume, China, and Hiro still close?” It is: what happens when work reduces spontaneous time together, and what beliefs about friendship make the bond durable anyway?

### Secondary thesis — social inheritance is an active practice

This tranche also makes **inheritance** more concrete than a broad theme. Gakumas repeatedly presents traditions as things someone must actively reproduce:

- Rinami chooses to reveal a private festival spot to China and explicitly asks her to reveal it to a future junior.
- Mao explains that dorm seniors have passed down the technique for bonfire sweet potatoes.
- Rinami teaches `Campus mode!!` because a senior once taught her.
- Mao and Rinami practice critique because juniors will eventually require instruction from them.
- Sena and Tsubame treat the song as an expression of the quality of their generation, not just an available piece of repertoire.
- Ume's gifts to Saki and Rinami's personalized Valentine's chocolates require learning another person's tastes rather than reproducing a generic ritual.

> **Tradition is not static preservation. It is remembered care converted into a responsibility toward the next recipient.**

This gives Phase 6 and Phase 8 a strong bridge: relationship analysis and institutional analysis should not be separated too aggressively when the institution survives precisely through relationship-mediated transmission.

# 2. `story_0026` — Sumika and Lilja: reward, play, and a childhood persistence memory

Sumika's lunch behavior is already highly predictive. She buys a limited cream sandwich, immediately admires Lilja's richer homemade lunch, accepts a shared bite, and then tries to turn an inconvenient class obligation into something tolerable through anticipated pleasure. Lilja knows exactly what works: mentioning homemade cookies restores Sumika's motivation to clean the pool.

Part 2 converts the same structure into play. Sumika teases Lilja about a new swimsuit, initiates a water fight, and then proposes a race to finish cleaning, with juice for the winner. Lilja accepts specifically because the game makes Sumika work seriously.

The important point is not merely that Sumika likes rewards. **Lilja has learned how to use Sumika's pleasure-oriented motivational grammar constructively.** This is the reverse side of Sumika using crepes, karaoke, and invitations to regulate other people's mood. Their relationship contains mutual behavioral calibration.

Part 3 gives a historically important childhood memory: Sumika once taught Lilja to swim. Lilja was unable to do it even after an entire day, but cried and insisted on continuing until she succeeded. Sumika remembers the moment as evidence of Lilja's long-standing seriousness and persistence, and explicitly says that she loves that aspect of Lilja and will continue supporting her from beside her.

### Claim transitions

- **Lilja — STRENGTHEN:** persistence to the point of tears predates Hatsuboshi and therefore should not be treated as a Producer-created work ethic.
- **Sumika ↔ Lilja — STRENGTHEN:** the pair has reciprocal motivational knowledge; Sumika once supplied courage, while Lilja now knows how to make Sumika follow through on mundane obligations.
- **Sumika — STRENGTHEN:** her praise of Lilja is specific and historical rather than generic affection.
- **Phase-7 continuity:** present domestic friendship is rooted in a much older pattern of Sumika witnessing Lilja attempt difficult things and refusing to withdraw support.

### Modeling rule

When Lilja hesitates, Sumika plausibly encourages through certainty and action. When Sumika drifts, Lilja plausibly redirects through concrete shared pleasure rather than moralistic criticism.

# 3. `story_0027` — Ume, China, and Hiro: vacation becomes proof of a new category of life

China's family property is effectively an idol-training resort: equipment, a large lesson room, outdoor pool, and carefully balanced meals. Ume treats the facilities as exciting; Hiro treats the training itself as the attractive part of the holiday. China wants a recognizably “summer” vacation and deliberately combines recreation with their idol obligations.

The second part shows how much better China has become at handling her friends. When Ume hesitates about the trip because of idol work, China reveals that the property has a new training machine not yet available at school. Ume accepts instantly. Hiro remarks that China is becoming skilled at handling Ume.

This is a meaningful social-development marker. China's hospitality is no longer only indiscriminate enthusiasm. She is learning **recipient-specific invitation design**.

Part 3 is more important. All three describe the stay as unprecedented. China has never spent summer vacation with friends. Hiro has never spent several consecutive days with friends. Ume has attended sports camps and trained with Saki, but says this “today” belongs to a different experiential category. They want to repeat it, and Ume proposes eventually bringing them to the Hanami family home.

### Claim transitions

- **Ume ↔ China ↔ Hiro — PROMOTE/STRENGTHEN:** this is not merely a school-based friendship cluster; they deliberately create private multiday life together.
- **China — STRENGTHEN:** hospitality becomes strategic and individualized without losing sincerity.
- **Hiro — STRENGTHEN:** “training” and “vacation” are not opposites for her; desired difficulty can itself make an experience pleasurable.
- **Ume — STRENGTHEN:** friendship with peers occupies a category distinct from sibling training or sports-team group life.

### Socioeconomic caution

China's wealth materially expands what the trio can do. That does not make the friendship transactional. The correct model tracks **resource asymmetry plus mutual emotional contribution**, not one or the other.

# 4. `story_0028` — Temari and Misuzu: practical truce reveals preserved operating knowledge

Temari and Misuzu remain formally estranged. Misuzu asks whether they can reconcile during a joint lesson. Temari refuses. Misuzu then reframes the request as a temporary ceasefire: they remain enemies, may continue disliking each other, but cooperate for a bounded purpose. Temari recognizes that this is functionally similar and is irritated by being talked into it.

The scene matters because the pair's old interaction grammar returns before the relationship label does. Both explicitly call the verbal exchange nostalgic.

Part 2 moves straight into bodily familiarity. Misuzu asks whether Temari can prepare in the mornings by herself and then notices weight gain. Her phrasing is possessive and invasive—she cannot accept Temari gaining weight “where she does not know about it”—but the practical proposal is measured swimming and food adjustment rather than punishment. Temari complains yet lets Misuzu take the problem over.

Part 3 proves the intervention worked. Temari thanks Misuzu for diet lunches; Misuzu celebrates by proposing tonkatsu and curry, immediately reintroducing the same pleasure/risk cycle that has always marked her care.

### Claim transitions

- **Temari ↔ Misuzu — STRENGTHEN:** practical care can resume under a formally adversarial label because embodied knowledge of the other remains available.
- **Misuzu — STRENGTHEN:** care remains anticipatory and highly recipient-specific, but can still blur into possessive jurisdiction over Temari's body.
- **Temari — STRENGTHEN:** she may reject relational access verbally while accepting concrete care whose competence she trusts.

### Phase-6 caution

Do not romanticize the possessive body-management language into proof of healthy care. The relationship's strength and its boundary problems are both part of the evidence.

# 5. `story_0029` — Saki, Temari, and Kotone: friendship as performance, joke, and real access

Saki is frustrated because Ume is staying with friends and boasting about a good time. Kotone proposes retaliating by making their own summer memory at the river. Temari joins while insisting that she has little reason to be there.

The water fight is revealingly ordinary. Saki treats it competitively; Kotone attacks before Saki finishes a speech; Temari and Kotone coordinate an ambush; Saki complains that the pair are unfair while having explicitly invited a two-on-one fight. This is one of the clearest low-stakes examples of the three first-years functioning as a socially fluent group without needing professional stakes.

Part 3 turns the friendship label into comedy. Saki exaggerates the closeness while talking to Ume, calling the others close friends and Kotone her best friend. Temari denies the label. Kotone is surprised, then visibly affected when Saki simply answers that she loves her. Kotone tries to perform exaggerated sleepover intimacy for Ume's benefit; the joke misfires and Ume interprets it as sexualized impropriety, to Saki's horror.

### Claim transitions

- **Saki ↔ Temari ↔ Kotone — STRENGTHEN:** the trio supports coordinated play, teasing, embarrassment, and casual time together beyond training.
- **Saki — STRENGTHEN:** Ume's increasing peer independence can trigger sibling jealousy that Saki answers by emphasizing her own friendships.
- **Kotone — STRENGTHEN:** she can accept sincere affection while immediately parodying overperformed intimacy.
- **Temari — STRENGTHEN:** friendship-label denial coexists with active participation and teamwork.

### Modeling rule

The trio's banter should not be written as uniformly warm. Much of its warmth is carried through contradiction, mock refusal, tactical alliance, and arguments that would read harshly outside established access.

# 6. `story_0030` — Rinami and China: a student-council kindness becomes an inherited ritual

China is assigned her first festival patrol with Rinami and is overwhelmed by ordinary festival culture: baby castella, glowing bracelets, spiral food, shaved ice. Rinami correctly infers that China has never attended a festival and decides that they can enjoy it while completing the patrol.

This is Rinami's consent-sensitive care in institutional form. She does not abandon the duty. She changes the way the duty is inhabited so the junior can have a first experience without becoming irresponsible.

Part 2 gives the motive. Rinami remembers being intimidated when she first entered student council because everyone seemed glamorous and confident. Older members made the work enjoyable for her, so she wants China to experience the same thing. This is not generic maternal temperament; it is **remembered receipt converted into deliberate reproduction**.

Part 3 makes the inheritance explicit. Rinami takes China to a quiet place to watch the fireworks. A graduated senior once showed Rinami the same location when Rinami became separated and anxious during her own first external volunteer job. Rinami has kept the place private, but now asks China to show it to a future junior.

### Claim transitions

- **Rinami → China — PROMOTE/STRENGTHEN:** senior care is explicitly intergenerational; Rinami reproduces care she once received.
- **China — STRENGTHEN:** her sheltered background includes absence of common festival experiences, not merely lack of technical/social competence.
- **Student council — PROMOTE:** institutional culture includes informal memory transmission and protected rituals that are not written rules.

### Phase-8 formulation

> Hatsuboshi's institutions reproduce themselves partly through remembered interpersonal kindness, not only through formal offices and procedures.

# 7. `story_0031` — Lilja and Temari: teaching competence and a shared theory of fleeting brilliance

Lilja impulsively buys handheld fireworks because they are a Japanese summer custom she wants to try. Sumika is unavailable, so Lilja asks Temari. Temari is surprised but keeps the promise and shows up.

The second part is a useful role reversal. Lilja does not know fireworks procedure; Temari does. Temari takes the bag, teaches safety, plays with two fireworks at once, and invites Lilja to imitate her. This is a soft, playful Temari register that does not require Misuzu, Producer, Saki, or Kotone to activate.

Part 3 moves from ordinary pleasure to a shared theory of performance. Lilja prefers the sparklers and wonders whether beauty is partly intensified because the light ends. Temari accepts the idea but dislikes imagining idol life as similarly short. Lilja answers that their task is unchanged: run so that the instant lasts as long as possible. Temari converts that immediately into rivalry—she will not lose.

### Claim transitions

- **Temari ↔ Lilja — PROMOTE:** a viable casual friendship/peer relation exists outside the more obvious first-year cluster.
- **Temari — STRENGTHEN:** she can teach patiently, play visibly, and reciprocate philosophical reflection when the context does not threaten professional status.
- **Lilja — STRENGTHEN:** she can articulate transience without fatalism; awareness of endings intensifies commitment rather than reducing it.

### Theme

The scene distinguishes **ephemerality** from **futility**. A finite career can still justify disciplined continuation.

# 8. `story_0032` — Sena learns that talent scouting does not equal producer readiness

Sena complains to Rinami that the idols she recruited are all problem children. She insists that Ume and China are good, straightforward children, then labels Misuzu the bad one because Misuzu's calm exterior concealed profound resistance to normal training expectations. Sena explicitly admits the failure: on the day she scouted them, she looked almost entirely at ability and talent.

This is one of the most useful ordinary-life checks on Sena's producer identity. Her eye for talent remains excellent. **Her first-pass human model is incomplete.**

Part 2 puts Sena against Ume's physical baseline. Sena insists on running Ume's usual pace and is internally exhausted almost immediately, but refuses to show weakness because she wants to remain a reliable senior. Ume then asks for a dance demonstration, extending the demand beyond endurance. Saki silently recognizes the cost; Sena admits internally that being Ume's dependable senior is physically difficult.

Part 3 adds direct institutional pressure around China. The principal relays China's grandfather's request that China become the summer H.I.F Prima Stella, effectively beating Sena. Sena calls the request unreasonable but assures China that she has a plan, while explicitly asking China to defeat her someday.

### Claim transitions

- **Sena — REVISE/STRENGTHEN:** talent recognition is extraordinary but not equivalent to complete person-reading; her producer maturation includes learning the personalities attached to talent.
- **Sena ↔ Ume — STRENGTHEN:** Sena is willing to absorb physical strain to maintain a reliable-senior role, even when Ume's body exceeds her own endurance baseline.
- **Sena ↔ China — STRENGTHEN:** mentorship contains both protective confidence and a genuine desire for China to surpass her.
- **Institution — PROMOTE:** external family expectations can be transmitted through Hatsuboshi leadership into production goals; this pressure should remain source-bounded rather than generalized.

# 9. `story_0033` — Temari and Misuzu after reconciliation: repair does not restore SyngUp

Part 1 is a critical Phase-6 relationship checkpoint. Temari and Misuzu are living together again and watching the moon under the same blanket. Misuzu says she is glad they reconciled. Temari questions whether the word is fully accurate because they still disagree.

Misuzu supplies the decisive revision: they have reconciled, but **not everything has returned to how it was**. She will not ask Temari to sing together again. She no longer intends to become Temari's “wings.” Instead, she intends to help Temari shine by her own method.

This is continuity without restoration in explicit dialogue.

Part 2 revisits Temari's first live after the rupture. Temari invited Misuzu believing she could communicate everything through song. Misuzu was furious precisely because the performance looked like Temari destroying herself. The fight became the first time they fully shouted their feelings at each other. Both conclude that genuine conflict was necessary for reconciliation.

Then Misuzu notes there is still one more person with whom they need a real reconciliation. Temari delays: “someday.” In corpus context this is a high-priority Rinha route, but the support story does not name her directly; cross-routing should preserve that evidentiary distinction.

Part 3 shows the repaired everyday system. Temari calls for Misuzu when weight rises; Misuzu reassures, helps reduce it, then celebrates with food that causes the cycle to start again. Saki and Kotone observe Temari's improved body management without seeing the dependence underneath it.

### Claim transitions

- **Temari ↔ Misuzu — MAJOR REVISE:** repair is a negotiated new relationship, not restoration of SyngUp's old co-dependent ideal.
- **Misuzu — REVISE:** “support” after repair is intentionally separated from becoming Temari's wing or sharing her performance path.
- **Temari — STRENGTHEN:** she can accept recurring help while publicly framing the result as ordinary self-management.
- **Rinha dossier — OPEN/STRENGTHEN ROUTING:** one unresolved reconciliation remains salient; identification requires cross-evidence rather than this scene alone.

# 10. `story_0034` — Mao and Rinami: ordinary intimacy through laundry, tea, and craft

The first part is almost entirely mundane and therefore highly valuable for behavioral reconstruction. Mao and Rinami do laundry together, discuss sleep, room fragrance, and recent purchases, and compliment one another's qualities after the other has walked away. Rinami likes Mao's tendency to look after dorm residents; Mao likes Rinami's ability to state another person's good qualities directly.

Part 2 shows Rinami decompressing after student-council work. She talks at length about the eccentric younger members and the logistical difficulty of school events alongside idol schedules, then apologizes for monopolizing the conversation. Mao tells her that seeing Rinami enjoy herself is enjoyable for him and asks her to continue.

Part 3 turns shared waiting time into schoolwork. Mao is making an eco-bag with a cat embroidery. Rinami finds it cute, asks to match it, and Mao offers to teach her.

### Claim transitions

- **Mao ↔ Rinami — STRENGTHEN:** the relation supports domestic silence, long-form casual talk, mutual admiration, and learning small crafts together.
- **Mao — STRENGTHEN:** cute craft choices coexist comfortably with prince authorship; they are not evidence against it.
- **Rinami — STRENGTHEN:** she can move from institutional caregiver into excited talkative peer when she feels safe.

### Phase-6 note

This kind of low-drama mutual liking should be preserved when later analysis considers romantic coding. The relationship's importance does not depend on proving romance; its ordinary intimacy is already textually substantial.

# 11. `story_0035` — Saki and Ume: rivalry as a copied life system

Saki is studying nutrition because she increasingly cooks for people beyond Ume. This is a small but important sign of care circulation: knowledge developed inside the Hanami household now serves a broader network.

Ume recommends a romance comedy she enjoys, including mildly provocative material, and wants Saki to read it so they can talk more. Saki agrees. The exchange is useful because sibling connection includes media sharing, not only competition and body management.

Part 2 shows a rest day governed by Saki's normal discipline. Ume arrives to spend the day together and is immediately pulled into studying. Ume admits she dislikes study but understands that poor grades would interfere with idol activity. Saki then asks what Ume wants after defeating her. Ume answers that she will think about it after she wins.

Part 3 explains why Ume's life resembles Saki's so strongly. She explicitly copies Saki's “best possible day”—study, sleep, stretching, performance review—because Saki is both model and opponent. Saki accepts that this makes Ume a formidable rival.

### Claim transitions

- **Saki — STRENGTHEN:** accumulated expertise originally developed around Ume is becoming generalizable care competence.
- **Ume — STRENGTHEN:** imitation of Saki is conscious strategy, not lack of individuality.
- **Ume — OPEN:** the self beyond the goal of defeating Saki remains genuinely underdeveloped in this scene.
- **Saki ↔ Ume — STRENGTHEN:** rivalry is not merely a contest outcome; it organizes routines, education, recovery, media sharing, and future imagination.

# 12. `story_0036` — Temari and Sumika: learning to enjoy what cannot be optimized

Temari sees culture-festival preparation as pointless compared with training. Sumika enjoys the non-routine collective atmosphere and tells Temari that if the work is mandatory, they may as well enjoy it.

The second part becomes unusually explicit. Sumika wants Temari to enjoy the festival, while Temari says she does not know how: she has cut away things unnecessary for becoming a top idol. Sumika does not challenge the ambition. She says she likes that part of Temari and offers to teach the enjoyment side herself.

This is an excellent example of a relationship that **adds a missing behavioral option without demanding identity conversion**.

Part 3 gives the conceptual bridge. Temari dismisses decorations because they will soon be removed. Sumika points out that idol stages are also temporary. Temari accepts the analogy, sees her own softened expression in a photo, and thanks Sumika.

### Claim transitions

- **Temari — REVISE/STRENGTHEN:** non-instrumental enjoyment is an underlearned skill, not evidence that she is incapable of pleasure.
- **Sumika → Temari — PROMOTE:** Sumika can teach social enjoyment without framing Temari's professionalism as pathology.
- **Sumika — STRENGTHEN:** her “have fun” ethic is philosophically compatible with impermanence; pleasure need not be permanent to be worthwhile.

### Modeling rule

Sumika is especially plausible as a bridge when a high-discipline character does not know how to enter a low-stakes social activity. She lowers the interpretive cost of participation rather than making an abstract argument against ambition.

# 13. `story_0037` — China and Hiro: novelty, incompetence, and the promise of continued friendship

China enthusiastically lists autumn foods and discovers that Hiro has barely experienced many seasonal dishes. She immediately invites Hiro to her home for matsutake rice. Food again becomes a route by which China expands another person's ordinary-life experience.

Part 2 turns an errand into slapstick. China and Hiro struggle with bags of supplies; Hiro treats the weight as training; China's bag has a hole and the tape rolls away downhill. Hiro genuinely enjoys the chase. The pair's weakness does not create humiliation because they can narrate failure as shared amusement.

Part 3 moves into dorm imagination. Hiro describes domestic failures such as making the floor foam during laundry, enjoying the gap between theoretical understanding and practical failure. China imagines the catastrophe if the two were roommates. China then says Hatsuboshi has made every day feel new and wishes the time could last forever. Hiro says she feels the same and promises that they will keep making memories. China explicitly says they will remain friends forever.

### Claim transitions

- **China ↔ Hiro — STRENGTHEN:** shared incompetence is a friendship resource rather than merely a deficit.
- **China — STRENGTHEN:** food invitation is a habitual form of cultural inclusion.
- **Hiro — STRENGTHEN:** practical failure can be pleasurable precisely because theory does not automatically produce embodied competence.

### Continuity theme

The wish that school life “last forever” is not literal stasis. The promise becomes to keep creating memories, which anticipates `story_0050`'s more mature account of friendship surviving change.

# 14. `story_0038` — Sena and Rinami: role-model performance and a safe place to loosen it

Sena is nervous before addressing new students. The vice president mocks the claim because Sena performs under pressure constantly. Sena distinguishes the two situations: as Prima Stella and student-council president, she must appear as the incoming students' target and ideal. The pressure is not stage fright but **representational responsibility**.

After the speech, Sena scans the new cohort and claims she can already see who is developing. She tells Rinami that Rinami herself is one of Sena's rivals. Rinami is startled; Sena privately believes Rinami possesses latent potential that has not yet found its catalyst.

Part 3 provides the relational counterweight. Rinami notices Sena's fatigue and lack of sleep, offers tea, and is one of the few people before whom Sena admits that she can relax. Sena rejects the offered shoulder massage because she does not want to indulge too far, but the admission remains.

### Claim transitions

- **Sena — STRENGTHEN:** public confidence includes consciously maintained representational labor; nervousness and mastery are compatible.
- **Sena — STRENGTHEN:** talent perception extends to latent potential, not only current ranking.
- **Sena ↔ Rinami — PROMOTE/STRENGTHEN:** Rinami is a low-pressure relational space in which Sena can acknowledge fatigue without losing role legitimacy.
- **Rinami — STRENGTHEN:** her care often begins with noticing load before being asked.

# 27. Cross-tranche behavioral-model deltas

## 27.1 What changes from Series 3 Part 001–025

The previous tranche established **network position** as a first-class modeling variable. This tranche adds **network persistence rules**.

A character model should now track at least four relationship-state dimensions:

1. **Function retained:** what the relationship still does after change.
2. **Function rejected:** what the characters explicitly refuse to restore.
3. **Function rerouted:** what old care behavior returns in a different form or through a different institution.
4. **Future durability belief:** what each person thinks distance, success, graduation, or conflict will do to the bond.

Examples:

- Temari/Misuzu retain body knowledge and emotional witnesshood, reject the old shared-wing/SyngUp arrangement, reroute support into separate paths and domestic assistance, and still have unresolved reconciliation work around a third person.
- Ume/China/Hiro retain enjoyment and mutual aid, accept increasing work demands, and explicitly believe liking each other can survive reduced contact.
- Mao/Rinami retain mutual encouragement while converting senior status into responsibility to pass knowledge forward.
- Sumika/Lilja retain an old courage relationship while becoming reciprocal peers in study, domestic labor, and idol aspiration.

## 27.2 Care is increasingly recipient-modeled

This tranche repeatedly shows that mature care requires a model of the recipient:

- Rinami asks what Saki likes before designing Valentine's food.
- China learns what will persuade Ume to combine vacation and training.
- Lilja knows cookies can make Sumika finish a boring obligation.
- Sumika knows competition or play can make Saki/Temari enter unfamiliar leisure.
- Rinami knows China needs permission to enjoy a festival while doing council work.
- Lilja knows Temari needs extremely gradual bodily assurance on ice.

Miscalibration therefore becomes more diagnosable. Sena and Saki often care intensely, but when they use the wrong baseline, the error is no longer “too much caring”; it is **incorrect recipient modeling**.

## 27.3 Strong roles require safe off-ramps

Sena, Temari, Saki, and Mao all carry strong public/self-authored roles. Support scenes reveal when those roles can loosen:

- Sena admits fatigue to Rinami.
- Temari allows Lilja to hold her hand because she is frightened.
- Saki can play arcade games when the activity preserves challenge.
- Mao can show cute craft preferences around Rinami without destabilizing princehood.

A simulation should therefore not treat persona consistency as emotional flatness. The relevant question is **who has access to the off-ramp and under what circumstances**.

# 29. Institutional and thematic promotions

## 29.1 `Campus mode!!` as institutional infrastructure

Across `story_0044`–`0046`, the song has four simultaneously supported functions:

1. **school identity** — it is repeatedly called Hatsuboshi itself;
2. **generational comparison** — current performers are evaluated against predecessors;
3. **scarce opportunity** — even Mao can practice for years without a chance to perform it;
4. **intergenerational duty** — learning becomes an obligation to teach the next cohort.

The song therefore belongs in both the thematic/institutional synthesis and the song/musical-identity layer.

## 29.2 Student council as memory-bearing institution

The council does not merely assign work. It accumulates practices and memories:

- festival patrol techniques;
- private viewing places;
- reciprocal small gifts;
- individualized Valentine's rituals;
- senior/junior teaching;
- a space where Sena's production project and ordinary civic work coexist.

Rinami's handoff to China is especially strong evidence that **institutional continuity is enacted through personal transmission**.

## 29.3 Idol success creates relational time scarcity

`story_0050` makes a mature point that should recur in full-series synthesis: professional success creates less unstructured time with friends. The text does not frame this as betrayal. China and Hiro explicitly integrate busyness into a positive model of development.

This helps avoid a common synthesis error in idol fiction: assuming that relational health requires preserving school-era frequency forever.
