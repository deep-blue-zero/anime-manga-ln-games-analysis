# Revision 86 support-card readings

## Scope and method

This reading covers all 42 new support-card A1 objects in revision 86, representing 16 story families and 841 extracted messages. It also records the orthographic change in `adv_csprt-3-0063_03.txt` and the two Saki startup clips. [The per-object delta](../../01_CORPUS_AUDIT_AND_SOURCE_LOCK/GKM_SOURCE_DELTA_20261112.tsv) pairs each original filename with its archive-relative raw path and SHA-256. The source-facing observations below come from the A1 messages, speaker fields, inner-speech flags and scene commands. Official card names were not recoverable from these script headers. Story identifiers locate the authored units.

Support scenes are C4 relative to individual Produce, Dear and Unit tracks. Internal scene progress is C1 where dialogue and setting establish the sequence. The first summer vacation after Hiro made friends, the summer festival shoot, the autumn Halloween event, and the post-cultural-festival/pre-winter-Selection interval are anchors inside their respective scenes. Cross-story synchronization requires separate evidence. There are no explicit player-choice commands in these 42 new support scripts. Memories, fantasies, self-reports, roleplay and conditionals are retained inside each account. The script lane supports words, attribution and staging cues.
