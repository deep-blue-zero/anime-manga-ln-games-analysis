# GKM SUPPORT SERIES 02 PART 001–025 DEEP READING

## 0. Scope, method, and principal finding

This is the second canonical Phase-5 support-card reading for the Gakuen Idolmaster V2 corpus, following `GKM_SUPPORT_SERIES_01_DEEP_READING.md`.

The architecture-defined input is `support_series_2_part_001-025.dialogue.txt`. A bundle audit reveals a small but important naming mismatch: the file contains **story_0000 through story_0025 inclusive**, not only 001–025. The true locked boundary is therefore **26 support stories / 52 unique scripts / 961 messages**, with two parts per story. This artifact retains the architecture-defined filename for stable routing but states the actual source range everywhere provenance matters.

As in Support Series 1, support-story IDs are not treated as chronology, and the two parts grouped under one story number are not automatically interpreted as adjacent scenes. Each part is modular `M-*` evidence unless an explicit memory, date, institutional state, or relational reference earns a stronger continuity classification.

The bundle's `derived_character_*` fields are blank. Focality is therefore inferred from scene centrality and should not be cited as official card ownership without a UI/card manifest.

The reading surface is A2 dialogue. A1 raw paths remain the governing source locators. No facial animation, card illustration, voice performance, camera work, costume detail, or visual composition is inferred beyond the text.

### Principal tranche thesis — intimacy is often behavioral before it is declarative

Support Series 1 showed each character's **default care language, avoidance strategy, and model of what another person needs**. The first Series-2 tranche shows what happens after those defaults begin colliding with people who know them well enough to answer back.

The dominant pattern is:

> **Gakumas relationships often become intimate in practice before the characters agree on the label.**

Closeness appears through tolerated intrusion, routine correction, shared food, knowledge of habits, accepted bodily care, teasing that assumes durability, and the right to see an embarrassing or unpolished self.

Examples recur across very different relationships:

- Saki tells Ume not to tackle-hug anyone else, then ultimately accepts the physical affection because it is *Ume's* affection and because Saki trusts herself to receive it.
- Temari complains about a middle-school friend who obstructed training, then reflexively defends that friend's good qualities when Kotone criticizes her; the relational attachment survives inside the grievance.
- Mao scolds Kotone about chores and food management while repeatedly discovering that Kotone is more responsible than her presentation suggests.
- Rinami and Mao exchange study methods and ideals of idol longevity without either relationship needing a dramatic confession.
- China and Hiro remember their first-day physical dependence as an ordinary friendship anecdote.
- Temari denies that Hiro is a friend while rescuing her from drowning, teaching her to swim, and accepting sincere thanks.
- Saki and Temari can push one another to injury-adjacent effort, administer first aid, insult each other's limits, and immediately return to rivalry because the relationship can carry correction without collapsing.
- Kotone treats Sena's pursuit as something to evade, yet she also observes and revises her image of Sena when she sees the council president with children and local vendors.
- Lilja's gratitude to Mao becomes an explicit wish to reproduce Mao's kind-senior behavior for future juniors.

This yields the second Phase-5 formulation:

> **Relationship depth is often better measured by what kinds of imposition, correction, embarrassment, dependence, and ordinary access the relationship can survive than by whether either person says “friend.”**

A second cross-tranche finding concerns **role permeability**. Public roles remain real, but ordinary life exposes how they are maintained:

- Sena's charisma coexists with childish competitive frustration and unusually transparent excitement about Kotone.
- Mao's prince behavior coexists with anxiety about a cute swimsuit, weak rote memorization, and dependence on Rinami's advice.
- Temari's professional severity coexists with katsudon temptation, diet anxiety, and being flustered by Kotone's casual closeness.
- China can be wealthy, sheltered, physically weak, and still possess concrete cultural knowledge such as formal yukata dressing.
- Sumika can be socially sophisticated enough to style China while remaining playfully irresponsible in schoolwork elsewhere.

The tranche therefore strongly supports the project's existing anti-mask thesis:

> **Ordinary contradiction usually expands a character's repertoire; it does not expose the public self as fake.**

# 2. `story_0000` — Saki and Ume: affection with impact force

**Sources:** `story_0000/part_01` — 17 messages; `part_02` — 12 messages  
**Participants:** Ume, Saki  
**Functions:** sibling physicality, safety calibration, ordinary affirmation

Ume's default physical affection is extreme enough to be hazardous. She tackles Saki from behind while calling it a hug. Saki objects not to the affection but to the delivery mechanism: a surprise impact capable of causing injury is not, in her terminology, a hug.

The first important distinction is recipient-specific safety. Saki explicitly tells Ume never to do this to other girls. Ume replies that she only does it to Saki. Saki initially accepts that exception, catches herself, and still protests—before finally telling Ume to come from the front so Saki can receive her full force.

The joke reveals a stable sibling bargain:

> **Saki does not demand that Ume become less Ume; she tries to convert Ume's intensity into a form she can safely receive.**

The second part is quieter. Ume chooses to eat lunch with Saki simply because she feels like having “older-sister time.” Saki checks on her lessons, hears that she is often scolded but enjoying herself, and concludes that this is a good sign. She then guarantees that Ume will become the best idol—after Saki herself.

Ume responds by patting Saki's head and saying she likes her. Saki's answer is casual and unembarrassed: `はいはい。わたしもよ。`

### Claim transitions

- **Saki — STRENGTHEN:** care does not always mean reducing intensity; she often seeks a safe channel for it.
- **Ume — STRENGTHEN:** physical affection is direct, exuberant, and recipient-specific rather than indiscriminate.
- **Saki ↔ Ume — STRENGTHEN:** explicit affection can be ordinary and low-drama inside the rivalry system.

### Behavioral modeling note

Saki's irritation and affection are simultaneous. A plausible reconstruction should not “resolve” a scene into either anger or tenderness. She can scold Ume loudly while choosing to receive the same underlying need.

# 3. `story_0001` — Temari, Misuzu, and the relational contradiction inside grievance

**Sources:** `story_0001/part_01` — 19; `part_02` — 20  
**Participants:** Temari, Kotone; Misuzu, China  
**Functions:** SyngUp retrospective, grievance, defensive attachment, future possibility

Part 1 begins with Kotone asking whether Temari always trained this obsessively. Temari says high school is easier because nobody interferes. She describes a middle-school friend who refused to train seriously and also obstructed Temari's training, insisting that moderation was best.

Kotone casually agrees that this sounds like an awful friend.

Temari immediately turns on **Kotone**.

Her defense is diagnostically useful. She says the friend lacked motivation *but* was attentive outside training, fun to be around, had many good qualities, and cooked well. Temari's grievance therefore cannot be modeled as emotional detachment. The very act of criticizing Misuzu activates a protective counterclaim when an outsider joins too enthusiastically.

Kotone's conclusion—`めんどくさぁ`—is socially accurate. Temari wants the right to complain without granting Kotone the right to flatten Misuzu into “bad friend.”

Part 2 provides Misuzu's side. China asks about unit activity. Misuzu admits she repeatedly stopped Temari from training even though she understood Temari was hardworking. She insists the intention was never malicious, but the relationship still ended in dissolution. China replies that both remain on the idol path and may cross again.

Misuzu accepts the possibility warmly.

### Claim transitions

- **Temari — STRENGTHEN:** complaint about Misuzu coexists with active defense of Misuzu's total personhood.
- **Misuzu — STRENGTHEN:** she understands the specific behavioral conflict that contributed to rupture and does not rewrite it as simple victimization.
- **Temari ↔ Misuzu — STRENGTHEN:** attachment remains active inside grievance; neither perspective reduces the other to the conflict.
- **China — STRENGTHEN:** hopeful mediation does not require historical intimacy; she naturally frames separation as potentially temporary without denying the rupture.

### Phase-6 implication

This is strong evidence against any model in which Temari's SyngUp anger proves that prior warmth was fake, or Misuzu's low-effort philosophy proves indifference. The pair's conflict is precisely painful because the relationship contains substantial care that cannot solve a genuine incompatibility by itself.

# 4. `story_0002` — Kotone and Mao: responsibility behind the evasive surface

**Sources:** 16 + 20 messages  
**Participants:** Kotone, Mao  
**Functions:** dorm labor, work schedule, nutrition, intergenerational correction

Mao catches Kotone hurrying to work and asks whether she has completed her dorm cleaning duty. Kotone flees with her usual verbal fog—jokes, pseudo-English, quick reassurance—before Mao can inspect.

Mao expects corner-cutting. Instead she finds the corridor genuinely spotless.

The scene is valuable because it documents a gap between **presentation and execution**. Kotone performs irresponsibility more readily than she actually behaves irresponsibly. She is rushed because she has another job, not because she ignored the shared task.

Part 2 repeats the broader conflict in a cafeteria context. Kotone is hungry after work followed by lessons. Mao argues that idols need disciplined calorie management. Kotone replies that health cannot be reduced to nutritional optimization; eating only “correct” food while accumulating stress can itself be unhealthy.

Mao concedes the point.

### Claim transitions

- **Kotone — STRENGTHEN:** evasive speech should not be used as a proxy for task unreliability.
- **Kotone — STRENGTHEN:** her health reasoning integrates economic reality, fatigue, pleasure, and sustainability rather than rejecting professionalism.
- **Mao — STRENGTHEN:** rule enforcement is principled but corrigible; she can update when a junior supplies a better argument.
- **Mao ↔ Kotone — STRENGTHEN:** dorm-senior supervision increasingly operates as negotiated care rather than one-way discipline.

### Socioeconomic note

Kotone's work schedule is not abstract background. It changes meal timing, energy availability, and how she experiences “good health” advice. Behavioral reconstruction should preserve that material constraint.

# 5. `story_0003` — Mao and Rinami: studying through identity, imagining a long career

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** Mao, Rinami  
**Functions:** academics, learning style, routines, career ideal

Mao struggles with rote Japanese-history memorization. Rinami notices that the weakness is not lack of intelligence but lack of emotional entry. She suggests approaching historical periods through manga, films, and drama—finding human stakes first, then using interest to support factual recall.

Mao immediately connects the idea to her own experience of becoming interested in French history through theater and its source manga.

This is a small but important cognition marker:

> **Mao learns more naturally through embodied narrative and role than through decontextualized memorization.**

Part 2 extends the pattern. Rinami describes a study routine built from lesson discipline: she stretches before studying so that her body associates the session with the same concentrated state as idol training. Mao recognizes the logic as a routine and adopts it.

The two scenes are useful precisely because they are not idol crises. They show Rinami giving practical advice without maternal overreach and Mao accepting help without status anxiety.

### Claim transitions

- **Mao — STRENGTHEN:** role/narrative imagination is not only stagecraft; it is a general cognitive access route.
- **Rinami — STRENGTHEN:** she translates one domain's successful regulation into another domain rather than merely comforting.
- **Mao ↔ Rinami — STRENGTHEN:** peer intimacy includes ordinary competence-sharing and reciprocal respect.

# 6. `story_0004` — China and Rinami: admiration that asks for technique

**Sources:** 20 + 20  
**Participants:** China, Rinami, visual trainer  
**Functions:** dance tutoring, concentration, sister-role language

China asks Rinami for extra help because she fears disappointing Sena. Rinami initially tells her not to carry so much pressure but still joins the review because Rinami herself wants practice.

Rinami slows the movement down until China can execute it. The important pedagogical behavior is **pace reduction without humiliation**. China's response is exuberant, but the success is real.

A later visual lesson produces a “sisters” framing: energetic younger sister and dependable older sister. China objects—not because she rejects Rinami, but because she sees herself as an “eternal little sister” who has always been served by maids and wants to learn how to be dependable.

The socioeconomic element matters. China's desire for competence is not only idol aspiration. It includes wanting skills that ordinary peers may acquire through family and daily responsibility.

### Claim transitions

- **China — STRENGTHEN:** admiration frequently converts directly into requests for transferable technique.
- **Rinami — STRENGTHEN:** effective tutoring begins by reducing tempo and creating a success state, not by demanding confidence first.
- **China — STRENGTHEN:** wealth can create a perceived deficit in ordinary self-sufficiency rather than simply privilege.

### Register note

China's `お姉さま` / `お師匠さま` escalation is sincere and comic at once. Rinami's embarrassment does not require rejecting the bond; she specifically asks for a less grandiose label.

# 7. `story_0005` — Lilja and Sumika: intimacy through low-stakes sabotage and repair

**Sources:** 15 + 19  
**Participants:** Lilja, Sumika  
**Functions:** physical teasing, studying, Japanese orthography, reciprocal support

Lilja repeatedly tries to surprise Sumika by poking her cheek. The attempt fails because Sumika notices her. This is useful low-stakes evidence that Lilja can initiate playful physical teasing; timidity does not mean she is permanently passive in familiar relationships.

The pair then agree to study together if Lilja makes snacks. Lilja accepts because she enjoys baking.

Part 2 centers on kanji difficulty. Sumika does not romanticize it: Japanese people make mistakes too. She reminds Lilja that slow cumulative work is precisely something Lilja is good at, offers help, and then discovers the target kanji is difficult for her as well.

The humor equalizes them without erasing Lilja's second-language challenge.

### Claim transitions

- **Lilja — STRENGTHEN:** familiar intimacy permits mischievous initiative that would be unlikely with a distant peer.
- **Lilja — STRENGTHEN:** baking is ordinary pleasure and relational labor, not only a special-event behavior.
- **Sumika — STRENGTHEN:** reassurance works by accurately naming the recipient's existing strength (`コツコツやるの、リーリヤ得意`) rather than generic praise.
- **Lilja language model — REVISE/STRENGTHEN:** kanji remains a genuine task-specific difficulty; it does not imply broad conversational incompetence.

# 8. `story_0006` — Saki and Sumika: unsolicited optimization meets social refusal

**Participants:** Saki, Sumika

A realistic Saki conversation can drift into technical advice or Ume-talk from very small triggers. A realistic Sumika often recognizes the drift before Saki does.

# 11. `story_0009` — Ume and Temari: bodily confidence meets diet anxiety

**Sources:** 19 + 19  
**Participants:** Ume, Temari  
**Functions:** nutrition, body image, running, competitiveness

Ume offers Temari one of the Hanami training drinks and cheerfully advertises its effects, including weight loss for people who are overweight. Temari instantly becomes interested, then insists repeatedly that she herself is thin and only wants the information hypothetically.

The comedy depends on transparent anxiety. Temari is not written as oblivious to food/body management; she is highly attentive to it and embarrassed by that attentiveness.

Temari's repeated `仕方ない` while voluntarily drinking the product is a classic self-exculpating register move: desire/interest is displaced onto circumstance.

# 13. `story_0011` — Kotone and Sena: recruitment collides with debt ethics

**Sources:** 15 + 16  
**Participants:** Kotone, Sena  
**Functions:** scouting, repeated pursuit, money, consent, socioeconomic boundary

Sena repeatedly approaches Kotone before or after class, requests cute poses, and continues soliciting her despite Kotone's obvious exhaustion with the attention. Kotone treats the encounters as something to survive tactically rather than as flattering recognition.

- **Sena ↔ Kotone — STRENGTHEN:** strong talent recognition does not generate consent.

### Continuity note

# 15. `story_0013` — Rinami and Mao: two models of surviving the idol career

**Sources:** 19 + 20  
**Participants:** Rinami, Mao  
**Functions:** third-year spring, graduation, finitude, career philosophy

This is one of the strongest continuity-bearing support stories in the tranche. Rinami observes cherry blossoms and realizes that, as third-years, they will see this school spring only once more. Mao initially counters the sentimentality: they did not enter Hatsuboshi to enjoy school life; they entered to become idols, and only one year remains to achieve results.

Rinami accepts the urgency.

Part 2 then reveals a deeper philosophical difference. Mao compares cherry blossoms' brief peak to idols who bloom beautifully and then disappear. Rinami says that is not her ideal. She wants to remain on stage as long as possible and grow together with fans. A cherry tree remains itself after blossom season: green leaves, autumn colors, winter snow can each hold beauty.

Mao calls the ideal very Rinami and genuinely admirable.

### Claim transitions

- **Rinami — STRENGTHEN:** continuity is not only a relational method; it is an explicit career ideal. She values long-duration co-development with audiences.
- **Mao — STRENGTHEN:** finitude intensifies her urgency, but she can recognize a different authentic model of idol life.
- **Mao ↔ Rinami — STRENGTHEN:** disagreement over artistic/career ontology does not threaten intimacy.

### Continuity promotion

**C1 local anchor:** third-year spring, approximately one school year remaining until graduation.

### Thematic promotion

Rinami's seasonal metaphor provides unusually strong evidence for **identity continuity across changing forms**: remaining the same person/work does not require remaining in the same peak-performance phase.

# 16. `story_0014` — Asari and the Producer: the Producer Course is socially strange on purpose

**Sources:** 16 + 18  
**Participants:** Asari Neo, Producer  
**Functions:** Producer Course social ecology, age/status asymmetry, Asari pedagogy

# 28. Cross-story synthesis

## 28.1 Intimacy before acknowledgment

This tranche repeatedly demonstrates that declared relationship labels lag behind enacted relational permissions.

### Temari and Kotone

Kotone inserts herself into Temari's listening session, takes the next seat, proposes food-sharing, and physically/socially normalizes joint activity. Temari protests each step while continuing the interaction.

The correct model is not “Temari secretly wants everything Kotone does.” It is:

> **Temari experiences uncontrolled closeness as embarrassing while nevertheless granting Kotone unusually broad access.**

### Temari and Hiro

Temari denies friendship while repeatedly performing the duties Hiro uses to define friendship: rescue, warning, teaching, accepting gratitude. Hiro's definition is behavioral; Temari's is label-sensitive.

### Saki and Temari

The pair can train hard enough to expose injury, then remain inside the same rival grammar during first aid. The ability to stay blunt after vulnerability is itself evidence of relational durability.

### Mao and Rinami

Their intimacy appears through ordinary memory, studying, food logistics, protective reversal, and philosophical disagreement. Neither requires dramatic relational confirmation because access is already normalized.

### Sena and Kotone

This pair shows the opposite case: repeated access attempts **do not equal** intimacy. Sena's confidence and resources cannot substitute for Kotone's consent. Kotone's positive reappraisal at the festival does not erase the boundary.

This contrast is important for Phase 6. Relational depth must be inferred from **reciprocal permission and response**, not merely interaction frequency.

## 28.2 Care is recipient-model dependent

The first Series-2 tranche strengthens a central Gakumas proposition:

> **Good intentions are only as useful as the model of the recipient they operate through.**

- Saki's optimization helps Ume because Ume's body and trust fit the system; the same impulse can overwhelm China or annoy Sumika.
- Rinami helps Mao study by recognizing Mao's narrative cognition instead of repeating generic study advice.
- Sumika helps Lilja by naming a real Lilja strength: incremental work.
- Misuzu supports Hiro by accepting that Hiro genuinely enjoys hard tasks while still limiting unsafe excess.
- Sena's offer of money is generous inside Sena's problem model and unacceptable inside Kotone's debt model.

Character modeling should therefore represent not only “how X cares,” but **what X assumes care is supposed to solve**.

## 28.3 Ordinary bodies are socially interpreted, not neutral data

Food/body/training material is dense across the tranche:

- Saki reads Sumika's quadriceps as optimization potential;
- Temari is anxious about dieting while insisting she is thin;
- Ume eats extraordinary quantities within an extraordinary training ecology;
- Misuzu asks about staying lean on behalf of an unnamed friend;
- Hiro experiences ordinary lessons as severe physical resistance;
- Mao and Kotone debate nutritional optimization versus stress;
- Saki and Temari turn flexibility/stamina into rivalry.

The evidence argues against a single “idol body norm” operating identically for everyone. Bodies are interpreted through character-specific histories, goals, appetites, and anxieties.

## 28.4 Ordinary competence is distributed unevenly

The cast repeatedly possesses strong competence in one mundane domain and weakness in another:

- Mao: stage/role intelligence, weak rote memorization;
- Rinami: strong study regulation and social care;
- China: traditional dressing knowledge, limited ordinary fashion experimentation;
- Sumika: fashion/peer-culture fluency, weaker academic discipline elsewhere;
- Ume: extraordinary body management via Saki, limited technical explanation of why it works;
- Kotone: excellent practical task completion despite evasive presentation;
- Sena: high social capital and technical observation, poor calibration of Kotone's consent signals.

This is precisely the kind of heterogeneity needed for novel-situation reconstruction.

## 28.5 Role permeability, not mask exposure

Several scenes place polished public identities beside embarrassing ordinary states:

- Mao in a cute swimsuit;
- Sena losing to Ume in swimming;
- Temari agonizing over katsudon;
- Saki drinking a popular beverage and declaring it disgusting;
- Rinami buying far too much beach food;
- China recoiling from a crop top;
- Kotone meticulously cleaning while sounding evasive.

None of these scenes “debunks” the public identity. They explain how much additional person exists around it.

## 28.6 Succession begins in miniature

Phase 4 emphasized institutional succession. Support Series 2 shows the same process at micro scale:

- Lilja wants to become the kind of senior Mao was to her.
- China asks Rinami to teach dependability.
- Ume and China learn that seniors' poses cannot simply be copied.
- Sena judges China partly by how well she receives instruction.

The micro-level formulation is:

> **Inheritance succeeds when a function is learned and re-authored, not when a surface is duplicated.**

## Durable findings to carry forward

1. **Behavioral intimacy often precedes declared friendship.** Tolerated access, correction, shared food, rescue, and bodily care can be more diagnostic than labels.
2. **Care quality depends on the carer's model of the recipient.** The same helpful impulse can fit one person and violate another's material/body/social assumptions.
3. **Ordinary contradiction expands role identity rather than exposing fakery.** Public excellence and private appetite, embarrassment, academic weakness, or frustration coexist.
4. **Consent is a separate variable from admiration and talent recognition.** Sena/Kotone is the clearest case.
5. **Body, food, and training evidence must remain character-specific.** Ume, Hiro, China, Temari, Sumika, and Saki inhabit materially different physiological/psychological regimes.
6. **Inheritance operates in small acts before institutional succession.** Lilja wants to reproduce Mao's kindness; Ume/China learn not to copy seniors' surfaces.
7. **Producer Course peer ecology is structurally unlike idol-class peer ecology.** Age and professional-status heterogeneity are direct textual facts.
