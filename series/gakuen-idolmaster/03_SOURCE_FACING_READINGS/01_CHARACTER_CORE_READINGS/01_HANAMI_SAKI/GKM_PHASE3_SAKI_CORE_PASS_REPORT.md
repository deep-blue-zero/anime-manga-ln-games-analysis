## Principal findings

1. Saki is a **constructed all-rounder**, not adequately described as either a natural invincible genius or a secretly talentless fraud.
2. Her confidence is a maintained practice: self-naming, routine, bodily preparation, public pose, older-sister promise, and recovery technology.
3. The historical `無敵のお姉ちゃん` identity depended partly on `勝ち逃げ`: changing fields before Ume could fully catch her.
4. Idolhood removes that escape by creating a durable shared field in which Saki and Ume can continue to meet.
5. The ordinary failure micro-cycle—explosion, discharge, analysis, renewed action—is real but not universal. Ume's summer-H.I.F. victory produces prolonged grief and identity collapse despite immediate recommitment.
6. Saki's mature state is better named `不屈` than `無敵`: she can fall, break, need witnesses, and continue.
7. Older-sisterhood does not disappear in favor of a purely personal dream. It expands into a public idol vocation of leading, feeding, challenging, cultivating, and accepting pursuit.
8. Care develops from universalized athletic optimization toward recipient-specific craft, while remaining oriented toward competitive capability.
9. The Producer becomes witness and co-author after abandoning the unilateral “eternal legend” ideal, but permanent/exclusive language leaves a live dependency and enclosure question.
10. Fans evolve from pressure and strategic `外付けの筋肉` into participants and aspirational “little sisters,” without losing their instrumental role.
11. Unit Story proves that Saki can build a unit when collective excellence intensifies individual rivalry rather than suppressing it.
12. Songs function textually as controlled self-disclosure and appropriation technology, but all sonic, vocal, arrangement, and MV conclusions remain pending direct AV inspection.
13. Elite family resources and the Hanami Training Center materially complicate any pure-grit underdog reading.
14. The V1 “competitive love” thesis survives only after adding control, hierarchy, instrumentality, resource advantage, prolonged collapse, and the `無敵` → `不屈` revision.

- whether later evidence permits a genuinely horizontal sister rivalry;
- whether Saki can accept refusal, withdrawal, or noncompetitive goals without reframing them as growth;
- whether she can continue after collapse without the Producer as exclusive witness;
- how often the Producer is materially wrong and whether Saki can refuse him;
- whether actual music reveals a stable sonic identity rather than procedural versatility;
