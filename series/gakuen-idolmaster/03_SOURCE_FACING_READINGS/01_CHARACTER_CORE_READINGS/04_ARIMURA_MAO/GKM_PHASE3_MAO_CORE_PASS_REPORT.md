## Principal findings

1. **Mao is the idol of reclaimed aspiration.** The route does not cure her of wanting to be a prince; it changes the relation between Mao and the prince she performs.
2. The strongest stage model is: **fixed aspirational self-erasure → embodied self-acceptance → voluntary performance → relational, transformable princehood**.
3. Mao's childhood prince desire is genuine. The later problem is not that it was always false, but that bodily change and lost casting categories make one once-possible ideal impossible to inhabit unchanged.
4. Puberty produces a **role/category injury**: she becomes more conventionally feminine in body while remaining short, loses boy/prince casting, and begins using boyish presentation partly to resist what that loss means.
5. The analysis should discuss gendered embodiment, presentation, and category availability without diagnosing a modern gender identity the text does not name.
6. `可愛い` initially functions as a defeat category: it is associated with adults asking her to surrender the prince. Later it becomes an expressive resource she can possess without allowing it to replace the prince.
7. Dear 004 blocks the simple reading “fake prince → authentic cute girl”: merely **acting cute** would reproduce the same fixed-performance problem.
8. `カッコイイ` progressively moves from visual correspondence with a prince role toward an ethic of conduct: protecting others, persisting, taking responsibility, failing visibly, and continuing.
9. Dear 015 is a decisive hinge. Mao calls the old fixation `呪い / 呪縛`, yet calls the will that kept aspiring `ちゃんと本物`; mature princehood is therefore reclaimed rather than discarded.
10. Acting remains real technical capital. The route criticizes compulsory role-identification and audience-blind projection, not theatrical craft itself.
11. `Fluorite` supplies the clearest explicit self-theory: `ボクらしさ` is not determined by Mao alone but arises `ボクと、みんなとの関係の中で`. Identity becomes relational without becoming passive conformity.
12. Change itself becomes part of continuity. Mao can keep stable symbols—`ボク`, princehood, theatre, `0番`—while allowing what those symbols mean to change.
13. Dorm leadership and protector behavior are both spontaneous disposition and **inherited institutional care**. Mao explicitly frames care for juniors as repayment to seniors who cared for her.
14. Protector identity has a cost: Mao is better at expressing other people's needs than her own. The summer-memory commu and the summer-H.I.F. loss expose desires and grief that first appear through service or public composure.
15. Kotone helps horizontalize Mao's seniority by insisting that N.I.A. makes them rivals, not merely senior helper and junior beneficiary.
16. Shion is a hostile mirror: she attacks Mao as a `偽物の王子様`, but the conflict works because Mao has already lived through a related logic of self-hatred and fixed comparison.
17. Gekka prevents the route from confusing self-acceptance with denial of standards. Mao can win crowd response and still acknowledge a severe external skill gap.
18. Sena becomes summit, rival, and someone Mao wants to “bring down from the tower.” The rescue metaphor is generous but still retains prince/protector hierarchy and must later be tested from Sena's side.
19. The summer-H.I.F. loss again confirms the project-wide methodological rule: **behavioral recommitment is not emotional recovery**. Mao can speak forward publicly and then privately collapse into grief.
20. The Producer earns legitimacy by first seeing conduct and researching Mao's history rather than simply demanding a marketable image. Yet his early insistence on `可愛い` remains a real exercise of interpretive authority.
21. Dear 007 is therefore an ethical improvement: he refuses to supply a final definition of `ボクらしさ`, making room for Mao to author the concept relationally herself.
22. The 100Pro conflict makes Producer/Mao coauthorship explicit. Ryuusei's case for an experienced professional producer is rational; Mao nevertheless chooses this Producer as the person necessary to her own chosen path.
23. That choice is autonomy-enhancing and dependency-risking at once. “The person who sees me correctly” can become a basis for self-authorship and for unusually concentrated attachment.
24. `Campus mode!!` converts Mao from recipient of Hatsuboshi aspiration into a possible future source of it. Teaching Kotone forces intuitive actor knowledge into transferable craft, and Mao imagines future students inheriting and changing the same song.
25. `SUGAR FLAVOR` with Rinami tests a further shift from solitary spotlight ontology toward relation as performance resource.
26. Fans move from certifiers of image toward participants in identity. `リトルプリンス`, `Fluorite`, and later communications allow public naming to be accepted and reauthored rather than simply obeyed.
27. The professional-agency conflict is not reducible to school-good / industry-bad. 100Pro possesses real resources and expertise; the dispute is over which relation may author Mao's future and how much professionalization can preserve self-authorship.
28. Mao's winter Prima Stella speech treats visibility generatively: a visible star can help others recognize a sky and their own light. This is **Mao's ethical appropriation of the title**, not proof that Hatsuboshi's title system is universally benevolent.
29. Theatre's `0番` survives to the endpoint. Mao does not reach a final “true self”; she chooses a center position from which she can keep stretching toward an ideal and changing.
30. The best current summary is therefore: **Mao learns not to stop performing the prince, but to stop using the prince to disappear.**

## Key correction to the legacy interpretation

The older insight that “the prince survives by including cuteness” remains strong but is incomplete. The V2 corpus adds four constraints:

- the prince was both authentic aspiration and, later, defensive fixation;
- `可愛い` is not Mao's hidden essential truth but one previously disallowed part of her range;
- performance itself is not opposed to authenticity—fixed compulsory performance is the problem;
- mature identity becomes relational and transformable rather than an isolated inner essence.

The stronger formulation is:

> **Mao's development is not the replacement of a false masculine-coded prince with a true cute self. It is the recovery of authorship over aspiration: body, cuteness, theatricality, failure, care, rivals, fans, and other people's light can all enter a princehood she now chooses rather than one she must continuously prove.**
