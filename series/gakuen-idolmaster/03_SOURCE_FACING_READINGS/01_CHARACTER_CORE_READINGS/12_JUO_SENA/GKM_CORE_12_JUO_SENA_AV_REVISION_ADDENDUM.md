## 3. Canonical replacement paragraph

> **Juo Sena is the idol of generative supremacy performed as answerable sovereignty. She controls the geometry of attention through three genuine registers: a regnant queen who organizes height and office, a glamorous diva who governs proximity through style and desirability, and a receptive apprentice/fan who can be embarrassed, frightened, instructed, and supported. Her development does not discard hierarchy or ambition. It makes the crown contestable, co-authored, and capable of sharing the frame. `小さな野望` opens the measured ceiling, `Our Chant` mobilizes the constituency, `赤裸々` removes immunity, and `Star-mine` allows plural centers. Whether the institution she builds can ultimately exceed Sena as its organizing horizon remains open.**
