## Governing result

The Phase-3 bounded thesis is **generative supremacy**.

Sena begins as an elite title-holder whose accurate perception of visible ability has hardened into fatalism. She believes she has reached her ceiling and tries to convert the dream she can no longer realize into the production of a successor. Producer refuses that displacement. The route then expands her ontology of idol power through support, human vulnerability, story, external rivalry, public legitimacy, institutional design and differentiated production.

Her mature movement is:

> **measurable plateau → displaced retirement → first-person dream restoration → perfect-image rupture → supportability → external comparison → integrated idol/producer identity → successor-to-rival revision → production of a plural field → H.I.F. reform → contested Prima Stella victory → world-scale reopening.**

She does not become modest or renounce the crown. She makes supremacy more legitimate by creating conditions under which it must be repeatedly re-earned.

Her producer vocation is genuine, but the original plan uses another person's future to avoid naming her own defeat.

**Transition:** STRENGTHEN.

### 4. The perfect image is both cage and political office

It isolates Sena personally while teaching Hatsuboshi that legitimate excellence must appear complete and unsupported.

**Transition:** STRENGTHEN.

### 5. Support is part of ability

The route repeatedly demonstrates that audience and peer support can change courage, public meaning and developmental possibility without appearing in Sena's original parameters.

**Transition:** STRENGTHEN.

### 6. The successor model is explicitly rejected

Dear 016 moves from replacement-after-retirement to producing rivals while Sena remains in the field.

**Transition:** REJECT / REVISE.

### 7. Kotone is reciprocal star, not heir

Kotone's childhood fanhood reveals that Sena had already been producing her through public existence. Kotone returns that received light as opposition.

**Transition:** REJECT successor shorthand; STRENGTHEN fan–idol–rival cycle.

### 8. Sena's institution-building is ethically mixed

H.I.F. reform, scholarships, songs and differentiated production are constructive. Scouting possessiveness, secrecy, coercive challenge and founder-centered contest design remain real pressures.

**Transition:** DOWNGRADE clean benevolent-queen formulation.

### 9. Humility is not the endpoint

Sena becomes more openly greedy. Progress lies in admitting first-person desire and making the crown contestable, not in stepping aside.

**Transition:** REJECT.
