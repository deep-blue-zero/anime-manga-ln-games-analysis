## D-SENA — an office that can be handed on without retiring from the rivalry

### External proof and the right to continue producing

**Direct evidence (T-A, C1 @ D-SENA).** In Dear 028, Sena recounts a third consecutive title after the summer event and distinguishes two uncertainties. Whether H.I.F. has become a legitimate top-idol contest is contested outside school. Whether an active top idol should also produce other idols is contested inside 100 Pro. President Juo confirms post-graduation affiliation and asks her current Producer to continue, with terms left open (428–461). He calls her production 「プロデューサーごっこ」 and wants it stopped. Sena refuses, citing how teaching and being taught have both increased her ability (480–530). He closes by telling the Producer 「楽しみにしている」. The Producer's apparent dismissal in Dear 029 at 112–125 is a counterfactual illustration, while the earlier praise and recruitment are the actual meeting. Asari's inserted explanation at 240–251 calls the 六大オーディション an external gateway for top idols. Asari uses the ruby グランドスラム for 「完全制覇」, winning all six, and says fans confer a special title on those who achieve it. This differs from either 二冠 or 三冠.

The Producer deliberately proposes the three auditions expected to be hardest for Sena. The interface presents a single offered response at Dear 029 line 340. The Producer explains that Sena could reach two without the Producer (445); Sena accepts the higher test. The ordered consequences are VisuaL master (Dear 030 lines 5–22), VocaL master (031 lines 24–44) and DANCE MASTER (033 narration lines 4–9), plus two other Hatsuboshi titles in this track: Kotone's 舞道場 (032 lines 175–219 and 263–427), and Saki's 歌道場 (033 lines 210–259). Five of six fell to Hatsuboshi idols. These scripts name five auditions and do not identify the sixth. The President retracts 「プロデューサーごっこ」 at Dear 033 lines 10–23 and expressly accepts 「プロデューサー兼トップアイドル」. This occurs before the winter H.I.F. result. Sena says that either outcome could favor 100 Pro (111–113), her reading of the President's incentives.

**Interpretation (T-C).** The President's concession gives Sena's dual vocation an institutional consequence that the summer victory could not settle. Sena demonstrates the role in both directions: results of her pupils remain theirs, while training and watching them change her performance and judgment. In Dear 032 she says the experience of watching Ume, China and Kotone yielded more growth for her than anyone else in their group and calls the combined role her most suitable form (471–565). This is Sena's self-assessment, supported by the concrete results and by the pupils' responses (641–677). CIDOL 020 records the workload cost for Sena and her Producer. SENA-C19, C29, C35, C42 and C56 **STRENGTHEN**. SENA-C28 **STRENGTHEN** for genuinely shared goal escalation in this track, with the Producer authoring the decisive raise and Sena actively accepting it.
