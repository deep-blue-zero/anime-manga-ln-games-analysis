## Principal findings

1. **Kotone is the idol of material security becoming self-authorship.** Her route begins where family debt, tuition, overwork, and exhaustion make ordinary failure potentially catastrophic.
2. The V1 claim that her greed is “survival realism” survives only after revision. Scarcity explains why money became morally urgent, but the source preserves a genuine love of money, sales, royalties, and upward mobility after acute crisis begins resolving.
3. `成り上がり` is not an impure motive the route must cleanse. It becomes an affirmative language of self-made professional and class ascent.
4. The Producer's first meaningful work is **constraint removal**: rest, workload reduction, better-paid idol work, nutrition/material support, debt refinancing assistance, budgeting, and family/logistical intervention.
5. That support materially increases Kotone's autonomy while also concentrating exceptional power in the Producer. Effective care and paternalism coexist.
6. Kotone's confidence is **domain-split**. She can believe strongly in her cuteness, social reading, service ability, and fanservice while doubting durable comparative idol legitimacy.
7. `世界一可愛い私` does not purify greed or weakness out of the cute image. It makes those supposedly embarrassing materials singable.
8. Commerciality and authenticity are therefore not natural opposites in Kotone's self-model. Good work can sincerely delight an audience, generate praise, and deserve a fee at the same time.
9. Her strongest performance telos is **audience hospitality / reciprocal enjoyment**. Dear 016 dissolves fan ownership during performance; Dear 024 turns ticket price into an obligation that every buyer leave happy; `Yellow Big Bang！` identifies lost visible enjoyment as a real performance defect even when technical quality is high.
10. Competition remains motivationally important, but the best performance state often suspends rank in favor of the room itself.
11. Sena begins as a star Kotone admired before the current summit. Fanhood survives inside mature rivalry.
12. Kotone's rejection of Sena's successor plan is a major act of self-authorship: `後継者` is replaced by `ライバル` so that neither person becomes merely the other's inherited role.
13. `スター性` names a socially visible remainder after measurable performance dimensions; it should not yet be treated as mystical essence.
14. The H.I.F. loss confirms a cross-character methodological rule: **professional or behavioral recommitment is not the same variable as emotional recovery**.
15. Dear 027's repeated `普通` is the class/psychological climax of the current route. The girl introduced dreaming of riches experiences ordinary training, auditions, lives, and progression without emergency as almost unbelievable luxury.
16. Debt/payment vocabulary is a major intimacy grammar. Kotone makes receiving care tolerable by translating it into repayable obligation.
17. The late career-as-payment exchange is moving and ethically unresolved: the Producer speaks her reciprocity language, but the entire idol life becomes something that can be imagined as owed.
18. Producer/Kotone romance coding is strong—dates, exclusivity, naming, jealousy, explicit preference, `実質告白` language—but the Phase-3 corpus does not establish a formally declared canonical couple.
19. Family recognition helps make idolhood real; family later becomes more than a dependent burden, including spectatorship and creative participation.
20. U1/Re;IRIS demonstrates Kotone can occupy a trio with Saki and Temari without becoming merely the support member, but that remains a separate continuity track.

## Key correction to the legacy interpretation

The older rhetorical question “can charm become materially real enough to save a life?” is now too compressed.

The stronger formulation is:

> **Can worked charm, social intelligence, audience relation, paid labor, and institutional support create enough material floor that a precarious teenager can possess an ordinary future—and then decide what extravagance she actually wants?**

This distributes causality more accurately and avoids turning the Producer or “cuteness” into a singular savior.
