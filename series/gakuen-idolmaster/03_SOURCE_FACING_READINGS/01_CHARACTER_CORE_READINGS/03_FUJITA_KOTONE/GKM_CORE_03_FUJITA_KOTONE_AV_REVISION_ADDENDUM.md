# GKM CORE 03 — FUJITA KOTONE AUDIOVISUAL REVISION ADDENDUM

## 0. Function

This addendum routes the provisional textual findings in `GKM_CORE_03_FUJITA_KOTONE.md` through the completed Phase-3 audiovisual pass.

It does not replace the source-facing character core. It records which claims are preserved, strengthened, revised, downgraded, rejected, or left open after inspection of dialogue performance, official MVs, 3DMVs, song commus, full-mix sources, and the Re;IRIS performance.

Governing transition vocabulary:

> `PRESERVE · STRENGTHEN · REVISE · DOWNGRADE · REJECT · OPEN`

---

# 1. Revised character thesis

## 1.1 Textual thesis preserved

> **Fujita Kotone is the idol of material security becoming self-authorship.**

The AV corpus strongly supports this. The early Dear performance makes material compression visible; the late route gives `普通` emotional and bodily force; `White Night! White Wish!` and `ふわふわ` show ordinary adolescent time becoming legitimate artistic material rather than wasted time outside work.

## 1.2 Audiovisual refinement

> **Kotone is also the idol of performed reciprocity. Once enough material floor exists for ordinary development, she turns the social skills acquired under scarcity into a chosen public craft: reading what others want, modulating cuteness, making attention feel personal, receiving audience energy, and returning it as visible pleasure.**

The two formulations should be read together:

- material security creates room for choice;
- performed reciprocity is what she chooses to make from capacities that were once primarily adaptive.

# 3. Revised voice model

The textual register map is preserved and formalized as seven states:

| State | Function |
| --- | --- |
| `K-CUTE-SERVICE` | reduce friction, sell, flirt, thank, make attention pleasurable |
| `K-BLUNT-WORK` | protect time, reality-test, signal comfort, refuse over-romanticization |
| `K-AUDIT-ANXIOUS` | search for hidden flaw and obtain reassurance |
| `K-PEER-COMP` | preserve equality through insult-affection and comparison |
| `K-ORDINARY-INTIMATE` | receive care, admit missed youth, ask for presence/naming |
| `K-PUBLIC-HOSPITALITY` | command participation and return audience investment |
| `K-OVERLOAD` | expose what current social tools cannot contain |

The states do not form a maturation ladder. Later Kotone gains conscious integration and wider choice rather than ceasing to use early modes.

# 4. Revised musical identity

## 4.1 Preserve

Kotone can make performed cuteness feel socially meaningful rather than merely decorative.

## 4.2 Revise

Her musical identity is not adequately described as one bright/cute sonic family.

The corpus includes:

- foundational idol pop;
- crowd-oriented festival performance;
- novelty/self-esteem pop;
- soft illustrated interiority;
- shared and inherited repertoire;
- dark theatrical electronic staging;
- cybernetic aggression;
- and digital-hyperpop spectacle.

## 4.3 Current formulation

> **Kotone’s stable musical identity is procedural: direct intelligibility, comic exposure, fast affective pivot, audience orientation, and authored portability.**

This is a Phase-3 conclusion, not a final stem/score-level vocal profile.

---

# 5. Revised audience model

Earlier language of “audience hospitality” remains useful but should be narrowed.

Hospitality can imply one-way service. Kotone’s mature performance is reciprocal:

1. spectators commit time, money, and expectation;
2. Kotone notices that investment;
3. she produces directed attention and visible enjoyment;
4. spectators respond;
5. the response enlarges her confidence and energy;
6. she returns the enlarged performance.

Preferred current term:

> **performed reciprocity / reciprocal audience circulation**

This term should be carried into the Character State, Song/Musical Identity, Theme/Motif, Voice Performance, and Relationship ledgers.

# 6. Revised relationship notes

## 6.1 Producer

`STRENGTHEN` mutual recognition; `OPEN` refusal/autonomy.

The relationship is not one-way rescue. Kotone interprets the Producer’s work, refuses his self-disparagement, and returns artistic/emotional value. Yet her entire-career-as-payment language remains a structural risk.

## 6.2 Sena

`STRENGTHEN` reciprocal rivalry without destroyed admiration.

Kotone can remain a fan while refusing succession and demanding equality.

## 6.3 Saki and Temari

`STRENGTHEN` peer ecology.

`がむしゃらに行こう！` and Re;IRIS show that Kotone can borrow force/discipline from peers without losing her social signature. Insult-affection keeps admiration horizontal.

## 6.4 Audience

`STRENGTHEN` as principal mature relationship.

The audience is not only market, judge, or emotional abstraction. It is the partner that helps produce the performance.

## 6.5 Family

`STRENGTHEN` recognition as public-confidence infrastructure.

Family love becomes a training ground from which Kotone can project attention outward. Debt completion changes but does not erase the relation.

# 7. Preserved counterpressures

The following must not be lost when later synthesis compresses the character:

1. Performing reciprocity can still become compulsory emotional labor.
2. Audience response can become another unstable measure of worth.
3. Commercial literacy can protect against exploitation and also colonize intimacy.
4. External confidence is not automatically immature; absolute non-replacement demands remain dangerous.
5. Broad range does not prove unlimited vocal or stylistic mastery.
6. Shared choreography cannot be promoted as unique personal authorship without comparison.
7. Re;IRIS proves distributed focality within its continuity, not universal ensemble leadership.
8. The exact Kotone `ガラクタロード` comparison is open, not negatively resolved.
9. Later event/support material may materially redistribute the Producer-heavy social map.
10. `普通` is an achieved floor that future success could again threaten.

---

# 8. Current bounded formulation

> **Fujita Kotone begins with an acute awareness that value disappears unless someone acknowledges it materially. The Producer first creates enough floor for her existing ability to appear. Kotone then learns to turn calculation into craft: cuteness becomes a relational interface, fanservice becomes distributed recognition, and audience investment becomes mutual energy rather than one-way purchase. Her mature form is not purified idealism. She remains commercial, jealous, praise-hungry, funny, rough, and ambitious. What changes is that these traits increasingly operate inside chosen relation rather than emergency.**

This formulation should govern Phase-3 comparative work until events, support cards, ensemble synthesis, and the definitive monograph revise it.
