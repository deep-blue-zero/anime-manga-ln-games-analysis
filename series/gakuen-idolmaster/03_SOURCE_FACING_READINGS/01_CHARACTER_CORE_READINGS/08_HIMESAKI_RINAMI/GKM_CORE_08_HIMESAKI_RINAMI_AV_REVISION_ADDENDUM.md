## 4. Reconstruction additions

The textual core should now route reconstruction through these AV-safe rules:

1. Rinami first reads and stabilizes the relational field.
2. Hands commonly gather near chest/waist before difficult self-disclosure.
3. Conscious onee-san activation is higher-monitoring, not a different person.
4. Receiving care narrows composure but does not infantilize her.
5. Competition increases directness while respect remains.
6. Romantic directness is earned longitudinally and remains continuous with public dual address.
7. Technical language should coexist with warmth.
8. Breadth repertoire permits cool, forceful, athletic, dark, and comic states.
9. Late desire is explicit but still relational.
10. Professional/romantic fusion remains an ethical question, not a solved fact.
