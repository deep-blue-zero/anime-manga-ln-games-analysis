## Canonical bounded thesis

> **Himesaki Rinami is the idol of reciprocal care becoming self-authored desire: a girl whose earliest way of surviving loneliness was to make another lonely person smile, who later turns that relational capacity into an `お姉さん` idol language, and who must learn that caring for other people does not require making her own wants secondary. Her development is not from fake persona to natural essence. It is from care used as identity and refuge toward a reciprocal form in which hospitality, ambition, competition, romance, technical craft, fandom, and the desire to be cared for can all become parts of one authored idol life.**

## Principal Phase-3 gains

1. **Late blooming remains real.** Rinami begins after substantial public failure and with years of technique already accumulated.
2. **The old `妹キャラ` is revised, not erased.** It was badly fitted but sincerely performed.
3. **Childhood care is co-regulation.** Dear 026 reveals Rinami herself was lonely and crying; making the Producer smile also regulated her own pain.
4. **`お姉さん` is produced naturalness.** Dear 003 explicitly requires role rehearsal and natural behavior at the same time.
5. **Care is also pleasure.** Rinami likes cooking, hosting, helping, and making people smile; mature reciprocity additionally permits her to receive.
6. **Romance is explicit.** Dear 010 contains internal date/confession framing; Dear 037 contains direct `好きだよ`.
7. **Romance and profession deliberately fuse.** Dear 037 uses marriage-vow syntax before asking for lifelong production. This is not literal marriage and does not erase Producer-role ethics.
8. **Competition creates something new.** Rinami develops summit desire rather than merely recovering an older childhood self.
9. **Dear 027 is the self-authorship hinge.** The Producer refuses to command her return; Rinami says `優勝したかった` and chooses top-idol ambition herself.
10. **Technique becomes care.** Dear 031 frames whole-body fanservice and elite precision as `愛` toward fans, songs, staff, and idols.
11. **H.O.F. joins audience love to self-love.** Rinami receives audience smiles and uses them to become a self she loves.
12. **Prima Stella is warmth carried by craft.** D-RINAMI's summit does not imply that warmth substitutes for technical history.
13. **Peer relations broaden the model.** Sena peerization and RippleSign/Mao show Rinami can relate through rivalry and reciprocal partnership rather than only asymmetrical caregiving.
14. **The Producer is enabling and structurally powerful.** His restraint in Dear 027/CIDOL007 is real counterevidence to enclosure, while H.O.F. environmental design and final romantic-professional fusion preserve governance questions.
