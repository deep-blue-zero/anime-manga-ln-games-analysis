## Bounded thesis

> **Sumika is the idol of self-authored expectation: a former ballet prodigy whose injury taught her that being believed in can feel like a threat, who first survives by making herself unserious and later learns to choose which expectations she will answer, which ideals she will author, and which vulnerabilities she will make public.**

The largest V2 gains are:

1. `gyaru brightness = armor` is narrowed. Her sociability, leisure, fashion, teasing and public fluency are genuinely pleasurable; the defense is the strategic use of casualness when seriousness would expose her to judgment.
2. Injury is separated into physical event, psychological anticipation, and later biomechanical compensation. The route never needs a fake/real injury dichotomy.
3. Lilja's belief becomes an expectation problem **inside Sumika**, not evidence that Lilja objectively values only the old prodigy. Lilja's independent V2 reading supports this correction.
4. Dear 008's `強いあたしの仮面` is not repudiated. It becomes the route's theory of chosen performance: accept weakness, choose an ideal, practice the lie until lived history makes it increasingly true.
5. N.I.A. scales the problem from intimate expectation into social expectation: success changes friendship, creates envy, enables rumor and makes `天才` publicly reappear.
6. Tsubame's dance defeat shows that Sumika has become invested enough in idol craft that failure can threaten identity; technical rebuilding then grounds later transcendence in ordinary strength work.
7. `わがまま` is not selfishness-as-vice. It means recovering preferences that are not merely answers to other people's images of her.
8. CIDOL 012 / `Love & Joy` turns self-authorship into artistic process: Sumika contributes lyrics, choreography, styling, ballet-derived motion and fanservice because only she fully knows what she wants to communicate.
9. Dear 031–032 converts trauma disclosure into vocation. Sumika does not simply reveal a hidden true self; she makes autobiographical vulnerability into a promise that her dance will give courage to people who are broken or afraid.
10. Dear 034 explicitly redefines strength: fear is a condition of courage, not its opposite.
11. REVERSI is no longer safe to model as permanent fusion. Dear 033/037 independently confirm from Sumika's side that mature connection requires companion/rival differentiation and separable future dreams.
12. Dear 037 closes the courage circuit when the Producer says Sumika's performance gave **him** courage. That relational effect becomes evidence that she achieved the idol self she wanted.
