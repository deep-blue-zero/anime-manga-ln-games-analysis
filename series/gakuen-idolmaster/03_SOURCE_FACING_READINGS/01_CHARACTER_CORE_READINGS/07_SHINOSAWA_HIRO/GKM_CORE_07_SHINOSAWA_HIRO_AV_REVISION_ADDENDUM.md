# GKM CORE 07 — SHINOSAWA HIRO AV REVISION ADDENDUM

## 0. Authority

`GKM_CORE_07_SHINOSAWA_HIRO.md` remains the canonical source-facing textual core. This addendum records what the completed AV pass preserves, strengthens, revises, downgrades, rejects, or leaves open. It must be read alongside `GKM_HIRO_COMPLETE_AUDIOVISUAL_BASELINE.md`.

## 1. Core thesis

**PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN**

> Hiro is the idol of chosen impossibility becoming accountable attachment.

AV refinement:

> **Her audiovisual mechanism is resistant form: mismatch among body, voice, musical language, and idol convention is not erased but composed into attention, craft, and relation.**

## 2. Major transitions

### `Hiro loves failure`

**REVISE.** She loves resistance and initially enjoys consequence-light failure. Attachment makes some failures painful and professionally unacceptable.

### `Hiro's weakness is a comic premise`

**REJECT as sufficient.** Comedy is real, but breath, recovery, visible effort, and training adaptation keep the body materially present.

### `神格 is Hiro's essence`

**DOWNGRADE.** It is one strong reception mode generated by restraint, contrast, opacity, and risk. Cute/social Hiro is equally canonical.

### `Hiro's music is experimental`

**STRENGTHEN / SPECIFY.** Experimentalism is curated across multiple Hiro-specific works and creators. Common repertoire proves it is not an invariant genre property of every performance.

### `Crisis makes Hiro better`

**STRENGTHEN / ETHICALLY OPEN.** The packet supports a crisis-concentration distinction. It does not prove that Producer is entitled to escalate emergency indefinitely.

### `Hiro chose idolhood only because it was unsuitable`

**REVISE.** The answer is true but incomplete. `かわいくなりたかった` establishes an ordinary positive desire that Hiro found more embarrassing than radical unsuitability.

### `Improvement means normalization`

**REJECT.** Improvement creates expressive surplus while preserving distinctive mismatch.

### `Producer and Hiro are equal partners`

**DOWNGRADE.** They are reciprocal co-conspirators in affect and dream, but production authority remains institutionally asymmetric.

### `Prima Stella proves stable conventional mastery`

**REJECT.** Hiro explicitly frames the summit as a bounded peak, `瞬間最大風速` and `今夜だけ`.

## 3. New canonical propositions

1. **Mismatch is composed, not removed.**
2. **Experimentalism is repertoire-level character construction.**
3. **Micro-affective deadpan is a major voice mechanism.**
4. **Visible improvement is part of reception appeal, but derivative montage cannot define chronology.**
5. **Cute desire and goddess distance are compatible partial modes.**
6. **The shared dream makes risk morally heavier, not automatically safer.**
7. **Hiro's mature professional problem is sustainable access to concentration without permanent emergency.**

## 4. Cross-route consequences

- China/Hiro synthesis should use `みちなるひろがる` and `標`, while marking the missing song commu.
- Ume/Hiro comparison should distinguish physical abundance from physical scarcity without ranking courage simplistically.
- Sena/Hiro analysis should examine how institutions name nonmetric charisma and whether `神格` aestheticizes risk.
- Producer ethics synthesis must treat Hiro as the strongest case of consensual productive pressure that still fails to resolve authority asymmetry.
- Music synthesis should compare Hiro's curated experimentalism with Misuzu's scale/contrast, Sena's attention geometry, and Temari's vocal identity without flattening all unconventional songs into one category.

## 5. Remaining open claims

- sustainable noncrisis peak access;
- later failure after full dream ownership;
- full `みちなるひろがる` commu meaning;
- creator-confirmed intent behind the body/music homology;
- live-performance translation.
