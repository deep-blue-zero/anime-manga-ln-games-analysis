## Track-bound verdict notes

- `D-HIRO` Prima Stella victory is **not** merged into Series-3 conditional failure/normal fragments.
- `P3-C[HIRO]` failure scenes remain useful evidence that separation and ending have become frightening, without implying the D-HIRO victory also occurred in those states.
- Modular Produce Events and idol communications supply trait/ordinary-life tests but are not automatically assigned to every longitudinal result state.
