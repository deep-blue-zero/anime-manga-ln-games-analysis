## Current core thesis

> **Katsuragi Lilja is the idol of courage made circulatory: a girl who initially treats lack of skill as a fixed premise and compensates through self-erasing effort, then learns to convert courage received from Sumika, her Producer, family, rivals, teachers, and fans into an audience-facing performance that returns courage to other people.**

Developmental shorthand:

`fearful desire -> borrowed belief -> compulsive effort -> governed effort -> technical competence -> discovered/cultivated charisma -> reciprocal support -> differentiated partnership -> public transmission`

## Principal Phase-3 findings

1. **Beginnerhood is real.** Lilja begins technically weak, lacks formal foundations, and often has too little attentional bandwidth to think beyond basic execution.
2. **"No talent" is too broad as a mature taxonomy.** Later text identifies an intermittent visual/charismatic capacity, but it becomes usable only through foundations, styling, direction, emotional conditions, and performance craft.
3. **Effort requires governance.** Her strongest early virtue can become self-erasure; injury/overwork material makes rest, pacing, technique, and trust part of mature courage rather than concessions to weakness.
4. **Performance is address.** Her central performance ethic combines the will to reach someone with the most complete version of the self she can presently offer.
5. **Fans become persons rather than metrics.** Mature fanhood is reciprocal: supporters lend courage to Lilja, and Lilja attempts to return courage through visible action and performance.
6. **Shion is an adversarial but analytically important teacher.** Harshness does not make her technical diagnosis false; she forces Lilja to confront foundations, reproducibility, and logic rather than romanticizing grit.
7. **The visual/mystical Lilja is coauthored.** `Katsuragi barrier` and related fantasy vocabulary turn visual direction, costume, gesture, atmosphere, and Lilja's own otaku imagination into an authored performance world.
8. **REVERSI matures through differentiation, not fusion.** Sumika first opens Lilja's world, but Lilja later supports and challenges Sumika. `Teammate and rival`, separate wants, and eventually separate dreams become signs of a healthier bond.
9. **Intimacy becomes strategy.** Their relationship history is deliberately made part of H.I.F. interpretation/publicity. The material is authentic, but its competitive instrumentalization remains an ethical question.
10. **The Producer is confidence scaffold, coauthor, and regulator.** He helps Lilja borrow belief until she can act, but the relationship also develops mutual-vindication and possible enclosure pressures that remain open for later phases.
11. **Sweden, family, and fandom are bridges rather than exotic essence.** Lilja is Japanese-proficient and culturally connected before arrival; the important stressor is environmental transition and family distance, not linguistic incapacity.
12. **Failure becomes legitimate grief.** Early defeat confirms what Lilja expects; later defeat hurts because she has learned she is entitled to want the result.
