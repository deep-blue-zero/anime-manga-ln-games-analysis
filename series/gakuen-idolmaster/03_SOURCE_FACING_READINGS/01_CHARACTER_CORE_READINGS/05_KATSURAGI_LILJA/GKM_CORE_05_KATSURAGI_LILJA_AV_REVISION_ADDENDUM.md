# GKM CORE 05 — KATSURAGI LILJA AV REVISION ADDENDUM

## 0. Authority and use

`GKM_CORE_05_KATSURAGI_LILJA.md` remains the exact-text and source-facing Phase-3 authority. This addendum records what the complete acted/rendered packet changes. It should be read with `GKM_LILJA_COMPLETE_AUDIOVISUAL_BASELINE.md`, not as a replacement for the textual core.

Revision vocabulary:

- **PRESERVE** — current claim survives without material alteration;
- **STRENGTHEN** — AV adds direct performed support;
- **REVISE** — formulation changes materially;
- **DOWNGRADE** — claim becomes narrower or less central;
- **REJECT** — claim is incompatible with the packet;
- **OPEN** — the packet cannot close the question.

---

# 1. Executive transition

Textual thesis:

> **courage made circulatory**

AV refinement:

> **courage made circulatory through permeable world-making**

Formal mechanism:

> **inward gathering -> stabilized center -> outward field.**

Lilja's audiovisual development does not replace timidity with fearlessness. It increases the reliability with which a contracted, pause-rich self can stabilize, complete a claim, and direct it toward another person.

# 3. Section-level routing

## Core section 1 — courage made circulatory

**STRENGTHEN.** The body repeatedly makes the circuit visible: hands gather near the chest, posture stabilizes, gaze lifts, arms open, and the audience enters the image. Dear 033 proves that fear remains true during late leadership.

## Core sections 2-3 — first idol act and talent taxonomy

**PRESERVE / REVISE.** Initial weakness remains real. `Atmosphere` and Shion's diagnosis require a performer-specific world-making category, but the effect is coauthored rather than a concealed natural essence.

## Core section 4 — effort

**STRENGTHEN / GOVERN.** N.I.A. acting makes proof obligation look frightened and bodily dangerous. `ガラクタロード` promotes pleasure into the taxonomy so that mature Lilja is not modeled as permanent strain.

## Core section 5 — performance as delivery

**STRENGTHEN / SPECIFY.** Technique is not secondary in the sense of dispensable. It creates the attentional bandwidth required for `届ける`. Open-arm choreography and camera address make the telos visible.

## Core section 6 — fans

**STRENGTHEN.** Audience scale, direct address, and the Prima Stella speech support a reciprocal courage circuit rather than one-way admiration.

## Core section 7 — Shion

**STRENGTHEN / NARROW.** Shion supplies useful technical correction. The packet does not redeem her hostility or prove that cruelty is necessary pedagogy.

## Core section 8 — `魔性` and `葛城結界`

**STRENGTHEN / REVISE.** `Atmosphere` realizes the symbolic system through costume, exposed face, staff-like microphone, controlled center, camera, lighting, and authored environment. The phenomenon is gravitational legibility in a supported field.

## Core section 9 — Sweden, family, and culture

**DOWNGRADE fairy imagery as explanatory center.** The packet supports a much broader visual range. Swedish/fairy material remains a bridge and selectable production resource.

## Core sections 10-11 — Sumika and REVERSI

**STRENGTHEN / REJECT fusion.** The shared 3DMV and official MV preserve two readable bodies and outlines. Intimacy, rivalry, and separate desire coexist.

## Core section 12 — Producer

**STRENGTHEN / HUMANIZE / OPEN.** `桜フォトグラフ` shows fallible selection and mutual reading. N.I.A. and Dear 037 preserve enclosure risk.

## Core section 13 — ordinary life

**STRENGTHEN / PROMOTE pleasure.** `ガラクタロード` shows that fandom and enjoyment are not decorative. They protect against total professional proof logic.

## Core sections 14-16 — failure, Prima Stella, wanting

**STRENGTHEN / BOUND.** Late acting permits grief and singular desire without erasing relational credit. D-LILJA Prima Stella remains track-scoped and does not prove universal destiny.

## Core section 17 — Japanese register

**REVISE from provisional to performed baseline.** The late change is reduced apology/self-erasing stammer and greater clause ownership, not the disappearance of ellipsis or softness.

## Core section 20 — provisional model

**STRENGTHEN / ADD.** Add permeable world-making, pleasure, technical bandwidth, and synchronized differentiation. Preserve overwork, external-validation, and separation risks.

# 4. Current reconstruction model

## Stable core

- moves while afraid;
- gathers emotion inward before acting;
- seeks precise technique after valid criticism;
- relies on named relationships without treating dependence as automatically shameful;
- uses fantasy/fandom vocabulary as an authorship tool;
- understands performance as delivery to a specific other;
- returns received courage through public form.

## Principal risks

- converting another person's belief into bodily proof obligation;
- treating rest as legitimate only when it improves performance;
- narrowing confidence around Producer recognition;
- renouncing scarce desire to protect attachment;
- allowing institutional use of relationship history to outrun consent.

## Mature capacities

- admitted fear without withdrawal;
- technical questions and repair orientation;
- support returned to Sumika;
- public credit-sharing without denial of action;
- singular ambition without necessary betrayal;
- dark, bright, comic, and fantasy performance portability;
- pleasure alongside effort.

---

# 5. Claims that remain open

- stable self-regulation when the Producer is absent;
- intrinsic value of rest;
- sustained disagreement or refusal with the Producer;
- privacy/consent under expanding REVERSI commercialization;
- post-victory self-worth outside idol achievement;
- durability of pleasure under higher professional pressure;
- whether support cards show a materially more casual peer register;
- whether later sources commodify Swedish/family identity more aggressively.

## Current authority statement

The current Phase-3 formulation is:

> **Lilja does not prove that weakness was false. She learns to organize weakness into a permeable world: support can enter, technique can stabilize it, and courage can leave toward someone else.**
