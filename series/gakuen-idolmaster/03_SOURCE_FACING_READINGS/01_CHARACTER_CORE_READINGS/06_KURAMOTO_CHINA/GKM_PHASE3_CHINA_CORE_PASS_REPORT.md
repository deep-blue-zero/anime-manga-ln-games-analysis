## Current core thesis

> **Kuramoto China is the idol of accountable inheritance: a girl born into extraordinary material and affective support who initially experiences that support as evidence that her achievements are less truly hers, then learns to distinguish coercive command from legitimate aid, inherited advantage from personal abdication, and dependence from irresponsibility. She matures not into self-sufficiency but into the ability to inherit, govern, redistribute, and publicly answer for the forces that make her possible—until a relationally produced victory can finally be claimed as genuinely hers without denying how many hands built it.**

Developmental shorthand:

`sheltered noncommitment -> accurate weakness -> accepted scaffolding -> moral anxiety about privilege -> rules for legitimate support -> reciprocal network formation -> delegated leadership -> nondelegable exposure -> socially ratified self-credit -> summit obligation`

## Principal Phase-3 findings

1. **China's deepest starting poverty is a lack of self-authored stakes, not material deprivation.** Her P1 true ending describes a comfortable life without difficult commitments, completion, or serious frustration; idolhood creates a personally owned field of consequence.
2. **Early technical weakness is real.** Bottom-rank self-description, stamina/flexibility limits, weak fundamentals, and remedial training are not false humility.
3. **Low defensive pride is a developmental asset.** China can accept humiliating correction without converting it into status injury, which makes her unusually trainable.
4. **`人に好かれる才能` is a distinct route-specific talent claim.** It does not mean technical ability, and it cannot be reduced to family nepotism because ordinary public/work/friend evidence repeatedly generates affection.
5. **Privilege is made explicit rather than erased.** Family name, money, appearance, upbringing, manners, visibility, and institutional leverage are treated as real resources.
6. **Her mature privilege ethic is stewardship.** China rejects `濫用` and commanded affection/votes while accepting legitimate production support; inherited advantage creates responsibility rather than a duty to pretend the advantage does not exist.
7. **Nadeshiko is a negative control for inherited power used as command/ownership.** The contrast should remain structurally useful while leaving Nadeshiko's own eventual independent perspective open.
8. **Fans become constitutive persons rather than aggregate metrics.** Dear 020 explicitly defines fans as part of the force by which China exists as an idol.
9. **Hatsuboshi's support-as-ability doctrine is explicit but normatively open.** `本来の実力` includes the powers supporting an idol; this helps China self-credit but may also naturalize resource inequality.
10. **Leadership is not self-sufficiency.** China adopts a Liu-Bang-like model of assembling strong people, distributing roles, and using social power deliberately.
11. **Interdependence contains nondelegable responsibility.** The election climax is intentionally personal: China insists on facing Sena alone so voters can compare them and reject her if she is unworthy.
12. **China can redistribute inherited resources horizontally.** Later material includes using Kuramoto resources to strengthen Hiro, converting some vertical patronage capacity into reciprocal rivalry.
13. **Hiro becomes friend and rival rather than dependent beneficiary.** Full bilateral adjudication awaits Hiro's own core.
14. **Sena remains admired elder and genuine rival.** Succession does not require de-idealization.
15. **`ふにゃ` and `きり` are integrated person-states.** The next-president `仮面` is explicitly accepted as one real part of China rather than a false self hiding the soft girl.
16. **Technical competence eventually becomes real.** By the late Dear route, improvement matters; victory is no longer merely money substituting for craft, even though China's raw ability remains below some elite peers.
17. **`まぐれ勝ち` becomes psychologically untenable once trusted others recognize her as a genuine contender.** Self-credit is relationally licensed rather than atomistically discovered.
18. **D-CHINA victory remains support-saturated and personally claimable at once.** She can say `勝ったのは、わたくしです` without pretending she won alone.
19. **Self-credit remains unfinished.** Dear 037 shows she still cannot comfortably call herself `トップアイドル` in her own voice.
20. **The Producer is teacher, strategist, confidence scaffold, and chosen attachment; enclosure remains open.** He is a Producer-Course student rather than an outside adult manager, but China repeatedly imagines her career as functionally impossible without him.
21. **Family care has a history.** Kanae and grandfather material prevents “privilege” from becoming an abstract pile of money; it is lived through hierarchical but evolving relationships.
22. **China critiques atomized merit rather than merit itself.** She still values fair comparison, technical skill, noncoercion, direct judgment, and accountability.
