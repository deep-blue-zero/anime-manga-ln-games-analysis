# GKM CORE 06 — KURAMOTO CHINA AV REVISION ADDENDUM

This addendum routes the Phase-3 textual model through the complete China audiovisual pass. It does not silently mutate the textual core.

| textual claim | transition | AV formulation |
|---|---|---|
| Accountable inheritance / relational legitimacy | **STRENGTHEN** | formalized as receptive authority: China receives resources and expectations, visibly registers them, organizes a response, and accepts public answerability |
| China begins genuinely weak | **STRENGTHEN** | elite manners and photogenic poise visibly coexist with physical strain, panic, and beginner-level acquisition difficulty |
| Her initial deficit is lack of self-authored stakes | **STRENGTHEN** | early performance turns being known and learning under pressure into the first arena she cannot simply abandon or purchase |
| Epistemic humility is a learning technology | **STRENGTHEN** | correction reaches the body before the answer; embarrassment does not make information unusable |
| `人に好かれる才能` | **REVISE / NARROW** | best modeled as affective legibility, receptivity, trainability, and invitation rather than mystical universal charm |
| Privilege is inseparable from her actual life | **STRENGTHEN** | family, servants, costume, access, production scale, and institutional mediation remain materially visible |
| `濫用` is the ethical hinge | **STRENGTHEN** | commanded affection is rejected; production support is accepted after purpose and audience are defined |
| Fans become constitutive power | **STRENGTHEN** | gratitude and plural audience address enter her performed body; support is named without erasing the performer |
| `愛された責任` | **STRENGTHEN / OPEN** | `雪解けに` gives the claim family history and warmth; risk of permanent over-obligation remains |
| Hiro relation risks vertical patronage | **REVISE / STRENGTHEN RECIPROCITY** | collaborative song authorship and `みちなるひろがる` distribute gaze, creative agency, and future-making |
| Leadership through recruitment/delegation | **STRENGTHEN** | D-CHINA visibly moves from ally formation to public scene control |
| Legitimate dependence contains nondelegable acts | **STRENGTHEN** | Dear 026 makes solitary comparison with Sena the condition of electoral legitimacy |
| Next-president persona is a mask | **REVISE** | an effortful, permeable public role that China explicitly owns as part of self |
| `ふにゃ` and `きり` are separate selves | **REJECT** | distinct organizational states inside one stable `わたくし/ですわ` linguistic identity |
| China’s ojou-sama register is affectation | **REJECT / REFORMULATE** | marked but habitual social formation; emotional intensity changes timing/body more than sociolect |
| `Wonder Scale` is weakness-coded | **REVISE / EXPAND** | beginner smallness is transformed into invitation, warmth, and world-attentive charm rather than apology |
| Curiosity is an early-stage trait | **REJECT** | `日々、発見的ステップ！` and later visual materials preserve curiosity after competence |
| `Campus mode!!` is inherited legitimacy | **STRENGTHEN / SPECIFY** | legitimacy comes through trained participation and predecessor memory, not simple access |
| `空と約束` is self-authorship | **STRENGTHEN / SPECIFY** | not solitary creation; China defines present intention and audience while accepting collaborative production |
| `まぐれ勝ち` is accurate humility | **DOWNGRADE** | initially useful, later ethically evasive once trust, labor, and rival seriousness must be honored |
| D-CHINA Prima Stella can claim victory | **STRENGTHEN** | speech names support, raw-skill asymmetry, and personal victory together |
| Top-idol self-credit is complete | **REJECT** | Dear 037 preserves inability to self-name despite public title and victory |
| Gentle/elegant repertoire defines musical identity | **REVISE / EXPAND** | wonder remains central, but darker authority and common-song portability are real |
| Producer enhances agency | **STRENGTHEN / OPEN** | he creates interpretive tasks rather than supplying every answer; disagreement and separation remain under-tested |
| Responsible use resolves class inequality | **REJECT** | AV grandeur may aestheticize advantage; distributive fairness remains a Phase-8 question |

## Revised compact model

> **Kuramoto China is the idol of accountable inheritance realized as receptive authority. She receives love, money, correction, tradition, rivals, and trust into a visibly permeable self, organizes them without pretending they are hers alone, and makes them answer through choices she must finally own.**

Formal shorthand:

> **receive → register → organize → answer.**

## Claims not closed by the AV packet

- whether China can sustain public authority during prolonged unpopular governance;
- how she reacts when fans criticize, leave, or divide;
- whether family love can be resisted when the family interest conflicts with public duty;
- durability of China/Hiro reciprocity under serious disagreement or resource disparity;
- stable Producer disagreement, refusal, or separation;
- post-D-CHINA self-naming as top idol;
- support-card evidence about casual register and ordinary friendship;
- cast-wide distributive comparison of wealth, access, production, and institutional legitimacy;
- how habitual `ごきげんよう` is distributed across addressees beyond the Producer, for whom repeated use is already textually established.
