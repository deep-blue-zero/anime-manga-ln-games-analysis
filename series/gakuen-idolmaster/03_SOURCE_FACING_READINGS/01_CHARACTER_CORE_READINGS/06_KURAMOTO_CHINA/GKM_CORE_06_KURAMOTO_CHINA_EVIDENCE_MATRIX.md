## Claim-transition summary

### STRENGTHEN
- genuine conventional beginnerhood;
- `人に好かれる才能` as route center;
- privilege as explicit ethical problem;
- N.I.A. as favorable broad-social battlefield;
- `ふにゃ/きり` persona integration;
- fan support as a real dimension of professional idol power.

### REVISE
- “responsible dependence” → **accountable inheritance / relational legitimacy**;
- “leadership by dependence” → **delegated leadership plus nondelegable accountability**;
- “weakest idol with strongest support” → true origin, incomplete mature description;
- “anti-meritocratic” → critique of **atomized merit**, not of skill/fair comparison;
- “support makes victory legitimate” → legitimacy additionally depends on consent, noncoercion, craft threshold, public accountability, and honest naming of advantage.

### OPEN
- distributive fairness of Hatsuboshi’s support-as-ability doctrine;
- long-term Producer dependency/enclosure;
- whether gratitude becomes compulsive repayment;
- actual performance of student-council governance;
- ability to self-name `トップアイドル` later;
- audiovisual realization of soft/regal state switching.
