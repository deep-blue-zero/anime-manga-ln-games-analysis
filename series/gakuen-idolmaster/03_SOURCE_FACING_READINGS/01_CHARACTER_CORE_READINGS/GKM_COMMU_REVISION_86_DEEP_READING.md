## Mao 018 — Succession and professional agency

The younger students congratulate Mao after H.I.F. and say the senior performers rekindled their own competitive desire (`_01` L61–120). Mao feels the responsibility of `プリマステラ` before feeling fully changed. The Producer describes 100 Pro's proposed interview as both a reveal for Mao and promotion for the previous titleholder, Sena (L135–202). Mao explicitly distinguishes her familiar classmate from the idol she admires and on whom she had superimposed `あの人` under the spotlight (L218–261). The referent is implicit in this script. Mao now describes herself as capable of facing Sena as an idol and receiving something from her.

At the interview, Mao credits younger idols for teaching her different forms of appeal. Sena recognizes those juniors in Mao's performance and proposes that a former actor's observational skills helped her learn (`_02` L25–67). Their private exchange turns `一番星` into paired Venus imagery. Mao asks Sena to keep her personal star status despite succession. Dawn and evening stars describe parallel sources of light, and the joint `ダブル` title is a joke (L154–291). Preserve the official transfer and both women’s evaluations separately.

The 100 Pro president puts ready papers before Mao and the Producer. The Producer asks for review because they concern Mao's life; the president allows until the next day (`_03` L85–140). The script establishes the offer, immediate publicity incentive and deferred signature. The Producer privately wonders whether this was a test. Mao reports anticipation alongside anxiety. The one-option `トップアイドルにする` at L187–189 selects the continuation to the Producer's promise and Mao's own prince vow (L193–210). This is a gated utterance, with no second route. Route to Mao and Sena cores, their relationship synthesis, story state, institutional ledger and branch ledger.

## Misuzu 018 — Relational repair and rehearsal

In the café, Rinha asks when Misuzu and Temari reconciled. Temari yields explanatory initiative to Misuzu, then interrupts her very early starting point. Rinha infers the two repaired contact by attending one another's live performances, and Misuzu explicitly confirms it (`_01` L7–180). Temari draws the same analogy to Rinha. Rinha pushes back, cites episodes before and after the lives, and finally says `別に仲直りなんてしてなかった` after Misuzu repeats her competitive ranking line (L195–264). Rinha's account of the Gokugetsu encounter and public crying is her recalled account here. This A1 object contains **16 exact Rinha speaker messages**. The teasing turn and complaint leave room for a substantive disagreement over what counts as reconciliation. The contact and recollection support the established bounded-repair reading (Relationship Systems I, `RS1-SYNGUP-05`). The scene adds access to Rinha's own position and no group reunion announcement.

Misuzu's café reflection says she did not expect these days to return. A fade to a waiting room marks the embedded `人生最高の日` line, before the script returns to the café where she describes experiencing acceptance as herself (`_01` L279–322, background switches L289 and L303). Preserve that split when citing the line. Part 02 begins with narration placing the rehearsal shortly before the H.I.F. main tournament (`_02` L6). Temari reads Misuzu's focus as intensity, and Misuzu says an unnamed person's advice gives her a comparatively easy way to progress. The Producer points to the gap in repeated stage exposure to `ガラクタロード`. Temari recalls hearing Misuzu's same language when Rinha taught them idol work (L85–204). The Producer internally infers frustration. The two performances discussed within the script do not verify a perceptual comparison.

The rehearsal continues in part 03. Misuzu tests the praise with a same-conditions qualification, then accepts recognition of her work. Temari says a jointly invested peer cannot offer the same outside praise; Misuzu values that Temari will `一緒に困ってくれる` (`_03` L45–125). Producer recognition lets Misuzu say she can rest openly after effort (L172–259). This extends the established account of strategic pacing and links repair to the capacity for both support and competition. Route to Misuzu and Temari cores and matrices, Rinha crosswalk and matrix, Relationship Systems I, the voice/register ledger and named rehearsal story state.

## Misuzu 020 — Campaign fit and relative beauty

## Saki 022 — Building future capabilities

Saki anticipates the cosmetics proposal, notes that more rest leaves capacity for the shoot, and analyzes the brief's blend of healthy freshness and impish allure (`_01` L24–198). Saki helps evaluate costume and positioning. In take one she emphasizes cuteness. She later explicitly asks the Producer to hear and answer her concern that her appearance has already peaked. The Producer treats her appearance as dynamic and names previously considered redesigns (`_02` L38–68, L159–247). Saki's request is an active use of support rather than a retreat from agency.

The second take intentionally changes the presentation. The Producer reports that the staff want both takes. Saki describes putting a trace of future, adult Saki into her fingertips, then depicts the future as selecting parts and building a new self together (`_03` L18–149). The Producer explicitly abandons the prior `大器晩成` model of measuring a pre-existing capacity, and Saki proposes making a larger vessel (L161–186). This is an in-story revision of the Producer's conceptual model, with both present and future versions valuable. The two proposed edits of the advertisement are reported decisions; the script cannot determine how they look or whether both were released. Route to the Saki core, Producer dyad, professional-state and evidence matrix.

The episode ends with Ume declaring that `現実` (read `あたし`) will surpass `理想` (read `ライバル`, L127–186). Ume claims her present self as a challenger to the Producer's ideal, with the Producer revising that ideal in response to the shoot. This elaborates Ume's ambition beyond the external comparison with Saki. Route to Ume's core/matrix, Producer and Hanami relationship rows, and artistic-development ledger. The ruby and the distinction between a one-off improvised cue and a repeatable capability are material.

## China 018 — Incumbency, succession and style

China has won H.I.F. and is called Prima Stella, while the experience feels increasingly unreal. The Producer recommends the ordinary uniform with `一番星の証` read `ピン` (`_01` L134–218). China treats this as a historical custom and adds a procession; the Producer endorses the uniform and pin, but has never heard of that latter detail. The script marks imagined Hiro explicitly as `心の篠澤広` (L101–118). China’s loss of momentum does not supply an observed Hiro intervention.

In the schoolyard, students' affectionate response differs from the reaction to Sena that Ume recalls. China names a concern about dignity. Misuzu, Ume and Hiro each join her attempt to produce a stronger public character, and Tsubame stops the improvised procession (`_02` L106–346). In part 03, the Producer validates the current electorate's choice and China's own style. Kanae calls for preserving China's characteristic qualities while commissioning a future president's uniform (`_03` L48–235). China postpones the redesign until after term, explicitly stating that she is the **next** president and has yet to become president (L251–321). This preserves a transitional office state and makes personal growth a prerequisite China sets herself. Route to China's and Tsubame's cores, Kanae evidence, student-council succession, political imagery and institution ledgers. Keep the early goal of outward gravitas alongside the positive audience response to affection.

China remains attached to her singular `先生` and accepts an invited `家庭教師` within that arrangement. Hiro, Ume and Saki accompany China to owl's live. Owl later reports that China mastered relatively few cues, applied the ones she learned at a high level and increased her vocal ability beyond prediction (L143–229). This supplies concrete scope for China's iterative growth and shows a structured mentorship that remains open to individual results. Owl's judgment establishes the trainer's assessment. The text supplies no independent measurement of vocals or live arrangement. Route to China's core and matrix, owl and Serina institutional/side-character records, the Saki/Ume/Hiro cross-track entries and song identity ledger.

## Lilja 022 — Sincerity inside a role and participation as a buyer

Lilja serves sweets learned from Misuzu and mentions Sumika will help finish the remainder. The second-round campaign offer unsettles her because she doubts both halves of the required brief. She accepts after seeing the costume (`_01` L42–95, L130–244). Lilja tries the imperative `可愛いって……言え！`, then drops it in concern for the Producer. The Producer notices her genuine enthusiasm for the fragrance and reports that the client responds well to it. He tells Lilja she can remain `自然体` while performing, a combination Lilja still finds puzzling (`_02` L53–129, L190–242). Alongside the support story about cosplay craft, this supplies a concrete case of trying a role without requiring that the public role absorb her private identity.

After filming, Lilja wants to purchase the fragrance with her own money to feel the result of her work. The Producer reports that he buys her merchandise privately and identifies himself as both fan and Producer (`_03` L109–197). Lilja names and welcomes that second role. The money and products become evidence of a participant testing the audience's position, with professional and private payment distinguished by the characters. Lilja also reports Sumika's makeup instruction and references professional products from the shoot (L65–105). Route to the Lilja and Sumika cores, Lilja's Producer dyad and the commercial production ledger.

## Sumika 018 — Using the title, pausing REVERSI and renewed family contact

The title brings students to Sumika for fashion, dance and makeup instruction. She distinguishes this high attention from what she fears about her old `神童` label: she wants to answer requests from people who have found courage to change. The same popularity puts pressure on her lunch break and raises an issue over copied skirt lengths, which she sets out to stop (`_01` L14–251). This expands the existing self-authored-expectation account into the role of an incumbent with a bounded institutional responsibility.

Temari asks about the `休止` after hearing from Lilja. Sumika explicitly says it is a pause (`解散するわけじゃない`), with separate ambitions following their shared promise. Temari says the pause feels wasteful, supports their decision, and declares her own pursuit of the title (`_02` L24–165). Both peer recognition and competitive challenge belong in the same row. Do not turn this state into the fate of every REVERSI route.

In part 03 Sumika's mother calls, recognizes the title and says the live helped her understand Sumika's motives. Each acknowledges harm in their earlier conflict. Sumika says she won't return to ballet, identifies how its training still serves her idol work, and accepts her mother's support. Her mother invites her to Hokkaido, and Sumika plans to bring Lilja. The father still believes a hurtful chat message, and Sumika realizes she forgot to follow up (`_03` L139–335). This is direct evidence of a specific repair with the mother and an open follow-through with the father. It strengthens the core's claim that ballet knowledge survives a change of career. Route to Sumika and Lilja cores, REVERSI, family and succession rows, and Temari's cross-track record.

## Temari 021 — Professional reward includes praise

Temari reports a taunt from Nadeshiko and Shion about Gokugetsu's airline campaign. The script stages this within her account, then returns to the Producer. The Producer reveals that Temari has already been selected for the first Hatsuboshi apparel tie-up and describes a successful bid that relies on her existing achievement (`_01` L47–273). Treat the airline details as the scene's report of the rivals' claims. Temari recognizes the delay between filming and next summer's release and decides to bring back a usable performance experience in the meantime. The Producer and Temari explicitly call themselves professionals while preparing (`_02` L22–123, L161–225).

Temari says she was brusque despite the crew's care and struggled to receive praise. She explains that Misuzu or Rinha was usually beside her when she was praised, and that the old appearance complex still makes praise of idol-Temari feel impersonal. The Producer says an idol has a duty to receive praise. Temari calls praise compensation (`報酬`) and requests an immediate attempt at practice (`_03` L29–169). This adds explicit reflective evidence that Temari's technical confidence can coexist with difficulty taking approval personally. It connects to Misuzu's differently framed concern about fitting the same campaign. Route to Temari's core, Producer and SyngUp relationship records, voice/register and professional-state ledgers.
