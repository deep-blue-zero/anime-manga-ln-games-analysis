## Principal findings

1. Temari is organized by continuous escape from a hated childhood self, not merely by a gap between cool exterior and needy interior.
2. Self-love initially depends on effort and ascent; late present-tense self-love is genuine but not yet proven independent of achievement.
3. Her exceptional singing has a real energy/stamina cost, making SyngUp! support materially necessary.
4. She experienced care as proof of inferiority, obstruction, guilt, and dependency.
5. SyngUp! failed because its members did not share one understanding of pace, support, rivalry, or even their founding promise.
6. Reconciliation with Misuzu does not restore the unit; it permits friendship and later rivalry under separation.
7. Rinha is teacher, model, rival, and one of the authors of Temari’s cool persona.
8. Imitation becomes inheritance rather than fraud once influence can be admitted and transformed.
9. The Producer relationship replaces containment with chosen dangerous partnership; it is reciprocal but remains structurally asymmetric and potentially enabling.
10. `クール` matures from borrowed image to `青い炎`: complete combustion disciplined into form.
11. The artificial-wings sequence culminates in airplane and rocket imagery, reversing the shame attached to socially produced ability.
12. Food/body material is central to self-hatred, care, pleasure, trust, and control—not detachable comedy.
13. N.I.A. succeeds when public production integrates Temari’s contradiction rather than preserving pure mystery.
14. U1 independently shows she can inhabit a unit with direct conflict, distributed leadership, and nonfused relationships.
15. The V1 SyngUp! thesis is substantially confirmed but revised through physiology, incompatible promises, Rinha’s protection, and Producer-dependency risk.
