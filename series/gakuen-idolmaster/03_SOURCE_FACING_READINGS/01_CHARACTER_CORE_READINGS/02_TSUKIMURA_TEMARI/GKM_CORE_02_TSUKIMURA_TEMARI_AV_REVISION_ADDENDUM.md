# TSUKIMURA TEMARI — AV REVISION ADDENDUM

## 0. Purpose

This document records how direct music, MV, 3DMV, dialogue-performance, and scene-audio evidence changes the provisional textual character model established in `GKM_CORE_02_TSUKIMURA_TEMARI.md`.

The original core reading is preserved rather than overwritten. Phase 3 is designed to make interpretive change visible.

---

# 1. Core thesis

## Textual formulation

> Temari is the idol of authored combustion: a girl who builds artificial wings because she cannot love the self that stands still, then learns that imitation, dependence, bodily need, and emotional exposure do not invalidate those wings.

## AV revision

**Verdict: CONFIRMED / STRENGTHENED.**

The audiovisual corpus identifies the mechanism as **compressed overexposure**. Temari’s control does not signify low affect. It is the technical compression of high affect into selected form.

Revised formulation:

> **Temari is the idol of authored combustion. Her audiovisual identity consists in compressing excess feeling into deliberately selected form, then enlarging that form until inherited influence, childish need, domestic softness, theatrical artifice, comedy, unit belonging, and chosen dependence can all be cited as materials of one answerable self.**

---

# 2. “Fake cool girl versus true needy girl”

## Textual status

The core reading already rejected this binary, but direct performance was pending.

## AV revision

**Verdict: REJECTED decisively.**

The complete corpus shows multiple organized states:

- compact professional control;
- abrasive/direct correction;
- childlike panic/need;
- combustive protest;
- lowered intimate trust;
- formal public address;
- playful/cute performance.

No one state functions as the singular authentic voice. The developmental change lies in Temari’s increasing ability to choose what a transition communicates.

---

# 3. `青い炎`

## Textual status

The blue-flame metaphor was the proposed mature model of `クール`, but remained text-only.

## AV revision

**Verdict: CONFIRMED, with an important narrowing.**

`ガラクタロード` commu and 3DMV strongly support the metaphor:

- cool external palette and contained posture;
- high-activity full-mix profile;
- movement from compression toward open command;
- rapid conversion of received concept into public manifesto;
- visible post-rehearsal exhaustion.

Narrowing:

`青い炎` is an organizing principle, not a demand that all Temari music be dark, severe, or high-energy. `Unhappy Light`, `叶えたい、ことばかり`, `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！`, and Re;IRIS show that mature Temari can authorize quiet, comic, and shared modes.

---

# 4. Artificial wings and authenticity

## Textual status

The core reading argued that construction, imitation, and support do not make Temari counterfeit.

## AV revision

**Verdict: CONFIRMED / STRENGTHENED.**

The principal musical sequence embodies the argument:

- `Luna say maybe`: resemblance is disowned but performed;
- `アイヴイ`: plurality becomes curated image;
- `一体いつから`: other people’s methods become learnable techniques;
- `ガラクタロード`: the constructed concept becomes public category authorship.

`ENDLESS DANCE` and `Howling over the World` further show that overt theatrical or digital artifice can extend rather than corrupt identity.

The revised claim is stronger:

> authenticity in Temari’s arc means answerable authorship, not absence of derivation.

---

# 5. Softness and ordinary need

## Textual risk

The combustion model risked making all truthful Temari expression dependent on crisis or maximum effort.

## AV revision

**Verdict: NARROWED by counterevidence.**

`Unhappy Light` is the decisive correction. Its domestic room, bed, routine, cat, child imagery, pale light, and low-brightness/low-flatness full-mix profile make quiet vulnerability official image material.

The problem is not softness. The problem is softness imposed as a fixed inferior role.

This distinction should govern later body/care analysis:

- chosen quiet can be authorship;
- imposed limitation can be containment;
- the same outward behavior may have different ethical meaning depending on control and context.

---

# 6. Producer relationship

## Textual status

The core reading described the Producer as a chosen dangerous partner: more enabling and authorship-oriented than SyngUp! care, but still structurally asymmetric.

## AV revision

**Verdict: STRENGTHENED and specified as reciprocal co-authorship.**

Evidence for Temari’s agency:

- she asks how she appears in `アイヴイ`;
- judges the Producer’s selection;
- requests given-name address before the H.I.F. final;
- receives `青い炎`, recognizes it, and makes it public;
- reads his tears in Dear 037;
- lends a handkerchief and returns care;
- uses present-tense mutual liking rather than passive devotion.

Unresolved risk:

- most lowered intimate speech remains concentrated in one witness;
- the Producer controls bodily, temporal, artistic, and institutional conditions;
- accurate understanding can become unusually authoritative;
- his willingness to optimize full combustion may enable harm.

Revised formulation:

> **reciprocal co-authorship inside persistent institutional asymmetry.**

# 7. Misuzu

## Textual status

Misuzu’s care was materially necessary but relationally immobilizing; later viability depended on rivalry and separation.

## AV revision

**Verdict: CONFIRMED / STRENGTHENED.**

Dear 004, 019, 022, and `一体いつから` commu show four different Misuzu functions:

- body knowledge and protection;
- fixed-role risk;
- independent artistic world capable of making Temari a fan;
- expressive technique Temari can learn without becoming Misuzu.

This rules out both simple condemnation and simple vindication. Misuzu becomes viable when her care is one technique among others and when she can remain separately ambitious.

Her own core pass remains necessary.

---

# 8. Rinha

## Textual status

Rinha was model, rival, teacher, public shield, and co-author of Temari’s cool persona.

## AV revision

**Verdict: CONFIRMED strongly.**

Dear 016’s performed rupture establishes that the attachment is filial and professional at once. Temari’s childlike collapse does not reveal a non-idol truth underneath performance; it reveals that the idol identity contains a lineage she had been ashamed to name.

`Campus mode!!` commu and later recognition shift imitation toward cited inheritance.

---

# 9. SyngUp! ending

## Textual status

The core reading rejected reunion as the necessary healthy endpoint and proposed that a disbandment live could complete the unit without restoring it.

## AV revision

**Verdict: STRENGTHENED.**

The Dear 017/023/027 staging repeatedly preserves separation:

- different remembered promises;
- confrontation rather than sentimental equivalence;
- a proposed performance as an act of ending;
- three-person recognition without visual fusion.

The likely healthy form remains:

> shared acknowledgment and authored ending, not restoration of the old dependency structure.

---

# 10. Self-love

## Textual status

Temari’s `今日の私が一番好き` was treated as genuine but potentially achievement-dependent.

## AV revision

**Verdict: GENUINENESS CONFIRMED; UNCONDITIONALITY UNRESOLVED.**

Dear 037’s ordinary setting, food, humor, care reversal, soft direct gaze, and present-tense grammar make the affirmation credible. It is not merely a public title speech.

But the timing remains maximal success. Temari immediately opens another horizon. Later event/support and future-route evidence must test self-regard during prolonged nonachievement.

---

# 11. Unit capacity

## Textual status

`U1` suggested that Temari could inhabit distributed leadership and direct conflict without reproducing SyngUp!.

## AV revision

**Verdict: CONFIRMED within `U1`.**

`雨上がりのアイリス` 3DMV uses rotating focus, shared gaze, proximity, and warm group formation. Temari remains legible without monopolizing center.

The commu preserves jealousy and exclusive Producer desire. Unit capacity is real; possessive attachment remains.

# 13. Consequence for the future definitive monograph

The future Phase-7 monograph should organize Temari around five linked systems:

1. **authored combustion** — excessive feeling routed through selected form;
2. **state-switch authorship** — increasing freedom to decide what voice and bodily transitions mean;
3. **construction and inheritance** — imitation, technical help, and public image as real materials of selfhood;
4. **body and care** — the pressure vessel, its managers, its pleasure, and its cost;
5. **co-authorship and enclosure** — relationships that increase agency while concentrating dependence.

Events and support cards must test whether the capacities shown in climax-level Dear Idol and song commus generalize to ordinary life.
