# TSUKIMURA TEMARI — EVIDENCE AND FALSIFICATION MATRIX

## 0. Use of this document

This matrix prevents the core reading from becoming a self-confirming interpretation. Every major claim is paired with:

- supporting source families;
- counterevidence or an alternative explanation;
- current confidence;
- the later evidence that could strengthen, narrow, or overturn it.

`D-TEMARI`, `P1–P3`, and `U1` remain separate continuity scopes. Branch variants are evidence of conditional response, not additive biography.

## 10. Phase-3 falsification priorities

The most consequential possible corrections are:

1. Misuzu’s core pass may show that Temari substantially misreads restraint as possession.
2. Rinha evidence may show that Temari’s demand for continued rivalry burdens someone who genuinely wants to stop.
3. Support cards may reveal that Producer exclusivity is less operationally total than the core route suggests.
4. AV evidence may show that Temari’s repertoire is sonically discontinuous, weakening the authored-combustion musical thesis.
5. Post-victory events may establish stable self-regard independent of winning, overturning the “movement-dependent” limitation.
6. Ordinary food/rest stories may show meaningful pleasure without productivity, softening the labor critique.
7. Future unit material may reunite SyngUp! in a transformed form, requiring the non-reunion thesis to become historical rather than final.

Until those tests are complete, the main document should be read as a strong provisional model, not a closed interpretation.
