## Major V2 claim transitions

### STRENGTHEN — No.2 as refuge

The old insight that `学園No.2` is a comfort zone survives but becomes more precise. The title lets Tsubame preserve real pride while maintaining Sena as the one permitted exception. Dear 006 makes the avoidance structure explicit: she had been pretending to pursue while already accepting the wall and looking downward for reassurance.

### REVISE — “becoming Sena's real rival” is intermediate, not terminal

Dear 006 provides the genuine-rival declaration, but Dear 021–024 supplies the later individuation test. Tsubame's decisive development is the ability to remain an idol even if Sena disappears.

### STRENGTHEN — independent hero ideal

Dear 019 and the N.I.A. material establish that the dark-hero vocation is not merely Producer branding. A childhood hero aspiration predates the mature rivalry grammar. Tsubame wants an idol who refuses abandonment and gives courage through confrontation.

### REVISE — saving Sena

The mature Tsubame does not assert that Sena must remain an idol for Tsubame's sake. Dear 024 explicitly allows genuine retirement if it is truly Sena's will. The intervention targets false resignation and culminates in Sena's own renewed choice.

### REJECT — Tsubame remains wholly dependent on Sena for idol identity

Dear 024 directly falsifies this. Tsubame states that becoming a hero-like idol is independent of Sena's presence and that she will continue being an idol.

### STRENGTHEN — rivalry becomes renewable

Dear 027 ends with a future-facing wager, not a terminal crown. Tsubame and Sena can use contest to keep each other unfinished rather than treat victory as closure.

### REJECT — technique exhausts idol value

Dear 027 explicitly recognizes that technical skill alone does not exhaust idol attraction. The late `スター性` problem becomes a major bridge into AV and later synthesis.
