## Rivalry survives the title

Tsubame enters her last winter H.I.F. seeking Sena’s title, but she retrospectively describes her break with the old `学園No.2` posture as reconstructing pride. The ruby `プライド` reads both `殻` and `牙` in Dear 028 lines 236 and 243. The script’s distinction is between the former armor and a new means of competing. Tsubame can recover the informal No.2 label by placing second in summer and still call it an `曖昧な幻想` before the winter final (`035` 59–87). Tsubame’s explanation treats that label as mutable and inferior to an earned, public, time-limited office. This strengthens TSB-001’s distinction between actual standing and a defensive self-description.

The principal new outcome is C1 within D-TSUBAME. Tsubame wins winter H.I.F. and Sena accepts the loss, explicitly reports both frustration and the stronger pleasure of competing, hands Tsubame the title, and promises renewed rivalry at 100 Pro (`036` 221–517). Tsubame asks Sena to pursue her next, reversing who sets the mark. The victory fulfills the practical consequence of the winter wager (`027`). Tsubame’s move into professional work and choice of Producer follow in `037` 76–174. Those are a reported offer and a choice. The route stops before a demonstrated long professional career or a world championship.

The new P3 final-failure branch supplies the counterfactual control. The headmaster names Sena, and Tsubame congratulates her for four consecutive titles. Tsubame expects Sena to keep performing irrespective of their promise and decides to turn professional and continue competing (`final-failure-01` 5–81). A selection loss is a distinct ending. Tsubame accepts some responsibility for her concentration but says `今はまだ、前を向けんな` and Producer proposes rest before restarting (`selection-failure-01` 15–65). TSB-065 strengthens across the final winner/loser contrast and remains bounded at selection loss, where future work is encouraged and immediate recovery is expressly unavailable. No P3 win ending is supplied by the four new character-specific P3 fragments. The two `normal-02` fragments prepare for rounds. They do not establish placement or champion identity.

## Star quality and reciprocal help

Producer’s three conditions in `029` 100–167 are a character-specific pedagogical hypothesis: distinctive individuality, confidence shown in bearing and `物語` that draws others to follow a future. The producer frames targeted rescue as insufficient to reach a mixed audience or a panel acting as judges (`029` 24–85). Tsubame describes experiencing awakened Sena as physically larger (`029` 108–113), a report of subjective presence rather than a staging measurement. Producer later reduces his explanation to `ギャップ`, anchored in the school’s knowledge of the severe stage persona and everyday diligence (`030` 413–447). Those explanations differ in abstraction and are complementary in this episode. They do not prove a universal scoring rule.

Tsubame arrives at gratitude through a disagreement about the cost of reform. She is already revising the H.I.F. proposal so student input is heard (`030` 66–81). When Sena reads her assurance as self-persuasion, Tsubame distinguishes `不満` from `感傷` and names loneliness at rapid change (`030` 136–213). Tsubame resolves to return the school’s support through performance, with title victory as proof of her gratitude’s scale (`030` 233–354). Producer tells her to keep the `牙` of pride and her anger at her own weakness while adding gratitude (`031` 13–50). After defeating Mao, Tsubame and Mao each recognize the other’s progress (`032` 7–67). Their exchange supports a perceived change in brilliance and leaves its precise performance form open.

The strongest textual expansion is Tsubame’s revision from `私の物語` to `私たちの物語` (`034` 406–443). Misuzu’s trust, Shion’s reminder about fans, rival resistance and Producer’s care all count. Tsubame names Producer’s anxiety about resting her and promises to make his decision right (`034` 456–498). Producer identifies himself as the target of her effort to help, and Tsubame accepts that identification (`034` 502–542). The next chapter scripts Producer’s `俺のヒーローになってください` as a one-option player prompt, then Tsubame accepts and promises to take him to the star (`035` 312–341). Producer casts himself as `ヒロイン` earlier in this exchange. The hero language is reciprocal: Tsubame says Producer had been her hero, calls him `私のヒーロー`, and describes actively choosing to walk with him when 100 Pro gives her a choice (`037` 119–237). The offer and her decision strengthen TSB-018 and TSB-059. They leave formal role asymmetry and the legitimacy of earlier manipulation separately reviewable.

The public address enacts a different scale of obligation. Tsubame thanks fans, peers, juniors she learned from, trainers, teachers, staff and the school. She names the opportunity cost of narrowing her attention to Sena and pledges to carry the title into the professional world. `私の背中を追ってこい` turns the seniority relation into an invitation/challenge to later idols (`036` 644–1036). The “follow my back” formulation retains hierarchy while creating a successor horizon. TSB-062 can be strengthened as a textually corroborated account of reciprocal care as one enabling condition within this route. Keep the AV signature open. Gratitude, technique, rivalry, audience familiarity and rest all have separate textual warrants. The line about rescuing Producer supplies neither a necessary nor a sufficient cause of victory.

## Common repertoire and voluntary rest

The `ガラクタロード` three-part idol communication is C1 within the episode and C2 relative to Produce and Dear. Tsubame credits Misuzu’s efficient self-paced training after accepting Producer’s admission that his former high-risk tactics lacked reproducibility (`part_01` 63–182). Tsubame then challenges Producer’s selective consultation. Producer commits to `報告・連絡・相談`; Tsubame permits occasional improvisation (`part_02` 6–131). This adds negotiation evidence to TSB-017 and TSB-037. Neither speaks for other idols’ consent to comparable risks.

Tsubame calls the song `100プロの代表曲` and has prepared it for a professional future. Producer hears near-perfection as potentially remote from `ライブ感`. Tsubame proposes deliberate `崩す`, voices discomfort with showing pleasure and accepts a cue to look at fans during a live (`part_03` 30–194). The scene authorizes an artistic question about the portrayal of enjoyment within disciplined public performance. Whether a specific take realizes the prescription remains a performance claim. The A1 music cue `sud_bgm_adv_inst-all-016` at 7 marks an instrumental in the commu, and the separate Tsubame solo or rendered live requires its own source identity.

The two P3 activities supply C3 evidence under distinct choices. Tsubame’s hidden eagerness for a robot-anime menu, public rejection of a proposed cute photo, and willingness to test adapted Japanese food belong to three different responses. At the aquarium, Tsubame affirms jellyfish for recuperation, admires the giant isopod’s armor-like form, or elects to prioritize Producer’s rest depending on the choice. Use these as tests of TSB-033, TSB-041 and TSB-043. The shared lines give the safest trait evidence for calming routines, and the choices expand her represented behavioral range.
