## Phase-3 evidence judgment

The core model is well supported by the Dear longitudinal route and reinforced by P1/P2/M material. The claims carrying the greatest later-phase falsification burden are **TSB-037** (paternalism risk), **TSB-062** (star quality through rescue), and the broad synthesis **TSB-066**. These should be actively attacked rather than merely confirmed in Phases 4–7.


## Source Lock 1.1 — transcript refresh, 2026-11-12

**STRENGTHEN** TSB-001, TSB-025, TSB-040 and TSB-065 with the title's bounded tenure and renewed rivalry. **STRENGTHEN** TSB-017 with the later commitment to consultation (`adv_cidol-atbm-3-015_02.txt` L006–131), and keep TSB-037 open given continuing tactical assertiveness. **TSB-066, SL1.1 note (2026-11-15, GKM-SAA-095) — REVISE (qualifying).** In SL1.1 the Producer explicitly subordinates rivalry to rescue: Tsubame's real reason for wanting to beat Sena was to save her, so her essence is not that of an idol made for fighting, and Tsubame accepts this as the explanation of her summer loss (`adv_dear_atbm_029` L171–L210; L191–L195; his theory, which he marks as his own view at L199). In Dear 031 he tells her to keep the `牙` of pride and her anger at her own weakness alongside gratitude (`adv_dear_atbm_031` L13–L50). Within SL1.1, "heroic rivalry" is therefore qualified: rescue and gratitude are primary, and rivalry is instrumental but retained. Counterevidence: Dear 031 itself keeps the fang, and Sena promises renewed rivalry at 100 Pro (`adv_dear_atbm_036` L221–L517).
