## Revised compact model

> **Hanami Ume is the idol of renewable rivalry realized as reciprocal propulsion. She receives fear, comparison, inheritance, fans, and rivals into a body that briefly compresses around pressure, then returns them as larger motion—raised fists, open arms, direct address, and invitations to run.**
