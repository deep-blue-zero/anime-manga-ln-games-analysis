## Bounded thesis

> **Ume is the idol of renewable rivalry: a slow-start, explosively adaptive former athlete whose dream begins as pursuit of Saki, becomes self-authored idol vocation after she finally wins, and then expands into a circulation system in which rivals teach, fans add strength, songs carry changing inheritance, fear gives desire weight, and victory is meaningful because it creates the next challenge rather than ending the relation.**

The largest V2 gains are:

1. **“Fearless Ume” is revised.** Dear 009 and 033 show fear intensifying when equality and large dreams make loss meaningful. Her courage is continued action under fear.
2. **Directness is separated from simplicity.** Produce Story's secret-box passage proves Ume can openly emote while also compartmentalizing difficult mixed states.
3. **Saki is upgraded from motivation to constitutive infrastructure.** She shapes Ume's body, training, diet, competition ethics, learning model, songs, and early idol identity.
4. **Individuation no longer means separation from Saki.** Dear 019–020 establishes differentiation without severance: Ume will not automatically follow Saki, because she now loves idolhood for independent reasons.
5. **Producer's role is revised from liberator to accelerator/widener.** He supplies the first real start dash and deliberately exposes Ume to rivals/teachers other than Saki.
6. **Ume's talent is revised from “physical monster” to embodied abstraction.** She begins slowly but can extract another person's performance logic and reintegrate it into her own body.
7. **Fan-love is recognized as learned.** `Campus mode!!`, China/Hiro, defeat, and inherited repertoire make audience relation substantially more mature.
8. **Numbers vs heart is rejected as a false binary.** Measurable capacity remains real; the corpus shows it is insufficient without attraction, interpretation, history, and audience circulation.
9. **Competition becomes an ethics.** Full victory celebration gives losers permission to grieve; false humility can insult defeated rivals; legitimate wins require real encounter; rivals can be teachers.
10. **Burnout romanticization is explicitly revised.** Dear 024 allows the destructive “burn to the end” fantasy; Dear 027–029 replaces it with rivals adding fuel and relighting one another.
11. **Songs become longitudinal evidence.** `グースーピー` can become “too small” for the later Ume and must be reinterpreted rather than discarded; `真っ白いページと水彩の主人公` becomes the late `全部盛り` self-history.
12. **“World's strongest” is generative, not merely dominant.** Dear 037 defines strength as making the world smile, making idolhood feel fun/interesting, and scattering heat among other idols.
13. **Family resource inequality is explicit.** Hanami athletic expertise, training infrastructure, and professional support materially enable Ume's acceleration and must remain visible in institutional synthesis.
14. **Cute recognition remains an open AV issue.** CIDOL 012 shows Ume does not want to be perceived only as a weapon/talent; she wants a close Producer to see her as cute too.
