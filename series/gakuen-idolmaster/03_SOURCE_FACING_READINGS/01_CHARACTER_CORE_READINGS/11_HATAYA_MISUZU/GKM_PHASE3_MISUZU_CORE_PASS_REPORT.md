## Governing result

The Phase-3 bounded thesis is **night-sky sovereignty**.

Misuzu's central contradiction is not “lazy versus ambitious.” It is that the form in which she prefers to live—slow, quiet, domestic, low-friction, caring—supports an exceptionally large appetite for attention, indispensability and artistic dominance.

The route develops that appetite without purifying it. The major movement is:

> **post-SyngUp! depression → chosen sustainable pace → admitted possessive ambition → conflict and plural rivalry → meaningful defeat → temporary compulsive overexertion → return to owned tempo → Prima Stella → reopened infinity.**

## Strongest V2 corrections

### 1. `マイペース` is not pure invulnerable confidence

Dear 037 retrospectively establishes that Misuzu's low confidence at the route opening was genuine. SyngUp!'s collapse had depressed her. Her pace remains a philosophy and strength, but its early form also shelters a wounded person.

**Transition:** REVISE.

### 2. Care is neither pure benevolence nor fake manipulation

Dear 004 and ordinary-life events show genuine pleasure in service. Dear 005/027 show why being needed is emotionally rewarding. Care is both gift and attachment technology.

**Transition:** STRENGTHEN + QUALIFY.

### 3. SyngUp! does not need permanent restoration

Temari and Rinha remain precious while moving separately. A possible one-night reunion becomes meaningful precisely because it is not compulsory return.

**Transition:** REJECT the restoration-as-health model.

### 4. Direct conflict becomes relational competence

Misuzu learns that quiet containment can fail. `ちゃんと喧嘩をしましょう。わたしたち、友達なんですから` is a major ethical hinge.

**Transition:** STRENGTHEN.

### 5. Misuzu is not emotionally unshaken by defeat

Dear 022 preserves resentment as fuel; Dear 032 reveals private crying after earlier defeat. Calm presentation is emotion governance, not absence of pain.

**Transition:** REJECT.

### 6. Running is not inherently a betrayal of self

The Hanami training period materially improves her. It becomes destructive because broken-promise distress turns it into compulsive self-punishment. Chosen intense effort remains available to her.

**Transition:** REVISE.

### 7. Producer is both good environment designer and ethically non-neutral manipulator

The road-clearing model is strongly supported. So is deliberate use of jealousy, rivals and the “lost” training period. Effectiveness should not be confused with moral innocence.

**Transition:** STRENGTHEN + COMPLICATE.

### 8. The night sky is not a closed final enclosure

Dear 037 recognizes unseen stars beyond the visible heavens. The Prima Stella summit therefore enlarges rather than completes the dream.

**Transition:** REVISE.
