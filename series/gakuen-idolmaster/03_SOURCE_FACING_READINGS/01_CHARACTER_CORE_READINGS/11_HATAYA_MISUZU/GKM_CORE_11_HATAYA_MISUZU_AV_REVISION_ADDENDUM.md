## 1. Executive transition

### Textual formulation

**Night-sky sovereignty**: post-collapse withdrawal becomes chosen sustainable pace, admitted possessive ambition, plural rivalry, summit, and reopened infinity.

### AV formulation

**Centripetal sovereignty** is the mechanism. Misuzu creates a low-friction center around which attention organizes. Softness is not separate from conquest; it is the medium through which care, enclosure, need, and public charisma become persuasive.

## 3. Canonical replacement paragraph

The following paragraph should govern future short-form character modeling:

> **Hataya Misuzu is a highly capable idol who protects the right to author her own tempo. Her softness is neither a fake mask nor proof of harmlessness: it is a centripetal performance technology that makes care, attention, possession, and public charisma feel continuous. Defeat genuinely wounds her, and frantic effort can make her less coherent without making effort itself alien to her. She grows by letting conflict answer her care, permitting Temari and Rinha separate paths, integrating tears and dependence into public authority, and turning nocturnal enclosure into a night-sky identity large enough to hold rivals and future possibility. Whether that sky ultimately preserves the independence of its stars remains an open ethical question.**
