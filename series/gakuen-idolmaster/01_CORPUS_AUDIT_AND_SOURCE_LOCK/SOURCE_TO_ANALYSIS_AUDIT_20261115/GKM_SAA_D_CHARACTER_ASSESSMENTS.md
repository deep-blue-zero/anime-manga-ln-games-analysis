represented through Source Lock 1.2 (thirteen original idols plus Hiwatari Kanae) and the distributed subjects: Kaya Rinha, the Producer, families/staff/institutions,
and the event, support-card and relationship-system layers.

register and the index (`B_FINDINGS_INDEX.md`). Three integration drafters produced the sections below from the frozen records. The principal
reviewed them for consistency with the register and corrected one register anomaly that the drafters flagged (a misfiled reviewer note, CMP04-C005 → CMP09-C005).
