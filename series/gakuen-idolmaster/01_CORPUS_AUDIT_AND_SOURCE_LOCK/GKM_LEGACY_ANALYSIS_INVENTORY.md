## 5. Legacy claim revision vocabulary

Every imported V1 claim will eventually receive one of:

- **CONFIRMED** - V2 evidence substantially supports the claim as stated;
- **STRENGTHENED** - V2 adds broader or more direct support;
- **NARROWED** - core insight survives but scope/wording was too broad;
- **REVISED** - significant reformulation is required;
- **REJECTED** - source evidence contradicts or fails to support it;
- **UNRESOLVED** - evidence/continuity remains insufficient.

Phase 0 does not award positive verdicts. All substantive V1 claims enter the ledger as **UNRESOLVED**.
