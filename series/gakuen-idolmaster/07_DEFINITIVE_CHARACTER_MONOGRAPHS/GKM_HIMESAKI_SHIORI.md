# Himesaki Shiori

Shiori (汐莉) is Rinami’s younger sister, still in elementary school when Rinami comes home in Dear 024. She knows which version of the honey-pickled plums her mother sent, what her father has chosen for an alarm, and which question will make a visiting stranger explain himself. She has a claim on Rinami’s attention that predates the public’s. She is also one of the people who helped create that public. Her difficulty begins when the sister she wants to tell everyone about becomes somebody she has to wait to hear from. [S1, S2](GKM_HIMESAKI_SHIORI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md)

## The girl who makes the call

Shiori calls because Rinami has not been phoning. Rinami says lessons have kept her busy. Shiori asks whether she is eating their mother’s plums because they should be good for fatigue and for her throat. When Rinami asks about their father, Shiori describes him watching the streams, making goods to give the neighbors and using Rinami’s recorded voice as his alarm. Shiori has been spreading the word at school, too. She likes being the person who can tell her sister how pleased people are. This is something Shiori can do from home, even though she has only a partial view of the life those people admire. [S1](GKM_HIMESAKI_SHIORI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md)

It takes a few more turns for her to say how the change has felt. Perhaps she is a *little* lonely now that Rinami has become everybody’s older sister. She can tell Rinami this and still promise to watch her. A reading in which Shiori resents the fans would have to explain why she brings them news herself. A reading in which pride makes the arrangement costless would have to discount the fact that she picked up the phone. Rinami tells her that her Producer said something similar. Shiori does not pursue that comparison. What she tells her sister is that she is glad she seems well. [S1](GKM_HIMESAKI_SHIORI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md)

## Finding a way to give care back

At home Shiori offers watermelon. When Rinami asks what she would like for lunch, Shiori asks for pizza. Rinami starts working out what she can make with toast. Shiori explains that the point was to *order* the pizza so her sister could rest. For a moment the child responsible for being looked after is the one who notices the cost of making the meal. Rinami replies that keeping busy helps take her mind off things. Their family is dealing with their father’s hospitalization, and Rinami has assured her mother that she can manage the house and look after Shiori. Both sisters have understood something true about the other. Their conversation leaves open what they actually ate. When the Producer visits, Rinami serves him the family udon and says Shiori loves it. Here we get Shiori’s usual preference from her sister. The request for pizza was Shiori’s own attempt to give her a break. [S2, S3](GKM_HIMESAKI_SHIORI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md)

Her interest in idol school has the same ordinary specificity. What kinds of classes are there? What do they do at lessons? It sounds fun to her. Rinami answers with the sore feet that come with doing it every day. Shiori wants access to the hours of her sister’s life that she has not seen. The questions give her no settled ambition of her own to enter the academy. [S2](GKM_HIMESAKI_SHIORI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md)

## On whose terms can the Producer stay?

Shiori guesses that the young man at the door is Rinami’s boyfriend. The explanation that he is a little brother is, to her, still stranger. She objects that nobody told her a new brother was appearing, that he is older, and that Rinami is *her* big sister. Rinami’s reply makes excellent sense for a performer. She can be an older sister to fans on either side of her own age. It does little to explain why a child must accept a new kinship term in her own home. [S2, S3](GKM_HIMESAKI_SHIORI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md)

Later, Shiori warns the Producer that he will get bitten by insects if he works in the garden in short sleeves. Her next words reiterate that she refuses to acknowledge him as a younger brother. The warning is worth taking seriously. She is perfectly able to care whether something unpleasant happens to him while continuing to object to what he calls himself. She tests his claim to understand Rinami. When he says her appeal cannot be reduced to being an older sister, she grants that he knows something after all. She asks why he does not explain her appeal to Rinami herself. He says he wants it to emerge naturally. Shiori can admit the question is more complicated than she thought and still find an odd man calling himself a little brother intensely irritating. [S3](GKM_HIMESAKI_SHIORI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md)

When he proposes staying at a hotel, Rinami is the first to ask him to remain. Shiori then invites him in her own right. She wants more stories about Rinami at school, and with their parents away she would feel safer with someone whom Rinami trusts in the house. Those are Shiori’s stated reasons. The invitation settles where the guest may sleep. She gives no comparable speech accepting the sibling title. The next morning the Producer says he asked her permission to use the food in the fridge. Shiori tries the breakfast he made and finds it delicious. Small decisions about another person’s presence and use of the house are already within her reach. [S3, S4](GKM_HIMESAKI_SHIORI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md)

## What she does with another admirer

While they wait for dinner, Shiori and the Producer play a word-chain game naming good things about Rinami. Shiori offers her kindness, then her mischievous side. The second answer makes the game something more than a competition in praising an unfailingly good older sister. Shiori likes things she would not learn from a public description alone. When Rinami asks what they are doing, Shiori matter-of-factly names the game. It is Rinami who asks the two of them to stop out of embarrassment. Shiori has found something she likes doing with the guest without having to agree with every account the two older people give of him. [S3](GKM_HIMESAKI_SHIORI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md)

Her teasing survives that accommodation. When Rinami and the Producer prepare to go out, Shiori calls it a date. Rinami immediately objects. When he later goes to see their father, Shiori remarks on his going to greet him. The text leaves room to hear a joke in the repetition, but it gives Shiori no special knowledge of a romance. A suggestion of sukiyaki draws an enthusiastic response in the very same exchange. In Dear 027, when Rinami abruptly goes out, Shiori calls after her to ask where she is going. Her sister’s changing circumstances keep presenting perfectly practical questions. [S4, S5](GKM_HIMESAKI_SHIORI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md)

In Dear 034, Shiori clears her throat in the written dialogue and tells Rinami everyone is watching. The Producer explains that her family is connected by camera. Their mother says she supports Rinami even though she knows little about idols. Their father tells her to pursue the path she has chosen and to come home occasionally. Shiori’s contribution is different. She gives her sister information about who can witness this declaration. Earlier, it was Shiori who was surprised at who belonged to Rinami’s public. Here she is the one able to alert Rinami to her audience. [S6](GKM_HIMESAKI_SHIORI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md)

Rinami remembers crying around the time Shiori was born because their parents no longer seemed to have attention for her. That is the older sister’s childhood memory. Shiori’s later admission of loneliness gives the family an echo across the years without assigning her a memory of her birth or making the girls’ experiences identical. In a separate Halloween support story, watching the Hanami sisters play makes Rinami think of showing Shiori a zombie photograph. Whether she sent it, and what Shiori thought of it, are unanswered. [S4, S7](GKM_HIMESAKI_SHIORI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md)

A new scene faithful to Shiori could give her a small decision with a practical consequence. She might object to a name while warning its bearer about the insects, or urge Rinami to rest while asking what it is like to do the work that tires her. An honest explanation from her sister or a guest can change what she consents to. Her ability to revise one answer gives neither person permission to supply all her others. The available scenes give little access to her time away from Rinami beyond the classmates with whom she shares news. Her exact age, future occupation, singing and independent performed voice remain open. [Source locators and individual evidence boundary](GKM_HIMESAKI_SHIORI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md)
