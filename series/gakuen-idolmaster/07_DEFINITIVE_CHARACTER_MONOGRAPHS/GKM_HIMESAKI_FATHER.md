# Rinami’s father

The stories identify this man as Himesaki Rinami’s father and give him no personal name. He had wanted her to take over work at the family brewery. When she later declares that she will become Prima Stella, he reminds her how rarely she asked for anything as a child and tells her to pursue the path she chose. Then he asks her to come home once in a while. She laughs and acknowledges how long she has been away. In his dealings with his daughter, support can mean helping her leave home and admitting that he would like to see her more often. [Dear 025 and 034](GKM_HIMESAKI_FATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#the-family-and-the-work)

## Hearing an unusual request

Rinami characterizes her father as fairly strict. The account of his initial plan for her, and of what it took to change it, is hers. She says she worked up the courage to tell him that she wanted to become an idol. He encouraged her to do what she liked best and persuaded her mother. Rinami remembers a promise to become a top idol and come home for a triumphant live. The promise gives her a reason to take her family’s investment seriously. The story gives no account of who will undertake the brewery work after her departure. [Dear 022 and 025](GKM_HIMESAKI_FATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#the-family-and-the-work)

Event 028 stages a family conversation about attending Hatsuboshi. Her mother asks whether she is serious. Rinami cites her music teacher’s praise and her standing at karaoke. Her father asks if she has thought it through. She answers that she wants to be an idol. He tells her she can come home whenever the going gets hard. He keeps the option of returning home open. The scene ends with Rinami taking responsibility for her choice. Her account elsewhere supplies the part about persuading her mother. [Event 028 part 05](GKM_HIMESAKI_FATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#the-family-and-the-work)

There is another side to this acceptance. He later describes it as the first time his daughter, usually so considerate of her parents, voiced a selfish wish. The word carries his recognition of what it cost her to ask. On that occasion he tells the Producer that, as her father, he wants somehow to help make the dream come true. Both the safety of coming home and the seriousness of letting her go follow from his sense of this particular child. [Dear 027](GKM_HIMESAKI_FATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#at-the-hospital)

## An audience at home

Shiori describes what happens when her father becomes a fan. He watches Rinami’s live streams daily, makes handheld fans and other goods to give to the neighbors, and uses Rinami’s recorded greeting as his alarm. Rinami is surprised. Her father has made her work part of the neighborhood’s life without waiting for her to come home and tell people about it. Shiori publicizes her older sister to her own school friends. Each has found a different audience. The mother’s contribution in the same call is a package of honeyed pickled plums. [Dear 021](GKM_HIMESAKI_FATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#at-home-and-away)

Even before Rinami was an idol, a small part of her father’s daily routine traveled with her. On a trip she returns with snacks that sound to her peers like accompaniments to drinks. She explains that her father likes an evening drink and that she often used to receive such things instead of sweets. In another support story, Rinami remembers that both parents were frequently away working and she watched anime with Shiori. A food habit can reach a daughter across years in a household where work often kept the parents out. The sources give no specific beverage or individual work schedule. [Event 013 part 03 and support 3-0071 part 02](GKM_HIMESAKI_FATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#at-home-and-away)

## Asking what success will do for her

Before an important performance, Rinami takes a call from her father’s phone. It is her mother on the line. She tells Rinami that her father has been in a bicycle accident and is in surgery, and relays the doctor’s assurance that his life is not in danger. The choice to hesitate and then tell Rinami is the mother’s. The father’s own response comes later. He apologizes for causing worry at the time of an important live. Rinami reassures him. He addresses the worry she carried into the performance. Rinami later tells Sena that her own uncertainty would have stood in her way even without the accident. That is Rinami’s judgment about a contest that could have gone differently. [Dear 023, 024 and 029](GKM_HIMESAKI_FATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#at-the-hospital)

He remembers the Producer’s earlier visit in spring and tells him what his daughter has said about him. She considers him trustworthy. The father’s written declaration that he will not give his daughter away can read like conventional gatekeeping. He immediately admits it was a joke he had always wanted to try. The player’s sole offered reply borrows the language of courtship by asking for his daughter. The father reacts with surprise. The Producer then pledges to devote his life to making her a top idol. The father gives no assent to marriage in this exchange. Rinami’s separate reading of the Producer’s notebook is intercut with the conversation. [Dear 026 and 027](GKM_HIMESAKI_FATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#at-the-hospital)

Later the father asks a question the professional pledge has left unanswered. Will reaching the top make Rinami happy? The Producer says plainly that she has faced hardship and will face more, and that some things can be seen only by following a path one has dreamed of. Having heard that answer about the cost, her father asks the Producer to make Rinami happy. He makes a serious personal request of someone she trusts. It also shows a limit to the father’s willingness simply to stand back. Rinami chooses the work, and he still wants the person accompanying her to answer for how she fares. [Dear 027](GKM_HIMESAKI_FATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#at-the-hospital)

At the later family call, the Producer has arranged the camera connection and Shiori alerts Rinami that everyone is watching. Her mother admits how little she knows about idols and says she supports her. Her father speaks about the daughter he remembers, urges her to have no regrets, and uses his last turn to ask for a visit. He has room to voice something he wants for himself once he has recognized what she wants. [Dear 034](GKM_HIMESAKI_FATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#the-family-and-the-work)

His words and reported habits allow a bounded reconstruction. Offered another grand plan on Rinami’s behalf, he has reason to ask where her own wish enters into it, what it might cost her and whether the person making promises knows her well enough. The revision-88 Dear scenes supply the family and hospital arc. Separately, Event 013 supplies Rinami’s account of an ordinary habit, Event 028 stages her school request, and support 3-0071 records her childhood memory of both parents working. His age and precise brewery role remain unknown. There is little access to his life outside the family, his relationship with Rinami’s mother in private, or how he speaks to Shiori alone. Five scripts label sixteen dialogue boxes as his. None assigns a character-voice command to those boxes. His joke, hesitation and questions are legible in the writing. The available evidence does not establish the pitch or performed sound of his voice. [Source and individual Gate E assessment](GKM_HIMESAKI_FATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#individual-gate-e)
