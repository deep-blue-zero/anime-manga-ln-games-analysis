# Kotone Fujita’s eldest younger sister

This portrait follows the eldest of Kotone Fujita’s younger siblings through Event 016 at Source Lock 1.2. It includes spoilers for the outing. [Primary sources and scope of review](GKM_KOTONE_ELDEST_YOUNGER_SISTER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md)

Kotone wants to give her eldest younger sister a day at the sports center. The girl usually looks after the smaller children and seldom tells Kotone where she would like to go. Kotone explains this to Saki herself. The child’s own first substantial choice is smaller and more specific. When Saki asks what she would like to do, she picks the batting machine. Kotone thinks it may be too difficult, and the girl is ready to choose something else. Temari asks if she would like to try it together. What the girl does with that offer takes most of the afternoon to discover. [S1–S3](GKM_KOTONE_ELDEST_YOUNGER_SISTER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#s1)

### The person her sister brings along

The game gives the visitor the nameplate `ことねの妹`, “Kotone’s younger sister.” Kotone identifies her as `一番上のちび`, the eldest of the younger children. She is both somebody’s little sister and an older child who helps at home. That arrangement explains how the outing comes about. It tells us little about what she enjoys on a day of her own. For that, we have her quiet request to try hitting a ball, her surprise at the size of the center and her irritation when Kotone teases her about making an especially polite first impression. She really does address Saki and Temari respectfully. She also tells her sister she is putting on no such act. The two registers coexist within a few lines. [S1–S2](GKM_KOTONE_ELDEST_YOUNGER_SISTER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#s1)

Temari makes the batting machine an activity they can share. She says she likes it herself. The girl tries and misses. Temari notices tension in her shoulders and tells her to turn from the waist. “Like this?” the girl asks. The child can admit that the exercise is difficult and still thank her. She then has a question of her own. Kotone tells stories at home about her school friends, she explains, but very little about herself. What is her sister like at school? Temari can answer that question because she has changed her mind. She once mistook Kotone’s hours at work for a lack of ambition. She has since seen how well Kotone dances and how hard she works. [S2–S4](GKM_KOTONE_ELDEST_YOUNGER_SISTER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#s2)

The child gives Temari something in return. She repeats Kotone’s description of a friend who sings beautifully, demands a great deal of herself and is kinder than she looks. She even quotes Kotone’s insistence that she would never say to Temari’s face how much she respects her. The joke depends on the younger sister delivering the compliment to its intended object anyway. Temari asks whether Kotone really said it. The girl who began by trying to follow instructions has become the person who knows something the older student wants to hear. When Kotone comes back, the child asks her to watch, earns praise for her swing and gives Temari credit for teaching her. Her pleasure has room for both the skill she acquired and the person who helped her acquire it. [S3–S4](GKM_KOTONE_ELDEST_YOUNGER_SISTER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#s3)

### Changing the plan

The sharpest evidence of how she thinks comes on the futsal court. Saki offers to play alone against the other three. When Kotone asks about strategy, her sister wonders why a side with three players would need any. Saki’s play supplies an answer. Kotone wants to get at least one goal back. The child now says, “お姉、作戦があるんだけど” (“Sis, I have a plan”). Temari backs it. The first time through, Temari distracts Saki and the girl gets past her. Kotone celebrates the goal, and Saki herself credits the combination. [S5](GKM_KOTONE_ELDEST_YOUNGER_SISTER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#s5)

The change from her first judgment to her second matters more than winning the bet. The girl can underestimate a problem, learn from the opponent and offer something the two older teammates agree to try. She takes part in executing the surprise. Later, when Saki says it will only work once, Temari proposes changing tactics again. All three narrowly win. Giving the younger sister her share of the first goal also gives her teammates their due in the final result. [S5](GKM_KOTONE_ELDEST_YOUNGER_SISTER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#s5)

### What she can tell Kotone

At the end of the day she thanks Saki for winning stuffed toys for the younger children as well. Then she notices someone from Kotone’s school singing on a free stage. The suggestion that Kotone perform comes from Saki. Kotone invites the child to cheer, draws Temari into the impromptu performance and afterwards asks her sister how she did. The answer is emphatic and personal. “世界一可愛かったよ！” (“You were the cutest in the world!”). Earlier the girl had to ask Temari what her sister was like at school. Now she has seen a performance for herself, and Kotone wants her judgment. She can be proud of Kotone in her own words without having to decide what she herself will become. [S4, S6](GKM_KOTONE_ELDEST_YOUNGER_SISTER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#s4)

One support story returns to that curiosity. Saki and Temari recognize a visiting sister, who thanks them for their recent help. She signed up for the campus tour herself. When Saki wonders whether she plans to apply, the visitor gives a more immediate reason. She has seen Kotone’s concerts and wants to see what her ordinary day is like. Her visit is a surprise to Kotone. The two-way recognition makes this a plausible return of the sports-center child, though the story uses the general sister label and gives no birth order. The distinction matters because a sister in Kotone’s Dear 034 does explicitly want an idol career and a future stage shared with Kotone. That is the Dear speaker’s own ambition. The link identifying her as the eldest child of Event 016 remains open. [S7–S8](GKM_KOTONE_ELDEST_YOUNGER_SISTER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#s7)

The same care applies to the separate Event 019 phone call. A sister accepts a postponed trip home and tells Kotone that seeing her shine at work would make her still happier. The eldest sister of Event 016 has her own occasion to return praise, on the day Kotone made time for her. The anonymous caller has hers, when Kotone cannot come home. Each scene gives the girl speaking a reason to answer as she does. [S6, S8](GKM_KOTONE_ELDEST_YOUNGER_SISTER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#s6)

Event 016 supplies 37 written turns for the eldest sister. Two independent reviews of the batting scene describe her participation through text with no rendered figure or audible speaking performance of her own there. The child learns from Temari. The recollection of Rinha teaching the same skill belongs to Temari. For the child herself, the available expressive evidence lies in the changing addressee, the reported compliment, the request to be watched and the final superlative. The game leaves her personal name, age and private life beyond this visit unspecified. It gives her enough of one afternoon to change her mind in public, teach someone what her sister says at home and answer for herself when Kotone asks how she did.
