# Kuramoto China’s grandfather

When a student Producer asks him to support Hiwatari Kanae’s proposed transfer to Hatsuboshi, Kuramoto China’s grandfather checks that he has understood the proposal. Kanae would become an idol student, which the Producer thinks would also let her do more for China. The Producer proposes that she pass the entrance exam herself. The grandfather laughs in the written dialogue, calls him impressive and agrees to the plan. Then he asks what the Producer will do if it fails. The Producer starts to offer his job as a stake and then revises that to accepting any punishment. The grandfather’s question adds pressure without supplying a punishment of his own. The scene gives one of the clearest examples of how he uses his authority. He can welcome a plan from someone with less power and still make that person feel answerable for proposing it. [Kanae’s admission](GKM_KURAMOTO_GRANDFATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#kanaes-admission)

The sources identify him as China’s grandfather and, in accounts from China and the headmaster, as chairman of the Kuramoto Group. He is called `お爺様` by China and `倉本爺` by the headmaster and in his own script nameplate. No personal name is established in the admitted material. The chairman’s economic reach is visible through the household, patronage and educational requests. Those capacities yield little about his private assets, his professional path or the exact business structure. He is distinct from Juo Kunio, his counterpart at Hatsuboshi Academy and Juo Sena’s grandfather.

## A busy grandfather makes time

China remembers a childhood in which her parents and grandparents were busy and in which crying would not bring her parents to comfort her. The person who did comfort her was a maid apprentice of her own age. In China’s account the apprentice risked overstepping because her family had long served the Kuramotos and she had been taught to observe that hierarchy. She appealed directly to China’s grandfather. China says that he gave up several positions that day, ordered relatives to make time for family, and established gatherings several times a year for relatives and their associates. Kanae’s Dear story names her as the petitioner and has her remember listening to a lonely China. China supplies the description of what her grandfather did in response. His exact words, the offices in question and the moment he made up his mind remain unknown. [The petition](GKM_KURAMOTO_GRANDFATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#the-petition)

The reported response is unusually concrete. He can treat a child’s loneliness as a reason to change his schedule and ask other adults to change theirs. The petition is also evidence that an attendant’s insight can change what he does. It tells us little about how safely another subordinate could challenge him, and it leaves the family’s wealth and service hierarchy in place. Years later, in a song communication about a Hinamatsuri gathering at the family home, China distinguishes the regular party for her from the corporate salon the Producer expects. She says her grandfather invited the guests and recalls that the custom began before she could perform for them. The Producer’s concern about a room of executives is reasonable. China knows part of the gathering as time that adults make for a child. [The petition](GKM_KURAMOTO_GRANDFATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#the-petition)

Smaller references make this a particular family relationship. China says her grandfather likes suspense dramas. In one Produce filming choice, she recognizes the hammock as a favorite of his and is excited to try it herself. For Valentine’s Day she plans to make him chocolate without the maids’ help this time, hoping to surprise him. That plan belongs to China. It does not identify his favorite food or show his reception of the gift. In a different event she offers her grandfather’s villa as a possible site for a student-council project, then says she has to ask him and intends to persuade him. The event subsequently takes place there. That outcome leaves the actual discussion and terms of permission outside the scene. China can approach him with a project and exercise the access that the family gives her. She does not speak as if it is her property alone to grant. ## When support becomes a demand

China learns very early in her Dear story that her Producer came because her grandfather requested one through Hatsuboshi’s headmaster. She had pictured being sought out on her own merits. The arrangement opens a door and strips that first approach of part of what it meant to her. Soon the headmaster relays a demand that the Producer make her Prima Stella at the coming summer Hatsuboshi Idol Festival. Kunio also says he provoked a quarrel over which of them had the cuter granddaughter. China’s grandfather is a patron who can request a result on a timetable an instructor considers bad for the learner. We have Kunio’s account of the quarrel and the Producer’s guess about losing his job, without the grandfather’s own exact rationale or an actual firing condition spoken by him. The Producer refuses to enter China that summer. A later support scene assigns a similar demand through Kunio to Sena. It is a separate version of the role and deadline, with Sena herself acknowledging the pressure of the assignment. [Patronage and refusal](GKM_KURAMOTO_GRANDFATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#patronage-and-refusal)

China calls him both kind and frightening. In Dear 009 she reports going to him herself and getting his acceptance that she will continue as an idol with this Producer. Her own account supplies a meaningful change in how she deals with him. The next request the headmaster carries comes after the family saw her live. It names a later festival and carries no penalty, and Kunio presents the renewed support as a proposal from the relatives. This shows how a grandchild’s refusal can coexist with a continuing family wish to advance her. It leaves open the grandfather’s own words about the change. The same arrangement still feels difficult for the Producer to decline because the leadership of the group wants in. Formal relaxation of a demand can leave social pressure in place. [Patronage and refusal](GKM_KURAMOTO_GRANDFATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#patronage-and-refusal)

In China’s song communication, when she mentions wanting to perform in a unit, the headmaster relays the grandfather’s call to make it happen soon. The Producer says the grandfather was going to hold a large audition and that he and Kanae stopped him. Kanae says that sometimes he will listen if she explains that another course is better for China. In Hiro’s parallel song communication Sena says the same kind of request came from the grandfather. As China’s Producer in that story, she arranges the audition with the Kuramoto Group. China wishes Sena had consulted her first. The stopped audition is a specific outcome of the first track. In the other, a different Producer puts the intervention into effect. In another Dear scene Kunio reports talking him out of becoming a festival judge. In Hiro’s separate Dear route Sena reports getting a one-line letter from him asking what happened. Sena reads it against an event China did not enter and wishes he would look up the rules. That frustration belongs to Sena. The examples support a man who reaches quickly for available levers, sometimes abandons a particular intervention, and can still leave another person uncertain about what he expects. They do not establish how well he knows every event’s rules or that the household’s offers to mobilize votes came from him personally. ## Speech and reconstruction

The three boxes attributed to him in the depicted memory of the transfer negotiation show a short questioning opening, the written laugh `がっはっは`, the older-man ending `じゃ`, and the direct `きみ` in the consequential question. The switch from enthusiastic assent to asking about failure is evidence of his conversational sequence. It supplies no heard laugh or acoustic manner. Kunio can quote an emphatic demand and China can describe someone intimidating. Those are other people’s renderings of him. His rhythm, pitch, bodily expression, singing, private conduct and intimate relationships remain unknown beyond the specific acts and preferences attributed to him. [Speech and evidence limits](GKM_KURAMOTO_GRANDFATHER_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#speech-and-evidence-limits)

A source-bounded reconstruction can give him a concrete problem affecting his granddaughter and expect him to consider a concrete intervention. It can give him a credible petitioner and evidence that his first idea might hurt her, while keeping his response uncertain. Kanae’s petition, China’s insistence and the Producer’s conditional plan supply three different ways of reaching him. He can give time personally, put an expectation through an institutional intermediary, or agree to support a younger person who must meet the conditions herself. Influence and affection make him unusually able to respond and unusually able to impose. How he weighs someone’s autonomy once their interests cease to align with China’s is beyond the admitted evidence.
