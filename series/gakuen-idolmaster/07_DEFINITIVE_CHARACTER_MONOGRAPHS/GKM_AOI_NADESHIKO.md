# Aoi Nadeshiko

Aoi Nadeshiko uses her family’s buying power to keep rival idols’ merchandise out of fans’ hands. When Shinosawa Hiro asks whether she would also bribe an audition judge, Nadeshiko is taken aback and calls the suggestion an appalling thing to think of. She has already told Hiro that she wants to interfere with the rival school’s fan recruitment. She objects to bribery while taking for granted that her own tactic will discourage fans from attending. The difference is central to how she acts. Nadeshiko takes rank, loyalty and personal courtesy seriously. She can carry them into a competition, backstage encounter or friendship in ways the other person experiences quite differently. [China, Hiro and the merchandise](GKM_AOI_NADESHIKO_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#china-hiro-and-the-merchandise)

Nadeshiko identifies herself as an idol of Gokugetsu Academy, `極月学園`. The scripts give her the speaker name `撫子` and identify her public persona with self-reference such as `あたくし`, emphatic `ですわ` and praise of her looks and lineage. She can put on the same recognizably ornate style to act grand, welcome a stranger, complain about a loss or plead that she is alarmed. She says she uses her family’s means to interfere with rivals’ fan recruitment. In a separate Dear route, a Producer reports that she uses family money to raise her own profile, and Juo Sena recognizes her as a daughter of the Aoi household. These establish an available resource and its particular uses. The admitted scenes leave its amount, legal control, her parents’ own intentions, her childhood and the details of her upbringing unknown. ## Competition and the people her tactics affect

In Kuramoto China’s Dear route, Nadeshiko buys up Hiro’s goods and explains why. China and Hiro are considered dark horses. Nadeshiko may call them fifth-rate, yet takes enough care about the result to interfere before the audition. She says fans without the goods will stay away because of the way Hiro’s fandom works. That is her account of how the tactic is supposed to operate. It neither proves her model of every fan nor clears her of using the fans as leverage. She says she will extend the buyout to the rest of Hatsuboshi and tells China that only her revered elders can give her orders.

Nadeshiko is pleased when China says she would like an autograph and agrees to sign one. When China remembers why she came and asks for the goods back, Nadeshiko refuses. Then China uses a fact suggested by her Producer. Their fathers and grandfathers know each other well, and the Aoi household is associated with a subsidiary of the Kuramoto enterprise, according to China. Nadeshiko is visibly surprised within the script’s presentation and asks what China wants. China asks for the goods to reach the intended people and for a fair contest. She wants acknowledgment of her improved ability if she wins. Later China reports both friendship and that the goods now look likely to reach fans. The encounter reveals how quickly Nadeshiko can reconsider terms when she recognizes a standing she had overlooked. Its resolution gives no private explanation of how much she is persuaded by the request, moved by China personally or constrained by the new hierarchy. [China, Hiro and the merchandise](GKM_AOI_NADESHIKO_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#china-hiro-and-the-merchandise)

Hiwatari Kanae’s Dear story exposes a different cost of Nadeshiko’s understanding of rank. She says she came to investigate a young woman whose photos drew attention and who might threaten the person she calls `お姉さま`. Recognition that the woman is China’s attendant changes her argument. She tells Kanae that someone who works behind the scenes should remain there. Kanae agrees, and Nadeshiko is surprised that she does not fight back. When China appears, Nadeshiko becomes evasive about what they were saying and leaves. China later tells Kanae she was delighted that people praised her. Kanae privately agrees with Nadeshiko’s judgment and also finds herself pleased that China welcomes her visibility. The loyalty claimed by the accuser diverges from what China says she wants. Kanae’s private conflict belongs to Kanae. The encounter openly attributes a protective aim to Nadeshiko and leaves the named identity of the threatened elder implicit in that particular exchange. ## Elders, opposition and unwanted help

In the Shirakusa support story, Nadeshiko already calls Shion `お姉さま`. She wonders how to address Shion’s literal older sister Gekka and settles on `大姉さま`. Gekka tells her to use whatever address she likes. Later Nadeshiko brings Shion a drink and notices that she is unusually quiet. In her own private thought, she wonders whether Shion is nervous about talking to Gekka and sees an opportunity to repay her for looking after her. She volunteers to bring Gekka over. Shion tells her to stop. Nadeshiko takes that instruction as modesty until Shion asks whether she can follow orders and calls the concern misplaced. Nadeshiko then attends to the scouting assignment and quickly returns to admiring what Shion says she will accomplish.

Her care here is grounded in something ordinary she can do and something she wants to understand. She has a private reason to help that is narrower than school prestige. She also assumes she understands the other person’s trouble and carries her plan past the first refusal. Elsewhere in the same support episode, Gekka shows Kuroi the unanswered messages she sends Shion asking for exam results and lesson details. Nadeshiko is absent from that discussion. The communication problem runs deeper than simply putting the sisters in the same room. Neither Gekka’s claim that she is being a proper older sister nor Shion’s rejection proves what an acceptable reconciliation would look like. Nadeshiko’s perspective matters because she is observant enough to identify discomfort and eager enough to misjudge the remedy. [The Shirakusa sisters](GKM_AOI_NADESHIKO_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#the-shirakusa-sisters)

Nadeshiko is loyal to Shion when Shion goes out to recruit Shiun Sumika. Shion makes the offer of preferential treatment. Nadeshiko introduces herself alongside her. When Sumika chooses to stay at Hatsuboshi, Nadeshiko later tells her she would have been better off as Shion’s pawn and contrasts her with what Nadeshiko calls real talent. Following her own loss she complains about the result. She nevertheless agrees to pass on Sumika’s challenge to Shion and says Shion’s ability is far beyond her own. Defending her elder’s hierarchy lets her acknowledge a limit to her own standing while continuing to compete. A teacher or rival can later stand in a different relation to her. In Temari’s Dear route she describes an apology circuit, time away and a return to idol work under Kaya Rinha’s strict guidance after N.I.A. Temari says she trained under the same teacher and offers Nadeshiko the option of addressing her as an older sister if Temari wins. Nadeshiko does use the address after the contest and again when she greets Temari late in that Dear story. The route gives her a changed relationship with Temari and her own account of trying to improve. It does not establish the same history in the other Dear routes or independently verify every reason for her time away. ## Ordinary company and performed address

In Misuzu’s Dear story, Nadeshiko sees an academy visitor who needs help finding his way and guides him. She says that is how a Gokugetsu student should behave. The Producer names her and praises her reputation. Nadeshiko briefly suspects there was something objectionable in his words, admits she is too pleased to think straight, and starts inviting him to a tea gathering. The counterexample matters. She can perform prestige by doing a favor and welcoming an outsider. She can also be distracted by praise of her own standing. When she cuts off the invitation with a yelp, Misuzu has yet to speak. Nadeshiko later stammers that people at Hatsuboshi are frightening. The exact edition makes the shifts in her delivery audible, while her reason for the first alarm remains open. The contrast is with the more projected taunting laugh she gives Sumika before their bout and the frustrated sounds after her defeat, both heard in a separately witnessed game compilation. These named performances give local evidence of how she changes address. The sources do not yield an isolated vocal instrument, a singing repertoire or a fixed emotional meaning for every laugh. [Hospitality and performed voice](GKM_AOI_NADESHIKO_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#hospitality-and-performed-voice)

Outside competition she can buy Shion a drink, think of including a visitor in tea and notice an appealing waffle stall at the Hatsuboshi festival. Shion reminds her they are there to scout, then becomes entangled in the stall’s payment problem herself. Nadeshiko’s appeal to a food describes that impromptu outing. The story gives no habitual diet or confirmed purchase. In one Mao Produce branch, Nadeshiko claims she arranged delays so that Mao, who stopped to help other idols, would miss the audition and be disqualified. After losing, she assumes Mao has come to laugh at her and says she has twisted her foot. When Mao insists on carrying her to the infirmary, Nadeshiko protests and tells her to put her down. She privately notices her heart racing. That reaction gives no outward assent to being carried. In a separate Rinami Produce outcome, Nadeshiko apologizes to Shion for losing, then feels warmth when Rinami praises her performance and offers advice. She thinks Shion should be the only person she calls her elder. Out loud she refuses what she calls a rival’s pity. Each encounter lets someone she confronted show her some regard. Her private responses are more complicated than her refusals, and neither scene establishes a romantic relationship. [Small preferences and care](GKM_AOI_NADESHIKO_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#small-preferences-and-care)

A bounded reconstruction can give Nadeshiko a particular elder, rival or guest rather than one universal manner. With an elder whose approval she values, she may anticipate what that person needs and exaggerate the value of her own help. With a rival she can make an announcement or threat theatrical and look for an advantage that preserves a boundary she recognizes. A refusal can change her conduct when she sees a relation or status she had failed to notice. An apparent insult can still make her defensive, and new hospitality remains possible if she thinks the situation calls for it. The limits are as important as these recurrent patterns. There is no basis to fill in her household accounts, schooling before Gokugetsu, stable romance, unobserved leisure or a complete career progression. The clearest recurring risk is that she can know how she wants to show loyalty before she knows how the other person wants to receive it.
