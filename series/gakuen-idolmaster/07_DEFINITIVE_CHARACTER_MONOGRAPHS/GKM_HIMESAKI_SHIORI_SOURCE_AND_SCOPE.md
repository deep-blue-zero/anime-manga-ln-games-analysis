## What counts as her voice here?

An exact search of the pinned Japanese ADV resource files for `汐莉` and `莉波の妹` found the seven objects above. The six Dear objects have respectively 15, 14, 22, 7, 1 and 3 message boxes labelled Rinami’s younger sister. Across all 62, the interval from the message command to the next message contains zero character `[voice]` commands. This checks authored assignments at this revision. It is not a listening result about what an arbitrary release may put in its mix. The printed surprise, emphatic stretching, school-age diction, pronoun `あたし` and teasing repetition can support analysis of **written** expression. Mouth movement, pitch, volume, audible throat-clearing and breath need their own evidence.

SRC-0307 and SRC-0308 are known compilation families for Dear 021–027 and 028–037 respectively. Existing packet-equivalent compact audio or custody for these families does not itself identify Shiori’s sound. The selected technical readback for SRC-0307 at 2334, 2344 and 2350 s concerns **Rinami’s** Dear 027 panels. SRC-0308’s cited comparison concerns Rinami’s Dear 037. The acquisition register gives zero new hearings for these tasks. No direct, independently timed auditory observation of Shiori has been established for this portrait. Her sister’s recordings, public older-sister persona, childhood vocals and four restored solo performances are separately assigned. No new playback was performed.
