# Kana of Love☆しすたぁず

Kana (カナ), whom Himesaki Rinami calls *Kanapoyo*, once performed with her in Love☆しすたぁず, a four-member idol unit whose performers played four different little sisters. By the time they meet again, Kana has transferred to Hatsuboshi Academy’s General Department. She still watches idols and still has an idea of what she might contribute to a show. When Rinami asks what she is doing now, Kana can explain both the decision she has made and the work she would like to learn. This portrait follows Rinami’s Dear story through chapter 033 and keeps a memory in a song communication and two separate support-card conversations in their own settings. It includes spoilers through that boundary at GAKUMAS V2 Source Lock 1.2, Japanese ADV revision 88. [Primary sources and evidence limits](GKM_FORMER_LOVE_SISTERS_KANA_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md)

## What she noticed on stage

The most revealing glimpse of Kana as a working idol comes in Rinami’s memory. After a show, Kana remarks that the audience’s response that day had been disappointing. Rinami wonders whether the timing of the calls was difficult and suggests using the talk segment to practise them with the audience. Kana notices what happened on the other side of the stage. Rinami supplies the diagnosis and the remedy. Years of professional experience would be too large a claim for this exchange. It gives a concrete example of where Kana’s attention went when she was one of the performers. [Rinami’s idol card 3-017, part 02, lines 15–59](GKM_FORMER_LOVE_SISTERS_KANA_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#the-former-unit)

The group could care about one another and still struggle to work together. Rinami tells Mao that they wore matching accessories, got along well and had too little time or attention for communication while each was struggling with the immediate demands. It was Rinami who tried to play the energetic little sister. Her removal and fear of returning to a stage are experiences she describes in her own right. She later speaks of the unit dissolving. None of these accounts identifies who first left, who decided Rinami should go or which of the other sister roles belonged to Kana. Kana’s account of why she changed departments can stand on its own. [Rinami Dear 002 and 032 and support 3-0064 and 3-0101](GKM_FORMER_LOVE_SISTERS_KANA_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#the-former-unit)

## Giving an honest answer to an old friend

They recognize each other by their old nicknames. Kana tells Rinami that she followed the N.I.A. stream, watched the final live and cheered with friends. Before Rinami asks about Kana’s own plans, Kana asks whether she is on her way back from a lesson. Her move to the General Department is already a fact. College or vocational school is still ahead, and she would like to study stage direction. She reminds Rinami that thinking up ways to present a stage was something she was good at. She entered Hatsuboshi because she loved idols and has come to think that supporting idols she loves suits her better than performing as one herself. She gives attention to a performance as a fan and imagines a future making decisions behind the scenes. She has already begun the first. In a separate event, Rinami tells Tsubame that the unnamed leader of her first-year unit transferred to General Education and plans vocational training to help stage idols’ performances. The parallels make Kana a plausible identification. Only the event gives the former leader that title and the specific vocational plan. Rinami never names her there. [Rinami Dear 021 and 022 and Event 028, part 02](GKM_FORMER_LOVE_SISTERS_KANA_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#recognition-and-a-change-of-direction)

The difficult moment comes when Kana tells Rinami she respects her because continuing to shine as an idol is remarkable. She starts to ask her friend to do it for Kana’s share too. Then she laughs in the written dialogue, recognizes how convenient such a request would be for her and settles on cheering Rinami on at H.I.F. The correction matters because the wish was real enough to begin saying. Cheering still lets Kana have a stake in her friend’s career, while leaving Rinami free to say what that career is for. Rinami later wonders why she alone, whom she considered the least successful, is still pursuing this life. In this exchange Rinami keeps that doubt to herself. [Rinami Dear 022, lines 62–109](GKM_FORMER_LOVE_SISTERS_KANA_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#recognition-and-a-change-of-direction)

## Coming to see her perform

At the Hatsuboshi Older-Sister Idol Festival, a different event from H.I.F., Kana calls out to Rinami again. Rinami notices a banner and asks about it. Kana explains that the old unit’s members have come to cheer her, and confirms that Miho and Yuka are there when Rinami asks for them by nickname. She says they quit being idols and still love idols. The old four-little-sisters group has come to see its former member perform as an older sister. What Kana actually does that day is turn up, speak to her friend and give the group’s reason for coming. Who made the banner, arranged the outing or directed the show remains uncredited. [Rinami Dear 032, lines 285–303, and 033, lines 172–225 and 267–274](GKM_FORMER_LOVE_SISTERS_KANA_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#showing-up)

Kana’s written speech preserves the easy familiarity of *Rinapoyo* and the ordinary first name *Rinami*. She calls herself *atashi* and breaks off a sentence when she recognizes what it would ask of the person in front of her. Later she says plainly why the visiting friends have come. Where supporting Rinami is concerned, there is good reason to expect Kana to watch closely and to let her friend know she came. How she would direct a reluctant performer, handle disagreement at work or react to a public failure of her own remains open. Her love of idols is clear. Her aptitude for devising stages is her own assessment, and she wants to develop it. The sources give no exact age, surname, household, romantic history, private hobbies apart from that interest, or later professional appointment. They also give no securely attributable solo, sung part or performed speaking register for her. The words, the self-correction and the attendance carry this account.
