# Kuroi

Source Lock 1.2, admitted revision 88. This account covers Kuroi's Gakuen appearance across the named Dear Idol continuities and support stories. It contains story spoilers. Its direct performance evidence comes from one separately published recording of the compliance reply in Ume Dear 016.

Kuroi (黒井崇男) chairs the board of Gokugetsu Academy and heads the talent agency 961 Pro. He talks about Hatsuboshi Academy as an institution to beat, then devotes great attention to its strongest idols. He can scorn a student festival offhand and stay to watch it. He invites a producer and an idol to transfer after a result that disappointed his own side. He can ask someone what she actually does for her sister, look at the messages, and point out that the sister has given no answer. These changes of conduct matter alongside his taste for declaring that his side will crush the other one. In these scenes, he consistently looks for a way to make other people's talent or choices matter to his institution. Whether he gives their purposes independent weight depends on what he is dealing with and whom he is speaking to. ## What the rival school sees at Hatsuboshi

When Kuroi visits Hatsuboshi's idol festival, H.I.F., he calls it grandiose for student make-believe and says pupils showed poor judgment by enrolling there instead of at “our” Gokugetsu. The former is his evaluation and the latter his institutional allegiance. Shirakusa Gekka, an accomplished older idol and the elder sister of the Gokugetsu student Shirakusa Shion, immediately sees promise among the performers. Kuroi says she came by choice. Gekka herself explains that a spectator can enter on the day and that she might recruit and personally train someone promising. When she singles out one idol's distinctive hair and judges her worth a look, Kuroi tells Shion to scout Gekka's picks. He supplies a purpose of his own, weakening Hatsuboshi from within. He accepts Gekka's assessment as an input and hands implementation to Shion. His contempt for the school does not erase his interest in individual ability there. [K2](GKM_KUROI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#k2-hif-scouting-and-the-sisters)

The middle part follows Shion and her devoted junior Aoi Nadeshiko after their seniors leave. Shion thinks simple recruitment would be dull and declares that she will take control of Hatsuboshi herself. Kuroi had ordered scouting and spoken of subversion. The specific escalation is Shion's declaration, with no evidence there that he dictated her means. Later, when Gekka asks him about a prospect, he says the current level of polish is too low and tells her to train the idol until the work meets a minimum audience standard. Gekka takes this as a decent opinion of the raw material. That is her reading of the practical assignment. He changes the subject to the delayed scouts without explicitly confirming it. The advice distinguishes a developmental possibility from a current standard. It leaves open how often he would spend resources on a rough candidate, and what he judges when he has more than one performance to see. [K2](GKM_KUROI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#k2-hif-scouting-and-the-sisters)

A recurring cutaway at the end of several different students' Dear 010-01 chapters shows a similar division of effort. Kuroi says he already has a plan, judges that with Hatsuboshi's reigning Prima Stella absent he need not recall Gekka, and charges Shion with demonstrating Gokugetsu's ability. That is a direct threshold for committing his stronger performer, even if the unspecified measures he boasts of cannot be independently recovered from the boast. These ten versions preserve the same six Kuroi voice assets and the same substantive exchange. They are a recurring construction across routes, with one example of the decision pattern. In Tsubame's separate N.I.A. storyline, Sena and the Producer interpret Gokugetsu's school-versus-school publicity as entertainment and a bid for recognition. The Producer credits Kuroi and says he learned of it only once it was public. That informed attribution adds one context for his reputation but cannot supply every backstage choice. In Sena's own story the Producer qualifies Kuroi's reputation for using any means, and Hatsuboshi headmaster Juo Kunio treats Gekka as the instigator of a challenge Kuroi may not even know about yet. The outburst the script frames under Kuroi's name there is embedded in Kunio's anticipation of his reaction. It cannot make every Gokugetsu move Kuroi's or identify the unnamed recipients of his later insult in another track. [K3](GKM_KUROI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#k3-competitive-control-and-its-ceilings)

## The exchange with Gekka

The H.I.F. scenes give Kuroi an interlocutor who can challenge his own work. Gekka says Shion has failed to change and that Kuroi's producing shares responsibility. He returns the question, asking whether Gekka can say she is fulfilling her own role as elder sister. Gekka says she regularly contacts Shion, despite usually being in America, to make her reconsider her ways. Kuroi expresses surprise and asks what kind of contact it is. Gekka shows him. He reads out two of her messages, calling Shion a foolish sister and ordering her to report test results and provide her lesson menu. He points out the absence of an answer. Gekka blames Shion for that too. Kuroi drops the subject and returns to the festival and Hatsuboshi. [K2](GKM_KUROI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#k2-hif-scouting-and-the-sisters)

The sequence makes reciprocal contact visible as a problem. Gekka counts regular directives as evidence of doing her duty. Kuroi examines the content and notices that the recipient has not replied. He neither instructs Gekka to apologize nor offers to intervene with Shion. The initial question is also a professionally useful retort after Gekka challenges his producing. Both readings fit. He can make a pointed observation about an interpersonal problem even as he has an incentive to turn a criticism around. A generous intent, a sarcastic intent, or a durable concern about these sisters requires more evidence than his choice to ask and verify. The independent story between Shion and Nadeshiko supplies a useful control: when Nadeshiko imagines an opportunity to mediate, Shion explicitly tells her to stop. Shion's lack of a reply to the older sister's texts remains a separate circumstance. [K2](GKM_KUROI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#k2-hif-scouting-and-the-sisters)

## Offers, refusal and actual leverage

In Hanami Ume's Dear 016, Kuroi begins by remarking that both sisters defeated Kaya Rinha, formerly of the idol unit SyngUp!, and congratulates Ume's Producer. He acknowledges that Gokugetsu dislikes the result but suggests the stage benefited both sides. When the Producer asks whether the benefit to 961 Pro concerns Rinha, Kuroi answers with his own stated purpose of advancing the idol industry. The Producer replies that everyone in the business might say the same. Kuroi laughs, tells the student to learn more about society, recognizes their ability and asks him to bring Ume to 961 Pro. He addresses the Producer as the route to Ume and as part of the prospective move. The actual transfer terms, Ume's permission and Kuroi's complete reasons for taking an interest remain open. [K4](GKM_KUROI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#k4-ume-dear-016-shared-and-alternative-replies)

The Producer says no. Kuroi asks why and offers to hear the objection. Three distinct player replies follow.

- **Position.** The Producer questions whether the agency employs people under the title “Producer.” Kuroi asks whether the title is so important. The Producer says it is, and Kuroi answers with an acknowledgement. There is no counteroffer or proof of a corporate rule in that exchange.
- **Compliance.** Kuroi says Hatsuboshi is circulating ill reports about him. He calls two unassigned referents “the old fossil” and “the cold-blooded one,” and asks the Producer to relay his claim that the school's lessons are false and defamatory. That is his protest. The source neither adjudicates the charge nor names the two people in this choice.
- **Compensation.** The Producer estimates a future compensation level for himself at 100 Pro and uses Ume's expected impact to set a minimum for considering a move. Kuroi protests that the Producer is asking an excessive price. No payment is offered or agreed.

The voices and actions in each choice belong to its own route. In the common close Kuroi puts off further discussion and expects them to make the “right choice.” The later Ume, in Dear 035, asks about a rumor of a move next year. Her Producer acknowledges repeated approaches from multiple agencies and says replies are on hold. Ume names 100 Pro as her first choice and wants to discuss timing after H.I.F. She had genuinely considered competing against Hatsuboshi idols for 961 and heard that its president often grants idols' requests. Her attraction to the prospect and her ultimate ranking are her own. Her praise of what she has heard does not establish Kuroi's private habits. [K4](GKM_KUROI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#k4-ume-dear-016-shared-and-alternative-replies) · [K5](GKM_KUROI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#k5-recipients-and-counterexamples)

Temari's track gives a stronger example of a negotiable exchange. In Dear 025 her Producer says he arranged for Temari and Hataya Misuzu to train with 961 idols. He says he promised both would transfer after graduation if their side lost against Rinha’s side. Temari objects to the promise being made before she was consulted. The terms reach the reader through the Producer's account and Temari understands Kuroi to have granted permission. In Dear 026 Kuroi directly anticipates getting all of SyngUp! and is surprised that Temari has already won twice. He notices the disadvantage of fighting her on Hatsuboshi's ground and asks whether the confident Producer has rigged the waiting room. Informed that his party and Temari share that room, he registers surprise and warns that a disturbance before the performance would hurt the hosts too. Temari says she won against Rinha. Rinha later acknowledges that Temari overtook the idol she once admired. The win checks the certainty with which Kuroi expected acquisition. This is a negotiated rivalry between capable opponents, and the suspicion of dirty tricks is a question he asks. It supplies no proof that anyone rigged anything or that he owned a right to the students themselves. [K5](GKM_KUROI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#k5-recipients-and-counterexamples)

Rinha supplies a bounded alternative to a recruiter who cares only about winning. In Temari Dear 016 she says Kuroi approached her and offered to help conclude her idol career after her attempt to say goodbye properly to her fans had failed. It is Rinha's account of the offer and her own eventual plan to entrust the fans to another idol. His brief invitation to 961 Pro is directly quoted and assigned a Kuroi asset inside a memory montage in Support 3-0097 part 01. Together these establish a recruitment approach and Rinha's understanding that it could serve her aim. The source does not give us the negotiation or disclose his reasons. The offer can serve both professional leverage and a departing idol's own concern. [K5](GKM_KUROI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#k5-recipients-and-counterexamples)

## Written and performed address

In the locked script he can use 私, call the Producer by title, address that team as 君たち, and use the familiar superior's お前 with the Shirakusas. With professionals he offers a question about their judgment or asks how they value a term. He gives younger subordinates blunt instructions and often uses a short first name. Sound-marking written laughter, a drawn-out line or pauses can frame an entrance, a mocking reply or a change of tack. A subordinate's deferential response should be weighed with her actual action. Gekka calling him by surname and criticizing his work, for example, has a different force from Shion addressing “Chairman” and accepting an assignment. The scene samples show him as a silhouette, alongside voiced Kuroi, unvoiced selectable Producer dialogue and tense electronic scoring. These layers separately contribute to how the exchange presents confrontation. They cannot establish that the charge is true, when his anger is strategic, who the epithets identify or how the other two written choices would sound. The same degree of delivery cannot be carried over to Shion or Gekka. There is no supported sung voice, song repertoire or personal musical preference for Kuroi in this portrait. ## How far the portrait travels

When an opponent reveals high talent, Kuroi is likely to acknowledge the result and investigate whether there is a future competitive or contractual advantage. If the opponent rebuts him, he may ask the grounds, use those grounds to question the person or defend his own position, and postpone a commitment. Status, ethics and compensation are separate triggers with separate written replies. If he has a colleague with recognized judgment, as with Gekka, he can use her assessment and assign work while challenging her justification for a different set of choices. When a rival's plan confounds his expectations, he can show surprise and probe what was arranged. These are observed conduct patterns or bounded extensions within the same institutional settings. They do not predict his actions with an ordinary family, guarantee another negotiation's outcome or establish that apparent respect will protect a student's right to decide. [K2](GKM_KUROI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#k2-hif-scouting-and-the-sisters) · [K4](GKM_KUROI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#k4-ume-dear-016-shared-and-alternative-replies) · [K5](GKM_KUROI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.md#k5-recipients-and-counterexamples)

His ordinary conduct here consists of going to performances, scrutinizing work, arranging scouts and following up on what others did. The scripts supply neither a recoverable private biography nor a grounded account of food, family, money outside negotiation, leisure beyond the expressed wish to be entertained by the festival, health or intimate attachments. The evidence follows varying demands on an established executive. It gives no secure developmental account of his own childhood, former career or internal changes of belief. Retain the differences between Gekka's motives, Shion's conduct, Rinha's account, Ume's own choices, a Juo's report and Kuroi's actual words.
