The review keeps song communication, rendered performance, authored/lyric imagery, static-cover presentation and fan montage distinct. The new work is direct **sampled-still** inspection plus native-channel whole-recording analysis. It is not a new listening or continuous choreography pass. Every current visual point has original PTS, a frame hash and a displayed contact-sheet receipt in [the observation log](SUPPORTING_DATA/FULL_REBUILD_HIRO_OBSERVATIONS.md).

They do not identify genre, character essence, vocal effort, gain decisions or developmental skill.

These results establish failure of exact whole-recording identity in the stated native format and clock. They do not establish audible difference, different takes, musical causes or unmatched sections being perceptually dissimilar. A static cover cannot be treated as the same recording with only the camera removed.

## 1. Musical thesis — curated experimentalism, portable resistance

**Inherited musical interpretation:** a number of Hiro-specific works favor unusual form, and their relation to the story can be read as making nonfit productive. This remains a qualified qualitative thesis because the current reviewer has not listened directly.

**Fresh visual finding:** authored works employ materially different graphic strategies: soft patterned figures becoming fragmented in H14, flat body-part/prop fragments in H24, repeated portraits under typographic and glitch-like overlays in H32, and changing urban/abstract imagery in H18. This supports varied authored visual construction, rather than one uniform “experimental Hiro” look.

Common repertoire is not an optically neutral control. H21 duplicates bodies and breaks space into digital monitors; H25 distorts room perspective and figure scale. Conversely, foundational H12 already contains broad limb extension and a raised-leg pose. “Curated experimentalism” and “portable resistance” must therefore remain interpretive distinctions. Neither a camera device nor a whole-mix scalar can assign one entirely to repertoire and the other entirely to Hiro's skill (H-AV-009/010/018).
