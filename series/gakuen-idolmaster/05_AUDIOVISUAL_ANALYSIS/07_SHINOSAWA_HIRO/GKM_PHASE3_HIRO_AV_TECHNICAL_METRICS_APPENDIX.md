## 4. Safe uses

- identify sources worth closer qualitative comparison;
- distinguish a static/full-mix source from a rapidly edited MV;
- compare source-level dynamics and explicitly defined spectral descriptors within the packet;
- detect when a genre or performance claim is being inferred from source format rather than character behavior;
- reproduce the audit after later backfills.

## 5. Unsafe uses

- ranking characters by talent from different MVs;
- claiming Hiro's pure pitch/timbre from mixed scenes;
- treating higher motion as better dancing;
- treating an experimental-proxy composite as a musicological genre verdict;
- converting crisis-linked performance change into a safety recommendation.
