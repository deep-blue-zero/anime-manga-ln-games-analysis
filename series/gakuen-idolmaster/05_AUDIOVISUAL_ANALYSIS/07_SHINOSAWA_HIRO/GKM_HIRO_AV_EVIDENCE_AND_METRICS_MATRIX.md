## Measurement discipline

- Loudness and spectral values are full-mix/source measurements.
- Dialogue sources include other speakers, BGM, interface sounds, and edit transitions.
- Tempo and form proxies can be unstable for rubato, half-time/double-time, spoken openings, or highly edited songs.
- Visual motion/cut proxies are sensitive to encoding and source type.
- No metric by itself determines “experimental,” “better,” “more emotional,” or “more skilled.”
