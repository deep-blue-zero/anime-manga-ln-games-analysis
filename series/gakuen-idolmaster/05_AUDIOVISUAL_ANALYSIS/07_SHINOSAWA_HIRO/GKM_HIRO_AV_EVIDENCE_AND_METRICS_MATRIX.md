## Source-form crosswalk

| source form | what it can establish | what it cannot establish alone |
|---|---|---|
| Dear compilation | acted story progression, speech timing, visible reactions, scene transitions | isolated Hiro vocal metrics; alternate-route universality |
| song commu | character-authorship context, intended addressee, relation to route | final arrangement/choreography meaning without performance source |
| 3DMV | rendered body, virtual-camera relation, expression, game performance form | authorial symbolism of an independent MV; real-stage performer behavior |
| official/authored MV | symbolic montage, graphic/editorial world, official audiovisual interpretation | literal in-story chronology unless separately established |
| static/full-mix presentation | recording-level audio comparison | choreography or dynamic visual dramaturgy |
| fan derivative | reception salience, candidate comparisons, editorial argument | primary-source chronology, causation, animation intent |

## Measurement discipline

- Loudness and spectral values are full-mix/source measurements.
- Dialogue sources include other speakers, BGM, interface sounds, and edit transitions.
- Tempo and form proxies can be unstable for rubato, half-time/double-time, spoken openings, or highly edited songs.
- Visual motion/cut proxies are sensitive to encoding and source type.
- No metric by itself determines “experimental,” “better,” “more emotional,” or “more skilled.”
