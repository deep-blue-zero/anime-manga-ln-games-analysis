# HANAMI SAKI — AUDIOVISUAL BASELINE AND REQUEST SET

## 0. Evidence boundary

This document identifies the audiovisual objects needed to test Saki’s provisional musical and performed-character model. It makes **no claim yet** about:

- vocal timbre, register, tessitura, breath, attack, vibrato, diction, or expressive control;
- composition beyond officially published credits;
- arrangement, harmony, rhythm, mix, or instrumental texture;
- choreography, editing, camera, color, costume, or visual dramaturgy;
- the emotional delivery of requested dialogue scenes.

Those claims require the raw audio/video supplied by the user. Public pages are used only to verify identity, release metadata, credits, and the existence of official MVs or character versions.

## 4. Analytical questions for the baseline

### 4.1 Speaking voice versus singing voice

- Does Saki’s singing preserve the forward, commanding quality of her ordinary speech?
- Where does singing expose softness, breath, strain, or inwardness unavailable in dialogue?
- Does the performed voice change after STEP3 in ways that support the text’s `無敵` → `不屈` distinction?

### 4.2 Composition and arrangement

- Is there a recurring harmonic, rhythmic, or instrumental grammar across Saki’s individual works?
- Do `Fighting My Way` and `Wildest Flower`, both officially credited to HIROMI/Giga, form a deliberate before/after pair?
- Is `Boom Boom Pow` a reinforcement of confident physicality, a complication, or a separate public image?
- Does `EGO` expose a part of Saki not legible in the ADV corpus?

### 4.3 Vocal dramaturgy

- What must Saki do vocally to turn boasting into vulnerability without losing propulsion?
- Does she attack phrases with athletic precision, theatrical force, controlled brightness, or another profile?
- How do chorus expansions, breaks, drops, bridges, or climactic notes relate to self-reconstruction?

### 4.4 MV/visual dramaturgy

- Is the body shown as weapon, laboring instrument, idealized image, or vulnerable surface?
- How often does Saki look directly at the viewer?
- Does the camera isolate her, multiply her, or embed her in accumulated memories/relationships?
- What visual differences separate the pre-defeat and post-defeat songs?

### 4.5 Shared-song individuation

For `Campus mode!!`, `がむしゃらに行こう！`, `ガラクタロード`, `GO MY WAY!!`, and `ENDLESS DANCE`:

- Which choices belong to the composition and which to Saki’s rendition?
- Does her version create a stable individual signature across unlike songs?
- How does she compete with prior singers or simultaneous variants?

## 6. Submission naming recommendation

To avoid YouTube-title and transcript-ID mismatch, supplied files can use:

```text
AV-MUS-SAKI-001_Fighting_My_Way_official_MV.<ext>
AV-MUS-SAKI-001_Fighting_My_Way_full_audio.<ext>
AV-DIA-SAKI-001_adv_dear_hski_023.<ext>
```

The original public title can remain in metadata or an accompanying text file. The AV crosswalk will preserve official, community, and machine identifiers simultaneously.

## 7. Baseline sufficiency gate

Saki’s sonic identity is considered minimally established only after direct inspection of:

- `Fighting My Way`;
- one confident/contrasting second work (`Boom Boom Pow` or `EGO`);
- `Campus mode!! [Saki Solo]` or another shared-song solo version;
- `Wildest Flower`;
- one prescribed/inherited song (`ガラクタロード` or `GO MY WAY!!`).

The remaining works deepen coverage but do not block the next textual character-core pass.
