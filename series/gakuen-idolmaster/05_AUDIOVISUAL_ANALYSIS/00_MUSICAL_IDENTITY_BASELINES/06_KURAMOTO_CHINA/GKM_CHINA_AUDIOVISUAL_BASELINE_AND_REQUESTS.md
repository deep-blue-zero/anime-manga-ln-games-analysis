Lead with **public/uploader-facing Japanese identity**, not `adv_*` filenames. Prefer complete MVs, 3DMVs, song commus, and STEP/親愛度 compilations over manually cut clips. Internal IDs below are provenance/crosswalk keys only.

# Key voice-acting hypotheses to test

1. Is China's high-polite `ですわ` register sonically stable across panic, determination, grief, and public command, or does it reorganize substantially?
2. How does early cheerful incompetence sound relative to late `きりっ` authority?
3. Is self-deprecation played as comic certainty, genuine shame, strategic humility, or different combinations by story state?
4. How does Dear 006's privilege anxiety sound relative to Dear 011's more governed stewardship language?
5. Does China sound qualitatively different when she is **asking for help** versus **commanding/delegating** versus **accepting nondelegable responsibility**?
6. How is the `ふにゃっ♪ / きりっ！` integration performed in Dear 034/037?
7. Does Dear 036's summit command preserve audible fragility or become a fully stabilized regal register?
8. How does her speech toward Hiro differ from Sena, Kanae, the Producer, grandfather, and anonymous fans?

# Key musical/MV hypotheses to test

1. `Wonder Scale`: whether beginner vulnerability is framed as weakness, invitation, charm, aspiration, or some combination.
2. `日々、発見的ステップ！`: whether wonder/curiosity remains China's stable center after professionalization.
3. `Campus mode!!`: inherited school ideal becoming something she can personally inhabit.
4. `空と約束`: whether STEP3 self-authorship and summit obligation become more expansive/authoritative in staging and singing.
5. `みちなるひろがる`: whether relational power becomes genuinely dyadic with Hiro rather than China-centered patronage.
6. Across works: whether China's sonic identity is primarily regal/cinematic, bright/curious, comic/flustered, or a deliberately integrated range analogous to her `ふにゃ/きり` self-model.
