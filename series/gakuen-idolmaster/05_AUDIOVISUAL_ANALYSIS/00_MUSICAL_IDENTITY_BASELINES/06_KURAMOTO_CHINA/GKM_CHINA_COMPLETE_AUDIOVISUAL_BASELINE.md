# 1. Executive thesis — accountable inheritance realized as receptive authority

The textual core defines China through **accountable inheritance and relational legitimacy**:

> China becomes capable of treating wealth, love, education, fans, collaborators, and institutional trust neither as shameful contamination nor as unconditional entitlement, but as forces she must govern, redistribute, and answer for.

The audiovisual pass preserves that thesis and identifies its formal mechanism:

> **Kuramoto China is the idol of accountable inheritance realized as receptive authority. Her characteristic audiovisual grammar is receive → register → organize → answer. She first lets another person, demand, correction, or gift become visible in her body—wide-eyed attention, a hand at the chest or face, lowered posture, clasped hands, delighted fluster, or open uncertainty. She then organizes what she has received into a public response: a bow, an offered hand, a carefully stated rule, a delegated task, a frontal declaration, or a stage address that names the people supporting her.**

A compact formulation is:

> **She governs what she has received by making herself the point at which it must answer.**

This is not a solitary-sovereign model. China does not mature by proving that she never needed the Kuramoto family, Kanae, the Producer, Hiro, fans, trainers, predecessors, or institutional resources. She matures by acquiring three capacities:

1. to distinguish aid from coercion;
2. to convert received resources into accountable action;
3. to stand alone at the moment when judgment cannot be delegated.

Her softness is therefore not the opposite of authority. It is the **receptive phase** that makes her authority relational rather than merely imposed. Her late `きりっ` presence is not a replacement for `ふにゃっ`; it is an organized use of the same person’s attention, gratitude, fear, and social intelligence.

The complete AV sequence can be summarized as follows:

> **Dear 001–010 — China’s cultivated polish surrounds real technical weakness; the route teaches her that accurate self-knowledge can begin rather than cancel a vocation.**  
> **Dear 011–020 — support is divided into legitimate aid and commanded affection; fan relation becomes part of the idol’s actual body of power.**  
> **Dear 021–027 — leadership becomes recruitment, delegation, and finally nondelegable exposure before Sena and the electorate.**  
> **Dear 028–033 — the “fluke win” strategy becomes ethically untenable once others invest trust in her; Hiro emerges as a rival whose growth China genuinely desires.**  
> **Dear 034–037 — `ふにゃ/きり` becomes an integrated self-model; D-CHINA victory is claimed without revisionist meritocracy, while top-idol self-naming remains unfinished.**  
> **`Wonder Scale` — beginner smallness becomes invitation and public charm rather than apology.**  
> **`日々、発見的ステップ！` — curiosity turns technical growth into a way of attending to the world.**  
> **`雪解けに` — family privilege is restored to its affective history: being loved creates warmth, debt, and obligation.**  
> **`Campus mode!!` — inherited school tradition becomes inhabitable without becoming privately owned.**  
> **`空と約束` — China helps author a public object for people beyond her existing fan base; office and performance converge.**  
> **`みちなるひろがる` — vertical support gives way to dyadic creation, shared focality, and reciprocal future-making.**

The deepest audiovisual correction is not “China is secretly stronger than she thinks.” That would flatten both her genuine beginnerhood and the route’s critique of inherited advantage. The stronger conclusion is:

> **China’s legitimacy does not emerge when weakness disappears. It emerges when she becomes capable of receiving help without surrendering judgment, using power without commanding love, and accepting public responsibility without pretending she authored herself alone.**

# 2. The audiovisual grammar — receive, register, organize, answer

## 2.1 Receptivity is active

China’s most recurrent acted shapes are not conquest poses. They are forms of reception:

- hands clasped in front of the chest;
- one hand touching the chest, cheek, or mouth;
- a slight forward lean when listening;
- quick widening of the eyes;
- delighted or alarmed recoil;
- lowered gaze during difficult self-assessment;
- bows and carefully completed gestures;
- smiles that visibly include embarrassment rather than eliminating it.

These gestures can look conventionally “soft,” but their narrative function is not passivity. China is exceptionally willing to let information reach her. She can be hurt by Temari’s correction, frightened by a new demand, embarrassed by praise, or overwhelmed by inherited expectations without making the information unusable.

This strengthens the textual claim that epistemic humility is one of her most important talents. She does not begin as the best singer or dancer. She begins as someone able to learn because bad news is not automatically converted into resentment or denial.

## 2.2 Registration precedes action

China’s audiovisual scenes frequently allow a demand or realization to remain visible for a beat before she answers. The face and posture register:

- “this is larger than I expected”;
- “I am not presently capable”;
- “this gift is frightening because it matters”;
- “this person’s support has created a debt I cannot ignore”;
- “this role asks me to become publicly legible.”

Her route does not make this delay a defect. It is the interval in which inherited or relational power becomes morally processed rather than reflexively used.

## 2.3 Organization is the mature skill

Later China increasingly turns reception into organization:

- asking Kanae to remain her first ally;
- recruiting people whose strengths differ from hers;
- delegating tasks while retaining responsibility for the whole;
- addressing different audiences according to their actual relation to the event;
- deciding when family resources are legitimate production support;
- refusing to command affection or votes;
- accepting that some contests require her to stand alone.

Her leadership grammar is therefore not “become the strongest body in the room.” It is closer to:

> **recognize the field → identify the people and resources already present → give them a legitimate relation to the goal → expose oneself where authority requires personal answerability.**

## 2.4 The answer becomes public

In the late route and stage materials, China’s gestures become more frontal and declarative:

- open-palmed address;
- direct gaze toward the interlocutor or virtual audience;
- pointing or extending a hand without losing the smile;
- upright stage posture in darker, more authoritative costuming;
- deliberate acceptance of the `次期生徒会長` and Prima Stella frames.

Yet the public body never fully removes the earlier receptive one. The late material repeatedly returns to fluster, tears, hand-to-chest reassurance, or delighted fan response. This is not failure to complete the transformation. It is the transformation’s content.

> **AV finding:** China’s authority is credible because it remains visibly permeable to other people.

# 3. `わたくし`, `ですわ`, and the cultivated register

## 3.1 The lexical register is stable identity infrastructure

China’s frequent `わたくし`, `ですわ`, `くださいませ`, and highly courteous request forms are not temporary costume language. They remain available across:

- technical embarrassment;
- panic;
- gratitude;
- anger at her grandfather;
- friendship with Hiro and Ume;
- deference toward Sena;
- command and delegation;
- public office;
- victory speech;
- post-victory collapse.

This matters because it prevents a simplistic “real China versus aristocratic mask” interpretation. Her cultivated speech is genuinely habitual. Even when the sentence becomes comic, breathless, or broken, the underlying social vocabulary often survives.

## 3.2 Stability does not mean emotional uniformity

The register remains lexically recognizable while scene control changes dramatically. China can speak polished Japanese while:

- visibly and structurally losing composure;
- stretching a cry of alarm;
- reducing herself to hesitant fragments;
- rushing through delighted realization;
- performing a formal public declaration;
- returning to a smaller, intimate cadence with the Producer or Kanae.

The strongest model is therefore:

> **stable social language, variable organizational control.**

Her `お嬢様` register is neither a lie nor a complete transcript of her emotional state. It is the linguistic home through which many emotional states pass.

## 3.3 `ごきげんよう` as institutional self-placement

The `Campus mode!!` communication begins with a conspicuously cultivated greeting. In China’s case, `ごきげんよう` belongs to the same social field as `わたくし` and `ですわ`: elite-school tradition, ceremonial femininity, and an awareness that speech itself places the speaker within an institution.

The line is not enough to prove that she uses `ごきげんよう` in every ordinary encounter. What the scene supports is narrower: China can inhabit that greeting without parody because it matches both her upbringing and her idealized understanding of Hatsuboshi tradition.

## 3.4 `ふにゃ/きり` is not pronoun switching

Dear 034 explicitly trains China to alternate between the relaxed `ふにゃっ♪` self and the organized `きりっ！` public self. The raw script confirms that the alternation is built not only through words but through different facial overrides, body motions, camera emphasis, and eventually comic overload.

Crucially, China does not switch to another first-person pronoun or abandon her cultivated sentence endings when relaxed. The distinction is not between two sociolects. It is between two levels of bodily and temporal organization inside one identity.

> **Revised formulation:** `ふにゃ` and `きり` are not “authentic self versus false role.” They are receptive softness and organized authority, both carried by `わたくし`.

# 4. Genuine beginnerhood and the dignity of accurate weakness

## 4.1 Dear 002 — polish does not erase incompetence

China’s early route makes weakness unmistakable. She is winded, physically limited, poor at song and dance, and capable of describing the gap without pretending it is noble. Her composed vocabulary can briefly make her sound more accomplished than she is, but the body and timing refuse the illusion.

The important audiovisual effect is a productive mismatch:

- cultivated manners;
- photogenic appearance;
- high affective legibility;
- actual idol technique near the bottom of the school.

This gap is not merely comic. It gives the route a precise question: **what kind of talent can be real before technical command exists?**

## 4.2 `人に好かれる才能` is performed as legibility, not mystique

The Producer’s doctrine that China has a talent for being liked should not be universalized into a complete theory of idolhood. Within her route, however, the AV layer clarifies why people respond to her.

China is easy to read. Delight, panic, gratitude, admiration, confusion, and determination move visibly across the face and body. Her cultivated register does not make her opaque. It gives emotionally legible reactions a recognizable social form.

That helps explain why she can remain valuable in scenes where she is technically weak. People do not merely project perfection onto her. They are invited to witness effort without being asked to deny the weakness.

## 4.3 Beginner charm is not a permanent exemption

`Wonder Scale` initially turns novice status into a public identity. But the route does not allow charm to become a substitute for craft forever. `Campus mode!!`, the late Dear route, and `空と約束` require actual acquisition, rehearsal, and the ability to carry more demanding public obligations.

The AV corpus therefore supports a two-part judgment:

1. beginner vulnerability can be legitimate stage material;
2. the right to continue using it depends on visible work and increasing responsibility.

# 5. Privilege — from shame to stewardship without distributive absolution

## 5.1 Dear 006 makes advantage visible

China’s wealth, family name, servants, appearance, and cultivated upbringing are not background flavor. They enter the route as real causal forces. She understands that others must spend more to reach similar opportunities and initially treats this asymmetry as evidence that her achievements cannot be fully hers.

The acted route does not solve the discomfort by declaring that everyone receives different advantages. China’s panic and hesitation preserve the moral asymmetry. Her request—do not `濫用` the power—becomes a durable rule.

## 5.2 Legitimate aid versus commanded affection

Dear 011 offers the clearest boundary. China rejects the idea that family subordinates can simply be ordered to like or vote for her. She accepts a fan club and ordinary production support, but insists that affection must emerge through idol activity.

The distinction is not metaphysically perfect. Publicity budgets, access, and institutional leverage still shape what affection becomes possible. The route nevertheless makes an ethically meaningful separation between:

- creating conditions for encounter;
- commanding the evaluative outcome.

## 5.3 `空と約束` makes full resource use conditional on audience responsibility

The song communication is especially important because China does not invoke the Kuramoto apparatus merely to magnify herself. She first identifies that the audience has changed: school peers, seniors, institutional stakeholders, and people who are not already “her” fans. Only after defining what she owes that audience does the production scale become legitimate.

The sequence is:

> audience recognition → self-authored intention → complex proposal → full production support.

That is a more mature use of privilege than either refusal or indulgence. The resources answer to a public purpose China has articulated.

## 5.4 The AV layer cannot close distributive fairness

Regal costumes, polished camera language, photogenic warmth, and impressive production can make inherited authority look deserved. That aesthetic persuasiveness is itself part of the problem.

> **Counterpressure:** the fact that China uses privilege responsibly does not make the privilege equally available, nor does beautiful stewardship prove a just distribution of opportunity.

This remains open for Phase 8 institutional synthesis and cast-wide comparison.

# 6. Fans — support becomes constitutive power and public obligation

## 6.1 From abstract audience to remembered persons

China’s route repeatedly turns `ファンの皆さま` from a numerical mass into people who have spent time, money, attention, and hope. The acted communications make gratitude physically visible: chest-centered gesture, softened posture, attentive gaze, and direct address recur around fan recognition.

The mature doctrine in Dear 020 is not merely “fans help me.” It is:

> **fans and China together constitute the idol.**

This differs from claiming that individual agency disappears. Rather, the social practice cannot exist without both performer and audience.

## 6.2 The danger of under-crediting

China’s interdependence can become a defense against self-credit. If every success belongs to the network, she can avoid claiming the part that was nondelegably hers. The late route corrects this by forcing her to say both:

- many people made this possible;
- **the person who won was Kuramoto China.**

The D-CHINA victory speech is powerful because it refuses the false choice. China names support and victory in the same address.

## 6.3 `雪解けに` — old fans and family

`雪解けに` gives fan obligation a pre-professional history. The Hinamatsuri performance is directed toward the people who loved China before she had public competence. The communication moves through family absence, Kanae’s care, the grandfather’s transformation, and China’s sense that love creates responsibility.

The scene’s intimacy and relatively restrained visual envelope distinguish it from the high-energy institutional materials. The dominant action is recollection and acknowledgment rather than conquest.

This strengthens the `愛された責任` claim while preserving its danger:

> love can generate gratitude and vocation, but a child who experiences love as debt may over-obligate herself.

# 7. Hiro — patronage becomes reciprocity

## 7.1 Why Hiro matters

Hiro is initially someone China can admire, worry about, support, and materially assist. That can reproduce a vertical pattern: the wealthy, socially capable girl helps the fragile genius.

The later route refuses to stop there. Hiro defeats, teaches, challenges, observes, and eventually becomes the person whose growth China wants even when it threatens China’s own chance at the summit.

## 7.2 `空と約束` already contains collaborative authorship

When China is asked to define a new song, Hiro does not supply the answer. She helps China look at the actual people before her and articulate an intention. China then asks for practical help producing the proposal.

This division is healthy:

- Hiro does not disappear into service;
- China does not outsource the decision;
- collaboration enables rather than replaces authorship.

## 7.3 `みちなるひろがる` shares focality

The rendered performance repeatedly positions China and Hiro as a pair rather than a center and satellite. They face one another, exchange gestures, occupy complementary sides of the frame, and move through shared psychedelic space. The authored MV goes further by giving each a distinct visual road—solitude, sky, travel, reflection—before bringing them into parallel and mirrored relations.

The duet therefore supports a strong revision:

> **China/Hiro is not mature because China becomes Hiro’s ideal patron. It becomes mature when each can be the other’s audience, rival, collaborator, and source of a future neither could privately author.**

## 7.4 Shared warmth without merged identity

The unit commu’s painting sequence matters. China remembers praise and criticism, turns attention into an image, and invites Hiro into another practice. Hiro’s response is not passive gratitude; she proposes a future activity for the group. Their intimacy produces further participation rather than closure.

The open question moves from “is this patronage?” to:

> how durable is the reciprocity when resources, technical ability, and institutional position diverge sharply?

That belongs to Phase 6 relationship synthesis.

# 8. Sena — aspiration, succession, and rival legitimacy

## 8.1 Admiration remains bodily

China’s response to Sena is not abstract respect. The route repeatedly lets admiration disrupt composure: widened eyes, delighted fluster, fan-like enthusiasm, and an almost devotional memory of performances. Sena is a living tradition China wants to enter.

## 8.2 Succession cannot be inherited by affection alone

The student-council route forces China to confront the problem of dynastic-looking succession. Being loved by the institution, supported by powerful adults, and personally favored cannot by itself legitimate office.

Dear 026 makes the ethical requirement explicit: on the final day, China chooses to face Sena alone so that the electorate can compare them and decide whether China is fit to be entrusted with the school.

This is the nondelegable core of her leadership theory.

## 8.3 D-CHINA victory does not erase the raw gap

When China defeats Sena in Dear 036, she refuses a revisionist story in which support proves she was secretly the strongest technical idol all along. She acknowledges that her raw skill remains near the bottom of the elite field and still says: **I won.**

That is the route’s most important act of self-credit.

## 8.4 Rivalry begins after inheritance

The transfer of title does not close the relation. Sena promises future competition outside school; China answers that Hatsuboshi’s Prima Stella will not lose even to a top idol. Succession becomes rivalry rather than replacement.

The relation therefore moves through:

> fan → candidate inheritor → publicly tested successor → rival.

# 9. The public-role problem — from mask to permeable office

## 9.1 Why the mask was necessary

China’s early social ease is not the same as commanding authority. The `次期生徒会長` persona helps her occupy a role whose expectations exceed her spontaneous confidence. Calling it a mask initially captures the effort and distance involved.

## 9.2 Dear 034 rejects the false-self model

China explicitly says the mask is part of her and not a lying version of herself. The raw script reinforces the claim by assigning the declaration controlled talk motion, a gentle facial state, and a deliberate negative gesture when she rejects falsity.

The scene then turns the issue into embodied practice. `ふにゃ` and `きり` receive distinct facial/motion configurations; rapid alternation eventually overloads the distinction and produces comic collapse.

The comedy performs the thesis:

> the two states can be distinguished, but they cannot be cleanly separated into two people.

## 9.3 Permeable authority

China’s best public role remains open to correction, affection, and visible strain. She can say “of course I am forcing myself” without treating effort as evidence of fraud. She can accept an authoritative costume while retaining ordinary embarrassment.

This produces the mature formulation:

> **The role is authentic when China can enter and leave it, explain what it costs, and use it to answer public expectations rather than escape private inadequacy.**

## 9.4 Office as an additional obligation

The risk remains serious. Once others depend on the reliable `きり` China, the role can become another form of inherited debt. She may feel required to preserve everyone’s faith at the expense of rest, dissent, or uncertainty.

Support cards and Phase 6 must test whether the role remains permeable under prolonged governance rather than only at the triumphant threshold.

# 10. H.I.F. and Prima Stella — socially produced strength becomes claimable

## 10.1 `まぐれ勝ち` reaches its limit

The “fluke win” strategy was initially an honest way to describe asymmetry. China could prepare conditions in which a weaker idol might defeat a stronger one without pretending the underlying technical hierarchy had disappeared.

By Dear 033, however, others have invested too much trust for the language to remain ethically neutral. China can no longer describe the prospective victory as mere accident because doing so would demean:

- the work she completed;
- the people who chose to support her;
- the rivals whose strength she intends to overcome;
- the audience prepared to judge her.

The route moves from probabilistic modesty to answerable confidence.

## 10.2 Dear 036 — claim without purification

China’s victory speech performs the mature synthesis:

- she names her weakness;
- she names the training that raised her threshold;
- she names the people who supported her;
- she names the strong rivals;
- she claims the victory as hers;
- she promises to keep shining **because** the relation continues.

This is not meritocratic purification. The network remains inside the claim.

## 10.3 Dear 037 — incomplete self-naming is preserved

After the public declaration, China returns to panic. The route explicitly states that the flustered and reliable versions are both loved. When the Producer invites her to call herself a top idol, she cannot yet say it in her own voice.

That refusal is not simply low self-esteem. She understands that title, technical superiority, public recognition, and self-concept are not identical. The route leaves the final internal ratification open.

> **AV conclusion:** D-CHINA closes the right to claim victory, not the full problem of self-authored rank.

# 11. Song and performance architecture

## 11.1 `Wonder Scale` — smallness as invitation

### Song communication

The first song is designed to introduce China. She is startled by the idea that the gentle work could represent her and has almost no confidence that she can learn it. The communication preserves real weakness while giving it a public form.

The culminating image—an atmosphere like warm sunlight—defines early China’s stage ethic more accurately than “princess”: she wants the room to feel welcomed and warmed.

### Rendered 3DMV

The dark theatrical stage, curtain, garden-like imagery, and China’s relatively small body in the frame give scale material form. Open arms, raised hands, and direct camera address gradually turn that smallness outward. She does not dominate the environment; she becomes the figure through whom wonder is shared.

### Authored official MV

The paper-cut forest, water, fish, stars, flowers, and cosmological imagery repeatedly place China inside worlds larger than herself. The symbolic grammar treats smallness as permeability to discovery, not humiliation.

> **Finding:** `Wonder Scale` does not apologize for weakness. It makes the capacity to be surprised a legitimate source of charisma.

## 11.2 `日々、発見的ステップ！` — curiosity after competence

The middle-development song tests whether China’s appeal survives technical improvement. Its brighter, faster arrangement and outdoor/festival staging emphasize movement, pointing, noticing, and direct participation.

China’s mature identity is therefore not a march from childish wonder toward regal distance. Curiosity remains central after she becomes more competent. The route’s social intelligence depends on it: she can govern because she keeps noticing people rather than treating them as fixed roles.

## 11.3 `雪解けに` — affection has a history

The communication provides the missing affective genealogy of privilege. The Kuramoto family is not merely capital; it is a network transformed by a child, loss, care, institutional duty, and deliberate reunion. The song is dedicated to old fans and family rather than the newest or largest audience.

This narrows a potential overgeneralization. China’s gratitude is not an abstract ideology imposed after she becomes an idol. It predates professional performance and helps explain why she experiences support as obligation.

## 11.4 `Campus mode!!` — inherited repertoire becomes inhabitable

China has watched, studied, and loved the school’s traditional performance. Her first reaction to a new costume is to assume it belongs to someone else. That reaction is central: inheritance initially appears as something she reveres from outside.

Technical difficulty prevents reverence from becoming entitlement. Temari’s instruction and China’s labor mediate entry into the tradition. When China finally wears the costume, she does not claim to supersede every predecessor. She treats prior performances as accumulated meaning and asks to add her own.

> **Finding:** institutional legitimacy is not possession of tradition; it is trained participation in a form that remains larger than the individual.

## 11.5 `空と約束` — public self-authorship

The Producer asks China to define a song around her current intention. China initially resists by correctly saying that earlier Producer-made songs are also hers. The new demand is not to reject relational authorship; it is to state what **this present China** wants the work to do.

Hiro helps her imagine the actual audience. China identifies that election and H.I.F. stages include people who are not already her fans. The resulting work is oriented toward school, predecessors, and public obligation.

The rendered performance’s darker blue/red arena, formal black-red costume, microphone-stand discipline, and open-handed address make the change visible. China can now carry a more authoritative visual field without losing warmth.

## 11.6 `みちなるひろがる` — shared future as performance form

The unit song is not merely two solo identities placed side by side. The game staging distributes attention, while the official MV gives China and Hiro distinct visual trajectories that become parallel without becoming identical.

The title’s logic—unknown paths widening—fits the relationship. China and Hiro do not validate one another by promising sameness. They make each other’s future less closed.

## 11.7 `ときめきのソルフェージュ` — cultivated fantasy as personal wonder

The authored MV uses theatre, storybook, cinema, curtain, and miniature-heroine imagery. It draws on China’s cultivated aesthetic environment without reducing her to social class. The crucial movement is from spectator to performer: she opens the frame, enters it, grows within it, and directs the musical object outward.

This is a mature form of the `Wonder Scale` principle. Large inherited worlds remain meaningful because China can still be delighted by them and invite others inside.

# 12. Portability tests — common repertoire and darker surfaces

## 12.1 `がむしゃらに行こう！`

The school-pop/sports-like performance places China inside a vigorous, less aristocratic frame. Her identity remains legible through earnest commitment, visible effort, and social openness. This argues against treating elegance as the only carrier of her appeal.

## 12.2 `ENDLESS DANCE`

The dark green/purple stage and angular, cooler vocabulary test a more controlled performance body. China can enter severity without becoming opaque. The material strengthens the view that `きり` is a usable mode rather than a fragile imitation.

## 12.3 `Howling over the World`

The cybernetic/glitch surface is the most aggressive range test. China’s presence remains recognizable because the performance cannot fully erase affective legibility and directness. The result should not be overread as proof that every harsh genre is equally “hers,” but it demonstrates more portability than the stock gentle-princess model predicts.

# 13. Kanae, grandfather, family, and the ethics of love

## 13.1 Kanae is not a prop

Kanae appears as caregiver, administrator, gatekeeper, fan, political ally, and person with her own attachment. The dedicated Dear 021 excerpt adds higher-resolution inspection but does not constitute new narrative evidence beyond the complete route packet.

China’s relation with Kanae is intimate and hierarchical at once. She can command, apologize, rely, tease, and express gratitude. The hierarchy cannot be erased by affection; the affection cannot be dismissed because hierarchy exists.

## 13.2 Grandfather’s love is causally dangerous

The grandfather’s requests create opportunities and coercive pressure. China’s route repeatedly requires her and others to restrain his tendency to convert love into command. The moral issue is not whether he loves her. It is whether love grants jurisdiction over outcomes that should remain chosen.

## 13.3 `雪解けに` complicates the debt model

China’s family-history performance reveals that gatherings and care are partly responses to earlier absence and loss. That history makes the support emotionally intelligible. It does not automatically make every later intervention legitimate.

The mature rule remains:

> love may explain power; it does not exempt power from governance.

# 14. The Producer — authorship, concentration, and enclosure risk

The Producer’s strongest function is interpretive and procedural. He repeatedly helps China distinguish:

- weakness from worthlessness;
- privilege from fraud;
- support from coercion;
- delegation from abdication;
- modesty from refusal of credit;
- role performance from falsehood.

He also creates tasks that require China to articulate an intention rather than merely obey an expert. `空と約束` is the clearest example.

The concentration risk remains. China’s language of teacherly devotion, her desire for praise, and the Producer’s role as privileged interpreter can make him an enclosure point through which every achievement must pass.

The AV packet supports trust and reciprocal affection, but it does not fully test:

- sustained disagreement;
- professional separation;
- China rejecting his framework and remaining secure;
- authority after his absence.

This remains **OPEN**.

# 15. Counterpressure register

## 15.1 Responsible privilege is still privilege

China’s ethics can regulate use without equalizing initial conditions. Cast-wide comparison must not turn good stewardship into distributive innocence.

## 15.2 Affective legibility can become marketable vulnerability

The same visible panic and gratitude that make China likable can be industrially packaged. The corpus does not yet settle when genuine openness becomes a demand to remain endearingly weak.

## 15.3 “Fans are my power” can burden fans and self

Constitutive fanhood may produce reciprocity, but it can also make criticism or departure feel like bodily loss. The packet contains little evidence about hostile, divided, or withdrawing audiences.

## 15.4 Delegation can reproduce class hierarchy

China’s ability to recruit and coordinate is real. It is also enabled by people trained or employed to answer her requests. Later phases must distinguish consensual collaboration from the frictionless responsiveness produced by status.

## 15.5 Public role can harden into compulsory poise

`きり` becomes authentic, but authenticity does not make it costless. Prolonged office may turn an owned role into a cage.

## 15.6 D-CHINA cannot become universal continuity

Prima Stella victory is authoritative within D-CHINA. It should inform thematic synthesis without overwriting conditional Produce outcomes.

# 17. Provisional reconstruction profile after AV

### Core desire
To turn a lightly begun dream into something worthy of the people who chose, loved, taught, supported, and trusted her.

### Core fear
That relationally produced achievement is not truly hers—or, conversely, that claiming it as hers will betray the people and advantages that made it possible.

### Governing deficit
Not material scarcity but insufficient practice attaching the self to difficult, non-purchasable stakes.

### Primary early defense
Accurate self-deprecation, deference, and cheerful dependence; these make correction usable but can also prevent self-credit.

### Mature competence
Receptive authority: noticing people and conditions, distinguishing legitimate from coercive power, recruiting differentiated allies, delegating work, and accepting nondelegable exposure.

### Stable linguistic identity
Cultivated `わたくし/ですわ/くださいませ` speech across a wide emotional range. Emotional rupture changes organization and tempo more than it replaces the sociolect.

### Stable bodily identity
Reception before declaration: chest/face-centered gesture, visible fluster, attentive lean, bow, then open hand, direct address, and organized public posture.

### Performance philosophy
A stage should make received support answerable. China’s best performances remember who is present, why they came, and what she owes that audience now.

### Relationship philosophy
Support is legitimate when it preserves choice and becomes reciprocal. Love creates responsibility but not unlimited jurisdiction.

### Institutional philosophy
Authority is legitimate when it recruits and delegates without hiding the moment at which the officeholder must stand alone and accept judgment.

### Failure mode
Over-obligation, under-crediting, aesthetic naturalization of privilege, and the conversion of public poise into compulsory self-management.

### Open developmental question
Can China retain receptive authority when fans withdraw, allies resist, the Producer disagrees, family support becomes unavailable, or office requires an unpopular decision?

# 19. Conclusion

The integrated AV corpus does not reveal a hidden, ordinary girl imprisoned beneath an aristocratic shell. It reveals something more demanding:

> **China’s cultivated language, receptive softness, public role, family inheritance, and relational dependence are all real. Her development consists in learning how to organize them without letting any one become an excuse for coercion, abdication, or self-erasure.**

`Wonder Scale` begins with a small figure inside a world larger than herself. `空と約束` gives that figure a public task. `みちなるひろがる` places her beside another person whose road cannot be reduced to hers. Dear 036 lets her claim victory; Dear 037 refuses to let the claim become easy.

The strongest current formulation is therefore:

> **Kuramoto China is the idol of accountable inheritance realized as receptive authority. She receives love, money, correction, tradition, rivals, and trust into a visibly permeable self, then makes them answer through choices she must finally own.**

The shorter retrieval phrase is:

> **receive → register → organize → answer.**
