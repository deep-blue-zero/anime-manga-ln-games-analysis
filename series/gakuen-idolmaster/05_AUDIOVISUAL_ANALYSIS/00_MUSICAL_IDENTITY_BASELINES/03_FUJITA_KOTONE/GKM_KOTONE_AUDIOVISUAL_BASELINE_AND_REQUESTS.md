# Fujita Kotone — Audiovisual Baseline and Whole-Video Request Plan

## 0. Acquisition rule

**Do not cut scenes.** Whole commu compilations, complete song commus, official/full music videos, and full game 3DMVs are preferred. They preserve setup, pauses, BGM, reaction shots, performance transitions, and post-scene emotional release.

Primary human-facing identifier order:

> **character — song / STEP / episode range — commu category**

Internal `adv_*` IDs are secondary crosswalks only.

720p is sufficient for dialogue commus when audio, subtitles, face animation, and timing are intact. 1080p or better is preferred for authored MV/3DMV visual close reading when readily available.

# 1. Minimum gate for the complete Kotone Phase-3 AV baseline

The minimum integrated baseline should contain:

### Dialogue / longitudinal acting

1. **藤田ことね 親愛度コミュ 1～10**
2. **藤田ことね 親愛度コミュ 11～20 / N.I.A.編**
3. **藤田ことね 親愛度コミュ 21～27 / STEP3 / H.I.F. late arc**

These three whole packets are preferable to extracting individual Dear scenes. Together they test the movement from precarity/delegated confidence through N.I.A. public identity to Sena rivalry, H.I.F. loss, debt repayment, naming, and `普通`.

### Music / performance core

4. **世界一可愛い私** — full audio and preferably official MV plus game 3DMV
5. **Yellow Big Bang！** — full audio plus 3DMV/MV if available
6. **Campus mode!! [藤田ことね]** — Kotone 3DMV/full performance
7. **自己肯定感爆上げ↑↑しゅきしゅきソング** — full audio/MV/3DMV as available
8. **ガラクタロード [藤田ことね]** — Kotone H.I.F. performance, if a full game capture is publicly available

### Minimum song commus

9. **世界一可愛い私 楽曲コミュ**
10. **Yellow Big Bang！ 楽曲コミュ**
11. **Campus mode!! 楽曲コミュ**
12. **自己肯定感爆上げ↑↑しゅきしゅきソング 楽曲コミュ**
13. **ガラクタロード 楽曲コミュ** if Kotone has a corresponding full public communication in the current game state/source set

This is enough to test the core claims about performed cuteness, money/authenticity integration, audience reciprocity, longitudinal voice state, and late competitive identity.
