# FUJITA KOTONE — COMPLETE AUDIOVISUAL BASELINE

## 0. Executive finding

The textual Phase-3 reading defined Fujita Kotone as **material security becoming self-authorship**. The audiovisual corpus confirms that thesis, but it also identifies the performance mechanism through which that development becomes publicly legible.

> **Kotone is the idol of performed reciprocity. She turns cuteness into a distributed recognition technology: she reads what another person wants, gives that person a felt moment of special attention, receives the resulting pleasure and confidence, and returns the energy at a larger scale.**

This does not replace the material-security thesis. It explains how the thesis becomes performance.

Kotone begins from a life in which attention, time, money, praise, food, sleep, work, and opportunity are scarce enough that every exchange must be monitored. Her first response to value is therefore often quantitative. What will the job pay? Will the song be sold? Are there royalties? How much did the ticket cost? Did every spectator receive enough pleasure to justify that cost? How many fans can she win? Is another girl receiving the affection or opportunity that might have belonged to her?

The audiovisual evidence does not show her escaping this logic. It shows her transforming it.

At her best, Kotone does not treat an audience as a pile of money. She treats money and attendance as evidence that people have already risked something: time, expectation, travel, attention, and disposable income. She therefore owes them not submission, but an answer. The answer is a performance in which each spectator can briefly feel individually recognized even inside a crowd.

That is why the strongest visual and dramatic through-line is not simply “cute imagery.” It is **directed attention**:

- eye contact played toward the camera;
- hands extended toward the crowd;
- repeated shifts between close facial framing and large audience reverse shots;
- call-and-response structures;
- explicit discussion of fanservice as making many people feel personally seen;
- the conversion of family recognition into confidence that can be broadcast across an arena;
- and the repeated claim that Kotone must visibly enjoy herself before the audience can fully enjoy her.

Her “cute” register is therefore not a mask hiding a rougher real self. It is one instrument within a larger adaptive system. The audiovisual corpus preserves at least six stable Kotone modes:

1. **sales/service cuteness** — deliberate sweetness, elongated address, bright solicitation, playful reward language;
2. **blunt working-girl speech** — clipped irritation, rough jokes, fast practical judgment, visible fatigue;
3. **competitive idol speech** — challenge, comparison, target-setting, and professional evaluation;
4. **anxious self-audit** — spiraling doubt, repeated questions, reassurance-seeking, and compulsive review;
5. **ordinary adolescent intimacy** — embarrassment, romance-coded curiosity, peer teasing, family warmth, and the desire for experiences she missed;
6. **public hospitality** — a stage voice that combines command, pleasure, and the promise that the audience’s investment will be returned.

No one mode is the hidden truth beneath the others. Hikaru Iida’s performance and the game’s staging make the transitions themselves part of Kotone’s identity. She can move from sugary flirtation to a deadpan complaint, from cash calculation to genuine gratitude, from professional post-loss analysis to crying, and from private insecurity to audience command without the work treating the earlier or later state as fraudulent.

The principal musical development is likewise not a movement away from “cute.” It is a widening of what cuteness can carry.

- `世界一可愛い私` establishes that weakness, greed, self-love, dependence, and ambition can all enter one idol persona.
- `Yellow Big Bang！` revises technical seriousness by making visible enjoyment the condition of audience pleasure.
- `Campus mode!!` turns fanservice into scalable recognition.
- `自己肯定感爆上げ↑↑しゅきしゅきソング` makes reassurance a deliberately manufactured collective ritual while exposing how strongly Kotone still depends on exclusive recognition.
- `ふわふわ` gives softness, idleness, missed youth, and bodily exhaustion a legitimate aesthetic form.
- `がむしゃらに行こう！`, `GO MY WAY!!`, and Re;IRIS test whether her identity survives shared or inherited material.
- `ENDLESS DANCE`, `Howling over the World`, and `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！` demonstrate that she can inhabit dark theatricality, cybernetic aggression, and internet-hyperpop excess without becoming unrecognizable.

The strongest bounded conclusion is therefore:

> **Kotone’s stable musical identity is less a single genre than a performance procedure: rapid social reading, direct address, emotional legibility, comic self-exposure, and the ability to make authored material feel like a personal exchange.**

That flexibility should not be romanticized as effortless versatility. It is partly labor acquired under precarity: customer awareness, price sensitivity, fast persona-switching, and the need to stay useful. Her development makes those capacities more freely authored, but does not erase their origin.

One requested comparison remains open. Neither a publicly accessible Kotone `ガラクタロード` song commu nor a Kotone-specific 3DMV could be established in the active source set. The exact Saki/Temari/Kotone same-song comparison must therefore remain unmade. `がむしゃらに行こう！` and `GO MY WAY!!` provide functional substitutes for testing portability, but they do not answer the work-specific `ガラクタロード` question.

That absence does not block the baseline. The current corpus is sufficient to close Kotone’s Phase-3 audiovisual pass.

## 1.3 Separation of audiovisual forms

This baseline does not flatten unlike objects.

### Official 2D MVs

These are authored visual arguments. Their illustration, editing, typography, motif systems, and spatial logic can be read as paratextual interpretations of the songs and character.

### In-game 3DMVs

These are embodied performances within a reusable game system. They provide valuable evidence about costume, gesture, camera relation, facial animation, crowd address, and how Kotone occupies a stage. However, choreography and camera may be shared with other character versions. A gesture appearing in Kotone’s version is not automatically unique to Kotone.

### Song commus

These provide the strongest bridge between text and performance. They explain what problem the song is solving, what Kotone thinks she is doing, how peers and Producer interpret her, and how emotional stakes are translated into stage intention.

### Static full-mix sources

`The Cute!!!` and the supplied full-song `Yellow Big Bang！` source support audio/full-mix comparison, not visual narrative claims.

## 1.4 Analytical limitations

1. **No isolated vocal stems.** All signal metrics describe complete mixes or game captures. They cannot by themselves establish vocal tessitura, breath pressure, resonance placement, or timbral brightness.
2. **Capture families differ.** Official MV audio, static full-mix uploads, and in-game 3DMV captures have different level and mastering conditions. Absolute LUFS is recorded for provenance, not treated as character evidence.
3. **Two staged files were not materialized.** The 11–20 Dear packet and 4K `GO MY WAY!!` 3DMV exceeded the connector’s working transfer ceiling. The exact dialogue and source metadata remain available; direct frame claims are not made from them.
4. **`ガラクタロード` remains open.** A Kotone-specific song commu and 3DMV could not be established. Functional comparison is possible through other shared/inherited songs, but exact work-level substitution would be dishonest.
5. **Shared choreography is not personal authorship.** Character conclusions require agreement among song context, facial/voice performance, camera relation, dialogue intention, and comparison—not merely the existence of a pose.
6. **This remains Phase 3.** Story events and support cards may later redistribute Kotone’s social center away from the Producer/Sena-heavy Dear structure.

# 2. The audiovisual thesis: Kotone as a circulation system

## 2.1 Why “the cute money girl” is inadequate

The simplest Kotone pitch is effective because it names visible features: she is cute, she talks about money, she works hard, she complains, and she craves praise. The AV corpus shows why that pitch is not false—and why it is not enough.

Money and cuteness are not two unrelated gimmicks. Both are parts of Kotone’s theory of exchange.

Money is concrete recognition. It proves that labor entered the world and produced consequence. A ticket proves that a spectator committed resources. Royalties prove that a song travelled. Merchandise proves that an image became portable. A paid job proves that the institution judged her useful enough to compensate. Her delight in these things is not reducible to trauma, but scarcity gives them moral weight.

Cuteness is likewise concrete labor. It is not simply a visual attribute she possesses. In the commu corpus, she studies when to deploy it, how to modulate it, which audience fantasy it serves, and how to give many people the sensation that the moment is meant for them. It is a service practice, a form of control, a source of pleasure, and eventually a self-authored art.

The two systems meet in a recurring loop:

> audience investment → Kotone’s recognition of obligation → directed attention/fanservice → audience pleasure → Kotone’s increased energy and confidence → a better performance → renewed investment.

This is what `Yellow Big Bang！` articulates most clearly, but it is already latent in `世界一可愛い私` and made explicit at crowd scale in `Campus mode!!`.

## 2.2 The audience is neither customer only nor abstract love

Kotone’s audience ethic is unusual because it does not ask us to choose between commercial realism and idol idealism.

She repeatedly remembers that spectators paid. That can sound mercenary when isolated. In context it often functions as a safeguard against self-absorption. The audience is not obliged to reward her merely because she worked hard. Work is input. The relevant output is whether the person who gave time and money received something worth carrying away.

Dear 024 is the cleanest late expression. Kotone is calm before H.I.F. because the work is done. Her stage goal is not to stare at Sena until the result arrives. It is to make the expensive ticket feel justified. Once she has performed, she becomes a fan at the festival because anxiety can no longer improve the result and enjoyment is now the most profitable use of attention.

The language is comic, but the ethic is mature:

> **value is maximized not by hoarding attention, but by making the whole event more alive.**

This is why the phrase “audience hospitality” remains useful, with one qualification. Hospitality can imply a host who owns the space and serves passive guests. Kotone’s strongest performances are more reciprocal. The crowd does not merely receive. It gives her the energy by which she can become the performer they came to see.

## 2.3 Individual recognition at crowd scale

`Campus mode!!` provides the most explicit technical formulation. The trainer expects to teach fanservice but discovers that Kotone has already developed much of it informally. Her key skill is not a catalogue of poses. It is situational reading.

The communication moves from a question—how can one idol make a large crowd feel addressed?—toward a model of **distributed specialness**. Kotone imagines eye contact and affective targeting not as deception, but as a way to let many people momentarily receive the concentrated force normally reserved for one person.

The 3DMV makes that proposition spatial:

- a large arena rather than an intimate room;
- direct frontal camera address;
- arm extension and salute-like gestures;
- close-up facial frames alternating with distant scale;
- catwalk/rail staging that carries the body toward spectators;
- and an ending that enlarges gratitude rather than retreating into private triumph.

The performance does not prove Kotone literally individuates every spectator. It stages the ideal that her love can be projected without becoming generic.

## 2.4 The private self is not discarded when the crowd appears

`世界一可愛い私` repeatedly moves private imagery into public performance.

The 3DMV uses a room-like field of plush objects, hearts, vanity/bedroom cues, and saturated personal color. The stage is not a neutral concert platform on which Kotone adopts a professional role. It is an enlarged interior. Her need to be praised, her childish delight, her weak confidence, and her commercial imagination become décor.

The official MV is even more explicit. Its chibi and illustrated forms multiply Kotone across classroom, casual clothing, idol costume, and graphic interludes. The recurring `存在か??` typography turns “world’s cutest” into a comic existence problem. The question is not only whether she is objectively the cutest. It is whether the many versions on screen can be accepted as one existing person.

The answer supplied by the commu is yes. Saki’s intervention is decisive: Kotone does not have to remove weakness to synchronize with an optimistic song. Weakness, self-love, greed, doubt, and the knowledge that someone believes in her are the material of the song.

The audiovisual conclusion is not “be yourself” in the abstract. It is more precise:

> **a public persona becomes truthful when it can carry the parts of the person that seem least compatible with the persona’s slogan.**

## 2.5 Performance begins under constraint and becomes increasingly chosen

Kotone’s flexibility can be admired too easily.

She learned to read customers because work mattered. She learned to switch quickly because one register would not survive every environment. She learned to calculate value because the margin for error was small. She learned to flatter, joke, and recover because social friction could cost opportunity.

That history means performance agility is partly adaptation to precarity.

The late Dear route changes the moral meaning of the same capacity. After debt completion, the ordinary sequence—lesson, audition, live, step-up—becomes available. She can still calculate, but calculation no longer has to defend the family from immediate collapse. She can choose ambition rather than experience ambition as the only acceptable escape.

This is why `普通` remains essential to the AV reading. The most extravagant Kotone performances are made possible by a newly secured right to ordinary development.

# 3. Speaking voice and acted personhood

## 3.1 Register mobility is the stable baseline

The most stable audible/visible feature is not one pitch, timbre, or sentence ending. It is **controlled mobility among social states**.

Kotone can shift within a few lines among:

- bright greeting;
- mock-romantic teasing;
- money/job speculation;
- deadpan challenge;
- vulnerable hesitation;
- and renewed performance sweetness.

The opening of the `世界一可愛い私` commu demonstrates the pattern economically. She arrives with a bright `お疲れ様でーすっ！`, converts “important conversation” into a possible confession, pivots immediately to the possibility of lucrative work, then loses the performative surface when told the song is hers. The change is not simply from fake to real. Each state answers a different social possibility.

The acting makes Kotone socially fast. She often reaches the joke before the Producer can define the scene. That speed gives her control. It also prevents silence from becoming a space where fear can settle.

## 3.2 `ぷろでゅ～さぁ～` is a relation, not merely a pronunciation

The elongated, cute address functions as a micro-performance. Depending on context it can solicit, tease, accuse, flatter, or make dependence comic enough to be bearable.

Plain `プロデューサー` tends to appear when the work problem or emotional demand cannot be held inside the same playfulness. The distinction is not absolute; the elongated form can be sincere and the plain form can be light. The important point is that Kotone hears address as an instrument.

By Dear 026–027, naming becomes a direct narrative object. She wants to be called `ことね`, but tries to control the dosage: once, because more would destabilize her. When the Producer finally uses the given name, the close framing and visible emotional arrest give the moment more force than a generic romantic blush. It completes a long movement from being financially and professionally handled as “Fujita-san” toward receiving a name that confirms singular recognition.

Her immediate attempt to retract the limitation—wanting more after asking for once—preserves the comic truth that controlled exchange cannot fully contain desire.

## 3.3 Cute and rough speech are co-authored modes

The audiovisual evidence strongly rejects the model:

> rough Kotone = authentic; cute Kotone = fake.

Roughness often appears where she is comfortable enough to stop smoothing every edge. It can also be defensive, performatively comic, or strategically intimidating. Cuteness often appears where she is selling or flirting. It can also be a sincere expression of pleasure, gratitude, and trust.

In `がむしゃらに行こう！`, peer intimacy is built through insults that carry precise recognition. Saki, Temari, and Kotone praise one another by naming the irritating trait that survives comparison. Kotone’s rougher responses do not destroy the idol relation; they are the language by which the trio prevents admiration from becoming hierarchy.

In `Campus mode!!`, sweetness is deliberate and technically discussed, yet the resulting fanservice is not therefore emotionally false. Technique gives affection a distributable form.

## 3.4 Emotional overload narrows control, but does not reveal a single hidden voice

The late H.I.F. material provides the clearest test.

Before the result, Kotone speaks like a prepared professional. She distinguishes work completed from outcome pending, and stage obligation from private rivalry. After losing, she initially produces a rational account: the live succeeded, her visibility and career benefited, work can continue.

That account is true. It is also insufficient.

When the Producer refuses to let professional language close the scene, she admits that one moment of lowered guard will make her cry and asks him to remain. The acting/staging does not treat the rational voice as a lie exposed by the crying voice. Instead it reveals that she can understand the career consequence correctly while still grieving the symbolic loss.

The most mature reading is:

> **Kotone’s competence does not cancel feeling, and feeling does not cancel competence.**

This distinction matters because her rapid recovery behavior could otherwise be mistaken for emotional shallowness.

## 3.5 The voice after loss is not purified

Dear 026–027 does not transform Kotone into a serene, non-transactional heroine.

She is embarrassed about mutual crying. She immediately wants work. She establishes a one-million-yen intermediate target. She celebrates debt repayment. She calls ordinary idol life dreamlike. She uses payment language to answer the Producer’s request that she become a top idol.

The continuity is the point. The route gives her more freedom without stripping away the vocabulary by which she understands obligation.

## 3.6 Public command grows from service rather than dominance

Kotone’s stage authority differs from Saki’s challenge-command and Temari’s song-centered concentration.

Her strongest public posture is invitational but not passive. The extended hand, frontal camera relation, open-arm gesture, and call-friendly compositions say:

> come closer; respond; let me know that you received this; give me enough back that I can amplify it.

This is command through reciprocity.

She does not merely ask to be watched. She organizes watching into participation.

# 4. Singing identity and musical profile

## 4.1 A stable genre is not supported

The supplied corpus spans:

- bright idol pop;
- crowd-oriented pop-rock/festival staging;
- self-esteem novelty pop;
- soft illustrated interiority;
- inherited franchise repertoire;
- shared/unit material;
- dark theatrical electronic staging;
- cybernetic aggression;
- and digital hyperpop spectacle.

That range makes a one-genre definition unhelpful.

The full-mix measurements likewise show a broad field rather than one narrow sonic family. The solo/capture set spans comparatively low and high spectral-centroid profiles, low and high onset-density environments, and widely different dynamic/capture conditions. `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！`, `Howling over the World`, `ENDLESS DANCE`, `ふわふわ`, and the foundational singles do not converge on one arrangement signature.

## 4.2 What remains recognizable across the range

The corpus supports five recurring performance functions.

### 1. Direct intelligibility

Kotone’s material tends to preserve conversational legibility. Even when the arrangement or visual field is busy, the performance identity is anchored in address: exclamation, invitation, complaint, boast, or confession.

### 2. Comic exposure

Embarrassment, greed, weakness, and overreaction are not edited out of the image. They are rhythmic and visual resources.

### 3. Fast affective pivot

She can move from anxious to delighted, from teasing to sincere, and from self-deprecation to command without requiring a complete scene reset.

### 4. Audience orientation

The visual grammar repeatedly returns to frontal address, crowd reverse shots, response cues, and the transformation of private feeling into something designed to circulate.

### 5. Authored portability

Kotone does not erase the host work. She adapts to it while retaining a social signature: “this is being given to you by someone who knows that being watched is an exchange.”

## 4.3 Signal evidence and its limits

The full-mix metrics are useful as negative and comparative evidence.

- `世界一可愛い私` occupies a less spectrally bright profile than several later or supplemental works; “cute” is not reducible to maximal high-frequency density.
- `Yellow Big Bang！` and `Campus mode!!` have relatively high event density in the supplied game-capture family, consistent with outward propulsion and crowd interaction.
- `Howling over the World` combines one of the highest onset-rate estimates with a visually aggressive field, supporting—but not proving by itself—the reading of mobilized intensity.
- `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！` has the highest spectral-centroid estimate in the set, consonant with its bright digital/hyperpop visual world.
- `ふわふわ` is spectrally bright in the full mix despite its soft visual dramaturgy, a useful reminder that “soft” is not the same as dark, slow, or sonically sparse.
- `ENDLESS DANCE` and `The Cute!!!` demonstrate that low onset density does not necessarily mean low theatrical intensity.

No full-mix statistic isolates Kotone’s voice. The metrics constrain metaphor; they do not replace listening and form analysis.

# 5. The central developmental sequence

## 5.1 `世界一可愛い私`: whole-self cuteness before stable self-belief

The first character song is often remembered as a declaration. The commu reveals that it begins as a problem.

Kotone can inhabit the boastful and entertaining parts easily. She struggles with the portion grounded in belief received from another person. The difficulty is not that she secretly hates herself. She describes a more contradictory condition: she is fundamentally weak in confidence and still likes herself.

Saki’s intervention provides the correct model. Kotone need not synchronize by becoming uncomplicatedly positive. The song belongs to her precisely because it can carry the oscillation.

The official MV multiplies her forms rather than choosing one. The 3DMV turns private room-like objects into stage architecture. The result is a public identity that does not cure insecurity. It makes insecurity performable.

This is the first version of performed reciprocity:

> someone believes she can carry the song → she borrows that belief → she gives the audience a confident image that still contains the borrowing.

## 5.2 `Yellow Big Bang！`: from self-presentation to mutual energy

`Yellow Big Bang！` is the most important revision of her performance ethic.

After a successful live, Kotone searches for criticism. The action can look like simple insecurity, but the commu makes the audience stake explicit. If even one person spent money and time, she wants to know whether that person left dissatisfied.

Her technical error is then identified: she became so serious about executing well that she stopped looking like she was having fun.

The solution is not “relax because scores do not matter.” It is:

> visible enjoyment is part of the product, part of the relationship, and part of the truth.

The 3DMV’s outdoor crowd, handheld microphone, open-arm address, close-up/distant alternation, and reverse shots of spectators make the loop visible. Kotone is most legible not when she seals herself into flawless performance, but when the crowd can see itself changing her.

## 5.3 `Campus mode!!`: recognition becomes scalable technique

Here the private loop becomes institutional.

Kotone learns to conceptualize special attention at arena scale. Family recognition provides an emotionally secure starting point; she can then project the same force beyond the family. The crowd is not loved identically in a psychological sense. The performance creates a form in which many people can receive a concentrated signal.

This is the point at which cuteness becomes less a personal defense and more a public craft.

## 5.4 `自己肯定感爆上げ↑↑しゅきしゅきソング`: the power and danger of manufactured assurance

The song’s official MV uses variety-show, meme, quiz, chibi, oversized typography, and comic-excess grammar. The 3DMV turns hearts, vanity/bedroom objects, audience light, and bodily unguardedness into a collective machine.

Self-esteem here is not discovered in solitude. It is manufactured through repetition, praise, spectacle, and audience response.

That is empowering. It is also ethically unstable.

The commu begins from replacement anxiety involving Kotone’s younger sister and the Producer. The Producer answers with extreme exclusivity. Kotone reads the song as constant praise and then converts reassurance into money, romance, and marriage jokes.

The scene strengthens the claim that her self-belief is relationally co-produced. It also preserves the unresolved question: can she eventually feel secure without another person proving that no rival could replace her?

## 5.5 `ふわふわ`: ordinary softness as counterargument

`ふわふわ` is the most valuable counterweight to a purely energetic Kotone model.

Its illustrated domestic field uses couch, phone, drifting clouds, lying down, falling, dreaming, and a return toward school. The body is not a sales engine or competitive machine. It is tired, suspended, and allowed interiority.

This matters because Kotone’s early body is overworked. The song does not simply show another aesthetic. It gives rest and ordinary adolescent drift a place inside her authored image.

The dream of `普通` is therefore not only economic. It is audiovisual: a life containing unprofitable softness can become visible without apology.

# 6. Range, portability, and shared material

## 6.1 `がむしゃらに行こう！`: borrowing without self-erasure

The song commu places Kotone beside Saki and Temari, whose specializations can make her feel comparatively undefined. The Producer’s answer is provocative: satisfy the fans who came for their strengths.

Kotone responds not by copying technique alone, but by studying what each girl’s performance does socially.

The trio then names one another’s traits through abrasive praise. Saki says Kotone can make any song cute. Temari recognizes the shameless, unguarded quality of her effort. Kotone values precisely the traits that make the others difficult.

The 3DMV’s smartphone/interface and classroom-social field strengthen the idea that effort becomes shareable content. Kotone’s awkwardness is not hidden until mastery. It is converted into something an audience can enjoy.

This is the best functional substitute for the unavailable `ガラクタロード` comparison, while remaining a different work.

## 6.2 `GO MY WAY!!`: inheritance as future reciprocity

The commu begins in fandom. Kotone is thrilled to sing 765PRO repertoire but is embarrassed to request it directly. She asks the Producer to curate surprise because indirect receipt protects her from seeming presumptuous.

The decisive movement is forward rather than nostalgic. Kotone wants earlier idols someday to sing her songs.

That desire is not conquest. It is reciprocity across generations. She receives inherited material and imagines becoming material for someone else.

The 4K 3DMV was staged but could not be materialized in the current working environment. No detailed frame claim is therefore promoted. The commu and source metadata are sufficient for the inheritance argument, not for a complete visual comparison.

## 6.3 Re;IRIS and `雨上がりのアイリス`: distributed center

The directly inspected Re;IRIS 3DMV places Kotone in a three-person field where focality circulates.

She receives solo framing, but the work repeatedly returns to triangular formation, mutual approach, paired interaction, and a shared final address. Kotone’s identity does not depend on monopolizing the camera. Her practical/social capacity remains visible through her ease in transition among center, partner, and ensemble member.

The song commu adds a dream-comparison structure. Kotone recognizes Saki and Temari as reliable friends, then distinguishes the actual Producer through the history of material care: scholarship, sleep, food, reduced work burden, jobs, and belief. Her declaration that she has only one Producer is therefore emotionally intense because it rests on infrastructure, not abstraction.

The unit evidence confirms that she can inhabit distributed center. It does not yet prove that she is always the trio’s mediator; that remains a Phase-6 ensemble question.

## 6.4 `ENDLESS DANCE`, `Howling over the World`, and `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！`

These works demonstrate range by pulling Kotone away from the foundational yellow/pink service-idol field.

### `ENDLESS DANCE`

The dark geometric stage, green/magenta/red lighting, frames, giant-eye imagery, skewed architecture, and controlled abstraction place Kotone inside a role more ominous than her ordinary public image. She can sustain theatrical distance without compensating through constant comic reassurance.

### `Howling over the World`

Cybernetic screens, red/blue opposition, glitch, chains, hard directional light, forceful arm movement, and solitary scale produce an aggressive performance environment. Kotone remains legible because directness survives the costume change: the gaze still meets the viewer, but the invitation becomes confrontation.

### `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！`

Pixel, toy, digital crowd, pastel overload, and novelty-speed imagery move in the opposite direction. This is cuteness as internet-native spectacle rather than domestic sweetness. Kotone’s social fluency fits the form because the work treats rapid circulation and playful excess as its natural environment.

Together these works support a bounded claim:

> **Kotone’s identity is portable across authored worlds because it resides in how she establishes relation, not only in what aesthetic surrounds her.**

# 7. Relationship revisions produced by audiovisual evidence

## 7.1 Kotone and the Producer

The AV corpus strengthens the relationship’s reciprocity while leaving institutional asymmetry unresolved.

The Producer does not merely praise Kotone. He creates conditions in which praise can become usable:

- reduces overwork;
- secures food and sleep;
- arranges paid work;
- helps address tuition and family debt;
- gives songs and stage opportunities;
- interprets weakness without treating it as disqualification;
- and supplies a stable witness when Kotone’s self-belief collapses.

Kotone returns value through labor, performance, trust, flirtation, and the promise of her career. The relationship is emotionally mutual in a way a simple rescuer/client model cannot capture.

Yet her payment grammar remains dangerous. `あたしのアイドル人生、喜んでお支払いします` is moving because it lets her answer care in her own vocabulary. It is ethically unstable because a life should not become a debt instrument.

The audiovisual pass therefore changes the question from “is the affection genuine?” to:

> **can genuine affection survive Kotone eventually refusing, leaving, or choosing a value the Producer did not authorize?**

## 7.2 Kotone and Sena

Sena is fan object, elder-sister figure, evaluator, desired summit, and rival.

The late Dear acting makes those roles coexist rather than replace one another. Kotone can cry watching Sena become top idol, use fan language, feel pride, and still accept the Producer’s statement that she must defeat her.

This is a strong form of rivalry because love does not have to be erased for equality to become possible.

The unavailable Kotone `ガラクタロード` source prevents one intended same-repertoire comparison with Saki and Temari. It does not weaken the Dear evidence that Kotone has moved from successor candidate toward reciprocal rival.

## 7.3 Kotone, Saki, and Temari

The trio’s audiovisual relation is built through different performance philosophies.

- Saki tends to convert weakness into forward challenge.
- Temari tends to treat song as a site where the self must become technically and emotionally answerable.
- Kotone tends to convert attention into reciprocal pleasure and value.

`がむしゃらに行こう！` and Re;IRIS show that these are not sealed archetypes. The girls borrow from one another. Kotone can become more physically forceful; Saki can expose mess; Temari can enter comic peer rhythm. The unit works when borrowing increases range without converting difference into hierarchy.

## 7.4 Kotone and family

Family recognition is not background sentiment. It is one of the engines by which public confidence becomes possible.

In `Campus mode!!`, family functions as emotional training wheels: a known recipient of love lets Kotone learn how to project affection across a much larger crowd. In the Dear route, debt completion changes the family relation from emergency obligation toward continued attachment.

The later audiovisual corpus should test whether family remains a source of recognition that does not require repayment in the same form.

## 7.5 Kotone and the audience

This is the most strongly revised relationship.

The textual core already identified audience enjoyment as stage telos. The AV pass shows the form of that ethic:

- Kotone needs response;
- response changes her visible pleasure;
- visible pleasure reassures the audience that its investment mattered;
- the crowd is invited to participate in producing the idol it came to see.

The mature relation is not ownership. Dear 016 already dissolves the distinction between “my fan” and “someone else’s fan” during a successful stage. `GO MY WAY!!` restates the practical version: regardless of prior allegiance, everyone present becomes her responsibility for that performance.

# 9. Revision to the Phase-3 character thesis

## 9.1 Revised compact thesis

The textual thesis should be preserved and expanded:

> **Fujita Kotone is the idol of material security becoming self-authorship—and the performer of reciprocal value. Once enough material floor exists for ordinary development, she turns the skills acquired under scarcity into a chosen public craft: reading people quickly, pricing obligation concretely, modulating cuteness, making attention feel personal, receiving audience energy, and returning it as visible pleasure.**

## 9.2 Revised account of cuteness

Earlier formulation:

> cuteness is worked public technique rather than false mask.

AV-strengthened formulation:

> **cuteness is a relational interface. It can sell, soothe, flirt, command, thank, expose weakness, and distribute special attention. Its sincerity depends not on spontaneity, but on whether it carries the whole relation rather than concealing its stakes.**

## 9.3 Revised account of musical identity

Earlier open question:

> stable personal sonic identity or portable technique?

Current answer:

> **primarily portable performance technique, with recurring audience-facing and conversational functions rather than one narrow genre.**

This conclusion remains bounded because isolated-vocal and complete franchise-wide comparison are unavailable.

## 9.4 Revised account of confidence

Confidence is not a possession she eventually stores permanently.

It is produced through:

- material security;
- accumulated work;
- accurate praise;
- family recognition;
- rivalry;
- audience response;
- direct naming;
- and visible proof that her performance changed another person.

The mature goal is not independence from every external source. It is the ability to participate knowingly in the circuit without treating every gift as a debt that can only be repaid through self-surrender.

## 9.5 Revised account of commerciality

Kotone’s commercial orientation is neither a joke to be outgrown nor an uncomplicated virtue.

It protects her from romanticizing unpaid labor and reminds the narrative that idolhood is work. It can also turn affection, obligation, and self-worth into accounts that never close.

Her most compelling moments occur when price remains visible but value exceeds the price—not by denying money, but by giving more life than calculation predicted.

Future source access could test:

Kotone’s procedure should eventually be compared against:

- Saki’s conversion of deficiency into propulsion;

Only then should franchise-wide terms such as `スター性` be stabilized.

# 11. Final baseline statement

Fujita Kotone does not become sincere by ceasing to perform.

She becomes more fully herself by gaining enough security to choose what performance is for.

Her early social agility helps her survive. Her later idol craft turns that agility into a public relation. She can be weak and self-loving, greedy and generous, calculating and emotionally excessive, flirtatious and professional, ordinary and spectacular. The work’s achievement is not to reconcile those traits by deleting one side. It gives them a form in which they can circulate.

The most mature Kotone performance says:

> You spent something to be here. I noticed. I will give you a moment that feels addressed to you. Your response will make me stronger. I will let you see that strength returning to you.

That is why her cuteness is not ornamental.

It is an economy of attention made visible—and, at its best, transformed from scarcity into abundance.
