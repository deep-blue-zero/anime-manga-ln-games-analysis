# 1. Executive thesis — renewable rivalry realized as reciprocal propulsion

The textual core defines Ume through **renewable rivalry**:

> pursuit begins as dependence on one person ahead of her, then becomes a social practice through which rivals, fans, songs, and institutions keep one another moving.

The audiovisual pass preserves that thesis and identifies its formal mechanism:

> **Hanami Ume is the idol of renewable rivalry realized as reciprocal propulsion. Her stable audiovisual grammar is not uninterrupted explosiveness but compression, rebound, and outward address. She receives fear, comparison, inherited expectation, and other people’s heat into the body—hands closing near the chest, gaze lowering or turning inward, tempo briefly tightening—then releases that pressure through raised fists, open arms, running and jumping shapes, direct camera reach, and public invitation.**

A compact shorthand is:

> **She does not merely run forward. She loads, rebounds, and pulls the room into motion.**

“Reciprocal” matters. Early Ume’s force is largely directional: Saki runs ahead; Ume follows. Later Ume can receive movement from Saki, Rinha, China, Hiro, Sena, Misuzu, Producer, fans, inherited songs, and the institution. She then returns motion in altered form. The strongest late scenes do not end with Ume alone at maximum speed. They end with another person, an audience, or the whole school being asked to answer.

The complete AV sequence can be summarized as follows:

> **Dear 001–010 — an excellent body is attached to a humiliatingly slow start; apparent fearlessness is exposed as performed confidence.**  
> **Dear 011–020 — non-Saki defeat expands the field; beating Saki produces tears, attachment disclosure, and autonomous idol desire rather than closure.**  
> **Dear 021–027 — material support, burnout risk, Worst Three friendship, observation, and rival-as-fuel produce a wider developmental ecology.**  
> **Dear 028–033 — old songs are reread; `全部盛り` becomes additive self-authorship; Ume and Saki admit mutual fear.**  
> **Dear 034–037 — career agency, D-UME Prima Stella, and `世界最強` convert summit language into generative public force.**  
> **`The Rolling Riceball` — effort becomes joyful, comic, and shareable.**  
> **`グースーピー` — sister challenge becomes a self-naming performance object.**  
> **`真っ白いページと水彩の主人公` — relational history becomes a public book rather than a purified essence.**  
> **`Campus mode!!` — inherited audiences become participants in Ume’s own strength.**  
> **`つよつよ最強エクササイズ` — strength and cuteness become one playful body.**  
> **`GO MY WAY!!` — inherited idol grammar is carried with learned respect without erasing Ume’s physical directness.**

The deepest audiovisual correction is therefore not “Ume is more vulnerable than she looks.” That would preserve the false opposition between bright force and hidden softness. The stronger conclusion is:

> **Vulnerability is one phase of Ume’s motion grammar. She becomes mature not by ceasing to compress difficult feeling, but by learning to reopen the body and return what she received without pretending the pressure was absent.**

# 2. The body grammar — compression, loading, rebound, release

## 2.1 Expansive baseline

Ume’s most recognizable acted and rendered shapes are large and legible:

- both fists raised near shoulder or head height;
- hands on hips with the chest open;
- broad arm lifts and overhead reaches;
- quick forward lean toward the interlocutor or camera;
- energetic pointing, saluting, jumping, and stepping;
- smiles that occupy the whole face rather than only the mouth;
- direct gaze that treats interpersonal distance as something to cross.

This is not simply “genki-girl animation.” It gives Ume a low-friction relation to action. Once she has decided what the scene means, the body commits before abstraction can slow it down.

## 2.2 Compression is recurrent, not exceptional

The Dear corpus repeatedly interrupts that expansive baseline with a smaller vocabulary:

- hands clasped or folded near the sternum;
- one hand pressed to the chest;
- shoulders drawn inward;
- lowered or closed eyes;
- head angle turned away from the addressee;
- blush and held reaction frames;
- pauses before a declaration;
- arms crossed not as confidence but as self-containment.

The recurrence matters. Ume does not only contract during one catastrophic breakdown. She contracts when facing equal rivalry, attachment fear, inherited audience expectation, anxiety about the Producer, the weight of a larger dream, and the wish to be recognized as cute rather than merely powerful.

> **AV finding:** her brightness is not a permanent absence of inhibition. It is an accomplished reopening after pressure has been registered.

## 2.3 Rebound rather than simple recovery

When Ume returns from contraction, she rarely restores the exact prior state. The release is usually more relationally specific:

- a fist rises after she has named who or what gives her strength;
- arms open after a fan relation has been reinterpreted;
- a direct address follows an inward admission;
- a cheer arrives after fear has been allowed to count;
- a stage gesture reaches toward the audience rather than merely proving personal energy.

This is why **rebound** is more precise than “mood recovery.” The compressed state stores information. The next expansive state contains the fear, person, rival, or inheritance that caused it.

## 2.4 Slow-start talent and audiovisual rhythm

The body grammar also mirrors Ume’s learning architecture:

> poor start → concentrated observation → principle capture → explosive application.

Early Dear material makes awkwardness and frustration visible, but the later route repeatedly shows Ume becoming still enough to watch. At the Aichi camp and in inherited-performance work, attention is not passive. It is the loading phase before rapid embodied transfer.

The AV pass therefore strengthens the textual claim that Ume’s talent is not effortless surface mimicry. Her characteristic acceleration depends on a prior interval in which she receives form into the body.

# 3. Voice and acting — directness with hidden internal volume

## 3.1 Bright speech is high-commitment speech

Ume’s ordinary delivery privileges:

- strong sentence attack;
- exclamation-heavy endings;
- rapid discovery of thought through speech;
- physical metaphors stated as immediate facts;
- laughter or breath embedded in enthusiasm;
- low hesitation once a conclusion has been reached.

The performance makes `あたし` feel socially close and embodied. `お姉ちゃん` remains emotionally primary even when Saki becomes a horizontal rival. Politeness is fully available in professional or public contexts, but it does not replace the colloquial baseline.

## 3.2 Direct is not simple

The textual “secret boxes” metaphor established that Ume can seal mixed feelings away. The acted route supplies the bodily complement: she can speak loudly about one component of a state while other components remain compressed in posture, timing, or gaze.

This prevents two bad models:

- “Ume always says everything she feels”; and
- “the bright Ume is a mask covering a quiet true self.”

Neither is supported. Brightness is genuine. So are the boxes. Her expressive surface is accurate but not exhaustive.

## 3.3 Fear changes timing more than identity

Dear 009 is the decisive early case. Ume names the terror of facing an equal rival and says that the more confidently she carries herself, the more frightened she becomes. The delivery slows, the body closes, and the gaze loses its ordinary frontal certainty. Yet the vocabulary remains Ume’s; fear does not turn her into another character.

Dear 033 repeats the principle at a larger scale. Ume and Saki admit that both have been afraid and trembling. Ume’s excitement after that admission is not a retraction. Fear and ignition occupy the same scene.

The mature vocal rule is therefore:

> **Ume does not overcome fear by sounding untouched. She lets fear lower and narrow the delivery, then rebuilds projection around a more accurate reason to move.**

## 3.4 Tears after victory

Dear 019 is an essential correction to any domination-centered reading of Ume’s Saki victory. The scene does not stage the win as clean conquest. Ume’s body folds, tears arrive, hands tighten near the chest, and the long pursuit discharges as an attachment plea: do not leave me behind.

The later `もう、追いかけない` is calmer and more organized. It is not rejection of Saki. It is the first statement possible after the vertical chase has done its work. The acting makes the sequence clear:

> victory permits grief; grief permits differentiation.

## 3.5 Public projection remains personal

Dear 036–037 does not replace colloquial Ume with a ceremonial stranger. The Prima Stella address expands her scale while preserving recognizable features:

- refusal of false modesty;
- direct naming of strong rivals;
- bodily metaphors of heat and collision;
- fist and open-arm gestures;
- explicit invitation to compete;
- comic anxiety over whether “world’s strongest” sounds barbaric;
- renewed insistence that strength means making people smile and find idols exciting.

The result is not refined distance. It is Ume’s ordinary interpersonal grammar made capable of addressing an institution and world.

# 4. Saki — vertical pursuit becomes reciprocal horizon

## 4.1 Early verticality

Early sister-facing material visually preserves asymmetry even when Ume is loud. Saki is the person whose judgment matters, whose body authored Ume’s preparation, and whose continued forward motion threatens disappearance. Ume’s energetic challenges do not erase that she is still looking upward.

The route’s most important emotional fact is not merely “Ume wants to beat Saki.” It is:

> **Ume wants to become fast enough that Saki can no longer become unreachable before Ume’s real growth begins.**

## 4.2 Equality creates fear

Dear 009 gives equality a new bodily cost. When defeat was predictable, Ume could treat the gap as developmental. Once the contest becomes real, loss can reveal something about her rather than merely about time. Confidence performance therefore intensifies fear.

This strengthens the textual reversal:

> competence does not make Ume invulnerable; it makes defeat meaningful enough to frighten her.

## 4.3 Victory opens rather than closes the bond

Dear 019–020 stages three movements:

1. winning collapses the pursuit structure;
2. attachment grief becomes speakable;
3. Ume discovers that she loves idolhood beyond the pursuit.

The acting rejects both severance and fusion. Saki remains `お姉ちゃん`; Ume no longer promises to follow any road Saki chooses. The relationship becomes capable of divergence because it has survived direct encounter.

## 4.4 Mutual fear is horizontal intimacy

Dear 033 provides the late bilateral correction. Saki is not the fearless summit. She too is afraid. Their relation becomes horizontal not because age, history, or sisterhood disappear, but because both can now disclose the cost of desire without assigning one person the role of invincible guide.

The late sister bond is therefore best modeled as:

> **constitutive asymmetry remembered inside reciprocal vulnerability.**

# 5. Other rivals and friends — a larger ecology of ignition

## 5.1 Rinha expands the world

Rinha’s importance lies partly in being the first non-Saki loss that Ume cannot assimilate through childhood habit. Ume’s response—dreaming, reviewing, studying, extracting principle—makes her observational method visible. Rinha becomes rival, teacher, and proof that the competitive world can become larger without creating a substitute Saki.

## 5.2 China and Hiro become more than training objects

At the Aichi camp, Ume repeatedly becomes quieter and more observational. The later `宝物` language matters because it prevents a purely extractive interpretation. She does not only take techniques. She learns that another person’s road contains a life-specific value that cannot be ranked beneath the Hanami sister story.

The audiovisual rhythm reinforces this. Ume’s attention can become still without becoming passive; the subsequent rebound carries something recognizably learned but still embodied as Ume.

## 5.3 Rivalry as added fuel

Dear 027 gives the mature theory: rivals can add firewood to the heart until burning out no longer feels like the only proof of seriousness. The performance of this realization moves from reflective comparison toward renewed bodily heat.

This is the ethical center of **renewable rivalry**:

> the rival’s function is not to justify mutual exhaustion but to create another future tense.

# 6. Fans — from audience excitement to incorporated strength

## 6.1 Early joy is real but incomplete

`The Rolling Riceball` communication already shows Ume wanting the pleasure of learning and singing to reach the whole venue. The early relation is generous. It is not yet a mature theory of fan difference, inheritance, defeat, or divided expectation.

## 6.2 `Campus mode!!` is the blocking correction

The `Campus mode!!` communication begins with Ume physically and vocally compressed by the audience problem. The room includes not only people who arrived for her, but people attached to Saki, Rinha, defeated rivals, and institutional inheritance. Ume cannot rely on generic cheerfulness because the audience’s prior loyalties are now visible.

Her resolution is specifically Ume’s:

> fans become part of the strength through which idol Ume can move.

This metaphor has a possible possessive danger and remains open to later testing. In this scene, however, it does not erase fan difference. It lets multiple histories enter her body without requiring them to become identical.

The 3DMV formalizes that shift through:

- direct reach toward camera and room;
- broad arena views and the sea of lights;
- large traversal gestures;
- open-arm invitation;
- momentum that repeatedly returns from audience scale to Ume and back outward.

## 6.3 Prima Stella as ecological victory

Dear 036 makes fan and rival relation institutional. Ume refuses modesty because strong rivals deserve an honestly celebrated win. She then invites the school to continue competing. The summit is not represented as the disappearance of everyone beneath her. It is a platform from which more motion can be generated.

# 7. The principal three-song developmental spine

## 7.1 `The Rolling Riceball` — effort becomes playable

The song communication emphasizes Ume’s wish to transmit joy before technical mastery is complete. The rendered performance uses:

- high kicks and broad athletic shapes;
- raised arms and call-and-response gestures;
- orange/geometric staging;
- large audience views;
- an uncomplicated willingness to occupy the center.

The authored MV adds an important correction. Its doodle, scrapbook, rice-ball-creature, exercise, stumble, and comic-failure imagery prevents effort from hardening into solemn martyrdom. The first song makes bodily labor **playable and shareable**.

This is a stronger beginning than “raw power.” Ume’s first solo identity already includes the ability to turn awkward effort into a social pleasure.

## 7.2 `グースーピー` — challenge becomes self-naming

The song communication makes Saki’s coauthorship explicit. Ume seeks a work that challenges Saki, feels like Ume, and can survive as a signature. The rendered performance sharpens address through pointing, saluting, quick close/wide alternation, and festival-scale crowd contact.

Compared with `The Rolling Riceball`, the force is more targeted. Ume is not merely broadcasting joy; she is naming herself through confrontation.

The later route says Ume initially outgrows the old song and must reread it. The present packet does **not** contain two separately rendered early/late versions that would permit a controlled audiovisual comparison. Therefore:

> **Text and acted dialogue strongly support re-authorship; a directly observed early-versus-late performance transformation remains OPEN.**

## 7.3 `真っ白いページと水彩の主人公` — the self becomes a book of relations

The song communication refuses purification. Ume wants an `全部盛り` work capable of carrying herself, Producer, fans, rivals, and the road traveled.

The official MV supplies the strongest formal confirmation in the packet:

- a literal book/notebook architecture;
- white-line and sketch versions of Ume;
- chibi and paper-cutout scenes;
- scrapbook and pop-up-page logic;
- photographs and images tied to Saki and prior stages;
- the classroom and page as spaces from which a larger public stage emerges.

The visual argument is not that Ume discovers one empty white page uncontaminated by others. It is that the page becomes hers through accumulated marks, people, and scenes. Authorship is additive and relational.

The song therefore completes the developmental spine:

> **playable effort → self-naming challenge → plural self-history.**

# 8. Public and inherited repertoire

## 8.1 `つよつよ最強エクササイズ` — cuteness and force are one system

The lyric video is deliberately playful: exercise grammar, chibi Ume, animals, bright orange/yellow design, repeated fist and jump motifs, and a final bow. It does not ask the viewer to choose between “cute Ume” and “strong Ume.” Physical force is the vehicle of cuteness.

This closes much of OPEN-UME-07:

> **Strength and cuteness are not separate performance modes. Ume’s cuteness is frequently produced through overcommitted bodily seriousness, comic exertion, and the joy of being visibly powerful.**

The question is not fully closed for intimate recognition—Producer can still overvalue the weapon and undervalue the girl—but the stage identity is integrated.

## 8.2 `GO MY WAY!!` — inherited literacy without disappearance

The communication makes predecessor-awareness and responsibility audible and visible through a more careful rehearsal state. Ume’s ordinary exuberance is temporarily narrowed by the weight of the song’s history. The rendered performance then restores breadth inside conventional idol grammar: direct smile, open hand, raised fist, crowd address, polished costume and stage.

The result supports the textual claim that inherited repertoire becomes proof of acquired idol literacy. Ume does not imitate a predecessor by suppressing her body-led identity. She carries the inherited form through her own propulsion.

## 8.3 Breadth sources

`ENDLESS DANCE`, `Howling over the World`, and `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！` are nonblocking breadth objects. They demonstrate portability:

- `ENDLESS DANCE` permits dark, angular, stiller, more controlled choreography;
- `Howling over the World` supports cyber/glitch vulnerability and defiance;
- `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！` supports bright digital/cute performance with athletic punctuation.

These sources reject the idea that Ume’s identity requires one sonic genre or constant maximal movement. Her stability is procedural: pressure is received and turned into direct relation.

# 9. Prima Stella — the summit becomes a starting gun

Dear 036 is continuity-bounded to D-UME. Within that track, the audiovisual staging makes the ethical meaning of victory unusually clear.

Ume stands in ceremonial red, names the strong rivals she defeated, and refuses false modesty. Close-ups, hand-to-chest gestures, raised fists, open arms, and cuts to listeners distribute the scene between winner and field. Sena and Saki remain visually present; the speech is not isolated self-coronation.

The address then shifts from retrospective victory to prospective challenge:

- students are asked to compete;
- collision is framed as mutual growth;
- inherited institutional pressure is accepted rather than denied;
- the whole school is invited to burn and move together.

Dear 037 provides the semantic correction to `世界最強`. Ume wants to:

- make people around the world smile;
- make idolhood feel fun and interesting;
- keep running;
- distribute heat and fire among idols.

The acting moves through comic panic about sounding barbaric, inward sincerity, soft emotion, and renewed declaration. The final model is not solitary supremacy.

> **For Ume, the strongest idol is the one whose victory creates more runners.**

This strengthens UME-C31, C33, and C34 while preserving the D-UME continuity lock.

# 10. Relationship revisions

## 10.1 Ume ↔ Saki

**Textual model:** constitutive sister/rival; pursuit contains abandonment fear; victory permits differentiation.

**AV refinement:** the relation’s mature formal movement is not verticality erased but **vertical memory converted into reciprocal vulnerability**. `お姉ちゃん` remains intact. Ume’s body can still become small around the possibility of loss. The late relation becomes horizontal because both people can disclose fear and answer one another without one remaining invincible.

## 10.2 Ume ↔ Producer

Ume’s Producer register combines direct affection, jealousy, professional trust, bodily metaphor, and a strong expectation of shared motion. Producer often gives her the interpretive pause that allows rebound: distinguishing goal from message, rival from shackle, effort from burnout, and inherited path from chosen path.

The relation is autonomy-enhancing in this packet, but the textual open question remains. Ume’s future declarations often presuppose Producer’s continuing presence. Robust disagreement and separation are still under-tested.

## 10.3 Ume ↔ China and Hiro

The AV pass strengthens their role as both friends and teachers. Ume’s quieter observational state is visible, and later excitement is not merely copied behavior. Their different paths become material Ume values before extracting performance principles.

## 10.4 Ume ↔ fans

Fans move from generic recipients of joy toward differentiated histories incorporated into Ume’s strength. The healthiest formulation is circulation, not ownership: fans add force; Ume returns motion and excitement. Fan departure, criticism, and divided loyalty remain open tests.

## 10.5 Ume ↔ institution

The Prima Stella speech makes Ume an institutional successor who refuses closure. Hatsuboshi’s competitive ecology is legitimized only insofar as ranking produces people capable of answering, learning, and continuing—not merely a winner above the sorted remainder.

# 11. Complete voice/personality reconstruction model

A later monograph or derived character model should preserve the following state system.

### Ordinary explosive brightness

- `あたし`, exclamation-heavy syntax, body-first metaphors;
- quick sentence attack and visible commitment;
- both fists, hands on hips, forward lean;
- delight in effort, food, training, praise, and challenge;
- low shame about wanting to win.

### Slow-start frustration

- irritation is immediate but not nihilistic;
- body can tense or fold briefly before another attempt;
- asks for concrete explanation or a new drill;
- does not naturally romanticize being bad at something;
- recovery is fast once a usable pattern appears.

### Observational loading

- quieter delivery and less purposeless movement;
- gaze tracks another performer or explanation;
- questions become more specific;
- apparent stillness precedes rapid embodied application;
- admiration does not require self-devaluation.

### Equal-rival fear

- lowered or closed gaze, hands at chest, pauses;
- confidence can become more performed as fear rises;
- openly names bodily fear once trust permits;
- does not abandon the contest;
- fear increases because the possibility of winning is real.

### Saki intimacy

- `お姉ちゃん` remains emotionally nonnegotiable;
- teasing, pleading, anger, admiration, jealousy, and challenge coexist;
- post-victory vulnerability may be much larger than ordinary speech predicts;
- does not need to choose between sister love and rivalry;
- mature differentiation is “I will not follow every path,” not “I no longer need you.”

### Producer attachment

- direct affection and reliance;
- often converts emotional negotiation into training, performance, or future planning;
- wants recognition as cute/person-specific, not only as exceptional weapon;
- can become anxious about counterfactual absence;
- expects the relationship to remain active and forward-moving.

### Fan-facing public register

- deliberate projection and large gestures;
- audience invitation more important than polished distance;
- responsibility becomes visible when inherited or divided audiences are involved;
- strength is framed as something shared or returned;
- mistakes false humility for disrespect when it diminishes rivals.

### Prima Stella / world-address state

- ceremonial scale without loss of colloquial identity;
- boast, gratitude, challenge, and invitation coexist;
- open arms and raised fists alternate with hand-to-chest sincerity;
- strongest-idol language must remain generative, not imperial;
- the speech should create future competitors rather than passive admirers.

### Unlikely portrayals

- permanently fearless Ume;
- a hidden quiet “true self” that invalidates brightness;
- maturity as emotional restraint or conventional elegance;
- independence requiring rejection of Saki;
- fans treated only as votes or generic cheering mass;
- a final state satisfied by one conclusive victory;
- strength and cuteness presented as mutually exclusive;
- effortless technical mastery from the outset;
- cruelty toward defeated rivals or false modesty after a serious win.

# 12. Revision dispositions

## PRESERVE

- **Renewable rivalry** as the governing Phase-3 thesis.
- Saki as constitutive infrastructure rather than disposable motivation.
- the extreme slow-start / explosive-acquisition talent architecture.
- Ume as direct but not psychologically simple.
- fan orientation as learned rather than innate.
- D-UME Prima Stella as a track-specific summit.
- mature `世界最強` as generative social force.

## STRENGTHEN

- fear-bearing courage rather than literal fearlessness;
- victory over Saki as relational decompression and individuation;
- rivals as teachers and added fuel;
- `全部盛り` as additive self-authorship;
- `Campus mode!!` as fan-relation correction;
- inherited repertoire as learned idol literacy;
- refusal of false modesty as respect for strong defeated rivals;
- strength/cuteness integration in public performance.

## REVISE

- “Ume’s formal mechanism is continuous acceleration” → **compression, rebound, and outward address**.
- “brightness reveals everything” → **brightness is genuine but partial; difficult states are often loaded inward before release**.
- “Saki rivalry becomes simply horizontal” → **constitutive vertical history survives inside reciprocal vulnerability**.
- “old-song re-authorship is audiovisually proven” → **acted/textual re-authorship is strong, but paired rendered early/late comparison remains unavailable**.
- “stage power is mainly bodily force” → **body becomes a medium for circulating fear, fans, inheritance, and rival heat**.

## DOWNGRADE

- Ume as merely loud, straightforward, or emotionally transparent.
- the assumption that every large gesture means confidence rather than deliberate projection.
- the idea that `ENDLESS DANCE`/dark styling is outside her plausible range.
- any reading that treats cuteness as an artificial mode separate from her athletic identity.

## REJECT

- beating Saki completes Ume’s arc.
- `世界最強` means solitary hierarchical domination.
- maturity requires devaluing the person who originally shaped the dream.
- fans merely validate a preexisting self without changing it.
- Ume is an effortless natural idol whose early incompetence is only comic decoration.

## OPEN

- a controlled early-versus-late rendered comparison of `グースーピー`;
- whether renewable rivalry can become compulsive escalation;
- self-worth during extended periods without contest;
- whether fan-as-body metaphor remains healthy under criticism or departure;
- long-run Producer/Ume disagreement and separation tolerance;
- how family capital should modify later meritocratic interpretation;
- whether non-D-UME tracks support the same world-scale summit rhetoric.

# 13. Final synthesis

Hanami Ume does not begin as a self-created projectile.

Her body, training habits, competitive grammar, and first dream were built through a sister who always seemed to move first. Producer gives her a start dash, but not an invented capacity. Rinha gives her a world beyond childhood defeat. China and Hiro give her paths that cannot be reduced to ingredients. Fans teach her that a room arrives carrying histories. Songs preserve versions of herself that growth can neither simply repeat nor discard. Inherited repertoire teaches her that movement also carries memory. Fear grows as competence makes loss meaningful. Victory creates tears before it creates autonomy. Prima Stella becomes a starting gun rather than a finish line.

The audiovisual layer shows how these developments inhabit one body.

Ume closes around pressure. She clasps her hands, lowers her gaze, holds feeling near the chest, and briefly loses the smooth certainty of forward motion. Then she reopens—not as though nothing happened, but with the pressure converted into a more accurate direction. A fist rises for someone. An arm reaches toward the room. A challenge becomes invitation. A song becomes a page marked by other people. A summit becomes a reason for the field to keep running.

The complete Phase-3 formulation is therefore:

> **Hanami Ume is the idol of renewable rivalry realized as reciprocal propulsion. She becomes strongest not by escaping dependence, fear, inheritance, or defeat, but by receiving their force, metabolizing it, and returning it as motion that keeps rivals, fans, and institutions alive.**

Her final question is not merely whether she can catch the person ahead.

It is whether she can create a world in which no one’s forward motion has to mean disappearance.
