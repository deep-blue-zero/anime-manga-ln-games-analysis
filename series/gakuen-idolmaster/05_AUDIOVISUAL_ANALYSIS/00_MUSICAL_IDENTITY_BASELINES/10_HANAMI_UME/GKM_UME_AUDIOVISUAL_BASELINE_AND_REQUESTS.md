## 1. Purpose

This register converts the Phase-3 Ume textual reading into a practical whole-video acquisition plan. It asserts no uninspected timbre, acting, choreography, camera, animation, or MV conclusion.

The principal hypotheses to test are:

1. bright/direct Ume versus fear, jealousy, grief, and internal compartmentalization;
2. the bodily difference between slow-start incompetence and later explosive command;
3. Saki-facing vertical pursuit → equal fear → post-victory horizontal rivalry;
4. the developmental sequence `The Rolling Riceball → グースーピー → 真っ白いページと水彩の主人公`;
5. whether `グースーピー` visibly/audibly changes when later Ume reinterprets an outgrown early song;
6. fan orientation before and after `Campus mode!!`;
7. whether strength and cuteness are separate performance modes or one integrated Ume identity;
8. how inherited repertoire such as `GO MY WAY!!` carries acquired idol literacy;
9. whether late H.I.F./Prima Stella projection preserves fear, gratitude, invitation, and reciprocity beneath triumph.

Whole commus, full MVs, and complete 3DMVs are preferred. Do **not** manually clip scenes unless no full public/user-supplied source exists.
