# 1. Executive thesis — disciplined ignition

The textual core defines Tsubame through **heroic rivalry**:

> Tsubame builds an idol vocation that can survive Sena’s absence, then chooses Sena again as a beloved, renewable rival.

The AV pass preserves that thesis and identifies the formal mechanism by which it becomes visible and audible:

> **Tsubame is the idol of disciplined ignition. She turns upright posture, complete sentence shape, ceremonial challenge, dark-hero costume, and controlled rupture into a performance grammar that does more than dominate. At her best, she uses conflict to return agency to another person—to make a rival, junior, fan, or audience member answer rather than submit.**

A second formulation describes the relational structure:

> **Ceremonial combat becomes hospitality.**

“Hospitality” here does not mean softness or reassurance. Tsubame often helps through sharpening, provocation, challenge, and public demand. The mature ethical difference is whether the confrontation leaves the other person more capable of choice. Her best interventions create a stage on which someone can re-enter action; her worst risk is deciding that her insight gives her jurisdiction over the person she intends to save.

The complete AV sequence can be summarized as follows:

> **Dear 000–010 — discipline is exposed as both competence and shelter.**  
> **Dear 011–020 — the copied hierarchy becomes an authored dark-hero vocation.**  
> **Dear 021–024 — separation makes rescue legitimate: Tsubame proves she can remain an idol without Sena before asking Sena to choose again.**  
> **Dear 025–027 — healthy defeat converts rivalry into renewable future architecture and forces Tsubame beyond technique toward `スター性`.**  
> **`理論武装して` — antagonism becomes controlled public form.**  
> **`Campus mode!!` — inherited brightness becomes institutional welcome without erasing severity.**  
> **`クライアイ` — attack acquires allure, invitation, and relation.**

The deepest audiovisual correction is therefore not “Tsubame is less harsh than she looks.” That is too simple. It is:

> **Harsh form and caring purpose are not opposites in Tsubame. The governing question is whether the hard form intensifies another person’s authorship or merely enforces Tsubame’s preferred outcome.**

# 2. The body of `学園No.2`

## 2.1 Uprightness as social control

Across Dear 000–010, Tsubame’s most stable visual baseline is highly organized:

- upright torso and squared shoulders;
- hand on hip, arms crossed, or one hand placed at the chest;
- direct frontal or three-quarter gaze;
- complete gestures that begin and end cleanly;
- relatively little purposeless motion;
- expressions that return quickly to a composed neutral or confident smile.

The pose vocabulary does not merely communicate “coolness.” It lets Tsubame govern the tempo of an encounter. She corrects, names, orders, evaluates, and challenges. Even when she is not formally acting as vice president, her body behaves as though a scene should have rules.

This is why `学園No.2` is not only a spoken title. It is a performed spatial claim. Tsubame occupies the visual center as the person who already knows what the encounter is about.

## 2.2 Dear 002 — copied effort cannot picture arrival

Dear 002 tests the shelter inside discipline. Tsubame can perform confidence while describing a training program built from Sena’s model and increased volume. When Producer asks her to imagine the Tsubame who has surpassed Sena, the body remains formally controlled but the conversational rhythm no longer moves with the same certainty. The route does not depict laziness; it depicts effort without a positive self-image after victory.

The AV layer strengthens the textual claim that early Tsubame prefers **quantitative escalation** because it leaves the old hierarchy conceptually intact. She can do more while still remaining “the one behind Sena.”

## 2.3 Dear 006 — collapse without falling

The Misuzu loss is the first decisive fracture. The direct clip and the embedded Dear packet show a carefully bounded breakdown:

- eye closure becomes more sustained;
- blush and visible agitation enter the face;
- the head lowers;
- hands move inward toward the chest or fold across the body;
- sentence timing becomes less sovereign;
- reaction frames remain longer than in ordinary combative exchange.

Yet Tsubame does **not** collapse to the floor, flee the scene, or lose all posture. She remains standing. This is important.

> **AV finding:** Tsubame’s discipline is a containment system, not evidence that affect is absent.

The scene therefore revises a possible overreading of “vocal collapse.” Misuzu does produce genuine rupture, but the rupture occurs inside a body trained to preserve enough public form to remain answerable. Anger and challenge do not erase the shame; they give it structure.

# 3. Voice and register — controlled combat, not generic roughness

## 3.1 Formal competence is real

The 20-scene outing compilation is indispensable because it demonstrates that Tsubame’s rough close-register speech is selective rather than the result of social incompetence.

In `ビジネス燕`, she uses polished institutional language with a vendor, including proper self-identification as Hatsuboshi student-council representative and conventional gratitude. Other outings show her discussing calligraphy, shops, food, appearance, films, and public situations with context-sensitive control. The support-card communication similarly places her in a consultative vice-president role with juniors and peers.

Tsubame can therefore speak politely, professionally, and administratively when the relation calls for it.

## 3.2 `貴様` as relational intensity

With Producer, Sena, close rivals, and people admitted into her combat grammar, Tsubame’s language becomes theatrical:

- `貴様`;
- imperatives;
- challenge formulas;
- martial and predatory metaphors;
- extravagant declarations;
- laugh-shaped escalation;
- mock threats and taunts.

The AV evidence supports the textual distinction between **hostility** and **combative intimacy**. The same register can carry:

- anger;
- trust;
- embarrassment;
- gratitude;
- dependence;
- flirtation-adjacent tension;
- admiration;
- the invitation to fight again.

Calling this merely tsundere speech misses its function. Tsubame converts emotional exposure into a contest grammar because contest preserves both parties as active.

## 3.3 Rupture is temporal

Tsubame’s emotional acting is best modeled through her control of time rather than through a simple “low voice/high voice” opposition.

Stable states tend toward:

- completed clauses;
- decisive sentence attacks;
- minimal filler;
- controlled laugh or scoff;
- gestures that punctuate rather than spill.

Rupture introduces:

- repetition;
- stammering;
- delayed response;
- visible blush;
- eye closure;
- inward hand movement;
- a temporary inability to dictate the scene’s next beat.

Recovery does not require pretending the rupture did not happen. Mature Tsubame can return to command while allowing the emotional fact to remain.

## 3.4 Private imagination is not a minor gag

The outing compilation materially expands her ordinary voice. It includes:

- excitement over robot anime and power-up scenes;
- near-disclosure at a model shop;
- enthusiasm for robot piloting and a figure lottery;
- elaborate naming habits;
- ninja play;
- bug avoidance and denial;
- sweets, soba, rice-ball fillings, and shop recommendations;
- maid-café and dress-up discomfort;
- fighting-game competitiveness;
- calligraphy and motto discussion;
- fan enthusiasm.

This is not detachable comic garnish. The dark hero works because it translates a pre-existing imaginative life. Her severe public register is not the opposite of fantasy; it is fantasy disciplined into social form.

# 4. From copied hierarchy to authored hero

## 4.1 Dear 009–010 — being named rival

The first victory over Sena is staged as more than rank movement. Tsubame’s long-controlled body finally receives external confirmation that the relation can be reciprocal. The childhood material also clarifies that Sena was not always an unreachable sovereign. Tsubame once knew the ordinary, clumsy child and originally wished to free her from being reduced to idol destiny.

That history matters audiovisually because the route moves between ordinary school spaces, remembered intimacy, and performance language. “Rival” restores a relation that hierarchy had frozen.

## 4.2 Dear 018–019 — the hero ideal becomes embodied

The hero-origin sequence initially uses sparse or memory-like space. The childhood wish appears as recollection before it becomes a present body.

After the relevant contest/performance material, adult Tsubame is visibly exhausted—bent, breathing hard, temporarily reduced from ceremonial control—then rises again into the dark-hero body. The transition matters:

> **The hero is not merely a childhood image she remembers. It becomes a form she must physically inhabit after exertion, failure, and relation.**

This prevents the dark-hero concept from becoming only branding. The costume and pose system externalize an ethic already present in the text: refuse abandonment, ignite courage, and remain answerable to the people addressed by performance.

## 4.3 Dark hero versus heel

The AV corpus makes the distinction clearer.

A **heel** creates antagonistic excitement. It uses hostility, opposition, and controlled threat as entertainment.

Tsubame’s mature **dark hero** retains that antagonistic energy but changes its purpose. The audience is not merely invited to enjoy her aggression. It is invited to stand, answer, challenge, or continue.

The visual vocabulary therefore remains dark—black, purple, red, green, chains, shadow, hat brim, angular line—but the relation shifts from enclosure to activation.

# 5. Dear 021–024 — separation before rescue

## 5.1 The strange leniency of Dear 021

When Sena announces retirement, Tsubame’s initial behavioral anomaly is not explosive rage but misplaced leniency. Juniors notice that the strict vice president has become unnaturally permissive. Her body remains readable as Tsubame, but the motivational system underneath the discipline has hollowed.

This is an important correction to the idea that rivalry is only energy. The absence of a summit does not make her gentle in a mature way; it temporarily removes the reason she has been using to organize intensity.

## 5.2 Distributed rescue

Dear 022–023 refuses the fantasy that Tsubame alone “saves” Sena.

- Kotone insists that Sena’s resignation is false to her desire.
- Producer constructs the confrontation.
- Tsukika supplies an external, harsh counterposition.
- Shion remains evidence that Tsubame’s prior interventions can alter another person’s road.
- Tsubame must decide what she herself wants independently of Sena.

The staging alternates school, practice, outside consultation, and performance preparation. Rescue is assembled through several people and spaces; it is not one charismatic speech.

## 5.3 Dear 024 — the oath precedes the demand

Dear 024 is the route’s decisive audiovisual scene.

Tsubame performs in the red-and-green dark-hero costume for an audience of one. After `クライアイ`, the scene progressively tightens from full body to closer views. The key declaration receives increasingly direct framing:

> `私には理想のアイドル像がある！`  
> `ヒーローみたいなアイドルになる！`  
> `その想いに、貴様の有無は関係ない！`  
> `私はアイドルで在り続けるぞ！`

The performance does not strip away costume to reveal sincerity. The costume carries sincerity. Hat, cape, direct gaze, hand-to-chest gesture, and declarative posture turn independence into an oath.

Only after establishing that she can remain an idol without Sena does Tsubame challenge Sena’s resignation. That sequence is ethically essential:

> **Tsubame first relinquishes existential ownership of the rival. She then claims the right of long witness to ask whether Sena’s resignation is truly chosen.**

Later, the delivery softens without abandoning the dark-hero form:

> `私は覚悟を決めた。貴様がいない世界で生きる覚悟を。`  
> `私は、貴様のアイドルとしての可能性を信じているよ。`  
> `ひとりの最も古いファンとして。`

The AV finding is not that softness replaces combat. It is that the same body can hold command, grief, release, and faith.

## 5.4 One-person audience and public ethics

The Sena-only live is an extreme case of targeted performance. It risks becoming private emotional coercion. The route contains two safeguards:

1. Tsubame explicitly allows genuine retirement if it is Sena’s own desire.
2. The song and later public rendering do not remain private property; `クライアイ` becomes fan-facing performance.

The distinction remains ethically open. Tsubame’s confidence that she knows “the real Sena” could become paternalistic. But the scene’s sequence—independence, permission, challenge, response—keeps it from being simple possession.

# 6. Dear 025–027 — healthy loss and renewable rivalry

## 6.1 Dear 025 — joy at the rival’s transformation

Sena’s emergence into `スター性` produces one of Tsubame’s most expansive positive reactions in the packet. She laughs, opens the body, publicly praises Sena, and then defensively denies being happy.

The important fact is not the denial. It is that Sena’s superiority now **energizes** rather than ontologically diminishes her.

> **Healthy rivalry means the rival’s growth can be experienced as both threat and good news.**

Tsubame recognizes that technique no longer exhausts idol value. She does not abandon technique; she becomes curious about the remainder.

## 6.2 Dear 026 — defeat without devaluation

The summer H.I.F. result directly tests whether the new model survives losing.

Tsubame enters with high technical confidence and performs through a sharpened dance strategy. Sena nevertheless wins through a newly integrated attraction that the route calls `スター性`.

Afterward, Tsubame remains upright and verbally coherent, but the visual acting allows mixed affect into the body:

- closed eyes and lowered head;
- hand-to-chest gestures;
- a more sustained pause before answer;
- a close framing that makes disappointment visible;
- then renewed stance.

She names both joy at Sena’s brilliance and anger at her own insufficiency. She does **not** claim the judging was wrong, reduce Sena to luck, or retreat to “only Sena can beat me” as a fixed excuse.

This is one of her healthiest losses.

The stronger rupture arrives when Producer revives his melodramatic promise to take responsibility by leaving or self-punishment. Tsubame rejects that future. Her demand that he remain beside her is not voiced through conventional tenderness; it is converted into a threat/challenge:

> she will become Prima Stella so completely that he can never again speak of leaving her side.

The relation therefore preserves her active grammar even while exposing dependence.

## 6.3 Dear 027 — embarrassment becomes planning

Dear 027 opens with Tsubame hiding outside the room and internally replaying her prior line. The confident rival is visibly embarrassed by the intimacy she has allowed.

She then returns to analytic planning. The movement is characteristic:

> exposure → blush and delay → restored scene structure → renewed joint action.

Crucially, she chooses to develop `スター性` rather than merely increase numerical advantage. The loss has changed what she believes an idol can be.

The final Sena wager turns uncertain continuation into explicit future architecture. Its terms are comically asymmetrical, and Tsubame’s “anything” counteroffer is hardly rigorous contract design. But its relational function is clear:

> **Rivalry becomes a renewable agreement to keep each other from treating the self as finished.**

The route ends not with rank settled but with a future made answerable.

# 7. The three-song system

## 7.1 `理論武装して` — armor becomes public antagonism

The song communication begins with conflict over Producer’s heel framing. Tsubame refuses the idea that private rivalry alone may define the performance. She explicitly asks whether the fans can receive the image and insists that her live belongs to the people who support her.

The rendered 3DMV uses:

- black cropped costume, chains, boots, and industrial platform geometry;
- precise pointing, fist, reach, and directional steps;
- strong frontal address;
- controlled rather than chaotic attack.

The authored official MV intensifies the psychological layer through:

- black/white/purple contrast;
- shadow or silhouette doubles;
- falling body and wings;
- grasping hands or crowd-like forms;
- fractured line art and purple force;
- repeated self-image under pressure.

The 3DMV gives the public body of antagonism. The authored MV gives its inner cost: being armed is also being enclosed by the need to remain armed.

## 7.2 `Campus mode!!` — brightness as inherited public service

The communication asks whether inherited Hatsuboshi repertoire can remain tradition without becoming submission. Tsubame approaches it analytically, including questions of form, costume, and what the song asks of a representative.

The 3DMV answers with a strikingly bright performance system:

- orange athletic/baseball-inflected costume;
- toy-like, multicolored stage;
- large audience;
- open arms, salute-like gestures, smiles, and direct crowd address.

This does not replace the dark hero. It reveals another function of the same command ability.

> **In `Campus mode!!`, Tsubame’s severity becomes institutional welcome.**

She can lead a crowd into inherited joy without pretending that her own style is naturally cute or unconflicted. Tradition becomes a medium she can inhabit, alter, and transmit.

## 7.3 `クライアイ` — attack learns to beckon

The song communication makes rival-love explicit as artistic material. Tsubame struggles to write because the relevant emotion is not merely aggression. The requested performance qualities include `余裕`, adult allure, and beckoning authority.

The 3DMV preserves dark-hero costuming—red, green, hat, cape, dark industrial and fire-lit spaces—but changes the movement relation:

- steadier direct gaze;
- hat-brim framing;
- forward walking;
- controlled smiles;
- gestures that invite or draw rather than only strike;
- alternating blue/industrial restraint and red/fire intensity.

Compared with `理論武装して`, the performance is less about breaking through a wall and more about making the addressed person step closer.

> **`クライアイ` is Tsubame’s hard form becoming relationally permeable.**

The song’s private Sena origin remains legible, but the public 3DMV converts dyadic intensity into fan-facing invitation. The precise audience reception remains outside the available packet, so the full public effect remains **OPEN**.

# 8. Star quality — strengthened, but not reduced to one visual trick

The textual core proposed that Tsubame’s `スター性` may intensify when competition is joined to responsibility for another person’s future.

The AV evidence **STRENGTHENS** the hypothesis, but revises its expected form.

There is no single repeatable signature such as “camera always moves closer” or “Tsubame always becomes still.” Instead, the pattern is relational:

- the target becomes more explicit;
- direct gaze and declarative posture increase;
- challenge is tied to a road the other person may take;
- Tsubame’s own self-image becomes less central than the possibility she is trying to reactivate;
- after the challenge, the other person must answer.

Dear 024 is the clearest case. Shion and other later textual cases support portability, but the current AV packet does not contain enough equivalent rendered rescue performances to declare a fixed audiovisual formula.

Current status:

> **STRENGTHEN / OPEN:** heroic star quality is best modeled as targeted agency ignition, not as one camera or vocal effect.

# 9. Relationship revisions

## 9.1 Tsubame ↔ Sena

The AV pass strengthens the full relationship sequence:

> childhood familiarity and care → idolized wall → defensive pursuit → named rivalry → independent hero vocation → separation test → rescue without total ownership → healthy defeat → chosen renewable rivalry.

Two corrections matter.

First, Sena is not merely Tsubame’s summit. She is also the person before the summit: childhood friend, institutional peer, fan-object, admired idol, and someone Tsubame once wished to free.

Second, Tsubame’s final Sena-centered future is not proof that independence failed. It follows the scene in which she demonstrates she can remain an idol without Sena. The choice is intense, perhaps overbinding, but it is no longer ontologically compulsory.

The material supports profound love and competitive intimacy. It does not require a canonical romantic classification.

## 9.2 Tsubame ↔ Producer

Producer begins as the provocateur who can imagine a winning Tsubame before she can. The route later becomes reciprocal:

- he supplies strategy and engineered confrontation;
- she supplies veto, fan ethic, and performance judgment;
- he asks for trust;
- she explicitly demands mutual trust;
- his self-sacrificial responsibility language becomes something she rejects;
- she requests durable presence through the only grammar that still leaves her active: challenge.

The AV layer strengthens **chosen dependence without conventional softness**. It does not erase the institutional asymmetry or the ethical concern that Producer sometimes uses humiliation as developmental technology.

## 9.3 Tsubame ↔ juniors and institution

The outing, support, Dear 000, and event materials prevent the vice-president role from being reduced to domination. Tsubame:

- corrects;
- teaches;
- manages vendors and school obligations;
- helps juniors despite complaint;
- accepts counter-challenge;
- becomes playful in ordinary contexts;
- treats institutional role as something to perform well.

Her care is often best described as **sharpening**. The later event/support pass still needs to test how she behaves when someone clearly rejects that form of care.

# 10. Complete voice/personality reconstruction model

The most reliable simulation rule is:

> **Begin from complete, stage-readable control. Then ask what the relation does to Tsubame’s control of time.**

### Baseline vice-president / competent senior

- upright and efficient;
- complete clauses;
- direct correction;
- appropriate politeness when the relation is institutional;
- practical willingness to help;
- satisfaction in being useful without sentimental display.

### Rival / Producer combat intimacy

- `貴様`;
- imperatives and challenges;
- martial or predatory metaphor;
- deliberate provocation;
- laughter, scoff, or mock threat;
- affection translated into active contest.

### Defensive No.2

- increased certainty around rank language;
- imitation of Sena disguised as harder effort;
- downward comparison;
- irritation when asked to imagine the self after victory.

### Humiliated or emotionally ruptured

- delayed response;
- repetition or stammer;
- blush;
- closed eyes and lowered head;
- inward hand movement;
- temporary loss of tempo;
- posture often preserved enough to remain socially present.

### Dark-hero public register

- ceremonial command;
- strong direct address;
- precise gesture punctuation;
- antagonistic excitement;
- explicit audience/fan orientation;
- conflict framed as a road into action.

### Rescue / heroic star-quality state

- target-specific address;
- willingness to state difficult truth;
- challenge joined to belief in the other’s possibility;
- acceptance that the other may answer differently;
- more important than generic warmth is the transfer of agency.

### Ordinary private enthusiast

- faster comic reaction;
- embarrassment around robot/hero interests;
- elaborate naming;
- competitive play;
- food and hobby specificity;
- more visible susceptibility to teasing than her public image suggests.

### Unlikely portrayals

- chronically soft, deferential, or apologetic speech with close peers;
- cruelty for its own sake;
- indifference to fans;
- a final state in which she no longer cares about technique;
- a final state in which Sena is the sole reason she remains an idol;
- effortless emotional transparency;
- a “true gentle self” hidden beneath a wholly fake harsh persona.

# 12. Final synthesis

Tsubame’s story is not finally the story of a severe No.2 becoming kinder, nor the story of a technician discovering that feeling matters more than skill.

It is the story of a person who learns that **control must become transmissive**.

Early control protects her from imagining a self beyond Sena. It lets her work, rank, correct, and remain excellent without risking the meaning of arrival. Misuzu breaks that defense. Tsukika enlarges the sky. Hero fantasy becomes a public ethic. Fans turn victory into obligation. Producer turns provocation into mutual trust. `理論武装して` gives antagonism a stage body. `Campus mode!!` lets her inherit and transmit institutional brightness. `クライアイ` teaches attack to beckon. Sena’s retirement crisis forces independence. The summer loss proves that another person’s brilliance can threaten and delight her at once. The winter wager keeps the future open.

The complete AV pass therefore refines heroic rivalry into a performed principle:

> **Tsubame fights so that neither side may disappear into passivity.**

At her best, she does not rescue by carrying the other person away. She builds a confrontation in which the other person must stand, answer, and choose. Her discipline becomes ignition. Her ceremony becomes a road. Her rivalry becomes hospitality.
