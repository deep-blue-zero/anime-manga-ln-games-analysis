# JUO SENA PHASE-3 AV TECHNICAL METRICS APPENDIX

## Method limits

- LUFS/LRA and tempo are full-mix source measures.
- Visual brightness, saturation, frame-difference motion, and major-cut counts use uniform frame sampling.
- Dear measures are chapter-scene mixtures; chapters with high Sena dialogue share are the strongest voice-state proxies.
- Dear 11–20 boundaries are text-length modeled and visually checked.
- Song vocal proxies use center-channel, high-passed harmonic extraction and conservative pitch tracking. They are not isolated stems.
