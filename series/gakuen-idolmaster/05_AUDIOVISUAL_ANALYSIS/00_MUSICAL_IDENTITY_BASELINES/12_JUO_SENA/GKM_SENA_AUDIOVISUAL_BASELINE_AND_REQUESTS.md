# JUO SENA — AUDIOVISUAL BASELINE AND WHOLE-VIDEO REQUEST PACKET

## 1. Purpose and acquisition rule

This packet defines the minimum audiovisual corpus needed to test `GKM_CORE_12_JUO_SENA.md`. It does not assume that every existing song, 3DMV or commu must be collected. The priority is to recover the states the textual core cannot establish by itself:

- public command versus private uncertainty;
- perfect-image control versus visible supportability;
- idol and Producer registers;
- fear under equal or stronger opposition;
- foundational solo identity;
- the proposed two-register musical identity: theatrical/regnant crown versus glamorous/sensual contemporary-pop diva;
- whether those registers are separate masks, complementary performance modes, or two expressions of the same underlying self-possession;
- the move from solitary crown to institution-addressing challenge;
- group focality in Begrazia and shared repertoire;
- and the difference between textual performance intention and realized voice, music, choreography and camera.

Default acquisition rule:

> **Request complete public commu videos and complete MVs/3DMVs. Do not ask the user to pre-cut scenes. The analyst will timestamp the relevant intervals after inspection.**

The textual core is already sufficient for the Phase-3 character model. Missing AV is **verification and refinement**, not a blocker on the existence of the textual analysis.
