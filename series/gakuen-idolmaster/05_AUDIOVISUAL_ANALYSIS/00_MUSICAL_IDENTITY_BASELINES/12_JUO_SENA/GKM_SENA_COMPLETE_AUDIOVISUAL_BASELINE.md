# GKM — JUO SENA COMPLETE AUDIOVISUAL BASELINE
## Phase 3 integrated acting, music, staging, and character synthesis

## 0. Authority, purpose, and evidence discipline

This is the canonical integrated Phase-3 audiovisual baseline for **Juo Sena / 十王星南**. It does not replace the source-facing textual core. It tests, revises, and expands that core through directly inspected Dear/親愛度 compilations, song communications, in-game 3DMVs, authored music videos, promotional video, full-mix song presentations, and controlled Begrazia comparisons.

The inspected packet contains **33 AV objects**:

- **30 staged Sena objects**:
  - four Dear compilations, including a higher-resolution 1–10 control and a separate 1–10 + True End source;
  - six song-communication compilations;
  - ten in-game 3DMVs;
  - five Sena-owned authored MVs;
  - four static/full-mix song presentations;
  - one promotional video;
- **three reused comparative objects** already preserved in the Misuzu AV corpus:
  - Begrazia `Star-mine` 3DMV;
  - Begrazia `Star-mine` authored MV;
  - `Howling over the World` Ume/Misuzu/Sena full-mix comparison.

Dear 001–027 is covered without a narrative gap. Sena's current Dear route ends at 027; there is no missing 028–037 tranche to infer. The 21–27 source is 480p but preserves clean 44.1 kHz stereo audio, complete chapter context, burned-in Japanese text, facial animation, posture, and performance transitions. Its lower image resolution is therefore a limitation for fine texture, not a blocker on acting analysis.

Evidence classes remain distinct:

- **AV-DIRECT** — visible staging, gesture, facial state, framing, editing, movement, timing, and source-audible event structure;
- **AUDIO-METRIC** — source-level or chapter-level loudness, dynamic range, tempo, spectral balance, motion/cut measures, and conservative vocal-proxy measurements;
- **TEXT-CONTROL** — exact story/dialogue authority from Source Lock 1.0 and `GKM_CORE_12_JUO_SENA.md`;
- **INFERENCE** — an interpretation produced by combining the preceding classes;
- **OPEN** — a claim the current packet cannot settle.

The acoustic measurements are not isolated-stem laboratory measurements. Dialogue chapters contain BGM, interface sounds, and sometimes other voiced characters. Song “vocal proxy” values use a center-channel, high-passed harmonic estimate from full mixes. They are useful as constrained comparisons, not as pure measurements of the performer’s unaided voice.

# 1. Executive thesis — answerable sovereignty

The textual core identified Sena's mature structure as **generative supremacy**: the strongest idol proves the value of her position not by preserving weakness beneath her, but by creating conditions in which other people can approach, challenge, answer, and possibly defeat her.

The audiovisual pass preserves that thesis and identifies its performance mechanism:

> **Sena exercises answerable sovereignty. Her stable skill is control over the geometry of attention: she can place the audience below a ceremonial queen, gather it into a collective rally, draw it close through glamorous contemporary-pop intimacy, or reverse the current and become an apprentice, fan, frightened competitor, or grateful recipient of support. Her growth does not abolish the crown. It makes distance variable and return traffic possible.**

The user's proposed two-register musical model is therefore **STRENGTHENED but REVISED**.

Two major idol registers are real:

1. **theatrical/regnant Sena** — ceremonial, vertical, institution-addressing, competitive, and queen-coded;
2. **glamorous/diva Sena** — fashionable, sensual, rhythmically contemporary, bodily self-possessed, and organized around desirability rather than rank.

But the binary is incomplete without a third register:

3. **receptive Sena** — apprentice, fangirl, embarrassed girl, fearful rival, and person capable of being supported.

The first two explain how she commands attention. The third explains how attention can reach her without being converted immediately into proof of supremacy. It is the ethical hinge of her mature image.

A compact formulation is:

> **rank organizes distance; glamour organizes proximity; receptivity makes either form answerable.**

# 2. The queen/diva hypothesis

## 2.1 The regnant register is real

`小さな野望`, `Our Chant`, and major parts of `赤裸々` repeatedly give Sena an explicitly sovereign visual grammar:

- stairs, raised platforms, curtains, circular arenas, and throne-like centrality;
- a solitary figure against a large crowd or field of lights;
- upward arm lines and declarative gestures;
- black, red, gold, and royal-purple environments;
- uniforms, cape/flag imagery, ceremonial accessories, and controlled entrances;
- camera positions that first establish scale or hierarchy before moving toward close address.

This register does not merely illustrate that Sena is “confident.” It gives the Prima Stella office a body. The public title becomes legible through posture, spatial privilege, and the expectation that other people will orient themselves toward her.

## 2.2 The diva register is also real—and broader than `Cosmetic`

`Cosmetic` is the clearest counter-register because its visual presentation replaces academy, crown, and rivalry with cosmetics, roses, perfume, fabric, reclined glamour, mature styling, and direct gaze. The full source contains no rendered choreography, so no bodily-performance claim should be invented. Yet the song's visual and sonic presentation unmistakably positions Sena as a contemporary pop woman whose control works through fashion, beauty, intimacy, and self-display.

The register is not confined to one birthday song. It recurs in:

- `Choo Choo Choo` — magenta graphic design, crop-top styling, silhouettes, sleek movement, and playful fashion spectacle;
- `ENDLESS DANCE` — industrial nightlife, mirrors/doubles, exposed midriff, sharp angles, heart imagery, and aggressive bodily confidence;
- `Howling over the World` — dark digital space, doubled selves, direct aggression, and stylish force;
- `ハッピーミルフィーユ` — flirtation, finger hearts, Valentine framing, sweetness, and personally targeted charm.

These objects demonstrate that Sena's beauty is not just shorthand for institutional prestige. Glamour is an active performance resource.

## 2.3 Queen and diva are not unrelated masks

The stable trait across both registers is **self-possession under observation**.

Regnant Sena says, in effect:

> Look upward; measure yourself against this standard.

Diva Sena says:

> Come closer; discover why you cannot stop looking.

The mechanism changes from hierarchy to desirability, but neither mode is passive. Both control the audience's relation to her. The mature distinction is therefore not “public false queen versus private true woman,” nor “serious Sena versus fanservice Sena.” Both are authored and genuine.

## 2.4 The receptive register prevents closure

Dear 003, 004, 009, and 010 reveal states neither queen nor diva can fully explain:

- failed scouting and comic humiliation;
- nervousness exposed by Kotone;
- fangirl excitement after witnessing a powerful live;
- gratitude that approaches tears;
- junior-coded admiration for Producer as a model;
- willingness to ask for instruction.

These are not defects accidentally leaking through the idol image. They are the states that let Sena remain transformable. A sovereign who can only project cannot learn; a diva who can only attract cannot receive. Receptive Sena is therefore not the “ordinary truth” beneath two performances. She is a third performance capacity and a necessary developmental faculty.

# 3. Dear 001–010 — the crown becomes supportable

## 3.1 Dear 001–002: self-abdication performed with control

Dear 001 begins with Sena already framed as a public emblem. The opening definition of Prima Stella places her at the top of a field of aspiring students. In the classroom, however, the audiovisual grammar narrows: composed frontal close-ups, hands folded or drawn toward the chest, crossed-arm self-command, and small worried shifts coexist with her polished declarative language.

This matters because the retirement proposal is not acted as theatrical collapse. Sena remains controlled while describing herself as `凡人` and moving her first-person dream into production. The surface competence makes the abdication more, not less, serious. It is a highly capable person presenting surrender as a reasoned institutional plan.

The text-first claim that `凡人` is comparative rather than literal self-denigration is preserved. The AV evidence adds that she uses poise to make the proposition administratively credible. Her mask is not simply hiding panic; it is helping fatalism pass as policy.

Dear 002 similarly preserves confidence in the stat eye while allowing uncertainty about what lies outside it. The crucial problem remains epistemic: accurate partial knowledge has been promoted into a closed forecast.

## 3.2 Dear 003: the evaluator becomes ridiculous without becoming incompetent

The Kotone scouting episode begins with a familiar evaluator grammar: chin-in-hand observation, narrowed gaze, composed posture, and precise confidence. The humor comes from how rapidly a socially incomplete model encounters a person whose material life does not fit it.

Sena's visual command does not vanish. It becomes misapplied. The scene therefore supports a more exact ethical finding than “Sena is bad at people”:

> **recognition of capacity is not recognition of circumstance.**

The failed invitation `私のモノになりなさい` exposes how easily an accurate evaluator can confuse seeing potential with possessing jurisdiction.

## 3.3 Dear 004: image rupture increases supportability

Kotone's exposure of Sena's nervous pre-stream state is one of the strongest AV corrections to the perfect-idol model. The scene allows Kotone to become the more visibly kinetic and socially dominant presence while Sena's composed image contracts into embarrassment, softness, and reluctant admission.

Nothing collapses. Fans do not abandon her; the human Sena becomes easier to support. The scene therefore verifies that vulnerability can increase charisma when it creates reciprocal access rather than demanding pity.

**Verdict:** the perfect image is politically useful but relationally expensive. Dynamic excellence is more durable than finished perfection.

## 3.4 Dear 005–008: fear belongs inside authority

Dear 005 keeps fear and office in the same body. Sena uses hand-to-heart and self-containing gestures while explaining why the perfect image matters and why stronger idols threaten more than her record. The fear is not a separate broken self. It is governed inside the public figure.

Dear 008 restores the signpost function of the top idol. The backstage white stage dress, fan held near the face, controlled hand-on-hip posture, and worried eyes allow regnant intention and anxiety to coexist. She does not become legitimate by ceasing to fear. She becomes legitimate by entering the contest despite fear.

## 3.5 Dear 009: the clearest register discontinuity

Dear 009 moves from luminous stage command to dressing-room fangirl, blush, fan-to-mouth gestures, hand-to-chest gratitude, and near-tear relief. The scene-level pitch proxy is a conspicuous outlier: with Sena holding approximately 79% of the dialogue, its median F0 estimate is about **341 Hz**, compared with roughly **222–231 Hz** in several high-Sena-share public/crown chapters. Because these are scene mixes rather than isolated speech, the numbers cannot prove vocal placement by themselves. Their direction nevertheless matches the visible state change.

This is the strongest evidence that public command and fangirl excitement are intentionally differentiated performance states.

The scene also changes the moral meaning of supremacy. Sena is not only the object of reception. She is a fan whose own future can be reopened by another performer.

## 3.6 Dear 010: apprenticeship without loss of stature

Dear 010 narrows from public command into classroom admiration, hand-to-heart declaration, shyness, and intimate address. Sena identifies Producer as the model for her second vocation and asks to learn at his side.

The junior-coded register is not regression. It is a relation in which Sena can temporarily occupy the position of the person who does not already know. That capacity is indispensable to her producer development.

# 4. Dear 011–020 — the crown leaves the local system

The middle tranche is structurally about expansion: external competition, the inadequacy of local closure, produced idols, and the replacement of a successor project with a rival field.

The 11–20 compilation lacks embedded chapter markers. Its boundaries were modeled from text length and visually checked, so precise shot claims are made only where the scene identity is secure. The broad AV pattern is nevertheless clear: Sena retains her formal school-uniform command while the social frame fills with idols whose trajectories she cannot reduce to one standard.

Dear 015 is especially useful as a visual counterpoint. Sena can speak from the familiar composed, evaluative posture while another idol's presence forces the conversation away from a self-enclosed crown. The recurring N.I.A. title interstitials and multiple-speaker scenes make chapter-wide acoustic values weaker evidence here than in 1–10 or 21–27.

Dear 020 returns to a stronger Sena-centered state. The text says she now believes that the stronger she becomes, the more her rivals will shine. The audiovisual sequence moves from classroom declaration to performance/ending imagery and then to the Producer's institutional data view. The shift matters: the crown no longer functions only as an endpoint. It is a device for increasing the output of the entire field.

**Verdict:** the successor model is audiovisually less dynamic than the rival model. A successor waits for vacancy; a rival creates present-tense risk.

# 5. Dear 021–027 — H.I.F. and the crown entering its own experiment

## 5.1 Dear 021: production becomes first-person again

Dear 021 opens with Sena describing the stable position she has achieved as top idol, then catches herself as the ending approaches. She explicitly states that her true dream should be realized by her own hands. The contact sheet repeatedly returns to hand-to-chest gestures, direct gaze, and a visibly softened smile.

This scene integrates rather than replaces her two vocations. Production is no longer the honorable substitute for an abandoned idol dream. It becomes one of the practices through which she makes her own idol future uncertain again.

## 5.2 Dear 022–024: differentiated production and material context

Dear 022 places China and Misuzu in the frame and emphasizes that production is not standardization. The visual distribution among several characters supports the textual point: Sena's task is not to make copies of Sena, but to understand different sources of strength.

The Kotone intervention remains ethically ambiguous. It demonstrates care beyond optimization and attention to family material conditions, but it also demonstrates the reach of Sena's institutional power. The AV pass cannot resolve the recipient-side legitimacy of that intervention; Kotone-side event/support evidence remains necessary.

## 5.3 Dear 025: rival recognition and visible uncertainty

Dear 025 begins with Sena acknowledging that she can no longer evade the situation. Her hands draw inward; the classroom composition alternates contained anxiety with declarative confidence. Tsubame then enters in black, using a more openly confrontational visual grammar.

The scene supports the core distinction between institutionally protected supremacy and supremacy made answerable. Sena is not validated because no one can reach her. She is validated only if someone is permitted to try.

## 5.4 Dear 026: the field she made becomes frightening

Dear 026 intercuts multiple performers, stage footage, and Sena in red ceremonial dress. She describes the H.I.F. as a field in which students serious about becoming top idols will collide. The montage no longer belongs exclusively to her. Other idols receive their own forceful close-ups and performance spaces.

This is the audiovisual form of generative supremacy: her authority is represented through a field of independently legible dangers.

## 5.5 Dear 027: victory does not restore closure

Dear 027 gives Sena the Prima Stella summit, but the sequence does not end by returning everyone beneath her. Close-ups of Sena in the red dress alternate with Kotone's emotional aftermath, nighttime conversation, and Sena's renewed attention to numbers and future possibility.

The important point is not that Sena becomes humble. She remains certain, ambitious, and pleased to win. The important change is that victory no longer proves the field is finished.

Her declaration that the stronger she becomes, the more her rivals will shine is not self-effacement. It is a theory of recursive escalation.

**Verdict:** the summer crown is an office reopened by contest, not a trophy sealing the route.

# 6. Music and performance — song-by-song findings

## 6.1 `小さな野望`: closed measurement becomes a road

The in-game 3DMV gives Sena the clearest early regnant frame: palace-like curtains and stairs, a solitary raised body, blue opening light that turns red and gold, crown/ornament imagery, a sea of audience lights, and upward declarative gestures.

The authored MV complicates that sovereignty. It begins with book and constellation imagery, numerical overlays, a road at night, an enclosed circular red arena, and a small figure inside systems of measurement. It gradually opens into moon, space, color, and forward movement.

The pair decisively answers the textual question: this is **not** a valedictory final song. It begins inside the logic of closure—measured ceiling, enclosed arena, completed office—and then discovers a path beyond the known graph.

**Verdict:** `小さな野望` is the origin of answerable sovereignty: the queen retains the crown while admitting an unmeasured future.

## 6.2 `Our Chant`: command becomes mobilization

`Our Chant` uses black flag/cape/beret/cane imagery, sunset, outdoor festival scale, portals/circular staging, and repeated turns between Sena and a crowd. The choreography retains command. It does not make her one anonymous participant among equals.

But the object of command changes. She is no longer merely displaying the standard from above. She is organizing collective motion.

The result is not soft egalitarian invitation. It is closer to a rally:

> **follow, answer, sing, and become strong enough that the chant no longer belongs only to me.**

This is the institutional song in which office becomes performance argument.

## 6.3 `赤裸々`: the crown descends without disappearing

The 3DMV uses a red/black circular arena, fire, descent/appearance framing, large crowd scale, and alternation between cool exposed close-ups and fiery command. The authored MV adds starburst, falling/floating Sena, doubles, firebird imagery, circular diagrams, feathered transformation, explicit typography, and `COME TOGETHER NOW`-type collective address.

The song commu identifies the work as a co-produced “child” of Sena and Producer and focuses its addressee on rivals. The audiovisual evidence supports that logic.

“Nakedness” does not mean reducing Sena to helpless authenticity beneath the crown. It means removing enough ceremonial armor that challenge can travel both ways. The soft, moist-eyed closeups at the end do not cancel the firebird; they make the battle invitation personal.

**Verdict:** `赤裸々` is not de-crowning. It is **the crown made contestable and co-authored**.

## 6.4 `Cosmetic`: diva sovereignty

`Cosmetic` provides the most concentrated glamorous/diva object. Its static lyric presentation places Sena in an editorial tableau of roses, cosmetics, perfume, patterned fabric, reclined posture, and direct gaze. Because the source is visually static, claims about choreography or animated body language remain prohibited.

The song's full-mix tempo estimate is approximately **144 BPM**, with a relatively wide loudness range (**LRA about 10.8**) compared with several flatter common-song controls. The conservative center-channel vocal proxy places the median tracked melody around **278 Hz** with a wider interquartile span than `小さな野望` or `赤裸々`; because extraction quality varies among mixes, this supports only a cautious observation that `Cosmetic` uses a lower and more variably inflected melodic presentation than the most ceremonial high-line material.

The crucial finding is conceptual: intimacy does not make Sena less controlling. It changes the mechanism of control. The listener is no longer primarily a student below the office or a rival called into battle. The listener is brought near enough to be affected by styling, breath, polish, and desirability.

**Verdict:** the diva register is **STRENGTHENED** as a core component of Sena's musical identity.

## 6.5 `Campus mode!!` and `初`: beginning after mastery

`Campus mode!!` places Sena in bright shared-school pop, toy-like platforms, direct audience address, and institutional color. She remains legible without dark royal staging. The song commu adds family, school publicity, Kotone, and the memory of being a novice.

`初` is even more direct as a control. Sena inhabits “beginning” language on a comparatively open standard stage. The outfit and choreography are less ceremonial, and the performance allows bright vulnerability without asking her to pretend she is technically unfinished.

These songs show that institutional inheritance can be carried by the academy's most established student without turning every shared song into a coronation.

## 6.6 `Choo Choo Choo`: playful fashion authority

The authored MV uses saturated magenta/purple graphic design, typography, silhouettes, train movement, crop-top styling, and sleek contemporary spectacle. The promotional video and full-mix source reinforce the song's self-aware theatrical flexibility.

This is not merely comic relief from “serious Sena.” It demonstrates that she can surrender ceremonial gravitas without surrendering command of the frame. Play becomes another form of professional control.

## 6.7 `ENDLESS DANCE`: aggressive diva/action register

`ENDLESS DANCE` uses industrial green/purple/black space, mirrors and doubles, heart motifs, exposed midriff, aggressive angles, and nightlife-coded bodily confidence. Its authored MV is the most kinetically edited object in the inspected Sena packet, with high sampled motion and more than fifty major cut events.

The song therefore bridges the two-register model. It retains force and supremacy, but the force is neither courtly nor institutional. It is corporeal, fashionable, nocturnal, and contemporary.

## 6.8 `ハッピーミルフィーユ`: personally targeted charm

The in-game version gives Sena a pink Valentine stage, finger hearts, direct camera address, smiles, and cute/flirtatious framing. The authored MV redistributes attention through chibi/graphic ensemble comedy and ordinary school-peer chemistry.

The associated commu is valuable because the feminine and romantic-coded material occurs in a relational context rather than as abstract fanservice. Sena can be embarrassed, amused, and personally expressive without the scene interpreting that femininity as a fall from authority.

## 6.9 `Howling`, `ミラクルナナウ`, and `がむしゃらに行こう！`

These common-song controls reject a narrow repertoire model.

- `Howling over the World` places Sena in dark digital aggression, doubled selves, dramatic angles, and stylish force.
- `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！` tests bright digital absurdity, sporty color, novelty energy, and comic elasticity.
- `がむしゃらに行こう！` places her in school/classroom, effort, camera-UI, and messy striving rather than finished excellence.

Sena's identity survives all three because the stable trait is not regal genre. It is deliberate control of how attention is directed.

## 6.10 Begrazia / `Star-mine`: sovereignty without permanent center monopoly

The 3DMV rotates focus among Ume, Misuzu, and Sena. Ume often supplies kinetic frontality; Misuzu supplies low-friction gravity; Sena receives forceful close-ups and declarative moments without owning every shot. The authored MV develops a cosmic three-star structure with distinct individual and relational passages.

This directly falsifies the assumption that Sena can remain Sena only by monopolizing center position.

**Verdict:** Begrazia supports **circulating focal authority**. Sena remains a strong center while accepting that the unit contains other irreducible centers.

# 7. Voice and register

## 7.1 Public crown

The public-crown register is defined less by constant loudness than by clean intention. Declarative syntax, controlled pacing, and gestures that complete without visible uncertainty make her statements feel institutionally binding.

High-Sena-share chapter proxies place Dear 001, 005, and 008 in a comparatively lower central range than the fangirl outlier in Dear 009. The exact values are scene-level and include BGM, but the contrast aligns with the visible performance: public command remains contained; excitement lifts and accelerates.

## 7.2 Evaluator/producer

When Sena evaluates talent, the voice-state is paired with chin-in-hand or arms-crossed posture, direct questions, and a sense that the scene is being categorized. The weakness is not coldness alone. It is premature closure.

As Producer-apprentice, that register gains genuine curiosity. She can ask for the answer, admit that she did not predict an outcome, and treat Producer as `先輩` without collapsing into childish dependence.

## 7.3 Human/cute embarrassment

Dear 003, 004, 009, 010, and the Valentine material show a large expressive expansion: blush, softened eyes, fan-to-mouth concealment, self-touch, smaller posture, faster reactions, and shifts toward personally targeted speech.

The “cute/human Sena” is neither accidental leakage nor purely strategic acceptance. It is a real state that becomes increasingly available for professional use without being fully instrumentalized.

## 7.4 Fear and gratitude

Fear is usually contained inside composure rather than expressed through immediate collapse. Gratitude creates a different breach: the close gaze softens, the body draws inward, and the voice-state permits the possibility of tears.

This difference matters. Fear tests whether the office can remain intact under threat. Gratitude tests whether the person inside the office can receive something she did not author.

## 7.5 Regnant singing versus diva singing

The full-mix proxy does not justify a claim that Sena simply sings “higher as queen, lower as diva” across all sources. Mix structure and extraction quality vary too much. It does support a narrower finding:

- the regnant songs tend to organize the vocal line inside large declarative environments, collective hooks, or ceremonial climaxes;
- `Cosmetic`, `Choo Choo Choo`, `ENDLESS DANCE`, and `ハッピーミルフィーユ` make phrasing, styling, intimacy, and bodily rhythm more central to the listener's experience.

The two registers are therefore better distinguished by **address and production geometry** than by one pitch statistic.

# 8. Relationships and institutions after AV inspection

## 8.1 Producer — senior, co-author, and authority risk

The AV evidence strengthens the claim that Sena's Producer relation is genuinely reciprocal in affect and asymmetrical in structure.

Sena becomes more junior-coded, playful, admiring, and openly receptive around the person she names as a model. `赤裸々` then makes their joint authorship visible in a work aimed at rivals. Yet Producer still controls opportunities, interpretation, and production design. Shared possession language can be affectionate while formal authority remains real.

**Verdict:** reciprocity is **STRENGTHENED**; resolution of power asymmetry remains **OPEN**.

## 8.2 Kotone — from object to person who can alter Sena

The audiovisual contrast between the failed scouting scene and the supportability stream demonstrates the relationship's reversal. Kotone begins as a potential object inside Sena's talent model, then becomes someone who can expose, embarrass, support, and transform Sena's public image.

That is one of the clearest examples of generative supremacy becoming reciprocal rather than merely patronal.

## 8.3 Rivals — the addressee changes

`Our Chant`, `赤裸々`, Dear 025–027, and Begrazia progressively change the addressee of Sena's authority:

- students who look upward;
- a crowd called into motion;
- rivals summoned into direct contest;
- peers sharing the center.

The crown is not abolished. Its social geometry is altered.

## 8.4 Fans — constituency, audience, and source of return pressure

Fans do not merely certify Sena's perfection. Dear 004 and 009 show them supporting an incomplete, excited, and visibly affected person. The fan relation therefore becomes one of the mechanisms by which the office can remain human.

## 8.5 Prima Stella — answerable office

The AV pass strengthens the constitutional reading of Prima Stella. Its legitimacy depends on visibility, exemplary force, and willingness to enter the risk structure. The crown that refuses contest becomes stage décor; the crown that creates and faces rivals becomes an office.

# 11. Canonical synthesis

The strongest bounded formulation after AV inspection is:

> **Juo Sena is the idol of generative supremacy performed as answerable sovereignty. She begins as a perfected local crown who uses poise to make self-abdication look rational. Her voice, body, and music reveal not one fixed queen persona but a controlled repertoire of distances: regnant Sena organizes the field vertically; diva Sena governs through glamour and proximity; receptive Sena becomes apprentice, fan, frightened rival, and supportable person. `小さな野望` opens the measured ceiling into a road, `Our Chant` turns command into mobilization, `赤裸々` makes the crown co-authored and contestable, and `Star-mine` proves she can share focal authority without disappearing.
