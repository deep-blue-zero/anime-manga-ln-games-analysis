# JUO SENA — MUSIC, MV, AND PERFORMANCE CLOSE READING

## 0. Evidence architecture

Three layers are kept distinct:

1. **song commu** — narrative authorship, intended addressee, institutional and relational framing;
2. **in-game 3DMV** — embodied stage performance, choreography, virtual camera, expression, costume motion, and audience relation;
3. **authored MV / lyric / promotional presentation** — independent symbolic montage and graphic design that need not be literal story continuity.

Full-mix metrics support comparison of tempo, loudness range, spectral balance, motion, and editing. They do not isolate Sena's vocal stem.

## 1. Musical thesis — three registers, one attention geometry

Sena's musical identity is not “queen songs plus exceptions.” It is a three-register system:

- **regnant/theatrical** — rank, ceremony, challenge, institution, verticality;
- **glamorous/diva** — fashion, sensuality, proximity, bodily self-possession, contemporary pop;
- **receptive/relational** — beginner memory, fanhood, play, embarrassment, shared center, and personal address.

The registers overlap. Their common denominator is Sena's ability to determine how the listener approaches her.

> **Regnant Sena makes the audience look up. Diva Sena makes the audience lean in. Relational Sena allows the audience or rival to answer.**

## 2. `小さな野望` — the measured world opens

### 2.1 3DMV

The 3DMV builds a royal theater: curtains, stairs, isolated elevation, blue-to-red-gold transformation, ornament/crown motifs, and an audience rendered as a field of light. Sena's movements are broad enough to command scale but controlled enough to preserve hierarchy. The camera repeatedly establishes her as a singular body above or before the crowd.

### 2.2 Authored MV

The authored MV gives the song a different ontology. Book and constellation imagery, numerical overlays, roads, moon, space, an enclosed circular arena, and an initially small figure connect the song to Sena's stat eye and self-imposed ceiling. The later movement into color and open space reframes ambition as a path beyond the measurable system.

### 2.3 Finding

The song begins with completed-crown imagery but refuses valediction. It is the first audiovisual statement that the known summit is too small.

**Claim:** `小さな野望` is Sena's transition from **closed supremacy** to **future-bearing supremacy**.

## 3. `Our Chant` — civic command

The 3DMV's black flag/cape/beret/cane styling, sunset rally, circular stage, and crowd address make the song explicitly political in visual grammar. Sena turns away from and back toward the crowd, raises the field, and occupies the role of organizer.

The estimated tempo is relatively moderate (about 94 BPM), but the visual motion and cut structure create scale through rallying gesture rather than speed. This is useful: authority does not have to become frantic to become collective.

The song does not dissolve hierarchy. Sena remains the person who calls. What changes is that the audience is asked to become an active chorus.

**Claim:** `Our Chant` converts sovereignty from **displayed office** into **mobilizing office**.

## 4. `赤裸々` — exposed firebird

### 4.1 3DMV

The stage is circular, red/black, and fire-dominated. Sena appears through descent and emergence, alternates between controlled closeups and forceful full-body command, and ends with a softer, exposed gaze.

### 4.2 Authored MV

The MV uses starburst, falling/floating body, doubles, firebird transformation, diagrams, typography, feathers, and repeated demands for convergence. Its wide dynamic range and highly fragmented editorial motion make revelation itself feel unstable and active.

### 4.3 Song commu

The commu makes the song a co-authored work of Sena and Producer and narrows the addressee toward rivals. Sena descends from star-like distance to call others into a shared contest.

### 4.4 Finding

The song does not uncover a powerless “real Sena.” It exposes desire, fear, and dependence while retaining the force of the crown.

**Claim:** `赤裸々` is **sovereignty without immunity**.

## 5. `Cosmetic` — glamour as power

`Cosmetic` is visually static but analytically indispensable. Roses, cosmetics, perfume, plaid fabric, reclined pose, mature styling, and direct gaze place Sena in a fashion-editorial world almost entirely outside Hatsuboshi's ceremonial vocabulary.

Its production is brisk (approximately 144 BPM) yet dynamically roomy. The conservative vocal proxy suggests a lower and more variably inflected tracked melody than the most ceremonial high-line songs, but stem isolation is unavailable and the result must remain provisional.

The decisive difference is addressee geometry. The viewer is not primarily below a queen or before a student-council office. The viewer is placed near a glamorous woman who understands observation as pleasure and leverage.

**Claim:** `Cosmetic` confirms that Sena has a core **diva sovereignty** organized by desirability rather than rank.

## 6. `Campus mode!!` — the office remembers being a student

The performance uses bright institutional color, playful platforms, direct crowd address, and standard school-pop choreography. Sena does not have to darken or monumentalize the material to remain legible.

The commu's family and novice-memory context gives the work a double function:

- she embodies Hatsuboshi as its highest student representative;
- she remembers that she once needed the institution rather than simply personifying it.

**Claim:** shared repertoire can soften distance without dissolving authority.

## 7. `初` — firstness without pretending incompetence

Sena's `初` performance is comparatively open and direct. The stage and styling are less ceremonial, and the song's beginning-language is not treated as ironic merely because she is already accomplished.

The performance distinguishes **novicehood** from **renewal**. Sena does not need to be technically unformed to begin again.

## 8. `Choo Choo Choo` — playful fashion theater

The official MV is one of the packet's most saturated and rapidly cut objects. Magenta/purple graphics, typography, silhouettes, train imagery, crop-top styling, and sleek movement produce a contemporary fashion spectacle.

This source complicates the queen/diva binary in a useful way. It is theatrical, but not regnant. It is glamorous, but not primarily sensual. Its governing affect is playful self-styling.

**Claim:** Sena's diva register includes wit, graphic modernity, and theatrical flexibility—not only eroticized glamour.

## 9. `ENDLESS DANCE` — nocturnal bodily command

The authored MV has the highest sampled motion in the current packet and more than fifty major cut events. The 3DMV uses industrial green/purple/black space, mirrors and doubles, heart motifs, low/high camera angles, and exposed-midriff styling.

The source demonstrates that force can move from the throne into the body. Sena's command is fashionable, kinetic, and nocturnal rather than ceremonial.

**Claim:** `ENDLESS DANCE` is the strongest bridge between regnant force and diva embodiment.

## 10. `ハッピーミルフィーユ` — charm that can be personally received

The 3DMV gives Sena pink Valentine staging, finger hearts, smiles, and direct flirtatious address. The authored MV distributes attention through school comedy, chibi graphics, and peer relation.

The commu is essential because it prevents the song from becoming an abstract “cute mode.” Sena's charm emerges in relation to peers, gifts, embarrassment, and personally directed affection.

**Claim:** femininity and romantic-coded play are not escapes from Sena's authority; they are forms of relational breadth.

## 11. Aggressive and comic common-song controls

### `Howling over the World`

Dark digital space, ghosted doubles, crop-top styling, and dramatic angles permit overt aggression without requiring royal iconography.

### `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！`

Bright digital absurdity, sporty color, novelty motion, and comic energy test Sena's willingness to abandon perfect dignity. She remains legible because precision and camera control survive the tonal shift.

### `がむしゃらに行こう！`

Classroom/school staging, effort imagery, and camera-UI make unfinished striving visible. The song is an important corrective to any model in which Sena can only perform polished completion.

## 12. Begrazia / `Star-mine`

The 3DMV rotates center among Ume, Misuzu, and Sena. Ume's kinetic force, Misuzu's centripetal calm, and Sena's declarative command remain distinguishable. The authored MV's cosmic three-star system gives each member individual and relational passages.

Sena does not disappear when she ceases to monopolize the frame. This is the strongest visual proof that generative supremacy can become plural rather than merely expansionist.

**Claim:** Begrazia is both Sena-designed strongest-unit logic and genuine three-way authorship.

## 13. Register map

| register | principal objects | spatial/addressee model | risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| regnant/theatrical | `小さな野望`, `Our Chant`, `赤裸々` | audience/rivals arranged in relation to crown and office | hierarchy mistaken for total personhood |
| glamorous/diva | `Cosmetic`, `ENDLESS DANCE`, `Choo Choo Choo`, `Howling` | viewer drawn close through fashion, body, rhythm, desirability | proximity becomes another unilateral control technology |
| relational/receptive | `Campus mode!!`, `初`, `ハッピーミルフィーユ`, `Star-mine` | family, peers, fans, rivals, and partners permitted to answer | reception converted back into proof or debt |
| common-song elasticity | `がむしゃら`, `ミラクルナナウ`, shared repertoire | Sena enters material not authored solely around her | over-reading generic choreography as character-specific |

## 14. Final musical formulation

> **Sena's musical identity is the disciplined modulation of attention geometry. The crown repertoire creates height, ceremony, and collective obligation. The diva repertoire creates proximity, fashion, sensuality, and bodily self-possession. Relational repertoire lets novice memory, fanhood, embarrassment, and shared center travel back toward her. None is the hidden truth behind the others. The mature Sena is powerful because she can change the mechanism of command without losing continuity—and because `赤裸々` and `Star-mine` begin to make that command answerable.**
