## Governing thesis

> **Rinami remains the idol of reciprocal care becoming self-authored desire. The audiovisual pass identifies reversible hospitality as the formal mechanism: she makes a space in which another person can feel held, then learns to remain inside that space as someone who can also want, compete, confess, and be held.**

Compact shorthand:

> **receive -> hold -> offer -> be answered -> expand**

R2 adds a higher-resolution late-route correction:

> **Mature desire is not composure replacing tears. It is a holding frame strong enough to let tears, blush, lowered gaze, breath disruption, competition, and direct confession count—then return as completed address without retracting them.**

## R2 revision over R1

R1 was analytically complete but used 480p30 and 360p30 fallback encodes for Dear 021-027 and 028-037. R2 integrates the user-supplied 720p60 and 720p30 direct-upload materializations. Their audio elementary streams are byte-identical to the R1 fallback copies, so the revision changes visual confidence rather than dialogue timing or delivered audio.

The high-resolution review **strengthens rather than overturns** R1. It closes five fine-grained questions:

1. Dear 027 makes tears and full affect integral to self-authored desire rather than evidence of failed composure.
2. Dear 031 shows technical authority retaining relational softness.
3. Dear 033 shows self-love as visibly audience-returned rather than solitary self-branding.
4. Dear 036 shows public authority remaining permeable to tears and gratitude.
5. Dear 037 is a sequenced multi-register passage—familiarity, embarrassment, solemn gathering, avowal, mutual-duty syntax, and professional future—not one undifferentiated romantic voice.
