# GKM — HIMESAKI RINAMI AUDIOVISUAL BASELINE AND REQUESTS

## 0. Purpose and acquisition rule

This document defines the minimum and extended audiovisual corpus required to test `GKM_CORE_08_HIMESAKI_RINAMI.md`. It is a **retrieval and verification register**, not an audiovisual analysis.

Acquisition rules:

- Prefer **whole commus, whole MVs, whole 3DMVs, and complete song videos/audio**. The user does **not** need to cut clips.
- Human-searchable Japanese title/source-family identity leads every request. Internal `adv_*` IDs are provenance keys only.
- 720p is normally sufficient for dialogue acting; 1080p or better is preferred for MV/choreography/visual-form analysis.
- If one whole STEP compilation contains several requested acting scenes, acquire that whole compilation once rather than clipping each scene.
- CD-exclusive solo recordings are nonblocking unless they answer a specific unresolved hypothesis.

## 1. Core AV hypotheses to test

### AV-RINAMI-H01 — performed naturalness
Does voiced/staged Rinami distinguish consciously activated `お姉さん` performance from ordinary unforced care, and does that distinction narrow as the route progresses?

### AV-RINAMI-H02 — romance without collapse into romance-only identity
Do Dear 009–010 and 037 create a distinct intimate register while preserving the same relational voice recognizable in fan/public care?

### AV-RINAMI-H03 — vulnerability → forwardness
Does the musical sequence `clumsy trick` → `36℃ U･B･U` audibly/visually move from hesitation and indirectness toward more assertive romantic/performative agency?

### AV-RINAMI-H04 — care broadens beyond soft/maternal coding
Do `Campus mode!!`, `Howling over the World`, and `ガラクタロード` show force, brightness, genre elasticity, competition, and encouragement without making Rinami cease to sound like herself?

### AV-RINAMI-H05 — technique as love
Can Dear 031's `全身でファンサ` / `愛` claim be seen or heard in actual performance precision, phrasing, gesture, camera address, and audience-facing timing?

### AV-RINAMI-H06 — reciprocal rather than unilateral intimacy
In late Dear, can Rinami audibly receive comfort, ask for presence, expose fear, and confess desire rather than remaining only the emotionally regulating party?

### AV-RINAMI-H07 — peer collaboration
Does `SUGAR FLAVOR` / RippleSign stage Mao and Rinami as reciprocal peers with differentiated strengths, or does one performance identity dominate?

## 6. Dialogue acting questions

Timestamp after acquisition rather than asking the user to clip.

Key register hypotheses:

1. **ordinary warm care** — does it sound less consciously “performed” than public onee-san activation?
2. **professional/technical Rinami** — how does veteran third-year competence alter pace, diction, confidence, and command?
3. **receiving/depending** — activity_020, Dear 020, 023–027; does vulnerability reduce the mature register or coexist with it?
4. **romantic hesitation** — Dear 009–010, CIDOL 000/013.
5. **competitive desire** — N.I.A., `優勝したかった`, H.I.F.
6. **late reciprocal confidence** — Dear 031–037.
7. **confession/vow** — does Dear 037 preserve playful onee-san familiarity, become solemn, become lover-coded, become professional, or deliberately pass through several modes?

## 7. Visual-performance questions

When 3DMV/MV sources are available, inspect:

- gesture direction: enclosing/welcoming versus frontal challenge;
- use of eye contact and camera-address as fanservice;
- mature styling versus awkward/hesitant lyrical content;
- whether choreography gives Rinami spatially caretaking positions or peer/summit positions;
- whether `SUGAR FLAVOR` distributes stage center and call-response symmetrically;
- whether `ガラクタロード` changes the familiar softness/maturity visual language;
- whether visual framing aestheticizes “older sister” as body/type rather than behavior, and how Rinami herself does or does not contest that framing.
