# HATAYA MISUZU — AUDIOVISUAL BASELINE AND WHOLE-VIDEO REQUEST PACKET

## 1. Purpose

This register converts the Phase-3 Misuzu textual reading into a whole-video acquisition plan. It asserts no uninspected timbre, acting, choreography, camera, animation, costume-motion, facial-performance or MV conclusion.

The principal hypotheses to test are:

1. whether soft/polite Misuzu and possessive/threatening Misuzu are acoustically one register or deliberately differentiated;
2. whether `マイペース` has a stable vocal/bodily signature across ordinary rest, competition, jealousy and grief;
3. how concealed defeat pain differs from open emotional exposure;
4. Temari-facing childhood intimacy versus Producer intimacy, Rinha conflict, fans and formal student-council speech;
5. `ツキノカメ` as the first solo test of calmness plus danger;
6. `Campus mode!!` as re-authored SyngUp! inheritance / `穏やかなる下剋上`;
7. `Superlative` as dream-possession / godlike fan-image construction;
8. `VEIL` as night-sky sovereignty and deliberately unfinished representative work;
9. group blocking and musical identity in Begrazia/`Star-mine` and the Sena/Ume shared-song layer;
10. whether late Prima Stella confidence remains visibly compatible with tears, vulnerability and dependence.

Whole commus, complete story compilations, official full MVs and complete game 3DMVs are preferred. Do **not** manually clip unless no full source exists.
