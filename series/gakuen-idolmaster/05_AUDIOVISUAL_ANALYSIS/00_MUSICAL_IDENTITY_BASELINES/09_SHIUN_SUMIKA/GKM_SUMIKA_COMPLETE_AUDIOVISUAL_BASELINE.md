# GKM — SHIUN SUMIKA COMPLETE AUDIOVISUAL BASELINE
## Phase 3 integrated acting, music, choreography, visual authorship, and character synthesis

## 0. Authority and evidence discipline

This document is the canonical Phase-3 audiovisual baseline for **Shiun Sumika / 紫雲清夏**. It does not replace `GKM_CORE_09_SHIUN_SUMIKA.md`. The textual core remains authoritative for exact Japanese wording, route-state boundaries, chronology, and claims about what Sumika or other characters explicitly believe. This document asks what the voiced and rendered material adds: how Sumika occupies time, how brightness changes function, how her body carries the history of ballet without returning to ballet as destiny, how her principal songs reorganize vulnerability, and how REVERSI stages intimacy without erasing differentiation.

The staged packet contains **22 AV objects**:

- four Dear compilations covering Dear 001–037;
- five song-communication compilations;
- eight rendered 3DMVs;
- four authored official music videos;
- one full released static audio presentation for `カクシタワタシ`.

All **22 logical AV objects** are now directly inspectable at the current boundary. Exact wording and route semantics remain controlled by Source Lock 1.0; the direct upload supplies the previously missing performed layer. Mixed-scene acoustic measurements remain source-level aids rather than isolated measurements of Minato Miya's vocal instrument.

Evidence labels used below:

- **AV-DIRECT** — visible or audible feature of a locally materialized source;
- **TEXT-CONTROL** — exact meaning, wording, chronology, or route boundary established by Source Lock 1.0;
- **SOURCE-METRIC** — source-level technical measurement of a mixed file, never an isolated vocal stem;
- **INFERENCE** — synthesis requiring both direct evidence and textual context;
- **OPEN** — plausible but not settled within the current boundary.

Source forms are kept separate:

1. **Dear/song commu** establishes acted story context, speech rhythm, relation-specific behavior, and intended meaning.
2. **3DMV** establishes rendered body, choreography, facial performance, virtual camera, and audience relation.

4. **Static full-mix presentation** establishes released audio and lyric/recording continuity, not authored moving-image symbolism.

Most importantly, `カクシタワタシ` has **no authored official music video in the inspected corpus**. It is analyzed through its full released song, game 3DMV, and complete song commu. No claim below treats the static audio presentation as an MV.

# 1. Executive thesis — kinetic self-authorship

The textual core identifies Sumika as the idol of **self-authored expectation**. Her injury did not simply make her afraid of pain; it made belief itself dangerous. Other people had already written a future around the ballet prodigy. After that future collapsed, casualness, play, skipped effort, and the possibility of saying “I never really tried” became ways to keep judgment from reaching the deepest self.

The AV pass preserves that thesis and identifies its principal formal mechanism:

> **Sumika is the idol of kinetic self-authorship. She does not mature by exposing a serious true self beneath a fake gyaru exterior. She learns to make genuine brightness, strategic unseriousness, technical memory, fear, fashion, and chosen performance move in the same direction. The body once written by ballet expectation becomes the medium through which she selects, stylizes, and redistributes expectation.**

A compact formulation is:

> **Brightness becomes technique. Technique becomes address. Address turns expectation into a promise she chose.**

This requires rejecting three simpler stories.

First, the gyaru is not merely armor. `Kira Kira`, ordinary commu behavior, common-song performances, and her relaxed social presentation show that Sumika genuinely enjoys fashion, play, shopping, teasing, public contact, and a low-friction relation to other people. The defense lies not in the falsity of brightness but in its **strategic deployment** when silence would expose stakes.

Second, healing is not a return to ballet. Ballet survives as line, balance, extension, spatial intelligence, teaching vocabulary, and bodily memory. It becomes available for new authorship without reclaiming Sumika as the future it once prescribed.

Third, maturity does not remove performance. The `強いあたしの仮面` is not peeled away to reveal the only real Sumika. It becomes less exclusively defensive and more technically, aesthetically, and vocationally useful. Sumika learns when to use brightness as invitation, when to slow it into protected confession, and when to turn it outward as courage.

The principal audiovisual sequence is therefore:

> **`Tame-Lie-One-Step` — brightness makes movement possible.**  
> **`カクシタワタシ` — stillness protects a hidden self long enough to be heard.**  
> **`Love & Joy` — self-authored movement turns weakness into public direction.**  
> **`ときめきエモーション` / REVERSI — public direction becomes relationally plural rather than singular or fused.**

# 2. The five interacting systems

## 2.1 Body: injury, technical memory, and expressive surplus

The early Dear footage does not reduce Sumika's injury history to a line of exposition. Her relation to movement is repeatedly staged against places where motion should be easy: training rooms, school grounds, streets, stages, and recreational spaces. The route's basic paradox remains visible: she is socially mobile and physically expressive, yet high-stakes dance can become the point where her body remembers judgment.

The important distinction is not “can she move?” She plainly can. The distinction is between movement that feels socially playful and movement that becomes evidence about whether the former prodigy still exists.

`Love & Joy` supplies the strongest formal revision. Its 3DMV repeatedly gives Sumika open diagonals, extended arms, balanced turns, controlled torso orientation, and broad shapes that read cleanly across a large stage. These are not proof of literal ballet choreography. They are evidence that ballet-derived line and spatial awareness have been repurposed inside an idol grammar: frontal address, camera awareness, crowd relation, and rhythmic accessibility.

> **AV finding:** ballet survives as technical inheritance rather than restored destiny.

Competence therefore appears as **expressive surplus**. Earlier, fear and evaluation consume attention. Later, more of the body can be allocated to gaze, styling, audience address, partner awareness, and deliberate shape. Sumika does not become valuable because injury disappears. She becomes increasingly capable of deciding what the recovered capacity is for.

## 2.2 Social brightness: authentic pleasure and defensive continuity

The acted commus and breadth performances support a stable social fact: Sumika likes being bright. She likes joking, styling, addressing other people casually, and making an encounter feel less ceremonious. Her gyaru register is not an empty façade imposed after trauma.

The defensive version is better understood as **forced continuity**. When a scene threatens to stop on fear, shame, envy, or dependence, Sumika can keep the social surface moving—joke, tease, redirect, or immediately soften a direct admission. The emotional defense is not “sound cheerful at all times.” It is “do not let the moment remain still long enough to become a verdict.”

This is why `カクシタワタシ` matters. The 3DMV radically reduces locomotion and external play. Sumika uses a handheld microphone, dark formal costume, closed or lowered eyes, chest-level gestures, and controlled stillness. The performance does not prove that quietness is the true Sumika. It proves she can inhabit stillness without being annihilated by it.

> **AV finding:** `カクシタワタシ` is not the unmasking of the real girl. It is the construction of a protected temporal space in which the usually mobile self can stop evading exposure.

## 2.3 Mask: from defense to artistic technology

`Tame-Lie-One-Step`'s authored MV visualizes public identity as an interface. Cubes, profile frames, cosmetics, digital streets, multiple scales of Sumika, hearts, chibi forms, and self-images create a world in which personality is assembled and circulated. This is not framed simply as corruption. The interface is playful, attractive, and productive.

The MV's strongest contribution is to make “mask” less like a solid object covering a hidden essence and more like a **set of selectable surfaces**. Sumika can move among stylized versions of herself without any single one exhausting the person.

`Love & Joy` takes that model further through the phone/camera/social-post interface. The MV includes recording, playback, gallery selection, posts, comments, portrait fragments, and repeated acts of reaching. The self is not merely displayed. It is made, reviewed, sent, answered, and returned.

This produces the mature formulation:

> **Sumika's mask is not a prison she escapes. It becomes a tool whose authorship, audience, and purpose she increasingly governs.**

## 2.4 Expectation: from verdict to chosen public promise

The visual field of Sumika's performances is consistently social. Audience lights matter. The stage is not an empty private therapy room. `Tame-Lie-One-Step` makes the crowd an inviting field; `カクシタワタシ` makes the crowd witness a carefully bounded private register; `Love & Joy` turns direct address into an attempt to move someone else.

The relationship to expectation changes accordingly:

- early expectation is anticipatory judgment;
- middle expectation is something managed through a strong-idol image;
- fan expectation recreates the danger of overfitting;
- self-authored performance lets Sumika decide what promise she is making;
- public testimony turns autobiography into vocational address.

The difference between coercive expectation and chosen promise is not that one has an audience and the other does not. Both do. The difference is who controls the terms of answerability.

## 2.5 Relationship: support must produce differentiation

The packet repeatedly tests whether closeness produces capacity or enclosure.

With the Producer, Sumika's casual `Pっち` relation creates low-friction intimacy, but the song commus show a more serious professional movement: she begins to state preferences the expert cannot infer for her. `Love & Joy` is decisive because the Producer's productive act is partly to step back. Sumika initially experiences that as distance, precisely because his guidance has become emotionally central.

With Lilja, REVERSI is the strongest audiovisual correction to fusion language. The 3DMV gives equal focality, alternate solos, symmetrical staging, mirrored gestures, hand contact, back-to-back separation, passing positions, and side-by-side audience address. They are linked, but they do not occupy one body or one point of view.

> **AV finding:** mature REVERSI is not inseparability. It is synchronized differentiation.

# 3. Dear and acted-story trajectory

## 3.1 Dear 001–010 — the body can play before it can safely be judged

The first packet establishes Sumika's breadth before it asks for depth. She can be relaxed, funny, fashionable, socially quick, and physically animated. The same packet also produces moments of reduced gaze, guarded posture, and emotional slowdown when ballet, injury, Lilja, or weakness become explicit.

The strongest early acting distinction is therefore not cheerful versus sad. It is **self-moving versus evaluatively fixed**. Sumika is comfortable when she controls the encounter's tempo. She is more vulnerable when another person's belief seems to freeze her into one expected identity.

Dear 008's chosen-mask logic is audiovisual as well as textual. The route does not ask her to abandon performance. It gives performance an explicit purpose: acknowledge weakness, then practice a stronger public form with full knowledge that it is aspirational.

Dear 010's ballet farewell should not be read as the return of the authentic dancer. Its force lies in completing an unfinished relationship to the past before selecting idolhood.

## 3.2 Dear 011–020 — popularity makes the social gift dangerous

The second packet places Sumika's easy sociality under public pressure. Friends, fans, rivals, and institutional opportunities expand the number of people who can interpret her. The same fluency that makes her professionally accessible can no longer guarantee belonging.

Lilja scenes matter because the bond's emotional asymmetry becomes visible. Sumika's bright protective role cannot survive if it requires hiding the frightened person. The audiovisual arc supports the textual revision: the claim that Lilja loves only the old prodigy is Sumika's fear, not a settled truth about Lilja.

The N.I.A.-era culmination does not make public support uniformly safe. It demonstrates that expectation can become lift when Sumika chooses the relation, while preserving the possibility of relapse.

## 3.3 Dear 021–027 — technique becomes explicit, then threatens to become another verdict

The third packet is the most important body/voice bridge in the directly inspected Dear material. Sumika confronts dance-specific defeat, bodily imbalance, training monotony, and the need to verbalize technique for Lilja. The former prodigy's embodied knowledge becomes transmissible craft.

That change matters because tacit brilliance can feel like fate. Teaching requires decomposition, patience, and relation. It converts “I was talented” into “I know something I can choose to give.”

The same tranche then restores the expectation trap at a higher level. Fans and experts have more reason to expect elite dance. Sumika can technically satisfy them and still disappear inside their ideal. The route's answer is not lower standards. It is the requirement that the ideal Sumika be authored from inside.

## 3.4 Dear 028–037 — direct late-route backfill: vulnerability can remain, then brightness can return

The directly uploaded 720p30 Dear 028–037 compilation closes the only material acted-story gap in the original release. The backfill does not overturn the textual model. It makes the late mechanism much sharper:

> **Late Sumika's strongest performed achievement is reversible permeability between registers. She can enter fear, testimony, and intimacy without losing the gyaru self, and she can return to brightness without using brightness to invalidate what was disclosed.**

A more diagnostic formulation is:

> **The mature distinction is not brightness versus seriousness. It is whether brightness arrives before or after vulnerability has been allowed to count.**

### Dear 032 — confession becomes testimony, then encouragement

The public-history scene is not performed as one uninterrupted inspirational speech. Sumika begins with controlled autobiography, but the body tightens as she approaches the part she had hidden. When she names the knee trauma, fear of pain, nausea around dance lessons, and the weight of Lilja's belief, her posture is more contained and her gaze repeatedly lowers. Lilja's physical arrival beside her matters: public disclosure is **co-regulated**, not staged as autonomous heroic self-extraction.

After that fracture has been allowed to remain visible, Sumika reopens. On `あたし、強くなったんだよ！` and the promise that her dance will push forward people whose hearts have broken, the body becomes more audience-facing and expansive. The fans answer; Sumika becomes visibly tearful again; only then does she restore the familiar social current with humor about ending `告白タイム` and waiting for the tears to dry.

**AV finding:** the humor is not an eraser. Early defensive brightness often arrives quickly enough to keep exposure from settling. Here brightness returns **after** fear, relational dependence, and tears have already been publicly acknowledged. The same gyaru competence has changed function from evacuation to social repair.

### Dear 034 — courage is borrowed through chosen contact

Sumika opens in her ordinary bright register, then explicitly marks the transition: `今から本音を言うね。` The visual performance narrows with the admission that she could not sleep because she was afraid. Closed or lowered eyes, inward hand placement, reduced gestural scale, and the challenge `こんなに怖がってるのに？` keep fear present rather than immediately converting it into banter.

Producer's answer does not magically produce fearlessness. Sumika asks for his hand `ちょっとだけ`, physically reaches toward him, receives the contact, and only gradually reopens into a relieved smile and renewed forward orientation.

**AV finding:** courage is relationally embodied. Sumika can ask for temporary dependence without treating dependence as proof that she failed to change. The sequence is **fear → chosen support → renewed agency**, not fear → denial.

### Dear 036 — Prima Stella projection integrates rather than deletes the frightened self

The victory begins with shock rather than instant sovereign confidence. Sumika needs Lilja's confirmation, thanks her for dancing together, receives Sena's statement that Sumika's dance gave even Sena courage, and enters the title through relation before public projection.

Her Prima Stella speech then starts with a small hesitation — `ええっと` — and immediately becomes recognizably Sumika: `紫雲清夏で～す！`. She does not adopt a distant ceremonial prestige register. Autobiographical memory is accompanied by hand-to-chest containment; the later `ねぇみんな！ あたし、強くなったよ！` and `キミの背中は、あたしが押してあげるからね！` expand into direct, casual, high-projection address.

**AV finding:** the summit does not restore the old fantasy of an invulnerable strong Sumika. It demonstrates that fear, support, colloquial intimacy, technical legitimacy, and public authority can occupy one performance system.

### Dear 037 — intimacy requires a temporary role boundary before reciprocity

The first half with Lilja quietly confirms that fulfillment of the childhood promise no longer requires identical futures. Their closeness survives discussion of separate ideals; REVERSI differentiation therefore persists after the competitive climax.

With Producer, familiar `Pっち` teasing and gratitude gradually narrow into a highly consequential request: `今からちょっとの間だけ、あなたはプロデューサーじゃない。` Sumika does not simply collapse professional and personal roles. She **names the institutional role, temporarily suspends it, and asks for the person's own feelings**. During Producer's disclosure of insecurity and thoughts of giving up, Sumika becomes notably still rather than filling the space.

When he says that her lives gave him courage, the result becomes vocational proof from the person whose belief once threatened to define her. She absorbs the statement, then brightness re-expands into future desire and the final request that he continue supporting and making her happy.

**AV finding:** the dependency question remains open, but the scene strengthens evidence of **role-conscious boundary management**. Emotional indispensability and professional asymmetry coexist with an explicit attempt to separate role-authored speech from personal reciprocity.

### Late-route acoustic control

Source-level mixed-scene probes broadly track the visible expansion: private fear/trauma segments yield lower pitch/projection proxies than several public-promise, Prima Stella, humor-restoration, and future-facing segments. Because the file includes BGM, other speakers, effects, and edits, these are **SOURCE-METRIC** corroboration only. They do not establish pure vocal F0, timbre, breathiness, or isolated dynamics.

**Late-route synthesis:** Dear 032–037 turns elastic social brightness into **reversible register permeability**. Sumika can remain still long enough for vulnerability to become socially real, accept another person's support, and then choose brightness as renewed relation or public direction.

# 4. Voice-performance model — elastic social brightness

Sumika's voice is best modeled as **elastic social brightness**.

“Bright” alone is insufficient. The same broadly casual register can perform several different jobs:

- genuine enjoyment;
- social invitation;
- comic pressure release;
- avoidance of evaluative stillness;
- affectionate teasing;
- technical command;
- self-protective smoothing;
- direct emotional request.

The elasticity lies in how quickly Sumika can change the purpose of casualness without changing into a different character. Mature seriousness does not require linguistic purification.

Important states:

### Ordinary gyaru enjoyment
Fast social entry, low ceremonial distance, expressive face and body, pleasure in fashion, outings, jokes, and new people. `Kira Kira`, common repertoire, and low-stakes commus are essential controls proving this state is genuine.

### Defensive brightness
The interaction keeps moving immediately after exposure. Humor or teasing prevents fear from occupying the entire scene. The defense is often temporal and relational rather than a simple pitch shift.

### Vulnerable stillness
Reduced motion, longer holds, lowered or closed gaze, more concern about how the listener will interpret the admission. `カクシタワタシ` provides the clearest performance analogue.

### Technical/dance command
Instruction becomes cleaner and more directional. The former prodigy's authority appears as actionable knowledge rather than glamorous aura. Dear 021–027 and `Love & Joy` development are the main controls.

### Producer-directed intimacy
`Pっち` can carry teasing, professional trust, reliance, testing, and romance-coded attachment. The stable nickname does not imply one stable emotional state. The song commus show a movement from being interpreted by Producer toward telling him what only she can know.

### Lilja-directed register
Protective brightness becomes accusation and hurt, then coaching, then equal-rival reciprocity. REVERSI is the strongest audiovisual evidence because vocal and spatial alternation prevent one-sided guardianship from defining the mature bond.

### Public address
Friendly accessibility becomes testimony and encouragement. The mature public register is not generic inspirational command; it is Sumika's ordinary social reach made accountable to a chosen promise.

The specialist voice document develops these states through the directly inspected Dear 001–037 spine and the song-commu controls.

# 5. Principal musical/performance sequence

## 5.1 `Tame-Lie-One-Step` — movement before certainty

### Song-commu function

The song begins from hesitation rather than its absence. Sumika chooses an approach that combines brightness with gentleness. Courage is not maximum energy; it is making the next step emotionally reachable.

### Authored MV function

The MV constructs a digital, fashion-oriented identity laboratory. Cubes, social-profile imagery, makeup, pixel space, multiple scales, chibi movement, hearts, and self-framing make public identity visibly compositional.

The crucial point is that compositionality is not treated as moral falsity. Sumika is active inside it.

### 3DMV function

The dark arena and wide audience field return the conceptual interface to a social body. Open hands, forward reach, broad crowd address, and shifts between playful and more concentrated closeups make brightness a means of decreasing the distance to the audience.

### Finding

> **`Tame-Lie-One-Step` makes performed brightness a bridge across hesitation.**

It is not yet the full self-authored synthesis. Producer and song structure still supply much of the route. But Sumika is already more than a passive image.

## 5.2 `カクシタワタシ` — protected stillness

### Song-commu function

The song is built from ballet, injury, Lilja, fear, and the hidden self, but the production rule is that Sumika must want to sing it. She materially participates in lyric formation. Exposure is therefore bounded by authorship.

### Full released song

The static presentation supplies the released full mix and complete song duration. It is an audio control, not a moving-image interpretation.

### 3DMV function

The 3DMV reduces movement, brightness, and costume informality. Sumika stands in a dark formal costume with handheld microphone. Lowered gaze, closed eyes, chest-level gestures, isolated side light, and limited locomotion create a protected confession space.

This does not reveal the “real serious Sumika.” It expands the range of forms in which the existing person can be received.

### Finding

> **`カクシタワタシ` lets Sumika stop moving without surrendering authorship.**

The song is developmental because weakness can first be communicated through a protected aesthetic form before it becomes public testimony in ordinary speech.

## 5.3 `Love & Joy` — authored momentum

### Song-commu function

The governing line is:

> `あたしが伝えたいことは、あたししかわからない`

Sumika claims choreography, styling, ballet-derived motion, presentation, hair, fanservice, and stage expression as domains in which expert assistance cannot substitute for her knowledge of what she means.

### Authored MV function

The vertical-phone interface depicts recording, playback, gallery selection, posts, comments, portrait fragments, mask language, monochrome reaching hands, and a final live image. The self is produced through mediation, but mediation is reciprocal: an image is sent into a social field and answered.

### 3DMV function

The performance translates that mediated authorship into bodily direction. Extended arms, diagonals, turns, balanced full-body shapes, direct gaze, and the neon heart-circle stage make movement both stylish and addressive. Ballet-derived line is not displayed as museum evidence of a former career. It has been metabolized into public idol motion.

### Finding

> **`Love & Joy` is the AV hinge where play becomes craft, craft becomes self-authored movement, and movement becomes a promise to push someone else's back.**

It strengthens the textual claim that weakness is not merely disclosed; it is transformed into usable public form.

# 6. REVERSI / `ときめきエモーション` — synchronized differentiation

The song commu establishes REVERSI as a project built from Sumika and Lilja's real history. The official MV abstracts that relation into paired forms, repeated reversals, divided circles, blurred identities, silhouettes, and language of repetition and promise. It should not be treated as literal route footage.

The 3DMV supplies the strongest relational evidence:

- equal focality rather than one persistent center;
- alternating solo address;
- symmetrical and complementary spacing;
- mirrored gestures without identical characterization;
- hand contact followed by separation;
- back-to-back orientation;
- passing and rotating positions;
- simultaneous audience address;
- final closeness that preserves two faces and two vocal bodies.

This directly answers the main hypothesis.

> **REVERSI stages mirroring, intimacy, rivalry, and differentiation as changing combinations. It does not resolve them into fusion.**

Sumika's maturity is partly the ability to be beside Lilja without remaining only the strong friend Lilja follows. Lilja's maturity is partly the ability to be supported without existing only as the person Sumika must protect.

The mature unit is therefore best understood as a **bilateral engine of courage** whose members remain independently evaluable.

# 7. Common and breadth controls

## 7.1 `Campus mode!!`

The daylight school setting, shared institutional grammar, buoyant movement, and direct audience address test whether Sumika can inhabit Hatsuboshi's collective identity without needing every source to retell her injury. She can.

Her portability lies in social ease, fashion-conscious movement, and quick crowd relation—not in turning the common song into autobiography.

## 7.2 `Kira Kira`

The official MV's collage/paper-doll aesthetic, dressing room, clothing, shopping, leisure, sports/mobility, and handmade construction provide the strongest nontrauma control. Sumika's interest in appearance and ordinary fun is not merely a defense. It is part of the life idolhood is meant to preserve.

## 7.3 `がむしゃらに行こう！`

The outdoor school-stage presentation, tracksuit styling, props, and comic physicality show effort rhetoric entering a playful register. This is useful precisely because Sumika's route rejects the moral equation “serious effort alone proves worth.” She can inhabit an effort song without pretending that grind is her only truth.

## 7.4 `Howling over the World`

The dark red/black arena, sharp lines, forward pointing, and aggressive attack test force. Sumika can project intensity without making aggression her stable center. The common choreography carries much of the demand, while her identity remains legible through precision and frontal address.

## 7.5 `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！`

The bright cyber-pop environment provides a comic/cute elasticity test. It prevents the protected seriousness of `カクシタワタシ` from being misread as the only artistically mature state.

# 8. Recurrent audiovisual motifs

## 8.1 Hands and reach

Sumika's MVs and 3DMVs repeatedly use hands as a relation technology:

- reach toward the audience;
- frame the face;
- touch the chest;
- hold a microphone;
- clasp Lilja;
- extend along diagonals;
- answer an unseen hand in `Love & Joy`.

The body does not merely prove technical recovery. It establishes direction between self and other.

## 8.2 Screens, profiles, cameras, and posts

`Tame-Lie-One-Step` and `Love & Joy` are both deeply mediated. Profile cards, cosmetics, cubes, phone cameras, galleries, posts, comments, and playback turn identity into something revisable and distributable.

For Sumika, mediation is ethically ambivalent. It can let others write the ideal. It can also let her edit, frame, and circulate the ideal she chose.

## 8.3 Crowd-light fields

The crowd is often represented as a colored field before it becomes individual people. This mirrors the structure of expectation: public belief begins as scale and pressure. Mature performance makes that field relational by choosing what is being addressed to it.

## 8.4 Darkness and protected disclosure

`カクシタワタシ` uses darkness differently from aggressive common repertoire. The dark stage is not threat or domination; it reduces social noise and makes small expression visible.

## 8.5 Symmetry without identity collapse

REVERSI repeatedly uses balance, alternation, and mirroring. The important formal choice is that symmetry does not require sameness. The pair can be visually coherent while retaining distinct costumes, faces, vocal entries, and trajectories.

# 9. Relationship consequences

## 9.1 Sumika and Producer

The AV pass strengthens the relation as **witness → coauthor → selectively relinquishing guide → emotionally central partner**.

The positive achievement is not that Producer perfectly understands Sumika. It is that the `Love & Joy` process makes misunderstanding productive: he cannot know what she wants to communicate, so he returns authorship.

The open ethical problem remains. The person who made weakness survivable becomes someone Sumika says is necessary to her happiness. Emotional reciprocity does not eliminate professional authority or the risk of enclosure.

## 9.2 Sumika and Lilja

The AV pass strongly revises any account in which Sumika's role is permanently protective or Lilja's role permanently receptive. Coaching and support remain, but REVERSI's form requires alternation, independent address, and competitive readability.

> **Current formulation:** best friends become companions capable of producing courage for one another, then rivals capable of wanting separately without treating separation as betrayal.

## 9.3 Sumika and fans

Fans initially recreate the expectation trap because their admiration can turn one visible capacity into a total identity. Mature Sumika does not solve this by refusing audience desire. She chooses a public promise whose terms she can endorse.

The risk remains that the “idol who gives courage” brand could become another compulsory ideal. Phase 6–8 synthesis should test how often Sumika is permitted to be supported rather than inspiring.

## 9.4 Sumika and ballet

Ballet becomes technical ancestry, not romantic destiny. The audiovisual evidence makes this more precise than the text alone: line, balance, extension, teaching, and spatial confidence survive, but the stage relation has changed from adjudicated form to audience-facing authorship.

# 11. Open questions

1. **Sustainable body management:** the packet establishes repurposed capacity, not a medical claim that chronic risk is solved.

# 12. Final integrated formulation

> **Sumika's audiovisual development is not a journey from play to seriousness. It is a journey from play used partly to stay unevaluable toward play made technically and morally answerable. `Tame-Lie-One-Step` turns brightness into movement; `カクシタワタシ` gives stillness a protected form; `Love & Joy` makes the recovered body an instrument of self-authored public direction; REVERSI proves that relational courage can be synchronized without becoming fused; Dear 032–037 proves that the resulting self can move back and forth between vulnerability and brightness without either state invalidating the other. Ballet remains in the line of the body, gyaru pleasure remains in the social tempo, weakness remains in the history, and the mask remains in the art. What changes is who authors their relation.**

The mature Sumika does not ask the audience to believe she was never frightened. Nor does she need brightness to arrive quickly enough to make the fear disappear.

She can let fear count, receive another person's hand or presence, and then choose where the returning movement points. That is the late form of **reversible register permeability**.
