## 3. Measurement discipline

- Loudness, range, true peak, bitrate, and duration are source-level measurements.
- 3DMV captures and official released masters use different mastering levels; raw LUFS comparisons do not measure emotion or ability.
- Dialogue sources contain other voices, BGM, effects, and transitions.
- Contact sheets and sampled-frame comparisons are locators, not substitutes for full-motion inspection.
- Motion quantity is not dance quality.
- No metric determines authenticity, courage, charisma, recovery, or moral health.
