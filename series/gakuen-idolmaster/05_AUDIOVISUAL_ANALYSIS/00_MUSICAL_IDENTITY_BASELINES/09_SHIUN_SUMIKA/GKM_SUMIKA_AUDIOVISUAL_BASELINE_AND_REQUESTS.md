## 1. Purpose

This register converts the Phase-3 Sumika textual reading into a human-searchable AV acquisition plan. It does **not** assert timbre, acting, arrangement, choreography, or MV meaning before direct inspection.

The main hypotheses to test are:

1. genuine gyaru brightness vs defensive brightness;
2. the developmental sequence `Tame-Lie-One-Step → カクシタワタシ → Love & Joy`;
3. the persistence/transformation of ballet line inside idol choreography;
4. weak-self/private-fear register vs public strong-idol register;
5. whether public trauma disclosure sounds like confession, command, encouragement, or a changing mixture;
6. how fear becomes courage in Dear 034–036;
7. how REVERSI stages connection vs rivalry/differentiation;
8. Producer-directed teasing, reliance, romance, and professional trust as potentially distinct voice states.

Whole commus/full videos are preferred. The user should **not** cut clips manually unless a source is otherwise unobtainable.
