# SHIUN SUMIKA — MUSIC, MV, AND PERFORMANCE CLOSE READING

## 0. Method and source-form correction

The pass separates:

1. **song commu** — story authorship, intended meaning, relationship, and production context;
2. **3DMV** — rendered body, choreography, facial performance, virtual camera, and audience relation;
3. **authored official MV** — official symbolic/editorial interpretation;
4. **static full-mix presentation** — complete released audio without moving-image authorship.

`カクシタワタシ` is represented by a song commu, a 3DMV, and a static full released song presentation. It does **not** have an authored official MV in the current corpus. The analysis does not invent one.

Source-level loudness and dynamic measurements are used only to distinguish delivery files and mastering contexts. The released official sources are substantially hotter than game captures; this is a source/mastering fact, not evidence that one Sumika state is more emotional or skilled.

## 1. Musical thesis — movement is a grammar of authorship

Sumika's music is not organized around one genre or one fixed emotional color. Its coherent feature is the changing relation among motion, exposure, and address.

> **Her principal sequence turns movement from avoidance into authorship.**

- `Tame-Lie-One-Step` treats brightness as a way to make the next step possible.
- `カクシタワタシ` suspends movement so the hidden self can be heard safely.
- `Love & Joy` returns to motion after Sumika claims what only she can know about the intended meaning.
- `ときめきエモーション` distributes movement across two independently legible people.

The repertoire therefore supports **kinetic self-authorship**, not a simple escalation from quiet to loud or weak to strong.

## 2. `Tame-Lie-One-Step`

### Song-commu function

The foundational song links uncertainty and motion. Sumika wants an upbeat, danceable surface, but she and Producer retain the hesitation in the lyrics. Her intended delivery combines brightness with gentleness.

### Authored MV

The visual world is digital, fashion-oriented, and modular:

- a labeled cube;
- cosmetics and profile frames;
- social-image fragments;
- chibi and full-size Sumika;
- pixel streets and repeated geometric objects;
- hearts and face/hand framing.

Identity is something assembled and moved through. The MV does not reveal a hidden essence behind the interface. It gives Sumika agency inside the interface.

### 3DMV

The arena version emphasizes audience relation. Open-hand reach, direct gaze, broad stage motion, and the field of penlights turn the “one step” into a social invitation.

### Finding

> **The song's strength is not confidence achieved before movement. It is movement engineered so uncertainty can travel with it.**

## 3. `カクシタワタシ`

### Song-commu function

The production draws on ballet, injury, Lilja, and hidden fear. Sumika worries that the song may contradict the strong public fantasy, but participates materially in the lyric process so the result is something she wants to sing.

### Full released song

The static presentation provides the complete released recording. It is the cleanest full-mix control in the packet for this work.

### 3DMV

The performance is built around controlled reduction:

- dark indigo/black stage;
- black/silver formal costume;
- handheld microphone;
- limited locomotion;
- lowered gaze and closed eyes;
- chest-level and face-level microgesture;
- isolated light;
- an audience that witnesses rather than being constantly energized.

The body is not absent. It is governed at a smaller scale.

### Finding

> **`カクシタワタシ` makes stillness a protected form of agency.**

It should not be interpreted as proof that serious quietness is more authentic than gyaru brightness. It is the first work in which Sumika can remain with weakness without immediately converting it into social momentum.

## 4. `Love & Joy`

### Song-commu function

The commu announces a production-method change. The current theme is `弱さを強さに変える曲`, and Sumika claims choreography, styling, ballet-derived movement, hair, fanservice, and stage expression because `あたしが伝えたいことは、あたししかわからない`.

### Authored MV

The vertical-phone visual language includes:

- camera and video interfaces;
- gallery selection;
- posts and comments;
- portrait fragments;
- repeated 11:11 timestamps;
- the word/image of `仮面`;
- monochrome hands reaching;
- full-color Sumika images;
- a final live-performance register.

The self is recorded, selected, posted, answered, and re-entered. Mediation is not merely alienation; it becomes a circuit of authored relation.

### 3DMV

The performance uses a fashionable black/mint/pink look and neon circular/heart space. Important bodily features include:

- open diagonals;
- extended arms;
- balanced turns;
- full-body poses readable from distance;
- direct eye-line to camera/audience;
- chest and hand motifs carried outward;
- crowd-facing rather than inwardly absorbed movement.

### Ballet inheritance

The choreography should not be called ballet. Its significance is that ballet-derived line and spatial control become available within idol address. Sumika does not return to an adjudicated dance identity; she reuses embodied knowledge inside a performance she helped author.

### Finding

> **`Love & Joy` is authored momentum: weakness is not hidden or merely confessed but converted into motion intended to affect another person.**

## 5. `ときめきエモーション` / REVERSI

### Song-commu function

The project makes Sumika and Lilja's real history part of public meaning. That introduces an ethical question: intimacy is both relationship and production capital.

### Authored MV

The MV abstracts the pair through divided and recombined circular forms, repeated reversal, blurred identities, silhouettes, typographic fragmentation, and promise language. It should not be used as literal story chronology.

### 3DMV

The rendered performance is the strongest source for bilateral structure:

- equal solo opportunities;
- alternating focality;
- symmetric and asymmetric spacing;
- mirrored gestures;
- hand contact;
- back-to-back separation;
- positional exchange;
- pair address and individual address;
- final closeness with both performers independently visible.

### Finding

> **REVERSI is a choreography of synchronized differentiation.**

Connection does not require fusion. Rivalry does not require relational rupture. Sumika's body can support Lilja and still be evaluated separately; Lilja can stand beside Sumika without existing only as the recipient of Sumika's courage.

## 6. `Campus mode!!`

The common institutional song is a critical portability test. The daylight school exterior, red/white/blue costume, buoyant motion, and communal address do not organize themselves around Sumika's injury.

Sumika remains legible through:

- social ease;
- fashion-conscious presentation;
- quick crowd relation;
- confident full-body orientation;
- pleasure in shared school identity.

> **The performance proves that Sumika's mature identity is portable beyond autobiographical custom songs.**

## 7. `Kira Kira`

The official MV's cutout/collage aesthetic contains dressing, shopping, city life, leisure, sports/mobility, and handmade visual assembly.

Its analytic value is corrective:

> **ordinary pleasure is not an immature state the serious songs supersede.**

Fashion and social play are part of the life Sumika is trying to preserve through idolhood. They are also artistic materials, not distractions from art.

## 8. `がむしゃらに行こう！`

The tracksuit, outdoor school stage, props, and comic physicality make effort playful. This creates productive friction with Sumika's history: she knows that effort can become moralized and coercive, yet she can inhabit effort rhetoric without surrendering to it.

The performance reads as energetic participation rather than confessional autobiography.

## 9. `Howling over the World`

The dark red/black stage and sharp aggressive choreography test attack, force, and forward pressure.

Sumika can occupy the register, but the source does not establish aggression as her artistic center. The common song supplies much of the force demand. Her portable identity lies in precise frontal relation and the ability to style intensity without losing social readability.

## 10. `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！`

This is the strongest digital-comic/cute range control. The bright cyber-pop space prevents the principal trilogy from becoming a one-way movement toward solemnity.

Sumika's mature breadth includes absurdity and fun. The ability to return to playful spectacle after autobiographical seriousness is evidence of integration, not regression.

## 12. Formal-development model

The principal sequence is best represented as:

> **`Tame-Lie-One-Step` — authored brightness makes a step possible**  
> **`カクシタワタシ` — authored stillness lets weakness remain audible**  
> **`Love & Joy` — authored movement turns weakness into outward force**  
> **`ときめきエモーション` — outward force becomes bilateral and differentiating**

`Kira Kira` proves ordinary joy remains intrinsic. `Campus mode!!` proves the identity is institutionally portable. The forceful/comic common songs demonstrate breadth without requiring each source to carry the full autobiographical thesis.

## 13. Final musical formulation

> **Sumika's catalog does not advance from a superficial bright song toward increasingly serious truth. It expands the number of truthful forms available to the same person. Brightness can invite; darkness can protect; choreography can author; a duet can differentiate; common repertoire can preserve ordinary fun. The decisive artistic change is not mood. It is control over the relation among body, image, audience, and expectation.**
