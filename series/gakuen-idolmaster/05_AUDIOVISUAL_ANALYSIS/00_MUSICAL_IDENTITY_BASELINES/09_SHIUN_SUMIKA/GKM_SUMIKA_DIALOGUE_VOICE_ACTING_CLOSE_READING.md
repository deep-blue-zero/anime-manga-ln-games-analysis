# SHIUN SUMIKA — DIALOGUE AND VOICE-ACTING CLOSE READING

## 0. Scope and measurement limits

This specialist document isolates acted rhythm, pause, facial/physical performance, speech-function changes, and relation-specific register. It does not treat mixed-scene loudness or pitch as a pure measurement of Minato Miya's voice. Dialogue compilations contain other speakers, BGM, interface sounds, and hard edits. Dear 028–037 is now directly inspected through an equivalent 720p30 direct-upload analysis materialization, closing the former late-route acted-source gap while preserving mixed-source acoustic caution.

## 1. Voice thesis — elastic social brightness

Sumika's voice is best modeled as **elastic social brightness**.

The stable trait is not constant cheerfulness. It is the capacity to lower social friction quickly. She enters encounters casually, teases, names the Producer `Pっち`, uses colloquial contractions, and often creates the impression that emotional traffic can keep moving.

That same capacity serves different purposes:

- pleasure;
- hospitality;
- deflection;
- flirtation;
- embarrassment management;
- peer equalization;
- technical instruction;
- refusal of solemn hierarchy;
- preservation of distance from judgment.

The key diagnostic question is therefore not “does she sound bright?” It is:

> **What is brightness doing in this moment?**

## 2. Genuine gyaru enjoyment

Ordinary commus, low-stakes outings, styling scenes, and breadth works establish an authentic pleasure register. Sumika is not merely performing sociability because she fears stillness. She likes the world of fashion, shopping, games, entertainment, casual food, and rapid new-friend contact.

This state is important as a control. Without it, every laugh could be overinterpreted as trauma defense.

Performed tendencies:

- quick entry into conversation;
- direct casual address;
- teasing that does not require hostility;
- face and posture open toward the other person;
- fast recovery after minor awkwardness;
- pleasure in making the interaction feel less formal.

## 3. Defensive brightness — continuity as protection

When the stakes rise, brightness can become **forced continuity**. The scene threatens to stop on fear, shame, jealousy, dependence, or disappointment; Sumika keeps it moving through a joke, a lightly dismissive phrase, or an immediate tonal softening after directness.

The defense is not necessarily louder or higher. Its most reliable feature is temporal:

> do not let the admission remain exposed for long.

This explains why the same gyaru diction can carry genuine enjoyment in one scene and self-protection in another. The text controls the stakes; the acting controls how long the stakes are allowed to remain visible.

## 4. Vulnerable stillness

The most exposed Sumika often reduces rather than abandons her established voice. The acting can narrow through:

- lowered gaze;
- closed eyes;
- more stationary posture;
- fewer large gestures;
- a phrase allowed to end without an immediate joke;
- concern about the listener's interpretation;
- slower visual recovery after disclosure.

`カクシタワタシ` provides the clearest performance analogue. Its handheld microphone and limited movement create a voice-space in which vulnerability can be held rather than instantly converted back into social momentum.

The important conclusion is:

> **quiet Sumika is not the true Sumika replacing bright Sumika. It is an expanded capacity to remain present when brightness cannot safely solve the scene.**

## 5. Dear 001–010 — choosing the strong voice consciously

The early Dear material shows that Sumika can already perform confidence. The problem is not technical inability to sound strong; it is the cost of letting strength become an expectation she must always satisfy.

Dear 007–008 clarify the distinction between concealment and authored performance. Sumika does not want Producer to see the weak self, then consciously chooses the `強いあたしの仮面` after weakness has been acknowledged.

Voice significance:

- the strong register ceases to be an unconscious reflex;
- the Producer becomes a witness who knows the gap between current state and aspirational form;
- performance becomes a practice shared with someone rather than a solitary lie.

The ballet farewell in Dear 010 is not vocally important because it returns an old prodigy voice. It is important because Sumika can speak from a present that has chosen what the past will mean.

## 6. Dear 011–020 — popularity fractures effortless sociability

Rising visibility makes Sumika's social gift less reliable as protection. Friends can resent or misread her; Shion can weaponize recognition and isolation; Lilja can refuse the version of friendship that depends on concealment.

With Lilja, the register becomes more discontinuous:

- protective ease;
- defensive accusation;
- hurt;
- shame about being seen;
- eventual permission to rely.

The key revision is that Sumika's claim “Lilja believes only in the old prodigy” is not treated as objective truth. Lilja's acted presence interrupts the projection.

The N.I.A. movement broadens public address, but does not establish a permanent post-trauma voice. It establishes that support can sometimes be experienced as lift.

## 7. Dear 021–027 — technical command

This tranche introduces a distinct professional state. Sumika's former ballet knowledge becomes explicit when she teaches, diagnoses, and rebuilds.

The technical register is not simply harsher. Its distinguishing property is **directional clarity**:

- less playful ambiguity;
- more concrete instruction;
- visible confidence about bodies and movement;
- willingness to correct another person;
- separation of technical problem from global personal worth.

This is a major developmental gain. Sumika can use the expertise that once defined and trapped her without requiring that expertise to define the whole self.

The same tranche makes fan expectation dangerous again. Voice and posture can remain professionally effective while preference is being suppressed. Competence therefore cannot be treated as proof of authorship.

## 8. Song-commu registers

### 8.1 `Tame-Lie-One-Step`

Sumika discusses performance in a socially available way. The early song relation is collaborative, but Producer interpretation remains comparatively strong. Brightness and gentleness are both deliberate; she does not equate courage with overwhelming force.

### 8.2 `カクシタワタシ`

The commu allows more sustained self-reference. Sumika fears that autobiographical weakness may violate the public fantasy, then participates in making the song something she wants to sing. Her concern is not simply exposure. It is exposure without authorship.

### 8.3 `Love & Joy`

The voice becomes more professionally assertive. The crucial shift is from answering Producer's interpretation to supplying knowledge he cannot possess:

> `あたしが伝えたいことは、あたししかわからない`

This is not anti-collaboration. It is collaboration after domain boundaries become clearer.

### 8.4 `ときめきエモーション`

Sumika and Lilja's relational history enters a public unit framework. The commu moves among intimacy, memory, work, image construction, and competitive ambition. Sumika's voice must no longer stabilize Lilja from above; the unit depends on alternation and mutual challenge.

### 8.5 `Campus mode!!`

Institutional repertoire provides a register control. Sumika can speak about shared Hatsuboshi identity without making every source a trauma confession.

## 9. Producer register

`Pっち` is not one emotional state. It is a stable address form through which several subregisters travel:

- **teasing familiarity** — reduces hierarchy in felt interaction;
- **evaluative testing** — asks whether he understands without stating the whole need;
- **vulnerable request** — asks to be seen beyond the strong surface;
- **professional coauthorship** — negotiates song, choreography, styling, and stage meaning;
- **dependency disclosure** — says his presence is tied to happiness;
- **romance-coded pressure** — turns sincerity into a moment that destabilizes ordinary professional language.

The AV pass strengthens emotional reciprocity. It does not establish institutional equality. The Producer remains able to define opportunities, withdraw guidance, and structure risk.

## 10. Lilja register

The Sumika↔Lilja voice relation evolves through four states:

1. **protective brightness** — Sumika opens the world and manages difficulty;
2. **expectation conflict** — Lilja's belief becomes heavy, concealment breaks equality;
3. **technical reciprocity** — Sumika teaches while Lilja becomes someone she can rely on;
4. **companion-rival** — REVERSI permits separate wanting and alternate focality.

The mature relation is not proved by tenderness alone. It is proved by the capacity to remain connected while saying different things, occupying different positions, and competing.

## 11. Late Dear 032–037 — reversible register permeability

The direct late-route pass confirms and sharpens the earlier model. Sumika's maturation is not a one-way progression into solemnity. It is an increased ability to move **both directions** across emotional registers without using one to cancel the other.

> **The mature difference is not brightness versus seriousness. It is whether brightness arrives before or after vulnerability has been allowed to count.**

### 11.1 Dear 032 — public testimony

The performed sequence is **controlled autobiography → visible fracture → Lilja co-regulation → explicit trauma naming → reopening → public promise → audience return → tears → humor-restored continuity**. Sumika's familiar brightness returns only after the confession has been socially witnessed. That makes the final joking qualitatively different from early avoidance: it repairs the social field rather than preventing vulnerability from becoming real.

Her public voice therefore becomes more than friendliness. It can turn autobiography toward another person's future while still admitting the cost that produced the message.

### 11.2 Dear 034 — private fear and chosen reliance

`今から本音を言うね` functions as an explicit register marker. On `昨日は、怖くて眠れなかった` and `こんなに怖がってるのに？`, the performance narrows through lowered/closed gaze, inward posture, and reduced gestural scale. Sumika does not immediately smooth the moment over.

Her request for Producer's hand is the decisive acted event. Courage is not heard as self-sufficient confidence; it is assembled through contact she asks for. The smile and ordinary brightness return gradually after the support is received.

### 11.3 Dear 036 — Prima Stella public projection

The title first produces shock and tearful relational acknowledgment. Onstage, the public speech begins with `ええっと` before returning to signature casual address: `紫雲清夏で～す！`. Even at the summit, Sumika does not convert herself into a ceremonial prestige speaker.

The later `ねぇみんな！ あたし、強くなったよ！` and direct `キミ` address expand projection without abandoning colloquial intimacy. Her strong-idol state is therefore integrated rather than purified: fear, gratitude, history, gyaru sociality, and public authority remain mutually legible.

### 11.4 Dear 037 — role-conscious intimacy

The Producer scene makes register management explicit in language: `今からちょっとの間だけ、あなたはプロデューサーじゃない。` Sumika temporarily suspends the institutional role because she wants personal truth rather than role-appropriate reassurance. She then becomes unusually still while Producer admits insecurity and past thoughts of giving up.

When he says her live gave him courage, Sumika receives evidence that the public vocation she authored actually reached the person most structurally important to her. Brightness then re-enters the scene as future desire rather than defensive exit.

This does **not** resolve the dependency/enclosure problem. It does show that Sumika has some awareness that professional and personal speech are not identical and can require an explicit boundary operation.

### 11.5 Source-level acoustic corroboration

Mixed-scene measurements broadly support the qualitative contrast: fear/trauma segments sit lower on several pitch/projection proxies than public-promise, Prima Stella, humor-restoration, and future-facing segments. These values include BGM, other speakers, effects, and edit transitions. They are useful only as **SOURCE-METRIC** corroboration of a visually and semantically established state change, never as isolated claims about Minato Miya's pure F0, timbre, breath, or dynamic range.

## 11.6 Public/fan register

Sumika's ordinary friendliness gives her an unusually plausible route into public address. Yet friendliness alone can become image maintenance. The mature public register adds responsibility:

- decide what autobiographical material to disclose;
- turn disclosure toward someone else's courage;
- preserve the right to remain more than the inspirational story;
- accept that an audience will project while retaining authorship of the promise.

Dear 032 and 036 now directly verify that this public state is not simply louder ordinary brightness. It is brightness returned after exposure and redirected toward an audience.

## 13. Final voice formulation

> **Sumika's voice matures not by becoming less gyaru, less playful, or uniformly solemn. It matures by becoming more functionally differentiated. Brightness can remain pleasure, but it no longer has to erase fear; casualness can remain intimacy, but it can coexist with technical authority; public friendliness can become testimony without turning the whole self into an inspirational product. Her strongest late capacity is not a new timbre. It is **reversible register permeability**: the ability to let an emotionally consequential sentence remain inside her own established language long enough to count, receive support without treating that need as defeat, and then return to brightness without retroactively trivializing the disclosure.**
