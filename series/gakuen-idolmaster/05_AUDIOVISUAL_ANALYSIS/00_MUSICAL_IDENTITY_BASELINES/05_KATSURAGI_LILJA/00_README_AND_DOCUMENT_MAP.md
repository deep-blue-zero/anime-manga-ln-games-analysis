## Governing thesis

> **Lilja remains the idol of courage made circulatory. The audiovisual pass identifies permeable world-making as the formal mechanism: inward gathering becomes a stabilized center, and the center opens into a field through which received courage can be returned.**

Compact shorthand:

> **gather inward -> stabilize -> open outward -> return courage.**

The route does not reveal a fearless stage monster beneath a timid private girl. It shows one continuous person acquiring enough technique, trust, pleasure, and symbolic authorship to let inward intensity become public form.
