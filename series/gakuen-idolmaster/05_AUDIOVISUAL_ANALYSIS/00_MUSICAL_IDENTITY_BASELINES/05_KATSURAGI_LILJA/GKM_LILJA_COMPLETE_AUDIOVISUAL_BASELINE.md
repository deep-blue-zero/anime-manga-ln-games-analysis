# 1. Executive thesis — courage made circulatory through permeable world-making

The textual core's governing proposition survives:

> **Katsuragi Lilja is the idol of courage made circulatory.**

The audiovisual pass identifies the formal mechanism that makes that proposition visible and audible:

> **Lilja turns courage into a permeable performance field. She gathers fear, gratitude, desire, and borrowed belief near the self; technique gives that inward density a stable center; gesture, gaze, song, camera address, partner relation, and stage environment then let it travel outward without requiring vulnerability to disappear.**

A compact formal sequence is:

> **inward gathering -> stabilized center -> outward field -> returned courage**

This sequence appears across dialogue and performance.

In dialogue, Lilja often contracts before she claims a difficult sentence: hands or forearms gather near the sternum, gaze lowers or turns away, and the line pauses. Early in the route, the pause frequently ends in apology, deferral, or a request that another person decide whether she may continue. Later, pause remains common, but it increasingly ends in an owned proposition: a technical question, an accusation of concealment, a rival declaration, an admission of fear, a wish for Prima Stella, a public account of received support, or a request for future togetherness.

In performance, the same grammar becomes spatial. Emotion is gathered at the chest; a stable body line, prop, costume, or partner structure holds it; then open arms, forward reach, camera address, stage traversal, or a surrounding visual ecology expands it into a field another person can enter.

The packet therefore rejects five tempting simplifications.

First, Lilja does **not** become fearless. Fear remains present in late private and public scenes. Growth changes what she can do while fear is present.

Second, she does **not** reveal a hidden elite performer who was always technically complete. Her beginnerhood is real, and later charisma requires foundations, attention, pedagogy, and production support.

Third, her stage self is **not** a separate monster or alternate personality. Public scale is built from the same soft, pause-rich, relationally attentive person after the contraction-to-address sequence becomes more reliable.

Fourth, `魔性` is **not** a pure innate essence. A performer-specific capacity is visible, but its reproducible form is coauthored through technique, styling, prop, camera, lighting, imaginative vocabulary, and audience relation.

Fifth, effort is **not** sufficient virtue. The N.I.A. acting makes self-erasing effort physically and socially dangerous. Mature courage requires rest, trust, technical efficiency, pleasure, and the ability to receive support without interpreting reception as failure.

The strongest integrated formulation is therefore:

> **Lilja does not prove that anyone can succeed by trying hard enough. She demonstrates that fear can become socially useful when courage is received, technically governed, imaginatively shaped, publicly risked, answered by others, and returned.**

# 3. The stable audiovisual grammar

## 3.1 Inward gathering

Lilja's most recurrent bodily center is the sternum. In dialogue and music, hands return toward the chest before a difficult claim or release. The gesture is semantically flexible:

- fear held close enough to remain manageable;
- gratitude before it becomes speech;
- concentration before technical action;
- desire whose ownership is not yet secure;
- affection waiting for permission to become direct;
- a performance state gathering energy before address.

This repeated center matters because it prevents a crude reading of stillness as passivity. Lilja's stillness is often preparatory. It can be overwhelmed and self-erasing, but it can also become gravitational: a point from which the scene organizes itself.

## 3.2 Pause as protected authorship

The Source-Lock-derived register data clarifies the longitudinal change:

| route tranche | ellipsis-bearing lines | question-bearing lines | apology-bearing lines | crude stammer-marker lines |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dear 001-010 | 60.2% | 11.5% | 2.75% | 15.7% |
| Dear 011-020 | 70.9% | 11.1% | 4.27% | 13.2% |
| Dear 021-027 | 47.0% | 23.9% | 0.87% | 19.6% |
| Dear 028-037 | 50.5% | 10.6% | 0.52% | 5.4% |

These counts do not make every ellipsis fear or every question growth. Their safe use is comparative.

N.I.A. increases ellipsis and apology while the body is converted into proof of legitimacy. STEP3 sharply increases questions: Lilja is not simply more uncertain; she is actively trying to understand technique, audience effect, jealousy, rivalry, and the reproducibility of `魔性`. Late Dear reduces apology and crude stammering far more than it reduces pause.

The mature change is therefore not fluency replacing hesitation. It is **ownership after hesitation**.

## 3.3 Stabilized center

Technique supplies a center that early desire lacks. Shion's criticism is harsh, but the underlying diagnosis is crucial: insufficient foundations consume Lilja's attentional bandwidth. When the body is preoccupied with executing the next movement, there is little capacity left for audience relation, facial address, partner awareness, or deliberate atmosphere.

Training does not erase the inward state. It prevents the inward state from monopolizing all available attention.

This is why technical development and emotional delivery are not opposites. Technique gives feeling duration, repeatability, and bandwidth.

## 3.4 Outward field

The outward phase appears in several geometries:

- hands opening from the chest;
- arms extended toward audience or camera;
- running and broad traversal in `Campus mode!!`;
- forward reach in darker common material;
- direct eye-line and open-arm release in `白線`;
- relative stillness while the whole environment organizes around her in `Atmosphere`;
- side-by-side address and exchanged center in REVERSI;
- playful whole-body expansion in `ガラクタロード`.

Lilja's authority is therefore not defined by aggression or domination. It is defined by the ability to create a coherent field in which another person can recognize movement.

## 3.5 Permeability

The term `結界` might imply exclusion. Lilja's mature performance field works in the opposite direction.

- support enters from Producer, Sumika, family, Shion, teachers, staff, rivals, and fans;
- fear remains visible rather than being sealed away;
- production elements help sustain the world without replacing the performer;
- audience attention enters and changes the performer;
- courage exits toward the audience and partners;
- the field can dissolve without annihilating the person who made it.

`葛城結界` is best understood as **permeable world-making**, not defensive enclosure.

# 4. Dear 001-010 — the public beginner

The first Dear tranche establishes that Lilja's founding capacity precedes technique. She is socially careful, spatially and institutionally uncertain, and ready to list the obvious visible qualifications she lacks. The Producer names `強い意志` as the talent of stepping toward a dream while afraid.

The audiovisual evidence preserves the asymmetry of that act. Lilja does not immediately adopt the Producer's interpretation. She often holds herself physically small, lowers her gaze, bows or rounds her shoulders, and speaks in polite clauses that trail before completion. The opportunity feels revocable.

Early training scenes make technical weakness visible without humiliating the character into comic worthlessness. She can be earnest, concentrated, and physically active while still misallocating attention and requiring elementary correction. The packet offers no hidden elite line, timing, or effortless command.

The first-live sequence is decisive because it does not wait for technical completion. The body remains visibly beginner-like; stage scale is larger than the performer; fear does not disappear. What changes is the existence of an addressee. Sumika is not an abstract motivational symbol. She is the first specific person to whom Lilja wants the current self to arrive.

This strengthens three textual claims:

1. the first idol act is the decision to arrive and continue;
2. performance is organized around `届ける`, not merely executing correctly;
3. causal performance does not require completed mastery.

The important correction is that “visible effort” should not be treated as automatically inspirational. The first-live effect works because effort is made legible inside a relationship and because the performance has a specific direction. Strain alone is not the aesthetic.

### Phase disposition

- **PRESERVE:** real beginnerhood;
- **STRENGTHEN:** strong will as action while afraid;
- **STRENGTHEN:** Sumika as first addressee and first proof of causal performance;
- **REJECT:** hidden stage monster;
- **OPEN:** how much early Producer reassurance creates dependence alongside courage.

# 5. Dear 011-020 — proof obligation, bodily danger, and trust as technique

N.I.A. is the packet's strongest correction to any moral romance of grit.

Lilja's ethic that fans are persons rather than numbers remains sincere. Yet evaluation, ranking, criticism, and injury to the Producer's judgment can transform her relational gratitude into proof obligation. The internal logic becomes dangerous:

> because another person believed in me, failure would invalidate that person; therefore the body may be spent to prevent failure.

The acted material makes that structure bodily. Secrecy narrows the social field. Gaze reduces. Lines become more apologetic and pause-heavy. Physical depletion compresses performance and conversation. The statement that the body may break is not delivered as triumphant shounen resolve. It belongs to a state in which social relation and bodily self-preservation have collapsed into one test.

Care must therefore interrupt courage. Rest is not the opposite of resolve. Trust, pacing, stopping, and allowing another person to regulate the scene become acquired technical capacities.

The recovery does not prove permanent self-regulation. Later sources preserve the risk. What changes is the available repertoire: Lilja can now understand that accepting care may be part of keeping a promise rather than abandoning it.

Failure also begins to hurt differently. Earlier, defeat can be absorbed into a premise of inferiority. As self-regard and actual capacity grow, loss becomes grief rather than confirmation. The acting supports a girl who is more wounded because she now believes the desired result was genuinely available.

### Phase disposition

- **STRENGTHEN:** effort as both strength and self-harm vector;
- **STRENGTHEN:** Producer-directed vindication as part of the injury mechanism;
- **STRENGTHEN / OPEN:** rest and trust as trainable competencies;
- **PRESERVE:** fans as persons before metrics;
- **REJECT:** suffering as sufficient evidence of authenticity;
- **OPEN:** whether rest ever becomes intrinsically valuable rather than instrumentally justified by future work.

# 6. Dear 021-027 — technique, jealousy, `魔性`, and active inquiry

The third tranche is not a simple confidence phase. It is a phase in which Lilja learns to ask better questions.

Sumika's unit proposal, changing relational balance, jealousy, Shion's hostile expertise, and the discovery of intermittent charisma all increase the number of things Lilja must interpret. The unusually high question-bearing density is consistent with active technical inquiry rather than pure regression.

Shion's central diagnosis is visible in the contrast between effort and bandwidth. Lilja can work hard while remaining trapped inside the next movement. Foundations matter because they free attention for the audience, partner, face, gesture, and performance world.

The `魔性` discovery must be handled carefully. A performer-specific visual and relational effect is real enough to organize multiple sources. Lilja can draw attention into a scene. But the effect is intermittent, technically unstable, emotionally conditioned, and heavily supported by styling, staging, and interpretation. The route does not reveal a pure natural genius whose previous weakness was false.

Lilja's response is characteristic. She does not defend the old self against the new category. She asks how the effect works and how it can become usable. Fantasy vocabulary supplies the missing conceptual bridge. `葛城結界` lets her imagine audience relation not as vague aura but as a temporary world with entry conditions, props, movement, and return flow.

This is a major promotion of what could otherwise be dismissed as otaku gag texture. Familiar symbolic systems reduce the cost of authorship. The shy speaker becomes verbally and imaginatively expansive when she possesses a vocabulary in which the problem can be modeled.

### Phase disposition

- **STRENGTHEN / REVISE:** “talentless” is too broad;
- **STRENGTHEN:** charisma is coauthored and cultivated;
- **STRENGTHEN:** Shion as technical corrective to romanticized grit;
- **STRENGTHEN / NARROW:** low defensive vanity creates repair orientation but should not justify cruel pedagogy;
- **STRENGTHEN / PROMOTE:** fantasy imagination as professional authorship language;
- **REJECT:** feeling versus technique as an either/or choice.

# 7. Dear 028-037 — differentiated partnership, singular desire, and soft authority

The late route broadens Lilja's scene responsibility without replacing her voice.

She can now detect when Sumika is using brightness or competence to avoid disclosure. She can ask Sumika to rely on her, name fear, preserve affection, and still refuse the premise that one partner's singular desire betrays the relationship.

The most important development is the ability to want a scarce individual title. Early gratitude makes self-assertion morally dangerous: wanting something for herself can feel like taking away from Sumika, Producer, fans, or the support network. Late Dear allows the pause to end in desire rather than renunciation.

This is not individualism replacing relation. The desire becomes healthy precisely because relationship can survive it.

At the summit, Prima Stella begins in disbelief rather than sovereign inevitability. Lilja's public authority remains relational and credit-sharing. She can claim the action while naming the network that made it possible. That balance is the mature form of accountable agency:

- others caused real parts of the achievement;
- Lilja still acted;
- gratitude does not erase authorship;
- authorship does not erase dependence.

The late performance state also preserves tears, softness, comic decompression, and private uncertainty around public projection. This is not a defect in authority. It is evidence that authority no longer requires a false hard self.

Dear 037 reopens rather than closes the Producer relation. Asking for future togetherness demonstrates trust and desire, but it does not prove stable separation tolerance or nonachievement self-worth. The victory is track-scoped within D-LILJA and must not overwrite conditional P3 failure branches.

### Phase disposition

- **STRENGTHEN:** Lilja becomes someone Sumika can rely on;
- **STRENGTHEN:** singular ambition without betrayal;
- **STRENGTHEN / SPECIFY:** mature authority can remain soft, pause-rich, and relational;
- **STRENGTHEN / BOUND:** Prima Stella completes the D-LILJA courage circuit but is not universal continuity;
- **REJECT:** fused REVERSI identity;
- **OPEN:** rest, separation, Producer enclosure, and self-worth outside achievement.

# 8. Performed voice and register

## 8.1 One voice, not two

The packet does not support a private timid Lilja and a separate fearless stage Lilja. It supports one voice with multiple governed states.

Stable properties include:

- polite baseline;
- explicit gratitude;
- reflective pause before large desire;
- careful relation to status and authority;
- quick self-comparison under evaluation;
- readiness to ask a technical question once a credible path appears;
- direct affection with Sumika after trust is established;
- verbal acceleration around anime, game, fantasy, costume, prop, and stage-world concepts;
- public credit-sharing rather than self-made mythology.

The mature difference is what the voice can carry. Softness can now bear rivalry, command, correction, technical curiosity, grief, ambition, and public testimony.

## 8.2 Producer / `センパイ`

The Producer begins as the first authoritative interpreter of Lilja's courage. `桜フォトグラフ` humanizes that authority. His original approach was improvised; the chapel errand was an excuse; he was drawn into the encounter rather than executing a perfectly foreknown destiny.

Lilja has also learned to read him. She can anticipate the logic behind comparative training footage and complete parts of his explanation. This shifts the relationship from one-way legibility toward reciprocal interpretation.

The risk remains that his belief becomes a verdict she must vindicate. She accepts tasks more readily than identities and often seeks confirmation that his belief still holds before fully disclosing fear.

## 8.3 Sumika / `清夏ちゃん`

Sumika receives Lilja's widest increase in directness. The relation moves from guide and first addressee toward partner, patient, rival, and separate center.

Lilja's directness is rarely punitive. When she detects concealment, she names it because the relationship should become more truthful and usable. She can be jealous, hurt, or competitive without interpreting those states as proof that affection was false.

## 8.4 Shion

Shion activates hurt, literal processing, and technical hunger at once. Lilja's capacity to use valid information delivered with contempt is real. It should not be mistaken for endorsement of contempt or proof that she has no boundaries.

The safe characterization is epistemic humility and repair orientation, not passive submission.

## 8.5 Audience and fans

Public voice grows from `please accept what I can currently do` toward `let what I received move through me to you`.

Even at maximum public scale, Lilja does not narrate herself as self-made. Credit-sharing and agency coexist. That coexistence is central to her ethical and aesthetic identity.

# 9. Musical and visual development

## 9.1 `白線` — situated ownership

`白線` begins from an ownership problem. Lilja likes the song and recognizes its fit before she believes she has the right to call it hers. Technical inferiority appears to disqualify ownership.

The commu does not answer by declaring technique irrelevant. Sumika redirects the problem toward delivery and situated meaning. The song belongs to Lilja because it names her current movement, is made for her present state, and can carry the effort and relation that another singer would not reproduce in the same way.

The rendered 3DMV is dark blue and arena-scaled. Lilja repeatedly appears small relative to the audience and stage. Open-arm poses read as achievements against visible vastness, not as the entitlement of a performer who has always owned public space. Alternation between close facial access and distant stage framing asks whether the private body can remain itself while becoming public.

The official MV is radically brighter. Sky, running, air, height, white/blue space, typography, and upward movement externalize aspiration. The source-level luminance difference is large, but the metric merely corroborates the visible editorial contrast.

The two forms divide the song's work:

- **3DMV:** the beginner must act before a real audience field;
- **official MV:** the direction of flight becomes an authored symbolic world.

**Disposition: STRENGTHEN / SPECIFY.** Ownership is situated meaning, relation, present effort, and address—not technical monopoly.

## 9.2 `Campus mode!!` — recursive inheritance

The commu begins with reverence and distance. Costume and song belong to a Hatsuboshi tradition Lilja once watched from outside. Her caution records the ethical weight of inheritance rather than empty modesty.

The decisive change is second-person purpose. She wants to use the inherited form to tell another frightened beginner that a first step is possible because the form once told her the same thing.

The 3DMV is bright, mobile, and expansive. Running shapes, raised arms, broad traversal, and frontal address make this the clearest kinetic invitation in the packet. Lilja does not disappear into institutional house style; she inhabits it by giving it a personally specific return route.

**Disposition: STRENGTHEN.** Legitimate inheritance is trained participation plus recursive return.

## 9.3 `極光` — controlled dark authority

`極光` expands the viable product beyond luminous beginner and white fairy. Black and military-fantasy styling, cool color, portal/aurora imagery, vertical line, and solemn completion allow Lilja to carry darker formality without hardening her face into aggression.

The performance matters less as a final thematic summit than as a range correction. Softness can support authority under low light, black costume, and more severe spatial grammar.

**Disposition: STRENGTHEN / EXPAND.** Dark formal authority belongs to her range.

## 9.4 `Atmosphere` — deliberate `葛城結界`

`Atmosphere` is the decisive coauthorship object.

The Producer and staff propose a white/fairy visual concept and greater facial exposure. Showing the forehead increases legibility and therefore vulnerability. Lilja does not simply submit to branding. She appropriates it:

- asks for a staff-like microphone;
- imagines magical movement;
- connects costume, prop, face, gesture, and stage world;
- supplies fantasy vocabulary;
- names the audience field as `葛城結界`.

The 3DMV often gives her a stable vertical center while camera, lights, screens, audience, and surrounding geometry move. A white costume and staff-like microphone organize the silhouette. Kneeling, chest-level gesture, soft close-ups, and moments of visible delicacy keep centrality from becoming domination.

The performance demonstrates that Lilja can create scale by becoming the point around which a scene coheres.

The official MV translates the field into luminous forest, water, sky, reflections, flowers, paths, and light. Lilja remains inside an ecology rather than standing outside it as an omnipotent author. The active environment makes entry, passage, and transformation more important than conquest.

The correct causal model is neither natural essence nor staff manufacture:

> **dispositional visual legibility + technique + styling + prop + camera + production ecology + Lilja's imaginative appropriation = reproducible world-making**

**Disposition: STRENGTHEN / REVISE.** `魔性` is gravitational legibility inside a coauthored permeable field, not innate magical dominance.

## 9.5 `桜フォトグラフ` — fallible encounter and reciprocal reading

The acquired object is the complete Lilja song commu. It functions primarily as Producer-history and relational-authorship evidence.

It establishes comparative training analysis tailored to Lilja's physique, reconstructs the improvised first approach, and reveals that the Producer was drawn into the encounter. Lilja can now anticipate and complete parts of his reasoning.

This replaces destiny with encounter without reducing the encounter's importance. Being seen remains transformative; the witness is simply human and fallible.

The trio official MV is outside this Lilja-local R1 packet and can enter later as shared event evidence. Its absence does not block the present relational conclusion.

**Disposition: REVISE / HUMANIZE.** The Producer is confidence scaffold and coauthor, not omniscient discoverer.

## 9.6 `ときめきエモーション` / REVERSI — synchronized differentiation

The canonical 3DMV and official MV are shared sources already used in Sumika's AV pass. Lilja cross-references the same semantic home.

The 3DMV provides the strongest bilateral evidence:

- focality alternates rather than remaining monopolized;
- center is shared or exchanged;
- gestures mirror without becoming identical;
- black/white contrast preserves difference;
- proximity is followed by separation;
- hand contact does not become permanent fusion;
- each girl receives individual close-up authority;
- side-by-side audience address creates coordination between two centers.

The official MV abstracts relation through circles, reversals, lines, paired colors, silhouettes, repetition, and promise. Its graphic grammar can tempt a `two halves` reading. Separate outlines, alternating authority, and the continued existence of negative space resist ontological collapse.

Real relationship history becomes competition-relevant and publicly marketable. Within the supplied boundary, both participants narrate and perform that history rather than being pure objects of institutional narration. Commercial instrumentalization, privacy, and long-term consent remain open.

**Disposition: STRENGTHEN / SPECIFY.** REVERSI is synchronized differentiation: attachment, rivalry, shared history, and separate desire coexist.

## 9.7 `ガラクタロード` — pleasure as capacity

This object is indispensable because it prevents the analysis from reproducing Lilja's own pathology.

The commu establishes pre-Hatsuboshi singing, anime and idol-song interest, learning through Sumika, trying choreography for fun, and pleasure before performance became proof of legitimacy.

The 3DMV is colorful, comic, expansive, and less solemn than `白線`, `極光`, or `Atmosphere`. Lilja can carry novelty, playful movement, direct reach, and bodily enjoyment without ceasing to be recognizable.

If every performance is interpreted as visible strain, the critic merely rewards the self-harm circuit. Pleasure is not a soft supplement to serious courage. It is a protective and generative resource that keeps performance from becoming only an examination.

**Disposition: STRENGTHEN / PROMOTE.** Recovered enjoyment belongs in the mature talent model.

## 9.8 `ENDLESS DANCE` and `Howling over the World` — portability

These are breadth objects, not character-owned story anchors.

`ENDLESS DANCE` uses dark carnival/door imagery, sharper movement grammar, angular framing, and a more stylized environment. `Howling over the World` uses red/glitch/cyber imagery, black costuming, forceful reach, and more aggressive visual pressure.

Lilja remains legible in both. Delicacy does not prevent darker or more forceful material, and the performance does not require a hard alternate face.

**Disposition: DOWNGRADE.** White/fairy and Sweden-coded visuality cannot remain the explanatory center of Lilja's product identity.

# 10. Fans, audience, and the courage circuit

The route's fan ethic begins by refusing reduction to numbers. The AV layer adds scale and direction.

In `白線`, the audience field is visibly larger than the performer. In `Campus mode!!`, the inherited school form becomes direct invitation. In `Atmosphere`, audience relation is transformed into entry into a temporary world. Late Dear turns success into public testimony about received courage.

The circuit is therefore:

1. another person names or supplies courage;
2. Lilja acts before fear disappears;
3. technique and production make that action legible and repeatable;
4. audience or partner answers;
5. the answer changes Lilja's available confidence;
6. Lilja returns the accumulated courage through later performance.

This is not a closed exchange in which the performer simply gives and the audience receives. Nor is it a demand that audiences validate every effort. The circuit depends on craft, truthfulness, and the possibility of answer.

The phrase `courage made circulatory` is therefore not metaphor alone. It describes a repeated social and audiovisual structure.

# 11. Technique, charisma, and the talent taxonomy

The AV packet supports a layered talent model.

## 11.1 Technical skill

Initially weak and genuinely trained. The packet offers no evidence of concealed formal mastery. Foundations matter because they free attentional bandwidth.

## 11.2 Strong will

Behaviorally real: Lilja repeatedly moves while afraid. It is not automatically healthy. Without governance, the same will can convert the body into proof.

## 11.3 Relational transmissiveness

People can recognize her visible effort, vulnerability, gratitude, and forward motion. This capacity is not reducible to technical weakness, but it also should not be romanticized as `being bad is charming`.

## 11.4 Visual-charismatic world-making

A performer-specific capacity becomes visible in `Atmosphere` and related sources. It is partly dispositional and partly cultivated. It requires technical and production conditions to become reliable.

## 11.5 Imaginative authorship

Fantasy and fandom vocabulary let Lilja model a stage world, request props, imagine movement, and appropriate external concepts. This is professional material, not only comic characterization.

## 11.6 Pleasure

Preprofessional enjoyment and low-stakes enthusiasm provide energy not organized by proof obligation. `ガラクタロード` promotes pleasure from background texture to mature capacity.

The integrated correction to `talentless hard worker` is therefore:

# 15. Strong counterarguments and limits

## 15.1 Production may be doing most of the work

`Atmosphere` depends heavily on costume, prop, camera, lighting, stage screens, environment, editing, and staff concept. The correct response is not to deny production support. The commu shows Lilja appropriating and extending the concept. The claim is coauthorship, not unaided aura.

## 15.2 Visible effort can be audience manipulation

The packet cannot prove that every audience response is pure or that institutions never commodify vulnerability. REVERSI already demonstrates that authentic history can become competitive framing. Later event/support/ensemble phases must test consent, privacy, and institutional extraction.

## 15.3 Shion's effectiveness could excuse cruelty

The analysis must not infer that harshness is the necessary cause of technical growth. Lilja's ability to extract information is a capacity of Lilja, not a moral defense of hostile pedagogy.

## 15.4 Credit-sharing may conceal weak self-worth

Late public gratitude can be both ethically accurate and a residual difficulty claiming self-authorship. The packet supports improvement but not closure. The strongest state is the ability to say both `others caused this` and `I acted`.

## 15.5 `ガラクタロード` may be an isolated exception

One pleasure-centered object cannot prove stable enjoyment under prolonged professional pressure. It is enough to reject a model in which no such register exists; it is not enough to close the future risk.

## 15.6 Low-resolution late Dear limits facial claims

D04 does not support delicate microexpression arguments. Its conclusions rely on text control, voice timing, large facial state, tears, posture, blocking, and broad gaze. A later higher-resolution equivalent could strengthen visual detail without changing the logical source count.

# 16. Reconstruction-safe character model

This Phase-3 model is strong enough to guide character simulation, but it is not the final Phase-7 monograph.

## 16.1 Stable core

Lilja is:

- polite and relationally attentive;
- genuinely shy under unfamiliar evaluation;
- behaviorally courageous rather than subjectively fearless;
- technically repair-oriented;
- vulnerable to moralizing effort and bodily expenditure;
- grateful without being permanently ego-less;
- unusually open to valid correction once a concrete path appears;
- direct with Sumika when concealment threatens the relationship;
- imaginatively fluent in anime, game, fantasy, costume, prop, and stage-world domains;
- capable of dark, bright, solemn, playful, and forceful performance without personality replacement;
- most powerful when inward feeling acquires a stable outward address.

## 16.2 High-stakes response sequence

A plausible high-stakes Lilja response usually proceeds as follows:

1. contract physically and temporally;
2. compare herself unfavorably or identify the risk of disappointing a trusted person;
3. seek a relational anchor or a concrete technical proposition;
4. risk overwork if legitimacy appears threatened;
5. respond better to specific repair than generic reassurance;
6. act before fear disappears;
7. disclose fear more directly once action remains possible;
8. return support to the person who helped stabilize her;
9. decompress through tears, softness, gratitude, or small humor rather than hard triumph.

## 16.3 Low-stakes response sequence

A plausible low-stakes Lilja can show:

- warm politeness;
- domestic competence;
- literal earnestness;
- gentle teasing;
- rapid fandom enthusiasm;
- pleasure in songs, choreography, food, games, and shared interests;
- relief when the moment is not converted into evaluation.

## 16.4 Relation-specific behavior

**Producer:** deferential but increasingly reciprocal; seeks confirmation; can complete his logic; vulnerable to turning belief into proof obligation.

**Sumika:** affectionate, candid, and increasingly corrective; can support, accuse concealment, compete, and preserve separate desire.

**Shion:** hurt but technically attentive; extracts usable correction; does not need to like the teacher to use the information.

**Fans/audience:** treats people as persons; addresses them through courage circulation rather than metrics alone; shares credit publicly.

## 16.5 Speech and performance errors to avoid

Do not simulate Lilja as:

- constantly stammering;
- generically helpless;
- an aggressive alternate-stage personality;
- a stereotyped foreigner with weak Japanese;
- a saintly hard worker without envy, anger, ambition, fandom, or pleasure;
- someone who accepts self-harm as uncomplicated heroism;
- one half of an ontologically fused Sumika unit;
- fully autonomous after Prima Stella;
- permanently fairy-coded or limited to white/soft visual material.

# 18. Final integrated formulation

The Phase-3 AV packet confirms that Lilja's power is not the moral spectacle of a weak girl suffering nobly until she wins. It is the construction of a usable passage between inward fear and another person's movement.

She begins by needing others to name her courage. She then risks turning that borrowed belief into a demand that the body prove everyone right. Technique, rest, trust, criticism, imagination, pleasure, rivalry, fans, and production gradually diversify the ways she can act. The resulting performer is not fearless, self-made, or separate from support.

She is more difficult and more durable than that.

> **Katsuragi Lilja is the idol of courage made circulatory through permeable world-making. She gathers what she cannot yet say, stabilizes it through craft and relation, gives it a form another person can enter, receives the answer, and returns the courage changed.**

The compact developmental arc is:

> **fearful arrival -> borrowed belief -> proof-obligated effort -> governed effort -> technical bandwidth -> coauthored world-making -> differentiated partnership -> pleasure restored -> public return**

This baseline is canonical for Phase-3 audiovisual claims. It remains subordinate to Source Lock 1.0 for exact wording and continuity, and it remains provisional relative to later events, support cards, relationship synthesis, and the definitive Phase-7 monograph.
