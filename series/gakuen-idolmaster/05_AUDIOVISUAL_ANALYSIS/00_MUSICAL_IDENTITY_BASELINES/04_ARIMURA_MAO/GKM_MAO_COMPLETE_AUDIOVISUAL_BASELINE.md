## Phase 3 integrated acting, voice, music, theatricality, visual authorship, and character synthesis

## 0. Authority and evidence discipline

This document is the canonical Phase-3 audiovisual baseline for **Arimura Mao / 有村麻央**. It does not replace `GKM_CORE_04_ARIMURA_MAO.md`. The textual core remains authoritative for exact Japanese wording, continuity, route-state boundaries, and claims about what characters explicitly believe. This document asks what the voiced and rendered layer changes: how Mao's prince register occupies the body, how embarrassment and grief interrupt that register, how acting changes from enclosure to technique, how songs turn one fixed role into a repertoire, and how audience, partner, institution, and chosen witness enter the construction of `ボクらしさ`.

- five acted Dear sources covering Dear 001–037 plus the distinct `adv_dear_amao_010-01` / 親愛度10.5 bridge;

Dear 021–027 and 028–037 are 480p30, but faces, gaze, posture, broad gesture, and interaction timing remain readable; their AAC-LC 44.1 kHz stereo tracks are approximately 128 kbps. Evidence labels:

- **AV-DIRECT** — a visible or audible feature of a locally materialized source;
- **TEXT-CONTROL** — exact wording, chronology, or route logic established by Source Lock 1.0;
- **SOURCE-METRIC** — complete-file or mixed-scene measurement, never an isolated measurement of Mao's vocal instrument;
- **INFERENCE** — synthesis requiring multiple evidence layers;
- **OPEN** — plausible but not settled at this boundary.

Source forms remain separate:

1. **Dear and song communications** establish acted story context, speech rhythm, relation-specific behavior, facial/body acting, and intended meaning.
2. **Authored official MVs** establish official symbolic/editorial interpretations, not literal in-story chronology.
3. **Rendered 3DMVs/game performances** establish stage body, choreography, audience relation, costume, virtual camera, and performance portability.
4. **Static full-song presentations** establish complete released audio but no moving-image authorship.
5. The supplied `ガラクタロード` source is an **approximately 88-second A-ending/game performance**, not a complete full-release object. It can support performance-procedure claims but not exhaustive song-form analysis.

# 1. Executive thesis — refractive role authorship

The textual core defines Mao through **reclaimed aspiration**:

> **Mao learns not to stop performing the prince, but to stop using the prince to disappear.**

The AV pass preserves that thesis and identifies its principal formal mechanism:

> **Mao is the idol of refractive role authorship. Her mature self is not hidden behind performance and is not identical to any single role. Body, cuteness, coolness, grief, acting craft, audience recognition, inherited forms, and chosen relationships pass through one another like light through fluorite. Princehood survives because it becomes a renewable grammar of address rather than the only silhouette in which Mao is permitted to exist.**

The compact formulation is:

> **Role becomes repertoire. Repertoire becomes relation. Relation lets aspiration remain true without becoming a cage.**

A second formulation captures the route's ethical claim:

> **A role is not false because it is performed. It becomes harmful when it monopolizes the conditions under which a person can be recognized.**

The audiovisual material rejects four easier stories.

First, the prince was never simply fake. Child Mao's `わたしも王子様になるっ！` is staged as immediate wonder and desire. The later nightmare in the `見て` communication does not reveal that the dream was invented; it reveals how an authentic dream became fused to one body, one casting category, and one rule for what Mao was allowed to like.

Second, cuteness is not the buried true self that replaces the prince. Early embarrassment visibly contracts Mao's body and disrupts her usual poise, but later cute smiles, soft outfits, sweets, plush-toy memory, and flirtation coexist with red prince costume, theatrical command, and `0番`. Cuteness becomes one available state rather than a verdict.

Third, acting is not a pathology cured by sincerity. `SUGAR FLAVOR`, `Campus mode!!`, Osaka stage direction, and `ガラクタロード` show acting as observation, physical memory, role decomposition, timing, image control, and partner awareness. The failed variable is compulsory performance, not performance itself.

Fourth, maturity is not an end to fracture. Dear 027, `見て`, and Dear 037 retain crying, fear, dependence, and the need to be witnessed. Mao becomes more convincingly princely because these states can remain visible without revoking the role.

The principal formal sequence is:

> **`Fluorite` — identity becomes refractive and relational.**  
> **`Feel Jewel Dream` — coolness becomes a selectable style rather than a defensive obligation.**  
> **`Campus mode!!` — inherited aspiration becomes transferable craft.**  
> **`SUGAR FLAVOR` — relation becomes a performance resource rather than an image threat.**  
> **`見て` — the role archive is reclaimed; the prince becomes chosen address.**  
> **`ガラクタロード` — actorly individuation becomes portable into prescribed material.**

# 2. Six interacting systems

## 2.1 Body and category: the injury is not the body alone

Dear 003 stages Mao's history through repeated contraction. The childhood account begins in controlled recollection. As she reaches puberty, chest development, height, lost casting, and the claim that the present body has moved away from the prince, gaze and posture become more guarded; hands move toward the chest and face; blush and interruption enter a previously composed presentation. `可愛い` then produces not one stable reaction but a collision of embarrassment, threat, anger, and reluctant attention.

The AV layer strengthens the textual distinction between bodily fact and social category. Mao's body changes. What becomes injurious is the conclusion that changed embodiment makes the desired social/stage role unavailable and that every feminine or cute sign proves defeat.

Mature performance does not visually erase the changed body. `Feel Jewel Dream` and `見て` instead stage it as compatible with strong line, tailored costume, direct gaze, elegant hand vocabulary, and audience command. `SUGAR FLAVOR` goes further: Mao's size difference beside Rinami becomes part of the duet's visual design rather than evidence that Mao cannot occupy a princely relation.

> **AV finding:** the body is not restored to an old casting fit. The criteria for princehood are reauthored around the body that exists.

## 2.2 Voice and poise: from compulsory steadiness to permeable theatrical control

Mao's most stable acted baseline is a composed senior/prince register: measured politeness, controlled smile, upright posture, hand-to-chest or hand-on-hip framing, and an instinct to organize the encounter. The register is real. It carries dorm leadership, professional discussion, protective attention, and stage confidence.

Early defensive princehood treats that poise as something that must not break. Cute praise, fear, unexpected intimacy, and body-history discussion therefore produce conspicuous rupture: stammer, repetition, enlarged reaction, blush, narrowed posture, and vocal/visual acceleration. The contrast is not merely “low cool voice versus high cute voice.” It is a broader loss and recovery of temporal governance.

Late Mao does not eliminate rupture. She gains **permeable theatrical poise**: embarrassment, grief, Kansai play, tears, flirtation, and public command can enter the same voice system without making the prince illegitimate. Dear 037 is decisive. Mao and Producer cry openly, then she explains continuing transformation, negotiates entry into professional life, enters the empty stage, names `0番`, speaks a shared future, and ends with `ボクを、見ていて`. The role survives contact with need.

> **AV finding:** mature princehood is not acoustic sameness. It is the ability to modulate, break, and return without treating variation as self-betrayal.

## 2.3 Acting craft: role becomes a method rather than an ontology

The acted packet repeatedly shows that Mao's actor history is practical capital.

- In `Campus mode!!`, she knows choreography through inherited observation, then has to translate intuitive metaphors into concrete instruction for Kotone: fingers, toe placement, timing, posture, and smile.
- In the Osaka communication, she proposes theatrical lighting and uses venue space as a stage designer rather than merely a performer.
- In `SUGAR FLAVOR`, generic romantic role-play fails because it produces technically recognizable but emotionally hollow delivery. Mao and Rinami solve the problem by using a real shared longing—their three-year desire for idol performance—as the affective referent.
- In `ガラクタロード`, Mao rapidly switches gesture, facial address, salute, point, wink, strong line, and comic/cute accents inside prescribed material.

The mature actor is therefore not someone who never performs. She can separate role from ontology, select a role for a purpose, ground it in lived affect, and revise it in relation to partner and audience.

> **AV finding:** acting becomes authentic not when it disappears, but when Mao can say what the role is for and remain answerable to the people it addresses.

## 2.4 Mirror, crystal, costume, and role archive

`Fluorite` and `見て` provide complementary official visual theories.

`Fluorite` uses crystals, refraction, water, reflected/doubled figures, multiple Mao images, hands that almost meet, and light supplied from outside. The self is not represented as one intact core escaping fragments. Fragmentation itself becomes a means of recomposition.

`見て` uses theatre seats, toys, mannequins, mirrors, dressing-space imagery, costume changes, shadowed figures, the red prince uniform, flowers, and the umbrella callback. The succession of roles does not peel away until one uncostumed essence remains. It accumulates an archive from which Mao can choose.

The two works answer different fears:

- `Fluorite`: change and relational influence need not dissolve the self.
- `見て`: continuing to perform need not imprison the self.

> **AV finding:** Mao's mature self is less a hidden substance than an authorship relation among remembered roles, current body, chosen audience, and future possibility.

## 2.5 Relation and witness: being seen must not become being fixed

Mao needs witnesses, but the route repeatedly tests what kind.

The Producer first legitimizes himself by seeing coolness in conduct—the umbrella given to a junior—rather than costume. He researches the child-actor history and gives Mao language for separating the aspiration from the rigid category. Yet the same interpretive power can become enclosure. Dear 025 makes the risk explicit: Mao wants the person who understands and loves her most to remain her Producer, even when a professional agency offers more experience.

The AV layer complicates rather than resolves this dependence. `見て` ends in deliberate romantic/princely address to the Producer; Dear 037 turns `0番` into a shared future and asks him to keep watching. But `Fluorite` distributes reflected light across fans, juniors, rivals, peers, and Producer. `SUGAR FLAVOR` makes Rinami an independent witness and partner. `Campus mode!!` lets Kotone and future entrants inherit from Mao. The best evidence therefore points toward widening witness rather than exclusive revelation.

> **AV finding:** Mao's development is healthiest when no single observer owns the final definition of Mao, even if one observer remains emotionally central.

## 2.6 Institution and audience: private aspiration becomes generative visibility

Mao's stage expands from personal proof to institutional succession.

`Campus mode!!` is inherited repertoire. Its communication explicitly links the song to the welcome performance that kept Mao inside idol aspiration. The 3DMV places Mao in a huge multicolored audience field and repeatedly turns her toward the crowd. The communication then makes her a teacher. She is no longer only the person reached by institutional form; she can alter and pass it on.

Dear 036 similarly transforms Prima Stella. The title remains scarce and competitive. Mao does not abolish that structure. Her speech nevertheless interprets the first star as orientation: everyone entered with a light, walls can obscure it, and visible stars can help others find their own. The visual staging—red prince costume, trophy, Sena's transfer, crowd address, hand-to-chest gratitude—makes succession a public act rather than simple possession.

> **AV finding:** Mao's mature princehood becomes vocational. It protects, teaches, challenges, receives inheritance, and makes visibility answerable to people who may follow.

# 3. Dear and acted-story trajectory

## 3.1 Dear 001–010 — one role loses monopoly

The early packet shows a character who is socially functional and visually composed until the conversation reaches the conditions of her recognizability. Prince Mao can scout, advise, protect, and negotiate. Body-history Mao becomes smaller, more interrupted, and more self-covering. Cute praise becomes a force that reorganizes the whole scene.

Dear 004 is especially important because the acted exchange refuses a visual substitution. The Producer does not ask Mao to install a generic cute-idol performance over the old prince. The route's own phrase is that this would change nothing `本質的には`. The visual experiment instead asks Mao to permit ordinary reaction, present embodiment, and unforced pleasure to appear onstage.

Dear 010's horror outing is not disposable comedy. Mao's frightened body, request to hold hands, and reflection on the former prince actress establish that changed roles can remain continuous with admired qualities. The prince becomes less dependent on never looking afraid.

The 10.5 bridge confirms the first integration: Mao can name both cute and cool Mao as `ボクらしく`, ask for more work, and sound/appear ready for a larger stage. The bridge does not prove permanent resolution; it establishes a usable repertoire before N.I.A. stress-tests it.

## 3.2 Dear 011–020 — the prince is reclaimed as a chosen role

N.I.A. mobilizes contrast. Cute styling can carry cool material; hard styling can carry softer expression. Mao's image becomes a set of combinations rather than one defended costume.

Dear 015 is the decisive role declaration. Mao acknowledges `呪い` and `呪縛`, but the acted scene does not end in discarding the costume. She claims the aspiration as `ちゃんと本物`, says she will wear and love the role, and turns toward Shion with direct stage-combat force. Because she has already admitted the wound, command no longer depends on denying it.

This is the first clear form of **chosen princehood**:

> the role can be performed without being the only permitted self.

Gekka then prevents the route from confusing psychological integration with technical supremacy. Mao's admiration and strain remain legible. Self-acceptance creates capacity for work; it does not erase the skill gap.

## 3.3 Dear 021–027 — coauthorship, dependence, and real defeat

The 100Pro sequence moves the central question from self-image to production relation. Dear 025 is not performed as a casual rejection of professional expertise. Mao sees the facilities, artists, and opportunities. She still chooses the Producer because their shared difficulty and his first-fan position have become part of `有村麻央`.

The scene strengthens self-authorship and exposes dependency at once. Mao is capable of choosing an apparently less efficient path for reasons she can articulate. Yet the sentence that she needs the person who understands and likes her most can make artistic autonomy difficult to imagine outside that relation.

Dear 027 then proves that prince poise is no longer equivalent to emotional invulnerability. Mao can congratulate Sena, frame the loss as a step, moderate the dorm party, and speak forward. Her face and body later contract; the inner monologue admits the desire to win now; Rinami's care permits a full breakdown. The next morning Mao can resume planning without retroactively making the grief unreal.

> **AV finding:** behavioral recommitment and emotional recovery are separate variables. The mature prince can lose ugly and still continue.

## 3.4 Dear 028–037 — succession without fixed arrival

The late packet expands the prince into observation, pedagogy, rivalry, public office, and future contract.

Dear 033's prince photo-book material is a compact summary of the mature grammar. Mao can direct and enjoy a deliberately princely image, then visibly fail at the horror attraction, reject some photographs, laugh at a bad title, notice Shion, and keep the day's images as part of the work. The prince contains staged elegance and “uncool” reaction.

Dear 036 visually gives the title to the red-costumed Mao whom earlier material would have treated as either too cute for princehood or too theatrical for authenticity. Sena's transfer, Mao's gratitude, and the audience-facing speech turn the role into succession rather than solitary center capture. The speech's formal politeness broadens into the claim that other people also carry a first-star light.

Dear 037 refuses closure. Producer and Mao cry; Mao says the ideal remains ahead; 100Pro becomes a start line; the empty stage is converted into the theatre coordinate `0番`; and the final `ボクを、見ていて` asks for continued witness rather than certification of a finished identity.

> **AV finding:** the endpoint is not “Mao finally becomes the real prince.” It is “Mao acquires the right and capacity to keep revising what princehood can do.”

# 4. Voice-performance model — permeable theatrical poise

Mao's voice is best modeled as **permeable theatrical poise**.

“Prince voice” is too narrow. Mao's stable performance identity is a social-temporal procedure: compose the body, measure the line, preserve courtesy, orient another person, and give the exchange a readable theatrical shape. Early Mao uses that procedure to prevent contradictory states from counting. Mature Mao uses it as one mode among several.

## 4.1 Ordinary senior/prince register

- measured, courteous, and slightly ceremonious;
- strong sentence completion and calm turn-taking;
- hand-to-chest, hand-on-hip, or presenting gestures;
- `ボク`, `～たまえ`, `ボクでよければ`, and princely address can appear without full stage exaggeration;
- often organizes the needs of juniors before naming her own.

This state is genuine care and also a potential hiding place.

## 4.2 Professional/stage-command register

- firmer projection and forward body orientation;
- direct audience or opponent address;
- actorly control over pose, gaze, and line ending;
- capable of becoming threatening in competition without losing formal clarity;
- late examples are less defensive because vulnerability has already been admitted elsewhere.

Dear 015, `Feel Jewel Dream`, `見て`, Dear 036, and `ガラクタロード` are the strongest controls.

## 4.3 Cute-embarrassment collision

- blush, stammer, repetition, delayed answer, widened reaction, self-touch, or posture contraction;
- praise is not merely pleasant information: it challenges the category through which Mao has protected the dream;
- later embarrassment becomes playful or intimate rather than purely threatening.

The stable signature is not a single pitch. It is the interruption of Mao's usual governance.

## 4.4 Wounded-child / defensive-fixation state

The `見て` communication makes this state explicit. Child `わたし` speaks wonder. Nightmare Mao repeats `違う`, rejects the princess role and formerly loved objects, and emerges breathless. The acted contrast shows how `ボク` and princehood became emergency continuity devices after the old casting relation failed.

This state must not be treated as proof that `ボク` is false. It shows that a true form of self-reference can also be recruited into defense.

## 4.5 Reflective grief

Mao often reduces motion before she can accept another person's care. Dear 027 and the theatre aftermath in `見て` are the clearest examples. She does not immediately convert tears into a speech about resilience. In `見て`, she first asks the Producer not to look at her face, then claims the painful years as `ボクのもの`.

Grief becomes authorship when it can be retained rather than erased.

## 4.6 Relation-specific teasing and flirtatious princehood

With Producer, Mao can turn the prince into deliberate flirtation: umbrella sharing, `水も滴る王子様`, `惚れなおしましたか`, `もっともっと、ボクに惚れさせてみせますよ`. The performance is knowingly theatrical and emotionally real. The route therefore refuses the assumption that deliberate role-play reduces romantic or intimate sincerity.

With Rinami, teasing is more peer-historical. `SUGAR FLAVOR` works because Rinami has seen failed, awkward, and unfixed Mao. Their relation supports both stage play and correction.

## 4.7 Osaka/Kansai play

`ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！` supplies the clearest regional register. Mao deliberately tries Kansai speech, asks for the Producer's comic response, slips into `そんなに泣いてへんし`, and moves between local food enthusiasm, child-actor embarrassment, technical stage planning, parental vulnerability, and public homecoming address.

This register is not a hidden “real Mao” beneath standard Japanese. It is a local, familial, comic, and memory-bearing resource unavailable in the ordinary polished prince baseline.

## 4.8 Public succession register

Dear 036 combines formal gratitude, autobiographical claim, institutional metaphor, and audience invitation. The register is neither the intimate softness of Producer scenes nor the hard challenge to Shion/Sena. It is a prince voice made publicly answerable.

### Source-level acoustic corroboration

Mixed-scene proxies broadly align with the visible state changes: body/category rupture, Osaka play, flirtation, and the `0番` future segment show wider or higher mixed-source pitch activity than several defeat and Producer-choice windows. The result is only **SOURCE-METRIC** corroboration. BGM, other speakers, effects, and segmentation prevent pure claims about Mao's F0, timbre, or vocal range.

# 5. Principal musical and performance sequence

## 5.1 `Fluorite` — refraction makes relation compatible with continuity

### Song communication

Mao initially approaches the song as an object to master. Rinami's suggestion that `流れる` need not mean weak will lets Mao move from possession toward participation. By the performance, Mao defines `ボクらしさ` as something born in relation with fans, juniors, peers, rivals, and Producer. Fluorite shines by receiving light.

### Authored official MV

The MV places Mao in cosmic, aqueous, and crystalline spaces. She falls or floats, appears in multiple forms, is fractured into angular fields, stands beside reflections, reaches across discontinuities, and encounters versions of herself rather than one enemy double. Hands nearly meet. External light does not expose a fake self; it enables refraction.

### 3DMV

The 3DMV translates that symbolic logic into crowd relation: crystal forms, blue-purple lighting, spotlight, open/reaching hands, and a large field of audience light. Mao's soft costume and controlled gestures do not cancel the cool voice/presence. The stage makes received light into public return.

### Finding

> **`Fluorite` does not reveal a final Mao. It establishes that Mao can remain continuous by changing in relation.**

The textually proposed cute-appearance/cool-voice mismatch is strengthened, but the stronger result is not contrast for its own sake. Multiple qualities can refract through one performer without requiring a single dominant category.

## 5.2 `Feel Jewel Dream` — coolness becomes selected rather than compulsory

### Source correction

The square 1080×1080 file is a static complete-song presentation with approximately 256 kbps AAC. It supports full-mix analysis, not moving-image claims. The 1080p60 source is the rendered performance.

### Communication

Mao explicitly treats the work as a return to adult, stylish coolness after cute and cool have both gained fans. The point is not regression. She can now choose cool because cuteness no longer signifies defeat.

### 3DMV

Black/navy glitter, angular jewel architecture, extended body line, chin/face framing, direct camera address, and controlled sensuality establish a mature cool image. Yet the performance repeatedly permits warm smiles and visible pleasure. The role does not require emotional frost.

### Finding

> **`Feel Jewel Dream` is coolness after plurality: not “the prince restored,” but “this is the mode Mao has selected for this song.”**

The full-mix source has the highest broad spectral-centroid proxy in the supplied music packet, consistent with a relatively bright/edged mix. This remains a source-level production descriptor, not a claim about Mao's isolated timbre.

## 5.3 `Campus mode!!` — inheritance becomes transmission

### Communication

The costume and song carry the memory of the welcome performance that renewed Mao's desire to become a stage star. The individualized costume preserves institutional form while incorporating Mao's image. Teaching Kotone then forces Mao to convert actor intuition into shareable technique.

### 3DMV

The 3DMV uses a massive multicolored stadium, mobile stage space, star-shaped audience lights, open reach, and repeated crowd orientation. Mao is legible within institutional repertoire without needing to overwrite it with a private symbolic world.

### Finding

> **Mao moves from recipient of institutional aspiration to translator and transmitter of it.**

The static full-song presentation adds complete audio but no moving visual layer.

## 5.4 `SUGAR FLAVOR` — synchronized difference and real affect

### Communication

Mao once refused units because another performer might destabilize her image. She chooses Rinami because Rinami has watched the uncool and unfinished Mao. Generic romantic acting proves inadequate. The pair grounds the love song in an actual shared longing: three years of desire for idol performance.

### Authored official MV

Mao and Rinami occupy paired but distinct visual worlds—different colors, rooms, frames, mirrors, doors, phones, and dream spaces. The edit repeatedly aligns them without collapsing them. Upside-down pairing, split composition, and exchanged visual motifs construct relation as parallelism and crossing.

### 3DMV

The duet alternates focality, eye-line, side-by-side and back-to-back arrangements, hand contact, separate solos, and shared audience address. Mao's smaller body is not visually corrected toward Rinami's silhouette. The contrast becomes choreography.

### Finding

> **`SUGAR FLAVOR` makes relation a performance resource precisely because neither partner must disappear into the other.**

This is Mao-side authority. A final bilateral account should wait for Rinami's independent AV baseline.

## 5.5 `見て` / `Face Me` — the role archive becomes renewable princehood

### Communication

The communication juxtaposes child aspiration, nightmare rupture, confrontation with the transformed former prince, grief, retrospective ownership, and chosen flirtation. The line `ぜんぶ、誰にも渡さない。ボクのものだから` reclaims pain as history rather than evidence that the dream was false.

### Authored official MV

The MV begins with stage/audience distance and moves through childhood objects, miniature theatre, shadow, mirrors, dressing-space imagery, mannequins, and multiple costumes. Different Mao roles accumulate around her. The red prince emerges late, followed by flowers, bouquet, and the umbrella/rain memory. The work does not reveal one costume as the real one. It converts costumes into an archive.

### 3DMV

The red prince/military costume carries strong line, direct reach, crystal stage architecture, crowd command, and close-ups that retain softness. Mao's prince image is maximal precisely when the larger corpus has already made fear and grief visible.

### Finding

> **`見て` is not a demand to recognize one fixed image. It is a demand to face the changing person who authors the image.**

The English subtitle `Face Me` is analytically apt: Mao asks not merely to be looked at as a finished spectacle, but to be met across change.

## 5.6 `ガラクタロード` — actorly portability under prescribed form

The supplied source is a short A-ending game performance. Within that limit, Mao remains distinctive through rapid micro-role transitions, salute, point, wink, strong line, cute/comic interruption, and deliberate audience timing. The performance supports the claim that acting procedure—not costume alone—individualizes shared material.

A full controlled comparison against equivalent Saki, Temari, and later cast sources remains **OPEN**. The current file is sufficient to strengthen portability, not to rank which rendition most transforms the composition.

# 6. Supplemental communications as voice and relationship controls

## 6.1 `キミとセミブルー`

Mao organizes a chaotic all-at-once dorm summer event. Her senior voice coordinates juniors and protects Rinami from extra labor. Only later does she admit that the event was also for her own final-summer memory. The communication confirms a recurring pattern: Mao can articulate others' needs earlier than her own.

## 6.2 `雪解けに`

Handmade hina dolls, junior observation, public prince address, embarrassment at `麻央ちゃん先輩`, and the final Producer-pocket gesture create a compact sample of inherited care, cute reaction, stage leadership, and intimate dependence. The communication supports the textual claim that dorm leadership is received-and-repaid practice, not effortless maternal essence.

## 6.3 `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！`

The Osaka communication supplies register breadth unavailable elsewhere: deliberate Kansai play, local food pleasure, child-actor memory, venue/stage-direction craft, `ちびっこ王子`, embarrassment over childhood dependence, parental witness, and homecoming projection. Mao's parents recognize the current idol as a prince without requiring the old child body or casting category.

> **AV finding:** family recognition completes no identity test. It validates continuity across transformed form.

# 7. Recurrent audiovisual motifs

## 7.1 Refraction, mirrors, and doubles

`Fluorite`, `SUGAR FLAVOR`, and `見て` repeatedly place Mao beside reflections, split frames, other versions, or paired bodies. The double is not simply imposture. It is a method for showing identity as relation and selection.

## 7.2 Costumes as archive

School uniform, soft/cute styling, black jewel attire, institutional `Campus mode!!`, duet costume, and red prince uniform coexist. No single costume receives exclusive ontological authority.

## 7.3 Hands, umbrellas, and reach

Hands protect, teach, invite, receive a partner, orient an audience, and ask to be held. The umbrella begins as ethical coolness toward a junior, returns as Producer intimacy, and appears again in the `見て` visual field. Princehood moves from silhouette to relation enacted through contact.

## 7.4 Spotlight, audience rows, and crowd-light fields

The spotlight first appears as the place Mao fears she can no longer occupy. Later works show the audience not merely judging but supplying light, affect, inherited form, and future entrants. The stage becomes reciprocal without ceasing to be competitive.

## 7.5 Split frames and synchronized difference

`SUGAR FLAVOR` and `Fluorite` reject fusion. Relation appears through parallel spaces, exchanged motifs, alternation, and contact that preserves two focalities.

## 7.6 `0番`, center, and the unfinished future

Theatre-center symbolism remains hierarchical. Mao wants the center and wins the scarce title. Dear 037 changes its meaning by turning the coordinate into a place of shared witness and continuing transformation rather than a final proof of fixed fit.

# 8. Relationship consequences

## 8.1 Mao and Producer

The AV evidence strengthens four propositions at once:

# 11. Final integrated formulation

Mao's audiovisual arc is not an unmasking narrative.

The child prince was an authentic dream housed in a temporarily compatible body and professional category.

The defensive prince was the same dream forced to perform continuity after that compatibility ended.

The mature prince is neither the old category restored nor the role discarded. It is a repertoire Mao can author through the changed body, in relation to people who do not require one fixed form.

`Fluorite` makes the self refractive. `Feel Jewel Dream` makes coolness selectable. `Campus mode!!` makes inheritance transferable. `SUGAR FLAVOR` makes partnership productive. `見て` turns roles into an archive and princehood into chosen address. `ガラクタロード` shows that the resulting procedure travels.

The most complete current formulation is therefore:

> **Arimura Mao is the idol of reclaimed aspiration realized through refractive role authorship. She becomes authentic not by ceasing to act, but by acquiring the freedom to choose, revise, share, and survive the roles through which she reaches other people.**

And the final compact sequence remains:

> **Role becomes repertoire. Repertoire becomes relation. Relation lets aspiration remain true without becoming a cage.**
