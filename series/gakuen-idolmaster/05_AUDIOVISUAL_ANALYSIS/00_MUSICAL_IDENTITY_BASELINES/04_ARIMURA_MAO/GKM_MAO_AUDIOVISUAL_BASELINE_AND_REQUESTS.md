## Purpose

This register translates the Phase-3 Mao textual findings into a **human-retrievable whole-video audiovisual queue**. It is an acquisition and verification document, not a substitute for the later integrated audiovisual baseline.

The original version was intentionally narrow. The current revision expands the boundary after two findings:

1. Mao's frozen textual corpus contains several high-value modular communications not represented by the four Dear compilations or the original four song-commu requests.
2. Mao's released musical repertoire now extends beyond the five works originally entered in the textual song map, most importantly the STEP3 song **`見て`**, plus 1st/2nd/3rd-single shared and secondary tracks useful as controlled performance comparisons.

Priority rule:

> **complete scene / clean original voice audio > exact target isolation > maximum resolution.**

For dialogue commu, 720p is preferred but 480p is fully acceptable when facial acting remains legible and the voice track is clean. For MV/3DMV/performance close reading, 1080p or better is preferred when readily available.

### Priority classes

- **P0 — core/gating:** needed before Mao's integrated Phase-3 AV baseline should be considered complete.
- **P1 — high-value supplemental:** materially improves voice, relationship, or musical-identity modeling; strongly preferred before closure when reasonably retrievable.
- **P2 — breadth/control:** useful for cross-song and cross-idol calibration but does not block the baseline.
- **P3 — optional/future:** reception, live/seiyuu, remix, or other non-core comparative material.

### Acquisition status vocabulary

- **STAGED** — source is already present in the current Mao AV source folders.
- **REQUESTED** — desired source not yet staged.
- **DEFER / NON-BLOCKING** — analytically useful but not required for Phase-3 closure.
- **FORM VERIFY** — staged object exists but exact source form should be classified during inspection.
