### Signal interpretation limits

The most defensible comparative findings are:

- `Unhappy Light` is the least spectrally bright/noise-like measured work.
- `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！` is the brightest/noisiest measured work.
- `ガラクタロード` has one of the densest onset profiles.
- `一体いつから` and `初` carry the highest pulse estimates.
- alternate edits of the same song may have very different dynamic spans because of silence, lead-in/out, and stage-edit structure.

No singer-only range, resonance, or breath conclusion is inferred from these numbers.
