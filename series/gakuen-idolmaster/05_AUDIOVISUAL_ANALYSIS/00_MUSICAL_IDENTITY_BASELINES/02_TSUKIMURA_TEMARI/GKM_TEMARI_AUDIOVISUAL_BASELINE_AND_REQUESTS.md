## 0. Evidence boundary

This document selects the material needed to test Temari’s textual character model. It does not yet assert observed facts about:

- Ojika Nao’s speaking or singing timbre;
- tessitura, register, breath, vibrato, resonance, attack, or diction;
- composition, harmony, rhythm, instrumentation, mix, or mastering;
- MV color, camera, editing, choreography, costume, or visual symbolism;
- dialogue pauses, crying, laughter, posture, facial animation, or BGM as rendered.

Public metadata establishes titles, release relationships, and search identity. Actual interpretation waits for the supplied files.

## 7. Analytical questions

### Speaking performance

- How sharply does Ojika Nao separate controlled public Temari, irritated private Temari, childlike collapse, food pleasure, and intimate self-disclosure?
- Does the `私だけのプロデューサー` line sound comic, possessive, frightened, triumphant, or layered?
- How is Rinha imitation audible in persona scenes?
- What happens to register when Misuzu uses `まりちゃん`?
- Does the late `手毬` request produce an audible change in relation?

### Singing identity

- Is the singing voice colder, hotter, cleaner, rougher, or more expansive than the speaking image suggests?
- Can the same voice embody `Luna say maybe` indirectness, `アイヴイ`, `Unhappy Light`, STEP3 reconstruction, and shared repertoire?
- Is `青い炎` a useful sonic metaphor after direct inspection, or only a textual self-description?

### MV and bodily dramaturgy

- Does the body appear contained, combustive, fragile, disciplined, hungry, exhausted, or liberated?
- How do cool color palettes relate to heat and complete combustion?
- How are imitation and plurality represented?
- Do STEP3 works visually integrate Misuzu/Rinha/Producer without making Temari secondary?

### Shared-song authorship

- What remains Temari-like when composition is held constant?
- How does her `ガラクタロード` compare with Saki’s after both are available?
- Can Re;IRIS distribute focus while preserving Temari’s distinct voice and physical identity?
