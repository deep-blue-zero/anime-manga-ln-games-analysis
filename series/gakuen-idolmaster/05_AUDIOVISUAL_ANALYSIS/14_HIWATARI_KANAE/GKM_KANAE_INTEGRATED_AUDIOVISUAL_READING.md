# Hiwatari Kanae — dialogue, song and the decision to appear

A stand-in’s job gives Kanae an excellent reason to take the center mark. It gives her little preparation for what happens when the light comes on and a member of staff asks her to sing. Later, alone in front of an audience, she can address strangers with the same respect she uses in service. The strongest comparison is between the purposes her voice serves in these scenes. A fixed personality trait such as shyness leaves out her pleasure in singing, her professional competence and her ability to refuse an assignment.

## What Kanae agrees to perform

In Dear 004 the Producer asks Kanae to hum China’s *Wonder Scale* as she walks for the camera. She declines on grounds of faith and explains that reproducing her lady’s video and singing the song herself would presume too much. Singing it in her head is acceptable. Later she readily accepts a stand-in job. Stage staff ask her to sing **anything** for the sound check. Under the lights she manages 「……ひとつの夢～……♪」, the opening words of *初*. The paired accounts of SRC-0023 describe the sudden gasp under the lights and the shaky, trailing phrase at 1582–1586 s. She is wearing her maid outfit. The staff member’s reassurance that ordinary speaking volume will do reaches her after she has experienced the test as exposure. The scene gives the listener a scale of vulnerability that the bare instruction to take the center mark cannot convey. Earlier in the same capture she sings the words while doing chores. Being willing to sing, being willing to imitate China and being ready to sing under scrutiny are three different conditions. The mirror in Dear 005 lets her see a version of herself she has not yet agreed to show. China gives her the lily-of-the-valley hair ornament. The fitting-room reveal puts it with the green capelet. In the bedroom Kanae imagines standing with China in the light and rebukes herself for entertaining the idea. Dear 007 supplies another test of how the image is being used. Kanae’s apparent advancement there ends in a waking jolt. The actual assessment takes place in Dear 009 and has consequences in the surrounding scene. ## Who gets to hear her choose

In the greenhouse the Producer tells Kanae that China wants her to have a dream of her own. He even corrects her answer from becoming an idol to becoming a *top* idol. The decisive statement comes later and belongs to Kanae. She lists the things she cannot do, says that being watched frightens her, repeats 「でも」 and makes herself say 「アイドルに、なりたい、です」. The pauses on the page break up a formally polite sentence. The immediately ensuing run introduces urgent inner imperatives as well as outward breath. At 3274.5–3280.5 s in SRC-0023 she is visibly running as the wording concerns doing something for herself without being ordered. The run’s orchestral accompaniment helps give that decision momentum. Scripted inner voice, spoken exchanges and bodily exertion have different addressees. The retained evidence supports the contrast without assigning an individually verified stress pattern to every line. A measured opening gives way to 「皆様、一緒に！」 at 17.0–18.3 s. At about 104–115 s she asks everyone to put their spirit into it and to run through the last stretch together. ## A song chosen with her and claimed by her

The first-song communication opens with a question about ownership before the music even starts. Kanae assumes the demo is China’s. The Producer tells her he wants her onstage with a song of her own and that China contributed ideas. Gratitude to her lady briefly makes the gift intelligible. Once the demo enters at 81.93 s, Kanae asks whether the file is wrong. It is Kanae herself who calls it bright and pop. She hears an invitation to act in ways she does not recognize as hers. In the next part she challenges an unruly slogan on a garment and agrees to try other clothes. The difference matters. China and the Producer can widen what Kanae imagines possible without determining every choice she will make. [SRC-0059 and Japanese song communication](GKM_KANAE_EVIDENCE_AND_INSPECTION.md#dear-and-performance-editions)

The maid uniform feels like armor to her. A change of outfit and repeated experience of singing the song allow a different ease of movement. The communication gives this claim an interval the listener can follow. At 446.7–451.4 s the backing builds under her centering breaths. Her final exchange moves from China’s expectations, through the Producer’s invitation to show herself, to 「私のための歌、私のためのステージ」. She accepts what others have helped prepare and gives the coming performance a first-person purpose. This is where the commu leaves her. There is no sung rendition of the concert to cite from this ending. Its place in a single calendar after every Dear or Produce outcome remains unspecified. ## What the released performances add

In the standalone 3D *メタモルフォーゼ* capture, a suitcase accompanies Kanae’s entrance. The polished solo in the finished soundtrack runs roughly 34–190 s. Around 67–80 s the accompaniment thins, the staging turns to the cool-lit stairs and her delivery becomes airier on the thought that the brightness frightens her. The full groove returns for the chorus. The conjunction of words, framing, arrangement and delivery lets the listener hear apprehension as part of this performance’s pleasure. The apparent poise is compatible with moments of doubt. This edition furnishes an audible performance in its mix; separating the singer’s contribution from all production choices would require other material.
