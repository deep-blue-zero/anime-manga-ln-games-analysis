# Audiovisual file index

Use this index to reach the established character artifacts across their different directory homes. The [current-state map](../CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) governs project state. The [file organization audit](../01_CORPUS_AUDIT_AND_SOURCE_LOCK/GKM_FILE_ORGANIZATION_AUDIT_20260909.md) records the rationale for preserving these homes and identifies later routing improvements.

A link establishes where an artifact is stored. It does not independently certify every acquisition or measurement claim in that artifact. Retain source-manifest limitations, release-generation boundaries, and the separate recovery/rebuild dispositions when using these records.

The [recovery audit](../10_RELEASE_MANIFEST_AND_ARCHIVE/GKM_RECOVERY_INTEGRATION_AUDIT_20260909.md) records the restored evidence and remaining collection status. Its [integration manifest](../10_RELEASE_MANIFEST_AND_ARCHIVE/GKM_RECOVERY_INTEGRATION_MANIFEST_20260909.json) binds original archive members and hashes to repository paths and declared transformations. Hiro and Misuzu's baseline documents are delivered, while their technical documentation remains qualified by the [prospective targeted rebuild register](../01_CORPUS_AUDIT_AND_SOURCE_LOCK/GKM_TARGETED_REBUILD_CANDIDATES.md); this integration performs no new inspection or measurement.

## Placement and version rules

All 13 AV revision addenda remain in their corresponding homes under `../03_SOURCE_FACING_READINGS/01_CHARACTER_CORE_READINGS/`. They qualify the preserved textual cores and are not duplicate integrated baselines. Exact Japanese wording and continuity remain governed by the source lock and source-facing readings.

Saki, Temari, and Kotone keep their AV completion reports, delivery audits, and internal checksum manifests in those textual-core homes. The controls above route there explicitly. Their integrated baselines, AV evidence matrices, and source manifests remain in the AV character homes.

Hiro, Sumika, Misuzu, Sena, and Tsubame keep dialogue and music specialists with their baselines. The other eight characters use the role directories. Lilja's music specialist occupies the music-role directory root. Each table entry resolves the actual file; no parallel copy is required.
