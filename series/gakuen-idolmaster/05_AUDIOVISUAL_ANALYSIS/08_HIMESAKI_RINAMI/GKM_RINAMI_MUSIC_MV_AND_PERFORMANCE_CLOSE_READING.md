# HIMESAKI RINAMI — MUSIC, MV, AND PERFORMANCE CLOSE READING

## 0. Source-form discipline

This document keeps four audiovisual forms separate:

- **song commu:** intended story state, authorship, rehearsal, and explicit interpretation;
- **3DMV:** rendered body, choreography, costume, camera, stage, and audience address;
- **official MV:** authored symbolic/editorial interpretation, not literal route footage;
- **static/song video:** complete audio continuity or common-song evidence without equivalent moving-image authorship.

The same song can therefore support different claims depending on form. A brighter official master, more cuts, or symbolic collage is not proof that the in-story performance was louder, more emotional, or chronologically identical.

R2 changes the visual confidence of Dear 021-037, not the musical packet. The song, choreography, and authored-MV findings are preserved; the higher-resolution Dear material strengthens how the later musical identities are anchored to grief, competition, technical authority, public self-love, and direct desire.

## 1. Governing musical thesis

Rinami's musical identity is not “mature soft love song.” Its stronger continuity is **relational address that progressively includes the performer**.

The principal sequence is:

> **`clumsy trick` — protected disclosure.**  
> **`L.U.V` — desire becomes pleasurable style.**  
> **`36℃ U･B･U` — interpreted forwardness demands response.**  
> **`Campus mode!!` — inherited form becomes self-authored public craft.**  
> **`ガラクタロード` — received encouragement is returned with force.**  
> **`SUGAR FLAVOR` — care becomes peer-shared center.**

The broader repertoire proves that this continuity survives cyber force, dark dance, athletic brightness, novelty, and institutional common material.

## 2. `clumsy trick`

### Communication

The song is built around Rinami's current honest feelings. Fear of rejection is not an obstacle to remove before recording; it becomes part of what the work can contain. The implied addressee is dual: public audience and a private `you`.

### 3DMV

The performance uses a dark purple intimate concert field, restrained travel, chest and face framing, measured close-ups, and a large audience whose lights remain an enveloping field. Rinami is exposed but not pushed into declarative triumph.

### Official MV

The authored MV uses clock, keyhole, computer/desktop, layered windows, note-like marks, split images, and repeated graphic fragments. Access is mediated. The work visualizes self-disclosure as composition, delay, and indirect viewing rather than transparent confession.

### Finding

> **Vulnerability becomes publicly usable because it is shaped, not because mediation disappears.**

## 3. `L.U.V`

### Communication

The commu expands romantic/relational self-awareness beyond fear. Rinami can discuss songcraft, audience effect, and personal proximity with greater pleasure.

### 3DMV

Warm sunset/orange-pink light, a festival crowd, broader hand/face play, direct lens address, and larger smiles make desire socially enjoyable. The body is less contained than in `clumsy trick`.

### Static full-song presentation

The separate full-song video supplies complete released audio and cover continuity. It is not an authored MV and cannot support symbolic moving-image claims.

### Finding

> **`L.U.V` is the missing middle term: not merely hesitation overcome, but desire discovered as an aesthetic pleasure.**

## 4. `36℃ U･B･U`

### Communication

Sena and the Producer provide two interpretive routes: disciplined imagination and transfer from competitive courage. Rinami combines them. Authenticity is not literal diary equivalence.

### 3DMV

Door/portal staging, paired silhouette imagery, pink-violet costume, frontal close-up, blushing challenge, and more provocative gestures turn the performance toward response. The addressee is no longer only asked to understand; the addressee is asked to move.

### Official MV

Collage, caution graphics, chibi multiplication, `2D` language, handwriting, repeated faces, and explosive pink romance insist on constructed feeling. The MV's artificiality is not an alibi against truth. It is the medium through which interpreted emotion becomes legible.

### Finding

> **Forwardness is crafted and playful, not a proof that fear or mediation vanished.**

## 5. `Campus mode!!`

The commu withholds the Producer's design notebook to prevent his image from becoming Rinami's instruction. The 3DMV places Rinami in a large green-gold institutional field with open reach, mobile stage space, and repeated crowd orientation.

She does not privatize the song into onee-san symbolism. She inhabits the inherited form and changes what the tradition now contains.

> **Institutional authorship becomes reciprocal when preparation creates room rather than a final answer.**

## 6. `ガラクタロード`

The song was once an older idol's gift to Rinami and a private survival resource. The 3DMV uses sneakers, bright color, action gestures, jumps, larger traversal, and pop-rock force.

The song therefore tests two claims at once:

1. Rinami's identity survives beyond conscious older-sister performance;
2. encouragement can be returned through force and bodily pleasure rather than maternal containment.

Both are strengthened.

## 7. `Howling over the World`

The commu parodies older-sister metaphysics inside science fiction. The 3DMV gives Rinami cyber styling, dark space, crouch/command silhouettes, hard contrast, and action framing.

> **The onee-san image becomes a genre resource, not a law of seriousness.**

## 8. `SUGAR FLAVOR` / RippleSign

### Communication

Rinami chooses Mao because she can trust her back and hand. Mao supplies cool/actorly technique; Rinami supplies love-song interpretation. Generic role-play fails until the pair grounds the work in a real shared longing.

### 3DMV

The duet rotates focality. Separate solos, offset positioning, scale contrast, face-to-face and side-by-side alignment, touch, and shared audience address preserve two performers. Rinami is not assigned permanent relational center.

### Official MV

Distinct color worlds, rooms, frames, mirrors, phones, reversals, and paired compositions align the two without merging them. The authored object uses synchronized difference rather than fusion.

### Bilateral result

Mao's earlier AV baseline concluded that relation becomes a resource because neither partner disappears. Rinami's independent packet confirms the same structure from the other side.

> **Peer collaboration is not identical behavior. It is mutual correction, redistributed center, and permission for difference to remain visible.**

## 9. `歌声は君いろ`

The official MV uses filmic frames, coastal road, travel, seasonal light, past/present imagery, and open landscape. Voice becomes memory and continuity beyond a single dyad. Without a paired commu in this packet, the work remains supplementary rather than a route-state anchor.

## 11. Recurring visual vocabulary

### 11.1 Hands at chest -> hands offered outward

The most stable transformation is from inward holding to outward address. The gesture can carry fear, gratitude, desire, or concentration before becoming invitation.

### 11.2 Frames, doors, screens, and thresholds

`clumsy trick` uses keyholes/windows/computer mediation. `36℃ U･B･U` uses door/portal and graphic threshold. Rinami's romantic art repeatedly asks how private feeling crosses into public form.

### 11.3 Audience-light fields

The crowd is not neutral background. Lights create the visible return path through which care comes back to Rinami.

### 11.4 Dual addressee

Many songs are public and privately pointed at once. This is not automatically evidence that every lyric is autobiographical confession. It is a stable structure in which private attachment intensifies public address.

### 11.5 Full-body fanservice

Face, hand, torso, line, travel, and camera timing distribute audience relation across the body. Technical precision is one of the forms through which warmth becomes scalable.

### 11.6 Paired difference

SUGAR FLAVOR makes relation visible through alternating center, contrast, and contact rather than one fused silhouette.

## 13. Final musical formulation

> **Rinami's music does not move from fake role to true voice. It moves from protected disclosure toward a repertoire in which desire can become pleasurable, forward, institutionally authored, physically forceful, and peer-shared. The stable continuity is relational address; the major development is that the performer increasingly remains one of the people the address is allowed to serve.**
