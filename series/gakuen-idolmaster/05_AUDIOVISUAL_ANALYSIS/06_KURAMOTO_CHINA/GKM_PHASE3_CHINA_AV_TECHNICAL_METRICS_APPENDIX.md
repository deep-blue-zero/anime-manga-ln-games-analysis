## 8. Raw-script acting spot check — Dear 034

The raw script for Dear 034 was checked directly to verify that `ふにゃ/きり` is formally staged rather than inferred only from dialogue text.

Verified features include:

- distinct facial-motion/override assignments;
- different body motions for relaxed and organized states;
- explicit camera changes;
- controlled line durations for the mask declaration;
- progressive acceleration of the alternation;
- camera shake and comic overload at `ふにゃきりっふにゃにゃぁ～～～～～！`.

This supports the integrated-state reading. It does not license generalized claims about every performance scene without separate inspection.

## 10. Inspection and provenance limits

- No isolated dialogue or singing stems were available.
- Exact speech wording comes from Source Lock, not automated transcription.
- Audio metrics describe complete mixes, not performer physiology or emotion.
- Sparse visual metrics describe edited whole frames, not isolated body motion.
- Rendered 3DMVs are official game presentation, not documentary records of a physical concert.
- Authored MVs are symbolic first-party readings, not literal chronology.
- D-CHINA Prima Stella remains track-scoped.
- Support-card and numbered-event AV evidence remains outside this release.
- Common-song performances are breadth tests, not equal substitutes for character-owned works.
