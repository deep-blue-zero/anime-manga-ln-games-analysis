# TSUKIMURA TEMARI — COMPLETE AUDIOVISUAL BASELINE

## 0. Executive finding

The audiovisual corpus strongly confirms the Phase-3 textual model of Tsukimura Temari as the **idol of authored combustion**, but it also gives that model a more exact performative mechanism.

Temari does not become `クール` by suppressing emotion until little remains. She becomes cool by forcing too much feeling through a deliberately narrow form: clear words, controlled posture, selected image, technical attack, direct eye contact, and a song capable of carrying what ordinary speech cannot hold. Her stable audiovisual identity is therefore best described as **compressed overexposure**.

> Temari’s public control is not low emotional temperature.  
> It is high emotional pressure made legible through chosen form.

This explains why the apparently contradictory Temaris do not cancel one another:

- the clipped and severe evaluator;
- the rapidly irritated rival;
- the needy and openly childish girl;
- the low, searching speaker who names shame;
- the formally poised titleholder;
- the playful performer willing to look ridiculous;
- the singer who turns indirect feeling into a public object.

The audiovisual evidence rejects the simple binary of “fake cool idol” and “true needy private girl.” Both are performed states, but neither is thereby false. Early Temari treats the existence of multiple states as proof that the idol is counterfeit. Later Temari learns to select, cite, combine, and publicly answer for them.

Her principal musical sequence makes that development unusually clear:

1. **`Luna say maybe`** — resemblance is disowned in speech and confessed through song. The authored MV fragments Temari into repeated cyan-blue images, divided panels, moon/night space, lines, and textual/diagrammatic frames. The 3DMV converts that divided interior into restrained direct address.
2. **`アイヴイ`** — plurality becomes curation. The dark, angular 3DMV does not expose one hidden “real Temari”; it selects a harder, more self-possessed stage body through a Producer gaze that she actively solicits and accepts.
3. **`Unhappy Light`** — softness, sleepiness, loneliness, domestic routine, childhood imagery, and unguarded need become official image material without being treated as failure. This is the decisive counterexample to any theory that Temari can be truthful only at maximum heat.
4. **`一体いつから`** — the later self no longer has to expel its sources. Temari studies China’s gentleness, Misuzu’s audience-oriented expression, Rinha’s inherited model, and her own full-force method as distinct techniques rather than as mutually exclusive claims to authenticity.
5. **`ガラクタロード`** — `青い炎` becomes a public artistic category. Temari recognizes herself in the Producer’s phrase, performs the prescribed song inside a common comparison field, visibly pays the bodily cost, and vows to make her version of coolness socially real.

The complete sequence is therefore not:

> mask → unmasking

but:

> disowned resemblance  
> → curated plurality  
> → admitted softness  
> → learned expressive plurality  
> → public category authorship.

The dialogue corpus provides the corresponding acted proof. Early Temari moves abruptly among controlled resentment, combustive protest, and childlike need because she experiences those states as mutually humiliating. In Dear Idol 010, the remembered child and the artificial-winged idol are spoken in a lowered, searching mode before ambition is reconstructed. In Dear Idol 016, Rinha’s threatened disappearance produces a visibly and acoustically extreme filial rupture; the cool persona does not vanish so much as reveal the attachment it had been built from. In Dear Idol 023, post-loss Temari becomes unusually compact and judicial—`負けは負けです`—while the Producer carries the more openly volatile frustration. By Dear Idol 035–037, she can move among embarrassment, command, appetite, intimate naming, formal stage address, care for the Producer, and present-tense self-love without insisting that only one state is the authentic one.

The strongest revised formulation is:

> **Tsukimura Temari is the idol of authored combustion. Her audiovisual identity consists in compressing excess feeling into selected form, then gradually enlarging that form until imitation, bodily need, childish attachment, ordinary softness, theatrical artifice, rivalry, and chosen dependence can all be cited as materials of one answerable self.**

This remains ethically unstable. The same form that makes Temari communicable can make self-endangerment admirable. The same Producer who accurately names her blue flame also controls food, stamina, schedule, image, repertoire, and competitive opportunity. The same public that values her contradictions may reward the exposure of pain. The AV corpus confirms authorship and reciprocity; it does not prove that the resulting system is safe.

## 1.4 Limitations

- No isolated vocal stems were supplied. Singer-only tessitura, formants, breath noise, compression, and dynamics cannot be separated cleanly from the mix.
- Full-song sources differ in duration, lead-in/out, mastering, and whether they contain an authored MV or a game-stage edit. Raw loudness and dynamic values are therefore comparative aids, not mastering judgments.
- Dialogue pitch proxies contain BGM and other speakers. They are used only when a large within-scene change corroborates visible and textual evidence.
- Visual claims distinguish official authored MVs from standardized game 3DMVs. A 3DMV demonstrates bodily performance and portability; it does not necessarily encode a song-specific narrative argument.
- Re;IRIS remains `U1`. Its performance capacity cannot be inserted into every solo-route biography.

# 2. Audiovisual identity: compressed overexposure

## 2.1 Why “cool beauty with a gap” is too weak

The common “cool outside, needy inside” description notices contrast but misdescribes its structure. It implies that the public image is static and the private state is primary. Temari’s audiovisual corpus instead shows a person who repeatedly **authors the relation among states**.

Her control is active:

- she selects when to shorten or sharpen a reply;
- she changes posture and projection when evaluation becomes performance;
- she can use high, mobile need as protest, attachment, comedy, or panic;
- she can lower and slow speech when naming origin shame;
- she can formalize emotion into public address;
- she can deliberately perform cuteness or absurdity without treating it as involuntary exposure.

The mature self is therefore not the discovery of one stable voice. It is increased freedom to move among voices without experiencing each transition as proof of fraud.

## 2.2 The body as pressure vessel

Temari’s audiovisual body is not simply elegant or attractive. It is the visible apparatus that contains and pays for the performance.

Across the corpus, the body functions as:

- source of vocal force;
- site of weight and appetite shame;
- object of training and Producer management;
- record of exhaustion;
- instrument of angular stage command;
- comic body capable of awkwardness;
- relational body capable of proximity, listening, and distributed formation.

`ガラクタロード` is especially important because the commu and performance hold the two meanings together. Temari insists on complete combustion, is recognized as a blue flame, and then appears visibly spent after rehearsal. The metaphor is aesthetically accurate because the body has actually been used as fuel.

This is why the Producer’s role remains ethically difficult. Improving the pressure vessel can be care, production, and enabling at once.

## 2.3 Direct address and answerability

Temari’s major 3DMVs repeatedly use direct eye contact, hand-to-chest motion, forward extension, pointed or cutting gesture, and a frontal relation to camera/audience. Yet the function changes over time:

- in `Luna say maybe`, address is difficult and intimate;
- in `アイヴイ`, it becomes selected self-possession;
- in `一体いつから`, it carries historical self-questioning;
- in `ガラクタロード`, it becomes public category-making;
- in `Campus mode!!`, it becomes institutional invitation;
- in Re;IRIS, it becomes one node in rotating mutual attention.

The audience is not merely a receiver. It is the field before which Temari must answer for the self she has made.

# 3. The developmental music sequence

## 3.1 `Luna say maybe`: confession through disowned resemblance

The official MV’s cyan, white, black, and deep-blue palette initially resembles the cold image attached to Temari. Its actual grammar is unstable rather than serene:

- repeated portraits;
- divided frames and panels;
- lines, wires, or diagrammatic structures;
- typography and image fragments;
- moon/night space;
- running or forward-moving images;
- photographic/live-performance material embedded within graphic construction.

Temari is not represented as one continuous interior person. She is repeatedly framed, doubled, and translated.

The song commu gives this form a precise narrative function. Temari disowns the song’s twisted and difficult girl as unlike herself while continuing to hum and inhabit it. The contradiction is not simple hypocrisy. Ordinary speech cannot yet tolerate the resemblance; song can.

The 3DMV strips away much of the authored graphic argument and tests bodily performance instead. Temari remains isolated on a dark-blue stage, often close to the camera, with restrained but decisive movement. Hand-to-chest gestures, frontal gaze, and later expansion convert indirect feeling into addressed performance.

`Luna say maybe` therefore establishes Temari’s first musical law:

> song can say “this resembles me” before speech can survive the admission.

## 3.2 `アイヴイ`: plurality as curation

The full-song source is visually static and should not be treated as an authored MV. The game 3DMV supplies the primary moving-image evidence:

- dark high-contrast space;
- black costume with metallic and cool-toned accents;
- angular bodily lines;
- sharp changes in silhouette;
- half-lidded, sideward, or direct gaze;
- gestures that cut, point, clench, or extend rather than merely plead.

This is not simply “Luna but colder.” The performance is more actively self-possessed. Temari’s plurality has become something that can be selected.

The commu makes the Producer’s gaze central. Temari wants to know what appears Temari-like to him; when the resulting image feels accurate, she responds with pleased recognition. The desired public image does not include every possible Temari equally. Its truth lies in **chosen selection**, not exhaustive disclosure.

That makes `アイヴイ` both an achievement and a power problem. Temari gains authorship by delegating part of the edit to the one observer whose understanding she trusts most.

## 3.3 `Unhappy Light`: softness without defeat

`Unhappy Light` is the strongest adversarial source in the baseline.

Its official MV leaves the arena for:

- a room and bed;
- alarm clock and small routine;
- pale natural light;
- window, dusk, and night;
- a cat;
- younger/child imagery;
- sleepiness, solitude, and unguarded domestic presence.

The full-mix signal profile is also the least spectrally bright/noise-like in the set: it has the lowest median spectral centroid and flatness among the measured works. That does not identify exact instrumentation, but it confirms that the contrast is not only visual.

This work prevents `青い炎` from becoming a totalizing one-mood theory. Temari can be made musically legible through low-pressure, ordinary, and vulnerable material when the exposure is not imposed as proof of weakness.

The ethical difference is authorship:

> being cared for against one’s chosen direction can feel like containment;  
> allowing quiet need to become one’s own image can feel like truth.

## 3.4 `一体いつから`: learning without self-erasure

The 3DMV uses luminous white, violet, and blue space, with alternating washed-out brightness and saturated depth. Temari’s body repeatedly moves between folded or inward poses and outward reach. The visual field is less abrasive than `アイヴイ`, but it does not become placid.

The commu clarifies the developmental change. Temari studies several expressive systems:

- China’s capacity to find and externalize small good things;
- Misuzu’s audience-oriented emotional delivery;
- her own tendency to pour everything into the performance;
- the Producer’s ability to distinguish and combine methods.

Earlier Temari experiences support or imitation as evidence that the self is not real. Here she can learn technique from others while preserving distinct purpose.

The supplied full-mix estimate is also one of the fastest in the corpus. The developmental song is not maturation through slowing down. It is greater expressive plurality at continued high momentum.

## 3.5 `ガラクタロード`: blue flame and public category authorship

`ガラクタロード` is prescribed material. Its analytical value lies in the fact that Temari must become legible inside a common compositional frame.

The 3DMV uses dark arena space, neon or cold light, close framing, centered control, and repeated movement from drawn-in posture toward open-arm command. The performance is less about inventing an entirely private world than about occupying a shared arena more intensely than others.

The commu gives the decisive formulation. Temari cannot recognize her many modes as one person until the Producer offers:

> `冷ややかな氷ではなく、超高温で完全燃焼する青い炎`

She accepts the image immediately because it explains both the appearance of control and the internal fact of excess. Then she turns private recognition into public project:

> if the world’s idea of coolness differs, she will make this version the real one.

The full-mix onset density is among the highest in the measured Temari set. More important than the number is the dramatic relation among concept, performance, and body: after insisting on total commitment, Temari is visibly exhausted. The category is made by expenditure.

This confirms `青い炎` as the mature organizing metaphor while preserving its danger.

# 4. Portability and range

## 4.1 `Campus mode!!` and `初`: institutional belonging

The bright, celebratory, school-centered visual fields of `Campus mode!!` and `初` test whether Temari remains legible without darkening common material.

She does. Her signature survives through:

- direct eye line;
- precise movement;
- strong phrase endings and embodied commitment at the full-mix level;
- a slight edge of challenge inside encouragement;
- disciplined formality in `初`.

Institutional belonging does not require self-erasure. Yet the meaning is different from the individual songs: Temari is entering a tradition whose vocabulary precedes her.

`Campus mode!!` is especially tied to Rinha, Kei, and school inheritance in the commu. The song is not merely happy common repertoire; it is a contested lineage through which Temari compares herself with teachers and predecessors.

## 4.2 `がむしゃらに行こう！`: awkward effort and peer comparison

The colorful classroom/arcade/pixel visual field gives Temari permission to be kinetic, comic, and visibly imperfect. Exaggerated movement and shared-song comparison make “coolness” less rigid.

The commu binds this to Saki and Kotone. Temari’s competitiveness and possessiveness remain, but the performance can be discussed through friendship, shared repertoire, and mutual observation rather than solitary image protection.

This is not a collapse of coolness. It shows that mature coolness can absorb embarrassment.

## 4.3 `ENDLESS DANCE` and `Howling over the World`: theatrical and aggressive expansion

`ENDLESS DANCE` uses a darker theatrical vocabulary—ornament, throne/card/heart-like imagery, black costume, sweeping sideward poses, and deliberate artificiality. Temari does not need naturalistic self-expression to remain truthful. Deliberate theater is compatible with the artificial-wings thesis.

`Howling over the World` turns multiplicity outward through glitch, duplicated image, cyan/red contrast, and large projected movement. Where `Luna` fragments Temari under difficult self-address, `Howling` makes duplication into attack.

Together these works show that the self can be authored through overt artifice rather than rescued from it.

## 4.4 `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！`: deliberate uncoolness

The novelty performance is visually hyper-bright, digitally cluttered, comic, and intentionally excessive. It contains the highest measured spectral centroid and flatness in the set, consistent with its bright/noisy full-mix profile.

Its importance is adversarial. If Temari’s coolness were only rigid image policing, this work would expose failure. Instead she performs the absurdity competently and intentionally.

The result is not that coolness disappears. It becomes elastic enough to choose uncoolness.

## 4.5 `叶えたい、ことばかり`: ordinary desire

The supplied source is a full-song object with static visual art, so no choreography or MV narrative claim is made. Its inclusion nevertheless matters as an affective counterweight: Temari’s repertoire can sustain ordinary wanting and softer orientation without treating them as regression.

## 4.6 `雨上がりのアイリス`: distributed focality

The Re;IRIS 3DMV is a separate `U1` object. It uses warm white/purple staging, rotating visual focus, shared gaze, proximity, and group formation. Temari remains distinctive without monopolizing the center.

The commu deepens the result. She listens, accepts mutual exchange, experiences embarrassment, and still reveals jealousy over Producer attention. Unit capacity and exclusive attachment coexist.

The correct conclusion is bounded:

> Temari can inhabit distributed focality without identity loss.  
> She has not thereby ceased to be possessive or rivalry-driven.

# 5. Performed speaking states

## 5.1 State model

The complete commu corpus supports seven recurrent performed states:

| code | state | characteristic function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `T-V0` | compact professional control | evaluation, post-loss judgment, planning, public composure |
| `T-V1` | abrasive/direct Temari | boundary, correction, rivalry, refusal of vague consolation |
| `T-V2` | childlike need/panic | attachment, fear of loss, embarrassment, pain, comic exposure |
| `T-V3` | combustive protest | expanded projection under anger, tears, or competitive challenge |
| `T-V4` | lowered intimate trust | origin shame, chosen dependence, direct request for closeness |
| `T-V5` | formal stage/public address | titleholder speech, professional gratitude, public authorship |
| `T-V6` | playful/cute performance | deliberate brightness, teasing, food comedy, accepted absurdity |

No state maps one-to-one onto truth or falsity. A high/mobile register may express pain, delight, anger, need, or chosen cuteness. A lower/compact register may express trust, professionalism, defeat containment, or suppression.

## 5.2 Early body shame and dangerous recognition — Dear 004

Dear 004 shows why the Producer’s support feels different from Misuzu’s. Temari’s physical animation moves rapidly among hands-on-hips defensiveness, turning away, hand-to-head strain, downcast shame, and renewed forward posture.

The crucial emotional event is not being told to rest. It is being authorized to work in a way that preserves the extraordinary upper range. Relief and danger are fused.

The scene supports both claims:

- the Producer understands what Temari wants from effort;
- his accuracy can validate self-endangerment.

## 5.3 Reconciliation and possession — Dear 009

The scene begins with stage focus, moves into close Misuzu intimacy, and later intensifies Producer exclusivity. Temari can apologize and accept continued relationship without restoring the old unit.

`一緒に破滅` and `私だけのプロデューサー` are delivered within a blend of comedy, embarrassment, desire, and actual dependency. The joking register reduces neither the attachment nor the warning.

The scene-level signal proxy becomes more compact in the exclusivity phase, with fewer detected pauses than the preceding Misuzu reconciliation. Because the measure is BGM-contaminated, the important use is comparative: the possessive declaration is not a long uncertain confession. Temari rapidly closes around it.

## 5.4 Hated prior self and artificial wings — Dear 010

Dear 010 is the clearest early `T-V4` scene. Temari’s posture and face soften, the address becomes searching, and the remembered child is named without immediate comic conversion.

The scene-level pitch proxy falls substantially during the hated-self disclosure compared with the earlier domestic triangle. It then remains relatively low through the artificial-wings promise. This supports the visible impression of lowered, deliberate origin speech, while remaining only a full-scene signal aid.

The key development is that the future command is built **after** the shame is spoken. The idol is not authenticated by proving the child unreal.

## 5.5 Rinha rupture — Dear 016

Dear 016 is the strongest proof that childlike collapse can communicate structural truth.

The cool persona gives way to agitation, lowered head, fist/hand tension, visible tears and flush, pleading, and eventual reassembly. The scene-level pitch proxy rises sharply in the breakdown/model-confession phase relative to the preceding controlled public mode.

What is revealed is not a secret non-idol essence. Temari names that Rinha helped author the idol she is trying to become. The rupture is filial and professional at once.

## 5.6 Misuzu as rival — Dear 019–020

These episodes are visually more horizontal and less physiologically crisis-driven than the early arc. Temari and Misuzu can smile, answer one another, and place hands toward the chest without immediately restoring caretaker/child roles.

Temari’s command that Misuzu become a rival is therefore not mere aggression. It is a request for a relationship in which both parties can set pace, refuse, and compete.

The later artificial-wing/top-Producer promise does not abolish dependence. It makes dependence part of a jointly escalated project.

## 5.7 Admiration, inferiority, and loss — Dear 022–023

After Misuzu’s performance, Temari appears in dark stage costume with increasingly constrained expression. Awe and injury coexist. Becoming Misuzu’s fan is not sentimental reconciliation; it is the admission that the former caretaker can produce a world in which Temari becomes spectator.

After Sena’s victory, Dear 023 is notably compact. Temari’s `負けは負けです` is not a dramatic collapse. The full-scene pitch proxy is among the lower values in the selected set, and the visual performance holds arms-crossed containment while the Producer carries more open frustration.

This is a new distribution of affective labor. Temari does not have to perform both the judgment and the explosion.

## 5.8 Airplane and rocket — Dear 027

The three-person Temari/Misuzu/Rinha staging shows recognition without restored unit fusion. The participants retain separate physical positions and identities.

The final flight metaphor rises from the more reflective recognition phase into renewed projection. The scene-level pitch proxy likewise rises in the airplane/rocket phase. Again, the number is not an isolated Temari measurement; it corroborates the visible shift from receiving history to launching the next form.

## 5.9 Chosen name, title, and present self — Dear 035–037

These scenes show the greatest integration of performed states.

Dear 035 allows comedy, embarrassment, rivalry, pleading softness, and determination to coexist. The request:

> `呼び捨てで、送り出してください`

turns given-name address into a chosen pre-performance ritual. Intimacy is requested, not merely extracted through breakdown.

Dear 036 moves into `T-V5`: composed titleholder exchange, public gratitude, acknowledgment of lineage, and then continued attack. Temari does not receive Prima Stella as a protected possession. She treats it as another launch platform.

Dear 037 returns to ordinary embodied space—night, food, teasing, folded arms, soft expression, direct gaze. Temari reads the Producer’s tears, lends a handkerchief, and says that today’s self and today’s Producer are the versions she likes most.

The grammar matters. `今日の私` and `いまのあなた` are present-tense affirmations, not timeless promises that the relationship can never change. This makes the scene more credible and less absolute than a totalized romantic closure.

The self-love is genuine. Its unconditionality remains unproven because it follows maximum success and immediately opens another goal.

# 6. Song commus: music as self-theory

## 6.1 `Luna say maybe`: ordinary friction becomes musical truth

The commu moves among classroom interaction, peers, calls, backstage anxiety, and live preparation. Temari cycles through challenge, self-consciousness, teasing, warmth, and stage focus.

This prevents the song from floating free as abstract mood. It emerges from failed ordinary communication and from other people recognizing resemblance before Temari can.

## 6.2 `アイヴイ`: being seen as edited self

Temari actively asks what appears Temari-like in the Producer’s perception. Her pleased `わかってるね` registers not passive objectification but recognition that the selection feels accurate.

The relational achievement is real: she can allow another person to edit public image without experiencing all editing as erasure.

The power concentration is also real: one Producer holds unusual authority over which Temari becomes visible.

## 6.3 `Campus mode!!`: lineage without disappearance

The commu links school repertoire to Rinha, Kei, fans, and inherited comparison. Temari can enter a song already saturated with other people’s meaning and still treat the performance as an opportunity to become legible.

This is one bridge from imitation shame to cited inheritance.

## 6.4 `雨上がりのアイリス`: listening and jealousy

The commu gives Temari a unit mode based on listening rather than fusion. She promises to hear the Producer, receives shared attention, and participates in relational warmth.

Yet jealousy and exclusive attachment remain visible. Unit belonging broadens her; it does not abolish the desire to be singularly chosen.

## 6.5 `がむしゃらに行こう！`: shared song, differentiated persons

Saki and Kotone provide a peer field in which Temari’s forwardness is comparable rather than isolated. The song can be common while the motives and bodily styles remain distinct.

The commu also lets possessive Producer language appear inside friendship and comedy, showing that exclusivity has become socially performable rather than only crisis speech.

## 6.6 `一体いつから`: technique can be borrowed without being stolen

The communication explicitly distinguishes expressive methods. Temari can study China’s small-good-things practice and Misuzu’s audience-oriented imagination without concluding that her own method is fraudulent.

The Producer’s role is to assemble conditions and assign differentiated help. Temari’s role is to choose and perform the resulting combination.

This is the mature artificial-wings principle at the level of technique.

## 6.7 `ガラクタロード`: private recognition becomes public manifesto

The commu moves from self-recognition crisis through the Producer’s `青い炎` formulation to deliberate category creation. Temari then repeats the conceptual model to others and commits to making it publicly true.

That sequence is central:

> concept received  
> → concept recognized  
> → concept performed  
> → concept claimed as public authorship.

# 7. Relationship revisions

## 7.1 Producer: from exclusive witness to co-author and risk concentrator

The AV evidence strengthens Temari’s agency in the Producer relationship.

She does not merely receive interpretation. She:

- asks how she appears;
- evaluates whether the image is accurate;
- requests intimate naming;
- uses his conceptual language and makes it public;
- notices his tears;
- returns care;
- insists on present-tense mutual liking;
- keeps naming the next goal.

This is genuine reciprocity.

It does not erase concentrated authority. The Producer controls many of the conditions under which Temari can continue combusting. Because he understands her better than most people, his interpretations may become unusually difficult to contest.

The mature relationship is best described as:

> **reciprocal co-authorship inside persistent institutional asymmetry.**

## 7.2 Misuzu: care becomes one technique among others

The AV corpus rejects both “Misuzu was simply toxic” and “Misuzu was simply correct.” Her knowledge of Temari’s body is materially real. The relational form made that knowledge oppressive because it fixed Temari as the dependent child and made Misuzu indispensable.

Later Temari can:

- become Misuzu’s fan;
- study her expressive method;
- ask her to become a rival;
- accept care without automatically restoring the unit;
- preserve conflict and difference.

Misuzu’s own core pass remains necessary before making a bilateral final judgment.

## 7.3 Rinha: origin can be cited
