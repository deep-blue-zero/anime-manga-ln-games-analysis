## Principal findings

1. Kotone’s stable AV identity is **performed reciprocity**, not one fixed “cute voice” or genre.
2. Cuteness functions as a relational interface: it can sell, thank, flirt, reassure, command, and distribute special attention.
3. Cute, blunt, anxious, competitive, intimate, and public registers are all real; switching is the stable trait.
4. `世界一可愛い私` integrates weakness, self-love, greed, and borrowed belief rather than opposing persona to authenticity.
5. `Yellow Big Bang！` establishes mutual audience energy: Kotone’s visible enjoyment is part of what the audience is owed, and audience response helps produce that enjoyment.
6. `Campus mode!!` turns fanservice into scalable recognition, with family love functioning as an affective starting point.
7. The self-esteem song confirms that confidence can be deliberately manufactured while leaving replacement anxiety and exclusivity unresolved.
8. `ふわふわ` makes rest, ordinary adolescence, and nonproductive interiority part of Kotone’s authored image.
9. Shared, inherited, dark, aggressive, novelty, and unit material supports a procedural/portable musical identity rather than a narrow genre essence.
10. Dear 025 demonstrates that professional reframing and grief coexist; neither exposes the other as false.
11. Dear 027 makes `普通` an economic, bodily, temporal, relational, and aesthetic achievement.
12. Re;IRIS confirms that Kotone can inhabit distributed focality without loss of identity.
13. The Producer relation is emotionally reciprocal but remains institutionally asymmetric and structured by debt/payment language.
14. Sena remains simultaneously fan object, elder-sister-like figure, summit, and reciprocal rival.
15. The exact Kotone `ガラクタロード` comparison remains open because no usable Kotone-specific rendered source was established.
