### Valid uses

- The corpus spans a broad full-mix field; no single narrow arrangement family explains Kotone.
- `Howling` combines the highest measured onset rate with a visibly aggressive field.
- `ミラクルナナウ` has the highest centroid and an unusually bright digital visual world.
- `ふわふわ` demonstrates that visual softness can coexist with a bright full mix.
- Foundational “cute” does not equal maximum spectral brightness.

### Invalid uses

- inferring isolated vocal brightness from mix centroid;
- treating absolute LUFS difference as performance intensity;
- equating onset rate with aggression without visual/contextual agreement;
- treating key/tempo estimates as stable where capture/arrangement ambiguity remains.
