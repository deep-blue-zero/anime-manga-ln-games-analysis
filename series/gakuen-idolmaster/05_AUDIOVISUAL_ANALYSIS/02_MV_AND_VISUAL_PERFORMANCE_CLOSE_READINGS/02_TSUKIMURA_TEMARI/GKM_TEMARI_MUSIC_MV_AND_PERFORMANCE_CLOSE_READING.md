# TSUKIMURA TEMARI — MUSIC, MV, AND PERFORMANCE CLOSE READING

## 0. Specialist thesis

Temari’s musical identity is not reducible to “cool songs,” one arrangement family, or one stable singing persona. Its durable feature is the conversion of emotional excess into **selected technical form**.

Her principal songs chart a change in who controls that selection:

- `Luna say maybe` allows a resemblance she cannot admit in speech to become audible;
- `アイヴイ` makes plurality an image curated through a trusted gaze;
- `Unhappy Light` grants private softness official representational status;
- `一体いつから` treats expressive methods as learnable and combinable;
- `ガラクタロード` turns the Producer’s `青い炎` description into a public artistic claim.

The mature signature is therefore neither coldness nor maximum loudness. It is **compressed overexposure**: the impression of control produced when high emotional pressure is routed through precise diction, direct address, bounded gesture, selected image, and deliberate stage form.

Common, inherited, novelty, and unit songs are indispensable because they show that this identity is portable. Temari remains legible in bright school pop, prescribed competition material, gothic theater, aggressive digital staging, comic excess, ordinary softness, and distributed unit focality. The `青い炎` metaphor is central, but it is not a genre prison.

# 1. Method and limits

## 1.1 Forms distinguished

The analysis distinguishes three source types:

**Authored song-specific MV**

A visual work capable of constructing a particular symbolic or narrative argument around the song. `Luna say maybe` and `Unhappy Light` are the clearest examples in this corpus.

**Game 3DMV/live performance**

A rendered performance source giving evidence about character model, bodily movement, camera relation, costume, audience address, and portability. It should not automatically be treated as a song-specific narrative text.

**Full-song/static visual source**

Useful for the complete audio object but not for moving-image interpretation. The supplied `アイヴイ` and `叶えたい、ことばかり` full sources belong here.

## 1.2 Signal evidence

The music metrics are `SIG`, derived from the complete mono full mix after conservative trimming. They include:

- estimated pulse/tempo;
- onset density;
- relative RMS dynamic span;
- median spectral centroid, bandwidth, rolloff, and flatness;
- pitch-class/key-profile estimate;
- twelve-bin section energy curve.

They are used comparatively. They do not identify singer-only range, precise orchestration, or mastering quality. Tempo and key estimates can select half/double or relative interpretations and are not treated as musicological transcription.

# 2. `Luna say maybe`: fragmented address

## 2.1 Authored MV

The official MV creates an aggressively mediated Temari:

- cyan, blue, white, and black dominate;
- the image is divided into panels and repeated portraits;
- lines, wires, diagrams, and typography surround or segment her;
- moon and night motifs place the self inside distant, cold-looking space;
- photographic/live-performance fragments are embedded in graphic construction;
- running or forward motion interrupts apparent stasis.

The palette can be read as “cool,” but the editing and multiplication make calmness impossible. The visual form is a person attempting to become addressable through repeated translation.

This is why the song’s indirectness matters. The commu establishes that Temari repeatedly denies resemblance to the stubborn, difficult girl while humming and performing the song. The MV does not reveal one hidden person behind the idol. It shows the labor of moving between incompatible self-descriptions.

## 2.2 3DMV

The 3DMV reduces graphic mediation and tests embodiment:

- dark-blue stage;
- solo isolation;
- frequent close camera relation;
- hand-to-chest and contained gestures;
- later outward extension;
- direct gaze that treats the camera as one addressee rather than a general crowd.

The contrast between forms is analytically productive. The official MV dramatizes fragmentation; the 3DMV demonstrates that a fragmented self can still inhabit a coherent body and complete a public performance.

## 2.3 Musical function

The two supplied versions have different durations and edits, so raw dynamics are not directly comparable. Their shared pulse estimate nevertheless indicates that the central difference is not basic tempo. The authored MV’s much lower onset density is partly an artifact of its extended visual/audio structure; the 3DMV concentrates the performance edit.

The important conclusion is literary and performative:

> `Luna say maybe` is not the song of a naturally cold girl. It is the song through which a girl who cannot admit resemblance in speech manages to address someone anyway.

# 3. `アイヴイ`: selected multiplicity

## 3.1 Static full source and 3DMV

The full-song source provides audio and cover design—blue-black portrait, distorted typography, scratch/streak texture—but not a moving narrative. The 3DMV supplies the central visual evidence:

- dark geometric stage;
- black costume and cool metallic accents;
- angular limbs and enlarged silhouette;
- half-lidded, sideward, and direct gaze;
- pointed, clenched, or cutting gesture;
- less visible pleading and more controlled confrontation than `Luna`.

Temari’s body no longer looks like an unstable image struggling to cohere. It looks like a deliberately edited weapon.

## 3.2 The commu and Producer gaze

The `アイヴイ` commu is a theory of public image. Temari asks what appears Temari-like to the Producer and becomes pleased when his selection feels accurate. Her `わかってるね` matters because it is evaluative: she is not passively accepting whatever he sees.

The work therefore stages a partial delegation of authorship:

- the Producer selects and frames;
- Temari judges whether the selection corresponds to a self she chooses to perform;
- the public receives the edited image;
- the private multiplicity remains real without requiring exhaustive display.

That is mature compared with her earlier fear that selection itself proves falsity. It also concentrates representational power in one relationship.

## 3.3 Sonic role

The full-song source has a comparatively broad measured dynamic span and high onset density. The 3DMV file includes a much larger apparent dynamic span because its stage edit contains near-silence/lead-in/out and should not be compared as though it were the same master.

The stable point is not a number. `アイヴイ` gives Temari’s “cool” image harder edges and greater bodily command without requiring emotional emptiness.

# 4. `Unhappy Light`: the necessary counterexample

The official MV’s domestic space is radically different from Temari’s arena image:

- bed, room, clock, and routine;
- pale teal, white, gray, and natural light;
- window, sunset, and night;
- cat and small-scale companionship;
- childhood/younger self imagery;
- sleepiness, solitude, crying, and unguarded presence.

The full-mix measurements reinforce the contrast. This is the least spectrally bright/noise-like object in the set by median centroid and flatness.

Its interpretive importance is not that it reveals “the real Temari” while stage songs lie. It establishes that quiet need can become *chosen public material*. Temari’s problem was never softness in itself. It was softness imposed on her as a fixed inferior identity.

`Unhappy Light` makes low-pressure exposure compatible with authorship.

# 5. `一体いつから`: expressive plurality

## 5.1 3DMV

The stage alternates luminous white and violet-blue depth. Temari’s costume and lighting produce division without the brutal black geometry of `アイヴイ`. Her movement alternates inward folding, kneeling or lowered posture, reaching, and renewed direct address.

This is not a simple visual reconciliation. The stage keeps different affective zones visible.

## 5.2 Commu

The communication explicitly turns other people into techniques she can learn from:

- China identifies and externalizes small good things;
- Misuzu imagines the audience and guides emotional delivery;
- Temari pours herself into the song at maximum force;
- the Producer diagnoses which method answers which problem.

Early Temari hears “you need other people” as “your wings are false.” Here she can accept differentiated help without surrendering authorship.

## 5.3 Musical role

Its high pulse estimate and moderate onset density matter because the developmental move is not toward stillness. Temari becomes more plural while preserving urgency.

The song’s place in the sequence is therefore:

> not less combustion, but more ways to shape it.

# 6. `ガラクタロード`: prescribed self-definition

## 6.1 Visual performance

The 3DMV uses:

- neon-black and cold arena light;
- centered framing;
- close-ups that emphasize held expression;
- movement from drawn-in posture toward open arms;
- direct command rather than dreamy withdrawal.

Because the work is prescribed, these features cannot be treated as wholly private composition. Their analytical value is comparative: Temari can occupy common material as a distinctive public argument.

## 6.2 `青い炎`

The commu begins with self-recognition failure. Temari cannot explain how cold, violent, needy, professional, and emotional states belong to one person. The Producer offers the blue-flame formulation. Temari’s response is immediate recognition.

The sequence then becomes artistic manifesto:

- receive the concept;
- recognize the self;
- perform it;
- repeat it to collaborators;
- promise to make the category socially real.

This is stronger than branding. Temari intends the performance to change what `クール` means.

## 6.3 Cost

The onset-density estimate is among the highest in the set. More importantly, the rendered commu shows exhaustion after total commitment. The blue flame is not just a visual metaphor for intensity; it is a production model that consumes bodily resource.

The song confirms the thesis and exposes the ethical bill.

# 7. Common and inherited repertoire

## 7.1 `Campus mode!!`

Bright multicolor staging, balloons/confetti, open smiles, and institutional celebration test whether Temari must darken every song to remain herself. She does not.

Her signature survives as directness, precision, and a trace of challenge within encouragement. The commu adds lineage: Rinha, Kei, former fans, and school history make the song an inherited comparison field.

## 7.2 `初`

White/gold formal staging, ornate star imagery, and symmetrical pose create ceremonial discipline. Temari is stately rather than icy. The song places her inside the school’s foundational narrative without erasing the fact that she approaches belonging competitively.

## 7.3 `がむしゃらに行こう！`

The game-like, colorful, comic world allows awkwardness and shared effort to remain visible. Temari’s performed coolness survives not by rejecting embarrassment but by choosing to inhabit it.

The commu’s Saki/Kotone context turns common repertoire into differentiated peer comparison. This is a social rather than solitary version of full commitment.

## 7.4 `ENDLESS DANCE`

The darker theatrical vocabulary—black costume, ornate stage, throne/card/heart-like motifs, sweeping gesture—tests overt artifice. Temari remains legible precisely because she treats theatrical construction as a resource, not a threat to truth.

The lower pulse estimate and higher spectral brightness/flatness than `Unhappy Light` distinguish it from quiet vulnerability. It is controlled theater, not domestic withdrawal.

## 7.5 `Howling over the World`

Glitch, duplicated Temari images, cyan/red contrast, and forceful projection turn multiplicity outward. The performance is less about containing emotion than weaponizing an authored image.

## 7.6 `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！`

The novelty field—chat/stream-like graphics, plush/cyber imagery, exaggerated choreography, high visual brightness—deliberately threatens rigid coolness. Temari performs the absurdity as a chosen challenge.

The highest centroid/flatness profile in the set matches the bright, noisy full-mix impression. The significance is not that this is secretly her preferred style. It is that the public image has become flexible enough to survive deliberate uncoolness.

## 7.7 `叶えたい、ことばかり`

Only a static full-song visual is supplied. It supports no choreography or MV symbolism claim. As an audio object, it expands the baseline toward ordinary desire and softer affect, preventing the individual-song map from becoming an all-crisis corpus.

---

# 8. Re;IRIS: unit performance

`雨上がりのアイリス` uses warm white-purple staging, rotating focus, proximity, and mutual gaze. Temari is visually distinct but not isolated.

The commu adds a behavioral principle: listening can become active reciprocity. Temari promises to hear rather than merely demand. Yet jealousy around Producer attention persists.

The unit work therefore confirms a capacity, not a total cure:

> she can share center and remain herself;  
> she still wants singular relational priority.

All such claims remain bounded to `U1`.

---

# 9. Musical identity verdict

Temari has recognizable recurring tendencies:

- direct address;
- high-pressure emotional material;
- selected cool-toned imagery in principal solos;
- precise, angular bodily authorship;
- conversion of contradiction into declared concept;
- willingness to use common material as a comparison field.

But her identity is broader than those tendencies.

The full corpus shows three compatible truths:

1. **She has a strong principal image-song family.** `Luna`, `アイヴイ`, `一体いつから`, and `ガラクタロード` form a developmental argument about indirectness, curation, technique, and category authorship.
2. **She has adversarial counterprofiles.** `Unhappy Light`, `叶えたい、ことばかり`, and `ミラクルナナウ` demonstrate quiet, ordinary, or comic states without identity collapse.
3. **She has performance portability.** School, prescribed, inherited, aggressive, theatrical, and unit works remain Temari-like through commitment, address, precision, and the tension between controlled form and excessive motive.

The final specialist formulation is:

> **Temari’s musical identity is the practice of making selected form bear more feeling than it appears able to contain. Her coolness is the visible compression of that pressure; her development consists in enlarging the form until inherited influence, domestic softness, comedy, shared center, and bodily cost can enter without being classified as fraud.**
