# ARIMURA MAO — MUSIC, MV, AND PERFORMANCE CLOSE READING

## 0. Method and source-form corrections

This document separates four forms:

- **song communication** — narrative/artistic negotiation;
- **authored official MV** — official symbolic/editorial interpretation;
- **3DMV/game performance** — rendered choreography, costume, crowd, and camera relation;
- **static full-song presentation** — audio only for analytical purposes.

Two corrections govern the pass:

1. `[学マス] 『Campus mode!!』 有村 麻央ver [親愛度10]` is static album art with a complete audio track, not a moving story-live scene.
2. `ガラクタロード` is an approximately 88-second A-ending/game performance. It is not a full-song substitute.

Source-level loudness and spectral descriptors are retained for reproducibility, but different mastering/capture chains prevent direct character-vocal rankings.

## 1. Musical thesis — performance becomes a refractive repertoire

Mao's music does not form a simple movement from cute song to cool song to prince song. The works assign different jobs to performance:

- `Fluorite` makes identity relational and refractive;
- `Feel Jewel Dream` makes coolness selectable;
- `Campus mode!!` makes inherited form transmissible;
- `SUGAR FLAVOR` makes partnership productive;
- `見て` makes the role archive answerable to a changing self;
- `ガラクタロード` tests whether the procedure travels into prescribed material.

> **The stable musical identity is not one genre. It is Mao's ability to treat role, costume, gesture, and audience address as selectable instruments without losing continuity.**

## 2. `Fluorite`

### 2.1 Communication

Mao first assumes authorship means mastering the song and making it hers. Rinami reframes `流れる` as acceptance of change. Mao then states that `ボクらしさ` is born among self, fans, juniors, peers, rivals, and Producer. The final light metaphor is explicitly relational: fluorite shines by receiving light.

### 2.2 Authored MV

The official MV works through:

- fall/float in a cosmic-aqueous void;
- crystal bloom and angular fragmentation;
- alternation among soft, school, and prince-coded Mao forms;
- reflections and doubled figures;
- hands separated by discontinuity;
- a late meeting of figures within a luminous field.

The image grammar does not move from broken false selves toward one intact essence. Refraction is the constructive operation.

### 2.3 3DMV

The rendered stage places Mao among crystal structures and an audience-light field. Open hands and direct reach repeatedly turn the song outward. Soft styling and controlled cool delivery coexist; the mismatch becomes a capacity for refraction rather than a gag.

### 2.4 Finding

> **`Fluorite` supplies Mao's musical ontology: continuity does not require sameness, and external light need not be external control.**

## 3. `Feel Jewel Dream`

### 3.1 Communication

Mao has already demonstrated cute and cool forms. She chooses an adult/stylish cool image and says her expressive range has expanded. The work therefore tests whether coolness can return without restoring compulsory rigidity.

### 3.2 Full mix

The static presentation supplies the complete track. Broad source metrics place it at the highest spectral-centroid proxy in the packet, consistent with a relatively bright-edged production profile. This describes the full mix, not Mao's isolated voice.

### 3.3 3DMV

The performance uses:

- black/navy glitter costume;
- jewel/crystal geometry;
- long body line and angular framing;
- finger-to-lips/face gestures;
- controlled close-ups and direct camera address;
- warm smiles within a cool visual system.

### 3.4 Finding

> **Cool is now a role Mao can select, enjoy, and leave. Its authority comes from choice, not from proving that cute Mao never existed.**

## 4. `Campus mode!!`

### 4.1 Communication

The song reactivates Mao's first Hatsuboshi hope. Institutional costume is altered for Mao rather than preserved as untouched relic. Teaching Kotone then changes inheritance into technique: intuitive actor metaphors must become concrete instructions.

### 4.2 3DMV

The vast multicolored stadium, star lights, mobile stage, crowdward reach, and visible delight place Mao inside collective repertoire. She does not require a private symbolic set to remain distinctive.

### 4.3 Static full-song presentation

The square source is complete audio with static art. It does not establish a second moving performance layer.

### 4.4 Finding

> **Mao becomes an inheritor by altering the form enough to inhabit it, and a senior by explaining the form well enough for someone else to alter it later.**

## 5. `SUGAR FLAVOR`

### 5.1 Communication

Old Mao rejected units because a partner could destabilize her image. Present Mao chooses Rinami, who witnessed the uncool self. Generic romance acting fails; the pair grounds the song in their actual three-year longing for idol performance.

### 5.2 Authored MV

The MV's major devices are paired differentiation:

- blue/purple versus red/pink spaces;
- separate rooms and phones;
- mirrors, doors, frames, and exchanged portraits;
- upside-down or split composition;
- parallel movement toward a shared night-stage image.

The pair is connected through editing while preserving two visual centers.

### 5.3 3DMV

Alternating focus, eye-line, hand contact, back-to-back and side-by-side positions, separate solos, and shared crowd address make relation visible as choreography. The size and silhouette difference between Mao and Rinami becomes compositional material.

### 5.4 Finding

> **The duet is not the dilution of Mao's image. It is evidence that a stable self can be made more legible by a second focality.**

## 6. `見て` / `Face Me`

### 6.1 Communication

The story arranges five states in sequence:

1. child `わたし` encounters the prince and wants the role;
2. nightmare Mao refuses princess casting and formerly loved cute objects;
3. present Mao approaches the transformed former prince;
4. grief becomes ownership of the painful years;
5. the rain scene converts princehood into chosen flirtatious address.

The work does not cure aspiration. It disentangles aspiration from compulsory form.

### 6.2 Authored MV

The official MV uses theatre seats, childhood toys, miniature stage, shadow audience, mirrors, mannequins, costume display, multiple Mao forms, the red prince, bouquet, and umbrella imagery. The role sequence accumulates rather than peels away.

The title's visual logic is therefore not “see the true face under costume.” It is “face the person who keeps authoring faces.”

### 6.3 3DMV

The red prince costume supplies maximal theatrical command—crystal stage, direct reach, strong line, crowd scale—while close-ups retain softness. The mature prince is visually strongest after the story has already shown fear, tears, and changed embodiment.

### 6.4 Finding

> **`見て` turns the prince from a fixed identity claim into a renewable address: look at what I choose now, and remain present as I change again.**

## 7. `ガラクタロード`

The short performance tests portability. Mao individualizes common material through rapid role-switching, clean gesture punctuation, strong/cute alternation, direct crowd timing, and actorly facial modulation. The stage is less symbolically bespoke than `Fluorite` or `見て`, which makes legibility more dependent on procedure.

The source is too short for exhaustive musical-form or full cross-idol ranking. Current status: **STRENGTHEN / OPEN**.

## 8. Supplemental communication controls

### `キミとセミブルー`

The work adds ensemble coordination, final-summer desire, and the difference between caregiving rationale and personal wanting.

### `雪解けに`

The communication combines cute craft, junior-facing prince language, inherited senior care, public minilive, and Producer intimacy.

### `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！`

The Osaka setting adds local speech, food/comedy, stage direction, child-actor memory, parental witness, and homecoming projection. It is the strongest control against overfitting Mao to one polished standard-Japanese prince mode.

## 11. Formal development model

Mao's musical development can be represented as six transformations:

1. **fixed image → refracted self** (`Fluorite`)
2. **compulsory cool → selected cool** (`Feel Jewel Dream`)
3. **received aspiration → transmissible craft** (`Campus mode!!`)
4. **image isolation → synchronized difference** (`SUGAR FLAVOR`)
5. **role cage → role archive** (`見て`)
6. **bespoke symbolism → portable procedure** (`ガラクタロード`)

None requires performance to disappear. Each increases Mao's authorship over what performance is doing.

## 12. Final musical formulation

> **Mao's musical identity is a refractive repertoire. She remains recognizable not because every song sounds or looks princely in the same way, but because she repeatedly turns role into deliberate address: to fans, inherited institution, partner, rival, parents, Producer, and the future self still ahead of her.**
