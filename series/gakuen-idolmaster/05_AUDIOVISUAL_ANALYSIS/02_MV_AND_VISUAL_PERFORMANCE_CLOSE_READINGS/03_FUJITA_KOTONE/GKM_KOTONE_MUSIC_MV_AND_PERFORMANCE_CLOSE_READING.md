# FUJITA KOTONE — MUSIC, MV, AND PERFORMANCE CLOSE READING

## 0. Purpose and conclusion

This document isolates the musical, visual, and embodied performance layer of Fujita Kotone’s Phase-3 audiovisual corpus.

Its main conclusion is:

> **Kotone’s musical identity is procedural rather than genre-bound. Across bright idol pop, crowd performance, soft illustrated interiority, dark theatrical space, cybernetic aggression, inherited repertoire, and unit staging, she remains recognizable through direct address, comic self-exposure, fast affective pivot, and the conversion of audience attention into visible pleasure.**

This is not the claim that she sounds identical in every song. The corpus argues the opposite. Her arrangements and visual worlds vary widely. Nor does it mean any performance becomes uniquely hers merely because her model occupies it. Several 3DMVs use shared choreography and camera systems.

The stable element is the relation she builds inside the material.

Kotone tends to make a song feel as though it is being **given to someone now**. Even when the aesthetic is abstract or aggressive, her performance remains oriented toward an addressee. When the work is cute, that address can be solicitous, excessive, or self-mocking. When the work is dark, it becomes challenge. When the work is inherited, it becomes gratitude that wants to circulate forward. When the work is shared, it becomes the ability to occupy center, partner, or ensemble without losing social legibility.

The resulting musical thesis is narrower than “she can sing anything” and broader than “she is the cute one”:

> **Kotone specializes in making authored material socially immediate.**

# 1. Method

## 1.1 Objects inspected

The corpus includes:

- three official authored 2D MVs: `世界一可愛い私`, `自己肯定感爆上げ↑↑しゅきしゅきソング`, `ふわふわ`;
- ten directly materialized Kotone 3DMV/performance sources: `世界一可愛い私`, `Yellow Big Bang！`, `Campus mode!!`, `自己肯定感爆上げ↑↑しゅきしゅきソング`, `がむしゃらに行こう！`, `ENDLESS DANCE`, `Howling over the World`, `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！`, `初`, and the shared Re;IRIS `雨上がりのアイリス`;
- two static/full-mix sources: `Yellow Big Bang！` and `The Cute!!!`;
- eight complete song commus;
- source metadata for the staged but non-materialized 4K `GO MY WAY!!` 3DMV.

## 1.2 Visual method

Visual claims distinguish:

- official-MV authorship;
- in-game embodied performance;
- character-specific facial/costume occupation;
- and reusable choreography/camera.

Contact sheets were used for whole-work structure, followed by full-motion inspection of the supplied files. Screenshots and source video are not redistributed.

## 1.3 Sonic method

Full-mix signal measurements record broad comparative properties:

- spectral centroid;
- onset/event rate;
- loudness range;
- level span;
- duration and source metadata.

They are used as constraints and comparisons, not as substitutes for musical interpretation. No isolated vocal stem was available. Absolute level differs by capture family and is not treated as a character trait.

### What the table supports

The range is the finding.

A narrow “cute = bright and busy” model fails. `世界一可愛い私` is less spectrally bright than several works that are not foundationally framed as her cute identity. `ふわふわ` is visually soft but sonically bright. `ENDLESS DANCE` is theatrically intense despite a lower onset estimate. `Howling over the World` aligns high event density with forceful visual movement. `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！` occupies the brightest measured full-mix field without exhausting Kotone’s idea of cuteness.

The metrics therefore strengthen the procedural model: Kotone does not rely on one arrangement family to remain legible.

# 3. `世界一可愛い私`

## 3.1 Narrative position

The song is the foundational attempt to give Kotone an image large enough to contain her contradictions.

She initially understands the song as catchy, call-friendly, and fun. The difficult section is not the boast. It is the claim that belief from another person can sustain action.

The commu resolves this by refusing purification. Kotone’s weak confidence, self-love, greed, royalties, and nervousness all belong.

## 3.2 Official 2D MV

The official MV uses a chibi/illustrated, yellow-blue-pink visual field. Kotone appears in classroom, idol, casual, and graphic forms rather than one continuous realistic body.

The recurring `存在か??` graphic is central. It turns a self-praise song into a comic ontological question:

> which Kotone exists, and can all of them exist together?

The MV answers through multiplication. It does not select the polished idol and discard the overworked student. The image keeps changing clothes, spatial context, and degree of caricature while preserving recognizability.

This is an authored visual equivalent of the commu’s argument: contradiction is not a defect to edit out before the slogan can be sung.

## 3.3 3DMV

The in-game performance brings room-like personal imagery onto the public stage:

- plush objects;
- hearts;
- vanity/bedroom-like props;
- saturated purple, pink, and yellow;
- direct frontal address;
- hand-to-ear invitation;
- and poses that alternate boast with visible softness.

The stage resembles an enlarged interior rather than a generic concert platform. Kotone does not abandon private need to become public. Private need becomes scenery.

## 3.4 Character conclusion

The song’s cuteness is not invulnerability. It is the ability to make insecurity enjoyable without pretending it is gone.

That is why commercial jokes do not rupture sincerity. The fantasy of sales and royalties is one of the things the song lets her celebrate.

# 4. `Yellow Big Bang！`

## 4.1 Developmental center

This is the work in which Kotone’s audience philosophy becomes explicit.

She searches for dissatisfied spectators because money and time create obligation. The identified flaw is not lack of technique but the disappearance of visible pleasure.

Her mature statement is that even when exhausted, depressed, or at the edge of crisis, she must find a way to enjoy singing and dancing or the result will not be a Kotone live.

The claim should not be moralized into “always smile through suffering.” The commu does not ask her to deny suffering. It identifies the audience relation as something deadened when technique becomes self-enclosed.

## 4.2 3DMV visual field

The performance uses:

- a large outdoor/festival crowd;
- handheld microphone;
- yellow/black/white graphic design;
- sporty costume and cap;
- direct eye contact;
- open-arm invitation;
- repeated crowd reverse shots;
- and wide frames that let the audience visibly surround the performer.

The camera alternates between close facial address and crowd scale. Kotone is not pictured as a distant object admired by anonymous masses. The work repeatedly shows the circuit between face and crowd.

## 4.3 Full-mix role

The supplied 3DMV source has one of the higher onset-rate estimates in the core set, consistent with outward propulsion and continuous event. The full-song source is denser/louder by mastering condition, so absolute level is not compared.

## 4.4 Character conclusion

`Yellow Big Bang！` transforms performance from self-display into **mutual ignition**.

Kotone’s strongest stage self appears when the audience can see that its response matters to her.

# 5. `自己肯定感爆上げ↑↑しゅきしゅきソング`

## 5.1 Official MV: self-esteem as variety machinery

The authored MV uses:

- extreme facial closeups;
- chibi/meme grammar;
- oversized text;
- quiz and variety-show frames;
- visual puns;
- rapid comic reclassification;
- and a willingness to make Kotone’s desire for praise look excessive.

Self-esteem is not represented as quiet inward discovery. It is an entertainment format.

This is not trivialization. Public form makes reassurance repeatable and shareable.

## 5.2 3DMV: collective love machine

The performance brings hearts, neon pink/yellow, vanity/bedroom-like objects, lightsticks, and a heart-centered podium into one stage field. Kotone kneels, sprawls, recoils, poses, and exposes comic bodily unguardedness rather than sustaining elegant distance.

The crowd’s participation is part of the apparatus. The performance does not merely say “love yourself.” It organizes a situation in which affirmation is thrown at the performer and returned.

## 5.3 The commu’s ethical pressure

The originating anxiety is replacement by a younger sister. The Producer responds with exclusivity. Kotone then amplifies the reassurance into romance and financial fantasy.

The song therefore reveals both sides of relationally produced confidence:

- another person’s love can genuinely enlarge agency;
- the need for absolute non-replacement can make confidence fragile.

## 5.4 Character conclusion

This is Kotone’s most explicit performance of self-esteem as production technology. The song validates manufacture while leaving dependence open.

# 6. `Campus mode!! [藤田ことね]`

## 6.1 Institutional song, personal function

`Campus mode!!` is shared institutional repertoire. The character-specific question is how Kotone makes the common form hers.

The commu gives the answer: special attention at scale.

## 6.2 3DMV

The staging uses a large arena, catwalk/rail relation, salute-like address, frontal thanks, open arms, and future-oriented brightness. The movement from close face to institutional scale is especially appropriate for Kotone’s development from private rescue toward public profession.

## 6.3 Character-specific claim and limit

The choreography cannot be treated as uniquely hers without other versions. What can be claimed is that the commu aligns the shared form with her established social skill: making many recipients feel the concentrated force of one-to-one recognition.

The family medal functions as affective recharge. Love received in a specific relation becomes energy she can distribute across the hall.

## 6.4 Character conclusion

Kotone personalizes institutional aspiration as a promise of address:

> no matter how large the venue becomes, attention should not become faceless.

---

# 7. `White Night! White Wish!`

## 7.1 No separate performance source in the active packet

The song commu is the evidentiary center. Visual narrative claims are therefore limited to the rendered communication.

## 7.2 Ordinary Christmas as missing repertoire

Kotone recognizes that romance and ordinary youth do not easily fit her lived schedule. The outing becomes “practice” for experience she may need as an idol.

That professional rationale protects a simpler truth: she wants the experience.

## 7.3 Material restraint

She resists expensive consumption and values the handmade gift. Her response does not abandon price awareness. It shows price awareness functioning as care: she wants intimacy that does not create an impossible obligation.

## 7.4 Character conclusion

The work places ordinary adolescent life inside artistic development. Kotone’s future cannot be healthy if professionalism consumes every experience from which performance might draw meaning.

---

# 8. `ふわふわ`

## 8.1 Why it matters

`ふわふわ` is the strongest formal counterexample to a model of Kotone built only from high-energy cuteness and crowd command.

## 8.2 Official MV

The MV uses pink line-art domesticity, couch and phone, drifting clouds, lying down, falling/sleeping, weightlessness, and eventual return toward school.

The image is intimate without requiring romantic exclusivity. It lets the body stop being productive.

## 8.3 Sonic/visual tension

The full mix has a comparatively high spectral-centroid estimate despite the visual softness. This is useful negative evidence: softness is not the absence of brightness or event. It is a different organization of attention.

## 8.4 Character conclusion

The MV makes unprofitable interiority visible. For a girl whose early body is a labor ledger, that is a substantive expansion of self-authorship.

---

# 9. `がむしゃらに行こう！ [藤田ことね]`

## 9.1 Peer comparison

The commu asks Kotone to stand beside Saki and Temari without borrowing one total identity from either.

She studies what their strengths do, then lets comparison change her performance without becoming imitation.

## 9.2 3DMV visual form

The stage uses smartphone/interface framing, classroom-social imagery, varsity-red costume, and comic effort. The performance looks shareable: exertion and awkwardness are already content rather than rehearsal debris hidden before publication.

## 9.3 “Making any song cute”

Saki’s phrase is best understood as transformation capacity. Kotone does not necessarily make every song pastel or sweet. She makes it socially immediate, readable, and directed.

## 9.4 Comparative function

Because Kotone’s `ガラクタロード` source is unavailable, this song becomes the strongest direct shared-material test presently available. It does not replace `ガラクタロード` work-for-work; it answers the broader portability question.

# 10. `GO MY WAY!! [藤田ことね]`

## 10.1 Inherited repertoire

Kotone enters the song as a fan and potential contributor to tradition. She is embarrassed to request the repertoire directly, so the Producer’s curation mediates desire.

## 10.2 Recognition from the predecessor

The request to sing `世界一可愛い私` returns the fanservice logic toward Kotone. An admired earlier idol gives her a concentrated signal of recognition.

## 10.3 Future reciprocity

Kotone wants 765PRO idols someday to sing her songs. Inheritance therefore points forward.

## 10.4 Visual limit

The 4K 3DMV is staged and identified but was not materialized because of transfer size. No fine-grained camera or gesture claim is made here.

## 10.5 Character conclusion

Kotone does not honor tradition by shrinking. She honors it by imagining herself becoming useful to it.

---

# 11. `The Cute!!!`

## 11.1 Source boundary

The supplied object is a static-cover full-mix source. It supports musical comparison, not choreography or authored visual interpretation.

## 11.2 Comparative function

The track occupies a comparatively bright full-mix profile with lower onset density than several stage-oriented works. That combination complicates the assumption that “cute” must mean constant rhythmic event.

## 11.3 Character conclusion

The title confirms that cute remains an active later category. The wider corpus prevents the category from becoming a prison: by this point, “cute” includes self-conscious branding, relation, range, and the ability to return from darker or softer forms without contradiction.

---

# 12. `ENDLESS DANCE [藤田ことね]`

## 12.1 Theatrical abstraction

The 3DMV uses dark geometry, skewed architecture, frames/mirrors, green-magenta-red lighting, and giant-eye imagery. Kotone’s ordinary comic/social world is absent.

## 12.2 Role occupancy

She sustains controlled distance and stylized menace without repeatedly reassuring the viewer through ordinary sweetness.

The continued direct gaze matters. Relation remains, but invitation becomes surveillance and challenge.

## 12.3 Character conclusion

Kotone can inhabit an abstract role without needing the role to resemble her biography. This is professional range, not evidence that the dark image is a truer self.

---

# 13. `Howling over the World [藤田ことね]`

## 13.1 Visual field

Cyberpunk screens, red/blue duality, glitch, chains, violent directional movement, and solitary scale place Kotone in a high-pressure confrontational environment.

## 13.2 Signal relation

The supplied 3DMV has the highest onset-rate estimate in the measured set. The metric does not prove aggression, but it agrees with the visible event density and hard movement.

## 13.3 Character consequence

Kotone’s directness becomes force rather than solicitation. The viewer is still addressed, but not comforted.

This is one of the strongest pieces of counterevidence against defining her through sweetness alone.

---

# 14. `ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！ [藤田ことね]`

## 14.1 Digital novelty

The performance uses pixel, toy, pastel overload, digital/chibi crowd, bright color collision, and internet-native novelty grammar.

## 14.2 Signal relation

It has the highest spectral-centroid estimate in the set, broadly consonant with the bright electronic visual world.

## 14.3 Character consequence

This is cuteness optimized for circulation rather than domestic intimacy. Kotone’s quick social reading and willingness to make excess entertaining fit the form naturally.

---

# 15. `初 [藤田ことね]`

## 15.1 Foundational institutional baseline

The 3DMV places Kotone inside an orthodox school-idol performance form: clear stage, blue/light costume field, purple/gold crowd light, and shared institutional uplift.

## 15.2 Limit

The choreography and camera are common enough that character-specific uniqueness should not be overstated. The source level also differs markedly from the rest of the set.

## 15.3 Character consequence

Its value is baseline. Kotone can occupy conventional idol brightness without requiring novelty gimmick or biographical crisis to remain present.

---

# 16. `雨上がりのアイリス` / Re;IRIS

## 16.1 Distributed focality

The 3DMV repeatedly circulates center among Saki, Kotone, and Temari. Triangular staging, paired approach, individual closeups, and a shared ending prevent any one member from owning the entire visual field.

## 16.2 Kotone’s position

Kotone receives independent framing and participates comfortably in transitions between center and partner. She does not disappear when focality moves away.

Her visual function is not reducible to “middle” or “cute member.” The performance shows social continuity: she can hand attention onward and receive it back.

## 16.3 Commucation context

The dream/unit commu gives Re;IRIS emotional weight as a possible deeper friendship state. It also distinguishes the actual Producer through concrete material history.

## 16.4 Character consequence

Re;IRIS confirms distributed-center capacity. Whether Kotone is the unit’s principal mediator remains unproven and belongs to later ensemble synthesis.

# 18. Final music/MV thesis

Kotone’s musical identity is not a stable genre waiting to be named.

It is a stable method of relation.

She makes songs personal by showing what is at stake in being watched. Sometimes the stake is money. Sometimes it is proof that she cannot be replaced. Sometimes it is the audience’s ticket. Sometimes it is ordinary youth. Sometimes it is the chance to stand beside Saki and Temari without becoming secondary. Sometimes it is the wish that an inherited tradition will one day inherit something from her.

Across those settings, she remains recognizable because she does not treat performance as unilateral display.

She looks for the return signal.

Her finest performances make the audience aware that its answer changes the idol—and make that change part of what the audience came to receive.
