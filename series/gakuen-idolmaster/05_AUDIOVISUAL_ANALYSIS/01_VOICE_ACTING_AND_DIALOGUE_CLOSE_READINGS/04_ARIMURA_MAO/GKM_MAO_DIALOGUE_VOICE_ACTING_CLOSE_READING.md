# ARIMURA MAO — DIALOGUE AND VOICE-ACTING CLOSE READING

## 0. Scope and limits

This specialist document models Mao's performed speech and acting across Dear 001–037, the 010-01 bridge, and all eight staged `cidol` communications. Exact Japanese wording and route logic remain controlled by Source Lock 1.0. The AV sources supply pauses, interruption, gaze, posture, gesture, reaction timing, projection, and relation-specific performance.

No source is an isolated Mao vocal stem. Numerical scene probes contain BGM, other speakers, effects, and cuts. They are used only after a state has been identified by text and direct acting.

## 1. Voice thesis — permeable theatrical poise

Mao's stable voice identity is not one pitch range or one “ikemen” timbre. It is **permeable theatrical poise**:

> **Mao habitually gives speech a composed, courteous, stage-readable shape. Early on, she treats that control as necessary proof that the prince still exists. Later, embarrassment, grief, Kansai play, intimacy, and tears can interrupt the shape without invalidating it. She becomes more recognizably princely because she no longer requires princeliness to sound emotionally uniform.**

The key variable is **governance of the scene**. Prince Mao tends to complete lines, orient others, and hold the encounter together. Threatened Mao loses that governance through stammer, repetition, reaction, contraction, or silence. Mature Mao can permit the loss, receive help, and resume without rewriting the interruption as failure.

## 2. Baseline register — courteous senior and ordinary prince

Across early Dear and song communications, Mao's default combines:

- `ボク` self-reference;
- polite but warm address to Producer;
- slightly ceremonious care toward juniors;
- presentational gestures and upright posture;
- calm line endings and controlled smiles;
- readiness to escort, teach, protect, or organize.

This state is not mere theatre. The umbrella, dorm work, consultation, and junior care show continuity between speech and conduct. Yet it can hide desire. In `キミとセミブルー`, Mao first frames the event as service to the dorm; only after the event exists does she admit wanting a memory from her own final summer.

## 3. Body/category rupture — the prince loses scene control

Dear 003 provides the clearest early contrast. Mao narrates childhood stage desire with relative composure. As the account reaches feminizing puberty, chest, height, and lost boy/prince casting, the performed state becomes more inward: lowered gaze, hands near the body, visible embarrassment, and interrupted delivery. The Producer's `可愛い` diagnosis produces escalation rather than one simple blush.

The point is not that a high or soft voice is “the real Mao.” The point is that the category threat disrupts the temporal procedure by which Mao normally remains legible to herself.

The mixed-source proxy for the body-rupture window shows wider/high-end pitch activity than the calmer origin window, but this is only consistent with the visibly intensified state. It is not an isolated acoustic measurement.

## 4. Cute embarrassment — from defeat alarm to playful permeability

Early cute embarrassment has three components:

1. **category alarm:** cute implies that the former prince body/category is gone;
2. **social exposure:** another person sees preferences Mao has disciplined out of view;
3. **ordinary shyness:** praise and intimacy affect her regardless of theory.

These components separate over time. Dear 004 prevents a generic cute role from replacing the prince. Dear 010 lets horror and hand-holding expose vulnerable preference. `雪解けに` shows embarrassment at `麻央ちゃん先輩`, delight in cute craft, and intimate dependence without collapse. `Feel Jewel Dream` then lets Mao choose cool styling after cuteness has ceased to be disqualifying.

Mature cute reaction is therefore not a healed absence of embarrassment. It is embarrassment that no longer decides the ontology of the role.

## 5. Chosen prince register — command after admission

Dear 015 marks the decisive change. Mao names the old prince relation as `呪い / 呪縛`, then claims the continuing aspiration as `ちゃんと本物`. The declaration moves from reflective admission into opponent-facing force. The role is not defensively maintained by refusing its history; it is chosen after the history has been spoken.

The later red-prince states in `見て`, Dear 036, and Dear 037 inherit this structure:

- theatrical diction remains;
- the body occupies strong line and central space;
- direct address gains force;
- but fear, tears, blush, and need have already been permitted elsewhere in the same performance system.

The late prince voice is therefore more—not less—credible because it is not the only voice Mao can use.

## 6. Failure and grief — recommitment does not cancel breakdown

Dear 027 is the best test of emotional honesty. Mao can publicly name the skill gap, announce winter victory, accept Sena's challenge, moderate the party, encourage others, and smile. None of that is fake. The inner monologue and later collapse with Rinami show that it is incomplete.

Mao's crying does not reveal the composed public register as a lie. It shows two true timescales:

- professional/senior behavior can resume quickly;
- emotional recovery can lag behind behavior.

The next morning's renewed planning therefore should not be read as instant reset. The route makes the crying part of persistence.

## 7. Producer-directed register — coauthorship, testing, and flirtation

Mao's Producer register develops across four functions.

### Professional deference and testing

Early Mao asks how he intends to produce her and tests whether he will repeat prior adult demands. Politeness coexists with resistance.

### Recognition and dependence

Dear 025 makes the relation vocational. Mao chooses the Producer because he saw conduct, researched history, and became the first fan. The register is deliberate and argued, not merely romantic surrender. The dependency risk remains because the person who helps define Mao becomes difficult to replace.

### Grief and care

In Dear 027, long phone contact follows the collapse. Dear 037 reverses care: Mao offers a handkerchief, then cries herself. Mutual feeling appears without dissolving the professional project.

### Deliberate flirtatious princehood

The rain scene in `見て` is overtly authored: Mao pretends to have forgotten her umbrella, asks to be escorted, calls herself a rain-drenched prince, tests whether he has fallen again, and promises to make him fall further. This is performance and sincere desire at once.

> **Finding:** Mao's most intimate register does not abandon theatricality. It uses theatricality to make desire speakable.

## 8. Rinami-directed register — witness without fixed definition

Rinami receives ordinary softness, historical shorthand, teasing, dependence, and peer challenge. She is the person Mao chooses for `SUGAR FLAVOR` because she has watched the uncool Mao.

The communication includes a prior refusal: old Mao feared that standing beside taller Rinami would make the prince image fail. Present Mao asks Rinami to help draw out each other's strengths. The shift is audible/visible as reduced image defensiveness and greater willingness to pause for correction.

The duet's emotional solution is also relational. Generic love acting sounds insufficient inside the story; shared idol longing supplies the real affect.

## 9. Junior and care register — orientation can become hierarchy

Mao often speaks to juniors through composed reassurance, instruction, and presenting language. `Campus mode!!` exposes the limit of intuitive authority. Her metaphorical direction—“like a hummingbird,” “express the spirit”—does not help Kotone until she converts it into specific body instructions.

That conversion is important for voice modeling. Mature authority is less ornamental and more actionable. It can also become reciprocal: Kotone's difficulty teaches Mao what she knows.

`雪解けに` and `キミとセミブルー` show a second limit: service language can delay self-disclosure. Mao is more practiced at saying what others need than what she wants.

## 10. Osaka/Kansai register — local memory and comic de-formalization

`ミラクルナナウ(ﾟ∀ﾟ)！` adds a high-value state that the Dear spine could not supply. Mao deliberately performs Kansai speech, then demands the missing comic response. Later `そんなに泣いてへんし` slips out when the shopkeeper exposes childhood dependence.

The communication cycles among:

- food pleasure;
- local comic performance;
- stage-direction expertise;
- child-actor shame and recognition;
- filial vulnerability;
- high-projection homecoming speech.

Kansai register is neither a permanent secret dialect nor a joke detached from identity. It carries locality, family, childhood celebrity, and the embarrassment of being known before the current prince image stabilized.

## 11. Public/fan register — from display to orientation

`Fluorite` changes the audience from evaluator to participant in `ボクらしさ`. `Campus mode!!` changes inherited song from object of admiration to a form Mao can pass forward. Dear 036 changes Prima Stella from exclusive trophy into a personal ethic of visible orientation.

Mao's public register is more formal than Sumika's colloquial address and less absolute than Sena's sovereign command. It uses gratitude, stage clarity, metaphor, and invitation. The red prince costume and formal speech do not make the moment inauthentic; they make the ethical claim legible at crowd scale.

## 13. Acoustic measurement discipline

Mixed-scene proxies are retained because they provide reproducible support for broad state contrast. They do not isolate Mao. Examples:

- body/category rupture shows broader high-end activity than calm origin narration;
- summer defeat and Producer-choice windows have lower mixed-source medians than several Osaka, flirtation, and `0番` windows;
- Osaka play and late future address show wide activity consistent with visibly expansive performance.

BGM, other speakers, and effects make exact F0, breathiness, resonance, and timbre claims invalid. The canonical model therefore rests on direct acted-state review plus Japanese wording, with metrics subordinate.

## 14. Final voice formulation

Mao should not be reconstructed as “always low, cool, princely” with occasional comic breaks. That would reproduce the early defensive model.

A better reconstruction rule is:

> **Begin from composed, courteous, stage-readable poise. Then ask what the relationship and threat do to Mao's control of time. Praise may contract her; a junior may activate orientation; a rival may sharpen command; Rinami may permit unguarded history; Osaka may de-formalize her; grief may stop the scene; Producer intimacy may turn the prince into deliberate flirtation. Mature Mao can return to poise without treating the detour as failure.**

That is **permeable theatrical poise**: a prince voice strengthened by its capacity to admit other voices.
