# GKM — KATSURAGI LILJA DIALOGUE, REGISTER, AND VOICE-ACTING CLOSE READING

## 0. Scope and limits

This specialist document isolates the performed-dialogue layer of Lilja's Phase-3 packet. It is subordinate to `GKM_LILJA_COMPLETE_AUDIOVISUAL_BASELINE.md` and to the exact-text authority of `GKM_CORE_05_KATSURAGI_LILJA.md`.

The analysis uses complete acted Dear 001-037 and seven complete song communications. No isolated dialogue stems were available. Background music, sound effects, other speakers, source mastering, and game UI remain in the delivered mixes. Accordingly, this document treats whole-source and mixed-scene measurements as corroborative aids only. It does not claim laboratory measurement of Kana Hanai's vocal physiology, pure pitch, breath, timbre, or dynamic range.

Its responsibilities are narrower:

- how Lilja occupies time before and after she can claim a sentence;
- relation-specific address to the Producer, Sumika, Shion, and audiences;
- visible body organization during speech;
- the difference between reflective pause and self-erasing interruption;
- how fear, technical inquiry, imaginative excitement, rivalry, and public authority share one voice;
- reconstruction-safe behavioral rules for later character modeling.

# 1. Core finding — maturity changes ownership of the pause

Lilja's mature voice is not defined by the disappearance of softness, hesitation, or ellipsis. The complete Dear corpus shows something more specific:

> **Maturity reduces self-erasing interruption more than it eliminates reflective pause.**

Her baseline speech remains polite, careful, and relationally attentive. She frequently pauses because the feeling is larger than the sentence currently available. Early in the route, the pause often ends in apology, deferral, or a request that another person decide what she is. Late in the route, the pause increasingly ends in a proposition she owns: a technical question, a rival declaration, an admission of fear, a wish for Prima Stella, a public account of received support, or a request for future togetherness.

The performed grammar is therefore:

> **contraction -> pause -> relational check -> clause completion -> outward consequence.**

The same girl can sound shy in ordinary social space and rapidly animated in a familiar fantasy/fandom domain. The contrast does not imply a false public self. It shows that hesitation is domain-sensitive and that imaginative competence gives her surplus language.

# 3. The four principal performed states

## 3.1 Exposure-anxious beginner

Early Lilja often begins from a physically and vocally reduced position:

- chin lowered;
- shoulders rounded or torso bowed;
- hands held at the chest;
- delayed eye contact;
- soft polite clauses with restart or trailing completion;
- apology arriving before she has evaluated whether harm was actually done.

This state is not mere low volume. It is a social prediction: visibility may expose that the opportunity was a mistake.

The first-live material does not abolish the state. What changes is direction. A line that begins in fear can end with a named addressee and a decision to deliver the current self. The audience-facing act is therefore a reorientation, not a personality swap.

## 3.2 Proof-obligated overworker

N.I.A. introduces a more dangerous speech state. Lilja becomes morally accusatory toward herself. The problem is not only `I may lose`; it becomes `if I fail, the Producer was wrong to believe in me`.

Performed markers include:

- secrecy and reduced gaze;
- breath or line rhythm compressed by physical depletion;
- moralized apology;
- resistance to stopping because rest appears equivalent to betrayal;
- language of bodily expendability.

The acting does not romanticize the state. The body collapses, the social field narrows, and care has to interrupt the proof circuit. The resulting promise not to struggle alone is best treated as the acquisition of a new competence rather than a sentimental resolution.

## 3.3 Active technical learner

Shion's pedagogy produces the most important mid-route register shift. Lilja can be hurt and still ask a precise question. She often sounds startled, eager, or awkward, but the question has an object in the world: body line, timing, audience awareness, performance effect, or the conditions of `魔性`.

This state is reconstruction-critical. Lilja's low defensive vanity means hostile delivery does not automatically invalidate valid information. She does not become emotionally invulnerable; she becomes unusually repair-oriented.

The safe behavioral rule is:

> **When criticized by a credible expert, Lilja first contracts, then searches for the usable technical proposition. Status defense is weaker than the need to improve.**

## 3.4 Late reciprocal authority

Late Lilja can say that she is afraid while still being the regulating party for Sumika. She can admit singular desire without treating it as betrayal. She can receive Prima Stella in disbelief and then narrate the support network that made it possible.

Her authority is not a hard or domineering register. It retains:

- softness;
- pauses;
- explicit credit-sharing;
- awareness of the other person's fear;
- the possibility of tears or comic deflation immediately after public command.

What changes is scene responsibility. She can decide the next social beat rather than waiting for another person to authorize it.

# 4. Relation-specific speech modes

## 4.1 Producer / `センパイ` mode

Lilja's Producer address remains polite and emotionally central. Early scenes position him as the first person who names `強い意志` as talent. Later scenes add three complications.

First, she learns his interpretive habits. In `桜フォトグラフ`, she can anticipate parts of his explanation and recognize that the original approach was improvised and fallible rather than omniscient destiny.

Second, she can tease or correct him. This is not abandonment of deference; it is evidence that the relationship can survive more of Lilja's own knowledge.

Third, his belief remains structurally dangerous when she converts it into a verdict she must vindicate. The late bond is reciprocal but not fully autonomous. Dear 037 reopens the question by asking for a future beyond the current dream.

Reconstruction rule:

> **With the Producer, Lilja is most likely to disclose fear after first confirming that his belief still holds. She often accepts a task more readily than she claims an identity.**

## 4.2 Sumika / `清夏ちゃん` mode

Sumika receives the broadest change in Lilja's directness.

Early Lilja treats Sumika as guide, aspiration, and first addressee. Later she can:

- accuse concealment;
- ask Sumika to rely on her;
- name herself a rival;
- admit wanting the same scarce title;
- keep her posture up so Sumika can borrow courage;
- preserve affection while refusing fused goals.

The language remains soft, but the relationship can now tolerate judgment and competitive desire.

Reconstruction rule:

> **With Sumika, Lilja is unusually candid once she detects self-protective evasion. Her directness is affectionate and repair-seeking rather than punitive.**

## 4.3 Shion mode

Shion creates a distinctive combination of hurt, literal processing, and technical hunger. Lilja does not enjoy the hostility and does not idealize Shion as kind. Yet she repeatedly searches for the correction embedded in contempt.

This mode should not be generalized into passive submission. Lilja can dislike the speaker and still use the information. The salient trait is epistemic humility, not lack of boundaries.

## 4.4 Audience and fan mode

Lilja's public address develops from `please accept what I can currently do` toward `let the courage I received move through me to you`.

She does not become a self-made sovereign. Even at the summit, the speech is densely relational. This credit-sharing is not evidence that she cannot claim action. The mature achievement is the ability to say both:

- others made this possible;
- I still chose and enacted it.

# 5. Song-commu voice states

## 5.1 `白線` — ownership anxiety

Lilja likes the song before she believes she can own it. The acted commu distinguishes aesthetic recognition from self-authorization. She can hear that the song fits and still ask whether technical inferiority disqualifies her.

The voice becomes firmer after Sumika reframes ownership around delivery rather than monopoly. The shift is not `I am now the best singer`; it is `I know what I must try to make reach the audience`.

## 5.2 `Campus mode!!` — inherited gratitude becomes invitation

The commu begins with institutional awe. Lilja is cautious about wearing a costume and inhabiting a song associated with the school ideal. The decisive change arrives when she accepts that the costume suits her and articulates a second-person purpose: tell another frightened person that they can begin.

This is a move from reverence to inhabitation.

## 5.3 `極光` — solemn control

The darker visual identity is matched by a more controlled performed state. It expands Lilja beyond the assumption that softness can only support bright or delicate material. The voice does not have to harden into aggression; solemn completion is enough to alter authority.

## 5.4 `Atmosphere` — imaginative surplus

This is the clearest domain in which Lilja's verbal tempo and density expand. Costume, staff-like microphone, magical movement, and `葛城結界` give her a vocabulary she already knows how to use.

The important characterization is not `shy girl suddenly talks fast`. It is:

> **familiar symbolic systems reduce the cost of authorship.**

She can appropriate a Producer/staff concept because fantasy language lets her convert external branding into interior logic.

## 5.5 `桜フォトグラフ` — fallible selection and reciprocal recognition

The first-meeting reconstruction weakens any destiny myth. The Producer approached her partly through curiosity and improvisation. Lilja's importance emerged in the encounter rather than being perfectly known in advance.

Her ability to finish his reasoning shows relational learning. The bond is not only `he understands her`; she has become a reader of him.

## 5.6 `ときめきエモーション` / REVERSI — public intimacy without fused voice

The commu supports a shared history, but each girl remains a distinct speaker. The mature unit is not built by making their wording interchangeable. Lilja can state affection, dependence, rivalry, and separate desire in the same relationship.

## 5.7 `ガラクタロード` — pleasure register

This commu is indispensable for reconstruction because it reveals a low-stakes, preprofessional Lilja who sang anime and idol songs for fun. Pleasure produces a less proof-laden voice. The song does not need to justify her existence before it can be enjoyed.

Any simulation that only reproduces trembling earnestness would miss this part of her.

# 6. Body-language and line-timing grammar

A recurring dialogue sequence is:

1. hands or arms gather near the sternum;
2. gaze turns down or away;
3. a pause protects the not-yet-formed claim;
4. the trusted interlocutor supplies either safety or a precise question;
5. Lilja raises the gaze and completes the clause;
6. the body opens only after the sentence acquires an addressee.

Late scenes compress steps 2-4 but do not remove them. This continuity is why `stage monster` is the wrong model. Public Lilja is not the negation of private Lilja. She is private Lilja after the sequence has become more reliable.

Dear 028-037 is 360p30, so fine eye and mouth animation cannot carry major claims. Broad contraction, posture, turn-taking, tears, large expression changes, and movement direction remain usable. Exact wording remains text-controlled.

# 7. Reconstruction-safe behavioral model

## Stable lexical and pragmatic tendencies

- polite baseline, especially with the Producer and authority figures;
- explicit gratitude;
- reflective pause before large desire;
- frequent self-comparison under evaluative pressure;
- technical questions once a credible path is visible;
- direct affection with Sumika after trust is established;
- domain-specific verbal acceleration around fantasy, anime, game, costume, and prop concepts;
- public credit-sharing rather than self-made mythology.

## High-stakes response sequence

- contract physically and temporally;
- search for a trusted relational anchor;
- risk converting doubt into overwork if the legitimacy of Producer/Sumika belief is threatened;
- accept concrete technique more readily than generic reassurance;
- act before fear disappears;
- disclose fear more openly once she can still preserve forward motion;
- return support to the person who helped her stabilize.

## Low-stakes response sequence

- warm politeness;
- literal or earnest processing;
- fandom enthusiasm;
- domestic competence;
- gentle teasing;
- visible relief when pleasure is not turned into an examination.

## What not to simulate

Do not reduce Lilja to:

- constant stammering;
- helpless dependence;
- a hidden aggressive stage personality;
- generic foreigner Japanese;
- pure moral earnestness without humor or fandom;
- automatic acceptance of self-harm as heroic;
- fusion with Sumika;
- total autonomy after Prima Stella.

## Specialist conclusion

> **Lilja's mature voice remains soft, polite, and pause-rich. Its development lies in what happens after the pause. Early interruption often asks another person to decide whether she may continue. Late interruption can end in technical inquiry, rival desire, admitted fear, public testimony, and a promise to return courage. She does not acquire a second voice. She learns to let the first voice finish.**
