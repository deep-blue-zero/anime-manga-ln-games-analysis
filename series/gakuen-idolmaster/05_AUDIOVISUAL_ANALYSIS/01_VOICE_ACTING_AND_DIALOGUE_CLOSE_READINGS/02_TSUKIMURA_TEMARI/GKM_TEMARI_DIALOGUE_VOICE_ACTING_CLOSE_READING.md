# TSUKIMURA TEMARI — DIALOGUE AND VOICE-ACTING CLOSE READING

## 0. Specialist thesis

Temari’s speaking performance does not reveal a single authentic voice hidden behind idol professionalism. It reveals a **stateful person whose relation to state change matures**.

Early Temari treats vocal and bodily transitions as humiliating loss of control:

- professional compression gives way to sharp irritation;
- irritation becomes pleading or tears;
- need is covered by insult, debt language, or renewed command;
- softness is tolerated mainly when nobody can use it to fix her in the role of dependent child.

Late Temari still moves rapidly among these modes, but the transitions no longer automatically threaten identity. She can request first-name intimacy, make a formal public address, become embarrassed, enjoy food, read the Producer’s tears, offer care, and name the next ambition inside one continuous sequence.

The governing acting development is therefore:

> **from involuntary state rupture to authored state-switching.**

This does not mean every late performance is calm or healthy. It means Temari increasingly decides what a state change will communicate.

---

# 1. Method and signal caution

## 1.1 Direct AV evidence

The analysis uses:

- complete Dear Idol compilations 1–37;
- isolated Dear episodes 004, 009, 010, 015, 016, 017, 019, 020, 022, 023, 027, 035, 036, 037;
- complete song commus for `Luna say maybe`, `アイヴイ`, `Campus mode!!`, `雨上がりのアイリス`, `がむしゃらに行こう！`, `一体いつから`, and `ガラクタロード`;
- exact Japanese dialogue extracts and source identities;
- rendered expression, posture, camera distance, transitions, and scene rhythm.

## 1.2 Scene-level signal metrics

Broad phases within the selected episodes were measured for:

- relative RMS dynamics;
- low-activity/pause rate;
- a Praat-derived pitch proxy.

These metrics contain BGM and other speakers. They are **not isolated Temari F0 measurements**. They are used only when a large within-episode change supports the same conclusion already visible in acting and text.

Examples:

- Dear 010’s hated-prior-self phase has a substantially lower scene pitch proxy than its earlier domestic triangle;
- Dear 016’s breakdown/model-confession phase rises sharply above the preceding controlled public mode;
- Dear 023’s post-loss scene remains among the lower proxies in the selected set;
- Dear 027 rises from three-person recognition into the airplane/rocket phase;
- Dear 037 rises from post-victory banter into direct present-self/attachment speech.

The numbers refine timing and contrast. They do not replace close reading.

# 2. Temari voice-state taxonomy

| code | state | audible/visible organization | typical contexts | interpretive caution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `T-V0` | compact professional control | measured pace, precise words, limited outward movement, held face | evaluation, defeat judgment, planning | may be integration or suppression |
| `T-V1` | abrasive/direct Temari | short correction, sharp attack, fast answer, confrontational gaze | Producer/Misuzu friction, rivalry | directness may be care or boundary, not coldness |
| `T-V2` | childlike need/panic | mobile high register, elongated vowels, pleading, sobbing, visible bodily contraction | fear of abandonment, pain, embarrassment, comedy | not automatically more authentic than other states |
| `T-V3` | combustive protest | expanded projection, clear words through anger/tears, forward posture | overwork, refusal, challenge | agency and self-endangerment may coexist |
| `T-V4` | lowered intimate trust | softer projection, longer pause, searching cadence, downcast or close gaze | origin shame, chosen naming, direct reliance | often concentrated in one witness relationship |
| `T-V5` | formal public address | stable projection, declarative rhythm, disciplined posture | stage, title receipt, public manifesto | professional form is not evidence of falsity |
| `T-V6` | playful/cute performance | deliberate brightness, comic timing, chosen embarrassment, expansive reaction | food, teasing, novelty songs, peers | can be freely performed rather than involuntary “gap” exposure |

The taxonomy is relational rather than essential. Temari can move through several states in one conversation.

# 3. STEP1: body shame, care, and exclusive partnership

## 3.1 Dear 004 — harder work as recognition

The scene begins from bodily exposure. Temari’s animation moves through defensive hands on hips, turning away, eyes closed, hand-to-head strain, downcast shame, and renewed attention.

The Producer’s significance lies in refusing the binary Temari fears:

- either sing at full force and collapse;
- or protect the body by becoming an ordinary singer.

He proposes a body and schedule capable of preserving the upper range. Temari’s relief is visible because the plan recognizes the desire she experiences as most essential.

The acting prevents easy moralization. The same response can be read as:

- accurate recognition;
- permission to continue self-endangerment;
- trust created through technical competence;
- transfer of bodily authority to a new caretaker.

The scene should therefore be cited whenever later analysis claims either that the Producer simply liberates her or simply controls her. It supports both more complicated possibilities.

## 3.2 Dear 009 — reconciliation without reunion

The opening live mode is focused and contained. Backstage with Misuzu, Temari’s facial and bodily scale becomes smaller: lowered gaze, softer expression, close proximity, and pauses that permit another person to answer.

This is not a return to SyngUp!. The relationship becomes viable because reunion is not demanded.

The later Producer language—`一緒に破滅`, `私だけのプロデューサー`—uses comedy and embarrassment to say something structurally serious. Temari can frame the bond as outrageous while closing around it quickly. The scene-level signal proxy shows fewer detected pauses in the exclusivity phase than in the preceding Misuzu reconciliation.

That difference fits the scene’s emotional logic:

- reconciliation with Misuzu requires careful relational negotiation;
- exclusive claim on the Producer feels frighteningly easy.

## 3.3 Dear 010 — origin disclosure

Dear 010 is the early model of `T-V4`.

Temari’s remembered child is not shouted into existence. The scene becomes lower, slower, and more searching. The scene-level pitch proxy falls markedly during the hated-self disclosure compared with the earlier domestic exchange.

The artificial-wings metaphor appears in this register because it is not a public slogan yet. It is a confession that everything she respects about herself may depend on construction, imitation, and help.

The final move toward future command matters. Temari does not discover that the child was secretly worthy on unchanged terms. She decides that constructed flight can still be pursued.

# 4. STEP2 / N.I.A.: Rinha and the broken unit

## 4.1 Dear 015 — challenge before confession

Temari confronts Rinha through competition because rivalry is safer than filial dependence. The direct register lets her demand response without first admitting how much of her current self comes from the other idol.

The challenge is therefore a preliminary form of attachment speech.

## 4.2 Dear 016 — childlike collapse as structural truth

This is the strongest `T-V2/T-V3` sequence in the baseline.

The visual progression is unmistakable:

- public persona and controlled argument;
- rising agitation;
- lowered head;
- fist/hand tension;
- tears, flush, pleading;
- continued verbal clarity inside collapse;
- eventual reassembly toward direct gaze.

The scene-level pitch proxy rises from roughly the mid-240s in the controlled section to the low-320s during the breakdown/model confession. Because other voices and BGM are present, the value is not a pure Temari measurement. The size and timing of the shift nevertheless match the visible rupture.

What becomes speakable is not merely “I miss you.” Temari admits that Rinha helped author the cool idol she had been treating as proof of independence.

The childlike state therefore conveys a professional and ontological truth. It should not be reduced to gap comedy.

## 4.3 Dear 017 — incompatible promises

The café framing keeps Temari and Misuzu in repeated shot/reverse-shot comparison. Temari’s sharp challenge and Misuzu’s gentler control remain visually and rhythmically distinct.

The episode establishes that the three SyngUp! members did not fail to remember one shared promise correctly. They carried different promises inside one unit.

Temari’s directness is productive here because it refuses the sentimental shortcut of calling all three memories equivalent.

## 4.4 Dear 019–020 — care becomes rivalry

These scenes are warmer and more horizontal than the earlier body-care conflict. Temari can receive praise, issue a challenge, and accept Misuzu’s refusal to run at Temari’s pace without interpreting the refusal as abandonment.

The voice remains direct, but its function changes:

> early directness defended against being fixed as weak;  
> later directness invites another person to become independently dangerous.

The N.I.A. culmination and top-Producer promise preserve artificiality rather than resolving it. Temari asks the Producer to help make the constructed self fly higher.

# 5. STEP3: admiration, defeat, and inherited flight

## 5.1 Dear 022 — becoming Misuzu’s fan

Backstage after Misuzu’s performance, Temari is visually compressed: dark stage costume, constrained face, hand-to-chest movement, direct close-up, and increasing strain.

The acting holds admiration and injury together. Temari is not merely jealous; she has been placed in the audience by someone she had understood primarily as support.

Becoming a fan is a loss of hierarchy and a gain in perception.

## 5.2 Dear 023 — `負けは負けです`

The post-H.I.F. scene gives Temari `T-V0` control. Arms-crossed containment and compact judgment replace the expected explosion.

The full-scene pitch proxy is among the lower values in the selected corpus. The Producer expresses more open frustration, allowing Temari to maintain the factual statement:

> a loss is a loss.

This is a major redistribution of affective labor. Temari no longer has to carry both precise evaluation and emotional eruption alone.

The later disbandment-live proposal turns loss into a new relational task without pretending the old unit should be restored.

## 5.3 Dear 027 — airplane and rocket

The Temari/Misuzu/Rinha triangle is staged as recognition among separate people. The camera does not visually fuse them into one restored unit.

The airplane metaphor receives the history; the rocket metaphor launches beyond it. The scene-level pitch proxy rises in the final phase compared with the preceding recognition phase, matching the visible increase in forward projection.

This is the point at which construction becomes pride:

- wings are artificial;
- artificial things can fly higher;
- the next construction can exceed the current one.

# 6. STEP4 / H.I.F.: chosen intimacy and public authorship

## 6.1 Dear 035 — first-name sendoff

Late Temari moves among comedic embarrassment, rivalry, softness, and determination without experiencing the shifts as catastrophic exposure.

Her request:

> `呼び捨てで、送り出してください`

is structurally new. Earlier intimacy often appears when control fails. Here Temari requests a precise relational form in advance of performance.

The scene-level pause proxy decreases in the first-name/sendoff phase compared with the preceding comic integration. The request is vulnerable but decided.

This is chosen dependence, not dependency erased.

## 6.2 Dear 036 — titleholder address

Temari receives the title through disciplined public form:

- controlled posture;
- stable front-facing address;
- formal recognition of Sena and supporters;
- expansion from gratitude into further attack.

The public voice does not conceal a private truth. It organizes several truths—lineage, victory, ambition, and noncustodial succession—into a form the institution can hear.

The relatively low pause rate and tighter dynamic proxy in the formal-address phase fit the impression of rehearsed or professional continuity.

## 6.3 Dear 037 — food, care reversal, and present-tense liking

The scene returns Temari to ordinary space after title spectacle. She folds arms, teases, becomes embarrassed, enjoys food, reads the Producer’s tears, lends a handkerchief, and directly evaluates the present relationship.

The signal proxy rises from the banter phase into the present-self/attachment phase, but the crucial evidence is grammatical and relational:

- `今日の私`;
- `いまのあなた`.

She does not promise that nothing will change. She affirms that the constructed present is lovable now.

The Producer is no longer only the person who reads Temari. She reads him and returns care.

# 7. Song-commu acting

## 7.1 `Luna say maybe`: denial that keeps performing resemblance

The commu lets Temari move among classroom challenge, peer interaction, calls, embarrassment, and live preparation. Her spoken disavowal is repeatedly undermined by humming and bodily investment.

The acting establishes a key distinction:

> she may deny autobiographical identity while still recognizing performative truth.

## 7.2 `アイヴイ`: pleasure in accurate selection

The visual rhythm is comparatively intimate and conversational. Temari watches how the Producer frames her, asks questions, and reacts with pleased recognition when the image feels correct.

This is not passive acceptance of branding. The performance shows her evaluating the editor.

## 7.3 `Campus mode!!`: inherited comparison

The commu brings Rinha, school lineage, fans, and common song into one field. Temari’s direct register becomes less defensive and more comparative: she wants to know how her rendition stands inside inherited meaning.

## 7.4 `雨上がりのアイリス`: active listening

Temari’s warmth and embarrassment are more sustained here. She promises to listen, participates in a unit dream, accepts mutual care, and still displays jealousy.

The acting therefore supports nonfused belonging: warmth does not require loss of possessive desire.

## 7.5 `がむしゃらに行こう！`: peer-comic voice

Saki/Kotone interaction gives Temari `T-V6` room. Comic timing and chosen embarrassment coexist with comparison and Producer exclusivity.

This is evidence that the “gap” can become a deliberate social performance rather than involuntary exposure.

## 7.6 `一体いつから`: learning mode

Temari’s voice alternates between irritation, curiosity, technical attention, and vulnerable admission as China and Misuzu supply different methods. The state changes correspond to different learning tasks rather than to a simple collapse of coolness.

## 7.7 `ガラクタロード`: conceptual ignition

The commu begins in self-description failure. Once the Producer offers `青い炎`, Temari’s recognition is rapid. The acted sequence then shifts toward audacity, public repetition, and post-rehearsal exhaustion.

This is perhaps the clearest voice-performance example of concept becoming energy:

> accurate name → relieved recognition → projected manifesto → bodily bill.

# 8. Relationship findings from performed dialogue

## Producer

The voice corpus confirms reciprocal co-authorship:

- Temari asks how she appears;
- accepts or rejects frames;
- requests intimate naming;
- makes received concepts public;
- reads Producer affect;
- offers care;
- keeps the relationship future-oriented.

It also shows why the bond can become enclosing. Most lowered intimate speech is concentrated around one witness whose interpretations carry professional force.

## Misuzu

Temari’s voice with Misuzu changes from defensive protest against bodily containment to direct rivalry, admiration, and method-learning. The relationship becomes viable when Misuzu’s difference remains audible rather than being translated back into caretaking.

## Rinha

Rinha produces the most extreme rupture because she is origin as well as rival. Temari’s collapse is evidence of lineage, not simply abandonment anxiety.

## Fans/public

The public voice becomes more truthful as it becomes more constructed. Dear 036 demonstrates that professional address can contain actual lineage and desire rather than flattening them into generic gratitude.

# 9. Voice-acting verdict

The performed corpus rejects a hierarchy in which:

> low voice = true/serious  
> high voice = fake/childish  
> stage voice = mask  
> tears = authenticity.

Temari’s modes have functions, not fixed truth values.

Her development consists in gaining control over the transitions:

- she can let childlike need communicate attachment without making it the whole self;
- let lowered trust name origin shame;
- let directness repair rather than only repel;
- let public form carry lineage and ambition;
- let comedy be chosen;
- let ordinary appetite coexist with titleholder status;
- request intimacy before performance rather than only after breakdown;
- return interpretation and care to the Producer.

The final specialist formulation is:

> **Temari’s voice acting makes authored combustion audible as state-switching. The mature achievement is not permanent composure. It is the ability to move among professional compression, anger, need, trust, play, and public declaration while increasingly choosing what each transition means.**
