# GKM — KATSURAGI LILJA MUSIC, MV, AND VISUAL-PERFORMANCE CLOSE READING

## 0. Scope and source-form discipline

This specialist document examines Lilja's musical and visual identity across song communications, rendered 3DMVs, and authored official MVs. It is subordinate to `GKM_LILJA_COMPLETE_AUDIOVISUAL_BASELINE.md` and the exact-text authority of the textual core.

Source forms are kept distinct:

1. **Song commu** establishes acted story context, intended meaning, relation-specific authorship, and song-state chronology.
2. **3DMV** establishes rendered body, choreography, costume, facial acting, virtual camera, stage geometry, and audience relation.
3. **Authored official MV** establishes a first-party symbolic/editorial interpretation. It is not literal route footage.
4. **Common-song performance** is a portability test, not evidence that the song was authored around Lilja.

The shared canonical REVERSI sources are housed under `VIDEO/00_SHARED_AND_ENSEMBLE/DUETS_AND_OTHER_ENSEMBLE`. Lilja's local 1038p capture is duplicate provenance and is not counted as a second logical performance.

No isolated vocal stems were available. Whole-mix metrics describe delivered sources, not Lilja's pure singing instrument.

---

# 1. Core finding — a stable process, not a fixed genre

Lilja does not have one immutable sonic or visual genre. Her stable signature is procedural:

> **inward gathering -> stabilized center -> outward field.**

That process can be carried by:

- dark arena and upward flight (`白線`);
- multicolored kinetic invitation (`Campus mode!!`);
- solemn black/military fantasy (`極光`);
- white gravitational stillness (`Atmosphere`);
- bilateral black/white relational staging (`ときめきエモーション` / REVERSI);
- playful cosmic fandom (`ガラクタロード`);
- dark angular common repertoire (`ENDLESS DANCE`, `Howling over the World`).

The range evidence rejects the idea that Lilja's only viable product is a shy white-haired snow fairy. The fairy image is one selectable production grammar. It becomes powerful when she appropriates it, not because it exhausts her identity.

# 2. Performance-body grammar

## 2.1 The sternum as emotional center

Across songs, Lilja repeatedly returns hands and forearms to the chest. The gesture gathers emotion before release. It can indicate fear, gratitude, concentration, or the effort to hold a desire still long enough to sing it.

## 2.2 Open arms as transmission

When the choreography opens the arms, the movement reads less as conquest than as delivery. The route has already defined performance through `届ける`. The body repeatedly translates that verb into geometry: something held near the self is extended toward camera, partner, or audience.

## 2.3 Kinetic invitation versus gravitational stillness

Two apparently opposite modes are both authentically Lilja.

- `Campus mode!!` creates scale through running, bright traversal, raised arms, and direct invitation.
- `Atmosphere` creates scale through controlled center while environment, screens, lighting, and camera move around her.

The first says `come with me`. The second says `enter this world`.

## 2.4 Soft face, large field

Even when the stage becomes dark or visually forceful, close-ups preserve a soft eye line and visible delicacy. Authority is produced by coherence, not by replacing softness with hardness.

# 3. `白線` — situated ownership and the public beginner

## 3.1 Commu

The foundational question is whether a technically weak singer can truthfully call a song hers. The commu refuses a simple relativist answer. Skill still matters. Ownership arises from situated meaning:

- the song is made for Lilja's present state;
- its flight image names the direction she is trying to move;
- Sumika teaches that delivery matters alongside execution;
- the first public object turns imperfection into legible effort rather than hiding it.

## 3.2 Rendered 3DMV

The 3DMV is dark and blue. Lilja often appears small relative to the stage and audience field. This scale relation is essential. Open-arm poses are achievements against visible vastness, not signs that the performer has always felt entitled to the space.

The rendered form repeatedly alternates close facial access with large-distance shots. It asks whether a private body can remain itself while becoming public.

## 3.3 Authored MV

The official MV is radically brighter than the game performance. Whole-frame sampling confirms a large difference in mean luminance. The editorial grammar is sky, running, flight, white/blue negative space, typography, and the line between ground and open atmosphere.

The two forms divide the song's work:

- 3DMV: a small performer must act before an actual audience field;
- official MV: aspiration is externalized as air, height, and movement.

## 3.4 Disposition

**STRENGTHEN / SPECIFY:** the song is Lilja's through situated effort, flight imagery, relationship history, and address, not technical monopoly.

# 4. `Campus mode!!` — recursive institutional inheritance

The commu begins with distance from the Hatsuboshi ideal. Costume and song belong to a tradition Lilja once watched from outside. Her initial caution is not empty modesty; it registers the ethical weight of inheritance.

The decisive movement is from gratitude to second-person invitation. Lilja wants to say `you can do it` to another frightened beginner because that sentence once moved her.

The 3DMV is bright, mobile, and institutionally expansive. Its choreography gives Lilja running shapes, raised arms, broad traversal, and repeated frontal address. She does not disappear into house style. She turns common repertoire into a personally owned social circuit.

**STRENGTHEN:** inheritance becomes legitimate through trained participation and recursive return.

---

# 5. `極光` — solemn range and controlled center

`極光` uses black/military-fantasy styling, cool color, portal or aurora imagery, and a more solemn stage posture. It is a necessary comparator because it breaks the false binary between fragile beginner and luminous white fairy.

Lilja does not need an aggressive face to carry dark formality. Controlled completion, vertical line, and deliberate timing are enough to change the meaning of softness.

The source mix has higher whole-mix dynamic range than most rendered performances. That is a property of the delivered source and should not be treated as isolated vocal evidence.

**STRENGTHEN / EXPAND:** dark formal authority is part of her viable range.

# 6. `Atmosphere` — conscious coauthorship of `葛城結界`

`Atmosphere` is the decisive object for `魔性`, world-making, and the relation between performer and production system.

## 6.1 Commu: imposed concept becomes interior logic

The Producer and staff propose a white/fairy visual concept and greater facial exposure. Lilja's fear of showing the forehead matters because the styling choice increases legibility.

She then appropriates the concept:

- asks for a staff-like microphone;
- imagines magical choreography;
- supplies fantasy vocabulary;
- connects costume, prop, movement, and performance world;
- names `葛城結界` as an audience field.

This is neither pure natural essence nor pure staff manufacture. It is coauthorship.

## 6.2 3DMV: gravitational stillness

The rendered performance often leaves Lilja relatively stable while the environment moves around her. White costume and staff-like microphone make a central vertical line. Kneeling, face-level gestures, and soft close-ups prevent the centrality from becoming domination.

The key formal result is:

> **Lilja can create scale by becoming the point around which a scene coheres.**

## 6.3 Official MV: ecosystem of entry

The authored MV translates the stage field into luminous forest, water, sky, reflections, and paths of light. Lilja is a figure inside an ecology rather than an omnipotent author outside it.

The source-level visual measurements show high saturation and high inter-sample change. Those values describe the edited visual field, not charisma itself. They corroborate the claim that the MV constructs an active environment rather than a static portrait.

## 6.4 Ethical model

A conventional barrier excludes. Lilja's `葛城結界` is permeable:

- support enters;
- fear remains legible;
- the audience can inhabit the temporary world;
- courage exits back toward the audience.

**STRENGTHEN / REVISE:** `魔性` is gravitational legibility inside a coauthored performance field, not innate magical dominance.

# 7. `桜フォトグラフ` — selection history rather than solo identity

The acquired object is the complete Lilja song commu. It is used primarily for Producer-history and relational authorship, not as a central solo-performance claim.

The commu reveals:

- the Producer supplied comparative training footage keyed to Lilja's physique;
- his original approach used an excuse and was partly improvised;
- he was drawn into the encounter rather than acting from perfect prior knowledge;
- Lilja has learned enough of his thinking to anticipate or complete explanations.

This revises the selection story from destiny to encounter. Being seen remains transformative, but the witness is human and fallible.

The trio's authored MV is not part of this Lilja-local packet. It may be added later as shared event evidence without changing the present closure.

# 8. `ときめきエモーション` / REVERSI — synchronized differentiation

## 8.1 Source authority

The canonical 3DMV and official MV are shared objects. They were already used in Sumika's AV pass. Lilja's analysis cross-references them so the relationship has one semantic source home.

## 8.2 3DMV

The rendered duet uses:

- alternating focality;
- equal or exchanged center;
- mirrored but nonidentical gestures;
- black/white contrast;
- proximity followed by separation;
- hand contact without permanent fusion;
- side-by-side audience address;
- distinct facial and bodily readability.

The unit is therefore not one merged identity. Its power comes from coordination between two centers.

## 8.3 Official MV

The authored MV abstracts the relation through circles, reversals, line art, paired colors, silhouettes, repetition, and promise. The graphic language can tempt a `two halves` reading. Separate outlines and alternating visual authority resist ontological collapse.

## 8.4 Public intimacy

Dear and commu material show that real relationship history is made competition-relevant. The framing is strategic and institutionally useful. It is also participant-owned within the supplied boundary: both girls narrate and perform rather than merely being narrated.

**STRENGTHEN:** mature REVERSI is synchronized differentiation: attachment, rivalry, shared history, and separate desire coexist.

**OPEN:** later phases must continue testing commercial instrumentalization, privacy, and consent as the narrative value of the relationship grows.

# 9. `ガラクタロード` — pleasure as a mature performance capacity

`ガラクタロード` is not minor nostalgia. It is the packet's strongest corrective to an effort-only model.

The commu establishes that Lilja sang anime and idol songs before professionalization, learned songs through Sumika, tried choreography for fun, and experienced singing as pleasure before it became evidence of legitimacy.

The 3DMV is playful, colorful, and physically expansive. It supports a less solemn body than `白線`, `極光`, or `Atmosphere`.

The analytical importance is ethical:

> **If every Lilja performance is interpreted as visible strain, the analysis reproduces her own overwork pathology. Pleasure must be allowed to count as real stage energy.**

**STRENGTHEN / PROMOTE:** recovered enjoyment belongs in the mature talent model.

# 10. `ENDLESS DANCE` and `Howling over the World` — portability tests

These objects are not character-owned narrative anchors. They answer a narrower question: does Lilja remain recognizable in dark, angular, forceful, or high-energy shared material?

The answer is yes.

- `ENDLESS DANCE` supplies dark carnival/door imagery and sharper movement grammar.
- `Howling over the World` supplies red/glitch/cyber imagery, black costuming, forceful reach, and a more aggressive field.

Neither requires her to become hard-faced or erase delicacy. The sources reject a narrow product model based only on beginner/fairy imagery.

# 12. Integrated musical identity

Lilja's musical identity is not a single mood. It is the recurrent conversion of inward emotional density into a shareable field.

| object | principal function |
|---|---|
| `白線` | imperfection becomes public movement |
| `Campus mode!!` | received institutional courage becomes invitation |
| `極光` | solemn and dark authority becomes viable |
| `Atmosphere` | world-making becomes conscious coauthorship |
| REVERSI | intimacy becomes differentiated bilateral form |
| `ガラクタロード` | pleasure protects courage from proof obligation |
| breadth performances | signature survives genre and image transformation |

Compact formulation:

> **Lilja's stable musical home is not white, black, bright, dark, shy, or forceful. It is permeable world-making: a performance field in which vulnerability remains legible, craft gives it duration, and address lets another person move.**
