# Synthesis architecture

## 1. Scope, purpose and authority

PO-G1 belongs to `series/you-and-i-are-polar-opposites/` with one [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) and paired [method](PO_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md). No prior architecture is superseded. Canonical status governs INITIAL procedure, not narrative findings, owner approval or reconstruction readiness.

The source inventory contains Japanese main manga V01–08 and a shared artbook with 氷の城壁. Main volume count is available coverage, not an independently established official endpoint. The intended literary corpus explains individual development, directed relationships, ordinary and exceptional behavior, Japanese register and page form without forcing people into a title-derived opposition.

The shared artbook has a single evidence home here but remains outside narrative admission. It does not collapse this project with The Ramparts of Ice. Comparison or paratext use requires its own scope, component classification and evidence role.

## 2. Source/artifact model

The source/paratext map owns main witnesses, shared-object identity and admission controls. Covers, profiles, notes, jokes, previews and artbook components require classification; creator statements, illustration/design and any fictional components have different permitted uses. Alternate editions, serialization, translations and adaptations remain excluded.

Day-one homes are method, architecture, source/paratext map, person/relationship ledger, ordinary/register ledger, claim ledger, entrypoint, producer review and descriptor. Ordinary/register evidence has an independent home so major relationship events cannot crowd out contextual baseline and speech samples. Page-form arguments live in full prose and FORM-tagged claims until recurring independent retrieval needs earn another home.

Future readings, checkpoints, studies, reconstruction and audits appear only with substantive artifacts. Do not create empty monographs, ensemble profiles or generic personality fields. The corpus is source-driven, not a pairing catalog.

## 5. Studies and preservation of analytical depth

An independent monograph is earned when an individual's distinct developmental question requires sustained evidence across situations/relationships. A directed relationship study needs independently supported orientations on each side, meaningful change and causal consequences. Group patterns must retain named actors, their differing knowledge and missing edges rather than replace them with types.

Mandatory mature responsibilities are character/causality, directed relationships, ordinary/contextual behavior, Japanese expression and page form. A bounded integrated study may cover several in substantive sections. The role-gap review must identify those sections and explain why independent specialists are unnecessary. A social-context or thematic specialist becomes required only when actual recurring evidence cannot be responsibly handled elsewhere.

Counterexamples to simple opposition deserve preservation, as do context-dependent similarities. This is a methodological safeguard, not a claim that the source contains any specific inversion. An integrated synthesis must explain the evidence rather than promote a catchy symmetry.

No universal word ceiling governs mature prose. Check causal mechanisms, inferential warrants, significant support/counterevidence, rival readings, named subjects, temporal state and boundaries. Ledger rows and abstracts are retrieval aids, not stand-ins for full arguments. During transfer compare complete versions for lost substance.

## 6. Contradictions, dependencies and gates

Before reconciling an apparent personality/relationship conflict, verify source/witness, attribution, scene context, knowledge and horizon. Distinguish self-description, another's impression, transient condition, learned behavior, changed relationship, new disclosure or actual inconsistency. Keep supported alternatives OPEN. Paratext cannot automatically settle a narrative ambiguity.

Production: source binding and authorized OPEN run → main-volume readings with synchronized state/register/claims → checkpoints → actual declared-scope completion → longitudinal/claim reconciliation → role-gap/specialist readiness → studies → convergence → integrated synthesis → validation → declared-scope freeze.

## 7. Derived character reconstruction

The [generic reconstruction method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md) §§9–12 and 14–18 are explicitly adopted for model layers, coverage crosswalk and validation. The following local constraints apply; no model or simulation exists now.

Evidence maps bind subject/state, main-volume witness and horizon, situation/stakes, interlocutor/audience, direct speech/behavior/interiority/report/observer/inference class, exact locator, full reading/claim route, Japanese status, counterexample and limitation. Ordinary contexts and exceptions to role/type labels are necessary coverage tests. Missing opportunity remains a gap.

Keep evidence map, operational model and validation record separate from the literary monograph. Bind evidence-base commit, source/continuity/temporal boundary, exclusions and version. Distinguish stable disposition, developmental state and temporary condition, with explicit change causes including REVEALED_NOT_NEW. Do not model two people as inverse parameter sets because of the title.

Rules specify trigger + perceived stakes + current state → response family with inhibitors, overrides, named exceptions, counterevidence, rival explanations and falsifiers. OBSERVED/CANDIDATE_POLICY/PROMOTED_POLICY depend on actual evidence and testing. Appraisal leads to decision objective, speech act, directed relationship filter, Japanese register and wording; private thought is not public speech. Score decision and surface fidelity separately.

Use HIGH/MODERATE/LOW/OPEN confidence; distance D0 attested, D1 near variant, D2 new within-world/state, D3 cross-scenario. D3 names chronology, relationship and setting assumptions and abstains when unconstrained. Shared-artbook illustrations, creator comments or another title's characters cannot fill main-story behavioral gaps.

For genuine held-out validation, withhold evidence from the model author before fitting and disclose prior exposure. Freeze rule-linked predictions, modeled state, assumptions, confidence/distance and falsifiers before releasing authorized test material. Already seen cases yield RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST, not prospective success.

An independent reviewer adjudicates diagnostic opportunity and exact evidence as SUPPORTED/PARTLY_SUPPORTED/CONTRADICTED/NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY/CONFOUNDED/UNRESOLVED with reasoning. Include ordinary cases, changed-interlocutor/context tests, plausible decoys, abstention controls and generic-personality baseline as appropriate. Report qualitative fidelity and counts/limits, not unsupported accuracy percentages.

Generated dialogue/scenes/predictions are synthetic outputs, never canonical observations or source evidence. No simulation is authorized. Coverage describes twelve evaluator dimensions and six scenario types without self-grading. Discovery/capability records belong to the designated agents.
