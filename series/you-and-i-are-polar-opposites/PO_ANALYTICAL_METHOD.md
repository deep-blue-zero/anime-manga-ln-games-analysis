# Manga analytical method

## Let people exceed the title's opposition

The available Japanese source is 正反対な君と僕, creator metadata 阿賀沢紅茶, main V01–08 plus an unadmitted shared artbook with 氷の城壁. The title may prompt a question about contrast; it does not establish two fixed psychological types, a specific cast, relationships or any plot.

This method asks future authorized readings to preserve individual behavior across contexts, self-perception versus others' accounts, Japanese expression and directed relationships without reducing people to contrasting labels. These are methodological expectations, not findings. [The source/paratext map](PO_SOURCE_AND_PARATEXT_MAP.md) owns actual availability and gaps; [the architecture](PO_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) owns cumulative and synthesis duties.

This adapts the [generic manga method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MANGA_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md). Lock CLOSED, no run. Before future reading require owner-defined units, bound witnesses, verified STILL_IMAGE/Japanese route and consistent OPEN gate.

## Main volumes, apparatus and shared artbook

Read main volumes in verified collected order, preserving actual chapter labels and causal movements within them. Map spine/image occurrences, real pagination and facing-page layout rather than deriving them from web serialization or processing chunks. Historical archive integrity is not page/legibility/completeness inspection.

Classify covers, title/contents pages, profiles, jokes, notes and previews by role/spoiler horizon. The shared artbook is separately gated; its illustrations or comments cannot supply missing narrative facts, a character's interiority or a cross-title shared world. Any later artbook review must classify components and work assignment while retaining one source home. It cannot be opened merely because the main manga run is authorized.

Disclose prior knowledge/adaptation exposure. Freeze entering state before each next unit. Keep scene knowledge, completed-volume interpretation and project admitted horizon distinct. An ending or later disclosure cannot be written back as an earlier person's knowledge. Different people occupying similar social roles are not interchangeable.

## Full causal reading and named subjects

For each consequential movement explain entering conditions, named people's perceived options/stakes, choices or constraints, consequences and the evidence linking them. Distinguish an individual act, a group expectation, an asserted social rule, misunderstanding and coincidence. A label such as outgoing, reserved, popular or awkward is not an explanatory mechanism unless the source evidence supports its scope and exceptions.

Use personal names once established within the relevant horizon. Keep source-required anonymity and aliases. “The opposite,” “the friend,” “the girl/boy” or another role may serve an analytical purpose, but cannot habitually replace a known identity. Attribute actor, thinker, speaker, observer and recipient clearly.

Separate OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, EVALUATION and UNRESOLVED, with HIGH/MODERATE/LOW/UNRESOLVED confidence. A person's self-description is not automatically a stable trait, and another's impression is evidence of that observer's belief. Record presented thought separately from spoken account and conduct.

Model relationships directionally. A toward B and B toward A need independent evidence for desire, trust, recognition, pressure or reciprocity. Preserve knowledge and agency asymmetries. Group context, if material, should retain each member's own knowledge and action rather than create a group mind.

## Ordinary life, register and counterexamples

Ordinary conversation, routine, humor, play, care, work/study or quiet behavior where present are first-class evidence. Compare them with salient conflict before inferring disposition. Record genuine opportunity before interpreting an absent response; an unrepresented context is a gap. A behavior can be learned, performed or relationship-conditioned without being automatically “fake.”

Japanese language governs voice/register. Inspect address, self-reference, politeness, endings, omission, ruby, discourse markers and pragmatic purpose in context. Preserve speaker/thinker and addressee/audience attribution. Use brief verified Japanese forms and labeled glosses, compare baseline and exceptions, and distinguish private thought from what someone says aloud. Translation or a catchy phrase cannot substitute for situated written-register evidence.

Every major personality/relationship claim needs counterexamples or an explicit account of where they were sought, a competing explanation and a possible falsifier. Do not force evidence into a binary contrast because of the title. OPEN is a legitimate conclusion.

## Page form and ethical distinctions

Read silent pages and visual reactions fully. For panel scale, framing, distance, gaze, grouping, backgrounds, gutters, repetition, withholding, tone/colour if present and rendering-register shifts, describe feature → proposed effect → plausible alternative. A drawing is not uniquely transparent emotion evidence. Verify page/spread relationships before claiming a reveal's timing.

Graphic sound/state effects are written conventions; selected panel endpoints are not continuous movement. No acting, acoustic delivery or adaptation score is in scope. A formal similarity with the artbook or another title requires independently admitted evidence and an explicit comparative question, not automatic narrative continuity.

When sensitive conduct occurs, separate depiction, explanation, intention, consent, consequence, framing and analyst judgment. Use only necessary description with locators. Later affection or sympathetic framing cannot establish earlier consent or moral endorsement.

Keep per-image REVIEWED_MATERIAL/NO_MATERIAL_ADDITION/DUPLICATE/EXCLUDED/UNRESOLVED_READABILITY states and raw source/derivatives private. Git records actual boundary/exclusions, safe aliases and concise quotations. No private ID/path, full dialogue or raw media enters the repository.

Material revisions use PRESERVE/STRENGTHEN/REVISE/DOWNGRADE/REJECT/OPEN with old formulations and prospective freezes preserved. A changed central premise requires dependent-study review, not an appended note that leaves the argument unchanged.

### Current-unit story synopsis (initiation policy §5.3)

Each authorized collected-volume reading must include a clearly labeled **Current-unit story synopsis** near the front of the canonical artifact, after witness/coverage and entering context, before the developed thesis/rival, extended interpretation and ledger inventories. Identify inherited developments as inherited, then recount what newly happens or is newly disclosed within this volume.

Write coherent, accessible prose giving the opening situation, source-established participants and stakes, consequential actions and decisions, turning points/disclosures and the ending state. Preserve meaningful physical and scene mechanics, including who acts toward whom, practical arrangements and the sequence of interaction where established. Follow the verified chapters, causal movements and panel presentation rather than imposing a cast pairing or opposing personality types from the title. Ordinary scenes retain their consequential events; a relationship label or group outcome cannot replace individual actions. Detail follows narrative density, with no fixed length or mandatory chapter template.

Use only the current admitted unit and explicitly marked inherited context. Distinguish depicted events from speech/thought, narrator presentation, testimony, conjecture, contested attribution and unresolved outcomes; newly disclosed earlier events are not new story-time events. Do not assign an individual's knowledge or intention to a whole group. Material event claims require retrievable witness/chapter/image/panel locators under the locator contract, or stable evidence references resolving to them, while keeping the prose readable. Later volumes, adaptations and the shared artbook cannot complete missing events. The artbook's shared publication identity does not establish shared cast or continuity with The Ramparts of Ice.

Compose or finalize this unit-end, spoiler-bearing orientation after applicable prospective captures are frozen, and shield analysts assigned an earlier chapter, movement or volume horizon from the full spoiler-bearing reading. It is neither a prospective freeze nor independent evidence. All causal, character, directed-relationship, Japanese, page-form and counterreading work, cumulative updates and exit uncertainty remain required. A separately admitted plot-bearing artbook component receives the same duty within its classified boundary; only a genuinely non-narrative separately scoped pass may record inapplicability with a reason.
