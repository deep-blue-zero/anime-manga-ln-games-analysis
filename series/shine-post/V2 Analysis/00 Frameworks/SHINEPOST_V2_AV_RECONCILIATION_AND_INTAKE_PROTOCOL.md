## 2. Recover the candidate before designing its audit

An authorized receiver obtains complete, retrievable contents and verifies their identity. Search excerpts, a completion message, a list of filenames and a producer's final summary are insufficient substitutes. Register missing items explicitly rather than reconstructing them from memory.

The intake record must recover:

- Producer, product surface, actual model and settings where observed, execution dates and accountable author; use UNKNOWN for unrecoverable facts.
- Original analytical generation, method and architecture, artifact identities and responsibilities, original paths, base commit or snapshot, and any original acceptance/freeze records.
- Source language, edition/encode, episode identities, source and derivative hashes, stream choices, durations, time bases and excluded material.
- Actual inspected coverage by episode, interval and evidence channel, with gaps, failed inspections, transport transformations and technical limitations.
- Actual prior familiarity, V1/candidate exposure, later-episode exposure, and any prospective records frozen before their own resolution became available.
- Actual independent contributions, critical review and dispositions, if they occurred; absence is recorded without inventing retrospective participation.
- All candidate analytical outputs, linked evidence routes, unresolved obligations and producer-reported completion criteria.

Compare the recovered candidate against the current repository state. Identify overlapping claims, incompatible boundaries, stale dependencies, private material and missing referenced artifacts. Preserve useful findings even when the candidate cannot yet support its completion claim.

### Original identity and intended destination

Create an explicit mapping for each received artifact. Original names and V2 names serve different purposes and must remain recoverable.

An imported filename containing V2 does not establish this project's generation identity. Retain the producer's original identity and declare a mapped derivative when adaptation changes it. Preserve immutable originals in their authorized home. Corrections and audit findings attach to them; they do not rewrite the original record to make its history look compliant.

Create actual audit and handoff artifacts under `../07 Audits and Handoffs/` only when there is a real receipt, operation or finding to preserve. Their real IDs and paths are recorded in the intake register. No placeholder audit, reconstructed execution log or invented completion proof belongs in the bootstrap.

Recover what the owner actually authorized, what source work actually occurred, when it occurred and which governance applied. 2. If a completed-source publication mechanism applies, recover its exact required receipts and scope. Never manufacture an active run or high-water mark merely to make an import pass.
3. If an independently frozen noncurrent generation cannot be represented by ordinary mechanisms, evaluate initiation §3.9.5. Its special route requires the owner's separately merged, exact governance binding on main before publication; a binding on the candidate branch is ineffective.
4. Do not preselect that exceptional route before intake. Its applicability depends on the recovered candidate and existing controls.

A missing authorization or historical receipt is a recorded limitation. A local series task cannot silently change global controls, source seals, the V1 baseline or publication permissions. Administrative recovery may continue within scope while dependent publication or source analysis remains pending.

## 4. Standing roles and inspection lanes

The [lane policy](../../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_MEDIUM_AND_INSPECTION_LANE_ROUTING_POLICY.md) retains the default anime route, `BUNDLE_MEDIATED`, and its channel permissions. The [AV reconciliation protocol §1](../../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_AUDIOVISUAL_RECONCILIATION_PROTOCOL.md) permits a mirror route for declared AV-primary responsibilities. SHINE POST adopts that route for the received continuous-AV candidate and performance, musical dramaturgy, temporal form and performed characterization when the recovered source and capabilities support it. The default and mirror retain the full unit's narrative and linguistic obligations; no provider name establishes a lane or authority.

The intended relay is **preserved continuous-AV candidate → independent source-facing audit → critical review and accountable integration**. This is an order of work, not an authority ranking. A candidate remains a candidate while required verification is outstanding.

| Responsibility | Required contribution |
| --- | --- |
| Principal analyst | Complete required unit inspection and coherent narrative, causal, character and audiovisual account; actual historical coverage must be recovered |
| Independent specialist/auditor | Direct source-aware work on Japanese/text evidence, character/ordinary-life evidence, or performance/form as assigned by verified capability |
| Independent critical reviewer | Inspect decisive evidence, challenge material claims and omissions, and examine losses or distortions in proposed integration |
| Accountable analytical integrator | Reconcile significant findings and disagreements; write the accepted artifact and shared cumulative changes |
| Repository integration owner | Verify receipts, scope, authority metadata, complete diffs and publication controls |

For newly undertaken substantive episode readings, the standing configuration is a principal, at least one independently contributing specialist, and an independent critical reviewer normally distinct from that specialist. The principal normally integrates. An unusually bounded source unit may use only the reduced configuration permitted by [topology §4.1](../../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EXECUTION_TOPOLOGY_AND_CAPABILITY_ROUTING_POLICY.md), with its reason recorded; a normal episode is not automatically eligible.

Reviewers work in separate contexts with their own authorized evidence access. Provider diversity is neither necessary nor sufficient. A second voice role-played by the principal, or approval based only on its prose, does not satisfy independence. Only the designated integrator writes shared ledgers and the entrypoint.

Whole-episode continuous inspection is justified when temporal form or dispersed performance patterns cannot be localized adequately beforehand. Otherwise inspect diagnostic intervals with sufficient surrounding context. Recover the candidate's real procedure; do not retrofit an invented escalation decision. A future audit can require further viewing proportional to material gaps, including broader coverage when targeted checks cannot answer them.

## 5. Evidence-channel permissions

The following distinctions remain recoverable in every claim and event record. These are the analytical method's shared evidence and statement codes, not a separate classification scheme.

| Channel or statement class | What it can support | What it cannot establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `TXT` — verified primary-language text | Japanese wording, address terms, textual lyric allocation and transcript order within its verified fidelity | Performed delivery, motion or fine AV synchronization |
| `FRM` — clean still images | Composition, visible costume/setting and isolated expression at the anchored instant | A gesture trajectory, continuous gaze, timing between unseen frames |
| `AM` — deterministic measurement | Measured cue, frame/PTS or waveform timing and explicitly defined acoustic quantities | Subjective timbre, motive, speaker identity or emotional certainty |
| `AO` — actual auditory observation | Attributed auditory observations within actual listening competence and coverage | An authoritative text witness or unsupported singer identification |
| `HL` — attributed human report | The identified person's listening/viewing observation, with source and limits preserved | Unqualified direct observation by the receiving analyst or automatic resolution of disagreement |
| `CAV` — verified continuous AV | Motion, temporal form, delivery and sound–image relations within effective resolution | Uninspected intervals, exact wording without its required text route, or direct access to inner states |
| `INF` / `INT` — inference / interpretation | Supported accounts of meaning, motive, emotion and relationship change with alternatives | An observed fact merely because analysts agree |

Record missing Japanese TXT without substituting translations or listening transcription as TXT. Its absence is not automatically a blanket episode-admission prohibition; it leaves dependent exact-wording claims unresolved. Required unresolved claims or channels block their dependent acceptance. Optional limitations remain visible and cannot carry a settled conclusion.

Attribute an untagged voice or singer only with appropriate corroboration: verified allocation, visible performance, or comparison with an established in-source voice, with uncertainty retained. Camera focus and recognition of a voice actor are insufficient. Distinguish a character's self-report, another character's account, observed conduct and the analyst's interpretation.

## 6. Clock and capability calibration

Before cross-object verification, bind the exact source and derivative hashes, streams, duration, offsets, cuts and time bases. For a complete episode, normally verify three or more anchors across beginning, middle and end. Record offset, drift, residual uncertainty and any excluded or unmatched regions.

Prefer verified subtitle/dialogue cues, then distinctive AV events, verified audio/waveform features, and frame/shot-index or presentation-time metadata. Use enough independent anchors for the precision of the actual question. A single matching cue does not establish synchronization across an episode.

The default ±0.5-second tolerance serves ordinary navigation only. A shorter pause, onset, glance or cut–lyric relationship requires correspondingly finer verification. Uncalibrated timestamps cannot support cross-object timing claims; mark the affected questions blocked while continuing independent questions that need no mapping.

Record actual continuous-video capability, audio access, source-language competence and effective visual sampling. Use UNKNOWN when sampling is undisclosed. Source frame rate and codec metadata do not establish the model's inspection rate. Coarse sampling cannot support events it could have skipped. Escalate diagnostic fast motion or synchrony to adequate targeted video, frame/PTS evidence, acoustic measurement or competent human playback.

An observation is **REPORTED** when it is only an attributed report. It becomes **ANCHORED** when its supporting observations have adequate source mapping, a reinspection receipt, sufficient temporal resolution and required corroboration. An observation that bears analytical load and is materially challenged remains **DISPUTED** pending resolution. Interpretations remain argued interpretations even when their observations are anchored.

## 7. Performance and musical-event audit

Use M0 incidental, M1 supportive, M2 diagnostic and M3 state-changing significance to allocate scrutiny. These labels describe analytical consequence, not musical quality. M2/M3 events require a complete linked event record and source reinspection. M3 also requires explicit updates or justified no-change dispositions for affected character, relationship and claim records.

For each diagnostic event, preserve:

- Witness/episode and calibrated interval; song/cue identity and verification level.
- Performers, listeners, audience configuration and relevant absences, with attribution confidence.
- Diegetic/nondiegetic/ambiguous status and any transition between them.
- Japanese lyric evidence separately from heard delivery, visible performance and analytical interpretation.
- Material internal changes in texture, density, dynamics, vocal distribution, choreography, framing and editing.
- The proposed narrative or relational consequence, its counterevidence and strongest alternative explanation.
- Effective sampling and channel limitations; exact event, evidence and claim references.

Do not treat a repeated song, melody, arrangement, recording and thematic association as interchangeable recurrence. An external commercial recording cannot establish an episode's mix, singer allocation or timing. Technical observations about pitch, instrumentation or performance quality need suitable evidence and competence; uncertainty is preferable to invented precision.

Review ordinary conversations and backstage conduct as well as performances. Compare delivery against the same character's interlocutor and situational baseline. A softer voice, silence, tremor or smile does not directly establish affection, fear or consent; retain the observation and test its contextual interpretation separately.

## 8. Blind-first audit and actual exposure

Use two packets where practical. The observation packet contains neutral questions, exact permitted sources, calibrated intervals, necessary surrounding context and technical limitations. The adjudication packet contains the candidate's claims, argument, disconfirmation criteria and affected artifacts.

1. The independent auditor inspects its assigned source scope before receiving the candidate's interpretation.
2. It records independent observations, alternative readings and useful findings outside the queue.
3. It answers the neutral questions and freezes the pre-exposure log.
4. Only then does it read the adjudication packet and compare findings claim by claim.
5. Any later observation is labeled post-exposure; it never overwrites the frozen log.

Record `blind_order_achieved: true` only when that order actually occurred. Otherwise record false, the material seen and the affected independence limits. An auditor who has read V1 or the whole series may still provide valuable review, but cannot claim an uncontaminated first-view forecast. A hidden candidate interpretation and an unknown future narrative are different kinds of blindness.

E01–E12 familiarity makes tests against that material retrospective for the exposed analyst. Never manufacture early-episode prospective freezes from a completed-series account. Preserve any genuinely contemporaneous original records with their actual timestamps, source horizons and limitations.

## 9. Adjudication and bounded return

For every material claim, record its evidence, critical objection, principal response, strongest competing explanation, remaining uncertainty and disposition: **PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT or OPEN**. Preserve useful independent findings that expand the account without contradicting its thesis.

An inaccessible evidence channel deserves serious source-grounded consideration, not automatic acceptance or dismissal. A contradiction requires evidence the objector can inspect. A verification request can instead identify a concrete reliability concern such as inadequate sampling, clock failure, missing corroboration or uncertain speaker allocation. Such a request makes a load-bearing observation disputed, not rejected.

Return a disputed item once with its exact question and grounds. If it remains material and unresolved, permit one independent verification through a fresh blind AV context, adequate targeted inspection, deterministic measurement or competent human viewing. Continued disagreement remains OPEN with all routes preserved. Do not repeat model polling until agreement appears.

Required unresolved dependencies block acceptance of the scope they support. A bounded partial result must declare its precise limits; it cannot relabel a required channel optional merely to claim completion. An accepted interpretation includes significant counterevidence and uncertainty, not only the winning formulation.

The acceptance record preserves actual assignments and context separation; inspected materials and exposure; independent findings and objections; dispositions and return results; required-channel coverage; surviving gaps; affected record IDs; and exact accepted artifact receipts. Meaningful findings must survive in public-safe analytical prose, not only in private execution logs or a short agreement summary.

Before acceptance, check source fidelity, narrative and causal completeness, character-state precision, ordinary-life evidence, Japanese/performance distinctions, and the handoff contract's named-subject clarity. Check that integration retained important exceptions and rival readings. Later monographs require their own separate self-containment and fidelity review; an episode audit does not certify reconstruction readiness.

Advance a V2 accepted boundary only through the authorized, complete coupled transaction and consistent entrypoint state. Structural validation does not establish literary correctness. Semantic acceptance does not itself grant publication authority.
