# SHINE POST V2 — Analytical Method

## 1. Purpose and governing family

Read the anime as an enacted narrative: what happens, what each person knows and chooses, how relations change, and what voice, music, movement, framing and editing contribute. These are responsibilities to investigate, not predetermined findings about the series. A coherent thematic account must remain answerable to ordinary behavior, exceptions and source detail.

This method pairs with the [synthesis architecture](SHINEPOST_V2_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md). The [AV/intake protocol](SHINEPOST_V2_AV_RECONCILIATION_AND_INTAKE_PROTOCOL.md) owns technical calibration, candidate transfer and reconciliation; the [reconstruction protocol](SHINEPOST_V2_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_PROTOCOL.md) owns the bridge from evidence to self-contained portraits and bounded models. Together they form one method family. The [current entrypoint](../../CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) controls present scope and admission.

The [generic anime method](../../../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_ANIME_EPISODE_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) and [continuous-AV method](../../../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CONTINUOUS_AUDIOVISUAL_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) are adopted reference procedures. Live [initiation](../../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md), [medium routing](../../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_MEDIUM_AND_INSPECTION_LANE_ROUTING_POLICY.md), [topology](../../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EXECUTION_TOPOLOGY_AND_CAPABILITY_ROUTING_POLICY.md), [reasoning](../../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_REASONING_AND_MODEL_ROUTING_POLICY.md) and [handoff](../../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md) policies govern their respective controls. A local method cannot grant source admission or waive them.

## 2. Source, chronology and knowledge

Use exact admitted Japanese episode witnesses. E01–E12 is the intended TV scope; no source hashes or edition facts are inferred from the V1 corpus. The finished anime is primary narrative evidence. Matched subtitles, audio, frames and bundles are derivatives with declared provenance. Translation is a secondary aid. Paratext and other media require separate admission and cannot silently establish anime motives, chronology or characterization.

Keep presentation order, reconstructed story time, each character's knowledge, audience disclosure and analyst knowledge separate. A later explanation can change the reading of earlier behavior without proving that the person changed then. A character's statement is evidence of what they say; its truth, motive and self-understanding require argument. Preserve named subjects, recipients and attribution, especially in scenes with several speakers.

Disclose prior viewing, V1 exposure, candidate exposure and whole-season access. A retrospective reread may reconstruct what an early episode discloses, but cannot recreate a genuine first-encounter forecast. Only actually bounded exposure and a recorded freeze before later material support prospective claims. Reading all of Antigravity's report first contaminates a later claim-blind comparison with it; document that condition honestly.

## 3. Evidence and claim discipline

Use the existing corpus evidence vocabulary:

| Class | Admissible role |
| --- | --- |
| `TXT` | Verified primary Japanese wording from a matched subtitle, transcript or onscreen text witness |
| `FRM` | Static evidence from a clean frame at its declared time |
| `CAV` | Synchronized audiovisual observations within verified inspection resolution |
| `AO` | Actual auditory observations with inspected interval, provenance and confidence |
| `HL` | Attributed human listening/viewing report; retains its reported source |
| `AM` | Deterministic measurements with source, procedure and parameters |
| `INF` | Inference supported by identified observations and alternatives |
| `INT` | Interpretation argued across evidence, rivals and counterexamples |

Observation, inference, interpretation, speculative hypothesis and value judgment must also be distinguishable in prose. Confidence is not an evidence class. An interpretation may be well supported without becoming an observed fact; aesthetic approval is not proof of narrative function.

Exact wording and lyric-dependent claims need adequate Japanese textual evidence. Audio-derived transcription remains attributed listening evidence until verified; OCR and ASR retain their production uncertainty. Missing TXT limits dependent claims rather than automatically excluding every episode observation. An English subtitle does not establish the exact Japanese address, ellipsis or register.

Every load-bearing claim needs an observation/claim ID, exact witness, episode and recoverable interval/cue/frame route, reasoning, a serious alternative, counterevidence and a disconfirmation condition. Do not invent timestamps or promote a whole-episode citation into exact support. Record genuine absence only within the inspected scope and channel.

## 4. Lanes and independent execution

The default anime route is `BUNDLE_MEDIATED`, with `CONTINUOUS_AV` required where temporal performance bears analytical load. This project explicitly adopts the mirror reconciliation route for the reported candidate if intake confirms a continuous-AV pass, and for AV-primary performance responsibilities: continuous-AV principal candidate → independent bundle/text and relevant perceptual verification → accountable integration. It remains subject to the full episode's narrative and linguistic obligations. An AV label or provider name supplies no authority or verified capability.

Each new substantive episode transaction has an accountable principal/integrator, at least one genuinely independent source-aware specialist, and independent critical review, normally by a distinct participant. The principal covers the whole unit; specialist and reviewer scope is diagnostic and explicitly bounded. Use independent character/ordinary-life, Japanese register and AV/music work where the source demands it. A justified reduced arrangement must meet the topology policy; a single context pretending to be several roles does not.

Before source work, verify admitted scope, an accurate OPEN run, source identity, inspection capabilities, clocks and role exposure. Only the integrator writes shared cumulative state. Specialists return their own findings, counterexamples and missing-evidence reports. Preserve actual assignments, inspected intervals/modalities, independent findings, critical objections and their dispositions in the unit's execution/reconciliation record.

Continuous visual sampling may be sparse. Native file frame rate is not model sampling. Static frame endpoints do not establish motion trajectories, and audio cannot establish an unseen gesture. Vocal quality requires actual audio inspection; numerical timing requires measurement. A delivery observation never directly proves emotion, intention or attachment.

## 5. Episode procedure

1. **Bind the unit.** Read the entrypoint, method family, admitted witness, prior accepted exit state and relevant open claims. Record role assignments, inspection routes, prior exposure and required/optional channels. Before the crew inspects the unit's narrative evidence, freeze its entering state with the prior accepted frontier, relevant open questions, any expectations actually held and known exposure. Identify the recoverable receipt in the episode checkpoint. The reviewer also records a blind response after source inspection and before seeing colleagues' drafts. If entry evidence is missing after exposure, identify what predates exposure and qualify any prospective claim that it cannot establish.
2. **Inspect the complete authorized unit.** Include relevant titles, credits, opening/ending variations and preview material within the witness. Identify scenes and event boundaries from the source. Log gaps, interruptions and sampling. A synopsis or selected clips cannot substitute for claimed full-episode inspection.
3. **Write a concise story synopsis near the front.** State who does what, under which conditions, and what changes by the episode's end. The synopsis contains spoilers only through the declared source boundary and remains a causal account, not a list of themes.
4. **Reconstruct causal and epistemic structure.** Trace triggers, options, decisions and consequences; chronology; what each person can know; concealment, misrecognition and delayed disclosure. Distinguish explanation from excuse, and capacity from willingness.
5. **Examine character and directed relationships.** Record durable disposition, local mood, developmental state, public/stage role, private conduct, stakes and constraints. Track A→B and B→A independently. Test dramatic behavior against ordinary-life controls and counterexamples.
6. **Read Japanese expression and performed voice.** Preserve speaker/addressee, wording and witness; address forms, politeness, hesitation, ellipsis, teasing and register shifts. Then separately examine audible contour, pace, breath, laughter, overlap and delivery against an appropriate within-person baseline.
7. **Examine audiovisual form and complete performances.** Track staging, spatial relations, gaze, camera, cuts, music, silence, offscreen sound and audience configuration. Ask what the form changes in the interpretation and whether dialogue alone would support the claim. Apply §6 to significant musical events.
8. **Test interpretation.** Consider the strongest rival, disconfirming scenes, production conventions and the difference between source evidence and genre expectation. Do not make every joke, costume or lyric an expression of one master thesis. Record uncertainty and negative findings.
9. **Reconcile independent work.** Resolve claim-level differences under the intake/AV protocol. Preserve unresolved material disputes. Critical review must directly inspect decisive evidence on a capability it possesses; agreement alone is not verification.
10. **Close the unit transaction.** Update all affected ledger homes, claim revisions, evidence routes, coverage and exit state together. Report actual accepted boundary and next candidate operation. A polished reading with stale ledgers or missing required evidence is incomplete.

## 6. Music, performance and visual form

Screen musical events by narrative significance: `M0` incidental, `M1` supportive, `M2` diagnostic, `M3` state-changing. This is not a quality ranking. M2/M3 require a full event record and focused reinspection; M3 also updates affected character, relationship and claim state. Keep the complete event available even when the argument centers on one moment.

The [performance ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/SHINEPOST_V2_PERFORMANCE_AND_FORM_LEDGER.md) holds the interval, song/cue identity and verification, diegetic status and changes, performers and observers, internal segments, vocal distribution, arrangement/density/dynamics, choreography, staging, editing and consequence. Link exact lyric evidence separately. Distinguish a melody, composition, arrangement, recording and thematic recurrence; resemblance does not establish identity.

Inspect rehearsal, backstage preparation, stage performance, audience reception and aftermath when present. Changes in fluency, bodily coordination or vocal distribution can support a development claim only with comparable evidence and alternatives. A shot of a character does not identify the singer. Voice-actor recognition does not establish speaker identity. Separate recorded soundtrack releases cannot establish the anime's exact mix or performance.

Default escalation is claim-specific: `VIDEO_NOT_REQUIRED` when accessible evidence suffices; `VIDEO_TARGETED_ESCALATION` for bounded delivery, movement or sound–image questions; `VIDEO_FULL_EPISODE_ESCALATION` when dispersed temporal patterns, editing or performance development require the whole unit. Diagnostic musical events receive continuous-AV coverage of the whole event. The imported candidate's declared coverage must be verified, not inferred from this design.

The [episode-bundle specification](../../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EPISODE_BUNDLE_SPECIFICATION.md) controls whether a derivative is a complete bundle. A frame-only, subtitle-only or audio-only object remains a partial derivative, even if useful for one question. Contact sheets locate evidence; clean frames and their exact timestamps support static claims. Failed clock calibration blocks dependent timing claims. The inherited ±0.5-second navigation tolerance never licenses subsecond synchronization conclusions.

## 7. Ordinary life and reconstruction

Actively retain low-pressure evidence: routine work, waiting, travel, food, amusement, playful disagreement, private conversation and small choices when shown. Do not infer unseen habits just to fill a profile. Compare public competence with private comfort, performed confidence with willingness, and one-off choices with durable preference. A dramatic crisis should not become a person's universal operating rule.

The reconstruction protocol requires self-contained monographs for every evidence-qualified individual, including secondary characters or adults if warranted. It separately governs operational models, voice/register rules, transfer limits and validation. The seven V1 indexed people are legacy routes, not a fixed V2 roster or qualification verdict. Unknown romantic, domestic or other preferences remain unknown.

## 8. Required output and revision

Each future episode reading must contain source/inspection boundary; synopsis; causal and epistemic account; character and directed-relationship analysis; Japanese-sensitive evidence; visual, musical and performed form; rival interpretations and counterevidence; open questions; source-bounded exit state; and a complete ledger/reconciliation delta. Weight sections by what the episode earns rather than forcing equal-length coverage. Add a specialist module only for a recurring responsibility with independent analytical value.

The [evidence and claim ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/SHINEPOST_V2_EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIM_LEDGER.md) owns `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT` and `OPEN` transitions. Preserve V1 and the incoming candidate's original formulations; record what survives as well as what changes. Frozen artifacts receive attributable errata or successors, never silent rewrites. Report blocked evidence-backed corrections with their exact obstacle.

This INITIAL method is current design authority. It contains no episode findings and makes no claim that the incoming analysis followed it. Revise the design when actual source or intake evidence demonstrates a need, recording the reason, affected responsibilities and any justified backfill.
