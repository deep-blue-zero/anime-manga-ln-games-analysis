# SHINE POST V2 — Character Reconstruction Protocol

## 1. Responsibility and present boundary

This protocol governs how V2 will turn admitted character evidence into independently usable portraits and, where useful, separately testable behavioral models. It adapts the [generic reconstruction method](../../../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md) and applies the [self-contained monograph standard](../../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md#self-contained-character-monograph-standard). The [analytical method](SHINEPOST_V2_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) governs readings and claim discipline; the [synthesis architecture](SHINEPOST_V2_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) governs artifact responsibilities and production dependencies.

Canonical authority here is methodological. This document establishes no character findings, qualification decisions, operational models, validation outcomes, or capability grades. The target is the Japanese audiovisual TV anime E01–E12; a target range is not an inspected or admitted source range. Other adaptations, games, publicity, performer biographies, and franchise knowledge do not enter that continuity by default.

The user reports a completed Antigravity analysis, but its full delivery, source coverage, exposure history, and inspection capabilities are unverified at bootstrap. V1 evidence remains in its existing home pending targeted reconciliation; creating this method neither invalidates nor silently imports it. The [current entrypoint](../../CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) controls the live source boundary and sequential lock.

## 2. One home for each responsibility

| Home | Responsibility for character work |
| --- | --- |
| [Source and intake register](../01%20Source%20and%20Intake/SHINEPOST_V2_SOURCE_AND_INTAKE_REGISTER.md) | Witness identity, authorized and inspected coverage, source-language status, capability receipts, incoming-artifact status, and declared exposure. |
| Source-facing episode readings | Original observations and scene-local interpretations, including ordinary behavior and evidence that challenges the favored portrait. |
| [Evidence and claim ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/SHINEPOST_V2_EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIM_LEDGER.md) | Stable claim references, precise evidence routes, counterevidence, current formulations, and revision history. |
| [Character state ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/SHINEPOST_V2_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md) | Person/state chronology, dispositions versus changes, qualification decisions, evidence gaps, and links to real portraits and their acceptance records. |
| [Relationship ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/SHINEPOST_V2_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md) | Directed relational conditions, asymmetries, knowledge and disclosure, changes, and contrary instances. |
| [Performance and form ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/SHINEPOST_V2_PERFORMANCE_AND_FORM_LEDGER.md) | Audited performed voice, embodied behavior, staging, music, and differences between performance evidence and psychological inference. |
| `04 Character and Relationship Syntheses/` | Future self-contained individual monographs and independently warranted relationship studies, with distinct responsibilities. |
| Future operational model and its separate validation records | Formal conditional predictions and their independent assessment; create only when useful and give each an explicit canonical route in the architecture. |

Do not reproduce a second evidence ledger inside a model or monograph. Summarize enough evidence for understanding and cite the stable records. A monograph's operational profile is a reader-facing summary, not proof that a formal model exists. A model can be useful before a literary portrait qualifies; it never cancels the production obligation for a character who does qualify.

## 3. Bind identity, knowledge, and state before inference

Every substantive portrait or model declares the subject's stable identity, anime continuity, source witness, admitted episodes, temporal and spoiler boundary, basis commit, inspection lanes, excluded evidence, and exposure to existing interpretations. Use a readable state label such as `Character@E06`, adding a scene boundary when a consequential change occurs inside an episode. The label specifies an evidence boundary, not permission to fill missing history.

Keep three clocks distinct: when an event happened in the story; when the audience learned of it; and what the character or interlocutor knew at the modeled moment. Later disclosure can revise the analyst's explanation of earlier conduct without giving the earlier character later knowledge. Declare any retrospective V1 or candidate exposure; do not call that context a blind first viewing.

Maintain these layers separately:

- **Disposition:** a tendency supported across contexts or by a sufficiently diagnostic concentrated sequence, with contrary evidence retained.
- **Developmental state:** current knowledge, priorities, relationships, responsibilities, resources, and self-understanding, with validity boundaries.
- **Situational condition:** fatigue, anxiety, audience pressure, privacy, immediate conflict, or other observed temporary circumstances.
- **Presented role:** rehearsal, public performance, promotional speech, professional duty, private interaction, or another attested frame. A role can shape conduct without exhausting personality.

Tag a material change by its cause: `DISPOSITION`, `KNOWLEDGE`, `RELATIONSHIP`, `CONTEXT`, `ROLE`, `RESOURCE`, `REVEALED_NOT_NEW`, or `UNRESOLVED`. Do not turn a late revelation into development or a momentary response into a stable trait. Earlier-boundary portraits remain intelligible on their own terms.

## 4. Capture a person beyond the dramatic peak

For each useful observation, preserve the episode and calibrated time/cue or frame locator, witness and inspection lane, character state, interlocutor or audience, situation, observed conduct or brief Japanese anchor, evidential route, interpretation, alternative, and claim reference. Distinguish direct behavior, speech/self-report, depicted interior thought, another character's belief, performed presentation, supported inference, bounded extrapolation, speculation, and unknown information. A visible or audible cue does not directly reveal a motive.

Deliberately seek the ordinary-life comparison sample where the source supplies one. Capture routine work, waiting, meals, idle conversation, small favors, leisure, minor frustration, mistakes and repairs, and behavior when there is no dramatic audience. Record when the source provides no such opportunity. Do not infer a favorite food from one meal, a hobby from one prop, or a stable aversion from a situational refusal.

Compare humor and quiet behavior with crisis behavior: who initiates play, who becomes the target, whether teasing affiliates or distances, how embarrassment is managed, when a character lets a joke pass, and how silence changes with company. Describe private or low-pressure behavior only where it is actually available; an apparently private scene still requires a supported interpretation.

Separate ability from the conditions of its exercise. A successful performance may establish an instance of skill; consistency, improvisation, instruction, recovery, and limits need their own evidence. Refusal can concern willingness, values, fear, fatigue, relationship, or circumstance rather than incapacity. Failure can concern conditions rather than an enduring ceiling. Any specialized or unusual ability remains bounded by what its source actually establishes.

An absent behavior is negative evidence only if the character had a genuine opportunity to display it. Record the opportunity and competing explanations. Otherwise mark an evidence gap. Avoid filling ordinary preferences, private history, attraction, romantic experience, or intimate boundaries to make the portrait feel complete. Where romance or intimacy is unsupported or inapplicable, say so briefly; for minors remain within what the source depicts.

## 5. Japanese communication and performed voice

Reconstruct speech through appraisal, objective, speech act, relationship, register, and wording. The question is first what the character is trying to accomplish, then how that character would express it under the stated conditions. Catchphrase repetition is insufficient.

Maintain a compact evidence-backed register matrix by interlocutor, audience, and mode. Track self-reference, address and honorifics, politeness, endings, vocabulary, hesitation and repair, indirectness, conversational initiative, humor, silence, and meaningful shifts under pressure. Distinguish inner thought from outward speech. Include contrary instances and limits rather than treating every attested form as universally preferred.

| Claim | Required basis and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Exact Japanese wording, address, or grammatical register | Verified primary-language subtitle/transcript layer. Label OCR, ASR, and listening transcription as derivatives with their production method and uncertainty. Translation aids navigation and explanation, not Japanese voice authority. |
| Breath, tremor, prosody, vocal quality, approximate perceived pause | Actually inspected audio through a verified audio-capable or continuous-AV route. Text alone supports written register, not delivery. |
| Exact pause, response latency, or synchronization measurement | Measured and calibrated source interval; distinguish the measurement from its interpretation. |
| Gesture, gaze trajectory, turn timing, or changing expression | Appropriate continuous inspection, bounded by effective sampling; isolated frames support endpoints, not the unobserved path. |
| Singer or speaker attribution | Corroborated attribution, not camera focus or recognition of a voice actor alone. |
| Motive or emotion inferred from delivery | Labeled inference, with competing readings and scene context retained. |

Apply the [inspection-lane policy](../../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_MEDIUM_AND_INSPECTION_LANE_ROUTING_POLICY.md) and the project's AV reconciliation procedure when channels disagree. A less informative lane cannot settle an auditory dispute by assertion. Queue targeted review, narrow the claim, or leave it open.

Compare conversational voice with rehearsal, stage speech, singing, and any other attested performed persona. Lyrics or a role's words are not automatically autobiographical testimony; identify the textual speaker, performer, scene framing, and interpretive bridge. Musical intensity or vocal polish does not establish private confidence. Store detailed AV evidence in its performance home and explain its person-level consequences in the monograph.

For generated Japanese, evaluate naturalness separately from character fidelity. Fluent wording can still select the wrong speech act, disclose what the character would conceal, flatten politeness differences, or fit the wrong relationship. Mark invented dialogue as synthetic; never return it to the evidence corpus as an attested example.

## 6. Directed relationships and conditional behavior

Model A toward B separately from B toward A. Record trust, responsibility, dependence, rivalry, admiration, disclosure, boundaries, perceived obligations, and behavioral changes only where supported. Distinguish the relationship's public presentation, each person's belief about it, and what their conduct establishes. Conduct toward one person does not automatically generalize to peers, authority figures, strangers, or an audience.

For each useful behavioral rule, record:

1. The applicable subject/state, trigger, information available, perceived stakes, and relationship conditions.
2. The likely attention/appraisal, priority, response family, and communication strategy.
3. Inhibitors, overrides, alternative responses, and cases in which the rule should abstain.
4. Supporting claim references, counterexamples, a rival explanation, and an observation that would disconfirm the rule.

Use `OBSERVED` for an instance, `CANDIDATE_POLICY` for a supported but unvalidated tendency, and `PROMOTED_POLICY` only after relevant validation. Promotion applies to that bounded rule, not to every trait or scenario involving the character. A rule that accommodates every conceivable response is not useful.

The operational model separates stable dispositions, developmental state, temporary conditions, values/appraisal, decision rules, Japanese register, performed-voice evidence, directed relationships, and known limits. Include ordinary behavior, humor/play, cooperation/conflict, ethical deliberation, and professional conduct to the extent evidenced. Do not fabricate a domain merely to populate the model.

## 7. Qualification and self-contained monographs

Assess every character for whom cumulative evidence supports differentiation, including supporting characters, adults, and antagonists. There is no fixed roster ceiling. Qualification rests on convergent evidence, meaningful contextual or interpersonal variation, and constraints or counterexamples that exclude a generic sketch. One concentrated sequence can suffice; prominence, repeated appearances, popularity, or citation totals cannot substitute for the test.

Record decisions in the **character state ledger**, no later than specialist-synthesis readiness and again after material evidence or revision. Each decision gives stable identity, assessment boundary, source-grounded rationale, largest gaps, next trigger, outcome, and the monograph path once the file exists. Use `QUALIFIED_PENDING`, `COMPLETE_AT_BOUNDARY`, `DEFERRED_EVIDENCE`, or `REASSESS_AFTER_REVISION` with their initiation-policy meanings. An unassessed character is neither qualified nor rejected.

Qualification creates a production obligation. A bounded tranche can leave recorded work pending, but the character layer cannot be complete while any qualified character lacks an accepted monograph. Do not create placeholder portraits, invented decisions, or an empty qualification index. A dyad study cannot discharge two individuals' portrait obligations unless each has an independently usable canonical monograph.

Each monograph carries `artifact_type: character_monograph`, full authority metadata, one subject/boundary, and a coherent explanatory portrait. The following are coverage responsibilities, not fourteen compulsory headings:

| Responsibility | What the portrait must make recoverable without another file |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Identity and circumstances | Names, roles, background and situation, plus continuity, source, temporal, and spoiler boundaries. |
| 2. Personal history | Formative events and their bearing on present conduct, with event, disclosure, and knowledge clocks. |
| 3. Personality and psychology | Goals, motives, values, fears, vulnerabilities, contradictions, coping, and self-conception. |
| 4. Cognition and decisions | Attention, reasoning, prioritization, uncertainty, and conflicting obligations. |
| 5. Behavioral patterns | Ordinary conduct, cooperation, conflict, stress, mistakes, correction, and meaningful exceptions. |
| 6. Speech and communication | Natural Japanese register and audience shifts, politeness, humor, silence, inner/outward differences, and written/performed limits. |
| 7. Relationships | Major directed differences, trust, attachment, rivalry, dependency, disclosure, and development. |
| 8. Preferences and ordinary life | Supported habits, likes, dislikes, comforts, food, leisure, and aversions; stable preference versus one-off choice. |
| 9. Romantic and intimate psychology | Only source-supported attraction, history, boundaries, and other relevant aspects; otherwise an explicit short unknown/inapplicable statement. |
| 10. Capabilities and limitations | Skills, knowledge, abilities, constraints, and capacity versus willingness. |
| 11. Longitudinal development | What changed, why, and what was merely disclosed late. |
| 12. Operational behavioral profile | Concise conditional tendencies, triggers, counterconditions, support, and abstention contexts. |
| 13. Interpretive uncertainty | Alternatives, counterevidence, contradictions, gaps, and extrapolation limits. |
| 14. Source traceability | Precise locators or stable evidence/claim references and routes to current deeper records. |

Depth follows evidence and responsibility. Unknown dimensions remain explicit, without padding or invented completeness. Explain relational and specialist findings' consequences for this person inside the portrait; links support verification and deeper inquiry but cannot carry essential personality, speech, behavior, or development. Use clear names and attributable claims while preserving the stated knowledge boundary.

## 8. Accept usability and fidelity separately

Before a monograph becomes current or receives `COMPLETE_AT_BOUNDARY`, record two distinct results in its acceptance record and link that record from the qualification decision:

- **Self-containment/usability:** read only the monograph and document question-by-question whether it explains personality and motives, typical and exceptional decisions, reasoning, natural speech, relationship differences, ordinary habits, development, a novel but comparable situation, and the limits of knowledge. An explicit source-bounded unknown can pass; an external link standing in for the answer cannot.
- **Fidelity:** check load-bearing interpretations against the admitted source, precise locators, current claim formulations, contrary evidence, state boundaries, and actual inspection capabilities. Attractive prose or internal consistency is insufficient.

Prefer a reviewer other than the author. A fresh monograph-only probe is recommended for central characters or suspected gaps; check its answers against the source separately. Record reviewer/context, tested document version, questions and findings, both outcomes, and any unresolved defects. One property can pass while the other fails. Neither establishes simulation readiness or validates a formal reconstruction model.

## 9. Transfer, uncertainty, and model validation

Every prediction states its modeled state, setting, interlocutor relationship, assumed knowledge, relevant capacities/resources, and changed premises. Use qualitative support (`HIGH`, `MODERATE`, `LOW`, `OPEN`) with reasons, and distinguish distance:

| Distance | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| D0 | Attested situation, summarized as evidence rather than advertised as a novel prediction. |
| D1 | Close variant of an evidenced situation. |
| D2 | New situation within the anime's continuity and modeled state. |
| D3 | Counterfactual, crossover, or materially altered setting with explicit assumptions. |

Greater distance normally reduces confidence. Preserve age, knowledge, relationship history, motivations, and ability limits unless the scenario explicitly changes them. A changed premise produces a conditional extrapolation, not new canon. Do not manufacture biography, romance, preferences, or private thought to connect a scenario's missing pieces. Offer bounded alternatives or abstain when the decisive condition is unobserved, incompatible with the source boundary, or contested.

Validation is a separate activity against an exact model version and evidence basis:

1. Declare prior exposure, recognizable franchise knowledge, training/test boundaries, inspection channels, and who performs independent adjudication. A source-aware critic needs a genuinely separate context; relabeling the author's own passes does not supply independence.
2. Use prospective prediction only when testing material was genuinely unseen by the analyst: commit a frozen register of rules, predictions, distance, and support before exposure. Preserve it unchanged and adjudicate in a separate record. Never invent held-outs or call an already read episode unseen.
3. Label tests against already known material **retrospective**, including V1 or candidate-derived exposure. They can identify contradictions and overfitting but cannot establish prospective predictive success.
4. Select diagnostic challenges: change one condition, compare plausible decoys, require abstention for an unsupported situation, test ordinary versus crisis behavior, and separate speech-content fidelity from surface naturalness. Compare with a generic personality sketch when it clarifies whether the rules add discrimination.
5. Report `SUPPORTED`, `PARTLY_SUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`, `NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY`, `CONFOUNDED`, or `UNRESOLVED` with source reasons. Lack of a diagnostic opportunity is neither a success nor a contradiction.

Audit important rules as `PASS`, `PASS_WITH_BOUND`, `NEEDS_TARGETED_SOURCE_REVIEW`, or `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`; record counterexamples and required narrowing. Report qualitative findings with test counts and exclusions, not accuracy percentages or invented probability estimates. Keep the prediction register, adjudications, and audit distinct from the model and from portrait acceptance.

A model exposes where evidence addresses the evaluator's psychological model, longitudinal state, voice/register, mundane behavior, relationship conditioning, interiority, conflict, ethical deliberation, humor/play, source completeness, negative evidence, and temporal specificity dimensions. It describes support and gaps for dialogue, cross-scenario, mundane-social, ethical, romantic/relationship, and professional use without assigning grades. Only the designated [reconstruction evaluator](../../../../governance/policies/CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_MAINTENANCE.md) owns repository capability and scenario-readiness assessments.

## 10. Revision and first evidence-bearing use

When a portrait or model conflicts with the admitted corpus, resolve the underlying literary claim in the evidence and claim ledger. Use `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT`, or `OPEN`, retaining the earlier formulation, counterevidence, decision, and affected artifacts. Propagate the result through character states, directed relationships, performance interpretations, portraits, and models as applicable. Record unresolved disagreement rather than voting it away.

Keep one current portrait per subject/boundary and one declared current route for each model responsibility. Preserve earlier states and frozen tests; issue a bounded successor or attributable correction under the archive rules. A model that may already have been assessed receives a new version with reciprocal supersession, not a silent rewrite or a status-only promotion. Revisions can invalidate assessments; project-local updates do not edit the global evaluator's registry.

Before the first substantive use of this protocol, consult the current entrypoint and verify the actual intake, source authorization, coverage, capability receipts, exposure declarations, and required reading/review roles. A closed sequential lock remains closed until the governing gate is satisfied; framework completion does not authorize a reading. Use admitted evidence to populate the existing ledgers, then assess actual qualification and production dependencies. Create downstream artifacts only when their evidence and responsibilities justify them.

**Bootstrap record:** no source observations, qualifying roster, monograph acceptance results, models, prospective freezes, validation results, or reconstruction grades are established by this document.
