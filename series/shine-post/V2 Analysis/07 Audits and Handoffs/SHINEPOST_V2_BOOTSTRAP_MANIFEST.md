## Foundation decisions

1. **Keep method and architecture distinct.** The method governs observations and argument; the architecture assigns their cumulative homes and completion gates.
2. **Keep V2 design separate from source acceptance.** The entrypoint is CLOSED, source admission is NONE, exact-source reconnaissance is incomplete, and no active/completed sequential-execution block is invented.
3. **Receive Antigravity's actual work before judging it.** Preserve original identity and exposure; inspect full delivered contents, source/coverage records and publication eligibility. Do not infer historical procedure from the new framework.
4. **Give AV evidence a capability-bounded route.** Adopt the scoped AV-primary mirror with independent bundle/text verification, relevant perceptual review, critical scrutiny and one accountable integrator.
5. **Retain characters beyond dramatic arcs.** Ordinary life, humor, directed relations, Japanese voice, capability versus willingness, uncertainty and counterexamples feed self-contained monographs and any later models.
6. **Create only useful day-one infrastructure.** Four ledgers have concrete schemas and sole responsibilities; no fabricated readings, placeholder portraits, qualification verdicts or reconstruction grades exist.
7. **Preserve earlier thought.** V1/candidate comparisons use claim-specific transitions and recoverable originals. Frozen records and prospective boundaries are never rewritten to resemble a different history.
