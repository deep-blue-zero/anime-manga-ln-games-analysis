## How to run a case

Choose an exact case ID and its native programme interval from the table. An authorized operator can recover its blind prompt and competing-position sheet from the matching episode in the indexed case manifest. The complete private manifest has SHA-256 `9fc70f5eaa95045e158dad27680aace3985bf396974356237c1589b32839a3b8`. The [source and intake register](../01%20Source%20and%20Intake/SHINEPOST_V2_SOURCE_AND_INTAKE_REGISTER.md) identifies the original Japanese release and the separate comparator clocks.

1. Assign an independent Gemini worker whose actual prior exposure is recorded. Give the worker only the episode, case ID, neutral question, permitted primary sources and a short interval of surrounding context. Use the release’s first Japanese stereo audio stream and native picture clock. The hardsub and ASS are separate written witnesses. For a disputed Japanese syllable, listen first without text, then compare the subtitle and record each account. For music or movement, specify the actual sensing channel and what it can resolve.
2. Before showing the competing positions, retain the worker’s original blind return and hash its bytes. It must identify the episode and case, worker and model, prior exposure, source and stream hashes, precise native intervals, clock mapping, method and actual modalities, independent observations, counterexamples and limits. A picture, numerical energy measure or prior report cannot establish a new hearing.
3. Give that same worker the positions for that single case. Request a fresh test of the strongest evidence for each. Record the blind hash, source evidence newly inspected, calibration, confidence, evidence against the worker’s own conclusion, remaining uncertainty, revised wording and affected claims. Use one outcome per case: `SUPPORTED_CODEX`, `SUPPORTED_GEMINI`, `BOTH_PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`, `ALTERNATIVE_FINDING` or `UNRESOLVED`. Preserve the original answer and hash. A revised answer receives a separate version and hash.
4. Have a separate evaluator verify the dispatch, real worker identity and model, actual tool and sensory capability, evidence that the blind return preceded disclosure, source hashes and native coordinates, evidence for and against the proposed answer, and the change warranted in dependent records. Distinguish a received submission from an evaluated return. Where the verified answer still leaves a consequential disagreement, prepare one narrow question for an authorized human with the required Japanese-language or perceptual expertise. Record delivery and the human’s own answer separately; preserve an indeterminate outcome where the same stereo mix cannot decide it.

Keep the original neutral manifest, workers’ blind receipts, comparative replies, source evaluation and any human decision in the controlled handoff. The public case IDs below connect the [season audit](SHINEPOST_V2_CROSS_MODEL_RECONCILIATION_AUDIT.md) to those receipts.

For a structured return, supply `episode`, `dispute_id`, `worker_conversation_id`, `actual_model`, `actual_method_and_input_tool`, `true_exposure`, `source_witness_hashes`, `native_intervals`, `clock_calibration`, `actual_modalities`, `blind_file_sha256`, `outcome`, `evidence_newly_inspected`, `method`, `calibration`, `limits`, `confidence`, `remaining_uncertainty`, `proposed_corrected_wording`, `evidence_against_own_conclusion`, `human_review_reason_if_any` and `affected_claims`.
