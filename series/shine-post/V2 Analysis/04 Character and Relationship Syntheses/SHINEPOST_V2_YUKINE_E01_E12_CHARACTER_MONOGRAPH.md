# Yukine Gionji and the work of looking after people

Yukine Gionji can tie a shoelace right in the middle of refusing someone. Kyoka Tamaki wants Yukine to come back to TINGS. She offers help with practice, asks why Yukine left and even offers her the benefit of a joke every day. Yukine considers Momiji sufficient company and the jokes dull. She also notices the loose lace, stops Kyoka, crouches down and ties an Ian knot fit to run in. Kyoka thanks her. The offer to the five is still outstanding. What makes Yukine a difficult friend to persuade is also what makes her a friend one wants back. A promise about the future is subject to conditions she takes very seriously. A friend with a loose shoe can have her help immediately. (SPV2-OBS-E07-25, JP cues 1011–1034.)

祇園寺雪音 is a child actor with stage and television experience who also wants to be an idol. Brightest’s undated recruitment profile lists her as sixteen, born August 8. She is the G of an original five-member TINGS that practiced together before three members debuted as TiNgS. When we first see her, she and Momiji Ito have a lesson to get on with while they wait for Nanami. The series names them to the visiting manager in E02. The stable identifier SPV2-CH-DOOR-COMPANION preserves the narrower identification possible in their first brief doorway exchange. By the end Yukine has stood on the Nakano Sunplaza stage alongside Momiji, Kyoka, Rio Seibu and Haru Nabatame. For much of the intervening story she is entitled to speak for her own undertaking with Momiji and determined to keep the trio outside it. (SPV2-OBS-E01-12, 842.20–856.34 s · SPV2-OBS-E02-23, 194.98–315.87 s · SPV2-OBS-E07-02/19.)

## What the actress saw from her seat

At home Yukine is trying to learn a part. The boy in the script speaks of a world of real smiles. What could make one smile more real than another? Her mother hears her difficulty out, produces a ticket for Hotaru’s 2018 Budokan finale and sends her off to study with rehearsals approaching. In recollection Yukine singles out the people watching. She saw what their faces looked like and wants to be one of the people who can make others smile that way. She tells Haru this at a time when the first five still train together. She also wants to keep acting. Being able to move an audience gives her another way to understand the part she was trying to play at home. We see her mother again at her daughter’s Nakano concert. There is no account of her occupation, a plan to send her daughter into a second profession or a verdict on the anniversary show. Other girls were at Hotaru’s concert too. There is no established early acquaintance between them at the venue. (SPV2-OBS-E08-01/08, 5.05–49.79 and 643.81–714.41 s · SPV2-CLM-E08-01/08 · SPV2-OBS-E11-10, 598.000 s · SPV2-OBS-E12-04/22.)

President Yuki Hinase gives the inexperienced group materials and room to decide how to use them. Yukine can see a first task when the others are waiting for direction. Warm up against injury, then see how much choreography they can manage. She cites her stage and television work and acknowledges being new to idols. Kyoka picks things up quickly and looks stiff performing them. Rio needs the foundations and, more immediately, a hand up from the floor. Momiji dances particularly well and struggles to finish the outdoor run. Yukine asks for her advice. Momiji demonstrates counting, Yukine explains the lag, and they can try the section again together. Knowing which girl can solve a problem is an important part of Yukine’s skill. She can do this with matters as prosaic as admission to another group’s show. When Kyoka tells a customer she cannot come in, the customer takes her seriously. Yukine apologizes and welcomes her in. There are leaflets to pick up and a wrong date on a flyer. Yukine would like, just once, to eat an ice cream in peace. (SPV2-OBS-E08-02/03/04/05, 147.55–411.09 s · SPV2-CLM-E08-05.)

Momiji finds her on a bench with the ice. Yukine offers her half. Momiji thanks her and has to explain what for. She means all the work Yukine has done for the five. She has also noticed how Yukine *plays* the cool Yukine. The actress believed she had fooled everybody. The girl who can repair a stranger’s welcome is uneasy about the cuteness she sees in the others and doubts she can give that to an audience herself. Making herself useful is an answer within reach. Momiji finds the girl who admits this cute too. Yukine asks her to keep the confidence and asks her to help lead TINGS. Momiji joins their names to make ゆきもじ, Yukimoji. The name belongs to a partnership *inside the five* before it belongs to a rival unit. To call the coolness an act is to recognize training and self-protection as well as theater. The work for Rio and for the customer would hold up even if nobody knew the person doing it felt insecure. Later, when the whole group manages a run-through, the leader is quite ready to demand another rehearsal. The friends who protest also know what she has done for them. (SPV2-OBS-E08-06/09, 414.15–561.74 s and cues 994–1019 · SPV2-CLM-E08-06/09.)

Listen first to the pronoun on the page. The boy in the script says 僕. At the agency Yukine’s imposing 私様 goes with the scrupulous courtesy of a colleague’s surname and さん. On the bench with Momiji she allows herself the plain 私. Next morning 私様 is back, and the girls who had received surnames hear their given names. Much later, breaking away in the street, Yukine tells the trio to give up familiar address. Access can be granted and can be withdrawn in ordinary ways an English “I” would conceal. Yukine and Momiji understand each other’s habits, but privacy does not always survive their jokes. Momiji lets slip that Yukine compared obstinate Kyoka to a tissue stuck to the laundry. Yukine is embarrassed in front of the very friend under discussion. The expression will survive to E11 as a way the members can tell one another what they are seeing. The simile establishes an everyday irritation and occasion for private wit. It gives little basis for a household routine. (SPV2-OBS-E08-01/06/31 · SPV2-OBS-E07-23 · SPV2-OBS-E03-05/06, 471.83 s onward · SPV2-ADJ-E11-039.)

## The cost of believing her own assessment

One girl troubles the early leader’s judgment. Haru does everything competently and nothing Yukine sees in rehearsal especially distinguishes her. Yukine first advises her as if this were the fear of a novice dancing in front of other people. Later she asks whether the independent practice she organized was wrong for Haru and offers to change it. Haru thanks her. The audience learns their desires in conversation. Yukine wants to see those smiles, and Haru wants a world where anyone can freely say which idol they love. Neither ambition gives Yukine the missing facts about Haru’s experience. It is Momiji who suspects Haru of holding back and tells her. Yukine doubts her. Following up brings them to a doorway from which they can see Haru dance alone at a level Yukine has never seen her show beside them. Images of changed poses and a stylized background give the viewer the contrast. The watchers see the dance. Neither gets a direct statement of motive from Haru. (SPV2-OBS-E08-03/07/08/10/11, 228.26–277.50, 566.46–714.41 and 806.09–962.29 s · SPV2-CLM-E08-03/07/11.)

Yukine feels deceived. President Yuki tells Yukine and Momiji that Haru has held back for their sake. Two scoped Japanese listeners agree on 君たちのためだよ at 987.5–988.6 s, where the subtitle garbles the phrase. Yuki goes on to describe the distance in ability and the possibility that full exertion would hurt the others. There are tears and embarrassment at the thought of the lessons Yukine thought she had been giving. One mistaken diagnosis begins to outweigh all the things her leadership actually did for the five. The president asks if she resents what has happened and wants Haru to give her all. Yukine does. Yuki proposes that the pair leave and become convincing rivals, so Haru will have a reason to draw on her ability to protect her group. The plan could cost the two their places even if it works. They take it up, train under Nanami, watch the trio’s debut news and go to its shows. Those choices require sustained professional ambition as well as affection. Neither girl is shown privately authorizing the other to feel exactly what she feels. Rio, Kyoka and Haru get a public declaration in the street without the preceding explanation. The explanation reaches us in E08. (SPV2-OBS-E08-13/14/15, 963.38–1152.07 s · SPV2-XREV-E08-01 · SPV2-CLM-E08-13/14.)

Seen from the trio’s side beforehand, Yukine gives a cutting estimate of their prospects, then turns up in the audience for the sellout she predicted they could not manage. She spots that Haru adjusts to the girls beside her. She spots trouble with Kyoka by the disappearance of a bad joke. When Kyoka agrees without a quip that she has failed to grow, Yukine goes to Haru for an explanation. She agrees to help, endures Haru’s embrace, then discovers she is supposed to do something useful after Haru’s improvised attempt at hypnosis. Later Kyoka asks Naoki to ease off and separately tells Haru and Yukine to keep the matter private. Haru remembers that instruction when Naoki asks them about Kyoka. Yukine takes Kyoka’s repeated insistence as a comic cue to do the opposite. She tells him how Haru covered the missed entrance and takes responsibility for the disclosure. Knowledge and goodwill make her an effective informant. The available exchange gives her an ambiguous claim to permission at best. E04 finds her publicly reproaching Kyoka for wasting an opportunity that a performer still waiting for a stage would value, then admitting she went too far. She tells Naoki where to find Kyoka and later speaks to Kyoka alone backstage. An intervening gold effect questions her claim to have discovered the location by chance. It does not show how she found it. Kyoka later says Yukine helped her hold on to her dream. Neither the thanks nor the better outcome erases the original intrusion. (SPV2-OBS-E02-23/29 · SPV2-OBS-E03-05/07/08/25/26, 827.27–963.28 s · E03-authority and E03-mute · SPV2-OBS-E04-07/13/21/27, especially 858.399 s · SPV2-OBS-E05-22.)

It matters whom she is looking after. With Rio she answers a specific fear that Rio’s shortcomings drove them away, wiping sauce from her face while she gives a firm no. She will also see Rio initiate a repair with Momiji at the lockers. That glimpse comes *after* the night Naoki waited for Rio to come and practice, and concerns a pot of pudding and a conditional request for teaching. It confers no knowledge of the private conversation with him. With Kyoka she can leave the reason for the split undisclosed while fixing a knot. Distracted by the failed recruitment efforts, Haru gets to a shop counter without the 130 yen she needs for a two-piece ice. Yukine puts down the 130 yen and then points out where Haru got the ice she offers as an inducement to return. With Momiji, in the quiet of the locker room, she can actually name something she herself receives. Momiji’s company keeps her from brooding alone. Yukine also calls Momiji away from coaching Rio because their own practice matters. An account of secret devotion to the trio that gives her no professional or personal stake in the duo cannot explain that objection. (SPV2-OBS-E06-07/08, 702.04–930.93 s · SPV2-OBS-E07-01/03/24/25/26/35, 0–40.31 and 641.72–715.85 s.)

## A rival who wants a place beside Haru

When Naoki asks the two to return, Yukine refuses. Haru treasures their old practices. Yukine says she has never wanted to go back to *those days*. There is a specific grievance in that choice of words. A restoration that reproduced Haru’s restraint would bring back the very condition Yukine risked their places to change. When Haru cites their faithful attendance, Momiji gives a flustered denial. Yukine follows with 私様たちはただ ライバルの調査を, a plural explanation about scouting their rivals. Distinguishing those two speakers matters. Yukine can supply a reason in the name of the pair while Momiji has one of her own. Yukine then puts her own measure before Haru. She has worked hard and still cannot approach Haru’s level. She cannot forgive the lack of change or join while Haru keeps back what she can do. When Haru says giving everything would hurt everyone and leaves, Yukine drops to her hands and knees and apologizes to Yuki for their failure. Momiji comes down beside her and says they did what they could. One assesses the outcome of the wager and the other what they put into it. (SPV2-OBS-E07-05/23/28, especially 1057.97–1175.36 s · SPV2-XREV-E07-01 · SPV2-OBS-E08-16/17, 1153.96–1254.97 s.)

Only after Haru leaves does Yuki disclose earlier group membership. The four hear the account that connects Haru’s restraint to HY:RAIN. They lack the viewer’s access to Haru’s memories and to the old locker room. Yukine can now understand the scale of what two rivals tried to provoke. Kyoka ends the rival arrangement, calls them back, and stakes the five’s future on what they can show Haru together. The four take up the proposal. Haru still has a decision to make. Naoki goes to her, then gives her time apart to answer. The shared song, *Be Your Light!!*, is the first they had put together as five. Returning costs Yukine the independent route she and Momiji had been pursuing. It also makes their earlier practice available to everybody again. She invokes the work under Nanami, introduces herself with a small hesitation, then regains her expansive manner. All five perform. The sound moves from a layered mix to the exertion that follows; a scoped hearing cannot allocate individual breaths. In the dressing room, four are bent over and Haru remains upright. Yukine wants to catch Haru. Momiji means to pass her. Both apologize when Kyoka takes them to task for leaving. Yukine has agreed to carry a difference in ability *inside* a working group and to let a younger friend correct her there. (SPV2-OBS-E08-18 · SPV2-OBS-E09-07/08/24/27/28/29, 433.57–546.44 and 1202–1267.99 s · SPV2-CLM-E09-24/28 · SPV2-XREV-E09-01.)

Her first experience of being somebody’s particular favorite momentarily deprives her of the manner with which she might have been expected to receive it. A young woman at the table says she fell for Yukine at the recent regular live. Yukine recovers and later speaks of responsibility. The work of making an audience smile has acquired an address. A publicity challenge supplies a smaller deflation. Yukine solemnly refers to この祇園寺雪音, this very Yukine Gionji, and promises to endure an ordeal for TINGS, only to cry out when someone presses her foot. She appears on a cooking programme too. These jobs support composure under some demands and a sense of humor the show can exercise at her expense. They do not identify a culinary specialty, private hobby or favorite food. An ice shared as a reward has a history; one appearance with a cook does not. (SPV2-OBS-E10-02/06/21/25, 51.20–109.94 and 367.20–455.01 s · SPV2-CLM-E10-21 · SPV2-ADJ-E10-011/014.)

Ren Kurogane, Haru’s former partner, questions Yukine’s confidence in her expressive ability. Yukine asks whether Ren has even seen their work. She rejects being reduced to Haru’s backup and applauds Kyoka for challenging the visitor, even though Haru herself did not give Kyoka the right to wager where she would perform. Yukine too speaks of keeping Haru. After seeing HY:RAIN perform on a screen she is visibly shaken. Later she recognizes the depth of Ren’s attachment and objects to admitting defeat before the five stand on stage. Kyoka names expressive power as a distinctive thing Yukine brings. Yukine in turn offers Kyoka company should anything go wrong with the plan for Nakano. She does not promise a flawless routine. At the concert, the *On Your Mark!!* first-verse assignment comes to Yukine after Momiji and before Haru’s pre-chorus. The published parts and aligned recordings support that handoff; exact overdub layers remain unresolved. Afterward the agency offers a joint competitive Budokan appearance. Kyoka asks the others. Yukine wants people to see how much they have grown. Her future aim is public again, in the company of the people against whom she once measured the failure of her leadership. The encounter itself remains unplayed at the boundary. (SPV2-OBS-E10-13/17/26/28/29 · SPV2-OBS-E11-18/29 · SPV2-OBS-E12-21/29, 6.66–111.46 and 1146.64–1283.92 s · SPV2-ADJ-E12-013.)

## Where she is easier and harder to anticipate

After Nakano, suppose a member she knows well drops her characteristic joking answer, then misses an entrance during routine rehearsal. There is strong support for Yukine noticing the first change and looking for the particular difficulty behind the second. She might ask a trusted peer or propose altering the exercise. If only an immediate practical problem is visible, reaching down to help has especially good support. Make the friend explicitly request privacy and a manager ask for the story. Yukine’s reading of Kyoka’s repeated prohibition is a genuine adverse precedent. Her later ability to work openly with Kyoka sits beside it. There is moderate support for conflict between discretion and intervention, little for certainty about which she would choose. With a new acquaintance whose ordinary behavior is unknown she loses the very cue she used to recognize Kyoka’s trouble. Acting skill supplies no independent license to read a stranger’s mind.

The bench conversation supports a specific intimacy with Momiji, including the choice to keep a confidence and ask for partnership. The series gives no comparable account of Yukine’s romantic attraction, private dating life or future with Momiji outside their work. Her relationship with her mother spans two concerts and an acting problem. The mother’s private view of the rivalry, and what either would choose if acting and TINGS came into direct conflict, also remain open.
