# Nanami on either side of the stage

Before TINGS performs for two thousand people at Nakano Sunplaza, Nanami is sitting in the audience with a lightstick close to her face. She can already picture Yukine Gionji missing choreography and Momiji Ito forgetting words. A woman behind her reminds her who is going on stage. Nanami knows perfectly well that it is her pupils’ turn. The knowledge makes waiting in her seat no easier. Earlier, Yukine and Momiji asked Nanami to give them an especially demanding session. Naoki Hinase passed their practice room and saw both girls on the floor. In the practice room Nanami has something definite to do about fatigue. She can tell them to get back up. From the audience all she can do is watch. What looks at first like a contradiction between professional confidence and anxiety describes two positions the same person can occupy in another performer’s life. (SPV2-OBS-E05-03/22/43, 180.18–345.20 s · SPV2-OBS-E12-05/22, especially 263.4–279.7 s · SPV2-ADJ-E12-023/034.)

兎塚七海 is an established performer with FFF. Naoki credits her group with a million-selling single and its own television programme. The success belongs to FFF collectively. At Brightest we meet her as the person Yukine and Momiji expect for a lesson, then as the person Haru Nabatame is delighted enough to embrace when she arrives early from a recording. At work on a regional food programme she is Rio Seibu’s senior on camera. With Naoki she can invoke their prior acquaintance and talk over employment and professional trust. These are distinct privileges. Training two girls and being able to introduce a group to a concert audience confer no general authority over TINGS’s management, bookings or lineup. The story gives no childhood, recruitment scene or private account of why Nanami first wanted this work. Her history is clearest where it has become somebody else’s practical resource. (SPV2-OBS-E01-12, 842.20–856.34 s · SPV2-OBS-E05-03/05/22/26/36 · SPV2-OBS-E10-25.)

## A teacher her pupils ask for

When the duo wait for a lesson in E01, the ordinary thing to do in Nanami’s absence is get started. They can expect her without needing her permission for every move. E05 gives the expectation a face and a professional setting. She reports that a recording for FFF finished early. Haru rushes to greet her. Nanami names her responsibility for educating Yukine and Momiji and approves their eagerness. Yukine starts the request and Momiji completes it. They would like a harder session than usual. Later the pair are down at her feet and Nanami wants them up again. In the adjacent room the trio are working on balance and Rio goes down too. Naoki passes both rooms. The connection tells us what several young bodies are being asked to do on one working day. It would be easy to give Nanami credit for a new training method or blame her for how hard Rio finds balance. Neither conclusion follows from the doorway. Rio is in another room. We have the duo’s own request for greater difficulty and the cost visible to a visitor, without a count of repetitions or a measure of any lasting improvement. (SPV2-OBS-E01-12 · SPV2-OBS-E05-03/22/32/43, especially JP cues 849–865.)

E08 tells us what that routine once cost in decisions. Yukine and Momiji had practiced in the original five-member TINGS. President Yuki Hinase proposed they leave and become rivals capable of drawing Haru’s exceptional performance out of hiding. Both accepted a risk that even success might never restore the five. Nanami says Yuki has told her the circumstances. She asks the two whether they appreciate what they are taking on, hears their responses and calls for determination. Pictures show weight-bearing exercises, supported inversions and outdoor running. Yukine earlier slowed down for Momiji on a run. They now work as a pair in order to put pressure on a group they still care about. Nanami can prepare them for a challenge others chose. Yuki made the proposal and the girls entered into it. Precisely how much Yuki disclosed to Nanami about Haru’s prior group, the conditions on her return to idol work or the possibility of emotional harm remains unknown. It matters because otherwise every good intervention by this instructor begins to look like a judgment on the whole plan. (SPV2-OBS-E08-14/15/27, 1033.33–1152.07 s · SPV2-CLM-E08-14/15.)

When the plan fails to persuade Haru on its original terms, the four other girls agree instead to ask her to stand beside them as five performers. At the regular live Yukine invokes Nanami’s training in addressing Haru. A pupil can take the demanding work of the rival period into a changed working relationship. Nanami does not deliver the invitation, negotiate the return with Haru or choose the five’s song. Later Kyoka’s public thanks at Nakano explicitly include exacting and kind seniors among the many people who made the show possible. Those collective thanks give the instructor’s contribution a place. Yukine’s earlier appeal is stronger evidence that *this* preparation meant something to *this* pupil. Momiji’s request for a tougher session and later willingness to compete at full stretch show an active student rather than a product Nanami can claim to have made. (SPV2-OBS-E09-08/09/14/24/27, 898.63–949.62 s for Yukine’s appeal · SPV2-OBS-E12-17/28.)

## Giving Rio something to say on camera

The choices that put Rio beside Nanami are quite important to what Nanami actually does for her. President Yuki claims the group’s three offers as a present. A sweetshop manager wants Kyoka; the shop needs a second girl on the same day as a regional television assignment. Rio chooses television before she knows who will be on location with her. Only then does Nanami disclose her participation. Nanami welcomes her junior. Rio makes an expansive promise of success. At the farm Rio gives the crew her own name, professional greeting and deep bow. Then the camera rolls and the new presenter begins supplying the ends of the sentences Nanami has started. She repeats “we’ve come,” a verbal ending and an invitation to go and look. The introduction progresses because Nanami can present the location, make room for a second voice, and carry on even when the second voice has very little to add. Nobody watching the finished programme would necessarily know how much scaffolding the exchange required. The series shows no finished broadcast. (SPV2-OBS-E05-04/05/06/14/24/34/46, 346.96–539.58 and 707.35–843.10 s · SPV2-CLM-E05-04/05.)

Director Sato wants two distinct things, a harvest at Kobayashi’s farm and a subsequent food report. The programme camera frames Nanami presenting a vegetable, while Rio’s encounters with roots and a small creature produce usable surprises. Nanami can make an interruption part of the work. When Rio mispronounces tomato, she supplies the correction and turns Rio’s more elaborate second try into an occasion to credit the pronunciation. The written source distinguishes トムト, トマト and トメェト. It does not by itself establish the exact sound of the joke. Later Rio’s attempted flourish about which food suits her jaw needs the ordinary word for mouth. Nanami supplies that too. These are useful indications of her professional wit. She returns attention to the business of the segment while allowing Rio a real contribution. Concern for a junior is one plausible motive. A working presenter also has an immediate duty to the farmer and to Sato to produce something they can use. The same acts meet both obligations, so the degree of private affection cannot be read straight off the recording. (SPV2-OBS-E05-06/07/34/46/48, JP cues 1001–1018, 1040–1053 · SPV2-CLM-E05-05.)

The tasting makes it possible to see where that method ends. Nanami comments on freshness and gives the crew an enthusiastic report. Naoki begins a remark about her and eggplant and leaves it incomplete. The gold effect appears on her during the appraisal. Together these give him a reason to notice how much expertise is involved in what she is saying. Neither gives the spectator Nanami’s exact taste in vegetables, her reason for choosing a particular word or an instruction to juniors to disguise every dislike. Rio had received different advice *from Naoki*, to give an honest impression. What she says about the green pepper is that it is bitter, several times. Sato has good material from Nanami and will lose Rio’s tasting. She had already approved material from the harvest. Nanami’s fluency cannot solve the practical conflict between the instruction and the format after the food is on the table. Her usable report preserves an option for the editor. Sato and Naoki settle the cut. No resulting broadcast or audience verdict follows. (SPV2-OBS-E05-06/07/25/35/48, 853.53–1010.41 s · SPV2-CLM-E05-05.)

After the discussion with the director Nanami checks on Naoki. When he apologizes for having troubled her, she says that *she* is fine, leaves the thought unfinished and draws his attention beyond herself. Rio then gives him a much more sweeping apology for being a burden again. A senior can help by quietly directing the manager toward the person for whom a spoiled take has become a judgment on herself. Nanami has seen the same failure as Rio and Naoki and responds on a different scale. She gives neither an invented assurance about what television viewers will see nor a lecture to Rio about gratitude. This is a small act, supported by the pictured order and words of the exchange. The extent of what Nanami privately understood about Rio’s feelings remains undemonstrated. (SPV2-OBS-E05-07/14/25, particularly native 971.512–978.102 s.)

## The colleague who can tease a manager

Away from the camera Nanami calls the manager ナオくん, Nao-kun. She floats an FFF submanager job. He points to her group’s existing achievements. Coming from *him*, the praise sounds ironic to her. She knows enough about his past work to notice how little the three girls know about it and to urge him to tell them. He gives his reason for declining to trade on that history. He wants them to trust what he can do for them now. There is professional respect on both sides and sufficient familiarity to disagree about how a newcomer earns credibility. The exchange does not name a previous client or make either of them the other’s former employer. Nanami’s offer can be an expression of respect, a real attempt to recruit someone useful, or both. She does not persist when he keeps his position. The other young woman who telephones about a single and asks him to write during E05 is Kei. That request, the private penalties and the unfinished lyric business are not Nanami’s. (SPV2-OBS-E05-05/08/26/36, 635.88–679.54 and 1011.34–1048.48 s · SPV2-CLM-E05-08/26.)

Her register has a shape beyond the familiar address. She can put a direct demand to two trainees who have just asked her to press them harder. Her public greeting at the farm carries a sequence of linked clauses a less experienced colleague can echo. She can give one correct word on camera before a mistake hardens into embarrassment, leave a sentence to a competent colleague to finish, or make an ironic reply to an old acquaintance who applauds her seniority. None requires an inference from an uninspected inflection. E10 offers a plausible extension to a larger audience. A stage sign bills TINGS as the opening act at an FFF NEXT STEP tour event; three FFF members stand opposite five TINGS members. The blonde green-eyed presenter closely matches Nanami in E05. This is a probable appearance in a public welcome. There is no individually credited spoken introduction, specified song from the slot or evidence that she procured the booking. What the invitation means for TINGS is substantial regardless of who signed it. The host group offers the five a public their own theatre could not supply. (SPV2-OBS-E05-22/34/36 · SPV2-OBS-E10-10/25/48, E05 f0404 and E10 f0842–0847, chiefly 791.29–799.298 s · SPV2-PF-E10-08.)

## Sitting through somebody else’s number

At Nakano Nanami is the addressee of a reassurance. She is also the person who specifies what might go wrong for whom. In her imagination it is Yukine who might miss choreography and Momiji who might forget lyrics. Neither mistake is attested by her fear. The three in the front turn back, and the reverse view at 266.5 s places the responder in the row behind FFF, where Eiko Kikuchi and the Yura Yura Sisters sit. The heard 立つわけではない conveys that *Nanami herself* will not be going on stage. The printed 立つだけではない would supply a different construction. Natalya is the leading candidate among the individual speakers. The combination of picture and scoped hearing still leaves her identification open. At 923.339 s there are tears in Nanami’s eyes. She has seen her pupils join the number. Her precise evaluation of every step, her private judgment about the rival strategy and the point at which the tears began are unavailable. Those modest bounds preserve what the performance changes for her. Someone who once had to keep the two practicing can now sit among the people they prepared to perform for. (SPV2-OBS-E12-05/22/32 · SPV2-CLM-E12-05 · SPV2-ADJ-E12-023/034 · [Audience attribution erratum](https://github.com/deep-blue-zero/anime-manga-ln-games-analysis/blob/63dcf84612f493fba35a71a4e7f77bbcfab849c6/series/shine-post/V2%20Analysis/07%20Audits%20and%20Handoffs/SHINEPOST_V2_E12_AUDIENCE_ATTRIBUTION_ERRATUM.md).)

## What her work predicts

After Nakano, suppose a novice in a fresh farm segment freezes after a greeting. With the recording under way, an available prompt and a familiar assignment, Nanami has strong grounds to continue the exchange herself, make a small correction usable and leave the novice room to contribute. She did all three with Rio. If a director then rejects a take and the novice blames herself for the whole shoot, there is moderate support for turning the manager’s attention toward her without promising editorial control. If instead the trainees have personally requested a difficult exercise and the session is hers to conduct, a direct demand for another effort fits better than that quiet cue. Remove her working responsibility and put her in the seats. Concern has a strong precedent there, intervention from the audience has none. In each case her relationship to the particular task changes what help she can give.

Her demonstrated range is professional. Haru’s warm greeting and the ease with which Nanami can tease Naoki offer glimpses of ordinary social ease, alongside a frank display of nerves when the pupils perform. We have almost no sustained private speech at home, stated hobbies, habitual food choices or account of how she reconciles her own career with teaching. No romantic or intimate history is established with Naoki or anybody else. The five accept the proposed joint competitive appearance at Budokan in the final Brightest meeting. Nanami’s role in their future, including any further coaching or booking, remains unknown. (SPV2-OBS-E12-29/39, 1146.64–1283.92 s.)
