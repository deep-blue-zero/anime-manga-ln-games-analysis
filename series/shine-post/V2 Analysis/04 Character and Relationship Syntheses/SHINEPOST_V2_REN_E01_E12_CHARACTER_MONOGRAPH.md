# Ren Kurogane and the promise to keep up

Ren Kurogane (黒金蓮), leader of HY:RAIN, has one particularly costly habit. When she discovers work she wants to do, she imagines who she will do it with. As a child she invites two friends to audition and takes seriously the one who declines. As a star she walks into another group's ticket sale convinced that the progress her colleagues have made entitles her to bring Haru Nabatame home. Between those acts stands a year of work Ren understands as keeping a promise. The question she learns to ask differently is who gets to say when that promise has been kept.

## Before anyone had a place at the front

The earliest Ren belongs to a group of three. In the childhood scenes she recalls in E11, she rushes ahead, Haru follows smiling, and Homare (虎渡誉) raises objections and gives advice worth having. They eat together, work on addition, copy a performer on television and admire the shapes they have baked. In middle school, Ren enlists Haru to back her up on the difference Hotaru's commercial supposedly makes to the taste of doughnuts. Homare points out it is an ad, then wears the same concert shirt as the other two. Ren can enjoy this small disagreement without having to bring Homare around. Her later choice of a doughnut bag to take to Haru draws on a shared practice. Buying someone the familiar thing is much better evidenced than any ranking of Ren's own favorite foods. At the Budokan Ren corrects herself. The crowd is absorbed, 夢中になってる. Hotaru is actively drawing them in, 夢中にさせてる. Ren attends to how voice, gaze and movement can produce an effect across a whole room. She wants to be able to do that. When she offers both friends a part in the venture, she has a different reason for each. Haru can turn her hand to things, Homare knows idols. Homare sees the possibility that Ren is afraid to try alone. That is a friend's perceptive tease. It leaves room for Ren's precise account of why the work itself matters. Homare enjoys watching and promises support. Ren and Haru clasp hands and pass the audition on their first try. Haru's separate meeting with Hotaru after that concert becomes known to the viewer in E12. Ren went to the concert, but Haru went back for her bag without her. Ren has no demonstrated access to the exchange that gave Haru's ambition its name. (SPV2-OBS-E11-23, SPV2-OBS-E11-24, SPV2-OBS-E11-34, SPV2-OBS-E12-25.) ## What Haru saw, and what Ren did afterward

In E09 Haru recalls ordinary work with Ren, Yawara Naekawa, Itoha Karabayashi and Aoba Karabayashi. The girls watch a reference on a tablet. Haru and Ren fall asleep leaning against each other on the train. When Haru masters a new pattern on her first attempt and calls it luck, Ren still praises her and says 負けないからね 春, “I won't lose, Haru.” Admiration gives Ren something to do on her own next turn. At the center selection, the difference in what the two can currently do seems to Ren to put their entire common future out of reach. Haru sees her crying and hears 私も春みたいに 上手になりたいよ, a wish to become as good as Haru. Haru takes the distress seriously and leaves. Neither the judges' choice nor a request by Ren that Haru withdraw is heard. When Yuki later tells Naoki and the other four TINGS members about Haru on the roof, Haru has gone and Ren is absent. The young woman there in the viewer's memory has no chance to answer the account of what her tears meant. (SPV2-OBS-E09-07, SPV2-OBS-E09-21, SPV2-OBS-E09-22, SPV2-OBS-E09-33, SPV2-CLM-E09-22.) [E09](https://github.com/deep-blue-zero/anime-manga-ln-games-analysis/blob/63dcf84612f493fba35a71a4e7f77bbcfab849c6/series/shine-post/V2%20Analysis/02%20Sequential%20Episode%20Readings/SHINEPOST_V2_E09_DEEP_READING.md)

Ren's own account, given much later at the waterside, lets those tears have another consequence. She blames herself for failing to keep pace or fulfill their promise. She addresses an empty locker. Haru had been at the door during the earlier crying, and is absent for this apology. Yawara, Itoha and Aoba find Ren. Ren goes back to basic vocal work, doubles her dance lessons, changes meals and takes acting work. The pictures give her three working partners with different tasks, Aoba in voice training, Itoha in dance, Yawara over food. Nanoka Hiumi joins later. Ren's criterion has changed from wanting her own turn at a new combination to being able to receive everything Haru can give on stage. She can describe that ambition in the singular, 今の私なら, across a montage of the group. The other four helped make HY:RAIN good enough to headline Budokan. Their individual reasons for putting in the work are their own. (SPV2-OBS-E11-25, SPV2-OBS-E11-26, SPV2-OBS-E11-35, SPV2-OBS-E11-36, SPV2-CLM-E11-25.) ## The visitor at the ticket table

By E10 Ren can make a case that HY:RAIN has the skills Haru once needed her original group to develop. She has watched Haru belonging to another group, too. E11 shows her in the trio's audience and shows the mall embrace. In her own telling she objects both to the stage she thinks inadequate and to Haru standing with other people. Jealousy and a credible judgment of professional capacity can move the same person. Neither qualifies Ren to decide where Haru should work. In the invitation だから帰ろ、春 the short verb and separately addressed name make returning sound as natural as going home together after school. Ren turns to the other four and calls them backup dancers. She reduces Rio to the difficulty of dancing while singing, Momiji to dancing alone, Yukine to her expressive claim, and, after a hesitation, Kyoka to getting things right. Rio notices how detailed these verdicts are for someone who says 見なくてもわかる, “I can tell without watching.” Her later recollection establishes some actual viewing. It does not identify the source or accuracy of every assessment. Kyoka hears hers again long after Ren has left. ## Asking again, and coming to see

The next day Ren asks to put off a work review, takes the doughnuts and messages Haru to check she is free after her lesson. This time Ren apologizes for yesterday's intrusion. Asked if that's all, she briefly treats the apology as sufficient, corrects herself, and asks Haru to work with her again. Strangers recognize レンレン under her hood. Kyoka gets all three of them out of the crowd, then gives Ren a justifiable scolding about her disguise. Ren accepts help from the performer whose competence she recently treated as negligible. When Kyoka supposes the “promise” means their wager, 違う interrupts her. It matters to Ren that Kyoka understand which two children made it. Her substantial account at the waterside follows that correction. Ren can both brush a rival off and give the same girl an explanation she did not owe her. Haru refuses before the concert. She identifies herself as TINGS' Haru and invites Ren to watch. Ren pauses, says she will persist, gives her the doughnuts and asks for secrecy. Her colleagues have already guessed her destination. Ren denies running away as the others catch up. Humor briefly restores something of the older friendship without undoing the refusal. In the anniversary audience Ren teases Homare for buying too much merchandise, then introduces her to her colleagues as a childhood friend. Homare knows about the visit and thinks Ren wants the earlier pair back. She has watched them for years. Ren disputes her right to claim that knowledge. How the news reached Homare is unshown. Ren has already given her own account of being hurt by Haru's closeness to others, which makes Homare's reading plausible without making it exhaustive. Their closeness and the limits of its disclosure can be described without assigning Ren a romantic history or sexual motive. (SPV2-OBS-E11-27, SPV2-OBS-E11-37, SPV2-OBS-E12-23, SPV2-OBS-E12-32.) At Nakano Ren has the opportunity Haru offered. Haru and Kyoka share the front in a number all five perform. A short catch in the chorus gap is strongly associated with the shot of Ren, but a breath cannot tell us the content of a judgment. Her own words supply it after the performance. Seated near Homare, she identifies TINGSの青天国春, Haru Nabatame of TINGS. The once confident visitor can revise her estimate because she has asked to be shown, has heard Haru's own choice, and has now seen a place where her old friend's ambition is intelligible. The edit leaves the contribution of each of those things to her change open. This is a private acknowledgment in the seats. A direct concession to Haru or an apology to Kyoka is still unshown. ## How far this portrait travels

When a friend makes a difficult movement look easy in a shared practice room, Ren is likely to praise the achievement and want another attempt herself. Where the gap seems to end a common future, the same comparison can bring withdrawal and private self-reproach before she is ready to act. When Ren believes sustained work has repaired the gap, approaching the friend directly, perhaps with a familiar gift, fits her better than waiting for an agency negotiation. The risk increases when she treats a rival as the obstacle. Her claims become categorical and she can presume to speak for colleagues who then have to come and fetch her. Face-to-face refusal alone has failed to change her position at E11. A refusal accompanied by an invitation and a performance she can inspect gives the E12 revision a specific precedent. Continued insistence after acknowledging the friend's present place, or a separate case of quietly accepting refusal on first hearing, would change the weight of these tendencies. Nothing here fixes how she would answer an unfamiliar business proposal, which friend she would tell first, or how she sounds when singing without a commercial mix.
