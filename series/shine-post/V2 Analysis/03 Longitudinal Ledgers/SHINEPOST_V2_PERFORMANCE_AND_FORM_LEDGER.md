# SHINE POST V2 — Performance and Form Ledger

## Responsibility and present state

This ledger owns cumulative musical-event and audiovisual-form records under [method §6](../00%20Frameworks/SHINEPOST_V2_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md#6-music-performance-and-visual-form) and the [AV protocol](../00%20Frameworks/SHINEPOST_V2_AV_RECONCILIATION_AND_INTAKE_PROTOCOL.md). **No V2 events, vocal baselines or formal motifs have been established.** V1's Yellow Rose analysis remains a V1 route, not a preaccepted V2 event record.

Use stable `SPV2-PF-…` identities for real events/patterns. Separate the descriptive evidence from its psychological, relational or thematic interpretation. Required details depend on the claim; unavailable channels remain disclosed rather than filled by inference.

## Complete musical-event contract

| Field group | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Source/clock | Witness and derivative IDs, episode, full event interval, calibrated cue/frame anchors and precision |
| Identity | Song/cue title or provisional identity, verification route, composition/arrangement/recording distinctions |
| Significance | `M0` incidental, `M1` supportive, `M2` diagnostic, `M3` state-changing, with narrative rationale |
| Dramatic configuration | Conditions before the event, stakes, performers, listeners/viewers, absent persons and knowledge boundaries |
| Sound status | Diegetic/nondiegetic/ambiguous position, transitions and audible mix context |
| Internal segmentation | Observable entrances/exits and changes in sections, texture, density, dynamics, arrangement and vocal distribution |
| Verbal layer | Matched Japanese lyric/wording evidence and provenance; translation separated from text and heard delivery |
| Embodied performance | Movement/choreography, coordination, gaze, posture, breath and vocal delivery within actual capability |
| Screen construction | Framing, spatial relations, camera/editing, reaction shots, temporal order and sound–image interaction |
| Consequence | What changes or is revealed; links to affected character, directed relationship and claim records |
| Alternatives/limits | Rival explanations, production conventions, uncertain attribution, missing channels and disconfirmation |
| Verification | Inspected interval/modality, sampling, focused reinspection, independent findings and actual disposition |

M2/M3 receive the complete record and focused reinspection; M3 writes through to affected character, relationship and claim state. M0 need not become a long record. M1 earns a cumulative entry when recurring function matters. The significance scale measures narrative consequence, not musical quality or personal liking.

Preserve the whole event's configuration and development even when a thesis depends on a brief passage. Do not infer solo/ensemble allocation from camera focus or voice-actor recognition. Credit/track/lyric metadata and visible or audible corroboration must be adequate to the exact attribution. Song similarity does not establish the same recording, and a studio release cannot supply the anime's timing or mix.

## Voice, sound and visual pattern records

For performed voice, record person/addressee, situation, public/private/stage context, exact inspected interval and evidence class. Describe contour, pace, intensity, breath, laughter, overlap and voice quality against a relevant within-person baseline. Numerical latency/duration belongs to `AM`; perceived delivery to `AO`/`CAV` or attributed `HL`. Register wording belongs to `TXT` with provenance. Psychological meaning remains `INF`/`INT`.

Track sound bridges, silence, offscreen sound, ambience, recurring motifs, blocking, framing, editing and camera motion when they bear on the reading. Distinguish an isolated shot from a recurring grammar. Static frames establish endpoints, not continuous paths. Source frame rate does not certify effective inspection cadence. A missing image in a sampled bundle is not proof that a gesture never occurred.

For each recurring pattern retain supporting instances, contrasting instances, changed conditions and the boundary within which recurrence was sought. Explain the claimed function and a serious alternative. "No additional function established" is an acceptable result; avoid treating every formal choice as symbolic evidence.

## Reconciliation and write-through

The source register owns source identity and clock/capability receipts; the [evidence ledger](SHINEPOST_V2_EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIM_LEDGER.md) owns observations, claims and transitions. This ledger owns their complete event/pattern organization. Link both rather than copying competing claim formulations.

Accept load-bearing temporal claims only under the protocol's anchoring and resolution rules. Reported observations, disputed observations and interpretations remain distinguishable. The integrator records all dispositions, including preserved claims, and propagates accepted changes to [character](SHINEPOST_V2_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md) and [relationship](SHINEPOST_V2_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md) state. Frozen earlier event analyses remain intact with forward revisions.
