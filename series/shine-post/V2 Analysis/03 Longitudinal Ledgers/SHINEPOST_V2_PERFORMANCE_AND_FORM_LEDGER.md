Track sound bridges, silence, offscreen sound, ambience, recurring motifs, blocking, framing, editing and camera motion when they bear on the reading. Distinguish an isolated shot from a recurring grammar. Static frames establish endpoints, not continuous paths. Source frame rate does not certify effective inspection cadence. A missing image in a sampled bundle is not proof that a gesture never occurred.

For each recurring pattern retain supporting instances, contrasting instances, changed conditions and the boundary within which recurrence was sought.
