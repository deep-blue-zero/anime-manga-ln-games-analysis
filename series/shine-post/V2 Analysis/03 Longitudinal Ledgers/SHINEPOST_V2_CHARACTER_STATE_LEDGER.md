# SHINE POST V2 — Character State Ledger

## Responsibility and present state

This ledger owns cumulative individual state and monograph qualification under the [architecture](../00%20Frameworks/SHINEPOST_V2_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) and [reconstruction protocol](../00%20Frameworks/SHINEPOST_V2_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_PROTOCOL.md). The record contract below governs the episode updates following it. Monograph qualification remains subject to its own review.

V1's six character documents cover Naoki, Haru, Ren, Kyoka, Rio and the Momiji/Yukine dyad, as routed by the [V1 map](../../V1%20Analysis/00_README_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md). Those routes remain usable within V1's scope. They neither qualify nor exclude anyone from future V2 monographs and confer no reconstruction grade.

## Individual record contract

Assign a stable `SPV2-CH-…` record only when actual evidence is entered. Preserve verified names/aliases and distinguish the source person from analysis-subject or dyad identities. Every material state entry links evidence/claim IDs and its source boundary.

| Field group | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and circumstances | Verified name/aliases, role, relevant circumstances and source disclosure; unresolved identification stays unresolved |
| Boundary | Episode/presentation cutoff, reconstructed story time, continuity/witness and analyst exposure |
| Personal history | What the source establishes, when it happened, when it is disclosed and who knows it |
| Stable disposition | Repeated tendencies, values and priorities, with exceptions and alternatives |
| Developmental state | Current goals, self-understanding, skills, constraints and changes; separate development from later revelation |
| Situation | Stakes, mood, fatigue/pressure when evidenced, options and immediate context; no medical or diagnostic inference from mannerism |
| Knowledge and cognition | Beliefs, uncertainty, misunderstandings, attention/appraisal and limits on what the person can know |
| Behavior | Actions and non-actions, triggers, available alternatives, consequences and counterexamples |
| Ordinary life | Habits, preferences, amusements, comforts, teasing, routine cooperation and low-pressure choices actually shown |
| Voice and register | Japanese wording/register by interlocutor and state; separately linked heard delivery and performed/public voice |
| Capability | What the person can do and under what conditions; distinguish capacity, confidence, motivation and willingness |
| Relationships | Links to directed edges; retain how this person appraises/responds to each other person |
| Operational hypotheses | Conditional response families with inhibitors, overrides, uncertainty and disconfirmation tests; never a validated grade |
| Revision/dependencies | Evidence/claim IDs, prior state, reason for update and affected episodes, monographs or other outputs |

Unknown information stays explicit. Do not fill preferences, romantic attitudes, private habits or diagnostic labels from a template, genre expectations or another adaptation. An isolated choice may reflect necessity, accommodation or publicity rather than disposition. Keep the observation and interpretation separate.

## State changes and ordinary-life controls

Maintain a compact current view per person plus recoverable changes: prior state → new formulation, source boundary, evidence, counterevidence and linked claim transition. Preserve the original earlier reading. Record whether apparent change is development, context variation, strategic presentation, a new disclosure or unresolved.

Whenever a major dramatic pattern is proposed, seek a low-pressure control and a counterexample within the admitted source. Record unavailable controls as gaps. Do not invent missing scenes or let crisis behavior determine every everyday response. Stage, professional, peer, private and unfamiliar-person behavior may differ without contradiction.

The [relationship ledger](SHINEPOST_V2_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md) owns edges. The [performance ledger](SHINEPOST_V2_PERFORMANCE_AND_FORM_LEDGER.md) owns AV event detail. The [evidence ledger](SHINEPOST_V2_EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIM_LEDGER.md) owns claim formulations and revisions. Link them rather than maintaining competing copies of their facts.

## Monograph qualification and readiness

At specialist readiness and after material new evidence, review all evidence-bearing individuals, including supporting characters and adults. Qualification depends on differentiated, useful, traceable evidence for an independently understandable person, not centrality, popularity, file count or the original V1 roster.

| Required decision field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Subject and assessed boundary | Person identity, admitted source, evaluator and actual review scope |
| Evidence/rationale | Breadth, development, everyday behavior, relations, voice, limits and whether gaps prevent a sufficient portrait |
| Qualification state | `QUALIFIED_PENDING`, `COMPLETE_AT_BOUNDARY`, `DEFERRED_EVIDENCE`, or `REASSESS_AFTER_REVISION` |
| Production obligation | One canonical monograph path when created, owner, missing work or precise reassessment trigger |
| Self-containment review | Separate finding on whether the monograph explains the person without retrieving ledgers |
| Fidelity review | Separate evidence-grounded finding, decisive source routes, objections and disposition |
| Dependencies | Changes that invalidate or require reopening either review |

**No qualification rows exist yet.** Unassessed is not a disguised `DEFERRED_EVIDENCE` decision. Every future qualified person remains an open production obligation until the monograph satisfies its boundary. A dyad or ensemble document does not substitute for each qualified person's independent monograph.

## Update rule

Only the accountable integrator updates this shared ledger, alongside the accepted episode/reconciliation transaction. Each update records changed persons, actual inspection boundary, unresolved required evidence and downstream consequences, including a reasoned no-material-change result where appropriate. Later monographs and operational models must cite the state they consume; model validation remains a separate task under the reconstruction protocol.

### SPV2-CH-HARU · Haru Nabatame, 青天国春

**Role, history and attainable aim.** Production name card f514 gives なばためはる. The orange-haired girl holding a pink lightstick at Hotaru's 2018 finale tentatively wonders about joining the profession. Shared facial design, ornament and continuity into the present support identifying her as Haru. In the dedicated theatre she already fronts a thank-you to supporters but is shown alone questioning her suitability. Nine months into TiNgS, it is possible both to be someone’s favorite and to be commercially precarious. Her signpost ideal has two objects: broad love of idols, and the ability of each fan to speak openly of their own favorite. Motive should not be collapsed into acquiring fame for herself. [OBS 01–03, 18; CLM 01–03, 11; PF-E01-01/02.]

**Ordinary and professional conduct.** Braids, glasses, a school uniform, ice-pop sticks with winning messages and favorable traffic timing introduce a morning on which she makes chance events encouraging. At the agency she changes clothes and lets her hair down. The lively message signed in her own diminutive recurs in the final boast and wink. Her delight in imagining costumes, a CD or TV appearance makes an office message appear more promising than it is. She enters twice to test unwelcome news, guesses a stranger's identity, runs ahead and has to double back verbally to introductions. She can give a welcoming experience and miss a listener's attempt to object in the same encounter. One good day is an inadequate sample for a universal cheerful disposition. [OBS 03–04, 08, 22; CLM 03, 08, 15; REL-HARU-NAOKI, REL-HARU-KYOKA.]

**Development, knowledge and limit.** She does not know the history of Naoki's earlier refusal. She notices the difference between his determination against management and lack of desire; after an unsuccessful personal appeal she proposes that he look at the work. Her repeated maxim happens to rhyme with his earlier one. She does not witness the employment flashback or the cousins' private conversation. After the lesson she momentarily takes his response about a lack of shine as cause for disappointment. She hugs Kyoka before Kyoka begins her unfinished question. She ends as a member who has recruited a provisional manager, with both attendance tasks ahead. Foresight, technique, vocal superiority and an inability ever to lie remain unestablished. Hypothesis for later comparison: she can lower the cost of another person's choice once a direct appeal fails; her earlier rush shows that this accommodation is context-dependent. [OBS 13–22, 25; CLM 08, 10–13, 15.]

### SPV2-CH-KYOKA · Kyoka Tamaki, 玉城杏夏

The pink name card f517 gives たまききょうか. A polite professional greeting contrasts with her casual working relationship with Haru. In the first office she half-expects the reveal of a prank; at the meeting she herself stages a floor-sitting joke, theorizes tension and release, and cannot account for the expected laugh failing to materialize. Later Naoki anticipates her treasured joke before she tells it. This sequence establishes interest in comic construction, alongside her practical habit of questioning a plan. It supplies no heard vocal baseline or measure of her general success at comedy. Her specific industry aspiration in the meeting is a CD debut. [OBS 02, 04/05, 08/09/10, 13; CLM 09; REL-KYOKA-NAOKI, REL-NAOKI-KYOKA.]

She initially considers Naoki’s lack of willingness a serious disadvantage. She chooses to join Haru because the members share their fate, a different justification from trusting Yuki’s judgement. After rehearsal she describes wanting the group to be special even if she herself is not special to someone. The golden contour spans parts of this answer and recurs in her affirmative response to the confidence instruction. Neither tells us what she would rather have said. Public assent and practical action in solidarity have independent evidence. In the celebratory embrace she tries to learn why Naoki agreed, then settles for the happy result. Her desire for an explanation need not disappear because she smiles. Her present willingness is stronger evidence than any proposed enduring self-sacrifice. A later candid account or a low-pressure choice between individual recognition and a group benefit could distinguish conflicting readings. Personal or vocal skill ranking remains open. [OBS 13, 17, 19, 21, 25; CLM 09, 10, 13, 15; REL-KYOKA-HARU, PF-E01-04/06.]

### SPV2-CH-RIO · Rio Seibu, 聖舞理王

The yellow name card f521 gives せいぶりお. One fan already uses Rio-sama. Rio introduces herself through an elaborate unpacking of the kanji in her name and her claim to royalty, describes Budokan as the venue suited to her and talks of a personal legend. These repeated, chosen formulations work in audience, peer and introductory settings. Their boundaries are especially revealing: after the other two hurry away, she stops to give Naoki a deep bow and a polite greeting. When the group's first approach fails, she holds out her own pudding while her face shows the cost of the offer. After Naoki tells her to eat it, her relief is plain. She can be grandiose, considerate and attached to her snack in one encounter. That she hoped to persuade him remains a live alternative to general generosity. [OBS 02, 05, 08, 10, 13; CLM 09; REL-RIO-NAOKI, REL-NAOKI-RIO.]

She supports bringing this particular man into the group, participates in the lesson, and states a wish to be universally admired and praised. Glow spans both portions of the response, ruling out any easy assignment to exactly one false phrase. A second visible change at the next morning's group assurance likewise leaves its private cause unknown. She joins the embrace by 1198.364. Effortful facial expression and differing leg/arm positions in rehearsal constitute evidence of participation under observation; neither determines general dance or vocal skill. A future first-person comparison or an independently timed exercise with a reference phrase could change the motive or capability entry. [OBS 14, 16–17, 19, 21, 25; CLM 09/10/13/15; PF-E01-04/06.]

### SPV2-CH-NAOKI · Naoki Hinase, 日生直輝

The former manager has a record of work admired for client bookings and agency business in his own remembered workplace. He says advancement matters less than bringing idols' work to a worldwide public. Later memory places him listening from a hallway to resentment, clenching a hand and apologizing to a superior concerned about harm to all. We learn his subjective rationale for avoiding management. The original personnel action, precise cause of the group's difficulties and full intentions of colleagues stay open. He has joined Brightest, understands his own brief to include errands and administration, and regards active idol management as a different responsibility. He makes no use of the eye to expose strangers publicly. His lunch exchange with Kei, answer to her exact protest and indulgence toward a pudding he can see Rio is loath to give up establish ordinary accommodations and a capacity to tease. [OBS 06/07, 10/11, 13; CLM 06/07/09; REL-NAOKI-KEI, REL-NAOKI-RIO, REL-COLLEAGUE-NAOKI.]

Initial refusal → permission to watch → poor current prognosis → request for honest aims → provisional appointment. He initially lacks both the job proposition and the three-month condition. Haru’s phrasing and the lesson change what he can test. Her stated wish resembles his own professional ambition, and he later names the absence of shine as decisive alongside the group's talent and stance. He explains the trial separately as his obligation to prove his own fitness for the girls’ acceptance after refusing them. Constructive professional capacity coexists with reluctance to exercise it and fear of repeating harm. He begins the next morning by asking them to hold themselves confidently. His small smile after the differential glow cannot be treated as knowledge of the content of either hidden intention. Later self-disclosure could change his own account; it would not change the conditions the girls heard in the studio. [OBS 14–21, 25; CLM 07/08/10/11/12/13/15; REL-NAOKI-HARU, REL-NAOKI-TRIO, REL-NAOKI-YUKI, PF-E01-04/06.]

### SPV2-CH-YUKI · Yuki Hinase, 日生優希

Company president and Naoki’s older female cousin in the Japanese testimony. Her measured formulation that dissolution is being considered leaves a route to change. She knows the nine-month attendance problem, stakes the group on a much larger anniversary, promises a manager and anticipates his unwillingness before the girls discover it. Text messages at the point of refusal make them perform the persuasion. Her professional admiration is strong enough to risk both the girls and her cousin on the match; it does not tell us she would protect them from the stated consequence. Between cousins she takes his complaint as a greeting, calls the girls prized protégées, speaks of legends and follows playful justification with a formal request. She knows about his eyes. An explanation of the ambition behind her intervention is available to Naoki and the spectator, not to the group at recruitment. One family/work overlap provides an imperfect low-pressure control. A future decision under failure would test the force of the threat. [OBS 04/05/10/19/20; CLM 04/05/12/14; REL-YUKI-TRIO, REL-YUKI-NAOKI.]

### SPV2-CH-KEI · Kei, ケイ on Naoki’s phone

Kei presents a childhood bond as carrying sibling-like obligation. She disputes his assertion that leaving prevents harm, says she might give up the activity he has left, calls him mean and asks whether this is the end. The earlier lunch invitation and exaggerated rebuke over a missed meal make teasing part of the evidence without dissolving the seriousness of this call. Naoki calls them childhood friends, apologizes and privately regards something as finished. Kei does not hear that final remark in the supplied sequence. Her formal affiliation, legal identity, appearance on this call and future intention remain open; a cast list or absent reverse shot cannot establish them. Further credit-level or direct narrative evidence would be needed. [OBS 07/11; CLM 06/07; REL-KEI-NAOKI, REL-NAOKI-KEI.]

### SPV2-CH-HOTARU · Hotaru, 螢

The 2018 tour board and 2022 poster/credit identify a professionally established artist across the two times. The stage offers her a central, commanding address and dancers help frame her; the later broadcaster claims age eighteen, an annual audience of two million and triple-million sales. Those are represented publicity figures, unverified calculations. The role played in the song and its speaker's assertions about perceiving lies provide no evidence of her offstage powers or beliefs. Audience influence is recoverable through Haru. Ordinary private behavior, awareness of Haru, and responsibility for an individual's choice await another source. [OBS 01–03, 24; CLM 01/02/16; REL-HARU-HOTARU, PF-E01-01/03.]
