# SHINE POST V2 — Relationship Ledger

## Responsibility and present state

This ledger owns directed relationship state under the [architecture](../00%20Frameworks/SHINEPOST_V2_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md). The independent episode sections record observed directed relationships. It does not assume a network, romantic relation or hierarchy from the planned source or V1 document titles.

Use a stable `SPV2-REL-…` identity for each materially evidenced A→B edge. A→B does not establish B→A. Shared events may link both edges, but each person's knowledge, attitude, reliance and conduct require their own evidence. Group membership is context, not a substitute for these differences.

## Directed-edge record contract

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Participants and direction | Verified A and B identities, aliases where needed, and explicit observer/recipient |
| Source and time boundary | Episode/presentation cutoff, story time, continuity and exact evidence IDs |
| Formal relation | Position, responsibility, status or obligation actually established |
| Experienced relation | Trust, attachment, respect, irritation, fear, rivalry or other evidenced appraisals; preserve uncertainty |
| Expectations | What A expects from B, believes B knows/wants, or feels entitled/obliged to do |
| Conduct and voice | Address/register, approach/avoidance, disclosure, care, humor, conflict, repair and public/private differences |
| Capacity and willingness | What A can do for/with B versus what A chooses to do, under which constraints |
| Reciprocity | Separate evidence for B's response; unobserved reciprocity remains unknown |
| Change | Prior formulation, trigger/event, revised state, development versus new disclosure |
| Rival explanations | Role compliance, situational necessity, strategic presentation, convention and counterexamples |
| Dependencies | Claim/evidence IDs, linked performance/character states, affected monographs/syntheses |

Do not collapse professional obligation, admiration, dependence, friendship, attachment or romance into one axis. A relationship can be warm and hierarchical, skilled and tense, or asymmetric without requiring a single label. Claims about intimacy or romance require their own source-grounded argument; physical proximity, kindness or musical pairing alone is insufficient.

## Events, persistence and non-events

Link a shared event once, then state the separate implications for each directed edge. Distinguish an expressed feeling, an inferred appraisal and observable conduct. Preserve the possibility that a person misreads another. Identify who is present, who hears a line, who sees a performance and who has only a later account.

Record ordinary interactions alongside turning points: teasing, small accommodations, routine requests, silence, disagreement and cooperation when actually shown. Ask whether an apparent turning point persists under later comparable conditions. Absence claims require defined inspected opportunities; not seeing a response is not proof that the person lacks concern.

Group-level dynamics belong here only when they add something irreducible to a single edge, with the supporting directed evidence linked. Repeated institutional constraints belong in the [claim ledger](SHINEPOST_V2_EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIM_LEDGER.md); individual appraisal/state belongs in the [character ledger](SHINEPOST_V2_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md). Musical/staging event detail belongs in the [performance ledger](SHINEPOST_V2_PERFORMANCE_AND_FORM_LEDGER.md).

## Updates and synthesis

Maintain the current edge state with a recoverable change history and linked claim transitions. Only the integrator writes shared state. A material revision checks both participants' monographs, any relational synthesis and affected operational rules, without assuming both directions changed.

Create a relationship synthesis only when a cross-episode argument warrants it. It must explain asymmetries, chronology, ordinary interaction, counterexamples and uncertainty. It cannot replace required individual monographs or infer a reconstruction capability grade.

The reciprocal relationship between Naoki and Yuki changes professional form without a change to their kinship. In the first office memory, the second colleague’s hand on her distressed colleague’s back and the superior’s warning provide context for the abandonment of management; neither figure speaks for every person in the workplace. At the E01 cutoff there is no witnessed concert result, reconciliation with Kei, or reciprocated attachment from Hotaru toward Haru. These limit the inferences that may be drawn from their respective edges.
