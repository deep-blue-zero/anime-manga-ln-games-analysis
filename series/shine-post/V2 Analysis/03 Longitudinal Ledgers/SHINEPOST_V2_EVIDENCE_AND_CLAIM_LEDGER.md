# SHINE POST V2 — Evidence and Claim Ledger

## Responsibility and present state

This ledger is the canonical cumulative home for observation routes, claims, competing interpretations, uncertainty and V1/candidate→V2 revisions under the [method](../00%20Frameworks/SHINEPOST_V2_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) and [architecture](../00%20Frameworks/SHINEPOST_V2_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md). The independent episode sections own observation IDs, claims and unresolved questions. Administrative bootstrap inventory belongs in the source register and manifest.

Preserve source fact, inference, interpretation, speculative hypothesis and value judgment as distinct statement kinds. `TXT/FRM/CAV/AO/HL/AM` identify evidence routes; `INF/INT` identify analytical reasoning. Confidence, artifact authority and claim transition are separate dimensions. A high-confidence report is not a primary source.

## Stable records

Assign IDs only to real records. IDs remain stable when formulations change; revisions have their own IDs.

| Prefix / record | Required fields |
| --- | --- |
| `SPV2-OBS-…` observation | Exact witness/derivative, episode and bounded locator, inspected channel/route, observer, exposure, observation, evidence class, uncertainty, resolution/reinspection and reported/anchored/disputed status where applicable |
| `SPV2-CLM-…` claim | Current wording, statement kind, source boundary, supporting observations, reasoning, strongest rival, counterevidence, confidence/rationale, disconfirmation condition, topical home and dependent outputs |
| `SPV2-REV-…` revision | Claim ID, earlier formulation and exact original route, transition, proposed/current formulation, new evidence, alternative readings, reviewer objection/disposition, authority scope and propagation results |
| `SPV2-OPEN-…` unresolved question | Precise question, affected claims, missing evidence/channel, materiality, competing answers, next resolving operation and any blocker |

A locator combines stable witness, episode, calibrated source interval and cue/frame/content anchor as appropriate. Source text keeps Japanese wording, speaker/addressee, witness/provenance and translation notes separate. Quote only the amount analytically needed. Unverified listening or derivative transcription is not silently promoted to TXT. Contact-sheet positions alone are navigation, not exact visual citations.

## Chronology, disclosure and constraints

Record recurring story chronology, audience disclosures and institutional/world constraints here when they shape more than one local reading. Preserve presentation boundary versus inferred story time, who knows each fact, confidence and counterexamples. Individual knowledge state links to the character ledger; it does not duplicate a second incompatible chronology.

A proposed world/institution rule is a claim with scope and exceptions, not a universal fact by default. Model familiarity, genre patterns, another adaptation or V1 prose can suggest a question, but cannot supply fresh V2 source evidence. Bibliographic and administrative claims identify their own source category and carry no narrative evidentiary weight.

## Claim transitions and intake comparison

| Transition | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `PRESERVE` | Reviewed earlier formulation remains supported at its stated scope |
| `STRENGTHEN` | Additional evidence supports a specified stronger or more secure formulation |
| `REVISE` | Evidence supports a materially different formulation |
| `DOWNGRADE` | Narrow the claim, confidence or scope to what the evidence supports |
| `REJECT` | Identified evidence defeats the claim at the reviewed scope |
| `OPEN` | Material uncertainty or disagreement remains unresolved |

No transition has been assigned merely because V1 exists or Antigravity is reported complete. On review, retain the exact original artifact identity and claim passage, not a weakened paraphrase. Record preserved findings as well as disagreements. No V1-wide supersession follows from accepting one corrected claim or a new method.

For candidate integration, compare original/base, returned candidate and current target. Resolve mechanical drift separately from literary disagreement. A substantive correction requires evidence, counterevidence and a recoverable reasoning chain. If authorized correction is blocked, record the affected claim, evidence, exact obstacle and narrow remedy; do not leave the uncertainty hidden in a peripheral note.

## Observation disputes and historical integrity

Use the [AV protocol](../00%20Frameworks/SHINEPOST_V2_AV_RECONCILIATION_AND_INTAKE_PROTOCOL.md) for capability-limited disputes: one specific return, then at most one independent verification route where warranted. Continued disagreement stays OPEN. Do not confuse inability to inspect with refutation, or agreement between models with source certainty.

Frozen candidates, source receipts, independent logs and prospective predictions preserve their original bytes. Current claim views may change with append-only revisions linking those originals. A post-exposure observation must be marked as such. Do not reconstruct a supposedly prospective freeze after reading the ending or the candidate's interpretation.

## Propagation and acceptance

Only the accountable integrator writes shared state. Each material accepted revision records affected character, relationship and performance IDs; episode or synthesis sections; and any model/validation dependencies. Update them together or leave the dependent completion visibly blocked. Record actual reviewers and inspected evidence, not fictional role labels.

Before semantic acceptance, perform the handoff contract's bounded name/reference review of the synopsis/introduction, a substantive middle section and the conclusion/exit state, extending to consequential relationships. Record sampled scope, ambiguous references, corrections and remaining limits. This tests whether a reader can follow who says, believes or does what; it is not a name-frequency exercise.

The bootstrap's empty analytical record state is intentional. Populate it through actual admitted work and preserve the distinction between a usable schema and an evidentially supported conclusion.
