# SHINE POST E11 — Kyoka Tamaki will not lose heart

Haru Nabatame tells Ren Kurogane that she is staying in TINGS. It takes most of the episode for the two old friends to reach the point where one can say this and the other must hear it. Ren asks Haru to meet at the place where they used to wait for each other. When people recognize the star of HY:RAIN, Kyoka Tamaki gets them out of the crowd. She has reason to wish Ren had stayed away. At a fast-food counter a few minutes earlier, she was still turning over the criticism that reduced her to a girl who never gets a move wrong. Yet she is also a member of Haru's new group who gets to hear what Ren thinks she owes Haru. The girls once promised to become idols together. After Haru left their agency, Ren understood the rupture as her own failure to keep pace. She and her colleagues began again. Now Ren can point to a Budokan success, a new member and painstaking work on almost every aspect of performance. She wants the person for whom she did so much of it to come home.

At Inokashira Park, Haru asks her to watch what TINGS can do at Nakano Sunplaza. Back at the agency it is Kyoka who doubts that she can live up to the invitation. She has an ear for Rio's singing, an eye for Momiji's dancing and a ready account of Yukine's expressiveness. Asked, in effect, what she brings to the same stage, she finds herself repeating Ren's verdict. Naoki Hinase faces her across a table with the others gathered around. He says the scale of the house makes her worry legitimate. Then he tells her there is something only she can do for TINGS. Her last words ask him what it is. The episode ends on the question. Haru's choice of company is settled as far as she can settle it. Making a show for the people who have bought its tickets is another matter. [SPV2-OBS-E11-01, SPV2-OBS-E11-15, SPV2-OBS-E11-20]
