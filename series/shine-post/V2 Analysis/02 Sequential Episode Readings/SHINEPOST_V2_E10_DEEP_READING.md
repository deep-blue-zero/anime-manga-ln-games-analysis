### The question carried into Nakano

Before E10, a rehearsal seemed the likeliest place to find out how Haru's four partners would use what they learned at the last live. The episode keeps the promised lesson in its opening, then spends its time elsewhere. The group learns what its members can do for each other by working five different publicity jobs. Its friends and fans act on commitments made years, weeks or days ago, sometimes in places no other member will ever see. Ren makes good on the less certain part of the earlier expectation. We have direct testimony to what she has trained for. Her choice to come for Haru and her colleagues' choice to come after her disclose a difference of intent within HY:RAIN that the earlier group photograph could never have supplied. These changes belong to particular relations and occasions. None shows us Haru allocating a new part in rehearsal or consenting to let Ren and Kyoka decide her future.
