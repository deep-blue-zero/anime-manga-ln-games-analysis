# SHINE POST E12 — TINGS takes the stage

## Causal synopsis

The day before their anniversary concert, five young women stand on the stage at Nakano Sunplaza looking at an empty bank of red seats. Tomorrow people will sit there for whom a year’s work with TINGS might have meant five quite different things. Kyoka Tamaki knows she can remember what her manager has asked her to do.
