# 『変身』 / *Henshin*
## First-Pass Claim Revision Ledger

# 1. Audit result

The Japanese-primary second pass does **not** overturn the first English-source analysis at the level of its central architecture.

The major first-pass theses that survive are:

- Saki's first transformation is genuinely self-authored;
- recognition becomes a regulator of self-worth;
- pornographic scenarios leave cumulative residue rather than resetting;
- local choice and global decision-environment authorship diverge;
- Hayato's principal early power is interpretive before it becomes materially managerial;
- images preserve behavior while deleting coercive context;
- Saki retains agency under severe constraint;
- social expansion precedes fragmentation and contraction;
- the final pregnancy creates a real alternative future;
- the work's ethical contradiction lies in preserving Saki as subject while erotically consuming her objectification.

The Japanese changes the **resolution** of these claims rather than their broad direction.

It adds:

- exact refusal language;
- exact option-exclusion grammar;
- kinship-language resistance;
- causal accusations such as `ハヤトの所為`;
- ownership and obedience chains;
- `価値` and `居場所` as explicit conceptual terms;
- disposal/non-abandonment wordplay;
- `生まれ変わる` as the final transformation verb.

- "the reader-indictment interpretation is proven."

# 3. Major Japanese-only gains

## A. Option-set grammar

`しか…ない` makes perceived alternatives measurable:

- no one except Hayato;
- this is the only useful thing Saki can do;
- no route except Obata;
- drug use as the only thing weakness allows.

## B. Refusal precision

The second pass separates:

- desire to refuse;
- inability to speak;
- explicit refusal;
- refusal ignored;
- testimony interrupted;
- reproductive refusal successfully converted into strategy.

## C. Causal language

Saki repeatedly preserves environment-level explanation:

- `ハヤトの所為`;
- `無理矢理`;
- `言うこと聞いてれば…やり過ごせる`.

The tragedy is partly that others' explanations acquire more social force than hers.

## D. Final title vocabulary

The movement from `変わる` to `生まれ変わる` clarifies that the imagined final metamorphosis is survival and temporal integration, not another makeover.

# 4. Claims requiring continuing caution

1. **Exact drug identity** remains unknown.
2. **Deliberate reader indictment** remains unproven.
3. **Specific Hayato premeditation** is sometimes inferential even when later exploitation is textual fact.
4. **Clinical diagnosis** remains outside the manga evidence standard.
5. **The final overdose** is strongly self-destructive and intended as fatal in paratext, but narrative pages do not clinically certify death.
6. **The child telos** restores strategic authorship but may not restore self-worth independent of relationship.
7. **Institutional impossibility** cannot be inferred from narrative omission.

# 6. Final audit thesis

> **The first English-language pass correctly identified the work's principal structural problems: recognition becoming vulnerability, local choice surviving inside authored environments, pornography becoming chronology, and Saki remaining a continuous subject while being visually commodified. The Japanese-primary pass makes those claims auditable and more discriminating. It most often strengthens the first pass, but its highest value lies in showing exactly where earlier English summaries were too coarse: refusal can be spoken and ignored; care can be real before it fails; preference can be sincerely internalized after being relationally authored; and a real recovery branch can be closed by contingent violence without proving that recovery was never possible.**
