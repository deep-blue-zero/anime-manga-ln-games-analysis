# 『変身』 / *Henshin*
## 08 — Evidence Locator and Claim Revision Ledger
### Phase 7 reader-facing audit layer

# 0. Purpose and routing standard

This document does **not** create a new interpretation of 『変身』. Its job is to make the interpretations already stabilized in Documents 01–07 auditable.

Every mature claim should be traceable through the following chain:

> **mature synthesis claim → specialist document → longitudinal ledger/checkpoint → chapter-local artifact → evidence ID → printed page / CBZ image → Japanese wording or visual fact**

When the chain stops at interpretation rather than textual or visual fact, that limit is stated.

The key rule is that retrospective synthesis may **recontextualize** a chapter but may not falsify its prospective truth. The midpoint and pre-finale checkpoints therefore remain immutable even when later evidence makes their open possibilities emotionally painful.

The hierarchy used here is:

1. verified Japanese text / manga image (`TF`, `VF`);
2. character belief (`CB`);
3. strong inference (`SI`);
4. linguistic, thematic, ethical, or genre interpretation (`LI`, `TI`, `EI`, `GI`);
5. retrospective correction (`RC`);
6. unresolved ambiguity (`UA`);
7. speculation (`SP`).

Agency codes remain analytical rather than jurisdiction-specific legal conclusions. Exact drug identity and clinical diagnosis remain outside the evidence standard unless the text explicitly supplies them.

# 1. Audit summary

The exhaustive first-pass revision ledger contains **159 normalized chapter/finale claims**:

- `STRENGTHENED`: **122**
- `NEWLY_VISIBLE_IN_JAPANESE`: **16**
- `COMPLICATED`: **12**
- `STILL_UNDERDETERMINED`: **9**

No load-bearing first-pass thesis was fully overturned. This does **not** mean the Japanese reread merely confirmed an English summary. Its largest contribution was discrimination: it separated preference from consent, speech from effective refusal, tactical initiative from strategic authorship, perceived from objective alternatives, narrative implication from authorial confirmation, and structural reader implication from deliberate moral indictment.

## C.1 Immutable checkpoint rule

Two checkpoints deserve special protection:

- **Midpoint after Chapter 3:** Saki is hyper-visible in forms she cannot control; Hayato is the only option she *perceives* as live, but later dependency cannot be projected backward as inevitability.
- **Pre-finale after Chapter 6:** Saki has restored strategic authorship around pregnancy, care, non-abandonment, intended drug cessation, and change. The Finale may close that future; it may not retroactively prove the future was never genuine.

This is the main safeguard against fatalistic rereading.

## D.1 Agency principles frozen by Phase 7

1. **Remaining agency does not negate exploitation.**
2. **Victimhood does not require passivity.**
3. **Bodily response does not supersede expressed refusal.**
4. **Tactical initiative and strategic authorship must be scored separately.**
5. **A preference can be sincere and still be dependency-shaped.**
6. **A refusal can be linguistically clear and socially ineffective.**
7. **Objective alternatives and psychologically/materially live alternatives are not identical.**
8. **Agency codes describe the decision environment; they are not legal verdicts.**

## E.1 Translation cautions

The most important recurring risks are:

- flattening `しか…ない` into generic sadness instead of option exclusion;
- converting failed refusal into consent;
- translating `価値` only as abstract self-esteem and losing market resonance;
- translating `居場所` merely as physical “place”;
- softening `モノ` / `俺の金` ownership language;
- turning `クスリも もうしない` into proof that recovery has already occurred;
- turning `全部使っちゃえ` into an explicit textual declaration of suicide;
- translating `生まれ変われた` as ordinary “changed” rather than “was able to be reborn.”

## F.1 Formal evidence rules

A visual locator can establish composition, fragmentation, gaze, panel density, object prominence, and juxtaposition. It cannot by itself establish:

- a character’s unspoken legal or moral consent;
- an exact drug identity;
- authorial intent to accuse the reader;
- a single exclusive symbolism for the mirror or glasses;
- clinical death.

Those stronger claims require textual, sequential, or paratextual support.

# H. Residual ambiguity register

The following questions remain open after Phase 7 and should **not** be silently resolved in the continuous synthesis:

1. **Exact substance identity.** The work depicts escalating unidentified drugs and routes of administration; pharmacological naming remains `UA`.
2. **Clinical diagnosis.** Dependence-like prioritization, withdrawal-like distress, impairment, and compulsive use are strong narrative evidence; a formal diagnosis remains outside scope.
3. **Reader indictment.** The subject/object contradiction structurally implicates the reader; deliberate authorial accusation remains unproven.
4. **Hayato’s degree of premeditation.** His exploitation, management, ownership, and economic extraction are textual. A total master plan covering every loss is not.
5. **Objective institutional alternatives.** Their narrative absence is load-bearing, but absence does not prove they were literally unavailable in the implied world.
6. **First pregnancy paternity.** Explicitly unresolved.
7. **Second pregnancy at Chapter 6 endpoint.** Saki strongly believes she is pregnant; medical confirmation arrives only through later visible advancement, not at p.200 itself.
8. **Independent self-worth in the child telos.** Strategic authorship returns, but Saki still organizes change around another person.
9. **Mirror/glasses symbolism.** Continuity, attempted restoration, self-gaze, rage, and failed return are all supported; no single meaning is textually fixed.
10. **Narrative death status.** The narrative strongly implies death; p.243 retrospectively confirms intended death. These truth types remain separate.
11. **Hana sequence phenomenology.** Counterfactual/vision is strongest; the text does not name a precise ontological mechanism.
12. **Degree of visual moral normalization in adult Saki.** The future is more conventionally presented, but the work never states that conventional appearance itself is moral recovery.

# I. Worked traceability examples

## Example 1 — “Saki cannot say no” is rejected

**Mature claim:** Saki’s refusal capacity changes by environment; she sometimes cannot speak, sometimes speaks clearly and is ignored, and eventually converts refusal into strategy.

Route:

> Document 03 → agency ledger → C2-E006 (`何も…言えないや`) → C2-E014 (`入れるのはダメ`) → C3 kinship refusals → C5-E014–E018 attempted exit → C6-E019–020 (`絶対イヤ` → `産む`).

Result: the accurate thesis is not inability to refuse but **repeated failure of Saki’s internal state to acquire social force**.

## Example 2 — “Saki becomes a different person” is rejected

**Mature claim:** Saki changes radically without undergoing simple person-replacement.

> Document 02 → Document 05 → stable `私`, hesitation, relational gratitude, fear of burdening, care language → title verb chain `変わりたい → 変われる → 変わってみせる → 生まれ変われた` → visual p.202 where strategic authorship returns without visual purification.

## Example 3 — “Recovery was already impossible” is rejected

**Mature claim:** Chapter 6 opens a genuine alternative future.

Route:

> pre-finale audit → C6-E019–020 → Finale F-E001–008.

The Finale then verifies advanced pregnancy, child-directed savings, fetal-risk awareness, and drug rationing. The branch is materially enacted before F-E009–014 introduces new external catastrophe.

Result: terminal tragedy is **contingent inside the fiction**, even though F-P001 proves it was predetermined at the production level.

## Example 4 — “The work indicts its reader” remains too strong

**Mature claim:** the reader is structurally implicated in a contradiction.

Route:

> Document 06 → visual anchors → reader receives Saki’s causal interiority while also receiving eroticized access to scenes of dependence/coercion.

No verified line in the narrative or p.243 afterword says the reader is being morally accused.

Result: **structural implication = high confidence; deliberate indictment = UA**.

# J. Phase 7 audit verdict

The Phase 7 evidence layer confirms that the mature synthesis is unusually traceable for a work of this size. Its strongest conclusions do not depend on one notorious scene or on retrospective reputation; they are distributed across chapter-local freezes and repeated lexical/visual structures.

The best-supported full-work claims are:

1. Saki’s first transformation is genuinely self-authored.
2. Recognition becomes increasingly conditional and exchange-like.
3. Local choice persists after strategic authorship begins collapsing.
4. Coercion repeatedly produces appearances that observers misread as character.
5. Hayato’s power evolves from interpretation to dependency concentration, management, ownership, and internalized supervision.
6. Pornographic scenarios accumulate material consequence rather than resetting.
7. Saki remains a continuous desiring and caring subject through radical outward transformation.
8. The Japanese text makes option contraction, refusal, obedience, value, belonging, disposal, and rebirth much more exact than the English first pass could.
9. The manga’s visual grammar repeatedly oscillates between subject access and erotic objectification rather than simply erasing Saki’s subjecthood.
10. Chapter 6 restores a genuine strategic future, and the Finale demonstrates partial enactment of it.
11. The terminal catastrophe is diegetically contingent while authorially predetermined.
12. The tragedy partially transforms pornographic machinery into causal biography but does not ethically redeem the machinery’s continued erotic use of coercion, dependency, and vulnerability.

The best-supported final classification therefore remains:

> **an ethically compromised cumulative pornographic tragedy whose strongest literary achievement is preserving the personhood and causal history of a girl whom its own genre grammar repeatedly renders consumable.**

This document should now function as the routing layer for Phase 8. The continuous synthesis should cite this ledger or its underlying artifacts when making load-bearing claims rather than rebuilding provenance from memory.
