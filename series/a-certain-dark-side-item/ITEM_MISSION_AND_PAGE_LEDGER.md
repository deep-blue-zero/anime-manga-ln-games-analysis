# Mission and page ledger

This ledger begins with no admitted evidence or claims. [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) routes current state, [method](ITEM_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) governs claims about pages and [architecture](ITEM_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) locates later synthesis. Keep a diagnostic page route alongside its corresponding event or claim; no second complete page inventory belongs here.

| Record | Retain for retrieval |
| --- | --- |
| Task, action and result | Mission ID/horizon, origin and certainty of directive, recipient's understanding and authority, who can act or refuse, material choice and result, direct witness vs interested source, event time, disclosure/knowledge time, page refs, rival agency or cause |
| Team or personal state | Person’s evidence name and alias context, performed role vs proved affiliation, actor → recipient and setting, what changes in consent, reliance, protection or apparent trust, ordinary behavior/opportunity and limiting evidence, page/lettering evidence IDs |
| Diagnostic page form | Witness/image/panel or spread endpoints, reading path, lettering vs depiction vs editorial matter, what the panels actually show versus infer, whose view the design supports, alternate route or control panel, effect on an identified claim |
| Claim revision and forecast | Stable claim ID; earlier wording/criterion and freeze boundary, decisive and adverse evidence, original and later argument anchors, adjudication and dated transition; exposure limits and current version |
| Monograph qualification | Person ID and continuity, assessed boundary, outcome, independent evidence for portrait, largest gaps, reassessment trigger and actual canonical path; separate standalone/fidelity checks |

The reading owns narrative detail and page coverage, and the contribution record preserves source-aware review. A successful action is not evidence that the issuing party intended that result. A gutter inference stays labeled as such even when a later chapter confirms the event. Nothing in the ledger up to this horizon establishes the manga's cast or plot.
