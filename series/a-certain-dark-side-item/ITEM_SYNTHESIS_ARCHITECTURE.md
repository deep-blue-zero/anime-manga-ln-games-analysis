# Synthesis architecture

This is the INITIAL architecture for A Certain Dark Side ITEM, governed by [method](ITEM_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md), the [initiation policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md), the [archive authority policy](../../governance/source-policies/ARCHIVE_AUTHORITY_AND_SUPERSESSION_POLICY.md), and the [continuation policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_SEQUENTIAL_EXECUTION_SCOPE_AND_CONTINUATION_POLICY.md). [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) is the sole first-read surface. This generation has no predecessor or admitted analytical claims. [source register](ITEM_SOURCE_REGISTER.md) scopes candidate units, conflicts, and exclusions one witness at a time.

## Keep visual attribution and team attribution together

The [mission and page ledger](ITEM_MISSION_AND_PAGE_LEDGER.md) is the sole day-one analytical tracker. A reveal of who's behind a task or a withheld angle can change the same attribution, so the tracker keeps action and diagnostic page-form retrieval together. The source register owns witness provenance; exact page coverage and full source-facing argument stay with the reading. Scope all identities and affiliation to the adaptation as admitted, with actual alias and designation evidence. A central event may have multiple observers, disputed sources and a single current claim with dated revisions.

| Reading responsibility | Capture boundary | Destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tasking, discretion and control | Directive and relayed version, whose alternatives constrain action, depicted vs alleged result, consequences and rationale rival | [mission and page ledger](ITEM_MISSION_AND_PAGE_LEDGER.md); anticipated argument on covert work and how orders become collective action |
| Person and team change | Directed reliance, refusal, protection, uncertainty and ordinary conduct; distinctive speech and reasons under varying pressure | [mission and page ledger](ITEM_MISSION_AND_PAGE_LEDGER.md); team/relationship synthesis if supported and character qualification, then `characters/ITEM_<SUBJECT>_MONOGRAPH.md` |
| Page presentation and exposure | What each visual and lettering layer establishes; view versus reader privilege; when the next panel/page changes attribution | Unit reading with only recurring or decisive evidence indexed in [mission and page ledger](ITEM_MISSION_AND_PAGE_LEDGER.md); expected input into interpretation of secrecy |
| Unresolved or disputed revisions | Competing agent, evidence horizon, contradiction, counterpanel and outcome | [mission and page ledger](ITEM_MISSION_AND_PAGE_LEDGER.md) routes to full dated argument; [source register](ITEM_SOURCE_REGISTER.md) for witness/pagination disagreements |

A difference between a drawn image, dialogue and meaning attributed by the story may be focalization, deception, stylization or a deliberate panel omission. Check original pages and sequence before calling it contradictory. A different collected/serialized witness, prose or an anime adaptation never resolves an apparent inconsistency in this manga without new authority. Dated claim transitions use `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT` or `OPEN`; preserve contradictory and less decisive evidence and the pre-reveal exposure state.

## Standing source-reading topology

The proposed configuration requires an accountable principal analyst who inspects the whole admitted unit and is also the analytical integrator; separate independent specialists for manga composition, Japanese lettering and disclosure form, and for task attribution, team choice and differentiated person-level evidence; and a distinct independent critical reviewer. The specialist contexts form their own observations from assigned original-language evidence before receiving the principal's interpretations. The critical reviewer directly inspects decisive and contradictory source evidence, checking page and spread order, balloon attribution, whether a gutter or lettering inference was misreported as a depiction, who could know what, retained ordinary character behavior and unsupported import from the prose or parent franchise, before acceptance. The reviewer receives the interpretation after inspecting decisive evidence where practical. No present unit type qualifies for the reduced configuration; record any later authorized change under [topology §4.1](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EXECUTION_TOPOLOGY_AND_CAPABILITY_ROUTING_POLICY.md#multi-agent-source-reading). Verify these roles, evidence access and separation before first start; a runtime without adequate independent contexts is a required-capability stop. An owner-authorized deviation must state its exact scope and is recorded as a deviation, without ordinary acceptance or high-water advance unless explicitly authorized.

The unit checkpoint is the contribution and reconciliation record. Keep participant identity/role and separation, assignment, actual exposure and inspected coverage, material locator-backed observations, rivals and corrections, unresolved evidence obligations, and explicit adoption, qualification, rejection or open disposition for each material finding. Keep the finding and its reasoning recoverable; no generic participation summary, duplicated canonical artifact, or permanent per-agent ledger. Freeze prospective captures before resolution, separately by context where needed. Only the integrator updates shared readings, cumulative state and entrypoint after reconciliation. Record observed execution facts without inferring model settings. Keep source material and private transcripts in the evidence plane.

## Convergence and acceptance

After V01 or the first authorized complete volume, audit whether actual page turns, team members' separate information horizons and counterpanels survive retrieval. Once the authorized tranche closes, reconcile witness and alias ambiguities and cumulative revisions. The account of tasking/team discretion must use the visual-disclosure specialist's verified page effects; the team and person studies must use the operative constraints that survive both. If page-form evidence recurs independently, a focused formal specialist argument is required before integration; otherwise retain it in the evidence-led tasking argument. Make a separate group or dyadic account only where there is a genuine relational mechanism to explain. Reconciling those threads permits a manga-only synthesis; it cannot certify franchise facts or future volumes.

The principal closes each source transaction only with full assigned primary coverage, source-grounded independent contribution, independent critical examination, reconciled material disagreements, preserved evidence and counterexamples, consistent cumulative history and truthful exposure and authority boundaries. This is the sequential completion gate; the admitted, committed run boundary must also reconcile with its declared stop. Confirm longitudinal state and still-open evidence before a role-gap and specialist readiness review; require the qualified character work and the material specialist roles before authorizing a full synthesis. Review fidelity, locator retrieval, counterreadings, referential clarity and handoff acceptance separately from the decision to freeze or release. Any separately commissioned reconstruction model needs evidence-bounded validation of its own; a monograph or qualified portrait supplies no capability grade.

Use [initiation §5.5.1](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md#self-contained-character-monograph-standard) for qualification and complete standalone portraits. Assess every person with differentiated evidence, not merely leads; record stable identity, boundary, outcome, rationale, largest gaps, reassessment trigger and a path once a monograph actually exists. A qualified person creates a production obligation. An untested dimension remains insufficient evidence; an apparent absence needs an opportunity test. Include supported ordinary preferences, speech and address, decision patterns, relationships, changes, exceptions and limits. Document separate self-containment and source fidelity results before acceptance; no current portrait silently replaces a frozen earlier one.

The preferred local reading phase requires each assigned participant's actual still-image access to the authorized pages and Japanese lettering. A fresh retrospective context can operate against the admitted, frozen input snapshot after role-gap review; route any material visual claim back through direct page inspection and independent verification before accepting it. No audio or continuous audiovisual completion is planned. Repository publication and housekeeping stay with their authorized owners.

Under the [reasoning policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_REASONING_AND_MODEL_ROUTING_POLICY.md), use `ROUTINE_FAST` for deterministic bookkeeping, `BOUNDED_STANDARD` for metadata and locator checks, `SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS` for unit interpretation, `DEEP_SYNTHESIS` for sustained specialist/character work and `PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST` for foundation, high-impact disputes and final integration review. A scoped transfer under the [analytical handoff contract](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md) includes the frozen admitted/exposure horizon, retrievable evidence and argument records, open obligations, and named output scope and receiver; reconcile against live state, retest material claims and names/references (§15) before any promotion.

Keep the entrypoint and cumulative views mutable; dated claim changes preserve the older version, counters and new evidence. Complete prospective captures, accepted historical checkpoints and any authorized release remain intact; later repairs use linked errata, claim revision or an attributable successor. Revisit INITIAL design at the first meaningful source boundary, at the planned title-specific checks above and before integrated synthesis. Add cumulative structure only when independent recurrent evidence or a retrieval gap warrants it; revise the architecture and entrypoint together and backfill only a material gap existing records cannot responsibly repair.
