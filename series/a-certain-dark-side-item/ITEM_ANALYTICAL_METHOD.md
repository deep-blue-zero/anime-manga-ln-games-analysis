## Follow the page as evidence

Review each admitted page occurrence with full-page context and suitable crops; classify duplicates, omission, unresolved legibility and nonstory material. Maintain the time of the action, first visual or textual disclosure, subsequent attribution and knowledge acquisition separately. Candidate packaging or device-pagination sequence alone does not determine panel or spread order.

Use an order-to-outcome trace: issuing authority and stated task, what is relayed to a member, available alternatives and ability to refuse, chosen action, actually visible outcome and the account a later party receives. Preserve target and bystander viewpoints where present instead of treating a squad's interpretation as the book's judgment. Attribute Japanese speech by tail, panel context and source layer. Separate conversation, interior thought, narrational or editorial captions, diegetic signs/records and drawn sound or manner effects. Graphic scale, emphatic lettering and stylized rendering can guide attention or framing without measuring volume, vocal performance, physical movement, emotion or who actually caused an outcome. Check confidence on ambiguous tails, symbolic figures, peripheral activity, gutter duration and double-page sequencing rather than using OCR to settle them.
