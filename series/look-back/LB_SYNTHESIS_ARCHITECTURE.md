# Look Back synthesis architecture

## 1. Scope, authority and proportionality

This is generation LB-G1 under the single `series/look-back/` root. [The current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) routes the [method](LB_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md), this architecture and initialized controls. No prior architecture is superseded. Canonical means procedural authority for this INITIAL foundation; it does not mean the unread work has been analyzed or the owner authorized a run.

A standalone manga needs substantial scene-to-whole interpretation, not a copied multi-volume apparatus. The intended corpus must explain character and relationship development, causal and temporal relations, Japanese expression and visual form, including live alternative readings. A single full reading and a compact retrospective synthesis may carry these responsibilities if they preserve their arguments. No predetermined plot, character or thematic conclusion is installed.

The available source is one Japanese publisher EPUB of ルックバック, creator metadata 藤本タツキ. Its scene structure and completeness remain uninspected. Adaptation, other Fujimoto works and author-wide psychology are excluded. The source inventory owns witness controls; the scene/state ledger owns indexed evidence; the claim ledger owns revisions. Full analytical prose remains the explanatory core.

## 5. Substantial synthesis and earned specialists

The full reading must preserve sequence-level causal explanation and an exit account of what remains uncertain. Retrospective synthesis then explains how the work's parts support, qualify or challenge that account. It must integrate character, relationships, language and page form into an argument, not concatenate section summaries.

Create a monograph, directed relationship study, formal study or thematic specialist only when a distinct question requires sustained treatment that the integrated account cannot adequately contain. Before deciding no specialist is needed, the role-gap review names the substantive integrated sections carrying each duty. Absence of extra files is acceptable; absence of the analytical responsibility is not.

A character argument distinguishes actual change from altered reader knowledge, stated motive from behavior, ordinary versus crisis evidence and interpersonal conditioning. A relationship argument identifies both people and each direction, including asymmetry and counterevidence. A formal argument explains the effect of observed composition and sequence, testing alternatives rather than assigning symbolism by image prominence.

No universal word cap governs these artifacts. Acceptance requires preserved inferential steps, representative support, exceptions, rival accounts, subject identity and limits. During handoff/revision compare full versions for lost intellectual content. An outline with headings or a ledger densely populated with labels cannot pass as mature synthesis.

## 6. Contradictions, dependencies and gates

For apparent contradiction, check exact witness, wording, speaker/thinker, narrative frame, event time and disclosure horizon first. Then test changed context, mistaken belief, retrospective presentation, alternate-edition difference or actual inconsistency. Keep competing supported accounts OPEN when evidence is insufficient. Any revised central premise triggers review of dependent synthesis rather than a cosmetic note.

Production is source binding and authorized gate → full standalone reading with internal freezes and synchronized state → source completion audit → longitudinal/claim reconciliation → role-gap/specialist readiness → substantive studies or justified integrated sections → convergence → integrated synthesis → validation → declared-scope freeze.

| Gate | Required condition |
|---|---|
| Bootstrap review | Source-limited paired foundation and meaningful schemas; CLOSED/no run |
| Admission | Owner-defined standalone scope, verified witness/image/language route and consistent OPEN state |
| Sequential completion | Actual authorized pages and exclusions accounted for; full causal reading and exit freeze |
| Longitudinal reconciliation | Scene chronology, subjects, knowledge, directed edges and expression samples aligned |
| Specialist readiness | Role-gap review, source/claim stabilization, ordinary/contextual limits stated |
| Specialist convergence | Material competing arguments compared; explicit justification if no independent specialist artifacts |
| Integrated-synthesis readiness | All required responsibilities have substantive inputs and remaining debts are legitimately bounded |
| Final validation | Source/claim/argument/counterreading and name/reference checks plus repository requirements |
| Release/freeze | Exact accepted scope and immutable artifacts identified through the proper integration route |
| Reconstruction acceptance | Separate model evidence, independent tests and constraints; no self-certified capability |

Reading completion does not automatically authorize synthesis, source expansion or modeling. The owner instruction must cover the next work.

## 7. Reconstruction pathway, limits and independent evaluation

Reconstruction is intended as a source-informed derived option, not a duty to create unsupported profiles. A short standalone may give rich literary characterization but insufficient behavioral range. Record that limit honestly; a monograph cannot certify operational readiness.

For each earned subject/state keep distinct future evidence map, model and validation artifacts. The evidence map records subject, exact scene/temporal horizon, continuity/frame, situation/stakes, interlocutor, evidence class (DS speech, DB behavior, NI interior presentation, RB report, OS observer/role claim, INF inference), locators, reading/claim routes and counterexamples. Ordinary-life, Japanese register, directional context and genuine opportunities for negative evidence are required coverage checks; where absent they remain gaps.

The model binds identity/state, evidence-base commit, source/temporal boundary, exclusions and version. Separate disposition, developmental state and transient condition. Rules specify trigger + perceived stakes + current state → likely response family with inhibitors, overrides, exceptions, alternatives and falsifiers. Distinguish OBSERVED, CANDIDATE_POLICY and PROMOTED_POLICY. A state disclosed late is not automatically a change at that moment.

Written voice follows appraisal, objective, speech act, interlocutor and Japanese register. Thought is not public speech; translated summaries are not Japanese voice evidence. Keep decision fidelity separate from surface-language naturalness. Confidence is HIGH/MODERATE/LOW/OPEN; distance D0 attested, D1 close variant, D2 novel within-world/state and D3 cross-scenario. D3 states chronology/relationship/setting assumptions and often requires abstention.

A genuine held-out test requires material withheld from the model author before exposure. This may be impossible after a complete standalone reading; then use the label RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST and leave prospective validation unestablished. Do not pretend to regain blindness by rereading or choose known scenes after fitting the model. A separately scoped evidence split must be decided before exposure and cannot require unauthorized source access.

Freeze model predictions before release of test evidence. An independent reviewer records diagnostic opportunity, exact test locators, outcome and reasoning, using SUPPORTED/PARTLY_SUPPORTED/CONTRADICTED/NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY/CONFOUNDED/UNRESOLVED. Use decoys, changed-context perturbations, abstention controls and an ordinary-personality baseline as applicable. Report qualitative fidelity PASS/PASS_WITH_BOUND/NEEDS_TARGETED_SOURCE_REVIEW/INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE with counts/limits, not unsupported accuracy percentages.

Generated dialogue/scenes/predictions are synthetic outputs, never source evidence or canonical observations. They cannot repair missing pages, motives or contexts. No simulation is authorized now. Capability grades and discovery rows remain with designated repository agents. Coverage can be described for the twelve evaluator dimensions and six scenario types without self-grading.

## 8. Lifecycle, history and extension

Lifecycle INITIAL. Review after the first substantial mapped sequence reveals whether the schemas fit, at full-reading completion, before specialist/integrated synthesis and before release. A narrative frame or recurring dimension genuinely absent from the architecture triggers review, not automatic retrospective reinterpretation.

Role-gap findings use SATISFIED, PARTIALLY_SATISFIED, MISSING, MISROUTED, TOO_THIN or NOT_APPLICABLE, with exact home, evidence, consequence and repair owner. Add a ledger/specialist only for independent recurring evidence and retrieval need. Backfill only material gaps from full readings or authorized source review.

Current method/architecture, entry and indexes are mutable through targeted changes with history. Source bindings, event records, predictions and prospective freezes are append-preserving. Frozen prose is corrected by attributable errata/revisions; a model version assessed against exact evidence is preserved when superseded. No fictitious run seal is created at bootstrap.
