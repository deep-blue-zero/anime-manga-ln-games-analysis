# Look Back analytical method

## A single work, with internal disclosure frontiers

This method is specific to the available Japanese standalone manga ルックバック. It treats the work as one literary whole while preserving scene/sequence-level knowledge and causal structure. There is no multi-volume run to imitate. The actual internal divisions and narrative content are unknown; the architecture must not preinstall remembered characters, plot, themes, frames or an adaptation's interpretation.

[The source inventory](LB_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md) records one uninspected witness. [The architecture](LB_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) supplies cumulative and integration responsibilities. The [generic manga reference](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MANGA_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) is adapted here, not substituted for this project method.

The foundation is CLOSED. A future reader needs owner authorization for the standalone work, an OPEN initiation state, an immutable witness binding and a demonstrated STILL_IMAGE/Japanese lettering route. Historical EPUB/package validation does not prove page legibility or actual inspection.

## Source preparation without inventing structure

Map image occurrences in actual EPUB reading order, including covers and apparatus. Determine real reading direction, facing pages, spreads and any visible section labels before making formal claims. Classify covers, titles, notes, extras and previews independently; they may reveal information beyond a scene's frontier. A printed page label is not an image index. Retain page order and duplicates in the evidence plane.

The main analytical unit is the complete admitted work; its internal causal segments are identified from changes in goal, configuration, time, viewpoint, disclosure or formal organization. Do not create artificial “volumes” or decide dramatic sections from file processing batches. Local freezes before and after consequential disclosures preserve the first-pass encounter within a one-work reading.

Disclose prior recognition and adaptation exposure. Quarantine outside information. The reader's final knowledge of the work may support a separately labeled retrospective integration, but cannot rewrite what an earlier scene reveals. If a hypothetical, remembered or otherwise distinct frame is actually found, record its source signals and confidence before assigning its ontological status. The method expects the possibility of ambiguity; it asserts no such frame now.

## Causal prose and individual subjects

The future reading must reconstruct each meaningful movement in connected prose: entering conditions, named actors and their apparent alternatives, decision or constraint, consequence, and the evidence connecting those steps. Explain how the work's sequences relate, rather than merely give a synopsis or list symbols.

Use source-established personal names once known within the applicable horizon. Preserve deliberate anonymity, uncertain identity and alias changes. Role labels may explain a position, but may not habitually substitute for an identifiable person. Keep agent, observer, thinker, speaker and recipient explicit in causal sentences.

Separate shown behavior, direct speech, self-account, inner presentation and reports by others. Interpretive labels are OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, EVALUATION and UNRESOLVED, with HIGH/MODERATE/LOW/UNRESOLVED confidence. An expression is not a uniquely known emotion; an interior passage does not automatically make its beliefs objective truth.

For each material person distinguish disposition candidate, current developmental state and transient situation. Observe ordinary activity and humor where present alongside crises. Absence needs a real opportunity before it supports a negative claim. Keep relationship states directional: one person's desire, recognition, dependence or trust cannot establish the other's. No cast or relationship rows are created before inspection.

## Language, page form and temporal explanation

Inspect Japanese speech, thought, narration, small asides, diegetic writing, effects and ruby as distinct layers. Ground address, self-reference, register and pragmatic claims in brief source-verified examples with context and a labeled gloss. Do not turn private thought into public speech or translation into Japanese voice authority.

Treat panel arrangement, repetition, contrast, gutters, scale, distance, grouping, withheld reactions, backgrounds and rendering register as evidence only when observed. Explain feature → proposed effect → plausible alternative. Silent pages need full reading. Repeated composition may link scenes, but similarity alone does not establish causation, symbolism or shared narrative time.

Verify spread/page-turn claims against actual layout. Selected panel endpoints do not show unobserved motion between them. Graphic sound/state effects are lettering, not measured sound; no performed voice, music or adaptation staging is inside the boundary.

For temporal or causal uncertainty, maintain presentation order, event time, character knowledge and retrospective interpretation separately. A later scene may change an analyst's reading without changing an earlier character's knowledge. Preserve counterevidence and unresolved alternatives rather than choosing the most familiar account.

Contentious actions, if encountered, require source-grounded distinction among depiction, explanation, framing, consent, consequence and evaluation. Use only the detail needed for the argument. Neither sympathy nor later outcome establishes endorsement or earlier consent.

## Locators, inspection and revisions

Use `LB-JP-W01 / segment <mapped ID> / i0001 / p<visible label or NONE> / panel 1 / balloon 1`. Image occurrence is one-based in the bound witness's reading order. Segment IDs are assigned only after actual mapping; the source does not presently have invented scenes. Panel/balloon order is analyst-defined from the inspected page and ambiguity is recorded. Crops retain coordinates and full-page parent.

L1 exact image/panel/balloon supports contested claims and quotations; L2 identifies a bounded sequence; L3 is work-only navigation; L4 patterns cite representative L1 instances and checked scope. Pending locators cannot support a benchmark. A claim routes through the ledger to a stable reading heading and exact source location.

Keep image-review states in the evidence plane: REVIEWED_MATERIAL, NO_MATERIAL_ADDITION, DUPLICATE, EXCLUDED or UNRESOLVED_READABILITY. Git records declared coverage, exclusions, unresolved regions and safe receipt alias. OCR/search is retrieval, not reading. Source pages, OCR dumps, full dialogue, private paths/IDs and acquisition receipts do not enter Git.

Material revisions use PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT or OPEN. Keep original formulations and prospective freezes. A reread after complete exposure is retrospective; do not call it a first-pass prediction.

## Complete future reading and acceptance

A single future `readings/LB_WORK_DEEP_READING.md` carries `artifact_type: deep_reading` and covers witness/admission/inspection, prior exposure, entering state, governing and competing theses, substantial movement-by-movement causal prose, person/directed relationship states, ordinary contexts, Japanese expression, page form, recurrence, counterreadings, revisions and exit uncertainty.

A separate retrospective section integrates the whole work without corrupting movement-level disclosure records. Update the scene/state evidence ledger, claim revision ledger, source coverage and current state in the same accepted transaction. Reconstruction controls change only with real evidence. No-change is a reasoned manifest statement, not filler analytical rows.

The complete work can support a compact corpus, but not compressed reasoning. No universal word cap applies. Explain causal mechanisms, transitions, alternative readings and meaningful formal detail at the depth the evidence warrants. A table of scenes or final thesis is not a deep reading.

Acceptance includes the handoff §15.6 bounded name/reference check on opening, substantive middle, conclusion and every material relationship. Classify source-required anonymity, purposeful roles and knowledge limits separately from ambiguous reference, genericization and misattribution. Record scope and repair supported defects. The reviewer tests consequential evidence and full reasoning, not only file syntax or length. Essential unread pages, unresolved load-bearing attribution or lost counterreading hold the corresponding completion claim.
