# GENSHIN_FURINA_OPEN_EVIDENCE_BOUNDARY

## Purpose

This document records the evidence that the V1 corpus does not currently possess or cannot safely order. It prevents the active-provisional monograph from being mistaken for exhaustive final authority.

## Open source boundaries

### Main-story parent/index joins

The controlled-scale Genshin adapter classifies major quest roots and materializes Talk families, but broader parent/index joins remain partial. Some dialogue can be read reliably without a complete parent chain; exhaustive appearance claims cannot.

### Cross-surface chronology

Archon and Story Quest order is secure. Limited events, ambient Talk, repeatable interactions, and profile unlock material do not all have one safely reconstructed diegetic order.

### Continuation gaps and unresolved TextMaps

The full Genshin build retains continuation gaps and thousands of TextMap findings. CHS is substantially authoritative, while some JP/EN cells remain missing.

### Acoustic performance

Furina has no approved audio-locator corpus in the present normalized build. Claims about pitch, timing, breath, laughter quality, vocal contraction, or actor performance remain outside current authority except as unsystematic recollection, which is excluded here.

### Relationship completeness

Corrected counterpart identity is better than the earlier aggregate scene view, but the current monograph does not claim exhaustive pair chronology for Traveler, Neuvillette, Navia, Clorinde, Arlecchino, or others.

### Long-term post-crisis state

The corpus represents significant integration, not a final endpoint. Full institutional reconciliation, public historical recognition, and future ordinary-life stability remain open.
