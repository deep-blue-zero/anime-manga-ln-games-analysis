## One-artifact-per-responsibility rule

The monograph is the reader-facing integrated authority. It does not replace the sequential readings for scene-specific questions, the ledgers for revision history, or the source index for exact wording. Specialist syntheses are subordinate topical homes and should be updated rather than multiplied by later "final thoughts" documents.
