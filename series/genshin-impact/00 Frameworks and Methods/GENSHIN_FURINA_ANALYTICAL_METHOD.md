# GENSHIN_FURINA_ANALYTICAL_METHOD

## Governing question

How does Furina think, choose, speak, regulate herself, relate to other people, and transform when her five-century role is read prospectively rather than reduced retrospectively to either fraud or martyrdom?

## Core methodological commitments

### 1. Character as stateful process

Furina is modeled as a changing person rather than a static bundle of traits. Each tranche records entry state, pressures, choices, speech modes, relationship changes, and exit state.

### 2. Prospective truth freeze

A tranche may use later evidence to revise earlier claims only through an explicit revision transition. The early public Furina is first read as the world encounters her: a theatrical ruler who is socially powerful, institutionally dependent, audience-sensitive, and difficult to calibrate. The later revelation explains why those properties developed; it does not make their earlier effects unreal.

### 3. Performance is not equated with falsity

The analysis distinguishes:

- consciously strategic role performance;
- socially habitual persona;
- professional acting/directing craft;
- defensive reframing;
- ordinary playfulness;
- embodied traits acquired through centuries of repetition;
- post-crisis self-authored performance.

The question is not simply whether an utterance is "real." It is what the performance protects, communicates, conceals, or creates.

### 4. Source classes remain distinct

- narrative action demonstrates behavior under situated constraints;
- profile voice gives self-description and ordinary register;
- character stories provide authorized paratextual narration;
- third-party speech gives perception filtered through access and interest;
- event/ambient material increases situational breadth;
- player-choice branches are conditional observations, not simultaneous facts.

### 5. Stimulus-response modeling

Behavioral observations use a compact schema:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| stimulus | What changed or demanded a response |
| information | What Furina could know |
| constraint | Duty, secrecy, reputation, fear, social role, practical limit |
| response | Speech/action actually chosen |
| immediate function | What the response accomplishes |
| inferred rule | Provisional decision principle |
| counterevidence | Cases that limit the rule |
| confidence | high / medium / low |

### 6. Speech and register

CHS governs wording. JP is used to observe stable self-reference and register; EN is used for readable presentation. The ledger tracks:

- self-reference;
- address and honorific shifts;
- formal/theatrical syntax;
- improvisational exaggeration;
- hedging and repair;
- self-deprecation;
- pleading;
- intimate/plain speech;
- humor;
- audience-dependent code-switching;
- professional-directorial speech;
- post-crisis continuity and change.

### 7. Relationship evidence

Co-presence does not equal intimacy. A relationship claim requires observed interaction, explicit description, or repeated source-grounded orientation. Each relationship is evaluated for:

- knowledge access;
- power asymmetry;
- trust;
- disclosure;
- mutual expectation;
- conflict;
- practical dependence;
- affection;
- changes in address and behavior.

### 8. Third-party evidence

A third-party judgment is classified by speaker, access, period, motive/valence, whether Furina is present, and whether observed behavior confirms it. Name mentions and logistical references do not count as characterization.

### 9. Claim discipline

Major claims are tracked from their earliest formulation through final synthesis using:

- `PRESERVE`: later evidence leaves the claim materially intact;
- `STRENGTHEN`: later evidence adds independent support or explanatory depth;
- `REVISE`: core idea survives but wording/mechanism changes;
- `DOWNGRADE`: claim remains possible but loses confidence or scope;
- `REJECT`: contradicted or based on defective source structure;
- `OPEN`: current source cannot adjudicate.

## Sequential procedure

For each tranche:

1. define source boundary and chronology confidence;
2. state Furina's entry condition;
3. read scenes in source order while controlling duplicates;
4. record public/private speech and action;
5. identify choices under constraint;
6. update relationship states;
7. record new claims and counterevidence;
8. update all longitudinal ledgers;
9. state exit condition and questions carried forward.

## Synthesis procedure

After sequential readings:

1. complete the dedicated profile/self-description pass;
2. adjudicate third-party characterization;
3. consolidate relationships;
4. write specialist syntheses;
5. perform claim-revision audit;
6. write the character monograph from current claims, not from first impressions;
7. update corpus map and manifest;
8. retain open claims and source gaps for differential V2 review.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- no personality typing from surface vibes;
- no treating every theatrical line as deception;
- no treating suffering as automatic moral exoneration;
- no assuming that role coercion eliminates agency within the role;
- no using raw mention counts as relationship strength;
- no double-counting CodexQuest/Talk duplicates;
- no claiming complete chronology from filename order;
- no converting localization differences into source facts without CHS review;
- no writing a definitive monograph while readiness remains partial.
