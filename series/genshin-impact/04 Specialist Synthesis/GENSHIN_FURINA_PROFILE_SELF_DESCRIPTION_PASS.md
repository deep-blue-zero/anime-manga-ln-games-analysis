## Scope and evidentiary role

This pass covers the normalized Furina profile corpus: one description, seventy-one profile voice lines, and seven character stories. These records are not inserted indiscriminately into the sequential narrative. They provide self-description, authorized paratext, ordinary preferences, relationship statements, and retrospective interpretation. Claims are cross-checked against observed behavior.

## Profile description

The description presents Furina through public image and post-crisis context. It is useful as concise authorized framing but cannot replace scene-level evidence. Its most important function is to identify which traits the game itself treats as durable after the divine office: theatricality, artistic presence, pride, and newly human ordinary life.

## Retrospective self-understanding

### The audience wanted the role

At `hoyo://GENSHIN/7.0.0/profile/GENSHIN:AVATAR:10000089/89017/voice_line`, Furina reflects on the period after the prophecy. She describes long depression and the belief that people had wanted the role rather than her. The line corroborates the post-role withdrawal observed in TR07.

It also clarifies why solitude initially feels like gain. Solitude is the first environment in which Furina is not being asked to reproduce an image. The source does not portray solitude as final flourishing; later character stories show that isolation becomes another problem.

### Role-image fusion

At `.../89032/voice_line`, Furina says she once envied people who possessed past and future while she had neither, created a stronger and more godlike self, and eventually found the distinction between image and self difficult or unnecessary.

This is the strongest direct support for the developmental-fusion thesis. It does not mean coercion disappears. It means identity formed under coercion becomes real identity rather than removable costume.

### Acting as herself

The profile corpus describes loathing the compulsory acting and later returning to a stage where she can act as herself. This language supports a jurisdictional account of authenticity: Furina does not become authentic by eliminating performance, but by choosing a performance that does not make her existence depend on deceiving the audience.

## Ordinary preferences and habits

### Interest and boredom

At `.../89030/voice_line`, Furina describes becoming interested easily and sometimes bored easily, while the Traveler sustains joy through continued novelty. Observed event material supports this as attraction to aesthetic, social, and structural stimulation rather than pure fickleness.

### Shopping and compensation

At `.../89031/voice_line`, Furina describes compensatory shopping after long self-denial. The habit makes psychological sense as an attempt to exercise ordinary choice over material desire after centuries when personal preference had almost no jurisdiction. The source does not warrant diagnosing compulsive behavior; it establishes the symbolic value of choosing for herself.

### Food and social ritual

Macarons, sweets, tea, and meals recur as social media. Food permits care and reciprocity without requiring direct confession. Navia's macarons and Furina's tea-related bids are especially relational.

### Household life

The Story Quest and profile material show incomplete domestic competence. This is not evidence of general incapacity. It reflects a life in which ordinary household tasks were historically handled by institutions and staff while Furina performed a public office.
