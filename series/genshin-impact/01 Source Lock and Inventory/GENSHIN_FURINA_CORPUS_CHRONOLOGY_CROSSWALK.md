## Main state transitions

1. **Audience-constructed divinity:** authority depends on fulfilling public expectations.
2. **Error and adaptation:** early trial defeat produces learning rather than simple collapse.
3. **Sincere duty under epistemic secrecy:** belief in rescue and sustained labor coexist with practical ignorance.
4. **Guilt without disclosure:** Poisson converts role-protection into acute moral injury.
5. **Identity trial:** she chooses bodily risk and social annihilation rather than expose the secret.
6. **Retrospective revelation:** the role began as a sacrificial human assignment and became embodied personality.
7. **Post-role void:** freedom initially means uselessness, isolation, and loss of social place.
8. **Self-authored art and relationship:** she discovers that performance can express rather than erase her.
9. **Mature repertoire:** theatricality, humor, standards, empathy, and social need persist as integrated traits.
