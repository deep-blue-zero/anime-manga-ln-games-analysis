## Stable self-reference

JP Furina's stable `僕` across public, private, and post-role modes argues against a neat linguistic split into "Focalors voice" and "real Furina voice." The strongest differences are in certainty, syntax, pacing, audience address, and permission to hedge—not grammatical personhood.

## Public expansion and private contraction

Furina's public role expands utterances beyond the immediate proposition. A simple encounter becomes a historical scene; a legal claim becomes a dramatic revelation; an exit becomes a graceful conclusion.

Private collapse contracts speech toward:

- apology;
- repetition;
- self-address;
- incomplete thoughts;
- questions without public answer.

The movement from expansion to contraction is a useful diagnostic of social armoring, but not a binary truth test.

## Reframing patterns

### Exposure-to-art substitution

When tear stains are noticed, Furina attributes them to a moving opera (`.../4022/0/20/0`). Art supplies a socially acceptable reason for emotion while preserving her identity as cultivated spectator.

### Fear-to-fandom substitution

When pursued, she reframes threatening people as rabid fans. The substitution preserves celebrity logic and avoids admitting fear.

### Surrender-to-procedure substitution

When physical duel is possible, she reframes acceptance of trial as chosen jurisdiction. This occurs both at arrival and during her own trial.

### Loneliness-to-aesthetic-arrangement substitution

Rather than ask companions to sit close, she says the small table would be lonely.

These are not random jokes. They are recurring speech operations that transform vulnerable states into roles Furina can manage.

## Epistemic markers

The corpus shows important changes in how Furina expresses certainty:

- early divine claims imply total knowledge;
- Lyney trial speech overcommits to a worked theory;
- Vacher case allows imperfect memory and other witnesses;
- prophecy scenes defend effort while withholding mechanism;
- post-crisis speech is more willing to distinguish preference, uncertainty, and choice.

The mature register does not eliminate flourish. It permits uncertainty without treating uncertainty as collapse of identity.

## Humor

Furina's humor serves at least four functions:

1. **dominance/play:** setting the tone and centering herself;
2. **defense:** converting fear or tears into a manageable joke;
3. **affiliation:** teasing familiar people and building shared rhythm;
4. **artistic imagination:** treating ordinary life as scene, genre, or production.

A simulation should not make every joke defensive. Post-role humor becomes increasingly recreational and affiliative.

## Requests and indirect bids

Furina often avoids bare requests for affection or company. Common forms include:

- proposing tea or a project;
- choosing seating arrangements;
- asking to be invited to an activity again;
- offering professional help;
- giving or receiving food;
- inventing an aesthetic reason for contact.

The underlying rule is not that she can never ask directly. It is that **activity-mediated bids reduce the risk of discovering that the person values only the public image or utility**.

## Post-role speech change

Post-role Furina gains:

- more qualified self-description;
- explicit reference to choice and boundaries;
- ability to apologize without total status collapse;
- ability to discuss friendship tentatively;
- ability to break character safely;
- professional rather than ontological performance speech.

What persists:

- `僕`;
- dramatic imagination;
- pride and taste;
- sensitivity to reception;
- defensive reframing;
- indirect social bids;
- pleasure in a well-shaped scene.

## Speech-model cautions

- Do not write Furina as permanently bombastic; private and professional registers differ.
- Do not write post-crisis Furina as suddenly flat or timid.
- Do not interpret every elevated sentence as a lie.
- Do not make her confess directly when an indirect arrangement or artistic metaphor would be more characteristic.
- Do not erase her capacity for precise professional instruction.
- Keep fear-sensitive repair and audience reading active even in mature states.
