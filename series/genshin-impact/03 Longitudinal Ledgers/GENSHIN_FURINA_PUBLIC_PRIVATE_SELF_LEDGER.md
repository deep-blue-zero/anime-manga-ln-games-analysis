# GENSHIN_FURINA_PUBLIC_PRIVATE_SELF_LEDGER

## Governing distinction

This ledger does not divide Furina into a false public self and a true private self. It tracks how different contexts permit or require different degrees of disclosure, control, theatricality, and self-authorship.

## TR01–TR03: public self as interactional authority

Furina's public self is sustained through audience feedback. Silence disrupts her more than visible opposition because it withholds the shared frame. The public mode is not mechanically recited; it is improvised. Her early errors show that performance pressures can distort reasoning, while the Vacher case shows that the same mode can learn and become more procedurally receptive.

## TR04: private need appears through design

The small tea table reveals loneliness indirectly. Furina creates proximity rather than naming need. Around Arlecchino, public fluency becomes effortful; small talk and cake are attempts to build a safer script. The private self is visible through failure of the public mode, but it should not be treated as the only authentic layer.

## TR05: automatic reconstruction

After Poisson, Furina's solitary apologies are followed by near-instantaneous public reconstruction when observed. This establishes that the public mode has become reflexive. It is a learned emergency procedure: exposure triggers performance before Furina has time to deliberate fully.

## TR06: the persona's origin and fusion

The accession speech reveals that the natural, modest Furina did not satisfy public expectations. She consciously constructs a more forceful god. Repetition over five centuries makes the distinction between role-generated and pre-role traits increasingly unhelpful.

Profile voice `hoyo://GENSHIN/7.0.0/profile/voice-atlas/89032/voice_line` explicitly states that she created a stronger, more godlike self and that the image gradually merged with the real self.

The correct model is **developmental fusion**:

1. human Furina has baseline tastes, fears, intelligence, and moral capacity;
2. coercive role requires amplified confidence and theatrical divinity;
3. public reinforcement and daily embodiment stabilize the pattern;
4. Furina acquires real skills and preferences through the role;
5. post-crisis recovery must choose what to keep, not discover an untouched core.

## TR07–TR09: jurisdiction replaces purity

Furina's first boundary—no more acting—is protective. It is revised only after she is free to refuse and understands the bounded purpose of the performance. Later roleplay and directing show that the goal is not to abolish performance but to establish jurisdiction:

- who requests it;
- what it means;
- whether everyone knows the frame;
- whether Furina may stop;
- whether the role has an endpoint;
- whether the work expresses or erases her.

## Current conclusion

Furina's authenticity is best defined as **self-authorship across multiple registers**, not unperformed plainness. A statement or action becomes more authentically hers when she controls its purpose, audience, duration, and relation to her broader life.
