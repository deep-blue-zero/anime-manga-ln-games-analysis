# GENSHIN_FURINA_THIRD_PARTY_CHARACTERIZATION_LEDGER

## Method

The normalized third-party file contains 870 explicit-name candidate records. Many are logistical or duplicate mentions. This ledger retains only judgments or observations that materially characterize Furina and classifies them by access and corroboration.

## Evidence classes

| Class | Meaning | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Direct-access observation | speaker has sustained interaction or institutional knowledge | high when corroborated |
| Public reputation | reflects what Fontaine believes about the role | evidence about social image, not inner truth |
| Adversarial judgment | may reveal weakness accurately while distorting motive | medium, requires counterevidence |
| Affectionate judgment | may reveal ordinary traits but idealize | medium-high with behavior |
| Post-crisis retrospective | benefits from later knowledge but risks simplifying prior state | high if source access is clear |
| Mention/logistics | name without characterization | excluded from claims |

## Neuvillette

### Observation

Neuvillette identifies Furina as neither superficial nor foolish, but inconsistent. He has unusually high institutional access and long observation, though not access to the central secret before the reveal.

### Adjudication

**STRENGTHEN.** Direct behavior supports this: Furina can be diligent, perceptive, and morally serious while public behavior appears frivolous or contradictory. Neuvillette's formulation is valuable precisely because it preserves the discrepancy without claiming total understanding.

## Arlecchino

### Adjudication

## Charlotte

### Observation

This reputation is causally important. It teaches Furina how to perform and later becomes the standard against which ordinary humanity is judged.

Public love should therefore be read as both genuine attachment and attachment to an image.

## Rejected candidate uses

This project rejects:

- raw mention frequency as relationship strength;
- audience praise as proof of private happiness;
- hostile suspicion as proof of selfish motive;
- post-crisis admiration as erasure of political consequences;
- generic "Lady Furina" logistical references as characterization.

## Current third-party synthesis

The strongest triangulated conclusion is:

> People with partial access consistently perceive a discrepancy between Furina's theatrical surface and her deeper seriousness. No ordinary pre-crisis observer fully understands the discrepancy. After the reveal, sympathetic observers risk overcorrecting toward martyrdom. The direct record supports both genuine sacrifice and real limitations, errors, fear, pride, and political opacity.
