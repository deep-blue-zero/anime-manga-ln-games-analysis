# 〈物語〉シリーズ V2 — Volume and Arc Evidence Ledger
## `21_VOLUME_AND_ARC_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md`

## 0. Function

This is the primary **evidence-routing appendix** for the Monogatari V2 corpus. It does not replace the thirty canonical volume deep readings, the Japanese primary sources, the live L01–L09 longitudinal ledgers, or the frozen season checkpoints. Its job is to make those materials traversable.

The reader should be able to begin with a volume, arc, character development, oddity case, Japanese term, relationship transition, or intervention-ethics problem and answer four questions quickly:

1. **What did the source establish at that publication boundary?**
2. **Which later evidence revises or qualifies that reading without back-projecting later knowledge?**
3. **Where is the strongest locator-grade evidence?**
4. **Which eventual specialist document owns the mature claim?**

The ledger therefore treats the unit of analysis as:

> **publication source → internal position → narrator/focalizer → evidence state → publication-local meaning → retrospective V2 revision → canonical topical destination**

It is deliberately not a single chronological retelling. Monogatari repeatedly publishes prequels, retrospective insertions, alternative focalizations, frame stories, embedded testimony, and later-written scenes located earlier in the fiction. A publication-order ledger that silently pretended to be story chronology would destroy precisely the distinction V2 is designed to preserve.

### 0.1 Evidence labels

- **TF** — textual/source-form fact
- **NR** — narrator report
- **CD** — character/diegetic claim
- **SI** — strong inference
- **IT** — interpretation
- **UA** — unresolved ambiguity
- **RC** — retrospective correction/revision
- **VJ** — value judgment

### 0.2 Claim-transition vocabulary

- **PRESERVE**
- **STRENGTHEN**
- **REVISE**
- **DOWNGRADE**
- **REJECT**
- **OPEN**

### 0.3 Non-collapse rules

This ledger must never silently collapse:

- event fact into narrator framing;
- vivid memory into reliable memory;
- specialist theory into omniscient truth;
- later causal connection into proof of original foreshadowing;
- origin into authentic essence;
- victimhood into absence of agency;
- agency into retroactive loss of victimhood;
- consequence into cosmic punishment;
- help into ownership of another person's recovery;
- present consent into retroactive consent;
- a changed relationship into erasure of earlier harm;
- a later self into proof that an earlier self was fake.

### 0.4 Locator rule

Preferred form:

`VXX — Japanese title — chapter/arc/section — short scene anchor`

Where exact section precision is not necessary or the source itself keeps chronology broad, an arc-level locator is preferable to invented precision.

# 1. Authority hierarchy

For event and wording verification:

1. locked Japanese primary source;
2. canonical V2 volume/supplementary deep reading;
3. current L01–L09 cumulative ledgers;
4. frozen season/boundary checkpoint;
5. this routing ledger;
6. later specialist or full-series synthesis.

This ledger is canonical **routing infrastructure**, not a substitute for primary evidence.

The numbered novels remain governing evidence for major plot, durable oddity mechanics, mature relationship state, and season-scale causation. Admitted supplementary evidence can be highly authoritative for local voice, mundane biography, ordinary relational procedure, historical self-state, and locally explicit event detail without becoming a second master canon.

# 4. First Season evidence routes — V01–V07

## V01 — 『化物語（上）』 — 「ひたぎクラブ」 / 「まよいマイマイ」

**Source code:** `V01`  
**Principal narrator:** Araragi Koyomi.  
**Publication-local arc thesis:** the opening volume establishes the series' first durable rescue problem: the afflicted person's authorship is indispensable, but meaningful help is materially collaborative. It also establishes that a socially assigned role can become the first obstacle to seeing a person accurately.

### Decisive source findings

- Hitagi enters already socially classified as `病弱な女の子`; Araragi's first useful act is not diagnosis but noticing a mismatch between that role and her literal weight.
- Oshino's `勝手に助かるだけ` formulation is already qualified by his actual practice: he classifies, mediates, prepares the ritual, limits his own role, and leaves the burden decision to Hitagi.
- Hitagi's recovery is not “suffering is good.” Oshino explicitly permits flight. The important fact is that Hitagi chooses to receive memory and weight rather than have someone else decide which burdens she should carry.
- Hachikuji's May 14–15 route demonstrates distributed rescue more clearly. Araragi's persistence, Hitagi's local knowledge, Oshino's interpretation, urban redevelopment, and Hachikuji's own destination all matter.
- Hachikuji remains dead and Hitagi's lost family/mother are not restored. The series begins by separating **case closure** from **life repair**.

**Oddity/case route:** weight crab / lost cow. The cases combine literal mechanics with human semantic pressure, but they are not one master psychological metaphor.

**Character-state delta:** Araragi's “ordinary” helping identity becomes visible as unusual; Hitagi moves from defensive isolation toward chosen partiality; Hachikuji becomes an ongoing relation rather than a solved disappearance.

**Relationship delta:** Hitagi values that Araragi would help others and wants to become special after that fact, rather than asking him to become kind only to her. Hachikuji's initially adversarial routine becomes a voluntary request for future conversation.

**Japanese targets:** `重さ / 重み / 思い / 記憶 / しがらみ`; `沈黙と無関心`; `助ける / 助かる`; `迷い牛`; `ただいま`; `好きになる努力`; `戦場ヶ原、蕩れ`.

**Intervention ethics:** authorship of burden; collaborative rescue; limits of specialist ownership; help may matter without creating entitlement to another person's future.

**Retrospective V2 revision:** the first-pass slogan “people save themselves” is too causally absolute. Preserve it as an anti-appropriation rule, not a denial that others can materially help.

## V02 — 『化物語（中）』 — 「するがモンキー」 / 「なでこスネイク」

**Source code:** `V02`  
**Principal narrator:** Araragi.  
**Publication-local arc thesis:** desire can be internally contradictory without one hidden layer becoming the “true” person, while harm can also arrive from outside. V02 therefore blocks a single psychological theory of oddities almost immediately.

### Decisive source findings

- Kanbaru inherits a hand and participates in a Rainy Devil contract whose front/back structure binds incompatible wishes. Her murderous wish is hers, but so is the wish that constrains it.
- Hitagi directly identifies Araragi's willingness to spend himself for another person as a problem. The self-sacrifice critique is no longer merely reader inference.
- Kanbaru physically restrains Araragi during Nadeko's case and warns him not to confuse whom he is trying to save. This is the first clear limit on indiscriminate rescue.
- Nadeko is a genuine recipient of an externally sent curse. Later evidence of agency must not be used to rewrite V02 into a self-generated victimhood story.
- The second snake returns toward its sender, leaving a social consequence beyond the cured body.

**Oddity/case route:** Rainy Devil contract; `蛇切縄`-type curse ecology. The former exploits internal contradiction; the latter is externally inflicted. Semantic relevance does not imply a single causal source.

**Character-state delta:** Kanbaru's divided self becomes a problem of owning contradiction rather than discovering one “dark truth.” Nadeko is introduced through Araragi's low-confidence remembered child role, an important later misrecognition seed.

**Relationship delta:** Hitagi becomes an explicit ethical counterweight to Araragi's self-sacrifice. Kanbaru becomes both rival and limiter of his rescue field.

**Japanese targets:** `表 / 裏`; Monkey's Paw / Rainy Devil naming; `暦お兄ちゃん`; third-person `撫子`; curse-return language; the two-hole/snake-tail explanation.

**Intervention ethics:** who is actually being helped; bodily self-payment; external victimization versus later agency; diagnostic nudity and sexualized gaze must be held in the same record.

**Retrospective V2 revision:** later Nadeko culpability does not revoke V02 victimhood. Later Kanbaru integration does not make the violent back side more authentic than the constrained front.

## V03 — 『化物語（下）』 — 「つばさキャット」

**Source code:** `V03`  
**Principal narrator:** Araragi.  
**Publication-local arc thesis:** idealization can be a form of perceptual failure. Hanekawa's crisis and Shinobu's missing presence expose the cost of assigning people stable moral roles while assuming that the helper himself remains outside the problem.

### Decisive source findings

- Hanekawa's April 29 parental violence and Golden Week cat history enter the record only partially here; V07 later provides the authoritative backfill.
- Araragi's rhetoric `委員長の中の委員長` elevates Hanekawa into a role that makes suffering harder for him to see.
- Black Hanekawa returns under accumulated stress. V03 knows the recurrence before it knows the full family structure that V07 will recover.
- Araragi's June 13 date with Hitagi and June 14 Hanekawa crisis place romance, idealization, jealousy, and rescue in direct proximity.
- At the decisive point Araragi says `助けて……忍`; Shinobu's action materially resolves the immediate oddity crisis despite her prior disappearance.

**Oddity/case route:** `障り猫 / 触り猫` and Black Hanekawa recurrence, with ontology still incomplete at this publication boundary.

**Character-state delta:** Hanekawa is simultaneously idealized helper and person under stress; Araragi's rescue confidence reaches a limit that requires Shinobu; Shinobu becomes causally necessary without verbal reconciliation.

**Relationship delta:** Hitagi relationship becomes embodied as ordinary romance; Hanekawa's unreturned desire and Araragi idealization become a triangular pressure that V08 later re-narrates from Hanekawa's side.

**Japanese targets:** `翼`; `何でもは知らない…`; `恩人 / 恩返し / 友情`; `表 / 裏 / 裏面`; `障り猫`; `助けて……忍`; `お人よし`; `本物`.

**Intervention ethics:** receiving help; the danger of placing the afflicted person into a moralized “good girl” role; rescue can require asking a harmed partner for aid.

**Retrospective V2 revision:** V07 and V08 materially revise what V03 can explain. They do not make V03's ignorance dishonest; they show why publication-local focalization matters.

## V04 — 『傷物語』 — 第零話「こよみヴァンプ」

**Source code:** `V04`  
**Principal narrator:** retrospective Araragi.  
**Publication-local arc thesis:** the Araragi/Shinobu bond begins not as clean mutual salvation but as **mutually entangled survival through coercive compromise**. The volume is also the origin point of Hanekawa idealization and of Araragi's tendency to make his body the payment.

### Decisive source findings

- Pre-vampire Araragi formalizes isolation through `人間強度`; Hanekawa's approach directly contradicts that self-story.
- Araragi responds to Kiss-shot's `死にたくない` by giving blood and becoming her thrall. The act combines compassion, impulse, erotic/body framing, and catastrophic consequence.
- The three hunters, Oshino's negotiation, limb recovery, and Araragi's escalating vampiric capacity establish a distributed technical field rather than a solitary hero story.
- Kiss-shot's later desire for death is real but does not erase her earlier desire to live.
- Hanekawa explicitly redirects “responsibility” away from dying and toward handling consequences.
- Oshino's final compromise preserves both lives but imposes a diminished dependent condition on Kiss-shot against her stated preferred ending.

**Oddity/case route:** vampire ontology, thrall relation, body regeneration, blood/appetite, `心渡`, negotiated power reduction.

**Character-state delta:** Araragi's preexisting isolation and rescue impulse become bound to vampirism and guilt; Kiss-shot becomes Shinobu through an imposed survival arrangement; Hanekawa becomes moralized as lifesaver while also directly resisting Araragi's sacrificial logic.

**Relationship delta:** Araragi/Shinobu is founded through debt, predation, coercion, shared survival, and later possibility—not through symmetrical consent. Araragi/Hanekawa friendship becomes intensely consequential and idealized.

**Japanese targets:** `人間強度`; `友達、できちゃったね`; `眷属`; `従僕`; `交渉人`; `刃の下に心あり`; `自己犠牲 / 自己満足`; `美しくはあっても正しくはなかった`; `死に場所 / 生きる場所`; `加害者`; `傷物 / 傷物達`.

**Intervention ethics:** mirrored sacrificial rescue; coercive preservation; distributed burden; responsibility as aftermath rather than death-payment.

**Retrospective V2 revision:** V09, V17, V18, V28, V30, and Mijikana can show later mutuality, reinterpretation, and explicit consent audits. None may retroactively turn the V04 settlement into informed symmetrical consent.

# 7. Off Season evidence routes — V20–V23

# 8. Monster Season evidence routes — V24–V29

# 15. Final routing thesis

The evidence architecture now supports a stronger but still testable series-level proposition:

> **Monogatari repeatedly gives people histories, roles, wounds, bodies, names, rescuers, creators, lovers, families, specialists, and institutions that genuinely help explain who they became. The series' mature ethical pressure appears when explanation is mistaken for ownership: when origin becomes destiny, care becomes jurisdiction, guilt becomes self-execution, intimacy becomes epistemic possession, expertise becomes unquestionable authority, or a prior self becomes the only self the present is allowed to be.**

The counterpressure must remain equally visible:

- relationships genuinely shape people;
- origins genuinely matter;
- families create obligations;
- creators incur responsibility;
- institutions sometimes need coercive power;
- specialists sometimes know more;
- emergency intervention can be necessary;
- bodies constrain choices;
- history cannot simply be discarded.

The V2 synthesis must therefore resist two symmetrical errors:

> **Nothing owns the person completely.**  
> **Nothing that helped make the person is therefore unreal or irrelevant.**

That tension—not a simple celebration of limitless self-definition—is the state this ledger hands forward to Document 01 and the later Phase 4–7 synthesis corpus.
