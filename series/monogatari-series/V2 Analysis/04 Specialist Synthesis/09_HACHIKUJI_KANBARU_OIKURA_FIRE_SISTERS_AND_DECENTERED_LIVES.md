# 〈物語〉シリーズ V2 — Hachikuji, Kanbaru, Oikura, the Fire Sisters, and Decentered Lives

## Characters who become more than their original Araragi-facing role

## 0. Function and governing thesis

This document is the canonical V2 ensemble synthesis for **八九寺真宵 / Hachikuji Mayoi**, **神原駿河 / Kanbaru Suruga**, **老倉育 / Oikura Sodachi**, and the **Fire Sisters — 阿良々木火憐 / Karen Araragi and 阿良々木月火 / Tsukihi Araragi**.

The architecture deliberately places these characters together without treating them as a miscellaneous supporting-cast appendix. They share one formal problem rather than one psychology.

Each is first made legible through a role that is unusually useful to Araragi's story:

- Hachikuji is the lost child he can help and later the comic road companion who helps teach him friendship;
- Kanbaru is Hitagi's junior, Araragi's rival, the Rainy Devil case, and eventually one of the people most willing to constrain his rescue reflex;
- Oikura is the angry childhood/classroom wound through which Araragi learns how memory, social privilege, and guilt distort retrospective explanation;
- Karen is the physically reckless younger sister whose justice allows Araragi to argue about `偽物` and responsibility;
- Tsukihi is the sister whose hidden phoenix ontology lets the series test whether causal origin can erase a lived person.

Those functions are real.

The error would be to mistake **narrative usefulness to Araragi** for **the character's final ontology**.

The Off Season makes that error increasingly impossible. Oikura and Kanbaru receive first-person narration while Araragi is absent from the room. Tsukihi becomes the center of an Ononoki-narrated professional failure that Araragi does not control. Karen narrates her own mountain ordeal. Hachikuji's deity role develops into local work whose meaning is not reducible to being available for Araragi's conversations. By V30, Kanbaru is a medical professional in formation, Oikura is a municipal worker with a practical relation to the married couple, Karen is an adult police officer and judo champion, and Hachikuji can criticize family procedure rather than simply serve as nostalgic school-era residue.

The strongest ensemble formulation is:

> **Monogatari's decentering does not reveal that the earlier Araragi-facing roles were false. It shows that a role can be causally formative, relationally real, and still cease to possess jurisdiction over the person's future.**

This principle produces different trajectories.

Hachikuji's history is about **lostness becoming guidance without deleting death or farewell**.

Kanbaru's history is about **desire becoming responsibility without converting guilt into a permanent vocation**.

Oikura's history is about **hatred, injury, and difficult usefulness becoming portable parts of adulthood without requiring sweetness, reconciliation, or personality replacement**.

Karen's history is about **borrowed justice and physical bravado becoming chosen commitment while rejecting the fantasy that solitude reveals one hidden true self**.

Tsukihi's history is about **relationally authored identity proving compatible with genuine personhood even when supernatural origin, suggestibility, and regeneration radically complicate ordinary human assumptions**.

The compact D09 thesis is:

> **A continuing character becomes decentered when influence is preserved but ownership is denied: Araragi, family, lovers, parents, specialists, injuries, inherited roles, bodies, and old names can all help make a person without acquiring permanent authority to decide what that person must remain.**

# 1. Why this is an ensemble document rather than five shortened monographs

D09 needs a different method from D04–D08.

Araragi, Hitagi, Shinobu, Hanekawa, and Nadeko each organize enormous portions of the series around one concentrated longitudinal problem. The present characters are different. Their evidentiary density is substantial, but much of their importance emerges **relationally and structurally**.

The relevant question is therefore not:

> what single thematic axis defines all five?

It is:

> **what becomes visible once the corpus stops treating Araragi's first encounter with a character as the privileged center of that character's life?**

Five forms of decentering recur.

## 1.1 Narrator transfer

V10 gives Kanbaru sustained first person.

V20 gives first-person authority to Oikura and Kanbaru and places Tsukihi inside Ononoki's first-person field.

V21 gives Karen first person.

Leaving Araragi's narration does not uncover a neutral truth. It supplies another interested narrator with different blind spots.

The gain is not omniscience.

It is **access to a self-model Araragi could never fully supply**.

## 1.2 Route divergence

V09's adult Route X Hachikuji is one of the clearest demonstrations that the same origin does not generate one inevitable person.

She has recognizable temperament without the months of road-comedy history that produced main-route Hachikuji's exact relationship with Araragi.

The result is not a true-versus-false comparison.

It is evidence that relation becomes biography.

## 1.3 Role succession

Hachikuji ceases to fit `迷い牛`, dies/departs, later returns, and accepts a deity office.

Tsukihi can contemplate the end of the Fire Sisters role without ceasing to be Karen's sister.

Karen can continue valuing justice after the original middle-school unit no longer defines her entire social position.

Kanbaru can stop being the basketball star while still valuing sport.

Roles can end without making the time lived inside them fraudulent.

## 1.4 Independent relationship networks

Hitagi and Kanbaru rebuild contact without Araragi owning the relation.

Tsukihi visits Nadeko after the god crisis.

Hachikuji acts with Ononoki as a local corrective professional.

Oikura acquires guardians, classmates, coworkers, property, and independent adult standing.

Decentering is therefore not merely internal psychological growth.

It is **network expansion**.

## 1.5 Institutions and material adulthood

School.

Household.

Shrine.

Medicine.

Municipal work.

Police work.

Specialist ecology.

The characters increasingly occupy systems that continue whether Araragi is present or not.

This is one of the clearest ways the series escapes the danger of making every life a permanent satellite of its original protagonist.

# PART A — HACHIKUJI MAYOI

# 2. V01: the lost child is already more agentic than the rescue frame

Hachikuji enters through one of the series' most deceptive role reversals.

She looks like a lost elementary-school child.

Araragi therefore sees an obvious object of help.

But the living person actually caught by the `迷い牛` condition is Araragi, because he has his own reason not to return home.

Hachikuji is both:

- endangered/dead child with a real unresolved destination;
- supernatural mechanism that causes qualifying living people to become lost.

Neither role is exhaustive.

## 2.1 She rejects the helper before he understands the situation

The first encounter includes:

- rejection;
- biting;
- physical resistance;
- the misnaming routine that later becomes comic intimacy.

Retrospectively, these behaviors are partly protective. Hachikuji has learned that being followed can ensnare the follower.

The apparent helpless child is already attempting to regulate who enters her field.

## 2.2 Her desire is not simply "find the house"

The Mother's Day destination is relational.

Hachikuji died while trying to reach her mother after divorce and separation.

The importance of arrival is concentrated in:

`ただいまっ、帰りましたっ`.

The physical house no longer exists.

The speech act still matters.

The resolution therefore does not restore:

- life;
- childhood;
- the pre-divorce family;
- a literal building.

It completes a relational movement that had remained unfinished.

## 2.3 Distributed rescue is present from the start

The V01 rescue depends on:

- Araragi's persistence;
- Hitagi's local knowledge and navigation;
- Hanekawa's perception and mediation;
- Oshino's explanation;
- urban redevelopment invalidating the old informational map;
- Hachikuji's own destination and disclosure.

The series begins by making Hachikuji a case against the later slogan that one person simply saves another.

No one actor owns the outcome.

## 2.4 Resolution creates a new state rather than restoring an old one

Hachikuji remains dead.

She becomes a floating ghost capable of future encounter.

The next day she asks Araragi to speak to her when they meet.

This is important for D09 because the enduring relationship begins **after** the original case has already been resolved as much as it can be.

She is not retained as a permanent rescue object.

She becomes a voluntarily continuing person in Araragi's social world.

# 3. Road comedy: formality as intimacy rather than distance

Hachikuji has one of the most distinctive relationship-conditioned voices in the corpus.

Hyakumonogatari gives unusually clean dialogue evidence.

## 3.1 Surface grammar

High-confidence markers include:

- `です / ます` politeness;
- Araragi as `阿良々木さん`;
- lexical precision;
- elaborate wordplay;
- deliberate misnaming;
- mock-deference;
- the `失礼。噛みました` routine.

A weak reconstruction mistakes this for social distance.

That is nearly the opposite of its mature function.

## 3.2 Formal surface / intimate function

The politeness permits Hachikuji to attack more aggressively while keeping the exchange theatrically controlled.

She can:

- insult Araragi;
- expose an insecurity;
- make him perform indignation;
- then offer real companionship.

Hyaku repeatedly gives the grammar:

> **tease → expose vulnerability → offer or accept companionship.**

`HYAKU-043` and `HYAKU-068` are especially useful examples.

In the latter, Araragi's old practice of eating early to hide the fact that he ate alone becomes the object of mockery; the scene ends with Hachikuji accepting a present invitation to eat together.

The joke does not cancel the care.

The care does not retroactively make the joke insincere.

## 3.3 Direct refusal remains available

`HYAKU-086` matters because Hachikuji simply rejects Araragi's invitation to discuss romance.

Her intimacy is not an obligation to remain available for every conversational demand.

That is a useful simulation constraint:

> **Hachikuji's banter is voluntary ritual, not unlimited access.**

---

# 4. V09: saving Hachikuji means accepting a Hachikuji who may never know Araragi

V09 performs one of the series' strongest non-possession tests.

Araragi and Shinobu travel into the past and prevent Hachikuji's death.

The act can erase the relationship Araragi values most about saving her.

He chooses her survival anyway.

## 4.1 Survival is treated as sufficient benefit

When Araragi later meets adult Route X Hachikuji, she does not know him as the main-route companion.

His response is not:

- make her remember;
- demand gratitude;
- recreate the old intimacy;
- insist that the familiar ghost Hachikuji is more authentic.

He thanks her for being alive:

`生きていてくれて、ありがとうございました`.

This is one of Araragi's cleanest non-possessive rescue moments.

## 4.2 Adult Route X Hachikuji is neither truer nor less real

She retains recognizable temperament while lacking shared history.

Main-route Araragi explicitly recognizes that the Hachikuji he knows emerged through months of mutual exchange.

That produces the D09 relationship rule:

> **relationship can co-author personality without granting either participant ownership of the other.**

## 4.3 Hachikuji changes Araragi too

V09's Route X causal reconstruction suggests that without the month of Hachikuji friendship Araragi may not have become capable of asking Shinobu for help during the Black Hanekawa crisis.

Hachikuji is therefore not merely someone Araragi changed.

She changes what he can later do.

The reciprocal effect is important because it turns the road-comedy relation into moral development without making either participant a teacher by institutional title.

---

# 5. V12: farewell, role failure, and Hachikuji's refusal to let love become totalization

V12 is Hachikuji's most important character volume after V01.

Her old Mother's Day problem is solved.

She no longer wants to make strangers lost.

In ordinary moral language, this is growth.

Under the oddity rule Gaen describes, it also means she no longer fulfills the function that licensed her continued `迷い牛` existence.

The result is a brutal contradiction:

> the person has morally outgrown the role that metaphysically sustains her.

## 5.1 She does not choose to resume harming strangers

A technically available path would be to return to lost-cow function.

Hachikuji refuses.

Her development therefore becomes an ontology problem rather than being rewritten as an error.

## 5.2 Araragi responds with life-totalization

His immediate solution is to remain lost with her for decades.

This sounds romantic as sacrifice.

It spends:

- his future;
- his relationship with Hitagi;
- family obligations;
- education;
- ordinary adulthood;
- Shinobu's linked future.

Hachikuji sees the cost more clearly than he does.

`全部は選べませんよ`.

The line is not anti-love.

It is anti-totalization.

## 5.3 The grasping image is a consent image

`八九寺はもう、僕の手を握ってはいない。僕が、つかんでいるだけだ。`

The asymmetry is decisive.

Araragi still wants proximity.

Hachikuji has stopped choosing that form of proximity.

The ethical act is release.

## 5.4 Hachikuji authors the farewell

She chooses the familiar routine.

She asks for the mispronunciation/biting callback.

The routine becomes a kiss.

Then:

`大好きでしたよ、阿良々木さん`.

The scene does not reveal a solemn true Hachikuji beneath comedy.

It shows that the comic Hachikuji can use the same relationship language to perform farewell.

Her voice does not need to become unrecognizable in order to become serious.

## 5.5 `わたしは帰るんですよ`

The girl whose first arc was about returning home uses `帰る` again.

This time the destination is departure from Araragi rather than arrival at a mother's address.

The recurring word gains history without becoming a deterministic motif.

# 6. V18: caring coercion, separate consent, and a new home

Hachikuji's return is ethically complicated enough that D09 must preserve the sequence.

## 6.1 Extraction from hell is not consented

Araragi catches Hachikuji and drags her upward by surprise.

She screams.

She had expected to return to Sai-no-Kawara.

The action is emotionally understandable.

It is also coercive.

Later happiness cannot rewrite the action into prior consent.

## 6.2 Godhood is a separate decision

After return, Gaen gives broad alternatives.

Hachikuji does not understand every technical responsibility.

She does understand the part most relevant to her decision:

- the role permits continued existence;
- it permits continuing relation;
- it gives her a legitimate place in the living world.

This is **bounded consent under uncertainty**.

The project should not require omniscience before a choice can count as meaningful.

## 6.3 `わたしの家`

At Kita-Shirahebi Shrine, Hachikuji eventually calls the place her home.

The spatial sequence is extraordinary:

- child unable to reach mother's home;
- road as dwelling;
- departed ghost;
- hell;
- returned person without legitimate living-world ontology;
- shrine-home attached to a public role.

The new home does not restore the old one.

It is authored later.

## 6.4 Role change does not invalidate V12

The V12 farewell remains real.

The V18 deity choice is later.

A changed decision does not prove the earlier decision was false.

This becomes especially explicit in SHORT-033, where Hachikuji argues that a choice can have been right in the past and no longer govern the present.

Historical validity and present sovereignty are different questions.

---

# 7. Hachikuji as local deity-professional

Once Hachikuji becomes a god, the corpus refuses to freeze her as Araragi's recovered friend.

## 7.1 V20: local knowledge corrects specialist error

Ononoki's `つきひアンドゥ` intervention goes wrong.

Hachikuji supplies a rule-aware local correction that a formally trained specialist missed.

This does not establish:

> deity outranks specialist.

It establishes:

> **different situated models can create useful redundancy.**

Hachikuji's relation to place and oddity function becomes expertise of a kind.

## 7.2 V25: `これはわたしの仕事です`

The Kujaku case is a major maturation of Hachikuji's deity role.

She is not simply present to help because Araragi is nearby.

She identifies the lost child as a person within her domain.

`これはわたしの仕事です`.

The line matters because work becomes self-claimed role.

## 7.3 `あなたの人生の、つらい部分`

Kujaku believes her life is over.

Hachikuji does not deny the pain.

She changes the object of finality:

> the painful part can be over without the whole life being over.

The intervention is powerful precisely because it avoids empty optimism.

Hachikuji does not promise:

- family repair;
- happiness;
- success;
- easy attachment.

She protects futurity.

## 7.4 Attachment is rehabilitated locally

Kujaku has learned to treat attachment itself as weakness.

Hachikuji tells her to attach.

This should not be universalized into:

> all attachment is good.

For a child trained to erase dependence, permission to care is the needed correction.

The mature Hachikuji can therefore act as a guide not because she has stopped knowing what lostness feels like, but because she does not confuse the old condition with the only possible future.

---

# 8. V27 and V30: Hachikuji becomes a process critic

## 8.1 Forgiveness can be revised

During the apology crisis, Hachikuji gives Araragi an unsettling example.

She once accepted his unilateral extraction from hell.

She can still imagine later saying:

`あのときは許しましたけれど、今から思うと、やっぱり許せなくなりました`.

The point is not that she has in fact revoked forgiveness.

The point is that **earlier forgiveness does not permanently remove the harmed person's interpretive standing**.

Consent and forgiveness are not once-and-for-all ownership transfers.

## 8.2 V30: family procedure

By V30, Hachikuji is not principally used as nostalgia.

She helps critique Araragi's benevolent plan to reorganize Shinobu as daughter.

The adult Araragi has improved enormously.

He still initially acts as though he can design a family structure and then reveal it.

Hachikuji's role is procedural:

- people with standing must be consulted;
- earlier specialist/name/seal structures cannot be ignored merely because Araragi dislikes them;
- family formation is prospective governance, not a sentimental naming act.

This is far removed from V01's lost-child rescue while remaining recognizably Hachikuji.

# 9. Hachikuji reconstruction profile seed

## Stable personality core

- verbally agile;
- strongly relationship-sensitive;
- capable of rapid boundary-setting;
- playful without being indiscriminately available;
- unusually capable of exposing Araragi's self-deception through humor;
- attached to home/destination language without treating one physical place as essence;
- capable of direct seriousness when necessary.

## Mutable developmental traits

- `迷い牛` victim/mechanism → floating ghost companion;
- companion → departed person who authors farewell;
- coerced return → voluntarily accepted deity role;
- deity role → active local work and process criticism.

## Speech baseline

- `わたし`;
- `阿良々木さん`;
- dense polite morphology;
- mock-deferential aggression;
- misnaming/wordplay routines;
- serious statements often remain inside recognizable formal/comic syntax.

## Dialogue procedure

Typical mature Araragi/Hachikuji interaction:

1. Araragi offers premise or overstatement;
2. Hachikuji reframes through precise language or ridicule;
3. Araragi performs outrage/tsukkomi;
4. underlying vulnerability becomes visible;
5. companionship, advice, or a clear boundary appears.

## Stress mode

Under existential threat she can become direct without losing self-command. V12 demonstrates that she may be more capable than Araragi of accepting a painful limit when he tries to spend his whole future to avoid it.

## OOC traps

Do not write Hachikuji as:

- merely a cute ghost child;
- permanently dependent on Araragi;
- a generic wise deity;
- deferential because she uses polite Japanese;
- automatically consenting to Araragi because later outcomes are good;
- available to him without boundary;
- restored to an original pre-death life.

# PART B — KANBARU SURUGA

# 10. V02: Kanbaru begins as contradiction, not hidden darkness

Kanbaru's first major arc is one of the foundational anti-essentialist cases in the series.

The Rainy Devil structure contains incompatible wishes.

She wants:

- athletic improvement through her own effort;
- revenge on children who humiliated her;
- proximity to Hitagi;
- Araragi removed because he occupies that proximity.

The violent wish is real.

The loving wish is real.

The disciplined athlete is real.

The jealous attacker is real.

The source explicitly rejects treating one side as the true person and the other as a mask.

## 10.1 `表 / 裏` is not a true-self hierarchy

Oshino's correction from Monkey's Paw to Rainy Devil reallocates responsibility.

The Monkey's Paw story permits a morally cleaner self-story:

> I made a pure wish; an external object perversely twisted it.

Rainy Devil says the violent wish already existed as another side.

But the text is equally explicit that the back exists because the front exists.

The correct D09 formulation is:

> **Kanbaru's development begins by owning contradiction without allowing the most shameful desire to monopolize authenticity.**

## 10.2 Her athletic discipline is part of the resistance

The Rainy Devil does not simply pilot Kanbaru's body.

Her training contributes technique.

Her conscious restraint also delays full activation.

The same embodied discipline is both:

- resource for danger;
- resource for restraint.

This complexity should persist into later sport/medical development.

## 10.3 Hitagi refuses false reciprocity without expelling Kanbaru

Kanbaru says she loves Hitagi.

Hitagi does not pretend to return romantic love.

She asks Kanbaru to remain nearby.

The resulting relation is asymmetrical but durable.

This matters because Kanbaru's future is not built on "getting over" Hitagi by declaring the old love fake.

It is built on learning to live near a nonreciprocal attachment without converting it into entitlement.

## 10.4 Kanbaru becomes an ethical limit on Araragi almost immediately

During Nadeko's crisis, Kanbaru physically restrains Araragi and tells him not to confuse whom he is trying to save.

The junior he helped becomes someone willing to stop him.

This is one of the earliest reversals of the one-directional rescue model.

# 11. Kanbaru's voice: formal earnestness is the engine of transgression

Hyakumonogatari gives a strong relationship-conditioned voice profile.

## 11.1 Baseline morphology

High-value markers include:

- first person `私`;
- `阿良々木先輩`;
- `ふむ`;
- declarative `～のだ` forms;
- composed, almost argumentative setup;
- frank bodily and sexual vocabulary;
- little embarrassment about transgressive conclusions.

The comic mechanism is not merely "Kanbaru is perverted."

It is:

> **serious sentence architecture carrying content that becomes increasingly outrageous.**

## 11.2 Araragi is often the boundary tsukkomi

With Kanbaru, Araragi becomes the person who says that formal syntax does not make the proposal normal.

This relationship mode differs from:

- Hitagi's premise control;
- Hanekawa's explanatory correction;
- Hachikuji's polite mock-combat.

A generic "Araragi banter" model loses this distinction.

## 11.3 Kanbaru can be the one who thinks Araragi has gone too far

Hyaku includes several reversals in which even Kanbaru judges his Hanekawa fixation excessive.

That is useful reconstruction evidence.

Her transgressive comic register does not erase ordinary standards of context or affection.

---

# 12. V10: responsibility without self-punishment

*Hanamonogatari* is Kanbaru's main independence document.

Araragi is no longer the narrator.

Hitagi is not physically present to organize the scene.

Kanbaru must interpret:

- her body;
- the lost devil arm;
- Numachi Rouka;
- old sport identity;
- guilt;
- inherited family material;
- future action.

## 12.1 `暗黒面` becomes a rejected self-model

Kanbaru explicitly criticizes her old tendency to split her `自分の暗黒面` into an external other.

This is the mature response to V02.

The solution is not:

> admit the darkness was the real Kanbaru.

It is:

> stop using otherness to avoid responsibility for one part of yourself.

## 12.2 Araragi's correction: punishment is not reflection

The volume's important formulation is:

`自分を責めることは反省じゃない`.

Self-punishment can become a second avoidance.

If guilt becomes Kanbaru's permanent vocation, then she never has to answer the more difficult question:

> what do I do next?

## 12.3 Numachi prevents moral purification

Rouka listens to suffering partly for selfish reasons.

People still experience relief.

Kanbaru dislikes her.

They can still share a moment of rival/athletic recognition.

The relation blocks several simplifications:

- impure motive does not erase beneficial effect;
- helpful effect does not make the method fully good;
- rival need not become friend;
- disliking someone does not make every exchange meaningless.

## 12.4 Sport remains joy without becoming destiny

Kanbaru's basketball history is deeply important.

The series does not require her to return to basketball as proof of authentic selfhood.

SHORT-003 is especially useful here: lost practice can remain genuine joy without becoming a mandatory true vocation.

This prepares the adult sports-doctor trajectory without making that outcome a prewritten destiny.

---

# 13. Tooe, inheritance, and the right not to become one's parent

Kanbaru's maternal history is one of her most persistent later pressures.

Gaen Tooe can be:

- mother;
- admired authority;
- inherited voice;
- source of occult material;
- model of charisma;
- person Kanbaru may eventually need to disagree with.

## 13.1 Internalized maternal speech is not resurrection

SHORT-021 presents a dreamlike/internal conversation with Tooe.

The source ceiling matters.

The voice is psychologically and relationally meaningful.

It is not automatically direct testimony from the dead.

## 13.2 The "parent's back" formulation is deliberately double

Tooe's voice observes that if a child is watching a parent's back, the parent has also turned away.

This is useful because it refuses idealized inheritance.

Admiration does not erase absence.

## 13.3 Influence is not fusion

V20 makes the rule explicit:

`神原駿河と臥煙遠江は別人`.

And:

`私は私にならないと`.

Kanbaru does not need to purge maternal influence.

She needs to prevent influence from becoming jurisdiction.

---

# 14. V17: corrective friction is a form of care

Kanbaru's confrontation with Shinobu is one of her strongest relationship functions.

Araragi's intimacy with Shinobu creates a blind spot around the first-thrall problem.

Kanbaru can say what he cannot.

She refuses deferential master-language and calls Shinobu socially avoidant.

Her rhetoric is harsh enough that the source does not certify every formulation as ideal.

But the function matters.

She accepts multiple sincere answers from Shinobu.

What she refuses is indefinite non-answer.

This becomes a mature D09 principle:

> **a continuing friend can care by introducing friction that a more intimate relation has become structurally unable to generate.**

Araragi eventually trusts Kanbaru enough to send her rather than occupying every rescue field himself.

Kanbaru's importance therefore shifts from:

- rescued junior;

to:

- person whose independent judgment can be trusted when Araragi should not be the one acting.

# 15. V20: personal closure does not dispose of worldly residue

`するがボーンヘッド` is the crucial Off Season decentering text.

Kanbaru is the narrator.

The Rainy Devil reappears not simply as her bodily problem but as distributed material residue.

## 15.1 V10 remains true

Kanbaru's personal achievement is not revoked.

Her arm can be restored.

Her guilt can be differently governed.

The world can still contain fragments.

This distinction is fundamental:

> **personal resolution ≠ material disposal.**

## 15.2 Prevention expands beyond known intimates

Kanbaru chooses to search for dangerous fragments that could harm strangers she may never meet.

This is ethically attractive.

It also creates a risk.

If every possible unknown future victim creates unlimited personal duty, Kanbaru could reproduce Araragi's self-erasure at larger scale.

The current project remains healthier because:

- she authors the choice;
- Ougi participates;
- other specialists are already involved;
- Kanbaru retains ordinary life goals.

Sustainability remains an OPEN question rather than assumed virtue.

## 15.3 Chosen inheritance

Kanbaru's history includes:

- Tooe;
- Rouka;
- Araragi;
- Hitagi;
- Rainy Devil;
- Ougi.

V20's answer is not to identify the one true mentor.

`私は私にならないと`.

The mature self is assembled through differentiation rather than purification.

# 16. Adult Kanbaru: medicine, sport, Hitagi, and networked competence

V30 supplies an important later state.

Kanbaru is twenty-three and a fifth-year medical student on a sports-doctor trajectory.

This future makes sense with her history.

It should not be treated as destiny retroactively visible in V02.

## 16.1 Sport becomes transferable knowledge rather than identity prison

Basketball mattered enormously.

Its loss mattered.

Adult Kanbaru can carry athletic history into medicine without being required to become the old star again.

## 16.2 Hitagi relation is independent adult fact

The renewed closeness between Hitagi and Kanbaru exists independently of Araragi.

Kanbaru is not merely his conduit to his wife.

This is one of the strongest D09 decentering results.

## 16.3 Consultation discipline

Kanbaru demonstrates:

- local judgment;
- network awareness;
- ability to delegate;
- role-specific use of others' competence.

Her call to Nadeko explicitly distinguishes:

`漫画家じゃなくて、専門家として`.

That line is about Nadeko, but it also reveals adult Kanbaru's own improved social/professional classification ability.

People can be called under one role for one task without reducing them to that role.

## 16.4 Family-name reflection

Kanbaru values `神原` because of the grandparents who raised her.

At the same time, she can understand why someone might choose a beloved partner's surname.

Her concern about same-sex family recognition further expands her from private erotic junior to an adult who notices how legal structures distribute public legitimacy.

# PART C — OIKURA SODACHI

# 18. V16: the person Araragi could not classify

Oikura is especially important to D09 because her first large role in Araragi's story is already a warning against protagonist-centered classification.

Araragi does not simply fail to notice that she is suffering.

He sees much of the evidence.

He sees:

- a damaged building;
- broken windows;
- a girl who summons him there;
- mathematical lessons conducted inside the space;
- a person whose behavior is unusual enough to remain memorable.

His mistake is categorical.

He calls the place an abandoned house.

It is Oikura's home.

That distinction is the moral center of `そだちリドル`.

A secure child can look directly at material evidence of domestic violence and misclassify it because his concept of `家` does not include a home that looks like that.

The failure is therefore not adequately described as:

> Araragi should have been more observant.

He was observant.

The stronger formulation is:

> **observation is limited by the social categories available to the observer.**

This matters to Oikura's later decentering because she begins as a person who already existed outside the protagonist's conceptual world.

Her later first-person story does not create that independence.

It reveals how long it had been there.

## 18.1 The class trial: Oikura's grievance is institutional before it is personal

`おうぎフォーミュラ` initially presents a school grievance that can be remembered as an interpersonal clash between Araragi and Oikura.

The reconstructed event is larger.

The classroom uses majority procedure to convert a distorted situation into a socially authorized answer.

The teacher participates.

Oikura is scapegoated.

Araragi's anti-majoritarian reaction later becomes one source of his `人間強度` posture.

For Oikura, however, the event is not primarily an origin story for Araragi.

It is something that happened **to her**.

D09 should preserve that asymmetry.

The same event may be:

- formative defense for one person;
- institutional betrayal for another;
- evidence about a teacher;
- evidence about the class as a social system.

A protagonist-centered reading risks turning Oikura's public injury into explanatory material for Araragi's loneliness.

The decentered correction is:

> **Araragi is one participant in Oikura's history, not the owner of its significance.**

## 18.2 Domestic abuse and social legibility

The damaged home is not merely symbolic.

V16 gives material evidence of violence.

Ougi's reconstruction—that young Oikura may have selected Araragi because his parents were police officers and hoped he would tell them what he saw—is strong, but remains reconstruction rather than recovered first-person confession.

That evidence-state distinction is important.

We can say with high confidence that:

- Oikura's home was violent;
- Araragi misclassified it;
- Oikura knew his parents' occupation;
- the encounter mattered to her.

We should not silently convert the inferred SOS into a verbatim childhood intention.

The difference is not pedantic.

Oikura's entire corpus is about the danger of a person becoming too legible through somebody else's explanatory confidence.

## 18.3 Memory taxonomy: forgotten is not unreal

`そだちロスト` adds a more difficult layer.

Araragi's parents say Oikura once stayed in his home under protection.

Nadeko independently remembers the quiet girl.

Araragi has no episodic memory of the event.

The afterword supplies the durable principle:

`忘れたからといって、なかったことにはならない`.

For D09, this should be treated as a character principle as much as a narrator principle.

Oikura's relation to Araragi contains events that mattered to her even when he did not retain them.

This makes their relationship radically asymmetric in memory.

It also prevents a sentimental shortcut:

> if Araragi remembers now, the old injury is repaired.

No.

Recovered knowledge changes the present evidentiary state.

It does not recreate the attention or rescue that did not occur then.

## 18.4 The mother's death: plausible truth does not erase disclosure ethics

Ougi and Hanekawa reconstruct that Oikura's mother died in the closed room and that Oikura continued caring for the decomposing body because she could not recognize the death.

The reconstruction is powerful.

It remains an inference rather than forensic omniscience.

More importantly, Hanekawa and Araragi disagree about what to do with a likely truth.

Knowledge and disclosure separate.

Araragi ultimately returns to speak with Oikura.

The mature point is not that truth-telling always wins.

It is that Oikura cannot be treated simply as an object on whom the investigators perform moral correctness.

She is a person to whom one must answer.

## 18.5 The letter remains withheld

Oikura leaves a letter.

The corpus does not disclose its contents at this boundary.

This is a useful formal restraint.

The reader is not automatically entitled to every piece of the relationship merely because Araragi is the usual narrator.

The letter's existence is evidence.

Its content is not.

That boundary should remain intact in reconstruction.

# 19. Hatred as continuity without romance or reconciliation

Oikura's hatred of Araragi is easy to sentimentalize because Monogatari repeatedly turns hostility into intimacy elsewhere.

D09 should resist that habit.

The line comparing the force of hatred to love does not reclassify hatred as concealed romance.

The stronger reading is that hatred can become **identity-bearing continuity**.

If a person has spent years organizing causal explanation around:

- who failed to notice;
- who forgot;
- who was protected while she was not;
- who symbolizes the life she lacked;

then relinquishing hatred can feel like relinquishing the archive itself.

This is psychologically and narratively different from romantic attraction.

## 19.1 Oikura does not need to become sweet to become better

The series repeatedly tests a common recovery expectation:

> if she heals, she will stop being abrasive.

V20 and V23 reject it.

Oikura can become:

- safer;
- housed;
- employed;
- more socially capable;
- more capable of giving advice;
- more capable of accepting support;

while remaining verbally harsh with Araragi.

This is not failed growth.

It demonstrates that recovery is not personality convergence toward pleasantness.

## 19.2 Relation can survive through argument

Oikura and Araragi repeatedly break contact.

They also repeatedly become useful to one another.

The later relation is not adequately captured by:

- friends;
- enemies;
- siblings;
- romantic tension;
- reconciled victims and apologizers.

It is a durable adversarial intimacy with practical consequence.

The important V2 rule is:

> **a relationship may remain real without being morally purified or sentimentally renamed.**

That rule becomes explicit in V30 when Oikura rejects Araragi's attempt to call her `准家族`.

# 20. V20 `そだちフィアスコ`: ordinary life as a constructed project

Off Season changes Oikura most radically not by giving her a new personality but by giving her the narrator chair.

The reader can now observe how much engineering goes into what other people call ordinary life.

Her project is:

`目指せ普通`.

This is aspirational.

Not descriptive.

Oikura knows that changing town, uniform, and school does not automatically change the person who enters them.

She therefore attempts to design ordinary social participation.

## 20.1 `普通` is local competence, not universal baseline

Oikura studies:

- classroom hierarchy;
- gender grouping;
- smartphone norms;
- dress;
- introduction strategy;
- who appears socially isolated;
- how friendship might be initiated without catastrophe.

Her attempt itself reveals something important.

"Normal" is not one neutral human default.

It is a bundle of institution-specific practices.

What counts as socially legible at Naoetsu does not map perfectly onto Shishikurazaki.

Oikura's desire for ordinary life is therefore not simple conformity.

It is desire for access to:

- school attendance;
- a home;
- friendship;
- future education;
- mundane mistakes that do not become disasters.

## 20.2 `家に住みたかった`

The Hakobe household matters because Oikura's acceptance is not emotionally pure.

She remains suspicious.

She dislikes feeling indebted.

She worries about motive.

And she stays because:

`家に住みたかった`.

The line is one of the most powerful ordinary desires in the corpus.

V16 showed a child whose home could not be recognized as a home by another child.

V20 shows that same person choosing a household even before she can feel uncomplicated gratitude.

The mature rule is:

> **care can become useful infrastructure before the recipient feels morally clean about receiving it.**

This is the opposite of a rescue fantasy that demands immediate trust and gratitude.

## 20.3 Public support enters the recovery model

Oikura recognizes that:

- public assistance;
- scholarships;
- public school;
- adult guardianship;
- institutional safety nets

exist as parts of a future.

The text does not pretend these institutions would have automatically prevented childhood abuse.

The significance is narrower and stronger.

Her earlier survival model rendered some support systems almost invisible.

Recovery includes becoming able to perceive infrastructure that is not intimate rescue.

That is a major decentering from Araragi.

## 20.4 Friendship as optimization—and the failure of the model

Oikura selects a seemingly isolated classmate as a low-risk friendship target.

This is partly instrumental.

It is also sincere.

She genuinely wants a friend.

Her error is not wanting too little.

It is believing that enough analysis can eliminate social uncertainty.

She over-invests.

Over-interprets.

Interrogates.

Presses boundaries.

The apparently ordinary class turns out to contain its own histories of exclusion and guilt.

The world refuses to behave like a proof.

## 20.5 `間違おうと思って間違えたことなんてない`

This is perhaps the most important Oikura epistemic line.

She never set out intending to be wrong.

That makes error harder, not easier.

A person acting under a model she believes correct cannot use sincerity as proof of correctness.

For a character associated with mathematics, this is an important transition.

The problem is no longer simply finding the right answer.

It is preserving corrigibility while acting.

## 20.6 Invasive intervention remains invasive

Oikura's investigation includes acts that should not be cleaned up merely because partial classroom reconnection follows.

She:

- tracks;
- interrogates;
- enters spaces aggressively;
- uses an old ID;
- accesses a phone;
- reads private communications.

The outcome has some socially useful effects.

The methods remain ethically mixed.

This is one reason Oikura is a strong decentered adultward character.

She is not being rewritten into the morally correct girl the reader should now admire without reservation.

She is learning through action while still capable of coercive overreach.

## 20.7 `阿良々木派`: portable influence without emotional surrender

The line appears while Araragi is absent:

`阿良々木派よ`.

Oikura has not forgiven him.

She has not become his disciple.

She imagines he may have handled the situation more smoothly.

Yet she can take one aspect of his intervention ethic and use it.

This is one of D09's cleanest decentering formulas:

> **influence can become portable without requiring allegiance.**

Araragi's role in her life becomes material she can appropriate.

He does not need to be physically present.

He does not need to receive gratitude.

He does not get to own what she makes from the influence.

# 21. V23: adult Oikura builds infrastructure without losing abrasive voice

V23 confirms that Oikura's Off Season project produces real adult structure.

She has:

- stable work in local government;
- accounting competence;
- property;
- practical knowledge;
- renewed contact;
- capacity to intervene in other people's relationships.

The source deliberately does not translate this into emotional softness.

She still insults Araragi.

She still counts `絶交`.

She still uses grotesque jokes.

## 21.1 Professional code-switching is additive

V23 demonstrates that Oikura can produce an entirely competent public-facing service register.

That proves she is not incapable of politeness.

It also proves the abrasive private register is not simple social incompetence.

She has **added** a professional voice.

She has not replaced the personal one.

This is high-value D18/D25 evidence.

Mature functioning can mean register differentiation rather than global smoothing.

## 21.2 The old house becomes chosen material

Her adult home thesis is:

`思い出を私色に塗り替える。あの家で、幸せな家庭を築くのよ`.

This should not be read as magical overwriting.

She buys the site.

She plans to make a new life there.

The old memory remains the reason the sentence matters.

The mature act is prospective authorship over place:

> **the past owns causal history; it does not own every future use of the site.**

## 21.3 She can help Araragi and Hitagi without becoming their emotional property

Oikura helped create conditions for reconciliation during the couple's university period.

Later she advises Araragi against impulsive unilateral sacrifice in career decisions.

This is significant because an early reading could cast Oikura as the person Araragi failed and must somehow repay.

Adult Oikura instead becomes someone who can correct him.

The direction of help reverses without producing a debt settlement narrative.

## 21.4 Adult contact is not a sentimental endpoint

The fourth `絶交` ends in renewed contact.

That does not certify:

- forgiveness;
- close friendship;
- family status;
- romanticization.

It certifies a practical fact:

> the relationship remains usable.

That is enough.

# 22. V27: apology, self-punishment, and the danger of making another person carry your repentance

V27 is not primarily an Oikura volume, but its apology theory is essential for D09 because it supplies language that clarifies the Araragi/Oikura relation.

The central warning is that apology is not automatically a harmless moral good.

A person can apologize in ways that:

- transfer stigma;
- demand forgiveness;
- force the recipient to reassure the apologizer;
- end dialogue through total self-condemnation;
- comply with an external command rather than address the person harmed.

The volume names several structures:

- `謝罪という名の暴力`;
- `自罰傾向`;
- `自己犠牲`;
- `関係性の放棄`;
- `試し行動`.

These terms retrospectively improve how we read Araragi's guilt around Oikura.

## 22.1 Total self-blame can erase the harmed person again

If Araragi says:

> everything that happened to Oikura is my fault,

that can look morally serious.

But the statement risks another appropriation.

It turns Oikura's history into evidence about Araragi's moral identity.

It can also terminate discussion:

> I am entirely guilty; therefore there is nothing left to negotiate.

V16 had already resisted this through Hanekawa's distinction between blaming the past self and reflecting.

V27 gives stronger procedural language.

## 22.2 Forgiveness is not required to freeze

Hachikuji's thought experiment matters to Oikura too.

A person can once have forgiven and later reconsider.

A person can also never forgive.

The series does not make emotional absolution the price of adult functioning.

This gives Oikura's persistent hostility a legitimate place in the archive without making it the whole future.

## 22.3 A relationship can improve without being renamed reconciliation

This is the key Oikura result across V16–V30.

She can:

- speak;
- help;
- receive help;
- advise;
- share contact;
- perform administrative work;

without accepting a sentimental label for the relation.

That becomes explicit in V30.

# 23. V30: municipal work, surnames, and the rejection of `准家族`

By V30, Oikura works in municipal administration and becomes practically involved in Araragi and Hitagi's marriage paperwork.

This is an excellent adult endpoint for a character whose earlier life was repeatedly damaged by failures of family, school, public support, and social classification.

She now operates inside civic procedure.

That does not make her an institutional saint.

It does demonstrate an adult competence whose center is not Araragi.

## 23.1 `准家族` is a boundary violation in affectionate language

Araragi attempts to describe Oikura as quasi-family.

She rejects it.

This is not a rejection of all history or help.

It is a rejection of his authority to **rename the relationship**.

That is one of D09's strongest non-possession rules:

> **history, practical help, and emotional importance do not entitle one party to upgrade a relation into kinship.**

## 23.2 Name change does not erase continuity

Oikura reveals that she had another surname before `老倉`.

Her line is:

`名前が変わっただけで、私であることに違いはないもの`.

This belongs with D05–D08's name/identity work.

Names matter.

They organize:

- family;
- administration;
- history;
- social recognition.

They do not monopolize personal continuity.

## 23.3 Adult Oikura remains a practical corrector

She continues to give concrete planning/weather advice.

The woman who once existed in Araragi's narrative primarily as a buried failure now exists as a functioning civic adult who can tell him when his plans are stupid.

That is decentering in its most ordinary and convincing form.

# PART D — THE FIRE SISTERS

# 25. Karen and Tsukihi: one brand, two people

The Fire Sisters are easy to flatten because the series itself gives them a unit name.

`ファイヤーシスターズ` is socially real.

It organizes:

- local reputation;
- volunteer/justice activity;
- sibling identity;
- Araragi's protective narration;
- later retrospective memory.

But the unit does not erase the sisters' differences.

V05 and V06 already make that distinction explicit.

Karen and Tsukihi do not mean the same thing by justice.

They do not have the same body.

They do not have the same future.

They do not have the same speech.

They do not relate to Araragi in the same way.

The D09 rule is therefore:

> **a shared identity can be real without converting the people inside it into one character.**

This is a small-scale version of the project's larger anti-essentialist architecture.

---

# 26. Karen in V05: justice, imitation, and `後始末`

Karen's early justice is deliberately difficult to classify.

Araragi calls the sisters' activity `正義の味方ごっこ`.

That judgment cannot be accepted neutrally.

He also feels:

- envy;
- inferiority;
- irritation at their local social reach.

His fake/real classification is interested narration.

## 26.1 Borrowed justice can have real consequences

Karen's justice is partly:

- youthful;
- theatrical;
- imitative;
- under-owned conceptually.

None of that makes the consequences imaginary.

She confronts Kaiki.

She is harmed.

Her network of local action exists.

V05's fake/real argument is strongest when it refuses purity:

> **a borrowed ideal can become more genuinely one's own through practice and reflection without needing to have originated in perfect authenticity.**

This does not mean every imitation eventually becomes virtuous.

It means origin is not the only variable.

## 26.2 Will and power are not the same

Karen has extraordinary physical confidence.

She is still a child confronting an adult operator whose methods she does not fully understand.

The gap matters.

Her willingness to fight injustice does not automatically produce:

- competence;
- authority;
- proportionality;
- safety.

This is one of the earliest places where the series separates heroic disposition from professional capacity.

## 26.3 `後始末` matters more than defeating the villain

After Kaiki leaves, the sisters continue cleanup because the social damage he exploited remains.

This is an important adultward seed.

Justice is not only:

> identify bad man → defeat bad man.

It includes residue.

People remain hurt.

Rumors remain.

Trust remains damaged.

The Fire Sisters' decision to keep doing `後始末` is analytically more valuable than imagining them as miniature superheroes.

## 26.4 Karen's voice

High-confidence baseline:

- `兄ちゃん` for Araragi;
- blunt directness;
- physical bravado;
- energetic rhythm;
- relatively little ornamental hedging;
- action-oriented framing.

Later material complicates but does not erase this.

# 27. Karen in V21: the solitary training fantasy collapses into distributed authorship

`かれんオウガ` gives Karen a first-person story and therefore performs the same decentering move V20 performs for Oikura and Kanbaru.

The story begins from an old genre temptation.

Go into the mountains.

Suffer.

Meet the real self.

Return stronger.

The volume dismantles that logic.

## 27.1 The exhausted body is not automatically the true person

Karen rejects the idea that the version of herself hanging from a cliff under severe stress must be the authentic one.

The Karen who exists:

- at school;
- in the dojo;
- with family;
- under a teacher's influence;
- in ordinary food/sleep conditions

is equally constitutive.

This is one of V21's strongest anti-ascetic statements.

> **deprivation strips away supports; it does not necessarily reveal essence.**

## 27.2 Domain expertise remains bounded

Karen is physically formidable.

She is not automatically good at:

- hiking;
- maps;
- provisioning;
- wilderness planning.

This is essential reconstruction material.

"Athletic" is not a universal competence token.

## 27.3 Secret support does not invalidate the trial

Araragi and Shinobu support Karen without fully advertising that support.

Her supposedly solitary journey is therefore relationally saturated.

The immature interpretation would be:

> therefore Karen did nothing herself.

The text gives a better line after assistance:

`そこから先は、自分の意志で、前に進んだ`.

External help and self-authored continuation coexist.

## 27.4 `己のありようも、一通りではない`

Karen explicitly reaches a plural-self formulation.

There is no one authorized Karen.

This protects against reducing her to:

- Fire Sister;
- martial artist;
- Araragi's little sister;
- ascetic trainee;
- future police officer.

Each can become real without monopolizing identity.

## 27.5 Justice remains a chosen project

V21 does not reveal that Karen's justice was only childhood mimicry.

It is better understood as a project whose ownership increases.

She can retain justice after becoming more self-aware about:

- training;
- support;
- competence;
- plural identity.

## 27.6 Adult endpoint: police officer and judo champion

By V30, Karen is a police officer and national police-judo champion.

This later state **strengthens** rather than erases the adolescent material.

Her physical and justice interests find institutional expression.

But the correct retrospective formulation is not:

> she was always destined to become police.

It is:

> **the adult career is a later consolidation of several earlier chosen continuities.**

The institutional setting also matters.

Adult justice is no longer purely a neighborhood volunteer brand.

It now sits inside:

- law;
- procedure;
- professional accountability;
- public authority.

D10 and later system documents may develop that further.

# 28. Tsukihi in V06: fake justice before the phoenix reveal

Tsukihi becomes much more interesting if V06 is read in the order the text gives it.

Before the `しでの鳥` revelation, she already demonstrates metacognition about the Fire Sisters.

She distinguishes her own justice from Karen's.

Karen's justice is closer to a goal.

Tsukihi can call hers a hobby.

She recognizes influence from Karen and Araragi.

She can call the ideology `偽物`.

Most importantly, she can imagine the Fire Sisters ending.

This means the later phoenix ontology does **not** create her capacity for self-reflection.

The person is already visible before the hidden body is classified.

## 28.1 `偽物` here concerns ownership of an ideology

Tsukihi's use of `偽物` does not mean:

> I am secretly not a real person.

She is evaluating the provenance of justice.

This distinction becomes critical after the reveal because Araragi temporarily collapses ideological fakery and ontological non-human origin into one emotional field.

D09 must keep them separate.

## 28.2 Borrowed values can still be knowingly shed

Tsukihi's ability to imagine the Fire Sisters ending demonstrates that influence is not possession.

She can say, in effect:

> this role came partly from people I love; I may not carry it forever.

That is already adultward agency.

# 29. Phoenix personhood: origin does not invalidate the life already lived

The `しでの鳥` revelation creates one of the series' most direct challenges to origin-essentialism.

Mechanically, the oddity enters pregnancy through a brood-parasitic model.

Tsukihi's body is immortal/regenerative.

Those facts are literal.

They do not settle personhood by themselves.

## 29.1 Tsukihi has a continuous human social biography

She has:

- memories;
- relationships;
- preferences;
- habits;
- a sibling role;
- friends;
- justice activity;
- reflective capacity;
- bodily continuity as experienced from inside.

Calling the origin non-human does not make those lived facts counterfeit.

## 29.2 The "fake becomes real through striving" formula is insufficient

V05's fake/real rhetoric can tempt a neat solution:

> Tsukihi is fake, but by trying to be human she became real.

That is too simple because Tsukihi is not a conscious counterfeit performing humanity in order to earn it.

She does not know the origin.

A better basis for personhood is:

- lived continuity;
- agency;
- relation;
- practice;
- current subjectivity.

The person does not need to pass an authenticity exam.

## 29.3 Specialist truth does not decide moral worth

Kagenui's diagnosis is mechanically serious.

Her value judgment is separate.

Correctly identifying Tsukihi as an oddity does not automatically prove that extermination is morally required.

This is an early version of the series' specialist-ethics problem.

## 29.4 Araragi recognizes sisterhood and withholds self-knowledge

Araragi's defense is relationally powerful.

Tsukihi is his sister because that is the life they have lived.

Yet he also conceals the truth from her.

This makes the family response ethically mixed.

Love preserves her life.

Love also becomes paternalism.

The D09 rule is:

> **recognizing a person against a reductive ontology does not automatically grant the recognizer the right to control what that person knows about herself.**

## 29.5 Suggestibility is not proof of absent selfhood

Tsukihi can be extremely suggestible.

She can rapidly enter social/genre premises.

That does not establish that she has no self or that phoenix programming writes her personality.

The same person is also:

- strategically observant;
- socially fluent;
- capable of motive reflection;
- capable of ending roles.

# 30. V20 `つきひアンドゥ`: the monitored object becomes the rescuer

This story is narrated by Ononoki, not Tsukihi.

That matters.

The reader sees Tsukihi through the professional assigned to surveil and potentially kill her.

The story then repeatedly embarrasses the professional model.

## 30.1 Tsukihi is being watched during ordinary life

Ononoki has monitored her for nearly half a year.

The absence of obvious abnormal activity becomes data.

Tsukihi is not spending every day performing supernatural threat.

She is living.

## 30.2 The magical-girl lie works because Tsukihi is socially imaginative

Ononoki invents `正義の魔法少女` as a cover after being caught behaving inconsistently with her doll disguise.

Tsukihi accepts the premise and commits to helping.

This is often comic.

It also demonstrates that narratives can become operational because a recipient integrates them into an existing justice/genre schema.

The lie is not harmless.

It causes action.

## 30.3 Tsukihi rescues the person monitoring her

When the fabricated monster exceeds intended scale, Tsukihi pushes Ononoki out of danger.

She is pulverized.

She regenerates.

This reversal is structurally important.

The target of surveillance becomes the rescuer of the surveillance agent.

The relation is no longer cleanly:

> professional subject → dangerous object.

It now includes reciprocal causal obligation.

Ononoki tries to insist gratitude will not affect professional judgment.

Her own narration makes the separation less secure.

## 30.4 Regeneration can externalize cost

Tsukihi's body resets.

The environment and other people do not necessarily reset with her.

The story's formulation:

`周囲にだけ、その失敗が堆積する`

is one of the most important material-body rules in the corpus.

Immortality can make the individual look consequence-free while residue accumulates elsewhere.

This complicates Araragi's earlier family defense without invalidating it.

Tsukihi is a person.

Her body can still create public-risk questions.

## 30.5 Memory repair remains a hypothesis, not transparent law

Tsukihi forgets nearly the entire crisis.

Ononoki theorizes a cognitive reset that removes memory when nonordinary experience becomes incompatible with ordinary life.

The observation is strong.

The full mechanism remains source-attributed.

Do not turn it into a universal phoenix psychology rule.

## 30.6 Tsukihi's independent Nadeko friendship matters

V20 also establishes that Tsukihi visits Nadeko after the god crisis.

The relation is not routed through Araragi.

This is exactly the kind of independent social continuity D09 is designed to preserve.

# 31. Fire Sisters and the limits of the Araragi household perspective

Araragi's narration is affectionate.

It is also controlling.

He routinely frames his sisters through:

- protection;
- ridicule;
- bodily comedy;
- fake/real judgment;
- sibling authority.

The sisters' later independent narration and adult states show why the family perspective cannot be the only character source.

## 31.1 Karen is not simply the sister Araragi must stop from doing reckless justice

V21 gives her an independent philosophical problem:

- what counts as the self;
- how much other people author one's action;
- what assistance means;
- what training can and cannot reveal.

Adult Karen then carries selected continuities into public work.

## 31.2 Tsukihi is not simply the immortal secret Araragi protects

She has:

- her own account of borrowed justice;
- social relationships;
- strategic reputation awareness;
- friendship with Nadeko;
- capacity to save Ononoki;
- capacity to change roles.

The hidden phoenix origin is one fact about her body.

It is not the sole subject of her life.

## 31.3 Sibling comedy should not be promoted into desire doctrine

Supplementary material contains bodily/sexualized sibling comedy.

Karen can react strongly to Araragi's girlfriend relation.

She can fear dental treatment despite combat confidence.

These are useful for voice and domain-specific vulnerability.

They should not be generalized into:

- incest desire;
- consent;
- a single erotic theory of the sibling relation.

Comedy is relationship infrastructure and exaggeration.

It does not become evidentiary immunity or evidentiary absolutism.

## 31.4 Family protection does not equal disclosure

Tsukihi's later behavior remains ordinary partly because family and specialists keep major truths from her.

The arrangement may be protective.

It still creates an epistemic asymmetry.

This unresolved state belongs in the archive.

# 34. Influence without ownership

D04–D08 repeatedly established a project-wide principle:

> causal importance does not create sovereign jurisdiction.

D09 demonstrates the rule across less centralized characters.

## Hachikuji

Araragi helps create the relationship-history version of Hachikuji the reader knows.

He does not own her farewell, return, godhood, work, or later forgiveness judgments.

## Kanbaru

Hitagi, Araragi, Tooe, Numachi, Ougi, grandparents, and athletic culture all contribute to Kanbaru.

None becomes the whole author.

## Oikura

Araragi's failures and later influence matter.

She can call herself `阿良々木派` and still reject his family label.

## Karen

Araragi, teachers, martial arts, Tsukihi, and the Fire Sisters brand all shape her.

She still says the later forward movement is by her own will.

## Tsukihi

Karen and Araragi influence her justice.

A phoenix origin shapes her body.

Specialists monitor her.

None of those facts reduces current Tsukihi to property, copy, or program.

The ensemble makes the rule harder to dismiss as a theme only for the main cast.

# 35. Ordinary adulthood is infrastructural

One of D09's strongest aggregate findings is that later life becomes increasingly material.

The early series can organize characters around:

- curse;
- apparition;
- desire;
- rescue;
- dramatic family crisis.

The later corpus repeatedly asks about:

- housing;
- school;
- public assistance;
- university;
- career;
- professional training;
- municipal work;
- medicine;
- policing;
- legal family recognition;
- property;
- surveillance procedure.

This is not thematic dilution.

It is the series testing whether character development survives outside the crisis form.

## 35.1 Hachikuji: home and work

The shrine becomes `わたしの家`.

Deity work becomes `これはわたしの仕事です`.

The formerly lost child now has both residence and office.

## 35.2 Kanbaru: medicine

Sport history is translated into a professional future without requiring return to the exact lost basketball life.

## 35.3 Oikura: home and civic work

The girl whose home could not be socially read now acquires property and works in local administration.

That is not poetic justice in the simplistic sense.

It is infrastructural authorship.

## 35.4 Karen: institutional justice

The neighborhood justice project eventually intersects with formal policing and competitive judo.

Again, continuity without destiny.

## 35.5 Tsukihi: the refusal to invent an endpoint

The supplied corpus does **not** establish an equally clear adult vocation for Tsukihi.

This asymmetry should be preserved.

A synthesis becomes less reliable when it fills every cell for symmetry.

The absence of a certified adult career is an OPEN state, not a defect in the character.

D09 is also an upstream reconstruction document.

## 36.1 Relationship-conditioned voice must remain explicit

A character model should not extract one canonical sentence generator.

Hachikuji's mock-polite intimacy with Araragi is not identical to her deity-professional mode with a lost child.

Kanbaru's erotic formalism with Araragi is not identical to her adult medical/professional language.

Oikura's private Araragi register is deliberately unlike her public-service register.

Karen's sibling speech is not her police-professional speech, for which the supplied evidence remains thinner.

Tsukihi's sibling register is not identical to her conversations with Nadeko or Ononoki.

This is exactly why D18 and D25 remain necessary.

D09 supplies character-specific constraints.

It does not replace the corpus-wide voice atlas.

# 41. Mature synthesis

D09 exists because the phrase "supporting character" becomes increasingly misleading as Monogatari continues.

At the beginning, Araragi's narration gives other people powerful roles.

Lost child.

Junior.

Class enemy.

Little sister.

Justice sister.

Those roles are not inventions.

They are real relations.

The problem begins when relation is mistaken for biography.

Hachikuji is the cleanest early example.

Araragi meets a girl who appears to need directions.

He helps.

Hitagi helps.

Hanekawa helps.

Oshino's explanation helps.

The city itself constrains the route.

Hachikuji is active inside the event.

Even the first rescue is already distributed.

Then the road comedy forms.

`失礼。噛みました`.

The joke is small enough to look disposable.

It is actually the opposite.

Repeated language makes relation.

A formal girl who keeps "accidentally" saying the wrong name and a boy who keeps responding develop a grammar that could not be produced by plot summary alone.

That is why Route X matters so much.

Araragi can save Hachikuji's life and lose the relationship.

Adult Hachikuji is recognizable.

She is not the same **relational state**.

The series gives a rare ethical test:

would you save somebody if the price of saving her were erasing the version of the relationship you treasure?

Araragi says yes.

`生きていてくれて、ありがとうございました`.

For once, rescue clearly chooses the other person's life over the rescuer's preferred narrative.

Then V12 turns the test around.

Araragi cannot accept Hachikuji's departure.

He wants to remain lost with her.

Decades, if necessary.

His future.

Hitagi.

Family.

Education.

Shinobu's linked existence.

The proposal is emotionally magnificent and ethically grotesque.

Hachikuji answers with adulthood in miniature:

`全部は選べませんよ`.

And the body tells the truth:

she is no longer holding his hand.

He is holding hers.

The relationship became beautiful enough that beauty itself can tempt possession.

So Hachikuji leaves.

Later she returns.

The return does not prove the farewell was fake.

It proves a later life can exist after a real ending.

Even that return is ethically impure because Araragi pulls her from hell before she can consent.

Then the consent event changes.

She accepts deity life.

The shrine becomes home.

`これはわたしの仕事です`.

The lost child now guides lost children.

That symmetry could be unbearably neat if the series stopped there.

It does not.

Hachikuji later becomes one of the people who tells Araragi when **his** caring procedure is wrong.

By V30, the character once positioned as dependent is a process critic on family standing.

That is decentering.

Not independence from Araragi.

Authority to answer him.

Kanbaru's path is different.

Her first problem is not being lost.

It is contradiction.

She loves Hitagi.

She hates Araragi.

She wants speed.

She wants classmates punished.

She trains hard enough that the trained body itself resists some of the violence she wants.

The Monkey's Paw story offers a seductive division:

front.

back.

good desire.

alien consequence.

Rainy Devil makes that division harder to maintain.

But the answer is not:

> violent Kanbaru was the real one.

V10 refuses that kind of cheap revelation.

Kanbaru herself eventually criticizes the language of a separate `暗黒面`.

The harder task is to own contradictory impulses without letting the worst impulse monopolize identity.

That is also why self-punishment becomes inadequate.

`自分を責めることは反省じゃない`.

A guilty person can spend so much energy proving guilt that she never develops a better practice.

Numachi sharpens this.

She can help people for selfish reasons.

She can collect misery.

Relief can still occur.

Monogatari repeatedly denies its characters moral purity in both directions.

The helpful person may be impure.

The guilty person may still deserve a future.

Kanbaru's development then becomes increasingly public without becoming Araragi-shaped.

She can confront Shinobu where Araragi cannot.

She can inherit Tooe's voice without becoming Tooe.

She can discover Rainy Devil residue after her own case has been resolved and understand that personal closure did not clean the world.

She can choose preventive work for strangers.

She can also become a medical student whose athletic history remains useful without dictating a return to basketball.

The woman who was once introduced as the dangerous junior with a crush becomes someone who can call Nadeko **as specialist**, maintain a renewed adult relation with Hitagi, and think about institutional family recognition.

The early role remains true.

It simply becomes too small.

Oikura is the harshest demonstration of that principle because Araragi's role for her is morally flattering to him.

She is the person he failed.

That framing admits guilt.

It still centers him.

V16 dismantles the frame by showing that his earliest failure was not simply lack of kindness.

He lacked a category.

He looked at Oikura's home and saw an abandoned building.

His protected childhood made a certain kind of home socially illegible.

Then the memory structure becomes worse.

Oikura had once stayed in his own home.

He forgot.

She did not cease to have stayed there.

`忘れたからといって、なかったことにはならない`.

This is one of Monogatari's most important anti-protagonist principles.

What the protagonist does not remember is not therefore narratively unreal.

Off Season finally lets Oikura narrate.

And what does she want?

Not revenge.

Not Araragi.

A home.

`家に住みたかった`.

An ordinary life.

`目指せ普通`.

These desires sound modest until one remembers her biography.

Ordinary life is not a personality trait she failed to have.

It is infrastructure.

Housing.

School.

Money.

Public support.

A phone.

A friend.

A future.

She tries to engineer it too aggressively.

That is Oikura.

She is smart enough to turn friendship into a constrained optimization problem and suspicious enough to overrun the very boundaries she is trying to respect.

The first-person narrator does not reveal a secretly gentle person.

It reveals why abrasive intelligence is both adaptation and obstacle.

Then she says the line that may define mature corrigibility better than almost anything else in the project:

`間違おうと思って間違えたことなんてない`.

Nobody intends to be wrong while using the model that currently feels right.

The only durable answer is not perfect proof.

It is the ability to revise after the world refuses the proof.

Araragi remains in her life even when absent.

`阿良々木派よ`.

The line is funny because she still hates him.

That is exactly why it matters.

Influence no longer requires emotional surrender.

She can steal a useful ethical posture from someone she resents.

That may be more mature than reconciliation.

By V23, Oikura has a job, a home, accounting competence, and a plan to transform the meaning of the old house.

She is still rude.

Good.

If the series had softened her into gratitude, it would have made recovery another personality correction imposed from outside.

Instead she acquires a professional register **in addition** to the abrasive one.

She can help Araragi and Hitagi.

She can tell him not to sacrifice his career unilaterally.

She can resume contact.

And when he later calls her quasi-family, she can say no.

That no is one of the most successful relationship outcomes in the series.

History can be close without becoming kin.

Araragi does not get to rename it.

The Fire Sisters make the same point inside the household.

Karen and Tsukihi begin as a unit.

That unit is real.

Their justice work has a name, reputation, social field, and shared history.

But V06 already shows that the sisters have different theories of what they are doing.

Karen's justice is a goal.

Tsukihi can call hers a hobby.

She can see how much of it comes from Karen and Araragi.

She can imagine the unit ending.

This happens **before** the phoenix reveal.

That ordering is crucial.

Tsukihi is already a reflective person before the reader learns her body has a non-human origin.

The reveal therefore cannot logically explain away the prior personhood.

The `しでの鳥` is real.

The regeneration is real.

The brood-parasitic origin is real.

So are years of siblinghood, friendship, speech, preference, justice play, embarrassment, social knowledge, and choice.

The specialist can be right about ontology and wrong to imagine ontology answers the moral question.

Araragi understands that.

Partly.

He insists Tsukihi is his sister.

Then he withholds the truth from her.

Family recognition and paternalism arrive together.

This is exactly the kind of mixed ethical state the series gets better at preserving over time.

Love can be right about personhood and still wrong about jurisdiction.

V20 makes the monitored sister even harder to classify.

Ononoki watches Tsukihi as a possible target.

Then Tsukihi saves her.

The object of professional surveillance becomes the rescuer.

The body is destroyed and regenerates.

The cost does not disappear.

It moves outward.

`周囲にだけ、その失敗が堆積する`.

This is an extraordinary late correction to immortality.

A body that resets can conceal externalized consequence.

The immortal person's lack of scar is not evidence that nothing happened.

Again:

forgotten does not mean unreal.

Healed does not mean costless.

Karen receives her own first-person correction in V21.

The mountain-training story appears ready to reveal the true warrior through deprivation.

Karen rejects the premise.

The starving, exhausted cliff-hanging girl is not more authentic than the girl who eats, studies, trains, jokes, argues with siblings, and receives help.

`己のありようも、一通りではない`.

This is the Fire Sister version of the same architecture that D07 found in Hanekawa and D08 found in Nadeko.

Plural selfhood is not a special solution for one psychologically fragmented character.

It becomes a series-wide account of personhood.

Even assistance receives a correction.

Araragi and Shinobu help Karen.

That does not make her later action fake.

`そこから先は、自分の意志で、前に進んだ`.

The series keeps returning to this boundary because its slogan about self-rescue is easy to misunderstand.

Self-authorship does not mean causal isolation.

People are helped.

Fed.

Housed.

Carried.

Taught.

Corrected.

Introduced.

Protected.

Sometimes coerced.

What matters is what standing those interventions create afterward.

Usually less than the helper imagines.

By V30, Karen's justice has become police work and judo excellence.

Kanbaru's athletics have become medical continuity.

Oikura's broken-home history coexists with municipal work and owned property.

Hachikuji's lostness has become a deity office helping lost children.

Tsukihi's future is less fully specified, and the corpus is better for leaving it that way than for forcing a symmetrical achievement chart.

These are not reward endings.

They are proofs of narrative surplus.

A person once introduced through one crisis can continue after the crisis stops being the most interesting thing about her.

That is what D09 adds to the specialist architecture.

The major monographs D04–D08 show how Araragi, Hitagi, Shinobu, Hanekawa, and Nadeko become increasingly difficult to reduce to their introductory roles.

D09 shows that the principle is not reserved for protagonists or the most philosophically obvious characters.

It reaches the ensemble.

The final formulation is therefore:

> **Monogatari's decentered lives are not lives that become independent of relationship. They are lives that become too large for any one relationship to function as their final description.**

Araragi can matter without owning.

Family can matter without naming.

A shared unit can matter without fusing.

A parent can matter without becoming destiny.

A wound can matter without becoming vocation.

A role can end without becoming fake.

A farewell can remain real after return.

A person can continue.

The architecture-defined next artifact is:

**`10_KAIKI_OSHINO_GAEN_KAGENUI_AND_SPECIALIST_ETHICS.md`**

D10 should inherit D03's oddity/metaphysical source discipline, D04's intervention audit, D06's creator/origin non-sovereignty, D08's professional-motive and means distinction, and D09's evidence that specialist authority is only one form of situated competence among many.

In particular, D10 must preserve:

- Meme's balancing/non-heroic posture and the limits of neutrality;
- Kaiki's fraud, epistemic usefulness, coercion, paid motive, and anti-purity value;
- Gaen's extraordinary information advantage without omniscient moral jurisdiction;
- Kagenui's categorical justice and later evidence of revision/exception;
- Ononoki as created professional whose tool self-story does not exhaust her agency;
- knowledge versus authority;
- diagnosis versus justified intervention;
- payment, contract, institutional role, and conflict of interest;
- surveillance and secrecy;
- creator/specialist responsibility without ownership;
- when a specialist should refuse, hand off, supervise, or accept a non-model solution;
- D25 reconstruction-relevant voice and relationship modes for Kaiki, Meme, Gaen, Kagenui, and Ononoki where architecture requires them.

The transition is deliberate:

> **D09 shows people increasingly surviving the roles through which Araragi and the reader first knew them. D10 can now examine the professionals whose occupation is precisely to classify, explain, and intervene in other people's crises—and ask when expertise itself becomes another role that mistakes explanatory access for jurisdiction.**
