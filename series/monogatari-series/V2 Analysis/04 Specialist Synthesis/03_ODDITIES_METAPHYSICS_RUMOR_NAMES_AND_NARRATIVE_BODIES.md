# 〈物語〉シリーズ V2 — Oddities, Metaphysics, Rumor, Names, and Narrative Bodies

## Literal monsters and the stories that give them form

## 0. Purpose, governing question, and method

This document creates the V2 metaphysical/oddity reference for the supplied corpus.

Its governing question is:

> **What kind of reality do oddities possess, and how do names, belief, rumor, folklore, human contradiction, specialist intervention, bodies, places, and social systems jointly shape that reality?**

The answer cannot be reduced to either of the two most tempting master theories.

The first is the **psychological-allegory reduction**:

> oddities are merely metaphors for trauma, desire, repression, adolescence, or interpersonal conflict.

That fails immediately. Kiss-shot is an autonomous ancient predator/person with material appetite and bodily power. Nadeko's V02 curse is externally sent. The V28 vampire pandemic kills vampires regardless of whether a particular victim has the "right" psychological conflict. Araragi's and Shinobu's altered bodies obey conditions that are not exhausted by symbolism.

The second is the **hard bestiary reduction**:

> oddities are simply a catalog of supernatural species with stable mechanics independent of story, language, human expectation, social role, and interpretation.

That also fails immediately. Hitagi's case operates through the semantic cluster of `重さ / 重み / 思い / 記憶`. Hachikuji is both person and `迷い牛` function. Rainy Devil contract logic depends on contradictory wish structure. A fabricated `囲い火蜂` genealogy can coexist with bodily effect. Nadeko's invented Kuchinawa story becomes the path by which she reaches a real talisman and actual apotheosis. Names and social recognition affect Ougi and Hachikuji. Nadeko's drawings can become causal material. A rumor can alter conduct even when no oddity exists.

The V2 corpus therefore requires a third model:

> **Oddities are literally causal but ontologically heterogeneous. Their reality may be bodily, contractual, relational, ecological, linguistic, representational, institutional, inherited, self-generated, or some combination of these. Human meaning can participate in supernatural causation without becoming the sole cause of every supernatural event.**

D02 established the epistemic discipline required for this document. Specialist statements remain attributed. Narrator analogy remains analogy unless the case promotes it. Later explanations may revise present reader understanding without rewriting earlier knowledge states. A correct outcome does not retroactively validate an incorrect proof.

Accordingly, D03 separates:

- **literal mechanic** — what the source demonstrates the phenomenon can do;
- **human conflict** — desire, trauma, contradiction, need, or relationship surrounding the case;
- **social substrate** — rumor, family, market, institution, shrine, law, role, infrastructure;
- **name/representation layer** — words, stories, images, titles, categories, and recognition;
- **specialist model** — an in-story explanation with a speaker and evidence horizon;
- **intervention** — what is done to alter the condition;
- **residue** — what remains after the event appears "resolved."

This structure is not decorative. It prevents the central Monogatari metaphysical error:

> **confusing a compelling interpretation with a complete ontology.**

# 1. The first rule: there is no single oddity mechanism

V01 already destroys the possibility of a one-rule cosmology.

Hitagi and Hachikuji are both "oddity cases," yet almost every structural feature differs.

## 1.1 Hitagi: person affected by a burden-responsive oddity

Hitagi retains an ordinary-looking body but nearly all expected physical weight is absent.

The case is tied to:

- severe illness;
- maternal religious fixation;
- cult exploitation;
- attempted sexual assault;
- family collapse;
- Hitagi's wish to lose `重み / 思い / 記憶 / しがらみ`.

The literal supernatural condition and the semantic field are tightly linked.

Yet the resolution does not restore the lost family or erase what happened.

Hitagi asks to carry her own weight again.

The first rule opened by the series is therefore already double:

> **meaning matters, and meaning is not sufficient to undo history.**

## 1.2 Hachikuji: the person is also the mechanism

Hachikuji is not an ordinary living person suffering from an attached oddity.

She is the dead child and the `迷い牛` configuration.

The observer condition matters: people who do not want to go home can perceive and become entangled with her. Hitagi initially cannot.

Material urban change matters: newly constructed roads can alter the ghost's practical navigation field.

Relationship matters: disengaging may allow the living person to go home, but ethical resolution requires taking seriously Hachikuji's own goal.

The Mother's Day arrival removes one lost condition while leaving death and personhood intact.

The next day Hachikuji continues as an altered floating ghost.

This establishes another fundamental rule:

> **an oddity can be person, mechanism, memory, and relationship at the same time.**

## 1.3 Heterogeneity is not an exception; it becomes the system

Across V01–V30 the corpus adds:

- separate oddity transactions;
- devils with contract logic;
- externally sent curses;
- hybrid person/oddity configurations;
- autonomous vampire species/persons;
- reincarnating oddity-persons;
- self-generated affective bodies;
- ghosts who persist beyond original function;
- gods tied to site and recognition;
- impersonal corrective law;
- constructed shikigami/persons;
- environmental accumulation fields;
- route-dependent supernatural ecologies;
- social rumors with no supernatural mechanism;
- real curse commodity chains;
- representational creation;
- disease-like supernatural systems;
- negative cases where the professional conclusion is ordinary nature.

The metaphysics is therefore best understood not as one ontology but as a **family of causal regimes**.

The series' unifying property is not one mechanism.

It is the repeated requirement to ask:

> **what kind of thing is this case, what kind of access do we have to it, and which level of explanation is actually doing the causal work?**

# 2. A practical V2 classification of oddity reality

The following classification is analytical infrastructure rather than a claim that the series itself supplies one official taxonomy.

A single case may occupy more than one class.

## 2.1 Autonomous oddity-persons

These possess agency not reducible to the immediate human host.

Examples include:

- Kiss-shot / Shinobu;
- Suicide-Master;
- Hachikuji after death;
- Numachi's posthumous continuation;
- some created persons after individuation;
- Ougi after emergence, despite self-derived origin.

Their actions can still be shaped by:

- human recognition;
- contract;
- naming;
- site;
- hunger;
- role;
- history.

Autonomy therefore does not mean independence from conditions.

It means the case cannot be explained as a mere symptom inside someone else's psyche.

## 2.2 Attached, contracted, or externally imposed mechanisms

Examples include:

- Hitagi's weight-crab/god complex;
- Rainy Devil contract;
- Nadeko's V02 snake curse;
- `囲い火蜂`;
- later curse-service cases.

Here a human subject and the mechanism remain meaningfully distinguishable.

This class is essential because it blocks the theory that afflicted persons always create their own oddities.

V02 Nadeko is a literal victim of externally directed occult harm.

Later Nadeko agency does not retroactively change that.

## 2.3 Hybrid person/oddity configurations

Examples include:

- Black Hanekawa;
- God Nadeko after talisman ingestion;
- Araragi's partial vampire state;
- first-thrall composite body;
- Ougi as Araragi-derived but independent spirit;
- some later constructed or curse-altered persons.

Hybrid means responsibility must be partitioned rather than transferred wholesale.

Black Hanekawa is neither:

- pure external possession;
- the one "true Hanekawa";
- a morally independent stranger with no Hanekawa-linked agency.

The configuration includes:

`Hanekawa pressure + oddity catalyst + shared knowledge + distinct operational strategy`.

## 2.4 Coextensive oddity-persons

Tsukihi is the cleanest example.

The `しでの鳥` is not an illness attached to a pre-existing ordinary Tsukihi who could simply be "cured."

The socially continuous Tsukihi and the incarnation are coextensive from birth.

This changes the ethics of diagnosis.

A specialist can correctly identify an oddity while still being wrong to infer that exterminating the oddity leaves the "real child" behind.

There is no demonstrated separable patient.

## 2.5 Self-generated oddities and represented selves

Examples include:

- Hanekawa's `苛虎`;
- Nadeko's fabricated Kuchinawa persona before real apotheosis;
- Ougi's self-critical derivation;
- Nadeko's V22 drawn self-agents;
- Uroko's own account of counter-self/daughter formation.

"Self-generated" must not be translated into "fully intentional."

Hanekawa does not consciously choose every feature of the tiger.

Nadeko's drawings disobey her.

Ougi emerges from Araragi-related conditions and becomes an independent mental/spiritual entity.

Self-origin does not erase alterity.

## 2.6 Environmental and infrastructural supernatural fields

Examples include:

- the ruined shrine accumulation field;
- Kita-Shirahebi `エアスポット`;
- shrine talisman infrastructure;
- deity-domain management;
- black-mirror containment;
- public oddity cleanup.

These cases show that supernatural causation can be spatial and ecological.

A person need not be the sole source.

A town can contain:

- accumulation;
- neglected religious infrastructure;
- residue;
- specialist interventions;
- sites that gather `よくないもの`;
- materially changed routes that alter ghost navigation.

## 2.7 Law-like or command-like phenomena

The real `くらやみ` and V27 `妖魔令` are not ordinary monster organisms.

The darkness is described as impersonal correction.

`妖魔令` is law/edict-like and can compel apology/self-punishment while no longer being continuously puppeteered by the human substrate.

These cases require analysis of **rule** rather than creature psychology.

## 2.8 Fictional, fabricated, or socially real constructions

This class includes several importantly different cases:

- false oddity genealogy with bodily effect;
- fake oddity intentionally built into an intervention;
- social rumor with no supernatural mechanism;
- guardian-tree fiction with real protective effect;
- Nadeko's drawing representations that become materially causal.

"Fake" therefore has no single ontological consequence.

A fake history can support a real mechanism.

A fake monster can be physically dangerous.

A socially believed story can alter behavior without summoning anything.

A representation can become a supernatural object under specific demonstrated conditions.

## 2.9 Negative supernatural cases

Professional metaphysics must include the conclusion:

> **no oddity is required here.**

Examples include:

- many V15 calendar mysteries;
- V20 Oikura's institutional crisis;
- V23 uniform-slasher rumor;
- V30 Nasu killstone.

A mature oddity system is not a doctrine that everything strange is supernatural.

It contains procedures for refusing the supernatural explanation.

# 3. Naming: classification, relation, power, and the refusal of one "true name" theory

Names matter throughout the series.

The mistake is to turn that observation into a universal "true name = essence" rule.

The supplied corpus supports several distinct name functions.

## 3.1 Descriptive names

`くらやみ` is deliberately ordinary.

Gaen emphasizes that the phenomenon lacks an authorized proper name. Human labels such as:

- `くらやみ`;
- `神隠し`;
- `バランサー`;
- `イレイザー`;
- `ブラックホール`;

are explanatory handles.

The name helps thought.

It does not create an ordinary creature-species.

Likewise `人間病` in V28 is an in-story disease-name/model, not a guaranteed objective biomedical taxon.

## 3.2 Specialist taxonomic names

Names such as:

- `迷い牛`;
- `障り猫`;
- `雨魔` / Rainy Devil;
- `しでの鳥`;
- `うつろいねじり`;
- `妖魔令`;

locate a case within lore or specialist language.

Yet taxonomic naming does not settle:

- ethical treatment;
- individual motive;
- universal mechanics;
- personhood.

Kagenui can classify Tsukihi correctly and still face a separate normative question about extermination.

## 3.3 Semantic scaffolds

The series repeatedly makes word-fields analytically and sometimes causally significant.

Hitagi's case operates through:

`重さ / 重み / 思い / 記憶 / しがらみ`.

Hanekawa recognizes jealousy through `焼きもちを焼く`, while `苛虎` links multiple interpretive associations rather than one exhaustive etymology.

Hachikuji's `迷い牛` carries lostness more importantly than literal bovine morphology.

`心渡 / 夢渡` encode a paired kill/revive boundary technology.

The proper rule is:

> **semantic resonance can be part of the mechanism, recognition, or interpretive structure without proving that wordplay alone creates the phenomenon.**

## 3.4 Relational and socially conferred names

Ougi's V18 continuity depends on naming and relation.

Gaen routes `忍野` through `忍野忍`, and Meme's `僕の可愛い姪っ子` supplies a socially valid relation under which Ougi can continue.

This is not a revelation of hidden biological genealogy.

It demonstrates something subtler:

> **socially recognized relation can confer ontological legitimacy without becoming retroactive biological truth.**

Hachikuji's deity role works similarly.

The local god position gives her supernatural `市民権 / 住民票`.

The role is offered and chosen.

It is not uncovered as destiny.

## 3.5 Names as restriction, seal, or power relation

V30 explicitly has Shinobu state that oddity name and power can be closely related.

The broader corpus gives several distinct name/power relationships:

- inherited names;
- specialist-assigned categories;
- reduced or renamed identities;
- social-family names;
- legal names;
- protective aliases;
- relational address;
- names that make role contradiction legible.

The project must resist merging these into a single mystical "true name" law.

## 3.6 Public/legal names do not exhaust personhood

V30 expands the naming problem outside supernatural mechanics.

Marriage raises:

- `阿良々木ひたぎ`;
- counterfactual `戦場ヶ原暦`.

Witness protection then withholds/replaces public names.

Family continuity survives.

This matters to D03 because it demonstrates that the series' interest in names is broader than spell mechanics.

Names can structure:

- legal standing;
- public identity;
- relation;
- concealment;
- belonging;
- supernatural role.

Powerful does not mean exhaustive.

## 3.7 Translation caution: imported labels can distort ontology

`つきひフェニックス` is the strongest example.

The title encourages the grand Western "phoenix" image, but Shinobu pushes the case toward `しでの鳥` / hototogisu-associated local language.

The translation problem is metaphysical.

If every term is flattened into a familiar imported species, the reader may imagine a cleaner bestiary than the Japanese text supports.

D03 therefore adopts the L08 rule:

> **diegetic etymology and translation convenience are not ontological proof.**

# 4. Rumor, belief, fiction, and causal efficacy

One of Monogatari's most distinctive metaphysical patterns is that **false history and real consequence can coexist**.

This must be stated carefully.

The corpus does not support an unrestricted principle that belief creates reality.

It supports several narrower mechanisms.

## 4.1 `囲い火蜂`: fake genealogy, real symptom

V05's bee case is foundational.

Shinobu recalls lore connecting the hornet to a historical epidemic.

Kaiki calls that history `偽史`.

Yet Karen's fever is materially real.

Araragi's correct response is anti-genealogical:

> the origin story can be false without making the present condition unreal.

This yields D03's first fiction rule:

> **genealogical falsity does not entail causal falsity.**

## 4.2 Social rumor can matter even before the supernatural arrives

Kaiki's fake charms damage relationships whether or not every user successfully invokes a supernatural mechanism.

This means rumor can create:

- fear;
- expectation;
- retaliation;
- social isolation;
- occult experimentation;
- markets.

The social field is not a disposable preface to the "real" monster.

It is one causal layer.

## 4.3 Nadeko: fabricated story becomes a path to actual godhood

V11 is the sharpest case.

Before talisman ingestion:

- the revived snake god is not independently present;
- the apparent mission is fabricated/hallucinatory narrative;
- the rough `俺様` voice participates in self-performance.

Then Nadeko finds and eats a real Gaen-linked talisman.

Actual deity power follows.

The chain is:

`fabricated god-story → behavior organized by story → real talisman → chosen ingestion → real god-state`.

The correct conclusion is not:

> "Kuchinawa was secretly real all along."

It is:

> **a false story can become causal infrastructure.**

This is one of the series' central metaphysical/ethical bridges.

Narrative can organize action toward material supernatural conditions.

## 4.4 `蛞蝓豆腐`: intentionally fake oddity, operational result

Kaiki explicitly calls the V13 slug-tofu oddity `でっち上げられた、偽物の怪異`.

It still functions under the case's symbolic/material balance.

This again blocks binary ontology.

"Fake" can describe provenance while the present mechanism is operative.

## 4.5 V15: useful fiction without supernatural instantiation

V15 broadens the pattern beyond literal oddities.

A fabricated guardian-tree history can redirect fear toward `畏敬`.

A nonexistent Seed object can organize preventive behavior.

A fictional death-marker can alter rooftop conduct.

The tea "eighth member" story can persist after factual/logical disproof because its social function differs from a literal ghost claim.

This produces a crucial distinction:

> **causal fiction does not require supernatural embodiment.**

## 4.6 V23: rumor can imitate oddity grammar while remaining human

The uniform-slasher case looks supernatural.

The damage is actually produced by student harassment inside school.

Mirrors reveal backs; they do not attack.

The rumor still has operational effects.

This is one of the strongest anti-overnaturalization cases in the entire corpus:

> **oddity-shaped social reality can exist without an oddity.**

## 4.7 V27: rumor becomes commodity chain

The snake-curse network adds an economic layer.

Kaiki describes himself as a `小売店`.

Curse material is:

- acquired;
- forged/cheapened;
- distributed;
- used by ordinary people;
- traced upstream toward a real curse provider.

This is neither pure belief nor pure autonomous monster activity.

It is a **supply chain** in which supernatural mechanism is embedded in ordinary market and social incentives.

The ontological lesson is also a responsibility lesson:

> tracing a curse upstream does not erase the agency of the person who used it downstream.

## 4.8 Belief and recognition have bounded authority

Worship strengthens or stabilizes god roles.

Social recognition matters to Ougi.

Nadeko's acceptance matters to the fake oddity intervention.

Yet belief is never shown to be an unrestricted universal engine capable of creating any reality whatsoever.

The V2 lock is:

> **belief, acceptance, rumor, or recognition may be necessary or amplifying variables in some cases; they are not sufficient master causes for all oddities.**

# 5. Bodies: symptom, substrate, archive, interface, and residue

Oddity metaphysics in Monogatari is relentlessly bodily.

The body is not simply where metaphor is displayed.

It is often where causal claims become testable.

## 5.1 Missing, altered, or excessive body as diagnostic evidence

Examples include:

- Hitagi's near-zero weight;
- Kanbaru's beast arm;
- Nadeko's constriction marks;
- Black Hanekawa's hair/ears/body;
- Araragi's lost mirror reflection and regeneration;
- Tsukihi's impossible healing;
- vampire age/power shifts;
- first-thrall composite reconstruction;
- Uroko's curse scales.

These bodies create hard constraints on interpretation.

A theory that cannot account for the body is incomplete.

## 5.2 Body history does not equal identity essence

The series repeatedly refuses the inference:

> if the body reveals the supernatural truth, it reveals the whole person.

Tsukihi's immortal body changes the explanatory field.

It does not prove conscious fraud.

Ononoki's constructed body does not make her a nonperson.

Kujaku's adult-looking transformed body does not make her an adult in biography, memory, or social development.

Araragi's vampire body does not settle every moral question about whether he is human.

V23 adults whose bodies permanently carry mermaid, clay, or werewolf histories are not merely patients waiting to return to an original baseline.

D03 therefore uses:

> **body-state truth ≠ personhood totality.**

## 5.3 The body can be a supernatural archive

Some conditions persist after apparent resolution.

Araragi's partial vampirism remains.

Kanbaru's devil material persists ecologically even after her arm is restored.

Hanekawa retains striped/white hair after tiger integration.

Tsukihi's body restores itself while surrounding people retain the memory/consequence.

The first thrall can reconstitute through vampire residue.

V21 shows old Hanekawa vampire blood remaining materially activatable.

Bodies carry history without dictating one meaning of that history.

## 5.4 Constructed bodies are interfaces for relation

V14's statement about Ononoki is especially important:

her human-shaped body exists neither simply `人であるため` nor `人になるため` but `人といるため`.

The body is relational interface.

This creates a third category beyond:

- biologically human;
- deceptive imitation.

A constructed human form can be a practical medium for social existence.

## 5.5 Severe regeneration can externalize residue

V20's Tsukihi case opens one of the most important body rules.

Tsukihi can return to baseline after catastrophic bodily destruction while losing memory of the incident.

Ononoki's formulation suggests:

`周囲にだけ、その失敗が堆積する`.

This means resilience can move cost outward.

The subject's restored body does not imply restored system.

Consequences can accumulate in:

- family members;
- secrets;
- records;
- institutions;
- environments.

## 5.6 Representation can become body

V20, V22, V25, and V29 progressively expand Nadeko's art into causal material.

But the functions must remain distinguished.

V29 requires at least four:

1. expression;
2. instantiation;
3. reconstruction;
4. externalization.

The project must not compress these into:

> "Nadeko can draw anything into reality."

The observed rules depend on:

- object;
- substrate;
- prior relation;
- memory;
- design;
- oddity context.

The edible-looking doughnut that is not functionally the original is a critical limit case.

Representation can produce body without guaranteeing perfect ontology.

## 5.7 Bodily cure can redistribute vulnerability

V28 is the strongest disease/body example.

Suicide-Master's near-humanization protects against the vampire-targeting condition.

It also makes ordinary human COVID-19 lethal.

The series refuses a cure fantasy in which one body-state is simply invulnerable.

The rule is:

> **ontological change redistributes vulnerability rather than abolishing it.**

# 6. Gods, shrines, place, and supernatural infrastructure

The shrine is one of the corpus's clearest demonstrations that oddity metaphysics is not merely personal psychology.

## 6.1 Ruined shrine as accumulation field

V02 establishes the ruined site as a former spiritual center that now accumulates `よくないもの`.

Oshino describes the field cautiously.

He prefers redistribution over dramatic cleansing because creating a vacuum may draw worse phenomena elsewhere.

He also suggests strong specialist presence can itself stimulate the field.

Even at this early stage, supernatural intervention has ecological consequences.

## 6.2 Nadeko's godhood is psychological, material, spatial, and institutional

V11 links the god-state to:

- real talisman;
- chosen ingestion;
- shrine site;
- worship;
- municipal rebuilding;
- public religious recognition.

Nadeko's psychology matters.

It is not the whole mechanism.

The shrine is not merely a metaphor for her narcissism.

It is physical and social infrastructure.

## 6.3 Kiss-shot and the danger of role mismatch

V12's ancient history shows Kiss-shot receiving worship and producing a real miracle-like rain effect.

She performs a god role without intending a permanent category transformation.

Gaen's model ties the darkness response to this mismatch.

The critical rule is:

> **social recognition can make a role causally consequential without completing ontological transformation.**

## 6.4 Hachikuji: supernatural citizenship rather than recovered essence

Hachikuji's deity role solves a category problem.

She had genuinely changed after Mother's Day.

Continuing indefinitely as `迷い牛` created mismatch.

The later shrine role gives a legitimate place to continue.

This is not the discovery that she was "always really a god."

It is an offered future category.

The language `市民権 / 住民票` is decisive.

Metaphysics is expressed through civic legitimacy.

## 6.5 `エアスポット`: prevention rather than case-by-case rescue

V17 expands the shrine into ecological infrastructure.

Gaen describes the site as a place that gathers pre-oddity material.

Properly managed, this is preventive.

The first thrall's residue overloads the system.

Crucially, Gaen explicitly rejects a master-cause theory.

The ash may raise probability modestly.

It did not create every local oddity.

The field model therefore introduces:

> **background causal amplification without causal monopoly.**

## 6.6 V19: shrine as containment and governance

The black mirror routes supernatural remainder toward Kita-Shirahebi Shrine.

Hachikuji's deity role becomes operational public infrastructure.

The site has progressed from:

- ruin/amplifier;
- god seat;
- managed accumulation field;
- chosen home/office;
- remainder containment.

The supernatural world therefore acquires governance history.

## 6.7 V25: deity jurisdiction has limits

Hachikuji can draw a genuinely lost child toward the shrine.

Ononoki immediately raises a jurisdiction question.

Different gods need not have identical remit.

This is a mature institutional development:

> **having supernatural capacity does not prove unlimited supernatural authority.**

## 6.8 V30: local miracle, exported weather cost

Shinobu uses `祢々切丸` to cut clouds and produce desired stars.

The local outcome is beautiful and successful.

Gaen warns that atmospheric burden may have shifted elsewhere, potentially producing heavy rain or landslide.

The metaphysical point is inseparable from physical infrastructure:

> **local supernatural success can export cost through a shared material system.**

The series' late oddity ecology is therefore not insulated fantasy.

It interacts with weather, cities, schools, hospitals, markets, police, roads, and households.

# 7. Vampires: autonomous supernatural persons, predation, dependence, and altered vulnerability

Vampires are the strongest evidence against reducing oddities to internalized psychology.

## 7.1 V04: literal predator ontology

Kiss-shot has:

- blood dependence;
- regeneration;
- extreme physical power;
- sunlight vulnerability;
- variable body age/power;
- thrall-making capacity;
- master/thrall relation.

Human beings are materially legible as food.

Araragi's psychological and ethical relationship with her becomes profound.

That does not make the predation figurative.

## 7.2 Thrall-making is distinct from feeding

V04 establishes that Kiss-shot does not simply feed on Araragi.

She makes him a thrall.

Oshino interprets this as a life-preserving action that also delays her own recovery.

The distinction matters because it produces a durable relation:

- Araragi lives;
- his body changes;
- Kiss-shot later depends on him;
- both lose the simple possibility of returning to the pre-contact state.

## 7.3 Resolution by engineered incapacity

The V04 settlement is not a cure.

Kiss-shot is prevented from ordinary predation largely by being stripped of capacity.

Araragi remains partly vampiric.

They become mutually dependent.

The case therefore introduces a late-series theme very early:

> **risk can be managed by limiting capacity rather than by restoring an imagined original purity.**

## 7.4 Route dependence

V09 proves that supernatural ecology is historically contingent.

Different choices produce:

- different shrine states;
- different Kiss-shot outcomes;
- mass thralling/zombie-like residue;
- different energy conditions.

Route X must not be merged into the main route.

It demonstrates that oddity rules operate inside history rather than outside it.

## 7.5 The first thrall: immortality without romanticized intention

V12/V17 show that vampire-derived immortality can preserve/reconstruct a body across centuries without guaranteeing coherent will.

The first thrall's return is not reducible to four centuries of romantic purpose.

His later body is composite.

Talisman interaction can strip that body toward accumulated supernatural material.

Shinobu's final consumption ends the recurrence cycle.

The text explicitly separates:

- ending;
- reconciliation;
- salvation.

## 7.6 Vampire governance becomes institutional

V21 and later volumes reveal vampire relations beyond one master/thrall pair.

Dramaturgy's organization regulates threat rather than simply exterminating a species.

Twin vampires demonstrate autonomous relations outside the classic sire structure.

This broadens vampire ontology into social and institutional ecology.

## 7.7 Hunger, selective appetite, and V24 mummification

Suicide-Master's post-Acerola food selectivity becomes life-threatening.

V24 demonstrates:

- living mummification through severe draining/failed vampirization;
- successful vampirization in Kie's local case;
- starvation-linked age regression;
- treatment constraints;
- restoration methods that cannot be copied without risk.

The case rejects universal treatment inference.

A remedy safe for one vampire may complete vampirization in a human victim.

## 7.8 V28 pandemic and failed humanization

V28 initially uses pandemic/virus vocabulary phenomenologically.

Later reconstruction suggests a supernatural process tied to:

- Acerola's old curse;
- Tropica residual will;
- Suicide-Master;
- reunion conditions.

This remains `CD/SI`, not omniscient fact.

The V24 mummy analogy is tested and fails in straightforward form.

This is crucial.

The series allows a narrator to propose a plausible supernatural analogy and then physically disconfirm it.

`人間病` reframes the process as failed humanization rather than simple murder.

Again, the name is a model, not the whole mechanism.

## 7.9 V30: domestic integration does not erase predation history

Shinobu's current non-predation and changed conduct matter.

They do not rewrite `人を食う`.

Family inclusion can be ethically meaningful while historical predation remains part of the risk/accountability record.

Vampire metaphysics therefore cannot be detached from governance.

Power, appetite, name, body, family, oversight, and public externality remain connected.

# 8. Devils, curses, and outsourced harm

Curse cases are the best evidence that Monogatari distributes agency across human and supernatural layers.

## 8.1 Rainy Devil: the hidden wish is real but not the whole person

Kanbaru's contract preferentially realizes the violent "back side" of a wish.

Yet the back side must remain compatible with the surface wish.

The resolution works by making the contract conditions mutually incompatible.

This rejects two opposite moral simplifications:

- Kanbaru is innocent because the devil did it;
- violent impulse is her sole true self because the devil revealed it.

The correct structure is hybrid responsibility.

## 8.2 Nadeko's V02 curse: external malice is literal

The snake curse is sent by peers through occult practice.

Nadeko's marks are bodily.

Multiple curse manifestations are present.

Cleansing her does not close the case because detached curse material may return toward the sender.

The case establishes:

> **victim release and causal closure are different things.**

## 8.3 Amateur ritual can have disproportionate consequence

V02 is an early warning that human intention and supernatural scale can diverge.

A cheap or poorly understood ritual can produce effects beyond what the user comprehends.

Later volumes industrialize this problem.

## 8.4 V26: real curse mechanism embedded in real-estate economics

The apartment `蛇切縄` service is especially important.

Repeated hanging attempts share physical marks.

The curse is used instrumentally to stigmatize property.

The client motive is economic.

The lethal mechanism is outsourced to a real curse provider.

Kaiki is explicitly excluded because the relevant operation is not his kind of fake.

This demonstrates:

> **ordinary incentive can purchase literal supernatural violence.**

No psychological allegory can replace the economic layer.

No supernatural explanation can replace the human motive.

## 8.5 V27: the curse supply chain

Old Nadeko curse residue returns in former classmates.

Kaiki describes himself as retailer.

The chain runs upstream toward `洗人`.

Responsibility becomes distributed among:

- provider;
- distributor;
- user;
- institutional context;
- victim.

Tracing the source does not absolve downstream choice.

## 8.6 Uroko: curse work can leave bodily professional residue

V29 shows Uroko's scale-covered limbs.

She interprets this as accumulated backlash from years of curse work.

The body state is observed.

The accounting law remains her claim.

Nadeko's later externalization removes the scales.

Recovery does not erase:

- prior victims;
- professional history;
- culpability.

This repeats D03's residue rule at occupational scale.

# 9. Apparitions, ghosts, and role ontology

Ghost stories in Monogatari are rarely simple "unfinished business" formulas.

They force a distinction between:

- person;
- death;
- supernatural category;
- continuing social role.

## 9.1 Hachikuji: resolution changes category without ending relation

Mother's Day resolves the lost destination.

Death remains.

Hachikuji returns.

Later, the darkness targets the mismatch between her continued `迷い牛` category and the fact that she no longer wants to make people lost.

This is one of the clearest demonstrations that:

> **metaphysical category can become causally dangerous without exhausting the person's ethical identity.**

## 9.2 Numachi: a dead person may not know she is dead

V10's Numachi case introduces ontological self-ignorance.

Her posthumous identity preserves a coping practice organized around collecting misfortune and devil parts.

Human relational completion and supernatural disappearance coincide.

Exact causal metaphysics remain unresolved.

The series therefore refuses to turn emotional completion into proof of a mechanical law.

## 9.3 River child: harmful effect without malicious motive

V23's drowned child remains water-bound and continues experiencing drowning.

Later children are pulled under through desperate help-seeking.

The effect is harmful.

The motive is not predatory.

This creates another anti-monster stereotype:

> **harmful causal action does not prove hostile intention.**

## 9.4 Ghost and deity are not mutually exclusive identity stories

Hachikuji's later deity role does not negate:

- her death;
- her ghost history;
- Mother's Day;
- the farewell.

A later category can be genuinely new without rewriting the prior category as fake.

This is the metaphysical version of D02's historical-self rule.

# 10. Constructed beings, self-generated persons, and creator responsibility

The later corpus increasingly asks whether a being's origin grants anyone ownership over what that being may become.

The metaphysics says no.

## 10.1 Ononoki: constructed function does not exhaust personhood

Ononoki is a shikigami/constructed being within specialist networks.

She has:

- commands;
- technical design;
- a body made for relational operation;
- professional roles.

She also demonstrates:

- embarrassment;
- preference;
- judgment;
- concealment;
- conflict of interest;
- attachment.

The project must not "humanize" her by denying construction.

The stronger formulation is:

> **constructed status and personhood coexist.**

## 10.2 Black Hanekawa: creation through incorporation

Hanekawa does not merely suffer standard cat possession.

Oshino's reconstruction says her consciousness pulls the departing cat back in.

A new hybrid is made.

Creation here is not clean authorship.

It emerges from:

- stress;
- oddity contact;
- adaptive self-structure;
- partial agency.

## 10.3 `苛虎`: learned psychic separation becomes an oddity technology

V08 explicitly frames repeated oddity use as learned coping.

Hanekawa becomes capable of separating jealousy into a tiger.

The tiger is literally dangerous.

It is also affective externalization.

The resolution is integration/return rather than annihilation.

This is perhaps the corpus's strongest proof that literal supernatural body and psychological meaning can coexist without one reducing the other.

## 10.4 Ougi: self-derived origin does not erase independent personhood

V18 resolves Ougi as tied to Araragi's `自己批判精神` and multiple material/social enabling conditions.

Yet Gaen calls Ougi an `独立した精神体`.

`Ougi = Araragi` is true and incomplete.

This is a general constructed-person rule:

> **causal derivation from a person does not make the derived being metaphysically disposable as "nothing but" that person's psyche.**

## 10.5 Nadeko's V22 creations disobey

The four drawn Nadekos are current creations based on remembered self-models.

They are not time travelers.

Naming, props, limiter design, and representation correlate with behavior.

Yet all four refuse the original labor assignment.

Creator intention is therefore not equivalent to created-agent command.

A shikigami can even create another lower-fidelity shikigami.

The exact ontology remains open.

## 10.6 Created utility creates continuing duty

V26 Ononoki creates a teddy familiar by embedding/removing her right eye.

The familiar is instrumental.

Ononoki nonetheless accepts containment/destruction responsibility if it becomes dangerous.

Unauthorized creation triggers Gaen discipline.

The governing rule is:

> **creating something useful does not make the creation morally neutral equipment.**

Creation introduces a responsibility relation.

## 10.7 V29 reconstruction and numerical identity

Nadeko's star-sand Ononoki case produces functional continuity after crash/damage.

The corpus does not settle whether this is:

- repair;
- reconstruction;
- new instantiation;
- numerically identical continuation.

That uncertainty is philosophically important.

Functional continuity is observed.

Metaphysical identity remains `UA`.

D03 must preserve that gap.

## 10.8 Uroko: origin, counter-self, daughter

Uroko identifies herself as Gaen's daughter and analogizes her formation to other counter/self-generated configurations.

The daughter relation is strongly established.

Exact biological/metaphysical mechanism remains open.

Do not force the case into either:

- ordinary biological maternity;
- "mere projection."

The series repeatedly occupies the middle space where **origin matters without exhausting ontology**.

# 11. The darkness, correction, law, and category mismatch

`くらやみ` is the closest the corpus comes to a general metaphysical law.

It still must not be overextended.

## 11.1 `非存在`, not ordinary monster

Gaen denies that the darkness is an ordinary oddity.

It has:

- no stable proper name;
- no free consciousness;
- no ordinary intention.

Human labels describe it.

They do not personify it.

## 11.2 The target condition is category falsification/mismatch

The darkness appears when an oddity persists under a role/category it is no longer genuinely enacting.

Ancient Kiss-shot's god performance and later Hachikuji's stale `迷い牛` status are central cases.

Yet Gaen explicitly distinguishes:

`偽るのは駄目だけど変わるのは、いい`.

This is the most important metaphysical anti-essentialist sentence in D03.

The system does not require a being to remain forever identical to origin.

Genuine transformation is permitted.

The danger is persisting under a category that no longer truthfully describes the current state.

## 11.3 Law does not equal moral judgment

The darkness is compared to gravity.

Its causal action can erase.

That does not mean the erased person deserves it.

D03 therefore rejects cosmic moralization.

Araragi's later `報い → 対価` correction is consistent with this.

A supernatural cost can be real without proving cosmic punishment.

## 11.4 Ougi is not the darkness

V18 resolves the earlier rhyme.

Ougi imitates/occupies a correction function.

She is not the impersonal law.

She is a hybrid oddity-person with:

- origin;
- relation;
- independent mentality;
- social continuation.

This distinction prevents a powerful metaphor from becoming false identity.

## 11.5 Flexibility and stability coexist

V19 Ononoki warns that radical enough self-role change could approach darkness conditions.

This does not mean persons cannot change.

It means some oddity configurations retain rule stability even while personhood remains flexible.

The series therefore refuses both extremes:

- static essence;
- unlimited self-definition unconstrained by causal history.

# 12. Disease, epidemiology, and supernatural medicine

Several arcs borrow medical language.

The series uses it productively without collapsing oddities into ordinary medicine.

## 12.1 Symptom removal is not stressor removal

Black Hanekawa provides the foundational clinical-style rule.

Shinobu can remove the oddity component.

The family environment and romantic stress remain.

This distinction recurs everywhere:

> treatment of manifestation is not necessarily treatment of causal substrate.

## 12.2 `囲い火蜂` and contested disease history

Karen's fever is severe and bodily.

The epidemic genealogy is contested/fabricated.

A medical-looking symptom can therefore coexist with unstable supernatural historiography.

## 12.3 V24 living mummification

The human victims remain alive in mummy-like state after vampire-related harm.

The treatment problem is constrained by species/body transformation.

A medicine appropriate to Suicide-Master may be dangerous for human victims.

Treatment is case-specific.

## 12.4 V28 pandemic method is epistemically staged

The vampire catastrophe initially appears pandemic-like.

The investigators use disease vocabulary because it fits observed population-level symptoms.

Later supernatural reconstruction changes the model.

The earlier "pandemic" state was not irrational.

It was the best available description at that stage.

D03 therefore inherits D02's epistemic rule:

> **a later supernatural explanation does not make earlier provisional medical reasoning stupid or dishonest.**

## 12.5 Failed analogy is evidence

Araragi compares V24 and V28 mummies.

Corpse inspection rejects straightforward equivalence.

This is one of the best methodological scenes in the corpus.

A plausible analogy is tested against bodies and downgraded.

Metaphysics advances by failed hypothesis, not only revelation.

## 12.6 Humanization as treatment changes the risk landscape

Shinobu drains Suicide-Master toward humanity.

The acute vampire-targeting problem changes.

Later human disease kills her.

The metaphysical/medical lesson is not irony as punishment.

It is:

> **every ontological body-state has a vulnerability profile.**

# 13. From personal cases to social systems

By Monster Season, oddity causation increasingly operates inside institutions and economies.

This changes the scale of metaphysics.

## 13.1 V20: residue can outlive the personal case

Kanbaru's arm can be personally restored while devil material remains elsewhere.

Personal resolution does not guarantee ecological disposal.

Likewise artificial `蛞蝓豆腐` residue can become substrate for a physically dangerous staged monster.

The fake/real problem becomes material infrastructure.

## 13.2 V22: representation becomes production system

Nadeko's drawing power is not only private expression.

It can create:

- agents;
- objects;
- copies;
- revised designs;
- mass-produced low-complexity forms.

This immediately raises:

- containment;
- creator responsibility;
- replacement risk;
- public cleanup.

The supernatural becomes an engineering/governance problem.

## 13.3 V25: creation and deity jurisdiction

Nadeko's drawn white snakes are accepted as `本物` / near-living by specialists.

Exact consciousness and reproduction remain open.

The important change is institutional.

Gaen/Kagenui now have to decide how to treat the output.

Hachikuji's deity power likewise raises `領分`.

Power becomes a question of scope.

## 13.4 V26: household trauma, supernatural fabric, and professional creation

The Iezumi case combines:

- fabricated family representation;
- animated fabric;
- changing attachment;
- possible partial oddityization;
- Ononoki familiar creation;
- paid contract;
- discipline for unauthorized creation.

The point is not that family trauma "causes clothes monsters" as a universal law.

The point is that human attachment, objects, constructed agents, and professional intervention can enter one causal system.

## 13.5 V27: law-like oddity and curse market

`妖魔令` is tied to human burnout/resentment but is not continuously puppeteered by the human subject.

The curse network is economic.

The same volume insists that supernatural explanation does not erase:

- ordinary grievance;
- relationship history;
- user responsibility;
- distributor responsibility.

This is the mature social-oddity model:

> **a real supernatural mechanism can coexist with complete ordinary causal layers rather than replacing them.**

## 13.6 V29: creator practice becomes professional identity

Nadeko's drawing now serves:

- expression;
- intervention;
- reconstruction;
- externalization.

The creator is not merely a teenager with a strange symptom.

She is becoming a specialist whose representation practice has public consequences.

Metaphysics has entered vocation.

# 14. Residue: why "resolution" is rarely metaphysical erasure

D03 treats residue as a first-class category.

This is necessary because Monogatari repeatedly closes an arc while leaving part of the condition active.

## 14.1 Social residue

Hitagi's weight returns.

Her family history does not.

Nadeko's curse clears.

Peer conflict and returned curse consequence remain.

Kaiki leaves town.

The relationships damaged by his market do not automatically repair.

## 14.2 Bodily residue

Kanbaru retains or later redistributes devil material.

Araragi remains partly vampiric.

Hanekawa's body retains visual traces after integration.

Tsukihi remains an immortal incarnation.

Uroko carries curse-work residue before externalization.

## 14.3 Relational residue

Araragi/Shinobu become deeply mutual.

The asymmetrical V04 origin remains historical fact.

Hachikuji gains new roles.

Her death remains.

Hanekawa integrates difficult affect.

The family wound is not thereby made harmless.

## 14.4 Environmental residue

Shrine fields accumulate risk.

First-thrall ash modifies local probability.

Black-mirror containment manages surplus.

Weather intervention may export cost.

## 14.5 Informational residue

Even when a body self-restores, other people remember.

Secrets accumulate.

Professional files and institutions retain consequences.

This is especially visible in Tsukihi's resilience externality.

## 14.6 Metaphysical residue can become infrastructure

Some residue is not garbage.

It becomes:

- shrine capacity;
- black mirror;
- specialist tool;
- oddity sample;
- future case evidence;
- created familiar substrate.

The distinction between "leftover" and "resource" is itself ethically dangerous.

A thing that can be reused still requires responsibility.

## 14.7 Resolution language must therefore remain precise

The project should distinguish:

- acute symptom ended;
- oddity component removed;
- contract disabled;
- person integrated;
- body restored;
- category changed;
- risk managed;
- recurrence prevented;
- causal mechanism unknown;
- social wound unresolved;
- environmental residue retained.

The word "cured" is often too strong.

# 15. Limits of specialist knowledge

The oddity world contains experts.

It does not contain a universally infallible narrator.

## 15.1 Oshino: model builder, negotiator, anti-totalizer

Oshino repeatedly supplies useful classification and technique.

He also:

- distinguishes help from being helped;
- warns that knowledge can increase what remains unknown;
- resists Araragi's attempt to make "family itself" Hanekawa's total oddity;
- favors ecological redistribution over dramatic cleansing.

His authority is methodological rather than omniscient.

## 15.2 Gaen: extraordinary information, still speaker-bounded

Gaen often has the strongest available system model.

Yet D02's rule remains binding:

> `私は何でも知っている` is epistemic theater as well as characterization.

Her models can be:

- highly privileged;
- technically successful;
- later revised;
- explicitly probabilistic.

The first-thrall field is a major example.

She gives a modest probability-amplification model and rejects master-cause totalization.

## 15.3 Kagenui: diagnosis does not settle ethics

Kagenui can correctly identify Tsukihi.

That does not make `無害認定` normatively decisive for her policy.

The case separates:

- what a being is;
- what a specialist should do about it.

This is one of D03's strongest anti-technocracy rules.

## 15.4 Kaiki: fake techniques can still expose real metaphysical structures

Kaiki's domain includes:

- fraud;
- rumor;
- fabricated genealogy;
- staged oddity.

Because he lies, the reader must audit claims.

Yet fake mechanisms are not irrelevant to D03.

They reveal where:

- belief;
- acceptance;
- social expectation;
- markets;

can interact with real effects.

## 15.5 Ononoki: technical competence includes uncertainty and conflict of interest

Ononoki is often closest to direct mechanism.

She still distinguishes:

- professional role;
- hypothesis;
- personal involvement;
- jurisdiction.

Her household placement and later specialist work make conflict-of-interest analysis unavoidable.

## 15.6 V24's proof/answer distinction

`証明は間違えたけど解答はあっていた` should govern the entire metaphysics document.

A correct endpoint does not validate every inference used to reach it.

This applies to:

- oddity diagnosis;
- creator theories;
- cure mechanisms;
- causal analogy;
- moral justification.

## 15.7 `解釈` is not `解答`

V15 supplies the complementary rule.

A strong interpretation can remain valuable without being the exclusive final answer.

This protects literary analysis from becoming counterfeit cosmology.

## 15.8 Professional maturity includes negative findings

V30's killstone conclusion is exemplary.

A famous supernatural site is investigated.

Current evidence supports ordinary splitting/weathering and sulfurous environmental effects.

`今のところ` preserves revisability.

It is not a secret clue that the supernatural explanation must be true.

A metaphysical system that cannot say "ordinary" is not rigorous.

## 16.1 How to use the case matrix without flattening the cases

The compact matrix should be read horizontally before it is read vertically.

A vertical reading—collecting every row under a single heading such as "human conflict" or "name"—is useful for comparison, but it can create a false impression that every case contains the same metaphysical ingredients in different quantities. The horizontal record preserves the stronger rule: each case has its own causal architecture.

Three examples show why this matters.

**Hitagi and Nadeko V02** both involve bodily symptoms and socially meaningful language. That does not make their causal structure equivalent. Hitagi's condition is bound to a burden-responsive oddity transaction and semantic field. Nadeko's V02 condition is an externally sent curse. Treating both as "psychosomatic manifestations of distress" would erase literal victimization in the second case.

**Black Hanekawa and the tiger** both externalize material connected to Hanekawa. Their origin structures still differ. Black Hanekawa begins with an external `障り猫` catalyst that Hanekawa later reincorporates into a hybrid. The tiger is produced through a learned self-separation process without the same independent animal catalyst. Calling both "Hanekawa's repressed feelings made flesh" is descriptively tempting and mechanically incomplete.

**Ougi and the real darkness** both occupy correction roles. Their ontologies are nevertheless specifically distinguished by V18. Ougi is a self-derived but independent oddity-person whose social relation can be stabilized. The darkness is an impersonal corrective phenomenon described through `非存在`. Functional resemblance is therefore evidence for comparison, not identity.

The matrix also preserves **negative and failed cases** because they are part of the metaphysical record. The V23 uniform-slasher rumor and V30 killstone prevent the database from becoming a confirmation machine in which every strange event is retrospectively forced into supernatural causation. The V24/V28 mummy comparison records a failed same-mechanism hypothesis rather than quietly removing it after correction.

Finally, residue must be read as part of ontology rather than as an epilogue column. If the body, environment, role, memory, curse sample, shrine infrastructure, or professional institution persists after the dramatic climax, then the case has demonstrated something about what the oddity *is*. Monogatari repeatedly refuses a videogame-like model in which defeating the encounter despawns the metaphysical problem.

For downstream use, D03 therefore recommends the following retrieval order:

1. identify the exact case and publication horizon;
2. identify the narrator or specialist supplying the claim;
3. establish the literal mechanic actually demonstrated;
4. separate human conflict and social substrate from that mechanic;
5. ask whether naming, belief, representation, or recognition is merely interpretive, necessary, amplifying, or materially constitutive in this specific case;
6. record the intervention;
7. record residue;
8. only then compare the case to others.

That order preserves the system's most important methodological feature:

> **comparison should emerge from case integrity rather than being purchased by erasing it.**


# 17. Cross-case metaphysical rules now mature enough to freeze

The following are D03-level rules. They are intentionally phrased more narrowly than a universal cosmology.

## Rule 1 — Literal supernatural causation and symbolic meaning are compatible

A phenomenon may be literally supernatural and symbolically structured.

Neither cancels the other.

## Rule 2 — Human contradiction is one oddity substrate, not the only oddity substrate

Kanbaru, Hanekawa, Nadeko, Ougi, and several later cases support internal contradiction as causally relevant.

Kiss-shot, external curses, pandemic-scale effects, and autonomous oddity-persons prevent universalization.

## Rule 3 — False genealogy can coexist with present causal efficacy

V05 and V13 establish this directly.

Historical fabrication and current mechanism must be audited separately.

## Rule 4 — Belief is sometimes causal and never demonstrated as unlimited omnipotence

Recognition, acceptance, rumor, or worship can matter.

No supplied source proves that belief alone can create any arbitrary oddity.

## Rule 5 — Names have multiple powers

Names can:

- classify;
- frame;
- bind;
- legitimize;
- relate;
- conceal;
- provide public/legal identity.

No single true-name hierarchy governs every case.

## Rule 6 — A useful metaphor is not a complete ontology

Family-as-oddity, disease language, `反怪異`, black-hole analogies, and monster labels may clarify.

They do not become literal species law automatically.

## Rule 7 — Self-generated does not mean fully intentional

A person can participate in oddity creation without consciously designing the final mechanism.

## Rule 8 — Hidden or violent affect is not the sole authentic self

Rainy Devil, Black Hanekawa, tiger, and Nadeko all reject hidden-side essentialism.

## Rule 9 — Origin does not settle personhood

Tsukihi, Ononoki, Ougi, Uroko, and represented/created selves make this explicit.

## Rule 10 — Creator does not equal controller

Nadeko's agents refuse assignments.

Ononoki's created familiar requires containment duty.

Created beings can exceed creator intention.

## Rule 11 — Category can have causal force without becoming moral judgment

The darkness is the clearest case.

Lawful correction does not equal deserved punishment.

## Rule 12 — Genuine change is metaphysically permitted

`偽るのは駄目だけど変わるのは、いい`.

Later identity need not remain origin-identical to be legitimate.

## Rule 13 — Nonperception is not nonexistence

V23 states this directly.

It does not imply that every unperceived rumor is supernatural.

## Rule 14 — Background field is not master cause

V17's probability model explicitly rejects total causal convergence.

## Rule 15 — A correct answer can rest on a wrong proof

V24 makes this explicit and it applies to all specialist inference.

## Rule 16 — Supernatural explanation does not erase human responsibility

Curse networks, Nadeko, `妖魔令`, creator cases, and professional interventions all preserve ordinary agency layers.

## Rule 17 — Resolution does not imply residue erasure

Body, relationship, environment, memory, institution, and social history may all survive the acute case.

## Rule 18 — Professional responsibility can include incomplete cleanup

V23 makes bounded delegation and documented incompleteness legitimate alternatives to heroic total control.

## Rule 19 — Strong power creates externality problems

V30's weather case literalizes this at atmospheric scale.

The same structure appears in curse markets, shrine ecology, regeneration externality, and creator systems.

## Rule 20 — A mature specialist can conclude "ordinary"

The V30 killstone case closes the loop opened in V01.

Oddity expertise is not the ability to supernaturalize everything.

It is the ability to distinguish when the supernatural explanation is and is not supported.

# 21. Mature synthesis: what kind of reality do the monsters possess?

The supplied V2 corpus supports the following answer.

Oddities are real enough to:

- change body weight;
- kill;
- heal;
- curse;
- regenerate;
- feed;
- create thralls;
- alter weather;
- survive death;
- accumulate in places;
- form contracts;
- respond to roles;
- inhabit social systems;
- produce new bodies;
- emerge from representation;
- persist through residue;
- require institutional management.

They are also narratively plastic enough to be shaped by:

- names;
- recognition;
- rumor;
- belief;
- inherited folklore;
- wish structure;
- family roles;
- professional categories;
- images;
- stories;
- markets;
- shrines;
- law-like commands;
- memory;
- social expectation.

The key is that these two facts are not opposites.

The series does not ask the reader to choose:

> literal monster **or** human story.

It repeatedly constructs cases in which:

> **the monster is literal, the story about the monster matters causally, and neither level is allowed to swallow the other.**

That is why the same corpus can support all of the following without contradiction:

- Nadeko is externally cursed in V02.
- Nadeko later fabricates a god story in V11.
- That false story helps route her into real godhood.
- Nadeko later creates causal representations through art.
- None of those facts proves one universal theory that "Nadeko's psyche creates all snakes."

Likewise:

- Hanekawa encounters a real cat oddity.
- Hanekawa actively reincorporates it.
- Black Hanekawa becomes a real hybrid.
- Hanekawa later creates a tiger through learned affect separation.
- The family and jealousy meanings are real.
- None of this means the animals were merely metaphors.

The same double refusal governs personhood.

Tsukihi's oddity origin is real.

Her family personhood is real.

Ononoki's construction is real.

Her affect and professional agency are real.

Ougi is Araragi-derived.

Ougi is also an independent entity.

A created or derived person does not become less real because the cause can be identified.

The same double refusal governs names.

Names can matter to power.

They can stabilize relation.

They can expose contradiction.

They can structure law, family, and public identity.

Yet no name alone supplies the total essence of the named person.

The same double refusal governs specialists.

A specialist can identify a mechanism correctly.

That does not make the specialist omniscient.

A proof can be wrong while the answer is right.

An interpretation can be powerful without becoming the only answer.

A natural explanation can be the correct professional conclusion at a famous supernatural site.

The same double refusal governs resolution.

An oddity can be removed while the family remains broken.

A body can heal while consequence accumulates in others.

A person can become more human and therefore more vulnerable to human disease.

A deity role can solve one category mismatch while creating a jurisdiction question.

A curse can be stopped while the market, victims, distributors, and social history remain.

A local miracle can succeed while atmospheric cost moves elsewhere.

The most precise D03 thesis is therefore:

> **Monogatari's metaphysics is a system of causally real narrative bodies: oddities are not merely stories, but stories, names, beliefs, roles, representations, and institutions can become part of the conditions through which supernatural reality takes form.**

The necessary qualification is equally important:

> **Because meaning participates in causation, interpretation matters; because causation exceeds meaning, interpretation must never be mistaken for total ontology.**

That is the philosophical center of the oddity system.

The monster is real.

The name matters.

The body remembers.

The rumor moves people.

The shrine changes the field.

The specialist may still be wrong.

The cure leaves residue.

The creator does not own the created.

The past does not dictate the present.

And sometimes, after all the folklore and expectation, the correct answer is that the stone simply split.
