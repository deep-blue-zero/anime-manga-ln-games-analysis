# 6. Compact V01–V19 analytical state

## V01 — 化物語 上

- Rescue is framed through who decides what a person carries.
- `助ける / 助かる` begins the agency distinction.

## V02 — 化物語 中

- Hidden does not equal authentic.
- Divided wishes need not reveal one true self.
- A person's body is not private moral currency for the helper.
- Universal concern does not automatically create situated responsibility.
- Nadeko's V02 victimhood is real and must not be revoked by V11.

## V03 — 化物語 下

- Hanekawa's goodness is authentic but also compulsory adaptation.
- Black Hanekawa is not simply possession and not “true Hanekawa.”
- Araragi's idealization is itself a perceptual failure.
- “Saving oneself” means agency locus, not isolation.

## V04 — 傷物語

- Kizu is mutual wounding and mirrored sacrificial rescue.
- Araragi's self-sacrifice is powered partly by self-devaluation.
- Responsibility is not punishment.
- Oshino's settlement resists scapegoating.
- Later love does not purify the damaged origin.

## V05 — 偽物語 上

- Fake/real is not a simple value hierarchy.
- Owned imitation can become ethically real.
- `後始末` becomes important to responsibility.
- False origin does not imply unreal consequence.
- Araragi–Shinobu begin `歩み寄り` without requiring total forgiveness.

## V06 — 偽物語 下

- Fake/real develops into personhood.
- Origin does not exhaust the person.
- Correct retrospective truth can still overfit.
- Family can be enacted history rather than biological essence.
- Kagenui can diagnose correctly while being normatively wrong.
- Tsukihi's life is protected more strongly than her right to full self-knowledge.

## V07 — 猫物語 黒

- Black Hanekawa is a hybrid formation, not authentic-core revelation.
- Hanekawa's goodness is real and compulsory.
- V06/V07 create a family/biology inversion.
- Araragi's idealization blocks recognition of need.
- `物語は完結するけれど、問題は解決しない` is a major residue principle.

## V08 — 猫物語 白

- Narrator handoff enables counter-narration without omniscience.
- Hanekawa's problem is emotional subtraction, not hidden evil.
- The tiger is self-generated; jealousy is tied centrally to belonging/home.
- `戻る → 帰る → ただいま` tracks home becoming possible.
- Confession/rejection relinquishes outcome control.
- `すべてが私` establishes plural continuity rather than one authentic core.

## V09 — 傾物語

- Reject “saving Hachikuji destroyed the world.”
- People can genuinely save one another in bounded senses without owning recovery or future.
- Route X turns on relational character formation: Hachikuji friendship makes Araragi capable of asking Shinobu for help.
- Later catastrophe does not make earlier rescue evil; later benefit does not justify earlier harm.
- Route X Kiss-shot turns loss of one reciprocal bond into billions of coerced bonds.
- `目の前の女の子` is an anti-abstraction principle.

## V10 — 花物語

- Kanbaru graduates from self-punishment, not responsibility.
- Rainy Devil is hers but not her one true self.
- Numachi's misery-listening can be self-serving and genuinely helpful simultaneously.
- `いいだけの人間はいない / 悪いだけの人間はいない` resists total verdicts.
- Rivalry can become recognition.
- Araragi's helper role matures toward support and non-centrality.

## V11 — 囮物語

Central lock:

> Nadeko is not a false shy girl exposed by a true monster. She is genuinely vulnerable, while her coping defenses also shape how others respond to her.

Preserve:

- victimhood is relational, not essential;
- initial Kuchinawa is self-generated narrative/persona, not an already-revived external god;
- real godhood begins after Nadeko knowingly ingests the real talisman;

- `撫子 / 俺様 / 私` are voice/agency technologies, not an authenticity ladder;
- cuteness is social legibility/power and unwanted reduction, not proof of manipulative intent;

- being rescued by Araragi becomes emotionally rewarding without proving she manufactured V02;
- Araragi's failure is protective misrecognition;
- Tsukihi's correct diagnosis does not authorize the haircut boundary violation;
- classroom rage includes a coexistence ethic rather than simply revealing a true angry self;
- Nadeko has a self; the problem is an authorship deficit, not emptiness;
- Hitagi's call converts “rival role” into encountered person and creates time rather than instant reform;
- V11 §024 is a `予告編`, not completed chronology.

- Hachikuji morally outgrows `迷い牛` before the system offers a sustainable category.

- Shinobu's first thrall reveals prior attachment, loneliness, coercive rescue, and relational harm.

- Ougi/darkness resemblance remains unresolved at V12.

## V13 — 恋物語

- Kaiki's professional traffic-accident deception fails.
- Low suspicion is not trust; Nadeko is relationally closed.
- `俺はもう自分を騙せなかった`: Kaiki's key failed lie is his own detachment story.
- `千石` marks movement from target/category to person.
- The hidden manga proves plurality, not a hidden true Nadeko.
- Kaiki restores plural future rather than guaranteed happiness.
- `騙されてあげる` converts imposed deception into knowingly accepted uncertainty.
- Fake oddity efficacy is relational and conditional.
- Hitagi demonstrates gratitude without forgiveness.
- Araragi must learn that causal responsibility does not entail continued relational centrality.
- Ougi becomes strongly causal as an information/prompt node, but ontology remains open.

## V14 — 憑物語

- Araragi's condition is accumulated habituation: repeated emergency exception has become embodied disposition.
- Text corrects `報い` to `対価`: consequence without cosmic retribution.
- There is no purity reset; the mature problem is living responsibly with irreversible residue.
- `予防` is the rescue breakthrough: sustainable care before catastrophe.
- Yotsugi's form is `人といるため`, not proof of aspiring to human essence.
- Human/monster vocabulary is contested self-judgment, not a transparent ontology.
- “It saved someone” is not a complete defense of violent means.
- Teori recognizes himself as `キャスティング` / a piece moved by someone else; arranger remains unresolved.
- Araragi/Shinobu's shared body-state makes self-sacrifice relational rather than purely private.
- Hitagi increasingly represents ordinary continuity.

## V15 — 暦物語

Project-wide methodological lock:

> Later connection is not automatically earlier foreshadowing.

Also:

- the collection tracks meaning accumulating after origins are forgotten/mistaken;
- `解答` and `解釈` must remain distinct;
- false origin can still yield real social consequence;
- `こよみシード` shows successful prevention may produce no story;
- self-saving is non-transferable agency, not causal self-sufficiency;
- boundaries are part of expertise (`こよみナッシング`);
- Gaen is extraordinarily informed but not omniscient;
- V15 ends with three separate facts: Gaen kills/dismembers Araragi; Araragi later wakes alive; Hachikuji appears. The bridge remains open until V18.

## V16 — 終物語 上

- Araragi repeatedly turns “I missed something” into “I caused everything.”
- Self-blame can be severe while still evading causal partition.
- Oikura's apparently abandoned house was her inhabited home; protected assumptions made abuse illegible.
- Missing an indirect SOS does not make a child responsible for adult abuse.
- The classroom trial is centrally institutional betrayal because Tetsujo participates in scapegoating.
- Araragi's `人間強度` isolation ethic is partly historicized as acquired defense.
- `どれも老倉育`: Oikura's anger is real but not authenticity monopoly.
- Hanekawa separates knowing truth from the ethics of disclosure.
- Ougi's detective procedure can be epistemically productive and ethically coercive.
- Memory taxonomy: forgotten / misremembered / reconstructed / retained causal effect.

## V17 — 終物語 中

- The first thrall's return is multi-stage causation, not romance resurrecting him.
- Gaen's ash/shrine model is a causal field, not universal master cause.
- Shinobu's final answer is recognition without reunion: remember, apologize, forgive, choose Araragi, refuse resumption.
- Historical priority does not grant present relational jurisdiction.
- `特別` is maintained through continuing choice, not chronology.
- Intimacy can reduce corrective independence; Kanbaru supplies external friction.
- Araragi's rescue grammar improves from “use Shinobu's strength” toward “become strength for Shinobu.”
- Trust can mean allowing other agents to act where Araragi cannot center himself.
- First-thrall ending is finality without guaranteed redemption.
- Ononoki rejects unhappiness as moral payment.

## V18 — 終物語 下

- V15's death is literal; resurrection is a managed boundary operation that returns the human component.
- Hachikuji restores self-inclusion: Araragi himself is one of the lives that count.
- `僕は今、自分を助けたんだ` is the core correction.
- Ougi = Araragi is true but incomplete; Ougi is a composite constructed person with independent continuity.
- Ougi is not the real `くらやみ`.
- Meme's “niece” declaration is recognition/role creation, not secret genealogy.
- Hachikuji's extraction from hell is nonconsensual; later deity acceptance is a distinct autonomy event.
- Araragi/Shinobu voluntarily rebuild their bond and move away from synchronized annihilation.
- Hitagi supplies an ordinary post-crisis future.
- Ending adolescence means abandoning self-destruction as privileged moral currency, not abandoning care.

## V19 — 続 終物語

- The “mirror world” is not a clean opposite timeline; Araragi pulls ordinarily unreflected remainder into his own world, producing an overfull `120%` state.
- Reverse manifestations are residue/unrealized possibility, not truth machines.
- Post-graduation role loss does not reveal a pure pre-social self; environments and roles genuinely constitute identity.
- Ougi becomes sustainable critic / `安全弁` / `相棒` because overlap plus distance enables correction.
- Tooe adds `間違えて知っている`: knowledge possession is not understanding.
- Intimacy does not create a right to exhaustive access to another's hidden interior.
- Oikura's cheerful remainder is unrealized possibility/regret, not hidden authentic Oikura.
- Black Hanekawa's return does not invalidate V08 integration; integration does not require simultaneous expression of every feeling.
- Repair is not restoration: `二割増しで前進する`.
- `向き合う` is not `背負う`: face remainder without carrying every possibility forever.
- V18 remains a real ending even though V19 explores its residue.
