# L05 — Relationship and Address Ledger

## R01 — Araragi Koyomi ↔ Senjougahara Hitagi

### Entering state
Three-year classmates who are effectively near-strangers. Araragi knows a social role rather than Hitagi's private life.

### V01 progression

| Stage | Relationship state | Key language / action |
|---|---|---|
| R01-A | accidental bodily contact | Araragi catches Hitagi and learns secret weight |
| R01-B | coercive asymmetry | Hitagi demands `沈黙と無関心`; `阿良々木くん` is used within threat/control |
| R01-C | provisional mediated trust | Araragi introduces Oshino; Hitagi accepts process despite not trusting either fully |
| R01-D | important friendship | Hitagi explicitly calls him `大切な友達` after weight restoration |
| R01-E | ordinary conversation desired | May 14 Hitagi says she came because she wants to talk with him |
| R01-F | Araragi's general kindness recognized | she concludes he did not help because she alone was special: `誰でも助けるだけなのね` |
| R01-G | chosen partiality | she wants to become special to someone whose help was general |
| R01-H | explicit confession | `I love you` |
| R01-I | romantic contract | she wants time to know him and `好きになる努力`; Araragi accepts with `戦場ヶ原、蕩れ` |
| R01-J | epistemic promise | neither will pretend to see/unsee; disagreements should be spoken and discussed |

### Address / voice
- Hitagi → Koyomi: primarily `阿良々木くん`.
- No sudden intimate first-name normalization in V01; intimacy grows through content and voluntary touch rather than address collapse.

### Leaving state
Explicitly romantic partners, but with very little chronological time spent together. V01 emphasizes **future knowing**, not completed mutual understanding.

### Non-possession note
Hitagi does not require that Araragi's kindness have been uniquely directed at her. Her romantic move depends on choosing specialness **after** acknowledging his general concern for others.

## R02 — Araragi Koyomi ↔ Hachikuji Mayoi

### Entering state
Unknown high-school boy approaches apparently lost fifth-grade girl; Hachikuji immediately rejects him.

### Address / comic grammar
- Hachikuji repeatedly deforms `阿良々木` in the misnaming routine.
- Correction formula: `失礼。嚙みました`.
- Polite forms coexist with insult, biting, and slapstick resistance.

### Relationship turning points
1. intrusive approach / fight;
2. shared travel and verbal play;
3. revelation that Hachikuji is dead/oddity;
4. Araragi rejects self-protective disengagement;
5. Hachikuji reaches home through accompaniment;
6. next day she asks him to speak to her if they meet.

### Leaving state
An ongoing voluntary road/acquaintance relation becomes possible after the compulsive `迷い牛` mechanism is loosened.

### Non-possession note
Araragi does not “restore” Hachikuji to life or absorb her into his household. The relationship remains intermittent and spatially open.

## R04 — Hitagi ↔ Hachikuji

There is no ordinary reciprocal dialogue in V01 because Hitagi cannot perceive Hachikuji. Apparent avoidance is therefore **not interpersonal hostility**. Hitagi's behavior is a perceptual concealment strategy aimed at Araragi.

This entry is a permanent reminder that relationship ledgers require a **shared-perception check** before assigning motive.

### Karen ↔ Tsukihi

Karen answers without hesitation that she would die for Tsukihi. Because she is not told Tsukihi's nature, this proves unconditional **experienced sibling loyalty**, not informed acceptance of the oddity revelation.

### Araragi ↔ Shinobu

V06 marks an important post-Kizu consent development. Araragi says his command power over Shinobu is effectively absolute, but precisely because it is absolute he is reluctant to use it casually: `強過ぎる力` becomes a weakness/constraint on legitimate action.

Shinobu's side of the relation also becomes more responsive: she presses her own mundane requests, negotiates for him, and declines a further fight partly because she does not want to alienate him.

**R06-03:** later mutuality is being built through *restraint and reciprocal responsiveness*, not retroactive purification of the imposed bond.

### Kagenui ↔ Ononoki

Locally describable as simultaneous:

- specialist / oddity;
- handler / instrument;
- senior / junior;
- `姉妹`-like pair.

Kagenui physically carries the injured Ononoki away. This lived care complicates any simple doctrine that oddity/human mixture is inherently fraudulent.

### Family non-possession test

V06 produces a mixed result:

- **PASS:** Araragi refuses Kagenui's right to erase Tsukihi based on category.
- **FAIL / OPEN:** Araragi claims the right to manage Tsukihi's self-knowledge based on being family.

# V07 update — relationship states

## Araragi ↔ Hanekawa

### Entering state

The relationship is already structured by Spring Break gratitude. Araragi treats Hanekawa as `恩人` and exceptionally good; Hanekawa treats Araragi as a friend she can approach but still hides the deepest household facts from almost everyone.

### V07 changes

- Hanekawa discloses that she has no family in the meaningful sense, then apologizes for having vented.
- Araragi heals her facial injury without directly asking because he predicts she would reject aid.
- Hanekawa refuses being taken to Araragi's home and later refuses direct intervention in the cat case.
- Araragi enters her house despite that refusal, making the non-possession verdict mixed: the trespass reveals crucial truth but violates her expressed boundary.
- Araragi declares a feeling exceeding ordinary romance and expresses it as willingness to die for her.
- Hanekawa/Black Hanekawa challenge the utility of that sacrifice: destroying himself cannot repair the household.

**State:** intimacy deepens through knowledge, but asymmetry remains severe. Araragi can now see Hanekawa's suffering yet still translates care into unilateral bodily payment.

## Hanekawa ↔ household parents

The relationship is structurally nonfamilial despite legal/domestic co-residence. Her statement `ただ、家族じゃないだけ` is supported materially by the house itself: there is no Hanekawa room, little trace of her personal presence, and no reliable parental attention to her face or injuries.

V2 caution: later recognition that cohabitation with Hanekawa may also have been difficult for the adults does **not** excuse physical violence or parental neglect.

## Araragi ↔ Shinobu

V07 continues the coercive-origin relation through a new reciprocity pattern:

- Araragi spends days requesting aid from silent Shinobu rather than issuing a magical command.
- Shinobu ultimately acts decisively to save him and suppress Black Hanekawa.
- Araragi imagines her motive as “you forced me to live, so you do not get to die freely,” but explicitly identifies this wording as hallucinated/imaginative.

**State:** action-based mutual dependence is becoming real even before ordinary speech is restored.

## Araragi ↔ Araragi family

Oshino explicitly contrasts Araragi with Hanekawa: Araragi has a family that worries about him. This turns his self-sacrifice into a relational cost rather than purely private expenditure.

## Help-without-request rule

V07 complicates the project's consent vocabulary. Oshino notes that failure to say `助けて` does not prove no help is desired, just as failure to say `好き` does not prove love is absent: `軽はずみに言えない言葉は誰にだってある`.

This does **not** abolish refusal. It requires distinguishing:

- inability to ask;
- implicit help-seeking;
- explicit refusal;
- paternalistic override;
- emergency intervention.

# V09 update — Hachikuji non-possession and Araragi/Shinobu partnership

## R-HACHI — Araragi ↔ Hachikuji

### Non-possession test

V09 gives this relationship one of the strongest non-possession results in the corpus. Araragi knows that successfully preventing Hachikuji's death will erase their Mayoi-era friendship from that route and chooses her life anyway.

When he later meets adult Route X Hachikuji, he does not demand recognition, gratitude, or replacement intimacy. His farewell—`生きていてくれて、ありがとうございました`—treats her survival as sufficient benefit.

### Relational co-authorship

Back in the main route, Araragi states that the Hachikuji he knows emerged through their months of interaction just as he himself was altered by her. This is mutual formation, not possession.

### Address-state distinction

- Route X adult Hachikuji: `阿良々木くん` toward a strange young man.
- Main-route ghost Hachikuji: established `阿良々木さん` grammar inside their familiar teasing relation.

The difference should remain indexed as relationship history, not merely politeness.

## R-SHINO — Araragi ↔ Shinobu

### State advance

Araragi explicitly calls Shinobu `僕の最高のパートナー`. The volume operationalizes that claim through travel, risk negotiation, causal reconstruction, combat planning, shared guilt, and willingness to remain together if stranded.

### Power/consent continuity

V09 does not erase Kizu's coercive origin. Shinobu explicitly distrusts what restored power might do to her behavior; Araragi's trust does not settle that risk. Later mutuality therefore continues to be evaluated as **practiced restraint under inherited asymmetry**.

### Asking for help as relationship product

Route X's inferred hinge is that Hachikuji-less Araragi cannot ask Shinobu for help. This makes V03's `助けて……忍` not an isolated line but evidence of a relational capacity acquired through broader social life.

## R-ALT — Present Shinobu ↔ Route X Kiss-shot

Treat as **same-origin / divergent-self** relation.

Present Shinobu can rationally recognize the alternate catastrophe as a possible version of herself without being personally guilty of murders she did not commit. Route divergence matters ethically because relationships changed the selves.

## R-XKISS — Araragi ↔ Route X Kiss-shot

Final request is explicitly non-transactional: `ただのお願い` to have her head stroked. Araragi answers `喜んで`. The moment is recognition without acquittal and should remain separate from a claim of full redemption.

## V12 relationship-state updates — 『鬼物語』

### Araragi ↔ Hachikuji

The relation reaches its strongest non-possession test. The decisive image is asymmetrical: Hachikuji is no longer gripping Araragi's hand; **he is the one still holding hers**. His offer to remain lost with her for life would preserve proximity by consuming all other futures. Hachikuji refuses with `全部は選べませんよ` and chooses `わたしは帰るんですよ`.

The farewell is authored by Hachikuji rather than by Araragi: she requests the familiar mispronunciation routine, uses the kiss/`失礼。噛みました` callback, and says `大好きでしたよ、阿良々木さん`.

**State:** intense attachment + release. The kiss is strong intimacy and possibly romantic coding, but the text does not require a single exclusive category for the relationship.

### Shinobu ↔ first thrall

Kiss-shot recreates the first oddity killer because loneliness and fear make losing the last survivor intolerable. He experiences the imposed vampire existence as betrayal rather than gift. This is an early relation in which attachment produces **preservation without consent**.

**State:** historical rupture; coercive rescue precedent.

### Araragi ↔ Shinobu

Shinobu shares history she had not previously narrated to him, strengthening epistemic intimacy. Yet Araragi's panic proposal to spend his entire life lost with Hachikuji implicitly spends Shinobu's linked future as well.

**State:** profound interdependence with continuing need for non-possession/third-party accounting.

### Araragi ↔ Hitagi

Hitagi is largely absent from the physical V12 action, but the relationship becomes ethically present when Araragi imagines abandoning ordinary future altogether. Hachikuji herself names the impossibility of choosing everything.

**State:** absent commitment functions as real third-party constraint on rescue fantasy.
