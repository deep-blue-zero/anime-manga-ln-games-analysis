### Benefactor distortion rule

V03 opens a reusable narrator warning:

> **Idealization can be a positive-valence form of misrecognition.**

Araragi does not misread Hanekawa because he despises her. He misreads her because gratitude makes her too exceptional to imagine as jealous, frightened, sexual, needy, or interested in him.

### Retrospective-narration warning — Golden Week

Statements such as “telling me was probably the final trigger” should remain `NR/SI`, even when psychologically persuasive. V07 must not be forced into V03's reconstruction.

### Black Hanekawa as focalization-adjacent informant

Black Hanekawa is not a narrator but temporarily functions as an epistemic countervoice. Its evidence should be classified by domain:

- stress-source perception: relatively strong within the current diegetic model;
- Hanekawa knowledge: shared but filtered;
- memory: discontinuous;
- general oddity ontology: CD hypothesis;
- moral judgment / solution proposal: not authoritative.

## V04 update — Araragi retrospective origin narration

### Narrator state

Araragi narrates the chronologically earliest mainline events **after knowing their damaged aftermath**. V04 therefore introduces a distinct mode: retrospective origin testimony under acknowledged guilt.

### Explicit epistemic manifesto

At the opening Araragi states that he can only observe from his own viewpoint, that observers assign different meanings to the vampire, and that what exists for him is not pure `真実` but `認識`. This is not license to discard his report. It is a built-in instruction to separate event report from motive/theory.

### Reliability dimensions added

| Dimension | V04 state |
|---|---|
| Action/sequence recall | generally high for decisive crisis beats |
| Other-character motive attribution | corrigible; Oshino/Hanekawa/Kiss-shot repeatedly revise him |
| Self-motive attribution | unusually self-indicting; may over-totalize guilt |
| Moral judgment | changes materially within volume; `美しい` vs `正しい` is explicit self-correction |
| Metaphysical explanation | dependent on Oshino/Kiss-shot accounts |
| Aesthetic framing | high-risk; self-sacrificial action can be narrated as beautiful before consequences become visible |

### New correction pattern: self-condemnation can also distort

V2 should not equate Araragi's harsh retrospective guilt with omniscient moral truth. V04 makes him both victim and `加害者`, but not sole causal source of every choice made by Kiss-shot, hunters, Hanekawa, or Oshino.

### External narrator-correctors

- Hanekawa: friendship includes shared joy; dying is not sufficient responsibility; her actions are her choices rather than saintly essence.
- Oshino: Kiss-shot also saved Araragi; Araragi should stop adopting a pure victim posture.
- Kiss-shot: Araragi's rescue was generalized; her rescue of him was particularized.

### Longitudinal rule

**Araragi is best read as corrigible, not generically unreliable. V04 adds retrospective guilt and aesthetic self-dramatization as distinct correction dimensions.**

## V06 update — Araragi Koyomi in 『偽物語（下）』

### High-value narrator correction: correct cause, excessive explanation

V06 provides a textbook second-pass warning. Once Shinobu correctly identifies the `しでの鳥`, Araragi retrospectively recognizes real bodily evidence—vanished scars, extreme regeneration, hair/nail growth. He then extends the same explanatory frame into personality, treating Tsukihi's dependence on Karen's justice as evidence that there is no `本人` and sliding rhetorically toward `人でなし`.

**Ledger decision:** split the inference.

- bodily anomalies → **strongly source-supported retrospective correction**;
- relational suggestibility → **NR/IT**;
- absence of personhood → **VJ / narrator overreach**, materially countered by Tsukihi's earlier metacognition.

### New reliability dimensions

| Dimension | V06 evidence | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Retrospective body-pattern recognition | scars/hair/nails become legible after phoenix reveal | high for physical mechanism |
| Personality etiologizing | borrowed justice becomes “bird has no self” | high overfitting risk |
| Family judgment | “always my sister” rests on continuous biography | emotionally interested but evidentially meaningful |
| Disclosure ethics | he decides secrecy is protective and analogizes Hitagi/Kanbaru | corrigible; analogy is incomplete because those subjects control their own secrets |
| Self-classification | recognizes his own oddity experience shapes tolerance | useful partial bias awareness |
| Sexual comedy | uses taboo performance as narration and even as a “test” | unreliable as direct relationship evidence; consent must be audited separately |

### N06-C01 — family familiarity can hide anomalies

Araragi explicitly considers that he looked away because Tsukihi was family. This adds a new narrator risk: **intimacy can normalize evidence rather than improve recognition**.

### N06-C02 — later truth must not biologize the whole person

The V2 reread must resist reproducing Araragi's own mistake. A later correct ontology does not license rewriting every prior trait as a symptom of that ontology.

### N06-C03 — moral recognition can be right for interested reasons

Kagenui notes that Araragi's own hybrid condition gives him a special basis for tolerating Tsukihi. This does not invalidate his acceptance. It shows that **bias can produce morally valuable recognition as well as distortion**.

### Governing longitudinal rule after V06

> Araragi's focalization is most dangerous not when it invents facts, but when a real fact becomes a theory of the whole person.

## N07 — Araragi Koyomi, 『猫物語（黒）』

| Dimension | V07 evidence | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Narration time | told after Golden Week, with later school-year hindsight | explicitly retrospective |
| Truth claim | says exhaustive narration cannot reproduce “the truth” | strong self-limitation marker |
| Hanekawa perception | elevates her toward god/demon-level exceptionalism | idealization is an evidence problem |
| Motive attribution | often infers from Hanekawa's behavior and later testimony | corrigible |
| Memory continuity | flags unconscious/hazy sequences as inference/hearsay | unusually explicit epistemic hygiene |
| Moral judgment | calls Hanekawa's defense of parents `気持ち悪い`; later recognizes he made her say it | VJ plus self-correction |
| Romance self-classification | insists his feeling is not `恋`, then describes desire to die for her | narratorial category should not be treated as neutral fact |
| Shinobu speech | narrates an archaic rebuke but explicitly calls it hallucination/imagination | **not literal Shinobu dialogue evidence** |
| Oddity explanation | depends heavily on Oshino's later reconstruction | separate narrator report from specialist claim |

### N07-C01 — benevolent idealization can be a severe perceptual failure

Araragi does not fail to see Hanekawa because he dislikes or distrusts her. He fails because he thinks **too highly** of her. The idea that “Hanekawa is Hanekawa, therefore she must be fine” becomes a mechanism for rendering her suffering unbelievable.

### N07-C02 — retrospective honesty does not remove retrospective construction

V07 is unusually candid about memory gaps and the limits of narration. That makes Araragi more useful as a source, not omniscient. A narrator can accurately flag his own blind spots while still constructing the emotional meaning of the past.

### N07-C03 — quoted interior voices require ontological labeling

The imagined Shinobu rebuke has high interpretive value because it reveals how Araragi understands their bond, but low value as direct evidence of Shinobu's wording or stated motive. Future synthesis must label such internalized/imagined speech accordingly.

### Governing longitudinal rule after V07

> A narrator's idealization can distort as strongly as hostility; explicit self-awareness improves source handling but does not make the narration transparent.

# V09 update — Araragi route narration, retrospective framing, and embedded Oshino letter

## N09-A — Later Ougi frame around an earlier event

V09 begins with Araragi discussing Ougi even though she has no causal participation in the summer adventure. He openly says that memories of this “bad” episode bring Ougi to mind. This makes narration-time association visible: a later relationship reorganizes how an earlier event is told.

**Rule added:** chronological nonparticipation does not mean narratorial irrelevance. Later figures may frame earlier material without becoming evidence for what event-time characters knew.

## N09-B — Teleology self-corrected by narration

Araragi interprets the accidental jump to Mother's Day as fate arranging Hachikuji's rescue. His retrospective narration immediately undercuts this confidence (`運命を理由にする者が…`). Preserve the distinction:

- event-time Araragi: strong teleological confidence;
- narration-time Araragi: recognizes the danger/foolishness of that confidence.

## N09-C — Apocalypse reconstruction is inference-rich

The June 14 causal chain combines direct evidence, Shinobu's self-report, newspaper absence, knowledge of V03 events, and Araragi's own counterfactual model. The statement `僕は忍に助けを求めることができなかった` is analytically powerful but remains **SI/NR**, not camera-level fact from Route X.

## N09-D — Oshino's letter: expert voice with explicit fallibility

Embedded letter voice is unusually authoritative, but Oshino explicitly states `僕にだって全てがわかるわけじゃあ、ない` and asks Araragi to repair wrong interpretations. Route mechanics are therefore **CD / specialist model**, strongly supported locally but not omniscient narration.

## N09-E — Route labels are explanatory models

`ルートＡ`, `ルートＸ`, `成功した阿良々木くん`, and parallel-world/game vocabulary are narratorial tools for making divergence legible. Do not silently convert them into an exhaustive cosmological taxonomy.

## Current V2 narrator principle after V09

**Corrigibility now applies to experts as well as protagonists.** A later narrator or specialist can provide superior information while remaining perspectival. The corpus should track improvement in explanation without declaring any speaker a transparent authorial mouthpiece.

### N11-C01 — darker voice does not equal truer narrator

The Kuchinawa `俺様` register releases content Nadeko already thought, especially in the classroom, but it remains a constructed/disinhibiting role. Treat its assertions as evidence of **available thought**, not total personhood.

### N11-C02 — self-lucidity can coexist with self-deception

Nadeko is unusually candid about some avoidance mechanisms (`ごめんなさい` as conversational termination; desire to be rescued) while constructing an entire supernatural story around a desire she will not directly name. “Unreliable” is therefore less useful than **selectively self-lucid / causally self-obscuring**.

### N11-C03 — `撫子 → 俺様 → 私` is a voice-state sequence, not an authenticity ladder

The volume experiments with first-person technologies. Do not encode `私` after apotheosis as automatically mature/authentic, nor `俺様` as the hidden essence beneath `撫子`.

### N11-C04 — narrator can mark her own fabrication boundary

The line `これ以上捏造する気はないです` before the §024 preview is itself a focalization signal. The narrator distinguishes remembered/reported event, admitted fabrication, and wished-for staging—though not always before those categories have done causal work.

## Governing narrator rule after V11

> **A narrator's willingness to incriminate herself increases evidentiary value but does not grant omniscience. Later/darker self-accusation must be audited with the same discipline as earlier self-exoneration.**

## V12 update — layered testimony, edited memory, and delayed farewell

### N12-A — Araragi's past-self model

V12 opens by allowing a past self to feel almost like another person while explicitly refusing responsibility-evasion. Narratorial discontinuity and ethical continuity therefore coexist: psychological foreignness does not cancel accountability for the acts of an earlier self.

### N12-B — Shinobu as embedded long-form narrator

Shinobu supplies the four-hundred-year history from within Araragi's present narration. Her testimony has exceptional first-person access but is not transparent archival fact. Two cautions are mandatory:

- she literally destroyed part of her own brain to force forgetting, then later recovered the memory;
- she often moralizes her earlier motives as selfish after the fact, while Araragi explicitly questions whether that self-condemnation is complete.

Treat concrete remembered events as NR with strong local weight; treat motive totalizations as CD/NR subject to counterreading.

### N12-C — Gaen as successful specialist model, not omniscient narrator

Gaen predicts and explains the present pursuit with unusually high success. Her account of `くらやみ` is therefore strong specialist evidence, but vocabulary such as `嘘`, `裁き`, and `罰` remains explanatory language inside the world. Her later comparison to an impersonal law without will is crucial to interpreting those moral metaphors.

### N12-D — Hachikuji retains motive authority

Hachikuji's account of why she remained after Mother's Day—because talking with Araragi and the others was fun—must not be overwritten by Gaen's category language. Gaen explains causal vulnerability; Hachikuji remains the strongest local witness to her own desire and decision.

### N12-E — Ougi frame is over-informed

The §033 interlocutor knows more than the visible conversation appears to justify and uses correction/ending/punishment language that rhymes with the darkness. This is a focalization warning, not identity proof.

### N12-F — loss, narration, and goodbye are distinct events

Hachikuji leaves in August. Araragi narrates the loss roughly four months later. Only then can he say `さようなら`. The gap is evidence: experience, tellability, and emotional closure have different timelines.

## Governing narrator rule after V12

> **Memory is an authored evidentiary medium, not a passive recording. Expertise, confession, and retrospective self-indictment all require source-state labels even when they are highly informative.**
