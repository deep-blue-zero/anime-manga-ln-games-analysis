# L07 — Body, Appetite, and Material Residue Ledger

V01 establishes that bodies and material environments are not decorative carriers of metaphor. They determine who can act, what can be known, and how supernatural events resolve.

## B01 — Hitagi's weight

- Visual body appears ordinary while measurable weight is radically abnormal.
- Disclosure occurs through touch: Araragi catches her.
- Missing `重さ` is linguistically entangled with `重み／思い／記憶`, but the literal physical condition remains real.
- Restoration changes actual body weight.
- V01 postscript comedy indicates supernatural restoration can be materially imprecise rather than symbolically elegant.

**Residue:** body normalizes; family/history do not.

## B02 — Araragi's regenerative body

- Stapler wound heals with abnormal speed.
- Body becomes evidentiary proof that Araragi knows supernatural abnormality firsthand.
- The altered body reduces the ordinary practical cost of some injury.
- V01 does **not** yet establish the full causal thesis that regeneration produces self-sacrificial morality; carry that as a later hypothesis.

**Residue:** supernatural body history remains unexplained locally and tied to silent Shinobu.

## B03 — Hachikuji's ghost body

Despite being dead/oddity, Hachikuji can:

- be seen by qualifying people;
- be heard;
- be touched;
- possess warmth/softness;
- bite and cause pain;
- cast a shadow;
- physically struggle.

This blocks any simple equation of “ghost” with immaterial hallucination.

**Residue:** after `ただいま`, she remains embodied enough for renewed encounter as a floating ghost.

## B04 — Stationery as bodily boundary technology

Hitagi's staplers, cutter, and other school supplies convert ordinary school objects into a portable threat system. Their significance is simultaneously:

- practical control;
- comic exaggeration;
- school-world materiality;
- bodily boundary enforcement.

Avoid reading every object as symbolic; the important V01 fact is that Hitagi has organized everyday material around not being approached.

## M01 — Erased houses

Two homes are materially gone:

- Hitagi's former family house has become a road.
- Hachikuji's mother's former house is an empty lot.

The volume therefore refuses physical restoration as emotional resolution. Memory/relation persist across material erasure.

## M02 — New roads as oddity intervention

Newly constructed streets are outside Hachikuji's older informational map and become the technical route by which the group reaches her destination.

This is the first entry in a future **social/material oddity ecology** ledger: mundane infrastructure can causally change supernatural possibility.

## Appetite status

No major appetite system is yet mature in V01. Do not back-project later vampire/food motifs into this volume beyond what the text explicitly supplies.

## V02 body/material updates

### B04 - Kanbaru's athletic body and beast left arm

- Athletic body is already a social identity: speed, jumping, basketball stardom.
- Left arm becomes physically nonhuman while the rest of the body remains Kanbaru's.
- Rainy Devil later uses Kanbaru's trained legs/footwork; supernatural force and human technique combine.
- Conscious bodily discipline inhibits full activation for several days.
- Araragi's regenerative body allows him to choose combat but does not prevent near-fatal deformation.
- Hitagi's arrival shows the moral danger of treating regenerative durability as permission for self-expenditure.

**Residue:** beast arm remains; basketball ends. Body loss changes vocation/social role.

### B05 - Nadeko's constricted body

- Scale-like marks from toes toward collarbone materially record invisible constriction.
- Curse threatens lethal progression toward face/head.
- Long clothes conceal marks; bodily shame affects disclosure.
- Removal of curse fully removes marks.

**Residue:** social causes and curse-return uncertainty remain after body normalizes.

### B06 - gaze and exposure

- Diagnostic need requires seeing Nadeko's skin, but scene construction uses near-nudity, bloomers, school swimsuit, and sexual commentary.
- Nadeko explicitly worries whether Araragi experiences erotic feeling while looking.
- Ritual requires sustained observation and includes a direct `watch me` request.

**V2 caution:** body-as-evidence and body-as-spectacle operate simultaneously; one does not excuse the other.

### B07 - poison and healing limits

- Snake injury disrupts Araragi's normal confidence in regeneration.
- Pain/toxin spreads while he continues fighting.
- Shinobu's later blood-drinking is used to boost recovery enough for ordinary function.

**V2 significance:** supernatural resilience is conditional rather than an unlimited moral resource.

### M02 - ruined shrine ground

- Soil/grass physically reveal movement of otherwise invisible snake during ritual field.
- The same oddity would have been harder/impossible for Araragi to track on asphalt/concrete or in the cram-school ruin.
- Environment therefore participates materially in what can be perceived and survived.

### Blood

V04 makes blood a multi-function material:
- food;
- rescue resource;
- transformation medium;
- master/thrall bond;

The final arrangement makes Araragi's body literal life-support infrastructure for the diminished vampire.

Restored Kiss-shot's human-eating is not symbolic shorthand. Guillotine Cutter's consumed body makes predation materially present and forces Araragi to confront consequences excluded from his first rescue fantasy.

### Araragi body as moral currency

V04 is the strongest source yet for the pattern: Araragi repeatedly imagines injury, blood, or death as what he can spend to solve moral problems. Hanekawa's injuries expose the relational externalities of this strategy.

### Kiss-shot bodily autonomy

Final settlement:
- near-total power loss;
- childlike diminished body;
- loss of name/voice status;
- inability to feed normally;

Oshino's pet analogy explicitly frames this as bodily domination, preventing sentimental erasure.

### Permanent residue

- partial vampirism;
- bite scars;
- mutual nutritional/ontological dependency;
- body-state changes that encode unresolved responsibility.

## V05 update — body, burden transfer, and residue

### Karen's fever

The `囲い火蜂` case is materially anchored in temperature, weakness, sweating, sleep disturbance, and impaired mobility. Whatever the genealogy, the consequence is bodily.

### Araragi's altered body as resource

Residual vampirism allows him to absorb part of Karen's burden at lower expected cost. This is the first clear case where his altered body becomes a **comparative-capacity resource** rather than simply death currency.

Ethical flag: capacity advantage does not itself settle consent or decision authority.

### Burden-transfer result

Only approximately one-third to one-half transfers. The partial result matters: rescue is not magical substitution. Karen remains afflicted; Araragi does not fully become the sole bearer.

### Social residue

Kaiki's departure does not reverse damaged peer relationships. Fire Sisters explicitly distinguish future-harm prevention from repair of existing injuries. Add permanent ledger category:

> **Material/social residue can outlive the oddity producer and the oddity event.**

### Comedy/body caution

The transfer uses a sibling kiss and is sexualized as taboo comedy. Diagnostic/ritual function and exploitative comic framing coexist; neither cancels the other.

## V06 update — immortal body, mundane appetite, and violence

### Tsukihi's regenerative body

V06 adds the strongest body-as-ontology case yet:

- catastrophic tissue loss can restore near-instantaneously;
- ordinary injuries heal slowly enough to preserve human plausibility;
- scars disappear;
- hair and nails grow unusually fast;
- metabolism/regeneration provides a material basis for long-observed quirks.

**B07-R01:** a body can be mechanically nonhuman while the narrative still treats personhood as an open ethical question.

### Immortality and the permissibility of force

Kagenui repeatedly applies lethal-level attacks to Araragi because regeneration means those blows are not final. The fight demonstrates that durable bodies alter violence norms: death-level force becomes dialogue, test, deterrence, or technique.

**B07-R02:** regenerative capacity is not morally neutral; it invites others to treat destruction as cheaper than it is psychologically or relationally.

### Araragi/Shinobu material interdependence

Araragi raises his vampiric capacity for the fight through blood exchange, then later returns blood to Shinobu to reduce his state and restore hers. Their relationship remains literally metabolic.

### Shinobu appetite

Mister Donut becomes more than lure/reward. Shinobu now wants to visit the shop, inspect choices, and pursue pleasure on her own initiative.

**B07-R03:** appetite can be a mundane form of self-directed participation rather than only predation.

### Hair as material change field

V06 clusters:

- Karen's severed ponytail;
- Hitagi's shorter hair after growth;
- Hachikuji's inability to physically change;
- Tsukihi's supernaturally rapid hair growth.

Do not impose one symbolic equation. Record hair as a recurring material surface where **change, fixity, biology, memory, and chosen presentation** intersect.

### Sexual-comedy boundary caution

The sibling/minor comedy in V06 remains intentionally provocative and self-aware. It should not be mined as transparent erotic truth. Where consent is violated, comedy does not neutralize the violation.

# V07 update — body, house, violence, and material residue

## Hanekawa's face as hidden family evidence

- father strikes Hanekawa hard enough that Araragi judges scarring likely;
- residual vampirism allows Araragi to heal the visible injury;
- Hanekawa says her parents likely would not notice the change anyway.

The body temporarily carries evidence of family violence; supernatural healing removes that evidence without changing the source condition.

## The house as materialized non-belonging

The Hanekawa residence looks ordinary externally, but there is **no room belonging to Hanekawa**. Her objects are dispersed through functional household locations—uniform, books, underwear, futon, charger, bag—without a concentrated personal space.

This is stronger than symbolic homelessness. It is a materially organized household in which approximately fifteen years of a girl's life leave almost no private trace.

## Araragi's body as tool

V07 escalates the material logic already visible in V03–V06. Araragi hides `心渡` in/along his own torso and effectively turns his body into a sheath/trap. His flesh becomes equipment.

**Longitudinal rule:** regeneration enables rescue tactics but also trains Araragi to imagine bodily integrity as expendable infrastructure.

## Shinobu's blood

Shinobu's blood restores Araragi after the near-fatal trap. This is another instance in which the Araragi–Shinobu bond is literally material: blood, proximity, regeneration, and survival are relationship media.

## Stress as quasi-material burden

Black Hanekawa converts accumulated stress into energy-draining action and later into something Shinobu can absorb/drain from the hybrid. V2 should retain the literal supernatural mechanics while also tracking the psychological vocabulary they materialize.


# V08 body/material updates

## B12 — Old Hanekawa house and fire

- Old house is materially organized as three parallel lives.
- Hanekawa minimizes physical traces of herself.
- Destruction makes her spontaneously call it `私の家`, showing attachment can survive non-belonging.
- Fire is both literal damage and tiger mechanic; the legal/world explanation later normalizes it as near-accidental ignition.

## B13 — Food taste / appetite preference

Hitagi notices Hanekawa's weak preference structure around food. V08 treats mundane taste as evidence that selfhood includes the capacity to prefer and reject rather than merely accept nutritionally adequate input.

## B14 — Hair residue

After integration, Hanekawa retains partly white/tiger-striped hair and dyes it for school. She interprets the daily act as communication with the selves she now carries. The body does not reset to pre-crisis purity.

## B15 — Crying

After romantic rejection Hanekawa cries openly. This is a material-emotional breakthrough: sorrow moves through the body without requiring another oddity split.

## B16 — First private room

Hanekawa asks parents for and receives a six-tatami room in the rental house. The series converts abstract “homecoming” into a concrete material boundary: key, door, room, belongings, and a place she can call `私達の場所`.

## Material rule after V08

> **Psychological integration in Monogatari is repeatedly made credible by bodily and architectural residue rather than by clean symbolic reset.**

# V09 update — bodies as rescue, enslavement, appetite, and route energy

## Araragi body — proportional rescue use

Araragi uses residual vampiric durability to absorb/prevent the truck collision that would kill child Hachikuji. Unlike several earlier self-sacrificial acts, this is a bounded redistribution of immediate physical risk toward the body best able to survive it.

**Caution:** his own inappropriate approach helped create the road danger, so bodily heroism does not erase intervention error.

## Kiss-shot眷属 body politics

Route X apocalypse turns vampirization into forced bodily/social dependence. Shinobu describes full眷属 as slave-like extensions rather than free community. The body becomes the medium through which bereavement is converted into domination.

## Appetite differentiation

V09 sharply distinguishes forms of consumption:

- donuts: ordinary pleasure;
- Araragi blood: established negotiated dependency;
- anonymous humans as route fuel: present Shinobu explicitly refuses;
- alternate Kiss-shot as route energy: offered by the alternate self as a final act.

This supports a cumulative thesis that ethical maturation often appears not as disappearance of appetite but as **differentiation, restraint, and consent around appetite**.

## Route X Kiss-shot body

Her ruined body carries failed immortality, failed suicide, and catastrophic power at once. The final conversion of that body into spiritual energy should be tracked as materially reparative but not automatically romanticized as a healthy suicide ideal.

# V11 update — body, gaze, hair, ingestion, and god materiality

## Nadeko touch/body boundary

V11 directly establishes aversion to touching/being touched, including discomfort with mixed body heat. Treat this as embodied vulnerability, not merely a cute behavioral sign.

## Bangs / face visibility

Long bangs function as a material social interface:

- reduce eye contact;
- obscure facial contradiction;
- support lowered-head/silence script.

Tsukihi's cutting of the bangs is therefore both symbolic exposure and a real bodily/property boundary violation.

## Talisman ingestion

Nadeko literally eats the talisman. The supernatural transition is carried through ingestion rather than remaining metaphorical or discursive.

**Material rule:** stories/belief can organize causation, but V11 still requires a material supernatural carrier for full apotheosis.

## Hair → snakes

Post-ingestion hair becomes an enormous field of white snakes described as part of `私`. Hair moves from **concealment** to **offensive supernatural embodiment**.

## Gaze inversion

Opening Nadeko avoids seeing/being seen. The §024 prospective god-image pulls all hair/bangs back and imagines her looking directly forward. This is a major body/gaze reversal, but the preview remains prospective rather than completed-event fact.

## Residue

No bodily restoration occurs by volume end. God-body status is the active unresolved condition.

## V12 body/material updates — 『鬼物語』

### Kiss-shot body loss and regeneration

The ancient darkness consumes roughly three quarters of Kiss-shot's body. Her later regeneration confirms extreme vampire bodily plasticity while also showing that the corrective phenomenon attacks material integrity before interpretation can catch up.

### First thrall: wrist → person

The first oddity killer survives as a right wrist gripping Kiss-shot. Kiss-shot later uses that remnant/blood to regenerate him into a vampire thrall.

**Material question:** bodily continuity from a fragment does not by itself resolve personal identity, consent, or whether the restored form is a life the person accepts.

### Blood as reproductive material

V12 distinguishes feeding from thrall creation again. Blood here is not merely appetite/sustenance; it is material for supernatural reproduction and coerced continuity.

### Hachikuji backpack / shell

The backpack remains a powerful material role-marker and memory object, but possession of the familiar shell is not itself an ontological switch that restores `迷い牛` function.

### Hand-holding at the farewell

The body records the non-possession problem before dialogue does: Hachikuji has stopped gripping back while Araragi continues to hold her. Release must therefore become a physical act, not only an intellectual concession.

### Final kiss and consent-direction

The final kiss reverses the usual comedic pursuit grammar because Hachikuji authors the action and its framing. That makes the moment materially important without retroactively neutralizing the series' earlier sexual-comedy consent problems.

# V14 body/material update — 『憑物語』

Araragi's body no longer appears in mirrors outside the former ordinary active-vampire pattern. The symptom is materially real and psychologically destabilizing.

Kagenui's finger-breaking test reveals body-repair capacity linked to Araragi's vampireward baseline. The body has learned an emergency state.

No current cure is available. V14 therefore changes the body ledger from “transformation used and reversed” to **permanent accumulated residue managed prospectively**.

Araragi's refusal of vampire power also restricts Shinobu's available combat body. Their material capacities are coupled.

# V17 body/appetite/material-residue update — 『終物語（中）』

## First-thrall ash/body cycle
Sunlight destroys the current vampire body but inherited immortality repeatedly rebuilds from ash. Body persistence exceeds conscious intention.

## Environmental feeding
At Kita-Shirahebi, gathered `よくないもの` becomes minute food/material enabling stronger reconstruction over ~15 years.

Humanoid/armored first-thrall form is assembled from heterogeneous supernatural residue. Talisman contact causes component forms to spill outward before losing coherent person-shape.

Armor may function as more than clothing; Ougi treats it as possible `血肉/骨身` and weapon material. Disposition remains UA.

Weapon is made from oddity-killer body material, carried internally by Shinobu, contested by first thrall, ritualized by Gaen, and ultimately handled by Araragi. Body and tool ontology interpenetrate.

## Kanbaru drain/injury
`エナジードレイン` turns Kanbaru's weakening body into evidence of the armor's growth. Later Shinobu confrontation produces visible bodily injury without negating Kanbaru's agency.

## Araragi deliberately remains near-human for duel
He rejects restoring pairing/boost for competitive advantage because he wants to become Shinobu's support. Material vulnerability is chosen but remains ethically risky.

## Shinobu consumption
Eating first thrall reabsorbs vampire-derived body/material and ends recurrence. Appetite here is neither ordinary feeding nor purely symbolic reconciliation; it is the physical mechanism of finality.

## Happiness coda and immortal time
The final body/time metaphor treats Araragi/Shinobu as having enough time for corpses to rot to ash—until perhaps their time itself becomes finite. Material decay language remains tied to relation and ending.

# V18 body / material-residue updates — 『終物語（下）』

## B-Araragi — human reset
Gaen/Teori's operation strips the accumulated vampire condition by killing the hybrid and returning the human component. This is not a moral purification; it is a category/boundary intervention with irreversible bodily history behind it.

## B-Shinobu — full body after severance
Complete separation restores Shinobu as full Kiss-shot. Her later return to Araragi's shadow is therefore not merely mechanical persistence of the old bond; V18 first demonstrates a physically independent state.

## B-Araragi — right arm loss
When Araragi tackles Ougi away from the darkness, his restored human body loses the right arm and does not regenerate vampirically. Rescue now carries ordinary irreversible human bodily cost.

## B-Teori — puppet media
The V14 destroyed body is retrospectively classified as a puppet controlled by a death-side core. Body, agent, and person are therefore non-identical in Teori's specialist ontology.

## B-Hachikuji — extraction before stabilization
Hachikuji is physically pulled from hell before any sustainable living-world role is assigned. Later deity status stabilizes the returned person but does not change the coercive bodily character of the extraction itself.

## B-Ougi — composite material history
Ougi's body/person arises through psychological impulse plus accumulated supernatural/material residues and social forms. The case further weakens any simple body = essence equation.
