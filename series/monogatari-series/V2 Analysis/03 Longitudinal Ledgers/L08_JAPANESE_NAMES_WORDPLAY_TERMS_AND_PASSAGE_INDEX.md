# L08 — Japanese Names, Wordplay, Terms, and Passage Index

This ledger records language that materially affects characterization, oddity mechanics, relationships, or translation. Diegetic etymologies are not automatically treated as historical linguistic fact.

## T01 — `重さ / 重み / 思い / 記憶 / しがらみ`

**Arc:** ひたぎクラブ

- `重さ` — literal measurable physical weight.
- `重み` — heaviness, gravity, burden.
- `思い` — thought/feeling/attachment.
- `記憶` — memory.
- `しがらみ` — entanglement, binding ties.

**Analytical use:** the crab/god case operates inside this semantic field. Translation should preserve the distinction rather than flatten all terms into “weight” or all into “feelings.”

**Key locator:** V01 — ひたぎクラブ — 006 — Hitagi requests return of `気持ち／思い／記憶／重み`.

## T02 — `沈黙と無関心`

**Speaker:** Senjougahara Hitagi

Her requested condition from Araragi is not kindness, secrecy alone, or sympathy but “silence and indifference.” The phrase anchors her initial relational defense.

**Locator:** V01 — ひたぎクラブ — 003.

## T03 — `優しさも──敵対行為と看做すわよ`

**Locator:** V01 — ひたぎクラブ — 003.

## T04 — `助ける / 助かる`

**Speaker cluster:** Oshino / Araragi / later volume-wide ethics.

Oshino's grammar distinguishes transitive “save/help” from intransitive “be saved/recover.” V01 supplies the exact foundational formulation, but Mayoi complicates any doctrine that recovery is solitary.

**Key locator:** V01 — ひたぎクラブ — 004.

# Voice baseline — Hitagi

High-frequency stylistic cluster to track longitudinally:

- `～わ`
- `～のよ`
- `～かしら`
- `～て頂戴`
- controlled complete sentences
- mock-formal threats
- `阿良々木くん`

**V01 function:** polished femininity is used as control/boundary; later within the same volume the same precision becomes romantic negotiation.

# Voice baseline — Hachikuji

- polite register toward an older teen;
- intentional/apparent lexical errors;
- misnaming ritual;
- pedantry and idiom play;
- abrupt serious philosophical statements inside comedy.

# Voice baseline — Hanekawa

- correct, calm, explanatory;
- `何でもは知らないわよ。知ってることだけ` as self-limiting epistemic signature.

# Translation-sensitive principle opened by V01

Translate **function as well as denotation**. Hitagi's hostility is not merely what she says; it depends on the contrast between polished feminine control and violent semantic content. Hachikuji's relation to Araragi likewise depends on recurrent error/correction rhythm, not only literal sentence meaning.

## V02 language additions

### T07 - `omote / ura` (front / back)

**Arc:** Suruga Monkey

The Rainy Devil reads the `back` of a wish, but the climax explicitly restores the `front` as equally necessary contractual content. Do not translate the pair automatically into clinical conscious/unconscious or true/false.

**Analytical use:** hidden negative motive is real without becoming sole authentic self.

**Locator:** V02 - Suruga 006 - front/back double-bind.

### T08 - Monkey's Paw / Rainy Devil

**Arc:** Suruga Monkey

Kanbaru's label foregrounds ironic unintended fulfillment. Oshino's reclassification foregrounds a devil that exploits negative desire in exchange for soul/life/body.

**Analytical use:** naming changes moral causal interpretation.

### T09 - `Koyomi-onii-chan`

**Arc:** Nadeko Snake

Nadeko retains a childhood older-brother address for Araragi that he has largely forgotten.

**Analytical use:** relation asymmetry; address term assigns a role before Araragi reconstructs the person.

### T10 - third-person `Nadeko`

**Arc:** Nadeko Snake

Nadeko refers to herself by name in emotionally exposed speech.

**V02 interpretation:** younger/cute/role-bound speech effect is supportable; later self-performance thesis remains OPEN.

### T11 - `watch me` marker

**Arc:** Nadeko Snake ritual

Nadeko asks Araragi to watch her carefully. Literal ritual monitoring and charged being-seen language coexist.

**Longitudinal route:** later Nadeko gaze/art/authorship analysis.

### T12 - curse return / two holes

**Arc:** Nadeko Snake climax

Snake's departure becomes return-to-sender logic, linked to the proverb that cursing another digs two holes.

**Analytical use:** externality and moral residue of curse removal.

### T13 - `snake tail` / superfluous explanation

**Arc:** Nadeko Snake epilogue

Araragi explicitly labels the possible second-sender theory unsupported speculation, then ends with the idiom/pun that the explanation is a "snake tail" -- superfluous.

**Analytical use:** metanarrative warning against explanatory closure.

### T14 - Hitagi's asymmetrical answer

**Arc:** Suruga Monkey climax

Hitagi flatly states she does not like Kanbaru to the same degree, then asks her to stay nearby and apologizes for the delay.

**Analytical use:** direct speech refuses fake reciprocity while preserving relationship.

## T19 — `私は変わらない`

Hanekawa's declared self-program: whatever her household background, she decided she would not change because of it.

**Use:** resilience/identity defense that can harden into prohibition on ordinary change.

## T20 — `いい子になろうとした`

Hanekawa describes goodness as something she deliberately tried to become from childhood.

**Use:** directly supports over-adaptation without implying fake virtue.

## T21 — `何でもは知らないわよ──何にも、知らない`

V03 fracture of Hanekawa's familiar epistemic catchphrase.

**Use:** under stress, controlled modest omniscience collapses into declared unknowing. Track later self-narration and knowledge identity.

## T22 — `恩人 / 恩返し / 友情`

Araragi's lexical field for Hanekawa becomes a relationship mechanism. He explicitly ranks repayment above friendship; Hanekawa's unguarded voice calls that lonely.

**Use:** gratitude as positive-valence distancing.

---

## T23 — `表 / 裏 / 裏面`

V03 calls Black Hanekawa a rear/reverse side. Carry forward V02 rule: `裏` is not automatically “more true” than `表`.

---

## T24 — `障り猫 / 触り猫`

Touch-based energy drain activates wordplay between harmful/afflicting cat and touching cat.

**Use:** name, mechanic, and bodily contact converge.

---

## T25 — `怪異`

Black Hanekawa explicitly builds an argument from the semantic sense of “strange/different.”

**Translation note:** a fixed English franchise term can obscure the ordinary Japanese semantic play being used in the ontology argument.

---

## T26 — `助けて……忍`

Direct request by Araragi at the climax.

**Use:** minimal language with maximal structural importance; direct counterpoint to theoretical `助ける／助かる` discourse.

---

## T27 — `お人よし`

Collective final label for Oshino.

**Use:** reconciles anti-savior rhetoric with repeated helping behavior; kindness without ownership.

---

## T28 — `本物`

Araragi/Hitagi apply `本物` to Hanekawa's frightening competence/genuineness.

**Caution:** praise can become dehumanizing idealization. Track `本物` against later fake/real discourse.

### Translation-sensitive note — `傷物`

Do not flatten to a purely sentimental “wounded souls.” `傷物` can carry the sense of something damaged/marred as an object or good. The closing plural `傷物達` deliberately makes Araragi and Kiss-shot beings whose damaged state is constitutive, not merely a hurt awaiting cure.

`僕はお前を、助けない` answers Kiss-shot's request that killing her count as `助ける`. The force depends on repeated rescue vocabulary across the volume; isolated translation can lose the semantic argument.

The title encourages “phoenix,” but Shinobu explicitly pushes back against the grand Western/peacock image and roots the case in `しでの鳥` / hototogisu language. Translation should preserve that the label is partly a **reframing**, not the complete indigenous category.

# V08 language additions

## T31 — `戻る / 帰る / ただいま`

- Opening household language: `戻る` explicitly contrasted with `帰る`.
- Black Hanekawa later gains `帰る場所` / `帰る家`.
- Final room scene culminates in Hanekawa's first `ただいま` and `やっと帰ってこれたんだ`.

**Analytical use:** do not flatten all as generic “return.” The lexical transition tracks earned home/belonging.

## T32 — `白い / 白々しい / 白無垢 / 潔白`

A white semantic field evolves from apparent purity to excessive/uncanny blankness and finally rejection of innocence as achievable identity.

Key phrases:
- tiger: `白くて──白々しい`
- Hitagi: `白過ぎる──白無垢過ぎる`
- coda: `潔白なんてありえない`

## T33 — `焼きもちを焼く`

Ordinary idiom for jealousy supplies literal fire semantics and allows Hanekawa to recognize `嫉妬` as the tiger's affective basis.

## T34 — `苛虎`

Hanekawa relates the name to `苛政は虎よりも猛し`, while Hitagi proposes alternate association. Gaen predicted the name in advance. Treat as dialogic naming scaffold, not exhaustive etymological essence.

## T35 — `灰色の大人`

`黒も白も併せて呑める。灰色の大人になりたい。`

V2 use: adulthood as capacity to contain contradiction/impurity rather than purify the self.

## T36 — `本物 / 本体 / 主人格 / 主導権`

The coda denies a hierarchy in which one part is authentic original, main body, principal personality, or permanent controller: `すべてが私`.

## T37 — `キャスティングボード`

Hanekawa's inability to ask for help includes not wanting to surrender decisive control over her life. This term links self-authorship to control anxiety.

## T38 — `何でもは知らない / 私は何でも知っている`

Gaen's formula directly pressures the established Hanekawa knowledge refrain. The contrast is characterization and epistemic theater, not merely information quantity.

## Signal-color motif

V09 opens through Ougi's red/green traffic-light discourse. Araragi later recalls the omitted yellow state as what her caution should have indicated. Add as a formal-language motif:

- red = stop/danger convention;
- green = go/safety convention;
- all-red = paradoxically safest intersection interval;
- universal green = dangerous;
- yellow = caution / transition / nonbinary judgment.

Do not over-systematize the colors into fixed character symbols; their strongest use is epistemic and ethical rather than heraldic.

## Mijikana title/function lock

`短物語` (“short stories”) is analytically apt not because the stories are minor, but because short form isolates local states. The collection's own utility depends on resisting a single totalizing chronological or identity narrative.
