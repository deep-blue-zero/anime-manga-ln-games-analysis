# L03 — Oddity Case, Rule, and Residue Ledger

## Case O01 — Hitagi weight-crab / god complex

| Field | V01 record |
|---|---|
| Human subject | Senjougahara Hitagi |
| Ontological class | oddity/god/crab-name complex; not stabilized to one simple animal morphology |
| Observable condition | body retains normal appearance but almost all expected physical weight is absent |
| Pre-oddity social cause | severe illness; mother's religious fixation; cult exploitation; attempted sexual assault; maternal betrayal; financial/family collapse |
| Human desire / transaction | relief from `重み／思い／しがらみ` and associated memory-burden |
| Linguistic substrate | `重さ`, `重み`, `思い`, `記憶`, crab/god/name variants |
| Specialist account | Oshino supplies variant folklore/etymology and rejects simple savior role |
| Decisive agency | Hitagi asks for return of her own weight/memory/thought and accepts carrying it |
| Literal resolution | physical weight restored |
| Social/psychological residue | mother does not return; house/family history remains lost; concealment habits and oddity knowledge persist; Araragi relationship begins |
| V2 caution | do not reduce to “trauma metaphor”; do not universalize suffering as duty |

### Rule propositions opened

- Oddities may answer or participate in a human wish without being framed as straightforward malicious attack.
- Semantic language around the oddity can be causally/interpretively important.
- Resolution can restore a supernatural condition while leaving social history unchanged.

## Case O02 — Hachikuji Mayoi / `迷い牛`

| Field | V01 record |
|---|---|
| Oddity-person | Hachikuji Mayoi |
| Ontological class | ghostly child / `迷い牛`; snail/cow/name associations are conceptual rather than fixed morphology |
| Origin | dies in traffic accident while trying to reach estranged mother on Mother's Day |
| Observer qualifier | living person has a reason not to go home |
| Manifestation | qualifying observer can see/hear/touch her; nonqualifying Hitagi cannot |
| Mechanism | following the lost child obstructs return; disengaging permits the living follower to go home |
| Informational limit | lacks ordinary accumulation of newer spatial knowledge; contemporary road changes can defeat navigation effect |
| Self-protective behavior | tells encountered people she dislikes them / bites / resists following, plausibly discouraging entanglement |
| Specialist workaround | use newly constructed roads outside the ghost's older informational map |
| Practical executor | Hitagi's local geography + Araragi's accompaniment + Hachikuji's goal |
| Literal resolution | reaches former mother-home site, says `ただいま`, disappears from earthbound-lost condition |
| Residue | returns next day as self-described floating ghost; personhood/relation continue in altered oddity state |

### Rule propositions opened

- An oddity can be **person + mechanism** simultaneously.
- Some oddities manifest relationally/observationally without being imaginary.
- The “victim” of a mechanism may differ from the person whose unresolved desire created/constitutes the oddity.
- Safe disengagement from an oddity is not necessarily equivalent to ethically resolving the oddity-person's problem.
- Material urban change can alter supernatural possibility.

V03 supports neither pure possession nor pure psychologization. Black Hanekawa is best modeled as **distributed/hybrid agency**:

`Hanekawa pressure + oddity catalyst/mechanics + shared knowledge + distinct reductive strategy`.

### Rule O07-R2 — symptom removal is not stressor removal

The narrative explicitly states this after Shinobu's intervention. This is one of the strongest residue rules in the corpus so far.

### Rule O07-R3 — hidden/rear-side material is partial

Carry V02's Kanbaru rule forward: `裏` is real without monopolizing truth. Black Hanekawa can reveal disowned pressure and still not be “the real Hanekawa.”

## V04 update — vampire / thrall system and professional ecology

### Case V04-A — Kiss-shot Acerola-orion Heart-under-blade

- Class: vampire / high-order oddity.
- Observable traits: blood dependence, regeneration, extreme strength, sunlight vulnerability, variable body age/power state, thrall creation, master/thrall command relation.
- Social ecology: hunted by specialized vampire hunters; negotiable through Oshino's specialist field.
- Moral ecology: human bodies are materially food to restored vampire; V04 does not reduce this to metaphor.

### Case V04-B — Araragi thrall state

- Origin: Kiss-shot stops ordinary feeding and makes Araragi a thrall rather than killing him.
- Consequences: vampiric body, appetite/norm instability, regenerative power, subordinate relation, later partial-human residue.
- Key rule: feeding and thrall-making are distinct actions; Oshino interprets thralling as a life-preserving choice that costs Kiss-shot recovery.

### Oddity-rule additions

1. **Oddity embodiment can alter normative perception.** Araragi fears that human persons may become legible as food.
2. **Oddity dependence is material.** The final Kiss-shot state requires Araragi's blood for survival.
3. **Resolution can be engineered incapacity rather than cure.** The final settlement prevents predation largely by stripping capacity.
4. **Oddity professional ecology exists early.** hunters, negotiators, fees, promises, and trust form a proto-institutional system.
5. **Observer-dependent meaning does not imply ontological unreality.** Araragi explicitly distinguishes meanings assigned by observers while the vampire's body and appetite remain causal.

### Residue

- Araragi remains partly vampiric.
- Kiss-shot survives radically diminished and unnamed.
- both remain biologically/causally dependent.
- bite marks and blood exchange become persistent relational residue.
- humanity retains nonzero risk.

### Anti-reduction rule strengthened

V04 decisively blocks a universal “oddities are internal psychological metaphors” theory. Kiss-shot is an autonomous ancient predator/person whose relationship with Araragi becomes psychologically meaningful without ceasing to be materially supernatural.

## V05 update — `囲い火蜂`, rumor ecology, and false genealogy

### Case O05 — `囲い火蜂`

| Field | V05 state |
|---|---|
| Host / afflicted | 阿良々木火憐 |
| Apparent trigger | Kaiki encounter / touch-based operation |
| Material symptom | extreme persistent fever and weakness |
| Shinobu model | hornet oddity remembered from Oshino lore; supposedly linked to historical epidemic |

| Intervention | improvised transfer of part of burden to Araragi |
| Outcome | fever reduced; later recovery within days |

### Rule additions

**O-R08 — False origin does not erase current mechanism.** Araragi's `出自がどうであろうが` response is a key anti-genealogical rule: a fabricated story can become causally operative.

**O-R09 — Social rumor and supernatural realization are separable layers.** Malicious charms damage relationships even when no oddity manifests; supernatural mechanisms may emerge inside that social field rather than replacing it.

**O-R10 — `舞台裏` knowledge is not a master key.** Hachikuji explicitly warns that oddity knowledge can increase what remains unknown.

**O-R11 — Burdens can sometimes be redistributed.** Karen's bee condition is partially transferable into Araragi's altered body; oddity effects are not always host-locked.

### Residue

Stopping Kaiki prevents new deliberate distribution but does not repair already damaged peer relations. V05 therefore strengthens the ledger distinction between **oddity cessation** and **social/material residue**.

## V06 update — `しでの鳥`, constructed personhood, and immortal residue

### Case O06 — `しでの鳥` / hototogisu-associated immortal oddity

| Field | V06 state |
|---|---|
| Current incarnation / social identity | 阿良々木月火 |
| Entry mechanism | `托卵` into a human pregnancy; strict target described as the mother/pregnancy rather than a later possessed child |
| Core identity | `死なぬこと` — not dying |
| Human camouflage | constitutive adaptation; lives from birth as human-like person rather than toggled disguise |
| Aging | ages normally enough to pass through human lifespan |
| Death | apparent human death followed by reincarnation |
| Regeneration | scales sharply with injury severity; lethal injury triggers near-immediate restoration |
| Ordinary markers | scars disappear; hair/nails/metabolism unusually fast |
| Harm profile | Shinobu classifies it as broadly harmless in itself |
| Removal/cure | no separable affliction-removal model established; extermination would destroy the current person |
| V06 outcome | Kagenui grants exception; Tsukihi remains unchanged and uninformed |

### O-R11 — person and oddity may be coextensive

V01–V05 mostly allowed some conceptual separation among afflicted person, oddity mechanism, and symptom. V06 introduces a harder category: the oddity is not merely attached to Tsukihi; its incarnation and the socially continuous Tsukihi are coextensive from birth.

### O-R12 — harmless oddity does not imply specialist non-intervention

Kagenui explicitly says she can ignore `無害認定`. Oddity mechanics and specialist policy are therefore separate layers. A correct diagnosis does not entail a unique normative response.

### O-R13 — adaptation can conceal by ordinary embodiment

The `しでの鳥` does not hide through invisibility or deception strategy. Its immortality modulates itself so human life remains plausible. Small injuries heal slowly; catastrophic injury forces the concealed rule into visibility.

### O-R14 — origin narrative must not replace agency analysis

`托卵` and replacement-child stories tempt characters to treat Tsukihi as a deceiver. Yet no conscious deception by Tsukihi is established. The ethical category “fraud” therefore requires a separate agency analysis.

### Case O06-B — Ononoki Yotsugi

V06 locally establishes Ononoki as a powerful oddity/`式神` partnered with Kagenui and named/bound through a specialist network. Later construction details remain **OPEN** until their own primary-source revelation.

### Residue

- Tsukihi remains immortal/reincarnating.
- family does not know except Araragi/Shinobu.
- Kagenui's exception is policy, not a metaphysical cure.
- disclosure risk remains active.
- specialist awareness now exists beyond the Araragi circle.

# V07 update — Case O07: sawarineko → Black Hanekawa

## Case O07-A — standard `障り猫`

| Field | V07 record |
|---|---|
| Initial object | road-killed white cat |
| Contact condition | Hanekawa buries it after encountering the corpse |
| Folkloric template | low-level possession/role-reversal tale associated with sympathy/contact and `猫をかぶる` language |
| Initial host | Hanekawa Tsubasa |
| Expected strength | low / ordinary by specialist standards |
| Unexpected amplification | Hanekawa's retained consciousness, knowledge, and abnormal adaptive structure |
| Initial apparent endpoint | parental attack/draining; Oshino later says standard possession had effectively ended |

## Case O07-B — Black Hanekawa as a new hybrid

According to Oshino's post-crisis reconstruction, Hanekawa's consciousness returns after the first possession phase and **pulls the departing cat back into herself**. The later Black Hanekawa is therefore not merely the original `障り猫` wearing Hanekawa's body.

The hybrid:

- possesses Hanekawa's knowledge;
- does **not** automatically possess transparent access to every feeling;
- exists to discharge/embody accumulated stress;
- drains energy from others as `憂さ晴らし`;
- is restrained enough to avoid most killings, suggesting continued internal constraint;
- can formulate solutions Hanekawa herself cannot consciously permit, including retaliatory violence against the household.

### O-R15 — hybrid agency defeats the possession/true-self binary

The case requires simultaneous recognition of:

- external oddity causation;
- Hanekawa's victimization;
- Hanekawa's active re-incorporation of the cat;
- Black Hanekawa's distinct operational agency;
- real responsibility for subsequent harm;
- structural family pressure.

No single term—“possessed,” “evil alter,” “true Hanekawa,” or “pure victim”—is sufficient.

### O-R16 — symptom disposal is not cure

Black Hanekawa can reduce stress through violent discharge, but the household remains unchanged and the stress can recur. The story explicitly separates oddity-event completion from problem resolution.

### O-R17 — oddity explanation can function as explanatory mercy

Oshino observes that “oddity,” “Black Hanekawa,” or even quasi-dual-personality language can provide a less crushing explanatory container than the bare proposition that family stress drove an ordinary girl into violence. Supernatural classification can therefore redistribute narrative responsibility without making the supernatural unreal.

### O-R18 — representation is not total ontology

When Araragi suggests “family itself” may be Hanekawa's oddity, Oshino pushes back through a map/territory-style analogy. A useful metaphor does not become a total causal explanation merely because it organizes evidence well.

### Residue after V07

- household structure remains unchanged;
- Hanekawa loses/does not retain relevant Black Hanekawa memory;
- Black Hanekawa becomes a named internal oddity configuration capable of recurrence;
- Araragi now knows the family facts but still lacks a durable social solution;
- Shinobu's ability to drain the hybrid becomes part of the Araragi–Shinobu supernatural relation.

- Repeated oddity use can become a **learned coping technology**.
- A coping oddity can both reduce acute distress and perpetuate the inability to feel directly.

- An oddity may be resolved by **integration/return** rather than destruction or exorcism.
- Linguistic recognition (`焼きもち`, `苛虎`) can be necessary to understand a case without being a complete ontology.
- Human/oddity boundaries can be relationally and psychologically porous without becoming meaningless in mechanics.

# V09 update — route transfer, Hachikuji survival, and Route X vampirization

## O-V09-01 — Hachikuji case under prevented-death conditions

**Baseline change:** Araragi prevents the Mother's Day traffic death and helps child Hachikuji reach her mother's address. In that route she does not become the familiar `迷い牛`/ghost case and survives into young adulthood.

**Analytical consequence:** oddity removal/prevention can remove not only pathology but also later relationships that formed through the oddity. Those later goods do not justify the original harm.

## O-V09-02 — Route/world transfer model

Oshino's letter proposes that Shinobu's “pastward time travel” is better described as:

- `時空間移動ではなく異空間移動`
- `世界から世界への移動`
- `ルートからルートへの移動`

**Confidence:** strong local specialist model, explicitly provisional because Oshino disclaims total knowledge.

## O-V09-03 — Route X Kiss-shot mass眷属 event

After Route X Araragi's inferred death, Kiss-shot regains extreme power and attempts mass眷属 creation. The event is framed as grief-driven `自暴自棄 / 八つ当たり`, not ordinary feeding.

**Rule:** full vampire眷属 are described as coercively dependent/slave-like rather than reciprocal community. Mass multiplication is therefore the dark inverse of the slowly negotiated Araragi–Shinobu partnership.

## O-V09-04 — failed-vampire / zombie residue

Route X contains zombie-like humans rather than ordinary full vampires. Oshino/Shinobu explanation links this to failed眷属 mechanics and Kiss-shot's unsuccessful self-destruction. Preserve as CD/high-confidence local explanation, not universal vampire law until cross-checked later.

## O-V09-05 — route-energy conversion

Route X Kiss-shot offers her own remaining supernatural body as sufficient spiritual energy for one route transfer. Present Shinobu accepts by consuming her.

**Open:** whether this is repeatable mechanics or a singular full-vampire-energy case.

## Oddity ontology update

V09 adds **counterfactual absence** to the ontology ledger: what does not become an oddity can still matter structurally because the absent oddity removes a relationship and thereby changes later human action.

**Rule O-R22:** oddity ecology can be simultaneously psychological, narrative, spatial, material, and institutional.

## V12 oddity/system update — 『鬼物語』

### Case O13-A — `くらやみ` / nonexistence / corrective law

Gaen explicitly denies that the phenomenon is an ordinary oddity and calls it `非存在`. It has no stable proper name, no free consciousness, and no intention; labels such as `くらやみ`, `神隠し`, `バランサー`, `イレイザー`, and `ブラックホール` are human explanatory handles.

Observed/modelled behavior:

- appears when an oddity continues under a category/role it is no longer actually enacting;
- can erase witnesses as part of correction;
- can consume bodies and supernatural beings without ordinary combat grammar;
- ceases or pauses when the mismatch is contingently removed;
- cannot meaningfully be “defeated” in the ordinary antagonist sense;
- is compared by Gaen to an impersonal law such as gravity.

**Rule O-R23 — law is not moral personhood:** causal correction does not imply desert, benevolence, malice, or cosmic ethical judgment.

### Case O13-B — Kiss-shot's temporary god-role mismatch

Kiss-shot's arrival produces a real miracle-like rain effect and real worship, but she explicitly performs the social role of god without intending permanent transformation into one. Gaen's model identifies this mismatch—not mere popularity—as the ancient trigger.

**Rule O-R24 — recognition can make a role causally consequential without completing ontological transformation.**

### Case O13-C — genuine change is permitted

Gaen's key distinction is `偽るのは駄目だけど変わるのは、いい`. Present Shinobu's weakened condition is not treated as a false Kiss-shot role because she has genuinely changed.
