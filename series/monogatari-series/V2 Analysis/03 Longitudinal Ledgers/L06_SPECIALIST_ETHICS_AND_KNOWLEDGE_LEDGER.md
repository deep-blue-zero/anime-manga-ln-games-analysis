# L06 — Specialist Ethics and Knowledge Ledger

## S01 — Oshino Meme, V01 baseline

### Core self-definition
Oshino rejects the proposition that he simply “saves” people:

- `助ける？ そりゃ無理だ`
- `きみが勝手に一人で助かるだけ`
- later formulation: he will lend force rather than claim salvation.

### V2 correction to slogan-only reading

V01 shows at least six additional components of his practice.

1. **Diagnosis / naming** — supplies oddity classes, variants, conditions, and semantic structures.
2. **Balance** — treats oddities as beings/phenomena to be mediated rather than presumptive enemies.
3. **Agency** — refuses to substitute his will for the afflicted person's decisive choice.
4. **Boundary** — rebukes Araragi for socially irresponsible mediation, including sending Hitagi alone to an adult man she does not independently know/trust.
5. **Selective disclosure** — does not automatically volunteer every relevant fact; answers depend on what is asked and what action is being chosen.
6. **Practical intervention** — can restrain entities, arrange rituals, charge fees, and provide technical exploits such as the Mayoi new-road workaround.

### Knowledge-state labels

| Claim type | V01 treatment |
|---|---|
| Directly observed mechanics | can approach TF when independently shown in event sequence |
| Oshino's classification | CD unless corroborated |
| Oshino's etymology | CD; some explicitly described as his own theory |
| Moral doctrine | character ethic, not series-wide law |
| Prediction of Araragi behavior | strongly supported locally but still character inference |

### Personhood treatment
He does not treat oddity status as sufficient reason for extermination in V01. His Mayoi solution recognizes mechanism while anticipating Araragi's attachment to the person.

### Violence
Capable of physical/supernatural restraint, but violence is not framed as the default endpoint of diagnosis.

### Money
Professional exchange is explicit in Hitagi's case; Hitagi contrasts Oshino's paid service with Araragi's voluntary intervention. This does not by itself make Oshino mercenary; it establishes that professional and personal care occupy different relational categories.

### Failure / risk mode opened by V01
His distance can look cold or opaque. Because he refuses to explain unasked things, other characters may encounter truths only at the point of consequence. Later volumes should test whether this restraint protects agency or sometimes becomes avoidable harm.

## Specialist-system questions opened

- Is “balance” stable across different oddity classes?
- How much should a specialist disclose when the client cannot know what to ask?
- How do professional boundaries change when minors are involved?
- Does charging money clarify responsibility or distort it?
- Which later specialists share, reject, or invert Oshino's personhood assumptions?

## S01 - Oshino Meme, V02 update

### 7. Naming as responsibility allocation

Oshino's Monkey's Paw -> Rainy Devil correction changes more than taxonomy. The former model makes violence sound like an unintended ironic consequence of a clean wish; the latter says the negative back-side belongs to the wish structure itself.

**V2 rule:** specialist naming can be ethical interpretation because causal classification changes who is responsible for what.

### 8. Outcome specification before "help"

When Kanbaru asks whether Oshino will help, he asks what she actually wants help *with*: wish fulfillment, cancellation, arm restoration, and so on are different target states.

**V2 rule:** a specialist cannot ethically optimize an undefined outcome.

### 9. Proportional intervention / ecological balance

At the ruined shrine Oshino prefers a weak redirection over dramatic cleansing. Sudden purification can create a supernatural vacuum or shift harm elsewhere.

**V2 rule:** minimal effective disturbance may be safer than maximal power.

### 10. Expert observer/intervention effect

Oshino says his own specialist status would stimulate the accumulated phenomena, while Araragi/Kanbaru are weak enough to perform the job without provoking full defensive response.

**V2 rule:** expertise can alter the system and therefore create reasons *not* to intervene directly.

### 11. Debt and exchange

Kanbaru's shrine work settles her debt; Araragi's work also settles a large prior debt. Oshino's practice continues to use explicit exchange as a boundary against unlimited dependency.

### 12. Strategic severity

Oshino knowingly allows Kanbaru to experience distress at the shrine as part of settling accounts. This prevents idealization of his balance ethic as simple benevolence.

### Knowledge-state cautions opened by V02

- Oshino knows enough about `Gaen Tooe` to react, but publication-local relevance is explicitly denied/withheld.
- His shrine ecology is a diegetic theory; V2 should not convert every statement into objective cosmology without later corroboration.
- His correct ritual for the reported snake case does not prevent failure when the report itself is incomplete (two snakes vs one).

## S01 — Oshino Meme, V03 expansion

### Stress/catalyst model

Oshino distinguishes:

1. external oddity catalyst/medium;
2. host person;
3. accumulated stress;
4. stressor;
5. manifestation;
6. containment/removal;
7. recurrence if stress remains.

This is a more differentiated intervention model than “exorcise the monster.”

### Epistemic restraint

Oshino says he has clues about the current stress source but avoids asserting a delicate personal motive without confidence. This adds a principle:

> **specialist suspicion is not sufficient warrant to define another person's inner life.**

### Preventive monitoring

He had arranged a warning mechanism around recurrence/headache but expected the prior equilibrium to last much longer. This is explicit evidence that specialists can misestimate risk.

### Withdrawal

At V03 end Oshino has removed his belongings and left town.

Evidence separation:

- **TF:** departure occurred without note.
- **NR/SI:** Araragi infers that Shinobu's escape and Black Hanekawa's release were incorporated into a final test/parting gift.
- **IT:** structurally, resident specialist scaffolding is removed after the initial *Bakemonogatari* cycle.

### `お人よし`

The ensemble's final label is important. Oshino's anti-savior language does not make him indifferent. V03 consolidates the distinction:

> **He refuses ownership of another person's salvation while repeatedly performing real helping labor.**

# V07 update — Oshino's Golden Week specialist practice

## Competence does not equal guaranteed control

Oshino repeatedly loses to a phenomenon that should, by folklore category, be low level. Rather than proving specialist incompetence, the failures reveal that **host-specific interaction can invalidate template expectations**.

## Division of knowledge

Oshino explicitly divides labor:

- he can investigate the oddity/grave/rules;
- Araragi, as Hanekawa's friend, may know person-specific facts he does not.

V07 then humiliates Araragi's claim to be the “Hanekawa specialist” by showing how much of her home life he has failed to understand.

## Professional-boundary rule

Oshino tells Araragi that responsibility does not imply **personal responsibility for direct resolution**. One may be responsible and still step away; the situation may resolve through someone else. His instruction is effectively: do not obstruct professional intervention merely to satisfy a rescuer identity.

Key distinction:

> `命懸けになること` ≠ `死んでもいいと思うこと`

Risking life for a task and regarding one's death as acceptable currency are ethically different.

## Grey settlement

V07 ends without cure of the household. Oshino's `白黒つけない、グレーの決着` becomes an important specialist-ethics marker: a correct intervention may settle an acute danger without claiming total explanation or complete recovery.

## Oddity language as mercy

Oshino explicitly recognizes that supernatural/explanatory language can soften a brutally human account of behavior without making the oddity false. Specialist terminology therefore has ethical/narrative effects in addition to descriptive ones.

## Anti-totalization

When Araragi proposes “family as Hanekawa's oddity,” Oshino resists converting a useful explanatory representation into the whole territory. This is a direct specialist warning against interpretive monoculture.

# V09 update — Oshino's explicit qualification of rescue and knowledge

## Oshino Meme — epistemic humility

Route X letter states: `僕にだって全てがわかるわけじゃあ、ない`.

**Ledger consequence:** Oshino's explanations may be high-value specialist evidence without being omniscient authorial law. Competence includes explicit uncertainty and invitation to correction.

## Oshino Meme — `勝手に助かる` revision

Oshino says of Hachikuji's prevented death:

`さすがのこの僕さえ、八九寺さんが勝手に助かっただけとは言えない`

and credits the rescue to Araragi.

**Claim transition:** `人は一人で勝手に助かるだけ` is **not** a universal causal denial of external rescue. Its durable ethical force is better understood as resistance to helper-ownership of another person's total recovery/personhood.

## `目の前の女の子`

Oshino's practical recommendation is `目の前の女の子は救ったほうがいい`, while his request to “save the world” is explicitly framed as his own optional request.

At the climax the “girl in front of you” includes Route X Kiss-shot. This does not erase systemic responsibility; it prevents moral abstraction from making the immediate person invisible.

## Route theory

Oshino's `異空間移動 / 世界から世界 / ルートからルート` model is the current strongest local explanation but remains tagged **CD — provisional specialist model**.

## Specialist coalition

Oshino's letter implies cooperation among otherwise ethically distinct specialists under Route X catastrophe. Add to institutional specialist thesis: disagreement over methods/values does not preclude coordinated response, but specialist power remains insufficient against some problems where relational recognition is decisive.

## V12 specialist update — 『鬼物語』

### Ancient specialist infrastructure

Shinobu's testimony establishes an organized network of roughly fifty specialists surrounding the first oddity killer. Specialist practice therefore predates the modern Oshino/Kagenui/Kaiki/Gaen cohort and already contains hierarchy, technical inheritance, sacred tools, and political authority.

The paired tools encode specialist power over oddity death and revival: `怪異殺し` and `怪異生かし`. They demonstrate technical capacity without guaranteeing ethical adequacy or ontological completeness.

- **not established:** omniscience or moral identity between law and justice.

Ougi's §033 claim that the family business corrects wrong things, ends what should end, and punishes liars is analytically important but remains unverified at the V12 boundary.

# V18 specialist ethics / knowledge updates — 『終物語（下）』

## S-Gaen — lethal reset
Gaen's March 13 intervention is technically effective and ethically consent-deficient. She literally kills Araragi so Teori can return the human component without the accumulated vampire condition. ## S-Gaen — prediction limits

## S-Gaen — Hachikuji appointment

## S-Meme — minimalist recognition intervention
Meme resolves Ougi not by destroying or fully explaining her but by recognizing her as `姪っ子`, creating a viable relational role. His intervention demonstrates that specialist action can be ontologically decisive while verbally minimal.

## S-Hanekawa — search/retrieval infrastructure

## S-Teori — death-side technique
Teori's puppet bodies and death-side core revise V14's apparent death. His expertise is ethically ambiguous but operationally indispensable to Gaen's human-only reset.

### V18 specialist rule
Competence, consent, explanation, and moral legitimacy are separate dimensions. A specialist can be correct and effective while still acting paternalistically; an improvised relational recognition can be ethically cleaner than a perfectly engineered lethal solution.

# V19 specialist / epistemic update

### S19-01 — Tooe: knowledge is not binary
Tooe rejects `知ってる / 知らない` as an exhaustive opposition. Add a third high-value state: **`間違えて知っている` — possessing an answer/classification that is wrong.** This directly supports the V16 memory/classification framework.

### S19-02 — `理解` is distinct from information possession
Tooe argues that knowledge has little value if it cannot be used and that partial ignorance can sometimes permit better situated understanding. Treat `理解` as an analytical task, not a synonym for having more facts.

### S19-03 — specialist humility can coexist with insight
Tooe says she cannot claim to know everything and Ononoki says she is far from understanding the event completely. Expertise therefore remains high-value without becoming omniscience.

### S19-04 — Ougi's model is best available, not universal physics
The 80/20 explanation is current V19 authority for this event. Do not export it into a universal law of all mirrors/oddities without later evidence.

### S19-05 — infrastructural prevention

# V27 specialist ethics/knowledge delta — apology, law, success stories, and forgiveness

## E27-1 — Apology audit

V27 adds a reusable intervention matrix for apology:

1. **truth/accuracy** — what is being acknowledged?
2. **recipient jurisdiction** — whose experience is being described?
3. **intent** — remorse, strategy, self-punishment, reassurance test, coercion?
4. **effect/burden** — what stigma, exposure or labor moves onto the recipient?
5. **publicity** — who now knows and what role does that impose?
6. **timing** — repair, reopening, or self-relief?
7. **form/performance** — how is regret made legible?
8. **coercion** — law, institution, oddity, dependency, social pressure?
9. **proportionality** — does the response fit the harm?
10. **closure claim** — is the apologizer attempting to decide the relation is now finished?

**Core rule:** sincerity is neither necessary nor sufficient for every social function of apology, and sincere apology can still harm.

## E27-2 — Forgiveness audit

- forgiveness is not compulsory therapy;
- forgiveness can be revised later;
- forgetting is not the same as forgiving;
- settlement/cooperation is not identical to emotional reconciliation;
- material dependence can constrain refusal;
- a reason/excuse to forgive may help someone move forward without rewriting the harm as good;
- victimhood does not grant unlimited punitive jurisdiction.

Question added to professional ethics: **who can afford not to forgive?**

## E27-3 — Law ≠ goodness

`妖魔令` shows rule-based coordination becoming morally overbroad. A `令` can compel socially recognizable conduct without resolving evidence, proportionality, recipient preference, or relational legitimacy.
