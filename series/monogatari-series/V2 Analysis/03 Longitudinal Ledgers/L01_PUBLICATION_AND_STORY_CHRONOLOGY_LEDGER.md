1. **Spring-break blank:** referenced as foundational but not narratively supplied.
2. **Golden-Week blank:** Hanekawa/Araragi share history unavailable to the V01 reader.

### Chronology interpretation

V06 compresses almost its entire crisis into **one day plus the following-morning coda**. This matters formally: the life-changing revelation does not produce an extended institutional transition. ### Chronology knot opened

The `しでの鳥` itself is a reincarnating phenomenon with a history extending far beyond Tsukihi's birth. Do **not** place its earlier incarnations into the series timeline without direct later evidence; V06 establishes recurrence but not a complete prior-incarnation chronology.
