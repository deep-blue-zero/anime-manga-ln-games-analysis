# L04 — Character Role and Self-Story Ledger

This ledger distinguishes **public role**, **self-protective story**, **textual pressure**, and **current revision state**. It does not presume that an early role is simply false; roles can protect, constrain, and acquire real social force.

## C01 — Senjougahara Hitagi

- **Assigned public role:** `病弱な女の子`, explicitly a `立ち位置を与えられている`.
- **Private protective story:** discovery is dangerous; silence and indifference are safest; kindness can be hostile.
- **Material truth:** near-weightless body hidden beneath ordinary visual appearance.
- **Historical pressure:** illness, mother's religious obsession, cult abuse, maternal betrayal, family collapse, divorce, lost home.
- **Defensive technology:** stationery weapons, informational control, polished verbal aggression, emotional non-display.
- **Desired state at opening:** remain unobserved/unintervened upon.
- **Desired state at ritual:** reclaim her own thought/memory/weight because she chooses to carry it.
- **Desired state by Mayoi:** know Araragi more, become special to him, pursue love actively.
- **V01 revision:** role-prison begins to loosen; voice remains sharp but shifts from deterrence to negotiation/desire.

## C02 — Araragi Koyomi

- **Public position:** irregular high-school student with poor attendance; supernatural history mostly private.
- **Self-story:** helping someone in trouble is obvious enough that “anyone” would do it.
- **External correction:** Hitagi identifies that behavior as peculiar to him: he simply helps anyone.
- **Strength:** moves toward isolated/abnormal people despite personal risk.
- **Risk:** moves before knowing, assumes responsibility, crosses social/epistemic boundaries.
- **Body story:** residual supernatural healing makes physical risk unusually available.
- **V01 revision:** begins to recognize that his behavior is a personal disposition, not neutral baseline morality.

## C03 — Hachikuji Mayoi

- **Surface role:** lost elementary-school girl.
- **Oddity role:** `迷い牛` / ghostly child who obstructs return for qualifying followers.
- **Protective behavior:** immediate rejection and biting; later legible as efforts to discourage following.
- **Hidden history:** dead for roughly a decade; was trying to reach mother after divorce.
- **Core desire:** preserve active attachment to mother by actually returning/visiting rather than allowing time to erode love into abstraction.
- **V01 revision:** reaches home/destination, shifts from earthbound lost ghost to floating ghost, permits future contact.
- **Autonomy note:** she tells her own story and has been warning followers; she is not merely an object of Araragi's rescue.

## C04 — Hanekawa Tsubasa

- **Araragi-assigned role:** `委員長の中の委員長`; nearly mythologized correctness.
- **Self-limiting formula:** `何でもは知らないわよ。知ってることだけ`.
- **V01 anomaly:** Sundays involve walking because home is difficult to remain in; she can see Hachikuji.
- **Social function:** fair mediator; can correct Araragi and handle Hachikuji without Hitagi's discomfort.
- **V2 caution:** do not fill her full family psychology from later sources in publication-local entries.
- **Current status:** idealized by narrator; text has already inserted counterevidence.

## C05 — Oshino Meme

- **Public/diegetic role:** wandering oddity specialist in ruined school.
- **Self-position:** not savior; lender of force/knowledge; balancer.
- **V01 behavior:** explains, charges, restrains, withholds, warns, sets boundaries, supplies workaround.
- **Ethical tension:** refuses ownership of another's recovery but is not passive.
- **Current status:** specialist authority, but his own explanations must remain CD rather than omniscient TF.

## C06 — Shinobu Oshino

- **V01 narrator frame:** silent/diminished childlike vampire presence; described by Araragi as his own problem/`業`, not Hitagi's concern.
- **Known local fact:** Oshino says he named her `忍野忍`, explaining `刃の下に心あり`.
- **Narrative function in V01:** evidence of Araragi's still-unexplained prior supernatural history and current bodily abnormality.
- **V2 retrospective flag:** later subjectivity will sharply exceed the V01 narrator's containment of her; preserve this early misrecognition as data rather than retroactively “fixing” it.

## Role-prison / role-protection notes opened by V01

1. A role can be socially assigned before the person speaks.
2. Concealment can be rational protection without being a sustainable life.
3. “Helper” can become a self-story requiring correction from others.
4. “Lost child” can conceal agency and danger as well as vulnerability.
5. “Perfect class president” can be narrator-produced mythology even before later direct contradiction.

### C04 role paradox

## V06 update — character roles and self-stories

### Tsukihi — relationally authored does not mean selfless

Before the phoenix reveal, Tsukihi explicitly distinguishes herself from Karen. She says Karen's justice is internally passionate while her own is borrowed from Karen and Araragi; she can call that justice `偽物` without treating herself as fake. She also considers whether Fire Sisters should dissolve as Karen enters high school.

**C06-T01:** Tsukihi possesses metacognitive distance from the role she performs.

**C06-T02:** her self-story is relationally formed but revisable.

**C06-T03:** Araragi's later `本人がない` judgment is therefore not adopted into the character ledger as fact.

### Karen — signature identity can be discarded

Karen cuts the ponytail associated with her for roughly a decade. The gesture is impulsive, but the coda's joke that she already acts as though it was always this way makes an important point: identity continuity does not require visual continuity.

### Hitagi — growth rather than return

Araragi explicitly rejects describing Hitagi's post-Kaiki change as simply “returning,” “getting back” an earlier self, or becoming nice. He chooses `成長`. Her hair change participates in that forward-facing model.

### Shinobu — desire and relational responsiveness

V06 adds:

- self-initiated ordinary desire (Mister Donut trip);
- learned bluff/negotiation from Oshino;
- willingness to protect Araragi's family;
- moderation of her violence when Araragi is disturbed by it.

Her development is not monster → human purification. It is **greater social participation while remaining recognizably ancient, appetitive, proud, and violent**.

### Kagenui — justice specialist capable of exception

Initial role: categorical exterminator of immortal oddities / `正義の味方`.

Pressure: Tsukihi's harmless continuous family life and Araragi's resistance force Kagenui to confront the inadequacy of a pure original/fake hierarchy.

Result: she does not abandon her profession but creates an exception. Her role becomes corrigible.

### Ononoki — performed artificiality

Ononoki's flat speech plus `僕はキメ顔でそう言った` announces a consciously stylized character surface. V06 is insufficient to decide how much is self-authored versus imposed. Keep OPEN.

# V07 update — character-role pressure

## Hanekawa Tsubasa — goodness as discipline, not innocence

V07 materially revises the early “perfect class president” role.

- Hanekawa says she tried to be daughter-like despite receiving essentially no parental care.
- She treats venting about her family as `八つ当たり`, `憂さ晴らし`, and an imposition requiring apology.
- She defends the father who hit her and imagines herself as the disturbing variable in an otherwise “balanced” household.
- Black Hanekawa later describes her central project as trying to be an `普通の女の子`, where “ordinary” means doing what is ethically correct.

**Revision:** her goodness is real action, but it has become a nearly juridical self-regulation system. She does not need to “really be evil” for that system to be damaging.

## Hanekawa — family/nonfamily self-story

Key contradiction:

> `家族は大切にしなきゃね` / `ただ、家族じゃないだけ`

She values family normatively while denying that her own household qualifies. This is not simple cynicism; it is an attempt to preserve the ideal even when her life cannot instantiate it.

## Black Hanekawa — emergency hero and dangerous outsourcing

Black Hanekawa functions as a form Hanekawa can use to do what conscious Hanekawa cannot:

- retaliate;
- complain;
- discharge stress;
- violate the rule of perfect fairness;
- become temporarily freed from ordinary self-discipline.

But the “hero” harms outsiders and eventually threatens Hanekawa's continuity. It is therefore a self-protective role that becomes predatory.

## Araragi — idealization as misrecognition

His role for Hanekawa—benefactor, saint, impossible good person—protects his gratitude but prevents ordinary recognition. The house discovery forces the correction: “Hanekawa must be fine because Hanekawa” is absurd.

## Araragi — self-sacrifice as desire rather than duty

V07 sharpens his self-description. He denies that he has a formal duty to die; he says he **wants** to risk/destroy himself for Hanekawa. This is more honest than calling sacrifice an obligation, but it does not make the impulse healthy or relationally non-coercive.

## Shinobu — action before recovered speech

Silent Shinobu is not passive. She stays near Araragi, restores him, and directly ends the Black Hanekawa crisis. Her agency must be tracked through action even when Araragi supplies imagined verbal meaning afterward.

## Oshino — professional humility as role discipline

He knows enough to say “do not interfere,” loses repeatedly, and ultimately accepts a grey settlement. V07 makes specialist competence partly the capacity to know that a clean cure is unavailable.

# V09 update — relationally constituted selves

## Araragi Koyomi

**Prior self-story under pressure:** solitary `人間強度`, helper/savior, person who can pay costs with his own body.

**V09 evidence:** Araragi explicitly says friends may lower human strength but are worth the decrease, naming Hanekawa and Hachikuji as teachers. Route X functions as a counterfactual character experiment: without the month of Hachikuji friendship, he infers that he cannot say/perform the help-seeking act required in the Black Hanekawa crisis.

**Current formulation:** Araragi is not merely becoming “more social.” His capacities are partly **relational achievements**. Other people help produce the self who can later choose, ask, restrain, or intervene differently.

**Rescue-role correction:** V09 validates bounded rescue while attacking Araragi's fantasy of total causal authorship. He tends to overclaim both credit and guilt.

## Hachikuji Mayoi

**Role revision:** comic lost-child conversational partner → demonstrably character-forming friend.

Route X adult Hachikuji shows substantial temperament continuity while lacking the shared relationship history that defines main-route Hachikuji's social self. Main-route Araragi explicitly recognizes the familiar Hachikuji as one “born” through months of mutual exchange.

**Current formulation:** Hachikuji is a particularly clear instance of **relational co-authorship without ownership**.

## Oshino Shinobu / Kiss-shot

V09 places three self-states in tension:

- remembered spring-break Kiss-shot who was happy to be saved;
- present Shinobu who names partnership, ordinary appetite, and restraint as part of current selfhood;
- Route X Kiss-shot who answers bereavement with catastrophic domination.

Present Shinobu's refusal to reduce humans to route energy, followed by `今の儂にとっては、これが儂らしさじゃ`, strengthens the rule that later values need not be dismissed as fake because earlier selves differed.

## Route X Kiss-shot

**Self-story:** sovereign predator / abandoned immortal / world-destroyer after Araragi's death.

**Pressure:** seeing Araragi and present Shinobu cooperate gives her the image `誰かと共にある自分`, a relational possibility she had wanted across centuries.

**Current formulation:** recognition can produce a final reparative choice without erasing responsibility for catastrophic harm.

## V09 general character rule

**Shared origin is not complete identity.** Route divergence demonstrates that relationship history materially changes what a person can do. Alternate selves are neither wholly unrelated strangers nor interchangeable copies.
