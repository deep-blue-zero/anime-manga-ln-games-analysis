# L09 — V1 → V2 Claim Revision Ledger

This ledger records how direct Japanese-source rereading changes prior analytical propositions. A V01 status is **not** a final series-wide verdict unless explicitly marked local.

## Status vocabulary

- **SUPPORTED** — relevant evidence found, but scope remains too broad for final judgment.
- **STRENGTHENED** — source provides stronger or more precise support than V1 used.
- **NARROWED** — useful claim remains but needs reduced scope.
- **COMPLICATED** — claim remains productive only with an added tension/countercondition.
- **WEAKENED** — source materially undercuts it.
- **OVERTURNED** — source contradicts it.
- **OPEN** — V01 cannot yet judge.

## Highest-value V01 revision

### V1 shorthand
> Recovery means reclaiming the pain one tried to discard.

### V2 revision
> **V01 does not moralize pain that simply. It permits flight, but insists that the decision to carry or relinquish weight must belong to the person whose life bears it.**

This changes the project-wide rescue framework from a virtue-of-suffering model toward an **authorship-of-burden** model.

## Second major V01 revision

### V1 shorthand
> People save themselves; others lend force.

### V2 revision
> **The afflicted person's participation is indispensable, but recovery can be irreducibly collaborative.** Hachikuji cannot reach home through individual will alone; the result uses specialist knowledge, Araragi's refusal to disengage, Hitagi's local knowledge, urban redevelopment, and Hachikuji's own destination.

## Highest-value V02 revision 1 - hidden does not mean truer

### V1 shorthand
> Kanbaru's oddity reveals the violent wish she refused to admit.

### V2 revision
> **The violent wish is real, but V02 explicitly calls it only the back side. The front wish remains causally binding. The moral task is to own contradiction without letting hidden violence monopolize personhood.**

## Highest-value V02 revision 2 - victimhood without totalization

### V1 shorthand
> Nadeko's shy innocence is unstable and later revealed as incomplete.

### V2 revision
> **V02 itself already warns that Araragi barely remembers the child he is describing. Yet Nadeko's current victimization is literal and externally caused. Later agency must complicate the role without retroactively revoking victimhood.**

## Highest-value V02 revision 3 - universal compassion vs situated duty

### V1 shorthand
> Araragi wants to save everyone and sometimes moves harm elsewhere.

### V2 revision
> **The snake climax makes the conflict explicit: limited power forces priority. Kanbaru's command not to mistake whom to save is triage, not a celebration of retaliation. Universal moral concern does not eliminate situated responsibility.**

## Highest-value V03 revision 1 — perfection without falsity

### V1
> Hanekawa is not fake-good; she is over-adapted.

### V2 through V03
> **The source now directly establishes the developmental self-story: Hanekawa decides to become a good, ordinary child so her family history cannot define her. Her virtues are authentic; their compulsory consistency is the problem.**

## Highest-value V03 revision 2 — Black Hanekawa is partial/hybrid agency

### V1
> Black Hanekawa is an emergency exhaust system.

### V2 through V03
> **Functionally yes, but not merely a symptom. Hanekawa supplies pressure and knowledge; the oddity supplies mechanics and brutally reductive strategy. “Dark side” is too simple, but so is “mere exhaust.”**

## Highest-value V03 revision 3 — positive idealization can dehumanize

> **Araragi does not fail to see Hanekawa because he thinks too little of her. He thinks too much of the benefactor-role. Praise becomes a perceptual filter that excludes ordinary desire and need.**

## Highest-value V03 revision 4 — self-saving is not isolation

> **V03 is the first volume to make the correction explicit in the climax. The slogan describes agency; it does not forbid calling for help, being present, or allowing other people the freedom to save you.**

## Highest-value V03 caution — do not canonize endurance rhetoric

Araragi's claim that Hanekawa should have endured her headaches and that reliance on the cat is weakness contains a legitimate responsibility argument about harm displaced onto others. It also contains victim-blaming risk and mirrors his own self-sacrificial ideology. The same climax forces him to ask for help.

**Status:** `COMPLICATED / OPEN`. Revisit in 『猫物語（黒）』 and 『猫物語（白）』 rather than treating the claim as mature series ethics.

### Highest-value V04 revision 1 — rescue pathology is relationally mirrored

V1 correctly centered Araragi's self-erasing rescue. V04 shows that Kiss-shot later performs the same structure in reverse: she makes her death the proposed price of his restoration. The origin wound is therefore **a dyad of mirrored sacrificial rescue**, not a flaw located in one person.

### Highest-value V04 revision 2 — “mutual salvation” is too clean

The final arrangement preserves both lives but is imposed against Kiss-shot's stated wish and strips her autonomy/capacity. Use:

> **mutually entangled survival through coercive compromise**

rather than “they save each other” when describing V04 itself.

### Highest-value V04 revision 3 — responsibility is not self-punishment

V04 makes the distinction source-visible. Death offers Araragi and Kiss-shot a clean payment; Hanekawa/Oshino redirect the problem toward aftermath, distributed cost, and ongoing maintenance.

### Highest-value V04 revision 4 — later desire does not cancel earlier desire

Kiss-shot's initial `死にたくない` and later `死に場所を探しておった` are both genuine. V2 now has three independent cases (Kanbaru, Hanekawa, Kiss-shot) against the shortcut **hidden/later = truer**.

- whether later Shinobu independently endorses the life Araragi forced her to continue;

### High-value revision R-V05-01

**Old shorthand:** fake may become “more valuable than real” through effort.

**New formulation:**

> A borrowed or performed ideal can become ethically real only when the performer owns it as a personal choice, accepts responsibility for consequences and inferiority, relinquishes claims to moral purity, and continues the repair work after dramatic performance stops.

### High-value revision R-V05-02

**Old shorthand:** Araragi and Shinobu move from bad ending toward mutual salvation.

**New formulation:**

> V05's first mature relational step is explicitly **not forgiveness**. They preserve the wrong, decline to erase it, and choose `歩み寄り`. Later mutuality should be modeled as relationship built across remembered injury, not as retrospective purification of the origin.

### High-value revision R-V06-01 — two routes beyond fake/real

V05 emphasized **responsible enactment**: a borrowed ideal can become ethically real through ownership and repair.

V06 adds **continuous personhood**: a supposedly counterfeit origin can become morally secondary because a subject has lived one continuous biography, formed relationships, exercised agency, and become answerable to others.

Tsukihi belongs primarily to the second route.

### High-value revision R-V06-02 — relational formation is not absence of self

**Old risk:** Tsukihi's dependence on Karen's justice can be explained as phoenix mimicry and therefore lack of authentic self.

**V2 correction:** the source lets Araragi make that inference but supplies immediate counterevidence. Tsukihi can analyze the dependence, name it fake, imagine separating from Karen's role, and choose how to respond to change. Treat relational suggestibility as characterization, not proof of nonpersonhood.

### High-value revision R-V06-03 — family recognition versus family authorship

Araragi's strongest ethical achievement is refusing to let Kagenui's ontology erase Tsukihi's lived sisterhood.

His unresolved failure is deciding that Tsukihi should remain ignorant of her own ontology.

> **V06 protects the right to continue existing more clearly than the right to know and author one's own condition.**

### High-value revision R-V06-04 — specialist ontology does not settle specialist ethics

Kagenui can be factually right that Tsukihi is a `しでの鳥` and normatively wrong that this makes extermination obligatory. From V06 onward, ledgers must route every specialist claim through two questions:

1. Is the diagnosis/mechanic supported?
2. Is the proposed intervention ethically justified?
