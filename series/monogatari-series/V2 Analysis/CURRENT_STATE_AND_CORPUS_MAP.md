## 4. Evidence and interpretation discipline

Evidence labels:

- `TF` — textual fact
- `NR` — narrator report
- `CD` — character/diegetic claim
- `SI` — strong inference
- `IT` — interpretation
- `UA` — unresolved ambiguity
- `RC` — retrospective correction
- `VJ` — value judgment

Claim transitions:

- `PRESERVE`
- `STRENGTHEN`
- `REVISE`
- `DOWNGRADE`
- `REJECT`
- `OPEN`

Required dual reading:

1. publication-local reconstruction;
2. retrospective V2 revision.

Do not collapse:

- event fact and narrator framing;
- specialist theory and omniscient truth;
- later causal connection and proof of original authorial foreshadowing;
- origin and authentic essence;
- being a victim in one relation and lacking agency in another;
- consequence and cosmic punishment;
- help and ownership of another person's recovery.

Canonical volume locator form:

`VXX — Japanese title — chapter/arc/section — short scene anchor`

## 9. Current mature claim locks through V30 + controlled supplementary integration

The following are compact routing formulations, not substitutes for the underlying deep readings and ledgers.

### Rescue and agency

- Help is real and can be causally indispensable without giving the helper authorship over another person's recovery.
- `助ける / 助かる` is not a simple helper/recipient hierarchy.
- Self-saving identifies the non-transferable locus of agency; it does **not** imply causal self-sufficiency.
- Non-possession sometimes means staying, sometimes leaving, sometimes accepting another person's refusal, and sometimes surrendering jurisdiction over their next chapter.
- Araragi's recurring danger is turning his body, then his entire future, into rescue currency.
- Final Season revises this: self-critique is necessary; self-annihilation is not. `僕は今、自分を助けたんだ` is a central mature correction.
- V14 adds prevention: repeated emergency exception can become embodied habit; sustainable care requires intervening before catastrophe makes self-destruction seem necessary.

### Origin, authenticity, and personhood

- Hidden, darker, earlier, later, mirrored, or supernatural states are **not automatically truer selves**.
- Black Hanekawa, Rainy Devil Kanbaru, Nadeko's `俺様`, Ougi, and V19 reverse manifestations must not be turned into authenticity ladders.
- Origin explains; it does not exhaust personhood.
- `偽るのは駄目だけど変わるのは、いい`: genuine change is not fraud.
- Constructed beings can possess personhood without aspiring to biological human essence; Yotsugi's human form is for `人といるため`.
- Ougi is Araragi **and** an independently functioning composite person once formed. `Ougi = Araragi` is true but incomplete.
- Meme's “niece” declaration is role-recognition that makes an already-existing person socially/ontologically sustainable, not a secret genealogical reveal.
- V19 locks: genuine remainder does not possess authenticity monopoly.

### Memory, narration, and retrospective method

- Araragi is corrigible rather than generically unreliable.
- V16 distinguishes forgotten event, misremembered event, reconstructed event, and retained causal effect.
- Later connection is not proof that an earlier detail was consciously understood, causally controlled, or authorially planted as foreshadowing.
- V15's afterword-derived anti-overfitting rule is project-wide: hindsight is necessary; retrospective connection is not evidence of original intention.
- `解答` and `解釈` must remain distinct.
- Ougi's interrogative method can produce strong answers while remaining ethically non-neutral.

### Oddity metaphysics

- Oddities are literal supernatural realities and also psychologically/socially meaningful; do not reduce them to allegory.
- V12's `くらやみ` is an impersonal corrective law without demonstrated moral consciousness. Law is not justice.
- Ontological noncompliance does not imply ethical desert.
- Gaen's V17 ash/shrine model is a probability-changing causal field, not a universal master cause.
- Fabricated origins can produce real effects when they meet material/supernatural infrastructure and recipient acceptance.
- Ougi is modeled partly on the real darkness but is not the real darkness.

### Nadeko

- A genuine coping mechanism can become socially useful without becoming fake.
- Initial V11 Kuchinawa is self-generated narrative/persona, not an already-revived external god.
