# 〈物語〉シリーズ — V1 First-Pass Claim Inventory

## 1. How to use this document

Every claim below is a **V1 analytical proposition to be tested against the Japanese primary corpus**. Inclusion here does not certify truth.

### V1 maturity

- **Mature** — repeatedly used in final V1 synthesis.
- **Strong** — developed across several prior analyses but not yet fully source-routed.
- **Provisional** — plausible but broad, late, or under-located.
- **Caution** — deliberately retained as a possible overclaim/correction target.

### V2 priority

- **H** — central claim; must be audited with volume-level locators.
- **M** — important specialist claim.
- **L** — useful but can wait until relevant specialist synthesis.

### V2 status

All claims begin **UNAUDITED**. Final statuses will be CONFIRMED / STRENGTHENED / NARROWED / COMPLICATED / WEAKENED / OVERTURNED / UNRESOLVED.

# 2. Highest-risk V1 overclaims to actively challenge

These should receive adversarial attention rather than confirmation-seeking:

1. **“Oddities are narrative bodies.”** Useful, but may become totalizing if it obscures autonomous supernatural rules.
2. **“Monster Season = systems produce monsters.”** Strong synthesis, but some cases may remain primarily personal, folkloric, or historical.
3. **“Araragi's rescue ethic becomes institutional.”** True at one scale, but adult Araragi may remain more improvisational/private than the phrase implies.
4. **“Hitagi gives Araragi ordinary future.”** Potentially reductive if it treats her mainly as a function in his arc.
5. **“Shinobu is educated into relation.”** Powerful formulation, but risks paternalism or implying that Araragi civilizes her.
6. **“Nadeko's recovery is labor.”** Strong, but should not make artistic/professional productivity the sole measure of healthy personhood.
7. **“Family is shared truth and burden.”** Strong V30 thesis, but must not be back-projected into every earlier family relationship.
8. **“Second Season morally corrects Araragi.”** Likely strong, but each narrator shift may have distinct formal purposes.
9. **“Sexual comedy critiques the gaze.”** Must never become a blanket defense; many scenes may simply indulge it.
10. **“Kaiki's lie is more humane than truth.”** Must be localized to particular interventions and not converted into a general ethical doctrine.

# 3. Known V1 strengths worth preserving unless source audit disproves them

The following are especially productive because they already resist simplistic readings:

- oddities are literal and interpretive at once;
- narrator performance is part of characterization;
- early reader archetype-recognition is deliberately unstable;
- self-sacrifice is not automatically moral;
- victimhood can be true without exhausting personhood;
- fake/real is relational rather than a purity binary;
- recovery does not require forgiveness or sweetness;
- leaving a protagonist's orbit can be character growth;
- later professional/institutional life changes the scale of ethical questions;
- sexual self-awareness does not erase ethical discomfort;
- later revelations must revise rather than erase publication-local ambiguity.

These are methodological assets V2 should retain while upgrading their evidence base.
