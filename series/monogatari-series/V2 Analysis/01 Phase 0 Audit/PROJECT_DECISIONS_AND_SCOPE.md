# 〈物語〉シリーズ V2 — Project Decisions and Scope

## 1. Governing purpose

V2 is a **source-traceable second-pass reread**, not an attempt to expand V1 by memory. Its purpose is to determine:

- which V1 claims survive direct Japanese-source audit;
- where those claims first become supportable;
- which narrator/focalization state produced the evidence;
- how later works revise earlier interpretations;
- which claims were compressed, overstated, or under-supported in V1;
- how Japanese voice, names, register, wordplay, and address terms alter interpretation;
- how the series' scale changes from private adolescent cases to adult, institutional, and familial problems.

No new full-series synthesis prose is authorized during Phase 0.

---

# 2. Locked corpus boundary

The initial V2 production corpus is:

- 30 numbered Japanese EPUBs, `01 化物語 上` through `30 戦物語`;
- `短物語`;
- `佰物語オリジナルドラマCD シナリオブック`;
- `化物語 アニメコンプリートガイドブック`.

This is the **supplied corpus boundary**, not a claim of franchise-publication completeness.

If another numbered novel is later supplied, it is appended through a source-lock revision. V2 architecture intentionally does not hard-code “30” as the eternal full-series count.

---

# 3. Primary-source authority

## Decision P0-PS-01

The original Japanese EPUBs govern factual, linguistic, narratorial, metaphysical, relational, and thematic claims.

## Decision P0-PS-02

V1 analysis is not evidence. It is a claim inventory and provenance layer.

## Decision P0-PS-03

Anime adaptations, English translations, subtitle tracks, fan wikis, and external commentary must not contaminate a publication-local novel reading unless the task explicitly calls for adaptation/translation comparison.

## Decision P0-PS-04

Exact quotations intended for publication-quality use must be checked against the Japanese source at the point of citation. A remembered line or V1 paraphrase is not quotation-grade evidence.

---

# 4. Reading order and epistemic discipline

## Decision P0-RO-01 — publication order is the production spine

Phase 1 proceeds V01 → V30 according to the locked Drive numbering.

## Decision P0-RO-02 — every volume receives two readings

Each deep reading must distinguish:

1. **Publication-local reconstruction** — what the reader could responsibly infer at that point.
2. **Retrospective V2 revision** — what full supplied-corpus hindsight later changes.

Later knowledge cannot be back-projected into the first layer.

## Decision P0-RO-03 — chronology is a separate axis

Publication order, narrated order, internal chronology, and retrospective knowledge order are not interchangeable.

Any claim sensitive to chronology must identify which order it relies on.

## Decision P0-RO-04 — narrator state is mandatory

A claim based on a narrated event must identify:

- narrator/focalizer;
- narrator's knowledge at that moment;
- likely reliability dimension involved;
- whether later narration corrects or complicates it.

---

# 5. Side-story protocol

The three supplementary EPUBs are not to be dumped into a single “bonus” appendix and forgotten.

## Decision P0-SS-01

Each side story receives two positions where possible:

- **release/publication position**;
- **internal-story position**.

## Decision P0-SS-02

`短物語` is a collection spanning multiple release contexts. Individual stories must be indexed independently rather than assigned the collection's compilation date as their only analytical location.

## Decision P0-SS-03

`佰物語` is especially valuable for:

- character voice;
- address terms;
- ensemble rhythm;
- ordinary school-life relation;
- repeated verbal routines.

It should not be treated as stronger evidence for major plot/metaphysics than the numbered novels.

## Decision P0-SS-04

The anime guidebook's prose stories may be used as authorized paratextual fiction. Production commentary in that volume remains paratext, not novel-canon narration.

---

# 6. V1 revision protocol

Every reusable V1 claim receives one of the following V2 outcomes:

- **CONFIRMED** — source audit supports the claim substantially as written.
- **STRENGTHENED** — source audit supports it and adds important evidence/precision.
- **NARROWED** — the claim is useful but too broad.
- **COMPLICATED** — the claim survives only with countervailing evidence or multiple layers.
- **WEAKENED** — support is substantially poorer than V1 implied.
- **OVERTURNED** — source audit contradicts the prior claim.
- **UNRESOLVED** — evidence does not justify closure.

Claims should also receive evidence-type labels from the analytical method:

- TF — textual fact
- NR — narrator report
- CD — character/diegetic explanation
- SI — strong inference
- IT — interpretive thesis
- UA — unresolved ambiguity
- RC — retrospective correction
- VJ — value judgment

---

# 7. Anti-overinterpretation lock

The following are explicitly prohibited as default shortcuts:

1. “Oddity X simply equals trauma Y.”
2. “A pun proves ontology merely because it exists.”
3. “A joke is a confession.”
4. “Narrator error means intentional lying.”
5. “Kaiki is lying because Kaiki says he lies.”
6. “A specialist explanation is the final metaphysical truth.”
7. “Araragi's rescue impulse is automatically admirable.”
8. “People save themselves” is the series' sole ethical rule.
9. “Integration” means forgiveness or normalization.
10. “Adulthood” means becoming ordinary or losing oddity.
11. “Family” is resolved by legal naming alone.
12. “Self-awareness” automatically absolves sexualized/fetishistic material.
13. Later revelation erases the ambiguity the reader originally faced.
14. Internal chronology is more “true” than publication form.

---

# 8. Character and relationship policy

## Decision P0-CR-01 — no character is reduced to Araragi-facing function

For every major character, audit:

- independent goals;
- relationships not mediated by Araragi;
- work, school, art, profession, family, or institutional role;
- narrator corrections to Araragi's view;
- ability to leave or become irrelevant to him.

## Decision P0-CR-02 — non-possession is a core longitudinal test

Track whether relationships can survive:

- refusal;
- distance;
- changed role;
- altered name;
- lack of proximity;
- one party becoming less narratively central;
- independent future.

## Decision P0-CR-03 — relationship labels remain evidence-sensitive

Do not force every bond into romance, friendship, parent-child, sibling, or master-servant categories when the text preserves hybrid or unstable forms.

This is especially important for Araragi/Shinobu.

---

# 9. Japanese-language policy

Every volume must update a voice/language ledger for material that matters analytically.

Audit, where relevant:

- pronouns and self-reference;
- address terms and name changes;
- sentence-final forms;
- politeness and stylized character language;
- commands, benefactives, hedging, and modality;
- repeated catchphrases;
- dialect/slang;
- kanji/ruby divergence;
- punning and false etymology;
- baseline register changes over time.

A translation-sensitive claim must distinguish what is explicit in Japanese from what is preserved or flattened in English.

---

# 10. Comedy, sexuality, gaze, and genre policy

V2 will neither sanitize nor flatten the sexual/comedic material.

For relevant scenes, separate:

- narrator gaze;
- character action;
- reciprocal banter;
- coercion/boundary crossing;
- comedy function;
- erotic function;
- genre-market function;
- psychological function;
- metatextual critique;
- ethical discomfort.

Do not assume the material is all satire. Do not assume it is all meaningless indulgence.

---

# 11. Chronology policy

The Phase 0 chronology map is intentionally coarse. Exact dates, overlap, and arc adjacency are to be verified from source text as Phase 1 advances.

Chronology entries carry confidence levels:

- **A — explicit/structurally secure**
- **B — strongly supported but exact date not yet audited**
- **C — provisional relative placement**

No exact date should be added to a V2 artifact from memory alone.
