# 〈物語〉シリーズ V2 — Initial Internal Chronology Map

## 1. Purpose

This is **not** a final chronological encyclopedia. It is a Phase 0 orientation map designed to prevent publication order, narrated order, and internal chronology from being conflated during the V2 reread.

Exact day/month placements should be added only after direct Japanese-source verification.

Confidence labels:

- **A** — explicit or structurally secure from the supplied corpus/V1 provenance.
- **B** — strongly supported relative placement; exact date not yet re-audited.
- **C** — provisional placement requiring source confirmation.

# 3. High-priority chronology knots for V2

These require explicit longitudinal ledger entries because interpretive errors are likely if order is flattened.

## Knot C-01 — 『傷物語』 / 『猫物語（黒）』 / 『化物語』

Why it matters:

- Araragi's rescue ethic and body are already wounded before the opening staircase scene.
- Hanekawa is already a rescuer and already has a prior Black Hanekawa incident before 『つばさキャット』.
- Publication order and internal chronology invite different first impressions.

V2 rule: preserve the publication-local experience of first meeting characters while separately recording what chronologically occurred earlier.

## Knot C-02 — 『猫物語（白）』 / 『傾物語』

V1 reading treated Araragi's absence from Hanekawa's self-narrated crisis as structurally meaningful and connected to his simultaneous involvement elsewhere.

V2 action: verify exact overlap and do not let thematic pairing substitute for chronology evidence.

## Knot C-03 — 『花物語』 inside Second Season

Kanbaru's story occurs after the graduation of major seniors, despite its publication position within Second Season.

V2 action: explicitly track the difference between **publication function** and **internal future position**.

## Knot C-04 — 『暦物語』

Its component stories are distributed across prior high-school chronology while the compilation participates in the Final Season movement toward death/graduation.

V2 action: each chapter gets both compilation position and internal-time locator.

## Knot C-05 — 『業物語』

Contains material from substantially different eras, including deep vampire history and other character periods.

V2 action: never assign the whole volume a single internal date.

## Knot C-06 — 『結物語』 versus Monster Season

Publication order can make the adult institutional future appear to precede university-era Monster Season internally.

V2 action: separate **publication foreshadowing of adulthood** from **internal university progression**.

## Knot C-07 — side stories

Compilation order, original release order, and internal story position frequently differ.

V2 action: side-story index must record all three when available.

# 4. Chronology-sensitive V1 claims to retest

1. **Second Season as moral decentering of Araragi.** Chronology must show when narrator substitution is structural rather than merely chronological necessity.
2. **Araragi's maturation from bodily rescuer to institutional participant.** Must not use 『結物語』's adult endpoint to overread earlier university behavior.
3. **Shinobu's ancient moral history.** Later origin material must not erase the uncertainty of 『傷物語』-era reader knowledge.
4. **Nadeko's artist/specialist trajectory.** Later labor should revise, not retroactively invalidate, the truth of earlier victimhood.
5. **Hachikuji's death/ghost/god sequence.** Later godhood does not nullify the ethical force of prior farewell.
6. **Hanekawa's departure/globalization.** Later autonomy should not be projected backward as if her early home crisis were already consciously understood.
7. **Ougi's identity.** Final revelation cannot be used as the only lens for every earlier appearance.
8. **Family Season household claims.** Marriage-era solutions must not be treated as implicit destinations all earlier relationships were “really” moving toward.
