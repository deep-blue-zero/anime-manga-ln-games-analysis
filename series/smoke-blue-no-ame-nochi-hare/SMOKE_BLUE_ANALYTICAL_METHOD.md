## Relationship evidence

- For a consequential exchange, identify who initiates, each person’s information and expectations, observable uptake or refusal, any delay, and later conduct that corroborates or complicates the exchange. Record both directions for a relationship where one person’s account leaves the other’s position uncertain.
- Preserve ordinary patterns. Trace repeated tasks, work arrangements, leisure, habits and small offers as changes in time, trust and obligation where the manga supports that connection. Compare where similar conduct serves work, politeness, distance or care. In a scene involving translation, distinguish the character’s work within the story from the analyst’s gloss and from any separate translated edition.
- Read setting, gaze, gesture, distance, repeated objects, blank space and page turns as the source’s arrangement of access to a relationship.
