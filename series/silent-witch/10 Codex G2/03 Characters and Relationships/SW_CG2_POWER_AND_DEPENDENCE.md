# Power and dependence: unlike bodies, particular companions

The wider cast repeatedly breaks the inference that abundant power makes somebody independent, or that dependence makes a relationship morally identical to every other dependence. Glen's reserves prolong pain; Ray's capacity intensifies an involuntary danger; Raul's inherited power frightens him through its history. Nero can destroy a curse-dragon and lose a street encounter with crows. Will's small protected surface can make a prince's spectacular action possible. These are not balancing weaknesses attached to otherwise complete powers. They organize what each person notices, wants, can ask and can refuse. Their relations become intelligible through particular recipients rather than a shared scale of humanization or confidence.

This study preserves existing subjects **SW-CG2-20261019-S-GLEN/S-NERO/S-WILL/S-LIN/S-RAY/S-RAUL**, with Kara, Romaria, Sezdio, Darrel/Ruferno and Frida at their established routes. It uses the exact sixteen-witness boundary; current continuing events stop at V12. C020/RV064, C022/RV083, C024/RV069, C028/RV075, C029, C030/RV080 and C032–033 remain scoped registrations in the [claims ledger](../02%20Cumulative/SW_CG2_CLAIMS_AND_REVISIONS.md). Their cross-comparison here does not silently extend each other's evidence.

## Glen: a cheerful face is not an account of its cost

Glen's first dragon encounter gives him real courage and real incompetence at the same time. He protects soldiers, regrets neglected accuracy and receives credit for Monica's concealed shot. His pleasure in the apparent victory belongs to his limited knowledge; it is not proof he knowingly accepts stolen credit. Dance similarly lets confidence cause harm before he learns how another body needs to be led. Butchery, food and flight are genuine competencies where written theory is weak. Neither school failure nor a wrong account of the dragon makes those abilities illusory. [V02:E001–002/E006/E010/E012–013.]

V05 changes Monica's appraisal before it gives Glen's own full history. Ray reports an earlier runaway and narration clarifies that the broken meter's ceiling was 250. Monica wonders what conflict might lie behind the cheerful chime vow. That is her sympathetic hypothesis, not a fear confessed to her. Glen separately compares current pain to Louis's punishing mountain training and makes bed rest seem preferable. The statement can coexist with pain worse than remembered muscle soreness. Cheer is partly a chosen performance to spare Eliane worry; his collapse after she leaves is affirmative evidence of its cost. [V05:E020–024 and repair supplement.]

V06 then narrates the childhood practice that the earlier report could not specify. Class contempt and poor foundations make praise from Hubert attractive. The safe barrier prevents wounds but preserves pain and removes reserves. Glen wishes they would empty; they do not. Abundant power keeps the assault going and supplies the uncontrolled fireball formed through fear and anger. Exhaustion is not the cause of the breakdown. This matters to character as well as mechanics: the boy's inability to escape cannot be explained as having already spent the resource that others envy. The immediate damage remains outside the remembered endpoint. [V06:E012, p-007:P90–114.]

Lower supplies the intervening confinement and release. Louis offers apprenticeship inside restricted alternatives and discloses escape only afterward. The first fire exercise has an enabling feature distinct from the insults: a corps actually contains the result, and Owen promises, 「僕達がちゃんと助けるから」. Glen sees adults looking at him as a child rather than a catastrophe. His admitted fear can coexist with revived competitiveness. Louis adapts fire and flight to his operational strengths, while painful discipline and weak foundations continue. The benefits do not prove that domination was necessary to produce them. [Lower:E004–006, p-005:P76–300.]

Glen's other teachers matter. Cyril's steady effort and chosen trust offer a different model; Monica breaks simultaneous maintenance into manageable work and directs him toward tracking rather than imitation of unchanted glory. Albert supplies theory while Glen supplies flight instruction. Isaac later adapts language and questions to his practical knowledge. V12's passed lower examination is an actual result of this network; Louis's solo-dragon task is not the kingdom's ordinary qualification standard. Learning has several owners, and a pass does not certify every foundation or safe exercise of power. [AFTER04:E009–013; V05:E011; V06:E015; V07:E018; V11:E003; V12:E014/E020.]

His unauthorized sealing of Rosalie gives the strongest resistance to a merely sunny-helper account. He initially knows changing another person's memory is wrong, then acts after falsely construing the fall as suicide. Loyalty to Louis and care for Rosalie combine with insufficient theory to suppress far more than intended. Gratitude can motivate harm to a third person's mind. Rosalie's refusal to prosecute is her later choice, not prior permission; supervision does not prove a learned internal limit. His gifts of meat and medical concern remain real alongside this failure. [Lower:E016/E020/E027–028.]

The strongest rival is that a reckless prodigy requires overwhelming discipline. Credible collective safety, recipient-fitted instruction and repeated theory gaps resist necessity. The opposite rival makes cheer an entirely false mask. Self-chosen work, cooking pride, friendship and the decision to oppose Louis for a school life he enjoyed resist it. Glen's literary importance lies in keeping capability, learning, gratitude and willingness to overrule another person on the same record without making them one motive.

## Nero and Monica: affection without a human conscience

Nero's origin makes their familiar language unstable from the beginning. Monica hears pain in a dragon's mouth and chooses to remove the bone; a human crowd frightens her more. He tries a cat body and pads when his large human form frightens her. He also uses her responsibility for displacement to secure a home and issues commands inside a promise of obedience. EXTRA09's later-disclosed mountain scene makes attention an acquired practice: pain and shouting fail, soft pads reach her, an apology follows, and she finally asks what the named Nero eats. The soup is disappointing. Affiliation begins through calibration and imperfect provision, not a naturally therapeutic pet saving a uniformly benevolent mistress. [V05:E022; EXTRA09:E005.]

Fiction gives him roles, pleasures and mistakes. He wants a palanquin Monica flees, a stylish card game whose objective disappears, a detective case instead of a direct question, and a romance triangle the supposed beneficiary does not want. Monica can protect his pleasure by withholding an Abram spoiler; that low-stakes concealment is not equivalent to hiding a royal murder. His jealousy and praise demands remain independent wants, sometimes burdensome. Her affection includes complaint and corrective command rather than endless reverence for the creature who saves her. [V03:E011/E013/E042; AFTER04:E007; EXTRA09:E005–009.]

His black-dragon revelation joins protectiveness to nonhuman danger. He ends a fellow dragon's degraded torment, destroys the curse and threatens disclosure with devouring. His commitment not to attack people while Monica remains master is conditional. V08 lets him understand human necessity without adopting their moral vocabulary; V10's explicit trust rests on Monica being stronger. She receives that criterion with relief, not because it has become a human promise of unconditional safety. He can also know which alley works when her map memory does not. Street wandering and appetite become useful knowledge without being retroactively undertaken for her welfare. [V05:E019/E022; V08:E025; V10:E013/E022.]

The strongest sentimental rival calls him a disguised humanlike caretaker whose excesses are only jokes. His willingness to harm allies and differing standards resist it. The opposite creature-as-instrument account loses chosen gifts, named preferences, jealousy, accommodation and mercy toward another dragon. Their relation is reciprocal and unequal in several directions: she is stronger in a fight's opening, he supplies a body and worldly routes she lacks, and neither shares the other's whole account of what should be preserved.

## Will, Isaac, Eileen and Brendan: service chooses its object

Will's reserve expression repeatedly tempts human observers to supply an emotion. His lack of understanding of human formulae makes Isaac's enthusiasm lonely; later anatomy errors can turn effective restraint into a danger. Neither ignorance proves lack of feeling. V08 gives feeling without a settled human category: trust, respect, reverence, intimacy, longing or love might name his attachment to Eileen. What he wanted is clearer than the label—her happiness, then her child's, then the fulfillment of that child's wish for Isaac. These are successive interpretations of another's desire, not one command transmitted unchanged. [V02:E021; V08:E031, p-016:P210–245; V10:E012.]

Contract limits make devotion painfully incomplete. After Eileen dies he can watch the prince suffer but cannot prevent it. Isaac's hard-won contract gives an ally to a child living under surveillance. Will later reminds that ally of the neglected freedom wish, then chooses a new tactic instead of executing the V06 killing order. Isaac praises the judgment. That is a real permission for initiative in one costly situation, not proof that every order allows dissent. The proposed fatal illusion in V08 repeats self-sacrifice as the answer to somebody else's chosen death; Monica refuses it. His V09 decision to continue the contract is then actual chosen continuity rather than a universal species fact. [V06:E023/E029; V08:E014–015/E027/E031; V09:E020.]

V10 gives him indispensable small skills: air, concealment, removing salt and protecting a board-sized surface against a stronger water spirit. His “only” identifies a contribution's scope, not insignificance. He can honestly deny knowing whether trust names his relation to Isaac while acting for particular wishes. Brendan, whose devotion to Eileen is explicitly neither paternal nor romantic, supplies a fellow witness and emotional vocabulary. Recognizing Diana lets Will mourn and understand loneliness without acquiring a human heart. The new steward still does not know the substitution. Intimacy in one shared history is no omniscience about another. [V10:E004–005/E010–012/E018/E024.]

Monica's V11 complaint route offers another possible accountability: Will agrees to tell her when Isaac asks too much. Narration distinguishes solidarity under a common person's demands from ordinary friendship. The route remains unexercised through V12. His holiday inquiry there is self-chosen and partly modeled on respected humans, yet his own preference remains tap water. An accommodation can be received while the giver guesses its pleasure wrongly. The strongest service-as-automatic-obedience account cannot explain the warning about the aphrodisiac, reminders, tactical initiative or agreed recourse. A complete emancipation account cannot supply a costly refusal not yet shown. [V11:E008/E020; V12:E006.]

## Lin and Kara: a limit need not be repaired to be felt

Lin's maid identity, landings, idioms and fiction are serious interests inside comedy. Powerful wind does not make human-risk calibration automatic. She can be reliable at transport and dangerous at choosing its speed, contradict Louis and still transmit his coercion literally. Her week of vacation defeats his assumption of permanent availability. Her attachment to Kara is separate from the contract that allows proximity. [V01:E018/E023; V02:E014/E028; AFTER04:E004; Lower:E008/E016.]

V07 gives an unusually precise distinction. She adjusts the landing to hover, stops excessive thanks because she knows Kara dislikes it, recognizes opened arms as permission and stands close. Kara embraces her. Lin regrets lacking human warmth. She does not change temperature or withhold the embrace. The distinction matters because a wished-for bodily capacity is not the same character mechanism as a successful accommodation. Feeling is present without the world supplying a repaired body. V12's unanswered Kara inquiry preserves that particular attachment and the limit of Louis's knowledge, rather than turning an absence into an established danger. [V07:E013, p-014:P78–104, corrected; V12:E014.]

## Romaria and Ruferno: genuine bonds do not settle a third body's claim

Romaria's history prevents a simple innocent-spirit rescue. Genuine assistance to humans attracts military capture; the defensive blizzard kills unrelated villagers. Sezdio's love leads toward sacrificing humans and overruling Romaria himself. Cyril can refuse acquittal while giving the promised flower. Monica preserves a fading person for Cyril's sake, without a confirmed will to contract. Later self-questioning keeps that emergency act answerable to an absent response. [V06:E026; V07:E003/E005/E008–009; V10:E019.]

Darrel and Ruferno provide a resistant comparison. Their affection is affirmed, and lack of resources is real; they nevertheless install her in sleeping Rick as a living contract stone. Rick did not share their agreement. His brother's old “it does not matter” relieved shame and later licenses disposal of Lana's importance. The difference from Romaria is not purity of motive—Monica acknowledges her partiality—but burden, setting, available choice and continuing responsibility. Genuine reciprocal attachment between two people cannot authorize a third body for their project. Rick's eventual protest receives rejection, not a painless cure for divided loyalty. [V10:E014–019/E021.]

Kayan makes another limit explicit. The unnamed ice spirit presumes the winter king wants the vanished age restored; Kayan disputes the right to speak for that will. Shared grief does not produce a shared desired future. The murder and consumption are reader-known, not knowledge Will obtains from mourners or Monica files in her report. The dying ruby holds condemnation and rescue together—base 「断罪して」, reading 「救つて」—without performing either. Their opposition should not become a rule that nonhuman attachment is intrinsically false or that similarity identifies every unnamed participant. [V12:E009/E013/E015.]

## Raul, Ray and Frida: longing does not supply reception

Raul wants magic that can bring friends, after childhood rejection and an inherited reputation for destructive black flame. V11 lets him receive concrete contrary evidence: Monica takes his hand while he holds the feared grimoire, and his friends share a game. He stops when they need sleep. That accepted limit is real but not a global social competence. V12 has him read a private letter, arrange an encounter without consent, miss tears and offer a hundred points of praise that do not answer Ray's shame. Work and good intention can still misfit the recipient. His actual thorns and sedative rose then help stop a crisis; triumphant spinning frightens Monica again. [V07:E004; V11:E005–009/E015–016; V12:E017–023.]

His inherited combat method also has a cost beyond its user. Reruva is consumed while controlled; the roses do not liberate her or permit ordinary return to the spirit king. Timed sleep curbs the fortress, and the nightmare reveals that he wanted to spare possible friends the sight. Cheerful presentation and actual concern coexist with lethal technique. The strong rival that loneliness merely sentimentalizes intrusive power must retain that death. The opposite harmless-gardener account cannot survive it. Agriculture, flowers, household obligations and convivial pleasure are nonetheless independent aims, not decorations concealing a single cruel self. [V06:E003/E029; V07:E004; V11:E009.]

Ray's repeated demands for love are burdensome and also incomplete evidence of his affect. He draws, writes poems, buys a rose and performs difficult rescues whose bodily costs he complains about. Courtesy is easily misread as personal love. V12 reveals a more specific contradiction: he longs to hear the words he fears saying because the parental story he knows makes love sound like enslavement. Complaints are practiced small outlets; terror can overwhelm them and make self-blame literally dangerous through curses. Henrik's killing pressure is real, and Ray interposes himself for Frida before knowing that she loves him. He flees to avoid hurting her. [AFTER04:E002; V05:E020/E025; EXTRA09:E020; V12:E018/E020–022.]

The narrator later corrects the parental story for the reader: the mother believed herself unlovable and mistook actual love for a curse, causing real deaths while trying to remove it. Ray does not receive that truth. His crisis stops through a finite barrier, failed attack followed by revised tactics, misdirection, pharmacological magic and aftercare. An embrace matters without being a miracle cure or a delivered family explanation. [V12:E021–022.]

Frida refuses the convenient ending. Her household accounting, sacrificed dress and hair, brothers' needs and wish for farm tools make a wealthy match materially attractive without exhausting her motive. Henrik's protection becomes force against her choice. She investigates the Albright home by its actual pay, food and treatment, values shared meals and receives eccentric anonymous attention warmly. Her hand kiss is regard; 「いえ、別に愛してはいませんが」 refuses a feeling impossible to certify at their first real encounter. 「好きになれると思いますよ」 opens a future she does not guarantee. The poem gives a language for praising living eyes instead of requiring him to become a jewel. Raul's petals picture a completed romance before the words earn it. [V12:E016/E018/E020/E023, p-018:P24–61.]
