His reconstruction of the intended illness, nursing and sexual/dynastic trap is addressed to Baldia; the father's assertion of his daughter's assent remains his account, not her independent speech (P81–91).

Isaac could prosecute the attempted administration but chooses to preserve a useful ally. He permits the cup's removal, then warns through his knowledge of Doris's affair, keeping Baldia inside a tacit compact (P92–110). The chapter title's “gentlemen” therefore marks an agreement among interested men, not the absence of threatened sexual control or equal law. The next scene finds Isaac in the guest room with a bottle confiscated from Baldia, identified as what was put in his tea (P114–115). Will reports its very strong aphrodisiac enchantment; Isaac recognizes the traditional love potion and explicitly says, 「僕は毒物に耐性をつけているけれど、流石に魔法薬には抵抗できないからね。今回は飲む前に気づいて良かった」 (P117–120). Ordinary poison tolerance and inability to resist magical medicine are his stated capacities; no ingested-drug resistance is demonstrated. Will also warns of mana poisoning from a mistaken dose (P121). The ensuing explanation (P124–154) specifies a lineage bargain: the family can make strong temporary and permanent preparations, the Crown tolerates relatively lax permanent-product circulation because rulers profit from access, and protected sage succession compensates restricted circulation. Melissa broke an actual family arrangement. Its existence does not make it an impartial safety regime.

The retained bottle then becomes a test of chosen means (P155–168). Will watches Isaac pocket it and asks his purpose; Isaac states a magical apprentice's interest in researching such a rare preparation (P159–160). That interest is his explanation, not a performed experiment or proof of harmless future custody. Will presses the separate question of using it on someone. Isaac answers no, initially in the language of enjoyment—「しないよ。だって、それじゃあ楽しくない」—then narration relates his choice of means to being able to smile before Ark and Monica (P162–165). He wants a result whose manner he can own before particular valued witnesses. His next question explicitly imagines obtaining a girl he likes (P166), locating this choice in a romantic aim rather than an abstract universal prohibition. Will still identifies a tendency to disregard means for ends, and Isaac cannot deny it. This is neither a universal consent pledge nor an empty refusal: an available coercive instrument is rejected while blackmail and compromised alliances remain actual. Detection, preservation/confiscation, blackmail and the spoken refusal are **OBSERVABLE_TEXT_EVENT**; P120's capacity distinction and the stated research purpose are **CHARACTER_REPORT_BELIEF**, Will's potency/dose warnings are attributed technical reports, and the moral-witness rationale is **NARRATOR_PRESENTATION / focalized appraisal**. Chosen restraint is the inference these distinctions support. Will's care includes both a practical warning and a question that could resist the master's chosen method. In the final exchange Brendan already knows the apprenticeship through bloodline inference, while still not knowing the substitution (P171–188). Will himself is surprised. Attentive service does not make every servant equally informed.

<a id="e009"></a>
**E009 - adults can authorize play without making its value instrumental.** Chapter five **わくわくお泊まり会**, p-008:P4–162, starts with Highorn respecting Monica's preference for an inn rather than pressing his own desire for a visit. Mild magical depletion changes the practical opportunity: he arranges a doctor, bath and rooms. Monica is allowed to recover rather than having to prove she deserved rescue. Care still exerts pressure locally: servants insist on Claudia's costly blue dress despite her having brought clothing; she refuses the offer to keep it (P34–39). Hospitality can be welcome in its total effect and coercive in a detail.

Highorn's intervention about board games is addressed to Cyril's particular inherited prohibition. His biological father called them vulgar; the adoptive father whom he calls 父上 supplies historical, respectable precedents (P45–80). This does not erase the former parent or prove every anxiety cured. It changes what the present boy can permit himself. Monica openly discloses her counting advantage, and Highorn chooses a simple game involving luck and narrated incidents. Experiencing another person's life as something like reading becomes its rationale (P83–100). The game does not need to train better civil servants to warrant the evening. Its comic injuries, lost purse and stalled journeys echo earlier real vulnerabilities inside a rule-bound space where a friend's help can be gratuitous. Raul moves his already finished piece to assist Cyril, although the written rules did not require it. The exasperation and eventual welcome at the finish matter more than optimal competition. Highorn observes their intimacy without demanding a confession from Cyril.

Raul's subsequent viewpoint (P163–211) gives that pleasure a costed history. He stops despite wanting another game because his friends are tired: a real received limit. Earlier attempts to make friends by smiling failed; children threw stones without malicious intent, and their innocence wounded him especially (P179). The Roseburg household made him a hero while outsiders inherited the image of Rebecca the evil witch. Vegetable and flower research is his attempt to make magic that can create friends rather than demand admiration for destructive power. The danger is not imaginary: even Raul cannot activate Rebecca's black-flame grimoire, whose ancient mana requirement is speculative rather than measured. His independent motives make friendship desire intelligible without making every intrusive behavior harmless. Later consent cannot be assumed merely because he long wanted company.

### Partial knowledge protects differently

<a id="e010"></a>
**E010 - ordinary care and disciplined force precede the spectacular emergency.** Chapter six **霧の結界、落ちた片眼鏡**, p-009:P4–92, returns to Owen's regret, specifying the threat to his father's treatment. His judgment that this remains an excuse belongs to him; the narration does not simply erase coercion. Protection of Rosalie and Leonora includes frustrating rules about escorts and a broken doll. Lin and Byron cannot sew it properly, Owen knows his own limits, a landlady provides repairs, and Byron offers his wife's embroidery. Byron's marriage after graduation is now narrated, but this does not retroactively prove the earlier handkerchief's meaning was communicated at its first opportunity. Leonora tugs the doll's head; Byron kneels and speaks through the doll's hurt, eliciting concern. Louis's remembered sewing is also a real domestic skill that resists a uniformly violent caricature. Lin's ominous story and the doll's braided likeness create a joke, not evidence of actual prophecy.

After a full day of rest, Monica can study and work again (P96–126). Her admiration of Bradford's sixfold formula is specific: it balances velocity and power beautifully, but even dividing the work she cannot reproduce it. His vast reserves do not eliminate exceptional control. The public paper's availability does not make mastery transferable by reading. His response to the university fog (P130–207) likewise refuses the obvious “cannon” stereotype. He declines Ugo's proposal to blast the barrier because people, buildings and surrounding risks matter; checks a small opening with low-power fire; tests toxicity; enters alone; and sends his pupil to the right patron. Boastful reassurance is addressed to Ugo's anxiety without making the underlying capacity fictitious. The pupil trusts his master and performs the assigned communication. Monica later finds this trust startling in comparison with Louis and Glen, so the book supplies competing models of a teacher rather than a generic mentor virtue.

The institutional route is itself a resource (P211–249). Highorn can request the right sages more quickly than the guild's hierarchy, Cyril asks about people before books, and all initially underestimate the danger. In the last section (P253–287), the prose withholds the disoriented man's name while giving his monocle, braid and staff. His belief that he is a student and his suspicion of altered corridors arrive before explanation. The reader is made to infer a current identity from material inconsistencies; this formally prepares the counter-cues that will later return him to himself.

<a id="e011"></a>
**E011 - “only a misunderstanding” changes what a person can do.** Chapter seven **勘違いの魔術**, p-010:P4–217, lets the rescuers distinguish a powerful construction from Louis's style before knowing its purpose. Wasteful design, remembered dangerous roses, dry curls and dry skin are particular clues, not omniscient sensing. Monica can read advanced theory yet falls while maintaining wind and walking. Once the wind fails she calls Cyril Barney, while Raul calls him Mary. Crucially, Cyril has not become younger or more polite through his own enchantment: he has never visited Minerva, so the spell cannot attach to that memory (P170–187). Monica's altered manner produces an impression through her relationship to the person she imagines.

The narrator calls the effect weaker than complete control, 「ただ少し勘違いさせるだけの魔術」 (P181). Its danger is nevertheless substantial because it lets the target's own memories supply identity and available action. A sage's staff, a friend's correction and a cleared view can interrupt the error, but they do not permanently immunize Monica. Her insight into the compound fog also leads to a responsible refusal: breaking containment and blowing it outdoors risks exporting quasi-forbidden mental interference to others (P188–196). Expertise accepts an additional burden rather than defining success as private escape. The discarded staff and scarred wall are then read through Louis's habits: his maxim about hitting before chanting explains throwing it (P221–267). Cyril cannot quite understand, but can accept evidence through his own prior encounters. Knowing another person is a practical interpretive resource; it need not entail approving him.

<a id="e012"></a>
**E012 - confidence in rank does not confer intimacy.** Chapter eight **無言のエヴァレットと初代悪童**, p-011:P4–182, makes the title's cross-generational meeting a frightening “miracle.” The p161 low-angle image towers Louis above Monica's hunched, trembling body. Her remembered student rumors are not a complete independently witnessed history, while his current tongue-clicking and coarse questions are directly present. Cyril places his body between them and tries an intelligible explanation; his back reminds Monica that she is the Silent Witch and can act. Fallon's attack knocks him away, and she supplies a wind cushion and then a successful shield. Protection moves both ways rather than establishing permanent adult/child roles.

The division of labor follows particular susceptibility and skill. Monica repairs the barrier formula in five seconds, preserves containment, and identifies thirteen linked stakes; Raul breaks the fixing magic through reserves that look like mere strength to Cyril (P113–147). The imagined Louis commentary calling them a monstrous pair is explicitly counterfactual narration, not something he actually says or learns here. Cyril's farewell energizes Monica through council memory. His later focalization, however, supplies a limit to that cherished route: she gave the imagined Barney a trusting casual face she has not shown him. His anger is explicitly toward himself, not toward her or an asserted romantic rival (P160–176). Useful reliability and rank have not won identical access. Whether the unease includes other kinds of desire remains open; the text's stated self-reproach must not be replaced by jealousy as a settled diagnosis.

<a id="e013"></a>
**E013 - a teacher recognizes a wish that a formula cannot explain.** Fallon's viewpoint (p-011:P186–235) supplies evidence independent of MacLagan's later interpretation. He planned friendship, marriage and patronage as a route to sagehood, and once played at being the Cannon magician. Louis's success offends that designed future, while Louis's perceived indifference denies the equality Fallon simultaneously disclaims. His sense of dispossession does not establish that Louis actually stole a position. The lake-freezing test and the fog now demonstrate the bell's efficacy, but operation still requires stolen tools, rewritten ground magic and Thornley's adapted formula. Two of five uses are spent; three remain. The revelation gives Thornley a new role as a technical source without proving a fresh conversation or mental recovery.

MacLagan's intact cognition is an instructive counterexample to Monica's duration hypothesis (P239–313). He has not disproved memory-dependent susceptibility: he protected himself with a stationary barrier before exposure. Waiting is a deliberate choice because his powerful water solution would flood the school. His seemingly vague manner coexists with experienced restraint. On learning the culprit was his own laboratory pupil, he chooses to intervene rather than remain an evacuated civilian. His regret about the first arrest is spoken; Monica's theory that an honorary professorship pressed him toward retirement is explicitly her inference, not established institutional history (P314–318).

Their ensuing discussion (P319–373) links teaching with knowledge of undesired wishes. Monica can decode an inefficient formula more easily than a motive that wants an impossible past. MacLagan's account—that Fallon wants a dream of beating Louis as a student—is supported by the prior focalization but is still an interpretation rather than a psychiatric diagnosis. His question about fantasy gives Monica a partial bridge through her own numerical retreat after bereavement. The bridge is limited: she still cannot feel Fallon's demand to attract hostile attention, and narration traces her thin demand for attention even to childhood. She elects to learn that such feelings exist, recalling the break with Barney, 「理解も共感もできずとも」 (P361). Teaching here adds attentive recognition without requiring universal emotional likeness. It extends the earlier acceptance of unshared value into a concrete pedagogy of dangerous motives.

<a id="e014"></a>
**E014 - returning a self requires a personally legible discrepancy.** Chapter nine **二大怪植物、奇跡の共闘**, p-012:P4–165, inhabits Louis's mistaken student self. Narration gives both a survival practice against being underestimated and an independent native appetite for fighting (P13–18). Class hostility explains part of the violence without exhausting or excusing it. His adolescent repertoire cannot answer an adult enemy's shortened remote attacks, and injury accumulates. Monica's imperfect Mirror Prison both interrupts the attack and confronts him with his own later invention. He recognizes something he could not yet know as that schoolboy. The pronoun correction 「俺が……私が」 and returned monocle enact recovered public identity (P73–83), rather than a mere costume restoring wisdom by itself.

Her formal disgust at her own uneven barrier does not erase its success. Louis recognizes an extraordinary feat in merely activating it without chanting; she sees departures from his standard, and he infers that she knowingly violated her aesthetic rule to restore him (P91–102). This is useful counterevidence to treating perfectionism as absolute incapacity to act imperfectly. Nor does rescue confer tactical infallibility: the captured Fallon turns out to have been replaced by an illusion after the reflected attack. Louis's quick bodily protection and barrier are needed in return. Monica then publicly says the enemy wants his attention, sincerely conveying MacLagan's diagnosis while inadvertently humiliating him into silence (P126–145). Her ignorance of the insult's force produces a tactical effect, not proof that she manipulates affect knowingly.

<a id="e015"></a>
**E015 - a failed experiment becomes a tool without becoming a successful crop.** The other half of chapter nine (p-012:P169–274) reunites the monster pea and rose-dragon as mana absorber and stake-extractor. Their remains were brought to the university for analysis and disposal, so the availability is causally prepared. Useful reuse does not validate the earlier dosage or establish safe food production. The pea remains only partly controlled: Cyril explicitly asks how they will prevent it attacking the missing people (P202–205). Raul can precisely govern roses, not every plant. His whimsical names and Cyril's literal objections coexist with actual hazard management, while repeated “Mary” slips let Raul enjoy the novelty of being scolded by a friend. The rebuke is not the same affection on both sides.

Bradford returns carrying two staff members with a guard beside him. He too was affected, but student and adult both rescue ill people; continuity of conduct limits any equation between regression and inevitable violence (P209–225). Raul envies the reassurance he can give merely by being present. MacLagan directs civilians outward and asks Raul to go toward the library. Raul had wanted to avoid the inherited black-flame stigma there. Cyril's request, 「私の後輩を頼む」 (P253), changes the choice by entrusting a friend to a friend. This is a particular address that overcomes fear, not proof that the fear was trivial or external suspicion imaginary. Cyril himself accepts that wanting to help does not make him the appropriate combatant. His later plan to lend the brooch to the poisoned guard is prospective; the brief glow and his question to Romaria do not yet constitute the spirit's answer or a completed transfer.

### Adult strength can include what another person supplies

<a id="e016"></a>
**E016 - an apparently absolute weapon has relations, timings and a bodily price.** Chapter ten **一〇秒の明暗**, p-013:P4–195, progressively changes the reader's account of the bell. Monica deduces a missing mana source from the formula; Louis cautiously adds his sound-memory and confidentially transmits Darrel's testimony. Darrel is reported to be cooperating quietly in questioning (P28); this institutional aftermath is not repentance or a repaired relation with Rick. Their ignorance remains narrower than the reader's access to Fallon. A fresh ring and an enormous reaction then bring the two knowledge routes together. The third use breaks the library seals; the fourth amplifies Fallon's body enough to activate the grimoire. Black flame actually consumes spells and barriers, so the threat is not a merely vaunted trick. Yet the small floral flames and trailing vines make its danger formally continuous with the Roseburg family whose convivial magic the volume has been exploring. The p215 portrait pairs bell and open book in Fallon's hands, putting borrowed resources inside a visibly chosen action.

Louis's airborne appraisal of Monica (P145–151) prevents a heroic scale from erasing tactical fragility: instant attack, defense and support widen a team's options, while poor mobility and reactions make her a rational first target. She receives the explanation with mixed fear and relief, rather than adopting invulnerability from praise. The grimoire's device status also matters. Its operation does not consume one of the two spells a magician ordinarily sustains, letting Fallon fly and shield simultaneously (P171–174). Their answer must exploit trajectory, time and shield shape, not simply raise a larger number. Louis's spoken contempt and private assessment diverge: he knows this is a skilled, strengthened enemy. His taunt therefore cannot be treated as the narrator's proof of the opponent's worthlessness.

The counterattack (P196–259) exploits roughly ten seconds of burning through massive plants. Raul's inherited shame becomes a specifically voiced refusal of misuse—「ご先祖様の本を、悪用しないでくれよ」—rather than renunciation of his family. Roses close the ground, ascent narrows Fallon's choices, one enormous gate draws the limited-range flame, and two more open at separated vertices. Louis and Monica each act; her simultaneous pair reuses the stabilization developed for V10 rather than being invented here. The p224–225 spread joins their spoken names and faces across the luminous doors, giving shared action a visual form. It does not dissolve their unlike knowledge or the computational fatigue stated afterward.

Raul catches the falling book (P263–291), then freezes in fear that holding the ancestral weapon will make Monica recoil. Her actual hand takes his to stand up. It is decisive received counterevidence to this predicted rejection, although she is weeping about Louis rather than consciously delivering an antistigma speech. Louis has dropped her to retrieve and seal the dangerous book. His tactical reason is real, and so is her terror. Friendship is secured here through an ordinary accepted hand inside the aftermath of spectacular force; it does not require her to certify his entire lineage or pretend that his ancestor's weapon was harmless.

<a id="e017"></a>
**E017 - the lesson of defeat is a wider network, with continuing violence.** Chapter eleven **大人の戦い方 ～金と暴力、あとは人脈（コネ）～**, p-014:P4–64, initially belongs to Louis's assurance. His decision to drop Monica preserves speed and assigns sealing to a competent specialist, while assuming her nonchant landing can absorb the fear cost. The enemy's borrowed black flame has not been mastered; Louis prefers Bradford's researched balance of speed and power. But his judgment that a last fireball is containable fails immediately. Fallon uses his actual specialty—long-distance remote shooting—and the fifth bell use to divide it into seven differently timed attacks on the capital. Borrowed amplification does not make his native skill unreal. Louis infers that amplifying a body imposes a cost absent from amplifying a ground formula or device; Fallon's hemorrhage and later reported poisoning support the bodily danger.

The response makes an apparent contradiction productive. Louis announces that someone who distrusts his own power and depends on another's will not beat him (P23), then wins precisely through other people's work. The distinction supported by the sequence is between substituting an unmastered borrowed weapon for developed skill and combining developed, differently limited skills; it is not self-sufficiency. He explicitly credits losing to Monica at the hearing with learning to add connections to money and violence (P57–64). This is an actual narrated update from the V09 planning defeat, not a retrospective discovery that he secretly intended to lose. His family now gives him something to protect and therefore changes which tactics he can afford.

MacLagan, Bradford and Ugo intercept six shots (P68–106). The pupil shares sensed positions but cannot supply altitude without sight; the old teacher's eyesight and water's reach require a divided response. Bradford destroys three high shots, MacLagan consumes three lower ones and catches the fallout. Ugo misses the delayed seventh and admits it aloud. His mastery of support is genuine and fallible. Louis's earlier private expenditure, Lin's restricted contract communication and Owen's device-assisted barrier catch that last attack (P110–159). Lin cannot operate the device herself because excessive magical strength would misfire it; Owen's human limit is the needed fit. He doubles the supplied barrier with his own, redirects pressure rather than simply enduring it, and Lin removes scattered fire. The p235 composite makes the observing pupil indispensable between two powerful adults; p241 gives Owen the active defensive posture. Narration credits the Barrier magician's victory while explicitly displaying the people through whom it happens. Such credit need not be mistaken for sole labor.

The captured man's 「どうして」 is read as accusation directed outward rather than inquiry into his choices (P163–194). Louis refuses to grant a rivalry to someone who repeatedly strikes those around him instead of him. This is his judgment, supported by both attacks, but it cannot make Louis an impartial assessor of every prior fight. The distinction also matters for the larger ethical design: bonds enlarge vulnerability and legitimate protection without authorizing every method used in their name. His imminent plan to smoke the prisoner remains a proposed punishment accompanied by tying, not a narrated completed torture. MacLagan's dry correction of his teaching and Glen's ordinary smoked ham supply present criticism (P283–295). The student is more normal than the master expects. Care and learned cooperation have changed Louis without abolishing domination or violence.

<a id="e018"></a>
**E018 - discovering responsibility does not mean having done nothing before.** After the rescue (p-014:P198–325), Cyril has actually lent the brooch to the poisoned guard and coordinates officials while watching the rescued people's health. Monica sees a way to be an adult without sage-scale force (P198–203). MacLagan's teacherly duty then makes her ashamed of having told Isaac what kind of magician she wants him to be without sufficiently considering her own responsibility. Her thought that she has done nothing teacher-like is a new, severe self-appraisal (P228–234), not a warrant to erase the actual V10 primer, listened-to preference and teaching. She is learning an additional obligation: know a pupil well enough to intervene against harm.

Cyril crouches to meet her eye, acknowledges his own junior standing in library scholarship and insists his junior can grow before calling her back to work (P251–259). The severity is now received as serious attention; the reader nevertheless retains the intimacy gap his own viewpoint disclosed. Raul's scolding names even enter Cyril's practical instructions, then need translating for Monica. Ordinary cleanup checks pollution, damaged seals and remaining vegetation; the successful battle is not sufficient verification that those systems are safe. Raul's assurance that the pea has absorbed the mana is his confident appraisal, not Cyril's completed inspection.

Monica assembles models: Cyril's reliability, MacLagan's understanding and Bradford's ability to inspire proud trust. She explicitly rejects Louis as a teaching ideal. The rejection does not prevent him giving correct professional advice (P299–325). He has read all her published papers, notices the new stabilization and warns that repeated textbook revisions impose costs on publishers and teachers. Her own mentor edits her work. Unpublished cross-attribute testing and writing still lag behind discovery. The volume therefore lets a disliked model contribute useful knowledge without making him her moral template, and lets an exceptional scholar recognize the downstream work her brilliance creates.

### A received performance can mean something its maker never intended

<a id="e019"></a>
**E019 - a successful lecture acquires an unintended public meaning.** Chapter twelve **本気のお忍び**, p-015:P4–146, changes focalizer to Conrad. He reads the hurried cleanup as routine university trouble and Raul's work clothes as a gardener's, while correctly recognizing Cyril's unchanged courteous explanations. His scholarship is not merely fandom: he studies history alongside practical magic and dislikes an institutional emphasis on power. Bradford's safety-oriented lecture revises the public symbol of brute force from within. Dragon resistance has helped produce the power bias, while formula quality grounds Conrad's admiration (P69–96). A social history of technique prevents “battle magician” from standing in for intellectual poverty.

Isabelle respects Conrad across rank as a co-worker in the earlier rescue and fellow enthusiast. Her selective contact network matters without making her every action coercive. Conrad guesses that withholding Monica's name from publicity accommodated her fear; this is his plausible explanation, not an organizer's disclosed policy. The long calculation requested in the questions could legitimately be deferred, and his belief that the questioner probably means well is also a focalized assessment (P99–110). Monica supplies the entire answer instead, then says 「記録したければ、どうぞ」. In his viewpoint the cold composure beautifully silences an inconsiderate man. The later backstage passage explicitly denies this intention: she was simply offering the record and suppressing fillers so she would not bite her tongue (P138–143). The title card's phrase thus acquires, and then loses, an apparently domineering intent.

This is not failure masquerading as competence. Her formula is correct, the work is received with genuine gratitude, and successful performance gives her a bounded piece of courage. The misreading concerns the manner's social meaning, not whether she did the job. Conrad's mistaken belief that the expressionless guard is new to magical enthusiasm is corrected next: Will has altered Isaac's body proportions as well as face, and the concealed pupil is delighted. The reader learns more than the focalizer without being invited to distrust every admiration. A person can be encouraged by an unintended public self and still need private acknowledgment of what it cost to produce.

<a id="e020"></a>
**E020 - an attempted intervention gives Will another place to turn.** The backstage reunion (p-015:P147–279) tests Monica's newly adopted duty almost immediately. Isaac and Isabelle planned the disguise, and she directs criticism toward the load it places on Will. Her imagined terrible face fails as intimidation, but her actual words are intelligible: tell her when Isaac asks too much, and she will speak as his teacher. Will explicitly accepts. Repeated addressed names culminate in narrator-affirmed mutual understanding, then a precise qualification: 「友情とは少し違う」, solidarity between those buffeted by a common person (P189–204). This revisits the V10 shore conversation through a new agreement, rather than retroactively declaring that conversation complete equality or newly inventing its affection.

Isaac's apology acknowledges the burden, yet he admits choosing Isabelle's support because it would make Monica less able to scold him (P205–220). His sincere thirst for learning and deliberate management of her response are simultaneously visible. Calling her face a sick squirrel is both funny and evidence that her desired authority has not been received as she intends. Her alliance with Will is therefore a real partial result inside an unsuccessful display of mastery, not a total stopping of the pupil's methods. Agatha, independently attentive to time, gently interrupts the two enthusiasts' praise.

The new Kerbeck trip also has an information limit. Monica notices that Isaac's participation was decided suddenly, asks, and receives an answer about business with the count plus a smile she reads as withholding purpose (P259–269). He recalls their winter two years earlier; she answers with the earlier wish to see another paper. His brief surprise and actual prepared paper make this exchange more reciprocal than the failed stern pose: her chosen intellectual address reaches him. It does not settle the unshared purpose of his trip, the household's full terms or a mutual romantic category. The reference cast's retrospective description of Will asking Brendan for help when the master might run away supplies a further reported practice, distinct from a new performed refusal in this chapter.

<a id="e021"></a>
**E021 - return home closes one threat while “winter” opens another.** The epilogue **冬が来る**, p-016:P4–56, gives Louis his family back at home and an actual period of rest. Exaggerating injury to secure leave does not make the stitched wound imaginary. Rosalie's medical knowledge defeats the exaggeration, and she enforces the rest he claimed to need. His boast about her worry and Owen's refusal to hear more keep marriage a recipient-specific comic relation rather than a universal softness. The reported severe mana poisoning and unstable interview capacity of Fallon are new bodily/institutional consequences. The bell has melted after shattering, limiting physical verification. Owen and Louis infer an external bell-assisted prison attack from testimony; they do not yet know the figure's full identity or plan.

The final focalization (P60–82) goes beyond their information. The childlike figure calls the object a **冬精霊の氷鐘（オルテリア・チヤイム）レプリカ**, says making it cost part of the self, regrets Fallon's failure, and plans to finish before spring. An apparently ten-year-old form now contrasts with the earlier early-teen appearance; do not turn manifested age into human chronology. Once-loved human vitality is now hated, while mourning for vanished lullabies, wishes and letters persists. The final paired phrases—mocking humans while cherishing irrecoverable days—refuse undifferentiated malignity without excusing the gift of destructive power. The strong ancient ice spirit, snow mountain and audience gate are a plan needing another's capacity, not an already recruited ally or opened gate. Romaria's earlier glow is available as a possible connection, not an identification of the intended old ice spirit.

<a id="e022"></a>
**E022 - the secret episode puts a commercial clock beneath the winter visit.** **ディー夫人の画策**, p-018:P4–98, takes place on a summer afternoon after the Grail, before the main winter action. Vanessa's efficient room and clipped speech are given an independent economic aim: she missed an investment opening because Hubard did not report the Grail in time. His refusal to maximize rights lets him preserve a personal debt from Monica; even its repudiation could gratify him. Two different kinds of desired return inhabit the same enterprise. Narration identifies Vanessa as Bradford's sister and gives her coercive control of her husband's property through signatures, a gun and a conditional valuation of his life as a resource (P42–47). Her retention of his social skill is neither a sentimental rescue nor proof that networks are ethically neutral. It darkly echoes the main chapter's money, force and connections.

Her hoped-for workshop depends on a high-mana Kerbeck site, six months of investigation and legal/material conditions (P67–98). A furnace can enchant metal without a magician, but its extraction is vastly greater than a tournament barrier's; land already occupied by spirits or dragons is barred. A location just outside a designated danger zone does not thereby become empty, stable or harmless, especially when new beings may settle and the furnace attracts fire spirits. Thus economic expansion supplies a concrete institutional mechanism for later habitat questions without proving a future violation. Hubard accepts and anticipates Isabelle bringing Monica at the winter break. His final “hospitality” is an ominous intention, not an encounter already suffered. It does not identify Isaac's undisclosed business or complete the journey beyond this volume.

<a id="e023"></a>
**E023 - image, reference and authorial address have different evidentiary jobs.** The cover's prominent Louis and framed winter scene foreground the exposure of an adult past; the color spread's airborne carrying joins practical partnership with bodily dependence. The front color illustration previews the comic severe face that later fails to frighten Isaac, while the handshake, sleeping cart and rescue illustrations give vulnerable postures weight beside formula spectacle. These are original views of this witness, not evidence imported from an adaptation.
