## Causal synopsis and organizing argument

Hilda prepares to receive Monica at winter solstice after the hearing. Remembered failed baking and an almost empty festival walk establish how carefully, imperfectly and bodily she tried to give the frightened child an accessible world. Her own research and an old ally's planned medical reforms also continue independently. Monica now arrives with stories she wants to tell and a future near Lana that will take her farther from Hilda. Her mother's assent mixes pleasure, loneliness and an invitation to return.

The collection then moves back before school and through its seasons. Mountain solitude offers real intellectual pleasure and relief from ridicule, while concealing neglected food, cold, unanswered work and very incomplete attention to Nero. At school, short encounters make care answerable to particular needs: a place at tea, a wanted dance, an affordable gift, a hidden scar, an injured hand. Help also presumes, misreads and burdens. Two longer spirit stories turn apparent supernatural menace into a problem of recognition and communication. The mirror spirit's depleted appeal requires detection and requested protection; the homesick wind spirit needs a particular weaving song before it will accept institutional care. Cyril must confront his own shame, and Benjamin must adjust his virtuosity to that listener. Monica's failed mathematical account of music finally becomes a particular felt response rather than universal expertise.

The later school stories expose hidden labor and divergent motives behind familiar public roles: Cyril's checks had obstructed Thornley's fraud, exhaustion pays for Glen's passing grades and teachers' holiday, Lana prepares a rest she cannot secure through combat or mediation, and Elliott keeps class contempt beside chosen costly help. After the hearing, graduated friends gather with affection and unfinished antagonism. Plant research supplies a continuing institution in which Monica, Raul and Cyril need each other's different skills. On the move's first afternoon, Lana's forceful practical help includes room for Monica's own choice; a new ribbon elicits joy and an unbroken thank-you. The secret domestic episode carries that changed home outward through cookies, a visit to Lana and a letter promising Hilda the same shared work.

The strongest sustained proposition is that attachment develops through **adjusting how an act reaches someone**, not merely through receiving more benevolence. Counterexamples matter as much as successes: a golden ratio is not a textile motive, faster pulse is not proof of romance, a better song is not the remembered song, passing is not Cyril's full standard, accurate data is not the sponsor's report, and an accomplished rescue is not legal vindication. The collection adds previously withheld ordinary processes to the run's already nonzero history. ## Close reading

### A parent receives the life she could not live for her daughter

<a id="e001"></a>
**E001 — failed baking can carry real shared work.** The prologue, **送り出す日**, opens through Hilda's shopping and recollection (p-033:P11–145). A scheduled homecoming makes her insist on leave despite absorbing Grail work. Nuts had been convenient life maintenance; choosing sweets for a daughter now supplies a recipient and occasion. Childhood Monica's 「だって、すうじは、こわくないから」 is her reported answer, followed by Hilda's and the narrator's explanation of wounded language and defensive withdrawal (P40–52). Hilda also remembers earlier numerical interest and a child who could smile. This is affirmative counterevidence to treating all mathematical pleasure as an effect of abuse.

Hilda deliberately selects joint work, reachable spoken tasks and praise. Yet her research expertise fails ordinary cooking: precise measurement supposedly finishes the job; melted butter is rationalized, 「ふるい入れる」 becomes vigorous throwing, and cutting-mixing becomes knife work. Narratorial corrections let the reader see what neither participant can repair. Her ventilation warning about combustible powder is technically informed and misapplied to a situation her own error created. The comedy does not make her desire to protect false. Monica's respectful response to controlled fire and the little edible remainder allow shared accomplishment despite failed cookies. Hilda's hope that Monica experienced the same satisfaction remains her hope, not a fully shared interior fact. Successful attachment here involves fallible labor, not a perfect parent teaching a deficient child.

<a id="e002"></a>
**E002 — the survivor's work has other custodians.** The anonymous medical authority in the shop had helped Hilda escape with Venedict's designs (p-033:P146–169). His informal manner coexists with influence in the medical association and magicians' guild. Their secrecy protects both sides; it is neither proof of criminal aims nor an already public reform. He reports having already spoken to both professional bodies. Medical-device deregulation may lead to reconsideration of forbidden research and the charges under which Venedict died (P154–159). That supplies an independently pursued institutional route toward Q048, while the promised movement “next year” remains prospective in this scene. The Grail's real advance is narrator-presented; legal vindication, provenance disclosure and enacted regulatory change are not. Hilda's life is not exhausted by mothering or by passively storing a relic for Monica.

<a id="e003"></a>
**E003 — sending her out requires keeping an invitation open.** Hilda remembers choosing an almost empty solstice evening for a short walk, offering a finger to the child gripping her clothes; she did not force ordinary crowded exposure (p-033:P179–201/P299–313). Present Monica calls to her across an actual crowd. The contrast establishes a particular changed action, without claiming all crowds have become easy. At home she wants to tell stories, praising the council's different capacities and insisting the prince who ate barbecue is an accomplice (P211–272). Hilda's bafflement at villainess lessons and frightened check on flight keep the listener's own limits audible. The catalogue becomes reciprocal telling, not an administrative report in which perfect usefulness earns attention.

The announcement of another move initially activates Hilda's memory of mountain withdrawal. Sazandor instead means chosen proximity to Lana (P274–292). Hilda affirms the choice while privately feeling loneliness; 「でも、たまにはこっちにも顔を出してね？」 asks for continuing relation rather than making care a claim to residence. Her original wish for an open, healthy future remains unchanged even when its fulfillment takes the daughter away. Rival readings of painless maternal triumph or possessive abandonment both lose the mixed affect and the noncoercive request. This newly disclosed conversation precedes the accomplished V09 move; it does not make the earlier entering analyst foresee it.

### Solitude has pleasures, neglected bodies and selective openings

<a id="e004"></a>
**E004 — institutional labor and care do not share one motive.** Chapter one's **新年の空の旅 / 〈沈黙の魔女〉の執務室 / 〈沈黙の魔女〉と万能調味料** place Louis beside a newly appointed sage (p-035:P20–201). Her completed formula brings delight to a body that has forgotten warmth, food and sleep. Louis's bound flight and cheek-grip are forceful administration; Mary's correction of “the only female sage” exposes his dehumanizing joke. The office's unanswered invitations and lack of clear footing make his burden real, without authorizing every means. A staff later used as a drying pole illustrates practical indifference to rank's display rather than magical ignorance.

The later spring visit adds an especially diagnostic sequence. Louis walks because Lin is occupied and flight costs power/visibility, admits to himself that local directions would have helped, suggests paid household assistance, then hears Monica's wish to be alone. He does not know the cause and does not care to learn it (P172–178). Defeated pride makes her condition a problem for his own standing; he nevertheless lights her stove and offers jam. The “universal condiment” immediately acquires a recipient limit: putting it on his wife's food cost a week of speech. This is Louis's account of that consequence, not a new Rosalie viewpoint. His next thought converts Monica's paper into a dragon-mission resource while she ponders fish and jam. Care, appetite, paperwork, power recognition and exploitation coexist without one hidden benevolent motive explaining them all. These newly read earlier events do not undo his already dated V04 model revision.

<a id="e005"></a>
**E005 — the familiar relation is not born complete.** In **〈沈黙の魔女〉、逃亡中**, gratitude pursues Monica past her refusal: the proposed feast and palanquin turn being saved into an unwanted public role (p-035:P217–248). The villagers who do not recognize the underdressed witch offer clothes, giving an ordinary care response distinct from official worship. Nero wants the palanquin; her need is escape. **心無い魔女の使い魔** then returns them to the mountain after the black-dragon encounter (P264–391). Exhaustion, not secure trust, makes her indifferent to being eaten. Curiosity about the transformed body's organs and feeding remains speculative; she stops partly because it is outside her specialty and because she would want to dissect him despite being poor at dissection. She declines to devise a binding familiar contract, expects he may leave and thinks a renewed threat could be shot dead in an instant (P307–328). This is neither an original equal pact nor evidence that later affection is fraudulent. It establishes the unequal, initially attenuated attention from which the relation develops.

Nero first demands attention as a self-proclaimed irresistibly cute cat. Claws hurt her without securing uptake; he then deliberately avoids claws and uses soft pads, followed by an actual apology (P338–372). The viewpoint shift lets touch reach consciousness where shouting and pain did not. On returning to bodily hunger she asks what **Nero** eats, after he insists on his name, and plans more provisions. Yet the meat soup disappoints him. Care begins as calibration and failure, with his self-interest and her limited domestic skill intact. The strongest rival, a naturally wise therapeutic pet restoring a wholly benevolent master, misses both their initial appraisals and the work of acquiring a particular name and bodily signal.

<a id="e006"></a>
**E006 — useful action need not begin with a public-minded self-description.** **星流しの夜** begins with terror at villagers near the cabin, then lets Nero investigate the blocked river (p-035:P407–516). Monica knows why fishing and the festival matter; her explicit reason after clearing the trunk is that people will stop coming. Nero calls the answer cold. Neither the practical communal benefit nor the represented self-protective aim should erase the other. The precision that cuts the obstruction requires no sociable performance. Looking at meteors is enough for her then; the cat's insistence that he is present and her small answering laugh qualify “alone” without converting her into a festival participant.

Nero later brings a flowering branch because girls wore it while dancing. Monica's delighted thanks is real, but she hangs it as insect repellent and forgets the festival occasion (P497–508). This is a compact early version of unshared gift meaning: his disappointing uptake and her useful happiness coexist. It does not require either secret romantic knowledge or an uninterested recipient. Retain this moment beside the later roses and chosen shared coffee, rather than flattening every early action into foresight of that ending.

### School care: knowing a need, presuming a need, inventing a need

<a id="e007"></a>
**E007 — a lie can make room for someone else.** In **〈沈黙の魔女〉の小さな噓**, Monica notices that Cyril leaves when tea arrives and eventually elicits his reason: his cold body spoils everyone else's hot drink (p-036:P20–54). Calling herself **「猫舌」** gives him a reason to stay. The closing disclosure that she drinks hot morning coffee establishes deliberate accommodation, not a truthful confession of shared physiology. Her fear of asking him persists; nevertheless she observes a repeated absence and changes a small social arrangement. This is important counterpressure against treating all concealment as self-protection or all speaking as emancipatory. The recipient's inclusion is purchased by a particular fiction, whose benefit is visible but whose long-term necessity is untested.

**子リスの食事事情** makes the opposite problem palpable (P70–103). Felix recognizes real hunger and a real bodily startle: his request that she hold out her hands elicits a posture ready for shackles or whipping. His three cake slices become her plan for five further meals after she eats half a slice, because avoiding the dining hall matters more to her than quantity. Taking the cake and feeding her himself addresses undernourishment while overriding that plan, with intimate contact and a promise of tomorrow's provision. The scene is affectionate, funny and unequal. Neither “he understands all her needs” nor “nothing but manipulation” accounts for the correct recognition and unilateral remedy together. These are earlier school interactions, not evidence that the uninformed V09 pupil bargain has already been clarified.

<a id="e008"></a>
**E008 — friends' help has its own pleasures and mistakes.** Lin and Nero's all-night **スタイリッシュ神経衰弱** values stylish card flips until they forget the memory game's objective; Monica solves it briskly and declines its grand title (p-036:P119–158). Their private amusement exceeds their service roles. In **メイドの矜持**, Lin hears Monica praise Isabelle's nursing and proposes that Monica fall ill so she can demonstrate superiority (p-037_split_000:P64–102). Redirected toward Louis, she asks when he will be near death. The joke depends on a recognizably independent desire for maidly distinction, not an absence of feeling. It also makes useful service capable of manufacturing a need in order to satisfy itself.

Isabelle's dancing produces a different adjustment (same member P118–165). Monica's admiration is bodily and aesthetic before she can formulate it; numbers are not its hidden object. Isabelle wants to learn the male part and cross-dress for her, but Monica explicitly begs her to retain the villainess she loves, and the plan stops. The distinction from the imposed Cecily/Wanda education in AFTER04 matters: this scene supplies an actual refusal that changes a helper's proposal. It does not retrospectively make all Isabelle's protective arrangements informed or voluntary. Her orphanage read-aloud work also has an independent aim—making friends with reading—and a feared separation to negotiate (p-036:P1144–1188). Making the children heroic allies is a tactful story for departure; it is not proof that they no longer fear losing her.

<a id="e009"></a>
**E009 — misreading is distributed across the council.** Monica's tape measure and the carpet's golden triangles bring delighted, purposeful investigation; Cyril's agreement turns out to come from textile enthusiasm, not shared mathematical motivation (**魅惑の黄金三角形**, p-036:P174–202). In **モニカの自慢**, Felix initially assumes her absorbed smile concerns numerical patterns and is corrected by her story of choosing a comb with Lana (p-037_split_000:P181–212). His subsequent “cute” aims at Monica; she receives it as praise of the comb and friend. Both can care and still have the wrong account of an object of pleasure. The scene corrects a reductive model of Monica without confirming reciprocal romance.

The cat teaser anecdote gives Cyril an unperformed wish: Nero flees anticipated revenge, while Cyril only wanted to pet him; his “weeding” cover then recruits Monica's earnest offer of labor (p-036:P218–259). The dog/cat debate lets council members have ordinary preferences; Monica's answer **「肉球があれば、どっちでも」** hurts Nero's claim to singularity enough to cost her a week of access to his pads (P275–328). These are not interchangeable mascot jokes. They show how bodies and apparently trivial preferences make affiliation specific, and how a recipient may refuse a pleasure without ceasing to belong.

<a id="e010"></a>
**E010 — mathematical attention is neither general competence nor emotional incapacity.** The scattered archive boxes create an actual danger: Cyril catches Monica while holding a box, she asks him to save himself, and her hidden identity inhibits the magical escape available to her (**生徒会の散らかし魔達**, p-036:P344–447). Elliott's untidiness and provocation help cause the trap; his own box then traps him too. Felix's strength resolves it, followed by compulsory cleaning. Monica's number-based organization is meaningful to her but inaccessible to colleagues. The comedy distributes failed anticipation and useful skill rather than merely exhibiting one clumsy girl rescued by perfect men.

**天才に紙とペンを与えるな** supplies Elliott's legitimate frustration as well as his forceful response: a post-game review becomes Benjamin's composition and Monica's eight-queens problem (p-037_split_001:P318–347). Their absorption is joyful and intellectually real, yet neglects an agreed collaborative task. Conversely **モニカ・ノートンと温度計** follows Monica's attempt to prevent another baking failure into school-wide temperature measurements (P686–749). She pursues an ambiguous instruction with extraordinary initiative, while missing the material difference between softened and melted butter. Cyril's ordinary household knowledge and the teacher's supervision make the eventual cookies succeed. The prologue's failed joint baking is therefore not answered by newly sufficient genius; learning includes knowing when precision is irrelevant and another person's practical discrimination is necessary.

The earlier **お菓子作りは計量が命** is more cooperative (p-036:P571–616). Casey proposes a modest preparation of three unattractive apples; Lana arrives with expensive ingredients supplied by her household servant, Monica with exact measurement. Casey's private pressure and limited time prevent calling this uncomplicated generosity. The cake succeeds through differentiated work, but the enlargement of her task is a cost that the helpers do not initially see. A recipient-specific account of help must preserve that cost alongside the happy result.

### Two spirits, different needs; a song that cannot be replaced by technique

<a id="e011"></a>
**E011 — an uncanny mirror is a failed route of address.** **鏡に映る未来** begins with Glen proposing a horror gathering and then proving especially frightened himself (p-036:P632–1128). Mechanical explanations of earlier ghost stories do not eliminate bodily fear. The school mirror's rumored future, whispers and drain are linked to five students reporting illness with anemia-like symptoms; the council owes reassurance as well as an explanation. Its deceased owner, サムス・ホレイソン〈水鏡の魔術師〉, provides an archival route; his family donated the mirror. Claudia's account of exploited relatives and skeletal death adds a character's warning, not a narrator-certified law that Highorn attracts every nonhuman being. Her jealous interruption and the investigators' screams actually worsen the rumor. Competent institutions and anxious children coexist.

Monica is initially diverted by a self-similar pattern; Cyril asks whether it explains the phenomenon, and it does not. A displaced triangle later becomes the diagnostic opening. She feigns illness to Lana, whose worry is real, in order to return secretly with Lin and Nero. The hidden gem holds an upper water spirit that chose sleep after its contractor died. Depleted power, the distorted reflection and desperate attempted absorption explain the “future” and faintings. Its **「みつけてくれて、ありがとう」** answers discovery as recognition, not conquest. Monica supplies power and asks Louis, through Lin, to arrange release or institutional management. The final disposition is **not shown**. Preserve this limit: finding and requesting custody do not establish either completed liberation or a new contract. This spirit is not Romaria; the later reader's similarities must not merge their histories.

The mirror also gives several futures without predicting them. Neil's hopeful taller reflection, Cyril's and Monica's privately unwelcome prospects, and Felix's declaration that an unwanted future can be changed occupy different epistemic positions. Monica admires his apparently innate royal resolve. The EXTRA09 reader already knows the impersonation and cannot adopt her appraisal as proof of rightful princely identity. Her own final wish—to complete the mission with pride—still belongs to this earlier knowledge boundary. A slight smile at friends' voices gives the ending its modest formal answer: no guaranteed future appears, but the present relation alters the face looking toward it.

<a id="e012"></a>
**E012 — the right song is particular, and protection must hear the protected.** **パタンと一織り、紡ぐ歌** first stages Monica's music failure as a misleading interrogation of her secret (p-037_split_000:P480–809; p-037_split_001:P2–302). The **無詠唱／口パク** joke joins public performance to magical invention without making the substitution work at school. Cyril is right that the assignment is not a paper, but his command to sing forces a sobbing performance. Benjamin's defense of individual response opposes a flattening “ordinary” appreciation; his metaphors of vibrating souls nevertheless send Monica into literal calculations. The narrator explicitly marks their conversation's mismatch. Nobody's useful specialty is a complete translation of someone else's experience.

Glen's hidden cooking first receives a practical alternative to a fire hazard: Felix arranges kitchen access, with unexpectedly good bacon reaching the dormitory (p-037_split_000:P228–269). The later discovery of a spirit-possessed puppy complicates that familiar solution. Glen's care is genuine while his “very clever dog” explanation is wrong; Monica correctly detects overlapping powers but hesitates to disclose expertise. Felix asks the being itself and proposes MacLagan and a specialist institution. The spirit says **「イヤ」**. The best available medical arrangement initially fails its expressed wish to hear a particular song. Glen's plea and Felix's revised search make protection responsive without denying the danger of depletion.

Benjamin recognizes the loom rhythm and names the ordinary weaving song of Ashendalte. Fine hymns have pleased the spirit without being the desired song; expertise must distinguish aesthetic quality from remembered attachment. Its displaced habitat and scattered companions give the wish a history. Cyril stopped singing at work after learning the prince heard him, from shame rather than royal prohibition. His interior adds a further source of shame: his changed adolescent voice no longer carries the rare childhood maternal praise attached to their shared song. He refuses even Felix's request. That actual refusal is decisive counterevidence to an account in which reverence always compels immediate obedience.

Felix's reminder that Cyril made Monica sing exposes a real asymmetry but does not persuade him. Glen does: this listener wants **his** song, not any better singer's. The hesitant **「一回だけだからな」** sets a limit rather than proving the shame cured. Benjamin first plays an ostentatious introduction, then rejects his own display as inappropriate to the homesick recipient. His simpler accompaniment is a consequential exercise of judgment. Monica hears colors, gentleness and a particular voice; she does not suddenly acquire universal taste or a method for reading all minds. The spirit sings with Cyril, its breath and light enact gratitude, and it subsequently accepts transfer through MacLagan to Minerva's magical-organism laboratory. Transfer is narrated here, unlike the mirror spirit's requested arrangement; later treatment is still unknown.

Felix's favorable reading of the rewritten appreciation paper and Monica's boast that she has read Cyril's soul close the academic plot while preserving comic misapplication. A teacher's actual grade is not shown. Cyril's alarm at becoming the subject and her flight prevent a sentimental finish in which everyone transparently agrees. The scene supplies evidence of a specific nonnumerical response, of Cyril's care overcoming one refusal, and of expertise revised for a recipient. It supplies neither a permanent cure for public singing nor permission to ignore the original coercive demand made of Monica.

### Responsibility can be shared without making its beneficiary a dependent

<a id="e013"></a>
**E013 — Cyril's accounting was protective before it was understood.** **会計の責務** begins inside Thornley's problem, not Cyril's self-explanation (p-037_split_001:P571–670). The teacher could not embezzle during Cyril's year: monthly cash checks and interest in old records forced replacement of money and selective disclosure. Extra work deliberately blocked investigation; Cyril mistook it for confidence in him. This establishes real protective effect and a mechanism of authority's deception at once. It changes the reader's causal account of “not detecting the fraud”; it does not make Cyril secretly omniscient or prove every prior angry response justified.

When the records return, Cyril apologizes for shouting at Monica and then tries to do all the shelving as penance. The narration distinguishes his present self-directed bitterness from anger at her. Monica's unprepared intervention claims work as **her** present responsibility, rather than declaring his pain baseless. Felix adds his own failure to notice the deception, offers three-person efficiency, then asks for chocolate. Monica recognizes the attempt to prevent crushing self-blame. The request restores Cyril's upright eagerness through his familiar reverence. Thus the same structure that can overburden him also supplies a usable route out of solitary punishment; this is a situated redirection, not evidence that service has become unconditional self-worth. Compare the later V09 interruption of sacrificial self-blame without projecting its resolution backward.

<a id="e014"></a>
**E014 — Lana's silence protects an unrequested disclosure.** **ラナの手紙** grants Lana a private recognition Monica never notices (p-037_split_001:P765–811). The old back scars shock her. She does not know the perpetrator or circumstances; the reader's prior knowledge does not become hers. Suppressing an outcry avoids making Monica explain what she may not wish to tell. Lana also imagines violently confronting anyone who adds a new wound: restraint here is purposeful and limited, not indifference or universal gentleness.

Her dress letter translates knowledge into construction: cover the worst scars, adjust volume, avoid jewels and conspicuous extravagance that would make Monica shrink. The desired response is Monica's pleasure. Her worry that she may be imposing remains audible, and the request for her father's resources preserves the material basis of the gift. This is unusually strong evidence against reducing her clothing interest to vanity or making silence merely ignorance. It also resists a perfect-helper reading: the adjustments are her considered proposal, not a scene of fully informed joint design. In the comb anecdote Monica receives a friend's shared outing as happiness; here the reader learns some of the labor and withheld knowledge behind such pleasure.

<a id="e015"></a>
**E015 — friendship need not remain a pair.** The festival cake first recalls Monica's earlier half with Felix, now understood as friendship through Isabelle's explanation (**仲良しを半分こ**, p-037_split_001:P519–555). Isabelle's attempt to recreate festival informality produces unequal halves; Monica divides her larger portion to include Agatha. Isabelle follows with an unusually childlike tug and request. Agatha's initial professional demurral and eventual acceptance keep rank visible while allowing this particular shared pleasure. The scene offers an enacted redistribution, not merely an inclusive slogan, and does not imply abolition of the household hierarchy. Read beside the poison reprisal's newly disclosed background: Count Norton supplied the adoptive family connection that allowed Louis's intervention; Isabelle's family's gratitude and her fear that royal favor might protect Caroline drive retaliation (p-037_split_000:P11–48). That feared impunity is Isabelle's expectation, not an actual narrated decision by Felix.

The school diversions resist deterministic character charts from within their own comedy. Glen's borrowed affinity horoscope labels frost possessors rejecting and exclusionary, precisely while Cyril adjusts his collar and scarf and is thanked for being attentive (**属性診断**, p-036:P463–496). Magical affinity is a real constraint; the personality diagnosis is contradicted by observed conduct. Claudia's chess exertion likewise begins from the word “together,” not love of the game; being placed across Felix from Neil redirects her romantic frustration into overwhelming play (p-037_split_000:P406–464). Her independent aim is visible without being admirable in every effect. Finally **いってきます** discloses that Monica's mountain departure once meant relief from ridicule and hope that Barney might forgive her (p-036:P512–555). Returning to dance practice now includes bodily pain and friends waiting. Disclosure order makes the contrast intelligible; chronology prevents reading either the old relief as a new retreat or the present departure as an extinction of solitary pleasure.

### Service has different recipients, costs and standards of success

<a id="e016"></a>
**E016 — Louis's domestic preference can interrupt institutional sociability.** **新婚魔術師は早く家に帰りたい** makes fine magical work depend on hearing a sealing barrier's minute warning sounds; earplugs would sacrifice necessary information (p-037_split_001:P363–398). The sentient Mira's possessive courtship turns his wife into an obstacle. Louis counters with a sustained account of wife and expected child, exhausting his voice while finishing the release. The technique is comic but specific to the device's willingness to listen while he talks, not a universal spell defeated by marital purity. Mary offers food and summons Monica; Louis wants home but decides recent assassination and tournament incidents warrant checking on his colleague. This private weighing gives both responsibility and resentment evidence beyond his elegant public manner.

**グレンの礼服** then separates receiving a gift from knowing its funding (p-038:P20–65). Glen respects Louis's ability while explicitly distrusting his character and suspecting a bill or ulterior motive. Lin reports the expense rationale; only the narrator identifies the king as the party billed through the protection mission. Glen's free schooling and costly clothes need not be fraudulent benefits, but neither makes him informed about the mission. Later Glen's efficient first aid has two causes: frequent injuries in Louis's training and observation of Rosalie treating him (**合理的な住処**, P421–458). Do not call the resulting skill proof that the injuries were necessary. Monica's genuine delight at a well-equipped infirmary also fails to receive Claudia's barb as intended. Literal uptake can be self-protective pleasure as well as social failure; the resident doctor retains his own objection to becoming her landlord.

<a id="e017"></a>
**E017 — Lin's inquiry exposes the inadequacy of a bodily sign.** In **心の臓を持たねども**, Monica initially consents to a pulse check during the princess-carry experiment (p-037_split_001:P414–503). She distinguishes fear of falling from the excitement of beautiful formulas even though both make the heart race. Nero's gender generalization is explicitly undercut by his having seen her fail to swoon over princes. Lin's male transformation precipitates panic and fainting; her initial inference of a stronger romantic effect is wrong. Later she herself revises it from Monica's distressed sleep. Consenting to one check is therefore not proof that the whole improvised sequence was understood or safe.

The focalization then leaves Monica to give Lin's question its own urgency. She remembers overwhelming excitement without possessing blood or a heart and uses humans' measurable bodies because she cannot inspect such a signal in herself. The flat assertion that she and Louis have **「信頼、友愛、敬愛、恋愛等の感情は一切ありません」** is a defensive character utterance beside that remembered sensation, not a narrator's certification of emotional emptiness. Nero's unfinished identification does not establish romance either. The unresolved object and category of feeling are worth retaining. Her delivery of Ray by terrifying experimental flight later produces an actual injury to his sense of safety (**愛されたがりの休日**, p-038:P530–566); an offered menu of landings supplies no narrated informed selection. Curiosity, service, humor and disregard for a recipient's body coexist.

<a id="e018"></a>
**E018 — Cyril's care runs through duty but exceeds a single authority.** Helping Neil finish festival work is planned rather than impulsive (**お兄様、奮闘中**, p-038:P145–193). Cyril works ahead to free a dance with Claudia, fears she would call it meddling, values Neil despite the rank gap, and asks for secrecy so the invitation will not seem compelled. Neil privately welcomes this future brother. Their two viewpoints give the relationship a reciprocal but differently articulated value. This is not Cyril simply ordering a lower noble to satisfy a sister: the work and thanks are actual, and the concealed facilitation aims to leave their encounter its own meaning.

The study sequence refuses an equally flattering ideal of effortless tutorly care (P274–355/P732–783). Monica explains arithmetic step by step but knowingly settles for procedural success before full understanding. Her own numerical mnemonic is unusable as a general method; Glen adapts it into meat names, risking royal disrespect. His trade communication and practical magic reveal strengths that written examinations miss, while Claudia correctly identifies the limits of improvisation for factual history. Cyril's notes and later all-subject labor help avert remedial classes. His royal-duty vocabulary is not the whole effect: the teachers get their holiday back at the expense of his sleep. Once Glen barely passes, Cyril immediately notices omissions and assigns more work. Relief, kindness, severe standards and institutional reliance coexist; neither the teachers' “savior” nor Glen's praise establishes sustainable distribution of labor. Elliott's braid prank exploits the exhausted tutor's sleep; Monica apologizes although Elliott is the instigator, and Bridget's cold look supplies a dissenting response (P209–258).

**副会長の配慮** offers a quieter reciprocal arrangement (p-039:P139–189). Cyril exchanges heavy archive searching for a light errand. He gives limited space, cold and efficiency as reasons; the text does not explicitly disclose that he has discovered Monica's hidden injured hand. Her fear of that discovery must not be promoted into his knowledge. Monica then notices that frost affinity does not make him immune to cold and wants to bring warm tea. Help becomes an opportunity to give something suited to him, rather than another proof of her incompetence. This returns to E007's tea accommodation through a different sequence and with continuing incomplete knowledge.

<a id="e019"></a>
**E019 — Elliott's class model meets an exception it cannot comfortably name.** His winter-market thoughts are emphatically hierarchical (p-038:P632–663). Shopping like commoners shames him; Cyril's reluctance to use servants makes him unfit for nobility in Elliott's appraisal. The narrative supplies Benjamin's dissent and a closing embarrassment, not an egalitarian conversion. Wreath making then reveals the inferior novice behind his competition: he learns by watching, wishes Cyril's craft were as bad as his drawings, and takes Monica's selected pinecone despite her protest (P679–716). The new ordinary evidence preserves selfishness as well as competent contribution.

**卒業パーティの後で** makes a later action costly in his own terms (p-040:P20–127). Aid against Clockford threatens his family's position and perhaps his arranged engagement. He affirms that he chose to stop being a spectator, while still detesting the impostor and wanting him frustrated. Knowledge that Monica is a magic count collides with familiarity and her wish against reverence; he can neither tease nor properly defer. His envy of Cyril's untroubled “Norton accountant” is a precise failure of his rank-based model, not its explicit repudiation. He reads the rose-and-eye-color signal that Cyril misses, then notices Cyril's private softened face over Monica's bouquet and urges him to join the group. Affectionate perception does not cancel rivalry or establish a romance on Cyril's behalf. The final toast accepts a noisy present without claiming its hierarchy or future settled.

Two private fan stories make the other side of that difficult rank picture newly available. **隠れファンの葛藤** begins in Felix's technically precise appreciation of Cyril's duel, then lets mention of the witch's book interrupt his perfect public composure (p-038:P582–616). Wanting other people to know her greatness competes with wanting fewer people ahead of him in the library queue. He summons Cyril before the title is given, recognizes the selfishness and feels guilt when sincere concern answers him. His public smile nevertheless conceals the actual motive. The imagined pleasure of someday talking about the witch with Cyril is immediately rejected as a future he cannot have. Reader knowledge of Isaac prevents reducing this to an ordinary prince's quaint hobby; it is a forbidden possible companionship that he both wants and obstructs.

**王子様の宝物** then shows what the public role's luggage contains (p-039:P81–123). Diplomatic papers are already memorized, whereas hidden books and Monica's detailed corrections repay delighted rereading. Her **「貴方の論文が、また見たいです」** invites another contribution by him, not merely more consumption of an idol's work. He values criticism of formula construction, underwater range and insufficient argument as well as her attention. The “if something happens to me” instruction initially follows the logic of concealment: burn forbidden possessions. It stops when that would include the witch's work. Now he wants a worthy recipient and considers Cyril or Glen, postponing the disposal decision while Will silently imagines a human sigh. The humor shows real aesthetic and intellectual obligation inside the memorial program before the diary changes its course. It is consequential counterevidence to a clean division between a wholly empty former self and a wholly new pupil; it does not erase the later revelation's force or make his imagined future already available then.

<a id="e020"></a>
**E020 — Ray's appetite for affection makes politeness dangerously persuasive.** At the festival, a receptionist's constrained smile becomes, in Ray's appraisal, personal kindness; a flower seller's practiced social smile feels almost divine (p-038:P81–129). The narrator explicitly distinguishes the student's skilled public manner from his reading. Buying a rose and charity goods brings actual enjoyment—he likes flowers and drawing despite an imagined stereotype of curse workers—and Louis envies his freedom. The scene does not prove the sellers contemptuous or deliberately predatory; it proves that Ray reads more personal love into courtesy than the text warrants. His own repeated demands for love also impose on the receptionist. Later expecting extortion from anything associated with Louis and interpreting Monica's request as being loved are his opposed projections (P530–566). Retain both the burden he creates and the distress he suffers, without assuming he is merely a contemptible victim or a reliable witness to everyone's motives.

The night carriage gives an unusually clear check on such interpretation (p-039:P426–464). Ray builds a threatening ancestral portrait from Raul's silence and beauty, only for a snore and explanation of early gardening hours to replace it with sleepiness. The rescue mission's gravity remains real. Raul's friendly sleep-talk belongs beside his private wish for sage colleagues who will be friends; it does not certify that every instance of silence hides danger or every friendly person ignores national responsibilities.

### Friendship's ordinary work, and the meanings it cannot guarantee

<a id="e021"></a>
**E021 — Lana learns the scale of a gift and invents a different kind of help.** The bazaar story supplies the prior work behind the pouch already known as a received gift (p-038:P474–514). Birthday gifts in Lana's social experience are selected by parents and function as influence. She wants to choose for a friend herself. Monica's polite agreement about jewelry is not mistaken for enthusiasm; fear of making her apologetically shrink forces Lana to consider price, use and handwork. Embroidering violets promises excitement precisely because her own work may be less polished than a professional's. The result cannot be reduced to poverty romanticism: Lana values expensive presents too, and her resource-rich background makes moderation a new skill.

**〈茨の魔女〉は大張りきり** follows Monica's reciprocal initiative (p-039:P20–65). Lana requested coffee, but Monica wants something lasting as well. Asking Raul for suitable roses takes effort because refusal frightens her. He supplies an appropriate fragrant variety, declines payment, and wants a report of the friend's happiness. Receiving here allows a third party's delighted investment without establishing a debt or public display. Lana later privately cherishes the small sachet alongside her luxurious family presents and dreams of showing Monica Sazandor (**室内ピクニック**, P262–410). That wish predates the actual shared city; the source has newly disclosed it now, not supplied an analyst's earlier successful prediction.

The picnic's preparation is a substantial drama of inadequate possible help. Lana sees uncombed hair, sleeplessness, poor eating and probable vomiting; she delays questioning and grooms Monica. Neil has actually attempted mediation after the duel was arranged and failed to persuade Hubert. Glen promises victory. Lana lacks either capacity and asks what she can do. Claudia's suggestion of rest becomes a warm indoor gathering so that Monica need not be isolated or ashamed of convalescing. Food, cushions, room choice, attendance records and disclosure routes all matter. Claudia plans to use Cyril's expected alarm and Felix's management, but Lana challenges her into accepting the labor of being organizer. This is a negotiated alliance with distinct stakes, not an omnipotent friend's solitary rescue. The text stops at the decision and Lana's intended cover phrase; this episode does not itself show the picnic occurring or the institutional maneuver working.

<a id="e022"></a>
**E022 — Eliane hides the wrong signal for a reason she cannot yet name.** After Glen's illness she bakes cookies, burns her left hand and invents a servant's authorship (**背中に隠した左手**, p-039:P480–519). His unexpected recovery makes the care feel embarrassingly excessive; his uncomplicated thanks almost elicits the truth. The circulating rumor that a left-hand injury marks Felix's possible wife then makes her hide the burn from Glen. Her disquiet supplies emerging preference that she has not formulated, while his immediate tasting gives her pleasure despite its bad manners. It is not an avowal, and he does not learn who baked. No current curse is invoked. Preserve this ordinary emotional agency beside C018's precise earlier distinction between amplified and residual anger, without extending that claim's registered V04 evidence automatically.

<a id="e023"></a>
**E023 — Mary claims a right to grieve, not access to an inevitable future.** **貴方に最期の日が来たら** is internally set in early summer after the Worgan expedition, although collected under chapter four (p-039:P535–630). It is not mechanically dated by its neighbors. Bradford's nice clothes, wine preference, sister's social demands and wish for magical combat give the listener an ordinary life beyond battle. Mary's young servants are paid, can attend school if they wish, and leave at sixteen with resources and introductions; her insistence that this is a hobby and that gratitude is unnecessary keeps her self-interest explicit. Bradford's favorable response is his appraisal. The material arrangements prevent calling the relation simple charity, while supplying no comprehensive account of every former servant's freedom or experience.

Mary's remembered first prediction concerned her then-fiancé's father's death. Her youthful fear that prediction caused it remains a past belief, not a rule of magic. The unnamed former fiancé refused belief in prophecy; she now understands him as trusting no one and treating others as pieces. That later account is hers. Most decisively, the anticipated lonely end is said to be discernible **「星を詠まずとも分かる」**: this is an interpretation of his course, not a newly performed death oracle. Her resolve **「あたくしだけは、貴方の最期を嘆き、悲しんであげる」** persists even if he would not want it, and she calls it her ego. It resembles her protective imposition elsewhere without making care unreal. The refusal of anticipated gratitude distinguishes it from service meant to possess a recipient's approval; her choice nevertheless makes no claim to his consent. Prior V09 context makes Clockford the strong identification, but this EXTRA09 scene never names the fiancé. Preserve the textual limit and the difference between likely identity and local explicit naming.

<a id="e024"></a>
**E024 — research joins unequal kinds of competence through an institution.** **呼び方の話** is set more than half a year after the mission, before the announced summer move (p-040:P143–257). The famine project has an investor, separated lands, caretaking disciples, reports and a purpose. Enchantment improves resilience but can poison edible parts and contaminate soil, attracting magical beings and ultimately making land uninhabitable. Raul's knowledge that some beans absorb soil magic enables Monica's surrounding-field proposal; Cyril translates it into a plan, with slope and drainage still requiring calculation. This is a testable proposal, not completed remediation or a harvest already made safe.

Monica's detailed data initially makes her fear she has burdened Cyril. He distinguishes well-organized detail from his own task of selecting a report for his father. The solution is a division of work, not making her numbers less valuable; it differs from the unusable communal filing of E010. Raul prefers actual growth to reports, receives a justified correction about fertilizer, and openly values the pair's coordination. Scheduling accommodates Monica's move. The three share neither the same skill nor a single preferred mode of familiarity. “Roseburg lord” is Cyril's deliberate concession, not the first-name intimacy Raul thought he requested.

Monica's address to Louis has a historically specific origin: Rutherford's stories made “Louis” familiar before his title. She explicitly distinguishes the institutional mentor who let her work from a direct master who taught her much; do not assign Rutherford a teaching role she denies. Cyril's sudden “Monica” then disrupts her work and makes her privately happy and unsettled. The text registers selective affect, not an explicit romantic classification. Name-use is a mechanism of received familiarity, not a reliable scale on which every relationship occupies the same step.

<a id="e025"></a>
**E025 — a new ribbon lets gratitude become an act of pleasure.** The epilogue returns to the accomplished move's first afternoon (p-042:P11–130). Lana discovers the practical gaps behind Monica's substantial paper-and-book luggage: insufficient everyday clothing, no skin-care equipment and no curtains. The sage robe is Monica's efficient public cover, but the hearing dress cannot even be worn without help. Lana takes charge of a prioritized list and initially chooses the clothes; the pressure is represented, not hidden by calling friendship purely equal. She then explicitly assigns Monica one choice because it will be worn on her own body. Reluctance to relinquish the old ribbon reveals affection to Lana, who proposes a separate commemorative hairclip and practice choosing a daily ribbon.

The successful choice comes through Monica's own taste: stripes and a lace curve, not the discovery that she must like whatever a stylist likes. Lana's **「可愛いっ」** comes with the open smile of a close friend rather than an elegant young lady's manner (P105–119). The first tying of hair returns as a memory whose excitement now feels different. Most consequentially, the narrator contrasts earlier received kindness—confusion, self-reproach, inability to thank—with joy now filling consciousness first. **「ラナ、ありがとう！」** is deliberately registered as an unstumbled utterance. This is a concrete changed sequence of appraisal and expression, not merely an increased quantity of friends. A strong narrower reading attributes her ease to this familiar giver and a choice Lana has carefully prepared. The narrator's general retrospective contrast supports real growth, but the scene cannot establish its transfer to an unfamiliar or less accommodating recipient. Continuing indecision, practical dependence and forceful advice prevent calling it complete autonomy or a permanent end to fear. V09's old ribbons retained in a box and EXTRA09's earlier-afternoon new purchase are compatible moments at distinct points of presentation; neither should overwrite the other.

<a id="e026"></a>
**E026 — there is no prince on the cookie because “Ike” is someone here.** The secret episode turns the prologue's lettered separation into an invitation back home (p-045:P11–188). Monica reports crowds still make her nervous, enjoys a rash geometrical-carpet purchase, and recounts actual visitors: the newlyweds on honeymoon, Glen with meat, Cyril's chocolate. The barbecue is a promise, not an already held party. Her postscript withholds the pupil's politically dangerous circumstances from Hilda. A month includes about a week at Sazandor; the narrator still calls him an uninvited pupil and preserves his public Felix identity and abdication. Nothing here supplies the informed residence discussion left open at V09.

The kitchen supplies real shared time beyond emergency and calculation. Isaac knows Nero's favored salty cheese cookies and limits sampling; their contest over “senior,” names and territory keeps the familiar's independent claims alive. Monica's own “my house” interrupts Nero's claim to its food. Her flour advice is hilariously inherited from Hilda's mistaken sifting, connecting present happiness to an imperfect earlier home. Isaac's uncomprehending accommodation is not a correction magically understood. Monica recognizes both the beauty of icing and her inability to reproduce a shape with a bag rather than pen. Skill remains medium-specific. She requests participation, fails, accepts his apt joke about the drooling Nero, then makes Ike in an apron using a skirt-shaped blank because its silhouette fits.

The viewpoint moves to Isaac: **「モニカにとっての『アイク』はキラキラした王子様ではなく、エプロンをした弟子なのだ」** (P150–163). Her representation gives him a privately valued personhood outside the public costume, without making him cease being politically Felix. He receives it with joy she probably does not recognize. That asymmetry qualifies the scene's tenderness; it does not invalidate it. A serious rival sees a servant's dependence transferred from dead prince to idealized master: an apron is still a role in someone else's life. His wanted scholarship and received recognition discriminate against calling the act wholly imposed, yet neither proves a future independent of Monica's approval. Her request to join the craft and his assistance with the ambitious Lana cookie keep this encounter collaborative and extend its circle. The final recap gives work, a little baking, delivered cookies and tea with Lana, more work, dinner and well-aired bedding equal narrative dignity. The postscript promises Hilda cookies next visit. The home learned here can be carried outward; no princely cookie or declaration of mutual romance is needed to make the pleasure consequential.

### The collection teaches the reader to correct a first impression

<a id="e027"></a>
**E027 — comic focalization is an argument about evidence.** The fragments repeatedly present a genre-shaped first account, then change its causal owner: Glen's knife threatens only bacon; a beautiful silent Raul is sleepy; an interrogation concerns lip-synching rather than exposed identity; a “future” mirror contains a stranded caller; an ominous mark on Eliane's hand belongs to an embarrassed gift. The corrections are neither a rule that appearances never tell truth nor license to dismiss bodily distress as a joke. The falling boxes are dangerous, the panic produces actual fainting, the magical contamination matters, and Neil's mediation genuinely fails. Some uncertainty remains deliberately uncorrected: Cyril's third drawing, the unnamed fiancé within this story, the object/category of Lin's remembered excitement, the mirror spirit's eventual custody. Formal closure comes in different strengths.

Repeated patterns also become diagnostic of change. The prologue's culinary failure returns in the thermometer investigation and Monica's misinformed dust-explosion warning. The same limited knowledge can persist inside a happier life. By contrast tea, hair, names, ribbons, handwork and letters acquire new routes of mutual attention. The final letter includes the daughter, friend and master without erasing the researcher. Hilda's early worry that Monica may merely be conforming gives the final unprompted request to decorate and spontaneous thanks particular weight. These are represented acts of initiative, not an author's assurance substituting for scenes.

<a id="e028"></a>
**E028 — visual reference organizes accumulated life without becoming omniscient narrative.** The actual cover places a smiling Monica among coffee equipment, books, chess pieces, robes, dresses, jewelry and Nero. Its crowded belongings counterpoint the early room whose only habit is research, while not identifying every depicted object as newly purchased or every gift as equally understood. The Hilda-and-Monica color frontispiece uses a framed domestic image, giving maternal relation a visible place beside the collection's retrospective form. The other color frontispiece's Nero, flowers and papers accompanies the contents; neither image supplies an unprinted dialogue. The title, credits and ornamental magic-circle dividers stage a curated collection rather than a continuous newly occurring plot. The final p340 card says “To be continued in the Silent Witch X.” It contains no scene and earns no later-narrative knowledge. The epilogue's actual image title uses **貴女**, whereas the NCX entry says **貴方**; the image, not the navigation's substitution, controls the title cited here.

The thirty character-design pages, p003–p032, make clothes, concealed underlayers, fastenings, hair and tools available to scrutiny. Likes/dislikes add ordinary tastes and sometimes comic asymmetry: the king likes enchantment reports and dislikes carrots; Cyril likes textiles but dislikes laziness; Louis's insect dislike is explicitly dated “since volume nine.” These are **REFERENCE_PARATEXT**, not newly enacted scenes or timeless psychological laws. The prequel-labeled sheets for younger Louis/Rosalie and Bardland, Owen Wright, Adolf Fallon and younger/current Lionel are admitted designs here only. They do not confer reading credit for another work. This prevents design familiarity from silently becoming source history.

The afterword describes a collection of V01–V07 bonus stories plus new writing, and its origin list identifies particular retail/anthology/Web origins. Those statements explain the assemblage's varied lengths and retrospective distribution; they do not mean this run previously read those editions, or that a listed Web origin authorizes consulting it. The author calls the book a growth trajectory and the cover a happy collection of treasures. That corroborates the framing intention but cannot decide whether each gift was understood or each helper acted well. Omitted-episode wishes and announcements, including the later volume and adaptations, are promotion/plans encountered within the admitted afterword, excluded from narrative evidence and never followed. No reception, adaptation or outside chronology is imported.
