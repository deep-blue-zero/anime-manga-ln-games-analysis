### What expertise can ask for, and what it cannot settle

**E020 — chapter eight 愛されたがり、到着, p-015:P4–150/P205–309.** Five days pass before Ray arrives; destroyed curse and healed body are distinct. The news about Glen also revises the meaning of a familiar cheerful face. Ray calls his capacity an unofficial record, 「非公式記録」, and reports that it apparently exceeded 250 (P133). Narration then supplies a firmer clarification: the earlier meter's upper limit was 250, and Glen's power far exceeded its capacity (P135–139). The old malfunction uncertainty can therefore be resolved at this V05 disclosure boundary, without supplying an exact official score or treating capacity as skilled control. Ray next reports a past 「魔力暴走事件」 caused by excessive power: others could not manage Glen, and Louis took him in (P140). The ending 「って聞いた」—analyst gloss, “I heard”—qualifies that history. The following general account of developing capacity and the danger of enormous reserves before skill gives the report an intelligible risk mechanism; it does not narrate what that particular incident did to anyone.

Monica's response changes the relationship through appraisal rather than through a new confession. Ray's dismissive reassurance about Glen's thick nerves (P149) does not resolve her hypothesis; his jealousy and his appeal to Louis's notorious training make it a different appraisal. The scene preserves both her newly attentive uncertainty and Glen's unavailable interior response. He is not shown receiving this sympathy, and Cyril is not shown hearing the report.

Monica's vision and his residue diagnosis thus constrain a novel explanation rather than establishing a universal exception. **E026 — p-017:P271–340.** Peter's frightened confusion supplies names and an apparent confession: he says he sold Venedict to his patron and fears becoming another Arthur. ### Home is an answer someone must receive

**E027 — chapter eleven それぞれの帰省, p-018:P4–113; original p269 illustration.** Cyril's visit advances the actual V04 question about Myra's changed intention. He arrives afraid that home might no longer be his, dressed for a neighborhood where noble display would expose his mother to notice. She is neither merely his trauma's cause nor an endlessly available maternal resource.

When asked for news, he supplies reports about his prince. She asks for **his** life instead. **E028 — p-018:P118–228.** Felix leaves on the eighth day with concrete diplomatic gains: wheat, information and joint dragon exercises. **E029 — p-018:P232–300.** Hilda's professional exactitude and domestic failure are deliberately adjacent. **E030 — p-018:P304–428.** Hilda gives a new causal account of Venedict's downfall: a curse researcher's proposal to control living beings was refused; one week later the accusation concerned resurrection research and prohibited books were found. ## The volume's larger arguments

V05 changes the question from whether Monica can enter society to what kinds of **action and dependence** become possible once she already has relations worth preserving. It gives no single trajectory from numbers to people. Numbers make diplomatic words inaudible, fail as refuge when a friend's pressure occupies her mind, measure the body of a dangerous man and later open a possible medical/forensic research program. The same learned skill can distance, accuse or reconnect; intention matters, but so do recipient and information. The oath, graded flames, staff repair, banquet accompaniment and hiring form a chain of small choices. Their causal sequence is stronger evidence of agency than either a spectacular arrival or a climactic attack considered in isolation. Yet an independent filial project can increase concealment and risk. Growth is newly effective choice under incompatible commitments, not a guaranteed ethically superior choice.

Glen's part in the courage circuit must also be read through the revision of Monica's attention. A vow that first helped her speak is later reconsidered when she hears about his past. The return to his exact words lets the same utterance acquire a possible burden without changing what was audible in the market. Her teaching has supplied him a manageable exercise; his subsequent practice supplies her a reason to attempt the banquet. This reciprocity neither requires him to confess fear nor authorizes her—or the analyst—to know it for him. It makes encouragement compatible with incomplete access to another person's difficulty. His sickroom comparison adds a less consoling limit: readiness to persist can coexist with severe pain and with a training history whose violence has become his reference point for what is bearable. Louis's taking him in, as Ray reports it, may explain an important practical relation without settling the means of instruction. The repeated figure of the table is correspondingly epistemic and social. Louis's correct lesson about knowing the rules is compromised by cheating and compelled participation. Felix later prepares the questions and reads the body of an opponent who knows his answers are incomplete. Monica finally stages a different encounter using the information and colleagues she has assembled. These are not identical moral acts, but each shows that knowing the truth of one statement does not guarantee power to determine the exchange. Her new investigation may redistribute that power while reproducing withholding. The source has not yet decided whether her conviction about Venedict will permit unwelcome evidence. This is the strongest rival to an uncomplicated emancipation reading.

The puppet metaphor also acquires incompatible literal and ethical uses. Royal staging seeks an exemplary victory; a researcher expects to control a dragon; the mother's vengeance makes that experiment fail; Felix fears his own instrumental body and considers accepting an exemplary death. His rescuer is injured precisely where he might have offered gratitude. Nero's nonhuman mercy ends a catastrophe which human political plans neither anticipated nor mastered. The diplomats nevertheless gain from the public story. Calling the event simply fake would erase deaths and choices; calling it destiny would erase its manufacture. The new clue about literal puppetry is still Felix's inference, not a license to identify every sponsor or settle every identity.

Against demanded exemplary selves, the novel offers recognitions that can admit limitation. Felix is delighted by corrected research, not flawless homage. The closing chimes organize that form: one actual sound holds shared public pleasure, a student's technical admiration, a senior's borrowed courage and a daughter's private pledge.
