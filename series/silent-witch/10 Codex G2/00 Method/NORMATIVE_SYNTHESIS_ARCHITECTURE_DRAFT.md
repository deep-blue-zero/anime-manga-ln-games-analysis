# Silent Witch synthesis and corpus architecture

## 1. Purpose, authority, and the paired foundation

One corpus under `series/silent-witch/` builds distinct products: substantial sequential volume readings; longitudinal state; character, relationship and specialist arguments; an integrated published-LN synthesis through an authorized boundary; optionally bounded textual-history/adaptation studies; and separately validated reconstruction models. None substitutes for another.

The single first-read surface remains the established canonical entrypoint. Current-eligible method governs inspection; architecture governs cumulative destinations/convergence; character method governs readiness and separately authorized reconstruction; template implements these responsibilities. Permission, initiation, semantic acceptance and integration are distinct. Proposed G2 routes are in the bindings file; no populated V1 record is an input.



Live repository authority, the current owner instruction, initiation, archive, continuation, capability/handoff, publication and integration controls govern local defaults. This projection changes neither authority epoch nor canonical scope.

## 2. Source model and storage planes

The proposed G2 source/admission register owns verified source identities, qualifications, exclusions and admission/release receipts. The owner boundary is Japanese LN V01-V12 plus AFTER04, EXTRA09 and ANOTHER-UPPER/LOWER in the exact owner order. Reuse witness IDs/faithful infrastructure only after integrity and usability checks. Availability and prior technical use imply no G2 admission or coverage; all other witnesses remain excluded.

Publication date/order, diegetic placement, reader disclosure order, and admission order are independent fields. A prequel's early story time does not make it safe before V01. A short-story collection can contain multiple temporal positions and spoilers. Admission requires an explicit unit/story boundary and safe knowledge limit rather than numeric interpolation.

Git holds authored interpretation, methods, public-safe routing, state and provenance. Governed existing evidence storage holds original sources, faithful/normalized derivatives, raw locators, alignments and images. Working builds/transport are temporary. Deterministic processing does not make evidence interpretation. Raw text/large derivatives never enter Git; current owner scope retains governed evidence locally and authorizes no competing evidence system.

## 3. Exclusive G2 responsibilities — activated initial homes, stage-gated later outputs

Retain the single canonical entrypoint identity and current V1 analytical authority. The steward owns administrative permission/isolation/execution and prospective entrypoint state; the source custodian owns technical verification/release receipts; the principal owns sequential candidates; one longitudinal integrator alone writes shared cumulative/claim state; independent reviewers own source-first sampling and acceptance/recheck receipts; the Git custodian owns transactions/freeze archives.

The approved required initial homes are the generation-specific source/admission register, empty typed master and separate empty claims/revisions ledger in the bindings file; this role may start only after their actual initialized zero state is accepted. No source/claim/identity/readiness rows are inherited or instantiated. One reading owns each source-bearing observation. Character/relationship/specialist/synthesis/readiness artifacts are created only when authorized and warranted from G2 evidence. A screened packet is a technical projection, never another canonical method, source archive or entrypoint. No authority promotion is authorized.

## 4. Why one master ledger

Interacting character, relationship, knowledge, institutional and formal dimensions may share one typed master initially. Preserve independent typed responsibilities, stable IDs and retrieval. Do not split merely for length/symmetry or copy another project. Separate claims/revisions have a current normative maintenance contract and a proposed empty G2 home.

The ledger owns cumulative interpretation, not a second copy of every scene. The volume reading owns the source-bearing observation. A record can link multiple typed records without duplicating their ownership: a disclosure event's participant knowledge belongs to knowledge state, its directional trust effect to relationships, and its hypothesized cause to claims.

Split only when recurring evidence, independent retrieval/revision, separate specialist dependency, or conflicting ownership makes the combined home inadequate. Record the reason, exact transferred IDs, destination, and necessary material backfill. Preserve IDs/history and leave routes in the old home; do not maintain two live copies. Mere length or another project's folder count is insufficient.

### 5.1 Preservation obligations at each transition

**Source to reading.** The unit author preserves diagnostically consequential observations with witness/hash and recoverable primary locators, attribution/focalizer, explanatory context, evidence class, alternatives and uncertainty under the analytical method. An equivalent prose-plus-record layout is acceptable only when those distinctions remain recoverable; dropping interpretation or attribution columns must not drop their information. A synopsis, trait label or action list is not a substitute. Do not duplicate the whole scene or build another source archive.

**Reading to cumulative state.** The integrator routes character mechanisms to the master's character section; directional relationships to its relationship section; participant knowledge to knowledge/chronology; institutional constraints to world; and thematic propositions or literary mechanisms to form/evaluation, with cross-links where needed. Load-bearing interpretations and revisions belong to the separate claims and revisions ledger. Preserve enough explanatory specificity, attribution, conditions and uncertainty to distinguish the mechanism; use a concise observation pointer for supporting detail rather than copying the reading.

A single observation may merit promotion when it reveals a distinctive behavioral mechanism, meaningful contradiction/counterexample, relationship distinction, significant thematic proposition, private motivation, reconstruction-relevant evidence or a discriminator between competing interpretations. Frequency and cast prominence are not thresholds. Neither is promotion automatic for every minor observation.

**Justified local retention.** The reading remains the exclusive owner of its source-bearing observation. Its cumulative-delta section identifies a consequential observation retained locally, the reason and a stable section/observation route searchable by subject, relationship or topic. Where a later target would otherwise miss that evidence, add a compact pointer with its diagnostic relevance and reconsideration trigger to the existing relevant typed cumulative record or unit-closure receipt. This pointer is navigation, not a promoted finding. Group related observations when their route and rationale are shared. Incidental details need no individual disposition. At checkpoint/dispatch, review these routes as well as promoted claims; absence from the claims ledger must not erase local evidence from the evidence search.

**Cumulative state to synthesis.** The retrospective author must be able to recover the primary basis of important character, relationship and thematic interpretations. A compressed ledger is a route, not adequate evidence for an interpretation it cannot discriminate. Retrieve the full relevant reading; if it omits a consequential distinction, conflicts with another record, or cannot settle the substantive interpretation, reinspect the original admitted source in context. Record the affected claim/section, missing distinction, source route, required next action and resolution in the existing target closeout or checkpoint/audit home. If access or authorization prevents reinspection, hold the affected conclusion/acceptance or seek a legitimate bounded scope decision; never manufacture an absence or silently downgrade required evidence to optional.

**Acceptance ownership.** The integrator prepares and routes the frozen cumulative/claim candidate under method section 7.1, checks that an independent acceptance/recheck receipt covers its actual returned state, and performs required repairs. It does not independently approve its own work. The separate Japanese-source semantic reviewer selects and preserves the original source-first sample and makes the acceptance decision; retrospective argument/synthesis acceptance likewise belongs to an independent reviewer. Unit and target closeout receipts carry sampled evidence, severity/disposition and unresolved dependencies. Check source coverage completeness and analytical retention adequacy separately; neither implies the other. Preserve historical knowledge states through attributable revision/erratum.

## 6. Sequential contribution and closure contract

One numbered volume is one atomic source transaction. Internal chapter/scene working units do not independently advance the volume high-water mark. A transaction contributes:

1. verified unit source lock and exclusions in the source map or a warranted dedicated lock receipt;
2. inherited prior-volume developments and timed entering freeze before new narrative inspection;
3. full admitted coverage, connected current-volume synopsis, sustained close reading, and compact diagnostic evidence records;
4. material changes to typed cumulative sections, or explicit reviewed no-material-update outcomes;
5. claim revisions and adjudicated genuine predictions, with UNTESTED opportunities retained;
6. an exit truth freeze and next-unit questions excluding later witness knowledge;
7. local readiness changes only when evidence supports them;
8. semantic review, durable persistence, and truthful entrypoint/coverage advancement.

Coverage, accepted interpretation, and repository publication remain distinct. A completely read but unresolved unit is not declared accepted; accepted prose without cumulative updates is not transaction-closed; a commit is not a remote audit. Preserve partial state and do not advance the high-water mark on incomplete closure.

## 7. Character, relationship, and specialist responsibilities

Substantial character work must eventually explain time-indexed motivations, appraisal, public/private presentation, ordinary behavior, speech, relationship conditioning, contradictions, choices and literary function. A dedicated home is earned by explanatory complexity and independent retrieval across eligible units, not cast prominence. A relationship synthesis is earned when reciprocal asymmetries, shared history, information/obligation, and repair require an argument beyond incidental character sections. Ensemble work should preserve autonomous goals.

A **thin** character or relationship record means inspected, adequately retained evidence supports only a bounded interpretation in the named domain and state. It does not mean substantial evidence was discarded because the subject seemed secondary. A subject without a monograph can require substantial preservation in readings and cumulative state. Record whether apparent thinness is a source limit, capture/routing gap or unresolved conflict; retrieve or escalate before using it as a readiness judgment. Deferral decisions name the evidence basis, local route and reconsideration trigger and remain revisitable when recovered or newly admitted evidence changes the basis. No monograph is required for every supporting character,.

Anticipated specialist responsibilities are investigative: disclosure/knowledge and narrative withholding; expertise/mathematics and magic constraints; school/aristocratic institutions and available agency; loyalty/deception/self-authorship; affiliative or romantic ambiguity and directional obligations; Japanese voice, silence, humor and ethical treatment. These are candidate domains, not mandatory separate files or conclusions.

At review, classify each domain WARRANTED, MONITOR, MERGE_INTO_EXISTING, DEFER, or NOT_SUPPORTED and explain its evidence/dependency basis. A warranted specialist has one thesis responsibility, traceable diagnostic evidence, strongest counterargument, and explicit source boundary. It makes substantial connected arguments; an outline or compilation of recap bullets cannot satisfy it.

## 8. Evidence retrieval, revision, and temporal continuity

Mature claim -> claims and revisions ledger claim/revision ID (or relevant typed master record) -> canonical volume observation/context -> witness/hash/locator -> original retained primary evidence. The retrieval and escalation duties in section 5.1 apply at every step. A character evidence index, if earned, is a curated route through these homes, not another source dump. Short Japanese citations stay with the argument using them; raw normalized corpora do not enter Git.

Maintain source order, diegetic order, revelation order, and participant knowledge separately. A character belief, third-party attribution, narrator presentation, and reader inference cannot collapse into one omniscient state. Later disclosure can be REVEALED_NOT_NEW rather than developmental change. Knowledge updates do not grant an earlier model later information.

For revisions use PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT or OPEN, retaining old wording/source boundary, new support/counterevidence and affected downstream homes. Review every applicable claim at each unit and receipt the review; routine no-change reviews create no event. Frozen readings/checkpoints remain historical. Correct objective errors visibly and propagate dependencies; never rewrite a prospective prediction.

Cross-source contradiction taxonomy: chronology/admission mismatch; ignorance/deception; focalization/reliability; later revelation or textual revision; edition/normalization defect; translation variation; supplemental retrospection; adaptation/continuity divergence; creator-paratext conflict; genuine inconsistency; unresolved cause. Record both incompatible formulations with witness/unit/time and knowledge status. Validate source/locator identity first, then investigate causes. Significant conflicts require a bounded audit and claim/dependency review rather than forced reconciliation.
