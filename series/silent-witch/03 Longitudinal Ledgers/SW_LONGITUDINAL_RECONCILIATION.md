# Silent Witch — longitudinal reconciliation at completion

## 1. Responsibility

The [synthesis architecture](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/SW_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) sets a longitudinal-reconciliation gate before any full-series product: claims, state, chronology and unresolved contradictions must be consistent through the authorized boundary (section 10). The [completion checkpoint](../04%20Checkpoints%20and%20Reviews/SW_COMPLETION_CHECKPOINT_AND_EVIDENCE_GAP_REVIEW.md) assigns that gate to this document.

This document reads the [master ledger](SW_MASTER_LONGITUDINAL_LEDGER.md) and does not replace it. The ledger remains the sole cumulative home. *Routing note, version 1.1:* after this reconciliation the claims and revision events it audits in section 3 moved, unchanged in ID, to the [claims and revisions ledger](SW_CLAIMS_AND_REVISIONS_LEDGER.md); the findings below are unaffected. Where the audit found that a ledger record needed a note, the note is made in the ledger itself, by targeted edit, and listed in section 9. No frozen sequential reading is altered by this document.

Sixteen units are reconciled, in admission order: V01 to V04, IV -after- (AFTER04), V05 to V09, IX -extra- (EXTRA09), V10 to V12, then the prequel volumes -another- (upper) and -another- (lower) (ANOTHER-UPPER, ANOTHER-LOWER).

## 2. Snapshot and the one correction

The input is the snapshot of the checkpoint, commit `60b21a7`. One factual error in that snapshot was found while this audit gathered its chronology. The -another- (lower) reading placed the Sage selection about a year after the court soirée of its first chapter. The text gives about three years:

- Carla's resignation lies half a year before the soirée and about three and a half years before the selection.
- Glen, eleven when Louis takes him in, is fourteen in the summer before the selection.

The reading (version 1.1, with a corrections table) and the ledger's SW-K-0160 and record defaults were corrected in the checkpoint's commit. The corrected interval is used throughout this document. No claim, observation or freeze statement depended on the earlier figure. The prequel readings record the ages they give for the second prince as stated (ANOTHER-UPPER reading §10; ANOTHER-LOWER obs. 031) and draw no arithmetic from them, because such arithmetic bears on the substitution disclosed at V08. This reconciliation keeps that discipline: the correction rests on Carla's and Glen's intervals alone.

- **Source order** is the order of admission: the numbered volumes with their companions at their recorded points, then the prequel last.
