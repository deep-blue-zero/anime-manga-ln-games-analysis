## Enforcement weaknesses and amendments

The analytical method already required a causal observation chain, ordinary behavior, recipient distinctions, focalization, rival interpretations, source locators and proportionate prose. The character method already required conditional rules and noncircular tests. The master ledger already had typed causal and uncertainty fields, and the claims ledger had a material-event review contract. Repository handoff policy already rejected compressed summaries as sufficient evidence for distinctions they omit. These requirements are retained.

Enforcement was incomplete at the transitions. Architecture section 5 categorically kept a one-off detail without recurring consequence local, equating recurrence with promotion eligibility. The routing matrix and mature-claim route also still named the master as the claim owner after the claims split. Closure did not separately test coverage versus semantic retention. Thinness/readiness language did not consistently distinguish source insufficiency from lost or poorly routed evidence. The [2026-10-14 ledger correction report](SW_LEDGER_ARCHITECTURE_CORRECTION_REPORT.md) had already identified projection loss and disappearing observation fields and expressly said the capture cause was not structurally prevented. Its findings are prior audit evidence, not newly rerun results.

The amended method defines permissible compression and diagnostic significance, including one-off thematic propositions and supporting-character/ordinary interactions. It supplies a risk-sensitive, source-grounded semantic-retention gate with Minor/Moderate/Major/Critical dispositions. The architecture assigns capture, promotion/local retrieval and reinspection duties to existing owners. The template requires the checks and separate coverage/retention judgments in closure. The limited character-method amendment diagnoses capture, routing, source and conflict gaps without changing tier criteria or awarding readiness. Existing repository escalation rules are linked, not duplicated by a new exception registry.

Architecture section 11 defines a future model-neutral independent generation: verified technical reuse, screened normative inputs, actual exclusion of V1 interpretations and known omissions, qualified namespaces, separate qualification and run authorization, immutable completion before comparison, and later source-grounded adjudication preserving disagreements. The normative requirements contain no expected conclusion drawn from the owner's diagnostic examples.
