## Error and source verification

The erroneous sentence in [SW-QP1-CE-002](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_CUMULATIVE.md#sw-qp1-ce-002) is:

> His final pressure, shoulder-grip and warning obtain a reflexive nod rather than an unforced commitment.

The coordination treats the warning as one of the causes obtaining the nod. In this context the warning is Louis's statement that failure could mean execution, which occurs later. It must not be read backward into the earlier reflexive response.

## Corrected SW-QP1-CE-002 formulation

The following is the corrected record paragraph for use together with the frozen cumulative file. The subject, classes, evidence routes and bounded uncertainties of CE002 remain as originally recorded. Only its compressed causal account is revised:

> Louis is gracious to Annie while mocking Monica's speech and calling her plain and amateur. A former corps commander and fellow recent Sage, he has no narrated general rank superiority over Monica, but asserts delegated royal staffing authority for this task. He offers specific covert-protection reasons, claims a week of labor and urgent response to the broken brooch, and has secured cover cooperation in advance. His polite self-presentation strains under fake laughter and the crushing of the damaged ruby. Monica's retaliation interpretation remains hers. After asserting that she has no right to refuse, he grips her shoulders and stares at close range; Monica nods reflexively rather than making an unforced commitment. Louis then releases her shoulders and warns that failure could mean execution. Monica shudders and, treating the already-given nod as closing escape, reluctantly resolves to carry out the assignment. Reported duty and effort complicate, but do not excuse, those means.
