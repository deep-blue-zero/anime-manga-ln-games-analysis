## SW-QP1-C-001

**Subjects:** sw.qp1.monica_everett; sw.qp1.constraint_and_invention. **Class:** STRONG_INFERENCE, based on NARRATOR_PRESENTATION and OBSERVABLE_TEXT_EVENT. **Boundary/status:** supported within the opening; wider explanatory reach untested.

**Claim.** The opening makes Monica's technical mastery and social difficulty coexist, and explicitly makes one episode of that difficulty the impetus for technical invention. The public-to-domestic transition revises the apparent meaning of her quietness without withdrawing the achievement.

**Support and mechanism.** The rescue includes both accurate killing and controlled descent, establishing a fine-grained achievement before her private state is available. Chapter 1 then demonstrates comparable precision in paper-moving and spell recognition. Narration explains the examiner problem and her decision to remove incantation, while preserving the approximately eighty-percent limit. Her fear remains after attainment. Her unsupported charitable-selection story supplies a different, self-disparaging interpretation of the same institutional status. Source routes: [003](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_READING.md#sw-qp1-e-lnjp-v01-003) → S12:E000003–E000060; [009](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_READING.md#sw-qp1-e-lnjp-v01-009) → S15:E000357–E000383; [010](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_READING.md#sw-qp1-e-lnjp-v01-010) → S15:E000389–E000432; [012](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_READING.md#sw-qp1-e-lnjp-v01-012), [015](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_READING.md#sw-qp1-e-lnjp-v01-015). Typed routes: [CE001](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_CUMULATIVE.md#sw-qp1-ce-001), [WO003](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_CUMULATIVE.md#sw-qp1-wo-003), [FO001](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_CUMULATIVE.md#sw-qp1-fo-001), [FO002](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_CUMULATIVE.md#sw-qp1-fo-002).

**Strongest rival.** The revelation is simply a comic deflation of a heroic image. **Counterevidence/limit.** Narration does ridicule the conventional inspirational origin, and Louis calls domestic use wasteful. But the work is repeatedly demonstrated rather than exposed as fraud. Conversely, those successes do not prove Monica can protect an unfamiliar prince socially or tactically. **What could change the claim.** Evidence that the narrated origin is unreliable would alter the causal component; later social performance could refine conditions, not erase this frozen opening. No such evidence is currently admitted. **Disposition:** OPEN via CR001.

## SW-QP1-C-002

**Subjects:** sw.qp1.annie; sw.qp1.public_reputation; sw.qp1.monica_everett. **Class:** STRONG_INFERENCE. **Boundary/status:** supported local mechanism, not a general claim about social class or all observers.

**Claim.** Annie's failure to recognize Monica is produced by an intelligible social expectation about what successful magical expertise looks like, reinforced by Monica's hesitation and retreat. The irony depends on competent everyday judgment meeting an exceptional case.

**Support and mechanism.** Annie is literate, capable with numbers, efficient at delivery and attentive to payment. She sees advanced calculations without understanding them and assumes a magician would work visibly in the capital. Narration explicitly makes that expectation reasonable amid restricted training and prestigious career paths. Monica allows her conclusion to stand and gives an approximate mathematical occupation. Annie's earnest fame-talk then addresses the person it fails to identify. **Strongest rival.** Annie is merely a child missing a hidden fact because Monica does not disclose it; no social mechanism is needed. **Counterevidence/limit.** Concealment does matter, and she has no decisive visual proof. Yet the text gives her inference and its institutional rationale rather than only withholding information. It also marks her discernment over money. **What could change the claim.** A source showing Annie already knows the identity would change the irony; a narrower reinspection showing the career inference was not hers would weaken attribution. Neither is present. No universal conclusion about what all villagers think is supported. **Disposition:** OPEN via CR002.

## SW-QP1-C-003

**Subjects:** sw.qp1.louis_miller; sw.qp1.monica_everett; sw.qp1.coerced_assignment. **Classes:** STRONG_INFERENCE and NORMATIVE_EVALUATION, separated below. **Boundary/status:** selection rationale plausible on reported premises; coercion directly supported; motive weighting unresolved.

**Claim.** Louis gives a coherent practical reason to choose Monica for hidden protection, while obtaining her participation by coercion. The legitimacy of the task and suitability of some traits do not establish voluntary assent or justify silencing operational concerns.

**Descriptive support.** His conspicuous identity, factional obstacle at the school, Felix's reported detection of professional guards and Monica's silent magic make the selection reasoned. Monica nevertheless refuses and cites inexperience; Louis's pressure escalates from a duty argument into imperative, claimed absence of refusal rights, shoulder-grip and threat. The nod is narrated as reflexive. Her later inability to tell him she cannot recognize Felix shows an immediate practical cost of the fear. His week of labor and urgent response after the brooch alert resist the claim that he simply wants to do nothing. Evidence: [011](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_READING.md#sw-qp1-e-lnjp-v01-011), [013](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_READING.md#sw-qp1-e-lnjp-v01-013), [014](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_READING.md#sw-qp1-e-lnjp-v01-014) → especially S15:E000572–E000662; [018](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_READING.md#sw-qp1-e-lnjp-v01-018) → E000846–E000875. Typed routes: [CE002](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_CUMULATIVE.md#sw-qp1-ce-002), [RE003](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_CUMULATIVE.md#sw-qp1-re-003), [FO003](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_CUMULATIVE.md#sw-qp1-fo-003), [KN002](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_CUMULATIVE.md#sw-qp1-kn-002).

**Evaluation and alternatives.** Criterion: meaningful opportunity for the affected person to refuse, understand stakes and disclose limitations. On that criterion the encounter fails Monica. A less forceful briefing, verification of her target knowledge and negotiation of support were imaginable alternatives, although the source does not test whether Louis would accept them or whether another agent could succeed. The strongest rival sees necessary professional direction, exaggerated comically, applied to someone undervaluing real ability. That explains the duty argument but does not convert an explicitly reflexive nod or concealed recognition gap into informed assent. Monica's suspicion that Louis wants revenge is a plausible motive hypothesis, not this claim's settled cause.

**What could change the claim.** Direct information about royal authority, urgency or omitted negotiations could refine evaluation and feasibility. It could not silently convert the present nod into a free choice. Actual later support or harm would require an attributable update. **Disposition:** OPEN via CR003.

## SW-QP1-C-005

**Subjects:** sw.qp1.fictional_scripts; sw.qp1.rin; sw.qp1.nero; sw.qp1.isabel_norton; sw.qp1.monica_everett. **Class:** STRONG_INFERENCE for the observed uses; WORKING_HYPOTHESIS only for whether the cover will work. **Boundary/status:** uses established, outcomes untested.

**Claim.** Fiction supplies materially different scripts to the participants: operational camouflage for Louis, interesting cruelty for Rin, reading pleasure and prince expectations for Nero, reported role-play excitement for Isabel, and a bewildering reputational burden for Monica. The opening stages the risks of treating a script as knowledge of a real person.

**Support.** Louis explicitly names a novel as the cover's source and says complicated circumstances discourage inquiry. Rin's account values the ingenious bullying; Nero is interested in the new book, while Monica struggles to absorb it. Isabel's enthusiasm is reported by Louis, consistent with but not identical to her directly witnessed gratitude. Monica worries about making the family look bad. Nero's prince cliché does not persuade her and instead leads to disclosure that she cannot identify Felix. **Strongest rival.** These are lightweight jokes introducing a familiar romance plot rather than a connected exploration of scripted identity. **Counterevidence/limit.** Comedy and a popular fictional convention are explicit; not every book reference must carry a thematic program. The cover nevertheless changes the assigned conduct and perceived reputation of named people, making its use consequential. ## SW-QP1-C-006

**Subjects:** sw.qp1.surveillance; sw.qp1.louis_miller; sw.qp1.monica_everett; sw.qp1.felix. **Class:** STRONG_INFERENCE with bounded NORMATIVE_EVALUATION. **Boundary/status:** overlapping functions and explicit contest supported; final work-level verdict unknown.

**Claim.** The brooch discussion makes concealed protection and concealed surveillance overlap, then exposes disagreement over whether nondiscovery settles the legitimacy of that arrangement.

**Support.** Monica decodes functions that include tracking. Louis confirms constant knowledge of the sapphire bearer's location and response to attacks; he says the gift concealed his authorship. Monica asks whether surveillance is the actual aim and worries about discovery. Louis states that not being found out is enough, with clerical purity in the narrative simile, while Monica continues inwardly to doubt. The critical distinction survives even though the device can genuinely protect. Evidence: [012](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_READING.md#sw-qp1-e-lnjp-v01-012) → S15:E000486–E000542; [013](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_READING.md#sw-qp1-e-lnjp-v01-013). Typed routes: [WO005](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_CUMULATIVE.md#sw-qp1-wo-005), [FO003](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_CUMULATIVE.md#sw-qp1-fo-003), [KN002](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_CUMULATIVE.md#sw-qp1-kn-002).

**Evaluation criterion.** Being useful to a protected person does not itself establish that person's informed agreement to monitoring. The scene supplies no such agreement. This is a bounded evaluative premise, not a quotation of a complete theory from the novel. **Strongest rival.** Secrecy is part of the reported royal assignment, and monitoring a charge is a practical protective necessity. **Counterevidence/limit.** The protective mechanisms and barriers to ordinary guarding support that rationale. They do not establish that Louis's maxim is endorsed by Monica, the narrator or the work as a whole. Monica's expressed concern partly concerns punishment on discovery, so it should not be inflated into a comprehensive privacy doctrine. **What could change the claim.** Direct evidence about Felix's knowledge/consent or the king's purpose would qualify the evaluation. A later source cannot retroactively erase the stated disagreement. **Disposition:** OPEN via CR006.

## SW-QP1-C-007

**Subjects:** sw.qp1.monica_everett; sw.qp1.sage_selection. **Class:** STRONG_INFERENCE about belief formation, not a replacement factual selection history. **Boundary/status:** supported local self-appraisal mechanism.

**Claim.** Monica converts a remembered interview failure and an extra vacancy into certainty that her appointment was charitable, using comparison with Louis's visible achievements to discount her own specialist achievement. The account explains her fear without establishing that she truly lacked merit.

**Support.** She incompletely remembers the interview and reports collapse. The vacant place is part of her account, but narration explicitly says she was never told the supposed original sole-winner result. Her certainty and repeated self-description feed the conclusion that protecting a prince is impossible. Narrated silent-magic uniqueness and the earlier rescue supply strong counterevidence to her global discounting, while leaving the actual committee reasoning unknown. Evidence: [015](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_READING.md#sw-qp1-e-lnjp-v01-015) → S15:E000815–E000838 and E000645; [009](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_READING.md#sw-qp1-e-lnjp-v01-009), [003](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_READING.md#sw-qp1-e-lnjp-v01-003). Typed routes: [CE001](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_CUMULATIVE.md#sw-qp1-ce-001), [KN003](SW_QUALIFICATION_PILOT_CUMULATIVE.md#sw-qp1-kn-003).

**Strongest rival.** Her practical interview difficulty accurately identifies skills needed for public service, and the extra vacancy may really have changed the outcome. **Counterevidence/limit.** Both points may be true; neither entails charitable selection or absence of exceptional merit. Conversely, magical achievement alone does not prove readiness for close protection. **What could change the claim.** An admitted selection record or direct selector testimony could clarify actual reasons; this opening has neither. No diagnostic label is needed to describe the unsupported inference. **Disposition:** OPEN via CR007.
