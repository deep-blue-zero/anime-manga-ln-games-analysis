# Silent Witch — completion checkpoint and evidence-gap review

## 1. Responsibility and authority

The [synthesis architecture](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/SW_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) places a bounded checkpoint and evidence-gap review between complete prospective readings and every retrospective product (section 9). This document is that checkpoint. It records:

- the frozen input snapshot;
- the state of each completion gate;
- the classification of evidence gaps;
- the decision on the master ledger;
- the final classification of the candidate specialist domains;
- the character and relationship arguments that the evidence warrants, with acceptance criteria;
- the execution topology for the synthesis phase.

The owner's 2026-10-14 instruction authorizes the sequential run through every supplied Japanese work and "all artifacts per the synthesis architecture". This review treats that instruction as the separate authorization the architecture requires for full-series synthesis and for a reconstruction-readiness assessment (section 10, gate table). The instruction does not authorize reconstruction models, synthetic dialogue, global character enrolment, or admission of any web, adaptation or translation witness, and none is produced.

## 4. Evidence-gap review

Each gap is classified as the long-series protocol requires: a material absence that blocks a target, a bounded omission that supports a scoped result, or an optional enrichment.

### 4.1 Material absences

None blocks a target named in section 7. All prose, all narrative illustrations and all paratext channels of every admitted unit were inspected, except the members classified at lock as excluded (unrelated previews, notices, store images).

### 4.2 Bounded omissions

These are properties of the source boundary, not failures of coverage. Every product must state them where relevant.

1. **The series is unfinished at V12.** Several threads are open at the boundary. The products treat them as open and register no predicted answers:
   - the ice spirit's plan and the gate of audience (SW-C-0091; SW-P-0059, SW-P-0062);
   - Mary's foreseen event (SW-P-0018);
   - the selection of a seventh Sage (SW-P-0053);
   - Isaac's foretold loss (SW-P-0066);
   - Monica's and Cyril's unnamed feelings (SW-C-0093; SW-P-0069).
2. **Identities the text withholds.** Five identities remain withheld, and products must keep them so:
   - the patron called 閣下 (SW-C-0039; SW-P-0029);
   - the person Mary does not want to die, and the hidden small star;
   - the seventh Sage;
   - Hilda's collaborator (SW-C-0076; SW-P-0057);
   - the prison's attacker by name.

   The ice spirit's identity with the pale-gold girl is implied, not stated (SW-C-0086, SW-C-0091).
3. **Prequel retrospection.** The two -another- volumes are read after V12. Their disclosures are recorded as supplemental retrospection and do not revise frozen numbered readings. Products must keep diegetic order distinct from reading order. For example, Louis's school years precede V01, yet were learned last.
4. **Narrated interiority is uneven.** Focalization rotates. Some figures (Monica, Isaac, Louis, Cyril) have extensive interior access. Others (Mary, Clockford, Raul, the Sages) are seen mostly from outside. Arguments about motive must respect that asymmetry.
5. **Written speech only.** All evidence is Japanese prose. No voice, timbre or performance evidence exists, and none is claimed.
6. **Paratext separation.** Character cards, design materials, afterwords and jackets were read but are kept apart from narrative. Products may cite them only as paratext.

### 4.3 Optional enrichments, left open

- Printed pagination, which the locator grammar does not need.
- The covers, title pages, store images, inline glyphs and preview images classified as not viewed at lock.
- The web serial, the web side stories, the related Rosalie web work, the comic adaptations and translations. All are outside the run, and no comparison is commissioned.

## 5. Method fit and the ledger decision

The method held across sixteen units without strain:

- short verified Japanese fragments;
- exposure disclosed at every entry freeze and no registered predictions after exposure;
- paratext kept apart from narrative;
- supplemental retrospection labelled by witness.

Two standing practices emerged during the run and are recorded here for the products. The first is the named-subject and referential-clarity standard of the [handoff contract](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md), section 15, with its knowledge-boundary safeguards. The second is the rule that withheld identities are never resolved by inference in analytical prose.

**Ledger decision: retain the single master ledger.** At 179 identities and 104 claims it is long, but length alone is not a reason to split (architecture section 4). No typed section has conflicting ownership. Specialists retrieve through stable record IDs without needing separate files. The synthesis products cite claim, revision, record and observation IDs, and do not copy ledger rows. A split is reconsidered only if the reconciliation finds independent revision pressure.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Every product must:

1. bind to the snapshot in section 2 and state its source boundary;
2. make a connected argument, not a compilation of recap bullets (architecture section 7);
3. trace each consequential claim to master claim or record IDs and to unit-prefixed observation numbers (for example V08 obs. 294; ANOTHER-UPPER obs. 210);
4. keep source order, diegetic order, revelation order and participant knowledge distinct, and mark retrospective prequel evidence as such;
5. state the strongest counterargument and the counterevidence it rests on;
6. preserve every withheld identity listed in section 4.2 and mark implied identities as implied;
7. pass the name-and-reference check of the handoff contract's section 15.6, with the sampled scope recorded in its closeout;
8. quote no Japanese beyond what already appears, verified, in the readings. A product may repeat a reading's verified fragment only with the same locator.

Role-specific criteria:

- **Character monographs.** They make recoverable:
  - development and state transitions, with mechanisms;
  - motive and self-report against conduct;
  - relationship conditioning across named recipients;
  - ordinary behaviour and humour;
  - written-speech features where the readings record them;
  - contradictions and counterevidence;
  - thematic function;
  - limitations.

  They do not diagnose.
- **Relationship arguments.** They argue each direction separately: initiative, dependence, refusal and repair, obligation and information asymmetry. Each dyad states what remains unresolved at the boundary.
- **Specialist arguments.** Each has one thesis, traceable diagnostic evidence, the strongest counterargument and an explicit source boundary.
- **Integrated synthesis.** It reconciles the specialists and monographs into one account, names their disagreements and resolves or bounds them. It does not concatenate abstracts.
- **Reconstruction-readiness assessment.** It applies the [character method](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/SW_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md) by subject, state and domain. It awards no tier that has not been tested, and records gaps separately from affirmative limitations.

## 9. Execution topology

The long-series protocol prefers fresh cloud sessions for retrospective synthesis and local integration afterwards. For this phase the local integrator, the session that ran the sequential readings, is the authorized executor under the owner's instruction. Fresh-context analysts receive the monograph, relationship and specialist targets of section 7, as bounded and separately scoped assignments.

The reason for this route is practical. The products must bind to a local, not yet fully published snapshot, and the owner asked for the whole program in one run.

- Each analyst works from a read-only copy of the snapshot, writes only its assigned draft, and edits no shared ledger or entrypoint.
- The integrator owns the reconciliation, the convergence, the integrated synthesis, the readiness assessment, and every change to shared state.
- Each delivered draft is checked against section 8 before it enters the repository. Material defects are repaired or recorded in its closeout.
