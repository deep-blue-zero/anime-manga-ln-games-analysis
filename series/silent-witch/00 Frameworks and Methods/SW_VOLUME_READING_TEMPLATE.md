# Volume reading template

This reusable contract implements [the analytical method](SW_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) and [architecture](SW_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md). It is not a source reading, frozen volume, literary conclusion, or permission to execute. Copy its responsibilities into an actual authorized unit only after source lock and foundation-gate verification; do not copy this file's artifact identity or template status into a reading.

An actual reading uses accurate `artifact_type: deep_reading`, the complete authority quartet, a unique artifact ID/canonical home, Japanese witness/edition/hash, source/knowledge boundary, generation, entry/exit state, observed coverage, and truthful inspection/acceptance/publication state. The future naming convention is `SW_V<NN>_DEEP_READING.md` in `02 Sequential Readings/`; that directory/file is not created here.

## 1. Identity, authorization, and source contract

State the exact source unit, Japanese title and edition, witness ID/hash, admitted parts, exclusions, source-lock and Drive evidence routes, locator/normalization version, prior exposure, input commit, and authorized terminal boundary. State separately which prose, illustrations and paratext were inspected.

## 2. Prior-volume developments — prepared before opening this source

For V01, say that no prior LN unit has been analyzed. For later volumes give a connected account of the inherited story situation, consequential prior developments, relationships, and unresolved tensions from closed units only. Preserve its preparation boundary and keep it distinct from the entry freeze.

## 3. Current-volume story synopsis — composed after full coverage

Write a connected causal account of the volume's starting situation, events and choices, reversals, relationship movement, ending, and admitted extra-story placement. Explain what happens before asking readers to follow a diagnostic argument. Qualify uncertain/reported events. This substantial prose section does not replace full coverage or close reading.

## 4. Entering prospective truth freeze

Preserve the pre-inspection record: prior boundary, current character/world/knowledge state, live claims, alternatives, uncertainties, questions, and any genuine predictions with discriminating opportunities. For an initial volume this is explicitly zero literary state. Reference a timed preserved record when available; never repair predictions using later knowledge.

## 5. Full admitted chapter/scene coverage

Use the fields below for every admitted unit, including prologue, epilogue, interludes and in-volume extras. The table is a coverage/accounting aid, not a compressed substitute for the analysis.

`unit/chapter | spine and paragraph range | content class | focalizer | actual inspection state | causal movement | gaps/exclusions and rationale`

Identify unrelated samples/previews and separate paratext and images. Resolve any admission ambiguity before claiming complete coverage.

## 6. Sustained diagnostic close reading

Develop arguments in connected prose around central episodes, language, narrative construction and consequential alternatives. Each diagnostic record has:

`observation ID | witness/unit/hash/locator | focalizer/speaker/recipient | observed feature or attributed report | evidence class | interpretation | rival account/counterevidence | uncertainty`

Use short necessary Japanese quotations with analyst glosses and recoverable source locators. Show why wording, address/register, hesitation/silence, focalization, humor, imagery, pacing or withholding changes interpretation. Explain character process and institutional/material choice constraints. Include scenes chosen independently of hypotheses and ordinary behavior.

## 7. Character, directional relationship, world and formal development

Explain state transitions, public/private differences, who knows or misreads what, A-to-B versus B-to-A changes, obligations, independent aims, competence and rules, and consequential prose/form/ethical patterns. Avoid timeless trait lists and invented diagnoses. Distinguish direct events, reports, narratorial presentation and inference.

## 8. Alternative readings, absence, and contradiction

For load-bearing claims state the strongest contrary evidence, rival explanation, limits, and possible falsifier. Record meaningful negative evidence only within a defined inspected opportunity; missing access remains a gap. Route contradictions by witness, source unit, time and knowledge status without forcing harmony.

## 9. Claims, predictions and cumulative delta

List affected master-ledger IDs and prior/new formulations with PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT, or OPEN. Adjudicate only predictions this volume genuinely tests; absent opportunity is UNTESTED. Document material updates or explained no-material-update for every required typed ledger responsibility. Preserve previous state.

## 10. Exit truth freeze and next questions

Freeze the reached LN-only boundary: established changes, unresolved questions, current knowledge/relationships/world state and evidence limitations. Any next-unit expectations are source-bounded hypotheses, not claims about unread text. A next candidate is an informational route unless separately authorized within an active continuous run.

## 11. Closure receipt

Record full admitted coverage; source/locator/wording checks; prose/image/paratext inspection separately; semantic review; ledger reconciliation; readiness changes or abstentions; durable transaction; entrypoint advancement; and unresolved mandatory versus optional evidence.

Use truthful separate fields for local authorship, semantic acceptance, committed closure, branch push, author preflight, housekeeping, exact-head Repository integration audit, and main integration. Git history or a later receipt records a containing commit; never invent a self-referential commit hash. A partial reading cannot advance the high-water mark.

Quality comes from the significant causal story, literary mechanisms, specific evidence and viable counterreadings. There is no fixed word target or ceiling. Filled headings, short plot bullets and generic character lists do not complete a deep reading.
