# Silent Witch synthesis and corpus architecture

## 1. Purpose, authority, and the paired foundation

One corpus under `series/silent-witch/` builds distinct products: substantial sequential volume readings; longitudinal state; character, relationship and specialist arguments; an integrated published-LN synthesis through an authorized boundary; optionally bounded textual-history/adaptation studies; and separately validated reconstruction models. None substitutes for another.

The single first-read surface is [CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md](../CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md). The [analytical method](SW_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) governs how evidence is read; this architecture governs where accumulated knowledge goes. The [character method](SW_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md) governs future behavior/speech models, and the [volume template](SW_VOLUME_READING_TEMPLATE.md) assists production. These V1 documents are current-eligible branch methodology for owner review. Main integration, semantic owner acceptance, and permission to read are distinct pending acts.

Architecture lifecycle is INITIAL. No predecessor is superseded. Live authority records, the [initiation policy](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md), archive authority, continuation, execution/handoff, publication, and integration controls outrank local defaults. Creating this corpus does not change the epoch-1 migration boundary.

## 2. Source model and storage planes

The [source/continuity map](../01%20Source%20Lock%20and%20Inventory/SW_SOURCE_AND_CONTINUITY_MAP.md) owns identities, integrity qualifications, exclusions, and admission. Numbered Japanese LN I–XII form the intended prospective primary track. No individual unit is execution-admitted or locked yet. Published IV -after-, IX -extra-, and -another- upper/lower are inventoried separate supplemental units. Web main N8356GA, web gaiden N5194GP, and shared-world Rosalie N8978FV have distinct witness and continuity identities. No correspondence or harmonization is assumed.

Publication date/order, diegetic placement, reader disclosure order, and admission order are independent fields. A prequel's early story time does not make it safe before V01. A short-story collection can contain multiple temporal positions and spoilers. Admission requires an explicit unit/story boundary and safe knowledge limit rather than numeric interpolation.

Git holds interpretation, methods, public-safe routing, state, and provenance. Governed Drive evidence holds EPUBs, snapshots, faithful/normalized text, raw paragraph locators, source alignments, and image collections. Temporary builds and transport copies are working state. Deterministic processing alone does not turn evidence into Git interpretation.

## 4. Why one master ledger

Concealment, knowledge, role pressure, competence, and relationship conditioning may interact strongly. Before reading, there is no observed evidence volume or independent retrieval burden that justifies five or six separate cumulative files. One master preserves the distinctions through exclusive typed sections and stable record IDs. This adapts Bookworm's proportional pre-split design while retaining MT's disciplined distinctions among state, knowledge, claims, and form.

The ledger owns cumulative interpretation, not a second copy of every scene. The volume reading owns the source-bearing observation. A record can link multiple typed records without duplicating their ownership: a disclosure event's participant knowledge belongs to knowledge state, its directional trust effect to relationships, and its hypothesized cause to claims.

Split only when recurring evidence, independent retrieval/revision, separate specialist dependency, or conflicting ownership makes the combined home inadequate. Record the reason, exact transferred IDs, destination, and necessary material backfill. Preserve IDs/history and leave routes in the old home; do not maintain two live copies. Mere length or another project's folder count is insufficient.

## 6. Sequential contribution and closure contract

One numbered volume is one atomic source transaction. Internal chapter/scene working units do not independently advance the volume high-water mark. A transaction contributes:

1. verified unit source lock and exclusions in the source map or a warranted dedicated lock receipt;
2. inherited prior-volume developments and timed entering freeze before new narrative inspection;
3. full admitted coverage, connected current-volume synopsis, sustained close reading, and compact diagnostic evidence records;
4. material changes to typed cumulative sections, or explicit reviewed no-material-update outcomes;
5. claim revisions and adjudicated genuine predictions, with UNTESTED opportunities retained;
6. an exit truth freeze and next-unit questions excluding later witness knowledge;
7. local readiness changes only when evidence supports them;
8. semantic review, durable persistence, and truthful entrypoint/coverage advancement.

Coverage, accepted interpretation, and repository publication remain distinct. A completely read but unresolved unit is not declared accepted; accepted prose without cumulative updates is not transaction-closed; a commit is not a remote audit. Preserve partial state and do not advance the high-water mark on incomplete closure.

## 7. Character, relationship, and specialist responsibilities

Major character work must eventually explain time-indexed motivations, appraisal, public/private presentation, ordinary behavior, speech, relationship conditioning, contradictions, choices and literary function. A dedicated home is earned by explanatory complexity and independent retrieval across eligible units, not cast prominence. A relationship synthesis is earned when reciprocal asymmetries, shared history, information/obligation, and repair require an argument beyond incidental character sections. Ensemble work should preserve autonomous goals.

Anticipated specialist responsibilities are investigative: disclosure/knowledge and narrative withholding; expertise/mathematics and magic constraints; school/aristocratic institutions and available agency; loyalty/deception/self-authorship; affiliative or romantic ambiguity and directional obligations; Japanese voice, silence, humor and ethical treatment. These are candidate domains, not mandatory separate files or conclusions.

At review, classify each domain WARRANTED, MONITOR, MERGE_INTO_EXISTING, DEFER, or NOT_SUPPORTED and explain its evidence/dependency basis. A warranted specialist has one thesis responsibility, traceable diagnostic evidence, strongest counterargument, and explicit source boundary. It makes substantial connected arguments; an outline or compilation of recap bullets cannot satisfy it.

## 8. Evidence retrieval, revision, and temporal continuity

Mature claim -> master claim/revision ID -> canonical volume observation -> witness/hash/locator -> original Drive evidence. A character evidence index, if earned, is a curated route through these homes, not another source dump. Short Japanese citations stay with the argument using them; raw normalized corpora do not enter Git.

Maintain source order, diegetic order, revelation order, and participant knowledge separately. A character belief, third-party attribution, narrator presentation, and reader inference cannot collapse into one omniscient state. Later disclosure can be REVEALED_NOT_NEW rather than developmental change. Knowledge updates do not grant an earlier model later information.

For revisions use PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT, or OPEN, retaining old wording, prior source boundary, new evidence/counterevidence, and affected downstream homes. Frozen sequential interpretations and checkpoints remain historical. Correct objective errors visibly and record downstream consequences; do not silently rewrite a prospective prediction.

Cross-source contradiction taxonomy: chronology/admission mismatch; ignorance/deception; focalization/reliability; later revelation or textual revision; edition/normalization defect; translation variation; supplemental retrospection; adaptation/continuity divergence; creator-paratext conflict; genuine inconsistency; unresolved cause. Record both incompatible formulations with witness/unit/time and knowledge status. Validate source/locator identity first, then investigate causes. Significant conflicts require a bounded audit and claim/dependency review rather than forced reconciliation.

## 9. Dependency graph, execution owners, and handoff

```text
Live governance + sufficient reconnaissance
  -> paired method/architecture + source map + initialized master + owner review
  -> separately authorized next-unit source lock and OPEN foundation gate
  -> complete prospective readings + synchronized cumulative state
  -> bounded checkpoint / architecture and evidence-gap review
  -> frozen target-specific input snapshot
  -> character, relationship and warranted specialist arguments
  -> source/claim checks + cross-specialist convergence
  -> integrated published-LN synthesis through the authorized high-water mark
  -> validation, publication, and an explicitly scoped release

Evidence/readiness review -> separately authorized reconstruction models and tests
Separately admitted WN/supplement/adaptation lane -> bounded comparison -> scoped convergence
```

Substantial literary monographs may precede operational models. Reconstruction depends on diagnostic evidence and domain tests; it is not automatically certified by a literary synthesis. A full-series argument must reconcile the specialists, not concatenate their abstracts.

Preferred future topology follows the [long-series protocol](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_LONG_SERIES_HYBRID_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md): adequately grounded fresh architecture; local Work/Codex sequential inspection and shared state; fresh substantive retrospective synthesis against a frozen snapshot; local evidence completion and publication. This explicitly requested local bootstrap is an adequate local architecture exception. No additional session or subagent is commissioned here.

One authorized integrator owns mutable source/cumulative/current state. Distinct specialists may receive unique targets in a later expressly scoped dispatch. Housekeeping owns five global routing outputs; the designated character-curation agent owns both global character outputs.

Handoff must carry the full produced prose, exact source/commit boundary, artifact IDs/paths and hashes, coverage/inspection states, claims and counterreadings, unresolved mandatory versus optional evidence, next owner/action, and preservation obligations. Apply the [handoff contract](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md), comparing dispatched base, returned candidate, and current target. Never substitute a short summary for a substantial incoming argument.

Reasoning classes: inventory/locks and mechanical locator work BOUNDED_STANDARD; normal readings SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS; architecture, checkpoints, monographs, comparisons and reconstruction DEEP_SYNTHESIS; propagation-sensitive convergence/full-series synthesis and adversarial audits PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST when justified. Resolve actual model/effort/capability at the assigned phase; no product label confers authority.

## 10. Amendments, reviews, and completion

Review after the first closed volume, at an evidence-supported arc/disclosure boundary, before a supplemental admission, when recurring dimensions outgrow the ledger, before specialist dispatch, and before a release. No volume numbers are asserted to be narrative arc boundaries in advance.

Material amendment records lifecycle/revision, reason, evidence/dependencies, transferred ownership, necessary backfill, and updated routes in this architecture and the entrypoint. Add infrastructure only for a defined responsibility. A changed central premise reopens affected specialists and convergence; an appended note cannot conceal an argument invalidated by new evidence.

|---|---|
| Bootstrap author completion | Exact identity/path scope, complete eligible design, sufficient qualified reconnaissance, initialized zero-state schemas, scoped validation. |

Entrypoint, source map and cumulative state are mutable through targeted verified patches. Frozen unit interpretations/checkpoints and release artifacts retain historical boundaries.
