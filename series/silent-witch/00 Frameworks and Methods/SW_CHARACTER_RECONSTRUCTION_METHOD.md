# Silent Witch character reconstruction method

## Separate responsibility and zero state

This method defines a future route from inspected Japanese-LN evidence to bounded behavioral and written-speech models. It creates no character profile, model, simulation, readiness award, or global capability assessment. The [corpus architecture](SW_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) owns activation and dependencies. [The master ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/SW_MASTER_LONGITUDINAL_LEDGER.md) is the sole initial home for local identity/state and readiness records; it currently has zero character records.

A literary monograph explains a person's development, relationships, contradictions and narrative function. An operational model describes constrained response ranges under specified knowledge, recipient, circumstance and stakes. Fluency of invented dialogue cannot substitute for either. A simulation is a test/output, never canonical evidence.

The separate method is justified because later reconstruction requires diagnostic sampling, conditional rules, testing and abstention beyond literary explanation. It adapts MT's state/domain discipline and Youjo Senki CMR's separation of interpretation from reconstruction without importing their characters, field inventories, or folder trees.

## 1. Identity, continuity, and time

Every future model must record:

- verified project-local identity and Japanese names/title/address aliases with ambiguity and source boundary;
- continuity and witness IDs, exact inspected coverage, evidence-set/basis commit, and model version;
- applicable diegetic interval or source-unit state, knowledge cutoff, recipient relationships, role and material constraints;
- excluded witnesses, prior franchise exposure, and unsupported contexts.

Names, concealed identities, titles, or disguises do not automatically constitute new entities. Preserve identity uncertainty until source evidence resolves it. A later alias may assist retrieval but cannot give an earlier participant later knowledge.

Use project-local keys until the designated character-curation agent establishes global identities. Do not invent or enroll global IDs, create parallel global discovery registers, or populate the production reconstruction-capability registry. If a future global assessment is separately commissioned, bind to the exact curated analysis subject and follow [RECONSTRUCTION_CAPABILITY_SPEC.md](../../../characters/RECONSTRUCTION_CAPABILITY_SPEC.md) and [characters/README.md](../../../characters/README.md). Local output tiers below are not global A–E capability judgments.

Separate four epistemic projections: what the person knows/believes; what other people attribute; what narration presents; and what readers learn. A correct reader interpretation does not become a character's belief, and a public reputation does not become an internal motivation.

## 2. Diagnostic sampling before modeling

Build evidence coverage beyond memorable crises. Sample relevant low-stakes conversation, routine work/study, courtesy, small frustrations, mistakes, humor, leisure, practical coordination, refusal, failed repair, ambiguous silence, and changes across recipients. Balance hypothesis-selected scenes with scenes selected independently for narrative or social centrality.

Track internal motivations and beliefs only to their evidentiary degree. Direct interior presentation can be biased or incomplete; external behavior alone may permit several appraisals. Seek cases where a self-description matches conduct and cases where it fails. Record meaningful negative constraints separately from missing evidence.

For each diagnostic scene reconstruct, with explicit/inferred links marked:

`circumstance -> perceived evidence -> appraisal -> competing goals/affect -> available options -> choice -> outward behavior/speech -> consequence`

Follow subsequent correction where it becomes eligible. Distinguish persistence, disposition change, knowledge change, role/context change, relationship change, revealed-not-new information, and unresolved difference. A failure of one domain does not erase competence in another; exceptional success cannot certify general mastery.

The source-bearing observation belongs to a volume reading. Evidence routes link to that observation and original source. Repeated descriptions of one episode do not count as independent corroboration. Do not publish raw scene/transcript collections to establish diagnostic coverage.

## 3. Required explanatory domains

A proposed model addresses each relevant domain through evidence or explicit uncertainty:

| Domain | Recoverable questions |
|---|---|
| Motivation and self-concept | What is wanted, feared, protected or pursued? How does self-report differ from demonstrated priorities? |
| Appraisal and affect | What is noticed or misread? Which perceived risks change behavior, and what regulates response? |
| Competence and agency | What can be done, what fails, and which resources, helpers or rules enable options? |
| Role and public/private state | How do school, institution, rank, duty, concealment and audience alter conduct? |
| Relationship conditioning | Which particular people activate trust, dependence, caution, teasing, disclosure or conflict? |
| Ordinary life and humor | Which non-crisis habits and interactions constrain a broad model? What kind of humor is source-supported? |
| Written speech | Pronouns, address, honorifics, register, hedging, hesitation, repair, turn-taking, indirectness and silence. |
| Conflict and deliberation | How are competing commitments, refusal, authority and consequences weighed? |
| Development | Which feature changes, when, through what mechanism, and where does an old tendency persist? |
| Contradiction and uncertainty | What would falsify the explanation? Which source observations resist it? |

Do not diagnose withdrawal or coping from a title, trope, remembered adaptation, or convenient clinical label. Describe source-supported behaviors and mechanisms. Do not make competence, loyalty, a social persona, or a single joke the whole person.

## 4. Operational rule and evidence schemas

Future rules use stable local `SW-MR-<sequence>` identifiers. Each rule records: applicable states and witness range; trigger/context; recipient/audience; knowledge prerequisites; likely appraisal; competing motives; response range; inhibitors/escalators; speech constraints; negative constraints; supporting observations; counterevidence; plausible alternatives; confidence basis; discriminating opportunity; evidence distance; and validation history.

A useful rule excludes some plausible responses and states when it is inapplicable. An elastic rule that explains every opposite outcome after the fact cannot support reconstruction. Avoid invented numeric psychological thresholds.

Use qualitative distance: DIRECT_SOURCE, NEAR_ANALOG, STRUCTURAL_ANALOG, EXTRAPOLATED, or SPECULATIVE. Larger distance requires narrower specificity, disclosed assumptions, and wider alternative ranges.

Readiness records in the master ledger name subject/state, domain/context, evidence/coverage, rival model, gaps versus affirmative limitations, tests and result, restrictions, date and reviewer. Any rule/model path appears only when the artifact actually exists. A local readiness update preserves the preceding assessment and its boundary.

## 5. Tiered outputs and activation

| Output | Minimum basis | Boundaries |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition profile | Distinctive, source-supported identity/behavior/voice anchors and declared uncertainty | Recognition and bounded canon questions; no broad novel-scenario competence. |
| Domain-bounded model | Coherent conditional rules supported by varied relevant scenes, ordinary behavior, recipient contrast and counterevidence review | Only named states/contexts; unsupported domains remain unassessed. |
| Fuller reconstruction | Broad relevant domain coverage, longitudinal transitions, contradictions, independently checked rules, and adequate tests | Still source-, state- and context-bounded; never arbitrary omniscient simulation. |

Local readiness vocabulary is UNASSESSED, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, BOUNDED_PROVISIONAL, or DOMAIN_READY. Assign no state from popularity, protagonist status, scene count, prose length, or successful roleplay. A domain-ready account of professional coordination can coexist with insufficient evidence about unfamiliar intimacy or politics.

A substantive character monograph is warranted when independent retrieval and a sustained causal/literary argument are possible. Its existence does not prove operational readiness. A useful narrow model may be possible before a mature series-wide monograph; it must meet its own evidence/test contract.

## 6. Testing without circular confirmation

Freeze the tested rule and input boundary before revealing the target when practical. Use:

- source reproduction from only the preceding eligible state, labeling retrospective fitting honestly when the target was already known;
- contrasts across recipients, social roles, stakes and public/private settings;
- genuinely prospective expectations with defined opportunities and falsifiers;
- mundane social/work exchanges so that all tests are not catastrophes;
- negative-constraint checks against later-knowledge leakage, out-of-state maturity, invented abilities, stereotype dialogue and analyst vocabulary;
- held-out or independently sampled source scenes where available, with familiarity/pretraining limitations disclosed.

An absent opportunity is UNTESTED. A plausible invented scene is not source confirmation. Record actual failures and revise or reject overbroad rules rather than accumulating unfalsifiable exceptions.

A scenario specification states character/state/continuity, participants, knowledge and relationship conditions, changed premises, setting/stakes, applicable rules, source distance, alternatives and abstentions. Source dialogue, analyst translation and synthetic dialogue must remain labeled separately. Written-speech evidence does not establish actor timbre or acoustic performance. Any audiovisual extension needs a separately admitted witness and verified inspection route.

## 7. Acceptance, revision, and reconstruction limits

Accept a proposed tier only after the model's scope, evidence trail, ordinary behavior, recipient specificity, counterevidence, uncertainty and test history support that tier. Keep thin supporting characters explicitly thin. Preserve alternatives that cannot be discriminated.

Use the master claim/revision history for material mechanism changes and preserve rule IDs/testing history. A consequential failure may expose wrong knowledge, recipient conditioning, applicability or causal model; record affected monographs and dependencies. Do not rewrite earlier prospective freezes to make the model appear predictive.

Global discovery and global reconstruction assessments remain separate, owner-controlled responsibilities. No populated global capability file or A–E grade is an output of this bootstrap. Future assessment requires specific authorization and exact committed evidence binding.

State at bootstrap: NO_SOURCE_UNIT_ANALYZED; zero identities modeled; zero readiness assessments; no recognition profile, domain model, fuller reconstruction or synthetic dialogue created.
