## 2. Prior-volume developments

*Prepared 2026-10-14 before any V07 narrative member was rendered for reading, from the closed V01 to V06 readings, the closed IV -after- reading, their exit freezes and the master ledger only.*

Monica Everett, the Silent Witch, is seventeen and in her second term at Serendia Academy. There, as Monica Norton, the student council's treasurer, she guards the second prince, Felix Arc Ridill, on Louis Miller's order. Her familiar Nero sleeps through the cold. Her left hand still aches from the cursed dragon.

At New Year she met the other Sages. Raul Roseburg, the Thorn Witch, overheard her and Ray Albright suspect Duke Clockford of staging the dragon, and joined them as a friend. Ray reported that Felix had asked whether a living thing can be controlled by curse, and that Clockford had obstructed his inquiry. Clockford asked the Silent Witch to guard Felix, offered her office, and said Felix moves at his will. In an anger she later named, she surrounded him with a mental-interference formula and refused. Mary Harvey warned Louis that the Sages' star was darkening. Bartholomeus has since reported that the footman Peter Sams worked for Clockford from about eight years ago, shortly before her father's execution; Monica infers a link, unconfirmed.

At school, Felix searches for a small girl with an injured left hand. Hubert Dee, the Minerva senior who once hunted her in magic battles, transferred, found her out, and forced a duel for her place. She defeated him in fog by turning his own ring tools on him; Cyril, fainting, heard Hubert call the small figure the Silent Witch. Felix ordered his spirit to kill Hubert, though no one knows. He carried Monica to the infirmary, recalling a weeping friend who called him Ike. The third prince Albert has made friends with Glen and Monica. Robert Winkel courts Monica by chess. Lana has offered her work after graduation.

A boy-shaped ice spirit and a wolf spirit carried Cyril and Glen to Kellylinden forest to stop a "piper". It is the Jewel Mage, Emmanuel Darwin, who holds the lost ancient whistle Galanis. With it he controls spirits, including Louis's spirit Lynn, and powers armour with them, for a patron who wants war. The Sages act in secret to destroy the whistle and his tools. Raul and Ray have found Cyril and Glen; Mary and the former Sage Carla Maxwell are helping; Felix is in the forest unseen. Monica, the diversion, has just been attacked by Lynn.

## 3. Current-volume story synopsis

**The prologue.** In Kellylinden forest, Lynn, under the whistle, attacks Monica and Bartholomeus from a treetop. A spirit needs no chant, and Monica holds her off with barriers, calculating to last. Bartholomeus offers a plan, and Monica, after hesitating, asks him for help.

**Chapter one.** Bartholomeus's past is told. He was a generalist craftsman in the Empire. He told his talented younger sister that taking any work was a craftsman's pride. She was later arrested for forgery, and he heard she was executed. He has lost his pride, but not his stubbornness. He recognizes the armour as parts he once made for Emmanuel, rigs an exploding device and pins Lynn inside an empty armour while Monica seals her. Elsewhere Louis, calling himself a passing woodcutter, cuts armour apart with a borrowed axe while Bradford conserves his mana.

**Chapter two.** Raul offers Cyril and Glen turnips and apples. The armour arrives, and a gold-trimmed suit that drinks mana pierces the ice spirit, which proves to have no arms. Raul has Ray curse him to fall asleep in ten minutes, sends the others away, and in the manner of the first Thorn Witch raises the man-eating rose fortress. It consumes the armour and the fire spirit Relva. Ray tells Cyril that spirits are more like weather than neighbours. Glen says that if they can help it, they want to. Sezdio privately resolves to make the two humans the ice spirit's offering.

**Chapter three.** Monica finds Louis and Bradford among wrecked armour and tells them that the armour is spirit-powered. A Galanis-bound earth spirit is defeated by Bradford's quadruple fireball, Louis running along a strip of barrier, and Monica's barriers. Monica refuses to split a formula for quadruple strength because it is not beautiful. Emmanuel, alone, compares himself with the Sages he thinks irreplaceable. He became a Sage through Clockford's backing when Carla Maxwell fell. He resents Louis, and resents that Clockford courted the Silent Witch while he has served "that person" far longer.

**Chapter four.** A framed legend tells how the winter spirit Orteria's bell reached the spirit god. Ray tells Cyril that Glen still bears the curse's residue, and that the Silent Witch's left hand is cursed. Sezdio pins Glen and Ray to make them the ice spirit's offering. The ice spirit freezes him instead and sings a lullaby, and Cyril recognizes it as Romaria of the legend. She says she once killed humans and should not have asked for help. At the hut, Louis explains that the whistle has appeared in every war and may take over its user.

**Chapter five.** Louis baits Emmanuel; Emmanuel's spirits and hidden armour attack. Bradford's quadruple fireball is reflected by a buried tool, the Paulo-Schmell execution mirror, a first-class reflection barrier. Monica and Raul's roses stop the rebound. Louis hints that the fight is won if Emmanuel does not look at the sky.

**Chapter six.** Ray arrives on cursed armour he has made his own. Bradford calls the fire spirit king and fires a six-fold shot. Emmanuel spends his barrier tools and reflects it, as Bradford expected. Carla's seven-spell Star Spear then falls through Mary's illusion and breaks the mirror, while Monica shields Emmanuel with a stream of barriers. The whistle takes over Emmanuel and floods the land with mana. Mary and the ancient tool Mira draw it back into the forest, and Monica cuts the whistle in two with a split quadruple formula.

**Chapter seven.** Mary recruits Bartholomeus to help the Silent Witch; Lynn thanks Carla. Romaria, dying, tells Cyril that she once froze a village while protecting Orteria from men who wanted the god's gift for war. Cyril gives her a frozen rose as a gift. Monica seals Romaria into Cyril's brooch, which holds his surplus mana. Cyril thanks the Witch formally, and Monica wishes he had praised his treasurer instead. Louis notes that Monica knew the name of the armour before Emmanuel said it, and decides to withhold information from her.

**Chapter eight.** Louis tests whether Monica would seal Cyril's and Glen's memories; Mary forbids it and closes the affair. Sezdio calms the angry spirits and tells Monica he will not thank her. Raul slips Monica a letter. Mary senses something stirring: a hidden star near the Witch and the holder of the star of loss. In the attic Bridget asks Monica who she is. On the road Felix meets the fleeing Emmanuel and offers to help him.

**Chapter nine.** Bridget, who hired the detective, takes Monica for a Kerbeck spy. She offers to end Felix's search in three days in return for help in learning a secret about him. Raul's letter invites Monica to infiltrate Clockford's house on Sheffield's holiday. Bridget remembers Felix at seven: shy, stammering and kind, making her a rose from his scarf. A scarred page boy stopped her from shaming the prince's kindness.

**Chapter ten.** Cyril and Glen have colds; Cyril apologizes for losing the duel. Rumours that Felix will marry a girl with an injured left hand fill the school with bandaged hands, and Felix's search stalls. Elliott recalls that it was always Felix who caught cold in the snow.

**Chapter eleven.** Bridget tells Monica that after an illness about ten years ago Felix came back a different person, and that she believes the present Felix false and the real one confined at Clockford's. Monica decides she wants to know him. Felix interrupts and passes Bridget's test by making the blue rose. Bridget insists on joining the infiltration.

**Chapter twelve.** Monica asks what love is, reads philosophy and consults Lana and Claudia, and concludes that it differs for everyone and should be respected. Nero wakes; Monica tells him what she has kept from Louis. She remembers that Mary lost sight of Felix's fate about ten years ago.

**Chapter thirteen.** Disguised as gardeners with Raul, Monica and Bridget enter Clockford's grounds. An old gardener's medicine was prescribed by Peter Sams. He tells them that Sams came about ten years ago, replacing a Dr Arthur. A fire then killed Arthur and the prince's page, Isaac Walker. The butler calls the iris by its Imperial name. Clockford appears, and Raul turns aside an invitation by naming his sister.

**The epilogue.** No captive prince is found. Bridget learns that Arthur's Imperial form is Artur, and Monica says that everything connects. The gardener remembers the scarred boy who smuggled a forbidden astronomy book to the prince. A newspaper notice from "the silent woman" asks "the blue-scaled clay man" for an answer. Yuan shows it to his master, who resolves to come to Ridill.

**The secret episode.** Mary sets Bartholomeus up in a workshop of his own, because there is someone she does not want to die.

## 6. Close reading

### 6.1 Structure: the forest, then the house

V07 is built in two halves:

- Chapters one to eight finish the Kellylinden affair that V06 opened.
- Chapters nine to thirteen and the epilogue turn from the Sages' secret to Felix's.

The hinge is chapter eight. Two scenes there open the second half: Bridget waits in Monica's attic (obs. 146), and Felix waits for Emmanuel on the road (obs. 150).

**Help asked.** V06 left Monica unable to ask for help (V06 obs. 114, 130). V07 opens with her asking. Hesitating to rely on a civilian, she says 「お、お願い、しますっ！」 to Bartholomeus (S11:E000104; obs. 005), and his plan seals Lynn (obs. 015). The theme runs on through the volume:

- The framed legend tells how a dying winter spirit's call for help was answered through a bell (obs. 060).
- Romaria, who once killed in answering such a call, believes she may never ask again (obs. 069, 122).
- Albert offers his help, and Monica takes it (S28:E000172–E000178).
- Bridget asks Monica's help in her own way, by bargaining (obs. 153).

The reading records that Monica's first request for help is made to a civilian she had not counted as a fighter (S11:E000098), and that it succeeds.

**Uniqueness and competence.** Chapter three's title names the aesthetics of geniuses and the value of being unique. The volume sets its characters on that scale:

- Emmanuel measures himself against the Sages he thinks irreplaceable and finds himself replaceable (obs. 055, 056).
- Louis answers that being able to do most things as a matter of course is greater (obs. 078), and wins with an axe (obs. 022, 048).
- Bartholomeus is second-rate at everything and stubborn without pride (obs. 008, 014).
- Monica refuses to split a formula because it is not beautiful, then splits one to destroy the whistle and laments it (obs. 053, 110, 111).
- Bradford shares her aesthetic (S15:E000367).

Emmanuel's envy leads him to the whistle (obs. 081). The volume does not reward uniqueness for its own sake. It is won by planning in which each part is ordinary: a decoy, a cue, a shield (obs. 100).

### 6.2 The Sages united, and divided

The forest battle is the first time the Sages fight as a body (obs. 052). Its cover is preserved to the end. Mary swears the students to silence and closes the affair, forbidding both sanction and memory sealing (obs. 139, 141).

Inside the body, trust is thin:

- Louis, seeing that Monica knew the armour's name, infers a helper she has not disclosed (obs. 135).
- He thinks she may side with Clockford through Felix (obs. 134).
- He resolves to withhold information and test her (obs. 136); his proposal of memory sealing is narrated as such a test (obs. 138).
- Monica, for her part, withholds four matters from him (obs. 143).

Lynn is busy with "Louis's schemes" (obs. 191). The reading records the Sages' unity in action and their mutual concealment as twin facts of the volume.

### 6.3 Spirits and humans

The volume stages a dispute about whether humans can help spirits:

- Ray: spirits are near to natural phenomena; one can only watch them; seeking more brings ruin (obs. 040, 041).
- Louis: spirits act for their reasons and humans for theirs (obs. 112).
- Sezdio regards contracts as servitude, and humans as the cause (obs. 045; S23:E000313–E000315).
- Glen and Cyril simply want to help (obs. 042, 124).

Romaria's history complicates both sides. She loved humans, froze a village to protect Orteria from men who wanted the god's gift for war, and gained strength from the frozen lives (obs. 120, 121). Sezdio meant the students as her offering (obs. 046, 063). Mira's redistribution of the pooled mana saves the forest for human reasons, and the narration says so (obs. 109).

Monica's act settles nothing in principle. She seals Romaria into Cyril's brooch from what she calls her own ego, to spare Cyril and Glen grief (obs. 126, 127). Raul's response gives the dispute a personal turn: those who grieve for spirits might grieve for someone like him (obs. 131).

### 6.4 Emmanuel, Galanis and the war faction

Emmanuel's interior is given at length:

- He envies the Sages' uniqueness and Louis's patron (obs. 055, 057, 077).
- He repaired the whistle and powered his tools with spirits as his life's work (obs. 081, 088).
- He hid the whistle from Clockford, meaning to present it with an army to a war-minded patron (obs. 083).

In his thoughts the Clockford who courted the Witch is 「あの方」, whom he has long served (S15:E000458; obs. 058). V06's secret episode used the same words for a patron who wants war (V06 obs. 211). The reading takes the same speaker's same words as referring to the same man, and records the V06 patron as Clockford on that textual basis (SW-C-0057). It does not extend that identification to the 閣下 of Sams or the great noble of Hilda.

The whistle's own purpose is shown when it takes over Emmanuel:

- It speaks in his head and floods the land with mana to breed spirits (obs. 103, 105).
- It calls itself the maker of kings (obs. 106).

This bears out Louis's account of its history and of tools that take over their users (obs. 071, 074), and Monica's forecast of land pollution (obs. 075). Defeated, Emmanuel flees, and Felix meets him with an offer of help (obs. 148, 150).

### 6.5 Felix through other eyes

Felix appears on stage less than in any volume since V02, but is described more. Four witnesses give accounts that do not fit the man the school sees:

- **Bridget**: a timid, stammering, kind boy, who after a long illness about ten years ago returned changed (obs. 159, 165, 171, 173).
- **Elliott**: the one who always caught cold in the snow (obs. 170).
- **The gardener**: a sickly child, little seen, whose page died in a fire (obs. 201–203).
- **Mary**, as Monica recalls her: she lost sight of his fate about ten years ago (obs. 193).

The present Felix passes Bridget's test of the blue rose, saying he practised as a child (obs. 180). Bridget concludes that he has studied the past (obs. 181).

His acts in V07 are three:

- the search for the Witch, stalled by Bridget's rumour (obs. 168, 169);
- the tea-room visit (S27:E000243);
- the approach to Emmanuel (obs. 150).

The card calls the motive for the last unknown (section 7.5).

### 6.6 Monica: wanting to know

Monica's development in V07 is the wish to know another person. Hearing Bridget, she finds she wants to know why Ike sometimes looks as if he has given something up (obs. 179). The narration marks this as the first time she has wanted to know someone, and the first time she has stepped forward of her own accord (S27:E000233–E000235). Several moments mark the change:

- She decides on the infiltration herself (obs. 157).
- She looks into love because Bridget's devotion should not be slighted. She arrives at a provisional answer: love differs for everyone and is cherished as she cherishes numbers (obs. 186, 190).

## 8. Alternative readings, absence and contradiction

### 8.1 "The present Felix is not the prince"

The volume assembles evidence that points this way:

- Bridget's memory of a different boy and her belief (obs. 171–176);
- the illness and the fire about ten years ago (obs. 173, 202);
- Mary's lost sight of his fate (obs. 193);
- Elliott's memory of a sickly child (obs. 170);
- Yuan's "trace" of Artur (obs. 213);
- Monica's conviction and Bridget's foreboding (obs. 211).

Against settling it here:

- No narrator or character states who the present Felix is.
- The present Felix passes Bridget's test (obs. 180).
- Bridget's and Monica's views are beliefs.
- The cards speak of Felix only by his title and name.

The reading registers the beliefs as beliefs and offers no identification of the present Felix, of the page Isaac Walker, or of the friend who called Felix Ike (SW-C-0058, SW-P-0016).

### 8.2 "Arthur is Artur"

The gardener names a Dr Arthur who died in the fire (obs. 199, 202). Sams cried that he would not share Arthur's fate (V05). Yuan spoke of a traitor Artur's trace on Felix (V04). Monica links all three (obs. 200, 213). The text gives the link as hers. The reading records it as her inference and does not merge the references (SW-C-0031, SW-P-0027).

### 8.3 Normative evaluation: the test of memory sealing

Criterion: whether the volume weighs the mission's secrecy against the autonomy of the people it protects. Louis proposes sealing Cyril's and Glen's memories, narrated as a test of Monica's willingness (obs. 138). Monica objects on grounds of harm. Mary forbids it and ends the affair (obs. 141). The reading records that the volume lets the proposal be made by a trusted ally and refused by authority, not by Monica's own refusal.

### 8.4 Normative evaluation: saving a spirit

Criterion: whether helping a being that cannot consent is presented as a good. Ray calls it self-satisfaction and Louis calls each side's motives its own (obs. 112, 130). Monica binds Romaria to the brooch without a contract and calls it her ego (obs. 126, 127). Romaria earlier refused help because of her own killings (obs. 122). The volume shows the act's warmth through Cyril and Raul (obs. 124, 131), and keeps the objections standing.

### 8.5 Meaningful absences

- Hubert, Isabelle and Albert appear only in passing.
- Felix's motive for meeting Emmanuel is withheld.
- Raul's letter contains no account of his own aims.
- The name of Yuan's master is withheld.
- The person Mary does not want to die is unnamed (obs. 225).
- Monica's conclusion about Felix is not spoken (obs. 211, 213).
- 閣下, the great noble and the black chalice are not advanced.

### 8.6 The afterword, the jacket and the cards

The afterword says the Sages work together and Monica approaches the story's central mystery (S43:E000011). It announces that the upper volume of the Louis companion is out, and the lower due in spring 2024 (S43:E000044). That companion is admitted only after V12, as the owner's instruction requires. The jacket frames the second half as a student seeing through Monica (obs. 228). The cards are listed in section 7.5. All of these are paratext.

### 8.7 Apparent contradictions

- Sams entered Clockford's service about eight years ago by Bartholomeus's report (V06) and about ten years ago by the gardener's (obs. 199). These are two approximate reports; neither is preferred.
- Louis is called the Magic Corps' former head in narration (obs. 079) and trained there with weapons (card). These are consistent.
- The kuchie-004 caption anticipates chapter thirteen's line (obs. 194, 229). This is not a contradiction.
