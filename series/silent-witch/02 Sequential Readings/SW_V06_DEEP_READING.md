## 2. Prior-volume developments

*Prepared 2026-10-14 before any V06 narrative member was rendered for reading, from the closed V01 to V05 and IV -after- readings, their exit freezes and the master ledger only.*

Monica Everett, the Silent Witch, is seventeen and about three months into a secret mission at Serendia Academy. There, as Monica Norton, the student council's treasurer, she guards the second prince, Felix Arc Ridill, on Louis Miller's order. Isabelle Norton covers her. Her familiar Nero is the Worgan black dragon, who chose her as master after she pulled a bone from his throat; he can take human and cat form, and sleeps through the cold.

Over the winter break she guarded Felix officially, veiled and silent, as the Silent Witch, at Duke Leinbourg's estate during talks with Falfolia, with Glen Dudley as the second guard. A cursed green dragon attacked. Glen was cursed protecting others and survived, his mana found to exceed 250. Felix shot the dragon through a gap in Monica's barrier, and later went out alone to finish it. Nero burned it in his true form, and threatened Felix into silence about him. Monica's left hand was cursed; the pain is expected to last about a month.

The caster was the footman Peter Sams, once Barry Oates, the curse-caster who betrayed the Albrights. Before dying by his own curse he called Monica by her father's name, said he had sold Venedict Rain to a patron he called 閣下, and spoke of Arthur's fate. Hilda Everett then told Monica that a curse-caster her father had refused had him charged with necromancy on planted evidence, and that a great noble pressed the execution through without trial; she would not name the noble. Monica vowed to prove her father innocent. She has hired the handyman Bartholomeus, who mistakenly believes she and Felix are lovers, and shares a secret inquiry with Ray Albright, the Abyss Curse Mage. A recent note in her father's book, apparently from the bookseller Porter, mentions "the truth of the black chalice".

Felix learned that the Silent Witch belongs to Serendia Academy and that her familiar is a black dragon. He means to find her by her injured left hand. He believes his grandfather, Duke Clockford, staged the dragon to make him a hero and means to make him a true puppet, and he resolves to act. He holds a water spirit although his middle name points to earth, and a private vow to his best friend ends with the word Arc. No explanation is registered.

Cyril was reconciled with his mother and taken to the New Year audience as heir. The third prince, Albert, is leaving Minerva for Serendia and intends to find Felix's weaknesses. Bridget has hired a detective to watch Monica Norton. Bernie has written to warn Monica that Hubert Dee, who once pursued her for magic battles, is transferring to Serendia. The king is ill.

## 3. Current-volume story synopsis

**The prologue** is set in Kerbeck before the solstice. A detective hired by an unnamed young lady comes to check on Monica Norton. The farmers tell him the cover story: a girl taken from a convent by the late countess and now kept in the stable. He sees Isabelle, her maid Agatha and the earl, Azul Norton, abuse the girl and leaves convinced. The girl is Sandy, a carrot farmer's twelve-year-old daughter, chosen by audition to stand in for Monica over the break. Someone had been asking the county's convents about a girl called Monica, and the earl, his family and his tenants staged the scene to answer him.

**Chapter one.** At the New Year offering Monica's ice chime is admired, but she fainted standing up afterwards. The Thorn Witch, Raul Roseburg, never arrived, and Mary covered his absence with an illusion. The Jewel Mage, Emmanuel Darwin, courts Monica for the second prince's faction, and Louis mocks his small mana. Mary sends Monica and Ray to find Raul. In the garden Cyril, sent out by his adoptive father to calm his nerves, meets a red-haired gardener who talks about fertilizer and lavatories. Ray tells Monica that Barry Oates's curse tool was a failed attempt to control a target by curse. Felix recently asked Ray whether such control is possible, and Duke Clockford, Darius Knightley, obstructed Ray's inquiry for a time. Monica fears the cursed dragon was Clockford's farce and wonders how much Felix knows. Cyril finds the gardener is the fifth Thorn Witch and senses he has met the Silent Witch before; the first prince, Lionel, chasing his mother's cats, praises Cyril and says Felix is fitter to rule. Raul has overheard Monica and Ray through his flowers. He offers his access to Clockford's house and confesses that he wants friends, and the three youngest Sages form a group.

**Chapter two.** The ailing king, Ambrose, presides in silence over a ceremony run by Lionel and his mother. At the banquet Felix greets the Witch warmly, and Ray covers her retreat with a harmless glowing curse. Cyril, courteous to a stranger, asks whether they have met; Monica pushes him away and runs, sick at being treated as a stranger by him. Mary tells Louis that the Sages' star is darkening and asks how much he knows of the king's illness. Louis decides to have Lynn investigate a rumour about Emmanuel. Clockford summons the Witch, offers her the post of Felix's guard, subordinates and a proposed head-of-Sages office, and says Felix moves as he wills. In anger she cannot yet name, she lets the white butterflies of a mental-interference formula surround him. She tells him she wants nothing from him. Felix, who knows nothing of the offer, meets her in the corridor; she passes him in fear.

**Chapter three.** Bartholomeus, now working as a delivery man to reach Monica, gives a lift to a red-haired youth who parodies his love song. Isabelle tells Monica that someone has been asking about Monica Norton, and gives her a family-written winter diary to memorise. A letter from Bernie warns that Hubert Dee, who chased her at Minerva, is transferring. At school Glen hides the curse's after-effects, and Monica's friends take her real bread-and-pickles solstice as abuse. At the first council meeting Felix asks the members to look for a small girl with an injured left hand. Monica flexes her painful hand to deny it, and Cyril watches her.

**Chapter four.** Three transfers arrive: Albert in the middle school, Robert Winkel, the chess player from Randall, and Hubert Dee in the third year. Glen dreams of the night at Minerva when a red-haired senior hunted him in a magic battle and his mana ran away. Eliane contrives to meet Glen and finds Claudia beside him. The red-haired transfer asks the way, does not remember Glen, and calls him a loser. Robert reads Monica a chess poem, shows her a drawing of his dogs and asks again for her hand, on his four brothers' advice.

**Chapter five.** Monica studies biology for her father's book and looks for the black chalice. She advises Glen to learn a homing formula before short chanting, attributing the advice to Cyril. Albert, half a hostage at Serendia, sets his servant Patrick to find Felix's weak points. He hears that Felix treats Monica as a pet, and decides to win over Glen and Monica. Felix feeds Monica cake, and says he would like her to ask him for help. Albert's staged dropped handkerchief ends in a tea party, where Glen declares everyone friends.

**Chapter six.** Hubert defeats the magic-battle club, firing arrows without apparent chant. On Lana's birthday Lana asks Monica to work with her after graduation, and Monica, who will leave in half a year, cannot answer. Hunting Hubert with Cyril and Elliott, Monica hides under Cyril's coat. Hubert pulls her out, flies off with her and, alone with her, works out that she guards or watches a prince. Cyril binds him with ice and Glen snatches Monica away. Before Felix, Hubert proposes a magic-battle duel for her: if he wins she leaves the council and is his. Glen and Cyril accept; Felix orders Cyril to win.

**Chapter seven.** Her friends skip class to feed and rest Monica, and no one asks what lies between her and Hubert. Robert joins the duel. Glen hits Hubert with a homing fireball, but the three challengers are then struck down by arrows from behind, and Glen loses consciousness thinking it is chantless magic. Felix, watching, orders his spirit Wildianu to kill Hubert and make it look an accident, and goes after Monica. She has slipped out and flown to the forest.

**Chapter eight.** Monica covers the field in fog, tells Hubert she is angry, and joins the duel. Hubert, who has longed since Minerva to hunt her, reveals his ring tools; Monica turns them against him with a new homing formula and a rewrite of his own tools, and strikes. She collapses in a corridor, and Felix carries her to the infirmary, seeing in her a weeping childhood friend who called him Ike. A boy-shaped ice spirit and a wolf spirit carry off the unconscious Cyril and Glen, with Wildianu clinging to the wolf's tail. Louis and Carla, MacLaggan's helpers, find them gone and Lynn's contract cut. In a workshop north-east of the school, an old man with an ancient whistle that controls spirits holds Lynn.

**Chapter nine.** MacLaggan warns Hubert off; Isabelle threatens to have him driven from the kingdom. Louis wakes Monica at midnight. Glen and Cyril are missing, Lynn is lost, and Mary suspects Emmanuel of holding the lost ancient tool, the Galanis whistle, in Kellylinden forest. In a cave there the ice spirit and the wolf Sezdio ask Cyril and Glen to stop the "piper" who rules the forest's spirits; Cyril tells the apologising spirit that asking for help is never wrong. Wildianu reports to Felix, who sees a use for Emmanuel and leaves to "do a job".

**Chapter ten.** Louis flies Monica to the forest and tells her she has learnt anger; the Sages will hush the affair to keep the noble assembly from seizing them, destroy Emmanuel's tools and destroy the whistle. Monica is the diversion. In the cave Cyril sings for the small spirits and promises the ice spirit frozen flowers.

**Chapter eleven.** Monica saves Bartholomeus from a self-moving armour powered by a captured spirit; he once worked for Emmanuel, who sold his workers' tools as his own. Felix uses his white uniform to lead the Sages to Cyril and Glen. A fire spirit attacks them, and Raul's roses and thorns drive it off. Raul and Ray introduce themselves as a passing gardener and a passing poet.

**The epilogue.** Monica dismantles the armour soldiers, frees and seals their spirits, and hears Bartholomeus's report: Peter Sams entered Clockford's service about eight years ago, shortly before her father's execution. Bartholomeus refuses payment and tells her a child may ask adults for help. Lynn, under the whistle, attacks them from a treetop.

**The secret episode.** Emmanuel orders the intruders taken alive, except Louis, whose death would please a patron who wants war. On a hill Mary, carrying a voice-filled jewel box, accepts the help of Carla Maxwell, Louis's senior and a former Sage, the Starspear Witch.

## 6. Close reading

### 6.1 Structure: anger named, and help asked for

V06 returns to the school, but it opens at court and ends in a forest. Two threads organize it.

**Anger.** At Clockford's table Monica is seized by a feeling she cannot name and answers with the butterflies of a mental-interference formula (obs. 064). Chapter eight names it: standing over Glen, she recognizes the same heat and calls it anger (obs. 154), and announces it to Hubert before she strikes (obs. 155). Chapter ten has Louis license it. He tells her she seldom grew angry only because she did not care about others, and that fighting without anger made her uncanny (obs. 184, 185). The volume thus moves anger from an unnamed loss of control to a recognized feeling with a cause. It does not settle whether the anger is right; Monica herself asks whether a Sage should cast in anger (S24:E000052), and Louis's answer is not offered as the book's.

**Help.** The volume returns repeatedly to whether one may ask for help:

- Felix wants Monica to ask him for help, and she refuses (obs. 114, 115).
- Carried off by Hubert, Monica cannot say the word to Cyril (obs. 130).
- Felix, holding her, remembers a friend who apologized for being a burden and thinks he wanted to be relied on more (obs. 165, 166).
- Cyril tells the apologizing ice spirit that asking for help is never wrong (obs. 182).
- Bartholomeus refuses payment and tells her a child may lean on adults (obs. 207).

Monica's habit of apology, shown in her collapse (S22:E000095), links these scenes. The volume shows three people asking her to accept help and does not yet show her doing it.

**Villainy as performance.** The prologue's "villainous family theatre" is a kindness disguised as cruelty (obs. 005, 007, 010). Isabelle calls herself a villainess when she threatens Hubert (S23:E000158). Louis's plan to ruin Emmanuel makes Monica feel she has joined the villains (obs. 190). The volume repeatedly asks how far appearances of cruelty or righteousness can be trusted, from Clockford's courtesy to Emmanuel's signed tools (obs. 194).

### 6.2 Monica's two faces

V06 is the first volume in which Monica meets her school friends while she is the Silent Witch:

- Cyril treats the Witch with formal courtesy; she cannot bear it and pushes him away (obs. 047, 048).
- She misses Monica Norton's ordinary days, knowing they are a fiction (obs. 049).
- Lana offers her a future, which she cannot accept because she will leave when Felix graduates (obs. 123, 124).

Her concealment is pressed from three sides, as she counts them herself (obs. 095):

- **Felix** searches the school for a small girl with an injured left hand (obs. 079, 080).
- **The Kerbeck inquirer** tests the cover story (obs. 008); the reader learns from the prologue that a young lady hired him (obs. 002), but Monica suspects Felix (obs. 070).
- **Hubert Dee** knows who she is and bargains with it (obs. 111, 133, 134).

The left hand is the volume's sign of exposure. It hurts when the Witch lifts a cat before Cyril (obs. 032), when she flexes it to deny Felix's description (obs. 082), and when she clenches her fists (S20:E000088). Cyril watches it twice (obs. 032, 083), and before he faints he hears Hubert address the small figure in the fog as the Silent Witch (obs. 152). The volume records what Cyril saw and heard; it does not show him drawing a conclusion.

### 6.3 Felix: concern, search and the order to kill

Felix is shown in three registers.

**Toward the Witch.** He greets her with delight, worries over her hand and longs to discuss her formulas (S14:E000171–E000174; obs. 131). He also searches for her methodically: he has had the staff checked and commissioned a report (obs. 079, 131).

**Toward Monica.** He feeds her cake, offers to deal with Robert, and says he wants her to ask him for help (obs. 114). Carrying her to the infirmary, he recognizes that he overlays a childhood friend on her, and in private speech uses 「僕」 (S22:E000138), where he usually says 私 (obs. 167).

**As a strategist.** Ray reports that Felix asked whether a living thing can be controlled by curse (obs. 023). At the duel Felix had already planned to dispose of Hubert through his spirit if the challengers lost, and then orders it outright, to look like an accident (obs. 147, 149). After Wildianu's report he judges that Emmanuel may be useful, and Wildianu observes that his master takes the best move even when others could not accept it (S23:E000568–E000573). He leads the Sages to Cyril and Glen by showing his uniform and stays hidden (obs. 197).

Clockford tells the Witch that Felix moves at his will (obs. 063). Felix's own scenes show him knowing nothing of the offer (obs. 066), acting alone, and choosing ruthless means. The volume lays these side by side without reconciling them. Monica's fear of him rests on her inference that he is Clockford's puppet (obs. 026, 066); Albert's distrust rests on a feeling that Felix regards his family as strangers (obs. 108). Glen's view that Felix is plainly good stands against both (obs. 116).

### 6.4 Clockford and the father's case

The inquiry into Venedict Rain's death advances through three reports, each partial:

- Ray: the traitor's last tool was a failed control curse; Felix asked about control by curse; Clockford obstructed the inquiry for a time (obs. 021–024).
- Clockford's own words: he would place the Silent Witch beside Felix and lead the Sages through a post the noble assembly might create (obs. 061, 063).
- Bartholomeus: Sams was in Clockford's employ, doing unknown work, from about eight years ago, shortly before Venedict's execution seven years ago (obs. 204, 205).

From these Monica infers that Clockford is likely connected to her father's death, and Felix possibly also (S26:E000232–E000233). The text marks this as her inference. It also marks its limit: she cannot see why a scholar outside politics should be killed, and asks whether his research or the black chalice made him inconvenient (obs. 206). Monica and Ray judge the farce theory too weak to charge (obs. 027). The volume does not confirm that Clockford staged the dragon, that he is the patron Sams called 閣下, or that he is the great noble Hilda would not name; the reading holds all three as unidentified (section 8.2).

### 6.5 The Seven Sages as an institution

V06 shows the full company of Sages for the first time. The offering, the seating by seniority, the factions and the banquet set out the institution (obs. 012–016). Its politics are explicit:

- Louis is for the first prince, Emmanuel for the second, the rest nearly neutral (obs. 015).
- A movement in the noble assembly would put the Sages under it, and Clockford holds the assembly's strongest voice (obs. 051; S24:E000103).
- Clockford can ask a Sage but not command one (obs. 062).

Mary's private warning that the Sages' star is darkening (obs. 050) is borne out within the volume. Emmanuel's private research uses captured spirits to power tools (obs. 193, 196), and his workshop sells his workers' tools under his name (obs. 194). Mary suspects him of holding the lost whistle Galanis, which brings war (obs. 180, 188).

The Sages answer by concealment. The affair must be hidden because a scandal would let the assembly seize them (obs. 187). Louis would rather Emmanuel vanished, but Mary wants it kept "gentle" (S24:E000124–E000127). The volume shows the Sages acting together outside office: without staves, as a passing gardener and a passing poet (obs. 201). Monica sees no justice on either side (obs. 190). The reading records that the volume presents the cover-up as institutional self-protection, named as such by Louis (S24:E000111), rather than as justice.

### 6.6 Hubert Dee and false chantless magic

Hubert is introduced by name in Bernie's letter (obs. 073) but enters unnamed. A red-haired passenger with fine rings rides to Serendia humming (obs. 068), a red-haired senior hunts Glen in a dream of Minerva (obs. 088, 089), and a red-haired transfer calls Glen a loser (obs. 094). Narration links the name to the figure only when Elliott names the transfer who flattened the club (obs. 125), and the plate caption confirms it (S18:E000605).

His magic is staged as an imitation of Monica's. He fires arrows while humming, and three observers in turn take it for chantless magic: Byron, Glen and Felix (obs. 118, 145, 146). Cyril notices the missing rings (obs. 151). Monica explains the trick: tools planted before the start, fired from his earring, mixed with ordinary spells (obs. 160). Her contempt, 「無詠唱魔術なんかを」 (S22:E000023; obs. 162), matches her earlier view that chantless casting only saves time (obs. 103).

The bilingual plate S12 states, before the narrative, that her accuracy is not a human act (obs. 011). Chapter eight gives the words to Hubert, remembering their first battle (obs. 156). His question whether the club's champions are "monsters" (obs. 119) thereby gains its referent: the "monster" he means is Monica. Monica's own view of the battle is the opposite of his. She cannot see why it is enjoyable and prefers an afternoon with friends (obs. 164).

### 6.7 Glen, Cyril and the spirits

**Glen.** His past is opened. At eleven Mary's prophecy took him from the family shop to Minerva at the state's cost (obs. 086, 087). Mocked there, he practised in secret, was drawn into a night battle by the red-haired senior, and lost consciousness after pouring his mana into a broken formula. What the fireball caused is withheld (obs. 088–090). He hides his curse pain to spare Eliane and his friends (obs. 091), studies to fight, and has someone he will not lose to (obs. 101). He keeps his vow from V05 in the duel, hitting Hubert with a formula learned for it (obs. 143, 144).

**Cyril.** Shown first at the castle as a nervous heir (obs. 018), he praises Raul's research (obs. 019) and doubts his own fitness beside Claudia (S13:E000689–E000690). At school he is protective of Monica (obs. 127), and in the duel he directs Glen's attack (S19:E000497–E000505). In the cave he takes a senior's duty to bring Glen back (S23:E000473). He sings for the small spirits, thanks the ice spirit, and insists on visiting the "piper" at a polite hour (obs. 182, 192; S24:E000451). The song is paraphrased here: a bird is asked to keep autumn's last trace through the winter of the three winter spirits until spring.

**The spirits.** The forest's spirits are victims. They are bound by the whistle, used up as power inside armour, and hunted by spirits made to kill (obs. 172, 193, 199). The ice spirit is courteous to the humans it took (obs. 168, 181). Sezdio calls the trouble humanity's own doing (S23:E000453). Monica separates and seals the armour spirits instead of destroying them (obs. 203). Lynn, under the whistle, closes the book attacking her (obs. 209).

## 7. Character, directional relationship, world and formal development

### 7.1 Monica Everett

Monica spends the volume between her two selves. Her developments:

- She names her anger and uses it, against Clockford and against Hubert (obs. 064, 154, 155).
- She meets Cyril as the Witch and cannot bear his courtesy (obs. 048).
- She refuses Felix's offer of help and cannot call for it when carried off (obs. 115, 130).
- She advances her father's inquiry to Clockford's door by inference (obs. 058, 206).
- She invents a high homing formula, rewrites Hubert's tools and frees the armour spirits without harming them (obs. 159, 160, 203).

Her friends shelter her without questions (obs. 137, 138), and Lana offers her a future she cannot take (obs. 124). Her left hand still hurts and weakens her grip (obs. 028; S25:E000013). Ledger: SW-S-0121 to SW-S-0125.

### 7.2 Felix Arc Ridill

Felix searches for the Witch by report and by asking the council (obs. 079, 131). He speaks of her as a benefactor he must meet (obs. 080). Toward Monica he is attentive and wants to be relied on (obs. 114). He knows nothing of his grandfather's offer (obs. 066). His own methods are hard: he planned and then ordered Hubert's death, and he means to use Emmanuel (obs. 147, 149, 183). He rides to Kellylinden alone and guides the Sages to his students unseen (obs. 197). A memory of a weeping friend who called him Ike returns as he carries Monica (obs. 165–167). Ledger: SW-S-0126 to SW-S-0128.

### 7.3 Glen, Cyril, Ray and Raul

**Glen.** His Minerva past is told up to the runaway, its result withheld (obs. 086–090). He hides the curse's pain, takes Monica's advice and hits Hubert with homing fire (obs. 091, 104, 144).

**Cyril.** He is presented at court as heir and doubts himself (obs. 018; S13:E000689–E000690). At school he is a protective senior (obs. 127, 139). He watches Monica's hand and hears the Witch named before he faints (obs. 083, 152). In the cave he thanks the spirits with a song and a promised offering (obs. 182, 192).

**Ray.** He carries the inquiry forward and shields Monica from Felix with a harmless curse (obs. 021–024, 046).

**Raul.** He appears for the first time: a gardener-scientist with the kingdom's largest mana, a listener through flowers, and a man who wants friends (obs. 019, 038, 039). Ledger: SW-S-0129 to SW-S-0132.

### 7.4 Others

- **Hubert Dee** hunts strong prey, imitates chantless magic with tools, bargains with Monica's secret and loses to her (obs. 118, 133, 142, 160).
- **Emmanuel Darwin** sells his workers' tools as his own, powers armour with spirits, holds the Galanis whistle and serves an unnamed patron who wants war (obs. 194, 196, 210, 211).
- **Duke Clockford** offers the Witch office and tells her Felix moves at his will; Sams once worked for him (obs. 061, 063, 204).
- **Isabelle** runs the Kerbeck performance and threatens Hubert (obs. 005, 175).
- **Albert and Patrick** plot against Felix and end as friends of Glen and Monica (obs. 107, 117).
- **Lionel, Robert, Lana, Bartholomeus, MacLaggan, Carla, Mary and Louis** are recorded thinly (obs. 034, 098, 124, 207, 174, 215, 050, 185).

Ledger: SW-S-0133 to SW-S-0138.

## 8. Alternative readings, absence and contradiction

### 8.1 "The old man in the workshop is Emmanuel"

Chapter eight shows an unnamed old man in a workshop north-east of the school, holding Lynn and the whistle (obs. 171–173). Louis and Mary connect the forest with Emmanuel by ownership and suspicion (obs. 169, 178, 180). The secret episode then places Emmanuel in the house by the spring with the whistle at his neck (S28:E000009; S28:E000025). The reading therefore records the identification as made by the narration itself, at the secret episode, and not before. The patron he calls 「あの方」 (S28:E000042) is a separate question (section 8.2).

### 8.2 "Clockford is the patron who wants war, and 閣下, and the great noble"

Several details point toward the duke:

- Emmanuel is his ally and manages his tool collection (obs. 015, 183).
- Felix in V05 believed Clockford wanted the garrison as a supply base for war with the Empire (V05 obs. 070).
- Sams worked for him (obs. 204).
- The card says he aims at war with the Empire (section 7.5).

Against identification:

- No line of narrative names the patron who wants war (obs. 211).
- The card is paratext.
- Monica's own conclusion is marked as likelihood (S26:E000232).
- The text keeps 閣下, the great noble and the patron as separate references.

The reading registers no identification (SW-C-0039, SW-C-0053, SW-P-0038, SW-P-0041).

### 8.3 Normative evaluation: the Sages' cover-up

Criterion: whether the volume weighs institutional self-protection against accountability. It names the motive plainly: Louis hides Emmanuel's crime because a scandal would let the noble assembly take the Sages' independence (obs. 187). Mary wants gentleness; Louis would prefer Emmanuel gone (S24:E000124–E000127). Monica judges that neither side has justice (obs. 190). Against this the volume sets the harms: captured spirits used up as power (obs. 193), workers' tools sold under a master's name (obs. 194), and Cyril's defective brooch (obs. 195). The reading records that the volume shows the cover-up's cost and its reason, and does not resolve them; the affair is unfinished at the volume's end.

### 8.4 Normative evaluation: Felix's order

Criterion: whether lethal means in a student matter are registered as wrong. Felix orders Hubert killed "as an accident" after having planned it (obs. 147, 149). Wildianu complies, hesitantly (S19:E000630). The narration through Wildianu describes a master who chooses the best move whatever others would accept (S23:E000568–E000573). The order is overtaken by events; Wildianu follows the spirits instead. No character learns of it (SW-K-0087). The volume records the act without punishment or explicit judgement, beside Felix's care for Monica in the same chapter.

### 8.5 Meaningful absences

- Nero sleeps through the whole volume.
- Lynn appears only as a captive and an attacker.
- Bridget's detective is never connected by Monica to Bridget; the reader knows it from V05 and the cards.
- The result of Glen's runaway at Minerva is withheld.
- Emmanuel's "secret weapon" and the whistle's ambition are withheld (S28:E000034; obs. 212).
- The rumour Louis raises about Emmanuel is unstated in the banquet scene (obs. 052).
- Mary's jewel box speaks of a beloved nearby without saying whom it means (obs. 213).
- The friend in Felix's memory is unnamed (obs. 165).

### 8.6 The afterword, the jacket and the cards

The afterword says that every Sage appears in V06 and apologizes for the classmates' ordeal (S39:E000012–E000016). It announces that V07 will appear about the following winter, preceded by an upper volume of a two-part side story about Louis at Minerva (S39:E000022–E000024). That is a placement lead for the companion -another-; under the owner's instruction it is admitted only after V12. The jacket blurb frames the volume as a sudden climax for the mission (obs. 218). The cards are listed in section 7.5. All of these are paratext.

### 8.7 Apparent contradictions

- Monica thinks only she and Ray know the dragon was man-made (obs. 060), though Raul overheard them (obs. 038). This is a character's belief, not a narrative contradiction.
- Hubert praises Cyril's brooch as made by Emmanuel with first-rate protection (obs. 128); Monica recalls that the brooch bearing Emmanuel's mark lacked a protection formula (obs. 195). The text does not say whether the brooch Hubert examined is the one Monica remembers. Recorded, not resolved.
- One block spells Hubert's name 「ヒュバード」 (S24:E000465), a source misprint.
