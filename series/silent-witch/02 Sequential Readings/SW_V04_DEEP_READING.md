## 2. Prior-volume developments

*Prepared 2026-10-14 before any V04 narrative member was rendered for reading, from the closed V01 to V03 readings, their exit freezes and the master ledger only.*

Monica Everett, the Silent Witch, is about two months into a secret mission at Serendia Academy, where as Monica Norton, a second-year student and the council’s treasurer, she guards the second prince, Felix Arc Ridill, without his knowledge, on the order Louis Miller, the Barrier Mage, pressed on her. Isabelle Norton covers her by playing her tormentor. Nero, her familiar, helps her; Louis has lent her his wind spirit, Lynn. The school festival is next, and Duke Clockford, Felix’s grandfather, has overruled its cancellation because it is to be Felix’s public debut.

Her past is now largely disclosed. Her father, a learned man, was executed as a heretic for forbidden research and his writings burned; his book on heredity and magical properties, by Venedict Rain, was burned with him five years ago, and Monica now owns a surviving copy, bought for her by Felix in a Coalrapton bookshop whose owner, Porter, was her father’s friend and writes novels as Dustin Günther. Her uncle then beat her into silence, and the scars remain. She arrived at the mage academy Minerva having gone so long without speaking that words would not come. There Bernie Jones befriended and helped her while wanting her dependent; when his plan to shorten her chant ended in her casting with no chant at all, he turned away in envy, and when she became a Sage at fifteen he told her to shut herself up alone in a mountain hut, which she did. Professor Rutherford sent her to the selection; an adoptive mother, unnamed in the narrative, took her in after her father.

In V03 Monica met Bernie again at an inter-school chess tournament, renounced his approval after defending her friend Lana, and beat him. An imperial agent impersonating the Minerva teacher Pittman, using body-altering magic that only the Empire permits, attacked Bernie; Monica defeated him with a single silent shot. Bernie then hid her identity and her mission, which he now knows, demanding that she be grateful to him for life. The agent, Yuan, later escaped by a staged suicide with the real Pittman’s body, and with his pupil Heidi has commissioned a forged Serendia uniform from the craftsman Bartholomeus Baal, apparently to infiltrate the festival and expose Felix’s secret and Monica’s. Louis suspects that Randall and the Empire are allied behind the device Casey Grove used in V02; Casey, now at a northern convent, has sent Monica a package through Louis.

On the night of the Coalrapton bell-ringing festival Monica, meant to be guarding Felix, spent the night with him as his fellow “ghost”, a persona he called Ike: he showed her a self he says will not survive his becoming king, admitted that he studies magic in secret because it is forbidden to him, called his admiration for the Silent Witch’s magic first love, mourned an unnamed friend whose wish he means to fulfil, and in the morning gave her a peridot pendant, saying he had briefly suspected her of being an assassin and asking her to remember Ike. Monica, using riding technique he taught her, flew for the first time to recover an ancient tool, 〈星紡ぎのミラ〉, which the Star-reading Witch Mary Harvey had let be stolen, she said, by the stars’ guidance, and which Monica subdued with a dream. Felix has not connected Monica with the Silent Witch. He holds a ten-year vow to inscribe the name Felix Arc Ridill in history, and a large cluster of unexplained details surrounds his childhood, his name and a page boy in his dream; no explanation is registered. Mary sees beside Monica’s star someone’s star with a fate of loss.

## 3. Current-volume story synopsis

The prologue returns to Monica’s childhood. After her father, Venedict Rain, was executed for forbidden research when she was ten, her paternal uncle took her in, beat her and starved her whenever she defended her father, and shut her in a winter barn. She fled into formulas until she could no longer recognize people as people or understand speech, answering only in numbers, and was near death when a woman continued the number series she was reciting and called her by name: Hilda Everett, her father’s former assistant, a researcher at the Royal Magic Research Institute. Hilda took her in and adopted her. Within months Monica could speak again. When she designed a compound spell to bake Hilda’s cookies evenly, Hilda proposed that she sit the Minerva entrance examination.

Four days before the school festival, Monica negotiates with the magic-history club over its exhibit. She is won over by its research, and so is Cyril; Felix arrives, refuses the club’s requests, and calls in Neil, whose family is known for mediation, to find a solution. Claudia’s memory shows how she met Neil at twelve and decided to marry him, which led her father to adopt Cyril as his heir. Cyril asks Claudia to lend Monica a dress for the ball, and learns that she is borrowing one from Lana.

On the eve of the festival Isabelle plans the guard with Monica: her mother and servants will watch the middle-school building, and a touch of the left ear will summon help. At dawn Nero finds a purple-haired man cursing the school from the woods: Ray Albright, the Abyss Curse Mage, a fellow Sage, who recognizes Monica and learns of her mission. He has come to recover a curse tool, a necklace that steals the wearer’s composure, which a traitor stole from his family ten years ago and which has found its way into Serendia, but the whiteness of the uniforms has broken his nerve. Bridget, watching through opera glasses, sees Monica flying back to the attic on a broom and decides it is not yet time to act; she has endured for ten years, she thinks, for the prince. Cyril takes a white rose from the gardening club.

At the festival Felix resolves to make the name Felix Arc Ridill remembered. Robert Winkel renews his proposal; Monica refuses without calling for help, and Cyril sends Robert off and gives her the white rose, tied with his own blue ribbon, telling her it is a charm against embarrassment. Bernie keeps her away from Professor Rutherford, her Minerva mentor, and tells her that he is leaving Minerva to become his family’s heir after his brother’s death, that he had given up being a Sage long ago because of her, that he never wanted to be her friend but her equal rival, and that she may rely on him in future.

Louis arrives in full Sage’s robe, unworried that Ray knows, and goes to keep Rutherford, his own master, away from Monica. Monica works out that the curse tool is the queen’s necklace in the festival play. Isabelle, playing the villainess, helps her swap it while Monica cuts its chain with a silent wind; Isabelle’s mother and younger brother then rescue her from Rutherford. Ray absorbs the curse into his own marks, and Monica asks him to stay and enjoy the festival. Louis tells Rutherford that his disciple attends the school; privately he calls Monica a creepy, inhuman girl who sees people as numbers, and a true genius.

The play’s lead breaks his arm; the actress playing the queen, Eliane Hyatt, Felix’s second cousin, still under a fragment of the curse, had sabotaged his ladder. Felix proposes Glen as the replacement. In the second act Eliane casts a real wind spell at the stage powder to humiliate Glen; Monica, seated beside Felix, shields Glen and smothers the fire, Glen flies Eliane out of the collapsing set, and Louis’s barrier contains the debris. The audience takes it all for staging. Felix watches the crowd Glen has delighted and says that people like him are called heroes. Louis then seizes Glen for flying without permission: Glen is Louis’s disciple, sent to the school as a decoy for Monica without knowing it. Monica has not told Louis what Felix confided to her as Ike.

Elliott and Benjamin tell Monica that Cyril is an adopted son of a man without a title, and Elliott explains that flower ornaments are given in the giver’s colours as an invitation to dance; Monica concludes that wearing Cyril’s colours lets her borrow his composure. A shy woman looking for Cyril asks Monica whether he bullies quiet girls; Monica tells her he is kind and shows her the rose. The woman is Cyril’s mother, Myra Wayne, who had kept her distance from the son who reminded her of her abusive husband; she leaves resolved to invite him home for the winter.

At sunset a perfect copy of Cyril approaches Felix. Nero stops him, and Monica unmasks him by his tie, his brooch and his manner of address: it is the agent Yuan, by imperial body magic. She cages him, eats his partner Heidi’s lightning spell with a web of matching lightning, and freezes his dragon form, telling him she can attack someone with Cyril’s face by thinking of him as a block of numbers. Louis arrives and is drawn away by a dummy 螺炎. Yuan then threatens to have the real Cyril killed; Monica, who can calculate that it is a bluff, cannot stop herself fearing it, and Heidi poisons her. Yuan reveals two aims: the second prince, already achieved by seeing at close range a trace that he calls the work of the traitor Arthur, and the capture of the Silent Witch. Nero, in human form and half losing his shape in rage, rescues her; Ray curses Yuan with the pain of his worst memory; Heidi flies him away. Monica weeps that she could no longer turn the people of the school into numbers. Louis takes the blame for the plan and gives her Casey’s package, a handkerchief embroidered with a yellow flower.

Monica goes to the ball in Lana’s green dress because she will not be there next year. Glen tells admirers that his type is a capable, older woman like the queen Amelia; Felix dances with Eliane while wishing he could have given Monica a rose; Neil gives Claudia his flower at last. Monica collapses after making ice for the kitchen, and Cyril, carrying her, notices the magical ice and her small, pen-callused hand. He commends her for volunteering, tells her she will be Neil’s right hand next year, and dances with her alone. She leaves her head free of numbers so as to remember the dance.

In the epilogue Felix climbs a tree to look at the stars, which he says a friend taught him to love, dances with Monica outside, says he is jealous that she did not wear his pendant, gives her his friend’s wish to find something she loves, and says that he has a wish he will pursue even at the cost of breaking that promise. Pointing at the constellation of the hero Ralph, whom the queen made into stars so that he would not be forgotten, he asks whether it would not be happiness to leave such a light after death. Monica feels a quiet obsession in him. From a balcony, Bridget resolves to take back her prince.

The secret episode shows Louis on the hall roof forming a hypothesis from Yuan’s words that he will not share with Monica, and the traitor curse-caster, hidden in the Duke of Leinbourg’s carriage, fearing that the master he calls 閣下 will abandon him as he abandoned Thornley unless he completes a puppet curse, and cursing the memory of Venedict Rain.

## 6. Sustained diagnostic close reading

V03 ended with Louis telling Casey that Monica is a heartless witch who sees people as numbers. V04 prints that verdict on its opening plate (S12:E000004; obs. 124), has Louis repeat it to his own teacher (obs. 050), has the antagonist Yuan say it to her face, and has Monica agree with it, and then disproves it in the one way that matters to the plot: she fails. The volume’s argument, stated in advance, is that Monica’s numerical vision, which began as a child’s escape from her uncle, has become both her weapon and her limit, and that the people of the school have made it impossible for her to use it on them. Around that argument the book arranges a festival of misunderstood gifts. A flower she reads as a charm, a necklace that carries a curse, a pendant she will not wear, a handkerchief that keeps a promise, and a friend’s wish that Felix gives away all turn on what an object means to its giver and to its receiver. Formally the volume opens and closes outside Monica: a childhood prologue in a narrator’s voice, and a secret episode in the minds of Louis and of a traitor who knew her father.

Observation numbers in the form “obs. 007” point to the register in section 6.13.

### 6.1 The girl who forgot language

The prologue answers, in a sober narrative voice, what V02 delivered in delirium. After her father was executed when she was ten, her uncle beat her and starved her whenever she defended him (S11:E000035–E000039; obs. 002). She survived by repeating her father’s formulas, a perfect world that does not hurt (S11:E000055–E000057; obs. 003), until the defence consumed its owner: she could no longer see people as people, only measurements, or understand speech, and answered in computed numbers (S11:E000067–E000083; obs. 004). The uncle’s word for this, 『数字ばかり口にして、気持ち悪い！』 (S11:E000084), returns almost verbatim in Louis’s judgment of her as 「気持ちの悪い小娘」 in chapter 4 (S16:E000975; obs. 050). The volume lets the reader hear the uncle in the colleague.

The refrain 「──世界は数字でできている。」 is placed here at its origin: it was her father’s saying, and she clung to it to look away from reality (S11:E000098–E000100; obs. 005). Rescue comes through the same series of numbers. A woman continues the Uncle Sam’s pigs series that Monica is reciting, names it as Rain’s formula, and calls her by name (S11:E000120–E000142; obs. 006). Hilda Everett, her father’s former assistant, is the first adult in the series to honour him in Monica’s presence (S11:E000153–E000172; obs. 007). The prologue’s comic coda, a cookie-baking compound spell, gives the volume its first and last word on what numbers can be when they are not a refuge: a gift to someone at home (S11:E000192–E000221; obs. 008). Monica reuses the same spell, coldly, against Yuan in chapter 8 (S22:E000048; obs. 087).

The prologue also unsettles a fact the ledger had recorded. V03 said that Venedict Rain’s book was published five years ago and burned at his execution; V04 says he was executed when Monica was ten (S11:E000015; obs. 001). Section 8.8 records the tension, and section 9.1 corrects the ledger’s V03 wording, which had stated the execution itself as five years past.

### 6.2 Help refused, help accepted

Chapter 2 gives Monica an ally’s signal, a touch of the left ear (S14:E000099; obs. 019), and the book then tests when she will use it. Against Robert she will not: a proposal is her own problem, not the mission’s, and she forces out a refusal herself (S15:E000158–E000166; obs. 031). Before the dressing room she will not either, because the curse tool is the Albrights’ secret, and she stands paralysed, able to resolve but not to act (S16:E000247–E000265; obs. 042). Isabelle, who saw her lower the hand, comes anyway, and Monica vows never to forget the hands held out to her (S16:E000269–E000313; obs. 043). In the kitchen she refuses to ask for help with a tub of ice for fear of being a burden after her failure, and collapses (S25:E000067–E000111; obs. 107). Cyril’s verdict makes the lesson explicit: report illness early, rely on others (S25:E000272; obs. 109).

The pattern bears on SW-P-0012, whether her speech gains generalize. V04 shows Monica initiating speech to a stranger who looks lost, the clearest instance yet (S19:E000310–E000327; obs. 073), while still unable to ask anyone for anything for herself.

### 6.3 A charm, a flower, a reservation

Cyril’s white rose is the volume’s central misunderstanding and its kindest joke. He removes his own ribbon tie, pockets the brooch he cannot live without, ties the ribbon to the stem and calls the flower a charm so that she will not be embarrassed (S15:E000215–E000252; obs. 032). Monica, who does not know that a flower in the giver’s colours is a request for the first dance (S13:E001040–E001042; obs. 018), takes it as a folk charm, and when Elliott explains the colours she concludes that wearing Cyril’s colours lets her borrow his composure (S19:E000159–E000193; obs. 071). The charm works on her terms: she speaks to a stranger relying on it (S19:E000322), takes the kitchen post relying on it (S24:E000509–E000516), and thanks Cyril for it in the epilogue (S26:E000026; obs. 111).

Every other reader of the rose reads it correctly. Lana smiles knowingly and says Cyril will be pleased (S24:E000057; obs. 099); Felix notes it, then privately calls Cyril unfair for using the word charm (S20:E000099; S24:E000629; obs. 081, 105); Myra Wayne hears of it and recognizes the son who brought her flowers (S19:E000698–E000699). Cyril himself never names his motive; the narration gives him only a tightness in the chest that he explains away as concern for the council’s dignity (S14:E000783–E000795; obs. 028). The reading records the rose as an unspoken invitation that Monica receives as protection, and records no romantic interest on Monica’s side.

### 6.4 Bernie, rival

V03 left Bernie’s protection of Monica open between repentance and possession. Chapter 3 gives his own account. He helped a Sage as a Ridill noble’s duty, not as her friend (S15:E000428–E000433; obs. 035). He is leaving Minerva because his elder brother died, and is relieved, because he had long since given up hope of becoming a Sage (S15:E000461–E000504; obs. 036). It was her fault, he says, and then the sentence that recasts V03’s prologue: he never wanted to be her friend, 「僕は対等なライバルになりたかったんだ」 (S15:E000537–E000544; obs. 037). He will be a count of her rank and invites her to rely on him (S15:E000550–E000556). Monica answers as the Silent Witch, with a formal thanks, and he smiles as he used to when she cried for his help (S15:E000570–E000576; obs. 038). The reading takes his stated motive as given and records the friendship as ended and a relation of rivals and future colleagues as begun.

### 6.5 Composure and responsibility: Eliane

The curse tool steals composure (S14:E000572; obs. 025), and its first victim is Eliane Hyatt, Felix’s second cousin and the duke’s preferred candidate as his bride (S20:E000068). The narration gives her a careful double account. While she wears the necklace, her irritation is accompanied by reddened vision and pressure in her head (S16:E000409; obs. 044); after its removal, a fragment of the curse still surfaces as red in her eyes when she sabotages a ladder (S17:E000272–E000273; obs. 054) and when she attacks Glen on stage (S18:E000307). Then the narrator withdraws the excuse: the fragment had dissolved by the time Glen rescued her, and her fury at his refusal of her stage kiss is her own (S18:E000639–E000657; obs. 063). In between, she sees that she could have killed someone (S18:E000556–E000557). Section 8.3 evaluates this structure.

### 6.6 Heroes, memory and stars

Felix’s vow, stated in V03 as inscribing his name in history, recurs in V04 in a sequence that moves from ambition toward something darker. At the festival’s opening he resolves to answer “閣下’s” expectations and to make the name Felix Arc Ridill remain in people’s memory (S15:E000043–E000045; obs. 030). After the play he watches the crowd Glen has delighted, his face emptied and his eyes moving mechanically, and says that people like Glen are called heroes, fit to remain in people’s memory (S18:E000685–E000710; obs. 064). In the epilogue he points to the constellation of Ralph, the founding king who feared being forgotten and whom the queen had the dark spirit king set among the stars, and asks Monica whether it would not be happiness to leave such a light even after death (S26:E000322–E000343; obs. 117). Monica feels her neck prickle and names what she sees: 「静かな妄執」 (S26:E000357).

The sequence is framed by two gifts of language. Felix gives Monica his friend’s words, to find something to love for her own sake, because he has little freedom left, and says he has a wish he will pursue even at the cost of breaking that promise (S26:E000290–E000312; obs. 116). The epilogue’s title, the happiness of the hero who became a star, takes his last question as its theme. The reading records three facts without interpreting their union: the wish is to be remembered; it is expressed as a hero’s posthumous light; and the speaker says it overrides a dead friend’s wish for his happiness. The character card adds, as paratext, that his perfection exists only to leave his name in history like the star-hero (S31:E000004; obs. 125).

### 6.7 Numbers fail

Chapter 8 stages the verdict of the plate and then breaks it. Faced with Yuan wearing Cyril’s face and voice and asking whether she can attack him, Monica answers 「できます」 and 「あなたを、数字の塊だと思えばいい」 (S20:E000370–E000372; obs. 085), and the narration follows her into the number world where she can be merciless (S20:E000373–E000375). What follows is the most technical display of power in the series so far: from the last line of Heidi’s chant she identifies an imperial school of mana sequencing and spins a web of matching lightning that eats the attack (S20:E000432–E000480; obs. 086), then chills Yuan’s dragon form in a sealed barrier, remarking that the same barrier with a micro-flame bakes cookies (S22:E000048–E000053; obs. 087). Yuan names her: she removes lives like pieces from a board (S22:E000072), and she agrees that she is a heartless witch who sees people as numbers, and resolves to use it to protect those dear to her (S22:E000085–E000090; obs. 088).

The plot then exposes the agreement as half false. When Yuan threatens the real Cyril, Monica computes that it is a bluff and still cannot act, because the number world will not hold Cyril (S22:E000233–E000253; obs. 090). Heidi poisons her. In chapter 9 Monica tells Louis what happened in her own words: thinking only of numbers had been easy, but with Cyril’s life as a shield she could not calculate anything (S23:E000475; obs. 096). She concludes that her father’s saying must no longer be an excuse or an escape (S23:E000576–E000577; obs. 097). Louis, who has said twice in this volume that he does not care about her humanity, answers that the failure was his plan’s, not hers, and points out that she had not treated Ray as a number, which is why Ray was there to save her (S23:E000483–E000501). The scene does not contradict Louis’s verdict about the past; it records its expiry.

### 6.8 Cyril and his mother

The chapter titled for Cyril’s invitations is the volume’s parallel to the prologue. Cyril’s father, a man of Ashley blood without a title, berated and struck his wife and died of drink; his mother, Myra Wayne, feared that her clever son would become his father and withheld the praise he worked for, and when the marquis offered to adopt him told him that he was a noble’s child after all (S19:E000630–E000673; obs. 077). Like Monica, Cyril grew up with a violent man and an absent approval, and like her he responded with effort. The difference is the instrument: Cyril’s letters to his mother, revised many times, invite her every year (S19:E000583–E000604; obs. 076).

Monica is the hinge. Searching for her son, Myra asks whether Cyril bullies quiet girls, and Monica, who cannot ask for help for herself, testifies fluently for him and shows her the rose (S19:E000403–E000429; obs. 074). Myra decides to write and invite him home (S19:E000686–E000810; obs. 078). Cyril learns only that a woman with a mole came, and thanks Monica with a trembling voice (S19:E000530–E000534; obs. 075). The memory that closes the chapter, the child Cyril saying he would make a sweet drink for someone dear instead of striking them, ties the chocolate of V02 and the ice of chapter 11 to the same disposition (S19:E000797–E000807).

### 6.9 The cluster around Felix, extended again

SW-C-0022 records an unexplained cluster around Felix. V04 adds to it from four directions and, as before, explains none of it.

- **The intruders’ aim.** Yuan did not try to kill Felix. Disguised as Cyril he reached for Felix’s hair (S20:E000144) and later says he confirmed at close range a trace, 「裏切り者のアルトゥールの仕事よ」, and that “that person’s” reading was right (S23:E000049; obs. 091). Louis notes that Arthur is a common imperial name (S28:E000089).
- **Louis’s hypothesis.** From those words Louis forms an outlandish hypothesis that would fit Mary’s inability to read Felix’s fate, wonders whether the king suspected it and sent him for that reason, and expects that if it is true the duke will fall together with the second prince; he decides to keep it from Monica (S28:E000091–E000137; obs. 120). The text does not state the hypothesis.
- **The stars and the friend.** Asked whether he loves the stars, Felix says no: a friend who knew them told him so much that he learned them (S26:E000137–E000139; obs. 113). The child in Elliott’s V03 memory and the boy in Felix’s V03 dream loved astronomy.
- **Bridget.** Watching Monica fly, Bridget thinks that she has endured for ten years and pictures 「（……殿下）」 (S14:E000686–E000695; obs. 027); at the ball she resolves 「……返してもらうわ。あたくしの殿下を」 (S26:E000396; obs. 118). Ten years is the span of Felix’s vow and of Mary’s blindness.
- **Elliott.** The sesame-marked 「あいつ」 recurs among the odd people Elliott cannot reject (S19:E000230; obs. 072).

The secret episode adds a second thread that may or may not touch the first. A traitor curse-caster, who stole tools from the Albrights ten years ago, rides hidden in the Duke of Leinbourg’s carriage; he relies on a master he calls 閣下 to protect him, notes that the same master discarded Thornley, whose approach was through mind interference, and must complete a puppet curse before he too is discarded (S28:E000212–E000231; obs. 122). He knew Venedict Rain and hates the memory of his catchphrase (S28:E000265–E000277; obs. 123). Felix calls the duke 閣下 in this volume (S15:E000043), and the traitor’s 閣下 is unnamed. The reading records the words, registers no identification, and keeps SW-C-0022 and the new questions in section 9.3 open.

### 6.10 Concealment in every direction

V04 is the volume in which almost every major character hides something from the person who matters most. Monica reports the night in Coalrapton to Louis but withholds what Ike told her, feeling that to report it would betray her first friend of the night (S18:E000898–E000902; obs. 067). Louis keeps from her both Glen’s role, until Glen exposes it himself (obs. 065), and his hypothesis about Felix, because she has grown too soft (S28:E000132–E000137). Felix knew from Glen’s enrolment that he was a Sage’s disciple and used him to manage Eliane (S20:E000048–E000054; obs. 079); with Louis he maintains a mutual pretence about the brooch (S18:E000865–E000875; obs. 066). Glen is told nothing, so that his friendship with Monica is the one bond in the book that rests on no secret (S18:E001006–E001017; obs. 068). Cyril calls his flower a charm. The secrets are not equivalent, but their accumulation makes the volume’s closing image, Felix and Monica looking at the same stars and seeing different things (S26:E000366), a picture of the whole cast.

### 6.11 The visual channel

The images continue to disclose ahead of the prose: the opening plate prints Louis’s chapter 4 verdict (S12:E000004; obs. 124), and the frontispieces print the broom flight and the dress before their chapters (obs. 128). Two name-framed plates introduce Ray and Eliane; the Eliane plate shows the dark-framed necklace on her (S16:E000349), so that the reader sees the curse tool before Monica identifies it. The spread of Monica casting her lightning web places Cyril’s rose on her uniform in the centre of her most merciless act (S21:E000004). The dance with Cyril (S25:E000346) and the stargazing with Felix (S26:E000366) are composed as a pair: one face to face, one back to back looking outward. The final image, Bridget alone at a balustrade, is a tall panel without a smile (S26:E000397).

### 6.12 Japanese speech and address

Four features carry arguments here.

First, address as identity. Monica unmasks the false Cyril because he calls her ノートン嬢 instead of her full name or ノートン会計 (S20:E000250), and she likes the office title because it makes her a member of the council (S20:E000264). Bernie and she end on formal titles, エヴァレット魔法伯 and his full name (S15:E000550; S15:E000570).

Second, Felix’s 僕 is now the voice in which he speaks to Monica privately as the prince, not only as Ike: in the tree scene, with the sesame mark on 僕 (S26:E000100; obs. 113), and in the jealousy over the pendant (S26:E000197). His 私 returns before the duke and in public denial of magic (S18:E000863).

Third, Monica’s speech failures persist as bitten syllables at moments of courage, 「お困りでひょうかっ」 and 「案内しまふっ」 (S19:E000327; S19:E000350), while her full declarations come in defence of others (S19:E000411–E000416).

Fourth, the sesame emphasis again marks the unexplained: Elliott’s 「あいつ」 (S19:E000230) and Felix’s 「僕」 (S26:E000100).

## 7. Character, directional relationship, world and formal development

### 7.1 Monica Everett

V04 completes the outline of her childhood: her father’s execution when she was ten, a year with an abusive uncle, a dissociation in which she lost speech and the ability to see people as people, rescue by her father’s former assistant Hilda Everett, adoption, recovered speech, and preparation for Minerva (obs. 001–008). Her present conduct develops along three lines. She uses numerical dissociation deliberately as a combat technique (obs. 085–087), then finds that she cannot apply it to the people of the school, and renounces her father’s saying as an excuse (obs. 090, 096, 097). She refuses help for her own problems and accepts it when offered (obs. 031, 042, 043, 107), and initiates speech to a stranger (obs. 073). And she chooses, knowing that it deceives her friends, to remain Monica Norton until the mission ends with Felix’s graduation (obs. 098, 110). She expects to return to the hut and does not wish to marry (obs. 115). Ledger: SW-S-0065 to SW-S-0069.

### 7.2 Felix Arc Ridill

Felix’s viewpoint now states an attachment to Monica: he would give a flower to her if he could, he resents Cyril’s charm, he feels his chest burn when she calls Cyril aside, and he is jealous that she did not wear his pendant (obs. 081, 082, 105, 114). His wish to be remembered moves toward a hero’s posthumous light, which overrides his dead friend’s wish (obs. 030, 064, 116, 117). He knew Glen’s status from the start and used him (obs. 079), denies studying magic to Louis (obs. 066), and is constrained to dance first with Eliane, the duke’s candidate (obs. 080). The cluster grows (section 6.9). Ledger: SW-S-0070 to SW-S-0071.

### 7.3 Cyril Ashley

New background: the son of an untitled man of Ashley blood who beat his wife and drank himself to death; his mother withheld praise in fear of his father’s pride; the marquis adopted him at thirteen because Claudia’s engagement to Neil required an heir; he learned magic to have a weapon of his own and over-absorbed (obs. 015, 070, 077, 078). In the present he gives Monica a white rose disguised as a charm, covers Neil’s duties so Neil can dance with Claudia, carries Monica when she collapses, notices the magical ice and her pen-callused hand, and dances with her alone (obs. 029, 032, 103, 106, 108–110). His mother has resolved to invite him home. Ledger: SW-S-0072 to SW-S-0073.

### 7.4 Bernie, Louis, Ray and the Sages

Bernie leaves Minerva as heir to the Amberd county, having wanted to be Monica’s rival, not her friend (obs. 035–038). Louis is Rutherford’s pupil, Glen’s master, the author of the decoy plan, and the speaker of the verdict on Monica; he takes the blame for the festival’s failure, gives her Casey’s handkerchief, and keeps from her a hypothesis about Felix (obs. 040, 049, 050, 065, 096, 097, 120). Ray Albright, the Abyss Curse Mage, is introduced: the only line of curse-casters, two hundred curses carved into his body, a craving to be loved, an aversion to bright places, and a curse that reproduces a victim’s worst pain (obs. 022–024, 047, 094, 095). Ledger: SW-S-0074 to SW-S-0076.

### 7.5 Friends, households and rivals

Isabelle’s mother Sylvia and brother Henry join the cover as a family of performers (obs. 046). Lana dresses Monica and reads the rose (obs. 099); her father, Baron Collette, remade the dress (obs. 052). ## 8. Alternative readings, absence and contradiction

### 8.1 “Monica is the heartless witch Louis describes”

The rival reading has more support in V04 than ever: the plate, Louis twice, Yuan, and Monica herself (obs. 050, 087, 088, 124). Against it, the volume’s plot turns on her inability to treat Cyril as a number (obs. 090, 096), and on a series of acts that are not computations: testifying for Cyril to his mother, inviting Ray to stay, choosing to help a stranger (obs. 048, 073, 074). The reading keeps SW-C-0033 and revises it: the numerical vision is real, began as a child’s defence, and is now a technique she can choose; what Louis calls heartlessness she can no longer apply to the people she knows.

### 8.2 “Cyril’s rose is only a charm”

Cyril calls it one and never says otherwise (obs. 032, 111), and his own viewpoint rationalizes his feeling as concern for the council (obs. 028). Against that, the custom is explained twice as an invitation in the giver’s colours (obs. 018, 071), he chose the flower for “that girl” after deliberating over colours (obs. 029), and four other characters read it as an invitation (obs. 081, 099, 105). The reading records the rose as an invitation he disguised, and records that the volume leaves his feeling unnamed. It records no romantic interest on Monica’s side.

### 8.3 Normative evaluation: Eliane and the curse

Criterion: whether responsibility for harm is assigned when a character acts under external influence. The narration assigns it with care: the curse amplifies aggression, its fragment surfaces at both of her attacks, and then the narrator states that her final anger is her own (obs. 044, 054, 063). She also sees for herself that she could have killed (S18:E000556–E000557). The volume does not punish her: Felix infers her guilt and says nothing, and the fire is publicly an accident (obs. 079). The reading records that the volume holds her morally responsible in the narration and lets her go unexamined in the world, an arrangement that repeats V02’s handling of Casey’s attempt.

### 8.4 Normative evaluation: Louis’s decoy and withheld hypothesis

Criterion: whether the costs to the deceived are registered. Glen was enrolled as a decoy without knowing it, and the narration calls Louis heartless for it through Monica (S18:E001003); his friendship with Monica is valued precisely because it rests on no secret (obs. 068). Louis withholds a hypothesis that, if true, would ruin Felix, and enjoys the prospect (obs. 120). The volume presents Louis comically and lets him take responsibility for the festival’s failure (obs. 096). The reading records both the care and the instrumental use, and leaves the justice of the withheld hypothesis to its later consequences.

### 8.5 Monica’s concealment from Louis

Monica keeps Felix’s confidences as Ike from the man who commissioned her mission (obs. 067). Read as loyalty to a friend, the choice is sympathetic; read as a breach of the mission, it is the first time she withholds operational information from Louis. The volume gives only her reason, that the confidences belong to Ike and not to the prince. The reading records the act and its stated reason.

### 8.6 The afterword, the advertisement and the jacket

The afterword says the web version’s festival arc of about eighty thousand characters became a whole volume with free additions, that V05 will be the winter-break arc, and that before it a newly written IV -after- will cover the time between the festival and the winter break, in October 2022 (S35:E000012–E000027; obs. 126). The back-matter advertisement gives its release date as 2022-10-07 (S36:E000004; obs. 127). These are paratextual placement data and are used in section 10 for the admission decision; they are not narrative evidence. The jacket copy, read after the narrative, summarizes the festival plot (S42:E000004).
