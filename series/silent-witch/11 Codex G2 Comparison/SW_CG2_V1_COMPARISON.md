# Silent Witch: what survives an achievement, and what passes between people

This is a postfreeze comparison of two completed analytical generations, not a revision of either. Its central finding is a difference of explanatory emphasis within extensive substantive agreement. V1 explains the series especially well through what people hold and pass on: names, methods, debts, secrets, wishes and the authority to interpret them. G2 explains especially well why possessing a capacity, accomplishing a rescue or intending care does not settle another person's experience or future. Read together, they make development less solitary and less linear. A person can acquire a genuine good through a troubling relationship, change one practice without changing another, and discover that a successful act has left its most important interpersonal question unanswered.

Neither generation owns the recognition that care and coercion coexist. V1 gives substantial attention to violence, dependency, compromised institutions and unreciprocated desire; G2 gives substantial attention to pleasure, ordinary competence, affection and comic proximity. The meaningful comparison concerns their selection, causal organization and precision. There are local errors and internal inconsistencies in both. There are also apparent corrections that the Japanese does not support. The accompanying [claim crosswalk and source adjudications](SW_CG2_V1_CLAIM_CROSSWALK_AND_SOURCE_ADJUDICATIONS.md) records those distinctions, including the operative content of all 39 G2 and 104 V1 registered claims.

## Monica: inherited language, plural numbers, and a chosen ordinary life

V1's most useful large claim about Monica is broader than any single registered proposition. Its character argument and synthesis propose that she learns to speak and act through sentences received from others. Lana's friendship language, the father's recognition of what matters to her, Cyril's invitation to ask for help and the differentiated models of masters give her forms in which an initially difficult wish can become an act. This is an explicitly interpretive account, not a claim that every sentence is literally borrowed. It connects the early frightened treasurer, the V09 rescuer and the later teacher without treating confidence as an unexplained gift. Its force lies in transmission: an answer once received becomes available for another person.

G2's counterweight is the plurality of purposes that this account can compress. Numerical attention is aesthetic pleasure, paternal intimacy, threatened archive, bodily management, tactical abstraction and defensive retreat at different moments. It does not originate entirely in abuse, and it does not become valuable only when turned outward. The original V01 paternal memory and V03 book encounter retain a child's joy and the legitimacy of treasuring something personally important. The V04 dance does not replace mathematical beauty with people as a universal hierarchy. Monica can still find numbers most beautiful while electing to preserve this imperfect experience. G2 C002/RV032, the Monica study and synthesis's “Numerical beauty and the social work of a school” organize this distinction more persistently than V1 C0002's strong shelter-to-evidence trajectory.

This qualifies rather than defeats V1. V1's numbers specialist distinguishes beauty, observation, defense and weaponization, and its Monica argument admits ordinary desire and coping without diagnosis. Its economic account of magic is also a substantial contribution: computation has breath, time, reserve, slot and material constraints; genius changes some costs rather than abolishing them. G2's many qualifications are strongest when they explain a particular failure or changed option, weaker if read as a reason never to say that development occurred.

V03–V05 make the distinction consequential. Accepting that a book matters to her is not merely acquiring a friend's script. The father's inquiry becomes her chosen project, potentially conflicting with guarding a prince and maintaining the admired witch's image. Neither purpose is automatically impartial. V1's account of inherited numbers turned outward explains continuity; G2 explains the new selection of an aim and its risk of motivated certainty. Both retain Barney's genuine early help, envy, dependency and subsequent protective action without treating rescue as an apology or completed reconciliation. Both distinguish the old claim about a five-year interval from the corrected chronology: five years dates publication of the book, not the father's execution. Obsolete V1 wording does not become an operative error by being easy to quote. The surviving “Unresolved” classifications in V1 reconciliation §7 item 3 (line 209) and V04 §8.8 are false-conflict residue concerning different events, not current C0014/C0032 claims or unresolved story chronology (A03).

The school and companion works prevent either developmental account from becoming purely heroic. AFTER04 makes ordinary craft, tea, tutoring, backstage labor and social restraint consequential. EXTRA09's ribbon scene explicitly changes the sequence of Monica's reception: joy comes before inadequacy and an unstumbled thank-you follows. **V1 already preserves this**, notably EXTRA09 observation 171. G2's contribution is the finer process and its limit: a friend's forceful plan can precede space for Monica's own taste; actual pleasure does not retroactively make every stage freely chosen. Early Nero likewise begins in exhaustion and indifference but already contains bodily adjustment, apology, naming and an attempt to improve food. V1 C0075's collection-wide movement from distance toward thanks is illuminating; G2 resists making the early household a static condition.

By V10–V12, giving care tests what receiving it has taught her. V1 emphasizes the relay of teachers and methods, including learning sideways and upward. G2 gives greater weight to the teacher's own error. Monica's advanced theoretical expectations deprive Isaac of practical knowledge; she asks his desired direction and accepts his choice of an original first spell. That is a real alteration of instruction. It is not a completed spell or performed practical curriculum. Her responsibility toward Will and Norman also remains fallible. A complaint route is agreed but not yet exercised; recommending a teacher does not make that teacher safe. Most sharply, the V09 blood sample exposes Monica as an imposing agent herself. Hubard restrains Barney while she cuts his finger and ignores his grievance. The source resists any reading in which collaborative expertise is automatically consensual or Monica is merely the recipient of other people's coercion.

## Isaac and Monica: saving an individual future does not define the relationship

Both generations recover multiple causes in Isaac's election to live as Felix: ducal coercion, childhood affection, grief, culpability, self-blame and the appropriation of Ark's remembered ideal. They correctly distinguish the replacement project from the prince's later death. They also retain V09's narrator-confirmed accidental fall, correcting the earlier suicide interpretation without pretending anyone knew the correction in V08. The diary does not merely disclose a fact. Its delivery permits a dead friend's imagined reunion with a freely living Ike to challenge the program supposedly undertaken for him.

V1 is especially strong on names and double lives. The continuing public Felix and privately recognized Isaac are neither simple masks nor neatly separated authentic selves. His intellectual pleasure precedes rescue; he already values serious criticism and another paper. G2 also makes that chronology explicit, preventing the diary from manufacturing the first real scholar or the first sincere attachment. The local V1 statement that Ark gave him “Ike” needs correction: Isaac offers an existing nickname. V1 elsewhere gives the scene correctly. This is a local inconsistency, not the collapse of its name argument.

The important shared correction is to a moral simplification. A constrained child can become an adult who chooses coercive means. V06's killing order is actual; its performance is not. Will follows and deviates, and Isaac praises the altered judgment. Genuine concern and lethal delegation therefore coexist without one becoming a synonym for the other. Similarly, V09's throat scene permits neither a harmless romance nor the erasure of intimacy. Isaac uses physical pressure while offering himself for revenge; Monica refuses the death script and elects rescue at a cost to her father's full vindication. The political settlement protects lives through a real machine and falsified provenance. It does not acquit him or resolve the father's case.

The later household gives both generations resistant affirmative evidence. Homecoming, scholarship, a fitting gift, food, craft and a private name are actual pleasures. His V11 refusal to use the aphrodisiac matters precisely because it is a chosen limit within an otherwise manipulative repertoire. The tea is detected before he drinks; no observed immunity occurs. His joy before imagined Ark and Monica witnesses supplies a specific moral constraint, not a general theory of consent. G2's frozen correction already says this. Recharging its superseded immunity wording would misrepresent the comparison.

V12 gives the most discriminating test. In the first wall encounter, Monica distinguishes pain from other discomfort while spectators receive a romantic-novel tableau. Their excitement is not her consent. In the night encounter, she clearly asks to be released and he refuses. She manually tickles his flank, escapes and then creates a barrier. **G2's Monica–Isaac retrospective locally conflates this into a “tickling barrier”; V1's Isaac argument preserves the correct sequence.** Isaac's ensuing remorse is inward. He changes distance, makes a spoken noninterference promise and offers a hand she accepts. The frozen G2 claim's explicit repair removes an erroneous apology noun. The affirmative objection to G2 deserves more than a defensive footnote. Mutual awkwardness, nostalgia, pleasure and Monica's wish to repeat outings alter the relationship's texture. Treating them only as missing guarantees would fail to explain why this imperfect future is wanted. Conversely, V1's stronger courtship and genre emphasis cannot turn accepted contact into retroactive permission or convert unequal knowledge into shared meaning. The two readings are most productive when they keep both facts. They converge on substantial attachment without establishing matched romantic self-understanding. His genuine national purpose and her appreciation of it also exceed an exclusively private dependency story. Neither sincerity nor policy ambition settles accountability.

## Cyril: care received, care generalized, and a desire he cannot yet name

Cyril's severity, procedural fairness, rank assumptions and need to earn worth are substantial common ground. The generality of care is an objection to treating every act toward Monica as uniquely romantic. G2 agrees but follows recipient differences more closely: some forms of ease, embarrassment and remembered affirmation are specific, and V12 narration names self-deception without supplying its final category.

V04 demonstrates why knowledge distinctions affect the literary reading. Cyril apologizes for work consuming her dance opportunity; Monica accepts the private dance and enjoys it. Its ending retains familiar severity about her poor dancing. When he speaks of next year, her thought that she will not be there is not an utterance to him. V1 Cyril §2.3 locally turns it into something she tells him, although its own knowledge ledger preserves the secret. That error removes the very asymmetry the scene makes poignant. ## Louis, Rosalie and the prequel: transmission changes its meaning by recipient

V1's transmission argument is at its strongest here. A name can preserve a teacher without an official epithet; medicine, formula revision, vocational opportunity, money, practical shelter and ordinary domestic knowledge all travel beside harshness. Major's continuing expertise and Rosalie's chosen medical work prevent Louis's genius from becoming sole authorship. Learning is not one-way. An experienced teacher must keep studying; a powerful student can recognize a neglected teacher; a lover can learn respect for the beloved's other bonds.

G2 retains this structure but objects to the conversion of selected benefits into general authorization. Rosalie's physiological limit is not laziness. Medicine is her choice, not merely Louis's solution for an inadequate magician. His fear of imposing his future on her is not her verdict that he has done so. The Upper confession contains a promise to accept refusal, her own affirmative response and mutually wanted contact. The source supports real romantic reciprocity and pleasure, not merely a list of coercive risks. It does not perform the counterfactual test of a refusal he must accept.

The Lower carriage conversation repairs a different problem from spell release or public appointment. Withheld conditions, intercepted letters, acquired gentility and an overheard sentence make Rosalie's mistaken explanation intelligible. He speaks his chosen burdens; she names her preferred familiar boy and claims her own years of waiting. V1's attention to returning voice and G2's attention to received explanation are complementary. The northern register is affectively important, but the explanation cannot be reduced to accent. Birdland's private regret and wedding attendance remain distinct from a narrated father-to-daughter acknowledgment.

G2's acquired-refinement account is also a viable alternative to V1's mask idiom. V1 itself says both modes remain Louis's; it does not simply discard the adult self. The V11 fog makes acquired repertoire inaccessible and his own later invention helps restore it. Refinement can be strategic and genuinely learned. The past does not expose one timeless authentic brute, and professional skill does not certify ethical development.

The strongest disagreement concerns comedy's relation to recognized harm. V1 C0097 rightly identifies Rutherford's goodwill lesson as a hinge: Louis has failed to recognize help and social preparation already given to him. The complete Upper scene supports that reading. It also includes painful blows, convulsion, binding, missed class and an injured hand. The question is which process the analysis makes central, not whether goodwill or injury exists. G2 makes the unequal price more continuously visible.

In Lower, however, “harm is not registered at all” is too strong. Adolf assaults Rosalie; Louis stops him and then continues excessive retaliation. The narrator and other characters identify excess and near-death, and Nate covers it. Lack of formal sanction and comic favoritism are supportable; absence of recognition is not. Similarly, the limited supervision imposed on Glen is real and supports V1 C0101. V1 MASTERS_AND_TRANSMISSION §6.5 concludes, “Rutherford's smoke and heel drops do not pass down to Glen. The responsibility does.” Its local subject is the private penalty for sealing Rosalie's memories; selective transmission is a viable reading of that bounded response. Louis §9.6 and Lower §6.3 likewise concern this punishment. Wider extrapolation to teaching is resisted by the early painful head compression and window assault (Lower p-005:P167; p-006:P45–55). The same specialist §5.3 already calls the method “fists over formulas.” The particular penalty can mark a real change while G2 gives greater weight to continuing bodily coercion elsewhere; the broadened paraphrase is not V1's sole operative view.

The final Vivian/Rutherford flashback gives the reader a history the boy did not know. In the present, Louis is told that the village is gone; the text does not show Rutherford transmitting the secret agreement to him. The V1 Lower synopsis and knowledge row/master SW-K-0158, Louis §9.2 and MASTERS_AND_TRANSMISSION §§6.1/6.4 overstate his knowledge: §6.1 says he “was never told until his honeymoon,” and §6.4's order point presupposes that later telling. His later speculation about the women knowing the closure is not equivalent. The formal parallel between the two apprenticeships and the hidden enabling care remain viable without giving Louis the reader's knowledge (A16). The distinction preserves the epilogue's unresolved autobiography: family life and ordinary talk at the grave become possible without an omniscient account or resurrection.

## Supporting lives, nonhuman difference and institutions

The comparison changes most when supporting people cease to be evidence only for the principals. Both generations preserve more independence than their product titles suggest. Lana has taste, business and labor aims; Casey's convent life is not suspended friendship; Neil and Claudia have different desires and time commitments; Frida's practical marriage inquiry includes her own household and work; Major keeps teaching; Rosalie leaves to study; Owen's research and hospital dependence are consequential. G2's school ensemble gives these aims more continuous argumentative space. V1's relationship and specialist bodies already contain many of them. An absent standalone monograph is not a literary omission.

G2 particularly clarifies how care's success is recipient-specific. Lana notices scars without demanding their account, moderates gifts and sometimes imposes. She can make Rick's hated hair newly bearable while failing to obtain assent to the preceding bath. Isabelle's staged rescue of Cecily reduces danger but manufactures perceived inevitability; gratitude is real without making the official account transparent. Casey does not apologize for the assassination in the V02 farewell; her limited apology and Cyril's separate apology must retain their objects. V1's institutional and class account explains why protection was valuable enough to bind Casey; G2 resists converting that explanation into necessity or acquittal.

Nonhuman cases resist a single human standard of successful intimacy. Lin stops a thank-you because Kara dislikes it and embraces without supplying human warmth. Romaria accepts a gift, not absolution; Monica's preservation lacks a contract because mutual wills cannot then be obtained. Will's fallibility, names and fear of failing coexist with chosen attachment and indispensable exact work. V1's long account of transmitted wishes from Eileen through Ark to Isaac makes this more than service comedy; G2 adds sharper attention to unanswered will and the difference between agreement and an exercised refusal.

Darrel and Ruferno are the strongest resistance to moralizing that starts by denying affection. Their reciprocal rescue and material grievance are genuine. The human contract stone nevertheless displaces costs onto sleeping Rick. V1 C0080 and G2 C029 substantially agree. V10 explicitly requires the Spirit King's ritual chant, while Lower locally presents Monica's summoning, appraised by Louis, as wordless. V1 C0103 has real textual support. The scope tension remains unresolved; neither generation should solve it by ignoring one context or inventing a technical exception. Likewise, the V11 ice-bell gift is created after the prison explosion. A common power or related mechanism is plausible; the particular replica's causal use in the prior event is not established.

V12's Ray/Frida sequence combines reader correction, emergency cooperation and an honest refusal to claim love. Mary explicitly knows the parental outcome; Ray does not receive the correction. Adeline already knows her daughter's history. Frida's hand kiss and hope of future liking do not secretly mean the love she denies. V1 preserves the dual force of Kayan's wish to judge/save; G2 gives particular attention to the Japanese base/ruby opposition. These are complementary readings. The narrator's truth about grief, love and danger prevents the comic ending from becoming either a cure or an empty joke.

Institutions are similarly neither pure fraud nor pure protection. V1's most important contribution is the organized account of how ranks and institutions authorize truth and distribute knowledge. G2 C026 and C038 distinguish a valid test from authenticated provenance and a technical option from permission to impose its cost. The Grail is real; Albert's blood is substituted. The settlement prevents immediate disaster while leaving the father's public vindication incomplete. V10's prologue stages a near-settled penal reform, and its ending explicitly confirms that the Grail has changed the forbidden-magic offense. V1's present-tense penalty claim is therefore supported. G2's staging precision adds historical sequence, not a refutation.

## Japanese form, pictures and the pleasures the comparison must preserve

V1's disclosure specialist offers the fuller sustained account of two directions of irony: readers sometimes know more than actors, but actors can also carry plans readers have not yet been given. Frontispieces and illustrations may anticipate prose rather than merely repeat it. G2's narrative specialist is more insistently concerned with unequal ethical effects: comic closeness can accommodate a frightening person, recipient thought can resist a pleasing tableau, and a private correction can remain unavailable to the person who needs it. These approaches belong together.

Japanese punctuation and voice are not decoration. Monica's parenthetical next-year thought cannot be made dialogue. Private remorse cannot become an apology. Louis's shifts in pronoun and register have social history rather than automatically revealing one true self. Explicit narrator correction is not merely another unreliable report. Kayan's base 「断罪して」 and ruby 「救つて」 hold opposed wishes in one utterance without enacting either. The exact large つ in the admitted source is retained in the companion record rather than silently normalized.

The actual inspected pictures reinforce differences in reception. V12's wall-pin image and the spectators' excited doorway image belong to different perspectives. Lower's tears with smiles, reciprocal faces and wedding contact support actual pleasure and cannot fairly be redescribed as unrelieved victimization. The grave and jar preserve ordinary continuity. V11's low-angle Louis and frightened Monica make an imbalance visible but do not by themselves establish the entire moral interpretation. V09's tiny jacket blurb remains unreadable at its admitted resolution; claiming an image was viewed does not repair illegible text. Source capture, visible staging and inferred function are distinct.

V1's genre objection is therefore substantial: laughter, spectacle, competence, suspense and courtship are not merely covers under which ethical facts hide. They are part of the works' achievement. G2's strongest reply is narrower than a universal condemnation: the prose itself repeatedly separates pleasing closure from an answer still withheld, a refusal ignored or a cost transferred. The unresolved relation between that pattern and the latitude granted favorites remains an interpretive tension. Neither critic can infer a complete authorial ethical doctrine from it.

## What a future reconciled analysis would need to preserve

A future analysis should preserve V1's transmission model, material economy of magic, two-way irony, ordinary comedy and the independence already retained in its supporting relationships. It should preserve G2's plural numerical purposes, recipient-sensitive causal sequences, limits on authority, nonhuman particularity and explicit separation of narrated fact, actor belief and reader access. Combining them should produce a more discriminating account of change, not a compromise in which every assertion becomes equally uncertain.

Concrete corrections would include the local identity-joining, chronology, speech/thought, knowledge-transmission and plan/performance errors recorded in the companion. G2's manual-tickle/barrier conflation and wind/winter-spirit slip require preservation as G2 errors; the latter reaches V06 E026 and CYRIL's “Consent, partial knowledge and an ice flower” (line 43), while MONICA_CYRIL's “particular providers” wording is unaffected. Wind providers, the song's winter-spirit subjects and the separate ice boy must stay distinct (A06). V1's supported law, Mary-knowledge, Thornley-patron and limited Glen-penalty findings require preservation against false correction. Superseded five-year, suicide, barrier-count, donor, tea-immunity and apology formulations must remain dated histories rather than operative charges. The source-level ritual tension and unconfirmed ice-bell mechanism must remain open.

Continuing questions survive both completed analyses: the original royal commission motive; the full father-case evidence and public accountability; unresolved patrons and identities; Romaria's own answer; durable teaching and Norman's safety; received complaints and costly respected refusal; named romantic desire; Ray's inherited explanation; future spirit arrangements, research and reforms; and the ominous forecast's referent. Completing an analysis of the admitted boundary does not complete the story. A reconciled reading would need to preserve these exits while retaining real achieved goods: a welcome received, a wanted profession pursued, a friend's sentence made one's own, a shared ordinary meal, a lesson altered and a life chosen.
