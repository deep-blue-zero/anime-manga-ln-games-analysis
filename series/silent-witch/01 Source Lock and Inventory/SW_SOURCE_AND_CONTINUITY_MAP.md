## Prospective witness-crosswalk design

A future authorized comparison uses bounded many-to-many links, not presumed chapter equivalence. Each interpretive crosswalk record must carry:

`crosswalk ID | left witness/revision/hash/unit/locators | right witness/revision/hash/unit/locators or explicit absence | relation | factual/character/form change | continuity/time/endpoint | evidence inspected | confidence/alternatives | LN disclosure ceiling | admission/review state`

Relation types include correspondence, split, merge, reorder, addition, deletion, changed characterization, conflicting event, different endpoint, and unresolved mapping. A title match is only a lead. Establish absence within an exhaustively inspected bounded comparator range before calling a deletion; missing access remains UNRESOLVED.

Retain conflicting versions rather than combining them into synthetic continuity. Label every claim by witness/source unit. Comparisons may revise current interpretation through the claims ledger but must not alter an earlier LN-only freeze. Use isolated comparison work after eligible freezes or a later authorized checkpoint; do not feed later-revealing web matches into the next prospective reader.

Raw episode snapshots, deterministic alignments and locator databases belong to Drive evidence. Git may later contain an authored compact variant argument/crosswalk with its own responsibility. No comparison file or mapping row exists yet.
