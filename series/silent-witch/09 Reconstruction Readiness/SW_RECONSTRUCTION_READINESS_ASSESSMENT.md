# Silent Witch — reconstruction-readiness assessment

## 1. What this assessment is, and what it is not

The [character reconstruction method](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/SW_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md) defines a route from inspected evidence to bounded behavioural and written-speech models. The [completion checkpoint](../04%20Checkpoints%20and%20Reviews/SW_COMPLETION_CHECKPOINT_AND_EVIDENCE_GAP_REVIEW.md) authorizes an assessment of readiness for that route under the owner's instruction. It does not authorize the route itself.

This assessment therefore:

- reviews the evidence for each warranted subject, by state and by domain;
- records what is missing separately from what the text affirmatively limits;
- names the tests a future model would have to pass;
- adds readiness records SW-RD-0057 to SW-RD-0066 to the [master ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/SW_MASTER_LONGITUDINAL_LEDGER.md).

It creates no recognition profile, domain model, fuller reconstruction, operational rule (`SW-MR-`), scenario specification or synthetic dialogue. It touches no global identity, global discovery file or global capability registry. The local readiness states it records are not global A–E judgments.

**The rule for states.** The method's vocabulary is UNASSESSED, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, BOUNDED_PROVISIONAL and DOMAIN_READY (section 5). The last two require a model that has been tested (sections 6 and 7). No model exists, so neither is awarded. This assessment uses:

- **UNASSESSED** where the evidence would support drafting and testing the named tier, but no tier has been tested;
- **INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE** where it would not, with the gap named.

A character monograph's existence does not prove readiness (method section 5). The four monographs are cited here as organised routes to evidence, not as proof of any tier.

## 2. Conditions that apply to every subject

Four conditions bound every judgment below.

1. **Prior exposure.** The analyst disclosed prior exposure to the franchise at every entry freeze. No prediction was registered after exposure. A model built by this analyst could not be tested prospectively against these units.
2. **All units read.** Every supplied unit has been read. Any test of reproduction from an earlier state against a later scene is a retrospective fit, and must be labelled as one (method section 6).
3. **Japanese text only.** Written-speech evidence is the Japanese prose as the readings record it: pronouns, address, register, hesitation and silence. It establishes nothing about voice, timbre or acoustic performance (method section 6). No audiovisual witness is admitted.
4. **Withheld identities.** A model must not resolve what the text withholds: the patron called 閣下, the person Mary does not want to die and the hidden small star, the seventh Sage, Hilda's collaborator and the prison's attacker. It must also keep implied identities implied. Each would otherwise become an invented ability or invented knowledge.

### 4.2 Gaps and affirmative limitations

**Gaps (missing evidence).**

- Adult social life beyond one year after graduation.
- Court politics from her own viewpoint, beyond the council.
- Intimacy and romance. Her feeling at V12 is new and unnamed.

**Affirmative limitations (the text limits the model).**

- Her feeling concerning Cyril and her response to Isaac's declared care are deliberately left unnamed (SW-C-0093). A model must not name them.
- Much of her interior is narrated in a comic register. A model must not convert the register into literal belief, for example taking her self-deprecation as her settled self-assessment.
- Past harm is described as behaviour and mechanism, not diagnosis (method section 3). A model may not supply a clinical label.

## 9. Test routes for any future model

A separately authorized model would need tests that do not merely confirm what the modeller already read. The method requires honest labelling of retrospective fitting (section 6). These routes are available:

1. **Retrospective reproduction with a frozen boundary.** Freeze a state's rules from evidence up to a stated unit, then compare them with later ordinary scenes. The fit is retrospective and must be labelled as such. The strongest available split for Monica is a model frozen at V08 and tested against V10 to V12's ordinary scenes. For Louis it is a model frozen at ANOTHER-LOWER and tested against his numbered-track conduct, read as a later state.
2. **Separation of modeller and tester.** A fresh-context modeller given only the ledger and readings through a frozen unit, with a separate tester holding the later scenes, reduces but does not remove contamination. Pretraining exposure to the franchise must still be disclosed.
3. **Recipient contrasts.** Each subject's relationship section supplies named recipients with recorded differences in address and disclosure. A model must reproduce the differences, not an average.
4. **Negative-constraint checks.** These are the most important tests for this series:
   - no later knowledge in an earlier state, above all around V08's disclosure and between the prequel and the numbered track;
   - no resolution of withheld or implied identities;
   - no naming of feelings the text leaves unnamed;
   - no clinical labels;
   - no invented abilities or numerical psychological thresholds.
5. **Prospective tests.** The only uncontaminated route is a later unit admitted after a model is frozen, with expectations registered before it is read. The run registers no predictions because of prior exposure. A prospective test of a model would need separate owner authorization and its own exposure statement.

An absent opportunity is UNTESTED. A plausible invented scene is not source confirmation.

## 11. Limits

- **Evidence route.** The assessment relies on the monographs, the ledger and the readings' observations. It does not reread the Japanese source.
- **No tests.** No model exists, so none has been tested. Every UNASSESSED state remains a judgment about evidence, not about a model.
- **Single assessor.** The integrator of the sequential run made this assessment. An independent review would strengthen it.
- **Boundary.** V12 and the companions. A later unit may change any state, especially intimacy and adult life.
