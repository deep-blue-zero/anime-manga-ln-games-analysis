## 6. Counterevidence and operational result

The reviewed model succeeds locally by keeping contrary material operational:

- actual hurtful mana extraction and ejection remain alongside healing; neither is used to erase the other;
- Puck warmth does not make Subaru's uninvited access welcome or authorize every Puck-tagged request;
- E007 deadline flexibility prevents a literal contract automaton, while the old binding role still carries genuine weight;
- blankness/own history reject continuous book puppetry without deleting E014's actual defensive assertion;
- remembered compassionate visitors and deliberate restraint prevent a biography of zero agency, without blaming her for every constraint;
- E017 child-enemy judgment prevents universal-pacifist collapse; her death protects a particular person without creating a general future killing script;
- chosen hand/first rank makes a real new relation but does not retroactively validate pressure, promise eternity or transfer body rights;
- technical exclusivity and spent reserve prevent the useful-oracle/limitless-combat-service model;
- the private continuity confession changes Beatrice's old belief without becoming all-camp knowledge or descendants' consent;
- partial, deliberately uninformed forgiveness and actual new friendship prevent a falsely completed redemption/cure.

Permitted reuse is evidence-bounded analysis or explicitly noncanonical reconstruction that selects the full coordinate and follows the missing-information policy. Prohibited reuse includes unmarked early/late blending, new literary facts generated by the probes, full-series Beatrice, an unconditional prediction oracle, a universal psychological explanation, a global capability label or a claim of independently demonstrated downstream fidelity.

Ordinary gaps are not perfection prerequisites: the model can support library boundaries, old shared work, specific company, particular preferences and an actual dance while declining a favorite title, exact schedule, full wardrobe practice or total life history. Likewise it can support changed attachment without proving all loneliness repaired, or a resource disclosure without inventing its future solution. These are the named limits of PASS_WITH_BOUND.
