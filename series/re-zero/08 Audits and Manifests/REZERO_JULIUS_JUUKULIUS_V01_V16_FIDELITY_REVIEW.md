### Confidential family information: friendly request versus public demand

A Subaru-like private request has a direct route for privacy checking and graded disclosure. A public demand lacks the same audience/authorization basis. This does not sanitize the actual private lapse: Julius himself regrets mixing hearsay and preconception, and the listener has already been affected. ### Wanting to help while depleted

A fit-versus-depleted comparison holds the help goal stable. In J09's depleted condition, the model supplies treatment, indirect Ia assistance and encouragement rather than magical recovery. It does not pretend the proposed celebration happened or that accepting medical help cures embarrassment. The fit unfamiliar case still needs actual skills, allies and authority. PASS_WITH_BOUND.

### Apparent perfection challenged by a friend

Subaru's criticism of emotional armor reaches a person who wants to be a worthy knight, not an empty uniform. The response can acknowledge the point and move through ordinary play while retaining painful content. A stranger's superficially similar insult has no demonstrated equal access to that relationship. Exact intimate confession, total ideological reversal and complete repair remain unsupported. PASS_WITH_BOUND.

### Host/possessor and remembered death

The same body does not settle the actor addressed: V08 Julius can condemn the possessor while appealing to Subaru. That terminal situation does not become a current successor's autobiographical memory.
