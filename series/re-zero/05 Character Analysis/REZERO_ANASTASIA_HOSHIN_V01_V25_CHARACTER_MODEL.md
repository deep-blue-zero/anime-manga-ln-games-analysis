# Anastasia Hoshin through V25

## 1. The guest she wanted to surprise

Anastasia manages to keep a secret from Julius in Priestella. The kimono arrived just before the trip. She spins to show the blue cloth and its falling petals, waits for her knight to arrive, and shows it off again. Julius has worried about being kept out. He praises her, she enjoys it, then she wants to know where Mimi is. Later that morning the same host confidently works two spatulas over the communal griddle and produces a lump of charcoal. Julius eats it and asks if the next one could spend a little less time on the heat. Anastasia is happy to oblige. Such a delicate palate for a boy, she teases. The scene supplies the request, the joke and the thing he actually ate. The next pancake never gets its review. V16 p-013 B0152–0174, B0200–0211, B0224–0234.

In the tower Julius kneels again before the owner of that voice. This time he needs to introduce himself. She gives her name, declares how much of the world she wants, compliments the handsome man in front of her and asks his name. He tells her, then waits for a response to the service he offers. She cannot call up their old relation. A look at him is enough to make her want him for her own. The knight gets a real answer. The woman answering has lost the very episodes that made it such fun to keep something from him. She will say aloud how angry that loss makes her. Her composure at their meeting and the tremor of her lips later are evidence about the same person. V25 part0014 B0388–0425, part0018 B0085–0102.

Both encounters turn on what she means by *mine*. The garment has value partly because she can show it to someone whose reaction matters. Making a knight hers requires his offer and her answer. Neither purchase nor command can give her back the experience of having known him yesterday. At court she says that success enlarged the field of things she could see and reach. In the tower she meets a costly limit to that account. She can take a new interest in the man, make room for his testimony and seek another witness. She can even begin to sound like his old companion immediately. She still has to ask him his name. V04 p-019 B0213–0236, V25 part0014 B0394–0422 and part0018 B0089–0102.

This is the first derived-use human Anastasia model. Its subject is the Kararagi merchant and Lugunican royal candidate who resumes control of her own body. Echidna, the artificial spirit who often looks like a white fox scarf, has a separate, historically bounded model. A third person, the Witch of Greed, also bears the name Echidna. All three can be consequential to a reader of the tower. Only one is the human who reassures her companion and checks Emilia's assent about Roy. This model uses the frozen Japanese readings, event states and claims as its evidence routes. Its unfamiliar cases are tests of interpretation. ## 3. What a bigger shop did to her freedom

At the selection Anastasia identifies herself to the council with a polite explanation of her origin in the lowest stratum of Kararagi's free-trade cities. Julius supplies part of her business biography. She bought the established Ryushika concern and renamed it. She supplies the order in which her world grew. A girl doing errands suggested changes to the way a little shop operated. After some of her suggestions paid off, the shop let her take part in larger deals. There came a point when yesterday's precarity should have felt very far away. She tells Miklotov it felt like *less* freedom. The more she could see and reach, the more things she wanted to get hold of. Her argument for her candidacy begins with experience of being trusted with somewhat more work. It ends with a country large enough, she hopes, to find the limit of what she can want. V04 p-019 B0171–0191, B0213–0230.

Her candor is politically daring. She lets the council ask what becomes of a possession that falls short of expectations. Her answer includes her employees, her business and her Kararagi life among the things she has once made her own. Their place in her desire puts a continuing claim on her attention and labor. Julius interprets that as ambition and depth of feeling, tempered by an executive's capacity to resist sentiment. He adds the kingdom's fiscal need and his own reasons for seeking contact abroad. His endorsement tells the court what this knight sees in her. It could not by itself establish that every subordinate wants to belong to her. When her camp’s turn ends, she fans her red face and says she may have said too much. The hearing continues with Emilia. Even an argument she has chosen to make in front of everyone can cost her some embarrassment. V04 p-019 B0228–0256.

She chose Hoshin as a surname because of her admiration for the old merchant and founder. She tells Subaru plainly that the connection is not one of blood. Taking this public name risks looking ridiculous if she fails. It also gives her something to live up to. She insists that what she has acquired so far represents part of the journey. The same young woman learns about the outside world from identifiable people and falls short of other inquiries. Later in the sequence, the fox reports her investigation into Subaru passed back to testimony about the incident around Reinhard's discovery of Felt. She had very little usable information about his earlier life. In failed E010 he had actually used the words for Japan and Earth when she asked. Even in that branch she had no basis for turning a puzzling answer into an established world history. From the successful branch she cannot even claim that exchange. V06 p-007 B0214–0235, B0246–0250 and V22 part0014 B0603–0625. Her public story explains why reaching further creates further appetite. Someone who has lived beside her offers a relational reason she will decline to give up the candidacy. The second speaker does not identify a particular person who commanded her, give a medical timetable, or retract the first speaker's desire. No election outcome lies inside this model. V22 part0014 B0675–0696.

Julius's practice bout offers a narrower view of how she treats authority. When a guard reports that he and Subaru are fighting, Anastasia wants to know who proposed it. On hearing that it was Julius, she opposes the attempt to stop him. Her crack to Emilia about the treatment bill hurts. From another standpoint she is extending discretion to a knight whose conduct she knows. The costs of that discretion fall in part on someone else. In the later negotiation she says Julius got into trouble for doing it without her authorization. The order of the two scenes matters. She first supports a choice on learning who made it. She later acknowledges that he acted independently. There is no warrant to turn her intervention into a secret instruction, his motive into her certain knowledge, or her joke into evidence of indifference to all injury. ## 4. What happens on the two sides of the café table

In one of Subaru's worst mornings in the capital he meets Anastasia and Mimi. He needs transport and has few friends left to ask. When Anastasia remarks on the difficulty of getting a carriage, Mimi pulls her sleeve. Why doesn't the lady just let him borrow one? She can arrange a vehicle. She would also like the company of the young man who until a moment ago had been trying to get rid of her. The price sounds as small as a conversation. At the counter she brings food for the group and explains that one ought to buy something in a shop even though it is a bit early for lunch. A real courtesy makes it easier to agree to a bad bargain. The scene gives no reason to dismiss her small scruple about the shopkeeper merely because she came with an ulterior purpose. V06 E010 p-007 B0143–0169.

With the promised referral still under Anastasia’s hand, the transaction gives way to Mimi’s second helping. Anastasia tells her to order as much as she likes, wipes the sauce from her face and finds her just as cute with it on. Subaru asks how a child this small can be a deputy commander. Anastasia happily looks after her, can scarcely resist cuddling her, and insists that Mimi earned the post by skill. The very reason the pair can walk in the capital together is that this child can guard her. She is willing to tell him that much. Details of the rest of the company stay hers. Mimi’s delight in being looked after and her work as a protector occupy the same moment at the same table. V06 p-007 B0195–0211.

The referral stays out of Subaru’s hands while her questions range from the origins of Subaru's clothes to demand for carriages and iron. She makes him look at the faces in the street. Has he noticed how the selection has brought different traders to town? Which kinds of goods are now moving? Even a rush on weapons could be a political investment in commercial confidence, among other possibilities. His experience in Crusch's house adds a piece her own network cannot furnish quite the same way. She can notice what a name makes him remember, even if he gives her little worth quoting. She knows about his falling-out and has judged this a good time to catch a slip of expression. At the end he really has the paper. What he did not know was how much he had already paid for it. V06 p-007 B0181–0197, B0214–0225, B0267–0315. V06 p-010 B0003 confirms the practical result.

Her treatment of him when he notices is a better guide to her limits than a rule that she is either sincere or fraudulent. She stands by the use she made of an opening. She can regret putting a finger on a sore place while defending the transaction. When Mimi puts herself between them she thanks the girl and asks her to leave him alone. Later Mimi heals his split lip at her cue. He feels humiliated by the help as well as the ambush. Mimi offers her own view of what her patron is like and gets a mild reprimand for saying too much. The customers who leave together with Anastasia show how much preparation went into the visit. Even Mimi sounds surprised to see all of them. It would be unsafe to treat the whole company as a single accomplice who knew every part in advance. Subaru leaves with an injury healed, a genuine referral, a usable lesson and every reason to resent how he got them. V06 p-007 B0300–0318, B0329–0367.

The next negotiation brings a different Subaru to a different Anastasia. A message from Rem that concerns the White Whale is enough to make a woman who would otherwise turn a visitor away come to the table. She listens to a case that she can question in front of the people it concerns. She finishes one of Subaru's thoughts for him, then asks if he has interpreted the evidence altogether too conveniently. He concedes the possible error and offers the time and place information on its own merits. The agreement with Crusch is Crusch’s to make. Anastasia can see why her presence matters even before she offers supplies. With two candidates and a leading merchant listening, a promise is harder to shrug off. She also expects orders, and whether a merchant can use the roads with the Whale still out there is a question of livelihood. From the way the discussion is proceeding she guesses that time is short. Subaru still has to supply the particular time and place. Russell can organize the guild’s traders. Anastasia volunteers to reach the others, including people working in the gaps the guild leaves. Her attraction to favors is precise. They take no space on a shelf, leave no unsold stock and come without a printed price. A future obligation that can be called in when it matters is useful to her even if no coin changes hands now. V07 E011 p-001 B0162–0198, p-003 B0047–0062, B0075–0088, B0103–0124, B0137–0153.

It is tempting to say he has finally earned her trust. He has given her a reason to do business. The people at the table still have good reasons to doubt how he knows what he knows. Subaru has done his homework using a lesson this Anastasia never gave him. She takes the proposal that exists in front of her. Later Ricardo teases her about having so few friends and offers Subaru as a first one. She tells him he is bad at concealing things and ought to watch what he says about her. He is one of the few people who can tease her so freely about the social price of making a calculation in nearly every conversation. Mimi had attributed her lady’s difficult manner to a lack of friends. Ricardo goes further. He worries that moving away from Kararagi without her acquaintances might have left her lonely. The degree of loneliness is his concern about his employer. Her easy rebuke gives us firmer evidence about the freedom he has to tease her. V06 p-007 B0343–0357, V07 p-004 B0249–0260 and B0353–0368. ## 5. Whose surprise, whose letter, whose idea of family

Priestella offers an unusual view of Anastasia with the time and standing to arrange a meeting to her taste. Each invitation has a different reason to be welcome. The high-quality crystal could help Emilia reconnect with Puck. Its actual owner is Kiritaka. Anastasia can provide a lead and an introduction. Emilia and the owner will have business of their own. The prospect for Crusch's camp concerns Gluttony. Subaru would dearly like that information for Rem. When he reacts to hearing who got priority, Anastasia explains her judgment about who would value it most and lets him know that he can hear the news too. Felt's camp has a prior point of contact. She had little expectation of a conversation with Priscilla and found no effective pretext for inviting her. Priscilla contrives to arrive anyway. The host has an excellent network and an imperfect estimate of who else is listening. C180, C296, C418. V16 p-005 B0044–0056, B0111–0113, p-006 B0483–0530, p-010 B0446–0473, p-017 B0172–0182.

Emilia can bring her elaborate preparation to a halt with a thank-you. While Subaru presses her about how she found out what Emilia wanted, Anastasia teases him about prying and guards her source. To Emilia the significant thing is that someone found a lead on what she had been looking for. She thanks Anastasia and offers to return the kindness when she can. Anastasia falls silent. Julius enjoys the unusual sight of his lady being taken by surprise. She sees him smiling, challenges him, and when he compliments the unguarded expression she recovers her footing. She revises what she had thought about the effect of the past year on Emilia. Her next attempt to take Emilia's measure also requires an adjustment. The host can admit she has something to learn while keeping a commercial secret. The young woman whose courtesy is underestimated has agency in changing the conversation. V16 p-006 B0313–0329, B0483–0514.

The same household has time for petty disappointments. Subaru takes the lead in showing others how to eat the fish at dinner. Anastasia had been looking forward to that surprise. She can explain the work of dressing in a kimono and the Kararagi craftsmen who have handed down the art. She can put a meal ahead of worry, call the staff at the right moment and keep a room interested. On the griddle one can see exactly where her expertise ends. The charred result, Julius's tact and her teasing acceptance give a playable interaction even when money, danger and politics are absent. They tell us little about her favorite meal in private. C311. ## 6. When the person doing the sums is in the room

Anastasia knows people and shelters in Priestella. She uses the mirrors her camp had retained and brings useful people into contact. She wants the whereabouts of the Witch's bones checked and is alert to what a frightened council member might give away. Knowing what an enemy is trying to obtain and giving it to them are separate decisions. Some particularly important information is spoken out of her hearing. Al's private identification to Subaru does not become a city official's verified dossier by passing through the same scene. The mirrors connect three actual endpoints. A chairperson with access to one still needs someone at another to answer. C297, C342, C344. V17 p-004 B0189–0214, B0376–0407, B0423–0436.

The Muse Company scene gives access to something the nickname “merchant” ordinarily hides. Other people have gone out to do work she cannot do. Waiting for them is an old experience. She gets through it with prayer and trust. When the windows break, childhood vigilance helps her get to the floor and her hand closes on the conversation mirror. A moment later the fearful feeling in her chest grows until her legs will scarcely obey her. Sirius's presence alters the conditions under which anyone in the room can act. The girl who reads a council's predicament feels fear growing, her breath worsening and her courage failing to reach her legs. For an instant even remaining conscious seems uncertain. Hearing and seeing the two small figures who have come to her aid helps her get moving again. An account based entirely on threats and prices could predict her conference-room decision and still get this person wrong. V18 p-004 B0395–0398, B0438–0446, B0461–0479.

The two brothers are already sharing their sister's potentially fatal wound. They are also choosing to put themselves between Sirius and the others. Anastasia knows what has already been asked of them. One challenges her to let them do this. She swallows, looks at the backs of the two boys and asks whether they can give her two minutes. The boys supply answers of their own. Ferris gets the job of getting Mimi, Beatrice and the others out. Kiritaka volunteers his people to draw attention and cover the retreat. She wants him to live. She knows why his decision needs to be his. The scene offers just enough access to her perception to see what it costs her to accept an arrangement she judges capable of saving the most people. She does not grant herself a right to spend subordinates painlessly because she loves them. Their offers give her options she did not possess alone. V18 p-004 B0424–0433, B0475–0517, B0521–0549.

## 13. Rival readings and evidence boundaries

A calculating profiteer explains the café exceptionally well. That reading gets worse when applied to the brothers' already shared wound, the city's claim on the woman who escaped, or an unpriced watch over sleeping Rem. A benevolent guardian explains her effort to keep people safe. It cannot account by itself for her enjoyment of a commercial opening, her public aspiration to possess, the sting of her mockery before Emilia or the way she makes use of Subaru's grief. A perfectly candid merchant is another failure. On one occasion she protects an informant with humor. On another she asks a dangerous question while withholding part of the answer her own companion could supply. Conversely, a model that treats every smile as a business device misses the work she puts into surprising a familiar knight and how pleased she is when her guests enjoy dinner. These rival readings are strongest when each can predict something specific, fail on a second scene, and give up the claim to explain the whole person.

A fourth reading makes the return a cure. Speech, manners, affectionate impatience and confidence in her wants have returned to the foreground. Julius has renewed service and the fox survives in a distinguishable body. Old shared episodes remain inaccessible, the extent of physical recovery is unknown, and anger sometimes breaks through the cheerful manner. A fifth reading makes the whole tower a history of the human's choices. It cannot survive the distinction between the operator's private motives and the human's remote consent or the different speakers in the three encouragements to Julius. A source-facing test of either account must be able to say who can remember, who can act, who can hear and whose equally real experience does not cross that boundary.
