## 1. Reconstruction thesis: offered help, chosen duty and imperfect protection

Reinhard repeatedly turns unusual capability into an offer of help, a professional duty or personal service. He notices immediate danger, credits people who bring him information, thanks collaborators and can enjoy ordinary company. His problem is not an absence of concern. The available record repeatedly separates wanting to protect somebody from knowing what that person wants, obtaining their assent, possessing the needed information and actually producing a good outcome.

The first street encounter already joins power with social ease: he interrupts intimidation, checks injury, introduces himself, jokes about his burdensome family name and offers help on a day off. At the loot house he depends on Felt finding him, Emilia handling healing and Subaru's warning. Elsa survives an apparent finish and Subaru is injured. Reinhard's private regret is real; Emilia refuses total blame and asks him to help again. He can receive that correction. None of this makes his later seizure of Felt consensual. The insignia changes his priority; her protests and Emilia's promise lose to his asserted responsibility.

Court service develops through an initially one-sided declaration and Felt's eventual chosen entry, rather than one uninterrupted act of mutual commitment. He can justify a kingdom's interest, selectively explain a theft, predict Felt wrongly, be outmaneuvered by Rom's intervention, then support her chosen demand. His sincere estimate of another person's promise can feel patronizing. He recognizes Julius's knightly value beyond sword strength, yet misunderstands what Subaru values in their duel and persists with reconciliation advice after the first refusal.

Ordinary encounters give him a life beyond a rescuing title. There are clothes, baths, food, competent cooking, sweets, a proposed library visit, training and discipline of retainers, compliments that annoy Felt, dry humor and enjoyment of a meal. He can withdraw a hand when Garfiel recoils and can smile as a young man when his grandfather asks a cooking question. These moments do not cure the family. Heinkel interrupts the opening, and the next day a hostage threat makes Reinhard stay even when Felt tells him to go. He later credits Otto's actual help and accepts responsibility for his own delay without making the coercer innocent.

The fight against Regulus shows intelligence, trust, humor and enormous force alongside hard limits. Reinhard can die, revive by an identified blessing, suffer an unexpected later injury, fail a test, be thrown away and lose telepathic contact. He cannot personally solve the authority by strength. Subaru's deductions and Emilia's saving of the wives materially enable the finish. Reid's refusal to draw is no finding that Regulus is harmless; Reinhard eventually uses the sheathed sword because other implements are risky. The later drawn Reid against Theresia marks a different situation.

Family duty does not make the resulting emotional cost disappear. Reinhard saves Heinkel from a lethal corpse attack and insists he has stopped an animated dead body rather than killed a living predecessor. Wilhelm has just experienced Theresia's real brief final response and cannot receive that distinction as consolation. Reinhard attempts physical support, stops at the refusal, pauses before affirming he will not regret a right action, then reacts bodily to the grandfather's distant dismissal. He still advises the men to seek safety. His explicit position, the correctness of an immediate rescue, the final farewell and the relational wound can all be true without an exhaustive diagnosis of his inner life.

The latest council and corridor preserve further agency. Reinhard differs from Felt over Sirius, seeks institutional custody and accepts escort responsibility; Felt chooses to accompany him while retaining her disagreement. He does not remember Julius but can respect the unfamiliar knight and be troubled by the lost friendship. He offers Subaru discretion even against disclosure to his own patron, reports an earlier failed cure mission with regret, smiles about Felt, says he is all right and accepts a future invitation to lean on a friend. The source does not show that all hurt has healed or that he has actually made that later disclosure.
