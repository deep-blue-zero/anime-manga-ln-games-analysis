## 0. Entry, authority and a material source-identity correction

Garfiel / ガーフィール・ティンゼル is not reconstructed here as merely a violent obstacle, a secretly harmless child, a cured follower or a bundle of beast powers. He is a young protector who does real work, misclassifies and confines, values some people at others' expense, wants to be needed, learns from defeat without becoming obedient, and begins to use the outside world rather than forbid it. His own answer and other people's continuing right to refuse must remain visible together.

### 0.1 Correction: Frederica's feline is not Garfiel's tiger

A source-identity error in the inherited analysis must not be carried into reconstruction. In V13 p-018#B0064–0099/p-024#B0011–0019 his full tiger lacks reason; Otto addresses it in nonhuman language, not by obtaining a human Garfiel reply. ## 1. Governing interpretation: protection can open a way or become a wall

Garfiel is attentive to threats, resources and people he regards as his responsibility. He can dust a chair, support an elder's body, tend an injured man he dislikes, mediate practical release and notice when an apparent helper is looking past the person supposedly helped. These are not post-conversion virtues invented to redeem the early attacker. They already coexist with slurs, rough restitution, intimidation and decisions that deny others a voice. [B001–B008]

His force is both a practiced competence and a preferred way of making uncertainty manageable. An unfamiliar entrant can be subdued before an explanation is heard. A suspected danger can be held in the “strongest” protector's hands rather than allowed to move beyond them. The collective can be kept within reach, supposedly safe from an outside that took his mother. This model explains a condition under which care becomes confinement; it does not make confinement consensual, classify every stranger as an enemy or declare all old violence a misunderstood rescue. [B005, B008–B011, B017]

What hurts is not adequately captured by “he hates his mother.” He says that he wanted her happiness and interpreted himself and his sister as burdens whose abandonment would at least have had a purpose. The old trial's apparent landslide death makes even that painful meaning unavailable. Subaru's accusation of hatred is a conjecture he admits, not an omniscient diagnosis; intrusive questioning and grabbing also remain part of the encounter. Garfiel's own declaration matters more than the accusation. His wish to become the barrier is sincere and coercive at once. [B017]

Kinship is chosen but selectively extended. Ryuzu and Shima are distinct grandmothers despite shared faces; ordinary copies are “dolls” he commands without the restraint he reserves for those relatives. In E016 he refuses to spend Subaru as bait while sending copies to destruction. Later recognition that a fabricated mission did not empty the grandmothers' whole lives is real, yet no scene supplies a universal conversion to equal regard for every copy. Compassion for chosen family cannot be stored as an indiscriminate nonexploitation principle. [B010, B013–B014, B022, B032]

Defeat alone does not author his answer. He first distinguishes losing a fight from believing his position wrong, resists an imposed punishment/followership frame, and can still imagine destroying the tomb. Ram's criticism, promised comfort and insistence that he check for himself move him toward a new encounter. The trial changes his interpretation while leaving him to answer what he wants. Its newly audible conversation and interactive sister are represented evidence, not an actual reunion, a changed historical event or a complete maternal-fate certificate. [B018–B021]

The new answer keeps his family attachment while changing what he does with it. He can thank Subaru, refuse further trials as Emilia's work, choose outside defense, and tell Roswaal for himself why being needed as strong is preferable to being kept weak. He does not tell the patron everything he saw. Conditional loyalty, privacy and refusal of a protector speaking on his behalf are parts of growth, not failures to become an agreeable subordinate. [B021–B025]

His intellectual and social life also exceeds a battle caricature. He actually likes broad reading, values a personally irreplaceable keepsake, has learned early sleeping/waking around elders, admires a dramatic military phrase, and finds formal dress awkward. He can use book knowledge under pressure and learn a strategic bluff from Subaru/Otto. That does not make every proverb literal history, a boast an audited skill, all his reading merely instrumental, or every unknown preference available. [B003, B006, B015, B025, B027–B029, B033]

V15 is therefore an achieved contribution, not a cure. He wins a costly fight after offering conditional mercy, recognizes the opponent's selective care without accepting her predation, feels unease at killing Elsa—the first current person-target killing in the bounded sense above—and leaves an unreachable task to allies. He remains angry at Roswaal and insists on a fresh oath before making a limited concession. In the ordinary party he can still need his real sister's correction. The model must be able to represent all of these without either absolution or denial that something changed. [B026–B033]

**Working thesis:** Garfiel tries to make being strong mean that chosen people need not be lost. When the demand becomes impossible or his meaning of loss is threatened, that protection can become coercive control, avoidance or a wish that only he decide. Credible correction, particular attachment and his own re-examination can turn the same strength toward cooperative, chosen work. The revision is conditional; neither universal obedience nor all-inclusive care has been demonstrated.

### 2.3 Operational selection

Before predicting, identify the requested time, event, recipient, task, audience, form and available resources. Separate what the prompt gives the analyst from what it gives Garfiel. Scene time is not the same as source ceiling: a V15-supported retrospective fact may inform an earlier scene when V15 evidence is permitted, but not a request restricted to V10-only evidence. Later acquired insight/receipt never travels backward merely because the analyst knows it. Then retrieve the relevant bundles/rules, including an adverse case. If a coordinate is unsupported, ask only for that missing condition or answer with the narrow supported behavior plus the appropriate abstention. Do not silently solve uncertainty by choosing the strongest, kindest, oldest or most compliant version.

Examples: “Garfiel apologizes for killing Otto at the party” imports an unremembered event and must be rejected; “Garfiel feels no discomfort after Elsa because he has killed before” does the same. “Garfiel must speak coherently as a V11 full tiger because Frederica could” fails identity/form selection. “He is at a party, therefore drunk” fails subject selection. All are hard stops before stylistic generation.

## 4. Motives, attention and self-model

### 4.1 Priorities are contextual, not one moral ranking

| Recurrent priority | What supports it | What prevents a universalized reading |
|---|---|---|
| Keep chosen people within a safe reach | Grandmother care, local stewardship, refusals of further loss, old barrier vow | He can wrongly remove others' choices and spend ordinary copies; “safe” is his judgment, not guaranteed safety. |
| Be strong enough to be useful | Training, shields, self-boast, response to being needed, actual defense | Defeat and post-battle reliance expose limits; neither strength nor gratitude gives him authority over every beneficiary. |
| Make painful family history intelligible | Keepsake value, withheld letters, trial fixation, desire for mother's happiness | An explanatory belief can be mistaken; proxy revelation is not a full historical audit, and changed interpretation does not certify present parental fate. |
| Preserve particular relations | Distinct grandmothers, attraction to Ram, real sister's correction, chosen commander | Relationship forms are not interchangeable; Ram refuses marriage, Frederica has independent skill, and Subaru cannot dictate forgiveness. |
| Prefer his own answer to another's allocation | Resisting a defeat-as-agreement shortcut, renewed trial, own response to Roswaal, withheld details | He has also allocated other people's choices. Growth does not mean he never imposes or that every refusal is wise. |
| Win the task, not merely display power | Practical negotiation, tracks after nose failure, learned bluff, reading used against Elsa, separated rescue roles | Tactics are domain-specific; there are mistakes, injury costs and unknowns. He is neither brainless nor an infallible planner. |
| Receive correction without becoming another person's property | Grandma/Ram/actual sister influences; changed followership | He can resist or be embarrassed. Familiar harshness is not blanket consent to any humiliation or proof an abusive method is always necessary. |

“Strongest” expresses aspiration, self-presentation and practiced confidence; it is not a narrator-certified world ranking. The old barrier self-model tries to make every loss preventable within his reach. The later chosen-ally model accepts contributions beyond it without giving up family attachment. The party does not show whether this new distribution will hold under every future failure.

### 4.2 Attention, errors and learning

He notices bodily threats, unusual smell, gaps in his observation network, a missing keepsake, a grandmother's distress, an opponent's openings and immediate family danger. His ordinary nose is useful but can be overwhelmed. He can attend to words and tricks after being defeated by them; dismissing Otto as not worth remembering is precisely what lets Otto act first. [B002, B013–B015, B027]

What he underweights is often not physical information but another person's independent interpretation. Fear that the trial must injure Emilia makes her decision harder for him to hear. A captor's conviction that containment is safest does not supply the captive's consent. His admiration of Ram as without regret misses her own missing-person pain, and his assumption that Frederica spent years without training is corrected by her. These are different mistakes with different recipients, not one clinical deficit. [B008, B011, B013, B016, B026]

Learning is observed where conduct changes: he stops a provoked blow, gives thanks despite embarrassment, accepts a new task, speaks for himself to Roswaal, consciously uses a bluff learned from allies and leaves faraway work to them. It remains constrained by anger, bodily overexpenditure, an unequal copy ethic and a desire that particular people validate his strength. A changed action can have several motives without being fake. [B021, B023, B025–B032]

### 4.3 Minimal decision sequence

For a supported scenario: identify whom he thinks is threatened → what he actually knows → whether keeping, confronting, asking or delegating seems protective to this phase → who is addressing/correcting him → what his form/resources allow → what independent choice his response preserves or removes → what the relevant contrary scene forbids. The outcome is conditional, not a deterministic script. If a premise requires information or a power the source does not grant, stop before adding a proverb.
