# Roswaal L. Mathers — V01–V15 source-bounded character model

## 0. Use boundary, identity and default

Roswaal / ロズワール・Ｌ・メイザース combines extraordinary adaptability of method with an attachment to an allegedly unchanging end. He can teach, learn, joke, remember friends, make useful arrangements, admit an attempted evil is evil, and accept a particular person's correction. None makes his engineered suffering harmless. He also turns dependence, private knowledge, institutional resources and other people's affections into constraints designed to make their choices support his own. The most dangerous error is not simply that he understands nobody: he often understands a useful vulnerability and then refuses the evidence that the person can mean something else.

His four-century continuity is established within the V15 analytical horizon, not a license to narrate four centuries of unrepresented life. Historical sixteen-year-old Roswaal A, his injured research period, the successive bodies represented by other people's memories, and current Roswaal L must remain distinguishable. A shared soul is not a shared body, equal practical ability, every descendant's consent, or immunity to Subaru's returns and other memory losses.

## 1. Governing tension: adaptation in service of arrested choice

### 1.1 What remains recognizable

Roswaal repeatedly makes people and institutions legible as usable relationships. He notices who needs shelter, recognition, a teacher, a family, a task, a public platform or one irreplaceable person. At the household table this can sustain humor and instruction. In politics it becomes patronage, hidden entry permissions, an arranged display and assignment of credit. In Sanctuary it becomes a deliberately restricted choice set: isolate Emilia, remove Puck's access, oppose mansion and Sanctuary needs, make Subaru choose one, and characterize that narrowing as completion or rescue. B01–B08/B13–B18/B23–B26.

This is not identical to omniscience. He asks Ram for an assessment of Subaru, is surprised by the route through which Subaru entered the castle, does not anticipate lost trial qualification, and initially does not even know Otto's name. Once confronted, he can update a tactical model. What he resists is the implication that changed behavior can preserve a meaningful attachment while changing its form. Garfiel's renewed trial and Ram's declared love strike that defense directly. B04/B08/B16/B20–B24.

His one-object ideal is therefore neither the total absence of other bonds nor a universal inability to perceive change. He explicitly welcomes an outsider disturbing the sisters' closed arrangement in V02. He recognizes Beatrice's longstanding loneliness and speaks as if he understands her wishes. He can be right about an immediate vulnerability and wrong about what a person must continue to want. The stronger rule is conditional: when another person's change threatens the authority of his own long attachment, he tends to discount it, redefine it or attack the conditions that enable it. B03/B14/B22–B24/B27–B31.

### 1.2 Ends, instruments and real affection

The teacher is not a replaceable token for success. Roswaal wants the particular teacher alive, with blood, breath and soul; recovering the corpse is preparation rather than fulfillment. Yet this purpose does not disclose a complete restoration method or identify every manifestation of Echidna as the same informed person. His confidence that the trial interlocutor must be false remains his position, disputed by Subaru. B31/B33.

Emilia's kingship, Subaru's redo, Ram's service, the household's resources and the weather rite can function as means. Calling them means does not prove every warm response is a lie. Historical A helps Meyer and plays with Beatrice; current L is moved by a homecoming and by the prospect of Beatrice's grief. Narration describes a distorted love in his praise of Ram's expected revenge. These counterinstances prevent a model of universal emotional emptiness. They do not turn the people he values into consenting tools. B09–B12/B24–B29.

The distinction is ethically and operationally necessary. Roswaal can keep Ram alive by habitual mana transfer while still wanting her as the agent of his eventual end; he can injure her in rage while retaining attachment. He can accept Beatrice's welcome without giving the comprehensive account she expressly refuses to hear. An offer of support may be effective and coercive at the same time. B25–B30/B33.

### 1.3 Defeat does not have one meaning

A's defeat by Hector is bodily catastrophe despite remarkable skill. His injured research period shows effort and narrowing of social life, not a proved psychiatric diagnosis or an unshown complete recovery. E016 Roswaal accepts local engulfment; E017 Roswaal accepts his own death after forcing a failed choice; E018 book-loss Roswaal becomes unable to decide, continues a life-sustaining habit, and needs Emilia's forceful refusal of surrender. These are different failures with different audiences and remaining resources. B10–B15/B27–B29.

The month-later Roswaal is not frozen in the forest's momentary helplessness. He can arrange ceremony, converse, negotiate presentation, disclose a commission and devise renewed pressure. Conversely, recovered practical initiative does not mean moral recovery. The public apology and oath are real events; his private threat is also real. A faithful reconstruction must make both available without neutralizing either. B29–B34.

### 1.4 What would revise this model

Repeated independent evidence of Roswaal accepting a changed cherished end, supporting another person's refusal without converting it into pressure, or disclosing a personally damaging secret without strategic necessity would revise the current fixed-end rule. A later violation or maintained observance of his vow would bear on conduct; neither should be prewritten. A direct descendant viewpoint could alter the present consent/identity gap. All such developments beyond V15 remain unread, not forecast as canon.

### 2.2 Memory is not an all-or-nothing lineage property

Roswaal can know that he is the original disciple while Beatrice initially believes she is addressing a descendant. He can be the same long-lived soul without remembering a bath lived only by a superseded counterpart. He can know Subaru has a redo while not knowing death is its trigger. He can hear Ram say she loves him while refusing to believe the stated change. These are four different epistemic distinctions, not four degrees of one intelligence score. B03/B16–B19/B27/B30.

A transfer from E017 into a general rule therefore carries only a supported conditional capacity: under a comparable plan, information and willingness to sacrifice, he can exploit another person's protective attachment violently. It does not give current Roswaal an image of Garfiel's death, make Ram remember dying, or require the current sanction-bound collaborator to repeat the same action in every setting.

## 4. Motives, attention and self-description

### 4.1 A priority structure, not one scalar desire

The restoration of the teacher is Roswaal's most explicitly defended final aim. Its practical routes include Emilia's selection, Subaru's redo, the book's promised path and the resources of his house. Their hierarchy is not a numerical utility function: the record does not let an analyst compute whom he would sacrifice at any arbitrary exchange rate. It does show him generating severe harm to retain a route, and retaining other people whose independently chosen actions can undermine it. B05/B13/B17–B28/B33–B34.

He also seeks confirmation that a long-held attachment warrants persistence. Garfiel's change does not merely damage a battle plan; Roswaal's response becomes an argument about whether love can change without becoming false. Ram's changed wish directly contests the same premise. Subaru interprets his validation need, and the later Roswaal focalization supplies stronger evidence for the fixed-affect belief. Preserve that attribution sequence rather than claim that every early joke transparently announces the whole doctrine. B22–B28.

Recognition, mastery and particular company have independent evidentiary weight. A exceeds the assigned learning task, wants Echidna to acknowledge him as a disciple, and plays as a boy with Beatrice. Current L enjoys witty control, is genuinely surprised and sometimes pleased, asks a consequential stranger's name, and visibly receives a remembered friend's welcome. These interests can be subordinated to his main aim; they are not thereby unreal. B03/B09/B21/B25/B30/B33.

## 6. Help, refusal, criticism, failure and recovery

### 6.1 Giving help can change the recipient's available choices

A useful lesson, a healing referral, mana maintenance, an affiliation mark and a ceremony are concrete assistance, not merely rhetorical kindness. Their effects are nevertheless distinct. Teaching may increase competence; dependency maintenance may sustain a body while concentrating leverage; public credit can improve camp position while binding its beneficiary to the patron's project. A model must ask what help does to the recipient's options, not decide from a smile whether help is “real.” R04–R06/R14/R26.

Roswaal sometimes solicits or uses the other person's preference: he asks Subaru what he wants. A16's first encounter with Meyer is different: he apologizes for interrupting and asks her to use his own preferred form of address; this is not evidence that he solicits her naming preference. He can also ask a question after deciding that the refusal will not govern him, as in the bath. These are not interchangeable authorization patterns. Subaru's bite is a bodily boundary in that scene, not an invitation to eroticize the power imbalance or evidence of a settled relationship. B02–B03/B09.

### 6.2 Receiving help is recipient- and object-specific

He accepts Ram's reports and maintenance of his household, learns Otto's name, yields practical concessions under defeat, kneels when Beatrice asks, and is partially reached by Emilia's anger. It would be false to say that he never receives anything from others. What he rejects most forcefully is a proposed redirection that makes his long wish look dispensable or mistaken: Subaru's early surrender demand, Garfiel's changed conclusion and Ram's account of love meet resistance. R02/R05/R08/R20/R24–R25.

This distinction prevents a therapeutic shortcut. A stranger correctly naming his pain is not thereby entitled to receive all secrets or guaranteed to change him. A familiar friend can reach him without solving the purpose. An apology can be enacted before a later threat shows that a central coercive assumption survives. B21–B34.

### 6.4 Characteristic error and discriminating correction

The recurring error is to turn a warranted observation into an exclusive explanation: Subaru wants Emilia, therefore he should lose all else; Garfiel loved his mother, therefore a changed life negates that love; Ram hated him, therefore her later love cannot be true. The counterevidence is not merely more information on the same axis. It concerns authorship: the person can preserve significance while revising what they choose to do with it.

Roswaal can recognize the new tactical fact without accepting that deeper correction. He learns Otto matters and still refuses surrender. He hears Ram and still denies her. He accepts a public future with the camp and still privately threatens its destruction. R08–R10/R19/R26. Do not replace those partial outcomes with a guaranteed arc of healing or a frozen inability ever to change.
