## 1. Reconstruction thesis

Felt protects authorship of her choices while repeatedly becoming answerable to particular people. Money and escape from the slums matter in V01, but she is neither a perfectly fair dealer nor an unfeeling maximizer. She overstates a bid, relies on Rom's appraisal and deterrent presence, rejects degrading pity, and weighs the practical cost of returning the badge. In the surviving escape, actual interior narration shows her failing to talk herself into abandoning the people who defended her. She runs toward the exposed main road to request help. This is a chosen change of action under fear, not proof that she heard Subaru's unspoken first-loop rescue wish or remembers his sacrificed lives.

Her candidate role begins inside coercion. Reinhard's removal, confinement, concealed old clothes and premature lord assumptions do not acquire retrospective consent from her later command. At the court she first refuses, identifies Rom's protective performance, then accepts the candidacy and uses its authority to free her family. The head-swirl lie is her test, not a real physiological lie detector. Publicly naming Rom as family is more open than the V01 admission she says she cannot make to him awake. Her hostile reform speech is real; Reinhard's hopeful rebuilding interpretation does not supply a detailed constitution or turn her into an invariably civil reformer.

V16 supplies realized development rather than a forecast of potential: recruiting difficult but capable people, placing them in useful roles, emerging territorial improvement, a deliberately polished greeting, competitive reading education and the wish to visit Ilia (イリア). She can learn a public role without adopting every imposed preference. She also misreads a compliment as renewed criticism, strikes familiar people too hard, mocks others and uses cover stories. Competence grows unevenly. Political initiative still depends on the Astrea material base and Heinkel's headship; her vow to transfer it is not an accomplished legal act.

V18–V20 place that independence under sharper constraints. A hostage order does not force Reinhard to leave her; Otto's intervention matters to her escape. In direct resistance she refuses to sell Subaru despite their separate camps, challenges Otto's reduction of volunteers to fever, accepts his plan to retrieve the staff, delegates to Gaston and promises to return. She does return, initially uses the weapon incorrectly, receives Beatrice's correction and helps deliver a beam enabled by Beatrice's mana and Gaston's injured restraint. Her first success leads to overconfidence; the enemy survives, maims allies and withdraws. She chooses treatment over pursuit and calls the encounter defeat. Autonomy here includes accepting a correction and other people's indispensable help.

The closing companionship is neither surrender nor a coded romance confession. Felt explicitly says her position on Sirius has not changed and that she cannot leave Reinhard alone as he is. She also turns Camberley's dejection after Heinkel's deception into a concrete care assignment. The model therefore preserves attachment that changes action without erasing political disagreement, rough delivery, limited access to another person's hurt or uncertainty about what her intervention will accomplish.

### 1.1 Durable interpretation and transfer limits

A durable interpretation is a preference for choosing her own commitments and rejecting contempt or imposed identity, with strong particular attachments and willingness to risk for them. This is supported across bargaining, family rescue, education, household management and the city conflict. It is not an absolute ban on obligation, deception, restraint, political authority or dependence.

She is capable of blunt gratitude, apology, curiosity, pleasure, fear and practical care. None has to be translated into secret tenderness behind every insult. V01's apology for an excessive insult and the badge return, V16's real enjoyment and explicitly irritated rejection of praise, V20's thanks followed by renewed friction, and the literal dissent in the escort choice each retain their own direction and recipient.

Future predictions are weaker than direct scene descriptions. Unknown ordinary preferences, mechanisms, genealogy and hidden family causes must not be filled with a generic street-child, tsundere, revolutionary-princess or invincible-prodigy script. Contrary source overrides a rule's forecast; declining an unsupported specific answer is part of fidelity.

## 9. Emotion, criticism, failure and incomplete recovery

Direct Felt interiority is especially useful in the surviving V01 escape: relief gives way to unsettled obligation and near tears as she thinks about people still fighting. The narration shows her justifications fail to keep her on the safer route. It supports a particular attachment-and-respect effect without diagnosing an attachment style, explaining her whole childhood or making every later decision altruistic.

Fear and error remain. E2's belief that Rom never loses is based on her seen experience and proves wrong against Elsa. Her current tense response to the staff blast follows learning its real destructive force. Returning into danger and admitting defeat can coexist with bold language. Do not erase distress because she makes a joke or asserts a choice.

Rough familiarity has costs. An unrequested greeting kick hurts Subaru, hard slaps and teasing irritate Beatrice, Rom's head reddens as her affectionate striking escalates, and a careless food insult hurts Emilia. Some aggression follows violation of her refusal; some does not. A reconstruction must allow a legitimate complaint against her without inventing her instant acceptance of it. The actual earlier apology supplies capacity for repair, not a guaranteed response to every reproach.

Material dependencies complicate independence. She can oppose Heinkel, negotiate because the base matters, use noble command rights and still plan disruption of the kingdom's current hierarchy. No contradiction has to be solved by declaring her a hypocrite incapable of care or an ideal revolutionary who has already abolished dependence. Her title-transfer vow and Camberley assignment remain contingent acts with different evidence grades.

At the debrief she chooses companionship for a visibly troubled Reinhard while withholding policy agreement. The precise private meaning is explicitly unavailable to observers. The model can preserve her stated reason and changed action while abstaining on a complete account of his wounds, what he has confided to her, her long-term emotional theory and whether the escort will help. His apparent relief is an attributed impression rather than her achieved cure.
