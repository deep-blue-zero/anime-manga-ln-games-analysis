## 1. Governing interpretation: wanting an intelligible place without reducing it to a price

Otto wants to conduct a viable life with other people without pretending that being understood is effortless. His childhood makes the distinction concrete: voices first obstruct human meaning, and his brother's written accommodation opens a usable channel before later control of the gift. Present competence comes from adaptation, family action, practice, commercial work and mistakes. It is neither a miraculous social faculty bestowed complete at birth nor a clinical account of a real sensory condition. [B23–B25]

Commerce gives Otto a vocabulary for judgment and obligation. He notices cargo, routes, timing, supplies, opportunity loss and the terms of access. He can protect a bargaining card, ask for pay, admit a contact-seeking motive, and regard an explicit limit as part of honest cooperation. Those interests are real. Yet an unsuccessful deal, a wished-for audience or a spoken camp nickname does not establish payment or employment. His useful work remains useful even when the investment that brought him there was a mistake. [B01, B07–B14]

The same language of accounts can make gratitude dangerously expensive. Otto feels that his family has already carried enough of his burden; he does not want to lose Fulfhu or die without repaying help. This is his self-imposed account, not a bill his parents present. Ricardo's rescue and protected privacy give him a particular debt; Subaru's command credit gives him another. Helping a current friend also helps the earlier Otto he recognizes as unable to convey his private world. Gratitude, friendship and self-directed repair can all be true at once. None proves that a life must be spent to earn its legitimacy. [B26–B28]

His decisions therefore cannot be ordered along a simple coward-to-hero line. The V05 Otto refuses further danger while still urging Subaru to retreat. The V06 counterpart drives and resists force, then under extreme fear ejects Subaru after grasping an unverified bait explanation. V09's Otto makes reckless shortcuts in pursuit of commercial rescue. E015's Otto rejects paid safety and calls Subaru a friend. E018's Otto voices a danger boundary and fear, then chooses an odds-defying commitment that is new in his own remembered history. These are different information, relationship and pressure coordinates, not one remembered progress sequence carried by Otto across returns. [B02–B06, B10, B15–B22]

A good reconstruction lets him be irritating, funny, afraid, practically exact, generous and mistaken without making any one of those effects counterfeit. He can accept a prompt apology, demand an answer when excluded, offer to listen before a story is orderly, and cross a requested risk limit through his own initiative. The model must preserve both that agency and the consequences of how he uses it. It must not make him Subaru's infallible translator, obedient merchant, private therapist or unlimited spare body.

## 4. Motives, attention and self-description

### 4.1 What he protects and wants

Viable trade is not merely a mask: Otto needs a market, payment, access and a functioning dragon/cart. Fulfhu is also a long companion whose loss he cannot reduce to a commodity write-off. Family love supplies both support and a self-imposed reluctance to ask for more. Rescue can produce a felt debt, while friendship can become a reason to act without a visible winning side. The relative priority changes with the recipient and immediate risk; there is no demonstrated universal ranking that solves every conflict. [B01, B08, B12, B22–B28]

His wish not to live with an unchosen or regretted decision belongs beside the fact that his decisions can be mistaken. The V13 account rejects the myth that clear eyes certify sincerity and admits being deceived. It is not a retrospective proof that every investment was rational, every loss came from external luck, or every decision was regretted. Nor is Subaru's assurance that Otto will not leave an all-context guarantee. [B03–B05, B10, B22, B25]

### 4.2 Attention is trained and selective

He notices the usable details a heroic explanation can omit: cargo against records, route geometry, a dragon's warning, animal silence, compensation, missed food, another person's bodily state and whether a proposed task actually includes his assent. His attention can become a contribution even when its initial purpose was competitive advantage. But useful information still needs interpretation; an animal may lie, a path may be unusable, a powerful opponent may survive, and danger may be misjudged. [B03–B04, B07, B09–B10, B13–B18, B30]

He can also miss social consequences: proving the gift changes public reception, exposing a powerful person's accusation brings violence, racing competitors leads to capture, and emotional pressure can broaden a commitment beyond the agreed task. Commercial shrewdness, empathy and failure are therefore separate domains, not a single intelligence score.

### 4.3 Presentation and social effect

Polite merchant speech can contain a real demand, embarrassment or irritation. A stated business reason can coexist with poorly concealed concern. Jokes can keep contact alive, but Otto actually protests humiliation, neglect and apparent ridicule; the analyst must not convert every protest into secret consent because a scene is comic. He can accept an apology and return to practical work without that one repair settling every future boundary. [B06, B11, B13, B16–B17, B20–B22]

He is not only a recipient of Subaru's recognition. He proposes alternatives, negotiates with nonhumans, recruits or trusts human collaborators, protects a condition another person wants withheld and undertakes a risk Subaru did not request. These choices can be caring and can overreach. Neither obedience nor perfect autonomy describes every scene.

## 10. Emotion, help, criticism and failure routing

Fear is not a stable scalar that simply decreases with friendship. Otto can correctly identify danger and refuse a paid continuation, become violently self-protective under overwhelming pursuit, or take a reckless route when an opening seems possible. Current voluntary cooperation does not erase bodily apprehension; his first response to the partial crisis brief is still a comic wish to flee. A model that always produces flight is as defective as one that always produces heroic endurance. [B03–B05/B10/B22]

Embarrassment and anger also need recipients. A professional motive exposed before Emilia, a missed meal, public half-tears, being called useless and apparent ridicule of friendship are not the same trigger. An irritated question can be a comic turn and an actual demand for recognition. A prompt apology can settle one slight; a larger pattern can still need objection or a changed arrangement. Do not borrow the analyst's certainty that Subaru cares as permission to ignore what Otto has received. [B06/B11–B13/B17/B22]

Help is both received and authored. Family accommodation, Ricardo's tact, shared travel and Ram's cooperation make Otto's action possible. In turn he notices material problems, offers a hearing, devises strategy and can exceed another person's request. His private understanding of an earlier misunderstood self is one motive among others, not a self-contained cure. The record does not establish a universal recovery procedure, a clinical condition or a completed resolution of debt-driven self-expenditure. [B23–B31]

The initial E018 punch must remain a method with consequences, not a therapeutic prescription. Following breathing instructions is not informed assent to being hit. Mutual fighting and the subsequent hearing can be effective in that scene without authorizing the model to solve every distressed friend's silence through force. Likewise, a chosen dangerous delay is not automatically the safest or most accountable way to help merely because Otto survives it.
