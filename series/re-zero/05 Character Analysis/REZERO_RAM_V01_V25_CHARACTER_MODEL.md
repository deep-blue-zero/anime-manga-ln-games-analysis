# Ram through V25

## 1. When somebody else pays

At the estate, Subaru tells Ram a story in which one ogre gives up his place in the other's life. Subaru watches the one who leaves. Ram watches the one who gets what he wanted. She asks what the red ogre himself was prepared to lose. Her answer is startlingly literal. He could have broken his own horn. When she gets up to go, she asks Subaru to keep the story from Rem. These are two distinct decisions. Ram will tell this guest where she thinks responsibility lies. She also claims the right to decide which story her sister should hear. The Japanese gives her both the judgment and the intervention. Rem has no speaking turn in that conversation. V02 p-019 B0286–0303, B0363–0373.

Much later, facing someone who has taken other people's memories, Ram breaks the staff that holds her own severed horn. That looks, briefly, like exactly the answer she gave the ogre. The next act makes the old answer difficult to live by. She makes her sleeping sister's horn function and sends part of her own burden through their connection. Her weapon is spent and her own injuries are real. So is the cost in Rem's body. Ram asks forgiveness as she acts. Later, speaking to Subaru, she allows that Rem might hate her and says she does not regret her choice and intends to improve their future contact. Whether she would repeat it in the face of an awake sister’s refusal is untested. Her admission gives a future Rem room to object in a relationship Ram intends to keep. It cannot give Rem a choice in the action already performed. V25 part0015 B0245–0269, B0358–0390, B0568–0585 and part0018 B0268–0281.

The two scenes support a model of how Ram takes responsibility, including the occasions when her own standards expose a failure. She pays close attention to who is weak, who has undertaken a task and who is being made to pay for another person's wish. She usually wants a useful say in the allocation. Given time she can ask to be left alone, offer instruction, refuse a proposed marriage, or let another person keep a confidence. Under threat she can compel, exclude a dissenting witness, use a lord's authority or impose an intimate cost. Her attachment predicts whom she will work to protect. It predicts far less about whether that person gets to decide how. The beneficiary, the cost bearer and the person to whom she gives an account can be three different people.

Ram's own body complicates the question. The child in the burning village refuses the elder's ambition for her. She experiences an urge to destroy that grows as she acts. She will not save herself alone. At the instant the horn flies free, the pain is severe and the inner incitement falls silent. The relief belongs to Ram. V03 had already shown Rem's privately felt reaction and the guilt that followed it. Putting these two interiors beside one another reveals how little either girl could infer about the other from that one visible injury. It changes how we understand Ram's resistance to being somebody's instrument. It also makes her use of Rem at the tower harder to excuse by appealing to a shared identity. V03 p-021 and p-023 B0004–0045. V25 part0015 B0003–0070, B0358–0386.

### A reader's view of the past and Ram's access to it

V03 p-023 B0034 and B0045 place nearly seven years of guilt, and seven years of experience of the odor, in Rem's earlier account. V14 p-024 B0219–0226 places the oath with Roswaal nine years earlier. V25 supplies further relative intervals for the long use of restraints and the making of the staff, in a later present. It supplies no exact synchronization of the first two clocks. Preserve the events and the attributed numerals. An exact birth date, a second attack, an edition correction and a newly remembered sister conversation have no support here.

The torn words in V25 part0015 do more work than a retrospective label like “Ram remembered her sister.” During the assault the reader encounters a broken call and black squares where a name or relation would go. Ram can recall the outstretched hand, the urgency, the horn flying and her private relief. During the later fight she calls the unconscious girl by name in ordinary present speech. Her retrospective negotiations with Roswaal likewise retain holes. Someone who can now say **Rem** can still lack the scene that would let her specify what Rem once told her. She can want an answer precisely because she lacks it.

## 4. Work, attention and voice at lower pressure

Ram names steamed potatoes as her cooking specialty (V02 p-011 B0598–0617). The reader can carry that earlier claim of skill into the later meal. Ram cannot recover from it why the dish matters to her. After a night checking the village barrier, Ram carries a basket of salted steamed potatoes. She puts a hot one in Subaru's mouth when he mocks her, offers another and goes toward villagers who have gathered for reassurance. Subaru specifically praises the seasoning. The same food can interrupt a tease, feed a hungry person and accompany a visit to frightened neighbors. At the tower Emilia and Ram prepare dinner together. Shaula praises Emilia, while Subaru privately estimates roughly seven tenths of this particular meal should be credited to Ram (V22 part0014 B0355–0363). Ram describes yielding ordinary kitchen work at the estate to Frederica and Petra. She singles out steamed potatoes, then wonders aloud why they alone seem special to her. Subaru, who remembers the sisters' old division of work, entertains an explanation he chooses to leave unspoken. His inference helps explain his silence. It gives Ram no remembered motive. V03 p-018 B0085–0101 and p-020 B0006–0014. V22 part0014 B0328–0350.

**Source correction for current model use.** RZ-V25-C311 and C516 inherit V22's description of a potato-cooking defect. Neither cooking failure nor an inability to recognize a bad dish occurs at the cited V22 meal. The relevant contrast is between ordinary duties she usually leaves to other household workers and a dish she picks out as special without knowing why. V02 gives Ram’s own designation of a specialty. The V03 sample gives an actual eater's favorable judgment. Neither establishes her favorite food. Distinguish an unknown personal significance from a defect. Use that qualification also for RZ-K-854, RZ-I-447, RZ-RT-483 and the master ledger §10 V22 row ‘Ram, cooking/work choice.’ The frozen rows preserve what the earlier analysis said. The current source-facing revision must adjudicate C311 and C516 individually at V26 and reconcile all four projections. The correction does not establish the origin of Ram's attachment to the dish, the authorship of every potato she distributes or the private reason for conceding other work to Rem.

**Learning, hospitality and privacy.** Ram can teach a script that will let a colleague shop or leave a message. A tired teacher falls asleep. An invited or familiar guest can get conversation after work, contact at the door and an abrupt end to the visit. She can let Subaru's thanks pass unheard, be attentive to what Petra would lose if someone displaced her work, and insist on giving the household's witches and books less conversational access than the kingdom's familiar story of the dragon. These acts allocate time and privacy. They do not establish a favorite tea or an exact weekly timetable. V02 p-016 B0330–0425 and p-019 B0240–0373, V16 p-006 B0030–0039.

**Talk without a remedy.** V21 Ram would like to speak with Rem after she wakes even if they cannot remember their shared past. She would rather make her own reunion than have Subaru stage it for her. After the victory she imagines filling the missing yesterdays together, talking night after night. She tells the silent Rem that she loves her. The narrator brings her words close to Lye’s interrupted declaration and marks their different resonance. V25 part0015 B0581–0592. Earlier refusal of a secondhand reminiscence and later desire for conversation agree about who should have a turn. The second scene follows an act in which Rem could have no turn at all. Braiding these two wishes together as recovered memory would discard what makes them poignant. V21 part0010 B0612–0630, V25 part0015 B0572–0586.

**Working with Emilia.** Ram can apologize to Emilia for unintended familiarity. Emilia explicitly welcomes the freedom she has seen Ram grant Subaru. Ram says the once fragile glass ornament now resembles one spun from sugar. Emilia hears a possibly delicious object. The exchange ends with Ram's shoulders easing. In another room she prepares dinner alongside Emilia while keeping flame out of Emilia's assignment. After the loss of Emilia's name, Ram treats the pain of trying to place her as evidence that the stranger mattered. Emilia introduces herself and the two agree to act together. Later Ram’s gust adds height to Emilia’s ascent and diverts a potentially fatal head blow. Emilia tucks her knees, the dragon’s tail grazes her toes and sends her spinning, and Emilia checks the spin with her own ice. Emilia reports recognition from Ram to the others. Each of apology, relaxation, cooperation, active aid and recovered recognition has a separate witness. RZ-V25-C717 and its white-scar row in the V25 deep reading compress the gust into complete clearance. Apply the observed toe contact and Emilia’s own braking to RZ-K-1032 and RZ-M-659 as well. The lifesaving intervention in C718 is well supported. Adjudicate C717 at V26 while retaining the old formulations. During the tower experiment the description that makes Emilia something like a friend or a troublesome younger sister to Ram is Subaru's perspective. V21 part0011 B0276–0292, V22 part0014 B0300–0342, V25 part0012 B0153–0175 and B0229–0237, part0016 B0574–0600, part0018 B0040–0077.

**Selecting the interlocutor.** The artificial Echidna observes that Ram's manner changes once Ram knows who speaks through Anastasia's body. The operator tells Subaru and Meili that Ram waited while they were gone. Ram's practical concern can reach them as someone else's report. Later it is human Anastasia who reports having watched Rem while Ram went to help Emilia. Keep the body, the speaker, the witnessed task and the person receiving the report distinct. Similarly Shaula's disappearance reaches Ram through an account. Ram can dislike the way Shaula spoke and sincerely object to what Subaru makes of her loss. Meili's “like siblings” judgment about Subaru and Ram belongs to Meili. It creates no literal household kinship. V24 part0010 B0383–0395, V25 part0018 B0182–0209, B0219–0262.

### Japanese choices that change the reading

- In V02 p-019 B0363–0373 a plain refusal cuts off an unwelcome subject. Ram then calls Subaru **お客様**, “guest,” in the course of concluding the visit, and turns back to make a request about what he tells Rem. One exchange goes through withdrawal, public service and sisterly gatekeeping.
- In V11 p-003 B0092–0099 the request to leave the sisters alone is phrased as a question. In V21 part0011 B0251–0268 **お願いだから** introduces a direct plea to stay with the expedition. Her later clipped acknowledgment says she has heard Subaru. It does not promise he may overrule every decision concerning her body.
- V03 p-021 B0086–0094 to p-023 B0001–0004 and V25 part0015 B0062–0070 end with virtually the same thought, **やっと折れてくれた**, “at last it broke.” In the first passage the person who sees, remembers and feels guilt is Rem. In the second the speaker of the interior thought is Ram and a lifelong destructive voice has fallen silent. Neither hears the other's thought. The reader's recognition of the repeated sentence changes the significance of a shared image.
- When V16 Subaru says **姉様**, the narration registers Ram's unsettled eyes. In V25 part0015 B0193–0221 Lye takes the sister address into a stolen voice. Ram had looked forward to the first time the waking Rem would address her. For a moment the imitation disables her even though the physical Rem is elsewhere. Ram subsequently identifies the sleeper through their connection. Pronouncing the right address does not confer the right identity.
- **馬鹿ね**, “fool,” has two explicitly different implications within Ram’s focal passage. The second is about the distant Subaru, who does not hear it. V25 part0015 B0377–0385 explicitly differentiates what the same words mean in relation to Lye and to Subaru. Later Ram includes her own vulnerability when she tells Subaru to weep for Shaula and adds **ラムもそうだから**, “because I would feel that way too.” By then a quiet forehead flick suffices. V25 part0018 B0249–0258.
- The warning **レムが泣くわよ**, “Rem will cry,” is addressed to Subaru at multiple levels of access. In the V21 left passage he hears and reacts. When Ram releases her third restraint in V25 part0013 B0214–0225, the text expressly makes the words inaudible to him. At part0018 B0374–0378 her urgent voice actually reaches him across the shadow. The formula draws on Ram's idea of Rem. Rem has said none of these lines.

### Ability, belief and what it costs to be right

A twin connection already has differentiated testimony before the erasure. V05 Rem describes vague affect and on some occasions strongly intended words. What she experiences varies with distance and the state of the participants. Ram's borrowed sight is a different channel. In V21 a faint link suffices to tell Ram that the sleeping Rem lives. She expressly denies knowing where. In V25 Ram can see with one eye through Lye and with the other keep her own view, until fatigue blurs the combination. His physical changes cease to be an adequate disguise while the match holds. When he cuts his wrists and writes on the wall, he deliberately looks at the writing. His voice cannot reach her that way. Ram describes what she sees as harassment. The Japanese gives the reader no wording of that inscription. The original illustration at V25 part0015 B0538, between his interrupted utterance and the return to Ram's viewpoint, shows the end of the attack. It supplies no legible remedy or text of the message. V05 p-013 B0059–0067, V21 part0013 B0059–0067, V25 part0015 B0073–0095 and B0506–0554.

Subaru receives part of Ram's daily physical distress and learns something of the cost of her ordinary composure. Ram receives an explanation of this specific power. When it weakens in battle, she initially considers his death, judges the returning strain too light for a complete break, and narrows the likely additional burden to Beatrice or Meili. The reader can learn of Meili's injury elsewhere. Ram has not been given a distant view of Meili. Nor has she heard Subaru's private comparison between her determination to improve the future and his capacity to change the past. Identify which of the two has actually felt, seen or been told what. V25 part0012 B0276–0301, part0013 B0388–0395, part0015 B0082–0095 and part0018 B0279–0288.

When Ram tries the twin arrangement she puts a theory and an analogy to work. She remembers a library book which offered an explanation in terms of connected Od and whose explanation was dismissed as popular conjecture. She personally likes it. She has experienced the effects of Subaru's burden-taking and supposes that his ability connects Od in a similar way. Subaru’s much earlier private encouragement that Rem could stand in for Ram’s horn addresses a girl who feels replaceable. Subaru then insists that Rem herself is irreplaceable and explicitly admits he does not know the full circumstances. That V03 p-027 B0178–0187 conversation supplies neither a technique Ram heard nor advance consent to the tower experiment. She can imitate this effect **only with Rem**, through the twins' pre-existing connection. The experiment works in an identifiable direction and costs both women. During the emergency Ram briefly wishes she still had her horn, then corrects herself about what she actually has in hand. That moment of need coexists with her childhood relief and with her later judgment that losing it helped her become who she is (part0015 B0242–0250 and B0408–0416). Afterward the shared activation is released and physical backlash for Rem is stated. Ram expects her own. No general healing law or common origin of every Authority follows. Subaru's later transfer to a willing participant belongs to a distinct operation. V25 part0015 B0358–0390 and B0568–0570, part0016 B0211–0227 and B0273–0297.

Ram also reasons that Lye would have offered a cure to save his life had he known one. She watches him try to use his siblings as leverage. She judges him and acts. Ram considers that a brother genuinely risking himself for his siblings might have moved her to mercy. She judges that Lye instead used them as bait to escape. It is the difference in conduct that matters. His actual knowledge of every remedy exceeds the scope of the inference. In E029 his death by white shots precedes Ram's explicit report of continued nonrecognition. After the E046 killing, Emilia has regained public recognition while Rem and Julius are still forgotten by the companions tested. The return of Emilia's name is observed unevenly across witnesses. Julius himself leaves the cause open. Never substitute an expedition member's hope for a verified universal restitution. V24 part0013 B0236–0254, B0297–0321, B0517–0527, V25 part0015 B0539–0554 and part0018 B0040–0077, B0124–0127.

### Counterreadings and reassessment

Ram sometimes sounds as though she thinks herself entirely transparent to herself. The woman who recognizes the likely size of the returning physical load is a formidable witness to her own body. The woman who wonders why one ordinary dish is special and the woman who concedes she might be flattering Roswaal have explicitly named gaps. Confidence in one domain is poor evidence about the other. More direct evidence of a remembered episode or a received disclosure could change a specific judgment. It would not erase the earlier gap.

Reading Ram as simply obedient explains the material dependence, her pride in being useful to Roswaal and her desire for his trust. It fails on the book, her chosen departure and her readiness to admit his actions merit consequences. Reading her as having become independent of him altogether fails on the ordered daily adjustment, her declared love and the continuing power of their pact. Reading every insult as care fails on the villagers' constraint, Emilia's reprimand, her treatment of the captive and the hurt she produces in the tunnel. Reading every insult as cruelty would erase a weary applicant's plea, practical estate work, Subaru's relief and her rare disclosure over Shaula. A useful future test would compare Ram's choice under pressure when a formerly unresponsive person can now give or refuse an answer. V25 has not given us that conversation.

For an earlier request choose the exact bodily and remembered state in §2 and the named receiver, then apply the relevant row from §3. Use the full V01–V15 predecessor for questions confined to that earlier horizon. For a present reply after the tower separation, the unavailable scene limits the answer to established history. IF routes, the web novel, anime performance, adaptations, side stories and V26 or later supply no evidence for this model. A voice actor's timing, an alternative biography or a subsequent repair cannot set the cause of an earlier decision. Exact food favorites, unreported private dialogue, a diagnosis, the patient's forgiveness and future romance remain open unless the selected Japanese source actually establishes them.
