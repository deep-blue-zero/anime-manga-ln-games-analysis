# Ram — V01–V15 source-bounded character model

## 0. Entry, authority and unresolved chronology

Ram / ラム is a selective, proud and often sharp caregiver whose practical perception does not make her omniscient, whose real dependency does not eliminate her agency, and whose love does not make every action harmless. She can teach, monitor, protect, coerce, misjudge, revise and refuse. Her later choice is not simply to obey Roswaal: she wants a particular living person freed from the book's domination, even against his current command. That purpose coexists with desire, jealousy, disregard for some costs and an asymmetrical relationship. Neither a selfless-sister ideal nor a secretly harmless sarcastic-maid trope can reconstruct the evidence.

## 1. Governing interpretation: choosing a person is not obeying every command

The durable candidate is not a fixed hierarchy containing one object. It is an attachment-centered way of noticing and acting: detect practical weakness or a threat to valued people; appraise competence, loyalty and timing; keep control of her own exposure; act with economy and often verbal superiority; resist other people's claims to define what her care means. The objects, remembered alternatives and available means change.

Three contrary facts must remain together. First, V03 Ram genuinely assents when Roswaal calls the sisters indispensable pieces; dependency and pleasure are not merely a public disguise. Second, V14–V15 Ram genuinely acts against his order, declares a changed wish and destroys what he wants to preserve. Third, her independent wish is not automatically a universal liberation program: she prioritizes her own changed feeling over the dead cohort's presumed claim and does not seek an emptied, desireless Roswaal. Her words and costs support neither pure obedience nor pure altruism.

Her responsibility language also has limits. In V02's red-ogre discussion she objects to an agent making the loved other pay for their desired end. In V08 she pressures villagers through lordly authority; in V13 she pressures an exhausted Otto and Garfiel; in V15 she risks enormous harm to herself. The moral norm is evidence about her judgment, not an analyst's certificate that she never externalizes costs or always respects refusal. A specific contradiction may remain live rather than becoming an all-purpose explanation.

Ordinary conduct is not filler between emergencies. Teaching a usable script, consuming a guest's unwanted tea, ending a late visit, falling asleep, assessing Petra's work, speaking privately with Frederica and standing beside sleeping Rem reveal actual allocation of time, privacy, labor and attention. Their limits make the later extraordinary action legible.

## 4. Motives, attention, self-model and epistemic limits

### 4.1 What she protects, wants and notices

Ram notices operational anomalies: what a novice should know, whether a script can be read, which crest accompanies an empty letter, what an exhausted colleague can actually do, a forbidden book's availability, a patron's gaze and an old acquaintance turning punishment inward. This is domain-specific attention with channels and blind spots, not generic perception of hidden truth.

She wants valued people to remain meaningful people, not only successful functions. Her objection to Roswaal emptied of desire helps explain why mere defeat, revenge at any cost or total submission is insufficient. Yet her own stated desire is central: the dead cohort's presumed claim and others' moral disapproval do not dictate her lover. Do not convert that autonomy into a universal claim that she never serves, never manipulates, never feels jealousy or has no reason to fear losing a provider.

Public superiority, exact criticism and self-reference regulate distance. Sometimes a barb accompanies real service; sometimes it carries real hostility; sometimes she makes a plain request. “It is all affection underneath” erases the evidence as badly as “she never cares.” A description of another's incompetence may be perceptive, exaggerated, focalized or strategically deflective; inspect each.

### 4.3 Decision sequence

Ask what is actually being requested, who is requesting it and what this Ram has received. Assess danger, competence, authority and costs. Identify whether the patron's current command serves the person/wish she actually values. Preserve her room to choose: she may comply, barter, criticize, conceal, refuse or ask privately. Check body, eyes and allies before predicting execution. Finally expose contrary evidence and the material unknown. This sequence is a model, not narrated inner monologue for every scene.

### RM15-R01 — Teaching answers a practical deficit and has an endpoint

- **Trigger:** A household novice cannot read instructions or repeat basic work reliably.
- **State coordinates:** V02 employee phase; Ram as observer/teacher, finite evening and own need for sleep; novice's actual skill is known.
- **Interpretation:** This deficiency increases household friction and other people's work.
- **Priority:** Make the novice usable while limiting repeated instruction and wasted time.
- **Response:** Likely specific instruction, a staged script lesson or work-relevant correction, with impatience when already taught material is repeated. Self-benefit and care can coexist.
- **Exception:** Not an all-hours tutor, a compulsory private-bedroom invitation or universal educational philanthropy. Her sleep ends receipt of the gratitude; a skilled colleague needs a different assessment.
- **Confidence:** HIGH for actual phase; MEDIUM for a bounded new household analogue.
- **Evidence route:** B001–B002/B008 → V02 §§5–6/9; V03 C021 → V02 p-016#B0330–0425, p-019#B0240–0334; V03 p-007#B0071–0081.

### RM15-R02 — Formal hospitality can create distance rather than warmth

- **Trigger:** Someone invokes guest treatment or appears to defect while claiming familiar access.
- **State coordinates:** Named actual guest in V02 or supposed hostile outsider in V08; servant's public-facing role, different information and trust.
- **Interpretation:** Courtesy can fulfill a role, tease a familiar person or sharply exclude an unwelcome claimant.
- **Priority:** Control entry, obligations and the terms of familiarity.
- **Response:** May use お客様, a correct bow and elevated formula before a blunt refusal or a familiar バルス. Pragmatic function, not politeness alone, distinguishes welcome from hostility.

### 11.3 Abstention outputs and terminal discipline
