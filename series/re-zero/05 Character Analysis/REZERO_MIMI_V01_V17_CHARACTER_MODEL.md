## 1. Thesis: forward engagement is not flawless understanding

Mimi readily moves toward people, food, novelty, recognition and immediate action. She can interrupt her own patron's unkindness, offer concrete help, notice another person's distress and turn company into something felt rather than merely declared. Her small body and light speech do not make her a decorative dependant: she holds an actual deputy role, fights effectively, coordinates with her brothers, protects allies and can formulate the better tactical boundary.

That forward engagement also crosses boundaries. She presses through Beatrice's protest, hits Tibby to interrupt a legitimate question, mocks caution in gendered terms, carries out an excessive corpse-disposal action and sulks when corrected. Affectionate energy is not proof of harmlessness or unusually complete understanding. Being indulged by other people does not absolve the action, and it does not prove she consciously engineers their indulgence.

Her relation to recognition is reciprocal but not a simple payment rule. She enjoys praise, boasts and values visible effort; she also credits Hetaro's work and Subaru's contribution. Promised thanks can be enough in a particular aid exchange without making all her labor permanently free. She can trust someone because she saw them work hard, yet that is a fallible social inference, not a truth-detection power.

Family is a practical and stated commitment. Her own account names the triplets' unknown parents, a finder called ローシ, and life with Anastasia and the commander as family. The inferred reason for abandonment is not verified parental testimony; a light telling does not establish a painless life or a universal defense against grief. Earlier behavior with her brothers gives the commitment both tenderness and friction.

Garfiel brings out a distinctive combination: companionship, actual affection, sensory perception, comfort, embarrassment, chosen distance, warning and direct criticism. Mimi explicitly says her love need not be returned for her to offer comfort. The model must preserve that positive declaration rather than dilute all intimacy into uncertainty; uncertainty concerns reciprocity, agreed relationship, full motives and outcome. Her real support does not cure his fear or make every contact welcome.

V17's retreat negotiation is particularly discriminating. Mimi recognizes danger, insists that the two of them are insufficient, proposes a strike only to create escape and obtains Garfiel's agreement. She performs that plan, returns when he continues fighting and criticizes his breach. The rescue is effective but her protection is not absolute: a later strike penetrates the barriers and leaves her critically wounded. This is an agent with judgment, limits and costs, not merely a reward or punishment in Garfiel's development.

A narrower rival remains possible: some early/later differences reflect different recipients and immediate danger more than durable maturation. The intervening year and later competence do not prove one linear learning story. The model predicts conditional action while retaining that unresolved causal distinction.

## 4. Motives, attention, self-presentation and blind spots

Mimi often notices the immediately accessible: blood smell, a hurting face, food, inviting company, a sibling's behavior, an opening for movement. E01's bodily attention and E16's scent comparison are real sensory evidence. E05's itching guess, E12's child/vocabulary errors and E18's admitted family incomprehension demonstrate that perceptiveness is selective. She can infer a consequential truth without knowing what it means to the person involved.

Her own presentation is expansive: named self-reference, boasts, exclamations, play, physical contact and frank demands. These can seek enjoyment or recognition without being a comprehensive manipulation scheme. A soft, nearly theatrical service phrase may be part of play; an urgent repeated prohibition carries serious tactical content without becoming an entirely different person.

Her loyalty is not mechanical obedience. She challenges Anastasia's unkindness, credits brothers' different work and sometimes interrupts their attempts to deliberate. The camp provides family, employment, expertise and protection. These dimensions can support gratitude and commitment without proving every decision is patron-directed, commercially optimal or ethically uncomplicated.

The foundling account has two levels. Unknown parents, the triplets and a finder belong to Mimi's self-report; the reason they were apparently abandoned is her explanation, not independently verified parental motive. Her knowledge of her own earlier life can predate telling it in V17. Analyst silence at V06 does not mean Mimi then lacked her biography, but the later disclosure does not license an invented canonical earlier speech.

Mimi's affection can reduce the demand that Garfiel be impressive before he receives comfort. This is not limitless emotional labor or proof that she has no needs. She demands release of her painful wrist, is startled by his later head pat, and chooses neither maximum closeness nor total separation. Her momentary concern without resentment is actual positive evidence; it is not retroactive consent to the grip or permanent forgiveness of any later harm.

The crucial professional contrast is between a social/cognitive miss and effective coordinated action. Misreading the Whale's behavior does not erase following Hetaro's cue; an intuitive hunch does not erase the near-hit risk; emotional concern does not erase the withdrawal boundary. Nor does one correct tactical judgment make every prior fear/caution judgment correct.

## 8. Knowledge, competence, care and failure

### 8.1 Knowledge surface

Mimi has ordinary senses, firsthand work, local conversation and her own biography. She can detect residual blood smell, notice a fearful face, compare scents and judge visible threat. ### 8.3 Giving, receiving, refusal and repair

Useful help does occur. The lip is healed, the barrier buys time, the sibling gets comfort and Garfiel actually cries with Mimi's soothing. Those effects do not need to be denied to preserve ambiguity about complete motives or long-term outcomes.

Help can also be intrusive. Physical interruption of Tibby and continued hair-pulling are adverse instances, not affectionate exemptions from others' agency. The corpse blast is performed, not softened into a suggestion. Group rebuke and being carried do not demonstrate an apology or completed reform.

Receiving help is equally real: requested mouth-wiping, welcomed hospitality, sibling transport, praise and later involuntary medical dependence. Sulking transport is a chosen refusal to walk in that event, not general bodily helplessness. Unconscious treatment is a different loss of present choice, not proof that her family's choice is her newly voiced assent.

Mimi can demand a stop and refuse a plan. The bruised wrist belongs to her; Garfiel apologizes. Her later concern is described without resentment then, not as permission for the painful grip. In danger she insists the pair cannot win, extracts a bounded compromise and later criticizes its breach. A faithful reconstruction must allow a warm person to say no.

Recovery is not one thing. Morning composure, play, food and proximity negotiation show continued functioning; they are not a permanent psychological cure for either participant. Her nonclosing wound remains physically unresolved at the endpoint. No model rule promises that an apology, sleep, affection, potion, healing surge or shared risk fixes every harm.
