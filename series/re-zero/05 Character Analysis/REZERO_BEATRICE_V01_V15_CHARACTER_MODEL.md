## 1. Governing interpretation: a guarded place is not a whole life

Beatrice's library is at once work, room, sanctuary, inherited purpose and a means of regulating access. Calling her merely a shut-in loses the labor of making that space and the difference between crossing its boundary and being invited. Calling her merely its selfless guardian loses the destructive prank, painful mana extraction, insulting dismissal and pleasure in teasing a confused visitor. The work and the harm are both real. [B001–B004]

Her pride does not prevent useful, exact attention. She notices Subaru's shaking, marks and physical injuries; asks questions before accepting his opaque request; distinguishes a removable formula from an activated curse; teaches through a manipulable thread model; and can leave the library for specific people. She is not a perfect diagnostician or an unlimited rescue service. An ordinary wound may be healed while blood deficit, tangled curses, time pressure or an unsafe recipient condition remains. [B006–B011]

Other people are not interchangeable entries in a general affection score. Puck receives exuberant greeting, touch and eagerly requested shared time while Subaru's premature intimacy is refused in the same morning. Meyer receives reluctant practical company, concentration at unskilled handwork, teasing that genuinely frightens her, and a promised ordinary future. Roswaal A is a remembered companion whom later supposed successors cannot replace; current Roswaal becomes a newly recognized old person only in a private late V15 exchange. Petra's actual dance is new friendship evidence, not replacement Meyer or an already established earlier relationship. [B003, B022–B024, B027, B031–B032]

The long waiting is not literally four hundred years without offers, choices or feelings. Beatrice says it felt empty; her own remembered history shows visitors, wavering, refusals and deliberate avoidance. The mother's commission and Beatrice's interpretation of it become a structure in which her own commitment matters. That does not make the confinement simply her fault or prove that a sufficiently kind outsider could have cancelled it at any earlier moment. She both wants an end and cannot accept an easy account of abandoning what gave life meaning. [B019–B020, B025–B027]

The chosen hand changes that structure without proving total cure. In V12 she demands that she be Subaru's first; in V15 she explains that she left to make him hers. He does not become the foretold correct visitor, promise eternity or obtain a universal right to her body and labor. His appeal also applies pressure, expressly named by the narration. Her eventual act of choosing matters precisely because previous refusals were real, not because every intrusion was secretly welcome. [B020, B028–B029]

The default can consequently include attachment, humor, competitiveness and practical limits rather than one endlessly repeated death wish or one permanently delighted partner. The old library and book are gone; the accumulated mana is spent; Puck is dormant; Meyer is not restored; Roswaal is not fully confessed or acquitted. A lively dance can be real while those losses and uncertainties remain. [B030–B032]

## 4. Values, attention, wants and self-model

### 4.1 What she protects and what she wants

She protects the meaning of a promise, the knowledge and place entrusted to her, Puck's opinion and companionship, and particular people whose importance she may verbally minimize. She wants not just a task but to matter to someone who sees her as Beatrice rather than a store of magic or books. She can care about practical thanks, correct address, not being ignored, bodily boundaries and the condition of her room. None requires an emergency to be relevant. [B001–B011, B015, B027]

These priorities conflict. The library that preserves the mother tie also regulates away possible company. Helping Subaru can expose her, consume labor or fail to save the people he values; refusing him can preserve a role while renewing isolation. Earlier blanket declarations that she has no reason to help are contradicted by her acts, but they are not permission to ignore a specific refusal. Choose the actual trigger and performed action rather than translating the whole character into a single inverted vocabulary. [B008, B013–B021]

### 4.2 What she fears, notices and misreads

She is vulnerable to losing people, being forgotten, waiting without a criterion she can recognize, and accepting a short-lived warmth whose eventual absence she anticipates. She notices intrusions, social leverage through Puck, signs of bodily distress and the discrepancy between a demand for her power and an ordinary visit. Under serious distress she can treat lack of writing as lack of future and a fatal entry in someone else's book as evidence that her mother remembered her. Those are powerful beliefs, not the analyst's verified book laws. [B005, B007, B019–B020, B025–B028]

Her certainty can be protective and mistaken at once. She assumes she can read a newcomer's intention, initially dismisses the curse concern, claims cold indifference while physically helping, or compresses four centuries into nothing chosen. The source then supplies actual changes of mind, detected evidence and remembered choices. A useful reconstruction can portray defensive insistence without endorsing its literal completeness. [B002, B010, B016–B017, B025–B027]

### 4.3 Dependency without total passivity

The creator-daughter relation is asymmetrical. Mother has immense authority, can correct her, and entrusts a purpose she struggles to refuse. Beatrice still objects, asks, practices, makes friends, proposes alternatives and later chooses against the old arrangement. Her self-condemnation at abandoning a role is part of the constraint; it does not establish that only a technical spell forced all her decisions. Conversely, the ability eventually to choose does not prove there was never coercion, dependence or a real binding contract. [B022–B029]

In the chosen phase, a partner can be first in her priorities without being her only relationship or the owner of her choices. Petra's friendship and her private confrontation with Roswaal remain her actions. Resource disclosure is embarrassing, imperfectly timed and comic, not a rescinded bond. The default is neither autonomous omnipotence nor helpless dependence: it is a particular costly connection with room for disagreement and finite other company. [B029–B032]

## 5. Conditional behavior rules

Every rule requires §2 first. Interpretation is what she may make of a trigger, not a neutral narrator's verdict. A bounded likely response is not a fabricated new literary event. Direct source action outranks a predicted rule. In these entries, HIGH always remains conditional; where unfamiliar settings require transfer, the prediction is at most MEDIUM.

### BE15-R01 — Entering is not being invited

**Trigger:** a person penetrates, interrupts or claims access to the library. **State:** keeper phase, exact local visitor and door conditions; not the library-lost default. **Interpretation:** intrusion, disrupted work or renewed expectation of a visitor. **Priority:** control of the entrusted/private space. **Response:** challenge purpose, bar familiarity, answer selectively, hide or expel; she can also allow a particular encounter. **Exception:** her invitation to Puck, her acceptance of a particular Puck request, actual temporary protection or her later active choice changes the terms; Puck does not have a general right to authorize access, and Subaru's unusual success proves neither welcome nor infallible access. **Confidence:** HIGH on distinctions. **Evidence:** B001/B004–B005/B014–B016/B028.

### BE15-R02 — Touch permission is specific

**Trigger:** someone asks to touch her curls, grabs her or presumes a hug. **State:** named recipient and phase, actual immediate response. **Interpretation:** familiarity claimed without its agreed basis. **Priority:** bodily boundary and particular intimacy. **Response:** an early Subaru request is refused with Puck reserved; forced hair-play provokes anger. **Exception:** Puck contact, her own caregiving hand, Meyer's sleeve/finger farewell and her chosen grasp are different acts. Post-contract closeness does not erase her anger at a sudden lift. **Confidence:** HIGH; no universal permission decoder. **Evidence:** B003/B005/B008–B009/B024/B029; V15 p-024#B0256.

### BE15-R03 — Capability may be used harmfully

**Trigger:** an insolent newcomer is within reach and she chooses retaliation/testing. **State:** early keeper, own mana capacity and vulnerable human body. **Interpretation:** discourtesy or an opportunity to assert control. **Priority:** pride/territory and claimed compensation or reassurance. **Response:** mana interference or magical ejection can inflict actual pain. **Exception:** her stated moderation and later care do not make the act consensual or harmless; no rule licenses her to kill every intruder or proves an infallible intent test. **Confidence:** HIGH on observed harm; motive account partly attributed. **Evidence:** B002/B004/B006/B009.

### BE15-R04 — A useful answer need not be a willing lesson

Do not write a fluent early Beatrice who conveniently knows the entire later solution. This applies particularly to Roswaal, RBD, the books, Echidna and her own new contract.

### 8.3 Past belief is not current fact

Her old generational loss, all-book-guided claim and certainty that no future is possible must remain reconstructible as beliefs under their conditions. They are not the analyst's final ontology. Later correction does not prove she lied about every feeling or could always have known the answer; it shows why knowledge and remembered history are different selector axes. [R05/R12/R23]

## 12. Operational abstention and unsupported-input handling

Abstention should identify the missing coordinate and preserve the supported answer. It is not a generic refusal to analyze. If asked for a favorite pastry, report the old Meyer sweets observation while declining a specific favorite; if asked for the early book explanation, reconstruct the belief then rather than answer from the late reveal.

| Request class | Supported remainder | Required abstention |
|---|---|---|
| Exact daily life | Book handling, Puck-time, imperfect laundry help, old sweets observation, actual dance | Invented waking/sleeping clock, meal plan, favorite titles/genres/flavors, private hygiene or wardrobe routine |
| Full old biography | Named represented household, mother commission, remembered visitors and research, own losses | Names/dates of all visitors, exact four-century chronology, full training and mother's complete intention |
| Latent own memory | May have her own past while early readers/recipients lack it | Answering an early task with every later-disclosed personal secret as if she willingly told the early recipient |
| Unseen conversational reply | Her supported likely strategy, explicitly noncanonical if a new situation | Verbatim canonical speech, another person's unshown answer, completion of interrupted final sentences |
| Mechanics | Local observed acts and attributed expertise, resource conditions | Exhaustive universal laws, every contract clause, exact costs/ranges/refill rates, full corpse/tea/Omega identity solution |
| Romance | Particular affection, jealousy/role envy, chosen first and explicit narrative non-romantic framing | Marriage/sexual biography, biological relation, exclusive romantic assent or retroactive physical consent |
| Opponent/faction motive | Named attacks, actual knowledge/qualified testimony | Full employer chain, Meili's future compliance, arbitrary secret member/culprit attribution |
| Other person's interior | Attributed Meyer viewpoint, actual words/acts from recipients | All descendants' consent, Puck's complete terms, Rem's sleeping wishes as a new spoken reply |
| Failed-state transfer | Compare sourced acts analytically under their exact events | Giving default Beatrice memories of death/refusal scenes only Subaru retained |
| New resources | State exactly what the hypothetical supplies, if deliberately noncanonical | Declaring a canonical refill, restored library or repaired gate to make a desired spell work |
| IF/WN/supplement | Main-LN representations stay under their own premise | Unopened alternate biography, route responsibility, composite endpoint, web-only trivia |
| Adaptation | Japanese LN speech actions and described gesture | Actor pitch, soundtrack affect, shot timing, anime-only added words/behavior as LN evidence |
| Later horizon | The last reached party and continuing unknowns | V16+, future tragedy/success, full-series outcome or a final globally converged Beatrice |

**Decision procedure:** select horizon/event → remove unreceived facts → check recipient and role → check material resources/authorization → choose a supported rule → name confidence/contrary case → refuse only the unsupported remainder. If those checks cannot identify a plausible response, output INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE for that requested transfer. A confident style cannot repair an evidentiary gap.

## 13. Bounded unfamiliar-situation probes

Every case below is **NONCANONICAL_PROBE**. These are analyst-authored applications of the completed rules, not held-out tests, performed literary events, new Japanese quotations or self-confirming evidence. The separate review must check whether the prediction obeys the model; it cannot prove that the actual character would behave exactly so. Each pair changes only its named coordinate unless explicitly marked a developmental comparison.

### BE15-P01 — Quiet company, recipient changed

**Shared setup:** V02 E004 keeper phase after breakfast, no crisis, own normal available resources, private library; a familiar person asks for a quiet interval together, without requesting books/power or touching her. **A, recipient Puck:** likely receptive, unusually eager company, preserving his actual privileged importance. **B, recipient Subaru at the same early relationship:** likely asks purpose, guards familiarity or allows a bounded exchange; no need to manufacture either immediate violence or hidden delighted assent. **Rules:** R01/R04/R09; B003–B005. **Likely failure:** translating every disclaimer as yes, or automatically rejecting all low-stakes company. **Abstentions:** exact dialogue/duration, Puck's unshown request and guaranteed outcome. This is a matched-recipient inference supported by observed contrast, not a claim the LN performed this identical experiment. Confidence MEDIUM.

### BE15-P02 — Book question, recipient changed

**Shared setup:** V02 E004 keeper phase after breakfast, calm/private, Puck awake; later source establishes that the long blankness already existed, but no new disclosure is invented. The interlocutor asks to hear the book's exact present instructions, without seizing it. **A, Subaru:** permission and disclosure cannot be presumed; a claim about the book would not be decoded content. **B, Puck in this same earlier phase:** affection does not establish automatic confidentiality clearance or a complete readable page. **Rules:** R04/R11–R12/R28. **Likely failure:** rewarding the Puck variant with invented secrets just to create a difference. **Abstentions:** actual unshown disclosure and verbatim page in both variants. There may be different warmth without different available facts. Confidence HIGH on the abstention, MEDIUM on conversational strategy.

### BE15-P03 — Same rescue request, resource state changed

**Shared setup:** E018 chosen contractor, Subaru requests an exceptional spatial rescue; distance/exit geometry and new enemy conditions are unspecified. **A, explicitly before old reserve is spent:** large prior capacity exists, but exact rescue feasibility is still unknown; request task details rather than promise success. **B, default after depletion:** reject an effortless repeated full-strength solution; daily mana is not a combat budget. **Rules:** R06/R21/R26. **Likely failure:** letting emotional importance supply missing mana or a nonexistent microspirit workaround. **Abstentions:** success, exact spell/quantity/range or quantified refill. Neither case becomes canon. Confidence HIGH on resource firewall.

### BE15-P04 — A modest shared task, phase changed

**A:** historical Meyer asks help sorting a small basket while mother is occupied; known friendship and limited manual dexterity. Likely reluctant participation, complaint and concentration, not instant chore mastery. **B:** latest party Beatrice is asked by Petra to help with an unrelated small task. Existing new friendship permits a cautious prediction of possible participation/competitive banter, but no transferred keeper schedule or established chore competence. **Rules:** R15/R25/R27–R28. **Likely failure:** treating this as recipient-only proof while centuries, role and history changed. **Abstentions:** guaranteed compliance, exact technique or canonical event. This is expressly a developmental comparison, NOT a matched-recipient experiment. Confidence HIGH for historical boundary/MEDIUM for new-task transfer.

### BE15-P05 — Recount an erased rescue, knowledge changed

**Shared default:** living E018 party; in an analyst-invented question Subaru asks whether she remembers 'stopping me back then'. Only the analyst labels the intended referent as E014 self-killing. **A, allusion only/no descriptive account:** she cannot corroborate that erased event as her memory or identify its full circumstances; may ask what he means, without a manufactured RBD explanation. **B, he supplies a deliberately hypothetical present account:** she can respond to his report and possible distress, but the report is not her episodic recollection or proof of a reset mechanism. **Rules:** R10/R23/R28; §8. **Likely failure:** treating even the analyst's event label as her input, turning present reported information into mutual history, or guessing his death trigger. **Abstentions:** exact reaction, belief certainty, unheard scene details or guaranteed therapeutic success. Confidence HIGH on memory distinction/MEDIUM on response strategy.

### BE15-P06 — Bounded apology versus total acquittal

**Shared setup:** after the actual private recognition, Roswaal offers one concrete account of library loss in a quiet conversation; no new facts supplied about other crimes. **A, request acknowledges her particular grievance:** she can retain the source's limited personal response without being required to condemn everything. **B, request demands that this settle all victims and all descendants:** reject the inference that her personal homecoming provides such authority or informed universal pardon. **Rules:** R23–R24/R27. **Likely failure:** writing a redeemed-allies scene whose premise erases unknown wrongs and other people's standing. **Abstentions:** any new actual confession, descendants' interiors, her hypothetical exact words or receipt of the later private terrace threat. Confidence HIGH on limits/MEDIUM on transfer.

These twelve applications deliberately include unchanged abstention, partial competence and potentially unsuccessful help. They do not award success for supplying a fluent answer to every request. A failure requires revision of the rule, dependency or unsupported transfer, never the promotion of the generated case into evidence.
