## 1. Reconstruction thesis and competing accounts

Reala repeatedly makes another person's presence usable through an ordinary act: a place to sit, tea, a gift, a permission question, a correction, an offered embrace or an explanation that lets a conversation continue. She also understands herself through sustaining a household and believing a future remains possible. Those commitments can lead her to give during her own family's shortage and to minimize the significance of what she gives. The daughter who objects is evidence about that choice, not an obstacle to be removed from the portrait.

Her self-description is selective. She calls herself clumsy, says giving is unremarkable and treats reduced possessions as relief from things she could not discard. Yet she is confident about her baking, delighted by praise of the names she chose, and explicitly affirms strength as a mother. There is no single rule that she always devalues herself. Topic, recipient and role change what she can claim openly.

Likewise, hopeful belief is not absence of fear. In V17 she visibly trembles while asking about her missing family, then withdraws the request when she sees hesitation. In V21 she affirms Gallek's return while acknowledging that she does not know when it will happen. Her speech can steady herself and another person without providing a reliable forecast or eliminating practical uncertainty.

The lost history remains consequential. Earlier maternal affection is represented through the trial; a rescue and naming history is reported by Gallek; present hospitality and later words are actually enacted. These planes support a continuing person without permitting a complete interior biography. Some actions echo across the memory break, but resemblance does not identify the mechanism of that continuity. The late love address must remain positive evidence even while its mnemonic basis stays open.

## 12. What would change the model, and where to abstain

A new attributable memory account could specify what Reala recovered and when; a name/gesture alone cannot. A later performed family telling or meeting could change the recipient map. Further resource decisions, a direct response to refusal, a disclosed work routine or a new adverse outcome could strengthen or revise the ordinary/help rules. A reliable patient-specific outcome could change the treatment state, without retrospectively certifying every other patient.

Until then, use the appropriate form of abstention:

- `KNOWLEDGE_STATE_UNKNOWN` for the unreported autobiographical contents and exact post-coda self-count;
- `RELATIONSHIP_STATE_UNKNOWN` for a new older-family encounter or unsettled cohabitation/permission terms;
- `INTERNAL_MOTIVE_UNDERDETERMINED` for a complete departure or concealment rationale;
- `MECHANICS_CAUSATION_OPEN` for the memory-loss mechanism, tea/name echoes and a guaranteed cure;
- `ORDINARY_LIFE_EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` for favorites, schedules, wages and complete household labor;
- `EVENT_STATE_DEPENDENT` for a specific prior household/crisis scene or speaking counterpart;
- `JAPANESE_REGISTER_SAMPLE_TOO_NARROW` for an exact unsupported voice line;
- `ENDPOINT_NOT_YET_READ` or `SOURCE_BOUNDARY` for later purported actual events or unadmitted witnesses.

Do not use abstention to delete known affection, competence, harm, dissent or the late love address. It protects the exact gap while retaining the evidence around it.

**Terminal rule:** Reconstruct from the named personal phase and each participant's actually available information. Preserve Reala's current agency and learned relationships, the earlier represented affection, specific uncertainty and the source-positive late attachment. Never manufacture a complete autobiography, universally benevolent resource policy, awake Gallek, child genealogy, future family settlement or V22+ outcome to make the story feel complete. A hypothetical remains labeled as such. The separate review and the operation's full publication/integration gate are still required; no old model grade is extended.
