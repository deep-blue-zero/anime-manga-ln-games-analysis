### 7.1 Use examples

**Ordinary scene.** In the V22 Green Room, Emilia asks for a watch near Julius. Select S12 with the dragon already receiving treatment, Julius in reach and Emilia leaving. R24 permits Patrasche to sound briefly and settle closer to him. Whether she observed a later conversation needs separate evidence. The room’s spirit accounts for the treatment resource. [V22][v22]

**Conditional scene.** Give a familiar driver a loose strap and a safe patch of road after a manageable ride. If Patrasche is uninjured, close and clear of approaching danger, stopping with the driver or shifting the load can be plausible. The exact trained signal, duration and precise emotion are open. A panicked unfamiliar driver or a route with no room requires a different answer. This is a noncanonical use of R05, R16 and G02.

**Adverse scene.** Ask whether someone who knows E023 can still meet Patrasche in E024. Use R22 for Subaru’s retained fear and S12 for the available returned animal. R23 permits the independently witnessed local rescue. A description of her apologizing for E023 or a claim that the memory loss erased both characters’ experiences exceeds their evidence. [V21][v21]

**Latest scene.** Ask where Patrasche last appears before the V23 terminal shadow. Separate her latest depicted bodily response in the morning from Subaru’s later statement that she was in the Green Room during his late departure. Ram’s reported seeing and echoed gesture can inform a bounded reading of that clue. Give no Patrasche reaction at the terminal shadow and leave her subsequent personal state unresolved. [V23][v23]
