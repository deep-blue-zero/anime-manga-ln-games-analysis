# Julius Juukulius through V25

## 1. The outstretched arm

Julius offers the six lights his arm on the journey to the tower. They have followed him from the city. There is every reason for a stranger to suppose that they belong together, until the lights hover and the arm stays empty. Julius knows what they once did when he held it out. They cannot draw on that history. Subaru can remember Julius and can call his tension ordinary, almost the way a friend might tease someone who fusses on every journey. Julius treasures the word “ordinary.” Almost nobody can make that comparison for him any longer. Subaru's company gives him something the blessing that attracts spirits cannot supply. V21 part0011 B0016–0043.

In the second layer of the tower the lights actually settle on the offered arm. Julius has wanted their old response so badly that the first result one might expect is relief. Instead he apologizes for holding on to what had survived, breaks the bond himself and feels it tear. The old intimacy gave him a reason to offer his arm. It also gave him a reason to take his hand off the decision. A new invitation follows the severance. The six hesitate for about a second and come to him. The interval matters as much as the light that follows. It contains the possibility that six beings who once chose him might go elsewhere. He can describe what it costs him to be prepared for that answer. He cannot report six identical private reasons for returning. V25 part0014 B0089–0149.

There is a different first meeting a few pages later. He kneels before the human Anastasia Hoshin, whom he served before she forgot him. She cannot remember that service. He gives her his name and lets this woman's judgment of the man in front of her count. She wants to secure him for herself. Her appetite for a valuable acquaintance sounds wonderfully familiar to him. His familiarity with that voice cannot do her remembering for her. The fox whom he has also promised to help stands beside them. The meeting has three participants with three different histories. V25 part0014 B0377–0425 and part0018 B0080–0099.

This makes a troubling earlier Julius legible without exonerating him. In the capital he insisted in public that a man's qualifications to stand beside a candidate could be judged by birth, preparation and standing. Ferris watched the beating and could have stopped it. In private Ferris articulates the deterrent, Marcos identifies the risk of lethal retaliation, and Julius explains why he took that role. Subaru, who paid the price, was absent from that conversation. Years of practicing a chosen ideal made him capable of standing when even his household no longer knew him. Those same years gave him considerable power to decide what someone else was fit to do. The distance between asking Subaru to earn standing and asking the six to choose him gives this reconstruction its central question. When does he use a form he has worked for to make himself answerable, and when does he use its authority to settle a question somebody else ought to answer? His answer changes with the person in front of him, what each of them can remember, and the cost of being wrong.

## 3. The cost of being the exemplary knight

**Who gets to wear a title.** In the capital Julius kisses Emilia’s hand in a courtly register. Subaru reads the gesture through his own jealousy. When Subaru later claims the standing of a knight, Julius's objection in front of others is political, ethical and classed. An unprepared man can bring discredit and real distress upon the person he says he serves. Julius also uses ancestry and the distinction between knight and commoner against Subaru and Al. The public wrong cannot be explained away as a later friend's secret kindness. At the training ground, the professional chooses the terms of a radically unequal encounter and inflicts injuries serious enough to require Ferris's care. Subaru's surprise can surprise a better swordsman without making him an equal. V04 p-021 B0116–0147, p-022 B0201–0224, B0264–0272 and p-026 B0006–0030, B0036–0057.

There is a second audience to whom he has to give an account. He takes personal responsibility before the commander, accepts the five-day suspension and hands over his sword. Ferris, who presided on the training ground, says the beating deterred other knights and says he would have acted if Julius had not. Marcos identifies a risk that someone else might have killed Subaru. Julius answers that Ferris needed to heal the injured party, that his own grievance made him a plausible aggressor, and that he trusted himself to carry it off. The desire to divert that danger, Julius's actual irritation with Subaru, faith in his own ability to control the encounter, and institutional damage are all material. The person who suffered the beating was absent from this exchange. Friendship later gives Subaru grounds for entrusting something precious to Julius. It never requires Subaru to say that the pain was deserved. The private account also matters to a future Julius who claims to be quite certain what another person needs.

His professional standing has real content. Reinhard attests to his ability and standing under Marcos. A younger Joshua goes much further in anticipating what brilliant future his brother deserves. A brother's pride is a poor source for promotion orders. Julius can explain why a realm with disrupted trade would care about the Hoshin Company's international connections. In V25 he gives more intimate reasons for his own eventual service. A wish to set himself alongside Reinhard was one inducement. Narration says he would still have wanted a share in Anastasia's dream without it. Early institutional reasoning, ambition and freely given allegiance can coexist. V04 p-019 B0194–0205, B0238–0255, V16 p-005 B0017–0038, V25 part0014 B0200–0216.

**What an apology can buy.** When Subaru has helped achieve what Julius had underestimated him enough to doubt, Julius can recognize the achievement and serve under Yuri to make coalition aid presentable. The alias is a practical role with the same person inside it. In the reciprocal exchange neither man undertakes to retract everything he said. In V05 Reinhard had called the duel pointless and supposed Julius regretted it. Subaru privately doubted the regret. Julius was absent. V05 p-006 B0143–0166. Julius owns his former estimate. Subaru can express dislike and real thanks in one conversation. This awkward, qualified settlement is more predictive than a complete reconciliation performed offstage. Later, when the use of his own senses and the safety of his body are at issue, Julius asks the former opponent whether Subaru can actually trust him. He waits for this Subaru's answer. V07 p-020 B0462–0490, V09 p-010 B0413–0444.

Their cooperation needs two bodies with different capacities. In the lost E011 operation, a hostile occupant uses Subaru's body, asks Julius to discard his spirit partners and Subaru, while still resisting possession, asks Julius to kill him. Julius appeals to his contracted purpose of helping him. Subaru invokes the promise to save Emilia. Wilhelm cannot reach him in time. Subaru turns to Ferris. Ferris acts first through the bodily connection established during treatment. Julius hesitates. His voice and the cold steel at Subaru’s neck mark the end. V12 gives Subaru’s retrospective identification of Julius’s finishing mercy and separately represents a possible aftermath in which he wishes he had called Subaru a friend. Neither the act nor the wish is a personal memory in E012. V08 p-023 B0044–0065, B0077–0104 and V12 p-018 B0254–0288. In the renewed operation Julius can apologize for putting Ia with Subaru without full prior explanation. Under Nect he receives another field of vision and suffers the cost of sensory sharing. He chooses to close his own eyes and uses Subaru's view to direct his own trained body. When possession comes in this course, Julius releases Nect at Subaru’s signal and calls Ferris through the mirror Subaru threw to him. Later he lends Ia and stays under Ferris’s treatment. **The other guests at the inn.** By V16 he can greet Subaru as a fellow knight. On Reinhard's family and the ruptured meal he has loyalties a professional designation cannot settle for him. ## 4. What a name used to do

**A younger brother whom he can no longer introduce.** In Priestella Julius follows the warning to withhold Subaru's name when Roy identifies himself. He can damage an adversary and still be quite unable to keep the damage from being reversed. His account after the city hall encounter acknowledges injury and escape in the black dragon and flood emergency. It offers no captured enemy. Earlier comrades know whom they are talking to. That condition changes twice, with different losses. V17 p-006 B0587–0609, B0677–0689, B0712–0725, V18 p-004 B0248–0260.

Ricardo teases him about becoming a son-in-law. Julius denies harboring that intent toward Anastasia. Ricardo knows him well enough to see strain even in his familiar movement of the hair. Julius admits that Roy's familiarity with him is inexplicable. He ventures the idea that someone from their camp has been eaten and neither man can now identify whom. His big companion makes the problem sound tractable. The face he turns away from Ricardo still shows dread. On the battlefield Roy calls him elder brother and supplies the story of a sick child, a particular garden fruit, an initial refusal and eventual compliance that produced resentment in the very child who had asked. Julius has no memory with which to answer this apparent witness. He feels an intimate word being spoiled. Roy ends by saying he hated his elder brother for giving in. The enemy is using access to someone else's intimacy to cause harm. His plural voice and declared grievance cannot restore a childhood to the man who hears him. V19 p-002 B0277–0329 and V20 p-002 B0299–0323, B0335–0344. Earlier in the encounter the narrator attributes to Julius the belief that every person contains good and circumstances account for much evil. He finds a living contradiction in the Cult and strikes without holding back. This makes his later insistence on preserving Roy for the victims a consequential choice. V20 p-002 B0197–0209.

Now other people lose Julius's name. Ricardo brings in the wounded man and is himself gravely injured. The missing right arm is Ricardo’s. Emilia asks whether the stranger is acquainted with Subaru. Julius can identify himself. It is other people's prior recognition of him that has gone. Subaru still recognizes him and tells him the name and relation of Joshua. Beside Joshua he asks which is worse, forgetting or being forgotten. Subaru refuses the ranking. Subaru is angry at the comparison. Julius is first astonished, then laughs. V20 p-009 B0549–0582, p-010 B0177–0179, B0198–0224, B0306–0332. **Four parents and a tense correction.** Julius tells his companions that the elder of two brothers adopted the younger brother's son. The child ran about in everyday clothes with friends. The adult allows that this child might be the raw person Reid wants to see. **Losing one's usual world.** On the road Subaru yawns. Julius has a standard of public conduct even for that. ## 6. What survives the last trial

**A witness may choose where to stand.** When Julius encounters an Emilia whose name he cannot remember, he takes seriously the very oddity of failing to recognize her. ## 10. Rival readings, corrections and use

A model that makes him a benevolent protector in every age must explain the public teeth and bones Ferris had to repair, his claim about station, his resentment, the secret assignment of Ia and the sword at the fox’s throat. A model that makes him an aristocratic fraud must explain why he can personally accept public discipline, take a stranger's evidence about a lost brother seriously, admit a clean defeat, and free the partners on whom his power depends. A model that makes him obey anyone he serves has trouble with the V16 command, advice to shorten cooking time and his insistence on a slow decision about Roy. A model that makes him an entirely new person after erasure loses the man whose habits can be read by someone who has never seen him before. The surviving interpretation has limits of its own. A beautifully formed request can still carry class contempt or shame. His renewed pledge cannot answer what will happen to his brother or to those he could not rescue.

**Two source qualifications from this reconstruction.** RZ-V25-C734 and its carried formulations compress the household into “commoner parents.” Julius grew up as a commoner with a commoner mother and a father who had left his noble family. V21 already identifies the later adoptive father as his biological father's elder brother. RZ-V25-C739 describes his use of Al Cranvel in the renewed bout in terms that can imply its invention there. V25 expressly calls it his independently devised art and says he had never successfully performed this full-body technique before. The new event is its first successful manifestation with the renewed six. See V21 part0009 B0002–0019 and V25 part0014 B0055–0063, B0222–0230. At current use qualify C424/C734 and the associated deep reading, RZ-K-1049, RZ-I-559, R176 and Arc 6 §9 for parentage. Qualify C739 and its knowledge, mechanics and institutional projections RZ-K-1054, RZ-M-674 and RZ-I-563 for technique chronology. At the next source-facing operation adjudicate each affected field against its original, retaining the frozen text and provenance. The pre-existing C736 wording tension has distinct evidence and stays open. The earlier Subaru and Ram qualifications remain governed by their signed reviews and master §13.

Use a dated source scope and the seven axes where invented dialogue would otherwise be easy. An exact return of Joshua's memories, a missing childhood mentor, the recipe, the unspoken thoughts of six separate spirits, a universal name cure, Anastasia's romantic intentions, the outcome of a wooden-sword match, and Julius's whereabouts after the shadow all require evidence absent at the endpoint. So do claims derived from anime delivery, game or IF routes, EX, the web novel, a later numbered novel or a global character benchmark. For a literary portrait, the empty then occupied arm, the brother one learns by testimony and the pair of first greetings provide an earned subject. The operational model, a separate literary qualification, the historical dossier, and the [local documentary assessment][review] answer different requests.
