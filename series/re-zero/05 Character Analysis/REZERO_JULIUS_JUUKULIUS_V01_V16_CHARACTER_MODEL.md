## 1. Reconstruction thesis: making care answerable to the role

Julius tries to make action worthy of a publicly intelligible role. Skill, bearing, loyalty, restraint and the reputation of one's associates belong together for him; they are not decorative manners pasted over a wholly different private person. His sword training is real. So are the spirit partnerships, concern for Reinhard, allegiance to Anastasia and capacity to thank or rely on somebody he does not find easy. Yet the same model must retain the harm done when Julius makes institutional dignity the measure of another person's standing.

V04 will not support a merely benign knight. Julius invokes inborn station and bloodline as evidence of loyalty, denies Subaru and Al qualification, and insists that good intentions without force or preparation do not make a knight. His attention to Emilia's unhappy face is a real recipient-centered objection to Subaru's self-appointment. It coexists with class doctrine, contempt and the severe coercive lesson that follows. A later protective explanation must not absorb these into an innocent misunderstanding.

The private post-duel scene gives more information than Subaru receives. Marcos and Ferris understand Julius's intervention as forestalling possible lethal retaliation from offended knights; Julius acknowledges role allocation and his confidence that he could perform it. He also accepts blame and initially names private resentment. The avoided killing is an assessed possibility, not an observed alternative. The beating actually goes beyond practice, requires extensive treatment and leads to five days' suspension and surrendered sword. Protective intent, personal reason, institutional pressure and actual injury are compatible findings, not mutually exclusive moral verdicts.

V07 develops a different kind of correctness: one can recognize contribution without forcing affection. Julius thanks Subaru's enabling role in the Whale victory while preserving others' credit. He works around status restrictions with the obvious Yuri alias, accepts a face-saving account for continued mercenary aid, and sincerely apologizes for underestimating Subaru. The apology explicitly does not retract every former word or act. Their spoken dislike does not prevent cooperation; it also cannot be silently translated into uncomplicated friendship already achieved.

V09 makes trust a costly distribution of agency. Julius asks whether the man he hurt can put him beside him without residual hesitation. He retains a reason for the beating but recognizes that his self-justification does not answer Subaru's experience. In combat Julius closes his own eyes beyond the agreed plan and entrusts perception to Subaru while retaining responsibility for movement and judgment. The result requires trained skill, companions and painful sensory overlap. It is not a convenient fusion of their minds, biographies or abilities.

V16 supplies ordinary and personal counterweights to the public ideal. Julius can eat Anastasia's burned food and then ask for a useful change, compete in flexibility, joke under a transparent alias, worry seriously about Joshua and accept criticism from Subaru. He restrains Subaru and rejects Heinkel's present order without being indifferent to the injury to his friends. In private he thanks Subaru for showing anger, is challenged for assigning that anger a proxy purpose, and accepts the image of a heart armored by knightliness. Changing the mood together leaves memory and friction intact.

The source-bounded model is therefore neither flawless gentleman nor exposed hypocrite. Julius's form can organize help, restrain escalation and make loyalty legible; it can also preserve hierarchy, disguise a need or make another person's pain difficult to answer. Development is visible in whom he can recognize, trust and learn from. A full renunciation of class doctrine, complete repair of the duel, permanent emotional ease or exhaustive private motive is not supplied.

## 5. Relationships change the decision

Subaru is not merely a rival to be impressed. He is first an insulting self-appointed claimant, then an awkward ally whose achievements Julius can acknowledge, later a man whose perception and judgment Julius entrusts with his body, and finally a peer/friend able to give unwelcome instruction. The bodily pain and resentment are not dissolved by that arc. Nor should source-local contempt be repeated unchanged in every later interaction.

Anastasia conditions public deference, camp interests and delicate criticism. Julius praises her presentation, preserves her face in aid negotiation and finishes the burned food before asking for a shorter cooking time. These show genuine service and a constrained way to disagree, not a complete theory of romance or an absence of taste. With Subaru, competitive correction can be much blunter; holding the same low-stakes problem constant does not require the same delivery.

Reinhard's humiliation matters personally, while Joshua's health changes Julius's tone and attention. Neither relation grants complete inner access. Julius can recount only part of Reinhard's earlier obedience and regret what hearsay does; Joshua remains a distinct younger brother with his own speech and choices, not an extension of Julius's attitude to Subaru.

The spirits are partners through whom Julius wants to become worthy, not replaceable ammunition. His vow not to discard them even if he is rejected is strong source evidence. It does not fill the entire contractual procedure or turn every expression of affection into romantic possession.

Ferris shares professional coordination, irreverence and difficult limits, but not a single moral response. In terminal V08 Julius recoils where Ferris undertakes the lethal intervention; in V09 they execute a different planned trap. Marcos can command discipline that Julius accepts. Heinkel, by contrast, has a real rank whose present command does not reach these three knights. Respectful address and a refusal can coexist.

## 8. Competence, pressure and failure

Julius's effortless victory over a novice does not eliminate later effort or vulnerability. V09 shows injury, sensory disorientation, adaptation, mana exhaustion and need for treatment. The stone-assisted breach of an enemy wall is an immediate counter to unrestricted cutting rhetoric. His spirit bonds help explain a particular possession exclusion; they are not immunity to every supernatural effect.

He can recognize a narrow expert contribution he lacks. Subaru's view and planning matter even when Subaru cannot fight as he does. That does not turn Julius into a passive implement: the unplanned eye closure is his own hazardous judgment. Conversely, he sometimes overcontrols help or explanation. Covert Ia protection, the V04 lesson, the V07 injury joke and the private family account each produce a different problem; they must not be compressed into either perfect care or one universal pathology.

Criticism can penetrate when it identifies a failure in his own desired conduct. He accepts legitimate discipline, the current objection to unannounced protection, and Subaru's criticism of emotional armor. Acceptance is not proof that every ensuing choice improves. In V16 he can regret unguarded hearsay immediately after sharing it; being able to state a limit does not undo the effect on the listener.

The supported recovery pattern is bounded cooperation: treatment, accepted division of labor, self-criticism, humor, or ordinary competition may make another action possible. Pain, resentment, institutional constraint and unfinished repair remain. Clinical diagnosis, permanent cure and exact distributional frequency are unavailable.
