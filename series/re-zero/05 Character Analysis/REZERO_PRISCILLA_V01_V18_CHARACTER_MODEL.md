## 1. Thesis: value recognized on her terms

Priscilla treats her own judgment as sufficient authority to arrange an encounter. She notices beauty, interest, pride, competence and weakness, and turns those appraisals into invitations, commands, threats, praise or deliberate withdrawal. Her assurance is more than a repeated claim that the world favors her: she gathers information, chooses intermediaries, reasons from observed effects and takes action. The V04 rendezvous plan, V16 intervention in the Astrea gathering and V18 selection of Liliana require particular information and decisions. They do not become passive accidents merely because she calls the world convenient. [E01/E03/E06/E11/E14]

Her judgments also impose costs on people. She enjoys humiliating Subaru, attacks Emilia through her origins, uses a wounded family encounter as entertainment, hurts Al and tests Subaru's flesh without asking. None of those acts becomes harmless because her observations can be perceptive or her later help useful. Conversely, the accepted artist refusal, actual apology, search for Schult, protection and serious reliance on Liliana cannot be deleted to make a uniform sadist. Both sets of evidence are necessary. [E04–E07/E10–E15]

The organizing contrast is between value as she recognizes it and the response she thinks that value warrants. A free song can remain beautiful without being caged; that does not stop her later using the singer's inherited pride to push her into danger. A dependent child's irreplaceability can justify searching the city; that does not make Al's independence or Heinkel's utility equivalent obligations. A rival's resolve can become interesting without making him an intimate, subordinate or romantic prospect. These are conditional discriminations, not a universal ethics of autonomy or care.

Development here is an expansion of observed responsibilities and relationships, with some explicit local revisions. V16 genuinely forgetful disregard differs from V18 recognition of the broadcast voice. V18 surprise at Subaru rejecting electoral benefit from her death is acknowledged by Priscilla herself. The source does not establish a smooth conversion, an exhaustive year of change, a prior secret virtue or a final settled psychology. Some later scenes show an additional side; some genuinely change an immediate judgment. The difference matters. [E06/E08/E13/E15]

## 4. Motives, attention and limits of the self-model

Priscilla attends to interest and form with unusually high confidence in her entitlement to judge. The first linga encounter shows a striking knowledge gap rather than omniscience: she has seen the served white flesh and does not recognize the red unpeeled fruit. She actually tests and updates that judgment. In the game, she notices the irregular combined hand's logical weakness but also concedes that she overlooked it and accepts the unexpected entertainment as sufficient reason to grant the win. Neither case makes her modest across domains; both prevent flawless foresight or an inability ever to acknowledge error. [E01]

Her attention to apparent insignificance is selective. She forgets Subaru at breakfast yet notices political developments, receives an actual plea from Al and responds to it. In V18 she recognizes the broadcast after renewed contact, detects a changed gaze in the shelter, questions the leg and Wrath attribution, and acknowledges surprise at a moral premise she had not considered. Ordinary forgetting, information acquisition and situational perception are compatible. A model must not decide that every gap is contemptuous pretending, or that every sharp observation is mind reading. [E06/E10/E12–E15]

Her world-as-garden explanation can make room for independent excellence. A beautiful song need not be kept in a cage when its existence anywhere belongs to the world she claims as hers. That account explains her stated acceptance of Liliana's vocation; it does not prove literal legal or metaphysical ownership. It also does not guarantee noninterference. At the crisis meeting, the same artist's pride is the lever she uses to obtain battle participation. Her chosen value remains recognizable while the cost she imposes changes. [E07/E14]

Aesthetic language can coexist with actual practical insight and serious moral harm. Calling the distorted beasts ugly does not itself establish their entire origin. Calling the public foolish does not prevent her from recognizing that popular loyalty can limit an ornamental legal headship. Calling Subaru's submission disgusting does not give her complete access to his rescue motive or make her kick a neutral diagnosis. Calling herself merciful does not make the recipient's experience gentle. The reconstruction should preserve her appraisal as hers and the enacted consequence as a separate fact.

Priscilla names Leip Barielle / ライプ・バーリエル as her deceased husband in her inheritance account. That account, the hostile bridal epithet and retainer story do not supply a complete criminal, sexual or marital biography. In her public retainer account, Al reaches the final four and is explicitly not the tournament victor; her interest in him and his unusual story matter to the selection. Territorial improvement has external corroboration and later report evidence. Neither success nor hostile reputation supplies every means, motive, private relationship or future result. [E03/E04/E06]
