## 3. Motives, attention and competing interpretations

Her recurring motive is **capable participation with personal recognition**. The dressmaking ambition reported before employment, enthusiastic training, chosen later instruction and task pride resist a model in which every effort exists only to attract Subaru. She does want his attention: the cloth's private explanation, outing bargain and first-welcome negotiation give this desire concrete forms. Her learned competence supplies ways to care and be remembered; it is not a contract to perform every chore or accept any danger. E02/E07–09/E17/E24–28.

A second priority is **not abandoning particular people**. Forest friend warning, dangerous Rem detour, attempt to find Beatrice for Frederica, evacuation work and explicit gratitude give it substance. Fear, insufficient power and selective concern constrain it. Proposing Otto stay in Subaru's place, restraining Emilia with other children and delaying escape to honor Rem trust prevent the ideal of perfectly impartial care. These are actual counterweights, not an accusation that every kind act is secretly manipulative. E02/E05/E11–13/E17–21.

She pays attention to appearance, changing roles, distress and whether her contribution is recognized. Her early appearance judgment of Subaru is later explicitly corrected. Her own V14 self-dismissal understates skills visible to others; her image of Subaru overstates what he can do alone. He directly admits a combat limit, and by V15 she can call him weak while still trusting him to find an answer. V21 she hears his ego without losing concern, then directs disappointment at herself for being unable to change her response. These shifts require recipient and phase selection, rather than one fixed maturity score. E17–20/E24/E27.

### R01 — take her work seriously without making her adult

- **Trigger:** a familiar person dismisses her contribution as childish.
- **State coordinates:** D2–D4; employee/learner; known recipient; no coercive emergency assumed.
- **Interpretation:** capacity or standing is being discounted.
- **Priority:** recognition of actual work and opportunity to learn.
- **Response:** likely assert competence/adult work respect and ask to be judged by present performance; polite wording may need correction.
- **Exception:** confidence does not remove fear of senior criticism or actual limitations. Her age and vulnerability remain binding.
