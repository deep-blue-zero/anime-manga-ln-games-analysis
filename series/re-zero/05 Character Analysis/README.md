## Promotion threshold

Create a monograph only when the available source record can distinguish most of the following:

- stable tendencies versus bounded developmental states;
- information effects versus personality effects;
- recipient-conditioned behavior;
- route/event-state effects;
- role/status/faction effects;
- crisis behavior versus ordinary-life baseline;
- self-conception versus observed social effect;
- relationships and disclosure patterns;
- contradictions and counterevidence;
- Japanese speech/register evidence where relevant.

A cast list, synopsis, or one dramatic scene is not enough.

Readiness remains in `../04 Longitudinal Ledgers/REZERO_MASTER_LONGITUDINAL_LEDGER.md` until independent retrieval burden earns another home. Prominence, popularity, or template symmetry does not establish readiness.

## Minimum monograph responsibilities

A mature character artifact should state:

1. source boundary and analytical horizon;
2. identity/names/titles/roles by source state;
3. goals, values, fears, attachments, and self-model;
4. stable and conditional behavioral tendencies;
5. knowledge state and major information asymmetries;
6. relationship network and recipient effects;
7. agency, constraints, status, and material dependencies;
8. ordinary-life preferences and habits when evidenced;
9. stress/crisis behavior and recovery evidence without speculative diagnosis;
10. route/event-state consistency and divergence;
11. Japanese voice/register findings when material;
12. rival interpretations and falsifiers;
13. unresolved questions and abstentions.

These are minimum routing responsibilities, not mandatory headings. A character packet may split current state, ordinary life, relationships, voice, evidence, or fidelity into separate files only when each file has a real maintenance purpose. Do not create empty packet scaffolds.

The first character promoted to `MONOGRAPH_READY` must pass the separate fidelity audit required by the reconstruction protocol before the model becomes a reusable reconstruction surface.

## Route discipline

Main-route monographs should not silently import alternate-route biography, acts, relationships, or moral responsibility. Alternate-route evidence may appear in a clearly bounded counterfactual section when it illuminates a conditional tendency.

Likewise, anime-only audiovisual characterization belongs in an adaptation section or separate anime profile unless the monograph explicitly declares a multi-witness scope.
