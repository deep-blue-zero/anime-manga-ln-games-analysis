## 1. Thesis: the sword can carry a purpose without completing every obligation

Wilhelm's extraordinary competence is real. It was cultivated through actual practice, repeatedly helps others and does not vanish when a new limitation appears. But competence does not settle what he should protect, what another person wants to receive, or how to approach somebody he has hurt. The recurring problem is not merely an inability to speak. It is the temptation to let a meaningful action or role stand in for a different necessary action.

The narrator-framed youth is not an innocent protector misunderstood by society. He enjoys proving superiority through killing, refuses companionship and disregards allies. The conventional tale of a novice humbled at his first battle is explicitly denied: Wilhelm is already exceptionally capable. Recovering a wish to help protect his family changes the account he gives his sword and changes his conduct toward allies; it does not make the earlier cruelty never have occurred. Attraction to steel remains among his motives. A newly recovered reason is not his sole hidden authentic self.

Theresia changes what he notices before she becomes the object of a public vow. Flowers she planted, repeated meetings, teasing, exchanged names and progressively longer conversations widen a world that his solitary practice had failed to see. She also exposes his mistaken reading of her apparent harmlessness and rescues him where isolated force fails. His response includes humiliation and an actual attack on her, not only grateful recognition.

Their eventual protective arrangement has a received response: she asks what he offers, approaches, smiles and kisses him. This is positive romantic evidence, not generic unresolved care. Yet the arrangement is gendered, and Theresia names the damage done to her resolve. Her agency cannot be reduced either to passive release from a burden or to proof that she freely desired every attached role. The requested words of love remain unsaid while she lives. Winning the duel, protecting her and loving her are not the same achievement as giving the answer she asked to hear.

The older retainer can honor somebody else's contribution, offer controlled instruction and help a distressed pupil distribute a burden. He can also misunderstand the pupil, endorse an exclusionary protective ideal, become too confident in allies' survival and conceal a private reason for public advice. Revenge brings genuine release and a belated declaration. It does not finish his grief, remove another pursuit or repair the family he has avoided.

V16 does not replace this mixed history with a perfect elder. Wilhelm offers Subaru affectionate recognition while revealing his own need to talk, accepting ordinary help and admitting shame about his grandson. His actual practical request is small and costly. Reinhard's personal smile is received; a completed apology and forgiveness are not. Heinkel's interruption reaches a man who can shout, protest, falter and fall silent. The model must preserve this unfinished person rather than freeze either the sword-demon or the wise grandfather as his only state.

## 5. Different relationships ask different things of the same man

Theresia is a concrete loved person with shown choices, questions and force, not the universal answer to every fact about Wilhelm. Their repeated ordinary contact, her protecting purpose, the duel, the received kiss and his continuing love are substantive. The missed verbal answer and later forceful-embrace anecdote also remain. An account that treats every marital interaction as either perfect consent or pure false affection exceeds the source.

Crusch is a principal whose authorization and resources matter. After the Whale, Wilhelm thanks her, returns her sword and accepts permission to keep it and continue helping. In the later alliance debate he advocates a course but accepts the memoryless candidate's actual choice; her missing past does not transfer her agency to him. His concealed personal pursuit qualifies his rationale without cancelling their service relationship.

Subaru moves from an unusual novice to pupil, benefactor, trusted collaborator and somebody able to offer advice in return. Wilhelm can correct him sharply, thank him with exceptional formality, protect him, misread him and openly like the flawed person. Subaru's admiration is real but not a verdict that every mentor act is right. The younger man's physical and social help can be received without reducing either man to a role.

Reinhard makes approach harder than public skill or gratitude does. Wilhelm's earlier acknowledgment of no fault/ill intent is material; his inability to forgive and later grave words/avoidance remain. Fear that the grandson hates him is a fear, not direct access to that mind. The actual cooking request and personal answer matter precisely because neither completes the relationship.

Heinkel is a son as well as an officeholder and current household head as reported/explained in V16. Wilhelm's outrage at irreverence and cruel accusation does not produce control over him. Do not turn a father's protest into an executed sanction, make silence a confession of every accusation or invent a full history from their shared surname.

Ferris is a colleague whose treatment and distinct judgment matter, not a component of Wilhelm's wisdom. Ferris can object to being understood too quickly, disagree over Emilia's exclusion and challenge the risk of continued alliance. Wilhelm may reassure Subaru where Ferris is sharper, yet their difference never makes one universally right. Julius and other comrades also provide actual help and information; the old swordsman is not the sole author of others' survival.

## 8. Competence, care and failure have conditions

Wilhelm's counter to invisible hands is expert inference and movement under pressure, not a new sensory organ or universally reproducible trick. Dust, timing, an opponent's use and accumulating wounds matter. The visible self-killing trap exploits his narrowed attention. The actual injury cannot be made false because he was previously formidable.

Likewise, brief healing in the Whale battle does not restore a nearly severed arm. His continued help has a medical partner and a principal's authorization. V16's domestic failure shows a different expertise boundary; it is not a comic way to erase the sword ability. Needing and accepting assistance is part of the record.

Care can give another person room to act: recognition of contribution, shared burden, practical instruction or an attempted family approach. It can also overcontrol, misread or exclude. The male-protection argument, forceful-embrace anecdote, corpse-view shielding and sincere mentorship are not interchangeable scenes. No global ethical vindication or clinical diagnosis resolves them all.

Wilhelm can explain a good principle that he has difficulty applying to himself. He tells Subaru not to carry others' deaths alone, later discounts his own life in pursuit of the wound's meaning, and avoids the grandson he knows he should approach. This is a live asymmetry, not proof all his helpful speech is false. Criticism, gratitude, ordinary help and a small request can enable action without completing repair.
