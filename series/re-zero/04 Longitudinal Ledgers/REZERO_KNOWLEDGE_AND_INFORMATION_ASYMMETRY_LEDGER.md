## 6. Disclosure and restoration safeguards

Before updating a holder, identify the exact proposition and evidence of receipt. Subaru hearing an instruction does not mean he knows its motive; Rem receiving an emotional confession does not mean she knows ten deaths; Puck assigning blame does not mean he saw the penalty; a room's absence of possessions does not prove every mind was rewritten.

Before carrying a holder across states, distinguish precheckpoint shared past, retained private experience, current enacted conversation, reader-only view and material artifact. A claimant may be sincere and wrong. A witness may forget without lying. An actor may have partial expertise without either total ignorance or omniscience.
