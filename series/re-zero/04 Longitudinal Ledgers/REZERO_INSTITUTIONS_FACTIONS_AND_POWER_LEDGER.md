## 6. Agency tests and live contradictions

Every agency judgment must condition on information, memory, status, bodily/mana constraints, time, money, allies, duties, stigma, credible violence and available refusal. In particular:

- Formal consent may be required while practical circumstances constrain refusal: Felt/Rom is the clearest V04 test.
- Equal qualification does not erase unequal treatment: Emilia has a jewel but needs force-backed access to fair consideration.
- A protector may impose harm while preventing another harm: Julius's intention is supported, but alternatives were not exhausted by the narrative.
- Scarce care can confer political leverage without becoming fake care: Ferris treatment and Emilia's payment remain both medical and political.
