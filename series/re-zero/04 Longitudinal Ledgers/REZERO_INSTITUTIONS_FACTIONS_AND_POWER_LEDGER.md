## 6. Agency tests and live contradictions

Every agency judgment must condition on information, memory, status, bodily/mana constraints, time, money, allies, duties, stigma, credible violence and available refusal. In particular:

- Formal consent may be required while practical circumstances constrain refusal: Felt/Rom is the clearest V04 test.
- Equal qualification does not erase unequal treatment: Emilia has a jewel but needs force-backed access to fair consideration.
- A protector may impose harm while preventing another harm: Julius's intention is supported, but alternatives were not exhausted by the narrative.
- Scarce care can confer political leverage without becoming fake care: Ferris treatment and Emilia's payment remain both medical and political.
- Greater force does not equal higher practical office: Reinhard admires Julius's knightly qualities despite his own sword supremacy.
- Friendship does not erase factional opposition: Julius/Ferris explicitly wish one another well and contest the throne.
- Publicly claimed origin, birthplace and prior residence differ: Al's geographical accounts need not be forced into a false identity contradiction.
- Explicit racial slur, clinical diagnosis, Witch odor and actual Witch affiliation are not interchangeable categories.
- Care can confer bodily influence as well as political leverage; Ferris's admitted influence and Rem's sedation have different aims but both require consent analysis.
- An opponent may freely assist after a contract ends; generosity does not erase a boundary, and respecting a decision does not certify its wisdom.
- A finite fee cannot buy unlimited risk or substitute for transport, route knowledge and the contractor's practical expertise.
- A cult leader's operational competence does not validate his moral labels, Gospel authority or assignment of culpability to a victim.
- Bodily maintenance during captivity does not erase deprivation of speech/contact; political reasons to postpone killing do not make detention voluntary.
- A freely chosen friend or volunteer can incur a real cost not purchased by earlier payment; that choice is state-specific, not a reusable entitlement after return.
- A truth oath may allow silence; a private planning monologue remains reader-only even when it explains an earlier public posture.
- Obedience under command pressure is not volunteer consent; promising later freedom does not undo the earlier bodily cost or make an infinite reserve.
- A true account of benefits can omit a decisive cost; forced disclosure by parties with different motives can materially improve a choice without making those parties one benevolent institution.
- Manufactured danger can exploit remembered shared care and social prejudice to redirect blame; a patron's status does not certify the target's guilt or the scheme's necessity.
- Loyalty, tenderness and a soul pledge do not establish informed advance agreement to a fatal instrumental use.

- A bounded admission can identify one commissioner without proving every old crime or every handler term; confidence must not propagate beyond the admitted scope.
- Voluntary friendship, self-imposed repayment and an unasked risky escalation coexist; the commander did not necessarily request or know the enacted plan.
- Care promised until morning is not fulfilled by a loving artifact made during the absence; received affection can leave apology outstanding.
- An examiner's hostility, a protector's imposed closure and a patron's life-over-will program are distinct pressures; chosen re-entry is not consent to all surrounding harms.
- Contract cancellation, new assistance and basic wound healing do not erase differentiated resources or supply a new complete agreement.

These are conditional current models, not permanent judgments of entire institutions. A future volume must record actual rule/application changes and counterexamples rather than overwrite the V04 structure as though it had never operated.
