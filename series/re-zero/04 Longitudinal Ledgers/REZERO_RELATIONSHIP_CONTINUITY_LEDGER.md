The source horizon is V10. Latest reached endpoint is terminal RZ-E-013/V10-E1 after death13; restart is declared without arrival. Select a supported preterminal scene for living interactions; no postrestart E014 response is available. A row's latest observed mutual scene can be earlier; absence proves neither erasure nor new receipt. V09's retrospective killing still does not independently timestamp every earlier conversation or give allies RBD. V10's generated parents are a separate interaction condition, not actual Earth receipt.

For each relationship select:

1. Subaru's retained history, if he is a participant;
2. the counterpart's actually demonstrated history or present inability to respond;
3. current mutually enacted conduct;
4. reader-only discarded, private or route-local evidence;
5. practical power, consent, dependency, obligation, affection, fear, rivalry and address relevant to the decision;
6. the unresolved condition that prevents a stronger claim.

Subaru's retained bond is not the counterpart's retained bond. Counterpart gratitude is not consent to every plan. A formal alliance is not personal intimacy; a friend address need not erase hurt; care need not be romantic reciprocation. A person's being forgotten does not make helping that person impossible for someone newly informed.

Rem's current body is alive and unresponsive. Her own present remembered content is not tested; loss of others' recognition must not be rewritten as her own demonstrated amnesia. For Rem's speech select an actually observed pre-ambush phase, not an invented answer from the bed. For the internal escape-family fragment select its eight-year flight premise; its marriage, children and exclusive vows are not the active route's terms.
