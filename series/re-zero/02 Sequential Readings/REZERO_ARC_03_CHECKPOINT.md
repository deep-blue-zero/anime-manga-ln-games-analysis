## 0. Boundary, evidence and responsibility

The arc changes the terms on which Subaru can help without making him permanently capable of surviving every loss. He begins by demanding that his accumulated sacrifices establish his place beside Emilia. He learns to ask, supply usable information, recognize finite expertise and answer the person rather than his imagined audience. The sequence also shows him secretly restricting Emilia, taking extreme bodily risks and actually killing himself after a later catastrophe. Its endpoint is neither a restored invulnerable hero nor the discovery that he has learned nothing. It is a more specific achievement: he can accept care for a burden whose remembered history cannot be shared, while the person he wants restored remains unrecovered.

## 1. The structural result is not a single recovery curve

The arc repeatedly separates an apparent answer from the task it cannot yet answer.

| movement | answer actually gained | answer not thereby gained |
|---|---|---|
| V04 ordinary life → public self-appointment → rupture | helpful work and affection exist; Emilia's refusal and the missing appointment are real | remembered sacrifice does not buy office, romance or exemption from promises |
| V05 warning → failed rescues → responsiveness | Subaru's danger knowledge is grounded; Rem can reach him and also oppose him | regaining speech and hatred does not constitute recovery or a negotiated plan |
| V06 refused proposals → failed evacuation/disclosure → requested support | false total accounts are challenged; a real request and specifically authorized offer become possible, its acceptance still unshown | private pain does not establish political authority; a new resolution is not yet sustained conduct |
| V07 coalition → Whale victory → next operation | specific mutual agreement, work and a real kill are achieved | erased victims do not return; Emilia has not received repair; self-disposability persists |
| V08 operation → costly trust → death eleven | allies' help, Emilia's defense and Subaru's limited agency remain real conditional evidence | a killed body is not final entity extinction; terminal witnesses do not automatically share the successor |
| V09 revised operation → received repair → separate loss → care | true entity end, actual apology/welcome and shared care are now enacted | Rem's restoration, full consent, reciprocal romance and a permanent cure remain unachieved |

This sequencing is itself an argument against treating a moving speech or a battle victory as a complete person-model update. The narrative permits a decision to be sincere before its limits are tested. It also permits later conduct to improve without cancelling the harm or information gaps of earlier conduct. Revisions must change the supported scope, not convert every earlier instance into either secretly cured behavior or fraud.

The V09 ending changes what counts as help. Emilia cannot validate Subaru by remembering everything he remembers, and she does not pretend to. Her actual offer is to care about someone important to him and share worry without claiming complete understanding. Subaru's acceptance is consequential because it gives others a place in the burden without surrendering Rem or demanding impossible knowledge. It does not demonstrate an available cure.

## 3. Subaru's bounded help/self-worth phase

### 3.1 He devalues real competence while demanding heroic status

V04 establishes wages, repayment, literacy, exercise, small crafts and village acquaintance as real contributions. Subaru resents their insufficiency as the kind of recognition he wants; that does not mean the village withholds all value. His competent inference and considerate conversation also vary with the situation. The later assembly failure is especially conditioned by threatened inclusion and romantic/status worth, not a timeless absence of intelligence (V04 §4; `p-003#B0033–0055`; `p-008#B0024–0067`).

V05 supplies a discriminating reception test. Crusch advises effort toward a contribution that can reach another person; Subaru turns the advice into an exclusive weapon that will prove him right. His later welcome of danger and wish that Emilia need him are actually narrated, not merely inferred from rescuing her. They are not the cause of the Cult's attacks. His embarrassment at Otto independently supporting Emilia is likewise evidence against a purely recipient-centered motive: an ally should help that goal, yet threatens his imagined exclusive position (V05 §4.2–4.3; `p-010#B0232–0287`; `p-013#B0255–0268`; `p-016#B0028–0053`).

These adverse observations do not retrospectively erase genuine attachment, fear or previous help. A total fraud model cannot explain his concern, ordinary labor and responses to actual bodies without discarding counterevidence. A total innocence model cannot explain agency bypass and unrepayable-debt language. The bounded model is a conflict among real care, vulnerability, recognition hunger and a learned willingness to spend his life.

### 3.2 A request changes the arrangement; it does not complete the transformation

V06 tests the punitive victory condition directly: killing Petelgeuse does not restore Rem, Emilia or their relation. Subaru's pain cannot supply bargaining authority or exact evidence for the failed proposals, while their different recipients have different legitimate limits and harmful actions. His offer to flee then gives Rem an ultimatum under concealed danger. Her refusal and hearing make possible a different disclosure of his self-contempt and a request for support/correction (V06 §§4–7).

The change is real because Rem hears his Emilia-directed purpose, can name his harshness and nevertheless chooses help; Subaru asks rather than merely presumes. It is incomplete because he still knows he is using her affection, still wants a place beside Emilia that she has not offered, and still hates himself. His sweeping account of wasted effort is not a neutral audit that annuls sewing, training or previous rescue. The later force of the arc depends on testing this new arrangement rather than declaring every fault corrected by its eloquence.

V07 gives practical tests: Subaru supplies a useful forecast, recognizes delegation, values Ferris's nonattacking support and hesitates to demand further aid from an injured camp. Ordinary grip practice, expert labor and animals matter. Yet he calls himself useless while recognizing others' necessary support, performs strength before Rem and conditionally says he would have tried to die had she died. That is a stated intention, not an additional death (V07 §4; `p-018#B0112–0116`).

### 3.3 Later failure and later repair both count

V08's deaths, lost wagons, possession and requested lethal intervention deny an easy progression from cooperation to guaranteed safety. The failed attempt remains evidence that Subaru can ask, adapt and trust under losses, and that those capacities have costs and limits. The new V09 attempt converts retained failure into handwriting Ram can read, living-host neutralization and a better plan, while the missed cargo exposes an explanation that had become too complete (V08 §§4–9; V09 C004–010/C029/C068–070).

V09 also tests whether he can answer criticism without demanding that Emilia supply the answer. His explicit admission that he used her to enjoy a heroic image is received; he does not offload responsibility onto a temporary person who was not really him. That is stronger evidence than the analyst inferring repentance from a rescue. Subaru's imposed answer to Beatrice and covert restrictions on Emilia remain same-volume counterexamples to a universal new autonomy policy (V09 C037–039/C045/C049/C074).

Finally death twelve makes catastrophic self-disposability actual again. The bedside point defeats his attempted reversal; it does not reveal that all future remedies are metaphysically impossible. Emilia's help reaches him afterwards. The phase therefore supports **conditional capacity for accountable and shared care**, not an invariant tendency to choose survival or a final trauma diagnosis. Its default is E013; earlier roles and pressures must be selected rather than collapsed into one self.

## 4. Subaru and Emilia: rescue, receipt and the right to be a person

V04's conflict already contains more than an uninformed woman failing to appreciate a hidden hero. Emilia wants a say in her life, keeps arranging treatment, requests trust and objects to promises being broken. Subaru evades the route through which she might refuse him, appoints himself publicly before she can answer and later makes his sacrifices an unrepayable debt. His memory of an earlier rescue is true, but her failure to share it is not ingratitude. Taboo restriction and his avoidant, coercive response are separate causes of the failed conversation (V04 §5; `p-011#B0029–0046`, B0114–0116; `p-024#B0170–0250`).

Her care is not beyond scrutiny: supervision and treatment arrangements can restrict Subaru's choices. The symmetry is not total, however. Concern for his damaged body does not supply his consent to everything, and her continuing care does not authorize him to claim office, bodily risk or unlimited understanding. Similarly, political discrimination gives context to her need for ordinary personal recognition without making his romantic specialness identical to that prejudice.

The intervening attempts do not automatically supply her with Subaru's retained development. V06's Emilia asks why he is crying and becomes the victim of externally enforced disclosure; that scene is not her active V09 memory. V08 gives her actual protective work and combat, not reunion words before Subaru dies. Its unexplained tear must not be uniquely decoded by later interiority or a guessed hidden memory (V06 §4.3; V08 §7; V09 C047/C065).

V09 finally adds independent positive evidence. Petra's concrete acquaintance counters Emilia's total isolation model; Emilia's own viewpoint shows low worth and anticipated rejection, not simply disbelief that Subaru cares. Her shielding/comforting of children and prepared magic remain agency even while others restrict her options. She actually hears an urgent declaration and then calm accountability, acknowledges the desire to help, welcomes particular attention and reciprocates touch. Subaru gives time for an answer; she does not make an immediate equivalent romantic declaration or appoint him a knight (V09 §§4/6; C033–039/C047–050).

The arc's last reversal is therefore not simply the cancellation of this repair. Emilia can offer help for the Rem she no longer remembers. Her response values Subaru's grief without claiming its whole history. New care is possible where old shared memory is unavailable. The result is both warmer and more bounded than a complete cure: restoration, disclosure of every secret and negotiated romantic/political terms remain unfinished (V09 §10.5; C051–053/C083/C100).

## 5. Rem, refusal and the internal family that is not the current future

Rem's care has distinct recipients, information and constraints. V04's household affection, sister warnings and service differ from V05's private critique of possessive comfort. She protects Subaru against Ferris's influence, teaches and works, yet later secretly induces sleep to override his demand that they travel together. That intervention is independent refusal and bodily compulsion at once; its benevolent end does not certify informed consent (V04 §6; V05 §5.1–5.3).

The private V05-E2 fantasy of escape is then rejected partly because it would keep Subaru unrecovered for her benefit. Her real refusal coexists with self-worth organized around exhausting herself in return for help. Her final cave rescue preserves his life long enough to act, but he carries hatred rather than a wholly received life-preserving lesson. Active later Rem does not remember that dying woman's experience. Neither a puppet-of-orders model nor an omniscient therapist model can represent these distinctions (V05 §5.4–5.5).

V06 gives an actually enacted refusal of Subaru's escape proposal. Rem can imagine work, budget, study, family and happiness; she could want them under a different choice, but rejects a flight that would abandon commitments and the self he still values. She explicitly cannot claim to know his pain. Her ordinary descriptions and the shared estate rescue challenge his total self-erasure, while hero/trust-chain language still risks making performance the condition of worth (V06 §5; `p-022#B0285–0339`, B0398–0401, B0498–0551).

V07's known-lie trust, disagreement over fatal risk, staged dying pressure, asked-for affection and accepted rest extend—not resolve—that mixed model. A second-wife proposal is conditioned on absent Emilia's acceptance. V09's attempted disclosure to Emilia is interrupted by nonrecognition; it does not complete those terms. Rem's final convoy action treats Crusch, draws ambient mana and chooses an attendant/hero identity before overwhelming defeat. Her living unresponsive body is not an available present speaker whose wishes the analyst may manufacture (V07 §5; V09 C051/C083/C089).

The internal V09 family fragment must be useful without being smuggled into that active relation. Eight years after both flee, Subaru and Rem are married in Banan with Rigel and Spica. Work, care, pregnancy planning, teasing and repeated reassurance give local ordinary complexity. Rem's anxiety about being a compromise persists despite happiness; Subaru's exclusive words matter inside that premise. They do not supply current spouse consent, prove that children cure dependency or erase the cost of abandoned commitments. The star hobby may concern earlier biography but still needs an explicit transfer bridge; the children do not establish a general oni inheritance law (V09 §9; C090–092).

The contrast is not “the active route is virtuous, the family meaningless.” It is that different choices make different represented relations possible, and real local capacities do not automatically transfer their history or terms. At the active endpoint Subaru remembers and wants Rem restored; she cannot answer, tested others have forgotten her, and Emilia can newly join the care. That is the bounded state the checkpoint must preserve.

## 6. Other relationships, communicated repair and protective power

### 6.1 Julius: resentment can become a condition of trust rather than its denial

The V04 beating cannot be made consensual by later utility. Julius's concerns about appointment, competence and harm to Emilia are distinguishable from hereditary exclusion; his protective rationale and acknowledgment also have different recipients. V07's reciprocal apologies are qualified, not universal retraction, effortless affection or a full rationale already delivered to Subaru. V08 deepens practical entrustment and refusal to abandon or immediately kill the possessed Subaru, yet leaves a further conversation uncompleted before death (V04 §§5.3/8; V07 §7.4; V08 §5).

V09 must therefore enact a further exchange rather than merely label the old one retained. Subaru names hurt and jealousy; Julius recognizes the limits of his own self-justification and asks for trust. The friend address and Subaru's invitation to celebrate are actual positive evidence. His continued profession of dislike is also actual. Neither element cancels the other (V09 C041–043).

The shared-sense battle makes this differentiated reliance practical. Julius chooses to close both eyes; Subaru supplies a viewpoint and still needs bodily protection; trained movement, mana, six partners and limited endurance remain Julius's capacities. Trust permits the other person's initiative rather than downloading his skill. The later inability to join pursuit and the still-prospective party limit a crisis-only model. No independently shown post-bedside face-to-face encounter is invented (V09 §§5/7; C012–017/C065).

### 6.2 Wilhelm and Theresia: a narrated counterpoint, not the same life repeated

V07's historical Wilhelm is actually a prodigy, not a disguised version of Subaru's weak swordsmanship. His cruelty, rejected comrades and narrowed attention nevertheless make force an inadequate account of his life. Theresia grows flowers; recurring conversation changes what he notices before a grand protective vow. He also discovers that fighting alongside comrades was never fighting alone (V07 §6.1–6.3; `p-014#B0107–0146`, B0203–0231).

Theresia has her own reason to wield a sword and actually responds to the later protective terms. That response cannot justify a general rule that a woman's martial burden is properly removed by a man. Nor does Wilhelm's duel transfer a blessing, prove she deliberately lost or supply the whole marriage's consent/history. Her later request is for spoken love, not only protective action; his deferral makes the post-Whale declaration belated. Revenge succeeds without recovering the living opportunity to answer her (V07 §6.4–6.5).

V08's limited marriage anecdote adds a countertexture, not a complete domestic model. V09's reopened wife-given scar supplies Wilhelm a new reason to investigate the Cult after the Whale dies. The bleeding trace is observed, proximity causation is his explained rule, and Theresia's present state remains unverified. It is neither witnessed resurrection nor proof that all earlier grief was false. His help can be genuine while his advocacy conceals a private stake; Subaru can object to the mentor's self-disregard rather than merely receive wisdom (V08 C068; V09 C057–058).

The comparison with Subaru is consequently structural, not biographical identity: power, rescue and communicated care solve different problems. Their talents, histories, recipients and missed opportunities are not interchangeable. The earned Wilhelm/Theresia scope remains V07's narrated courtship→belated-declaration case with a separate current Wilhelm overlay.

### 6.3 Crusch and Ferris: losing the past does not forfeit the present decision

The host house first supplies limited medical access, standards and duties, not unlimited forces awaiting Subaru's need. Ferris's concern and skill coexist with admitted influence on a patient; Rem both interrupts that access and later uses her own covert mana intervention. Crusch can decline an unsupported military proposal without ordering every past interference, and can later accept a materially different, specifically authorized bargain (V05 §6; V06 §7; V07 §7.1).

V08 complicates a universal clinical limit through Ferris's particular self-recovery account and actual inability to recover other destroyed lives. His enemy-saving efforts and distressed lethal compliance are neither unrestricted benevolence nor enjoyment of killing. The terminal request is not consent informed by RBD. V09 coordinates a newly nonlethal use of prior bodily access without making the old outcome unreal (V08 §6; V09 C017/C028/C054).

At the final meeting Ferris's helplessness is real, and so are his scapegoating of Emilia and malicious retort. Crusch, now without autobiographical familiarity, rebukes him and insists on learning and choosing. Thanking protective concern does not require letting it replace her judgment. An uneven apology and continued alliance are actual outcomes; cancellation was a proposal, not the enacted result (V09 C055–056/C082/C086).

This is not proof that lost memory leaves every value intact, that a soul has been empirically located or that Crusch already understands every new risk. It is a specific counterexample to the claim that memory injury removes all present agency. Ferris can remain distressed and capable of care while being wrong about what he may decide for her. The new relationship ledger preserves that phase separately from the older Crusch/Ferris/Fourier history; the names of old regrets are not completed by analogy.

## 7. Political power, civilian choice and ordinary infrastructure

The assembly establishes a political system in which jewel qualification, accepted service, birth, reputation, public voice and force-backed access do different work. Five camp programs do not become five equivalent personalities. Felt's acceptance under Rom-related pressure, Julius's office doctrine, Emilia's demanded fair consideration, Priscilla's humiliation and Anastasia's commercial extraction each require their own actors and conditions. No complete election procedure or national implementation is demonstrated by a speech (V04 §7; institution I001–040).

The later bargaining corrects a false opposition between pure compassion and wholly corrupt interest. Crusch's preparation is independent; profit does not make Anastasia's allies' costly help unreal; Rem supplies specific unfinished mission/authority rather than a universally transferable political will. Wilhelm and Ferris cannot be loaned as though injuries and current patients do not exist. Material benefit, delegated limits and received evidence make the accepted coalition different from the earlier failed appeals (V06 §7; V07 §§4/7; I041–061).

Money is not a substitute for other people. Otto needs workable cargo terms, route knowledge, animals and a livelihood. In one discarded attempt Subaru conceals danger and brings merchants into a coercive situation; current collaboration does not erase that responsibility. In V09 Otto's ordinary inspection of competitors' cargo supplies what Julius's enchantment screening and Subaru's bodily-formula explanation missed. The creature-conversation gift supplies a different channel. His actual catch-up promise and later qualification remain a textual tension, not a reason to silently rewrite the bargain as perfectly clear (V06 §4.2; V09 §8; C059–063/C068–070).

Civilian participation changes without becoming unconstrained. V08's rejected refuge, false cover and lordly compliance are not cured by a protagonist's certainty. V09's children have real acquaintance with Emilia; Subaru's appeal wins a chance to know a particular person. Ram's practical work and promised compensation remain necessary. Subaru plans to use the children's attachment, while Puck secretly takes extra mana to keep Emilia asleep; incomplete threat disclosure adds another constraint on her options. The children's genuine feelings do not remove those restrictions (V08 §7; V09 §4; C031–039).

Puck's offered containment makes the final bomb choice particularly diagnostic. Subaru does not lack every other life-saving option; he risks his and Patrasche's bodies to avoid frightening the villagers and damaging Emilia's political relations. The source supports that tradeoff, not an inference that reputation must always outrank immediate safety. The frozen Whale corpse and tree become resources; burns and animal care remain costs. Similarly, a report of no deaths in one current Cult operation does not cover the separately attacked patient convoy, whose half dead-or-wounded report must not become half certainly dead (V09 C040/C064/C071–072/C077).

Ordinary work is thus not decorative relief between the important fights. Literacy, wage repayment, attendance stamps, food preference, grip practice, driving, inventory, rest, medicine and animals make practical choices available and make persons more particular than their roles. The internal family's domestic life remains premise-local; the proposed Julius party remains future. These observations are sufficient to challenge all-or-nothing usefulness, not to manufacture a complete crisis-independent routine for every character (V04 §4; V05 §4.1; V07 §9; V09 §11.1).
