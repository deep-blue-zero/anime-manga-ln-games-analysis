# Re:Zero — V02–V03 estate-story source-verified checkpoint

## 0. Boundary and status

This is a bounded synthesis of the frozen Japanese main-light-novel V02 and V03 readings. It contains no V04 or later evidence and does not alter either prospective freeze.

The checkpoint is source-verified through publication paratext, not a fandom arc label. V02 calls itself the estate story's `問題提起編` and V03 its `解決編`; V03's afterword and preview mark a break before the main LN returns to the capital. No opened source provides a formal arc ID, so `formal_arc_id` remains unassigned.

Current claims and later revisions belong in `REZERO_MASTER_LONGITUDINAL_LEDGER.md`. This checkpoint owns only the V02–V03 convergence: what the problem/solution unit establishes when read prospectively in sequence.

## 1. Structural result

V02 first constructs an ordinary household life, repeatedly removes its shared history, and turns retained intimacy into danger. Subaru's knowledge makes exact replay impossible; the twins' care and surveillance coexist; Rem attacks him under a Witch-conditioned threat model; a separate depletion process kills either Subaru or Rem; direct disclosure is externally punished; and the volume ends with Subaru choosing death to recover a state in which both sisters might live.

V03 confirms restoration at the mansion checkpoint and solves the immediate incident through a single surviving branch. The solution is not superior foreknowledge alone. It requires trauma regulation, expert inquiry, controlled testing, village knowledge, distributed roles, reciprocal rescue, current-state trust, and finally overwhelming force from Roswaal. The mansion's fifth-day morning is reached with the household and all seven children alive.

The unit's central movement is therefore:

`erased shared life -> suspicious retained knowledge -> isolated self-sacrifice -> present-state evidence -> distributed survival -> mutually planned future`.

## 2. Causal reconciliation

The V02 mystery cannot be solved by one culprit. V03 verifies a layered system:

1. puppy/Ulgarm bites implant dormant curse formulas at the contact wound;
2. a hungry operator activates its own formula and drains the target's mana as food;
3. killing that operator stops the feeding, while its inactive formula may remain;
4. numerous overlapping formulas become too tangled for timely removal;
5. Ulgarm react strongly to Subaru's Witch odor;
6. the odor intensifies after attempted disclosure of `死に戻り`;
7. every inspected Ulgarm was horn-broken and therefore controlled;
8. an unknown girl introduced the puppy, led the children through the barrier, and disappeared;
9. a separate lure drew Roswaal toward Garfiel at the crisis point.

This structure retrospectively explains why village-exposed Subaru dies from depletion in V02-E1/E2 and why Rem can die instead when Subaru avoids the village. It does not erase the separate household violence in V02-E2/E3. Nor does it identify the human handler's sponsor, exact objective, or relationship to Elsa's employer.

## 3. Repetition, memory, and the ethics of current state

The estate sequence demonstrates that retained knowledge is never neutral. Skills, literacy, routines, preferences, fear, and bonds all change Subaru's behavior, so a “same choices” strategy produces different work assignments and social reactions. Repetition is causal participation, not detached observation.

The harder problem is relational. Subaru alone remembers Rem's teaching, promises, torture, death, and later trust. In V02, this private continuity prevents him from defending himself cleanly against a current-state attacker. V03 finds a more legitimate use: turn remembered patterns into hypotheses, ask questions whose answers others can verify, acknowledge current distance, and create new promises rather than demand recognition of erased ones.

This does not make forgotten history morally irrelevant. It remains the source of Subaru's fear, caution, debt, and insight. But the checkpoint rejects converting one person's memory into the other person's unchosen intimacy, consent, or obligation.

## 4. Subaru: courage without mastery

Across the unit, Subaru accumulates procedural and relational knowledge but no simple power fantasy. His body resets, then is scarred; his gate is weak, then damaged; his one independently cast spell nearly empties him; his physical victories require bait, environmental improvisation, gifts, luck, and rescue.

V03 makes the cost of apparent confidence explicit. He cannot sleep, cannot retain food, vomits, rehearses cheer, and treats every free interval as optimization. Emilia's nonspecific recognition—`大変だったね`—works because it acknowledges burden without demanding an impossible disclosure. Even after regulation, fear shortens Subaru's first horn strike and his confidence collapses when no one is watching.

`勇気` is therefore not fearlessness. It is fear admitted and action made possible by obligation, relationship, and shared capacity. The sequence also preserves a warning: Subaru learns to assign restoration and his body rescue purposes, then generalizes that logic into repeated “all-in” exposure. Success proves that the coalition can save one another, not that unilateral self-sacrifice is a safe governing ethic.

## 5. Ram and Rem: history, divergence, and mutual centrality

The twins' behavior becomes legible only when ability hierarchy, horn mechanics, settlement destruction, and differentiated loyalty are held together.

Oni twins split the normal two horns and are condemned as `忌子`. Ram's extraordinary ability saves both at birth but makes Rem's ordinary ability a constant comparison. Witch-odor-bearing attackers later destroy the settlement; Ram loses her single horn while shielding Rem. Rem's involuntary relief at that moment becomes seven years of guilt. She tries to reproduce “the life Ram should have had,” turning work, vigilance, and sacrifice into penance.

This history explains Rem's rapid movement from risk detection to preemptive violence and from remorse to solitary extermination. It does not make either action inevitable or blameless. V03 demonstrates that close observation can also correct her: she sees Subaru's effort, names and promises concerning the children, honest awareness of insufficiency, and rescue conduct. Current evidence becomes strong enough to outweigh—but not remove—the Witch odor.

Ram's role is equally distinct. She restrains Rem, manages risk, repairs the barrier, feeds villagers, searches at severe bodily cost, and defines sisterhood as a responsibility independent of comparative ability. Yet her first loyalty is direct devotion to Roswaal, while Rem's service is primarily protection of Ram. The sisters are mutually central without being psychologically or politically interchangeable.

Their survival turn occurs when hierarchical `姉様` gives way to childhood `お姉ちゃん`: correctness and sacrificial allocation yield to direct relational appeal. The checkpoint ends with Rem beginning to imagine a future not wholly organized as repayment. That is a developmental opening, not a completed cure.

## 6. Emilia, Beatrice, Puck, and constrained care

Emilia is causally decisive without receiving the forbidden secret. She repeatedly notices distress, permits nonspecific testimony, uses her resources to heal, predicts that cornered Subaru will cast, and instructs Roswaal to watch from above. Her aid is based on current observation rather than erased history.

Her agency is also repeatedly constrained by people protecting her. Puck hides Subaru's residual curse to prevent her from risking herself; Beatrice physically restrains her; Subaru would again withhold danger despite understanding the pain of exclusion. Care and paternalism coexist.

Beatrice supplies the sequence's clearest expert bridge. Her abrasive presentation coexists with healing, curse theory, formula removal, village action, and disclosure of bad odds. Puck's and Beatrice's protection is real, but neither their motives nor their contracts are fully explained at this checkpoint.

## 7. Roswaal, patronage, and hidden purpose

Roswaal is simultaneously host, sponsor, expert, rescuer, medical dependency, and interested strategist. He judges Subaru more favorably than Subaru knows, orders restraint around Emilia's political position, entrusts Emilia to Subaru, and ultimately annihilates the Ulgarm. He also controls the resources that sustain Ram after horn loss.

The terminal interlude prevents these benefits from becoming a neutrality claim. Roswaal calls Ram and Rem indispensable `駒`; Ram accepts that status as valued recognition. Emilia's candidacy must succeed for his own purpose, which he states as `龍を殺す`. The exact Dragon, motive, means, and Emilia's knowledge remain open.

Estate patronage extends to Arlam village through protection, barrier maintenance, food, investigation, and accountability. Villagers, in turn, search, lend equipment, carry children, and give the small resources that make rescue possible. The result is unequal but reciprocal practical agency, not a one-way lord/saved-subject model.

## 8. Ordinary life as causal infrastructure

The unit repeatedly makes low-stakes life carry high-stakes weight:

- chores and literacy expose retained competence and create observable character evidence;
- sewing, cooking, teaching, teasing, and food preparation keep surveillance from becoming the whole relationship;
- a village outing supplies both the curse vector and the knowledge needed to value individual children;
- radio calisthenics create a promise that makes indecision morally costly;
- children's gifts include the `ボッコの実` that lets Subaru move after Shamac;
- a discarded haircut promise is rebuilt through current-state consent;
- hand-holding and a proposed date turn survival into mutually remembered future time.

Ordinary life is not protected from crisis; it is the network through which crisis can be understood and survived.

## 9. Checkpoint findings and limits

At this checkpoint, the strongest stable conclusions are:

- the mansion checkpoint is confirmed and can support a surviving fifth-day branch;
- restoration preserves memory/learning while erased mutual history remains ethically asymmetric;
- direct disclosure is coercively restricted, but alliance through present evidence is possible;
- Witch odor connects restoration/disclosure risk to Rem's threat perception and Ulgarm attraction without proving one common controller;
- curse application, feeding, operator death, and overlapping-residue mechanics are substantially established;
- the Ulgarm crisis is human-directed, while handler identity and political sponsor remain open;
- Ram/Rem history explains both mutual devotion and dangerous self-erasure;
- present-state trust is revisable through observation and reciprocal action;
- victory has distributed causal authorship and leaves bodily, psychological, political, and moral residue;
- the estate story reaches a source-verified structural break without a formal arc name.

The checkpoint does **not** identify the restoration provider, disclosure enforcer, black figure, mabeast handler, Elsa's employer, Garfiel, or Roswaal's intended Dragon. It does not establish that Rem's dependency, Subaru's self-sacrificial risk, Emilia's constrained agency, or Ram's instrumental loyalty are resolved.
