## 4. Executive V02 state

The central revision is that **the memory-holder becomes a cause of branch divergence**. Subaru cannot replay the same estate days because retained literacy, procedures, expectations, fear, and relationship memory change his behavior, which changes other people's responses.

The largest mechanics findings are bounded but major: the observed return point can move; procedural learning persists while bodily injuries reset; direct disclosure of `死に戻り` triggers an externally observed suppressive experience; and Rem plus Beatrice independently detect a Witch-associated odor on Subaru. None of these observations identifies the restoration's provider or metaphysical mechanism.

The threat model also splits. Rem is a direct chain-weapon attacker in one state, acting from her own security judgment rather than a stated Roswaal execution order. Yet Rem herself dies in E4 from a curse-like depletion process resembling Subaru's earlier unexplained collapse. V02 therefore requires at least **two causal layers**: discretionary household violence and a separate depletion curse whose caster, vector, target rule, and purpose remain open.

Finally, restoration now costs Subaru **mutually remembered social history**. He remembers promises and bonds that the people before him no longer possess. That retained attachment can motivate rescue but can also constrain self-defense against a currently hostile person.

## 5. Event-state deep reading

### 5.1 `V02-E1`: building and losing ordinary life

Subaru wakes at Roswaal's estate after V01 treatment. Emilia remembers only the surviving V01 encounter. Subaru nearly speaks from discarded intimacy, recognizes the mismatch, and self-corrects. V01's relationship-asymmetry rule is therefore immediately preserved. (`第一章; p-006–p-010`)

Offered a reward, Subaru asks to work at the estate. Motives remain mixed: a base in an unknown world, proximity to Emilia, practical learning, and the value of letting the household observe an unexplained outsider. (`第二章; p-011`)

Household routine supplies a low-stakes baseline. Subaru is poor at many chores, unusually good at sewing, initially illiterate in local scripts, and capable of improvement through correction. He cleans, cooks, trains, studies, runs errands, and develops teasing/routine with Ram and Rem. Rem offers a haircut; Emilia agrees to a village outing. Subaru explicitly values becoming able to do what he could not do before and distinguishes merely easy (`楽`) from enjoyable (`楽しい`). (`第二章; p-013–p-015`)

This does not prove permanent reform from Earth-side avoidance. It shows that chosen purpose, feedback, community, and attachment can make difficult effort rewarding.

Ram and Rem are already non-interchangeable: Rem is much stronger at domestic work; Ram confidently occupies elder-sister status despite weaker practical performance; Rem reacts defensively to the idea that she should differentiate herself from Ram.

Reader-only information also matters. Ram privately reports Subaru's poor but improving work and low spy likelihood; Roswaal tentatively treats him as a genuinely well-intentioned third party. Subaru does not know this when later constructing threat models.

After several days, Subaru sleeps expecting the promised morning and instead wakes at the mansion-arrival point. Ram/Rem return to `お客様`; accumulated work wounds and the village dog's bite are gone; promises and mutual household history disappear for everyone except Subaru. The exact E1 death is not shown. Later curse evidence makes depletion plausible but not directly proven for E1.

The new cost is social bereavement: people remain physically alive while the shared history Subaru valued is gone.

### 5.2 `V02-E2`: retained learning makes exact replay impossible

Subaru tries a game-like replay strategy—repeat choices, reconstruct E1, remain awake through the expected transition. The plan immediately diverges because his retained competence makes him a different input. Ram assigns different work; new lessons occur; conversations change. (`第三章; p-016`)

Roswaal explains `ゲート` as mana intake/output and distinguishes fire, water, wind, earth, plus rarer `陰` and `陽`. Subaru is assessed as strongly `陰`-aligned but low in overall magical talent; Beatrice is an `陰` specialist. These are high-quality expert claims, still speaker-bound rather than narrator omniscience.

Ram teaches local scripts, and Subaru retains that literacy after restoration. V02 therefore extends carry-forward from episodic memory to declarative/procedural learning. Bodily rollback and cumulative learned competence coexist.

In Arlam village, Subaru is bitten by a dog and deliberately leaves the wound as a reset marker. (`第三章; p-018`) V02 does **not** yet establish the bite as the curse vector.

On the critical night he stays awake, then develops abnormal sleepiness, cold, weakness, breathlessness, and progressive collapse. A heavy chain weapon separately attacks him and ultimately kills him. Later states identify Rem as a wielder of the same weapon, making her the leading E2 attacker; V02 does not directly assign every unseen E2 blow to her. Depletion and assault remain analytically distinct.

### 5.3 `V02-E3`: Rem's threat model and asymmetric attachment

Subaru changes role: he stays briefly as a guest, plans to leave before the danger window, and observes the estate externally. (`第四章; p-019`) Role changes alter obligations and exposure, so E3 is not a controlled repeat.

Ram's discussion of `泣いた赤鬼` has personal salience without yet being a decoded backstory. Subaru dislikes an ending where sacrifice becomes permanent separation and wants both ogres preserved. Ram argues that if the red ogre wants an end, the red ogre should bear its cost rather than let the blue ogre sacrifice; if necessary, break its own horn first. She asks Subaru not to tell Rem. (`第四章; p-019`) V02 safely supports Ram's responsibility norm, not a one-to-one autobiographical mapping.

The same literacy/story material introduces Lugunica as `親竜王国`: Ram describes a royal covenant with a dragon and the recent extinction of the royal family. Exact covenant terms remain open.

Outside the estate, Subaru confronts the chain attacker and discovers Rem. (`第四章; p-019–p-021`) Rem explains that she suspects him because of unexplained behavior, possible rival royal-candidate affiliation, Roswaal's `悲願`, and especially a `魔女`-associated odor connected in her mind to people who harmed Ram and their home. Roswaal's order to host Subaru explains why she observed rather than attacked immediately. Her action is not presented as Roswaal directly ordering an execution.

Context explains Rem's threat model without erasing responsibility. She wounds, interrogates, heals to prolong questioning, and continues after Subaru cannot provide expected answers.

Subaru has a knife and opportunity to attack Rem but cannot separate the current hostile Rem from the remembered Rem who taught him household work and made promises. Retained bond therefore becomes a constraint on self-defense, not only a motive for gratitude.

Wind magic later removes part of Subaru's leg. His throat is then cut by an attack that appears to surprise Rem; the last line he hears is `姉様は、優しすぎます`. (`第四章; p-021`) E4 comparison with Ram's demonstrated wind magic strongly implicates Ram in the lethal sequence, but exact terminal-strike ownership and motive remain inference.

### 5.4 `V02-E4`: coercive secrecy, protection, Rem's death, chosen death

Subaru restores in severe accumulated distress: screaming, lashing out before orienting, distrusting food, fearing sleep, self-injuring to stay awake, withdrawing, and becoming hypervigilant. (`第五章; p-022`) These are source behaviors under extreme stress; no clinical diagnosis is inferred.

Emilia tries to help. Subaru decides that surviving alone has failed and attempts to disclose `死に戻り`. Sound disappears, motion/time appears suspended from his perspective, black mist forms a hand-like shape, enters his chest, and grips/crushes his heart; ordinary interaction then resumes with Emilia unaware. (`第五章; p-022–p-024`)

This directly establishes a **disclosure-suppression phenomenon**. It does not establish that the Witch personally acts, objective cosmological time stops, suppression equals restoration, or the volume-end silhouette is the same agent. Subaru's secrecy is therefore externally constrained, not reducible to pride or distrust.

Beatrice later says Subaru's `魔女の臭い` is stronger. Rem had detected the odor earlier, so the disclosure incident does not create it from nothing. Rem and Beatrice independently corroborate detectability; exact source and causal relation to restoration/disclosure remain open. (`第五章; p-024`)

Beatrice also becomes the clearest relational counterexample to Subaru's despair. She reveals that she completed the healing after V01 and accepts an explicit protection `契約`. She treats it as binding enough to oppose Ram's wind magic, confront Roswaal, and continue protection beyond Subaru's narrower interpretation. (`第五章; p-024–p-030`) Abrasive disclaimers and concrete care coexist.

Protected by Beatrice, Subaru survives the night—then Rem is found dead. Roswaal assesses gradual loss of life force during sleep, closer to `呪術` than ordinary magic. (`第五章; p-028`) The finding breaks the one-culprit model: Rem can be a direct attacker and still be a victim of a separate curse-like process.

Ram's response to Rem's body is catastrophic grief. She demands an explanation Subaru cannot safely source after the demonstrated disclosure penalty. Beatrice honors the contract; Emilia independently offers trust and asks Subaru to help if he can. Subaru nevertheless flees. The conflict is an information tragedy: Ram's demand is rational from her perspective, while Subaru's inability to answer safely is not indifference.

At a cliff, Subaru first contemplates death as escape and cannot make himself jump. Fear remains fully operative. (`第五章; p-028–p-030`) Beatrice finds him and offers continued protection/escape. Subaru then re-evaluates evidence: remembered warmth of hands holding his while he slept, plus Emilia's report that Ram suggested gentle food and Rem prepared it. This does not prove every warm act was sincere; it disproves the claim that surveillance made every act counterfeit.

Subaru reaches a more useful conclusion: he does not actually know the sisters as well as discarded familiarity made him feel, and ignorance is reason to learn rather than abandon them. When Ram arrives, he tells her that across histories she cannot remember he has come to care for both sisters.

He then chooses the cliff death as an attempt to recover a state where Rem is alive and both can be saved. `使い方は、俺が決める` frames the decision as authorship over his own life under desperation. The distinction is essential: he cannot die merely to escape, but can choose death once he assigns it a rescue purpose. V02 therefore revises strategic use of death without supporting desensitization.

After impact, a black liminal scene contains an obscured human-like silhouette and the line `まだ、会えない`. (`第五章; p-030`) This is direct reader evidence with extremely weak ontological interpretation. No restoration is shown.

## 6. Character and relationship revisions

### Subaru

V02 turns restoration into cumulative development: literacy, household procedures, social knowledge, and practiced routines survive bodily rollback. Growth helps him function but makes exact replay impossible because others notice the changed competence.

His ordinary-life baseline also changes conditionally. Under purpose, feedback, belonging, and attachment he can enjoy mastery through difficult work. This is stronger than either “Subaru is lazy” or “Subaru has become hardworking.”

Relationship memory becomes motive **and vulnerability**. Remembered bonds make him repay and rescue, but can inhibit defense against someone whose current-state agency is hostile. The ethical problem is no longer only whether debt survives forgetting; it is what claims the sole memory-holder may legitimately carry forward when the other person's present trust, consent, and reasons differ.

Death remains horrifying while becoming strategically usable under narrowed alternatives. V02 does not support “retries are cheap.” It supports instrumental self-risk under relational stakes despite fear.

### Emilia

Emilia repeatedly offers care across altered states without retaining discarded histories, strengthening V01's prosocial-tendency inference. Subaru becomes more disciplined about not assigning lost intimacy to her.

V02 also identifies her as a `王候補`, Roswaal as sponsor, and the dragon badge as proof tied to candidacy. This materially strengthens V01's institutional-significance claim.

### Ram and Rem

Rem's threat perception is conditioned by Witch odor, unexplained conduct, possible rival-camp affiliation, loyalty to Roswaal, and past harm involving Ram. Those reasons make suspicion intelligible but do not neutralize her extreme violence.

Care and surveillance coexist. E4 evidence of food preparation and likely hand-holding rejects a total-fraud model. Their mutual centrality is stronger still: Rem's threat model centers Ram, Beatrice describes a mutually constitutive sister bond, and Ram's grief at Rem's death is devastating.

Ram's `泣いた赤鬼` response establishes a strong value statement about owning the cost of one's desired end; backstory mapping remains open.

### Beatrice

Beatrice guards the Forbidden Library, uses `扉渡り`, manipulates/drains mana, detects Witch odor, and treats explicit contract as unusually binding. Her harsh speech coexists with healing and protection. Deeper ontology, contract history, and relation to Puck remain open.

### Roswaal

Roswaal is a margrave/border lord, leading magician, and Emilia's sponsor. His `悲願` structures household concern but remains undefined. He gives valuable expert testimony on magic and institutions while remaining an interested actor. His private favorable assessment of Subaru is reader knowledge unavailable to Subaru.

V02 adds a coercive agency constraint: at least one direct `死に戻り` disclosure attempt is externally punished. “Why didn't he tell someone?” is therefore not a complete choice-set reconstruction.

## 8. Institutions and world model

The estate is politically embedded. Emilia's candidacy and Roswaal's sponsorship shape household security. V02 describes recent extinction of the king and descendants, governance by the `賢人会`, reduced foreign relations, and outsider suspicion. These are in-world institutional claims, not a complete constitution.

The badge's candidacy function makes its theft politically meaningful and strengthens—but does not prove—the V01 inference that Elsa's employer may have wanted to prevent Emilia from retaining it.

Magic evidence now includes `ゲート`, mana, four common affinities plus `陰/陽`, Subaru's strong `陰` affinity but low talent, Ram's wind magic, Rem's water-aligned healing, Beatrice's `陰` specialization, and Roswaal's exceptional multi-attribute skill. Beatrice and Roswaal distinguish ordinary magic/mana effects from curse-like depletion enough to narrow the model without identifying the curse source.

## 9. Ordinary-life evidence

Subaru's sewing, cleaning/cooking failures, literacy lessons, training, errands, joking, and pleasure at improvement provide a non-crisis baseline. Emilia's patience and willingness to spend ordinary time with him distinguish care from emergency rescue. Ram's teaching/teasing/reporting and Rem's competence/healing/promises complicate simple hostile-role labels. Beatrice's reading routine and boundary-setting coexist with hidden healing and contractual care.

These details matter because later crisis behavior should not be mistaken for each character's entire personality.

## 10. Japanese wording notes

- `楽` / `楽しい`: Subaru distinguishes ease from enjoyment; mastery can be rewarding even when work is difficult. (`第二章; p-015`)
- `お客様`: after restoration the sisters' reversion to “guest” marks erased social history.
- `魔女の臭い`: a detected signature/odor; physical ontology and source remain open.
- `魔女教`: Rem uses it as a threat category tied to prior harm; V02 does not yet support a full institutional history.
- `死に戻り`: remains Subaru's model-name; checkpoint movement and suppression do not make its deeper mechanism narrator-certified.
- `使い方は、俺が決める`: frames the cliff decision as self-authorship over a cost he believes only he can choose to spend.
- `まだ、会えない`: direct terminal wording; speaker and ontology unresolved.

## 11. Causal threat model

The strongest V02 reconstruction has four layers:

1. **restoration/disclosure/Witch association** — observed correlations, causal integration open;
2. **household threat perception** — Rem's discretionary violence and probable Ram involvement under their information state;
3. **separate curse-like depletion** — can kill Rem when Subaru is not the apparent victim;
4. **social feedback** — Subaru's retained learning changes roles, suspicion, timing, and exposure.

This explains more evidence than “Rem did everything,” “the curse did everything,” or “Roswaal ordered it all.”
