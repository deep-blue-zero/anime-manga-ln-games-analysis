## 0. Governing claim and evidence boundary

V14 makes love a reason to examine an inherited purpose, not a guarantee that the purpose is freely chosen, correctly understood or harmless. Emilia discovers that her forgotten life contained real affection as well as catastrophe. Meyer knows her refuge began instrumentally yet values the home she made there. Ram's old promise appears to authorize revenge, while her present desire requires Roswaal to remain capable of wanting and living rather than becoming an empty prize. These are different histories. Their common question is not whether affection can be reduced to either manipulation or purity, but what the person now wants and what conditions made that desire possible.

The past does not arrive through one transparent channel. Emilia's first trial includes scenes she did not witness as a child; Echidna attributes this extension to the Book of Wisdom. Shima has acquired another person's life in a trial, then arranged fragments into an account for listeners outside. The reader receives both reconstructions and private current scenes that Subaru, Emilia or Otto do not all share. Fidelity therefore requires retaining who received which account, under which representation rule, rather than converting the volume's expanded perspective into everyone's recovered memory.

The distinction matters causally and ethically. Pandora's original alteration selectively removes her presence while leaving contradictions, guilt and promises. Puck's later contractual lid is a separate intervention; his protection cannot be treated as either the only original erasure or fully informed consent from Emilia. Emilia's own uncontrolled power freezes the forest, but an invader's deliberate violence and misidentification do not become her moral responsibility merely because she was weak, broke an earlier household rule or refused to betray a promise. First-trial success is her present decision about that regret, not a finding that nobody died or that she can already save everyone.

Current action remains distributed and imperfect. Garfiel answers Roswaal for himself instead of being defended into silence. Otto takes a disclosure risk to obtain Ram's cooperation; the conditions are newly disclosed to readers, not newly known to Otto. Frederica extracts the target list while fighting. Petra's courage includes disobeying an escape order to protect Rem, then recognizing that she cannot rescue Frederica alone. Subaru answers that need by delegating the fight he cannot win. Yet his insistence on taking Beatrice out anticipates overriding her refusal. Accepted aid has changed him without eliminating performance, secrecy or control.

## 4. Emilia's ordinary childhood and the price of a promise

### 4.1 A lived mother is not an unnamed biological mother

Fortuna identifies herself as Emilia's paternal aunt while functioning as her mother. Household love has material texture: hidden visits, books, dolls, drawing, snacks, tree climbing, bed comfort and the difficulty of giving a child reasons adults do not want to disclose. Emilia lies about mud/ink after escaping, and a forgiving response to what Fortuna believes was an accident is not informed approval of the deception. The child can be loved, inventive and culpable for an ordinary broken rule without thereby causing the invasion.

The intensifier すごーく is especially diagnostic. Emilia notices Fortuna using it with sadness and tries to put it into happy contexts. A supposedly decorative vocal manner is a remembered act of care directed at a particular person. This gives later Emilia's voice continuity without making all later sweetness an innocent default or every adult phrase transparently understood. Her jealousy at Geuse's closeness to Fortuna gives way to comfort when he weeps; fingers and head-patting carry learned care, not demonstrated telepathy. p003B1–138/B239–332; p005B38–138.

Fortuna's promise lesson is strict and consequential. Caring for another person does not excuse leaving the agreed place; blaming a microspirit does not transfer the child's responsibility. The child later applies the trust rule back to Fortuna to obtain a promise of parent information. That reciprocal use matters to the future seal refusal and to current overnight accountability. It does not make every violation equally serious or imply that a loving parent never conceals information.

### 4.2 A Cult visit is not one uniform institutional will

Geuse's visitors bring material help and defer to the village's protective arrangement. Later his followers oppose Regulus's invasion, and an unnamed elder dies helping the child's escape. “Cult” therefore does not authorize one purpose across every time, faction and person. The explicit Petelgeuse identification is a major reader correction, but earlier Subaru does not automatically learn it and earlier cruelty is not morally canceled by a loving phase.

Geuse explains microspirit contracts and argues against forever confining Emilia; Fortuna's concern for danger and his support for development are an actual disagreement within care. Their emotional intimacy and Emilia's wished parent-like grouping do not supply marriage, paternity or a legal bond. The blurred parental 健在 clause remains deferred information, not an exact parent-fate certificate. p003B139–238; p005B88–138; p011B101–161.
