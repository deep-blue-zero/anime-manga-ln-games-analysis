## 0. Governing thesis and boundary

V17 makes the capacity to care depend on something the bishops repeatedly refuse: another person's separate response. Sirius makes shared feeling stand in for mutual understanding; Regulus makes his own designation stand in for a wife's assent; Capella makes bodily attractiveness and compulsory praise stand in for being loved. The three projects are not one decoded Authority or one perfectly coordinated Cult doctrine. Their observed acts nevertheless expose different ways to appropriate another person's feelings, body and answer. The text gives no warrant to accept those speakers' definitions merely because they use familiar words such as love, rights, consideration or gratitude.

The allies' more defensible care is neither costless nor uniformly successful. Beatrice trusts an incomplete warning and chooses to help injured strangers, but her resources run out. Emilia chooses reciprocal protection rather than the safety Subaru has selected for her, yet the linked hostages make strength difficult to use without harm. Ferris treats people while exhausted, acknowledges a wound he cannot close and buys time through physical medicine and a dangerous sharing procedure; his useful work does not make every painful or poorly explained intervention consensual. Julius supports Subaru while admitting that he too is not calm. Each relationship matters through an actual choice, limit and recipient, not a title that guarantees virtue.

Subaru's failed interventions test the difference between acquiring information and possessing control. A successful signal brings Reinhard, but it also changes Sirius's timing. Reinhard's overwhelming martial superiority does not protect a child from shared bisection. Subaru's later coordination, bait and learned whip work genuinely help, without preventing his mistaken rescue of Capella's guise or the injury of a transformed hostage. His leg's temporary lack of pain is not healing; his drive to save somebody does not make his body expendable by definition. The final severing, blood intrusion and fire are a crisis, not a completed death or a license to invent another return.

Garfiel's history is revised by a living person, not by a corrected theory of strength. His mother's face, scent, manner and hospitality defeat the death inference he had attached to the trial's landslide. They do not return her memory of him. The new household's warmth is real, and so is his grief at a bond no longer mutually remembered. When he predicts that disclosure can benefit nobody, the narrative gives his protective self-erasure, not all affected people's informed answer. Gallek asks a question; he does not explicitly decree that the older child must disappear.

Mimi's response makes the distinction especially concrete. Her affectionate play can be poorly received and her kick or his bruising grip can hurt; neither fact cancels her perceptiveness. She offers comfort without requiring reciprocal love, accepts a changing boundary of proximity and recognizes a tactical mismatch that Garfiel refuses to respect. Garfiel's tears show received help, not permanent cure. His later breach of their retreat agreement exposes Mimi to a wound neither his trained healing nor Ferris's greater magic can close. Continued action and responsibility therefore do not form a simple progression from weak shame to recovered strongest protector.

The narrative ending preserves these unfinished responses. Emilia wakes, asks, compares and rejects; Regulus tries to convert her frightened silence into agreement. She hears his name but does not receive the reader's complete reconstructed past or the city-hall catastrophe. His final question is unanswered. V17 closes a volume, not Arc5, a marriage, a rescue, a medical recovery or an ethical settlement.

## 5. Character states, self-models and material revisions

Subaru's useful contribution now has several independent sources: retained information, actual training, reputation that can recruit a retainer, explicit requests for Beatrice's judgment, cooperation with Julius and willingness to accept a task smaller than personally defeating every enemy. These are neither imagined achievements nor proof he is medically fit. His poor crowd tactic, exclusive plan for Emilia, Gluttony-triggered loss of composure and false-hostage rescue preserve errors beside the gains. The recoil from Invisible Providence and the actual leg loss prevent the protective wish from becoming an abstract inexhaustible resource.

Emilia's action is especially poorly described by either permanent helplessness or effortless empowerment. She argues for reciprocal help and understands the practical proposal she overheard. She uses powerful techniques and recognizes the crowd's pain; she also hesitates where using greater force may harm hostages. Her capture does not make that hesitation proof of incompetence. In the final room, her wish for Subaru's rescue coexists with her own getting up, questioning and comparison of care. Regulus's declaration does not become her identity merely because she is frightened or uninformed.

Beatrice's chosen bond includes acting for other injured people, admitting a mistaken first analogy and refusing to enforce Subaru's exclusion of Emilia. Her body/resource state is not her historical library reserve. Dormancy follows expenditure under a particular contract; Ferris's expectation that replenishment will help is not a completed waking, measured schedule or explanation of Rem. Beatrice's regret for Emilia is known to readers after Subaru blacks out, not received through an unshown apology conversation.

Garfiel's mother's survival changes a fact on which an earlier self-story rested. The old trial did not display an independently certified corpse; he could not imagine that a living mother would fail to return. The source supplies an amnesia account and a loving new household, not remembered rejection. His denial to Gallek and imagined all-party burden are actions/predictions under grief. They do not become uniquely right because violence cannot solve the problem, nor a permanent ban on informing Frederica/Ryuzu. The narrower V13 reread checks actual age/trial wording: fourteen in the earlier scene is compatible with fifteen after the common year, while the ten-year references concern old trial/scar experience rather than proving a ten-year maternal departure. No exact birthday, retcon, second departure or forced chronology bridge is supplied.

Mimi is not simply the person who heals Garfiel's mood. She has her own stated affection without reciprocity, a reported chosen family, ordinary play, changing touch preferences and a reasoned danger assessment. Her wrist pain and his apology remain visible before later welcomed comfort. When she returns to save him she criticizes his broken word; affection has not made every risk acceptable. Her latest body cannot supply new dialogue: she is unconscious under critical treatment, with her brothers knowingly accepting intensified linked risk. An earlier waking override is required for a supported response.

Crusch remains an actor without her old autobiography. Retraining yields actual sword/wind action and a timely rescue; the wrong dragon target reflects deceptive information, not intentional civilian harm or absence of skill. Choosing public duty over immediate Gluttony pursuit does not show that her old memories or political self have returned. The later convulsion is a distinct, unresolved Capella-associated crisis.
