## 4. Subaru: from a necessary crisis to asking for a place in the work

### 4.1 Hatred recruits help badly, but not because he has no care

Subaru begins by reducing victory to killing Petelgeuse (`p-002#B0011–0027`). He has genuine evidence of danger that he cannot explain, but also conceals his suspicion of Roswaal's absence while arguing that a lord's existing force is insufficient (`B0148–0153`). Crusch's suspicion is not pure obtuseness. He lacks demonstrated authority to pledge Emilia's political future, cannot substantiate exact timing/location, and offers a rival little benefit that inaction would not already supply (`B0219–0228`, `B0261–0272`, `B0286–0288`). Sincere pain is real; it does not answer these separate questions.

Crusch accurately identifies current hatred but incorrectly imagines that Subaru approached Emilia originally to lure out the cult (`B0312`, `B0334–0339`). The reader must not adopt that false chronology because a composed expert states it. Conversely, Subaru's willingness to sacrifice candidacy for lives is a real concession (`B0203–0209`), not proof that pride is his sole motive. The scene makes several partly accurate models collide. His unconscious reverse grip on a teaspoon, Wilhelm's interception and Rem's alarm mark an actual escalation, not a successful attack or an order by Crusch to kill him (`B0361–0373`).

Subaru initially rules out Julius/knightly avenues because of his own injury and pride; Reinhard has left town, rather than personally rejecting him (`p-005#B0023–0041`). Rem later does appeal to guards in Roswaal's name; they hear her but do not act on uncertain reports (`p-009#B0006–0010`). These different failures cannot become “everyone refused because everyone is selfish.” Priscilla's degradation test and Anastasia's manipulation deserve their own analyses, not an undifferentiated hostile world.

### 4.2 Practical action survives inside the failure

The evacuation pivot is substantive. Subaru chooses saving people over obtaining a punitive army, rents the actual vehicle via Anastasia's referral, organizes money, purchases Otto's oil and recruits merchants (`p-009#B0019–0025`; `p-010#B0003–0009`, `B0084–0093`). Ten of fourteen merchants join the hired journey, four continue toward the capital; together with the original vehicle the convoy has eleven carriages (`B0084–0093`, `B0139`). Otto coordinates a profit-sharing/guild prospect. Subaru uses others' commercial competence instead of suddenly becoming its master.

This is not ethically clean cooperation. The account of a planned beast hunt is materially misleading; the old wounds and mabeast danger are real, but a coming Roswaal operation is not established (`B0118–0135`). Subaru's self-defense that he has merely omitted information is not the analysis's verdict. He withholds the risk against which contractors would decide. He also realizes that villagers had slipped from view while Emilia monopolized his concern (`B0111–0116`). Later willingness to use merchants as shields is thought, not an action already performed (`p-012#B0110`). These distinctions preserve responsibility without inventing additional harm.

His phone supplies finite, ordinary help: remembered battery management, illumination and reading a map (`p-010#B0214–0225`). The dragon/carriage's wind protection does not clear outside dust or make every ride safe. After Otto ejects him, Subaru acknowledges the coercive danger into which he brought the man rather than simply treating betrayal as proof of universal malice (`p-014#B0174–0188`). Injured and without skillful driving, he nonetheless improvises with the abandoned vehicle. Actual competence and serious ethical failure coexist.

### 4.3 Rescue becomes control, then a different kind of disclosure

Rem's admission of a limited instruction to respect Subaru's capital wishes prompts his absolute conclusion that all her care was false obedience (`p-010#B0164–0181`). That conclusion contradicts the reader's earlier Rem interiority and her present sacrifices/refusals. Her order is qualified by scope and feasibility; it is not a complete cause of affection. At the estate, a similarly total account turns Emilia's questions and duties into offenses against his rescue. He threatens abduction, orders obedience and deliberately uses hurt to monopolize attention (`p-017#B0156–0194`).

Emilia asks why he is crying; the question punctures that performance (`B0199–0203`). She wants to understand, yet does not know Rem or his death history. Subaru chooses to speak the forbidden explanation, intending to bear his own expected pain. The actual incomplete utterance is `『死に戻り』し──` after claiming to have seen the future (`B0237`). Enforcement kills the listener instead. This is actual causal involvement, not an imagined wish murdering villagers; it is also not an intentional decision to kill Emilia. The external coercive agent remains causally indispensable.

When Beatrice refuses his request to be killed, Subaru interprets cruelty where the reader sees another person's grief. Petelgeuse's immediate willingness supplies the dreadful contrast. Finally the bishop dies without restoring Rem, Emilia or their relationship (`p-021#B0298–0303`). The initial victory condition is directly falsified. Puck can preserve Subaru's wounded body to intensify punishment; “healing” and being alive are not sufficient outcomes.

### 4.4 Flight, confession and a restart that does not cure the self

The next return restores Subaru's body, not his capacity to accept another loss. He tries to take Rem out of the country. He recognizes that her elapsed second is not his several days and apologizes when she demands explanation (`p-022#B0058–0068`), but later withholds the coming danger while deliberately forcing a choice between him and others (`B0199–0221`). Disclosure fear is genuine; strategic dependency leverage is also explicit. Neither excuses the other away.

His Earth schooling/work self-report supplies new bounded history: truancy/dropout, middle-school graduation as his highest completed schooling, and no work experience beyond the servant apprenticeship, not a full independently verified biography (`B0228–0230`). His extended self-hatred accounts for unused time, postponed effort, display-oriented study/training and blaming others (`B0440–0487`). It revises the motive model. It does not erase actual learning, sewn goods, calculation, coalition work or the V03 rescue. Narrated all-or-nothing self-judgment is not a neutral competence audit. The text itself says naming faults does not immediately change them.

At the turn, Subaru says he loves Emilia and asks Rem for help and correction (`B0601–0615`). He now explicitly rejects expecting total understanding merely because he loves someone (`B0608`). The change from an ultimatum to a request is enacted, as is admitting he cannot walk alone. Yet he still wants to be beside Emilia even if unwanted and explicitly knows that he is using Rem's affection (`B0605`, `B0610–0614`). Emilia has not consented or forgiven him. Self-hatred persists at `B0629`. A future model must test whether renewed heroic performance again becomes the condition of worth.

## 5. Rem: ordinary love, a costly no, and a burden shared imperfectly

### 5.1 Care can protect and override

In E1 Rem objects to Subaru's escalation, heals injuries, warns him and attempts to keep him safe; these are not a featureless permission to do anything. Against the Whale her ice lances shatter; she nevertheless saves him from the destroyed original carriage, fights with horn/flail, wounds the jaw and uses the chain to rescue him (`p-012#B0009–0016`, `B0094–0103`). None demonstrates a kill or immunity.

Her farewell sends money/reporting toward Mathers territory, values her life through this service, and overrides his refusal with a neck-chop (`B0124–0145`). This is bodily compulsion, not mana sedation or a resurrection. Her actual death is not witnessed. The subsequent erasure/absence does not retroactively give the reader a corpse. Her “born for this” self-account preserves the self-spending danger despite courage and genuine care. It also matters that Otto first remembers the girl who stayed behind: the later denial is not simply his original ingratitude.

### 5.2 The imagined future is not discarded as worthless

Active E2 Rem does not simply comply with escape. She imagines rent, work, budgeting, further study, a shop, children and old age in Kararagi (`p-022#B0285–0318`). She describes employability grounded in Roswaal-funded education and can imagine Subaru doing housework/manual work while studying. Gentle but rigorous teaching belongs inside the fantasy. These are preferences, practical estimates and a conditional domestic script, not accomplished employment, children or evidence about interspecies fertility.

She says she could want that future if Subaru truly wanted it and could smile. Her refusal therefore need not mean only “be useful in a crisis or I will not love you.” She rejects a flight that abandons the self and commitments he still values (`B0318–0339`). That costs her the exclusive future he offers. It converges with the reader's V05-E2 evidence of refusing possessive escape, but active E2 Rem does not remember that discarded interior decision. The present choice must earn its own agency.

### 5.3 Neither omniscient therapist nor merely blind idealizer

Rem offers rest, a hearing and trust without knowing the return penalty or clock. Her initial “giving up is easy, but…” is interrupted; her completed answer admits that she does not know his pain and cannot claim to understand it (`B0356`, `B0398–0401`). Quoting the opening alone would reverse her actual epistemic stance. She supplies a different kind of knowledge: how she encounters him.

Her account includes voice, eyes, fingers, head-patting, walking and sleeping habits (`B0498–0506`). Ordinary affection is not reducible to saving her. It remains her testimony, not a universal trait inventory: E1 Subaru had walked without looking back (`p-009#B0044–0045`). She can be hurt and still trust after the confession (`p-022#B0515–0525`). The V03 rescue provides an actual shared counterexample to “I saved nobody,” not a grant of his private histories.

Calling him her hero renews purpose and risks a new chain of worth through expectation. The `呪い`/trust-chain imagery explicitly permits both readings (`B0538–0551`). It is not a new literal curse formula. Her love utterance belongs to this human speaker and scene, not proof of identity with the dark feminine figure. The “zero” turn shares a load and starts from the present; it is not a moved checkpoint or an erased past.

### 5.4 Honest asymmetry is not a completed reciprocal romance

Subaru's declaration for Emilia disappoints Rem, and she names his harshness before choosing to remain his ally (`B0601–0620`). The scene does not certify exclusive Rem/Subaru romance, erase her separate wishes, or show an independent self-worth model completed. It does improve the honesty of the immediate arrangement: she hears the declared rescue goal, he asks for correction, and both choose support, unlike V05's opposed concealed intentions.

The next chapter gives her another desire: to be asked about the mission she has failed to complete. Her relief and tears when Subaru finally asks are professional/relational evidence, not only romantic response (`p-024#B0135–0140`). She can transfer specific bargaining authority. Recognizing her knowledge and unfinished work is one practical test of the restart.

## 7. Negotiation, markets and the authority to offer something

### 7.1 Crusch's first refusal and what it does not prove

Hosting an injured rival's associate is not an alliance. Crusch separates the care contract from army deployment, benefit, evidence and the requester's authority (`p-002#B0170–0179`, `B0219–0288`). Her earlier threat that borrowing help could end Emilia's candidacy is later identified as pressure, not enacted automatic disqualification law. The analysis must read that qualification rather than turn a negotiating threat into a constitutional rule.

Her assignment of territorial responsibility to Emilia remains an ethically contestable stance; it does not make the expected massacre unreal or its killers innocent. She is also capable of misidentifying a true report as delusion: her self-claimed rough lie detection establishes that she believes Subaru sincere, not that his story is false or that she knows its origin (`B0384–0389`). No named blessing is supplied here. Sincerity, factual accuracy, explanatory inference and willingness to pay a cost remain distinct.

After refusing, she orders his lip healed and offers dinner; he refuses both (`p-004#B0005–0008`). The episode is not expulsion from care. Wilhelm, Ferris and Rem do not all share her role, judgment or manner. Internal differentiation is essential to a current institutional model.

### 7.2 Priscilla's test is a political humiliation, not a rescue offer accepted

Priscilla reads before judging and burns books she dislikes; Al describes her morning/bedtime and staffing habits (`p-005#B0003–0010`, `B0080–0090`, `B0149–0155`). These make her a situated person, not proof of a providence mechanism or a fully administered welfare program. Her warning against leaking weakness has tactical content. The fan threat and foot-licking test make her control concrete. Her earlier territorial-prosperity report remains corroborated by Marcos at the V04 horizon; this new humiliation neither erases that success nor turns success into universal ethical or supernatural vindication.

She offers to consider the proposal if Subaru submits; he kneels, but she kicks him before a lick/touch is accomplished (`B0197–0212`). There is no purchased army, successful act of submission or contract she then breaches. Her subsequent threat against Emilia's camp is serious declared hostility, not a deployment already carried out (`B0222`). Al's limited deescalation and Rem's actual healing do not exonerate the test.

### 7.3 Anastasia's real help and real extraction coexist

Subaru initially excludes alliance with her for his own reasons; the lunch is not a third completed military negotiation and refusal (`p-007#B0123`, `B0256–0258`). Anastasia supplies food, a signed shop referral and useful bargaining instruction while extracting intelligence about Russell's visit, iron/weapons purchases, garden storage and night movement (`B0143–0179`, `B0285–0303`). The referral later produces an actual rental, not a gratuitous carriage or an artifact Subaru carries through death.

Her foreknowledge of the earlier quarrel supports a staged encounter/network explanation, not Return by Death (`B0312`). The apparent customers leave with her as a concealed escort; Mimi's surprise prevents treating the helper's every prior impulse as consciously rehearsed (`B0360–0366`). Anastasia's feeding/wiping Mimi's face is ordinary care coexisting with commercial instrumentality, not automatically fake tenderness.

Subaru tells her of Earth/Japan and his unusual jersey (`B0217–0225`). She reports hearing of people from beyond the waterfall and describes Hoshin's founder/name association, denying blood descent and claiming the name (`B0225–0230`).
