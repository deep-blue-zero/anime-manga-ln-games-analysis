# Re:Zero — fourth arc 第四章, V10–V15 checkpoint

The fourth arc makes a future with other people possible by refusing to make them identical to the roles, remembered counterparts or satisfactory answers someone needs. That future is not the restoration of an undamaged beginning. It contains a lost library, an exhausted reserve, a broken gate, a sleeping Rem and a patron whose coercion survives his defeat. Its affirmative result is more exact: Emilia can undertake her own task, Beatrice can choose a particular finite bond, Garfiel can reconsider what protection requires, Ram can act on a changed wish, and Subaru can receive help without possessing every helper's reasons. None becomes permanently free of pressure, error or dependence.

This source-free synthesis was begun only after the final contributing V15 transaction's complete publication gate at `98acb7201088307023f6007e2314b44dd8c72897`. Its own accepted frozen content and synchronized routing take effect only after this separate checkpoint's complete publication gate. V16 and separately acquired non-spine narrative remain unopened.

## 1. Six volumes do not make one uninterrupted recovery curve

The arc repeatedly supplies a real answer and then tests the additional claim someone wants that answer to guarantee.

| movement | achievement actually gained | claim that remains unsafe |
|---|---|---|
| V10: family trial and return to the mansion | Subaru can receive parental love without becoming his father or earning a first-place identity; ordinary care has a prior history | a passed trial does not make him receptive to Beatrice, fully consult Emilia or protect a solitary search from death |
| V11: death, captivity, rescue and second tea | Otto supplies and receives a fresh friendship; Subaru can articulate RBD grief to an actual local listener | bodily restoration is not emotional restoration; a helpful confidant is not omniscient or safe under every disclosure condition |
| V12: blank book, failed rescue and formal bargain | Beatrice's plea is actually heard; Subaru distinguishes a wanted destination from the possible cruelty of the route | hearing a plea does not settle its terms; rejecting Echidna does not undo his commands or immediately rescue Beatrice |
| V13: accepted help and collective Garfiel defeat | Subaru wants to live, shares part of the crisis, and accepts others' contributions; Garfiel chooses a new first-trial answer | friendship does not disclose every subplan; affectionate encouragement does not keep Emilia's specific promise or repair the broken gate |
| V14: represented pasts and divided intervention | Emilia passes FIRST; Garfiel answers Roswaal himself; Ram's changed wish and distinct rescue roles become explicit | a new answer does not complete all trials, establish perfect history, persuade Roswaal or turn Beatrice's trembling into assent |
| V15: chosen bond, local survival and settlement | an actual hand/contract, three passes, two-site release, joint defense, apology and accepted knighthood are enacted | survival is not unanimity, restored resources, complete forgiveness, national office, fulfilled origin telling or an end to private coercion |

Calling every later adverse act a relapse to an unchanged original self would erase the new acts of reception and choice. Calling the first moving resolution a cure would erase the tests that make its limits intelligible. The appropriate unit is a changed conditional capacity: what Subaru can now receive or attempt with this recipient, this knowledge and these resources. The same principle applies to Emilia, Garfiel, Ram and Beatrice. A later choice can be sincere without reaching every possible application of the insight that helped produce it.

The ending also resists an all-failure interpretation. The villagers' voluntary waiting, Frederica's extraction, Petra's practical route support, Emilia's difficult enclosure and Beatrice's actual grasp are not devalued because coercion remains elsewhere. The arc permits improvement without moral purification. Its immediate future becomes workable through particular agents doing enough, not through one agent becoming enough for everyone.

## 2. Event-state, representation and unequal survival

The preceding V09 boundary is E013 after twelve counted deaths. V10 begins within that same segment; it does not create E014 merely by changing volume or location. The arc adds five actual Subaru deaths, reaching seventeen deaths/eighteen arrived segments. E018 begins in V12 and continues through V15's month-later ceremony and party.

These rows consolidate V10–V15 §3 and the event owner; they do not retroactively expand what a predecessor freeze had reached. Material tests matter: a dirty cloth can identify continued captivity; an empty eye socket survives a spatial transfer but is restored at an actual arrival. Waking, fainting, receiving memories, changing focalizer and opening a chapter are not interchangeable with RBD.

### 2.2 The arc presents several different kinds of not-present life

Subaru's generated parents can give an answer that changes him without receiving a real message on Earth. The second trial's grieving aftermaths can injure him without proving the independent persistence of every discarded world. Garfiel's interacting sister proxy can change his interpretation without becoming actual Frederica's new testimony. Emilia's first trial expressly adds unwitnessed scenes through Echidna's attributed Book correction; Shima orders fragments of another person's life into an account. Emilia's impossible grown family and possible calamity images have different modal status from those past reconstructions. None becomes transparent history merely because it is emotionally persuasive.

V12 `p-021#B0063–0078` explicitly separates a constructed examination from the unresolved time-reversal/parallel-overwrite question. V13 `p-025#B0183–0282` preserves Garfiel's question about whose memory he sees. V14 `p-011#B0085–0106` supplies the Book-correction account rather than perfect eyewitness recall. V15 B07–11 distinguish an impossible domestic continuation, a proxy, contingent futures and an unnamed guide. The fact that all can be called a trial does not authorize one reliability rule for all their propositions.

The coda adds a different distinction. Rabbit exile is an actual spatial outcome with the Rabbit's own subsequent perspective; it does not settle RBD cosmology. Gradual soul domination and forcible overlay in V15 `p-028#B0019–0021` are reader evidence, not another Subaru return or a revelation received by Emilia. The cast's interpretation of Shima's disappearance and the reader's expanded account must coexist as different states of knowledge.

### 2.3 A retained debt is not a counterpart's remembered agreement

Subaru carries E015 Otto's rescue and death into later encounters. Current Otto must still receive a new request or enact a new friendship. Current Garfiel does not remember the tiger's discarded-state killings, even though those acts remain strong evidence of what he could do under their conditions. Ram's renewed Rem lesson in one segment does not survive automatically in the next. Beatrice's earlier protection matters to Subaru without obliging the present Beatrice to confirm his desired account of it.

Consequently the checkpoint cannot call every survivor a participant in Subaru's whole rescue history. His grief and obligation may be real while the relation is not mutual. A model may use the earlier violence or rescue as state-bound evidence; it may not make the current counterpart confess, remember, consent to or forgive an experience the source has not given them. V11 §§3–4/6, V12 §§3/6 and V13 §§5/8/9 supply the discriminating cases.

## 3. Subaru: receiving value without purchasing indispensability

V10's family trial does not reveal that Subaru's prior care was fake. It explains why care he actually received could become hard to accept. Childhood distinction, comparison with Kenichi, social performance, withdrawal and the fear that affection should have ended form a developmental problem. Naoko's and Kenichi's simulated responses let him receive love that does not require a successful imitation. The grief is partly that such care cannot now be repaid to the real parents. V10 `p-015#B0324–0408/B0478–0482` and `p-017#B0167–0239` preserve that specific history.

The later arc does not simply repeat the same parental lesson in louder forms. It tests different obstacles to receiving it. In V11 Otto asks whether Subaru is all right because he looks too calm; Subaru initially converts the concern into praise for his reasoning. Captivity shows that being fed and kept alive can coexist with intolerable isolation. Otto's refusal of a profitable silence then breaks Subaru's advantage-only explanation. The actual friendship and received thanks are an event, not a timeless relationship label. V11 `p-008#B0069–0104/B0528–0564/B0653–0706`; `p-010#B0001–0038`.

Echidna supplies another genuine good: a place to speak the forbidden burden and be heard without hurry. Her interest in his account does not make the relief imaginary. The trouble is what Subaru infers from the relief. Probably unlimited returns becomes confidence beyond the evidence; protecting him from a fatal impasse becomes his responsibility to prevent everyone else's loss by spending only himself. The narrative then shows copies spent, helpers grieved or excluded, and bodies that matter to people other than their owner. His arithmetic of one cheap life has omitted much of the cost. V11 §8; V12 §§5/8/9; V13 §4.

V13's desire to live is therefore a new answer, not an oath never to risk himself or never to use RBD. Familiar voices matter partly because their provenance is not literal arrival of all those people into the tea. Patrasche's costly rescue and Otto's anger show available care outside the exclusive confidant he imagined. Accepting an unordered partial account gives Otto a place in the problem while preserving the real dangerous limit around RBD disclosure. This is a consequential alternative to both total secrecy and total transparency. It cannot be generalized into a claim that every omitted fact is now justified.

Subaru continues to perform. He wants to look impressive before Emilia, uses bluster against Roswaal and manages fear before the Rabbit. Some performance sustains action; some obscures what another person is asking. The actual distinction is not authentic speech versus every mask. In V15 he can state a need for Beatrice rather than merely offer his service, but that need also becomes pressure. His dust explosion fails; the others' oil fallback works at cost. The ending rewards neither infallible expertise nor a wholly purified motive.

A durable candidate tendency is his effort to convert care into a reason he can justify occupying someone's future. The changed capacity is that he can sometimes accept being wanted before proving uniquely necessary and can ask for help from people with their own aims. The adverse cases remain real: Beatrice's refused entry, the copies' coerced expenditure, Emilia's unfulfilled specific request and conscious gate sacrifice. The supported model is DEVELOPMENTAL and RECIPIENT/STRESS_BOUND, not a universal cure or an assertion that he has learned nothing.

## 4. Emilia: her own task is not the refusal of all support

Emilia repeatedly distinguishes doing the trial from being left alone to do it. In E014 she names responsibility, political opportunity and concern for Subaru's injuries when refusing his substitution. Later she asks for someone beside her effort, watching and cheering. These are actual requested terms; her distress does not secretly authorize the replacement she refuses. Sedation and an unconsulted departure remove opportunities for her answer even when Subaru has urgent protective reasons. V11 `p-001#B0139–0175/B0407–0446`; V13 `p-007#B0084–0161`.

The overnight hand gives the abstract problem a measurable duration. Emilia asks him to stay until morning; he agrees and she wakes to an empty hand before it. His wall writing is eventually received and changes what the absence means to her. It does not keep the promise. Her refusal to forgive without an apology survives affectionate touching of the message. V13 `p-015#B0095–0108/B0130–0138`; `p-019#B0060–0068/B0170–0178`; `p-027#B0120–0153`. The important comparison is not kind words versus no kind words, but the support actually requested versus the support the helper chooses to supply.

V14 gives promise a childhood history. Fortuna's ordinary discipline joins love to trust without making every breach equally serious. Emilia learns to turn the sad maternal intensifier すごーく toward happy uses, comforts Geuse and applies the promise rule back to an adult's concealed parent information. Those ordinary acts make later language and obligation intelligible. Fortuna is her lived mother and paternal aunt, not thereby her biological mother. Geuse's tender phase and Cult relief work coexist with later catastrophic history; one institutional label cannot erase time or faction. V14 §§4–5/11.

The trial confronts deliberate violence and altered perception, not merely a child whose inadequacy caused everything. Geuse's fatal strike is misidentified; Pandora's manipulation and threat remain causal. Emilia's own power freezes the forest, but that is not intent to freeze every inhabitant. First success is a present decision about regret and the value of love received, not a reversal of those deaths. The first trial's correction account, Puck's later lid, Pandora's original selective edit and Roswaal's separate contract interference have different agents, times and recipients. V14 §5; V15 B18.

The second trial then offers a wanted life that is explicitly impossible. Fortuna's hairdressing, Geuse's picnic and proxy Archi's conversation do not become a verified adult routine or surviving family. The third trial's possible disasters do not become advance convictions of named future actors. An unnamed guide's tenderness and claimed maternal knowledge are not a named biological mother. Emilia can accept a meaningful encounter while leaving its withheld answer unforced. V15 B07–11.

Practical leadership remains corrigible after all three passes. Emilia first misunderstands the barrier shutdown, listens to Shima's correction and undertakes the second stage. She proposes moving the whole crystal before accepting Shima's stated key-role. She promises copies a life beyond their function without already implementing freedom for each. On the battlefield she receives a concrete task with an alternative, objects to Subaru monopolizing the worst burden and makes the difficult enclosure. Her self-icing and the others' aid prevent this becoming perfect magical mastery. V15 B12/B21–23/B29–31.

The ending does update the relationship: spoken gladness at Subaru's confession is stronger than private warmth, knighthood is actually accepted and the dance is shared. But the kiss-pregnancy mistake and offpage correction do not settle her understanding at the earlier kiss. No own reciprocal romantic love declaration or accepted multi-partner terms are supplied. The new finger promise remembers the old breach and schedules an origin conversation; EOF does not show that telling or the missing overnight apology. Roswaal's performed apology is a different person's response to a different demand. V15 B35/B38–43. Emilia's affirmative agency includes choosing help, correction, limits and future discussion, not becoming the reward for Subaru's improved self-image.

## 5. Beatrice: choosing a person instead of fulfilling a vacant title

The earlier library is both a place where Beatrice helps and a place where Subaru can presume access to that help. V10's tears and tool refusal already give a contrary proposition; later success cannot erase it. V11 complicates the rejection rather than exposing all affection as false. She heals, interrupts Subaru's self-killing, consults her book and claims her conduct followed it. Subaru's final attempt to delay Elsa protects Beatrice even after the claim wounds him. Neither person's totalizing interpretation exhausts what the scene shows. V10 `p-004#B0131–0175`; `p-006#B0021–0046`; V11 §§4/7.

V12's blank pages remove the simple explanatory machine. Beatrice's request for help is real, and so is her subsequent return to a death request and first-priority condition Subaru cannot honestly accept. Previous compassionate visitors did not settle what would make her life worth continuing. Her protection, combat and final ejection of Subaru are choices inside that contradiction, not proof she never meant refusal or wanted nothing but death. The analysis need not decide that a good rescuer should have found the right phrase on the first attempt. It must decide that asking cannot determine in advance what the recipient's answer is allowed to mean. V12 `p-009#B0176–0422`; §§7/9.

V14 and Beatrice's own V15 remembered life give waiting a social past. Meyer wanted literacy; the sleeve release and promised ordinary future make loss particular. Beatrice's frightening door prank received an objection. She later saw many would-be rescuers, sometimes wavered, and chose distance from innocent Emilia amid joy and envy at Puck's fulfilled role. These are not four hundred years of no action or no relations. The mother's limited knowledge-preservation commission and two imperfect copies must remain distinct from Beatrice's later fear that everything, even neglect, was preplanned. V14 §6; V15 B01/B24–25.

Subaru's repeated current entries are not a completed ethic of consent. He does not understand the shrinking door choices, and actual ejections remain actual refusals. His eventual appeal changes the direction of help: he asks her to help him live, acknowledges that he cannot know all her centuries and refuses to impersonate the designated visitor. Yet narration preserves the selfish pressure of making his need a reason for her existence. The finite tomorrow is not a warranty that no loneliness or death can recur. V15 `p-018#B0161–0230/B0416–0523`.

Her grasp and naming are later acts she actually performs. She chooses Subaru as her first rather than demanding to be his exclusive first; the text distinguishes 親愛 from 恋愛. The contract's exclusivity is a costly artificial-spirit exception, not a retroactive romantic property right. Its existence permits immediate defense but does not create an inexhaustible combat asset. The reserve and library are lost; Subaru ordinarily supplies daily existence, not another four-century store. V15 B28/B37.

This is why the resolution is a selected future rather than the recovery of a true original role. Beatrice can make an attachment without validating the waiting experiment or every method used to reach her. Her limited homecoming with Roswaal expressly stops further confession; her later awkward friendship with Petra is new low-stakes evidence, not replacement of Meyer. The rival readings remain falsifiable: pure hero-waiting fails against refusal and rejected designation; total inability to choose fails against old wavering and the hand; all-methods-justified fails against narrated pressure; romance/free-power fails against wording and resources. V15 B02/B24–28/B33/B37/B43.

## 6. Garfiel and the people a protector cannot contain

Garfiel's position begins as a combination of care, grievance, selective valuation and force, not a timeless aggression score. Magical blood eligibility differs from political confinement. His own exit and different ancestry cannot be generalized to every resident. The miasma channel is later specified through Shima; scent does not independently prove Cult membership or explain every response. In E015 his tiger kills rescuers; in E016 he rescues Subaru and refuses to spend him as bait. Both remain conditional evidence, not actions remembered or confessed by the current Garfiel. V11 §§3/5/6; V12 §§3–4; V13 §9.

His mother's departure becomes a reason to protect by preventing departure. He wanted her happiness; an apparent failure to reach it makes separation unbearable. Subaru's conjecture of simple mother-hatred is denied, and his intrusive questioning is not neutral therapy. The renewed trial allows a different hearing and an interacting sister proxy, without independently inspecting the real mother's body or obtaining current Frederica's full testimony. Garfiel distinguishes defeat from believing every victor right. His subsequent choice to help retains the condition that his grandmothers not become unhappy. V13 `p-022#B0087–0171`; `p-025#B0183–0282`; `p-027#B0021–0082`.

The defeat itself is a demonstration against the solitary protector. Otto's environmental cooperation and costly spell, Ram's finite burst, Puck's drain, Subaru's gate sacrifice/invisible strike and Patrasche's final charge supply different conditions. Patrasche has a current remembered first-day defeat, not an erased-state revenge memory. The power of numbers is not merely a way to rename Subaru's victory. It is the failure of Garfiel's claim that one body can be everyone's barrier. V13 §§8–9.

V14 lets Garfiel answer Roswaal himself. V15 adds reading that he actually likes, partly sustained by attention to Ram's reaction, and fallible book knowledge used to interpret Elsa. His eyesight-range claim is a bluff; real nearby earth alteration, ground contact and incomplete bodily repair retain limits. Willed beast use changes the relation to his bloodline, not the amount of pain or the availability of infinite recovery. V14 §7; V15 B14.

Elsa's costly protection of Meili matters causally without exonerating her attacks. Her reported childhood is not independently verified national culture or a clinical explanation. Garfiel thinks that protection makes his victory possible; he then faces unease at his first current human killing. The analyst's memory of E015 cannot replace his lack of that memory. Apparent crushing, the will-less remnant and final fire are distinct stages, not an exact universal vampire life quota. V15 B15–17.

Frederica and Petra prevent a brother/hero-only causal story. Frederica's continued training, actual rear guard and rescue of Meili contradict Garfiel's assumption that service meant inactivity. Petra supplies child-bounded work, remembered navigation, repellent and a moral refusal to forgive. Their competence does not make the risks assigned to them costless. The sibling reunion, drink instruction, awkward formal clothes and Petra/Beatrice dance then widen ordinary recipient coverage without proving Garfiel's future loyalty, a complete maternal history or permanent self-command. V14 §9; V15 B03/B06/B17/B35/B43.

## 7. Created persons, a chosen role and a compromised account of choice

Sanctuary cannot be understood through either a benevolent refuge or a laboratory cover story alone. Its protection has given Meyer a home she values while collecting people for the creator's safety and a compatible barrier core. Meyer's stated offer matters; age, danger, indebtedness and withheld alternatives also matter. Beatrice did not know the life-cost and proposes appeal, combat, healing and door evacuation. Those alternatives are considered, not independently disproved by the urgency of the scene. V14 `p-006#B0304–0370`; `p-008#B0057–0169`.

The copies further separate manufacture from acquired personhood. Bilma's tea preference can matter even under her account that the body need not eat. Named representatives have a shared office without one shared interior; provisional Piko and every old caretaker body do not become securely identified because later names are available. Alma rejects Subaru's pity and chooses what help she wants. Garfiel can protect his grandmothers while commanding ordinary copies as dolls. Subaru can explicitly disavow rank-by-origin and still exploit command authority. V12 §5; V13 §6.

V12's secrecy order and body expenditure are therefore actual choices with unequal power, not neutral mechanics to be erased with the state. V13's refusal to compel Alma's truthful answer is a meaningful contrasting act, not proof that all remaining leverage is gone. A command right is neither a new Cult office nor free agreement by the commanded. A promise to let surviving copies do as they please after reaching the tomb does not supply consent before the explosions.

V15 complicates an apparently final sacrificial role. Emilia proposes moving the whole crystal; Shima explains why she is the key and names the remaining representatives. The rite's observed disappearance and Emilia's sacrifice interpretation are genuine cast-side events. The reader-only coda then states gradual domination by an implanted soul fragment and forcible overlay onto the original body. That is strong evidence against transparent annihilation and wholly informed choice. It is not grounds to declare every earlier attachment counterfeit or to invent the final fate of every residual personality. V15 B12/B23/B44–45.

The continuity problem stays bounded. The departing Witch directly chooses the name Omega; context strongly suggests the tea Echidna, while creator, examiner, corpse and composite are not thereby completely equated. Beatrice recognizes her original mother's corpse, which differs from the tea face. Roswaal's confident fake designation is contested, not made analytical law. The Witch's report that an unnamed youth collected burnt remnants does not name Otto, restore a legible book or give the camp receipt. V15 B13/B32/B44–45. A model that fills those gaps from later canon would destroy the checkpoint's most important nonknowledge.

## 8. Ram and Roswaal: received change, refused recognition

Ram does not merely reveal the meaning of a single unchanging loyalty. Earlier maid labor, wit, practical judgment and selective affection remain context. Her E014 meeting with Rem receives kinship information but not recovered autobiography; she refuses Subaru's ownership of the response he wants. Her later recruitment of Otto is distinguished from simply executing Roswaal's order. Current cooperation has private terms, bodily limits and an independent method. V11 §§4/6; V13 §8; V14 §8.

The nine-year oath preserves real old hatred and a projected revenge ending. Ram's present petition seeks a Roswaal still capable of wanting and living, not the emptied person the old script would award. She asks Emilia for help, acts with Puck and tells Roswaal she loves him. His denial is therefore not lack of delivery. He has heard the claim and refuses its possibility through his model of people organized by unchanging purpose. V14 `p-024#B0110–0189/B0219–0301`; V15 `p-015#B0139–0178`.

Book-burning tests that misrecognition in action.
