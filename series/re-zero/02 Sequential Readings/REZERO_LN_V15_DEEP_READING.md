## 0. Governing thesis and prospective boundary

V15 makes a chosen future possible without making choice pure, rescue omniscient or survival equivalent to freedom. Beatrice's eventual hand matters precisely because she has really refused before it. Emilia's leadership matters because she can learn that her first explanation of the barrier was wrong and still take responsibility. Ram's book-burning acts on changed love rather than the old revenge script Roswaal insists she must follow. Garfiel can choose his inherited body without acquiring unlimited healing or forgetting what violence costs. The volume gives these people actual decisions and actual outcomes; it does not certify permanent recovery.

The central ethical tension is not resolved by the success of the rescue. Subaru's appeal asks Beatrice to help him live, instead of only presenting himself as her savior, but narration preserves the selfish pressure by which he makes his need a reason for her existence. His own uncertainty about why he can keep reaching her prevents an analyst from converting repeated ejections into concealed consent. The accepted hand and new contract are later acts, not a retroactive license for every intrusion. Likewise, Shima's declaration of a chosen key-role is meaningful to Emilia while a reader-only coda destabilizes any claim that the whole choice was transparent or that disappearance settled the soul's fate.

Practical success is distributed and revisable. Subaru's science stunt fails. Otto and Petra make the fallback work at a genuine commercial cost, Frederica completes extraction and chooses further protection, Elsa saves Meili at the cost Garfiel thinks decides his duel, Puck exhausts himself, Emilia shelters and leads, and Beatrice spends a reserve Subaru cannot promptly replace. A victory produced by these distinct acts is neither proof of his perfect plan nor evidence that everyone consented to the same risk. Weather survives destruction of the book because its spell was already set up; a beautiful symbolic defeat does not repeal a maintained causal process.

The public resolution and private sequel actively disagree. The household can obtain an oath, impose blows and receive an apology without unanimously forgiving Roswaal or informing the outside victims. He remains indispensable through patronage and privately threatens to incur his own sanction to force Subaru's perfect outcome. The vow therefore is not a guarantee that he cannot act. The reader's Rabbit perspective similarly prevents “defeat” from becoming biological extinction: exile leaves continuing suffering. The closing dance and accepted knighthood are genuine new mutual history, but the next room conversation is still a promise, romance remains unnegotiated and the old overnight breach has not received its own apology.

## 4. Source-facing findings and exact evidence routes

### V15-B01 — Blank pages and desire to matter

Beatrice opens the actually blank book, says she tried to alter its blankness, and describes the fear that even death would go unnoticed. Her inference that Roswaal's book foretells her death is not a read page or certain forecast; Subaru's suggestion that her book is faulty is explicitly sophistry.

Evidence: p-001#B0137–0200.

### V15-B02 — Refusal before changed choice

The initial request to become the designated person is refused; Beatrice ejects Subaru and later repeats that action. Her eventual changed decision cannot retrospectively turn those refusals into assent or prove that Subaru always understood her door selection.

Evidence: p-001#B0245–0270; p-018#B0161–0230.

### V15-B03 — Completed room extraction and divided escort

Frederica rescues Subaru, Otto and Petra to the lawn with sleeping Rem secure on her back. Subaru then carries Rem and Otto Petra while Frederica elects rear-guard action; these are actual handoffs, not a later universal casualty census.

Evidence: p-001#B0291–0314; p-003#B0003–0051.

## 5. Beatrice: a selected finite future, not refusal erased

The book's blankness is visible and long investigated by its holder; her interpretation of that blankness as evidence that she never mattered is not itself a page of prophecy. The mother's actual farewell contains a purpose and contingency, whereas the role's four-century emotional meaning emerges from Beatrice's own life. Many visitors tried to bring her out, she sometimes wanted to accept, and Puck's fulfilled companionship could make her happy and envious at once. Her avoidance of innocent Emilia is a decision made in that mixed condition, not proof that she never chose anything or hated the girl for a romantic rival's reason. B01/B24–25 separate actual instruction from later totalization.

Subaru is distinctive because he repeatedly addresses Beatrice rather than only the library's power. That recognition is meaningful to her but still does not mean that every knock is welcome. The present sequence contains actual rejection and actual ejection. He initially acts as if entry proves permission, yet his own focalization admits he does not know the changing door conditions. A narrator can reveal a protective ejection without turning an express refusal into an invitation. The coercive side of his offer also remains explicit: he makes his need for companionship a reason she should continue living. The ethical reading must keep pressure and meaningful eventual agency together rather than choosing a clean rescuer or a wholly false choice. B02/B26–28.

Her final grasp and naming are new action, not his fantasy of future agreement. Rejecting the designated-person title permits a bond whose time scale is finite. She does not demand that she be his exclusive first; she selects him as hers. The contract's later exclusivity is a mechanical exception with costs, not a romantic property right. Explicit affection rather than romance and the depletion disclosure prevent the power reward from swallowing the person rescued. Losing the library and stored power does not make the rescue fail; gaining a contractor does not prove loneliness, resentment or social difficulty permanently solved. Her awkward friendship dance and bounded Roswaal homecoming supply a low-stakes next phase with remaining boundaries.

Rivals remain discriminable. “She was simply waiting for a hero” fails against repeated refusals and the rejected title. “She could never exercise choice” fails against old wavering/avoidance and the actual hand. “Every method is justified because she is alive” fails against narrated pressure and his ignorance. “Contract means romance or free unlimited power” fails against explicit wording and resource conditions. A later source could materially revise the current arrangement through received terms, refusal, changed supply or an actual different choice; none is prefilled here.

## 6. Emilia: revisable knowledge, chosen responsibility and uncompleted intimacy

The second trial offers what the first could not restore: an adult household future in which care survives. Its value does not depend on being literal history. The duplicate flower and proxy Archi prevent the reader from treating the picnic as an actual adult preference record or the original brother's knowledge of her current future. The reflection scene ties present bodily estrangement to a specific wake-after-growth history; it does not license an abstract clinical age or a claim that a trial jump is an actual death. Echidna's near-tears hatred and the later guide's tenderness are separate encounters, neither of which supplies a named biological mother. B07–11.

After passing, Emilia still has practical lessons to learn. She initially believes the coffin break ends the barrier; Shima's explanation corrects that causal model. Her ability to accept correction is part of agency, not evidence that the earlier claim was secretly complete. She proposes a whole-crystal evacuation rather than immediately endorsing a sacrifice, then accepts the key's stated choice and takes on aftermath responsibility. Her promise of lives beyond the copies' role recognizes subjects not exhausted by surveillance function, but it remains a promise. The covert coda further restricts the analyst's assurance that every component of Shima's choice was transparent. B12/B23/B44.

Her leadership includes anger and physical insistence toward the defeated Roswaal as well as shelter, transport commands and requested help. ## 7. Garfiel, Frederica, Elsa and Petra: selective care and the cost of winning

Garfiel's acceptance of his bloodline changes how he fights, not the fact that bodies have limits. The eyesight-range boast is explicitly false; the real nearby earth alteration and ground-contact recovery remain useful without becoming limitless range or immunity. Reading becomes diagnostic ordinary evidence because he likes it, because Ram's sadness matters to the habit, and because book knowledge actively informs his interpretation of Elsa. It is neither merely a comic idiom tic nor universal knowledge. Pain, partial repair and his estimate of remaining fatal blows keep the power model contingent. B14.

Elsa's account of childhood and blood warmth is speech to a particular opponent, not a verified biography of an entire country or a clinical cause excusing her conduct. Her protection of Meili is directly costly. Garfiel later thinks it enables his win, so care has a causal place in a lethal antagonist's action without becoming redemption or reciprocal romance. The apparent ending requires later qualification: crushed defeat is followed by a moving remnant, the still-breathing beast's death and final backdraft destruction. Combining them into one unlimited regeneration or treating the earlier failed dust blast as the final mechanism would erase the actual sequence. B15–17.

Frederica is not a helpless sister defined by Garfiel's assumption that household service meant no training. Her return, continuing practice, defense and Meili rescue are her own contributions. Petra's remembered route, repellent, Rem-care priority and moral nonforgiveness likewise exceed the role of frightened beneficiary. Neither child's courage nor sibling care licenses all dangerous expenditure. Petra's later competent dance and Frederica's comfort/etiquette instruction matter because they show agency after immediate peril rather than making combat the whole person.

Garfiel's first-current-human-kill unease cannot be replaced by the analyst's knowledge that an earlier Garfiel murdered people in a discarded state. That history remains probative about conditional violence but is not his remembered practice here. His continued anger at Roswaal is not backsliding that disproves all growth; it is a justified conflict inside conditional followership. The current rite's child language, rejected marriage terms and different recipients remain in force. A character model must preserve these contrasts rather than turn one victory into obedience, absolution or adult certainty.

## 8. Ram and Roswaal: love that changes its target, control that survives its book

Ram's original hatred and present love are both temporally real. Roswaal's error is not that he never heard the new claim: he hears and denies it, insisting that someone organized around an unchanged purpose could not have changed in this way. The book strike acts against that misrecognition. She targets an object because she wants a living capacity to want, not an emptied patron awarded by an old revenge script. Her finite body and dependency do not cancel that agency, but they also prevent a clean autonomous victory story. B18–21.

The book is thrown into existing green-burning fallen-log flames; no unsupported new Ram fire spell is needed. Roswaal's anger blast is an actual injury, and his later reflexive maintenance is not permission for it. Emilia verifies life rather than reversing a completed death; Beatrice's treatment prevents scars without restoring a horn. Ram can thank a healer and reject that healer's authority over whom she loves. Neither this refusal nor the analyst's recognition of coercive conditions should erase the real harm or convert the relationship into a healthy consensual whole.

Roswaal's different speech to Beatrice reveals continuity that a mere family-inherited-longing theory cannot retain. Narration confirms soul transfer through descendants; it does not establish their consent, every technical step or universal immortality. Beatrice's grief-driven slap, recognition and cutoff of further confession make the homecoming expressly limited. Her claim about descendants' standing to judge is a character's moral rule, not the analysis acquitting original or current crimes. His personal care and instrumental cruelty are co-present rather than mutually exclusive.

The lost guide creates a real crisis, but not a new benevolent goal. The ceremony, apology and retained patronage can coexist with private perfect-save coercion. His threat to kill survivors and accept the mark's consequence directly matters: a penalty may deter or punish without making action impossible. Emilia's later attention-seeking explanation is her limited interpretation without hearing the threat, not evidence that the threat is harmless theater. A current model must select public patron, private original disciple and private coercive ally without leaking their confidences to one another's recipients. B32–41.

## 9. Institutions, resources and causal restraint after success

The collective settlement is not a unanimous moral vote. Frederica invokes debt and gratitude; Garfiel disputes that debt licenses loss of life; Otto explicitly acknowledges anger and negotiates concessions; Petra refuses to forgive while declining to obstruct; Emilia requires actual apology. Subaru's camp needs the patron's resources. These distinct positions explain continued cooperation without conferring innocence. Petra is present as staff, not as a village's elected representative, and the outside victims lack the mastermind disclosure. Sleeping Rem's body is not informed assent to the group's arrangement. B34–36.

Material accounting continues after the emotionally decisive scene. Otto's oil is actually consumed and he demands repayment; no sale, payroll or paid appointment is shown. The mansion/library are lost and the temporary related household has accommodated about a month. Actual knighting gives Subaru a new accepted relation and political credit before a merely announced capital ceremony, not national office, marriage or a skill grant. Practical authority, public credit and private service must be tracked separately.

Causal restraint is equally important. Destroying the book does not cancel the maintained weather channel. Passing a trial does not switch off both barrier sites by itself. A short warp and a separate-dimension exile are spatial effects, not returns. Beatrice's simultaneous-population account is a useful theory, while the actual gathered swarm and its later perspective establish displacement without universal species extinction. Exile's continuing starvation is not a neutral word for painless destruction. The volume has solved an immediate defense problem without proving every model used to solve it.

The coda then exposes the limit of a cast-side cost ledger: Shima's and the original body's disappearance did not settle the entire soul process. Gradual domination and forcible overlay are directly narrated, but exactly how all past/dream/corpse/composite identities map remains unresolved. Likewise, the Witch's report of a youth collecting remnants does not authorize naming Otto or declaring a fully repaired legible book. Neither gap justifies a later-source lookup. It becomes a sharply stated horizon question, with cast nonknowledge intact.
