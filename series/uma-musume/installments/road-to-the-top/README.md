# ROAD TO THE TOP

**Uma Musume: Pretty Derby — ROAD TO THE TOP / ウマ娘 プリティーダービー ROAD TO THE TOP** is the initial four-episode 2023 web-anime target; animation: CygamesPictures. Production metadata is verified through the [official anime site](https://umamusume.jp/contents/anime/roadtothetop/). The [production register](01_Sources/UM_RTTT_PRODUCTION_METADATA.md) separates these facts from secondary English labels and from source availability.

The research program asks how Narita Top Road, Admire Vega and T.M. Opera O understand excellence, supporters, obligation, personal identity and rivals; how their spoken self-presentations compare with conduct; and how racing, music, voice, composition and editing shape viewers' experience. Grief, guilt, theatricality, recognition and intimacy are questions to test, not findings. Historical adaptation is a separate contextual pathway.

## Source readiness

Technical decoding and track tags do not establish exact edition identity, Japanese wording, subtitle synchronization or a completed audiovisual reading. No narrative source has been admitted and no episode has been analyzed. See the [readiness audit](05_Run_State/UM_RTTT_READINESS_AUDIT.md) before claiming readiness to execute.
