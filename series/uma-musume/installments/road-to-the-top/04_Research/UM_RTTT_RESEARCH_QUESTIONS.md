# Road to the Top — Research questions and hypotheses

These questions turn the user's requested directions into tests. **No listed psychological description, formal pattern or adaptation choice is an established finding.** All narrative-evidence links are initially absent; question rows are usable research records, not source observations. Read the [method](../00_Method/UM_RTTT_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md), [architecture](../00_Method/UM_RTTT_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) and [current-state boundary](../../../CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) before testing them.

## Register rules

`UNTESTED` means no admitted-source investigation has answered the question. Other states are `PARTIAL`, `SUPPORTED_WITHIN_BOUNDARY`, `CONTESTED`, `NOT_SUPPORTED_WITHIN_BOUNDARY`, `REVISED`, `DEFERRED_CONTEXT` and `OPEN_AFTER_CORPUS`. A status is always paired with the episode/source frontier and actual evidence. “Not observed” becomes disconfirming only when coverage and the test make absence meaningful.

Question IDs are permanent. A precise hypothesis may be appended under its question before the relevant new exposure, with the competing explanation and what would disconfirm it. Do not backdate a prediction after seeing its answer. Later reinterpretation retains the earlier formulation and exposure boundary. Substantive supported answers become `RTTT-CLM...` records linked to `OBS`, `CS`, `RS`, `RACE` and context IDs; updating a status is not itself evidence promotion.

## Hypothesis transaction form

Append an actual test below its stable question or in a linked claim record. Do not populate the fields with imagined episode content.

| Field | Required entry when a test is undertaken |
| --- | --- |
| Question/hypothesis | `RTTT-HYP...`; retain the original question and proposed precise statement |
| Recorded before exposure | Actual date/run and entering knowledge; disclose spoilers or retrospective formulation |
| Permitted test frontier | Episode(s), source ID/edition, context sources and inspection scope |
| Expected observation | Observable result that would support the bounded proposition |
| Counter-test | Specific evidence or adequately covered absence that would weaken it |
| Actual evidence | `OBS`/state/race/context IDs, routes and timestamps; no general reference to “the episode” |
| Assessment | Scope-limited state, confidence reason, strongest rival explanation and unresolved channels |
| Disposition | Linked `CLM` ID if an argument is established; otherwise retain a question |
| Revision | Prior formulation/status, transition, changed evidence boundary and downstream affected artifacts |

## First E01 research priority

Once the [E01 next-run prerequisites](../05_Run_State/UM_RTTT_E01_NEXT_RUN.md) are satisfied, begin with identity/goal/relationship baselines and whatever formal construction the whole admitted episode actually presents. Questions 0001–0017 are observation prompts, not a checklist demanding every phenomenon in E01. Create race, grief, vocal-cue or intimacy entries only when relevant source material is encountered. Preserve unanswered questions for later authorized units without consulting those units early.

Historical context and the theatrical comparison are separate deferred pathways. They do not block an adequately sourced E01 narrative reading. No franchise-wide conclusion may be inferred from this initial question register.
