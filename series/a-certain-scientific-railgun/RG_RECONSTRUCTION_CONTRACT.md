# A Certain Scientific Railgun — reconstruction pathway

Character reconstruction is an intended derived responsibility where a named subject and state have sufficient evidence. It is not a narrative source, a replacement for literary analysis or an automatic dossier for familiar franchise characters. Current subject roster, evidence dossiers, models, simulations and capability grades: **none**. Readiness: **NOT_ASSESSED**; manga V01–20 is unadmitted.

Use the [generic reconstruction method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md). Capture evidence prospectively during complete reading: action and motivation, values in conflict, ordinary repertoire, directional relationship behavior, knowledge and developmental intervals, Japanese voice/register/address, performance or role context, contradictions, negative constraints and actual unobserved opportunities. Source names are identity leads, not model parameters. Manga can support inspected drawn behavior and text register; it cannot support unseen voice acting or motion.

Each dossier is work-, edition-, continuity-, time- and relationship-qualified. No state from Railgun, Accelerator, Mental Out, Astral Buddy or another franchise work is silently interchangeable. A cross-title identity bridge does not by itself license transfer of biography, knowledge, behavior or dialogue register. Any later mixed-witness model needs independently justified correspondence, a declared comparison source lock and explicit contradiction handling.

Full readings/claim reconciliation precede a bounded evidence dossier; a separate operational model follows only if its proposed use is supported. A monograph integrates psychology, development, relationships, language, visual form and thematic function; it is a distinct literary argument, never operational certification. The model states valid contexts, negative constraints, unknowns and abstention rules. Sparse or partial evidence may justify only a narrow model or none; no trait universal is inferred from a prominent scene.

Before construction, an independent evaluator controls held-out source/context allocation and an exposure record, keeping evaluation answers out of the fitting packet. Test ordinary and high-stakes cases, conflicting values, recipient-conditioned language, time-state selection, counterexamples and impossible knowledge. Shared or near-identical source appearances are not independent cases. Use qualitative failure analysis, not invented probabilities. Already consumed material supports only `RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST`; a fresh session does not make it held-out. If true held-out material is unavailable, retain that gap and provisional/unusable scope openly.

Generated dialogue/scenarios are labeled synthetic and kept outside literary evidence. They neither validate themselves nor feed later source observations. Only the designated reconstruction evaluator grades capability in its governed registry; no grade or global discovery/registry edits are made here.

## Operational model contract

A future model must be more than a personality summary. It names its exact source and valid state, separates relatively stable dispositions from developmental state, current knowledge, relationship context and temporary conditions, and expresses conditional rules: given this situation and information, these goals or appraisals make this response plausible; these exceptions, rival motives and falsifying observations limit the rule. Every rule routes to specific evidence and counterevidence. Preserve the evidence's original scope instead of treating a repeated phrase as a universal law.

Separate appraisal and decision from their expression: what the subject notices or misunderstands, competing goals and value priorities, choice/avoidance/repair, then interlocutor-conditioned wording, address, register and silence. A linguistic model carries native examples, permitted variants, negative constraints and uncertainty; it does not imitate an adaptation's uninspected performance. Describe confidence through evidence support and distance from observed contexts, with explicit abstention outside supported identity, time, knowledge, relationship, continuity or modality. No invented numerical likelihoods substitute for calibration.

Before an authorized prediction test, freeze the model version, state selector, input situation, permitted evidence, predicted response/rationale and falsifiers without access to the withheld outcome. The independent evaluator then adjudicates against actual source evidence and a documented simpler baseline, preserving failures, competing interpretations and leakage limits. Testing a revised model on the answer that motivated its revision is retrospective consistency, not fresh confirmation. Keep prospective prediction, observed outcome, adjudication and model revision as distinct records. This operational specification supplements the adopted generic reconstruction method's §§9–12 and 14–18; no model or prediction is created by defining it.

**Readiness row unit:** one source-earned subject at one valid state. Fields: work/subject identity; source lock and admitted horizon; chronology/continuity/knowledge/relationship selectors; coverage across behavior, ordinary life, register, development and contradictions; exact evidence/readings; missing versus negative evidence; dossier/model routes; held-out membership/exposure and test class; reviewer, failure/abstention limits, predecessor and next operation. Update only on inspected evidence or independent assessment, preserving previous state decisions. Current rows: **none**.
