# A Certain Scientific Railgun — synthesis architecture

## 1. Purpose and proportion

This is an `INITIAL` architecture for long manga sequence, adapted from the [generic architecture template](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MULTI_DOCUMENT_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE_TEMPLATE.md). Its organizing burden is **longitudinal causal and knowledge transitions across the twenty-volume acquisition**. It governs bootstrap responsibilities only; no owner run approval, source admission or literary conclusion is asserted.

Twenty observed volumes justify resumable longitudinal infrastructure from the first unit, while no narrative arc, cast state or chronology is yet established. Preserve chapter arguments and volume transactions, with checkpoint freezes at source-earned causal/state boundaries and session handoffs. A sequence of volume synopses is inadequate: later synthesis must be able to recover causal chains, perspective changes and the visual construction of action or ordinary interaction across the supplied span. Existing franchise familiarity is a particular contamination risk because the manga shares a setting with other titled works whose evidence has not been read here.

## 4. Character, relationship and specialist synthesis

Character and relationship interpretation remains required whether separate monographs exist or not. A subject study is justified by a sustained question across contexts, temporal development or a reconstruction target, with ordinary-life and contrary evidence represented. A protagonist name or high citation count alone is insufficient. Monographs must integrate behavior, psychology, language, relations, visual form and thematic role, citing claim versions and precise sources rather than broad “see volume” references.

**Self-contained character monographs.** Individual monographs follow the [initiation policy's self-contained character monograph standard](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md#self-contained-character-monograph-standard) (§5.5.1). Each qualified character receives one canonical portrait per declared boundary at `studies/RG_<CHARACTER_ID>_<BOUNDARY>_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md`, readable on its own for that person's identity, history, psychology, reasoning, conduct, Japanese speech, directed relationships, ordinary life, capabilities, development, compact conditional profile, uncertainty and locators. Eligibility is assessed with the standard's evidence test and outcome vocabulary at the character/specialist readiness gate (§6), recorded in that gate's audit and carried forward by each later boundary audit, which adds outcomes and monograph paths, and reassessed when admitted evidence materially expands. Supporting characters and antagonists are assessed on the same test as leads. A qualified character's portrait is not left indefinitely to State rows, Claims/Form entries or relationship studies; a character who does not yet qualify keeps that coverage until reassessment.

Railgun portraits rest only on this manga's admitted, inspected pages. Drawn behavior and exact Japanese lettering support written register and visual presentation, not voiced performance or animation. A Certain Magical Index, the other spin-offs, adaptations and analyst familiarity supply no history, motive or trait. A portrait whose evidence ends at the acquired V01–20 horizon states that boundary and never presents it as the character's whole story. Production follows accepted readings and reconciled State and Claims/Form records, returning to decisive pages where a portrait depends on contested history, motive, preference or wording. Monographs synthesize and link State, Claims/Form, readings and studies rather than copying them, and their compact profile claims no reconstruction model, test or capability grade. No roster, qualification result, index or monograph exists at this bootstrap.

The anticipated specialist question is **institutional explanations versus demonstrated constraints**. Institutions, rules and collective constraints, if recurring, need both asserted-rule and demonstrated-consequence records. Do not treat a character's explanation or a diagram as a proven universal rule. A dedicated institutional/power study is conditional on recurring evidence and an independent interpretive burden; ordinary-life, language and relationship evidence retain equal architectural standing. It activates only when direct reading establishes recurrence, independent accumulation, later interpretive consequence, no adequate existing home and retrieval need. Otherwise the continuous synthesis explicitly discharges the dimension, recording why no independent specialist was needed. A shared-franchise study is not created just to route four titles.

Reconstruction follows its separate evidence dossier → state-bounded model → independent evaluation pathway. Evidence capture begins during sequential work; modeling is future derived use and never replaces the source-facing literary layer. Its dossier, model and any monograph are separate responsibilities, and evaluator capability grades remain outside this author's authority.

## 5. States, contradictions and revision

State selectors include work, immutable edition/language, source disclosure frontier, depicted time/uncertainty, continuity/frame, character state, knowledge and relationship direction. The supplied manga is one witness track, not an assertion of linear story time. A new frame or continuity is added when source-established. Shared franchise identity does not erase local horizons.

Retain previous states and claims append-only. Later knowledge is a new retrospective layer, not a correction to what an earlier character knew. Conflicts first receive a source/attribution/knowledge/time/edition/franchise/interpretive classification; unresolved alternatives remain OPEN. Mutable current pointers reference full history; frozen readings/checkpoints retain bytes and receive errata or superseding generations with reciprocal authority metadata. A source change gets a new alias and locator crosswalk before any citation moves.

## 6. Dependency order and distinct gates

Production order is verified source map → explicit source/run admission → complete chapter/causal-unit readings and ledger transactions → volume/boundary reconciliation and checkpoints → longitudinal reconciliation → role-gap/readiness review and monograph qualification → earned studies and qualified-character monographs → convergence → continuous bounded synthesis → final validation → release/freeze. The analyst's retrieval order begins with the current entrypoint, source/state horizon and relevant full artifacts. A public reader guide, if needed, is written last.

| Gate | Concrete exit condition |
|---|---|
| Bootstrap | Method, architecture and required controls initialized, metadata limits explicit, lock closed |
| Source/run admission | Witness/locator/perception verified for exact scope, exposure and owners recorded, bounded authorization recovered |
| Sequential coverage | Every admitted page/chapter/extra has a disposition, full readings and all transactions accepted; gaps cannot hide behind last-page frontier |
| Longitudinal reconciliation | State, claims, form/register, source and reconstruction coverage consistent with full readings and all material revisions |
| Character/specialist readiness | Role-gap review checks peripheral perspectives, ordinary-life contexts, directionality, language and visual-form omissions; independent questions justified; monograph qualification recorded for each assessed character (§4) |
| Specialist convergence | Required studies and qualified-character monographs complete, conflicts resolved or retained explicitly, responsibilities disjoint; explicit evidence-backed no-study decision permitted |
| Integrated-synthesis readiness | Exact input/horizon frozen, every recurring dimension assigned, counterreadings and limits visible, full artifacts available |
| Final validation | Independent return to decisive pages, causal and alternative-interpretation checks, name/reference review, source locator and authority verification |
| Release/freeze | Exact admitted scope and validated files, limitations, authority state, retained immutable history and next operation recorded |
| Reconstruction evaluation | Separate state/coverage/held-out criteria met and evaluator decision recorded; literary release alone never suffices |

V01–20 coverage alone cannot establish a final whole-series endpoint; a future release may be explicitly limited to the acquired horizon. Later acquisition requires intake and scope review rather than automatic continuation.

## 7. Extension, roles and handoff

Amend the architecture only with source-earned need, changed responsibility, dependency impact, full-row/ID crosswalk and backfill/review plan. Lifecycle becomes EVOLVING through actual reviewed use, STABILIZED/FROZEN at real corpus boundaries, never merely because files were published. No empty ledger/folder exists for visual symmetry.

Reasoning classes: metadata/locators BOUNDED_STANDARD; substantive reading and interpretive updates SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS; character/contradiction/reconstruction DEEP_SYNTHESIS; architecture and integrated synthesis PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST with independent source/visual/knowledge/compression review. The intended reliability gain is detection of propagating source/attribution errors and omitted interpretive roles. Resolve actual models and tools at dispatch under current routing. Fresh-context substantial synthesis receives the full admitted corpus and returns to sources; summaries are navigation only.

The future run owner controls scope/admission/coordination; readers deliver full units and cumulative transactions; independent reviewers test semantics/compression; housekeeping owns routing outputs; discovery and reconstruction evaluators own their global products. Handoffs include complete files and hashes, target root/branch, source and knowledge boundary, drift assessment, pending defects and next owner. Verify full delivered content before authority promotion; never replace a maintained artifact from a partial preview.

### Team for a source reading

Before each authorized chapter or causal sequence, resolve the [source-reading configuration](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EXECUTION_TOPOLOGY_AND_CAPABILITY_ROUTING_POLICY.md#multi-agent-source-reading). The principal inspects the whole admitted unit, authors its full reading and integrates the findings. Give the specialist and a distinct critical reviewer separate contexts, direct STILL_IMAGE access to assigned Japanese pages, and the accepted entering state at this manga's disclosure frontier. Each forms source observations independently of the principal's working account. Freeze prospective findings before later panels or the unit-end synopsis disclose their resolution. Record earlier franchise exposure and withhold affected independent or prospective credit.

**Specialist assignment.** Pair a stated institutional rule or explanation with the depicted sequence that tests its practical effect. Preserve the conditions on that inference, the perspective of a person affected by it, and any ordinary counterexample.

**Critical review.** Return to the full sequence and intervening pages. Test whether an official explanation fits the observed consequences, whose choices the candidate omitted and whether familiarity with Index or an anime supplied a missing causal step. The reviewer records the source routes, counterevidence and any loss of diagnostic evidence during integration. Where practical, the reviewer inspects decisive pages before seeing the candidate reading.

The principal preserves material observations and counterreadings in the full reading, explains each contested disposition and alone coordinates shared state and claim updates. The [unit transaction](RG_CLAIMS_AND_FORM.md#transaction-freeze-and-amendment) records actual roles, context separation, source coverage and reconciliation. Use SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS for a focused interpretive assignment and escalate conflicting attributions or high-impact revisions under §7. A later specialist study or reconstruction evaluation follows its own gates.

An unusually bounded unit may combine specialist and review duties in one context independent of the principal when it can both contribute source-grounded findings and challenge the candidate directly. Justify each use in the transaction. Missing independent participation or required page inspection holds acceptance and frontier advancement. Record the capability gap at the current entrypoint.
