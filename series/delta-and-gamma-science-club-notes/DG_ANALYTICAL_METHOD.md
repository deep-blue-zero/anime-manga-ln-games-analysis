# Analytical method

Read [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) and verify [source register](DG_SOURCE_REGISTER.md) before assigning a source unit. This prose method applies the [medium reference](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_NOVEL_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) and [inspection-lane policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_MEDIUM_AND_INSPECTION_LANE_ROUTING_POLICY.md) to the witness and analytical questions of Delta and Gamma - Science Club Notes. [architecture](DG_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) assigns the resulting records and responsibilities. Its rules are prospective; no example below is a claim about an unread event.

## Build the clue trail before judging the conclusion

Read each authorized Japanese book in witnessed disclosure order, segmenting by the opening of a question, changes in the available evidence and decisions to test or act. A clue first shown to the reader is not necessarily visible to every student; a retrospective narrator can make an earlier moment salient without giving the earlier self that information. Mark scene time, reported past events, disclosure, in-story acquisition and publication/admission separately. Distinguish physical observation, another person's account, experimental assumption, inference and uncertainty.

For each consequential inference identify the rival explanation, data that each would predict, the admissible observations before the solution, the conditions and limits of any test, and whether the result discriminates between the rivals. Seek a negative case: a pattern that fits chance, an unreliable measurement, social convention, or ordinary care as well as an intended clue. Keep a mechanism, the agent responsible, attributed motive and the reasoning chain used to reach them as separate claims. A reveal may establish who acted without validating every step in the investigator's reasoning; plausibility in a textbook is no substitute for what the novel establishes.

Follow the same sequence for interpersonal interpretation without treating a person as an experiment. Capture disclosure, invitations, silence, questions, repair and refusal together with what was at stake and whom the act addressed. Record ordinary club work and differing preferences as evidence of character, without taking cooperation as proof of affinity, absence of disclosure as deception, or contact as reciprocity. Test how the Japanese narration, internal judgment, dialogue and speaker-specific register present knowledge and revise commitments. Distinguish recollection from the current action; preserve ambiguity in scientific wording, honorifics, self-reference and ruby where it changes attribution. Repeated behavior across contexts is stronger than a convenient clue read in hindsight.

## Evidence loci

Use the original Japanese prose and record narrator presentation, attested observation, character report/self-report, inference, hypothesis and unresolved status separately with confidence. Cite V/section/paragraph and public witness/evidence key linked to a tested private EPUB spine/member/paragraph/range for exact wording. Preserve ruby form/readings and label gloss and verified short quotations; retrieval OCR, reflow page numbers, scientific fact-checking and translated wording do not establish what the novel says. Classify images and extras before opening them. Only authorized same-edition illustrations are read in a verified still-image lane, and any claimed fact must still distinguish paratext from narrated event.

## Read-through transaction

Obtain the approved configuration and record actual exposure separately for every participant. Freeze the entering state, unresolved rival readings and any diagnostic prospective tests before exposure; within the unit stop at disclosure boundaries that can resolve those tests. Record what each person or group was in a position to know, and what the text or page has told the reader, at that boundary. Quarantine outside knowledge and accidental later exposure. Document the affected claim and remove prospective credit for affected contributors; a separately frozen uncontaminated account is attributable in its own right.

After brief witness and entering context, place a clearly labeled **Current-unit story synopsis** before the thesis, close reading or ledger inventory. Once the applicable prospective captures are frozen, write coherent, accessible prose following the participants, stakes, concrete actions and mechanics, material disclosures, causal turning points and ending. Attribute testimony, inference and disputed outcomes; retain actual source limits and give stable evidence pointers for load-bearing events. It is a spoiler-bearing orientation to the admitted unit only, kept from all earlier-horizon participants. It neither substitutes for causal argument nor supplies independent evidence.

Complete the canonical reading with a witness/coverage receipt, entering and exit records, the synopsis, causal and formal close reading of decisive and countervailing scenes, character and directed relationship changes, independent review and reconciliation, and a ledger delta or explicit no material change in each required home. Cite source evidence, distinguish reports and inferences, and keep each unresolved material question or coverage debt visible. Freeze prospective judgments separately from later outcomes and synopsis. Unit completion requires the architecture's source and contribution checks, not just a readable draft.
