# Case and relationship ledger

[current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) governs authority; [method](DG_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) supplies observations and [architecture](DG_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) defines destinations. This ledger is initialized with no narrative rows. A unit's checkpoint documents how exposure and independent findings were reconciled. The reading owns the scene and its full observations.

| Row purpose | Required fields |
| --- | --- |
| Clue, account or test | Stable case and evidence IDs; event/deposition order and first reader disclosure; observer, source of report, premise or measured result, protocol/conditions if given, omissions or reliability limits, characters who could have known at that time, exact locator |
| Case decision | Question; competing explanations; predictions under both; datum discriminating between them or nondiagnostic; mechanism distinguished from who designed or noticed it; solution presented, evidential warrant, status and unresolved constraint; argument anchor |
| Qualified person/relationship change | Actor → recipient and context; ordinary behavior, preference or silence before/after with opportunity to act; expectation versus stated feeling, cost and response; rival explanations; current state and retrieval; never impute reciprocity |
| Claim and prospective capture | Claim/test ID and original dated freeze/horizon; deciding evidence and when it became available; exact argument and rival/source IDs; adjudication or later dated transition, current formulation and reason; preserve failed and contaminated tests |
| Monograph qualification | Person ID; assessed boundary, outcome, diagnostic evidence, largest gaps, reassessment trigger, and actual monograph path when created; separate self-containment and fidelity acceptance references |

Key case records by the question the characters can frame, with cross-references when a later disclosure makes two initially distinct cases relevant to one another. A case ending and a relationship update are separate events even when one prompts the other. Map only material changes here; in-unit clue details and localized form stay with the reading.
