# Synthesis architecture

This is the INITIAL architecture for Delta and Gamma - Science Club Notes, governed by [method](DG_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md), the [initiation policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md), the [archive authority policy](../../governance/source-policies/ARCHIVE_AUTHORITY_AND_SUPERSESSION_POLICY.md), and the [continuation policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_SEQUENTIAL_EXECUTION_SCOPE_AND_CONTINUATION_POLICY.md). [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) is the sole first-read surface. This generation has no predecessor or admitted analytical claims. [source register](DG_SOURCE_REGISTER.md) scopes candidate units, conflicts, and exclusions one witness at a time.

## Cumulative homes and specialist dependency

A single [case and relationship ledger](DG_CASE_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md) is sufficient at this initial scale: clue access and social knowledge need the same disclosure clock. Independent retrieval is by stable case/person/claim ID, not one workbook per motif. Full arguments stay at precise anchors in the unit reading and later specialist or character prose; the ledger routes old and current versions, material evidence, rivals and conclusions.

| Question | Sequential capture | Cumulative home and later use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What could be inferred before the solution? | Case problem, genuine options, data and trial conditions, who saw which clue; hold rival readings past the reveal where warranted | [case and relationship ledger](DG_CASE_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md) by case; anticipated specialist comparison of reasoning and disclosure |
| What could a student know or say about another person? | Directed interactions, differing interpretations, costs, permission, nonresponse, ordinary cooperation and missed opportunities | [case and relationship ledger](DG_CASE_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md) by actor and recipient; possible study of club obligations and interpersonal trust |
| What reveals a person across tasks? | Distinctive preferences, registering uncertainty, communication and decisions in and out of investigation scenes | [case and relationship ledger](DG_CASE_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md) qualification section when evidence exists, then `characters/DG_<SUBJECT>_MONOGRAPH.md` |
| Which observed feature supplements the prose? | Diagnostic illustration or text form, locator and effect, with provenance | Unit reading; route into a specialist argument only if a pattern recurs |

Carry forward how the narrator and the people in a scene learn, not the solved case as universal knowledge. Classify contradictions as revised hypothesis, unreliable report, retrospective framing, ambiguity in language, materially different witness, or true unresolved tension. Update the ledger with `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT` or `OPEN`; retain the original forecast and separately determine outcome and validity of its earlier reasoning. Audit discrepancies between official cover metadata and the supplied edition in [source register](DG_SOURCE_REGISTER.md) before interpretive use. A translation, online serialization or manga supplies no missing clue.

## Standing source-reading topology

The proposed configuration requires an accountable principal analyst who inspects the whole admitted unit and is also the analytical integrator; separate independent specialist contexts for Japanese clue provenance, test design and focalization, and for ordinary club life, directed relationships and character judgment under uncertainty; and a distinct independent critical reviewer. The specialist contexts form their own observations from assigned original-language evidence before receiving the principal's interpretations. The critical reviewer directly inspects decisive and contradictory source evidence, checking evidentiary warrant independent of hindsight, cross-case identity, who knew and could test what, whether a claimed relational change follows, and whether the integrated account retained failed tests, ordinary behavior and counterexamples, before acceptance. The reviewer receives the interpretation after inspecting decisive evidence where practical. No present unit type qualifies for the reduced configuration; record any later authorized change under [topology §4.1](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EXECUTION_TOPOLOGY_AND_CAPABILITY_ROUTING_POLICY.md#multi-agent-source-reading). Verify these roles, evidence access and separation before first start; a runtime without adequate independent contexts is a required-capability stop. An owner-authorized deviation must state its exact scope and is recorded as a deviation, without ordinary acceptance or high-water advance unless explicitly authorized.

The unit checkpoint is the contribution and reconciliation record. Keep participant identity/role and separation, assignment, actual exposure and inspected coverage, material locator-backed observations, rivals and corrections, unresolved evidence obligations, and explicit adoption, qualification, rejection or open disposition for each material finding. Keep the finding and its reasoning recoverable; no generic participation summary, duplicated canonical artifact, or permanent per-agent ledger. Freeze prospective captures before resolution, separately by context where needed. Only the integrator updates shared readings, cumulative state and entrypoint after reconciliation. Record observed execution facts without inferring model settings. Keep source material and private transcripts in the evidence plane.

## Convergence and acceptance

After the first completed case-bearing volume, audit that relevant clue states, opportunity and distinct forms of interpersonal evidence are searchable. After the authorized tranche closes, stabilize the case/disclosure crosswalk and material claim revisions. A comparative study of inference and disclosure becomes required if repeated case constructions change the series interpretation. A relationship or club specialist study earns a separate home only where the sequence across cases exposes a directional difference with independent support. Those arguments inform each other through locators and current claims before full integration; synthesize only the admitted prose. The form of a solution, the social action that follows it and growth evidenced across opportunities are three separate conclusions.

The principal closes each source transaction only with full assigned primary coverage, source-grounded independent contribution, independent critical examination, reconciled material disagreements, preserved evidence and counterexamples, consistent cumulative history and truthful exposure and authority boundaries. This is the sequential completion gate; the admitted, committed run boundary must also reconcile with its declared stop. Confirm longitudinal state and still-open evidence before a role-gap and specialist readiness review; require the qualified character work and the material specialist roles before authorizing a full synthesis. Review fidelity, locator retrieval, counterreadings, referential clarity and handoff acceptance separately from the decision to freeze or release. Any separately commissioned reconstruction model needs evidence-bounded validation of its own; a monograph or qualified portrait supplies no capability grade.

Use [initiation §5.5.1](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md#self-contained-character-monograph-standard) for qualification and complete standalone portraits. Assess every person with differentiated evidence, not merely leads; record stable identity, boundary, outcome, rationale, largest gaps, reassessment trigger and a path once a monograph actually exists. A qualified person creates a production obligation. An untested dimension remains insufficient evidence; an apparent absence needs an opportunity test. Include supported ordinary preferences, speech and address, decision patterns, relationships, changes, exceptions and limits. Document separate self-containment and source fidelity results before acceptance; no current portrait silently replaces a frozen earlier one.

The local integrator owns witness binding, sequential reconciliation and the shared ledger. A fresh evidence-bounded synthesis context may take frozen case/relationship records and full argument locators; substantive interpretation returns for local evidence checks. Only the authorized repository operator handles publication; no continuous audiovisual completion is planned for the prose.

Under the [reasoning policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_REASONING_AND_MODEL_ROUTING_POLICY.md), use `ROUTINE_FAST` for deterministic bookkeeping, `BOUNDED_STANDARD` for metadata and locator checks, `SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS` for unit interpretation, `DEEP_SYNTHESIS` for sustained specialist/character work and `PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST` for foundation, high-impact disputes and final integration review. A scoped transfer under the [analytical handoff contract](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md) includes the frozen admitted/exposure horizon, retrievable evidence and argument records, open obligations, and named output scope and receiver; reconcile against live state, retest material claims and names/references (§15) before any promotion.

Keep the entrypoint and cumulative views mutable; dated claim changes preserve the older version, counters and new evidence. Complete prospective captures, accepted historical checkpoints and any authorized release remain intact; later repairs use linked errata, claim revision or an attributable successor. Revisit INITIAL design at the first meaningful source boundary, at the planned title-specific checks above and before integrated synthesis. Add cumulative structure only when independent recurrent evidence or a retrieval gap warrants it; revise the architecture and entrypoint together and backfill only a material gap existing records cannot responsibly repair.
