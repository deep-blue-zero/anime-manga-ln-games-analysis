# Goran: concealed instrumental purpose and a forced confession

Status: scoped Tier 2 monograph for ゴラン, who appears as ウォーリー in the Advanced TRPG edited replay. The [cohort 31 index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_TRPG_REPLAY_COHORT31_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) governs source and identity claims. No generative model is accepted from this single tightly engineered hostile project.

## Identity and evidential position

The wanted-person disclosure joins the client Wally to Goran, titled 幻惑, through a newspaper and Gilbert's successful perception check (ADV S076-077). The rank is 魔王軍千本槍, Thousand Spears, not Ten Spears. The GM explicitly treats the hierarchy as an improvised replay device; it does not expand the main novels' executive roster. His claim of descent from Zamzar is false in the revealed account. A disguised introduction, a later disclosure and an assumed family connection are not separate established people.

## Chronology

The client obtains the party's work gathering jewels and thereby gains access to Zamzar's basement. Investigation finds that Zamzar had no child and no grandchild; the will and dragon remains are discovered before the final confrontation (S074-075, S083-089). Wally then blurts the destructive purpose, the GM tries to recast it as imagined, and the party presses the threat. Goran denies kinship, dismisses the will's attachment and animates dragon bones/residual thoughts (S090-092). Combat ends with jewel self-destruct, his disappearance as charcoal/dust and a defeated plot (S104-106). Axel's destruction never occurs.

## Motives

Goran treats the dragon as a powerful instrument and wants the lively town blown away, even presenting the aftermath in terms of cleanliness or relief (S091). The dismissive response to the will contrasts with Zamzar's reported valued companionship. The contrast is grounded in his speech and actions; it need not imply he has no attachments in any possible context. The scene offers little neutral evidence about his own work or life outside the plot.

## Affect

He can perform a benign client identity and later speak contemptuously of love and the dragon's beauty. When 21 values human warmth over town destruction he reacts with disbelief and continues the attack (S092). As the jewels become unstable he is alarmed; he cannot simply exit the final danger and fails the evasion (S104-105). These situational reactions do not identify clinical sadism, an inability to feel or a comprehensive emotional repertoire.

## Competence and limits

The evidence-erasing cheat creates a practical obstacle to public accusation and tracing the arrangement (S077). Disguise and the false family story help mobilize the party, while dragon animation makes the corpse a mindless combat enemy. This is not restoration of the original companion's will. Combat, poisoning, failed evasion and the final self-destruct also expose limits. The final neutral magic damage is not a magic attack, a distinction that changes the party's available defenses. The same rules do not give Goran universal escape or limitless deception.

## Decision structure and disclosure

The confession is a major methodological constraint. The GM says the scenario requires him to confess because the evidence-erasing ability would otherwise prevent discovery (S091). Gilbert invokes lie detection at S090, but no actual check is shown. Thus confession cannot validate an ordinary liar-detection mechanism or a durable habit of volunteering secrets. The party's earlier charitable possibility that an army member might have reasons precedes this threat; it is not knowing endorsement of it. His revealed instrumental goal, rather than a hypothesized generic evil essence, supports opposition.

## Relationships and coercion

Zamzar is a false claimed ancestor, the dragon a resource, and the adventurers tools in obtaining access and jewels.21's interference is rejected; the group refuses the destructive demand despite the real house benefit. This is not a freely negotiated common project once the concealed purpose is known. The party jokes about protecting Goran from the blast and then defeating him later, but never performs that option (S104). The unperformed jokes neither establish mercy shown nor add another combat outcome.

## Ordinary life and place

The borrowed client role and house reward give a transactional surface, but not a reliable domestic biography. The mansion belongs to the deceased researcher's history. Its later availability to the party does not prove Goran's legitimate ownership. Following the revealed plot the guild apologizes for the referral and pays compensation (S106); that institutional response does not establish a prior guild conspiracy. The source provides too little independent ordinary conduct for a resource or household model of Goran.

## Japanese voice

ウォーリー and ゴラン name successive presentations of the same revealed replay antagonist. The grand concealed scheme, impatient dismissal of affection and alarm at self-destruct are different speech acts. The wanted identity is established with a perception result and public printed clue, not the author's translation preference or a voice similarity. The native 千本槍 matters for rank precision. There is no full linguistic profile or audio evidence.

## Comic and serious framing

A secrecy mechanism collides with a scenario-required confession; the architect of destruction is caught in the explosion. These are strong comic reversals whose causal conditions are unusually authored. The attempted town destruction, corpse use and deception still give serious grounds for the party's resistance. A serious transfer must not preserve compulsory confession as if it were naturalistic psychology. Removing that convention could require a different detection mechanism and might change the whole sequence.

## Countercases and rival explanations

The disguise, cheat and contracted jobs show organization, not pure incompetence. The blurted threat and failed final escape show failure, not unlimited planning. GM plot necessity is a direct rival to a personality explanation for disclosure, while combat rules explain some inability to avoid harm. Dense appearances within the same project are correlated evidence. Neither quantity of dialogue nor the final defeat identifies how he would respond to ordinary embarrassment, fair employment or a stable relationship.

## Limits and disposition

Tier 2 is warranted by identity, project, motive statements, tactics, relationships, source-aware comic analysis and a detailed ending. Tier 3 is withheld because cooperation, neutral choices and the central concealment-to-confession mechanism are insufficiently differentiated from scenario requirements. The source narrates destruction/disappearance; an irreversible cosmological fate is not separately certified. No model, hypothetical first, capability grade or independent-observation count is supplied for Goran.
