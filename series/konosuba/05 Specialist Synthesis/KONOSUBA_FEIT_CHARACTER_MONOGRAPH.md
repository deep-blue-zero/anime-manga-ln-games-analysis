## Current interpretation and the reason to stop at a monograph

Feit's most defensible current interpretation joins particular attachment, concrete preferences, exceptional practiced capacity and uneven understanding. She is neither an adult strategic equal disguised as a child nor an inert pet resource. She can ask, agree, warn, redirect an outing, share, select an object, give finite material and execute familiar cooperation. Adults can care for her and still exploit her dependence or overlook the limits of a particular answer.

A separate model would presently risk restating broad caregiver attachment and appetite as if they were uniquely fitted mechanisms.
