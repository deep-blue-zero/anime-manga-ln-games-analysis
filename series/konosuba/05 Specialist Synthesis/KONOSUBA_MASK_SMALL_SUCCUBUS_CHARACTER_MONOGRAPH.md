# The unnamed small dream-service worker — imagined patron, revised danger and selective solidarity

## 1. Identity through an event rather than a borrowed name

This account uses existing census row132 for the small worker who visits Kazuma's mansion in MAIN02 and the second, younger-looking succubus in MASK. The bridge is specific: MASK U019P472 recalls work at a mansion, capture by the same priest's barrier and near-erasure. MAIN02 item/xhtml/p-007.xhtml P432–488 enacts that combination and confirms the visitor's escape. The [cohort index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_MASK_CLIENTS_COHORT32_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) and [native provenance](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_MASK_CLIENTS_COHORT32_NATIVE_PROVENANCE.json) record this qualified event-based identification.

No legal name is supplied. DUST/CONT's invented alias ロリーサ remains an unresolved personal-name join, and the frozen Lolisa model excludes MASK support. The MAIN02 counter woman is explicitly different from the smaller house visitor. The MASK leader is likewise another person. Similar employment and admiration alone cannot transfer their powers, biography, decisions or lines into this account.

## 2. Chronology and knowledge boundaries

MAIN02 supplies a service visit that fails at the barrier. The worker apologizes, proposes a cover protecting the customer and escapes through his intervention. MASK U017 later gives her own explanation of being a fan, fear in the face of Yunyun's attack, pressure over Vanir's supposed romance and subsequent participation in criticism of his cruel reveal. U019 adds ordinary accompaniment, help-seeking during the shop attack and revised choices when Aqua's identity becomes explicit.

There is a small knowledge tension: Aqua calls herself a goddess within MAIN02, whereas Vanir describes the worker as not yet recognizing the goddess in MASK. An uncredited self-claim and accepted identity can differ, but the source does not narrate that explanation. The tension remains visible; no amnesia, new past incident or invented conversation is added to resolve it.

## 3. Motives and competing values

Vanir is a particular admired figure, not an interchangeable authority. The worker owns a photo book, calls herself a fan and wants him to embody the confident image she admires (U017P221–255). Her appeal can move from offering herself to supporting the presumed pursuit of another woman. Later tears and renewed advocacy preserve competing feelings rather than a single simple preference.

Work also carries an explicit responsibility. At MAIN02P451 she apologizes for the failed entry, attributes fault to her own inadequacy and tries to protect the customer's anonymity. That is one high-information proposal under danger. It supports a narrow professional hypothesis while leaving business dependence, shame, placation and collective survival as rivals. Her own safety remains valuable: she accepts the opportunity to escape rather than insisting on dying for the proposed cover.

## 4. Affect, pressure and regulation

The worker is frightened without being uniformly passive. Yunyun's attempted attack makes her recoil, yet she does not leave; Vanir then offers a signature. At the guild she is distressed by the supposed romance but actively advocates for him. Once the planned humiliation is revealed, both succubus fans join the condemnation (U017P230–267,432–509). The criticism is joint evidence, not an invented exact individual rebuke.

U019 makes the sequence of fear more explicit. She is initially ashamed of relying on Vanir, accepts his pride-framed challenge and declares readiness against human attackers. Recognition of Aqua changes the appraisal and brings a plea for help. Soon afterward she tries to protect Vanir. These transitions do not establish cured fear, stable bravery or a single threshold measured across encounters.

## 5. Capability, work and actual dependence

The worker describes covert bedside access as a professional strength in MAIN02, but the current attempt has failed. The advertised range and operational rules at the shop belong to the counter woman. Kazuma's mistaken dream encounter and Darkness's manipulation theory do not show that this worker has performed a dream, charmed him or caused his conduct.

MASK gives useful ordinary acts: collecting scattered apples, noticing a threatened vendor, accompanying Vanir and fetching help for the shop. It does not uniquely credit her with the group's defeat of the racketeers. She is absent from that defense while obtaining help. Her offer to buy time against Aqua is an initiative, not a performed victory. Lolisa's Sleep, Paralyze, infiltration and other source-local skills remain excluded.

## 6. Decisions and revised appraisal

The apple-stall exchange shows that concern can be redirected by a valued speaker. She raises the woman's difficulty, follows Vanir's reframing and then warns the recruiter about the dangerous visitor he has agreed to face (U019P67–116). This is neither independently established civic indifference nor proof of sound judgment in every social scene.

During the night she initially chooses self-reliance under Vanir's explicit demonic-pride explanation. That framing is supplied by him, so the scene cannot isolate an entirely endogenous pride mechanism. Learning that the apparent priest is Aqua changes her response. The model must represent received information and perceived capacity, not make the two answers inconsistent by deleting the intervening revelation.

## 7. Particular relationships and uneven solidarity

Toward the customer, she uses quiet, apologetic speech and proposes to bear the public blame. Toward Vanir, she seeks approval, values proximity and still asks for help. Toward Luna, devotion can become harmful pressure: the fans encourage acceptance of a relationship they have misunderstood, before joining criticism of the reveal. Concern for one person does not make her a general guardian of every person's boundaries.

With coworkers she shares work and danger; the later apology scene places her alongside the leader. Some alternating lines about secrecy, encouragement and refusal of Aqua's offer lack individual tags. Their joint participation can be retained without manufacturing a uniquely owned sentence. Shared rejection of an outsider's offer under Vanir's hierarchy does not authorize all future commands.

## 8. Ordinary life and desired objects

The photo book is a directly handled valued object, and Vanir's offer to sign it changes her expression. Accompanying his search for business opportunities, singing while following him and discussing the shop's regulars provide ordinary social evidence. The hierarchy is lived through attention, access and small actions as well as crisis.

Her wage, savings, ownership share, home routine, food preferences and long-term independent career remain unprovided. The leader's gift, initial revenue proposal and later group arrangement cannot become her personal budget. Looking young does not establish a human-child age: Vanir privately narrates that this worker is older than Wiz in U019P434. No exact age or species-wide developmental rule follows.

## 9. Japanese voice and pragmatic changes

Her speech repeatedly uses 私, customer address and バニル様, with a polite surface that can carry apology, warning, self-assertion and pressure. Emotional repetition marks some frightened or emphatic exchanges, but she also offers relatively sustained explanations. A simulation that stammers in every sentence would lose that range.

The MAIN02 cover proposal is addressed quietly to the customer, limiting who receives its meaning. The MASK fan declaration is public and emphatic, seeking recognition of a shared ideal. The short plea after the goddess disclosure has a different communicative purpose. These contrasts constrain new speech more usefully than copied catchphrases. Written Japanese supports the analysis; audio, accent and human native-speaker adequacy have not been tested.

## 10. Comedy and serious costs

The worker's idealized account of Vanir helps move a mistaken romance interpretation toward a cruel public outcome. The later condemnation exposes the gap between the admired image, the information available to the fans and his actual appetite. The source's full-moon explanation is a state account that overlaps with constituent pressure; it does not isolate their causal weights or supply an automatic excuse.

The threat from Aqua is serious within the source world. The worker's claim that a recently gained remaining life would make one loss tolerable is followed by Vanir's private narration that full erasure is possible (U019P528–529). No warning received by her is shown; her offer cannot establish informed acceptance of that full risk. No life loss is ultimately reported. Survival preserves neither a harmless-threat interpretation nor a completed self-sacrifice. Transfer requires the new setting to provide its own capacities and consequences.

## 11. Contrary cases and alternative explanations

Her devotion does not make all answers obedient: the fans criticize Vanir after the reveal. Her fear does not prevent every initiative, and her initiative does not remove help-seeking. The privacy proposal does not establish universal truthfulness because it includes a false stray-monster explanation. Her concern for the customer does not imply consistent protection of Luna's freedom from pressure.

Generic occupation, admired-person attachment, collective business interest, immediate fear and comic escalation explain much of the same evidence. The professional-risk proposal has especially sparse replication. The qualified MAIN02/MASK identity bridge is itself open to later correction; if disproved, the source scopes would need an attributable split rather than alteration of the frozen history. None of these limits is repaired by an internally consistent hypothetical response.

## 12. A narrow reconstruction candidate

The differentiated source contexts warrant this monograph and an asymmetric provisional reconstruction. Three hypotheses concern idealized-patron pressure with a possible criticism branch, revised threat appraisal with help and reciprocal initiative, and professional cover after self-ascribed failure. The last is particularly tentative. Each must retain concrete contrary evidence, live rivals, application limits and a possible disconfirming observation.

The model is stronger for these social and work situations than for solitary life, unfamiliar institutions, broad moral reasoning or physical combat. Its [separate reconstruction](../06%20Character%20Modeling%20and%20Reconstruction/KONOSUBA_MASK_SMALL_SUCCUBUS_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md) does not inherit Lolisa's biography or certify a final model. Known-source internal examinations may expose overreach; the same informed author's answers cannot provide independent accuracy, a capability grade or a confidence gain. Full census, mature final batteries and the wider program remain open.
