## 1. Start with the communicative act

A useful interpretive sequence is: independently evidenced situation and state; relationship and audience; current purpose; then wording, stance and form. The sequence prevents a circular explanation in which solemn language proves a serious role and that supposed role then explains the solemn language. A performed action, received request or externally imposed task can establish a role before a later utterance is assessed. Semantic content can also establish a commitment, but the same surface wording cannot independently confirm its own predicted register effect.

The series supplies many reasons to distinguish literal proposition from communicative act. A polite negative question can pressure rather than defer. A self-title can tell the truth and still demand an acknowledgment the listener refuses. A deliberately incomplete answer can leave its literal words intact while inducing a false inference. An indirect complaint can request a change without naming an imperative. The relevant alternative is therefore not “literal meaning or real meaning.” Both can operate at once, with different evidence for what the speaker intends and what a listener infers.

Kazuma’s trial gives a clear early case. He asks Sena to put the allegation straightforwardly, denies the particular teleport target, then turns the inquiry into personal counterattack. 「ストレートに聞いてくれませんか」 at MAIN03 C1 P234 is a request to narrow the relevant question. The later polite challenge 「検察官失格じゃあないんですかね」 at P242 exerts pressure on the questioner. Its form is not a reliable indicator of respect. When Sena recovers a firm stance, his P256 apology is brief. The oscillation depends on perceived leverage, factual scope and the immediate interlocutor, not a newly acquired or lost capacity for politeness. (C1 P219–265.)

That distinction matters across the series. The V10 coin wager’s “where” versus “which [hand]” changes an agreement through ordinary semantic scope and a misleading display. The V09 additive も lets Megumin challenge performed nonrecognition. These are important small forms, but their importance alone does not make them Japanese-only mechanisms. A faithful ordinary equivalent can preserve the contrast between an asserted proposition and the inference it invites.

## 2. Kazuma’s repertoire: explanation inward, pressure outward

Kazuma’s dense first-person narration often supplies qualifications, game analogies and self-exonerating sequences before or after a clipped external answer. This produces a distinctive distribution of information. The reader sees a calculation the addressee does not hear, while the addressee receives an order, denial, question or insult. The difference need not mean every calculation is false. It can instead reveal selective attention, a genuine competing motive, a missing fact or a revision after the event.

His ordinary connective repertoire includes factual inquiry, conventional requests and thanks, task sequencing, capability questions and acknowledgment of limits. He does not need to insult someone in every turn. Strategic politeness with officials and nobles demonstrates available competence; bluntness among familiar companions reflects a relationship and purpose, not an inability to organize a formal sentence. Game-like language can structure an accurate local hypothesis, as the theft exploit does, or make an attempted violation disappear from his preferred moral account through “no count” reasoning. The content and consequence determine which use is present.

The contrast becomes unusually sharp in the middle-series property dispute. Aqua explicitly cries at MAIN11 C2 P431 and P444. Kazuma approaches at P445 and asks whether he may enter the mansion in P446’s polite permission form, addressing her as water goddess. The police’s independently established ownership procedure gives him leverage. In that context the question is a taunt, not respectful reassurance. Later relenting at P450 does not change its earlier function, and the actual tears preserve the retired Gen03 K04 failure. The source supports legitimate property recovery and chosen humiliation together; one does not cancel the other. (C2 P404–451; the author’s own fresh preparation rechecked P425–451, with the longer window received as root verification.)

Care also remains compatible with roughness. Kazuma’s questions, offers, protective commands and reluctant concessions do not require a global switch to gentle diction. MAIN17 F P35 independently states that the king cannot be allowed to return. The later 「まあ待てよ」 at P45 can literally delay that return while its continuation taunts and directs attention to his status card. Protective task function and discourse management can coexist. A criterion requiring allocation to a companion or a lexical statement of the protective goal would be an added floor. The current fixed-window interpretation remains mixed/compatible rather than a second full urgent-care opposite. (F P18–48.)

His relational voice is equally particular. He can deny a grand attachment label while taking costly action, disclose an unattractive part of himself to comfort Megumin, negotiate an intermediate relation or become embarrassingly defensive before a direct appeal. These are not interchangeable forms of sincerity. They depend on which person is present, what has been asked and what he is prepared to acknowledge. His final harem declaration remains actual speech with social exposure, despite the absence of an implemented household; an analyst cannot turn an inconvenient utterance into a non-event by asking for a later achievement the forecast never required. (MAIN07 C5 P458–494; MAIN09 F P752–815; MAIN12 C1 P335–376; MAIN17 Ep P33–44.)

## 4. Megumin’s repertoire: polite structure does not resolve competition or vulnerability

Megumin’s grand self-introduction and Explosion performance are socially and personally meaningful, but they are not her only syntax. Her ordinary negotiation can retain 私 and polite endings. She can explain a technical limit, minimize a gift, ask a concrete question, request an answer, protect another person’s dignity or sharpen a complaint with blunt address. A voice grammar that emits theatrical naming every time she is interested would erase the contrast that makes the naming consequential.

Her first specialization explanation uses ordinary polite structure for an uncompromising preference. 「爆裂魔法だけが好きなのです」 and 「私は爆裂魔法しか愛せない」 make an affirmative identity claim after acknowledging the convenience of other spells. The claim is neither an incapacity report alone nor proof that every later loss of versatility is harmless. The firmness lies in what she values, not in an obligatory plain ending. (MAIN01 C1 P461–482.)

V16 gives a useful chronological contrast. Megumin asks an ordinary question at C3 P312. Kazuma’s new-magic claim prompts a stammer; his advanced-magic plan precedes her grabbing him at P319 and specialist complaint at P320. The later 「カズマがそれを言うんですか」 at P322 points to his earlier interference with her build. P321 is a spoken stop request from Kazuma, not proof of physical restraint or her compliance. The contextual reply at P322 cannot be moved before the initial grabbing to make all action a response to that later argument. (C3 P312–324.)

Personal vulnerability has a different organization. A sincere request can be direct, forceful and polite at the same time. Her V09 hesitant 「……ダメですか？」 seeks a particular companion’s help after other inducements fail; her V10 direct admission of jealousy removes the expected teasing defense; her V12 requests negotiate a gradually public relation rather than demand an immediate completed romance. Existing politeness does not prevent directness, and newly plain words do not independently establish sincerity. (MAIN09 C2 P314–372, P478–486; MAIN10 C1 P175–196; MAIN12 C1 P268–285, P335–376.)

The final gift scene combines gratitude and rivalry rather than replacing one with the other. At MAIN17 F P734 she tells Kazuma to use the object, then hands over manatite at P735. Megumin’s shared routines also carry different care directions. Giving Kazuma an お守り in V09 C1 P61, minimizing it at P81 and watching its reception differ from entrusting Darkness with 「カズマのお守り」 at P116. ## 6. Familiarity is a relationship, not permission

The dyads change the available act. Kazuma and Aqua share repeated grievance, practical dependency, status attacks and ordinary company; he may address an actual goddess sarcastically, while her familiar calling can be a real appeal. Kazuma and Megumin share technical appraisal, carrying, identity investment and negotiated intimacy; a familiar specialist script can become support when a new burden makes choice difficult. Kazuma and Darkness combine protection, coercive jokes, real objections, bodily attraction and unresolved belonging or reciprocity. No single “party register” erases these differences.

The other three central pairings matter as well. Aqua and Megumin can alternate between practical cooperation, rival claims and corrector/target roles; Aqua’s criticism of their ritual is not automatically literal devaluation of Explosion itself. Aqua and Darkness can use a genuine work hierarchy at the wall, challenge a false attribution to household maids, or negotiate an unwanted public event. Megumin and Darkness can recognize shared care while competing over Kazuma, exchange protective gifts and direct an indirect accusation at each other. Same group, different addressee and local purpose produce different utterance expectations.

Nor can the relationship be inferred from an unchanging honorific alone. Aqua’s カズマ様 spans coerced supplication, chosen maid play and a later adjustment once memory returns. Chris/Eris address contrasts are identity clues within a supported deduction, while Iris’s お兄様/お兄ちゃん alternation accompanies actual changing intimacy and explicit requests. These forms are evidence of presented relation and stance, not automatic proof of legal kinship, adult romance, total subordination or settled exclusivity. (MAIN11 C2 P443–534; MAIN07 Japanese-register evidence; MAIN10 Ep P34–60.)

Familiarity can also make a joke effective without making its conduct acceptable. Repeated sexualized framing does not invalidate a refusal. V12’s sequence must retain Darkness’s imposed contact, Kazuma’s stop requests, his free-hand discovery, his instruction to restrain it and later coaching. ## 7. The written surface includes narration, quotation and ruby

The observable source is a page of written Japanese. Punctuation, repetition, represented stammering, short answers, scripts, ruby and explicit description of delivery are available evidence. An uninspected pitch contour, actor’s pause, accent or audience laugh is not. When the narration calls a voice gentle, the evidence is the narrator’s description in context; it is not an acoustic measurement.

The distinction between base and ruby is consequential. The frozen contract numbers every body paragraph, including blank or image-only slots, separately within its exact member. Base extraction removes rt/rp contents while retaining base characters and element tails. A material reading is then recovered from native ruby, not invented from the base projection. A chapter with continuations is not renumbered as one concatenated stream. These rules keep a short wordplay claim independently retrievable and prevent the loss of an aside, quoted voice or reading.

V09 お守り supplies an economical example. The gift at C1 P61 is a protective charm. P116 writes 守 with ruby **も**, selecting おもり, looking after or babysitting Kazuma. The later request reverses the care direction and elicits his objection. Its written relation to the earlier gift matters. In V10 C3 P104 a babysitting usage does not recreate the two-sense sequence by itself; V11’s genuine charm should not inherit the joke merely because the spelling recurs. The mechanism requires the actual scene, not a search hit.

V16’s farewell exposes a different constraint. Vanir says 「心置きなく逝くがいい」 at C3 P162, with native 逝 ruby い. Kazuma’s P163 correction explicitly sets 行く against 逝く. The same reading and different script are part of the written effect. Treating it as a measured spoken distinction would manufacture an acoustic contrast the shared reading does not supply. The broader sendoff also includes malicious pet labels and hospitality in hell; those independently portable components are why the entire fixed premise remains unresolved rather than being reduced to the wordplay. (C3 P157–173; U16-083.)

Source forms can also be irregular. In MAIN02 C2 P325, Kazuma’s narration about Wiz supplies ruby リツチー with the source’s full-size ツ. It is not Aqua’s quoted utterance and should not be silently normalized into a new inspected reading. This is a transcription and attribution caution, not a claim that the spelling itself is a new central joke. Similarly, no ruby should be invented for a visually suggestive title such as ネトゲハイ神 where the admitted source supplies none.

Unattributed speech needs the same restraint. MAIN17 C3 P427 is an untagged protest. Aqua’s position nearby and Yunyun’s later explicit persuasion at P438 leave more than one possible attribution. P437’s depicted loaded staff cannot newly establish a cue before P427 without a labeled continuity inference. The companion objection remains available without certifying every cry’s speaker. Original fixed windows stay fixed; factual qualifications are external, not a license to move them to a better baseline.

## 8. Three kinds of dependence that should not be collapsed

**Causal or structural dependence** concerns the events and expectations carrying the joke. The golem head’s weight, an unexpectedly expensive victory or a truthful identity nobody believes can survive ordinary changes of wording. They still need accurate causal and social context.

**Phrasing and pragmatic dependence** concerns timing, scope, social register, understatement, address, implicature or an indirect act. An ordinary equivalent may preserve the mechanism, but a flat literal paraphrase can lose much of the comedy or characterization. L1 and L2 are not negligible losses. 「逃げませんよね、魔王様」 is a negative question and status address used as a taunt; its text is not merely a proposition about future movement. A shortened answer after long internal reasoning similarly supplies an asymmetry a plot summary removes. (MAIN17 F P33.)

**Japanese-form dependence** concerns a particular reading, script, lexical ambiguity or construction requiring reconstruction or annotation. The matching of お守り readings is one case. 頭が足りない in V16 C2 P356 initially suggests limited intelligence; Wiz’s P358 question about arithmetic makes that uptake explicit. Vanir’s P359 answer specifies a missing rear head, and Wiz’s P360 reply names the physical reinterpretation. Both original assignments retain U16-054 as L3. The claim is that preserving the actual double use requires a lexical solution, not that no translator can invent a successful replacement. A new clever target-language joke would be adaptation, not proof of lossless ordinary equivalence.

Culture crosses these distinctions. RPG classes, skill points, the villain’s final speech, noble audience expectations, confession routines, tourist scripts and Crimson Demon declarations provide learned expectations. Some are fictional institutions; some draw on broader recognizable genres. Their presence does not make all Japanese readers alike or license a claim about what Japanese people generally believe. Crimson Demon performance is an in-world social genre that Megumin inhabits enthusiastically and Yunyun may resist. Serious communal duty can make Yunyun perform more theatrically. That is stronger evidence than a universal national-cultural explanation of “Japanese theatrical speech.”

Charged gender or sexual frames require similar care. A character’s objectification, an invasive inference or an offensive term may be necessary to describe the interaction. Reporting the mechanism does not certify the narrator’s evaluation or turn the scene into a cultural generalization. Humor and moral appraisal can differ without one erasing the source event.

## 10. Nine mappings remain nine questions

The rejected service tendency must not be replaced with either blanket confidence or blanket skepticism about role-conditioned voice. The accepted nine-branch assessment preserves distinct uncertainties. Aqua’s divine-status contest concerns self-legitimation and acknowledgment, not service success or mere denigration of a rival. Megumin’s specialist branch needs the actual specified challenge, rather than any inconvenience to the party; her relational branch is not restricted to Kazuma, arousal or a spoken recipient name. Darkness’s dignity branch does not inherit all of D02’s behavioral antecedents; her belonging branch still needs independent membership/equal-standing stakes and adequate speech. Kazuma’s quiet-care functions differ from urgent protective commands and from internal concern alone.

A role can intensify while already active. Different recipients or unequal windows may reduce causal identification, but neither creates a universal exemption. Missing speech remains missing even after helpful action; two short turns are not automatically adequate or inadequate by a new word-count rule. If a cue is the speaker’s adoption statement, that statement’s formal feature cannot also supply the positive outcome. Later role-conditioned turns remain available where the fixed window contains them. The point is to preserve a testable relationship between independently evidenced role and specified output.

The J01 service failure exposes a limit in that relationship. It suggests future comparisons separating execution, management of aftermath, social complaint and embodied boundary negotiation. Those refinements are OPEN for later models. They do not relabel the old service cases or make their two-opposite criterion newly universal. Other supported functions remain conditional evidence, and narrow untested branches remain untested. The companion [humor synthesis](KONOSUBA_HUMOR_SYSTEM_SYNTHESIS.md) develops why practical success can coexist with conversational divergence.

## 11. A generative voice needs ordinary continuation

A useful later model should be able to produce a neutral bridge as well as a recognizable high point. For Kazuma, that means a relevant question or plan without obligatory contempt, with internal qualification where his self-image is at stake. For Aqua, it means technically fitted explanation and ordinary acceptance without an obligatory goddess boast, while retaining her capacity for pride, alarm and abrupt demands. For Megumin, it means coherent everyday politeness, technical preference and precise personal requests rather than permanent incantation. For Darkness, it means actual duty, capability limits, direct objections and belonging-sensitive speech without eroticizing every line.

Recognition should then depend on compatible purpose, relationship and available information. A theatrical declaration can serve a serious rescue; a polite question can injure; a crude complaint can preserve a practical boundary. These are compositional constraints, not a new accepted reconstruction model. They must be tested later in both canonical and realistic modes without copying a famous utterance as a substitute for the mechanism.

Transfer into another setting must distinguish what can change from what would falsify the source model. A realistic version cannot retain the same casual expectation of resurrection, magical bodily safety or exceptional mana access. It can retain particular attachment, a valued craft, temptation by acknowledgment or fear of unwanted exposure. Surface register must fit the new institution and audience rather than preserve a noble title or spell formula solely for recognition. Ethical boundaries and speaker agency remain constraints in both modes; humor is not permission to erase them.
