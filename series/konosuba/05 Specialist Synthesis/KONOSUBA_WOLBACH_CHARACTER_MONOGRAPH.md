# Wolbach: considerate acquaintance, divided power and committed opponent

## 1. Identity and narrative position

Wolbach (ウォルバク) is a speaking, choosing subject whose help, pleasures, uncertainty and warfare need one account. Her relationships to the small creature called Chomusuke and to the earlier hooded rescuer are important, but neither relation licenses attributing every being's thought or act to her. This is a provisional substantial monograph with a separate conditional reconstruction model, not a claim that her allegiance motive or complete divine mechanism is solved.

The MAIN04 bath encounter has an explicit reader-facing name cue: Hans calls the woman Wolbach at C3 continuation, item/xhtml/p-010.xhtml P53. The earlier census description “unnamed bath woman” therefore loses available information. Kazuma later remembers that naming only uncertainly (MAIN09 C4 P242), before she formally introduces herself at P245. His recollection and the reader's access have different strengths. MAIN09 C3 P371 explicitly recalls the Arcanretia bath encounter. Short red hair and yellow eyes support continuity, but those traits alone are insufficient: the separately named devil Arnes also has red hair and yellow eyes (EXP02 U023 P13–16).

The EXP03 carriage woman is unnamed in Yunyun's earlier viewpoint. Her appearance, treatment destination and invitation are available in IMG032–039; Yunyun realizes she forgot to ask the name. In MAIN09 F P179–182, named Wolbach recognizes Yunyun and recalls that particular carriage invitation, which Yunyun confirms. This supplies the substantive reader backjoin. It does not backdate named recognition into the earlier girl or certify every insider-sounding implication of the carriage conversation.

The earlier hooded rescuer actually uses Explosion, protects Megumin and calls the black beast her half (EXP01 U014/U016). The later Wolbach reports teaching a Crimson girl and fighting her half (MAIN04 C3 P103–106); MAIN09 joins the remembered naming exchange and instruction to the encounter. Megumin's inference of remembered recognition remains her inference. Wolbach's denials are not rewritten as a confession of knowing deception. Exact age, biological history and the complete relation among divided divinity, body, memory and power remain unresolved.

## 2. Evidence chronology and unequal access

In the early rescue, the hooded woman blasts the beast chasing the child, checks for injury, initially assumes an adult opened the seal, then revises that assumption after seeing the child handle its puzzle. She drains the weakened beast, which visibly shrinks and disappears, and says it has only been resealed. Megumin's childhood opening, that resealing and the later Komekko opening are different stages. EXP01 U043 P570–572 distinguishes Megumin's earlier experience from her later confirmation of her sister's act; MAIN09's retelling is not a new independent unsealing observation.

The EXP03 carriage encounter gives Yunyun private access to food, conversation, an invitation and a real separation. The woman initially speaks of subordinates leaving this world, then explicitly qualifies the separation as temporary, not never meeting again (IMG035–036). Permanent deaths cannot be inferred from the first page alone. Her mutter about Arcanretia plan timing and surprise at the reported incident are informative but do not depict her completing the operation.

MAIN04 gives a different viewpoint. Hans reports sabotage; Wolbach says she came for treatment and asks not to be involved. She hears him nevertheless, warns Kazuma and leaves. MAIN09 then develops another bath conversation, misidentification of the sought creature, a formal enemy encounter, a sustained siege and its defeat, and a final fight. Damage, cast sounds, witness accounts and directly watched actions are distinguished in the evidence index. The analysis does not turn every report into another witnessed choice.

## 3. Motivations and valuations

Personal comfort is real evidence. She takes treatment after incomplete power recovery, enjoys hot springs and later describes bathing as a reward for daily work (MAIN04 C3 P103–116; MAIN09 C3 P395–398). These interests should not be reduced to cover stories merely because she also commands an enemy operation. The text does not certify that every treatment explanation is complete, but it gives a repeated ordinary preference and a finite recovery concern.

She also helps particular people: checking the rescued child, offering thanks for release, giving Yunyun food and offering company, and warning Kazuma about danger. The carriage gift is actually handed over and eaten, not merely proposed (EXP03 IMG033–035). Her invitation includes occasional adventuring and an appraisal of Yunyun's strong magic; reducing it to pure selfless nurture would omit her own companionship and practical interest. Her later pleasure that the invitation mattered coexists with surprise at how heavily Yunyun received it.

Those preferences do not dissolve other commitments. She says she dislikes fighting people she has talked with, yet confronts the raiders after repeated losses and her investigation of defeated generals (MAIN09 F P331–359). She refuses conditional nonaggression and release, invoking manufactured weapons and her need for Chomusuke's power (F P499–509). The account retains her concern and her chosen hostility together. The ultimate reason for joining the Demon King's army is explicitly withheld; neither hidden benevolence, revenge, political necessity nor a wish to die is established.

## 4. Personality and affect

Her common manner is conversational, gently amused and sometimes self-deprecating. It has limits. In the rescue she discovers that her broad wish offer exceeds what she can provide and corrects it after the child's impossible demands (EXP01 U016 P37–52). In the carriage she is kind but flustered by a lifelong-memory promise. In the bath she is embarrassed and afraid under staring, sexual comments and an actual death threat. These are responses to what Kazuma does, not evidence that her power makes all social harm irrelevant.

She can become angry, defensive and physically involved. Aqua's taunting about divinity and followers produces indignation, threats and mutual grabbing (MAIN09 C4 P256–298). Her threatened holiday-sloth punishment is speech, not a performed curse. The comic exchange does not prove every divine claim or erase the physical conflict. She also becomes embarrassed when Kazuma invokes bathing during a military confrontation (F P337–351). Amusement, shame, concern, pride, frustration and fear coexist; a single “gentle mentor” description is inadequate.

Her wounded approach and labored manner in the final encounter are observable descriptions. Kazuma's impression of a momentary smile at Megumin's gratitude is explicitly uncertain and something he hopes was real (F P564–565). It cannot bear an account of forgiveness, approval of being killed or a deliberate death plan.

## 5. Competence, means and practical limits

The early rescue gives actual destructive and sealing-related action. The later siege combines long-range Explosion, retreat by Teleport and nearby military protection. Mitsurugi reports the repeated tactic and its difficulty (MAIN09 C4 P136–143); Kazuma sees the damaged wall and hears attacks but does not watch every initial casting and departure. Later named Teleport casts are directly printed, including escape from Aqua's water and departure after meeting Yunyun.

This is effective, finite competence. Aqua's actual repair skill and the helpers' work make the wall stronger despite repeated attacks (MAIN09 F P54–99). Kazuma, Megumin and Yunyun imitate the attack-and-retreat strategy against the camp. Wolbach eventually seeks them after the raids; the earlier tactic does not guarantee success against altered conditions. Kazuma's assumption that one Explosion leaves no further usable power is his reasoning, not a universal schedule or a measured rule for every caster.

At the final encounter she shows a faded right hand and explains that depletion will lead to absorption into the other half (F P501–504). The visible hand and her mechanism account have different evidentiary status. Her statement that she would destroy Chomusuke is not an observed fusion or destruction. Power, access, recovery, survival, willingness and the consequences for the creature remain separate questions. Neither the small body's continued existence nor later bathing supplies a complete mechanism retrospectively.

## 6. Decision-making and information

She makes consequential classifications from incomplete information. During the bath conversation, Kazuma's selected description of himself and the cat's timidity leads her to reject the sought-creature match (MAIN09 C3 P410–425). His self-flattering report is not neutral data. Her misunderstanding is retained rather than repaired into secret perfect recognition. Her caretaker-affinity explanation for their mutual familiarity is a hypothesis offered jokingly, not demonstrated shared consciousness.

She first treats Aqua as an insolent human priest, dismisses the goddess claim, then updates when visible water preparation supplies contrary evidence (MAIN09 C4 P269–295). That local update does not make her an infallible detector. Later she combines Kazuma's four-general record with the tale of a hero called Satou; the Japanese narrator knows the surname is common and infers she is treating it as an ancestry cue (F P347–363). Her evidence is intelligible in her local setting while her conclusion remains underidentified.

Disclosure is also bounded. She can talk freely about treatment and a lost partner, insist on her own goddess designation, or decline to answer why she serves the army. Repeated recognition denials and deflection permit a description of guarded interaction. They do not license omniscient narration that she secretly remembers every detail, lies for a known noble purpose or deliberately arranges her own defeat.

## 7. Relationships and recipient agency

Megumin's gratitude and aspiration belong to Megumin; they do not compel reciprocal recognition, surrender or forgiveness from Wolbach. The early rescuer's instruction has a long effect, but the later fight retains incompatible interests. Yunyun's invitation memory matters intensely to her, while Wolbach says the original gesture was less weighty than that reception (EXP03 IMG038; MAIN09 F P181–182). Neither interpretation cancels the other.

Kazuma's liking for the bath woman is genuine to his viewpoint and incomplete as a safety assessment. He stares after recognizing a manners problem, initially dismisses her request not to watch, and later threatens her life before offering an explanation. Her later warmth does not make those earlier acts invited. Her eventual decision to fight likewise does not mean the earlier warnings were false or that she never cared.

Chomusuke is called her half, partner and another self, but the creature has separately observed resistance, approaches and vulnerabilities. Chomusuke tries to reach her and she reaches back (MAIN09 F P152–154). Kazuma restrains the creature and makes release conditional. Wolbach's later plan would destroy it by her own account. Relational closeness does not establish identical interests, mutual informed assent or ownership permission. Arnes's worshipful naming of the creature is another actor's commitment, not proof that the current small body shares Wolbach's full memories.

## 8. Ordinary life and finite care

Food, conversation, leisure, baths and company are part of the account, not decorative contrast to combat. The carriage woman gives a modest gift, listens, invites Yunyun and accepts the eventual separation. After learning the destination, Yunyun declines accompanying her to Arcanretia; the woman does not compel her to continue. That finite encounter is a better description than an adopted daughter, permanent party or unconditional offer of protection.

Treatment has practical cost and lost convenience. In MAIN04 she anticipates needing a new treatment place after the warning; in MAIN09 her desired partner has not been found and she partly resigns herself to that failure. Her ordinary preferences can coexist with harmful affiliations. The text does not establish an occupation history beyond the actual role descriptions, a domestic household, complete finances, a lover, or a general willingness to satisfy strangers' requests.

## 9. Japanese voice and viewpoint

Her speech commonly uses 私 and sometimes the situational self-address お姉さん. Gentle questions, hesitation, laughter and softened refusals occur beside direct assertions, angry address and formal self-naming. The carriage's modest clarification, the bath's request for privacy, the goddess-status dispute and the military ultimatum require different registers. A reconstruction should not flatten all of them into motherly reassurance or villainous grandeur.

The source-first distinction is especially important for modifiers. Kazuma's “seemed,” “perhaps” and desired interpretation of a smile do not become certain mental states in English paraphrase. Megumin's recognition argument is hers. The present recheck uses known BASE text and eight separately inspected EXP03 prose images with visible ruby; it is not a new full Japanese traversal or native-human language adequacy judgment. Hypothetical English exercises cannot certify her Japanese voice.

## 10. Comedy, seriousness and harm

Comedy exposes real mismatches: the child's and rescuer's different meanings, the overbroad wish offer, a goddess failing to identify another goddess, and the enemy's uncertainty about an ordinary surname. These do not automatically become nonliteral events. Magical power does not make embarrassment, restraint, damaged defenses, battlefield losses or a threatened creature harmless.

The war account also preserves others' adverse choices. Megumin continues Explosion raids through pleas and surrender gestures; Kazuma's speculation about punishment for desertion is not a watched order by Wolbach (MAIN09 F P288–317). Aqua's damaging water and later excellent repair are both real. Wolbach's response to repeated raids is her own, while the exact deaths, each soldier's belief and the complete command structure remain uncounted. Victory does not identify the full causal or ethical responsibility of every participant.

## 11. Contrary evidence, rivals and reconstruction limits

The strongest rival to a special benefaction rule is ordinary hospitality, gratitude and practical interest. The strongest rival to a distinctive combat rule is generic finite-resource tactics. Information errors can follow generic misleading reports and unfamiliar local names. These rivals are not defeated simply by citing additional appearances or writing a fluent response in her voice. The separate model must make conditional claims, retain actual mistakes and specify what would distinguish its account.

A pure gentle-mentor reading fails against continued siege, refused nonaggression and actual chanting against acquaintances. A pure enemy reading loses warnings, gifts, the rescue and discomfort at fighting conversational partners. An omniscient-divinity reading fails against the cat classification, Aqua recognition sequence and Satou inference. A universal consent-respecting account fails if it turns warmth into permission to use Chomusuke's power; a universal cruel-controller account likewise loses actual finite care and accepted separation.

Unresolved branches include full allegiance motive, what she remembers at each denial, limits of attack/recovery, what she knows of each allied operation, the exact status of separated subordinates, and complete post-disappearance continuity. These are substantive limits. No independent observation count, confidence percentage or capability grade is inferred from paragraph or image totals.

## 12. Current interpretation and next questions

The current interpretation is a considerate but committed and fallible actor. Particular help can be real while duties, threat appraisals and the need for power lead her to opposing choices. She has impressive means and finite recovery, can update a mistaken assessment, can remain confidently wrong, and can guard information without the analyst knowing her hidden reason. Chomusuke's distinct present agency stays visible within the reported half relation.

A separate provisional model is warranted by these conditional conflicts across rescue, ordinary company, recovery, classification and warfare. That is a current analytical disposition, not independent validation or a final ceiling. Known-source hypothetical cases may examine some branches but cannot establish blind predictive accuracy. Further work should distinguish acquaintance from obligation, changed resources from changed goals, trustworthy evidence from social cues, and refusal to disclose from failure to remember. Outcomes, inputs and model bytes must remain frozen once their respective stages begin.
