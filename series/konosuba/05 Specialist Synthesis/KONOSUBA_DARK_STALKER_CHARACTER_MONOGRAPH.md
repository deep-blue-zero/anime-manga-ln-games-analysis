# Dark Stalker: retirement, watchfulness and the life she wants to keep

## A class name attached to a particular woman

Dark Stalker is the female first-person narrator of YORI04 U014, a retired professional who makes Axel her home after Aqua restores her lost arm. This is an intentional class-only identification, not a discovered personal name. Her account describes civil service, espionage rather than military service, a pension and a healthy body still in her twenties17-22/33. Those are her statements about her position. Neither the class name nor her interest in Kazuma joins her to an unnamed TRPG professional, a succubus or the separate woman called Assassin.

The present source reading includes all558 paragraph slots of U014, the four other YORI04 fiction members, bounded MAIN17 context and DUST-CONT U01095-175, with ruby retained. It returns to known local Japanese editions. Current images, residuals, whole-edition completeness and independent observations receive no credit. Unprefixed locators below are U014; another member is stated explicitly. Her vivid private narration improves access to what she attends to and tells herself. It does not make her an impartial witness of other minds.

DUST-CONT U010120/162 reports an unnamed Dark Stalker spreading a private declaration at the guild. Dust does not know who that person is126/163;164 explains the class. Keith and Lolisa's reports, Dust's accidental confirmation and the crowd's actual response do not give a name, sex or restored-arm bridge. The thematic resemblance to this woman's interest in romance is insufficient for a positive individual join. Keep that route unjoined, without counting it as a second certified person or a confirmation of her behavior.

## Watching a romance she has not lived

Her early attention settles on Kazuma and Megumin choosing a play25-70. She has views about suitable romance but acknowledges having neither dating nor theater experience40-41. She interprets their exchange before either person gives her an explanation. The ticket seller's objection54, Megumin's account of their relationship56, actual handholding58 and proposed kiss60 are different kinds of evidence. No kiss is performed here, and the ticket seller's eventual couple label61 is not a permanent relationship contract.

This scene gives her a pleasure of her own. She enjoys following an unfolding relationship and tries to regulate her excitement so she can remain concealed63-68. She withdraws before the performance because she expects more than she can comfortably watch. That is a deliberate allocation of attention, not simply a failed mission. Her romantic reading can track visible embarrassment while overconfidently assigning its meaning. The embedded play summaries74-103 likewise reveal her taste and social assumptions; they do not establish actual royal law, inheritance rules or the lives of real people in Axel.

## A recovered ordinary life

The arm restoration174-175 matters because she understands it as the recovery of possibilities, not merely a combat upgrade. She can live in a town she likes, find ordinary pleasures and keep watching people whose presence helps make that life possible. Her gratitude to Aqua and desire for Axel to remain peaceful are connected to enjoyment rather than standing apart as pure duty. Her inference of Aqua's divinity182 and later account of her own Axis affiliation520 belong to this history. They do not make Assassin an Axis convert: the other woman declines521.

The source also preserves incomplete knowledge. The partner's lost leg and contact with Aqua178-179 come through this narrator's account. Her hope that the masked professional might be a man gives way when Assassin is introduced as a woman156-163. Whatever earlier familiarity they have, this is not evidence of a prior romance. Her very capacity to be surprised is part of the portrait: professional observation coexists with an imagined social world that outruns what she knows.

## Pension, appetite and uneven self-management

A meal consumes roughly a week's pension121, while the next payment is still ahead33. Later she asks for food320-328 and recognizes how her grateful drinking has contributed to a growing problem330-331. She can describe practical constraints without consistently protecting herself from them. The reported ability to find small coins329 is not an unlimited income source. Nor does a pension establish freedom from every expense or a universally secure retirement.

Her comparison with Assassin's restrained spending325-326 is useful adverse evidence. A generator that made both women identical disciplined professionals would lose a concrete difference. Dark Stalker's generosity, appetite, pride and desire to enjoy a recovered body can consume resources she also needs. A purely selfless interpretation cannot explain the pleasure; a purely indulgent one cannot explain unpaid volunteering or readiness to help. The text does not identify stable weights for these motives.

## Rivalry that leaves real injuries

The two professionals repeatedly turn each other's presence into a contest. In the first fight, calling the outcome a draw219 sits beside the account that Assassin leaves her behind221 and Aqua rescues her222. The arm and rib recollection332 returns to this episode; it is not another independent fight. Dark Stalker's drink gift245 also contributes to the later difficulty of finding Aqua available. Her retrospective narration acknowledges a cost she helped create.

The second challenge360 ends in her claim of complete victory364, but the consequences make victory an inadequate description. Clothing is taken, the partner is distressed and Dark Stalker has a broken rib366. The idea of arranging care through a drunken Aqua367 is her later explanation, not a documented plan frozen before the fight. Assassin then feeds her369. Competitive injury and practical care coexist without establishing either a wholly hostile relationship or a secretly confirmed romance.

## Skilled observation does not guarantee secrecy

She has real specialist capacities. She notices danger, uses concealment, reads social scenes and prepares to act. Yet her account of a successful secret presence must be compared with other focalization. YORI04 U0163-14 shows the main party already knows about the watcher. Her confidence in remaining unseen therefore cannot be adopted as an objective detection score. Kazuma's tolerance of some familiar watching is limited social accommodation, not permission for every intrusion.

The disguise sequence394-412 gives another limit. Her imitation of a neroid is camouflage, not evidence that another animal appeared. Assassin's finger flick408 resolves one immediate problem, while the second flick409 hurts her. The photo conversation410-411 concerns a proposed and accepted gift; no handover is shown before the sequence ends412. Technical capacity, social wish and comic vulnerability all remain visible. None can be used to erase the others.

## Readiness without a performed attack

During the emergency415-449, the professionals remain observers, anxious and ready. Weapons and poison are prepared423-427, but neither woman is shown carrying out the anticipated attack. Megumin's actual emergency Explosion445 is a separate action. This matters for a model of restraint: readiness is an action choice with costs and risks, while the unperformed intervention is not a success that can be credited to them.

The poison's origin and price432-435 are Assassin's reported information. Her correction of Dark Stalker's expensive-price guess is another instance in which the narrator's confident appraisal needs repair. The guild prohibition on teaching and transfer436-440 is explicit in this conversation. MAIN17 U011330-331 later credits the known Dark Stalker with teaching Eavesdrop, which Kazuma actually uses326. That is retrospective attribution of a contribution. It does not stage the original lesson, explain every condition of permission or establish a contradiction by a known calendar. The native later passage does not supply a masculine pronoun. The older census115 supplement's phrase “his own defensive account” cannot establish a male teacher; the accepted MAIN17 report itself already uses neutral wording.

## Volunteering does not volunteer the partner

Luna acknowledges retirement492. Dark Stalker nevertheless offers her own work without pay495 and recognizes the guild's budget problem496. This is evidence of a chosen contribution, not forced enlistment merely because she has a skill. It also differs from a completed night patrol: the story does not show the following shift being worked.

Assassin explicitly has not accepted529. Dark Stalker's enthusiasm cannot be substituted for that woman's answer. Their remembered mission532-533 supplies reciprocal rescue and injury, but does not entitle either to the other's future labor. The eventual account of a peaceful morning together549-550 receives an affirmative response553. That concrete shared prospect may help explain assent; it does not prove that every unpaid arrangement, every patrol task or every future day is accepted. Her hope is a social future she values, rather than a general authorization she possesses.

## The bottle encounter prevents an ideal portrait

YORI04 U018342-350 returns to her from another observer's position. Angie reports that a frightening woman confronted her over pressing an expensive bottle on a boy344. Kazuma sees the familiar watcher sharing the bottle with veterans346 and receiving cheers347. Angie calls the bottle a voluntary gift350; Kazuma interprets it as tribute349. The accounts do not certify explicit theft or a single private motive.

They do, however, make a spotless noninterference rule untenable. Dark Stalker can enforce a social boundary while benefiting from the resulting drink. She is capable of pressure, enjoyment and uneven judgment as well as grateful protection. An analysis should keep Angie's discomfort and the opposed accounts in view. Reading the scene only as admirable defense of Kazuma would erase the cost to another independently motivated person.

## Written voice and invested interpretation

The first-person register moves between a self-styled older-sister stance, quick inward commentary, repeated encouragement of the pair she watches and abrupt alarm when her own body or secrecy is at risk. The sequence35-68 is especially rich in a distinction between what she thinks Kazuma means and what Megumin says about payment. Her comic internal coaching is not dialogue that the pair hears. Under rivalry and need, the voice becomes more direct; it can also admit error, cost and dependence.

She reports other people's words and sometimes supplies their supposed motivation. Those layers must remain separate in reconstruction. An English response can attempt comparable pragmatic shifts between inward enthusiasm, self-defense and practical concern, but it cannot certify Japanese fluency or an acoustic performance. Repeating an older-sister label, a class name or a shipping reaction is not itself a generative model.

## A narrow candidate, with rivals retained

Two provisional mechanisms are worth testing after foundation review. First, a recovered ordinary life and gratitude to its protectors can support vigilant readiness and personally offered work, while pleasure, rivalry and poor calibration interrupt any ideal restraint. Second, she uses familiar romance narratives to interpret ambiguous interpersonal cues, while direct corrections and embarrassing consequences can reveal the gap between her appraisal and others' intentions. These mechanisms concern information, valuation and action; neither assumes she is always right or always helpful.

A generic retired spy, an enthusiastic romance observer, local indebtedness and the needs of comic focalization remain serious rivals. Her long-term behavior away from Aqua, Axel and Assassin is weakly identified. Exact danger thresholds, stable spending limits, private professional history and response to a clear refusal of watching remain open. Dense first-person evidence earns a dedicated study and restricted Tier3 assessment, not mature transfer performance. Any later informed same-root hypotheticals must retain failures without repairing the originals, adding missing consent or granting independent accuracy, grade or confidence credit.
