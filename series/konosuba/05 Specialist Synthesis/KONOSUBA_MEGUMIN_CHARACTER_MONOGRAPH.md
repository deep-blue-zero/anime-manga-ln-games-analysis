## 1. Choosing a limitation

Megumin's first important distinction is between what she can presently cast and what she wants a life of magic to be. Her exclusive build is not adequately explained by ignorance of convenient alternatives. In MAIN-01 C1P461–482 she moves from saying she can use only Explosion to saying she can love only Explosion. She concedes the utility of other spells while refusing to make utility the governing value. The serious cost—collapse after one cast and dependence on someone to recover her—is already part of the arrangement she seeks.

This choice organizes more than combat. It supplies a daily practice, a cultural persona, an aesthetic standard, a reason to seek particular targets, a vulnerability to mockery and a route into companionship. It also exposes her to exclusion. A party needing flexible magic can reasonably judge the build inconvenient without proving that its possessor lacks intelligence or skill. Megumin can understand that judgment and still find its social meaning intolerable.

Across the series, her power becomes more controlled and more effectively supplied. Her relation to the choice remains less settled than either total rigidity or simple maturation would suggest. She can seriously consider another role when she accepts that her build burdens valued companions. She can also impose ordinary costs to defend it, continue destructive reward-seeking after a task weakens, and welcome a cast that she knows may kill companions. The first cases prevent a caricature of unthinking obsession; the latter prevent turning flexibility and affection into dependable safety.

The monograph's central argument is that she wants to preserve both a chosen art and a place among particular people. Usually the party finds ways to accommodate both. When they conflict, the result depends on how she understands contribution, humiliation, target meaning, reward and possible loss. Those appraisals sometimes yield care, sometimes coercive framing, and sometimes conflict that has no harmless resolution. ## 2. A theatrical person with practical knowledge

Crimson Demon theatricality is a social and aesthetic language for Megumin, not an exhaustive account of her mind. She can enjoy an extravagant name, title or declaration, know that an audience finds it embarrassing, and deploy the same register to control an encounter. Yunyun's different relation to the same cultural practices is important: shared background does not generate identical preferences. Megumin's fluency includes knowing what will expose another person's discomfort.

The ordinary evidence is unusually useful here. She notices practical uses of Kazuma's beginner magic in MAIN-01 C2 and knows how Create Earth can matter to crops. Her gathering and cooking in V10 are efficient enough to teach Iris; she admits that a supposed secret family recipe was a phrase she wanted to use. The competence and the invented pedigree belong to the same performance. Knowing how to prepare food does not mean she respects ownership of Aqua's expensive liquor when using it in that preparation.

Poverty, food insecurity and care for Komekko help explain why materials and household access matter, but these are not a license to invent a whole childhood outside the principal text. Recruitment while hungry, acceptance of food and basic expenses, coupons, remittances and reasons for limiting money sent to her father are represented evidence. They support resource awareness and specific family responsibilities. They do not make every later expenditure prudent. She can preserve money for family and welcome destructive experience rewards or striking equipment.

Daily Explosion is similarly both ordinary and extraordinary. It is not only a symptom awaiting removal once the plot supplies a proper military use. She wants its practice, judges its beauty and invests her points in it. The carrying arrangement gives Kazuma a repeated role in her day; later battlefield knowledge grows out of those walks. A plan that preserves the spell can therefore preserve art, accumulated mastery and a shared relationship at once. Its relational value does not make every practice site safe or every companion's labor free. MAIN-01 C2P440–480; V09 PrP9–44/C1P61–83; V10 C2P369–458; V11 C2P168–175/C3P243–249. ## 3. Belonging makes specialization negotiable, but does not erase intention

The V05 ending is an essential correction to the idea that Explosion is absolutely non-negotiable. Megumin asks Kazuma to spend her saved points on advanced magic. His recognition of what she means is confirmed by her nod. This is not merely an opponent saying she should be more useful: it is her own accepted contribution problem and an offered change with a loss she cares about. Her wish to belong can bring the protected art into serious conflict.

The resolution must retain both agents' intentions. At F219 the narration expressly says Kazuma acts against Megumin's intention when he allocates the points to Explosion. He believes preserving the identity is the better answer; her later joy supplies evidence that the result matters to her. It does not establish prior joint consent or make her advanced-magic request unreal. Calling the event a consensual joint build would erase the very willingness to sacrifice that made it consequential. The accepted older reading's general point about flexibility survives; a stronger gloss of jointly chosen allocation does not. MAIN-05 F138–219, especially169–171/219, `item/xhtml/p-019.xhtml`.

V16 supplies a later, distinct consideration episode. At F399–408 she herself attributes a substantial contribution problem to her build and confronts alternatives. The conflict is serious even though the narrative later supplies resources that let her keep the chosen art. CP06 M02 is CONFIRMED for meaningful consideration at moderate confidence. No new permanent rebuild, promise or point expenditure is required by that tested effect. Conversely, one fulfilled consideration event does not show that she will reliably change under every future criticism.

External criticism and self-accepted burden must therefore remain separate. Yunyun's trial judgments in V13, reserve status in V15 and Kazuma's useful-point retort in V16 can wound competence or standing without producing the full contribution-guilt state. The timing in V16 is particularly limiting: Megumin lunges/grabs at Kazuma at C3P319; she demands the card320 and its handling is then evident321–322. The physical lunge precedes the later retort321, so her protest322 can respond to that retort but the earlier lunge cannot. No performed point allocation, physical restraint or proven compliance follows in that window. These exact orderings are more informative than a simple label of stubbornness or obedience.

The longitudinal development is not that she learns to abandon Explosion. It is that the text repeatedly exposes why continuing it matters and what she may be willing to give up to remain useful and included. The party's solutions can relieve the dilemma without proving it was false. A successful accommodation also leaves a question about who funds, carries, restrains and repairs its costs. ## 4. Identity defense can defeat acknowledged ordinary cost

Megumin's sensitivity is more exact than a general dislike of criticism. A rank dispute, an administrative reserve decision, a direct denigration of Explosion and an inferior imitation can overlap, but are not interchangeable. An account that labels every competitive exchange an explicit Explosion-legitimacy challenge would appear more consistent by discarding the frozen trigger's distinctions.

Where the trigger is strong, ordinary companion utility does not reliably restrain her. V09's manufactured imitation is a decisive adverse witness: Kazuma's money and labor are known, and she disposes of the object anyway. Attachment has not made his ordinary costs overriding. V13 supplies another bounded attempt. Kazuma explicitly values watching the spell duel at F221 and denigrates the one-shot attack's tactical exchange at224 before Megumin begins the chant232. His later235 warning is not the first possible access to ordinary cost. A disclosed wish to watch can be a real companion cost; it need not be money, bodily harm or a formally worded instruction. The attempt is not a completed cast, and jealous attention is a stated competing cause rather than a uniquely isolated identity mechanism.

V15's summons introduces a different mix. Reserve status follows her readiness; Yunyun hedges that lacking advanced magic is probably the reason. Megumin knows other recipients need the letter before destroying its area with Explosion and declaring herself first-team material. The administrative cost is real. Whether the decision explicitly denigrates or replaces Explosion under M01 remains a separate question; no formal-debate or named-rival-spell floor should be invented to force an answer. Yunyun's desired town defense and Megumin's resentment also coexist. An intervention can help a friend gain an excuse and still purchase that help through someone else's document and an identity payoff.

At the final volume's dynamite report, anger at an inferior substitute and the difficulty of covert production are represented. Physical destructive sabotage is not shown at F170–171. The distinction prevents an analytical escalation from disapproval to an unwitnessed act. Similarly, the king's late bluff challenge has to be assessed for what Megumin receives and can next choose; it is not a generic extra confirmation merely because it sounds dismissive.

The guarded conclusion remains CP06 M01 PARTIAL: identity sensitivity is well supported descriptively, but the new tranche incompletely tests ordinary-cost priority at the exact explicit-specialty subtype. Earlier adverse cost choices remain important to the literary account. Neither their presence nor a later caring act turns every competitive scene into the same experiment. ## 5. Mastery grows; the whole operation remains distributed

Megumin's progression is materially real. A one-cast attacker becomes able to perform more precise placements, usefully separate stages of an engagement, omit the incantation while retaining the spell name, and exploit external mana to repeat an otherwise terminal act. The source does not make each improvement proof of unrestricted versatility. She has selected a narrow field and becomes exceptionally accomplished within it.

MAIN-02's Destroyer chain is a compact example of distributed efficacy. Wiz and Megumin's paired Explosions remove the legs after Aqua's barrier work and Kazuma's timing. A separate dangerous stone, continued motion and residual heat still require additional work. In the final relay Megumin proposes help, resists the back-contact arrangement and accepts neck contact instead. Aqua→Kazuma→Megumin supplies the mana; the resulting attack is useful. Later success does not retroactively establish consent to the rejected contact or sole authorship of the entire victory. MAIN-02 C5P260–345/548–659 and EpP1–46.

V06's large-scale war again routes artillery through institutions and other people. V07 converts threatening magical theater into part of community and relationship defense, while allies' restraint and planning remain conditions. V09's raids work through range, enemy geometry, concealment, Teleport and the fort's repair system. Her new incantation-free control at the Wolbach confrontation is directly demonstrated; the spell name is still spoken. The date she acquired that control is not fixed simply because the reader sees it there.

V13's airburst and V14's changing deployment further separate destructive output from fixed execution ritual. Yet Kazuma's hedge about fine control of the V14 forest trajectory must remain: the shot visibly passes between trees, while his explanation of the mechanism is qualified. In the V14 robot fight, entry logistics, injury, recovery and terminal fire are different moments. In V16 an ogre-targeting benefit still knocks out allies. In V17 the barrage breaches the barrier and defeats a gatekeeper; it also produces physiological overload and calls for restraint.

This is why CP06 M03 can be NOT_TESTED while capacity is strongly evidenced. Its full prior arrangement requires safe friendly geometry, resources and exit/recovery planning before the cast. Successful damage does not fill missing prior safety, and an eventual carry does not prove a plan existed. Known Aqua inside the barrier is not to be removed because the attack succeeds. The monograph can credit extraordinary practiced power without certifying a fully matched safe-artillery reliability comparison. ## 6. Wolbach: the art's origin becomes the target

Wolbach's importance cannot be reduced to humanoid appearance or a generic reluctance to hurt an acquaintance. She is tied to the origin of Megumin's chosen art. An encounter Megumin had imagined as a report of achievement becomes a demand to destroy the person associated with that achievement. The power she has cultivated is available; taking the action has changed meaning.

V09 stages this difference before explaining it to Kazuma. The planned ambush has roles and contingencies, and Megumin has expressed concern about exposing Darkness to the enemy's spell. Recognition of the target then disrupts mechanically prepared action. Later she apologizes for not firing without being able to disclose the history. Kazuma's eventual help is effective partly because he asks what she wants while admitting he does not know the relationship. The alternatives are constrained: other adventurers still intend to kill the hostile opponent. Offering agency is not offering an emotionally costless outcome.

The final exchange keeps uncertainty on both sides. Wolbach refuses nonaggression and says restoring her power requires destroying Chomusuke; her allegiance motive remains unanswered. Megumin uses an additive wording clue to infer that her teacher remembers her despite denial. The inference has a verifiable linguistic basis, but is still an inference about another mind. Her quiet thanks and mastered control accompany the actual destructive act. Gratitude and mastery are not separate episodes in which only one can be sincere.

Victory then fails to settle the personal problem. Megumin describes herself as having killed a benefactor, and the party forgoes celebration because of her distress. Kazuma's possible sight of Wolbach's smile is uncertain and followed by a wish that it was real; it is not verified forgiveness. His consolation is a reframe, not proof that the original rescue was engineered to create a later pupil. Chomusuke's subsequent behavior cannot certify restored identity or memories. The volume deliberately leaves facts that would make the grief simple unavailable.

This is strong evidence for target-specific relational friction and a consequential aftermath. It is not a sufficient sample for every future bonded target. CP06 M04 remains NOT_TESTED in V16–V17: a companion at collateral risk is a different relation to the attack from a bonded person who is the actual opponent. Neither later absence of such a target nor the target's enemy status erases the V09 case. ## 7. Affection grows through ordinary practice, negotiation and imperfect agency

Kazuma's carrying role begins as a practical answer to the build. Repeated walking, watching and recovery become an ordinary relationship in which both know something specific about the other's values. Megumin can articulate attraction to his mixture of complaining, helping and flawed conduct. That is more particular than liking a generic heroic savior. It also helps explain why the same person's disregard, gifts and attention can matter differently from another person's.

The progression should not be accelerated. V09 gives reciprocal expressions of liking and, in Megumin's own epilogue, uncertainty about what they now are. Her first-person account checks Kazuma's uncertainty rather than resolving it by omniscience. V10 permits direct jealousy and a genuine choice about assisting Iris; she elects to help after he offers to seek another method if she refuses. A later apparent bedroom invitation belongs to Ragcraft's impersonation and is not Megumin's consent. V11 ends with the precise proposal `仲間以上恋人未満`. V12 enacts and accepts an intermediate relationship with secrecy and postponed sexual conduct. These are different advances, not interchangeable proof of a final exclusive settlement.

Material and social acts are especially telling. She secretly supplies money when Kazuma's resources are low and helps him through the window. Her belief that he deliberately chose her room overstates what he actually planned. In front of Komekko she prefers losing face to humiliating him or making kind adventurers lie. In V16 she chooses a private encounter and actually kisses and holds him; his response and later interruptions do not make all earlier or future boundaries automatic. The closet discovery includes distinct knowledge: Kazuma does not know Darkness is hidden, while Megumin identifies her before opening. Equal innocence or equal consent cannot be presumed.

The relationship's care also coexists with reciprocal self-serving interpretation. V12 Kazuma's initial rejection of Darkness is real, yet later arousal and available agency lead to active coaching. His generalized helpless-victim account omits that agency. Megumin's look is not omniscient access to the whole private episode, although his kneeling and her reply establish a local accountability concession. In V13 possessive wording pleases him without jointly renegotiating the relationship. In V15 Megumin chooses intimate display and helps restrain the altered Kazuma, but a failed one-hand hold and later teammate-directed hug are distinct configurations; she ends the continued hug after the cure. Consent is specific to a moment and act.

By V17, shared practice is also a tactical inheritance. Kazuma credits what he learned from watching her spell; her stones materially support the duel and transport. His relation labels and the epilogue do not establish marriage, completed sex or a stable exclusive household. The importance of the bond does not depend on inventing those endpoints. It consists in repeated chosen time, gifts, knowledge, negotiated advances and continuing disagreements whose costs are not all resolved. ## 8. Other relationships expose the limits of a romance-centered account

Yunyun is not simply an obstacle to Megumin's love plot or a passive supplier of Teleport. Megumin can welcome her visits, notice her exhaustion, care about her solitude and use the language of friendship as leverage. She can also defend an exclusive rival position, disregard a technically sound objection and make trial participation harder. V13's staff-surrender plan is an actual attempted adjustment, but further destruction contributes to another failed trial. The failures are chiefly Megumin's report, with Yunyun's complaint corroborating repeated failure; the source does not give unrestricted observer access to every trial step. V14's costly false confession helps Yunyun while concealment of the Axel welcome helps impose chiefdom. Help and paternalism coexist.

Their Wolbach histories are different. Yunyun's invitation memory and difficulty maintaining a duty declaration are not Megumin's origin story. Her refusal to adopt a life-defining Explosion build is an autonomous limit, not rejection of all friendship. V15's town-defense desire is likewise hers. Megumin's forceful solution gives it a route while retaining resentment and administrative damage as competing causes. A loving rivalry can be genuinely asymmetrical and costly.

Darkness is both rival and valued companion. Megumin makes her amulet particularly sturdy, worries about exposing her to enemy Explosion and later allows room for her confession without conceding unrestricted intimacy. Darkness restrains harmful acts and comforts Megumin's contribution crisis; their comparison of chosen limitations matters beyond competition for Kazuma. The V17 emergency-fire compromise has exact actors: Darkness catches Megumin at C1P185 and explains the load186; Kazuma forbids routine casting191, Megumin proposes an emergency exception192, and Kazuma yields194. It is not Darkness who sets that compromise.

Iris elicits mentorship, games, ordinary teaching and genuine defense, as well as jealousy and magical naming competition. An argument about seats can end with the two playing together instead of competing for Kazuma. Their relation should not be reduced to a triangle. Equally, an old accepted V06 household summary misidentifies one letter: in the actual Ep2, Aqua and Kazuma guess Iris, but Megumin explicitly says she is writing a fan letter to the masked thief. Her admiration of that heroic figure is not proof of romance or of knowing Kazuma's hidden identity. This bounded correction concerns that letter; other main-principal Iris friendship witnesses remain.

Family care supplies another axis. Megumin protects Komekko, weighs remittances, cooks and values the household's noise; status and control do not disappear from those interactions. The same person who can accept embarrassment to spare Kazuma can weaponize embarrassment elsewhere. The character's social intelligence is real because it can read, protect and pressure particular people, not because it produces uniformly benevolent results. ## 9. The danger that affection and mastery do not reliably brake

Megumin's combat success often makes the ethical question easier to overlook. A productive spell can create new incentives to repeat it. In V09, separated raids and Teleport are tactically effective; experience and growth become rewarding enough to weaken the original stopping point. Enemy pleas, surrender and claimed disarmament appear in the text. Their every biographical claim is not independently verified, and the panic is comically amplified, but those limits do not erase the party's choices. Kazuma participates in targeting before restating the mission. This is shared agency, not proof Megumin acted alone or that only he failed to supervise her.

V17 supplies a different, bodily visible continuation. The barrier collapses and the gatekeeper falls, yet Megumin wants further fire at C1P183 and needs external restraint185. Overload is real. Later balcony attacks renew the nosebleed. These are actual failures of stopping and self-regulation; a later transport success cannot make them internally safe choices. CP06 M06 is PARTIAL because the complete guarded operation conjunction, especially prior safe friendly conditions, is not established. That formal missingness is not a rescue of the observed desire to continue, nor permission to redefine her old goal after the fact by Kazuma's inference that she wants to return to the castle.

The starkest relational limit is V17 C3P425–438. Darkness promises shielding and orders a lethal response; Megumin accepts possible mutual death426 and loads the threat437 despite wounded protection and other allies' appeals. Known grave risk is represented before the choice. No completed release or actual death is needed to make that acceptance adverse to broad claims that affection reliably inhibits grave companion harm. Survival and the later intervention by Aqua cannot be backdated into her information. Darkness's authorization is a separate agent's choice, not a guarantee of safety or a transfer of all Megumin's responsibility.

The formal M05 result remains AMBIGUOUS because the exact best tactical option, feasible alternatives and decision conditions are unresolved. That preserves a real distinction: the guard may not be fully identified, while the shared-death appraisal is an important contrary branch. It would be wrong either to confirm reliable inhibition from the absence of a completed blast or to silently replace the frozen formal category with a broad moral verdict. The literary finding is firm at its own scale: love of companions does not make destruction morally or practically subordinate in every represented state.

Her care elsewhere must remain beside this. She can seek less harmful placements, shield a person, restrain a weakened cast, make protective gifts and suffer after harming a valued target. Those acts are not cancelled by the shared-death scene. They cannot cancel it either. An adequate account gives her the capacity for both and asks what appraisal is governing the actual choice. ## 11. The observer does not own her whole interior

Kazuma's affection gives him detailed practical knowledge; it also creates interpretive temptations. He can infer what a reluctant request costs her and still choose against the request. He can enjoy possessive wording and call it a relational promotion without her negotiating a new status.
