# Chomusuke: creature agency within a disputed divine continuity

## 1. Identity and narrative position

Chomusuke (ちょむすけ) is a small, responsive creature with relationships and bodily stakes of her own. Kuro (クロ), the earlier household name, and Megumin's teasing Kuro variants refer to the same creature in the relevant exchanges; they are not extra individuals. Megumin explicitly assigns Chomusuke at EXP01 U043 P611, after the creature twists and before an immediate startle. Yunyun calls the creature female at P607. Neither that address nor the small body supplies an exact human age, an adult human mind or complete divine personhood.

Arnes calls the creature Wolbach and her master (EXP02 U023 P37–59). Later named Wolbach calls Chomusuke her half, reaches toward her and says she would destroy the creature to recover power (MAIN09 F P148–154/501–509). These are substantial identity and relationship evidence. They do not establish that the small body currently remembers everything the speaking Wolbach remembers, shares every intention, or authorizes what other people do to it. The separate Wolbach monograph keeps those interests and access planes distinct.

The early black beast is actually attacked, drained, shrunk and resealed in the rescue sequence (EXP01 U014/U016). The source subsequently develops a small creature living with Megumin and Komekko, later sought by subordinates and a half. The earlier rescue, resealing, later opening and domestic naming are chronological stages, not a single event whose opener must be whichever child appears in the latest retelling. Complete mechanics of the transitions remain less explicit than the relational continuity. Arnes, despite similar hair/eye descriptions to the speaking Wolbach, is separately named and acting; she is not merged with either half.

## 2. Chronology and source access

Early domestic scenes show both care and danger. The creature trembles inside a cooking pot and is removed by Megumin; Komekko offers food but also bites it, producing a cry (EXP01 U023 P201–247). Megumin then brings it into school-life negotiations. It approaches Yunyun's working hand and nuzzles, while the girls interpret its action through a wager. That is actual behavior within their social use of it, not a signed endorsement of the wager.

The later monster episode includes the creature crawling out of a torn bag, being carefully held without resistance, and being carried away (EXP01 U043 P75–110). Megumin argues that docility means little danger, an interested inference rather than complete knowledge of consent or fate. The eventual household handling, familiar proposal and naming show other people's choices; the current window does not depict a separate contract spell or the creature's informed assent.

EXP02 supplies a particularly clear refusal. When Megumin offers a paid handover, Chomusuke clings and resists (U023 P69–73). Later throws, threatened use as a shield and a dangerous proposed Explosion are separate events. The creature struggles, escapes a distracted holder and is caught by Yunyun. MAIN04 shows resistance above water; MAIN09 shows approaches toward Wolbach and a later actual warm-water entry. MAIN15 and MAIN16 then offer different household viewpoints. Repetition through retellings, indices or another narrator does not make these additional independent tests.

## 3. Motivations and valuations that the behavior supports

Food, warmth, contact, familiar proximity and avoidance of unwanted handling are reasonable bounded interpretations. They are not complete verbal goals supplied by the creature. Chomusuke actually eats, approaches people, purrs, clings, withdraws, claws, bites, struggles and curls in warm water. The account describes those actions before naming a motive.

In EXP02 U023, remaining on Megumin's belly and purring under touch are followed by forceful resistance to delivery to Arnes. These observations support a current preference for staying and resisting transfer. Megumin's assertion that the creature is completely satisfied with its entire life exceeds the local observation, especially given the pot, bite and later dangerous uses. Familiar attachment does not certify adequate care in every respect.

The fish theft and eating in MAIN15 Pr P22–26 support a present food interest. Aqua's belief that the creature ranks her below Kazuma is an interpretation, not a narrated internal hierarchy. In MAIN16, Chomusuke seeks Aqua's hand while rejecting belly contact. Contact-seeking and a particular bodily boundary coexist. The possibility that it is trying to delay Aqua's departure is raised by Aqua; the text does not give the creature an explicit statement of understanding or purpose.

## 4. Affect and personality without invented interior speech

The creature is often quiet, apparently self-directed and difficult for humans to manage. “Unhurried,” “brazen” and similar descriptions belong to particular observers. A relaxed posture does not establish global fearlessness. Pot trembling, clinging during a handover, struggling before a throw and claws above water retain their immediate bodily contexts. The text's comedy does not convert them into absent distress.

Affection is similarly specific. Purring under Megumin's touch, nuzzling Kazuma, staying close after Wolbach's disappearance and headbutting Aqua's hand are actual forms of contact. They need not all have identical causes. Nor do they prove a universal preference ordering among the household. MAIN16's unusual approach to Aqua is contrary evidence to a fixed “always hates Aqua” account; its belly refusal is contrary evidence to an equally simple “now permits everything” account.

The startle at naming, tremor during Megumin's ambition statement, and ear movement at Wolbach's name are observations with suggestive contexts. They do not independently expose a complete remembered biography. The analyst should resist turning every movement into a knowing divine joke, just as it should resist treating every movement as meaningless scenery.

## 5. Competence and bodily limits

Current small-form competence includes walking between people, clinging, scratching, biting, slipping from a holder and locating a place to curl up. Those are meaningful acts, even when their significance is ordinary creature behavior. In EXP02 U044 P555, Chomusuke slips from distracted Arnes, but Yunyun catches the falling body at P559. The escape does not demonstrate controlled flight or an ability to make the earlier throw safe. Other actors' protection remains necessary to the observed outcome.

Power claims have unequal access. Arnes's master and comprehension claims are hers. The speaking Wolbach's divided-power account is hers, alongside a visibly faded hand. In MAIN15, Kazuma sees the fish theft and recognizes a scratch, but explicitly does not look when Aqua cries out about heat; her report of present fire follows (Pr P27–38). This particular scene therefore does not become Kazuma's watched fire-breath event. His reference to earlier fire and fish-cooking retains its own earlier access rather than being disproved by his present averted gaze.

The weak-creature episode is also bounded. Megumin reports finding Chomusuke limp at the inn; Yunyun asks whether water and food were left (EXP03 U020 P610–611). That question is not a supplied answer, quantified feeding record or diagnosis. Exhaustion, illness, inadequate care or magical conditions cannot be selected merely to complete a tidy explanation. The creature's vulnerability is real without the analyst inventing its cause.

## 6. Choice, information and learning

Nonverbal action can discriminate between available alternatives without providing human-style deliberation. The clinging refusal during the paid handover is stronger evidence of present resistance than a narrator's preceding family rhetoric. Turning away from Arnes while continuing to rest and purr is another current response, not proof of forgotten or retained master-subordinate memories. Arnes's prediction that the creature understands some human language is not a performed comprehension test.

Likewise, approaching Yunyun's hand during arm wrestling may reflect smell, contact-seeking, food association or a social effect. Megumin uses the creature as leverage and Yunyun reacts, but deliberate cooperation in Megumin's scheme is unproved (EXP01 U023 P271–320). A future model should compare such alternatives rather than smuggle a strategic human collaborator into the scene.

After Wolbach disappears, Kazuma observes warm-water interest, basin testing and entry, plus an ear movement at the name (MAIN09 F P841–860). He asks whether it is coincidence, anthropomorphism, a future human form or slight growth. These remain questions. The change deserves an account and temporal indexing, but neither learning nor absorption is conclusively isolated as its cause. Apparent growth is not a measured developmental trajectory.

## 7. Relationships and differing uses by others

Megumin supplies familiarity and some care while also exploiting, endangering and threatening the creature. She removes it from the pot, brings it along and pets it; she also changes family rhetoric into a paid handover, throws it, uses it as a shield, and begins an Explosion while it is in Arnes's arms. Her later attachment does not retrospectively authorize those acts. A familiar label does not make expendability a fact of the creature's own values.

Yunyun repeatedly gives concern and practical protection. She objects to abandonment, notices danger, interrupts a chant and catches the falling creature. She can also hold or restrain it while the group acts; protection does not automatically confer access to its private mind. Komekko provides food and affectionate familiarity alongside food-directed threat and an actual bite. Those different choices belong to Komekko, not a single universal cruelty or care label.

Arnes treats the creature as a valued master, postpones transfer on seeing resistance, catches it before impact and looks relieved after Yunyun's later catch. She also threatens people in pursuit of it and creates danger through her own hostile acts. Wolbach reaches toward it and needs its power, while describing a destructive plan for it. Kazuma provides contact and protection at times, but also restrains it and makes its release conditional on Wolbach's compliance. Different claimants' affection is not interchangeable ownership, shared consent or guaranteed welfare.

## 8. Ordinary life, care and finite costs

Domestic behavior is substantial evidence. Meals, sleeping positions, baths, contact, carrying and interruption make up much of this character's realized life. EXP01 includes feeding offers and a vocal response around salmon skin, not only the household's threats. EXP02 includes ordinary purring before its instrumental use escalates. MAIN15 gives theft and eating in a noisy breakfast; MAIN16 gives head contact, a rejected belly reach and a lap visit during a late-night farewell.

Care has material and temporal requirements. A claim that the creature will be raised well is not a complete care record. A question about food is not a neglect diagnosis, but it identifies an unanswered practical issue. A caught fall is a successful protection at that moment, not proof that throwing was safe. A name, contract proposal, meal offer, received food and completed transfer occupy different stages.

The actual bath contrast is especially useful. MAIN04 C3 P193–197 distinguishes Megumin's report of usual bath resistance from current claws while Kazuma holds the creature above water. MAIN09 F P852–854 then depicts it testing and entering a provided warm basin. This is a real later contrast, not merely another person's unverified preference report. It still does not establish all future bath choices, an exact causal transition or permission for forced washing.

## 9. Voice and perspective

Chomusuke vocalizes and moves; the source does not give a continuous first-person human account or a demonstrated adult dialogue style in these scenes. Rendered cries, purrs and meows should not be expanded into fluent private speeches that resolve every identity question. The human characters frequently supply what a look or movement supposedly means. Those interpretations are valuable relationship evidence and remain attributed.

Narrator changes matter. MAIN15 is Kazuma's view: he averts his gaze during the reported fire. MAIN16 I1 is Aqua's view: she reports her own contact, smell and departure hypotheses. EXP01–03 often provide Megumin's interested accounts, including rationalizations for abandoning or using the creature. The EXP03 carriage interlude is Yunyun's access to the speaking woman, not Chomusuke remembering that conversation.

The current preparation directly rechecks known BASE text and eight integral EXP03 prose images with visible ruby. It does not supply a native-human adequacy assessment or a complete new ruby traversal. A future reconstruction could preserve nonverbal agency without requiring human dialogue. The present lack of such dialogue is a constraint on claims, not proof that creature characters can never support models.

## 10. Comedy, seriousness and adverse evidence

The creature often serves as comic punctuation, but some of the jokes depend on actual ignored resistance and bodily danger. The bite and cry, pot trembling, claws during attempted transfer, throws, and struggle before aerial capture are not erased by the family's familiar treatment or a warm ending. Conversely, survival and recurring household life should not be replaced by an invented fatal injury or a complete clinical trauma account.

EXP02 U044 makes the distinction sharp. Megumin threatens a haircut, which is not carried out; then she actually throws Chomusuke and begins Explosion while Arnes holds it. Yunyun physically stops her. Megumin argues that a familiar may die for its owner and resumes chanting; only later does a separate interruption allow escape and a catch. The safe outcome does not become voluntary restraint by Megumin, informed sacrifice by Chomusuke, or proof of the plan's safety.

The same source includes real concern from an enemy and harm from friends. Arnes's catches and relief do not erase her threats. Yunyun's effective protection does not erase the danger that prompted it. The analysis retains both causal contributions and recipients' stakes rather than sorting every participant into a permanent benevolent or harmful role.

## 11. Rival explanations and current artifact scope

Generic creature behavior is a serious rival to many interpretations: food-seeking, response to touch, avoidance of restraint and a preference for familiar people can account for much without a hidden adult divinity. Conditioning, context, fatigue and changing bodily state are further rivals. The half relation, changing bath behavior and naming reactions make simple reduction insufficient as a complete identity account, but they do not select one elaborate mechanism by themselves.

The current disposition is Tier2: a substantial monograph and evidence index, with no new reconstruction model or generated case battery. This is not a ceiling based on appearance count, pet status or unwillingness to model nonhuman action. Presently, a model claiming distinctive human-like goals, theological knowledge or transferable discourse would outrun the observed distinctions; a generic feeding/handling rule would have little demonstrated character-specific discrimination. A narrower behavioral model could later be earned by explicit comparisons, careful state indexing and adverse cases.

No paragraph total is an independence estimate, and no outcome already known to the author becomes a blind test by being restated as a hypothetical. The creature's opacity is not an excuse to omit agency or practical care. It is a reason to show what the observations constrain and what they leave open.

## 12. Current interpretation and outstanding questions

Chomusuke is attached and resistant, vulnerable and active, ordinary in many behaviors and linked by substantial source evidence to divided divinity. Her life cannot be reconstructed solely as Wolbach's dormant asset or Megumin's prop. Current preferences are often visible through bodily choices even when precise beliefs and mechanisms are not.

Open questions include memory continuity, comprehension, power access in each state, the causal basis and durability of changed bathing, the extent of any growth, adequate care at the weak episode, and the relation between the speaking half's plans and the creature's interests. Earlier MAIN03 and later MAIN10/12/17 analytical leads retain their separate accepted access planes; they require exact reconciliation where a future claim depends on them. Creator hypotheticals and reference descriptions must not become performed in-story tests.

Future work should compare actual offered contact with imposed handling, current approach with consent to further acts, reported capability with witnessed use, and observed change with explanations of change. It should preserve errors and coercion in any model of the human household, and preserve the creature's distinct agency in any account of Wolbach. The wider individual/event/alias/incidental census and full program remain active.
