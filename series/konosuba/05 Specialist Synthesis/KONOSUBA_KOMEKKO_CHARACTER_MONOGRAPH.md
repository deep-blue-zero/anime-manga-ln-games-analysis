# Komekko — play, food, bargaining and concrete attachment

## 1. Identity, evidence and disposition

Komekko, こめっこ, is Megumin's younger sister and a child growing within the Crimson Demon village's material and verbal habits. Megumin says she has just turned five at EXP01 U018 P407. Later appearances retain their own chronology; the present audit does not turn that age into a constant. Komekko calls herself the person minding the house and performs a grand little-sister identity. Those are meaningful parts of her self-presentation, alongside wanting a meal, a puzzle, a ride, a returned relative or an impressive future.

The strongest unusual evidence is five integral, first-person image-prose interludes in EXP01: IMG026–043. They give Komekko her own attention and descriptions, beyond Megumin's or Kazuma's appraisal of a resourceful younger child. The eighteen pages form a continuing relationship and staged seal problem. They are not eighteen independent confirmations, a witnessed act of diary writing or separate episodes merely because a new image begins. Their background Megumin art is decorative and does not change the prose speaker.

The current audit also actually receives 1,602 unique base-text member slots across fifteen units in EXP01, EXP02, MAIN05, MAIN11 and MAIN14. Those bounded rechecks overlap the admitted corpus and prior exposure; they confer no new whole-volume, ruby or independent-observation credit. Six native EPUB identities and twenty prepared text units were mechanically bound; only the actually read ranges receive current semantic credit. Existing EXP03, MAIN09 retelling and separate game/reference analyses retain their original planes. No game mechanics or adaptation evidence supplies a new child capacity here.

The resulting disposition is **scoped Tier 3: a substantial monograph and provisional generative model**. Komekko presents differentiated choices across play, provision, ownership, sibling parting, public admiration, a household dispute and a visitor's return. The evidence supports defeasible mechanisms and plausible departures from them. It does not establish mature, independently validated prediction, a complete developmental account or reliable conduct in unfamiliar severe situations. The [separate reconstruction model](../06%20Character%20Modeling%20and%20Reconstruction/KONOSUBA_KOMEKKO_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md) owns operational rules; the [evidence index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_KOMEKKO_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) owns locators, source planes and event joins. Adoption and publication have separate receipts.

## 2. A child with her own purposes

Adults repeatedly discover that Komekko does not simply enact the role they prepared for her. Hoost wants a seal solved. Megumin wants a safe, admiring younger sister and, at parting, a visible demonstration of dependence. Luna wants stalled work accepted. Kazuma wants his own dramatic weapon introduction. Komekko wants things that intersect with those projects, sometimes helping them and sometimes frustrating them. The apparent success of the adult's larger scene should not erase the child's different objective.

In the first interlude Komekko treats the seal as her puzzle, admires Hoost's appearance, fails to understand his dangerous explanation fully, and rapidly progresses at a task on which he reports spending months. When hunger makes further work unattractive, Komekko leaves the puzzle to him; his request changes toward food and deference. In the second visit she attempts flattery, an exaggerated hardship appeal and a future threat. Hoost explicitly resists those tactics. A practical explanation about hunger and a polite food request precede permission to eat. After the meal she becomes sleepy, demonstrating that successful feeding does not automatically purchase the effort the provider wants. (EXP01 IMG026–031.)

In the third interlude real progress gives way to boredom. The next attraction is an excursion: Komekko insists on riding with the apparently powerful Hoost. In the forest she wants to catch a large lizard, considers whether making a monster flee would prove her stronger than him, and runs toward bears. The group ends with Hoost's alarm. No defeat of the bears, completed rescue or demonstrated understanding of the hazard is shown. Komekko's initiative is real; the reader cannot fill the missing outcome with invulnerability or heroic success. (IMG032–035.)

The offering episode displays an equally important limit. Komekko cannot read the inscription and asks Hoost to do so. He only partly reads it, then gives money for a hen and its child. She brings an actual oyakodon meal. Her concrete, food-centered implementation frustrates his ritual plan. The title's food joke is resolved by the bowl; it supplies no adult sexual motive for the child. Hoost eventually leaves to prepare the offering himself and explicitly lets her eat. Komekko guards that transferred meal against a small beast, persists through interference and returns home with the exhausted animal as a trophy. Megumin's subsequent kitten scene is the same encounter's aftermath, with Komekko's additional reported claim of biting; it is not a second independent victory. (IMG036–039; U018 P417–439.)

These scenes identify agency without requiring omniscient strategy. Komekko acts on hunger, play, novelty, ownership and perceived strength. Some appeals work, others fail, and a real misunderstanding redirects a purchase. Hoost has his own goal, knowledge gaps, authority and refusals. Neither participant is merely the other's mechanism.

## 3. Food, scarcity and the possibility of giving

Food interest has a material basis. At the MAIN05 family visit, Komekko asks whether the gift is solid, filling food rather than the thin porridge she says she usually eats. At MAIN11 she jumps to make room for more, explains that such abundance is rare, treats pudding as a birthday luxury and carefully thanks the people offering their portions. Reassurance about present resources does not immediately remove the expectation that food should be taken while available. (MAIN05 U020 P223–241; MAIN11 C3 P221–241.)

The material claim needs calibration. EXP01 Komekko reports using a three-days-without-solid-food appeal on a newspaper visitor and shows meal vouchers. The negotiation itself is reported, not witnessed. Hoost explicitly counters a similar appeal by recalling yesterday's food. The record therefore contains both actual scarcity-related context and a contradicted hardship tactic. It warrants neither certifying every extreme claim nor dismissing the household's poverty as invented. A clinical account of deprivation or a complete audit of parental care would exceed the evidence. (EXP01 U018 P410–413; IMG031.)

Komekko's giving is particularly revealing because the food remains wanted. She pauses over Megumin's sandwich and offers part of it, then accepts the sister's stated fullness. On a later visit she studies the two buns in her hands, swallows and offers the untouched one to Kazuma while unable to take her eyes off it. Only after he says he is full does she settle beside him and resume eating. The visible cost gives these offers more significance than a generic kindness label. Reciprocity toward a provider, a learned offering custom, simple fairness and affection remain plausible competing explanations. (EXP01 U018 P435–439; MAIN05 U020 P230–241.)

At Megumin's departure, Komekko provides a rice-ball lunch and gives the treasured picture book associated with their bedtime reading, explicitly framing the book around her sister's loneliness. Megumin infers the lunch was prepared for her; the wrapped rice ball and book transfer are actual. The book is treasured property, not evidence of Komekko's authorship. These acts extend the child beyond consuming other people's resources. They also do not imply that she always shares or accepts care work: a recovered food-object is guarded, a practical carrying request can be rejected and a hidden chocolate can outweigh immediate reconciliation. (EXP02 U023 P608–620; EXP01 U043 P391; MAIN11 C4 P572–577; MAIN14 C4 P90–94.)

## 4. Play and competence have boundaries

Komekko has exceptional local puzzle skill. She solves stages the powerful Hoost cannot complete and makes substantial further progress while he complains of difficulty. The same child becomes bored before finishing the interaction he wants. Competence and motivation must therefore be represented separately. Her skill does not supply general literacy, a measured intelligence quotient, foresight about every released being or readiness for the adult tasks around her. (EXP01 IMG026–037.)

Ordinary curiosity also matters. Komekko admires Aqua's impossible-seeming entertainment and asks how it works; after becoming full she still excitedly reports the performance to her parents. At bedtime she asks what a word in the picture book means, receives an explanation, resumes listening and falls asleep on Megumin's shoulder. MAIN11 supplies Aqua's account of losing a board game with a tiny handicap; it does not supply a complete game transcript or a universal strategic ranking. Tea service at the later family home is actual practical competence. These are different domains rather than interchangeable genius evidence. (MAIN05 U020 P174–185/U022 P39–63; EXP02 U013 P8–20; MAIN11 F P24–30; MAIN14 C1 P272–274.)

Her attempts at useful action can be flawed. During the EXP01 escape Komekko offers to hold Yunyun's feet while Megumin carries her. The resulting posture hurts Yunyun. A helper can mean to solve the visible problem and still lack the physical understanding needed to do so. At MAIN05's battle, Komekko notices a blinking display and pulls the weapon trigger while held by Aqua. Kazuma recalls the mechanism and aims; Megumin's magic charges it. Komekko makes an actual decisive contribution without becoming the designer, gunner, energy source or sole tactical author. Her stronger-than-a-general pose follows the outcome, which makes the self-appraisal understandable without certifying general combat superiority. (EXP01 U043 P364–367; MAIN05 U027 P655–697.)

## 5. Bargaining, refusal and uneven regulation

Komekko often responds to the literal practical terms of what she hears. When Megumin says pleading will be futile, Komekko agrees not to plead. That reply disappoints the sister's wish for reassurance without proving emotional indifference. When a task is inconvenient, Komekko can suggest a simpler alternative: leave the sleeping mother for now and bring father later. The father retrieval is a proposal; the shown outcome is Komekko dozing and Megumin covering the adults with blankets. (EXP02 U023 P541–560; MAIN14 C4 P90–94.)

The MAIN11 kitchen dispute shows stronger local calculation. Komekko anticipates a scolding before its explanation, refuses the city-stranger warning, runs into the kitchen and apparently locks it. A threatened missed dinner loses force because the room contains food. Finding hidden chocolate then changes Megumin's demand to reconciliation. Komekko still says she will emerge only after eating it. The scene demonstrates resource selection, resistance and a changing balance of leverage. It does not prove that she knew about the chocolate from the start, ate every supply, ultimately came out at a specified time or judged stranger safety reliably. Her statement that she would follow a person offering food is alarming in context but remains speech rather than a newly performed abduction. (MAIN11 C4 P538–577.)

Compliance is possible but uneven. Komekko asks a question, thanks a giver, accepts another person's stated fullness, agrees to some instructions and responds to concrete explanations. She also repeats a label after correction, refuses to surrender a food-object, asserts that she will fight and interrupts a dramatic introduction. During EXP01 she notices Megumin's tears and later appears uneasy about her changing eyes. That concern prevents blanket fearlessness from becoming a character law. Yunyun physically shields her during Explosion; the shielding is the older girl's action and cannot be recast as the child's independently well-calibrated tactical discipline. (EXP02 U015 P8–17; EXP01 U043 P239–278/P310–314/P391/P428–429/P472–493.)

## 6. Particular relationships and real attachment

Megumin is provider, teacher, model of speech, valued sister and person to resist. At the EXP01 crisis Komekko immediately says she likes her, then answers the prospect of a less-powerful sister by volunteering to become strongest herself. At the EXP02 parting, public bluntness gives way to a returned hand squeeze, a hug and a quiet request for an early return. The remembered book and lunch make attachment concrete. The later refusal to recognize a returning sister after missed visits and absent souvenirs preserves disappointment and material expectations within that bond. Megumin's own interpretation that Komekko is hurt for attention remains an alternative to Komekko's stated gift complaint; the narrator need not settle the exact balance. (EXP01 U043 P253–258; EXP02 U023 P565–620; MAIN14 C1 P261–269.)

Hoost begins as an imposing stranger with a project and becomes Komekko's first named friend in her own farewell narration. Provision and companionship grow together. Komekko admires his wings, touches his foot, bargains over work, wants a ride and dislikes his departure. Hoost's promise of possible future service is explicitly conditional on ending his existing contract and her ability to summon him; his devil-user appraisal is prediction. Komekko's intended-master introduction is an aspiration. Her sustained waving until he disappears is an actual farewell act. The bond contains food, but its shown affect does not end with eating. (EXP01 IMG026–035/040–043.)

Kazuma can be a provider, a recipient of her offered bun, an ally against scolding and a selectively welcomed visitor. Aqua supplies food, entertainment and play; she also physically carries or protects the child. Yunyun is admired after a visible spell and receives attempted practical help. Parents and neighbors supply reported teachings, instructions and real care arrangements: the shoemaker actually agrees to help and says the parents had already arranged it. That last distinction matters when interpreting parental absence. Komekko's ability to find food and company is not proof that she has no dependence on others. (MAIN05 U020 P230–248/U027 P679–686; MAIN11 C4 P568/F P24–68; EXP01 U043 P224–225/P364–366; EXP02 U023 P640–641.)

Luna's relationship with Komekko is asymmetrical. Luna supplies ice cream, asks the child to listen, prompts a sympathetic answer and then announces what the adventurers will do under the child's admiring gaze. Komekko's admiration can be sincere while being used by an adult institution. Her acceptance of listening does not authorize the adults' risks or establish her as the architect of the labor system. Later she actively asks for work in which her unimpressive-seeming sister can look impressive, then accepts a food-producing toad quest even after being told it is humble work. Her preference is concretely legible; a sophisticated moral program for labor dignity is not demonstrated. (MAIN11 C3 P376–444; F P97–120.)

## 7. Knowledge, reports and written voice

Komekko's knowledge cannot be copied from the reader. The first-person interludes expose Hoost conversations Megumin does not witness. Megumin later reports asking at home and confirming that Komekko undid the seal; the village's divine battle account is a different, false interpretation. Komekko repeats that adult account to Hoost in the final interlude. Her report does not make her an eyewitness to a goddess battle or a reliable cosmological authority. Hoost's refusal to inspect her small creature similarly prevents his kitten guess from becoming an informed identification. (EXP01 U043 P538–572; IMG040–041.)

MAIN11 explicitly shows Komekko writing in a memo as Megumin looks over her shoulder. Komekko names Bukkorori as the source for particular labels and reports her mother's assignment to record events around the party. The epilogue supplies Yuiyui's reading of that memo. The child's sparse achievement descriptions include an account she does not understand despite Megumin's explanation. The private relationship phrase is, by contrast, accurately overheard and repeated. Correct quotation, causal understanding, direct witnessing and awareness of adult social implications are separate capacities. (C3 P204–213; Ep P93–99.)

Written pragmatic range is more informative than a catchphrase list. Komekko corrects a dismissive address with her name, thanks Hoost while acknowledging incomplete understanding, tries a formal practical food appeal, copies his grudging permission formula and performs the grand Crimson Demon introduction. Hoost explicitly acknowledges teaching that particular phrase. EXP02 supplies Komekko's report that Bukkorori taught the attention-seeking/spoilt barbs. MAIN14's attribution of a different stinginess label to Bukkorori is Megumin's guess. These cannot become one universal teacher. Ordinary brief thanks, direct questions, tea service, blunt appraisal and the quiet request for return also belong to the voice map. No printed page supplies acoustic performance or native-human validation. (EXP01 IMG026–031/043; EXP02 U023 P550–588; MAIN11 C3 P240–241; MAIN14 C1 P272–284.)

## 8. Contrary evidence and competing interpretations

| Simple account | Evidence it loses | Current interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Food exclusively determines conduct | Costly bun/sandwich offers, treasured book, returned hug and farewell waving | Food is highly salient; particular giving and attachment have independent explanatory work. |
| A perfect little manipulator always gets what she wants | Hoost resists several tactics, the offering purchase fails, he retains the birds after rejecting their intended uses | Local leverage and learned tactics coexist with errors and another person's effective refusal. |
| A universally gifted strategist | Reading difficulty, vocabulary questions, harmful carrying help, poor causal retelling and others' weapon expertise | Capacities are task-specific and knowledge routes matter. |
| An angel who always helps | Kitchen resistance, rejected carrying request, food guarding and refused pleading | Particular care does not guarantee obedience or universal generosity. |
| Fearless combat superiority | Unshown bear outcome, physical shielding by Yunyun, concern at Megumin's eyes and conditional future aptitude | Initiative is stronger evidence than calibrated risk or transferable power. |
| Adult captions reveal the child's mature intentions | Future-seductress or glutton-god framing, adult guild design and incomplete memo comprehension | Preserve adult interpretation and child action as separate evidence. |
| Adult predictions establish later pet/plant eating | Actual remarks and fearful responses coexist with no shown meal | Treat future consumption and motives as unresolved, even when plausible. |

The comedy often comes from adult expectations meeting a child's concrete classification. It can expose an adult's vanity, make a dangerous thing look edible, or redirect the emotional farewell the older sister wants. Those functions do not exhaust Komekko as an individual. The same record supplies ordinary skill, curiosity, selective courtesy, bodily states, costly giving, disappointment, particular affection and real mistakes. Literal fantasy scale and comic amplification should be retained at the source plane while transfer claims remain cautious. All treatment and generation involving the child remain nonsexual and age-appropriate.

## 9. Current limits and work this enables

The generative model should be strongest in ordinary resource, play and named-relationship contexts. It can distinguish a hungry child from a sleepy one, an unsolicited taking from an offered gift, an unwanted performance from a quiet affectionate act, and a directly observed action from a heard explanation. The source does not identify numerical value weights or promise one unique action whenever motives compete. The same-author four-pair exercise produced eight first recorded answers and exposed a location error in the snack-room answer. That adverse body is preserved, with an attributable location-check clarification in model draft 02. Other contrasts have strong generic or explicit-input rivals, and the hidden-knowledge pair gives identical answers with no behavioral discrimination. The exercise supplies no independent accuracy, language adequacy or repair of consumed-source validation history.

The broader project should carry these findings into the expanded Megumin and party/transfer revisions through their prescribed versioned route. Megumin's private understanding must not absorb reader-only Hoost scenes. Her care does not erase Komekko's refusals; the child's admiration does not supply others' consent. Aqua's competence and care require their own acting-subject attribution. No entering model or frozen principal evidence is changed by this monograph.

Long-term development, peer relationships, unfamiliar serious fear, bereavement, formal duties, controlled magical training and stable behavior under prolonged scarcity remain open. The separate game novelization, retellings, commentary and reference portrayals cannot silently close these gaps. Character discovery and reconstruction evaluation retain their designated owners; a new monograph/model does not itself complete curator enrollment, final census dispositions or the full program.
