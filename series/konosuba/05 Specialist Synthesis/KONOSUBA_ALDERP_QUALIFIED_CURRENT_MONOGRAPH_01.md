# Alderp: possession, delegated power and the failure of an authored reality — current reference-qualified version

This substantial current version retains the accepted actor-specific argument and makes the individually reviewed Kazuma references explicit. The original public paper and private experiment foundation remain exact historical representations; no tested model, rule, input, first response or score changes. The [authority record](../09%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/KONOSUBA_EARLIER_MONOGRAPH_AUTHORITY_ADOPTION_01.md) records every correction.

Status: initial scoped Tier3 monograph, source-known and provisional. The separate model owns conditional proposals; AL28 locators in the [cohort evidence index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_ALDERP_MAXWELL_RAGCRAFT_COHORT28_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) own recoverable native contexts. No final maturity or independent validation is claimed.

## 1. Identity and social location

Alexei Barnes Alderp / アレクセイ・バーネス・アルダープ is the named territorial lord introduced in MAIN03. Balter is introduced as his son. Ignis is the father of Darkness, whose personal name is Lalatina; he is a different noble. Aulip is identified as a relative by Rain's later account, without a demonstrated complete genealogy. The unnamed young photographer in MAIN07 has no secured Alderp/Aigis/Undain identity (AL28-01–02, AL28-12).

Alderp's office, household, money and artifact-based access to a devil are concrete resources. They do not make him the author of every decision around him. His public authority meets higher family standing, truth-device resistance, contractual limits and rivals willing to incur costs. His private focalization matters because it exposes desired explanations that his public behavior alone could conceal.

## 2. Chronology and evidence planes

MAIN03 begins with real property damage and a prosecution in which some official errors are corrected. Alderp demands death, loses an intervention to the truth device and appears linked to a disturbing reversal. Darkness obtains a delay through her family position. The scene does not name the exact malign operator or show Sena knowingly conspiring; later knowledge of Maxwell cannot be silently inserted into her mind (AL28-01).

The son's marriage interview precedes MAIN06 hospitality, voyeuristic preparation and private plans for body theft. MAIN07 develops illness, debt pressure, an interrupted wedding, public repayment and a private reckoning. Subsequent confiscation, refund and hell accounts are later reports, memories or involved-actor narration. They qualify the outcome without supplying continuous access to every hidden step (AL28-02–13).

## 3. Wants and valuation

His repeated demand for Lalatina presents her as something he should own. The desire survives the son's unsuccessful interview, riskier artifact plans and a payment that should alter the debt relation. He also values class precedence, public reputation, gratifying sensation and cheap compliance. His own explanation of using Maxwell because payment can be evaded shows an economic preference embedded in the abuse, not merely a spontaneous temper (AL28-05, AL28-07–09).

Darkness reports a fixation dating to childhood. That report establishes the disturbing claimed duration; it is not an observed full history or permission to narrate unshown abuse. Her earlier comic excitement about an imagined captor does not establish her preference for this marriage. Her own wedding focalization explicitly rejects that reading (AL28-02, AL28-07).

## 4. Emotion and self-presentation

Public indignation relies on rank and insult; private frustration is displaced into repeated kicks. Both try to convert obstruction into another actor's fault. The hospitality sequence complicates a simple image of tireless control: guests exhaust his resources and patience, and his invitation becomes something he would like to retract. That cost is real even though his peeping proposal remains culpable (AL28-03–05).

The final private scene changes the distribution of fear. He initially treats Maxwell as a defective cheap instrument, then discovers an actor able to harm him after release. His panic, apology, bargaining and first prayer are real local responses to pain and expected continued torment. They are not demonstrated moral remorse. Conversely, his prior conduct does not make those sensations fictitious or the new harm harmless (AL28-09–10).

## 5. Competence and material limits

Alderp can mobilize a household, invite guests, press an accusation, collect money and issue orders. He understands that public statements before powerful witnesses can damage him and asks for a memory cover-up. He has actual access to the summoning stone and knowledge of a body-swap scheme. These are specific capacities rather than a general intelligence score (AL28-01, AL28-03–05, AL28-09).

Several options fail. The truth device disrupts his demand, a higher-ranked family secures delay, guests exploit hospitality, hidden-object searches do not restore his scheme, guards and adventurers decline inducements, and the church limits the devil's effective intervention. Kazuma produces the two billion Eris; Alderp collects coins and still attempts pursuit. Neither resources nor desire guarantees a corresponding outcome (AL28-01, AL28-04–10).

## 6. Appraisal, choice and error

He repeatedly treats an asserted entitlement as evidence that a route must be available. When a knock follows the order to bring Darkness, he assumes success despite Maxwell's explicit statement that nothing has been done. He then releases the contract and promises payment, making the desired explanation materially consequential. Vanir's disguise exploits this mistake; it is not a successful transport performed by Maxwell (AL28-09–10).

This supports a bounded account of wish-led appraisal and delegated correction. A rival explanation is a single exceptionally effective deception, while another is ordinary panic after a disastrous wedding. The earlier private blame of Maxwell, the persistence of plans despite setbacks and the immediate inference from a knock strengthen the broader hypothesis. They do not prove that every mundane judgment is equally impaired or that all earlier achievements were imaginary.

## 7. Relationships and asymmetry

The Alderp–Maxwell relation combines domination, unpaid work, incomplete understanding and the devil's own preference for cruel emotion. Alderp recognizes the usefulness of forgetting while misunderstanding his counterpart's rank and appetite. Maxwell is exploited and still actively likes cruelty; the later broken arms are Maxwell's act. Vanir has his own contract interests and openly acknowledges amusement. No member of this triangle is merely another's personality (AL28-05, AL28-09–10).

Balter's courtesy and refusal have their own reasons, even while Alderp imagines his body as a resource. Darkness's debt obligation is not erotic assent. Household servants who are threatened, exhausted or beaten bear costs distinct from their employer. The public rescue does not erase those costs, and a servant's obedience does not prove voluntary endorsement (AL28-02, AL28-04–08).

## 8. Ordinary life and preferences

Domestic evidence includes a residence, a maintained voyeuristic mirror, hosted meals and alcohol, possessions, a rebuilt-house claim and a capital villa claim. Mirror cleaning is actually observed; naked-maid viewing is not. Alderp's claim that harassment is included in wages does not establish a term accepted by the workers. The smashed mirror's breaker is unspecified (AL28-03–04).

These details identify a preferred arrangement of comfort, access and service. They do not establish all shopping, food or leisure tastes, enduring friendships, or a pleasant unobserved family life. Reports about past marriage proposals and widowhood must remain attributed. His private accounting is richer evidence than inventing sympathetic hobbies to complete a checklist.

## 9. Japanese voice and referential control

The printed voice combines repeated vocatives and possessive demands, class-marked insults and abrupt imperatives. Private MAIN06 uses ワシ; the final MAIN07 focalization also uses 私. A reconstruction should preserve situational register and the documented variation rather than mechanically normalize every sentence to one pronoun. No audio, accent or measured performance is available (AL28-05, AL28-09–10).

His designation of Maxwell as low-grade or broken is his judgment, contradicted by Vanir's identification. The final accusation about discarded girls belongs to Maxwell's speech, not a separately watched catalogue of victims. Clear subject assignment matters more than importing a stock lecherous-lord voice. Formulaic Japanese alone earns no L3-specific claim.

## 10. Comedy, coercion and narrative function

The hospitable host overrun by guests, coins spilling across a wedding and a wish apparently arriving at the door create reversals of status. They work because Alderp has concrete goals and misreads other people. They are not a license to make him omniscient beforehand so that the reversal looks cleverer (AL28-04, AL28-07–10).

The marriage threat, attempted coercive access, exploitation and actual violence retain seriousness. Kazuma's rescue includes intimidation and other actors' real costs; Vanir's punishment is not thereby necessary benevolent justice. MAIN08 records his admission that other resolutions existed. Comic mode can retain the reversals; serious transfer must keep responsibility and recipient constraints without importing magic that the new setting does not supply (AL28-08, AL28-11).

## 11. Contrary evidence and rival accounts

Pure omnipotence fails against the repeated limits and surprises. Pure stupidity fails against effective use of institutions, reputation management and opportunistic nonpayment. Purely ceremonial villainy fails against actual kicks, threats and deliberate exploitation. A purely self-made success account is challenged by the private dependence on Maxwell and Vanir's explanation, while a claim that Alderp never did anything himself would also exceed the evidence (AL28-01–10).

The useful interpretation is conditional: he uses available authority and external correction to pursue possession, and opposition can elicit blame, pressure or a more hazardous route. It does not predict identical escalation under every small inconvenience. Terminal bargaining occurs when his control collapses; whether it would yield durable correction in a survivable setting is unknown.

## 12. Outcome, uncertainty and reconstruction responsibility

The wedding ceremony is interrupted and Darkness leaves. Aqua later reports morning registration before the vows; the received scope lacks a registry document or annulment. A claim of no consummation/interrupted ceremony must therefore remain distinct from certainty that legal marriage never existed. Disappearance, confiscation and transfer are later reported; the refund is reported returned in MAIN08 after its earlier prospective state (AL28-11–12).

Tier3 is justified by differentiated own choices, private error, public authority, mundane costs and adverse cases. It authorizes a provisional conditional model, not a clinically complete person or validated general generator. Exact curse mechanics, every earlier institutional distortion, a full abuse history and the whole future hell treatment remain unresolved. Source-known exercises cannot turn those unknowns into facts or supply independent accuracy. The wider census, final battery and full program remain open.
