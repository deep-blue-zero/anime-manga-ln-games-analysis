# Soketto: selective foresight, personal boundaries and a preferred future

## 1. Identity, source position and scope

Soketto (そけっと), called Socket in accepted English reports, is the named village fortune-teller. The native name, shop role and recurring relation to Bukkorori support the alias join. She is a separate agent from the girls who observe her, the man who describes her routines and the officials who later interpret her warning. Neither someone else's confidence nor the narrator's access makes her omniscient.

This revisable Tier3 candidate receives a substantial monograph and a [distinct conditional model](../06%20Character%20Modeling%20and%20Reconstruction/KONOSUBA_SOKETTO_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md). Her own consultation, selective disclosure and conditional response to harm supply more than a list of traits. Identification remains provisional: ordinary professional pride, paternalistic advice, personal preference, defensive anger and narrative selection are serious rivals. Transfer to unfamiliar contexts is weakly supported; no independent prediction accuracy or capability grade is earned.

The [S-series evidence index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_ARUE_SOKETTO_BUKKORORI_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) owns exact locators. Its shared preparation covers 3,154 unique known BASE context slots/twenty members/five objects and three actual earlier image rechecks. ## 2. Chronology and changes in available knowledge

The forest encounter starts with other people seeking access to her. Bukkorori supplies extensive surveillance claims and recruits intermediaries. Soketto herself purchases a dragon-carved wooden sword and later uses it during training. The shopping preference and the fact of being followed belong to different actors; the purchase is no permission to observe her privately (S01).

Bukkorori's Tornado launches her into danger. She uses her own wind magic to land. Later she fights bears with Lightning Strike; when his Inferno includes her, her own water protection keeps the flames off. Useful damage to enemies does not make his intervention safe, and her survival does not erase the risk. The scene supplies her capacities independently of his skill-level boasts (S02–S03).

What Bukkorori reads as a promising mutual confession is a mismatch of premises. Soketto thinks he hates her and wants a duel after following and attacking her. His apology briefly changes her response; the girls' disclosure of his earlier intrusive motive prompts renewed attack. At the shop she offers one reading under a qualified rescue account. The crystal shows nothing. Her interpretation and attempt at comfort are not proof of permanent romantic exclusion (S04–S05).

The later invited consultation adds a previously unavailable limitation: she says matters involving herself cannot appear. Megumin connects it to Bukkorori's blank result. That is a later hypothesis, not knowledge available to him at the earlier reading or proof of a future marriage. The same consultation shows deliberate withholding to leave a future unchanged. Subsequent reports and the MAIN14 tourist encounter extend the account without converting every reported forecast into direct observation (S06–S10).

## 3. Goals, valuations and professional self-understanding

Soketto describes advising people away from unfortunate futures as her work. She also summons Megumin out of curiosity about what she will accomplish. Work, curiosity and a view of desirable outcomes coexist. She begins the reading willingly, tries favorable descriptions of the future companions, hesitates and then sees something that changes her expression. When Megumin requests the details, she decides that this future should remain unchanged and refuses to say more (S06).

The source does not identify every reason for that preference or prove a hidden material benefit to Soketto. It does establish that the information provider's judgment of the future can override the recipient's immediate request. A model of automatic truthful assistance, maximum disclosure or client autonomy would miss the actual choice. A merely greedy service provider would miss the curiosity, offered reading and limited free service.

Her personal safety and privacy also matter. She objects to stalking and intrusive attacks and uses force. Her later conditional account of Bukkorori emphasizes employment and changed conduct. These are her stated conditions for someone finding a partner, not an acceptance of a current romantic relationship or a commitment to wait for him (S04/S10).

## 4. Personality, affect and regulation

Soketto can be amused, exasperated, confident, angry and uncertain. Her composed shop presentation is not invulnerability. She reacts to a supposedly impossible blank result, considers a limitation in her later explanation and visibly changes during Megumin's reading. The record supports shifts tied to current information rather than a permanently serene oracle.

Her defensive response is substantial and sometimes violent. The bandaged Bukkorori has been beaten with her wooden sword. This is not softened into a harmless expression because the scene is comic. Nor does it prove that every future boundary will be enforced by the same means. Her grip relaxes after apology, then new information changes the situation again; the sequence matters (S04–S05).

A single pleasant expression is particularly easy to misread. Bukkorori and the girls supply romantic interpretations, while Soketto is operating with the belief that she faces hostility. Later amusement at Megumin's future likewise does not reveal her entire private valuation. The monograph keeps her own explanation separate from others' facial-reading theories.

## 5. Competence, procedures and access limits

Soketto performs wind-assisted landing, Lightning Strike and water protection in the forest. The first prevents a dangerous landing; the latter two show actual battle work and defense. Her ability to survive another person's bad intervention is her own achievement, not proof that his process was harmless or that she required that intervention (S02–S03).

The consultation is performed with a crystal, gesture and supplied magic. The displayed blank is observed. Statements about future changeability, likely partners and her own-involvement limitation are her explanations of the procedure. Those explanations constrain reconstruction but do not supply a measured accuracy rate. The exact workings of the purported borrowed power in later discussion are reported by others, not freshly witnessed here (S05–S07).

Bukkorori's printed monologues claim detailed routines, powerful training and a near-fifty level. They are actual text of his speech, with a disclosed interest in observing her. Her later spells support real competence but do not independently verify his timetable, every number or every inference about shared interests. The portrait image is not an additional block of testimony.

A prior report incorrectly says Socket treats the injuries. EXP01 U028 P427 explicitly assigns current treatment to Yunyun. The [bandaging erratum](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_SOKETTO_BANDAGING_ATTRIBUTION_ERRATUM_01.md) qualifies that report and the census's loose later-healing phrase. Shop location, actual bandaging, offered reading and healing magic are different things. No new healing spell is credited to either woman from that passage.

## 6. Decisions, inference and selective information

Soketto's decision process is visible at several different resolutions. In the forest she acts on danger; at the shop she makes a bounded concession under an account that includes attempted rescue. She does not thereby certify the whole account, forgive every prior act or offer unlimited future readings (S03–S05).

In the later consultation she first offers a broad service, then withholds particulars when she prefers the apparent future left in place. She also tells Megumin not to become short-tempered even if sexually harassed. That advice is adverse evidence for a universal recipient-protection rule. It does not authorize harassment or show that Megumin's objection is irrational. The outcome Soketto values can coexist with costs the recipient has reason to resist (S06).

Officials and acquaintances later use her reputation to draw a false conclusion about Zesta. The knight quotes a warning about an abnormality and the bath manager, while Yunyun reports delivering Soketto's letter. Zesta's subsequent release and the police apology show that their application was wrong. They do not show that Soketto herself accused him, nor settle every eventual target of the warning. Reputation, reported prediction, interpretation and actual culprit remain separate (S07).

## 7. Relationships, boundaries and unequal information

With Bukkorori, Soketto has been observed and attacked while he imagines a route to intimacy. She names the following and dangerous spells as reasons for her perceived hostility. A one-time service and later conditional positive appraisal do not backdate consent to surveillance. Her statement that his occasional presence near the shop is not presently troubling her likewise does not erase the earlier incidents (S04–S06/S10).

Megumin is both a younger acquaintance and an invited subject of curiosity. Soketto claims secrecy about a particular matter, offers a reading and then refuses requested details. Megumin grabs and shakes her during the exchange. The interest in a good future does not make that physical pressure acceptable; nor does the pressure justify inventing an all-purpose privacy contract or neutralizing Soketto's own withholding choice.

MAIN14 supplies another asymmetry. Soketto knowingly plays a tourist ritual; Aqua initially offers help sincerely. Kazuma knows the fiction and prolongs the situation, with beauty among his stated motives. Yunyun's intervention stops the teasing. Soketto's excuse about insufficient power is part of the staged escape effort, not a demonstrated failed cast or a confession of general magical weakness. Aqua does not automatically receive Kazuma's prior knowledge (S08).

## 8. Ordinary life, work and resources

The shop is a place where Soketto receives people and undertakes readings. A fee matters to Bukkorori's earlier plan, but the record does not establish that every service is paid or that every consultation is solicited by a client. She offers one free reading and later initiates another for her own curiosity. This is a discretionary practice, with incomplete financial detail.

The wooden sword shows an ordinary preference expressed through a particular purchase and training use. It does not establish that every cute object will override her goals. Bukkorori's long account of meals, washing, walks and daily hours remains his surveillance narrative. None of it becomes a neutral biography merely by appearing in an illustration's printed dialogue (S01).

No full household budget, daily diary, family history or private romantic commitment is available. The later report of repeated consultation attempts and training attacks comes from Bukkorori, a participant with his own stake. It supplies a return and an unresolved relationship condition, not a newly observed schedule or a diagnosed state of mind (S09–S10).

## 9. Japanese register, address and pragmatic force

Soketto uses conversational 「私」 and direct second-person address in consultation and dispute. 「まあ、一回だけね？」 marks a bounded concession, while 「未来は変えられるもの」 introduces her stated professional rationale. Her later decision to say nothing changes the pragmatic force of that offer; a smooth English paraphrase should not silently turn it into full disclosure (S05–S06).

Questions, exasperation and amused laughter coexist with confident explanation. Her surprise at the blank is different from Bukkorori's statement that nothing is visible. Attributing every line near the crystal to the fortune-teller would erase the participants and misdescribe her confidence.

These are textual observations, without audio or native-human assessment. English hypothetical behavior does not validate Japanese voice. Neither a generic mystical register nor a permanently flirtatious tone represents all these contexts. Shared village style and interlocutor-specific responses remain relevant alternatives to a uniquely personal verbal algorithm.

## 10. Comedy, seriousness and harmful means

The misunderstood confession works because the participants assign the same conversation different premises. Bukkorori anticipates romantic progress; Soketto anticipates a duel. The later blank and Megumin's marriage hypothesis extend the joke through unequal knowledge. None of those devices proves actual mutual romance (S04–S06).

The forest sequence also contains real danger: unwanted observation, a fall, fire and a protective shield. Her counterattack and the injured man's treatment remain consequential acts. A successful escape or defeated bear does not transform the entire process into safe cooperation. The free reading is a later concession with limits, not complete repair.

Tourist fiction and reported divination-based embarrassment likewise need typed access. A fictional ritual can involve sincere unwanted assistance and knowing teasing. A report about an embarrassing oracle outcome is not a new direct demonstration of perfect mind access. Humor does not make the recipients' refusals or knowledge differences disappear (S08–S09).

## 11. Counterevidence and serious rival accounts

A perfect truth oracle fails on finite display/access, admitted limits and selective non-disclosure. A neutral service professional fails on her personal choice to preserve a future despite the client's request. A universal privacy or anti-harassment protector fails on the advice to endure future harassment and the limits of what is actually promised. A helpless pursued woman fails on her independent effective spells and vigorous boundary action.

A wholly hostile view of Bukkorori fails on limited free service and her later conditional appraisal. A secret guaranteed romance fails because the inference belongs to Megumin, the rule is disclosed later and Soketto does not confirm that inference. A current healer profile fails at the precise treatment scene: Yunyun is the actor. Each contrary point narrows a claim rather than erasing the rest of the person.

Generic professional confidence, uncertainty management, paternalism, self-protection and personal preference explain much of this. The conditional model must keep those rivals viable and state weak transfer boundaries. Reproducing a model-consistent hypothetical does not distinguish these explanations or measure future prediction quality.

## 12. Current interpretation and open questions

Soketto is an information provider with her own interests and an effective actor beyond that occupation. She initiates, performs, qualifies and withholds readings; she defends herself and can offer bounded service after conflict. Her preferred outcome does not automatically match a recipient's request or comfort. These linked distinctions support a provisional three-rule reconstruction candidate alongside the descriptive monograph.

Open questions include how she chooses among competing clients' interests, what changes her preferred future, reliability outside the stated access limits, sustained responses to Bukkorori's changed or unchanged conduct, and her choices away from the village.
