## 2. Chronology and changing access

The school setting first supplies ordinary social talk. Dodonko asks Yunyun what sort of previous-life partner she had, with an audible self-correction from preferred type to the group's fiction. Yunyun describes a quiet listener. The following two dismissive judgments are not individually tagged, so the exact phrasing cannot be divided mechanically between Dodonko and Funifura (D01).

The group later pressures Yunyun toward the rabbit task. Megumin puts the knife into Dodonko's hand and constrains her body. Dodonko explicitly asks her to stop. A conditional prospect of the blade entering is not a completed rabbit killing by Dodonko. Funifura's objection and their flight belong to the same coercive sequence, not an invariant in which one character always rescues and another always exploits (D02–D03).

The family-money dispute is built through different reports. Yunyun says that Dodonko proposed a contribution for Funifura's brother; this is not a direct demand heard by Megumin. Dodonko later stresses urgency and directly insists that the brother really is ill. Megumin's hostile inference and threat remain separate from that testimony. Later medicine-work testimony and Funifura's denial of an invented illness qualify the earlier suspicion without independently establishing treatment success (D05–D06/D08).

The farewell, later village reunion and escort supply additional behavior and claims. Dodonko is present for a real send-off. MAIN11 gives her an actual escort context, a claim to upper-magic qualification and an explicit challenge to Megumin's intimate-story implications. These are not freshly witnessed village battle or cast sequences. Dust and Yorimichi add intended or reported correspondence rather than direct new Dodonko performances (D09–D13).

## 3. Goals and valuations

Dodonko's reported meeting with the sword hero and wish to join when her magic is ready express an aspiration. Neither the meeting account nor the intention is completed recruitment. Attraction, admiration, adventure and concern with readiness are possible ingredients; the scene does not rank them or prove that every later choice serves a romantic objective (D04).

She also participates in peer belonging and comparison. Asking Yunyun about a partner includes her in conversation while placing her under a social frame. Later disbelief about Yunyun's friends and insistence on getting Megumin's real story show competitive interest. They do not justify treating every question as a fully planned campaign to control both girls.

Dodonko's defense of the brother's illness may express loyalty to Funifura, knowledge of a genuine problem, resistance to Megumin's accusation, or several at once. It should not be explained away solely because it is stammered under threat. Nor does taking her testimony seriously license the pair's guilt pressure or prove that Yunyun freely understood every financial obligation (D05–D06).

## 4. Affect, fear and persistence

The forced-knife scene establishes a local refusal and fear under physical constraint. Dodonko's stop requests are not evidence of incapacity to care, general cowardice or a standing policy against violence. She had participated in pressure on Yunyun before becoming the person burdened with the task. The transfer changes who is resisting; it does not erase either person's agency or earlier conduct (D03).

Her persistence in defending the illness is more specific than a generic description of timidness. She continues speaking while Megumin is visibly angry. By contrast, the pair returns funds under intimidation, and their later protests during Megumin's retaliation do not turn the encounter into consensual banter. A single global label such as timid or assertive loses these changes in who holds power and what is at stake (D06).

In MAIN11 Dodonko's self-persuasion and renewed questioning follow Funifura's defensive response to Yunyun's friendships. She then supplies her own skepticism about Megumin's claims. Group momentum is a live explanation, but the tagged turn still belongs to Dodonko. A shared emotional scene is not evidence of identical private reasoning (D11).

## 5. Competence and practical contribution

Dodonko's own farewell statement reports medicine work. The report matters because it complicates the claim that the need was wholly fabricated. It does not show every ingredient, dose, labor step or medical outcome in front of the reader. Funifura's father's reported staff manufacture is not Dodonko's craft skill, and the material collection described by other speakers should retain its access limits (D08/I01–I02).

MAIN11 directly shows the pair bringing Komekko to the house. Their stated purpose, village conflict and future tasks are reports attached to a performed escort. Dodonko says that both have learned upper magic and have been called to participate. Qualification, being summoned, arriving, being ready to cast, actually casting and winning are distinct states. None of the missing stages should be supplied simply because later village news is favorable (D11).

At school the imposing weapons are props, while Arue actually strikes the lizard. Teacher staging and eventual rescue are separate acts. Dodonko's proximity does not give her either feat. The record therefore supports practical participation and reported learning without inventing a full combat profile from ensemble scenes (C01).

## 6. Decisions, appraisal and error

Dodonko can ask, revise a conversational formulation, press urgency, defend a contested fact, refuse an imposed physical task and question an implausible story. These choices are sufficiently varied for sustained interpretation. The sources provide less access to how she chooses between her own needs and a friend's when both alternatives are costly.

The money sequence especially resists a single label. Early speakers use different contribution and loan language. Funds are actually offered back under Megumin's pressure and later handed to Yunyun by Megumin. The windows do not count a precise total or verify all settlement. The later illness denial neither proves the early arrangement transparent nor proves the family concern fictitious (D05–D06/D08).

In MAIN11 Dodonko tests the implications of Megumin's earlier story rather than merely accepting the first display forever. That is a local epistemic contribution. Megumin then stages another intimate-looking response, and the pair reacts and leaves. Dodonko has not been given privileged access to all relevant events. Her mistaken or incomplete belief need not mean low intelligence, and her skepticism is not universal resistance to manipulation (D11).

## 7. Relationships and asymmetry

Funifura is a recurring partner whose brother Dodonko defends. Funifura's protest when Megumin forces Dodonko to hold the knife supplies a specific reciprocal action. This supports a relation more differentiated than two interchangeable bullies. It does not show every private exchange, equal influence or an exhaustive account of their loyalty (D03/D06).

With Yunyun, inclusion and pressure coexist. Dodonko's conversational question, donation suggestion as Yunyun reports it, later reassurance and presence at the send-off can matter to Yunyun. Yunyun also refuses the rabbit task, values company and later notices that letters focus on Megumin. Her recipient perspective should not be reduced to universal gullibility or treated as a complete account of Dodonko's intentions (D01–D02/D05/D08–D09/D13).

With Megumin, ridicule, rivalry, gifts and physical coercion form an uneven relationship. Dodonko is responsible for her own pressures; she is also the recipient of Megumin's forced knife and later assault. A protagonist's protective goal does not make those acts harmless. Conversely, Megumin's later counterexample about her own restaurant conduct does not cancel the peers' earlier antagonism (D03/D07/C02).

## 8. Ordinary life and aspirations

Conversation over lunch, shared walks, correspondence and a farewell party are substantial ordinary-life evidence. Dodonko's own question about a partner is not proof that the invented previous life literally occurred. Her reported sword-hero encounter and stated wish to join him are possibilities and aspirations, not an established relationship or completed career change (D01/D04).

The restaurant scene contains teasing, demands and personal taste, but several turns about a recommendation and a different order are untagged. They should not become Dodonko's stable favorite food. Megumin's thought of exploiting Dodonko's reported timidity is an intended retaliation, not the performance of that particular plan. Other customers restrain Megumin's attempted casting; she then actually forces hot food into both classmates' mouths, and they leave crying. Those acts are distinct from her earlier plans and from a completed Explosion (D07).

At the send-off the classmates actually wait and smile. This shows a willingness to spend time on a departure, without showing that every contribution was equally hers. A shared promise of another birthday gathering is a future commitment, not an event already completed. No detailed budget, household routine or stable private pastime can be reconstructed from the available context (D08–D09).

## 10. Comedy, seriousness and narrative access

The class's invented romantic histories, grand poses and intrusive curiosity carry humor, but the knife is physically imposed and Dodonko asks it to stop. The text's threatened injury is conditional rather than a completed killing. Neither treating every gag as nonliteral nor turning the conditional outcome into actual injury preserves the scene (D03).

Megumin narrates much of the formative material and has her own appetite, rivalry and protective self-image. Kazuma later contributes a different view, while Dust's relevant occurrence is Yunyun's plan to write. Neither a repeated allegation in ledgers nor the presence of another narrator creates an independent trial of Dodonko's hidden psychology (D10–D13).

## 11. Counterevidence and explanatory rivals

A pure predator account is weakened by the defended illness, later medicine testimony, contribution to shared occasions and reciprocal objection when Dodonko is constrained. A wholly benevolent friend account is weakened by pressure on Yunyun, guilt language and intrusive questioning. A fixed timidity account is weakened by her persistence in defending the illness and challenging Megumin, while a global assertiveness account fails at her changing position under force.

Ordinary peer behavior under different stakes remains a strong rival to a distinct portable reconstruction. Much dialogue is collective, practical feats are reported, and the sources seldom display her privately choosing among costly alternatives. These limits justify leaving modeling open without diminishing the descriptive value of her own monograph.
