# Bounded profiles: Keele's beloved, the Winter Shogun and Prince Jatice

These three profiles have different access limits. They do not share a generative model or a final person-denominator claim. Native paragraph/ruby contexts were received in cohort47; source-known status, historical records and the wider program's unfinished work remain explicit.

## Keele's unnamed beloved noblewoman

The woman is a distinct subject whose carefully arranged remains Kazuma sees on the bed MAIN02 U015306. The public history52-70 and Keele's subsequent account291-305 place her in a noble family and at court. Kazuma summarizes her parents' political offering as a royal mistress, neglect, and conflict with the king's other women303; Keele agrees305. Neither exchange presents a witnessed complete court life or supplies her personal name, exact age, diagnosis or relatives' names.

Keele says she accepted his proposal immediately305. He also says she smiled without complaint during the flight327, while the narrative reports her eventual death in the dungeon326. These are substantive attributions of a choice and a response under constrained conditions. Her own speech, private deliberation and assessment of alternatives remain unavailable. The account should retain attributed consent without making Keele's affection a substitute for her perspective.

His question about whether he made her happy328 remains unresolved by his confidence elsewhere. Aqua reports that she has passed peacefully without regrets321; Darkness later asserts that the journey must have been happy400-401. Both are identifiable speakers, and Darkness's confidence is not the woman's testimony. The disappearance of the remains after Aqua's rite341 is observed. A reunited couple, a completed reincarnation or an originally trapped soul is not observed; Aqua has already said that she was at peace.

The relationship gives the woman a place in the story beyond a generic corpse or anonymous reward. It still cannot supply an independent Japanese voice, a stable ordinary-life preference, battlefield competence or a universal adventurous temperament. Smiling through hardship might reflect enjoyment, support, composure, limited access in the retelling, or the lover's retrospective framing. The analyst cannot choose among those explanations by inventing a monologue.

A Tier1 historical profile is earned by the connected political, relational and material evidence. A dedicated dense monograph or generative model would currently require more inference than the access supports. This is a scoped evidential decision, without an anonymity or deceased-character ceiling. She is not Anna, Eris, a known named queen, or every other noblewoman in the corpus.

## The Winter Shogun in the MAIN02 encounter

The white-armored swordsman appears abruptly MAIN02 U014144-152. Aqua calls him the snow sprites' lord157 and a winter spirit178, explaining form through thought; Kazuma infers an earlier Japanese visitor's image182. The origin explanation is attributed. No original namer or image-maker is identified, and a title need not be a personal name or proof that every depiction represents the same individual.

His own physical contribution is clear: he advances, attacks the nearest party member Darkness172, breaks her sword175, and uses distinct sword stances171/215. Aqua begins freeing captured sprites187 and prostrates189. He turns from her193 to the others194. Kazuma forces Darkness's head down201/204, and the swordsman sheathes205 while Kazuma still holds his sword209. Only afterward does Kazuma drop it210 and inadvertently raise his head211; the draw, strike and resheathing follow213-219. Kazuma subsequently knows that he has been killed224.

The precise order limits the explanation. Continued weapon possession alone does not explain the entire sequence; the sheath already occurred. Surrender posture, motion, gaze and weapon handling vary together. The source gives no direct Winter speech or private judgment of remorse, and no controlled single-factor test. Eris reports that he seems to have disappeared after the strike234; Kazuma does not directly witness his departure or defeat. Aqua still keeps one captured sprite346-351, so a claim that all four were released would also overstate the encounter.

Megumin reports a roughly estimated bounty, usual noninterference when sprites are undisturbed338, and difficulty killing a spirit king with one Explosion343. None is a performed immunity experiment. Kazuma's actual death and subsequent recovery restrictions324 remain material consequences, despite the comic frame. Darkness's replacement sword is still pending U01534. The consequences do not reveal a full emotional personality for their author.

The recurrence audit distinguishes actual encounter from a samurai plush MAIN03 U017484/499, origami MAIN06 U014516-518, a newspaper comic MAIN09 U013288/397, household-ice jokes MAIN05 U023420-422/MAIN08 U01312-16, and later memories. YORI04 U012206 reports a ski facility closed because sprites created a risk of his arrival; it does not witness an attack. YORI02 U020351 repeats a heard durability comparison with a different storm spirit. The artificial Winter Hatamoto in the Advanced replay remains a separate creature and record.

A Tier1 encounter profile is warranted by observable tactical agency and consequential reactions. No model of universal surrender law, speech, benevolence, wrath, everyday preference or all winter spirits is added. That limit concerns the present evidence, rather than nonhuman status or the number of books in which the title appears.

## Prince Jatice as a public and relational figure

The printed name is ジャティス. Aldarp names him first prince, fighting with the king at the front, MAIN06 U013486/488. He proposes Jatice as a match for Darkness and discusses succession486. The prince is not present, and no reply or assent is shown. Claire separately says that the body-exchange necklace was tribute originally intended for him and refuses unilateral disposal before his return U015567. Her opinion that short exchanges are not dangerous is challenged by Chris's later explanation608/613, rather than validated by the prince's supposed consent.

Aldarp's private interlude U02023-27 supplies a different motive for his matchmaking: taking the prince's body after marriage to obtain both status and Lalatina. He regrets the artifact's theft and sealing12/26. This is fictional Aldarp focalization, explicitly corrected from a mistaken preparation label of author paratext. It reveals the planner's intention, not Jatice's own desire, a performed takeover, a completed engagement or a death. Balter, an alternative intended body in Aldarp's regrets29-32, remains separate.

MAIN10 U014624-628 has bystanders and the minister mistake Kazuma, calling himself Iris's brother, for Jatice. Levi later asks whether he is not Jatice U016744, and Kazuma supplies his own name745. These are explicit mistaken-identity contexts. They do not give Jatice the impostor's dialogue or join the Belzerg prince to Elroad's Levi. Speculation about appearance and heroic ancestry627 belongs to the minister.

Other references develop public desirability. A child's letter MAIN11 U012230 uses Jatice as a comparison ideal. Lean reports his handsome frontline reputation DUST03 U01326, while Claire admits originally wanting his educational appointment23/518-524. The projected memory in that later scene shows Claire's past response to young Iris, not the prince performing a remembered act. Luna imagines Vanir impersonating Jatice and speaking a marriage line MASK U017529. Her requested line is not Jatice's own proposal, and the inspected scene does not perform the transformation.

These relationships justify a Tier1 public-role profile beyond a bare name index. They also mark the limit of reconstruction. Attributed strength, royal value, admiration, political exploitation and a target's vulnerability are meaningful literary functions, but none supplies his own affect transitions, Japanese voice, motives, preferences or choice under pressure in the inspected passages. A generic handsome warrior-prince model would mostly reproduce other people's projections. The profile therefore retains the named individual and the differentiated reports without fabricating an autonomous model or asserting absence from every unread passage.
