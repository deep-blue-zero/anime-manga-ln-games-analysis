# Arue: authored performance, practical participation and the cost of a story

## 1. Identity and analytical scope

Arue (あるえ) is Megumin and Yunyun's named Crimson Demon classmate and aspiring, later occupational writer. She is not Nerimaki: MAIN14 C3 directly names Arue in the publication, book and pay exchange, whereas Nerimaki identifies herself in the liquor-store encounter. The rhetorical 「あるえー？」 in MAIN17 F P33 is Kazuma's interjection, not this person. A name search supplies a lead, not an appearance (A01/A10–A13).

This revisable Tier2 monograph joins school, practical work, gifts, fiction and later village encounters. Her own choices sustain a substantial account without requiring a separate reconstruction model. A cultural taste for theatrical presentation, an ordinary writer's investment in work and status, and Megumin's interested narration explain much of the pattern. Private reasoning and choices outside these relationships remain thinner. Future modeling stays open; Tier2 is no fixed ceiling.

The [individual A-series index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_ARUE_SOKETTO_BUKKORORI_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) owns exact source access. The shared preparation comprises 3,154 unique known BASE context slots in twenty members/five local objects and three earlier actual image rechecks. It includes other actors, blanks and a false-positive search window. It is neither an Arue observation count nor a new whole-source, ruby, admission or independent-validation pass.

## 2. Chronology and evidence that changes the account

School first establishes a student rewarded within a peculiar institution. Arue is third in one test and second in a later one; these local rankings are not permanent measures of intelligence or every ability. Her grammar answer addresses bad names and effective battle introductions. The teacher rewards it. Her eye-patch presentation likewise fits the teacher's taste. Actual success at that rubric coexists with the rubric's flaws (A01).

Her invitation to pair with Megumin is real. Megumin accepts and then invents an illness and leaves; no completed pair exercise follows. In another context Arue accepts a refereeing role, removes her patch and starts the contest before Yunyun finishes her request. She supplies a fictional thirty-second death rule and declares Megumin the winner. Those are her actions, without a performed death or proof that she is an impartial adjudicator (A02/A04).

The field lesson supplies a useful correction. Arue lifts a large sword theatrically, but Yunyun exposes the weapons as metal-coated wooden props. Arue later actually strikes the head of a lizard immobilized in ice. It stops moving; she looks at her card and appears to have gained a level. The scene does not show a card number, decapitation or a new strength-enhancement feat. She subsequently notices a dangerous unfrozen creature, warns the group, runs and discards the cumbersome prop. Yunyun shields her and Megumin. A real warning, reliance on another pupil and a practical change of equipment belong together (A05–A06).

Recipe preparation later puts her alongside Megumin in ordinary work. Arue catches an escaping ingredient, accepts the request for help, asks which part is needed and removes/cuts leeks. At the farewell she states her writer ambition, gives a patch, invents an impressive protective explanation and then retracts its magical properties. Much later, a manuscript and publication dispute show that writing has become consequential work and a defended occupational identity (A07/A09–A12).

## 3. Motivations and valuations

Arue explicitly says she wants to become a writer and produce something that pleases someone. She asks Megumin for adventure stories and imagines writing the future party's tale. This is her stated goal, not a guarantee about what she will write or how readers will respond. The patch's supposed magical history makes a small gift an impressive narrative; her subsequent explanation treats invention as part of the writer's craft (A09).

The record supplies material investment too. She offers a newly completed chapter; after Kazuma tears it she calls it the product of a week of sleepless work and collapses holding the pages. The week is her report, while the destruction and distress are present action. Neither her proud evaluation of the chapter nor Kazuma's contempt establishes its literary quality. Her loss is real within the scene without requiring a flattering judgment of her art (A11).

Later she asserts that newspaper/book publication brings pay when Megumin attacks her as idle. Income, authorship and dignity matter in that exchange. It does not establish a complete income history or the amount and regularity of payment. Her selection of stories, performance and audience response may connect these scenes; ordinary occupational pride and local genre conventions remain strong competing explanations (A12).

## 4. Personality, affect and regulation

Arue often appears composed, playful and ready with a constructed explanation. That appearance is not emotional invulnerability. The torn manuscript produces a response Megumin says she has not seen before. The later defense of her profession and remembered destruction likewise show irritation and grievance. Calm presentation changes with what is at stake (A09/A11–A12).

Her outward theatricality can coexist with cooperation. She helps with ingredients and notices danger; she can also start a contest prematurely, pull Megumin's patch despite a stop request and seize Kazuma during an argument. Those acts qualify a uniformly considerate-friend interpretation. Conversely, one invasive act does not establish that all her help is manipulation or all her exchanges disregard permission.

Megumin's comments about Arue's body, mysteriousness and apparent composure are the narrator's judgments. They do not disclose Arue's concealed motives. The monograph therefore retains behavior and the narrator's appraisal as separate records rather than converting an amused description into a complete personality explanation.

## 5. Competence, practice and dependence

Arue has observed classroom success and actual practical participation. The lizard strike is a finishing action against an already immobilized animal. The preparatory freezing belongs to the teacher context, not Arue. Large props do not demonstrate extraordinary lifting capacity, and her later card glance does not show that she successfully acquired or cast a spell during danger (A01/A05–A06).

Her recipe assistance is directly attributable and task-specific. It does not establish independently verified medical efficacy or make every participant a clinician. Megumin's potion production, Arue's ingredient work and the farewell's apparent-result testimony remain distinct; the [medicine erratum](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_VILLAGE_PEERS_MEDICINE_ATTRIBUTION_ERRATUM_01.md) preserves that qualification (A07/A09).

The farewell reports upper-magic learning/graduation, while the later writing exchange supports occupational activity. A report of a qualification is not a newly watched personal battle feat. Arue's stories and displayed manuscripts support authorship as a serious undertaking, but no directly observed writing process, independent sales record or complete assessment of technique is available here.

## 6. Decisions, appraisal and error

Arue can tailor an answer to an audience: classroom style earns approval, a ceremonial gift explanation impresses the other girls, and a new chapter is presented as an achievement. This is an interpretive connection across actions, not direct access to one stable hidden calculation. She also changes means under practical pressure, abandoning the oversized prop when running becomes necessary (A01/A05–A06/A09).

Her card glance during the monster encounter is observed. Megumin's suggestion that she is checking whether she can learn powerful magic is an inference. The teacher ultimately performs the saving spell after an admitted delay for style. Neither that success nor Arue's interest in options licenses a claim that she controlled the rescue or always chooses efficiently.

The patch story contains an explicit correction: it has none of the magical effects she just described. Earlier actual removal during refereeing also conflicts with later absolute language about never removing it. Possible exaggeration, memory failure or continuity error should remain visible. A perfectly consistent secret-eye mechanism would conceal the contradiction (A04/A09).

## 7. Differentiated relationships and agency

With Megumin, Arue offers company, cooperation, a personal object and an audience for future adventures. Megumin accepts some help and objects to other conduct. In the farewell, Megumin throws the offered old patch and takes the new one; this is not Arue voluntarily presenting both or consenting to an upgrade. The final compliment and Megumin's promise to value the patch retain ordinary friendship after that conflict (A02/A07/A09/A13).

With Yunyun, Arue's competitive reassurance is corrected by Yunyun's own account. Megumin then says Yunyun intentionally lowered a ranking to align graduation. The exchange limits any universal superiority claim. During the field danger Yunyun actually stands in front of Arue and Megumin; her protection is her own choice, not a capability supplied by Arue's presence (A06/A08).

Kazuma first encounters Arue through fiction read by other people. The author signature and postage explanation matter: readers mistake the enclosure for a future account, but deliberate deception by its absent writer is not established. Later he destroys her manuscript. Her outrage is not evidence that his romantic disappointment justified the act. MAIN14 adds her own intrusive physical conduct and selective welcome to Darkness; friendliness toward one recipient is not a general policy toward everyone (A10–A12).

## 8. Ordinary life, resources and aspiration

Arue's ordinary interests include stories, distinctive clothing and the people and incidents that might become material. The childhood patch is, in her corrected account, a fashion object bought by her grandfather. The reported grandfather remains a separate report-bound person, not a newly witnessed magical benefactor (I03).

The cat's acceptance of her finger and her proposed Norisuke name show a small interaction and naming preference in that moment. They do not give her ownership of Chomusuke, privileged knowledge of Wolbach or a permanent cross-context bond. Likewise, recipe help supplies actual labor without a complete domestic skill inventory (A03/A07).

The manuscript's reported time cost and later pay defense make work and resources visible beyond a generic enigmatic classmate. No complete household budget, comfortable income, favorite food or lifelong schedule is supplied. An aspiration to please readers coexists with using recognizable acquaintances in fiction; the source does not establish a comprehensive privacy ethic.

## 9. Japanese language and pragmatic shifts

Arue's self-reference includes 「私」. Her 「なんだい」 and address to the 「外の人」 in the manuscript encounter are conversational, while introductions, answers and gift explanations can become deliberately elevated. The shift is part of what she does with an audience, not proof that ordinary speech is unavailable to her (A01/A09/A11–A12).

The later occupational defense uses writing, publication and pay as practical answers to an insult. The earlier grand language about the patch is explicitly retracted. English should preserve the difference between a confident invented explanation, a literal work claim, a request and an objection. Rendering every turn as mystical solemnity would flatten both the joke and the grievance.

These are textual Japanese observations from the admitted native passages. No audio performance, native-human adequacy judgment, universal catchphrase generator or independently assessed Japanese simulation is claimed. Shared Crimson Demon conventions remain a major rival to treating one elevated line as uniquely Arue's mechanism.

## 10. Comedy, seriousness and consequences

The fictional death rule, impressive prop and exaggerated patch history produce comedy through performance and mistaken expectations. They do not make a literal death, magical object or superstrength real. Conversely, the comic setting does not erase Kazuma's manuscript destruction, her distress or the ignored stop request during the later patch argument (A04–A05/A09/A11–A12).

Her authored enclosure creates consequences because readers treat a story as an account of their future. The source then reveals its author and packaging. The distinction among fiction, delivery, reader interpretation and author intention is analytically more useful than treating the whole episode as either true prophecy or a proved malicious lie.

Real work and stage conventions repeatedly coexist. The teacher's dangerous rescue delay and the group's escape are different acts; the student's classroom compliance is not consent to every hazard. Humor classification cannot supply an ethical exemption or turn a warm farewell into complete retrospective repair.

## 11. Contradictions, rivals and adverse evidence

A pure poseur model fails on ingredient assistance, practical warning, abandonment of an awkward prop and costly attachment to written work. A uniformly kind creative-friend model fails on the early start/rule invention, invasive patch play and willingness to use acquaintances as story material without a demonstrated general consent policy. A permanently unreadable or invulnerable personality fails on visible distress (A04–A07/A10–A12).

The claim of never removing the patch should not be harmonized with the earlier explicit removal by inventing a hidden condition. The magical explanation should not survive its explicit retraction. Kazuma's charge that she stayed home writing through the entire battle remains his accusation, rather than an independently observed schedule. The apparent level gain and declared income keep their original access limits.

Cultural display, ordinary artistic pride, local competition, friendship and narrator selection remain serious rivals. They can explain the pattern without a uniquely identified portable decision procedure. Their persistence is a reason to retain a substantial monograph and leave modeling open, rather than assigning every gesture to one elegant psychological rule.

## 12. Current interpretation and open questions

Arue is an active participant who performs for audiences, does some practical work, invests in writing, values her artifacts and reacts when they are attacked. Her cooperation and her intrusive conduct belong to the same individual. The account supports a revisable Tier2 monograph across school, work, gifts, fiction and grievance; it does not currently isolate a distinctive transferable generator from generic writer and cultural rivals.

Open questions include private choices away from familiar classmates, how she responds to sustained rejection by readers, actual financial constraints, deliberate revision of harmful story use and behavior where performance conflicts with an important obligation. These are research questions, not unobserved facts. No future source forecast, hypothetical first body or accuracy result is assigned to Arue here.

The monograph adds interpretation without revising frozen reports, models, scores or earlier responses. The source-known cohort remains part of an unfinished individual census and full KonoSuba program. Proportionate scope can change if later evidence supplies the missing distinctions.
