## 1. The expanded unit is a communicative act with an owner

The [main-only language synthesis](KONOSUBA_JAPANESE_LANGUAGE_AND_COMEDY_SYNTHESIS.md) treats voice as a situated repertoire rather than a phrase bank. Expanded fiction and mixed formats make correct ownership even more important. For each consequential claim, the useful order is source identity and component, actor/speaker, addressee or referent, represented channel, received information and local purpose, then actual form and pragmatic interpretation. That order prevents a polite ending from proving the role it is supposed to explain. It also prevents the reader's later knowledge from becoming the speaker's earlier premise.
