# Megumin across the expanded corpus: a chosen art, contribution and the people who bear its costs

## 1. The prequel supplies an agent before it supplies a weapon

The [main-only monograph](KONOSUBA_MEGUMIN_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) treats Explosion as an informed aesthetic choice that competes with belonging, contribution, ordinary costs and attachment. The expanded books make that competition visible before the main-party arrangement exists. Megumin is an academically strong pupil with selective attention, limited ordinary resources, practical knowledge, social perceptiveness and uneven inhibition. Her art is a value before it becomes a usable spell. The material does not support a choice between an ignorant obsessive and a secretly safe genius.

The in-world earlier setting does not make this a new prospective derivation. These books were read after main-series and holdout exposure, under the declared expanded-source gates. Their new observations can qualify the current account but cannot become what the main-only model knew before its tests. The [unchanged-R1 reconciliation](../04%20Checkpoints%20and%20Model%20Validation/KONOSUBA_WITHHELD_SOURCE_VALIDATION.md#four-book-unchanged-r1-reconciliation-and-stage-gate) and original outcomes remain exact. Formal holdout missingness is also preserved: fourteen contracts were untested across all four books, including several Megumin branches. A later source-known fit is not a recovered old success.

The expanded interpretation is that Megumin wants her chosen art, a consequential place with particular people, recognition of contribution and opportunities for interesting performance. None always dominates. Her knowledge of a cost can produce restraint, a search for accommodation, an attempted sacrifice of the art or a knowingly harmful continuation. The useful explanation identifies the appraisal she owns at the moment, the actual means and the other people whose choices make the outcome possible.

## 2. Willingness to change precedes the fortunate discovery

EXP01's final conflict offers stronger evidence of reconsideration than an ending in which Explosion conveniently solves everything. Megumin accepts a sister-protection burden and prepares to buy advanced magic while trembling. Yunyun independently learns intermediate magic, defeats the immediate enemy, protects the cat and sends the sisters toward adults. Megumin recognizes that sacrifice and returns; Komekko refuses a proposed solo exit, requiring another protective adjustment. No advanced purchase has yet occurred. (EXP01 U043P0183–0284; [reading](../10%20Expanded%20Corpus/Explosions/KONOSUBA_EXP_V01_DEEP_READING.md), §3.)

Her renewed consideration later is also prior to the favorable card discovery. Yunyun is initially winning without her, additional enemies and depletion change the situation, an actual safe-return promise is invoked, and Megumin again prepares an available alternative. Only then does the card reveal sufficient points for Explosion from the earlier wild duck. (U043P0288–0441.) The discovery rescues the dream; it does not create the willingness to lose it. Nor does that willingness erase her earlier refusal under a danger plea.

The first cast remains operationally imperfect. She gives a get-low warning; Yunyun supplies the child's actual shielding. The blast succeeds at its target, fells trees and exhausts the caster, while adults later provide transport and reprimand. Those later provisions were not an already secured recovery plan. Another seal is subsequently reported broken. Her stated intention to value companions is meaningful, but not a demonstrated permanent policy. The near-village restraint that follows is an actual local choice under Yunyun's boundary, rather than evidence that every later site will be safe. (U043P0445–0614.)

This sequence sharpens the later MAIN05 dilemma while preserving both histories. In MAIN05 Megumin's advanced-magic request is real, and Kazuma allocates against her expressed intention. Neither the prequel's fortunate outcome nor her later pleasure turns his override into prior joint consent. Contribution can threaten a valued identity without making that conflict an illusion to be solved by someone else deciding what she really wants.

## 3. Affection does not reliably inhibit known lethal risk

EXP02 provides severe contrary evidence to a broad affection brake. After the fake-mage/status taunts, Megumin throws the cat toward Arnes and begins Explosion. Yunyun explicitly warns that the cat is with the target. Megumin accepts prospective death, invokes a supposed familiar's duty, imagines a grave and says she cannot retreat after the insult. The first sacrifice choice is in motion before Arnes later charges the fireball and Megumin adds a mass-danger argument. Later urgency therefore cannot become the original sole motive. (EXP02 U044, chapter five; [reading](../10%20Expanded%20Corpus/Explosions/KONOSUBA_EXP_V02_DEEP_READING.md), §3.)

No actual familiar agreement, pet assent, harmlessness knowledge or revival insurance is supplied. Affection alone supplies no assent to being used as a shield. The eventual geometry changes because an unforeseen aura distracts the opponents and Yunyun catches the cat. The completed cast consequently has a separated target, but another person's intervention does not make the earlier attempt safe or voluntarily inhibited. Dead horse, injured guards, depletion and recovery remain consequences despite praise for the decisive result. Backup-soul lore also limits a claim of permanent demonic extinction.

The opposite error would make her always cast under any slight. EXP02 also gives real city/night friendly-fire restraint. EXP03 differentiates a taunt set aside for group cohesion, a target concern, refused church destruction, dangerous-position refusals and a later art-efficacy challenge that reopens a grave trip. Means, site, target, status stake and competing purpose change. A chant without mana and an available lethal cast are not the same action capacity. ([EXP03 reading](../10%20Expanded%20Corpus/Explosions/KONOSUBA_EXP_V03_DEEP_READING.md), §§3–6.)

The late main-series shared-death scene remains alongside these earlier events. Darkness's protective promise does not guarantee safety; Megumin's acceptance of possible mutual death does not reveal Darkness's private suicidal motive. The later escape alternative cannot be backdated. CP06 Gen05 M05's ambiguity and other guarded missingness remain at their original scope, while the actual adverse choices are not softened into reliable restraint. The expanded evidence adds an earlier distinct contrary sequence without rewriting either adjudication.

## 4. Yunyun and Komekko constrain the meaning of contribution

Yunyun repeatedly supplies labor, protection, transport, money, information and chosen help. She also wants company, achievement and a route beyond her father's status. EXP02's repeated invitations are real offers, not an analyst's inference that lonely people accept anything. Megumin misses or fails to receive them amid envy and selective attention; the final claim of being a burden is Yunyun's appraisal under a peak-output comparison, not a neutral inventory of who kept the convoy alive. Megumin's private longing does not supply the reply Yunyun needed when she actually offered partnership.

The relationship supports care and exploitation together. A half-lunch grant differs from a later all-you-want meal. A chosen fight does not authorize the hidden pet shield. A protective intervention need not wait for proof that a medical story is fraudulent. Yunyun can suspect a scheme, state a boundary, keep a promise, impose protective Sleep and demand an apology. Her competence does not make every decision correct, but it prevents her from becoming Megumin's automatic carrier or transport permission. The [Yunyun study](KONOSUBA_YUNYUN_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) owns her fuller independent account.

Komekko likewise acts rather than merely raising the stakes for an older sister. Her refusal changes evacuation choices; ordinary breakfast, food brought home, cleaning and a delayed departure give sister care material form. Komekko's own attachment, resource goals, puzzle skill and dealings with Hoost belong to her evidence, not an extension of Megumin's intelligence. A panel sequence is not a diary, and its image count is not a count of independent observations. The [Komekko study](KONOSUBA_KOMEKKO_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) and current image/source routes retain that distinction.

Scarcity helps explain why food, gifts, clothing, fees and income matter without authorizing every extraction. The prequel's sympathetic circumstances do not make deception, unwanted exposure or rough treatment harmless. Conversely, those acts do not make patient waiting, returned money, protective decisions and particular grief insincere. The source's multiple agents supply the counterweights that a single affectionate label would omit.

## 5. Organizing others changes which responsibility she can recognize

Continued Explosions adds a useful contrast between receiving another person's warning and accepting a leader's own responsibility appraisal. At U019P283–319 in the first book, that appraisal stops a raid before outcome; it is not inferred from lucky success. A later rescue involves an acknowledged risk that she chooses for endangered people. Responsibility can therefore inhibit one exciting action and motivate another dangerous one. The difference is what task and cost she has actually accepted. ([Continued01 reading](../10%20Expanded%20Corpus/Continued%20Explosions/KONOSUBA_CONTEXP_V01_DEEP_READING.md), §§3–6.)

That development is partial. Forced deputy status, unasked recurring transport, ignored signals and public teasing retain imposed costs. Yunyun's newly learned Teleport has registration, time and recovery conditions. Iris's royal access and power do not make arbitrary raids ethical, and Chris's different recognized identities do not erase her stopping interventions or leisure aims. Megumin's useful item identification is a real contribution beyond artillery, without proving a new magical build or general management mastery.

The second book preserves selected practical learning and explicit gaps in tracking or direction. Megumin can protect Iris, withhold Aqua's embarrassing crying, refuse false mastermind praise, accept blame in a conservation cover and deliberately spare a creature after a bond cue. Those are actual particular choices. Known costly interference, retaliation, concealment and royal false testimony remain beside them. They support neither permanent reform nor a never-learns rule. Iris, Yunyun and Barton retain their own capabilities, errors and resource conditions. ([Continued02 reading](../10%20Expanded%20Corpus/Continued%20Explosions/KONOSUBA_CONTEXP_V02_DEEP_READING.md), §§3–6.)

## 6. Fantastic Days tests the difference between output and an operation

In the game-derived novel, Megumin receives the cart-preservation instruction before the shot and destroys the cart. The later cargo valuation changes the scale of loss, not whether the constraint was received. Her cast attempt after the explicit joke-spell label is an attempted action, even though the crowd disarms her. The melon shot later spends a resource against a live-capture call while the enemy obtains the objective. Eventual horror and a request for Lia's recovery are real; they are not a fully narrated build-policy reconsideration. (FD-U012P0073–0081; FD-U014/U016; [FD reading](../10%20Expanded%20Corpus/Fantastic%20Days/KONOSUBA_FD_DEEP_READING.md), §§3–6.)

The final operation supplies the constructive side: she conserves the daily cast, other people provide powers and timing, and a pre-impact signal enables contained destruction. Lia's danger warning precedes acceptance of the timing plan. These are genuine prior arrangements, with barrier range and exhaustion still finite. Exact exit/recovery provisions for every caster are not exhaustive, and no performed permanent enemy deaths need be invented. A useful narrow success does not certify universal safe-artillery reliability.

Artistic identification also lets Megumin support Lia's chosen vocation. She can ask to stop Kazuma's excessive reproach and help save a show while wanting her own spectacular role. That combination adds ordinary and relational possibilities without absorbing this novel's events or mechanics into the main-novel sequence. The novel's selective game provenance remains part of the claim, not an inconvenience to omit.

## 7. Written self-presentation and represented wishes remain source-specific

Megumin's polite structure can carry technical explanation, a vulnerable request, competition, sarcasm or a direct boundary. Her own narration supplies embarrassment, pride, selective hearing and admitted inference; it does not independently verify every cause she proposes for another person's behavior. Another speaker's later correction remains evidence even when her first account is funny. Voice is a pattern of situated acts, not permanent incantation or proof that every formal ending is deferential.

The artbook wish of a thousand-floor dungeon is an attributed imagined preference. Cheers recomposes Main04 assets; it does not create another performed expedition. A fan-facing comic and its blast have different continuity from an unshown prose ending. The optional Crimson Mage sample sheets in the TRPG books are source-local personas, not additional Megumin biography or learned skills. Reference, tutorial and scenario speech also has an explanatory purpose. None is new audio, a native-human imitation judgment or a fresh independent J01/J02 trial.
