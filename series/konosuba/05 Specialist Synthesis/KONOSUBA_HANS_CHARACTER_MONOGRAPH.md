# Hans: patient infiltration, chosen priorities and costly loss of cover

Status: cohort27 initial monograph, proposed scoped Tier3 with a separate provisional reconstruction model. HA27 locators and source-plane qualifications are owned by the [cohort27 evidence index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_BELDIA_HANS_SERENA_COHORT27_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md). Source material was already admitted and known; retellings are not new trials. Semantic receiving and the exact containing publication remain pending at authorship.

## 1. Identity and narrative position

Hans / ハンス is the executive identified by Wiz as a mutant Deadly Poison Slime. His human form, consumed-manager disguise and enlarged slime body are states of one agent, not separate managers to be merged with every inn or food worker. The original spring manager is a reported victim with a distinct life, even though Hans appropriates his appearance (HA27-03–05).

His role is active subversion at Arcanretia. Ordinary slime incidents elsewhere, a bottle said to contain his poison and later references to being defeated do not independently establish surviving fragments of a living Hans. In particular, the MASK report of freezing belongs to an earlier episode; it is not a correction that he survived the later MAIN04 resolution (HA27-09, HA27-12–13).

## 2. Evidence and chronology

The MAIN04 bath conversation presents a patient planner describing a long operation and asking for reports. Wolbach says she is there to rest and explicitly declines involvement. Hans's persistence does not turn her into a consenting collaborator. He misreads Kazuma's sexual gaze as assurance that the conversation was not overheard; Kazuma did hear it (HA27-01).

At the source, initial denial and an offer to search his belongings try to preserve cover. Wiz's recognition and explanation of his slime nature defeat that defense; his attempted departure is blocked. He invokes an agreement with Wiz, pursues Kazuma after a boast, then allows the weak Adventurer to go and returns to poisoning the spring. This is a change of priority, not an interrupted attempt to kill every person he sees (HA27-03–04).

His admission that he ate the manager triggers Wiz's stated civilian exception. She freezes his arm and later lower body; he breaks off the arm, regenerates, runs and throws fragments toward the water. Kazuma intercepts several fragments, then runs out of arrows. One succeeds in reaching the source. Enlargement changes the combat problem; Explosion fragments the body, and the exhaustive freezing/purification is supplied in the account after Kazuma loses consciousness (HA27-05–08).

## 3. Motivations and valuations

The operation's goal is destruction of a religious and economic base through its water supply. Hans states a willingness to spend years rather than treating immediate public spectacle as necessary. Once exposed, he chooses direct poisoning and prioritizes the source over a departing weak opponent. This supports strategic persistence, while his original estimate of how long the process will take remains a self-report (HA27-01, HA27-04).

Food and bodily continuity also matter, but should not be romanticized. His claim that eating is a slime instinct accompanies an admission of killing a civilian. The Demon King later complains about Hans consuming military stores without notice and contaminating the food supply. That report places appetite in tension with group logistics; it is adverse evidence against a flawless, exclusively mission-rational infiltrator (HA27-05, HA27-11).

## 4. Affect and self-presentation

Hans tries calm administrative denial before anger, fear, threats and urgent flight become visible. Recognition by a colleague is inconvenient rather than welcome: Wiz's friendly manner exposes him while he tries to keep the source-manager role credible. His irritation and defensive pleading grow when the presumed noninterference agreement no longer protects him (HA27-03–05).

He can show relief at an opponent's retreat and surprise at his own fragment's success. Neither reaction establishes universal mercy or precognition. Kazuma's later conjecture that the huge form is close to instinct may explain its response to food, but should not erase the earlier agent who plans, talks, bargains and changes tactics (HA27-04–06).

## 5. Competence and resource boundaries

Disguise, concealment of poison in his own body, long preparation, regeneration and separable material give Hans several routes to the objective. A search for a carried poison bottle is poorly matched to the actual body-based mechanism. He exploits that mismatch until Wiz supplies the missing identification. He is not merely an enlarged damage target (HA27-01, HA27-03–05).

His resistance is neither trivial nor unlimited. Wiz demonstrably freezes the arm and lower body; he escapes by sacrificing material rather than proving the spells ineffective. The enlarged body exceeds what the earlier tactic can easily control, and available matter affects its scale. Explosion, distributed freezing and purification require coordinated work. No numerical universal immunity, guaranteed instant contact death under all conditions or unlimited regeneration rate was independently measured (HA27-05–07).

Aqua can purify poison yet still be burned by the spring's heat. Wiz's cooling works where Kazuma's weaker Freeze is inadequate. Denied source access, insufficient cooling and poison removal are different constraints; collapsing them into one failed healer score misstates the encounter (HA27-02–03).

## 6. Reasoning, errors and correction

Hans makes a plausible but faulty information inference in the bath: an apparent voyeur seems not to be attending to the conversation. Exposure later prompts tactical correction, including abandoning the human form and exploiting fragments. His operational focus lets him stop spending effort on Kazuma when the latter's low-status job makes him appear less relevant (HA27-01, HA27-03–05).

That same appraisal is vulnerable to uneven information. Kazuma's boast and Wiz's familiar greeting misdirect attention away from the more dangerous response. Hans invokes the broad noninterference agreement without reckoning adequately with its civilian-murder exception. After countermeasures begin, his body tactics are adaptive, but the fragment that finally reaches the spring also surprises him. A fortunate outcome is not evidence he knew Kazuma's ammunition count (HA27-04–06).

## 7. Relationships and asymmetries

With Wolbach he seeks work information while she asserts her separate rest purpose. With Wiz he appeals to colleague status and a rule that she explicitly limits. Her recognition first defeats his disguise, then her civilian commitment defeats his expectation of protection. Neither friendly address nor shared executive rank is permission to murder or sabotage (HA27-01, HA27-03–05).

Kazuma initially functions as an apparently harmless witness, then a loud threat, a fleeing weak target and an obstructive ranged defender. Aqua is technically central to poison removal and final purification; Megumin supplies fragmentation; Darkness bears protective costs. Their combined success does not make Hans incapable or make the whole sequence Kazuma's solitary achievement. Ozma's later thanks reports a companion lost to Hans without supplying the victim's identity or exact fate (HA27-07–08, HA27-10).

## 8. Ordinary life and embodied appetite

The bath exchange shows Hans occupied with his operation rather than Wolbach's rest agenda. It does not demonstrate a failed attempt to enjoy bathing or prove he cannot appreciate any leisure. The reported food-store conduct is the principal ordinary-life counterpoint: his poisonous embodiment can impose a logistical cost even outside an overt attack (HA27-01, HA27-11).

His private tastes, friendships, domestic routines and positive attachments remain thinly supported. The narrator in YORI04 hears that a companion bought a Hans-poison bottle from Wiz for thirty Eris. That is evidence of an attributed commodity history and a warning against handing it to Kazuma, not a completed assay, watched extraction or a new Hans choice (HA27-12).

## 9. Japanese voice and pragmatic action

The dialogue moves from denial of a name and administrative reassurance to defensive invocation of a bargain, anger, explanation and urgent threats. Wiz's repeated use of his name does practical work: familiar address breaks the intended manager identity. The exact point of disclosure matters more than decorating every line with a stock sinister tone (HA27-03–05).

His food-instinct explanation is partly interrupted; do not silently complete it into an experimentally verified law that every disguise always requires consuming a person. Conversely, the interruption does not erase the admitted manager consumption. Printed pragmatic shifts support cautious characterization; no voice actor, accent or universal verbal tic is inferred. Comic phrasing is not automatically L3.

## 10. Comedy and seriousness

Mistaken threat appraisal, the discrepancy between a feared executive and a game-trained novice's assumptions, and interruptions make the battle comic. The poisoned water, admitted civilian killing, bodily danger and economic disruption remain consequential. This novel does not show Kazuma being consumed or killed by Hans; importing another adaptation's scene would manufacture evidence (HA27-04–08).

The food diversion is observed, while the reduced-reason explanation is Kazuma's hypothesis. A serious analogue may preserve persistent mission choice, concealment, rule misuse and resource tradeoffs without inventing slime physics for a human actor. An unfamiliar institution with no specified agreement must not inherit Wiz's exact charter or an assured retaliation.

## 11. Contrary evidence and rivals

The patient professional account is weakened by the gaze mistake, reliance on an incomplete charter and the Demon King's logistical complaint. The indiscriminate killer account is weakened by his choice to let Kazuma leave and return to the source. The harmless eater account fails against the manager's killing and continued poisoning. The mindless monster account fails against his conversation, concealment and adaptive material tactics before enlargement (HA27-01, HA27-03–06, HA27-11).

Generic mission persistence, a stock slime's appetite, fear-driven escape and narrator hindsight remain rivals. A useful model must distinguish pursuit from the objective and specify when body sacrifice or retreat becomes available. It must not explain every choice as secretly optimal or read fortunate fragmentation success as perfect planning.

## 12. Current interpretation and uncertainty

Hans supports a provisional Tier3 model because the same agent moves through covert planning, social concealment, exposure, choice between targets, bargaining, material escape and a changed-scale battle. Those are linked episodes, not independent experiments; they offer conditional contrasts without a validation percentage.

The strongest interpretation combines persistent harmful goals with local appraisal and a bodily repertoire that buys tactical options at a cost. Evidence is weaker for sincere loyalties, ordinary pleasure, exact biological limits and the independent cognitive state of the enormous form. The final distributed cleanup is reported after a focalization gap; a later bounty allocation or proposed holy-water business is not proof all victims recovered or every financial harm was repaired. Source-known diagnostics, if authored after foundation freeze, will remain consistency checks with no blind accuracy, capability grade or confidence gain. The broader program remains open.
