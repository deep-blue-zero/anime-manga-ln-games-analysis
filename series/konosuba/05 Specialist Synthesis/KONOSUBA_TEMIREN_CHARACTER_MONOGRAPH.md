# Temiren: desired recognition, practical skill and continuing disappointment

Status: scoped Tier 3 interpretation of てみれん in the Core and Advanced TRPG replays. The [separate provisional model](../06%20Character%20Modeling%20and%20Reconstruction/KONOSUBA_TEMIREN_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md) owns hypotheses; the [cohort 31 index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_TRPG_REPLAY_COHORT31_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) owns source boundaries and qualifications. This is no independent validation or all-context voice model.

## Identity and source position

Temiren is introduced as a female sixteen-year-old Crimson mage, played by みかみてれん (Core S021-022). The similar player and PC names do not erase the distinction between them. Crimson origin does not join her to Megumin or Yunyun. The profile, free construction, table banter, PC speech, edited narration and resolved checks remain separate planes. A romantic preference or large magical bonus at construction is not an observed lifelong outcome or universal competence.

## Chronology

The Core formation scene ties Temiren's desire for a prince to Gilbert's presentation. She joins the investigation and combat while seeking particular attention and sometimes reinterpreting an unwelcome response. Advanced returns her at a higher level and with Suite-quality bedding, adds21 and tests the group's professions against each other. The later will scene raises the language of love and renewed invitation; Gilbert acknowledges a fault and promises a future hunt. The finale retains shared home, welcome and baths, without performing that hunt or declaring a settled romance.

## Motives and valuations

Recognition by a desired prince matters, but so do excitement, pleasure and the party's work. In Core S030 she requests a romantic wall-pin; Gilbert substitutes a slap and she reports pain before reframing it as encouragement. That is a local attempt to preserve a desirable meaning, not a general consent rule. At Core S049 she defends the farmer's decision to survive rather than accept criticism of his flight. Her concern can therefore recognize someone else's limit even when her own attraction leads to questionable reinterpretation. Advanced S065 combines useful monster identification with a wish to be admired. Practical competence and desired recognition occur together.

## Affect and regulation

Disappointment does not automatically dissolve attachment. After the will discussion she says she has no expectations yet asks for another prince invitation and responds to Gilbert's proposed future hunting (ADV S084). The inconsistency matters more than choosing one statement as her whole true preference. She can criticize him, express pain and continue seeking attention. There is no evidence that disappointment is permanently repaired, that she endorses every earlier act or that the player's laughter cancels the PC's stated hurt. The replay permits changing appraisals within an ongoing relation.

## Competence and limitations

Temiren's magical expertise is specific. She identifies the Winter Hatamoto with a result19 against 16 and learns its artificial jewel origin and defensive properties (ADV S065). It is not the Winter Shogun. At S080 her purchased Turn Undead scroll succeeds with 28 where Gilbert's attempt failed, troubling his professional pride. That success is not permanent clerical superiority or unlimited magic. Her large construction benefit carries an MP burden (Core S022-023). Combat still injures her: she takes 31 at ADV S099 and fails the final self-destruct evasion (S104). Intelligence, tools and social appraisals should not be collapsed into one competence score.

## Decisions and errors

She can check a monster and defend a sensible survival choice, while also being drawn into attention, recruitment or faulty explanations. Gilbert conditions a head-pat offer on a200 KE Axis donation, and she agrees (Core S048); no transfer receipt is shown. Her acceptance cannot become an already paid donation. At ADV S080 her Eris claim coexists with mechanically chosen Faith:Aqua described as for show; at S091 her95% Eris retort reverses the party's95% Axis claim. These are local claims and game decisions, not verified population data or a completed stable conversion. The model must permit pragmatic inconsistency without pretending every claim reveals an immutable creed.

## Relationships

Gilbert is a desired prince and a flawed companion, not a settled romantic partner. She may criticize his treatment, seek renewed invitations and contribute to the party that keeps him alive. Her attention to Otohime's admiration does not automatically create a second romantic relation (ADV S065). She calls21 cute at the introduction, participates in the later party and can agree to its welcome (S031, S108). None of these acts proves universal acceptance of every undead being. Her exchanges with Chiho include shared Economy choices in Core and differing comfort later. Relationships are differentiated by actual requests, answers and actions rather than a single devotion variable.

## Ordinary life

Temiren asks whether cohabitation can reduce upkeep; the GM says no (Core S023). She initially chooses Economy and later uses Suite bedding in the shared house (ADV S031, S108). Enjoyment of bathing and the home matters alongside grand romance. The hypothetical elemental bath uses of the powerless jewels at S106 remain conditional wishes, not completed facilities. The final narrator presents the welcome and pleasure of bathing; further adventures remain future. These details supply an ordinary dimension without inventing a housekeeping competence, income stream or private domestic routine.

## Japanese voice

The native てみれん is preserved, with player みかみてれん named separately. Her speech can invoke a prince, ask for attention, express hurt, rebuke another's lack of gratitude and explain a successful identification. A transfer should preserve those different pragmatic acts rather than turn every sentence into a romantic catchphrase. Claims made for show or in sectarian banter require attribution. The published edited replay is not an audio performance, a reliable count of habitual constructions or an exhaustive dialect corpus.

## Comedy and seriousness

Romantic expectations collide with the companion's actual conduct, while the magic specialist can outperform the priest using a scroll. Those mismatches produce comedy through appraisal and professional status, not only through a stock foolish-girl label. The will's beautiful ending is openly revised after Chiho's suggestion; Temiren's interpretation of love follows that coauthored artifact. Its emotional force does not certify an independently discovered biography. Serious transfer can retain conditional admiration, disappointment, skilled help and wishful reinterpretation without requiring a literal prince or reproducing the slap as romantic proof.

## Countercases and rival readings

A romance-only Temiren misses identification, practical cost questions and the farmer defense. A uniformly credulous Temiren misses criticism and revised expectations. An all-purpose expert misses MP costs, damage and failed evasion. The mechanism behind a particular agreement can be affection, a useful resource, table momentum or a bid for attention; source density alone cannot separate them. Her own expectation statements, actual asks and the other person's response must be tracked independently. A renewed request is evidence of continued interest, not evidence that an earlier refusal never occurred.

## Gaps and warranted depth

The evidence warrants a provisional conditional model across recognition and typed competence, with pragmatic affiliation retained as an unresolved state. It leaves sustained rejection, long-term independent work, durable consent boundaries, stable religious commitment and romance after the printed ending open. Exact capability thresholds cannot be inferred from one successful check. The current model precedes exact hypothetical inputs and no original response may be polished into success. Two dependent diagnostic pairs, if later authored, cannot complete the final nine-category battery or supply an independent reconstruction grade.
