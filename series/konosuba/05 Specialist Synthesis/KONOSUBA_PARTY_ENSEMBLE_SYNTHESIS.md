# A party that makes more than its members can supply alone

Root accepted this main-only specialist at **2026-10-17T13:04:45.202871+00:00**, after full new-prose receiving, the recorded bounded primary checks and exact successor review. The [specialist receiving record](../09%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/KONOSUBA_MAIN_ONLY_SPECIALIST_SYNTHESIS_RECEIVING_REVIEW.md) binds the current artifact, actual review scopes and preserved initial drafts. Candidate-stage wording retained below is historical authorship context, superseded for acceptance only by this paragraph.

Kazuma, Aqua, Megumin and Darkness do not form an effective party because their defects cancel neatly. Their combined activity can make a task possible, make it more dangerous, or create the next task. The same familiarity that lets Kazuma fit a weak spell to Darkness's strength lets him select a humiliation she will find difficult to resist. The same belonging that recruits costly rescue can support secrecy, coercion and claims of entitlement. An ensemble account has to explain those opposed results with the actual interaction, rather than assigning “teamwork” to the victories and “comedy” to the damage.

The mature main-series claim is that these people possess powerful, uneven capacities whose usefulness depends on a sequence: recognizing the problem, becoming available, selecting and accepting a role, supplying resources, executing, recovering people and objects, and handling consequences. They do not always need Kazuma for each phase. They do not always need a single organizer. When they do coordinate, the result need not respect everyone affected. What becomes durable across the series is a particular household and history of reciprocal expectation, not a proof that its combat arrangement is optimal.

## Uneven capacities make sequence matter

An additive description would give the party one planner, one healer, one destructive caster and one shield. It misses the chief causal difficulty: each capacity has conditions, and satisfying one person's conditions can change another person's task. Aqua can restore a person without ensuring that immediate resurrection is safe. Megumin can destroy a target while exhausting her movement and consuming the only terminal strike. Darkness can redirect attacks without reliably killing the attacker. Kazuma can discover a rule without possessing the mana, class bonuses, protection or practiced execution needed to exploit it alone.

There are therefore several different kinds of failure. A capability may be absent, present but asleep, available but diverted, correctly deployed into a bad plan, or effective at its immediate objective while damaging the next state. Aqua's delayed waking in V09 is not a failure of undead purification. Megumin's one-cast limit is not proof of poor magical understanding. Darkness's missed offense is not a failed interception. Kazuma's second-order mistakes do not erase an accurate first-order solution. The [competence ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/KONOSUBA_COMPETENCE_CONTEXT_LEDGER.md) and [decision/error ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/KONOSUBA_DECISION_ERROR_LEDGER.md) keep these distinctions separate.

Recovery is often the missing variable. Who carries Megumin? Who retrieves a corpse? Who has mana after the first attempt? Who can remove a restraint or cross the barrier again? A plan that specifies the successful hit but assumes recovery has not yet specified a successful operation. This is not a theoretical objection imposed on the comedy. V02's continued Destroyer emergency, V03's sealed possession, V12's binding problem, V16's barrier and V17's death and return all make the unfinished phase visible.

The resulting dependence is uneven rather than reciprocal in every scene. One person may perform most of the work. An outsider may supply the indispensable final step. A member may be useless for one task and pivotal for another. Calling every contribution equally necessary would conceal those differences as thoroughly as crediting only the final blow.

## Early victories create a shared world of debt and obligation

The early party forms around material need, recruitment and recurrent disturbance before it has a coherent emotional vocabulary. Labor, winter housing, meals, debt and equipment establish the practical stakes. The mansion later becomes home partly because it solves ordinary scarcity, but its value grows beyond shelter. That development needs the ordinary-life evidence: not merely the high-risk rescues that later make affection easy to recognize.

V01 already makes victory exceed four-person agency. Adventurers, the guild and the city are not background scenery. The Beldia fight uses Darkness's interposition, Aqua's extraordinary magic, Megumin's earlier provocation and Kazuma's opportunistic tactics; it also uses a larger group exposed to the consequences. The accepted curse sequence distinguishes Megumin's guilt and Darkness's offered body from Aqua's effortless-seeming removal. Successful sacred expertise can make a lethal setup anticlimactic without making the preceding fear insincere. [V01 §§7–8](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V01_DEEP_READING.md).

Aqua's flood provides a clear limit to evaluating only the enemy's defeat. The received bounded source notes place her cast at C4P483 and the sweep of participants and town-directed water at P492–497. The epilogue separately gives a 300-million reward and a 340-million compensation demand. Their difference is 40 million; it is not evidence that the bill has already been paid. Darkness's later family-finance history will reveal still more displaced cost. The local success and the public damage belong to one causal account.

The useful generalization is consequently limited. Their powers can produce a jointly possible victory that none would deliver in the same way alone, while also externalizing risk to residents. Shared success creates claims, debts and reputation. It does not create an ethical exemption for the winners. A later rescue funded through those relationships is part of the consequences of earlier conduct, not a gift arriving from outside the story's social system.

## The party-swap case demonstrates competence without proving interchangeability

V02's one-day swap is important because it changes companions rather than merely inviting Kazuma to imagine a normal party. He can cooperate effectively with more conventional adventurers. His own breadth becomes more legible, and his companions' disruptive habits become visible from another side. The result rebuts the idea that he has no usable adventurer skills or that the familiar four are the only people with whom any of them can function. [V02 §4.4](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V02_DEEP_READING.md).

It is not a controlled trial of permanent replacement. The duration, encounter, resources, knowledge and relationship stakes are limited. A temporary successful outing does not observe a full comparable campaign, sustained household satisfaction or every future recovery dependency. The later formal ensemble forecasts deliberately ask narrower questions than “can anyone else work with them?” Those historical contracts must be preserved, not declared satisfied by every substitute team.

The Destroyer episode then shows why breadth and specialist power must be combined across phases. After the core's disposal, the crisis continues. In the bounded shared source check, Wiz lacks a verified safe empty destination, Kazuma accepts the random-teleport risk and the subsequent fortification emergency remains. His report that the work is finished is corrected by continued shaking and residual heat. A first problem has been removed; a second has not.

The final energy handoff is more specific than a generic friendship power. Aqua's mana, Kazuma's intermediary Drain Touch and Megumin's spell are compatible in a way direct holy transfer to Wiz may not be. Wiz acknowledges a prior adverse experience; the scene does not perform a lethal transfer to demonstrate the warning. Megumin's consent, contact negotiation and finite resources remain part of the execution. The eventual second blast is not an independent solo feat by whoever receives last-hit credit. [V02 C5P552–659, EpP8–45; accepted §8](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V02_DEEP_READING.md).

The later accusation concerning the displaced core also belongs on a separate evidence plane. State suspicion is not independent observation of Kazuma deliberately attacking a noble's house. The ensemble story repeatedly turns an incomplete or hostile account into a new institutional burden; reproducing that burden as objective fact would repeat the narrator and authorities' own inference problem.

## A weakness can become useful without becoming a good general policy

V03's possession uses a particularly nonadditive interaction. Darkness's resistance and reward structure obstruct Vanir's expected coercion. Her durable body also protects the demon against Aqua's magic. The very capacity that makes her hard to control makes the removal problem harder. Kazuma's seal temporarily contains the enemy but extends the captive arrangement; burning it does not complete extraction. Only later does Darkness accept the risk of Explosion and move away, while Megumin hesitates and Kazuma assumes responsibility. [V03 C4P380–438/585–694](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V03_DEEP_READING.md).

This is not four stable roles acting independently. One person's body changes what another person's spell can do, a tool changes the enemy's escape options, the shared target changes Megumin's willingness, and Kazuma must obtain a feasible transition rather than win a direct duel. Some cooperation follows actions that created or worsened the predicament. Repair does not make the earlier choices retrospectively correct.

The same principle should not become a defense of every eccentricity. Darkness's chosen offensive weakness can create a coercion-resistant situation without becoming the best build for all tasks. Megumin's identity commitment can sustain a spectacular solution without licensing unsafe geometry. Aqua's high output can solve a domain problem while generating expensive collateral. Kazuma's manipulative familiarity can secure execution while imposing an unacceptable cost. Local usefulness and general policy must remain separate.

## V04: a plan can outlive its planner

The Hans sequence is one of the strongest early demonstrations of a multi-stage solution: bait, destructive reduction, protection, freezing and purification. The arrangement fits asymmetric abilities, including Wiz's independent expertise. It also continues after Kazuma becomes unconscious. Darkness's cover, Wiz's ice work and Aqua's terminal purification cannot be described as his contemporaneous perception or as a sequence he personally performs. The accepted retrospective report is evidence of their activity through a different access route. [V04 C5P587–619/634–660](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V04_DEEP_READING.md).

Aqua's prior appraisal is also causally important. The contaminated springs matter to her as followers' resources; distress and obligation keep her at the problem when flight would be safer. Megumin volunteers concern and investigative help outside the terminal cast. Darkness initially yields to Aqua's distress and later bears the bodily protective work. The plan succeeds because the actors have their own reasons to remain engaged, not because one command temporarily replaces their motives.

The victory still changes the spring system and threatens Wiz through purification. These are capacity and compatibility consequences, not evidence that sacred duty was fake. The party's achievement should be described together with its overdeployment and external costs. The same broad claim will recur in the final volume: a successful attack does not prove a safe stopping, recovery or bystander plan.

## V06: institutions change the opportunity, not every person's choice

V06 is central to the question of Kazuma's necessity because the three specialists perform conspicuously after he dies in conventional mass combat. The bounded fresh source check makes the institutional claim more precise than the accepted early shorthand. There is a public call, an assembled force, knights, adventurers, deployment and subsequent medical and transport help. Yet at C4P42 the announcer explicitly says there are no special instructions and permits free fighting: 「特別な指示はありません」 and 「自由に戦って」. At P43 Kazuma only infers that the speaker is a capital-guild employee; no standing military rank is established. The announcement does not allocate Aqua, Megumin and Darkness to their textbook roles.

The organized encounter still matters. There are targets, allies, patients, a front and a retreating enemy formation that can make specialist work useful. The physical and social environment offers opportunities the four do not have to invent from nothing. Darkness first tries to mobilize the household; Aqua resists ordinary risk and Megumin wants an early display; Kazuma privately sees prestige and castle access before presenting a heroic departure. Once present, he becomes overconfident, overpursues a weak-looking target and dies. Institutional mobilization does not erase the actors' choices or prevent bad ones. [V06 C4P1–87](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V06_DEEP_READING.md).

The aftermath has distinct access levels. Kazuma sees injuries, soot, a crater and others carrying Megumin after resurrection. Aqua reports why resurrection was delayed and how Darkness protected his corpse. Knights report the specialists' contributions. The claim that Darkness used Decoy at P160 is his inference, not a witnessed institutional command or a directly narrated cast. Aqua's delay at P133 is a reasoned choice to avoid repeated death, not evidence that she could not revive him. Those distinctions preserve the reality of the contribution without inventing a single source of organization.

The exact older reading says, **“The party requires routing more than it requires Kazuma personally. Kazuma is the most flexible endogenous router identified so far, but external institutions or domain experts can substitute when the problem already has a stable structure.”** Its accompanying list includes **“role assignment”**; the character-state ledger says **“Conventional military institutions naturally structure Aqua, Megumin, and Darkness into effective roles.”** These formulations overcompress the evidence if “routing” is read as explicit personal assignment. The attributable current qualification is: institutional mobilization, allied infrastructure, participant initiative and compatible encounter conditions can support effective specialist phases without continuous Kazuma direction. The source does not prove an indispensable coordinator of any kind, and does not observe a complete comparable campaign replacement. The old formal E01 disposition and all frozen wording remain historical evidence, not silently rescored.

Claire's later proposal is a proposal. At C4P558–561 she imagines a stronger arrangement and group follow-up. Kazuma experiences exclusion and anxiety; that is evidence about his preference, not a performed reorganization, Mitsurugi's agreement or the other three's permanent wishes. “They could do useful work elsewhere” and “this household means more than its utility” can both be true without deciding an unobserved counterfactual campaign.

The heist supplies a different distribution of competence in the same volume. Chris has a tool that actually breaks a barrier Kazuma had thought only Aqua could break; she lays wire traps and offers a viable retreat. Kazuma supplies local knowledge, indirect magic and an Iris-specific reason to continue. He also knowingly takes the book that triggers the alarm and harasses Chris into noise. The pair's recovery from the new danger does not prove the danger was an intended part of a perfect plan. Darkness later gives a weak defense that Kazuma reads as cooperation; Aqua breaks the Bind and attempts capture without understanding the intruders' purpose. Characters with intimate knowledge can still be operating on different accounts of the task. [V06 C5P72–205/320–431; admitted C5 continuation P1–12](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V06_DEEP_READING.md).

## A household is maintained through ordinary acts and contested costs

The party's value cannot be inferred only from winning. V06's epilogues show actual tea, snacks, proposed shopping, drinking, bargaining and invitations to shared amusement. Darkness's tea reaches Kazuma; her snack preparation is visible. Megumin leaves to shop, but the checked passage does not show the purchase or return. Aqua eventually shares after negotiation. These ordinary distinctions matter because a home is partly made by performed routines rather than declarations of belonging.

The same domestic scene includes Kazuma trying to seize Aqua's bottle while she objects. Darkness's separate fantasy does not consent on Aqua's behalf. Later sharing and celebration do not retroactively authorize the taking. Familiarity provides a rich repertoire of bargaining, jokes and offenses; it does not eliminate possession or privacy boundaries. [V06 Ep2P9–17/35–64](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V06_DEEP_READING.md).

A second exact old claim needs qualification. The V06 reading says Megumin **“later writes to her”** in an Iris-affiliation paragraph, and related ledgers repeat the route. In the complete admitted Ep2, Kazuma at P19 infers Iris as recipient; at P24 Megumin states that the fan letter should be handed to the masked thief if encountered. Her actual intended recipient is specified by the handoff statement, not by the word “fan letter” alone. Actual delivery and other possible correspondence remain unknown. Her direct Iris friendship has other evidence; this letter is not verified direct Iris contact. The revision corrects an inference about the recipient, not the sender or the absence of every possible Iris letter.

V12 and V13 give the household a less picturesque material account. Date arrangements become a family picnic; child care is shared; meals, clothes, textbooks and treatment must be supplied. Chores include cooking, house cleaning, bath and toilet cleaning and the garden. The allocation is negotiated and contested, and a nominally useful power can spoil a seasoning. Darkness can improve baseline cooking while Aqua seeks rotation away from an unwanted job. Ordinary division is neither a permanent natural role for each woman nor a frictionless display of complementarity. [V12 §11 and epilogue](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V12_DEEP_READING.md); [V13 C2P122–136](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V13_DEEP_READING.md).

The haunted-house recovery in V02 is a valuable shared-source counterweight. Aqua works through the night and purifies the last spirit, but her earlier shortcut caused displacement that put the household at risk. The agency checks the adventurer card; the owner still wants them to keep the house. Kazuma undertakes grave care and finds Anna's name, corroborating a narrow part of Aqua's account without certifying all its biography. Repair includes actual work, acknowledgment, waived reward and continued institutions; it is not simply a comic reset to a free mansion. [V02 C3P439–525](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V02_DEEP_READING.md).

## V07: costly membership needs a community, not only a hero

Darkness's departure turns the ordinary group's value into a concrete possible loss. Her self-sacrificial solution excludes the others' willingness to bear cost for her. Kazuma's empty-house feeling and refusal to substitute a new frontliner are particular preference evidence. Aqua's repeated performances are aimed at retrieving Darkness rather than collecting tips. Megumin refuses resignation, recruits Yunyun and uses the reputation of a loaded spell to open escape. Their conduct is costly without becoming emotionally uniform. [V07 §§4–9](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V07_DEEP_READING.md).

Vanir supplies information and a financial route; Yunyun and the adventurer community provide actions the core four cannot simply count as their own. Citizens' earlier compensation claims and Dustiness borrowing connect the crisis to public consequences. At the wedding, adventurers refuse to become Alderp's convenient enforcement force and create a route out. This is accumulated reciprocity and selective solidarity, not the world universally rewarding the protagonists.

The payment is **2 billion Eris**, not twenty billion: C5P475's 二十億 and its two thousand one-million coins agree. Old English ledger claims overstate it, and remain exact historical objects in the delivered qualification register. Kazuma's report of liquidating rights and accumulated money is still a serious sacrifice. A tenfold numerical correction does not require either diminishing the act to nothing or preserving the error to keep the emotional magnitude.

The rescue itself includes Darkness's resistance and Kazuma's degrading ownership demand. Her subsequent pleasurable response is not legal ownership or retroactive permission. Homecoming rejects her self-erasure, yet the return immediately restores accusations and teasing. The party keeps her because her presence matters; it does not thereby become a procedure that gives everyone the right to decide for her. Costly attachment and coercive means have to be accounted for separately.

## Incentives can organize a task or destroy it

V09's kill-count contest shows that explicit organization is insufficient. A clear metric rewards taking the kill rather than creating an opening, and grievance turns partners into targets. Aqua and Darkness pursue Kazuma; noise and distraction contribute to encirclement. Kazuma then recognizes that comparing their unlike roles is the wrong shared objective. Buffing and protection resume, only for Megumin's own scene-taking incentive to disrupt the repaired arrangement. [V09 C2P172–271](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V09_DEEP_READING.md).

The repair-captain sequence supplies the constructive alternative. Darkness attaches a title and recognition to Aqua's actual maintenance skill. Workers respond to real improvement; Aqua enjoys the role, does the work, shares drink and gains helpers. Praise does not automatically replace the goal because the praised identity has been attached to accomplishing it. This is a conditional success of incentive design, not a general proof that flattery controls Aqua or that she will always prefer routine maintenance. [V09 FP43–110](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V09_DEEP_READING.md).

V10 and V11 expand the same distinction. A distributed false flag can lack contingency planning; an assigned healer can sleep through a critical handoff; disabling flight can leave a collision because inertia remains. In captivity, Darkness's gag-release idea, Kazuma's small ignition spell and her physical strength create an actual escape. Iris nevertheless defeats the immediate threat independently before their rescue reaches her. The pair's fitted cooperation is real without being the cause of every favorable result. [V10 C5P622–707](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V10_DEEP_READING.md); [V11 F](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V11_DEEP_READING.md).

V12 gives a particularly explicit failed recovery plan. Shared strong Bind acquires the ingredient, but the binding cannot be cut; wounds create more apertures, Darkness becomes a living plug, and water buys time rather than finishing release. Eris ultimately frees her. In the returning household, healing has maintained Sylphina without curing the disease. Apparent wellness is therefore not evidence that the expedition was unnecessary. Technical expertise, continued care, ingredient acquisition and release are separate tasks with separate providers. [V12 FP312–438](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V12_DEEP_READING.md).

## Reciprocal knowledge includes methods of injury

By the middle volumes, each person carries a model of the others. They know which skill exists, which request is likely to work, what name embarrasses, what challenge invites overcommitment and what reassurance matters. That knowledge supports rapid rescue and ordinary companionship. It also makes exploitation efficient. The series does not reserve accurate psychological perception for morally good uses.

Darkness can know a diplomatic threat is real and choose coercive restraint or drugging. Kazuma can know her public standing matters and deliberately use true private details to injure it. Megumin can understand Yunyun's need for belonging and recruit a valued friend through a costly identity challenge. Aqua can correctly anticipate Kazuma's likely mischief without acting responsibly herself. The [relationship ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/KONOSUBA_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md) records those pair-specific permissions and limits; affection cannot be inferred from insult volume, and permission cannot be inferred from affection.

The V12 intimate conflict is a warning against treating the household as a collective consent mechanism. An honest refusal, gratitude, unwanted initiation, ignored stop requests and later active coaching occur in sequence. Each changes the current interaction; none erases the preceding act. Megumin's intervention and Aqua's key search address a practical recovery problem without proving perfect knowledge of everything before their arrival. This event is not merely a triangular romance label. It displays how familiarity, desire, restraint, material constraint and self-serving narration interact. [V12 C3P318–537](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V12_DEEP_READING.md).

The same restraint on interpretation applies to V05's skill allocation. Kazuma's affectionate inference about Megumin's identity and the resulting restoration of Explosion can be meaningful without making his override jointly authorized. The admitted F passage explicitly says the allocation is against her expressed intention. A satisfactory outcome from the narrator's view cannot substitute for prior consent. The ensemble often repairs another person by deciding what they “really” want; sometimes the reading endorses the insight emotionally, but analysis still has to preserve the overridden statement and practical risk.

## V14–V16: useful alternatives have their own preferences and limits

Aegis and Yunyun make the alternative-party question unusually concrete in V14. In the admitted in-spine continuation, Yunyun cannot use magic or skills while inside the armor. Aegis asks for a different fighting emphasis; her valued dagger and her own uncertainty matter. The later account describes alternating armored rest with outside casting and mana recovery. That is a workable adapted phase through an attributed report, not Kazuma's direct observation of a full campaign and not proof that a willing mage is interchangeable with a defensive wearer. [V14 I13P58–71; C4P8–12](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V14_DEEP_READING.md).

V15 extends cooperation under asymmetric knowledge. Darkness gathers information and supplies authority; Megumin infers anomalies and later restrains Kazuma during cure; Aqua supplies buffs, cure and resurrection; Yunyun provides a specific stealth capability; Vanir and Eris supply information and institutional access. Kazuma's secrecy means that support is not based on a fully shared account. His eventual loss of autonomy under Serena cannot be reinterpreted as a perfect plan because the hostile operation collapses. Conversely, the others' actual repairs cannot be credited to his retrospectively imagined foresight. [V15 §§4–6](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V15_DEEP_READING.md).

The final Wiz scene in that volume shows an external relationship constraining available power. Aqua can in principle purify Wiz, but Wiz's trust and voluntary willingness make the act emotionally intolerable; Aqua withdraws. Vanir's project and objection are his own. The scene shows a selected stop, not proof that all harm ceases when someone is valued. It is compatible with Aqua and Vanir earlier injuring Wiz while competing. Their bond does not belong to the core party alone.

V16 then supplies actual alternative travel. Mitsurugi, Aqua and Yunyun function effectively in ordinary encounters; Wiz and Vanir navigate, train and repair dangerous situations without continuous Kazuma orders. The familiar groups can experience smoother travel and still miss their old disorder. These are preferences, not a proof of inability to work with anyone else. Nor does the current source provide a clean, fully specified permanent reorganization whose entire campaign and domestic outcome have been observed. [V16 C1P245/449; C3P329–338; FP65/154–194](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V16_DEEP_READING.md).

The barrier episode is a sharper dependence test than generic nostalgia. Megumin's inadequate attack contribution, Darkness's acknowledgment of her own weakness and the need for an external provider expose both material limits and social comfort. Darkness embraces rather than merely competes with her. This is reciprocal regulation by a person with comparable experience, not simply Kazuma reassuring every specialist. The household's relational knowledge has become distributed too.

## V17: the campaign's result exceeds its original plan

The final operation is genuinely organized before key attacks. Signals, separation, mana supply, defense, transport and resurrection are causal provisions, not just names assigned after victory. They also fail locally. Kazuma forgets a known undead detection limitation and that Aqua remains attached to a rope. Megumin overheats despite her technical excellence. Darkness bears real injuries. Aqua's domain knowledge and response timing matter independently of her divine title. The fixed public histories preserve these errors alongside the eventual success. [V17 C1–C3](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V17_DEEP_READING.md).

The gravest collective-restraint problem is explicit before the final separation. At C3P425 Darkness tells Megumin to kill the enemy and offers her body as a wall, citing prior Explosion survival. Megumin at P426 accepts that the protection might fail and that everyone might die together. A protective promise and an informed acceptance of its possible failure are different states; neither guarantees party safety or proves Darkness privately desires death. P427’s warning has no certified speaker. The staff already lit with Explosion and Darkness’s wounded, unarmed shield position are then observed at P437, while Yunyun and the king’s aides try to persuade Megumin at P438. That observation is later evidence, not a cue available before P427. Aqua’s proposed Teleport and balcony disposal at P446 is a subsequent alternative, not their prior agreed safety plan. No indoor cast is shown, but nonrelease and later survival cannot establish willing compliance or an effective autonomous brake. Thus one member’s protector identity can encourage another’s lethal commitment while other participants oppose it: seriousness and restraint are not synchronized across the group. CP06 retains D01 CONFIRMED, M05 AMBIGUOUS and M06 PARTIAL; missing M05 tactical/alternative conjuncts and M06’s unsafe exposure remain, without erasing the actual continued-fire/overload failure already recorded. [V17 C3P425–446](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V17_DEEP_READING.md).

His isolation plan and last resources exploit breadth, but do not give him unlimited power. His arrows underperform, ordinary Heal is insufficient and his final Explosion depends on prior daily shared life, an acquired spell, resources and lethal risk. At FP45 the actual wait directive after the king's renewed intention to return can itself serve his independently stated protective purpose. Discourse management and taunting can be compatible with concrete protection. The withdrawn claim that this supplied a second fully opposite urgent-command failure remains historical, not the current reason for J01's formal result.

The others continue fighting after his death despite the intended recovery arrangement. That can express loyalty and create danger simultaneously. Yunyun later supplies the actual transport organization from stated capacity and resources without continuous Kazuma direction. Aqua and Eris together recreate his body under a bounded exception; a divine provider's friendship is not an unlimited service right. Mitsurugi's unresolved healing remains a distinct recovery gap after the enemies have been defeated. No summary should close it merely because the major objective is complete. [V17 FP671–751 and Ep](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V17_DEEP_READING.md).

The final world choice is stronger evidence of particular attachment than a slogan about the party. Kazuma understands an attractive Japan alternative and still chooses the troublesome world he knows. That preference includes its people, pleasures and future possibilities, not only an abstract duty. It does not mean everyone makes the same comparison, that no one may leave, or that every past harm has been forgiven. The later household expulsion, romantic uncertainty and Eris's refusal of habitual office intrusion keep ordinary limits alive. [V17 FP553–581/625–627; Ep](../02%20Sequential%20Readings/KONOSUBA_V17_DEEP_READING.md).

## What the validation histories permit the synthesis to say

## A practical account of the whole

The party's durable achievement is not that everyone becomes less troublesome. It is that each acquires a thicker history of what the others can do, what they want, how they fail and what their absence would change. That history makes appropriate trust possible: allowing a specialist to finish after the organizer has fallen, choosing an indirect spell because another person's strength can complete it, or paying a price that an isolated utility calculation would decline. It also gives each sharper tools for leverage and injury.

A good explanation must therefore ask several separate questions of a successful scene. Was the capacity present? Was it available in time? Did the person choose the relevant task? Did the plan fit the target and the next state? Who carried the risk and recovery? Who else supplied authority, money, transport, knowledge or restraint? Were the people affected permitted a choice? These questions do not require every answer to favor the party. They make visible why an apparently ridiculous group can repeatedly accomplish difficult things without becoming either an ideal team or a secretly perfect machine.

The familiar insults can resume after a rescue because the relationship was never based on respectful language alone. But durable membership is not sufficient proof of an agreed boundary, and affection does not guarantee a stop when someone is hurt. The retired tears-rule failure and continuing sexual coercion prevent that comforting inference. The strongest account retains actual repairs, material generosity and chosen company alongside abuse, jealousy, secrecy and unfinished consequences.
