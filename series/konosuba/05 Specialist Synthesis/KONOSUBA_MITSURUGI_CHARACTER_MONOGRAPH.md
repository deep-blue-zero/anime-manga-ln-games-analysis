# Mitsurugi Kyouya: capable protection, wanted recognition and the limits of the hero role

Mitsurugi is an effective, sometimes brave adventurer whose wish to protect people is entangled with attachment to Aqua, conventional heroic expectations and a wish to be recognized. His failures do not establish general incompetence; his real help does not erase pressure on the people he wants to help. His willingness to revise a particular belief or release a particular hand also falls short of a universal consent rule. This monograph earns a provisional Tier 3 examination alongside a separate [reconstruction model](../06%20Character%20Modeling%20and%20Reconstruction/KONOSUBA_MITSURUGI_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md). Neither document certifies simulation readiness.

## Identity, position and the two companions

The main text gives 御剣響夜 already at MAIN01 C3 P592 and again at MAIN17 C2 P336. ミツルギ and the reference reading ミツルギ・キョウヤ identify the same named subject. Kazuma's repeated wrong names are his addressing behavior, not aliases of additional people. The TRPG advanced reference calls Mitsurugi a high-level Sword Master, apparently of high-school age, and leaves exact age unknown. The monograph does not supply an unprinted birthday, age or full Japanese biography. His own MAIN01 level37/class introduction is self-report rather than a newly inspected card.

Gram is a specific goddess-given resource and a valued connection, not a synonym for all his innate ability. MAIN01's bent cage bars occur while he wears it; the later explanation connects it with increased strength. The memorial equipment table likewise describes a Mitsurugi-specific enhancement. That does not isolate what he could do without it. Nor is the armor アイギス, whose refusal is reported elsewhere, his sword. The table distinguishes that armor from 聖盾イージス; equipment types and names must not drift into one object.

The recurring party includes a spear-user and a thief, with their own appraisal and attachment. MAIN17 C3 P54 names the pair フィオ and クレメア; P57 explicitly says Kazuma does not know which name belongs to which woman. Fio and Clemea here are working renderings, not verified official Latin spellings. Neither word order nor a familiar character design resolves the assignment. The two existing census people remain two people; the names do not create two additional entries. Their separate role-bounded profiles belong in the character-state ledger. Early warrior/thief descriptions, a later noble's archer/spear description, shared or untagged speech and the late individually tagged commitments retain their own attribution limits. Mitsurugi's class composition report cannot silently decide every earlier voice.

## Motivations and valuations

Protecting weaker people and defeating the Demon Lord are credible commitments. They precede or exceed some immediate applause: he stops Dust's harassment, returns for an apparent village girl after being told Aqua is safe, risks himself against dangerous opponents, and objects when Darkness is ordered to strip under threat. The FD opening offer to fight precedes Kazuma's hero flattery. Explaining every action as a performance for Aqua fails these cases.

Aqua nevertheless has special priority. He goes looking for her, values Gram as their connection, treats the origami she gives him as precious, seeks time and membership arrangements around her, and admits liking her. In the Dust trap he initially cannot divert from his Aqua search for a generic village rescue; the invented claim that Aqua went to that village changes the decision. This distinguishes a relationship-weighted priority from indiscriminate rescuing. The same staged scene does not prove he knowingly preferred a lie: the reader knows the production; he does not.

His protector frame can override another person's expressed destination. MAIN16 Final gives an explicit request to return, Yunyun's intervention and a later request to wait; he argues for the world mission and against Kazuma as a partner. Tactical urgency, real casualties, affection, rivalry and preferred recognition can jointly explain this. The text does not identify one exclusive cause. This is a substantive failure of listening even while the campaign's stakes are real. The different MAIN17 decision to accept returning is a real contrary case; it cannot be smoothed into either permanent controlling behavior or a permanent newly repaired ethic.

## Personality, affect and regulation

Mitsurugi is often courteous, earnest, eager to help and visibly sensitive to exclusion or nonrecognition. He corrects wrong names, reacts to forgotten introductions, is hurt by rejection and can recover enough to continue a task. In MAIN16 he accepts Yunyun's refusal of permanent membership and resumes the search after embarrassment. That local recovery does not establish emotional invulnerability. FD's social collapse after Aqua forgets him is comic narration, not a medical diagnosis.

His confidence can be excessive without being uniformly baseless. He expects his strength and arrangement to offer Aqua a better life and sometimes imagines reciprocity beyond the evidence. Her gratitude, bluffing replies, discomfort, forgetting and private comparisons all constrain the romance he would like. Yunyun's startle and formal Mitsurugi-san likewise do not establish attraction or acceptance of first-name familiarity. He also expresses regret about failing to protect others. The FD self-condemnation after Ria is struck is his appraisal; Ria chose to interpose, and the scene does not make him solely responsible for every injury.

Anger is not absent beneath manners. He physically grabs Kazuma, reacts to insults, and draws Gram after the Dust deception and overpricing disclosure. No blow is watched in that latter confrontation. Conversely, he does not immediately attack the revealed succubus merely because Dust identifies her species. Do not invent either a universal nonviolence rule or a universal demon-killing rule.

## Competence: effective force with specific dependencies

Direct main-novel action establishes rapid weapon handling, a supported manticore kill and substantial fighting against Demon Lord forces. MAIN17's Rune of Saber is his own named attack, distinct from Yunyun's Light of Saber. The narrative supports effective multiple attacks, not an independently counted complete kill ledger. Eris's assessment that his sword specialization suits a dark dungeon is a counterfactual tactical judgment, not an actual comparative trial; his named attack also prevents turning it into a claim that he can never use any magic-like skill.

His two losses to Kazuma reveal different mechanisms. The first uses Steal and the stolen sword; he later carries decoys against that known technique. The masked encounter instead exploits a frozen scabbard and water/Freeze control. The attempted punch is not a landed counterattack. This is specific learning alongside a remaining gap, rather than failure to learn anything or automatic mastery of every adjacent trick. Kazuma's own statement that he would lose a fair rematch is also a situated appraisal, not a completed rematch.

Capacity, access, readiness and objective differ. Gram's loss removes a major resource; a hostage condition changes the cost of fighting; a Teleporting foe changes reach; armor preferences affect acquisition; exhausted return in FD does not restore full capacity. FD's Ancient Dragon kill is Mitsurugi's report, while the missed strike, ground collapse, return and exhaustion are staged action. Dust's orc aftermath shows him among defeated opponents and supports a strong contextual attribution; it is not a directly narrated stroke-by-stroke battle. Do not import a game-novelization feat into an unqualified main timeline.

## Decision process, information and correction

He often begins with a legible role story: captive goddess, threatened weak people, honorable contest, unhelpful rival or heroic mission. The first MAIN01 rescue inference is understandable from appearances, as even Kazuma acknowledges. The adverse evidence is his persistence after further information and his willingness to wager another person. A plausible first inference is not an excuse for every later action.

He can update. A companion corrects the false tipping custom in Dust. He accepts a declared low-level limitation in MAIN16. He revises an account of enemy attacks in MAIN06 and adopts a known-technique countermeasure. In the Dust trap he returns for a safety check, detects incompatible familiarity and asks why the supposed stranger knows Dust's name. These defeat a blanket gullibility model. They do not prove calibrated skepticism in all domains. A dream, fortune-teller report or flattering rescue narrative can still receive more weight than an adversarial reading would give it.

Actor knowledge must stay temporal. MAIN16 explicitly gives him only one known defeat by Kazuma, despite the reader's knowledge of the masked second fight. MAIN17's pressure follows Kazuma's training and newly displayed skills; it is not simply continued disbelief in the unchanged level-one state he accepted earlier. MAIN16 Aqua's private fear and Kazuma's later subtle reading of her expression are not automatically available to him. His criticism of Kazuma's deceptive or boastful method can coexist with cooperation in a concealed plan. Honor language is not an absolute refusal to use every tactic he dislikes.

## Differentiated relationships and ordinary life

**Aqua.** He offers protection, expensive or symbolically important gifts, physical closeness and an imagined shared heroic future. She is an agent with different wishes. MAIN01 Darkness's release request eventually works; MAIN17 Aqua's injury-check interruption leads to apology and release. Those bounded responses coexist with the MAIN16 onward pressure. Neither blushing companions nor Aqua's occasional thanks provide blanket permission.

**Kazuma.** Rivalry, name corrections, moral criticism, admitted defeat, requests to protect Aqua and a stated wish to become friends all occur. MAIN17's proposed friendship through victory is not mutual friendship; Kazuma rejects the overture. Kazuma's useful tactical contributions and cruel or self-serving treatment can coexist. The analysis need not choose one man as the ethically coherent alternative.

**His companions.** He explicitly calls them important comrades rather than followers in MAIN06 and reports a separate leveling arrangement because he gets most kills. The report does not show every training session or prove an equal opportunity structure. Later he thanks them, discusses training and returning alive, and they have their own fatigue, willingness to follow, death-together statement, protective stance and pleas for treatment. The thief's dramatic commitment cannot be assigned to the spear-user, or used as permanent consent to death for either woman.

**Yunyun and others.** He offers Yunyun membership; she declines while retaining her own reasons for helping. Her intervention on Aqua's behalf challenges his preferred plan. He opposes Darkness's coerced degradation without acquiring authority over her body. In Dust he checks on the supposed village girl even after his original priority appears resolved. These relations support aid beyond a single romantic object while leaving paternalistic scripts and status needs visible.

His ordinary-life record is selective. Shopping manners are reported by the shopkeeper; payment for Gram is watched. The ring and origami show different gift meanings without establishing a reciprocal engagement. Aqua's folded napkin is visibly elaborate art admired by both men, so Mitsurugi's appreciation is not merely a gullible response to any scrap. There is no established favorite food, general entertainment preference or audited continuing income. Companions' adjacent noisy lodging is an overheard report. The attempted hostile dream is never delivered; apparent deep drunken sleep does not establish consent to the companions' night visit, immunity to magic or lasting trauma.

## Japanese voice and the work done by comedy

The main and reference material use 僕/ボク, but DUST01 p-005 P222 also gives 私 in a polite explanation. A generator must retain context and variation rather than impose one pronoun spelling. Aqua-sama marks reverence and attachment; it does not by itself certify Axis membership, as Dust's inference illustrates. His recurring correction of his own name, formal introductions, reassurance, task declarations, fairness complaints and shifts into emotional protest do pragmatic work. Translation can preserve that direction without pretending English exercises test Japanese register. Katakana, ruby, an honorific or a familiar heroic phrase alone is not an automatic L3 claim.

The conventional hero is repeatedly placed inside someone else's comic framing: a name joke, a gift whose recipient forgets him, an unorthodox defeat, an unwanted comparison, or Dust's resentful description. Humor amplifies embarrassment and expectation failure. It does not make actual gripping, threats, sexual harassment, injuries or another person's refusal nonliteral. Gram's supernatural strength is a world mechanism, not automatically a gag. Nor does later healing, successful fighting or a warm exchange settle earlier permission, collateral cost or emotional harm. H-class decisions remain local to their claims; this monograph assigns no blanket code to Mitsurugi as a person.

## Rival accounts, current interpretation and open work

A purely vain rival fails real aid and concern away from Aqua. A flawless chivalric hero fails the wager, pressure, selective attention and physical overreach. An incapable comic foil fails actual fighting and recognized limits. An uncorrectable fool fails the decoys, accepted corrections and detected contradiction. A fully reformed listener fails the later uneven sequence. A generic earnest protector with a favored person can explain much of the same evidence; the separate model must retain that serious rival rather than declare unique psychological identification.

The best current interpretation is a capable, relationship-weighted protector whose heroic self-conception can narrow listening and amplify recognition needs, and whose learning is concrete rather than universally transferable. This is an explanatory hypothesis with contrary cases, not a diagnosis or measured probability.
