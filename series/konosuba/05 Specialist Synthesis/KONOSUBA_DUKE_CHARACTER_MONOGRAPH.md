# Duke — ambition, selective restraint and costly escape

Duke pursues a military office through a demonstration of strength. His demand is mistaken for courtship, but the misunderstanding does not explain all his behavior. He threatens weaker interlocutors, qualifies hostility when Vanir's standing becomes relevant, enjoys an apparently congenial conversation with Aqua, gathers military intelligence, and eventually sacrifices a declared aversion in pursuit of power. His last romantic appeal is a new survival maneuver. It must not be projected backward as the sincere purpose of his arrival.

FinalP309–318 displays the army emblem and black wings; Aqua identifies him as a fallen angel. His own FinalP326 account of prior divine service and unpaid compensation is testimony about his grievance. No complete mortal-to-angel biography, precise rank in the divine hierarchy or administrative appointment is supplied.

The later white-winged gatekeeper is another figure. MAIN17U011P84 has Kazuma compare that figure with Duke; it does not identify a returned Duke. Vanir's FinalP462 statement about lower angels' unfixed sex is attributed fictional lore. It neither provides a general human psychological explanation nor licenses inventing Duke's private sexual history.

## 3. Motives, values and claimed justification

Duke explicitly wants Wiz's Demon King army office, believing her inactive while officers disappear and military projects fail (FinalP305–308/361–366). Showing superior force is important to his claimed entitlement; ordinary persuasion to abandon a job would not meet that end (C3P427/433). His aspiration to magical mastery and his opposition to undead can coexist with joining an army that includes them. The source gives a conflict rather than a fully reconciled moral system.

He values standing sufficiently to distinguish the weak from Vanir and to demand a serious contest from Wiz. That does not make every decision a status display. His comfortable shared dislike of demons and Eris, actual drink provision and willingness to accept trivia in the reported gambling settlement also have ordinary social content. No original romantic goal is established. The final love story is authored after his defeat and imminent destruction, under sharply different incentives.

## 4. Affect and regulators

Early composure becomes intimidation when Dust solicits money and when Kazuma names Wiz (C3P357–407). Apparent support recruits surprise and reduced vigilance. Darkness's exposed noble identity does not visibly intimidate him. Aqua's unfamiliar trivia first produces confusion, then shared categorical hostility and enjoyment (C4P371–405).

Wiz's failure to attack seriously provokes accusations of contempt and escalation to sacred magic (FinalP240–265). The marriage interpretation produces genuine bewilderment, then exasperation and explanation (P281–308). Vanir's arrival recruits caution, a polite request and physical retreat (P341–342). The source does not make pride invulnerable to recognized power.

Near suffocation he nods surrender and collapses on release. He then resorts to the lich ritual. After purification and draining, he pleads, invents a shared-immortality account, notices Wiz's response and continues with a friends-first appeal (P404–457). Fear and opportunistic adjustment are visible without an independently exposed full interior plan.

## 5. Competence and its limits

Duke has real capacity: rapid knife placement, intimidating presence, offensive fire spells, sacred Sanctuary, a military information trail and the ritual that changes his condition. Those are different abilities. His self-confidence about judging people and sensing the evil generated by human lies is a claimed diagnostic theory (C4P382–384), not an independently calibrated lie detector. It fails to supply him Aqua's identity or the other speakers' actual referents.

Wiz's defensive versatility and later superior force matter. His advertised lich upgrade immediately creates an adverse match-up against Aqua's holy power, then Wiz's draining touch (FinalP439–447). More power is not equivalent to usable advantage. No general inability, universal immunity, exact level, unlimited magic reserve or competence outside supplied domains follows.

Vanir's first failure to locate him has a possible blocking explanation, not a proven universal shield; later divination works. His report to the army receives later attribution from Serena. Actual intelligence acquisition does not make every inference sound.

## 6. Decisions and conditional reconstruction

One bounded generator is earned: coercion is conditioned by perceived standing and connection to Vanir. Dust's solicitation gets an intimidating response. Small's identified demon status initially recruits suspicion of Vanir's interference, but Duke explicitly lets her go because of that connection. With Vanir himself he requests noninterference politely and backs away, while retaining his claim to decide the challenge (DUST05P395–406; MAIN13FinalP341–342). Absolute demon hatred or uniform fearless aggression misses these variations.

This is not unlimited patronage obedience. Duke insists he will not withdraw from the Wiz dispute merely because Vanir might object (C3P441). He has not been given a direct prohibition from Vanir in the Small scene, and no established irresistible enforcement device is shown. The rule therefore predicts a narrower change in treatment, not automatic surrender of his central project.

Other transitions remain explanatory hypotheses with lower generality. Defeat precedes the costly lich conversion; threatened destruction precedes the improvised romance. These show that a declared aversion and earlier self-presentation can yield to immediate power or survival. One escalating climax does not establish a separate all-context law about sacrificing every conviction or lying whenever cornered. No additional rule is padded from the sequence.

## 7. Relationships and particular recipients

Wiz is researched opponent, officeholder to displace, wrongly assumed admirer and eventual possible survival resource. Her refusal, actual power, town attachment and humiliation have their own causal roles. Her protective escalation follows the threatened town attack; it is not adequately described as retaliation for romantic rejection. The last Explosion follows another disclosure and decision.

Vanir is a recognized powerful figure whose connection constrains Duke, including his treatment of Small. Small's own aim is to promote the supposed marriage for her imagined shop opportunity. She is not sent by Vanir merely because Duke suspects it. Dust is an attempted extortionist who retreats under a test cover. Duke sits down after the cover; the source does not reveal whether he sincerely believes it or permits a face-saving exit.

Kazuma is first dismissed, then temporarily accepted as a knowing lich associate, later misread as a strategist who deliberately surrounded Duke. Aqua is a congenial stranger and trivia companion before her clerical clothing and fallen-angel disclosure invalidate their impressions. Her divinity claim is dismissed, not acknowledged. None of these people receives all the others' private knowledge by default.

## 8. Ordinary life and personal history

Duke repeatedly drinks alone at the tavern, accepts a drink after Dust's reversal and pays for Aqua's drinks (C3P363–364; C4P360/405; DUST05P370). Their later games and trivia settlement are Aqua's account. The source gives ordinary sociability and finite transactions alongside military ambition. It supplies no full income, home, diet, daily routine, prior partner or enduring hobby hierarchy.

The reported long journey, research and training show his claimed investment in this particular goal. They do not expose all the decisions during those years. His unpaid-wages analogy is an interested justification for using sacred power after rebellion, not a verified divine employment contract. A reconstruction should preserve the grievance without inventing its administrative truth.

## 10. Comedy, seriousness and consequences

The courtship comedy depends on referents and information: accurate access to words can coexist with a false interpretation, and apparent courage can be appraised under a goal Duke never had. Aqua's successful-looking seduction is actual rapport about strange creatures and shared hostility. The lich upgrade is an actual transformation with immediate unfavorable consequences, not a nonliteral power-up exempt from world mechanics.

The military report and town threat also matter outside the joke. At FinalP372 Duke says he sent a report and predicts Axel will become a key target. Wiz attacks after hearing the threat. In MAIN15P783 Serena says the report arrived, while P788 expressly says the town was already targeted before this attention. Duke's contribution must not be inflated into the sole origin of the campaign.

## 11. Counterevidence and competing accounts

A uniform bully account underpredicts restraint around Vanir's connections; a uniform deferential subordinate account underpredicts the continued challenge. Ordinary threat avoidance and known-power respect remain strong rivals to a distinctive personal mechanism. A single recognized patron may not generalize to other names, institutions or invented social hierarchies.

A sincere-suitor account fails the explicit job correction. A universal antiundead principle fails his own lich decision, but that does not establish that every expression of hostility was fake. An omniscient investigator account fails the job/courtship and Aqua-identity gaps. A general fool account fails real information gathering and capability. Each extreme erases a different observed choice.

MAIN14C1P7 calls him repelled. MAIN15P780 calls him missing, alongside the received report. MAIN17U011P84 has Kazuma retrospectively call him blasted to death by Wiz. Preserve that later attributed death description. It is stronger than saying later sources never mention a lethal result, while still providing no new witnessed return, restored career or independent permanence examination. Do not edit earlier notes to make that later knowledge appear available sooner.
