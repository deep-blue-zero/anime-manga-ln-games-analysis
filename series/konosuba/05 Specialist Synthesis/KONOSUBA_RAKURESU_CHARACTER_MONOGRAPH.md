# Rakuresu — courtesy, protective duty and a reputation she does not choose

## Identity and evidential limits

ラクレス introduces herself as a Crusader at YORI-01 U024 P50–52, `text/part0022.html`. Rakuresu is a working Roman label. She is the visiting knight, distinct from Darkness. Similar appearance, occupation and self-introduction are used for comparison within the episode; they do not establish a shared person or identical desires. The [cohort index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_YORIMICHI01_VISITORS_COHORT43_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) binds the complete 472-paragraph context. The source is Kazuma-narrated; her disclosed past and reported earlier deeds retain their access layers.

## Courteous engagement and willingness to revise a plan

Rakuresu apologizes to the apparent boy for Ozuma's intimidating approach and asks him not to fear the man (P50). Her introduction at P52 names her livelihood and asks his name. Courtesy is attached to an immediate interpersonal task: keeping the conversation possible while acknowledging the companion's effect. It is not proof that the recipient's assumed identity or the subsequent account is true.

She asks about Kazuma's reputation using タナカ殿 at P88. The account of an unfair debt evokes anger, and she actually strikes the table at P102–103. Kazuma’s suggestion that she is fundamentally earnest remains his interpretation; her anger does not independently establish the reported injustice. Her agreement that the party should leave him undisturbed and return to its town is explicit at P116. That is a proposed change based on what she has been told. Its premise is compromised by Kazuma's selective history and alias. Her later participation in the confrontation and work means that the initial intention did not simply settle the visit. Do not convert her sympathetic assent into either an actual immediate departure or knowledge of the concealed facts.

Her own later explanation, alongside Megumi, identifies a reputational grievance (P425–427). Resemblance in names and professions led the visitors to suspect impostors; that is their interpretation, while the reported exceptional victories make the comparison consequential. This is an independent practical interest in public identification. It can coexist with concern for others; it is not merely jealousy of greater power. The untagged comparison turns at P207–211 remain unallocated unless their speaker is actually fixed.

## Protection as a choice, not an inferred completed feat

The guild's thanks at P255 reports Rakuresu saving someone in danger. The present passage supplies acknowledgment, not the full rescue mechanics. During the wolf encounter she sees the risk that some animals will pass the front line and reach the rear magician. She offers to buy time with Ozuma and actually readies her shield (P344–345). Darkness interrupts and goes forward herself, explaining the risk from Megumin's spell as well as the wolves (P346–347).

Rakuresu's action therefore supports willingness to take a protective position and some appraisal of the rear line's exposure. It does not show her failing while tanking, surviving Explosion, or being unable to protect anyone. Darkness's comparative estimate is another character's judgment. A stronger replacement may make a particular plan unnecessary without retrospectively nullifying the volunteer's agency. Her assessed danger and proposed allocation are distinguishable from the strategic bluff Kazuma presents elsewhere.

## A personal project behind the professional role

At P444 Rakuresu says she is a surviving knight of a territory destroyed by the Demon King's army. Restoring the house and avenging companions and residents are her stated purposes. The wording does not establish that she is the sole survivor, personally noble by birth, already restoring the land or successful in revenge. It also does not make Ozuma's lost companion or Megumi's grandfather her own biography. The three reports explain different investments in the journey.

At P467 she recounts an unwanted approach that presumed humiliating sexual preferences and says she struck the person down. This is her report, not a directly watched confrontation or a warranted injury measurement. It gives the reputational confusion a specific cost and records resistance to the imposed identity. Darkness's desires must not be projected onto Rakuresu. The unwanted approach and the reported violent response both remain in the account; neither is erased to make her a uniformly decorous contrast.

The actual sendoff at P453 shows the visitors leaving on their stated further journey. The house, territory, private recovery and future handling of harassment remain unresolved. There is little direct evidence about leisure, household work, finances or intimate preference beyond the explicit rejection in this account. Professional commitment should not be expanded to fill those gaps.

## Voice, relationships and the comic comparison

Rakuresu's printed voice uses 私, a measured apology and a profession-based self-introduction (P50–52); タナカ殿 makes a different social move from Ozuma's direct request. Her rear-line warning explains a causal risk before offering herself (P344), rather than merely declaring courage. The later personal disclosure at P444 and matter-of-fact account of the unwanted approach at P467 expose stakes the polished introduction did not reveal. These patterns do not mean she is polite in every emotional state or that every similar quartet line is hers.

Her conduct toward the assumed young informant, Ozuma, the endangered rear line, Darkness and the people named in her former duty differs with the relationship. Courtesy and force can coexist without a contradiction requiring a hidden personality. The doubles joke makes Darkness's comparison uncomfortable; it does not confer Darkness's durability, fantasies, family or future decisions on Rakuresu.

## Scope decision

The direct social, protective and reputational choices, together with her separately attributed life project, justify a source-concentrated Tier 2 monograph. A generative model is not yet earned: ordinary courtesy, occupational training, survivor obligation and local humiliation remain overlapping explanations, and several consequential deeds are reported or preempted. A later source that shows how Rakuresu balances house restoration, a new dependent and her own safety could meaningfully change this assessment. No model or hypothetical first is created for her in this cohort.
