# Wiz: capable help, chosen company, and value without a reliable price

## Authority and the person in scope

Wiz (ウィズ) earns a scoped Tier 3 candidate because the evidence supports independently chosen work, protective commitments, a particular partnership, real refusals, technical expertise, affective change and consequential mistakes. Her [model](../06%20Character%20Modeling%20and%20Reconstruction/KONOSUBA_WIZ_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md) specifies conditional mechanisms; the [index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_WIZ_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) separates locators, perspectives and limits. A detailed model at this boundary is compatible with provisional predictive reliability and an unfinished wider census.

She is the same subject across the human Archwizard in MASK's origin narrative and the later lich shopkeeper. Those phases have different resources, risks and social roles. A later remark contrasting them is not a controlled experiment proving that lichhood caused every change in temperament or commercial judgment. Her undead status is consequential, not a comprehensive explanation of her personality.

The source planes differ. Human Wiz narrates the origin chapter herself. Vanir narrates much of the established shop material, often selecting facts for superiority, irritation and emotional reward. Kazuma supplies most cited main-series observations, and can fail to know what happened after losing consciousness. Direct speech by Wiz, performed action, reported history and another person's motive attribution must remain distinct.

The core uses seven already received complete MASK BASE-TEXT units, 2,862 slots, and a current bounded MAIN04 C5 check of 439 slots. The MASK reception is inherited from the shared source audit, not newly performed again for this character. The 3,301-slot sum describes eight member scopes, not independent episodes, a second whole-book reading, ruby/image reception or source validation. Other accepted derivative and late native routes retain their declared limits. No source text archive or raw image is published with this analysis.

## Human adventurer and lich owner: continuity without a simple conversion story

MASK's human-origin account presents an effective and sometimes aggressive adventurer. Wiz searches, recognizes a threat, attacks and revises tactics; her companions retain their own roles and objections. She can use advanced magic and still mis-model how a tool interacts with a particular opponent. The Teleport scroll and the barrier function according to their stated constraints; the failure lies in target or plan assumptions rather than universal magical incompetence. (MASK U021 P45–169,276–350.)

When companions face a curse, her intervention is costly. Her attempted final spell is not completed; offering her soul, the price refused by Vanir, the alternative technique, available foresight he does not use, and the later report of recovery remain separate acts and accounts. The narrative does not supply a safe, freely chosen solution to every participant at every moment. Her willingness to bear a price is real without establishing a taste for all suffering or permission to spend anyone else's life. (U021 P379–556; U023 P1–27.)

MAIN04 gives a later comparison through Hans: he remembers an Archwizard who hunted his side rather than seeking negotiation. Wiz responds that her former understanding was narrower. That is a meaningful self-account in a particular encounter; Kazuma's speculation about the earlier personality is less direct. Current kindness does not erase prior force, and prior force does not make current negotiation a fake persona. (C5 P270–286.)

Her owner role matters in its own right. The shop is not merely the site at which Vanir performs competence. She chooses stock, imagines customers, works, borrows, makes offers and values the shared environment. Her technical interests and company preferences can produce both good services and bad decisions.

The early accepted main reports add ordinary service and a different battle role. MAIN01 lines269–275 describe Wiz helping neglected dead whose families lack access to priestly service, Aqua's categorical attack and Kazuma's contextual intervention. Those are current derivative routes; they do not supply a fresh native paragraph or certify every reported spirit's history. Kind action and undead status are compatible without making every later act harmless.

MAIN02 lines195–245 routes synchronized Explosion, restricted registered Teleport destinations, the unstable-core decision and incompatible divine mana. Wiz is a substantial outside specialist rather than a single-purpose healer or a copy of Megumin's identity commitment. Kazuma's chosen responsibility for a random destination is distinct from the resource constraint and the later physical consequences. These phases are inherited accepted evidence, not new independent trials or a new whole-novel reading.

## The civilian boundary is conditional, explicit and actionable

The Hans encounter distinguishes recognition from a decision to fight. Wiz initially greets a familiar person and discloses information that defeats his cover. Kazuma calls the disclosure unknowing and then leaves natural versus deliberate intent open. Her exuberance is observable; a hidden master plan is not. Hans identifies their noninterference arrangement, while she asks for negotiation because the people present are friends and the springs matter. (MAIN04 C5 P240–284.)

The decisive transition follows Hans's admission that he consumed the actual manager. The consumption is not newly witnessed, and the manager has no supplied personal name here. Wiz actually freezes Hans and the contaminated spring, then states her neutrality condition: it applies to those who do not kill humans outside combat roles. She expressly distinguishes adventurers and knights, whose work includes reciprocal lethal risk, from the uninvolved manager. Her quiet sadness and accusation of his lack of fault belong beside the force. (P408–432.)

This is stronger evidence than a general label of compassion. It identifies what changes her appraisal of an actor she previously recognized as a colleague. It also prevents two overextensions. She is not a universal pacifist, and she is not simply an unrestricted Demon King loyalist. The condition she states has a specific in-world role classification; it is her moral account, not a legal doctrine or proof that every classification is correct.

Hans refuses, frees himself and continues toward the final spring. Her resolve is real without guaranteeing immediate victory or control of his body. Later freezing, his use of detachable pieces and the exhausted arrow supply create another problem. The opponent has his own counteractions. A moral boundary is an action-generating commitment, not an unlimited power. (P437–495.)

## Help can be skilled, finite and dependent on others

Wiz's abilities deserve domain-specific treatment. She diagnoses access problems, recognizes Hans and his capabilities, freezes a high-threat target, cuts only the contaminated pipe segment and supplies cooling during Aqua's dangerous effort. These are different contributions, not one scalar called intelligence. Her warning about actual bodily contamination is ignored; the resulting choice is Aqua's. (MAIN04 C5 P20–21,251–254,453–455,499–505,565–567.)

She states that she lacks enough mana to freeze the enlarged Hans and later qualifies what she might do if his size is reduced. Her advice is withdrawal, grounded partly in what Aqua's followers would lose if Aqua herself were hurt. She continues helping while making the limit explicit. A model that treats polite care as limitless availability would miss the actual refusal of the demanded technical outcome. (P522–525,565–588.)

The final sequence uses several providers. Kazuma authorizes a mana draw short of death; the drain and his loss of consciousness occur. The later account of freezing all remnants is retrospective to his narrative, since he was no longer continuously watching. Darkness shields and later provides vitality through Kazuma's Drain Touch. Casting mana and the vitality that stabilizes Wiz after purification are different source terms and phases. They are not a single infinite energy meter. (P611–648.)

Aqua's purification nearly destroys her, and the party must travel by carriage because the expected Teleport is unavailable. That adverse cost remains even though the town was defended. Her dependence does not invalidate her earlier competence; her competence does not remove the need for compatibility, supply or rescue. (P656–663.)

The promised replacement of Darkness's gifts also needs phase precision. She later holds repurchased gifts; the replacement outcome is actual by that point. The shopping scene, Kazuma's promised participation and delivery or approval by the intended recipients are not shown. A no-delivery caution must not become a false claim that no repurchase occurred. (P582–585,635–636.)

## Shop judgment: real quality can have the wrong customer

The repeated commercial difficulty is not that all goods fail to work. In MASK U011, Wiz defends high-quality manatite. The actual price/reward comparison shows that a technically effective solution can be uneconomic against alternatives. U013's frog-related product has a functioning capacity and a price badly fitted to the ordinary reward. A customer does not buy abstract usefulness detached from budget, frequency, risk and cheaper methods. (U011 P7–17; U013 P10–14.)

Her attention can favor what an item might do for an imagined user: protect a loved one, solve a memorable problem, provide an attractive magical function or meet a sympathetic need. That focus can exclude the operating conditions that make the use worthwhile. MAIN07's dangerous pendant is Vanir's reported example and an offered sale, not a witnessed customer death or completed sale. Its intended protective story and its actual described danger differ. (MAIN07 C5 P206–210.)

Property, capital and tax misunderstandings add another kind of error. A fantasy of passive income is not a completed financial plan. After explanation, borrowing and repayment are actually reported, with interest remaining a cost; a corrected local act does not demonstrate a permanent global improvement. Conversely, recurrence does not justify saying that she never hears or acts on new information. (MASK U013 P35–40,477–481; U015 P3–40.)

There are favorable economic outcomes too. Holy water is actually sold in U015. Later stock loss does not undo those sales; successful sales do not certify every new batch, ingredient or handling method. The visible aftermath and Wiz's explanation of a blast have different directness. The model must allow a useful product, a viable sale and a later avoidable loss to occur in the same business.

The source-specific mechanism concerns how technical enthusiasm, imagined benefit, generosity and weak operating economics interact. A new scenario must specify what she knows about customers and constraints rather than requiring her to repeat a famous stock gag.

## Kindness can begin in a misunderstanding and become deliberate

MASK's extortion episode prevents retrospective omniscience. Wiz initially intervenes under a mistaken understanding of what the men are asking. Their withdrawal is real, but the protective meaning is not initially her known intention. Once actual extortion is explained, she objects and asks Vanir to help. The changed information matters. (U019 P178–216.)

A later attempted mediation with Aqua does not succeed. Wiz then fetches Kazuma, another actual provider, rather than solving everything by rank or spell output. Requests, refusal, recruitment and eventual protective action remain separate phases. Wanting to protect the succubi does not give her authority to agree to every risk or contract on their behalf. (U019 P509–542.)

This pattern supports care with epistemic limits. She can be sincere before understanding the situation, revise her practical response when it becomes clearer, and seek appropriate help when her own route is blocked. It does not support a universal belief that all people are harmless, perfect foresight about recipients or an automatic correct judgment from benevolent intention.

Her role-based willingness to use force against Hans is also contrary evidence to an indiscriminate gentleness rule. Useful warmth and dangerous combat capacity are not alternating true and false personalities. They are capacities organized by information, relationship and a particular boundary.

## Vanir: desired company, actual control and an option to refuse

The paid-exclusion scene gives Wiz a particularly important independent choice. A profitable arrangement would keep her out of the shop for a long period. She refuses, values working together and imagines companions eventually returning. The choice is not merely a mathematical error. It identifies company and a shared place as goods a price does not fully replace. (MASK U011 P23–43.)

Vanir subsequently names both friend and dream as reasons to continue. That is his valuation, not hers, and his later choice to seek court work after another loss prevents a promise of permanent presence from being inserted into her world. A valued relationship can be unequal, unstable and costly.

His competent work also includes ridicule, overwork, coercive methods and interruption. Her wish to share the shop does not constitute assent to all those practices. Nor does her forgiveness in the origin episode authorize what happened to Rosary or Brad. She can retain the relation, reject one arrangement and fail to prevent another harmful act without those choices becoming logically inconsistent.

Her particular attachment should not be reduced to confirmed romance, maternal caretaking or passive dependence. The evidence establishes work, concern, disagreement, wanted company and a shared aspiration. It does not uniquely isolate their values under every irreversible future conflict.

## Other relationships have different affordances

Aqua is recognized and addressed respectfully, while her powers create actual risk for Wiz. Respect does not mean Wiz never warns, objects or asks to retreat. Even direct pleading may fail to alter Aqua's goal. That asymmetry is important for both models: one person's relationship script does not supply the other's compliance.

Kazuma can provide practical explanations, compatible resources, negotiation and skill-mediated support. His aid has costs and permission phases; the MAIN04 mana draw is expressly authorized, whereas no general entitlement to his life force follows. His narration can recognize her abilities while missing private motives or offstage details.

Human Wiz's companions are actors rather than a reason for her heroism alone. Brad restrains, proposes methods and retrieves a host; Rosary resists, attacks and has her own reactions. Wiz's later tolerance cannot stand in for their views. The unnamed later visitors in the epilogue do not acquire invented names, marriage or parentage from resemblance.

Customers, neighbors, ghosts and vulnerable petitioners may recruit help or favorable terms, but the precise request and response must be sourced. A widow's romantic inference about Vanir is not a joint commitment endorsed by Wiz's questions. Children remain age appropriate and nonsexual; a helpful adult's interpretation never becomes a child's implied agreement.

## Affect and Japanese voice: polite does not mean powerless

Wiz's ordinary written speech often uses polite requests, explanations, apologies, respectful names and an inviting shopkeeper stance. Her excited recognition of Hans can override the conversational purpose other people are pursuing. Polite form alone does not establish tact, low confidence or consent to the listener's position.

The moral confrontation is quiet and controlled. The question at MAIN04 P425 states a recalled condition rather than asking Hans to grant permission. The account of the manager's innocence uses restraint and sadness while actual magic imposes a limit. Formality can intensify an accusation. This is a change in communicative purpose and affect, not just a different sentence ending.

MASK U019 adds a less saintly contrast: an age-related insult elicits an actual threat before the mood changes. Own pride and irritation remain part of the person. A practical objection, self-defense, enthusiasm about a product, embarrassment and a serious conditional accusation should not all sound like one timid apology.

These are written Japanese-derived functions, not a measured stutter rate, audio performance or command to scatter honorifics through every English line. L2 role and pragmatic contrasts matter; katakana names and spell vocabulary are not themselves L3. In translation, preserve who is making a request, asserting a condition or offering care before choosing a surface voice.

## Ordinary aspiration and comic amplification

Wiz wants a viable shop, meaningful stock, shared work and a future that can include people she remembers. Her willingness to keep working despite poor outcomes is evidence of effort and desire, not proof that effort must produce income. Caring about function and caring about sales are compatible while the bridge between them fails.

Canonical comedy can amplify a purchase, partner's reaction, sudden revelation or the contrast between frightening power and awkward business. The underlying errors remain portable: imagining a use without pricing the context, assuming a tool fits its target, or accepting a local explanation without modeling operating costs. Technical capacity should remain impressive where it is actually established.

Literal harm, possession, imposed labor, mana depletion and near disappearance remain literal in their fictional setting. H3 requires gag-dependent weak transfer; H4 requires nonliteral staging. A transfer to a realistic world must not preserve resurrection, lich durability or instant magic as an unearned human ability. It can preserve care, stipulated expertise, polite warmth, context-limited forceful boundaries and the temptation to overvalue a helpful-looking solution.

## Rival accounts, adverse evidence and the current interpretation

A global fool model fails at advanced practical action, explicit limits and successful warning. A helpless subordinate model fails at paid-exclusion refusal and firm opposition to Hans. A universal pacifist fails at her stated combat distinction and actual force. An always successful healer or unlimited helper fails at depletion, blocked mediation and need for other providers.

A simple charity model is incomplete too. Chosen company, pride, remembered companions, project aspiration, product enthusiasm and social mistakes are independently relevant. Her own human narrative, Vanir's irritated framing and Kazuma's gratitude have different access and biases. None alone can supply a complete impartial portrait.

The strongest current account is of a technically capable and affiliation-valuing agent whose care can become costly, whose concrete moral boundary can displace noninterference, and whose imagined usefulness often outruns operating economics. She can refuse and act sharply without abandoning her ordinary warmth.

Unresolved questions include how far the civilian condition generalizes beyond Hans, how financial learning transfers between products, the threshold for challenging Vanir, and the ranking of company against irreversible security loss. Exact costs, reserves, offstage outcomes and every dispersed appearance remain limited by source access. Hypothetical contrasts may reveal whether a proposed simulation respects those limits; they cannot create new evidence that the person will always choose that way.
