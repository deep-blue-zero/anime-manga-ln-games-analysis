# The expanded ensemble: cooperation, unshared purposes and the unfinished work after success

## 1. The quartet is one durable relationship inside a larger task network

The [main-only party synthesis](KONOSUBA_PARTY_ENSEMBLE_SYNTHESIS.md) explains how uneven capacities become useful through actual availability, role selection, resources, execution and recovery. The expanded corpus preserves that account while making its larger social system harder to overlook. Dust's party, Megumin and Yunyun, the dancers, Wiz and Vanir, royal attendants, guild workers, clients and temporary allies have their own projects. They do not exist merely to complete one of Kazuma's missing functions.

The consequence is a distinction between three kinds of relation. A fitted handoff can accomplish a narrow task. Particular company can be valued beyond its output. Permission and obligation can constrain what one participant may demand of another. A successful scene need not establish all three, and one cannot stand in for the others. Familiarity supplies useful knowledge and exploitable vulnerabilities together; a helpful result does not prove a shared account of the problem.

This is retrospective expanded synthesis within the existing specialist home. The main-only body, R1 contracts and first outputs, four unchanged-source scorecards and later source-local inquiries remain independently retrievable. The [four-book reconciliation](../04%20Checkpoints%20and%20Model%20Validation/KONOSUBA_WITHHELD_SOURCE_VALIDATION.md#four-book-unchanged-r1-reconciliation-and-stage-gate) does not pool its overlapping dispositions into independent accuracy. The later complete editions and component programs add literary responsibilities beyond those tests, without becoming pristine new holdouts.

## 5. Gifts, payment and care have recipients with their own terms

MASK's linked enterprises repeatedly expose a mismatch between an offered benefit and the recipient's actual wish. Wiz wants returning companions and shared work, Yunyun wants particular company with selection rights, Iris wants recognition through an achievement, and Luna wants rest, pay and a meaningful adult relationship. A useful service can be wrapped in deception or attach an unasked obligation. Later gratitude or friendship is not prior assent to the omitted terms.

The same distinction shapes the FD producer relationship. A trial and a later accepted role are real agreements despite concealed expertise and original culpability. That role does not permit every room entry or contact exercise. Cielo's bare-skin boundary survives payment and another person's medical-sounding rationale. Lia's request for particular companions differs from replacing a convenient singer, yet her protective exclusion also constrains their choice. Later voluntary cooperation is a new phase, not proof that an earlier no was secretly yes.

Payment similarly requires its own completion. A promised fee is not a receipt; debt is not forgiven because the operation succeeds. Vanir's production, Wiz's labor, Chris's commission, transport, equipment and ordinary food have providers and costs. The FD ending's agreement to pay preserves unshown individual transfers. DUST07's unshown Wiz mana aftercare and MAIN17's unresolved treatment of Mitsurugi remain recovery gaps; later examples of competent healing elsewhere cannot fill them. The old E03 dispositions retain their exact predicates and rival readings rather than being silently rescored from a favorable group summary.

This account also makes ordinary company analytically necessary. Food, shared work, leisure, chosen gifts and household routines are not just symptoms of tactical dependency. They help explain why an efficient alternative can be less desired. They do not establish an unconditional reunion rule: actual departures, changed bonds and competing obligations remain. Nor does affectionate membership define the permission for every act within it.
