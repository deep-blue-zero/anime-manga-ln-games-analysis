# The expanded humor system: successful means, misfitted benefits and the authority of a performance

## 1. More viewpoints preserve the causal system and change whose costs are visible

The [main-only synthesis](KONOSUBA_HUMOR_SYSTEM_SYNTHESIS.md) finds comedy in abilities that work, competing purposes and incomplete accounts of consequence. The expanded corpus strengthens that account through new protagonists, work settings, staged performances and source formats. Megumin's power is real before it provides a livelihood. Vanir's commercial perception can identify a market and miss an expiry problem. A dancer's particular friendship can matter more than an efficient replacement. A scripted newcomer can receive useful help while revising an uncomplicated judgment of the helper.

These are not repetitions of a single incompetence joke. They distinguish the immediate effect, the task that effect was meant to serve, the next state, the recipient's actual wish and the actor's account of responsibility. The expanded source map also changes access: another narrator can reveal a concealed purpose without creating a second event, and an illustrated wish can represent desire without instantiating its outcome.

This is retrospective literary synthesis. The main-only body, all original H/L records, forecasts and source-local adjudications remain unchanged. The admitted novels, edition components, artbooks, fanbook and TRPG materials have individual authority dispositions. An actually read component is not a newly performed scenario, and a complete source inventory is not a population of independent jokes.

## 2. A tool can function exactly as described and still be the wrong solution

MASK provides unusually precise examples. An organism-only scroll acts on Wiz rather than the constructed target. A strong barrier blocks attacks in both directions rather than giving the protected user a one-way advantage. A cooler can function and show current profit while arriving after summer, with expiry defeating the apparently obvious next-year storage remedy. These are different failures of target model, constraint integration and timing. Calling the products defective would hide the actors' actual assumptions; calling Wiz globally incompetent would hide her earlier precautions, successful spells and fitted recovery work. ([MASK reading](../10%20Expanded%20Corpus/Masked%20Devil/KONOSUBA_MASK_DEEP_READING.md), §§3–6.)

The prequels make the same distinction material to an aspiration. Explosion can defeat the immediate target while exhausting movement, requiring another provider and breaking a seal. A successful hunt can produce no sale; praise for a peak output can conceal the repeated defensive work that kept a group alive. Megumin's desire for an impressive repayment can even make existing successful help feel insufficient. The joke does not require her art to be false. It requires a mismatch between the desired meaning of contribution and what the operation actually needs. ([EXP01](../10%20Expanded%20Corpus/Explosions/KONOSUBA_EXP_V01_DEEP_READING.md) and [EXP02](../10%20Expanded%20Corpus/Explosions/KONOSUBA_EXP_V02_DEEP_READING.md), §§3–6.)

FD's producer plot adds known constraints and unfinished settlement. The cart is ordered spared before it is destroyed; learning its valuable cargo afterward enlarges the loss without creating the original breach. Merchandise exists and sells while costs, provider fees and destroyed venues remain. A relic's activation condition, protective gloves and a later barrier solve different parts of a battle. Final success cannot certify that every earlier choice or unshown payment was correct. ([FD reading](../10%20Expanded%20Corpus/Fantastic%20Days/KONOSUBA_FD_DEEP_READING.md), §§3–6.)

The portable mechanism is an actor modeling one useful transition and neglecting another. Its source-world implementation may depend on magic, class rules, particular commodities or a revived body. Structural portability does not make those means available in a realistic setting or preserve the same ethical cost of risk.

## 3. A benefit can miss what the recipient wanted

MASK's clients and workers repeatedly prevent the transaction from being reduced to money or kindness. Wiz wants shared work and returning companions, Yunyun wants particular company with boundaries, Iris wants an achievement before reunion, and Luna wants rest, wages and a meaningful relationship. A nominal benefit can publicize vulnerability, attach unasked labor or replace a wish with an easier supplier-defined goal. Later genuine gratitude does not retrospectively disclose the omitted terms.

FD likewise makes particular membership stronger than interchangeable function for the dancers. Lia's cherished fox has relational value without proof that it speaks or is her powerful artifact. Her changed voice and Daniel's imitation have different sources. Kazuma's material help is real, and so are hidden culpability, unpaid obligations and his use of contact after a known limit. The comedy can invite enjoyment of an energetic producer while the narrative also exposes the account he withholds.

This recurring construction is not a universal fraud theorem. Some offers are clear and accepted, some help is genuinely fitted, and recipients make their own decisions. The question is which term, cost, identity or purpose is actually shared before the next act. A gift, a performed job and a full negotiated relationship are different accomplishments. Mixed motive does not make a beneficial act imaginary; a beneficial result does not settle permission.

## 4. Authority changes owners within a sequence

Expanded protagonists do not replace Kazuma as permanently reliable correctors. Megumin can recognize an impractical arrangement and protect her aspiration through selective hearing. Vanir can identify another person's mistake, exploit the ensuing emotion and misjudge information withheld until the final line. Wiz can execute a working spell under an incomplete target model. The Core guide's newcomer can revise an impression that Kazuma's self-presentation invited.

The fanbook Medusa owner is especially revealing. She has her own enterprise, concealed weakness, practical learning and selective objection. Kazuma's useful advice makes him a teacher whose methods can alarm the pupil. The crystal gives represented access to particular attacks, rather than omniscient narration. His final hope that she returns safely does not complete the threatened ending. The separate comic's blast has a different source identity. The joke's changing hierarchy must not become an invented rescue or death. ([C51 index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_COHORT51_ART_REFERENCE_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md); FAN-MEM S096–099.)

TRPG material changes the performance contract again. The guide uses tutorial appraisals and expectation correction; supplied scenarios prescribe branches; players, PCs, GM and reference narrator have distinct roles. Boren's fair-fight appeal protects the device sustaining his own advantage. The later tank can strike surviving Boren after control loss without a human feeling of revenge or an ethical judgment on his coercion. Reading the script does not play the reversal. ([C52 index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_COHORT52_TRPG_REFERENCE_GUIDE_SCENARIO_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md).)

## 5. Comic presentation does not remove knowledge, harm or refusal

EXP02's known pet-risk choice precedes the later mass-danger argument. Outside distraction and Yunyun's catch change the eventual geometry; they do not make the earlier intention safe. FD's death and revival preserve the first-timer's grief and the preceding sacrifice. MASK's particular protective contract and repeated physical intervention do not authorize every later demand on the weaker recipients. These are causal and relational facts the joke uses, not unwanted seriousness imposed from outside the text.

The same discipline preserves uncertainty. The dead pit goblin in Core and the later felled/shrieking goblin have different outcome evidence. A fanbook alarm is not a performed blast. Repeated Kobold Hero labels do not establish one individual's resurrection. ([C49 index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_COHORT49_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md), C49-L01.) Each outcome needs its own route rather than one franchise-wide survivability assumption.

## 6. Format, language and behavioral warrant are separate axes

H1–H4 concerns the warranted use of behavior and world events. H3 requires dependence on a local gag rather than serving as a synonym for uncertain attribution, weak confidence or paratext. H4 is actual nonliteral license; magic, resurrection or a static imagined scene does not automatically qualify. A wish can be evidence of an expressed preference without being a completed event. An analyst must identify which proposition receives the disposition.

Cheers recomposes Main04 assets, including a split wide image, without creating a second adventure. Blessing's album leaves its photographer unknown. A thousand-floor dungeon belongs to an illustrated wish. The fanbook's comic, prose stories, interviews and drawing-song address have different represented speakers, creators and outcomes. No single image count certifies psychological variety, and no mechanical CRC/OPF check supplies semantic reading of an image.

L0–L3 asks what the selected joke needs from structure, phrasing, pragmatics or Japanese form. EXP02's misread written accusation makes a script contrast material while leaving only one actual accusation. By contrast, a wrong English rank or currency conversion is an analyst error, not itself proof that the source joke is L3. An ordinary idiom's literal mistranslation also cannot establish central Japanese-form dependence without a genuinely exploited double meaning. Whole-premise portability and a nested form hinge can coexist.

## 7. What an expanded generative account should preserve

The durable system combines actual capacities, competing valued purposes, uneven knowledge, shifting authority and consequences carried by other people. Its reusable patterns include a solution that worsens the next state, an apparently generous substitution that misses a particular wish, a truthful identity that fails socially, a formal utterance used to hurt and a capable corrector who becomes the next mistaken actor. None requires every turn to repeat a famous gag.

Ordinary competence and neutral continuation remain necessary. People can cook, ask a fitted question, refuse a task, work for pay, care for a particular friend or enjoy a familiar evening without entering a spectacular mode. A realistic transfer must change resources and risk where magical repair is absent; a canonical mode must retain literal source-world powers without treating them as unlimited. Both must preserve the actor's actual information and the recipient's agency.
