# Otohime: fear, security and protection that does not settle the debt

Status: scoped Tier 3 monograph for the Core and Advanced TRPG edited replays. The [provisional model](../06%20Character%20Modeling%20and%20Reconstruction/KONOSUBA_OTOHIME_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md) owns generative hypotheses and the [cohort 31 index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_TRPG_REPLAY_COHORT31_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) owns exact source boundaries. Density supports interpretation; it supplies no independent capability grade.

## Identity and evidential position

御手洗音姫, read みたらいおとひめ, is the male nineteen-year-old replay PC introduced in Core S020. Otohime's player is 長月達平; that production fact does not join the PC to a ReZero protagonist or give him the other work's history. Construction dialogue, stated motives, GM adjudications and performed protection have different status. The optional Death Return ability is a local game option with consequences, not evidence of actual human death or a main-novel continuity event.

## Chronology

Core moves from character creation and a cheaply chosen lifestyle into a party pact, livestock investigation and Froger battle. Otohime initially presents a self-preservation limit, then incurs real game costs protecting Chiho and actually activates Death Return after the dangerous hit (S029, S074-075). Advanced grows the PC, records purchases and borrowing, adds comfort spending and brings the earlier relation into a requested counseling scene. He later protects Gilbert and the house, survives the final 82 damage without Death Return, and joins the five-person welcome. One return in Core and no return at that later climax are separate outcomes, not repeated uses inferred from danger.

## Motives and competing interests

Otohime seeks safety, useful preparation and comfort. His own Advanced explanation emphasizes fear, regret about not doing enough and earlier failure to model his own death or shared-area risk (S086-088). Actual protection makes that explanation plausible in part. It does not independently certify his only motive or erase Suite lodging, continued borrowing and a prior push whose meaning Chiho contests. His interest in the house also matters: protecting it serves the party and an asset he values. Care, fear and self-interest need not exclude one another.

## Affect and regulation

Fear is not merely an external insult. He speaks about it, prepares for it and uses protective skills under it. Earlier he limits his promise to protecting Chiho while he can survive (Core S029); the battle later takes him beyond an easy version of that assurance. Gilbert's premature mourning is not the actual endpoint: Otohime rejects a permanent-death ending and chooses the costly local return. After21's questions he acknowledges a communication gap and thanks it. That response does not show fear cured, debts ended or all conflict resolved. It is evidence of a local willingness to explain.

## Competence and mechanics

His shield, Covering and allied support can make protection effective, but not effortless. Core S078-080 shows a failed roll by Chiho, Otohime's Covering and Gilbert's Shield canceling the residual. In ADV S099 Otohime fumbles his own defense, protects Gilbert, receives Saint Shield support and still takes the source's small remaining damage. In S104 the final effect deals magic damage but is not a magic attack, so Wind Curtain does not apply. He chooses to Cover the house; 41 residual becomes 82 and he survives (S105). A model that grants universal interception, painless protection or invulnerability would discard these typed constraints. His success is also a team product, not personal defense alone.

## Decisions and errors

Preparation can be thoughtful and still incomplete. His own account admits a failure to consider the shared-area case. The source preserves an earlier push, his limited promise and reciprocal jokes about killing and saving; none by itself establishes a stable murderous intention or a secretly pure protective plan. During the last self-destruct, the party jokes about rescuing Goran and attacking him later; that option is not performed. Otohime instead protects the house. He can prefer not dying to a costly revival even though the latter is available. The practical choice should be represented before a morally flattering interpretation is assigned.

## Relationships and communication

Chiho gives material resources and expects the relationship to mean more than that. The2700 KE transfer in ADV S025 is actual, not merely promised support. Later he willingly talks to 21, explains fear and then spends Voice Mute and Wind Breath scrolls, costing800 KE, to communicate privately with Chiho (S085-088). The expenditure is real and gives the explanation weight, but money spent cannot require her acceptance. She retains doubt and a health-invulnerability claim. He explicitly says future borrowing continues; the renewed Oath therefore does not mark debt resolution.21's mediation is requested, accepted and bounded; it is not omniscient access to his motives.

## Ordinary life and resources

Core's cheap lifestyle declaration contrasts with the Advanced Suite, while Chiho and Gilbert remain Economy (S023; ADV S025, S031, S108). The recorded scrolls are useful preparation, but comfort is an additional benefit. Otohime appreciates the retained house and powerless gems as decorations, and participates in calculations: net2530 KE each after upkeep becomes4530 after guild apology compensation (S105-106). These are local receipts, not evidence of a lifetime budget or settled ownership of every shared resource. Bedding explains the within-house quality distinction; an entirely separate building is not required.

## Japanese voice

The native 音姫 and printed reading control the name. Otohime can use direct, colloquial tactical declarations, nervous explanations, joking complaint and an earnest account of fear. Calling the bartender's milk refusal a thug performance is his evaluation, not an objective identity finding about that NPC (ADV S032). His acceptance of the newspaper differs pragmatically from Gilbert's grand revelation or21's trust claim (S077). An English transfer should preserve the distinction between a joke, limit, admission and resolved action without importing another author's protagonist voice. No audio or exhaustive register reconstruction is available.

## Comedy and serious stakes

Protection becomes comic when the supposedly fearful borrower is difficult to damage or when a house receives the heroic interception. The comedy depends on real game contingencies: failed checks, purchased tools, allied reduction and Covering's extra loss. Death Return has a stated cost and is actually chosen in Core; the last Advanced success avoids needing it. These are not equivalent comic beats or proof that danger is unreal within the fiction. A serious transfer can retain preparation, resource dependence, fear and costly aid without literally reproducing revival or treating every sacrifice as altruism.

## Countercases and rivals

A pure coward model misses repeated protection; an ideal self-sacrificing guardian misses comfort spending, the explicit survival limit and continued dependence on Chiho. Generic party optimization, the Oath pact, desire for recognition, a valuable house and player spotlight all rival a single fear-to-care mechanism. Fear can coexist with an actual costly choice, while an actual choice cannot retrospectively cleanse every prior act. The useful representation keeps appraisals, paid resources, target of protection and costs separate, and allows an inadequate explanation as well as a successful one.

## Gaps and warranted depth

The replays support conditional hypotheses about bounded protection, security spending and partial communication repair. They leave long-term lending boundaries, sustained loss of allies, irreversible danger without a return option, unobserved private motives and durable changes after counseling underidentified. The final survival is one local outcome; future debts, repairs and adventures are not supplied. Source-known hypothetical outputs must retain failures and assumptions as data rather than revise this model to fit the first response.
