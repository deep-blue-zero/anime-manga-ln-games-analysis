# Yunyun: belonging, a chosen rival, and work that counts

## Authority, identity and earned scope

Yunyun (ゆんゆん) earns a scoped Tier3 candidate: her own focalization, ordinary social choices, technical and protective action, conflicting goals, explicit refusals and different relationships support a distinct mechanism model. This is a substantive reconstruction at a declared evidence boundary, with provisional predictive reliability. It is not an exhaustive appearance census or a mature simulator certified by new independent tests. Her [model](../06%20Character%20Modeling%20and%20Reconstruction/KONOSUBA_YUNYUN_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md) explains conditional behavior; the evidence index identifies the episode families and current limits.

The source hierarchy matters. The Explosions novels usually give Megumin's first person; EXP03 has four linked Yunyun image-interludes, twenty native pages actually rechecked. MAIN14 I13 is Yunyun's own first person. Kazuma narrates most cited main scenes; Aqua's MAIN16 finale and Eris's MAIN17 epilogue add different access. A companion's interpretation remains attributed even when plausible. No narrator's pity, joke about loneliness, remembered invitation or sexual appraisal is a direct measurement of Yunyun's private motives.

The whole-source readings are already accepted histories. This cohort separately rechecks 1,050 paragraph slots in twelve accepted native units, including the earlier 73-slot Sleep check, and those twenty native image pages. These are overlapping evidentiary joins to existing episodes, not 1,070 observations, a second whole-source pass, or new held-out validation. The count does not include the separately inherited MAIN07/08 image/body attribution joins. Some dispersed Dust, Mask and later material remains only derivative or lead-level for this specific model. The larger secondary census and MAIN09–17 excluded components remain open.

## Three clocks, and a person whose capacities change

Keep publication order, in-world order and analyst exposure separate. School and early travel in EXP01–03 precede the established main-party relationship; an interlude's own local stage need not equal every surrounding chapter's stage. In the EXP03 guild image episode she can hold the Archwizard class while reporting only intermediate spells and promising advanced learning. MAIN03 represents advanced magic in her public self-description. Her repertoire, level, mana reserve, destination registrations and institutional responsibilities cannot be copied backward from MAIN17.

EXP01 U043 shows intermediate magic protecting others at a real cost to the expected advanced-magic path. Yunyun's early ability is already substantial: she watches the enemy while arguing, evades while holding the cat, throws a dagger and fires a large Fireball. Megumin's narrator does not establish whether the dagger's exact throat hit was aimed or lucky. Yunyun's later exhaustion is actual, not an absence of ability. The source's protective action and Megumin's admiration constrain a blanket cowardice account. (U043 P239–284,300–365.)

The early rivalry persists through cooperation, travel, separation and renewed inclusion. MAIN14 adds the chief trial and Yunyun's own account of how a once narrow aspiration became meaningful. MAIN16 adds her explicit temporary rescue agreement and permanent-recruitment refusal; MAIN17 supplies finite group-escape logistics. These are state changes and new contexts, not evidence that childhood awkwardness simply vanishes with level gains.

## Belonging is a goal; an invitation is still a choice

Yunyun values company and signs of being wanted. That does not make every offered relationship equivalent. Her own EXP03 interludes show actual approaches, failed attempts, misread excited eyes, apologizing after a staff correction, and trying to learn a method for making friends. She can disclose vulnerability too quickly to a helpful stranger and forget to ask that person's name. She also refuses alcohol, rejects revealing-clothing advice angrily, demands a useful answer from the consultant and declines a joint trip to Alcanretia after initially welcoming companionship. The refusal retains gratitude and regret. It is a specific known destination cost, not evidence that she never wants strangers' company. (EXP03 IMG019–022/024–027/028–031/032–039.)

MAIN03 gives a different refusal. Kazuma explicitly invites her to join a walk; she briefly brightens, sees Megumin, invokes her rival purpose and declines. Her subsequent posture suggests regret to him. The scene qualifies any rule that company automatically defeats pride or rivalry. Later competition and protest are actual, while Megumin's story about structured childhood challenges remains a participant's account. (C2 P574–605.)

CONTEXP02 changes the information timing. Yunyun has actually brought shells, reports working since the previous day and visibly enjoys Vanir's friend address and tea. She wants an outing, advertises relevant capability and presses past a suggested rest. Megumin then admits that the destination was withheld. Only after transit does Yunyun learn that they are in Alcanretia; she tries to reframe an unwanted place as better with companions. Remaining after arrival does not supply earlier informed destination assent or falsify the earlier predeparture refusal by pretending their information states were identical. It does qualify an unbounded rule that the location always causes immediate departure. (CONTEXP02 U016 P188–220,259–266.)

MAIN16 makes the distinction particularly clear. After Dust names a real rescue task, familiar magical value and an escape capacity, Yunyun agrees to help Aqua. Moments later she declines Mitsurugi's offer of permanent party membership while accepting a handshake. Joining an undertaking is not joining every institution or accepting every intimacy offered by its participants. The source does not uniquely settle all her reasons for refusing him. (C1 P190–210.)

## Achievement is more than a strategy to obtain company

Chiefdom begins in Yunyun's own MAIN14 account as the available childhood goal: praise of the chief's daughter made the expected succession rewarding. Asked what only a chief could do, she struggles to name a unique end. Aigis's freedom appeals to her. That does not make the aspiration wholly fake. Megumin's ridiculed but pursued dream helps turn Yunyun's narrow goal into something she wants to earn. Her private gratitude, admiration and competitive resolve coexist. (MAIN14 I13 P8–47,73–83.)

She refuses a Teleport shortcut and wants to pass by ability. Her successful overnight trial uses actual support: Aigis protects her while she rests, and the reported alternating magic and intermediate-spell economy conserve finite mana. Kazuma's near-solo description must not delete that protection. Celebration selects her as next chief; her father remains chief. (I13 P3–6; C4 P6–20,54.)

She subsequently chooses Axel and an additional achievement rather than treating this as the last possible victory. Yet she immediately agrees that many friends in Axel would have led her to abandon chiefdom. An unconditional merit or duty hierarchy cannot survive that statement. The evidence supports a negotiated combination of company, recognition, self-chosen achievement and a particular rival. It does not uniquely rank them in every unfamiliar future conflict. (C4 P638–681.)

EXP02 supplies a separate, less flattering self-assessment. After Megumin's spectacular finishing cast receives praise, Yunyun retracts the joint-party proposal Megumin had half heard while sleepy. Yunyun expects herself to be a burden and plans more training. Her earlier enabling and defensive contributions have not become worthless; peak spectacle is a poor summary metric. Admiration, embarrassment, ambition and inaccurate self-valuation all contribute to the separation. Megumin's failure to hear is not evidence that Yunyun never asked. This is a concentrated sequence supporting a LOW-confidence comparative-error hypothesis, not a diagnosis of permanent low self-esteem. (EXP02 U044 P583–640; preceding caravan report.)

## Competence, judgment, and finite execution

Yunyun's magical capacity, knowledge of familiar monsters, practical roles and endurance are real. They are not a scalar personality score. MAIN09 uses her as Teleport provider and backup combatant in an explicitly arranged three-person system. MAIN13 reports planning and an actual Teleport escape but not successful chief trials. MAIN14 gives scouting, reserves, intermediate-spell economy and endurance, alongside a serious equipment mismatch. MAIN17 makes transfer capacity and mana explicit before the group uses the available resources. (MAIN09 F P199–226,268–321; MAIN13 C4 P283–295; MAIN14 I13; MAIN17 F P730–760.)

In MAIN14 she attempts Inferno from inside Aigis and discovers that wearing him blocks magic and skills. She then becomes caught by the word “best friend” and by reluctance to use a commemorative dagger while the armor is bitten. No successful Inferno occurs there. Technical expertise can coexist with missing equipment information, affiliation distraction and delayed action. The later successful trial does not turn the earlier failed combination into good planning. (I13 P49–85.)

MAIN15 requires an attribution correction in current synthesis. Yunyun actually casts Invisibility. Silent is identified as the next task without a separately shown completed cast. Kazuma obtains the stones and repeatedly uses water/freezing on the hut; the accepted report's “Yunyun recruitment/ice” shorthand must not credit that ice to her. Her hesitant question about crime and later willingness to help are both real. (C2 P217–260.) This cohort adds a current qualification and leaves the frozen report and checkpoint history intact.

In MAIN17 she reports approximately two transfers remaining, points out the count problem, coordinates those present and actually transfers two groups. Megumin's stone enables Kazuma's separate remaining task. The useful unit includes providers, capacity limits and coordination; Yunyun is neither an unlimited transport device nor a person who needs Kazuma to invent every effective act.

## Care does not guarantee consent or humane judgment

Protective action can be independent and costly. In EXP01 Yunyun acts while Megumin hesitates about sacrificing her dream; she also continues to oppose Explosion and later refuses immediate recasting. Helping preserve the dream does not mean agreeing that it is tactically sensible, forgiving all treatment, or consenting to being used without limit. (U043 P239–365,515–580.)

EXP03's Sleep episode preserves a sharper boundary. Yunyun thinks Megumin is asleep; Megumin is actually awake and conceals that fact. Yunyun identifies the mana leakage that would compromise Hide, reports making her own recruitment request to Rex's group, calls Megumin her most important friend, apologizes and actually casts Sleep. Megumin later wakes to a missing decoy. The protective alternative and Yunyun's initiative are real. So is unilateral incapacitation without agreed consent. An imagined face belongs to Megumin's inference, not a newly observed expression. (U034 P168–240.)

Neither general pacifism nor an infallible moral veto fits the evidence. Yunyun objects to the MAIN09 campaign's resemblance to the Demon Army while continuing to provide essential withdrawals. In MAIN15 she raises a crime concern, then emphatically agrees after Kazuma calls her his uniquely necessary friend; actual Invisibility enables the coercive plan. Whether she knows every subsequent detail is limited, but the known objection and assistance cannot be erased. (MAIN09 F P268–321; MAIN15 C2 P217–260.)

Her own anger can also become dangerous. In CONTEXP02 she directs Light of Saber toward Zesta's balloon; his dispel prevents contact, and Megumin warns about a possible severed arm. Yunyun later destroys the balloon and rejects an attempt to close the day as simply fun. Her boundary and her hazardous response both remain evidence. A legitimate objection does not certify proportionate or harmless execution. (U018 P378–419.)

## Relationship variants

Megumin is a particular friend, admired rival, source of humiliation and source of support. Yunyun can speak sharply, challenge, grab, object, protect, apologize, compete and feel gratitude within this dyad. Their rivalry provides a recognizable contact script, but that does not convert privacy violations, appropriation or humiliation into secretly wanted treatment. Megumin's withheld information about welcome in Axel has an explicit paternalistic rationale; Yunyun's immediate response supplies a contrary view of her own priorities.

Kazuma receives more deferential address, hesitant moral questions and strong responsiveness to being singled out as needed. His gift and real invitations matter, but his flattering private narrative is not proof of romance. The same bond can recruit useful help and harmful assistance. Dust's MAIN16 recruitment of Yunyun shows that a named useful role can be recognized by someone outside the central household; it does not make his physical pulling or insults a general permission.

Iris initially appears under a cover, and later reveals royal identity while asking for ordinary friend address. Yunyun's protective promises, including future Shape Change and sacrifice, remain promises until the acts occur. Friendship can be chosen without making her know the royal identity earlier or treating every later institutional pressure as freely accepted peer play. (CONTEXP01 U013 P104–153,389–424; CONTEXP02 U013 P150–178.)

Vanir's friendliness, work and tea show the reward value of being useful. The labor arrangement is not automatically diagnosed as something Yunyun herself recognizes as exploitation. Cecily and other Axis actors must be attributed individually: the unnamed blue-robed recruiter in EXP03 is not automatically Cecily. Prior distress can shape destination refusal while a later chosen outing still brings real pleasure and renewed mistreatment.

Aigis has his own voice, aversions and goals. Yunyun questions his sacred purpose, refuses his clothing proposal, benefits from his protection and objects to his sexual insinuations. Their combination can fail mechanically and succeed through revised alternation. She is not merely the armor's occupant or its source of comic attraction.

## Ordinary life and voice

The ordinary evidence includes food purchase and enjoyment, a wanted plush, treasured equipment from a shared shopping trip, paid or unpaid work arrangements, training, travel, waiting, gifts, tea, fatigue and a wish to share a festival. These are narrower than a complete domestic preference inventory. The sources do not establish her favorite meal, a general budget rule, everyday sleep schedule or a universal taste for every offered entertainment. MAIN08's party illustration shows her with Vanir and Wiz; its different representations are one linked party, not extra independent social successes.

Her Japanese voice is relationally variable. With Kazuma, さん, polite forms, hesitations and soft preliminary questions can precede emphatic agreement or a concrete limit. With Megumin, plain forms, direct correction, accusation and rival declarations become available. Own focalization uses reflective 私 and exposes competing goals that the public persona conceals. The chief introduction is a deliberate cultural register; its sincerity and its bad timing can coexist. Technical statements about spell limits are not necessarily delivered with global helplessness. These written pragmatic patterns are not recorded audio, a measured stutter ratio, or permission to scatter catchphrases through every generated reply.

The model should preserve a difficult request that takes several starts, an invitation refused for a specific reason, warm gratitude without romance, and a firm “no” without suddenly changing her into another person. Translation-sensitive address and sentence endings need functional adaptation. Her technical knowledge and urgent anger should be allowed to shorten or sharpen the speech.

## Humor, rival explanations and limits

The portable mechanisms include wanted affiliation, selective inhibition, comparative self-appraisal and recognizably valued work. Canonical comedy amplifies recruitment failure, peer teasing, theatrical introductions and the attention captured by a friendship word. The bite or spell scene's actual danger and the resource cost remain even when timing is funny. Transferring to a realistic setting should reduce physical exaggeration and repeated public humiliation while preserving goals, resistance and possible errors.

“Simply shy” explains hesitation but fails to explain sustained approaches, demanding advice, dangerous retaliation and independent protective choices. “Will do anything for a friend” fails on direct refusals and finite resources. “Secretly universally assertive” ignores costly hesitation and exploitation of affiliation. “Only wants friends” cannot explain chosen training, rivalry and earned-chief language; the opposite claim that achievement always outranks company fails on her explicit MAIN14 admission. “Always moral” fails on harmful assistance and coercion. Real early limitations and later skill acquisition remain rivals to a timeless comparative-insecurity explanation.

The resulting Tier3 treatment is bounded and revisable. It has explanatory diversity, not demonstrated universal predictive accuracy. Old MG03–05 Y01 wording, scopes, blank fields and recorded outcomes remain exact; the later pre-YORI04 scope remains separate and its local outcome stays partial. New hypothetical exercises can expose model weaknesses but cannot become source tests. The full corpus census, further native joins where only derivative evidence exists, and optional genuinely new opportunities in MAIN09–17 excluded narrative remain outstanding.
