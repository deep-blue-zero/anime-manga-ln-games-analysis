# Scoped authority adoption of deferred secondary monographs

Thirty-two existing monographs and studies from cohorts 44–48 and 51–52 have received explicit current provisional authority at their recorded scopes. The [machine record](KONOSUBA_DEFERRED_MONOGRAPH_AUTHORITY_ADOPTION_01.json) binds every original argument, added prefix, acceptance/foundation route and controlling qualification. This adopts reviewed existing arguments; it adds no primary reading, independent observation, new character identity, tier or capability grade.

The authority reviewer received each complete body and checked its introduction, substantive middle, exit and major actor/relationship references. The author received the complete 32-item proposal and controlling C46 qualification, verified the preserved published bodies and accepted this bounded adoption. Neither role treats CI or a historical hash alone as evidence of literary accuracy. Cohort context totals remain shared inputs, not individual decision or unique-person denominators.

The original complete argument in each file is retained byte for byte below the new prefix. Earlier pending-at-authorship statements are historical, governed by the later acceptance and containing publication. Explicitly immutable model/rule/first-response files are outside this administrative tranche. Source-required unnamed actors retain their actual identity limits.
