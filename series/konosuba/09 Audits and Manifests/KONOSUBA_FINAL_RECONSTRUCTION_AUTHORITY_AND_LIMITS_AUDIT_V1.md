# Final reconstruction audit: accepted versions, authority and known limits

The 77 preserved accepted public model files retain their exact hashes. The native authority graph classifies 40 as active provisional current and 37 as unclassified legacy. Those are artifact/version classifications, not77 people, capability grades or readiness findings. The [structured audit](KONOSUBA_FINAL_RECONSTRUCTION_AUTHORITY_AND_LIMITS_AUDIT_V1.json) binds every complete file, role, limit, version distinction and receiving scope.

## What the authority review establishes

The separately delegated reviewers received all 37 legacy model bodies and their actual source/freeze controls. The author received their complete role and adverse arguments and verified all 77 accepted public hashes and native classifications. The other 40 retain their already accepted scoped bodies; this audit does not claim a fresh whole-body reading of those 40 or a new primary-source pass. Missing authority fields do not imply that a legacy file is a private draft or invalid history. They do prevent its automatic direct use as current model evidence. Current qualified monographs can carry the supported interpretation without changing a frozen tested representation or creating a model merely to satisfy a registry count.

Vanir, Wiz and Yunyun require a precise version distinction: their accepted public model files contain declared historical receiving clarifications and differ from their initial privately sealed model foundations. Both versions remain pinned. A later clarification is not an old precase premise, and unchanged first responses cannot retrospectively test that later text. Complete-file hashes include YAML and line endings; analytical-body hashes after the closing YAML token and initial-private-model hashes are separate identities.

## Consequential qualifications

Angie’s historical model cannot establish an injury–Heal–recovery chain: C46-PFQ03 identifies Kazuma as the Heal recipient. Dark Stalker’s price comparison does not establish a paid bill, payer, pension debit or balance under C46-PFQ02. Their current monographs expose those operative source qualifications. C28-REF01/RG28-05 identifies Ragcraft as the failed impersonator and Kazuma as the restrained observer; current prose resolves that attribution while original model/input/first strings remain exact. Cielo’s retained historical K shorthand means Kazuma, as the existing monograph makes explicit. The knee-injured veteran’s branches retain separate knowledge and benefit states; only Kazuma remembers the routes.

## Principal suite and inference limits

The six distinct post-validation cores and 90 actual first texts remain preserved. F04-A-TR retains its typed SD1-QA-D01 actor/recipient failure; the separately marked later example is not its replacement. K04, M04 and E03 remain OPEN. Earlier Gen03 K04, Gen04 K03, Gen05 J01 and the source-local adverse findings remain historical results at their original boundaries. The [evidence matrix](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_POST_VALIDATION_MODEL_EVIDENCE_MATRIX_V1.md), [claim revisions](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_POST_VALIDATION_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER_V1.md) and [cross-model audit](KONOSUBA_POST_VALIDATION_CROSS_MODEL_CONSISTENCY_AUDIT_V1.md) own their detailed claims.

Source-known, same-assistant and visibly scaffolded diagnostics can expose internal consistency failures without establishing independent behavioral accuracy. Explicit speech or task procedures may support a tightly scoped conditional extrapolation; missing certification alone is not proof of incapacity. Conversely, fluent output, a valid authority quartet, completed reading or a passed repository audit does not establish general readiness. The evaluator must justify each exact dimension, scenario, limit and overall tier on the subject’s directly curated evidence. This author audit assigns no such grade.
