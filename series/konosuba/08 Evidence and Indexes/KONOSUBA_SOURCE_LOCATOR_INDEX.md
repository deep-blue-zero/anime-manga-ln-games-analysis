## Reproducing a route

Resolve a MAIN volume alias to its exact native filename and hash, then resolve its chapter/section alias to the exact member. Paragraph positions are 1-based and member-local over every body `p`, including blank and image slots. Do not remove empty paragraphs, concatenate split resources, or substitute sentence/search-hit positions. Base-text reading excludes `rt`/`rp` while retaining ruby base and tails; material ruby and raw typography require the separate source view. A matching base passage alone does not prove a complete raw/ruby inspection.

The member tables include 128 accepted unit starts and 9 metadata-affiliated continuation resources. Multiple members can share a chapter alias; a route into such a chapter needs its member as well as its local paragraph number. A short chapter-only alias is insufficient where it leaves two resources possible. Native bytes, member identity, narrator/speaker, scene phase and the actual access scope must be retained when rechecking a claim. MAIN03/04 explicit afterwords are excluded despite nearest-TOC positions. Complete principal coverage does not imply complete EPUB component coverage.
