# Post-validation claim revisions V1

## 1. Attribution and old authority

This companion records current integrated claims without rewriting the [existing claim ledger](KONOSUBA_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md), frozen main-only models, checkpoint forecasts, designated source-local results or their original blank fields. `REVISE`, `STRENGTHEN` and the other governing terms classify the present account. A restriction on an attractive shorthand is not an allegation that an older artifact formally asserted that shorthand. Only an explicitly identified old contract/result is treated as such.

The [current evidence matrix](KONOSUBA_POST_VALIDATION_MODEL_EVIDENCE_MATRIX_V1.md) binds the six exact tested versions and 27 contracts to their literary owners and actual diagnostic records. All changes here are retrospective and source-known. The [full-series synthesis](../07%20Full-Series%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_FULL_SERIES_SYNTHESIS.md) supplies the connected argument; source-specific readings and locators remain the authority for scene-level disputes.

## 3. What remains unadjudicated

K04 accountability, M04 grave-risk priority and E03 precise quiet selector remain OPEN. Most primary clause views in this battery were not fully prebound; nine were qualified and two literal danger triggers were removed by the transfer facts. They receive no retrospective full confirmation. The acknowledged pet-risk, shared-death and coercion histories do not disappear when another case supplies a caring or prudent response.

The revision states do not update an earlier source score, add a new generation to an old checkpoint or award independent observations. The current provisional versions carry their own derivation and failure record. Remaining source-class uncertainties—unshown endings, conditional scenarios, reported rather than witnessed acts and unresolved identity/dependency joins—must stay visible in any later use or designated evaluation.
