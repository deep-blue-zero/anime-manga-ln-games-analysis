## Decisions, deferrals and integration

Only Iris receives a new dedicated monograph and model in this cohort. Other candidates are deferred for the concrete gaps above, not rejected because they are secondary. No Tier0 or Tier1 individual assignment is final merely because a queue currently lacks consolidated evidence. Future work must split grouped queues into distinct actors and retain negative findings, low diversity, uncertain identity or inadequate information as reasons to limit an artifact.
