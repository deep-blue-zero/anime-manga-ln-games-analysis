This first scoped model concerns Zesta, ゼスタ. The [monograph](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_ZESTA_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) supplies the individual account and the [index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_ZESTA_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) binds event identity, access and action stage. This is a distinct secondary reconstruction candidate, not any of the six outstanding StageD principal/party/transfer successors.

The shared receipt covers 3,702 known BASE context slots in fifteen members/seven admitted native objects and twenty known image-prose pages. It includes other people, blank slots and narration. It is not Zesta's independent-action count, a new full-source pass, fresh global image/ruby credit or an independent test. Every source outcome informing the rules is already known to the author. Future hypothetical outputs have only disclosed internal consistency or sensitivity scope.

Four rules cover a particular devotional priority, applicable means, enjoyment and resentment, and epistemic presentation. They describe defeasible tendencies rather than exact utility weights. A generic self-indulgent cleric, ordinary religious in-group favoritism, a strong magical specialist and comic plot convenience remain serious rival accounts. The model must generate genuine useful help as well as unreliable assurances and coercion; neither side is an exception to omit.

## 3. Four conditional rules

### ZESTA-R01 — Aqua's particular welfare can abruptly reorganize attention and effort, without supplying missing means or universal charity

**Proposition.** A credible connection to Aqua can move Zesta from play, casual concern or self-interest toward focused attention, protective escalation or useful service. The concern can be genuine even while another part of his conduct remains self-serving or harmful. Priority does not guarantee competent planning, access, a safe method or equal treatment of everyone nearby.

**Support.** MAIN04 U025 P7–36 gives actual gratitude, a discretion order and acceptance of the reporting priest's proposed commercial/reimbursement route after news of Aqua. DUST04 U013 P179–190 explicitly changes his expression and declared effort when Aqua's involvement is named, before an escape exists. CONTEXP02 U018 P423–430 shows quiet concern for her wellbeing and visible relief after his own loss. MAIN16 U016 P245–327 turns Serena's threat against Aqua into cold, focused confrontation; useful information about Serena is supplied by Kazuma, not divined by Zesta.

**Contrary and rivals.** His protective mission also supplies a casino pretext (DUST04 P190); he cannot find an escape and remains distractible afterward. Concern about a deity, in-group identity or a valued patron is not unique to him. Calm concern, alarm, institutional benefit and possessiveness are not causally separated. He can give meaningful help to male Dust without an immediately explicit Aqua cue, so the rule is not an exclusive gate for all helping.

**Apply when.** The packet supplies a known credible association, message or threat and what Zesta can know about it. Track actual recognition rather than assuming every unnamed woman or religious label is Aqua. A named connection can change attention before it changes capability.

**Do not assume when.** The cue is hidden, merely reader-known, an unverified rumor treated as authenticated, or the action requires unavailable magic or transport. Do not infer a divine endorsement, secret full identity knowledge, justified retaliation or consent from his allegiance. Do not automatically protect every companion's interests equally.

**Failure and departure branches.** Focus can become threatening, coercive or partial; a promise can still outrun available means. Concern can also take the quiet form of asking after wellbeing and relaxing at an answer. A chance for pleasure can coexist with the mission rather than simply disappear. An absent connection permits ordinary helpfulness but supplies weaker grounds for predicting his strongest serious mode.

**Confidence and falsifier.** Moderate for the specific within-scene switches and quiet concern, low for broad thresholds. Repeated credible comparable Aqua threats that leave his attention and chosen effort unchanged would weaken the priority claim. Repeated equal mobilization for unrelated recipients under matched conditions would weaken its particularity. Neither an unknown cue nor an impossible remedy is a clean case of indifference.

### ZESTA-R02 — Use strong relevant means when available, but do not infer reliable assurance or general planning from success

**Proposition.** Zesta can apply powerful narrow skills, recognize a matchup limit and supply useful support. He does not consistently verify the practical conditions that make a promise usable. Capacity, present access, target fit, deployment and completed task must remain separate; the model permits an overconfident assurance as well as a competent intervention.

**Support.** EXP02 U038 P317–355 gives actual Reflect and a sacred attack. CONTEXP02 U018 P380–384 gives actual Dispel. DUST04 U013 P215–248 exposes the antidote assurance and cell suppression without an actual poisoning; P330–378 gives effective anti-demon/undead combat, P395–418 acknowledges the golem mismatch and heals Dust's wound, P430–441 supplies the spear Dust uses. MAIN16 U016 P474–492 and U018 P305–335 gives actual driving, the horses' limit, finite Teleport constraints and three buffs.

**Contrary and rivals.** He makes a usable matchup judgment and provides direct healing, so universal tactical ineptitude is false. The exact point at which he discovered the cell suppression is unknown; the earlier assurance is inadequately grounded, not thereby proved a knowingly impossible lie. The dangerously thrown spear and the enemy lost to harassment show that support can create costs or be diverted. A generic powerful priest with ordinary planning lapses may explain the pattern. Source conventions supply convenient species-specific magic; realism transfer cannot preserve those guarantees without a supplied analogue.

**Apply when.** A relevant means and its usable state are established. Check whether the task is in that means' actual domain. If the character has not checked a condition, permit a confident promise without treating it as evidence that the condition is satisfied. Identify what a collaborator must still do.

**Do not assume when.** High level or office is the only basis for a spell, immunity, accurate diagnosis, unlimited resources or successful escape. Do not turn blocked access into a failed cast. Do not infer moral purity from the harmlessness of an anti-demon spell to a human. Do not give him Mitsurugi's kill, Dust's vault or a companion's supplied information.

**Failure and departure branches.** He can recognize the golem limit and delegate. He can also eat before considering provider failure, improvise an assurance, throw the right tool unsafely, or neglect a risk after a successful cast. Later recovery is real but does not retroactively make the process safe. A useful observation can be correct without proving general occult discernment.

**Confidence and falsifier.** High for the performed skill distinctions, moderate for a tendency toward unverified assurance, low for unfamiliar technical domains. Repeated comparable promises preceded by explicit checking of the relevant access/provider risk would weaken the calibration defect. Repeated failure of an established usable narrow skill under its actual supported conditions would require narrowing the skill claim. A new target outside that domain is not such a failure.

### ZESTA-R03 — Desired amusement, recruitment or resentment can displace others' boundaries and the stated instrumental objective

**Proposition.** Zesta can treat an appealing interaction, sect target or personal grievance as a license to pressure, redefine a refusal or prolong another's distress. Strong means may be used precisely toward that purpose rather than toward efficient task completion. A person he genuinely helps can still be exposed to his intrusive conduct or later abandonment.

**Support.** EXP02 U038 P366–402 gives the unproved contamination accusation, pursuit and his report of deliberately grazing Arnes to enjoy her fear; P498–507 gives actual blessing for Megumin and refusal for Yunyun. DUST04 U013 P304–310 treats Small's deferral as assent, P361–378 shows deliberate harassment and an enemy's escape, and P470–480 shows him fleeing the regular soldiers while Dust is arrested. CONTEXP02 U016 P434–467 gives the orchestrated false public confession and Cecily's restraint of the Eris priest. MAIN16 U016 P350–395 supplies direct coercive threats against Serena, with no performed assault invented.

**Contrary and rivals.** He actually heals Dust and supplies a weapon; he can show quiet concern without extorting a reward. Small's clear refusal of a requested address at DUST04 P350 remains a refusal, and fear does not remove her agency. His cruelty to Arnes during the chase is a report rather than a separately narrated blow-by-blow observation. Generic hedonism, in-group prejudice and exaggerated comic escalation remain rivals. No single preference for dominance or submission explains every case.

**Apply when.** A desired opportunity, personal grievance or valued sect distinction is available to him. Keep the recipient's words and behavior separate from his interpretation. Retain the instrumental goal as a potentially competing motive rather than the automatically winning one.

**Do not assume when.** A person's appearance, orientation, clothing, work or species is being substituted for an actual harmful act. Do not create intrusive contact simply because the character is present. Do not infer that every emergency triggers cruelty, or that others privately welcome what they reject. Do not complete a threatened sexual or physical act absent an actual performed event. Retain harmful material at a non-graphic analytical level.

**Failure and departure branches.** He may bargain, flatter, reinterpret protest, retaliate selectively or squander a task advantage for amusement. He may equally provide real support when doing so is useful or locally wanted. Protection and coercion can alternate without either becoming insincere by definition. A recipient or ally can interrupt him, escape or refuse; the model must not guarantee his control.

**Confidence and falsifier.** Moderate for the actual recipient-cost displacement and local goal competition, low for its frequency or a stable global value order. Repeated matched opportunities in which he forgoes an appealing imposition to preserve an explicit refusal or the task's safety would weaken the displacement claim. A case where no such opportunity is available is not evidence that he has changed. Useful help alone is not a falsifier of this conditional coexistence.

### ZESTA-R04 — Present a favorable account of knowledge, role and intent; distinguish that account from what he actually knew

**Proposition.** Zesta readily gives a playful, authoritative or devotional account that credits his role, insight or purpose and can evade a practical or ethical objection. Sometimes this is knowingly improvised, sometimes a mistaken reading and sometimes a supported claim. Printed courtesy and impressive outcomes cannot adjudicate which branch applies.

**Support.** DUST04 U013 P120–158 supplies a private admission of retaliatory blame and playing along, followed by a public claim that he had detected Ragcraft all along. P217–248 gives an exposed unsafe assurance and deflection. P266–306 contrasts a seemingly exemplary introduction with coercive uptake. CONTEXP02 U016 P439–461 invents another priest's confession despite her denial; U018 P25–81 avoids the evident named-note route and blocks a police report.

**Contrary and rivals.** His actual direct magic, correct golem limitation and useful weapon judgment are not mere rhetoric. The quiet wellbeing question is supported by a visible affective response. Ordinary face-saving, sect ideology and a generic boastful official could account for the presentation pattern. Another character's suspicion cannot prove an undisclosed private motive; Dust explicitly leaves Small's identity recognition unresolved at DUST04 P458.

**Apply when.** An audience and a claim about knowledge, purpose or obligation are specified. Store the private admission, observable evidence and public account separately. A later successful outcome cannot be moved backward to create earlier knowledge. A title can be accurately stated even when its implication is misleading.

**Do not assume when.** The only evidence is his confidence, a high rank, narrator admiration or a favorable ending. Do not turn a named charge's withdrawal into innocence of every different act. Do not treat follower doctrine about death or Japan as independently verified fact. Do not let a polite claim that a deferral means yes overwrite the recipient's actual answer.

**Failure and departure branches.** He may invent an explanatory flourish, deflect a challenge, misread flattering evidence or acknowledge a narrow practical limit. His self-presentation need not be internally consistent across audiences. A genuine concern can use the same courteous form as coercion, so the generator needs content and context rather than a permanent sincere/insincere style switch.

**Confidence and falsifier.** High for the explicit private/public Ragcraft contrast, moderate for broader favorable framing, low for unobserved mental causes. Comparable cases in which he consistently names the accidental basis of apparent insight or candidly owns an available evasive excuse would narrow the claim. Clearly established correct prior detection is a contrary instance to an all-accusations-are-bluff account, but does not erase the demonstrated bluff.

## 4. Generation order and permissible failure

Begin with Zesta's actual information and the current opportunity, constraint and recipient. Determine whether a credible Aqua connection changes his attention. Then identify real available means and what remains unchecked. Allow a promise to precede adequate checking where the evidence supports fallibility; do not silently make the promise true. A relevant spell can succeed even when the aim or process is harmful.

Next consider enjoyment, grievance, recruitment and institutional face as competing motives. They may redirect a powerful means or produce a social bid. Preserve the recipient's response outside his control, including refusal, fear, escape or intervention by another person. Choose the public account independently of the analyst's knowledge. It can be partly true, playful, overclaimed, evasive or candid on a narrow point.

Stop at the required action stage. A healing can close a wound without repairing trust. A proposed payment is not a transfer. A mission's success does not entail safe means, consent or full recovery. A threatening statement is an actual threat but not every threatened act. If multiple actors contribute, name their separate acts rather than letting Zesta's prestige absorb them.

Do not force him to narrate all these distinctions. An English body in which he explicitly weighs every risk, respectfully obtains every permission or admits every self-serving motive may be more coherent and ethical than the source. An always-useless or always-cruel body can fail by deleting his demonstrated practical help and specific devotion.
