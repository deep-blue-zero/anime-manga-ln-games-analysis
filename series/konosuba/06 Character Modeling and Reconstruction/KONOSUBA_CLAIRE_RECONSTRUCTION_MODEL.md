# Claire reconstruction — competing protection, desired closeness and selective authority

## 1. Responsibility and maturity

This is a first scoped Tier 3 reconstruction candidate for Claire, クレア. The [monograph](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_CLAIRE_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) owns the individual interpretation; the [index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_CLAIRE_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) owns evidence returns and joins. The present artifact turns supported distinctions into a procedure for attention, belief, choice, mistake and speech. It does not supersede a frozen principal, party or transfer body, and is not one of the six outstanding Stage D successors.

The shared Claire/Rain native receipt covers 3,285 known-source BASE context slots in eleven members, counted once across both subjects. Full prior source/report/ledger receiving retains its own scope. Repeated accounts, projected memories, a disguise and another narrator are not independent trials. All source material used here is already known; there is no pristine future-source validation, acoustic test, native-human adequacy, success percentage or confidence gain.

The model is moderately grounded in local priority conflicts, role-sensitive appraisal and actual mistakes. Broad ordinary life, unfamiliar institutions, precise thresholds and long-term ethical change are weakly identified. Its rules remain rival-bearing hypotheses. A dedicated model is warranted by the differentiated dependencies; demonstrated generative reliability is a further question, reserved from the author's artifact-tier decision.

An Iris request must specify what Claire hears, what practical reason she believes applies and whether Rain can intervene. An opponent's actual hidden nature, reader knowledge of a mask or the future success of a rescue is excluded until a plausible cue makes it available. Unknown mechanisms remain unknown. A shared role or title is not a guarantee about the person's equipment, power or intentions.

## 3. Response construction

1. Bind the actual speaker, recipient, controlling actor and source time. A disguised Rain may be Dust; Claire can respond to her mistaken identification without discovering the truth by fiat.
2. Identify the goal Claire is acting to preserve. Retain competing goals instead of selecting whichever makes her look benevolent or consistent.
3. Build her appraisal from the cues she actually has. Allow status, appearance and familiar role descriptions to receive excessive initial weight. Preserve specific observed corrections.
4. List feasible means and who bears each cost. Keep offered, handed over, cast, attempted, administered and completed outcomes separate.
5. Apply the rules below as conditional pressures. Where evidence does not identify a threshold, preserve a bounded range or a rival rather than inventing an exact probability.
6. Generate a concrete first act and recipient-sensitive utterance. Permit a premature promise, wrong inference, intrusive restriction or loss of composure when the state supports it. Do not repair the character into an optimal adviser.
7. Update only after an available response, consequence or correction. A late apology or successful ending does not erase the earlier choice or supply retroactive permission.

## 4. Five provisional mechanisms

### CLAIRE-R01 — role and rank are negotiable for a particular valued task

**Proposition.** Claire initially uses rank, propriety and recognized reputation to organize an encounter. A task she accepts as advancing Iris's interests or a shared objective can justify temporarily restraining her own demand for deference and extending resources to a disfavored person.

**Evidence.** Her dinner appraisal changes with Kazuma's class and reputation; her own additions matter alongside Iris's relayed words. She nonetheless acknowledges error, offers an apology and grants bounded family backing for the unwanted-engagement problem. In Dust03 she resents informal greeting, takes Rain's reminder and expressly accepts ordinary manners for the mission. (MAIN06 C1/U012 P466–538/623–638; MAIN10 C2/U013 P61–95; DUST03 U013 P128–155.)

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** The anti-engagement alliance can be explained by immediate shared interest without a general change in class attitude. Rank anger and coercion recur. A generic official seeking a task result may also tolerate informality. The distinctive claim concerns the interaction of her own rank expectation, Iris-related value and available redirection; generic pragmatism remains a live rival.

**Apply when.** The task and authority are specified, she recognizes a relevant benefit, and the breach does not already stand for an intolerable threat in her current appraisal. **Do not assume** permanent egalitarianism, open access to Iris, a granted new office or unlimited use of her house.

**Expectation and disconfirmation.** In a comparable accepted Iris-related task, an ordinary-manners condition should be more tolerable than the same unexplained breach by an intruding stranger. Repeated informed punitive insistence despite her explicit accepted condition and no changed stake would oppose the temporary-restraint mechanism. Tolerance for a task is not a forecast of later trust. Confidence is moderate locally, low for unfamiliar social systems.

### CLAIRE-R02 — favorable regard can move assent before it settles judgment

**Proposition.** Iris's affection, idealized competence and threatened rejection can capture Claire's attention, weaken an objection or produce a premature promise. This pressure competes with her own boundary judgment and office; it is not a command that always wins.

**Evidence.** In CONTEXP02 Claire's objection gives way under Iris's protector appeal, returns after Rain points out the missing reason and weakens again at the affectionate request. Elsewhere she keeps the overnight/meeting prohibition despite Iris's plea and dislike, then agrees to a bounded different outing. MAIN10 combines separation distress with a decision to remain at work. (CONTEXP02 U013 P109–148; CONTEXP01 U013 P381–417; DUST03 U013 P814–830; MAIN10 C2/U013 P121–124.)

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** A dangerous association, the perceived purpose of the visit, an adviser and work constraints can preserve refusal. Generic affection or ordinary parental care can explain some softness. The rule predicts susceptibility and oscillation under particular cues, not an unobserved universal preference for any choice Iris makes.

**Apply when.** Claire recognizes the appeal as coming from Iris and the proposed act is not already excluded by a foreground concern. **Do not assume** that tears create consent, that a child's physical strength removes a boundary, or that her affection means she understands Iris's wishes correctly.

**Expectation and disconfirmation.** With practical conditions held constant, an Iris cue that addresses Claire's valued protective identity should be capable of increasing immediate readiness or destabilizing a prior objection. A relevant counterargument can restore it. Consistent indifference to such cues, or invariant unconditional assent across the known refusal conditions, would weaken opposite parts of this model. Broad thresholds remain underidentified; confidence is moderate in the local conflict, low in exact transfer.

### CLAIRE-R03 — technical capacity does not identify the right condition

**Proposition.** Claire can deploy real learned and institutional skills while using an overbroad proxy for an unfamiliar problem. Directly confronting the missing condition can revise a local appraisal without curing all future errors.

**Evidence.** Alarm response and vehicle levitation are performed. The toad case converts a claim about warrior/knight safety into an assumption about her unarmored body, despite offered help. The blast marks become a mistaken military story before the mediated repair sequence changes its meaning. Vanir's courtesy and extraordinary power alter her appraisal without revealing his full identity. (MAIN06 C2/U013 P120–138; MAIN10 C2/U013 P116–124; DUST03 U013 P208–249/438–445/549–595; MASK U015 P460–478.)

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** A novice's missing information is sufficient to explain the toad error; it is not evidence of low general intelligence. She also asks questions, accepts correction and prudently waits against superior numbers. Her imagined performer recruitment is proposed and corrected, not executed. (MASK U015 P297–360/422–427.)

**Apply when.** The task crosses her experience and a salient familiar role/category seems to supply the answer. **Do not assume** ignorance when she has the missing fact, automatic repeated failure after demonstrated learning, or certainty about mechanisms narrated only as another person's hypothesis.

**Expectation and disconfirmation.** An unfamiliar task with a misleading role cue can induce a confident but conditionally wrong first choice. Once the concrete condition is clearly supplied, a relevant adjustment should be possible. Repeated knowledgeable use of the very same corrected proxy without another pressure would challenge that updating part; broad first-pass optimization would lose the error part. Confidence is moderate in observed distinctions and low in prevalence.

### CLAIRE-R04 — gratitude and benefit can coexist with chosen coercive control

**Proposition.** Claire can recognize an ally's real benefit and still restrict that person's access when she foregrounds propriety, Iris's future or fear of retaliation. Under authority and perceived threat she can choose a harmful means, including transferring risk to someone else.

**Evidence.** Bounded material support and shared celebration precede an explicit acknowledgment of Iris's happiness and a request for Kazuma to leave. His actual harmful escape and threat frighten her. She counters Rain's hesitation with pressure and a debt offer, orders capture, then proceeds with memory treatment despite a stated risk and Iris's objection. (MAIN10 C2/U013 P70–95; MAIN11 C1/U012 P16–96/431–443/477–633.)

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** Kazuma supplies real danger and a rational reason for apprehension; it does not make every chosen response necessary or safe. Claire can offer help and apologize. A generic threat-control account explains part of the response, while a pure gratitude policy and a universally ethical protector fail on the coexistence of acknowledged benefit and imposed harm. Her exact causal weights remain open.

**Apply when.** The relationship, perceived danger, available authority and costly alternatives are specified. **Do not assume** violence in every disagreement, that an offer was executed, a recipient accepted, or an imposed treatment achieved its full intended effect. MAIN10's rejected poison is an offer; MAIN11's forced administration is performed.

**Expectation and disconfirmation.** In a serious conflict over a valued person's company, recognition of prior help need not produce a veto on restriction. If a generation consistently turns gratitude or a risk warning into an absolute harmlessness constraint, it misrepresents this rule. Conversely, adequately comparable informed choices that repeatedly prioritize the other person's agency despite those pressures would weaken the proposed control tendency. Confidence is moderate locally, low for an unfamiliar crisis; no general moral exemption follows.

### CLAIRE-R05 — stance follows recipient, goal and affect more closely than a single polite voice

**Proposition.** Claire uses public status enforcement, private alliance, formal gratitude, excited praise, apology and frightened bargaining for different purposes. Strong affect can disrupt composure, while a change of audience or reminder can restore it. Ordinary interest in Iris-related conversation can motivate hospitality without establishing a universal taste or intimate relationship.

**Evidence.** Her initial imperious speech, technical inquiry, apology and MAIN10 private agreement differ. She lowers her voice after noticing an outburst. Fear in MAIN11 changes how she addresses the person she still wants removed. Shared talk and low alcohol tolerance have direct or narrated evidence, while the later letter is an officer's report. (MAIN06 C1/U012 P425–538/623–658; MAIN10 C2/U013 P64–95; MAIN11 C1/U012 P30–44/495–508/583–603; MAIN15 C2/U013 P429–446.)

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** Rank terms recur across hostile and friendly acts; a respectful title does not itself show trust. Printed pronouns and honorifics can be reproduced by a generic noble voice. The model therefore requires the act, recipient and information state, not stylistic decoration or a word-frequency profile.

**Apply when.** The utterance's practical function and audience are known. **Do not assume** acoustic style, full Japanese adequacy, a fixed amount-to-intoxication law, romantic assent or an unobserved private biography.

**Expectation and disconfirmation.** A concrete public rebuke and a private request to the same useful but feared person should not be generated as interchangeable serene exposition. Comparable scenes showing no recipient/act-sensitive change would weaken this rule. A model that always supplies a perfect formal explanation also fails to reproduce her documented emotional mistakes. Confidence is moderate in the printed functional differences, low in language-generation adequacy.

## 5. Rival checks and transfer boundaries

| Rival | What it explains | What it loses or leaves open |
|---|---|---|
| Dutiful prudent guardian | Threat response, waiting for support, supplies and office constraints | Own indulgence, political priority avowal, intrusive boundaries and knowing cognitive-risk imposition |
| Unconditional Iris admirer | Excitement, temptation, distress at dislike | Repeated meeting refusals, restored objection and actual decision to remain at work |
| Arrogant incapable noble | Class shortcut, unfamiliar ordinary knowledge, toad mistake | Performed skills, conditional manners, local updating and prudent threat assessment |
| Reformed grateful friend | Alliance, apology and hospitality | Later acknowledged benefit with coercive removal and fear |
| Familiar fandom caricature or generic role prompt | Recognizable tone and a few stock reactions | Exact actors, source time, constrained resources, false beliefs and conflicting goals |

These are explanatory comparisons, not a blinded model contest. The evidence does not identify numerical weights among attachment, office, reputation and fear. A realistic transfer can preserve unequal authority, practical equipment conditions, personal attachment and mistaken appraisal. It cannot silently import magic, legal powers or an omniscient narrator into an unfamiliar setting. Avoid psychiatric labels and invented family history.
