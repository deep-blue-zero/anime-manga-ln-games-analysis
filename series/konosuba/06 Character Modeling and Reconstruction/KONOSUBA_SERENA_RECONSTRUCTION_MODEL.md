## SE-R01: a benefit must become a usable obligation before it supplies control

**Proposition.** Serena tends to offer or stage help and seek a recognized gratitude/debt opening that her religious power can exploit. Benefit value, existing affiliation and repayment affect whether that opening exists. Receiving help is not automatically consent, full control or an enduring obligation; an offer the recipient does not value may fail at the first step.

**Supporting evidence.** SE27-01–02: free aid, buffs, public gratitude and the admitted puppet demonstration build acceptance, while Kazuma initially refuses recruitment. SE27-04–05: actual loan repayment precedes a renewed gratitude opening and Kazuma control. SE27-15: Serena deliberately tries to create a debt through exposure, but Dust assigns it no value and remains focused on Feit. Her own complaint acknowledges that local failure.

**Contrary evidence.** Real healing occurs and cannot be reduced to a wholly imaginary service. Strong faith and repayment can limit leverage; some prior followers are released when allocation changes. Kazuma's later Regina prayer creates a consequence Serena did not anticipate (SE27-02, SE27-05–07). Failed solicitation does not prove broad immunity or recipient virtue.

**Rivals.** A generic transactional manipulator, automatic gratitude magic, sincere service creating ordinary trust, or a plot-specific reversal. The conditional account differs by preserving the recipient's actual valuation and relevant religious state. It must not invent hidden gratitude whenever an initially failed attempt later seems useful.

**Application conditions.** A specified offered or completed benefit, what the recipient understands and values, any relevant faith/debt facts, and a known opportunity for Serena's actual power. In a nonmagical transfer, only the stipulated interpersonal or institutional leverage exists. Unprovided faith is unknown, not automatically absent or strong.

**Do not assume.** That speech equals control, a gift creates sexual permission, gratitude requires accepting future commands, repayment is completed from a concession alone, or every citizen is already a Regina follower. Do not relabel Kazuma's coercive debt logic as a valid obligation. Actual loan, offered waiver, obtained funds and cash repayment are separate states.

**Confidence.** Moderate for benefit-sensitive leverage and the explicit failed Dust approach. Lower for exact thresholds, unseen religious mechanisms and other recipients. Control fractions, population counts and universal immunity remain unmeasured.

**Falsifier.** Comparable attributable encounters where unvalued or explicitly rejected offers reliably grant the same control without another opening, or repayment/faith never changes the relevant dependence despite equivalent allocation and information. Do not redefine refusal as secret enjoyment to rescue a failed prediction.

## SE-R02: public credibility and counterpart knowledge shape the usable persona

**Proposition.** Serena can switch between deferential aid, role authority, denial, private bargaining and rough anger as audience and exposure change. Public credibility is a practical resource, not evidence every kind act is false. She can keep strategizing while angry, but exhaustion and surprise can interrupt her performance.

**Supporting evidence.** SE27-01–03: formal party request and publicly useful services contrast with private alias/puppet admissions, tobacco habits and recruitment stories. During harassment, public witnesses prompt renewed clerical display and follower protection. SE27-04–05: she negotiates a truce and then exploits a new opening. SE27-13: learning Aqua's divine/Axis identity changes her appraisal and behavior.

**Contrary evidence.** The initial party request is refused, Vanir/Wiz can defeat cover, and failed exposure solicitation reveals desperation rather than smooth persuasion. Some aid is real. Her interlocutors do not always accept the intended frame, and she does not know every relevant identity or outcome in advance (SE27-01, SE27-13, SE27-15).

**Rivals.** A fixed two-faced archetype, ordinary context-sensitive politeness, panic, or narrator exaggeration. The candidate requires an actual audience or knowledge difference to justify the shift. It cannot infer that private anger is the sole authentic self or that formal language proves a deception succeeded.

**Application conditions.** Specify who observes, what cover remains plausible, which identity has been disclosed, and the immediate task. Serena initially lacks knowledge of Kazuma's resurrection and Aqua's divine identity in the road encounter. Reader knowledge is not automatically available to her or her followers.

**Do not assume.** Universal persuasion, sincere repentance from new address, trust from a shared laugh, a family relationship from a recruitment story, or a successful spell from its spoken name. Her later puppet admission qualifies an earlier anti-undead display without giving earlier observers that explanation retroactively.

**Confidence.** Moderate for observed register/appraisal shifts; lower for the private motive behind every service or for novel audiences. No accent, audio delivery or perfect social-intelligence result is inferred from printed dialogue.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable changes in audience, cover and explicit identity disclosure that never affect her framing or action, or durable accurate persuasion despite the documented exposure limits. Do not add an unprovided witness or hidden disclosure after seeing a contrary case.

## SE-R03: concentrating control creates releases, work and opportunity costs

**Proposition.** Serena can concentrate a finite control resource on a difficult target, but doing so weakens other uses and can force ordinary labor or narrower plans. She tries to manage those costs rather than obtaining unlimited followers, money and corpse control at once. A controlled subject may still resist, misinterpret or impose demands she dislikes.

**Supporting evidence.** SE27-05–07: focus on Kazuma releases prior followers, limits access to money and leaves the grave animation without sufficient capacity. She works, tries to obtain payment and asks to release him; he refuses. Adventurer-card disclosure and lack of usable commissions constrain income. Some labor is diary report, while digging and a failed animation are directly narrated.

**Contrary evidence.** She successfully controls several people before the concentration, and some public support has a real service basis. The source does not give a numerical budget or show every potential allocation. Work and tactical activity persist despite exhaustion; she is neither universally incapacitated nor unwilling to exert effort (SE27-02, SE27-05–07, SE27-14).

**Rivals.** Generic scarcity, a comic inversion of servant/master, weak will or complete magical omnipotence with voluntary restraint. The finite-allocation account is distinguished by coupled changes across target control, other followers and planned animation. It must not invent extra capacity or a hidden reserve to guarantee a selected result.

**Application conditions.** Use supplied current allocation, known demands, religious constraints, ordinary resources and an available release/reallocation. Separate what Serena wants from whether a target will cooperate with release. Unknown costs remain unknown; the local game-like vocabulary is not an independently measured interface.

**Do not assume.** Simultaneous maximal control of everyone, automatic poverty in all contexts, an inability to work, actual payment from a promise, or that control erases a subject's objections. Planned corpse encounters and contemplated devices are not performed merely because resources were discussed.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the observed allocation tradeoff and failed grave attempt; lower for exact capacity, unseen follower totals and future recovery. Some consequences are diary or peer reports, not isolated watched trials.

**Falsifier.** Comparable attributable cases with no loss of other functions after the same concentration, or reliable success of the constrained animation without changed allocation, faith or resources. Conversely, a recurring inability to redirect an explicitly available released resource would weaken the flexibility component. No hidden budget is added afterward.

## SE-R04: fear permits bargaining without erasing refusal or converting coercion into agreement

**Proposition.** When bodily safety, escape or essential information is at issue, Serena can refuse, bargain, threaten, trade or adopt a demanded outward affiliation. Fear changes her choices without mechanically producing total disclosure. A forced status or later useful work does not establish genuine conversion, repair or a new reciprocal relationship.

**Supporting evidence.** SE27-06–09: the altered Regina relation makes attacks rebound; she attempts to terminate an unwanted arrangement and bargains over restoration. SE27-13: despite visible fear and captivity, she refuses some Demon King details, then gives other intelligence in response to a promised rescue. Forced Axis membership is followed by junior deference and her objection that the affair is not settled.

**Contrary evidence.** She does disclose information under pressure and can comply with tasks. Her own earlier coercion and actual Death attack establish harmful initiative, not just passive survival. Later retained reflection at level1 contradicts a simple reset-to-harmlessness expectation (SE27-02–09, SE27-13).

**Rivals.** Automatic cowardice, sincere religious conversion, rigid loyalty, ordinary hostage bargaining or comic punishment. The candidate differs by preserving a refusal boundary and discriminating credible exits from mere threats. Neither a threatened assault nor religious rhetoric proves an unseen act or divine instruction.

**Application conditions.** State the actual restraint, what threat Serena believes, what she can disclose, what exit is credible and what remains unprovided. Her knowledge of an opponent's identity matters. A truthful answer under threat still requires independent source qualification; pressure is not a truth assay.

**Do not assume.** Consent from silence, new clothing/address or forced membership; completed rape from a threat; universal remorse from humiliation; completed confession, punishment or years of service from a future plan; or no prior murders from Kazuma's hope. Keep her own harmful conduct and her current right to refuse separate.

**Confidence.** Moderate for discriminating refusal/trade and outward forced affiliation; low for long-term sincere belief and postcaptivity behavior. No complete mind state or exact loyalty threshold is available.

**Falsifier.** Comparable attributable captivity cases where fear reliably yields full disclosure independent of credible terms and protected information, or repeated freely chosen postrelease affiliation that establishes the sincere change this model currently leaves open. Do not use imagined later repentance to explain away present objection.

## Voice, comedy and serious transfer

Use public modesty, private urgency, clerical authority, angry objection and constrained deference according to the supplied audience and power state. Her name/alias and puppet explanation have concrete interpretive importance. Printed speech is not an audio performance; a role formula or Japanese name is not automatically L3. H3 refers to local gag dependence, not uncertainty, and magical embodiment is not automatically H4.

Canonical-comic mode may retain reflection and obligation reversals where stipulated, but must preserve objection, hunger, work, actual death and harmful deception. Do not optimize the scene toward deserved punishment or make a villain's refusal irrelevant. Serious transfer keeps appraisal, scarce leverage and bargaining while explicitly replacing magical means; it does not assert a real-world technique for compelling people through gratitude.

Ordinary positive attachments, unforced long-term affiliation and a stable private moral hierarchy are weakly identified. Hypotheticals should not invent them to force either warmth or cruelty. Some choices remain underdetermined until the prompt supplies a goal, belief, resource or authority condition.
