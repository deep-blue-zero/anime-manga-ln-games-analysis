# Cross-context transfer: post-validation specification V1

## 1. Authority and purpose

This distinct Stage D model transfers the mechanisms in the four new principal specifications and party model under an explicit world contract. The [MAIN_ONLY_R1 transfer body](KONOSUBA_CROSS_CONTEXT_TRANSFER_MODEL.md) and its four constraints, original firsts and scores stay unchanged. The [extended synthesis](../10%20Expanded%20Corpus/KONOSUBA_EXTENDED_CORPUS_SYNTHESIS.md) and seven expanded specialists supply retrospective source-known support. A source-world description cannot empirically establish what an unseen world would make someone do. Suite acceptance and exact publication are separately recorded.

## 2. What is being transferred

Preserve a causal organization of attention, appraisal, competing goals, feasible means, affect, relationships and expressed stance. Do not merely swap names in an episode, translate a catchphrase, or make the characters generic cooperative adults. Disposition is neither a fixed action nor a license to explain every opposite after the fact. The particular ordinary life they value remains important even when magic is removed.

## 3. Two operating modes

CANONICAL_COMIC retains actual source-world powers, acquired resources, permitted interpersonal scripts and contextually supported amplification. CROSS_CONTEXT_TRANSFER uses supplied new physics, institutions and persistent consequences while retaining the underlying goal conflicts. Both modes keep actual knowledge, ownership and specific permission. Identical choices can be appropriate if the changed world does not alter the relevant means or incentives; forced divergence is not evidence of transfer.

## 4. Required world and actor input

State the chronology/slice; actual world facts; which facts each actor knows; available skills, tools and providers; bodily limits; institutions and enforceable authority; counterpart history; accepted scope; current goals; stakes; and decision interval. Distinguish unknown reality from a known false belief. If the prompt supplies a new skill or agreement, label it a hypothetical supplied fact, not newly discovered canon. The actor cannot use analyst-only facts without a route.

## 5. Chronology and source constructions

Earlier states do not acquire later intimacy, resources, disclosures or character names. Prequels, alternate routes with carried memories, another narrator, the game-derived FD novel, tutorial fiction and unplayed TRPG branches have different information contracts. Reprints and multiple views of one event retain dependency. When chronology or continuity cannot be fixed, constrain the affected claim instead of silently selecting the most capable or sympathetic state.

## 6. Common causal procedure

Bind information; identify salience; construct material rival appraisals; name the competing ends; generate only feasible means; separate implementation and recovery; select the supported bounded branch; address the actual recipient; update only received facts. The character can omit a useful check; the analyst must record the omission. An alternative is useful only if the unresolved variable and potential contrary observation are stated. Neither generic niceness nor generic dysfunction is a valid default.

## 7. Three portability questions

Mechanical portability asks whether an ability or institutional resource exists. Psychological portability asks whether a goal or error generator still has a meaningful counterpart. Pragmatic portability asks whether an utterance or role carries similar social force. They can differ: a practical bargaining motive can persist without Steal, a cherished specialty can persist without Explosion, and a polite surface can have a different effect under a different status order. Do not treat a successful mapping in one dimension as evidence in all three.

## 8. Ability mapping

Keep an ability only when the new-world input explicitly permits it or a demonstrably established ordinary skill supplies the actual step. Removing magic does not grant medical, engineering, legal, weapons or computing expertise. Testing a genuinely uncertain premise can be an initial act; repeated successful use of an explicitly known absent power is impossible under the contract. Ask, learn, negotiate, use a supplied tool, make a flawed attempt or refuse according to the person's goals.

## 9. Risk, safety and recovery

Recompute actual injury, mana, transport, repair, revival and liability. Familiar comedy cannot insure a new-world body. A personal endurance memory is not proof of group safety. A later remedy cannot make prior harm unreal, and survival cannot establish prior preparation. Keep acquisition, use, rescue, extraction, treatment and settlement separate. A safe plan can still be refused; a risky act can still be causally supported if known costs remain in the appraisal.

## 10. Institutions and accountability

A royal title, divine identity, adventurer role or producer commission needs a supplied local meaning. It does not automatically become legal command, clinical authority, employment rank or permission to enter a room. An institution can create a new enforceable cost that changes bargaining, concealment or willingness. Record what each actor knows of enforcement. Modern rules do not automatically give the actor perfect legal knowledge or reliable compliance.

## 11. Relationships and agency

Particular company, grievance, trust, vulnerability and ritualized conflict can persist while each new act still requires its own scope and information. Friendship, arousal, practical dependence, silence and later participation are not automatic consent. A protective aim can motivate an imposed method; model the violation rather than laundering it into agreement. An unmodeled outsider's answer remains unknown. A provider's proposed willingness does not complete payment or recovery.

## 12. Ordinary life

Novel situations should include waiting, expenses, meals, chores, work, leisure and familiar invitations. These expose the person beyond combat competence. Do not force a signature flaw or spectacular joke into a neutral task. A changed institution may make an ordinary option newly costly, attractive or unavailable. A small successful action does not demonstrate broad professional mastery or long-term reform.

## 13. Character-specific invariants and recalibration

Kazuma retains local means-search, discretionary effort, grievance, temptation, particular attachment and selective responsibility. New leverage can make a direct attack, inquiry, partial account or apology attractive; a civil surface is not necessary. Aqua retains pride, useful task capacities, appetite, art and particular company; actual capability, adoption, compatibility and execution remain distinct. Megumin retains chosen specialty, contribution, rivalry, practical reasoning and dangerous reward conflicts; a new craft needs real supplied means. Darkness retains protection, desired defensive experience, peer belonging, dignity and finite capacity; wanting an act does not make every extension wanted or safe.

## 14. Party transfer is nonadditive

Use separate participant states and a provider graph. Removing magic can eliminate a handoff, while an available outsider can enable another without Kazuma's command. A shared new-world task does not make resources collective property or private information public. A correctly represented stalled phase is not a fulfilled prediction that someone seeks recovery. Group-level compatibility does not erase an individual adverse choice. Unwanted quiet remains possible without being relabeled assent.

## 15. Voice and humor

Preserve interaction purpose, addressee, stance and source-supported range rather than a quote bank. An equivalent social function may require different literal words in a new institution. Japanese form can be generated for a diagnostic but is not acoustic imitation or human-native evaluation. Reduce unsupported physical amplification while keeping self-regard, appetite, rivalry and awkwardness. A realistic mode is not a command to remove humor or wrongdoing.

## 16. Seriousness and rival transfers

Recognized grave harm can increase concern while performance identity, fear, reputation or particular priorities still compete. Do not invent a universal moral switch. A cautious action does not prove goals were deleted; a reckless action does not prove they were retained. Require an actual appraisal trace or an observable supported conflict. Where new-world learning or motive weights are underdetermined, report bounded rivals and the fact that would discriminate them, not a claim that both eventual answers are successes.

## 17. Execution contract

For each case, save exact model identities and prompt before generating the first continuation. Record actual means, information, chosen act, words, unresolved result and limits. Freeze all actual firsts before feedback. Later corrections are diagnostic successors with their own identities. The same assistant authors and reviews these source-known hypotheticals; no masking or random API sampling is claimed unless actually performed. A serialization timestamp is a write/seal observation, not a fabricated token-generation time.

## 18. Stage D typed consistency constraints

The following are GENERATED_RESPONSE consistency checks, excluded from directional literary forecast totals. Findings use SUPPORTED, VIOLATED, UNOBSERVABLE or QUALIFIED. They do not enter Gen01–05 or old R1 behavioral C/P/F totals.

- **SD1-T01, means:** When a power is explicitly absent and that absence is known, successful action cannot use it or an invented replacement expertise/helper. A knowingly absent power used successfully is VIOLATED; a genuinely uncertain first test is a different state. The particular alternative action is not prescribed.
- **SD1-T02, known costs and motives:** A newly known persistent cost must not be erased, and the person's established relevant goals must not be replaced by an unrelated ideal-duty rule. A supported risky discounting or a cautious choice can each be coherent. Brevity alone is not a violation; an unavailable appraisal is UNOBSERVABLE.
- **SD1-T03, history:** Earlier slices do not obtain later knowledge, skills, relationships or permission without an explicitly supplied hypothetical premise. Reprints, alternate views and carried-memory routes do not become independent confirmations. Unknown chronology is QUALIFIED rather than favorable by default.
- **SD1-T04, provision:** A plan needing another person's finite resource, information, body or authority must use actual available provision, negotiate it, change plan, stall or depict a real violation. It cannot silently invent availability or assent. A harmful unauthorized attempt may be causally faithful; it is not agreed coordination.
- **SD1-T05, operation phase:** Claimed success, recovery and settlement each require their own observed or explicitly supplied event. An offer, promise or successful first stage does not perform later stages. A correctly unresolved phase supports only this typed trace, not a behavioral response forecast.

## 19. Evidence and revision routes

The four Stage D principal models and party model provide traceable local mechanisms and new working commitments. Their accepted expanded monographs, the [extended synthesis](../10%20Expanded%20Corpus/KONOSUBA_EXTENDED_CORPUS_SYNTHESIS.md), §§2–10, and the [expanded Japanese-language synthesis](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_EXPANDED_JAPANESE_LANGUAGE_AND_COMEDY_SYNTHESIS_V1.md) retain source routes and contrary cases. MAIN_ONLY_R1 §§18–20 preserves the old four typed constraints and rationale. A typed transfer success is internal consistency, not new observed behavior in another universe.

## 20. Uncertainty and failure diagnosis

Separate wrong input mapping, unknown information promoted to knowledge, invented skill, changed institutional meaning, assumed relationship, unsupported comic magnitude, missed ordinary competence and a wrong psychological mechanism. A source-world capacity can remain valid after a failed new-world transfer. One plausible output is weak discrimination against prompt-following, generic cooperation and broad-role-script rivals. No calibrated probability, human-native voice accuracy or independent character-fidelity grade is claimed.

## 21. Acceptance, battery and freeze

The later battery must cover all nine protocol families for each principal and the party in both modes, with actual saved first responses and separate feedback. It must record which commitments are genuinely triggered instead of converting every case into a test of every clause. An OPEN or typed contract is not a directional prediction pass. Freeze candidate model bodies before concrete case authoring, then freeze inputs before first responses; declare the common assistant/source-known lineage honestly. The suite record and current map govern acceptance and exact publication, while designated evaluation and release remain distinct.

## 22. Simulator failures and limits

Reject magic by analogy, perfect new-world expertise, generic moral sanitization, compulsory dysfunction, omniscient party coordination, guessed outsider consent, free repair and after-the-fact eligibility changes. Also reject a percentage derived from self-rated plausible outputs as canonical predictive accuracy. First-output defects must remain available after any correction. This provisional model supplies a disciplined transfer procedure, not completion by document existence.
