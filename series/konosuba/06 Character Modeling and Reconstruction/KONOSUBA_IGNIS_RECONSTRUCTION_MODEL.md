## IG-R01: acknowledged responsibility can lead to bounded delegation and personal risk

**Proposition.** When Ignis accepts a concrete family/public responsibility, he can request practical plans, allocate differentiated work and participate rather than entirely offload danger. Available body, money and equipment shape that participation. This is a capacity-and-choice account, not a guarantee of benevolent success.

**Supporting evidence.** IG28-04: accepts insufficient preparation of Darkness. IG28-05–06: allocates work/paid tasks, asks K's plan, accepts a partial plan/fallback and joins in armor. IG28-07: adapts to short wire using spear/gate and his own straining body.

**Contrary evidence.** Illness restricts him in MAIN07. Family distraction compromises attention. One adventurer dies/is revived despite no-loss assurance. Debt history and military efficiency are attributed reports; exact final financial account is unverified.

**Rivals.** Routine noble obligation, protection of household reputation, self-interest or episodic heroic staging. The candidate requires actual contribution and explicit responsibility, not interpreting every command as self-sacrifice.

**Application conditions.** Specify recognized obligation, known problem, body/health, equipment, workers and funds. Separate planning, promised payment and performance. A novel task without expertise must allow partial plans, help or uncertainty rather than inventing mastery.

**Do not assume.** No-harm success from revival, unlimited stamina, all costs paid from an offer, independent campaign competence, or that personal participation makes coercion consensual. Keep each worker's contribution and injury separate.

**Confidence.** Moderate for direct YORI04 accountability/participation and MAIN07 limits; lower for unseen administration and unrelated competence domains. No independent general capability rating is earned.

**Falsifier.** Comparable accepted responsibilities consistently offloaded without contribution despite feasible personal options, or reliable unlimited performance that removes documented health/equipment constraints. Do not define unavoidable incapacity as moral refusal to keep the rule true.

## IG-R02: administrative problem solving can recruit coercive leverage and contested accounting

**Proposition.** Ignis can organize cooperation through concrete incentives, amnesty, authority and threats. When costs or compliance become contested, he may invoke household/public losses and rank rather than negotiate as an equal. Helpful administration can therefore coexist with coercive asymmetry.

**Supporting evidence.** IG28-05: pressure on K, paid work, amnesty tasks and threatened Axis rename/devil governorship. IG28-06: flash-scroll ban. IG28-08: damage/treatment estimate offsets promised reward, followed by anger and threat. IG28-01 includes a mistaken summary execution order.

**Contrary evidence.** He acknowledges local grievances and his own error, actually participates and grants amnesty. Predicted royal refusal of tax money is not a performed refusal. K's feared execution and threatened institutional changes are not completed acts. No audited balance proves his estimate correct.

**Rivals.** Ordinary legal administration, comic exaggeration, fair reimbursement or personal anger. The candidate depends on unequal ability to impose terms and the stated constraints; merely counting a cost does not by itself prove abuse.

**Application conditions.** Supply actual jurisdiction, resources, earlier promise, claimed loss and credible refusal routes. Distinguish what he can legally do, what he asserts and what occurs. New institutions must specify their own checks rather than inherit unlimited ducal authority.

**Do not assume.** Every threat performed, harmlessness from joking tone, verified accounting, all recipients willingly cooperating, or identical motives to Alderp. K's interpretation cannot create a hidden execution order.

**Confidence.** Moderate for visible asymmetry and disputed accounting; lower for legal power and sincerity of particular threats. The material identifies tension rather than a quantified corruption rate.

**Falsifier.** Comparable allocation/cost conflicts repeatedly resolved through equal voluntary negotiation despite the same available leverage, or performed procedure that reliably rules out the threatened options. Do not call all courteous agreement covert coercion without an actual pressure route.

## IG-R03: paternal attachment can both support recognition and disrupt task appraisal

**Proposition.** Darkness's wellbeing, identity or display can change Ignis's affect and priorities sharply. Attachment can support gratitude, escape advice and acknowledgment of choice, but can also produce controlling pressure, distraction or surprise that reduces immediate competence.

**Supporting evidence.** IG28-01–03: matchmaking, reward adjustment, false-pregnancy belief, rescue encouragement, gratitude and marriage pressure. IG28-04: painful reunion hug and Axis-conversion summons. IG28-07: camera wish/debate during danger, upbringing doubt and confusion at possession.

**Contrary evidence.** He can perform public work and take bodily risk despite family emotion. His statement that the daughter should choose limits a purely controlling account. K's inferences about paternal sincerity/omniscience are not privileged access. No complete family history is observed.

**Rivals.** Generic fond father, rank anxiety, ordinary surprise at magic, or narrator comedy. The candidate matters when a specific family cue changes a task response; affection alone cannot predict every exact decision or justify a universal distraction claim.

**Application conditions.** Specify the daughter's actual state, what he believes, the competing task and available time. Distinguish affection, declared preference and the daughter's own choice. If she is absent and irrelevant, do not insert an invented crisis to trigger the rule.

**Do not assume.** Paternal authority as consent, perfect knowledge of her sexuality/wishes, automatic execution of her partner, completed marriage contract from an offer, or that everyone else values her as he does. Preserve the recipient's pain in the hug.

**Confidence.** Moderate for heterogeneous family-linked shifts; lower for precise trigger thresholds or unfamiliar relationships. Ordinary paternal attachment is a strong rival, so specificity must come from the observed mix of support, pressure and distraction.

**Falsifier.** Comparable salient daughter cues reliably leaving appraisal and priority unchanged under the same time constraints, or task distraction occurring equally without relational cues. Do not relabel all public concern as hidden family attachment to save the prediction.
