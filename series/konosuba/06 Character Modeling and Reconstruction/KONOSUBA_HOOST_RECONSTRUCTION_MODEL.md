# Hoost — provisional behavioral reconstruction

## 1. Purpose, authority and asymmetric reliability

This model generates plausible Hoost behavior from a supplied situation, available knowledge, actual resources and relationship state. Hoost is ホースト, the same named actor called Horst in earlier accepted English analysis. The [monograph](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_HOOST_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) owns interpretation and rival accounts; the [evidence index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_HOOST_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) owns locators, event identity and source planes. The current disposition is scoped Tier 3, with a substantial but explicitly provisional model. No principal-character format, appearance quota or full principal validation battery is imposed merely by tier.

Evidence is concentrated in EXP01's five Komekko interludes and EXP03's search/confrontation sequence. The current check reuses eighteen previously actually read integral image-prose pages and receives 1,172 unique EXP03 BASE slots. Five prepared members contain 2,000 slots, which is a mechanical preparation count, not completed reading. Other source and ledger work retains its historical scope. All source content is already known; nothing here is a pristine prospective trial, separate-runtime generation, independent judgment or native-human voice assessment.

Reconstruction is strongest for scarce-skill dependence, particular provider behavior, incomplete investigation, status-sensitive speech and negotiation under threat. Ordinary institutions, novel intimacies, a broad moral code, long-term development and post-defeat existence remain poorly constrained. Confidence below is qualitative support for a bounded mechanism, not a measured behavioral success rate. New internal exercises must remain separately recorded and cannot improve historical outcomes or frozen principal models.

## 2. Identity, values and the hierarchy that remains open

Hoost presents himself as a superior devil and Wolbach's subordinate. Rank matters, but the seal task repeatedly makes a child the scarce competent partner. A memory of that child later creates reluctance toward fighting Crimson Demons without ending his coercive mission. His operative priorities therefore compete rather than forming one fixed global order:

| Priority | Evidence-backed pressure | Known limit |
|---|---|---|
| Wolbach's release/recovery | Seal labor, ritual provisioning, travel, scent search and delivery demand | Does not confer correct identity; task may be pursued with faulty cues or rejected inspection |
| Effective agency and continued capacity | Conserving depleted mana, closing by force, proposed retreat while immobilized | Bravado, misrecognition and failed defense can worsen danger; no optimal-survival rule |
| Rank and recognition | Goblin correction, body display, demands for address, boasts | Particular dependency leads to actual deference; status is not an absolute prohibition on asking |
| Komekko relationship | Company, hunted food, outing, intervention over chicks and farewell | Instrumental need remains mixed; departure and conditional service impose finite availability |
| Amusement and expressive satisfaction | Enjoying praise, banter, comic mishaps and complaint | No general hobby or proof that amusement outweighs every task or risk |

The source does not establish that Hoost always sacrifices Komekko to duty, always sacrifices duty to her or follows a universal no-harm rule for children. An input demanding that ranking should receive a bounded range or OPEN state, rather than a fabricated certainty. (EXP01 IMG026–043; EXP03 U026 P588–598; U034 P315–424/485–493.)

## 3. State and knowledge representation

Before generating, record the following from the packet, distinguishing supplied facts from Hoost's beliefs.

- **Task:** present objective, time pressure, actual next step, required scarce skill, available alternative and whether secrecy prevents ordinary buying or recruitment.
- **Relationship:** known particular person, remembered interaction, local address arrangement, dependence, actual promise and what each participant has assented to. Crimson appearance alone does not establish kinship.
- **Information:** what Hoost saw, smelled, heard or was told; form expectations; candidate identity; time of correction; inspection offered or refused. Keep reader-only truth outside this state.
- **Capacity:** wounds, movement, usable mana, known effect resistance, an actual modifier to a spell, other participants' mobility and remaining means to finish or escape.
- **Appraisal and affect:** confidence, status irritation, impatience, concern, amusement, surprise and fear. Do not turn narration that qualifies a face-reading into Hoost's verified private thought.
- **Commitment:** quoted or paraphrased assurance, offered condition, recipient acceptance, duration, actual performance and any mechanism independently verified. A proposed ceasefire is not an executed one.

Keep location, possession, movement and access consistent with the packet. Do not create an unavailable object, an unperformed exchange, a new food delivery, a healed participant or a post-defeat body. If a needed fact is absent, mark it unknown or ask within the simulated interaction when plausible. Lack of knowledge is not evidence of low intelligence.

## 4. Six defeasible mechanisms

### HH-R01 — scarce competence can reverse a superior's interaction strategy

**Proposition.** When Hoost needs a particular person's task-specific skill and force or self-performance does not produce it, status assertion can give way to explanation, bargaining, provision and explicit requests. Pride may persist in the wording while the practical relationship changes.

**Evidence.** He takes over the puzzle, fails, needs Komekko's work, offers food and uses a deferential request. Further layers and the child's boredom keep her effort nonautomatic. He cannot shop openly in the village and must hunt or provide money. (EXP01 IMG027–037.)

**Counterevidence and rivals.** He resists cheap flattery, disputes a false hardship claim and rejects a future-power threat. Actual food can precede completed work, and later friendship may explain continuing help. Instrumental dependence is therefore not a sufficient explanation of every provision.

**Apply when** the dependency and the helper's control over participation are actually present. **Do not assume** general submissiveness, a magical obligation, or the same address with every child or expert. A usable alternative may reduce the need for deference, but whether he still offers company or food depends on the relationship and prior terms.

**Confidence:** moderate within the seal relationship, low for unknown institutions. **Disconfirmation:** repeated comparable cases where acknowledged failure and unique needed help lead him to abandon an important goal solely to preserve address, or where he routinely produces the missing solution by unestablished general power. A new truly usable alternative is a boundary change, not that disconfirmation.

### HH-R02 — particular attachment modifies a mission without erasing its conflicts

**Proposition.** A recognized relationship with Komekko makes company, finite provision, concern and remembered reaction relevant to Hoost's choices. Those values coexist with the Wolbach task and can qualify his willingness to fight people linked in his mind to the child.

**Evidence.** Actual food, carried outing, negotiation over formal address, objection to the chicks' proposed use, crouched farewell and conditional future service give the relationship texture. EXP03 explicitly connects his reluctance to fight Crimson Demons with the child who might bite. (EXP01 IMG029–043; EXP03 U026 P588–591.)

**Counterevidence and rivals.** Feeding and play serve task recruitment; self-interest and affection are not separated experimentally. He initially considers witness silencing, leaves, does not have a shown bear-encounter rescue and continues coercing the girls and city. He does not establish that he knows Megumin is Komekko's sister.

**Apply when** the relevant memory or person is actually accessible. **Do not assume** universal paternal care, automatic acquiescence to Komekko, a complete moral override or completed familiar availability.

**Confidence:** moderate for this dyad and explicit later reluctance; low for novel severe conflicts. **Disconfirmation:** repeated comparable recognized-Komekko cases in which her actual refusal, hunger, dangerous action or departure has no effect on his attention or conduct, without a separately evidenced competing constraint. Single opposed goals must retain their concrete tradeoff rather than being rescued by an invented higher duty.

### HH-R03 — goal-shaped cues can prompt premature action, followed by a local update

**Proposition.** Hoost searches using the expected master-form, familiar scent, place and remembered names. A plausible target cue can become an overhasty identification before close checking; sufficiently concrete incompatible evidence can produce recognition and correction. Updating in one encounter does not erase a prior missed inspection.

**Evidence.** He declines Komekko's offer to show the small creature, dismisses the kitten account, later follows scent, calls the captured beginner-killer Wolbach and attacks its captors, then recognizes ordinary prey. Close sniffing, village recognition and Chomusuke's name join further clues. (EXP01 IMG041–043; EXP03 U026 P533–598.)

**Counterevidence and rivals.** The scent is locally informative and the lookalike was deliberately selected by the adventurers; this is not wholly cue-free fantasy. He actually corrects the mistake. The source does not measure remote scent accuracy or prove that every identification is motivated wish fulfillment.

**Apply when** there is an important search and specified imperfect cues. **Do not assume** a required error in every search or perfect rational checking. If only the reader's hidden truth changes while Hoost receives the same cues, his behavior must not change because of that hidden truth.

**Confidence:** moderate for the shown error/update, low for its frequency. **Disconfirmation:** recurring matched cue cases showing either near-perfect unsupported identification or no update despite received, understood incompatible evidence. A changed cue set may legitimately change the result.

### HH-R04 — resource and control changes alter tactics despite continuing bravado

**Proposition.** Actual depletion, damage, distance and mobility affect Hoost's means. He may conserve mana, shift to force, intercept a suspected tactic and bargain when he cannot safely finish. Taunting can accompany real wariness; it does not prove that threat appraisal is absent or accurate.

**Evidence.** He complains about depleted power, reluctantly casts Inferno, loses it to cancellation and closes by force. He resists Flash, tries to stop potion use, is delayed by Rex and is immobilized by enhanced Paralysis despite an ordinary-immunity boast. Once stuck he argues spell duration and remaining mana, offers mutual retreat, and changes affect when Yunyun's immobility becomes clear. (EXP03 U034 P294–424.)

**Counterevidence and rivals.** His interception fails; the modified effect defeats him. Full-strength survival of Explosion is only his counterfactual claim. His late account of prior attackers and exorcism is a report. Generic combat incentives can explain much of the tactical response, so these outcomes do not uniquely identify Hoost.

**Apply when** actual resources and abilities are supplied, including exceptions already demonstrated. **Do not assume** universal immunity, a guaranteed optimized move, a fixed mana threshold or an available escape while immobilized. If the opponent is also stuck, reduced immediate finishing pressure can permit laughter and renewed leverage; hidden helpers remain unknown unless supplied.

**Confidence:** moderate in the shown combat, low outside analogous constraints. **Disconfirmation:** repeated matched condition changes that never affect choice or speech, or confident ordinary-immunity behavior after an actual modifier has been learned to defeat it. Continued boastful wording alone is insufficient to establish behavioral nonresponse.

### HH-R05 — promise language is a scoped tool and an attributed doctrine

**Proposition.** Hoost uses contract and promise claims to seek cooperation, justify conditional non-harm and make retreat credible. Generate his assurance as his belief or rhetorical claim; separately track what was offered, accepted and done.

**Evidence.** He attempts a contract argument over food, faces Komekko's correction of the actual progress terms, offers non-harm for Wolbach's delivery, and twice presses a one-day retreat while invoking devil reliability. His farewell and pre-defeat remarks retain conditions on later service and release. (EXP01 IMG030–031/040–043; EXP03 U026 P591–598; U034 P387–424/489.)

**Counterevidence and rivals.** No city exchange, completed mutual withdrawal, observed rebirth or verified new familiar contract tests a universal law here. Courtesy and casual play terms do not automatically constitute a magical pact. His friendliness claim coexists with actual assault and conditional city coercion.

**Apply when** a real proposed obligation could affect cooperation. **Do not assume** guaranteed assent, universal cosmic enforcement, a duty to deliver an unstated extra service or a retrospective safety assurance already known to Megumin.

**Confidence:** moderate for the speech strategy, unresolved for universal enforcement. **Disconfirmation:** recurrent comparable negotiations where promises and terms have no relevance, or actual comparable accepted terms are knowingly treated by Hoost as disposable. A recipient's refusal is not Hoost's breach, and performance would support only the terms actually tested.

### HH-R06 — register follows recipient, dependency and current speech act

**Proposition.** Hoost's rough rank presentation can coexist with polite requesting, master honorifics, a learned audience-specific introduction, factual correction, complaint and urgent bargaining. Select a speech act from the task; do not paste every mannerism into every line.

**Evidence.** Rank/body display and 俺様 contrast with the Komekko-san request; the honorific arrangement is later mutually relaxed. Wolbach is 様. Hoost acknowledges teaching the child's grudging phrase. He tries a Crimson-style grand introduction and asks for feedback; the girls flee. Later rough taunts, explanations, haste and the remaining-life phrase have different purposes. (EXP01 IMG027–031/036/043; EXP03 U020 P500–516; U034 P364–493.)

**Counterevidence and rivals.** Local wording does not establish a complete dialect, required profanity rate or acoustic delivery. Deference may be strategic and reluctant; roughness can be friendly, hostile or explanatory according to context.

**Apply when** recipient and interactional goal are known. **Do not assume** permanent formality after the mutual change or a grand introduction with every sentence. In translation, preserve stance and the act rather than inventing an accent.

**Confidence:** moderate for printed local contrasts, low for new social strata. **Disconfirmation:** repeated matched recipient/dependency shifts produce no corresponding pragmatic variation, or the generated register prevents the concrete request or correction that source-like behavior requires. Catchphrase resemblance alone is no supporting result.

He can ask for help and object to a proposed use of birds. Future talent and familiar service remain appraisals and conditional prospects, not present powers. **Wolbach.** Preserve the pursuit and self-described subordinate role. Hoost's knowledge is not the reader's, and loyalty does not supply guaranteed correct target recognition. No directly observed routine with Wolbach supports a complete superior-subordinate domestic script.

**Megumin and Yunyun.** Differentiate their actions. Megumin is the forest narrator and later hidden finisher; Yunyun questions trust, fights with resources, becomes immobilized and negotiates. ## 7. A generation procedure that permits mistakes

1. Establish the scene's actor, time, viewpoint and supplied facts. Keep fictional reader knowledge out of Hoost's accessible packet.
2. Separate the task's actual requirement from his current belief about it. Mark imperfect cues and the possible opportunity to inspect.
3. Identify relationship-specific pressures, existing terms, alternative methods and current resources. Do not make rank solve every domain.
4. Generate a plausible appraisal, including unsupported confidence or premature closure where the evidence warrants that possibility. Do not optimize him automatically or force the canonical error as a compulsory replay.
5. Choose means consistent with access and movement: command, request, provision, inquiry, refusal, force, changed tactic or bargain. Record a plausible rival explanation and uncertainty when the choice is not identified.
6. Give speech an actual purpose and recipient. A promise remains a proposal until the interaction supplies acceptance and performance.
7. Update only on received evidence. Preserve prior consequences when an error is corrected, and leave an unshown outcome open.

For a reconstruction response, return the proposed action and short dialogue, the decisive supplied facts, applicable rule IDs, at least one limiting alternative when material, and any missing fact that prevents a justified choice. This is an operational aid, not evidence of a new source event.

## 8. Canonical-comic and realistic transfer modes

In canonical-comic mode, preserve the rank reversal, expressive complaint, challenge, mistaken assumptions and awkward bargain that the supplied situation supports. Supernatural effects require the actual admitted world mechanism and current state. Do not infer harmlessness from comedy or supply unseen healing, resurrection and family consent.

In realistic transfer, preserve task-specific dependence, selective provision, pride with practical concession, incomplete checking and threat-sensitive negotiation. Supply a credible counterpart for capacity and contractual role, or leave that part unmapped. Hoost does not acquire explosives expertise, criminal authority, a scent laboratory or legal command over strangers through analogy. A request for help can be transferred more readily than a devil's body or asserted binding law.

Do not automatically sanitize coercion or invent universal moral repair. A realistic treatment makes injury and nonconsent legible and removes unsupported cartoon magnitude; it does not change his observed valuations into the writer's preferred conduct. Conversely, known friendliness and care must not disappear solely because he is a devil.
