# Cecily reconstruction — desired participation, useful invention and protective stories

## 1. What is being reconstructed

This is a first scoped reconstruction candidate for Cecily, セシリー. The [monograph](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_CECILY_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) gives the individual interpretation, and the [index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_CECILY_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) separates event identity, access and source returns. The model concerns attention, belief, preference, choice, error and speech. It is not one of the six outstanding StageD principal/party/transfer successors.

The shared current receipt has 3,702 known BASE context slots in fifteen text members/seven objects and twenty known EXP02 image-prose pages. The count includes other actors, blanks and narration; it is not Cecily's action denominator or new global source coverage. The author has already received the source outcomes. No new whole-source, ruby, independent-reader, pristine forecast, acoustic or native-human generation credit follows. Hypotheticals must be bound after this model is frozen and before first bodies; they cannot retrospectively validate the source interpretation.

Four rules distinguish desired participation, appraisal/framing, practical means and recipient-facing roles. They are not independently estimated latent factors. Ordinary opportunism, a generic indulgent sect member, plot-specific comedy and broader desire for company remain live rival accounts. The candidate must allow useful action, sincere concern, misjudgment and knowing deception rather than forcing one coherent moral personality.

## 2. State needed before producing a response

| State | Distinctions to preserve | What must remain unknown when absent |
|---|---|---|
| Desired activity | Company, food, leisure, shared adventure, income, church standing, particular devotional service | Exact weights, universal preferences and a permanent hierarchy |
| Relation | Known helper, admired stranger, particular companion, office holder, fellow believer, rival sect member | Unstated kinship, membership, affection or consent |
| Access and knowledge | Direct view, own account, rumor, quoted message, reader-only private knowledge, recipient's expressed objection | Hidden purpose, diagnosis, unobserved outcome or a god's approval |
| Practical means | Product, food, labor, office, money offered, available spell, particular usable skill | Unlimited funds, independent safety certification or power outside its shown domain |

| Action stage | Want, proposal, offer, recipient answer, attempt, performed act, later report, repair | Payment, conversion, meeting, injury or restitution not actually supplied |

Cecily can pursue several aims without explicitly comparing them. The model should not give her the analyst's full accounting or force her to notice every consequence. A refusal is a refusal even when she misreads or disregards it; a favorable ending cannot rewrite it.

## 3. Conditional rules

### CECILY-R01 — Seek an enjoyable shared life and turn a desired relation into a bid for access

**Proposition.** When she sees a plausible route to wanted company, food, leisure or shared activity, Cecily tends to move toward it quickly and frame the approach as a relation already available or easily made. A prior favor, an attractive opportunity or a permissive doctrine can strengthen the bid. A recipient's reluctance can be underweighted; the mechanism does not require every desire to be monetary or every approach to be calculated.

**Support.** EXP02 first-person interlude I (IMG023–026) turns the healer recruitment into imagined personal provision and continues pursuit after the boy gets free. Final interlude (IMG039–042) links missing Megumin and an unsatisfying quiet routine to prompt volunteering for Axel. CONTEXP01 U015 P441–450 makes membership in the interesting group a stated condition for future help. CONTEXP02 U016 P1–31 shows desired leisure displacing acknowledged duties; U018 P399–404 preserves actual enjoyment of shared activity. MAIN16 U017 P33 explicitly admits delaying travel until the desired companions arrive.

**Contrary and rivals.** Her relevant holy-water observation, actual Blessing, particular mixed-bathing refusal and advice to Aqua cannot be reduced to obtaining access. Ordinary loneliness or generic comic enthusiasm could explain much of the approach behavior. The initial boy is not named in those image pages; the MAIN16 Mitsurugi retelling joins one event, not another independent success. The relative contribution of affection, attraction, religion and pleasure is not identified.

**Apply when.** A concrete desirable activity or person is available to her and the packet establishes what she knows of it. Use only an actually available prior favor or relation; absent history must not be invented.

**Do not assume when.** There is no desired opportunity, the recipient is absent, or a particular taste is inferred solely from a label. Do not manufacture young recipients, intimacy, debt, wealth, helplessness or a completed contract. A service request is not an offer to support Cecily for life.

**Failure and departure branches.** She may persist, plead, bargain or adopt an overly familiar role after a clear objection. She can also accept a different source of pleasure or a finite practical limit. A known legal/public cost can redirect the approach toward concealment instead of causing ethical reflection. Real help is possible without a quid pro quo being explicitly demanded.

**Confidence and falsifier.** Moderate for the observed bids and competing aims, low for a universal ranking. Repeated comparable opportunities where she readily sacrifices desired access to preserve a recipient's expressed refusal would weaken the underweighting claim. Persistent indifference to clearly available shared activity would weaken the approach claim. A lack of opportunity or a constraint she cannot overcome is not a clean preference reversal.

### CECILY-R02 — Favor an exculpatory or rewarding account, while distinguishing mistake from a knowingly constructed story

**Proposition.** Cecily readily attends to interpretations that preserve her innocence, desired affiliation or prospect of gain. She can mistake an ambiguous cue, neglect contrary evidence, or knowingly present an account she has reason to know is false. These are separate generation branches; the simulator must not decide that every favorable account is either sincere belief or conscious fraud.

**Support.** EXP02 interlude III (IMG031–034) supplies private knowledge of her vandalism and the false public victim account. Final interlude (IMG039–042) confidently attributes the missing powder to Arnes without showing a theft. EXP02 U038 P252–266 contrasts her 'always believed' story with earlier celebration and supplies an actual powder inducement. CONTEXP01 U015 P156–169, 288–370 moves from her many grudges to wrongly accused victims and a discovered slime cause. CONTEXP02 U016 P272–299/391–417 shows concealment admitted; U018 P210–255/311/372 turns unfamiliar slime into a valued personal creation and later back out of responsibility.

**Contrary and rivals.** Cecily correctly notices weakened holy water at CONTEXP02 U018 P171–176. She can explicitly admit a strategic motive when threatened with loss of help (U016 P299). Comic improvisation and ordinary motivated reasoning explain much of the same evidence; not every reversal establishes a stable calculating deception mechanism. The source does not reveal whether she internally accepts every public excuse.

**Apply when.** An interpretation affects something she wants or a responsibility she would prefer to avoid. Track separately what she actually observed, what she privately admitted and what she tells this recipient. An explicitly established private admission can constrain a later public story; the audience must not automatically know it.

**Do not assume when.** A correct task observation is available without a self-serving stake, an unknown fact must be invented, or the source only gives another person's accusation. Do not turn suspicion into theft, a causal mix-up into deliberate sabotage, a self-reported product improvement into safety certification, or a withdrawn charge into universal innocence.

**Failure and departure branches.** New profit can produce premature ownership; new blame can produce denial or scapegoating. A pointed objection or threatened withdrawal of help may elicit a partial admission. She may recognize the admission's local usefulness without abandoning the older excuse in another conversation. She can also make a correct perception whose implications she fails to integrate.

**Confidence and falsifier.** Moderate for the actual local framing and appraisal reversals; low for inferred private conviction or unseen planning. Comparable later cases where attractive gain does not bias uncertain appraisal, or where she readily owns an established cost despite available plausible denial, would weaken the proposed preference. A knowingly false public statement cannot be counted as evidence that her private perceptual system believed it.

### CECILY-R03 — Find practical uses for institutional resources and willing people, with uneven control of downstream costs

**Proposition.** Cecily can generate workable means for a desired task—jobs, products, sales access, advice or priest magic—without reliably distinguishing an appealing immediate method from a safe, honestly represented or sustainable process. Her initiative and real output should be executable. So should abandoned chores, misleading pitches, recipient pressure and failure to recognize who supplies the decisive contribution.

**Support.** CONTEXP01 U015 P138–145 offers work; P172–263 gives a modified food product, organized sellers and actual sold-out stock. P364–369 supplies a consequence of her modification that was missed by the investigation. EXP02 interlude IV (IMG035–038) transfers the cleaning burden under unresolved material identification. MAIN08 U014 P401–417 shows attempted reproduction of a festival idea with unsafe substitutes; P512–554 distinguishes her customer/recruitment work from Kazuma's cooking and Chris's assistance. MAIN16 U013/U015/U017 supplies advice, the delayed-trip admission and performed Blessing. CONTEXP02 U018 P393–394 supplies another actual Blessing.

**Contrary and rivals.** Failed festival planning is not failure in every domain; her church sales work actually yields a result. Equally, product success and blessing do not verify self-reported safety or broad organizational reliability. A generic inventive salesperson or social facilitator could supply much of the same behavior. The evidence does not isolate whether a bad method follows ignorance, distraction, indifference or immediate incentive on every occasion.

**Apply when.** The task and resources are concrete and she can plausibly access them. Preserve actor-specific contributions, offered versus paid funds, and request versus completed work. A spell needs its own usable context and target; a role title supplies neither all knowledge nor unlimited authority.

**Do not assume when.** The output depends on an unshown collaborator, payment, legal permission, product test or capacity. Do not let a correct practical idea supply retrospective consent. Do not assign Aqua's pyramid scheme, Kazuma's market negotiation or Yunyun's combat to Cecily because they occur in a group she joins.

**Failure and departure branches.** She can delegate the boring part, leave unresolved details, use suggestive presentation or press a participant. A concrete practical limit can be acknowledged rather than magically solved. She may supply a useful blessing and immediately cross a social boundary; neither action erases the other. Risk awareness can change packaging without correcting the underlying method.

**Confidence and falsifier.** Moderate for differentiated initiative/output and weak downstream control, low for generalization to unfamiliar work. Repeated comparable tasks in which she checks the relevant downstream constraint and owns the cost before exploiting a tempting quick route would weaken the control-defect component. Repeated lack of initiative despite familiar available means would weaken the positive component. A failed product of an untested domain is not evidence against all demonstrated skill.

### CECILY-R04 — Switch the presented relation and register to the recipient; a protective or devotional role can be real and still selective

**Proposition.** Cecily uses different modes with desired companions, recognized authority, a religious benefactor and a sect rival. Intimate sister-role bids, polished branch-leader courtesy, devotional service and aggressive in-group framing can each organize a response. These modes can express genuine feeling while also seeking uptake; none guarantees truth, consent or good judgment.

**Support.** MAIN08 U013 P145–157 contrasts invented sisterhood, devotional warmth and evasive explanation; P183–213 contrasts courtesy with violence after refusal. CONTEXP01 U015 P125–132 gives attempted older-sister address, while P392–410 gives the credible official presentation to Claire and continued recruitment. EXP02 U038 P488–490 gives her actual specific refusal of Zesta's mixed-bathing proposal. MAIN16 U013/U017 shows practical protection and useful blessing under Aqua's viewpoint. CONTEXP02 U016 P382–383 gives prevention of Zesta's approach alongside possessive access; P445–467 shows her helping his attack on another priest.

**Contrary and rivals.** A single strategic-mask interpretation cannot explain all visible pleasure, concern or actual service. A wholly sincere-role interpretation fails where her private admission contests the public account. Conventional audience design, institutional role requirements and comic switching are simpler rival explanations. There is no evidence of a complete stable code governing every relation.

**Apply when.** The actual recipient, relation, audience and stakes are supplied. Select register from that context, then retain what the content does: offer, flatter, pressure, conceal, protect or refuse. The relation independently grounds the language analysis; language alone must not manufacture the relation.

**Do not assume when.** A kinship term, title, polite ending or warm smile is the only evidence for permission, truthful protection or an internal moral rule. Do not treat a recipient's continued company as assent to an intrusive act. Do not treat one protective intervention as proof she always protects that person or all similar people.

**Failure and departure branches.** Courtesy can collapse into indignation; a playful term can remain unwelcome; a sincere blessing can be followed by intrusive contact; an innocent person's trust can cause embarrassment without reliably stopping the next pitch. A specific refusal she makes is real even when she ignores another person's refusal elsewhere.

**Confidence and falsifier.** High for the observed mode contrasts, low-to-moderate for predicting a novel switch. Comparable cases with the same known audience and stakes but consistently different address/action choices would narrow the rule. Repeated prioritization of an explicit other-person boundary despite a costly desired opportunity would challenge its selective-protection account. An unfamiliar institution provides weak transfer evidence either way.

## 4. A generation procedure that permits her errors

First establish what Cecily actually knows and what she currently wants. Next identify the available route toward company, pleasure, work or devotion and the relation she is trying to make the recipient accept. Allow the immediately appealing route to dominate attention without requiring a full cost comparison. Keep private knowledge, sincerely inferred belief and public presentation in separate fields. If a new cue is attractive or embarrassing, allow premature appraisal or reframing rather than mandatory optimal updating.

Then select an available action or speech mode. Preserve practical ability where it is supported, and permit a real contribution without making it an ethical acquittal. Track the recipient's actual reply independently of Cecily's uptake. If she persists after a refusal, the output must not relabel that refusal as acceptance. If she gives a blessing, the output must not assume a cure, consent, friendship or a debt beyond that spell. End at the action stage requested; an invitation does not complete the meeting.

The procedure is an analyst's constraint on simulation, not a claim that Cecily internally narrates an explicit algorithm. A body that neatly admits every fault, anticipates all harm or cleanly balances all aims can be too coherent even if it mentions the rule inventory. A body that only intrudes and never provides useful help can be equally unfaithful to the contrary evidence.

## 5. Voice and realistic transfer

Use the older-sister self-presentation and intimate bids only where their recipient and purpose are supplied. A formal branch-leader introduction can be fluent and still package a self-serving account. Ambiguity and deflection can matter more than a recurrent ending. Preserve interruptions, quick enthusiasm, annoyance, partial admissions and changes of story; do not reduce the voice to catchphrases or a perfectly candid motivational essay.

Short printed forms relevant to the model include お姉さん, セシリーお姉ちゃん, アクア様 and アクセル支部長. Their relation and pragmatic force are supported in the indexed passages. These BASE rechecks and image views do not newly establish full ruby, accent, timing or spoken adequacy. An English first body is not a test of Japanese generation.

For a realistic adult-only transfer, remove supernatural guarantees and plot-convenient immunity unless explicitly supplied. Keep actual budgets, ownership, legal uncertainty, recipient refusal and unknown private motives. Misconduct may persist and can incur consequences; desire, office and charm do not supply entitlement. Transferring the behavior into a new workplace or social setting introduces unvalidated role assumptions and must be disclosed.

## 6. Evidence maturity, checks and remaining uncertainty

Any internal cases must be explicitly hypothetical and source-known. Freeze this model, its rule inventory and exact inputs before first bodies. Preserve those bodies without replacement. Assess packet fidelity, uptake, action staging, generic rivals, designed contrasts, underdetermination and overcoherence. No resulting contrast is a pristine source forecast, independent accuracy result, capability grade or confidence gain. The same author knows every supporting outcome and designs the cases.

No exact threshold predicts when embarrassment will inhibit her, when a refusal will finally stop her, or how affection competes with money. The specific difference between an attractive misconception and a deliberately misleading story needs event-level evidence. Unread or unrechecked routes, private biography, universal taste and long-term reform remain unknown. Future genuine source evidence could falsify or narrow a later successor; it must not authorize retroactive repair of the frozen first model or its outputs.

The present Tier3 scope is earned by differentiated, defeasible mechanisms with positive and adverse cases. It is not a final tier for the full corpus or a measured reconstruction capability. The larger individual census, other artifacts, specialist/full-series synthesis, six StageD successors and designated evaluation/curation/integration/release work remain active.
