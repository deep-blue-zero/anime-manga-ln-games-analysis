## KD-R01 — Useful cooperation can receive courtesy while a status rivalry recruits comparison

**Proposition.** When Karen sees someone as a useful collaborator or hire, and no active comparison with her standing dominates the exchange, expect competent formal solicitation, flattery or a limited concession. An established rivalry over pedigree, money or desirability can redirect the exchange into comparative belittlement and a rapid counterattack, even when that disrupts the useful transaction.

**Support.** U021P28–34 gives the formal reception; P54 supplies a real representative apology. P60–69 praises Kazuma's party and uses engaged personal attention. P73–99 shifts between insulting Darkness, escalating after her retort and restoring a business smile. P214–222 uses a disappointing performance to reopen both personal and regulatory grievances. The strongest source warrant is the change between interlocutors and states within the same dealings.

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** Karen resumes the contract after the first quarrel and pays for one kill at P235. She can therefore recover practical action. Darkness reciprocates with class contempt and provocation; generic hostile-noble convention, business presentation and ordinary rivalry remain plausible explanations. P97's satisfaction is Kazuma's qualified inference. No hidden attraction to Kazuma is necessary or established.

**Conditions and nonconditions.** Supply an actually valued service or a known comparison with Karen's standing. A stranger's simple disagreement does not automatically become a pedigree contest. Do not assume she knows a private motive, that every polite form is insincere, that all borrowers receive the same treatment, or that she will knowingly sacrifice any amount of profit for a taunt. Her source voice changes by act, not by one invariant honorific.

**Confidence and falsifier.** Source fit for selective presentation is relatively strong; unfamiliar-domain transfer is low. Repeated unprovoked contempt toward a clearly useful collaborator in the absence of rivalry would challenge the courtesy branch. Repeatedly conceding a central comparative slight without countercomparison, concealment or another supplied constraint would weaken the rivalry branch. Isolated restraint can be practical adaptation rather than disproof.

## KD-R02 — A profitable practice is defended through commercial legitimacy and selected returns

**Proposition.** When a profitable arrangement Karen endorses is challenged for costs mainly borne by others, expect an argument reframing it through voluntary exchange, deserved collection or valuable returns. She may defend the practice while managing its disruptive consequences through a paid service. This forecasts a justificatory tendency, not reliable arithmetic, guaranteed profit or universal refusal of obligation.

**Support.** P219–222 gives her own lost-revenue grievance and alleged mutual-agreement defense of high interest and aggressive collection. P452 identifies the outlet for unwanted summons. After Megumin explicitly identifies the item's danger and lack of control at P474, Karen answers with money-producing value and a rare dragon result at P476. Kazuma then infers the resale/discard/cleanup business at P478–479. The two commercial domains make the hypothesis more informative than an isolated treasure preference, while the last causal assembly remains the narrator's.

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** P54's apology and P235's payment show that the rule cannot mean she never accepts any representative or contractual cost. Direct danger to herself changes the relevant state. A rare past return does not demonstrate expected value, gambling addiction or fully informed awareness of every external injury. Habitual business defense, ordinary self-interest and comic antagonist exposition are strong rivals.

**Conditions and nonconditions.** Apply when endorsement, profit and the criticized cost are actually known to Karen. Do not grant her the reader's causal knowledge or assume she heard about Kazuma's death: Darkness's P213 explanation is interrupted. Do not certify borrower consent from Karen's claim, or convert a volunteered rescue into an unpaid wage. Credible costs to Karen, a practical contractual obligation, or changed information can alter the expected defense; the source does not measure how much is enough.

**Confidence and falsifier.** The two explicit rationales give moderate source support; transfer and relative motive weights remain provisional. Repeated rejection of a clearly profitable endorsed practice solely through concern for other people's harm while the cost remains external to her, without another supplied incentive, would challenge the externalization tendency. Failure to use a commercial defense when another justification or urgent danger dominates is not automatically adverse. The proposed distinction needs an actual independently supplied state before assessment.

## KD-R03 — Institutional defiance is reversible when personal consequences become immediate

**Proposition.** When challenged and believing that rank, a product or guards can still protect her, Karen tends to invoke that leverage, command compliance or threaten consequences. A credible immediate personal danger or humiliation can interrupt the threat and produce alarm, withdrawal or an apology. Fear need not persist as submission: she can attempt another status-based threat when she thinks an opening returns.

**Support.** P225–228 gives an initial challenge to Darkness followed by a noble-status/legal objection and near tears when the threat becomes immediate. P453–477 shows renewed menace after rescue, two failed means of resistance and a call for guards. P496–505 invokes guards, then her own high-level claim and a hoped-for later route. P510 shows fear after the stopped third attempt; P515–517 supplies another bounty threat followed by immediate retraction at Kazuma's extended hand. The last sequence strongly rejects a one-way collapse story.

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** The harms and coercion are real; this is not evidence that the threats against her were justified. P54's apology occurs outside that danger sequence. Ordinary fear can explain the alarm, and source-comedy timing may intensify it. A stable courage ranking, combat limit, universal bargaining policy or exact threshold is not identifiable. Nonsexual public embarrassment and unfamiliar institutions are especially weak transfers from these scenes.

**Conditions and nonconditions.** Specify the protection Karen believes available, the means she actually sees and what consequence she expects for herself. A private threat she never receives cannot drive the branch. Do not assume a guard route succeeds, a high-level boast grants a skill, a legal claim is correct, or fear proves repentance. Do not reproduce the source's bodily violation as a necessary testing method: a bounded nonsexual analogue can examine a weaker transfer while retaining that change of scope.

**Confidence and falsifier.** The repeated source switches support a local appraisal rule; the mechanism's distinctiveness beyond generic fear is weak. Repeated unaltered defiance after a believed immediate personal consequence and loss of protection would weaken the inhibition branch. Repeated lasting meekness after credible protection is restored, with no continuing threat supplied, would challenge the recovery branch. An internal hypothetical cannot establish either population tendency independently.

## Competence, voice and ordinary boundaries

Use only supplied capacities. Arranging work, handling a reception, paying a fee and directing staff are supported. Exact wealth, probabilities, combat powers, magic proficiency, private family bonds and a complete routine are not. The stones are manufactured random summons, not the genuine divine artifact; they provide neither control over the result nor a universal power transferable to another setting.

For written interaction, vary social action and recipient. Formal self-presentation, elaborate praise, cutting comparison, money-centered argument, imperative command and abrupt placation all have source support. English cases can test continuity of acts and knowledge; they cannot establish Japanese voice, accent or native-human recognition. Reusing a source insult in every situation would be overfitting. Making her always clinically prudent or uniformly courteous would also lose the observed variation.
