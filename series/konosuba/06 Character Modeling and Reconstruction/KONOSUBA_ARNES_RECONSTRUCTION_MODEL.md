## AR-R01: Retrieval loyalty includes immediate protection but can override present resistance

**Proposition.** Track the being she treats as master and her belief about its remembered relationship. Expect costly or immediate protective action and continued retrieval efforts. Present resistance may produce a concession, yet she may reinterpret it as lost-memory wariness rather than accept it as the final preference. A loyal self-description is not a complete account of the recipient’s interests.

**Support.** A01-A03: explicit master identification, invocation of will, name distress, paid silver and postponement after resistance. A05: rejection is reinterpreted as memory loss and capture attempted again. A07/A18/A20: actual catches and relief when Yunyun catches the creature.

**Contrary evidence and limits.** A03: she actually recognizes resistance enough to permit another night; not mechanically indifferent to it. A10: attachment to Megumin limits an expressed intent to harm her, but Arnes attacks Zesta. A17: she threatens other people to obtain the being; loyalty is not universal recipient-centered care.

**Serious generic rivals.** Generic loyal servant or attached pursuer; Generic paternalistic recovery narrative; Narrator anthropomorphism or inferred affection.

**Apply when.** A specifically valued recipient and known interpreted relationship are supplied. Separate current visible preference from her account of remembered identity. A concession or a rescue need not settle the longer retrieval objective.

**Do not assume.** No shared current mind, verified memory loss, global comprehension, biological kinship, precise magical pact or automatic consent. No rescue of every stranger or fixed willingness to sacrifice herself in every context. Do not invent remembered agreement that the scene does not provide.

**Current confidence.** Provisional moderate support for goal/response pattern, low-to-moderate for interpreting motive; generic loyalty remains a strong rival.

**Falsifier.** Repeated equally clear present refusals that she accepts without reinterpreting or pursuing would weaken the override component. Repeated opportunities for modest immediate protection of the specified master that she deliberately ignores would weaken the protective component. Source-known hypothetical agreement is not independent validation.

## AR-R04: Retaliatory pride competes with practical fear and a narrow offered withdrawal

**Proposition.** Retain injury, frustration and affront as motives alongside retrieval. She can enjoy making an opponent suffer or make cruel threats, yet retreat from credible superior force and offer a local apology when a particular judgment fails. A withdrawal offer is a meaningful action, not necessarily comprehensive remorse or abandonment of every goal.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparably credible superior threats with no revision or attempted withdrawal would weaken the practical-fear component.
