# Ozuma — original responses and retained diagnostic limits

The [model](../KONOSUBA_OZUMA_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md) and two rules were frozen before the [four exact prompts](OZUMA_SCENARIO_INPUTS_01.json). The four [original bodies](OZUMA_FIRST_OUTPUTS_01.json) were then authored directly once and sealed before these annotations. All bodies remain unchanged. The [structured annotations](OZUMA_FIRST_OUTPUT_ANNOTATIONS_01.json) bind each original hash. These are two dependent contrasts by the same informed root, with source knowledge, visible prompt differences and possible pretraining. They do not establish independent accuracy, a capability grade, mature transfer, Japanese performance or a confidence gain.

## Pair A — earned access versus an additional favor

**A1** preserves the remaining twenty minutes as part of payment and addresses Megumi rather than automatically blaming the guide. It allows waiting to be inconvenient without converting boredom into an emergency. This is directionally compatible with OZU-R01. The response also stages Megumi as about to shut the door, whereas the prompt supplies her instruction to Ozuma rather than that movement. This is an unsupported escalation of her supplied behavior, not an observed source fact. The line assigning responsibility to Ozuma for the agreement is plausible but more specific than the shared hiring arrangement explicitly establishes.

**A2** recognizes that the agreed access is complete, treats more time as a favor and asks for both shopkeeper permission and Megumi's assent. It does not grant access to someone else's map by fiat. No definite twenty-minute extension is performed; the scene ends with the question open. This is not a failed mandatory-refusal prediction: OZU-R01 does not prohibit voluntary help. The response's claim that Ozuma personally agreed for both companions again adds a role allocation not fixed by the prompt. Its unusually explicit concern about representing Megumi may be a generic fair-dealing script rather than a source-established Ozuma mechanism.

The pair differentiates obligation from an extra favor through reasons and conditionality, not through a clean binary action. That narrow compatibility is useful. It cannot determine how much cost Ozuma would accept, whether he would choose the same response with anger active, or whether the result is more character-specific than ordinary promise keeping. The original door staging and role additions remain in the evidence.

## Pair B — respected skill versus concrete contrary conduct

**B1** admires the real repair, becomes irritated with the bystander and lets useful capability support a favorable judgment about unrelated dealings. His internal inference that the rescuer hardly seems like someone who takes money and runs is an overextension. The prompt does not prove the deposit allegation; equally, bridge skill does not prove it false. The response begins to repeat the engineer's envy explanation and dismiss the report as rubbish. This enacts the kind of error proposed by OZU-R02 rather than repairing Ozuma into an ideal evidence reviewer.

**B2** retains admiration for the rescue but confronts the directly seen broken exchange. It asks for repair or return of coins and separately asks whether the bystander knows the earlier customer. It does not infer that one current wrong proves every older report. These choices are compatible with the model's open conflict between a credited benefactor and an acknowledged obligation. They do **not** validate this precise moral priority: the source does not show Ozuma resolving this engineered combination. The neatly separated evidence questions and proposed remedies may reflect the author's generic fairness and prompt cueing. Neither actual repair nor refund is narrated.

B2 adds concrete conduct and an exchange obligation; it is not a one-variable causal test. The pair cannot isolate gratitude from admired competence, a fairness norm from companion loyalty, or a character mechanism from ordinary social reasoning. Four bodies are not four independent trials. The engineer, guide and courier are hypothetical roles, not new canonical people or primary-source census entries.

## Voice, retained originals and consequence

The responses use direct address, a practical objection, irritation, rougher approval, a sigh and changing firmness. They do not certify Japanese particles, a dialect, an acoustic performance or a native-speaker imitation. The quieter A2 negotiation and highly organized B2 correction are especially uncertain transfers from the dense source episode. Plausible functional continuity should not be mistaken for empirical fidelity.

No body, model or rule was regenerated after this review. No score, source-first note, prospective history or earlier validation was altered. The current result is a provisional internal consistency/sensitivity readout with explicit adverse binding issues. Future evidence may warrant an attributable successor; this diagnostic supplies no confidence increase or reason to erase the retained original errors. It does not complete any principal/party/transfer Stage D successor or the final nine-category battery.
