"The bridge! Run where they can hear you and get those people off it! Stay out of the cart's path!"

My voice comes out much louder than I expect. Good. It needs to.

I reach for the lever, planting my feet before I close my hand around it. My wrist complains immediately. Of course it does. Of course this would happen with the one cart I actually paid for.

Don't look at the slope. Don't start imagining what happens if your feet go.

I pull, trying to put my weight into it without stepping in front of the wheel.

"Answer me! Did you hear? The people first!"

I can't tell yet whether I'm holding anything or merely making my hand hurt. I keep my eyes on the space between the cart and the bridge and wait for a voice to come back.
