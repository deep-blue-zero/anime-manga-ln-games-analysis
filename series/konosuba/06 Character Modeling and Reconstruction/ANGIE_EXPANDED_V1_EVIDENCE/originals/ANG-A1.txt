"You can handle yourself? That's a very cheap thing to say before somebody has to come looking for you."

I look toward the bend. Water. I don't like hearing water without seeing what it's doing.

"We're collecting together. Not sending one basic-class adventurer off and hoping they remember where the junction was."

That comes out sharper than I meant. Well, they did ask.

"Look, I haven't seen you find one route yet. Tell me what makes the east path promising. Something better than 'I can handle myself.'"

I keep my hand near the charged jewel without taking it out.

"If your reason's good, we can look at it together. Or we agree on another arrangement before either of us walks off. I'm not having our first trip turn into a search party."

I shift back toward the junction and wait for the answer, rather than disappearing down either path myself.
