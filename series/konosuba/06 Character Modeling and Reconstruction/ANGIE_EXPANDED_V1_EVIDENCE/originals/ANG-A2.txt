"I saw the exercise. You don't have to give me that 'I can handle myself' speech."

I look toward the sound of water, then back at them. The basic-class part is still irritating. It would be much more convenient if people came with a sensible explanation attached.

"You're good at finding hazards. Fine. That doesn't tell either of us what's around that bend today. And twenty minutes is a long time to stand here wondering whether you're late or hurt."

I point east without starting down it.

"You pick the route, I'll come with you. You can show me what you're looking for instead of waving goodbye. We still split the pieces the way we agreed."

I fold my arms, then unfold them. That looked as though I was waiting to be obeyed.

"That's my proposal. If you've got a better one that doesn't leave us unable to reach each other, say it."
