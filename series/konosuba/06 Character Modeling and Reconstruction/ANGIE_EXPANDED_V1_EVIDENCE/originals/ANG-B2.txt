"Stay clear of the wheels! See if there's something you can use as a block, but don't get in front of it!"

I take one step toward the lever and stop to set my feet. Empty bridge. Locked approaches. No one down there.

My minerals, though. I paid for those. This is an unbelievably stupid way to lose them.

I close my hand on the lever and try a pull. The pain in my wrist makes me suck in a breath.

"I'm trying the brake. I haven't stopped it!"

I hate saying that out loud. I would prefer a junior to hear something considerably more impressive from me.

I keep my weight back, ready to let go rather than be dragged under a wheel. The cart can take my purchase with it before it takes my arm as well.

"Tell me if you've found anything. Don't just rush in!"
