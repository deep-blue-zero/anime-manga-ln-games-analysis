For a moment the bookmark and the blush arrange themselves into a very familiar story.

No. Brother and sister. Their parents. Saturday. The librarian told you all of that, you ridiculous woman.

I turn back to the shelf and let the two of them have their surprise. A family occasion can be nice too. There doesn't have to be a confession hiding behind every handmade present.

My hand pauses on a spine. What sort of surprise needs a bookmark? Perhaps they're taking their parents somewhere. No, stop it. That is still inventing the rest because you've heard half a conversation.

I take the book out properly and look at its title.

"Excuse me," I say quietly to the librarian, keeping my voice away from their table. "Is there somewhere I can sit and read this?"

Something with a plot I'm actually entitled to finish.
