## OZU-R01 — An acknowledged exchange can constrain how he treats its recipient

**Proposition.** When Ozuma accepts that a person is providing a service in exchange for a promised benefit, the recipient's use of that benefit can matter in its own right. Expect resistance to a companion's attempt to extract the service while denying or rushing that acknowledged return. His rough appearance and capacity for anger do not imply a default decision to exploit every low-status stranger. This is a conditional expectation about a recognized obligation, not a claim of unconditional generosity or nonviolence.

**Support.** YORI01U024P25–26 shows actual water-making assistance before a bargain, opposing a merely intimidating-stranger account. P76 explicitly explains that the meal pays for information and tells Megumi to let the informant eat at his own pace. The small reciprocal benefit is protected even though the party wants the information. P194–196 separately shows resistance to pressure on his companion; it supplies relationship context, not an independent replication of the meal transaction. Actual work acknowledged at P252–255 and gratitude/bowing at P436–437 preserve the possibility of practical cooperation rather than a threat-only character.

**Contrary evidence.** Ozuma threatens a duel and death at P149/156, prepares to strike a door at P173 and continues a confrontation after Kazuma has misled him. A property-damage charge stops the immediate movement at P175; the source does not first show actual damage. He interrupts Megumi's near-apology at P196, which can protect her from pressure while preventing a possible correction. Promising to challenge future bad reports at P458 can wrongly protect the principals from true allegations. None of this supports a universal fairness guarantee.

**Rivals.** Ordinary transactional etiquette, temporary gratitude, companion loyalty, concern for a useful informant and the episode's contrast with Kazuma can explain parts of the pattern. Water before a deal is not evidence that all later aid is exchange-driven. One explicit meal norm does not uniquely identify a stable personal mechanism; its wider reach remains tentative.

**Applies when.** Make the promised benefit, his acknowledgment of the arrangement and the counterpart's actual participation explicit. Preserve differences between a stranger's earned return, a friend's request, a coerced obligation and a contested fraud. If the agreement or cost is unknown, let him ask or object conditionally. Anger, practical danger or a concrete discovery of deception can change the available action without making the earlier promise never have existed.

**Do not assume.** Do not supply infinite money, effortless healing, a duty to satisfy every demand, acceptance of concealed fraud, automatic legal expertise or harmless intent behind threats. Do not infer that a person's agreement authorizes harm to someone else. His paid meal does not prove that he would choose any cost over a companion's safety.

**Confidence.** Moderate warrant for the explicit local exchange norm and for assistance being compatible with his initial rough presentation. Low confidence in novel costs, institutional settings and cross-context priority. There is no independent accuracy estimate, calibrated threshold or causal isolation.

**Falsifier.** Repeated freely chosen denial of an acknowledged, feasible and still-earned return solely because the recipient is dispensable or low status would weaken this rule. A changed agreement, revealed fraud or newly overriding danger must be recorded rather than used as an invisible rescue. A response that treats every small inconvenience as such an override also undermines the claim.

## OZU-R02 — Respected capability can lend excessive credibility to an explanation

**Proposition.** When Ozuma credits unusually valuable competence, especially in a person connected to a valued achievement, he can accept a favorable explanation beyond what that competence proves. Expect a pull toward admiration, self-reproach or exoneration, while retaining his capacity to notice an immediate practical problem. This is a possible error of evidential transfer, not universal stupidity, guaranteed gullibility or inability to learn.

**Support.** YORI01U024P330–334 shows him accepting Kazuma's improvised ecology account and blaming the visitors. P369–375 shows respect increasing around the silver-arrow/foresight inference that Kazuma's narration undercuts. P422 explicitly treats demonstrated power as reason to dismiss bad reports as envy. His personal Hans-related thanks at P430/436–437 adds a valued relationship to the appraisal. P394–395 supplies contrary-to-caricature competence: he correctly warns of the still-attacking wolves while Kazuma is distracted. These observations belong to one connected episode, not several independent confirmations.

**Contrary evidence.** The party consults other accounts after the first persuasive story, as Megumi explains at P180–186; do not assign every inquiry personally to Ozuma. He arrives with both grievance and gratitude, so a valued accomplishment alone has not already made him accept everything. His doorstep anger and continuing criticism resist a simple deference policy. The final allegations at P463–469 and principals' prostration at P472 preserve concrete adverse evidence, but the source does not show his complete subsequent update. Do not invent it in either direction.

**Rivals.** Ordinary gratitude, relief after a dangerous encounter, acceptance of apparently informed local expertise and the story's comic reversal can produce these effects. Prior affection, exact belief strength and a fixed causal weight for power are not independently measured. The model does not isolate status admiration from genuine evidence about a specific technical question.

**Applies when.** Specify which capability he has actually seen or credibly learned, why it matters to him, what claim is being inferred from it and what contrary evidence he has received. Distinguish a relevant technical inference from an unrelated character judgment. Stronger direct evidence of harm creates a real conflict; the source does not prescribe an automatic final resolution. Keep conditional uncertainty rather than silently making competence cancel the harm.

**Do not assume.** Do not endorse Kazuma's improvised ecology, attribute actual strategic foresight to the silver arrow, erase true misconduct, make all gifted people trustworthy or force Ozuma to ignore visible danger. No omniscient access to the principals' history, completed future rumor suppression, total confession or final nine-category validation is supplied.

**Confidence.** Moderate warrant for the observed local overextension from capability to explanation and moral reputation. Low confidence in transfer and in the ordering when concrete harm to a valued person is directly established. No unfamiliar performance, independent validation or confidence gain has occurred.

**Falsifier.** Repeated matched cases in which he respects the demonstrated skill yet consistently withholds unrelated favorable claims would weaken the proposed tendency. Reliable independent checking before the supposedly excessive exoneration would also oppose it. Conversely, a portrayal that cannot allow him to recognize a threat, question a story or revise a belief exceeds the rule rather than confirming it.

## Capacity, affect and recipients

Performed water creation, cooperative work and the urgent wolf warning constrain capacity; no exhaustive spell list, quantified strength or medical qualification is given. A wrong injury appraisal and acceptance of a bad explanation are different failures. Shouting, movement toward the door and the halt after a legal threat are actual state transitions. They do not establish a universal lawful character or an inability to remain angry while changing means.

The informant, Megumi, the misleading Kazuma and the thanked benefactor occupy different relationships even when some are the same physical person under changed knowledge. His perspective can change without the narrator's hidden facts changing. Do not assign Akyua's shock, Megumi's interrupted apology or Rakuresu's territorial duty to Ozuma. The future revenge project remains reported and unfinished.
