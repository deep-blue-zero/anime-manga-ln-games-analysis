## Operating representation

Keep army origin, undead status, membership label, own chosen belonging and moral act separate. A later warm welcome cannot revise earlier pressured assent.

Preserve ordinary wants and errors. Do not make the outsider an infallible counselor or acceptance a debt repaid by unlimited emotional labor.

## N21-R01: pressured conformity and later chosen belonging are different states

**Proposition.** Under immediate social pressure21 may display uncertain conformity while still wanting integration. Concrete cooperation and welcome can later support an own claim of belonging and action for the town. The generator must not treat either the original denial or later pride as a timeless essence.

**Supporting evidence.** At ADV S032-03521 masks smell, first denies Axis membership and later stammers assent after the master's look/suggestion; Gilbert also needs practical lockpicking. S092 shows an own human-warmth statement and actually swallowing the jewels against town destruction. S100 claims pride in the manufacturing name and acceptance as an ordinary townsperson. **Contrary evidence.** The later speech repeats an unsupported 95% Axis claim and could reflect group influence.21 retains questions and can refuse another counseling loop. The welcome is local; it does not prove that every resident accepts undead or that early pressure was harmless.

**Rivals.** Generic need for safety, utility-based party membership, conformity to the player group, the reunion convention and a wish for shelter. Own declarations and actions distinguish later commitment from a bare nod, but do not independently reveal every private motive.

**Application conditions.** Specify the pressure and who supplies it, the actual opportunities to refuse, what concrete cooperation or welcome has happened and what21 says now. Expect tentative compliance under immediate pressure more readily than unprompted secure commitment; after real inclusion, allow active defense of home. Do not add an earlier welcome to force that change.

**Do not assume.** Freely chosen conversion, reliable 95% demography, universal acceptance, moral purity from outsider status, that current pride erases earlier refusal, or a new personal name supplied by the author.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the source's ordered contrast, lower for repeated exclusion, secure independent housing or a pressure-free religious choice. One arc is not several independent observations.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable pressure eliciting confidently unchanged refusal without conformity would weaken the initial response tendency. Repeated concrete wanted inclusion followed by no own belonging claim or protective interest would weaken the second tendency, while still allowing case-specific dissent.

## N21-R02: requested help favors questions and voluntary explanation, with a stopping boundary

**Proposition.** When a companion asks 21 for help understanding a relation,21 is likely to clarify the complaint and offer inquiry rather than claim already to know both minds. It can facilitate a local exchange and still decline more mediation; success need not mean full agreement or permanent repair.

**Supporting evidence.** Chiho privately requests help at ADV S085.21 asks whether the concern is giving money, only giving, or their earlier relationship, offers to speak with Otohime and obtains his willing account (S085-088). It distinguishes visible health from unseen hurt, while Otohime acknowledges a gap.21 declines another loop after Gilbert's role complaint. Borrowing and Chiho's doubt remain.

**Contrary evidence.** This is one linked requested conversation, and the contents are coauthored replay.21 does not independently verify Otohime's motive or end the conflict. Its broader competence and role may help; no evidence makes mediation an automatic duty.

**Rivals.** An author/player's social skill, narrative repair needs, a practical wish to keep the party together and general courtesy. Questions alone do not identify a unique therapeutic technique or real-world efficacy.

**Application conditions.** Specify who requests help and what they say; another person must choose whether to participate. Favor clarifying questions and a bounded offer before conclusions. If a person refuses to talk, retain that refusal. Preserve uncertainty, mixed acceptance and 21's own desire to stop. Do not manufacture consent or an ideal resolution.

**Do not assume.** Mind-reading, diagnosis, clinical therapy, compulsory disclosure, an obligation to mediate, ended debt, erased fear or forgiveness owed because the conversation was costly.

**Confidence.** Low to moderate for the local requested-help sequence and explicit boundary; low for unsolicited conflicts, coercive settings and unfamiliar people.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable freely requested conversations in which21 consistently declares hidden motives without questions would weaken the inquiry mechanism. Repeated unqualified acceptance of endless unwanted mediation would challenge the stopping-boundary prediction.

## N21-R03: belonging does not cancel finite utility, recovery needs or contingent mercy

**Proposition.**21 is likely to contribute practically while attending to its own unusual vulnerability and the possibility that a seemingly hostile being has a reason or deserves remembrance. Those concerns do not prevent combat once a concrete destructive purpose is revealed, and no wish by itself guarantees a rescue or restored life.

**Supporting evidence.** It contributes investigation/search and trap detection, yet the GM places event lightning outside the successful check (ADV S074-075, S079, S083). It worries about Turn Undead and receives a target-selection assurance (S080-082). It raises charitable possibilities before the client's threat, swallows jewels for human warmth after the threat, and later asks to mourn the dragon (S089-092, S108). The installed statue supplies actual recovery including undead21, explicitly as facility data (S100).

**Contrary evidence.**21 participates in enemy combat and can initially misjudge a client's purpose. The corpse is mindless, not the original companion restored. Target-selectable exorcism leaves it safe in that scene, and final evasion actually succeeds; vulnerability is not automatic injury or incompetence.

**Rivals.** Optimizing an unusual build, generic party cooperation, a player advocating a sympathetic monster, the house reward and narrative memorial convention. The source does not establish universal pacifism or a uniquely merciful nature.

**Application conditions.** Specify threat evidence, actual abilities, allies, recovery access and what the other being has done. Permit a question or charitable hypothesis before knowing a purpose; update once destruction is explicit. A detection result has only its defined scope. Hope for mourning is an intention until performed, and a facility's recovery follows its actual rule.

**Do not assume.** Omniscience, guaranteed detection, free healing, a direct Heal/miracle, harmlessness of every army being, immunity because undead, or restoration of the original dragon's agency.

**Confidence.** Low to moderate across several locally distinct decisions in one replay; lower for irreversible choices, strangers and conditions without a recovery facility.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable practical opportunities met with no contribution despite usable means would weaken the cooperation tendency. Consistent unquestioning attack before information would weaken contingent charity; consistent refusal to fight an explicit comparable destructive threat would challenge the combat qualification.
