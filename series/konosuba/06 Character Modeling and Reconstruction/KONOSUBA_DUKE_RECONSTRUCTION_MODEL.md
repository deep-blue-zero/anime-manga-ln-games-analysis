## DK-R01 — Recognized standing and Vanir connection can restrain coercion without settling the central dispute

**Proposition.** When an interlocutor appears weak and interferes with Duke's immediate concerns, intimidation or contempt is plausible. If he credits that person's connection to Vanir, expect the threat to be qualified and room for dismissal or negotiation rather than unrestrained attack. With Vanir himself, expect caution and deferential address while Duke can still assert that his own challenge should proceed. Affiliation changes treatment; it does not automatically transfer control of his goal.

**Support.** MAIN13 C3P357–363 gives intimidation after Dust's solicitation and subsequent seating after the test cover. C3P400–407 gives a knife threat when Kazuma invokes Wiz; P411–441 then gives partly reduced vigilance after apparent support, knowledge of the lich and explicit acknowledgment of Vanir's difficulty. The strongest affiliation witness is DUST05P395–406: Duke recognizes Small as a demon, suspects Vanir's order, threatens, then expressly says he is sparing her because she appears connected to Vanir. MAIN13FinalP341–342 supplies a polite noninterference request and retreat before Vanir. DUST05's earlier Dust/Darkness scenes are dependent MAIN13 retellings; Small's approach is a distinct encounter.

**Contrary evidence.** Duke says he will not withdraw from the Wiz dispute even for Vanir (C3P441). Darkness's noble identity does not by itself visibly constrain him. He threatens Small before crediting a reason for restraint and retains categorical hostility after letting her leave. No general ban on threatening known associates or proof of automatic obedience is warranted. His acceptance of Dust's cover does not reveal whether he believes it.

**Rivals.** Ordinary caution around a much stronger figure, simple fear of retaliation, temporary task priority, common face-saving and generic patronage can generate much of this behavior. One specific powerful connection may carry the whole effect. The source does not independently isolate affection, fear, etiquette and strategic advantage, or prove transfer to every institution.

**Conditions.** Bind his received information about power and the particular connection before the response. Specify whether an actual demand interferes with his goal. A credible Vanir link differs from a bare unfamiliar surname or an unverified boast. Preserve any supplied immediate danger, explicit prohibition or completed defeat as another material condition. The rule need not predict the same response to all three.

**Do not assume.** Do not invent Vanir's order, a hidden retaliation mechanism, a new demon identity, a universal hierarchy or authority carried by any noble title. No gratuitous violence is required when a warning, refusal or dismissal can serve the immediate purpose. Do not make politeness proof that the central challenge is abandoned. Do not treat all support claims as sincere shared plans.

**Confidence.** Moderate warrant for the local direction across distinct interlocutors, with the explicit Small explanation carrying particular weight. Distinctive cause, affiliation-only thresholds and unfamiliar transfer are weak. This is a provisional behavioral hypothesis, not a mature validated policy.

**Falsifier.** Repeated unqualified attacks on a weak, nonthreatening interlocutor whose Vanir connection Duke credibly accepts, absent an overriding supplied danger or prohibition, would weaken the affiliation constraint. Repeated automatic abandonment of his core challenge merely on hearing that connection would weaken its retained-goal branch. Unverified claims, unknown patrons or a materially different immediate threat are not matched counterexamples.

## Capacities, error and relationship boundaries

In the source world Duke can intimidate, use a knife, cast the demonstrated fire and holy spells, gather information and perform the depicted ritual. Actual resources and situation still govern their use. Lich transformation changes vulnerability; it does not grant invincibility or restore a body after the narrated outcome. MAIN15's received warning report is attributable evidence; its statement that Axel was already targeted blocks a sole-cause claim. MAIN17's retrospective description of Wiz blasting him to death is a later narrator report, not a witnessed new return or permanence audit.

He can overestimate his understanding of another person's meaning while possessing accurate facts. That explains the courtship/job mismatch without importing an original marriage intention. Actual social ease with Aqua and rejection of her divinity claim survive alongside military hostility. His final romantic invention under imminent destruction does not authorize fabricating a hidden long-term affection.
