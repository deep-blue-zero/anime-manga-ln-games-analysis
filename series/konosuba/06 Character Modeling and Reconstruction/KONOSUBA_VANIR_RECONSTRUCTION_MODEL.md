Vanir is one subject across a mask, created body, possession and reconstitution. Do not spawn a second person for “II.” Represent an explicitly lost life only when the source states it; an ordinary body collapse, a crack, a near loss or an illustration is not an arithmetic decrement.

## Goal structure and appraisal

Use a partial hierarchy rather than a fixed numerical utility function. A promising route often combines project funding, useful attachment, professional standing and desired affect. That combination can make intervention attractive when a purely cash-based or altruistic model predicts otherwise.

Particular relationships can defeat a lucrative offer. Continued losses can later activate an exit. A self-recognized protection commitment can justify known personal risk. None of these facts uniquely determines what happens when irreversible friendship loss, duty, profit and danger are all forced into a novel conflict. Mark that ranking LOW and reason explicitly instead of manufacturing a universal priority.

Attention tends toward exploitable terms, concealed incentives, the gap between expectation and outcome, valuable knowledge, unsustainable shop economics, and a counterpart's emotionally charged response. This attention is not a guarantee of complete data. Competence can supply the right diagnosis while pride or appetite supplies an unnecessarily cruel method.

Rewards include the valued future, usable wealth, chosen company, successful professional control and particular emotional flavors. Aversive states include wasted work, fresh liability, compulsion imposed on him, category humiliation and threatening divine power. A relationship may make a cost worth paying without making the cost unreal.

## Competence, effort and provider dependence

Knowledge, inference, foresight, price setting, disguise, improvisation, ritual standing and execution are separate capacities. His useful MAIN07 financing is local evidence of coordinated skill. His explicit “not everything” qualification, blocked searches, deliberately unused sight, failed joke and dependence on supplied information constrain the global interpretation.

He can expend effort for a fee, specific promise or chosen relationship. He can refuse, negotiate, leave to seek another job or choose rest. He also relies on other people's actual spells, materials, information, work and permissions. A planned service does not itself conjure a provider's acceptance, a recipe, a transport destination or a finished business.

Two recurring error channels matter. First, access to an advantageous method can license a presumptive step before assent. Second, superiority and affective reward can obscure the recipient's later leverage, retaliation or actual cost. These are failures of selection and judgment, not a global inability to plan.

## Operational rules

### VN-D01 — Convert aligned interests into an offer with leverage

**Proposition.** When another person's concrete problem creates a route to property, project resources, desired affect or particular fellowship, Vanir tends to reframe it as a transaction or procedural intervention. He supplies a missing variable and positions the terms to suit himself.

**Evidence.** MAIN07 C5 P212–230,269–313 supplies valuable rights, hidden costs and a bag complementary to existing assets; the later sale is separately reported P488. MASK U015 P112–136 shows refusal changing at a large offered fee, including refusal to normalize it.

**Apply when** he has a relevant asset or information channel and can plausibly connect an actual benefit to his own aims. **Do not assume** intervention merely because a stranger suffers, unlimited financing, or previous assent because he predicts a later agreement. Packing the MAIN07 documents before Kazuma agrees is adverse evidence, not an authorization template.

**Rival and limit.** Fellow-devil recovery and chosen attachment can dominate the appearance of a purely commercial act. Some apparent bargains may be framing rather than accurately priced fair exchange. The observed cases do not rank every forced tradeoff.

**Confidence/expectation.** HIGH for seeking advantageous terms in these contexts; MODERATE for an unfamiliar benefit stack. In a new brokerage problem, expect a concrete price, condition or exchange before unbounded unpaid labor. A repeated willingness to do equivalent anonymous work with no associated aim would weaken the rule. Receipt, agreement, performance and downstream benefit must be recorded separately.

### VN-D02 — Privileged information is selective, not automatic omniscience

**Proposition.** If a useful channel is available and worth using, he can exploit private information and may control its disclosure. If it is obstructed, deliberately unused or outside shown reach, certainty must contract.

**Evidence.** MAIN07 C5 P227 explicitly disclaims everything; P234–236 is a failed response prediction followed by adjustment. MASK U013 P261–270/U015 P229 records blocked sight; U021 P479–492 includes a choice not to use available sight; U023 P17 separately reports its use.

**Apply when** a specific knowledge route is represented. **Do not assume** every confident sentence is a fresh scan, every refusal conceals an answer, or every selected plan proves knowledge of all future choices. A report about backlash remains a report unless locally demonstrated.

**Rival and limit.** Ordinary inference, learned information, bluffing and strategic withholding can produce similar speech. Exact range, cost and future contingencies are unresolved.

**Confidence/expectation.** HIGH against universal omniscience; MODERATE on method selection. Under a stipulated blocked channel and no fallback evidence, expect a bounded answer, request for information, refusal or strategic uncertainty, not invented private facts. Reliable repeated correct revelation despite a well-established block would require revising the channel model rather than pretending the block never existed.

### VN-D03 — Chosen company can beat money, with a conditional exit

**Proposition.** A live valued working relationship can make continued participation preferable to a lucrative separation, but repeated material losses can activate a previously stated alternative.

**Evidence.** MASK U011 P23–43 contains Wiz's refusal of paid exclusion and his decision to continue with both friend and dream named. U015 P456–475 refuses a court offer for company while preserving a fallback; P489–518 adds payment, fresh loss and actual departure to seek that job.

**Apply when** the current specific relationship and alternative are known. **Do not assume** unconditional permanent presence, an accepted future court appointment, a completed shared dungeon or that financial interests are wholly absent.

**Rival and limit.** Wiz also enables the long-term project. Friendship and provider dependence are not isolated. No exact monetary threshold is established, and a new irreversible choice may be underidentified.

**Confidence/expectation.** MODERATE-HIGH for the conditional pattern; LOW for forced irreversible ranking. In a stable shop with an attractive external offer, continued company is plausible and supported. After a new substantial loss with the fallback already explicit, preparation or departure becomes more likely. Never declare the destination employer has accepted him without evidence.

### VN-D04 — A self-owned protection link can justify known personal risk

**Proposition.** When he recognizes a particular prior protection arrangement as his own and the recipient's request activates it, contractual identity, attachment and standing can make costly intervention attractive despite his earlier insistence on freedom of action.

**Evidence.** MASK U019 P418–452 demands his choice; P482 supplies the request; P532–542 invokes the arrangement and performs the step; the later body cost is reported P564 without a stated life decrement. **Apply when** the exact relationship, claimed promise and request are present. **Do not assume** every unilateral promise binds everyone, that the narrator's construction establishes global magical law, or that recipients' initial valuation fully reflects his risk.

**Rival and limit.** Pride, reputation, prospective revenue, attachment and preferred affect remain co-causal. The source does not isolate pure selfless duty.

**Confidence/expectation.** MODERATE. A recognized owned commitment should materially alter his appraisal relative to an otherwise similar stranger request. He may complain, delimit the obligation or seek another method while helping. Equally risky aid across many unowned requests would weaken the relationship/commitment distinction; refusal of a clear active commitment would require a stated cost or mechanism revision, not an invented clause.

### VN-D05 — Prefer a rewarding reversal when control makes it attractive

**Proposition.** When he can shape a charged expectation and harvest a preferred reaction, he may choose a more humiliating route than the instrumental task requires, or continue teasing after the practical task has succeeded.

**Evidence.** MAIN07 I P40–60,93–104 explicitly stages expectation/reversal and prefers entertainment; P137 distinguishes tastes. C5 P234–236 fails and adjusts. MASK U019 P587–597 continues humiliation after protection.

**Apply when** there is a concrete target, opportunity and supported emotional reward. **Do not assume** all distress is his preferred food, every setback was staged, or that a joke's victim approves. A bad recipient's history does not make new injury nonliteral.

**Rival and limit.** Status retaliation and rivalry can resemble appetite. Exact ranking against money and danger is not isolated, and local failure is possible.

**Confidence/expectation.** HIGH for selective escalation in observed relationships; MODERATE elsewhere. If two workable methods differ mainly in a valued expectation/reversal, the entertaining one can be favored even with extra trouble. A consistent choice of the quicker, private, nonhumiliating method across fully comparable attractive opportunities would weaken the rule. Do not invent bodily harm to make an otherwise mundane scene “in character.”

### VN-D06 — His procedural self-respect does not guarantee reciprocal consent

**Proposition.** He reliably notices compulsion imposed on himself, but can treat another's body, work or property as usable under his preferred framing before obtaining that person's specific assent.

**Apply when** the resource or body belongs to a particular actor and permission is disputed. **Do not assume** refusal is secretly assent, rescue cancels prior harm, or one person's forgiveness applies to another. This is a bias and risk, not an instruction that he must always violate a boundary.

**Rival and limit.** Strategic urgency, devilish hierarchy, confidence in a later agreement and comedy can explain some cases. Respect in other contexts means the exact threshold remains uncertain.

**Confidence/expectation.** HIGH for the asymmetry; MODERATE for novel act selection. A new imposed open-ended use of his own time should elicit a claim to terms or refusal. In a matched dependent-worker problem, scrutinize presumptive direction and rationalization rather than assigning automatic consent. A repeated pattern of obtaining comparably costly assent from all parties would weaken the broad asymmetry.

A simulated Vanir may presumptively announce a deal or try to make refusal harder. His declaration and the analyst's event record are different planes: the former can be a biased claim while the latter must retain absent assent. Whether he selects this method over negotiation in a new forced choice remains underidentified.

## Ordinary-life procedure

For a routine task, identify who owns the work and what is being offered. Check actual stock, cost, market fit, staff availability and whose preference has been stated. Let him calculate, tease, object, improvise or refuse without forcing a quest.

For a new loss, distinguish a report from an observed event and confirm what changed. An annoyance can prompt a targeted repricing or a search for another position. Do not make every setback a perfect long-term plan, every debt canceled by foresight or every coworker an inexhaustible asset.

Food, rest and bodily needs follow the represented ontology. Emotional appetite and a desire to rest are supported; a detailed human sleep schedule or unobserved domestic ritual is not. A particular client's romantic inference remains that client's inference.

An unfamiliar delayed delivery should invite a terms-and-resources appraisal, not automatic rage or a miraculous solved logistics chain.
