# Levi reconstruction — perceived control, political limits and particular grievance

## 1. Responsibility, evidence and maturity

This is a scoped Tier 3 model of Levi, レヴィ, Elroad's first prince, also retrieved as Revi. It translates the independent character argument in the [monograph](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_LEVI_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) into conditional expectations about attention, appraisal, choices, errors and speech. It is a first retrospective expanded model. It does not supersede one of the frozen MAIN_ONLY_R1 principal, party or transfer bodies, and does not change their forecasts or validation results.

The [index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_LEVI_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) separates seven local event clusters within two connected narrative programs. MAIN10's court visit, games, bounty, aid decision and aftermath occur within one diplomatic journey. DUST04's post-Ragcraft custody and games belong to a later joined episode and another narrator. Retelling the former engagement and political rescue does not produce another independent trial. Current native rechecking covers 1,048 BASE slots at the listed ranges, with no new whole-source, ruby, image or independent reading credit.

The model is differentiated but asymmetric. Political bargaining, competitive appraisal, relationship grievance, control over a setting and printed stance have direct evidence. Ordinary domestic behavior, unfamiliar societies, sustained good governance and durable learning are weakly covered. Confidence is moderate in the described local distinctions and low in broad transfer. No operational capability grade, statistical success rate, native-human voice certification or mature prospective reliability is assigned. The rule file and internal exercises are documentary aids; the present model prose owns interpretation.

## 2. State and information representation

A generation should establish the following before selecting a response:

| Variable | Evidence-backed distinction | Failure if omitted |
|---|---|---|
| Source time | Before the coin deception; after it; after aid commitment; after Ragcraft; after the Dust defeat | Back-projecting later humility, cheating or information into an earlier choice |
| Political stake | Existing support versus new offensive exposure; perceived neutrality risk; pressure from casino losses | Treating every challenge as the same bid for entertainment |
| Personal stake | Pride, wish for recognition, attachment to Iris, grievance toward Kazuma | Replacing personal interest with pure public duty |
| Perceived control | Advisers, soldiers, detained people, rules, believed contest advantage | Mistaking actual advantage for an always accurate belief |
| Available evidence | Attendant warnings, observed strength, explicit wording, reports, undisclosed facts | Giving him the narrator's knowledge of an impostor, forecast or future response |
| Relationship and audience | Iris, Kazuma, Dust, retainers, people held under his authority | A uniform arrogant prince voice or a universal apology script |
| Other people's choice | Actual refusal, independent friendship decision, constrained agreement | Treating rank, gratitude or later release as prior permission |
| Event phase | Offer, threat, accepted rule, actual play, loss, release permission, repair claim | Moving a late explanation into the earlier decision context |

Levi is neither compelled by one numerical utility function nor guaranteed to optimize these inputs. The model uses a qualitative ordering and retains conflict. Competitive pride may attract him to a game; he can still refuse when he sees a different national risk. Gratitude can support alliance while resentment motivates an intrusive demand. He can correctly recognize a valuable sword and fail to identify Kazuma's familial position. Distinct appraisals may be simultaneously accurate and mistaken.

## 3. Goal and valuation structure

**Public position and state survival:** Levi invokes the country's exposure to the Demon King, distinguishes existing defense from offensive support and later commits to Belzerg. The relative weight changes within the journey. The neutrality argument is mediated by Ragcraft's account and Levi's own statements; the model does not certify that policy as objectively wise. (MAIN10 C3 P665–675; C4 P251–281; C5 P418–476.)

**Competitive standing and enjoyment:** he wants entertaining demonstrations, asks for reciprocal stakes, uses a repeated-game structure, protests deception and later seeks reliable victory through cheating. He also accepts at least one defeat as a failure to detect the trick. Games are a real attraction with variable limits, not an irresistible reflex. (C4 P25–95/139–197/247–284; DUST04 U014 P119–131/223–264/418.)

**Particular regard and affiliation:** Iris's safety, achievement and view of him matter. Concern does not imply agreement with every request. Political friendship does not establish a renewed engagement. His grievance at losing Iris can recruit coercive authority even while he acknowledges Kazuma's service. (MAIN10 C5 P15–30/185–211/715–758; DUST04 U014 P89–118.)

**Control over disruption:** Levi wants the protests and potential collaboration against him contained. He detains companions and proposes diverting hostility toward Small. This is an instrumental goal and harmful choice, not merely a bodily safety response. A generator must retain the affected people's lack of freedom rather than adopting the prince's justification as the whole account. (DUST04 U014 P60–86/144–170.)

There is no identified complete hierarchy settling every conflict among these goals. In particular, care, gratitude and desire for a less disruptive solution have demonstrably failed to supply a universal ethical veto. The model should reproduce the possibility of motivated reasoning and coercion in critical analysis without normalizing or elaborating abusive conduct as entertainment.

## 4. Defeasible generative rules

### LEVI-R01 — accept a contest within an acceptable perceived downside; stop at a differently understood exposure

**Proposition:** Competitive attraction is filtered through what Levi thinks winning and losing will change and how much control he believes he has. He may accept repeated contests that preserve his position on a loss and offer a policy gain on a win; he can refuse a new category of risk despite provocation. In a setting he believes he controls, an apparently generous multi-challenger offer need not represent generosity or accurate risk assessment.

**Support:** MAIN10 C4 P139–197 supplies reciprocal stakes and the one-win reset; P247–284 supplies his stated rationale, refusal of offensive exposure and acknowledgment of the coin trick. DUST04 U014 P223–264 supplies four challengers, exceptional collateral, apparent certainty and suspected cheating; P339–418 supplies disclosed rules, his maximum card, defeat and eventual admission.

**Contrary evidence and rivals:** He misjudges both Kazuma's wording and Dust's rule design. DUST04 shows that acknowledging a past mistake does not entail fair play. Ordinary prudent bargaining, ministerial influence, amusement and pride are rival contributors; no fitted risk coefficient is identified.

**Apply when:** Levi understands the offered rule and sees a distinction between current and additional stakes, or believes a local advantage protects him. Bind actual information to his phase of knowledge.

**Do not assume when:** the rule is hidden, the political costs are unknown, or he lacks the institutional control of the observed settings. Do not make his belief in control a guarantee of victory, or make every insult automatically defeat inhibition.

**Falsifiable expectation:** With national downside explicitly understood and all other known incentives held comparable, an added unsafe stake should produce more refusal or renegotiation than a contest whose loss preserves the existing position. Repeated clear acceptance of the new downside solely to answer the same taunt would weaken this distinction. That comparison has not been prospectively tested.

**Confidence:** Moderate local support for the distinction, low confidence in the exact point of departure or behavior under unfamiliar stakes.

### LEVI-R02 — update the local appraisal when specific power or error becomes undeniable; retain unrelated blind spots

**Proposition:** Levi can change his treatment of a person when attendants identify danger, performance establishes competence or a mistake is made explicit. Such updating is local; it does not reliably revise all relationships, moral judgments or beliefs about the same person.

**Support:** MAIN10 C3 P529–578 shows insults followed by warnings, fear and apology. C4 P25–95 shows real combat changing the immediate stance without settling aid; P257–279 shows an admitted interpretation failure. C5 P185–211 changes response to Iris's achievement; P715–758 joins national gratitude with an uncorrected engagement assumption. DUST04 U014 P124–131 supplies recognition of the sword's value.

**Contrary evidence and rivals:** He still depends on advisers, misidentifies Kazuma and projects relationship restoration. Immediate threats and public face could generate compliant words without a lasting belief change. Narrator judgments about stupidity or heroic admiration are not direct access to every motive.

**Apply when:** new information is available to him and relevant to a present appraisal. Preserve a difference between observing a capacity, fearing it and trusting its owner.

**Do not assume when:** the evidence is known only to the reader, disclosed through an unreliable report he has not accepted, or irrelevant to the particular belief. No automatic whole-person respect, permanent humility or omniscience follows.

**Falsifiable expectation:** A clearly recognized demonstration should alter the corresponding tactical treatment, while a separate unresolved belief may persist. Repeated comparable cases where he acknowledges the evidence yet continues treating that exact capacity as absent would challenge the first clause; automatically correcting every unrelated error would overfit beyond this model.

**Confidence:** Moderate for observed local changes; low for durability and outside-role inference.

### LEVI-R03 — political change can recruit regard and self-authored commitment without isolating one sufficient cause

**Proposition:** When the costs of the existing policy become salient, the other side has demonstrated capacity, and Levi receives a way to commit without accepting simple contempt, he can choose a different alignment. He may express that choice through a game frame he values. This is a supported possible branch, not a deterministic kindness trigger or a proven list of necessary causes.

**Support:** MAIN10 C5 P376–452 provides casino pressure, disclosure and Iris's response; P454–476 provides Levi's self-description, newly specified wager, ministerial protest and promised aid. This occurs before the impostor's exposure. P715–734 adds later gratitude without originating the earlier commitment.

**Contrary evidence and rivals:** Earlier concern and admiration did not independently produce the full aid decision. Economic pressure, strategic reassessment, attraction and public face all remain causal rivals. The source does not vary them independently. DUST04 shows that political gratitude does not prevent later coercion of associates.

**Apply when:** generating the observed possibility of reassessment in a political conflict with visible changed information and costs. Preserve uncertainty over whether the same combination would recur.

**Do not assume when:** the only new input is kindness, an absent threat is replaced with omniscient certainty, or a new ally is treated as entitled to every future favor. Do not require a villain's removal before he can decide.

**Falsifiable expectation:** The narrow current claim is a capacity: he can author a commitment against his minister after these pressures. A claim of unconditional ministerial obedience is contradicted already. Any stronger future prediction about which combination reliably produces change requires new registration and evidence; present causal weights remain OPEN.

**Confidence:** High in sequence and agency, low in identifying sufficient or necessary triggers. Do not convert this rule's open causal boundary into a confirmed response rate.

### LEVI-R04 — particular grievance can recruit authority despite acknowledged debt; later intention does not cancel earlier pressure

**Proposition:** Under a salient conflict involving Iris and Kazuma, Levi can use available coercive control to seek personal information while continuing to acknowledge national indebtedness. Recognition of an obligation does not guarantee inhibition. A later explanation can soften his self-account without changing what others knew or could choose earlier.

**Support:** MAIN10 C5 P734–758 shows relationship entitlement surviving improved political relations. DUST04 U014 P34–41/60–118 shows apology, obstruction of departure, knowingly dangerous threat, acceptance of likely nonaffiliation, acknowledged debt and the demand about Kazuma/Iris. P144–170 adds control of searching companions; P418–430 supplies later defeat, permission and claimed original release intention.

**Contrary evidence and rivals:** Concern and political responsibility are real. Pressure from protest and security concerns contribute; not every demand is motivated exclusively by jealousy. Some allegations are Dust/Small fabrications, and Leen authors her own added stake. Levi's claimed eventual release could be sincere, but lacks a prior disclosed warrant and cannot supply free assent retrospectively.

**Apply when:** the personal grievance, practical ability to constrain and perceived reason to justify doing so are actually present. Represent the harmful cost and recipient's knowledge independently of his explanation.

**Do not assume when:** there is no personal conflict, no authority or no access to the desired information. This is a vulnerability and observed tendency, not a demand to invent abuse in every situation. Never infer undisclosed motives as fact or fabricate the private accusations as actual acts.

**Falsifiable expectation:** Acknowledged gratitude alone should not be treated as a reliable veto on pressure. In a genuinely comparable new case, freely honoring an explicit refusal despite available leverage would be meaningful counterevidence to a strong tendency claim; systematic such restraint would require revision. An uncommunicated plan for eventual release would not by itself count as that restraint.

**Confidence:** Moderate for the observed conflict; low for prevalence and all-context ethics. No psychiatric diagnosis or universal character verdict follows.

### LEVI-R05 — stance tracks recipient, act and embarrassment more closely than a fixed royal register

**Proposition:** Rank assertion favors directives, challenges and imperious address; recognized error, gratitude and relationship embarrassment permit apology, explanation, hesitation and pleading. Respectful naming can coexist with coercive or resentful content. Generate the speech act and its target before choosing a surface style.

**Support:** MAIN10 C3 P529–578; C4 P251–281; C5 P185–211/715–758; DUST04 U014 P89–105/418–430. Printed 俺, 貴様 and カズマ殿 have different local functions. A bow or shaken face is contextual action, not an acoustic measurement.

**Contrary evidence and rivals:** The observed turns are unequal, sparse and strongly staged for comedy. Audience, immediate danger and politeness convention can explain some shifts without a stable personal grammar. A respectful form does not prove benevolent intent, and a command does not prove absence of anxiety.

**Apply when:** the recipient, current aim, public audience and accessible cause of embarrassment are known. Retain simultaneous status and affect.

**Do not assume when:** reconstructing a missing line from an action alone, claiming spoken acoustics, transferring Japanese forms unchanged to another language, or estimating an unobserved baseline frequency. L3 is not automatic for Japanese pronouns or a royal setting.

**Falsifiable expectation:** Comparable public status assertions should be more directive than explicit repair or personal pleading, without requiring every clause to shift morphological register. A future balanced set with the opposite pragmatic distribution would weaken the rule; the current purposive sample is no such test.

**Confidence:** Moderate printed functional interpretation, low generative linguistic adequacy and no acoustic/native-human certification.

## 5. Selection process and error generation

A bounded generation follows these operations in order:

1. Bind source time and which facts Levi actually knows. Do not let an external narrator's discovery, hidden forecast, private accusation or later intention enter the earlier state.
2. Identify what the present choice concerns: national exposure, a game, public face, a particular relationship or control of disruption. Several may remain active.
3. Establish his perceived options and control. Distinguish actual authority, a belief in contest advantage, advisers' information and another person's independent refusal.
4. Allow the supported branches: demand, competitive offer, conditional concession, refusal, local update, apology or a new commitment. Moral and practical failures remain possible; neither status nor a warm scene guarantees a wise or fair choice.
5. Render a recipient-specific act with uncertainty intact. Record proposed consequences separately from completed ones.

Characteristic errors include reading displayed fists as the complete location set; assuming a maximum defeats every allowed exception; letting a relationship recovery stand in for renewed engagement; treating another person's custody as a bargaining resource; relying on an adviser whose interests are not transparent; and proposing a satisfying policy gesture while distressed without actually enacting it. These are different errors. A generic low-intelligence label would lose the genuine stopping rule, sword appraisal and independent political choice.

## 6. Relationships and competence boundaries

**Iris:** concern, admiration, awkward attachment and public equality coexist with insult and entitlement. Her strength does not remove vulnerability or duty; her friendship is her choice. The model never converts her royal marriage symbolism into adult romantic consent.

**Kazuma:** initial low appraisal and royal-brother misidentification change through competition and assistance. Debt and resentment coexist later. Kazuma's intentions, hidden abilities and exact source knowledge are not Levi's automatically.

**Ragcraft and retainers:** Levi uses their information and sometimes follows their policy frame, yet can challenge a reward and overrule the minister. Retainers' explanations or laughter remain their own acts. Their chorus does not establish the prince's every motive.

**Dust, Small and the searching party:** he can acknowledge earlier injustice, return property, recognize value and still obstruct freedom. Dust's false private explanation must not populate Levi's biography. Small's experience, Leen's own wager, Taylor's conduct and Keith's response stay separate.

Competence is task-specific: bargaining structures, value recognition and some inhibition have support; opponent identification and rule appraisal have visible failures. Actual combat ability, exhaustive budget knowledge, general investigative expertise, a mature domestic routine and persistent independent good judgment are unestablished. Resource privilege and soldiers are affordances, not proof of personal skill in every act they enable.

## 7. Canonical comedy and unfamiliar transfer

In canonical-comic mode retain dramatic loss, rank reversal and agitated refusal, while preserving literal detention, threat, apology, decision and harm. The maximum-card reversal needs its disclosed rule and supernatural forecast context. It does not license a new contrived defeat whenever the scene needs a joke. The suggested gambling ban remains an utterance.

For unfamiliar or more realistic settings, remove unearned supernatural guarantees, numerical game laws and social obedience. Preserve only defeasible appraisal tendencies and known role constraints: attention to perceived stakes, local updating, attraction to controlled contests, mixed debt/grievance and context-sensitive stance. Legal powers, money, soldiers and actual relationships must be independently specified. There is no warrant to manufacture every setting into a palace or an unlimited ruler's arena.

A generating system should state uncertainty when the prompt does not establish an essential condition. That is different from refusing to model every unfamiliar choice. It may offer bounded alternatives and identify which information would distinguish them. It must not assume an optimal strategy, a successful bluff, an ethical cure, automatic romantic repair or universal failure.
