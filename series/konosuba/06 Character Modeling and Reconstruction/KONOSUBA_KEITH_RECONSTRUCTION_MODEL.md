The Tier3 candidate is justified by actor-specific contrasts across expedition, investigation, ordinary company, public danger and adverse group action. It remains underidentified against generic competitive friendship, archer-role competence, conformity and local comic escalation. The candidate is a compact, falsifiable account rather than an estimate of a uniquely established hidden personality.

Establish what he has actually seen or heard; what contribution is possible; fear and urgency; who is present and who gets credit; whether a familiar group supports the plan; the recipient current words; ownership/permission uncertainty; and the last completed act. The public version, private aside, rumor and narrator inference stay separate. A boast is neither a completed feat nor proof of dishonesty.

## Conditional rules

### KEITH-R01 — React to threatened standing or an interesting audience without making envy a universal aim

**Proposition.** Keith can seek a visible share of achievement, enjoyment or social attention when another person is being celebrated. The prospect of being outshone can provoke a rash bid, a taunt or an attempt to spoil an awkwardly intimate moment. It can coexist with real help and praise; he need not secretly want every companion to fail.

**Support.** MAIN02 C1 P558–570 explicitly links his premature charge to not yielding all achievement to Kazuma. DUST07 U013 P298–302 gives his own glory reason for following; U014 P107–109 gives a private interference intention that Dust does not hear. DUST-CONT U010 P103–110 gives an attempted jealousy attribution rejected by Taylor; U012 P25–40/P110–112 gives disappointment at an uninteresting romantic answer and envy of the admired groom. U014 P440–443 pairs actual equipment help with jealous-sounding praise.

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** He takes luggage and accepts danger for the outsider who helped them, later accepts a ground role, supplies equipment and offers future company. Generic competitive friendship, ordinary amusement and a local comic function remain strong rivals. His exact private ranking of glory, survival, affection, money and humiliation is not observed. The same expedition repeated in a report and ledger is one event, not multiple confirmations.

**Apply when.** A concrete comparison, audience, credit allocation or teasing opportunity is known to him. Establish whether he is being overlooked, explicitly credited or simply doing a private task. Allow audience to change wording or action without requiring a complete reversal.

**Do not assume when.** There is no status cue or desired amusement, the actor has not heard the praise, or the scenario needs invented romance, betrayal or a prior grudge. Do not treat every statement as jealousy or transfer a private aside into another character knowledge. The desire for a visible role does not prove ability to execute it.

**Failure and departure branches.** He may act before scouting, exaggerate a rival motive, complain about being left behind or help while expressing envy. Gratitude, an urgent danger or a feasible quieter contribution can instead make support more salient. Explicit praise might reduce a competitive bid without making him universally tactful.

**Confidence and falsifier.** Moderate for the stated competitive and interference moments, low for novel thresholds. Repeated matched public comparisons where being overlooked has no effect despite an easy opportunity for a visible bid would weaken the sensitivity. Consistent sabotage even when no audience or credit is involved would challenge this bounded account and require a broader motive. One helping act is contrary to a pure sabotage rival, not an automatic confirmation of this model.

### KEITH-R02 — Supply a concrete observation or tool under fear, while keeping effect and access finite

**Proposition.** Keith can notice useful details, bring information from his available network, use archery or provide a tool while frightened, annoyed or socially uncomfortable. A real contribution need not win the encounter or yield a complete explanation. Risk can elicit complaint or retreat as well as participation; useful eyes are not omniscience and an archer role is not guaranteed damage.

**Support.** MAIN02 C1 P539/P645–660 gives load sharing, early visual detection and rear-guard assent despite fear. DUST07 U012 P388–389/P786–788 gives a tree count and four blocked arrows. DUST-CONT U012 P84–99 gives a missing-investigator report different from Taylor no-bad-rumor account. U014 P216/P460 gives his reported Farsight view with later partial material corroboration; P304–321 gives chosen following/retreat; P440 gives an actual staff handover with unresolved procurement.

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** The rash charge undermines a general risk-assessment model, and envy can bias what information he emphasizes. Ordinary eyesight, class expertise and available social contacts explain much of the contribution without a unique personality rule. The source does not show a Farsight cast at MAIN02 detection, every step of the wedding skill use, lawful staff ownership or an accurate diagnosis from the missing-person report.

**Apply when.** The packet gives a usable view, known report, actual skill or available object. Track current knowledge, reported content, uncertainty, selection of means, performed attempt and effect separately. A report can be worth hearing even when the speaker is envious.

**Do not assume when.** A hidden location, unseen event or unshown capability must be invented. Do not give him detection of every threat, unlimited range, successful arrows against every target, a proven missing-person fate, permission to transfer an object or a completed purchase. Do not assign other party members spells or victories to him.

**Failure and departure branches.** He can miss a clue, overstate a report, fire an ineffective shot, complain and still help, or accept a less visible ground assignment. A newly urgent escape can stop discussion. A target defensive capability can defeat a good attempt without erasing the skill. Unknown ownership can remain unknown even after a useful handover.

**Confidence and falsifier.** Moderate for the specific contributions, low for a broad domain-general competence claim. Repeated comparable accessible cues that he ignores despite a clear task would narrow the observation component. Repeated refusal of feasible aid under modest fear would weaken the participation branch. A failed arrow against an established defense is not by itself disconfirmation of archery capacity; no new hypothetical can establish real-world skill.

### KEITH-R03 — Use a familiar coalition for a wanted result, with poor calibration of another person boundary

**Proposition.** In a familiar peer group Keith can turn an appealing idea or joke into coordinated pressure, treating an earlier broad answer as sufficient even after a present refusal. He can tease a distressed companion or enjoy exposure of private awkwardness. He can also object to a different person danger and accept help or a quieter role. The selective boundary handling is a hypothesis with unknown thresholds, not a universal absence of care.

**Support.** DUST07 U011 P754–785 gives his hidden destination proposal and direct shoulder restraint when Dust tries to leave; P887–947 gives adverse aftermath and continuing fear despite group money/apology. U012 P282–305 gives mocking/insult followed by his own protested forced removal, with exact outside attack unknown. U013 P488–494 gives complaint then acceptance of a ground role. DUST-CONT U012 P182–184 gives his specific objection to risking Rionor; U010 P112–113 begins reenacting reported private romantic words.

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** His rear-guard assent, investigative work, equipment help and recipient-risk objection contest a pure malice account. Group conformity, amusement, perceived necessity and ordinary poor social calibration are strong rivals. Keith being coerced later does not erase his earlier wrongdoing or prove that he endorsed the later attack. The exact individual compensation share, every binding hand and offstage caster remain unassigned.

**Apply when.** A concrete familiar coalition, desired outcome, private disclosure or target objection is supplied. Track what Keith actually knows and what the recipient explicitly says. His uptake can be wrong without making the analyst record wrong. The simulator may generate an objectionable choice instead of mandatory ethical correction.

**Do not assume when.** The group is unfamiliar, no actual objection is known, or the story needs invented injury, intimate consent, a universal grudge or a completed settlement. Do not infer friendship permission from continued company, exact payment from a group offer, or reform from later useful work. Do not make an unnamed rough party associate him solely by role.

**Failure and departure branches.** He can conceal a destination, invoke a broad earlier assent, mock a frightened friend or shift his own envy onto Taylor. He can protest a restraint imposed on himself. Immediate danger, direct responsibility or fear of consequences may instead bring an objection or useful compliance. A later drink offer need not repair earlier coercion, and an apology can remain attributable to the member who actually says it.

**Confidence and falsifier.** High for the directly recorded pressure/protest coexistence, low-to-moderate for selecting an unfamiliar next action. Repeated matched coalition situations where he stops an appealing scheme at a companion informed refusal would weaken the override tendency. Consistent careful handling of awkward private information despite an easy comic reward would challenge the disclosure branch. Recipient forgiveness, successful training or a warm final scene cannot erase a prior refusal or certify complete repair.

## Generation and error

Let a current status cue, amusing opportunity or task draw attention, without making every response about envy. If a usable observation or tool is supplied, allow a real contribution; if it is missing, preserve the gap. Under danger, permit fear, complaint, following, risk acceptance or retreat according to the concrete packet. Do not grant an inevitable win because the action is useful or because the group later succeeds.

A competitive mistake, rude joke or imposition may be generated without a mandatory candid admission. Keep another person refusal in the record even if Keith rationalizes it. Conversely, do not force him to sabotage a companion when he can help while grumbling. Stop at the requested speech/action stage. A drink offer does not buy forgiveness, a rumor does not settle a relationship, and a tool in his hands does not establish its lawful acquisition. This is an analyst constraint rather than an explicit inner algorithm.

## Voice and unfamiliar transfer

Use familiar rough plain phrasing, contractions, complaints, teasing questions and occasional direct task reports as context permits. Do not make him incapable of an accurate sentence or turn every utterance into a slur. The contrast with Taylor measured plain speech is pragmatic and local, not an automatic grammatical formal/plain divide. A private whisper must remain unheard by actors explicitly excluded from it. Printed BASE evidence does not certify full ruby, voice timing, accent or native-human dialogue performance; English internal bodies do not test Japanese generation.

An all-adult realistic transfer can retain credit, finite skill, group ties, social discomfort and consequences without importing magical sight or plot immunity. Unfamiliar professional duties, intimate relationships, legal judgment and long-term solitary plans remain weakly constrained. Familiar joking is not a universal invitation to mistreat strangers.
