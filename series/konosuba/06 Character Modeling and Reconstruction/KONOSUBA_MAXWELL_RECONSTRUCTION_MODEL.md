## MX-R01: acknowledged access limits can coexist with active service-seeking

**Proposition.** Maxwell can state a concrete inability while continuing to solicit work or reward. Constraint does not eliminate desire or agency, and another actor's demand does not create a capability. He may persist in the relation rather than solve an unavailable route.

**Supporting evidence.** MX28-01: says unknown location and extraordinary seal prevent retrieval, then asks for work/reward and praises Alderp's emitted cruelty. MX28-02: reports church weakening and excessive light while responding to further demands.

**Contrary evidence.** No controlled cast assay isolates the environmental thresholds. His later physical harm shows capacity in another domain. Alderp reports forgetting prior performance, and no complete service log tests reliability. He does not successfully retrieve every desired object.

**Rivals.** Simple obedience, a script of excuses, global incapacity or perfectly rational contract calculation. The candidate differs by retaining both specific stated limits and a continuing positive wish for work rather than treating one as proof the other is absent.

**Application conditions.** Specify known target location, seal, environment, contract and task. Keep what is stated, attempted and performed separate. A novel scenario may stipulate a capability but must identify it as scenario fact, not source discovery.

**Do not assume.** A performed failed spell where he only declines, universal holy immunity, unlimited remote search, or that inability excuses every harmful act. Alderp's hostile labels do not prove clinical memory or intelligence deficits.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the paired private limit statements; low for exact thresholds or mundane task transfer. Linked reports are not independent technical experiments.

**Falsifier.** Comparable cases where clearly acknowledged limits never constrain his available action, or where the same accessible feasible tasks consistently eliminate service-seeking for reasons the candidate cannot explain. Do not invent a hidden cast or missing target to rescue it.

## MX-R02: contract state permits a change from pressured service to selective refusal and collection

**Proposition.** Maxwell's available actions can change materially with acknowledged release and payment status. He may refuse a demand against a fellow devil and pursue the claimed reward after release. This is selective agency, not an invariant promise of nonviolence or universal freedom from contract.

**Supporting evidence.** MX28-03: Alderp releases the contract after mistaken confirmation; Maxwell refuses the order to kill the fellow devil. MX28-04: payment collection becomes actual arm breaking and anticipated continued access to Alderp.

**Contrary evidence.** Earlier service contains preferences and stated limits, so it is not complete robotic obedience. Mechanics and years owed are partly Vanir's explanation. No broad comparison demonstrates every contractual boundary or a consistent moral solidarity across unrelated devils.

**Rivals.** Opportunistic retaliation, payment enforcement, a taboo against fellow-devil harm, or simply the new stronger ally. The candidate requires tracking release/payment rather than crediting any later action to a hidden omnipotent loophole.

**Application conditions.** State current binding terms, release, known obligations, counterpart identity and what payment is actually delivered. A promise, claim and performance differ. Preserve the possibility that a scenario does not specify enough to decide enforcement.

**Do not assume.** A universal no-harm code, fellow-devil identity by mere appearance, completed settlement from a verbal promise, or that exploitation makes all later collection morally harmless. Do not assign Vanir's impersonation to Maxwell.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the one decisive transition; lower for general contract law, unseen past demands or comparable nonmagical service relations. The evidence is linked and source-known.

**Falsifier.** Comparable explicit release/payment changes that never affect selective refusal or collection, or consistent behavior under fully known binding terms incompatible with the proposed transition. Do not alter the terms after seeing a synthetic response.

## MX-R03: valued proximity can preserve a recipient for harmful enjoyment

**Proposition.** Where a counterpart's cruelty or despair is rewarding to Maxwell, he can express liking, seek sustained proximity and preserve that person as the object of continued harmful enjoyment. Affectionate language does not imply reciprocal consent or a goal of the recipient's welfare.

**Supporting evidence.** MX28-01: explicit liking for cruel Alderp and desire for rewarding work. MX28-04: actual delight in broken arms, appeal interpreted as mutual liking, and promise to remain near/preserve unbroken torment.

**Contrary evidence.** Much of the future is promised, not watched. Different motives—unpaid obligation, release and Vanir's presence—coincide. Later doing-well report is Vanir's, not a new observed episode. Ordinary attachment and care outside this relation are weakly identified.

**Rivals.** Impersonal sadism, contract collection alone, naive literal misunderstanding or benevolent devotion. The candidate adds a preferred sustained relation and kind of emotion, while retaining rivals if a scene does not establish that reward.

**Application conditions.** Supply the actual target, known history and whether the rewarding emotion is present/perceived. Keep target refusal and physical limits explicit. In serious transfer translate only stipulated capacities; do not invent a need to torture in every ordinary friendship.

**Do not assume.** Mutual romance, care from nonlethal preservation, a future already performed, human biological sex from 僕, or that simple speech makes him innocent. The recipient's terror remains evidence against consent.

**Confidence.** Moderate for this specific coercive relation; low for new targets, ordinary affection or indefinitely sustained outcomes. No measured affective-reward model or independent validation exists.

**Falsifier.** Comparable explicitly rewarding cruel/despairing counterparts consistently treated with disinterested release or recipient-centered care, or repeated proximity choices unrelated to any hypothesized reward once alternatives are known. Do not relabel all positive emotion as hidden despair.
