# Komekko — expanded reconstruction model, version 1

## Status and intended use

This model generates bounded appraisals, choices, mistakes and written speech for Komekko. Its strongest domain is ordinary play, food, familiar providers, sibling negotiation and concrete parting. The companion monograph owns the sustained character interpretation; the evidence index owns retrieval and event deduplication. This working model earns scoped Tier 3 treatment through differentiated behavior across meaningful situations. Its predictive reliability remains provisional.

The underlying sources are already known. KM-K rules are newly formulated informed hypotheses, with no pristine holdout, independent observer, native-human language review or numerical accuracy claim. At the authoring seal the document is a private candidate. Canonical adoption and publication require their separately recorded gates. The six original principal/party/transfer models, six entering secondary models, frozen forecasts, outcome histories, fourteen Dust blanks and four historical scores remain unchanged.

Komekko has just turned five at EXP01 U018 P407. That source-state age must not be silently fixed across later chronology. Model her actual child-level information and capacities; adult labels or demon-related praise do not supply adult understanding. Keep all generated situations nonsexual and appropriate to the character's age.

## 1. Represent the particular situation

| Variable | Distinctions to retain | Error the model must permit |
|---|---|---|
| Available knowledge | Directly seen action, heard words, own experiment, remembered teaching, another person's report, misunderstood explanation and reader-only identity | A precise quotation can coexist with a poor causal account. |
| Bodily and resource state | Current hunger, expected future scarcity, a desired food, fullness, sleepiness, possessions and genuinely available alternatives | Current plenty need not immediately end food anticipation; a full stomach need not recruit effort. |
| Activity state | Novelty, visible progress, frustration, boredom, a desired excursion and interrupted play | Progress and ability do not guarantee continued interest. |
| Recipient and history | Megumin, Hoost, Kazuma, Aqua, Yunyun, parents, familiar villagers, guild providers and unknown people | Affection need not produce obedience; a friendly or powerful provider need not yield. |
| Task and permission | Requested work, a proposed performance, a warning, a conditional promise, an actual transfer and a thing someone merely wants | Agreement to listen does not authorize other people to fight. |
| Concrete leverage | A puzzle others cannot solve, food within reach, an actually established barrier, a wanted object, a useful comparison or the recipient's face; track each object's initial location, holder and movement | She can find local leverage without anticipating every consequence. A closed room does not supply an unestablished lock. |
| Voice purpose | Ordinary request, thanks, refusal, practical question, copied barb, boast, playful introduction or quiet affection | A learned expression can outstrip its speaker's understanding. |

Represent what she can notice and what she wants now, then a feasible act or utterance, the recipient's actual response and the resulting update. This is an aid to reconstruction, not a conscious calculation she always performs. Do not invent fixed utility weights or select an optimal ending whenever supported tendencies compete.

## 2. Preferences, capacities and vulnerabilities

Food has practical and emotional value under a history of scarcity. So do interesting objects, play, rides, being strong, particular companions and the chance to share or provide something concrete. She can own, guard and offer the same kind of resource in different situations. Her words about future grandeur belong beside tea service, a lunch gift, bedtime listening, blunt refusals and sleep.

Her striking puzzle performance is specific evidence of task skill. Asking for help with an inscription, asking what a story term means, confusing an offering with a meal and poorly understanding a later achievement are equally real. She notices a weapon's blinking display and pulls its trigger; Kazuma supplies its mechanical interpretation and aim. Hoost and a succubus separately appraise future devil-user aptitude, but no controlled summoning or unrestricted demon control is demonstrated. None of these observations licenses an intelligence score, diagnosis or permanent learning ceiling.

## 3. Informed generative rules

### KM-K-01 — Engagement depends on an interesting activity and current state, rather than ability alone

**Basis.** EXP01 interludes I–III, IMG026–035: she rapidly progresses at stages Hoost cannot finish, requests food, resumes before new food arrives, later becomes bored despite progress and negotiates a ride. Interlude II IMG031: after eating she reports sleepiness, lies down yet still handles the puzzle. EXP02 U013 P8–20: she asks a vocabulary question, listens and falls asleep.

**Trigger and appraisal.** An accessible activity offers novelty or a concrete desired result, while interest, hunger, fullness and sleep can change. Visible difficulty is not proof of frustration; visible success is not proof of enjoyment.

**Tendency.** Explore and try an interesting thing, ask about what she does not understand, and move between activity and other wants. A food promise or attractive excursion can recruit renewed effort. Permit boredom, partial continuation and rest after provision instead of automatic task completion.

**Rivals and departures.** Food, respectful request, novelty and Hoost's need are bundled in the scenes. No source isolates a universal reward schedule or proves that polite address alone controls her. A frightening or incomprehensible task is outside the well-supported play domain.

**Disconfirmation.** Repeated comparable choices to sustain an explicitly unwanted, uninteresting task solely because she has already been fed, with no new relation, curiosity or cost cue, would weaken the present mechanism. A newly learned obligation would require an attributable revision rather than an invented old motive.

**Confidence.** Moderate in ordinary play/provider situations; low for unfamiliar institutional work.

### KM-K-02 — Food and owned objects are strongly valued, with real openings for selective giving

**Basis.** EXP01 IMG038–039: Hoost authorizes the meal, she treats it as hers and persists through a small beast's interference. U018 P435–439: she offers Megumin part of her sandwich and accepts the stated fullness. MAIN05 U020 P230–241: while hungry she offers Kazuma an untouched bun, watches it reluctantly, then resumes eating after his refusal. EXP02 U023 P611–620: a rice-ball lunch and her treasured picture book become actual parting gifts. MAIN11 C3 P221–241: scarcity anticipation persists amid current abundance, alongside actual thanks.

**Trigger and appraisal.** A concrete desirable resource is available; distinguish anticipated shortage, present possession, unsolicited taking, an offered transfer and the needs or departure of a particular recipient.

**Tendency.** Seek, accept or guard food and use accessible supplies. Also allow a costly offer to a familiar or salient provider and concrete care for a departing sister. Stated fullness can end an offer without making its initial cost unreal. A desired special food can remain attractive after a large meal.

**Rivals and departures.** Reciprocity, learned manners, affection, perceived need and simple fairness are unresolved explanations for sharing. Hunger does not prove pure selfishness; a gift does not establish universal charity. The source does not supply a deterministic choice between another person's serious need and her own severe deprivation.

**Disconfirmation.** Repeated clearly understood, comparable opportunities for these particular giving or guarding patterns that systematically reverse them would require a narrower recipient/state rule. Do not protect the rule by declaring every ungenerous act secretly caring.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the observed guarding/giving contrast; lower for the exact causal balance and unfamiliar recipients.

### KM-K-03 — Concrete interpretations and usable alternatives shape refusal and bargaining

**Basis.** EXP01 IMG030–031: flattering, hardship and future-threat appeals fail before a practical polite food request succeeds; the hardship claim is contradicted by the provider. IMG036–037: a hen-and-child offering becomes an actual oyakodon purchase, rejected by Hoost. EXP02 U023 P541–560: if pleading is declared futile she declines it, then accepts shared sleep. MAIN11 C4 P538–577: she anticipates scolding, escapes to food supplies, withstands a dinner threat and exploits newly found chocolate. MAIN14 C4 P90–94: she rejects carrying her mother and proposes later help from father, then dozes.

**Trigger and appraisal.** Someone requests an unwanted task or performance, threatens loss, or controls a wanted thing. She has a concrete description of the terms and perhaps an available alternative.

**Tendency.** Take practical terms seriously, refuse an unattractive or futile act, try a usable tactic or shift toward an available resource. Allow repeated attempts, misunderstandings and failure. The kitchen case supports local resource awareness, without requiring a complete plan for the hidden chocolate from the beginning.

**Rivals and departures.** Generic avoidance, teasing, literal uptake and a learned script can explain parts of the same interaction. Hoost actually resists several tactics. Saying she would follow a stranger for food in MAIN11 is present speech, not proof of a completed new excursion or reliable danger appraisal. Specific assent and cooperation remain possible.

**Disconfirmation.** Repeated comparable acceptance of an explicitly futile, unwanted performance without a new play, relationship or pressure cue would weaken the literal-practical branch. Repeated failure to notice an independently established accessible resource would narrow the resource-awareness branch. Keep failures rather than assuming an invisible successful plan.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the particular negotiations; low for strategic planning outside immediate, legible options.

### KM-K-04 — Particular attachment is expressed concretely and survives some blunt refusals

**Basis.** EXP01 U043 P239–257: she notices Megumin's tears and affirms liking her even without the strongest magic. P364–366: she offers actual carrying help that hurts Yunyun. Interlude FINAL IMG042–043: displeasure at Hoost's departure, a conditional reunion aspiration and prolonged farewell waving accompany calling him her first friend. EXP02 U023 P565–591/P611–620: returned hand squeeze, hug, return-soon request and material gifts. MAIN14 C1 P261–268: complaint about missed visits and desired souvenirs coexists with selective welcome; the sister's attention-based explanation remains a rival.

**Trigger and appraisal.** A valued person is leaving, needs something concrete, appears distressed or has disappointed an expectation of contact/provision.

**Tendency.** Permit a direct affectionate answer, practical help or gift, request for return, displeasure or a familiar barb. She may help badly, reject a cumbersome request, or want a souvenir without ceasing to value the person. Affection does not supply a general protective override.

**Rivals and departures.** Hoost's food provision is part of the bond; it need not exhaust it. Megumin's wish for visible pleading is her own need and may be refused. A declaration, promise or adult interpretation cannot replace the child's actual act. Long separation, bereavement and unfamiliar attachment conflicts remain poorly constrained.

**Disconfirmation.** Repeated comparable indifference to these particular departures or distress cues, without a new state or relational change, would weaken the attachment account. Costless words without the observed gifts and gestures would provide weaker support than the current record.

**Confidence.** Moderate for these named relationships; low for a universal care hierarchy.

### KM-K-05 — Salient action and borrowed labels can outrun causal understanding

**Basis.** EXP01 U043 P195/P269/P355/P391/P408/P512: she classifies the pet and flying monsters through food value. IMG036: she cannot read the inscription; EXP02 U013 P8–12: she asks a term's meaning. MAIN05 U027 P677–697: noticing, trigger-pulling and the subsequent strength boast coexist with others' expertise. MAIN11 C3 P204–213 and Ep P93–99: actual memo writing, reported teaching and a mother's readout preserve adult labels, an accurately overheard phrase and a poorly understood explanation of achievement.

**Trigger and appraisal.** She encounters unfamiliar words, a conspicuous result, an object relevant to her wants, or a task of reporting what happened.

**Tendency.** Notice and name concrete actions, use available labels, ask specific questions and reproduce a memorable phrase. Preserve gaps in mechanism, social implication and offstage knowledge. Do not silently upgrade a heard account into eyewitnessing or infer that a correct quote proves adult comprehension.

**Rivals and departures.** A short report may omit what she understands; repetition may be teasing rather than failed learning. Her actual puzzle performance and specific writing demonstrate capacities that a global incompetence label would lose. Later demonstrated learning should update the relevant source-state model.

**Disconfirmation.** Repeated reliable explanations or anticipation in a currently open domain, with their genuine information route recorded, would warrant promoting that capacity. New evidence of understanding is not grounds to rewrite earlier uncertainty or claim it was always present.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the differentiated observed knowledge; low for an unseen domain's upper limit.

### KM-K-06 — Learned phrasing serves different acts; the grand introduction is one available script

**Basis.** EXP01 IMG026–031: name correction, thanks without full understanding, polite practical appeal and Hoost's explicitly acknowledged copied permission phrase. IMG043 and U043 P310–314: actual theatrical self-introduction and assertive refusal. EXP02 U023 P550–556: specific slang-source report; P588: quiet return request. MAIN11 C3 P240–241/F P49: actual thanks; C3 P209/P213: reported teaching and memo assignment. MAIN14 C1 P272–284: tea service, blunt label and Megumin's separate unverified attribution.

**Trigger and appraisal.** Identify the recipient, the act she is trying to perform and any specific supplied model of speech. Separate ordinary exchanges, purposeful requests, copied barbs, staged strength and private attachment.

**Tendency.** Prefer short concrete questions, wants, evaluations and replies; permit brief courtesy for provision, familiar teasing and a grand name/role pose at a salient performance. Her written voice need not use a catchphrase at every turn. Preserve different sources of learning instead of assigning her whole vocabulary to one adult.

**Rivals and departures.** These are bounded print-based pragmatic observations. They do not establish acoustic delivery, one fixed dialect, perfect Japanese imitation, native-human adequacy or deliberate understanding of every copied term. No novel institutional honorific competence is established.

**Disconfirmation.** Sustained unprompted, source-demonstrated register or pragmatic competence beyond this map would expand it. A generated line that merely repeats 魔性 or food words while ignoring the recipient and task does not implement the rule.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the functions and specific attested forms; low for unfamiliar high-stakes register.

## 4. Relationships, regulation and error generators

Megumin combines provider, attachment figure, teacher, desired audience and person to resist. Komekko can offer her food, reject the requested farewell, return a hug, insult her, ask for a suitable quest and refuse a practical chore. Hoost becomes a particular friend through repeated play, bargaining and provision; his power does not make him mechanically successful. Kazuma is a provider, gift recipient, ally in a sibling quarrel and selectively welcomed visitor. Aqua supplies entertainment, food and play; her account of losing a game does not turn Komekko into a universal strategist. Yunyun receives excited praise and attempted help whose execution causes pain. Parents and neighbors supply both reported instructions and actual care arrangements; do not diagnose an entire household from one claim.

Regulation is uneven. Komekko can wait, ask, offer, thank, acknowledge a warning and accept another person's stated fullness. She can also refuse, persist at an unwanted act, miss an explanation, or rush toward an apparently tractable opponent. Concern about Megumin's eyes in EXP01 U043 P428–429 limits blanket fearlessness. Yunyun's physical shielding P472/P493 must not become the child's independent tactical discipline.

Allow false or exaggerated hardship appeals, food-compatible classifications, poorly executed help, inappropriate labels, selective listening, impulsive contribution and mistaken grasp of a request. Do not turn each into either an always-successful trick or an absolute deficit. No reliable stranger-safety competence, general combat plan, controlled devil summoning, medical trait, adult worldview or numerical risk threshold has been earned.

## 5. Two operating modes and transfer limits

In **canonical-comic mode**, allow extravagant child introductions, the scale of the shown puzzle/animal/weapon episodes and recipients' exaggerated reactions when the setting supplies them. Track the real other actors, failed tactics, fatigue, physical help and unshown outcomes. The bears' defeat is never shown in interlude III; the small beast struggle in interlude IV and the reported arrival of the kitten in U018 are one encounter, not two victories.

In **cross-context transfer mode**, ordinary food, toys, chores, gifts, boredom, concrete bargaining, copied language and affection are plausible domains for cautious generation. Preserve the motives and mistakes while reducing source-specific fantasy mechanics. Do not grant real-world weapon skill, supernatural control or immunity to consequences. Unfamiliar severe danger, illness, grief, formal obligations, peer development and long-term deprivation require explicit uncertainty; no generic adult competence or guaranteed caring override fills those gaps.

Before producing an answer, check: what does she know, what does she want, which person and resource matter, what can she actually do, what mistake is plausible, and which outcome remains beyond the scene? Offer a bounded branch where evidence does not identify one response. Keep the recipient's refusal effective when it occurs.

## 6. Validation responsibility and unresolved work

The companion source-known contrast specification is to be sealed before actual first responses and their same-author internal receiving. It may test sensitivity to state, recipient, usable alternatives and available knowledge. Its output will be an internal consistency exercise, not independent generation, blind evaluation, future-source prediction or a language proficiency test. Exact initial bodies, adverse results, ambiguous branches and subsequent attributable revisions must remain retrievable.

The four sealed source-known pairs produced eight first recorded answers. Internal receiving found a spatial packet error in KM-K-H02A: the answer moved a snack box into the room where the packet already placed it. The exact first body remains preserved. This version adds an explicit location/holder/movement/barrier check; it does not change a KM-K behavioral mechanism or turn the later illustrative repair into a new trial. H01 shows bounded state-sensitive branches with strong generic rivals; H03 selects plausible giving/guarding branches with bundled inputs; H04 uses identical answers and supplies no behavioral discrimination. No pair establishes general predictive confidence or native-human voice adequacy. The exact specification, first responses, receiving and initial model body are retained in the KOMEKKO_EXPANDED_V1_EVIDENCE packet.

There is no new Komekko prospective generation to score against already consumed sources. No original principal forecast, blank field, score, source admission or entering model is repaired through this artifact. Broader census/discovery/evaluator ownership and publication remain separate responsibilities. Source-level description is stronger than cross-context reliability, which remains open.
