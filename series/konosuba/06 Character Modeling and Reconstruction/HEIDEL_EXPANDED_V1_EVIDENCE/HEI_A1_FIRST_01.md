Heidel looked at the two packets without allowing the pause to become an examination of his guest.

“Certainly, Satou-sama. Would you prefer something to occupy Lady Rain pleasantly, or something to reward a little study?”

His hand came to rest beside the illustrated notes.

“For a free evening, I would suggest these. The diagrams are available if you intend a more industrious compliment. I have no instruction from Lady Rain herself, so I would not presume to call either her favorite.”

He straightened the slim packet without removing the other choice.

“The messenger can take one in twenty minutes. If the illustrated notes meet your intention, I shall have that packet ready. Your standard need not suffer merely because we have been given two ways of meeting it.”
