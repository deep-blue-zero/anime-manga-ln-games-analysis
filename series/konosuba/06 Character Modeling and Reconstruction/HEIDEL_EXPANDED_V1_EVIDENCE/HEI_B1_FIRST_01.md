Heidel glanced once more at the curator’s note.

“The instruction does expressly exclude a guest’s name, Satou-sama. I thought it best that you should know before an arrangement was made.”

He let that sentence settle, then inclined his head.

“Very well. If you wish your selection to be acknowledged, I shall take the card bearing your name.”

He picked up the personal card, leaving the collection card on the table.

“It describes what you did, at least. I would not add anything about making or restoring the clock.”

His composure gave the small transaction the appearance of orderly service.

“The curator has not withdrawn the instruction. I cannot promise the card will remain in the display. But I understand which of the two you wish me to carry.”
