“Certainly, Satou-sama. Lady Rain has been kind enough to request the illustrated travel notes herself.”

Heidel selected the slim packet and left the mechanical diagrams on the table.

“A gift suited to its recipient is a very agreeable standard. I shall have this one ready for the messenger.”

He checked the title once more, as though choosing between the two had been a necessary ceremony rather than a difficulty.

“There is no need to burden her free evening with a second packet, and the messenger is limited to one in any case. The catalog can remain here. As for the impression it makes, I can arrange the selection you requested; Lady Rain must be allowed her own opinion when it reaches her.”
