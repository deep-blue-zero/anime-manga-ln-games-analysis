# Gilbert: provisional conditional reconstruction

Status: initial scoped Tier 3 model for the Core and Advanced edited replays, interpreted in the [monograph](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_GILBERT_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md). The [cohort 31 index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_TRPG_REPLAY_COHORT31_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) governs evidence. All source and broad contrast directions are known to this author. This model precedes exact inputs and original hypothetical responses. It is not an independent test, mature reconstruction, capability grade or confidence gain.

## Operating representation

Track Gilbert's claimed identity, current audience, religious relevance, money/comfort interest, actual team need, available support skills and costs, evidence visible to others, his reputation with each listener, challenges to professional dignity and the other person's actual request. Separate his explanation of an act from the act and its possible causes. A recruitment question, a lie, a correct observation and a useful shield can coexist in one encounter.

Generate a local appraisal and options before outcome: assert status, recruit, rationalize, ask, conceal, report, heal, shield, acknowledge fault or promise a future act. Costs and skill limits remain explicit. Preserve unhelpful and harmful options; do not turn a comic priest into an ideal counselor or a universal fraud.

## GI-R01: religious salience directs framing without enclosing practical aid

**Proposition.** A perceived opportunity for Axis recognition, recruitment or defense of Aqua tends to become salient for Gilbert and can redirect his stated priorities. His useful support nevertheless crosses the simplest doctrinal boundary; religious rhetoric does not prove that sect membership alone caused aid.

**Supporting evidence.** Core S066 openly adds an Aqua insult, after which he prioritizes the enemy and artifact. ADV S032-035 shows immediate recruiting, selective card presentation and a 95% Axis claim. Core S077-080 and ADV S099 show actual paid or rolled support for allies. His own account frequently gives doctrine an explanatory role.

**Contrary evidence.** Team survival, shared resources and status also matter. The enemy insult was improvised after a question, so it is not a fixed independent trigger test. Useful care occurs in a party whose affiliations are inconsistent; the source does not isolate an exclusive Axis preference.

**Rivals.** Generic party optimization, comfort or reward seeking, player momentum, attention to a GM cue and ordinary sociability. A theological phrase alone cannot discriminate these causes.

**Application conditions.** State whether religious relevance is actually known to him, what practical help is needed, who benefits and what tools/costs exist. Predict increased religious framing or recruiting under explicit salience, while retaining practical support as an option even when affiliation differs. With no relevant cue, do not insert an invented insult or cult affiliation.

**Do not assume.** That 95% is true, that every helpful act is altruistic or sect-exclusive, that disagreement licenses coercion, that he can heal any condition, or that the statue is a miracle.

**Confidence.** Moderate for local framing and recruitment, lower for allocation of scarce help between religiously different strangers. This is source-limited judgment, not independently calibrated probability.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable opportunities with explicit religious relevance that produce neither attention nor religious framing would weaken the salience hypothesis. Repeated refusal of ordinary team aid solely because of non-Axis affiliation would challenge the claimed cross-boundary support and require a new bounded version.

## GI-R02: presenting an accurate fact does not erase his credibility debt

**Proposition.** Gilbert is prone to enhance status through selective presentation or inflated claims. When he later offers a real discovery, listeners may demand external evidence rather than accept his declaration. The model should preserve the distinction between his confidence, the claim's truth and its reception.

**Supporting evidence.** He hides card rank with a finger (ADV S032) and knowingly upgrades Froger's rank in the guild report (Core S083). ADV S076-077 pairs a real perception/newspaper discovery with disbelief linked to his reputation; 21 professes belief while Otohime responds to the shown paper.

**Contrary evidence.** The discovery is correct and practically useful. He can acknowledge fault (ADV S084). Not every grand sentence is a lie, and not every listener refuses him; credibility is relational rather than a universal veto.

**Rivals.** Ordinary proof requirements, low official rank, the enemy's evidence-erasing cheat, audience skepticism and comic timing. Doubt alone cannot prove that earlier lying caused it.

**Application conditions.** Specify what he knows, the listener's actual history with him and whether inspectable corroboration exists. Generate a claim and possible challenge; if evidence is available, he may show it. Do not invent past deceit for a new listener. A bare speech can fail to persuade even when the claim is true.

**Do not assume.** Universal compulsive lying, a successful lie-detection roll, forged documents, immediate restored trust after proof, or executive rank inferred from Gilbert's embellished report.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the documented credibility contrast; lower for strangers, formal hearings and exact repair times. One linked narrative sequence does not create independent trials.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable claims to the same informed skeptics receiving immediate unqualified trust without changed evidence or relationship would weaken the reputation-debt prediction. Reliably candid adverse self-report would challenge the status-enhancement tendency.

## GI-R03: professional displacement provokes status repair, with continued cooperation possible

**Proposition.** When another party member occupies a role Gilbert treats as priestly, a likely immediate response is a complaint, doctrinal reframing or bid to recover dignity. This response need not prevent later practical support or a local acknowledgment of fault.

**Supporting evidence.** His Turn Undead fails before Temiren succeeds with a scroll, and he voices hurt priestly pride (ADV S080-082).21's requested mediation leads him to say confessions are his job (S088). Useful support continues elsewhere; he acknowledges a fault and promises a future hunt at S084.

**Contrary evidence.** He does not expel the competing helpers or stop the quest. The later hunt is not performed, and the source does not show durable restitution for every earlier harm. The Core slap is a harmful response in another context, not professional repair or a model of consensual romance.

**Rivals.** A generic joke about class identity, ordinary irritation, affection/attention bargaining and player competition for spotlight. A single complaint cannot prove chronic insecurity.

**Application conditions.** State the actual professional comparison and audience. Expect an immediate claim on dignity more readily than silent indifference, while leaving room to accept a useful outcome. A later repair requires an actual acknowledgment or act; do not manufacture one to ensure a sympathetic ending.

**Do not assume.** Retaliation is inevitable, a diagnosis, unlimited counselor skill, that apology completes restitution, or that an unperformed promise is already paid.

**Confidence.** Low to moderate for the local response across two different displaced roles; lower for lasting behavior, private comparison and non-priest occupations.

**Falsifier.** Repeated salient displacement receiving relaxed, unqualified credit without a status bid would weaken this response tendency. Consistent withdrawal of aid after displacement would challenge the cooperation qualification.

## Generation and evaluation limits

Keep PC speech distinct from player jokes, GM additions and narration. Transfer can preserve incentives, credibility and role conflict without literal magic, sect labels or the comic punchline. Keep adverse acts visible and do not use success as proof of good motives. Planned source-known contrasts require exact-input and original-first preservation after this foundation is frozen. No response may repair this version by feedback. Three dependent pairs are not the final nine-category battery, Japanese voice certification, independent accuracy or a reconstruction grade.
