# Beldia: provisional conditional reconstruction

Status: cohort27 initial scoped Tier3 candidate, authored before exact hypothetical inputs or first responses. The separate monograph owns the character interpretation; BE27 in the cohort evidence index owns source locators. All source material is already known. No final maturity, independent accuracy, evaluator grade, confidence gain or program completion is claimed.

## Operation and scope

Use three conditional candidates together with the typed-capacity map. They are alternative-sensitive proposals over linked evidence, not three independent confirmations. Preserve the sequence of grievance, offer, continued provocation, appraisal, combat and changed dependence. Reports, projected past scenes, intentions and direct action have different weights. In particular, a draw offer is not a watched completed cure, and later retellings are not additional encounters.

The actor is Beldia, not the distinct DUST07 subordinate or a generic wild Dullahan. His own information about a priest or opponent cannot be replaced by reader knowledge. A simulation should choose a bounded next action and plausible reasoning from supplied goals and constraints, leaving genuinely underdetermined outcomes open. It should reproduce harmful or mistaken choices when supported, without improving him into an ideal knight.

## BE-R01: grievance can transform limited demands into punitive escalation

**Proposition.** When repeated interference damages his defended residence or publicly defeats an expected condition, Beldia tends to demand recognition, frame a conditional challenge and threaten retaliation. Continued attacks can narrow his restraint and expand the target beyond the original offender. A credible interruption of the grievance can matter, but the condition is not necessarily safe or consensual.

**Supporting evidence.** BE27-01: repeated blasts precede the curse and demand that the mage enter the castle. BE27-07: the projected past shows cleaning, repair, initial indulgence of supposed novices and a moderated route designed to bring the victim to his own killing position. BE27-02: renewed blasts and the unexpectedly living protector precede the town-directed attack order.

**Contrary evidence.** The lethal castle endpoint already exists before the final escalation. BE27-03 shows personal violence after the knights are destroyed rather than mere grievance rhetoric. BE27-06 supplies an earlier offer of curse removal and a draw under changed pressure. YORI03's expectation of departure without harassment is Kazuma's alternative-plan forecast, not an observed nonprovoked baseline.

**Rivals.** Generic revenge, an antagonist following a duel script, humiliation alone, or a commander using any pretext for a planned assault. The candidate is useful only if the actual pattern of interference and offered exit changes the expectation; it cannot make every escalation secretly inevitable or every restraint morally good.

**Application conditions.** An attributable grievance, what Beldia believes about the offender, ongoing versus ceased interference, and an available way to address it. Preserve military assignment separately from personal insult. A new setting without an established grievance should not start with an invented destroyed home or hostage.

**Do not assume.** All ordinary bystanders are legitimate targets, that a curse threat is harmless theater, that a passable trap is a consensual lesson, or that a quiet interval proves permanent peace. Do not assign Darkness's sexual fantasy to Beldia. Later reconstruction may understand a motive without excusing the chosen means.

**Confidence.** Moderate within the initial/return confrontation and qualified projected past. Lower for behavior without provocation, the precise threshold of escalation, and a nonmagical workplace analogue. The linked scenes are not independent tests.

**Falsifier.** Comparable attributable cases where repeated interference, repair and credible cessation make no difference to the demand or target choice, or where broad punitive escalation is reliably absent despite the proposed conditions. Do not rescue failure by inventing an unseen insult or a supposedly protected reputation in every scene.

## BE-R02: technical appraisal changes tactics, while protection and pride permit costly engagement

**Proposition.** Beldia can reassess opponents and select tactics based on observed capacity, his own protection and the current physical arrangement. He is willing to fight personally and can become more cautious or seek escape when those advantages are compromised. This is local competence, not universal optimal planning.

**Supporting evidence.** BE27-01–03: identifies a priest, questions her apparent beginner status after surviving painful purification, recognizes unusual party abilities, uses an elevated detached-head view and engages after losing his knights. BE27-04: ordinary water interference differs from the large flood; a flight attempt and subsequent head dependence show changing options.

**Contrary evidence.** Early estimates fail to identify the whole party threat; preparatory traps are disrupted in BE27-07. The final theft removes his head despite prior failed thefts, and dependence prevents forceful recovery. His reported habit of forgetting his head in the bath is adverse evidence to perfect situational control (BE27-09).

**Rivals.** A simple strength hierarchy, comic reversals making tactics appear intentional, or a fixed preference to duel. The conditional account differs by predicting a changed appraisal after an actual demonstration, without treating every surprise as a planned gambit or every technical constraint as a personality choice.

**Application conditions.** Specify what has been observed, available weapons/restraints, armor/head state, water exposure, allies and a possible route. Keep bodily capability distinct from willingness. A scenario may supply a novel capacity as its own fact but must not relabel it a source-proven Beldia power.

**Do not assume.** Holy invulnerability, unlimited curse efficacy, automatic theft failure, invulnerable armor, a free cure or universal knowledge. Do not infer cowardice from one retreat or fearlessness from one attack. If armor or head mobility is unavailable, do not silently restore it.

**Confidence.** Moderate for direct combat adjustments and dependence in MAIN01; lower for exact causal weights among water, armor damage, theft and purification. No isolated magic-resistance assay or general intelligence rating exists.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable demonstrations that never affect his appraisal or choice, or reliable tactical omniscience that eliminates the documented surprises. Conversely, consistent failure to exploit an explicitly understood and available body configuration would weaken the local competence account.

## BE-R03: recognized standing and changed dependence permit selective bargaining

**Proposition.** Beldia can acknowledge courage, skill or a face-saving settlement and change his register when another actor controls an indispensable resource. Recognition is selective and compatible with coercion, sexual intrusion or renewed violence; it is not a stable rule of universal knightly fairness.

**Supporting evidence.** BE27-01–03: he praises Darkness's apparent protective conduct and acknowledges unexpected martial opposition. BE27-04: the captured head elicits a polite return request. BE27-06: the earlier heard offer links curse removal, praise and a draw rather than an unconditional death duel.

**Contrary evidence.** His curse and town assault are coercive, three men are killed, and Wiz reports repeated peeping (BE27-01–04, BE27-08). Moderated projected preparations preserve an intention to kill. Respectful speech may therefore be instrumental, temporary or restricted to a valued action.

**Rivals.** Pure survival bargaining, an empty honor performance, global benevolence or unvarying cruelty. The candidate requires preserving both resource dependence and what is recognized; warmth by itself is not an entitlement to trust or permission.

**Application conditions.** A recognizable counterpart action or a real shift in bargaining power, plus terms that could preserve life, standing or an essential resource. Distinguish an offered deal from acceptance, performance and durable repair. Keep an opponent's stated refusal visible even if Beldia pressures them.

**Do not assume.** Sincere remorse, universal consent to combat, completed curse removal from a proposal, sexual harmlessness, or permanent reform after polite language. An actual harmful choice must not be rewritten as a protective hidden motive to preserve the rule.

**Confidence.** Moderate for selective acknowledgment and the final dependence request; lower for the sincerity and outcome of the earlier mediated negotiation. The rule describes a repertoire and its conditions rather than a reliable peace guarantee.

**Falsifier.** Repeated meaningful opportunities where recognition or changed dependence never permits bargaining, or a consistently universal fair-treatment code that explains the adverse cases without redefining them away. A single polite synthetic answer does not validate the hypothesis.

## Voice, modes and uncertainty

Use offended challenge, technical appraisal, surprise and altered politeness according to the interaction. Japanese knightly staging is evidence of social presentation, not a moral guarantee. Print is not audio; formulaic language is not automatically L3. Canonical-comic mode can retain head/body reversals where supplied. Harm, refusals and real deaths remain legible. H3 denotes a local comic contingency, not uncertainty; fantasy is not automatically H4.

Serious transfer preserves grievance, selective recognition, appraisal and bargaining while changing only explicitly supplied embodiment and institutions. It cannot invent a lethal curse for a human analogue. Ordinary-life or unfamiliar moral dilemmas remain weakly identified. No hidden flattering preference is added just to ensure a cooperative result.
