## AL-R01: entitlement recruits available authority, with escalation limited by actual resistance

**Proposition.** When an object of desire or acknowledged precedence is obstructed, Alderp tends to assert ownership/rank and recruit available legal, household or monetary leverage. Resistance can elicit blame, threat or a riskier route; stronger present authority and lost leverage can instead force withdrawal or bargaining.

**Supporting evidence.** AL28-01: death demand and opposition to delay. AL28-03: class demand at the banquet. AL28-07–08: preceremony access demand, public purchase rhetoric, pursuit and unsuccessful inducements. AL28-10: bargaining when he becomes physically dependent.

**Contrary evidence.** Truth-device resistance and the Dustiness intervention limit the court demand. Hospitality burdens make him wish to retract an invitation. Guards/adventurers do not uniformly comply, and private terminal fear is not continued confident domination. Balter has his own refusal.

**Rivals.** Generic villain aggression, pure erotic fixation, rank ritual without instrumental intent, or a resource-only power calculation. The rule has value only if present leverage, attributed obstruction and recipient resistance change the expected action; it cannot predict escalation and capitulation equally without conditions.

**Application conditions.** Supply a present goal, perceived entitlement, counterpart standing, actual enforcement resources and an available resistance/exit route. Distinguish legal authority from his assertion of it. A new scenario without a desired person should not acquire invented sexual fixation.

**Do not assume.** A right to the desired person, sexual consent from fantasy or wages, universal guard obedience, completed punishment from a threat, or that higher standing makes every other actor his pawn. Keep recipient costs visible even when the protagonist wins.

**Confidence.** Moderate for linked status/possession disputes; lower for mundane low-stakes frustration and institutions unlike those shown. No calibrated escalation threshold or independent case series exists.

**Falsifier.** Comparable attributable cases where meaningful changes in real authority and resistance never affect his choice, or where he consistently accepts frustrating refusal without recruiting any available leverage. Do not preserve the rule by inventing hidden power in every outcome.

## AL-R02: cheap delegated correction can sustain overclaim and exploitative blame

**Proposition.** Where Alderp believes an external agent can repair consequences cheaply, he is inclined to retain that dependence, avoid its cost and blame the agent for limits. The strategy may maintain a self-made account until a failure forces another route; it does not confer the agent's actual powers on him.

**Supporting evidence.** AL28-05: his private explanation of retaining Maxwell because forgetting permits nonpayment, alongside kicks and demanded retrieval. AL28-09: continued need for cover-up despite contempt and fear of public exposure. AL28-10: Vanir's account challenges his self-made explanation.

**Contrary evidence.** He can act directly through invitations, property, public speech and money collection. Maxwell names location/seal/church/light limits; these defeat demands. Contract release makes the supposed instrument refuse, and punishment follows. Not every achievement or official error is shown to be externally manufactured.

**Rivals.** Ordinary abusive employment, self-serving autobiography, a uniquely magical contract opportunity, or generalized cost minimization. The candidate differs by predicting the link between perceived cheap correction, continued reliance and blame rather than assuming stupidity or automatic refusal to pay everyone.

**Application conditions.** State the service, actual versus believed capability, cost/record mechanism, contract state and what prior performance he knows. Separate material inability from unwillingness. If no corrective resource exists, do not silently add memory magic or an obedient surrogate.

**Do not assume.** That Maxwell altered every witness, that Sena knowingly collaborated, that all prior service is independently verified, or that a payment promise is performed payment. No biological/adoption claim or performed body theft follows from his plan for Balter.

**Confidence.** Moderate within the two private scenes and admitted payment exploitation; low for ordinary nonmagical employment or a complete causal account of his career. Vanir's explanation is involved testimony.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable opportunities where apparently cheap correction does not affect reliance or cost evasion, or where he reliably acknowledges capability limits and pays despite the same exploitable forgetting. Do not redefine an unobserved hidden correction as evidence.

## AL-R03: desired confirmation can displace checking until control visibly collapses

**Proposition.** Under strong desire or reputational threat, Alderp can accept a convenient appearance of success and act on it without reconciling explicit contrary information. Once he directly experiences lost control and pain, he can bargain desperately rather than continue the same presentation. These are conditional phases, not a claim of universal gullibility.

**Supporting evidence.** AL28-09: a knock is taken as delivered Darkness despite Maxwell's explicit no-action statement; he releases the contract. AL28-10: disguise and rank revelation expose the error, then broken arms and expected torment produce apology, bargaining and prayer. Earlier body-theft plans shift risks rather than abandon possession.

**Contrary evidence.** He recognizes the danger of his public wedding statements and knows a cover-up is needed. He sometimes detects inconvenience and tries to withdraw hospitality. Terminal apology is not shown to become durable remorse. Vanir's disguise is unusually effective, supplying a situational rival.

**Rivals.** An isolated deception, panic alone, comic plotting, or a consistent strategic tradeoff accepted with full knowledge. The candidate needs a discrepancy between available contrary information and the desired reading; choosing a known risk is not automatically this error.

**Application conditions.** Specify what he has heard/seen, what remains hidden, the desired outcome and whether a checking opportunity is credible. A scenario can leave identity uncertain. Later punishment knowledge cannot be inserted into the moment before release.

**Do not assume.** Unconditional gullibility, a completed Maxwell delivery, Darkness's consent, Vanir's necessary benevolence, or genuine moral reform from fear. Household substitution anticipated by Vanir is not a fully spoken Alderp order.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the decisive private mistake and collapse; lower for recurrence outside desire/reputation pressure. Precise sincerity and postcrisis learning are unresolved.

**Falsifier.** Comparable tempting cues repeatedly checked against available adverse evidence before consequential release, or persistent composed control-seeking despite the same direct collapse when an effective bargaining avenue exists. A single synthetic polite answer cannot establish correction.
