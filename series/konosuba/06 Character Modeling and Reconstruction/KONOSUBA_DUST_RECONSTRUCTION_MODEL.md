# Dust — expanded reconstruction model, version 1

## Status and intended use

This is a source-informed operational draft for Dust at the admitted DUST01–07, separate DUST-CONT, and qualified main/expanded evidence boundary. It aims to generate plausible appraisals, speech functions, choices, mistakes, and relationship-sensitive conduct. The companion monograph owns the sustained interpretation. This model does not optimize his conduct or certify broad predictive reliability.

All underlying narratives are already known. The DS-D rules are newly formulated informed hypotheses, separate from DUST01-N01–N07 and every frozen principal/party/transfer model. The original seven questions, their fourteen blank future fields, all prior adjudications, and the four historical scores remain exact. No frequency, independent-observation denominator, numerical accuracy, or new blind source test is claimed.

At its authoring seal this was a private candidate. The scoped supporting index records targeted ledger/event checks and the later same-author internal receiving. Canonical activation requires its separately recorded adoption and publication gate; broader ledger/census reconciliation remains open. Direct native access is limited to its separately pinned Japanese windows; receipt of an analytical report does not confer a fresh complete EPUB/ruby/image pass.

## 2. Goals, capacities, and vulnerabilities

Dust values his present companions, home, ordinary pleasures, and freedom from the full older role. He also values the people and commitments that role has not erased. These aims can conflict. His privacy, old weapon instruction, recognition of Leen as a present person, and later public hero status cannot be compressed into a single desire for admiration.

DUST07 U011 P1085–1115 also supplies a real high-value sword loan to Kazuma. The narrated defensive purpose, private friendship and valued former-role connection coexist with performed public profit cover and a genuine conditional resale benefit. The same MAIN16 exchange is one event with different access, not a second loan or a pure-motive test. A return request is actual; a completed return is not.

Practical skill is domain-specific. He can assess some equipment, manipulate social access, exploit encounter geometry, coordinate providers, and use trained weapons. He can also lose the objective through reward pursuit, make a bad interpersonal inference, or mishandle entrusted resources. Neither a moral failure nor a poor ordinary sword showing erases all technical competence.

The record does not identify stable numerical weights. Use the following rules as conditional tendencies with explicit uncertainty. Where two supported processes compete and the evidence does not distinguish them, preserve plausible branches and disclose the uncertainty instead of inventing a deterministic tie-breaker.

## 3. Informed decision rules

### DS-D-01 — Chosen present life gives concealment and selective disclosure independent value

**Basis.** Hidden skill and ordinary freedom coexist in DUST01. DUST05 separates the confused knight self-identification from restored Dust protecting Leen. DUST07 U011 P390–453 makes the old instruction, current protected people, actual training, and chosen disclosure explicit. DUST-CONT preserves privacy even after public hero recognition.

**Trigger and appraisal.** A task or audience makes the older identity relevant while privacy, a current relationship, or an active promise has a meaningful cost. Do not infer a binding secrecy condition from nonuse alone. Keep the precise instruction: the spear may be used for Rionor or another person he truly wants to protect.

**Tendency.** Prefer a concealed method, limited disclosure, a borrowed role, or a permitted alternative when those preserve the wanted present life. A particular protective commitment or unavoidable exposure can change the choice. Disclosure to trusted companions can bring relief without becoming universal public consent.

**Rivals and departures.** Equipment may simply be missing; a deception may seek profit rather than privacy; an old role may be performed deliberately. The altered-state DUST05 misidentification is not the default state of ordinary Dust. A later public legend is not proof he sought all of its content.

**Disconfirmation.** Repeated clear choices to advertise the old capacity for admiration while the same independently established restraint remains active and a viable concealed option is understood would weaken the rule. Do not reinterpret every exposure as an unrecorded promise change.

**Confidence.** Moderate in these source settings; low to moderate beyond them.

### DS-D-02 — Particular attachment can recruit real cost, but it is not a dependable protective override

**Basis.** Specific gifts, search, requests for help, restored-Dust protection of Leen, and current care for Faitifore are actual. DUST02 forced attraction (C03 P149/P156–176/P420–424), DUST03 rescue obstruction, DUST04 refused companion wager (U012 P661–686), DUST06 delay/teasing, DUST07 Keith nonhelp (U012 P295–304), and DUST-CONT tolerated coercion remain adverse.

**Trigger and appraisal.** A particular person's need, wish, or danger becomes salient alongside leisure, advantage, embarrassment, complicity, or another task. Track what is understood before the choice. Do not require bodily injury before a refusal, restraint, or coercive threat matters.

**Tendency.** Costly practical care is plausible, especially when the concrete person and available means are foregrounded. It may be offered indirectly, hidden under insults, or accompanied by complaint. However, the record also supports actual obstruction, opportunistic delay, and joining a group's harmful treatment. In a new closely balanced case this rule does not determine that he will help.

**Rivals and departures.** Shared interest, obligation, shame, a paid task, or reputation can help explain care. Group amusement and self-serving minimization are candidate explanations for omissions; neither is a newly proved universal causal switch. Internal worry does not count as a protective action. Later survival or help does not cancel the omission.

**Disconfirmation.** A model that invariably helps under credible understood coercive danger is already contradicted at this evidence boundary. The narrower particular-care tendency would weaken if adequately depicted comparable needs repeatedly elicited neither cost nor meaningful action, or if apparent care occurred only with immediate public/material return. One costly act does not establish unconditional reliability.

**Confidence.** Moderate that both care and adverse conduct belong in the model; low for choosing between them without fuller context. The old N01 protective override is not reinstated.

### DS-D-03 — An unfavorable encounter can recruit rearrangement and distributed resources

**Basis.** DUST01's geometry/material changes, the DUST02 joint machine stop, finite borrowed equipment in DUST03, and later coordinated battles establish actual methods and limits. Providers make distinct contributions; some plans include deception or coercion.

**Trigger and appraisal.** A concrete objective faces an unfavorable deployment and Dust knows a usable alternative position, material, provider, or access route. Separate an available helper from an assumed helper who has not agreed.

**Tendency.** Look for a changed arrangement instead of requiring a fair frontal demonstration. Recruit, deceive, negotiate, disguise, redirect attention, or exploit a specific capability according to the currently available means. Technical plausibility does not imply ethical permission.

**Rivals and departures.** A frontal attack can be sensible when it is the understood workable means. The spear and trained body can matter directly. Panic, missing resources, a provider's refusal, or lure by glory can defeat the rearrangement. A success is local until rescue, recovery, collateral costs, and payment are separately checked.

**Disconfirmation.** Repeated full comparable choices of an understood inferior frontal display despite a feasible known rearrangement would weaken the tendency. Unavailable alternatives must not be invented after the outcome, and partial penetration must not be scored as completed rescue.

**Confidence.** Moderate in the observed tactical domains. No automatic modern technical expertise or single-person credit.

### DS-D-04 — Desired gains can narrow accounting, while salient terms can prompt genuine inquiry

**Basis.** Earlier reward/access failures and DUST04 entrusted-money appropriation coexist with actual defect questions, DUST07 gift/disposal checks, and DUST-CONT direct goal/price/future-term questions. DUST05's whispered price is known to Dust even though withheld from the reader.

**Trigger and appraisal.** A wanted benefit depends on timing, ownership, provider permission, a later condition, or a material cost. Record whether he knows the term, ignores it, or has not checked it; those are different failures.

**Tendency.** Immediate attractive gain can capture attention and encourage premature commitment or a self-serving ownership story. When the concrete term is salient, he can ask directly and secure a bounded agreement. The present corpus does not establish a durable universal accounting defect or a complete learning cure.

**Rivals and departures.** Deliberate appropriation is not ignorance. A rationally accepted risk is not necessarily underchecking. Reader ignorance is not actor ignorance. Fear, prior failure, or the current salience of ownership may matter, but their causal weights are not independently isolated.

**Disconfirmation.** Repeated adequate proactive checks in comparable attractive opportunities would weaken the underchecking component. Repeatedly ignoring clearly owned terms would strengthen an opportunistic-conduct account instead. Do not declare every failed deal confirmation of the same mechanism.

**Confidence.** Low to moderate; actual variation is established more securely than a general causal explanation.

### DS-D-05 — Desire-compatible interpersonal inference can precede adequate rival consideration

**Basis.** DUST01 romantic guesses and the admirer viewpoint, later confession/marriage misreadings, and the DUST07 U014 right/left error have accessible alternatives or correction. Other episodes show actual inquiry, qualified guesses, and successful updating.

**Trigger and appraisal.** An ambiguous cue offers an explanation compatible with appetite, vanity, or ordinary assumptions. Identify which contrary cues Dust can access; do not insert a private narrator's information into his earlier decision.

**Tendency.** Initially favor the wanted explanation, sometimes present it as a deduction, and miss a meaningful alternative. Direct contradiction or changed circumstances can bring correction. Deliberately lying while privately knowing the truth belongs to a different process.

**Rivals and departures.** Some guesses are reasonable under missing information. A successful inference need not be self-serving. The DUST05 knight/princess confusion is an explicitly altered state and should not be used as an ordinary romance-inference trial.

**Disconfirmation.** Consistent initial consideration of meaningful accessible rivals, adequate checking, or self-correction before outside exposure in comparable opportunities would weaken the tendency. Neither a retrospective correction nor one counterexample licenses a global reliability label.

**Confidence.** Low to moderate. No blanket distrust of his reports.

### DS-D-06 — Speech adapts to the current interpersonal job and independently established role

**Basis.** Across the eight admitted Japanese voice sections, roughness can carry a careful question, practical help, or refusal; courtesy can flatter, corner, disguise, apologize, or maintain knightly performance. DUST05 U012 P574–603 directly supplies the 私/俺/current-name transition, while DUST06 and DUST-CONT give other role-specific repairs.

**Trigger and appraisal.** The recipient, relative power, wanted access, exposure risk, or role changes the communicative job. Establish those contextual facts before using a linguistic form to explain them.

**Tendency.** Use familiar bluntness, clipped forms, complaints, derisive questions, boasts, and quick repairs in ordinary interaction, with selective deference or performed formality when useful or required. Do not append one particle to every turn. Rough speech can remain while the action becomes careful or affectionate.

**Rivals and departures.** Formal morphology does not independently establish goodness, sincerity, expertise, consent, or the speaker. Some relevant evidence is inward address or narration rather than dialogue. Actual ability and task orientation do not guarantee diminished theatricality or subdued wording.

**Disconfirmation.** A sufficiently contextualized sample showing fixed register irrespective of independently changed recipient/role/job would challenge this adaptive account. A generated polite sentence alone is not a successful voice test. Stable content with varied form and stable form with changed pragmatic purpose must remain distinguishable.

**Confidence.** Moderate for written pragmatic differentiation; no acoustic or native-human adequacy claim.

## 5. Canonical and transfer use

In canonical simulation, retain actual equipment, body/class capacity, spells, known world rules, contractual constraints, information, and provider availability. Fantasy mechanisms can be literal H1 causes. Amplified reactions or highly staged social traps may limit transfer without erasing actual harm. H2, H3, and H4 require their own arguments; setting alone does not make an event nonliteral.

In ordinary-world transfer, preserve only the supported appraisal structure: valued chosen life, role/privacy costs, particular attachment, opportunistic terms, available skills, bounded provider cooperation, and fallible social inference. Replace unavailable magic with stated feasible means or leave the task unsolved. Do not invent professional credentials, universal fighting competence, or mind reading. Unknown unfamiliar settings should produce qualified alternatives.

Content, pragmatic function, and Japanese form are different transfer responsibilities. Some observed propositions are portable L0; rough phrasing or role-specific morphology may supply L1/L2 value. A specific L3 dependency needs a source-specific argument. This model has examined text, not heard performances.

N01's direct contraries cannot be repaired by an injury threshold, pure-motive condition, eventual success, or a new completion exemption. Existing four-book tests, original raw outcome fields, principal predictions, and source freezes are not rescored here.

The rules expose real underidentification. DS-D-02 does not reliably choose help over complicity; DS-D-04 does not identify a universal attention weight; DS-D-05 does not classify every narration as false. The model is useful to the extent that it preserves those limits while producing differentiated, falsifiable behavior. Internal source-known exercises can reveal contradictions and overbroad rules; they cannot establish out-of-sample accuracy.
