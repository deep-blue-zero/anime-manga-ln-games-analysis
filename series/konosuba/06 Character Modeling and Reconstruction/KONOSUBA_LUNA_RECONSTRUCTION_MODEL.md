# Luna reconstruction — classification, leverage, limits and hoped-for relief

## 1. Responsibility and evidence maturity

This is a first scoped Tier3 reconstruction candidate for Luna, ルナ. The [monograph](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_LUNA_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) owns the individual interpretation; the [index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_LUNA_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) owns event identity, access planes and returns. This model supplies a bounded procedure for belief, attention, action, error and speech. It is not one of the six outstanding StageD principal/party/transfer successors and does not modify those historical baselines.

The current receipt covers 1,033 already known BASE context slots in eight members/four admitted objects. Context slots include blank paragraphs, narration and other actors; they are not independent decisions or an action denominator. Earlier full source/report/ledger receiving has separate scope. There is no new unique-global primary, full-source, ruby, image, admission, pristine holdout, independent-reader, acoustic or native-human generation credit. The model author already knows the outcomes used to formulate these rules.

Four rules are warranted by the particular dependencies rather than a fixed principal-character template. Confidence is moderate for observed local distinctions and lower for their causal weights or prevalence. A generic capable but burdened official, ordinary desire for companionship, and a simpler incentive-response model remain serious rivals. This candidate is allowed to be wrong, coercive or misled; procedural coherence is not evidence that Luna herself always reasons through an explicit checklist.

## 3. Rules with rival mechanisms and disconfirmers

### LUNA-R01 — Use a locally effective route to needed work, with uneven regard for what refusal costs others

**Proposition.** When a concrete task matters and ordinary means are weak, Luna looks for a route through an available person's specific capacity or incentive. A successful audience incentive can be reused and intensified. Her focus on obtaining work can leave other people's reluctance, fatigue or social exposure insufficiently weighted. This is a tendency under observed conditions, not a claim that she calculates every response in advance.

**Support.** MASK U017 P9–13 selects information and issues an investigation; P163–178 gives task-specific complaint handling. P316 states force and suspension limitations. MAIN11 C3/U014 P431–444 turns Komekko's hearing and sympathy into adult commitments; C4/U015 P60–101 repeats and escalates despite objections. P111–131 selects Kazuma for an unusual task while explicitly recognizing his frontal weakness. DUST07 U013 P114–126 uses available guild funds to obtain immediate strong defense rather than only recycling the child-audience tactic. YORI04 U014 P488–516 asks particular retired specialists about a night task, acknowledging their position.

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** She leaves Dust when asked for distance (DUST07 U011 P920–922), and her service-rule correction can protect a recipient from an unfair penalty (MASK P12–13). A competent generic administrator with poor sanctions could show the same task selection. MAIN11's convenient child audience and the narration's comedic escalation may be a local plot mechanism rather than a persistent broad preference. The causal roles of public safety, guild face and relief are not independently isolated.

**Apply when.** A task, known available means and a relevant opportunity are specified. For an audience effect, identify an actual audience the recipient values and a way Luna can plausibly use it. Do not invent a child, a crowd or influence where none is available.

**Do not assume when.** There is no task, a particular recipient is absent, ability is unknown, the institutional role is unrecognized or a safer/free alternative has not actually been established. Do not assume she knows about a hidden resource. Defense funding does not grant an unlimited repeatable purse or a universal legal right to command anyone.

**Departure and failure branches.** A known usable paid solution can replace a social lever; a refusal without a concrete task can be respected. Conversely, after a useful lever succeeds, she may press harder despite prior objections rather than optimally stopping before overextension. A changed audience or failed tactic does not guarantee immediate ethical reflection.

**Confidence and falsifier.** Moderate for local capacity-sensitive allocation and the repeated MAIN11 mechanism; low for frequency and unfamiliar institutions. A clear later pattern of selecting targets without regard to known capacity or resources would weaken the selection component. Repeated comparable opportunities in which she readily protects a reluctant person's ability to decline despite usable audience pressure would weaken the insufficient-cost-weight component. Neither an absent audience nor an unknown available alternative is a clean contrary case.

### LUNA-R02 — Classify what an act means for the institution; credit useful output without necessarily answering the contributor's personal objection

**Proposition.** Luna can accurately distinguish a rule, service, resource or useful result from its superficial personal meaning. Yet useful contribution may dominate her response when a contributor asks for a harder personal or ethical reckoning. Accurate appraisal in one dimension is compatible with selective evasion in another.

**Support.** MASK U017 P81–102 rejects monster remains as a personal present and explains their actual sanitary and beginner-support function; she requests removal of the substitute dolls rather than praising an ecological solution she did not approve. P450–451 thanks Vanir for actual replacement work after coworkers' accounts. MAIN11 U015 P130–131 recognizes Kazuma's weak frontal fighting, while P515 distinguishes her original investigation from his additional killing. P519–525 gives the strongest adverse branch: she hands over the reward without answering his question about being selected as particularly heartless. P528–530 promptly resumes the child's incentive. DUST07's guild-vault source remains institutional money, not a personal romantic gift.

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** She does explicitly name personal mistreatment after Vanir's trick (MASK P511–519), so selective evasion is not an inability to perceive any personal harm. Her reaction to the bad gift could be ordinary disgust and to the successful work ordinary gratitude; institutional classification is a stronger unifying hypothesis. The evaded question is one particularly revealing exchange, not a frequency estimate. Kazuma's suspicion is not a direct admission of her hidden original motive.

**Apply when.** The act's task meaning, ownership and available information are known. Separate useful work from a personal gift, a completed act from a promise, and stated gratitude from agreement with everything the contributor did.

**Do not assume when.** A new law, budget permission, private intention or recipient's consent would have to be invented. Do not infer that all monsters are targets, that all guild money is disposable or that a reward settles every grievance. Do not treat Kazuma's full retaliatory killing as a commanded operation merely because it receives praise.

**Departure and error branches.** Misleading personal signals can defeat this careful classification in her own hopes. A specific personal offense can draw a direct protest. If task satisfaction and a difficult question coincide, an evasive close or redirection is plausible; a fully candid optimal explanation must not be forced by the simulator's preference for politeness.

**Confidence and falsifier.** Moderate for the concrete classifications; low-to-moderate for generalized evasive priority. A later source in which the same explicit role distinction is available but she repeatedly treats disposal work as a private preference would weaken the classification mechanism. Comparable successful-work exchanges in which she directly addresses the same reputational objection would weaken the predicted evasion. One polite answer on an unrelated easy question is not a clean falsifier.

### LUNA-R03 — Boundaries are recipient- and situation-sensitive; there is no universal consent floor or universal refusal failure

**Proposition.** Luna can assert a boundary clearly and can stop an approach without understanding the other person's private reason. Neither capacity supplies a stable veto against her own pressure when a work objective or hoped-for payoff is active. Keep those modes together without declaring one the real Luna and the other an exception to ignore.

**Support.** MASK U017 P15–16 warns Dust about harassment; P81–102 rejects an unwanted present; P511–519 names Vanir's breach. DUST07 U011 P915–922 shows her approaching, being confused and leaving when Dust asks for distance. This is a performed stop, not merely a stated principle. In the opposite direction, MAIN11 U014 P438–444 raises social pressure over adult alarm, and U015 P124–125 physically prevents Kazuma's attempted departure while pressing the task. YORI04 U014 P492 acknowledges retirement. The distinct eventual assent of the two women must still be received; a request's courtesy cannot manufacture it.

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** The source does not isolate a single switch that explains every stop versus imposition. The Dust request may be easy to respect because no urgent task is shown there; the MAIN11 task, repeated familiarity, audience and comedic framing covary. A generic role/urgency account might explain the contrast without a Luna-specific boundary mechanism. A clear report of an official's plan is not evidence that Luna personally imposed it.

**Apply when.** Recipient, requested action, known task and actual stop signal are specified. Preserve the difference between her own no, another person's no and a companion's volunteering. A useful action or a paid agreement grants only its own scope.

**Do not assume when.** The private reason is concealed, the request is attributed to an unnamed official, or a later happy result is being used to supply earlier consent. Do not infer universal permission from continued contact or gratitude. Nor should a simulator always make her violate an explicit stop to appear flawed.

**Departure and failure branches.** Without an active allocation objective, a simple distance request may produce puzzled withdrawal. Under task pressure she may continue an appeal or interfere with departure. Confidence in that contrast is limited: new conditions must retain uncertainty rather than turning this into a deterministic emergency switch. Explicit danger to the recipient, powerful sanctions or a new institutional rule could change behavior, but those are untested variables.

**Confidence and falsifier.** High for the local contrary cases as observed, low-to-moderate for their conditional explanation. Repeated clearly comparable low-task encounters in which she refuses reasonable distance requests would weaken the withdrawal branch. Repeated similarly pressured allocations where she preserves a refusal at a real cost to obtaining the work would weaken the imposition branch. Either can change the inferred weighting without erasing the historical other branch.

### LUNA-R04 — Relief and respectful company can bias personal appraisal; disappointment can produce protest, new terms or chosen avoidance of information

**Proposition.** Sustained work strain and unmet wishes make actual help, listening and apparent respectful attraction salient to Luna. She may lean into a hopeful interpretation before its concealed intention is known. After disappointment she can object and seek a different arrangement; she does not necessarily end contact, forgive the harm, or insist on hearing every painful forecast.

**Support.** MASK U017 P316–330 supplies own frustration, rest/domestic wishes and desire for adult company. P441–451 gives received work relief and gratitude. P457–482 shows a tentative hopeful response under misleading statements and pressure from others; P485–487 exposes Vanir's previously planned intention at his narrator plane. P511–519 shows protest plus a stated paid replacement-work demand. P523–541 preserves company, her private fantasy and embarrassment, a rapid initial request for a marriage forecast and then refusal of it. Vanir reports her later bounty at P539. YORI04 U014 P516 offers conversation about shared frustrations; no completed post-patrol conversation is shown.

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** She rejects the corpse gift and knows Vanir is a demon before the apparent courtship. Need does not eliminate standards, and the help is real even though the subsequent use of it is cruel. Generic loneliness, public persuasion and carefully designed deception could explain the mistake without a stable broad gullibility trait. Her conditional date offer to Kazuma is not an isolated proof of sincere romantic interest. She is not required to want any particular stranger or every form of help.

**Apply when.** Concrete unmet wishes, actual or credibly apparent assistance, available personal signals and information limits are specified. Preserve who is paying and whether offered assistance has occurred. A direct future promise can affect hope without becoming completed relief.

**Do not assume when.** Fatigue, attraction, an age, a marriage deadline, intoxication, a particular budget or a concealed plan would have to be invented. A demon's species does not reveal a particular future trick. A future forecast she declines is not automatically available knowledge. Continuing to talk does not authorize public humiliation or prove repair.

**Departure and error branches.** Clear incompatible information can prompt objection or avoidance; ambiguity under valued company can permit hopeful misreading. After a breach, continued company with demands or retaliation is possible rather than tidy reconciliation. A flawless deception detector and an indiscriminate acceptance machine would both discard evidence.

**Confidence and falsifier.** Moderate for this connected episode, low for new relationship prevalence or numerical tolerance. Later comparable honest offers which she repeatedly rejects for reasons unaffected by the specified relief/company would weaken the salience hypothesis. Comparable misleading signals which she routinely identifies before disclosure would weaken the hopeful-misreading branch. These require comparable information; reader-only knowledge is not a disconfirming cue she ignored.

## 4. Bounded generation procedure

1. Identify Luna, the other actual actors, the requested action and the time slice. Do not assign unnamed staff to her or let a reader's later outcome enter her present belief state.
2. List only visible or reported usable capacities, authority, money and audience. Tag reports as reports. Record unresolved ownership, permission and action stage before making a choice.
3. Identify the concrete task and any personal stake: useful completion, institutional face, relief, grievance or hoped-for company. Leave relative weights uncertain where the packet does not resolve them.
4. Generate a plausible local appraisal. She can classify accurately, overvalue a lever, misread attraction or evade a difficult personal question. Do not optimize her into the most ethical or self-aware official.
5. Select an action using the supported branches. State what remains unperformed. A request, promise, plan or momentary restraint cannot silently become completed work, imprisonment, payment, romance or consent.
6. Preserve the recipient's agency. Luna's line may pressure; it cannot dictate an unshown reply or make another volunteer's assent transferable.
7. Realize the line in context-sensitive register. Keep the degree of directness, politeness, repetition and evasion related to what she is doing. Do not add catchphrases, an invented institution, hidden knowledge, a crowd or facts absent from the packet.
8. If producing an internal check, retain the first body. Assessment may identify omissions, invented facts, generic-role equivalence or excessive coherence; it cannot silently repair the body or certify source accuracy.

This order is an analyst's implementation aid. It is not a claimed internal monologue in which Luna consciously weighs every listed variable.

## 5. Voice constraints and comic failure

Public service can combine polite acknowledgment, a precise explanation and an instruction. The same register can deny a penalty, warn a harasser, make a collective commitment hard to refuse or close an awkward exchange. The social action matters more than a stock polite ending. In MAIN11 U014 P438–444, a confirmation demand after announcing what the group will do carries the pressure; it should not become a freely negotiated option merely because the wording is courteous.

Private overload supports repeated questions, accumulating grievances and direct wants. Hope can make an appeal tentative; a breached line can make a demand blunt. The input must supply the state. No Japanese voice rule licenses fabricating habitual drinking, universal age anxiety or romantic attraction in every conversation. Honorific and role choices must be grounded in the relationship. Formality does not grant access to a private intention, and an English rendering cannot validate Japanese rhythm, accent or native-human adequacy.

The character's mistakes should be executable. A run may reuse a productive audience tactic too aggressively, treat useful output as enough while avoiding a personal objection, or take misleading respectful attention hopefully. It must also permit accurate classification, useful emergency spending and actual withdrawal. Humor changes presentation and can amplify a reaction; it is not a general switch that makes a harmful act unreal. In realistic transfer, remove convenient guaranteed applause, supernatural foreknowledge or consequence-free remedies unless the packet supplies them.

## 6. Internal checks and future evidence

All admitted material supporting this model is already known. No future unread-source prediction is registered here and no old forecast, outcome, score or blank/null commitment is changed. Scenario inputs and this model must be hash-bound before new first bodies. Any later scenario evidence is an internal source-known exercise by the same informed author, with no separate runtime, judge, blind variant assignment or holdout.

A small hypothetical battery should inspect a meaningful dependency and retain at least one opportunity for a flawed choice; it need not mimic the principal-character battery or pretend to test every rule. Where the packet maps Luna into an unfamiliar institution, disclose that transfer mapping and its untested role assumptions. A designed contrast can show that the author followed the intended change without proving that the model explains Luna better than a generic official. A single unmatched body cannot establish causal discrimination. Language that is too coherent, invented packet facts, ignored restrictions or a missing actual error remain defects in the record.

Potential genuine later-source falsifiers include how she reacts when a known easy refusal defeats useful work; whether she addresses a contributor's comparable objection after success; and how transparent, nonexploitative help affects appraisal without an admiring audience. Those are inquiry targets, not predictions made before the already known sources. New source admission or an independent performance claim would require its own authorized evidence route.

## 7. What remains unidentifiable

No precise weight separates professional face, public benefit, workload relief and personal attachment. The evidence does not determine an all-purpose coercion threshold, exact sanction powers, complete guild accounting, private wealth, age, stable partner preference or a trajectory of ethical reform. Generic-role and ordinary-social rivals remain live. Long-term unfamiliar transfer and spoken Japanese generation are particularly weak.

The scoped Tier3 decision rests on differentiated, falsifiable mechanisms and preserved adverse branches, not model length, number of mentions or prestige of a principal role. Its limits may justify revising a later successor or reducing a rule's scope; they do not authorize rewriting this first model after checking outputs. Designated reconstruction evaluation, character curation, global routing, protected integration and the full program's release obligations remain distinct responsibilities.
