# Otohime: provisional conditional reconstruction

Status: initial scoped Tier 3 model for the Core and Advanced edited replays. The [monograph](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_OTOHIME_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) and [cohort 31 index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_TRPG_REPLAY_COHORT31_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) own interpretation and source. All source and broad contrasts are known to the same author. This version precedes exact hypothetical inputs and original first responses, and claims no independent maturity or evaluator grade.

## Operating representation

Track perceived danger to self and others, preparation actually purchased, remaining tools, terms of a protective promise, concrete target of Covering, relevant rules, allied help, owned and borrowed money, comfort expenditure, recognition sought, information conveyed to Chiho and the recipient's actual response. Keep fear, useful protection and moral justification separate. The absence of a Death Return is not absence of danger; access to one is not permission to ignore its cost.

Generate appraisal and options before result: avoid a task, prepare, ask for resources, limit a promise, Cover, evade, accept allied help, use a costly fallback, explain, apologize or keep borrowing. Retain incomplete planning, self-interest and unsuccessful communication. Never supply a guaranteed rescue merely because he is modeled as protective.

## OT-R01: fear favors preparation and bounded protection rather than uniform flight

**Proposition.** When danger is salient, Otohime tends to seek control through equipment, tactics and limits. A salient pact or protectable party asset can still lead him to interpose at real cost; fear does not predict automatic desertion or painless heroism.

**Supporting evidence.** He removes dungeon requests and worries about the toad task, then participates after preparation/reassurance (Core S025-029). His survival-limited promise precedes actual protection of Chiho and a chosen costly Death Return (S074-075). Advanced S079/S099 records actual damage, ally support and protection, while S104-105 shows the inapplicable Wind Curtain, Covering the house,82 damage and survival without return.

**Contrary evidence.** Preparation misses the shared-area risk in his own later explanation. He protects a valuable house, so self-interest is present. Rules and allied mitigation strongly affect cost. The earlier push is not erased by the later protective acts.

**Rivals.** Optimal tank play, the Oath mechanic, seeking a spotlight, protecting an investment and wanting approval. Fear as stated motive does not identify its exclusive causal share.

**Application conditions.** Specify what he believes is dangerous, whom or what he has a relation to, actual tools and fallback costs. Predict preparation or explicit limits before an easy claim of fearlessness. Interposition is plausible when a concrete protected interest and usable means exist, but outcome must follow the stipulated mechanics and uncertainty. An unsupported impossible rescue remains impossible.

**Do not assume.** Universal courage, certain survival, a free Death Return, another work's protagonist history, fear cured by one victory, or a successful defense before the roll.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the repeated local mixture of fear and protection across two replays; lower for irreversible danger without tools, strangers and exact thresholds.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable danger with usable preparation producing no interest in control would weaken the first tendency. Repeated automatic flight despite the same explicit pact, available means and comparable cost would weaken bounded protection; repeated effortless success would contradict the cost boundary only with separately sourced mechanics.

## OT-R02: a security rationale can coexist with comfort and continued dependence

**Proposition.** Otohime is likely to explain resource requests through survival and protecting others, yet also pursue comfort and continue borrowing. His stated defensive purpose should not be used as a complete accounting of the money or its social cost.

**Supporting evidence.** ADV S025 records2700 KE actually given by Chiho, purchased scroll variety and10 KE remaining claims. S031 contrasts his Suite with her Economy bedding. In S086-088 he explains fear and preparation, actually spends two scrolls/800 KE to communicate and explicitly continues future borrowing. S108 retains the bedding difference.

**Contrary evidence.** Purchased tools and protective acts are useful; the entire request is not exposed as fraud. The source has no complete budget, repayment schedule or proof that every comfort expense came from the same borrowed money. He can recognize a communication gap.

**Rivals.** Ordinary party pooling, class/build costs, comedic bookkeeping, a genuine security project and opportunistic comfort. These can overlap; actual receipts matter more than an inferred pure motive.

**Application conditions.** Specify resource owner, request, purpose, available balance and any actual lending terms. Expect safety reasoning when risk is explicit, but keep discretionary comfort and the lender's separate interests in view. A gift is not automatically a loan, and a loan is not automatically shared property. Continued requests can remain after sincere protection.

**Do not assume.** Unlimited credit, repayment, free assent from prior giving, that comfort proves no care, that defensive utility authorizes spending another's money, or that explanation ends debt.

**Confidence.** Moderate within the repeated Chiho resource relation, lower for unrelated lenders, written terms and long-term repayment after the ending.

**Falsifier.** A repeated comparable fully specified record of forgoing comfort, asking no additional help and repaying under the same conditions would weaken continued dependence/comfort as a tendency. Evidence that none of the acquired tools or protection benefited the stated goal would challenge the useful-security qualification.

## OT-R03: invited questioning can elicit costly explanation with incomplete repair

**Proposition.** A willing private conversation about a specific relational concern can lead Otohime to admit a planning or communication failure and take practical steps to explain. Acceptance by the other person remains contingent, and a local repair can leave borrowing and fear intact.

**Supporting evidence.**21 asks to speak after Chiho's request; Otohime willingly participates, explains his feared counterfactuals, acknowledges a gap and thanks/handshakes (ADV S085-088). He then actually spends Voice Mute and Wind Breath scrolls to address her. Chiho partly accepts yet retains doubts, and he says borrowing continues.

**Contrary evidence.** This is one linked counseling sequence, not a repeated treatment effect. His account may be incomplete and comes from him. A costly explanation does not prove sole motive or establish changed behavior after the printed ending.

**Rivals.** A player-requested reconciliation, concern for party function, a tactical Oath renewal, desire to look better and ordinary willingness to talk to a friend. Exact private speech is not an independent mind-state measure.

**Application conditions.** State the invitation and his actual willingness, the concern being raised and what he knows. Generate an explanation and possibly an acknowledgment, not an omniscient confession forced by a mediator. Preserve the listener's stated response. When willingness or concern is unspecified, ask or leave uncertainty rather than invent assent.

**Do not assume.** Therapy efficacy, full truth, compulsory access to feelings, instant trust, ended borrowing, cured fear, romantic commitment or recipient forgiveness owed for cost.

**Confidence.** Low to moderate for a plausible local response supported by the performed conversation; low for repetition, unfamiliar mediators or explicit refusal to discuss the matter.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable willing private invitations about the same explicit concern that elicit neither explanation nor acknowledgment would weaken the response tendency. Consistent complete behavioral resolution after words alone would challenge the retained-partial-repair boundary.

## Generation and evaluation limits

The first-person motive account is evidence to weigh, not a license to overwrite costs or earlier acts. Serious transfer retains fear, resource ownership, limits and recipient autonomy without requiring a literal shield or return ability. Japanese names and pragmatic distinctions remain source-bound; English output is not Japanese voice validation. Three planned dependent pairs follow foundation/input freeze, with every original first preserved. They cannot complete nine-category transfer, independently calibrate accuracy or upgrade an evaluator grade.
