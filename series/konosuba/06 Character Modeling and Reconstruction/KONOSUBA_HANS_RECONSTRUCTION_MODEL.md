# Hans: provisional reconstruction of concealment, priorities and material tactics

Status: cohort27 initial scoped Tier3 candidate, authored before exact hypothetical inputs and first bodies. The separate Hans monograph and HA27 evidence index own factual interpretation and locators. All inspected sources are already known. This is not a mature final model, independent prediction result, capability grade, confidence gain or program-completion claim.

## Scope and operation

Hans is the same executive across human disguise and slime forms. The spring manager he admits consuming is another person. Ordinary slimes or a later poison bottle are not automatically surviving Hans. Preserve his knowledge, his perceived cover, the objective, the agreement he believes protects him and the available bodily resources. Later accounts of the same encounter do not create additional tests.

Three conditional candidates describe a fallible harmful agent. They share evidence and are not independent votes. A simulated action should be conditional on supplied priorities rather than an instruction to maximize damage or display cleverness. A nonmagical transfer changes the means explicitly; it does not import unprovided poison, regeneration or an institutional charter.

## HA-R01: objective value can outweigh pursuit, until exposure changes the route

**Proposition.** With viable cover, Hans can pursue a slow destructive objective through concealed infrastructure interference. When exposed, he can switch to direct completion and abandon a low-value pursuit rather than insist on defeating every available opponent. Appetite, status and threat appraisal can interfere with this goal priority; it is not perfect professional rationality.

**Supporting evidence.** HA27-01: describes a long infiltration plan and asks for work reports. HA27-03–04: denial and an offer of a belongings search precede blocked departure and disclosure. He pursues the boastful Kazuma, then lets the weak Adventurer go and returns to poisoning the source. HA27-05–06: repeated fragment attempts keep the source as the target despite counterattack.

**Contrary evidence.** The bath-gaze inference fails; he initially gives Kazuma more attention than Wiz's danger warrants. The Demon King reports unannounced consumption and contamination of food stores (HA27-01, HA27-04, HA27-11). Those facts oppose an exclusively optimal mission calculus. Letting one opponent go is not universal mercy.

**Rivals.** Generic persistence, fear of stronger opponents, a stock impatient villain abandoning conversation, or simple predation. The conditional account differs by keeping cover, objective access and target relevance separate. It must allow a genuine change of priority rather than treating all actions as pursuit of one retrospectively invented plan.

**Application conditions.** A known objective, a perceived cover state, remaining access, an assessed opponent and an opportunity cost of diversion. If the prompt provides no goal beyond escape, do not invent a water-supply campaign. If a target is believed to be able to stop the plan, do not automatically classify them as irrelevant from reader knowledge.

**Do not assume.** That patience is harmless, that letting someone leave repairs a civilian killing, that all interlocutors consent to cooperate, or that an announced ten-to-twenty-year plan was completed. A postponed objective can remain harmful without every interruption producing immediate murder.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the directly observed pursuit/return contrast and persistent source-directed tactics. Lower for unexposed long-term conduct and ordinary nonviolent tradeoffs. Much of the original preparation is his report, not a watched multi-year record.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable choices where clearly low-relevance pursuit reliably overrides viable objective access without a changed goal or constraint, or where loss of cover never changes the route. Do not save the rule by redefining every chased person as secretly essential after observing the result.

## HA-R02: role cues and presumed agreements can substitute for checking dangerous information

**Proposition.** Hans can rely too readily on a convenient social reading: an inattentive-looking witness seems safe, a weak job seems unimportant, and a colleague's noninterference promise seems protective. When that reading is explicitly contradicted, he attempts denial, appeal, threat or retreat. The model predicts fallible inference rather than omniscient manipulation.

**Supporting evidence.** HA27-01: Kazuma's sexual gaze is taken as reassurance despite actual overhearing. HA27-03: Wiz's recognition defeats the manager identity and the no-carried-poison defense. HA27-04–05: he invokes the executive agreement, then faces its civilian exception after admitting manager consumption. His threatened and pleading responses follow a real loss of expected protection.

**Contrary evidence.** Hans is not unable to update: he abandons cover and changes tactics. His original covert work shows some practical information management. Kazuma's apparent weakness is partly real even though it does not make him harmless to the plan. A mistaken inference is not automatically stupidity in every domain.

**Rivals.** Ordinary surprise, selective narrator disclosure, deliberate testing of others, or a fully understood agreement that he simply chooses to breach. The candidate remains a hypothesis about reliance on convenient cues; it cannot assert that Hans never knew the civilian restriction or that every denial reveals his private belief.

**Application conditions.** Distinguish the agreement's supplied terms, what Hans knows, what he thinks a witness noticed, and what the other actor actually reveals. A hypothetical must not give him access to a hidden disclosure. The original Wiz agreement is not a universal compact binding every colleague or employer.

**Do not assume.** Guaranteed deception, automatic belief from polite address, consent from shared employment, or an unseen exception invented to force retaliation. Kazuma's unprovided ammunition count cannot become Hans's foreknowledge. Wolbach's refusal remains a refusal despite his request for reports.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the concrete information mismatches; lower for exact private belief and a generalized tendency outside these contexts. Direct action and a narrator's later reconstruction remain distinct planes.

**Falsifier.** Comparable attributable encounters where he consistently verifies the relevant witness knowledge and exact exception before relying on protection, or contrary disclosures never affect his strategy despite a real available response. Do not recast every documented mistake as intentional without new evidence.

## HA-R03: bodily flexibility buys tactical alternatives, but scale creates new costs

**Proposition.** Under counterattack, Hans can spend or rearrange material to escape restraint and keep the objective accessible. Regeneration, fragments and enlargement provide different options rather than a single invulnerability. Available matter, opponent countermeasures and altered attention constrain the result. At enlarged scale, food diversion is observed while the degree of reduced deliberation remains uncertain.

**Supporting evidence.** HA27-05: Wiz freezes him; he breaks off the arm, regenerates, runs and throws pieces from the frozen lower body. HA27-06: one fragment reaches the source after Kazuma runs out of arrows; Hans enlarges using local matter and reacts to food. HA27-07: fragmentation by Explosion precedes reported distributed freezing and purification.

**Contrary evidence.** Freezing actually works at the earlier scale; fragments can be intercepted; his successful piece surprises him. The large body does not provide an unstoppable route to the source. Final cleanup after Kazuma's blackout is reported, so the record is not an uninterrupted watched test of every fragment.

**Rivals.** A stock slime's automatic biology, panic without strategy, overwhelming size or retrospective narration assigning purpose to reflex. The candidate preserves both the chosen material tactics and uncertainty about later cognition. It does not treat every bodily change as an ingenious conscious plan.

**Application conditions.** Use only supplied body state, available matter, freeze or containment, an actual route and known countermeasures. Human or institutional analogues require explicit replacement means such as expendable tools or delegated routes; they do not get literal regeneration. Separate intended completion from observed arrival.

**Do not assume.** Unlimited mass, universal magic immunity, instant regrowth under every condition, an unprovided antidote, or survival from a later bottle of poison. Heat and poison resistance are different. A rule's respect for stipulated physics is not an independent behavioral success.

**Confidence.** Moderate for direct escape/fragment choices; lower for exact material limits, subjective state after enlargement and the completeness of the reported cleanup. Biological self-claims are not universal experimental laws.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable cases where an available, understood expendable body route is never used despite the same objective and no competing cost, or reliable immunity contradicting the actually successful restraint. Evidence that enlarged behavior remains consistently discriminating despite comparable food distraction would weaken the reduced-control interpretation; absence of that evidence is not proof of mindlessness.

## Voice and operating modes

Public cover favors administrative denial and misleading cooperation; exposed interaction permits personal recognition, appeals to terms, threats and urgency. Use these pragmatic choices from supplied information rather than a stock evil accent. Printed Japanese is not audio, and a monster name or idiom is not automatically L3.

Canonical-comic mode can preserve the mismatch between game assumptions and dangerous biology where supplied. A food diversion can be funny while attempted poisoning and a reported civilian killing remain real. H3 is local gag dependence, not ordinary uncertainty; fantasy embodiment alone is not H4. Serious transfer retains harmful priorities, concealment, partial updating and resource costs, without granting a modern actor unprovided capacities.

Ordinary pleasure, affection, long-term loyalty and unrelated ethical choices remain weakly identified. Do not fill them with generic politeness or relentless cruelty. A role-based hypothetical can be underdetermined when objective, knowledge or authority is missing.
