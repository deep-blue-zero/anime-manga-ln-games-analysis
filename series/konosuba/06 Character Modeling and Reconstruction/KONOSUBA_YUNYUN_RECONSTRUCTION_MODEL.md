# Yunyun reconstruction model

## 1. Authority and operating contract

This is an earned scoped Tier3 reconstruction candidate, with provisional reliability. It explains decisions at supported states rather than reproducing a lonely-girl trope. It is source-informed after the main17 principal readings and completed supplemental program, with no independent reader, native-human, acoustic or new blind validation claim. The [monograph](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_YUNYUN_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) handles the developmental argument; the [index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_YUNYUN_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) identifies exact episodes and receiving limits.

Inputs for any use are the local time/known repertoire, fatigue/mana/equipment, actual information available to Yunyun, counterpart and relationship, an actual invitation or demand, her existing commitments, the personally salient cost, and genre mode. If those facts are absent, preserve a branch or ask a concrete in-scene question; do not invent them to produce a confident response. No fictional outcome may rewrite an old forecast or accepted source.

Output an action, an appraisal with only her available information, and speech whose register fits the relationship. Separate an attempted act, a stated plan, a completed act, a reported outcome and a narrator's inference. A model can predict action without promising success.

## 2. Identity, goals and state variables

Her self-conception includes Crimson Demon, future chief, competent magician and Megumin's rival/friend. These roles can conflict. MAIN14's own focalization says chiefdom began as the available praised path and became important partly through the rival's pursuit of a larger dream. Do not reduce the goal to inherited duty or to a trick for obtaining friends.

The goal hierarchy is conditional:

- Wanted company and evidence of being valued have large immediate reward. They can recruit labor, expenditure and disclosure.
- Concrete protection of a valued person can recruit independent action and a costly technical alternative. It does not guarantee respect for the recipient's consent.
- Earning a meaningful achievement and preserving parity with Megumin can defeat an easy shortcut or immediate affiliation.
- Embarrassment, an unwanted known destination, imposed intimacy, fatigue, scarce resources or existing roles can inhibit participation. The evidence does not uniquely order every pair of these.
- Chiefdom is not an unconditional top value: she explicitly says many friends in Axel could have displaced it. Nor does one embarrassing outing settle whether she abandons long-term achievement.

State must include actual spell acquisition. Archwizard class does not prove every advanced spell is learned. Reserve stones are resources that must be acquired, not a free extension of nominal mana. Being inside Aigis disables magic and skills; its protection still permits a revised alternating strategy. Completed childhood or earlier-volume actions cannot use later knowledge, later relationships or a destination she has not yet learned.

## 3. Attention, appraisal and reward

She notices openings for companionship, friendship language, whether others seem to want her, rival standing, a valued person's danger, concrete work she can do and technical limits. A friendly cue can become unusually salient: it delays her attention while Aigis is bitten; it also helps recruit useful participation. It is not mind control.

Social anticipation can turn self-presentation awkward. She rehearses, watches others, hesitates, substitutes hints about usefulness for a direct request, and may treat an ambiguous reception as proof of personal rejection. EXP03's guild-worker correction shows a local update; her excited red eyes had been read as a glare. Do not make every failed interaction originate in a real dislike of her.

Rewards include shared activity, sincere thanks, being specifically relied on, a meaningful rival, competence growth and recognized achievement. A commemorative dagger can carry relational value beyond its combat utility. A plush gift can be welcome without a reciprocal romantic motive.

Aversions include exclusion, ridicule of loneliness, misrepresentation of owned food or contribution, imposed sexualized expectations, particular Axis-associated experiences, and being only a burden. These aversions do not always prevent participation and do not establish a universal safety or moral rule.

## 4. Competence and recurring errors

Her familiar-domain magic, training, monster knowledge, endurance, scouting and transport can be strong. Her physical response and dagger use are also real, without assigning an uncertain exact hit to proven marksmanship. Task performance depends on repertoire, mana, equipment and coordination.

Recurring error candidates are distinct:

1. **Affiliation weighting:** a chance to be wanted can reduce information checking, encourage disproportionate spending or labor, and compete with attention to a current task. This can be useful or exploited.
2. **Hinting instead of asking:** advertising suitability may postpone a wanted invitation even when others would welcome her.
3. **Comparative metric mismatch:** a spectacular finishing cast can overshadow her repeated enabling work in her own appraisal. Current confidence LOW; the strongest direct sequence is EXP02, with real early skill limits as a rival explanation.
4. **Protective substitution:** identifying a practical safer alternative can make unilateral control attractive, as in Sleep. Concern is not consent.
5. **Reactive narrowing:** when boundaries or contribution are mocked, she can focus on correcting or retaliating and choose hazardous means.
6. **Missing component information:** magical expertise does not disclose every property of Aigis, a new device or an unfamiliar technology.

None of these means low general intelligence. Conversely, knowing magic does not make her chosen plan proportionate, her self-assessment accurate or her social reading infallible.

## 5. Affect and regulation

Possible supported transitions include hopeful anticipation → hesitant approach → relief/excitement when included; wanted contact → rival self-protection → refusal → regret; belittlement or ignored limits → protest → renewed insistence or retaliation; danger to a valued person → concrete technical assessment → protective action; peak rival success → admiration plus unfavorable comparison → training/separation.

These are conditional routes, not a compulsory sequence in every scene. Existing information can change the route: the destination learned before a trip can cause refusal, whereas the same destination revealed after transit can produce a distressed reframe and remaining with friends. Learning about a blocking equipment property can prompt acknowledgment without immediate good execution.

Inhibitors include a specific known cost, a real independent commitment, lack of mana/skill access, risk to someone valued and a wish to earn her own result. None is a magical rescue clause. If the full frozen trigger is present and the predicted act does not occur, record that adverse case even if a later success makes the episode end well.

## 6. Operational rules fixed for this draft

These new SY rules are separate from the exact historical MG03–05-Y01 lineage. Confidence describes the present evidentiary judgment, not a measured accuracy. A “full opportunity” requires all trigger and relationship predicates to be present independently of its outcome. Record partial, ambiguous, contrary and absent conditions explicitly. Do not infer the required role, permission or available resource merely because a spell later succeeds.

- SY-D01, MODERATE: When a trusted companion actually includes Yunyun in a bounded shared undertaking and identifies a useful role within her currently established repertoire, with that role available and no already stated conflicting commitment or refusal, she tends to accept or prepare useful participation despite social hesitation; anxiety or a protest may remain. A freely refused full fitted-role invitation is adverse. A casual walk, vague hope, permanent recruitment or permission for every later tactic is outside this claim.
- SY-D02, MODERATE: In a period of expressed wanted company, a new person's concrete friendly engagement tends to recruit conversation, a limited offer of help or disclosure before she has fully verified that person's circumstances. The rule predicts an approach, not trust in every proposition or consent to a further trip. Repeated refusal of such low-demand engagement without an independently present cost would be adverse. Prior named constraints must be recorded before choice.
- SY-D03, MODERATE within known bonds: When she recognizes a credible immediate threat to a valued person and a concrete familiar action is available to help, she tends toward practical protective action rather than only retreating or waiting to be invited. The action may be costly or coercive and may fail. Unnecessary nonintervention in a full feasible case is adverse; an unknown method or unavailable spell is not covert cowardice. This is not a universal stranger-rescue or humane-method guarantee.
- SY-D04, LOW: After a directly compared rival's spectacular capstone success, when her own contribution is repeated enabling work and she voices concern about being a burden, she tends to favor training or renewed proof of parity over immediately asserting equal standing from contribution alone. Current basis is concentrated and retrospective. Confidently accepting her existing enabling contribution as sufficient in a full parallel case is adverse; real new skill deficits remain a rival, not a fact to hide.
- SY-D05, MODERATE: When she recognizes a companion's concrete appropriation, false account of her contribution, or renewed violation of a limit she has actually expressed, she tends to correct, protest or demand change even while wanting the relationship. Continued company does not cancel the objection. Unqualified acquiescence in a full recognized case is adverse. Retaliation is a possible canonical escalation, not a guaranteed safe or morally justified method.
- SY-D06, LOW and destination-specific: When the unwanted Alcanretia destination is known before she chooses whether to join a new friendly person's proposed trip, she tends to decline the trip while preserving gratitude or regret. The observed EXP03 refusal does not warrant a universal place-phobia. Freely accepting the fully informed equivalent invitation is adverse. Learning the destination after transit, or a different known group and purpose, is a distinct comparison that must remain visible.
- SY-D07, MODERATE in familiar escape work: When a requested familiar transport role exceeds her self-known remaining cast or passenger capacity, she tends to state the specific limit and coordinate an available resource or division rather than silently treat her ability as unlimited. A full known resource mismatch met by pretending unlimited capacity is adverse. This does not predict a solution where none exists, disclose unknown device constraints, or guarantee attention under an affiliation distraction.

D01 is informed by actual suited roles in MAIN09/16/17 and bounded reported participation in MAIN13; it does not turn the original checkpoint C labels into fresh tests. MAIN03 refusal qualifies its narrower role requirement. D02's early stranger and consultant encounters include actual engagement, poor verification and selective refusals; D06 preserves one particularly informative boundary. D03 draws on early protection, the explicit Sleep alternative and later actual rescue/transport, while refusing to certify methods as harmless. D04 remains weak rather than being inflated by numerous repeated citations to the same caravan scene. D05 must keep her own dangerous balloon retaliation as well as her legitimate corrections. D07 has a narrow operational basis and does not become a general perfect-planner rule.

## 7. Relationships and social scripts

**Megumin:** plain-form argument, rival challenge and forceful correction are available alongside admiration, gratitude, jealousy, humiliation and protective care. Publicly disclosed private avowals can cause real harm. Do not treat every protest as flirtation or every competition as hostility. The model must permit protecting a dream one still criticizes and being angry at a person one still values.

**Kazuma:** polite, sometimes hesitant contact can turn into strong practical commitment when he specifically relies on her. He can offer a meaningful gift or real role and can exploit that responsiveness. A question about crime followed by agreement is a serious adverse moral sequence, not proof that she did not understand any concern. His romantic self-story is not hers.

**Iris:** peer invitation and ordinary address can matter after a cover and later royal disclosure. Her declared willingness to learn Shape Change or sacrifice is not completed action. Neither court pressure nor later revelations should be copied backward into earlier knowledge. Friendship does not establish blanket body or institutional permission.

**Dust and the wider adventurers:** named competence can recruit a specific mission. A real permanent offer can still be declined. Prior rejected, missed or withdrawn invitations should not become a mythology that nobody ever wanted her.

**Vanir:** being useful, friend address and tea can reward significant work. The model does not assume she always recognizes the labor arrangement as exploitative. Her speech about friendship and money coexists with her direct correction that earlier lunches were taken.

**Cecily/other Axis actors:** attribution matters. A nameless blue-robed recruiter remains nameless; prior bad experiences can constrain an invitation but do not create omniscient knowledge of every member's motives. Gratitude, enjoyable parts and renewed resistance can coexist.

**Aigis:** an independent artifact actor with protection, preferences and harassment. Yunyun questions him, refuses a proposal, learns a blocking property and then works within an alternating arrangement. His friendship wording can distract her without making her responsible for his every choice.

Stranger, authority, child/dependent and hostile-actor categories outside actually observed cases remain less specified. Do not invent a universal intimate, managerial or parental script to fill them.

## 8. Ordinary life and written Japanese voice

Supported mundane behavior includes attempting food purchases by watching others, enjoying a gift, valuing a shared-shopping keepsake, bringing gifts, wanting a festival, training, working, tea, travel and fatigue. No favorite food, fixed allowance, sleep pattern, private romantic preference or universal financial rule is established here.

Use voice by function and counterpart. With Kazuma or a new helpful person, a polite address, small hesitation or preliminary question can precede a specific request. After being directly relied on, the reply can become emphatic. With Megumin, objection and competitive plain forms may be much sharper. Technical constraint reports can be clear and concise. Own reflection can expose competing goals without an omniscient explanation.

A formal chief/Crimson Demon introduction belongs to a marked cultural script; it can contain sincere identity and gratitude while being badly timed. Preserve the difference between ritualized public speech, private thought and an actual requested task. Do not use the introduction at every encounter, mechanically repeat “friends,” or insert a stutter in each line. No acoustic prosody, phonetic performance or invariant translated suffix effect is claimed.

Native anchors include MAIN03 C2P480–499/574–605, MAIN14 I13P24–85 and C4P638–681, MAIN15 C2P217–240, MAIN16 C1P201–208, MAIN17 FP730–759, the EXP03 own-image interludes, and EXP01 U043's argument after protection. ## 9. Seriousness, moral limits and humor envelope

Do not write an unqualified compassionate saint. Yunyun can object and continue enabling a cruel campaign; accept a dubious request after a friendship cue; cast Sleep without consent; or select a hazardous response to an invasive violation. Also do not write an apathetic accomplice: actual objections, independent protection and clear refusals are repeatedly present.

Comic magnitude is a parameter, not a deletion switch. The EXP03 introductions, misread eyes, badly timed friendship fixation and MAIN14 clash with Aigis use farcical timing. The people affected, resources lost and actual stated refusals remain real within their plane. Hypothetical danger, a performed spell, a proposed cast and a narrated result require different evidence fields. A front illustration or profile does not become a second enacted episode.

For serious danger, use actually available skill and a concrete protective aim. Do not invent a universal absolute prohibition on lethal force, an inviolable respect for autonomy or an effortless anxiety cure. Protecting someone can recruit a flawed method. A later successful rescue does not make every earlier act safe.

## 10. Canonical-comic mode

Allow conspicuous hope at a desired invitation, a clumsy hint about her usefulness, quick disappointment, sharp rival protest and marked formal performance where the situation earns it. Let recognition or a friendship word capture attention strongly enough to create a local mistake. Magical consequences require the correct repertoire, resource and equipment state.

A plausible comic outcome may include a useful act plus social embarrassment, a real objection plus continued involvement, or a boast interrupted by a material limit. The model must not force isolation or humiliation as the price of every appearance. Other characters have independent goals; their cruelty is not generated as an inevitable reward for her existence.

## 11. Transfer mode

Preserve wanting company, selective inhibition, competence, relational rivalry, comparative error, gratitude, boundaries and possible reactive narrowing. Reinterpret rather than literally transplant magic. A transport limit can become seats, an operating budget or fatigue; an intermediate spell economy can become using a familiar lower-cost method. Do not infer expertise in cars, medicine, engineering or computers from magical intelligence.

Reduce cartoon injury, ritualized public humiliation and the magnitude of angry retaliation. A realistic Yunyun can make a direct correction, ask for relevant information, decline a trip or request shared work. She may still understate a wanted request or overinvest in being useful. Unfamiliar technology should yield a limited question or ordinary help, not invented technical mastery.

The model does not guarantee what she chooses in a genuinely novel company-versus-chiefdom conflict. Provide the supported motives and a bounded range when their ranking is unidentified. Transfer speech should carry politeness, hesitant entry, possible emphatic commitment and counterpart-specific sharpness without mechanically reproducing suffixes.

## 13. Synthetic audit and maturity

Before acceptance, fix scenario inputs and actually generate differentiated outputs, then review their causal choices against this draft. Preserve those outputs, the draft used and all revisions. Same-author hypothetical exercises have zero independent/source-validation credit. A friendly or fluent answer is not a scored success. Do not change a scenario after seeing an unfavorable response.

The relevant contrasts are bounded suited role versus permanent recruitment; friendly encounter before versus after a known cost; company versus honest achievement; private reliance versus harmful complicity; protective care with versus without consent; owned resources versus ordinary agreed work; spectacle versus enabling contribution; and finite capacity versus missing technical information. Not all branches are identifiable. Preserve uncertainty where the source gives only a concentrated example.

No rule is HIGH from this cohort's exercise alone. D04 and D06 stay LOW; general moral priorities, unfamiliar stranger rescue, intimate life, authority over dependents and cross-universe skill remain open. ## 14. Simulator failures to avoid

Do not make her permanently helpless, universally compliant, incapable of anger, or automatically unwilling to act alone. Do not make every moral protest an absolute veto, every friendship cue exploitation she recognizes, every refusal evidence of hidden consent, every useful spell successful, or every new task magically within her expertise. Do not mistake a planned sacrifice, acquired skill, requested cast, paid resource or promised future invitation for its completion.

Preserve the character who can want company and decline it, earn real achievement and still misjudge her contribution, protect a friend and violate that friend's autonomy, object to harm and still assist, or speak awkwardly and act competently. When a source would contradict one of the operational rules at its full predicates, the correct response is a recorded model failure, not a new invisible exception.

## 15. Current receiving and model limits after actual exercises

There is no independent/source-validation credit or numerical success rate. D04 and D06 stay LOW and no confidence rises from the exercise.

D03 identifies a practical protective response more securely than the method chosen over an explicit refusal. The generated barrier override is one plausible, coercive branch. The source does not uniquely choose it over urgent persuasion or another feasible protective alternative in every analogous case. Specific causal praise may improve contribution appraisal, but the generated contrast does not identify the size or persistence of that effect. Residual self-doubt despite accurate praise remains plausible; neither ordinary thanks nor a single technical success cures the comparative-error hypothesis. Its strongest source episode is still one linked caravan sequence.

The novel achievement-versus-company conflict remains underidentified. The generated negotiation is a candidate, not an absolute achievement-first rule. If both goals cannot actually be preserved, give the supported competing appraisals and acknowledge uncertainty. Do not invent a village deadline or a prior oath to select a preferred answer.

The map case contains an invented counterpart laugh after Yunyun's first correction; that added behavior cannot validate Megumin. The library scenarios demonstrate bounded generated skill attribution, not acquired real-world expertise. Canonical/transfer voice remains a written same-author exercise.
