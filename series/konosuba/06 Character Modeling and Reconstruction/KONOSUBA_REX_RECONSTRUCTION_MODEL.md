Rex is レックス, the leader of the greatsword/spear/axe party, distinct from Sophie, Terry and the Keith/Taylor/Leen group. Explicit names and recalled shared events join the early Axel and later capital encounters. A wrong-name correction is not a new alias-person. The [individual evidence rows](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_REX_SOPHIE_TERRY_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) and [monograph](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_REX_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) preserve the actual source planes.

This scoped Tier3 candidate has three conditional rules, not a completeness or accuracy certificate. The shared native provenance contains 1,556 known BASE context slots in six members/two objects, with other actors, reports, blank slots and hypotheses. Four EXP03 BASE members are complete; two CONTEXP02 members are partial. There is no new full-source, ruby, image, admission, independent observation or validation credit. Same-event recollection is not a second independent decision.

## State to carry into a scene

Track his present goal, relationship, believed ability of each person, evidence actually encountered, task and proposed contribution, available tools, physical readiness, other people's expressed choices and action already completed. Keep these states separate. His party's reputation does not give him perfect assessment; an apology to one mage does not establish the other mage's skill. A working greatsword against goblins is not adequate protection against every demon. A healed-enough-to-return report is not full recovery.

He wants work, stronger opportunities, earnings and a name for the party. He can boast, discount others, become irritated, joke, apologize, admire a particular performance and do useful work. He is not a polished modern coordinator whose actions have been optimized into one coherent ethical system. His protest at pressure on Yunyun and warning to Megumin coexist with his pressure against retreat and continued insulting labels. Recipient enjoyment, affection, permission, competence and successful completion are not interchangeable.

## Conditional rules

### RX-R01 — Selective ability judgment can update locally without becoming well calibrated

Start from the information Rex actually has. He may dismiss a newcomer from apparent status, literal limited denials or self-favoring reputation judgments. Concrete performance or a credible specific reminder can change a local claim and elicit apology or recognition. Do not automatically propagate that change to an unobserved companion, all other tasks or every prior insult.

Support: R01-R04: reputation disagreement, upper-magic denial overread, actual Yunyun display, local apology and renewed doubt toward Megumin. R06: admits he knows Yunyun’s display but not Megumin’s capacity. R16/R19: danger warning with old label, then actual extraordinary magic recognized and later recruited. R21-R22: contested recollections, reported apology, correction of exaggerated pursuit and actual name correction.

Contrary evidence: R05: claims he might have won after hearing of another fighter’s defeat; counterfactual is not demonstrated superiority. R04/R16: insulting label recurs after other correction, so updating is partial. R21: the later apology is his report, not a new independent watched apology.

Serious rivals: Generic pride and confirmation bias; Generic evidence-sensitive recruitment; Strong explicit ability cues and selected narrative occasions.

Apply when: A current belief, actual cue and scope of known performance are specified. Permit local concession and continuing awkward banter or adjacent error. A challenge can be wrong; not every contradictory literal answer warrants a global inference.

Do not assume: No hidden ability knowledge, deliberate concealment of explicitly supplied facts, universal stubbornness, inevitable apology, optimal calibration, diagnosis or complete memory. A nickname/address is not an independent motive measurement.

Confidence: Provisional moderate within-source support for selective rather than total updating; low incremental identification beyond generic rivals.

Falsifier: Repeated comparable known discrepancies that produce no local acknowledgment would weaken updating. Repeated careful transfer only where justified and absence of adjacent overgeneralization would weaken the selective-error component. Familiar-source retellings and model-authored hypotheticals cannot provide those independent observations.

### RX-R02 — Chosen work brings concrete team contributions and protective action under finite means

For an undertaken party task, expect Rex to seek useful contributions, offer his own available work and coordinate with specialists. A vulnerable participant can become the focus of an objection or escape warning. Keep poor threat assessment, incomplete protection and actual inability possible; willingness and reassurance do not create bodily readiness or successful execution.

Support: R03: objection to pushing a reluctant Yunyun. R07/R09-R10: promise of protection, actual greatsword work and rear warning; Terry intercepts and Yunyun casts. R13-R16: barely-fightable healing, apparent fracture, left-handed sword use/throw, renewed impact and urgent escape warning.

Contrary evidence: R07: protests when the girls take his invitation to retreat. R08/R11: mistaken target inference and sudden Hoost defeat limit expertise and safety. R15: encouragement before learning Yunyun’s paralysis does not establish a knowingly accepted impossible plan.

Serious rivals: Generic teammate duty and practical division of labor; Generic immediate care for someone in danger; Genre front-line fighter role.

Apply when: A present task, usable means, known limitations and other actors’ separate contributions are specified. Decide an attempted next act at this point. Warning, offer, tool use, effort, completion, result and recovery remain different stages.

Do not assume: No unlimited strength, healed injury by declaration, guaranteed success, invented tools or authority to conscript. Do not assign Terry’s interception, Sophie’s support or a mage’s spell to Rex. Neither always self-sacrifice nor an optimal risk/consent policy is warranted.

Confidence: Provisional moderate support for actual task work and some protective interventions; exact trade-offs under novel competing needs remain low-confidence.

Falsifier: Repeated similar chosen tasks with available modest means in which he offers only boasting and no work would weaken contribution. Repeated informed indifference to equally salient vulnerable partners would weaken the local protection expectation. New same-author output compliance is not independent confirmation.

### RX-R03 — Ambitious recruitment considers task fit and may end at an expressed refusal

Rex seeks stronger work, earnings and reputation through his party. Recruit for a concrete contribution whose limits can fit the proposed setting. In a later membership invitation an explicit competing attachment can end the exchange in banter and departure. This is a local pattern with contrary evidence, not a universal guarantee that he immediately honors every request to withdraw.

Support: R06/R10: observed mage capacity motivates work and membership proposals; discussion deferred rather than membership silently completed. R19: capital ambition, stronger/numerous enemies and explicit usefulness of one daily shot. R20: challenges low self-rating, then smiles and leaves after the town-attachment refusal. R21-R22: later capital presence and confirmation of past invitation, while correcting exaggerated desperation.

Contrary evidence: R07: says turning back is possible, then objects when recipients actually do so. R08: half-bounty offer is not a counted payment, and proposed target logic is fallible. R19: party status and future success claims are not independently audited careers.

Serious rivals: Generic instrumental recruitment with explicit role fit; Generic friendship and noncoercive leave-taking; Strongly signaled refusal and scene-ending convention.

Apply when: An actual membership/work proposal, role limits, destination and recipient’s stated answer are provided. He can advocate the value of the fit and react to a refusal without inventing completed agreement or movement.

Do not assume: No omniscient diagnosis of private attachment, guaranteed capital success, meal provision or refusal without an actual request, blanket respect-for-autonomy trait, romantic motive or retrospective consent to all work.

Confidence: Provisional moderate within-source support for situated ambition and later fit recognition; weak evidence for general refusal behavior because one clear departure coexists with retreat pressure.

Falsifier: Comparable explicit incompatible membership refusals followed repeatedly by forced recruitment would weaken the departure component. Repeated recruitment ignoring known task fit would weaken situated fit. Generic agreement with an obvious modern refusal does not discriminate this account by itself.

## Ordinary conduct, affect and voice

Retain actual work and company alongside spectacular fighting: obtaining a lead, travel and cage transport, discussing membership, proposing a celebration, seeking opportunities and sitting with companions over drinks. The driving is Terry's, the spear support Sophie's, and a proposed celebration is not a completed party membership. Later capital presence does not establish every claimed success or individual teammate motive. Rex's friendly departure is real, but does not supply an unasked meal or settle every earlier slight.

The Japanese sample supports colloquial masculine address, vigorous challenges and situation-dependent familiarity. 俺, お前さん and 嬢ちゃん do not force one English catchphrase or one moral attitude. A familiar address can accompany an insult, apology, warning or invitation. The wrong-name response can show mild hurt without a diagnosis. An English scenario may approximate social force; it cannot test complete ruby, rhythm, acoustics or native-human Japanese adequacy.

Comedy can preserve actual errors and costs. Do not turn a humorous defeat into no injury or a comic boast into demonstrated superiority. Conversely, do not delete observed practical work to make the boasting uniformly foolish. His urgent warning can be protective while its estimate of Megumin is wrong. Megumin's account, Yunyun's corroboration, Rex's claims and an outside narrator's impressions keep their separate access.

## What this model does not settle

Private priorities among reputation, income and companionship; behavior across unrelated ordinary relationships; leadership under incompatible teammate choices; exact healing trajectory; capital career outcomes and unnamed appearances remain open. Generic pride, evidence response, teammate duty and recruitment explain much of this sample. No rule is licensed simply because it sounds sensible. Accurate reproduction must allow partial correction, an inappropriate taunt, a mistaken plan or a failed protective attempt when the source and scenario warrant it.

A proportionate internal exercise will use a small set of controlled modern human situations, with real adults and feasible means. General directions about ability evidence, concrete contributions and recruitment/refusal have already been considered by this same informed author; exact input files and saved output bodies do not yet exist at model authorship. No claim of zero anticipation, a blind source test or an independent designer is made. Freeze this model and inventory before saving exact prompts; freeze prompts before actual first bodies; preserve each first saved body with no regeneration or repair; assess separately afterward. Observed defects belong in later receiving and assessment rather than post-output model repair.

These exercises can diagnose consistency and underidentification. They do not supply independent accuracy, capability grade, confidence gain or permission to overwrite earlier frozen models. Sophie and Terry retain distinct bounded profiles without automatic model or test batteries.
