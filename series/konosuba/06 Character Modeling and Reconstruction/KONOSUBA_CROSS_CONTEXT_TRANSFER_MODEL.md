## 2. The object being transferred

Transfer four linked structures: **valuations, appraisal tendencies, learned social scripts and domain-specific capacities whose premises remain available**. A visible source action is not itself an invariant. Its cause can include a spell, recoverable injury, an office, a particular relationship, an audience, a resource and a narrator's framing. Remove one of those premises and the same person can select another act without losing identity.

Conversely, changing physics does not authorize replacing the person's priorities with the analyst's preferred ethics or competence. Kazuma can remain attracted to ease, leverage, recognition and particular people; Aqua to esteem, immediate pleasures, meaningful usefulness and familiar attachment; Megumin to a chosen art and a particular place with others; Darkness to protection, duty, chosen belonging and some desired forms of exposure. Those statements are starting constraints, not sufficient action algorithms. Their competition, actual knowledge and current state select the response. [Four monographs; Humor§§2,10; Ensemble conclusion]

Do not infer that realism means low emotion, competent adults, generic niceness or suppression of comic failure. A realistic consequence can make a supported mistake more serious, less attractive after learning or costly to repair. A source-world success can become unavailable while the preference for that kind of achievement persists. The transfer is defensible when it explains the altered choice causally and preserves contrary dispositions, rather than merely reducing every reaction's volume.

## 6. A common causal procedure

**Step A — Recover premises.** Select the relevant source-backed disposition and chronological state. Identify material witnesses and counterexamples. Do not begin from the desired recognizable gag.

**Step B — Map changed conditions.** List retained, unavailable, changed and unknown capabilities/institutions. Distinguish an actual functional analogue from a metaphor. A prayer is not a medical license; a game analogy is not access to a computer system.

**Step C — Establish salience and appraisal.** Use what this person knows, including a plausible error if supported. Identify at least the material competing goal, rather than silently selecting one virtue or defect.

**Step D — Generate feasible branches.** Construct a small set of actions the person could actually perform: familiar choice, recalibrated analogue, inquiry, refusal, help from another, retreat or a known risky act. Eliminate physically impossible branches; do not eliminate inconvenient motives.

**Step E — Check inhibition, agency and aftermath.** Recognized costs, personal boundaries, another person's refusal, audience and recovery affect the choice. No universal seriousness brake or group consent is assumed. Separate action from success and release/recovery from initial execution.

**Step F — Select and express a bounded continuation.** State the predicted appraisal, dominant motive, action and addressee-conditioned voice. Offer a material alternative only where an identified uncertainty supports it. Label constructed speech as generated, not canonical text.

**Step G — Audit before explanation expands.** Compare with the chosen model rule/disconfirmation and supplied information. Record an adverse response or unsupported certainty. Do not add a new exception after seeing the result and call the original forecast confirmed.

The individual model owns the person's conditional mechanisms; the party model owns nonadditive interaction. This common procedure is not an excuse to fill every character's choices with the same prudent checklist. The analyst performs every check; the character can omit a check, and that omission must itself be evidence-compatible. [Kazuma W04/W13; Ensemble phases; Humor§1]

## 7. Three different kinds of portability

**Psychological portability** asks whether a supported goal, appraisal bias or relation mechanism can remain intelligible under the new conditions. **Mechanics portability** asks whether the actual capability/institution/consequence still exists. **Linguistic portability** asks what a particular joke or voice act needs from Japanese form, pragmatics or culture. They are not equivalent.

A source-present spell may be H1 literal, psychologically relevant and mechanically unavailable in a realistic setting. A literal complaint can rely on an L3 lexical hinge; another structurally similar complaint can transfer without that hinge. A familiar title may carry status pragmatics whose analogue requires a different form of address. H1–H4, E1–E7, inference confidence and L0–L3 retain their distinct questions. H3 is gag-contingent behavior, not a synonym for uncertainty; H4 is nonliteral license, not a synonym for magic. [Humor§8; Japanese§8]

## 14. Party transfer is nonadditive

Run the shared task through **recognition → availability → role selection/acceptance → resource provision → execution → recovery → consequences**. No member owns every phase automatically. Different actors may supply a local phase, an outsider can make the task possible, and an institution can permit self-selection without assigning each role. Familiarity may reduce negotiation, but actual refusal, ignorance or an unresolved safety objection remains causal. A new setting may remove the source specialties' complementarity. ## 16. Seriousness, moral pressure and competing models

Seriousness is an input to appraisal and priorities, not an omnipotent override. Each person has observed care, competence, real fear, particular commitments and also counterexamples to an attractive uniform rule. A comic self-description can accompany a serious practical act; a serious protective intention can encourage a dangerous collective choice.

Retain competing explanations where the main source cannot separate them. Concern can coexist with reward; a particular scene's restraint can reflect state, another person's intervention, resource limits or a chosen moral boundary. ## 20. Uncertainty, rival transfers and failure diagnosis

A transfer can fail because a premise was misclassified, an unknown fact became character knowledge, the ability analogue was unsupported, an institution's social meaning changed, an earlier state was replaced by a later one, a relation was assumed, a comic magnitude was mistaken for baseline, or the psychological mechanism itself was wrong. Keep these diagnoses separate. A source-world success is not proof that the same person would succeed elsewhere; a transfer failure need not erase the original source competence.

Different coherent outcomes can remain where the evidence underdetermines new learning or a particular conflict. State the precise unresolved variable and how each outcome follows. Do not use “anything could happen” as a model. Conversely, a novel prompt is not required to have a single correct answer for a consistency audit to detect impossible powers, wrong agency, unsupported certainty, caricature or voice mismatch.

Holdout fiction remains source-world evidence with its own chronology/viewpoint/register; even successful validation there cannot empirically establish cross-universe behavior. It can support or challenge the mechanisms proposed as portable. No claim here reaches VERY HIGH before validation, and no simulation percentage is described as predictive accuracy against real unseen canonical acts.
