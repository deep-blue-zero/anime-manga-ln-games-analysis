# Temiren: provisional conditional reconstruction

Status: initial scoped Tier 3 model for the Core and Advanced edited replays, interpreted in the [monograph](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_TEMIREN_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md) and bounded by the [cohort 31 index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_TRPG_REPLAY_COHORT31_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md). Source and broad contrast directions are known to the author. This version precedes exact hypothetical inputs and original first responses. Two differentiated rules are warranted; unstable sect labels are retained as state evidence rather than inflated into another general mechanism.

## Operating representation

Track desired recognition and from whom, what the other person actually asks or does, Temiren's explicit expectation and renewed request, pain/disappointment, task type, known tools and MP/costs, actual check results, available attention, religious claim versus mechanical choice, and practical obligations to the party. A prince label is not a relationship contract, intellectual competence is not reliable bodily defense, and a request is not proof of consent to a substituted act.

Generate local options before outcome: ask for attention, interpret optimistically, criticize, provoke a response, reassess a claim, identify a threat, use a finite tool, seek admiration, defend another's prudent choice or join a shared activity. Permit mixed and mistaken appraisals; do not force either a foolish admirer or an infallible specialist.

## TE-R01: desired recognition can preserve attachment through disappointed appraisals

**Proposition.** When recognition from a valued prince or companion is at stake, Temiren tends to keep seeking a response even after disappointment. She can reinterpret an unwelcome act, lower a stated expectation yet renew an invitation, or deliberately bother the other person for attention. Continuation is not proof that the earlier act was welcome.

**Supporting evidence.** The requested wall-pin is replaced with a slap, pain is stated and she reappraises it as encouragement (Core S030). At ADV S082 she explicitly says she wants to trouble Gilbert a little for attention after the professional comparison. S084 pairs no-expectations language with a renewed prince invitation and response to a promised hunt.

**Contrary evidence.** She criticizes Gilbert and states disappointment rather than simply endorsing him. No future hunt is performed, and no stable romance is established. She can also act competently or defend someone else's survival choice without serving the prince script.

**Rivals.** Comic romantic convention, player interest in a scene, generic attention-seeking, continuing party membership and an attempt to make the best of a bad exchange. The exact mix is not independently separated.

**Application conditions.** Specify the valued person, the request, actual response and current expectation. Expect a bid for recognition to remain live more often than a clean immediate loss of all interest, but allow criticism and a changed demand. A repeated no by another person remains a no; do not convert her renewed ask into their consent or her reinterpretation into the narrator's approval.

**Do not assume.** Settled romance, universal attraction, that pain proves affection, that a promise is fulfilled, an erotic preference beyond the specified request, or a diagnosis of dependence.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the recurring local attention/expectation pattern, lower for long-term rejection, different intimacies and behavior after an explicit durable boundary.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable disappointments from the same valued person producing stable disinterest without a new recognition bid would weaken the persistence tendency. Consistent accurate appraisal without optimistic reinterpretation or provocation would weaken those specific forms.

## TE-R02: task-specific reasoning and finite tools coexist with a wish to be admired

**Proposition.** In a task where Temiren has relevant knowledge or a usable spell/scroll, she is likely to make a practical contribution and may seek recognition for it. That competence does not generalize to all checks, safety or interpersonal judgment; cost questions and ordinary prudence can coexist with romantic distraction.

**Supporting evidence.** She asks about cohabitation/upkeep before the quest (Core S023), defends the farmer's survival decision (S049), identifies the Winter Hatamoto with 19 against 16 while wanting admiration (ADV S065), and successfully uses a Turn Undead scroll with 28 when Gilbert failed (S080-082). These are distinct contributions rather than a prince's compliment certifying expertise.

**Contrary evidence.** The large magical benefit carries MP cost. She takes 31 damage at S099 and fails the last evasion at S104. Successful identification does not guarantee correct social appraisal; a purchased scroll does not make her a universally better priest. Conditional elemental baths remain unperformed wishes after the jewels lose power.

**Rivals.** Build optimization, a generous GM, ordinary practical intelligence, player mechanics knowledge and a one-off lucky roll. Player expertise is not automatically PC foreknowledge. Several mechanisms can explain one successful check.

**Application conditions.** Specify task, information, tools, remaining costs and any resolution rule. Permit a useful question or attempt before the result; admiration can follow as a separate desire. In a domain shift without relevant knowledge, retain uncertainty or seek help. If no roll/result is stipulated, do not fabricate success. Practical caution may also appear outside magic.

**Do not assume.** Global stupidity, all-purpose genius, unlimited MP, mastery inferred from a class, permanent clerical superiority, immune defense, or a favorable check because she wants attention.

**Confidence.** Moderate for typed contributions across several tasks, lower for exact thresholds, unfamiliar work and separation of skill from game/player facilitation.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable relevant-knowledge/tool opportunities in which she supplies no practical reasoning would weaken the contribution tendency. Repeated dependable success across unrelated unprepared tasks would challenge the domain-limit hypothesis, but only if the evidence separates rules, help and luck.
