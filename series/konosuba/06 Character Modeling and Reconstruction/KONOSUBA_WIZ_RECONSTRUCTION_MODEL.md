## Goal and attention structure

Treat her priorities as partly ordered. A concrete threat to a valued companion or a recognized civilian boundary can recruit costly action. Ordinary money is not the only reward: wanted company and a useful-looking object can outweigh a financially superior option. Exact ranking under irreversible unfamiliar tradeoffs remains unresolved.

Attention is often captured by the benefit she imagines: a person protected, a companion recovered, a technically valuable item, a customer helped or a shared future maintained. That focus can support substantial effort and miss the surrounding operating system. It is not a global absence of intelligence.

Aversive states include harm to people she treats as uninvolved, distress of companions, a threatening loss of company, fading/depletion and particular personal insults. Warmth and politeness can coexist with firm refusal, irritation or actual dangerous force. Do not reduce her errors to innocence or her capacity to a surprise personality replacement.

## Competence, judgment and learning

Known capacities include high-level combat magic, freezing, Explosion, Light of Saber, Sleep, Teleport and Drain Touch-related resource handling where the particular source establishes them. Target ontology, available mana, compatibility, safety and support matter. No known spell grants mastery of an unobserved craft or the power to ignore a different body type.

Separate four failure types:

1. A tool works, but its target or combined plan was misunderstood.
2. A technically useful product is badly fitted to actual customers or operating costs.
3. A social situation is misunderstood, so an initially useful act has a different motive than observers infer.
4. A real limit or objection is understood but another person refuses to change their action.

The fourth is not automatically Wiz's own incompetence. MAIN04's warning and withdrawal advice are clear even when Aqua refuses. Local explanation can change an act, while repeated losses show that no general perfect accounting habit has been established.

## Operational rules

### WZ-D01 — Civilian harm can end conditional noninterference

**Proposition.** When credible information establishes that a former colleague has killed a human outside the combat roles covered by her stated neutrality condition, Wiz can switch from negotiation to controlled accusation and force.

**Evidence.** MAIN04 C5 P270–284 establishes the arrangement and attempted negotiation. P408–432 contains Hans's admission, actual freezing, her distinction between combatants and the uninvolved manager, and sadness. The admission is not a separately witnessed killing.

**Apply when** the act, actor and protected role are actually established at that phase. **Do not assume** every allegation is true, every person has the same role, or all combat harm triggers the same response. Her statement permits lethal risk within adventuring/knightly combat; it is not universal pacifism or unrestricted loyalty to the Demon Army.

**Rival/limit.** Friendship with the threatened party, the town's needs and Hans's continuing threat overlap with the explicit condition. One central confrontation cannot establish every generalization or the proportionality of all future force.

**Confidence/expectation.** MODERATE-HIGH within the stated role condition, MODERATE for transfer. In a new comparable admitted civilian killing by a known neutral colleague, expect actual opposition or a demand for accountability rather than treating the prior arrangement as overriding everything. Comparable continued neutrality after a clear admission would weaken the rule. A known combatant casualty alone is not a matched test.

### WZ-D02 — Legible need recruits help, but method can change at a limit

**Proposition.** When valued people face concrete harm and she sees a contribution, Wiz tends to offer effort or bear a personal cost. If her route is blocked or would not work, she can warn, urge retreat or recruit another provider rather than promise unlimited help.

**Evidence.** MASK U021 P379–492 gives costly action and a soul-price offer under companion danger, with separate refusal and alternative. U019 P203–216,514–524 moves from understanding extortion to asking aid, failed mediation and fetching Kazuma. MAIN04 C5 P499–505,522–525,565–588 includes warning, actual pipe cutting, insufficient mana and withdrawal advice.

**Apply when** she understands enough of the need and a contribution is represented. **Do not assume** perfect early knowledge, guaranteed rescue, that all strangers receive the same sacrifice, or permission to spend another person's resources. A request is not provider acceptance.

**Rival/limit.** Attachment, remembered obligation, fear and duty can overlap. Her willingness to offer a severe price in one crisis does not fix an irreversible all-person hierarchy.

**Confidence/expectation.** MODERATE-HIGH for familiar need and differentiated help; LOW for unlimited sacrifice. A new blocked mediation should leave asking a known appropriate helper, stating a limit or proposing retreat available. Sustained indifference to clearly understood comparable companion danger with feasible aid would weaken the rule. Failure to accomplish an impossible cure does not.

**Rival/limit.** Desperation and incomplete information can produce some failures without a stable planning deficit. Familiar-constraint explanation is better supported than a tendency always to investigate novel ontology before acting.

In a new task beyond the stipulated remaining supply, expect a limit, a narrower effect or a consent-dependent resource request.
