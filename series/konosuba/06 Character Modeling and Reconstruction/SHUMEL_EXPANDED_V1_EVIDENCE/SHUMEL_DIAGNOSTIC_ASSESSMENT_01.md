## A1: a personal threat and a spell that has not yet hit

The original responds to the forbidden given name with a closed fan, an address warning, a challenge to the clerk's familiarity and attention to the listening guests. It raises a hand, begins a curse and gathers dark light toward the clerk. It then declares the inspection over. The public-name boundary has behavioral force in this generated turn; it is not treated as harmless teasing.

The stronger source mechanism is only partly probed. This short continuation stops at spell preparation. It does not show a completed bolt, a hit, injury, a legal sanction or the tactical cost that mattered in the actual raid. Calling it another demonstrated costly attack would overstate the body. The declaration that the inspection is over is Shumel's assertion; the clerk's acceptance and her authority to terminate the agreed ledger review are not established. Its potential overreach should remain visible rather than being repaired into a perfectly lawful response.

The dark-light description, pacing, gestures and English menace are generated details. They are compatible with supplied magical capacity but do not recover an observed Japanese line or acoustic performance. The output does not make a guard obey, reveal contraband, manufacture a court judgment or complete a killing. Those limits matter, but avoiding an input violation is not the same as discriminating the proposed personality mechanism.

## A2: resistance survives respectful address

The control original keeps sarcasm and refuses the additional vault demand while making the agreed ledger available. It asks the clerk to specify an error and record the disputed demand accurately. Respect does not produce trust, a confession or immediate surrender of the drawer. This retains the important boundary that removal of the name trigger does not settle the independent conflict.

The response is also unusually tidy. Its procedural argument closely follows the prompt's explicit division between ledger authority and unestablished vault access. The source woman was capable of legal framing but also attacked, lost leverage and required restraint. This single polished continuation cannot establish that respectful treatment reliably produces strategic self-control, nor should its smoothness become a new rule. The available facts made a sensible procedural reply easy for many generic speakers.

## Disposition after feedback

The pair provides a limited demonstration that this frozen generator can express a different immediate emphasis under the two supplied addresses. It does not establish the full costly-escalation mechanism, causal identification, source accuracy, mature transfer, Japanese adequacy, a grade or a confidence increase. The new situations and exact English are authored hypotheses. Neither is evidence about a newly encountered actual clerk.

SHU-R01 remains `active_provisional` with the same serious rank, raid, exposure and generic-insult rivals and the same concentrated source basis. No original, rule, model or exact input is rewritten after this feedback. The limitations are recorded in the annotations and subsequent receiving, not repaired by substituting a more violent A1 or a less polished A2.
