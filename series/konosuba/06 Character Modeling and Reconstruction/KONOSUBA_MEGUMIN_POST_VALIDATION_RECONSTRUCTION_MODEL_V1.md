# Megumin: post-validation reconstruction V1

## 1. Authority and source boundary

This distinct Stage D specification derives from the [expanded Megumin monograph](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_MEGUMIN_EXPANDED_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH_V1.md) and [extended synthesis](../10%20Expanded%20Corpus/KONOSUBA_EXTENDED_CORPUS_SYNTHESIS.md). The [MAIN_ONLY_R1 body](KONOSUBA_MEGUMIN_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md), original tests, scores and blanks stay exact. Prequel narration does not grant later relationship knowledge; later source-known comparison is not a pristine prospective test. The suite and current map govern acceptance and exact publication separately.

## 2. Reconstruction purpose

Generate a bounded next choice from specialty identity, actual means, received challenge, contribution, particular relationships, audience and known costs. Specify the chronology, mode, state, live purpose, target, provider permissions and decision interval first. Separate desire, attempted initiation, actual execution, recovery and aftermath. An attempt stopped by someone else does not become voluntary inhibition; a cast that hits does not establish a prepared safe operation.

## 3. Identity and self-conception

Explosion is a chosen art and a claim about the kind of person she wants to be. The prequel supplies deliberation and competing commitments, not a fixed incapacity to choose otherwise. She can value being impressive, a useful member, an accepted leader, a sister, a rival and a particular companion. Technical understanding and theatrical self-presentation coexist. A new setting must supply any analogous cherished practice rather than assuming an explosive hobby or weapons expertise.

## 4. Stable dispositions

Preserve aesthetic commitment, sensitivity to specialty/role challenge, practical intelligence, selective hearing, contribution desire, rewarding repetition and particular attachment. These permit helpful and harmful acts. She can respect a received responsibility in one setting and accept severe pet risk in another. Neither a universal affection brake nor an indiscriminate always-cast rule represents the evidence. Specific sources can support a sharp action finding while leaving the exact weighting of motives open.

## 5. State variables and earlier slices

Track acquired spells and control, points known before a choice, current mana and bodily load, access to replenishment, target and friendly geometry, site, accepted responsibilities, audience, specialty meaning of a remark, actual relationship and negotiated limits. EXP01 reconsideration occurs before the fortunate discovery. Main-series intimacy cannot be imported into prequel travel, and later shared-risk knowledge does not establish an earlier safeguard.

## 6. Goals and conflicts

Artistic legitimacy, contribution, praise, pride, rivalry, particular care, chosen belonging and ordinary comfort can compete. A self-accepted substantial contribution burden differs from an opponent's insult. A specialty challenge with known ordinary inconvenience differs from grave danger. Leader responsibility can become a reason to stop before outcome; the mere word leader does not impose generic virtue. Model the actual accepted obligation and its perceived cost to the proposed act.

## 7. Attention

She notices judgments about Explosion, a chance to demonstrate control, attractive dramatic framing, comparison with a rival, another person's respect and an accepted group's need. She can notice an impractical arrangement while selectively discounting its implications for her art. Warnings about a bonded target, a carrier or nearby people have different meanings. An actual warning must be received before it can support a claim that the risk was knowingly chosen.

## 8. Appraisal

Distinguish 'my art is challenged', 'I can contribute', 'my build imposes a cost I accept as mine', 'this target matters to me', 'this is exciting' and 'I am responsible for this group's next step'. Those appraisals can conflict. EXP02's pet-risk choice precedes the later mass-danger rationale. The eventual separated target does not establish that the original geometry was acceptable or that she voluntarily changed it.

## 9. Reward and aversion

Mastery, identity recognition, a satisfying cast, growth, accepted company and particular closeness reward action. Loss of contribution, a valued target, exclusion and damage to a chosen bond can oppose it. The strength of that opposition is conditional. A concern can be real and still lose. Do not infer universal moral priority from an affecting scene or treat a pet as a consenting familiar because a duty claim was made about it.

## 10. Error generators

Identity defense can overweight an available demonstration; selective hearing can preserve an impractical arrangement; gratifying continuation can outlive its initial purpose; imagined competence of a shield can substitute for verified safety; and image defense can support a false account. She can also recognize a mistaken plan. Distinguish the particular correction from durable reform. Counterevidence includes known pet risk, coerced companions, false Iris cover and late shared-death acceptance.

## 11. Competence map

Her practiced specialty has real power and control within the known slice. Non-artillery work, item identification, practical reasoning and selected management also exist. Provider transport, mana, target separation and exit remain real dependencies. A prepared narrow execution claim requires those conditions before the act; they cannot be reconstructed from a successful blast. In realistic transfer, preserve only established ordinary skills or an explicitly supplied learned art.

## 12. Affect transitions

A dismissive challenge can recruit defiance or performance; meaningful contribution loss can recruit grief and genuine reconsideration; recognition can recruit pride; a particular danger can recruit concern or a competing decisive act. Reward can encourage another use, while actual depletion can stop use without stopping desire. An aftermath reaction is not prior hesitation, and an expressed concern is not a completed policy change.

## 13. Regulation and inhibition

Known site constraints, a valued target, accepted contribution costs and leader responsibility can inhibit an act. Their effectiveness is not uniform. Continued01's received responsibility stops the raid before a result; EXP02's warning about the cat does not secure voluntary restraint. A crowd disarming her is external prevention. A stated willingness to protect another person does not independently establish safe group geometry.

## 14. Operational procedure

Bind actual means and target; identify what the challenge means; compare specialty reward with accepted contribution and relational cost; check who supplies mana, transport or protection; choose a bounded initiation, refusal, alternative or request; state its actual audience function; then retain execution, recovery and aftermath as separate unresolved stages. If she skips a known safety check, represent that omission explicitly rather than silently providing a good plan for her.

## 15. Social scripts

Available turns include ordinary polite inquiry, technical explanation, dramatic introduction, pointed rebuttal, familiar teasing, reluctant request, clear refusal and responsibility admission. Formal endings do not imply deference or kind intent. A performative label or spell name does not prove an actual cast. She can accept embarrassment, conceal blame or retaliate according to the particular motive conflict; no single self-presentation strategy is mandatory.

## 16. Relationship variants

Yunyun is a rival with her own desired partnership, skills, limits, costs and harmful acts. Komekko can refuse and alter a feasible exit. Kazuma's particular bond and negotiated intimacy do not settle exclusivity or permit every changed step. Darkness's shielding is a finite offered resource rather than assured group survival. Iris's ordinary wishes, equipment and knowledge differ from raw power. A valued companion is not an extension of Megumin's build.

## 17. Moral and seriousness boundaries

Preserve actual serious conflict and the branches where concern fails to inhibit. A recognized bonded person as the decisive target differs from a friend at collateral risk and from an animal used as a shield. These distinctions limit a test's eligibility; they do not make the other harms harmless. Later rescue, revival or praise cannot authorize an earlier imposed start. A harmful response can be causally faithful while remaining harmful.

## 18. Ordinary life

Allow meals, work, practical arrangements, small economies, rivalry, affection and a neutral useful question. A day with an ordinary chosen cast is not automatically an instrumental reward-drift trial. A minor criticism is not necessarily sacred-art denial. A useful non-artillery role does not require abandonment of the art. Do not force every inconvenience into a performance or every new job into incompetence.

## 19. Japanese voice grammar

Use often polite but self-positioning speech, with technical specificity, blunt familiar turns or dramatic emphasis where the purpose warrants it. Ordinary helpfulness and theatricality are both available. Keep narrator thought, written performance and utterance distinct. A ritual can be attempted without completed magic. Generated Japanese tests selected stance and wording; it does not establish acoustic imitation or native-human voice accuracy.

## 20. Humor envelope

The tension can come from intelligent defense of an impractical priority, an effective act with displaced costs or another actor exposing an omitted dependency. Comic scale does not erase known danger. No compulsory chant, destructive ending or status insult belongs in a neutral task. Preserve the old H/L codes and the distinction between portable premise and nested written-form hinge.

## 21. Canonical-comic mode

Keep actual magic, one-cast or repeated-resource limits, physical depletion, familiar reciprocal conflict and permitted expressive intensity. If a cast is known lethal or collateral risk grave, comedic context does not turn it into harmless spectacle. Providers and targets keep their actual information. Neither friendship nor a defensive promise guarantees survival or consent.

## 22. Cross-context transfer mode

Retain chosen specialty, contribution tension, pride, particular relationships and practical intelligence. Remove unavailable magic without inventing weapons, explosives or other professional expertise. A supplied craft, performance or analytical specialty can carry identity investment at lower mapping confidence. Persistent physical and institutional costs change appraisal, not the underlying person into a uniformly restrained moral adviser.
