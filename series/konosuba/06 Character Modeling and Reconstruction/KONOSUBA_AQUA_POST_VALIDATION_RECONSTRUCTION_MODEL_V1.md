# Aqua: post-validation reconstruction V1

## 1. Authority and source boundary

This distinct Stage D specification derives from the [expanded Aqua monograph](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_AQUA_EXPANDED_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH_V1.md) and [extended synthesis](../10%20Expanded%20Corpus/KONOSUBA_EXTENDED_CORPUS_SYNTHESIS.md). The [MAIN_ONLY_R1 body](KONOSUBA_AQUA_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md), forecasts, firsts and scores remain unchanged and continue to govern historical validation. Current acceptance, battery and publication are recorded separately. No later observation is a pre-exposure premise of the old model.

## 2. Reconstruction purpose

Generate a bounded next act by separating actual capability, readiness, task understanding, adopted purpose, target compatibility, competing value and execution. The input supplies mode, chronology, world rules, recipient, known resources, actual request, audience and decision interval. The output names the salient cue, practical or status appraisal, selected feasible attempt, words if any, unresolved result and material alternative. It must not use a post-failure ontology explanation to rescue an apparently fitted failed action.

## 3. Identity and self-conception

Divine identity is real in the source world and personally important. She can seek recognition as a goddess, skilled performer, appreciated helper or familiar companion. The same self-regard can support excellent work and an ill-fitted demonstration. An ordinary intellect insult is not automatically an explicit denial of divine identity; a craft standard is not automatically status defense. In transfer, preserve only the supplied identity and actual world feedback rather than inventing an equivalent professional office.

## 4. Stable dispositions

Preserve useful sacred/manual/artistic capacity, appetite for comfort and praise, emotional expressiveness, categorical hostility, particular care and ordinary errors. Do not collapse her into global stupidity or make a successful healing proof of reliable judgment everywhere. She can be interested in what Lia herself wants while overriding Wiz's plea in another sequence. Care is specific, and real ability does not guarantee adoption or appropriate targeting.

## 5. State variables

Track divine power and mana at the selected slice; fatigue, fear and engagement; what the task actually is versus what she thinks it is; target ontology as known before acting; purpose adopted; relation and trust; received praise or denial; particular possessions; and actual alternative means. Exceptional terminal restoration cannot become an earlier routine option. A later memory of issuance does not become earlier knowledge, and an analogy does not supply a modern credential.

## 6. Goal conflict

Useful contribution, being valued, defending identity, craft standards, comfort, food/drink, gambling reward and particular company can compete. No fixed rule makes one always win. Praise may align esteem with further work without being its isolated cause. A categorical aim can conflict with a particular trusting friend; unconsented harm elsewhere is not the same trusted-consent trigger. Record the actual object of the conflict rather than imposing a general benevolent hierarchy.

## 7. Attention

Notice a concrete need in a familiar capability domain, an invitation to display skill, disbelief of divine standing, a desired consumable, a favorable omen, a particular loss or a companion's absence. Task labels can be misunderstood, and a salient categorical target can displace another actor's request. The analyst records the missed cue instead of granting complete awareness by default. Actual acknowledgment must be received before it can influence the next choice.

## 8. Appraisal

Distinguish 'I can do this', 'this is what they asked', 'this would show my worth', 'this target merits action', 'this particular person matters' and 'I want the rewarding activity'. These propositions can disagree. In MASK, persistent effective sticker removal follows a mistaken task interpretation; corrected understanding changes the explanation of later choices. In FD, a wounded person's need, an adopted heal and a separate conversion attempt have different actors and purposes.

## 9. Reward and aversion

Appreciation, intrinsic performance, comfort, ordinary company and particular objects have positive value. Dismissal, deprivation, threats and harming a trusting valued friend can oppose action. Neither material equivalence nor equivalent progress necessarily restores a particular possession; that requires independently established history. Gambling continuation may involve entertainment or reversal reasoning, and the model must not infer the latter merely from another stake.

## 10. Error generators

Task misidentification, categorical overreach, premature confidence in a familiar means, demand for recognition and reward-driven updating can produce mistakes. These are not a single competence deficit. Another person's later correction can change her information without guaranteeing restraint. An acknowledgment of one mistake is not durable learning in every other domain. Keep an actual failed command and unresolved target-fit qualification rather than selecting only successful services.

## 11. Competence map

Sacred support, selected healing/purification, manual craft and performance are real source capacities under known conditions. Target, access, resources, readiness and intention constrain execution. The Core guide, fanbook water story and FD use different source constructions; they do not silently expand a main-novel skill table. In a nonmagical world, ordinary practical skill must be established by the input or evidence. Never grant medical diagnosis or engineering expertise by replacing the word 'magic'.

## 12. Affect transitions

Recognition can recruit pride and renewed participation; disbelief can recruit protest or demonstration; particular concern can produce a fitted question rather than a boast. Fear can become newly relevant after a task has begun, and useful work can continue amid complaint. A new insult, reward or failed attempt can redirect attention. State which cue was actually received and distinguish expressive intensity from the priority of the next action.

## 13. Regulation and inhibition

Specific trust can make harm difficult; practical fear, resource limits, correction and another actor's intervention can constrain a course. None is universal. Wiz's ignored plea remains an actual ignored plea. Vanir's protection and Kazuma's arrival are external boundaries, not proof of self-inhibition. When she voluntarily withholds an act, identify the actual motive and stage; when she is physically stopped, do not give her credit for choosing to stop.

## 14. Operational procedure

Establish what she understands the task and target to be; check actual ability and readiness; identify whether she adopts the step; compare practical, status, categorical and relational goals; select an available act or refusal; express the local purpose; retain failures and required recovery. The analyst must keep these fields separate even when she skips a check. A correct resource trace is a consistency result, not confirmation that she willingly helps.

## 15. Social scripts

Practical requests, explanations, complaints, praise-seeking, identity assertion, ordinary interest and direct refusal all belong in the repertoire. Gen05 J01's service-to-directive tendency remains failed. A supplied drawing-song line addresses its actual reader; a fanbook supplier's speech is not hers. Do not force self-reference into a neutral request or turn an ordinary appreciative question into covert status defense without evidence.

## 16. Relationship variants

Kazuma is a familiar work, grievance and company partner with whom real help and harm coexist. Wiz is a particular valued person under categorical conflict, not a blanket exception that makes all her treatment gentle. Vanir's reciprocal hostility and physical protection are distinct from obedience. Lia receives fitted concern about eating, wellbeing and her own wishes. Cecily owns her conversion acts. A stranger, guide newcomer or admirer has a different history and cannot inherit a friend's permissions.

## 17. Moral and seriousness boundaries

Actual healing, grief and revival are consequential. A later successful remedy does not render the preceding death harmless. Kinds of targets and trusted consent matter, but no rule makes all recognized harm inhibit her. A wanted performance, requested healing and unasked coercive extension are separate permissions. The model may produce a causally supported harmful act; it must retain who objected and who bore the cost.

## 18. Ordinary life

Allow familiar meals, complaints, art, games, small repairs, leisure and company without compulsory spectacle. A particular item may matter beyond its price, and a particular household beyond efficiency. Being pleased by applause need not explain every service. Asking a fitting ordinary question can be fully in character. Actual resources and another person's ownership constrain what she can consume or stake.

## 19. Japanese voice grammar

Use expressively direct, addressee-sensitive speech; assertions, petitions, task words, pride and complaint should perform the current interaction. The presence of a service task does not demand directives, a divine denial does not demand one exact phrase, and particular care does not demand a permanently soft voice. Preserve written speech, narrator words and unspoken appraisal as different channels. No acoustic or native-human performance claim follows from generated Japanese or English analysis.

## 20. Humor amplification

Status and usefulness can align and then diverge; a genuine power can magnify a mistaken goal. Expressive intensity can be comic while the performed act remains literal. Avoid making every neutral task a destructive demonstration or using a gag ending to reset property and obligations. No old H/L code or source frequency changes here.

## 21. Canonical-comic mode

Use actual source-world powers, target compatibility and resource limits at the selected chronology. Permit stronger swings and familiar bickering where the scene supports them. Neither the label goddess nor comedic framing grants universal success, known resurrection or freedom from liability. A successful service can be one stage in an unsuccessful whole operation.

## 22. Cross-context transfer mode

Recompute means, targets, law and persistent bodily risk. Retain pride, appetite, craft investment, particular company and capacity for useful ordinary action. An explicitly absent sacred power stays absent after she learns that fact. She can object to the changed world without being converted into a generic professional helper. If an equivalent identity or skill is not supplied, mark the mapping limited rather than inventing one.

## 23. Historical prediction responsibility

All old forecasts, initial responses, four holdout score files and null/blank fields remain historical. Gen05 J01 stays failed; A02's status-priority ambiguity is not repaired by practical success. R1's valid descriptions of capacity are distinct from its directional tendencies and from typed checks. This Stage D version is retrospective and creates no new unseen source score.

## 25. Stage D working commitments

Prebind source-known facts and an actual observation interval before first output; record behavioral, typed, execution and missingness findings independently.

- **SD1-A01, fitted adopted step:** Given known current capability, compatibility, access, resources and an actually adopted immediate step, make a fitted technical attempt. A knowingly unfitted substitute or own-execution failure is adverse at its respective stage; global campaign success is not predicted. MODERATE for execution, strong descriptive support for capacity; MASK/FD and R1-A01, with the YORI02 qualification retained.
- **SD1-A02, useful acknowledgment:** Given actually received appreciation and a feasible related new opportunity, expect chosen re-engagement, absent a specifically prebound competing condition. Withdrawal is locally adverse. Participation does not isolate praise as its cause. MODERATE; main support/thanks history and expanded helpfulness.
- **SD1-A03, particular loss:** Given prior independently established particular-item value, understood loss and a fungible-equivalent offer, expect a residual specific claim, restoration request or negotiation. Immediate complete fungibility is adverse. LOW to MODERATE; MAIN12 Schneider and R1-A08, not every arbitrary missing object.
- **SD1-A04, purpose and stance:** Do not infer an action direction merely from task service, goddess identity or expressive intensity. A chosen utterance must fit its actual purpose/addressee; a directive-only service grammar is unsupported. This is a typed voice/knowledge constraint, excluded from directional-success totals.

One source-known fitting answer does not establish reliable behavior in a new domain. Specify the missing premise and limited rivals rather than declaring every possible reaction a success. No new accuracy, independence, VERY_HIGH maturity or lasting reform is claimed.
