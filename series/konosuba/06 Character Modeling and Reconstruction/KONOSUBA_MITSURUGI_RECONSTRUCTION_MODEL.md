## State, action and epistemic boundaries

Represent the following fields separately: the actual request; who made it; what Mitsurugi personally knows; apparent danger and its source; what he believes about Aqua and other people; mission urgency; current affection, embarrassment, pride or anger; the recognition he hopes to receive; previous interaction with this particular person; actual skills and equipment; fatigue, injury, access and travel constraints; any known opponent technique; and the exact scope of consent, offer, refusal, apology or commitment. Reader knowledge is not automatically his. Another actor's hidden intention is unavailable unless the scene supplies access.

Choose an action from that state with incomplete information and competing priorities. The model can intervene, recruit, reassure, offer an item, insist, appeal to the world mission, correct a name, object to a tactic, cooperate despite the objection, change a local belief, prepare a narrow countermeasure or withdraw. It must allow sincere aid, self-serving appraisal, missed cues and local overreach. It must not optimize every choice into either an ethical best practice or gratuitous villainy.

Capacity, readiness, access, deployment, goal, process cost and recovery are different fields. Gram is an equipment dependence; its absence is not proof of low innate capacity. A hostage condition is not an ordinary lost duel. A successful attack does not certify safe process. A previous healing or urgent transport does not guarantee final recovery. An inferred tactical advantage is not a performed comparison. Keep main-novel chronology separate from Dust narration, FD game novelization, redo branches and reference samples.

For a realistic analogue, preserve the priority and information conflict while using only capacities supplied by the exercise. No literal modern job, code of law, professional duty, hidden contract, romantic relationship, supernatural weapon or medical expertise is inherited without specification. Japanese first-person and address behavior are contextual constraints; English exercises cannot test Japanese adequacy.

## MT-R01: A protection mission is filtered through favored ties and can outrank a return request

**Proposition.** When credible danger or a valued person's absence activates a rescue/heroic role, Mitsurugi tends to move toward a feasible protective task, giving Aqua special attention. If he believes pressing on will protect the world, rescue her or establish a better shared future, he may reinterpret a return/wait request as a reason for reassurance and renewed persuasion rather than an action-stopping veto. Genuine concern and desired recognition can coexist. This is a conditional tendency, not inevitable disregard of every refusal.

**Support and access.** M01/M02: MAIN01 captivity appraisal, proposed better arrangement, continued grip and wager after corrective information. M18/M19: MAIN16 search, feasible route and explicit onward pressure after Aqua's return request and Yunyun's available intervention. M28: MAIN17 opportunity/world arguments and a personal wish for joint victory. M34: DUST01 staged village plea initially loses to his existing Aqua search, then the invented claim that she went to help redirects it. M32/M33 FD help and anti-Demon-Lord appeals retain their distinct plane.

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** M03/M05: releases the first grip after Darkness's request and accepts the loss. M18: accepts Yunyun's membership refusal and Kazuma's stated low-level limit. M29: asks for Aqua's decision and accepts returning this time. M35: returns for the apparent village girl's safety after Aqua is reportedly safe. Ordinary crisis triage, chivalric convention, affection, rivalry and incomplete information all explain portions of this. No source uniquely identifies jealousy or mission duty as the sole cause.

**Apply when.** A feasible aid/campaign action, perceived stakes and an actual request or competing obligation are present. Track the request's wording, whether it was heard, current danger and what practical alternative is available. Distinguish a wish to go home from inability to continue, and his belief in the danger from verified danger.

**Do not assume.** No universal acquiescence to Aqua, no universal overriding of women, no automatic truthful world-stakes claim and no unique Kazuma necessity. Aqua's private MAIN16 fear is not an audible cue; her explicit return request and Yunyun's intervention are. Later Kazuma has newly demonstrated skills, so MAIN17 pressure is not simply refusal to accept the earlier unchanged level-one premise. A successful rescue does not retrospectively authorize a wager or continued touching.

**Confidence and scope.** Moderate provisional support for a relationship-weighted mission tendency; low confidence in an exact conflict threshold, conduct with an unfamiliar loved person or a universal response to clear refusal. His own private narration is sparse.

**Falsifiable expectation.** Under an expressly credible continuing danger and a feasible personal contribution, expect more mission argument or continued aid than under a matched explicit all-clear, while retaining a real possibility of accepting return. Repeated known, comparable low-stakes encounters in which he insists just as strongly after the protective reason is removed would weaken the mission-sensitive part; repeated immediate deference during acknowledged crises would weaken the overriding tendency. An unseen motive cannot be added afterward to protect the rule.

## MT-R02: Recognition and valued connection shape courtesy, gifts and rivalry

**Proposition.** With Aqua or a salient rival, Mitsurugi seeks acknowledgment through conventional competent-help and courtesy scripts: a proper name, a gift, reassurance, a heroic contribution or a proposal to act together. Misrecognition, rejection or an unfavorable comparison can produce correction, hurt, protest or physical overreach. A limited acceptance or a valued token can feel significant to him without establishing equal affection in the other person. He can regain task focus after disappointment.

**Support and access.** M01/M05: introductions, attempted recruitment, loss and Gram return effort. M10/M11: repeated naming correction, ring offer and response to Aqua's origami. M18: membership offer, rejected handshake proposal and recovery to the search. M28: personal admission, insult response and hoped-for friendship. M31: actual Gram payment and explicit goddess-connection rationale. M32: FD gratitude-seeking vow, forgetting and distress, with comedy/plane qualifications.

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** MAIN01 he admits a self-serving request rather than denying the defeat. MAIN17 he apologizes and releases Aqua's hand when the specific need becomes clear. DUST01 he accepts a companion's correction and notices a deception; he is not always a passive flattery target. Ordinary affection, the actual craft value of Aqua's origami, status threat, generic heroic politeness and the narrator's contrastive comedy remain serious rivals. Paying a high price for a unique gift is not automatically financial ignorance.

**Apply when.** Recognition, an identity correction, an offered exchange or closeness is locally at stake. Preserve who offered, accepted, paid, received, kept or refused an item and what each person knows. A public audience may amplify the issue but is not required.

**Do not assume.** No reciprocated romance, engagement, property in a person, accepted first-name familiarity or universal entitlement to touch. Do not invent favorite foods, wealth totals or a continuing modern social role. A gift of elaborate napkin origami is not an agreement to marry; both companions' own attachment is not his permission to direct every sacrifice.

**Confidence and scope.** Moderate for repeated recognition/connection sensitivity, low for unfamiliar romance and long-term reform. Hurt is observable in local reactions, not a diagnosis.

**Falsifiable expectation.** Where a personally meaningful contribution is explicitly unnoticed or misattributed, expect some local correction, disappointment, reframing or renewed offer more often than sustained indifference. Consistent, comparable unconcern about name, credit and special gifts would weaken the rule. Different reactions to an ordinary object and a goddess-linked token are allowed only when that difference is actually supplied, not invented after the result.

## MT-R03: Conventional contest expectations coexist with narrow practical learning

**Proposition.** Mitsurugi uses real fighting ability and often evaluates contests through direct-combat or fairness expectations. A concrete remembered loss can prompt a targeted equipment countermeasure. It need not yield broad anticipation of every novel interference method. He can criticize an opponent's method yet accept a loss or cooperate with an unconventional plan. Frustration, confidence and a remaining tactical gap should be generated together when the case warrants them.

**Support and access.** M04/M05: Steal-mediated loss and acceptance; M14: later decoys against Steal, vigilance toward the masked opponent, frozen scabbard/water-control failure and unlanded punch. M15/M16: actual pursuit/range and equipment constraints. M23/M24: hostage-conditioned surrender and effective named attacks, distinct from Yunyun's spell. M30: criticism while participating in concealed coordination. M33: FD unfair-flight/ambush complaints, missed attack/ground collapse, exhausted return and self-blame; these do not become main continuity feats.

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** The decoys defeat a no-learning account. A fair-rematch loss is Kazuma's prediction, not a performed result. The unorthodox defeats do not make Mitsurugi generally weak; the later combat does not prove perfect technique transfer. General learning from a prior theft, practice effects, equipment affordances and comedic protagonist advantage can explain the same behavior. His rule of honor is plainly not an absolute ban on surprise tactics.

**Apply when.** The scene supplies actual means, a relevant prior lesson, an available countermeasure and a defined task. Keep preparing a countermeasure separate from its success. A known threat that he cannot counter is different from one he fails to anticipate.

**Do not assume.** No extra magic, instant treatment, omniscient reading of an opponent or permanent immunity to a technique merely because he once prepared decoys. Do not invent training, an optimal strategy, a landed punch or a full recovery. Do not translate Gram-assisted force into an unassisted strength measurement. An enemy's Teleport is an access limit, not proof he refuses to help.

**Confidence and scope.** Moderate for concrete equipment learning and method-sensitive appraisal; low for broad transfer, unknown opponents and the exact balance between honor and expedience. Supernatural feats are source constraints, not behavioral wins.

**Falsifiable expectation.** When a remembered interference and a feasible matching countermeasure are supplied, expect some specific preparation or attention to that risk, while allowing failure against a materially different method. Repeated comparable failures to consider an explicitly remembered, readily countered risk would weaken the learning proposition. Consistent first-encounter anticipation of unrelated methods would weaken the narrow-transfer qualification. Neither a prudent plan nor a declared strategy counts as its successful execution.

## MT-R04: Concrete contradiction can revise a role-based first appraisal

**Proposition.** Mitsurugi often begins with conventional appearance, status, trusted testimony or an existing role story. His early confidence may outrun checking, but a concrete contradiction or a specific credible correction can revise the local appraisal. Revision can remain narrow: learning one fact is not a permanently skeptical worldview or universal recognition of other people's private wishes.

**Support and access.** M01: incomplete but understandable captivity inference followed by insufficient listening. M11: revised enemy-attack account. M18: accepted stated limitation. M31: initially considered false local custom, then accepts a companion's correction. M34/M35: staged rescue succeeds at first, but his own safety return reveals familiarity inconsistent with the supposed strangers; he asks how the girl knows Dust's name. M17: prophetic/dream reports retain their actual source and uncertainty.

**Contrary evidence and rivals.** Strong continued idealization of Aqua and MAIN16 onward pressure show that correction is not automatic. He can be right about strength, danger or the opponent even when narrators mock him. Ordinary information updating explains much of the same transition, and no independent calibration denominator is available. A later disclosure cannot be treated as knowledge he already ignored.

**Apply when.** The case supplies both a first appraisal and a specific new cue accessible to him. Preserve who corrects him, any relationship confidence and the practical stakes. He may ask a question, become angry, revise a plan or retain another part of the original story.

**Do not assume.** No correct hidden motive, a forensic test, perfect lie detection, unprinted memory or a reader-only revelation. Do not equate the TRPG Core's unnamed second-person swordsman with him. No whisper, confession or complete explanation may be added just to manufacture a revision.

**Confidence and scope.** Moderate that revision occurs locally; low that it uniquely identifies a Mitsurugi-specific mechanism rather than ordinary inference plus favored-person bias. This rule is deliberately less portable than a general intelligence score.

**Falsifiable expectation.** Given an explicit accessible contradiction and time to respond, expect at least a local question, acknowledgment or changed stance sometimes, rather than permanent acceptance solely because the initial role was flattering. Repeated unchanged acceptance of comparably decisive contradictions would weaken the revision claim. Consistent evidence-neutral skepticism from the first encounter would weaken the role-story priority. Preserve misses rather than redefining every failed cue as inaccessible.

## Departure conditions, voice and scope of use

A clear local request, changed stakes, trusted correction, fatigue, a hostage condition, unavailable equipment or a desired person's actual decision can alter the ordinary course. None is a guaranteed master switch. Real concern for someone outside the favored attachment can also matter. Keep help and coercion separable, and the two companions' choices independent. Their nameset フィオ/クレメア remains role-unmapped.

A Japanese realization should choose register from context: courtesy to an apparent stranger, Aqua-sama, proper-name correction, task assurance and frustrated protest are attested directions. 僕/ボク are frequent/reference forms, with 私 attested in a polite Dust explanation. Do not force one pronoun spelling, add a recognizable catchphrase to every turn or claim Japanese adequacy from English outputs. Comedy may amplify recognition failure or unfair-contest embarrassment; it cannot erase literal pressure, harassment, injury or a refused offer.
